# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Casper is running a "Stay Cool Sale" and leading with 20% off cooling mattresses, which — given it's the middle of summer — makes sense seasonally. But honestly, the first thing I see when I open the site is a giant privacy consent modal completely covering the hero, which is a rough first impression for a brand I already trust. What little I can see signals a clean, premium sleep brand pushing a seasonal deal, but the modal makes it hard to evaluate anything below the nav.

## What's working

- **Announcement bar copy** — "Save 20% on cooling mattresses" is direct and immediately readable even over the modal mess. I clocked it in a second.
- **Category icon nav** — Mattresses, Pillows, Furniture, Take the Quiz are all one tap from the home screen. Pillows are literally the second icon, which matters to me because that's usually why I come back.
- **Seasonal alignment** — A cooling sale in late July is the right read. Casper tracks this well from the podcast ads I've heard; they know when to push the sleep-temperature angle.
- **Casper logo + brand recall** — Clean wordmark, minimal nav. The brand is not trying too hard. For someone who already trusts Casper, this feels familiar and low-noise (when the modal isn't blocking everything).

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal as the first UX moment** — This is the most damaging thing on the page. On mobile, it covers the entire hero. If I'm a new visitor I'm confused; if I'm a returning customer I'm annoyed. Casper's whole brand promise is "frictionless sleep" and this is pure friction.
- **Zero returning-customer acknowledgment** — I own the Original. I've been through the 100-night trial. There's no "Welcome back," no loyalty points balance, no "upgrade your sleep" hook for existing owners. The page talks to strangers.
- **Hero is completely obscured** — I can see "STAY COOL SALE" in what looks like a serif slab headline and the first line of "Save 20% on S…" but that's it. I genuinely cannot evaluate the hero because the modal kills it.
- **No pillow-specific offer visible** — Pillows are the reason I come back and add to cart to hit free shipping. The promo is mattress-centric; nothing hooks accessories buyers above the fold.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — That threshold is literally my decision variable for whether I add a pillow to cart. Not seeing it is a miss.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or auto-accept the cookie modal for returning visitors** (or at minimum use a slim banner rather than a full-page takeover on mobile) — the brand investment Casper has made in podcast trust evaporates in one second of cookie wall.
- **Add a returning-customer ribbon or personalized banner** — even a simple "Welcome back, Cassia — the Snow Pillow pairs with your Original" would outperform the generic sale hero for a logged-in or cookied returning buyer.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the announcement bar** — swap or rotate "Save 20% on cooling mattresses" with "Free shipping on orders over $[X]" so accessories shoppers like me have an immediate incentive to add to cart.
- **Separate the pillow/accessory offer from the mattress offer** — run a second announcement bar slide specifically for cooling pillows; the carousel arrows are already there, use them.
