# Hot sleeper? We've got you.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Casper breaks from the raw percentage drumbeat that defined their July sprint and instead leads with identity: "Hot sleeper? We've got you." is a persona-acknowledgment hook, not a discount hook. The body follows suit — instead of one product hammered home, the email walks through four distinct pillows with mini customer reviews and a quiz CTA. That's the right strategic instinct after twelve straight promotional sends.
- The problem is context. This is at minimum the thirteenth email from Casper in July, and the discount just *stepped back up*: the July 16 send offered 20% and this one is back to 25%. A subscriber who's paying attention doesn't feel a better deal — they feel incoherence. "While you still can" is soft urgency at best, and there's no hard date visible anywhere in the render.
- The creative is the strongest thing Casper has put out since the July 11 Snow Hybrid product-education email. The execution undermines it by not committing to a deadline and by not resolving the discount confusion.

## What's working

- **Identity-led subject line** ("Hot sleeper?") frames the email as a solution to a specific problem, not a sale announcement — genuinely differentiated from every prior send.
- **Four-product discovery structure** with individual Shop CTAs and review quotes gives the email legs; it's educational without sacrificing purchase intent.
- **Customer review quotes** are visible under each product — real names, specific language — and add social proof without feeling fabricated.
- **Visual hierarchy** is clean: hero banner → headline → pillow finder callout → individual product cards → quiz. The eye flows naturally.
- **"Take the Pillow Quiz" CTA** at the bottom intelligently captures undecided buyers instead of forcing a cold decision.

## What's weak

- **Offer stepped backward**: July 16 sent "Save 20%" and today's send reads "Save up to 25%." That's confusing for subscribers tracking these emails — it implies the 20% was either a mistake or the current "25%" is inflated from a higher baseline.
- **No hard deadline visible**: "Sleep cooler while you still can" is decoration, not urgency. There's no date, countdown, or badge that pins this sale to a real close.
- **Discount language is hedged throughout**: "Save up to 25%*" with asterisks on every instance trains readers to discount the discount.
- **Four products is one too many**: Going from the quiz to four separate product cards creates mild decision paralysis; a "top pick for hot sleepers" lead product would anchor the choice.
- **Cadence is deeply fatigued**: By email thirteen-plus in a month, even a strong creative pivot is arriving in an overheated inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — pin "Ends Sunday 11:59PM" next to the 25% banner. Every prior Casper send that landed above 8/10 had a visible date; this one doesn't.
- 2. **Pick one hero product** — the Snow Hybrid or the Original Cooling Pillow — and lead with it as the "hot sleeper's pick." Push the other three below the fold. One recommendation converts better than four equally presented options.
- 3. **Explain the discount step-up** — if 25% is legitimately better than last week's 20%, say "Back to 25%" or "Biggest cooling pillow discount of the month." Don't let it land as arbitrary.
- 4. **Strip the asterisk on the primary hero** — the "up to" hedge belongs in fine print, not in the 48px headline.
- 5. **Move the quiz above individual product cards** — quiz completion → specific recommendation → single product CTA is a cleaner conversion path than browse-all-four.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hot sleeper? 25% off cooling pillows — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Find your cooling pillow (quiz takes 30 sec)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Original, Foam Snow, or Hybrid — we'll match you. Sale ends Sunday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 cooling pillows, 25% off. Not sure which? Quiz inside.`
