# Homepage snapshot · casper.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I open casper.com and the first thing I get is a cookie consent wall plastered over the entire homepage. I can barely see a Memorial Day hero peeking out underneath, and the only thing I can actually read is a thin promo banner at the top saying "Save up to 30% on mattresses." This is a brand I have bought from before — I own the Original — and I still can't get past a privacy modal to see what they're selling. Not a great welcome back.

## What's working

- **Promo banner:** "Save up to 30% on mattresses" sits above the cookie modal and lands before anything else — as a returning mattress buyer, I clock this immediately and it's the right category hook
- **Top nav icons (Mattresses / Pillows / Furniture / Take the Quiz):** even with the modal up, I can see these four shortcuts; Pillows is one tap from where I'd naturally go to hit a free-shipping threshold
- **Memorial Day theming barely visible:** the glimpse of a holiday-sale hero tells me the timing is intentional; they're running a real campaign, not a stale evergreen page

## What's weak

- **The cookie wall is the homepage:** I cannot see the hero, the CTA, the product imagery, or any social proof — the entire experience is gated behind a consent modal on first load
- **Three cookie buttons, zero product CTAs:** "Reject Optional Cookies," "Accept Optional Cookies," and "Your Privacy Choices" are competing for my attention instead of a shop-now button
- **No 100-night trial mention anywhere visible:** that trial is the single biggest reason I bought Casper in the first place — it should be above the fold, not buried behind consent flows
- **No returning-customer recognition:** I'm a known Casper buyer; there's no loyalty hook, no member pricing badge, nothing that says "welcome back, we know you"
- **"Save up to 30%"** with no floor or product callout — "up to" is the weakest version of a discount claim; at least name the mattress model on sale

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 3-5 seconds or move it to a corner banner** so the hero, the CTA, and the Memorial Day offer load first — nobody is going to click "Accept" on a wall before they've seen one product
- **Add the 100-night trial callout to the promo banner** — even one line like "Save up to 30% · Free returns within 100 nights" turns a vague discount into a reason to click
- **Surface a member/returning-buyer ribbon** for logged-in users (or cookie-matched visitors) that says "Welcome back — your 30% off is live" to close the loop on loyalty
- **Make the free-shipping threshold explicit in the promo strip** — I literally add a pillow to every order to hit free shipping; just tell me the number so I can plan the cart before I even start browsing
