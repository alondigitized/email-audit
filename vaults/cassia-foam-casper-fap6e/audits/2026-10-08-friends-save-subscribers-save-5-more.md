# Friends save. Subscribers save +5% more.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-driven mattress catalog send. The hero says "Subscriber Exclusive: Extra 5% on top of up to 30% savings with code FF-EXT5.*". Below it sit six mattress cards (The One, Cloud One, Snow, Dream, Snow Max, Dream Max), each with a customer quote and a "Shop" button. The subject, "Friends save. Subscribers save +5% more.*", pays off in the hero. The code FF-EXT5 ties it to the Friends & Family run from 09/27 to 10/07.
- The social proof is the strongest part. Every mattress has a named testimonial, a star badge and a distinct label like "Bestselling Foam mattress," "Cooling Favorite" or "Maximum Support." A trust strip follows the cards: 110,000+ 5-star reviews, a 10-year warranty and a 100-night trial. That gives a shopper real reasons to pick one mattress over another.
- The offer stack is messy, though. Each card shows a different percentage (20%, 25%, 30%) plus "extra 5%" plus a code, all in small circular badges. The message is also long, at roughly six screens of mattress modules. It reads like the fourth Friends & Family variant in under two weeks: 09/27 "Save 25% for 3 days only!*", 09/29 "Last chance to save 25%!*", 09/30 "Friends and fam sale!", 10/07 "Final hours: Extra 5% on bundles*". Overall it's competent, well-proofed and fatigued, with a reasonable subscriber-exclusive angle.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer and the code (FF-EXT5) together, with a "Shop mattresses" button directly below.
- Each product card has a named customer quote, so the proof is specific instead of generic.
- The star and label badges ("Bestselling," "Cooling Favorite," "Maximum Support") help a shopper choose between mattresses.
- The "Loved by people who sleep" strip puts reviews, warranty and the 100-night trial in one scannable place.
- The "Subscriber Exclusive" framing gives the 5% a reason to exist, and the render has no broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero badge says "up to 30%" while the cards show 20%, 25% and 30%. The percentages are tiny and stacked with "+ extra 5%" and the code, so the real price is hard to work out.
- Six near-identical mattress cards make the email very long, and the repeated code on every badge is cluttered.
- The hero's "Our love language is discounts" line is fluff that pushes the offer down.
- The accessories and bedding modules at the bottom ("Shop adjustables," "Shop pillows") dilute a mattress-first email.
- The asterisks and the fine print at the bottom hide the terms, which the message never explains up front.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the six cards to the top three (The One, Snow, Dream) and add one "compare all mattresses" link. This shortens the email and sharpens the click.
- 2. Put one clear sale price or "from $X" on each card, not a stack of percentages. Say "Use code FF-EXT5 at checkout" once, in the hero, and drop it from the badges.
- 3. Replace "Our love language is discounts" with a concrete line such as "Your extra 5% ends Sunday." Add a real deadline, since the hero has none.
- 4. Move the accessories and bedding modules into a separate send, or use a single banner.
- 5. Space out the Friends & Family sends so subscribers don't see four variants in two weeks.
