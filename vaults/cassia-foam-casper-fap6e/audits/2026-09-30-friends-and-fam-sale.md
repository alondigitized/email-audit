# Friends and fam sale!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's third Friends & Family send in four days, and the weakest of the three. On 09/27 ("Save 25% for 3 days only!*") and 09/29 ("Last chance to save 25%!*") the offer was one flat 25% with the code FF2026 and an urgency badge. Today it becomes a tiered ladder with no code and no deadline. The hero says "Save up to 30% on everything.*" and the body walks down through 25% on Hybrids and Maxes, 30% on bundles, 20% on 2+ pillows and 10% on everything else. The hero's "up to 30%" is also lower than the "35% + extra 5%" the brand ran in mid-September, so the offer feels like it is shrinking.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan. The pale-blue field, the big blue percentage numerals and the one-button-per-tier structure make each offer obvious. The Casper stack, the bundle shot and the pillow shot carry the message well. The "Psst!" script and the "Friends & Family Sale" badge give it some personality.
- But the ladder is confusing. The hero says "up to 30%", while the headline mattress category gets 25%, so the hero number applies only to bundles. There is no visible end date, no code, and no explanation of what "everything else" excludes. Below the offers, the "Air your daymares" NYC FC block pulls the eye away from the sale. After the 09/29 "closing bell" send, a subscriber will see this as a reset and start expecting rolling extensions. Judgement: tidy execution of a muddled, undated offer. It is a solid mid-tier send that doesn't earn the 7s of the last two.

## What's working

- Large blue percentage numerals ("30%", "25%", "30%", "20%", "10%") make each tier readable at a glance.
- Every tier has its own CTA in a consistent pill button: "Shop mattresses", "Shop bundles" and "Shop pillows".
- The hero image of a person jumping on a bed is lively and fits the "Psst!" tone.
- Copy stays on brand: "Our love language is discounts" and "We never sleep on friendship."
- The SMS signup and the mattress quiz give useful secondary paths at the bottom.

## What's weak

- The hero promises "up to 30%" while the first section offers 25%. The top number doesn't match the first offer a reader sees.
- There is no deadline, urgency badge or code visible. Two days after "ENDS TONIGHT" it isn't clear whether this is a new window or a rerun.
- The "*Savings based on comp. value" footnote under the hero and the dense legal block at the bottom leave the terms feeling buried.
- The NYC FC "Air your daymares" promo takes a full block after the offers and has nothing to do with the sale. Its CTA, "Submit your daymare", competes with the shopping CTAs.
- The four-tile "everything else" grid has small pill buttons, and "Shop accessories" and "Shop mattress protectors" are generic for a 10% tier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible end date and urgency line to the hero, for example "Ends Sunday", and put the code or "auto-applied at checkout" next to the CTA. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Make the hero number match the first offer. Either lead with "30% off bundles" or put the 30% bundle section first.
- 3. Give the hero CTA a specific verb and noun, like "Shop bundles, save 30%", instead of "Shop Friends & Family Sale".
- 4. Move the NYC FC block below the footer nav, or cut it from sale sends.
- 5. Bring the fine print up: one line under the hero saying which items are excluded.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends & fam: up to 30% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off bundles for friends of Casper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bundles 30%, mattresses 25%, pillows 20%. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Friends & Family savings, tier by tier. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is well-formed, with a preheader, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals and `role="presentation"` layout tables. The source is truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s could not be inspected. Three of the four QA warnings come from the relay capturing headers, not from confirmed defects. The alt-text warning appears to be a false positive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and 23 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA data reports no failures, but the 23 skipped links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 18,714 of 20,948 characters (about 89%) are URLs, so the links are long tracked/redirect URLs. This is a deliverability risk (spam-filter heuristics on link-heavy text parts). It is not a functional break.
- **Open pixel:** `https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/CgxqCN4759eg…/open` is a 1x1 image with `style="display: none;"`. It is Bloomreach/Exponea tracking and works as intended. Clients that block images won't record opens.
- **No UTM parameters** are visible in the truncated source (see section 6).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA "Image missing alt text" is a false positive.** The flagged open pixel has `alt=""` in the source, which is the correct markup for a decorative or tracking image. No fix is needed.
- **Web fonts load through non-MSO `<link>` tags** to `brxcdn.com/.../media/original/9c4a4f1a-…` and `9c30fa4c-…`. Gmail and several other clients strip external stylesheets, so those clients will use fallback fonts. Whether the font stacks have safe fallbacks can't be checked in the truncated source.
- **Preheader is present but duplicated.** There is a hidden `<span>` ("Massive savings for our buddies…") and a second hidden `<div>` of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` padding characters. This is a standard pattern and not a defect. The `<div>` lacks `mso-hide:all`, but that is minor.
- **Mobile styles:** the `@media (max-width:620px)` block only stacks columns, hides `.mobile_hide` and resizes one heading. The rest of the responsive behavior couldn't be verified.
- **Other checks:** the `<title>` is present, and `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` on the layout tables are correct.
- Alt text on content images and link text couldn't be checked because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject line. The body was truncated, so this only covers the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it should be verified against raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. This isn't a confirmed failure.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link can't be confirmed in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was reported.
- The sending domain `e.casper.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is good for alignment. Alignment itself is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No `href` values with UTM parameters or landing-page URLs appear in the truncated source, and the probe results don't list destinations. The likely tracked-redirect structure (see the plain-text finding) would also hide the final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the unsubscribe headers.** Check the raw message at the origin (or through a seed inbox) for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Treat the QA warning as unresolved until then.
2. **Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC** with a direct inbox test, such as the Gmail "Show original" view. The relay strips this information.
3. **Suppress the alt-text warning for `alt=""` pixels** in the QA check. This is a tooling false positive.
4. **Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part.** Alternatively, confirm the text part isn't auto-generated from tracked links. This is low priority.
5. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML.** The current truncation blocks footer, UTM, alt-text, merge-token and fallback-font checks. Also probe the 23 skipped links, at least the unsubscribe and preference-center links.
