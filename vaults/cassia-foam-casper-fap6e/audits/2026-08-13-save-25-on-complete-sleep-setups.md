# Save 25% on complete sleep setups*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's clearest "Back to School" send yet, and it's the first in this run to actually match subject to hero: "Save 25% on complete sleep setups*" and "Save 25% on Bundles." sit right on top of each other with an unambiguous "Shop bundles" button, no scroll needed. Unlike the August 8th "extra 5%" send that buried its real offer below a bigger competing module, or the August 9th send that repeated its own structural mistake two days later, this one commits to one number (25%) and repeats it consistently across every bundle module below the fold — mattress bundles, build-your-own, the best-selling bundle, bedding bundles, Freshman Bundle, Comfy Bundle. That consistency is new for this brand's recent cadence.
- The email is long — nine distinct product/bundle modules stacked vertically — but it's organized cleanly into a bundle section and a bedding-refresh section, each with its own sub-hero and CTA, so it reads more like a well-organized bundle catalog than the confusing mixed-signal sends from earlier in the month. The asterisk on the hero offer ("Save 25% on Bundles.*") paired with the fine-print "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" is a mild honesty flag worth noting but it's disclosed on-screen, not hidden.
- Judgement: strongest structural execution of the "25%" campaign line so far — matches the August 6th send's clarity ("Vacation's Over. Sleep Isn't.") but with more product depth. It loses points versus that send for length/scroll fatigue and the asterisked value-based discount framing.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("Save 25% on Bundles.*") is legible and matches the email subject almost verbatim — no bait-and-switch between subject and hero, unlike the August 8th send.
- "Shop bundles" CTA button is high-contrast navy-on-white and sits directly under the hero copy, fully visible without scrolling.
- Every module below the fold reinforces the same 25% discount with a consistent red "Save 25%" badge, so the offer never gets diluted or contradicted by a competing bigger number.
- Section headers ("Start with the right mattress," "Build your own bundle," "Bedding bundles made for fresh starts") give the long email real scannable structure.
- Back-to-school seasonal framing ("dorm upgrades," "move-in day," "fresh starts") is current and consistent throughout, not just in the hero.

## What's weak

- The email is very long — nine product modules before reaching footer deals ("The ABCs of better sleep") — which risks fatigue and buries later bundles (Freshman/Comfy) that are arguably more targeted for back-to-school shoppers.
- The asterisk on "Save 25% on Bundles.*" with "*Pricing based on Comp. Value" fine print is a soft honesty ding — comp-value-based discounts read as less concrete than a straight markdown.
- Footer deals section ("Mattresses Save 20%," "Pillows Save 20% on 2+") introduces a second, lower discount tier late in the email, which slightly muddies the single-offer clarity built up above.
- No urgency/deadline language anywhere in the visible render — no "ends," no date — so there's nothing pushing a reader to act now versus later.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Freshman Bundle and Comfy Bundle modules (most relevant to the "back to school" hook) up near the hero, ahead of the generic "build your own bundle" and "best-selling bundle" modules — right now the most seasonally-targeted content is buried two-thirds down.
- 2. Cut the email roughly in half — combine "Start with the right mattress" and "Build your own bundle" into one module, since they're both selling the same idea (customize your setup).
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero ("Save 25% through Sunday") — every recent Casper send in this history lacks credible urgency, and it's the single easiest lift across the whole campaign.
- 4. Drop or shrink the comp-value asterisk, or move the disclosure inline near the badge instead of only in hero fine print, so the 25% reads as a direct discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bundle & save 25% on your whole sleep setup*`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dorm move-in? Save 25% on Casper bundles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mattress, pillows, sheets — one bundle, one 25% discount.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New room, better sleep: bundles start at 25% off.`
