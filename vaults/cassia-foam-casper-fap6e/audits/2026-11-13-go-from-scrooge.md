# Go from Scrooge...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a co-branded Casper x *Ebenezer* (Paramount) holiday send, and the creative is charming. The frosted navy hero, the "If Scrooge had a Casper…" headline and the script type all read as a real seasonal campaign. The trouble is that the money offer, "Save 30% on Hybrids and Maxes" plus an extra 10% with code 10BFCM, is buried at the bottom. The first four screens sell a movie tie-in and a pun.
- The email carries three different asks: a bundles offer (25%), accessories, and a movie ticket push ("NOW PLAYING"). Each gets a red button, so nothing wins. The "Ends Tonight" badge is the strongest urgency in the send, and it sits below the fold. A subscriber who saw the 11/12 "Ends tomorrow: Extra 10% off Hybrids and Maxes" send would find this one's deal identical, but it's hidden under a film promo.
- Verdict: good brand fun, weak at converting. It's a better brand moment than a promo, and it should have led with the code.

## What's working

- The hero is distinctive. The frost-bordered navy, the Casper x Ebenezer lockup and the script headline feel like a real seasonal event.
- The Scrooge puns ("stop being such a Scrooge," "Because Scrooge would never pay retail") keep the voice consistent and light.
- The offer block at the bottom is clear. "Ends Tonight" in a lime pill, a big "Save 30%", the code 10BFCM and a white "Shop now" button all stand out on navy.
- The product photos are clean and aspirational, with the adjustable base and the glowing Glow light.
- The bottom nav, SMS signup and quiz/store links give a recipient useful next steps.

## What's weak

- The real offer (30% + 10% with code) doesn't appear until about 70% of the way down. The hero has no price or percentage in it.
- Red "Shop Casper," "Shop bundles," "Shop accessories" and "NOW PLAYING" buttons all look the same, so the eye has no priority.
- "NOW PLAYING" sends the reader to a movie, which is off-category for a mattress buyer and competes with the sale.
- "Save 25% when you buy in bundles" conflicts with the 30% + 10% offer, so the deal is hard to read. The asterisks and the dense fine print make it feel hedged.
- The "Ends Tonight" urgency is placed last, though it's the best reason to click.
- The hero's "Casper × Paramount Pictures" sticker covers part of the bed image and adds clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put the "Ends Tonight: 30% + extra 10% with 10BFCM" band, with a "Shop Hybrids & Maxes" button, directly under the Scrooge headline. Keep the movie art as the backdrop.
- 2. Give the buttons a hierarchy. Make one primary (red or lime) for the sale, and turn the bundles, accessories and movie buttons into text or ghost links.
- 3. Drop or demote the "25% bundles" claim, or fold it into the same offer. Show a single price story.
- 4. Replace the "NOW PLAYING" button with a small footer-style tie-in, or put it above the footer.
- 5. Make the button copy specific: "Shop Hybrids & Maxes" rather than "Shop Casper."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 30% + extra 10% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Scrooge never paid retail. Neither should you.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 10BFCM stacks an extra 10% on Hybrids and Maxes. Last day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A better night's sleep, 40% off, ends at midnight.`

# Technical Audit: Casper, "Go from Scrooge..."

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is well-formed, with a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. Most warnings are capture limits of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed defects. The HTML is truncated, so body links and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Open pixel:** `https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/CgxqCN47…/open` is a 1×1 `<img style="display: none;">`. Some clients and privacy proxies block or pre-fetch hidden images, so open data from this pixel is unreliable.
- **Test-labelled token:** The pixel path is a base64 token. It appears to decode to `frequency_segment` and `Core Sleepers Test`, though I decoded it by eye from a truncated string. If that is right, the send was a test or a segment named "Test". Verify this isn't a mislabelled production segment in Bloomreach/Exponea.
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed with no broken-link failures reported. The other 18 were skipped by design as footer or social links, so their health is unverified.
- **Plain-text part:** 15,751 of 17,634 characters (about 89%) are URLs. This is consistent with long tracked or redirect URLs. It can raise spam-filter scoring on the text/plain alternative, and it is a real, low-severity warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text on the open pixel:** The QA warning "Image missing alt text: open" is a false positive. The tag has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative or tracking image.
- **External stylesheets:** Two `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags point to `brxcdn.com`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and several other clients strip `<link>` tags, so any web fonts will fall back to system fonts there. Confirm the inline font stacks have sensible fallbacks.
- **Preheader:** The `display:none` span, the `&#8199;&#847;` / `&shy;` padding block and the 1×1 pixel are all hidden correctly with `mso-hide:all`. No issues.
- **Structure:** The container table uses `role="presentation"`, and the mobile breakpoint is at 620px. No issues found in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The truncated body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA note says the relay may strip them. Check the raw message at the sending ESP (Exponea/Bloomreach). Without them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) would not be met.
- **Authentication-Results:** This header is missing. It is expected through the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.casper.com` are unverified. Do not treat this as a failure.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** These were not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be confirmed. Probing reportedly always includes an unsubscribe link, but no result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No `href` values or probe results (redirect chains, final URLs, UTM parameters) were provided, so no continuity issues can be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the original message headers at the ESP to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. This is the only item with real compliance risk.
2. Confirm the `Core Sleepers Test` segment label is intentional, and check the send wasn't to a test list.
3. Shorten tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or add more descriptive plain text around the links, to lower the URL ratio.
4. Suppress the "missing alt" QA warning for `alt=""` images, since `alt=""` is valid for tracking pixels.
5. Verify font fallbacks for clients that strip the external `<link>` CSS.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check UTMs, the footer address and the unsubscribe link.
