# Save 35%: Labor Day Doorbusters are here*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's biggest headline number of the campaign — "Save 35%" leads before any brand chrome even loads, with "Doorbuster" framing borrowed straight from Black Friday vocabulary. Visually it's the cleanest, most confident send in this run: clear hero, two unambiguous orange CTAs, a well-organized "Executive Track" mid-section for Snow Max and Dream Max, and a tidy four-up "More Ways to Restore" grid closing things out. No render bugs, no lorem ipsum, nothing broken.
- But it's also the fifth "35%" or "extra 5%" message in nine days (2026-08-23 through 2026-08-30), and this one doesn't even bother with a new angle — it's a straight discount blast with a doorbuster label slapped on top. The "Best Labor Day deal" starburst badge appears twice, verbatim, undercutting its own urgency. Compared to 08-24's "Save 35% on bundles + an extra 5%," this is a smaller ask (35% only, no stacked extra 5%) dressed up with louder packaging.
- Judgement: strong execution, weak novelty. A subscriber who opened any of the last three Casper sends has already seen this exact "35% off Max Mattresses" beat.

## What's working

- Hero copy is unmissable — "Save 35% on Max Mattresses" is the largest text on the page, no scrolling needed to see the offer.
- Two CTAs above the fold ("Shop Max Mattresses" and "Shop Labor Day Sale") give both a product-specific and category-level path with zero ambiguity.
- The "Executive Track" section cleanly separates Snow Max vs. Dream Max with distinct lifestyle photography, matching badges, and individual CTAs — easy to scan and compare.
- The closing grid (Hybrid, Premium Foam, Bundles, Pillows) gives a legitimate reason to keep scrolling even after the hero offer, each with its own visible % off.
- Clean rendering throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent Casper navy/orange/mint palette.

## What's weak

- The "Best Labor Day deal" badge is reused identically on both Snow Max and Dream Max — two products can't both be "best," and repeating the same badge twice reads as templated, not curated.
- "Doorbuster" implies a flash/limited-inventory event but there's no countdown, no stock indicator, no deadline anywhere in the email — it's just a discount with retail-event vocabulary borrowed for urgency it doesn't back up.
- Zero social proof — no star ratings, no review counts, despite "REVIEWS" sitting right there in the nav bar, unused in the body.
- This is the same 35%-off-mattresses offer subscribers have now seen in some form on 08-23, 08-24, and implicitly since — cadence fatigue is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the duplicate badge — give Snow Max and Dream Max distinct callouts (e.g., "Best for cooling" vs. "Best for support") instead of the identical "Best Labor Day deal" starburst on both.
- 2. Add a real deadline to justify "Doorbuster" — a countdown clock or "ends [date/time]" line under the hero headline turns borrowed urgency into credible urgency.
- 3. Insert a one-line review/rating snippet near the hero or under each Max product ("4.8★ from 12,000+ sleepers") — the nav already promises reviews, the body never delivers.
- 4. Vary the creative from the last four sends — new hero photography or a different value prop (e.g., trial/return policy, cooling tech deep-dive) would break the "same 35%, different day" pattern this brand's history shows.

- **Subject Alt B:** `35% off Snow Max & Dream Max — Labor Day only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our most advanced cooling + support, now 35% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbuster pricing on Casper's top-rated mattresses.`
