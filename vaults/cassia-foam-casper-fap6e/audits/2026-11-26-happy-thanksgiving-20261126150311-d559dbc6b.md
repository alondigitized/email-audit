# Happy Thanksgiving.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Thanksgiving gratitude email that turns into a Black Friday sale halfway down. The first half is testimonials: three customer quotes ("My back simply stopped aching"), each with a bed photo and a "Shop Snow / Max / The One" button. It's warm and on-brand. It's also soft: no offer shows until the "Empty plates, full carts" pivot, roughly 40% of the way down.
- The sale half is solid: "Save 35% on Max Mattresses*" and a six-tile grid (30% foam, 30% hybrid, 35% bundles). But the dashboard-style lead, "Happy Thanksgiving. We're thankful for you," gives a skimmer nothing to act on. Compared with the 11/24 send (giant "35%" hero, "Black Friday Doorbuster" pill, 8/10), this one buries the money. It's a decent holiday-goodwill send, but it dilutes a peak sales day.
- Judgement: good tone, wrong order. Lead with the deal and keep the thanks as the wrapper.

## What's working

- Three real-sounding customer quotes with specific outcomes (back pain, sleep scores) add credibility.
- Each testimonial links to a specific mattress (Snow, Snow Max, The One).
- The navy-and-lime palette and script headline match the earlier Black Friday sends.
- The tiered discount grid (35/30/20/10%) is easy to scan.
- The footer has a text-signup callout and a clear category nav.

## What's weak

- No offer above the fold. The hero is only "We're thankful for you."
- The three testimonial blocks push the actual sale roughly 1,000px down.
- Three near-identical small white buttons ("Shop Snow," "Shop Snow Max," "Shop The One") compete, and none says what the deal is.
- "Shop sale" is generic, and the asterisked fine print is tiny and dense.
- The hero text sits over a dark, faint photo, which is hard to read.
- No deadline or code is visible in the body. Earlier sends featured 5-BFCM and "ends tonight" urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Save 35% on Max Mattresses" hero and the Black Friday grid above the testimonials. Use one testimonial as a supporting block.
- 2. Put a single deadline in the hero (e.g., "Doorbuster live now through Monday").
- 3. Replace the three product buttons with one primary CTA such as "Shop 35% off Max."
- 4. Reduce the hero to one line of thanks plus the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Thanks, from Casper. 35% off Max starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our thank-you: up to 35% off mattresses`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Doorbuster pricing is live on Max, Hybrid and Foam. Plus what customers are saying.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dinner's done. The Black Friday Doorbuster is on.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO conditionals and a preheader. The automated QA flags no hard failures. Its four warnings are mostly header-visibility gaps, plus one real plain-text bloat issue. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 27 (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). It reports no failed probes, but the skipped links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display: none;">`. It is placed directly in `<body>` and uses `display:none`. Some clients and proxies skip hidden images, which can undercount opens.
- **The "Image missing alt text" warning is a false positive.** The source shows `alt=""` on this pixel, which is the correct form for a decorative or tracking image. No fix is needed.
- **Tracking IDs are long and opaque.** This feeds the plain-text issue in section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs (confirmed by QA).** 20,188 of 21,961 characters (about 92%) are URLs. That suggests the text alternative is dominated by long tracking links. Spam filters can penalize this, and screen-reader or text-only users get a poor experience.
- **Web fonts load from external stylesheets.** Two `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags point to `brxcdn.com/us1-app-storage/…/media/original/…`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. This is correct for Outlook. Whether fallback font stacks exist in inline styles can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Preheader is present.** The text is "Sleep better this holiday season." and it is hidden with `mso-hide:all` plus a padding block of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;`. No issue.
- **Mobile CSS is present.** There is a `@media (max-width:620px)` block with `.stack .column` stacking. No issue visible in the retained source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the retained source. The truncated remainder was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers, so it is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo make one-click unsubscribe mandatory, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The sender domain `e.casper.com` can't be validated from the data provided.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address** (CAN-SPAM) are not visible in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result is not reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
No findings can be made. The retained source shows no `href` values, so UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and redirect behavior can't be verified. Probe results for the 12 links were not provided beyond the absence of failures.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check raw headers** on the original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.casper.com`). Do not rely on the relay's copy.
2. **Shorten the plain-text alternative.** Use fewer or shorter links, or a concise text version, so URLs are not about 92% of the content.
3. **Disregard the pixel alt-text warning** and adjust the QA check to ignore `alt=""` on 1×1 tracking images.
4. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** to verify the skipped 27 links, UTM consistency, the footer unsubscribe link and postal address, and any merge tokens.
