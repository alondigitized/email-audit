# Save 25% for 3 days only!*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper flipping to a "Friends & Family Exclusive" 25% code (FF2026) after weeks of the "35% + extra 5%" hammer and the 09/24 "September Savings" send — same flat 25%-off-everything mechanic, just a new wrapper. The hero is clean: single model, clear code, one CTA, no render bugs. But structurally it's identical to 09/24 and 09/20 — hero banner, trust-stat band, then a long vertical stack of "Save 25% on foam / hybrid / Max" product-tier sections repeating the same discount three times with different mattress names.
- The "Friends & Family Exclusive" framing is doing real work for urgency and specificity (implies invite-only, not evergreen), which slightly elevates this above a generic discount blast. But the fine print immediately undercuts it — "*Savings based on Comp. Value" is the same soft asterisk language Casper has used all month, and nothing on the exclusions is disclosed above the fold.
- Verdict: solid, on-brand, no execution flaws — but it's the same 25% offer in different packaging for the third time in two weeks, and the redundant three-section repeat (25% on foam, 25% on hybrid, 25% on Max) adds scroll without adding information.

## What's working

- Hero is uncluttered: one lifestyle photo, one badge ("Friends & Family Exclusive," "3 days only"), one code, one button — easy to scan in under 2 seconds.
- Discount code (FF2026) is visible without hunting, and the CTA "Shop now" is a real button, not a text link.
- Social proof band ("4 out of 5 customers would recommend us") is a nice trust beat placed right after the offer, before asking for more scrolling.
- Each product tile pairs a callout bubble with a genuine differentiator (firmness, cooling, support) rather than generic marketing copy.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The "25% on foam / 25% on hybrid / 25% on Max" structure repeats the identical number three times — by the third section the eye has stopped registering it as an offer and just sees more beds.
- "3 days only" urgency has no visible end date or day-of-week anchor, so it reads as generic countdown language rather than a credible deadline (same issue the 09/15 Labor Day finale had).
- "Friends & Family Exclusive" is a strong hook but isn't explained anywhere — no mention of why the subscriber was selected, which makes the exclusivity claim feel like copy rather than a real segment.
- Six near-identical mattress tiles with six identical "Shop now" buttons dilutes the primary CTA — there's no single hero product to click, just a menu.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the three repeated "Save 25%" section headers down to one summary line ("25% off every mattress — foam, hybrid, or Max") and let the product grid do the work; this alone would shorten the email by a third and stop the discount from feeling repetitive.
- 2. Put a real deadline on "3 days only" — a date or day name ("Ends Tuesday 9/30") converts vague urgency into something a reader can act on now instead of later.
- 3. Justify the "Friends & Family" framing with one line of copy (e.g., "You're on the list because you're already part of the Casper family") — otherwise it reads as a discount-code alias, not a segment.
- 4. Promote one bed (likely Dream, their best-seller per the 09/24 send) to a true hero product directly under the banner, with the rest as a secondary grid — gives the email one obvious click instead of six equal ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends & Family: 25% off, 3 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your invite: 25% off any mattress`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code FF2026 before Wednesday — every mattress included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Foam, hybrid, or Max — 25% off, friends-only pricing.`
