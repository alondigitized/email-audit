# Best in Class. Best in Sleep.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper's cleanest Back to School send yet — "Save up to 25%" and the hand-drawn green circle sit right at the top with an unambiguous "Shop the sale" button, no scroll required. Unlike the August 8th send where a bigger 20% offer buried the subject line's headline promise, this one keeps the hero number matching the subject's implied value and doesn't contradict itself lower down.
- Where it falls apart is the report-card gimmick stretched across nine near-identical polaroid-style product tiles ("New to Class," "Class Favorite," "Best Group Project," "Perfect Attendance," "Most Dependable," "Brightest Idea"). It's cute for two tiles and exhausting by tile seven — this is Casper's most product-dense send since the July sprint, but density without hierarchy just becomes scroll fatigue. The subject line "Best in Class. Best in Sleep." also undersells the actual news (25% off, $100 off a specific item) in favor of wordplay that doesn't tell a subscriber whether to open.
- Net: solid top-of-funnel execution, weak subject, and a body that overstays its welcome. A trim to 4-5 hero products would make this one of Casper's better sends this cycle.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("Save up to 25%") is visible immediately with a clear, high-contrast "Shop the sale" button — no scrolling needed on open.
- The lifestyle photo (mother and child reading in bed) reinforces the Back to School / family angle without feeling forced.
- Each product tile has its own specific discount badge (SAVE $100, SAVE 20%, SAVE 25%) rather than one blanket claim, which reads as more credible than a single vague headline.
- Gift card module at the bottom gives an easy out for undecided shoppers.
- No visible render bugs — images, badges, and CTAs all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Nine full-width product modules in a row is too many; by the fourth or fifth ("Hyperlite Sheet Set," "The Foundation") the report-card conceit stops adding value and just adds scroll.
- The "A+ Sleep Starts Here" secondary hero repeats the same back-to-school framing as the top module without adding new information — it's a second CTA before any product is shown.
- Subject line "Best in Class. Best in Sleep." is vague wordplay — it doesn't signal an offer, a deadline, or a category, which hurts open likelihood on a subject scan.
- No visible urgency/deadline anywhere in this send (unlike the August 8/9 sends' "ENDS MONDAY"), so there's no reason to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product list from nine tiles to the top 4-5 (mattress, pillow, bundle, sheets) — the report-card superlatives lose their punch by tile six, and a shorter email converts better than an exhaustive catalog.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to lead with the number, not the wordplay — "up to 25% off" is buried behind a pun right now.
- 3. Merge the two top hero modules ("Save up to 25%" and "A+ Sleep Starts Here") into one — right now the email spends its first two screens saying the same thing twice before showing a single product.
- 4. Add a credible deadline back in — this is the first recent Casper send without one, and it costs urgency.
