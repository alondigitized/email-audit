# October is fully booked 📚
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a themed Casper send that tries to do too much. The National Book Month angle ("Booked for the night") is charming and the subject earns a smile. But the email stacks three offers: free mattress removal (top banner), 25% off bundles, and a Was/Now size-up price table. They compete, and the book theme only connects to one of them.
- The first thing the eye lands on is the "Free mattress removal" banner, not the book story the subject promised. A reader who opens for books gets a removal promo first. The Book/Movie table (Pride & Prejudice, Frankenstein, The Shining, Dune) is the cleverest part and the most on-brand. It is also a long, text-heavy navy block that sells nothing directly.
- Judgment: good creative voice, muddled focus. It is a 6, and it would be a 7 or 8 if it committed to one offer. It follows the 10/26 "Free mattress removal*" send closely, so the removal banner reads as a repeat.

## What's working

- The book/screen pairing table is a distinctive, shareable content idea that fits Casper's "sleep culture" voice.
- Copy is witty: "Mattress? Pillows? Check. An aggressively long TBR list? Definitely check."
- The Was/Now table is easy to scan, with strikethrough prices and clear savings ($1,649 to $1,149 on King).
- Coral CTA buttons are consistent and stand out against the navy and photo backgrounds.
- The hero photo of a woman reading in bed sells the "cozy" mood well.

## What's weak

- The removal banner outranks the themed hero, so the subject's promise is delayed and the focus splits.
- There are three offers (removal, 25% bundles, size-up pricing) with no single lead.
- The hero CTA "Get cozy" is vague and doesn't say what the click leads to.
- The Book/Movie table is a long block with no links or products, so it adds scroll without adding clicks.
- The removal offer repeats the 10/26 send, and the heavy fine print under the price table reads as clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer. Either put the size-up pricing in the hero or the 25% bundles, and drop the removal banner, which was just sent.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Shop bundles, save 25%".
- 3. Shorten the book table to two rows and link each row to a product, such as a bed or pillow.
- 4. Move the price table higher, since it is the strongest buying content.
- 5. Add social proof, such as a rating or review count, near a CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 25% on your bedtime story 📚`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Book Month: size up and save up to $500`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bundles are 25% off, plus pairings from Dune to Pride & Prejudice.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your nightstand is calling. See this week's mattress deals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard Bloomreach/Exponea table layout, and the visible source has no hard defects. The QA run reported 0 failures and 4 warnings. Most of those warnings are visibility gaps (headers, truncated HTML), not confirmed bugs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text bloat:** 15,664 of 18,215 plain-text characters (about 86%) are URLs. That points to long, redirect-wrapped tracking URLs. This is a real finding, though only a moderate one. It can hurt text-part readability and spam scoring.
- **Link probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 17 were skipped by design (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). No broken or redirecting links were reported among the probed set. The skipped 17 are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open` is a 1x1 image with `display:none`. It works as intended. It is hidden with inline CSS and sits before the main table.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML cuts off at `<table class="nl-container"…`, so I can't audit the click-tracking wrappers or the CTA hrefs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA alt-text warning is a false positive.** It flags the open pixel (`src: …/open`), but the tag already has `alt=""`. An empty alt is correct for a decorative or tracking image, so no fix is needed. The check should exclude 1x1 or hidden tracking pixels.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `<title>`, the viewport meta, the MSO/VML namespaces and `OfficeDocumentSettings` for Outlook, `x-apple-data-detectors` overrides, and a mobile breakpoint at `max-width:620px`.
- **Preheader:** there are two hidden blocks, a `<span>` with `mso-hide:all` and a `<div>` of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` filler. This is a standard preheader-padding pattern and it is applied correctly.
- **External CSS:** two `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags load from `brxcdn.com`, inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and several other clients strip `<link>` tags, so any web fonts will fall back there. I can't confirm that the `font-family` stacks have safe fallbacks, because the body is truncated.
- **Image alt text and `role="presentation"`:** I can't verify either from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject ("October is fully booked 📚"), the preheader and the visible markup contain no unrendered tokens, such as `{{ }}`, `%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The body is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven defect. Check the raw message headers to confirm. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `e.casper.com`. This is a relay-capture limitation, not evidence of a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure. The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmed issues. The CTA hrefs are in the truncated portion, so I can't evaluate UTM parameters or destination alignment. The link probe reported no failures on the material links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-capture the raw message with full headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, DKIM signature) from a direct send, not the relay. This confirms or clears the three compliance warnings.
2. Suppress the alt-text check for hidden or 1x1 tracking pixels that already carry `alt=""`. It is a false positive in the QA tooling.
3. Raise or remove the HTML truncation limit so the CTA hrefs, UTM parameters, footer and `role` attributes can be audited.
4. Review the plain-text part. Shorten the tracked URLs or reduce the link count so it isn't about 86% URLs.
5. Probe the 17 skipped links, at least the footer and preference-center links, in a periodic full-coverage run.
