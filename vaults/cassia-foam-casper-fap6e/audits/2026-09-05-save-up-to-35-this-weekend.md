# Save up to 35% this weekend.*
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Casper closing out its Labor Day arc with the broadest headline yet — "Save up to 35% on everything.*" — after a run of narrower category and bundle framing. It's structurally the strongest send in the sequence: a clean hero, then an exhaustive, well-organized catalog scroll (Mattresses → Bundles → Pillows → Furniture → Bedding) each with a "Save X%" sub-badge and a "Top pick" callout. But by the fifth straight Labor Day email in two weeks, "everything" is the tell — this isn't a sharper offer, it's Casper running out of new angles and defaulting to the broadest possible claim on the last weekend of the campaign.
- The catalog-dump structure does real work compared to 2026-08-26's "Deep sleep. Deeper recovery." mismatch — here the hero promise ("35% on everything") and the body (literally every category) are honest and aligned. But it's also the least differentiated CTA of the arc: six-plus "Shop X" buttons with equal visual weight means there's no single thing pulling the eye, unlike the tighter single-hero sends earlier in August.
- Judgement: solid execution, weak strategically — a "last call, buy anything" email that closes the campaign competently but adds nothing new for a subscriber who's seen "35% off Labor Day" in their inbox five times this month.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and unambiguous: "Save up to 35% on everything.*" with one orange "Shop Labor Day Deals" CTA above the fold.
- Category sections are well-organized with consistent pattern (category → % badge → top pick → product shot → CTA), easy to scan on mobile.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- Per-category discount transparency (35% mattresses, 30% hybrid, 20% pillows, 10% furniture) reads more honest than a blanket "35%" claim would alone.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth "Labor Day / 35%" email in under two weeks (08-23, 08-24, 08-26, 08-28, 08-30, 08-31, 09-04) — fatigue risk is real and "everything" signals the campaign has run dry on new hooks.
- No single dominant CTA — with 8+ "Shop X" buttons of equal weight, there's no visual hierarchy pointing to one hero action.
- The reused hero bed-with-person photo and orange button look near-identical to 08-30/08-31 creative — no new visual energy for the campaign's presumed final weekend.
- "This weekend" urgency in the subject isn't visually reinforced anywhere prominent in the body (no countdown, no explicit end-date near the hero).

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero product/category (e.g., the Snow Max mattress, the best seller across the arc) and lead with it exclusively above the fold — cut the CTA count from 8 to 2-3 to restore hierarchy.
- 2. Add a visible deadline ("Ends Monday 11:59pm ET") near the hero CTA to make "this weekend" urgency credible, not just implied.
- 3. Since this is likely the campaign's last send, say so explicitly ("Last weekend to save") — closing urgency converts better than repeating "35%" for the sixth time.
- 4. Vary the hero image — the bed/person shot has now run at least twice; fresh creative would help against inbox fatigue from repeat opens.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Monday — 35% off mattresses, bundles & more*`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Every category, one weekend. Shop before Labor Day sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mattresses, bundles, pillows, furniture — all on sale through Monday.`
