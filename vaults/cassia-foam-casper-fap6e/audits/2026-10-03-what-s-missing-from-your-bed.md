# What’s missing from your bed? 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized catalog send that stretches the "complete your bed" idea across mattresses, bases, pillows, and bedding. The hero's headline, "What's Missing From Your Bed?", pays off the subject line. Every product tile has a navy percent-off badge and a blue "Shop now" button, so the eye knows where to go.
- The weakness is that the email is a broad sweep with no single hero offer. It lists nine different discounts (10%, 20%, 25%, 30%) and a bundle block at the bottom, and none of them leads. It also follows the 09/27–09/30 Friends & Family run ("Save 25% for 3 days only!*", "Last chance to save 25%!*", "Friends and fam sale!"). This one has no code and no deadline, so it reads like a calm reset and loses the urgency those sends built. It is a pleasant, scannable send, but it won't beat the 09/24 "Your Dream bed is calling" or 09/20 "The proof is in the sleeping" emails.
- Verdict: solid and on-brand, but mid-pack. The offer is diffuse and nothing tells the reader to act today.

## What's working

- The hero copy ("What's Missing From Your Bed?" with "Complete your bed") continues the subject line's curiosity hook.
- Every product tile has a bold navy "SAVE X%" badge, so the discount is visible at a glance without reading body copy.
- The sections are sequenced logically: mattress, then foundation and base, then pillows, then bedding. The numbered "First… Next… Finally…" headings make a long scroll feel guided.
- The layout is consistent, with a two-column grid, one blue "Shop now" button per tile, and generous spacing.
- The "Save 30% on bundles" block near the bottom is a good upsell, and the SMS signup strip gives a secondary conversion path.

## What's weak

- No hero offer stands out. Percentages ranging from 10% to 30% across nine tiles dilute the message, and the strongest deal (30% off the Adjustable Base) gets no more emphasis than 10% off sheets.
- There is no deadline, code, or "ends" language anywhere, so nothing gives the reader a reason to click today.
- Every tile uses the same generic "Shop now" button. Copy such as "Shop pillows" or "Shop the Dream" would be more specific.
- The product tiles have no price, rating, or review count, so there is no social proof or detail to support a click.
- The bundles offer (30%) is buried at the bottom after four full sections. It is arguably the strongest and most relevant offer, since the whole email is about completing the bed.
- The white headline sits over a busy photo in the hero, and the faint "Max" product text behind it competes with the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the bundle. Move "Save 30% on bundles" up into the hero as the single offer, and let the category sections support it.
- 2. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown, to restore the urgency that the 09/27–09/30 sends built.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop mattresses," "Shop adjustable bases," "Shop pillows."
- 4. Add a star rating and a "from $X" price to each tile.
- 5. Cut the tile count. Four or five hero products would convert better than nine.
- 6. Put a darker overlay or solid panel behind the hero headline to improve legibility.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 30% off mattresses, bases, pillows & sheets, bundled.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From the foundation to the finishing layer. Build it your way.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is structurally sound: it has a `lang`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta, MSO conditionals, a preheader, and a mobile `@media (max-width:620px)` block. The automated QA run (50% pass, 0 issues, 4 warnings) is mostly relay or capture artifacts. The one signal that looks real is a text-part that is more than 90% URLs.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Text part is mostly URLs.** QA reports 21,484 of 23,258 plain-text characters (about 92%) are URLs, above the 70% warning threshold. That points to long tracking-wrapped links in the text alternative. It is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.
- **Incomplete link coverage.** 12 links were probed and 28 skipped, ranked by click likelihood. The QA output gives no per-link status, so no broken or redirecting links are confirmed. Footer, utility and social links were not verified.
- **Tracking pixel.** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display: none;">` is a Bloomreach/Exponea open pixel. Some clients ignore `display:none` pixels, and Apple MPP inflates their opens, so open data is unreliable.
- **Possible test segment in production.** The pixel token appears to base64-encode a segment label, `Core Sleepers Test`. This is my inference from the token's visible tail, not confirmed. If it is a real label, a test-named segment may have been sent to a live list.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA false positive on alt text.** The flagged image is the open pixel, which already has `alt=""`. That is correct for a decorative or tracking image, so no fix is needed.
- **Preheader.** Both hidden preheader blocks use `display:none`, with `mso-hide:all` on the text span. The filler block of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` entities prevents body text from leaking into the inbox preview. There are no issues here.
- **Other markup checks:**
  - Web font stylesheets are loaded from `brxcdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook skips them and falls back to system fonts.
  - `<html lang="en">` is present.
  - Alt text, heading structure and contrast on content images and CTAs could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). Subject and preheader render cleanly, with the emoji encoded as `&#128064;`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Bulk-sender requirements from Gmail and Yahoo expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the expected cause. No authentication problem is evidenced.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result is not reported. The footer was cut off in the truncated HTML, so neither the link nor the physical address is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data provided. The truncated HTML shows no destination URLs, and QA reported no redirect chains or landing-page results. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.
