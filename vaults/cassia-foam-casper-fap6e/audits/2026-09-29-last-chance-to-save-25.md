# Last chance to save 25%!*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing bell on the Friends & Family 25% send from 09/27 ("Save 25% for 3 days only!*"). The hero does its job. A big "Save 25%" sits next to the code FF2026, an "ENDS TONIGHT" badge and a solid blue "Shop now" button. The offer and deadline read in about two seconds, which is what a last-day reminder needs.
- The email loses focus below the hero. A full navy block for the NYC FC "Air your daymares" contest is roughly as tall as the offer panel. It sends people to a submission form instead of the sale that expires tonight. Below that come a nav row, an SMS signup and quiz and store links. A subscriber who has seen this 25% mechanic since 09/24 gets a lot of ways to not buy.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand deadline reminder, but not a sharp one. It's the third pass at the same 25% hook in a week and adds nothing new except urgency. The contest block should have been dropped or pushed far down on the last day of the sale.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Save 25% on mattresses" in large type, with the code FF2026 right below it.
- The "ENDS TONIGHT" badge and "discount is ending" copy give credible, specific urgency.
- The "Shop now" button is high-contrast, and the offer, code and CTA form one clean stack.
- The lifestyle photo is warm and human, and the "Friends & Family Exclusive" badge keeps the exclusivity framing from 09/27.
- The layout has no visible render bugs. Images load and text is legible.

## What's weak

- The daymare contest block is a large, competing focal point. Its "Submit your daymare" CTA pulls attention away from the sale on its final day.
- The hero CTA is a generic "Shop now" with no product, and it doesn't say which mattresses or what price.
- The offer feels thin: 25% off, the same mechanic as 09/27, with no price example or hero product. The fine print (exclusions, "savings based on comp. value") sits in tiny gray footer text.
- The bottom of the email is cluttered. There are four nav categories, an SMS signup, a quiz link, a store locator and a long block of legal text, all of it noise on a last-chance send.
- The footer still reads "© 2025 Casper Sleep Inc." in a 2026 send, which looks careless.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or shrink the daymare contest block, or move it below the fold. On the last day of the sale, the offer should be the only large call to action.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific and give it a price. For example, use "Shop 25% off mattresses" and show one hero product with a before and after price, such as the Dream or Snow mattress.
- 3. Add a countdown timer or the exact deadline ("11:59pm PT tonight") in the hero. It is stronger than the badge alone and makes the urgency verifiable.
- 4. Add one line of social proof, such as a star rating or review count, near the CTA. It's cheap and missing.
- 5. Remove the SMS signup and quiz links from a deadline send, and update the footer year.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 25% off mattresses (code FF2026)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Friends & Family code expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use FF2026 before 11:59pm PT. Mattresses only, no extensions.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last day for 25% off Dream, Snow and Nova. Sleep better tonight.`
