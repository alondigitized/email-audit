# Today only: Extra 5% on mattresses.*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact "Labor Day Subscriber Exclusive / extra 5%" hero Casper has now run at least three times — 08/23, 08/28, and again here on 09/07, with the same hand-circled "extra 5%," the same woman-on-bed photo, and the same orange "Shop Mattresses" / "Shop Labor Day Sale" button pair. The subject line says "Today only," which finally injects real urgency after a week of vague "35%" banner spam, and the body backs it up with a clean, well-organized category breakdown (Foam/Hybrid/Max) each with its own specific stacked discount and code.
- Where it falls down is freshness and cadence — this is visually identical to two prior sends, and a subscriber who opened 08/28's "A better sleep setup for every budget" or 08/23's "Extra 5% savings just for you.*" will recognize the layout instantly and may tune out. The Max Mattresses line also undercuts its own "Today only" subject by saying "Ends tomorrow," a small but real internal inconsistency.
- Net: solid execution, clear hierarchy, real specifics — but it's the fourth-plus reuse of the same creative in two weeks, and that repetition is now the dominant story for this subscriber.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy: eye lands on "Save an extra 5%" hero circle immediately, no competition.
- Each product tier (Foam, Hybrid, Max) states a concrete stacked discount and a real promo code (LDAY-EX5), not vague language.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, buttons are legible, spacing is consistent.
- Good structural variety below the fold: bundles, pillows, bedding, furniture cross-sell with individual discounts and CTAs.
- Two clear top CTAs (Shop Mattresses / Shop Labor Day Sale) give the reader an obvious next action.

## What's weak

- Same hero image, same green-circled "extra 5%," same layout as at least two prior Casper sends (08/23, 08/28) — visual fatigue risk.
- "Today only" (subject) contradicts "Ends tomorrow" (Max Mattresses section) — an internal urgency mismatch a sharp-eyed reader will notice.
- Fine print ("*Savings based on comp. value") sits directly under the hero CTA in tiny text — muddies an otherwise clean offer.
- Three near-identical CTA buttons (Shop premium Foam / Shop Hybrid / Shop Max) offer no differentiation in copy — all just "Shop [category] mattresses."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire the circled-hero-photo template** for at least one cycle — the repetition (three-plus uses) is now the biggest drag on this campaign; a new hero shot or layout would do more than any copy tweak.
- 2. **Fix the urgency mismatch** — align "Today only" (subject) with the Max Mattresses "Ends tomorrow" line so the deadline reads as one consistent promise.
- 3. **Differentiate the three category CTAs** with outcome-specific copy (e.g., "Shop cooling Hybrids," "Shop pressure relief") instead of three buttons that only vary by product name.
- 4. **Move or shrink the "*Savings based on comp. value" disclaimer** away from directly under the primary CTA — it currently reads as a hedge right where the reader is about to click.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: stack an extra 5% off mattresses`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack code LDAY-EX5 on Foam, Hybrid, or Max — today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your extra 5% expires at midnight. Here's what to shop first.`
