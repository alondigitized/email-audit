# Early Access to 4th of July savings
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- Casper has pivoted cleanly from its Memorial Day / summer marathon to a fresh holiday anchor: 4th of July early access. The hero is crisp — "up to 35%" with a promo code and a clear "Shop Sale" CTA, all above the fold. Below it, the email runs through five distinct savings tiers (mattresses, bundles, adjustable bases, pillows, everything else), each with its own product shot and "Shop [Category]" button. That's actually a smart move: a subscriber skimming fast can self-select their category immediately rather than landing on a cluttered sale page and hunting.
- The downside is the same pattern problem that's plagued this list since Memorial Day. Casper went 13+ emails deep on one sale, briefly did something genuinely different with the Hulu *Not Suitable for Work* bundle collab on June 9 (the best send this auditor has seen from this brand), and has now snapped straight back into percent-off promo mode three days later. There's no creative hook — no seasonal narrative, no lifestyle angle. "Early access" is doing a lot of heavy lifting where copy used to be.
- Execution is clean; the email renders without bugs and the tiered-savings structure is the sharpest layout this brand has sent in weeks. But it's a mechanical send, and this list has been trained to expect one every few days.

## What's working

- **Tiered savings hierarchy is transparent.** Showing 35% on bundles → 30% on mattresses → 20% on pillows → 10% on accessories tells the subscriber exactly where the best deal lives, no guessing.
- **"Early access" framing** gives the send a reason-to-open that isn't purely discount pressure — it implies list exclusivity.
- **July 4th anchor is timely.** Sent June 10, four weeks ahead, this is actually well-timed: not desperate, not last-minute.
- **Hero is clean and above-fold.** Headline, code, and CTA are all visible before any scroll, which is right.
- **Category CTAs are specific.** "Shop Bundles," "Shop Adjustables," "Shop Pillows" are better than a single "Shop Now" that dumps everyone on the same landing page.

## What's weak

- **Zero creative hook.** The June 9 Hulu collab showed what this brand can do when it tries; this email is purely functional and forgettable by comparison.
- **"Shop Sale" hero CTA is generic.** The body CTAs are category-specific, but the top-of-email button says "Shop Sale" — the weakest possible verb/noun combo.
- **No social proof anywhere.** The May 28 send (the Memorial Day peak) had a "Don't just take our word for it" review section that scored 9/10 for a reason. This email dropped it entirely.
- **The code ("EA-JUL4" or similar) isn't visually anchored.** It appears as a small text line between the headline and CTA. A box or pill around the code would make it feel like a real gift, not an afterthought.
- **Cadence fatigue is setting in.** Three days after the most creative send in this brand's recent history, we're back to a percent-off grid. A subscriber who opened the Hulu bundle email expecting a new creative direction will feel whiplash.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a one-line creative hook to the hero.** Even something light — "Independence from bad sleep starts here." — gives the email a reason to exist beyond "sale starts now." The Hulu collab showed this team can write; use it.
- 2. **Rewrite the hero CTA to match the best body CTA.** "Shop the 4th of July Sale" beats "Shop Sale" every time. Alternatively, lead with the highest-value tier: "Shop Bundles — Save 35%."
- 3. **Visually box the promo code.** Put `EA-JUL4` in a styled pill or dashed border. It signals exclusivity and is easier to copy.
- 4. **Pull one review into the hero section.** Even a single 5-star pull quote ("Best sleep of my life") under the CTA lifts trust without adding scroll depth.
- 5. **Space the next send.** Let the 4th of July anchor breathe for at least a week before the next reminder. The Memorial Day fatigue already proved that frequency kills urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your early-access code: 4th of July savings inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `35% off starts now — before everyone else`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bundles save the most. Mattresses, adjustable bases, pillows — shop by tier.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab your code before this opens to everyone.`
