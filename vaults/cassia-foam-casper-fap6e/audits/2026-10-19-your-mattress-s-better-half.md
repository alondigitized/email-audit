# Your mattress’s better half
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a focused, well-built product push for Casper's adjustable bases. The hero is "Better together" with a lime "SAVE 30% ON ADJUSTABLES" badge and a coral "Shop adjustables" button on navy. The offer and the action are clear in the first screen. The body is a clean good/better/best ladder: Adjustable Base, Base Pro and Base Max, each tagged 30% off.
- The email gets busy toward the bottom. After the bases it adds a Split King section, a mattress grid at 20% and 25% off, and an Ebenezer "Humbug Hush Cap" promo. That is three offers and two product categories in one send. The subject line also undersells the body. "Your mattress's better half" is cute, but it names neither the product nor the discount. Compared with the recent Friends & Family sends (10/13, 10/15, 10/16), this is a better-structured email, but the subject gives no reason to open.
- Verdict: a solid 7. The creative is good and the subject is weak.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer and CTA together: a "SAVE 30% ON ADJUSTABLES" badge, a one-line value prop, and a visible "Shop adjustables" button.
- The three-tier base ladder is easy to scan. Each tier has a short "For your everyday unwind / resting and recharging / full bedtime upgrade" label, a feature line and its own "Shop now" link.
- The 30% badges alternate sides down the page, which keeps the vertical scroll moving and the offer repeated.
- The Split King lifestyle section ("Same bed. Your own settings.") speaks to a real buying objection, couples who want different positions, and has its own "Explore adjustables" CTA.
- The mattress cross-sell is tied to the base ("What's a base without a bed?"), so it reads as a logical add-on and not a random grid.

## What's weak

- The subject never mentions adjustable bases or the 30% off. The offer is only visible after opening.
- The send carries three offers at once: 30% off bases, 20% and 25% off mattresses, and the free Humbug Hush Cap. The mattress discounts being lower than the hero's 30% mutes the headline.
- The Ebenezer promo is a hard tonal break from the sleep-comfort story. "Only in theaters November 13" plus a movie logo reads as an ad inside the email, and the asterisked fine print makes the "free" cap feel conditional.
- Every product tile uses a generic "Shop now" link, and there are no prices, reviews or ratings. Adjustable bases are a considered purchase, and nothing here helps the reader decide.
- No deadline is stated for the 30% off. Casper sent the same Friends & Family theme on 10/13, 10/15 and 10/16, so without an end date this reads as another entry in a long run of promos.
- The legal and fine-print block at the bottom is tiny and dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject so it names the product and the offer. This is the biggest lift, because the body is already good and the subject is the weak link.
- 2. Cut the Ebenezer block, or move it to its own send. Keep the email to one offer story: bases at 30% plus the matching mattress.
- 3. Add an end date to the hero, for example "30% off adjustable bases through Sunday", to give the offer credible urgency.
- 4. Replace the "Shop now" links with specific copy such as "Shop Base Pro" or "Shop Base Max", and add a "from $X" price or a star rating to each tier.
- 5. Add one line of social proof, such as a review count or a sleep-quality quote, under the Split King section.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off adjustable bases, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sleep your way: adjustable bases 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lift your feet, tilt your head, and get a wireless remote. Three bases, all 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Split King setups let you each pick your side. Plus 20% off mattresses to match.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is well-formed: it has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, and a preheader. It was sent through Bloomreach/Exponea (`cdn.us1.exponea.com`, `brxcdn.com`). The source is truncated, so link, UTM, footer and unsubscribe checks can't be fully verified. The QA run shows 0 failures and 4 warnings, and two of those warnings are relay artifacts.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-text part is link-heavy.** 19,157 of 20,919 characters (about 91.6%) are URLs, which points to long tracked or redirect URLs. This is a real deliverability and spam-filter risk. The text alternative is effectively unreadable and may be auto-generated.
- **Link probing was partial.** 12 material links were probed and 24 were skipped, which are mostly footer, utility and social links. No probe failures are reported, so no broken links are confirmed. The skipped 24 are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `<img width="1" height="1" alt="" src="https://cdn.us1.exponea.com/casper-prod/e/…/open" style="display: none;">`. This is a standard open pixel. Some clients don't fetch `display:none` images, so open rates may be undercounted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The "image missing alt text" warning is a false positive.** The flagged image is the open pixel, which has `alt=""`. That is the correct way to mark a decorative image, so no fix is needed. The QA check is treating an empty `alt` as missing.
- **The head loads two external stylesheets.** They are `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags to `brxcdn.com/.../9c4a4f1a-…` and `.../9c30fa4c-…`, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and several other clients strip `<link>` tags. Any web-font styling there will fall back, and the inline styles and `<style>` block should carry the layout.
- **The preheader is duplicated.** There is a text span ("Find your comfort zone. Save 30% on adjustables.") followed by a separate hidden `<div>` of `&#8199;&#847;` and `&shy;` padding. This is a common pattern, but the span has `mso-hide:all` and the padding div doesn't. This is minor.
- **Positives:** the mobile `@media (max-width:620px)` rules are present, there is a `role="present…"` layout table (truncated), and the `nl-container` is set to 100% width.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible portion. The remainder of the source is truncated, so this can't be confirmed for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header was found.** The QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed defect.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) was found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require this for bulk senders.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header was captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sending domain `e.casper.com` should be checked directly with DNS or a seed-list test.
- **The footer's physical address and unsubscribe link weren't visible.** They are in the truncated section. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The CTA hrefs are beyond the truncation point, and no UTM parameters or landing-page results are in the QA data. The only tracking evidence is the Exponea open pixel. The preheader promises "Save 30% on adjustables", so the landing pages should be checked for that offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw message headers from the original delivery, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.casper.com`.
2. Check whether the plain-text part is auto-generated. If it is, replace it with a short hand-written version, and shorten or consolidate tracked URLs.
3. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the CTA hrefs, UTM consistency, the footer address, unsubscribe link placement, and any merge tokens.
4. Suppress the QA's alt-text warning for `alt=""` tracking pixels so it stops producing false positives.
5. Confirm the 24 skipped links resolve, especially any footer links that aren't the unsubscribe link.
6. Make sure core styling doesn't depend on the external `brxcdn.com` stylesheets, since Gmail ignores `<link>` tags.
