# Welcome to the Casper dream team.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a solidly executed welcome email from Casper that leans hard into brand warmth over transactional efficiency. The structure is smart: lead with an exclusive member discount, reinforce with social proof (100k+ 5-star reviews), showcase bestsellers, and close with utility (sleep tips, contact info). The dark navy "Save an EXTRA 5% on everything" banner above the fold is the right move — it gives a skeptical new subscriber a reason to stick around before the brand storytelling starts.
- The weakness is the subject line. "Welcome to the Casper dream team." is cozy but opportunity-wasted: the 5% member benefit is the lead, and it's buried in the email body instead of earning clicks from the inbox. Whoever opens this because of the subject is doing it on brand recognition alone.
- Overall: a functional, well-designed welcome send that punches above average for the category, held back by a subject that undersells the actual offer.

## What's working

- **Discount banner above the fold** — "Save an EXTRA 5% on everything / on top of up to 20% Member Sale savings" is visible instantly, before any scrolling.
- **Social proof is prominent and specific** — "over 100k more 5-star reviews" with a pull quote ("An insomniac's dream") builds credibility without feeling forced.
- **Product hierarchy is logical** — leading with the mattress, then pillow, respects average order-of-consideration for a new sleep brand shopper.
- **Feature icon row** — the "stress-free ZZZs" section with guarantee/trial icons addresses purchase objections (likely free trial, warranty) at exactly the right scroll depth.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds at this width.

## What's weak

- **Subject line buries the lead** — "Welcome to the Casper dream team." is all brand and no offer; the 5% is the hook and it's invisible from the inbox.
- **"Shop Now" is the weakest CTA possible** — no specificity, no product noun, no urgency. At minimum it should say "Shop Mattresses" or "Claim Your 5% Off."
- **No urgency or expiry visible** — the 5% offer has no deadline shown; it reads as evergreen, which undermines motivation to click now.
- **Body copy too small to scan** — the paragraph under "Welcome to the sleep of your dreams." is rendered at a size that requires zooming even on desktop; key proof points buried there are lost.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to lead with the member benefit** — the 5% offer is real differentiation; don't hide it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your member perk: 5% extra off everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Welcome gift inside: extra 5% on your first order`
- 2. **Fix the CTA copy** — replace "Shop Now" with something specific to the first offer surface. "Shop Mattresses" or "Claim 5% Off" sets expectation and raises intent.
- 3. **Add a deadline** — even "offer ends Sunday" or "this week only" would convert fence-sitters who'd otherwise bookmark and forget.
- 4. **Preheader should be the offer, not the welcome copy** — lead with the savings line so inbox preview reads: *"Welcome to the Casper dream team. | Save an extra 5% on everything today"*
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your exclusive member perk is waiting — save an extra 5% on everything.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `5% extra off sitewide, stacked on top of your member savings.`
- 5. **Increase the hero body copy size or consolidate to 1–2 lines** — at current size, most users will skip past it entirely.

```html
<div style="display: none; max-height: 0px; overflow: hidden;">
  [padding chars]
</div>
```
The padding technique itself is correct; the content is wrong.
