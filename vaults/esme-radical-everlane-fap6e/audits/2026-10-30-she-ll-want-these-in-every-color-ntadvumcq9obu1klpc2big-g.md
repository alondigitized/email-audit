# She’ll Want These in Every Color
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide send that looks good and sells nothing. The hero is a stack of burgundy knits under "Better Gifts For Her," and the ribbon-wrapped flats and red scarf carry the holiday mood well. Everlane's restraint works here, but the email never tells you what to buy or why you should act now.
- The offer is the problem. "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS" sits in a muted blue-grey block at the very bottom. It is the only price lever in the email, and most readers will never reach it. The two mid-email sections are labeled "Gifts Under $100" and "Gifts Under $50." Those are price tiers, not products, and each links to a collection with no specific item named.
- The subject is a mismatch. "She'll Want These in Every Color" promises a color-variety product story, but the body shows no color range at all. It shows one pair of black flats and one red scarf. Compare the 10/29 "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" send, which led with the offer in the hero. This one buries the same offer and is weaker for it. Judgement: pretty, on-season, and under-merchandised.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and seasonal. The large white "Better Gifts For Her" over rich burgundy knits is readable and on-brand.
- The ribbon styling on the flats and scarf signals "gift" immediately and is consistent across the email.
- The black-maroon SHOP NOW buttons are clear and repeated, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The "Gifts Under $100 / $50" tiers give shoppers a budget entry point.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space and no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The only real offer, 25% off markdowns, is buried at the bottom and is visually weak on the grey-blue block.
- The subject promises "every color," but the body shows two single-color items and no color variants.
- The hero has no product, price, or deadline. The "Heirloom cashmere, cleaner denim..." copy names items that are never shown.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW," and the section links ("Gifts Under $100") don't name a product.
- The gift-guide framing excludes anyone buying for themselves. The body copy ("You clearly know her taste") is cute but vague.
- The offer expiry (11/01) is in the fine print only, so the urgency is lost.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% off markdowns up into the hero, with the 11/01 deadline stated visibly. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Replace the two mood images with product grids: named items, prices, and a color-swatch strip that makes the subject true.
- 3. Give each CTA a specific label, such as "Shop Cashmere Gifts" or "Shop Gifts Under $50."
- 4. Add proof, such as a star rating or "best-seller" tags on the cashmere and flats.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email (`_kx` params, `static-forms.klaviyo.com` font import) routed through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The one hard QA failure is the logo link, whose redirect target is a relative path. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML supplied was truncated, so only what's visible there and in the QA data is assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect.** The link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M66QXYPFE7ZWG25K2B4JMCMW_0` (text "Everlane Logo") returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect resolves to a relative path (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - Relative `Location` values are valid per RFC 7231. The probe's HTTP client may simply have failed to resolve it, so this could be a QA-tool artifact.
  - If the tracker really emits a scheme-less target, clients that don't resolve against the tracker host would break. Verify with `curl -sI` on that URL.
- Only 12 of 26 links were probed and 14 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. The 43% pass rate partly reflects this coverage limit, not 57% broken links. Only one link failed.
- The tracked URL is truncated in the QA output (`cm_flow_i…`), so I can't confirm the full parameter set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M66QXYPFE7ZWG25K2B4JMCMW` is an open-tracking pixel (`/o/` path). The flag is low severity, but the pixel should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is minor, since email clients mostly ignore it.
- The visible source includes the following:
  - MSO conditionals and `o:OfficeDocumentSettings`.
  - `mj-outlook-group-fix`.
  - A 480px media query breakpoint.
  - A `viewport` meta tag.
  - `lang` is not set on `<html>`, as far as the visible source shows.
- The `@import` of a Klaviyo custom-font CSS file is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook, but the fonts fall back to Arial/Helvetica. This is not a defect.
- No other issues are confirmable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip these, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. These checks are inconclusive, not failures.
- The From address is `support@everlane.com`, while the click tracker is `send.everlane.com`. Alignment can't be assessed without headers.
- The QA data doesn't show the footer unsubscribe link or the physical postal address. They may be in the truncated portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one resolved link target, `/pages/womens`, carries Klaviyo params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no visible `utm_*` params. They may be in the truncated portion of the URL, so this is unconfirmed.
- Landing page alignment can't be assessed for the other links because they weren't probed or their results weren't reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M66QXYPFE7ZWG25K2B4JMCMW_0`. If the `Location` header is genuinely relative, fix the tracker config to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`. If it resolves fine, treat it as a probe bug and make the QA tool resolve relative redirects with `urljoin`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
3. Give `<title>` meaningful content (the subject line) and set `lang` on `<html>`.
4. Re-run the compliance checks against raw headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Probe the skipped links, at least the footer unsubscribe link, and confirm UTM parameters on the final landing URLs.
