# What’s New At Everlane?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals grid with the Friends & Family 30% banner bolted on top. It's tidy and on-brand, and the nine-tile knit-and-denim grid sells the season. But it's the same promo Everlane has pushed all week, wrapped in a generic subject. "What's New At Everlane?" says nothing about the 30% off, which is the strongest thing in the email.
- The hierarchy is backwards. The offer sits in a thin blue strip, and the big type says "New Arrivals". A reader skimming on a phone sees product first and savings second. The deadline (10/13, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the fine print, so the urgency never reaches the top of the email.
- Judgement: competent but forgettable. It's a 6 at best, and it would be much stronger if the subject and hero led with the offer and the deadline.

## What's working

- The 30% off sitewide banner is at the very top, with Women and Men entry points.
- The nine-tile grid mixes flat-lays and two on-model shots. The brown cable knit, cream polo sweater and straight-leg denim fit the fall season.
- "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" is a solid black button, centered and easy to spot.
- The "Better prices, still Everlane" block reinforces value without being pushy.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and a simple footer with Find a Store and Help Center.

## What's weak

- The subject doesn't mention the 30% off or the deadline.
- The offer strip is small and low-contrast next to the large "New Arrivals" heading.
- The grid tiles have no names, prices or per-product links, so a reader can't tell what is on sale.
- The deadline appears only in the grey fine print at the bottom. The fine print also says "select styles only", which sits awkwardly against the "sitewide" banner.
- The beige "Better prices" block is a lot of empty space for a second generic "Shop Now". The nav list below it repeats the banner links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and the deadline: a larger hero strip reading "30% off sitewide — ends Tuesday 10/13", with the knit grid below it.
- 2. Make the grid shoppable. Add product names and sale prices under each tile, and link each tile to its own product page.
- 3. Cut the beige block, or replace it with a "Best-Sellers" row so the space does some work.
- 4. Resolve the "sitewide" versus "select styles only" contradiction, or add a short "exclusions apply" line near the banner.
- 5. Write a preheader that adds the deadline and the product, not just the brand name.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% Off New Knits — Ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Fall Sweaters, Now 30% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cable knits, cashmere and denim — Friends & Family ends 10/13 at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 30% discount applies to new arrivals, too. Shop before Tuesday night.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML build with a branded click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The one hard QA failure is a relative-redirect probe error, and most of the other warnings come from what the AgentMail relay and the truncated HTML source didn't capture. The QA pass rate is 43% (1 fail, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link error on the "Everlane" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4PBE1ZAV47FRX6KCGFNKJFW_3` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host. The probe's HTTP client couldn't resolve it.
  - Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so this is likely a probe artifact rather than a broken link for recipients. It's still non-standard: RFC 7231 allows relative references, but an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...` is safer for clients and scanners.
  - The final destination was not verified.
- The tracking-pixel `<img>` (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4PBE1ZAV47FRX6KCGFNKJFW`) is missing `alt`. Because it's an open-tracking pixel, the impact is minimal, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- Only 12 of 32 links were probed (20 skipped by click-likelihood ranking), so the remaining links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only). This is minor, but some clients and web views use it.
- The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/...)` web font is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The `Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks are present, so there's no functional issue.
- The MSO conditionals (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `.mj-outlook-group-fix`), the viewport meta, and the dark-mode `[data-ogsc]` rules are present and well-formed in the visible portion.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the markup, including image alt coverage beyond the pixel.

No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or subject. The tracking-URL parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`) are resolved, not placeholders.

## 5. Compliance

Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message at the origin.

- I can't confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` are present on any link. Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid` identify the profile and message but don't replace UTMs for analytics attribution.
