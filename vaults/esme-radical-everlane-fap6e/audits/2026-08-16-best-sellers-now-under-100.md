# Best-Sellers Now Under $100
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "Better Prices, Still Everlane" send in a week, and it's actually the strongest execution of the beat so far — it finally does the thing all five prior sends should've led with: real prices, real products, real customer quotes, all four under $100. That's a genuine improvement over 8/12's manifesto letter and 8/13's bare price announcement, which had nothing to click on.
- But the cadence problem hasn't gone anywhere. Subject line "Best-Sellers Now Under $100" is the sixth variation on the identical price-cut story, and a subscriber who opened 8/13, 8/14, or 8/15 has already seen this exact footer language and is going to feel the repetition even if this particular send is well-built. The duplicated hero image (same photo stacked twice with the headline sandwiched between) also reads as a render quirk, not a deliberate design choice.
- Judgment: solid execution, wrong week to run it. This creative deserved to be the *one* price-story send, not the sixth.

## What's working

- Four products, four real "Previously $X, now $88" price pairs — the clearest price communication of the whole week's series.
- Genuine-sounding customer quotes attached to each product (Carissa B., Catherine C., Ellen K., Julia S.) add credibility other Everlane sends this week lacked.
- Clean, consistent SHOP NOW CTA under every product — no ambiguity about what to click.
- Flat lay product photography is sharp and consistent, no broken images.
- Subhead "The styles you'll have forever at prices that won't change back" gives the discount a rationale instead of just announcing a markdown.

## What's weak

- The hero image appears to render as two stacked copies of the same photo with "Better Prices / Still Everlane" text wedged between them — looks like a layout bug, not intentional.
- This is the sixth near-identical "Better Prices, Still Everlane" beat in a week (8/12 manifesto, 8/13 price-drop, 8/13 denim, 8/14 favorites, 8/15 "Price Changed" letter, now this) — fatigue is real at this point.
- All four hero products land at the same $88 price point, which reads more like a $88 flash sale than a genuine "best-sellers" curation — undercuts the subject line's premise.
- Fine print at the bottom ("Offer valid until 09/07/2026 at 11:59 PM PST") is the only urgency signal in the whole email — nothing in the hero or subject conveys a deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the duplicated hero image — whether it's a real double-render or a design choice to show front/back, it reads as broken and should be one clean hero shot.
- 2. Given this is the sixth price-story send this week, this creative should have anchored day one, not day six — going forward, space price-focused sends at least 3-4 days apart from non-price sends.
- 3. Add the September 7 deadline into the hero or subhead, not buried in fine print — it's the one piece of real urgency in this email and it's invisible.
- 4. Vary the price points shown (not four products all landing exactly at $88) so "best-sellers" doesn't read as "everything is $88 today."
