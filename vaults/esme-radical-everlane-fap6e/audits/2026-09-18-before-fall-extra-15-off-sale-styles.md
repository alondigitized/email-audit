# Before Fall: Extra 15% Off Sale Styles
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-styled email with zero product imagery — just body copy, hyperlinked words, and a nav footer. The broken "Hi There, ," personalization bug that's plagued Everlane's last five-plus sends (dating back to at least 2026-09-12) is still here, unfixed, going on a sixth-plus appearance. Unlike the recent editorial run ("Corduroy Has Entered the Chat," "Do the Leg Work," "What To Wear Now"), this reverts to the bare-bones discount-blast format with no hero shot, no product grid, no visual hierarchy at all.
- The offer itself — extra 15% off sale already up to 70% off, no code — is genuinely a strong, concrete deal, and "fall starts Tuesday" gives real seasonal timing. But the execution completely undersells it: there's no image, no button, just underlined text links buried mid-paragraph. A subscriber skimming on mobile has to read three paragraphs before finding anything clickable.
- Judgment: the offer is good, the send is lazy. This is a template Everlane could ship in five minutes, and it shows.

## What's working

- Clear, stackable discount messaging: "extra 15% off sale styles already up to 70% off" is specific and easy to parse.
- "No code needed" removes friction at the point of decision.
- Seasonal hook ("Fall starts on Tuesday") gives the send a timely, non-generic reason to open.
- Footer fine print is honest and complete — expiration date, exclusions, and terms are all visible, not buried.

## What's weak

- The "Hi There, ," broken personalization bug persists — now a recurring, unresolved defect across at least six sends.
- No hero image, no product photography, no visual anchor at all — just plain paragraphs of text.
- No actual CTA button — "tops," "pants," and "prices" are underlined inline text links, which is weak and easy to miss.
- No visual hierarchy: the discount, the "up to 70% off," and the shopping links all carry equal (minimal) visual weight.
- The nav block (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) is more prominent than the actual sale offer above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "Hi There, ," personalization bug — this is now a longstanding, repeated defect and the single highest-leverage fix available.
- 2. Add a hero image of sale product (even a simple flat-lay of the "tops" and "pants" mentioned) with a real button — e.g., "Shop the Sale" — directly under the headline, so the offer is visible without reading three paragraphs.
- 3. Bold or visually isolate "Extra 15% Off" and "up to 70% off" as a standalone line, not inline sentence text.
- 4. Replace inline text links ("tops," "pants," "prices") with a single clear CTA button pointing to the sale collection.
- 5. Consider a countdown or day-count instead of "Fall starts on Tuesday" to sharpen urgency (e.g., "Ends in 5 days").
