# Giftable New Arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent product-grid send, but the subject promises more than the body delivers. "Giftable New Arrivals" sets up a gift-led email, yet the 40% off banner is a thin strip at the top. The nine-item knitwear grid in the middle is unlabeled, with no names, prices or sale prices. The gift guide sits at the bottom as an afterthought.
- The grid is the right idea. Cream, brown, navy and striped sweaters and cardigans are cohesive and seasonal. But every item is a floating product shot with one "SHOP NOW" bar underneath, so the reader can't tell what anything costs or which piece is the hero. After Nov 20's "Better Gifts Under $50", which had a concrete price promise, this feels like a step backward into generic merchandising.
- Verdict: tidy and on-brand, but low on specifics. It's the ninth or so "40% off" reminder in a few days, with a new-arrivals grid slotted in to differentiate it.

## What's working

- The knitwear grid is cohesive, seasonal and easy to scan against a neutral backdrop.
- The 40% off banner at the top states the offer clearly, with separate Women and Men entry points.
- Plenty of white space and a restrained palette keep it on-brand and uncluttered.
- The burgundy gift guide block gives a strong visual break and splits gifts into "For Her" and "For Him".
- The fine print states the expiry (12/02/2026) and the terms.

## What's weak

- The 40% off offer is buried in a small, semi-transparent banner over a dark photo, and the text is hard to read.
- The nine products have no names, prices or sale prices, and each links to nothing visible beyond one generic "SHOP NOW".
- The subject says "Giftable", but the gift content appears only in the bottom block, with no price anchor.
- The gift guide block has a lot of empty burgundy space below the links.
- The footer nav repeats the top links (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals), which adds length without adding value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a gift hero: "Gifts, 40% off" with a price band, then the grid. This is the single biggest lift because it makes the subject and the body match.
- 2. Add product names and "$X → $Y" pricing under each grid item, and link each to its own product page.
- 3. Promote the 40% off banner to a real hero with a clear deadline ("Ends Dec 2").
- 4. Cut the footer nav and tighten the empty burgundy space.
- 5. Replace "SHOP NOW" with category-specific copy, e.g. "Shop Gift Sweaters".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere, merino and cotton knits, all 40% off until Dec 2.`
