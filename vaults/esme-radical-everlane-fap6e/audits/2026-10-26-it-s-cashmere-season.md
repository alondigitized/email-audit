# It’s Cashmere Season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a product-first cashmere send with a strong hero and a weak offer. A model in a charcoal boxy crew, with a red cardigan knotted at the waist, fills the top. The pitch is the "richer weight" Cashmere Boxy Crew. The visual hierarchy is clean and the render has no bugs. The best-seller story is credible, and cashmere fits the late-October season.
- The offer is the problem. The 30% off is buried in a blue band near the bottom, and the fine print says the offer ends tonight at 11:59 PM PST. That makes it the same Fall Essentials Event as today's "Ends Tonight: Up to 30% Off" send. A subscriber who got that email this morning sees a second message for the same event. The cashmere framing never mentions the discount or the deadline, so the hero sells a premium product while the footer quietly discounts it.
- My judgment is a good-looking, on-brand catalog email that undersells its one hook. It is a mid-tier send, about the same as the "Your New Arrivals Are Here" grid from 10/25.

## What's working

- The hero is an editorial, tactile image. The model wears the sweater being sold, and the red cardigan adds color.
- The copy is specific: "best-selling Cashmere Boxy Crew," "richer weight," "softer," "effortless drape."
- The six-sweater grid on a neutral field is scannable and shows a range of colors and necklines.
- The black SHOP NOW bar sits directly under the hero, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The seasonal angle fits. "Cashmere season" matches late October and the gifting line.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. "Up to 30% off" doesn't appear until the blue band near the bottom, well after the hero and grid.
- The deadline is only in the grey fine print, so the urgency is invisible.
- The hero text "Cashmere Worth its Weight" is thin white type over the model's gray sweater and is hard to read.
- There are two identical "SHOP NOW" CTAs with no product names, prices or reviews. The grid items don't show names or prices either.
- This is the second Fall Essentials push in one day, following "Ends Tonight: Up to 30% Off," and it feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer and deadline in the hero or just under it, for example "Cashmere, up to 30% off. Ends tonight." The offer is the strongest hook and shouldn't sit below the fold.
- 2. Add a price, a name and a review count (Everlane has used "over 2500 reviews" before) to the grid, and make each tile its own link.
- 3. Make the hero headline legible with a darker overlay or a bolder weight, or move it onto the plain white background.
- 4. Give the CTAs specific copy, such as "Shop the Cashmere Boxy Crew" and "Shop women's cashmere."
- 5. If the 10/26 morning send already covered the event, make this one a cashmere-only send with a different hook, or fold the two into one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cashmere, up to 30% off. Ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our best-selling cashmere crew, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Boxy Crew in a richer weight. Last day for 30% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Softer, heavier, drapier. Gift it or keep it. Ends at midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email (Everlane, "It's Cashmere Season") with a 43% QA pass rate. The one confirmed failure is a tracked link whose redirect target is a relative path. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay-capture gaps, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** The "Everlane" link (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5WZCCRMJCN5RVNQYR600GGA_0`) errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker appears to answer with a relative `Location: /pages/womens?...` instead of an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host. If that is the cause, the click would land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is probably a 404.
  - This could also be a quirk of the probe client, so confirm it with a manual `curl -I` on the tracking URL.
- Only 12 of 28 links were probed and 16 were skipped (footer, social and utility links). Their status is unknown.
- The tracking URLs are Klaviyo-wrapped (`trk.send.everlane.com/l/…`), so destination URLs can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text** on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5WZCCRMJCN5RVNQYR600GGA`. This is the open-tracking pixel (`/o/` path), so the impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would still be cleaner.
- `<title> </title>` is blank.
- The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- The mobile media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` (max-width 480px). That is small enough to hurt legibility if h4 carries real content.
