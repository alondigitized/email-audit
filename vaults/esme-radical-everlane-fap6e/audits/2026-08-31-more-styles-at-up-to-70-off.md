# More Styles at up to 70% Off
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same broken plain-text-fallback template Everlane already sent twice this week — "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off" on 08/27 and "Employee Sale Access Ends Soon" on 08/29 — just with a slightly reworded subject line and a fresh personalization bug. No hero image, no product grid, no pricing shown, just stacked paragraph text on a white background with "Hi There, ," — a broken merge token — right at the top.
- At this point it's not a one-off render glitch, it's a pattern: three sends in four days using this stripped-down layout while the brand's other emails this week ("Boxy Sweaters," "Leather Up") show a full hero-plus-grid template is available and working. Whatever system or segment is generating this fallback needs to be found and fixed, because it's actively degrading trust with a broken name field on every send.
- Judgement: this is the weakest possible version of an Everlane email — no visual differentiation, a visible personalization failure, and zero product imagery to anchor the 70% off claim. Skip.

## What's working

- The 70% off ceiling is stated clearly and links directly to relevant categories (dresses, tops, more).
- Copy is short and scannable — three sentences before the sign-off.
- Footer discloses a hard expiration date and standard terms, which reads as honest rather than deceptive.
- Standard nav block (Shop Women/Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) and utility icons render cleanly with no broken images.

## What's weak

- "Hi There, ," — visible broken personalization token, a glaring, avoidable defect.
- No hero image and no product photography anywhere in the email — nothing to look at, nothing to visually confirm what's on sale.
- No specific product or price shown; "up to 70% off" is the only offer signal in an otherwise text-only layout.
- This is the third near-identical plain-text-fallback send in four days (08/27, 08/29, now), which reads as cadence fatigue and a recurring unfixed bug rather than a deliberate design choice.
- Visual hierarchy is flat — subject line, greeting, and body copy all carry equal weight with nothing pulling the eye to an offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the personalization token before this template sends again — "Hi There, ," is a visible, easily-caught QA failure that undermines trust in every other claim in the email.
- 2. Swap this fallback template out entirely for the hero-plus-grid layout used in "Boxy Sweaters" and "Leather Up" — pull in 4-6 actual sale products with prices so the 70% off claim has something concrete backing it.
- 3. Add a price anchor (e.g., "dresses from $XX") instead of leaving pricing entirely to the linked pages.
- 4. Investigate why this bare-bones template keeps recurring — audit the trigger/segment logic so the working creative is used consistently.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best sizes are going fast — shop the sale before 9/7.`

## Technical Audit: Everlane — "More Styles at up to 70% Off"

**1. Technical Summary**
Klaviyo-generated MJML email with functional broken-link tracking (`_kx` query param malformed inside redirect wrapper) and missing standard authentication/compliance headers per automated QA.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Two tracked links fail resolution per QA: the logo link and "Shop Men" CTA both route through `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M1AP1MP9CYXA4NJZDM2N82VS_0` and `..._6` respectively, but decode to malformed destination URLs — `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...'` and `'/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=...'`. The `_kx` Klaviyo exponea/click-tracking param appears to be truncating or corrupting the underlying redirect target, not just failing open-rate tracking — these are functional 404/error-risk links, not cosmetic.
- Both broken links carry `bxid` and `cm_flow_i` tracking params alongside `_kx`, suggesting a tracking-param concatenation bug in the redirect template rather than a one-off.
- 12 material links were probed by QA, 8 were deprioritized (footer/utility/social); no failures reported outside the two above.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M1AP1MP9CYXA4NJZDM2N82VS` (open-tracking beacon, id `01M1AP1MP9CYXA4NJZDM2N82VS`) is missing `alt` text. Low-impact since it's a 1x1 pixel, but worth flagging for template hygiene.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `mj-column-per-100` fallback rules are present and correctly structured for cross-client rendering.
- No other alt-text, table-structure, or client-compatibility issues surfaced in the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No unresolved or malformed Klaviyo merge tags (e.g. `{{ }}` or `[[ ]]`) found in the visible source. No issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from available data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) not present in what QA captured — authentication status unknown, likely also a relay visibility gap rather than a confirmed auth failure.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA (always included in probe set), satisfying baseline CAN-SPAM footer requirement.
- **Caveat:** these three warnings stem from header capture limits in the AgentMail relay, not confirmed absence — flag as "unconfirmed," not "failing."

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Cannot confirm alignment: the two failing links (`/pages/womens`, `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`) are the ones needed to verify destination-page match, and their query strings are malformed (`_kx` param appears to swallow/break the remainder of the URL rather than cleanly appending UTM/tracking params).
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params are visible in the decoded paths — tracking relies entirely on Klaviyo's `_kx`/`bxid`/`cm_flow_i` params instead of UTM, which is expected for Klaviyo sends but means GA/site-side attribution depends on Klaviyo's own click resolution working correctly.

**7. Recommendations**
- Fix the redirect/template logic generating the `_kx` param on the "Shop Men" and logo links — both resolve to `unknown url type` errors, meaning at least 2 of 12 probed links are currently broken for recipients.
- Add `alt=""` (decorative) to the open-tracking pixel for template hygiene.
- Independently verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` via raw headers from the original ESP send (not the AgentMail relay) to rule out a relay capture gap before treating these as real compliance/deliverability gaps.
