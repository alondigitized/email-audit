# Black Friday: 40% Off Everything You’ll Wear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Black Friday send, and it is the same one Everlane has sent for days. The hero is a model in a burgundy zip-neck knit, with "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type and a full-width black "SHOP WOMEN" bar underneath. The offer is clear within a second. The product grid and gift-guide block are tidy and easy to scan.
- It adds nothing new. Compared with 11/21's "The Best Merino Around" (a product story) and 11/20's "Better Gifts Under $50" (a price-point angle), this is the "40% off everything" template again. The asterisk and the "select styles only" fine print also weaken "everything." The 12/02 end date appears only in grey footer type, so the email has no visible urgency. Judgement: solid execution, low novelty, and it is likely to blend into a crowded inbox.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable: large white type over a high-contrast burgundy knit.
- The "SHOP WOMEN" button is a full-width black bar directly under the hero, so the primary action is obvious.
- The 2x2 category grid (sweaters, denim, fleece, accessories) is simple and has no render problems.
- The short body copy ties the offer to specific materials (cashmere, organic cotton, recycled fleece), which fits the brand's responsible-materials positioning.
- The burgundy gift-guide block carries the palette down the page and gives gift shoppers a clear next step.

## What's weak

- The email repeats the near-identical Black Friday creative from earlier sends this week.
- The deadline (12/02, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in tiny grey footer text, so there is no visible urgency.
- "Everything*" is contradicted by "applies to select styles only" in the footer, which weakens the offer's honesty.
- The grid links to categories, not specific hero products. There are no prices, reviews or social proof.
- The hero's only CTA is "Shop Women," with no men's equivalent. Men's appears only in the footer nav and gift link, so men's shoppers get a weaker path.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero or a banner, for example "Ends 12/2," and give the email a reason to open now.
- 2. Make one product the hero, such as the cashmere or Luxe Fleece, with before and after pricing.
- 3. Add a second CTA for men, or split the hero into two CTAs.
- 4. Add a small social-proof line, such as a review count or a best-seller badge, under the grid.
- 5. Resolve the "everything" versus "select styles" mismatch by naming the main exclusions near the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends 12/2: 40% off cashmere, denim & fleece`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your winter wardrobe, 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere, Luxe Fleece and organic-cotton denim, through 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters, denim and gifts, all 40% off until December 2.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo/MJML-style email from `support@everlane.com`. The one hard QA failure (a relative redirect target on the "Everlane" link) is probably a probe artifact rather than a real break. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay not exposing headers, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link error (likely probe artifact):** the QA probe reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - Link text is "Everlane" and the tracked URL is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M82KXXYCB9HTKE6PHVEE6T1D_0`.
  - The tracker's redirect `Location` is a relative path (`/pages/womens?...`). A relative `Location` is valid under RFC 7231 and browsers resolve it, but the probe client did not.
  - I can't confirm from this data whether a real user would hit a broken page, so this is unverified, not a confirmed break.
  - Only 12 of 25 links were probed. The other 13 (footer, social) were skipped, so the 43% pass rate reflects coverage limits as well as the single failure.
- **Click-tracking wrapper:** links go through `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…`, a branded Klaviyo-style redirector. That is standard and no problem in itself.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Image missing alt text:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M82KXXYCB9HTKE6PHVEE6T1D`.
  - This is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel, not content imagery.
  - Fix: add `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` if absent) so screen readers skip it. Low severity.
- **Link styling:** the `a:link/visited/hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` rules remove underlines on every link state. Links may not be distinguishable from body text without color or underline cues (WCAG 1.4.1). I couldn't check whether the truncated body adds its own affordances.
- **Template markup:** Outlook conditionals (`[if mso]`), `mso-hide` on the desktop/mobile toggles, a viewport meta tag and a 480px mobile breakpoint are all present. `<title> </title>` is blank, which is a minor accessibility gap.
- **HTML truncated:** I can't evaluate the rest of the body, including the rest of the media-query block, alt text on content images, and the dark-mode `[data-ogsc]` rules beyond what is visible.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
  - The QA note says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
  - Absence here is not proof the sender omitted them. Verify against the raw message at the final mailbox.
  - Everlane's sender domain, `everlane.com`, is a bulk sender, so Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note says this is expected via the relay. Check the DNS records for `everlane.com` or a direct-delivery copy.
- **Unsubscribe link:** the QA summary says one is always probed, but it reports no result for it. I can't confirm from this data that it resolves, and a physical address isn't visible in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The redirect target carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). `utm_*` parameters are either absent or cut off in the output, so I can't confirm campaign attribution.
- The landing path `/pages/womens` points to a women's landing page, while the subject promises "40% Off Everything". This is a destination-scope question rather than a code defect. It is unverified because the probe never loaded the page.
- Only the "Everlane" (logo/header) link was flagged, so I have no probe evidence on the actual CTA destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M82KXXYCB9HTKE6PHVEE6T1D_0` with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the request URL. If it returns 200 at `everlane.com/pages/womens`, close the FAIL as a probe bug.
2. Fix the probe's redirect handling (resolve relative redirects with `urljoin`) so a valid redirect isn't scored as a broken link.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy, not the relay.
4. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `everlane.com` directly.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel (`/o/01M82KXXYCB9HTKE6PHVEE6T1D`) and populate the blank `<title>`.
6. Raise the probe budget or log the skipped links so the unsubscribe and CTA links get explicit pass/fail results.
7. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check for `utm_*` parameters, content alt text and the footer address.
