# Have You Seen These New Arrivals?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, quiet product grid: nine knit sweaters and cardigans in a muted palette of brown, cream, grey, cobalt and powder blue. The "New Arrivals" framing is honest, and the grid is easy to scan. The problem is that the real offer, "Black Friday Preview: 30% Off Our Favorites," sits below the fold in a muted brown block. The hero grid carries only "New sweaters. New layers. New better prices." That line hints at a discount but never states it.
- This is the fifth or sixth Everlane send in about four days, and the brand has already stated the 30% plainly in the hero on 11/05 and 11/07. Putting the number below the fold here is a step backward. A subscriber who has seen "Selling Fast: 30% Off Cult Favorites" will skim this one. It looks pretty, but the offer is buried and the primary CTA doesn't carry it.
- Verdict: a well-composed catalog email with a weak commercial hook. The creative is fine, but the offer placement undercuts it.

## What's working

- The 3x3 grid is tidy and consistent, with products on a neutral grey ground. The eye lands on the knitwear immediately.
- The seasonal fit is right: sweaters and layers in early November.
- The SHOP NEW ARRIVALS button is a high-contrast black bar that is easy to spot.
- The Black Friday block has a clear structure, with a headline and a women's/men's split.
- There are no render bugs, and the layout is clean and uncluttered.

## What's weak

- The 30% offer is buried in the second block, and the hero never names it. "New better prices" is vague.
- The CTA says "Shop New Arrivals," which is generic. It's a category link, not a specific product or offer.
- The brown offer block has a lot of empty space below the links. The SHOP WOMEN and SHOP MEN links are small, underlined text rather than buttons.
- Nothing is labelled, so there are no prices, product names, or sweater details. Nine unlabelled tiles give no reason to pick one.
- Cadence: this is another near-identical Black Friday send, after four emails in four days around 11/05–11/07.
- The offer expiry (11/09 at 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the fine print, so the urgency is invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "30% Off Our Favorites" directly above or over the grid, and make the button say "Shop 30% Off Sweaters."
- 2. Label at least the top row with product name and price, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Turn SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN into real buttons, or fold them into one CTA. Show the deadline ("Ends 11/9") next to the offer, not only in the footer.
- 4. Cut the empty space in the brown block and the redundant nav links (Shop Women/Men/New Arrivals) to tighten the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Sweaters, Now 30% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Cardigans Just Landed (30% Off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Black Friday preview pricing on knits ends Sunday at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cobalt hoodie cardigans, cream V-necks and half-zips — shop before they sell through.`
