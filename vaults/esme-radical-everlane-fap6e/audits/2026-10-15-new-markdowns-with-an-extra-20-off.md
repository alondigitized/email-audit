# New Markdowns With an Extra 20% Off
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a bare plain-text note with no hero, no product image and no button. The offer is clear: an extra 20% off sale styles that are already up to 70% off. It's a strong deal, but nothing in the layout sells it. It reads like a placeholder, not a campaign.
- The greeting renders as "Hi There, ," with a stray comma, so the personalization slot failed. A subscriber would notice that at once. The deadline (10/18/2026, 11:59 PM PST) sits only in grey fine print and never appears in the body copy.
- This follows two mood sends today, "Scenes From Fall" and "A Softer Take on Corduroy" (10/14). - The offer is concrete and easy to read: "extra 20% off sale styles already up to 70% off."
- The subject matches the body, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The inline links (tees, pants, denim, dresses) give the reader category shortcuts.
- The footer nav (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is clean and tappable.

## What's weak

- The greeting renders as "Hi There, ,", a visible personalization bug.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with link tracking via `trk.send.everlane.com`. Two probed links (the header logo and "Shop Men") failed to resolve, and the compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay stripped the headers. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and most body content could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link ("Everlane")**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M503W4C3JRAB1Q3CPDBVC0C0_0` failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men"**: `…/l/01M503W4C3JRAB1Q3CPDBVC0C0_6` failed the same way, with `'/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** in both cases the tracker's destination is a **relative path with no scheme or host**. Either the redirect `Location` header is relative, or the destination is passed through unresolved.
  - If it is the `Location` header, a browser would resolve it against the tracking host, giving `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens…`. That would be a dead page and a real bug.
  - If the QA client simply failed to resolve a relative redirect, this is a probe artifact.
  - The data can't tell these apart, so verify manually. Both failing links are primary navigation, and the same-pattern links (`_0`, `_6`) suggest other `_N` links may share the issue.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 links were probed and 8 were skipped. The 38% pass rate is not a reliable measure of the whole email.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M503W4C3JRAB1Q3CPDBVC0C0` is flagged as an image missing alt text. This is the open pixel, so the impact is minor (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute. Use `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only). This is a minor issue.
- Custom fonts load with `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` inside `<style>`. Gmail and some other clients ignore `@import`. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this degrades gracefully and is not a defect.
- Dark-mode handling (`.ev-clay-dark` / `[data-ogsc]`) and MSO conditionals are present. Nothing could be confirmed as broken from the truncated source.
- Alt text on content images could not be assessed because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`). The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it does not show these headers are missing from the original message. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer and could not be checked. The QA run says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result is shown for it.
- No confirmed compliance defects.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The links carry Klaviyo/Everlane identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available data. The rest of each query string is truncated, so their presence can't be confirmed either way.
- The two failing links lead to `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`. Whether the final landing pages reflect the "extra 20% off markdowns" offer in the subject line couldn't be checked, because the redirects didn't resolve.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click the logo link and the "Shop Men" link, and inspect the raw `Location` headers from the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…` redirects. If they are relative paths, fix the tracker to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/…` destinations. Otherwise, fix the QA probe to resolve relative redirects.
2. Re-run link probing with a larger budget so the 8 skipped links are covered.
3. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
4. Capture the original message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct inbox copy rather than the relay.
5. Check that destination URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`, and that the landing pages show the markdown and 20% off promotion.
6. Give the document a meaningful `<title>`.
