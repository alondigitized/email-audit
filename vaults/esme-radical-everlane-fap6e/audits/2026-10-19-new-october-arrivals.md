# New October Arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain new-arrivals grid with a sale banner underneath. It is a near repeat of the 2026-10-18 "New Libra Season Approved Styles" send: the same nine on-season pieces (zip jackets, henley, joggers, hoodie, crewneck, turtleneck) in navy, grey, burgundy and brown, then the same brown "Extra 20% off markdowns" block. A subscriber getting this beside "New Puffers & Quilted Liners" and "The Reviews Are In", all from the same day, would read it as filler.
- The product grid is clean and the palette is on-season. But nothing is linked to a specific product, and the only offer is a markdown promo that sits below a "new arrivals" message. "New Arrivals" and "Extra 20% off markdowns" pull in opposite directions, and the sale block is what carries the price incentive.
- Judgement: competent but forgettable, with no reason to open or click now rather than later. It's a 5 in a batch that already leans on repeated creative.

## What's working

- The 3x3 grid on a neutral grey ground is tidy and renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlap.
- The autumn palette (navy, grey, burgundy, chocolate, cream) fits mid-October.
- The black SHOP NOW bar is high-contrast and easy to find.
- The brown banner gives the 20% offer a clear second block, with separate Women's and Men's sale links.
- The wordmark, header and footer nav are consistent with Everlane's brand voice.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 2026-10-18 "New Libra Season Approved Styles" creative almost item for item, on top of that day's other sends.
- Nothing in the grid is labelled, so there are no names, prices or colours, and one SHOP NOW covers all nine items.
- The offer is "limited time only" with no date in the body. The footer says it ends 10/18 at 11:59 PM PST, so the real deadline is buried in grey fine print.
- The 20% applies only to markdowns, but it sits under a "New Arrivals" header. A reader may assume it covers the new items shown, which weakens the offer's honesty.
- The grid has no models, reviews or fit detail, so it gives no social proof or personal relevance.
- The sale block has a lot of empty brown space below the links, which pushes the footer further down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with a few new arrivals with names and prices, or lead with the sale. Don't stack a generic arrivals grid on a separate markdown promo.
- 2. Cut the grid to 3-4 hero products, each with a name, price and its own link, so each click goes to a specific item instead of a broad "SHOP NOW".
- 3. Put the actual deadline in the banner ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT"). The footer date is the only deadline in the email, and it is too small to see.
- 4. Stop sending the same grid twice in a day. Rotate the product set, or fold it into the outerwear or puffers send.
- 5. Make the CTA specific, such as "Shop New Fleece" or "Shop Extra 20% Off".
- 6. Trim the empty space in the brown sale block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off markdowns ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `9 new fall pieces, now in the shop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fleece, joggers and a turtleneck in fall colors, plus extra 20% off sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New zip jackets and sweats just landed. Sale styles end at midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email with click and open tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. Two probed tracked links failed (one relative-redirect error, one 404). The header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The QA pass rate was 38% (12 of 30 links probed).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked link `_3` returns 404.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M58T08V74T1PPJS4RQY1Y164_3` is the "Luxe Rib Henley in Cashmere Blue" image link. A hero or product image link that 404s sends a click to a dead end. Re-test it from a non-datacenter IP before treating it as confirmed, since some trackers return 404 to bot user agents. The QA output also shows an empty resolved URL, so the redirect target couldn't be inspected.
- **Tracked link `_0` (logo, text "Everlane") has a relative redirect.** The probe failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`. This means the tracker's redirect `Location` is a path with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host, which would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com`. That would be a real break, but the probe can't tell whether it is one or whether it is a probe limitation, so verify manually. Check the destination host with `curl -sI`.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 material links were probed and 18 were skipped (footer, social, utility). The pass rate reflects only the probed set.
- **Open pixel has no `alt`.** The QA flags `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M58T08V74T1PPJS4RQY1Y164` as an image missing alt text. This is the tracking pixel, so it is low severity. Setting `alt=""` is the correct fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only), so there is no accessible document title or web-view title.
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>`). Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back to the Arial stack. This is an expected fallback and not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both. Check the raw message at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. Check `send.everlane.com` DNS directly.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be verified.** Both are likely in the truncated footer. The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used for marketing. This is a minor deliverability and reply-handling concern, not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Links are wrapped by the tracker, and the only visible destination parameters are Klaviyo's (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appear in the visible data. Confirm that Klaviyo UTM auto-tagging is on for this campaign.
- **Landing alignment is only partly checked.** The logo link targets `/pages/womens`. The "New October Arrivals" product links couldn't be resolved, because link `_3` returned a 404 and the rest weren't probed.
