# Don’t Miss 30% Off Savings
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day send of Everlane's Black Friday preview, and it's the plainest version yet. The hero is a dark, abstract shot of slatted light with no model and no product. White type reads "FINAL HOURS / BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW EVENT / 30% OFF OUR FAVORITES." The offer is easy to read, and the split SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN buttons are clear. The email gives me nothing to look at, though. There's no sweater, coat or face anywhere.
- It also repeats the same beat I've seen all week. "Ending Tonight: 30% Off Select Styles" went out earlier today with nearly the same dark, abstract hero. Before that came "30% Off Your Next Layer" and "Selling Fast: 30% Off Cult Favorites & More." A subscriber sees a fourth or fifth 30% reminder in four days. "Don't Miss 30% Off Savings" is also a weak subject line. "30% Off Savings" is redundant, since 30% off is the savings.
- Judgement: clean and functional, but it's a reminder with no new reason to click. It would work as a last-call nudge. It adds nothing a subscriber hasn't already seen.

## What's working

- The offer is stated plainly in large type: "30% OFF OUR FAVORITES."
- The "FINAL HOURS" label adds urgency, and the body copy backs it up with "the preview ends tonight."
- The SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN buttons are high-contrast white on black and sit inside the hero, so there's no scrolling to act.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered. The eye goes from the wordmark to the headline to the buttons.
- The fine print gives a real deadline: 11/09/2026 at 11:59 PM PST.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product, model or garment, so there's no visual reason to click. The email also looks almost identical to earlier sends this week.
- "Our favorites" is vague. The body copy says "sweaters, outerwear, and more," but nothing shows them.
- The deadline appears only in the grey footer fine print. The hero and body say "tonight" with no time or timezone.
- Below the hero, the nav list (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) repeats the hero buttons. The icon row and social links are generic footer filler.
- Nothing in the email speaks to a particular person: no member pricing, no category, no personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real product in the hero, such as a model in a sweater or coat, or a 3-up of best-sellers with prices. "Our favorites" needs to show the favorites. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT," instead of leaving it in the footer.
- 3. Fix the subject line. Drop the redundant "savings" and lead with the time limit.
- 4. Replace the nav list with two or three linked product tiles in categories the sender knows matter, like sweaters and outerwear.
- 5. Vary the creative across the send series. A dark abstract hero on consecutive "ending" emails reads as a repeat.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template whose links all go through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. Most automated warnings come from probing limits (429s, a 70s time budget) and the AgentMail relay, not from confirmed defects. The 57% pass rate reflects unverified checks, not failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations were not verified.** Of 12 material links, 7 were skipped. Four probes returned 429 (`..._2`, `_3`, `_4`, `_5`; "Shop Women"/"Shop Men"). The check itself says a 429 means the link "may be valid", so this is a probe artifact, not a broken link.
- **Unprobed links.** `..._0`, `_1`, `_6`, `_7` and `_8` were not checked. This is neither pass nor fail. One QA inconsistency: the warning says "7 link(s) unprobed" but lists only 5 URLs.
- **Wrapped links.** Every link is a redirect, so the final destination, UTM parameters and redirect chain can't be confirmed from the HTML or QA data.
- **Duplicate CTA labels.** "Shop Women" appears on `_2` and `_4`, and "Shop Men" on `_3` and `_5`. Each has a distinct tracking ID, which is normal. Different destinations behind identical labels can't be ruled out without resolving the links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the open-tracking pixel.** The image at `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M71J4HN9N084992R6EDH45EA` has no `alt`. It is a 1x1 tracking pixel, so this is low severity. `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Empty title.** The source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor and doesn't affect rendering.
- **Links not visually distinguishable.** Global styles set `a:link, a:visited, a:hover, a:active {color:#000; text-decoration:none}`. Unless button styling or surrounding context marks them, inline text links have no non-color cue (WCAG 1.4.1 risk). The truncated source doesn't show enough to confirm this.
- **Small mobile heading size.** `h4` drops to `font-size:10px` at `max-width:480px`. That is a legibility risk if h4 carries real content.
- **Web font loading.** The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Fallback stacks (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue"`) are declared, so degradation is safe.
- **Source coverage.** The source was truncated, so body markup (text alternatives for banners, table roles) could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|...|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated HTML means this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay. Treat it as unverified, not as a confirmed gap. It matters for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, so check the raw headers from a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this too is expected via the relay. It can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address.** An unsubscribe link was reportedly included in probing, but it isn't identified in the output. The postal address isn't visible in the truncated source. Neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All hrefs are opaque tracker URLs and none were resolved. The probes that ran returned 429 with no redirect data, so there is no evidence on UTM presence or landing page alignment with the "30% Off" subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probing with a lower request rate or backoff on 429, and a longer time budget, so the 7 skipped links are resolved. Capture the final URL and UTM parameters for each.
2. Get raw headers from a direct-delivery seed inbox to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC). Don't treat the relay warnings as sender defects.
3. Fix the QA tool's count mismatch (7 reported vs 5 listed unprobed URLs).
4. Exclude open-tracking pixels from the missing-alt check, or add `alt=""` to the pixel.
