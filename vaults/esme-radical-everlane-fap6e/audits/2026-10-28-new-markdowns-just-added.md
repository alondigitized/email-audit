# New Markdowns Just Added
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style note with no offer, no product and no imagery. The copy says "new markdowns" but never says how much is off. Nothing in the body is a button or a hero. The only links are four underlined words (tees, pants, denim, sweaters) and a generic nav stack. It reads like a personal note, which suits Everlane's quiet brand voice, but it gives a shopper nothing to act on.
- The most visible flaw is the greeting, "Hi There, ,". There is a stray comma and the name is missing. That is a render bug I can see, and it undercuts the "things you had your eye on" claim. That line implies behavioral targeting, but the email shows no product. Compared with the Skechers-style sends in recent history, such as the 10/27 "Our Best-Selling Tee" with its clear hero, this is much weaker at turning intent into a click.
- Judgement: a branded, quiet nudge that wastes a strong trigger (price drops on items the shopper viewed). Without a product image, a price or a button, it will mostly be skimmed.

## What's working

- The minimalist, text-forward design fits Everlane's understated brand and loads cleanly.
- The "things you had your eye on" framing suggests price-drop relevance.
- The category links (tees, pants, denim, sweaters) give a few fast entry points.
- The footer states a real deadline (11/01/2026 at 11:59 PM PST) and has clear legal and unsubscribe links.

## What's weak

- The greeting renders as "Hi There, ,", which looks broken and impersonal.
- There is no offer. No percentage, price or "up to X% off" appears anywhere.
- There is no hero image, product, or CTA button. The nav list ("Shop Women", "Shop Men") is the closest thing to a CTA.
- The deadline appears only in grey fine print and is not in the body copy, so urgency is buried.
- The email promises items "you had your eye on" but shows none of them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero showing the actual marked-down items the shopper viewed, with was/now prices. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put the offer in the headline, for example "Up to 40% off, through Sunday", and surface the 11/01 deadline in body copy.
- 3. Add a real button, such as "Shop the markdowns", in place of the underlined text links.
- 4. Fix the greeting fallback to "Hi there," with no stray comma.
- 5. Make the category links tiles or buttons, with a price anchor on each.

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "New Markdowns Just Added"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with Klaviyo click tracking (`trk.send.everlane.com`). Two of the 12 probed links failed in QA, and the pass rate was 38%. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect targets (2 FAIL):** Both tracked links failed with `unknown url type: '/…'`. The probe's redirect resolved to a path with no scheme or host.
  - Link text "Everlane" (logo/header): `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M61K38EMB5005WX4R8MVR3RJ_0` → `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`
  - Link text "Shop Men": `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M61K38EMB5005WX4R8MVR3RJ_6` → `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`
  - The redirector is returning a relative `Location` instead of an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...`. A browser would resolve that against `trk.send.everlane.com`, which probably gives a 404 or a wrong destination. This may also be a probe-side artifact (the tracker's own handling of relative paths). Either way it needs a manual click-through to confirm.
  - Both are top-of-email, high-click navigation links. The "Everlane" logo points to `/pages/womens`, so it may be a mislabeled destination.
- **Coverage gap:** 8 of 12 material links were skipped, so the remaining links' status is unknown.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** The `<img>` at `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M61K38EMB5005WX4R8MVR3RJ` has no alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** The `/o/…` open-tracking pixel lacks `alt`. This is low severity, but add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is a whitespace-only title.
- **Link styling:** The CSS forces all link states to `color:#000; text-decoration:none` (`a:not([name])`, `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:hover`). Inline text links are therefore indistinguishable from body text, which is a WCAG 1.4.1 concern if any inline text links exist.
- **Mobile text size:** `@media (max-width:480px){ .kl-text > div, … { font-size:10px !important } }` shrinks body text to 10px on small screens. That is below common legibility minimums.
- **Truncation:** The HTML was truncated, so I could not check the rest of the markup, including image alt coverage, footer structure and the preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** Neither header was captured. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message headers to confirm RFC 8058 one-click support.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sender is `support@everlane.com` on a `send.everlane.com` tracking subdomain, which is plausible alignment but unverified.
- **Footer:** The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) fell outside the truncated HTML, so I could not confirm it. QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but gives no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Klaviyo parameters are present on the destinations (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`), so Klaviyo attribution is intact.
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appears in the visible QA strings. The strings are truncated, so I can't confirm either way.
- Landing alignment could not be checked for the two failed links. A "New Markdowns" campaign sending the logo to `/pages/womens` and "Shop Men" to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` points to general landing pages rather than a markdowns collection. That is observable from the URLs but not verified against the rendered pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the redirect targets:** Make the tracked links `/l/…_0` and `/l/…_6` return absolute `Location` URLs (`https://www.everlane.com/...`), or confirm in a browser how they resolve. Then re-probe all 12 links, including the 8 skipped.
2. **Verify headers:** Inspect the raw message at the original inbox, not the AgentMail relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`.
3. **Fill the accessibility gaps:** Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, give `<title>` real content, and restore link underlines or another non-color cue for inline links.
4. **Review the mobile font size:** Check whether the 10px override on `.kl-text > div` is intended, and raise it if not.
5. **Add UTM parameters:** Add `utm_*` parameters if cross-tool attribution beyond Klaviyo is needed.
6. **Re-run on full HTML:** The truncated source left the footer, preheader and image-alt coverage unchecked.
