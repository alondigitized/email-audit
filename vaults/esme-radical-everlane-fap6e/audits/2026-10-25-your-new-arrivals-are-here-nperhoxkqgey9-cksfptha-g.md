# Your New Arrivals Are Here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-first sweater grid, and it works as a catalog. Nine knits on a neutral gray field (brown half-zip, cream V-neck, gray zip cardigan, cobalt hoodie, light-blue cardigan) make a quick, scannable hero. It fits late October well.
- The offer is the weak link. "Up to 30% off" sits in a muted blue block below the first CTA, and the hero says only "New better prices" with no number. The footer also says the offer ends 10/26 at 11:59 PM, but nothing in the body says so. A reader who sees the grid, the black button and "New Arrivals" will never learn there is a deadline. This is the third sweater/fall send in three days, after "Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters" and "30% Off: All New Alpaca" on 10/24. It reads as a repeat with the sale demoted.
- Judgement: tidy and on-brand, but it buries its best asset, the 30% off and the last-day deadline. A marketer should lead with them.

## What's working

- The 3x3 grid of nine sweaters makes the product obvious at a glance, with no hunting for what's new.
- The seasonal color palette (brown, cream, gray, cobalt, light blue) suits late October.
- The black "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" button is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The "Fall Essentials Event" block gives a clear secondary path, with separate Women's and Men's links.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The 30% off is below the fold of the first CTA, in a washed-out light-blue block with low-contrast thin white type.
- The 10/26 11:59 PM deadline appears only in the gray footer fine print, so there is no visible urgency.
- No grid item has a name, price or link cue, so the nine products give no reason to prefer one.
- The line "New sweaters. New layers. New better prices." is vague and promises prices without stating them.
- It is the third near-identical sweater promo in three days, and it has no reviews or social proof, unlike the 10/24 alpaca send.
- The blue block has a large empty bottom half, and the "Shop Women's / Shop Men's" links are small underlined text, not buttons.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "Up to 30% off sweaters. Ends tonight" above the grid, and make the primary button say "Shop 30% Off Sweaters".
- 2. Add the deadline in the body, near the CTA, not just in the footer.
- 3. Add a name and price, or a sale price, under 3-4 of the grid items, and link each to its product page.
- 4. Add one review or "best seller" quote, as the 10/24 alpaca send did.
- 5. Make the Women's / Men's links real buttons, and trim the empty space in the blue block.
- 6. Space out the sweater sends, or give each a distinct angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off sweaters, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: new sweaters, 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cardigans, half-zips and hoodies, now through 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The fall knits you've been eyeing, up to 30% off today only.`

The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect target (FAIL).** The link with text "Everlane" goes to `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5TD1B377276WJD37S8SDZ7T_0`. The prober reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=yTMOVl1X…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`. So the tracker's redirect carries a path-only destination (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - A browser would resolve that against `trk.send.everlane.com`, which is probably not the intended destination. That would break the click-through on what looks like the header or logo link.
  - It may be partly a prober limitation, because the error comes from the probe's URL handling. Confirm with `curl -sI` on the tracking URL and read the `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 30 links were probed and 18 were skipped. The skipped links, mostly footer and social, are untested.
- No other broken links were reported among the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
