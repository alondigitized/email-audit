# 30% Off Sweaters & More Starts Today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's cleanest, most confident send of the run — a real hero image with a folded-sweater stack, a legible "30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" offer stamped directly on the photo, and a black "SHOP NOW" bar immediately below it. It's a step up in production value from the 09-22/09-23 "Extra 15% Off New Markdowns" disasters and matches the quality of the 09-23 "Introducing Collegium x Everlane" editorial send, but with an actual commercial hook attached this time.
- Where it falls short is merchandising specificity and urgency. The 9-product grid is nearly monochrome — olive, oatmeal, camel, taupe — with no price tags, no "from $X" callouts, and no visible deadline anywhere above the fold (the expiration only shows up in 8pt fine print at the very bottom: "Offer valid until 09/28/2026"). It's a good top-of-funnel brand email, but it does little to convert someone who needs a reason to act today versus next week.
- Verdict: solid visual execution, weak urgency and product info — a 6/10 send that would benefit most from surfacing the deadline and adding price context near the CTA.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero shot (stacked sweaters in neutral tones) with the offer overlaid directly on the image — no separate banner needed.
- Clear visual hierarchy: eye goes hero image → "30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" → black "SHOP NOW" button in a natural top-down flow.
- 9-product grid gives real merchandising breadth (crewnecks, cardigans, turtlenecks, quarter-zip) without feeling cluttered.
- Secondary lifestyle photo ("Stock up while you can.") reinforces the sweater theme and adds a human/styling context.
- No render bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, all sections load cleanly.

## What's weak

- No visible urgency/deadline anywhere above the fold — the 09/28 expiration is buried in dense fine print at the very bottom, so a scanning reader has no reason to act now.
- Zero pricing or "% off" applied to individual products in the grid — all product tiles are shots only, no price, no discounted price shown.
- Grid images are all shot on the same flat beige background in a tight tonal palette (olive/oatmeal/taupe) — visually cohesive but makes it hard to distinguish styles at a glance.
- Only one CTA button ("SHOP NOW") for the whole email — the product grid and lifestyle photo aren't individually clickable-looking (no per-item "Shop" links), pushing everyone to the same generic landing action.
- "30% Off Sweaters & More" is vague on scope — "& More" gives no indication what else qualifies.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible countdown or deadline line near the hero copy ("Ends Sunday 9/28") — right now the only urgency signal is in 8pt legal text nobody reads.
- 2. Add prices (or "was $X, now $Y") under at least the grid thumbnails so the 30% off feels tangible rather than abstract.
- 3. Replace "& More" with a concrete second category (e.g., "30% Off Sweaters & Outerwear") so scope is clear before the click.
- 4. Add per-tile "Shop" hover/link affordance or category labels under the grid photos (Crewneck, Cardigan, Turtleneck) so browsing feels merchandised, not just decorative.
- 5. Vary the grid photography slightly (a couple of on-model shots mixed with flat-lays) to break up the tonal sameness and aid scannability.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy layers, cut to size — through Sunday only.`
