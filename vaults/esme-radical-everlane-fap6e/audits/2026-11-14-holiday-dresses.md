# Technical Audit: Everlane "Holiday Dresses"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is Klaviyo-built (`_kx` params, `static-forms.klaviyo.com` font import) and sent through a `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. Two of the 12 probed links (the logo and the SMS signup CTA) failed in the QA probe, and the header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. Most of these findings are probably artifacts of the relay or probe rather than defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7E0EQ0HP3C9KVGAY1HA3K6R_0` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" link:** `…/l/01M7E0EQ0HP3C9KVGAY1HA3K6R_16` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** both trackers redirect to a path-only `Location` (`/pages/...`) with no scheme or host. RFC 7231 permits relative `Location` values and browsers resolve them. The probe's HTTP client did not, so this may be a probe artifact. It is not confirmed as a user-facing break.
- **Still worth fixing:** a scheme-less redirect target is fragile. It also means the final landing URL (`everlane.com/pages/womens`, presumably) could not be verified for either link.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 26 links were probed. The other 14 (footer and social) are unverified, so the 38% pass rate is partly an artifact of the small sample.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang` attribute. This affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **[WARN] Image missing alt text:** `src: https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7E0EQ0HP3C9KVGAY1HA3K6R`. The `/o/` path pattern suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so it is low severity. It should still carry `alt=""` to be explicit. I can't confirm it is a pixel from the truncated source.
- **Link styling:** `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes underlines from all links. Text links are therefore not distinguishable by anything other than context. I can't see the link text in the truncated HTML, so I'm flagging this as a risk only.
- **MSO/dark-mode handling:** the source includes `mso` conditionals, `.mj-outlook-group-fix` and `[data-ogsc]` dark-mode rules. No issues found there.
- **Truncation:** the HTML was cut off, so the remaining body markup was not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) in the visible source. The per-recipient `_kx` and `bxid` params are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Likely cause:** the QA report itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. These are unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check them against the raw `.eml` from a direct send.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support-style address used for marketing. It is a minor hygiene point, not a violation.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA run included an unsubscribe link in the probe, but the truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking params present:** `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` ride on the redirect targets.
- **UTM params:** I can't confirm any, because the QA output truncates the query strings.
- **Landing pages:** the logo goes to `/pages/womens` and the SMS CTA to `/pages/sms-signup`. Neither resolved in the probe, so I can't confirm the landing pages or check that the "Holiday Dresses" CTAs point at relevant pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the two failing links with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. If a real browser lands correctly, treat it as a probe bug and have the tracker emit absolute URLs anyway.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message from a direct send, bypassing the relay.
3. Capture `Authentication-Results` the same way to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Add a `lang` attribute to `<html>` and populate `<title>`.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Raise the probe cap, or whitelist the footer and unsubscribe links, so the pass rate reflects more than 12 of 26 links.
7. Log full, untruncated redirect chains (including UTMs) so continuity can be checked.
