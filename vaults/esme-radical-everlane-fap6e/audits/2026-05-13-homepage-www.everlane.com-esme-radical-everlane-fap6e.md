# Homepage snapshot · www.everlane.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at is barely a homepage — it's a severely cropped, zoomed-in lifestyle shot in earthy terracotta and beige tones. I can't read a single word, I can't see a nav bar, I can't see a CTA, and I can't make out what product they're selling. Everlane built their entire brand on radical transparency — cost breakdowns, factory tours, honest pricing — and the first thing they serve me is a blurry close-up that tells me nothing. That's ironic in the worst possible way.

## What's working

- **Color palette** — the terracotta/rust and warm beige feel on-brand for Everlane's minimalist aesthetic. If this is intentional moodboarding, the tonal choice at least tracks with the brand I know.
- **No clutter above the fold** — there's nothing competing for attention, which would be a strength if there were anything worth competing for.

## What's weak

- **Zero readable copy** — I own five pairs of Way-High Jeans and I cannot tell this is Everlane's website. No wordmark, no tagline, no hero headline visible.
- **No CTA whatsoever** — the one job of a homepage hero is to move me somewhere. This image doesn't have a button, a link label, or even a scroll affordance in frame.
- **No offer, no hook** — if there's a sale, a new collection, or a cost-breakdown story (the one thing that made me a customer), none of it is visible here.
- **Image is unreadable at this crop** — the extreme zoom makes the lifestyle shot identifiable as neither a garment, a person, nor a scene. It communicates nothing.
- **No navigation visible** — I can't tap into Bottoms, Denim, or any category. Dead end.

## Recommendations

- **Pull back the hero image** so the full scene is legible — show a model in the Way-High or a Uniform item with the factory cost card overlaid. That's the Everlane brand proposition in one frame.
- **Put the headline copy in frame** — something like "The $128 Jean. Here's why." should be readable above the fold, not cropped out.
- **Add a visible Shop CTA** — even a text link in the hero. "Shop Denim" or "See the breakdown" is all I need to move.
- **Surface the cost transparency hook** — a small "Factory price / Our price" callout in the hero or an announcement bar is the one thing that makes Everlane different from every other minimalist brand; it needs to be above the fold.
