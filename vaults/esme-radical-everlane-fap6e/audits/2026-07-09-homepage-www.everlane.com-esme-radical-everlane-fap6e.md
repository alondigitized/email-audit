# Homepage snapshot · www.everlane.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- What I'm looking at is an extreme close-up of what appears to be a ribbed or corduroy fabric in a sand/beige tone — that's the entire viewport. No logo, no nav, no copy, no button, no brand signal of any kind. A tiny sliver of teal clips the left edge. Everlane markets itself on radical transparency but the homepage right now is radiating radical opacity — there is literally nothing readable above the fold.

## What's working

- The fabric texture itself is beautiful — close-up corduroy or rib-knit in a warm sand reads premium and on-brand for Everlane's aesthetic DNA
- The one sliver of teal on the left edge suggests there might be a second element partially in frame — at least it's not a white screen of death

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold UI is gone.** No logo, no wordmark, no nav bar, no hamburger menu, no hero headline, no CTA — nothing. This is a catastrophic above-the-fold failure
- **No brand identification whatsoever** — if I didn't know I typed everlane.com, I could not identify which brand this is
- **Fabric texture at 100% zoom with no context** is meaningless; I need to know *what* the fabric is, the name, the price, the radical-transparency cost breakdown — none of that exists
- **The teal sliver on the left** looks like a clipped nav element or badge that got cut off, suggesting a layout collapse
- **Zero conversion surface** — there is nothing to tap, nothing to read, no path forward visible

## Recommendations

- **Fix the render bug immediately** — whatever CSS, lazy-load, or scroll-position triggered the blank/texture-only state needs a hotfix; this is losing real revenue right now
- **Ensure the hero image always renders with overlaid copy** — if the bg-image loads before the text layer, add a minimum-height skeleton so at least the nav and headline placeholder hold space
- **Add a sticky nav bar** — even if the hero body fails to render, a fixed top nav with the Everlane logo and primary categories ensures the page is always minimally navigable
- **Implement a fallback content block** — if the hero module errors, show a static fallback: wordmark, one category CTA, and the free-shipping threshold
