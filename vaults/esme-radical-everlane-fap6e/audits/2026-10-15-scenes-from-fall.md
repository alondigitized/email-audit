# Scenes From Fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand fall mood send with no offer. It follows a week of Friends & Family pressure ("One Last Chance for 30% Off Sitewide," "Final Hours: 30% Off Sitewide & Storewide"). It reads as a palate cleanser, much like the two earlier brand-mood sends on 2026-10-14, "A New Season Comes Into View" and "A Softer Take on Corduroy."
- The hero is a four-photo street-style collage with "Scenes From Fall" in thin white type split across the seams, and a black SHOP THE COLLECTION bar below. Below that is a 3x3 grid of fall pieces in browns, creams and olive, then a large crosswalk photo and a second identical CTA. The creative is polished and consistent, and the palette is cohesive.
- It sells a mood, though, not a reason to click. The email has no price, no deadline, and no product named or linked individually. The pale hero text also has weak contrast over the light photos. It will do fine for subscribers who already like the brand, but it gives everyone else little to act on.

## What's working

- The hero collage is cohesive and seasonal, with a black SHOP THE COLLECTION bar that is easy to find.
- The 3x3 product grid shows nine fall pieces (jackets, knits, a turtleneck) in one tight palette, so it works as a quick catalog.
- The layout is simple: hero, CTA, grid, hero, CTA. The eye moves down without any friction.
- The second CTA after the closing photo catches people who scroll to the end.
- It rests the sale pressure of the past week. The "worth noting" line is quiet, honest copy with no fine print.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, or deadline anywhere, so the email gives no reason to act today.
- "Scenes From Fall" in thin white type over light photos has weak contrast, and the split across the collage seams makes it harder to read.
- Both CTAs say "Shop the Collection" and go to a generic collection. None of the nine grid products shows a name or price.
- It adds to a heavy cadence. This is the 4th send in about two days, and it follows the 30%-off close of "One Last Chance for 30% Off Sitewide."
- The tagline "Every piece is worth noting" is vague and says nothing about the clothes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the grid shoppable. Link each tile to its product and add a name and price, such as "Quilted Jacket $148." This is the biggest lift, because it turns a mood board into a catalog.
- 2. Add one concrete hook, such as a fall-edit callout, a free-shipping threshold, or a "new this week" tag.
- 3. Fix the headline contrast with a dark text overlay or a solid band, and keep it on one line.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop Fall Jackets" or "Shop the Fall Edit."
- 5. Space this send away from the promo sends, or fold it into the next one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nine pieces we're wearing on repeat this season.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Scenes From Fall"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, table-based layout with branded click tracking (`trk.send.everlane.com`). It has one confirmed broken link, the "Sign Up for Texts" CTA. The header and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and don't confirm defects. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, body and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failure on the SMS signup link (FAIL):**
  - Link text: "Icon | Sign Up for Texts".
  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4YGGZKRGZNSJR9TAXX1EZFX_18`.
  - Error: `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…'`.
  - The destination is a path-relative URL with no scheme or host. That points to a relative `Location` header on the tracker redirect, or a relative href passed through the wrapper.
  - Mail clients and browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host, so the click may land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/sms-signup…` instead of everlane.com. That would 404 or error.
  - This could also be a limitation of the probe's redirect handling. Confirm with `curl -sI` on the tracked URL and check whether `Location` is absolute.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 16 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The skipped links are unverified, not shown to be good.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the image at `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4YGGZKRGZNSJR9TAXX1EZFX` has no `alt`. The `/o/` path suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so impact is low. Add `alt=""` to keep it out of screen-reader output.
- **Empty title:** the source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Some clients and web-view versions show it, and it is an accessibility gap.
- **Link styling:** the `<style>` block sets `a:link, a:visited, a:hover, a:active { color:#000; text-decoration:none }`. Inline links in body copy are indistinguishable from surrounding text if they are black and not underlined. This is only a problem for text links, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- **Small mobile type:** `h4` drops to `font-size:10px !important` under 480px. If it carries meaningful copy, that is below common legibility minimums.
- **Font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by some clients (for example Gmail). The font stacks fall back to `Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`, so there is no breakage.
- **Outlook support:** MSO conditionals, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` and the VML namespace are all present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%…%}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message headers in a direct-to-inbox test, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay. Verify in a direct test or with the sending-domain DNS records.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** the QA tool always probes one, and no failure was reported for it. The footer markup was not visible, so the physical postal address could not be confirmed.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as the marketing sender. This is not a defect, but confirm replies are monitored.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking parameters:** the SMS link carries Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`) behind a custom tracking domain. No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available evidence.
- **Landing page alignment:** it can't be verified for the 16 unprobed links. The SMS link's destination is broken, as described in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the SMS signup link.** Make the destination absolute (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/sms-signup…`) in Klaviyo, then re-test the tracked URL end to end. This is the only confirmed defect.
2. **Check the raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on a direct send. The relay results are inconclusive.
3. **Add `alt=""`** to the tracking pixel and populate `<title>` with the subject line.
4. **Confirm `utm_*` parameters** on key CTAs, and re-run the audit with the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, merge tokens and skipped links can be checked.
