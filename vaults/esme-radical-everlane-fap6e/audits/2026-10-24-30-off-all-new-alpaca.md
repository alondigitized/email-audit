# 30% Off: All New Alpaca
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-led alpaca sweater send, and the reviews are the best part. Everlane opens with "over 2500 reviews," then shows two products, each with a real customer quote and a price. That is more credible than the usual lifestyle collage. The problem is that the subject promises "30% Off: All New Alpaca," and the body doesn't deliver it. The Cocoon Crew ($118) and Crew Cardigan ($168) show no markdown or strike-through. The 30% only appears in a blue block near the bottom, where it reads "Up to 30% off sweaters, outerwear, & more."
- This is also the third send for the Fall Essentials Event in four days. The first was "You're In: Shop Up to 30% Off" on 10/21, then "Fall Essentials Event: Up to 30% Off" on 10/22, and "Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters" earlier today. A subscriber has seen the offer three times, and this one hides it under a product story. The creative is clean and trustworthy, but the offer and the subject don't match, and the offer is buried.

## What's working

- Social proof carries the email: "over 2500 reviews," plus a named quote under each product.
- Prices ($118, $168) sit right in the product headlines, so there is no guessing.
- Each product pairs a model shot with a flat-lay, which answers "how does it fit" and "what does it look like on its own."
- The layout is clean and uncluttered. The hero has a full-width black SHOP NOW bar and the modules are evenly spaced.
- The blue event block does name both audiences, with separate Shop Women's and Shop Men's links.

## What's weak

- The subject claims "30% Off: All New Alpaca," but the body says "up to 30%" on "select styles." The alpaca products show no discount, so the offer feels like bait-and-switch.
- The only offer block is near the bottom, after two product sections. The hero has no mention of the sale.
- "Warm & Fuzzies" is thin white text on a light gray backdrop, over the model's sweater and jeans. It is hard to read and gives no offer or action.
- The deadline (10/26, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the gray fine print, so the urgency is invisible.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW." The buttons don't name the product, and the two product links are small underlined text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the discount true and visible. Put "30% off" and a strike-through price on the alpaca products and in the hero, or change the subject to say "Up to 30% off."
- 2. Replace the "Warm & Fuzzies" hero text with a high-contrast line such as "New Alpaca Shades. 30% Off Through Sunday." Move the event block up under the hero.
- 3. Put the deadline in the body, not the fine print: "Ends Sunday 10/26."
- 4. Use specific button copy: "Shop the Cocoon Crew" and "Shop the Cardigan." Make the buttons real buttons.
- 5. Give the offer a different angle than the last three sends, such as the new colors or a low-stock note.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML template with click and open tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The one confirmed defect is a header link whose redirect target is a relative path. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified. The HTML was truncated, so only the head and CSS were reviewed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** The link with text "Everlane" (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5QTT1C59X7X29EXAKYSKWJK_0`) resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. That target has no scheme or host, which the prober rejected as `unknown url type`.
  - If the tracker really returns a relative `Location`, browsers will resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`. The user would then land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com`.
  - I can't tell from the data whether this is a real redirect bug or a probe artifact. Re-test with `curl -sI` to see the raw `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage is 12 of 27 links.** 15 links were skipped, so their health is unknown.
- **Open pixel has no `alt`.** The [WARN] flags `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5QTT1C59X7X29EXAKYSKWJK`, which is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel. Missing alt is low impact for a 1×1 pixel, but `alt=""` is still correct.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` has none, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is blank.
- **Web-font `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The Arial/Helvetica fallbacks cover this.
- **Inconsistent font names.** The CSS uses `"MaisonNeue-Light"` for h1 but `"Maison Neue"` for h2 to h4, so the custom font may load for some headings and not others.
- **Small mobile h4.** `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` at `max-width: 480px` is small.
- **Underlined links removed.** `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` all set `text-decoration:none`, so links in body text may rely on position alone.
- **Outlook handling looks fine.** The MSO conditionals and VML namespaces are present, so there are no issues there.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source, but the truncated HTML limits this check.

**5. Compliance**
