# Homepage snapshot · www.everlane.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Everlane right now looks like a brand that forgot to turn the lights on. The top half of this homepage is a near-black void — whatever hero image or copy lives there is completely illegible. Below that, two lone product shots float on a gray field: black denim shorts and a nude kitten-heel sandal. That's it. For a brand whose entire identity is radical transparency and the "why behind the price," there is nothing here that tells me anything about cost, construction, or conscience.

## What's working

- **Seasonal product pairing** — black denim shorts and a nude sandal side by side is a coherent spring/summer moment. At least the merch team is awake.
- **Clean product photography** — the two items visible have that flat, no-distraction Everlane aesthetic. The sandal shot in particular has good negative space.
- **No noise** — there are no pop-ups, no fake countdown timers, no "10% off if you email us" modal in my face. I'll take that.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a black hole.** Top half of the viewport is pure dark — no headline, no subhead, no image of a person, nothing. If this is a video hero that didn't load, that's a critical performance failure. If it's intentional, it's branding malpractice.
- **Zero copy anywhere above the fold.** I am Everlane's most loyal customer segment — I know the markup math, I have five pairs of their jeans — and there is not a single word to give me a reason to scroll.
- **No CTA button.** Not one. "Shop now," "See the cost breakdown," "New arrivals" — nothing. How am I supposed to do anything?
- **No price transparency signal.** Everlane's entire moat is "here's what it costs to make, here's our markup." None of that is present. This homepage could belong to any minimalist DTC brand from 2019.
- **No navigation visible.** I can't see a hamburger menu, a nav bar, or a category strip. On mobile I'd be tapping blind.

## Recommendations

- **Fix or replace the hero immediately.** If that dark top panel is a failed video load, set a fallback still image with an overlay headline. Even "The Way-High Jean. Here's the math." in white type on a clean model shot would be 10x better than this void.
- **Add the cost-breakdown hook above the fold.** A one-liner like "Retail: $98. Our margin: 2.7x. Here's why." is what separates Everlane from every other $100 jean brand — put it where I can see it without scrolling.
- **Drop a visible CTA on the product cards.** Even a simple "Shop the Short" label below the shorts image gives me somewhere to tap.
- **Surface a nav strip.** One horizontal scroll of categories — Denim, Tops, Shoes, Sale — directly below the hero so I can route myself in one tap.
