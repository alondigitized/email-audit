# A Special One Day Offer: Extra 25% Off Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the same peony/tulip macro shot Everlane already ran today under "Today Only: Extra 25% Off Sale" — the hero, headline split, black "SHOP NOW" bar, and "already up to 70% off" callout are pixel-for-pixel identical to that earlier send. The only change is the subject line's wrapper ("A Special One Day Offer" vs. "Today Only"), which is a tell, not a refresh — this is the same creative, same offer, same day, sent again.
- By the fifth near-identical "extra 25% off" variant today, this stops reading as a campaign and starts reading as spam-adjacent behavior: multiple emails, same discount, cosmetically different subject lines. The creative itself is clean and legible, but the redundancy is the story here.
- Judgement: functional but exhausted. If this were the only "Extra 25%" email today it'd land a 6; as a repeat of an identical send hours later, it's a fatigue problem, not a design problem.

## What's working

- Clean, high-contrast hero shot with legible text overlay — no rendering bugs, images load fully.
- Offer is unambiguous: "EXTRA 25% OFF SALE (ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF)" stacks two concrete numbers.
- Single black "SHOP NOW" CTA is impossible to miss and sits directly under the hero.
- Deadline is specific and credible ("Offer valid until 09/08/2026 at 11:59 PM PST") in the footer fine print.

## What's weak

- Identical hero image, headline, and CTA bar to the earlier "Today Only: Extra 25% Off Sale" send from today — zero visual differentiation.
- Subject line is a thin rewrap of an offer already emailed once (or more) today, which reads as list fatigue/spam behavior rather than a distinct campaign beat.
- No product shown at all — a floral macro shot with zero apparel means the click has to happen on faith, not desire.
- Below the fold is a generic nav wall (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) with no merchandising, no bestseller imagery, nothing to justify the click beyond the discount already stated above.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop resending the same offer same-day.** This is the actual fix — no copy tweak fixes a duplicate send. Suppress subsequent sends to anyone who already got today's "Today Only 25% Off" email, or hold this variant for a different day/segment.
- 2. Swap the floral hero for actual product — even one styled outfit shot would give the CTA something concrete to click toward, since "deepest discounts of the sale" with no products shown is an empty promise.
- 3. Add a real urgency element to the hero itself (a countdown or "ends tonight" restated near the CTA), since right now urgency only lives in footer fine print most people won't read.
- 4. Segment the nav row — swap generic "Shop Women / Shop Men" for "Shop Dresses Under $50" or similar to give a next click that's about product, not just navigation.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Extra 25% Off Is About to Expire`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The deepest markdowns of the sale end at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack an extra 25% off styles already up to 70% off.`
