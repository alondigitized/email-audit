# Black Friday Preview: Shop 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident Black Friday preview send, and the 30% is stated plainly in the hero. It follows yesterday's "Early Access Granted: Black Friday Preview" (7/10), which teased the event. Today the offer lands. The "30% OFF OUR FAVORITES" headline sits over a model in a camel coat and burgundy scarf, with a black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath. The eye goes to the offer first.
- The weakness is the deadline and the scope. "Offer valid until 11/09/2026 at 11:59 PM PST" and "select styles only" are both in grey fine print at the very bottom. Nothing in the hero or body creates urgency. "Our favorites" is vague about what is actually discounted. The small "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW EVENT" line also fades into the tan coat behind it.
- Overall, it's solid and on-brand, and it is a real step up from the offer-free product stories of 11/01 to 11/04. It leaves conversion on the table by hiding its best urgency lever.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" headline is large and unmissable, and it is the first offer Everlane has put in front of me this week.
- The hero is a single clear idea with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar, so the primary CTA is obvious.
- The 2x2 grid (sweaters, outerwear, denim, accessories) matches the body copy and gives clear entry points.
- The seasonal fit is right: outerwear, knitwear and a beanie suit early November.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- The deadline (11/09, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the grey footer fine print, so the urgency is invisible.
- "Our Favorites" and "select styles only" leave it unclear what is discounted. The tiles show no prices, no original-versus-sale pricing and no product names.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW EVENT" eyebrow is thin white text over a busy tan coat, so it has weak contrast.
- Every tile CTA is a generic "SHOP [category]", and "SHOP ALL" is attached to a beanie, which is confusing.
- This is the second Black Friday Preview send in two days, and the creative style is close to the 11/04 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Monday 11/9", and add a short "Ends Monday" line near the CTA.
- 2. Name what is discounted with a line like "Sweaters, coats and denim, 30% off", and show sale prices on at least two tiles.
- 3. Strengthen the eyebrow with a darker scrim or a bolder weight so "Black Friday Preview" is readable.
- 4. Change the tile CTAs to something specific, such as "Shop 30% Off Sweaters". Use a lifestyle or best-seller image for "Shop All" instead of a beanie.
- 5. Add one social-proof element, such as a review count or "Best-seller" tag, to a single hero product.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, outerwear and denim, all 30% off through Monday night.`
