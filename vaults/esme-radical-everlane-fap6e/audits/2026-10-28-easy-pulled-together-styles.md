# Easy Pulled-Together Styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial Everlane send built around the "What to Wear Now" hero. A model in an olive knit and ivory slip skirt stands against a white column, with a black SHOP NOW bar directly under the image. The visual hierarchy is excellent: the hero comes first, then a zig-zag of three products, then a cream "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" banner.
- The weak point is the offer. "Now at better prices" and "Better prices" appear twice, but there is no percentage, no dollar figure and no price on any product. The email promises a deal and never shows it. Compare the 2026-10-27 "Ends Tonight: Up to 30% Off" sends, which at least gave a number. This one is prettier and more product-led, but it has no hook.
- Verdict: a polished, on-brand seasonal send that will get browsed but not acted on. A single concrete price or discount would lift it more than any other change.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. It has a clear seasonal theme, a model who fits the brand, and a full-width black CTA bar beneath it.
- The zig-zag layout of cardigan, shirt and skirt is easy to scan. Each product has a short, useful line of copy, such as "Sheer on purpose. Soft, lightweight, and easy to layer through fall."
- The copy is concise and fits the brand: "Polished enough for the office, easy enough for everything after."
- The cream "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block closes the email on a clear brand message.
- There are no render bugs. Images load and the spacing is consistent.

## What's weak

- No price, percentage or dollar amount appears anywhere. "Better prices" is vague and unproven.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". The four identical labels carry no product specificity, and the small underlined text links are easy to miss.
- No reviews, ratings, sizing or fit information appears for any product.
- There is no urgency or deadline, so nothing pushes the reader to act today.
- The product CTAs are small underlined text links, which are weak next to the full-width hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real price signal in the hero and the cream banner. For example, show "Cardigan now $98 (was $128)" or "Prices cut on 40+ fall styles". This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add the price next to each product name, and a star rating or "Best-seller" tag where one is earned.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop the Cardigan", "Shop the Shirt" and "Shop the Skirt".
- 4. Add a credible time frame, such as "New prices through Sunday", if one is true.
- 5. Change the subject so it states the news, which is that prices are lower.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Polished for work, easy for weekends. New lower prices on cardigans, shirts and skirts.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Easy Pulled-Together Styles"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML-style template with a mostly sound structure. The two QA failures come from link probing: the tracked redirects return relative `Location` values (`/pages/...`). These are probably probe artifacts, but I couldn't confirm that from the data provided. The authentication and unsubscribe-header gaps are unverified because of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two tracked links failed probing.** Both were `unknown url type`, which is a Python `urllib`/`requests` error for a URL with no scheme or host.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M624BP1A2JZYAQATDYQ4THR0_16` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M624BP1A2JZYAQATDYQ4THR0_0` ("Everlane", the logo/home link) resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…`.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker answers with a relative `Location` header. RFC 7231 permits this, and browsers resolve it against the tracker host, so real users would probably land on the tracker's host instead of everlane.com. That is a possible defect, but the data only shows a relative redirect that the probe couldn't resolve. Verify by following the redirect manually with `curl -sIL`. If `/pages/womens` resolves against `trk.send.everlane.com`, those two links would 404 for real users.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 14 skipped (footer, social, utility), so the 38% pass rate understates link health and shouldn't be read as an error rate.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M624BP1A2JZYAQATDYQ4THR0` is flagged for missing alt. This is a tracking pixel, so the impact is minimal (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pixel alt text:** the open pixel has no `alt` attribute. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. Nothing indicates a content image is missing alt, but the HTML was truncated, so the body images weren't fully reviewed.
- **Dark-mode handling:** the `.ev-clay-light`/`.ev-clay-dark` swap is keyed on `[data-ogsc]`, which is Outlook.com-specific. It has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` equivalent in the visible source, so Apple Mail and Gmail clients may not get the dark variant.
- **Mobile type size:** `h4` drops to `10px` at max-width 480px, which is below the typical 11–13px legibility floor. It matters only if `h4` carries real content.
- **Remote font:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The stack falls back to Arial and Helvetica, which is acceptable.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` is empty. This is minor, but it affects web-view and accessibility titling.
- The Outlook conditionals (`mso` XML, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and `lang`-less `<html>` are standard. Note that `<html>` has no `lang` attribute in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the truncated source. The `_kx` and `bxid` parameters are per-recipient Klaviyo identifiers, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Everlane sends through Klaviyo, which normally adds both, but check the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unverified. The From address is `support@everlane.com` while the tracking domain is `trk.send.everlane.com`, which appears to be a subdomain of the sender. That is compatible with relaxed alignment, but it hasn't been validated.
- **Visible footer and unsubscribe link:** not confirmed, because the source was truncated before the footer. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reported no pass or fail for it.
- **Sender address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support address used for marketing. That is a deliverability and reply-handling concern rather than a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none appear in the visible resolved URLs. Attribution relies on Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid` plus `cm_fl…` (truncated) parameters. If analytics expects `utm_*`, confirm the full `cm_*` set maps to it.
- **Landing alignment:** the two resolved paths (`/pages/womens`, `/pages/sms-signup`) are plausible, but neither was successfully fetched. Landing page alignment is unverified for those links.
- **Redirect hop:** every click goes through `trk.send.everlane.com`, which adds a redirect hop. That is normal for ESP tracking.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check the relative redirects.** Manually follow `…_0` and `…_16` with `curl -sIL`. If the `Location` header is relative, fix the tracker config so it emits absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URLs. That would make the logo and SMS signup links dead for real recipients.
2. **Add `alt=""`** to the open-tracking pixel `…/o/01M624BP1A2JZYAQATDYQ4THR0`.
3. **Confirm headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a direct-delivery inbox, not the relay.
4. **Add `lang`** to `<html>`, fill in `<title>`, and review the 10px mobile `h4`.
5. **Add `prefers-color-scheme` dark-mode support** beyond the `[data-ogsc]` hook.
6. **Re-run the QA on the full HTML** so the footer, unsubscribe link and remaining 14 links are covered. The tool truncated the source, so this audit covers only part of it.
