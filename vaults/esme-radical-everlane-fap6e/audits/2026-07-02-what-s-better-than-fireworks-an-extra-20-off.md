# What’s Better Than Fireworks? An Extra 20% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's tenth consecutive promotional email in seven days — and the third or fourth time this exact hero creative (folded-tee stack, "SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE" white overlay, black SHOP NOW button) has run with only the discount number changed. The July 4th hook in the subject is the best headline of the entire sale run: it's timely, the comparison lands, and the character count is under 50. Unfortunately, the email body never pays off the fireworks premise — it pivots immediately back to the same SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE asset and the same SHOP NOW CTA. A subscriber who's opened every email this week already knows the visual; the subject promised something fresh and the creative doesn't deliver it.
- The functional upgrade this send adds is the "UNDER $100" secondary section with split SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN CTAs. That's the first segmentation signal in the entire campaign and it's buried below the fold. The product grid (six items, no prices) also repeats the prior format exactly.
- Bottom line: the subject is the strongest of the run; everything beneath it is on autopilot. At ten emails in seven days, Everlane is deep into list-burn territory regardless of how good the individual send is.

## What's working

- **Subject line is the best of the run.** "What's Better Than Fireworks? An Extra 20% Off" is specific, timely, and hits 47 characters — mobile-clean.
- **Offer is unambiguous.** "EXTRA 20% OFF* SALE STYLES (ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF)" gives both the incremental and cumulative discount in one read.
- **"UNDER $100" secondary section** is a smart addition not present in the previous sends — a concrete price ceiling gives hesitant buyers a mental anchor.
- **SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN CTAs** are the first gender-segmentation signals in the campaign, which reduces click-to-relevant-product friction.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, text is legible.

## What's weak

- **Body never executes the fireworks premise.** The hero is the same SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE tee stack from June 26. The subject promises something new; the creative delivers a rerun.
- **Product grid has no prices.** Six items are shown with zero price information — in a sale email leading with a discount, this is a missed conversion lever.
- **"Long weekend" body copy is too soft.** "These styles won't be here forever" is a placeholder urgency line; a real deadline ("Sale ends July 4 at midnight") would sharpen it.
- **Cadence is unsustainable.** This is email ten of ten in seven days — the July 1 "Up to 70% Off" send already felt like the third iteration of the same concept. Marginal openers on this send are engaging despite the cadence, not because of it.
- **Asterisk on "20% OFF*"** is visible but fine print is unreadable at render size — introduces doubt about offer scope.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Use July 4th imagery in the hero.** The subject earns a holiday creative; even a single red/white/blue accent or a "JULY 4TH SALE" header instead of the evergreen SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE would pay off the subject line. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the hero.** Replace "won't be here forever" with "Sale ends July 4 at midnight" — converts implied urgency to real urgency.
- 3. **Show prices on the product grid.** At minimum, show the sale price. Even "Was $98 → $54" on one product outperforms a grid of unpriced thumbnails in a sale email.
- 4. **Move SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN above the fold.** These CTAs are the most targeted elements in the email and they're below the primary CTA. Swap the order or add them directly under the hero button.
- 5. **Rest the list after this send.** Ten emails in seven days is eroding the segment. A 3–4 day pause post-holiday would reset subscriber goodwill before the next campaign.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Extra 20% Off Ends July 4 — Up to 70% Off Already`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at midnight — shop before the long weekend is over.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Already up to 70% off. Stack an extra 20% through July 4.`
