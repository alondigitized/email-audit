# New Hues Have Arrived
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's latest editorial send following a week of promotional hammering — and the pattern is now very familiar. Wordmark at top, lifestyle hero with text overlay, product grid below, sustainability footer, nav links. What's new here is a mild pivot: the banner at the very top keeps the "Extra 20% Off Sale Styles (Already Up to 70% Off)" promo alive, while the body content pivots to a fresh editorial angle — "Inspired by the coast," ocean blues and neutrals. That top banner is doing a lot of heavy lifting, because the body email itself is a zero-urgency, no-price, no-CTA editorial spread that would feel thin standing alone.
- The product grid is solid — eight items shown in a clean flat-lay grid covering blues and tans, which directly illustrates the "new hues" subject promise. The lifestyle hero (a woman in a light blue button-down shirt) reads warm and aspirational. The sustainability block at the bottom is a brand-consistency play, not a conversion play. This email is essentially a soft new-arrivals editorial bolted onto a still-live promotional banner.
- Verdict: functional and on-brand, but the same structural template Everlane has shipped for two weeks straight. The editorial content underserves the subject line's specific promise — "new hues" implies color story, but there's no actual color-focused copy beyond the subhead. A subscriber who opened hoping for something fresh gets the same layout they've seen ten times.

## What's working

- The hero lifestyle image (model in blue stripe shirt, coastal vibe) directly mirrors the "ocean blues" copy — visual and copy are aligned.
- Product grid shows range across the color story without feeling cluttered — eight items, coherent palette, flat-lay presentation is clean.
- The top banner smartly keeps the live sale offer visible without making the whole email feel like another discount blast.
- "Ocean-inspired blues and versatile neutrals designed for the views" subhead is short, evocative, and accurate to what's shown below.
- EVERLANE wordmark placement and overall layout are clean — no render bugs visible.

## What's weak

- Zero price information in the body — if these items are part of the existing "Extra 20% Off" sale, that's not communicated at the product level.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA appears twice (hero + product sections) but with no label specificity — doesn't tell me what I'm shopping.
- The "new hues" promise in the subject lands weakly — there's no color-story narrative beyond a single subhead line; the grid just shows items without any color callouts.
- Sustainability block ("Read Our Latest Impact Report") at the bottom breaks the conversion momentum completely at the exact moment someone might have clicked through the grid.
- After six days of "Extra 20% Off" emails, dropping that mechanic to the top banner only — with no in-body reinforcement — will cause some subscribers to miss that these new arrivals are also on sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add price anchors or "Also on sale" callouts to the product grid.** If these new hues are included in the Extra 20% Off event, say so under each item tile. Silence at the product level after a week of promotional training reads as full-price, which kills clicks.
- 2. **Make the hero CTA specific.** Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop New Blues" or "Shop the Color Drop" — anything that reflects the specific editorial angle rather than a generic push.
- 3. **Move the sustainability block out of this email entirely**, or drop it to a footer footnote. Impact reports don't belong in color-drop emails — they interrupt purchase intent at the worst possible moment.
- 4. **Add a color story intro with 2–3 named hues.** If the thesis is "new hues," name them. "Pacific Blue, Sand, Navy" gives the subscriber something to latch onto and makes the editorial feel like a real curation, not just a grid dump.
- 5. **Subject and preheader refinement (see below).** Current subject is serviceable but generic; preheader is doing nothing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ocean blues + neutrals just landed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New colors for summer — shop the hue drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ocean-inspired styles, some already up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Blues, tans, and neutrals — now in the sale`
