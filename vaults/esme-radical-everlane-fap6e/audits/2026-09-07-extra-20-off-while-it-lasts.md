# Extra 20% Off While It Lasts
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same "End of Summer Sale" watercolor-hero template Everlane has been running since 08/01 — tan-dress model, olive/cream left/right headline split ("END OF SUMMER SALE" / "EXTRA 20% OFF SALE STYLES"), black "SHOP NOW" bar, and the blue-toned "UNDER $100" secondary banner. This is the third near-identical version of this exact creative in the audited history (09/06 "How About An Extra 20% Off?" and 09/05 "Extra 20% Off Sale Dresses & Shirts" both carry the same hero, same stack, same offer). The only thing that moves send to send is the subject line wording.
- The offer itself is clear and reasonably compelling (extra 20% off items already up to 70% off), and the layout has no render bugs — clean hierarchy, offer readable without scrolling on mobile. But there's zero novelty left here; a subscriber who's opened even one of the last three sends has already seen every pixel of this email.
- Judgement: functional, on-brand, zero-bug email that's now running on fumes creatively. The subject is fine but interchangeable with the last two sends — nothing here would make me open today's over yesterday's.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous offer stated twice (hero + preheader-adjacent body copy): extra 20% off, stacked on up to 70% off.
- Clean visual hierarchy — eye lands on model + headline split immediately, "SHOP NOW" bar is unmissable.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- Urgency line ("Customer faves are selling out and sizes are getting limited") reinforces scarcity without being spammy.
- Secondary "Under $100" banner gives a second, lower-commitment CTA path (Shop Women / Shop Men).

## What's weak

- Identical hero creative to the 09/05 and 09/06 sends — same model, same crop, same headline split, same black bar. Zero visual differentiation in three consecutive days.
- Subject "Extra 20% Off While It Lasts" is functionally the same claim as 09/06's "How About An Extra 20% Off?" — no new information for a subscriber who's already seen two of these.
- Fine print buried at the very bottom in light gray reveals the deadline (09/08/2026 11:59 PM PST) — that urgency detail should be surfaced in the subject/preheader, not hidden.
- No product imagery beyond the single lifestyle shot — no actual sale items shown, so there's nothing concrete to click toward besides "Shop Now."

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire this hero for at least one send — cadence fatigue is the single biggest issue three sends running the same creative. Swap in actual product tiles or a different model/crop even if the offer stays.
- 2. Move the real deadline ("Ends 9/8 at midnight PST") out of the footer fine print and into the subject or preheader — that's the one piece of copy that could justify opening *today's* email specifically.
- 3. Add a product grid (3-6 sale items) below the hero so there's something concrete to click besides a generic "Shop Now."
- 4. Vary preheader copy across the three near-identical sends this week — right now they all essentially restate the hero.
