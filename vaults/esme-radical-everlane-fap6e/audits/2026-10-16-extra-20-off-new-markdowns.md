## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The automated probe failed on two primary navigation links (2 FAIL), and the 38% pass rate reflects that plus several header checks the AgentMail relay can't verify. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and per-link content couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M512RQFYYYN1TWT071HSSMQB_0` returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M512RQFYYYN1TWT071HSSMQB_6` returned `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** The tracker appears to answer with a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?…`, `/collections/…?…`) instead of an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/…` URL. The probe could not follow it.
- **Why it matters:** Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the request host, which would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, probably a 404. The probe may only be failing on how it handles relative redirects. Verify with `curl -sI` on both URLs and check the `Location` header.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 links were probed and 8 were skipped. The other links, including the CTAs, are unverified.
- **Open-pixel alt text (not a real defect):** The "missing alt" warning is for `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M512RQFYYYN1TWT071HSSMQB`. This is the open-tracking pixel, which isn't content imagery. Alt text is irrelevant there, though an empty `alt=""` is best practice.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
