# 40% Off Everything. Favorites Are Going Fast
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday creative Everlane has run all week. It's the tan jacket and burgundy scarf hero from the 11/24 "Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?" send, with a new subject line. The "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" hero is clear and lands first, and the Women/Men buttons sit right under it. The subject promises urgency ("Favorites Are Going Fast"), but the body doesn't back it up. The only copy is "Still thinking it over?", which is a reminder, not scarcity.
- The body is a standard four-tile category grid (sweaters, outerwear, denim, accessories), then a lifestyle photo and a gift-guide block. It's clean and renders without bugs. But it names no favorites, shows no prices and gives no deadline. The deadline is only in the fine print, "valid until 12/02/2026", and the asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" adds to the sense that the offer is less simple than it looks.
- Verdict: competent and on-brand, but stale. A subscriber who has seen this hero for days has little reason to open it again or click on it.

## What's working

- The hero is large and the 40% off headline is high-contrast, so the offer registers in about a second.
- The black SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN bar sits right under the hero, so the first click is easy.
- The four-tile category grid on a neutral background is easy to scan and covers most of the catalog.
- The burgundy gift-guide block adds seasonal color and a second path to purchase.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject says "Favorites Are Going Fast," but nothing in the body shows a favorite, a low-stock cue or best-sellers. The email doesn't deliver on its own subject.
- The hero is the same creative as earlier sends this week, including 11/24's "Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?", so it looks like fatigue.
- Everything is generic. The tiles show no named products, prices or sale prices, so nothing makes 40% off feel concrete.
- The deadline is buried in gray fine print, so the urgency in the subject has no visible support.
- The lifestyle photo is large, has no CTA and isn't clearly linked to anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the subject. Replace the category grid with 3–4 named best-sellers (Column Jean, Alpaca Cocoon Crew and so on) showing the original price, the 40% price and a "low stock" or "selling fast" tag where it's true.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Dec 2" or "Ends Monday", instead of only in the footer.
- 3. Change the hero creative. After a week of the same image, swap in a product-led hero or add a countdown.
- 4. Cut the unlinked lifestyle photo, or tie it to a CTA, to shorten the scroll.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop 40% Off Sweaters", instead of "Shop Women / Men."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim and outerwear: the pieces everyone is grabbing. Ends Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved styles, 40% off. Sizes are selling out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML template with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The one hard failure is the probe error on the logo link, and it may be a probe artifact. Most compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link ("Everlane")**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8862YDE77H7Q0YQQMP15N21_0` returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...'`.
  - The tracker's redirect resolved to a relative path (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - If the `Location` header really is relative, some clients may not resolve it. That behavior is valid per RFC 7231, but it is unusual for a click tracker.
  - It is more likely the probe client didn't resolve a relative redirect. Re-test with a browser-like client before treating it as a live defect.
- **Coverage gap**: only 12 of 27 material links were probed and 15 were skipped. The 43% pass rate is therefore not representative. No other link failures are confirmed.
- **Open-tracking pixel**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8862YDE77H7Q0YQQMP15N21` has no `alt` attribute. This is a standard Klaviyo pixel, so the practical impact is negligible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`**: the source contains `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Screen readers and some clients show the empty title.
- **No `lang` attribute**: `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`. This is an accessibility gap, and it affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Outlook support**: the MSO conditionals (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`), the `-webkit-text-size-adjust` rules and the `max-width: 480px` media queries are all present. The dark-mode hooks (`[data-ogsc] .ev-clay-dark`) are also present. No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible source.
- **Truncated source**: the rest of the HTML wasn't available, so image `alt` text and button markup couldn't be checked beyond the tracking pixel.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered Klaviyo or Liquid tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|...|*`) in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
These are unconfirmed, not confirmed defects:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)**: neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so presence can't be verified from this data. If they are truly absent, that conflicts with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results`**: not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the expected cause.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address**: the footer was outside the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result isn't shown.
- **Sender**: `support@everlane.com` is the From address on a promotional send. Replies go to a support mailbox, which is an operational concern rather than a compliance failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The logo link resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. These are Klaviyo identity and flow parameters. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the captured URL, though the string is truncated. Check that the destination URLs carry UTMs, or that Klaviyo's UTM-append setting is on, so GA attribution works.
- The subject line promises "40% Off Everything", but the logo link lands on the women's page rather than a sitewide page. That is plausible for a logo link, but it is a minor mismatch. The primary CTA landings weren't in the probed data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8862YDE77H7Q0YQQMP15N21_0` with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. If the redirect is relative in a real browser trace, ask Klaviyo or the sending-domain owner to emit an absolute URL.
2. Get the raw message headers from the original send, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `everlane.com`.
3. Add a meaningful `<title>` and `lang="en"` on `<html>`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. This is low priority.
5. Raise probe coverage for the next audit so the 15 skipped links are checked. Also verify the UTM parameters on the primary CTA URLs.
