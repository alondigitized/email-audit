## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Klaviyo-generated email with two broken tracked links (unresolvable redirect destinations) and no observable compliance/authentication headers via the current relay path.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- Broken redirect: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M38E1CS6T2TBWRPMF8ZB7P03_5` ("Shop Men") resolves to malformed target `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=...&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCK` — QA flags "unknown url type," indicating a truncated/invalid destination URL.
- Broken redirect: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M38E1CS6T2TBWRPMF8ZB7P03_0` ("Everlane Logo") resolves to malformed target `/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i` — same "unknown url type" error, also truncated mid-parameter (`cm_flow_i`).
- Both broken links use Klaviyo click-tracking format (`trk.send.everlane.com/l/<msg_id>_<index>`) with `_kx` and `bxid` tracking params appended to relative paths — the relative path lacks a resolvable host, which is the likely root cause.
- 12 material links were probed, 7 skipped (footer/utility/social deprioritized per QA methodology) — coverage caveat, not a defect.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Missing `alt` text on open/tracking pixel: `src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M38E1CS6T2TBWRPMF8ZB7P03"`. Tracking pixels are typically decorative/1x1 and don't require alt text for screen readers, but flagged per QA output — low impact, verify it's not a content-bearing image mistakenly using this pattern.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@media` fallbacks are present and structured correctly (`.mj-outlook-group-fix`, `mso-table-lspace`, etc.) — no issues found here.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{ }}`, `*|...|*`) in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than absent at send time; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — same caveat; if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirement) is not supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data; this is a relay-visibility gap, not necessarily a sender-side failure.
- Footer unsubscribe link presence: QA notes "an unsubscribe link is always included" in probing methodology, implying one exists in the truncated-out footer, but it was not independently verified in the visible source.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters observed on the two probed destination URLs; instead they use Klaviyo-native `_kx` and `bxid` params. This is consistent with Klaviyo's own analytics tracking, not a defect, but means attribution relies on Klaviyo dashboards rather than GA-style UTM tagging.
- Landing page paths (`/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, `/pages/womens`) appear directionally aligned with link text ("Shop Men," "Everlane Logo" → womens), but cannot confirm final rendered page due to broken redirect resolution (see Section 2).

7. **Recommendations**
- Fix the two broken tracked links by verifying the full destination URL is correctly formed (not truncated) before Klaviyo shortens it — check for URL-encoding or template-variable truncation at `cm_flow_i` and `bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCK`.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually sent at the SMTP layer (not just missing due to AgentMail relay capture) — test with a direct header capture outside the relay.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status via a direct-inbox header check, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured through the relay.
