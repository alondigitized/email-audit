# Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day send for Friends & Family, and it's the cleanest of the week. The hero has the best copy in the series: "Friends & Family Ends Tonight / 30% OFF SITEWIDE" in large white type over a dark, moody crop. The subject, hero and offer all say the same thing. Compared with this morning's "Today Only: An Extra 10% Off", whose subject promised something the render never showed, this one delivers on its subject.
- The body is thin. Eight sweater tiles (cobalt cardigan, taupe crew, cobalt V-neck, brown mock-neck) have no names, no prices and no per-product links I can see. "Our biggest sale of the season is almost over" and "Make your picks before styles are back to full price" both restate the deadline without adding anything. It's the sixth 30% send in four days, so a subscriber is likely fatigued. The urgency is credible but the creative is generic.
- Verdict: a solid closer that will get opens on the "Ends Tonight" deadline. It leaves clicks to the hero button and doesn't give a shopper a reason to pick a product.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "30% OFF SITEWIDE" is the largest element, with the "Ends Tonight" deadline right above it.
- The subject, hero and body agree, so there's no mismatch between promise and render.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars give an obvious CTA, once under the hero and once under the second grid.
- The product grids are well photographed. The model shots alternate with flat lays, and the cobalt and brown palette suits fall.
- The footer fine print gives the exact deadline, "10/13/2026 at 11:59 PM PST", so the urgency reads as honest.

## What's weak

- None of the eight products has a name, price or sale-price callout, so "30% off" never becomes a concrete number.
- The two copy lines, "Our biggest sale of the season is almost over" and "Make your picks before styles are back to full price", repeat the hero and add no information.
- The "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites. BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block is a vague brand line that adds little to the offer.
- Both grids are sweaters only. For a "sitewide" sale, nothing shows men's, denim or accessories, and the footer nav is the only route to them.
- The 30% offer has been repeated all week, and the extra 10% sent earlier today is not mentioned, so this reads as a downgrade.
- The only deadline is "tonight". It doesn't name a time, and the 11:59 PM PST cutoff sits in the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put names and prices on the tiles, for example "Cashmere Crew, $178, now $125", with a link on each tile. This is the biggest lift for click-through, because the shopper gets a concrete reason to choose a product.
- 2. Put the real cutoff in the hero, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT". It sharpens the urgency.
- 3. Replace the two filler lines with one line that does work, such as the extra 10% from earlier today if it still applies, or a best-seller callout.
- 4. Show at least one tile from another category, such as men's, denim or accessories, to back up "sitewide".
- 5. Cut the second "SHOP NOW" bar's grid down to four tiles and let the best-sellers do the closing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart's 30% off disappears tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere, denim and tees all included. Sale ends 11:59 PM PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template (`kl-row`, `kl-column`, `_kx`/`bxid` params) that routes every link through the `trk.send.everlane.com` redirector. QA found one failed link probe (a relative redirect target). The header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) most likely come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link probe error** on the link with text "Everlane", `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4TZ8GNN4XGVTR1R4ERFTKNW_0`. The probe reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect appears to return a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) rather than an absolute URL. That is legal under RFC 7231, and browsers resolve it, but the probe could not.
  - This is probably a probe limitation rather than a user-facing break. I can't confirm the final destination resolves, because the probe never reached it. It should be re-tested with relative-redirect handling.
- 12 links were probed and 17 skipped. The skipped ones are mostly footer and social links, so they are unverified.
- The `/pages/womens` landing path is a generic category page. For a "Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sitewide" email, this one link was the only destination the data shows.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4TZ8GNN4XGVTR1R4ERFTKNW`. This is the open-tracking pixel, not content imagery. It's low severity, but the pixel should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. This check doesn't say whether the content images have alt text.
- **Links have no underline and no color distinction.** The `<style>` block sets `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` all to `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Any inline text link is therefore indistinguishable from body text (WCAG 1.4.1). This matters only if the body contains inline text links, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- **Mobile `h4` size is 10px.** The rule is `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` under `max-width: 480px`, which is below common legibility minimums (about 11–14px).
- `<title>` is empty (`<title> </title>`). This is minor: some clients and the web view use it.
- The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` web font is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, which is handled correctly.
- MSO conditionals and the VML/Office namespaces are present, so Outlook handling is in place.
