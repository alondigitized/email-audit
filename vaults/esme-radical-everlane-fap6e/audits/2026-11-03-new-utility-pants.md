# New Utility Pants
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-idea product launch for new utility pants. The hero is a model in a chocolate-brown barrel-leg pant with "New Utility" beside her. A black SHOP NOW bar sits under it, followed by three alternating image/text rows for the Twill Barrel ($98), Cigarette ($118) and Seamed Wide-Leg ($118). The layout is tidy and the product is easy to understand in seconds.
- It has no reason to act today. There is no offer, no deadline and no member benefit, and the three pants are nearly indistinguishable in the same brown. The "Better Gift Guide" block at the bottom competes with the pants message. After the 11/02 gift send ("The Gifts He'll Actually Wear") and the 11/01 "Merino Standard," this is another product-only send with no urgency. It will get a polite browse, not a purchase.
- Judgement: well executed and on-brand, but low-pressure. It works for a subscriber already shopping pants and does little for anyone else.

## What's working

- The hero is clear and uncluttered. A single model, a single colour story and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar make the eye land on the product first.
- The three rows alternate image and text sides, so the scroll has rhythm, and each row shows a name, price and fit note.
- The copy is specific and sells the product. "Heavier-weight organic cotton, softly brushed" tells you why this is new.
- The fit cues are useful: "slim, straight, ankle-length" versus "high at the waist, generous through the leg."
- Nothing is broken. Images load, text is legible, and there is no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- There is no offer or incentive. The $98 and $118 prices are shown with no reason to buy now.
- The three pants look almost identical in the same brown, and only one colourway is shown. That weakens the "new silhouettes" story and gives no colour choice.
- The "Better Gift Guide" banner pulls attention away from the pants. Its SHOP WOMEN and SHOP MEN links go to generic destinations.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW," including the three per-product links, so none names the product.
- There are no reviews, ratings or size and availability cues to lower the risk of buying new fits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to act now: a deadline, or a perk such as free returns or early access for members. A product launch without an incentive is the biggest gap.
- 2. Show colour range, or at least one non-brown pant, so the three silhouettes read as different choices.
- 3. Add social proof, such as a star rating or "most-tried fit" tag, on the two lead pants.
- 4. Rename the CTAs per product ("Shop the Barrel Pant") and drop the gift guide to a slim footer strip, or send it separately.
- 5. Make the subject name the benefit instead of the category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Utility Pant, Now in Heavier Cotton`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 New Fits Worth Trying On`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brushed organic cotton, three new shapes, from $98.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Barrel, cigarette or wide-leg: find your fit.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>` has no `lang`. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation rules.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. Some clients and assistive tech use it as the document name.
- **Links have no underline:** `a:not([name]){text-decoration:none}` and the matching state rules remove underlines, and `#000` is the same color for every state. Any inline link within body text would be distinguishable only by position, not by style. This is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk. Whether it applies depends on body copy I can't see.
- **Very small mobile heading:** `@media (max-width:480px){ h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` renders h4 at 10px on mobile.
- **External font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is low severity. The fonts are also named inconsistently: `"MaisonNeue-Light"` for h1 and `"Maison Neue"` for h2–h4.
- **Dark mode:** The `.ev-clay-dark` / `.ev-clay-light` swap depends on `[data-ogsc]`, which only Outlook.com and Outlook apps use. Apple Mail and Gmail would need `prefers-color-scheme` rules, which may appear in the truncated remainder.
- The viewport meta tag, `-webkit-text-size-adjust` and the Outlook conditional comments (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present and correct.
