# Extra 15% Off: Closet Upgrades
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's eighth promotional email in six days — and the creative is effectively unchanged from sends on June 26, June 28, and June 29. The hero is the same tight-crop denim shot with "SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE / UP TO 70% OFF SALE STYLES" in white text over it. The one genuinely new creative choice is the price-tier CTA grid (SHOP UNDER $50 / $100 / $150 / SHOP ALL SALE), which is smart segmentation and a real differentiator from the prior runs.
- The critical failure here is a subject/body disconnect. The subject promises "Extra 15% Off" — which is what should compel the open — but open the email and there is zero visible mention of that extra 15%. The hero just repeats the same 70% Off headline from every prior send. A subscriber who opened this because of the extra discount is immediately confused about where it applies and how to claim it.
- At eight sends in six days, Everlane is burning list trust fast. This send is more functional than the June 29 repeat of the June 26 creative, but the missing 15% callout is a near-fatal execution error for an email whose entire subject line promises exactly that.

## What's working

- **Price-tier CTA grid** (SHOP UNDER $50 / $100 / $150 / SHOP ALL SALE) is the strongest new element — it meets subscribers at their budget rather than forcing them to hunt.
- **"UNDER $100" blue panel** with SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN split reinforces the budget framing with a clean, scannable section.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, buttons are visible and labeled.
- **Hero visual hierarchy** — the eye goes straight to the sale callout and SHOP NOW button without distraction.
- **"No matter your budget"** body copy is the most human line Everlane has used in this entire sale run.

## What's weak

- **"Extra 15% Off" from the subject is completely absent from the email body** — the single biggest reason someone would open this send isn't explained, shown, or redeemed anywhere visible.
- **Same hero creative as June 26, 28, and 29** — subscribers are seeing the exact same denim crop + "SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE / UP TO 70% OFF" for the fourth time.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the primary hero CTA** — too generic; should name the extra discount or a specific category.
- **No deadline or urgency signal anywhere** in the body — with a sale that has now run for 6+ days, "ends tonight" or "final hours" would be credible and would help.
- **Cadence fatigue is real**: the June 28 send was "Extra 15% Off: The Best Sale Styles" — this subject is nearly identical. A subscriber who ignored that one will ignore this one too.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the Extra 15% Off the hero, not the footer.** Right now the subject promises the extra discount and the email delivers the same 70% off creative. The extra 15% should be in a prominent banner or a callout block immediately below the hero — "Stack it: extra 15% applies at checkout" — or the subject should not promise it at all.
- 2. **Add a deadline.** "SUMMER CLEAROUT SALE" has been running for a week. A hard "Ends midnight July 4" is credible and would close waverers. No deadline = no urgency.
- 3. **Refresh the hero creative.** The denim crop has run in at least three consecutive sends. Swap to a flat-lay of specific sale products with price tags showing both original and sale price.
- 4. **Change the primary CTA to match the subject.** "SHOP NOW" under the hero is a wasted slot — "SHOP EXTRA 15% OFF STYLES" is specific and pays off the subject promise.
- 5. **Lean into the price-tier grid as the email's lead concept.** Lead with the budget framing ("Pick your price") instead of the generic 70% Off hero. This is the differentiator from every prior send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 15% Off Ends Tonight — Your Budget Pick`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack the Extra 15%: Under $50, $100, $150`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your last chance to grab sale styles with an extra 15% stacked at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by price: under $50, $100, or $150 — all an extra 15% off today only.`
