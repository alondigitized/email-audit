# How We're Wearing Collegium
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Collegium x Everlane send in two days. Earlier today "For All Walks of Life" launched the collab, and "How They're Wearing Everlane" ran the same UGC format. This one is the editorial follow-up. Two named wearers, SoSuper Sam (DJ and content creator) and Garrett Cottle (interior designer), each get a full-bleed look and a pull quote. The photography is strong and the "two distinct voices" angle is a smart way to show versatility.
- The weakness is that it never shows the product. The shoes, which are the collaboration, are small in most frames. Only one tight loafer crop shows footwear clearly. There is no price, no shoe name, and no offer. Both "Shop this style" links go somewhere I can't tell from the image. A reader gets a mood, not a reason to buy.
- Judgement: a handsome, on-brand brand-building email, but it repeats the launch story with no new information. Expect a middling click rate.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. The model, the white G-Wagon and the "COLLEGIUM X EVERLANE" overlay all land at once, and the black SHOP THE COLLECTION bar sits fully above the fold.
- Named wearers with job titles (DJ, interior designer) give the looks credibility and make them feel like real people.
- The pull quotes ("something that keeps up with however the day actually unfolds") sell the use case better than product copy would.
- The layout is easy to scan. Large image, quote, then a single underlined CTA, repeated with no clutter or render bugs.
- The sustainability block and the footer nav are standard Everlane and cost nothing.

## What's weak

- No shoe is named or priced, and no product close-up carries a label. The email sells a collaboration without saying what it costs.
- Two of the three "Shop this style" links sit under photos where the footwear is barely visible, so it's unclear what the click delivers.
- It's the third send about the same collection in two days. This one adds no new product, story or offer beyond the 10-02 launch email.
- "How We're Wearing…" nearly duplicates the same-day "How They're Wearing Everlane" subject pattern, so the sends blur together in the inbox.
- The SHOP THIS STYLE and SHOP THE COLLECTION text links are small and thin compared with the full-width hero bar. The mid-email CTAs are easy to miss on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the shoes front and center. Add a clean product shot with the name and price (for example "The Collegium Loafer, $___") under each wearer, and link each CTA to that exact product.
- 2. Differentiate it from the 10-02 launch send. Lead with something new, such as a limited-quantity or sizing-availability line or a "now in all sizes" message.
- 3. Turn the underlined text links into bordered or black buttons so they match the hero CTA.
- 4. Space out the cadence. Bundle the two Collegium sends or drop one.
- 5. Break the "How We're/They're Wearing" pattern in the subject line.

- **Subject Alt B:** `A DJ and a designer walk into Everlane`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sam wears them to the club, Garrett to the studio. Same shoe.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The limited-edition footwear collab, styled by the people who wear it.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with a Klaviyo-style click-tracking redirect (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The QA run flagged two redirect-resolution failures. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible portion and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect targets (2 FAIL).** The probe got `unknown url type` on:
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3ZKMJYEGJF9XN4J3BHD4VD9_18` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts"). It resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…`.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3ZKMJYEGJF9XN4J3BHD4VD9_0` ("Everlane Logo"). It resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`.
  - Both redirect targets are path-only, with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header per RFC 7231, so real users are probably fine. The probe's HTTP client doesn't resolve it. This is likely a tracker quirk, not a confirmed broken link, but the final destination wasn't verified.
- **Odd destinations.** The logo link points to `/pages/womens`, not the homepage. That is deliberate-looking, so it isn't flagged as a defect.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 28 links were probed and 16 were skipped, so the 38% pass rate is not a reliable measure of overall link health.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is blank, which hurts web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Tracking pixel without alt.** The QA flag for missing alt text is on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M3ZKMJYEGJF9XN4J3BHD4VD9`. That is the open-tracking pixel, so it is not a content image. Adding `alt=""` would still be cleaner for screen readers.
- **Remote font import.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is used with Arial/Helvetica fallbacks. That is acceptable, but `@import` is ignored in Gmail and several other clients.
- **Otherwise sound.** The MSO conditionals, viewport meta, responsive media queries (`max-width: 480px`) and Outlook fixes are in place. Nothing beyond this is confirmed from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing. The QA output itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Treat them as unverified, not as confirmed failures.
- The QA notes an unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but the data doesn't report its result. The physical address in the footer wasn't visible in the truncated source.
- Sender `support@everlane.com` is a support-style address used for marketing. That is not a defect, but it is worth noting.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Visible tracking parameters are Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid`, plus `cm_fl…` (truncated). No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available data. I can't confirm whether they exist further along the truncated URLs.
- The landing pages (`/pages/womens`, `/pages/sms-signup`) are consistent with the link text. The email subject ("How We're Wearing Collegium") points to a collection, and no landing check was run for it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the two failed probes with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers, or follow the redirect manually, to confirm both destinations return 200.
2. Raw-fetch the original `.eml` to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't rely on the relay-stripped copy.
3. Increase probe coverage beyond 12 links, or exclude skipped links from the pass-rate calculation.
4. Treat the empty `<title>` as a low-priority fix in the template, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Check the full, untruncated HTML for `utm_*` parameters and the footer address and unsubscribe link.
