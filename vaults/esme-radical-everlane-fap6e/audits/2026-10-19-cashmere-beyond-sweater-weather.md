# Cashmere Beyond Sweater Weather
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand cashmere send with a real news hook buried in it: "Now at new better prices." The hero is strong, a model in a green cashmere cardigan with pearl buttons and a black SHOP NOW bar. The four-product stack below it is easy to scan. But the price story never becomes a number. There is no "$X, was $Y", no percentage, and no price on any product. The closing "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block asserts a deal without proving it.
- Against recent sends, this is a step up in intent. "New October Arrivals" (10/19) and "New Libra Season Approved Styles" (10/18) were near-identical product grids. This one at least has a single theme (cashmere) and a reason to care (price). It still follows the same template as those sends, though: a hero, a stacked module list and a generic SHOP NOW on every block. Four of the five CTAs are identical, so the email gives no sense of where to start.
- Verdict: a decent, on-season send that hints at a price drop but never states it. About a 6, the same band as most of Everlane's recent batch.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, tactile model shot. The green cardigan reads as cashmere and stands out in an inbox full of neutrals.
- The single theme, cashmere, is clear from the first screen. It is a better organizing idea than the generic grids of 10/18 and 10/19.
- "Now at new better prices" is a real hook for anyone who has looked at Everlane cashmere and balked at the price.
- Each product module has a name, a one-line fit or feel description and its own link. The copy is useful ("a slightly dropped shoulder and elongated ribbed hems").
- There are no render bugs. Images load, text is legible and the layout is consistent.

## What's weak

- No price appears anywhere. "Better prices" with no number is the biggest miss, since the price is the whole news.
- The "Nothing Better Than Cashmere" overlay is thin white text on a busy, mid-tone image. It is hard to read at inbox size.
- All five CTAs say SHOP NOW. None names a product or the price drop, and the four module links are small underlined text.
- The product tiles show no reviews, ratings, colors or sizing, and there is no hero product. It reads as a catalog with no proof.
- The beige "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block is the payoff, but it sits at the bottom and has no number. The footer nav and utility links add length with little value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price in the hero and the subject. For example, "Cashmere, now from $98" or "Cashmere, $40 less". Show the old and new price on every product tile.
- 2. Move the "Better Prices" block up under the hero and give it the number. Keep the "same quality, same materials" line, since it answers the obvious objection.
- 3. Make the hero text readable. Add a dark scrim or move the headline into a solid band.
- 4. Replace the generic CTAs with specific ones, such as "Shop the Cashmere Polo" or "Shop cashmere from $98".
- 5. Add one proof point: a star rating or a "most-reviewed" tag on the best seller, as the 10/18 "The Reviews Are In" send did.
- 6. Cut the module stack from four items to three, and make the polo or the cardigan the featured hero product.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Everlane cashmere just got cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same cashmere, new prices. Polos, cardigans and crews from $XX.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our softest layers, repriced. See what changed.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) send through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The QA run flagged two link failures. Both look like a probe limitation rather than a confirmed customer-facing break. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the capture path, and the HTML provided was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…` (link text "Everlane")**: the tracked URL `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5ADFCX9DCX019KHPRG7T0G1_0` returned `unknown url type`. Its redirect target is a relative path with no scheme or host.
- **[FAIL] `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…` (link text "Icon | Sign Up for Texts")**: same failure on `…G1_18`, same cause.
- **What these errors likely mean**: the tracker answers with a relative `Location` header, and the probe's HTTP client can't resolve it. Browsers resolve relative redirects against the tracker host, so real users probably land fine. But if the tracker host serves relative paths, those paths would resolve to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is likely a 404. I can't tell which from the data. Re-test with a browser-grade client that follows redirects, and check the raw `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage**: only 12 of 28 links were probed, and 16 were skipped (footer, social and utility links). The pass rate is 38%, and the two failures are on the highest-priority links, the header logo and the SMS signup.
- **Link styling**: the `<style>` block forces `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:hover` and `a:active` to `#000` with no underline. This isn't a bug, but it removes the default link affordance. That matters for the accessibility notes below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text** on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5ADFCX9DCX019KHPRG7T0G1`. This is the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip it. Low severity.
- The `<title>` is a single space (`<title> </title>`). Some clients and screen readers announce this as untitled.
- Link text such as "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" includes the literal word "Icon", which looks like an alt/label leak from an image-based link. Screen readers will read it aloud.
- The `.hide` rule in the `@media screen and (min-device-width: 10px)…` block was cut off in the source I was given, so I can't assess it.
- The remaining body HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm alt coverage on content images. The QA tool reported only the one missing alt.
- No other issues are confirmed. The viewport meta, `X-UA-Compatible`, MSO conditionals, `mso-hide` and dark-mode `[data-ogsc]` handling are present and look standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`). The `_kx` and `bxid` parameters are Klaviyo's per-recipient identifiers, rendered correctly.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers, so I can't confirm they're absent from the original send. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against a direct-to-mailbox copy.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `support@everlane.com`, while links go through `send.everlane.com`. A subdomain/organizational-domain mismatch is normal, but DKIM alignment needs checking.
- The sender is a `support@` address. If it is monitored, that is fine. I can't see the footer in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the physical postal address or an in-body unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination links carry Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_fl…` (flow ID) parameters. The `cm_fl…` value is cut off in the QA output, so I couldn't read it.
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the probed URLs. They may be appended in the part of each URL that was cut off, or attribution may rely on Klaviyo's `_kx`. Confirm the full destination URLs.
