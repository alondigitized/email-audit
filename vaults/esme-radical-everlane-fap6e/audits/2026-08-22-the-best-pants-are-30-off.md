# The Best Pants Are 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's cleanest, most product-confident send this week — a single striking hero shot (cream trousers hung on a weathered wood door), bold "30% OFF BEST-SELLERS" overlay, and a black "SHOP NOW" button, no scroll needed. It's a step up from 8/21's "30% Off Jeans" and a big step up from 8/20's flat mint-block send with no product photography at all.
- Where it stops short: the subject promises "the best pants," but the six-item grid below the hero mixes a skirt, trousers, and denim — not a pants-only edit — so the subject overpromises specificity it doesn't quite deliver. It also arrives the same week as 8/18's "Way-High Denim" and 8/19's "Linen Pairings," both bottoms-adjacent, so a subscriber opening five of these in a row would notice the category is on repeat even if the discount number stays flat at 30%.
- Solid, sellable creative — best hero image of the week — dragged down by subject/content mismatch and a discount that's now the fourth "30% off" framing in six days.

## What's working

- Hero photography is genuinely arresting — natural light, textured wood door, styled hang shot — the strongest single image in this brand's recent history.
- Offer is unmissable: large white "30% OFF BEST-SELLERS" text sits directly over the product, first thing the eye hits.
- Single unambiguous CTA — black "SHOP NOW" button, high contrast, impossible to miss.
- Clean grid of six shoppable items gives immediate next steps without overwhelming.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is legible, spacing is even throughout.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Best Pants" but the grid includes a skirt (top-left) — a literal mismatch between promise and payoff.
- No price anchor anywhere (no "$XX" or "was/now") — just a percentage, so there's no sense of actual savings.
- Secondary copy ("Your favorites are on sale — for a limited time only") is generic filler; doesn't name a deadline, unlike the fine print's actual 8/24 cutoff.
- Fourth denim/bottoms-centric send in six days (8/18, 8/19, 8/20, 8/21) — creative variety is thinning even though today's hero image is strong.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/content mismatch first — either swap the hero product to the actual best-selling pant and keep the grid pants-only, or retitle to match the mixed grid ("Bottoms You'll Actually Wear, 30% Off").
- 2. Add a real price example near the hero or in the grid captions ("$98 → $68") — percentage-only offers under-convert versus a visible dollar anchor.
- 3. Surface the 8/24 deadline in the visible body copy, not just the gray legal footer — urgency currently only lives in fine print nobody reads.
- 4. Vary next send's category away from denim/trousers — five bottoms-led sends in six days risks fatigue; pivot to tops, outerwear, or a full-outfit story.
