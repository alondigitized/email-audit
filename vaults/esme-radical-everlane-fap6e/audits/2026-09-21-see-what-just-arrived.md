## What's working

- Clean hero shot with clear "New Arrivals" overlay and a single unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button.
- Product grid is well-organized (9 SKUs, consistent crop/lighting) and scannable at a glance.
- "Extra 15% Off Sale Styles" banner has clear copy, a real discount, and two segmented CTAs (Shop Women / Shop Men).
- No personalization bug this time — a genuine fix versus the last several sends.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- This is a visual repeat of the 09-20 "See What Just Arrived" send — same hero model, same tank, same grid concept, same banner — with no distinguishing new content.
- Subject "See What Just Arrived" is generic and was already used; no specificity on what's actually new (no product names, no count, no category callout).
- Grid skews heavily monochrome/jewel-tone (black, maroon, green, navy) — no visual signal of broader seasonal range.
- Sustainability CTA ("READ OUR LATEST IMPACT REPORT") is disconnected from the shopping intent of the email and pushes users off the purchase path.
- No urgency, no loyalty/member callout, no size or fit detail visible for any product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't re-send the same creative under the same subject line within a week — differentiate hero product and grid selection, or hold the send if there's nothing new to show.
- 2. Add specificity to the subject: name a category or count of new items instead of generic "just arrived."
- 3. Move or cut the impact-report CTA — it's a distraction from a purely transactional email; save sustainability messaging for a dedicated send.
- 4. Add a size/fit or "back in stock" detail line under at least the hero product to give click-through a concrete reason.
- 5. Vary grid colorways/categories more (add lighter neutrals, not just black/jewel tones) to visually signal "new" rather than "same."
