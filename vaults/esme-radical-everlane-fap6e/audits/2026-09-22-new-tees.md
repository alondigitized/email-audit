# New Tees
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same Forever Jersey Tees send Everlane already ran on 09-21 ("Meet the Forever Jersey Tees") — same driving-with-the-top-down hero, same four-SKU grid (Shrunken Crew, Cropped Tee, Oversized Tee, Boxy Long-Sleeve Crew), same organic-cotton callout, same "Extra 15% Off Sale Styles" banner. The only visible change is the subject line has been flattened from a specific product name to the generic "New Tees."
- Execution is clean — no render bugs, clear hierarchy, legible product grid, consistent Everlane editorial voice. But this is a rerun with a weaker subject than the version it's copying, which is a step backward, not a new send.
- Judgement: fine as a resend/reminder touch, but if this is meant to be a distinct campaign it isn't one — and the subject downgrade actively hurts open rate versus the prior version.

## What's working

- Hero image is well-composed and on-brand — feels like a lifestyle shot, not a catalog shot.
- Product grid is scannable: name, price, one-line description, and a "SHOP NOW" link repeated per item.
- Organic cotton sourcing block adds a credible sustainability angle with a specific claim, not vague greenwashing.
- "Extra 15% Off Sale Styles" banner is visually distinct (olive block) and breaks up the page.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of the 09-21 "Meet the Forever Jersey Tees" send — same hero, same four products, same banner, same footer — sent five days later with a genericized subject.
- Subject "New Tees" is vaguer than the prior send's subject and gives no reason to open now versus any other week.
- No urgency or timing signal anywhere in the email (the 15% banner doesn't state a deadline in the visible creative, though fine print at the bottom has an expiration date buried in tiny gray text).
- No loyalty/member pricing or personalization visible despite this being a repeat subscriber touch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the identical creative — if the list overlaps with 09-21 recipients, this reads as an accidental duplicate rather than a fresh idea. Either hold this one or swap in different hero art/copy.
- 2. Restore subject specificity — "New Tees" is a downgrade from "Meet the Forever Jersey Tees"; name the product or the material story.
- 3. Surface the sale deadline in the banner itself (large type), not just in the fine print.
- 4. Add a size/fit callout near the grid (Everlane's own copy already talks fit — pull "true to size" or a fit-guide link into the email body).
