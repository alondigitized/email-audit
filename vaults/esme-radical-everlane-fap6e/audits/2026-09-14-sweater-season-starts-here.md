## What's weak

- Zero visible discount, price, or urgency — after a week of "extra 25%" emails, a full-price editorial send may feel like a letdown or a non-event.
- Primary CTA is generic navigation (Shop Women/Men) rather than a specific "Shop Sweaters" link tied to the product grid actually shown.
- The impact-report block and "READ OUR LATEST IMPACT REPORT" CTA compete with the shopping CTAs for the strongest button treatment on the page — it's the only impact/loyalty-style content, and it isn't what this email is nominally about.

- Product grid images repeat the same flat-lay sweater-on-white treatment for all six — visually monotonous compared to the two lifestyle shots below.

- 2. Make "Shop Sweaters" (not "Shop Women"/"Shop Men") the primary CTA directly under the hero, linking to the exact grid shown — right now the CTA and the content underneath aren't tightly coupled.
- 3. Swap the impact-report CTA button styling to something lighter so it doesn't visually compete with the shopping intent — it currently gets the same bold black-button treatment as a purchase CTA.
- 4. Label at least the hero sweater with fabric (alpaca/cashmere/wool) directly on the image or caption — the copy mentions "Alpaca to fine cashmere" but nothing ties a specific product to a specific material.
