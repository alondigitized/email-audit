# 30% Off Sweaters & More Starts Today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine step up from the wall-of-text disasters Everlane sent on 09-22 and 09-23 under nearly the same "Extra 15% Off New Markdowns" subject — there's an actual hero image, a real offer banner, and a merchandised 9-SKU product grid this time. The neutral-toned knitwear stack photography is on-brand and seasonally appropriate for late September, and the layout hierarchy (hero offer → grid → lifestyle shot → footer nav) is clean and renders without bugs.
- That said, it's still a fairly generic sweater sale that leans entirely on the discount and the product photography to do the work — no urgency deadline visible on-screen, no pricing shown anywhere (not even a strikethrough), and the copy is minimal to the point of being forgettable ("Stock up while you can."). Compared to the Collegium collab send from 09-23, this reads like a standard seasonal promo, not a standout.
- Solid, competent, mid-pack send — worth opening for the sweater category, but nothing here would make me click faster than any other 30%-off email in my inbox.

## What's working

- Clear hero banner with legible "30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" copy over on-brand, cozy knitwear photography.
- Unambiguous single black "SHOP NOW" CTA button directly under the hero.
- 9-product grid gives real texture/style variety (crew, turtleneck, quarter-zip, cropped) so shoppers can pre-browse before clicking.
- Lifestyle shot reinforces how the sweater is worn, adding styling context.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- No visible price or strikethrough anywhere — the 30% is abstract with no anchor to what things actually cost.
- No urgency/deadline shown in the render itself (the expiration is buried in fine print at the bottom, not in the body).
- Copy is thin ("For a limited time..." / "Stock up while you can.") — doesn't differentiate this from any other neutrals sale Everlane has run.
- Product grid has no labels, prices, or individual links visible — just a flat photo wall.
- This is the second 30%-ish-off knitwear-adjacent broad discount email in a short run, following the two "Extra 15% Off" sends — cadence is starting to feel discount-heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible countdown or explicit end date in the hero ("Ends 9/28") — the deadline exists (per the fine print) but isn't doing any persuasive work because it's invisible until the footer.
- 2. Overlay price or "from $XX" on at least the top row of the product grid to make the click decision concrete.
- 3. Swap the generic "Stock up while you can." line for something that names the actual audience benefit (fit, material, versatility) rather than pure urgency filler.
- 4. Differentiate this from the back-to-back 15%-off sends — either escalate the offer or explicitly frame it as a distinct "sweater event" so it doesn't read as the same promo recycled a third time.
