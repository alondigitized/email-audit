# 30% Off Better Cashmere
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand send with a weak creative-to-subject match. The subject promises "Better Cashmere", but the hero is a black-and-white model in a puffer jacket. The cashmere only shows up in a small "Including Cashmere" line under "30% Off Sitewide". A reader who opened for sweaters has to scroll to the six-tile grid to see what the subject meant.
- The cadence is the bigger problem. This is the eighth or so 30% Friends & Family send in about a week, after "A Classic Now 30% Off" and "30% Off Cashmere Worth Keeping" earlier on 10/10. The creative is a close cousin of those too. The "Ends 10.13" deadline is real, but it has been implied for days and has lost its edge.
- Judgement: competent and readable, but it does nothing the last two cashmere sends didn't. A subscriber would see it as more of the same.

## What's working

- The offer is unmistakable: "30% Off Sitewide" and "Friends & Family Ends 10.13" sit on the hero with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath.
- The subject is short and concrete, and it ties to the cashmere push the brand has been running.
- The beige "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" band reframes the sale around the brand's value story and adds a second CTA.
- The six-tile product grid is clean, with no broken images. Flat-lay and on-model shots are paired, which helps judge fit.
- The "Good Cashmere Standard®-certified" copy gives a quality reason to buy.

## What's weak

- The hero is a puffer jacket, not cashmere, so it doesn't match the subject.
- The white hero text sits on a busy grey image. "Including Cashmere" lands on the jacket and is hard to read.
- The grid has no product names, prices, or visible per-item links. There is no "was/now" price and no sign of which styles are cashmere.
- Both CTAs read "SHOP NOW", which is generic. The footer fine print says "select styles", which undercuts "Sitewide".
- No reviews or social proof appear anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a cashmere product hero, such as a sweater close-up, and put "30% Off Cashmere" in a high-contrast block outside the photo.
- 2. Label each grid tile with a name, a was/now price, and a link to that product page, so each tile can be clicked on its own.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Cashmere Sweaters" and "Shop 30% Off Cashmere".
- 4. Add a last-days line such as "Ends Monday 11:59 PM PT" to make the urgency credible after a week of repeats.
- 5. Space the Friends & Family sends out, or rotate the angle (fit guide, a reviews pull-quote) so the 30% doesn't read as the same email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cashmere Size Is Still Here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Certified cashmere crews, V-necks and polos. Friends & Family ends 10.13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same sweaters, 30% less. Last 3 days.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built message sent through Everlane's click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The QA run flagged one failed link probe, missing alt text on the tracking pixel, and three header warnings. The header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, and the failed probe looks like a probe limitation, so none of them is confirmed as a real defect. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body links could not be inspected directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failed probe on the logo link (QA FAIL):**
  - Link text is "Everlane Logo" and the URL is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4M6WVMC5SKVBXPF1YSGG1GW_0`.
  - The error is `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker redirected to a relative path with no scheme or host. Browsers and mail clients resolve relative `Location` headers per RFC 7231, so real users probably land on `/pages/womens`. The probe's HTTP client cannot resolve them.
  - Treat this as a probe limitation rather than a confirmed broken link. It is still non-ideal that the tracker emits a relative redirect.
  - A manual check should confirm the final URL resolves to `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens`.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 27 material links were probed (15 skipped, per the QA note), so link health for the rest is unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4M6WVMC5SKVBXPF1YSGG1GW` is a standard open-tracking pixel. No issue beyond the alt text noted below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor, but it affects web-view rendering and accessibility tooling.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged the image with src `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4M6WVMC5SKVBXPF1YSGG1GW`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Use `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Small mobile type:** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important } }` renders `h4` text at 10px on mobile, which is below common legibility guidance (about 14px or more). This matters only if `h4` carries meaningful copy.
- **Fine as written:**
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - MSO conditionals and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present.
  - The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica.
  - Link colors are forced via `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:hover` and `a:active`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible portion of the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN):** the QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers. It is unconfirmed, so don't count it against the sender yet. Verify against the raw message in a seed inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Check the original headers in Gmail's "Show original".
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in probing. The source and QA data provided don't show that link's URL or probe result, and the footer fell in the truncated portion, so neither can be confirmed here.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as the marketing sender. This is a deliverability and reply-handling consideration, not a compliance violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters on the logo link:** the redirect carries Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible, though the value is cut off and the HTML is truncated.
- **Unconfirmed:** whether the CTA links carry UTMs. The sub-30-character `cm_flow_i…` suffix suggests campaign identifiers may be present, but this can't be verified.
- **Landing page:** the logo link goes to `/pages/womens`, a general women's landing page. The CTA destinations weren't visible, so alignment with the "30% Off Better Cashmere" offer couldn't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4M6WVMC5SKVBXPF1YSGG1GW_0` and confirm it resolves to an absolute `everlane.com` URL. If the tracker emits only a relative `Location`, ask the ESP to emit absolute redirects.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't conclude anything from the relay-stripped copy.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
4. Set `alt=""` (plus `role="presentation"`) on the tracking pixel.
5. Raise the mobile `h4` size from 10px to 14px or more if it carries readable content.
6. Re-run QA with the full HTML and a higher probe budget. That would cover the 15 skipped links, the unsubscribe URL and postal address, and UTM presence on the CTAs.
