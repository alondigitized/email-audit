# Today Only: Extra 25% Off Sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from the watercolor-tan-dress-model template Everlane has run daily since 08/01 — a close-up white peony/tulip photo replaces the model shot entirely, and the offer stacks to the deepest discount of the whole cycle: extra 25% on top of up to 70% off, "Today Only." That escalation is real news for a subscriber who's seen "extra 20%" four times this week.
- But the execution undercuts the urgency. There's no product visible anywhere in the email — just a flower, a black SHOP NOW bar, and four bare text nav links (Shop Women/Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers). A "deepest discount" send with zero merchandise shown is a missed opportunity; this reads more like a generic seasonal mood board than a sale email.
- Net: strong offer, weak execution. The subject and hero copy alone would pull me to open, but the body gives me nothing to click toward.

## What's working

- Genuine template break from the daily watercolor/tan-model creative — visually fresh after a week of near-identical sends.
- Clear, single, unambiguous offer stated twice (hero + body copy): extra 25% off, stacked on already-up-to-70%-off sale.
- Explicit "Today Only" urgency with a hard deadline in the fine print (9/08/2026 11:59 PM PST).
- Clean visual hierarchy — headline splits left/right over the image, black CTA bar directly below, no clutter.
- No render bugs — image loads cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- No product shown at all — for the single deepest-discount day of the sale, there's not one item, price, or "sale styles" tile.
- Generic nav-link block (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) does nothing to reinforce the sale — Best-Sellers and New Arrivals aren't even part of the discount.
- The abstract floral hero, while a nice break, has zero connection to "sale," "fall," or the brand's product — could be any brand's email.
- Single CTA repetition ("SHOP NOW") gives no reason to click beyond the discount itself — no category-specific path (e.g., "Shop Women's Sale," "Shop Men's Sale").

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a product strip below the hero — 3-4 sale items with strikethrough pricing showing the stacked 25%-off math in action; this is the single highest-impact fix since "deepest discount of the sale" currently has nothing to prove it.
- 2. Split the CTA by gender/category ("Shop Women's Sale" / "Shop Men's Sale") instead of generic nav links, so the urgency carries through to the click.
- 3. Tie the hero image to product — even a styled shot of the flower with a garment, or swap for last week's model-in-tan-dress but with a "70% + 25%" badge, to keep brand recognition while keeping the discount escalation visible.
- 4. Move the countdown/deadline ("ends tonight 11:59 PM PST") out of the fine print and into visible hero or body copy — it's the strongest urgency lever and it's buried.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off, plus an extra 25% — gone at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The biggest markdown of the sale ends tonight.`
