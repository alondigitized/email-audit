# Extra 20% Off Best-Sellers
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "End of Summer Sale" beach-hero template from 08/01, just re-headlined for a deeper discount — "Extra 20% Off Sale Styles (Already Up to 70% Off)" replaces yesterday's plain "End of Summer Sale Starts Now." It's a clean, fully-rendered send: legible hero copy over a beach photo, two "SHOP NOW" CTAs, and a tidy 3x3 best-sellers grid (blazers, trousers, cardigans, a striped tee) with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The offer is genuinely strong on paper — stacking an extra 20% on top of up to 70% off is the kind of escalation that should move a subscriber who's been sitting on the fence all week. But the execution undersells it: no product names, no prices, no visible discounted price anywhere in the grid, and the body copy ("Our most sought-after styles are now marked down. Consider this your sign.") is generic filler that does nothing to reinforce the stacking-discount math from the hero.
- Given the brand history, this is a competent, on-brand send that beats the broken plain-text templates but doesn't clear the bar the "End of Summer Sale Starts Now" send (06.00/10) set — it's essentially the same asset with better headline math and no new visual work.

## What's working

- Hero headline math is easy to parse at a glance: "Extra 20% Off" stacked over "(Already Up to 70% Off)" communicates real savings depth in two lines.
- Full-bleed beach photography gives the email a premium, seasonal feel that fits early September.
- Two CTAs (top and after the grid) mean the shopper doesn't have to scroll back up to act.
- Product grid is diverse across categories (tops, pants, knitwear, outerwear-adjacent shirting) which broadens appeal within the "best-sellers" theme.
- Footer offer terms are visible and specific (expiration date/time, exclusions) — no buried fine print hidden off-screen.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or product names anywhere — the grid is nine unlabeled thumbnails, so there's no visible dollar amount to anchor the "70% off" claim.
- Subject says "Best-Sellers" but the hero banner says "Sale Styles" — the email doesn't actually confirm these are best-sellers, just generic sale product.
- Body copy between hero and grid is throwaway ("Consider this your sign.") and wastes an opportunity to spell out how the extra 20% applies (auto at checkout? code needed?).
- No urgency/countdown visible in the body itself — the deadline (09/08) only appears in tiny footer legal text, easy to miss on a skim.
- Visually near-identical to the 08/01 "End of Summer Sale Starts Now" send — same hero crop, same layout, same CTA style — which will read as repetitive to anyone who opened that one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices under each grid product (or at least a "Now $X" badge) — the single biggest lever here, since the entire pitch is a discount and there's no dollar evidence anywhere in the body.
- 2. State whether the extra 20% is automatic or needs a code, directly under the hero or in the body copy — ambiguity here is a common click-killer.
- 3. Swap the generic body line for something that reinforces the stack: e.g. "Already marked down up to 70% — take an extra 20% off at checkout."
- 4. Refresh the hero photo/crop from the 08/01 send so repeat openers don't dismiss it as a duplicate.
- 5. Move the 09/08 deadline out of the footer and into the hero or body as a visible countdown line.
