# Gifts For Him Starting at $50
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, gift-framed men's send, and it earns its relevance from the subject's "$50" price anchor. The hero is a grey cable-knit crewneck on snow with "Better Gifts For Him" in large thin white type and a maroon "SHOP NOW" bar. Two more modules follow: a navy polo in a gift box with a ribbon, and two folded tees. Each module has its own "Shop Now" button.
- The email doesn't deliver on its own subject. "Starting at $50" never appears as a price in the body. The only price cues are two small labels, "Gifts Under $100" and "Gifts Under $50", set in small black type under the images. There is no product name, price tag or hero product. The sweater in the hero is the most appealing item, but it isn't tied to a price or a specific link. Three identical "Shop Now" buttons and a fourth "Shop the Gift Guide" bar compete for the same click.
- Compared with the 11/13 "Everyone Deserves This Half-Zip" send (7/10), this one is weaker. That send named one product and had a specific CTA. This one is a gift-guide sampler with no products named. It reads as seasonal and well made, but it doesn't give anyone a reason to click.

## What's working

- The subject has a concrete price anchor ($50) and a clear audience (him).
- The hero is strong. The sweater image is warm and giftable, and the headline is large and readable.
- The gift-box and ribbon imagery in the second module fits mid-November gifting.
- The "Gifts Under $100" and "Gifts Under $50" tiers give shoppers a budget-based way in.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan, with no render bugs, broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The $50 promise in the subject isn't visible in the body, and no product shows a price.
- The "Gifts Under $50" label and the headline "Better Gifts For Him" are both small or generic, and the white copy overlaid on the grey photos has low contrast.
- The three "Shop Now" buttons are identical and don't say what they lead to ("Shop Now" under "Gifts Under $50").
- No reviews, product names, sizes or colors appear anywhere, so there's no product detail to act on.
- "Certified materials made to last" appears twice, which wastes space.
- The tone is promotional but there is no deadline, shipping cutoff or holiday date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real product and price in the hero, for example the $68 Cashmere Slim Crew or a $50 tee, with a name and price under each image.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Gifts Under $50", "Shop Gifts Under $100" and "Shop Sweaters".
- 3. Add a deadline such as a holiday shipping date, since the send is for gifting and nothing signals urgency.
- 4. Replace the repeated "Certified materials" line with a review quote or a fit note.
- 5. Merge the three buttons and the gift-guide bar into one primary CTA and one secondary one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts for him, from $50`
- **Subject Alt B:** `His gift, under $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tees, polos and sweaters he'll actually wear. Shop by budget.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, Klaviyo-tracked HTML template. All three probed links failed, but the error text points to a probe-side redirect-resolution problem rather than confirmed dead links. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay did not expose them, and the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 of 3 probed material links failed** with `unknown url type: '/pages/...'`:
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7DFEGSQ4CX8201WJDM5CSB5_13` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") resolves to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…`
  - `…_0` ("Everlane Logo") resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…`
  - `…_9` ("Shop Men") resolves to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect returns a path-only value (`/pages/...`) with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the request URL, so real users probably land fine. The probe client does not, which is why it reports "unknown url type". This is **unconfirmed**. Either the tracker emits relative redirects, which some strict clients and security scanners may mishandle, or the QA probe is at fault.
- **Coverage gap:** only 1 of 12 material links was probed, and the QA output reports 11 as skipped. The 33% pass rate is therefore based on a small sample.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7DFEGSQ4CX8201WJDM5CSB5` is the open-tracking image. It is a normal first-party tracking domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the open-tracking pixel above has no `alt`. This is low severity because it is a 1×1 tracking image, but `alt=""` is the correct attribute for it.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Outlook handling:** the template has MSO conditionals, `mso-table-lspace/rspace`, `mj-outlook-group-fix` and a `#outlook a` rule.
- **Mobile:** the 480px and 600px media queries are present.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/...custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Fallbacks (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue"`) are declared, so it degrades safely.
- **Link styling:** the global `a:link { text-decoration:none }` rules remove link underlines. Links then depend on other visual cues, which is a minor accessibility concern. I can't confirm anything beyond this because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or the QA data. The `_kx` and `bxid` values are Klaviyo's per-recipient tracking identifiers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping or not capturing these. Treat this as **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Everlane's sender, Klaviyo, normally adds both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. Check the raw `.eml` or a direct-to-Gmail copy.
- **Footer not inspected.** The HTML is truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address could not be checked. The QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the failures.
- **Sender mismatch:** the `From` address is `support@everlane.com` while the tracking domain is `send.everlane.com`. This is normal, but DMARC alignment should be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters:** the visible redirect targets carry `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_fl…` (apparently `cm_flow_i…`, truncated). These are Klaviyo and flow identifiers. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the truncated strings, so GA attribution can't be confirmed either way.
- **Logo destination:** the "Everlane Logo" link resolves to `/pages/womens`. In an email titled "Gifts For Him", the logo lands on the women's page. This is a real continuity mismatch. It could be intentional site-wide logo behaviour, but it's worth checking.
- **Men's CTA:** the "Shop Men" CTA correctly resolves to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`.
- **Offer landing page:** the "Starting at $50" landing page could not be checked because the probe failed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the probe or the tracker.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host in the QA probe, then re-run. If a `curl -I` of `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…_9` confirms the tracker returns path-only redirects, flag that to the sender as an interoperability risk.
2. **Raise probe coverage** above 1 of 12 links, especially the primary "Gifts For Him" CTAs.
3. **Capture raw headers.** Get `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the original message rather than the relay copy, then re-score compliance.
4. **Store the full HTML** so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and UTM parameters can be audited. The current source is truncated.
5. **Treat the sender-side findings as low priority.** These are the missing `alt` on the tracking pixel, the blank `<title>`, and the logo linking to `/pages/womens` in a men's-gifting send.
