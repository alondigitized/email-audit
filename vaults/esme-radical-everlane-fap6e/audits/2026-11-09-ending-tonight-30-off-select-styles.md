# Ending Tonight: 30% Off Select Styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day send of Everlane's Black Friday preview, and it is the plainest one yet. The hero is a dark, abstract shot of slatted light with no model and no product. White type reads "ENDS TONIGHT / BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW EVENT / 30% OFF SELECT STYLES", with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar underneath. The offer and deadline are clear, and the footer states the cutoff as 11:59 PM PST on 11/09. The creative itself is flat.
- The four-tile grid (sweater, blazer, jeans, scarf) is tidy but generic. The tile labels are "Shop Sweaters", "Shop Outerwear", "Shop Denim" and "Shop All". The "select styles" wording is vague. Compared with "30% Off Your Next Layer" on 11/08, which named outerwear and denim in the hero, this one tells the reader less about what is discounted.
- Verdict: a competent last-call reminder that leans on urgency instead of product. It will convert people who already intend to buy, but it adds little for anyone else. It is also at least the sixth 30% Black Friday preview email in about five days, so it will blur into the others.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" is stated twice, in the subject and the hero, and the 11:59 PM PST deadline in the footer backs it up.
- The offer is unmistakable: "30% OFF" is the largest type in the hero, and the SHOP NOW bar sits directly beneath it.
- The hierarchy is clean. The eye goes to the offer first, then the copy line, then the grid.
- The category tiles are well shot and easy to scan, and they cover sweaters, outerwear and denim.
- Nothing is visibly broken in the render.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product or model, so it feels cold and gives no reason to click beyond the discount.
- "Select styles" is vague, and the email never says which items are excluded.
- The tile CTAs are weak. "Shop All" sits under a scarf, which is a mismatch, and no product names, prices or sale prices appear.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and no member benefits.
- The body copy ("luxurious sweaters to cleaner denim") is generic, and the fine print is small and light grey.
- The nav and footer take up a lot of space after a short body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a product or model in the hero so the dark abstract image isn't carrying the email alone. Use a best-selling sweater or jean with "was/now" pricing.
- 2. Replace "select styles" with the real categories ("30% off sweaters, outerwear & denim") and say what is excluded.
- 3. Show sale prices on the tiles. Retitle "Shop All" as "Shop All 30% Off".
- 4. Add one proof point, such as a star rating or "best-seller" badge, and a countdown or a specific hour ("Ends 11:59 PM PT").
- 5. Shorten the nav block and move the fine print up, so the offer feels honest.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup) with every link wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The QA run couldn't verify link destinations, authentication, or unsubscribe headers, so most findings are unconfirmed rather than failures. Only 57% of checks passed, with 0 errors and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** The 4 probed CTAs returned HTTP 429 from the tracking host. This is rate limiting by the click tracker, not a broken link. The final landing pages were never resolved.
  - `.../l/01M70KER5445DNDQVAKG0XFYCW_2` ("Shop Now")
  - `..._3` ("Image | Shop Sweaters")
  - `..._4` ("Image | Shop Outerwear")
  - `..._5` ("Image | Shop Denim")
- **Incomplete coverage.** The probe stopped after 70s on its time budget. Those links are neither pass nor fail, and they include `_0`, `_1`, `_6`, `_7` and `_12`.
- **QA output is inconsistent.** It reports 8 unprobed links but lists only 5 URLs, and the "12 probed, 10 skipped" counts don't reconcile with that. The probe report itself is unreliable.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M70KER5445DNDQVAKG0XFYCW` is the open-tracking pixel. It is expected, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel** (`/o/01M70K...`). A decorative or tracking image should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips it cleanly. This is low severity.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Possible redundant link text.** The probe labels three links "Image | Shop Sweaters/Outerwear/Denim". If that comes from the `alt` attributes, the literal "Image |" prefix is noise for screen readers. I couldn't confirm this from the truncated HTML.
- **Web font import.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by several clients, such as Gmail. The font stacks include Arial/Helvetica fallbacks, so the impact is minimal.
- No other rendering issues are visible. Outlook conditionals (`mso`, `OfficeDocumentSettings`) and the viewport meta are present and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|...|*`, `{%`) appear in the available HTML. The source was truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are **unknown**. The headers are likely stripped by the relay, so this is not evidence of failure.
- **Not assessable.** An unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated HTML. The probe's "always includes unsubscribe" rule didn't surface one in the output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All hrefs are opaque tracker redirects, and no destination URL, UTM parameters, or landing page was resolved because of the 429s and the time-budget cutoff. UTM tagging is probably appended server-side by Klaviyo, but this audit has no evidence either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probing with throttling or retry/backoff on 429s and a larger time budget. Follow redirects to capture the final URL and its UTM params, and confirm the "Ending Tonight: 30% Off" offer is reflected on the landing pages.
2. Inspect the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and DKIM/SPF/DMARC results. Don't treat the relay-driven warnings as findings until then.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and populate `<title>`.
4. Check whether the "Image |" prefix in the linked-image alt text is real, and remove it if so.
5. Fix the QA tool's probe counts so skipped and unprobed links are listed in full.
