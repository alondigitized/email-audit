# Fleece Forward
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand fleece send with a strong hero. A model in a navy quarter-zip with the cuff of a light blue shirt showing, "Fleece Feels" overlaid, and a black SHOP NOW bar all land above the fold. Below it sit a nine-item grid of hoodies, joggers and a crewneck in a neutral palette, and a cream "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" banner. The render is tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The email has a news hook and doesn't use it. "Better prices" is the same price-cut story Everlane told in "Cashmere Beyond Sweater Weather" (2026-10-19), and again in "Cashmere for Every Forecast" (2026-10-20). Here there is no price, no percentage and no "was/now" anywhere. The nine products have no names or prices, so nothing tells you what you'd save. It is the third or fourth Everlane send in two days, and the sale framing is vaguer than in the cashmere emails.
- Judgement: a competent 6 that looks like the rest of the week's sends. Visual polish isn't the problem. The email needs one concrete number.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The navy quarter-zip is a clear fleece statement, and the black SHOP NOW bar is unmistakable.
- The nine-item grid is on a single theme (hoodies, joggers, a crewneck) in a cohesive neutral palette. It reads as a fleece lineup at a glance.
- The "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites." line gives some reassurance that lower prices don't mean a cheaper product.
- The layout is clean. It has generous white space and a clear top-to-bottom hierarchy, and nothing looks broken.
- The brand voice is consistent with the previous week of sends.

## What's weak

- "Better prices" has no number attached. There is no price, percentage or "was/now", so the headline claim can't be checked.
- The grid products have no names, prices or labels, and nothing shows which one is the hero item. Everything has equal weight, so nothing pulls the click.
- There is no urgency or deadline. Nothing gives a reason to act today.
- The cadence is repetitive. The "better prices" message echoes the cashmere sends from the last two days, and the grid format echoes "New October Arrivals".
- The hero copy "Fleece Feels" is vague, and the second SHOP NOW sends to the same generic place as the first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific price on the hero or the banner, for example "Fleece hoodies now $68, was $88". This one change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Add product names and prices under the grid items. Make the best seller or the biggest price drop a larger lead tile.
- 3. Link the CTAs to specific destinations, such as "Shop Hoodies" and "Shop Sweatpants", instead of two generic SHOP NOW buttons.
- 4. Add a credible time frame, such as "Prices valid through Sunday".
- 5. Vary the creative from the cashmere sends. Use a different hero angle, or a different module order, so the week doesn't read as one email repeated.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fleece, at better prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same fleece you love, new lower prices on hoodies and sweatpants.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nine fleece favorites, all repriced. Shop before the weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style HTML send with click and open tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run flagged two tracked links (the logo and the SMS signup) whose redirects resolved to relative paths. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link, "Everlane"**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5DH08BWJ401N2EM26HRG3YA_0` returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] SMS signup link, "Icon | Sign Up for Texts"**: `…_19` returned `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **What the failures mean**: both tracked links resolved to a path-only target (`/pages/...`) with no scheme or host. The cause is probably one of two things:
  - The tracker returns a relative `Location` header that the probe can't resolve. Browsers handle this fine, so the failure would be a probe artifact.
  - The destination URL was built without a base domain, which would break the link for real users.
  - The data doesn't say which. Request the `_0` and `_19` URLs with `curl -I` and check the `Location` header before treating these as user-facing breaks.
- **Coverage gap**: only 12 of 29 links were probed and 17 were skipped, so the 38% pass rate reflects a partial sample. Footer and social links were not verified.
- **Open-tracking pixel**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5DH08BWJ401N2EM26HRG3YA` was flagged for missing alt text. This is the tracking pixel, so the alt flag is not a real accessibility issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only), so there is no document title for assistive tech or clients that display it.
- The `<html>` tag has only `xmlns`, `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v`, with no `lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the right language profile.
- The `<style>` block has `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)`. Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip `@import`, so Arial and Helvetica fallbacks are in the font stacks. That is handled correctly.
- Mobile h4 is set to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`. Any text rendered as an `<h4>` will be hard to read on mobile.
- The source is truncated, so image alt text and the layout structure past the `<style>` block can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The truncated source means this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers from the original delivery. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The sender is `support@everlane.com` while links go through `trk.send.everlane.com`, so confirm the DKIM `d=` domain aligns with `everlane.com`.
- The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address can't be confirmed because the footer is cut off. The QA report lists no unsubscribe link failure, and it says an unsubscribe link is always probed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The two visible destinations carry Klaviyo-style identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V`, `cm_flow_i…`), but the output is cut off. I can't confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` are present.
- The logo resolves to `/pages/womens` for a "Fleece Forward" campaign. The QA data doesn't establish whether that is a campaign-specific landing page or a generic category page.
- The relative-path redirect failures above also mean landing-page alignment couldn't be verified for those two links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI` on `…_0` and `…_19` and inspect the `Location` header. If it is path-only, fix the tracker or destination configuration to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` URLs.
2. Check the raw delivered message for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Re-run this QA check against a source that preserves headers.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>` (for example, "Fleece Forward").
4. Review where `<h4>` is used. The 10px mobile size is below readable thresholds for body-like text.
5. Probe the 17 skipped links, or at least the footer unsubscribe and preference links, to complete coverage.
6. Confirm UTM parameters on the final landing URLs from the untruncated HTML.
