# “I want these pants in every shade.”
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Everywhere Pant send in two days. On 10-03 Everlane sent "“Obsessed. These pants are amazing!”" with the same barrel-leg hero and a colorway grid. This one swaps the review quote into the subject and the quote lower down. The creative is clean and easy to scan, but there's nothing new in it and no concrete offer.
- The strongest part is the single-product focus. The hero is a model in black pleated barrel-leg pants with a full-width SHOP NOW bar. A six-tile grid of navy, camel and black follows, then a SHOP ALL PANTS bar and a customer quote. The subject's "every shade" promise is only half kept, because the grid shows three colors repeated across flat-lay and on-model shots.
- The weak point is the offer. "Now priced even better" is the only price signal, with no number, no before/after and no deadline. A subscriber who got the near-identical send yesterday has little reason to open or click again. Judgement: well executed and unremarkable, and cadence is the biggest problem.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: one model, one product, a muted background, and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar right under it.
- The six-tile colorway grid shows navy, camel and black in both flat-lay and on-model shots, so color and fit are visible without clicking.
- The customer quote ("I am contemplating what color to buy next!") is social proof that matches the subject's "every shade" idea.
- The "organic cotton with a touch of linen" line gives a concrete fabric detail.
- No render bugs. Images load, text is legible and spacing is consistent.

## What's weak

- "Priced even better" is vague. There's no price, no percentage and no comparison, so it reads as a buried offer.
- It repeats yesterday's "Obsessed. These pants are amazing!" send. The hero and grid are nearly identical, and the subject is a different customer-quote framing of the same product.
- The grid shows only three colors, so "every shade" over-promises.
- The "We don't just say we're better" sustainability block with READ MORE is generic and pulls attention away from the product. It sits between the product content and the footer and has nothing to do with the pants.
- The hero overlay "The Everywhere Pant" is plain text with no price or hook. It doesn't carry the quote or the value message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real price or comparison in the hero and body, for example "Now $98, was $118". That single change lifts every score, because the email currently has no concrete offer.
- 2. Don't send a second near-identical Everywhere Pant email within a day. Space it out, or make the follow-up different (a new color, a restock, or a different pant).
- 3. Make the grid match the subject. Show more colors, or add color labels or swatches, or change the subject to fit what is shown.
- 4. Replace the generic SHOP NOW with specific copy such as "Shop the Everywhere Pant".
- 5. Move the review quote up under the hero and cut or shrink the sustainability block.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style table layout sent through a custom tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). Two tracked links (the logo and the SMS signup CTA) fail QA because their redirects resolve to host-relative paths, and the header checks couldn't be verified through the AgentMail relay.

- **Relative redirect targets (2 FAIL).** The probe followed the tracking redirects and got `unknown url type` errors, which means the final destination was a path with no scheme or host:
  - Logo ("Everlane Logo"): `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4261VAVCJ62H7HXE86FBXPG_0` → `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`
  - "Icon | Sign Up for Texts": `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4261VAVCJ62H7HXE86FBXPG_16` → `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…`
  - A browser would resolve these against the tracker's host, and `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/...` is probably a 404. If the redirect really emits a bare `Location: /pages/...`, both links are broken for real users. If it's a probe artifact, it needs a manual click-through to confirm. I can't tell which from the data given.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 14 were skipped as low-priority footer or social links. The 38% pass rate reflects the probed set, so the skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4261VAVCJ62H7HXE86FBXPG` is the open-tracking image. The QA flags it as missing alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Open-tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute.** The fix is `alt=""` (decorative), which keeps screen readers from announcing the raw URL.
- **Empty `<title> </title>`** in the head. This is minor, since most clients ignore it, but it is empty.
- **No other issues confirmed.** The HTML is truncated, so I can't audit image `alt` text, link text, or VML fallbacks beyond the head. What is visible looks fine:
  - MSO conditionals and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present.

  - Font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica.
- **`@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)`** is blocked in many clients (Gmail strips it), and the Arial fallbacks cover those cases.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA notes it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay," so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so it should be checked against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm the address or unsubscribe markup.

- Landing alignment can't be assessed for the two failed links because their redirects don't resolve to a full URL. The remaining probed links' destinations weren't provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the two relative redirects** (`…_0` and `…_16`) with `curl -sI` and inspect the `Location` header. If it is host-relative, fix the Klaviyo link or redirect configuration to emit an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` URL. Re-test the logo and SMS signup links in a real client.
