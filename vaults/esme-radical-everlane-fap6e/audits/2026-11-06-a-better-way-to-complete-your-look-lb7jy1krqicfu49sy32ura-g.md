# A Better Way to Complete Your Look
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Black Friday preview send in three days, and the cleanest at stating the offer. "30% OFF OUTFIT-MAKING ESSENTIALS" sits over a tight shot of ribbed knit and a zipper, with a black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath. It follows 11/05's "Black Friday Preview: Shop 30% Off" (7/10) with the same 30% and almost the same creative. That makes it a repeat, not news.
- The product grid is where it falls down. Six pieces (navy trousers, brown V-neck, black jeans, trench, green mock neck, cream V-neck) are shown with no names, prices or individual links I can tell. The subject promises a "better way to complete your look," but nothing shows a look. It's six loose items on grey. The deadline (11/09 at 11:59 PM PST) is only in the footer fine print, so the urgency is buried.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand promo that is mostly a rerun. Solid but unremarkable, and the subject undersells the 30%.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "30% OFF" in large white type over a calm knit texture, with an eyebrow reading "Black Friday Preview Event."
- The black SHOP NOW bar is high-contrast and unambiguous, and it sits right under the hero.
- The visual hierarchy is clean: hero, one line of copy, product grid, nav. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The product grid is tidy and seasonal (knits, trench, trousers), with a cohesive palette of browns, navy, green and cream.
- The nav (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) gives a way in for people who don't want the grid.

## What's weak

- The subject says nothing about 30% or Black Friday, so the strongest hook stays hidden from the inbox.
- The deadline is only in grey footer fine print, so there's no visible urgency in the body.
- The six products have no labels, prices or "from" prices, and there's no sign of which style leads. That adds friction and gives no reason to click one over another.
- This is the third consecutive 30% preview send (see 11/05 and 11/04), and it recycles the same offer and a similar hero with no new reason to act.
- "Outfit-making essentials" is vague, and the grid doesn't show anything styled as an outfit, so the subject's promise isn't delivered.
- The hero text sits over a busy texture. It's readable, but the eyebrow line is thin and low contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, e.g. "Ends Sunday 11/9," and lead the subject with the 30%. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Label each grid item with a name and a sale price (e.g. "$98 → $69"), and make each tile clearly link to its product page.
- 3. Deliver on "complete your look": pair items into two or three outfits (trousers + sweater + coat) instead of a loose grid.
- 4. Add a proof point such as star ratings or "best-seller" tags on one or two items.
- 5. Give the third send a new beat, like a tiered offer, a member perk or a last-chance reminder, rather than repeating the same 30%.
