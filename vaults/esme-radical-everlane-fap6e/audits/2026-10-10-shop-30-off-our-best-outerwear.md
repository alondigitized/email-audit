# Shop 30% Off Our Best Outerwear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth 30% Friends & Family send in five days. It follows "30% Off Outerwear" (10/9), "30% Off Better Sweaters" (10/8 and 10/9) and "30% Off Utility Pants" (10/9). The execution is clean: a strong hero, a nine-jacket grid, and a "Plus enjoy an extra 20% off markdowns" kicker. But "Shop 30% Off Our Best Outerwear" is nearly the same send as the 10/9 outerwear email, and a subscriber would notice.
- The hero image is good. Two models in dark outerwear and layers carry the season well. The weak spot is the overlay text. "30% Off Sitewide / Including Outerwear" is small white type placed over the busy white sweater and scarf on the right. "Sitewide" and "Outerwear" lose contrast there, so the offer is hard to read. The offer is the one thing that should stand out.
- The grid has nine jackets, no names, no prices and no reviews, so every click goes to a generic "Shop Now". The grid is attractive and not much more than that. This is a decent send, but it is a repeat with weak product detail.

## What's working

- The hero is on-season and on-brand, with a clear Friends & Family 30% message and a full-width black "SHOP NOW" bar right below it.
- The 3x3 jacket grid on a neutral background is easy to scan and shows range: bombers, chore coats, a hooded parka and a quarter-zip.
- The "Plus enjoy an extra 20% off markdowns" block adds a second reason to click, with separate Women's and Men's sale links.
- The copy "kind of brick outside, meet better layers" has some personality and is true to the brand voice.
- The deadline appears in the footer fine print (10/13/2026, 11:59 PM PST), so the offer feels honest.

## What's weak

- The white "30% Off Sitewide / Including Outerwear" text sits over the white sweater and scarf, so the key offer is partly washed out.
- The grid has no product names, prices or ratings, and no individual product is called out.
- There are three generic "Shop Now" CTAs and no verb-plus-noun CTA like "Shop Women's Jackets".
- The only urgency is in the grey fine print, with no visible deadline in the body.
- The send is repetitive: the subject and creative closely echo the 10/9 outerwear email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in a solid, high-contrast block, either a banner or text on a darker area of the hero. Add the deadline there: "30% off through Monday."
- 2. Make the grid shoppable. Show name and price for each jacket, with a "was/now" price and a review count on 3-4 hero pieces.
- 3. Replace the generic CTAs with specific ones: "Shop Women's Jackets" and "Shop Men's Jackets".
- 4. Don't resend near-identical outerwear creative. Change the angle: a weather hook, a best-seller-by-reviews cut, or a last-days reminder.
- 5. Give the markdown block a real concrete example: "Select jackets from $X".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML build with a tracked-link redirect failure on the logo link (`/pages/womens?_kx=…`). The auth and unsubscribe header checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay doesn't expose those headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the findings below cover only what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect.**
  - Link: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4HMGPDBES5Y0QX0E8F5VVS8_0` (text "Everlane Logo").
  - The probe failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`.
  - The redirect target is a relative path, with no scheme or host. This suggests the tracker returns a relative `Location`.
  - A browser would resolve that against the tracking host, giving `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`, which probably isn't a valid page. I could not confirm this. The probe client may simply be stricter than browsers, so test it with a real client.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 33 links were probed, and 21 were skipped as footer, utility or social links. Their status is unknown. Only the logo failure is confirmed.
- **Click tracking.** All tracked links go through the `trk.send.everlane.com` redirector, with `_kx` (Klaviyo profile token) and `bxid`. The redirector is on the sender's own domain, which is good for branding and deliverability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel.** `<img src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4HMGPDBES5Y0QX0E8F5VVS8">` has no `alt`. This is the `/o/` open pixel, so it is low severity. It should still have `alt=""` to be valid and screen-reader-safe.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is blank. Low severity.
- **Link styling.** The `<style>` block sets `a:link/visited/hover/active {color:#000; text-decoration:none}`. Any inline text link is then indistinguishable from body text, which fails WCAG 1.4.1 unless the links are styled in some other way. The truncated source doesn't show whether they are.
- **Mobile heading size.** `h4` drops to `font-size:10px !important` at max-width 480px, which is hard to read if h4 is used for real content.
- **Duplicate CSS.** The `.hide` media query is declared twice. This is harmless.
- **Fonts and viewport.** The `@import` of the Klaviyo custom font CSS has Arial/Helvetica fallbacks. The viewport meta tag, the `mso` conditionals and the `X-UA-Compatible` tag are present. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The `_kx` and `bxid` values are populated. The tail of the HTML is truncated and unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. Verify with a direct seed test.
- **Sender and footer.** The From address is `support@everlane.com`, a support mailbox used for promotional mail, which may affect reply handling. A visible unsubscribe link and a physical address could not be confirmed because the source is truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The logo link's destination, `/pages/womens`, is a general women's landing page, not outerwear. Whether the other links go to an outerwear collection can't be checked, because the other probe results weren't reported.
- The visible URL fragments show Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. The strings are cut off, so UTMs may be present.
- Because the redirect fails, the `_kx` identity and attribution parameters may be lost on that link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect at `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4HMGPDBES5Y0QX0E8F5VVS8_0` so it returns an absolute `Location` (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`). Re-test it with `curl -I` and a real browser.
2. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw headers, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC align, using a seed inbox.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `/o/` tracking pixel and put a real title in `<title>`.
4. Underline inline text links or give them a distinguishing colour. Raise the mobile `h4` size from 10px if it carries readable content.
5. Make sure the logo link and other CTAs carry `utm_*` parameters, and that the main CTAs go to an outerwear-specific destination.
6. Raise the probe budget, or rerun the QA, so the 21 skipped links are covered.
