# No Tricks, Just an Extra 25% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" send Everlane ran on 10/29, with a new subject line. The hero is a model in a light-blue oxford over "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS / ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF", with a black SHOP WOMEN'S SALE bar directly under it. The offer is legible, the hierarchy is clean, and nothing in the render is broken.
- The "No Tricks" subject is a Halloween pun on the 10/30 send date, and it's the only new thing here. The body doesn't continue the joke, so the pun is wasted. The "Offer valid until 11/01/2026" deadline sits in the grey footer fine print, not in the hero. That makes the urgency easy to miss, even though "before your size is gone" hints at scarcity.
- The email works but repeats itself. A subscriber who got "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" yesterday sees the same offer again with no new reason to act. The 2x2 category grid is tidy but shows no prices, no savings, and no reviews, so it never proves the discount is real.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS / ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF" in large white type over the photo.
- A full-width black "SHOP WOMEN'S SALE" bar is an unmistakable primary CTA.
- The 2x2 grid (sweaters, outerwear, tops, denim) gives clear category entry points on clean grey tiles.
- "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites." with "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" answers the worry that sale items are lower quality.
- The layout is clean and on-brand, with no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- The real deadline (11/01 at 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the fine print, so the email has no visible urgency.
- The hero text sits over a busy part of the photo (the model's arm and shirt), which weakens contrast on "ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF".
- No product tile shows a price, a before/after, or a discount, so the "up to 70%" claim is never backed by a concrete item.
- Men's and the other category links sit in a plain text list at the bottom, so the hero and grid are women's only.
- The email is a near-repeat of the 10/29 "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" send, with the same offer and no new angle.
- Footer social links are bare abbreviations (TT, IG, FB, X, YT), which look unfinished.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday 11/1", under the offer headline. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Show real prices in the grid, such as "Sale sweaters from $49 (was $98)". Concrete numbers make "up to 70%" believable.
- 3. Pick one proof point for the hero product, like a named bestseller with a price and star rating, instead of four generic categories.
- 4. Either commit to the Halloween angle in the hero or drop the pun from the subject.
- 5. Space the repeated offer out, or give it a different angle, like "last chance", so it doesn't feel like yesterday's send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built email has one probe failure on a "Shop Men" tracked link. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, address and most link checks are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link:** The probe failed with `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
  - The tracked URL was `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M678ZVBX0JP5A8WQG5HG0QM8_9`.
  - The redirect resolved to a **relative** path with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so this is most likely a probe limitation and not a user-facing break. I can't confirm that from the data.
  - If the tracker really emits a relative `Location`, it is non-conformant for strict clients, such as some in-app webviews and link scanners.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 1 of 12 material links was probed (11 skipped), so the 43% pass rate reflects limited coverage and shouldn't be read as 57% of links failing.
- **Click tracking:** Links go through the `trk.send.everlane.com` redirector, a first-party branded tracking domain. That is fine for deliverability.

- **No `lang` attribute:** The root `<html>` tag has `xmlns` attributes only, no `lang` and no `dir`.
