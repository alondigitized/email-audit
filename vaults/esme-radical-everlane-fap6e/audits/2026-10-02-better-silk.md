# Better Silk
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Washable Silk send in two days. On 10-01 Everlane sent "Silk, But Make it Washable" with almost the same cream-shirt hero and the same "no dry cleaner needed" line. This version adds two named products with prices, which is a real improvement. But "Better Silk" is a vague subject, and the body's only price signal is "Now at better prices" with no number to back it up.
- The layout is clean. A tight crop of a cream shirt sits over a full-width black SHOP NOW bar, followed by a two-product zig-zag with a dark top at $138 and a tan shirt at $148. Nothing is broken or overlapping, and it reads quickly. The "Washable Silk" overlay is small and sits on the busy part of the shirt, so the hero is mostly doing the work on its own.
- Overall it is a competent, forgettable repeat. The pricing claim is the weakest point. A "better prices" line with no reference price asks the reader to take it on trust, and Everlane showed on 09-30 with the Way-High Jean ("previously $148, now $88") that it can state a real number.

## What's working

- The hero is a clear crop on one garment, with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath it.
- Each product block has a name, a price ($138, $148), a one-line fit description and its own link, so it's easy to scan.
- The two-up photos (full look plus close detail) show drape and color, which is what a silk buyer wants to see.
- "Real silk, made for real life. No dry cleaner needed." gives a concrete reason to buy in plain language.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, and the footer nav is tidy.

## What's weak

- "Now at better prices" gives no reference price or percentage, so it can't be checked and carries no urgency.
- This is nearly a repeat of the 10-01 silk email, with the same hero idea and the same dry-cleaner line. That is cadence fatigue.
- "Washable Silk" is small, thin type over a busy part of the shirt, so the hero message is easy to miss.
- All three CTAs say "SHOP NOW", and the two product buttons are small underlined text.
- There are no reviews, ratings or sizing cues, and only two products are shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "better prices" with a real number, such as "Was $168, now $138" or "Washable Silk from $138". This is the single biggest lift and gives the subject something concrete to carry.
- 2. Differentiate from the 10-01 send. Lead with a new angle such as a "wash test" or a "30 washes later" proof point, or add a reviews line, instead of repeating the same hero.
- 3. Make the hero headline bigger and move it off the busy shirt area, so the message reads at thumbnail size.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Shop the Boxy Shirt" and "Shop the Must-Have Shirt".
- 5. Add a social-proof line, such as a star rating or "Best-selling silk" under each product.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built, MJML-style email with open tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run found two failing tracked links that redirect to relative paths. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated, and the HTML supplied is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane Logo"**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3X1A5WPYEHFRG0NNV2GN3KZ_0` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts"**: `…_14` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect target is a scheme-less, host-less path (`/pages/...`). The probe's HTTP client can't resolve that.
- **Not confirmed as user-facing:** a browser would resolve a relative `Location` against the tracking host. That would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is probably a 404. It could also be a probe-only artifact. Verify with `curl -sI` on both URLs and check the `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 12 material links were skipped, so no CTA or product link was validated. Link health is unknown beyond the two failures above.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only.
- **Missing alt (low severity):** QA flagged the image with `src=https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M3X1A5WPYEHFRG0NNV2GN3KZ`. The `/o/` path indicates an open-tracking pixel, so this is a technical non-issue. Adding `alt=""` would clear the warning.
- **Link styling:** the `a:link/visited/hover { text-decoration:none; color:#000 }` rules remove underlines and colour distinction for every link. Links are distinguishable only by position, which is an accessibility concern for text links.
- **Heading sizes:** mobile `h4` drops to `10px !important`, which is below a legible minimum if h4 carries content.
- **Not verified:** the HTML is truncated, so the rest of the markup, including alt text on content images, isn't verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw `.eml` before treating it as a sender defect, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible tracked URLs carry Klaviyo params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`) but no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` in the truncated portions. Whether UTMs are added at redirect time is unverified.
- The logo goes to `/pages/womens`, a gendered landing page, while the subject is "Better Silk". This isn't a code defect, but the destination of the product CTAs couldn't be checked because those probes were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on `…_0` and `…_14` to confirm the `Location` header. If it is relative, fix the Klaviyo/tracking-domain redirect config to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` URLs.
2. Re-run the QA probe with the skip cap raised so the CTA and product links are validated. The 38% pass rate reflects mostly unprobed links.
3. Fetch the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't score these from the relay copy.
4. Fill in `<title>` with the subject line or preheader text. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Restore a visible underline or colour on in-body text links, or confirm they're styled as buttons.
6. Confirm `utm_*` parameters are appended at redirect time on the final landing URLs.
