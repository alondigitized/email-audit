# Ending Soon: Extra 20% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's sixth "extra % off" send in two days, and by now the fatigue is showing in the copy, not just the creative. To their credit, the cloud/watercolor gradient hero is genuinely new — a break from the peony/tulip macro shot that's been recycled across every prior send today — but the layout underneath (headline split, black "SHOP NOW" bar, "already up to 70% off" callout) is the identical template, just re-skinned.
- The problem is the offer has now quietly stepped down. This says "Extra 20% Off," while the two "25% Off" sends earlier today and yesterday's "Don't Miss Out: Extra 25% Off" pitched a bigger discount. A subscriber who's seen five near-identical Everlane emails in 48 hours will notice the number went down, not up, right as urgency copy ("Ends Tonight") tries to convince them to act now. That's a credibility problem, not just a fatigue problem.
- Net: clean, on-brand execution with zero render bugs, but the cadence and downgraded offer undercut it. Score accordingly.

## What's working

- New hero visual (cloud/watercolor gradient) breaks the peony/tulip repetition from the last five sends.
- Clear, unambiguous single CTA — black "SHOP NOW" bar is impossible to miss.
- Legit urgency: fine print gives an exact deadline (09/08/2026, 11:59 PM PST), not vague hype.
- Layout is clean and renders correctly — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- Secondary nav (Shop Women/Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) gives an out for browsers not sold on the sale.

## What's weak

- The offer dropped from 25% off (today's and yesterday's earlier sends) to 20% off here — a visible downgrade mid-fatigue cycle, which reads as bait-and-switch to an attentive subscriber.
- This is the sixth same-shaped promo in ~48 hours; template, offer mechanic, and copy are otherwise indistinguishable from prior sends.
- Body copy ("Big markdowns on styles worth wearing now and later. Don't miss out.") is generic filler that doesn't add new information beyond the hero.
- No specific product, category highlight, or social proof — everything routes to broad category pages, not a merchandised hero item.
- Subject "Ending Soon" plus hero "ENDS TONIGHT" is slightly inconsistent language for the same deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer regression first — don't send a 20%-off email hours after a 25%-off one to the same list; if 25% already ran, this send should escalate or convert to last-chance "final hours," not quietly downgrade the number.
- 2. Cut sending frequency — six sends in two days on one mechanic is the single biggest drag on this campaign's performance; consolidate to one strong "last call" send per day.
- 3. Swap generic body copy for a specific merchandised item or bestseller callout to give clickers a reason beyond the discount.
- 4. Align subject and hero urgency language exactly ("Ends Tonight" everywhere) to avoid a jarring mismatch.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale styles, up to 70% off, plus an extra 20% — ends at midnight.`
