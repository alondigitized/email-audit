# Don’t Miss Out On The Employee Sale
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the third broken plain-text-fallback template Everlane has sent this week — same skeleton as "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off" (08/27) and "Employee Sale Access Ends Soon" (08/29), just reworded again. No hero image, no product shots, no pricing visuals — just stacked paragraph text, an underlined text link, and a broken personalization token: "Hi There, ," with a stray comma where a first name should be.
- The 70% off and "no code needed" copy is a genuinely strong, concrete offer, and the underlined "SHOP THE SALE" link is at least unambiguous. But this is the same creative failure mode as the last two sends in this exact thread — the brand appears to have a broken merge-tag or template-rendering issue that nobody has caught after three consecutive emails. That's no longer a one-off glitch; it's a pattern a subscriber will notice and it erodes trust fast.
- Judgement: functionally the weakest kind of promotional email — no visual merchandising, a visible personalization bug, and a stale beat repeated for the third time this week. It converts on default text-link behavior, not on any real engagement layer.

## What's working

- 70% off is a specific, motivating discount stated clearly in the second paragraph.
- "No code needed" removes friction at the point of decision.
- Single underlined CTA ("SHOP THE SALE") is unambiguous, even without a styled button.
- Footer terms (expiration date 09/07/2026, final sale, US-only) are visible and not buried.

## What's weak

- Broken personalization token: "Hi There, ," — visible unfilled merge field with a dangling comma.
- Zero product imagery or pricing — nothing to visually anchor "70% off" to an actual item.
- No styled CTA button — just an underlined text link, which reads as low-effort/unfinished.
- Identical stripped-down template to the 08/27 and 08/29 sends — third repeat in five days.
- Urgency language ("about to expire," "last call") isn't paired with a visible countdown or date until the fine print at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the personalization token before another send — "Hi There, ," is a visible, embarrassing bug that undermines every other claim in the email.
- 2. Add a hero product image or small grid tied to the 70% off claim — even 3-4 sale items would give the eye something to land on and make the discount tangible.
- 3. Replace the underlined text link with a solid CTA button — "Shop the Sale" in a filled box reads as an intentional send, not a fallback.
- 4. Surface the actual deadline (09/07 at 11:59 PM PST) near the top, not just in the footer legal block — real urgency needs a visible date.
- 5. Vary the template — three near-identical plain-text sends in one week trains subscribers to skim or ignore this format entirely.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: Your Employee Sale Discount Expires Soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — up to 70% off best-sellers before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your access closes September 7 at midnight.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane "Don't Miss Out On The Employee Sale"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built MJML/HTML email with standard multi-client CSS resets, but automated QA flagged two broken redirect links and missing authentication/unsubscribe headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken links (2 confirmed failures):** Both use Klaviyo's tracked-link redirector (`trk.send.everlane.com/l/...`) but resolve to relative paths instead of absolute URLs:
  - "Shop Men" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M1CKV0N2PXVKJCK63V6J3W34_3` → resolves to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=...&bxid=...` (no scheme/host)
  - "Everlane Logo" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M1CKV0N2PXVKJCK63V6J3W34_0` → resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...` (no scheme/host)
  - **Impact:** clients/tools resolving the redirect target as a bare relative path will fail to open a valid destination ("unknown url type"). This points to a misconfigured destination URL in the Klaviyo link block (missing `https://www.everlane.com` prefix) rather than a rendering issue.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M1CKV0N2PXVKJCK63V6J3W34` present (Klaviyo open-tracking beacon) — functioning as expected.
- 12 material links were probed automatically; 5 were deprioritized (footer/utility/social) per probing methodology — not evidence of additional breakage, just untested.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** tracking pixel image (`01M1CKV0N2PXVKJCK63V6J3W34`, src `trk.send.everlane.com/o/...`) has no `alt` attribute. Low severity since it's a 1x1 open-tracking pixel, not content, but should still carry `alt=""` to avoid screen-reader announcement of the raw URL.
- Head block includes standard Outlook conditional comments (`mso`), MSO font/pixel settings, and responsive breakpoints (`max-width:480px`) — no malformed conditional comments observed in the visible source.
- Cannot fully assess body-level accessibility (heading structure, image alt coverage beyond the flagged pixel, color contrast) — HTML source was truncated before the body content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved Klaviyo merge tags (e.g. `{{ '{{' }} first_name {{ '}}' }}`) visible in the available source. No issues found in the visible portion — note this is not a full guarantee given the truncated body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** not found. May be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence from Everlane's send — cannot confirm root cause from HTML alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058):** not found — if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) is not supported.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not found via the relay — authentication status of this send is unverifiable from available data.
- These three findings are header-transport warnings, not confirmed HTML-body defects; recommend verifying directly against raw SMTP headers from a non-relayed capture before treating as confirmed compliance failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Link query strings use Klaviyo's own tracking params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`) rather than standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` — this is consistent with native Klaviyo tracking and not itself a defect, but means GA/analytics attribution depends on Klaviyo's own integration rather than URL-based UTM parsing.
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment (e.g. whether `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` and `/pages/womens` are live, correct destinations) because the broken-link errors prevented resolution to an absolute URL for probing.
