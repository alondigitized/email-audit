## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) send routed through `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The one hard QA failure is a tracked link whose redirect target is a relative path. Most other findings are inconclusive because of probe rate limiting, the time budget, and a truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect target (FAIL).** The link "Everlane" (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M6VZKRAJH9JA0QT4C0E9HWPM_0`) resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`, with no scheme or host.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type`, so the tracker is returning a relative `Location`. That is legal HTTP, and browsers will resolve it against the tracker host.
  - It will then point at `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is likely a 404, or at least an unintended destination. Some clients and scanners will fail on it outright.
  - This is probably a tracker or redirect-config bug. A probe-tool artifact is possible, so I'd verify it with `curl -I` before treating it as confirmed.
- **Rate limited (429), inconclusive.** Links `_1` ("Image | On Good Form"), `_2` ("Shop Now"), `_10` ("Shop Women") and `_11` ("Shop Men") all returned 429. These are neither pass nor fail.
- **Unprobed links.** Probing hit the 70s budget. `_3` through `_7` are listed as unprobed, though the summary says 6, so one link is unaccounted for. A further 15 links were skipped as low priority.
- **Pass rate.** The reported 43% pass rate is not a reliable health figure, because it counts mostly unverified links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on the open-tracking pixel.** The flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6VZKRAJH9JA0QT4C0E9HWPM`, the open pixel. It is not content imagery, so the impact is low. Use `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is a whitespace-only title. Impact is low in most clients.
- **Small mobile heading size.** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` renders at 10px on mobile. That is below common legibility guidance of about 14px or more if the h4s carry meaningful text. I can't tell from the truncated source whether they do.
- **Web-font `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica Neue, so this degrades gracefully and isn't a defect.
- The source has a viewport meta, a dark-mode override (`[data-ogsc]`), and Outlook (MSO) conditionals. I found no issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`). Coverage of the rest of the body is unknown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Presence is unconfirmed rather than proven absent. Check the raw message at the origin or in a direct inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the QA tool attributes this to the relay. It is not evidence of failure.
- **Footer.** The unsubscribe link and physical address were not visible in the truncated source and can't be verified. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none is reported as failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters visible.** The only visible query parameters are Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…`, which suggests the click is attributed through Klaviyo rather than UTMs. If the site's analytics need `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`, I can't confirm they are appended after the redirect.
- **Final destinations are hidden.** Every link goes through the click tracker, and the 429s and unprobed links mean landing pages for most CTAs ("Shop Now", "Shop Women", "Shop Men") are unverified.
- **Landing page for the "Everlane" link.** The one resolved destination (`/pages/womens`) is a Women's landing page, and its host is unresolved because of the relative-redirect bug above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the relative redirect.** Make `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…_0` return an absolute `Location` (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…`). Check whether the other tracked links do the same.
2. **Re-probe the inconclusive links.** Re-run with throttling or backoff for `_1`, `_2`, `_10`, `_11` and the unprobed `_3` to `_7`, then reconcile the 5-versus-6 count.
3. **Verify headers at the origin.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on the raw message, not the relay copy.
4. **Tidy the pixel and title.** Set `alt=""` plus explicit dimensions on the open pixel, and give the email a meaningful `<title>`.
5. **Check mobile text size.** Raise the `h4` mobile size above 10px if it carries content.
6. **Confirm UTM handling.** Check that the destination site reads and persists campaign parameters for attribution.
7. **Get the full source.** Rerun the audit on the untruncated HTML so tokens, the footer and the remaining links can be checked.
