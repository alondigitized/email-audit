## What's weak

- The offer is buried. It is a small banner with no deadline, and "12/02" appears only in grey legal text at the bottom.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". It names no product, such as "Shop the boxy crew".
- The product copy gives no prices, sale prices or sizing, so the 40% off never attaches to an item.
- The three tiles overlap on white tees and cream trousers, so they read as one look, not three ideas.
- This is another Black Friday send on top of today's two earlier ones. The "Better Gift Guide" block at the bottom adds a second message to a send that should have one.
