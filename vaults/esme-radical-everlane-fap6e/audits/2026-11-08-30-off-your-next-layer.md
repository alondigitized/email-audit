# 30% Off Your Next Layer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident Black Friday preview send, and the offer is stated plainly: "30% OFF OUTERWEAR & DENIM" in large white type over a model in a cobalt sweater and wide navy trousers. A full-width black SHOP NOW bar sits directly under the hero. It is the sixth Black Friday-adjacent Everlane send in four days. It follows "Selling Fast: 30% Off Cult Favorites & More" and "A Better Way to Complete Your Look" from earlier this week, so the 30% offer is no longer news.
- The category focus is the strongest thing about it. Outerwear and denim in early November is seasonally right, and the six-item grid (three denim, a brown quilted jacket, a puffer, a trench) matches the headline. Unlike the Nov 7 "Softly Structured Tops & Dresses" send, the offer leads instead of being bolted on at the bottom.
- The weakness is execution after the hero. The grid has no prices, no names, and no per-product links I can see. The "Offer valid until 11/09/2026" deadline is only in grey fine print, even though it is the strongest urgency lever. The deadline is tomorrow night, and it should be in the hero.

## What's working

- The offer and category are stated in the hero: "30% OFF OUTERWEAR & DENIM" in large type, readable over the image.
- The black SHOP NOW bar is high-contrast and sits directly under the hero.
- The hero image is strong. The cobalt sweater against muted grey architecture draws the eye.
- The grid is on-category, and the brown and cream palette reads as seasonal.
- The copy is short and voicey: "The jacket you'll reach for all week."

## What's weak

- The deadline (11/09 at 11:59 PM PST) is buried in light grey 8pt-style footer text, so urgency is lost.
- The grid products have no prices, names, or "was/now" figures, so the 30% never becomes a concrete saving.
- "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW EVENT" is thin white text over a mid-tone part of the photo, and its contrast is weak.
- The footer navigation (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) has no clear connection to the offer. It also gives no hint that the discount covers only "select styles."
- The cadence is heavy, with near-identical 30% messaging again.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tomorrow, 11:59 PM PST," under the headline. It is the strongest unused lever, and the fine print says the offer ends 11/09.
- 2. Add the product name and a sale price under each grid item, such as "Quilted Liner Jacket, $XX → $XX." Add a "Shop Outerwear" and a "Shop Denim" button below the grid so the click goes to a category.
- 3. Darken the hero overlay slightly or bold the "Black Friday Preview Event" label so it passes contrast.
- 4. Say "select styles" near the offer to reduce fine-print friction.
- 5. Vary the creative and angle across the week's sends so the 30% doesn't feel repetitive.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-built send using `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The only hard failure is one logo link the QA probe couldn't resolve. Most other findings are inconclusive because of 429s, the probe time budget, and headers the AgentMail relay doesn't expose.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link (`..._0`, text "Everlane Logo"):** the probe raised `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...'`. The tracker's redirect appears to return a **relative** `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`). Relative redirects are valid under RFC 7231, so this is probably a probe limitation. Real clients resolve it against the tracking host (`trk.send.everlane.com`), which would be a 404 there. It's worth confirming with `curl -I https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M6YJ04Y8Q1FVPZ2SAFH2AJGE_0` and checking the `Location` header.
- **429s (inconclusive, not failures):** `..._1` (Black Friday Preview hero), `..._3` (Wide-Leg Jean) and `..._4` (Reversible Mid Liner). The tracker rate-limited the probe.
- **Unprobed:** `..._6`, `_7`, `_9`, `_10`, `_23` hit the 70s time budget, and the QA summary reports 12 material links skipped. The 43% pass rate mostly reflects this, so it is not a reliable health signal.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6YJ04Y8Q1FVPZ2SAFH2AJGE` is present and expected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title> </title>`** in the `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`. This is confirmed in the source.
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel** (`/o/01M6...`). This is low severity because the pixel is decorative, but it should have `alt=""`.
- **Web font `@import`** from `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css`. Gmail and some other clients ignore it. Stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is graceful degradation, not a defect.
- The Outlook (`mso`) conditionals and the 480px media queries are present and look standard.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|...|*`, `%%...%%`) appear in the subject ("30% Off Your Next Layer") or the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact.
- **Footer not visible.** The source is truncated, so the unsubscribe link and physical address (CAN-SPAM) can't be confirmed. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for one.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No `utm_*` parameters are visible.** The only landing URL fragment shown (`/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...`) carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`) and is cut off, so I can't confirm whether UTMs exist further along.
- **The logo deep-links to `/pages/womens`** rather than the site root. This is reasonable for a women's-focused send, but it is unverified because the redirect failed to resolve.
- **Hero and product links** (`_1`, `_3`, `_4`) couldn't be resolved because of the 429s, so landing-page alignment with "30% Off Outerwear & Denim" is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually inspect the `..._0` redirect. If `Location` is relative, ask Klaviyo to fix it. If the destination is absolute, treat this as a probe bug and add relative-`Location` handling to the QA tool.
2. Re-probe `_1`, `_3`, `_4` and the five unprobed links with backoff or a longer budget, so the results are a real pass or fail.
3. Re-check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on a direct copy of the message, not the relay copy.
4. Fetch the full HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link, physical address and any `utm_` parameters.
5. Add `lang="en"` and a meaningful `<title>`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. Everlane would make these changes in the Klaviyo template.
