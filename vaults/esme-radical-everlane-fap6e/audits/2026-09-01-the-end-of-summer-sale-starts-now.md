# The End of Summer Sale Starts Now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest Everlane send I've reviewed this week, and it's not close. After a string of broken plain-text-fallback templates ("Don't Miss Out On The Employee Sale" 08/31, "More Styles at up to 70% Off" 08/31, "Employee Sale Access Ends Soon" 08/29) and the barebones grid emails ("New for You" 08/30), this one actually has a hero: a clean model shot, a stacked offer callout (End of Summer Sale / Extra 20% Off Sale Styles, already up to 70% off), and a black SHOP NOW button sitting right on the image.
- Below the fold it's a tidy, well-organized four-category browse module (Tees, Tops, Denim, Pants) with consistent product photography and clear "Shop [Category]" links — functional, if generic. The offer itself is decent (extra 20% on top of up to 70% off) but the email never states final pricing anywhere, and there's no urgency beyond a buried footnote deadline (09/07).
- Overall this is a solid mid-tier promotional send — competent execution, real offer, no render bugs — but it's still a stacked-discount sale email that looks like every other end-of-season clearance blast, with nothing distinctly "Everlane" about the styling beyond the brand's usual minimalism.

## What's working

- Clean hero image with legible, well-balanced offer copy (stacked discount messaging: base markdown + extra 20%).
- Unambiguous primary CTA — black "SHOP NOW" button is visually anchored right under the hero.
- No render bugs: images load, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum, layout is intact top to bottom.
- Logical post-hero structure — four clearly labeled category modules (Tees, Tops, Denim, Pants) each with three product thumbnails and a "Shop [Category]" link.
- Consistent, minimal product photography that matches Everlane's brand aesthetic.

## What's weak

- No actual dollar or discounted price shown anywhere — "up to 70% off" plus "extra 20% off" makes the shopper do math instead of seeing a number.
- Urgency is weak: "For a limited time only" is vague copy; the real deadline (09/07/2026 11:59 PM PST) is buried in 6pt legal footer text, not in the body.
- Category grid is generic — plain product cutouts with no pricing, no color/style callouts, nothing to differentiate this from any other retailer's sale grid.
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or loyalty/member benefit shown despite this being a recognizable repeat subscriber relationship.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number in the hero — swap "up to 70% off" + "extra 20%" for an effective price or "up to 76% off," so the eye lands on a concrete win instead of doing subtraction.
- 2. Move the sale end date out of the footer and into the hero or a thin banner under the CTA — "Ends 9/7" next to the button adds real urgency at zero design cost.
- 3. Add a price or "from $X" under at least the top row of each category grid — right now every product looks interchangeable.
- 4. Consider swapping one static category (e.g., Pants) for a "Best Sellers on Sale" module to add proof/relevance instead of five nearly-identical grid layouts.
