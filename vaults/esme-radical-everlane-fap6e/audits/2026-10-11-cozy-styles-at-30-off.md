# Cozy Styles at 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-category sweater send, and the subject matches the body. "Cozy Styles at 30% Off" leads to a hero of a model in a zip-neck knit and a nine-sweater grid. The "Friends & Family / Ends 10.13 / 30% Off Sitewide Including Cozy Sweaters" overlay states the offer and deadline plainly. The layout is easy to scan.
- The problem is repetition. This is at least the seventh 30% Friends & Family send in about a week. It follows "30% Off Better Cashmere" and "A Classic Now 30% Off" (10/10), "30% Off Better Sweaters" (10/8–10/9), and the outerwear and utility-pants sends. A sweater grid also overlaps heavily with the cashmere and sweater emails already sent. Execution is solid, but a subscriber has seen this offer many times. The grid also has no prices or product names, so there's little new to click for.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, and it carries the same 7/10 as the previous sends. Cadence and the anonymous grid keep it from going higher.

## What's working

- The hero overlay puts the offer (30% off sitewide) and the deadline (Ends 10.13) right where the eye lands.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars are unambiguous, and the CTA appears twice, once after the hero and once after the grid.
- The nine-product grid on a neutral background is tidy and easy to scan. It covers crewnecks, cardigans, turtlenecks and a half-zip.
- The subject and creative agree: sweaters in the subject, sweaters in the hero and grid.
- The "Better prices, still Everlane" band reinforces the value message in the brand's usual voice.

## What's weak

- The grid has no prices, product names or sale-price callouts, so 30% off is never shown on an actual item.
- The "30% Off Sitewide" and "Including Cozy Sweaters" text sits over a busy, light knit texture. The second line is thin and hard to read.
- The grid is nine near-identical navy and black sweaters, so there is no clear hero product.
- The "SHOP NOW" bars and the band's text link all go to the same general place, with no category-specific CTA like "Shop Sweaters".
- This is the seventh 30% send in about a week, so the "Friends & Family" urgency has lost its credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the cadence. Combine the category sends into one or two sharper emails, and save the final-hours send for 10/13. Sending the same offer daily trains subscribers to ignore it.
- 2. Put prices on the grid, such as "$98 → $69", with product names, so the discount is visible on an item.
- 3. Feature one hero sweater with a short detail like fit or fabric, and shrink the rest into a smaller row.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, for example "Shop Sweaters" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Move the hero text onto a solid bar or darker gradient so the second line is legible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo/MJML-based email from `support@everlane.com`, with all links wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The QA run found one failure, which looks like a probe artifact rather than a broken link, and three header warnings that are probably caused by the AgentMail relay. The HTML source is truncated, so findings below are limited to what the source and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link probe error (likely a false positive).** The "Everlane Logo" link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4NTC7QT7D4BMZJ7RJZAG1M9_0` failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`.
  - The tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location` header (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host. The probe could not resolve it.
  - Browsers resolve relative redirects against the request origin, so this would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com`. I cannot tell from the QA data which behavior real clients get.
  - That would be a real issue if it happens in production. Re-test with a browser-like client and check the raw `Location` header.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 links were probed and 18 skipped, so footer and social links are unverified.
- **Tracking structure.** Links are opaque wrapped redirects (`/l/<ULID>_0`). The `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i` parameters are Klaviyo identifiers. No other tracking problems are visible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only. This is minor, since email clients rarely use it, but it hurts accessibility in web-view rendering.
- **Missing alt text [WARN]:** the flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4NTC7QT7D4BMZJ7RJZAG1M9`. The `/o/` path indicates it is the open-tracking pixel, so a missing alt attribute is low impact. Add `alt=""` for correctness.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is limited.
- **Outlook handling:** MSO conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings` and the `mj-outlook-group-fix` rule are present. `meta viewport` and mobile media queries are also present. No issues found.
- I can't check alt text on the other content images because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|`, or `%%`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message in a direct-delivery test, since Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be verified because the footer is truncated. The QA report says an unsubscribe link is always probed and raises no failure for it.
- The sender `support@everlane.com` is a support address used for marketing. This is not a violation, but it affects reply handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters** are visible in the reported link (only `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i`). They may be added at the tracker redirect or appear on other links. Confirm that the final landing URL carries `utm_source/medium/campaign`, or that analytics attribution is handled via Klaviyo.
- The logo resolves to `/pages/womens`, a generic category page. I could not verify whether the "30% Off Cozy Styles" CTAs land on a matching collection, because the CTA links were not in the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the logo link check with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. If the production redirect really is relative, have Everlane's tracker emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URLs.
2. Check the raw message headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `send.everlane.com`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Confirm UTM presence on the final destinations and that the promo CTAs land on a matching sale or cozy collection.
5. Probe the 18 skipped links, or at least the footer unsubscribe and preferences links.
