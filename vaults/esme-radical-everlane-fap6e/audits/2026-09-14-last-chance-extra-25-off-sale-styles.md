- Same broken personalization token that's now shown up across four straight sends — "Hi There, ," with an empty first-name field and a stray comma sits right at the top, unmissable. This is the fourth "extra 25% off sale styles" plain-text-styled blast in the last few days (2026-09-12, 2026-09-13, and now 2026-09-14), and this one leans on manufactured urgency ("ends tonight," "Last Chance") on top of a discount that hasn't actually moved from the prior sends.

- The offer itself is legible and specific: extra 25% off, stacked on styles already up to 70% off, with a clear VIP code (EXTRA25).
- Deadline is concrete and credible — "ends tonight," backed by fine print specifying 09/14/2026 11:59 PM PST.
- Category links (dresses, tops, more) give a fast path into inventory without forcing a homepage landing.

## What's weak

- "Hi There, ," — the broken personalization token is still unfixed after at least three prior sends with the same bug.
- Zero product photography — no hero image, no styled shot, nothing to visually anchor the offer or sell the actual clothes.
- Fourth near-identical "extra 25% off sale styles" email this week; the urgency framing ("Last Chance") rings false against a discount that's been running continuously.
- No single primary CTA button — four flat text nav links (Shop Women/Men/New Arrivals/Best-Sellers) compete equally, none tied directly to the sale.

- 1. Fix the first-name merge field or fall back to "Hi there" (no comma) when the token is empty — this has now shipped broken across four consecutive sends and is the single highest-priority fix.
- 2. Add a hero product image or grid of 3-4 sale items so there's something to visually click into, not just text links.

- 4. Since this is send #4 of the same offer, either escalate the discount for the true final push or stop sending — repeating "Last Chance" without a bigger incentive erodes trust in future urgency claims.
