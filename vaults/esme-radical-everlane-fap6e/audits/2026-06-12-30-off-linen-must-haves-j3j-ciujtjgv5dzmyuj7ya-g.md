# 30% Off Linen Must-Haves
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- This is yesterday's "30% Off Summer Styles" creative with a different subject line bolted on. The subject promises "Linen Must-Haves" — a specific, useful hook — but the moment the email opens, the hero shouts "30% OFF SUMMER STYLES" and the body copy says "breathable styles." Linen is never named inside the email. A subscriber who opened expecting a curated linen edit lands on a four-button category grid. That's a bait-and-switch at the subject level, and it damages the one thing this send had going for it.
- After eight consecutive editorial emails this month — the "Tee Party," "Give Denim A Break," "Feeling Blue" run — two near-identical 30%-off discount sends in 48 hours signals either an inventory push or a creative shortage. The June 11 send already made this trade-off explicit; this one makes it worse by adding a false linen premise on top.
- The email will convert deal-hunters who click anything at 30% off. For anyone who opened specifically because linen spoke to them, it's a let-down that erodes trust in the subject line over time.

## What's working

- **Subject is tight and specific**: "30% Off Linen Must-Haves" lands in 24 characters — mobile-perfect, gives both the offer and the category.
- **Hero image reads as linen**: the terracotta model on stone steps communicates warm, breathable summer fabric even if the overlay copy doesn't say so.
- **Four category CTAs are immediately scannable**: SHOP DRESSES / TEES / TOPS / BOTTOMS gives navigation without hunting.
- **Offer is above the fold**: 30% off is in the hero, no scroll required.
- **Clean render**: no broken images, no layout issues, no junk text visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject-hero mismatch**: subject says "Linen Must-Haves," hero says "30% OFF SUMMER STYLES" — the linen promise evaporates on open.
- **Recycled creative from June 11**: this appears to be the same "Vacation Essentials Event" hero used yesterday, giving subscribers genuine déjà vu on two consecutive days.
- **No linen-specific copy in the body**: "Breathable styles" is the closest it gets — not enough to deliver the subject's promise.
- **No sale deadline**: "VACATION ESSENTIALS EVENT" implies urgency but gives no end date, which defuses the pressure entirely.
- **No product specificity**: four generic category buttons instead of 2-3 hero linen SKUs with names and prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Align hero copy to the subject**: change the overlay from "30% OFF SUMMER STYLES" to "30% OFF LINEN MUST-HAVES," or swap in linen-specific creative. This is the single highest-impact fix — the subject-body mismatch is the email's defining flaw.
- 2. **Add a deadline**: "Ends Sunday" anywhere visible — hero, sub-copy, or button — is the cheapest urgency lift available.
- 3. **Feature 2-3 hero linen SKUs**: show the linen trouser, the linen dress, the linen shirt with names, prices, and percent off. Let the product carry the linen claim the subject made.
- 4. **Rewrite the body copy**: "Breathable styles to keep you cool" → "Garment-dyed linen that softens with every wash — 30% off through Sunday."
- 5. **Retire the creative**: the June 11 send already used this layout; this email needs genuinely fresh imagery to justify another open so soon.

## Technical Audit — Everlane "30% Off Linen Must-Haves"
**Source:** `support@everlane.com` | **ESP:** Klaviyo (confirmed by `static-forms.klaviyo.com` font CDN and `.kl-*` CSS classes)

---

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on Klaviyo's MJML-derived table layout with MSO/VML conditionals for Outlook. The truncated source exposes several rendering and compliance risks; a full-source audit is required to close out link, UTM, and merge-token findings.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Cannot fully audit** — source is truncated before `<body>` link elements. Full source required to verify click-tracking wrapper, redirect chain depth, and HTTPS enforcement on all hrefs.
- **Font asset request** — `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is an external HTTP request at render time. If Klaviyo's CDN is slow or blocked (corporate proxies, aggressive Gmail caching), the entire `<style>` block is delayed. This is a known delivery risk for `@import` inside `<style>` tags in HTML email — it is not fetched in parallel like a `<link>` tag would be.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>` tag** — `<title> </title>` (line 2). Some clients (Apple Mail, Outlook preview pane) surface the `<title>` value in the UI. A blank title degrades deliverability scoring on some spam filters that check for it.
- **Duplicate `img` rule block** — The stylesheet defines `img { border:0; line-height:100%; outline:none; text-decoration:none; -ms-interpolation-mode:bicubic }` twice (two separate rule blocks). The second block adds `height:auto; max-width:100%`. This is harmless in browsers but signals an unclean template merge — the first block should be removed or consolidated.
- **Conflicting responsive breakpoints** — `.hide` utility class is defined under `max-width: 600px`, while all layout column stacking (`.kl-row.colstack`, `.mj-column-per-100`) triggers at `max-width: 480px`. Elements using `.hide` will disappear 120px earlier than the layout reflows, creating a gap window (481–600px) where hidden elements vanish but columns haven't stacked yet.
- **`a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }`** — All link hover states are forced to black with no underline. On white backgrounds this makes hovered links visually indistinguishable from body text. Not a compliance issue, but a WCAG 1.4.1 (use of color) risk. Flagging as a rendering concern.
- **Alt text on images** — Cannot confirm from truncated source. Full audit required; missing `alt` attributes on product images fail WCAG 1.1.1 and degrade plain-text/image-blocked rendering.

---
