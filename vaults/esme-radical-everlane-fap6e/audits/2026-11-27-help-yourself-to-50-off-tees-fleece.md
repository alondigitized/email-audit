# Help Yourself to 50% Off Tees & Fleece
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's Black Friday day-of send, and it works. It finally gives the week a new offer, 50% off tees and fleece, "TODAY ONLY", on top of the 40% off everything that ran in the "Your Black Friday Reminder" and "40% Off Everything. Best-Sellers Are Going Fast" sends on 11/26. The grey sweatpants hero is a fresh creative after a week of quilted-jacket repeats. The white "50% OFF" on a mid-grey photo reads instantly.
- The weak spots are structural. The two-tier offer (50% on a category, 40% on everything else) is stacked in two separate hero blocks, so the second tier feels like an afterthought. "Plus 40% off everything else" is set over a busy sweater pile and loses contrast in the lower half. The body copy leans on "RWS-certified wool" and "recycled materials", which have nothing to do with the tees-and-fleece offer. There are only generic Shop Women / Shop Men buttons, with no specific product linked.
- Overall it's a solid, clear promo, with a better urgency hook than the earlier sends this week. It stops short of great because every CTA goes to a department rather than a product.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "BLACK FRIDAY 50% OFF TEES & FLEECE*" with a black "TODAY ONLY" tag. It's visible at the top of the email.
- The new sweatpants creative breaks the quilted-jacket fatigue of the 11/24 to 11/26 sends.
- The "Help Yourself" subject and the "Before the second helping…" body copy tie into the Thanksgiving to Black Friday weekend. This is the same food-and-gifting tone as "A Thanksgiving Treat: 40% Off Everything".
- The offer ladder is easy to scan: 50% on tees and fleece, 40% on the rest.
- Black full-width Shop Women / Shop Men buttons are unmissable and are repeated after the second block.

## What's weak

- The "PLUS 40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" text sits across a busy sweater pile and the contrast is weak, especially on the light stripe and cream knits.
- Both CTA rows say only "Shop Women / Shop Men". Neither names the offer or a product, so the copy is generic.
- The body copy talks about sweaters and recycled fleece, but the hero is tees and fleece. The sweater imagery supports the 40% offer, so the message mixes the two.
- The "Better Gift Guide" block and the long footer nav (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) dilute focus. The footer repeats the same links as the CTAs.
- The fine print says the offer is valid until 12/02, while the hero says "TODAY ONLY". That reads as contradictory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the CTAs by offer and name them: "Shop 50% Off Tees" and "Shop 50% Off Fleece" under the hero, "Shop 40% Off Everything" under the sweater block.
- 2. Fix or explain the deadline. Either state the real end time in the hero ("Ends tonight at 11:59 PST") or drop "TODAY ONLY" if the sale runs to 12/02.
- 3. Put a dark overlay or solid band behind the "PLUS 40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" text.
- 4. Show 3 to 4 actual tee and fleece products with prices, instead of one editorial sweatpants image.
- 5. Cut the footer navigation and gift guide, or move the gift guide above the footer links so there is one clear next step.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Tees & Fleece, Today Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today Only: Half Off Tees & Fleece + 40% Off the Rest`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off everything else. Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft tees, recycled fleece, and 40% off the rest. Before the second helping.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with tracked links through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run found one probe failure (a relative-path redirect on the "Everlane" link) and a missing `alt` on the tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from AgentMail relay capture limits, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated and 13 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect target (FAIL):** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8DAXB67HFX1AZ728RMK0SF2_0` (link text "Everlane") resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`, and the probe raised `unknown url type`.
  - The path has no scheme or host. This is most likely a relative `Location` header from the tracker, or a relative target that the probe could not resolve.
  - Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so real users probably land correctly. I can't confirm that from the data.
  - It should still be checked with a manual `curl -I`.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 25 links were probed. The other 13 (footer, social, utility) are unverified.
- No other broken links were observed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8DAXB67HFX1AZ728RMK0SF2` has no `alt`. This is low impact for an open pixel, but `alt=""` is the correct markup.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Mobile heading size:** the `@media (max-width:480px)` rule sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`, which is below the common ~11–14px legibility minimum on mobile.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is dropped by Gmail and several other clients. The stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is not a defect.
- **Other checks:** the Outlook handling (`mso` conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and the viewport meta are present.
- **Not assessed:** the truncated HTML means other images' alt text and any `role`/`lang` attributes were not assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not captured (WARN):** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and `Authentication-Results` were all not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not exposing them. This is unverified, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer not reviewed:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated HTML. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no failure was reported for it.
- **From address:** it is `support@everlane.com` (a support alias) while the sending and tracking domain is `send.everlane.com`. That is a normal setup, and SPF/DKIM alignment can't be verified without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Klaviyo params present:** the one resolved link carries Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid` parameters, plus a `cm_flow_i…` parameter (truncated), which identifies the campaign or flow. This is adequate for Klaviyo-side attribution.
- **No UTMs observed:** no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible data. This could be a truncation effect, so confirm whether GA attribution depends on them.
- **Landing alignment:** the subject promotes "Tees & Fleece", but the only resolved link lands on the generic `/pages/womens`. It is a header or logo link, so this is expected. The CTA links to the tees and fleece collections were not in the probed or visible set, so their alignment is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8DAXB67HFX1AZ728RMK0SF2_0` to check whether the redirect `Location` is relative. If it is, have the ESP or tracker emit absolute URLs.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `/o/01M8DAXB…` tracking pixel.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject line, or remove the whitespace-only title.
4. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px, or confirm `h4` isn't used for essential copy.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from a direct inbox copy instead of the AgentMail relay. Probe the 13 skipped links, including the footer.
6. Confirm whether `utm_*` parameters are expected on CTA links and add them if GA attribution is needed.
