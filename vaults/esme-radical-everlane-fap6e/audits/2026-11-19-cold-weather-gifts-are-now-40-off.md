# Cold-Weather Gifts Are Now 40% Off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday "40% off everything" send from Everlane in three days, and it is the weakest of them. The hero is a burgundy sweater beside a red knit hat on a tan scarf. "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is large and white, and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar sits underneath. The offer is clear and the eye lands on it first. But nothing here is new since the 2026-11-18 "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" and 2026-11-19 "Shop 40% Off Everything" sends.
- The subject promises "Cold-Weather Gifts." The body delivers one line of gift copy ("For the one who's always cold") under a generic sitewide hero. There are no gift products, no price points and no gift guide. The white subhead "Why not save more on already better prices?" is hard to read where it crosses the light tan and grey half of the image. The footer fine print also limits the offer to "select styles only," which cuts against "everything."
- Verdict: a competent template with a gift-themed subject on top and no gift content inside. A subscriber who has seen four near-identical sends this week has little reason to open this one.

## What's working

- "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is large and high-contrast, so the offer reads in about a second.
- The black SHOP NOW bar is full-width and unambiguous.
- The photo of knitwear in rich burgundy and red suits the season and the gifting angle.
- The expiry date (12/02/2026) is stated in the footer, and the exclusions are disclosed there too.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "Cold-Weather Gifts" but the body has no gift products, no gift guide and no price points.
- The white subhead "save more on already better prices" loses contrast over the light tan and grey side of the photo.
- This is the same offer in the same template for the fifth time in three days, so the cadence feels like fatigue.
- The footer says "select styles only," which contradicts "Everything" in the headline.
- The only product-specific copy is a single line of body text. There are no linked products, no sizing or fit detail, and no reviews.
- The post-hero navigation (Shop Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is a generic menu with no gift framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. Replace the generic hero with a gift-led layout: 3–4 linked cold-weather gift products with price points, such as a sweater, hat and boots, each showing the 40%-off price.
- 2. Move the real deadline into the hero. "Ends 12/2" currently sits in tiny grey type at the bottom.
- 3. Fix the subhead contrast with a darker overlay, or move the text onto the burgundy side of the image.
- 4. Change the CTA to "Shop Gifts 40% Off" and link it to a gift collection.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Fold this angle into one consolidated Black Friday send rather than a fifth near-copy.
- 6. Add a gifting-by-price row ("Under $100") to match how gift shoppers browse.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style template with standard MSO/dark-mode handling. The QA run found two link-probe failures, both on relative redirect targets, and one image without alt text, which is the open-tracking pixel. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the AgentMail relay. The 38% pass rate reflects probe and relay limitations more than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men"** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7TBCXCZTCYSQC421X3FJ3RD_4`): the redirect resolves to the relative path `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`. The probe reported `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Everlane"** (`…/l/01M7TBCXCZTCYSQC421X3FJ3RD_0`): same pattern, resolving to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`.
- **Likely cause:** The tracker's `Location` header is probably relative (or the probe didn't resolve it against the host). Relative `Location` values are valid under RFC 7231 and browsers resolve them. This is probably a probe limitation rather than a user-facing break. It is unconfirmed, though, because the destination HTTP status was never retrieved.
- **Coverage gap:** 12 links were probed and 6 skipped. Those 6 are untested, so the 38% pass rate shouldn't be read as the real link-health rate.
- **Tracking:** Click tracking goes through the first-party branded domain `trk.send.everlane.com`, which is good practice. The `_kx` and `bxid` parameters are Klaviyo identifiers. No other tracking problems are visible.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title> </title>` is empty** (whitespace only). This is minor, but it matters for web-view and screen-reader contexts.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** Only the `xmlns` attributes are present, and there is no `dir`. This is a real accessibility gap for screen readers.
- **Image missing alt text:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7TBCXCZTCYSQC421X3FJ3RD` is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel. It is a benign finding if the pixel is 1×1 or hidden, but add `alt=""` to be explicit.
- **Mobile heading size:** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` renders `<h4>` text at 10px on mobile. This is a legibility risk if `<h4>` is used for meaningful copy. The truncated source doesn't show where it's used.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is low.
- **Positives:** The source has a viewport meta, `X-UA-Compatible`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, and dark-mode selectors (`[data-ogsc]`).
- **Limit of this review:** The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the body markup, such as inline `alt` text on content images or button fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The subject line and the visible source contain no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Everlane sends through Klaviyo, which normally adds both headers.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay.
- **Not verifiable:** The QA data shows no result for the in-body unsubscribe link, and the physical postal address isn't in the truncated source.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support-role address used for a promotional send. This is not a compliance violation, but replies may go to a support queue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Redirect targets carry Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` (truncated) parameters. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the evidence. That may be because the URLs were truncated, or because attribution relies on `_kx` and Klaviyo, so it's not a confirmed gap.
- The two probed links land on `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` and `/pages/womens`. These are category pages rather than a dedicated "Cold-Weather Gifts" collection. I couldn't verify whether the 40% offer is reflected on those landing pages, because the probe never fetched them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the two failed links** with a resolver that handles relative `Location` headers, such as `urljoin` against the tracker host. Check that the final status is 200 and that the 40% offer shows on the landing page.
2. **Probe the 6 skipped links**, or at least confirm the unsubscribe link and its final status.
3. **Capture raw headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly from the sending ESP or a seed inbox. The relay can't be relied on for this.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, a meaningful `<title>`, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Check for `<h4>` usage in the body, and raise the mobile `<h4>` size above 10px if it carries content.
6. **Confirm the UTM or attribution scheme.** Check whether `utm_*` parameters are intentionally absent in favour of `_kx`.
7. **Tool fix:** Mark relative-redirect probe failures as "unresolved" rather than FAIL, so they don't lower the pass rate.
