# 40% Off Gifts They’ll Keep Wearing All Winter
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Black Friday "40% off everything" send from Everlane in about three days. It reuses the red knit hat and tan scarf hero from 2026-11-19's "Cold-Weather Gifts Are Now 40% Off", so it reads as a near-repeat. The offer is clear. "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" sits in large white type over the hat, and a black SHOP NOW bar follows directly below.
- The new angle is gifting. The subject and the line "For the one who's always cold" push gifts, and the "Better Gift Guide" block backs that up. But the hero says nothing about gifts, and the body is thin. The only product detail is "RWS-certified wool, winter coats, and boots", and no product is shown.
- Verdict: a clean, readable send, but the creative is stale and the gift hook is under-built. I'd call it a 6, in line with the 2026-11-19 sends.

## What's working

- The offer hierarchy is clear. "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" dominates the hero, and the black SHOP NOW bar sits right under it.
- The red hat on tan is warm and seasonal, and it suits a cold-weather gifting message.
- "For the one who's always cold" is a human, specific line that gives the gift framing a voice.
- The maroon Better Gift Guide block offers two distinct paths, "SHOP GIFTS FOR HIM" and "EXPLORE GIFT GUIDE".
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlapping text, or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- The hero is the same hat and scarf image used in the 2026-11-19 send, so it adds nothing for someone who has been getting these all week.
- The white text over the hat and scarf is busy, and "Why not save more on already better prices?" loses contrast in places.
- The gift guide block is large but empty. It has no products, only two small underlined links on a flat maroon field.
- The deadline (12/02/2026) appears only in the grey fine print. The body has no urgency, and "all winter" in the subject isn't a deadline.
- The hero CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW". The copy mentions sweaters, coats and boots, but none of them is linked or pictured.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the maroon text block with a 2x2 grid of real gift products at their 40%-off prices (sweater, coat, boots). This would lift the email most, because it turns an empty slot into click targets.
- 2. Put a credible deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Dec 2", instead of leaving it in the fine print.
- 3. Rotate the hero. Use a model in a gift-worthy sweater or coat so the creative differs from the 11-19 send.
- 4. Change the hero CTA to "SHOP GIFTS" or "SHOP SWEATERS" and link it to a gift landing page.
- 5. Add a social proof line, such as a star rating or "best-seller".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Klaviyo-built (MJML-style tables with a `trk.send.everlane.com` click domain). The automated probe flagged 2 of 12 probed links as errors. Those errors look like a probe limitation rather than a confirmed broken-link bug. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe errors, 2 links.** Both failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…'` and `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…'`. The affected tracked links are:
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7VAAQ6CN8X631PWJWEDNG4J_0` ("Everlane" logo link)
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7VAAQ6CN8X631PWJWEDNG4J_7` ("Shop Men")
- **Likely cause.** The tracker answers with a redirect whose `Location` is a relative path with no scheme or host. RFC 7231 permits this, and browsers resolve it correctly. The probe's HTTP client could not.
- **Severity.** This is probably a probe artifact, so I'm not asserting end-user breakage. It is still a low-severity robustness risk: some clients and link scanners (security gateways, preview bots) handle relative redirects poorly. A fully qualified `Location` (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`) would remove the risk.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 links were probed and 9 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. The 38% pass rate reflects this partial coverage and the failures above.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7VAAQ6CN8X631PWJWEDNG4J` is the open pixel. See section 3 for the alt-text flag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Open-pixel `<img>` has no alt text** (`src: …/o/01M7VAAQ6CN8X631PWJWEDNG4J`). This is a 1×1 tracker, so it should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Without the attribute, some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is only whitespace. This is minor, but some clients use it as a fallback label.
- **Mobile heading size.** `@media (max-width: 480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important } }` renders `h4` text at 10px on mobile. This is below common legibility guidance (about 14px or more) if `h4` is used for any meaningful copy. I couldn't confirm usage because the HTML is truncated.
- **Link styling.** `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes any non-color link affordance. Inline text links will be indistinguishable from body text. This only matters if there are inline links outside buttons. I couldn't confirm that.
- **Dark mode.** `[data-ogsc]` handling exists (`.ev-clay-dark` / `.ev-clay-light`), which is good practice. No issue found.
- **Web font.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial, so this is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated HTML and QA output show no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so it is worth checking against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results`** is absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment can't be assessed. Again this is a relay artifact. The sender is `support@everlane.com` while the click domain is `send.everlane.com`, which is a normal subdomain setup.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The probe always includes an unsubscribe link, and no failure was reported for it. The address and footer text fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The redirect targets carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). This is standard Klaviyo behavior.
- The truncated URLs prevent confirming whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` are present. I can't verify UTM completeness.
- The destinations (`/pages/womens`, `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`) are plausible for a gifting sale. Landing-page alignment with the 40% offer was not tested because the probe failed on those redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe with a redirect-tolerant client.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the tracker host, then re-test the two failed links and the 9 skipped ones. This will confirm whether these are real failures.
2. **Send a direct-delivery copy** (bypassing AgentMail) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. **Add `alt=""`** to the tracking pixel, and consider a non-empty `<title>`.
4. **Check the `h4` mobile size.** Confirm no meaningful copy is rendered at 10px.
5. **Pull full, untruncated link URLs** to verify UTM parameter completeness and the landing-page match.
6. **Everlane-side hardening:** have the tracker emit absolute redirect URLs.
