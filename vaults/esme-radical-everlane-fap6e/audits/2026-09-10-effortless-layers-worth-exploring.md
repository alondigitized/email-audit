- This is genuinely different from every other Everlane send this week — no peony/tulip macro, no "extra X% off" urgency bar, no discount messaging at all. It reads like a lookbook, not a markdown push.
- That's both its strength and its weakness. Visually it's the most competent, least fatigued email in the recent run — real hierarchy, a coherent story, no render bugs. - Strong, on-brand hero image (model on rocks at the beach) that's new creative, not recycled from the promo cadence.

- Clean 3x2 product grid with legible names (Forever Jersey Boxy Crew, Column Jean, etc.) — good category variety (tops + bottoms).

## What's weak

- No offer or discount anywhere — after a week of 20-25%-off sends, the sudden absence of any price incentive may read as a missed opportunity rather than a refreshing change.
- Subject "Effortless Layers Worth Exploring" is vague — no product, no urgency, no reason to open now.
