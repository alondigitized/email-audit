# The Merino Standard
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product send that gives people almost no reason to act today. The hero is a model in a cream Luxe Merino half-zip with "Better By Design" and a black SHOP NOW bar. The 3x3 sweater grid and the SHOP ALL SWEATERS button are tidy. But there are no prices, no product names and no reason to buy now.
- The only offer sits in the slate-blue "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS" block near the bottom. It is the same offer as the "Final Hours for Extra 25% Off" send earlier on 11/01 and the 10/29 and 10/30 sends. It also doesn't apply to the new merino sweaters the email is selling. The fine print says the offer ends 11/01 at 11:59 PM PST, so the email carries expiring-offer messaging with no urgency visible in the body.
- Verdict: it looks good and sells softly. A subscriber who got "Final Hours for Extra 25% Off" the same day will read this as the fourth Everlane email in a few days. The merino story is a good idea but needs a price and a reason to buy.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and premium, and the half-zip is clearly the star.
- The intro line "Setting the standard in merino sweaters... Perfect for gifting" gives a clear product story and a gift angle.
- The 3x3 grid shows range in colour and silhouette: zip, crew, V-neck, hoodie and cable knit.
- The two black CTA bars (SHOP NOW, SHOP ALL SWEATERS) are high-contrast and easy to find.
- The footer is clean and readable, with no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- The grid has no prices, names or fabric callouts, so nothing helps a shopper choose.
- The 25% markdown offer is buried below the grid and applies to markdowns, not the featured merino. It also reads as contradicting the "Better By Design" premium framing.
- "Limited Time Only" has no visible deadline in the body. The real end date is in grey footer fine print.
- "Better By Design" is vague and sits low on the hero, over the model's torso.
- The email repeats the same markdown block that appeared in the 10/29, 10/30 and earlier 11/01 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one price in the hero or intro, such as "Luxe Merino Half-Zip, $128". Without a price the email has nothing concrete to click on.
- 2. Add names and prices under the grid items, or shrink the grid to 3-4 hero products with prices.
- 3. Either move the 25% block up with a real deadline ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT") or drop it so the merino story stands alone.
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific, for example "SHOP THE HALF-ZIP", and link it to that product.
- 5. Add one proof point such as a review count or "best-selling" rating next to the half-zip.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Softer, sharper fit. Plus an extra 25% off markdowns through tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run found one failing link, where the tracked redirect resolves to a relative path. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers. The 43% pass rate reflects that one failure plus many unprobed or skipped links, not widespread breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Redirect resolves to a relative URL.**
  - Link: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M6CH7EVF3P1BC3H1SST4NMZK_0` (text "Everlane", likely the header logo).
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's response points to `/pages/womens?...` with no scheme or host.
  - A relative `Location` header is legal in HTTP, so this may be a probe limitation rather than a user-facing break.
  - If the tracker really does emit a host-less target, the logo click will 404 on `trk.send.everlane.com`.
  - Verify with `curl -sI` on the `_0` URL and check the `Location` header.
- **Two links were not probed** because of the 70s time budget. They are neither pass nor fail:
  - `…_6`
  - `…_30`
- **Coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 19 were skipped, so link health outside the probed set is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the open-tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6CH7EVF3P1BC3H1SST4NMZK` has no `alt`. This is a 1×1 beacon, so it's low severity. Add `alt=""` to keep it out of the accessibility tree.
- **Empty document title.** The source has `<title> </title>`. Screen readers and some clients show a blank title.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The root tag is `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`. This affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Viewport, Outlook conditionals and responsive media queries are present.** No rendering defects were found in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the remainder is unverified.

- **No `utm_*` parameters were visible** in the retrievable portion of the `_0` target. Because every link is wrapped in a tracker, final destinations and campaign attribution can't be verified for the unprobed links.
- **Landing page alignment can't be confirmed.** The only probed link that returned a target (`/pages/womens`) failed to resolve, so no landing page was verified for continuity.
