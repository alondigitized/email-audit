# Get Cozy While It’s 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-category sweater send. The subject, hero and grid all line up. A model in a zip-neck knit fills the top, with "Friends & Family / Ends 10.13" on the left and "30% Off Sitewide / Including Cozy Sweaters" on the right. A black "SHOP NOW" bar sits directly under it. The nine-sweater grid below reinforces the "cozy" promise. The creative is tidy and on-brand, and the offer is easy to find.
- It is also the same send Everlane has made all week. Compared with "Cozy Styles at 30% Off" (10/11) and "30% Off Better Cashmere" (10/11), the offer, hero treatment and grid are nearly identical. The only new beat is the "Better prices, still Everlane" band. A subscriber who has seen this 30% banner for eight days has no new reason to open.
- The deadline is the strongest part. "Ends 10.13" is credible, and it is the first email in the run with a deadline only two days away. The email doesn't push that urgency in the subject or the CTA copy. Overall it is solid but forgettable, a 7 on execution that loses points on novelty.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, human, on-season image. The model's knit and the zipper detail sell "cozy" immediately.
- The offer is clear: "30% Off Sitewide" with "Ends 10.13" directly beside it.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars are high-contrast and impossible to miss. One sits right under the hero and one under the grid.
- The nine-tile grid uses consistent flat-lay product shots on a neutral background, so it looks premium and scans fast.
- The subject, hero and grid all say "cozy sweater," so there is no creative mismatch (unlike the 10/11 "Better Cashmere" send).

## What's weak

- The hero text is white over a light taupe knit. "Including Cozy Sweaters" is hard to read against the sweater.
- The copy contradicts itself. The hero says "Sitewide," but the footer says "applies to select styles only." That undercuts the honesty of the offer.
- Both CTAs just say "SHOP NOW," and neither points to a specific product. The grid tiles show no names, prices or sale prices.
- The grid is nearly all navy and black. That is little visual variety, and there is no colour or new-arrival hook.
- There is no member or loyalty angle and no social proof such as reviews or best-seller flags.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the deadline in the hero and subject. "Ends tomorrow" is far more compelling than the same 30% banner repeated for eight days.
- 2. Add product names and "$X → $Y" sale prices under each grid tile. Make each tile its own link.
- 3. Fix the hero text contrast with a darker overlay or a text-box background, and move the offer text into a bolder lockup.
- 4. Change "SHOP NOW" to "Shop 30% Off Sweaters" so the CTA says what the click does.
- 5. Reconcile "Sitewide" with the "select styles" fine print, or tone the hero claim down.
- 6. Add one best-seller or review-count badge to build confidence.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo/MJML-based email with sound structure (viewport meta, MSO conditionals, mobile media queries). Its logo link redirects to a relative URL, and the HTML has accessibility gaps. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect is malformed.** The "Everlane Logo" link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4PS9YP09KXGHCMJM6WDXTP7_0` returned a redirect target of `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. It has no scheme or host (probe error: `unknown url type`).
  - A browser resolves a relative `Location` against the tracker host, so it would land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`. That is likely a 404 or dead page.
  - The probe may be stricter than a real client, so verify manually. This is the most important finding because the logo is a high-traffic link.
- **[WARN] Tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute.** The image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4PS9YP09KXGHCMJM6WDXTP7`, the open-tracking pixel. It is low severity, but `alt=""` is the correct value for a decorative or tracking image.
- Only 12 of 30 links were probed, so 18 are unverified, mostly footer and social links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>`), so screen readers can't select the right language.
- `<title> </title>` is blank, which hurts web-view and accessibility contexts.
- The global style `a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes any underline or colour distinction from links. Links inside body copy are then identifiable only by context, which fails WCAG 1.4.1 (use of colour).
- On mobile, `h4` is forced to `font-size:10px !important`, which is below a legible size if used for real content.
- The custom font loads through `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/…/custom_fonts.css)`. `@import` is stripped in many clients, but the stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is minimal.
- No other rendering problems are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed and shouldn't be treated as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check it on raw message headers.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. That is also expected via the relay.
- The truncated source doesn't show the physical postal address or the in-body unsubscribe link, so neither can be confirmed or faulted.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking uses Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) behind the `trk.send.everlane.com` redirector. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the sampled URL. They may be appended further along or applied at redirect, so this can't be confirmed from the truncated data.
- The logo-link redirect failure (section 2) is also a continuity failure: the click would not reach `/pages/womens` on the main site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4PS9YP09KXGHCMJM6WDXTP7_0` redirect manually. If it really sends a relative `Location`, fix the Klaviyo link or tracking domain config so it emits an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…`.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and give `<title>` meaningful text.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Restore a visible underline or colour on in-body text links, and avoid the 10px mobile `h4` for substantive content.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the raw message received directly, bypassing the relay.
6. Confirm the `utm_*` parameters, the footer address and the unsubscribe link against the full, untruncated HTML.
