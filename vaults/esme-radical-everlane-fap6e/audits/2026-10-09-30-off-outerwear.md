# 30% Off Outerwear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth 30% Friends & Family send in four days, and it's the first one that narrows to a category. The subject is concrete and the grid of nine jackets backs it up. It follows "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6), "30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family" (10/7) and "30% Off Better Sweaters" (10/8). That sweater send is the closest sibling, and this email is its outerwear twin.
- The hero is attractive, but the offer text is its weak point. "30% Off Sitewide / Including Outerwear" is white type over a busy photo. It sits on the model's white top and on the blurred background, so "Sitewide" and "Outerwear" lose contrast. The headline offer is the least legible thing in the hero. The black SHOP NOW bar below it is clear.
- The body is clean and shoppable: a nine-tile grid, a seasonal line ("kind of brick outside"), and an extra 20% off markdowns block. But the grid has no names, prices or per-product links I can see. Three SHOP NOW buttons all appear to lead to the same general place. The email is polished but generic, and it adds little over the 10/7 send. Judgement: solid, seasonal, under-differentiated. The cadence is the real risk.

## What's working

- The subject names the offer and the category, and the body delivers outerwear.
- The nine-tile grid of jackets in warm earth tones is cohesive and easy to scan.
- The seasonal copy ("days and nights when it's kind of brick outside") has a clear brand voice and fits the date.
- The extra 20% off markdowns block gives a second reason to click, with split Women's and Men's sale links.
- The fine print is legible and gives the expiry date (10/13/2026).

## What's weak

- The white "30% Off Sitewide / Including Outerwear" text over the photo has weak contrast. The key offer is hard to read.
- The grid has no product names, prices or sale-price callouts, so "30% off" never turns into a concrete number.
- Three generic SHOP NOW buttons give no category-specific verb, such as "Shop Jackets".
- The deadline is only in the fine print. There is no visible urgency in the body, though the offer ends 10/13.
- It's the fourth near-identical 30% send in four days, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put the offer on a solid band or a darkened overlay so "30% Off Outerwear" reads instantly. Move the deadline up into the hero.
- 2. Label the grid with product names and "from $X" sale prices, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with "Shop Women's Jackets" and "Shop Men's Jackets", and add a bestseller or review callout.
- 4. Put "Ends 10/13" in the body. A visible deadline would also separate this send from the previous three.
- 5. Cut cadence. Merge sweaters and outerwear into one send, or space the sends out.

## 1. Technical Summary

The compliance and authentication warnings look like relay-capture limits rather than confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and merge-token checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  - The redirect resolved to a relative path with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header, so this may be a probe limitation rather than a user-facing break. I can't confirm either way from the data given.

- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4GNJ5HSP4N8SDZNYDD2DXFZ` has no `alt` (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the QA check flagged the `/o/…` open-tracking pixel. This is low impact, but an empty `alt=""` is cleaner for screen readers.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Link styling:** the `<style>` block sets `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` to `color:#000;text-decoration:none`. Inline links will not be distinguishable from body text by color or underline. This fails WCAG 1.4.1 wherever links sit inside running text.
- **Small mobile text:** `h4` drops to `font-size:10px !important` under 480px, which is hard to read.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/…/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. Fallback stacks (Arial, Helvetica) are declared, so the impact is minor.
- **Working as intended:** the viewport meta, `X-UA-Compatible`, MSO conditionals (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and the mobile stacking media queries are all present.
