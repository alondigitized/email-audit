# Final Hours: 30% Off Sitewide & Storewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand closing-day send, but it's the second "ends tonight" email Everlane has sent today. The earlier one, "Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sitewide," used almost the same copy. The hero is a three-model street-style shot. "Final Hours for Friends & Family" and "30% Off Sitewide" sit in small white type over a busy concrete wall, so they have little contrast. The offer is readable but doesn't command the frame the way the dark, moody hero in the earlier send did.
- Below the hero is a tidy four-block grid: sweaters, denim, outerwear and tops. It sells the fall wardrobe, and each block has a "Shop ___" link. The weak spots are the body copy and the fine print. "Don't miss out on this offer." is filler that restates nothing. The footer says the offer applies to "select styles only," which undercuts the "Sitewide" headline.
- Verdict: competent execution, low marginal value. A subscriber who has seen 30% Friends & Family all week learns nothing new here. The only new element is the "Final Hours" label, and it has no clock or exact time to back it up.

## What's working

- The 30% offer is stated once, in the hero, with no competing promo.
- The black "Shop Women / Shop Men" split bar is unambiguous and easy to tap.
- The product grids are clean, evenly spaced and seasonal (knits, denim, jackets), with consistent flat-lay styling.
- Each category has its own underlined "Shop ___" link, so there are several relevant entry points.
- The layout is simple and has no visible render bugs: no broken images, overlap or gaps.

## What's weak

- The hero offer text is small and low-contrast (white on light concrete), so it doesn't dominate the hero.
- There is no visible deadline or countdown. The real cutoff, 11:59 PM PST, is only in the fine print.
- The "Don't miss out on this offer." line is generic and adds nothing.
- "Sitewide" in the headline conflicts with "select styles only" in the footer, which feels like bait-and-switch.
- The grid shows no prices, no "after 30% off" figures, and no best-sellers or reviews.
- It's a near-repeat of "Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sitewide," sent the same day, so it adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer and deadline the hero: large, high-contrast type reading "30% off ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT." Add a live countdown if possible. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Don't send two "ends tonight" emails the same day. Merge them, or make this one a genuinely last-chance reminder aimed at people who haven't clicked.
- 3. Replace "Don't miss out on this offer." with something concrete, such as "30% off sweaters, denim and outerwear through 11:59 PM PT."
- 4. Show sale prices ("$X → $Y") under the grid products, and add a best-seller or review badge to at least one tile.
- 5. Reconcile "Sitewide" with the "select styles" disclaimer. Use "most styles," or name the exclusions in the body.
- 6. Make a primary CTA for the hero offer, such as "Shop 30% off," rather than only splitting by gender.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends at 11:59 PM: 30% off everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: sweaters & denim 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Friends & Family closes tonight. Knits, denim and outerwear included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 30% off expires at midnight PT. Here's what's worth grabbing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email sent through Everlane's tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The only hard QA failures are two tracked links whose redirect target is a relative path, which is probably a probe artifact. The compliance and authentication findings can't be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Two links return "unknown url type"** (the "Everlane" logo link and the hero image link, `Image | Final Hours for Friends & Family | 30% Off Sitewide`):
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4VG7YA2YMMTTVVPY9EHCZ44_0`
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4VG7YA2YMMTTVVPY9EHCZ44_1`
  - Both redirect to the relative path `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`, with no scheme or host.
  - A relative `Location` header is permitted by RFC 7231, and browsers resolve it against the tracker's host. If the tracker host doesn't serve `/pages/womens`, the redirect would 404 in a real client.
  - The checker can't resolve it either way. Treat this as unconfirmed: re-test with a client that resolves relative redirects, and confirm where `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens` actually lands.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt text** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4VG7YA2YMMTTVVPY9EHCZ44`). This is the open-tracking beacon, so impact is minimal. An `alt=""` would be correct for a decorative image.
- Only 12 of 35 links were probed, so the other 23 (footer and social) are unverified.
- The overall QA pass rate is 38%. The two failures account for most of that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only).
- The `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` styles are all `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Text links have no color or underline to tell them apart from body text, and there is no hover or visited feedback.
- On mobile (`max-width: 480px`), `h4` is forced to `font-size: 10px !important`. That is below common legibility guidance, if h4 carries user-facing text.
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Fonts fall back to the `Arial, "Helvetica Neue"` stack, which is declared, so this is low risk.

## 5. Compliance

- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- Both probed links go to `/pages/womens`, and the offer is "30% Off Sitewide & Storewide." If these are the only primary CTAs, the site-wide offer lands on a women's-only page. The rest of the CTAs weren't visible to check.
