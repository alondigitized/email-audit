**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-product denim send, and it's the first Everlane email this week with a real price cut. The offer is previously $148, now $88. Everlane spent 09-27 through 09-29 on the 30%-off sweater run, then sent "New Jackets" and "Back to Alpaca" with no offer. This one brings a concrete discount back with a fresh hero.
- The execution is tidy. It has a full-bleed model shot, a black Shop Now bar, then fit detail, a customer quote and an organic-cotton note. ## What's working

- The hero is a strong editorial shot of the jean, with a full-width black "SHOP NOW" bar directly under it.
- "Previously $148, now $88" is a specific, honest price comparison, about 40% off.
- The fit copy ("A high rise. A straight leg. Premium organic cotton with just enough stretch.") answers the main denim questions quickly.
- The Katharine A. review adds social proof in a short, credible quote.
- The product focus is a welcome change after a week of repeated 30%-off sweater creative.

## What's weak

- The $88 price is small, centered body text below the CTA. The hero shows only "The Way-High® Jean", so the offer isn't visible where the eye lands first.
- There's no deadline or urgency anywhere. After "Ends Tonight" and "Few Hours Left" on 09-28 and 09-29, this reads flat by comparison.
- "Well several actually" is vague. It doesn't say which washes or fits are included, so the reader has to click to find out.
- There are five CTAs ("SHOP NOW" twice, "SHOP ALL DENIM", "READ MORE", plus nav links). The organic-cotton block and its READ MORE button pull attention away from the purchase.
- The reviewer has no rating or star count, and there's no size or fit range. The pricing and fine print are tiny and light grey at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "$88" (with "was $148") directly on the hero image or in a headline above the fold. The price is the strongest thing the email has.
- 2. Add a credible end date, such as "Through Sunday", to recreate the urgency the brand used on the sweaters.
- 3. Name the styles in the copy, for example "Way-High® in 3 washes, sizes 23–34".
- 4. Add a star rating next to the quote, and change the second "SHOP NOW" to something specific like "Shop the Way-High® Jean".
- 5. Shrink or cut the organic-cotton READ MORE block, or move it below the footer navigation, so the second half of the email stays on the purchase.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-built template sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. Two probed links failed with `unknown url type`, which most likely means the tracker redirects to a relative path. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect targets (2 FAIL).** Both links below returned `unknown url type: '/pages/...'`, so the tracker's redirect resolved to a path with no scheme or host:
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3T1BKXSW7S45SRHH6AB0A82_15` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") → `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…`
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3T1BKXSW7S45SRHH6AB0A82_0` ("Everlane Logo") → `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`
- **Probable cause.** The tracker likely sends a relative `Location` header, or the destination base URL is missing in the Klaviyo link config. Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so real clicks may still work. The QA prober did not resolve it, so these two failures are unconfirmed, not proven broken for subscribers.
- **Consequence if it is real.** The logo and SMS sign-up links would fail on clients or scanners that don't resolve relative redirects. Sign-up for texts is a conversion link.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 25 links were probed and 13 were skipped as footer or social. The 38% pass rate reflects the small probed set plus these failures and the warnings, so it is not a reliable indicator of overall link health.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The image with `src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M3T1BKXSW7S45SRHH6AB0A82"` has no `alt`. The `/o/` path indicates this is the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. This is low severity.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor, but some webmail and "view in browser" contexts show it.
- **Small mobile heading size.** The `@media (max-width: 480px)` rule sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`. If `h4` is used for visible copy, 10px is below readable minimums. I can't tell whether `h4` is used in the body, because the source is truncated.
- **Web font import.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. Font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is limited.
- No other issues found in the visible source. The MSO conditionals, `mj-outlook-group-fix`, and the viewport meta tag are present.
