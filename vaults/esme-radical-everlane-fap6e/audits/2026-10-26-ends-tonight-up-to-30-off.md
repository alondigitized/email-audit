# Ends Tonight: Up to 30% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a last-chance reminder for the Fall Essentials Event, and it is the most stripped-down send in the recent run. The hero is a blank dusk gradient with no product and no model. The only content is "ENDS TONIGHT / FALL ESSENTIALS EVENT" on the left and "UP TO 30% OFF — SWEATERS, OUTERWEAR & MORE" on the right. The offer and the deadline are clear. Nothing in the email makes you want a particular piece.
- Everlane has sent this same 30% event several times in three days: "Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters" and "30% Off: All New Alpaca" on 10/24, then two non-sale sends on 10/25. A subscriber has already seen the products and the reviews. Urgency is a fair reason to resend, but the email gives no product, no review and no price to act on.
- Verdict: a credible deadline and a clear offer in an empty container. It will get opens from people already planning to buy. It gives hesitant buyers little reason to click. The email feels like a placeholder for a creative that wasn't finished.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" in the hero, the subject and the body copy repeats the deadline consistently.
- The offer is stated plainly: "UP TO 30% OFF" with the categories named (sweaters, outerwear).
- The black "SHOP BEFORE THEY'RE GONE" bar is high-contrast and obviously a button.
- The fine print gives an exact deadline, 10/26/2026 at 11:59 PM PST, so the urgency is believable.
- The layout is calm and uncluttered, and it fits Everlane's minimalist brand.

## What's weak

- There is no product anywhere. The hero is a sky gradient, so nothing shows what's on sale.
- The white thin-weight hero text has low contrast against the pale blue-gray sky. "UP TO 30% OFF" is the key message and it is hard to read at a glance.
- The hero offer text sits in the middle of the image rather than at the top, so the eye has no clear entry point.
- The nav links (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) send the reader to generic category pages. There is no featured item, review or price anchor.
- Everlane's 10/24 alpaca send used customer reviews, which are credible. This one uses no social proof at all.
- The footer icons (gift icon for "Find a Store", chat bubble for "Sign Up for Texts") are generic and pull attention away from the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put product in the hero. Use a model in a sweater or coat, or a short "Top 6 going fast" grid with prices. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Increase the text contrast. Make "UP TO 30% OFF" bold, larger and placed in the upper third of the hero, over a darker part of the image.
- 3. Add one proof point near the CTA, such as a review quote or "Most-loved: Alpaca Crew, 2,500+ reviews", linking to that product.
- 4. Add a real countdown or "ends 11:59 PM PT" line near the CTA, not just in the fine print.
- 5. Replace "SHOP BEFORE THEY'RE GONE" with something more specific, like "Shop Sweaters & Outerwear".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML build sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The QA probe found two tracked links ("Shop Men" and "Everlane") whose redirects resolve to a bare relative path, which fails the probe. The automated pass rate is 38%. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two hard link failures.** Both probes failed with `unknown url type`, and the URL shown is a host-less path:
  - "Shop Men": `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=yTMOVl1X…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCK…`, probed via `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5WE7NVZHXK0BSD9W9YJMZFR_4`.
  - "Everlane" (likely the header logo): `/pages/womens?_kx=yTMOVl1X…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`, probed via `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5WE7NVZHXK0BSD9W9YJMZFR_0`.
  - The tracker's redirect target has no scheme or host. Everlane's tracker may be returning a relative `Location` header, or the destination may be stored without its domain.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host. That would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/collections/...`, which probably returns a 404.
  - I can't tell from this data whether real clients hit the same failure or whether it is a probe artifact. It needs a manual click-through.
- **Link coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 6 were skipped, so the other links are unverified. The 38% pass rate is also dragged down by the INFO-level warnings being counted.
- **Tracking parameters are present.** Parameters seen: `_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`. They are Klaviyo/Bluecore-style identifiers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is blank, which hurts web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Missing alt text.** The QA check flagged `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5WE7NVZHXK0BSD9W9YJMZFR`. The `/o/` path is the open-tracking pixel, so this is low severity. Give it `alt=""`.
- **Custom font via `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, which is acceptable.
- **Dark mode.** `[data-ogsc]` rules are included, but only Outlook.com uses that selector. There is no `prefers-color-scheme` handling in the visible portion.
- Outlook fixes (`mso` conditionals, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present. No other rendering defects were confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `_kx` parameter is a per-recipient Klaviyo identifier and is populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw `.eml` from a direct send.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified for the same reason.
- The footer, physical address and visible unsubscribe link were not visible because the HTML was truncated. The QA probe says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and no unsubscribe failure was reported.
- The sender is `support@everlane.com`, which is the same domain as the tracking host `send.everlane.com`. That alignment is good.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible link evidence. Only `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` were seen. If analytics rely on UTMs, they may be applied at redirect time, which can't be confirmed here.
- Because of the two failing redirects, the destinations ("Shop Men" to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, "Everlane" to `/pages/womens`) could not be verified. A mismatch is possible, since the logo links to a women's page while the CTA is for men.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually click `…WE7NVZHXK0BSD9W9YJMZFR_4` and `…_0`. If they land on `trk.send.everlane.com/collections/…` or return a 404, fix the destination URLs to absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` in the Klaviyo template.
2. Capture the raw message headers from a direct send, not through the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, address and the 6 skipped links.
6. Confirm that UTM or equivalent campaign tagging reaches the landing pages.
