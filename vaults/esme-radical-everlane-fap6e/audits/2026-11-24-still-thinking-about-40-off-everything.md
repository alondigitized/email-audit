# Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday creative Everlane has sent all week, now with a reminder subject. The hero is the familiar model in a tan jacket and burgundy scarf, with "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type and a Shop Women / Shop Men split bar. The subject, "Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?", promises a nudge. The body only adds one line: "Still thinking it over? Take another look at sweaters…". It echoes the subject but gives no reason to act today.
- The layout is clean and the offer is obvious. The 2x2 category grid (sweaters, outerwear, denim, accessories) is easy to scan. But nothing here is new. There's no deadline in the body, no hero product, and no social proof, and the real end date (12/02/2026) is buried in fine print. After the 11-22 "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything You'll Wear" send and the 11-24 "Your Favorite Fits Are 40% Off" send, this is the third or fourth near-identical promo in three days.
- Verdict: a competent abandonment-style nudge with no payload. It's fine as a reminder, but it won't beat the earlier, more product-led denim sends.

## What's working

- The 40% off headline is huge and readable over the hero, so the offer is clear immediately.
- The black Shop Women / Shop Men bar is an unambiguous CTA, and it sits right under the hero.
- The 2x2 category grid has clean product shots on a neutral background, which makes it easy to scan.
- The subject and the "still thinking it over?" line work together as a soft reminder tone.
- The burgundy gift guide block adds a seasonal second path with her/him splits.

## What's weak

- The creative is a repeat of the hero used in earlier Black Friday sends, so there's no new reason to come back.
- The category links are text labels under the images ("SHOP SWEATERS"). They are small and weak as CTAs, and they point to categories rather than specific products.
- The "Still thinking it over?" copy is generic. There are no reviews, no scarcity, and no deadline in the body.
- The end date (12/02/2026) is only in tiny grey fine print. The asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" hides the real terms.
- The big lifestyle photo of three models has no link or copy, so it takes up a lot of vertical space without a job.

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Dec 2" or "Last chance," so "still thinking" becomes urgency.
- 2. Replace the generic category grid with 3-4 specific bestsellers. Add review stars or a customer quote, as the 11-23 "40% Off Better Denim" send did, along with the sale price.
- 3. Drop or shrink the unlinked lifestyle photo. Use that space for social proof or a "why now" line.
- 4. Make the category links proper buttons.
- 5. Vary the hero from the prior sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, responsive HTML template with a proper viewport meta tag and a media-query structure. The automated QA flagged one failed link probe, a missing alt attribute on the tracking pixel, and three header checks that could not be verified. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body markup were not available for inspection.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link probe error** on the "Everlane" link (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M87767FBSBAM2WTDV1JXR0TJ_0`). The probe reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker answered with a relative `Location` redirect (`/pages/womens?...`). The probe could not resolve it to an absolute URL.
  - This is probably a probe or redirect-handling limitation, not proof that real users get a broken link. Browsers resolve relative redirects fine.
  - It still deserves a manual check. Emitting a relative `Location` from a click tracker is non-standard, and some clients or security scanners may mishandle it.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 27 links were probed (15 skipped), so most footer and utility links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M87767FBSBAM2WTDV1JXR0TJ` is an open-tracking pixel and has no `alt`. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text** on the open-tracking image (`/o/01M87767…`).
  - It is a 1×1 pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` (and `role="presentation"`) so screen readers skip it.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source shows `<title> </title>`. This is minor, but some clients use it for the preview or accessibility name.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html>` carries only `xmlns` attributes and no `lang` attribute. This is confirmed in the visible source. Screen readers cannot pick a pronunciation language.
- **Positives:** `viewport` meta is present, the MSO conditional comments and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present, and `@media (max-width:480px)` rules are present.
- **Not assessable:** the CSS and body are truncated, so I can't verify per-image alt text, button markup or contrast.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source or subject line. The truncation limits coverage.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message at the source.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Verify with a direct-delivery test, not through the relay.
- **Footer not inspected:** the physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion. The QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result isn't reported separately.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is the sender for promotional mail. That isn't a defect, but it differs from the `trk.send.everlane.com` tracking subdomain, so check alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination parameters visible on the redirect are Klaviyo's (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appear in the retrievable portion, but the string is cut off, so absence isn't confirmed.
- The "Everlane" logo link lands on `/pages/womens`, not a page tied to the "40% off everything" offer in the subject line. Whether the sitewide sale page was the intended target can't be determined from the data.
- Click paths beyond this one weren't validated, since the other links weren't resolved in the data given.

1. Re-test `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M87767FBSBAM2WTDV1JXR0TJ_0` with a client that resolves relative redirects (`curl -IL`). Ask the ESP whether the tracker should return an absolute `Location`.
