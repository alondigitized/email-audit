## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-style build, with every link wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The source was truncated and the relay captured no headers, so the compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive. The only confirmed code-level defect is the empty `<title>` plus a missing `alt` on the tracking pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links were confirmed.** Of 12 material links, 10 were skipped. Four returned HTTP 429 and four more were unprobed, so none of these is a pass or a fail.
  - 429 on `.../01M6VF8TSHKYYM5PWBYKRRBWD1_2` ("Shop Now"), `_3` ("Shop Sweaters"), `_4` ("Shop Outerwear") and `_5` ("Shop Denim"). This is the tracker rate-limiting the probe, not a defect in the email.
  - Unprobed after the 70s budget: `_0`, `_1`, `_6`, `_7`, `_12`, and others.
  - Final destinations were never resolved, so destination health is unverified.
- **All links are indirect.** Every link is wrapped as `trk.send.everlane.com/l/<id>_<n>`, so the destination URLs, including any UTM parameters, can't be seen in the source.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6VF8TSHKYYM5PWBYKRRBWD1`. This is expected, but see section 3 for the `alt` attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel (confirmed by QA).** The pixel at `/o/01M6VF8TSHKYYM5PWBYKRRBWD1` has no `alt` attribute. Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` so screen readers don't announce the URL. This is low severity.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`. This is low severity, since most clients ignore it, but it affects web-view and archive rendering.
- **Link-state colors are overridden to `#000`.** The `a:not([name])`, `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` rules all set `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Links therefore have no visual affordance outside of buttons, and hover and visited states are indistinguishable. Whether this is a real problem depends on the rendered body, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- **Image-link alt text:** the probe labels some links "Image | Shop Sweaters" and similar. I could not confirm in the truncated source whether those are `alt` text or generated labels, so I'm not flagging them.
- **`@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)`** is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is handled gracefully and is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects.**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
  - QA notes the AgentMail relay likely strips these headers. Verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy before treating these as failures. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this is worth checking.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown. Neither the unsubscribe link nor the postal address could be confirmed in the truncated source.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is used for a promotional send. It's not a violation, but replies would land in a support queue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. Click-tracker redirects hide the destination URLs, and no link was resolved. Neither UTM parameters nor landing page alignment with "30% Off Cult Favorites" (Sweaters, Outerwear, Denim) could be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with a slower rate or a retry/backoff on 429, and follow redirects to capture final URLs and UTM parameters. This will clear the 4 rate-limited and 8 unprobed links.
2. Check the unsubscribe and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message headers, not the relay copy.
3. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel and a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Store the full, untruncated HTML so merge tokens, the footer address and the unsubscribe link can be audited.
5. Add a known-relay allowance to the QA checks, so header warnings on AgentMail sends are flagged as "unverifiable" rather than "missing".
