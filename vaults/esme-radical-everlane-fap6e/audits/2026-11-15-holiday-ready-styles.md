# Holiday-Ready Styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy but generic send. The subject promises "Holiday-Ready Styles". The email opens with a plain "New Arrivals" header over a 3x3 grid of nine knitwear flat-lays: cardigans, striped sweaters, a zip-neck and a tie-front long sleeve. It's clean and on-brand, but nothing in the body says "holiday", gift or occasion. There is no price, no offer and no deadline.
- The grid has no hero, so the eye has nowhere to land. All nine products are the same size, none is named, and only one "SHOP NOW" button sits below them. The second half of the email is a sustainability block with a "READ MORE" button. That is a brand-values message in the middle of a mid-November gifting window, and it competes with the product CTA.
- Compared with Everlane's recent sends, this is weaker. "Everyone Deserves This Half-Zip" (11-13, 7/10) had a named hero and a specific CTA. "Keep the Fleece" (11-12, 7/10) had real prices. This one is closer to the "Holiday Dresses" and "New Accessories" sends, which were attractive but gave no reason to act now. Verdict: competent catalog filler, middling at best.

## What's working

- The grid is clean and consistent: neutral grey backdrop, even spacing, no render bugs.
- The knitwear palette (cream, navy, taupe, stripes) fits mid-November.
- "SHOP NOW" is a high-contrast black button, easy to find and tap.
- Everlane's minimal branding and the footer navigation (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) are consistent with the brand's usual sends.

## What's weak

- The subject says "Holiday-Ready" but the header says "New Arrivals", and nothing in the body mentions holiday, gifting or occasions.
- There is no price, offer, free-shipping line or deadline anywhere in the email.
- Nine products with no names, prices or individual links give the reader no hero and no reason to pick one.
- The sustainability block with "READ MORE" pulls attention away from shopping.
- The "SHOP NOW" copy is generic, and the footer repeats "New Arrivals", which adds nothing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real hero. Lead with one named product, such as the cream cardigan, with a price, and shrink the grid to 4–6 priced items linking to their own product pages.
- 2. Make the header match the subject: "Holiday-Ready Knits" or "Layers for Every Gathering". Add a gifting or deadline cue such as "Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery".
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, like "SHOP HOLIDAY KNITS". Move the sustainability block below the product content, or cut it from promo sends.
- 4. Link each product tile to its own page.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email (Maison Neue custom fonts, `kl-` classes) with all links routed through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The two QA failures are most likely probe artifacts, not confirmed breakage. The header warnings are unverifiable because the AgentMail relay may have stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures, same error.** Both read `unknown url type: '/pages/...'`.
  - `"Icon | Sign Up for Texts"` → `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7G1VRTFF1PRAB6T8JWVVBW7_18` resolves to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…`
  - `"Everlane"` (logo) → `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7G1VRTFF1PRAB6T8JWVVBW7_0` resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…`
- **Likely cause.** The tracker appears to return a relative `Location` header (path only, no scheme or host). Browsers and mail clients resolve that against the tracker host, but the QA probe's HTTP client doesn't. This is probably a tooling limitation, so I can't confirm user-facing breakage.
- **Open question.** If the tracker's relative redirect resolves against `trk.send.everlane.com` rather than `everlane.com`, clicks would 404. Verify with `curl -I` on both tracking URLs and check that the final host is `www.everlane.com`.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 28 links were probed. The 16 skipped (footer and social) are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7G1VRTFF1PRAB6T8JWVVBW7` is the open pixel and is functioning as expected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is empty in the source. This is minor, but it affects the browser-tab and "view in browser" title.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixel.** The QA flag for "Image missing alt text" targets the open-tracking pixel (`/o/01M7G1VRTFF1PRAB6T8JWVVBW7`). This is not a real accessibility defect for a 1×1 pixel. Setting `alt=""` would silence the check.
- **Small mobile heading.** The `h4` rule sets 10px on mobile (`@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size:10px !important } }`). It is only an issue if `h4` is used for readable content, and the truncated HTML doesn't show where it's used.
- **Font fallbacks.** The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in many clients. Stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is acceptable.
- **Outlook support.** Present (MSO conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are not found. The QA tool itself notes the relay may not capture them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation, and the raw message headers are needed to confirm. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results.** Missing. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Check `send.everlane.com` DNS records directly.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing and reports no failure for it. The physical postal address can't be verified because the footer is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen.** Redirect targets carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the truncated QA strings, so I can't confirm or rule out UTM tagging.
- **Logo link destination.** The logo ("Everlane") link points to `/pages/womens`, not the site root. This is a landing-page-alignment note for a header link, not an error.
- **Probe coverage.** Final landing pages weren't verified because of the probe failures above.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI` on the two failing tracker URLs to confirm the `Location` header and final host. If it is relative, ask Klaviyo/Everlane to emit absolute redirects, or fix the QA probe to resolve `Location` against the request URL (`urljoin`).
2. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` rather than relying on relay-captured headers.
3. Exclude the `/o/` tracking pixel from the alt-text check, or recognize `1x1` and `width="1"` images, to stop false positives that drag down the 38% pass rate.
4. Have the sender add content to `<title>`.
5. Confirm UTM tagging on the final landing URLs once redirects resolve.
