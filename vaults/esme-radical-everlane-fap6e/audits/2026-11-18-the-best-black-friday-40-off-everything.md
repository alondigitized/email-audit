# The Best Black Friday: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Black Friday send with one clear message: "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type over a cream V-neck cashmere sweater. The eye lands on the offer first, and a black SHOP WOMEN bar sits right under the hero. Nothing is broken, and the four-tile grid below (sweaters, outerwear, denim, shoes) is tidy and easy to scan.
- The problem is repetition. On 2026-11-17 Everlane sent "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" with the same offer and a nearly identical subject. Today's "The Best Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" is a reskin of that, and "The Best" is an empty superlative. "Black Friday starts now" also reads oddly when the previous send already said it started. The hero is women-only, and the only deadline (12/02) is buried in grey fine print. It's competent and forgettable.

## What's working

- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" hero is huge and legible, and the discount is the first thing you see.
- There are two clear black CTA bars, SHOP WOMEN and SHOP THE SALE, with the same styling as earlier sends.
- The four category tiles (sweaters, outerwear, denim, shoes) fit the season and give several entry points.
- The email is short and fast to scan, with nothing broken or overlapping.
- The body copy mentions cashmere, organic denim and gifting, which fits Everlane's values-led voice.

## What's weak

- It's the second near-identical "40% Off Everything" send in two days, following the 11/17 one, so it feels like cadence filler.
- The only deadline (12/02, 11:59 PM PST) is in the footer fine print, so the sale has no visible urgency.
- The hero and the first CTA are women-only. A male recipient has to scroll to the small "Shop Men" text link in the nav list.
- The asterisk on "Everything" points to "select styles" in tiny grey text, which undercuts the headline claim.
- The tiles show no prices, product names or reviews, so there is no reason to prefer one click over another.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add the deadline to the hero and the CTA, for example "Ends 12/2" or "40% off through Cyber Monday", and move it out of the footer. This single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Differentiate from the 11/17 send. Make this one an escalation or a new beat, such as "Early access ends tonight" or a spotlight on the $48 cashmere tee at its sale price.
- 3. Add a SHOP MEN CTA beside SHOP WOMEN, or split the hero in two.
- 4. Put sale prices or "from $X" under each category tile.
- 5. Drop "The Best" from the subject and lead with the concrete detail.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% Off Everything Ends Dec 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cashmere, Denim, Boots: All 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters from $48, plus outerwear, denim and shoes. Sale ends Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best Black Friday offer is live. Free returns on every order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup, tracked via `trk.send.everlane.com`) has two QA link failures. Both look like a redirect-resolution problem in the probe rather than confirmed user-facing breakage. The compliance and auth warnings come from header capture gaps, so they are unverified. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most CTAs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7P5KMCBSV6ASKCJ6JJZJ0M7_0` resolves to a relative path, `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. The probe error was `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link:** `…/l/01M7P5KMCBSV6ASKCJ6JJZJ0M7_9` resolves to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`. It fails the same way.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect returns a host-less `Location` header. Browsers resolve this against the tracker origin, so it may work for real users, but the redirect would then land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com`. The data can't tell us which happens. It needs a manual `curl -I` on both links to confirm.
- **Probe coverage is thin:** 12 material links were identified but 11 were skipped, so link health is largely unverified. The 38% pass rate reflects that limited sample, not 38% of links working.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7P5KMCBSV6ASKCJ6JJZJ0M7` is flagged as an image with no alt text. It appears to be the open-tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The `<html>` element has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>`). Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only).
- The tracking pixel has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is low.
- Outlook and VML conditionals are present and look correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message from a direct-delivery inbox, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Confirm from a direct-delivery copy.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** the footer is truncated, so neither could be verified. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result is shown for it.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is used for a promotional send. It is not a violation, but check that replies are monitored and that the sender aligns with the DKIM domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible links carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters were visible, but the URLs are truncated, so this can't be confirmed either way.
- Landing paths `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` match the "Shop Men" and brand-logo link labels. The destination pages themselves were not probed.
- Redirects through `trk.send.everlane.com` add a hop. If the relative `Location` issue is real, the landing page is wrong.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on both `_0` and `_9` tracking links. Confirm the final URL is on `everlane.com` and returns 200. If `Location` is relative, fix the ESP tracking-domain config.
2. Re-run link probing with a higher link limit, or probe all CTAs, so link health is actually covered.
3. Inspect the raw `.eml` from a direct-delivery inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Treat the current warnings as relay artifacts until then.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, set a meaningful `<title>`, and give the tracking pixel `alt=""`.
5. Retrieve the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer (unsubscribe, postal address) and UTM parameters.
