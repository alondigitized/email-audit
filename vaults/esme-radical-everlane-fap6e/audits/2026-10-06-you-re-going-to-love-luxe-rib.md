A subscriber who has seen four near-identical editorial sends in four days has little reason to act now.

- The hero is strong. The tight face-and-knit crop pulls the eye, and the white overlay type is legible against the dark knit.
- The hierarchy is clear: hero, one-line value prop, then three evenly spaced product modules.
- Model-plus-flat-lay pairing shows both fit and true colour for each piece.
- The copy is short and has a point of view, for example "A fall alternative to your everyday tee."
- Every module has a visible, consistent CTA, and the render has no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- No price or percentage is stated. "Better prices" is vague and gives no reason to click today.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW." None names the product, such as "Shop the Henley."
- There are no reviews, ratings or fit details. Everlane has strong customer quotes (it used one on 10-04), and none appear here.
- There are no colour or size cues, and no urgency or deadline. This is the fourth near-identical editorial send in four days.
- The subject says "You're going to love" while the hero says "Spotlight on." The two don't connect.

- 3. Make the CTAs product-specific, such as "Shop the Henley," and link each to its product page.
- 4. Break up the cadence. Vary the creative and the hook across the week instead of repeating the hero-plus-bar layout.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane Logo" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M48YBKBTNMPSQDCF8PKEFDWV_0…` resolved to a relative destination, `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. The probe raised `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" link:** `…/l/01M48YBKBTNMPSQDCF8PKEFDWV_17…` resolved to a relative destination, `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Cause not confirmed:** Both failures have the same pattern, a path with no scheme or host. Either the tracker redirect returns a relative `Location` header, or the QA probe lost the base host. The truncated HTML doesn't show which. A relative `Location` would be a real defect, because strict clients and some in-app browsers may fail to follow it. Re-test with a browser or `curl -I` on both tracker URLs before treating this as a production bug.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 27 links were probed. The other 15 (footer, social, utility) are unverified.
- **Alt-less image:** The image missing alt text is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M48YBKBTNMPSQDCF8PKEFDWV`. That is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel, not content. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (low severity):** The flagged image is the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. It is not a content-image failure.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace, so the document has no accessible title.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the language.
- **Font fallback:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in several clients, such as Gmail. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is not a defect.
- **Non-visible content:** Image content, heading structure and the remaining alt text were beyond the truncation, so they are unverified.

- **Landing-page alignment:** The two failed destinations (`/pages/womens`, `/pages/sms-signup`) couldn't be loaded, so alignment is unverified for them. The other 10 probed links reported no errors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI` on `…/l/01M48YBKBTNMPSQDCF8PKEFDWV_0` and `…_17` to check whether the `Location` header is relative. If it is, report it to Everlane's ESP configuration, since the destinations should be absolute (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/…`).
2. Treat the header and authentication warnings as relay artifacts. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC from the raw message source or a direct inbox, not through AgentMail.
