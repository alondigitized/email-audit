# 40% Off Everything on Your Gift List
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday 40% off creative Everlane has run all week, with a gift-list angle added. The hero is the quilted-jacket image under "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*". It is clean and the offer lands immediately. The send is effectively a reskin of the 11/25 "40% Off Everything. Favorites Are Going Fast" email, which used the same tan jacket creative.
- The only new thing is the "Better Gift Guide" block, and it sits below the fold with small underlined text links. The subject promises a gift-list frame, but the hero never mentions gifts. The email is competent and on-brand, but it adds little for someone who got the 11/24 and 11/25 sends.
- Judgement: a solid but repetitive send. The gift angle is the only fresh idea and it is buried.

## What's working

- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" headline is large, white on the dark jacket image, and readable at once.
- The two black SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN buttons are unambiguous and sit right under the hero.
- The Better Gift Guide block in burgundy gives a clear second section with her/him routing.
- The body copy ("RWS-certified wool, organic cotton") adds a short sustainability proof point in Everlane's voice.
- The footer states the end date (12/02/2026 at 11:59 PM PST) and the exclusions.

## What's weak

- The gift theme from the subject is absent from the hero. The jacket imagery has nothing to do with gifting.
- The Gift Guide links are small underlined text, and they sit in a large, mostly empty maroon block. They are easy to miss.
- No product is shown or priced, and there is no single hero item. Both CTAs go to broad category pages.
- The deadline appears only in grey fine print. There is no visible urgency in the body, and "going fast" is vague.
- The footer menu repeats Shop Women / Shop Men, which duplicates the CTAs above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift angle into the hero: "Gifts, 40% off" with a gift-oriented image, and a visible end date such as "Ends Dec 2".
- 2. Replace the maroon block with 3-4 priced gift products, for example "Alpaca Cocoon Crew, $X, now $Y", so there is something concrete to click.
- 3. Turn the Gift Guide links into real buttons, and label them with specific gift categories such as "Sweaters for her".
- 4. Add a line of social proof, like the customer quotes used in the 11/23 denim send.
- 5. Cut the footer menu, since it repeats the CTAs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts for everyone, 40% off through Dec 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your gift list, 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim and outerwear for her and him. Ends Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Better Gift Guide is here, with 40% off everything.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style table layout with a viewport meta, MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. The two QA link failures look like probe artifacts, since both redirects returned a relative `Location`. Header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed, because the relay didn't capture the headers. The 38% pass rate is skewed: only 12 links were probed and 10 of those were skipped.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane"** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M89SV186AC8SGE02NBAXY1DK_0`): the redirect resolved to the relative path `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`, and the prober raised `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men"** (`…_8`): same behavior, with the relative path `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Likely cause:** a relative `Location` header is valid under RFC 7231, and browsers and mail clients resolve it against the tracker host. So this probably isn't user-facing breakage. It does mean the QA tool never reached the final landing page, so the destination status and host are unverified.
- **Risk:** if the tracker's relative redirect resolves against `trk.send.everlane.com` rather than `www.everlane.com`, those paths would 404. Only a browser-level follow can rule this out, and nothing in the data shows it happening.
- Tracking parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i`) are standard Klaviyo click-tracking and look intact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (WARN):** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M89SV186AC8SGE02NBAXY1DK` is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`, but this is low severity and not a real content-image accessibility gap.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is blank, which affects web-view and archive rendering.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` has neither `lang` nor `dir`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Small mobile text:** `h4` drops to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`. That is small if h4 carries meaningful copy.
- **Link styling:** links are forced to `#000` with `text-decoration: none` in all states. Links are then distinguishable only by position or styling, not by an underline.
- **Fine:** viewport meta, `X-UA-Compatible`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`, and font fallbacks after the `@import` (Gmail ignores `@import`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Check the raw message at the origin or in a seed inbox, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so status is unknown. Validate on a direct-delivery seed.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA tool always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure. The footer and postal address are beyond the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The links are wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com`, so UTM parameters can't be seen in the source. The visible query strings carry only Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). UTM presence is unconfirmed, and the probe never reached the final URLs to check.
- The destination paths (`/pages/womens`, `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`) are generic category pages. Whether they match the "40% Off Everything on Your Gift List" subject can't be judged from the data.
- Only 12 links were probed and 10 were skipped, so link coverage is thin.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with relative-`Location` handling (`urljoin` against the previous hop) so the QA tool can confirm both landing pages return 200.
2. Do a browser-level follow of `…_0` and `…_8` to confirm they resolve to `www.everlane.com`.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on a seed inbox with raw headers, outside the relay.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`.
5. Add `alt=""` to the `/o/` tracking pixel.
6. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px if it carries content.
7. Confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirect to the final URL.
