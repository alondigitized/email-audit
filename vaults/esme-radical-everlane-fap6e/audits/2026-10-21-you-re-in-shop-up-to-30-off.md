- The hero has no product, no model and no texture. A sunset gradient says nothing about sweaters or outerwear.
- The headline and the offer are thin, small white type over a mid-tone gradient. The 30% is not the loudest element.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain. Two probed tracked links failed with "unknown url type", and the AgentMail relay hid the auth and unsubscribe headers, so those can't be verified. The automated QA pass rate was 38%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link.**
  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5GC8PRHKH29PCB6Y88JBR08_4`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
  - The redirect target is a bare relative path with no scheme or host.
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" (logo) link.**
  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5GC8PRHKH29PCB6Y88JBR08_0`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&cm_flow_i…'`, the same relative-path pattern.
- **Likely cause.** The tracker appears to return a relative `Location` header, or the destination was stored without the `https://everlane.com` origin. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host (`trk.send.everlane.com`), so users may land on a 404 there. The probe's HTTP client can't resolve it at all. Retest with a real browser to separate a probe artifact from a real defect.
- **Coverage gap.** 12 links were probed and 6 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- **[WARN] Open-pixel image.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5GC8PRHKH29PCB6Y88JBR08` has no `alt` attribute. It is a tracking pixel, so this is low severity. `alt=""` would be correct.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only. Some clients and screen readers use it for the document name.
- **Links look like plain text.** The CSS forces `a:link, a:visited, a:hover, a:active {color:#000; text-decoration:none}`. Inline text links have no color or underline cue.
- **Small mobile heading.** `h4` drops to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`, which is below common legibility minimums.
- **Web-font `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. Fallbacks are declared (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue"`), so the impact is cosmetic.
- **Missing alt text.** The source was truncated, so I couldn't audit alt text on content images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible HTML or the subject line. The remainder of the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, not confirmed defects.**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) was not found.
  - The QA output attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers.
  - Test against the raw message as received at a Gmail or Outlook inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Footer not auditable.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link was always probed, but no result for it is shown.
- **Sender address.** The From address is `support@everlane.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen.** Destination URLs carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). `cm_flow_i` suggests a triggered welcome flow, which matches the "You're In" subject.
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** None are visible in the truncated URLs. Analytics attribution may depend on `_kx` alone.
- **Landing pages unreachable.** The destinations (`/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, `/pages/womens`) could not be fetched because of the relative-path failure above. I can't confirm that the "Up to 30% Off" promise matches the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect targets for the tracked links `…_4` and `…_0` so they resolve to absolute `https://` URLs. Then verify with a browser `curl -IL` that they land on the everlane.com pages.
2. Re-run the link probe after the fix. The pass rate is currently 38%, and 6 links were skipped.
3. Check the raw message in a real inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, rather than relying on the relay copy.
4. Set a meaningful `<title>`, add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and give inline links a visible affordance such as an underline or color.
5. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px.
6. If separate analytics attribution is wanted, add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` to the links.
