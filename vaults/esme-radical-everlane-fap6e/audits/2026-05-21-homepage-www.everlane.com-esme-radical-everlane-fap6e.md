# Homepage snapshot · www.everlane.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane being exactly as Everlane as Everlane gets — stark, dark, minimal to the point of near-silence. I open the app and I'm staring at what looks like a shadowy hero image (a model in dark tones, barely readable) above two product shots: black denim shorts and a nude kitten-heel sandal. No copy visible. No CTA. No price transparency. The brand that built its identity on *showing the math* is currently showing me almost nothing.

## What's working

- **The denim short product card** — I own five pairs of the Way-High Jeans. A black denim short in that same cut family is genuinely my category, and seeing it in the grid is the one hook that almost works.
- **Clean product imagery** — the kitten-heel sandal shot is well-lit, isolated on a warm gray background, product-forward. No clutter, no lifestyle noise.
- **Dark hero tone** — at least it's not screaming millennial pink. The muted palette signals the brand I signed up for.

## What's weak

- **No visible copy anywhere on the hero** — the entire top two-thirds of the viewport is a dark, nearly illegible image. I cannot read a headline, a subhead, or a product name. This is the homepage, not an art gallery.
- **No price transparency above the fold** — the brand's core promise is cost breakdowns. Zero numbers visible. That's the whole Esme pitch and it's missing.
- **No CTA button** — there is literally nothing to tap. I'm looking at a product and I don't know where to go next.
- **Kitten-heel sandal feels off-target for me personally** — I'm a jeans person, not a sandal person. One of the two visible products doesn't speak to my basket history.
- **Hero image is unreadable** — if there's a model in that top section, I cannot identify gender, age, styling, or product. It's a dark rectangle.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the cost-breakdown story in the hero** — even one line like "French Terry Shorts — $48. Here's why." above the fold turns a dark image into a brand moment that only Everlane can own.
- **Add a visible CTA button** — "Shop New Arrivals" or "Shop Denim" with enough contrast to tap. The product shots imply something but don't invite action.
- **Brighten or caption the hero image** — if there's a model and a product in that dark frame, add a product name overlay or a light scrim. Right now it reads as a failed image load.
- **Lead with denim, not sandals** — if this page knows I own five pairs of Everlane jeans, show me the denim expansion. The sandal as a featured item alongside shorts is a weak pairing; a denim grid with alternating washes would hit me harder.
