# 30% Off Denim, Pants & More
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the most stripped-down send in Everlane's week-long "Better Prices" run — no product shots, no models, no fabric or fit detail, just a giant mint-teal block with "30% OFF* DENIM, PANTS & MORE" and a black Shop Now bar. It finally breaks from the "Better Prices, Still Everlane" wordmark cadence that's dominated every send since 8/13, swapping vague price-reassurance language for an actual number — which, per the brand history, is exactly what 8/16's "Best-Sellers Now Under $100" proved works best.
- But the execution is thin. There's a real 30% off offer with a real deadline (8/24, 11:59 PM PST — visible in the fine print), yet the hero shows zero products, so a shopper can't tell what "denim, pants & more" actually looks like or whether their size/style is even in stock. The body is just four text links (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) — no imagery below the fold at all.
- Given the fatigue already logged across 8/13–8/19, a concrete percentage-off subject is a step up, but the flat, product-free layout under-delivers relative to what a 30%-off denim promo could be. Solid subject, lazy body.

## What's working

- Concrete, specific offer (30% off) stated in both subject and hero — first numeric discount since 8/16's "$100" send.
- Clear, high-contrast single CTA ("Shop Now") impossible to miss.
- No render bugs — clean typography, no overlapping elements, no broken images.
- Legible fine print with a real deadline (8/24, 11:59 PM PST), which reads as honest urgency rather than vague hype.

## What's weak

- Zero product imagery — no denim, no pants, no models, nothing showing what's actually on sale.
- Category links (Shop Women/Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) route to broad sections, not the denim/pants category the offer is actually about — creates a click-then-search tax.
- No size, fit, or fabric info, and no indication of which "select styles" are excluded — the asterisk does a lot of unexplained work.
- Sixth-plus "better prices" themed send in ~10 days with no fresh visual identity — same teal/black/white palette, same layout bones as the wordmark sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a product grid beneath the fold — 4-6 actual denim/pants styles with prices shown pre- and post-discount. This is the single biggest lift: the brand history shows 8/16 outperformed (7/10) specifically because it showed real prices and real product, and this send has the offer but not the product.
- 2. Make the CTA and at least one secondary link point directly to the Denim & Pants category, not generic "Shop Women/Men."
- 3. Surface 2-3 concrete excluded/included categories near the offer (e.g. "Excludes new arrivals") so the asterisk isn't a black box.
- 4. Vary the hero visual — a denim texture or model shot would break the now-repetitive flat-color-block pattern.
