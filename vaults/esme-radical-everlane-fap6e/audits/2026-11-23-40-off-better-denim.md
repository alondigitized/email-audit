# 40% Off Better Denim
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the most product-led Black Friday send in the recent run. The subject promises denim, and the body delivers it. Three jeans are each paired with a real customer quote: the Column Jean, the Gardener Pull-On Jean and the Way-High Curve Jean. That is a clear step up from the 2026-11-23 "New for November" knitwear grid, which was thin and generic. The hero still carries the standard "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" lockup in white type.
- The weakness is that the hero does not say "denim." The headline is the same everything-sale line subscribers have seen for about four days, and the "better denim" idea only shows up in the small copy below the black bar. The three modules also repeat the same "SHOP NOW" link. The gift guide block at the bottom pulls attention away from the denim story.
- Overall it is solid and credible, and the review quotes are the best asset. It would be stronger if the hero committed to denim and the modules showed prices.

## What's working

- The subject and body match. Three denim styles sit under a "40% Off Better Denim" subject, unlike the 2026-11-22 "Giftable New Arrivals" send, which promised gifts and delivered a knitwear grid.
- Each module has a named customer quote, such as Roxanne H. saying she'll buy "a second pair soon." This is real social proof and is rare in promo email.
- The alternating image and text layout keeps the scroll rhythm lively. The jeans are shown in white, light wash and brown, so there is some range.
- The full-width black "SHOP NOW" bar under the hero is an unmissable primary CTA.
- The intro line "best-selling fits made with certified organic cotton" gives a fit and material reason to click.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is the generic "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" and never says denim. The model's jeans are visible, but the type does not link the offer to the subject.
- The hero image is a split collage with a paperclip graphic, and the thin "BLACK FRIDAY" text sits over a busy photo. The contrast is weaker than in earlier, cleaner heroes.
- All three product modules use identical "SHOP NOW" links with no price, no sale price and no fit or size cue. Nothing in them reflects the 40% discount.
- The maroon gift guide block is large and mostly empty space, and it competes with the denim story. The offer fine print is grey and tiny at the bottom, and the asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" is not explained near the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the hero headline to "40% OFF DENIM" or "BLACK FRIDAY: 40% OFF EVERYTHING, STARTING WITH DENIM." Use a cleaner hero image with higher-contrast type.
- 2. Add sale prices to each jean module, such as "$98 → $59," plus one fit descriptor like "wide leg" or "high rise." Replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with "Shop the Column Jean."
- 3. Shrink the gift guide block, or move it below the footer navigation, so denim stays the single story.
- 4. Put the offer end date, 12/02, in the hero or intro instead of only in the fine print. The long window weakens urgency, so lead with the code-free simplicity instead.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three fits shoppers buy twice. Offer ends 12/2.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent MJML build that renders defensibly. The automated QA found one hard failure: a tracked link whose redirect target is a relative path. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the relay didn't expose the headers. The QA pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.**
  - Link text: "Everlane" (likely the header/logo link). URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M84MTT08HFG7F4HJPREWD6ZT_0`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect resolved to a path with no scheme or host. Either the tracker returns a relative `Location` header, or the link resolves incorrectly.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host (`trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`). That would 404 or land on the wrong host instead of `everlane.com`.
  - I couldn't confirm which case applies from the data given. The probe may be failing on a redirect that real clients handle. Verify with `curl -sI` on the tracking URL and check the `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 15 were skipped (footer, social and utility links). Those 15 links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M84MTT08HFG7F4HJPREWD6ZT` was flagged. This is a 1×1 pixel, so the fix is only to add `alt=""`. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only). This affects web-view and browser-tab rendering and accessibility tooling.
- The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute (only the `xmlns`, `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` attributes are present). Screen readers can't determine the language.
- The mobile CSS sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` under `max-width: 480px`. That is below common legibility minimums if h4 carries meaningful copy.
- No other issues were found in the visible source. The MSO conditionals, Outlook group fix, dark-mode (`[data-ogsc]`) rules and font fallback stacks (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) are all present. The HTML was truncated, so the body markup wasn't audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The truncated body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing [WARN].** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing [WARN].** The same relay caveat applies. One-click support can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From address is `support@everlane.com` but the tracking domain is `send.everlane.com`, which suggests a sending subdomain. Alignment is unverified.

- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` values could be verified. They may be added at the redirect, but the data doesn't show it.
- The one resolvable destination (`/pages/womens`) is a broad category page. I can't tell from the data whether the denim-specific links land on denim collections, because they weren't surfaced.
