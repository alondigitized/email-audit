# Newly Arrived Accessories
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, seasonal accessories send that leaves the money on the table. The hero is a model in a camel beanie and scarf with a brown overshirt, "Bundle Up" over her shoulder, and a black SHOP NOW bar underneath. Below that is a clean 3x3 grid of beanies and scarves. It's pretty and on-season for 10/31, and nothing is broken.
- The best offer is in the wrong place. "Limited Time Only / Extra 25% Off Markdowns" sits in the slate-blue block below the grid, and it has nothing to do with the new accessories above it. It's the same promo from the 10/29 "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" and 10/30 "No Tricks, Just an Extra 25% Off" sends, now bolted onto a product story. A reader sees new arrivals, then a markdown banner that doesn't apply to them, and the two messages blur.
- Verdict: good merchandising, weak commercial logic. The 3x3 grid has no prices, names or individual links, so the click has nowhere specific to go. This is a solid 6-7, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero is on-season and warm. The camel beanie and scarf on a model in an overshirt sells "bundle up" immediately.
- The black SHOP NOW bar directly under the hero is unmistakable.
- The 3x3 grid on a neutral ground is calm and easy to scan, and the colors (black, grey, light blue, camel, cobalt) show range.
- The one-line copy, "New hats and scarves, in cozy, better-for-you fabrics", is short and says what the email is about.
- The layout is clean: no overlap, no broken images, no gaps.

## What's weak

- The 25% off markdowns banner is disconnected from the new-arrival accessories above it. It reads as a recycled footer promo.
- The grid has no prices, product names or visible links, so there's no reason to pick one item over another.
- The "Bundle Up" overlay is small and floats over the model's shoulder with no contrast. It's not a real headline.
- The email repeats the 10/29 and 10/30 markdown messaging, so it feels like a fourth near-identical beat in three days.
- The offer fine print is tiny and light grey. It says "online only," "select styles only" and "valid until 11/01/2026 at 11:59 PM PST", and none of that is surfaced near the banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer logic. Either apply the extra 25% to the accessories and say so in the hero, or drop the markdown banner from a new-arrivals send. If it stays, make it clearly a separate "Also on sale" section.
- 2. Make the grid shoppable. Give each tile a name, a price and its own link, or at least add a "Shop Hats / Shop Scarves" split so the click is specific.
- 3. Replace the faint "Bundle Up" overlay with a real headline. Put it above or below the image in bold type, and change the CTA to "Shop Hats & Scarves".
- 4. Put the deadline in the visible copy ("Ends 11/1") instead of only the fine print.
- 5. Space out the markdown-led sends so the accessories story gets its own day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hats & scarves just landed`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New beanies + scarves, ends 11/1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft, cozy fabrics in 5 colors — plus extra 25% off markdowns through tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wrap up your look: new beanies and scarves, ready to wear now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) send with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run found one confirmed link failure, on the "Everlane" link, and three header and image warnings that are probably capture artifacts. Only a truncated slice of the HTML was available, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** The "Everlane" link goes to `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M69A9EHMFE8MDKHHY0BBCV6R_0`. The probe got a destination of `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…` with no scheme or host, and failed with `unknown url type`.
  - If the tracker really returns a relative `Location` header, browsers will resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`. That would 404 or land on the wrong host.
  - It could also be a probe limitation, because most clients resolve relative redirects correctly. This needs a manual click test before it is treated as a production defect.
- The link text is "Everlane", so this is probably the header or logo link. It is high-visibility if it is broken.
- 12 links were probed and 18 were skipped, so link coverage is partial. That includes the footer and social links.
- The 43% pass rate is low, but QA output doesn't say which checks account for it beyond the items listed here.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text.** The image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M69A9EHMFE8MDKHHY0BBCV6R`. The `/o/` path is the open-tracking pixel, so this is low severity. A pixel should still carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
- The `<title>` is empty (`<title> </title>`). This has no effect in most inboxes, but it matters for the web-view version.
- Dark-mode handling is present (`.ev-clay-dark`, `[data-ogsc]`).
- No other rendering issues are visible in the truncated source. The MSO conditionals and the viewport meta tag are correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|…|*`. The tail of the HTML was truncated, so that part is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header, and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. That is plausible, since Klaviyo normally adds both, but it can't be confirmed from this data.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown, which the QA note attributes to the relay.
- All three warnings come from capture limitations, not confirmed defects. The sender is `support@everlane.com`, a support-style address used for marketing. Replies will go to a support queue, but this is not a compliance failure.
- The QA run says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result for it is listed. The physical postal address in the footer wasn't visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one resolved destination carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible. Because the string is truncated, they may be present further along.
- `/pages/womens` is a generic women's landing page. The subject is "Newly Arrived Accessories", so the continuity check can't pass unless an accessories-specific link exists elsewhere. Only one landing URL was resolved, so this can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M69A9EHMFE8MDKHHY0BBCV6R_0` in a real client and capture the raw `Location` header. If it is relative, fix the tracking-domain redirect to emit an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…`.
2. Capture the original `.eml` headers rather than the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel `/o/01M69A9EHMFE8MDKHHY0BBCV6R`.
4. Confirm `utm_*` parameters survive the redirect on every CTA. Also confirm the primary CTA lands on an accessories page.
5. Re-run QA on the full HTML and all 30 links, so the 18 skipped links (including the unsubscribe link) get a result.
