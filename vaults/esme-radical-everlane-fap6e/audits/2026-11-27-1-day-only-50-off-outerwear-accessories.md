# 1 Day Only: 50% Off Outerwear & Accessories
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean Black Friday day-of send, and it works. The hero is a tight crop of a model in a camel scarf and olive coat, with "50% OFF OUTERWEAR & WINTER ESSENTIALS" in large white type. A black "TODAY ONLY" tag sits under it. The offer lands in about a second. It is the same 50% / "Today Only" mechanic as this morning's "Help Yourself to 50% Off Tees & Fleece", pointed at a different category.
- The weak spot is the second block. It repeats the same coat and scarf photo and layers a second headline on top: "40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*". Two stacked discount tiers make the reader do math, and the asterisk undercuts the urgency. The cashmere editorial tile at the bottom is brand-building, but it sits in prime space on a day when the only job is to convert.
- Overall this is a strong, honest-looking send held back by redundant creative and a muddy offer hierarchy. It is better than the plain-text Thanksgiving nudge, and roughly level with this morning's tees and fleece email.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and high-contrast: "50% OFF" with a "TODAY ONLY" tag creates credible same-day urgency.
- The photography is cohesive. The camel, olive and burgundy palette matches the "Treating Yourself Is Encouraged" send and feels like the season.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars under each hero are full-width and unambiguous.
- The "EVERYTHING ELSE" wording frames the two tiers as a hierarchy: 50% on the hero categories, 40% on the rest.
- The footer is tidy. The nav (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is easy to scan, and the legal copy states the 12/02/2026 end date.

## What's weak

- The second hero reuses the same coat and scarf imagery as the first, so it reads as a repeat.
- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" text sits over a busy part of the photo. It is legible but less crisp than the first hero.
- Both blocks have a "SHOP NOW" CTA with no category or product specificity, and neither names a product.
- The cashmere "A Better Future for Cashmere" tile takes space from conversion, and its low-contrast "LEARN MORE" is easy to miss.
- There is no product grid, price point, review or social proof. The email is all mood and no product detail.
- The fine print says the offer is valid until 12/02, which sits awkwardly beside a "TODAY ONLY" claim and could read as bait.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the second hero with a 4-up grid of actual outerwear products with "was/now" prices. That gives a click target with product detail and cuts the repeated imagery.
- 2. Fold the 40% offer into one line under the hero ("40% off everything else") instead of a second full-height banner.
- 3. Add an urgency cue such as "Ends tonight at midnight PT". Reconcile it with the 12/02 fine print by naming which offer is today-only.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Coats & Scarves" and "Shop 40% Off".
- 5. Move the cashmere tile below a product grid, or cut it from the day-of send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off everything else. Black Friday ends tonight.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "1 Day Only: 50% Off Outerwear & Accessories"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-based build with every link wrapped in a `trk.send.everlane.com` redirect. The automated 18% pass rate comes almost entirely from 403 responses on those redirects, which probably reflects bot-blocking on the probe rather than a confirmed customer-facing break. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 11 probed links returned 403.** Examples are `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8EYE071ENACH720YRXJ64EJ_0` (logo), `_2` and `_4` ("Shop Now"), `_7` ("Shop Women") and `_8` ("Shop Men").
- **Likely cause:** a click-tracker that rejects non-browser user agents. A 403 on every link in the same domain and path pattern points to a systemic filter, not 11 separate dead links. I can't confirm real subscribers are affected. Re-probe with a browser-like UA or from a residential IP before treating this as an outage.
- **Redirect wrapping hides destinations.** Because every URL is a tracking redirect, the final landing pages can't be verified from the source.
- **Duplicate link text.** Two links are labeled "Shop Now" (`_2`, `_4`) and point to different redirect IDs. Tracking granularity is fine, but the labels are ambiguous out of context.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 22 material links were probed, so 10 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Open-tracking pixel flagged for missing alt:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8EYE071ENACH720YRXJ64EJ` is the `/o/` open pixel. That's expected for a 1×1 tracker and not a content accessibility defect. Adding `alt=""` would silence the check.
- **Small mobile heading size:** `h4` drops to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`, which is below common legibility minimums. It matters only if `h4` carries real content, and the truncated source doesn't show where it's used.
- **Web font loading:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and many clients. The `Arial, Helvetica` fallbacks in the font stacks cover this.
- **Outlook:** MSO conditionals, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` and `mj-outlook-group-fix` are present. No problems are visible in the truncated source.
- **Dark mode:** `[data-ogsc]` swaps `.ev-clay-light` and `.ev-clay-dark`, so dark mode is handled for Outlook.com.
- **Not verifiable:** The HTML is truncated, so image alt text on the promo images can't be checked. The QA link-text labels (e.g., "Promo Image | 50% Off Outerwear…") suggest alt text is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Everlane's use of Klaviyo suggests they are likely sent, but I can't confirm that from this data. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The header is lost in the relay.
- **Sender mismatch to check:** the From address is `support@everlane.com` while links use `send.everlane.com`. That's a normal subdomain setup, but confirm DKIM alignment with the raw headers.
- **Unverified:** the unsubscribe link and the physical address were not in the probed or visible content, so footer compliance is unconfirmed. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, yet none appears in the failures list.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** All hrefs are opaque `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…` redirects, and the 403s stopped the QA from following them.
- **Landing page alignment can't be assessed** for the same reason.
- **Recommendation:** resolve the redirects with a browser-like client and check the destination URLs for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` and the promo scope (outerwear versus sitewide).

## 7. Recommendations
