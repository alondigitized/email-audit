- This is the public launch of the Fall Essentials Event. It follows yesterday's "You're In: Shop Up to 30% Off" early-access send (2026-10-21) for the same event with the same offer. The hero is a stack of striped and brown knits, with "FALL ESSENTIALS EVENT" on the left and "UP TO 30% OFF / SWEATERS, OUTERWEAR & MORE" on the right. A black SHOP NOW bar sits directly below it.
- The body is tidy but generic. Three rows of three products (sweaters, outerwear, more sweaters) each end in a text link. There are no prices, no sale prices and no model shots. The only deadline, 10/26 at 11:59 PM PST, is in the grey fine print at the bottom. The offer reads as "up to 30%" with no indication of which items get the deepest cut.

- The hero offer is unmissable: "UP TO 30% OFF" is paired with the categories it covers, and the black SHOP NOW bar sits directly under it.
- The copy line "Because there's nothing chic about wind chill" gives the sale some brand voice.
- The three category blocks are clean and uniform, with consistent product-on-grey photography and underlined text CTAs.
- The palette (tan, grey, brown) matches the fall season and the recent sweater, fleece and cashmere sends.

## What's weak

- The deadline (10/26) appears only in the footer fine print. That is a missed urgency lever for a time-boxed event.
- No product shows a price or a "was/now". The 30% claim is never made concrete.
- The white hero text sits on a busy photo, and the right-hand text block is hard to read over the cream knit near "& MORE".
- The final "SHOP UP TO 30% OFF" link duplicates the hero CTA.
- It is the second send for this event in two days, so it adds little beyond the early-access email.
- There are no models and no styled looks, so the fit and feel of the pieces is hard to judge.

- 2. Show sale prices on at least the first row, for example "$118 → $94", so the 30% becomes concrete.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with tracked redirect links. The automated QA run found one failed link probe, which is probably a probe or redirect-handling issue. The remaining items are missing headers the relay didn't capture, plus two code-level accessibility gaps visible in the truncated source. Overall QA pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Redirect resolves to a relative URL.** The probe of `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5J4MAXTZ0Z81GKAZTXP15XG_0` (link text "Everlane") failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect target is a path-only value with no scheme or host.
  - A relative `Location` is legal under RFC 7231, so this may just be a limitation of the probe's HTTP client.
  - If a real client resolves it against `trk.send.everlane.com` instead of `everlane.com`, the click would land on the wrong host. I can't confirm which from the data, so replay the redirect with `curl -I` to check.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 30 links were probed, and 18 footer, utility and social links were skipped by design. Their health is unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5J4MAXTZ0Z81GKAZTXP15XG` is flagged for missing alt text. It is the `/o/` open pixel, so this is low severity. Adding `alt=""` is the correct fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **No language attribute.** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Small mobile headings.** Under `@media (max-width: 480px)`, `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`. This is small for mobile reading and below common minimums (about 13–14px) if `h4` is used for any readable copy.
- **Web fonts.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by many clients, including Gmail. The font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica, so this isn't a defect.
- The source is truncated, so I can't audit image alt text, MSO fallbacks or the rest of the body beyond the head and CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens or placeholder syntax appear in the visible source or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message at the source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Verify against the original delivery.
- **Not checkable from the data:** the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link, because the footer is outside the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, and no failure was reported for it.
- The From address is `support@everlane.com`, a support mailbox used for promotional mail. That is not a violation, but replies may go to a support queue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Click-through URLs carry only Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` parameters are visible in the available data, and the redirect parameters are cut off, so attribution can't be confirmed.
- The probed link's destination, `/pages/womens`, appears to be a generic women's landing page. The subject line promotes "Fall Essentials Event: Up to 30% Off", but the data doesn't show whether any link goes to an event-specific page, so I can't confirm alignment.
- The destination can't be validated until the relative-redirect failure in section 2 is resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the `_0` tracking link with `curl -IL` and confirm the final URL is on `everlane.com`. If the `Location` header really is relative, fix the tracker config to emit absolute URLs.
2. Inspect the raw original message, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px, or confirm `h4` is used only for non-essential text.
5. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
