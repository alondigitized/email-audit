# A Thanksgiving Treat: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Everlane send this week with a new hero offer. The earlier Black Friday emails, such as "40% Off Everything. Best-Sellers Are Going Fast" and "Your Black Friday Reminder", all used the quilted-jacket creative. This one leads with "BLACK FRIDAY 50% OFF TEES & FLEECE*" and a black "TODAY ONLY" bar, then stacks "PLUS 40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" underneath. The tiered offer gives repeat subscribers a reason to look again, and the urgency is real.
- The execution is clean, with a clear hero, plain black buttons and a short line of copy. The weak spot is the subject line. It leads with the 40% offer, but the hero's strongest deal is 50% on tees and fleece, so the best offer isn't in the inbox. The hero also shows a navy polo and grey sweatpants, which doesn't read as "tees." Every CTA is a generic Shop Women / Shop Men pair, so there is no product-level click path.
- Verdict: a better creative than the week's repeats, held back by a subject line that undersells it and by a lack of product specificity.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: large white "50% OFF" with "TODAY ONLY" in a black bar.
- The two-tier structure, 50% on tees and fleece plus 40% on everything else, breaks the week's repetition.
- The creative is fresh after days of the same jacket hero.
- The "Before the second helping" line fits the Thanksgiving timing and sounds like the brand.
- The footer fine print shows a clear end date, 12/02/2026, and the page has no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- The subject says 40% off everything while the hero's best deal is 50% on tees and fleece, so the strongest offer is hidden.
- The hero image shows a polo and sweatpants, not the tees and fleece it advertises.
- The asterisked offers have only dense grey fine print at the bottom, and "TODAY ONLY" sits oddly beside an expiry date of 12/02.
- Every CTA is a generic Shop Women / Shop Men button. Nothing links to a specific product, and there are no reviews or prices.
- The two Shop Women / Shop Men button pairs repeat each other, and the gift guide block adds little.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% offer in the subject and make the hero image show the product named in the offer (a tee or a fleece pullover).
- 2. Replace the second button pair with 3-4 product tiles that show the sale price and star ratings.
- 3. Make the urgency consistent. Either "Today only" with a visible countdown, or "Through Monday", but not both.
- 4. Rename the buttons to "Shop Women's Tees & Fleece" and "Shop Men's Tees & Fleece".
- 5. Move the fine print closer to the offer, or add a "details" link beside the asterisk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off tees & fleece`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off tees & fleece, 40% off the rest`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off everything else. Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy first, second helping later. Fleece and wool for the long weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built email has one confirmed probe failure: the tracked "Everlane" link redirects to a relative path (`/pages/womens?...`). The authentication and unsubscribe-header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the data. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and most CTA markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** The probe of `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8CC1BEZ6Q77C5Q94Y7B1R5W_0` (link text "Everlane") failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's response carries a scheme-less path, `/pages/womens?...`, instead of an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`.
  - Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so real clicks probably work. Strict clients and the probe do not.
  - It may be a probe artifact. Confirm with `curl -sI` on the tracking URL and check the `Location` header.
  - The path `/pages/womens` is also unusual for Everlane, so confirm it lands on a live page.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 25 material links were probed, and 13 were skipped. The other links have no verified status.
- **Tracking domain.** All links go through `trk.send.everlane.com`, a first-party tracking domain. This is good for deliverability, and no redirect-chain issue was observed beyond the one above.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Screen readers and some web-view clients have nothing to announce.
- **Missing alt on a tracking pixel.** The QA warning is for `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8CC1BEZ6Q77C5Q94Y7B1R5W`.
  - The `/o/` path is an open-tracking pixel, not content, so this is low severity.
  - `alt=""` would still be correct. It is not a real accessibility defect unless the same flag appears on content images.
- **Dark-mode handling.** The `.ev-clay-light` and `.ev-clay-dark` classes toggled via `[data-ogsc]` are present, so dark-mode is handled. Compatibility can't be checked from the truncated source.
- No other rendering problems are confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|…|*` appear in the visible source, and the subject has none. This covers the truncated portion only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Everlane sends through Klaviyo, which normally adds them. Verify on the raw message at the original recipient.
- **Authentication-Results: not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, which is a limitation of the relay. Nothing can be concluded.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it. The footer markup and postal address were not in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.
- **From address.** `support@everlane.com` is a support address used for promotional mail. It is a minor deliverability and reply-handling concern, not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one visible destination carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no `utm_*` parameters in the captured text. The string is truncated, so UTMs may follow.
- The landing page is `/pages/womens`, not a sitewide sale page. That matches "Everything" only loosely, and no landing-page content was fetched to check the 40% offer.
- The relative redirect (section 2) prevented the probe from reaching the destination, so landing-page validation didn't happen.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8CC1BEZ6Q77C5Q94Y7B1R5W_0` and check the `Location` header. Treat it as a defect only if it is genuinely relative and a normal client mishandles it.
2. Re-run the QA probe with a client that resolves relative redirects, then check that `/pages/womens` shows the 40% offer.
3. Fetch the original raw message, bypassing the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Until then, don't count those warnings as defects.
4. Fill in `<title>` with a meaningful value, for example the subject line.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel so the alt warning clears.
6. Capture the full, untruncated HTML and the remaining 13 skipped links. Confirm UTM parameters and the footer unsubscribe and address.
7. In the QA tooling, resolve relative `Location` redirects against the tracker's base URL, and exclude `/o/` tracking pixels from alt-text checks. The 43% pass rate is inflated by these artifacts.
