# Consider These Customer Approved
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a social-proof denim and pants send, and it's a better idea than most of Everlane's recent batch. The hero is a strong model shot in a denim-on-denim look, with "Favorite These" overlaid. Two product modules follow, each with a customer quote. It's the only email in the recent run that leans on reviews instead of just new arrivals, and that's a real differentiator.
- The execution undercuts it. The overlay "Favorite These" sits on the model's jeans, where it's hard to read, and it's vague. The two hero CTAs are category bars ("Shop Denim", "Shop Pants"), not product links. The best offer, an extra 20% off markdowns, sits at the very bottom in a brown block. Customers who stop at the quotes never see it. After the 2026-10-17 "Softest Sweaters" send and the 2026-10-16 "Air Alpaca" and "New Arrivals" sends, this is the 4th Everlane email in about 48 hours.
- Judgement: a solid mid-pack send with a good concept. It would lift with a clearer hero headline and a higher-placed offer.

## What's working

- Real customer quotes ("These jeans are among the best I've ever purchased." — Christine R.) give genuine social proof, which the subject promises.
- The hero image is strong and on-brand, with clean photography and a clear denim focus.
- Each product module shows a model shot next to a flat lay, so you see both the fit and the garment.
- The two black CTA bars under the hero are visible and scannable.
- The disclaimer is readable and gives the offer deadline (10/18/2026 at 11:59 PM PST), so the urgency is credible.

## What's weak

- The hero overlay "Favorite These" is small, low-contrast over the jeans, and vague.
- The 20% off markdowns offer is buried below the impact-report block, so the strongest incentive comes last.
- The sustainability block with its "Read Our Latest Impact Report" button is a large black CTA that competes with the product CTAs and has nothing to do with buying jeans.
- The product modules show no price, so there's no reason to click beyond curiosity.
- The "SHOP NOW" links are tiny, underlined text, and weak next to the black bars higher up.
- The brand has now sent four similar emails in two days, so this one reads as more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Extra 20% off markdowns" offer into the hero zone or a banner directly under it. It's the only price incentive in the email.
- 2. Replace "Favorite These" with a real headline such as "The Jeans Customers Keep Reordering", set in dark type on a clean area of the image.
- 3. Show prices, star ratings and a size or fit note in each module. Make the buttons solid black like the hero bars, and link the hero to one specific product.
- 4. Cut or move the impact-report block below the product modules and make it a text link. A big black button there pulls clicks away from product pages.
- 5. Space out sends. Combine this review-led angle with the sweater push instead of mailing four near-duplicates in a row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template sent via the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain. One probed link failed because its destination was a relative URL, and the header checks (unsubscribe, authentication) are inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose those headers. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body content past the `<style>` block could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative destination URL.** The tracking link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M558REQQY02AZDCH7PMXSKWZ_0` (link text "Everlane") resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. That has no scheme or host, and the QA probe errored with `unknown url type`.
  - **Likely cause:** either the redirect's `Location` header is relative, or the source `href` is a path-only value that the tracker passed through.
  - **Possible impact:** a browser resolves a relative `Location` against the tracking host. The click could then land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens…` instead of `everlane.com`, which would likely 404.
  - **Caveat:** it could be a probe artifact. I couldn't confirm which of these it is from the data given, and a manual click test is needed.
  - The failing link text is the logo or brand link, so it's probably in the header and high-traffic.
- **[WARN] Open-tracking pixel has no alt attribute.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M558REQQY02AZDCH7PMXSKWZ` has none. This is low severity, since it's a 1x1 pixel, but `alt=""` is still correct so screen readers don't announce the URL.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 29 links were probed and 17 were skipped. The 43% pass rate rests on that partial sample. Only one link failed; the rest of the percentage appears to come from the warnings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
Confirmed from the visible source:
- **Missing `lang`.** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is blank.
- **Link underline removal.** The global rule `a:not([name]), a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes underlines from every link. Any inline text link is then distinguishable only by position, not by color or underline.
- **Web font via `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in several clients (e.g. Gmail), but the stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica. This is low risk.
- **Outlook handling is present.** The MSO conditionals, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `.mj-outlook-group-fix` and the dark-mode `[data-ogsc]` rules are all in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appeared in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers at the source before treating it as an issue.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer not verified.** The truncated HTML doesn't show whether the body contains a visible unsubscribe link or a physical postal address. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't listed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one resolved destination (`/pages/womens`) carries Klaviyo/Customer.io-style parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no visible `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. The URL is truncated, so this is not conclusive.
- Landing page alignment can't be assessed. The only probed destination errored, and the subject ("Consider These Customer Approved") can't be matched to a destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the relative-URL link.** Make the href and redirect target absolute (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`). Manually click the logo link from a real inbox to confirm where it lands.
2. **Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers** to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. The relay currently makes these unverifiable.
3. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>`, and a meaningful `<title>`.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the `/o/…` tracking pixel.
5. **Restore underlines** on inline text links, or ensure each link is clearly styled as a button or has other non-color affordance.
6. **Verify UTM tagging** on the destination URLs, if the team wants GA attribution alongside the existing tracking parameters.
7. **Re-run with the full HTML and all 29 links probed** so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be checked.
