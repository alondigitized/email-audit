# Way-High Denim Now Way Lower Price
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's denim-category product story, structurally identical to 8/13's "All Of This Denim Is At Better Prices" — same hero mirror-image styling shot, same "Better Prices, Still Everlane" wordmark, same three-product-with-testimonial layout. The one new move is the subject line: "Way-High Denim Now Way Lower Price" is the first denim-specific send this week to name an actual product line in the subject instead of leaning on the generic price-drop beat.
- But the execution has the same core flaw as every "Better Prices" send this week: nowhere in the entire rendered email — hero, subhead, or footer — is an actual number. "Same fit. Same washes. Same denim you love. Only thing that changed is the price" promises a discount and then never states one. Compare to 8/16's "Best-Sellers Now Under $100," which scored 7/10 specifically because it showed real price tiers; this one regresses back to vague price language with zero specificity, closer to 8/15's "The Price Changed. The Quality Didn't." (3/10).
- The three product blocks with named customer quotes (Kimberly K., Molly B., Alaina T.) are a genuinely good trust signal and the strongest part of this send. But this is the eighth "Better Prices, Still Everlane" email in eight days, and a subscriber who's seen this exact visual template three times this week will scroll past on autopilot. Net: decent product-story craft, undermined by fatigue and a still-missing price number.

## What's working

- Subject line names a specific product ("Way-High Denim") rather than a generic "prices dropped" phrase, a first for this week's cadence.
- Three distinct denim styles shown with real customer testimonials (name-attributed quotes), which reads as authentic social proof.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no overlapping text, broken images, or render bugs visible.
- Clear "SHOP NOW" CTAs repeated under each product plus a hero CTA, so there's no ambiguity about the action wanted.

## What's weak

- No price or discount percentage appears anywhere in the email — "Only thing that changed is the price" is a promise with no payoff, the same gap that sank 8/17's send to 5/10.
- This is the eighth near-identical "Better Prices, Still Everlane" email in eight days; the hero visual template has now repeated at least three times.
- The footer fine print ("Offer valid until 09/07/2026... applies to select styles only") reveals exclusions that contradict the breezy "same denim you love" framing — the fine print does more price-relevant work than the body copy.
- Fashion product shots use one visible model archetype across all three products, with no size-range or fit diversity signaled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero and subhead — "20% Off Way-High Denim" beats "Only thing that changed is the price" every time; this is the fix that's cost this campaign points four sends running.
- 2. Retire the mirror-twins hero image for at least one cycle — it's been used across at least three sends this week and subscribers will start pattern-matching to "delete."
- 3. Move the "select styles only" exclusion out of 6pt footer text and into the hero subhead if it's material — burying it reads as bait-and-switch once a shopper clicks through and finds it doesn't apply.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy per product ("Shop Way-High 2.0," "Shop the Curve Jean") instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" links — more specific and slightly better for scan-ability.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Way-High Denim: 20% Off, Today Only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Jean, Finally Cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same fit, same wash — new price on Way-High denim`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real reviews, real denim, lower price tags`
