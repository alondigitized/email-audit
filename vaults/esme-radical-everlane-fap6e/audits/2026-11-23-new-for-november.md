# New for November
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but thin product-grid send, and it is nearly the same email as the 2026-11-22 "Giftable New Arrivals" send. A nine-item knitwear grid, a black "SHOP NOW" bar and a gift-guide block are stacked under a 40% off strip. The grid is clean and easy to scan. Nothing in it tells you why these nine pieces are new, what they cost, or what the discount works out to.
- The offer is under-served. "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" sits in a cropped hero strip at the very top. The lifestyle photo is cut off and the text sits over a busy image. The grid below carries no names, prices or sale prices. The "Better Gift Guide" block is a large maroon slab with only two text links and a lot of empty space. This is roughly the ninth Black Friday-era send in about four days, so the brand is leaning on volume, not on anything new to say.
- Verdict: tidy, on-brand, and forgettable. The subject "New for November" does not mention the 40% off, which is the only reason to click this week.

## What's working

- The 3x3 grid is clean, evenly spaced and on a neutral backdrop. The knitwear is easy to scan.
- The product mix of cardigans, stripes and a zip-neck suits the season.
- The 40% off message is visible at the top, with split "Shop Women" and "Shop Men" links.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bar is high-contrast and unmistakable.
- The footer is tidy, and the fine print includes the end date (12/02/2026).

## What's weak

- The grid has no prices, product names or sale prices, so 40% off never becomes a concrete number.
- The hero is a cropped, dim strip with small white text on a photo. It is not the first thing the eye lands on.
- The "SHOP NOW" bar sits under the whole grid, so the nine products do not appear to link to individual items. The subscriber is sent to a general page.
- The maroon gift-guide block is mostly empty space, and its copy ("Made with responsible materials. Now at better prices.") is vague.
- The nav, utility icons and social links take up a lot of scroll for little value. The social links are bare letters ("TT, IG, FB, X, YT").

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a clear hero that combines the new product and the offer, for example "New Arrivals, 40% Off". Put it above the logo, with a real headline in a legible size.
- 2. Add a name, the original price and the sale price under each grid item, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Replace the gift-guide block with one product story, such as a Luxe Merino or cardigan feature, in place of a second generic category pitch. The "Best Merino" send on 2026-11-21 did this better.
- 4. Cut the footer nav and the stacked utility rows. Keep one nav row.
- 5. Slow the cadence or make each send distinct, because this one repeats the rest of the week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `The season's new knitwear is included in Black Friday pricing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. One probe failure, on a tracked link whose redirect returned a relative `Location` (probably a checker limitation). The compliance and authentication warnings come from missing relay headers, so they can't be confirmed from this data. Overall QA pass rate was 43%, with 1 failure and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Redirect not resolved:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M831CZ6M3F160SC23VRF8Z43_3` (link text "Everlane") failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker responded with a relative `Location: /pages/womens?...`. RFC 7231 permits relative redirects, and browsers resolve them. The probe's HTTP client didn't.
  - This is more likely a QA-tool gap than a broken link. It is unconfirmed: the destination host was never probed, so `/pages/womens` was not verified to load.
  - A relative redirect from a tracking domain is unusual. If `trk.send.everlane.com` is resolving against its own host rather than `everlane.com`, `/pages/womens` would 404 there. Manually follow the link to confirm.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 33 links were probed, and 21 were skipped as footer, social or utility links. The other 11 probed links raised no failures.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so link count, `href` formats and duplicate CTAs beyond the visible portion can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M831CZ6M3F160SC23VRF8Z43`. The `/o/` path indicates this is the open-tracking pixel, so the missing alt text is low impact. Adding `alt=""` is still best practice.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. It is minor, but it is a screen-reader and some-client gap.
- **External `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to `Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`, so it degrades safely.
- **Dark-mode hooks:** the `[data-ogsc]` rules and the `.ev-clay-dark` / `.ev-clay-light` swap are present. I didn't verify that the dark images load.
- **No issues found** with the viewport meta, MSO conditionals, `X-UA-Compatible`, or the Outlook table resets (`mso-table-lspace`, `-ms-interpolation-mode`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. That makes this a capture limitation rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message at the original recipient.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reported no failure. The footer HTML and postal address fall in the truncated section, so they can't be confirmed here.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is the sender for a promotional email. This is a mailbox-type observation, not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The redirect carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). The visible portion shows no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, but the query string is cut off, so this is unconfirmed.
- Landing target `/pages/womens` is unverified because of the probe failure (see section 2). The link text is the generic "Everlane", and the destination appears to be a women's category page.
- `_kx` and `bxid` are per-recipient Klaviyo identifiers. They are expected for attribution, but they make the URLs non-cacheable and unique per recipient.

## 7. Recommendations

4. Make the QA capture fetch the full, untruncated HTML. Footer compliance, UTM parameters and merge-token checks can't be completed until it does.
