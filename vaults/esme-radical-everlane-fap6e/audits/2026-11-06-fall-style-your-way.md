# Fall Style, Your Way
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a UGC-style styling send, and it's the fourth Everlane email in four days with a Black Friday tie-in. The creative is attractive: a customer-sourced hero of a woman in a navy V-neck with a tan field jacket tied at the waist, then three handle-credited lifestyle shots. But the 30% offer only appears in a brown block below the fold, after two SHOP NOW bars. Compare 11/06's "A Better Way to Complete Your Look", which put "30% OFF OUTFIT-MAKING ESSENTIALS" in the hero. This one buries it.
- The email has no single idea. The hero pushes a field jacket, the grid shows a white tee, a cashmere sweater and a blue shirt, and the footer pushes a sitewide sale. Both SHOP NOW buttons are generic, and none of the products has a name, price or link cue. It looks good and persuades poorly.
- Judgement: a pleasant brand-mood send that undersells its own offer. Expect a 5.

## What's working

- The UGC framing with @handles ("A lesson in fall dressing, from you") feels authentic and gives social proof without star ratings.
- The hero is strong. The model, the jacket tied at the waist and the muted fall palette match the season.
- The layout is clean, with alternating image and text rows and plenty of whitespace. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW / 30% OFF OUR FAVORITES" block is legible and has split Women and Men CTAs.
- The fine print states an end date and time (11/09/2026 at 11:59 PM PST).

## What's weak

- The 30% offer sits roughly 60% of the way down. A mobile reader sees no offer in the first screen.
- Both SHOP NOW bars are generic and don't say what they lead to. The first one sits under a jacket caption and the second one under three unrelated outfits.
- The captions are tiny, light and low in contrast. They read like alt text rather than selling copy.
- The three grid items have no names, prices or colors, so nothing helps a shopper decide.
- The "30% OFF OUR FAVORITES" block is a large flat brown panel with small, thin type. The urgency is weak, and the deadline appears only in the grey fine print.
- This is the fourth near-identical Black Friday-adjacent send in four days. Fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "30% off our favorites — Black Friday preview" as the hero headline or a banner above the image, as 11/06's send did. This is the biggest lift available.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop the Field Jacket" and "Shop the Looks". Link each grid image to its own product.
- 3. Add product names and prices under each UGC image, for example "The Cashmere Crew, $118 → $83".
- 4. Show the deadline in the body ("Ends Sunday 11/9"), not only in the footer.
- 5. Enlarge the captions and darken them for readability.
- 6. Drop the second SHOP NOW bar, or make it the "Shop the sale" bar, so the email has a single path.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off the styles you're loving this fall`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Field jacket, 3 ways (30% off through Sunday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real outfits from real customers. Black Friday preview ends 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Styled by you, now 30% off. Shop women's and men's favorites.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email sent from `support@everlane.com`, with every link wrapped in the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The QA run reported 0 hard failures. Header-based compliance and authentication checks and most link destinations could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated, so the footer was not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** 12 links were probed and 4 returned HTTP 429 from the tracker. A 429 means the tracker rate-limited the probe, so these links are likely valid:
  - `.../01M6SD6AVZV0523ZNA1YGEFVQV_2` ("Shop Now")
  - `.../_6` ("Shop Now")
  - `.../_8` ("Shop Women")
  - `.../_9` ("Shop Men")
- **8 links were never probed.** Probing hit the 70s time budget. Unchecked: `_0`, `_1`, `_3`, `_4`, `_15`, and others in the truncated list. They are neither pass nor fail.
- **Destinations are hidden behind redirects.** All links are opaque tracker URLs (`/l/<id>_<n>`), so the final landing URLs can't be checked from the source.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6SD6AVZV0523ZNA1YGEFVQV` is the only image flagged for missing alt text. It is a tracking pixel, so this is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This matters for web-view and screen-reader contexts.
- **Missing alt text:** the tracking pixel has no `alt` (see above). QA reported no other images missing alt.
- **Links aren't visually distinct:** the style block sets `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` all to `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Any inline text links therefore have no color or underline cue. That fails WCAG 1.4.1 unless the links are styled as buttons.
- **Small mobile heading text:** `h4` drops to `font-size:10px` at max-width 480px, which is a legibility risk.
- **Outlook and dark mode handling is present:** MSO conditional comments, `#outlook a`, `ms-text-size-adjust`, and `[data-ogsc]` dark-mode rules. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output. Footer and body content past the truncation point were not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe header found** (WARN). QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **No List-Unsubscribe-Post header found** (WARN). This is the RFC 8058 one-click header. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so verify it against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** (WARN). The relay doesn't expose it, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. This can't be assessed from the data.
- **Footer not inspected:** the physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result for it was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The tracker redirects hide the final URLs, and the probes that returned 429 never followed the redirect. No UTM parameters or landing-page content were observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw `.eml` from the sender, or send to a seed inbox that preserves headers. Then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `everlane.com`.
2. Re-probe the 4 rate-limited and 8 unprobed links with a longer budget and backoff. Follow the redirects to record the final URLs, status codes, and UTM parameters.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. Set a meaningful `<title>`, such as the subject line "Fall Style, Your Way".
4. Give inline text links a visible affordance (underline or a color distinct from body text) instead of `text-decoration:none` for every state.
5. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer address, the unsubscribe link, and any merge tokens.
