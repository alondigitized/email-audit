# 30% Off Better Sweaters
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand sweater push, but it repeats a send Everlane already made. The subject "30% Off Better Sweaters" went out on 10/8, and it follows "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6), "30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family" (10/7) and "30% Off Online & In Stores" (10/8). A subscriber who has seen four 30% emails in four days has no reason to open a fifth. The creative also adds nothing new.
- The render itself is tidy. A model in a taupe half-zip fills the top, a black SHOP NOW bar sits under the hero, and a 3x3 grid of cream and oatmeal knits follows. A maroon block at the bottom adds an extra 20% off markdowns. The weak point is the offer overlay. "Friends & Family / 30% Off Sitewide, Including Sweaters" is thin white type over a light taupe sweater and grey wall, so the number that matters is the hardest thing to read. The product grid has no names, prices or per-item links, so it is a mood board, not a shopping tool.
- Judgement: competent but redundant. It would perform fine as a first sweater send. Here it mostly adds fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong, seasonal image, with the half-zip sweater front and centre and the black SHOP NOW bar directly under it.
- The offer is concrete: 30% off sitewide, plus an extra 20% off markdowns in the maroon block.
- The copy "Soft cashmere. Airy alpaca. Hard to choose. Easier to get both." has real voice and names materials.
- The 3x3 grid is visually calm, with consistent tones and no broken images.
- The footer is tidy, with Shop Women / Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers, Find a Store, Texts and Help Center.

## What's weak

- The "30% Off Sitewide / Including Sweaters" overlay is white on a pale sweater and wall. It is low contrast, and "Including Sweaters" is nearly lost.
- The nine grid products have no names, prices or individual labels, and every item is beige. There is nothing to compare or choose between.
- The deadline (10/13/2026, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the grey fine print. There is no visible urgency in the body.
- "Sitewide" in the hero conflicts with "Offer applies to select styles only" in the fine print, which weakens the honesty of the offer.
- Three of the four CTAs are the generic "SHOP NOW".

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending the same 30% message daily. Fold the repeated F&F sends into one, then send a differentiated follow-up such as a last-call email.
- 2. Put the offer on a solid band or darken the hero image. Make "30% OFF" large, high contrast and readable at thumbnail size.
- 3. Move the deadline into the hero ("Ends 10/13") and drop the "sitewide" claim or qualify it.
- 4. Label each grid item with its name, material and sale price, for example "Cashmere Crew, $XX". Link each item to its own product page.
- 5. Mix the grid with more colour (navy, charcoal, a rust accent) and tag best-sellers or low stock.
- 6. Use specific CTA copy: "Shop sweaters" and "Shop the markdowns".

- **Subject Alt B:** `The sweater edit, 30% off through 10/13`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere, alpaca and merino, plus an extra 20% off markdowns. Ends 10/13.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, table/MJML-style layout that routes all links through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The automated QA flagged one failing link (the logo redirect resolves to a relative URL) and could not verify authentication or unsubscribe headers. The source also has an empty `<title>` and no `lang` attribute. The HTML is truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect returns a relative target.**
  - Link text: "Everlane Logo".
  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4F1Z73ZNQCVATDRCHQD62MP_0`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The redirect's destination is a path-only value (`/pages/womens?…`) with no scheme or host.
  - This may be only a probe limitation, because some HTTP clients don't resolve relative `Location` headers. A browser resolves a relative redirect against the tracking host, though. If that happens, the logo click lands on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is likely a 404. This needs a manual click-test to confirm.
- **[WARN] Open-tracking pixel has no alt attribute.**
  - Element: `<img src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4F1Z73ZNQCVATDRCHQD62MP">`.
  - It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is low. It should still carry `alt=""`.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 of 33 links were probed and 21 were skipped (footer, social, utility). Their health is unknown.
