## What's working

- The hero is editorial, the jacket is the focal point, and a full-width black "SHOP NOW" bar sits directly beneath it.
- The four-product zigzag alternates image and text sides, so it's easy to scan and avoids a monotone grid.
- Each product has a name plus a useful detail: "PFC-free water resistance", "raglan sleeves", "contrast canvas collar".
- The intro line ("From rain-ready cotton twill to soft corduroy…") sets the theme quickly and ties to the season.
- It's a real break from the week's repeated 30%-off sweater creative.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, or urgency anywhere, and no reason to buy this week.
- The "Jacket Required" headline is small, pale and sits on the busy brown jacket, so it's hard to read.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". The four text links give no hint of what's behind them.
- No prices are shown on any product, which leaves shoppers guessing.
- The organic cotton block is a long paragraph of sustainability copy, and the "READ MORE" button pulls attention away from the products.
