# Best-Selling Denim
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-led denim roundup — no discount bar, no peony macro, no urgency copy. It's the third or fourth "no discount, straight editorial" send from Everlane this week, following "Sweaters Everyone Loves" and "Effortless Layers Worth Exploring." The hero image and headline ("Need-Now Denim") are strong and on-category, and the three-jean breakdown with named styles (Gardener Pull-On, Way-High 2.0, Way-High Curve) gives real product substance rather than vague lifestyle copy.
- Where it falls short: there's no price anywhere in the email — not on the hero, not on any of the three product tiles. For a "best-selling denim" email, that's a real gap; a shopper deciding whether to click wants at least a starting price or "from $X" anchor. The organic cotton callout at the bottom is a nice trust-building beat but is somewhat disconnected from the denim story itself.
- Solid, well-executed product email, but the total absence of pricing anywhere in the body caps how effective it can be at driving a click versus just browsing.

## What's working

- Clear hero with a single, specific headline ("Need-Now Denim") and full-bleed model shot.
- Three named styles each get their own image, description, and dedicated "SHOP NOW" link — easy to scan and pick from.
- Copy is specific about fit details (relaxed fit, elastic waist, straight leg with stretch, barrel leg cut high) rather than generic ("great fit").
- Organic cotton module adds a credible sustainability angle without overselling it.
- Clean layout, no render bugs, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- No price or "from $X" shown anywhere — not on the hero, not on any of the three jean tiles.
- "SHOP NOW" is repeated four times with zero variation; no differentiation in CTA copy by product.
- No social proof (review count, star rating, "best-seller" badge) despite the subject line claiming "Best-Selling."
- The organic cotton section, while nice, isn't tied back to a specific product or CTA relevant to denim shoppers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add pricing to each of the three product tiles (or at minimum a "from $XX" on the hero) — this is the single biggest lever since price is the #1 thing a denim shopper checks before clicking.
- 2. Add a small "Best-Seller" or star-rating badge to back up the subject line's claim, since the copy says "the three best-selling jeans everyone wants" but nothing visually substantiates it.
- 3. Vary the CTA copy per tile ("Shop the Gardener," "Shop the Way-High") instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" links.
- 4. Move the organic cotton module higher or tie it to a specific jean (e.g., a small "organic cotton" tag on each tile) so it reinforces the product story rather than reading as a separate unit.
