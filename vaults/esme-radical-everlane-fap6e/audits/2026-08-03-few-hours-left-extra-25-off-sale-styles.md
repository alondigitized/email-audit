# Few Hours Left: Extra 25% Off Sale Styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the weakest execution I've seen from Everlane's sale cadence — and that's saying something after four sitewide-sale bolt-ons in two weeks (7/27 "Meet Your Summer Denim Lineup," 7/29 "Our Best Denim," 7/31 "A Short Story."). Those at least wrapped the sale banner around a real editorial story with product photography. This one is just plain text: no hero image, no product grid, no photography at all — just paragraphs of copy sitting on a white background followed by a stack of generic nav links.
- Worse, the personalization token is visibly broken: "Hey friend, ," has a dangling comma where a first name should be. That's not a stylistic choice, that's a merge field that failed and shipped anyway.
- This reads less like a deliberate minimalist design and more like a rendering failure — possibly an image-blocked or plain-text fallback version that got sent as the primary creative. Judgement: this is a bottom-of-the-barrel send, and if this is genuinely how it rendered in-inbox for recipients, it should not have gone out.

## What's working

- The urgency framing ("Last call," "Few Hours Left") is clear and consistent between subject and body.
- The stacked discount math (up to 70% off + extra 25%) is spelled out in plain language.
- Footer fine print (deadline, exclusions) is present and not hidden — no bait-and-switch.
- Brand mark and typography are still recognizably Everlane even stripped of imagery.

## What's weak

- Zero product imagery — no hero shot, no product photos, nothing to anchor the offer visually.
- Broken personalization: "Hey friend, ," leaves a visible orphaned comma where a name should be.
- No real primary CTA button — just a plain text list of nav links (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) with equal visual weight, none of which is clearly "the" click.
- No link goes to a specific sale product or curated sale collection — everything routes to generic category landing pages.
- Zero visual hierarchy — the eye has nowhere obvious to land; it's a wall of undifferentiated text.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the render before anything else.** Ship this with actual hero imagery and a styled primary CTA button — this currently looks like a failed template render, not a finished email.
- 2. **Fix the personalization fallback.** "Hey friend, ," must default to "Hey there," when no first name is available — check the fallback logic on the merge field.
- 3. **Add one bolded, buttoned CTA** (e.g. "Shop the Sale") linking directly to the sale collection, not a generic nav list.
- 4. **Show 2-4 sale product thumbnails** with prices struck through to make the "up to 70% off + extra 25%" math tangible instead of abstract.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Chance: Extra 25% Off Everything on Sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Tonight — Stack an Extra 25% Off Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off, plus 25% more at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale styles won't last — extra 25% off ends at midnight.`
