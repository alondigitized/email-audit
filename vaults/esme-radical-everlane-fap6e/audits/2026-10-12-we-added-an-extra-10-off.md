# We Added An Extra 10% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is an escalation send. After a week of 30% Friends & Family emails, including "Get Cozy While It's 30% Off" earlier today, Everlane adds an extra 10%. "We Added An Extra 10% Off" is the right instinct for a subscriber who has seen the same 30% banner eight times. The subject is the only place the offer appears, though.
- The body never says "10%". The hero is a moody two-model photo, and the "Today Only" overlay is so faint against the dark image that it's nearly invisible. Below it is vague copy ("even more savings on our best styles"), a "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" banner and a nine-tile grid of cream and beige basics with no prices, names or badges. A reader who opens on the strength of the subject can't confirm the deal. The fine print also says it applies to "select styles only". The visuals are clean and on-brand, but the offer is not.
- Verdict: a good subject and a weak delivery. It is a mid-tier send, and the missing number is the main problem.

## What's working

- The subject is a real news hook: an extra 10% on top of the running 30% is a credible reason to open again.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered. The hero leads, the black "SHOP NOW" bars are high-contrast, and the grid is easy to scan.
- The "Before the event ends tomorrow" line gives credible time pressure that matches the 10/13 deadline in the fine print.
- The seasonal knit, jacket and trouser mix fits the current autumn campaign.
- Brand voice is consistent: "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites." matches Everlane's usual tone.

## What's weak

- No "10%" or "40%" appears anywhere in the rendered body, so the subject's promise isn't confirmed.
- The "Today Only" hero text is washed out against the dark photo. The only urgency cue in the hero is almost unreadable.
- "Today Only" in the hero sits awkwardly with "ends tomorrow" in the copy and the 10/13 fine print. It's unclear how long the extra 10% lasts.
- The grid tiles have no prices, product names or sale badges, and the tones are nearly identical, so nothing stands out as a deal.
- Every CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" and goes to a broad destination, not a specific product or collection. The fine print also limits the offer to "select styles", which weakens the "extra" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero as large, high-contrast text, such as "Extra 10% Off — Today Only". Add a tinted scrim or solid band behind it so it's readable. This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Resolve the timing conflict. Pick one deadline, either "Today only" or "Ends 10/13", and use it in the hero, body and fine print.
- 3. State the stack explicitly, for example "30% + extra 10% = up to 40% off", and name which styles qualify so "select styles" isn't a surprise.
- 4. Add price tags or "Now $X" lines to at least the top row of the grid, and give each tile a distinct click target.
- 5. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Extra 10% Off Knits" and "Shop Outerwear".
- 6. Vary the creative from the last week's sweater and cashmere sends, since this is the same promo cycle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 10% Off Ends Tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% Off, Plus 10% More`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Layer the extra 10% on knits, outerwear and denim. Ends 10/13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best styles just got cheaper. Prices drop at checkout.`
