# Treating Yourself Is Encouraged
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's gift-yourself pivot on Thanksgiving Day. The creative is new: a burgundy and camel palette with a tote and mohair knit, replacing the tan quilted-jacket hero used all week. The "Treat Yourself to Better" headline is big and warm, and it gives a reason to buy for yourself rather than for a gift list. That is a fresh angle after "40% Off Everything on Your Gift List" on 11/25.
- The offer hierarchy is muddled. The 40% sits in a thin top strip, the hero has only a generic "Shop Now" button, and the real "today only" deal, 50% off knits and fleece, is buried at the bottom. The two middle blocks (gifts for her, under $100) pull away from the self-treat message. They also don't match the "Women/Men" split the rest of the email uses.
- Verdict: a good-looking, on-brand send with a strong theme. It undersells its most urgent offer and is the fourth or fifth Black Friday email this week. Solid, not sharp.

## What's working

- The new hero, with its bag, mohair knit and rich burgundy, is cohesive and premium, and it breaks the week-long jacket sameness.
- "Treat Yourself to Better" is a clear emotional hook, and the "your own best gift giver" line supports it.
- The 40% off banner at the top keeps the offer visible on first load.
- The "TODAY ONLY 50% OFF KNITS & FLEECE" block has real urgency and a specific category, with separate Women and Men links.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with a legible footer and fine print that states the 12/02 end date.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is a generic "Shop Now" with no product or offer attached.
- The 50% off "today only" deal sits at the bottom and is likely missed by people who scroll only part of the way.
- The "Shop Gifts for Her" and "Shop Under $100" blocks contradict the self-treat theme and leave out men.
- The hero shows no price or product callout, and there is no social proof or reviews.
- The 40% off line in the top strip is small, and the footer disclaimer is tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Today only: 50% off knits & fleece" deal up into the hero, or replace the generic "Shop Now" with "Shop 50% Off Knits Today."
- 2. Make the middle blocks fit the self-treat theme. Show "Treat yourself" picks with named products and prices, and offer a Men's counterpart.
- 3. Add a countdown or "ends tonight" line so the Thanksgiving urgency reads credibly.
- 4. Add a rating or review line, or a best-seller tag, on at least one product.
- 5. Use a more specific CTA than "Shop Now" on every button.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Thanksgiving treat: cashmere for you`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `40% off everything, plus 50% off knits until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cashmere, merino and outerwear worth keeping for yourself.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML build behind a click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). One confirmed link failure: the logo link redirects to a relative URL. Header-level checks (auth, List-Unsubscribe) could not be verified because the AgentMail relay doesn't expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link resolves to a relative URL.** The "Everlane Logo" link is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8CX5JPC4KYPJW3D2MMA07EX_3`. The probe failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect target has no scheme or host.
  - A browser would resolve it against the tracking host (`trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`), which probably 404s. That resolution is inferred, not observed.
  - This could instead be a probe-client limitation on relative `Location` headers, so verify with a real client.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 28 links were probed and 16 were skipped (footer, utility and social). Those 16 are unverified, not clean.
- The remaining probed links produced no failures in the QA data.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** The flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8CX5JPC4KYPJW3D2MMA07EX`. The `/o/` path indicates the open-tracking pixel, so this is low impact. It should still carry `alt=""` for clean markup.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`. Some clients and accessibility tools use it, so it is minor.
- **Small mobile text.** The mobile media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`. Any content in `<h4>` is hard to read on small screens.
- **Webfont import.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/…/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The font stacks fall back to Arial, so this is not a defect.
- **Outlook handling.** The MSO conditional comments and `.mj-outlook-group-fix` are present and correct.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm alt coverage on the other images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML was truncated, so this covers only what was visible.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unverifiable: List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both headers are reported missing, but the QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping them. I can't confirm a real defect. Check the raw `.eml` at the source.
- **Unverifiable: Authentication-Results.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Sender identity.** The From address is `support@everlane.com`, a support mailbox rather than a marketing-specific sender. This is not a defect, but the mailbox is a bit unusual for promotional mail.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure for it. The footer is cut off in the excerpt, so I can't confirm the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The probed logo URL carries Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`) and `cm_flow_i…`. In the visible portion there are **no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`** parameters, and the string is cut off. I can't tell whether UTMs are appended later in the URL or absent.
- The logo lands on `/pages/womens` (a gendered landing page) rather than the homepage. That is a continuity point only if the redirect works.
- No other landing-page alignment data was captured, because 16 links were skipped.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix or verify the logo redirect.** Request the tracked URL with `curl -sI` and check that `Location` is an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…`. If it is relative, correct the Klaviyo link configuration.
2. **Confirm headers from the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from a direct delivery (for example, a Gmail "Show original").
3. **Confirm UTM parameters.** Check that the full link URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`, since the truncated output can't show this.
4. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel** and a meaningful `<title>` (the subject line).
5. **Review the `<h4>` mobile size.** Check that `h4` is not used for content that needs to be readable at 10px.
6. **Increase probe coverage** (or sample the 16 skipped links) so the 43% pass rate reflects the full link set.
