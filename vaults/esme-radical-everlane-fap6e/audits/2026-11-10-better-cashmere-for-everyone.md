# Better Cashmere for Everyone
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built product-and-proof send. It pairs three named creators with three specific cashmere pieces, each with a price ($68, $118, $48) and its own CTA. It reads as editorial rather than promotional, and that suits cashmere. The sourcing story (Good Cashmere Standard, recycled fibers) gives the "Better Cashmere" subject something to back it up.
- It loses momentum at the top. The hero is two models with white overlay text ("Everyone Deserves Better") sitting on their faces and sweaters, and it is hard to read. The only offer, "three cashmere styles starting at $48," is in small body copy below a generic "Shop Now" button. After the 30% Black Friday preview run of Nov 7–10 ("30% Off Your Next Layer," "Don't Miss 30% Off Savings"), this is a deliberate change of tone. It also drops the sale framing entirely, which may be confusing or may be a welcome break.
- Verdict: a strong mid-funnel brand-and-product email. It would convert better if it led with the price and fixed the legibility of the overlay text.

## What's working

- Each product module has a creator quote, a short description, a price and a specific CTA ("Shop the Slim Crew," "Shop the Half-Zip," "Shop the Boxy Tee"). The CTAs are clear and each links to one product.
- The prices are honest and concrete. $48 for the Boxy Tee is a strong entry price for certified cashmere.
- The creator quotes (@hannahlovey, @iamsooyounlee, @tamaramory) supply social proof and fit detail, such as "doesn't scratch me" and "wear it without a T-shirt underneath."
- The "Everyone Deserves Better" video block and the "Read More" sourcing section back up the quality claim without crowding the shopping modules.
- Black buttons, a consistent layout and generous spacing make the long scroll easy to follow.

## What's weak

- The white overlay text on the hero and on all three creator photos is low-contrast. It sits over faces, skin and light fabric, and the quotes are hard to read at inbox size.
- The hero doesn't state the offer. "Starting at $48" is buried in a small paragraph below the fold, and the hero CTA is a bare "Shop Now."
- The email has no urgency or deadline, even though the copy says "just in time for the holidays."
- It is very long. The video and sourcing blocks at the bottom add little for someone ready to buy, and the third-product module repeats the first two.
- Following the Nov 7–10 sale sends, there is no mention of the 30% Black Friday offer. That leaves it unclear whether these pieces qualify.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price in the hero as a headline, for example "Certified cashmere from $48," and move the quotes off the photos into a solid caption bar or the body copy. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop Cashmere from $48."
- 3. Add a credible reason to act now, such as a holiday ship-by date or a limited-run note, and say whether the Black Friday promo applies.
- 4. Move the sourcing block up under the hero as a one-line trust badge. Cut the video block or put it last.
- 5. Lead with the $48 Boxy Tee, the cheapest and most accessible piece.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The $48 cashmere tee creators love`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Three styles, soft-washed and sourced responsibly. Just in time for gifting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hear why creators are gifting the Slim Crew this season.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found 2 link failures and 3 warnings (38% pass rate). The two failures are on the logo and the SMS signup link. The tracked redirects return relative `/pages/...` targets, which the probe could not resolve, so these may be probe artifacts rather than broken links. The header warnings are relay-capture limits, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link** (text "Everlane"): `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M735KAHXRKEQZZNM41NA2W0V_0` resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`.
- **[FAIL] SMS signup link** (text "Icon | Sign Up for Texts"): `…/l/01M735KAHXRKEQZZNM41NA2W0V_17` resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…`.
- **Cause:** both errors read `unknown url type`. That is a Python `urllib`/`requests`-style failure on a scheme-less path. The redirect `Location` header is relative, or the probe did not join it to the origin.
  - A browser resolves a relative `Location` correctly, so this is likely a probe limitation.
  - Relative redirects from a tracking domain are still non-standard. Per RFC 7231 they are valid, but some clients handle them poorly. Verify manually.
  - The destination host is not visible in the QA output.
- **Coverage:** only 12 of 27 links were probed and 15 were skipped. Footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M735KAHXRKEQZZNM41NA2W0V` is an open-tracking image with no `alt` attribute. It is not a content image, but it still lacks `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the open pixel above has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. Other images were not flagged, but the HTML was truncated, so they were not fully reviewed.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **No link affordance:** the `<style>` block sets `a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover {color:#000; text-decoration:none}`. Inline links are therefore indistinguishable from body text by colour or underline. This weakens WCAG 1.4.1 (use of color) for any text links.
- **Small mobile text:** the mobile media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`. This is small for legibility, and it only matters if h4s carry content.
- **Font loading:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is acceptable.
- **Outlook handling:** MSO conditionals, `mj-outlook-group-fix` and `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` are present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is therefore unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed.
- **Not confirmed as defects.** Treat all three as unverified rather than failures. Everlane sends via Klaviyo (`_kx`, `static-forms.klaviyo.com`), which normally adds these headers. Check the raw message in a seed inbox such as Gmail's "Show original".
- **Truncated source:** the physical postal address and unsubscribe link could not be verified. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as the marketing sender. Confirm it aligns with the sending domain `send.everlane.com` (DKIM/DMARC).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Klaviyo params present:** both probed links carry `_kx=…`, `bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V` and `cm_flow_i…` / `cm_fl…`. Klaviyo's `_kx` is a profile identifier. `utm_*` params could not be seen because the QA output truncates the URL, so UTM completeness is unverified.
- **Landing pages not validated:** the redirect resolution failed (see section 2), so landing-page status, final URL and param preservation were not checked.
- **Logo destination:** the logo goes to `/pages/womens` rather than the homepage.
- **Probe coverage:** product and CTA destinations were among the 12 probed links, but the QA output reports only the 2 failures, so the rest could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow `…_0` and `…_17` in a browser or with `curl -IL`. Confirm the redirect `Location` and the final status. If the `Location` really is relative, have the ESP or link-tracking config emit absolute `https://` redirects. Otherwise fix the probe to resolve with `urljoin(response.url, location)`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel `…/o/01M735KAHXRKEQZZNM41NA2W0V`.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>`, since it is currently whitespace.
4. Restore a visible link affordance for inline text links (underline or distinct colour), or confirm that all links are button-styled.
5. Capture the raw headers from a seed inbox. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment to `send.everlane.com`.
6. Raise probe coverage by including the unsubscribe and footer links. Log the full URL so `utm_*` completeness can be checked.
