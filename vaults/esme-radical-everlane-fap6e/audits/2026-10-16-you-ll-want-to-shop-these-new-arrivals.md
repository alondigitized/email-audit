# You'll Want To Shop These New Arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Everlane send, but it buries its best asset. The nine-product new-arrivals grid is on-season: black turtleneck, brown zip hoodie, corduroys, striped knit, ruched dress. The strongest reason to click is the "EXTRA 20% OFF MARKDOWNS" block at the very bottom, below a sustainability blurb. The subject promises new arrivals and never mentions the offer.
- For a subscriber this is a repeat. The extra 20% markdown offer already went out as the plain-text "New Markdowns With an Extra 20% Off" on 10-15 and "Extra 20% Off New Markdowns" earlier today. This version at least has visuals, but it leads with product and hides the deal. The 10/18 deadline appears only in the grey fine print, so "limited time only" does the urgency work, and it does so weakly.
- Verdict: tidy and tasteful, but misaligned. The subject and hero sell newness, and the money-saving offer sits third in the scroll. It's a middling send.

## What's working

- The 3x3 product grid on a soft grey field is clean and scannable, and it matches the fall palette: corduroy, turtlenecks, browns.
- Black SHOP NOW and READ MORE buttons are unambiguous and consistent with Everlane's minimal look.
- The brown "EXTRA 20% OFF MARKDOWNS" band is a strong colour break and a legible offer.
- There are two sale CTAs, Women's and Men's, so the offer is split by audience.
- Nothing is broken: no overlap, missing images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The offer sits at the bottom, after the grid and a sustainability block. A skimmer may never reach it.
- The grid has no names, prices or labels, and nothing identifies a hero product. The single SHOP NOW goes to a generic destination.
- The recycling/materials block is off-message for a promotional push and pushes the offer further down.
- The deadline (10/18, 11:59 PM PST) is only in the tiny grey footer, so urgency is vague.
- This is the third send in two days that leads with or includes the extra 20% markdown message, so the cadence feels repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the extra 20% off markdown band to the top, or add an offer strip above the grid. The subject and hero should match what's actually being sold.
- 2. Put the deadline in the visible copy, e.g. "Ends Sunday, 10/18", instead of "for a limited time only".
- 3. Add short labels and prices to a few grid items, such as "Corduroy Pant", so there is something specific to click.
- 4. Cut or move the sustainability block below the offer. It's a good brand beat but a poor placement for a promo.
- 5. Differentiate this send from the plain-text markdown note, or consolidate the two into one stronger send per day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-built send with click and open tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Only one hard failure was flagged, and it looks like a probe artifact rather than a broken link. The authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" link (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M537GXSNP00AQ7BV44M87WX2_0`).**
  - The checker reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - That string is a relative path, so the tracker most likely answered with a relative `Location` redirect that the probe's HTTP client couldn't resolve.
  - Browsers resolve relative redirects, so this is probably not a user-facing break. It's still unconfirmed: the probe never reached a final status code.
  - Everlane's tracker returning a host-less `Location` is non-standard (RFC 7231 allows relative references, but some clients and security scanners fail on them).
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 19 were skipped, so the other 19 are unverified. The report doesn't say whether the unsubscribe link was probed successfully.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M537GXSNP00AQ7BV44M87WX2` has no `alt` attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title> </title>` is blank.** It contains only whitespace.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The opening tag only declares the `xmlns`, `xmlns:o` and `xmlns:v` namespaces, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation language.
- **Open-pixel image has no `alt`** (`/o/01M537GXSNP00AQ7BV44M87WX2`). This is low severity if it's a 1×1 tracking image. It would be better as `alt=""` with `role="presentation"` so assistive tech skips it.
- **Mobile `h4` size.** `@media (max-width:480px){ h4 { font-size:10px !important; … } }` forces any `h4` down to 10px, below common legibility minimums. I can't tell from the truncated source whether an `h4` is actually used for visible content.
- The MJML/Outlook conditionals, `viewport` meta and `X-UA-Compatible` are present and correct. I found no structural rendering defects in the visible source. The HTML was truncated mid-`@media` rule, so the body wasn't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this only covers the head and CSS.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. Treat this as unverified, not as a confirmed gap.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also attributed to the relay.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** the footer was cut off in the truncated HTML, so neither could be confirmed.
- The `From` address is `support@everlane.com`, which is a reply-capable address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The "Everlane" link resolves to `/pages/womens`, with Klaviyo identifiers `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` (cut off in the QA output).
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the captured portion. They may be in the cut-off part of the query string, or appended by the tracker.
- The logo link going to `/pages/womens` is a non-obvious destination for a header link. I can't judge the alignment further because the final URL was never fetched.
- The 19 skipped links leave the product-link continuity unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the tracking link** with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the tracker origin. Confirm it lands on `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…` with a 200. If the tracker really emits a relative `Location`, flag it to the ESP (Klaviyo).
2. **Fix the QA tooling:** resolve redirects with `urljoin`, and report a distinct "probe error" instead of a FAIL for unparseable `Location` values.
3. **Capture raw headers from the source mailbox, not the relay,** to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `everlane.com` and `send.everlane.com`.
4. **Add a title and `lang`.** Set `<html lang="en">` and a meaningful `<title>`.
5. **Mark the tracking pixel as decorative** with `alt=""`.
6. **Check the 10px mobile `h4` rule** against any `h4` used in the body.
7. **Raise probe coverage** to include all CTA and product links, and confirm that `utm_*` parameters survive the redirect chain.
