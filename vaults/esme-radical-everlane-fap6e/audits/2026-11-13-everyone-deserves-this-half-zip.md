# Technical Audit: "Everyone Deserves This Half-Zip" (Everlane)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style structure) with all links wrapped through `trk.send.everlane.com`. Two probed links failed with relative-URL errors. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so the footer was not inspected. The 38% pass rate mostly reflects these probe failures and unverifiable checks, not confirmed breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target on two links.**
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7BE0EQKT6636AYHYZJTXRTK_17` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…`.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7BE0EQKT6636AYHYZJTXRTK_0` (logo, "Everlane") resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…`.
  - Both failed with `unknown url type`, meaning the destination has no scheme or host.
  - **Cause not confirmed.** The tracker may be returning a relative `Location` header. That is legal under RFC 7231, but some clients and scanners handle it poorly. Or the QA probe may simply not be resolving it against the tracker host. Either way, a real mail client following the redirect would most likely resolve it correctly.
  - **Need to verify:** the raw `Location` header from the tracker, and whether the link actually lands on `everlane.com`.
- **Link `_0` (logo) points to `/pages/womens`, not the site root.** This is a destination choice, not a defect. It does mean the logo link is category-specific.
- **Probe coverage was partial:** 12 links probed, 15 skipped. The skipped links, mostly footer and social, are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is a single space.
- **[WARN] Image without alt:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7BE0EQKT6636AYHYZJTXRTK`. The `/o/` path indicates this is the open-tracking pixel. It is low impact, but `alt=""` is preferred so assistive tech skips it.
