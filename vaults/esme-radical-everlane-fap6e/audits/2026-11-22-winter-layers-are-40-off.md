# Winter Layers Are 40% Off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is another clean "Black Friday 40% off everything" send from Everlane, and it adds almost nothing new. The hero is a model in a cream fleece with white "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" type over a burgundy backdrop. The offer is unmistakable and the layout is tidy. But the subject "Winter Layers Are 40 Off" is the same blanket discount in a seasonal costume, close to the 11-21 "Get Comfortable With 40% Off Everything" and "Build Your Everyday Rotation for 40% Less" sends. The body copy even reuses "Get comfortable."
- The second half is stronger. A black hooded puffer on burgundy with a second "SHOP NOW" is a real product moment, and "Winter Layers" does match the outerwear and fleece shown. The tagline "Yup, we got a little carried away on savings" is a nice wink at the cadence. The Better Gift Guide block is muddy, with small text on a dark maroon field.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but it repeats the previous week's creative. A subscriber who has seen roughly nine of these in five days will skim and delete. It is stronger than the plain discount sends only because the subject names a category.

## What's working

- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is clear in about one second.
- The subject names a category (layers) that matches the outerwear shown in both images.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars are full-width and unmistakable.
- The "Yup, we got a little carried away on savings." line has real brand voice.
- The puffer image is a strong product visual and tonally consistent with the hero.

## What's weak

- Both CTAs read "SHOP NOW" and link to the same generic idea, so the verb and noun are not specific.
- The asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" hides its fine print, "select styles," at the bottom in tiny gray type. That undercuts the honesty of "everything."
- The Better Gift Guide block has small, low-contrast white text on dark maroon, and it spends a lot of vertical space on little content.
- The body copy is generic and repeats earlier subject language ("Get comfortable").
- The email shows no product names, prices or reviews, so nothing helps a shopper decide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the second image a real product module: "The Luxe Fleece, now $X" and "The Hooded Puffer, now $X" with before and after prices. This would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Replace the two "SHOP NOW" buttons with specific CTAs such as "Shop Fleece & Outerwear" and "Shop the Puffer."
- 3. Put the offer's deadline in the body ("Ends 12/2"), not only the footer. Add a visible "select styles" note near the hero.
- 4. Cut the gift-guide block, or move it up as a separate send. Give it a bigger heading and a lighter background if kept.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11-21 sends. Don't use another burgundy, cream-and-black "40% off" hero.

## Technical Audit: Everlane, "Winter Layers Are 40% Off"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML build with ESP click tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run's two "broken link" failures look like a probe limitation (relative redirect targets), not confirmed breakage. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so some checks are incomplete.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures are probably not real breakage.**
  - Both links return the error `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?...'` or `'/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?...'`. Those are relative paths, which suggests the tracker's redirect `Location` header is path-only. Relative `Location` values are valid under RFC 7231, but the probe doesn't resolve them against the host.
  - Affected: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M80EZQ1V0393NGQD25G7JC6X_0` ("Everlane" logo link) and `.../_9` ("Shop Men").
  - Verify with `curl -sI` and a browser. If real clients follow the redirect, no action is needed beyond fixing the QA probe. Some clients and security scanners resolve relative redirects less reliably, so an absolute `Location` would be safer.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 material links were probed and 11 were skipped. The "38% pass rate" reflects probe coverage, not email quality. The sale CTA's destination was not confirmed.
- **Tracking structure:** the redirect URLs carry the Klaviyo params `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i...` (truncated in the QA output). This is expected.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** the source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Some clients and assistive tech use it for the "view in browser" page title.
- **Missing alt text:** the flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M80EZQ1V0393NGQD25G7JC6X`. The `/o/` path is the open-tracking pixel. This is low severity, and adding `alt=""` would clear the flag.
- **Small mobile type:** the `@media (max-width: 480px)` rule sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`. If `h4` carries readable content (the base size is 15px), that is below the usual 11–14px minimum. I couldn't confirm what uses `h4` in the truncated source.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/.../custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is handled.
- **Outlook support:** the MSO conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings` and `mj-outlook-group-fix` are present and look correct.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|...|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source, though the truncated portion was not checked.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Everlane sends through Klaviyo, which normally adds both headers. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox to confirm.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From address `support@everlane.com` is on a different domain than the tracking host `send.everlane.com`. A subdomain relationship usually passes DMARC relaxed alignment, but that can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Footer not confirmed:** the unsubscribe link and physical address (CAN-SPAM) are not visible in the truncated HTML. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result was not shown.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the QA excerpts. The URLs are truncated, so the final landing URLs are unknown.
- The two probed links resolve to `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, which are general landing pages rather than a "40% off winter layers" page. This is inferred from the paths alone. It couldn't be checked because the probe failed before loading either page.
- Klaviyo identity params (`_kx`, `bxid`) are carried through the redirect, so attribution should work.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the QA probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host. Then re-run the 2 failed links and the 11 skipped ones, including the sale CTA.
2. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, since this data can't confirm them.
3. Get the full HTML and check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
4. Collect the final landing URLs after redirects and confirm the UTM parameters and that the sale CTA lands on the promoted collection.
5. Minor fixes for Everlane: give `<title>` real content, add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and confirm what the 10px mobile `h4` is used for.
