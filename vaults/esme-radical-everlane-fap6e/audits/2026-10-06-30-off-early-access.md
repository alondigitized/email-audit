# 30% Off: Early Access
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest offer Everlane has sent in the last few days. "30% OFF SITEWIDE" is set in huge white type over a stack of blue and cream knits, and a full-width black SHOP EARLY ACCESS bar sits directly beneath it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the send breaks from the no-offer editorial run of "You're Going to Love Luxe Rib" (10-06), "Cardigan Weather" and "It's a Shirt Thing" (10-05). Those sends only gestured at value with the "Better Prices, Still Everlane" block.
- The execution below the hero is generic. Two mirrored model-plus-product panels show a puffer, half-zip, jean, boot, crew sweater, long sleeve, trouser and flat, but there are no prices, no sale markers and no individual links I can see. The one line of body copy, "Be one of the first to shop our biggest sale of the season," is soft. The real deadline, 10/13 at 11:59 PM PST, and the "select styles" caveat are in grey footer text. "Sitewide" in the hero contradicts "select styles" in the fine print. This is a solid sale announcement that leaves urgency and honesty on the table.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. "30% OFF SITEWIDE" reads at thumbnail size and is the first thing the eye hits.
- The black SHOP EARLY ACCESS bar is a clear, full-width CTA and matches Everlane's other sends.
- "Early Access to Friends & Family" adds an insider feel, which is rare for Everlane's usually flat promos.
- The mirrored model and flat-lay panels are clean and on-season for fall, with outerwear, knits and boots.
- There are no render bugs. The images load, the layout is intact and the text is legible.

## What's weak

- The deadline (10/13, 11:59 PM PST) is only in the grey footer fine print. The body has no urgency.
- "Sitewide" in the hero conflicts with "Offer applies to select styles" in the footer. That reads as bait-and-switch.
- The product tiles show no prices, no "30% off" tags and no visible per-item links. The sale never gets applied to a product.
- The product captions are tiny, light and low-contrast, so they are hard to read on mobile.
- The body copy is thin. "Biggest sale of the season" makes a claim with no stakes or reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the deadline in the hero or just under the CTA.** For example, "Ends 10/13 at 11:59 PM PST." This is the biggest lift, because the only deadline is buried in the footer.
- 2. **Add sale prices to the tiles.** Show "$X → $Y" under each product and link each tile to its product page. This makes the 30% concrete.
- 3. **Resolve the "sitewide" vs. "select styles" contradiction.** Either list the exclusions in one visible line or change the hero to "30% off most styles."
- 4. **Explain "Friends & Family."** One line on why early access matters would help, for instance "Shop before the public sale opens."
- 5. **Enlarge and darken the product captions.**
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off everything, 7 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early access: 30% off ends 10/13`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Puffers, knits and boots — shop before the public sale starts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Friends & Family early access ends Oct 13 at 11:59 PM PST.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo/MJML-based email with Klaviyo-wrapped click tracking (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The one hard QA failure (a relative redirect target on the header logo link) is probably a probe limitation. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay-captured headers and can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Header logo link ("Everlane").** The probe of `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M49R0PSSHCYSTKHMNQ6AM15F_0` got a redirect to a relative path: `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The probe raised `unknown url type`.
  - A relative `Location` header is legal under RFC 7231. Browsers resolve it against the tracker host, so it may work for real users.
  - If the tracker host serves no `/pages/womens`, the link would 404 there. The QA data can't distinguish the two cases.
  - It's the only failing link, and it's a high-traffic one, so verify it manually.
- **[WARN] Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 28 links were probed and 16 were skipped, so the 43% pass rate reflects partial coverage. The skipped set is footer, utility and social links.
- **[WARN] Missing alt on `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M49R0PSSHCYSTKHMNQ6AM15F`.** This is the Klaviyo open-tracking pixel (`/o/` path), not content. It's a false positive for accessibility, though an empty `alt=""` would be cleaner.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **`<title> </title>` is empty.** It contains only whitespace, so web-view and some clients show no title.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag has only the `xmlns` attributes, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Links have no non-color affordance.** `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:hover` and `a:active` are all `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Links are indistinguishable from body text unless they're buttons or images.
- **`h4` drops to `font-size:10px !important` at max-width 480px.** This is below legible size if any body or legal copy uses `h4`. I couldn't confirm usage in the truncated source.
- **Custom font `@import` and dark-mode helpers are in place.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/...)` has the Arial/Helvetica fallback stack, and Outlook-only MSO conditionals are present. No issue.
- The source was truncated, so image dimensions, `alt` coverage on content images and the body structure couldn't be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Both are flagged as likely stripped by the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed missing. The Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements need both headers, so verify them against a raw copy of the message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown from this capture. The tracking domain `trk.send.everlane.com` is a subdomain of the From domain, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- The QA output says an unsubscribe link was always probed, but it reports no failure for it. The physical postal address in the footer wasn't visible in the truncated HTML, so it's unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Landing paths use Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` parameters, and they're intact in the one visible example.
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appears in the visible portion of any URL. The `cm_flow_i` value is truncated, so I can't tell whether UTMs are appended elsewhere. Confirm in the full probed URLs or in analytics.
- The logo link resolves to `/pages/womens`, a gender-specific landing page. Technically this is fine but it's a generic destination for an "Early Access" promo email. Whether it should point elsewhere is a content question, so I'm not weighing in.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow the logo link `…/l/01M49R0PSSHCYSTKHMNQ6AM15F_0` and confirm that the relative redirect to `/pages/womens` resolves. Teach the QA probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host so it stops flagging valid redirects.
2. Check a raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Treat the three warnings as unknown, not as failures, until you do.
3. Fill in `<title>` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and suppress or allowlist the `/o/` pixel in the alt-text check.
5. Confirm UTM tagging in the full URLs, and probe the 16 skipped links, or at least the unsubscribe and footer links, before relying on the 43% pass rate.
6. If `h4` carries meaningful text, raise the 10px mobile override to at least 12px.
