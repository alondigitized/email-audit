# Still Time: 50% Off Outerwear & More
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's third send of Black Friday day-of, and it reads like a re-run of the earlier ones. The hero is a clean, editorial crop of a model in a tan jacket with "50% OFF OUTERWEAR & WINTER ESSENTIALS" and a black "TODAY ONLY" tag. That is nearly the same offer as this morning's "1 Day Only: 50% Off Outerwear & Accessories", and the "Help Yourself to 50% Off Tees & Fleece" send that landed on the same day.
- The structure is sound. The big offer comes first, then Women/Men split buttons, then a second 40% off everything block with a trench-coat model. The hierarchy holds up, but the two offers compete. The "Still Time" subject also contradicts the hero's "TODAY ONLY", and the 12/02 expiry in the fine print contradicts both.
- Verdict: competent but redundant. This is the fourth or fifth near-identical promo of the day, and it adds urgency without adding anything new. Good for conversion on a warm list, poor for subscriber fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large, high-contrast white type on a muted photo, so the 50% lands instantly.
- The black Shop Women / Shop Men split gets shoppers into the right category in one tap.
- The "TODAY ONLY" tag gives the hero a clear deadline.
- The cashmere "Black Friday Fund" block adds some brand story and keeps the email from being purely transactional.
- The footer is clean, with nav links, store finder, texts and help center.

## What's weak

- The second hero ("BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE 40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*") is white text over a light, busy background. The small "BLACK FRIDAY IS HERE" line has weak contrast and cuts across the model's head and coat.
- "Still Time" in the subject clashes with "TODAY ONLY" in the hero. Fine print says the offer runs until 12/02, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Two stacked offers (50% and 40%) with two different heroes dilute the single message. The reader has to work out which one applies to them.
- The "Don't miss out puffers, liners…" copy is small, generic and sits in white space. No specific product, price or review is visible anywhere.
- The cashmere block's "LEARN MORE" is small and low-contrast over the illustration. It does nothing for a sale-day click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one offer. Either lead with 50% outerwear and drop the 40% block, or fold it into one line of text under the hero. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make the deadline honest and consistent: "Ends tonight" in the hero, in the subject and in the fine print, or drop "TODAY ONLY".
- 3. Replace the generic body copy with 2–3 linked product tiles (puffer, liner, overcoat) showing the sale price and a rating.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the second hero by adding a darker overlay or moving the type to a solid band.
- 5. Cap daily sends. Consolidate the morning and afternoon 50% emails into one, with an "ends tonight" reminder only for non-openers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 50% off puffers & coats`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call on 50% off outerwear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Puffers, liners and overcoats, half off until midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 40% off everything else. Free returns on US orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with fallback fonts and a viewport meta tag. The automated QA run shows a 38% pass rate. Two tracked links failed because their redirects returned relative paths. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated, and the HTML supplied is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two relative-path redirect failures** (`[FAIL]`):
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8FFHRHZMNYEG5V125FPXF8Y_0` (text "Everlane") resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8FFHRHZMNYEG5V125FPXF8Y_9` (text "Shop Men") resolves to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`.
  - The probe error `unknown url type` means the tracker's redirect target had no scheme or host.
  - Either the `Location` header is genuinely relative, or the probe failed to resolve it against the base URL. Browsers would resolve a relative `Location` against `trk.send.everlane.com`, which would send the user to a bad URL. A probe artifact is more likely, but this is **unconfirmed** and needs a manual check with `curl -I` on both links.
- **Low probe coverage:** only 12 of 23 material links were probed (11 skipped). The other 11 are unverified.
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8FFHRHZMNYEG5V125FPXF8Y` is the open-tracking pixel. Empty alt is acceptable for a 1x1 pixel, so this is not a real accessibility defect. Use `alt=""` explicitly, but this is low priority.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>`), so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation language.
- `<title> </title>` is empty (whitespace only).

## 5. Compliance

- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are outside the truncated HTML, so they cannot be verified. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure was reported.
- The sender is `support@everlane.com`, but the tracking domain is `send.everlane.com`. This is normal Klaviyo domain alignment. No issue found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- Tracked URLs are opaque (`trk.send.everlane.com/l/…`), so destination UTM parameters can't be seen.
- Destination query strings carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the truncated output. Verify that they are appended after the redirect resolves.
- Landing-page alignment with the "50% Off Outerwear" subject couldn't be checked, because the two probed links (`/pages/womens`, `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`) failed before reaching a page. Neither is an outerwear-specific URL. The offer-specific CTA links were not among the probed links.
