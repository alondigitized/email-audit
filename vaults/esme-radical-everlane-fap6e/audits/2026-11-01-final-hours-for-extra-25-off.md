# Final Hours for Extra 25% Off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" creative, after 10/29 ("Extra 25% Off Markdowns") and 10/30 ("No Tricks, Just an Extra 25% Off"). This time the only change is a countdown. The hero shows a model in a light-blue oxford with a brown sweater tied at the waist, with "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS / ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF" over her. "ENDS TONIGHT" sits at the right, and a black SHOP WOMEN'S SALE bar runs underneath.
- The offer is clear and the urgency is real, because the footer gives a 11/01 11:59 PM PST deadline. The execution is weak. The headline text is laid over the model's body and the white type is hard to read against the white garage door. The email also gives little new on the third send, and the four category tiles carry no prices.
- Judgement: it's a competent last-call email that wins on urgency. It would be stronger if it showed any prices and fixed the hero legibility.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" plus the 11/01 11:59 PM PST deadline in the footer makes the urgency credible.
- The 25% off and up-to-70% offer is the dominant message in the hero.
- The black SHOP WOMEN'S SALE bar is a clear, full-width primary CTA directly under the hero.
- The four-tile category grid (sweaters, outerwear, tops, denim) gives quick, scannable paths to the sale.
- The "Better Prices, Still Everlane" block reinforces the brand's quality positioning.

## What's weak

- The white hero text is hard to read. "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS" runs across the model's shirt and the pale door, and the "ENDS TONIGHT" label is small.
- The category tiles show no prices or discount amounts, so the "up to 70% off" claim has no proof.
- The creative is nearly identical to the 10/29 and 10/30 sends, so a subscriber who has seen both gets no new reason to act.
- The fine print says the offer is for "select styles only", and it is tiny and low-contrast at the bottom. The hero doesn't mention it.
- The email has three overlapping CTAs: the black bar, the four tiles, and the "Shop Now" link. There is no single hero product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix hero legibility. Put the offer and "ENDS TONIGHT" on a solid or darkened panel, or move them off the model. Make the deadline larger and put it with the offer.
- 2. Make the tiles show proof. Add "$XX, was $XX" or "Up to 70% off" to each tile. Show real sale prices for best-sellers.
- 3. Say when it ends in plain terms, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT", and put it near the top.
- 4. Surface the "select styles" caveat in the hero ("on select markdowns") so it doesn't read as fine print.
- 5. Change the creative on the third send. Lead with a single best-seller at its sale price instead of repeating the same hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: extra 25% off markdowns`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: sweaters, coats & denim up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 25% off already-reduced styles, until 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Best-sellers are going fast. Sale ends at midnight.`
