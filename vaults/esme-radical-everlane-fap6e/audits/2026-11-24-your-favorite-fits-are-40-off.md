# Your Favorite Fits Are 40% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's denim-led version of the Black Friday "40% off everything" email it has sent all week. The subject promises "Your Favorite Fits," and the body delivers. Three jeans (Column, Gardener Pull-On, Way-High Curve) each get a real customer quote. That echoes the 2026-11-23 "40% Off Better Denim" send (7/10), and it is better than the generic knitwear grids in "New for November" and "Giftable New Arrivals."
- The hero is the weak spot. A model in a cream V-neck sweater and jeans sits behind white "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" type, with a cropped close-up panel overlapping her. The photo is muddy and the type competes with it. The offer reads, but the hero shows a sweater while the subject promises fits, so the two don't line up. The black SHOP NOW bar is clear, though the three product CTAs are small underlined text links.
- Verdict: a solid, credible send with good social proof. It is also the Nth near-identical Black Friday email in four days, so freshness is the problem, not execution.

## What's working

- Customer quotes ("I'll be purchasing a second pair soon!") add social proof that most promo emails skip.
- The alternating image/text rows give the body a clear rhythm and make it easy to scan on mobile.
- The subject and the body line up: "fits" turns into three named jeans.
- The black SHOP NOW bar directly under the hero is unmistakable.
- The fine print is legible and gives the end date (12/02/2026), so the offer feels honest.

## What's weak

- The hero image is flat and gray. The overlaid white headline sits on a busy mid-tone photo, and the cropped close-up panel adds clutter.
- The hero shows a sweater, not denim, so it doesn't deliver on "Your Favorite Fits."
- The per-product SHOP NOW links are small, underlined text and are easy to miss.
- No prices or sale prices are shown on any jean, so the 40% is never made concrete.
- The asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" sends the reader to grey footer text, and the deadline appears only there.
- Everlane has now sent the same 40% offer under four to five different subjects in four days, which signals fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero denim. Show the Column Jean, put the headline in a solid color block beside the photo, and drop the overlapping inset.
- 2. Show prices on each jean ("$98 → $59") so the discount is visible, not just claimed.
- 3. Add a deadline line near the top, such as "Ends Dec 2," instead of leaving it only in the footer.
- 4. Turn the product links into proper buttons, e.g. "Shop the Column Jean."
- 5. Vary the creative across the week. Rotate the offer angle (gifts, denim, outerwear) instead of repeating the same banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Denim customers love, now 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `"I'll be buying a second pair." See the jeans reviewers keep reordering.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Your Favorite Fits Are 40% Off"

## 1. Technical Summary
The source is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style email with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. One automated check failed on a relative redirect target, which is likely a scanner artifact. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is truncated, so footer and body checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link error on the "Everlane" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M85KNWHR9KADJ50RYKF62X1J_0` resolved to `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...'`.
  - The tracker's redirect appears to return a path-relative `Location` header, with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the request host, so real users probably land on `/pages/womens`. The probe's HTTP client did not resolve it.
  - Treat this as low-to-medium severity. It is not confirmed as user-facing breakage. Check it with a real-browser or `curl -L` test.
  - If the relative `Location` is real, some strict clients and link scanners may mishandle it. An absolute redirect would be safer.
- **Link probing coverage:** 12 links were probed and 15 were skipped, so the QA is partial. Only the failing link is confirmed. The rest are unverified, not clean.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M85KNWHR9KADJ50RYKF62X1J` is the open-tracking beacon. It is expected, and it is the image the QA flagged for missing alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel (`/o/01M85KNWHR9KADJ50RYKF62X1J`):** The QA flagged it. A 1x1 pixel should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. Low severity.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`. Low severity.
- **`@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)`:** It sits inside a `<style>` block. Gmail and some other clients ignore or strip `@import`. The font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica, so the impact is cosmetic.
- **Positives (confirmed in source):**
  - viewport meta tag and charset are present.
  - Outlook conditional comments (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`) and `mj-outlook-group-fix` are present.
  - Dark-mode handling exists (`[data-ogsc]` rules for `.ev-clay-dark` and `.ev-clay-light`).
  - Mobile media queries are present.
- **Not assessable:** The source is truncated, so the rest of the images, alt text and button markup couldn't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (such as `{{ }}`, `*|…|*` or `{%`) are visible in the available source. The truncation limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is inconclusive, not a confirmed violation. Verify against the raw message from a direct-to-mailbox send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Verify with a direct-delivery test.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support-style address used as a marketing sender. It is not a violation, but check that replies are monitored and that the marketing stream's authentication aligns with this domain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Neither could be seen in the truncated HTML. An unsubscribe link was reportedly always included in probing, but no result is shown for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination for the "Everlane" link is `/pages/womens`, with Klaviyo tracking params only: `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…` (truncated). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the evidence. This could be a truncation effect. Confirm the full query string.
- The subject mentions "Your Favorite Fits Are 40% Off". The one link resolved goes to a generic women's landing page. Whether it points to a sale-specific collection can't be confirmed from the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…_0` with a real browser or `curl -IL`. Confirm the `Location` header resolves and, if it is relative, make it absolute.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `/o/…` tracking pixel and a real `<title>` (for example, the subject line).
3. Check the raw message headers from a direct send for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
