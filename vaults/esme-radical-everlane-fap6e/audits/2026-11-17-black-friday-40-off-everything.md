# Black Friday: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's first real Black Friday send, and it works because of the offer. "40% Off Everything*" is large, white and thin-weight over a warm brown-and-grey hero. The eye lands on the discount first, which is what a Black Friday email should do. Compared with the run of price-less sends (Holiday Dresses, The Sweaters He'll Love), this one finally puts a number up front, much like the $48 cashmere tee did on 11/15.
- The execution is safe and a bit thin. The only hero CTA is "SHOP WOMEN", so men are routed to a text link far down the page. The four-tile category grid (sweaters, outerwear, denim, shoes) is clean, but it has no prices, no "was/now" and no ratings. The copy says "Black Friday starts now" with no end date. The only deadline, 12/02/2026, is in the grey fine print at the bottom. The asterisk and "select styles" language also undercuts "Everything".
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand promo that will get opens and clicks mostly on the offer alone. It leaves urgency, gender targeting and product-level proof unused. I'd rate it a 6 to 7.

## What's working

- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" headline is large and instantly readable over the hero.
- The hero is on-brand and seasonal, with the brown cashmere knit and cream skirt, and it has no render bugs.
- The 2x2 category grid gives clear, scannable entry points into the high-intent winter categories.
- There are two black full-width CTA bars ("SHOP WOMEN", "SHOP THE SALE"), so the buttons are obvious.
- The cashmere and organic denim copy keeps the brand's better-made positioning in a discount email.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is "SHOP WOMEN" only, which sidelines male shoppers. "Shop Men" appears only as a plain text link near the footer.
- There is no deadline in the body. The 12/02 end date sits in the grey fine print, so there's no urgency.
- The asterisk and "select styles" fine print conflict with "Everything", so the offer feels less honest.
- The category tiles show no price, sale price or product name, and there is no social proof.
- The body copy is generic ("better-made staples… perfect gifts") and the footer link stack is long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends 12/2", and put a countdown or "Through Cyber Monday" line under the headline.
- 2. Split the hero CTA into "SHOP WOMEN" and "SHOP MEN", or add a men's tile row. The recent "Gifts For Him" and men's sweater sends show that audience is being courted.
- 3. Add sale prices to the tiles, for example "Cashmere Crew $118 → $71". Hero products should show a concrete number.
- 4. Resolve the "Everything*" tension. Say "40% off sitewide" and name the exclusions plainly, or drop the asterisk from the headline.
- 5. Add a loyalty or early-access hook for subscribers, and a rating line on one hero product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday starts now: 40% off, ends 12/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off cashmere, denim & outerwear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sitewide through 12/2. Cashmere, organic denim and gifts they'll actually keep.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best sale of the year is live. Shop before your size goes.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Everlane "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email whose links are wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` redirects. The two probed links failed, but the evidence points to a probe limitation rather than a confirmed broken link. The compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures, likely a QA tool artifact:**
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7N83N2XKNJFCRRE5JHPBBKJ_0` ("Everlane") fails with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...'`.
  - `.../01M7N83N2XKNJFCRRE5JHPBBKJ_9` ("Shop Men") fails with `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=...'`.
  - Both errors show a path-only value, which means the tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location` header. RFC 7231 allows that, and browsers resolve it correctly. The probe did not resolve it against the host, so end-to-end destination status is **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- **Low coverage:** 11 of 12 material links were skipped, so the 38% pass rate reflects the skipped probes and not 38% of links actually working.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7N83N2XKNJFCRRE5JHPBBKJ` has no `alt` attribute. This is an open-tracking image, so the impact is minor (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel:** an empty `alt=""` would be correct for a decorative or tracking image. It is a low-severity omission.
- **Links styled like body text:** the head CSS sets `a:not([name]), a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }`. Links are therefore indistinguishable from surrounding black text by color or underline. This fails WCAG 1.4.1 (use of color) wherever links sit inline in copy.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Small mobile text:** `h4` is set to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`. Anything that renders as an `h4` will be very small on mobile.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML is cut off, so image `width`/`height`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the rest of the body could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|...|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation, so it is not a confirmed defect.
- **Footer not visible:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified. QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is listed.
- **Sender:** `support@everlane.com` is used for a promotional send. This is not a violation, but a dedicated marketing subdomain is the usual practice. The tracking domain `trk.send.everlane.com` suggests one is already in use for links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen:** the destination URLs carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`). The visible portion shows no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, but the strings are truncated, so this is inconclusive.
- **Landing alignment is unverified:** the probes failed, so it is unknown whether `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` show the 40% Black Friday offer promised in the subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the QA probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host, then re-run. Raise the probe limit above 1 of 12 links.
2. Pull the raw source from the ESP or an unrelayed copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, plus the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
3. Add underlines or a distinct color to inline links, and add `lang="en"` and a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Review the `h4` 10px mobile size.
6. Confirm UTM parameters on the final destination URLs, and check that the landing pages show the Black Friday offer.
