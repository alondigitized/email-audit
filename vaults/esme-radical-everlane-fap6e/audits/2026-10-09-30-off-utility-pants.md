# 30% Off Utility Pants
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-category pants send. Subject and body match: "30% Off Utility Pants" leads to a hero in brown utility trousers and three named products with prices. The "Friends & Family 30% Off Sitewide & Storewide" banner at the top carries the offer. The product copy is practical and workwear-focused ("Office-ready pants that work well beyond nine to five").
- It is also the fifth 30% Friends & Family send in four days. It follows "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6), "30% Off Online & In Stores" (10/8), "30% Off Better Sweaters" (10/8 and again 10/9) and "30% Off Outerwear" earlier today. The category-by-category subject lines are concrete, but the cadence is wearing thin. The subject is also redundant with the template, since the same banner has run on every send.
- The execution is tidy, but it undersells the discount. Prices are shown at $118, $138 and $88 with no strike-through or sale price, so you can't tell whether 30% is already applied. The deadline (10/13) is only in the grey fine print. I'd call it a solid, well-laid-out send held back by fatigue and a missing price payoff.

## What's working

- The subject names the category and the offer, and the render delivers exactly that.
- The hero is a full-bleed model in brown utility pants with a black "Shop Now" bar, so the eye lands on the product first.
- Three products each get a name, price, one-line fit benefit and their own "Shop Now" link.
- The "Shop All Pants" button gives a clear fallback CTA.
- The banner has Shop Women and Shop Men links, so either shopper can get in.
- The copy is specific on fit (slim, wide-leg, curved barrel leg) and mentions stretch.

## What's weak

- The prices have no strike-through or "now" price, so the 30% never shows up in the numbers.
- The 10/13 deadline appears only in the fine print. There is no visible urgency in the body.
- The hero text "Clocked In" is small and floats at the right of the image. It is not a real headline.
- The "Shop Now" text links beside the products are small and underlined, so they're easy to miss. The product images also look cramped, stacked edge to edge with no gaps.
- The product grid is women's only, yet the banner and nav push both genders. There is no social proof, no reviews and no sizing or color cues.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the math: put the original price struck through next to the 30%-off price for each pant, e.g. "$168 → $118". This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Bring the deadline into the hero, e.g. "Ends 10/13", rather than leaving it in the footer.
- 3. Replace "Clocked In" with a real headline and a one-line offer. Make the product CTAs buttons instead of small text links.
- 4. Add a star rating or a "best seller" tag to one pant for social proof.
- 5. Space out the product stack, and consider separate women's and men's utility pant blocks.
- 6. Vary the cadence. Space out the near-identical 30% sends or give each a distinct angle.
