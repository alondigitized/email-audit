# Fall’s a Little Fuzzy
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a good product send under a weak subject line. The hero is a model in a cream sherpa half-zip, with "Better / Get Cozy" split across her shoulders and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar. The two product rows (Reversible Teddy Jacket and Half-Zip Pullover) each have a one-line benefit and their own link. The 40% offer is only a thin banner at the very top, so the eye goes to the model first and the discount second.
- Everlane has sent Black Friday emails on 11/17 and twice on 11/18 ("The Best Black Friday: 40% Off Everything"). Those led with the offer in big type. This one demotes the offer to a header strip. That is a deliberate choice, but it spends the discount's pull on a send that sells a category.
- The subject, "Fall's a Little Fuzzy", is charming but carries no offer, no price and no deadline. In an inbox full of near-identical "40% off everything" sends, it is the one most likely to be skipped. Verdict: solid creative, mildly under-powered envelope, and it should carry the deal.

## What's working

- The hero is focused: one product (the cream sherpa half-zip), one black SHOP NOW bar, and no clutter.
- "Better Get Cozy" ties the product to the season and to Everlane's "Better" brand voice.
- The two zigzag product rows each give a concrete benefit: "A cocoon shape in soft teddy fleece. Flip it for a new look." and "Layer it under a jacket or over a tee."
- The sustainability line, "Crafted from certified recycled materials", gives a reason to buy beyond price.
- There are no render bugs. Images load, text is legible, and spacing is clean.
- "Shop All Outerwear" is a clear secondary CTA for people the two products don't convert.

## What's weak

- The 40% offer is a small banner above the logo, easy to skim past, and the hero never mentions price.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW". There are five identical labels, and none names the product.
- The burgundy "Better Gift Guide" block pulls attention toward gifting just as the cozy-outerwear story should close. Its copy, "Gifts they won't return", is a bit smug.
- The deadline (12/02) and the "select styles" caveat appear only in the grey footer. The banner says "Everything*" while the footnote says "select styles".
- The product rows show no price, sale price or review, so the click rests on vibe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer into the hero. Add "40% off" and the deadline to the image or directly under it, so the first screen carries the product and the discount.
- 2. Name the product in the CTAs. Use "Shop the Teddy Jacket" and "Shop the Half-Zip" so the buttons say something the email doesn't.
- 3. Show a price in each product row, for example "$X → $Y with 40% off". A cozy product with a visible price gives a reader a reason to click now.
- 4. Add a credible deadline above the fold, such as "Ends 12/2", rather than hiding it in the footer.
- 5. Cut or move the gift-guide block. Don't end the email on a different job from the one it started with.
- 6. Tighten the "everything" claim. Either drop the "everything*" language here or state the exclusions near the banner.
