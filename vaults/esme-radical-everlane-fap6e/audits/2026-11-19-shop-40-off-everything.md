# Shop 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Black Friday "40% off everything" send from Everlane in three days. It is a near-copy of the 2026-11-18 "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" email: a charcoal text panel, a short brand-voice paragraph, "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type, and a black SHOP BLACK FRIDAY bar. The offer is impossible to miss, and the copy ("Why not save more on already better prices?") is on-brand.
- There is no product, no model and no image anywhere. The email is all message and no merchandise, so nothing gives the reader a reason to click beyond the discount. The nav links at the bottom (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) are the only route into a category. The subject "Shop 40% Off Everything" is also a flatter version of the earlier subjects.
- Judgement: clean and legible, but redundant. A subscriber who has already seen this offer three times this week has no new reason to open it. It works as a reminder and little else.

## What's working

- "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is huge and white on charcoal, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The black SHOP BLACK FRIDAY bar sits directly under the offer and is full-width, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The brand-voice paragraph ("we felt you deserved a little more in return") frames the discount as a reward, which is a calm contrast to shouty Black Friday sends.
- The email is legible with no render bugs. Contrast is strong and nothing overlaps or is broken.
- The disclosure is clear. "*Exclusions Apply" appears right under the CTA, and the footer gives a real end date of 12/02/2026.

## What's weak

- There is no product imagery, so nothing in the email is shoppable. The CTA goes to a generic Black Friday page.
- It repeats the 2026-11-17 and 2026-11-18 "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" sends almost word for word. This is fatigue, not news.
- The deadline is buried in 8pt grey footer text. The body has no urgency, and 12/02 is two weeks away.
- "Everything*" with "Exclusions Apply" and "select styles" in the footer undercuts the honesty of the headline. The reader never learns what is excluded.
- Roughly 400px of dark panel is spent on copy with no visual hook, so the email feels heavy and static.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the text panel with a hero image and a short, real offer line, or drop in a product grid. Four best-sellers under the CTA would give the click a destination. The text-only format has already been sent twice.
- 2. Say what is new. If this is a reminder, make it one ("Now through 12/2") or tie it to a milestone. Don't repeat the same announcement.
- 3. Move the end date into the body, next to the offer, and name the main exclusions in one line.
- 4. Make the CTA specific. "Shop Women's 40% Off" or "Shop Sweaters at 40% Off" gives a verb and a noun, and it matches the category nav.
- 5. Space out the sends. One announcement plus one product-led follow-up beats four near-identical emails in 72 hours.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML template with tracking links on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The probe flagged two redirect failures, which look like a relative-`Location` redirect artifact and are unconfirmed as real breakage. The authentication and unsubscribe header checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers. The 38% pass rate is mostly this noise, not 62% broken content.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men"** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7RQSSRPX6PAFQBYXT84YXJR_4`): the probe error was `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Everlane"** (`…YXJR_0`): the same error for `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker answers with a redirect whose `Location` is a relative path with no scheme or host. Per RFC 7231 that is valid, so the failure is probably the QA probe not resolving it against the tracker host. If the tracker really does emit relative `Location` values, a client that doesn't resolve them against the tracker host would also fail. The probe can't distinguish the two, so this needs a manual `curl -I` on those two links.
- **Evidence the redirect targets exist:** the paths are `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` and `/pages/womens`, with Klaviyo `_kx` and `bxid` tokens carried through. The relative targets also mean the destination host is unconfirmed, which blocks any end-to-end landing check.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 6 skipped, so about half the links are unverified.
- **Click tracking:** all links go through the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/<id>_<n>` redirector. That is expected. It adds a redirect hop and depends on that tracker domain staying healthy.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt:** the image at `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7RQSSRPX6PAFQBYXT84YXJR` has no alt text. The `/o/` path suggests it is the open-tracking pixel, which is a low-severity finding. A pixel should carry `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is blank. Some clients and screen readers use it, so this is minor.
- **Link styling:** `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes the underline from all links. Links are then distinguishable by position only, which is a WCAG 1.4.1 concern for inline text links.
- **Web font:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is an external font import. Clients that strip `@import` fall back to the Arial stack declared in the CSS, so this is not a defect.
- **Outlook:** the MSO conditionals and `mso-hide` rules are present and well formed.
- **Truncation:** the HTML was truncated, so the body markup, other `<img>` tags and the footer couldn't be audited.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The `_kx` and `bxid` values are resolved per-recipient Klaviyo identifiers.

**5. Compliance**
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** weren't found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results** is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also a relay artifact.
- **Sender alignment:** the From address is `support@everlane.com` while links and tracking use `send.everlane.com`. That is a normal subdomain setup, but DKIM and SPF alignment should be confirmed from the raw headers.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and the physical address are in the truncated portion. The probe says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown, so it is unverified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **Landing targets:** these resolve to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` and `/pages/womens`. The "Shop Men" link goes to a men's collection and "Everlane" goes to a women's page. The subject says "Shop 40% Off Everything", and no sale-specific landing page is visible.
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible on the destinations. Tracking relies on Klaviyo `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…`. If GA4 attribution is needed, the missing `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` will cause sessions to be misattributed. I can't confirm this because the redirect targets are truncated.
- **Unverified:** whether the 40% discount applies automatically or needs a code can't be checked, since the redirects weren't followed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Run `curl -sI` on the `_4` and `_0` tracking links. Confirm the `Location` value and that it resolves to an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URL with a 200 response.
2. Fix the QA probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirector's base URL (`urljoin`). This removes the false-positive FAILs and the artificially low 38% score.
3. Pull the raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and alignment.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and put a descriptive `<title>` in the template.
5. Add underlines, or another non-colour cue, to inline text links.
6. Verify that `utm_*` parameters are appended on the final destination URLs, and that the sale landing page shows the 40% offer.
7. Probe the 6 skipped links, including the footer unsubscribe link, to complete coverage.
