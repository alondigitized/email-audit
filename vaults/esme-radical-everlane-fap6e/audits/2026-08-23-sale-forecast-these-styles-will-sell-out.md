# Sale Forecast: These Styles Will Sell Out
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Everlane runs back the exact denim-macro hero from 08/20's "30% Off Denim, Pants & More" — same tight-crop indigo fabric texture, same white all-caps overlay, same black "SHOP NOW" bar — but slaps a new subject line ("Sale Forecast: These Styles Will Sell Out") on top that the creative doesn't actually deliver on. The hero still reads "30% OFF* DENIM, PANTS & MORE," and only the small body copy beneath it ("Best-selling styles are starting to sell out") gestures at the scarcity hook the subject promised.
- Below the fold it's flat, no-model product photography — a pair of jeans and a pair of trousers on a gray background, captioned "SHOP DENIM" / "SHOP PANTS" — which is a step down in production value from the 08/21 and 08/22 sends that used real lifestyle photography and scored higher for it. This is the same discount, the same layout family, and the third time this week Everlane has hit "30% off denim/pants" — the fatigue is starting to show, and swapping the subject line without changing the hero creative reads as a cheap way to re-bait an open rather than a genuinely new campaign beat.
- Judgement: functional but lazy — a subject-line-only refresh on a creative that's already run once this week, with a scarcity promise the hero doesn't visually pay off.

## What's working

- Clean rendering — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- Legible, high-contrast "SHOP NOW" CTA button sits directly under the hero with no scroll needed.
- The two-product grid (denim / pants) gives a concrete next click beyond the generic hero button.
- Discount is unambiguous and specific: 30% off, with a clear expiration date in the fine print (08/24/2026, 11:59 PM PST).

## What's weak

- Subject promises "sell out" urgency/scarcity; the hero creative still just says "30% OFF DENIM, PANTS & MORE" — the scarcity story only shows up as small gray body text, disconnected from the visual hierarchy.
- This is a near-exact reuse of the 08/20 hero (same fabric macro shot, same headline, same black bar CTA) — a subscriber who opened that email will recognize this as a repeat, not a new send.
- No models, no lifestyle shot, no fit/sizing context on either product — just two flat-lay images on gray, weaker than the photography-led 08/21 and 08/22 sends this week.
- No "which styles are selling out" specificity — no product names, no "X left" counters, no urgency visual (e.g., a stock bar or countdown) to back up the sell-out claim.
- Fourth 30%-off denim/pants-adjacent send in five days — cadence fatigue risk is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: replace or overlay the denim-texture hero with actual sell-out cues — a "Selling Fast" badge, specific style names, or a stock indicator — so the scarcity hook isn't buried in body copy.
- 2. Swap the flat-lay product shots for on-model photography, consistent with what worked in the 08/21 and 08/22 sends.
- 3. Name 2-3 actual at-risk styles by name/price in the body copy instead of the generic "Best-selling styles are starting to sell out" line — specificity sells urgency better than a vague statement.
- 4. Retire the fabric-macro hero template for at least a week — it ran 08/20 and again today; rotate in a genuinely new visual so this doesn't read as a duplicate send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Only a Few Left in These Best-Sellers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Size Is Almost Gone — 30% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The wide-leg and tailored trouser are down to single digits.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before we're out of your size.`
