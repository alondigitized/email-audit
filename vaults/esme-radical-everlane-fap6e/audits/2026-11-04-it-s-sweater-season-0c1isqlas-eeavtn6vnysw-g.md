# It’s Sweater Season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal product send with no offer. The hero is a model in a taupe ribbed belted cardigan, with "Sweaters With a POV" split on either side of her. A black SHOP NOW bar sits under it. It reads well and is on-brand, but it gives nobody a reason to act today.
- The best idea is "To gift or keep for yourself", which ties the sweater push to the gift season. The weakest part is the "Better Gift Guide" block. "Now at better prices" hints at a discount but never says how much. That is the one place a concrete offer could have gone. This follows the 11/03 "Effortlessly Ribbed" and 11/04 "Your New Favorite Pants" sends. That makes three product-only emails in about 36 hours, with no price and no deadline in any of them.
- Judgement: a polished, forgettable 6. It works as brand upkeep and does little for conversion.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. A single model, a big cardigan and an obvious black SHOP NOW bar make the eye land in the right place.
- The three-product strip (brown henley vest, striped knit, the cardigan again) shows range without clutter.
- The "SHOP ALL SWEATERS" bar is a specific verb and noun, in the category the email is about.
- The "To gift or keep for yourself" line broadens the audience to gift buyers.
- There are no render bugs. Images load and the type is legible.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer or deadline anywhere. "Now at better prices" is vague and sits low in the email.
- The gift guide block is a large maroon slab with a lot of empty space and only two small underlined links. It looks unfinished and is easy to miss.
- The copy "Rich color, mixed textures, and details" is generic. No product is named and no price or fabric is shown.
- The product strip has no names, prices or reviews. The cardigan appears twice, in the hero and as the third tile.
- The footer stacks four large nav links and a row of social links, which is more than this email needs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete hook in the hero or just under it, such as a price anchor ("Merino from $98") or a gift deadline. Without one there is no reason to click today.
- 2. Rework the gift guide block. Say what the "better prices" are, shrink the dead space, and make the two links real buttons.
- 3. Add names and prices under the three product tiles, and swap the repeated cardigan for a different sweater.
- 4. Spread the three recent product sends further apart, or give each a different angle such as an offer, fit or fabric.
- 5. Replace "Rich color, mixed textures" with something specific, such as the fabric or fit of the hero cardigan.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ribbed, belted and soft. Gift it or keep it.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the right pronunciation.
- **Links have no non-color cue:** every link state is `color:#000; text-decoration:none` (`a:not([name])`, `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active`, `a:hover`). Unless body text is a different color, links can't be told apart from surrounding text. I can't check this because body text styling is truncated.
- **Tiny mobile `h4`:** inside `@media (max-width:480px)`, `h4 { font-size:10px !important }`. Any content in an `h4` renders at 10px on mobile. Whether `h4` is used in the body is unknown.
- **Web font via `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks do fall back to Arial/Helvetica (`"MaisonNeue-Light", Arial, ...`), so this is low severity.
- **Outlook handling looks fine:** the `mso` conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings` and the `.mj-outlook-group-fix` rule are in place. The viewport meta and `X-UA-Compatible` are also present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
