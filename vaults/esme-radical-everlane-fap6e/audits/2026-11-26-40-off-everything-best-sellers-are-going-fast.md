# 40% Off Everything. Best-Sellers Are Going Fast
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday creative Everlane has sent all week. The hero is the quilted tan jacket under "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*", and the offer lands instantly. Only the subject line and a one-sentence "best-sellers" blurb are new. A subscriber who got "40% Off Everything on Your Gift List" and "Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?" on 11/24 and 11/25 has seen this hero several times.
- "Best-Sellers Are Going Fast" promises specific products, but the body shows none. No best-seller is pictured, named, or priced. The body has a gender split, a tiny copy block, a gift-guide panel and a footer nav. Urgency is also vague. The real deadline (12/02/2026 at 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the fine print, so "going fast" has nothing behind it.
- It is a clean, readable send but a repeat. It beats nothing the brand has already sent this week.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge, high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- The black SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN bar is an unambiguous, thumb-friendly CTA directly under the hero.
- The layout is clean with a clear hierarchy and no visible render bugs.
- The burgundy Better Gift Guide block adds a seasonal second path with her/him split.
- The material callouts (RWS-certified wool, organic cotton) fit Everlane's brand voice.

## What's weak

- The subject promises best-sellers, but no best-seller product appears anywhere in the email.
- The hero is a repeat of the creative from the 11/24 and 11/25 sends, so it feels stale and the cadence feels heavy.
- The CTAs are generic gender splits that link to category pages, not to specific products.
- The deadline is buried in 8pt gray fine print, and the asterisk exclusions ("select styles") are hard to read.
- The burgundy gift panel has a large empty lower half, and there are no reviews, prices or product imagery.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the body copy with a 3–4 product best-seller grid. Each item should show a name, a "was/now" price and a star rating, and link to its own product page. This would lift the email most, because it delivers on the subject.
- 2. Put the deadline near the hero, for example "Ends Dec 2" or a countdown, so the urgency is credible.
- 3. Rotate the hero. Use a best-seller product shot or a men's/women's split instead of the jacket seen all week.
- 4. Sharpen the CTAs: "Shop Women's Best-Sellers" and "Shop Men's Best-Sellers".
- 5. Tighten the gift guide panel and add product imagery to fill the dead space.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Top 10 best-sellers, 40% off through Dec 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The sweater everyone's buying is 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `RWS wool sweaters and organic-cotton denim, selling out by size. Ends 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-reordered pieces, all 40% off. Sizes are going.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup, `kl-` classes) with all links wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The two flagged link failures look like probe-side redirect-handling errors rather than confirmed broken links. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, not because they were shown to be missing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures:**
  - `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8AN1XMHBHHY47K0ZY74WNEM_0` (text "Everlane") errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...'`.
  - `.../01M8AN1XMHBHHY47K0ZY74WNEM_8` (text "Shop Men") errored with `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=...'`.
  - In both cases the tracker's redirect appears to return a host-relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`). Browsers resolve that correctly, and the probe's HTTP client apparently doesn't. This is **unconfirmed as a user-facing break**. A relative `Location` is unusual for a tracker, though, so re-probe with a client that resolves relative redirects.
- **Probe coverage is low:** only 2 of 12 material links were probed (10 skipped). The "38% pass rate" is therefore not a reliable measure of link health. The remaining 10 links are unverified.
- **Image missing alt:** the flagged `src` is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8AN1XMHBHHY47K0ZY74WNEM`, which is the open-tracking pixel. Missing alt on it is expected and harmless. I could not see alt coverage for content images because the HTML was truncated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is a minor accessibility and web-view issue.
- **Link styling:** the stylesheet sets `a:not([name]), a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }`. Links are indistinguishable from body text by color or underline unless a button or image wraps them. This is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk for any inline text links.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped or blocked by Gmail and several other clients. Fallbacks (`Arial, Helvetica Neue`) are declared, so this is a graceful degradation, not a defect.
- **Mobile mobile `h4`:** the mobile `h4` rule sets `font-size: 10px !important`, which is below a legible minimum if any copy uses `h4`.
- **Dark mode:** `.ev-clay-light` / `.ev-clay-dark` with `[data-ogsc]` selectors is present for Outlook.com dark mode, and I found no problems with it.
- **Truncation:** the HTML was cut off, so I could not check the rest of the markup (alt text on content images, footer, preheader).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The `_kx` and `bxid` values are Klaviyo recipient identifiers resolved at send time, which is expected.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unverified (WARN):** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed as missing at the sender**. Verify against the raw message at the original recipient or an independent seed inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Unverified (WARN):** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses the `send.everlane.com` tracking subdomain with a `support@everlane.com` From address, so check DMARC alignment externally.
- **Not assessable:** the body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address would be in the truncated footer. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it is listed.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` for a promotional send is a mild deliverability and routing concern. Replies are likely to land in a support queue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The visible destinations carry only Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). I saw no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` in the QA output. The URLs are truncated, so UTMs may sit later in the string. Confirm them on the resolved URLs.
- The probed targets are `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, both site-relative paths. Neither probe followed the redirect to a final page, so I could not verify landing-page status or whether the 40% offer carries through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probing with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. Then raise the probe cap so all 12 material links, including the footer unsubscribe link, are checked.
2. Capture raw headers from the original delivery or a seed inbox. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
3. Confirm UTM parameters on the final landing URLs, and confirm the promo applies on `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`.
4. Set a meaningful `<title>` and add `text-decoration: underline` to inline text links.
5. Exclude open-pixel images from the missing-alt check to cut false positives.
6. Re-run the audit with the full HTML, because the truncation hides the footer and the compliance checks that depend on it.
