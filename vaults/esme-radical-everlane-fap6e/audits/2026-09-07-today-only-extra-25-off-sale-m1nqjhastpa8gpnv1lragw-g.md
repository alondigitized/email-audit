# Today Only: Extra 25% Off Sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Everlane Men" send of the same "Today Only: Extra 25% Off" campaign already audited today — the peony/tulip macro shot replaces the tan-model watercolor entirely, which is a genuinely different hero from the "End of Summer Sale" template that's run since 08/01. But the men's product grid undercuts it: 9 tiles of oxford shirts and tees in near-identical light blue/white/tan colorways with zero differentiation, no price, no product name, no fit info.
- The offer is the strongest lever here — "Extra 25% off, already up to 70% off" is a real, escalating, time-boxed deal ("Today Only," valid until 09/08 11:59 PM PST), and that's what should carry this email. But the creative doesn't do the offer justice: no visible price anywhere, no hero product, and a grid that reads as filler rather than curated men's picks.
- Solid subject and hero concept, weak product merchandising. This lands mid-pack — better than the stale watercolor sends, worse than it could be if the grid showed actual discounted prices.

## What's working

- Hero flower macro is a clean break from the tan-dress-model watercolor Everlane's leaned on since 08/01 — visually distinct and not stale.
- Offer stacking ("Extra 25% Off" + "Already up to 70% Off") is a concrete, escalating discount, not vague language.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency is clear and legible in the hero, reinforced by the fine-print deadline (09/08 11:59 PM PST).
- Two unambiguous "SHOP NOW" CTA bars (hero + below grid) with no rendering issues.
- Simple nav footer (Shop Women/Men/New Arrivals/Best-Sellers) gives an easy secondary path if the grid doesn't land.

## What's weak

- No prices anywhere in the product grid — for a 25%-off sale email, that's a real miss; recipients can't gauge the deal without clicking through.
- All 9 grid tiles are shirts/tees in washed-out blue, white, and tan — visually monotonous, nothing pops as a hero product.
- No product names or fit/size details under any tile, so there's no way to judge what's actually on sale.
- Subhead "Deepest discounts of the sale. Don't miss out." is generic filler that doesn't reinforce the specific 25%/70% numbers already stated in the hero.
- This is at minimum the second near-identical "Today Only: Extra 25%" template hitting inboxes today (Everlane Women version scored 6.00/10 earlier) — cadence risk for anyone subscribed to multiple Everlane lists.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices (strikethrough original + sale price) under every grid tile — this is the single biggest lever; a discount email with no visible discount math under the products is leaving conversion on the table.
- 2. Feature one hero product above the grid (e.g., the striped long-sleeve, which is the only tile with real pattern/texture) instead of 9 flat tiles of similar basics.
- 3. Swap the generic subhead for something that repeats the numbers: "Shirts, tees, and more — up to 70% off, plus an extra 25% today."
- 4. Diversify the grid colorway mix — right now blue/white/tan repeats across all three rows; pull in at least one darker or patterned item per row for visual contrast.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: 70% Off Just Got Bigger`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shirts, tees, and layers — deepest markdowns of the season.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends tonight: stack an extra 25% off already-reduced styles.`
