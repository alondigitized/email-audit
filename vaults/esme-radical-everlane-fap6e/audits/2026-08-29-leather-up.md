## What's weak

- None of the six grid products have names, prices, or (visibly) individual click targets — it reads as a static collage, not a shoppable grid.
- No offer or price is shown anywhere in the body, yet the footer disclaimer references "*Offer valid until 09/07/2026" — confusing and inconsistent.

- No color/material variety context (e.g., "shop in 4 colors") that would nudge browsers toward a click.

- 3. Split the CTA hierarchy: keep "SHOP NOW" as the dominant action, demote "READ MORE" (sustainability) to a text link so it doesn't compete visually.
