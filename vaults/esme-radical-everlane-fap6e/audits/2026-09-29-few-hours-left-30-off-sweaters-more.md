# Few Hours Left: 30% Off Sweaters & More
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's third "last call" beat on the same 30% sweater promo. Yesterday's "Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sweaters & More" (09-28) already used the urgency, and this one repeats it with "Few Hours Left." The creative is clean. The stacked knit hero with "30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" is legible, the black Shop Women / Shop Men bar is unmistakable, and the six-sweater grid is tidy.
- The credibility problem is in the fine print. The footer says "Offer valid until 09/23/2026 at 11:59 PM PST," five days before this send. A reader who scrolls to the bottom sees that a "few hours left" deadline already passed. The hero also says "LAST CHANCE" while the subject says "Few Hours Left," so the urgency messaging is inconsistent. Below the hero the email goes flat: the grid has no names or prices, and the only copy is "Don't miss out on this offer."
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand 6-7 send. Repeating the same offer and urgency three days running, plus the expired date, keeps it from doing better.

## What's working

- The hero is a real, tactile photo of stacked knits, with "30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" large and readable.
- The offer is stated once, plainly, and is visible immediately without scrolling.
- The black two-button bar (Shop Women / Shop Men) is high contrast and gives a clear next step.
- The six-sweater grid on a neutral gray backdrop is clean and shows color range (rust, sage, black, gray, navy).
- The seasonal fit is right for late September, and the brand voice stays minimal and consistent.

## What's weak

- The footer says the offer expired 09/23/2026, which contradicts the "few hours left" urgency and undermines trust.
- The hero says "LAST CHANCE" but the subject says "Few Hours Left." No actual deadline (time or date) appears in the body.
- The sweater grid shows no names, prices, or sale prices, and nothing signals that the tiles are individually shoppable.
- "Don't miss out on this offer." is generic filler where a deadline or product proof point should be.
- The small "LAST CHANCE" label is thin, low-contrast white type over textured knit.
- The chair-and-sweater lifestyle image at the bottom has no CTA and adds length without adding a reason to click.
- This is the same 30% sweater offer, the same subject skeleton, and the same urgency as yesterday, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the deadline. Put a real, current one in both the hero and the fine print, e.g. "Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT." Remove the stale 09/23 date. This is the biggest credibility and click lift.
- 2. Add product names and "$X → $Y" pricing under the grid tiles, and link each tile to its product.
- 3. Replace "Don't miss out on this offer." with a specific line, such as "Merino, cashmere and cotton knits, all 30% off through tonight."
- 4. Make the hero label bolder and larger, and match its wording to the subject.
- 5. Add a third CTA below the lifestyle image or cut the image. Vary the creative from yesterday's send.
- 6. Differentiate the final-hours send from the "Ends Tonight" one, for example with a best-sellers or low-stock angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends at midnight: 30% off sweaters`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sweater pick, 30% off until 11:59 PM`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Merino, cashmere & cotton knits. Sale ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best-selling crews and quarter-zips are still in stock. Last call.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email was sent through Klaviyo, with links wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking. The three FAIL results come from relative redirect targets that the QA probe couldn't follow, and the authentication and unsubscribe headers couldn't be verified. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative `Location` redirects (3 links).** The tracker returned a relative target instead of an absolute URL, and the probe failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`. Affected links:
  - `…/l/01M3N6MMGKN606BHVXBR486YQF_0` ("Everlane Logo")
  - `…/l/01M3N6MMGKN606BHVXBR486YQF_1` (hero image)
  - `…/l/01M3N6MMGKN606BHVXBR486YQF_12` ("Shop Men", `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?…`)
- **Likely a probe limitation, not a user-facing break.** RFC 7231 allows relative `Location` values, and browsers resolve them against the tracker host. However, a redirect from `trk.send.everlane.com` to a bare `/pages/womens` path would resolve to the tracker's own host. That would 404 unless the tracker host proxies or rewrites it, or the tracker sends a different `Location` to real clients. This is unconfirmed, so verify it with a real browser or `curl -sIL` on the three URLs.
- **Only 12 of 26 links were probed.** The other 14 (footer, social) were skipped, so their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on `…/o/01M3N6MMGKN606BHVXBR486YQF`.** This URL is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel, not content imagery. It isn't a real accessibility failure, and the check should exclude tracking pixels. Confirm the tag has `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** It contains only whitespace (`<title> </title>`). This is minor, but some clients and web-view renderings show it.
- The HTML source includes the MSO conditional comments, `mso-hide`, and mobile media queries at `max-width: 480px`. No rendering defects are visible in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, and Klaviyo normally adds them.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Check the raw message headers or the `everlane.com` and `send.everlane.com` DNS records directly.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither was visible in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it was reported. Verify the footer contains both.
- **Sender.** The From address is `support@everlane.com`. This isn't a violation, but a support mailbox as the marketing sender can affect reply handling and reputation segmentation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The redirect targets carry Klaviyo params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no visible `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. The parameter strings are cut off, so this is unconfirmed. If GA-based attribution is expected, check the full destination URLs.
- The logo and hero image both go to `/pages/womens`. The subject promotes "30% Off Sweaters & More", but neither link goes to a sweater or sale collection, based only on the truncated URLs. Confirm the landing page shows the promoted offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on the three failing tracker URLs (`_0`, `_1`, `_12`). Confirm the final destination resolves to `everlane.com`, not a relative path on the tracker host.
2. Inspect the raw source message in the receiving mailbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
3. Confirm the unsubscribe link and postal address in the footer.
4. Check that the full destination URLs include `utm_*` parameters if attribution depends on them.
5. Fix the QA tooling:
   - Follow relative redirects by resolving them against the current URL.
   - Exclude `/o/` tracking pixels from the alt-text check.
   - Mark relay-dependent header checks as "unverifiable" rather than WARN.
