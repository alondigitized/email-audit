- Each module names a product and a fabric (Cashmere, Luxe Merino, Everyday Cotton), so the reader knows what they'd be clicking into.
- The layout is consistent: paired model shots, a product name, a quote and an underlined SHOP NOW. It scans quickly on mobile.
- The black SHOP NOW bar under the hero is unmistakable and matches Everlane's recent sends.
- The "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" cream block closes on a value message that fits the sale.

## What's weak

- The 30% offer is confined to the top banner, and the hero image and headline don't mention it.
- No deadline is visible above the fold. "Friends & Family" implies urgency, but the actual end date (10/13) is buried in the fine print.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW", including the three product-specific ones. None says what's behind the click, such as "Shop the Cashmere Boxy Crew".
- No prices or discounted prices are shown on any product. A reader doesn't see what 30% off actually means for a cashmere sweater.
- It's the third 30%-off-adjacent send in 48 hours, after 10-06 "Early Access" and 10-07 "Friends & Family". Cadence fatigue is a risk.

- 1. Bring the offer into the hero. Add "30% off sitewide, through 10/13" to the hero overlay or directly under it, and let the reviews support the offer.
- 2. Show prices under each product, with the original price struck through and the sale price next to it. The reviews are already doing the persuasion, so give the reader a number to act on.
- 3. Make each CTA specific: "Shop the Cashmere Boxy Crew", "Shop the Merino Half-Zip" and "Shop the Cotton Boxy Sweater".
- 4. Add a star rating or review count per product (for example "4.8 ★ · 1,200 reviews") to strengthen the social proof.
- 5. Move the end date out of the fine print and into the top banner: "30% off ends 10/13".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style HTML send with wrapped tracking links on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The visible issues are one probe failure on a redirect, an empty `<title>` and no `lang` attribute, and missing auth and unsubscribe headers. The headers are probably a relay artifact. The QA run's 43% pass rate reflects the probe limits (12 links probed, 20 skipped) more than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4C1TGH7FB8P65JFJXBK9QPJ_3` (link text "Everlane") failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`. The tracker answered with a host-relative path instead of an absolute URL.
  - Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so real recipients likely land on the page. The probe's HTTP client can't follow it.
  - I can't confirm user impact from the data. The tracker should still emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...` URLs.
- **[WARN] 429 on `…_2` ("Shop Men"):** This is rate limiting by the tracker, not a broken link. It is unverified, and I'm not counting it as a defect.
- **[WARN] Unprobed link:** `…_31` was not checked because the 70s time budget ran out. It is neither a pass nor a fail.
- **Coverage gap:** 20 of 32 links were skipped, so the pass rate understates link health.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a language.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is blank.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4C1TGH7FB8P65JFJXBK9QPJ` has no `alt`. It is the open-tracking pixel, so impact is low, but add `alt=""`.
- **Web font fallback:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is acceptable.
- **Viewport and mobile CSS:** The viewport meta tag, Outlook conditionals (`mj-outlook-group-fix`, `OfficeDocumentSettings`) and 480px media queries are present.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML is truncated, so body-level alt text, button markup and image sizing could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) are visible in the available source or in the subject line. The truncated body can't be fully ruled out.
