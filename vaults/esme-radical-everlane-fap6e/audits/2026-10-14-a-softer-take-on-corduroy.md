# A Softer Take on Corduroy
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product-mood email with no offer. The hero is an olive corduroy trouser under a tan jacket, with "Corduroy Connection" split on either side of the legs and a black SHOP NOW bar below. It looks good and fits the "A Softer Take on Corduroy" subject. It sells a fabric story, though, not a reason to act today.
- It follows a week of 30% Friends & Family sends, including "One Last Chance for 30% Off Sitewide" earlier today and "A New Season Comes Into View" before that. A subscriber who just saw "ends tonight" has no discount here to act on. The closing block, "Better prices, still Everlane," hints at a pricing story but shows no price, percentage or threshold. It's the third or fourth Everlane send today, so the cadence is heavy and the creative is soft.
- Overall it's a competent, forgettable send: good photography and a clear layout, with no urgency or concrete hook.

## What's working

- The hero is strong and on-theme. Olive cord trousers with a belt and ballet flat make the fabric obvious at a glance.
- The six-item product grid (shirts and trousers in brown, black, cream and burgundy) shows range and color options in one scan.
- The full-width black SHOP NOW bar under the hero is unambiguous and easy to tap.
- The lifestyle model shot in a denim jacket over brown cord trousers shows the outfit in use and gives the grid some context.
- The layout is uncluttered, with generous whitespace and consistent Everlane typography. No broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. After a week of 30% off, a send with no discount or deadline feels like a step down.
- "Better Prices, Still Everlane" is vague. It teases pricing but gives no number, so it reads as a claim without proof.
- The product grid has no prices, names or individual links, so there's nothing concrete to evaluate.
- The overlaid "Corduroy" and "Connection" is thin, dark text over a mid-tone photo and a bit hard to read. It also splits the headline around the legs, which is awkward.
- The body copy is generic ("Made with quality you can feel") and doesn't mention fit, sizing or the pinwale detail that distinguishes these styles.
- The cadence is repetitive: this is another same-day send after the F&F closing emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific number in the hero or the closing block, such as a price ("Cord trousers from $98") or a member perk. "Better prices" with no figure is the biggest gap.
- 2. Add price and name captions under each grid item, and link each to its product page instead of sending everything to a general shop.
- 3. Rewrite the hero overlay as a single, higher-contrast line, for example "Soft Cord, Just In", set on a lighter area or with a subtle scrim.
- 4. Replace the generic body copy with a fit or fabric detail: pinwale cord, relaxed barrel leg, available sizes.
- 5. Space this send away from the F&F closing emails, or fold it into one fall-collection message.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built email has an empty `<title>`, no `lang` attribute, and an unlabelled tracking image. Both QA link failures come from relative redirect targets that the probe couldn't follow, which is probably a probe limitation. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Sign Up for Texts"** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4Y2SAGDRXJN43F758XX0NDM_17`): the probe errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link** (`…_0`): the same error, with target `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect returns a relative path in its `Location` header. That is legal under RFC 7231, but a client that doesn't resolve it against the tracker host will fail. This looks like a probe limitation, not a confirmed broken link.
- **Not ruled out:** the tracker may be emitting a relative target on a host-less redirect, and a mail client could mishandle that. Re-probe by resolving the `Location` header against `https://trk.send.everlane.com` to confirm the final destinations return 200.
- **Coverage:** 12 links were probed and 15 skipped (footer and social), so those 15 are unverified. The 38% pass rate reflects this probe behavior, not 62% broken links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is a minor issue, but it hurts accessible naming and the web-view tab.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** the QA check flagged `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4Y2SAGDRXJN43F758XX0NDM`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so the real impact is low. It should still carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **External font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is blocked or stripped by many clients, including Gmail. The font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica, so this is acceptable.
- **Viewport and dark mode:** the viewport meta tag and the `[data-ogsc]` dark-mode handling are present.
- **Truncated source:** I couldn't check the body markup, such as the image `alt` values and table roles.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or the subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verified:** the QA warnings say `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The checker itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers. These are gaps in the data, not confirmed defects.
- **Needs checking:** the footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address. They sit in the truncated portion, and the QA note says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't listed.
- **Sending domain:** the From address is `support@everlane.com`, and tracking runs through `send.everlane.com`. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from a raw `.eml` header, not through the relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen:** the destination paths carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`), and the QA output truncates the URLs.
- **UTMs:** I couldn't confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` are present.
- **Landing pages:** the `/pages/womens` and `/pages/sms-signup` pages couldn't be fetched, so I couldn't check them. None of the corduroy landing pages the subject implies were visible in the probed set.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe, resolving relative `Location` headers against `trk.send.everlane.com`. Fix the probe if it's at fault. If the live tracker truly emits relative redirects, ask Everlane's ESP to emit absolute URLs.
2. Add a `lang` attribute to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`, such as the subject line.
3. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel `…/o/01M4Y2SAGDRXJN43F758XX0NDM`.
4. Capture raw headers by sending to a seed inbox that preserves them. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the `Authentication-Results` SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome.
5. Fetch the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and the UTM parameters on the product links.
