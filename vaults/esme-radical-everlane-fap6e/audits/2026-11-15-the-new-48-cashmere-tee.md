# The New $48 Cashmere Tee
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's strongest send in recent weeks, and the reason is the subject. "The New $48 Cashmere Tee" puts a hard price on the table, where the last six sends (Holiday-Ready Styles, Holiday Dresses, New Accessories, Meet Everpuff™ Cloud) carried none. The only recent exception was the "Gifts For Him Starting at $50" send. $48 for cashmere is a real hook, and the email follows through. The hero is a model in a taupe boxy tee with "Cashmere Boxy Tee" in large type and a black "SHOP THE BOXY TEE" bar directly below. Further down, "Priced Better at $48" is overlaid on a close-up crop.
- The execution is cleaner than it is sharp. The $48 appears only in the subject and on the second image, in thin white text over a busy knit and arm. It is absent from the hero, where the eye lands first, and the hero copy says "a better price" without stating it. The "limited quantities" and "head start on gifting" line is real urgency, but it sits in small type below the colorway row. The secondary CTA, "SHOP ALL SWEATERS", sends the click away from the tee. The GCS sourcing block adds trust but lengthens the email.
- Judgement: a good send with a strong subject, held back by a hero that buries its best fact and a CTA that dilutes the focus. It is around a 7.

## What's working

- The subject leads with a concrete price and a named product, which is rare in this brand's recent cadence.
- The hero has one clear CTA bar, "SHOP THE BOXY TEE", that names the product and sits right under the image.
- The creator quote ("doesn't scratch me") answers the main objection to cheap cashmere.
- The three-colorway row (cream, black, oatmeal) shows range and gives a sense of availability.
- The "Certified Cashmere / Trusted Factories / Priced Better at $48" overlay packs quality and price into one image.

## What's weak

- The hero never states $48. It says "at a better price", so the offer is hidden above the fold.
- The "$48" overlay is thin white text over a busy knit and skin crop. "Certified Cashmere" also crosses the model's shoulder edge, which hurts legibility.
- "Shop All Sweaters" is a generic CTA that dilutes the tee focus.
- The limited-quantities and gifting line is small and has no deadline or specific stock signal.
- The colorway thumbnails aren't labeled, priced or visibly linked to individual product pages.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "$48" in the hero headline, for example "The $48 Cashmere Boxy Tee", and make the CTA "SHOP THE $48 TEE". This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Make the colorway row a shoppable strip with color names, the $48 price and a "Shop Black / Cream / Oatmeal" link under each. Replace "Shop All Sweaters" with a tee-specific CTA.
- 3. Move the "limited quantities" line up under the hero CTA and bold it. Add a concrete cue such as "Black sold out last season".
- 4. Give the overlay text a darker backing or a plain background so the price reads clearly.
- 5. Shorten or move the GCS block below the footer nav links, or cut it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The $48 cashmere tee, in 3 colors`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Certified cashmere, soft without a layer underneath. Limited quantities.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tamara Mory wears it all day. Grab your color before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is Klaviyo-generated (MJML-derived) and has valid structure, a viewport meta tag and mobile media queries. The QA run's 33% pass rate is mostly caused by probe limitations (relative redirect targets, skipped link probes, missing relay headers), so only a few issues are confirmed from the source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Three FAIL results, probably probe artifacts rather than broken links.** The failures are `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`, `'/pages/womens?_kx=…'` and `'/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…'`. All three come from `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7GJTFQ5F734109AXW4STCVZ_{14,0,10}`.
  - The error means the tracker returned a redirect whose `Location` is a relative path, and the QA client couldn't resolve it. Relative `Location` values are valid under RFC 7231 and browsers handle them.
  - The data does not show a user-facing break. It does show that the click-tracking redirector emits relative `Location` headers. Strict clients or scanners may handle these inconsistently, so this is worth verifying manually.
- **Link health is unverified.** "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped" means no destination status codes were captured, including for the primary CTAs.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7GJTFQ5F734109AXW4STCVZ` is present, which is normal for Klaviyo.
- **Tracking parameters present:** `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…`. The `cm_fl…` value is truncated in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Confirmed: empty `<title> </title>`.** This is minor, but it affects the web-view title and some assistive tech.
