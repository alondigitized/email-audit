# Ends Tonight: Extra 20% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- At least this one has a new hero — a soft cloud/watercolor sky gradient instead of the same peony/tulip macro shot that's been recycled across five prior sends today and yesterday. But the offer, structure, and copy are unchanged from the entire "Extra 25%/20% Off" run: split headline over a photo, black "SHOP NOW" bar, "already up to 70% off" callout. The only real move here is dropping the discount from 25% to 20%, which reads as a downgrade, not urgency.
- The cloud imagery is abstract and disconnected from product — no clothing, no model, nothing that signals what's actually on sale. Combined with zero product shots anywhere in the body, this is pure text-on-photo with generic nav links below (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers). It's clean and on-brand but does nothing to earn a click beyond the discount number itself.
- Given this is the sixth near-identical "Today Only" discount email from Everlane in two days, fatigue is a real risk — and dropping from 25% to 20% is going to read as a worse deal to anyone who saw yesterday's sends.

## What's working

- Clear, legible headline hierarchy: "ENDS TONIGHT" / "EXTRA 20% OFF SALE STYLES" is scannable at a glance.
- "Already up to 70% off" stacks the discount for a bigger perceived value.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("SHOP NOW") with strong black-bar contrast against the pastel hero.
- Clean footer nav (Find a Store, Sign Up for Texts, Help Center) with no clutter.
- No render bugs — image loads fully, text is crisp, no overlap.

## What's weak

- No product imagery anywhere — just an abstract sky gradient with no connection to the sale merchandise.
- Discount dropped from 25% (prior sends) to 20%, which reads as a worse offer to a fatigued subscriber.
- This is the sixth "Today Only"-style discount email in ~36 hours; no acknowledgment of cadence, no differentiation beyond the hero art.
- Body copy below the fold is generic filler ("Big markdowns on styles worth wearing now and later. Don't miss out.") — doesn't add new information.
- No urgency reinforcement near the CTA itself (deadline only appears in the fine print at the very bottom).

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual product shot in the hero — even a small grid of 3-4 sale items would do more to drive clicks than another abstract gradient.
- 2. Fix the discount inconsistency — going from 25% to 20% within the same 48-hour window undercuts trust; if this is a genuinely separate/smaller promo, say so explicitly instead of implying continuity.
- 3. Move the countdown/deadline ("Ends 11:59 PM PST") up near the CTA, not buried in the footer legal text.
- 4. Segment or space out sends — six similar promos in two days is guaranteed to suppress future opens; alternate with non-discount content (new arrivals, styling) to reset fatigue.
