# How About An Extra 20% Off?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same "End of Summer Sale" template Everlane has been running since 08/01 — same tan-dress model, same olive/cream left/right headline split, same black "SHOP NOW" bar, same 9-tile product grid layout. The only variable across this entire week of sends has been the subject line and the discount framing in the hero, and today's subject ("How About An Extra 20% Off?") is just another rewording of the identical "EXTRA 20% OFF SALE STYLES" hero copy that's been unchanged for over a month.
- The product grid is a small plus — nine actual clothing items with real photography gives some browsing value — but nothing here signals urgency, newness, or that this is a distinct campaign versus yesterday's or the day before's near-identical send. A subscriber who's seen four of these this week has no reason to open this one over the last.
- Judgement: functionally fine, creatively exhausted. This is fatigue-inducing cadence dressed up as a fresh offer.

## What's working

- Clean, legible layout with strong visual hierarchy — hero image, headline, CTA bar all land in the right order.
- Product grid gives concrete shoppable items (dresses, tops, knits) rather than a generic lifestyle shot.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, CTA bars are crisp.
- Footer is complete and compliant — clear offer terms, expiration date (09/08/2026), unsubscribe link.

## What's weak

- Identical hero creative to at least 5+ prior sends this week (08/01 template, same model, same olive/cream split, same black bar) — zero visual differentiation.
- Subject line doesn't match or extend the hero copy in any meaningful way — it's a vaguer rephrase of "Extra 20% Off Sale Dresses & Shirts" sent just one day prior.
- No preview/preheader text visible distinct from junk — nothing complements the subject to earn the open.
- No urgency in the visible creative itself (expiration date is buried in the footer fine print, not in the hero).
- Cadence problem: this is the same discount tier and same template as multiple recent sends, which trains subscribers to ignore rather than open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this hero for at least a week.** The 08/01 template needs a real replacement — the 09/04 "Way-High Denim" mirrored-model creative proved Everlane can do a genuine visual reset; do more of that and less of this.
- 2. **Put the deadline in the hero, not just the footer.** "Ends 9/8" in the headline block would convert the vague subject into real urgency.
- 3. **Differentiate the subject from the hero instead of restating it.** If the hero says "Extra 20% Off Sale Styles," don't send a subject that says the same thing more weakly.
- 4. **Tighten the preview text to add new information** (e.g., name a category or bestseller) rather than leaving it blank/generic.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Dresses Just Got 20% Cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off, plus an extra 20% at checkout — through 9/8.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Dresses, knits, and tanks you saved — now with an extra 20% off.`
