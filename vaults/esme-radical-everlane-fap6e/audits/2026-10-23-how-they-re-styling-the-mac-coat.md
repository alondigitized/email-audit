# How They’re Styling The Mac Coat
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a UGC-style lookbook for one product, the Twill Oversized Mac Coat. It's a strong creative idea. A trench coat is the right piece for late October, and the real-people photos feel more credible than studio shots. The hero shows a model in a tan mac coat with jeans and a brown bag. "MAC-ro Influence" is a clever headline, and a black SHOP NOW bar sits right under it.
- The email doesn't give the reader a reason to buy now. No price, no coat details and no reviews appear anywhere. The Fall Essentials Event (up to 30% off) is pushed to a blue block at the bottom. Four rows of creator photos are each followed by a tiny underlined "SHOP NOW" link, so a reader has to scroll about 2,000 pixels before seeing any offer. Whether the coat is even included in the sale is unclear.
- Overall it's pretty and on-brand but low-converting. It's the fourth Fall Essentials-adjacent send in four days, after "Previously on Scenes From Fall" and "Fall Essentials Event: Up to 30% Off". It repeats the same lifestyle-led, offer-light pattern. Judgement: a solid 5.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: a full-bleed model in the mac coat, a playful headline and a clear black SHOP NOW bar above the fold.
- Four creator rows with @handles add social proof and show the coat styled in different ways (with jeans, over a striped top, with a sweater tied at the waist).
- The tan coat in a city setting fits the season.
- The copy is short and in the brand's voice: "We asked a few friends of the brand to show us how they wear..."
- The layout is clean, with consistent two-up photo pairs and plenty of white space.

## What's weak

- No price or product detail for the coat is shown. A reader sees no fabric, fit, sizes or colors.
- The sale offer sits at the very bottom, and the email never says whether the coat qualifies.
- The four per-row "SHOP NOW" links are small and underlined, and they're easy to miss on mobile. They all look the same, so it isn't clear whether they go to the coat or to a general page.
- Some hero overlay text near the bottom left (the creator handle) is faint and hard to read against the photo.
- It's the same lifestyle-first, offer-light formula as the last several sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the coat's price and a one-line benefit under the hero, for example "The Twill Oversized Mac Coat, $X, now with 30% off." Make the offer part of the main message instead of a footer block.
- 2. Replace the repeated "SHOP NOW" links with specific, button-style CTAs such as "Shop the Mac Coat". Add a small product card (name, price, colors) to each creator row.
- 3. Add proof: star ratings, a "best seller" tag or a line about the fabric.
- 4. Move the Fall Essentials block up, below the first or second creator row.
- 5. Cut to three creator rows to shorten the scroll.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-sent email (`_kx`/`bxid` params, `kl-*` classes, `static-forms.klaviyo.com` fonts) with sound responsive scaffolding and MSO/dark-mode handling in the visible source. The QA run found one probe failure, on a relative redirect target, and header checks that couldn't be completed because of the relay. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** The probe of `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5MQF7VC9QFQA2K8M9AC8XKA_0` (link text "Everlane") failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`. The tracker appears to return a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - Relative `Location` values are permitted by RFC 7231, and browsers resolve them against the tracker host. So this is probably a probe-tool limitation, not a broken link for recipients.
  - If the tracker host doesn't serve `/pages/womens`, though, the link would resolve to a 404 on `trk.send.everlane.com`. Re-probe with relative-redirect resolution to settle this.
- **Open-tracking pixel without alt.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5MQF7VC9QFQA2K8M9AC8XKA` was flagged for missing alt. It's a tracking pixel, so impact is negligible, but `alt=""` is cleaner.
- **Probe coverage.** 12 links were probed and 21 skipped. The 43% pass rate rests on a small sample and shouldn't be read as 43% of links being broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
