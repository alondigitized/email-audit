# “Best T-Shirt I’ve Ever Bought”
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's strongest send of the week, mainly because it drops the 30% sweater promo. After "Few Hours Left: 30% Off Sweaters & More" (09-29) and "Ends Tonight" (09-28), a product-and-proof email is a real change of pace. The subject is a customer quote, the body is two real reviews, and the offer is a plain $34 tee with a 3-for-$69 bundle.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan. It has a large hero, paired model shots, short review quotes, and a black SHOP NOW button after each block. The weak spots are that the offer is buried below the hero ("$34" and "Get three for $69" only appear in body copy) and that every CTA says the same generic thing. The hero says only "The 10/10 Tee".
- My judgement: it's a solid, honest send, better than the sweater blasts on creative variety. It would convert better if the bundle offer were the headline instead of a line of body text.

## What's working

- The subject and body use the same social-proof hook. The "Best T-Shirt I've Ever Bought" quote pays off with two named-reviewer quotes.
- The color range is visible: sage, burgundy, oat, brown, striped and forest green. It shows the range without needing a product grid.
- The offer is concrete and honest: $34 for the tee, three for $69. There is no fine print or exclusion language.
- The layout is consistent and mobile-friendly, with one hero, alternating two-up image rows and a centered CTA under each. I can't see any render bugs.
- The organic-cotton block adds brand values without pushing the tee out of focus.

## What's weak

- The bundle deal, the only real incentive, sits near the bottom as a small line of text, after two scrolls of reviews.
- The four SHOP NOW buttons are identical and generic, and the hero copy "The 10/10 Tee" carries no price or benefit.
- The two reviews are short and lightly attributed ("Allison M.", "Hayley G."). There are no star ratings or review counts.
- The email has no urgency, so it gives no reason to buy today. That is unusual after a week of "ends tonight" messaging.
- Almost all the models are shown in the same pose style and the imagery is all women's. Men's is only a footer link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the bundle into the hero: put "The 10/10 Tee: 3 for $69" on the hero image so the offer is the first thing the eye reads.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop the Box-Cut Tee" and "Get 3 for $69" instead of four identical SHOP NOW buttons.
- 3. Add a star rating and review count to the reviews, for example "4.8 ★ from 2,000+ reviews".
- 4. Add a soft deadline, such as a "bundle price through Sunday", if the offer is truly time-bound.
- 5. Cut one of the three review/image blocks so the email is shorter.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built, MJML-style HTML sent through a `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking wrapper. Two links (the logo and "Sign Up for Texts") have destinations that appear to lack a scheme and host, so they fail in the QA probe. The header-related warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative-path destinations (confirmed by QA, cause inferred).** Two tracked links fail with `unknown url type`:
  - Logo, link text "Everlane Logo": `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3PXMZXSB1Z2KZTSG17FCP8N_0` resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…`
  - "Icon | Sign Up for Texts": `…_18` resolves to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…`
  - Both resolve to a bare path, so the redirect target has no scheme or host. That most likely means the destination URLs were entered without the `https://everlane.com` prefix in the template.
  - A browser would resolve them against the tracker host, giving `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`. That is likely a 404 or wrong host. I did not test this, so it is unconfirmed.
- **Missing-alt image is a tracking pixel.** The image flagged for missing alt is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M3PXMZXSB1Z2KZTSG17FCP8N`. The `/o/` path is the open-tracking pixel, so this is not a meaningful defect. An empty `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Partial probe coverage.** 12 links were probed and 16 skipped as low-priority footer, utility and social links. The 38% pass rate reflects this partial sample plus the two failures, so it isn't a reliable measure of overall link health.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is blank. This is minor, but some clients and preview tools show it.
- The visible source has Outlook conditional comments (`mso`, VML namespaces, `mj-outlook-group-fix`), a viewport meta tag, and mobile media queries at 480px and 600px, so no rendering defects are evident.
- The source was truncated, so image alt text on content images and the body structure can't be assessed. The only alt finding is the tracking pixel above.
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is inside a `<style>` block. Many clients ignore or strip `@import`, so the fonts fall back to Arial/Helvetica as the font stacks declare. This is expected fallback behavior, not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source or subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed, and the headers should be checked on a direct-to-mailbox copy. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require them.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Nothing indicates a failure.
- **Footer not visible.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address may be in the truncated portion. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for one, so the footer link can't be verified from this data.
- The From address (`support@everlane.com`) is on the same organizational domain as the tracking host `send.everlane.com`, so no domain mismatch is evident.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible tracked URLs carry Klaviyo/ESP identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`), but no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` are visible. They may be in the truncated query strings, so this is unconfirmed.
- Only the two relative-path links have known landing paths (`/pages/womens` and `/pages/sms-signup`). Neither could be followed, so landing-page alignment is unverified for them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the destination URLs** for the logo and SMS sign-up links to absolute `https://` URLs. Then re-run the probe to confirm the redirects land on everlane.com.
2. **Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on a direct-delivery copy.** If they are absent there, enable them in the ESP.
3. **Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment** on a direct-delivery copy, since the relay hides them.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the tracking pixel and fill in the empty `<title>`. Both are low priority.
5. **Confirm UTM parameters** on the full, untruncated tracked URLs.
6. **Treat the 38% pass rate with caution.** Widen the probe beyond 12 links, or exclude known relay artifacts, before trusting it as a health metric.
