# Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand sale send, and it's the most direct one in the recent run. A close-cropped model in a forest-green crew sweater over a light blue collar fills the hero. "FALL ESSENTIALS EVENT" sits on the left and "UP TO 30% OFF / SWEATERS" on the right, with a black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath. The offer is clear within a second. This is the third send for the same event, after the 10/21 early-access email and the 10/22 public launch.
- The execution is thin. White text over a busy photo is hard to read, especially "FALL ESSENTIALS" where it crosses the light blue collar. One line of copy follows ("From cashmere to our famous Air Alpaca…"), then a text-only link stack. There is no product, no price, no review and no named hero item. The footer shows the deadline (10/26, 11:59 PM PST), but it is buried in grey fine print instead of being used as urgency in the hero.
- Verdict: a competent reminder with a credible deadline, but a subscriber who has already seen two nearly identical sends this week has no new reason to click. It's a 6.

## What's working

- The offer is readable at a glance: "UP TO 30% OFF" and "SWEATERS" are the largest type in the hero.
- The green sweater is seasonal and the crop is cohesive. It matches the late-October "colder days ahead" tone.
- The black SHOP NOW bar is full-width and unmissable, directly under the hero.
- The body copy names specific materials (cashmere, Air Alpaca), which gives a product cue.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping elements, and the footer has the offer terms.

## What's weak

- The white hero text has weak contrast over the light blue collar and pale background. "FALL ESSENTIALS EVENT" is the hardest to read.
- The deadline (10/26) appears only in the grey footer. The email creates no visible urgency.
- No product, price, or sweater is shown or linked individually. The email has one generic CTA for the whole category.
- The Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers links are plain text. They are unrelated to the sweater offer and split attention.
- It repeats the 10/21 and 10/22 Fall Essentials Event sends with no new angle, such as a new offer or a named product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends Sunday" or "Last 2 days" in the overlay. That is the only new information this send has over the previous two.
- 2. Add a row of 3–4 best-selling sweaters with name, price and a sale price or "30% off" tag, each linked to its own product page. Air Alpaca and cashmere would be natural picks.
- 3. Fix the contrast by placing the overlay text on a solid or gradient scrim, or moving it to a darker area of the photo.
- 4. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Sweaters" instead of "Shop Now". Put the deadline copy under it.
- 5. Add some proof, such as a review count or star rating on the Air Alpaca. Drop or demote the generic nav links.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last 2 days: up to 30% off sweaters`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Air Alpaca is on sale through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere and Air Alpaca, up to 30% off. Ends 10/26 at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Fall Essentials Event wraps Sunday. Shop the sweaters before they go.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Klaviyo/MJML-style build with a responsive viewport and dark-mode handling. The QA run scored 38% with 2 failures and 3 warnings. The failures are both redirect-resolution errors on tracked links. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men":** The tracked link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5Q9JZAQWT2GR69BBQAYDZ3W_4` resolved to a host-less path: `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCK…`. The probe reported `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" (logo/header link):** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5Q9JZAQWT2GR69BBQAYDZ3W_0` resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`, the same error.
- **What the failures mean:** The tracker is returning, or the probe is extracting, a relative redirect target. If the `Location` header really is relative, a browser would resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`. That would likely give a 404 instead of everlane.com.
- **Not confirmed:** The QA output doesn't separate a real relative `Location` header from a probe limitation. Check with `curl -sI <tracked URL>` and read the `Location` value.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 links were probed and 6 were skipped. The footer and social links weren't verified, so 50% of the material links have no status.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** The `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5Q9JZAQWT2GR69BBQAYDZ3W` image has no `alt`. The impact is low, but see section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`. This affects web-view and browser-preview contexts.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The root tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`. Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel:** If it's a 1×1 pixel, use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips it.
- **Web-font `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks do fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is cosmetic.
- **Fine in the visible source:** The viewport meta tag and the Outlook/MSO conditionals (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present. The `X-UA-Compatible` meta tag is also present. The source is truncated, so I can't check table `role` attributes, `alt` text on content images or the body markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and the subject line (`Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters`) show no unrendered merge tokens. The truncation means the body and footer weren't fully inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is **unverified**, not confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. This matters for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is **unknown**. The branded tracking subdomain (`trk.send.everlane.com`) is a good sign but doesn't prove alignment.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set, but no result is shown for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters:** The destinations carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_*` parameters appear in the captured URLs, which are truncated. Check whether UTM tagging is applied at the redirector or missing.
- **Landing alignment:** The two resolved destinations are generic: `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing` and `/pages/womens`. The subject promises "Best Sweaters", but neither probed link points to a sweater-specific collection. The sweater CTAs weren't among the probed results, so I can't say whether they align.
- **Redirect integrity:** Both resolved paths are relative (see section 2). That is a continuity risk until verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI` on the `_0` and `_4` tracked links. If `Location` is relative, fix the redirector to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URLs. If it's already absolute, treat this as a probe bug and make the QA tool resolve relative redirects against the request URL.
2. Probe the 6 skipped links, especially the unsubscribe link and the sweater CTAs.
3. Capture the raw message headers outside the relay (for example, a Gmail "Show original") to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Add a `lang` attribute to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Confirm UTM parameters are applied (on the redirect target or in the tracker config) and that sweater CTAs land on a sweater collection page.
6. Re-run QA with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, address and merge tokens can be checked.
