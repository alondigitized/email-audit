# 30% Off Cashmere Worth Keeping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth 30% Friends & Family send in six days, following "Shop 30% Off Our Best Outerwear" (10/10), "30% Off Utility Pants" (10/9) and "30% Off Better Sweaters" (10/8 and 10/9). The email is clean and on-brand. But the subject promises cashmere and the hero shows a black-and-white model in a puffer jacket over a half-zip. Cashmere appears only as a small "Including Cashmere" line on the right of the overlay.
- The six-sweater grid is tidy, with green V-necks, grey crews and striped polos. But it has no product names, no prices and no per-item links I can see. A subscriber who wants cashmere gets a generic "Shop Now" and has to hunt for it. The "Better Prices, Still Everlane" band is a good line, but it sits in a beige strip between the hero and the grid, with a second CTA that competes with the hero button.
- Judgement: competent execution on a tired, repetitive campaign. The subject and the hero disagree, which loses the click that a sharper cashmere-first creative would earn.

## What's working

- The hero overlay states the offer plainly: "Friends & Family / Ends 10.13" and "30% Off Sitewide / Including Cashmere".
- The full-width black "SHOP NOW" bars are unmistakable and repeat after the hero and after the grid.
- The grid stays on theme with sweaters. It pairs flat-lays with on-model shots, which helps judge fit and drape.
- "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites. BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" is a strong value line.
- The copy block ("recycled cashmere… Good Cashmere Standard®-certified") adds a sustainability angle that suits the brand.

## What's weak

- The hero is a puffer jacket in grayscale, which contradicts the cashmere subject.
- "Including Cashmere" is white text over a busy knit and is hard to read. "30% Off Sitewide" is also set small against the photo.
- The six grid products have no names, no prices and no visible per-item CTAs. Everything funnels to a generic "Shop Now".
- The footer fine print says "select styles only", which contradicts "30% Off Sitewide" in the hero. This undermines trust.
- The cadence is heavy. The same 30% offer has gone out near-daily, so this send adds no new reason to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a cashmere hero on the email: a model in a cashmere crew or V-neck, with "30% Off Cashmere" in large type on a clean area of the image. This lifts the email most because it makes the subject and hero agree.
- 2. Label each grid product with its name, a struck-through price and the sale price, and link each tile to its product page.
- 3. Replace the generic "Shop Now" CTAs with "Shop Cashmere Sweaters" and "Shop the Cashmere Edit".
- 4. Drop one of the two nearly identical "Shop Now" bars, or give them different jobs: one for cashmere, one for sitewide.
- 5. Reconcile the "sitewide" claim with the "select styles only" disclaimer.
- 6. Give the offer a reason beyond "30% off again". Examples are a countdown to 10.13 or a "last chance on cashmere" framing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cashmere, 30% off through 10.13`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last 3 days: cashmere at 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Recycled cashmere crews and V-necks, now 30% off until Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Good Cashmere Standard® knits, same quality, better prices. Ends 10.13.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "30% Off Cashmere Worth Keeping"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email with a click-tracking redirect domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The QA run found one link failure, on the logo link. It also found a few header and alt-text warnings, most of which look like relay or capture artifacts rather than confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body content could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect:** The link `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4K89S3Y40CKZZGAYDD05ZG4_0` (text "Everlane Logo") returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect target is a relative path, not an absolute URL.
  - A relative `Location` header is valid per RFC 7231. The probe's HTTP client may simply not resolve it, so this could be a QA tool limitation.
  - A browser would resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`, and `/pages/womens` would probably 404 there. That outcome is **unverified**. Manually click the logo and confirm where it lands.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 27 links were probed and 15 were skipped. The results say nothing about the skipped footer, utility and social links.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4K89S3Y40CKZZGAYDD05ZG4` is flagged for missing alt text. This is the open-tracking pixel, so it is low severity. `alt=""` plus explicit `width="1" height="1"` would be the clean form.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` has no `lang` attribute, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Small mobile headings:** `@media (max-width:480px){ h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` renders `h4` at 10px on mobile. That is below typical legibility guidance.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. Fallbacks (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`) are declared, so the impact is minor.
- **Outlook handling:** `mso` conditionals (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and a viewport meta are present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (e.g. `{{ }}`, `{%`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `_kx` parameter in the redirect target is Klaviyo's recipient-level exchange ID, as expected. The truncated HTML means body content could not be checked.

- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results`:** It was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. Verify with a direct seed test.
- **Footer content:** The visible-source excerpt cuts off before the footer. Physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link could not be confirmed, although the QA tool says an unsubscribe link was included in probing. No result for it is shown.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support-style address used as a marketing sender. That is a deliverability and reply-handling consideration, not a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
