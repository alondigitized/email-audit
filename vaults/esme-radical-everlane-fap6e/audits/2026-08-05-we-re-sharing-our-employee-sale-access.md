# We’re Sharing Our Employee Sale Access
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style "insider access" play from Everlane — no product shots, no hero image, just a stark all-copy layout built to feel like a personal heads-up rather than a blast. The "70% off and higher" employee sale is the most aggressive discount I've seen in this brand's cadence (well past the 25%-off sale emails from 8/1-8/3), and the "short list" framing is a real curiosity hook. But the execution undercuts the premise: the personalization token is broken — "Hi There, ," renders with a visible double comma and no name — which instantly kills the "you're on a short list" illusion this whole email is selling.
- Beyond that bug, there's simply nothing to look at. Every other recent Everlane send in this history (denim lineups, dress stories, cult-favorite testimonials) leads with product imagery; this one is 100% text with an underlined text link as the only CTA. For a 70%+ off employee sale — arguably the best offer this brand has run in weeks — showing zero product is a real miss on convertible curiosity.
- Net: strong offer and hook, sabotaged by a visible personalization bug and a visual layout that gives the reader no reason to believe there's anything worth "70% off" behind the link.

## What's working

- The 70%+ off employee-sale offer is the strongest, most specific discount in the recent brand history (vs. 25% off sale styles on 8/3).
- The "insider access" / "short list" copy device is a genuine curiosity driver — it reads differently than the usual sitewide-sale banner.
- The CTA text ("SHOP THE EMPLOYEE SALE") is clear, underlined, and impossible to miss even without a styled button.
- Footer fine print (offer end date, terms, final sale note) is transparent and not buried.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with no rendering breakage in the nav/social/footer blocks.

## What's weak

- Broken personalization token: "Hi There, ," shows a visible double comma with no first name inserted — a clear render bug that undermines the "you're one of a select few" premise.
- Zero product imagery anywhere in the email — for a 70%-off sale, there's no visual proof of what's actually on offer.
- No styled CTA button — the only click target is an underlined text link, which is far lower-contrast than this brand's usual button treatment.
- "Prices as marked" plus "all items are final sale" plus no visible discount code softens the urgency; the reader has to click blind to see any real numbers/products.
- No countdown or visible deadline in the body copy itself (the expiration only appears in tiny footer legal text — "Offer valid until 08/30/2026").

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the greeting token immediately — "Hi There, ," is a visible bug going out to every recipient with a blank/missing name field; either suppress the salutation entirely for unnamed contacts or default to "Hi there," with no trailing comma.
- 2. Add at least 2-3 product images (hero pieces at their sale price) — this offer is strong enough to carry a proper editorial layout instead of a stripped-down text email; showing nothing invites the reader to assume there's nothing worth 70% off.
- 3. Convert "SHOP THE EMPLOYEE SALE" from an underlined text link into a bold, filled CTA button to match this brand's usual visual weight and raise tap target size on mobile.
- 4. Surface the expiration date in the body copy, not just the footer legal — "Ends 8/30" next to the CTA adds real urgency the current layout lacks.
