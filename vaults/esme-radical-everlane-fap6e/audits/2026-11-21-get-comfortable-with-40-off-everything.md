# Get Comfortable With 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth or so near-identical Black Friday "40% off everything" send from Everlane in about four days. The creative is clean and the offer is unmistakable. The hero is a dark puffer on a burgundy set, with "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type. The "Yup, we got a little carried away on savings" line has some personality. It is competent and on-brand, but it adds nothing new.
- The body repeats itself. The second image is the same puffer jacket again, with a second identical "Shop Now" bar. The gift guide block at the bottom is a good idea, but it is buried and has no products. Compared with 2026-11-20's "Better Gifts Under $50", which led with a concrete price point, this send is generic. The subject, "Get Comfortable With 40% Off Everything", is mostly the offer plus a cozy pun.
- Judgement: it renders well and the offer is clear, but it is a cadence-fatigue send. A subscriber who has seen "40% off" every day will not feel any new reason to click.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large, high-contrast and readable over the burgundy and black imagery. "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The copy "Yup, we got a little carried away on savings" is on-brand and slightly playful.
- The black full-width "SHOP NOW" bars are unambiguous CTAs.
- The outerwear focus fits the season (cold-weather dressing in late November). The burgundy palette ties the hero, the second image and the gift block together.
- The expiry date (12/02/2026) appears in the fine print, and the footer nav (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is tidy.

## What's weak

- The second image repeats the same puffer jacket from the hero, so the email shows one product twice and gives no range.
- There is no price, sale price or named product anywhere. "Luxe fleece and responsibly crafted outerwear" is vague.
- The "BETTER GIFT GUIDE" block has no imagery. The "Shop Gifts for Her / Him" links are small, thin underlined text that is easy to miss.
- The offer has an asterisk and "select styles" in the fine print, which sits awkwardly against "EVERYTHING". No deadline appears in the body, only in grey footer text.
- Both "Shop Now" bars link to the same generic destination, with no distinct verb and noun such as "Shop Puffers".

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the duplicate puffer image with a 3-4 product grid (fleece, puffer, trousers) showing sale prices and a "Shop Outerwear" button. This would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Put the deadline in the body ("Ends 12/2"), not only in the footer, to add credible urgency.
- 3. Give the gift guide real product tiles and larger CTAs.
- 4. Vary the creative and angle from the recent run of "40% off" sends. Lead with a category, such as outerwear, or a price point, as the 11-20 gifts send did.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop Puffers" and "Shop Luxe Fleece".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) send routed through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain. Two of the 12 material links failed the automated probe with a "relative URL" error, and the authentication and unsubscribe-header checks were inconclusive. The HTML source was truncated, so findings below are limited to what the QA data and the visible source confirm.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures, both with `unknown url type: '/…'`.**
  - "Everlane" (logo link): `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7ZGKJ0N8BDE6NK82085GF0V_0` resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`
  - "Shop Men": `…/l/01M7ZGKJ0N8BDE6NK82085GF0V_9` resolved to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`
- **Likely cause:** the tracker returned a root-relative `Location` header with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve this against the tracker host, so the click would land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`. That path probably 404s or redirects incorrectly.
- **Possible probe artifact:** the probe client may simply not resolve relative redirects. The two failures should be verified manually before being treated as live breakage. They hit the highest-traffic links (logo and primary nav), so the impact is high if they are real.
- **Coverage gap:** 11 of 12 material links were skipped by the probe, so the remaining CTAs are unverified.
- **Missing alt text:** the `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7ZGKJ0N8BDE6NK82085GF0V` image has no `alt`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would be cleaner.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Missing language attribute:** the `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>`), which screen readers need for pronunciation.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients. Fallback stacks (`Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`) are declared, so the impact is graceful degradation only.
- **Positives:** the viewport meta tag, the Outlook `mso` conditionals and the 480px responsive breakpoints are present.
- **Not assessable:** the body was truncated, so I can't verify alt text on content images or the DOM order.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or in the QA findings. The truncated body can't be fully ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are flagged as not found. The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so it should be checked against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Sender address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as a marketing sender. This is only a note; no evidence of a problem.
- **Body footer:** the body-level unsubscribe link and physical address could not be confirmed in the truncated source. The probe's note says an unsubscribe link is always included, but its result wasn't reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Destinations:** the failing links point at `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`. Both are plausible landing pages for a storewide "40% Off Everything" offer.
- **Parameters:** the visible parameters are Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…`. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appears in the visible portions. They may be appended downstream of the tracker, but the data doesn't confirm it. If they are absent, analytics will rely on Klaviyo's `_kx` identity parameter.
- **Unverified:** final landing pages, whether the offer is carried through, and promo-code application weren't tested because of the probe failures and skips.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow `…/l/01M7ZGKJ0N8BDE6NK82085GF0V_0` and `…_9` with `curl -IL`. If the relative `Location` is real, fix the Klaviyo/tracker redirect to return absolute URLs, or fix the link destinations.
2. Fix the probe's redirect handling to resolve relative `Location` headers with `urljoin`. This will separate real breakage from tool artifacts.
3. Raise the probe cap, or at least probe all CTA-styled links, so more than 1 of 12 links is verified.
4. Pull the raw `.eml` headers to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC, rather than relying on the relay.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`, and give the tracking pixel `alt=""`.
6. Confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirect on the landing pages. If they don't, add them at the link level.
