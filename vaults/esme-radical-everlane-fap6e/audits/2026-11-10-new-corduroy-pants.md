# New Corduroy Pants
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a pretty, seasonal product launch with no offer in it. The hero is a model in a brown shirt and trousers with a cobalt sweater over the shoulders. The overlaid "It's Cord Season" is small, thin and low-contrast against the busy image. Four corduroy pants in forest green, bright green and two browns sit in a clean 2x2 grid. The palette is cohesive and the editorial feel is on brand.
- The email gives a shopper no reason to click now. There is no price, no discount, no urgency and no product name. Three of the four CTAs are generic ("SHOP NOW", "SHOP CORDS", "READ MORE"), and the organic-cotton block spends prime space on sustainability copy. Compare this with the 2026-11-10 "Don't Miss 30% Off Savings" send, which had a hard deadline, and the 2026-11-09 "Two Fits. One Better Price." send, which had a concrete $88. This one is a step back in commercial specificity.
- Verdict: a competent brand-building send that will underperform on clicks. It would be fine as a mid-cycle editorial beat but is weak in a Black Friday run-up where other Everlane sends carry 30% off.

## What's working

- The 2x2 grid shows four distinct colors and silhouettes. Shoppers can compare them at a glance.
- The hero is a strong, seasonal image and the sweater/cord pairing is stylish.
- The corduroy texture close-up adds tactility and sells the "velvety-soft" claim visually.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and consistent black button styling.
- The organic cotton callout supports Everlane's values-driven positioning.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer, or deadline anywhere, so nothing reduces friction or creates urgency.
- The "It's Cord Season" headline is small and thin, and it blends into the photo.
- The body copy has a visible typo: "littletexture" is missing a space.
- The grid products carry no names, prices, or links to individual items. Every product leads to a generic "SHOP CORDS".
- The organic cotton block is long and sits low in the email. It pulls attention away from purchase.
- The CTAs "SHOP NOW" and "READ MORE" are generic, and "READ MORE" invites a non-commercial click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or offer on the hero, for example "Cords from $98" or the current 30% Black Friday preview discount. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Make the headline larger and bolder, or move it onto a solid band below the image.
- 3. Add a name and price under each grid item and link each to its product page.
- 4. Fix the "littletexture" typo.
- 5. Shorten the organic cotton block to one line and move it below the footer nav, or cut it.
- 6. Make CTA copy specific, such as "Shop Corduroy Pants".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Klaviyo-built template with a reasonable responsive and Outlook scaffold. QA flagged 3 failed link probes, all caused by a relative `Location` value on the `trk.send.everlane.com` redirects. The header warnings are mostly relay artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 of 12 probed links failed** (33% pass rate). Each tracked URL on `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M73PT25A1C1B77B39YDBPFZW_*` resolved to a relative path with no host:
  - `_0` ("Everlane" logo) → `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…`
  - `_11` ("Shop Men") → `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`
  - `_15` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") → `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…`
- **Cause is unconfirmed.** It's either a real bug (the redirector emits a relative `Location`, which a client would resolve against `trk.send.everlane.com` and likely 404) or a probe limitation (the QA client doesn't resolve relative redirects, which Python `urllib` errors on as "unknown url type"). Verify with `curl -sI` on `…_0`, `…_11` and `…_15` and check whether the `Location` header carries an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URL.
- **Coverage is partial.** 13 links were skipped (footer, social), so their health is unknown.
- **Missing alt text.** The open-tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M73PT25A1C1B77B39YDBPFZW` has no `alt`. This is low impact, since it's a 1x1 tracker. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title> </title>`.** It contains only whitespace, so there's no document title for clients and previews that use it. Minor.
- **Mobile `h4` size.** `h4` is forced to `10px !important` at max-width 480px. This is only a problem if `h4` carries readable copy, which the truncated source doesn't show.
- No other issues found. The viewport meta, the MSO conditionals (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and the dark-mode hooks (`[data-ogsc]`) are present and well-formed. The rest of the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No confirmed violations.** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` is absent. The QA tool itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers, so these are unverified rather than defects.
- **Sender.** `From: support@everlane.com` is a support-style address used for marketing. The tracking domain `send.everlane.com` is a subdomain of the same organizational domain, so alignment is plausible but unverified.
- **Not assessable.** The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address sit in the truncated footer. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result is reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters.** The visible redirect targets carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`) and no `utm_*` parameters. Because the strings are truncated in the QA output, I can't confirm whether UTMs exist further along or are appended server-side.
- **Landing pages not verified.** All three tested destinations failed to resolve. The logo goes to `/pages/womens` and "Shop Men" goes to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`, but no landing-page content was reached.
- **Subject vs. link targets.** The subject is "New Corduroy Pants". No corduroy-specific destination was among the probed results, so alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI` against the three failing tracked URLs. If `Location` is relative, fix the redirector to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/...` URLs. If absolute, treat it as a QA probe false positive.
2. Re-probe the 13 skipped links, especially the unsubscribe link, and confirm it returns 200.
3. Get raw headers from a direct-to-inbox test (not via the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
4. Check whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` are present on the final landing URLs.
5. Set a real `<title>` and give the tracking pixel `alt=""`.
