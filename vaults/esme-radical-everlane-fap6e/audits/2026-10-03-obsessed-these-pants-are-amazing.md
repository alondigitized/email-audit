# “Obsessed. These pants are amazing!”
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean single-product send for the Everywhere Pant. The hero is a model in black barrel-leg pants with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar. Below it sits a six-tile colorway grid (navy, camel, black), a one-line customer quote, and a sustainability block. It is easy to scan and well built, but it has no price, no deadline and no reason to click today.
- The subject is the strongest thing about it. A real-customer quote ("Obsessed. These pants are amazing!") reads like a friend's recommendation and stands out from the usual promo language. But the quote in the subject never appears in the body. The body uses a different one, "The fit, color, and tailoring are just perfect!" from Catherine C. The follow-through is weaker than the hook.
- This is Everlane's second send today, after "How We're Wearing Collegium," and it continues the pattern in the brand history. Every recent send ("Better Silk," "The Jackets Lineup," the Collegium trio) has been editorial with no offer. The "now priced even better" line is the first hint of price in this run, but it gives no number. A subscriber would see this as one more good-looking, low-urgency Everlane email.

## What's working

- The subject leads with a customer voice, which feels more credible and less salesy than a promo line.
- The hero is a single product, the Everywhere Pant, with one clear SHOP NOW bar that is visible near the top of a mobile screen.
- The colorway grid shows navy, camel and black in both flat and on-model shots, so fit and color choice are visible without a click.
- The body copy names the shape and fabric: "barrel leg," "organic cotton with a touch of linen." That is useful product detail.
- The review quote adds social proof, and it hints at repeat buying ("contemplating what color to buy next").

## What's weak

- "Priced even better" gives no price, discount or comparison, so the email has no concrete offer.
- The subject's quote doesn't appear in the body. The two quotes don't match.
- The hero's "The Everywhere Pant" text is small and plain, with no price or reason to act now.
- The colorway grid has no color names or prices, and the tiles don't read as individually shoppable.
- The sustainability block with READ MORE sits between the product and the footer. It pulls attention away from the pants and adds a second CTA that leads away from purchase.
- The footer disclaimer and links are tiny and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the new price in the hero and in the first line of body copy, for example "Now $98 (was $118)." That one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Show the same quote that is in the subject. Put it right under the hero, with a star rating and the review count.
- 3. Add color names, prices, and a "Back in stock: 4 colors" or "Selling fast in navy" line under the grid. Link each tile to its own color.
- 4. Move the sustainability block below the footer nav or cut it. If it stays, make it a one-line text link.
- 5. Make the hero headline bigger and add a short line such as "The barrel leg, now in cotton-linen."

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Obsessed. These pants are amazing!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML template sent through a `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain. The QA pass rate was 38%. Two probed links (the logo and "Sign Up for Texts") failed on a relative redirect target. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what was captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4179HW2HE99F744WATSRFA3_0` ("Everlane Logo") errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] SMS signup link:** `…/l/01M4179HW2HE99F744WATSRFA3_16` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") errored the same way with `'/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** In both cases the tracker's redirect target is a path with no scheme or host. Either the tracker's `Location` header is relative, or the prober doesn't resolve relative redirects. QA data can't distinguish the two.
  - If the header is relative, browsers would resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`, which would 404 or break.
  - If it's a probe artifact, the links are fine.
  - Treat these as unconfirmed but likely real until someone checks the raw `Location` header.
- **Coverage:** Only 12 of 26 links were probed and 14 were skipped as footer or social. The logo and SMS links are the only confirmed failures, but the other 14 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4179HW2HE99F744WATSRFA3` is the open-tracking pixel. It is expected and has no functional issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang`:** The source shows `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` with no `lang` attribute, which screen readers use for pronunciation.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The `<title>` contains only whitespace (`<title> </title>`), so there is no accessible document title.
- **Tracking pixel alt:** The pixel `<img>` has no `alt`. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is the correct markup for a decorative pixel.
- **Small mobile text:** The `@media (max-width: 480px)` rule sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }`, which is below the usual ~14px minimum for legibility. It only affects content that uses `<h4>`.
- **Web fonts:** The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/…/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. This is acceptable because the font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica.
- **Not assessed:** Body-level alt text, table roles and Outlook conditional markup are cut off by truncation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the captured source or the subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found:** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Unverified items:** The footer, physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source and can't be confirmed.
- **Sender:** The envelope sender is `support@everlane.com` while links use `send.everlane.com`, which is a subdomain of the same org domain, so it should align for DMARC. This is unverified without the headers.
- **Conclusion:** These are verification gaps, not confirmed violations. The relay is the probable cause.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Tracking parameters:** The visible redirect targets carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`) but no `utm_*` parameters in the captured portion. The URLs are truncated, so UTM presence can't be confirmed.
- **Landing page alignment:** The logo link goes to `/pages/womens` and the SMS link to `/pages/sms-signup`. The product CTAs for the "pants" subject were not captured.
- **Broken redirects:** Both landing paths are relative, which is the same suspected redirect defect as in section 2. If it's real, those clicks would not reach the site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sI https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4179HW2HE99F744WATSRFA3_0` and check the `Location` header. If it's relative, fix the tracking-domain redirect to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/…` URLs. If it's absolute, patch the QA prober to resolve relative redirects against the request URL.
2. Verify the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, give `<title>` real text, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
4. Raise the mobile `h4` size from 10px to at least 12–14px.
5. Confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` are present on the final landing URLs, and probe more of the 14 skipped links.
