# October Arrivals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-duplicate of the "New for October" send from 10-04. It has the same 3x3 flat-lay grid in oatmeal, burgundy, black and navy, the same black "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" bar, and the same cream "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block. A subscriber who saw that email yesterday gets nothing new here. It follows the Everywhere Pant sends and the three Collegium sends from 10-02 and 10-03, so it reads as filler in a crowded week.
- The execution is clean. The grid is on-season, there are no render bugs, and one CTA dominates. But nothing in it gives a reason to click now: no hero, no product names or prices, no reviews, no deadline. "Better prices" is a claim with no number behind it, so it can't carry the email.
- Judgment: competent but forgettable, and a re-send weakens it further. It sits at the 6 mark for execution, but the repeat and the cadence drag its open and click odds down.

## What's working

- The 3x3 grid shows the season at a glance: corduroy shirts, henleys, barrel pants and a midi skirt in a coherent fall palette.
- The single black "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" bar is unambiguous and sits right below the grid.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and no overlap, broken images or gaps.
- The "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites." line is a reasonable reassurance on value, in a calm brand voice.
- The footer nav (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is tidy and useful.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 10-04 "New for October" creative almost exactly, with only the subject changed.
- There is no hero, so no single product or story pulls the eye. Nine equal tiles compete.
- "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" has no number, product or price to anchor it. A second "SHOP NOW" link repeats the CTA without adding specificity.
- None of the tiles show a product name, price or colorway, so there is nothing to click for.
- There is no urgency, loyalty hook or social proof anywhere in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't re-send the same grid. Swap in a differentiated angle, such as "The 5 pieces we'd buy first this October" with one hero (the corduroy shirt or the barrel pant) and 3-4 supporting items.
- 2. Add product names and prices under the tiles, or at least on the top row, so the email gives a reason to click.
- 3. Replace "Better prices" with a concrete fact such as "New fall prices from $X". Link each tile to its PDP, not the generic arrivals page.
- 4. Cut the repeated CTA, or make the second one a different action, such as "Shop Best-Sellers".
- 5. Space the sends out. This is the 4th or 5th email in about 3 days.
