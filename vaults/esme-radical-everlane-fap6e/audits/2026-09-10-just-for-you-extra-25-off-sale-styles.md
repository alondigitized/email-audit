# Just For You: Extra 25% Off Sale Styles
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a broken send — "Hi There, ," with a visible double-comma personalization failure right at the top, on an email that's supposedly a "VIP" and "Just For You" targeted offer. There's no hero image at all, just plain text on white, which after five days of peony/tulip macro shots and gradient skies is at least a format break, but it reads more like a fallback/plain-text template than an intentional design choice.
- The offer itself — extra 25% off sale styles already up to 70% off, code EXTRA25 — is the same mechanic Everlane has run repeatedly this week (2026-09-08's "A Special One Day Offer: Extra 25% Off Sale" and "One Day To Save An Extra 25% Off Sale" both used this exact discount level). Wrapping it in "VIP" and "Just For You" language is a stretch given the merge-field failure undercuts the personalization claim in the first line a reader sees.
- Judgement: this is the weakest execution in the recent run, not because the offer is bad but because the render bug destroys the credibility of the "just for you" premise, and there's no visual anchor to pull the eye anywhere.

## What's working

- Clean, readable plain-text layout with no clutter or competing CTAs above the fold.
- The offer and code (EXTRA25) are stated clearly and are easy to find on one read-through.
- Footer/legal block includes a real expiration date and timestamp (09/14/2026, 11:59 PM PST), which is more concrete urgency detail than several prior sends.
- Text links (effortless dresses, lightweight tops, easy pants) at least attempt category-specific routing instead of one generic button.

## What's weak

- "Hi There, ," — the personalization token is broken and visibly rendered as a blank field with a stray comma, right in the first line.
- No hero image, no product photography, no visual hierarchy at all — nothing for the eye to land on.
- No dedicated CTA button for the sale offer itself; the only buttons in the email (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) don't even link to the sale/discount styles.
- "VIP customers" framing feels hollow paired with a broken merge field and an offer identical to non-VIP sends this week.
- Zero product proof — no images, no reviews, no specific items shown despite claiming "something you've had your eye on."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken name token before this ships again — "Hi There, ," reaching a subscriber's inbox is a QA failure, not a style choice, and it directly damages every "just for you" / "VIP" claim in the copy.
- 2. Add a "Shop Sale" or "Shop Extra 25% Off" CTA button tied to the actual promo — right now the four nav buttons ignore the offer being pitched entirely.
- 3. Bring back at least one product image or a simple 2-3 item grid so there's something to click beyond text links.
- 4. Retire "VIP customers" language until this is actually a segmented/loyalty-only send — it's the same 25%-off-sale-styles offer given to everyone this week, so the exclusivity claim reads as false framing.
