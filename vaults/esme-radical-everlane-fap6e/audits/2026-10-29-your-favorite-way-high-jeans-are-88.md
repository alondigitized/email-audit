# Your Favorite Way-High® Jeans Are $88
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a decent influencer-style jeans send with a weak offer presentation. The subject promises "$88", but the body never gives that price a headline. The first thing you see is "Meet the Way-High® Family" over a street-style photo of a woman in a red cardigan and wide-leg jeans. The $88 appears only once, in small body copy under the first button: "they're both now $88." The email also has no deadline in the main body. The 11/01 end date is in the fine print.
- The creative is clean and on-brand. Three black SHOP NOW buttons are easy to find, and the lifestyle photos and the two jean tiles look good. But the layout is a stack of photos, each with a generic button, and none links to a specific style. A promo banner for "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" sits at the bottom. That is the same offer Everlane sent earlier today ("Extra 25% Off Markdowns", 10/29), so it competes with the $88 message and repeats a send the subscriber has already seen.
- Judgement: good taste, soft selling. It would work better with the price promoted into the hero, a real deadline, and buttons that name the jean.

## What's working

- The subject names a concrete product and price, "Way-High® Jeans Are $88", so the inbox promise is clear.
- The photos are editorial and the creator tagging (@zoelaz, @samanthamhorton) adds light social proof. The seasonal styling fits late October.
- The black full-width and block SHOP NOW buttons are unmistakable, and there are no broken images or layout bugs.
- The two jean tiles at the bottom of the product section let you compare washes and cuts quickly.

## What's weak

- The $88 price is buried in one line of small body copy, and the hero headline says nothing about price.
- All three buttons say "SHOP NOW" and nothing shows which jean each one links to. The two jean tiles have no names, no prices and no per-tile links I can see.
- The deadline is not visible in the body. "Limited time only" sits on the markdowns banner, and the real end date (11/01) is in the fine print.
- The "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" banner is the same offer as an earlier send today, and it competes with the jeans offer.
- The scroll is long for a single-product story, and the space between the tiles and the third button is empty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "WAY-HIGH® JEANS, NOW $88" on the hero image or directly under it, with the end date: "Through Sunday, 11/1."
- 2. Make the buttons specific, for example "Shop Way-High Curve" and "Shop Way-High Straight", and link each to its own product page.
- 3. Add style names and prices under the two jean tiles, and a review count or star rating if available.
- 4. Cut the markdowns banner here, or move it to a smaller line. Don't repeat the offer from the earlier send today.
- 5. Drop the third button and tighten the vertical spacing.
