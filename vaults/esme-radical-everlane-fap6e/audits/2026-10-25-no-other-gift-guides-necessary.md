# No Other Gift Guides Necessary
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, on-brand holiday gift guide with no offer. A wrapped-gift hero in deep burgundy carries "Better Gift Guide, Holiday 2026," and three lifestyle blocks follow: her, him, and stocking stuffers. Each block has a short copy line and a SHOP NOW button. The look is calm and premium, and it fits Everlane. The sections are easy to tell apart.
- The email has no price hook. The only value line is "Now at better prices," and it gives no number. That's weak after the 2026-10-24 sends, which were "Up to 30% Off Our Best Sweaters" and "30% Off: All New Alpaca." A subscriber who just saw 30% off will wonder why this one has none. The subject, "No Other Gift Guides Necessary," is a bit smug and says nothing concrete.
- The email is pleasant but low-pressure. It reads as brand-building and will probably get browsed rather than clicked. Final judgement: a competent seasonal send that needs a real offer and named products.

## What's working

- The burgundy hero with ribboned boxes sets a clear holiday mood.
- The layout is simple. Her, Him and Stocking Stuffers are each one block with one button.
- The product photography is tactile: cable-knit cashmere, stacked sweaters, and a red trapper hat with a scarf.
- Women and Men buttons sit right under the hero, so navigation is fast.
- The footer has a full-width "Shop the Gift Guide" bar and a short link list that repeat the main action.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer. "Now at better prices" is vague and gives no percent, dollar amount, or deadline.
- No product, price, or review is shown, so there's nothing specific to click on.
- The white copy sits over busy, light photos in the her and him blocks, and the text is hard to read.
- The three SHOP NOW buttons are identical, and the copy is vague ("Better Gifts for Her").
- There is no shipping or delivery deadline, which is the obvious urgency for a gift guide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific offer in the hero, such as "Gifts up to 30% off through Sunday." This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Name products with prices, for example "The Cashmere Crew, $98." Link each block to that product.
- 3. Add a gifting deadline, such as "Order by Dec 15 for holiday delivery."
- 4. Put the hero text on a darker overlay and shorten the body copy so it stays legible.
- 5. Use specific buttons, such as "Shop Gifts for Her," "Shop Gifts for Him," and "Shop Stocking Stuffers."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template (sender `support@everlane.com`) with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA probe flagged two link errors that look like redirect-handling artifacts rather than confirmed breakage. The source is truncated and the relay stripped the headers, so the compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe errors on two tracked links (unconfirmed breakage).**
  - Both failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/...'`.
  - "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5SVWCB39HSV52TM4C5S6SBS_16` resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_fl…`.
  - "Everlane Logo" → `.../01M5SVWCB39HSV52TM4C5S6SBS_0` resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…`.
  - The redirect target is a host-less relative path. Relative `Location` headers are legal under RFC 7231 and browsers resolve them. The probe's HTTP client most likely failed to resolve them.
  - Treat this as a QA-tool limitation until a browser-level check shows otherwise. The same tracker may be emitting relative redirects, which some strict clients and security scanners won't follow. Verify with `curl -I` that the `Location` header is relative and that the final URL returns 200.
- **Open-pixel alt text (not a real defect).** The "missing alt" image, `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5SVWCB39HSV52TM4C5S6SBS`, is the `/o/` open-tracking pixel. Adding `alt=""` would silence the check but has no user impact.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 26 links were probed. The 14 skipped were footer, utility and social links. That coverage gap, plus the skipped links, is the main reason the pass rate is 38%.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** The root is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, which screen readers need to choose pronunciation.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only. This is minor in email but affects some clients' accessible names.
- **Link styling.** The injected CSS sets `a:link, a:visited, a:hover, a:active { color:#000; text-decoration:none }`. Inline text links are therefore not distinguishable from body text by anything other than position. This is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk for any inline links.
- **Mobile and Outlook handling.** The source includes the viewport meta, MSO conditionals, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, and `max-width:100%` on images. No issues found there.
- **Not verifiable.** Image alt text for content images and table `role="presentation"` can't be checked because the HTML is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|…|*`. The truncated body prevents a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the original receiving mailbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `@everlane.com`, but the tracking domain is `send.everlane.com`. Confirm that the Return-Path and DKIM `d=` domains align with the From domain.
- **Footer and unsubscribe link.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure is listed. The physical postal address and the unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination URLs carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`) rather than visible `utm_*` parameters. The probe output is truncated, so UTM presence can't be confirmed. Verify that `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` survive the redirect, or that Klaviyo auto-tagging is enabled.
- The logo links to `/pages/womens` rather than the homepage. That is a destination choice, not a defect.
- No landing-page alignment data was captured, since the probes failed to resolve.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the two flagged links with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the tracker host. Also run `curl -IL` on both to confirm 200 on the final URL.
2. Capture raw headers from the receiving mailbox and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and give the `<title>` real content.
4. Underline inline text links, or otherwise distinguish them from body text, for accessibility.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel to clear the false-positive alt warning.
6. Confirm `utm_*` parameters on the final landing URLs. Re-run the audit with the full HTML so tokens, alt text and footer compliance can be checked.
