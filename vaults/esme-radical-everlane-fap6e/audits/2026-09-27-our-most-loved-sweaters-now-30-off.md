# Our Most-Loved Sweaters, Now 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane running the exact same "30% Off" template it's used all week — but this send narrows the offer to sweaters specifically and pairs it with a genuinely strong hero: a real editorial photo, legible "LIMITED-TIME EVENT / 30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE" copy stamped directly on the image, and an immediate black "SHOP NOW" bar. It's a near-twin of the 09-26 "Fan-Favorite Sweaters at 30% Off" send (8.00/10) — same category focus, same escalating urgency language ("Limited-Time Event" vs. that one's more casual framing).
- The 3x3 grid of sweater swatches is a smart addition this time — it lets a browser see color/style variety before clicking, which the plainer 09-26 and 09-25 sends didn't offer as cleanly. The second full-bleed lifestyle shot (cropped cardigan-tie-at-waist) is moodier and less immediately commercial, but reinforces the aesthetic.
- This is a well-executed, on-brand promotional email that will read as the fifth near-identical "30%" beat from Everlane in three days — that's the real weakness here, not the execution.

## What's working

- Hero photo is sharp editorial quality with the model actually wearing the featured sweater — no stock-photo feel.
- Offer is stamped legibly on the hero image itself ("30% OFF SWEATERS & MORE") so it's visible without reading body copy.
- Immediate black "SHOP NOW" CTA bar directly under the hero — zero scroll needed on most screens.
- 3x3 product grid shows real color/style range (cardigan, crewneck, turtleneck, mock-neck) which supports an informed click.
- Clean visual hierarchy: hero → CTA → supporting copy → grid → mood shot → footer nav, no clutter.

## What's weak

- No specific dollar or member-tier pricing shown — just a percentage, so the actual savings on any given sweater is invisible until click-through.
- Grid images have no product names, prices, or individual "shop" links visible — it's a static mosaic, not clickable merchandising.
- Second full-bleed lifestyle image (cropped hip/waist shot) is moody but doesn't clearly showcase a shoppable product — feels like filler before the footer.
- This is the third "30% Off" sweater/loungewear framing from Everlane in three days (09-25, 09-26 x2, now 09-27) — fatigue risk is real for an engaged subscriber.
- Subject line doesn't include the urgency ("Limited-Time Event") that's the actual hero headline — a missed connective thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline in the subject or preheader ("Ends tonight") to match the hero's "Limited-Time Event" framing — right now the urgency lives only in the image, not in the inbox.
- 2. Make the 3x3 grid clickable-feeling by adding price or "from $X" captions under each swatch — turns a mood board into a merchandising tool.
- 3. Swap the second full-bleed lifestyle shot for a second CTA block or a "shop the grid" repeat button — currently that whole section is a dead end with no path to click.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 09-26 "Fan-Favorite Sweaters" send more — a subscriber who opened both will notice the reused hero styling and grey/olive palette.
