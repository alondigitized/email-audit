# 30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's cleanest and most direct offer in days. "Friends & Family / 30% Off Sitewide, Including New Arrivals" is overlaid on a two-model outerwear-and-denim hero, with a black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath it. It follows the 10-06 "30% Off: Early Access" send (7/10). The offer is now open to everyone instead of gated, and an extra 20% off markdowns is stacked at the bottom. That makes it the stronger version.
- The execution is competent but generic. The 2x2 category grid (sweaters, denim, tops, outerwear) gives shoppers four easy entry points. The "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block repeats the 10-04 and 10-05 sends almost word for word. The hero text is white on a light concrete wall, so the offer, the most important element, has weak contrast and is easy to miss.
- Overall it's a solid, honest sale email. It's undermined by the low-contrast hero headline, by recycled mid-email modules, and by a fine-print deadline (10/13) that never appears in the body.

## What's working

- A single, specific offer: 30% off sitewide, including new arrivals. The "including New Arrivals" qualifier is a real draw for full-price shoppers.
- The black SHOP NOW bar is full width and unmistakable.
- The 2x2 category grid (sweaters, denim, tops, outerwear) fits the fall season and gives distinct click paths.
- The extra 20% off markdowns block, with separate Women's and Men's sale links, rewards sale shoppers and splits traffic cleanly.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is white on a pale concrete wall. "30% Off Sitewide" and especially "Including New Arrivals" are low contrast and small, and the text sits mid-image beside the models.
- The deadline appears only in the grey footer fine print ("valid until 10/13/2026"). There is no urgency in the body.
- The "SHOP SWEATERS / DENIM / TOPS / OUTERWEAR" labels are white text over light grey tiles and are hard to read.
- Many near-duplicate SHOP NOW and "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" elements echo earlier sends, so it feels templated.
- The hero's two models are tied to no specific product. There is no price, review or fit detail anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero contrast. Use a dark text scrim or move the offer to a solid band above the image, in larger type: "30% OFF EVERYTHING."
- 2. Put the deadline in the body, for example "Ends Monday 10/13", and add it to the subject or preheader.
- 3. Darken the grid labels or place them in a solid caption bar under each tile.
- 4. Replace the recycled "Better Prices" block with a bestseller row showing price and star ratings.
- 5. Add the "Early Access" members' callback ("Thanks for shopping early"), since the cadence is heavy.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template with a tracking redirect that the QA probe could not resolve (1 FAIL), an empty `<title>`, and no `lang` attribute. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML is truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **FAIL: unresolvable redirect target.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4BGXDRW1BCCYMTYYCXN7VMN_0` (link text "Everlane") returned a redirect to a relative path: `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The probe failed with `unknown url type`.
  - A relative `Location` header is legal under RFC 7231, so this may be a probe limitation rather than a real break.
  - If the header really is relative, it would resolve against `trk.send.everlane.com`, not `everlane.com`. That would be a broken click on the likely logo or header link.
  - Verify with `curl -sI` on that URL and check whether `Location` is absolute.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 27 links were probed, so the other 15 are unverified. The 43% pass rate rests on that partial sample.
- **Tracking pixel without alt.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4BGXDRW1BCCYMTYYCXN7VMN` is the open-tracking image. It should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions so screen readers don't announce the URL. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>`), so screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation.
