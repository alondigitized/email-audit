# The Prices Just Got Better For Good
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a permanent price-cut announcement, not a sale — and it's the most honest, least fatiguing email Everlane has sent all week. After a run of mint-teal "30% OFF" price blocks recycled across five subject lines (08/20–08/24) and the awkward "final hours" urgency slapped on the same creative (08/25), this is a clean reset: a mirrored twin-model hero, "Better Prices / Still Everlane," and body copy that plainly states "Only the prices have changed — for the better." No countdown clock, no fake urgency, no percentage math to do in your head.
- The problem is there's no number anywhere. "Better Prices" with zero specifics — no "up to X% off," no examples, no before/after price — is a strong visual concept undercut by a total absence of the one thing a value-conscious shopper actually needs to decide whether to click: is this 5% or 25%? The nine-product grid is generic basics (tees, shirts, trousers) with no prices shown, so there's nothing to anchor the claim to.
- Net: distinctive art direction, on-brand voice, no render bugs — but it asks for trust without giving proof, which caps how hard this pulls a click compared to 08/25's Field Jacket send (8/10) that led with an actual product story.

## What's working

- Clean mirrored-twin-model hero image renders correctly with no overlap or artifacting.
- Departs from the week's overused mint-teal price-block creative — genuinely fresh art direction.
- Copy is honest and low-pressure: no fake countdown, no "final hours" bait like 08/25's near-identical creative.
- Clear black "SHOP NOW" CTA button appears twice (hero and after grid), high contrast, easy to spot.
- Simple, uncluttered footer navigation (Shop Women/Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) with no broken elements.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete numbers — no "%," no "$," no before/after price anywhere in the render, so "Better Prices" is unverifiable at a glance.
- Product grid has no prices, names, or labels under any of the nine items — pure guesswork for the shopper.
- Hero headline is abstract ("Better Prices. Still Everlane.") rather than benefit-specific — weaker than 08/25's Field Jacket intro which named an actual product.
- Grid mixes men's and women's basics with no section labels, so it's unclear who anything is even for without clicking.
- No loyalty/member callout despite this being framed as a permanent pricing shift — a natural spot to reward repeat customers is skipped.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real number to the hero.** "Better Prices" next to a specific stat ("Up to 20% lower, permanently") would convert this from a vibe into an offer — this is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Label the product grid** with names and new prices (ideally struck-through old price next to new) so the "better prices" claim has visible proof directly under the image.
- 3. **Split the grid by gender or link each product to its own PDP** rather than an undifferentiated 3x3 wall — reduces friction on click.
- 4. **Pull one hero product out with its price** (e.g., the wide-leg denim shown) as a named anchor example, the way 08/25's Field Jacket send did.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our Prices Just Dropped — For Good`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Same Everlane. Lower Prices, Permanently.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No sale. No countdown. Just lower prices on the basics you already love.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Denim, tees, and shirts — now priced better, permanently.`

# Technical Email Audit — Everlane "The Prices Just Got Better For Good"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email built on Klaviyo (MJML/kl- templating) with two broken redirect links and several missing compliance/authentication headers; automated QA reports a 38% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken redirect — "Sign Up for Texts" icon**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M14GREYWW0TVY21ADK3RKTHV_18` resolves to an unparseable destination `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=...&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl...` — QA flags "unknown url type," indicating the underlying path is relative (missing scheme/host) rather than a full URL.
- **Broken redirect — Everlane logo/header link**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M14GREYWW0TVY21ADK3RKTHV_0` resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i...` with the same malformed/relative URL pattern.
- Both failures share the same root cause: the Klaviyo tracked-link redirect is pointing to a path-only URL instead of a fully-qualified `https://www.everlane.com/...` URL.
- 12 of 28 material links were probed (16 footer/utility/social links deprioritized per probing methodology); no other link failures reported among the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text**: tracking/open pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M14GREYWW0TVY21ADK3RKTHV` has no `alt` attribute. Low practical impact (it's a 1x1 open-tracking pixel, not content), but flagged by QA.
- MSO conditional comments, Outlook `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` namespaces, and mobile breakpoint media queries (`max-width: 480px`) are present and correctly structured — no structural rendering issues found in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- Klaviyo personalization/tracking tokens (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`) are present and appear correctly populated with values, not left as raw placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — could not be confirmed present; QA notes this may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown via the relay; cannot confirm authentication compliance from available data.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA methodology (always included in probing), but its destination/functionality was not independently verified in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracked links carry Klaviyo attribution parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`) but **no standard UTM parameters** (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in either probed URL — if downstream analytics (e.g. GA4) relies on UTM tagging, this campaign may not attribute correctly outside Klaviyo's own tracking.
- Landing page paths referenced (`/pages/sms-signup`, `/pages/womens`) are plausible Everlane site routes, but cannot be verified as live/correct destinations since the redirect resolution itself is failing (see Section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the two broken tracked links by ensuring the Klaviyo redirect targets are fully-qualified URLs (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/sms-signup`, `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens`) rather than relative paths.
2. Add `alt=""` (decorative) to the tracking pixel to clear the accessibility warning.
3. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually set at the ESP level (Klaviyo supports RFC 8058); re-test via a header-capturing relay other than AgentMail to rule out a capture artifact.
4. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication results directly at the sending MTA, since Authentication-Results wasn't observable via this relay.
5. Add UTM parameters to tracked links if cross-platform (non-Klaviyo) attribution/analytics is required on the landing pages.
