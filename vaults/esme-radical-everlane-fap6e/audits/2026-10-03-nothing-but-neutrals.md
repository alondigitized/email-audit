# Nothing But Neutrals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand seasonal knitwear send with no offer. The hero is a model in a grey ribbed crewneck with "Put it in Neutral" overlaid, followed by a full-width black SHOP NOW bar. Below that is a 3x3 grid of sweaters sorted by color: cream, grey and black. It looks good and the hierarchy is clear. It gives a subscriber almost nothing to act on, though.
- It is also the third Everlane send on 10-03, after the Everywhere Pant email ("“Obsessed. These pants are amazing!”") and "How We're Wearing Collegium". The Collegium collab and Washable Silk sends both ran two days in a row, so this brand is doing a lot of near-daily sends. This one has no price, no product names, no reviews and no deadline. The sustainability block near the bottom pushes the email further toward brand mood and away from selling.
- Judgement: it is pretty and competent, but low-intent. It is a mid-4s email that would score higher with one named hero product and a reason to click today.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The close crop, the neutral palette and the black SHOP NOW bar make the eye land in the right place.
- The 3x3 grid is visually organized by color (cream, grey, black), which supports the "neutrals" theme at a glance.
- The one-line body copy ("Warm creams, soft oat tones, and stony greys…") is short and fits the season.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images or overlaps, and spacing and alignment are consistent.
- The timing suits early October, with knitwear arriving as the weather turns.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or deadline anywhere, so nothing gives a reason to click now.
- The "Put it in Neutral" overlay is light grey type on a grey sweater, so contrast is weak.
- None of the nine grid products has a name or price. Shoppers can't tell cashmere from merino or what anything costs without clicking.
- The sustainability block with READ MORE is off-topic for a product push. It adds a second CTA that competes with SHOP NOW.
- The footer text is very small and pale grey, and the icon row ("Find a Store", "Sign Up for Texts", "Help Center") is low-value.
- The preheader is not visible in the render, and the cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name a hero product and a price in the hero or directly under it, for example "The Cashmere Crew, $148". Then link the hero to that product, not the generic landing page.
- 2. Add names and prices under each grid tile, with a sizing or fabric callout such as "100% Italian merino" or "Machine washable". Pair it with a rating or review count where available.
- 3. Change the CTA copy from SHOP NOW to something specific, such as "Shop Neutral Knits" or "Shop Sweaters".
- 4. Fix the hero contrast. Use white or black type with a subtle scrim, or move the text onto a clear area.
- 5. Move the sustainability block below the nav, or cut it from product sends.
- 6. Reduce the cadence of near-identical sends, or give each one a distinct angle.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo/MJML-based email with click tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. Two of the 12 probed links failed, the HTML has no `lang` attribute or title, and the relay hid the compliance and authentication headers, so those can't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M41RDNMP6DGG0NXDG7HVMHDG_0` ("Everlane Logo"). The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] SMS signup link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M41RDNMP6DGG0NXDG7HVMHDG_18` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts"). The error was `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** In both cases the tracker's redirect resolved to a **relative path** with no scheme or host. The most likely reason is that the tracker answers with a relative `Location:` header. Alternatively, the QA probe can't resolve relative redirects. The data doesn't show which, so I can't confirm that real users are affected. If the tracker does send relative redirects, a browser would resolve them against `trk.send.everlane.com`, and both links would 404 there.
- **Scope:** These are the header logo and the SMS signup, so the fix is cheap to check. The other 10 probed links did not report this error.
- **Not probed:** 16 footer and social links were skipped by design, so their health is unknown.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M41RDNMP6DGG0NXDG7HVMHDG` has no `alt` attribute (QA warning). It should be `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>`), so screen readers can't pick the language.
- `<title> </title>` is empty.
- The open-tracking pixel has no `alt`, as above.
- `<style>` forces `a:link`, `a:visited`, `a:active` and `a:hover` to `color:#000; text-decoration:none`. Any inline text link is then indistinguishable from body text, which is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk. This only matters where a link sits in running text, and the truncated source doesn't show whether any do.
- `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` renders h4 text very small on mobile.
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/…/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and some other clients. The stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica, which is fine.
- The MSO conditionals and `.mj-outlook-group-fix` are present and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved tokens such as `{{ }}` or `*|…|*`. The per-recipient `_kx` and `bxid` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA output itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message in a direct-delivery test inbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **Unsubscribe and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing. The footer was not in the truncated source, so I can't verify the postal address or the unsubscribe link target.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible links carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) but no `utm_*` parameters are visible. The parameter strings are truncated and wrapped in `trk.send.everlane.com` redirects, so UTM presence can't be confirmed.
- The two destinations that did resolve (`/pages/womens`, `/pages/sms-signup`) are plausible and relevant.
- The two failing links are the continuity risk, as in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw `Location:` header from `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…_0` and `…_18`. If it is relative, make the tracker emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/…` URLs. If it already is absolute, the probe has a bug to fix.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and fill in `<title>` (it can match the subject, "Nothing But Neutrals").
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Restore an underline or distinct colour on inline text links. Raise the mobile h4 size above 10px.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a direct-delivery copy, since the relay hides them.
6. Confirm `utm_*` parameters on the destination URLs by unwrapping the tracked links.
