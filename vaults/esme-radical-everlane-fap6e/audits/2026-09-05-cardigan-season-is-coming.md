# Cardigan Season is Coming
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine break from the "End of Summer Sale" template Everlane's run since 08/01 — no watercolor banner, no olive/cream split headline, no black discount-percentage stack. Instead it's a clean seasonal-transition send: single hero model in a cream cardigan, "Cozy Starts Here," followed by a three-product cardigan/sweater lineup. It reads as a merchandising email, not a promo blast, and there's zero discount language anywhere in the body — a real departure from the last six sends I've reviewed.
- That's also its weakness for a deal-driven inbox scan: there is no offer. No %, no BOGO, nothing — just "Shop Now." For a subscriber trained by the last three weeks of sale emails, the absence of a number is conspicuous, and the fine print at the bottom ("Offer valid until 09/08/2026") dangling with no offer stated anywhere above it is a real miss — it reads like copy that should've had a discount attached and didn't.
- Net: well-executed seasonal creative, clean layout, no render bugs — but low urgency and no visible incentive to click today rather than next week.

## What's working

- Genuinely fresh creative — first non-"End of Summer Sale" hero template in the recent run.
- Clean, uncluttered layout: one hero + three products + one "Shop All" CTA, easy to scan top to bottom.
- Product copy is specific and useful (merino/alpaca fabric callouts, fit descriptions like "cropped, close-fitting").
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.
- Consistent black-bar "SHOP NOW" CTA style ties hero and product rows together.

## What's weak

- No visible offer anywhere in the body, yet the footer fine print references an "offer" expiring 09/08 — mismatch between body and legal text.
- Subject "Cardigan Season is Coming" is soft/seasonal, not concrete — no product, price, or urgency signal.
- All three lineup CTAs say "SHOP NOW" — no differentiation, easy to skim past.
- After three weeks of sale emails, a full-price seasonal send may read as a step down in perceived value, not up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one visible incentive — even a soft one ("New arrivals, up to 15% off with code COZY") — since the footer already implies an offer exists; right now it's an orphaned disclosure.
- 2. Sharpen the subject to reference a specific product or fabric ("Merino Cardigans Just Landed") rather than the vague "Cardigan Season is Coming."
- 3. Vary the CTA copy per product ("Shop Merino," "Shop Alpaca," "Shop Full-Zip") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" three times.
- 4. Add a one-line urgency cue near the hero (e.g., "Back in stock — sizes going fast") since there's no time pressure anywhere in the send.
