# A Closer Look at Cashmere
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a decent cashmere product-mood send, but the offer is buried. The hero is a strong, tactile crop of a rust cardigan over a denim jacket and white knit, with a black SHOP NOW bar underneath. The "Extra 20% off markdowns" offer only appears in a brown block about two-thirds of the way down. It is the same promo as the plain-text "New Markdowns With an Extra 20% Off" send earlier today (3.00/10). The two sends say the same thing in two different ways, and neither does it well.
- The subject, "A Closer Look at Cashmere," matches Everlane's recent run of offer-free mood subjects ("Scenes From Fall," "A Softer Take on Corduroy"). A subscriber would read it as editorial and skip it. Meanwhile the real hook is a 20% extra markdown with a 10/18 deadline. This is the third Everlane send today, and the pattern is mood, then offer, then mood again. The inbox cadence is getting noisy.
- Judgement: a clean, on-brand layout with a weak commercial structure. The product row is nice but has no prices, names or links to specific items, and the offer is a footer-style afterthought. It is better than the plain-text note, but it still leaves value on the table.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and textured, with a seasonally right fall palette and a clear black SHOP NOW bar.
- The three-sweater row (burgundy cardigan, cream V-neck, wine crewneck) is clean, evenly spaced and on-brand.
- The "Close Up on Cashmere" overlay ties the hero image to the subject line.
- The short copy lines ("Coffee runs. Office days. Cozy weekends.") are on-voice and quick to read.
- The offer block gives the deal and has separate Women's and Men's sale links.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer sits below the fold and below two CTAs, so the hero never mentions it.
- The "Close Up" and "on Cashmere" overlay text is split far apart across the image, and the right half crosses the cardigan texture, which hurts legibility.
- Both SHOP NOW buttons are generic and say nothing about where they go.
- The product row has no prices, names or sale markers, so the "now at better prices" claim has no proof.
- The "Now at better prices" line and the "extra 20% off markdowns" block say different things. The fine print says the offer is limited to select styles. The cashmere items shown may not even be marked down.
- The brown offer block has a lot of empty padding below the links.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero or the subject line: put "Extra 20% off markdowns" on the image and make the CTA "Shop cashmere markdowns."
- 2. Show sale prices (was/now) under each sweater and link each to its own product page.
- 3. Close the offer block with the deadline ("Ends Sunday 10/18") instead of the vague "limited time only."
- 4. Fix the overlay so "Close Up on Cashmere" reads as one line, or place it in a clean area of the image.
- 5. Hold this send or merge it with the earlier markdowns email. Two sends on the same promo in one day trains subscribers to ignore both.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built on Klaviyo (MJML-style tables) and routes all links through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. One link in the QA run is broken because it has no scheme or host. Only 43% of checks passed. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Broken link (confirmed FAIL):** The link with text "Everlane" resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=yTMOVl1XHu7UHBDiF3ftE_vrVfv5mitFkHfllJRzCOILn81Cmts0GyJlgCoeJqhg.USb9mf&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The QA error was `unknown url type`. The href is a relative path with no scheme or host, so it can't be fetched or followed outside a browser that happens to resolve it against a base URL.
  - The tracked redirect URL is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M50N0MZS56ZTV7PXHY1FXM2W_0`. The relative path appears to be the destination the redirect returned. It is probably a `Location` header that the probe didn't resolve against the host.
  - This may be a QA tool limitation rather than a defect in the email. Confirm that the redirect works in a browser or with `curl -IL`.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 25 links were probed, and 13 were skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). The unsubscribe link was always included. Coverage of the skipped links is unknown.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M50N0MZS56ZTV7PXHY1FXM2W` is the open-tracking image, which is expected. See the alt-text note in section 3.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (confirmed WARN):** The open-tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M50N0MZS56ZTV7PXHY1FXM2W` has no `alt` attribute. This is low severity because it's a 1x1 tracking image. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. The QA check didn't report other images as missing alt text.
- **Web font via `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is cosmetic.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is minor.
- **Dark-mode handling:** The `.ev-clay-dark` and `[data-ogsc]` rules are present and correctly hide and show the light and dark variants.
- **Outlook:** The VML/`o:` namespaces and the `mso` conditional comments are present, which is correct.
- The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't evaluate the body, the `lang` attribute or the preheader.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|…|*`. The truncated body wasn't checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** The QA tool says this "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". The header's presence can't be confirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders) may not be supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool says this is expected via the relay.
- **Not an email defect:** All three warnings may be artifacts of the relay stripping headers. Verify them against the raw message headers from a direct send, for example a seed test to Gmail ("Show original").
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** These are in the footer, which was truncated. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't shown. I couldn't verify either.
- **Sender:** `support@everlane.com` is a support address used as the marketing From address. Replies go to the support queue. This is a minor concern and I can't confirm it from the data.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **No standard UTM parameters in the evidence:** The only destination I could see carries Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`), not `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. Klaviyo's `_kx` and `cm_*` parameters do attribution inside Klaviyo, but GA/analytics attribution may need UTMs. The parameters are cut off, so UTMs may be present further along.
- **Landing page:** The tested link goes to `/pages/womens`, which is a generic women's landing page. The subject, "A Closer Look at Cashmere", suggests a cashmere-specific destination. I could only verify the link text "Everlane", which is likely the header logo. The CTA links weren't visible.
- **Tracking domain:** `trk.send.everlane.com` is a branded subdomain of the sender domain, which is good for deliverability.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Confirm the `/pages/womens?...` redirect in a browser or with `curl -IL`. If it's broken, make the redirect return an absolute URL.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) in the raw headers of a direct send. Add them if they're missing.
3. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on a direct seed send, not through the relay.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Confirm the campaign UTMs (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present on CTA destinations.
6. Check that the primary CTA goes to a cashmere-specific collection page, not just `/pages/womens`.
7. Fill in the `<title>`. This is minor.
8. For the QA tooling, resolve relative redirect `Location` headers against the redirect host, and probe the remaining 13 links so the "broken link" result isn't a false positive.
