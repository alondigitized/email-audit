# Build Your Everyday Rotation for 40% Less
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is another clean Black Friday "40% off everything" send, and it adds almost nothing to the last several. The hero is a tight crop of a red knit polo with white "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" over it. The subject promises "Build Your Everyday Rotation", but the body never shows a rotation. There's one cropped garment, one line of copy, and no product grid.
- The brand history makes this worse. The 2026-11-20 "The Styles You Reach for Most Are 40% Off" send used this same red polo crop, and "40% Off Gifts They'll Keep Wearing All Winter" had a near-identical hero. A subscriber who has seen a week of these will treat this as a repeat. The Better Gift Guide block is a decent hedge toward gifting, but it is a flat maroon rectangle with a lot of dead space.
- Verdict: a competent template with a subject line that over-promises. It is fine for the offer and weak on newness, so it lands as an average promo.

## What's working

- The offer is unmistakable: "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is large, white and high-contrast, and sits at the center of the hero.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bar sits directly under the hero, so the CTA is easy to find.
- The visual hierarchy is clean. Logo, then hero, then one line of copy, then the gift block.
- The her/him split in the gift guide gives people a quick way to self-segment.
- The footer discloses the end date (12/02/2026) and the online-only terms.

## What's weak

- The hero crop is tight and shows no face or full outfit. The buttons run through "EVERYTHING*", and the white text partly overlaps a button on the right.
- The subject promises a "rotation", but there are no multiple products, no outfit pairings and no product links.
- "Shop Now" is generic and doesn't name a category. The only category-specific links sit below the gift block.
- The maroon gift block has too much empty padding and no imagery. It reads as unfinished.
- Urgency is missing from the body. The 12/02 deadline appears only in small gray fine print, and the asterisk on "EVERYTHING*" hides the exclusions.
- There are no reviews, ratings or product details anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the single cropped hero with a 4-6 tile grid of real products (layers plus pants). Each tile should have a name, a price and a link. This would lift the email the most and would make the subject line true.
- 2. Put the deadline above the fold, for example "Ends Dec 2". Show the hero outfit on a full-body shot, not a collar crop.
- 3. Change the CTA to something like "Shop 40% Off Layers" or "Build Your Rotation", and add a second CTA for pants.
- 4. Fill the gift block with two photos, one for her and one for him. Better yet, drop it from this send, since "Better Gifts Under $50" went out on 2026-11-20.
- 5. Add a line of social proof, such as a best-seller badge or a review count.
- 6. Break the creative repetition. Don't reuse the red polo from the 2026-11-20 sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Layers + pants, all 40% off through Dec 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 5 most-worn basics, 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best-selling knits and trousers, plus free returns. Ends 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pieces that go with everything, at Black Friday pricing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo/MJML-based email has two tracked links whose redirect probes failed, an empty `<title>`, no `lang` attribute, and a tracking pixel with no `alt`. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed because the relay likely strips those headers. The HTML was truncated, so only the head and CSS were inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures on 2 links.** The probe reported `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…'` for the "Everlane" link (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7XWQEZ9XN4YGZND0KX2W2YV_0`). It reported the same for "Shop Men" (`…_7`, destination `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`).
  - In both cases the tracker's redirect appears to return a relative `Location` header. A relative `Location` is valid under RFC 7231 and browsers resolve it, so this is probably a limitation of the probe client and not a broken link for recipients.
  - It is still unverified. A manual `curl -I` on these two URLs is needed to confirm they resolve to `https://www.everlane.com/...`.
- **Low probe coverage.** Only 3 of 12 material links were probed (9 skipped), so the 38% pass rate is not a reliable measure of link health.
- **Tracking pixel has no `alt`.** The image `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7XWQEZ9XN4YGZND0KX2W2YV` is the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` (minor).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Some clients and screen readers use it as the document label.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` doesn't declare a language. Screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation.
- **10px mobile heading size.** The mobile rule `@media (max-width:480px){ h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` sets `h4` to 10px, which is below common legibility guidance (about 14px or more).
- **Outlook and dark mode handling is present.** The source has MSO conditionals, `.mj-outlook-group-fix`, `[data-ogsc]` dark-mode toggles, and an Arial fallback after the custom font. No issues found there.
- **External font `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by many clients, but the font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica. This is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers are unconfirmed.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is an observability gap and not a confirmed violation.
- **Unsubscribe link.** The probe notes that an unsubscribe link is always included in probing. No failure was reported for it.
- **Physical address and footer.** These were not visible in the truncated source, so they cannot be assessed.
- **Sender.** `support@everlane.com` is a support address used for marketing. This is non-blocking but worth noting for reply handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen.** Links carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) behind the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the captured URLs.
- **Not confirmed.** The destination URLs were cut off and the probes failed, so UTM presence after redirect, and whether the "40% less" landing page matches the offer, can't be verified.
- **Redundant link.** The "Everlane" logo goes to `/pages/womens`, while "Shop Men" goes to the men's collection. This is not a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on `…_0` and `…_7` to confirm the redirects resolve. If they do, fix the probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the tracker host.
2. Add `<html lang="en">` and a meaningful `<title>`, such as the subject line.
3. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
4. Raise the mobile `h4` size from 10px to at least 14px.
5. Capture the raw `.eml` headers directly from the inbound message and not from the relay. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `everlane.com`.
6. Follow the redirect chain to the final URL and confirm `utm_*` parameters survive and the landing page shows the 40% offer.
7. Raise the probe budget above 3 of 12 links so the pass rate means something.
