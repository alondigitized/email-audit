# One Last Chance for 30% Off Sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand closing send. The hero reads "Friends & Family Ends Tonight / 30% OFF SITEWIDE" in large white type over a close-cropped model in a navy scarf. There is a black SHOP NOW bar directly under it, and the offer lands in about two seconds.
- The problem is cadence, not execution. This is the fourth or fifth closing-day nudge in two days. "Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sitewide" and "Final Hours: 30% Off Sitewide & Storewide" both went out on 10/13 with nearly identical creative. "One Last Chance" is a third rewording of the same message. A real subscriber has already decided by now, and this email gives them nothing new to decide on.
- The body is two generic 4-up sweater grids with no names, prices, or per-item links. The footer also says the offer applies to "select styles," which quietly contradicts "sitewide" in the hero. It's a competent send, but it's a repeat.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. "30% OFF SITEWIDE" is huge, and "Ends Tonight" sits directly above it.
- The black full-width SHOP NOW bars are clear and easy to tap, and one appears after each section.
- The product grids are clean and consistent. The staggered flat-lay and model shots feel on-brand and seasonal (cardigans, knits, brown mock-necks).
- The page has little text and no render bugs. Nothing overlaps, and no images are broken.
- "Make your picks before styles are back to full price" is a credible, plain-spoken deadline line.

## What's weak

- The cadence is stale. It is the same Friends & Family close for the third time in two days, with no new angle.
- The product tiles have no names, prices, or sale-price callouts. Nothing shows the 30% in dollars.
- There is only one hero CTA and one generic grid CTA, and no tile is clearly its own link. Every "Shop Now" reads as a homepage dump.
- The "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block is brand filler that adds no information, and its SHOP NOW is a small underlined link that competes with the black bars.
- The fine print says "Offer applies to select styles only," which undercuts the "sitewide" headline. It is also gray and tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific time in the hero, such as "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT", and add prices to the product tiles ("$98 → $68.60"). This turns a repeat blast into a decision aid.
- 2. Consolidate. Three or more "ends tonight" sends in a day trains people to ignore the last one. Send one morning reminder and one final-hours send, and suppress anyone who already purchased.
- 3. Link each tile to its own product page and label it (name, price, a "Best seller" tag).
- 4. Add one proof point, such as a star rating or "Selling fast" on a specific item.
- 5. Reconcile "sitewide" with the "select styles" fine print, either by changing the hero language or by listing exclusions.
- 6. Drop the "Better prices, still Everlane" block, or give it a concrete hook like "Cashmere from $68".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: cashmere & knits 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim, and outerwear all included. Prices go back up at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's last chance at Friends & Family pricing. Free returns.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a Klaviyo/MJML-built send with one confirmed broken link: a relative URL in the "Everlane" link text, which the QA probe could not follow. The compliance and authentication headers could not be checked, and one tracking pixel has no alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so I could verify only what the QA data and the visible head section show.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **[FAIL] Relative URL in a link.** The probe for the link with text "Everlane" (tracked as `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4VY3CQ3Y1B61B6VWDGAK921_0`) returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The `Location` target of that redirect is a path-only value (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracking host, so a real click may land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which likely 404s. It may also be a probe artifact, since the redirect may be handled differently for real clients. Test in a real client to confirm.
  - This link is the header/logo link, so it is high-traffic.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 17 were skipped. The result is not a full link audit, and the other 17 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel without alt text.** The `<img>` with `src=https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4VY3CQ3Y1B61B6VWDGAK921` is the open-tracking pixel. It is a 1x1 beacon, so a missing alt is low severity. It should carry `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`.
- All links use the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking wrapper with Klaviyo params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). Nothing else looked malformed in the data provided.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Not an issue: the document has the correct doctype, the VML and Office namespaces, an MSO conditional block, a viewport meta tag, and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, which is a standard Klaviyo/MJML structure.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The head has `<title> </title>`, which contains only whitespace. This is minor and has no effect in most inboxes.
- **Mobile `h4` size.** The media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` at ≤480px. If `h4` is used for readable copy, 10px is below the usual legibility threshold. I can't tell from the truncated source whether it is.
- **Remote `@import` for fonts.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial and Helvetica, so the impact is limited.
- The truncated HTML means I can't check the rest of the alt text, the `role="presentation"` attributes on layout tables, or the dark-mode blocks (`.ev-clay-dark` / `[data-ogsc]`).

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture this header, so this is unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- The unsubscribe link was probed according to the QA note, and no failure was reported for it. I can't see the footer in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the physical postal address.
- The sending domain (`support@everlane.com`) differs from the tracking domain (`send.everlane.com`). This is normal, and no alignment problem is evident.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`) are present on the broken link. I can't confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` are set, because the URL is truncated and the other links are not shown.
- The header link's destination `/pages/womens` is a general category page. It doesn't map to the "30% Off Sitewide" offer in the subject line. Whether the final landing page carries the offer can't be checked, because the redirect failed.
- The subject says "One Last Chance," but the QA data has no information about the send flow or the campaign. Alignment can't be assessed.

7. **Recommendations**
1. **Fix the redirect target for the header link.** Make the redirect destination an absolute URL (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...`) in the Klaviyo link or the tracking-domain config. Then re-test the link.
2. **Probe the remaining 17 links**, including the footer. This should confirm that there are no similar relative redirects and that the unsubscribe link resolves.
3. **Verify the headers in the original message**, not the relay copy. Check for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass in a direct-inbox test, for example Gmail's "Show original".
4. **Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.** Give it explicit `width="1" height="1"` as well.
5. **Add a title** or leave the empty one. It is low priority.
6. **Confirm UTM parameters** on the primary CTA links with the full, untruncated HTML, and check that the offer carries through to the landing pages.
