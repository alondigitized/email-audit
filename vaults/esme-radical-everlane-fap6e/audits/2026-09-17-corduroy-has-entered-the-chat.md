- "Everlane Logo" link (tracked via `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M2R0TFF0PJJBJ8CBDGSPH74J_0`) resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...` — same relative-path defect.

- 12 of 26 material links were probed (14 footer/utility/social links deprioritized per QA methodology); no failures reported among the other 10 probed links.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Image missing `alt` text: tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M2R0TFF0PJJBJ8CBDGSPH74J` has no `alt` attribute. Low-impact (open-tracking pixel, not content-bearing), but worth a blank `alt=""` for strict HTML validation.
- `@font-face` declaration references `https://yourdomain.com/fonts/MaisonNeue-Book.woff2` / `.woff` — a placeholder domain left in the stylesheet. If unresolved, custom font falls back to the declared stack (Arial/Helvetica Neue/Helvetica/sans-serif) rather than breaking rendering, but the asset itself will 404.
- MSO conditional comments, Outlook `mso-table-lspace/rspace` resets, and mobile media queries are present and structured normally — no other markup issues found in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected in the captured payload — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing it rather than the sender omitting it. Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) also not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown via this relay.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA methodology notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), though its exact URL wasn't in the probed/failed set.
- Recommend verifying header capture at the relay level before treating these as sender-side compliance gaps.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Both broken links carry `_kx` (Klaviyo exchange token) and `bxid` params but lack standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` params in the visible query string — attribution relies entirely on Klaviyo's own tracking params, not UTM. Not inherently a bug, but means GA/other non-Klaviyo analytics won't attribute these clicks.
- Target paths (`/pages/sms-signup`, `/pages/womens`) are relative — cannot confirm alignment with live Everlane site structure without resolving against a base domain (e.g. `everlane.com`).

7. Recommendations
- Fix the redirect targets for the two failing Klaviyo tracked links so they resolve to absolute URLs (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/sms-signup...` and `.../pages/womens...`) instead of relative paths.
- Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel image for HTML validation cleanliness.
- Replace or remove the placeholder `yourdomain.com` font-face URLs with the actual asset host.
- Confirm with the ESP/relay whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are being sent but dropped by the AgentMail relay, or genuinely absent — re-test by capturing raw headers at the MTA/inbox level rather than through this relay.
