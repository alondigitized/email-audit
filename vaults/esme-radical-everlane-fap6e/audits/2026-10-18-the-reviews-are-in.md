# The Reviews Are In
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a social-proof denim and pants send, and it's close to a repeat of the 2026-10-17 "Consider These Customer Approved" email. The hero is the same denim-on-denim model shot with "Favorite These" overlaid. The body is the same two products (Column Jean, Utility Seamed Wide-Leg Pant) with the same kind of quote under each. A subscriber who got both emails in the same weekend would see this as a resend with a new subject line.
- The idea is sound. Real customer quotes under specific products are the best content Everlane has sent this week. But the email buries its two strongest assets. The extra 20% off markdowns offer sits at the very bottom in a brown block, and the quotes are small, centered text under the product images. Nothing in the hero or the subject tells the reader there's a deal or a specific reason to click.
- Verdict: a competent 6, and Everlane's usual layout polish. It would be a 7 if the offer led, or if it were the first time this creative had run.

## What's working

- The review quotes are specific and attributed: "These jeans are among the best I've ever purchased." — Christine R., and the MJ quote about dressing the pants up or down.
- The two black buttons directly under the hero, SHOP DENIM and SHOP PANTS, are unambiguous and easy to tap.
- The hero is a strong, clean model shot, and the one-line "The pants and jeans customers can't stop talking about" ties it to the review theme.
- The product modules pair a styled look with a flat-lay of the garment. That helps a shopper judge fit and color.
- No render bugs: images load, spacing is even, and nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- The extra 20% off markdowns offer is at the bottom, after the sustainability block. Nothing above it hints at a deal.
- The hero copy is just "Favorite These", with no reference to reviews. That makes the subject line "The Reviews Are In" feel disconnected from what you see.
- The impact-report block ("We carefully consider our materials…") with its black READ OUR LATEST IMPACT REPORT button interrupts the shopping flow and competes with the sale CTA.
- It repeats the "Favorite These" hero from the 2026-10-17 "Consider These Customer Approved" send. It also continues the 10/16–10/17 run of near-duplicate product-mood emails.
- The deadline is vague ("for a limited time only"). The actual end, 10/18 at 11:59 PM PST, is only in the grey fine print.
- No star ratings, review counts or prices are shown, so the social proof is thinner than the subject promises.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer to the top. Put "Extra 20% off markdowns, ends tonight" in a slim banner above the hero, or in the hero overlay, and keep the review modules below it.
- 2. Make the proof quantifiable. Add star ratings and review counts ("4.8★ · 2,300 reviews") next to each product, and show prices.
- 3. Cut the impact-report block from this send, or move it below the sale block. It dilutes the one job the email has.
- 4. Don't send the same hero and product pair twice in a weekend. Rotate in different products and different quotes.
- 5. Replace the vague deadline with the real one: "Ends 10/18, 11:59 PM PST."

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "The Reviews Are In"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built (kl-row/kl-column, `_kx` params) MJML-style layout. The QA run passed 43% of checks, and the one hard failure is a tracked link whose redirect target can't be resolved. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts and don't confirm real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Broken redirect on the "Everlane" link.**
  - URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M567N97HDZSP91VDX9PV8DE3_0`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`. That means the tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the request URL. That would send the click to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com/pages/womens`, which would likely 404 for real users.
  - The failure could instead be a limitation of the probe's HTTP client. The only way to confirm is to request the URL with `curl -sI` and read the actual `Location` header.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 17 skipped (footer, social, utility). The other 17 are unverified, not clean.
- **Open-tracking pixel** (`https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M567N97HDZSP91VDX9PV8DE3`) is flagged as missing alt text. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. This is minor in email, but some clients and web-view versions use it.
- **Pixel missing alt:** the open-tracking `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. This is low severity because it is a 1x1 tracking pixel. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- **Outlook handling is present and correct:**
  - `mso` conditionals.
  - `OfficeDocumentSettings`.
  - `.mj-outlook-group-fix`.
  - `#outlook a` reset.
- **Not verifiable:** the source is truncated, so I can't audit alt text on content images, button markup, or `lang`/`role` attributes beyond the head.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered Klaviyo/Liquid tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`). The tail of the body is truncated, so tokens in the footer or later blocks are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.**
- The QA tool itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. So SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe are unverified, not confirmed absent.
- Because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, re-check these headers on the raw message.
- The footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) is outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked. The QA notes the unsubscribe link was always included in probing, but no result is reported for it.
- The From address is `support@everlane.com` while links use `trk.send.everlane.com`. The domains share an organizational domain, so alignment looks plausible, but it can't be confirmed without the headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The tracked link resolves (when it resolves) to `/pages/womens` with Klaviyo params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`).
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the captured portion of the query string, which was truncated. Whether UTMs exist is undetermined.
- If Klaviyo's UTM auto-tagging is off, analytics attribution would depend only on the `_kx` identifiers.
- Landing-page alignment couldn't be checked because the redirect never resolved in the probe (see section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the redirect on `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M567N97HDZSP91VDX9PV8DE3_0`.** Run `curl -sI` and read `Location`. If it is relative, fix the tracking-domain redirect config to emit an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?...` URL.
2. **Check headers on the raw message** (outside the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. **Confirm UTM parameters** on the final landing URL.
4. Fill in `<title>`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and remaining image alts can be checked. Also probe the 17 skipped links.
