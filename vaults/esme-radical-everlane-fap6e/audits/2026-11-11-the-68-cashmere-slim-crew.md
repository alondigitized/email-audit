# The $68 Cashmere Slim Crew
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-product send for Everlane's $68 Cashmere Slim Crew. The subject puts the price up front, which is the strongest thing about it. The body doesn't keep up. The hero is a tight crop of a model in a heather-grey sweater, with white "Everyone Deserves Better / Cashmere Slim Crew" type laid over a pale knit. The contrast is poor. The subhead, "We invited digital creator Salomé Mory...", is nearly unreadable.
- The second image repeats the problem. "Certified Cashmere / Trusted Factories / Priced Better at $68" is scattered in thin white type over light grey, and the $68 claim, the one hard offer, is the hardest line to read. The price appears nowhere in a legible place. The CTAs are clear black buttons, but the main one reads "SHOP THE SLIM CREW" and sits under a hero with no price. The sustainability block at the bottom is credible but pulls attention away from the product.
- Compared with the 2026-11-10 "Better Cashmere for Everyone" send, which had three creators, three prices and three CTAs, this one is thinner and its legibility is worse. It's a decent concept with weak execution. Fix the type contrast and it improves a lot.

## What's working

- The subject leads with a concrete price ($68) and a named product.
- Black full-width "SHOP THE SLIM CREW" button is unambiguous and sits right under the hero.
- The Salomé quote, "It feels like a hug in the middle of winter," is warm and gives some social proof.
- The three-colour swatch row (black, red, burgundy) shows range without clutter.
- Clean, restrained Everlane brand voice and layout, with no render breakage.

## What's weak

- White overlay text on the hero and the second image has very low contrast, so the subhead and the "Priced Better at $68" line are hard to read.
- The $68 price never appears in legible body copy. The offer is buried in a scattered image overlay.
- The three-colour row has no prices, names or individual links I can see, so it works as decoration rather than as a shopping row.
- "Available in limited quantities. Get a head start on gifting." is vague urgency with no deadline.
- The GCS sourcing block and "READ MORE" CTA compete with the sale of the sweater, and the footer nav is long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "$68" in large, dark, legible type below the hero, next to the button: "Cashmere Slim Crew. $68." Stop relying on thin white text over a photo.
- 2. Make the hero overlay darker or add a scrim. Cut the subhead, or move it into a solid band under the image.
- 3. Give each colour swatch a name, a price and a direct link to that colour's product page.
- 4. Replace the vague urgency with something real, such as "Holiday sizes selling fast" or a concrete restock or shipping-by date for gifting.
- 5. Move the sustainability story lower, or drop its separate CTA, so there is one clear action.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup, `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking) with no confirmed hard defects. The three QA "FAIL" results come from the probe failing to resolve relative redirect targets, and the compliance and authentication warnings come from headers the AgentMail relay didn't capture. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures (3 of 3 FAILs):** each failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/…'` or `'/collections/…'`.
  - `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7699R74B06K6CMM3GG3SJCF_14` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") → `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`
  - `…_0` ("Everlane" logo) → `/pages/womens?_kx=…`
  - `…_10` ("Shop Men") → `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect returns a **relative** `Location` path, which the QA probe's HTTP client couldn't resolve. Browsers and mail clients resolve relative `Location` headers per RFC 7231, so this probably doesn't break users. It is not confirmed as a user-facing break, but it is unusual for a tracking redirect and worth verifying in a real client.
- **Coverage gap:** "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped". No link, including the unsubscribe link, was actually verified. Link health is unknown.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7699R74B06K6CMM3GG3SJCF` is the open-tracking image. Open counts will be unreliable with Apple Mail Privacy Protection.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text:** the only alt-text warning is on the tracking pixel (`/o/01M7699R74B06K6CMM3GG3SJCF`). It is non-visual, so impact is low. Adding `alt=""` would satisfy the checker.
