# Cardigan Season Starts Here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's cleanest single-category send of the recent run — a legitimate seasonal hook ("Cardigan Season Starts Here") instead of a recycled neutrals or arrivals template. The hero shot (brown cardigan, camel leather sofa, white trousers) is genuinely well-styled and on-brand, and the two-product cardigan grid (Shrunken Crew in red, Cashmere Crew Cropped in black) is tight and legible. It closes with the now-standard Everlane trio: sweaters CTA banner, Extra 15% Off Sale Styles, and the sustainability impact-report block — all recognizable from the 09-20/09-21 sends.
- The weakness is that the "better prices" line over-promises and under-delivers — no price or percentage appears anywhere near the actual cardigan products, only a generic 15%-off-sale-styles banner further down that isn't clearly tied to the two hero cardigans. Compared to 09-20's "Introducing the Leather Boxy Tote" and the 09-21 neutrals/tees sends, this one has a sharper premise but the same execution gap: strong visual, weak offer clarity.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy are tightly aligned to a real seasonal moment (cardigans, layering) rather than a generic arrivals/color-story rerun.
- Hero image is high-quality editorial styling that reinforces the "lessons in layering" concept.
- Product grid is clean with clear product names and one-line styling tips ("Wear it buttoned up with denim or open over a tee").
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- Consistent brand footer/impact block matches prior sends, so cadence and brand recognition are intact.

## What's weak

- "Especially at our new better prices" sets up a price expectation that never appears — no strikethrough price, no % off shown near either cardigan.
- The Extra 15% Off Sale Styles banner is visually disconnected from the cardigans above it — unclear if it applies to them or is a separate sitewide sale.
- Two products is a thin selection for an email framed around "cardigan season" — feels more like a teaser than a full shop-this moment.
- No price, sizing, or fit information visible anywhere in the body, which is a real gap for a clothing purchase decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number next to "new better prices" — either show the actual price/strikethrough on the two product cards or drop the phrase and let the 15%-off banner do that work explicitly tied to cardigans.
- 2. Expand the grid to 4 cardigan styles (colors/cuts) to justify "cardigan season" as a category moment, not a two-SKU spotlight.
- 3. Move or duplicate the Extra 15% Off banner directly under the product grid so the discount reads as applying to what was just shown.
- 4. Add one line of fit/fabric detail per product card (e.g., "cropped, cotton-cashmere blend, true to size") since that's a real purchase-decision blocker with no info currently visible.
