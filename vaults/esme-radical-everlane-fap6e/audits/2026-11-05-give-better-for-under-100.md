# Give Better for Under $100
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide send with a Black Friday offer tacked on at the bottom. The hero is a red mohair hat on a camel scarf with "Better Gifts Under $100" in white type and a SHOP NOW bar underneath. It is attractive and seasonal. The real offer, 30% off, sits in a banner near the bottom, and the headline promises something else.
- The email has two jobs and does neither fully. "Under $100" is a price ceiling, not a discount. The 30% off is the only offer, yet it comes after the product tiles and the gift buttons. Earlier today Everlane sent "Black Friday Preview: Shop 30% Off", which led with that same 30%. This send repeats it as a footer banner, so the second email in one day feels like a rerun of the first.
- Judgement: a decent seasonal send with weak offer hierarchy and some execution gaps. It is better than the recent offer-free product stories, but it is not as clear as this morning's 30% send.

## What's working

- The hero image is warm, tactile and clearly wintry. The red hat against the camel scarf stops the scroll.
- The headline is large and states the value frame plainly: gifts under $100.
- Splitting the buttons into "Gifts for Her Under $100" and "Gifts for Him Under $100" gives clear, specific paths for gifting.
- The Black Friday Preview 30% off banner gives a real price reduction, and the fine print shows a deadline of 11/09.
- The dark maroon bars and the simple layout keep the brand look consistent and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The white headline sits over a busy, light image. "$100" fades into the snow at the bottom and "Under" crosses the scarf, so the key number is the hardest word to read.
- The copy has a visible typo: "Best-seling styles."
- The three product tiles (sweater, jeans, flat) have no names, prices or links I can see. They don't show that anything is under $100.
- The 30% off banner is a large flat brown block with a small text cluster and a lot of dead space. It looks unfinished, as if an image is missing, and the offer is buried below the fold.
- The email mixes "under $100" with "30% off our favorites". The fine print says the offer covers select styles, so it isn't clear whether the discount applies to the gifts above.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. For example: "Gifts Under $100, Plus 30% Off Our Favorites". Drop the separate banner, or shrink it to a single strip.
- 2. Fix the headline contrast. Use a darker text colour, a scrim over the image, or move the text onto a solid panel.
- 3. Add a name and price to each product tile, and link each one to its product page. Show the real prices, such as "$88", so the under-$100 claim is proven.
- 4. Replace the empty brown block with a tight banner: "30% off ends 11/9", with one button.
- 5. Fix the "seling" typo and state the deadline in the body, not only in the fine print.
- 6. Stagger the sends. Hold this one until tomorrow, or make it clearly different from today's 30% preview email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts under $100, plus 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Great gifts, all under $100`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, denim and flats for her and him. 30% off ends 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Best-sellers for every name on your list, no one over $100.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email with click and open tracking through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The source I was given is truncated and 8 of 20 material links went unprobed, so several checks are inconclusive. The confirmed defects are minor: an empty `<title>`, a tracking pixel with no `alt`, and a 10px mobile `h4`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** Four probed links returned HTTP 429, which is rate limiting from the tracker and not a dead link:
  - `.../01M6PTTVZRKRQ6EV33NDP3M1FN_2` ("Shop Now")
  - `..._8` ("Shop The Gift Guide")
  - `..._10` ("Shop Women")
  - `..._11` ("Shop Men")
- **Coverage gap:** probing stopped at the 70s budget. Links `_0`, `_1`, `_3`, `_4` and `_17` (plus 3 more) are unverified, so they are neither pass nor fail. The 57% pass rate reflects skipped and warned checks, not 0 issues versus failures.
- **Redirect-wrapped links:** every CTA goes through the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/<id>_N` wrapper. The final destinations were not resolved, so the target URLs cannot be validated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6PTTVZRKRQ6EV33NDP3M1FN` has no `alt`. It is an open-tracking pixel, so the fix is `alt=""`, not descriptive text. Severity is low.
- **Small mobile heading:** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important } }` renders any `h4` text at 10px on mobile. That is below common legibility guidance (about 11–14px minimum). I could not see which content uses `h4` in the truncated source.
- **Link styling:** the global rules `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` remove underlines. Inline text links, if any, would not be distinguishable by anything except position. I could not confirm that any exist.
- **Web font import:** the `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by many clients, including Gmail and Outlook. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is not a defect.
- **Outlook:** the MSO conditional comments and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present, with no problems observed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|...|*`) appear in the visible source. Nothing after the truncation point could be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed failure. Check the raw message in a direct-delivery test inbox before reporting it. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Verify with a direct-to-Gmail test, or by checking the DNS records for `send.everlane.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not visible in the truncated source. Compliance is unverified.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as a marketing sender. This is a minor point and may affect reply handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be assessed. Destinations sit behind the Klaviyo redirect, and the probes either returned 429 or never ran. Nothing in the data shows a mismatch, so I'm not flagging one.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 8 unprobed links and the 4 rate-limited links with a slower request rate or a browser User-Agent. Follow the redirects to capture the final URLs and UTM values.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers of a direct-delivery copy, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment there.
3. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and add a meaningful `<title>` (for example the subject line).
4. Raise the mobile `h4` size above 10px, or confirm it is only used for non-essential text.
