# The Pants People Keep Buying
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid product-led send under a Black Friday banner. The hero is a tight crop of black barrel-leg trousers with "The Pants People Keep Buying" in thin white type over the model's torso. The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" strip sits above the logo, so the offer is visible but not the star. The three zig-zag product rows (Everywhere Pant, Way-High Curve Jean, Utility Twill Barrel Pant) are clean and easy to scan, and they all show the same barrel silhouette.
- The weakness is how the offer and the product story relate. The 40% banner and the pants story don't connect: no price, no "now $X," and no sale tag on any product. The headline text also lands across the model's waist and the black fabric, so it competes with the image and reads faint. This is the sixth Black Friday "40% off" send in about three days, following "Cold-Weather Gifts Are Now 40% Off" and "Shop 40% Off Everything" earlier today. A subscriber is likely numb to it by now. The pants angle is the freshest thing in the run, and it works as a category send much like "Boot Season Is Here" on 11-17.
- Judgement: good creative and a decent subject, held back by a sale message that has become repetitive and by the lack of any price proof. It's a 6, and a stronger version of the same email could reach 7.

## What's working

- The hero is a single strong product image with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- The zig-zag layout with a short fabric and fit blurb per product is easy to scan and fits the brand's editorial tone.
- The "Is it the price? The fit? The fabric?" line is a good bridge into the products, and the product copy names materials like organic cotton and linen, which is the detail a considered buyer wants.
- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" banner with Women and Men links is up top, so the offer is seen before any scrolling.
- The footer terms are legible and state a clear end date (12/02/2026).

## What's weak

- The hero headline in thin white type sits over the model's torso and the dark fabric, where it is low-contrast and partly lost against the pale waistband.
- The offer is disconnected from the products. No product shows a price, a sale price or a "40% off" tag, so the discount never reaches the pants.
- The three SHOP NOW links are identical, small, underlined text links that don't say which product they go to.
- The maroon "Better Gift Guide" block at the end is a different campaign. It adds a second message after the pants story and dilutes the close.
- This is the sixth near-identical 40%-off message in three days. Nothing here signals why today's send matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the offer to the product: put "now $X" or "40% off" next to each pant, so the discount appears where the buyer is looking.
- 2. Fix hero legibility: move the headline to a solid band or a darker image area, or make it bolder, and add "40% Off" to it.
- 3. Make the product CTAs specific ("Shop the Everywhere Pant") and make them real buttons.
- 4. Cut or demote the gift guide block, or move it above the footer links with a single CTA. One story per send.
- 5. Vary the cadence: skip a send, or give each Black Friday email a distinct angle with a distinct subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Barrel Pant, Now 40% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our Best-Selling Pants, 40% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Organic cotton, linen and twill. 40% off through 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three fits people reorder, all 40% off.`

## Technical Audit: Everlane, "The Pants People Keep Buying"

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect not followed.** The link text "Everlane" points to `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7TWCJ8TVP22A5FH1NP1NFT9_3`. The prober failed with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - This means the tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve this against the tracker host, but the prober cannot.
  - If the relative path is resolved against `trk.send.everlane.com`, the click lands on a tracker-host path that may 404 instead of everlane.com. The probe result alone can't confirm this. Verify with `curl -I` on that URL.
  - The test supports a configuration or redirect-format problem in that tracked link, not just a probe artifact.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 18 skipped (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). The 43% pass rate is driven by this and the warnings, so it should not be read as 57% of links being broken.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7TWCJ8TVP22A5FH1NP1NFT9` is flagged for missing alt. It is a tracking pixel, so this is expected and low severity.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **No `lang` attribute.** The root tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, so screen readers can't select a language.
- **10px mobile heading.** `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important; … } }` is below common legibility minimums (about 14px or more).
- **Image alt.** The pixel image has no alt (see section 2). Alt text on the content images could not be verified because the source was truncated.
