## What's weak

- **The entire viewport** — it's a cropped, blurry close-up of a forearm on a railing. No headline, no logo, no nav bar, no product.
- **Zero cost-transparency signal** — Everlane's whole pitch to someone like me is "Cost ___. Transparency ___." I see none of that math anywhere.
- **No CTA at all** — just a tiny "x" close icon in the bottom right corner, presumably for a hidden modal I can't see the contents of.

- Put the cost-breakdown callout back above the fold — that's the one thing that makes me trust Everlane over a fast-fashion knockoff.
- Surface a real CTA ("Shop New Arrivals," "See the Factory Story") — right now there's nothing tappable but a stray close button.
- QA this on actual mobile viewports before it goes live — a five-pairs-of-jeans repeat customer bouncing off a broken hero is a lost order.
