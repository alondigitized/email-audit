# Homepage snapshot · www.everlane.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Everlane is doing its whole "let the product breathe" thing — which, fine, I respect the restraint — but what I'm looking at is a near-black top half with zero legible text and two products floating against a gray field below it. No headline, no price, no cost breakdown, no CTA button. This looks less like a homepage and more like a catalog page mid-load. If this is deliberate minimalism, it's working against them; if it's a render issue, nobody at HQ is watching.

## What's working

- **Product styling is clean** — the black denim shorts and the nude kitten-heel thong sandal are shot beautifully against a neutral ground; both read as current-season elevated basics
- **No visual clutter** — the two-product layout communicates "edited, not clearance rack," which does match Everlane's brand positioning
- **The sandal is aspirational for me** — I could see myself clicking on that heel immediately if there were a path to do so

## What's weak

- **The entire top half of the viewport is a dark void** — no text, no imagery beyond a faint texture; looks like a failed hero load or a black-on-black headline that never rendered
- **No CTA anywhere** — I cannot shop these products; there is no button, no "shop now," no product name, nothing to tap
- **No price or cost story** — Everlane's whole identity is radical pricing transparency; not showing me a single number is a brand-level contradiction
- **No navigation visible above the fold** — I can't even route myself to Women's / Denim / Shoes without hunting for a hamburger menu
- **Zero copy** — the page is silent; a first-time visitor has no idea what the brand is, let alone what's new

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero module immediately** — that black top half either has a failed image load or white-on-black text that isn't rendering; ship a fallback background color + visible headline
- **Overlay price + cost breakdown on the product shots** — even one line: "The Denim Short — $78 / cost to make: $31" would differentiate this from every other minimalist brand instantly
- **Add a sticky nav bar or at minimum a visible "Shop Women" CTA** — I should be able to reach my category in one tap without hunting
- **Put the free-shipping threshold or a member badge in a top banner strip** — even "Free shipping on orders over $75" gives me a reason to keep building a cart
