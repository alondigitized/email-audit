# Two Fits. One Better Price.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a denim product send dressed up as UGC. The "$88" price is the strongest asset: "Two takes on our signature high rise... they're both now $88." The concrete price is the only hard offer, and it sits in small body copy under the first hero. The headline, "Meet the Way-High® Family," says nothing about price or urgency.
- The send has three SHOP NOW buttons and a Black Friday 30% block at the bottom. That block competes with the $88 message and muddies the offer. The subject, "Two Fits. One Better Price.", promises a price the hero never shows. It is also the fifth or sixth Everlane send in about five days, after "Ending Tonight: 30% Off Select Styles" earlier today and "30% Off Your Next Layer" yesterday. A subscriber who sees "30% off" in one email and "$88" in the next will wonder which deal is real.
- The creative is attractive, but the product is weak. The two jeans appear only as small flat-lays with no names, no fit labels and no individual links. The recipient can't tell which fit is which, or what the "two fits" are.

## What's working

- A concrete price ("now $88") gives a clear, honest offer.
- The UGC-style hero photos (@zoelaz, @samanthamhorton) feel authentic and on-trend for fall.
- The layout is clean and scannable, with generous whitespace and consistent black buttons.
- The Black Friday preview block with split Women/Men links gives a second path for shoppers who aren't looking for denim.
- The footer states the offer terms and the 11/09/2026 11:59 PM PST deadline.

## What's weak

- The price is buried in small text under the first image, and no hero or button mentions $88.
- All three SHOP NOW buttons are generic and probably go to the same place. The flat-lay jeans have no labels, prices or individual links.
- The 30% Off block clashes with the $88 message and repeats the previous sends' offer.
- "Two fits" is never explained. Neither pair is named, and there are no fit descriptions such as straight versus barrel, and no sizing cues.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and no urgency in the body, even though the offer ends tonight.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price in the hero. Overlay "Way-High® Jeans, now $88" on the first image, so the offer lands before the scroll.
- 2. Label the two flat-lays ("Way-High® Straight" / "Way-High® Barrel") with the $88 price, and link each one to its own product page.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Way-High® Jeans" or "Shop the $88 Jeans."
- 4. Add "Ends tonight" near the price, since the offer expires 11/09 at 11:59 PM PST.
- 5. Cut or shrink the 30% block, or reframe it as "Also: 30% off our favorites." Two competing offers weaken both.
- 6. Add a star rating or review count next to the jeans.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Straight or barrel: both Way-High® styles drop to $88 until midnight PST.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Link error on the "Everlane" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M714HH8YEJJ68T0CKK3HG97T_0` produced `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...&bxid=...&cm_flow_i...'`.
  - The tracker's redirect appears to return a relative `Location` (`/pages/womens?...`) instead of an absolute URL.
  - Browsers and mail clients resolve relative redirects, so this is probably a limitation of the probe's redirect handling and not a user-facing break. It is unconfirmed.
