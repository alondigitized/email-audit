# New Sweaters Just Landed
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand sweater send with a strong hero. A model in a tan mock-neck sweater and matching skirt fills the top of the screen. "Always in Season" is overlaid, with a black SHOP NOW bar underneath. The structure is simple: one hero, two products, one "Shop All Sweaters" button, and a closing banner.
- The email has no concrete hook. The only offer language is "All at better prices" and "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE," with no number, no named price drop and no deadline. That is the same "better prices" beat as the 2026-10-19 "Cashmere Beyond Sweater Weather" send. Two more cashmere, pants and fleece sends followed on 10-20, so this feels like another entry in a steady daily drip. It's pleasant and easy to skim, but nothing in it pushes anyone to act today.
- Judgement: a competent 6. The email shows the product well but doesn't make a case for clicking now.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The tan sweater, neutral backdrop and full-width black SHOP NOW bar all sit above the fold.
- The theme is tight: sweaters only, matching the subject, with a clear seasonal fit for late October.
- The two product blocks carry useful fit copy. "Close-to-the-body fit… layered over a button-down" and "boxy cropped fit and chunky turtleneck" help a shopper picture the garment.
- The "Shop All Sweaters" button gives a clear path to browse after the two featured products.
- The layout is simple and uncluttered, and the footer navigation (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is easy to use.

## What's weak

- "Better prices" is stated twice but never quantified. There's no price, no percentage and no before/after, so the one news hook is wasted.
- Nothing creates urgency: no deadline, no stock cue and no member benefit.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW," six times in all, with no variation tied to the product. The product CTAs are small underlined text links that are easy to miss.
- The hero headline, "Always in Season," breaks into three short lines in white over a light tan sweater. It is legible but low-contrast, and it says nothing specific about sweaters.
- The two product images stack edge to edge with no gap. Neither product shows a price, color options or a reviews cue.
- The cadence is heavy. This is another daily send after "Fleece Forward," "Fall Pants Take Shape" and "Cashmere for Every Forecast" on 10-20, and it repeats the cashmere "new better prices" message from 10-19.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price news in the hero and the product blocks. Show the actual price next to each sweater ("Was $98, now $78") and say what changed. This single change would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Replace "Always in Season" with a headline that names the product and the news, such as "New sweaters, new lower prices." Use a darker text color or a text panel so it reads at a glance.
- 3. Add a credible reason to act now. Use a stock cue, a "new colors" count, or a short window on the price drop.
- 4. Make the product CTAs specific and button-styled. Use "Shop the Crew Cardigan" and "Shop the Boxy Turtleneck" instead of three identical "Shop Now" links.
- 5. Add social proof or a detail cue to each product, such as a star rating, "Organic cotton" or a color count.
- 6. Spread the sends out, and don't repeat the same "better prices" line from earlier in the week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New sweaters, now at lower prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 new sweater colors, all under $100`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Crew cardigans and chunky turtlenecks in organic cotton, at better prices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same quality, lower prices. See what's new in sweaters.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style template using a custom click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). Two probed tracked links, the header logo and the "Sign Up for Texts" link, failed because the redirect target is a relative path. The QA run reports a 38% pass rate with 2 failures and 3 warnings. Most of the warnings are header-capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect targets on tracked links.**
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5FJ7S8Z95A2ER2ZDD9N85ZQ_0` (text "Everlane") resolves to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`.
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5FJ7S8Z95A2ER2ZDD9N85ZQ_15` (text "Icon | Sign Up for Texts") resolves to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…`.
  - Both failed with `unknown url type`, meaning the destination has no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host. That would send users to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, which is probably a 404.
  - **Caveat:** the failure may come from how the QA probe handles relative redirects, not from the live redirect. Confirm with `curl -sI` on each link.
  - Both failing links are high-visibility: the header/logo link and the SMS signup link.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 25 material links were probed and 13 were skipped. The other links are unverified, so the 38% pass rate is not a full-email figure.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5FJ7S8Z95A2ER2ZDD9N85ZQ` is the open-tracking image. It is on the same domain as click tracking, so a single domain reputation or DNS issue would affect both.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** the QA flagged the `trk.send.everlane.com/o/…` open pixel. This is probably a 1×1 tracking image, so the practical impact is low. It should still carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Web font via `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this only affects the typeface, not layout.
