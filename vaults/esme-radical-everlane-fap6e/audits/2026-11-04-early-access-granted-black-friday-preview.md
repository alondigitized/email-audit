# Early Access Granted: Black Friday Preview
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, high-contrast Black Friday kickoff, and it is the first send in this stretch with a real offer. Everlane's last six audited sends (11/01 to 11/04) were all offer-free product stories scoring 6 to 7. Here the hero says "You've got early access to 30% off" in white type on black, with a SHOP EARLY ACCESS bar underneath. The eye goes straight to the offer.
- The body undercuts the hero. Below it are one line of copy and a six-item product grid with no names, prices, or individual links I can see. The offer's real constraints are in the grey fine print at the bottom: "select styles only," online only, valid until 11/09 at 11:59 PM PST, and no stacking. The deadline is the strongest urgency lever, and it is buried where nobody reads.
- Verdict: a solid, honest-looking early-access send that would convert better with the deadline in the hero and a visible product price. It sits above the recent run of no-offer emails, but it is not yet a standout.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in four words, "30% OFF", and frames it as early access. That is a clear, exclusive-feeling hook.
- The black hero with a full-width SHOP EARLY ACCESS bar is an unambiguous primary CTA, and the hierarchy is clear.
- The six-item grid mixes outerwear, a skirt, knitwear, denim and a blazer. It is on-brand, uncluttered, and fits the season.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- After a week of no-offer sends (for example "It's Sweater Season" on 11/04), this one finally gives a reason to act.

## What's weak

- The 11/09 deadline appears only in the fine print, so the time-bound urgency that could drive a click is hidden.
- "30% off select styles" is only clarified in small grey text. The hero implies a sitewide discount, which feels slightly less honest.
- The product grid has no names, prices, sale prices or per-item links. It reads as decoration and gives no reason to click a specific piece.
- Several body blocks add nothing: "Shop Women / Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers" repeats the hero CTA, and the Find a Store / Texts / Help Center row adds nothing for the offer.
- There is no loyalty or member signal, and the hero has no model or human element. It is a very cold, dark creative for a gift-season push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends 11/9", with a one-line "select styles" qualifier right beside the 30%. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add product names and "was / now" prices under each grid item, and link each one to its product page.
- 3. Cut the secondary nav and footer-style link blocks, or move them below one more product row.
- 4. Add a second CTA after the grid, such as "Shop all 30% off styles".
- 5. Use at least one model image to make the hero feel warmer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 30% off early access ends 11/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early access: 30% off before Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop select styles before everyone else. Ends 11/9 at 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Outerwear, knits and denim, 30% off, a week early.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, responsive HTML template that sends all links through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The automated QA run left most of it unverified: 12 links were probed but skipped, 3 returned HTTP 429, 5 went unprobed, and the header checks found nothing. The one hard FAIL is a relative redirect on the logo link, which is probably a probe artifact.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link: relative redirect target.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M6MC922CRGD1DF7016P1WJR5_0` ("Everlane Logo") resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The probe reported `unknown url type`.
  - The tracker appears to return a relative `Location` header with no scheme or host. Browsers resolve relative redirects, so real users are likely fine.
  - Some clients, link scanners and in-app webviews may not follow it. Confirm with `curl -sI` on the `_0` link and check the `Location` value.
- **[WARN] 429 on key CTAs.** `_1` (hero promo, "You've Got Early Access To 30% Off"), `_2` ("Shop Early Access") and `_3` (Re:Cycled Nylon Reversible Mid Liner) all returned 429. This is rate limiting by the tracker, not a broken link. Destinations are unverified.
- **[WARN] Incomplete coverage.** 5 links were unprobed after the 70s budget: `_6`, `_7`, `_9`, `_10` and `_23`. They are neither passing nor failing. Link health is only confirmed for the logo link, which failed.
- Every link uses an opaque tracker ID (`…_N`), so the final destinations can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text.** The `<img>` with `src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M6MC922CRGD1DF7016P1WJR5"` has no `alt`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip it.
- The `<title>` is empty (`<title> </title>`).
- The `<style>` block starts with `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)`. Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import`. The CSS font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this degrades acceptably.
- The mobile media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` at ≤480px. If `<h4>` is used for body or legal copy, this is below the usual legibility floor. I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- The `.hide` media-query rule appears twice in the stylesheet. This is harmless duplication.
- The HTML is truncated, so I can't audit content-image alt text, table roles or preheader markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the source provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check the sending domain's DNS records directly (`send.everlane.com` and `everlane.com`).
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical address fall outside the truncated source and the probed set, so I can't confirm them.
- From address `support@everlane.com` is a monitored-style address on the brand domain, but the tracking subdomain is `send.everlane.com`. Alignment depends on the DKIM `d=` value, which I couldn't see.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links carry Klaviyo's `_kx` parameter and `bxid`/`cm_flow_i…` identifiers. No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the QA output, but the URLs are truncated and the final destinations weren't resolved. UTM presence can't be confirmed or denied.
- The logo link lands on `/pages/womens`, not the site root. This is consistent with a women's-oriented send but is unverified for the other CTAs.
- Landing-page alignment with "Early Access / 30% off" can't be checked because the three CTA probes hit 429.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with throttling or retry/backoff against `trk.send.everlane.com`, and with a higher time budget. This should resolve `_1`, `_2`, `_3`, `_6`, `_7`, `_9`, `_10` and `_23`, and confirm final URLs and UTMs.
2. Check the `_0` redirect: confirm the `Location` header and have the ESP or tracker emit absolute URLs if it is relative.
3. Inspect raw headers at the receiving mailbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, `Authentication-Results`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and give the document a non-empty `<title>`.
5. Review the `h4` mobile font size (10px) if it carries meaningful text.
6. Capture the full, untruncated HTML in the QA pipeline so alt text, the unsubscribe link and the physical address can be audited.
