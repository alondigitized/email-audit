# New for October
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain new-arrivals catalog send. It has a 3x3 grid of flat-lay products in fall tones (oatmeal, burgundy, black, navy), one black "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" bar, and a cream "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" block. The render is clean and the grid is easy to scan. Nothing in it gives a reason to click now.
- The "better prices" block is the only commercial message. It has no number, no product, and no deadline, and its "SHOP NOW" is a small underlined link. The grid also has no prices, names, or per-item links that I can see. This is the fourth Everlane send in about three days. After "Nothing But Neutrals" and the two Everywhere Pant sends (10-03 "Obsessed. These pants are amazing!" and today's earlier "I want these pants in every shade."), this one feels like filler. The beige and burgundy pants and the knits look like a recap of those campaigns.
- Verdict: a competent but forgettable send. Everlane trades on editorial and UGC, and this has none of it.

## What's working

- The grid is evenly spaced on a soft grey field, so nothing competes and the products read at a glance.
- The palette is seasonally right for October: oatmeal, burgundy, black, navy.
- The primary CTA is a full-contrast black bar with a clear verb and noun.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images, overlap, or odd gaps.
- The "Same quality. Same materials. Same favorites." line gives a brand-consistent value message.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer. "Better prices" claims savings but names no amount, product, or price.
- The nine product tiles show no prices, names, or visible individual links, so there is no product detail to act on.
- The email has no models, UGC, or reviews. That drops the editorial voice that made the 10-02 sends ("How They're Wearing Everlane", 7/10) work.
- The second CTA, "SHOP NOW", is a tiny underlined link under the cream block and is easy to miss. Below it, the nav (Shop Women / Shop Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) duplicates the hero CTA.
- The cadence is heavy, and today's send adds no new beat beyond the Everywhere Pant and neutrals emails.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "better prices" with a specific, verifiable claim, such as "Our Corduroy Barrel Pant, now $78 (was $98)". That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Put prices and names on the grid tiles and link each one to its product page. Better still, cut the grid to 4–6 items.
- 3. Add one human element: a model shot or a customer quote, as in the 10-02 UGC send.
- 4. Make the second CTA a real button and make the offer's CTA name the category, for example "Shop Fall Pants".
- 5. Space out the sends. Fold this into a "what's new this week" digest instead of a standalone email.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Barrel pants, ribbed henleys and corduroy shirts. Same Everlane quality, new lower prices.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "New for October"

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built email (`_kx`/`bxid` params, `static-forms.klaviyo.com` font import) with a `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking redirector. The two QA failures come from the click-redirect step and are probably probe artifacts rather than confirmed broken links. Header-based checks are inconclusive because AgentMail doesn't preserve the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[Needs verification] Two tracked links fail in the probe.**
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M43SNR0X309BNZYQFSZZZR4R_0` ("Everlane Logo") fails with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - `…_19` ("Icon | Sign Up for Texts") fails with `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
  - Both errors show a path-only string with no scheme or host. That points to the redirector returning a relative `Location` header, or the QA probe failing to resolve one. Browsers resolve relative redirects correctly, so this alone does not prove the links are broken for recipients.
  - To confirm, run `curl -sI` against both URLs and check the `Location` header and final status. If it really is relative, set the redirector or Klaviyo link domain to emit absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` targets. The probe should also resolve relative redirects with `urljoin`.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 17 skipped as footer, utility or social links. The 38% pass rate is therefore not representative of the whole email.
- **Click-tracking parameters are intact** (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i`). All links go through `trk.send.everlane.com`, a first-party subdomain, which is good for deliverability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. It is minor but visible in the source.
- **Missing alt on the open pixel:** the flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M43SNR0X309BNZYQFSZZZR4R`. The `/o/` path indicates a tracking pixel, so the missing alt is low severity. An explicit `alt=""` would remove the warning and keep screen readers from announcing it.
- **Link styling:** the `<style>` block sets `a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover {color:#000; text-decoration:none}`. Links are therefore not distinguishable by color or underline unless a button or image wraps them. This is a WCAG 1.4.1 risk for any inline text links.
- **Mobile breakpoints:** `h4` drops to `10px` at max-width 480px, which is below legible size. This is visible in the CSS shown.
- **Outlook support:** present, via MSO conditionals, `OfficeDocumentSettings` and `mj-outlook-group-fix`. No issues found there.
- **Not assessable:** the HTML source was truncated, so image alt coverage and body markup beyond the `<style>` block could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery path. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Verify with a direct-to-Gmail seed test, or check DNS for `send.everlane.com`.
- **Sender address:** the From is `support@everlane.com`, a support-style mailbox on a promotional send. This is not a violation, but replies go to a support queue.
- **Footer checks:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source. The QA output says an unsubscribe link is always probed and reports no failure for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` are visible in the captured URLs. Attribution appears to rely on Klaviyo's `_kx` and `cm_flow_i`. The captured URLs are truncated, so UTMs may exist but could not be confirmed.
- Landing paths are `/pages/womens` and `/pages/sms-signup`. Neither could be verified because of the relative-redirect probe errors in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the two failing links.** Run `curl -sIL` on `…_0` and `…_19`. Fix the redirector to emit absolute URLs if it is returning relative `Location` headers.
2. **Fix the QA probe** to resolve relative redirects against the previous URL (`urljoin`), so it stops reporting false failures.
3. **Capture raw headers** (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a non-relayed copy to settle the compliance and authentication warnings.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the tracking pixel, and fill in `<title>`.
5. **Add visible underline or color** to any inline text links.
6. **Confirm UTM tagging** on the full, untruncated HTML.
7. **Raise `h4` mobile font size** above 10px.
