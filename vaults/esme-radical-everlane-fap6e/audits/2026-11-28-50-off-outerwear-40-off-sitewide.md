# 50% Off Outerwear & 40% Off Sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built Black Friday weekend send that is stale by the time it lands. The hero is a tight editorial crop of two models in coats and scarves. "50% OFF OUTERWEAR & WINTER ESSENTIALS" is large, and a black "TODAY ONLY" tag sits under it. A second panel stacks "40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" over an olive coat. The hierarchy is clear and the render has no bugs.
- The problem is repetition and credibility. This is the same outerwear creative and "TODAY ONLY" tag as the 11/27 sends "1 Day Only: 50% Off Outerwear & Accessories" and "Still Time: 50% Off Outerwear & More". A subscriber who has seen those three times already now sees "today only" again on the 28th. The footer says the offer runs through 12/02/2026, which contradicts the urgency tag.
- Judgement: good execution, tired idea. The subject does lead with both offers, which is its main strength.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge, high-contrast white type on a photo, so the eye lands on "50% OFF" first.
- Both offers appear in the subject and the body, so there is no surprise on open.
- The black "SHOP NOW" bars are unmistakable and repeat under each panel.
- The brand voice and editorial photography are consistent and premium, with a quiet one-line "Responsibly crafted for the colder days ahead."
- The layout is short and scannable: two offers, one brand-story block, then a simple nav.

## What's weak

- "TODAY ONLY" is not credible when the footer says the offer is valid until 12/02 and the same tag ran on 11/27.
- The 50% and 40% offers are stacked with equal visual weight, and "40% OFF EVERYTHING ELSE*" muddies the message.
- Both "SHOP NOW" CTAs are generic. They don't name outerwear, and they don't tell me which products I'll land on.
- There are no product callouts, prices, reviews, or loyalty or member benefits, so there is nothing to click on besides the hero.
- The "A Better Future for Cashmere" block is off-message and dilutes the sale. Its small, low-contrast text on a busy illustration is hard to read.
- The asterisk and fine print are tiny and grey. "Offer applies to select styles only" sits uneasily next to "everything".

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the urgency to match the real deadline. Replace "TODAY ONLY" with "Ends Dec 2" or "Cyber Monday ends soon", or add a countdown. This is the single biggest lift, because it restores trust and gives a reason to act now.
- 2. Make the 50% outerwear offer the single hero. Fold the 40% sitewide offer into a one-line banner rather than a second full-height panel.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Outerwear" and "Shop 40% Off" so the click has a destination.
- 4. Add a row of 3-4 priced best-selling coats or knits with ratings to give a second click path and some social proof.
- 5. Drop the cashmere brand-story block from a promo send, or move it below the nav.
- 6. Vary the hero creative from the 11/27 sends so the send doesn't look like a re-run.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Outerwear is still 50% off through Dec 2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your coat is waiting: 50% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off everything else. Cashmere, knits and tees included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Best-selling coats and layers at half price. Sale ends Dec 2.`

## Technical Audit: Everlane, "50% Off Outerwear & 40% Off Sitewide"

### 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email with branded first-party click tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. Both link probes that ran failed on relative redirect targets. The QA suite reported a 38% pass rate, and the header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML is truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8FX96VT904JT64KTTKPM6BQ_0` returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link:** `…/l/01M8FX96VT904JT64KTTKPM6BQ_8` returned `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…'`.
- **Likely cause:** Both errors come from the same place. The tracker's redirect `Location` is a scheme-less relative path (`/pages/womens…`), and the probe didn't resolve it against `everlane.com`. Browsers normally resolve a relative `Location`, so this is probably a probe limitation rather than a user-facing break. It is not confirmed, because the destinations were never fetched. Still, a relative `Location` from a click tracker is non-standard (RFC 7231 allows it, but some clients and scanners mishandle it).
- **Coverage gap:** Only 2 of 12 material links were probed, and 10 were skipped by design. The other 10 links are unverified.
- **[WARN] Missing alt text:** The open-tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8FX96VT904JT64KTTKPM6BQ` has no `alt`. This is low severity, since it is a tracking pixel.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Small mobile headings:** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important } }` sets h4 text to 10px on mobile. That is below common legibility guidance (about 14px or more) if h4 carries real content. I can't tell from the truncated source whether it does.
- **Link styling:** `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` makes inline text links look the same as surrounding text. This only matters where links are inline text rather than buttons.
- **Fallbacks:** The `@import` Klaviyo web font has Arial/Helvetica fallbacks. Outlook conditional comments (`mso`, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings`) are present. A `viewport` meta tag is present. No issues there.
- The rest of the HTML was truncated and can't be assessed.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) are visible in the available source. The tracked URLs carry Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`).

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe`:** The header wasn't found. The check itself says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed, for the same reason. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results`:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also attributed to the relay.
- **Verdict:** These three are unverified, not confirmed failures. Check them against the original message headers, not the relayed copy.
- **Footer not verifiable:** The physical postal address and visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source.
- **Sender:** The From address is `support@everlane.com`, a support-style mailbox used for promotional mail. That isn't a compliance violation, but it affects reply handling.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the captured link strings (`/pages/womens?_kx=…`, `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`). Attribution appears to rely on Klaviyo's `_kx`, `bxid` and `cm_flow_i…`. The strings are truncated, so UTM tagging may exist further along.
- **Landing alignment:** The probed links go to generic women's and men's pages. Neither probe completed, so I can't confirm that the "50% off outerwear / 40% off sitewide" promotion is visible on landing. I also can't confirm that any link goes to an outerwear collection.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Absolute redirects:** Make the tracker's redirect `Location` an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/…` URL, and re-run the two failed probes.
2. **Fetch the original headers:** Pull the raw message from the sending side and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
3. **Probe the skipped links:** Check the remaining 10 links, especially any outerwear CTA.
4. **Add UTM parameters:** Add explicit `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` values to the links if they are missing, so site analytics don't depend only on Klaviyo identifiers.
5. **Small fixes:** Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel, set a meaningful `<title>`, and check whether the 10px mobile h4 is used for readable content.
