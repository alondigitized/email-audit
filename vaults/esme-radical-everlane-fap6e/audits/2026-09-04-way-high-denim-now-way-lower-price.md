# Way-High Denim Now Way Lower Price
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different creative from the week-long "End of Summer Sale" olive/cream watercolor template Everlane's been running since 08/01 — a real visual reset. The mirrored twin-model hero with "Better Prices / Still Everlane" is a clean, confident concept, and it earns its subject line: this is specifically about denim price cuts, not a generic sitewide sale reheated again.
- The catch: there's no visible number anywhere. Every prior send in this history at least had a "20% off" or "70% off" figure; this one leans entirely on vibes ("Better Prices," "Only thing that changed is the price") with zero digits, a specific dollar figure, or even a "was $X now $Y." For a subject line promising "Way Lower Price," that's a real letdown once you open it — you scroll through three products with customer quotes and never learn what the new price actually is.
- Strong template, weak follow-through on the one thing the subject promised. Worth shipping as a design refresh, but the offer needs a number in the hero or product cards before it'll convert as well as it could.

## What's working

- Hero concept (mirrored models, "Better Prices / Still Everlane") is a clear break from the repeated End-of-Summer template seen 08/01–09/03.
- Subject line and hero copy are thematically aligned — both are about denim pricing, not a bait-and-switch to a broader sale.
- Product grid uses real customer quotes with named attribution (Kimberly K., Molly B., Alaina T.), which reads as credible social proof.
- Three distinct Way-High/Column styles shown with individual "Shop Now" links, not just one hero product.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout bugs; footer legal, nav links, and social icons all present.

## What's weak

- No actual price or percentage anywhere in the email — "way lower price" is asserted, never quantified.
- Product cards show no strikethrough/sale pricing under the product shots, so there's nothing to visually confirm the discount claim.
- Fine print at the bottom ("Offer valid until 09/08/2026... applies to select styles only") reveals exclusions that aren't flagged anywhere in the visible body — feels like it's burying the catch.
- CTA copy is generic "Shop Now" repeated five times instead of anything category- or offer-specific.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual price or percentage to the hero — even just "Now $XX" under "Better Prices" — since the subject line promises a number and the body never delivers one. This is the single biggest gap.
- 2. Show before/after or strikethrough pricing on each of the three product cards so the discount is visible where the click happens, not just implied.
- 3. Surface the "select styles only" restriction near the hero, not just in 6pt footer legal — avoids a bait-and-switch feel per the rubric.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy per product ("Shop Way-High 2.0," "Shop the Column Jean") instead of five identical "Shop Now" links.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Favorite Denim Just Got Cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same fit, same wash — new price, for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Way-High 2.0, Curve, and Column — all marked down.`
