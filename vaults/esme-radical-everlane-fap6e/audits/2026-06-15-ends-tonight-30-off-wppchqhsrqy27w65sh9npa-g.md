# Ends Tonight: 30% Off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's fifth consecutive send in the same 30% off promo cycle, and the only meaningful change is the subject line. "Ends Tonight: 30% Off" finally adds a legitimate urgency signal — something the previous four sends conspicuously lacked. The hero image, discount percentage, and "SHOP NOW" button are functionally identical to what landed yesterday and the three days before that.
- The urgency hook is the single upgrade. "Ends Tonight" is short, credible, and time-bounded — exactly the kind of signal that can lift opens from a fatigued list. But the body doesn't pay it off. After opening an "Ends Tonight" email, the subscriber hits the same hero shot, the same generic CTA, and then — inexplicably — a full brand ethics block about their supply chain. That sequence is: urgency → friction → restatement of urgency. The email is fighting itself.
- At this point in the cycle a countdown timer, a tight "here's what's still in stock" SKU edit, or a specific deadline hour would close harder than another pass of the same creative. Everlane has the right instinct on the subject; they didn't follow through in the body.

## What's working

- "Ends Tonight" is the first genuine urgency signal in this five-email run — short, specific, and credible
- The hero text ("30% OFF SUMMER STYLES") is large and immediately legible with clean contrast
- The four-category grid (Dresses, Shorts, Sandals, Tees) gives the subscriber real wayfinding below the fold
- Tagline "Better pieces at better prices. Just not for much longer." reinforces urgency in copy without feeling aggressive
- No render bugs; layout is clean and unbroken on this mobile-first render

## What's weak

- The brand ethics / supply-chain block completely destroys urgency momentum — wrong content for a 24-hour close
- "SHOP NOW" is the least specific CTA Everlane could have written; the four category buttons below are more useful but arrive too late
- No specific deadline hour visible anywhere ("midnight PST" converts better than "tonight")
- This hero image has now run five consecutive days — any engaged subscriber has seen this exact shot four times already
- Zero product-level specificity: no "here's what's moving fast," no inventory signal, no social proof

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the ethics block for a 3-4 SKU "moving fast" edit** — specific products with a low-stock or trending signal. "Ends Tonight" gets clicks when the subscriber believes something concrete will be gone.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline time** — "Ends at midnight PST" is definitive in a way "tonight" is not; it removes the timezone ambiguity that makes subscribers procrastinate.
- 3. **Elevate the category CTAs above the ethics section** — the Dresses/Shorts/Sandals/Tees grid is the most useful wayfinding in the email; it should appear immediately after the hero.
- 4. **Retire this hero shot** — five sends is too many. Fresh creative for a final-day push signals to the subscriber that this is genuinely the end, not another rotation.
- 5. **Kill the ethics section entirely for urgency closes** — Everlane's supply-chain credibility earns trust in editorial sends; pasting it into a 24-hour countdown dilutes both the urgency and the mission messaging.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Confirmed issue:** External font loaded via CSS `@import` inside `<style>`:
