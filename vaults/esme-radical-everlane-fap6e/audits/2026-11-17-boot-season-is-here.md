# Boot Season Is Here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a good category send. It's wrapped in a Black Friday frame that works against it. The hero is a model's lower half in black boots crossing a crosswalk, with a thin white "Boot Up" and a black SHOP NOW bar. The six-boot grid below it is the strongest part: the boots are clean and consistently shot, and the range runs from heeled booties to tall riding boots. The "From riding boots to booties. All in cleaner leather." line does more selling than the headline does.
- The 40% OFF EVERYTHING* banner appears twice, at the top and again above the footer. It competes with the boot story. Yesterday's "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" send already carried that offer at full volume. Here it's a bookend, and the subject doesn't mention it. A subscriber who opens on "Boot Season Is Here" finds a seasonal editorial that also happens to be on sale, and the discount never gets tied to the boots. The email is polished but undecided about whether it's selling boots or the sale.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand 6. Two SHOP NOW bars go to an unspecified destination, and no boot has a price or a link I can tell is separate. Everlane could win the click by showing a price anchor like it did with "The New $48 Cashmere Tee" on 11-15.

## What's working

- The 3x2 boot grid shows suede, leather, ankle and tall styles at a glance, so the shopper gets range without scrolling far.
- The "From riding boots to booties. All in cleaner leather." line is specific, on-brand and easy to scan.
- The Black Friday strip at the top makes the 40% offer visible immediately, and the fine print sits in the footer.
- The lifestyle shot of the model in a cream knit, black skirt and tall boots shows how to wear the boots, not just the product.
- The footer is tidy, with clear Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals and Best-Sellers links.

## What's weak

- The "Boot Up" hero has no offer, no price and no product detail. The headline is soft and the image crops out the model entirely.
- The 40% OFF EVERYTHING* banner appears twice, but nothing says it applies to boots. That's a missed link between offer and product.
- Both SHOP NOW bars are generic. They don't say "Shop Boots," and the grid items have no visible names or prices.
- The asterisk fine print is tiny and grey, and the 12/02 deadline appears only there, so there's no visible urgency.
- This is the third Everlane send on 11-17 or the day before with near-identical Black Friday framing, so cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the offer to the product. Retitle the hero "Boots, 40% Off" and put the deadline ("Through 12/2") next to it, so the discount is the first thing the eye reads.
- 2. Replace both generic SHOP NOW bars with "Shop Boots," and link each grid tile to its own product.
- 3. Add names and prices under each boot in the grid, something like "The Modern Boot, $198 → $119."
- 4. Remove the second Black Friday banner and use that space for reviews or a "bestseller" tag on one boot.
- 5. Move the headline text onto a flat area of the image so it reads more clearly than the thin white type does over the busy crosswalk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Boots, now 40% off through 12/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our leather boots just dropped 40%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Riding boots to booties, all in cleaner leather. 40% off ends 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Suede, leather, tall or ankle. Black Friday pricing on every pair.`
