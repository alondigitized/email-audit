# Sale Favorites Are Going Fast
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "End of Summer Sale" watercolor-hero template Everlane has run daily since 08/01 — tan-model-with-olive/cream headline split, black "SHOP NOW" bar, followed by a blue-toned "Under $100" secondary banner. The subject "Sale Favorites Are Going Fast" and the body copy ("Customer faves are selling out and sizes are getting limited") introduce a scarcity angle that the visual template doesn't reinforce at all — no low-stock tags, no "almost gone" badges on any product, no countdown. It's a copy change bolted onto a creative that's now run essentially unchanged for five-plus weeks.
- The offer itself (extra 20% off already-discounted styles, up to 70% off) is clear and the layout is clean, but there's zero product-level evidence of "favorites going fast" — no actual product grid, no specific items, just the hero and a generic $100-and-under banner. For a subscriber who's seen this exact creative six times this month, the scarcity subject line reads as recycled urgency rather than real signal.
- Verdict: functional but fatigued. The subject over-promises specificity the body doesn't deliver, and the creative is showing real wear.

## What's working

- Offer is crystal clear: extra 20% off sale styles already up to 70% off, with one unambiguous black "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Clean visual hierarchy — eye goes hero image → headline split → CTA bar in a logical top-down flow.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlap.
- Secondary "Under $100" banner adds a second, more specific entry point (Shop Women / Shop Men).

## What's weak

- Subject promises "favorites going fast" / scarcity, but nothing in the render shows actual products, stock levels, or bestseller callouts to back it up — it's an empty urgency claim.
- This is the identical hero/CTA template used in the 09/05 and 09/04 sends (tan-dress model, olive/cream split, black bar) — sixth near-identical send since 08/01.
- No actual product grid or specific items shown, despite the "favorites" framing implying named products.
- No loyalty/member pricing distinction shown for repeat subscribers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic hero with an actual product grid of the "favorites" referenced in the subject — show 4-6 specific selling-out items with sizes noted, since that's the entire premise of this send.
- 2. Add a visible scarcity signal on the creative itself (e.g., "Only a few sizes left" tag on products) rather than relying on subject-line copy alone to carry urgency.
- 3. Retire or meaningfully refresh the hero image — it's been running since 08/01 and open/click fatigue is a real risk at this point.
- 4. Tighten the mismatch between subject and hero: hero still says generic "EXTRA 20% OFF SALE STYLES," not "favorites" or "selling fast."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Low Stock Alert: Extra 20% Off Before They're Gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes are limited on these best-sellers — grab yours before checkout.`
