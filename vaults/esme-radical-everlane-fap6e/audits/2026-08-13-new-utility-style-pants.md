# New Utility Style Pants
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane back in its default single-category product-story mode — close-crop hero shot, two product cards, a soft-sell price footnote — the same structural playbook it ran on "The Details Make the Tee" (8/7) and "New Pants, Better Fabrications" (8/6). This time it's utility pants, and it's actually a clean, well-shot execution: crisp macro hero of the waistband, two on-model product cards with clear names and one-line descriptions, and a tidy "Better Prices, Still Everlane" reinforcement banner that ties back to the price-drop campaign running 8/12-8/13.
- The problem is there's no offer. "New Utility Style Pants" is pure product-launch copy with zero urgency, no percentage off, no price call-out on the products themselves — just three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons. Given this brand just spent two straight days leading with price ("From Us At Everlane" 8/12, "We Lowered Our Prices" 8/13), pairing this pants drop with an actual number would've been the obvious move and it's missing.
- Solid, on-brand execution of a beat this brand has now run at least six times in two weeks. It'll perform fine with existing customers already browsing pants, but it does nothing to convert someone on the fence — no urgency, no specific price, no differentiation from the last five product-story sends.

## What's working

- Hero image is sharp and on-message — the waistband macro shot with "New Utility" overlay text clearly communicates the workwear positioning.
- Two distinct products shown with clear names ("Utility Twill Barrel Pant," "Utility Seamed Wide-Leg Pant") and differentiated one-line descriptions.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text, no broken images, no placeholder content.
- Sustainability callout (organic cotton) is a legitimate value-add for a values-driven customer, not filler.
- "Better Prices, Still Everlane" banner smartly reinforces the ongoing price-cut narrative without being a full repeat of the last two sends.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere in the body — three "SHOP NOW" CTAs but zero pricing, discount, or urgency signal.
- Subject line is generic product-launch copy with no hook beyond "new."
- This is the same product-story template run at least five times in the prior two weeks — creative fatigue risk for anyone opening consecutive Everlane emails.
- No social proof or fit/sizing detail visible for either pant, despite fit being the #1 hesitation point for denim/pant purchases online.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual price or intro-offer callout to the hero or product cards — given the brand is mid price-drop campaign, a "$XX now $YY" on at least one product would connect this send to that momentum instead of floating separately.
- 2. Differentiate the CTA copy — "Shop the Barrel Pant" / "Shop the Wide-Leg" instead of three generic "SHOP NOW" repeats.
- 3. Add a one-line fit note under each product ("Runs true to size" / "Model wears a 27") to reduce the biggest purchase-hesitation point for pants.
- 4. Vary the subject line to earn a click rather than restate the hero copy verbatim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Workwear, Reworked for Fall`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Pant That Started as a Uniform`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Organic cotton twill, two new fits.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Barrel or wide-leg — pick your utility pant.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built HTML email (MJML/Klaviyo templating) with generally sound responsive markup, but link-tracking redirects are broken for at least two CTAs and delivery/compliance headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken redirect — "Icon | Sign Up for Texts"**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01KZXWZ7KQ2DPD73RP28CHY8GH_17` fails to resolve to a valid destination (`unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=...'`). The Klaviyo tracked-link wrapper is pointing at a malformed/incomplete relative path.
- **Broken redirect — "Everlane Logo"**: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01KZXWZ7KQ2DPD73RP28CHY8GH_0` has the same failure mode (`unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=...'`), meaning the header logo (a high-click element on every send) does not resolve.
- Both failing links carry Klaviyo tracking params (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i...`) appended to a relative `/pages/...` path rather than a fully-qualified `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` URL — consistent with the destination URL being stored/exported without its domain before being wrapped by the Klaviyo click-tracker.
- 12 of 27 material links were probed (15 lower-priority footer/utility/social links skipped by design); no other broken redirects reported among the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text**: tracking/open pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01KZXWZ7KQ2DPD73RP28CHY8GH` has no `alt` attribute. This is the open-tracking pixel, not content, so it's low severity — recommend `alt=""` to keep it out of the accessibility tree cleanly rather than leaving it unset.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`, `<!--[if lte mso 11]>`) and `.mj-outlook-group-fix` are present, indicating standard Klaviyo/MJML Outlook fallback handling — no issues found here.
- Responsive breakpoints (`@media max-width: 480px`) are defined for headings, tables, and stacking columns — no issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{ }}`, `*|...|*`) or broken personalization blocks found in the visible source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found**: could not be confirmed as present via the AgentMail relay — if genuinely absent, this is a CAN-SPAM/mailbox-provider risk (Gmail/Yahoo require it for bulk senders). Cannot confirm from HTML body alone since this is a header-level check; flagged as unverified rather than confirmed-absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)**: one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed for the same relay-visibility reason.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracked links use Klaviyo's `_kx`/`bxid`/`cm_flow_i` parameters rather than standard `utm_*` params — this is Klaviyo's native attribution scheme, not a defect, but means GA/other UTM-based attribution won't capture these clicks unless Klaviyo UTM tracking is separately enabled in the campaign settings.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (e.g. `/pages/womens`, `/pages/sms-signup`) because both destinations are the ones failing to resolve in the tracked redirects (see Section 2) — this blocks verifying whether the eventual landing pages match the email's content/CTAs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the two broken tracked links (`_17` sign-up-for-texts icon, `_0` header logo) — confirm the underlying destination URLs are fully-qualified (`https://www.everlane.com/...`) before being passed to the Klaviyo link wrapper.
2. Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel to resolve the accessibility warning cleanly.
3. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers via raw SMTP/MTA capture rather than the AgentMail relay, since the relay may simply be stripping/not surfacing them rather than them being absent at send time.
