# Today Only: An Extra 10% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Everlane send of the day, and it makes the weakest case for opening it. The subject promises "An Extra 10% Off", but nothing in the render says so. The hero shows two models in a quarter-zip knit and a puffer, with a "Today Only" overlay in thin, low-contrast type. It is nearly invisible against the darkened photo. The 10% figure never appears in the body, so the reader has to remember the subject line.
- Below the hero it is a clean, standard Everlane layout. A "Before the event ends tomorrow" line follows, then a beige "Better prices, still Everlane" band and a 3x3 grid of unlabeled neutral products. The grid has no prices, names or discount badges. It looks tidy but gives a reader little reason to click.
- My judgement is a mid-pack send. It follows "We Added An Extra 10% Off" from earlier today, and it sits on top of a week of 30% Friends & Family emails. A subscriber sees urgency and escalation fatigue, not a new offer.

## What's working

- The "Today Only" subject is a credible, time-bounded hook, and the fine print confirms a 10/13 11:59 PM PST deadline.
- The hero imagery is on-season: knitwear, outerwear and layering for mid-October.
- The black full-width "SHOP NOW" bars are unambiguous, and there are two of them (after the hero and after the grid).
- The grid is visually calm and consistent, with a tonal neutral palette that reads as Everlane.
- The footer is clean, with simple navigation (Women, Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) and no broken images.

## What's weak

- "Today Only" is barely legible over the hero photo, and no "10%" appears anywhere in the render. The offer is invisible.
- The copy contradicts itself. The subject says "Today Only", but the body says "Before the event ends tomorrow."
- The grid products have no prices, names, or sale markers, so the extra 10% has nothing to attach to.
- Both CTAs say "SHOP NOW", and the hero CTA doesn't say which product or category it opens.
- The offer is hedged by fine print: "select styles only", "online only", "cannot be combined with any other offer". "Even more savings on our best styles" is vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero as large, high-contrast text, for example "Extra 10% Off. Today Only. Ends midnight PST." Don't overlay faint text on a darkened photo.
- 2. Pick one deadline. Either "Today Only" or "ends tomorrow", not both.
- 3. Add product names and "was/now" prices to the grid, or at least a "Extra 10% Off" badge on each tile.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs such as "Shop Sweaters & Jackets" or "Get the Extra 10%".
- 5. Say in the body which styles qualify, and state the exclusions once, plainly.
- 6. Cut the cadence. This is the fourth near-identical promo in two days, so consolidate the escalation into one email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: extra 10% off, ends midnight PST`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 10% off expires tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Knits, jackets and denim, now with an extra 10% off. Ends 11:59 PM PST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stacks on the Friends & Family event. Last call.`
