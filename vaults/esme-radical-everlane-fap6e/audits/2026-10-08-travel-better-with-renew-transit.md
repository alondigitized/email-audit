# Travel Better With ReNew Transit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a product-led travel-bag send for Everlane's ReNew Transit line. It has a clean three-product layout: Backpack, Catch-all Case and Weekender. The "Friends & Family 30% Off Sitewide & Storewide" banner sits at the top. The product story is good. Each block names a practical detail: the 15" laptop sleeve, five interior pockets, luggage pass-through. The recycled-plastic-bottle line gives a reason to care.
- The offer is muddled, though. The banner says 30% off, but the Backpack copy says "Previously $118, now $98", which is about 17% off. The reader has to guess whether 30% comes off at checkout. The footer says the offer is valid until 10/13 and applies to select styles only. That is the only deadline, and it's buried in the fine print. The subject and hero say nothing about price or urgency.
- It also lands as the fourth 30% Friends & Family send in four days, after "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6), "30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family" (10/7) and "30% Off Online & In Stores" (10/8). Against those, a travel-bag theme is a welcome change of subject, but it's a softer sell than "30% Off Better Sweaters". Judgement: a decent, well-built category email whose offer and urgency are underplayed.

## What's working

- The hero lifestyle shot is strong, with a model carrying the backpack, and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar sits directly under it.
- Each product block has a clear name and a functional benefit (laptop sleeve, five pockets, luggage pass-through, water bottle holders).
- The 30% banner at the top gives a visible offer, with Women and Men links in the same banner.
- The layout is consistent and easy to scan: image, name, one-line copy, SHOP NOW.
- The sustainability line ("Made from recycled plastic bottles") adds a brand-values hook.

## What's weak

- The "Take Transit" overlay on the hero is small, thin white text over a light image, so it's hard to read.
- 30% off in the banner clashes with "Previously $118, now $98" in the Backpack copy, which undercuts the headline offer.
- There's no deadline in the main body. The 10/13 date appears only in the grey footer fine print.
- The product CTAs are all a generic "SHOP NOW" text link, and the Catch-all Case and Weekender show no prices.
- There are no reviews or social proof, even though a previous send, "What They're Saying About Our Sweaters" (10/7), used them well.
- It's the fourth near-identical 30% banner in four days, so the banner reads as wallpaper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear offer in the hero: "30% off ReNew Transit through 10/13" in bold type. Fix the price copy so the sale price reflects the discount, or say "30% off at checkout".
- 2. Make the hero overlay larger and higher-contrast, or move the text onto the black CTA bar.
- 3. Show a price for all three products, with the sale price next to the original.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific copy: "Shop the Backpack", "Shop the Catch-all Case", "Shop the Weekender".
- 5. Add a one-line review or star rating under the Backpack, the likely bestseller.
- 6. Add a short urgency line under the hero, "Ends 10/13", so the deadline isn't only in the footer.

- Links are wrapped in a click-tracking redirect domain, so the final destinations can't be checked from the source I have.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is blank, so the document has no accessible title.
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>`). Screen readers can't pick the right language.
- The global rule `a:link, a:visited, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes underlines and color differences from all links. Inline text links are then distinguishable only by context.
- The mobile rule `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` at max-width 480px is below common legibility minimums.
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in several clients (Gmail, Outlook). The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is graceful.
- The MSO conditionals (`OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`) and the dark-mode hooks (`[data-ogsc] .ev-clay-dark`) are present and well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated body can't be checked.

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found, so one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so check the raw headers.

## 7. Recommendations
