# Black Friday: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a text-only Black Friday announcement. A dark charcoal panel carries a short brand-voice paragraph, then "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in large white type, and a black SHOP BLACK FRIDAY bar at the bottom. The offer is the first thing the eye lands on, and the layout renders cleanly. But it is the third near-identical Black Friday send in about 48 hours: "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" on 11/17, "The Best Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" earlier today, and now this subject again.
- The creative has no product, no model and no category. It sells the discount and nothing else, so it asks the reader to take the brand's word that it's worth clicking. The copy ("With so many brands asking for your attention… we felt you deserved a little more in return") is a good, honest-sounding hook. The asterisk, the "*Exclusions Apply" line, and a deadline (12/02/2026) shown only in grey footer fine print all undercut it.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand but repetitive send. It's clear and uncluttered, but it has little reason to be opened again by someone who got the other two.

## What's working

- "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is huge, white on dark charcoal, and impossible to miss.
- The intro copy acknowledges inbox fatigue and frames the sale as a reward, which fits Everlane's "better prices" positioning.
- One CTA, a full-width black bar, with high contrast and a clear verb.
- The render is clean, with no broken images and nothing overlapping. The footer nav (Shop Women / Men / New Arrivals / Best-Sellers) gives a clear next step by category.

## What's weak

- There are no products or models anywhere, so nothing signals what is 40% off or draws a click.
- The deadline (12/02/2026) appears only in tiny grey footer text, so the email has no visible urgency.
- "Exclusions Apply" sits under the CTA with no hint of what is excluded. That muddies "everything", and it is the kind of language that feels bait-and-switch.
- This is the third near-identical Black Friday email in two days, including a repeat of the 11/17 subject line.
- The hero is mostly empty dark space, with a lot of text and a tiny CTA relative to the panel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add product imagery. Put 2–4 hero items (cashmere sweater, boots) with prices and sale prices under the headline, so the discount is tied to something people want.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Dec 2") and make the exclusions line specific, or drop "everything".
- 3. Vary the creative and send logic. If this is the same offer as 11/17, suppress it for people who already opened or clicked, or escalate the offer (extra 10% for members, free shipping).
- 4. Make the CTA category-aware ("Shop Women's Sale" / "Shop Men's Sale") or use split buttons.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday cashmere, 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best-sellers, boots and sweaters, all 40% off through Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Better prices all season. Now 40% better. Exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style template with click and open tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The two QA "FAIL" results come from relative redirect targets that the probe could not resolve, so they are probably a tooling limit rather than a user-facing break. The compliance and authentication warnings reflect headers the relay did not capture, not confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures:** both tracked links redirect to a **relative** `Location` (path only, no host), and the probe raised `unknown url type`.
  - "Shop Men" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7QRTBDC02NZRBC9GZB250E0_4`, redirecting to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`
  - "Everlane" (logo) → `…_0`, redirecting to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`
- **Likely cause:** relative `Location` headers are valid under RFC 7231, and browsers resolve them against the tracker host. If the tracker host is not `everlane.com`, the relative path would resolve against `trk.send.everlane.com` and could 404. That was not verified, so this is **unconfirmed**. The probe needs a re-run with relative-URL resolution before these two count as real failures.
- **Pass rate:** the 38% figure is inflated by these two probe errors and by 6 of 12 links being skipped. It is not a reliable measure of link health.
- **Logo link target:** the logo ("Everlane") points to `/pages/womens`, not the site root. This is a minor oddity, not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** `<img src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7QRTBDC02NZRBC9GZB250E0">` is the open-tracking pixel (`/o/` path). It should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. This is low severity.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is whitespace only.
- **No language attribute:** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` has no `lang`, which screen readers use to choose pronunciation.
- **Link styling:** `a:link, a:visited, a:hover, a:active { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` removes all link affordance. Any inline text link is then indistinguishable from body text.
- **Mobile text size:** `@media (max-width:480px) { h4 { font-size:10px !important } }` is below the usual 11–12px minimum for legibility.
- **Web fonts:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe support are **unverifiable**, not failed.
- **Alignment:** the From domain (`everlane.com`) and tracking domain (`send.everlane.com`) share an organizational domain, which is consistent with aligned DKIM and SPF.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion and were not evidenced in the QA output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking parameters:** the observed links carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the captured redirect targets, and the parameter strings are truncated in the QA output. UTM coverage is **unconfirmed**.
- **Landing targets:** the visible targets are a men's collection and a women's page. The subject promises "40% Off Everything", and neither target could be checked for that offer because the probe failed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the two failed probes with relative `Location` resolution against the final host, and confirm both land with HTTP 200.
2. Get the raw message headers (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and a non-empty `<title>`.
4. Restore underlines on inline text links, and raise the mobile `h4` size to 12px or more.
5. Confirm that UTM parameters are appended to the final landing URLs, and review the full untruncated HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and address.
