# A Classic Now 30% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-product cashmere send. It is the seventh 30% Friends & Family email in about a week. The hero is a burgundy crew on a model, with "Can't Go Wrong With A Classic" overlaid and a black "SHOP NOW" bar underneath. The "$148" price and the "everyone loves (and a few have tried to dupe)" line give it more personality and substance than most of the run.
- It also overlaps heavily with yesterday's "30% Off Cashmere Worth Keeping" (10/10). Both push cashmere crews at the same discount. A subscriber who got both sees the same sweater twice in a day, and the novelty is gone. The offer banner at the top is the only place the 30% appears, and it is a generic sitewide strip. The hero never says "30% off", and the price shown is $148, with no original price to anchor the saving.
- Overall it is well executed but a repeat. It is on-brand and easy to read, but it is not differentiated from the previous five or six sends.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. A single model, a rich burgundy sweater and a high-contrast "SHOP NOW" bar make the focal point obvious.
- The "$148" price in the body copy is concrete, and "priced better" frames the value.
- The "dupe" line has real brand voice, and it makes a specific product claim instead of generic hype.
- "The Fit" and "The Fabric" sections give sizing and material details, which a considered cashmere buyer wants. The GRS-certified recycled cashmere line is a credible sustainability point.
- The layout is tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the footer carries the offer end date.

## What's weak

- It repeats the cashmere push from earlier today, and it is the latest of a six-day run of near-identical 30% sends.
- The offer is not tied to the product. The 30% sits only in the top banner, and $148 has no struck-through original price.
- The hero text "Can't Go Wrong With A Classic" is thin white type over a busy sweater and is low contrast.
- There are three "SHOP NOW" CTAs with the same copy. Because they are not product-specific, it is unclear what each leads to.
- The deadline is only in the fine print (10/13 at 11:59 PM PST). The body has no urgency cue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer and price in the hero, for example "Slim Cashmere Crew, $148 (was $212)". The hero is the only thing most readers see, and it should carry the deal.
- 2. Differentiate from the other cashmere sends. Either consolidate them into one email or give this one a unique angle, such as the dupe story or a colorway or size-availability angle.
- 3. Replace the three identical CTAs with specific ones: "Shop the Slim Crew", "See the Fit", "Shop Cashmere Colors".
- 4. Surface the deadline near the top banner, for example "Ends Monday 10/13".
- 5. Increase the hero text weight and contrast, or add a subtle gradient behind it.
