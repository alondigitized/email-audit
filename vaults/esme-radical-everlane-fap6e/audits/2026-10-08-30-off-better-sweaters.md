# 30% Off Better Sweaters
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Everlane 30% send in as many days, and the cleanest sweater-specific version of it. The subject names the offer and the category. The hero is a model in a taupe half-zip with "Friends & Family / 30% Off Sitewide, Including Sweaters" overlaid. A black SHOP NOW bar sits right under it. The 3x3 grid of cream and oatmeal knits reinforces the category and ends with a second SHOP NOW bar.
- The execution is weaker than the strategy. The offer text in the hero is small, white, and sits on a busy grey wall, so it doesn't read as the headline. The grid has no product names, prices, or reviews, so nothing gives a reader a reason to pick one sweater over another. Compared with 10/7's "What They're Saying About Our Sweaters" (7/10), which had named products and customer quotes, this is thinner. It's also a repeat after "30% Off Online & In Stores" (10/8), "30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family" (10/7) and "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6). I'd call it competent but redundant.

## What's working

- The subject pairs the offer with the category in 23 characters, so it's easy to scan on mobile.
- The hero is on-season and on-category: a chunky knit half-zip, with "Including Sweaters" tying it to the subject.
- The black full-width SHOP NOW bars are unmistakable and appear twice, once after the hero and once after the grid.
- The "BETTER PRICES, STILL EVERLANE" banner addresses the quality-for-price question directly. The line above it, "Soft cashmere. Airy alpaca.", adds material cues.
- The maroon "Extra 20% off markdowns" block adds a second price lever with separate Women's and Men's sale links.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images or overlaps, and the footer and fine print are tidy.

## What's weak

- The "30% Off Sitewide" text in the hero is small and low-contrast over the grey wall and sweater. The offer isn't the first thing the eye lands on.
- The 3x3 grid has no product names, prices, sale prices, or reviews. It's a wall of beige knits with no hook.
- The offer has no visible deadline in the body. The expiry ("until 10/13/2026 at 11:59 PM PST") is only in the grey fine print, so there's no real urgency.
- "Sitewide" is contradicted by "Offer applies to select styles only" in the footer, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- Every CTA is "SHOP NOW" or a variant, so none names a product or a benefit.
- Cadence is a problem. This is the fourth near-identical 30% email in four days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Enlarge the offer and fix its contrast. Put "30% OFF SWEATERS" in a solid band or a large dark-on-light overlay so it's the clear headline.
- 2. Label the grid with product name, original price and sale price (for example, "Cashmere Crew ~~$145~~ $101"), and add star ratings. Link each tile to its own product page.
- 3. Put the deadline in the body, for example "Ends Monday 10/13", and add a countdown or an urgency strip.
- 4. Use specific CTAs such as "Shop cashmere sweaters" and "Shop the half-zip", not "SHOP NOW" three times.
- 5. Fold this into one stronger, differentiated send instead of a fourth 30% blast. The 10/7 social-proof angle worked better.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cashmere, now 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sweater weather: 30% off through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cashmere, alpaca & merino. Friends & Family ends 10/13.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best-reviewed knits, plus extra 20% off markdowns.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup) has one confirmed link failure. The logo's tracked link redirects to a relative path that the QA prober couldn't follow. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as real defects. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and unsubscribe markup weren't reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link redirect.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4E3EGC44Q2QKJJXD15D2XB7_0` (text "Everlane Logo") returned `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect target has no scheme or host (it starts with `/pages/womens`), so the probe couldn't follow it.
  - A browser would resolve that relative `Location` against the tracking host. The click would then land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens…` instead of `everlane.com`, which probably 404s.
  - I haven't confirmed that browser behavior. It needs a manual `curl -I` on the link.
  - The `_0` suffix marks this as the first link in the email, and it's the header logo, so most opens would see it.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 33 links were probed and 21 were skipped, so the rest are unverified. The overall pass rate was 43%.
- **Tracking pixel.** The open pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4E3EGC44Q2QKJJXD15D2XB7` is present, as expected for Klaviyo.
