# Air Alpaca Is Back
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product launch for a returning style. The hero is a model in a sheer blush alpaca sweater with "Back In Air Alpaca" overlaid on it, and a black SHOP AIR ALPACA bar sits right under it. The four-product grid is easy to scan and has a good fall-layering story. It reads as a proper merchandising email, a clear step up from the bare plain-text "Extra 20% Off New Markdowns" send earlier today.
- The weakness is that the email has no offer in its main content. The only price incentive is "Extra 20% off markdowns" in a brown band near the bottom, and that applies to sale items, not to the Air Alpaca styles being promoted. The product tiles show no prices, no sizes or colors, and no reviews. The ethical-sourcing block also takes up a lot of space for a send that is supposed to drive clicks.
- Overall it's a solid 6: pretty and clear, but it works as a mood-and-product drop, not a conversion email. It's better than the plain-text sends, but the offer is mismatched with the hero.

## What's working

- The hero is strong and tactile. The sheer alpaca knit is visible, and "Back In Air Alpaca" names the product at once.
- The black SHOP AIR ALPACA bar directly under the hero is an unmistakable primary CTA.
- Each of the four products has a one-line benefit, such as "The layer that barely feels like one."
- The visual hierarchy is clean, with generous whitespace and consistent SHOP NOW links.
- The email fits the season. Lightweight alpaca knits suit mid-October layering, and the send is not stale promo language.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. "Extra 20% off markdowns" only appears at the bottom, and it doesn't apply to the featured alpaca styles.
- No prices appear on any product tile, so there is no reason to click for anyone price-sensitive.
- The product tiles use thin, underlined text links instead of buttons. They look weak next to the black buttons elsewhere in the email.
- The Responsible Wool Standard block adds a second large grey panel plus a READ MORE button. It competes with the product CTAs and pushes the offer further down.
- No social proof, such as reviews or "best seller" cues, appears anywhere. The color range is also narrow, mostly blush and rose with one maroon and one brown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% offer up, directly under the hero, and clarify whether it applies to Air Alpaca. If it doesn't, say so plainly rather than leaving it ambiguous in the bottom band.
- 2. Add prices, plus a rating or "Back in stock" cue, to each product tile. The subject says "back", so lean into the restock urgency.
- 3. Make the product CTAs buttons or full-tile links, and shorten the sourcing block to one line with a link.
- 4. Add colors or size availability, such as "5 colors, XS–XL", so the click has a reason.
