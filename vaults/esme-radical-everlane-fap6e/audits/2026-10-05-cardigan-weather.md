- There is no price, offer or deadline anywhere, so nothing creates urgency.

- There is no social proof. Everlane used review quotes on 10-03 and 10-04, and a reviewer line would help here.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 `[FAIL]` link errors (likely false positives):** all three have the form `unknown url type: '/pages/...'`.
  - "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" → `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M46X9BYZFXVGT5P0YY17HKZE_12` resolved to `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`

  - "Shop Men" → `…_8` resolved to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…`
- The tracker returns a relative `Location` header, which RFC 7231 allows and browsers resolve against the request URL. The probe's HTTP client failed to join it to the host. The wording of the error points to a probe limitation, not a broken link. I can't confirm the final destinations return 200.
- **Unverified redirect targets:** the probe never resolved the final host and status for these links. They should be re-probed with relative-`Location` handling. Until then, the links are unverified rather than known-good or known-broken.
- **Probe coverage:** only 2 of 12 material links were probed (10 skipped). Coverage is too low to say the remaining links work.
- **Tracking:** links use a Klaviyo click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`) with `_kx` and `bxid` parameters. This is standard and shows no defect.
