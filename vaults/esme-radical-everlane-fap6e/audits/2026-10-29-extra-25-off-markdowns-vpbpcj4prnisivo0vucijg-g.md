# Extra 25% Off Markdowns
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the clearest Everlane send in the recent run. The hero says "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS / ALREADY UP TO 70% OFF" over a model in a brown overshirt, and a black SHOP WOMEN'S SALE bar sits right under it. The offer is concrete and sits where the eye lands first. Compare it with the 2026-10-28 "New Markdowns Just Added" send, which mentioned markdowns but gave no number. This one fixes that.
- Below the hero, a four-tile grid links to sweaters, outerwear, tops and denim. The grid is tidy but shows no prices, no sale badges and no before/after numbers. A shopper sees "70% off" in the hero and nothing that proves it on a single product. The beige "Better prices, still Everlane" block repeats the pitch with a weak underlined SHOP NOW.
- Overall it's a solid, honest markdown email held back by missing product-level proof and a fine-print deadline. The "until 11/01/2026" date is buried in the footer instead of the hero. It lands in the upper-middle range for a promotional send.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Extra 25% off markdowns, already up to 70% off."
- The black full-width SHOP WOMEN'S SALE bar is an unambiguous primary CTA.
- The "Shop the deepest discounts before your size is gone" line adds believable scarcity without shouting.
- The 2x2 category grid (sweaters, outerwear, tops, denim) lets a shopper jump straight to a category.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the Everlane brand voice is consistent.

## What's weak

- None of the grid tiles shows a price, a percent off or a sale tag, so the discount is never shown on a product.
- The deadline (11/01/2026, 11:59 PM PST) appears only in the tiny gray footer. The body has no urgency beyond "before your size is gone."
- The "Better prices, still Everlane" block is filler. Its small underlined SHOP NOW link is far weaker than the hero button.
- The hero overlay text is white on a light, busy part of the photo. "EXTRA 25% OFF MARKDOWNS" is thin and a bit hard to read.
- The footer nav and the Find a Store / Texts / Help Center row add clutter below the offer. The product grid is women's only, and the sale is not framed for men.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put real sale prices on the grid tiles, for example "Was $98, now $52 with extra 25% off." This single change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Move the deadline into the hero or the line under it, such as "Ends Sunday 11/1." Credible urgency would help both opens and clicks.
- 3. Darken the hero image or add a scrim behind the offer text, and make the "25% off" line bolder.
- 4. Replace the beige block with a best-seller or review callout, or cut it. Add a "Shop Men's Sale" link if men's markdowns are included.
- 5. Make the grid tiles link to specific sale collections, and show a rating on at least one item.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale styles up to 70% off, then 25% more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sweaters, outerwear and denim, now with an extra 25% off. Sizes are going fast.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email sent from `support@everlane.com` with click tracking on `trk.send.everlane.com`. The automated QA pass rate is 43%, mainly because one tracked link ("Shop Men") failed to resolve and the header checks could not be confirmed. The HTML is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and address markup were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **"Shop Men" failed the probe.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M645DN772J4Y7QSVSSX6R8WE_9` errored with `unknown url type: '/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…'`.
  - The tracker's redirect target is a relative path with no scheme or host.
  - Relative `Location` headers are valid under RFC 7231, and browsers resolve them. This is probably a limitation of the probing client, but it is unconfirmed.
  - Either way, the link's final destination was never verified.
- **Coverage is thin.** Only 1 of 12 material links was probed, and the other 11 were skipped, so the remaining destinations are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel has no `alt`.** `<img src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M645DN772J4Y7QSVSSX6R8WE">` is the open-tracking pixel. This is low severity, since it is not content.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title> </title>` is empty.
- The `<html>` tag has only `xmlns` attributes and no `lang`, so screen readers get no language hint.
- The tracking pixel has no `alt` (see above).
- The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` custom font has Arial/Helvetica fallbacks in the font stacks. No issue.
- Outlook conditionals (`mso`, `OfficeDocumentSettings`) and the viewport meta are present. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them. I can't tell whether Everlane sent them.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical address could not be assessed because the source is truncated. QA reports that an unsubscribe link was included in probing.

None of these are confirmed defects. They are gaps in what could be observed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only link with a visible destination, "Shop Men", goes to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`. Its parameters are Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid`.
- No `utm_*` parameters are visible on it. They may be added elsewhere or appended server-side, but that can't be confirmed.
- Landing-page alignment with the "Extra 25% Off Markdowns" subject could not be checked, because the destination never resolved in the probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe `trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M645DN772J4Y7QSVSSX6R8WE_9` with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the tracker host. This would confirm whether the failure is real or a probe artifact.
2. Raise the probe budget, or sample more of the 11 skipped links, so destination checks cover more than 1 of 12.
3. Capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from a direct delivery, or from the relay's raw message, to separate relay stripping from real omissions.
4. Fetch the full, untruncated HTML to audit the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and any UTM parameters.
5. In Everlane's template, fill in `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
