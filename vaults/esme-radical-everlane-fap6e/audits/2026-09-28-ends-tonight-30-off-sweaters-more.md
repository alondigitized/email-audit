# Ends Tonight: 30% Off Sweaters & More
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane closing out the week's "30% Off" run with real urgency for the first time — "ENDS TONIGHT" stamped directly on the hero, above the same navy chunky-sweater editorial the brand has been leaning on since 09-25/09-26. It's the strongest deadline framing of the whole cadence: unlike the 09-27 sends that just said "30% Off," this one gives a credible, time-bound reason to act today, and the fine print backs it up with a real cutoff (09/28 11:59 PM PST).
- Where it loses ground is the product grid — a 3x3 matrix of nine sweaters with zero labels, prices, or member-pricing callouts. It's efficient for browsing but does nothing to help me decide, and it's visually a step down from the single strong hero shot this format usually rides on. The closing full-bleed lifestyle photo is also pure repetition of hero-adjacent styling with no new information.
- Judgement: on execution this sits right alongside the 09-26 "Fan-Favorite Sweaters" send (8/10) in visual quality, but the added urgency and clean layout edge it slightly ahead on likely conversion, even if the grid is a wasted opportunity.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" is legible, high-contrast, and placed above the offer — genuine urgency this brand's week of sends had been missing.
- Hero photography is strong and on-brand: real editorial model, chunky navy quarter-zip, consistent with the 09-25/09-26 hero assets.
- Two black "SHOP NOW" CTA bars (post-hero and post-grid) are unambiguous and easy to tap.
- No render bugs — clean spacing, no overlapping text, no broken images.
- Fine print discloses exact deadline (09/28 11:59 PM PST) and standard exclusions, which reads as honest rather than vague.

## What's weak

- The 3x3 product grid has no prices, names, or "member pricing" — just thumbnails, so there's no reason to click one square over another.
- No loyalty/member benefit is surfaced despite this being a repeat-buyer audience by week's end.
- Second full-width photo (seated model) adds no new product or offer info — pure filler before the footer.
- Subhead copy ("Been eyeing these all weekend?") is soft and generic compared to the sharp urgency of the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add price + strike-through discount under each grid thumbnail — turns a static mood board into a scannable shop feed and is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Cut the second full-bleed photo (seated model) entirely — replace with a countdown timer or restated deadline near the final CTA to reinforce urgency right before the click.
- 3. Swap the soft subhead for something that echoes the urgency, not just "eyeing these all weekend."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Call: 30% Off Sweaters Ends at Midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Few Hours Left — 30% Off Sweaters & More`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab your size before the clock runs out tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sweaters, knits & more — 30% off until 11:59 PM PST.`
- 4. Add "Sign Up for Texts" or loyalty tie-in near the CTA for repeat subscribers who've now seen this offer 3+ times this week.
