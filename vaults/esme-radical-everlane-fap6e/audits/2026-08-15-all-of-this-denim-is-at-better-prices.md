# All Of This Denim Is At Better Prices
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's denim-category product story wrapped around the same "Better Prices, Still Everlane" price footer it's now run four times this week — after 8/12's manifesto letter, 8/13's straight price-drop announcement, and 8/14's "Your Favorites At Better Prices." Structurally it's identical to 8/13's "New Utility Style Pants" and "Dresses That Do It All": hero shot, product cards, price-footer bolt-on. The one thing this send does differently — and does well — is show four distinct silhouettes with real, specific copy per style ("Looks like you tried. Feels like you didn't.") instead of one generic hero.
- No offer is visible anywhere in this render — no percentage, no dollar amount, just "Better Prices" as unquantified brand language, which is the same vagueness that capped 8/13's price-drop email at 5/10. For a subscriber who's seen this beat four times in a week, the lack of a concrete number is the biggest miss. Solid execution, stale campaign, no hook.

## What's working

- Four genuinely distinct jean silhouettes (Cigarette, Way-High Curve, Bootcut, Gardener Pull-On) each with a one-line description that differentiates fit and use case.
- Clean, on-brand minimalist layout — no render bugs, no overlapping text, no broken images.
- Organic cotton / sustainability callout adds credible substance beyond "buy now."
- Consistent black "SHOP NOW" CTA repeated per product, so intent is unambiguous at every scroll stop.

## What's weak

- "Better Prices" appears with zero specific number — no %, no $, no "up to X off," for the fourth email running.
- Hero image shows only one jean style, not "all of this denim," creating a mismatch with the subject line's promise of breadth.
- No urgency or time-bound signal anywhere in the email.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the same price-tier framing as 8/14, in a category-story format nearly identical to 8/13's two sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero and subject — even a "20% off select denim" beats "Better Prices" repeated for the fourth time this week.
- 2. Show 2-3 of the four silhouettes in the hero collage instead of just one, to back up "All of This Denim."
- 3. Add a light urgency cue (e.g., "through Sunday") since nothing currently signals why to act now.
- 4. Vary the template — the price-footer-bolted-onto-product-grid format has run essentially unchanged since 8/12; a different layout would fight fatigue more than new copy will.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-generated Everlane campaign email with 2 broken material links (unresolved Shopify redirect paths) and missing standard compliance/authentication headers in the captured payload.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken redirects**: 2 material links fail resolution with "unknown url type" errors — both are Klaviyo tracked-link redirects (`trk.send.everlane.com/l/...`) that should forward to Shopify paths but instead surface raw, unresolved URLs:
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M031T4B09F02VGQJDKBDF7Y6_19` → intended target `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=...` (link text: "Icon | Sign Up for Texts")
  - `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M031T4B09F02VGQJDKBDF7Y6_0` → intended target `/pages/womens?_kx=...` (link text: "Everlane Logo")
  
  Both carry Klaviyo `_kx` and Bluecore `bxid`/`cm_flow_i` parameters, suggesting a redirect-mapping or URL-encoding failure at the Klaviyo tracked-link layer rather than a bad destination URL.
- 12 of 29 total links were probed (click-likelihood ranked); 17 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized, not tested. No conclusions can be drawn on untested links.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text**: tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M031T4B09F02VGQJDKBDF7Y6` has no `alt` attribute. Low impact (open-tracking pixel, not user-facing content), but flagged per QA output.
- Extensive MSO/Outlook conditional comments and mobile media queries present — no rendering failures detected in available source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{ }}`, `*|...|*`) found in the truncated source. `_kx` and `bxid` tokens present in link URLs are expected Klaviyo/Bluecore tracking identifiers, not merge-field failures.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm root cause from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown via this relay; not confirmed as a sender-side failure.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), but was not among the 12 probed links, so its resolution status is unverified.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) observed in the truncated source; links use Klaviyo `_kx` and Bluecore `bxid`/`cm_flow_i` tracking params instead. Cannot assess landing-page alignment given the two broken redirects and truncated source — insufficient data.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the Klaviyo tracked-link redirect mapping for links `..._19` and `..._0` (sms-signup and homepage logo) — both resolve to raw `/pages/...` paths instead of proper redirect targets.
- Add `alt=""` (decorative) to the open-tracking pixel for accessibility/HTML validity, even though it carries no visible content.
- Independently verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results headers via a direct SMTP capture (bypassing the AgentMail relay) before treating these as sender-side compliance failures, since the relay is a known blind spot here.
