# Cashmere for Every Forecast
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand cashmere send, and it is nearly a re-run of the 2026-10-19 "Cashmere Beyond Sweater Weather" email. The hero is strong: a model in a green cashmere cardigan with pearl buttons, a thin "Nothing Better Than Cashmere" overlay, and a black SHOP NOW bar. Four product rows follow, then a cream "Better Prices, Still Everlane" block.
- The news is "now at new better prices," and the email never says what that means. There is no percentage, no price, and no before and after on any product. A subscriber who saw yesterday's cashmere email gets the same story with a different subject line, and no new reason to click.
- Judgement: well executed and visually calm, but it repeats yesterday's pitch and makes a price claim with no numbers. Expect a mid-pack result of about 6/10.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong single image, and the black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath it makes the primary action obvious.
- The four-row product stack (polo, cropped cardigan, V sweater, U-neck) is easy to scan. Each row has a name, a one-line fit description and its own link.
- Spacing is generous and the layout is consistent, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The cream "Better Prices, Still Everlane" block closes the email with a reassurance that quality hasn't changed.
- The copy is on-season and matches the brand's quiet voice.

## What's weak

- "Better prices" has no figure behind it. There is no price, no percent off, and no "was/now" anywhere in the render.
- It is a near-repeat of 2026-10-19's cashmere send, which also led with "Now at new better prices." That makes it the second cashmere email in two days.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW", including all four product links, so none of them names the product or the benefit.
- The hero overlay is thin, small text over a busy image and is easy to miss. It also doesn't match the subject line's "Forecast" angle.
- There is no urgency, no deadline and no social proof, such as reviews or a best-seller tag.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price in the hero: "Cashmere from $XX" or "Was $XXX, now $XXX". This one change would do the most for the email.
- 2. Show a price under each product name, with a "new price" tag so the claim is visible at a glance.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, for example "Shop the Cashmere Polo" and "Shop Cashmere Cardigans", instead of four identical SHOP NOW links.
- 4. Add proof, such as a star rating or "best-seller" on one or two items.
- 5. Don't send a second cashmere email within 24 hours. If the send is needed, lead with a different angle, such as layering or colour, or with something that is actually new.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cashmere, now at new lower prices`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our cashmere just got cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Polos, cardigans and crews. See the new prices.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same Grade-A cashmere, lower price tags. Shop 4 favorites.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Only the first part of the HTML was available, and the QA pass rate was 38%. Two tracked links (the "Everlane" header link and the "Sign Up for Texts" link) failed because their redirect targets came back as relative paths. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be run through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5BCMFZ5RE09QXB3WFP40BHZ_0`.
  - The probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_fl…'`.
  - The redirect target has no scheme or host.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" link:**
  - Tracked URL: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5BCMFZ5RE09QXB3WFP40BHZ_18`.
  - The error was `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`, the same relative-path pattern.
- **Cause not confirmed.** The tracker's redirect may return a relative `Location`, or the probe may mishandle relative redirects.
  - If the `Location` header really is relative, browsers would resolve it against `trk.send.everlane.com`. The header and SMS links would then 404 on the tracking domain.
  - Other links on the same tracker were probed without this error, so it may be specific to these two. Manually follow `_0` and `_18` in a real client to settle it.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 links were probed and 16 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so those 16 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is low impact in email.
- **Small mobile heading text:** `@media (max-width: 480px) { h4 { font-size: 10px !important } }` sets any `<h4>` text to 10px on mobile.
- **Missing alt text (likely benign):** The flagged image is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5BCMFZ5RE09QXB3WFP40BHZ`. The `/o/` path suggests it's the open-tracking pixel, so alt text is probably not needed. Confirm it's 1×1 and not a content image.
- **Fonts:** The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` rule is ignored by many clients. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is not a defect.
- **Other:** The source includes Outlook conditionals (`mso`), `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, and `[data-ogsc]` dark-mode overrides. The rest of the CSS and body was truncated and not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear, though the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers were not found. The QA note says AgentMail may strip them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message at the origin or in a non-relay inbox. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results:** Missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `everlane.com` and the tracking domain `send.everlane.com` suggest a subdomain setup, but this can't be confirmed.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** Neither is in the truncated source, and the QA output reports no unsubscribe probe result. Both are unverified.
- **Sender address:** The From address is `support@everlane.com`, a support mailbox used for marketing mail. This is not necessarily a defect, but replies may route to a support queue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links go through the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…` redirector and carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_fl…`). Visible fragments show no `utm_*` parameters. The URLs are truncated, so full UTM coverage can't be confirmed.
- The header link lands on `/pages/womens` and the SMS link on `/pages/sms-signup`. Neither landing page could be loaded because of the relative-redirect failures. Cashmere product or campaign page alignment is unverified.

2. Make the QA probe resolve relative redirects against the final host, to separate probe errors from real bugs.
