# Your Black Friday Reminder: 40% Off Everything
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's plain-text-style Black Friday nudge, a change from the image-first sends earlier in the week. It opens with a letter-style greeting and a one-line offer. The familiar quilted-jacket hero follows, with "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*". The format is a good idea because it looks personal. The execution undercuts it.
- The greeting renders as "Hi There, ," with a stray comma, which looks like a broken personalization field. That is the first thing the reader sees, and it spoils the personal feel. The body copy is generic ("sweaters and everyday staples," "better gifts for them and a little something for you"), and the "Shop now" link points nowhere specific. This is roughly the sixth near-identical 40% send since 11/24 ("Still Thinking About 40% Off Everything?", "Favorites Are Going Fast"). It adds a text-first layout and nothing else. Offer fatigue is the main risk.
- Overall it is clear but forgettable, with a visible greeting glitch. The offer is easy to find and the deadline is in the footer, but it gives a repeat subscriber no new reason to click.

## What's working

- The 40% off offer is stated in the first line of copy and again in the hero.
- Shop Women and Shop Men split the hero CTA, so recipients can go straight to their category.
- The letter-style layout is a nice change of pace from the image-heavy sends earlier this week.
- The nav block (Shop Women, Shop Men, New Arrivals, Best-Sellers) is clean and easy to tap.
- The footer discloses the exclusions and the 12/02/2026 end date.

## What's weak

- "Hi There, ," renders with a stray comma and looks like a failed merge field.
- The hero is the same quilted-jacket creative as the earlier sends this week, so it is stale.
- The copy is vague ("everyday staples," "something for you") and names no product or price.
- The only deadline is in the grey fine print at the bottom, so the email has no visible urgency.
- The hero text sits on a busy photo and is only moderately legible. The "SHOP WOMEN / SHOP MEN" links are small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the greeting. Use "Hi Walker," or drop it ("Quick reminder:"). A visible glitch in the first line costs more than any copy tweak can recover.
- 2. Put the end date in the body ("Ends Dec 2 at 11:59 PM PST") and add a visible button instead of underlined text links.
- 3. Replace the repeated hero with a product grid of 3–4 best-sellers showing the original and 40%-off prices.
- 4. Make the copy specific: name the product category (outerwear, sweaters) and the benefit instead of "everyday staples."
- 5. Cut the cadence or segment it. Send a different angle to recipients who didn't open the last three.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Dec 2: 40% off, sweaters included`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 40% off is still on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quilted jackets, cashmere and denim, all 40% off until Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gifts for them and a little something for you. Ends Dec 2.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Your Black Friday Reminder: 40% Off Everything"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email (MJML-style markup, `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracking). Both probed CTA links failed on a relative-path redirect, 12 of 12 material links were skipped, and the QA harness could not see the auth or unsubscribe headers. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target, "Everlane" link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M8ARFW7HRKKZHRGHCR7D8V4G_0` resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. It has no scheme or host.
- **[FAIL] Same failure, "Shop Men" link:** `…_10` resolved to `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Probable cause (unconfirmed):** Relative `Location` headers are valid per RFC 7231, so this is probably a limitation of the probe, which couldn't resolve against the tracker host.
- **What to check:** If the tracker really returns a host-less `Location` from `trk.send.everlane.com`, browsers would resolve it against the tracking domain, not `everlane.com`. Run `curl -sI` on both URLs to see the raw header.
- **[WARN] Probe coverage:** "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped", so no CTA destination status was verified (200 vs. 404 vs. redirect chain).
- **[WARN] Missing alt on tracking image:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M8ARFW7HRKKZHRGHCR7D8V4G` is an open-tracking pixel. This is low severity, but it should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only.
- **Link styling:** `a:not([name]), a:link, a:visited, a:active, a:hover { color:#000; text-decoration:none }` gives every link the same black color with no underline. Unless the body adds other styling, links are not distinguishable from body text (WCAG 1.4.1).
- **Small mobile text:** `@media (max-width:480px) { .kl-text > div, .kl-table-subblock > div, .kl-split-subblock > div { font-size:10px !important } }` forces 10px body text on mobile, which is below common legibility minimums.
- **Web font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored or stripped by several clients, including Gmail. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is present, so this is informational only.
- **Dark mode:** The `[data-ogsc] .ev-clay-light / .ev-clay-dark` swap is handled correctly.
- **Outlook:** The MSO conditionals and VML namespaces are present and well-formed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{%`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA note says it may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown because the relay strips them.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion. The QA data says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is a support mailbox used as the marketing sender. This is a minor concern for reply handling.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters in the visible URLs.** Click URLs carry only Klaviyo identifiers (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). The tracker may append UTMs at redirect time, but that couldn't be confirmed because the probes failed.
- **Landing pages not verified.** Both destinations (`/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`) are category pages. Whether the "40% Off Everything" offer is shown or applied there is unknown.
- **Link text vs. destination:** The link labeled "Everlane" (likely the logo) points to `/pages/womens`, not the site root.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify the raw `Location` header of the `trk.send.everlane.com/l/…` links. If it is host-less, make the tracker return absolute URLs (`https://www.everlane.com/…`). If it isn't, fix the probe to resolve relative redirects against the request URL.
2. Fetch the raw `.eml` or SMTP source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't draw conclusions from the relay-stripped copy.
3. Re-run the link probe with a higher cap than 12, or probe CTAs first, so destination status codes are recorded.
4. Add explicit link styling (underline or distinct color) for in-body text links and raise the 10px mobile text floor.
5. Populate `<title>` and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Add UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) to the destination URLs, or confirm the tracker adds them.
