# 30% Off Online & In Stores
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Everlane's third 30% send in three days, and it adds almost nothing. The offer is clear: 30% off sitewide with 20% extra off markdowns. It follows "30% Off: Early Access" (10/6) and "30% Off Sitewide: Friends & Family" (10/7), and this render repeats that same Friends & Family hero. Only the subject changed, adding "Online & In Stores". A subscriber who got both of those has no new reason to open or click.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan: a hero, a black SHOP NOW bar, four category tiles and a maroon markdown block. The weak spot is the hero. "30% Off Sitewide" is small, white type over a grey concrete wall and a busy two-model photo. The offer doesn't land first, and the "Friends & Family" label next to it just adds noise.
- Verdict: a competent but redundant promo. It is fine as a reminder, but it adds fatigue and gives no urgency beyond what the subscriber already knows.

## What's working

- The 30% offer and "Including New Arrivals" are stated plainly in the hero, with a full-width black SHOP NOW bar directly beneath it.
- The four category tiles (Sweaters, Denim, Tops, Outerwear) give a fast, seasonal browse path.
- The maroon "Extra 20% Off Markdowns" block stacks a second incentive and has separate Women's and Men's sale links.
- The layout is consistent and trustworthy, with a quiet, minimal Everlane look and no broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero offer text is small and low-contrast over a grey wall. The eye goes to the models, not the 30%.
- The deadline (10/13) appears only in the grey footer fine print. The body copy has no urgency, even though this is billed as "our biggest event of the season."
- The tile labels ("SHOP SWEATERS", "SHOP DENIM") are thin white text laid over the products themselves, so they are hard to read, especially on the light-grey tiles.
- "Sitewide" conflicts with the footer's "applies to select styles" and the fine print on exclusions. That undercuts honesty.
- This is the third near-identical 30% send in three days. The "Better Prices, Still Everlane" band is filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero with a large, high-contrast "30% OFF" headline and the end date ("Ends 10/13") above the fold. Drop the "Friends & Family" label, which is stale after yesterday's send.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero and the subject, and use a countdown or "last days" framing so this send feels different from the first two.
- 3. Change the tile labels to dark text below or beside each product, or add a solid label bar. Include a price or "30% off" on each tile.
- 4. Remove the "Better Prices, Still Everlane" band. It repeats the SHOP NOW link without adding anything.
- 5. Cut the cadence or merge sends. One strong reminder with a real deadline beats three similar emails.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-built (MJML-style) email with tracked links through `trk.send.everlane.com`. The QA run found one broken-link failure (a tracked link whose redirect resolves to a relative path), plus missing alt text on the tracking pixel and a few markup gaps. The HTML is truncated and the auth and unsubscribe headers weren't captured, so compliance and continuity can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Relative redirect target.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M4CFVDD6QR5PQXEQ1V30MHS1_0` (link text "Everlane") resolved to `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The prober failed with `unknown url type`.
  - The tracker's redirect appears to return a host-less path, or the prober failed to resolve it against the tracker host.
  - Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header against the tracker host. That would send the click to `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens`, not `everlane.com`, so it may 404.
  - I couldn't confirm which case applies from the data. Manually follow the link and check the final host and status.
- The link text is the brand name, so this is likely the header or logo link. Even if it resolves, it targets `/pages/womens`, not the sale landing page.
- Only 12 of 27 links were probed and 15 were skipped, so the other links are unverified.
- The tracking links are opaque (`/l/<id>_<n>`), so the destination URLs can't be audited from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel.** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M4CFVDD6QR5PQXEQ1V30MHS1` has no `alt`. Low severity, but it should be `alt=""`.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The `<html>` tag has `xmlns` attributes but no `lang`. That affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Small mobile heading.** `h4` drops to `font-size: 10px !important` at `max-width: 480px`.
- **Inconsistent font names.** `h1` uses `"MaisonNeue-Light"` while `h2`–`h4` use `"Maison Neue"`. Both fall back to Arial.
- **Web font import.** The `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is ignored by Gmail and some clients, so those clients always get the Arial fallback. This is expected behavior, not a defect.
- The Outlook conditional comments and VML namespaces are present and well-formed.
- The truncated HTML means the body content (image alt text, buttons, dark-mode blocks) couldn't be checked. The `.ev-clay-dark` and `.ev-clay-light` classes show a dark-mode swap exists. If each variant carries its own images, both need alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The only personalization artifacts are Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid` parameters.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not captured.** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were all missing. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is an observability gap rather than a confirmed sender failure. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **In-body unsubscribe.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed and raised no failure. The footer is outside the truncated source, so the physical postal address couldn't be verified.
- **Sending domains differ.** The From address is `support@everlane.com`, while tracking runs on `send.everlane.com`. DKIM and SPF alignment for the subdomain can't be confirmed without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The one resolvable destination (`/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`) carries Klaviyo identifiers but no visible `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. The query string is cut off in the QA output, so UTMs may sit after the truncation point.
- **Landing page.** The subject says "30% Off Online & In Stores". The only resolved link goes to a generic women's page, and I couldn't verify whether it shows the promo.
- Click-through for the other 26 links wasn't verified (see §2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the relative redirect.** Check what the `Location` header of `…/l/01M4CFVDD6QR5PQXEQ1V30MHS1_0` returns. It should be an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens?…`. Then re-run the probe.
2. **Add `alt=""`** to the `trk.send.everlane.com/o/…` open pixel.
3. **Set `<html lang="en">`** and a meaningful `<title>`, or the subject line.
4. **Raise the mobile `h4` size** above 10px.
5. **Rerun QA with raw headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass directly from the ESP's send log.
6. **Check UTM tagging** on the final landing URLs. Make sure the promo links go to a landing page that reflects the 30% offer.
7. **Probe the 15 skipped links**, or at least sample them, before treating the link check as clean.
