# The Styles You Reach for Most Are 40% Off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-composed Black Friday send, but it is the seventh near-identical "40% off everything" message in about three days. The hero is a split-crop of a model in a red polo knit and black trousers, with "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*" in white over it and a full-width black SHOP NOW bar. The offer is clear and the eye lands on it first. The subject ("The Styles You Reach for Most Are 40% Off") promises something curated, but the body is only generic everything-is-40%-off copy.
- The creative is a slight refresh of 2026-11-18's "Black Friday: 40% Off Everything" and 2026-11-19's "The Pants People Keep Buying", which also led with the trouser look. A subscriber has now seen this offer in several wrappers. There is no new reason to click today, no deadline in the body, and the real end date (12/02/2026) sits in 8pt grey type in the footer.
- Judgement: competent execution, tired cadence. It is a middle-of-the-pack send that gets by on brand trust and a clear offer.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable. "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" is large white type over a dark, muted image.
- The SHOP NOW bar is full-width black, with an unambiguous tap target directly under the hero.
- The red knit against the black trousers gives a strong, seasonal, product-forward image.
- The Better Gift Guide block splits into "Shop Gifts for Her" and "Shop Gifts for Him", which gives two clear gifting paths.
- The layout is simple and uncluttered, with a clean hierarchy and no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "the styles you reach for most", but nothing in the body shows or names a best-seller.
- The hero crop has a visible seam, with a hard vertical edge where the full-length shot meets the zoomed close-up. It looks slightly like a collage glitch.
- The deadline appears only in the footer fine print (valid until 12/02/2026). The body has no urgency, such as "ends Dec 2".
- The body copy ("The layers you reach for. The pants that go with everything.") is vague, and no specific product is linked or priced.
- The gift guide block is a large maroon slab with a lot of empty space and no imagery.
- There are no reviews, ratings, or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject deliver on its promise. Put 3–4 actual best-sellers with prices and "was/now" pricing under the hero, and link each one directly.
- 2. Move the deadline into the hero ("Ends Dec 2") to make the urgency credible.
- 3. Fix the hero crop so it is one continuous image, or an intentional diptych with a clear gutter.
- 4. Add review stars or a "most-loved" badge to the products.
- 5. Tighten the gift guide block or add product imagery, so it does not read as a dead zone.
- 6. Space out the Black Friday sends or vary the offer story (for example gifts, then best-sellers, then a last-chance send).

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Chance Picks: 40% Off Through Dec 2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The red knit polo, barrel-leg trousers and more. Ends Dec 2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the pieces everyone's reordering, all 40% off.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "The Styles You Reach for Most Are 40% Off"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email, sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain, with 38% QA pass rate. The two hard failures are link-probe errors on redirect targets, and the header warnings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay. The HTML is truncated, so anything past the `<style>` block is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M7WYB6WKXKQMSV8QEMDR5J12_0` resolved to a relative path, `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…`. The probe raised `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Shop Men" link:** `…/l/01M7WYB6WKXKQMSV8QEMDR5J12_7` resolved to the relative path `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Likely cause:** the tracker answers with a relative `Location` header, and the probe doesn't resolve it against the origin. Browsers and mail clients resolve relative redirects correctly, so this may be a probe artifact rather than a broken link. I can't confirm either way from this data. Re-test with a client that follows relative redirects.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 9 were skipped, so link health is not fully verified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7WYB6WKXKQMSV8QEMDR5J12` is the open-tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[WARN] Missing alt text:** the tracking pixel image has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and keep it out of the accessibility tree. This is low severity because it's a pixel.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only.
- **Small mobile `<h4>`:** the media query sets `h4 { font-size: 10px !important }` at ≤480px. That is below the usual legibility floor if `<h4>` carries content.
- **External font `@import`:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in several clients, such as Gmail. The font stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so the impact is limited.
- **Not verifiable:** the truncated source doesn't show alt text on content images, the preheader, or the `lang` attribute (the `<html>` tag has none).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `*|…|*` appear. Body content past the truncation point was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so absence is not confirmed. Verify in the raw message at the original recipient inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is also attributed to the relay.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown. The footer isn't in the truncated source, so I couldn't confirm the in-body unsubscribe link or postal address.
- **From address:** `support@everlane.com` is used for a marketing send, while click tracking runs on `send.everlane.com`. Check that the From domain aligns with the DKIM `d=` domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Tracking parameters:** the destination URLs carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`), and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated strings. If GA/analytics attribution depends on UTMs, confirm they are present in the full URLs.
- **Landing pages:** the targets are `/pages/womens` and `/collections/shop-all-mens-clothing`. Neither was checked for status or for alignment with the "40% off" offer, because the probe failed first.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the two failed links with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. Then confirm the final landing pages return 200 and show the 40% offer.
2. Check the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they are present, no action is needed. If they are absent, treat that as a compliance defect.
3. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and add a real `<title>` and `lang="en"`.
4. Confirm `utm_*` parameters are on the full URLs if cross-channel attribution is needed.
5. Raise the mobile `<h4>` size above 10px if it carries content.
