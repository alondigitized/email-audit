# Flat Out Better
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand footwear send with a good hero: red leather flats on textured concrete, "Flats Are Here To Stay" overlaid, and a black SHOP NOW bar. It's one theme, one category and three products, and the render has no broken images or layout problems. It reads like an Everlane email.
- It gives the reader no reason to act today. There is no price, no offer, no deadline and no reviews. Compared with the 2026-10-19 "Cashmere Beyond Sweater Weather" send, which at least had "Now at new better prices," this one is pure mood. The leather-sourcing block at the bottom is a sustainability message, not a purchase driver. It's the third Everlane email in about 48 hours (after "New October Arrivals" today and "New Libra Season Approved Styles" on 10/18), and the second to rely on a vague "shop the collection" push.
- Judgement: well made but low-pressure. It's fine for browsing, and I'd expect modest clicks.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. The red flats against grey concrete are the clear focal point, and the black SHOP NOW bar sits directly beneath.
- The three-product zig-zag (Mary Jane, Glove, Keyhole) is easy to scan. Each product has a name, a one-line benefit and its own link.
- The product copy has personality: "Don't worry. Comfort like this is actually legal." and "No missteps here."
- The "Shop All Shoes" button gives a clear fallback after the product modules.
- The page is uncluttered, with plenty of white space and no render bugs.

## What's weak

- There is no price or offer anywhere, not even a "from $" figure. Nothing tells the reader why now.
- All four main CTAs say "SHOP NOW" (hero plus three product modules). The copy is generic and the links aren't distinguishable.
- The sustainability block ("Gold or Silver by the Leather Working Group") is jargon-heavy and sits below the products, where it dilutes the close. "READ MORE" is a weak CTA.
- There are no reviews, ratings, sizing or fit signals. Flats are a fit-sensitive purchase, and "cult favorite" is the only social proof.
- The hero's "Flats Are Here To Stay" is small, white text over a busy texture, so it's hard to read.
- No price or "new" tag appears on any product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to buy now. Put a price on each product ("$138") or a hook such as free returns, a restock or a member perk. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop the Day Glove" and "Shop Mary Janes" instead of four identical "SHOP NOW"s.
- 3. Move the leather-sourcing block into a short one-line badge under the hero, or cut it. If it stays, rewrite it in plain language.
- 4. Add a star rating or a short review quote under each product, for example "4.8★ · 1,200 reviews".
- 5. Increase the size and contrast of the hero headline.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-style template. Its two failed link probes are the only hard QA failures, and both come from relative redirect targets on the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking domain. The header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay artifacts and aren't confirmed defects. The source is truncated, so I limit findings to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] "Everlane" logo link:** `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M5AYSTYVGXFPSERQKTW17JBY_0` resolves to a relative target, `/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=01KRSAQRJKNNCKD9WS2NMXDB0V&cm_flow_i…`. The probe raised `unknown url type`.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts" link:** `…/l/01M5AYSTYVGXFPSERQKTW17JBY_16` resolves to the relative target `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`.
- **Likely cause:** The tracker's redirect `Location` is a host-relative path. RFC 7231 allows that, and browsers resolve it against the tracking host. The prober apparently didn't resolve it, so this is probably a probe limitation.
- **Risk:** If `trk.send.everlane.com` resolves `/pages/womens` against its own host, the click would land on the tracking domain and 404. The final destination is unverified, so re-probe with relative-redirect resolution before treating it as broken or fine.
- **Header link:** The logo link goes to `/pages/womens`, not the site root. That is a deliberate destination, not a defect.
- **Coverage:** Only 12 of 26 links were probed and 14 were skipped as footer or social, so the other links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` is blank.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`. This hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Missing alt text:** The only flagged image is the open-tracking pixel, `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M5AYSTYVGXFPSERQKTW17JBY`. It should carry `alt=""`, but this is low impact. The QA check reported no content images missing alt.

No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely reason.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Neither is in the truncated source, and the QA output has no result for them. I can't confirm either way.

- Landing alignment can't be checked because the probes failed before reaching the final pages.
