# Better Gifts Under $50
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident gift-angle send, and a welcome change of pace. After roughly a week of "40% off everything" repeats, "Better Gifts Under $50" leads with a price-point promise instead of a blanket discount. The hero is a white-ribboned gift box with the headline in oversized maroon type. The eye lands on the offer first, and a full-width SHOP NOW bar sits directly under it.
- The weak spot is the gap between the promise and the product. Three plain tees (a white long-sleeve, a dark green tee, a white tee) sit under a line about "organic cotton must-haves." They show no prices, so "under $50" is never proven. The real discount, "BLACK FRIDAY 40% OFF EVERYTHING*", is pushed down into a second banner. It also sits on a busy quilted-jacket photo, which makes it harder to read.
- Verdict: a good concept with thin execution. It's more distinctive than the last several sends, but it doesn't back up its own headline.

## What's working

- The hero headline is huge, legible, and specific. "Better Gifts Under $50" reads instantly and has a clear price anchor.
- The maroon SHOP NOW bar is full-width and unambiguous, directly under the hero.
- The gift-box image fits the season and the copy, so the visual and the message agree.
- The GIFTS FOR HER and GIFTS FOR HIM buttons let the reader self-segment.
- The layout is clean, with generous whitespace and consistent maroon accents. There are no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- No product shows a price. Three similar basic tees don't demonstrate "under $50" or feel gift-worthy.
- The three products aren't labeled or linked individually, so there is no hero product to click.
- The "40% OFF EVERYTHING*" banner is low-contrast white text over a busy photo, with small underlined SHOP WOMEN and SHOP MEN links.
- The "SHOP THE GIFT GUIDE" bar sits above the 40% banner, so it's unclear whether it labels that image. Two competing offers dilute the message.
- The body copy "Highly rated styles, including organic cotton must-haves for layering and lounging" is generic. It says nothing about gifting.
- The nav links and footer are standard. There is no loyalty or member benefit and no urgency beyond the fine print date (12/02).

## Recommendations

- 1. Prove the price. Put "$28", "$35" and "$45" under each product, with named, linked items such as "The Organic Cotton Crew, $30". This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Merge the two offers into one story, such as "Gifts under $50, plus 40% off everything". Put the Black Friday banner directly under the hero on a solid color panel so it's legible.
- 3. Make the gifting angle concrete. Use products people actually give, such as scarves, hats and socks. Three white-ish tees don't read as presents.
- 4. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Gifts Under $50" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Add a real deadline, like "Ends 12/2", in the hero area instead of only in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gifts under $50 (that don't look like it)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off + gifts under $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Organic cotton, cozy layers, and 40% off everything through 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best-rated styles, wrapped up for him and her.`

# Technical Audit: Everlane, "Better Gifts Under $50"

The email is a Klaviyo-built template (`kl-row`, `kl-column`, MJML-style `mj-column-per-100`) with every link wrapped in the `trk.send.everlane.com` click-tracking redirect. The QA run reported 0 hard failures, but link verification is incomplete and the header-based compliance checks could not be confirmed. The HTML is also truncated, which limits what can be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

  The 429 is almost certainly the tracker rate-limiting the QA prober, not a broken link. No final destination was resolved for any of them.
- **No hard failures:** no 404s or 5xx responses were observed in the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Tracking pixel has no alt text.** `<img src="https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M7XF3GQSRYGHAP5T9PTMN1JR">` is the open-tracking beacon. It should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip it. Impact is low.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. Some clients and web-view or archive renderings use it as the page title.
- **No other defects in the visible source.** It includes the viewport meta, the `X-UA-Compatible` meta, the MSO conditional comments, and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`. Mobile `@media (max-width: 480px)` rules are present.
- **External font `@import` is fragile.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` is unsupported in several clients. The font stacks do fall back to Arial and Helvetica, so this is a low-risk observation rather than a defect.
- **Alt text on the other images could not be assessed** because the source is truncated. The QA check flagged only the tracking pixel.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.
