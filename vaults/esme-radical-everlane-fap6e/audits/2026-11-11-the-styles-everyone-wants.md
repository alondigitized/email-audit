# The Styles Everyone Wants
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-driven "It List" send: four products, each paired with a customer quote, with a model-plus-detail image pair above it. It's a good idea. The copy "the styles getting the most love right now in the comments section" is honest and distinctive, and the testimonials do more selling than a headline would. The denim-shirt-and-wide-leg hero with the suede bag is strong, and the bag ties through to the last module.
- The weakness is that there's no offer at all, and no prices either. Two days ago "Better Cashmere for Everyone" at least showed $68/$118/$48. The week's Black Friday preview sends ("30% Off Your Next Layer", "Don't Miss 30% Off Savings") carried a concrete discount. Here a reader gets five identical black SHOP NOW buttons and nothing to act on today. It's a pleasant editorial send, but the commercial case is thin.
- Judgement: well-built social proof, undermined by missing price and urgency. A solid mid-pack send.

- The hero model in the denim shirt and wide-leg jeans is clean, and the eye lands on it first.
- The two-image pairing (detail shot beside full-length shot) shows fit and fabric.
- The layout is consistent and scannable, with named products and a centered rhythm.
- The bag in the hero comes back as the final module, which gives the email a loop.

- The hero's SHOP NOW is generic and doesn't say which product or category it goes to.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built template sent through the `trk.send.everlane.com` click tracker. The automated QA gave it a 38% pass rate. Two tracked links (the logo and the SMS signup) failed to resolve, the open-tracking pixel has no `alt`, and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Logo link, text "Everlane"**
  - The tracked URL is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M75S3TSVKDYTC620C3Y0SEV9_0…`.
  - The probe errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/womens?_kx=…&bxid=…&cm_flow_i…'`.
- **[FAIL] "Icon | Sign Up for Texts"**
  - The tracked URL is `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M75S3TSVKDYTC620C3Y0SEV9_20…`.
  - The probe errored with `unknown url type: '/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…'`.
- **What the error means:** both redirects returned a relative path (`/pages/...`) instead of an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` URL. The probe cannot follow a relative target. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` against the tracker host, so the click would land on `trk.send.everlane.com/pages/womens` rather than everlane.com.
- **Confidence:** this is not confirmed as a user-facing break. It could be a probe limitation, and the truncated URLs hide the full `Location` value. Verify with `curl -sI` on both tracked URLs.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 30 links were probed, and 18 were skipped (footer, utility and social). The status of those 18 is unknown.
- **Pixel alt text:** the open-tracking pixel `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M75S3TSVKDYTC620C3Y0SEV9` has no `alt`. Low severity, since it is a tracking pixel, but it should have `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This affects "view in browser" and some accessibility tooling.
- **Pixel alt text:** the open pixel has no `alt` (see section 2).
- **Font import:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/USb9mf/custom_fonts.css)` sits in a `<style>` block. Gmail and several other clients ignore `@import`. The font stacks fall back to `Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif`, so the impact is cosmetic only.
- **Everything else:** no other issues are evident in the truncated source. The VML/MSO conditionals, the viewport meta and the responsive breakpoints look standard. The source is truncated, so the full body was not audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ first_name }}` or `*|FNAME|*` appear in the provided source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three findings below are unverified, not confirmed failures.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058 one-click): not found.
- `Authentication-Results` header: not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Likely cause:** the QA output itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Check the raw message in a mailbox that preserves headers, such as Gmail "Show original".
- **Body unsubscribe link:** the QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it, and the footer is not in the truncated source. The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters seen:** the destinations carry Klaviyo parameters (`_kx`, `bxid`, `cm_flow_i…`). No `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible in the truncated URLs, so UTM presence cannot be confirmed either way.
- **Landing page alignment:** it cannot be assessed for most links. Only the two failed links have known targets (`/pages/womens` and `/pages/sms-signup`), and neither could be loaded.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on both failing tracked URLs. If the `Location` header is relative, fix the destination so it is absolute (`https://www.everlane.com/pages/womens` and `/pages/sms-signup`) in the Klaviyo template or tracking configuration. If it is already absolute, fix the QA probe to resolve relative redirects.
2. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel `trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M75S3TSVKDYTC620C3Y0SEV9`.
3. Set a meaningful `<title>`, for example the subject line "The Styles Everyone Wants".
4. Confirm the headers by capturing the raw message outside the relay. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results in `Authentication-Results`.
5. Verify the footer's unsubscribe link and postal address.
6. Probe the 18 skipped links, or at least the footer unsubscribe and preference links, and check the full URLs for `utm_*` parameters.
7. Make the harness store the full header set, so the three compliance and deliverability warnings can be resolved instead of repeating.
