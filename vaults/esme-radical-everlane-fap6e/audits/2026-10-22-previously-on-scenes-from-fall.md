# Previously on Scenes From Fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- The images carry this email, and the offer barely shows up. A model in a quilted taupe jacket fills the hero, with "Behind The Scenes" in thin white type and a black SHOP NOW bar. Three more lifestyle blocks follow: biking, a chunky knit, and navy "girl dinner" looks. The 30% off Fall Essentials Event, the only real commercial hook, sits in a muted blue box near the bottom. Most readers will never reach it.
- The subject "Previously on Scenes From Fall" promises a TV-recap story. The hero says "Behind The Scenes", which loosely echoes it, but nothing in the body recaps anything. Each block is a mood caption ("Bike The Scenic Route", "Better Late Than Wear Polyester") over a generic SHOP NOW button. It looks premium and reads as brand wallpaper. This is the fifth Everlane send in about three days, after "Fall Essentials Event: Up to 30% Off" and "You're In" earlier this week, and it adds nothing to that offer.
- Judgement: a handsome send with a clever subject and weak commercial drive. It is a 5-6 email dressed as a 7.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. Taupe outerwear, a clear model and a SHOP NOW bar all land at the top.
- The tonal palette (taupe, cream, navy) feels like fall and matches the week's sweater and cashmere sends.
- The layout is clean, with one button per block and no render bugs or broken images.
- The 30% off banner is legible and gives separate Women's and Men's links.
- Captions like "Better Late Than Wear Polyester" have real brand voice.

## What's weak

- The only offer, up to 30% off, is buried below roughly 80% of the scroll in a low-contrast blue block.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW" with no product, category or price attached, so the three mid-email buttons are interchangeable.
- The subject promises a "Previously on…" recap, but the body has no series or story structure.
- The hero text "Behind The Scenes" is widely spaced and thin, so it is hard to read over the busy image.
- The deadline (10/26) appears only in the fine print, so there is no visible urgency.
- No product names, prices or sizing show anywhere, and there is no social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off banner directly under the hero, or put it in the hero itself. Give the offer top billing and the mood imagery second billing.
- 2. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop Quilted Jackets", "Shop Sweaters", "Shop Navy Knits". Add a starting price or a product name under each image.
- 3. Make the subject and hero tell the same story. Either build a real recap structure ("Episode 1: The Jacket"), or drop the TV joke and lead with the offer.
- 4. Put the end date ("Ends Monday 10/26") in the banner, not the fine print.
- 5. Cut to two content blocks. That helps the cadence, since this is the fifth fall send this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall's best looks, up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Scenes From Fall: 30% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Quilted jackets, cozy knits and navy dresses, all part of the Fall Essentials Event.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 30% off sweaters and outerwear through 10/26. Here's how we're wearing it.`
