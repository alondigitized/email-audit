# How They’re Wearing Everlane
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a UGC-style editorial send. The hero is a woman in wide-leg jeans and a blue bandana, tagged #EVERLANEonYou. Below it are four "as seen on you" rows, each pairing a customer photo with the product shot, a handle credit and a product name. The grid is clean and easy to scan, and the social proof is the strongest thing about it. It is also a real change from the product-only sends earlier this week, such as the Washable Silk emails on 10-01 and 10-02.
- The weak spots are the offer and the cadence. There is no price or promotion above the fold. "Better Prices, Still Everlane" at the bottom is a vague claim with no number behind it. This is also the third Everlane send on 10-02, after "Better Silk" and "For All Walks of Life". Even a good creative gets less attention when it lands in the same inbox that day.
- Verdict: it's a solid, brand-consistent send that builds affinity but won't drive urgency. It works as a mid-funnel nudge, not a conversion email.

## What's working

- The hero is a real customer photo with an @handle and the #EVERLANEonYou tag, which feels more credible than studio imagery.
- The four rows each pair a customer look with the exact product, so you can see how it's worn and what to buy.
- Each row names the product (Made in Italy Ballet Flat, Everyday Oxford Shirt, Tissue Long-Sleeve Crew, Denim Chore Jacket), so the clicks are specific.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space, consistent row rhythm and no visible render bugs.
- The full-width black SHOP NOW bar under the hero is an unambiguous primary CTA.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer anywhere. "Better Prices, Still Everlane" is a claim without a number, so it can't drive a click.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW", and the per-row links are small underlined text that is easy to miss on mobile.
- The email gives no reason to act today. There is no deadline, no stock cue and no seasonal hook, despite the fall-appropriate jacket and denim.
- The hero's "What You're Wearing" header sits above the image as small text and adds little. Its wording also doesn't match the subject line ("How They're Wearing").
- The "Better Prices" block is buried at the bottom, below four full rows. It reads as an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a specific price message in the hero or directly under it, for example "Customer favorites, from $68". The email currently has a story but no offer.
- 2. Replace the generic CTAs with product-specific ones, such as "Shop the Chore Jacket", and make them buttons instead of small underlined links.
- 3. Add star ratings or a review count to each product, since the email already leans on social proof.
- 4. Move the "Better Prices" block up or fold it into the hero, and give it a number.
- 5. Space out sends. A third email in one day weakens the open rate for all three.
- 6. Match the header copy to the subject so the email reads as one idea.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Real customers. Real outfits.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 Everlane looks we love on you`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ballet flats, oxfords and denim, styled by customers like you.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See the pieces our customers wear on repeat.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-built MJML template sent through a click-tracking domain (`trk.send.everlane.com`). The QA run flagged 2 link-probe failures, both on relative redirect targets, and several header checks that can't be confirmed because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Two probe failures, both with the same signature.** The tracked links resolve to a relative `Location` (`/pages/...`) with no host:
  - "Sign Up for Texts" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3Z60TRS7FR2CQG9XD7GN12A_22` → `/pages/sms-signup?_kx=…&bxid=…`
  - "Everlane Logo" → `https://trk.send.everlane.com/l/01M3Z60TRS7FR2CQG9XD7GN12A_0` → `/pages/womens?_kx=…`
- **Likely a QA-tool limitation, not confirmed as a broken link.** Relative redirects are valid per RFC 7231, and browsers resolve them. The error `unknown url type` comes from the probe client failing to resolve a relative URL. The probe never recorded the final destination, so the real landing status is unverified.
- **Possible real issue.** If the tracker emits a host-less `Location` for these two links, resolution depends on the client's base URL. A relative `Location` on a different domain than the destination could misroute. This needs a manual `curl -I` on both links.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 32 links were probed; 20 were skipped (footer, social, utility).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** One image is flagged: `https://trk.send.everlane.com/o/01M3Z60TRS7FR2CQG9XD7GN12A`. The `/o/` path indicates the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is minimal. It is still worth adding `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Empty title.** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace. This is minor for email, but it affects some web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **No issues found in the visible portion of the source.** It has the Outlook conditional comments, VML/Office namespaces, viewport meta, mobile media queries at 480px, and a `#outlook a` fix. Link colors are forced to `#000` for all link states, which prevents default blue-link overrides.
- **Font loading.** The template uses `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)`. `@import` is stripped by Gmail and several other clients, but the stacks fall back to Arial/Helvetica, so this is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated HTML contains no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`). The `_kx` and `bxid` parameters are Klaviyo's per-recipient tracking identifiers, populated as intended.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Everlane sends through Klaviyo, which normally adds both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. Nothing in the evidence shows a failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither appears in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for it. This is not confirmed either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters were seen in the visible link evidence.** Attribution appears to rely on Klaviyo's `_kx` and `bxid`, plus `cm_fl…` (truncated; presumably `cm_flow_id`) parameters. This is acceptable if the tracker adds UTMs at redirect time, but that can't be confirmed from the truncated data.
- **The logo links to `/pages/womens`**, not the homepage. Combined with the unresolved redirect in §2, landing alignment is untested.
- **The SMS signup link targets `/pages/sms-signup`.** The final destination was not probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on `…_22` and `…_0` to confirm the redirect resolves to an absolute `https://www.everlane.com/pages/...` with HTTP 200. Then fix the QA prober to resolve relative `Location` headers against the request URL (`urljoin`) and avoid false FAILs.
2. Capture the raw RFC 822 headers directly (bypassing the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Re-grade compliance after that.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and set a meaningful `<title>`.
4. Probe the unsubscribe link and check for UTM parameters on the final landing URLs after redirects. Re-run on the untruncated HTML to check the footer address and unsubscribe markup.
5. Treat the 38% pass rate as inflated by tooling artifacts: both FAILs are likely probe issues, and most WARNs are relay-capture gaps. Only the missing alt on the pixel is a confirmed source-level finding.
