# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened Samsung's homepage as a guy who literally just traded in his Galaxy S24 Ultra last launch cycle, and the first thing I get is a full-screen cookie consent wall. Nothing else. No hero. No Galaxy S25 hype. No trade-in offer. Just a modal asking me to "Configure," "Accept All," or "Decline All." Behind it I can barely make out what looks like the camera module of a Galaxy device, but that's it. Samsung is one of my most-visited sites and this is what they lead with.

## What's working

- **Samsung wordmark** is instantly recognizable at the top — brand ID is clear even inside a modal
- **Camera module peek** in the background is faintly visible and hints at a Galaxy device, which is at least the right product category for me
- **Cookie modal itself is clean** — three clearly labeled options, no deceptive dark-pattern layout (Configure / Accept All / Decline All are all equally prominent)

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent wall** blocks 100% of the homepage content before a single shopping signal can land — this is the entire above-the-fold problem
- **Zero trade-in hook, zero Galaxy S25 hype, zero offer** visible — Samsung's biggest revenue lever (annual upgrade cycle buyers like me) gets nothing
- **No loyalty or Samsung Rewards callout** — I have points and I don't know if there's anything worth spending them on without dismissing the modal first
- **No product imagery that registers** — the blurred camera array behind the dialog is aesthetic filler, not a conversion signal

## Recommendations

- **Geo-target the cookie banner**: serve it as a bottom-of-screen slim bar (EU-compliant) rather than a full-screen takeover — let the hero breathe immediately on load
- **Pin a trade-in CTA to the hero** the instant the modal is dismissed: "Get up to $X for your Galaxy S24 — upgrade now" lands directly for annual traders like me
- **Surface Samsung Rewards balance or a member offer** in a sticky header element, so returning logged-in users feel recognized the moment the page loads
- **Make the Galaxy S series the unmissable hero** — the camera module tease behind the modal is the right product, wrong execution; go full-bleed, copy-forward, with a pre-order or trade-in deadline
