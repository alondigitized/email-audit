# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Man, I literally cannot see the homepage because a cookie consent wall is blocking everything. What I *can* tell is that Samsung is running some kind of phone hero behind the modal — I can make out a multi-lens camera array and a dark purple gradient that looks very Galaxy S-series — but the brand experience out of the gate is a hard wall of legal text. For a company whose entire identity is "tech that just works," making me fight a consent modal before I see a single product is a rough first impression.

## What's working

- **Samsung logo placement** — clean, visible at top even through the modal; brand recognition is instant
- **Modal design itself** — the three-button layout (Configure / Accept All / Decline All) is unambiguous and GDPR-compliant without being hostile
- **Background teaser** — the glimpse of that camera array behind the modal is doing work; it tells me "there's something premium behind this wall" and makes me slightly more willing to click through

## What's weak

- **The cookie wall kills everything** — this is the entire above-the-fold experience. Zero product, zero offer, zero brand story visible
- **No urgency or hooks** — I can't see a trade-in promo, a Galaxy launch countdown, nothing. For a guy who trades in annually at launch window, this is a miss
- **Hero copy is 100% hidden** — I have no idea what Samsung is pushing right now. S25 Ultra? Galaxy Ring? Tab S10? No clue
- **No loyalty/Samsung Rewards callout** — I'm exactly the returning customer they should be greeting with a "Welcome back, here's your rewards balance" moment

## Recommendations

- **Auto-accept analytics on return visitors who've already consented** — if I've been to samsung.com before, don't wall me again on every visit; this is a solvable session/cookie problem
- **Compress the modal** — shrink the legal text block and move the buttons higher so at least the hero headline and CTA peek above it; even 20px of visible hero copy changes the experience
- **Add a trade-in hook to the hero** that's visible the second the modal clears — "Trade in your S24 Ultra. Get up to $800 off S25 Ultra" is the exact copy that would make me tap immediately
- **Surface Samsung Rewards balance** in a sticky top bar so returning logged-in users see their points the moment they land
