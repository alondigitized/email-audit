# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I hit samsung.com is a giant cookie consent modal eating the entire screen. There's a Galaxy phone peeking out behind it — multi-camera array, looks like an S-series — but I can't read a single word of the actual homepage. Samsung's pushing what looks like the S-line hardware, which is exactly my world, but I have to dismiss a wall of GDPR legalese before I get anywhere near it.

## What's working

- **The hardware peek is enticing.** The camera module visible behind the modal is unmistakably Galaxy S-tier — multi-lens array, elegant gray-navy finish, and what looks like an S Pen tucked in beside it. Even at 20% visibility, I recognize my ecosystem immediately.
- **Modal layout is clean.** "Accept All" is the dominant dark blue button — Samsung's brand blue — so the path of least resistance is clear. No dark-pattern tricks where "Decline" is hidden or grayed out.
- **Samsung logo is immediately present.** Zero brand ambiguity.

## What's weak

- **The modal nukes the entire above-the-fold experience.** Nothing of the actual homepage — hero copy, product headline, offers, navigation — is readable. I'm stuck at a tollbooth before I can see the store.
- **No urgency or offer is readable.** If there's a launch promotion, a trade-in bonus, or a pre-order deal live right now, I have no idea. I came here ready to check the trade-in window for the next S-series drop and got a privacy lecture instead.
- **Zero navigation visible.** I can't tap to Phones, Galaxy AI, Accessories — nothing. The modal has locked out the top nav entirely.
- **The consent text is dense and long.** Four lines of small-print legalese before the buttons. I'm tech-native so I'll hit "Accept All" without reading, but it's a friction wall for no reason.
- **No loyalty hook visible.** As someone who trades in annually, I want to see my Samsung account, my upgrade status, or a "Members get early access" badge immediately. None of that is reachable behind this modal.

## Recommendations

- **Compress the cookie banner to a slim bottom bar.** One line of copy + "Accept / Manage" — every major tech brand does this. The current full-screen modal is an anti-pattern that kills first impressions before the actual page has a chance.
- **Surface a trade-in or pre-order CTA immediately post-dismiss.** The second I accept cookies, the hero should hit me with whatever the current S-series launch story is — price, trade-in value, or Galaxy AI headline. That's what I came for.
- **Pin a Samsung Account / Galaxy Club login badge in the top nav.** Even behind the modal, if the nav were visible, a "Hi Sam — your upgrade is ready" badge would keep me on the page.
- **Show the hardware upfront, not as a blurred background prop.** The phone I can barely see behind the modal looks great — make it the whole hero, with the headline reading over it clearly, not buried under a consent wall.
