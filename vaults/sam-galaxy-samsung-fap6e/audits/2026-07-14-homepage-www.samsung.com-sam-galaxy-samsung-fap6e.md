# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land on samsung.com is a cookie consent modal eating the entire viewport — not a hero, not a device launch, not a Galaxy Unpacked teaser. The brand feels premium just from the wordmark and the glimpse of what looks like a Galaxy Unpacked event graphic peeking out behind the wall of legal text, but I can't actually experience the homepage until I dismiss this gate. Frustrating start for a returning loyal customer who just wants to check what's new.

## What's working

- **Clean modal rendering** — the cookie dialog itself is readable, the three-button layout (Configure / Accept All / Decline All) is unambiguous, no confusing dark-pattern button sizing between Accept and Decline
- **Samsung wordmark** — immediately recognizable; I know I'm in the right place
- **Galaxy Unpacked teaser** — barely visible behind the modal, with what looks like a sparkle-effect graphic. For someone like me who pre-reads launch details twice before committing, that's enough to spike my curiosity about what's dropping

## What's weak

- **The modal IS the above-the-fold experience** — the entire first viewport is a consent wall. As a returning Galaxy loyalist, I've been on this site dozens of times; Samsung should be surfacing my trade-in window and the next launch, not legal boilerplate
- **No skip or "proceed without accepting" affordance** — the hierarchy pushes "Accept All" visually (blue, primary button) without an obvious neutral path, which reads slightly coercive
- **Zero brand storytelling above the fold** — the cookie text is the only copy I can read; Samsung's voice is completely absent
- **Galaxy Unpacked is teased but illegible** — I can see the graphic and the label but can't read any copy or dates. A returning customer like me checking for pre-order window info gets nothing actionable

## Recommendations

- **Persist cookie consent state** — I've visited before; Samsung should remember my preference (or use a smaller banner, not a full modal takeover) so loyalists aren't re-gated on every visit
- **Push "Galaxy Unpacked" above the consent wall** — even a slim sticky ticker above the modal saying "Galaxy Unpacked | Pre-order opens [date]" would keep me engaged while I deal with the consent UI
- **Flip the button visual weight** — make "Configure" or "Decline All" visually equal to "Accept All" to avoid the bait-and-switch feel; as a tech-native I notice that immediately
- **Surface trade-in value teaser in the modal footer** — one line like "Already a Galaxy owner? See your trade-in estimate →" costs nothing to add and speaks directly to the annual-upgrade loyalist
