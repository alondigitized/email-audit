# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Samsung — first thing I see is a cookie wall that takes up my entire screen. Like, I know you need consent, but it's blocking what looks like it might be a Galaxy S or Z series launch hero underneath. This is not the vibe for someone who pre-ordered his last two phones at midnight. The brand looks premium from what I can glimpse — dark backdrop, camera module shot — but I cannot actually experience the homepage right now.

## What's working

- **Samsung wordmark** in the top-left — bold, recognizable, immediately tells me I'm in the right place
- **Modal UI is clean** — three clearly labeled buttons ("Configure," "Accept All," "Decline All") with good contrast; the "Accept All" button pops in Samsung blue, which feels on-brand
- **Device imagery bleeding through** — I can see what looks like the multi-lens camera module of a Galaxy flagship in the lower half; that's bait that makes me want to hit "Accept All" fast

## What's weak

- **The cookie wall obliterates the entire viewport** — as a returning Galaxy loyalist I'm here to see the new S or Z launch, not read GDPR copy
- **No "skip" or condensed version** — the legal text runs six full lines before the buttons, which is a wall of grey on mobile
- **The actual hero, headline, offer, and CTAs are 100% invisible** — I'm scoring a ghost page
- **No personalization signal before the wall hits** — a logged-in Samsung account holder like me should get a lighter touch here

## Recommendations

- **Implement a compact consent banner at the bottom of the screen** instead of a full-page modal — one strip, two buttons, done. Every second the hero is hidden is a second I might bounce.
- **Surface the hero headline above the fold even with the modal active** — dim the background page but let the product name and CTA be legible so I know what I'm unlocking.
- **Detect returning Samsung account sessions** and either pre-accept or surface a condensed consent flow — I've already consented on my Galaxy phone, this should carry over.
- **Add a visible teaser** (e.g., "New Galaxy S25 Edge — see what's next") behind the dimmed overlay to give me a reason to tap "Accept All" immediately rather than "Configure."
