# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Samsung greets me — a card-carrying Galaxy loyalist — with a full-screen cookie consent wall before I can see a single product. Behind it, all I can barely make out is what looks like a dark purple Galaxy phone and its multi-lens camera array. Right now, www.samsung.com reads less like a flagship tech brand and more like a legal department running a website.

## What's working

- **Cookie modal button design** — "Configure," "Accept All," and "Decline All" are clearly labeled, high-contrast, and tap-friendly on mobile. No dark pattern hiding the decline option.
- **Background product glimpse** — the purple-toned Galaxy phone camera module peeking out behind the modal at least signals this is a premium Samsung device page. As a loyalist I recognize it immediately.
- **Brand mark** — Samsung logo is cleanly visible at the top even through the modal.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal** — the single biggest problem. 100% of the above-the-fold experience is a legal wall. I came here to look at the Galaxy S25 launch or check trade-in deals, and I'm reading about web beacons instead.
- **Zero product context** — I can't see a headline, a hero CTA, a current campaign, or even a category nav. Nothing tells me what Samsung is selling or why I should care today.
- **No loyalty/My Samsung hook** — I'm a returning customer who trades in every year. A "Welcome back, Sam" or "Your Galaxy Upgrade awaits" moment would kill it here. Instead I get GDPR boilerplate.
- **Wasted hero real estate** — that camera module background looks sick, but it's 80% obscured. The mood is premium; the execution is buried.

## Recommendations

- **Minimize the cookie banner to a bottom strip** — a slim "We use cookies. Accept / Customize" bar keeps the legal box checked without torching the entire above-the-fold experience.
- **Surface the Galaxy trade-in CTA immediately after consent** — I'm here in the launch window. A "Trade in your Galaxy S24 — get up to $XXX off the S25" hero with a hard deadline would convert me on the spot.
- **Add a "My Samsung" or loyalty greeting module** — returning users who are signed in should see their upgrade eligibility or rewards balance the moment the modal clears.
- **Let the camera module breathe** — that background image is genuinely beautiful hardware porn for a loyalist like me. A full-bleed version with a two-word headline ("Own the Night.") and a single CTA below would be far more impactful.
