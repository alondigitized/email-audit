# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I hit samsung.com is a full-page cookie consent wall — not a hero, not a Galaxy S25 Ultra, not a trade-in deal. The brand is Samsung so I know who they are, but the homepage is doing zero work for me right now because a legal modal is sitting on top of everything. Underneath I can barely make out a teaser graphic with some sparkle-star iconography — looks like an event or product tease — but I literally cannot read it. This is not a welcome mat, it's a bouncer.

## What's working

- **Modal renders cleanly** — "Configure," "Accept All," and "Decline All" are clearly labeled, no overlapping text, buttons are full-width and tappable on mobile.
- **Partial event tease below the fold** — I can just make out a date stamp ("July 22, 2026 | Live on samsung.com") and a sparkle-star graphic, which piques curiosity. Something is launching. As a Galaxy loyalist, that catches my eye even through the wall.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie wall on a mobile screen** — this is the entire first experience. Samsung is a brand I trust, I'm not reading six paragraphs of cookie legalese on a 6-inch screen before I can see what they're selling.
- **Zero commerce content above the fold** — no product, no price, no trade-in offer, no Galaxy branding beyond the wordmark. A returning Samsung customer deserves better than a legal gate as a homepage.
- **Three-button modal with no urgency-bypass** — there's no "X to dismiss" or "Continue without accepting." I have to make a deliberate legal choice before I can see anything. That's friction I shouldn't have to think about just to browse.
- **Hero content completely blocked** — the sparkle graphic behind the modal looks like it could be a product reveal or a Galaxy Unpacked-style event, but I have no idea. That's a missed hook.

## Recommendations

- **Float the modal as a slim bottom banner instead of a full overlay** — let me see the hero while the consent options sit at the bottom. Every other premium tech brand has figured this out.
- **Add a quick-dismiss ("Continue" or "Close") option** — give me a path forward without forcing a binary legal choice. GDPR compliance doesn't require blocking the entire viewport.
- **Expose the event date and CTA above the modal layer** — if July 22 is a live launch event, that teaser should be above the consent wall, not buried under it. Event countdowns are exactly the kind of urgency hook that keeps a Galaxy loyalist from bouncing.
- **Pre-populate "Accept All" as the highlighted default** — if Samsung is going to keep the modal, at least make the primary action visually dominant with a blue fill (it already is) and reduce visual weight on "Configure" so users aren't slowed down by decision fatigue.
