# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened Samsung.com and immediately got wall-blocked by a cookie consent modal — can't see the hero, can't see the nav, can't see any offers. Behind the dialog I can barely make out what looks like the multi-lens camera array of a Galaxy phone in a dark navy gradient, which is at least on-brand. This is not a homepage experience; it's a legal gate.

## What's working

- **Modal renders cleanly** — buttons are legible, text is clear, nothing is broken or overlapping
- **Brand wordmark** ("SAMSUNG") is visible at the top of the modal, so I at least know I'm in the right place
- **Background hint** — the blurred camera-module background suggests a Galaxy device hero is underneath, which would be dead-on relevant for me once I can actually see it

## What's weak

- **Full-page modal on first load** — the entire homepage is blocked; there is zero shopping content above the fold
- **Cookie copy is dense** — three lines of legalese in small type before I get to any buttons; this is the first thing a Galaxy loyalist like me reads on samsung.com
- **No dismiss-without-action option** — I have to make an active privacy decision before I can even browse; that's friction I wasn't expecting on a retail homepage
- **Background hero is completely illegible** — whatever Samsung spent on that camera shot, it's invisible to me right now
- **No urgency, no offer, no nav** — if there's a Galaxy S launch pre-order window or trade-in deal running right now, I have no idea

## Recommendations

- **Implement a banner-style consent notice instead of a full-screen modal** — a persistent bottom bar with "Accept" and a settings link lets the page breathe and the hero load immediately
- **Pre-render the hero and nav underneath the consent layer** so it's visible and creates pull; I'm more likely to accept cookies if I can see what I'm accepting them for
- **Reduce cookie copy to one sentence + a link** — "We use cookies to improve your experience. [Learn more]" with two buttons is all that's needed legally in most jurisdictions
- **Add a soft "Continue without accepting" text link** so loyalists who are privacy-conscious (hi, it's me) don't feel trapped before they've seen a single product
