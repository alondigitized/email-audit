# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a cookie wall over a Fold8 launch — annoying, but at least what's behind it is exactly my wheelhouse. Samsung's dropping the full trifecta right now: Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Fold8, and Flip8, Galaxy AI badge and all. This reads like a flagship launch site, not a general storefront, which for someone who trades in every year at the launch window is the correct posture.

## What's working

- **"The all-new Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra | Fold8 | Flip8" headline** — clean, confident, no fluff. Tells me exactly what dropped.
- **Galaxy AI badge next to the product names** — that's the differentiator I care about vs. iPhone, and it's front and center, not buried.
- **"Unlock exclusive benefits" + Buy button pairing** — implies pre-order perks are one tap away, which matches how I actually shop (I read the pre-order bonus fine print twice before committing).
- **Foldable hero image** — showing both the Fold and Flip open/closed in hand is the right visual for a foldable launch, it's the actual hook.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal eats the whole screen** — "Configure / Accept All / Decline All" is the first and only thing I can actually interact with. On a launch day, that's a bad first impression.
- **No price or trade-in number anywhere visible** — I read pre-order bonus details twice before buying, and there's nothing here to read yet. "Unlock exclusive benefits" is a placeholder for an offer, not an offer.
- **Hero talent doesn't read as "for me"** — hands with painted nails holding the phones; fine as a product shot, but zero lifestyle signal for a 32-year-old guy deciding whether to trade in again.
- **Nav is just icons (search/cart/profile/hamburger)** — no visible path to "compare trade-in value" or "Galaxy Watch/Buds" bundle, which is exactly the kind of cross-sell that keeps me in the ecosystem.

## Recommendations

- Kill the cookie modal's visual weight — shrink it to a bottom banner so the Fold8/Flip8 hero isn't fully obscured on first load.
- Put an actual number under "Unlock exclusive benefits" — trade-in credit ($ off), pre-order date, or Galaxy AI feature callout, since vague benefit copy doesn't convert someone who researches before buying.
- Swap or add a second hero frame with someone holding/using the Flip8 one-handed in a real setting (commute, gym, desk) — give it a "someone like me" moment instead of just a studio product shot.
- Add a visible "Trade-in estimate" or "Compare Fold8 vs Fold7" link near the Buy button — that's the exact decision point for an annual upgrader like me.
