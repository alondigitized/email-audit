# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Samsung.com and got a cookie wall first, then a big, clean foldables hero: "Available now – Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra | Fold8 | Flip8". It looks like a confident first-party brand page, and the product photography is premium. But on this first screen I see no offer, no button, no trade-in hook and no member perk, so nothing tells me what to do next.

## What's working

- **Foldable hero photo:** three Fold/Flip devices in hand show the colors, the camera bump and the Flip8 cover screen. That sells the hardware to someone who already knows what he wants.
- **"Available now" in Samsung blue:** it says the launch window is open, which is when I trade in.
- **Product lineup in one line:** "Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra | Fold8 | Flip8" tells me the whole lineup instantly.
- **Cookie modal buttons:** "Decline All" has the same weight as "Accept All", which I appreciate.
- **Header icons:** search, cart, account and menu are standard and easy to hit with a thumb.

## What's weak

- **No CTA on the hero:** there's no "Buy now", "Pre-order" or "Trade in" button, so I don't know where to tap.
- **Missing trade-in and bonus messaging:** I read pre-order bonuses twice and trade in every year. Nothing about trade-in value, storage upgrades or credits is visible.
- **Hero text placement:** the copy is small and sits mid-right, far from the phones. It's easy to miss behind the dimmed overlay.
- **Cookie modal:** it covers the top of the hero and greys out the whole screen. The "By continuing… you consent" wording also sits awkwardly next to Accept/Decline buttons.
- **Navigation:** it's a hamburger only. I can't see Phones, Watches, Buds or Tablets from here, even though I own all four.
- **Hero imagery:** hands only, with no face and no lifestyle context. It's pretty but generic.

## Recommendations

- Add a filled "Pre-order / Buy Fold8" button and a "See trade-in value" text link directly under "Available now" in the right-hand hero space.
- Put one line of offer copy under the model names, for example "Up to $X trade-in credit + free storage upgrade", with a visible end date.
- Show a slim quick-links row under the header (Phones, Watches, Buds, Tablets, Deals) so my Galaxy ecosystem is one tap away without opening the hamburger.
- Shrink the cookie modal into a bottom sheet so the hero and its message stay visible on first load.
