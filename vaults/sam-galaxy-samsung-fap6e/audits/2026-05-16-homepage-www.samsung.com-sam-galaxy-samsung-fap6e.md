# Homepage snapshot · www.samsung.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I hit samsung.com is a full-page cookie consent wall — not a Galaxy S25, not a trade-in offer, not a launch countdown. The actual homepage is almost entirely buried behind legal copy and three buttons. Behind the modal I can barely make out what looks like the camera array of a Galaxy phone, which is exactly the kind of dramatic product shot I'd normally be here for. This is a premium tech brand that's immediately leading with compliance friction instead of product desire.

## What's working

- **Samsung wordmark** — clean, immediate brand recognition. I know where I am.
- **Background camera array shot** — the partial glimpse of a Galaxy phone's multi-lens camera system behind the modal is genuinely compelling. It's the right product hero for me; I just can't get to it.
- **Modal renders cleanly** — three well-spaced buttons, legible font, no broken UI. At least the compliance layer isn't also a mess.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie wall** — this is a conversion killer. The entire above-the-fold experience is legal friction. I came here to look at Galaxy S25 Ultra specs, not GDPR copy.
- **Zero product information above the fold** — I'm a loyalist who visits samsung.com during launch windows. Samsung has no idea I've bought five Galaxy phones in a row; there's no returning-customer signal, no trade-in banner, nothing.
- **"Configure" button is the first CTA** — leading with the most complex consent option is UX backwards. Most users just want to click through; burying "Accept All" as the second button adds unnecessary cognitive friction.
- **No urgency or seasonal hook visible** — I can't tell if there's a Galaxy S25 launch window, a trade-in promo, or a pre-order deadline. Everything is hidden.

## Recommendations

- **Compress the consent modal to a bottom banner** — show the actual homepage hero and let me see the product while I decide on cookies. Apple and Google both do this; Samsung can too.
- **Surface the trade-in value calculator above the fold** once the modal clears — loyalists like me trade in annually and that calculator is a direct conversion tool. Make it the first thing I see.
- **Lead "Accept All" first** in the button stack — lower cognitive load, faster path to the actual site.
- **Pre-load a personalized returning-visitor hook** (even via first-party cookie) showing my Galaxy model and the upgrade path — something like "You're on Galaxy S24 Ultra. See what's new in S25 Ultra."
