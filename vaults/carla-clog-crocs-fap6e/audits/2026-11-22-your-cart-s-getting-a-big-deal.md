# Your Cart's Getting a Big Deal
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean Black Friday send with a subject that promises something the email never delivers. "Your Cart's Getting a Big Deal" reads as an abandoned-cart nudge. The body is a generic broadcast: 60% off select styles, kids' clogs under $25, and Buffalo Check under $40. There is no cart, no product the reader left behind, and no personalization. A subscriber who opens it expecting their cart will feel misled.
- The creative itself is solid. The pale blue hero with "60% OFF / SELECT STYLES" and a black pill CTA is easy to read. The kids' tile with the huge "$25" is the strongest visual in the email. Against the brand history, this is the seventh Black Friday send in about a week. It follows "Up to 60% off + an extra 25% off? 👀" and "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT STYLES" on Nov 21. It drops the extra 25% stack, so it is cleaner but no fresher.
- Verdict: good layout and clear offers, undermined by a misleading subject and heavy fatigue. It would earn a 6 on the layout alone, but the subject mismatch and the cadence pull it down.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "60% OFF / SELECT STYLES" in large black type on pale blue, with a visible "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill and three clogs below.
- The "kids' clogs under $25" tile has a big, price-forward "$25" that the eye lands on immediately.
- Each lower module pairs one product story with one specific price point ("under $25", "under $40") and a clear button.
- The footer states the deadline and terms: the offer ends Sunday, November 29th at 11:59pm MT.
- The black "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" bar sits at the top without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The subject says "Your Cart's…" but nothing in the email references a cart or a saved item. The promise and the content don't match.
- "DON'T WAIT – DON'T WAIT – DON'T WAIT" in the black banner is filler, and it is repeated three times.
- The two lower CTAs both read "Shop All Doorbusters". They are identical, generic, and not tied to kids' clogs or Buffalo Check. The hero button says "Black Friday Deals", which is a third label for the same destination.
- "Exclusions Apply." appears directly under the hero CTA, so the fine print is front and center. The "Free shipping on qualified orders" claim adds another qualifier.
- The holiday Buffalo Check module has no sizing, rating, or reviews, and it shows "select styles" only as a small red tag.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the content, or make the email match the subject. If this is a true cart-abandon send, show the shopper's actual item. If it is a broadcast, drop the cart language.
- 2. Replace the three-times "DON'T WAIT" with a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11/29".
- 3. Give each CTA its own specific label: "Shop Kids' Clogs Under $25" and "Shop Buffalo Check Under $40".
- 4. Lead with one offer. Seven near-identical Black Friday sends in a week is too many, so use this send to introduce one new angle, such as kids or gifting.
- 5. Add a social proof line, such as a star rating or "bestseller", under each product module.

- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off ends Sunday — your sizes are going`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Buffalo Check under $40. Free shipping on qualified orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters end Sunday 11/29. Kids' clogs start under $25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a standard responsive, MSO-aware template. The only confirmed code defect is a malformed closing tag after the open pixel. Compliance and authentication can't be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers, and the HTML source was truncated before the body links and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup (confirmed):** the open-tracking pixel ends with a stray closing tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10207&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching open tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and may trip strict parsers or sanitizers.
- **Third-party audience pixel missing alt (QA-confirmed):** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` has no alt attribute. It is a tracking image, so the accessibility impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Unverified links:** the QA run skipped the HTTP probe on 17 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`). Link validity and redirect destinations are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The visible markup is sound:
  - Viewport meta and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present.
  - Outlook DPI and PNG settings sit in an `[if mso]` block.
  - There is an Apple data-detector override and a Gmail `u + .email-body` hack.
  - A 639px mobile breakpoint is defined.
- `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. Screen readers and web-view tabs would get more from the subject line.
- The `@font-face` `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. The fallback font stack is not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- Image alt text, heading structure and link text in the body are not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The body was truncated, so this applies only to the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may be stripping or not capturing them, so this is unconfirmed, not a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers at the original receiving mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `email@crocs-email.com` and the tracking domain is also `crocs-email.com`, which is consistent. Alignment itself cannot be confirmed from the data provided.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. All 17 CTA and tracking links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects and were not resolved, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to 60% off for Black Friday" preheader are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel and add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
2. Inspect the raw original message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. If they are present at origin, treat the QA warnings as relay artifacts.
3. Follow the redirects on the 17 tracking links to verify destinations, UTMs and the deal landing page. Use a redirect-following probe, since the current one skips these domains.
4. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, not the truncated source, to cover alt text, footer compliance and merge tokens.
5. Replace the generic `<title>` with the subject line.
