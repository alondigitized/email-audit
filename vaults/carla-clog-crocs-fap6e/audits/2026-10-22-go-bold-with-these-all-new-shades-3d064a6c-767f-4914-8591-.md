# Go Bold with These All-New Shades
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- Two emails are stacked here, and they compete. The top is a blue Crocs Club member offer, "Get $30 Off $100". Below it is a teal "Deep Dive" color launch. The subject, "Go Bold with These All-New Shades," promises the color story, but the first thing a reader sees is the discount. The discount block is also the stronger half. It has a clear number, a white pill button and a visible member tag.
- The Deep Dive hero is attractive. Teal clogs on a teal tile, with pearl and shell Jibbitz, look cohesive. But "new hues that go deep" is a vague headline, and the email only introduces one new color. "Go Bold" and "all-new shades" overpromise. Deep Dive is a muted teal, so "bold" is a stretch.
- The fine print shows the offer ends tonight, Thursday, October 22 at 11:59pm MT. The body never says so. It is Crocs' fourth or fifth send in about a week, after the NBA, multi-buy and workwear emails, so this is the quietest of them. Overall it is a competent, on-brand send that isn't sharp. It is a 5 or 6, in line with the brand's recent run of 6s.

## What's working

- "Get $30 Off $100" is a concrete, easy-to-read dollar-off offer in large white type on blue.
- The "Crocs Club Member Exclusive" tag gives the offer a loyalty angle.
- The hero is cohesive. Teal on teal with pearl charms shows off the color and the Jibbitz upsell together.
- "Shop Classic Clog" is a specific verb-plus-noun CTA, and the black button stands out against the pale background.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the four category pills at the bottom are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The offer and the color story compete. The member promo sits above the hero, so the subject's "shades" promise isn't what you see first.
- The deadline is buried in the footer. Nothing visible says "ends tonight," so a real urgency lever goes unused.
- "Exclusions Apply." sits right under the CTA, which feels like a hedge on the main offer.
- "Shop and Save" is generic and doesn't say where it leads.
- The copy is thin. "Rich teal that stands out in the best way" has no price, no sizing and no reviews.
- Only one color is shown, so "shades" is plural but the email delivers one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the member block, for example "Ends tonight" above or beside "Get $30 Off $100". This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Pick one lead. Either put the hero first and move the offer to a slim banner, or rename the subject around the $30 off.
- 3. Change "Shop and Save" to "Shop Deep Dive Clogs" and link it to that color.
- 4. Show more than one new color, or change "shades" to "shade" in the subject.
- 5. Add social proof or a price, such as a star rating or "$X Classic Clog."
- 6. Move the "Exclusions Apply" disclosure down to a small footnote.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Meet Deep Dive, our newest Classic Clog color`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member-only: $30 off $100, plus a new teal Classic Clog. Ends 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Deep Dive just dropped. Add pearl charms and use your $30 off tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) template. It has a responsive layout, a preheader, and an Outlook/Apple/Gmail client-hack block. The source has one malformed tag and one unlabelled third-party pixel. The three automated warnings are mostly capture limitations, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so the body links and footer weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tag (confirmed):** the open-tracking pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>`:
  `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10202&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`
  `</custom>` is not a valid element. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Unverified links:** the QA check skipped 19 tracking links, which all point to the click-redirect domain. It did not probe them, so their destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** a Demdex (Adobe Audience Manager) beacon is embedded:
  `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`
  It fires on open, in addition to the first-party SFMC open pixel. This is a second tracking and data-sharing call that depends on image loading.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (confirmed by QA):** the Demdex `<img>` (`…/event?d_dpid=196107…`) has no `alt` attribute. It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- **Document title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. This has minor impact in email.
- **No other issues found in the visible source:**
  - `lang="en"`, `xml:lang`, the viewport meta and `format-detection` are present.
  - The MSO and VML namespaces and the `OfficeDocumentSettings` block are correct.
  - `x-apple-data-detectors`, the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack and the 639px media query are all in place.
  - The `TT Crocs` `@font-face` rules use `font-display: swap`. I couldn't check the fallback font stack because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. The Demdex `d_dpuid` value is a resolved ID. The rest of the body wasn't available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** QA notes the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". This is unconfirmed. It cannot be treated as a sender defect from this capture.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** the same caveat applies, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, so it is worth verifying against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay. The sending domain is `crocs-email.com`.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be confirmed. All 19 links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, and QA skipped the probe. The preheader says "Now available: the Classic Clog in Deep Teal", and I couldn't check whether the landing page matches it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the SFMC open pixel in the template.
2. Add `alt=""` to the Demdex pixel. Consider whether a second open-tracking beacon is needed alongside the SFMC pixel.
3. Re-run the QA with the raw RFC 822 message, not the AgentMail relay copy. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com`).
4. Resolve the 19 redirect links with a follow-redirects check. Confirm the final URLs, their UTM parameters, HTTP 200 status and that the landing page matches the Deep Teal Classic Clog offer.
5. Re-audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and any merge tokens.
