# New Harry Potter™ collection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-theme launch send. The Harry Potter Classic Clog in Gryffindor red sits in a maroon hero with a Platform 9¾ ticket, Hogwarts Express and Chocolate Frog Jibbitz charms. The eye lands on the shoe first, and the gold "All Aboard" block with a black "Shop Harry Potter™" pill follows naturally. It's on-brand and good for fans.
- It carries no price offer at all. The only concrete discounts are "Give 25%, Get 25% Off" in the footer and a vague "free shipping on qualified orders" banner. It also lands the day after "The Classic Ballet is back!" (Nov 4) and "Disney holiday styles are here ⛄" (Nov 3). That's a third collab launch in three days with no incentive, so it reads as catalog rather than a reason to act now.
- Judgment: a well-executed product reveal that depends on fandom pull. It will do fine with Harry Potter fans and little for everyone else.

## What's working

- The hero is strong. Red clog, gold trim, charm details and a legible Harry Potter wordmark make the theme obvious in a second.
- A single dominant CTA, "Shop Harry Potter™", is a black pill on gold with high contrast.
- The copy is short and themed: "All Aboard" and "The Hogwarts Express has arrived" fit the collection.
- The secondary row (Clogs, Jibbitz™ Charms, Sale, Sandals) gives a clear path for people who don't want the collab.
- The layout is clean, with no visible render bugs or broken images.

## What's weak

- There is no offer or urgency. Nothing says why to buy today, such as a limited drop, low stock or a deadline.
- The free-shipping banner is hedged with "qualified orders", and the footer fine print says the promotion can change.
- The "Shop Sale" button is buried in the secondary row with no discount named, which wastes a possible hook.
- The best incentive, Refer a Friend 25%, is in the footer, below where most people stop reading.
- The hero shows one colorway and one angle. There's no price, no reviews and no sizing or availability detail.
- This is the third consecutive collab launch with no price incentive, which risks fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to act now: "Limited-edition drop, while supplies last" or early access for app or loyalty members. Collab scarcity is the strongest lever this email isn't using.
- 2. Put the price in the hero block ("Classic Clogs from $X") and name a Jibbitz bundle deal.
- 3. Move the 25% referral up next to the primary CTA, or give the sale button a number ("Sale: up to 50% off").
- 4. Add a few product tiles (clog, charm pack, kids' size) with prices, so the second scroll has something to click.
- 5. Replace the "qualified orders" banner with a concrete threshold, such as "Free shipping over $X".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with generally sound responsive scaffolding: a viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, Gmail and Apple client fixes, and a preheader. The confirmed problems are seven `http://` image sources, a malformed closing tag, and one image with no alt attribute. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the capture path, and the HTML supplied was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** 24 links were skipped as tracking redirects on `click.crocs-email.com`. Their destinations could not be probed, so I can't confirm they resolve. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=10210&bmt=0` is well-formed with `width="1" height="1" alt=""`.
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel `<img ...>` is followed by a stray `</custom>` closing tag with no matching opener. Parsers ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and probably a template leftover.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` is an Adobe Audience Manager beacon. It has no alt attribute (a QA warning). It also carries a `d_dpuid` identifier in the query string, so that identifier goes to a third party on open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (7):** these load from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - `9c36c0ef-...png` (App Store)
  - `13246762-...png` (Google Play)
  - `c4df71ec-...png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-...png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-...png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-...png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-...png` (YouTube)

  Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or that block mixed content, may drop them. These are the footer app badges and social icons, and all of them have alt text.
- **Missing alt:** the demdex beacon `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is a tracking pixel, so the practical impact is minor, but screen readers may announce the URL. Add `alt=""`.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, `xml:lang`, a viewport meta tag, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, and the `u + .email-body` Gmail hack are all present. The `<title>` is the generic "Crocs".
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` loads via `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify one.
- **Not verified:** the HTML was cut off, so I can't confirm alt text on the hero or product images, or table `role="presentation"`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the supplied portion. The rest of the body is not visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `email@crocs-email.com`, and the click and image hosts are on the same `crocs-email.com` domain, which is good for alignment. I can't confirm the pass or fail result from this data.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't assess. All 24 CTA links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects that were not followed, so destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image URLs to `https://`.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon `<img>`.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Don't treat the current warnings as confirmed defects.
5. Make the QA tooling follow the `click.crocs-email.com` redirects (or log the resolved URLs). Then UTMs, landing-page status and redirect-chain length can be audited.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, postal address, merge tokens and image alt text.
