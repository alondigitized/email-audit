# Last day for up to 50% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the "Final Hours!" urgency version of the same 50% off sale Crocs already ran on 9/7 ("You scrolling? Up to 50% off right now!") and 9/4 ("Up to 50% off! No overthinking necessary") — same pink hero, same "Up to 50% Off / Select Footwear and Accessories" copy, same stacked-offer structure with a second "Buy 2, Take 30% Off" clearance block underneath. The one real addition is the "FINAL HOURS!" urgency flag and the legal fine print confirming the offer expires today, September 9th at 11:59pm MT — that's a legitimate, credible deadline and the strongest thing this send has going for it.
- Everything else is a rerun. Two competing percentage-off CTAs stacked vertically again, no hero product story, no new creative — just the deadline reframe on tired inventory. Compared to the genuine product launches this week (Dylan Platform Slide on 9/8, Echo Element on 9/6), this is the laziest version of the discount well, saved only by real urgency.

## What's working

- "FINAL HOURS!" plus the fine print's exact 11:59pm MT cutoff gives this send a genuinely credible, time-bound reason to act today.
- Free shipping banner is visible immediately at the top with no scrolling.
- Product grid (red clog, black slide, pink flip, ice-blue clog) is clean and renders without bugs — no overlaps, no broken images.
- "Shop the Sale" CTA button is high-contrast and unambiguous against the pink background.

## What's weak

- Two stacked discount blocks (50% off, then 30% off clearance) compete for attention again — the exact structural flaw called out in the 9/7 and 9/4 sends, unresolved three sends later.
- No specific hero product — just a generic 4-image grid, so there's nothing to click toward beyond "the sale" broadly.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears under both CTAs with zero detail on what's excluded, which reads as buried fine print.
- Subject and hero repeat prior sends nearly verbatim; a subscriber who opened last Monday's identical-structure email will feel no reason this one is different beyond the deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the second "Buy 2, Take 30% Off" block entirely for this final-hours send — a true last-chance email should have one offer, one CTA, one countdown, not two competing discounts diluting urgency.
- 2. Add a visible countdown timer or "Ends tonight at 11:59pm MT" directly under the hero headline instead of burying the exact deadline in the fine print — that's the one lever that would most improve click-through given urgency already tested well elsewhere in the "Final Hours" framing.
- 3. Swap the generic 4-up grid for one hero product (e.g., pull the Dylan Slide or Echo Element from this week's launches) with a price callout, since "last day" urgency pairs better with a specific item shoppers already have their eye on.
- 4. Replace "Exclusions Apply" with a one-line specific ("Excludes new arrivals and collabs") to reduce the bait-and-switch feel.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale closes at 11:59pm MT — grab yours before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clogs, slides & clearance — all gone by midnight`
