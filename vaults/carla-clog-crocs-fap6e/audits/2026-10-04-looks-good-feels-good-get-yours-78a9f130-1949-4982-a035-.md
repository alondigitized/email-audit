# Looks Good. Feels Good. Get Yours.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in three days built around the same 30% off + free shipping promo, after Oct 2's "Limited-Time Offer" and Oct 3's "Reminder". The Echo II lifestyle shot makes it look like a product campaign, but the offer is buried in the middle. Nothing in the subject or hero says there's a sale.
- The hero is a strong, on-brand street shot of the Echo II with a meme-style caption: "built for all-day comfort. styled for literally everything else." It's modern and scroll-stopping. But the first CTA, "Shop Echo II", sits below a very tall image and carries no price or offer. The real hook, "Take 30% Off + Get Free Shipping!", only shows up in the blue block after that. Anyone skimming the top half sees a shoe and a caption.
- Overall it's a decent but unfocused send. It splits the message between a single-product brand push and a sitewide sale, and it commits to neither. Given the Oct 3 near-copy, the subject also gives no reason to open this one.

## What's working

- The hero photo is bold and native-feeling, and the Echo II sole is clearly visible.
- The blue offer block is a clean, high-contrast headline with one pill button, "Shop the Sale".
- The four category pills (Clogs, Jibbitz Charms, Sale, Sandals) give easy secondary paths.
- The deadline appears in the fine print: "Offer ends Wednesday, October 7th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT".
- The "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" band adds a second incentive without competing with the main offer.

## What's weak

- The offer is below the fold. The 30% off headline sits roughly 1,000px down, behind a huge hero image.
- The hero and the sale block give two different CTAs ("Shop Echo II" and "Shop the Sale") with no clear priority.
- The "Exclusions Apply" line plus the fine print ("select styles only") undercut the "30% off your purchase" claim.
- The hero is a cropped legs-and-shoes shot with no price, no colorway name and no reviews, so there's little product detail to click on.
- The subject and hero never mention the sale, and the deadline is hidden in the footer. That's weak urgency for a send that follows two near-identical promos.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Overlay "30% off + free shipping, ends Oct 7" on the image, or put the blue block directly under the logo. Then the first screen carries both the product and the reason to buy now.
- 2. Put the deadline in the body and the subject ("Ends Wednesday"), not only the legal copy.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA. Make it "Shop Echo II – 30% Off" and demote the generic "Shop the Sale" pill.
- 4. Add product proof under the hero: price after discount, colorways or a star rating.
- 5. Space out the promo emails. A different creative angle, or skipping a day, would stop this reading as the third reminder.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Echo II is 30% off through Wednesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% off + free shipping ends Oct 7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Built for all-day comfort. Take 30% off the Echo II and ship it free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Wednesday at 11:59pm MT. Exclusions apply.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code is mostly sound. It sets a lang attribute, has a viewport meta tag, uses Outlook/VML fallbacks and has a media-query block. The confirmed problems are seven images served over plain `http://`, a malformed closing tag around the open-tracking pixel, and a third-party pixel with no `alt` attribute. The header-based compliance checks were inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup on the open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?O7WPP2DKAUSEBAESYI4FHNW7PQ.10198&d=10198&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` has no opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but strict parsers and some sanitizers may mishandle it.
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped 25 links because they sit on the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com` (a Salesforce Marketing Cloud pattern). Destination health and redirect chains were not checked, so don't treat them as confirmed good.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` is an Adobe Audience Manager call. It fires on open and adds a second tracker domain. Some clients and privacy proxies block it, so open data may be incomplete. This is a data-quality note rather than a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (7):** all are on `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. The assets are:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` (App Store badge)
  - `13246762-…png` (Google Play badge)
  - `c4df71ec-…png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)

  Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or proxied with mixed-content warnings. When that happens the footer app badges and social icons fall back to alt text, which is present on all seven.
- **Missing alt attribute:** the demdex `event` image has no `alt` at all. It is a hidden tracking pixel, so the impact is minor, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. The fallback stack wasn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- **Other checks:** no issues found with the doctype, `lang`/`xml:lang`, viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the Outlook DPI settings, or the hidden preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`). The HTML was truncated, so tokens in the lower half can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported as not found. QA itself says AgentMail may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `crocs-email.com` is distinct from the brand domain, so alignment is worth checking in the raw message.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these fall in the truncated section, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 25 CTAs go through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects that were skipped, and the destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible. There is no evidence of a problem and none of correctness.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch all seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` assets to `https://`. This is the only confirmed fix with a visible effect.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` in the raw message, because the relay may have stripped them.
5. Resolve a sample of the tracked links to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through, then re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, merge tokens and fallback fonts.
