# Scary-good deals! 😱
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer Halloween send. A deep maroon panel carries "31% Off, Select Styles", a white "Shop Spooky Deals" pill and a red Baya clog. The eye lands on the number first and the button second. That's the right order, and nothing breaks.
- The weakness is how generic it is. The subject, "Scary-good deals! 😱", says nothing about what's on sale. Inside, the only product is one red clog, and the CTA goes to a deals page. The offer also has a "Select Styles" qualifier and an "Exclusions Apply" line. The actual deadline, Sunday Nov 1 at 11:59pm MT, appears only in the legal text at the bottom.
- This follows the 10/29 Peanuts, 10/28 Fuzz and 10/26 Grinch product-story sends, and the Crocs "Extra 50% Off" run of 10/23–10/25 before them. A 31% discount is a visible step down from that 50% run, and the "Muahahahahaha" pun can't fully cover that. It's a competent last-day Halloween clearance email, not a standout one.

## What's working

- "31% Off" is the largest element on the page, so the offer registers in about a second.
- The white "Shop Spooky Deals" pill contrasts strongly against the maroon and sits directly under the offer.
- The seasonal theme is on time and consistent: "Happy Halloween!", the dark red panel and the pun all point the same way.
- The red clog on the red background is a bold, on-brand image, and the layout renders cleanly with no broken images.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" top banner adds a second benefit without competing with the hero.

## What's weak

- The subject doesn't name the 31% offer or the product, so it gives no reason to open beyond the pun.
- The deadline (Sunday, Nov 1, 11:59pm MT) is buried in the footer fine print. There's no urgency in the hero.
- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" are vague, and the single clog doesn't show what's actually discounted.
- The hero image is oversized and empty. It shows one shoe with no price, no colorways and no link to a specific product.
- The footer is heavy relative to the content. There are no reviews or social proof, and no other products.
- It's the daily cadence again, after six consecutive sends, and this one is a weaker offer than the 50% run.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the deadline into the hero, for example "Ends Sunday night". That is the single biggest lift, because it turns a pun into urgency.
- 2. Put a 3–4 product grid with sale prices under the hero, for example "Baya Clog $X → $Y", so the discount is concrete and each tile links to a product page.
- 3. Replace "Select Styles" with named categories such as "Clogs, Sandals & Jibbitz". If exclusions are limited, say what they are.
- 4. Make the CTA more specific, for example "Shop 31% Off Clogs".
- 5. Add a social-proof line, such as a review count or "best-selling clog".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud send with a sound responsive shell (viewport, `lang`, MSO conditionals, preheader). The QA run found 0 confirmed issues. Several checks were inconclusive because headers weren't captured and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 16 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`). Their destinations and status codes are unverified, so I can't confirm any are broken.
- **Malformed markup:** the open-pixel `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10207&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt="">` is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag. This is invalid HTML, though it is unlikely to affect rendering in most clients.
- **Third-party pixel:** an Adobe Audience Manager beacon, `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`, fires on open in addition to the SFMC open pixel. This is a second tracking call. Many clients and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will preload or block it, so open data from it may be unreliable.
- **Identifier in URL:** the beacon passes a `d_dpuid` value in its query string. That is a per-recipient identifier going to a third party. It's worth confirming it is covered by the privacy policy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the demdex beacon `<img>` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). Since it is a tracking pixel, it should have `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Positive checks:** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`) and the Outlook `sup` fix are all present. Mobile styles break at `max-width: 639px`.
- **Truncated source:** the document is cut off, so I can't check body-content alt text, table roles, or link text. The `<title>` is the generic "Crocs".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found (WARN). The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified omission.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found (WARN), with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the click-tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is consistent with aligned branded sending.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated portion, so they can't be verified.

Cannot be assessed. Links are wrapped in SFMC redirects and weren't followed, so the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible. The preheader ("31% off select styles") can't be checked against a landing page.
