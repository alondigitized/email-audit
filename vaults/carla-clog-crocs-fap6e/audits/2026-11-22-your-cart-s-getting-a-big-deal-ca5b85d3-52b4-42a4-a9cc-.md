# Your Cart's Getting a Big Deal
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Black Friday catalog send from Crocs in about a week, and it is a competent but crowded one. The hero is strong: a pale-blue field, "60% OFF + AN EXTRA 25% OFF" and a black "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill over three clogs. Below it the email splits into the Grinch clog, kids' clogs under $25, cozy styles from $25, and an Afterpay block. That is four offers competing for one click.
- The subject "Your Cart's Getting a Big Deal" is the weak link, and it is the same subject as the Nov 22 send already in the history. It reads as an abandoned-cart nudge, but the body is a broadcast with no cart or personalized content. The "DON'T WAIT – DON'T WAIT – DON'T WAIT" banner adds urgency, but the footer contradicts it. One disclaimer says the offer ends Sunday, Nov 29, and another says it ends Sunday, Nov 21, which has already passed. A reader who spots that will doubt the deadline.
- Overall: strong creative and clear price anchors, hurt by a misleading subject, a repeated send and conflicting end dates.

## What's working

- The hero puts "60% OFF" in large black type on a pale-blue field, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- A black pill CTA ("Shop Black Friday Deals") sits directly under the offer.
- The kids' clogs under $25 and cozy styles from $25 blocks give concrete price points, with the "$25" large in the image.
- The lime Grinch band ("A mean one. A merry deal.") brings seasonal, playful brand voice and a specific 45% + 25% offer.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" top bar is visible immediately.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a cart-style personal message, and the body is a generic broadcast.
- The footer legal copy lists two conflicting end dates, Nov 29 and Nov 21. The Nov 21 date is already past.
- Both "Shop All Doorbusters" buttons use identical copy for different categories (kids and cozy), and neither names a product.
- The stacked "60% off + extra 25%" math is unexplained, and "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the CTA.
- The email has four or five competing blocks, including a TikTok Shop strip and Afterpay, which dilutes the primary action. There are no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject so it matches the content, or make the email actually cart-aware. A broadcast should not use cart language, and Nov 22 already used this exact subject.
- 2. Fix the footer dates so one accurate deadline appears, ideally in the hero ("Ends Sun 11/29").
- 3. Cut to one hero offer plus one secondary. Drop the TikTok strip, and move Afterpay to a small footer line.
- 4. Rename the CTAs to be specific, for example "Shop Kids' Clogs Under $25" and "Shop Cozy Lined Clogs".
- 5. Show the effective price on one hero clog (for example "Classic Clog $__ after extra 25%") so readers don't have to do the stacking math.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids' clogs under $25 — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `60% off + extra 25%: ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grinch Lined Clogs are 45% off, plus an extra 25% today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on qualified orders. Cozy styles start at $25.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) build with a 1x1 open pixel, a Demdex audience pixel and click-wrapped links. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links could not be inspected. The header-level compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive rather than failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 24 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect health and landing-page status are unconfirmed.
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener. Browsers will ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and a template defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** QA flagged an `<img>` from `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` (Adobe Audience Manager). It is a second tracking beacon alongside the ExactTarget open pixel. Its `d_dpuid` value is embedded in the URL, so it is a per-recipient identifier.
- Open tracking with `bmt=0` relies on image loading, so it is unreliable under Apple MPP and image blocking. This is informational only.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the Demdex pixel:** the QA warning is confirmed (`alt` absent on the `demdex.net/event` image). It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`. The ExactTarget pixel already has `alt=""`.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` does not describe the message. This is minor.
- **Good practices present:**
  - Outlook VML/MSO conditionals are included.
  - The `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tags are set.
  - The Gmail hack and responsive classes at a 639px breakpoint are present.
  - Web fonts use `font-display: swap`.
- `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` are present.
- Body image alt text and `role="presentation"` on layout tables could not be assessed because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both headers were not found. The QA tool states the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified violation**. Check the raw message from a direct-to-inbox send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The same relay caveat applies. The sending domain is `crocs-email.com`, which matches the click and open tracking domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), a good sign for alignment.
- **Footer:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 24 links are wrapped in ExactTarget redirects, and the QA probe skipped them, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. The preheader (`extra 25% off for Black Friday`) makes a promo claim that cannot be matched to a landing page without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel.
2. Add `alt=""` and explicit dimensions to the Demdex pixel image.
3. Re-run the audit against the raw message, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Resolve the 24 click-tracked URLs with a redirect-following probe to check the final status, UTM parameters and promo-code landing page.
5. Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, body image alts and any merge tokens can be audited.
6. Make `<title>` descriptive (for example, the subject line).
