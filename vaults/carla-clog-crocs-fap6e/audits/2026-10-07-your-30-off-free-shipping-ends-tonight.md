# Your 30% Off + Free Shipping Ends Tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing beat of a week-long 30% off + free shipping campaign. It's clear and loud. The pink hero leads with "ENDS TONIGHT!", then "Take 30% Off + Get Free Shipping!" in heavy black type. A concrete one-day price sits beneath it ("Crafted Vegan Suede Clogs = $39.99"), followed by a black "Shop the Sale" pill. The eye lands on the offer immediately.
- The weakness is cadence. This is the fifth send in six days on the same promo, after Oct 2 "Limited-Time Offer", Oct 3 "Reminder", Oct 4 "Looks Good. Feels Good." and Oct 6 "Hurry! 30% Off + Free Shipping Won't Last". The "ends tonight" urgency is believable because the footer gives a hard deadline (Wednesday, Oct 7, 11:59pm MT). A subscriber who has seen four near-identical sends will discount it, though.
- The email also undercuts itself. The $39.99 suede clog is the only specific product, but the CTA goes to "the sale" rather than the clog. A big stretch of empty pink separates the button from the product shot. Solid but forgettable: a 6-7 send.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT!" sits above a huge, high-contrast headline, so the offer and deadline read in about a second.
- The "Today Only" $39.99 price on a named product gives the percentage-off promo a concrete anchor.
- The black pill button is high-contrast, has a clear verb + noun, and sits right under the offer copy.
- The hard deadline in the footer (Oct 7, 11:59pm MT) backs up the urgency.
- The suede clog photo is clean and on-brand, and the pink and tan palette is distinct from the earlier yellow and cyan sends.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth near-identical promo in six days, which wears down the urgency.
- About 200px of empty pink sits between the button and the product shot, and the product isn't linked or labeled with a CTA of its own.
- "Exclusions Apply." is tiny, and the real conditions (select styles only, no stacking, while supplies last) are buried in grey fine print at the bottom.
- The top bar, "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS," repeats the hero and hedges it ("qualified").
- The Refer a Friend, app download and social blocks are all the same weight, so the footer is cluttered and competes after the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the product. Put the suede clog directly under the headline, and add a "Shop the $39.99 Clog" button on it. Keep "Shop the Sale" as a secondary link.
- 2. Close the empty pink gap. Tighten the layout so the offer, product and button all fit in the first mobile screen.
- 3. Surface the conditions next to the offer ("Select styles. Free shipping on orders $X+"), so the offer reads as honest.
- 4. Add a live countdown, or at least "Ends 11:59pm MT" in the hero, instead of only the footer.
- 5. Collapse the footer: keep one secondary block (Refer a Friend) and move the app and social rows below it. Across the campaign, send fewer reminders and vary the creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: Vegan Suede Clogs $39.99`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off ends at 11:59pm MT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on qualified orders. Crafted Vegan Suede Clogs are $39.99 today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends at midnight Mountain. Pick your colors before sizes run out.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built on a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) template with sound responsive and Outlook scaffolding. The confirmed problems are mixed-content `http://` images, a malformed tracking-pixel tag, and an unlabeled Demdex beacon. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified because the AgentMail relay did not capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The open pixel markup is malformed: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?IZM4OSM2NTBEFC2QGRAEZPKLHA.10205&d=10205&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` closing tag has no opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- The Demdex beacon `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is a third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel with no `alt` attribute. This is a QA WARN. The `d_dpuid` value in the URL is an identifier unique to the recipient.
- 20 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified. The truncated HTML did not show the final landing URLs.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven images load over `http://`, all on `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - App Store: `9c36c0ef-333c-4e6c-bec8-9e0ac799d475.png`
  - Google Play: `13246762-1111-4174-bca8-2f84a6bf62a5.png`
  - Find Your Store: `c4df71ec-309f-44f4-b10a-27bb864eeb78.png`
  - Instagram: `e18949ff-2d29-4a62-9176-a67132186a90.png`
  - Facebook: `80f68cd8-0090-4258-b202-50e422ad1b5c.png`
  - TikTok: `51d8144e-818a-4a00-a812-5cf0429b2429.png`
  - YouTube: `1187ef58-07fe-4985-8349-93e338d86694.png`
  
  Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail may block or rewrite these requests, which would leave broken icons or "not secure" warnings. This is a confirmed issue.
- The Demdex tracking image has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). Because it is a hidden pixel, the practical accessibility impact is low.
- The hidden preheader uses `font-size:1px; display:none !important`, and the `<style>` block also hides `div.preheader`. This is redundant but harmless.
- The `<title>` is the generic "Crocs". This is minor.
- No other rendering defects were confirmed. The `viewport`, `format-detection`, Outlook `mso` conditionals and the `mobileHide` and `mobileView` utilities are present.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible source or the subject. The truncation means the rest of the body could not be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found (QA WARN). The QA tool notes the relay may have stripped them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. If they are absent in the original message, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a tooling limitation, not a confirmed failure. The sender domain `crocs-email.com` is consistent with the click and image domains.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
The destination URLs were not visible in the truncated source, and the 20 tracking links were not probed. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "30% off + free shipping" offer therefore cannot be assessed. No issues are confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Serve all seven social and app-store images over `https://`. The same host supports it, so this only requires changing the scheme.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the Demdex pixel.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. The relay may be dropping them, so test with a direct inbox capture.
5. Resolve the 20 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe, so the landing URLs, UTM parameters and offer alignment can be checked.
6. Pass the full, untruncated HTML to the audit so the footer, unsubscribe link and remaining merge tokens can be reviewed.
