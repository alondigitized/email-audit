# Black Friday Deals Start Now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a broad Black Friday kickoff, and it abandons the "2 for $50" drumbeat Crocs ran Nov 10–15. Yesterday's "Black Friday Early Access Ends Tonight!" was a single loud offer. Today's email is a catalog: up to 60% off, a free blanket, animal prints from $30 and kids' clogs under $25. The "Early Access ends tonight" urgency has turned into "deals start now," which reads as a clean handoff to the public sale.
- The hero is strong. A black fleece-lined clog sits on a red gift box with ribbon, and "UP TO 60% OFF" is the biggest type on the page. The black "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill sits directly under it. The lower modules are well built, with big price callouts ($30, under $25) and bold black buttons.
- The email tries to say too much. Four offers compete, and the free-blanket module is the weakest. It has a $100 threshold and a pile of caveats, and its copy ("A Post-Gifting Treat") is odd given Christmas is weeks away. Overall it's a solid, competent send, but it has less focus than the single-offer emails that came before it.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "UP TO 60% OFF" is the dominant type, with a CTA directly below and a seasonal gift-box visual.
- The free-shipping bar at the top is visible immediately.
- The price-anchored modules ($30 animal print, kids' under $25) give concrete, scannable offers.
- Button styling is consistent: black pills with white text, easy to tap.
- The footer includes an end date ("Sunday, November 29th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT") and a free-shipping disclosure.

## What's weak

- "Select styles" and "Exclusions Apply" under the hero weaken the 60% claim. "Up to" plus exclusions reads as hedging.
- The blanket module needs a $100 purchase, and its fine print says it "may end at any time." That is a soft, low-trust offer.
- "A Post-Gifting Treat" is off-season. It reads as a pre-written template line.
- Two buttons per module ("Shop Animal Print Doorbusters" and "Shop All Doorbusters") repeat the same destination and dilute the CTA.
- The "TikTok Shop Black Friday: Now on your FYP" strip is a dead-end banner with no clear action and no visible link.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer plus one secondary module. Drop the blanket or move it to a separate send. This would lift the email most.
- 2. Use a single CTA per module and send both "Shop All Doorbusters" buttons to one place near the bottom.
- 3. Show the deadline (Nov 29) near the hero, not only in the footer. Right now there's no urgency above the fold.
- 4. Put the real discount range in the hero ("20–60% off") instead of leaving it to "select styles" and "exclusions apply."
- 5. Make the TikTok strip tappable with a clear action, or remove it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Doorbusters are live: kids' clogs under $25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus animal prints from $30 and a free blanket at $100.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on qualified orders. Sale ends Nov 29.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) send with a mostly sound HTML skeleton. Confirmed defects are one malformed tag and a missing `alt` on the Demdex pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture through the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed from the data. The HTML source is truncated, so body links, footer and unsubscribe markup were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup on the open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` has no opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and can trip strict parsers.
- **Third-party pixel without alt:** QA flagged an `<img>` to `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`, which is an Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) call. It carries a per-recipient `d_dpuid` and an open-event flag. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so it isn't announced by screen readers. This pixel did not appear in the truncated source, so confirm where it sits in the full HTML.
- **22 tracking links not probed:** QA skipped them because they use click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`). Destination status and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Good:**
  - `lang` and `xml:lang="en"` are set.
  - The viewport, `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tags are present.
  - Outlook (VML/MSO) namespaces and a `sup` fix are included.
  - The Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`) is present.
  - Mobile breakpoint at 639px with `.mobileHide`/`.mobileView` utilities.
- **Hidden preheader:** The preheader ("Score up to 60% off select styles.") is followed by a padding buffer of `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` entities, which is the standard technique.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same for every send. It is only minor, since the title rarely shows in email clients.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` for `TT Crocs` loads WOFF/WOFF2 from `media.crocs.com`. Support is limited to clients like Apple Mail and iOS Mail. The fallback font stack was not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.
- Image alt text for content images could not be checked in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `qs=` values in the open pixel are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed gap. Check the raw `.eml` or a direct-delivery seed before reporting it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so it is worth confirming.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `crocs-email.com` is a dedicated sending domain, so alignment is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Footer not reviewed:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. All visible links are redirects via `click.crocs-email.com`, and QA skipped probing them. The final destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing page match to the "up to 60% off" promise can't be assessed from the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` (and explicit dimensions) to the Demdex pixel.
3. Re-run QA against the raw message, or a seed inbox with full headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
4. Resolve the 22 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to check final status codes and UTM presence.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and content-image alt text can be checked.
