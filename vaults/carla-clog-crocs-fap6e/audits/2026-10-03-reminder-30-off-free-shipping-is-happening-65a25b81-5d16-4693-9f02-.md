# ⏰ Reminder: 30% Off + FREE Shipping is happening!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a near-copy of the 2026-10-02 send ("Limited-Time Offer: 30% Off + Free Shipping"). The hero is a cyan block with a heavy black headline, "There's Still Time: Take 30% Off + Get Free Shipping!", and a black "Shop the Sale" pill below it. The offer reads in about a second. Under the copy, a single black Baya clog sits against a "SALE" watermark.
- It's clear but flat. The "Reminder" framing is honest, because it's the same promo. But it's the second send in two days with the same discount and nearly the same headline. Nothing new gives the subscriber a reason to act now. The real deadline, Wednesday, October 7 at 11:59pm MT, is buried in the last line of the legal copy. The hero never uses it.
- Verdict: competent reminder, low-effort creative, and a missed chance to use the one thing that gives a reminder its power, a visible end date.

## What's working

- The offer and CTA are unmistakable: "30% Off + Free Shipping" in large black type on saturated cyan, with a high-contrast pill button.
- The hierarchy is clean: eyebrow, headline, subhead, button, product. The eye never wanders.
- The Baya clog cutout is crisp, and the oversized "SALE" watermark adds energy without clutter.
- The footer has a useful secondary hook ("Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!"), plus app badges and a store locator.
- The "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" banner at the top reinforces the offer before the hero loads.

## What's weak

- The deadline is only in the fine print. The hero says "today!" but the email ends October 7, so the urgency is vague and not credible.
- It's the same offer, with the same headline structure, as yesterday's send. That's cadence fatigue, not a reminder with new information.
- The CTA is generic ("Shop the Sale") and goes to a sitewide sale. The single clog in the image isn't called out, named, or priced.
- "Exclusions Apply" under the button and "qualified orders" in the banner both hedge the headline. Neither says which styles are excluded or what the shipping threshold is.
- The "Crocs" text on the shoe's sole sits oddly against the SALE watermark. The watermark's bottom row is also clipped at the hero edge. Both are minor.
- There is no social proof, no product name or price, and no sizing or fit info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date in the hero: "Ends Wednesday 10/7" or a countdown. That's the single change that would lift this email most. A reminder without a visible deadline is just a repeat.
- 2. Make the hero product specific. Name the Baya, show a before/after price, and point the CTA at that product or a curated collection, not the whole sale.
- 3. Change the CTA copy to "Shop 30% Off Clogs" or "Claim Free Shipping" so it says what the click gets you.
- 4. Replace "Exclusions Apply" with a short list of what's excluded, or a link to it, and say what "qualified" shipping means.
- 5. Add a reason to send it, such as a new color, a low-stock note on best sellers, or review stars, so it isn't a second identical blast.
- 6. Space promo sends further apart, or change the creative when the offer stays the same.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cart's still waiting. Sale ends 10/7 at 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping + 30% off best-selling clogs. Ends Wednesday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a sound responsive and Outlook-hardened `<head>`. Confirmed defects are limited to 7 images served over `http://`, one image with no `alt` attribute, and a malformed closing tag after the open-tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 20 tracking links on the `click.crocs-email.com` redirect domain were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Malformed tag after the open pixel.** The pixel is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?NGL7VBQPYDHUTGBZY3XSTPESNI.10198&d=10198&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` has no matching opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` is a second open-tracking beacon on a different domain. It has no `alt` (see section 3).
- **Unescaped ampersands.** The query strings use raw `&` (`&d=10198&bmt=0`) rather than `&amp;`. This is tolerated in practice but is not strictly valid HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **7 images use `http://`.** All are on `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`:
  - "Download on the App Store" (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - "Get It On Google Play" (`13246762-…png`)
  - "Find Your Store" (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Some clients and proxies block or warn on mixed content. These are footer images, so the effect is limited to the footer.
- **Missing `alt` on the demdex image.** Its `alt` attribute is absent, not just empty. For a tracking pixel it should be `alt=""` plus the width and height attributes the open pixel already has.
- **No other issues confirmed.** The viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection`, MSO conditionals, Gmail hack, `lang`/`xml:lang`, and preheader handling are correct. The source was truncated, so the body markup could not be checked.
- **Minor.** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. It is not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the truncated HTML. Personalization in the lower body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** Both are reported missing. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. This is unconfirmed, not a verified violation. Check the raw message headers at the sending MTA.
- **Authentication-Results.** It is not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The cause is the same relay limitation. The visible From domain, `crocs-email.com`, matches the click and image domains, which is consistent with a dedicated sending domain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither was visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 20 click links all go through `click.crocs-email.com` and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "30% Off + FREE Shipping" subject could not be checked. Resolving the redirects would settle this.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Images.** Serve the 7 footer images over `https://`. `image.crocs-email.com` should already support TLS.
2. **Markup.** Remove the stray `</custom>` tag. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel and escape `&` as `&amp;` in tracking URLs.
3. **Headers.** Capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from the original message rather than the relay copy, so compliance checks can return real results.
4. **QA tooling.** Follow redirects on `click.crocs-email.com` links in a sandbox. This would let the QA check verify UTM parameters and landing-page status codes instead of skipping them.
5. **QA pass rate.** The 57% pass rate is inflated by relay-related unknowns. Don't treat it as a deliverability score.
