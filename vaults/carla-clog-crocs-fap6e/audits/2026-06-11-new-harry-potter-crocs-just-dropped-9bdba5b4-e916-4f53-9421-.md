# New Harry Potter™|Crocs just dropped
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Crocs' Harry Potter™ Marauder's Map Clog launch email — a collab drop announcement built around one product. The creative is clean: the clog sits on what appears to be aged parchment (the Map), the body copy names the design and the Jibbitz charms, and the CTA is unambiguous. It's a better-executed collab launch than the June 10 LoveShackFancy send, which had no single clear product hero and landed at a 5/10.
- The gap this email can't close is commercial: there's no price anywhere, no urgency signal, and no discount. For an HP fan who's been waiting for this collab, the email will convert cleanly. For anyone else on the Crocs list, "There's More to Explore" reads like a headline from a generic brand content email, not a marquee product drop.
- The referral section at the bottom ("Give 25%, Get 25% Off") is off-brand for a collab launch — it interrupts the magic-and-mystery narrative the top half was building.

## What's working

- Subject line is precise and compressed: "New Harry Potter™|Crocs just dropped" names both brands and signals a new drop in 36 characters, well within mobile-friendly limits.
- Body copy earns its space — it names the specific product (Marauder's Map Classic Clog), the print detail, and the Jibbitz charm add-ons, giving an HP fan something real to evaluate.
- "Shop Harry Potter™|Crocs" is a crisp, unambiguous CTA that reinforces the collab brand equity without resorting to a generic "Shop Now."
- Email renders cleanly with no broken assets or layout issues.
- Differentiated send in the context of recent Crocs volume — this isn't another clearance email.

## What's weak

- No price anywhere in the email — a curiosity-click becomes a bounce when the product page is the first place a price appears.
- "There's More to Explore" is the weakest possible headline for a Potter collab drop; it buries the lede and would work for any product in any category.
- The referral program block ("Invite your friends to step into comfort together!") kills the narrative tone mid-email — it belongs in a separate drip, not inserted between the product hero and the footer.
- The hero product image is small relative to the email canvas; the clog is identifiable but doesn't dominate the way a limited collab deserves.
- No urgency or exclusivity signal — no "limited edition," no countdown, no "while supplies last." Collab drops historically have scarcity; none is surfaced here.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add the price (or a "starting at $X" line) beneath the CTA.** Without it, the email creates awareness but can't seal the click-to-buy loop. One number would immediately lift Click Likelihood.
- 2. **Rewrite the headline to invoke the Map.** "Mischief managed — the Marauder's Map Clog is here" is more on-brand than anything generic. The copy team did the work in the body; the headline needs to match it.
- 3. **Add a limited-edition or "First Access" badge to the hero block.** Collab drops feel special; lean into exclusivity even if stock isn't technically scarce.
- 4. **Cut or relocate the referral section.** It's jarring after an immersive HP story. If it must stay, move it to the post-purchase flow or a separate lifecycle send.
- 5. **Set a real preheader.** The free shipping banner is leaking as the preview text. A Hogwarts-flavored line would hold the theme all the way to the inbox.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Marauder's Map Clog has arrived — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Mischief managed: Harry Potter™ x Crocs just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The wizarding world on your feet — Marauder's Map Clog + Jibbitz charms`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited collab just landed. Shop the Harry Potter™ Clog + charm your pair.`

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header: not found.** QA notes this may be a relay capture artifact, but if genuinely absent from delivered headers, the email violates Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since February 2024 for senders >5,000/day). Gmail will suppress the unsubscribe UI; Yahoo may route to spam.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058): not found.** Required for one-click unsubscribe compliance at the same sender thresholds. Must accompany `List-Unsubscribe` with a `mailto:` or HTTPS POST endpoint.

**`Authentication-Results` header: not captured.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from this snapshot. Inspect a raw delivered copy directly (Gmail → Show original) to verify all three pass.

**DMARC alignment risk** — Sending domain is `crocs-email.com`; site domain is `crocs.com`. DKIM signing must use a selector under `crocs-email.com` aligned to the `crocs.com` DMARC policy (`p=` setting), or authentication will fail alignment. Verify with `dig TXT _dmarc.crocs.com`.
