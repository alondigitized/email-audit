# Haunt them in style. 🎃
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal brand-play send, and it works as one. The hero is a peach field of Halloween Jibbitz charms (spider, bat, skeleton dog, alien, pearls) with a chunky orange-outlined "haunt them in style" headline. The eye lands on it immediately, and it's the most fun creative Crocs has sent in this stretch. It's a clear break from the Oct 2–4 run of near-identical 30% off + free shipping blasts (the Oct 3 "Reminder" and Oct 4 "Looks Good. Feels Good." sends).
- The weakness is that it gives the reader no reason to click today. The only offer is "free shipping on qualified orders" in a thin top banner, with no discount and no deadline. Below the hero, five identical black pills ("Shop the Boo-tique," "Shop the Witching Wardrobe," and so on) have cute names that don't say what's behind them. The Hello Kitty and Friends blind-box module is the strongest product story, but it sits below the fold of the first screen.
- Verdict: good creative, soft commercial case. It's a pleasant palate cleanser after three days of discount fatigue, but I'd expect browsing clicks and not purchases.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and on-season, and the headline reads instantly against the peach background.
- The send breaks the Oct 2–4 pattern of repeated 30% off blasts, so it feels fresh in the inbox.
- The Hello Kitty and Friends Jibbitz blind-box module has a clear collectible hook ("Can you collect them all?") and a single dedicated CTA.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images, overlapping text or gaps.
- The "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" band offers a real price benefit, and the footer is tidy (app badges, store locator, preferences).

## What's weak

- There's no real offer. "Free shipping on qualified orders" is a thin banner with no threshold, and the footer fine print ("Shipping promotion is subject to change") weakens it.
- The five stacked "Shop the…" pills use cutesy names that tell me nothing about the product. They're equal in weight, so nothing is prioritized, and they eat a lot of vertical space.
- The body copy under "No Tricks. All Treats." is generic and has no product, price or reason to act now.
- The 25% referral deal is buried at the bottom, and no urgency is shown, even though Halloween is under four weeks away.
- The bottom row of generic nav pills (Clogs, Jibbitz, Sale, Sandals) dilutes the Halloween theme at the exact point a reader decides whether to click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete offer in the hero or directly under it, for example "Buy 3 Jibbitz, get 20% off" or "Free shipping, no minimum, through Oct 31." Without one, this email is just a catalog.
- 2. Replace the five pill buttons with two or three descriptive CTAs such as "Shop Halloween Charms," "Shop Spooky Clogs" and "Shop Hello Kitty Blind Boxes." Make the first one visually dominant.
- 3. Move the Hello Kitty and Friends collectibles module up, right under the hero. Give it a specific hook such as "3-pack mystery charms, $X."
- 4. Rewrite the body copy to name products and a deadline ("Costume-ready by Oct 31"), and promote the referral band higher.
- 5. Cut the generic bottom nav row, or move it below the footer links, so the Halloween focus holds through the whole scroll.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Halloween charms, skeleton clogs and Hello Kitty mystery packs. Free shipping on qualified orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a standard responsive layout. The confirmed defects are seven `http://` image sources, one malformed tracking-pixel tag and a third-party pixel with no `alt`. The unsubscribe, authentication and UTM checks can't be confirmed, because the HTML is truncated and the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Not probed:** 30 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the QA probe, so redirect destinations and link health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Malformed tag:** the open pixel is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?RIOQ4PJLI46U5DWCVPNFHR7WGA.10204&d=10204&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` has no matching opening tag. Browsers and clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup and may trip strict parsers.
- **Unescaped ampersands:** the same URL uses raw `&` instead of `&amp;` in the attribute value. This is minor and clients tolerate it.
- **Third-party pixel:** the Adobe Audience Manager pixel `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). Many clients block or proxy this kind of pixel, so the `c_emailopened=Yes` event won't fire for those recipients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`http://` images (7, all on `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`):**
  - App Store badge: `9c36c0ef-…png`
  - Google Play badge: `13246762-…png`
  - Find Your Store: `c4df71ec-…png`
  - Instagram: `e18949ff-…png`
  - Facebook: `80f68cd8-…png`
  - TikTok: `51d8144e-…png`
  - YouTube: `1187ef58-…png`

  Mixed-content blocking or proxy rewriting can make these footer images fail to load. All seven have alt text, so the footer still degrades to text.
- **Demdex pixel:** it is missing `alt`, as noted above.
- **Preheader and client handling:** the preheader div is hidden with `display:none`, and the `&#847; &zwnj; &shy;` buffer is present. The viewport, `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` metas are set, and the Outlook MSO conditionals are in place. No issues found.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` for TT Crocs is hosted on `media.crocs.com` over HTTPS, with `font-display: swap`. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or AMPscript tokens appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA flagged both as missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not preserve them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect**. Check the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox before concluding.
- **Authentication-Results:** it is absent because of the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `crocs-email.com` and the SFMC tracking subdomains are consistent with an aligned setup, but that can't be verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these fall in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

Unverified. The CTA hrefs aren't in the captured source, and the 30 tracking links were not followed. UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and redirect behavior all need a re-run with the full HTML and link resolution.

## 7. Recommendations

1. **Sender:** serve the seven footer images from `https://image.crocs-email.com/…`, since the host already supports HTTPS.
2. **Sender:** remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel and escape `&` as `&amp;` in the pixel URL.
3. **Sender:** add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
4. **QA pipeline:** capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay, or flag these checks as "unverifiable via relay" instead of WARN.
5. **QA pipeline:** resolve the click-tracking redirects to check UTMs and landing pages (section 6), and process the full HTML rather than a truncated copy.
