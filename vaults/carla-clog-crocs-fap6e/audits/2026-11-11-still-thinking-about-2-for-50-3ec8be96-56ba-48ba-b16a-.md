# Still Thinking About 2 for $50? 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer send, and it is the second 2-for-$50 email Crocs sent on Nov 11. "2 FOR $50" is the biggest type on the page. The white "Shop Early Access" pill sits right under it, and a hero Baya clog in army green fills the lower half. The eye goes offer, then button, then shoe.
- The problem is cadence and novelty. This follows "Skip the Line: 2 Pairs for $50" on Nov 10 and "2 for $50. Need We Say More? 👀" earlier today, with the same offer and the same 👀. "Still thinking about it?" is an honest nudge, but the body is a recolored copy of the earlier send, with nothing new to reward the re-open.
- The real deadline, Nov 14 at 11:59pm MT, is buried in the last line of the fine print. A reminder email should lead with that date. The email is competent but redundant.

## What's working

- "2 FOR $50" dominates the hero. The offer is unmistakable in about a second.
- The white pill CTA stands out against the muted green panel, and the copy is specific.
- The Baya hero shot is crisp, and the pine-needle and snowflake texture feels seasonal without clutter.
- The "Good deals deserve a second look" line fits the "still thinking" subject.
- The layout is tight and nothing is broken. The footer is tidy, and the black free-shipping bar at the top adds a second value hook.

## What's weak

- The Nov 14 deadline appears only in the footer fine print, so the urgency is invisible where it counts.
- "Don't sleep on this deal" and "Good deals deserve a second look" are generic filler.
- The hero shows one olive clog, which doesn't show what "select styles & colors" covers. There are no prices, no color options, and no second product.
- "Shop Early Access" is confusing when the same offer has been running since Nov 10, and nothing explains who has early access or to what.
- There is no social proof and no loyalty or member angle.

- 2. Show 3–4 styles or colors, or a "most-added-to-cart" row, so "select styles" becomes concrete.
- 3. Change the CTA to "Shop 2 for $50" or "Pick Your 2 Pairs".
- 4. Throttle the cadence. Space the three 2-for-$50 sends out, or give this one a new angle such as a countdown or a bestseller rail.
- 5. Add one proof point, such as star ratings or a "bestseller" tag.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound (viewport meta, Outlook conditional comments, dark `display:none` preheader, tracking via Salesforce Marketing Cloud). It has one malformed tracking-pixel tag and one third-party pixel without alt text. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and CTA hrefs could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup on the open pixel.** The SFMC open pixel is closed with a stray end tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10206&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opener and is invalid HTML. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers and HTML validators will flag it.
- **12 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them because they are on click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`). Their final destinations and HTTP status are unverified.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is a cross-domain tracker that fires on open. This is a data-sharing and deliverability consideration, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the demdex pixel.** QA flagged the `event` image (`demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107…`) as lacking `alt`. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip it. The SFMC pixel already has `alt=""`.
- **Generic `<title>`.** `<title>Crocs</title>` does not describe the message. This matters for web-view and "view in browser" rendering. Severity is low.
- **No other issues found in the visible source.**
  - `X-UA-Compatible`, `format-detection`, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present.
  - The Outlook `sup` fix and `@font-face` declarations use `font-display: swap`.
  - Mobile styles break at 639px and the Gmail and Apple data-detector overrides are in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `qs` and `d_dpuid` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header found.** QA marks this `[WARN]` and attributes it to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header found.** This is the RFC 8058 one-click header, and QA marks it `[WARN]` for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. QA says this is expected through the relay.
- **Not verifiable.** The unsubscribe link, sender postal address, and sender identification are in the truncated footer. The `From` address (`email@crocs-email.com`) is consistent with the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`.
- None of these three header warnings is a confirmed defect. They are gaps in the QA capture, and the headers should be checked against the original raw message.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. All links are wrapped in SFMC click-redirects, and the QA probe skipped all 12, so the destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible. No findings are reported.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the SFMC open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net/event` pixel.
3. Set a descriptive `<title>`, or accept the generic one if the web-view page is not used.
4. Check the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) directly from the source MTA or SMTP capture. That will settle the QA warnings.
5. Resolve the 12 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to record final URLs, status codes, and UTM parameters.
6. Capture the full HTML, including the footer, to verify the unsubscribe link and physical address.
7. QA tooling: the summary reports "3 warnings" but five `[WARN]` lines are listed. Reconcile the count.
