# Time’s almost up!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Extra 50% Off email Crocs has sent today — the earlier "wait… it's extra 50% off 👀" send already covered the same sale. This follow-up leans into urgency ("ONLY HOURS LEFT," "Ends tonight") with a clean, minimal layout: one hero product shot of two Classic Clogs, one CTA button, done. The creative is competent and renders without issues.
- The problem is the subject line. "Time's almost up!" creates urgency but tells you nothing — no offer, no category, no discount. A subscriber who didn't open the morning email has no idea this is a 50% off sale until they're already inside. That's a wasted opportunity in the inbox, where most people decide in under two seconds.
- The refer-a-friend module below the fold ("Give 25%, Get 25% Off!") is a nice addition that the morning email lacked, but it's buried far enough down that most openers won't reach it. Overall: mechanically sound, strategically thin — a second send of the same promotion deserves more creative differentiation to justify the cadence.

## What's working

- **Urgency is explicit in-body**: "ONLY HOURS LEFT" + "Ends tonight" lands immediately without scrolling — the time pressure is real and visible.
- **Offer is unambiguous**: "Extra 50% Off Sale Styles" in large bold type with a clear green "Shop the Sale" CTA leaves no confusion.
- **Clean visual hierarchy**: logo → urgency label → offer headline → product → CTA. The eye moves exactly where it should.
- **No render bugs**: free shipping banner, product image, buttons, and footer all render cleanly.
- **Refer-a-friend add**: "Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" is a differentiated value prop the morning send didn't have.

## What's weak

- **Subject is offer-blind**: "Time's almost up!" withholds the actual deal; a subscriber making a 1-second inbox decision gets urgency but no hook.
- **Second identical promo in one day**: the morning "wait… it's extra 50% off 👀" already ran this offer — this send adds fatigue without meaningful creative lift.
- **Hero product is generic**: two unbranded Classic Clogs with no specific colorway call-out, no name, no price point — nothing actionable for the style-curious shopper.
- **Referral module is stranded**: "Invite your friends" lives below the fold with no teaser in the hero section to pull attention down.
- **Free shipping banner is fine print**: "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" at the very top is easy to miss and never reinforced near the CTA where it would reduce friction.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the offer in the subject**: the current line creates urgency with no payload. Name the discount.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 50% off ends tonight — shop now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: Extra 50% Off sale styles`
- 2. **Write a preheader that closes the deal**: anchor the referral program or the free shipping so the inbox preview adds a second reason to open.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: refer a friend and you both get 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on qualifying orders. Offer ends at midnight.`
- 3. **Feature one specific product in the hero**: name a colorway, show a price-after-discount, link directly to that PDP. "Extra 50% Off" over a generic product shot doesn't pull the click the way a specific "$22 after discount" does.
- 4. **Tease the refer-a-friend block in the hero**: add a one-liner below the CTA button — "Know someone who'd love Crocs? Give 25%, get 25%." — so shoppers don't have to scroll to find it.
- 5. **Reinforce free shipping near the CTA**: move or echo the free shipping callout directly beneath the "Shop the Sale" button where it does the most work on click hesitation.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — not confirmed present:**

The QA relay did not capture this header. If absent from the actual SMTP envelope, Gmail and Yahoo will suppress the native unsubscribe button in the client chrome, and the send may be flagged under Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements. Verify at the ESP level, not just via relay capture.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header — not confirmed present:**

Required for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe. Gmail requires this for bulk senders (>5k/day). If missing at the ESP, fix at the template or ESP header-injection layer.
