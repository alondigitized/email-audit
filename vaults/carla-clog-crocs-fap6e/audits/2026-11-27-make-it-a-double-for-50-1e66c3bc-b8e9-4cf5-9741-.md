# Make it a double for $50
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "2 for $50" send in about a day, and it is the thinnest of them. Earlier today's "Black Friday Hot Deal: 2 Pairs for $50" had a dark ornament hero and three product blocks. This one has a lavender panel, a huge "2 for $50", and one black pill button. The offer is legible in under a second, and the layout is clean. But there is no product, no model, no price context and no reason to care, so it works as a reminder and not much more.
- The subject, "Make it a double for $50", is a decent pun that matches the hero. After nine-plus Black Friday sends this week, though, a subscriber has seen this offer repeatedly. The email gives no new reason to open or click. The fine print is also lighter than it should be, since "Exclusions Apply" sits under the CTA with no hint of which styles qualify.
- Judgement: a competent, low-effort echo of the morning send. It is clear but generic, and it adds fatigue without adding value.

## What's working

- "2 for $50" is huge, high-contrast white on deep purple, and it is the first thing the eye lands on.
- One CTA, "Shop Black Friday", is a bold black pill on a pastel field, so there is no competing action.
- The "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" top bar adds a second incentive without clutter.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlap, and the Crocs logo with its snowflake "o" keeps the holiday tone.
- The offer deadline (Nov 29, 11:59pm MT) is stated in the fine print.

## What's weak

- There is no product imagery at all. A footwear email with no shoes gives nothing to react to.
- The copy "Black Friday is here! These holiday savings are too good to miss!" is generic filler. It is also stale, since the earlier send today already said Black Friday was here.
- The deadline is buried in the fine print. The body shows no urgency cue.
- "Exclusions Apply." with no detail invites distrust. The fine print says the discount covers "select styles only."
- The CTA, "Shop Black Friday", is vague. It doesn't say what you are shopping for.
- The hero is purple and the footer is plain text, so the email feels sparse beside the richer morning send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add three or four hero products (Classic Clog, Echo, a Jibbitz bundle) with the "2 for $50" pricing shown, so the click has a destination.
- 2. Move the deadline into the body: "Ends Sunday, Nov 29." Time-bound copy is the only fresh thing you can offer on the third send of the same deal.
- 3. Rewrite the CTA to something specific, such as "Build Your 2-Pair Bundle."
- 4. Replace the filler line with a concrete benefit, such as "Pick any two eligible styles. Pay $50."
- 5. Name the eligible categories in the body instead of only "Exclusions Apply."
- 6. Spread these sends out, or give each one a distinct angle (gifting, kids, Jibbitz).

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard ExactTarget/SFMC build with a hidden preheader, MSO/VML scaffolding and a mobile media query at 639px. The only confirmed code defect is a malformed closing tag after the open-tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication findings are visibility gaps in the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender faults. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and link inventory could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no matching opener, which is invalid HTML. Most clients ignore it, but strict parsers and some sanitizers may mishandle it.
- **Unverified links:** the QA run skipped 12 tracking links (`click.crocs-email.com` redirects) and did not HTTP-probe them. Link validity and destinations are unconfirmed, not shown broken.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It is a second open-tracking call beside the SFMC pixel. It loads a third-party domain, and it has the alt issue below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the demdex pixel:** QA flagged the demdex image for missing alt text. Add `alt=""` to mark it decorative. This is minor, since it is a tracking pixel.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it is `Crocs` and does not describe the message. This has low impact in email.
- **Rendering support:** no other issues were found in the visible portion. The source has a viewport meta tag, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, an Outlook `sup` fix, MSO DPI settings, `lang="en"` and `font-display: swap` on the `TT Crocs` web fonts. The rest of the body was not visible, so I can't assess image alt coverage or table semantics.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. This is limited by truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found:** QA flagged both, but its own note says AgentMail may not capture these headers. RFC 8058 one-click support is therefore unconfirmed, not shown absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected limitation of the relay. The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is consistent with aligned sending.
- **Footer not visible:** the physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link could not be checked because of truncation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The visible source has no landing-page hrefs. All clicks are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects that QA did not resolve. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>` in the SFMC template.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon (or its injection template).
3. Capture the raw headers from the original message, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
4. Resolve the 12 `click.crocs-email.com` links with a redirect-following check (no click registration needed if using a seed). Verify final URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), the image alt inventory and merge-token checks.
