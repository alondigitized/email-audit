# TikTok made us do it 😏
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a trend-chasing send that leans on TikTok/FYP culture to push the "Crafted" camo clog line — no discrete offer, no dollar figure, just vibes and a "Shop Viral Styles" CTA. It's visually clean and the hero shot (camo swatch + cargo-pant model in camo Crocs with charms) is genuinely striking, more editorial than most of Crocs' recent discount-mechanic sends. But there's no product name, no price, and no urgency anywhere in the body — this is a pure awareness play, not a conversion play.
- Coming right after two strong creative sends (Café Slipper 8/19, Florence by Mills 8/18) and a month of discount-stacking before that, this fits the "trend justification" beat Crocs occasionally reaches for. It's fine as a brand-voice palate cleanser, but weak as a standalone commercial email — there's nothing here that would make a lapsed or price-sensitive shopper click today.
- Judgement: solid creative, thin commercial hook. Good for brand equity, bad for near-term conversion.

## What's working

- Strong, cinematic hero image — camo texture panel juxtaposed with a full outfit shot sells the "trend" narrative better than a flat product photo would.
- Subject line ("TikTok made us do it 😏") is on-brand, culturally current, and distinct from every other subject in the recent history.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, all four "Recommended for You" product tiles load correctly.
- Free shipping banner and app/social footer are present and consistent with brand standard.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere — "Going Viral For A Reason" copy and the CTA give zero price, no % off, no product name for the featured camo clog.
- "Recommended for You" grid is generic (Classic Clog, Baya Clog, Kids' Classic Clog, Miami Toe Loop Sandal) and doesn't reinforce the camo/viral story at all — it reads like a stock personalization block bolted onto unrelated hero creative.
- Weak visual hierarchy: the hero image dominates but the actual shoe (small, bottom-right of a tall image) is easy to miss on a quick scroll.
- "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" buried below the fold in the black footer block is the only real % figure in the whole email — it's not the featured offer, so it undersells itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the product and add a price/offer directly under the hero headline (e.g., "The Crafted Clog — camo, charms, $X" ) so there's something concrete to click toward, not just vibes.
- 2. Swap the generic "Recommended for You" grid for the actual Crafted/camo lineup (different colorways) to keep the story consistent top to bottom.
- 3. Crop or re-frame the hero so the shoe itself is more prominent — right now the outfit dominates and the product is an afterthought.
- 4. Surface the 25%/25% referral offer higher, or add a second CTA under the hero pointing to it — it's the strongest concrete number in the email and it's currently footer-buried.
