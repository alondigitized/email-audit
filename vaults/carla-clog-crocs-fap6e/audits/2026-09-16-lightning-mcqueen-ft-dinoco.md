# Lightning McQueen ft. Dinoco ⚡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine collab launch — a Cars/Pixar Lightning McQueen clog in Dinoco blue with a dimensional spoiler and racing-stripe hero art — and it's the third distinct licensed-collab drop this week after Yu-Gi-Oh! (9/10) and Jibbitz charms (9/15). The hero image does the heavy lifting: the crocs-as-race-car concept is instantly legible and the track-stripe background gives it real motion, which is more creative work than Crocs has put into most of its percentage-off sends this week.
- Where it falls down is everything after the hero. There's no price, no release date, no "limited edition" quantity signal beyond the vague "while supplies last," and the single "Shop Dinoco" CTA gets buried under four more generic category buttons and a referral banner before you even get to the footer. It reads like a fun asset dropped into the standard template without much thought for what happens after the click.
- Judgement: a strong creative concept, undersold by a thin, generic template — solid mid-week send, not a top performer.

## What's working

- The hero shoe genuinely looks like a miniature Lightning McQueen — spoiler, "95," Dinoco logo, and lightning bolt details are all visible and crisp.
- The Disney·Pixar/Cars badge in the top right immediately confirms license legitimacy and recognizability.
- The racing-stripe background reinforces the "Be the Speed" headline visually instead of just as copy.
- "Shop Dinoco" CTA button is clean, high-contrast, and unambiguous.
- No render bugs — image, badge, and text are all crisp with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- No price is shown anywhere in the email — for a collectible collab drop, that's a real friction point before clicking.
- "This drop will go fast" urgency has no date, countdown, or stock indicator to back it up — feels like stock urgency language, not a credible deadline.
- The four secondary CTA buttons (Shop Clogs, Jibbitz, Sale, Sandals) dilute the single-product focus right under the hero — this email should be about one shoe.
- The "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off" banner competes with the Lightning McQueen offer for attention and has nothing to do with the collab.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price directly under "Shop Dinoco" — collectors decide based on price point immediately, and its absence is the single biggest gap here.
- 2. Cut the four secondary category buttons from this send — a collab drop this strong doesn't need a full nav menu competing with it.
- 3. Replace vague urgency ("will go fast") with a specific signal — a drop date, "limited to X pairs," or an early-access window for members.
- 4. Move or drop the Refer-a-Friend block for this send — it's off-theme and pulls focus from the collab.
