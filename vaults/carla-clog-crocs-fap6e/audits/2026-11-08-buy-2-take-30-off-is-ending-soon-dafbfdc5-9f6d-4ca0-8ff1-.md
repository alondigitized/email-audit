# Buy 2, take 30% off is ending soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Crocs "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" send in three days, following Nov 6 ("Starting today…") and Nov 7 ("We Couldn't Wait Until Black Friday!"). The yellow hero is clean. "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" sits in heavy black type, with a black "Shop The Sale" pill below it and a single red Baya clog beneath that. The eye lands on the offer first, as it should.
- The urgency is real, since the offer ends tonight. But the deadline, "Offer ends Sunday, November 8th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT," is buried in the fine print at the very bottom. The body copy only says "Ending soon!" and "Last chance." The 40% Off Accessories block is a smart cross-sell. Its pink panel is as loud as the hero, so the two offers compete.
- Overall it's competent but formulaic, and subscribers are likely to have stopped noticing it. The ending-soon angle is the only fresh thing here, and the email hides the one detail that makes it urgent.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is clear: "Ending Soon!" label, then a huge "Buy 2 Take 30% Off", then a black CTA pill.
- The yellow and black palette is high contrast and reads as Crocs brand voice.
- The 40% accessories block gives a second reason to click and a higher discount for people who don't want shoes.
- The "Free Shipping on Qualified Orders" bar at the top removes a common objection.
- The email has no render bugs: no broken images, no overlapping text, and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The real deadline (tonight, 11:59pm MT) appears only in the fine print. "Ending soon" and "Last chance" are vague.
- The hero shows one red clog with no name, price or colour options. It doesn't tell me what 30% off actually buys.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears twice, directly under each CTA, with no link or detail. It undercuts the offer.
- The creative is nearly identical to the Nov 6 and Nov 7 sends, so there's no novelty for a subscriber seeing it a third time.
- The yellow panel has a large empty gap between the CTA and the shoe, which pushes the product below the first screen on mobile.
- The generic "Shop The Sale" and "Shop Accessories" CTAs aren't specific. Neither link is tied to a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight at 11:59pm MT", with a real countdown or timestamp. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Replace the lone shoe with a 3-up row of best-selling clogs showing sale prices, or a "Buy 2 = $X" example. That makes the offer concrete.
- 3. Move the shoe closer to the CTA, or shrink the empty space, so the product shows on the first mobile screen.
- 4. Link "Exclusions Apply" to a list, or replace it with a short "Excludes X, Y".
- 5. Make the accessories block secondary, with a smaller headline or a muted colour, so it doesn't compete with the hero.
- 6. Vary the creative from the Nov 6 and Nov 7 sends. Even a new hero colour or a different clog would help.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off 2 pairs ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off accessories. Sale ends tonight at 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance to pair up, and free shipping on qualified orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) send with a responsive template and Outlook/Gmail/Apple client hacks. The confirmed defects are 7 footer and social images served over `http://`, one stray closing tag, and a missing `alt` on a third-party pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified tracking links:** 25 links on `click.crocs-email.com` and similar click-redirect domains were skipped by the QA probe. Destination validity, redirect chain and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Malformed markup around the open pixel:** the pixel is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10206&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no matching opener. Most clients ignore it, but it's invalid HTML.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…`. It has no `alt` attribute (QA flagged this). It's a 1×1 tracker, so the accessibility impact is minimal, but it adds a second open-tracking call to a third-party domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed-content images (7):** all are served over plain `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. Clients that enforce HTTPS may block them or show a mixed-content warning, which would leave blank slots in the footer. The affected images are:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` (App Store badge)
  - `13246762-…png` (Google Play badge)
  - `c4df71ec-…png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)
- **Alt text:** these 7 images do have meaningful `alt` values.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` doesn't describe the email. This is low severity.
- **Preheader:** "Add Another. Save More." is hidden correctly with `display:none`, and the zero-width padding suppresses fallback preview text. No issues found.
- **Responsive and client hacks:** the viewport meta tag, `format-detection`, the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag, the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block and the 639px breakpoint are all present. No issues found.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. The HTML cuts off before the font-family fallback stacks, so I can't confirm those.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA check itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. Treat this as unconfirmed, not as a defect in the send.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA output attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML is truncated before the footer, so these can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. The 25 tracking links were not resolved, and the truncated source doesn't show destination URLs or UTM parameters. The QA run therefore can't confirm that the "Buy 2, take 30% off" offer reaches an aligned landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Serve all assets over HTTPS.** Change the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` URLs to `https://`, and confirm the image host has a valid certificate.
2. **Remove the stray `</custom>` tag** after the open-tracking `<img>`.
3. **Add `alt=""`** to the Demdex pixel, or consolidate the two open-tracking pixels.
4. **Re-run the header checks on the raw `.eml`.** That would show whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are present. Both Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
5. **Resolve the tracking links** by following redirects with a real client. That would confirm landing-page destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. **Audit the full HTML**, including the footer, for the unsubscribe link, physical address and unresolved tokens.
7. **Replace the generic `<title>`** with something descriptive (low priority).
