# 30% Off. Free Shipping. No Brainer.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Crocs' first straight discount-only send since "The deal is delivering. Literally." on 7/24 — two site-wide percentage-off promos back to back, which breaks the pattern of the July run (licensed collabs, product launches, segment pivots). The email itself is clean and does exactly one job: it leads with "30% Off + Free Shipping!" in giant black type on a pale lilac field, backs it with a single unambiguous "Shop the Sale" button, and closes with a stacked pink/tan clog pile that's genuinely eye-catching. There's no confusion about the offer or the CTA — this is about as legible as a promo email gets.
- Where it falls short is specificity and freshness. The subject line trio ("30% Off. Free Shipping. No Brainer.") is nearly a rewording of yesterday's "The deal is delivering. Literally." — same underlying deal, different tagline — and "Exclusions Apply" sitting directly under the CTA is a small honesty tax that costs some trust. The "Recommended for You" grid is generic (all-black Classic/Kids/Baya clogs, one sandal) rather than tied to the sale or to any real personalization signal.
- Net: a well-executed but interchangeable discount email, arriving one day after a nearly identical discount email. Solid mechanics, thin differentiation — 6/10 territory.

## What's working

- Single, unmissable offer stated three ways (banner, hero headline, subhead) with zero ambiguity.
- "Shop the Sale" is a clear, specific, high-contrast CTA button that's impossible to miss.
- Hero image (pink Baya-style clogs stacked on tan) is bold, on-brand, and renders cleanly — no bugs, no lorem ipsum, no broken assets.
- Deadline is present in the fine print (ends Tuesday, July 28th, 11:59pm MT), giving the offer real urgency even if it's buried.
- Refer-a-friend and app-download modules are visible without cluttering the primary offer.

## What's weak

- "Exclusions Apply." sits directly beneath the CTA in visible body copy — a soft bait-and-switch signal that undercuts the "no brainer" subject line.
- The 30%-off headline gives no minimum threshold, promo code, or category scope, so the recipient has to click just to learn if this applies to what they want.
- "Recommended for You" is four generic best-sellers (three of them black clogs) with no visible connection to the sale, past purchases, or the hero product shown above.
- This is a second consecutive site-wide discount email in as many days (7/24, then 7/25) — cadence risk for fatigue, especially with such similar creative logic (bold claim + black CTA).
- No urgency shown above the fold — the actual deadline is buried in the legal footer, not in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the deadline in the hero.** Move "Ends Tuesday 7/28" (or "3 days left") next to the CTA instead of leaving it in footer legal — urgency drives clicks far more than a static "30% Off" banner alone.
- 2. **Cut or soften "Exclusions Apply."** If exclusions are minor, drop the line from the hero entirely and let the footer legal carry it; if they're significant, replace with something concrete like "Excludes new arrivals" so it reads as information, not a hedge.
- 3. **Tie the product grid to the sale.** Swap "Recommended for You" for "Top Picks at 30% Off" featuring the pink Baya clog from the hero plus 3 other actual sale-eligible styles — right now the grid feels bolted on.
- 4. **Space this out from 7/24's discount send.** Two discount-led emails in 48 hours risks list fatigue; if both are needed, differentiate the angle (e.g., today = "last call" framing) rather than repeating the format.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Chance: 30% Off Ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cart Called. It Wants 30% Off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on top. Ends 7/28 at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack the pink pair with 30% off — while it lasts.`

## Technical Audit: Crocs — "30% Off. Free Shipping. No Brainer."

### 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard responsive HTML template with MSO/Outlook conditionals and webfont fallbacks, but automated QA flags header-level compliance gaps, six mixed-content (HTTP) image assets, and an unredacted email address embedded in personalization/recommendation image URLs.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
28 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (standard behavior for redirect domains — not itself a defect). No broken or malformed links were confirmed in the reachable portion of the source. Full link inventory was not available due to HTML truncation, so link-destination validity cannot be fully confirmed from this review.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text (5 images):**
  - Tracking pixel: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...`
  - 4 product recommendation images from `https://10745993.recs.igodigital.com/rr/v2/685c6ddd06fd7c003f1cd785/i/{1-4}/carla-clog-crocs-fap6e@etell.app?category=Recomm`
  - Missing alt text on the tracking pixel is expected/harmless; missing alt on the 4 product recs images is a real accessibility gap for screen-reader users.
- **Mixed content — 6 images served over `http://` instead of `https://`:** App Store badge, Google Play badge, "Find Your Store," and Instagram/Facebook/TikTok/YouTube social icons, all under `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/...`. Many mail clients (and browser-based webmail) block or flag non-HTTPS image loads, which can cause these icons/badges to render broken.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
The four igodigital.com recommendation-engine image URLs embed a literal recipient email address in cleartext as a path segment: `.../i/{n}/carla-clog-crocs-fap6e@etell.app?category=Recomm`. This is functioning as a personalization key for the recs widget, but it means the recipient's email address is transmitted in the URL to a third-party recommendation domain (and left in browser/proxy/CDN access logs, referrer headers, etc.) rather than being tokenized/hashed. This is a data-handling concern worth flagging even though it doesn't block rendering.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** not found by automated check. Note: this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the ESP omitting it — recommend verifying against the raw source at the ESP.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058):** not found — if List-Unsubscribe is confirmed present, this should be added to support one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules).
- **Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM):** not found via the relay, so pass/fail status is unknown — needs verification at the receiving MTA, not just the relay capture.
- **Unsubscribe link / physical address in body:** not verifiable — the provided HTML was truncated before the footer, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance (unsubscribe link, mailing address) cannot be confirmed either way from this source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable — the truncated HTML did not include the primary CTA/link block, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be inspected in this pass.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Change the 6 `http://` image sources (App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube) to `https://`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product recommendation images.
3. Tokenize/hash the recipient identifier used in the igodigital.com recs image URLs instead of passing the raw email address in the path.
4. Confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are present in the raw ESP send (independent of the AgentMail relay capture) and add List-Unsubscribe-Post if one-click unsubscribe isn't already supported.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly at the receiving MTA since Authentication-Results wasn't captured via the relay.
6. Re-run this audit against the full (non-truncated) HTML to confirm unsubscribe link, physical address, and UTM/landing-page continuity.
