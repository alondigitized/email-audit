# Buy 2 = 20% off, Buy 3 = 30% off!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Crocs' third consecutive day deploying the 20%/30% tiered Buy-2/Buy-3 mechanic — the same core offer as the June 23 send (8/10) and the June 24 "Cue the Fireworks" send (9/10). Two genuine additions exist: a TikTok × Crocs Super Brand Day collab callout mid-scroll, and a continued "Star-Spangled Style" July 4th frame. Neither changes the fundamental reality that a subscriber who opened on June 23 has now seen this deal three days running with no visible expiry.
- The email itself is technically clean: bold hero typography, a clear three-offer ladder (pairs → accessories at 40% off → Super Brand Day event), and two unambiguous CTAs. The refer-a-friend strip near the footer provides a low-pressure secondary path. These are real strengths.
- The story is cadence, again. After the June 20 "before it's officially over…" send (which was itself the fourth "Extra 25% Off" in eight days), Crocs made a smart pivot on June 23. But three straight days of the same tiered offer — with progressively thinner differentiation — trains subscribers to wait out the clock rather than act. The TikTok partnership is the freshest element in this email and it's buried third. That's the wrong priority order.

## What's working

- Hero typography is large, bold, and instantly scannable — the 20%/30% offer requires zero effort to read.
- Three-section offer layering (pairs → accessories → event) gives the email structural reason to exist beyond the hero deal.
- TikTok × Crocs Super Brand Day is a genuine creative addition not present in either the June 23 or June 24 sends.
- "Shop the Sale" and "Grab them now" CTAs are verb-led, specific, and visually prominent.
- Free shipping banner anchors the top — adds value before the first offer line.

## What's weak

- Day 3 of the identical mechanic with no visible deadline. "Star-Spangled Style" implies July 4th but forces subscribers to infer the expiry date.
- The 40% Off Accessories section — arguably the sharper deal — is below the fold and will be skipped by mobile users who stop at the first CTA.
- The TikTok × Crocs Super Brand Day section is visually compressed and feels like an afterthought; it deserved lead placement as the one genuinely new element.
- Refer-a-Friend strip is doing work this email didn't earn — you need warmer sentiment than a repeated promo blast to convert non-buyers into advocates.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears twice without any indication of which styles are included or excluded, which adds friction to the decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the deadline in the hero.** Add "Through July 4th" or "Ends Sunday" directly to the hero block. Three days of the same offer with no visible end date trains subscribers to wait.
- 2. **Promote TikTok × Crocs Super Brand Day to the lead section.** That's the only genuinely new element; it should be the reason this email exists on day 3 of the promo, not the third section.
- 3. **Give 40% Off Accessories its own hero moment** — or at minimum move it to section 2 above the fold. It's a better headline discount than the pairs mechanic for a solo buyer.
- 4. **Drop the Refer-a-Friend strip from promo sends.** Deploy it post-purchase as a triggered email; it's wasted real estate here.
- 5. **Add a countdown or "Sale Ends July 4" timestamp** on the hero image for credible time-bound pressure.

- **Subject Alt B:** `TikTok x Crocs Super Brand Day — shop the collab`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off accessories & free shipping. Offer ends July 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the collab. Save on clogs, sandals, accessories & more.`

**Pixel tracker missing alt attribute:**
`https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?...&c_emailopened=Yes&...` — the open-tracking pixel has no `alt=""` attribute. Should be `alt=""` (empty) for screen reader hygiene.

**Font loading:** Custom `TT Crocs` web font loaded via `@font-face` with `font-display: swap`. Email client CSS support for `@font-face` is narrow (Apple Mail, Samsung Mail); Outlook and Gmail will fall back silently. No issues — this is expected behavior and the stack appears to handle it.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — captured by QA as absent at the AgentMail relay layer. This header is required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance (>5k/day threshold). Whether it exists upstream at the ESP and was stripped by the relay is unknown, but it must be confirmed present in live delivery headers.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected** — RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo since February 2024 for bulk senders) appears missing. If Crocs sends at bulk volume without this, messages will be penalized in Gmail placement.

**`Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown due to AgentMail relay interception. Cannot confirm DMARC alignment. This is a relay artifact, not necessarily an ESP failure, but should be verified against a direct seed send.

Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated source, but no missing footer block was flagged.
