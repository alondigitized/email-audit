# The Clock's Ticking on 2 for $50! ⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth "2 for $50" send in five days (Nov 10, 11, 11, 12 and now Nov 14), with a "Final Hours" banner on top. The execution is clean. "2 FOR $50" is the biggest type on the page, the white "Shop Early Access" pill sits right under it, and the two Baya clogs on the snowflake-green panel anchor the bottom half. The "FINAL HOURS" banner is the only new element since Nov 10–12.
- The real deadline, "Offer ends November 14th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT", is only in the last line of the footer fine print. The hero says "Final hours" and "Ending Soon" but never says when. The email is the same creative as Nov 11 and Nov 12 with a louder banner. Subscribers who ignored the first four will probably ignore this one too, but the urgency is real, since the offer ends tonight.
- Verdict: a competent, repetitive last-call send. It would be stronger with a visible deadline and a named product.

## What's working

- "2 FOR $50" is the largest element, so the offer reads in about a second.
- The white "Shop Early Access" pill contrasts well against the dark green and is the obvious next step.
- The "FINAL HOURS" repeated banner adds urgency that is new relative to Nov 10–12.
- The two Baya clogs (cream and black) with fleece lining fit the winter, Black Friday season.
- The layout is clean with no render bugs, and "available online & in-store" widens the offer.

## What's weak

- The actual deadline (Nov 14, 11:59pm MT) is buried in the footer fine print. The hero urgency is vague.
- "Exclusions Apply." sits right under the CTA with no detail, which weakens trust a little.
- Nothing is linked to a specific product, and there is no price, style name or review. It is a generic two-shoe image.
- It is the fifth near-identical 2-for-$50 email in five days, so fatigue is likely.
- "Free shipping on qualified orders" in the top bar competes with the main offer and is vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight, 11:59pm MT", in place of or beside "Your Early Access is Ending Soon!".
- 2. Name the product in the hero or CTA: "Shop Baya Clogs, 2 for $50".
- 3. Add a countdown timer or a "Today only" badge to make the urgency credible and visual.
- 4. Show original vs. deal pricing (for example "$50 for 2 vs. $__"), or a style-count line, to make the value concrete.
- 5. Cut the repetition. Skip a send, or vary the creative (a different product, a different story) instead of resending the same panel.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 2 for $50 clogs ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early access closes at 11:59pm MT. Baya clogs and more, 2 for $50.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tonight's the last night. Grab your pair before the offer ends.`

# Technical Audit: "The Clock's Ticking on 2 for $50! ⏰" (Crocs)

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) build with responsive CSS, MSO fallbacks and a preheader. There are no confirmed blocking defects, only one malformed tag and one tracking pixel without alt text. The source was truncated and the relay stripped headers, so the footer, links and authentication cannot be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 13 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Malformed markup:** the open-pixel `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt="">` is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag that has no matching opening tag. Most clients tolerate it, but it is invalid HTML and can break parsing in strict renderers.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It is a second tracker alongside the SFMC open pixel and is the image QA flagged for missing `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** the demdex beacon `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip it.
- **Minor:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic and does not reflect the email's content.
- **Minor:** `<style type="text/css"> div.preheader {…}` sits inside `<body>`. Some clients strip body-level `<style>` blocks, but the preheader also has inline `display:none`, so the impact is low.
- **Present and correct:** `lang`/`xml:lang`, viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, a 639px mobile breakpoint, and `font-display: swap` on the `TT Crocs` web fonts.
- **Unverified:** the remaining body markup, including image alt text, was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed:** QA reported no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and no `Authentication-Results` header. The QA note itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown rather than failing. Verify them against the raw message at the original receiving MTA.
- **Not verifiable:** the footer, with its unsubscribe link and physical address, is outside the truncated source.
- **Sender domain:** the From address is `email@crocs-email.com`, which is consistent with the click and open tracking domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Destination URLs are hidden behind SFMC click-redirects, and the probe skipped all 13. No UTM parameters can be assessed from the available data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` and close the open pixel as a plain `<img … />`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon image.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw message, not the relay copy. Bulk-sender rules at Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe.
4. Resolve the click-tracking redirects with a redirect-following check to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address, and image alt text.
6. Give the `<title>` descriptive text (low priority).
