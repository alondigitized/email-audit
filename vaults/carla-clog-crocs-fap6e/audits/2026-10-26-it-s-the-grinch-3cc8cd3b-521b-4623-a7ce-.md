# It’s The Grinch! 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-theme product launch. The Dr. Seuss Grinch clog collab leads, and it's the first Crocs send in this run without a price offer. After the 10/23–10/25 "Extra 50% Off" run (and the 10/19 "Stock up" multi-buy), this one trades discount urgency for novelty.
- The creative is charming. A furry green Grinch tail sits behind a fuzzy lime clog with a Santa-hat collar, on a yellow field with the Grinch logo in the corner. The red block below has a bold "Cozy Steals Christmas" headline, short copy, and a black "Shop The Grinch" pill. The eye path is hero, headline, button, and it works.
- The weakness is that nothing gives a reason to click now. There's no price, no deadline and no stock cue. The only offer is a generic "Free shipping on qualified orders" strip, and the six-row category nav below the hero dilutes the one message. It's a pleasant, on-brand seasonal send that will do fine with Grinch and holiday-novelty fans and little for anyone else.

## What's working

- The hero image is playful and instantly readable, and the Grinch logo and Santa-hat clog tie it to the holiday.
- The red panel has a strong headline, "Cozy Steals Christmas", and a high-contrast black "Shop The Grinch" button right under it.
- The copy is short and names the range: cozy clogs, socks and Jibbitz charms.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the footer is tidy and easy to scan.
- The 10/25 "Last Chance: Extra 50% Off!" send was a pure discount push, so this launch is a fresh creative beat.

## What's weak

- There is no price or deadline. Compared with the 10/23–10/25 "Extra 50% Off" sends, nothing here creates urgency.
- Only one product is shown, and it isn't labelled with a name or price. Socks and charms are named in the copy but not pictured.
- The six-row text nav (Women, Men, Kids, Crocs at Work, Jibbitz Charms, Sale) is a lot of vertical space for plain text. It competes with the Grinch CTA and adds nothing for a themed send.
- "Free shipping on qualified orders" is generic, and the footnote says "qualified," so it reads as hedged.
- The "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" banner is a stronger offer than anything above it, but it sits buried in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete hook near the hero, such as a price ("Grinch Classic Lined Clog, $64.99"), a limited-run or sell-out cue, or a ship-by-Dec-24 date.
- 2. Show three products in a row (clog, socks, a Jibbitz set) with names and prices, so the email earns more than a single click target.
- 3. Replace the text nav with two or three Grinch-specific links: Adults, Kids, Charms.
- 4. Move the Refer-a-Friend 25% offer up under the hero as a secondary strip.
- 5. Make the subject name the product. The Grinch is recognizable, but "It's The Grinch!" doesn't say it's a shoe.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Grinch clogs are here 🎄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Grinch Crocs: cozy, lined, limited`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New cozy clogs, socks & Jibbitz charms. Free shipping on qualified orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Steal the holiday spotlight before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Sent via Salesforce Marketing Cloud (click.crocs-email.com, Exact Target open pixel). The source has one malformed tag, a missing alt on a tracking pixel, and 7 `http://` image sources. The header-related warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed as sender faults. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, body links and tokens were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 28 tracking links on click-redirect domains were not HTTP-probed, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is closed with a stray end tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10201&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching open tag. It's invalid HTML, and strict parsers or some clients may handle it unpredictably.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event pixel. It has no alt text, and it adds a second tracking request on top of the SFMC open pixel.
- **Open pixel:** it is served over HTTPS. No issues beyond the stray tag above.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** 7 images load over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`: App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube. Clients that proxy or block mixed content may not display them, and they can trigger insecure-content warnings. All 7 have alt text.
- **Alt text:** the demdex pixel above is the only image flagged for missing alt. The Crocs open pixel has `alt=""`, which is correct for a tracking pixel.
- **Document title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic rather than descriptive. This is minor.
- **Boilerplate:** the viewport meta tag, MSO conditionals, the Outlook `sup` fix, the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack and the Apple data-detector overrides are present. The `@font-face` URLs use HTTPS with `font-display: swap`. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible portion has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`). Tokens in the truncated body or footer can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay. Treat it as unverified, not a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers on the original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is an expected relay artifact. The sender domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking and image domains, which is consistent for alignment, but alignment was not verified.
- **Footer:** the visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 28 links are SFMC click-wrapped and weren't probed, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm that UTMs survive and the destinations return 200.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix `</custom>` after the open pixel by removing it, or by closing with `/>`.
2. Move the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` assets to `https://`, and add an HTTP-to-HTTPS redirect or HSTS on that host.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel image, and consider whether the second tracking pixel is needed.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass, since the relay data is inconclusive.
5. Re-run the audit with a headless redirect resolver on the 28 tracked links to check UTMs and destination status.
6. Replace the generic `<title>` with a campaign-specific one. This is optional.
