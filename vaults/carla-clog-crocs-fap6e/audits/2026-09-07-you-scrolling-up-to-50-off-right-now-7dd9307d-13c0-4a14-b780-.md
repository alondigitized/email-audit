# You scrolling? Up to 50% off right now!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same bundle-discount well Crocs was already back in on 9/4 ("Up to 50% off! No overthinking necessary") — two stacked percentage-off blocks, pink 30% clearance on top, green 50% off select styles below, and again neither one wins as the hero. There's also a render bug at the very top: faint legal disclaimer text is bleeding through directly behind/under the Crocs logo, the same overlapping-text failure mode seen in the 9/5 "Your Weekend Dress Code" send. Three days later, same bug, same offer structure — this reads like a template that hasn't been fixed.
- The one genuine visual asset here is the "SALE" diagonal-stripe treatment with the triple-angle clog shot, which is a nice piece of art direction, but it sits below both CTAs, so by the time a scroller reaches it they've already made their click decision (or bounced) on the competing offer blocks above.
- Judgement: a weak, generic send — recycled offer architecture, a repeated unresolved render bug, and a subject that promises urgency ("scrolling?") the body doesn't really deliver on.

## What's working

- The green "Up to 50% Off / Select Footwear and Accessories" block has a clear black pill CTA ("Shop the Sale") with strong contrast.
- The "SALE" diagonal-block graphic with the three product angles (side, top-down, back) is a strong, distinctive visual asset.
- Free shipping banner is present at the very top, immediately visible.
- Footer is clean and complete — app download badges, store locator, social icons, and required links (unsubscribe, preferences) are all present with no broken elements.

## What's weak

- Overlapping/bleeding disclaimer text sits directly behind the Crocs logo at the top — a genuine render bug, and a repeat of the same class of issue from the 9/5 send.
- Two competing discount offers (30% vs. up to 50%) are stacked with no hierarchy — a reader can't tell which is the "real" deal.
- No product name, price, or specific style shown until deep in the scroll, and even then it's a generic clog, not tied to either advertised offer.
- Subject line urgency ("You scrolling? Up to 50% off right now!") isn't backed up by any countdown, timer, or visual urgency device in the body.
- This is the third bundle-discount send in about a week (9/4, 9/1, and now 9/7) with no new product story — cadence fatigue risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the logo-overlap render bug — this has now shown up in at least two sends (9/5, 9/7) and is the first thing the eye hits.
- 2. Pick one offer and lead with it. Either promote 30% off clearance OR up to 50% off select styles as the single hero — stacking both dilutes the whole email, as flagged on 9/4.
- 3. Move the "SALE" product shot up top and pair it with the hero offer so there's an actual product to look at before the CTA.
- 4. Replace generic "Shop the Sale" CTA copy with something offer-specific, e.g. "Shop 50% Off Now."
- 5. Add real urgency to match the subject line — a countdown or "ends Wednesday 9/9" call-out in the hero block, not just buried in the footer fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off — ends Wednesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart wants this: 50% off clogs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2, take 30% off clearance — plus 50% off select styles.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two ways to save on Crocs this week. Ends 9/9.`
