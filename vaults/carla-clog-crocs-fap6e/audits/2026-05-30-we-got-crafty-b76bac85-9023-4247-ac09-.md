# We Got Crafty!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- "We Got Crafty!" is a product-introduction email for Crocs' new Crafted Collection — canvas-finished clogs in neutral and color-pop colorways. It's a content/catalog email, not a promotional one, which is a deliberate gear-change after the Memorial Day sale blitz that ran through 5/27. The creative is clean and the render is bug-free, but the email suffers from a structural problem: it treats one launch as three separate mini-emails stacked on top of each other, which diffuses attention rather than building toward a click.
- The bigger issue is the subject. "We Got Crafty!" is a pun that sacrifices almost every signal that drives opens — no specificity, no offer, no urgency. A subscriber who just got hammered with 50% Memorial Day deals and then a welcome-code email earlier today has no reason to open a mystery subject line, even from a recognized sender.
- That said, for subscribers who do open, the email earns its keep: the Classic Crafted Clog product callouts are specific, the "Shop By Size" persistent section reduces friction, and the refer-a-friend offer is a nice bottom-of-funnel hook. This is a solid catalog email with a weak entry point.

## What's working

- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout bugs. The email loads as intended.
- **Classic Crafted Clog callout section** — the annotated product shot ("100% soft-painted canvas," "Luxuriously room for Jibbitz charms") is the most informative single frame in the email.
- **"Shop By Size" section** — presenting sizes for both Women and Men with direct CTA buttons is a friction-reducing feature; it respects that many shoppers already know their size.
- **Refer-a-Friend offer** — "Give 25%, Get 25% Off" is a clearly stated, mutually beneficial incentive, well-placed at the bottom for post-browse conversion.
- **Free shipping banner** — leads the email immediately; sets a baseline expectation without taking up creative real estate.

## What's weak

- **Subject "We Got Crafty!" is a dead end** — the pun is self-congratulatory and communicates nothing about the product, collection, or reason to open. It follows a day with two other Crocs emails; there's nothing here to break through the noise.
- **Three stacked "Shop Crafted" sections with no hierarchy** — "Neutrals Never Looked Better," "Classic Crafted Clog," and "New Crafted Colors" all compete for attention rather than building a narrative. The eye doesn't know where to land.
- **No price anchor** — after a week of 50% off messaging, dropping straight to a full-price collection launch with zero value hook ("starting at $XX," "new this season") makes the email feel like a step backward in value.
- **"The right colorway things are just what you needed" is broken copy** — "colorway things" reads as an incomplete edit; it undercuts an otherwise clean layout.
- **"Recommended for You" section feels generic** — Classic Clog + Kids' Classic Clog + Baya Clog with no personalization signal reads as a catch-all filler row, not actual recommendations.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to name the product or the concept.** "We Got Crafty!" tells the subscriber nothing. The reveal — canvas Crocs with a textile finish — is actually interesting. Lead with that.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Classic Clog in Canvas`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Crocs, now in crafted canvas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft-painted canvas. Room for Jibbitz. Neutrals that actually go with everything.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Classic Clog just got a new finish — see the Crafted Collection.`
- 2. **Collapse to one hero section.** Pick the annotated product shot (the "Classic Crafted Clog" callout) as the single hero and cut or subordinate the flanking lifestyle sections. The annotation with materials + charm capacity is the actual differentiator — lead with it.
- 3. **Add a price anchor.** "Starting at $55" or "Now available" with a seasonal badge does more than three "Shop Crafted" buttons with no context. Full-price launches need a reason-to-buy.
- 4. **Fix the body copy.** "The right colorway things are just what you needed" should be "The right colorway is just what you needed" — this is a visible copy error in the hero section.
- 5. **Earn the "Recommended for You" label.** Either remove the section or replace it with category-specific products (e.g., if the subscriber last browsed sandals, show sandals — not another Classic Clog).
