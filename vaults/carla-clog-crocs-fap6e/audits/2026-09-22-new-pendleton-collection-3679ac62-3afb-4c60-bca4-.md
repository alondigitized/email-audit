- This is the best Crocs send in this stretch — a real product launch (Pendleton collection, American-milled wool, Tucson pattern) shot on genuine lifestyle backgrounds (grassy field, wood-plank floor) instead of another recycled percentage-off banner. It reads like a brand collab worth caring about, not the fifth flash-sale wallpaper this month.
- That said, it's a no-price, no-urgency, no-sizing lookbook. There's real texture and story here, but nothing tells me what it costs or why to act today, and the wall of four generic "Shop X" pill buttons at the bottom dilutes the two strong CTAs above it.

## What's working

- Genuine collab launch with distinctive Pendleton wool pattern — visually distinct from the last five generic sale sends.
- Two clean, legible hero sections each with one clear CTA ("Shop Pendleton," "Shop Crafted").
- Lifestyle photography (grass, tree stump, rustic wood floor) gives the collection a premium, outdoorsy feel that fits the product story.
- Copy has a point of view ("timeless heritage woven seamlessly with modern trend") rather than generic sale language.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere — for a "limited-edition" premium collab, shoppers will want to know the cost before clicking.
- No urgency or scarcity cue despite "limited-edition" framing — no "while supplies last" or drop date.
- Bottom four-button grid (Clogs/Jibbitz/Sale/Sandals) is generic navigation, not related to Pendleton, and competes with the two hero CTAs for attention.
- Subject line "New Pendleton collection 🐎" doesn't hint at wool, heritage pattern, or limited-edition status — undersells what's actually a strong visual story.
- No social proof or reviews for a new, presumably higher-priced launch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or "starting at $X" under at least one hero CTA — this is the single biggest lift since collab clogs typically carry a premium and shoppers will hesitate without an anchor.
- 2. Add a scarcity line ("Limited-edition — while supplies last") near the top hero to justify the urgency implied by "premiere" and "limited-edition."
- 3. Cut or visually de-emphasize the bottom four-button grid, or swap "Shop Sale" for a Pendleton-specific link — it drags attention away from the launch.
- 4. Add a one-line trust/quality badge (e.g., "Made with American-milled wool") near the top hero image the way the 9/17 NFL email used "Officially Licensed Product."
