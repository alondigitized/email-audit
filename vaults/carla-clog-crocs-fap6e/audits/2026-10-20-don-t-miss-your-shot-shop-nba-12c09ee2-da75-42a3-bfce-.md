# Don’t miss your shot! Shop NBA.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure product-launch send for the NBA clog collab, and the creative is strong. The hero is five team-branded clogs (Warriors, Bulls, Celtics, Knicks, Lakers) lined up under the NBA logo. It's colorful, instantly readable, and a fan can find their team in a second. The subject, "Don't miss your shot! Shop NBA.", matches the creative and has a basketball pun that fits.
- The weakness is that there's no price hook. The only offer is a generic "Free shipping on qualified orders" bar. Just yesterday (10/19) Crocs closed a 40%-off multi-buy sale with "Stock up now, sale ends tonight!", and 10/18 and 10/11 were also deal-led. Going from a loud discount to a full-price licensed drop with no incentive is a jarring change. The copy is thin ("It's gametime. Shop your team."). Both CTAs say "Shop NBA" and go to the same place. The email also never shows which teams are available beyond the five pictured.
- Judgment: good creative with no offer and no urgency behind it. It will appeal to NBA fans and miss everyone else. Expect an average click rate and not much more.

## What's working

- The hero is eye-catching: five team-themed clogs with the NBA logo centered, so the theme registers in about a second.
- Licensed team details (Bulls, Celtics, Knicks, Lakers, Warriors) invite fans to self-select.
- The black pill "Shop NBA" buttons are high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The layout is clean, with a clear hero-then-CTA rhythm and plenty of whitespace.
- The "Shop Clogs / Jibbitz / Sale / Sandals" grid gives non-NBA readers a way out into other categories.

## What's weak

- There's no discount or price anywhere. "Free shipping on qualified orders" is the only offer, and the footer note says it's conditional.
- The copy is generic: "It's gametime. Shop your team." and "This collection is an easy win." tell the reader nothing about price, fit, or which teams are included.
- The second section repeats the first: another NBA product photo, another identical "Shop NBA" button. It adds length and no new information.
- Every CTA goes to the broad NBA collection rather than a specific team or product, so the reader still has to hunt for their team.
- There's no urgency or scarcity language, and no reviews or social proof.
- The Refer-a-Friend "Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" banner sits in the footer, though it's the only real incentive in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook. Lead with a price, a limited-quantity message, or an early-access angle for members. If there's no discount, say what's scarce: "Limited team drops."
- 2. Replace the second "Shop NBA" block with team-specific tiles (Lakers, Celtics, Knicks, Warriors, Bulls), each linking to its own clog. That gives the reader a direct path to their team and gives you click data by team.
- 3. Show a price on at least one hero product, and a rating if there is one.
- 4. Move the referral offer up beneath the hero, or fold it into the header bar.
- 5. Rewrite the body copy to carry information, such as which teams, which styles, and which sizes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your team's Crocs just dropped 🏀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `NBA Classic Clogs: Find Your Team`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lakers, Celtics, Knicks, Bulls & more. Free shipping on qualified orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New NBA clogs are here. Grab your team before sizes sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound, with a responsive meta setup, MSO conditionals and a preheader. QA flagged 7 `http://` image sources, a malformed tracking-pixel tag, and an alt-less third-party pixel. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel markup:** The open-tracking pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?NSD5S6DKIWUUHGEPONLQ3LIYKA.10200&d=10200&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener. Parsers will usually ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Unprobed links:** 26 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beacon, and it has no `alt` attribute. It also carries a `d_dpuid` value, which looks like a per-recipient ID. This is a second tracking layer on top of the ExactTarget pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources:** 7 images load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - App Store badge (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play badge (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Clients or webmail that block mixed content may drop these. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, so the impact varies by client.
- **Missing alt text:** The demdex beacon has no `alt`. It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is minor.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic, which is a minor issue.
- The other images checked in the visible source have alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. QA notes AgentMail may strip it, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same relay caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `crocs-email.com` is aligned with the tracking and image hosts.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no destination URLs, and the 26 tracking links were not probed. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the 7 `http://` image URLs on `image.crocs-email.com` to `https://`, or serve them over HTTPS-only.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon.
4. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source with full headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve the tracking redirects in a follow-up pass to verify landing pages and UTM values.
6. Re-run on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and any merge tokens.
