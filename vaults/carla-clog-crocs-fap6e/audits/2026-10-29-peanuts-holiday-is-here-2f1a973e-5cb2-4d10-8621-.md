## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML shell is mostly sound: it has a preheader, VML/MSO conditionals, a mobile media query and an `lang` attribute. The weaknesses are a malformed tracking-pixel snippet, seven `http://` images, and compliance and authentication headers the QA run couldn't verify. The source is truncated, so footer, link and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=..." width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` tag has no opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup inside the tracking wrapper `<div style="height:1px; overflow:hidden;">`.
- **Third-party beacon without alt:** the QA flagged `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` as an image with no `alt`. It is a second open-tracking call (Adobe Audience Manager) alongside the ExactTarget pixel. Not a defect on its own, but the missing `alt` is a real a11y warning.
- **26 tracking links skipped:** they are on click-redirect domains, so no HTTP probe was run. Destination status and redirect chains are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** seven images load over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/...`. They are the App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube badges. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or that block non-HTTPS content, may drop them. The rest of the email uses `https://`, so the Content Delivery Network (CDN) supports TLS and the fix is just changing the scheme.
- **Missing alt:** the demdex beacon has no `alt` (see above).
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` doesn't describe this message. This is minor and mainly matters for the web-view version.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` via `@font-face`. The fallback stack isn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it.
- No other issues found in the visible portion: the viewport meta tag, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the Outlook DPI settings and the Gmail hack are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The footer and later content were truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not evidence of failure. The sending domain `crocs-email.com` should be checked directly (DNS lookup or a raw `.eml`).
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
No issues found that I can confirm. All 26 links go through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be seen from the HTML. Destinations would need to be resolved by following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change all seven `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image URLs to `https://`. This is the only confirmed rendering defect with a clear fix.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>` and add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon.
3. Re-run the audit on the raw `.eml`, or fetch the headers from the sending ESP. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Resolve the 26 click-tracking links with a redirect-following probe. This would check the final status codes and UTM presence.
5. Give the `<title>` a descriptive value such as the subject line.
