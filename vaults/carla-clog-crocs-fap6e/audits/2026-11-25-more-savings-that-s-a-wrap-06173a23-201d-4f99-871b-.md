# More savings? That's a wrap. 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth Black Friday send from Crocs in about two weeks, and the hero is the same stacked offer as the Nov 24 "Your cart deserves a little treat." email: up to 60% off plus an extra 20% off with code COZY20. The hero copy is clear. "60% OFF" is the biggest element on the page, the code is bold, and a black "Shop Black Friday" pill sits right under it. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The rest of the email is weak. A green Minnie Mouse banner and a TikTok "Shop the Hype" block are stacked below the hero, and each pushes a different offer. The Minnie banner's "50% off today" competes with the 60% + 20% headline. Neither lower block has a button that links to a specific product, and the clog hero image has no CTA of its own. The creative is mostly recycled from earlier sends. The subject's "That's a wrap" also overpromises, because the footer says the offer runs through Sunday, Nov 29.
- Judgement: a competent but redundant send. It is clear on the offer, but it gives a subscriber who has already seen eight near-identical emails no new reason to open or click.

## What's working

- The "60% OFF" headline is huge and the visual hierarchy is clean.
- The code COZY20 is stated plainly, with the instruction to use it at checkout.
- The black pill CTAs have strong contrast and consistent styling.
- The red-bow hero photo with tan and cream slides fits the gifting season.
- The "Oh, bow-tiful! Maximum cute. Minnie price." line has some personality in Crocs' voice.

## What's weak

- The subject says "That's a wrap," but the email shows no sign of the sale ending. The footer deadline of Nov 29 is buried in fine print.
- The Minnie 50% off banner has no product image and no button. It competes with the main offer.
- "Shop Black Friday" and "Shop the Hype" are generic CTAs. Neither names a product or category.
- The slide hero and the TikTok fuzzy-clog photo are not linked to any visible product detail, price or reviews.
- "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" and "Exclusions Apply" read as hedging. Nothing says which styles are excluded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject honest about the timing. If the sale ends Nov 29, say so, and put the real deadline in the hero ("Ends Sunday").
- 2. Drop either the Minnie banner or the TikTok block. Keep one secondary message and give it a product image, a price, and a specific CTA ("Shop Minnie Clogs").
- 3. Show two or three named products with before and after prices under the hero instead of lifestyle imagery.
- 4. Replace "Shop Black Friday" with a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop 60% off clogs" or "Use COZY20."
- 5. Cut the cadence. Nine near-identical sends in two weeks trains subscribers to ignore the next one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) build with a responsive structure and `lang="en"`. It has one malformed tag and one alt-less third-party pixel. The source was truncated, so the footer, the unsubscribe link and the CTA hrefs could not be inspected. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay, not from the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open-pixel line closes with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10208&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. No `<custom>` element is opened, so this is invalid HTML. Most clients ignore it, but strict parsers and some Outlook/Word-engine renders can mishandle it. It sits in the pixel wrapper `<div style="height: 1px; overflow: hidden;">`.
- **Third-party pixel:** the QA check found an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beacon at `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`.
  - It carries a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`). Because it is a separate open-tracking pixel, it adds a second tracker alongside the Exact Target open pixel.
  - `c_emailopened=Yes` is a static parameter, so the pixel marks an open when the image loads.
  - The `d_dpuid` value looks short for a typical ID. That may be QA display truncation, so I can't confirm a defect.
- **Click tracking:** 15 links were skipped by the QA probe because they use the `click.crocs-email.com` redirect domain. Link health and destinations are unverified, but there is no evidence of breakage.
- The tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com` shares the sender's root domain (`crocs-email.com`), so branding and alignment are consistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the demdex tracking `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is a hidden 1×1 pixel, so the impact is low, but it will fail automated a11y linting. Add `alt=""`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same on every send. It does not describe the message, which is a minor accessibility and "view in browser" issue.
- **Present and correct:**
  - The `lang` and `xml:lang` attributes.
  - The viewport meta tag.
  - The `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tags.
  - MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, the Gmail hack and the Apple data-detectors override.
  - The `max-width: 639px` mobile breakpoint with utility classes.
  - A hidden preheader ("Take an extra 20% off Black Friday sales today!") followed by a padding buffer.
- **Webfonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. The source is truncated, so I can't verify that fallback font stacks are set on the elements.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`. The subject line has no personalization.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
Nothing here is a confirmed defect. All three items are relay-capture limitations.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both headers were not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not capture them. This is unverified, not a confirmed violation. Check the raw message headers at the original recipient to confirm RFC 8058 one-click support.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the click domain, which suggests DKIM alignment is plausible but unproven.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer is beyond the truncation point, so these could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. The click URLs are opaque Exact Target `qs=` redirects, and the QA probe skipped all 15 of them. I can't confirm the UTM parameters or the landing page. I also can't check whether the offer on the page matches the preheader ("extra 20% off Black Friday sales").

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel, or move it into the same hidden wrapper as the open pixel.
3. Make `<title>` descriptive, for example the subject line.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw `.eml` headers. Don't rely on the relay output.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, without truncation, to check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
6. Resolve the `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, for example with a HEAD/GET follow-redirect probe, to confirm the final URLs, UTM parameters and that the Black Friday landing page matches the offer.
7. Fix the QA metrics: the "57% pass rate" alongside "0 issues, 3 warnings" mixes skipped and relay-limited checks with real results. Report those as "unverified" instead.
