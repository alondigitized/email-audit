# Up to 60% off + an extra 25% off? 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Black Friday catalog send in about a week. It is the most crowded of them. The brown hero stacks "Up to 60% off + an extra 25% off" over a CTA, with two slides in tan and white as the product. The slides are summer footwear on a holiday sale, and the hero image never says what the offer applies to. The subject is a near-repeat of the Nov 20 "Plus an extra 25% off?!", so a subscriber has already seen this hook.
- Below the hero, the email stacks a Grinch clog strip, a $30 classic clog block and a $30 animal-print block. The $30 price points are the most concrete offers, but they sit under the hero's stacked-discount math. Two "Shop All Doorbusters" buttons are identical and lead to the same place.
- The footer is honest about the dates, which is a point against the urgency. The fine print says the offer ends Sunday, Nov 29 in one block and Saturday-night Nov 21 in the other. Combined with "today!" on the Grinch strip, the deadlines are confusing and the urgency feels fake. A competent but tired send.

## What's working

- The hero has a clear stack: "60% OFF" in large white type on dark brown, with a white "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill.
- The "$30" price points in the classic clog and animal-print blocks are concrete and easy to scan.
- The pink and red "classic clogs starting at $30" block has strong colour contrast and is the best-looking module.
- The "Prices Straight from the Past" copy gives the sale a retro-price angle that differs from the earlier sends.
- The free-shipping bar at the top is visible without scrolling.

## What's weak

- The two tan and white slides in the hero are a mismatch for a holiday sale. The gold ribbon and stars help, but the product doesn't say "deal".
- "Up to 60% off plus an extra 25% off" asks the reader to do the math, as in the Nov 20 send. "Exclusions Apply" sits right under the CTA and weakens it.
- The deadlines conflict: the Grinch strip says "today!", and the footer lists both Nov 21 and Nov 29.
- The two "Shop All Doorbusters" buttons are identical, with no product-specific CTA such as "Shop $30 Clogs" or "Shop Animal Print".
- "Hello, Big Savings" repeats the headline of the earlier "Say Hello to Big Savings" send, so the creative reads as recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the $30 clogs, the one concrete price, and put them in the hero. Drop the stacked-discount math, or show the final price in one line.
- 2. Fix the deadline. Pick one date, and say "ends tonight" only if that is true.
- 3. Replace both "Shop All Doorbusters" buttons with specific CTAs: "Shop $30 Clogs" and "Shop Animal Print".
- 4. Cut the cadence. Send one consolidated Black Friday email per day, not a near-duplicate of the Nov 20 hook.
- 5. Swap the slides in the hero for a clog with Jibbitz or a holiday gift visual.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Classic Clogs from $30 (yes, really)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: extra 25% off sale styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Retro prices on classic clogs, plus an extra 25% off sale styles. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grinch clogs 45% off, animal print from $30. Free shipping on qualified orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a mostly well-formed, responsive Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) email, with one malformed tag and one un-alt'd third-party pixel. The source is truncated and the 18 tracking links weren't probed, so compliance, authentication and link/UTM checks are unverified. The QA run reports a 57% pass rate, 0 issues and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10209&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` has no matching opening tag, so it is invalid HTML. Most clients ignore it, but strict parsers may mishandle the pixel's container.
- **Third-party pixel without alt:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…53c` (QA: "Image missing alt text"). This is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) pixel. It adds a second tracking call on open, in addition to the SFMC pixel.
- **Probe skipped:** QA skipped 18 tracking/click-redirect links, so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains were checked. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Good:** `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and VML/Office namespaces with an MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block. The preheader is present and hidden with an inline `display:none !important`. Mobile styles sit under `@media (max-width: 639px)`.
- **`<style>` in `<body>`:** `<body class="email-body"><style type="text/css"> div.preheader {…}`. Some clients strip `<style>` outside `<head>`. The inline style on the preheader div covers the failure case, so the impact is low.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>`. This is minor and is not the subject line.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com/raw/upload/marketing/*.woff2`. I couldn't confirm a fallback stack, because the body CSS was truncated.
- **Content image alt text:** Only the demdex pixel was flagged. The truncation means the content images were not fully reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the visible source. The truncated portion couldn't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. Header presence is unverified, so I can't confirm or rule out a one-click-unsubscribe gap. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click support.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is consistent with a branded sending setup, but it isn't proof of authentication.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Both are outside the truncated source, so they weren't verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All visible links are SFMC click-redirect URLs, the destination and UTM parameters are obscured by the redirects, and QA skipped probing them. There is no evidence of a problem, and none of continuity either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`, or wrap the pixel in a valid element.
2. Add `alt=""` (and `width="1" height="1"`) to the `demdex.net/event` pixel.
3. Move the preheader `<style>` block into `<head>`, or drop it and keep the inline style.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`, with full headers and untruncated HTML, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve the 18 click-tracking redirects, for example by following them with a headless fetch, to check final destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
6. Check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
