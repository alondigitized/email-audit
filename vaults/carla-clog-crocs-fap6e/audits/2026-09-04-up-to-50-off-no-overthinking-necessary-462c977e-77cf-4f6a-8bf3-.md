# Up to 50% off! No overthinking necessary.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Two competing offers stacked on top of each other — "Buy 2, Take 30% Off" clearance in pink, then "Up to 50% Off" select styles in blue — and neither wins out as the hero. This is the same bundle-discount well Crocs was in on 9/1 ("Up to 50% off styles worth obsessing over!"), just with a second promo bolted on top, which makes it feel even more like an inventory-clearing email than a campaign. There's no collab, no story, no seasonal hook — just two percentage-off blocks and a generic clog lineup shot.
- The product image (four clog angles floating on blue) is at least clean and legible, and the black CTA buttons are unmissable. But compared to the Pokémon, NFL, and Collegiate launches this brand has been running, this reads like a filler send between real campaigns — and stacking two discount tiers actually adds confusion rather than urgency.

## What's working

- Both CTAs ("Shop the Sale") are bold, high-contrast black pill buttons that stand out clearly against their colored backgrounds.
- Free shipping banner up top is a clean, simple value-add above the fold.
- Product shot shows recognizable, colorway-varied clogs (pink, gray, white, cream) with no render bugs or broken images.
- Refer-a-friend block (25% give/get) is a nice secondary CTA that doesn't compete visually with the main offer.

## What's weak

- Two stacked discount offers (30% off clearance, then 50% off select) with no clear hierarchy — the eye doesn't know which one is "the" offer.
- No hero product story, collab, or seasonal tie-in — pure generic bundle-discount, same beat as 9/1's "Up to 50% off" send two days ago.
- "Up to 50% Off" is vague — no indication of what qualifies, and "Select Footwear and Accessories" doesn't narrow it down visually.
- Product image only shows clogs, so anyone shopping accessories (mentioned in copy) sees nothing relevant to that half of the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one offer. Running "Buy 2, Take 30%" and "Up to 50%" in the same email forces the reader to do math to figure out which deal is better — pick the stronger, clearer one and lead with it alone.
- 2. Replace the generic clog lineup with a styled scene (worn, in-context) — every recent higher-performing send (Pokémon, camo boat shoe) used product storytelling; this one reverted to flat product shots.
- 3. If accessories are actually included in the 50% off, show at least one Jibbitz or accessory in the hero image — right now the visual only supports footwear.
- 4. Add a concrete floor/ceiling to "up to 50%" (e.g., "50% off clearance clogs") so the offer feels less like bait.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 2, get 30% off clearance — plus up to 50% off select styles`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart called. It wants 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the savings: clearance deals plus new markdowns, ends 9/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two ways to save before Wednesday — see what qualifies.`
