# Hurry! 30% Off + Free Shipping Won't Last
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send in five days pushing the same 30% off + free shipping promo. It follows Oct 2's "Limited-Time Offer", Oct 3's "Reminder" and Oct 4's "Looks Good. Feels Good. Get Yours." The email is clear and loud. A big pink block carries a heavy black headline, "Don't Forget: You Can Still Get 30% Off + Free Shipping!", with a black "Shop the Sale" pill right below it. You get the offer in about a second.
- It also reads as a copy of the Oct 3 reminder. There's no new product, story or reason to act beyond "the clock is ticking." The only real deadline, Oct 7 at 11:59pm MT, is buried in the fine print. The grey Baya clog under the "SALE" lettering is nice, but it isn't linked to anything specific and it sits below the CTA. A subscriber who saw Oct 2 and Oct 3 has already decided whether this offer is for them.
- Verdict: a competent, readable reminder with no new reason to click. The creative is stale and the urgency is vague.

## What's working

- The headline is huge and states the offer outright: 30% off plus free shipping.
- The black "Shop the Sale" button is high-contrast and sits directly under the copy.
- The black free-shipping banner at the top reinforces the offer before the logo.
- The pink hero with the grey Baya clog and "SALE" lettering is on-brand and playful.
- The footer is tidy. It has a referral offer (give 25%, get 25%), app download badges and a store locator.

## What's weak

- Urgency is vague: "ends soon" and "Hurry" with no date in the body. The Oct 7 deadline appears only in tiny footer text.
- The shoe is below the CTA, unlinked in appearance, and one generic clog stands in for the whole sale.
- "Exclusions Apply" and "select styles only" undercut the "Don't Forget: You Can Still Get 30% Off" promise.
- It's a near-repeat of Oct 3's "There's Still Time: Take 30% Off + Get Free Shipping!" with a recolored hero.
- There are no people, no reviews, no named products or prices, and no category cues to make it feel personal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends tomorrow, Oct 7, 11:59pm MT." A real date is stronger than "Hurry."
- 2. Replace the single clog with a 3–4 product grid showing sale prices ("Baya Clog $41.99, was $59.99"). Each tile should link to its own product page.
- 3. Split the sale by category (clogs, sandals, Jibbitz) with category CTAs such as "Shop Clogs on Sale."
- 4. Move the exclusions into a short line next to the offer so it doesn't read like fine print being hidden.
- 5. After three near-identical promo emails, make this final-call send different. Use a countdown, a "last day" framing, or a fresh hero.
- 6. Add one social-proof line, such as a bestseller badge or a star rating on the featured product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 30% off ends tomorrow night`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% off + free shipping expires Oct 7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clogs, sandals and Jibbitz are all included. Ends Oct 7 at 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping is already applied. Pick your pair before midnight tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (viewport meta, Outlook/VML conditionals, Apple/Gmail hacks, a hidden preheader). Confirmed issues are 7 `http://` image sources, a malformed closing tag after the open pixel, and a tracking pixel with no `alt`. The 3 header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay may have stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup on the open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?Y4QCY247UUXEFOIG7TSW2IK7FM.10204&d=10204&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML.
- **Unprobed links:** 20 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the QA probe. Their destinations, redirect chains, and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It is separate from the ExactTarget open pixel, so opens are tracked twice, and it carries a per-recipient `d_dpuid`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the demdex `event` image has no `alt` attribute. The QA check flagged it. Giving it `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions would mark it as decorative.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** these 7 images load over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`:
  - App Store badge (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play badge (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Clients that block mixed content, or proxy images and fail the upgrade, may drop these. The same host already serves HTTPS elsewhere in the email (the `click.crocs-email.com` pixel), so the fix is likely a scheme change.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` does not describe this message. It is minor, since email clients rarely surface it.
- The font stack loads `TT Crocs` from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. A fallback is needed for clients that block web fonts, but the truncated source doesn't show whether one is declared.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, truncated source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[First Name]`. The rest of the body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes the relay may not capture them, so this cannot be confirmed as a sender-side defect. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so neither could be verified.
- **Sender identity:** From is `email@crocs-email.com`, a dedicated sending domain consistent with the tracking and image hosts. No mismatch found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no destination hrefs, and all 20 tracking links were skipped by the probe. Whether the redirects land on pages that match the "30% Off + Free Shipping" offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Switch the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/…` images to `https://`.** This is the one clear, low-effort fix.
2. **Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel** and add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon.
3. **Confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) on the original send.** Check the raw message at the receiving mailbox rather than the AgentMail copy.
4. **Resolve the 20 click-tracking redirects** (follow the redirects rather than HEAD-probing) to verify destination status, UTM parameters, and offer alignment.
5. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML** to check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address, and any merge tokens.
