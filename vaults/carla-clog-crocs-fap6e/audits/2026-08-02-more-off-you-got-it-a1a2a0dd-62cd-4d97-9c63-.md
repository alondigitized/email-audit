# more off? you got it!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Crocs back to the discount well — "Extra 25% off" stacked on top of styles "already up to 50% off," which lands right after the 8/1 "Coastal State of Mind" product-launch send that broke the July discount-fatigue streak. So the brand had exactly one good beat (product story) and immediately reverted to stacked-percentage-off mode, which is the same fatigue pattern that dogged the 7/24–7/28 run.
- Visually this is thinner than either of the last two sends. There's no hero product shot with styling or context — just two Classic Clog silhouettes floating on a lavender field, cropped so tight you can't tell they're shoes without prior brand knowledge. No model, no lifestyle context, no size/fit info. It reads as a coupon email wearing Crocs' color palette.
- The one smart move is the Jibbitz banner up top ($2 singles, $7 packs) — that's a real, specific, low-price impulse offer that a Jibbitz collector persona would click before even reading the main deal. Otherwise this is a mid-pack promo: functional, on-brand colors, clear CTA, but no product story and a headline math problem ("extra 25% off already up to 50% off") that reads more like a workout than an offer.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop the Sale") with strong contrast against the lavender block.
- The Jibbitz sub-offer ($2/$7) is specific and gives a second, distinct reason to click beyond the main discount.
- Clean color-blocked layout with no render bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.
- "Free Shipping on Qualified Orders" banner up top reinforces value before the fold.
- Refer-a-friend block (25%/25%) is a reasonable secondary CTA for existing customers.

## What's weak

- No lifestyle or product-in-context imagery — just two isolated shoe caps with no visible silhouette, style name, or price.
- Headline stacks two percentages ("Extra 25% off" on "already up to 50% off") which is confusing math, not a clean single number.
- This is a straight discount-only send right after Crocs' best recent creative (8/1 "Coastal State of Mind"), so the brand is reverting to the fatigue pattern it just escaped.
- No urgency date visible above the fold — the "ends Monday, August 17th" deadline is buried in the fine print at the very bottom.
- Subject line "more off? you got it!" is vague and doesn't signal a specific number or product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, not just the footer fine print — "Ends 8/17" next to the CTA would add real urgency that's currently invisible until you scroll past unsubscribe links.
- 2. Replace the cropped shoe-cap crop with a full product shot (or two) showing the actual silhouette — right now it's unrecognizable as a Classic Clog without brand familiarity.
- 3. Simplify the headline math — "Extra 25% Off Sale Styles" alone is cleaner than pairing it with "already up to 50% off," which reads as two competing numbers.
- 4. Pull the Jibbitz $2/$7 hook down into the body as a real content block with images, not just a top banner — it's the most specific, high-converting offer here and it's underused.

## Technical Audit — Crocs "more off? you got it!" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard responsive MSO/Outlook-conditional template with acceptable structural markup, but fails several deliverability and compliance checks: missing unsubscribe/authentication headers, six non-HTTPS image sources, and one tracking pixel lacking alt text.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 21 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped from HTTP probing (QA: "21 tracking link(s) skipped: Tracking/click-redirect domains"), so live-link validity (200 vs. broken/redirect-loop) is unconfirmed for the majority of CTAs.
- One open/tracking pixel confirmed: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f7d8fbc329b0a8ea915c05b3c631d53c` (Adobe Audience Manager open-tracking beacon). Served over HTTPS — no issue there.
- Recommend a manual click-through pass on the 21 skipped links to confirm they resolve to live pages before send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Missing `alt` attribute on the demdex tracking pixel `<img>` (QA: "Image missing alt text: event"). Low visual impact (1x1 pixel) but still fails WCAG image-alt requirements and email accessibility scanners (e.g., Litmus, Email on Acid) will flag it.
- No other missing-alt warnings were reported by QA for content images (app store badges, social icons) — those only failed on the HTTP-vs-HTTPS check below, not alt text.
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and viewport meta — no rendering red flags found in the visible source.
- Hidden preheader "buffer" block (`&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` repeated ~20x) is a common technique to suppress client auto-preview fallback; not itself a defect but worth noting as it adds ~600 bytes of non-visual markup.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g., `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{first_name}}`, ESP-specific placeholders) are present in the visible HTML source. Cannot fully confirm absence beyond the truncation point — recommend re-checking the untruncated source for any unresolved token syntax that would render literally in-inbox.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: Not found (QA WARN). Required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules; if genuinely absent (vs. relay-stripped), this is a compliance gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: Not found (QA WARN). Needed alongside `List-Unsubscribe` for true one-click behavior.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): Not found (QA WARN) — status unknown via the AgentMail relay used for this audit.
- Note: all three WARNs are flagged as possibly relay-related ("may not be captured by AgentMail relay") rather than confirmed sender-side omissions — verify against raw headers from the original SMTP transaction (not the relay copy) before treating as a hard compliance failure.
- In-body unsubscribe link/footer was not visible within the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) from this excerpt.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot verify — all click-through links fall into the 21 skipped tracking/redirect-domain links, so UTM parameter presence/consistency and final landing-page alignment were not probed. Recommend resolving the redirect chains for at least the primary CTA(s) to confirm UTM tagging (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) lands correctly on crocs.com.
