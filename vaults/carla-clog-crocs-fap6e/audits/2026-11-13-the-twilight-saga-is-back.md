# The Twilight Saga is back
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a licensed-collab launch with no price offer, and it's a clear break from the 2-for-$50 drumbeat Crocs ran Nov 10–12. The hero is strong. The Twilight logo, moody teal-and-black styling and a model in the clogs say "fandom" immediately. The creative targets nostalgic Twilight fans and collectors, not deal hunters.
- Past the hero, the email loses focus. Two near-identical teal copy blocks ("Get Obsessed", "Your Next Obsession") each end in the same "Shop 'The Twilight Saga'" button. The only urgency is "limited restock" and "limited-edition", with no date or stock signal. There is no price anywhere, so a shopper can't tell whether a Jibbitz mystery pack is $15 or $50.
- Judgement: a good brand-moment email that is thin on commerce. It will pull in fans, but it will convert below the clean single-offer sends from earlier this week.

## What's working

- The hero ties the collection to a recognisable franchise, with the logo, product and model all visible in one frame.
- The collectibles box shot is a strong second beat and shows exactly what a mystery pack contains.
- Black pill CTAs on teal have high contrast and are easy to tap.
- It's a refreshing change of pace after four near-identical 2-for-$50 emails.
- The 2×2 secondary nav (Clogs, Jibbitz™ Charms, Sale, Sandals) gives an escape route for non-fans.

## What's weak

- No price, discount or member perk appears anywhere. The only offer-like text is the "Free shipping on qualified orders" banner.
- The two CTAs carry the identical label "Shop 'The Twilight Saga'", which wastes a chance to name a product.
- "Limited restock" and "limited-edition" have no deadline or quantity, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Both copy blocks are long and repeat the same character names, which makes the body feel padded.
- The teal copy panels sit far below the hero, so the first screen has no CTA. A mobile reader has to scroll past the image to find a button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a CTA and one concrete fact in the hero, such as a price or "Back in stock." Right now a reader sees a beautiful image with no action.
- 2. Add a price or a member perk, for example "Mystery packs $X" or "Members shop first."
- 3. Give the two buttons different jobs: "Shop Twilight Clogs" and "Shop Jibbitz Mystery Packs".
- 4. Cut each body paragraph to one or two lines and make the character names the hook.
- 5. Add real urgency, such as "While supplies last" or a restock date.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Twilight Crocs are back in stock 🦇`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Team Edward or Team Jacob? Pick your Jibbitz`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited restock: Twilight clogs and mystery charm packs`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bella, Edward & Jacob charms are back. Grab yours before they go.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard SFMC (Exact Target) markup with sound client-compatibility hacks. It has one malformed tag and a third-party Adobe pixel without alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed as sender defects, because the headers weren't captured by the AgentMail relay. The source is also truncated, so the footer and body links couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking was not probed.** 17 links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status codes and redirect chains are unverified. The open pixel and click links use `click.crocs-email.com`, which matches the sender domain `crocs-email.com`.
- **Malformed markup around the open pixel.** The tag is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` has no opening tag. It is harmless in most clients, but it is invalid HTML and strict parsers may handle it inconsistently.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` fires on open, and the QA check flags it for missing alt text. It is a tracking beacon, so this is low severity. It is also a third-party data-sync call that depends on a separate domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text.** The demdex image has no `alt` attribute (the QA warning). The first open pixel correctly uses `alt=""`.
- **Document title.** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. It does not reflect the subject, "The Twilight Saga is back", which is a minor issue for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Preheader.** The visible preheader is "Limited restock", followed by a hidden buffer of `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp; &#8199; &shy;` entities. This is a correct implementation.
- **Compatibility handling.** These are all present:
  - the MSO/VML namespaces and `OfficeDocumentSettings`
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`
  - `format-detection`
  - `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` overrides
  - the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack
  - a mobile breakpoint at 639px
- **Web fonts.** `TT Crocs` loads via `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. No fallback stack is visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.
- **Body structure.** The `role="presentation"` attribute on layout tables and the `alt` text on content images can't be assessed because the source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The body past the truncation point is not covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay. Treat these as unverified, not confirmed defects. Verify against the raw message at the original receiving mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain `crocs-email.com` should be checked directly with `dig TXT` for SPF and DMARC.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address.** Neither could be verified because the source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. Because click links are wrapped in SFMC redirects and weren't probed, the final destination URLs and any `utm_*` parameters can't be confirmed. The visible source shows no direct, unwrapped product or landing-page URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel, or load it in a way that doesn't render as an `<img>` content element.
3. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML. That would cover the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, `role="presentation"` usage, and content-image alt text.
4. Resolve the click-tracking links, either with a redirect-following probe or by pulling the SFMC link export. This would confirm the destinations and UTM parameters, and that none of the 17 links is broken.
5. Capture the raw headers at the original mailbox, bypassing the AgentMail relay. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
6. Optionally, set `<title>` to the campaign subject.
