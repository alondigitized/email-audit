# Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday email Crocs has sent all week. The hero is up to 60% off plus an extra 20% off (almost) everything, with code COZY20. That is the stacked offer from the Nov 24 "Your cart deserves a little treat." send and from both Nov 25 sends, including "More savings? That's a wrap. 🎁". Only the creative changed: a black platform clog on a red gift box with ribbon.
- The body is a stack of unrelated pitches: Minnie Mouse 50% off, kids' clogs under $25, TikTok viral styles, classic clogs from $30, and an Afterpay block. The hero code and the doorbuster prices all compete. The offers are concrete, and the hero CTA is clear. But the subject "Pssst... there's an EXTRA 20% off inside" teases a secret that has been public all week. There is also no urgency until the fine print says the offer ends Sunday, Nov 29.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but fatigued. It is a repeat send with a new subject line.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "UP TO 60% OFF" is big, with the extra 20% and the code COZY20 directly beneath it.
- The black "Shop Black Friday" button is unambiguous and sits above the fold.
- The red Minnie banner is a strong colour break and carries a specific 50% offer.
- Price-anchored modules ("under $25", "starting at $30") give concrete numbers, and the oversized price graphics are easy to scan.
- The module CTAs are consistent and the layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero repeats the stacked offer from the Nov 24 and Nov 25 sends, so there is nothing new for a subscriber who has already seen it.
- "Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside" implies exclusivity, but the offer is a public site-wide code.
- "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" and "Exclusions Apply" are right in the hero. The tail of the offer is hedged, and the fine print under the footer is dense.
- The CTA labels "Shop Black Friday," "Shop the Hype" and "Shop All Doorbusters" are generic. Two different buttons point at doorbusters.
- There is no deadline in the hero. The end date, Sunday Nov 29 at 11:59pm MT, is buried in the footer.
- The email has too many modules: Minnie, kids, TikTok, classics and Afterpay. The Afterpay block adds clutter after the last product module.
- There is no social proof or reviews anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday 11:59pm MT", and move the code into a bolder, copyable chip.
- 2. Cut the body to two or three modules and drop the Afterpay block, or move it to the footer.
- 3. Make the buttons name the product, for example "Shop clogs from $30" and "Shop kids' clogs under $25".
- 4. Align the creative and the subject. If the subject says "pssst", the hero should lead with the code and the reveal. Alternatively, send a different angle on the stacked offer to avoid the repetition.
- 5. Add a line of proof, such as a star rating, under one product module.
- **Subject Alt A:** `COZY20 = extra 20% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clogs from $30 + 20% off with COZY20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 60% off, then take 20% more at checkout. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Classic clogs from $30. Kids' clogs under $25. Use code COZY20.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crocs, "Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside."

Only a truncated part of the HTML (head, preheader and tracking pixels) was available. Anything in the body or footer, such as product links, UTMs and the unsubscribe block, could not be checked and is not assessed.

## 1. Technical Summary
The head markup is mostly sound: viewport, Outlook (MSO) conditionals, dark-mode-safe resets and `font-display: swap`. There is one malformed tag and an unverified tracking footprint. The QA run reported 0 issues and 3 warnings, and the two compliance warnings and the authentication warning most likely reflect AgentMail relay limits rather than sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 25 links on the click-redirect domain `click.crocs-email.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / ExactTarget) were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are **unverified**.
- The open pixel is `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0`. It is 1x1 with `alt=""`, which is correct for a tracking pixel.
- A second pixel at `demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` (Adobe Audience Manager) fires on open and carries a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`). This is third-party tracking, which privacy-focused clients and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or prefetch. Open counts from either pixel will be unreliable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed markup:** the tracking wrapper closes with a stray `</custom>`: `<img src="…open.aspx…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. No `<custom>` element is opened, so this is invalid HTML. Some strict parsers or clients may handle it unpredictably.
- **Missing alt text (QA warning):** the demdex `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is a hidden tracking pixel, so the impact is low, but it fails automated accessibility checks. Add `alt=""`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is the same for every send and is not descriptive. The impact is minor, mainly in the web-view and some screen readers.
- **Fine in the visible head:** `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` are set. `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present. The Gmail `u + .email-body` hack is present. There is a mobile breakpoint at `max-width: 639px`.
- **Not verifiable (truncated):** `role="presentation"` on layout tables, alt text on content images, and the fallback font stack for `TT Crocs`. The web fonts load from `media.crocs.com`, and clients that block remote fonts will fall back.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN) and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN, RFC 8058):** the QA note says these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message at the original recipient. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Verify against the sending domain `crocs-email.com` directly (DNS records) rather than from relayed headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are in the truncated part and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 25 destination links were behind SFMC redirects and unresolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "extra 20% off" offer could not be confirmed. The preheader says "Black Friday up to 60% off + an extra 20% off (almost) everything." Whether the landing pages carry the same offer is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel (or to the Adobe tag).
3. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating these as sender defects. If AgentMail strips them, adjust the QA check so it stops raising false warnings.
4. Resolve the 25 `click.crocs-email.com` links with a follow-redirect probe (HEAD or GET) and record the final URL, status and UTM parameters. This would close the gaps in sections 2 and 6.
5. Pass the full HTML, not a truncated copy, to the audit so the footer, `<img>` alts and link markup can be checked.
