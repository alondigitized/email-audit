# Plus an extra 25% off?!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean Black Friday sale email with a clear offer stack, but it asks the reader to do too much math. "Up to 60% off plus an extra 25% off" sits on a yellow hero, and a black "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill sits under it. The yellow block, bold type and large lifestyle photo make the offer the first thing you see. It has none of the rendering bugs that would hurt it.
- The weak spot is credibility. "Select Sale Styles," "Exclusions Apply" and two different expiry dates in the fine print (Nov 22 and Nov 29) undercut a loud headline. The Grinch banner and the TikTok "Shop the Hype" section pull in different directions. Compared with the Nov 15 "Black Friday Deals Start Now!" send, which led with a similar "up to 60% off" catalog, this is an incremental escalation, not a new idea. A subscriber who has seen seven promo variants in ten days will skim it.
- Overall it is a competent 6. It is clear and on-brand, but it is generic and its stacked discount is hard to trust.

## What's working

- The hero offer is large, high-contrast and readable in a second: "60% OFF" is the biggest type on the page.
- The single black CTA pill against the yellow background is unambiguous.
- The lifestyle photo is bright and aligned with the yellow, so the layout feels cohesive.
- The Grinch banner gives a specific product and a specific discount (45% off plus an extra 25%) that is more concrete than the hero.
- The free-shipping bar at the top adds a second incentive without competing with the hero.

## What's weak

- The hero is padded: a large empty yellow band sits above the photo, so the offer is crowded at the top and a lot of scroll is spent before any product detail.
- "Select Sale Styles," "Exclusions Apply" and "Up to" hedge the offer. The reader cannot tell what actually gets 85% off.
- Two expiry dates appear only in the fine print (Nov 22 and Nov 29), and the body has no countdown or deadline cue.
- No prices, ratings or product names appear outside the Grinch banner. "Shop the Hype" and "Shop Black Friday Deals" go to categories, not a hero product.
- The TikTok section feels like a different email, and its copy ("a whole lot more tempting") is vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the real deal. Replace "Up to 60% off plus an extra 25%" with a concrete example, such as "Classic Clogs from $X after code/cart discount," and show an actual price.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Sunday") and reconcile the two dates in the fine print.
- 3. Cut the empty yellow space above the photo so the hero image and CTA sit higher.
- 4. Promote the Grinch item into the hero, or link it to its specific product page with a price.
- 5. Replace "Shop the Hype" with a verb and noun such as "Shop TikTok Favorites," and add a review count or "#1 seller" proof.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off sale styles, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: Grinch clogs 45% off + 25%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on qualified orders, plus new styles just added to the sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The extra 25% is applied in cart. Exclusions apply, offer ends Nov 29.`

# Technical Audit: Crocs, "Plus an extra 25% off?!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) template with sound responsive and Outlook handling, plus one malformed tag in the tracking block. The HTML is truncated, so link, footer and header checks are mostly unverifiable. All 3 QA warnings are about missing data (relay-captured headers and skipped probes), not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tag (confirmed in source):** the open pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>`:
  `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`
  There is no matching `<custom>` opening tag. Browsers ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and may trip strict parsers or sanitizers.
- **Third-party pixel without alt (QA):** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beacon with no `alt`. It is decorative, so the impact is minor. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`. This pixel did not appear in the truncated source, so I couldn't check its markup.
- **15 tracking links not probed (QA):** these are click-redirect domains such as `click.crocs-email.com`, which the QA run skips by design. Link destinations and status codes are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Good:** `lang` and `xml:lang` are set. `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` are present. The Outlook `sup` fix, the Gmail hack (`u + .email-body #new-gmail-hack`), the `[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset and the 639px mobile breakpoint are all in place.
- **Minor:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic and gives no document context.
- **Unverifiable:** the rest of the body is truncated, so I can't check alt text on content images, table roles, or font fallback stacks. The `@font-face` rules for TT Crocs (`media.crocs.com/raw/upload/marketing/…woff2`) use `font-display: swap`.
- No other issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body could still contain some.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post missing (QA WARN):** the QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, so it is not a verified defect. Check the raw message at the original receipt point before treating it as a failure. RFC 8058 one-click support is now expected by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. The sending domain `crocs-email.com` should be checked directly (DNS or a seed-inbox test).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Landing pages not checked:** the 15 tracking links were skipped, so landing-page alignment is unchecked.
- **Open-tracking identifiers:** the demdex beacon carries `d_dpuid` and `c_emailopened=Yes`, so the open event is also sent to Adobe Audience Manager. Whether the consent and privacy disclosure covers this is a legal question, not something the source settles.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>` (the only confirmed HTML defect).
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex `event` beacon.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the original message, not through the relay. Until then, treat the 3 compliance and deliverability warnings as unresolved, not failed.
4. Run the QA probe on the 15 wrapped links with redirect-following enabled, so the final landing URLs and UTMs can be checked.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, or at least the footer, so the unsubscribe link, postal address and merge tokens can be checked.
