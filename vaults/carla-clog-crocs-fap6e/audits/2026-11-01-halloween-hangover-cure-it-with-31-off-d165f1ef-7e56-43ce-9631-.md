# Halloween hangover? Cure it with 31% Off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer last-day send. "LAST DAY TO SAVE! / 31% Off / Select Styles" sits in big black type on cream, with a black "Shop The Sale" pill directly beneath it. The eye lands on the number first and the button second. The only competing element is the free-shipping banner at the top.
- The hero is a bone Baya clog shown three ways on a chocolate-brown panel with cream swirls. It's tidy and on-brand, but it's a neutral shoe with no model, no price and no link-specific detail. This is the third 31% send in a row, after 10/30 "Scary-good deals!" and 10/31 "Flash Sale: 31% off select styles!". The "last day" framing is credible, but the creative is a small remix of the last two.
- Judgement: well executed and clear, but repetitive. The subject's Halloween-hangover hook is the freshest thing in it, and the body never picks that joke up.

## What's working

- "Last Day to Save!" sits directly above a huge "31% Off", which makes the deadline credible and easy to read.
- The black "Shop The Sale" pill has strong contrast against the cream panel and is the clear primary action.
- The free-shipping bar at the top adds a second incentive without competing with the hero.
- The Baya hero is large and clean, and the dark brown panel makes the pale clog stand out.
- Nothing is broken: no overlapping text, no missing images, and a simple layout.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Halloween hangover" joke is not echoed anywhere in the body, so the voice drops off after the open.
- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply." are vague. The email never shows which styles are on sale.
- The hero is one neutral clog with no price, no sale tag and no link-specific copy, so it adds little reason to click.
- The fine print sits at the very bottom. The deadline, "Sunday, November 1st, 2026 at 11:59pm MT", appears only there, not near the CTA.
- This is the third 31% send in three days, and the creative barely changes (cream or maroon panel, Baya clog, a "Shop…" pill). That is offer fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline next to the CTA, for example "Ends tonight, 11:59pm MT", and add a countdown or a time-of-day cue. This is the biggest lift, because the urgency is currently buried in the footer.
- 2. Carry the hangover joke into the hero, for example "Halloween's over. The savings aren't (yet)."
- 3. Replace or supplement the single hero with a 3-4 product grid showing sale prices, so "Select Styles" becomes concrete.
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop 31% Off Clogs".
- 5. Vary the creative against the 10/30 and 10/31 sends, or skip a send. Three near-identical 31% emails in a row train people to ignore them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard SFMC (ExactTarget) build with a responsive stylesheet, MSO conditionals and a 1x1 open pixel. The only confirmed code defect is a malformed closing tag near the open pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, not from the email, so they can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is closed with a stray tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML.
- **Third-party pixel:** the QA scan found a second pixel, `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`. It is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon that carries a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`). Some clients and privacy proxies block it, so open data from it is unreliable.
- **Unverified links:** 15 links on `click.crocs-email.com` were skipped as click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **Not assessable:** the HTML is truncated, so I can't check the CTA hrefs or the footer links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the demdex image has no `alt` (QA warning). It is a tracking pixel, so this is low severity. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` for consistency with the SFMC pixel.
- **Head and client fixes present:** doctype, `lang`/`xml:lang="en"`, viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`, the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack, and a mobile breakpoint at `max-width: 639px`.
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. I can't see the inline font stacks in the truncated source, so the fallback fonts are unverified.
- **Title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. This is minor, and screen readers and some web-view contexts read it.
- **Hidden elements:** the preheader and the `&#847; &zwnj; &nbsp;` padding block are hidden correctly with `display:none`, `max-height:0` and `overflow:hidden`.
- **Not assessable:** alt text on the content images, and the HTML-to-text ratio, in the truncated portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The tail of the HTML is truncated, so I can't check it.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both as not found. It notes the relay may not capture them, so these are unconfirmed rather than missing. Check them in the raw source headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click for senders at this volume.
