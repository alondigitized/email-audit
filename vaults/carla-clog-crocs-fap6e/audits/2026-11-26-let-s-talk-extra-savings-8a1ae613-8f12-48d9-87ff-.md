# Let's talk EXTRA savings.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same Black Friday email Crocs has sent all week, in a new order. The hero is still "UP TO 60% OFF + AN EXTRA 20% OFF (ALMOST) EVERYTHING" with code COZY20. That matches this morning's "Pssst...there's an EXTRA 20% off inside." and the Nov 24 "Your cart deserves a little treat." send. A subscriber who has seen nine of these will not find anything new here.
- The one new element is the red banner above the hero: "50% off Minnie Mouse Classic Clogs today!" It's a concrete, time-bound offer. It's also the only differentiated thing in the email, and it sits in a thin strip above a hero that repeats a different offer. The hero image is two neutral and blue Classic clogs with gold ribbon, with no Minnie product anywhere, so the banner promise has no visual support. The TikTok "From Your FYP" block with fuzzy lined clogs is a nice change of pace, but it gets a generic "Shop the Hype" button.
- Judgement: competent, clean and readable, but fatigued. It's a fourth or fifth rerun of the same hero, with a mixed message about which offer is the point.

## What's working

- The offer hierarchy in the hero is clear: a huge "60% OFF" with the code COZY20 right below it.
- The black pill CTA, "Shop Black Friday," is high contrast and unambiguous.
- The red Minnie banner is a loud, specific, same-day hook.
- The footer carries a clear expiry (Sunday, Nov 29th, 11:59pm MT) and the legal copy is visible.
- The TikTok-style lifestyle photo adds some warmth, and the "From Your FYP" framing fits the audience.

## What's weak

- The hero repeats the creative and offer from earlier sends this week (Nov 24, Nov 25 and this morning), so there's no sense of news.
- The Minnie banner promises a specific product but nothing in the email shows it, and it isn't clickable as a hero.
- Two offers compete: 50% off Minnie today versus 60% plus 20% off sitewide. The eye doesn't know which one matters.
- "Exclusions Apply" and "(ALMOST) EVERYTHING" put the fine print right next to the big claim. That feels slightly less honest.
- "Shop the Hype" is vague. The fuzzy-clog image isn't labeled with a product name or price.
- The email has no reviews, no loyalty or member benefit and no sizing detail.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero. If Minnie at 50% off is the day's news, put a Minnie product image in the hero and move the sitewide stack below it. If it isn't, drop the banner.
- 2. Stop reusing the same stacked-offer hero across a week of sends. Rotate in a real new angle, such as a last-chance countdown, a best-seller grid, or a price-anchored single product.
- 3. Replace "Shop the Hype" with something specific, like "Shop Fuzzy Lined Clogs," and add a product name and price under the lifestyle photo.
- 4. Add a time-bound element such as "Ends Sunday" near the hero CTA, not only in the footer.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings or "most-loved" tags, to the TikTok section.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 60% off and an extra 20% with code COZY20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The TikTok-viral fuzzy clogs are on sale. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) build with MSO and Apple/Gmail client hacks. It has one malformed tag and one unlabeled third-party pixel. The source was truncated, and all three QA warnings are unverifiable rather than confirmed defects, so the unsubscribe footer, CTA links and merge tokens could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Stray closing tag.** The open-pixel markup is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10209&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup and may come from a template or AMPscript artifact.
- **Unlabeled Adobe Audience Manager pixel.** QA flagged an `<img>` with no alt text: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`.
  - It is a third-party tracking beacon, not content, so it should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1x1 dimensions.
  - The `d_dpuid` value is a per-recipient identifier passed to a third-party domain (`demdex.net`).
  - Some clients and privacy proxies block this domain.
- **Unprobed redirects.** 15 links on `click.crocs-email.com` were skipped, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **No evidence of broken links.** No link failures were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The visible head is sound: `lang`/`xml:lang`, a viewport meta, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, an MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block and a mobile breakpoint at 639px.
- The preheader is correctly hidden (`display:none !important`) and followed by a filler buffer.
- The Demdex pixel's missing alt is the only accessibility finding in the visible source.
- The `<title>` is the generic "Crocs" rather than the subject line. This is minor and affects only web-view and tab context.
- Alt text on content images and the font fallback stacks fall in the truncated portion, so they could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. There are no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` tokens in the preheader, head or pixel markup. The body was truncated, so token resolution there is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers.
  - This is a capture limitation, not confirmed non-compliance.
  - Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay. It is not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer unverified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source.
- **From domain.** The sending domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking domain `click.crocs-email.com`, which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All visible links are wrapped in SFMC click-redirects, so the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not in the source or QA data. Because the redirects were not probed, landing-page alignment with the "extra 20% off, code inside" offer is also unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove `</custom>`** after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. **Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`** to the `demdex.net` pixel. Confirm the Adobe Audience Manager sync and the `d_dpuid` recipient ID are covered by Crocs' privacy disclosures.
3. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML**, and resolve the 15 click-tracker URLs to their final destinations. Then check UTM completeness, the status of each destination, and that the promo code shown in the email works on the landing page.
4. **Verify headers from the raw message** (not via the relay): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
5. **Fix the QA tooling.** A 57% pass rate with 0 issues and 3 warnings suggests most checks were skipped, so treat the result as low-coverage. Capturing full headers in the harness would avoid recurring false-positive warnings.
