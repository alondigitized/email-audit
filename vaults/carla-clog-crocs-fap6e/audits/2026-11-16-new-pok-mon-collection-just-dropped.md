# New Pokémon collection just dropped
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product-drop email, and it's a refreshing change of pace. After six near-identical "2 for $50" sends from Nov 10–15, followed by the Black Friday catalog email on Nov 15, a collab launch with no price push is the right move. It echoes the Nov 13 "Twilight Saga is back" send, which also scored well by leading with licensed IP.
- The 2x2 hero grid works. Three lifestyle shots show the Jigglypuff, Pikachu and Gengar clogs, and a Pokémon x Crocs logo tile sits in the fourth cell. The eye lands on the pink and yellow clogs first, then the logo, then the lavender "Calling All Trainers" block with a single black "Shop Pokémon" button.
- The weakness is commercial. There's no price, no member perk and no deadline. The only incentive is a generic "free shipping on qualified orders" banner. It's a good awareness send, but it's thin on conversion pull. Judgement: a solid, well-executed collab announcement that relies entirely on fandom to drive the click.

## What's working

- The 2x2 hero grid shows three distinct characters on colour-matched backgrounds, so the collection reads in under two seconds.
- The Pokémon x Crocs logo lockup and copyright line make the licensed collab unmistakable.
- "Calling All Trainers" is playful, on-theme copy that names all three products (Pikachu, Gengar, Jigglypuff).
- There is one dominant black "Shop Pokémon" pill on a lavender field, so the primary CTA is unambiguous.
- Compared with the "2 for $50" run, this send gives subscribers a new story instead of a sixth repeat.

## What's weak

- No price, discount or member benefit appears anywhere, and nothing reflects the Black Friday momentum from Nov 15.
- No urgency or scarcity is shown, so "just dropped" gives no reason to click today rather than next week.
- The four secondary pills (Shop Clogs, Jibbitz Charms, Sale, Sandals) are equal-weight and generic. "Shop Sandals" in mid-November is off-season and dilutes focus.
- The hero images don't link to specific products that I can see, and there are no prices, reviews or sizing cues.
- The "Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25% Off!" bar is the only real offer, and it's buried below the fold in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive to the hero or the lavender block. Examples are "Pokémon clogs + free Jibbitz charm" and "Members get early access." The collab alone is carrying the whole email.
- 2. Add price tags and individual links to each hero tile, so each character image goes to its own product page.
- 3. Add a real deadline or scarcity cue, such as "Limited run" or "Selling fast," to justify opening today.
- 4. Replace the four generic pills with Pokémon-specific ones, such as "Shop Pikachu," "Shop Gengar," "Shop Jigglypuff" and "Shop Pokémon Jibbitz." Drop Sandals.
- 5. Move the 25% referral offer higher, or tie it to Black Friday.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a responsive, MSO-aware template. Confirmed defects are 7 non-HTTPS image sources, a malformed closing tag on the open-tracking pixel, and a third-party pixel with no `alt`. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed open pixel:** `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The `<img>` is closed by a stray `</custom>`, which is invalid markup. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers may mishandle the surrounding `<div style="height: 1px; overflow: hidden;">`.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It has no `alt` (flagged by QA). It also adds a second open-tracking call and a third-party domain to the render path.
- **Unverified links:** 24 tracking links on `click.crocs-email.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains and final status codes are unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images (7):** these load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. They are the footer and utility assets:
  - Download on the App Store (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Get It On Google Play (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube (`e18949ff-…`, `80f68cd8-…`, `51d8144e-…`, `1187ef58-…`)

  Clients that enforce HTTPS may block or warn on these, which would break the app-store, store-locator and social icons. Their alt text is present.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` does not match the subject. This is minor and only matters in web-view or browser rendering.
- **Positive checks:** `lang` and `xml:lang` are set. The viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` metas are present. There are MSO conditionals, a `@media (max-width: 639px)` block, and `font-display: swap` on the `TT Crocs` fonts. The preheader (`Featuring Pikachu, Jigglypuff, and Gengar`) is hidden correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`). The source is truncated, so content further down was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unverified, likely a capture artifact:** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe`, no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and no `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool itself says these may be dropped by the AgentMail relay. I can't confirm them as sender defects, so the SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status is unknown.
- **Not visible in the truncated source:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address. I can't confirm either way.
- **Sender:** `email@crocs-email.com` is a consistent branded sending domain, and it matches the tracking domains (`click.` and `image.crocs-email.com`).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are wrapped in ExactTarget redirects, so destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the source. The probe skipped them, so I can't check whether they land on a Pokémon collection page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all 7 `image.crocs-email.com` assets over `https://`. This is the only confirmed, directly fixable defect.
2. Replace the stray `</custom>` with proper `<img … />` closure on the open pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net` beacon.
4. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` or the original headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat the current warnings as sender faults.
5. Resolve the 24 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
6. Optionally set `<title>` to the subject line.
