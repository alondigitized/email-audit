# Starting today: Buy 2, get 30% off select styles
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer sale email. The hero reads "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" in huge black type on hot pink. A black "Shop The Sale" pill sits directly under it, and three Baya clogs fill the rest of the panel. The eye hits the number first and the button second.
- It is also a plain, undifferentiated promo. It follows the Halloween 31%-off run on 10/30, 10/31 and 11/1, and the launch sends on 11/3 ("Disney holiday styles"), 11/4 ("Classic Ballet") and 11/5 ("Harry Potter"). So this is the brand's sixth or seventh send in a week, and the first Buy 2 mechanic. The Buy 2 condition makes it a weaker offer than the flat 31% earlier. "Starting today" signals a new promo, but the email never says why now.
- The deadline is only in the legal copy at the bottom: "Offer ends Sunday, November 8th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT". Urgency is the biggest missed lever, and the second banner competes with the hero. Overall it is competent and forgettable.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and unambiguous: "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" in heavy black type on pink.
- The single black "Shop The Sale" pill has strong contrast and sits right under the headline, so there is no hunting for the CTA.
- The three-clog product shot in bone, olive and grey shows the "buy 2" idea visually. The pink background makes it pop.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" top bar adds a second reason to buy without crowding the hero.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The deadline (Sunday, Nov 8) appears only in the footer fine print, so the hero has no urgency.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears twice directly under the CTAs. "Select styles" plus exclusions makes the offer feel hedged.
- The 40% Accessories block competes with the hero. A second percentage and a second CTA split attention, and 40% beats 30%.
- Nothing names a product, price or style. "Amazing styles, excellent prices. A win-win situation." is filler copy.
- The Refer a Friend, app download and social rows add a lot of footer weight and no product value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday" or a countdown. The date is already known and only sits in legal copy.
- 2. Pick one offer and let the accessories ride as a small add-on line. If both stay, make it a bundle: "Buy 2 clogs, add accessories at 40% off".
- 3. Name the hero products and show a price. For example, "Baya Clog, now two for $X". Specificity lifts clicks more than "Select Styles".
- 4. Change the CTA to "Shop Buy 2 Styles" or "Shop 30% Off Clogs" so it says what is behind the button.
- 5. Replace the filler line with a real reason to act, such as gifting, cold-weather layering or "ahead of Black Friday".
- 6. Check cadence. After a week of daily sends, vary the creative so it doesn't look like another identical promo.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Two Bayas, 30% off. Today through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off accessories to finish the look. Free shipping on qualified orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, responsive ESP template (ExactTarget/Marketing Cloud tracking) with solid client-compatibility handling (MSO/VML, Apple data-detector, Gmail hacks). The confirmed defects are 7 non-HTTPS image sources and a malformed tracking-pixel tag. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open-tracking pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener, so this is invalid HTML. Most clients will ignore it, but strict parsers or sanitizers could mishandle it.
- **Unprobed links:** 24 tracking links were skipped because they are on click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`). Their final destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event pixel. It has no `alt` attribute, which the QA check flagged. It is a hidden tracking image, so the accessibility impact is minimal, but the attribute is missing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** 7 images load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. These clients may block or rewrite them, which would leave broken or missing icons.
  - "Download on the App Store" (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - "Get It On Google Play" (`13246762-…png`)
  - "Find Your Store" (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)
- **Image alt text:** the images listed above have alt text. Only the demdex pixel lacks it.
- **Document title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic and doesn't describe the email. This is minor.
- **Other markup:** the viewport meta, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block are present. The preheader (`Plus, accessories for 40% off!`) is hidden correctly. The HTML was truncated, so the rest of the body could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the remainder is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found. This is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `crocs-email.com` matches the tracking and image domains, which is consistent with a dedicated sending domain.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** these sit in the truncated footer, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 24 links go through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, which were not resolved, and the visible source shows no UTM parameters or final landing URLs. Continuity can't be assessed without following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image URLs to `https://`, and confirm the image host serves a valid certificate.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass results. Don't treat the relay-dependent warnings as defects until then.
5. Follow the tracking-link redirects in a separate pass to check the final URLs, HTTP status and UTM parameters.
6. Rerun the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and any merge tokens.
