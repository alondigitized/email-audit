# We Couldn't Wait Until Black Friday!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, two-offer Black Friday preview. The hero is a lime panel with "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" in heavy black type and a black "Shop The Sale" pill. Two clogs sit at the bottom. The eye lands on the offer first, as it should. A second tan panel adds "40% Off Accessories" with its own button.
- It's the same mechanic as yesterday's "Starting today: Buy 2, get 30% off select styles" (Nov 6), now recoloured lime. It also follows the 31% flash sale (Oct 31) and the 31% last-day send (Nov 1). A subscriber has seen this discount several times in eight days, and "Early Black Friday" doesn't feel like news. The subject promises an event but names no number. The real deadline, Sunday Nov 8 at 11:59pm MT, is buried in the fine print.
- Verdict: tidy, readable and low-risk, but repetitive. It will perform like the rest of this run (about 6/10).

## What's working

- The hero offer is the largest thing on screen and reads in a second.
- One strong black pill CTA in each panel, with good contrast on both backgrounds.
- The "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" bar at the top adds a second reason to buy.
- The second panel (40% off accessories) gives a lower-commitment entry point.
- The footer is orderly: referral offer, app download, store locator and unsubscribe are all easy to find.

## What's weak

- The urgency is vague ("The countdown is on"). The actual end date (Sunday, Nov 8) appears only in the small print at the bottom.
- The hero has a dead gap between "Exclusions Apply." and the shoes. The clogs are cropped at both edges and shown with no name, price or colour.
- There are two stacked offers and no clear hero product. Nothing links to a specific shoe.
- "Exclusions Apply" appears twice under the buttons, and the "select styles" wording makes the offer feel less honest.
- The creative and mechanic repeat Nov 6's Buy 2 / 30% send, so "Early Black Friday Deals" doesn't read as new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Sunday 11:59pm MT") and drop the vague countdown line. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the dead space and show named, priced products (for example Baya at $X, now $Y with Buy 2) instead of two anonymous cropped clogs.
- 3. Say what is new compared with Nov 6, or space the sends out. Frequent near-identical promos train people to ignore the next one.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Buy 2 Clogs" and "Shop Jibbitz & Accessories".
- 5. Show the exclusions once, in a single line, instead of repeating them under each button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 2, take 30% off — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: 30% off clogs + 40% off charms`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on qualified orders. Offer ends Sunday, Nov 8 at 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pair up your favorite clogs, then add 40% off accessories.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a clean responsive scaffold. The confirmed issues are 7 `http://` images, a malformed open-pixel tag, and an unlabeled third-party pixel. The source is truncated, so unsubscribe, footer and CTA markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 25 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.crocs-email.com`), so no destination, status or redirect-chain data exists. Dead links and UTM presence can't be confirmed.
- **Malformed open pixel:** the open-tracking `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt="">` is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag instead of being self-closed. Parsers will mostly ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Third-party pixel:** the Adobe Audience Manager pixel `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` has no `alt` attribute. It also passes a `d_dpuid` identifier to a third party on open. The `alt` fix is trivial. Whether the identifier is per-recipient can't be confirmed from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (7):** all are served from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`.
  - Files: `9c36c0ef-…png` (App Store badge), `13246762-…png` (Google Play badge), `c4df71ec-…png` (Find Your Store), `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram), `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook), `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok) and `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube).
  - Gmail's image proxy and some clients block or upgrade non-HTTPS images. The footer badges and social icons may fail to render, and the alt text is the only fallback.
- **Missing `alt` on the demdex pixel:** see section 2.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it reads "Crocs", which is low value, though minor for email.
- **No issues found with the head and CSS:**
  - Doctype, `lang`/`xml:lang` and viewport meta are present.
  - The `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` metas are present.
  - The MSO conditionals and the `@media (max-width: 639px)` rules look sound.
  - Web fonts are `@font-face` with `font-display: swap`.
  - The preheader is hidden correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`):** not found, but the QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. This is inconclusive, so I'm not flagging it as a sender defect. Check the raw `.eml` or the DKIM/SPF/DMARC records for `crocs-email.com` to confirm.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All CTAs route through `click.crocs-email.com` redirects that weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified. Resolving the redirects would settle it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the 7 `image.crocs-email.com` assets to `https://`. This is the only confirmed rendering defect, and the host likely supports HTTPS already.
2. Fix the `</custom>` stray tag on the open pixel and add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-run QA with link resolution (follow the redirects) to capture final URLs, status codes and UTM parameters.
4. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication headers from the original message source, not the relay copy.
5. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to audit the footer unsubscribe link, the address and the alt text on the main images.
