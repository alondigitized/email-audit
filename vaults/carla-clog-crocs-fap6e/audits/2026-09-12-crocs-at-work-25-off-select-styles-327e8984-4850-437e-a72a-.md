## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered layout — hero, CTA, product shot, footer, no render bugs or broken elements.
- "Free Shipping on Qualified Orders" banner up top is a real, visible incentive before you even scroll.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("Shop the Sale") with good contrast against the yellow hero.
- Deadline is present and specific in the footer fine print (ends Tuesday, September 15th, 2026, 11:59pm MT), giving the offer real urgency once you read it.

## What's weak

- "Crocs at Work" is a headline concept with zero visual support — no workplace context, uniform, or slip-resistant callout anywhere in the creative.
- Product shot is the same black Classic Clog used across multiple recent sends (9/7, 9/4) — no visual differentiation for this "occasion."

- This is at least the fourth percentage-off-styled email in the last two weeks (9/4, 9/7, 9/9, now 9/12) — cadence fatigue is real for a repeat subscriber.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

**7. Recommendations**

- Remove the stray `</custom>` closing tag adjacent to the open-tracking pixel — invalid HTML.
