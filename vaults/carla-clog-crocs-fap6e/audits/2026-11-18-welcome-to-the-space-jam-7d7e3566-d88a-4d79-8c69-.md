# 🏀 Welcome to the Space Jam
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean Space Jam 30th Anniversary collab launch, and the hero is the strongest part. A white clog with red, white and blue striping and a Tune Squad Jibbitz sits on a blue gradient. Bugs Bunny dunks a basketball next to it, and the Space Jam 30th logo is top-left. It's nostalgic and instantly readable. It also follows the pattern of Crocs' Nov 13 "The Twilight Saga is back" and Nov 16 "New Pokémon collection just dropped": collab drops that break from the price-led sends.
- The email then spends its lower half on gifting. "Gifts under $25" and "Stocking stuffers under $10" are huge, loud blocks that fight the hero for attention. Neither links to a specific product, and they aren't tied to the collab. The result is two emails stacked in one: a collab launch and a holiday price ladder.
- Overall it's a decent, on-brand send. The creative is good, but the focus is split and the copy is generic. It's a clear 6. The hero earns attention and then the layout dilutes it.

## What's working

- The hero is vivid and has a clear focal point. The clog, Bugs Bunny and the 30th-anniversary logo tell the story in one glance.
- The product is shown with its collab details, the Tune Squad charm and the striping.
- The "Shop Space Jam" button is a high-contrast black pill directly under the headline.
- The "$25" and "$10" price blocks are scannable and give concrete price anchors for gift shoppers.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" banner at the top adds a benefit without clutter.

## What's weak

- The two price blocks are visually heavier than the Space Jam CTA. The "$25" and "$10" numerals dominate the lower half.
- Neither price block has a visible button or product, so it's unclear what you get or where they link.
- The body copy, "Ball out with your squad in the brand-new collection featuring styles for adults and kids," is generic and names no product or price.
- The four outline buttons (Clogs, Jibbitz Charms, Sale, Sandals) are a generic nav row. "Shop Sandals" in late November feels off-season.
- The free-shipping banner says "qualified orders" with no threshold in the body. The fine print is buried in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one job.** Either keep the collab as the only story, or make the gift blocks secondary. Shrink the price blocks to half height and attach a product image and button to each.
- 2. **Name the product and price in the hero copy.** For example: "Space Jam Classic Clog, $64.99. Tune Squad Jibbitz from $5."
- 3. **Link the gift blocks to the collab.** Show Space Jam charms under the "$10" block so the stocking-stuffer idea supports the launch.
- 4. **Swap the nav row for seasonal items** such as Bays, lined clogs or Jibbitz, and drop sandals.
- 5. **State the shipping threshold** in the top banner, or drop the banner.

## Technical Audit: Crocs, "🏀 Welcome to the Space Jam"

**Caveat:** The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and CTA markup could not be inspected. The 20 tracking links were skipped by the probe. Findings below are limited to what the visible source and QA data support.

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is an SFMC (ExactTarget) send with a standard responsive and MSO-compatible head. The visible source has one malformed tag and one third-party pixel missing alt text. The QA header warnings are inconclusive rather than confirmed defects.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** The open pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>` tag:
  `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`
  `</custom>` is not a valid element. Parsers will ignore it, but it is a template defect.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It is a second open-tracking call on a different domain, and it carries a per-user ID (`d_dpuid`).
- **Unverified links:** The 20 click-redirect links on `click.crocs-email.com` were not probed. Link validity and destinations are **unverified**, not broken.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** The demdex image has no `alt` attribute (QA warning). It is a tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` would be cleaner.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. This has no rendering impact in most clients.
- **Handled correctly:** The head includes the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` metas, the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack, and a mobile breakpoint at 639px. The preheader is hidden with `display:none !important`, and a padding buffer follows it. No issues found there.
- **Fonts:** The `TT Crocs` `@font-face` rules load from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. Many clients ignore web fonts, so the fallback stack matters. The truncated source doesn't show whether one is declared.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The only per-recipient values are the encoded `qs=` and `d_dpuid` parameters, which rendered normally.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN):** The headers were not found. The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping or not capturing them. This is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message at the origin MTA. If they are absent, one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) is missing, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the header is missing. The relay is the likely cause. Check `crocs-email.com` alignment at the source rather than through the relay.
- **Footer, unsubscribe link and physical address:** These fall in the truncated portion of the source, so they cannot be assessed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The destination URLs and UTM parameters sit behind the 20 skipped `click.crocs-email.com` redirects and the truncated source. The QA run did not resolve them.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the demdex beacon. Confirm it is needed alongside the SFMC open pixel, since it adds a third-party request and exposes `d_dpuid`.
3. Re-run QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Resolve the redirect chains with a headless or `GET` follow-through to check landing status codes and UTM persistence. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to audit the footer.
5. Improve the QA harness so that relay-stripped headers are reported as "indeterminate" rather than WARN, to reduce false positives.
