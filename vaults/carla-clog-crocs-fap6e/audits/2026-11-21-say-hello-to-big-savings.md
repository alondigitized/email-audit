# Say Hello to Big Savings
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Black Friday catalog send in a week. It follows "No Lines. No Waiting. Just Big Deals." and "Plus an extra 25% off?!" on Nov 20. It is cleaner than the Nov 20 sends because it splits the offer into three clear tiers. The hero says "UP TO 60% OFF" on a dark brown field with a white "Shop Black Friday Deals" pill. Below it come two $30 price-point modules: animal-print fuzzy slides and Classic Clogs.
- The $30 Classic Clog modules are the real offers, and they are concrete. "Prices Straight from the Past" is a good hook. But the hero image shows buckled two-strap slides that aren't in any module below, so the headline product has no follow-up. Both module CTAs are the identical "Shop All Doorbusters". Having "Shop Black Friday Deals" plus two identical buttons makes the email feel like three doors into the same sale.
- Overall this is a competent, on-brand send that adds little to a week of near-identical promos. I'd call it a mid-tier email: clear, but with nothing new to say.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "60% OFF" in large white type on dark brown, with a white pill CTA that stands out.
- The $30 price points are concrete and easy to compare, for animal print and for Classic Clogs.
- The "Prices Straight from the Past" retro-pricing angle gives the clog module a hook beyond a discount number.
- The imagery is bold and varied: the red clogs on pink and the leopard slides on green read at a glance.
- The footer deadline is stated clearly: "Offer ends Sunday, November 29th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT."

## What's weak

- Both module buttons say "Shop All Doorbusters". They are generic and identical, and neither names the product.
- The hero shows buckled slides that nothing below follows up on. The "Hello, Big Savings" banner and "Now's the time to shop" are filler copy.
- The real deadline appears only in the fine print. Nothing in the hero or modules creates urgency.
- "Exclusions Apply." sits directly under the hero CTA, which weakens the "60% off" claim.
- It is the fourth Black Friday email in about a week, and the "up to 60%" message is the same as Nov 15 and Nov 20.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Nov 29", and give the red $30 clog module the top slot. It is the most concrete, price-led offer.
- 2. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific: "Shop $30 Classic Clogs" and "Shop Animal Print from $30".
- 3. Make the hero product match the offer by showing the clog, or add a module for the buckled slides.
- 4. Reduce the number of CTAs, and move "Exclusions Apply" out of the hero or tie it to a specific line.
- 5. Vary the creative from the prior Black Friday sends, so subscribers don't see the same message again.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Classic Clogs from $30 (retro prices)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clogs from $30 + up to 60% off, through 11/29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Retro $30 Classic Clogs and animal-print slides. Sale ends Nov 29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 60% off, plus $30 clogs. Exclusions apply, while supplies last.`

# Technical Audit: Crocs, "Say Hello to Big Savings"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) build with hidden preheader text and an MSO/Outlook fallback. QA confirms one defect, an empty greeting merge field. The source also has a malformed closing tag on the open-tracking pixel. Most compliance and authentication warnings are likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, and the truncated source can't settle them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tracking markup (confirmed in source):** The open pixel is closed with a stray tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10213&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and could break strict parsers or sanitizers.
- **Third-party pixel (QA):** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) beacon. It has no `alt` attribute. It should have `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions, since a missing alt can leak the URL as fallback text in some clients.
- **Unverified links:** 17 links on `click.crocs-email.com` were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Present and correct:** viewport meta, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and the Gmail `u + .email-body` hack.
- **Responsive:** the `max-width: 639px` media query provides stacking classes (`.columnSingle`, `.mobileHide`/`.mobileView`).
- **Web fonts:** `TT Crocs` is loaded via `@font-face` with `font-display: swap`. Fallback stacks aren't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.
- **Title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic, so it adds nothing for screen readers or the browser-view tab.
- **Demdex pixel:** missing alt, as noted in section 2.
- **Unassessed:** the truncated source doesn't show the body images, so alt text, image-to-text ratio and contrast couldn't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot (QA):** The greeting renders with an empty name field ("Hi ,"). The merge value is blank and has no fallback. The greeting markup falls outside the truncated source, so this rests on the QA check alone.
- No raw unresolved tokens (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`) are visible in the supplied source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive. Verify against the raw original message before treating it as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay and is not a confirmed failure. The From domain (`crocs-email.com`) matches the tracking domain, which is consistent with a properly configured ESP.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 17 links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects and weren't probed, so final destinations and UTM parameters are unobserved. Whether the "up to 60% off select styles" promise matches the landing page is also unverified.

1. **Fix the empty greeting (high):** Add a default fallback to the personalization string, or suppress the greeting when the first name is null. Verify the audience data feeding this send.
2. **Remove the stray `</custom>` tag (low):** Fix it in the pixel template.

4. **Re-run header checks on the raw `.eml` (medium):** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) before treating them as defects.
5. **Resolve the redirect chains (medium):** Follow the 17 `click.crocs-email.com` links to their final URLs. Record the status codes and check that UTMs persist to the landing page.
