# Flash Sale: 31% off select styles!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer flash sale. "Flash Sale / 31% Off / Select Styles" sits in big black type on white, with a black "Treat Yourself" pill under it and a hot-pink Baya clog below. The eye goes to the number first and the button second, and the free-shipping bar adds a second reason to buy.
- It's also a near-repeat. The 10/30 send, "Scary-good deals! 😱", ran the same 31% off select styles with a maroon panel and a red Baya. Today's email is the same offer on a pink Baya with "flash" urgency added. A subscriber would see the sale as rolling and not exclusive. The real deadline, Sunday Nov 1 at 11:59pm MT, appears only in the fine print, so "flash" is never backed up where people will read it.
- Judgement: competent and legible, but low on novelty and weak on urgency. It is a solid 6.

## What's working

- "31% Off" is the largest element and lands before anything else.
- One offer and one button, with no competing stories.
- The black free-shipping banner at the top adds value without clutter.
- The saturated pink Baya on a soft pink panel gives a strong, on-brand product image.
- The footer links are clean and the layout shows no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The deadline (ends Sunday, Nov 1, 11:59pm MT) is buried in the footer, so "flash" has no visible clock.
- "Treat Yourself" is a vague CTA. It doesn't say what the shopper gets or where the button goes.
- A large empty gap between "Exclusions Apply." and the hero image pushes the product down and looks unfinished.
- The pink clog is not clickable-looking and the email shows no named style, price or social proof.
- The offer repeats yesterday's 31% with a new color and a new word, "flash".

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tomorrow, 11:59pm MT", and add a countdown timer if the ESP supports one. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Rename the CTA to something specific, like "Shop 31% Off Clogs".
- 3. Remove the dead space above the hero image so the product is visible on the first mobile screen.
- 4. Add a small product grid below the hero with 2–3 named, priced styles (was/now) and a review-star line.
- 5. Break the pattern with the 10/30 send. Either change the offer or tie the copy to the end of the weekend, such as "Last day".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 31% off clogs + free shipping`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles, free shipping on qualified orders. Ends Sunday 11:59pm MT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Baya clogs and more at 31% off. Exclusions apply, while supplies last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) infrastructure with a first-party click and open domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), responsive CSS, and an inline-backed preheader. The HTML has one confirmed malformed tag and one third-party pixel without alt text. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** The open-pixel wrapper closes with a stray `</custom>` tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener, so the HTML is invalid. Most clients tolerate it, but strict parsers and some Outlook/MSO builds may handle it inconsistently.
- **Unverified links:** The automated QA skipped 15 tracking links (click-redirect domains) and did not probe them, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** A Demdex (Adobe Audience Manager) pixel is present: `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`. It has no `alt` attribute, which the QA flagged. It also carries a per-recipient identifier (`d_dpuid`) to a third-party domain, which may need a privacy disclosure.
- **Open tracking:** `open.aspx?qs=…&d=10210&bmt=0` is the standard SFMC open pixel. The open-tracking pixel itself has no issues.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** The Demdex pixel has no `alt` attribute (QA-confirmed). The SFMC open pixel has `alt=""`, which is correct for a decorative image.
- **Head metadata:** The head is well-formed. It has `lang`/`xml:lang`, a viewport meta tag, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`.
- **Responsive CSS:** The `@media only screen and (max-width: 639px)` breakpoint and the Gmail/Outlook fixes (`u + .email-body`, `#MessageViewBody`) are present.
- **`<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. This is minor, and only worth noting for accessibility.
- **Body content:** The HTML was truncated, so I can't verify alt text on content images, table `role="presentation"`, or fallback font stacks for `TT Crocs`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[First Name]` appear in the visible source or subject. Because the source was truncated, this covers the visible portion only.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. The QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain (`crocs-email.com`) matches the tracking domain (`click.crocs-email.com`), which is consistent with aligned sending infrastructure.
- **Footer:** The visible footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) was cut off in the truncated source and can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable. All the links I could see are opaque SFMC redirects (`click.crocs-email.com`), and the QA did not follow them. I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment with the "31% off select styles" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
2. Add `alt=""` to the Demdex pixel, or drop it if it isn't needed.
3. Re-run the audit against the raw message headers (not the AgentMail relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. Resolve the 15 tracked links through the redirect chain, then check final URLs for UTM parameters and for a landing page that matches the flash-sale offer.
5. Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, and body alt text can be audited.
