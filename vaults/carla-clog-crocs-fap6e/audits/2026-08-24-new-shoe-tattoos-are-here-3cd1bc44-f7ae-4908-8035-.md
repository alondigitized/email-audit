# New Shoe Tattoos are here!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine product-launch send — Shoe Tattoos, a new peel-and-press decorative charm/decal system for clogs — and it's a clean creative break from the bundle-discount grind Crocs has been running (8/22, 8/23's "2 for $50" pair). The design is confident: bold "shoe tattoos" wordmark treatment, tight hero shot showing the platform clog styled up with tattoo decals, and a clear how-to-apply shot with hands mid-application. No overlapping text, no broken images, layout renders cleanly top to bottom.
- The problem is there's no offer anywhere in the body — three separate "Shop Shoe Tattoos" CTAs, a "Create Your Look" CTA, and a nav grid, but not a single price, discount, or urgency signal. For a subscriber trained by the last two weeks of "2 for $50" and "extra 25% off" emails, this reads as a nice palette-cleanser (like 8/19's Café Slipper or 8/18's Florence by Mills), but it won't drive the same click-through as the discount sends because there's nothing time-bound pulling a click today versus next week.
- Verdict: strong creative, weak conversion mechanics. Ships fine as a launch/awareness email, not as a revenue driver.

## What's working

- Hero image is striking — the tattoo decals on cream clogs pop against the light gray background, and the bold black wordmark is on-brand and legible at a glance.
- The "how it works" application shot (hands pressing a decal onto a clog) answers the obvious product question — how does this actually attach — without needing copy to explain it.
- Three clear, consistently styled CTAs ("Shop Shoe Tattoos" x2, "Create Your Look") in black pill buttons stand out against the white background.
- Secondary nav grid (Clogs, Jibbitz, Sale, Sandals) gives non-tattoo shoppers an easy off-ramp instead of dead-ending them.
- Free shipping banner up top and refer-a-friend footer are consistent, low-friction adds that don't compete with the hero message.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing or offer information — no price for the tattoo decal packs, no bundle price, nothing. A shopper can't judge cost without leaving the email.
- No urgency or news hook beyond "are here" — compare to 8/21's "Love Silver? Or Gold?" which at least framed a choice; this is just three repeated CTAs saying the same thing.
- Repetitive messaging: "Add More You," "Stick to Your Style," and "Designed by You" all say the same thing three times in a row without adding new information (compatibility, pack size, price).
- No social proof or product specificity (how many tattoos per pack, which clog styles are compatible, durability/wash claims) — all things a buyer would want before clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or pack detail somewhere in the first screen — even "Tattoo Packs from $8" would give the hero section a reason to click now instead of later.
- 2. Cut one of the three redundant CTA sections (Add More You / Stick to Your Style / Designed by You) — collapse to two beats: hero + how-it-apply, then straight to shop grid.
- 3. Add a compatibility line ("Fits Classic Clogs & Stomp") since a decal product lives or dies on "will this fit my pair."
- 4. Introduce a light urgency layer given the brand's recent pattern — even "New drop, while supplies last" — to differentiate from an evergreen catalog add.
