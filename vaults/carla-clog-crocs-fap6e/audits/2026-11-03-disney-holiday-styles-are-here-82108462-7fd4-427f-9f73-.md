## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ExactTarget/SFMC send with a responsive structure and web-font fallbacks. The confirmed issues are 7 images served over plain `http://`, a malformed closing tag after the open-tracking pixel, and a tracking pixel with no `alt` attribute. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. The HTML was truncated and the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **26 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains (likely `click.crocs-email.com`), so I can't confirm that any of their destinations resolve.
- **Malformed markup after the open pixel.** The source has `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10211&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is a second open-tracking beacon. Both it and the SFMC pixel are blocked when images are off, so open data will be incomplete.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources (7).** These images load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` (App Store badge)
  - `13246762-…png` (Google Play badge)
  - `c4df71ec-…png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)

  Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading, or flag mixed content in web views, may block these. That would leave the footer app and social icons blank. These images do have alt text.
- **Missing `alt` on the demdex pixel.** The `<img>` for `…demdex.net/event?…` has no `alt` attribute. It is a hidden 1x1 beacon, so the impact is low, but it can still trip accessibility linters.
- **Generic `<title>`.** The title is just "Crocs" and doesn't match the subject line. Few clients display it, so this is minor.
- The viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a hidden preheader and `font-display: swap` are all present. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FNAME]` appear in the truncated HTML. I can't confirm the rest of the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo expect one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.

Not assessable. All 26 tracked links were skipped, and the HTML was truncated before the link targets could be inspected. I can't confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirect, or whether the Disney holiday styles link to a matching landing page.
