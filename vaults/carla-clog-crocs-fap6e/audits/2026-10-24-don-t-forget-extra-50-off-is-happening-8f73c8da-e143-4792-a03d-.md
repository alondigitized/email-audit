# Don't forget: Extra 50% Off is Happening!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain reminder send. "Extra 50% Off" in heavy black type on a sage panel is the first thing you see, and the black "Shop the Sale" pill sits right under it. You get the offer and the action in about two seconds, and nothing is broken.
- It's also a near-repeat of yesterday's "Wait...Extra 50% Off?!" (Oct 23), with the same offer, a similar layout and a "don't forget" subject. A subscriber has already seen this deal. The "select styles" and "exclusions apply" lines make the 50% feel less real. The lone navy Baya clog behind a giant "SALE" shows no product detail, no price and no category.
- Verdict: competent and clear, but low on freshness and specificity. The deadline, Sunday Oct 25 at 11:59pm MT, is the only real urgency, and it's buried in the legal copy at the bottom.

## What's working

- "Extra 50% Off" is by far the largest element, so the offer leads.
- "Shop the Sale" is a high-contrast black pill on a light panel, and it sits above the fold.
- The tagline "A little reminder. A lot of savings." matches the reminder framing in the subject.
- The Refer a Friend module, app download badges and store locator are tidy extras that don't compete with the hero.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The deadline (ends Oct 25, 11:59pm MT) appears only in the bottom fine print, not in the hero.
- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" are vague. They hint at bait-and-switch and give no sense of which shoes qualify.
- The hero image is one generic navy clog behind "SALE", with no price, no named product and no category.
- The big SALE graphic uses most of the above-the-fold space and says less than the headline already does.
- This is the second "Extra 50%" email in two days, following Oct 23's "Wait...Extra 50% Off?!". The creative feels repeated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the deadline into the hero, for example "Ends Sunday 11:59pm MT", and set it as a countdown or bold line right under the headline.
- 2. Replace the SALE graphic with 3–4 real sale products showing the original price, the sale price and a "now $X" tag. This makes "select styles" concrete.
- 3. Make the CTA more specific, such as "Shop Clogs 50% Off" or "Shop Final-Day Deals", and add secondary category links.
- 4. Don't send a near-identical offer on back-to-back days. Make this the last-call version with a distinct angle, such as "last 36 hours".
- 5. Name the exclusions or link to them, so the fine print doesn't undercut the claim.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 50% off clogs expires tomorrow night`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Sunday 11:59pm MT. Baya, Classic & more, now up to 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last chance: grab your favorites before the sale ends. Free shipping on qualified orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard SFMC/ExactTarget send with a tracking pixel, an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) pixel, and 20 click-tracked links. The confirmed defects are 7 images served over `http://`, a malformed closing tag on the open pixel, and a missing `alt` on the demdex pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tag on the open pixel.** The source has `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10198&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The stray `</custom>` is invalid and has no opening tag. Most clients ignore it, but it is a template defect.
- **Click links not verified.** The QA run skipped 20 tracking links on `click.crocs-email.com` because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and expiry are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Third-party pixel.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event pixel. It carries a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`) and fires on open. It is an extra third-party request. Clients that block remote content or proxy images may drop it, which would skew open attribution.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images (7).** All are on `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - App Store badge (`9c36c0ef-…png`)
  - Google Play badge (`13246762-…png`)
  - Find Your Store (`c4df71ec-…png`)
  - Instagram (`e18949ff-…png`)
  - Facebook (`80f68cd8-…png`)
  - TikTok (`51d8144e-…png`)
  - YouTube (`1187ef58-…png`)

  Clients that don't proxy images, and some security gateways, may block or warn on these. They all sit in the footer and utility area.
- **Missing alt.** The demdex `event` image has no `alt` attribute. Unlike the open pixel (`alt=""`), it is not marked decorative, so screen readers may announce it. The seven `http://` images do have alt text.
- **Head metadata.** `<title>` is the generic "Crocs", `lang="en"` is set, and the viewport and format-detection metas are present. A preheader is present with `display:none`.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so I can't check the rest of the body for `role="presentation"` on layout tables, font fallback stacks or dark-mode handling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The truncated body can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** were not found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. I can't confirm they are absent from the original message, so I'm not calling this a defect. Verify against the raw message before concluding.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found. This is also expected through the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope looks aligned: the From domain is `crocs-email.com` and the tracking and image hosts use the same domain.
- **Footer.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 20 destination links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects and were not resolved, and the truncated HTML shows no UTM parameters. SFMC usually appends campaign parameters during redirect resolution, so none are visible here. Landing page alignment with the "Extra 50% Off" offer can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the 7 `image.crocs-email.com` image URLs from `http://` to `https://`. This is a one-line template fix per asset.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` from the open-pixel block.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel, or load it differently.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't act on these warnings until you've confirmed the relay isn't dropping them.
5. Extend the QA tool to follow `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, resolve the final URLs, and record the UTM parameters and landing page status.
6. Capture the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be audited.
