# New season 🏈 New style
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine licensed-collab launch — NFL team clogs (Eagles, Cowboys, Rams, Packers, and what looks like a red team) shot on-foot with an "Officially Licensed Product" badge, plus a five-team lineup shot that reads instantly as a real product drop, not a generic sale banner. It's a clean, confident hero: strong color contrast (teal background, bold team colorways), no clutter, one clear CTA ("Shop NFL") right under the headline.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the hero. "Recommended for You" mixes two black clogs, a kids' clog, and — bizarrely — the Lightning McQueen Dinoco Crocband that Crocs already ran as its own dedicated send on 9/16. There's no size/price/availability info anywhere, the Afterpay block eats a huge amount of vertical space for a secondary payment feature, and the subject line "New season 🏈 New style" undersells a real NFL license the way "Crocs at Work" undersold nothing (because there was nothing to undersell). Solid creative, weak information architecture.

## What's working

- The hero image is a genuine seasonal/license tie-in (NFL) with visible team logos — real news, not a percent-off refresh.
- "Officially Licensed Product" badge builds trust and signals this isn't generic fan merch.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop NFL") directly below concise copy.
- Clean color story — teal, black, and team colors — with no overlapping text or broken assets.

## What's weak

- "Recommended for You" reuses the Lightning McQueen Dinoco clog shown just yesterday (9/16) as its own hero — feels recycled, not personalized.
- No price, team-specific link, or sizing info anywhere in the email despite this being a multi-team product line.
- The Afterpay module is oversized relative to its importance and pushes the four-button shop menu (Clogs/Jibbitz/Sale/Sandals) far down the scroll.
- Subject line doesn't mention NFL at all, so the email's best asset (a real sports license) is invisible in the inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "NFL" in the subject line — it's the actual news here, and burying it behind "New season, new style" wastes the strongest signal in the send.
- 2. Replace the McQueen tile in "Recommended for You" with another NFL team clog or a "Shop by Team" grid — reinforce the hero instead of diluting it with an unrelated collab from yesterday's email.
- 3. Shrink the Afterpay block by ~50%; it's a financing footnote, not a hero-equivalent module.
- 4. Add a price or "Shop All 32 Teams" link under the 5-clog lineup so the reader knows this isn't just 5 SKUs.

- **Subject Alt B:** `NFL Crocs just dropped — find your team`
