## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ExactTarget/SFMC build with a valid doctype, language attributes, a viewport meta tag and Outlook conditionals. Confirmed defects are 7 `http://` image sources, a stray `</custom>` closing tag and one `<img>` with no `alt`. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** the QA run skipped 20 tracking links because they are click-redirect domains (likely `click.crocs-email.com`). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=...&d=10202&bmt=0` is 1x1 with `alt=""`. That is correct for a tracking pixel.
- **Stray closing tag:** the open pixel is written as `<img ... alt=""></custom>`. There is no matching `<custom>` opener, so this is invalid markup. Browsers and clients ignore it, but strict parsers and linters will flag it.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=...` is an Adobe Audience Manager pixel with `c_emailopened=Yes` hardcoded in the URL. That is a flat query parameter, not a merge field. The pixel also lacks an `alt` attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure image sources:** 7 images load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`:
  - `9c36c0ef-...png` (App Store)
  - `13246762-...png` (Google Play)
  - `c4df71ec-...png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-...png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-...png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-...png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-...png` (YouTube)

  All of them sit in the footer area. Some clients and proxies block or warn on non-HTTPS images. The same host is used elsewhere, so check whether the other `image.crocs-email.com` assets are HTTPS.
- **Missing `alt`:** the demdex `event` pixel has no `alt` attribute. It's invisible, but the attribute should be `alt=""`.
- **Passing checks:** `lang` and `xml:lang` are set, the viewport meta tag is present, and the preheader is hidden with `display:none !important`. Mobile classes and Apple/Gmail hacks (`u + .email-body`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]`) are present. The `@font-face` rules use `font-display: swap` over HTTPS.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic. This is minor and has no effect in most clients.
- **Subject emoji:** the subject contains `🏃‍➡️`, a ZWJ sequence from Unicode 15.1. Older clients may render it as separate runner and arrow glyphs. This is a rendering-compatibility risk, not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the remainder is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect. Re-check against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `email@crocs-email.com`, and alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both are beyond the truncation point. This is unverified and not a confirmed issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. All 20 links go through the `click.crocs-email.com` redirector and were not resolved, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing pages are unknown. The only visible parameters are the redirect `qs` blob and the demdex `d_dpid`/`d_dpuid`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` footer image URLs to `https://`. Confirm the host serves a valid certificate and that the ESP isn't rewriting the URLs.
2. Remove the orphan `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the demdex `event` pixel.
4. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers on the raw message. Capture `Authentication-Results` outside the relay to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. Resolve the 20 tracked links with a redirect-following probe. Check that the final URLs return 200 and carry consistent UTM parameters, and that the offer matches the landing page.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, physical address and any merge tokens.
