# Lined styles Starting at $35!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-offer send. The hero is a dusty blue panel with "Lined Styles Starting at $35" in heavy black type and a black "Shop Lined Styles" pill directly under it. Below sits a photo of two olive lined clogs with Jibbitz charms on a blue carpet, with a fuzzy white blanket in the corner. The eye lands on the price first, then the button, then the shoes. The cozy-season framing fits early November.
- It is also the weakest of Crocs' recent offers. After three days of "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" (Nov 6, 7 and 8), this send drops the percentage headline and moves to a "starting at $35" price anchor. That is a fresh angle, but "starting at" says nothing about what the typical lined clog costs. The deadline (Nov 10 at 11:59pm MT) appears only in the fine print, so the urgency is wasted. The "Early Gifting" eyebrow is a nice touch, but nothing in the body supports it.
- Verdict: solid and readable, but it undersells itself. Putting the deadline in the hero and showing a price tag on the product would make it much stronger.

## What's working

- The hero has a clear hierarchy. The price headline is dominant, with a single black CTA pill right below it.
- The product photo is warm and on theme. The visible fleece lining sells "cozy" without extra copy.
- "Early Gifting" ties the send to the holiday season and signals this is a gift purchase.
- The Jibbitz charms on the clogs hint at the brand's personalization upsell.
- The black footer is tidy. It has the referral offer, app download badges, store locator and social icons, and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- The deadline ("Offer ends Tuesday, November 10th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT") is buried in the fine print at the bottom, so the email has no visible urgency.
- "Starting at $35" gives no sense of the range, and no product shows a price or a "was" price.
- The whole image is a single visual with no clickable product detail, so there is no obvious hero SKU, no color options and no reviews.
- "Prices as marked." under the CTA reads like a legal disclaimer and weakens the offer.
- It follows three near-identical "Buy 2 Take 30% Off" sends, and the shift to a different offer with no explanation could feel like a bait-and-switch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the deadline into the hero, for example "Ends tomorrow, Nov 10", right above the CTA. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put a price tag on the product photo, such as "Classic Lined Clog $35 (was $59.99)", and link the image to that product.
- 3. Replace "Prices as marked." with a benefit line such as "Free shipping on qualified orders".
- 4. Add 2-3 lined styles in different colors below the hero, so the "styles" in the headline are visible.
- 5. Acknowledge the sale cadence, for example "Your Buy 2 deal ends tonight".

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fleece-lined warmth for early gifting. Sale ends Nov 10.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a sound responsive and Outlook scaffold. Confirmed defects are 7 non-HTTPS image sources, a malformed closing tag on the open-tracking pixel, and a missing `alt` on a third-party pixel. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified click targets:** 20 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`). Destination status and final URLs are unverified.
- **Malformed open pixel:** the pixel is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10209&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The `</custom>` closing tag has no matching opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is invalid markup and a strict parser or sanitizer could mishandle it.
- **Second tracking pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…` is an Adobe Audience Manager (demdex) beacon, in addition to the SFMC open pixel. It has no `alt` attribute (QA warning). It is a hidden pixel, so the accessibility impact is low, but an empty `alt=""` would match the SFMC pixel.
- **Per-recipient ID:** the `d_dpuid` value is in the URL, which suggests a merge-resolved ID. I can't tell from the capture whether it is recipient-specific or static.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS images (7):** these are served over `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/…`.
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` (App Store badge)
  - `13246762-…png` (Google Play badge)
  - `c4df71ec-…png` (Find Your Store)
  - `e18949ff-…png` (Instagram)
  - `80f68cd8-…png` (Facebook)
  - `51d8144e-…png` (TikTok)
  - `1187ef58-…png` (YouTube)

  Some clients, including Gmail's image proxy in certain cases, block or warn on mixed content. The footer app badges and social icons may fail to load. The same host already serves HTTPS elsewhere (`click.crocs-email.com`).
- **Alt text:** the 7 images above have alt text, so they degrade readably. The demdex pixel is the only one missing `alt`.
- **Positive:** the head includes a viewport meta, `format-detection`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, an MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, the `u + .email-body` Gmail hack, and a 639px mobile breakpoint. No issues found in these.
- **Web font:** `TT Crocs` loads from `media.crocs.com` with `font-display: swap`. No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is non-descriptive. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. Only the `d_dpuid` value noted above is of interest. The HTML is truncated, so the full body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this volume, so they should be checked in the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain is `crocs-email.com`, and alignment should be verified on a direct-to-mailbox send.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. They cannot be confirmed or flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be verified. All links are wrapped by SFMC click tracking, 20 were not resolved, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the captured source. The subject ("Lined styles Starting at $35!") and the preheader ("Get an early start on your shopping list") can't be checked against a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change all 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image sources to `https://`. This is the highest-impact fix.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open pixel. Add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers of a direct send. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and align for `crocs-email.com`.
4. Resolve the click-tracked links with a redirect-following probe to check destination status codes, UTM parameters and landing page alignment.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and any remaining merge tokens.
