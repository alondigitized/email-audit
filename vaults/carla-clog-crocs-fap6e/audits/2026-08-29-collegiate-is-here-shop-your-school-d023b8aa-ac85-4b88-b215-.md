# Collegiate is here! 📚 Shop your school.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a licensed-collaboration launch — Crocs Collegiate, a team-branded clog line (Longhorns, Roll Tide, Tigers, Kentucky, Bulldogs, Gators) with matching Jibbitz. It's a real creative swing, not another bundle-discount rehash — a welcome break after the 8/22/8/23 "2 for $50" pair. There's also a visible render glitch in the hero: a stray dashed border and thin colored rule lines cutting across the right-hand lifestyle image, which looks like a broken/leftover image asset rather than intentional design.
- The offer clarity is weak, though — there's no price, no discount, no "starting at $X," just "Find yours!" This is a discovery/launch email, not a promo, and it reads that way. The refer-a-friend banner (Give 25%, Get 25%) is actually the strongest concrete offer on the page, but it's buried below four secondary nav buttons rather than up top.
- Solid seasonal timing (back-to-school collegiate gear in late August) and a clean single hero CTA make this a reasonable open/click, but the visual bug and lack of a headline offer cap it below the recent Pokémon/NFL/NINJAGO launches.

## What's working

- Six-team shoe wall (Longhorns, Alabama, LSU/Tigers, Kentucky, Bulldogs, Gators) instantly signals broad school coverage without needing to say "50+ schools."
- Single clear primary CTA ("Shop Collegiate") sits directly under a plain-language value prop ("Rep your school on and off campus").
- Seasonal relevance is spot-on for back-to-school/college-football timing.
- Free shipping banner up top is a real, low-friction incentive.

## What's weak

- Visible render bug in the hero image — a dashed border and colored rule lines overlay the lifestyle photo, looking broken/unfinished.
- No concrete offer or price point anywhere in the primary hero — "Find yours!" isn't a reason to click today.
- The genuinely strong offer (Refer a Friend: Give 25%, Get 25%) is stranded below four generic nav buttons instead of leading.
- Four secondary buttons (Clogs/Jibbitz/Sale/Sandals) dilute focus right after the single hero CTA — visual hierarchy competes with itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero image render bug first — that dashed/lined overlay reads as broken and undermines a launch that's otherwise premium-looking.
- 2. Add a concrete offer or starting price to the hero copy (e.g., "Clogs from $59.99" or call out that Jibbitz charms are included) — right now there's no reason to act now vs. later.
- 3. Promote the Refer-a-Friend 25%/25% offer above the four secondary nav buttons, or fold it into the hero — it's the best concrete incentive in the email and it's currently buried.
- 4. Cut the four-button row to two (Shop Clogs, Shop Jibbitz) to keep post-hero focus tight; sale/sandals dilute a launch-specific send.
