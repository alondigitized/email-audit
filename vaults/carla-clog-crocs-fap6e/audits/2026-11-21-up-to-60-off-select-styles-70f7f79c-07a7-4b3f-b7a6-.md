## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) send with a sound responsive and client-hack scaffold. It has one malformed tag and one unlabeled third-party pixel. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay stripped the headers. The source is truncated, so the body, footer, and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open-tracking pixel is closed with a stray `</custom>`:
  `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10212&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`
  It is an invalid closing tag with no matching opener. Most clients ignore it, but it is a template defect, and strict parsers or sanitizers may mishandle it.
- **Unverified links:** 19 links on tracking and click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). Destination status and final URLs are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Third-party pixel:** an Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) pixel is present:
  `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`
  It sends an open event plus a persistent user ID (`d_dpuid`) to a third party. Image-blocking clients and privacy proxies (such as Apple MPP) will suppress or skew it.
- **Open-tracking skew:** the two open pixels (`click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx` and the demdex one) will report inflated opens under Apple MPP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed issue:** the demdex tracking `<img>` has no `alt` attribute (QA WARN). Adding `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"` would mark it as decorative.
- **Confirmed issue:** `<title>Crocs</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. It has minor value for web-view and screen-reader context.
- **Confirmed gap:** the `<head>` has no `<meta name="color-scheme">` or `supported-color-schemes`, and no `prefers-color-scheme` rules appear in the CSS. Dark-mode client inversion is left uncontrolled.
- **Good practices present:**
  - `lang` and `xml:lang` are set to `en`.
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - There are mso `OfficeDocumentSettings` and `x-apple-data-detectors` overrides.
  - There is a Gmail hack (`u + .email-body`).
  - Web fonts use `font-display: swap`.
  - The mobile breakpoint is 639px.
- **Not verifiable (truncated):** `role="presentation"` on layout tables, alt text on content images, the web-font fallback stack, and image-to-text ratio.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. These are **inconclusive, not confirmed defects**. They need checking against raw headers from a direct delivery, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **WARN:** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (same relay caveat). The envelope uses `email@crocs-email.com` on a dedicated sending domain, which is consistent with SFMC. Alignment can't be confirmed from this data.
- **Not verifiable:** the footer's visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Links are wrapped in `click.crocs-email.com` redirects, and the final URLs and UTM parameters were skipped by QA and sit beyond the truncation. No evidence of a problem, and none of correctness either.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the `open.aspx` pixel in the template.
2. Add `alt=""` (and `aria-hidden="true"`) to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-run QA against raw headers, from a direct send or seed-list inbox, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't treat the relay-induced WARNs as sender failures.
4. Resolve the 19 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe. That would check final status codes and UTM presence, and would enable the continuity audit.
5. Increase the HTML capture limit so the footer, the unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the content-image alt text can be audited.
6. Add `color-scheme` meta tags and dark-mode CSS.
7. Make `<title>` more descriptive, for example by matching the subject line.
