# Stock up now, sale ends tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing send of Crocs' multi-buy sale, and the offer is clear. A violet hero carries heavy black type: "Can't pick just one? Perfect. Grab 4 pairs and score 40% off." Below it, "Buy 2, take 20% off, 3 for 30% off, 4 for 40% off!" and a black "Shop The Sale" pill. It's the same tiered mechanic as the 2026-10-18 "Crocs at Work™: New Styles Out Now!" send, now aimed at the general audience.
- The email is clean and the hierarchy is right. The eye goes headline, then tiers, then button, then the Baya clogs in army green and bone. The weakness is urgency. "Ends tonight" appears only in the fine print at the very bottom ("Offer ends Monday, October 19th, 2026 at 11:59pm MT"), and the subject is the only place it shows up prominently. The hero never says it. Both the "Select styles" label and "Exclusions Apply" also hedge the promise.
- Verdict: a solid, honest sale email that stays in the 6-7 range. The creative is good, but the deadline is buried, the CTA is generic, and it's the third tiered or percent-off beat in about a week. The 10-07 "Ends Tonight" send put the deadline in the hero, and this one should have too.

## What's working

- The hero headline states the offer in plain words: 4 pairs, 40% off.
- The tier line ("Buy 2, take 20% off, 3 for 30% off, 4 for 40% off!") makes the ladder easy to read and rewards bigger baskets.
- The black "Shop The Sale" pill contrasts well against the violet and is easy to see.
- The product shot of the army green and bone Baya clogs is crisp, and the colors fit fall.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text. The black footer with refer-a-friend, app download and store locator is tidy.

## What's weak

- There's no deadline in the hero. "Ends tonight" appears only in the subject and in the legal copy at the bottom.
- "Shop The Sale" is generic, and the "Select styles" and "Exclusions Apply" hedges make it unclear which shoes qualify.
- The hero shows one product, the Baya clog, in two colors, with no price, no reviews and no sizing. That's thin for a "stock up" pitch.
- The "Plus, accessories up to 40% off" line is small and competes with the main offer.
- The refer-a-friend banner ("Give 25%, Get 25% Off!") sits right under the sale and muddies the message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example a "Ends tonight, 11:59pm MT" tag above the headline. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Make the CTA specific: "Shop 4 for 40% Off" in place of "Shop The Sale".
- 3. Show a 2x2 grid of 4 different styles, so the "grab 4 pairs" idea is visible, with 2-3 linked product tiles.
- 4. Cut the accessories line or move it below the fold, and drop the refer-a-friend banner on a last-day send.
- 5. Add a social-proof line, such as a star rating on the Baya.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix any 4 styles. Sale ends at 11:59pm MT.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) send with a standard responsive and MSO-aware template. The confirmed issues are mixed-content `http://` image sources, a malformed tracking-pixel wrapper, and a pixel with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the HTML.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 20 links point to tracking or click-redirect domains (for example `click.crocs-email.com`). The QA probe skipped them, so link validity and destination resolution are **unverified**. Nothing confirms they are broken.
- The open pixel is wrapped in a stray closing tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?4A4FVETRRIAUFNOHFFP3PHVIGU.10201&d=10201&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no opening tag, so the HTML is malformed. Most clients ignore it, but strict parsers may handle it inconsistently.
- The Adobe Audience Manager (Demdex) beacon `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is a third-party tracking pixel. It has no `alt` attribute (see section 3).

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Mixed content:** 7 images load over `http://` from `image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. They are the App Store, Google Play, Find Your Store, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok and YouTube images. Clients that proxy or block non-HTTPS images may not show them, which would leave the footer badges and social icons blank. Their alt text is present, so the failure would degrade gracefully. Switch the host to `https://`.
- **Missing alt:** the `demdex.net/event` tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute. This is low severity because it is a hidden tracking pixel, but add `alt=""`.
- No issues found with the viewport meta, `format-detection`, the `@font-face` fallbacks (`font-display: swap`), the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, or the `sup` fix for Outlook. The source is truncated, so layout and table structure beyond the head could not be checked.
- The `<title>` is the generic "Crocs". This is a minor accessibility and context point, not a defect.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Some of it was truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so the finding is **inconclusive**, not a confirmed gap. Check the raw message headers at the original recipient.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation. The sender domain `crocs-email.com` matches the click and image domains, which is consistent with aligned branding, but this is not proof of authentication.
- The visible HTML does not show the unsubscribe link or physical postal address because the source was truncated. Verify them in the footer.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not assessable. The destination URLs are hidden behind the 20 skipped tracking redirects, and the visible source has no UTM parameters. Resolve the redirects to check UTM presence and landing-page alignment with the "Last call for up to 40% off" preheader.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Change all 7 `http://image.crocs-email.com/...` image sources to `https://`, and confirm the host serves a valid certificate.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` tag after the open pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `demdex.net/event` pixel.
4. Re-run QA against the original message headers, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Follow the tracking redirects to check the final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP status.
6. Confirm the footer has a working unsubscribe link and a postal address, since the source was truncated.
