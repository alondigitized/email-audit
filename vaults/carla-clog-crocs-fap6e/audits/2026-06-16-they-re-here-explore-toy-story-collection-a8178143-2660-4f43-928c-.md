# They’re here! Explore Toy Story collection.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Crocs' Toy Story 5 collab launch email — a product announcement built around the Western-themed Jessie Boot and a broader Toy Story collection. The "Never Grow Up" headline is the best line in the email: emotionally resonant, true to the Toy Story DNA, and a credible brand fit for Crocs' playful positioning. The Jessie Boot gets a dedicated second section with its own pun ("Rootin' Tootin' Style") and CTA, which is better structural discipline than most collab sends.
- The problem is context. Crocs blasted Extra 25% off sale emails on June 13 and again on June 14 — back-to-back discount sends. Subscribers trained to expect a deal are now being asked to shop full price two days later. There's no launch offer, no limited-edition urgency signal, and no "early access" framing to justify paying list price. The Refer a Friend block at the bottom further muddies the message.
- This is a solid creative chassis with a strategy gap. The Toy Story 5 tie-in is timely (summer 2025 release), the copy is on-brand, but the email doesn't give the subscriber a reason to act *today* at full price after being discount-conditioned all week.

## What's working

- "Never Grow Up" headline earns its placement — emotionally resonant, nostalgic hook that suits both Toy Story and Crocs' brand tone.
- Jessie Boot gets its own section and dedicated CTA ("Shop Disney and Pixar's Jessie Boot") — specific product anchoring lifts click intent.
- "A Western-themed Jibbitz™ charms included" is a smart feature callout — positions the Jibbitz inclusion as added value, not an afterthought.
- Layout is clean with no visible render bugs, broken images, or layout breakage.
- The collab is genuinely timely — Toy Story 5 release makes this culturally current.

## What's weak

- No price incentive after consecutive 25% off sale emails on June 13 and 14 — discount hangover is real, and this send offers nothing to counter it.
- Subject line "They're here! Explore Toy Story collection." is generic — "They're here!" could be any collab from any brand.
- Preheader slot is consumed by "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" — wastes the inbox preview on a baseline condition subscribers already know, instead of teasing the Toy Story moment.
- The Refer a Friend block ("Give 25%, Get 25% Off!") appears mid-email before the footer and competes for attention with the product CTAs.
- No on-foot or lifestyle shot — both sections show shoes on a flat surface, making it harder to visualize the product in context.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a launch-specific incentive or framing.** Even "Free Jibbitz charm with any Toy Story purchase" or "Limited collab — shop before it sells out" would convert better than free shipping alone after two days of 25% off blasts.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject to name the hero product.** "They're here!" is anonymous. Lead with the IP and the product: `Toy Story 5 × Crocs — the Jessie Boot has dropped`.
- 3. **Replace the preheader with emotional copy.** "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" kills the Toy Story mood before the subscriber even opens. Put the headline concept in the preview slot.
- 4. **Move Refer a Friend to the footer or a standalone send.** A 25% referral offer sitting between product sections creates decision fatigue at the wrong moment.
- 5. **Add one lifestyle/on-foot shot** — show the Jessie Boot worn, not just staged.

- **Subject Alt B:** `To infinity and beyond your shoe rack`

## Technical Audit — Crocs "Toy Story Collection" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound with proper MSO conditionals, responsive breakpoints, and preheader suppression. The primary issues are a batch of HTTP (non-HTTPS) image sources in the footer, a missing open-pixel alt attribute, and unverifiable compliance headers due to the AgentMail relay layer.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **32 tracking links unverifiable** — all click-redirect links were skipped during HTTP probing. Destination URLs and redirect chain integrity cannot be confirmed. If any resolve to HTTP endpoints, mixed-content warnings may fire on HTTPS landing pages.
- No broken anchor `href` values detected in the visible HTML fragment.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **HTTP image sources (7 images)** — the following footer images are served over `http://` rather than `https://`, which will be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings in most modern email clients (Gmail, Outlook.com, Apple Mail with images on):
  - `9c36c0ef...` — "Download on the App Store"
  - `13246762...` — "Get It On Google Play"
  - `c4df71ec...` — "Find Your Store"
  - `e18949ff...` — "Instagram"
  - `80f68cd8...` — "Facebook"
  - `51d8144e...` — "TikTok"
  - `1187ef58...` — "YouTube"

  All sourced from `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`. A global find-replace of the scheme on that domain is sufficient to fix all seven.

- **Open pixel missing alt** — `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&...` has no `alt` attribute. While intentional for tracking pixels, the absence triggers a QA warning; add `alt=""` to make the omission explicit and suppress false positives.

- **Custom font fallback** — `TT Crocs` is loaded via `@font-face` from `media.crocs.com`. No system-font fallback stack is visible in the truncated CSS. If the CDN is unreachable or the client blocks remote fonts, text will fall back to the client default rather than a controlled stack.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%FIRSTNAME%`) are visible in the HTML fragment. No issues found.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — the AgentMail relay did not surface this header. Gmail and Yahoo Mail use this header for the native unsubscribe button; its absence (or non-propagation) risks inbox placement and violates Yahoo's bulk sender requirements (enforced since Feb 2024).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click machine unsubscribe is not confirmable. Required for Google and Yahoo bulk senders sending >5k/day.
- **`Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from the relay capture. This needs to be confirmed against raw headers in the ESP or MTA logs, not via AgentMail.
- Unsubscribe link and physical mailing address presence cannot be confirmed from the truncated HTML, but must exist per CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) and §5(a)(6).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- UTM parameter coverage cannot be verified because all 32 click links were skipped during probing. At minimum, confirm `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=toy-story` (or equivalent) are appended to all product and CTA links.
- No issues with domain mismatch are detectable from available data; all image assets appear to be served from `media.crocs.com` or `image.crocs-email.com`.
