# Extra 50% Off? Not Much Longer...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing beat of Crocs' "Extra 50% Off" run, and the top half does its job. A cyan hero carries "Last Chance: Extra 50% Off!" in heavy black type, and a black "Shop the Sale" pill sits right under it. The offer and the action are readable in about two seconds. It follows the 10/24 "Don't forget: Extra 50% Off is Happening!" send and the 10/23 "Wait...Extra 50% Off?!" send. That makes three near-identical sale emails in three days, so the "last chance" framing is credible but fatigued.
- The email gets weaker below the hero. A second module for TikTok Shop "Super brand day" pulls attention to a different destination, and its fuzzy brown clog has no price or discount attached. The offer also has a soft spot: "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" sit in the hero, and the footer says the offer ends Sunday, October 25th at 11:59pm MT. The email never states that deadline above the fold. The hero shows one grey Baya clog, but nothing says that style is part of the sale.
- Judgement: a solid, clear urgency send with a weak second half. It does not escalate the offer or give a reason to act that the last two sends lacked.

## What's working

- The hero headline "Last Chance: Extra 50% Off!" is the first thing the eye lands on, and the black pill CTA stands out against the cyan.
- The "We hate goodbyes too" eyebrow gives the urgency a bit of brand personality.
- The big "SALE" graphic and the clog image add visual energy without cluttering the hero.
- The black "FREE SHIPPING ON QUALIFIED ORDERS" bar at the top adds a second incentive.
- The footer has a refer-a-friend offer, app links and a store locator, so there are useful routes for non-sale shoppers.

## What's weak

- The Super Brand Day block competes with the main sale. It sends people to TikTok Shop with no price or discount, which dilutes the last-chance message.
- The real deadline ("Ends Sunday, Oct 25th, 11:59pm MT") appears only in the fine print. The hero says "Last Chance" but never says when.
- "Select Styles" and "Exclusions Apply" are vague, and the single clog shown is not tied to a price or a product link.
- This is the third "Extra 50% Off" email in three days, with the same offer and the same "Shop the Sale" CTA.
- The hero has no social proof, reviews or product detail, and the CTA is generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends tonight 11:59pm MT", ideally with a countdown, and make that the headline hook.
- 2. Cut the Super Brand Day module, or move it below the footer-level content. A last-chance send needs one job.
- 3. Show 3–4 specific sale products with before and after prices, and link each one. This replaces the single unexplained clog.
- 4. Make the CTA more specific, such as "Shop Extra 50% Off".
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier sends in the series, or give it a clearly final feel so it doesn't read as a repeat.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select styles, while sizes last. Sale ends 11:59pm MT tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (Exact Target) send with a sound responsive and Outlook scaffold. The confirmed defects are 7 non-HTTPS image sources, a stray `</custom>` closing tag, and one image with no alt text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers weren't captured and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **22 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains (`click.crocs-email.com`), so the QA tool skipped the HTTP check. Destination validity and redirect health are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- **The open pixel is malformed.** It is `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10198&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. The closing `</custom>` tag matches no opening tag. Strict parsers and some clients may mishandle it, and it can break the hidden wrapper `<div style="height: 1px; overflow: hidden;">`.
- **A third-party pixel is also loaded.** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=…` is an Adobe Audience Manager open-event beacon. It has no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Seven images use `http://`.** All are on `http://image.crocs-email.com/lib/fe66157070650c797512/m/1/`, and clients may block or rewrite them as mixed content:
  - `9c36c0ef-…png` ("Download on the App Store")
  - `13246762-…png` ("Get It On Google Play")
  - `c4df71ec-…png` ("Find Your Store")
  - `e18949ff-…png` ("Instagram")
  - `80f68cd8-…png` ("Facebook")
  - `51d8144e-…png` ("TikTok")
  - `1187ef58-…png` ("YouTube")
  
  These are the footer app, store and social icons. All have alt text, so a blocked image still degrades readably.
- **The demdex tracking `<img>` has no alt attribute.** This is minor, since it is a pixel, but `alt=""` would be correct.
- **`<title>` is the generic "Crocs".** It doesn't match the subject line. This is low severity.
- No other rendering defects are confirmed. The source has MSO conditionals, a dark-mode-neutral `.email-body` background, `format-detection` and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` metas, and mobile classes under a `max-width: 639px` breakpoint.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the truncated HTML. The rest of the body wasn't available, so this is not a full verification.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` is not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may have stripped it, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed violation.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) is not found** (QA WARN). Same caveat. If it is genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check against the raw message at the destination mailbox rather than the relay copy.
- The visible-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion, so they are **not verified**.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable from the available data. The links are wrapped in click-redirect URLs, and no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible. The QA tool did not probe the landing pages. Nothing is confirmed broken.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all seven `image.crocs-email.com` assets to `https://`. This is the one change confirmed by both the source and the QA data.
2. Remove the stray `</custom>` after the open-pixel `<img>`, and add `alt=""` to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-run the audit against the raw message, with headers captured from the destination mailbox rather than the AgentMail relay. That would settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
4. Follow the redirect chains on the 22 skipped tracking links, and record the final URLs and UTM parameters.
5. Re-run on the complete HTML, so the footer unsubscribe link, physical address and any merge tokens can be checked.
