# Two Pairs? Don't Mind If You Do. 😉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "2 for $50" send in four days. It follows Nov 10 ("Skip the Line: 2 Pairs for $50"), Nov 11 ("2 for $50. Need We Say More? 👀") and Nov 11 again ("Still Thinking About 2 for $50? 👀"). The creative is clean. "2 for $50" is the largest element, in white on a red-outlined badge over dark green. The white "Shop Early Access" pill sits below it, and the pixel-knit tree gives it a holiday feel. The eye lands on the offer first.
- It also has a visible typo: the headline reads "Shop Early Acccess", with three c's. The big white headline is the second-most prominent thing on the page, so the error is hard to miss. It undercuts an otherwise tidy send.
- The hero has no product, no model and no style cue. "Why choose? Save on two pairs!" is the only reason to click. A subscriber who has seen this panel three times this week gets nothing new. The send is competent but repetitive, and it looks careless because of the typo.

## What's working

- "2 for $50" is the biggest type on the page and the offer is unambiguous.
- The white "Shop Early Access" pill contrasts strongly with the dark green and is easy to find.
- The pixel-knit tree and snowflake logo give a seasonal feel that suits the run-up to Black Friday.
- The "Free shipping on qualified orders" top bar adds a second incentive without clutter.
- The deadline appears in the fine print (ends Nov 14, 11:59pm MT), and "online & in-store" widens the reach.

## What's weak

- The headline typo "Shop Early Acccess" is obvious and damages credibility.
- There are no product images. Nothing shows what the two pairs could be.
- The headline and the button both say "Shop Early Access", which is redundant.
- The deadline is buried in the footer fine print, so the urgency never reaches the hero.
- The hero panel closely repeats the Nov 10 and Nov 11 sends, so it adds to fatigue.
- "Exclusions Apply." sits right under the button with no detail, which makes the offer feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the typo first, and add a QA proofread step to the template.
- 2. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Saturday 11/14", so the urgency is visible.
- 3. Add a product row of 3-4 clogs, such as Classic, Baya and lined styles, each linked to its own page.
- 4. Swap the headline for something new, since "Shop Early Access" is repeated in the button. Try "Your pick of any two."
- 5. Reduce frequency. Four near-identical sends in four days is too many, and a different angle (such as gifting or lined styles) would hold attention better.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is Salesforce Marketing Cloud (ExactTarget) markup with standard responsive and Outlook handling. Its one confirmed defect is a malformed closing tag (`</custom>`) next to the open-tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from AgentMail relay capture, so they can't be confirmed as sender-side problems. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed markup:** the open pixel is closed with a stray tag: `<img src="https://click.crocs-email.com/open.aspx?qs=…&d=10210&bmt=0" width="1" height="1" alt=""></custom>`. `</custom>` has no matching opener. Browsers and most clients ignore it, but it is invalid HTML and some strict parsers or clients may mishandle it.
- **12 tracking links not probed:** these are `click.crocs-email.com` redirects (ExactTarget click-wrapping). The redirect destinations, status codes and chain length are unverified.
- **Third-party Adobe Audience Manager pixel:** `https://demandwarecrocs.demdex.net/event?d_dpid=196107&c_emailopened=Yes&d_dpuid=1c110d5f…`.
  - It fires a second open-tracking call to a separate domain.
  - It passes a per-recipient ID (`d_dpuid`) in the query string.
  - Image-blocking clients and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will pre-fetch or suppress it. Open counts from it are therefore unreliable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on the demdex pixel:** the QA check flagged it. This is a 1×1 tracking image, so the practical impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. The ExactTarget open pixel already has `alt=""`.
- **Generic `<title>`:** it is just "Crocs". This is minor, but it is not descriptive for assistive tech or web-view rendering.
- **Correctly implemented:**
  - `lang` and `xml:lang` attributes.
  - Viewport meta.
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`.
  - Outlook `<!--[if mso]>` `PixelsPerInch` block.
  - Gmail hack (`u + .email-body`).
  - `@font-face` with `font-display: swap`.
  - A 639px breakpoint.
- **Not assessable:** image `alt` text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and link text, because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Only the preheader and the start of the body were captured.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says AgentMail may strip these headers. **Unverified, not confirmed missing.** Check the raw `.eml` or a seed inbox such as Gmail's "Show original" before treating it as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** absent. This is expected for relayed mail. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `crocs-email.com` are unknown from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

Not verifiable. All 12 links are wrapped in ExactTarget redirects (`click.crocs-email.com`) and were skipped by the HTTP probe. The final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status are unknown. No UTMs are visible in the captured source.

1. Fix the `</custom>` stray closing tag after the open-tracking pixel.
2. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to the demdex pixel.
3. Re-test headers from the raw message, not the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
