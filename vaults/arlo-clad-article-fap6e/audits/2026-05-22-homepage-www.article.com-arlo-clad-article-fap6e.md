# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Article is running a full-court Memorial Day press right now — big bold "SALE" on a sage-green canvas, 40% off headline, and a generic "Shop Now" CTA. For a brand I actively track alongside Joybird and Inside Weather, the homepage reads clean and confident, but it's pitching a discount event rather than telling me why Article's sofa game is worth my once-a-year serious spend.

## What's working

- **The "SALE" typographic drop** — the oversized bold dark-green "SALE" on sage is eye-catching and on-brand. Article knows their color palette and it shows.
- **Top dismissible banner** — "Memorial Day Sale: Shop up to 40% off for a limited time" loads above the nav and has a real X. They're not trapping me.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — unambiguous, rounded, dark, sits right below the copy. Zero friction to get into the sale.
- **Seasonally locked** — I opened this on May 22 and they're running Memorial Day. They're paying attention to the calendar.

## What's weak

- **No sofa or living room anchor** — The hero copy calls out "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more." I came here because I want to compare the Sven sofa. The category I care about most is buried in "and more."
- **Zero loyalty or account hook** — I've bought from Article before. There's no "welcome back," no order history pull, no rewards program visible. I'm treated like a cold visitor.
- **The below-fold product peek is a dining table** — That half-visible lifestyle image is a wood dining set. Fine product, wrong category for me right now.
- **No free-shipping callout** — Article's white-glove delivery is a genuine differentiator I research every purchase cycle. Not a word about it.
- **No trust layer** — No review counts, no "X,000 reviews," no return policy mention. For a $2,000 sofa decision, I need more than a sale banner.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom" for a category grid** — Give me tappable chips: Living Room / Dining / Outdoor / Bedroom. I'll self-select in one tap and Article knows which segment I'm in.
- **Surface delivery info in the hero subtext** — Add "Includes white-glove delivery" or "Free delivery over $X" right below the "SHOP NOW" button. That single line converts furniture cross-shoppers like me who read every FAQ.
- **Pin a "Sofas & Sectionals" card immediately below the hero** — One lifestyle shot of the Sven (or equivalent) in a moody apartment context. That's the $1,500–$2,500 ticket item Memorial Day is made for.
- **Add a trust strip before the scroll** — "50,000+ reviews," "Free returns," "Assembled in your home." One horizontal band, one line. It's the difference between a browse and a tab left open for a week.
