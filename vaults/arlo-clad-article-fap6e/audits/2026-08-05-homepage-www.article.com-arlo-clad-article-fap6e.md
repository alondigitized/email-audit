# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Article's leading with a sofa-first hero that's basically speaking my language before I've scrolled an inch — "Sofas for more than just sitting" with a genuinely cozy, lived-in lifestyle shot. This reads like a design-forward direct-to-consumer brand that knows its core audience cares about the couch as a centerpiece, not just seating. Right now they're pushing versatility (lounging/storage/slumber) and a free interior design consult, not a hard discount.

## What's working

- The headline "Sofas for more than just sitting" — smart, it immediately reframes the sofa as a lifestyle piece, which is exactly how I think about my own Sven.
- The hero photo — burnt-orange tufted sofa with visible wood legs and a textured throw pillow, shot in warm natural light with real art on the wall. This is the kind of image I'd screenshot for a mood board.
- "SHOP VERSATILE SOFAS" CTA — unambiguous, tells me precisely where I'm going, no vague "Discover" nonsense.
- The top banner offering a free Interior Design Plan — as someone who agonizes over one big purchase a year, a free stylist consult is genuinely useful, not just filler.

## What's weak

- No visible discount or promo percentage anywhere above the fold — I'm the guy who waits for the rare 10%-off window, and there's nothing here telling me if I'm in one.
- No loyalty/rewards or "welcome back" signal — the account icon is just a generic silhouette, nothing personalized.
- The lifestyle imagery is mom-and-daughter, which is sweet but doesn't picture someone like me (30s guy furnishing solo/with a partner) — feels aimed at a slightly different household.
- No hint of white-glove delivery info, fit/dimension callouts, or fabric/leather options this high up — I always want that FAQ close at hand.

## Recommendations

- Swap in a stat or badge near the CTA — even something like "Free White-Glove Delivery" would immediately answer the question I always have first.
- If there's a live promo, surface the actual percentage in the hero or top banner instead of burying it — "10% off select sofas" would stop my scroll instantly.
- Add a second lifestyle frame in the hero rotation with a couple or solo adult in a design-forward space — broadens who sees themselves in it.
- Put a "New Arrivals" or "Best Sellers" strip right under the hero so people like me who cross-shop can compare styles in one tap.
