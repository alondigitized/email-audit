# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Article is a brand I actively cross-shop, so landing here feels familiar — clean Scandinavian-adjacent aesthetic, direct-to-consumer pricing story. Right now they're leading hard with a Memorial Day Sale, which is smart timing (it's May 15, the holiday is the 25th), but the above-the-fold experience on mobile is essentially one campaign card and a lifestyle dining shot. For a guy who spends three weeks researching before pulling the trigger on a sofa, this is a good entry point but it doesn't give me much to chew on yet.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day timing** — I'm in research mode for a dining table right now. Seeing "up to 40%" in a category I'm actively shopping snaps me to attention.
- **The "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more" callout** — listing room categories instead of just yelling "SALE" tells me exactly where the deal applies. That specificity matters when you're cross-shopping.
- **The lifestyle dining photo** — warm oak table, neutral upholstered chairs, bowl of fruit. That's the exact aesthetic I'm going for. Article knows its customer.
- **Announcement bar + hero redundancy** — the sale message hits twice (banner + hero), so even if I immediately dismiss the banner, the message lands.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — on mobile, you get one job per screen. Article gives me one message, one CTA. That's discipline.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — I'm a focused buyer. "Shop Dining Room Sale" or "Shop the Memorial Day Sale" would channel me into the right funnel immediately instead of dropping me on a catch-all sale page.
- **No urgency deadline** — "for a limited time" is a nothing phrase. Memorial Day is May 25 — show me a countdown or at least say "Ends May 26." That's a real reason to act today.
- **No free-shipping callout** — for furniture, delivery cost is a huge anxiety point. White-glove delivery fees can be $150+ and I always check this first. Not showing it above the fold is a miss.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no "4.8 stars from 12,000 reviews," no "winner of X award." Article has earned social proof; they're not using it.
- **No loyalty or account hook** — I've definitely visited this site before. No "sign in to see member pricing" or "save to wishlist" nudge. Leaving retention money on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for a room-specific CTA** — "Shop Dining Room Sale" given that the lifestyle image is a dining table. One tweak, immediately more relevant.
- **Add a deadline** — drop "Ends May 26" or a live countdown under the hero subhead. You've already set up the urgency framing; finish the job.
- **Surface the delivery promise above the fold** — a one-line strip ("Free white-glove delivery on orders over $X") would address the single biggest hesitation furniture shoppers have.
- **Add a star rating teaser** — even a small "★ 4.8 · 10,000+ reviews" line under the hero copy signals social proof without disrupting the layout.
