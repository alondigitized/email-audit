# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Article is leading with outdoor dining for what's clearly a summer push — clean, minimal, unmistakably Article in its aesthetic. As a guy who came here to lurk the Sven and compare it against Joybird's Briar, the outdoor dining hero is a slight misdirect, but I can't deny it looks good. The brand still reads premium, considered, and grown-up, which is why I cross-shop here in the first place.

## What's working

- **"Perfectly pre-paired." headline** — smart, low-friction copy that speaks to a millennial buyer who hates decision fatigue. I respect that.
- **CTA button design** — white pill on a dark-ish image background is high contrast and immediately tappable on mobile.
- **Hero image quality** — the outdoor dining vignette looks editorial, not catalog. Earthy tones, real materials, not a white-void photoshoot. That's why I trust Article.
- **Search bar placement** — right below the nav. I'm a high-tech-comfort shopper; I'll type "Sven leather" before I tap any category. Glad it's front and center.
- **Email subscribe banner** — non-intrusive, dismissible, and relevant to me specifically since I wait for that rare 10%-off promo drop.

## What's weak

- **Wrong hero for my visit intent** — I'm here to lurk sofas and I'm immediately looking at outdoor dining. Article makes great indoor seating; nothing above the fold says so.
- **Zero price signal** — if there's any active sale or promotion, I have zero idea from this screen. Waiting for promos is literally my buying strategy and the homepage gives me nothing.
- **No trust scaffold for a big-ticket buyer** — no review count, no "free white-glove delivery" badge, no assembly note. I'm about to spend $2,000+ and the homepage is content-silent on why that's safe.
- **Wishlist icon is cold** — there's a heart in the nav but nothing pushes me toward saving a product. If I've visited before, there's no "pick up where you left off" prompt.
- **No navigation label visible** — hamburger menu hides everything. I can't tell if there's a "Living Room" category without tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Add a secondary above-fold nav strip** — horizontal scrollable chips like "Sofas · Dining · Outdoor · Beds" directly below the hero. One tap to my category without opening the hamburger.
- **Surface a proof point in the hero** — swap sub-copy for something like "Free white-glove delivery. Free returns. Always." — that alone would stop a big-ticket hesitator from bouncing.
- **Drop a promo teaser in the subscribe banner** — change "Get new products and promotions" to "Be first for our rare sitewide sales — we run them maybe 4× a year." That's more honest and would double my subscribe rate.
- **Add a "Because you've been eyeing the Sven" type recently-viewed module** — or even just a "Shop Living Room" secondary CTA below the hero to catch shoppers who aren't in outdoor mode.
