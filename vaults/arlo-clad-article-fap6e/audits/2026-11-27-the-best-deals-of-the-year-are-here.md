# The best deals of the year are here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday finale, and it's the first send in the run that changes the creative. The "ENDS MONDAY" band on top is the one new element. The hero is the same monochrome "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" block with the coral "SHOP NOW" pill we saw on Nov 16, 19, 21 and 26. The "up to 40% off" number is buried in a paragraph under the hero instead of in the hero. The subject, "The best deals of the year are here," is generic and doesn't match the Nov 26 "Even more Black Friday deals" thread.
- The 8-tile room grid is the best part of this send. It lets a shopper jump straight to the room they're furnishing, which beats a single generic CTA. The tiles are also dark and low-contrast. The white "Shop Living Room" labels sit on busy photos with a heavy overlay, so they read as muddy rather than inviting.
- Judgement: the deadline is useful, but this is still the fifth near-identical sale email in two weeks. The "ends Monday" urgency is real. Fatigue is the main risk.

## What's working

- "ENDS MONDAY" is a clear, credible deadline at the very top, the first new signal in the Black Friday run.
- The 8-tile category grid lets shoppers self-segment by room.
- The copy "so you might want to make a move" is on-brand and light.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The Help Center, Satisfaction Guarantee and Shipping icons add reassurance in the footer.

## What's weak

- The "up to 40%" offer is in small body text below the hero, not in the hero itself.
- The hero is the same recycled "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" block as the last four sends.
- The category labels are low-contrast white text over dark, busy photos.
- The hero "SHOP NOW" pill is small and sits on a dark, noisy gradient.
- The secondary CTAs ("Explore what's new", "Shop best sellers") are weak and off-message in the final-days push.
- No specific product, price or customer review is shown anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "UP TO 40% OFF FURNITURE · ENDS MONDAY." Make that the headline instead of repeating "BLACK FRIDAY SALE."
- 2. Replace the dark overlays on the tiles with lighter ones, or add per-room savings ("Living Room: up to 40% off").
- 3. Add a countdown timer or a "Mon, 11:59 PM PT" cutoff for credible urgency.
- 4. Feature 2-3 hero products with sale prices and star ratings above the grid.
- 5. Drop "Explore what's new" and "Shop best sellers." Repeat the sale CTA, or add free-delivery or financing info.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source has several CSS defects in the dark-mode rules, an empty `<title>`, and three images without alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and are unconfirmed. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and UTM parameters could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 16. No broken links were reported, but 16 links are unverified.
- The images load through click-tracking redirects, for example `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/iYs9xSKPW_.../AAQVbhA~/tnxPSU98...` and `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Q4-MmRElYx5C_.../J5JOC4C1...`. If the tracker is slow or the link expires, these images fail to load.
- A second tracking domain, `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`, serves the image whose alt-text warning is labelled "27". The email therefore uses two redirect domains. The unrelated `spmailtechnol.com` domain is not aligned with the `email.article.com` sender, which can weaken reputation and trigger link-mismatch warnings in some filters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged three images. The sources are `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/iYs9xSKPW_...`, `links.email.article.com/s/eo/I7kP5okP3X_...` and `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Q4-MmRElYx5C_...`. These are unreadable to screen readers and blank when images are blocked.
- **Invalid dark-mode CSS in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma. That makes the selector invalid, so the whole rule is dropped and heading and paragraph text will not be forced white.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` puts the `;` before `!important`, so the declaration is malformed.
  - The block is missing its closing `}` before `</style>`, and `line-height:auto` is not a valid value. The second `[data-ogsc]` block ends with an extra `}`.
  - Dark-mode link and text colors may therefore not apply. Background switching via `.darkmode` and `.grey` may still work, which could leave dark text on a dark background.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- No other rendering issues are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. Unrendered tokens could not be checked in the truncated section.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If it is really absent, it is a deliverability risk with Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** the header was not found, with the same relay caveat. One-click unsubscribe is unverified.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found. QA expects this from the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, not failing.
- **Footer:** the visible visible-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in link probing, but gives no result for it.
- **Plain-text part:** URLs make up 2,367 of 3,323 characters (71%), just over the 70% threshold. This is a minor spam-filter signal, likely caused by long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page destinations cannot be assessed. The links are wrapped in redirects and the source is truncated.
- No landing-page mismatch evidence is available.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the three flagged images, or `alt=""` for decorative ones.
2. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{` to `h1,h2,p{`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Close the `@media` block and remove `line-height:auto`.
   - Remove the stray `}` at the end of the `[data-ogsc]` block.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject line.
4. Check the raw `.eml` or the sender's ESP, not the AgentMail copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating these as defects.
5. Serve images from a first-party domain, or stop wrapping image URLs in click-tracking redirects.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to check UTMs, merge tokens, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address. Also probe the 16 skipped links.
