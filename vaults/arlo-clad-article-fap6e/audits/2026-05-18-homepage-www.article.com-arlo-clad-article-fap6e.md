# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Oh, this is exactly what I've been waiting for — Article dropped a Memorial Day Sale right on the hero and they're not being shy about it. Clean sage-green canvas, big serif "SALE" that punches, a dining table lifestyle shot that looks like it could be my own apartment. This is Article doing what Article does best: Scandinavian restraint with a clear commercial reason to act right now.

## What's working

- **The sale banner cascade** — dual reinforcement (sticky top strip *and* full hero) means I cannot miss the offer. I opened the app and immediately know there's a reason to shop today.
- **"Dining Room, Bedroom, and more" copy** — calling out the specific categories I'm decision-making on right now does real work. This isn't "great deals on everything," it's targeted enough to feel relevant.
- **The dining table lifestyle shot** — barely peeking above the fold, but the wood tones, upholstered chairs, and minimal styling look expensive without looking precious. It's the scroll hook.
- **Sage-green hero palette** — feels fresh and seasonal without being garish. Fits the Article brand identity I trust.
- **Search bar placement** — prominent, labeled "Search products & help …" — the "& help" detail matters to me because I always end up in FAQs about white-glove delivery.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too broad** — as someone cross-shopping three stores, I want to go straight to Sofas or Dining Room, not a general sale grid. The CTA should be more specific or I lose momentum sorting through irrelevant SKUs.
- **Zero delivery or returns signals above the fold** — Article's white-glove delivery is a genuine differentiator vs. Joybird and Inside Weather, and it's invisible here. I read every delivery FAQ before I buy; a one-liner ("Free white-glove delivery on all orders") would have me leaning in harder.
- **No urgency specificity** — "for a limited time" is the weakest possible deadline. Memorial Day is literally a date — Article should say "ends May 26" and mean it. Vague scarcity doesn't move me.
- **No social proof in sight** — I know Article has reviews. A "4.8★ from 12,000 customers" badge somewhere in this hero would address the trust anxiety I always feel before a $1,500 purchase.

## Recommendations

- **Split the CTA by category** — replace "SHOP NOW" with two pill buttons: "Shop Sofas" and "Shop Dining Room." Friction goes down, intent matching goes up, AOV probably increases.
- **Add white-glove delivery as a hero footnote** — one line under the CTA: "Free white-glove delivery on every order." That sentence is the reason I pick Article over a competitor; don't bury it in the footer.
- **Put a real date on the sale** — change "for a limited time" to "Ends May 26" in both the top strip and the hero subhead. Credible deadline = I don't tab-close to come back "later" (which means never).
- **Drop one star-rating callout into the hero** — a tight "★★★★★ 50,000+ reviews" lockup near the CTA costs nothing to add and closes the trust gap for big-ticket first-time buyers like me.
