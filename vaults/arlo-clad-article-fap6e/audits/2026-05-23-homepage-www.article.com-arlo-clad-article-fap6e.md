# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Article is running a clean, on-brand Memorial Day Sale push — the sage green hero is very them, minimal and confident. Right now they're all about urgency and a 40%-off hook, which, honestly, is rare enough from Article that I'm already paying attention. The below-fold dining set image is nice but doesn't hit my sweet spot (sofas, seating, leather), so the page earns my interest without fully earning my session.

## What's working

- **The 40% off headline** — Article almost never runs this deep. "Act fast—these deals end Monday" in the dismissible banner, plus "while deals last" in the hero subtext, creates real urgency without feeling desperate.
- **Memorial Day timing** — showing up with a sale on the exact long weekend when furniture cross-shoppers (me, comparing Article vs. Joybird vs. Inside Weather) are actively pulling the trigger is smart calendar awareness.
- **Search bar placement** — right under the nav, always visible. For someone like me who already knows I want "Sven sofa" or "leather sectional," this is the fastest path to intent.
- **Sage green palette** — unmistakably Article. I know exactly what brand I'm on without reading the logo.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is too generic** — for a sofa shopper it doesn't route me anywhere. Is this sofas? Dining? Everything? I'd need to tap through just to orient myself.
- **Below-fold image is a dining set** — the visible lifestyle photo is a wood dining table with neutral chairs. I'm here for the Sven. Nothing in my viewport says "sofas" or "seating."
- **No delivery or white-glove mention** — I read every delivery FAQ before I buy a sofa. Zero mention of free shipping threshold, white-glove service, or delivery windows anywhere in the above-fold zone.
- **No social proof** — no review count, no star ratings, no "4.8 stars from 12,000 reviews" credibility strip. For a $2,000+ purchase, I want that reassurance early.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Sofas & Seating" (or split into two CTAs)** — let a returning sofa shopper bypass the sale grid and land in their category. One tap to intent.
- **Add a delivery credibility strip below the hero** — one line: "Free shipping on orders over $X · White-glove delivery available" pulls in high-AOV browsers like me who are already on the fence.
- **Replace or augment the dining lifestyle shot** with a leather sofa or living-room scene — seating is Article's flagship category and it's invisible in my first scroll.
- **Drop a trust strip above the footer** — "4.8 stars · 40,000+ reviews · 365-day returns" is enough to lower the conversion friction for big-ticket buyers.
