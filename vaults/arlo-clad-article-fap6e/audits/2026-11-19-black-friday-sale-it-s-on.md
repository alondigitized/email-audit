# Black Friday Sale: It’s on
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday sale email, and it is almost the same as the Nov 16 "Black Friday: It's go time" send. The same black "UP TO 40% OFF" band, the same monochrome "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero and the same coral "SHOP NOW" pill all appear again. A subscriber who got both emails three days apart sees no new reason to act. The only new element is the "Our biggest sale of the year is back for a limited time" line, which adds urgency but no date.
- The layout itself is tidy. The offer is stated first, the hero is bold and there is one clear button. Below it is an 8-tile room grid that lets a shopper pick a category. The weakness is that the grid is only labels on darkened photos. It shows no product, no price and no sale proof. "Up to 40%" is also repeated, and it is never tied to a deadline or a hero item.
- My judgement is that this is a competent reminder send but a low-effort one. It works as a navigation page and does little to create urgency or sell a specific product.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF" band sits above the hero, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The hero is high-contrast, with a single coral "SHOP NOW" pill that is easy to find.
- The 2x4 room grid is clean and scannable. It covers living, bedroom, dining, entryway, office, outdoor, decor and "All Sale".
- The footer has useful reassurance: a 30-day satisfaction guarantee, shipping and help links, plus "Explore what's new" and "Shop best sellers" buttons.
- No render bugs are visible. The images load and the text does not overlap.

## What's weak

- There is no deadline. "Limited time" is vague, and Black Friday emails live on a credible end date.
- Most of the email repeats the Nov 16 creative and the "up to 40%" message. Nothing signals that this send is different.
- The grid tiles have a dark overlay with white labels that sit over busy photos. "Shop Dining Room" and "Shop Entryway" are hard to read. No tile shows a price, a product name or a discount.
- No single product is featured and there are no reviews. Shoppers get room names, with nothing concrete to click on.
- "Up to 40%" is repeated in the black band, in the hero and in the footer line. It is the only offer, and it has no examples.
- The footer is dark grey on grey. The legal and unsubscribe text is very small and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline in the hero and the subject, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm PT". This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Replace at least four of the grid tiles with hero products that show a name, the original price, the sale price and a discount badge. Keep the room tiles below them as navigation.
- 3. Differentiate this send from Nov 16. Lead with something new, such as an extra-savings tier, a doorbuster, or a "now live" item count.
- 4. Make the tile labels legible. Use a lighter overlay or put the label on a solid bar under the photo.
- 5. Add one piece of social proof, such as a review quote or a "best seller" tag, since the footer buttons already point to best sellers.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 40% off every room, through Monday at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Article, "Black Friday Sale: It's on"

The audit covers only the truncated HTML and the QA output. The truncation cuts off the body and footer, so findings about those areas are limited.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard responsive, table-based layout with dark-mode support. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The confirmed defects are malformed dark-mode CSS, an empty `<title>`, and three images without alt text. The header and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 16 were skipped, with no broken links reported. The skipped links are footer, utility and social links, so their status is unverified.
- **Unbranded image host:** Images are served through `https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...~~/AAQVbhA~/...`, a third-party redirect domain. Other images use the branded `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. Two different tracking domains are in play. The unbranded one can be blocked or flagged by some filters and image proxies.
- **Plain-text part:** 2368 of 3306 characters (72%) are URLs, because the tracked URLs are long. This pushes the text part past the QA 70% threshold and can add spam-score risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images:**
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/12s3zFbbJzz3GHBvlh2_2w~~/AAQVbhA~/svbaUTRn...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/UD238K0Qef-...`
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/c-r1daqJwluWCPb-xDKEIg~~/AAQVbhA~/mdgEaqoQ...`

  The `/s/eo/` URL looks like an open-tracking pixel, where `alt=""` would be correct. I can't confirm that from the data. The other two may be content images, which need descriptive alt text.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid dark-mode CSS in the first `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma before `{`. The selector list is invalid, so browsers drop the whole rule and headings and paragraphs get no dark-mode colour override in standards-based clients. The `[data-ogsc]` block uses the correct `h1,h2,p` form, so Outlook.com gets the override and other clients don't.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `!important` after the semicolon. The declaration is ignored or loses its priority.
  - `a .link{...}` is a descendant selector. `a.link` was probably intended.
  - `line-height:auto!important` in `.dark-img` is not a valid value.
- **Lang attribute:** `lang="en"` is present and `charset=utf-8` is declared, so there is no issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. There are no unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%`. The truncated body is not covered.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. It is not a confirmed sender failure. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** The header was not captured, so the result is unknown. This is expected with the relay.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated section and could not be checked. QA always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable from the data. Every destination is wrapped in a click-tracking redirect, so final URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible. No probe failures were reported for the 12 links checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the dark-mode CSS: remove the trailing comma in `h1,h2,p,`, change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`, and replace `line-height:auto`.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the two content images. Set `alt=""` on the open-tracking pixel.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject or preheader text.
4. Check the original message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, before treating these as defects.
5. Serve images from the branded `links.email.article.com` domain instead of `post.spmailtechnol.com`.
6. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or use a lighter text version, to bring URL density under 70%.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the resolved redirect chains to check UTMs, the footer, and merge tokens.
