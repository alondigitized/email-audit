# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Article is in full Memorial Day sale mode and they're not being subtle about it — sage green hero, "SALE" in huge dark type, 40% off headline. This is exactly the kind of event I have a mental bookmark for. The homepage reads clean, tasteful, and direct: minimal Scandinavian energy that's been Article's whole brand identity, no confusion about what they want me to do. But as someone who'll spend three hours reading the white-glove delivery FAQ before pulling the trigger, there's almost nothing below the button to close the deal.

## What's working

- **Banner + hero redundancy on the same offer**: Both the dismissible top strip and the full hero say the same thing. No mixed signals about what's happening. I came to cross-shop furniture — Article's telling me to shop furniture. No wasted real estate.
- **"SHOP NOW" button**: Dark oval against the sage background is high-contrast and has a large tap target. On mobile this is exactly right. No "Explore Our Collection" ambiguity here.
- **Sage green palette**: Deliberate seasonal color — reads spring/outdoor without going full summer. As someone who follows Article drops, this feels like a purposeful campaign aesthetic, not leftover templates.
- **Dining table lifestyle image below the fold**: That warm-wood natural-finish table is a mood board screenshot. It signals Article's quality and aesthetic before I've scrolled twice.
- **Search bar front and center**: I already know I want a Sven or maybe a dining bench. Having search prominent on mobile is the right call for a high-intent returning visitor.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 40%" is a classic hedge**: I read every FAQ. "Up to" means one item on page twelve is 40% off and everything I actually want is 15%. I need a range or a category-specific callout before I trust the headline.
- **Sofas buried in "and more"**: The hero calls out Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom — and then "and more." The Sven sofa is Article's most-talked-about product and living room furniture is their highest-consideration category. Tucking it into "and more" on the homepage feels like a miss.
- **Zero delivery or logistics signals**: I'm buying one piece this year and I will read every word of the white-glove delivery FAQ. Nothing above the fold even hints that this exists. For a furniture brand selling $800+ sofas, this is a real gap.
- **"For a limited time" with no end date**: No countdown, no "Ends May 26," nothing. Every furniture brand says "limited time" for three weeks. This doesn't move me.
- **No trust signals**: No free-shipping threshold, no easy returns callout, no review count. Article has strong reviews — none of that is visible here.

## Recommendations

- **Add "Sofas" or "Living Room" to the hero body copy**: Changing "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more" to "Sofas, Dining, Outdoor, Bedroom" puts their highest-consideration category in the first scan zone — one word change, big impact.
- **Add a free-shipping callout or "white-glove delivery included" line** directly beneath the SHOP NOW button: This single line would reduce bounce from high-consideration buyers who need logistics clarity before they'll engage further.
- **Give the sale a real end date**: Even "Ends Monday, May 26" beats "for a limited time." I'll convert now instead of bookmarking it.
- **Add a secondary quick-link for 2–3 anchor products below the hero**: A small product rail showing a Sven variant, a dining table, and maybe an outdoor piece — each with the sale price visible — would let me skip the landing page and land directly where I know I'm going.
