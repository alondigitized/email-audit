# Last chance: Memorial Day Sale ends today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Article's sixth Memorial Day email in roughly ten days — and the "Last chance: ends today" framing finally pays off the countdown they've been running since May 19. The render is clean, the green-on-green color blocking makes "ENDS TODAY" land hard, and the "SALE" word is rendered massive so the eye knows exactly what's happening in under a second.
- But — and it's a real but — there is no discount number anywhere visible above the fold. The May 22 deadline send hit a perfect 10 because it married "ends Monday" with "up to 40% off." This one drops the offer entirely and asks me to trust that "Memorial Day Sale" still means the 40% I saw three sends ago. For a last-chance email, omitting the headline number is a real miss.
- Net: a competent, on-brand closer that captures the urgency moment but leaves money on the table by burying the offer. Solid 6, not the 9 it could have been.

## What's working

- "ENDS TODAY" in the top green bar gives instant, credible urgency.
- The "SALE" wordmark is huge — single dominant focal point, no competing elements.
- Four-up category grid (Outdoor / Dining / Bedroom / All Sale) gives a clean path for whatever I care about.
- "30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee" line right under the CTA is a smart trust nudge for a deadline-pressure send.
- Footer is restrained — Help Center, Satisfaction Guarantee, Shipping — reinforces confidence at the bottom of a panic-buy email.

## What's weak

- No discount percentage visible anywhere in the body. "Up to 40% off" was the whole hook of this campaign and it's gone.
- "SHOP THE SALE" CTA is generic — no verb-noun specificity, no price anchor.
- "Hundreds of deals (before they're gone for good)" is vague — which deals, on what?
- The two secondary CTAs at the bottom ("EXPLORE WHAT'S NEW" / "SHOP BEST SELLERS") dilute the last-chance message — they pull attention to non-sale browsing.
- Category tiles all read "Shop X" — repetitive, no individual hook or featured product per tile.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the discount number back in the hero.** "UP TO 40% OFF · ENDS TODAY" should be the headline. The whole campaign trained subscribers on that number; stripping it on the closer wastes the conditioning.
- 2. **Make the CTA specific:** "Shop 40% Off →" instead of "SHOP THE SALE."
- 3. **Drop the bottom "Explore What's New / Best Sellers" buttons** on a last-chance send — they invite browsing instead of converting.
- 4. **Add a real deadline:** "Sale ends 11:59 PM PT tonight" beats the abstract "ends today."
- 5. **Differentiate the four category tiles** — one featured piece per tile with a price (e.g., "Sven Sofa — $1,599") instead of identical "Shop X" labels.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 40% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only — Memorial Day Sale closes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of deals, gone at midnight. Free shipping on orders $999+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at 40% off Sven, Ceni, and the rest.`
