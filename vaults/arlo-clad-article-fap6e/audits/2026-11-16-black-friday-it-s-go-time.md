# Black Friday: It’s go time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday launch email, and it delivers what the Nov 8 teaser ("Black Friday's almost here") promised. "UP TO 40% OFF" sits in a black band above a monochrome "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero with a coral "SHOP NOW" pill. In the Nov 8 send that number was in small white text. Here it is the first thing the eye lands on.
- Below the hero, an 8-tile room grid (Living Room, Bedroom, Dining Room, Entryway, Home Office, Outdoor, Decor, All Sale) makes it easy to jump to a category. The weakness is specificity. There is no product, no price, no deadline and no promo mechanic. "Up to 40%" is the only number, "starts now" is the only urgency, and every tile is a bare category label. The hero's black-on-grey grain is stark next to Article's usual warm, photographic sends like the Travertine email on Nov 13.
- Verdict: a clear, competent launch that does the job. It reads like a template and has little to make anyone act today.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 40% OFF" band is the first thing visible and states the offer plainly.
- The coral "SHOP NOW" pill is the only colored element in the hero, so it is easy to find.
- The 2x4 room grid gives the shopper eight clear entry points, with "Shop All Sale" as a catch-all.
- The tiles use real lifestyle photography with legible white labels, in the brand's warm, premium look.
- The email is short and scannable, and it follows through on the Nov 8 teaser.

## What's weak

- No deadline or end date. "Black Friday starts now" gives no reason to act today.
- No hero product, price or example discount. "Up to 40%" could mean one item or the whole catalog.
- Room labels on darkened photos are readable but plain. Every tile has the same weight, so nothing leads.
- The footer cluster ("Explore what's new", "Shop best sellers", and the Help, Guarantee and Shipping icons) competes with the sale and offers no extra reassurance about price-matching or shipping cost.
- The hero's gradient is dark and flat, and the 40% band and hero headline repeat each other.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real product with a real price in the hero, for example "Sloan sofa, now $X (was $Y)". It would make the offer concrete and give people something to click.
- 2. Add a credible end date, such as "Ends Cyber Monday", and a countdown or a line about the sale window.
- 3. Say what is discounted: a few "up to 40%" callouts per category, or "from $X" on each tile.
- 4. Cut the footer to one secondary CTA. Add one free-shipping or financing line if it is true.
- 5. Drop the duplicate headline. Have the black band carry the offer and use the hero for imagery.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is on: up to 40% off furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our biggest sale of the year starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, beds and dining sets, all marked down through Cyber Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 40% off every room, with new markdowns this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with dark-mode support, but its dark-mode CSS has syntax errors, three images have no alt text, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers couldn't be verified. The QA run reported 0 hard failures and 4 warnings. The HTML I received is truncated, so body-level findings are limited to what the head and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 16 were skipped (footer, utility and social links). No broken links were reported, but the 16 skipped links are unverified.
- **Redirect-wrapped URLs:** Images and links go through third-party redirect hosts, `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...~~/AAQVbhA~/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. This means two different tracking domains are in use, and neither is the sending domain `email.article.com`. I can't verify the final destinations from the source. If either redirect domain has a certificate or DNS problem, both images and links will fail.
- **Plain-text part:** 2367 of 3323 characters (about 71%) are URLs, which trips the QA ">70% URLs" warning. The cause is long tracking-wrapped URLs, so the plain-text alternative is mostly unreadable link strings. This is a mild spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - `…B00IDbOpsaqZvEcjC1SyljjjNZhZKSpcRpxx-wypkO7RLGBKRp` (`post.spmailtechnol.com/q/lGVsBkmpQiBGIY1yj0nV0Q~~/…`)
  - `…26` (`links.email.article.com/s/eo/wA6A2Jxgf3T3ewfp2X2F7UcGAZKQWk4OumpUCx4LTv8a-…`)
  - `…8v94EmHTZRP3ybtm7KAgHfcyZZCrc_hnTjlN5Q1pK9Rx7Ncdfx` (`post.spmailtechnol.com/q/xiV48aWkK_Cd3OXL4DNbXg~~/…`)

  If any are tracking pixels, they need `alt=""`. If any are content images, they need descriptive alt text. The `/s/eo/` path may be an open-tracking pixel, but I can't confirm that from the source I have.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is present in `<head>`. This is minor, but some clients and assistive tech use it.
- **Dark-mode CSS errors** in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma in the selector. Strict parsers will drop the whole rule, so heading and paragraph colour overrides may not apply.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `;`, so the `!important` is invalid.
  - The `@media` block is never closed. The block ends with `a .link{color:#fff!important}</style>`, and browsers will auto-close it.
  - `line-height:auto!important` in `.dark-img` is not a valid `line-height` value.
- **Dark-mode risk:** If the `p` and `a` rules are dropped, text may stay dark on the forced `.darkmode{background-color:#000!important}` background. That would be a contrast failure, but I haven't rendered it to confirm.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible HTML or headers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** Neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) from bulk senders, so check the original message.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact. Verify with the raw message or a seed-inbox test.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Because every link is wrapped in a redirect, I can't inspect UTM parameters or landing-page alignment from the HTML. The probe reported no failures, but it didn't expose the final URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the three flagged images if they are tracking or decorative, or descriptive alt text if they are content.
2. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{…}` to `h1,h2,p{…}`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Close the `@media` block.
   - Replace `line-height:auto`.
3. Populate `<title>`.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be hiding them.
5. Check that the redirect domains (`spmailtechnol.com`, `links.email.article.com`) resolve with valid TLS, ideally on a branded subdomain.
6. Follow redirects on the 16 unprobed links and the primary CTAs to confirm final URLs, UTM parameters and unsubscribe behaviour.
7. Improve the plain-text version with shorter or fewer URLs, and descriptive text around them.
