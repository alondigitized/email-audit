# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Article and the hero is literally about leather. "A lesson in leather." is basically a Sven-patina love letter, so they got me in the first second. The page looks like a clean, modern DTC furniture brand that's pushing education (leather care) and a free design service rather than a price promo. That's on-brand, but I'm a guy who waits for the 10% drop, and nothing here tells me one exists.

## What's working

- **"A lesson in leather." hero + "much easier to care for than you think":** It answers the objection I actually have about leather, which is upkeep. It talks to me like someone who researches before a big purchase.
- **Macro shot of the stitched seam and cushion:** The close-up shows grain, stitching and patina, which is what I look for in leather. It reads as craftsmanship, not stock photography.
- **"SHOP LEATHER SOFAS" pill button:** The copy is literal and the contrast is strong. I know exactly where I'm going.
- **Top nav with Best Sellers, New Arrivals, Bundles and Sale:** I can jump to my shopping mode in one tap. "Article Pro" also signals they take design seriously.
- **Living Room tile in Shop By Room:** The first tile shows a cognac leather sofa in a real room, which is my aesthetic.

## What's weak

- **No price, discount or shipping info above the fold:** I buy one big piece a year and wait for a promo. Nothing tells me a promo is coming, so I have no reason to act now.
- **Top bar promo ("free Interior Design Plan today"):** It's a service, not savings. It's nice, but it isn't what gets me to buy.
- **No product in the hero:** I see leather texture but no actual sofa, name, price or rating. I can't tell what "Shop Leather Sofas" will show me.
- **Hero is a two-panel composite with a hard seam down the middle:** The left panel has a dark, blurry vertical shape that looks like an obstruction. It feels slightly unpolished next to the sharp right panel.
- **No trust signals visible:** No review counts, no white-glove delivery mention, no returns or free-shipping info. I read every delivery FAQ, and none of it is surfaced here.
- **No people in the imagery:** Nothing that says "someone like me lives with this."

## Recommendations

- Add a slim value strip under the hero, for example "White-glove delivery · Free returns · 4.x★ from X reviews." That's the stuff I go digging for in the FAQ, and it would cut my research time.
- Put one real sofa in the hero, either a full-frame leather sofa in a lived-in room or an overlay card with name, price and star rating next to the CTA. Keep the stitching close-up as a second slide.
- Replace or supplement the top bar with a concrete member or email offer, such as "Join for 10% off your first order." That's my hook, and it also gives you my email.
- Fix the hero seam: use one continuous image, or align the two panels so the dark blur on the left doesn't look like a glitch.
