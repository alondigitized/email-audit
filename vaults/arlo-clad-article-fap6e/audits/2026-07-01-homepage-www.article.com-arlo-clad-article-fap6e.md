# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Article is leading with a 4th of July sale and — okay, I'll admit — my thumb slowed down the moment I saw "up to 40% off." This is exactly the promo window I've been waiting for since I bookmarked three sofas in February. The homepage is clean and on-brand, but it's essentially a sale billboard: one big CTA, no depth, no product context. For someone who genuinely cross-shops this brand and reads delivery FAQs before committing, the page gets me in the door but leaves a lot of selling undone.

## What's working

- **The sale banner + hero are perfectly aligned** — the dismissible top strip and the full hero both say the same thing, which creates zero confusion about what this site wants me to do right now
- **"SHOP NOW" button color choice** — the deep red against the cream background is high-contrast and deliberately urgent without feeling cheap
- **Seasonal timing is right** — I'm actively in furniture consideration mode and a 4th of July sale is credible enough that I don't roll my eyes
- **The sofa lifestyle shot at the bottom of the viewport** — even cut off, seeing a gray sectional with throw pillows and a side table is the right image for a guy who posts Sven patina updates

## What's weak

- **"Save up to 40% on a wide selection of beautiful Indoor and Outdoor styles"** — this is boilerplate sale copy. It could be any furniture brand. No mention of specific categories (sofas, sectionals), no tease of what's actually discounted
- **Zero trust or delivery signals above the fold** — for furniture, white-glove delivery is a major conversion factor for me. Nothing here tells me if the 40% still comes with free in-home setup
- **No category entry points** — the hamburger nav is hidden behind a tap; I can't see "Sofas," "Sectionals," or "Living Room" without an extra step
- **"For a limited time" is intentionally vague** — I want a date. Does this end July 4th? July 7th? The uncertainty is mildly annoying for a deliberate buyer like me
- **No featured products** — if you're running a 40%-off sale, show me one sofa with the before/after price. The sale landing page is one tap away but this hero does none of that pre-sell work

## Recommendations

- **Add a price anchor to the hero** — something like "Sven Sofa from $999 → $799" would convert me 10x faster than "wide selection of beautiful styles"
- **Surface the sale end date** — swap "for a limited time" with "Ends July 7" to create real urgency without feeling manipulative
- **Add one category shortcut strip below the hero** — a horizontal scroll of Sofas / Sectionals / Dining / Outdoor would let me bypass the hamburger and land exactly where I want within two taps
- **Add a single delivery trust line below the CTA** — "Free white-glove delivery on orders over $999" would close a meaningful chunk of hesitation for buyers like me who read the FAQ before clicking anything
