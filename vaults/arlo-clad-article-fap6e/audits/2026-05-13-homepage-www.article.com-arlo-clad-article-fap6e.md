# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Article is doing exactly what I'd hope to see when I open their app in mid-May: a clean, on-brand Memorial Day sale push with real numbers and a familiar Scandi-warm aesthetic. The palette is sage-and-cream, the typography is confident, and that dining table shot below the hero feels like something I'd actually post. What it's pushing is unmistakably a sale event — not a new collection, not a lifestyle story — and for someone who's been waiting for the "right moment" to pull the trigger on a new piece, that's a meaningful signal.

## What's working

- **The sale banner + hero double-tap**: The sticky top bar and the full-bleed hero both say "Memorial Day Sale / up to 40% off" — I can't miss it. As someone who waits months for a legit promo, this two-touch signal is exactly right.
- **The sage green palette**: It's seasonal, it's premium, and it feels like Article rather than a generic retailer. I'd recognize this colorway in a scroll-by.
- **The dining table lifestyle shot**: Even partially visible, that warm Scandi table with the fruit bowl and the slatted-wall backdrop is the exact energy I'd repost. It makes me want to see what else is in the sale.
- **Clean search bar placement**: High tech comfort, I appreciate that search is prominent and functional-looking right below the nav. One tap and I'm hunting for Sven variants.

## What's weak

- **"Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more"** — Living room / sofas is buried in "and more." If I came here specifically thinking about the Sven or any sectional, this subtext is doing nothing for me. My category is invisible.
- **No delivery/white-glove mention**: I read every white-glove delivery FAQ before buying. There's zero signal about delivery quality anywhere above the fold. For a $1,500+ sofa purchase, that's a gap.
- **"SHOP NOW" goes nowhere specific**: The CTA is generic. No category destination, no "Shop Sofas," nothing that narrows the funnel for someone who already knows what they want.
- **"For a limited time" is limp urgency**: No end date, no countdown. I've seen Memorial Day sales "for a limited time" stretch into June. This doesn't move me.

## Recommendations

- **Name the sofa/living room category in the hero subtext**: Change "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more" to include "Sofas & Sectionals" explicitly — it's likely the highest-AOV category and the one millennial furniture obsessives actually buy.
- **Add a delivery trust snippet below the CTA**: A single line — "Free white-glove delivery on orders over $999" — would be conversion-relevant information that costs zero screen space and speaks directly to how I shop.
- **Replace "SHOP NOW" with a sale-specific CTA**: "Shop the Sale" or "See 40% Off Picks" is still simple but feels less like a WordPress placeholder and more like a brand that knows what it's selling.
- **Drop a credible deadline**: "Sale ends May 26" would immediately make this feel more urgent and trustworthy — I'd screenshot it, come back, and actually buy before the date.
