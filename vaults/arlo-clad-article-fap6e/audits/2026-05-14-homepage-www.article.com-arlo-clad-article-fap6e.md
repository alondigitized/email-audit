# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Article is running a Memorial Day sale and they are not being subtle about it — giant "SALE" in dark green on sage, hard to miss. The brand reads premium DTC furniture, the kind of brand I actually bookmark and watch for moments like this. But the hero forgets to mention the one thing I came here for: a sofa.

## What's working

- **40% off Memorial Day Sale:** I've been patiently watching Article for a reason to pull the trigger. I normally wait for a 10%-off promo — so 40% is a code-red, open-the-wallet moment for me.
- **Visual hierarchy:** The SALE treatment in that sage-green field is clean and punchy. I see it, I read it, I tap it. Zero ambiguity.
- **Dismissible sticky banner:** Reinforces the offer without holding me hostage. I can close it — which makes me feel respected, not manipulated.
- **Partial dining table visible below hero:** What I can see confirms the aesthetic I expect from Article — warm wood, clean lines, mid-century-adjacent. I'm in the right place.
- **Timing is perfect:** Memorial Day in mid-May is exactly when I'd expect a serious furniture promo. Not stale, not premature.

## What's weak

- **No sofa or living room callout:** The subtext explicitly names "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom" — but nothing about sofas or living room, which is the category Article is actually known for and the reason I came. Feels like an editorial oversight that leaves money on the table.
- **"SHOP NOW" sends me to a generic sale page:** More friction between me and what I actually want. I shouldn't have to navigate post-tap to get to sofas.
- **No white-glove or free delivery mention:** I read every delivery FAQ before I buy furniture. Article does offer white-glove delivery — not calling it out here is a missed conversion signal at exactly the moment I'm considering a purchase.
- **"For a limited time" is not urgency:** This is the laziest possible deadline. Give me a date. "Ends May 27." Without that I'll close the tab and revisit next week, maybe never.
- **Zero social proof above the fold:** No star ratings, no review count, no "as seen in" badge. I'm cross-shopping Article against Joybird and Inside Weather right now — peer validation matters.

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite the hero subtext to lead with sofas:** Swap "Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom, and more" for "Sofas, Sectionals, Dining, Outdoor — up to 40% off." Lead with the high-AOV, high-intent category.
- **Add a credible deadline:** "Ends May 27" or a countdown clock. "For a limited time" is not a deadline — it's a hedge. I need something that makes me act today.
- **Add a white-glove delivery callout to the hero:** One line below the CTA — "Free white-glove delivery on orders over $X." That single line converts furniture shoppers who are nervous about logistics.
- **Add category quick-nav pills below the hero:** Sofas · Dining · Outdoor. One tap to my category instead of dumping everyone into a general sale browse experience.
