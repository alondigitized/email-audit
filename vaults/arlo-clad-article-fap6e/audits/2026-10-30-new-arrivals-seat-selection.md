# New arrivals: Seat selection
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy, well-shot new-arrivals send for dining seating. It's organized as three product stories: dining chairs (Vila leather, Vila fabric, Ulka), the Malmo modular banquette, and the Torme bench. Each story has a lifestyle hero, a row of color swatches labeled "NEW", one line of copy and a mustard pill CTA. The warm oak-and-terracotta photography is consistent and appetizing, and the structure is easy to scan.
- There is no offer anywhere. The only commercial hook is the "Shop worry free / 30-day returns" box near the bottom. It's a catalog send, not a reason to act today. Compared with the 2026-10-09 "Up to 50% off clearance styles" send (7/10), this has no urgency. It also follows the same editorial, no-discount pattern as 10-23 "Give guests a cozy stay" and 10-27 "Steal some style inspo", both 6/10. It's the third no-price send in a week, and the cadence is starting to blur.
- Verdict: a competent 6. The creative is good and the CTAs are product-specific, but a subscriber has no reason to click now rather than later.

## What's working

- Photography is warm and cohesive, and the lifestyle heroes sell the dining-room context well.
- Three clear modules with identical structure (hero, swatches, copy, pill CTA) make the email easy to scan.
- Swatch rows with "NEW" labels show color and material range without making the reader click.
- CTAs name the product ("SHOP DINING CHAIRS", "SHOP MALMO", "SHOP TORME") rather than saying "Discover".
- The "Shop worry free" block with a customer quote and "Learn about 30-day returns" gives social proof and reduces purchase risk.

## What's weak

- There is no price, offer, or member benefit, and no deadline. Nothing creates urgency.
- Swatch products show no prices or ratings, so shoppers can't judge fit or budget before clicking.
- Headline text sits directly over the busy photos ("Easy updates.", "In your corner.", "Space savers."). Contrast is weak, especially on the light-toned images.
- The email is long, with three full hero images, and the "Seat selection." headline competes with the pill CTA at the top.
- Headlines like "Easy updates." and "In your corner." don't say what the product is, so the story depends on the swatches below.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete hook, such as "Free shipping on seating" or a starting price ("Chairs from $X"). This one change would lift click intent the most.
- 2. Put prices or "from $" under each swatch row, and add star ratings where they exist.
- 3. Add a solid or semi-transparent band behind the headline text on the hero photos to fix legibility.
- 4. Cut the email to the two strongest stories, or add a single top-of-email "Shop all new seating" CTA, to shorten scroll depth.
- 5. Rewrite the headlines to name the product ("New dining chairs, 4 finishes").

## Technical Audit

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images.** Their src values are:
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Z-r0d7wE-eTzR-yq7xqeGg~~/AAQVbhA~/sViLi4PE...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/dqdR9TIgcw1Q...` (reported as "26")
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/ENk1BRaMFzsmAWk7URd-Kg~~/AAQVbhA~/lMHCaTpA...`

  Images with no alt attribute are announced by screen readers by filename or URL. They also render as blank space when images are blocked.
- **Invalid dark-mode CSS in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma in the selector list. Browsers drop the whole rule, so heading and paragraph colors won't invert.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a stray `;` before `!important`. The color applies, but without `!important`, so inline link colors may override it.
  - The block's closing `}` is missing before `</style>`. Parsers usually recover, but strict clients may not.
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- The `.light-img` rule is paired with a `.dark-img` toggle. This is not an error, but the dark-image variants depend on `prefers-color-scheme` support, which is limited in some clients.
