- Clear numbered "3 Steps" structure gives the email a scannable narrative instead of a wall of products.

- Consistent visual rhythm — hero image → product tiles → rationale copy → CTA button — repeated cleanly across all three sections.

## What's weak

- No offer or discount anywhere — after a discount-fatigue streak, this swings fully to zero incentive to act now.
- No urgency or timeliness signal — this could run in any month, there's nothing tying it to a current season or moment.
- Subject and hero copy are generic home-content, not tied to any visible segment, loyalty tier, or past purchase.

- CTA copy is repetitive and generic across sections ("Shop Entryway Storage," "Shop Shelving," "Shop Benches") rather than product-specific.
