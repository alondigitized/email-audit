## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a dark-mode implementation. That implementation has malformed CSS, and 3 images lack alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the AgentMail relay did not expose the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so footer and body findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Wrapped tracking URLs.** Links go through redirect domains, not the sender's own domain: `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/…~~/AAQVbhA~/…` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/…`. These are opaque, so final destinations and UTM params can't be read from source.
- **Tracker URLs as image sources.** Two images use `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/…` URLs as `src` (`e7ZKa3bz…`, `--gcK-d3XP…`). That is the same path pattern as the click-tracking links. Confirm that they serve image bytes and not a redirect, which would show broken images.
- **Plain-text part is mostly URLs.** 2335 of 3300 characters (about 71%) are URLs, which is the QA warning. The cause is the long tracking-wrapped links.
- **Link probing.** 12 material links were probed and 17 skipped, with no failures reported. The skipped links, which are mostly footer and social, are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images**, per QA:
  - `src=https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/539UOh6r…`
  - `src=https://links.email.article.com/s/eo/RE4fGhjW…`, whose flagged identifier is the string `26:`
  - `src=https://post.spmailtechnol.com/q/uKenj0gU…`
- **Empty `<title></title>`.**
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma. Browsers should drop the whole rule, so the heading and paragraph overrides won't apply.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has `!important` outside the declaration, so it is invalid. The link color override is lost, and `a` gets no valid `!important`.
  - The `@media` block is never closed. The `[data-ogsc]` block that follows ends with an extra `}}` and has no opening `@media`.
  - Clients that don't forgive this parse error may mis-render dark mode. That risk is highest where paired `.light-img` and `.dark-img` swaps are used: if `display:none!important` on `.light-img` is dropped, both images could show.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` were present. The truncated portion was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay.
- **Sender alignment.** The From domain is `email.article.com`, and the QA data gives no evidence of misalignment.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address.** The unsubscribe link is always probed per QA notes, and no failure was reported. The physical address was not visible in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. UTM parameters and landing URLs are hidden behind redirect wrappers, and the visible source is truncated. No probe failures or redirect errors were reported for the 12 links tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{…}` to `h1,h2,p{…}`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Close the `@media` block properly and remove the stray `}`.
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 3 flagged images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images or tracking pixels.
3. Populate `<title>`, for example with the subject line.
4. Verify that the two image `src` URLs on `post.spmailtechnol.com` return image content and not a redirect.
5. Check the raw message with a direct SMTP capture, not the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check UTMs, the footer address and the 17 skipped links.
7. Consider a shorter or branded click-tracking domain to reduce the URL share of the plain-text part (about 71%).
