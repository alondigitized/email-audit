# Up to 50% off clearance styles
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the same clearance email Article sent on 2026-10-05 ("Clearance Event: Get up to 50% off"), with a new subject line and a four-tile category grid added. The coral-pink gradient hero and the huge black "Up to 50% off." headline give a clear message at a glance. The white SHOP NOW pill sits right under the copy.
- The email falls apart below the hero. The tiles are dim, desaturated photos with white "Shop Living Room / Bedroom / Decor / Outdoor" overlays. They show no product, no price, and no clearance cue. The coral strip under them repeats "Last-chance styles up to 50% off" and adds a second "Clearance Event" link. That is the third restatement of the offer in one scroll. Nothing says when the sale ends, so "before they're gone" is the only urgency.
- Judgement: a competent, readable promo. It is also a near-repeat of a send from four days ago, and it gives a subscriber no new reason to act. After the editorial run on 09-15, 09-18, 09-23, 09-29 and 10-02, the brand is back on discount mode. This version is no more persuasive than the 10-05 one.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is strong. The oversized "Up to 50% off." leads, with "Fall Clearance" above it, and the eye lands on the offer first.
- The saturated coral gradient stands out in an inbox and ties to the coral footer strip and logo.
- The 30-Day Satisfaction Guarantee in the hero copy reduces purchase risk.
- The 2x2 category grid lets a shopper jump straight to a room. Each tile has a verb plus a noun.
- The layout has no broken images, overlaps or clipped text, and it reads cleanly on mobile.

## What's weak

- The email repeats the 10-05 send's hero, offer and CTA almost exactly, so it feels like the same promo again.
- The tiles show no product detail, no sale price and no "clearance" badge. The dim photos with overlay text look like navigation, not deals.
- The offer has no deadline or end date, so "before they're gone" is soft urgency.
- The offer appears three times (headline, body copy, coral strip) while no single product is featured.
- The footer's "Explore What's New" and "Shop Best Sellers" buttons lead away from the clearance goal. The footer also has a lot of dark space for little content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic grid with 4 to 6 real clearance products. Each should show a was/now price and a "% off" badge. This is the biggest lift, because it turns a mood board into a deal.
- 2. Add a real deadline or inventory signal, such as "Ends Sunday" or "Selected styles while supplies last," and place it in the hero.
- 3. Don't re-send the 10-05 creative. Rotate the hero imagery or the angle, for example "Last chance on fall clearance."
- 4. Point the CTA somewhere specific, such as "Shop clearance sofas," and drop the redundant coral strip.
- 5. Remove the "What's New" and "Best Sellers" buttons from a clearance send, or move them below a stronger clearance block.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a standard responsive XHTML table layout with `lang="en"` and dark-mode support. The main code-level defects are malformed dark-mode CSS, an empty `<title>`, and 3 images flagged for missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because of the relay, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in ESP redirect domains: `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...` and `links.email.article.com/s/eo/...`. Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- Only 1 of 12 material links was probed (11 skipped, per QA). Link health is largely **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- The QA run found 0 broken links among the links it probed.
- Two tracking-wrapper domains are in use (`spmailtechnol.com` and `email.article.com`). If they are different ESP layers, that is an extra redirect hop and a point of failure. This is only an observation; I can't confirm it from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/q8qv9_.../Pep5WB-f6VFg6YG...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/1g5oIP1t...` (identifier "25")
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/Nb1n-IQ_.../IodUesqu3TSk...`
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this hurts accessibility and the "view in browser" tab label.
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS:**
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma that leaves an empty selector. Strict parsers may drop the whole rule, so headings and paragraphs may not get the dark-mode color.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `!important`. It is invalid, so the declaration is ignored and the link color is not forced.
  - The first `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark){...}` block ends at `a .link{color:#fff!important}</style>` with no closing brace. The `[data-ogsc]` block that follows ends with `}}`, so it carries a stray extra brace. Browsers usually recover, but behavior is parser-dependent.
- **Dark-mode risk:** `a` is set to `#fff` in dark mode while `.left-sidebar a{color:#85ab70}` and `.right-sidebar a{color:#70bbd9}` use fixed colors. Because the `a` rule is invalid, that override may not apply.
- `.dark-img` / `.light-img` swaps depend on `display:none!important`. This is a common Gmail/Outlook pitfall, but I can't confirm a failure from the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA flags that the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The From domain, `email.article.com`, is a subdomain, so alignment can't be verified here.
- An unsubscribe link was included in the probed set, but the QA output doesn't show its result. The footer's physical address and unsubscribe text are outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. UTM parameters are hidden behind redirect wrappers. 11 of 12 links weren't resolved, so landing-page alignment with the "up to 50% off clearance" subject couldn't be checked.

2. Add a meaningful `<title>`, such as the subject line.
