# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Article is running a Memorial Day Sale and putting it front-and-center — sage green, clean type, 40% off across the whole house. As someone who literally cross-shops Article, Joybird, and Inside Weather while waiting for the one sale per year that actually makes me move, this is the right message at the right time. The brand looks premium but approachable: Scandinavian-adjacent, no clutter, exactly the vibe I'd expect from the Sven sofa people.

## What's working

- **The 40% figure** — Article almost never runs real sales. Seeing "up to 40%" in the hero stops me cold. That's not a 10%-off email code; that's actually news.
- **Timing** — Memorial Day in mid-May is exactly when I'm budgeting summer furniture additions. This page feels current, not lazy.
- **Sage green hero background** — the color is on-brand and signals taste, not clearance-rack desperation. This could've easily been a red-and-yellow flash sale; it isn't.
- **Clean mobile nav** — search bar + hamburger + cart icons is all I need. Not cluttered. I can find my way around.
- **Partially visible dining table lifestyle shot** — even that sliver of a styled room at the bottom made me want to scroll to see the full image.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — I'm a one-big-piece-a-year buyer; I want "Shop Sofas" or "Shop Living Room." Send me somewhere specific, not to a wall of sale items.
- **No social proof above the fold** — I'm cross-shopping three brands. A star rating, review count, or even "as seen in" badge would help me justify staying here vs. clicking over to Joybird.
- **"Tons of styles" is sloppy copy** — Article's brand voice is usually tight and elevated. "Tons of styles" reads like a department store flyer, not a brand I'd trust with a $2,000 sofa.
- **No white-glove delivery callout** — I read every delivery FAQ before buying furniture. A quick "free white-glove delivery" line in the hero would close a ton of purchase anxiety for people like me.
- **No urgency specificity** — "for a limited time" (banner) is not a deadline. "Ends May 26" would actually make me act this week.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop the Sale → Sofas" or add two category pills** (Sofas, Dining) directly below the CTA so I can self-select in one tap — I don't want to scroll a generic sale grid.
- **Add one line of social proof under the CTA**: "Rated 4.7 stars by 12,000+ customers" or similar — this is above-the-fold real estate that directly counters Joybird's review advantage.
- **Replace "for a limited time" with a specific end date** in the sticky banner — "Ends May 26" creates real urgency without feeling manufactured.
- **Add "Includes white-glove delivery on orders over $X"** as a sub-line under the hero body copy — one line, huge purchase-barrier reduction for high-AOV furniture buyers.
