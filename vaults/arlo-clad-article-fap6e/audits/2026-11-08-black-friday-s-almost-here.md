# Black Friday’s almost here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday teaser, not an offer. The hero is a bold black-and-white "BLACK FRIDAY SALE COMING SOON" block. The one real number, "up to 40% off furniture," sits in small white text on a black band below it. The body then asks the reader to save favorites to a wishlist so they're "first to know" when items go on sale.
- The wishlist idea is smart: it builds intent and a retargeting pool before the sale. But the execution is weak. The product visuals are dimmed phone screenshots of the Article site with "ADD TO YOUR FAVORITES" callouts, so they read as a tutorial and not as something to buy. The CTA, "CREATE WISHLIST," asks for effort and gives nothing back. The email is clean and uncluttered, but it's low on urgency and low on product desire.
- Compared with the Nov 6 storage UGC send and the Nov 3 "Oh, hi, new favorites" catalog, this is a clear change of mode, from inspiration to pre-sale. That fits the season. A subscriber who got six no-discount sends in a row will take "almost here" as a hint to wait for the sale and not buy now.

## What's working

- The hero is high-contrast and unmistakable. The eye lands on "SALE" immediately.
- "Up to 40% off furniture for every room" gives a concrete, credible offer figure.
- The wishlist-to-be-notified mechanic is a sensible early-access play before the sale.
- A single coral CTA pill stands out against the white section.
- The footer trust row (30-day guarantee, shipping, help) reassures buyers.

## What's weak

- The 40% figure is buried in small text under the hero. The headline says only "coming soon."
- The product screenshots are greyed out, tiny and UI-heavy. They look disabled and show no clear hero product.
- "CREATE WISHLIST" is a high-friction ask. It gives no date, no perk and no reason to act today.
- There is no sale date or countdown, so "almost here" has no credible deadline.
- "EXPLORE WHAT'S NEW" and "SHOP BEST SELLERS" are demoted to dark footer buttons. They're the only direct path to shop, and they're easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the date and the offer in the hero: "Up to 40% off. Starts Nov 20." Make the number the headline, not the subtext.
- 2. Replace the dimmed app screenshots with bright, full-bleed product shots of the bed and chair, with price and star rating, and link each to its product page.
- 3. Give the wishlist a reward: "Save 3 favorites, get early access to the sale." Retitle the CTA "Save my favorites" or "Get early access."
- 4. Move "Shop best sellers" up beside the main CTA as a secondary button.
- 5. Drop the tutorial framing and show real social proof, such as the 5.0 rating, next to the products.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is sound (XHTML doctype, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, light/dark `color-scheme`). The confirmed issues are 3 images without alt text, malformed dark-mode CSS, and an empty `<title>`. The missing compliance and authentication headers are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so I can't confirm them from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA probe covered only 12 material links and skipped 11, so most links are unverified. No broken links were reported among the ones probed.
- Two different click-tracking domains appear:
  - `links.email.article.com` is a branded tracking domain.
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/...` is an unbranded, shared ESP tracking domain. It serves at least 2 of the 3 flagged images (`.../q/7aHgFUu1pTI5VRMfPQcJkA~~/AAQVbhA~/...` and `.../q/3RXmDWMO5ba7-fK7d3dRbQ~~/AAQVbhA~/...`).
  - Mixing a shared domain with a branded one can hurt reputation and spam-filter alignment. This is low severity.
- I can't confirm whether those `post.spmailtechnol.com` URLs are tracking pixels or content images. The source is truncated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, confirmed by QA):**
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/7aHgFUu1pTI5VRMfPQcJkA~~/...`
  - `links.email.article.com/s/eo/J-c19flM7-...`
  - `post.spmailtechnol.com/q/3RXmDWMO5ba7-fK7d3dRbQ~~/...`
  
  If any of these are content images, they need descriptive alt text. If they are pixels, they need `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Malformed dark-mode CSS** in the `@media(prefers-color-scheme:dark)` block:
  - `h1,h2,p,{color:#fff!important}` has a trailing comma that leaves an empty selector. Browsers drop the whole rule, so heading and paragraph colors are not forced in dark mode.
  - `a{color:#fff;!important}` has a misplaced `!important`. It is parsed as an invalid declaration, so the `a` color is not forced as intended.
  - The `@media` block ends at `a .link{color:#fff!important}` with no closing `}` before `</style>`. Most clients auto-close it, but stricter parsers may not.
- The `[data-ogsc]` Outlook.com rules are written correctly. The Gmail/Apple dark-mode path is the one that is broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`), and QA flagged none.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- The QA run reported an unsubscribe link and probed it, with no failure. The physical address in the footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't verify it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. All links go through redirect trackers (`links.email.article.com`, `post.spmailtechnol.com`), so final destinations and UTM parameters are not visible in the source or the QA data. Only 1 of the 12 material links was probed. Landing-page alignment can't be assessed from this data.

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 2 content-image candidates, and `alt=""` to any tracking pixels. Start with the three flagged URLs.
2. Fix the dark-mode CSS:
   - Change `h1,h2,p,{` to `h1,h2,p{`.
   - Change `a{color:#fff;!important}` to `a{color:#fff!important}`.
   - Close the `@media` block.
3. Populate `<title>` with the subject line.
4. Move the `post.spmailtechnol.com` tracking onto the branded `links.email.article.com`, or a similar branded domain.
