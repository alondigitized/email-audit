# Homepage snapshot · www.article.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Article is leading hard with Memorial Day and not pretending otherwise — the entire above-the-fold experience is one big "SALE" card with a sage-green background and dark green type. For me, a guy who has been eyeing the Sven for six months and would absolutely time a purchase to a 40%-off window, this is the right message at the right moment. The brand looks clean, Scandinavian-adjacent, confident — exactly what I expect from them.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner + hero alignment** — the sticky top strip and the full-bleed hero say the same thing, which reinforces the message without feeling redundant
- **Color palette** — sage green with dark green type feels intentional and seasonal, not like a templated promo skin slapped on
- **"Outdoor, Dining Room, Bedroom"** — listing the categories in the subhead gives me a mental map of what's in the sale before I tap
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — high contrast, centered, impossible to miss; the CTA does its job
- **Dining room lifestyle photo** — that warm wood table with slatted chairs is exactly the kind of aspirational-but-achievable shot that makes me want to keep scrolling

## What's weak

- **"Up to 40% off for a limited time"** — "limited time" is doing no work here; I want a date (Memorial Day is Monday, give me the deadline)
- **No sofa / living room above the fold** — Article's brand identity lives in sofas and sectionals; leading with a generic multi-category sale buries the hero product
- **Zero trust signals above the fold** — no free-shipping threshold, no white-glove delivery mention, no return window; for a high-AOV furniture brand this is a miss
- **No personalization layer** — I've definitely been to article.com before; seeing the Sven or a "your saved items" nudge would make this feel targeted instead of broadcast
- **Nav is pure hamburger** — one tap gets me nowhere specific; I have to dig to find sofas

## Recommendations

- **Add a deadline to the urgency copy** — swap "for a limited time" for "Ends Monday, May 26" so the Memorial Day window feels real and countdownable
- **Add a second CTA tile below the hero** — "Shop Living Room" alongside "Shop Outdoor" gives sofa shoppers a direct path without touching the hero layout
- **Surface the free-shipping / white-glove threshold** — a single line ("Free white-glove delivery on orders $999+") somewhere in the first screen would immediately earn trust from high-AOV shoppers like me
- **Swap the dining room lifestyle photo for a sofa lifestyle shot** — Article's couches are the brand; a Sven or Timber sectional in a warm-lit living room would resonate with the largest segment of intent buyers
