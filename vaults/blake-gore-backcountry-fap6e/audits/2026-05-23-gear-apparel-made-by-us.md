# Gear & apparel made by us 🐐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Backcountry's private-label product showcase — a brand-editorial email spotlighting their own-made gear lines (Apparel, Baselayers, Insulation, Snow Outerwear, Travel Gear). The 🐐 emoji signals brand identity; the goat is Backcountry's mascot. The creative is clean, photography is strong, and the editorial voice lands. But the timing is terrible: this drops on the same day as "More brands on sale! 👀" — making it the eighth send in four days — and the body content has nothing to do with the Memorial Day sale that the top banner is actively promoting.
- The structural problem: a welcome-style 15% discount offer is buried at the very bottom of a long scroll, after five stacked collection categories. If the goal is to drive purchases of Backcountry-branded gear, leading with "Bestsellers Made By Us" and hiding the price incentive three screens down is backwards. The hero should be the deal; the collections are the supporting cast.
- The cadence issue is real. After May 21's smart editorial pivot ("Gear questions? We're here to guide you!" — a 9/10 send) the brand has regressed to promotional volume. A second May 23 send undercuts whatever list recovery the May 21 editorial earned.

## What's working

- **Photography is sharp and on-brand.** The hero and per-category lifestyle images are editorial quality — models in credible outdoor contexts, not studio white-backgrounds.
- **"Bestsellers Made By Us" framing is credible.** The copy connecting "Gearhead Experts who actually use this gear" to product development is a genuine brand differentiator.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs.** Black-and-white structure, consistent typography, images load cleanly, no overlapping elements.
- **Goat emoji in subject is a brand-consistent touch.** Subscribers who know the brand will clock it immediately.
- **15% off offer is present**, even if poorly placed — a concrete price reduction exists somewhere in the email.

## What's weak

- **15% off is buried at the bottom.** After five stacked category blocks, the incentive that might actually drive a click is invisible on first scroll. The welcome-framing ("To Get the Experience Rolling") reads like an onboarding email dropped into a promotional blast — tone-deaf if this subscriber has already purchased.
- **Snow Outerwear in late May is off-season for most of the list.** Memorial Day weekend is camping/hiking/trail running territory, not insulation shopping.
- **The Memorial Day banner and the email body are completely disconnected.** The banner advertises 30% off sale brands; the email body promotes full-price Backcountry-branded gear. These two messages fight each other.
- **"SHOP NOW" everywhere kills specificity.** Five identical CTAs with no differentiated copy make every collection feel equal — and therefore uncompelling.
- **Eighth email in four days.** Cadence fatigue is real, and this send doesn't justify a slot in an already-crowded inbox week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the email structure: lead with the 15% offer, then show the collection grid below it.** "Made by us, 15% off for you" as a hero sells the same content with a reason to click.
- 2. **Cut Snow Outerwear from this send.** Replace it with Trail Running or Camping — categories that feel right for Memorial Day weekend.
- 3. **Give each CTA a specific label.** "Shop Baselayers →" beats "SHOP NOW →" every time. Five distinct labels = five distinct reasons to click.
- 4. **Fix the welcome-copy mismatch in the 15% block.** "To Get the Experience Rolling" sounds like a first-order email. If this is a retention offer, the copy needs to match ("Your Backcountry Crew Discount" or similar).
- 5. **Don't fire two sends on the same day during a promo week.** Batch this email to a standalone send day when no sale campaign is competing for attention.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our gear. Our people. 15% off this weekend.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Built by us, for exactly what you do 🐐`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backcountry-made apparel and gear — plus a discount that's actually yours to use.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The collections our Gearheads reach for first — and 15% off to try them.`
