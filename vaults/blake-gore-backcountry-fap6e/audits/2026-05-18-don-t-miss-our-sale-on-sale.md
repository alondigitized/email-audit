- The problem is three-fold. First, "Don't miss our sale on sale" is the weakest line in the email — cryptic wordplay that fails to communicate any offer value and buries the lede completely. ## What's weak

- Sending the same clearance offer twice in one day (this + "ends today") creates fatigue and dilutes urgency for both.
- No product images in the body — the personal format works, but even one "I'm packing this" item would anchor the click.

- 1. **Rewrite the subject** to surface the actual offer — "sale on sale" communicates nothing actionable to a subscriber scanning the inbox.
- 2. **Add one product image** — Alex packing for Canyonlands, with a specific gear recommendation pulled from clearance or the Memorial Day sale; turns a narrative into a conversion.

- 4. **Coordinate same-day sends** — don't run this and the "clearance ends today" blast in the same send window; pick one or stagger them by several hours with different product angles.
- 5. **Pull one specific product name into the body copy** — "I just snagged this [pack] at clearance pricing" makes the personal email feel genuinely personal, not a narrative wrapper around a generic offer.
