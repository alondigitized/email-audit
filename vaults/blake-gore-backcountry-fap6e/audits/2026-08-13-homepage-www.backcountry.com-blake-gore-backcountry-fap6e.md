# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- First thing on my screen is a full-bleed email popup shouting "Now Up To 60% Off" over a campsite photo — before I've even seen the site. Feels like a gear liquidation event dressed up as a semi-annual sale, which, fine, I've bought plenty at Backcountry's F&F windows before, but gating the whole homepage behind an email capture when I probably already have an account is a rough way to say hello to a repeat customer.

## What's working

- **"Up To 60% Off Summer Semi-Annual Sale"** — a real, specific number, not "up to X% off select styles" mush. That's the kind of drop I'd actually dig through for a discontinued cam set or last year's touring boots.
- **Stacked offer** ("Plus, get 15% off full-price items on your next purchase") — smart, gives me a reason to come back even if the sale rack doesn't have my size.
- **Hard deadline** — "Sale ends 8/22" with a real date, not vague urgency copy.
- **Summit Club / Member Perks teased in the header** — I know that program, and seeing it above the fold before I even click through is a decent nudge.

## What's weak

- **The modal is blocking literally everything** — nav, hero, categories, search. I can't even get to Men's or Activities without dismissing it first.
- **Camping-couple hero image** — nothing wrong with it, but it's not talking to a trad climber or ski-tourer. Could be a stock photo for any outdoor retailer.
- **No product visible anywhere** — I can't tell if this sale touches hardgoods, softshells, skis, or just clearance tents. As someone who wants to know if my brands are in it before I click, that's friction.
- **Forced email capture for a "Sign Up Now" that's redundant with "Sign In For Member Perks"** — if I'm already a member, this popup is just noise between me and the site.

## Recommendations

- Swap the generic tent-and-mountains hero for a climbing/ski-touring lifestyle shot — someone racking cams or skinning up a bootpack — to actually hook the technical-gear crowd.
- Make the modal dismiss-and-remember for logged-in / cookie-recognized returning users instead of blasting everyone with the email gate.
- Add one line of category proof inside the modal or right behind it — "Shop Skis, Climbing, Backpacks" — so I know the 60% touches my stuff before I commit to closing the popup.
- Surface the Summit Club member discount tier directly in the promo copy ("Members save an extra X%") since that's the lever that gets a guy like me to actually convert instead of just window shopping.
