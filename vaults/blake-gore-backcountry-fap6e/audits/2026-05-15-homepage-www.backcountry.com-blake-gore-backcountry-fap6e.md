# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Bro. I opened Backcountry and got a bouncer at the door. The entire viewport is a bot-check wall — "Let's confirm you are human" in orange, a "Begin" button, and a language dropdown. No hero, no nav, no gear, no deals. This isn't a homepage experience, it's a fire drill. I came here to look at climbing harnesses, not prove my humanity to a server in Phoenix.

## What's working

- The "Begin" button renders cleanly in Backcountry's orange and has a clear chevron — at least the brand color is recognizable
- Language selector dropdown is present, suggesting some attempt at localization readiness

## What's weak

- The entire homepage experience is absent — I'm looking at a Cloudflare-style bot interstitial, not a store
- No Backcountry branding, logo, or wordmark visible — I could be anywhere
- "Let's confirm you are human" is cold, clinical, and zero-warmth for a brand that's supposed to feel like a stoked gear shop
- Zero product, category, or campaign signal — a brand that sells $600 climbing harnesses just served me a blank room
- A logged-in user (I have an account, I know my Gearhead's name) gets zero recognition — the bot wall doesn't even try to lean on my session cookie

## Recommendations

- **Add a Backcountry logo and minimal brand context to the bot-check page** — even "Hang tight, we'll get you to the good stuff" in the Backcountry voice costs nothing and keeps the brand warm during the gate
- **Fast-path recognized sessions past the CAPTCHA** — if I'm logged in with a valid cookie, don't wall me; the friction hits your best customers hardest
- **Replace the generic "Let's confirm you are human" headline with something on-brand** — "Quick check before you gear up" keeps the voice alive and reduces perceived hostility
- **Feed the CAPTCHA completion into a personalized landing** — after I pass the gate, drop me directly into my recent category (climbing) rather than a generic homepage reset
