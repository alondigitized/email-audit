# 20% off full-price items | Stock up for summer ☀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Backcountry email on June 6 alone promoting the same "20% off full-price items" offer — and at least the third in the relaunch arc that kicked off yesterday with "You're in. Early access granted." The prior arc ran five emails from May 29 through June 1. By any measure, this offer has been beaten to death. A subscriber who opened both emails today already knows the deal; this one adds nothing except a generic category-browse layout they've seen before.
- The structural problem is the dual-offer confusion: the hero screams 20% off full-price, but the bottom of the email pivots to "Summer Sale UP TO 40% OFF." Those are two different campaigns with different mechanics, and cramming both into one send with zero explanation makes neither feel special. If 40% is available somewhere, why is 20% the headline?
- The "3 DAYS ONLY!" urgency ticker at the top is the single strongest element — but it's wasted on a subject line ("Stock up for summer") that carries no clock at all.

## What's working

- The "3 DAYS ONLY!" ticker creates genuine deadline urgency that the hero section benefits from.
- Hero hierarchy is clean — "20% OFF" reads immediately on mobile with a visible SHOP NOW button.
- Category tile layout (Men's Apparel, Women's Apparel, Hike & Camp, Footwear, Bike) covers the full product surface without feeling cluttered.
- Brand logos (Osprey, Black Diamond, Cotopaxi, Nemo, La Sportiva) lend credibility and give brand-loyal shoppers a specific scan target.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout failures.

## What's weak

- Two competing promotions in one email (20% off full-price AND Summer Sale 40% off) with no explanation of the difference — the subscriber has to figure out which one applies to what.
- Subject has no urgency; "Stock up for summer" is soft filler when the email is time-bounded ("3 DAYS ONLY!").
- This is the second send of the day from this sender with effectively the same offer — cadence abuse at its most visible.
- "SHOP NOW" repeats across seven CTAs with zero copy variation — no specific verb, no category context.
- No personalization anywhere; this is a pure blast to the full list.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the deadline in the subject** — the ticker knows it's 3 days; the subject line should too. The single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Pick one offer per email** — remove the Summer Sale footer or give it its own send. Two discount tiers in one email train subscribers to ignore both.
- 3. **Differentiate "SHOP NOW" CTAs** — use "Shop Men's Apparel," "Shop Footwear," etc. Specificity lifts click rate on category tiles.
- 4. **Suppress the second same-day send** or at minimum change the creative significantly (e.g., make this the "here are the top picks from the sale" product spotlight rather than a second hero banner).
- 5. **Pull a featured product** into the hero — one specific "deal of the 3 days" item with the original price crossed out anchors the 20% claim concretely.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open-tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute**
- Element: `<img src="https://trk.b.backcountry.com/ss/o/u001.s4HNEC6Qw5Htp4aPAN5Gdw/4r9/VbQPIdyCTGSL2NFIn_PXEA/ho.gif">`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS selector typo — 9-column fluid grid broken on mobile**
```css
/* Line in <style> block: */
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
/*                          ↑ should be .col-9, not .col-8 */
```
`.md-fluid-row .col-9` is never targeted; `.md-fluid-row .col-8` gets the rule applied twice. Any 9-column `md-fluid-row` cell will not receive its responsive width.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none !important` applied globally**
```css
div, p, a, li, span, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none !important; }
```
This suppresses iOS automatic font-size correction on all elements unconditionally. Can cause small text to remain unreadably small on narrow viewports. Best practice is `100%` or targeting only the container, not a blanket `none`.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
- Not visible in the truncated source. Standard accessibility requirement for screen readers (WCAG 3.1.1). Should be `<html lang="en">`.

**Tracking pixel alt text**
- Confirmed by QA: `ho.gif` has no `alt` attribute. See §2 above.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text MIME part is 0 characters**
- QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. CAN-SPAM requires that multipart/alternative emails contain a readable plain-text body. A zero-length text part also triggers spam filters on major ISPs (Gmail, Outlook). This is the highest-severity finding.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected**
- Header not captured by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe` for bulk senders (>5k/day) as of February 2024. If Bluecore injects this at the MTA layer and the relay strips it, deliverability and compliance are both at risk. Verify raw header presence at SMTP level, not just relay.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected (RFC 8058)**
- One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy) depends on this header paired with `List-Unsubscribe`. Same caveat as above re: relay stripping.

- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown through the AgentMail relay. Cannot confirm sender authentication chain for `b.backcountry.com`. Should verify DKIM signature on `b.backcountry.com` subdomain is active and DMARC policy aligns.
