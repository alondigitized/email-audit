# Up to 70% off! 2 days only...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Backcountry is throwing everything at the wall in this Memorial Day finale — a "70% off flash sale" hero on top, the usual category grid, the big "SALE SALE SALE" Memorial Day block, three orange bundle bars, and a Summit Club exclusive at the bottom. As a piece of inbox real estate it's loud and on-brand, but it's the seventh-ish blast in a week and the message density has gone from "shop the sale" to "look at every offer we have at once."
- The "Up to 70% off, 2 days only" framing is the strongest hook this brand has run in days — it finally feels like a real terminal event rather than another "last chance extra 20%" rerun. The render is clean, the hero leads, and the bundle bars are a nice mid-funnel tactic.
- Judgement: solid 7. The offer is the most credible of the week, but the email is doing too many jobs and the "70% off" claim is undercut by an entire second campaign (Memorial Day Sale, bundles, Summit Club) layered underneath.

## What's working

- "70% OFF FLASH SALE" hero in stark black/yellow lands instantly — clear, urgent, unmistakable.
- "2 DAYS ONLY" ribbon at the very top reinforces the deadline before you even see the hero.
- Featured product tiles (North Face, Sherpa) with strikethrough pricing give the deal teeth.
- Three orange bundle bars (Hike, Camp, Sun & Swim) are smart cross-sell — categorized rather than generic.
- Summit Club section with concrete member perks ($20 cash back, free blanket at $500) gives loyalty subscribers a reason to keep reading.

## What's weak

- The email is two campaigns stitched together — 70% Flash Sale AND Memorial Day Sale — diluting the "2 days only" urgency.
- "Up to 70% off" with featured products at ~25% off feels like the headline number lives in the long tail, not the showcase.
- Five separate "SHOP NOW" buttons stack the page; the eye doesn't know which is THE button.
- Category grid (Men's, Women's, Hike & Camp, Ski, Bike, Travel) is generic homepage navigation jammed mid-funnel.
- Subject "Up to 70% off! 2 days only…" leans on "up to," which softens the hook for anyone subject-line-savvy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one campaign. Either this is the 70% Flash Sale or the Memorial Day Sale — running both halves the urgency of each. Lead with Flash, demote Memorial Day to a footer mention.
- 2. Show actual 70%-off products in the featured tiles. The current tiles look like 25–30% off; a buyer scanning for "70%" doesn't see proof.
- 3. Cut the redundant category grid. After the 7th send this week, the subscriber doesn't need a homepage map — they need the deal.
- 4. Consolidate to two CTAs: "Shop Flash Sale" up top, "Shop Member Deals" for Summit Club. Drop the middle three.
- 5. Tighten the subject — drop "Up to" and the ellipsis, hard-code the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `70% off ends Monday — flash sale is live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days. Up to 70% off. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Final markdowns before Memorial Day closes — North Face, Sherpa, and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Flash sale prices end Monday night. Summit members get an extra $20 back.`
