# 20% off full-price items is ON 🧗‍♀️🚴
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Backcountry is relaunching the exact same "20% off full-price items" offer that ran through a five-email arc from 5/29 through 6/1 — complete with the same partner brand logos (The North Face, On, Osprey, Lowa), the same SHOP NOW green button, and essentially the same hero copy. Subscribers who received the 10/10-scored "Ends today!" closer on 6/1 and the 9/10 "Ends tomorrow" on 5/31 are now being told the same deal is "ON" again. That's a credibility problem no execution polish can fix.
- The email itself renders cleanly and communicates the offer clearly. The "Ends 6/8" date stamp gives real urgency, the brand logo row adds trust, and the TOP CATEGORIES grid is well-organized for browse intent. But there's a dual-offer conflict baked into the layout: the sticky Summer Sale header shouts "Up to 40% off" while the hero announces "20% off full-price." A subscriber trying to understand what they're getting has to reconcile two different numbers.
- The net read: a well-produced email undermined by both a relaunched-dead offer and a mixed message about which sale is actually running. If a recipient opens it cold (new subscriber, or one who missed the June arc), it converts fine. For anyone with context, trust is the problem.

## What's working

- **Concrete offer in large hero text** — "20% OFF FULL-PRICE ITEMS" is unambiguous at a glance.
- **"Ends 6/8" date stamp** — visible deadline gives real urgency, not vague "limited time" language.
- **Partner brand logo row** — The North Face, On, Osprey, Lowa visible; signals top-tier selection and justifies clicking.
- **TOP CATEGORIES grid** — men's/women's apparel, footwear, hike & camp, and bike protection give clear browse paths for different intents.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **Relaunched "ended" offer** — this same deal explicitly closed on 6/1 with a "Final hours 🚨" send and an "Ends today 😨" send. Running it again 5 days later erodes urgency credibility for anyone on the list.
- **Dual-offer confusion** — Summer Sale "Up to 40% off" banner at top directly conflicts with "20% off full-price items" hero. Two different numbers, no reconciliation.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — the button doesn't answer what you're shopping for; "Shop Full-Price Gear" or "Shop The Sale" would have done the work.
- **"START HERE" products are unidentifiable** — product cards are too small at render size to read names or prices; they add visual length without purchase intent.
- **No personalization visible** — no name, no "based on your recent views," nothing to signal this send is targeted.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Acknowledge the relaunch** — "It's back for 3 more days" or "By popular demand" in the subhead diffuses the credibility problem and makes the re-open feel like a bonus, not a broken promise.
- 2. **Resolve the offer conflict** — pick one story per email. Either "20% off full-price items (up to 40% off select styles)" or kill the Summer Sale banner entirely. Two headlines dilute both.
- 3. **Upgrade the CTA copy** — replace "SHOP NOW" with something offer-specific: "Shop 20% Off Gear" or "Browse the Sale."
- 4. **Feature one real hero product** — a specific item (name, price, category) in the "START HERE" block would anchor click intent instead of the generic 3-up grid.
- 5. **Add a preheader that earns its pixel** — the header countdown text leaking into preview is wasted real estate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The 20% off sale is back — 3 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `By request: 20% off full-price gear, ends 6/8`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The North Face, On, Osprey + more — full-price, 20% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `We brought it back. 3 days, no exclusions on full-price gear.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed bug — CSS grid selector mismatch:**

```css
/* As written: */
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```

`.md-fluid-row .col-8` appears in **two** rules (once at `66%`, once at `74.25%`), while `.md-fluid-row .col-9` has **no mobile override** at all. On medium-breakpoint clients, any 9-column `md-fluid-row` cell will not reflow correctly, and 8-column cells will get unpredictable width depending on cascade order.

**Viewport meta missing `initial-scale`:**

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width">
```

Standard practice is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1`. Some older WebKit-based clients may render at non-1x zoom without it.

**External font dependency — single point of failure:**

```
https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/backcountry/backcountry_fonts_1769208036.css
```

If this CDN is unreachable (timeout, outage), the email falls back to system fonts with no inline fallback stack defined. Not a rendering failure, but a fidelity risk.
