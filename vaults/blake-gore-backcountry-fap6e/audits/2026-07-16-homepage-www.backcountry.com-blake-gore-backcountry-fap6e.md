# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Opened Backcountry on my phone and got served a straight-up bot wall — "Let's confirm you are human" with an orange "Begin" button and a language selector. Zero product, zero nav, zero personality. Whatever algorithm flagged my session, the brand just hit me with the equivalent of a velvet rope before I even got to see the store. For a site where I've dropped serious coin on Mammut and Dynafit over the years, this is a cold welcome.

## What's working

- **Page renders without visual errors** — the interstitial itself is clean: centered orange heading, readable body copy, clear "Begin" button. No broken UI.
- **Language selector is present** — minor, but accessible UX consideration.

## What's weak

- **The entire page is a dead end** — a logged-in (or at least very familiar) customer shouldn't be hitting a human-verification gate before seeing any product.
- **No brand identity whatsoever** — no logo, no nav, no Backcountry branding beyond the orange accent color. I wouldn't know whose CAPTCHA this is without the URL bar.
- **Zero shopping momentum** — any intent I had to check the new Black Diamond drops or see what's on sale for Friends + Family evaporates the second I hit this wall.
- **No explanation of why I'm here** — the copy says "protects your account and prevents spam" but gives me no context for why *I*, a repeat customer on my own phone, got flagged.
- **Forced friction before value delivery** — this is the definition of a dark pattern; I've been stopped before seeing a single product.

## Recommendations

- **Recognize returning sessions and suppress the bot check** — if I've got a logged-in cookie or any known session signal, skip the interstitial entirely. Don't gate your best customers.
- **If a check is mandatory, brand it** — put the Backcountry logo and a one-line reassurance ("We'll have you on the mountain in a sec") so it doesn't feel like a third-party phishing page.
- **Add a bypass path for account holders** — a "Sign in to continue" option instead of a generic CAPTCHA would recapture the shopping session faster and feel less adversarial.
- **Instrument this drop-off** — every session that hits this wall and doesn't complete "Begin" is a lost visit. If bounce rate here is high, the threshold sensitivity on the bot detector needs tuning.
