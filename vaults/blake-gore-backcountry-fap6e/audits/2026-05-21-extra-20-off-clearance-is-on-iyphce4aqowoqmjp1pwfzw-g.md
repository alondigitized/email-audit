# EXTRA 20% off clearance is ON 🔓
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Backcountry's third activation of the same "Extra 20% off clearance" mechanism in four days — a campaign that was explicitly declared over on May 18, then resurrected as a "VIP Early Access" on May 20, and now relaunched again with a countdown ticker and new urgency framing. The creative itself is clean and well-structured: a strong hero with brand logos, a curated "Save Even More" product grid, category tiles, a secondary "SALE SALE SALE" block layered in for breadth, and a Summit Club exclusive (free Coalatree blanket at $200+) that is genuinely new creative and adds loyalty value.
- The problem isn't the design — it's the trust erosion. The "2 DAYS ONLY!" countdown means nothing when the identical offer has already died and come back twice. A subscriber with any memory of the last week knows this deadline is theater. The Summit Club exclusive section is the only meaningfully new element, and it's buried at the bottom.
- The email functions as a respectable sale blast. It does not function as a credible urgency play.

## What's working

- **Hero is instantly readable** — "Extra 20% OFF CLEARANCE" with a forest backdrop, brand logos, and SHOP NOW lands above the fold on mobile with zero ambiguity.
- **Countdown ticker at top** creates real-time urgency framing, even if the offer history undercuts it.
- **Product grid in "Save Even More"** shows tangible, shoppable items (jacket, backpack, shoes) rather than abstract category promises.
- **Summit Club exclusive section** is the best new piece of creative in this send — a free Coalatree Adventure Blanket at $200+ is a concrete loyalty hook Backcountry hasn't used in this campaign before.
- **Category tiles (Men's Apparel, Women's Apparel, Hike & Camp, Ski Clothing, Bike, Travel)** give non-clearance browsers an exit ramp without confusing the primary offer.

## What's weak

- **Offer credibility is shot** — "2 DAYS ONLY!" on an offer that already "ended" May 18 and then came back May 20 reads as manufactured urgency to anyone paying attention.
- **"SALE SALE SALE" block competes with the hero** — the secondary 50%-off promo feels like a different email grafted in mid-scroll; it muddies the message hierarchy.
- **Summit Club exclusive is bottom-of-email dead zone** — the single most differentiated element for loyal customers is the last thing anyone sees before the footer.
- **No prices on product tiles** — the "Save Even More" grid shows items but no original-vs.-sale price comparison, which is the one thing that would make clearance shoppers click.
- **Brand logos row is present but unclickable-feeling** — logos with no CTA read as social proof decoration rather than direct pathways to brand-filtered clearance.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the Summit Club exclusive to just below the hero** — it's the only new offer in this send; leading with it (or placing it second) gives loyalty members a reason to click that isn't stale.
- 2. **Add original + sale price to every product tile** — "Was $189, now $113" on the product grid would convert clearance browsers immediately. "Save Even More" as a headline is vague.
- 3. **Kill or demote the "SALE SALE SALE" block** — this creative is doing the heavy lifting in a different email (the Memorial Day send from May 20); layering it here creates two competing offers with no clear winner.
- 4. **Reframe the countdown** — instead of a repeating ticker at the top that nobody trusts anymore, put a hard date: "Offer ends Sunday 11:59 PM MT" — specific, verifiable, harder to dismiss.
- 5. **Drop the brand logo rail or make it clickable** — either add filter links ("Shop The North Face Clearance →") or remove it to tighten the scroll experience.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Summit Club perk: free blanket + extra 20% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Coalatree blanket on $200+ — plus the 20% you've been waiting on.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Countdown's real this time. Ends Sunday night.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS selector collision — mobile `.md-fluid-row .col-8` defined twice**

In the `@media screen and (max-width: 600px)` block:
```css
/* Rule A — correct */
.fluid-row .col-8,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 66% !important; }

/* Rule B — collision: .md-fluid-row .col-8 is re-used as a proxy for .col-9 */
.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```
Rule B overrides Rule A for any `md-fluid-row col-8` cell, rendering it at 74.25% instead of the intended 66%. This is a template authoring bug in Bluecore's grid CSS.
