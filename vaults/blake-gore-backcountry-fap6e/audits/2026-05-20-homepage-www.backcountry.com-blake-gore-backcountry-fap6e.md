# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened backcountry.com on my phone and got a CAPTCHA wall. Not a homepage — a Cloudflare-style bot-check screen that reads "Let's confirm you are human" with a single orange "Begin" button and a language dropdown set to English. No hero, no gear, no Friends & Family deal, no Gearhead callout. Just a holding pen asking me to prove I'm alive before I can even see what's on sale.

## What's working

- The "Begin" button is large, orange, and tappable on mobile — if I have to interact with it, at least it's not a tiny link I'll miss with my thumb
- Language selector is present, which is a minor accessibility nod

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** I came here to check if the Friends & Family sale is live and instead I'm staring at a CAPTCHA prompt
- **No brand identity whatsoever.** There's no Backcountry logo, no wordmark, no color that signals I'm even at the right URL — I could be anywhere
- **Zero commerce intent acknowledged.** A returning customer (I have an account, I DM my Gearhead rep) gets zero recognition — no "sign in to skip" path, no loyalty shortcut
- **The orange accent color is the only visual element** — and it's being used to confirm I'm a human, not to highlight a gear deal
- **Mobile experience is a dead end.** There's nowhere to go but tap "Begin," complete some challenge I don't want to do, and hope the actual site loads on the other side

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Backcountry logo and a one-line brand reassurance** ("You're on backcountry.com — just verifying before we open the vault") so I don't feel like I landed on a phishing page
- **Add a "Sign in to continue" path** that bypasses or fast-tracks the challenge for known logged-in customers — I have an account, use it
- **If the CAPTCHA is Cloudflare Turnstile, switch to the invisible or managed challenge mode** so mobile shoppers don't hit a full interstitial on every cold visit
- **At minimum, show a header strip with the logo** above the verification card so the brand is visible before I click anything
