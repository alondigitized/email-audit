# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Backcountry didn't show me a homepage. They showed me a CAPTCHA wall — orange headline, one button, a language dropdown. That's it. Whatever Gearhead-friendly, gear-pr0n experience they've built behind that door is completely invisible right now. As someone who DMs their Gearhead rep when I can't find a Metolius #3 in blue, having to prove I'm a human before I can even browse feels like getting carded at the gear closet.

## What's working

- **Page renders cleanly** — no layout chaos, no broken images, no lorem ipsum. If this were the actual homepage, I'd at least say it loads.
- **Orange button is the obvious tap target** — single action, no ambiguity about what to do next if I decide to proceed.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing** — I can't see a hero, a promo banner, a category nav, or a single product. None of it.
- **"Let's confirm you are human"** is the first brand interaction I have — not exactly the Friends + Family sale energy I showed up for.
- **Zero personalization signals** — no "Welcome back, Blake" or any hint they know me from 12 orders and a Gearhead relationship.
- **The bot wall itself is the dark pattern** — I came to shop, not to pass a pop quiz.

## Recommendations

- **Recognize logged-in sessions and bypass the CAPTCHA gate entirely** — if I'm cookied and account-authenticated, I've already proven I'm human. Don't make me do it again.
- **If a CAPTCHA is unavoidable, brand it** — swap the generic orange-text-on-white for something with a photo, a quip, or at least a Backcountry logo so I know I'm still in the right place.
- **Pass me directly to a personalized landing state post-verification** — not the generic homepage, but a view that reflects my recent browsing (trad rack, ski touring boots) so I feel the wait was worth it.
- **Surface a reason to complete the check** — even a single line like "Big deals on climbing gear on the other side" gives me a reason to tap Begin instead of closing the tab.
