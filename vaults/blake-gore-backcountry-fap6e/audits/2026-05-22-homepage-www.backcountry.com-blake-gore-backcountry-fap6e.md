# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I tapped over to Backcountry expecting to hunt down some new trad gear and instead got a bot wall. The only thing "above the fold" is an orange "Let's confirm you are human" gate — no hero, no deal, no product, nothing. As a brand that built its whole identity on hardcore outdoor authenticity, throwing a generic security-check screen at mobile visitors is a pretty rough first impression.

## What's working

- The "Begin ›" button is clearly visible and tappable on mobile — at least the one interactive element on screen is legible and sized correctly.
- The language selector (English dropdown) is a minor accessibility nod.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is blocked. No brand identity, no hero, no product, no offer — nothing that says "Backcountry" at all.
- The orange color is the only brand-adjacent element, and it's used on a CAPTCHA heading, not on a product.
- Zero navigation visible — I can't one-tap to Climbing or Ski Touring to rescue the session.
- No explanation of *why* I'm being challenged — as a logged-in Gearhead customer, this feels like being carded at a bar I've been going to for a decade.
- The page is mostly blank white space below the bot-check module; looks broken.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-page bot gate for recognized return visitors.** If I'm on a device that's hit Backcountry before, pass me through silently or use a lightweight inline challenge — don't nuke the entire homepage experience.
- **If the gate must show, inject brand context:** drop the Backcountry logo, a tagline, and one hero image behind the challenge module so the session doesn't feel like a phishing site.
- **Add a navigation strip above the gate** so power users (hi, it's me) can deep-link directly to Climbing or Ski Touring while the check resolves.
- **Log device/session signals server-side** and suppress the challenge for accounts with purchase history — my Gearhead knows my name; the bot wall shouldn't act like I'm a stranger.
