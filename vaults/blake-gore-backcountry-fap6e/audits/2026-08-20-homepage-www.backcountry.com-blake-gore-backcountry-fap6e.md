# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-08-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get is a full-screen paywall for my email before I've even seen the site — classic Backcountry, still leaning hard on the "Semi-Annual Sale" hammer. Feels like a warehouse-clearance brand right now, not the curated gear shop I go to when I'm dialing in a ski touring setup. The 60% off number is loud enough to make me pause, but I came here to actually shop, not sign up for another list.

## What's working

- **"Up to 60% Off" banner** — big number, easy to read, and it's a real percentage not some vague "save big" nonsense.
- **HSA/FSA callout in the top strip** — smart, I didn't know Backcountry did that, mildly interesting even if it doesn't apply to my trad rack.
- **"Sign In For Member Perks"** in the header — at least it acknowledges returning shoppers exist instead of treating everyone like a first-timer.
- **Sale ends 8/22 date stamp** — a real deadline instead of a fake countdown, I respect that.

## What's weak

- **The email modal eats the whole screen.** I'm a Sign In / Expert Help guy already — I don't need to be captured, I need to browse. This is the single biggest miss on the page.
- **Hero image is a tent and two people sitting around camp.** Nothing here says climbing or ski touring — could be any outdoor retailer's stock photo.
- **No nav access until I dismiss the popup.** Men / Women / Activities / Brands are all sitting right there behind the modal, completely unclickable.
- **"Exclusions apply" right under a 60%-off headline** — my gearhead brain immediately assumes the stuff I actually want (hardgoods, skis, technical outerwear) is the excluded category.

## Recommendations

- Kill the full-page email gate on first load — push it to a slide-in after 10-15 seconds of scroll, or make the X way more obvious than a tiny corner icon.
- Swap the camping hero for a lifestyle shot that actually reads "technical" — someone racking gear at a crag or skinning up in the backcountry, not sitting at a campsite.
- Put "Sale ends 8/22" and the exclusions link directly on the hero banner itself, not buried only in the modal, so I know what I'm dealing with before I even engage.
- Surface Summit Club member pricing/benefits directly instead of a teaser link — if I'm a member-tier shopper, show me my status or savings up front.
