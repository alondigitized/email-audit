# Up to 70% off ends TODAY
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Backcountry's Memorial Day finale — the "ENDS TODAY" closer to a week-long flash sale we've now seen escalate from "2 days only" (May 24) to "ends tomorrow" (Alex's note May 25) to this final-hours blast. The hero is a hikers-in-action shot with a "FLASH SALE" lockup and "last chance to save big on select gear & apparel," followed by the now-familiar four-tile product grid (Scarpa, Lowa, Mountain Hardwear, Scarpa again), a category grid, the giant orange "SALE SALE SALE" Memorial Day block, a Sunglasses Savings module, and the Summit Club perks footer.
- It renders cleanly, hits the urgency note immediately with the red "ENDS TODAY!" ticker bar, and the structure is identical to the May 24 send — which is exactly the problem. By the seventh or eighth near-identical Memorial Day creative this week, the "last chance" feels like the boy who cried wolf. Walker-relevant signals are thin: hiking is in the hero, but the featured gear is technical trail/mountaineering boots (Scarpa, Lowa) rather than walking-friendly product.
- A competent, urgent close to the campaign — but creatively exhausted and undifferentiated from yesterday's send.

## What's working

- Red "ENDS TODAY!" ticker at the very top sets urgency in the first glance.
- Hero photo of hikers on a trail is genuinely on-brand and Walker-adjacent.
- Clear single primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") repeated through the email.
- Memorial Day "SALE SALE SALE" block is bold and unmissable.
- Summit Club benefits (20% cash back, free Carhartt blanket) are tangible loyalty perks.

## What's weak

- This is the third "ENDS TODAY" in four days — urgency is hollowed out.
- Featured products are mountaineering boots, not walking gear — weak fit for the persona.
- "Up to 70% off" headline isn't backed by visible 70% prices on the tiles shown.
- Creative is a near-copy of the May 24 send — no new hook to justify reopening.
- Four product tiles, four "Shop Now" buttons, no price ladder or "doorbuster" anchor.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a doorbuster, not a category grid.** Pick one product at the deepest discount, show the original-vs-sale price, and make it the hero below "ENDS TODAY." That earns the 70% claim.
- 2. **Differentiate the finale from yesterday's send.** A countdown clock, a "final hours" timestamp, or a "what's still in stock in your size" personalization would justify the second send.
- 3. **Swap the boot tiles for walking-friendly footwear** (Hoka, On, Altra trail-to-town) — same brand, better persona fit.
- 4. **Shrink the SALE SALE SALE block.** It's screaming the same thing the top banner already says.
