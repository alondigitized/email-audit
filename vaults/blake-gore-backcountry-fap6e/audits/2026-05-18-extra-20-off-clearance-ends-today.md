# EXTRA 20% off clearance ends today
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the final urgency close on the same Extra 20% off clearance sequence that launched May 16 and escalated May 17 with "ends tomorrow." By the third consecutive send on the same offer — same hero image, same CTA structure, same clearance pitch — the creative is running on fumes. The urgency signal ("ends today") is the only new element, and it's genuinely credible as a final push, but the surrounding email is essentially the same render.
- The email itself is clean: the hierarchy works, the offer is front-and-center, and there are no render bugs. The product grid and category tiles add some browsability below the fold. But there's nothing here a subscriber couldn't predict from yesterday's send. The only decision this email is asking is: do you finally click before it's gone?
- Verdict: solid execution on a tired creative. The urgency close is the right tactical move — it just can't paper over three days of near-identical sends.

## What's working

- **Urgency strip at the top is impossible to miss** — "ENDS TODAY" repeating across the header banner reinforces the close without competing with the hero.
- **Hero is clean and unambiguous** — "EXTRA 20% OFF CLEARANCE." in large type with a single "SHOP NOW" button, no distraction.
- **No render bugs** — every image loaded, layout is tight, no overlapping text or orphaned elements.
- **Category grid adds browsability** — the six-tile "Top Categories" block (Men's Apparel, Women's Apparel, Camp & Hike, Bike, Travel, Accessories) gives a subscriber who doesn't want clearance a second entry point.

## What's weak

- **Third identical creative in three days** — the hero shot, offer language, and layout are all carry-overs. Cadence fatigue is real; a subscriber who skipped May 16 and May 17 is probably not moved by repetition alone.
- **Product tiles are too small to drive desire** — the "Don't Miss These Deals" row renders at a size where product names and prices are barely legible; they're decorative, not persuasive.
- **No personalization or segmentation signal** — no name, no category hint, nothing that distinguishes this from a mass blast to every subscriber on the list.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — after three sends, "Shop Clearance Now" or "Grab It Before It's Gone" would carry the urgency of the final close; "Shop Now" just restates the button archetype.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero shot for fresh creative** — even a different lifestyle image breaks the déjà-vu effect. If creative resources are limited, a tight product flatlay of the best clearance items communicates "new things to see" where the current hero says "same email again."
- 2. **Make "ends today" the headline, not the subject-line footnote** — the hero says "EXTRA 20% OFF CLEARANCE." The subject says "ends today." Swap: the hero should lead with "Last Chance — Ends Today" and the offer in subhead.
- 3. **Pin a specific featured deal above the fold** — one clearance item at a named price (e.g., "Patagonia R1 Pullover — $79, was $130") would convert browsers who've been sitting on this offer for two days.
- 4. **Resize the product tiles** — two tiles per row, not four, so prices and product names are actually readable without zooming.

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Bluecore (sending domain `b.backcountry.com`, tracking domain `trk.b.backcountry.com`) with a functional responsive layout, but has a **critical missing plain-text fallback**, missing one-click unsubscribe headers, and unverifiable authentication status — all of which affect deliverability and bulk-sender compliance.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| All click links route through Bluecore redirect domain | `trk.b.backcountry.com` — expected for ESP, but UTM chain cannot be confirmed from truncated source |
| Tracking pixel lacks alt text | `https://trk.b.backcountry.com/ss/o/u001.s4HNEC6Qw5Htp4aPAN5Gdw/4qq/fmLUJ9BjQDOK1gGqGic0Dw/ho.gif` — confirmed by QA |
| External font CDN is a hard dependency | `https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/backcountry/backcountry_fonts_1769208036.css` — if this CDN is unavailable, rendering degrades silently with no defined fallback stack in `<style>` |

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS grid selector bug** in the mobile media query:

```css
/* Line in <style> block */
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```

The second selector is `.md-fluid-row .col-8` — it should be `.md-fluid-row .col-9`. This means the responsive `.col-9` width is never applied to `md-fluid-row` elements; instead it incorrectly overrides `.col-8` width a second time.

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute | Not present in source — screen readers cannot infer document language |
| Tracking pixel `ho.gif` has no `alt` attribute | QA WARN — `alt=""` minimum is required for screen readers and image-off rendering |

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. Bluecore renders personalization server-side before delivery; no raw `{{token}}` or `%%token%%` syntax is visible in the rendered HTML source, which is the expected state post-send.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Severity | Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **FAIL** | Plain-text MIME part is entirely absent | QA reports text version = 0 chars — CAN-SPAM requires a plain-text equivalent; also a strong spam signal for Gmail/Outlook filters |
| **WARN** | `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured | QA: header absent from relay capture — may be present on actual SMTP envelope but unverifiable; Google/Yahoo require this for bulk senders |
| **WARN** | `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not captured | One-click unsubscribe required for senders >5,000/day to Gmail per Google's 2024 bulk sender policy |

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs route through `trk.b.backcountry.com` redirects. UTM parameter injection (if any) happens at redirect resolution time, not in the HTML source, so the attribution chain cannot be audited from the source alone. **Recommend spot-checking a live redirect** to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are appended before the final `backcountry.com` landing URL.

---

### 7. Recommendations

- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present on the actual SMTP envelope via Bluecore's sending config — the AgentMail relay may be stripping them, or they may be absent.

**Priority 2 — Fix this sprint:**
