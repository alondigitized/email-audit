# You've got 15% off! Still trying to decide?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a welcome-series follow-up — Backcountry noticing you received the 15% welcome discount and haven't used it. The "Still trying to decide?" framing is a smart lifecycle trigger: it acknowledges the hesitation without being pushy. The render is clean and the brand identity is tight.
- The problem is architecture. The 15% off offer doesn't land until the third content block. You scroll past an action-shot hero, then a "GearHead Experts" value-prop section, then a "Our Gear Closet Is A Warehouse" section, before the actual reason this email exists appears. By that point you've already decided to close it or stay. The offer should be the first thing you see, not a reward for scrolling.
- Also: this is the fourth Backcountry email in roughly 24 hours (three sends on 5/16, this one on 5/17). Even subscribers who opened the welcome email yesterday are at saturation risk by now. The urgency of a "still deciding?" nudge is undercut by the sheer volume.

## What's working

- **Subject line lands the offer immediately** — "You've got 15% off!" is concrete and action-oriented before the question hook.
- **GearHead Experts section** builds genuine brand differentiation with service-oriented copy, not just discount-pushing.
- **Summit Cash Back section** reinforces the loyalty ecosystem — smart to remind a new subscriber that buying earns future value.
- **Unique code visible in body** — the 15% code is shown, which reduces friction at checkout (no hunting for it).
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout issues on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Offer buried below the fold** — the 15% off CTA is the third scrollable section; the hero real estate is spent on lifestyle/brand copy instead.
- **"Is Bringing Yours To Life"** hero headline is incomplete/cryptic without the brand name context — reads like the top line got cut.
- **No deadline on the 15% offer** — "Still trying to decide?" implies urgency but there's no visible expiry to act on it.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "Shop Now" or equivalent appears in multiple blocks; nothing pulls you toward a specific category or product.
- **Volume cadence problem** — this send on 5/17 follows three Backcountry sends on 5/16; the brand history shows no throttling between promotional blasts and lifecycle triggers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 15% off block, not the hero lifestyle shot.** Move the code + CTA to the top of the email. Every pixel of above-the-fold space should be doing the job the subject line promised.
- 2. **Add an expiry date to the offer.** Even "Offer expires in 3 days" turns "still deciding?" from a shrug into a reason to act now.
- 3. **Suppress this send if the subscriber opened/clicked any of yesterday's three emails.** The cadence is the real conversion killer — throttle lifecycle nudges when promotional sends just fired.
- 4. **Fix or complete the hero headline.** "Is Bringing Yours To Life" needs context — either integrate the brand name into the visual or rewrite the headline so it stands alone.
- 5. **Make the CTA category-specific.** If the subscriber browsed skis, the CTA should say "Shop Ski Gear" — not "Shop Now." This would require browse data, but even a seasonal anchor ("Shop Spring Apparel") beats the generic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 15% off expires soon — grab it before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One nudge: your welcome discount is still waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code MYFIRST15 at checkout — no minimum, no catch`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Everything from Patagonia to Arc'teryx, 15% off your first order`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on tracking pixel**
- Element: `<img src="https://trk.b.backcountry.com/ss/o/u001.s4HNEC6Qw5Htp4aPAN5Gdw/4qp/TgyJCgoyTECK-pdd1rDzYg/ho.gif">`
- Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Absence can cause screen readers to announce the raw filename or URL.

**CSS selector bug in fluid grid:**
```css
.fluid-row .col-9, .md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```
- `.md-fluid-row .col-8` is duplicated — the selector targets `.col-8` instead of `.col-9`. This means `.md-fluid-row .col-9` has no mobile-breakpoint width rule, and `.md-fluid-row .col-8` gets two conflicting declarations (66% from the `.col-8` rule, 74.25% here). Layout breakage risk on any fluid 9-column section in medium-breakpoint clients.

**External font dependency:**
- `<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/backcountry/backcountry_fonts_1769208036.css">`
- Stylesheet fetch blocked by corporate firewalls or strict clients (Outlook, some iOS Mail configs with load-remote-content off) will silently fall back to system fonts. No `font-family` fallback stack is visible in the truncated source — needs verification in the full source.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing**
- QA measured 0 characters in the text/plain MIME part.
- CAN-SPAM and general deliverability best practice require a readable text alternative. Many spam filters penalize missing plaintext. Gmail and Apple Mail use plaintext for notification previews in some configurations. **This is the highest-priority fix.**

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent or not captured**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected by AgentMail relay.
- RFC 2369 compliance gap. Gmail and Yahoo (as of Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) require `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) for senders above 5,000/day. Backcountry almost certainly exceeds that threshold.
- If Bluecore injects these headers at send time but AgentMail strips or doesn't forward them, the relay configuration needs review. Either way — confirm headers are present in raw delivered message via Gmail "Show original."

**[WARN] Authentication-Results not captured**
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown from relay. Sending domain `b.backcountry.com` should have a published SPF record and DKIM signature from Bluecore's infrastructure. Verify via `nslookup -type=TXT b.backcountry.com` and a raw header check on a delivered copy.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot fully audit without seeing the full link href values, but Bluecore's click wrapper (`trk.b.backcountry.com`) should be passing UTM parameters through to `backcountry.com`. Spot-check one CTA click in the browser and confirm:
- `utm_source=email` (or equivalent)
- `utm_campaign` maps to this specific send
- `utm_medium=email`

The 15% discount code (if promo-code-based rather than account-linked) must match exactly what is presented in the email body — a mismatch here causes the single highest drop-off point in retention flows.
