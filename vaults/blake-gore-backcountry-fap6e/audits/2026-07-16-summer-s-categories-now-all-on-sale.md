# Summer’s categories now all on sale 🚵 🧗🏾
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Backcountry's return-to-full-price-sale play after a week that cycled through 70%+ Outlet positioning and a three-part "Extra 20% off clearance" urgency close. The structural bet here is smart: organize the Summer Sale by activity category (Bike, Climb, Hike & Camp) rather than just stacking a generic discount banner. The "TOP PICKS FOR YOU" personalization row reinforces that logic and is the single strongest thing in this email. The visual hierarchy is clean, the outdoor imagery is appropriately matched, and there are no render bugs.
- But the offer is identical to what landed in the subscriber's inbox on July 10 — "up to 40% off" across summer gear — and this email adds no escalation, no new hook, and no deadline. After six promotional sends in six days, Backcountry is asking the subscriber to take action on the same ceiling discount with even less urgency than the FINAL HOURS sends from July 13. The subject line "Summer's categories now all on sale" is the weakest part: purely descriptive, no %, no deadline, no FOMO. The email earns its click if opened, but it will struggle to earn the open at all.
- The 3-day gap since July 13 gives the cadence some breathing room, but this brand has burned a lot of goodwill with repeated false deadlines. A "Summer Sale" with no end date, after three "FINAL HOURS" sends last week, will read as ambient noise to most of this list.

## What's working

- Category-by-activity navigation (Bike, Climb, Hike & Camp) makes the offer immediately scannable for a multi-sport outdoor persona rather than a generic "shop everything" page
- "TOP PICKS FOR YOU" personalization row with real product tiles breaks the generic promo pattern and is the clearest click driver in the email
- SUMMER SALE hero loads first, discount badge prominent — the eye lands on the offer before anything else
- Clean render with no broken images, overlapping text, or layout bugs
- Outdoor imagery (mountain biking, climbing) is authentically matched to the subscriber persona

## What's weak

- Same 40% off ceiling as July 10, with no escalation, no new hook, and no deadline — offer fatigue is fully set in by now
- Subject line is purely descriptive and earns zero open lift: no %, no urgency, no specific category called out
- Every category CTA reads "SHOP NOW" — a category-organized email should have "Shop Bike Sale →", "Shop Climbing →", etc., so the click feels intentional
- No time-bound element anywhere visible — "now" is not a deadline, and this brand trained its list to expect one
- Top-of-email bar (Summit Club / loyalty nav) appears to be leaking as preview text in the inbox, wasting the single line that could have reinforced the sale offer

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline** — even "Ends Sunday" transforms browsing intent into action. The July 13 sends proved Backcountry can credibly close; apply that here from the start instead of waiting until day 3.
- 2. **Make every CTA category-specific**: "Shop Bike Sale →" / "Shop Climbing →" / "Shop Hike & Camp →" — three identical SHOP NOW buttons collapse three clear reasons to click into one forgettable one.
- 3. **Pull one TOP PICKS product into the hero** — the personalization row buried below the fold is doing heavy lifting that belongs in the hero tile, where it would anchor the entire email in something specific.
- 4. **Fix the preview text** — set explicit preheader copy so the loyalty bar stops leaking. Right now the inbox preview is wasted on navigation noise.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject line with a signal that actually creates pull:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bike. Climb. Hike. Up to 40% off — ends Sunday 🚵`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer activity list, now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop by category — Bike, Climb, Hike & Camp all 40% off through the weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Top picks picked for you — plus savings across every summer activity.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[ISSUE] CSS selector typo — col-9 responsive rule broken:**

```css
.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```

The `.md-fluid-row .col-8` selector is duplicated (`.col-8` appears twice). The correct rule should be `.md-fluid-row .col-9`. Any `md-fluid-row` element with class `col-9` will not receive its intended mobile width, and `col-8` within `md-fluid-row` will be double-targeted with conflicting widths. Impact is layout breakage at ≤600px for any col-9 column inside an `md-fluid-row` container.

**[WARN] Tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute:**

```
src: https://trk.b.backcountry.com/.../ho.gif
```

The `ho.gif` open-pixel has no `alt` attribute (not even `alt=""`). While a blank alt is correct for decorative/tracking images, the *absence* of the attribute triggers screen reader fallback behavior and will fire an accessibility lint warning in tools like Litmus Accessibility. Fix: `alt=""`.

**[WARN] External font dependency:**

CSS is fetched from `https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/backcountry/backcountry_fonts_1769208036.css` at render time. Gmail and several corporate clients block external CSS; font will silently fall back to system fonts in those clients. This is expected behavior for email but worth noting for QA baseline.

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is empty (0 chars):**
CAN-SPAM does not mandate plain text, but a zero-length text part is a significant spam-filter signal. Gmail, Outlook, and major MTAs score this negatively. Bluecore should auto-generate a plain-text version from the HTML; its absence here suggests the multipart/alternative assembly is misconfigured or the text part was not populated.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not captured:**
The QA relay did not observe a `List-Unsubscribe` header. This header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced since Feb 2024) for senders exceeding 5k/day to Gmail. If Backcountry's volume qualifies, missing this header risks inbox demotion. May be a relay capture gap rather than a true absence — should be verified against raw SMTP headers in the Bluecore send log.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058):**
One-click unsubscribe (the `POST` method variant) is now required by Gmail for bulk senders. Without it, Gmail renders a slower two-step unsubscribe flow, which increases complaint rates. Again, verify in raw SMTP headers before escalating to Bluecore.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results not observable:**
SPF/DKIM pass status cannot be confirmed from the captured headers. Sending domain is `b.backcountry.com` (Bluecore subdomain). Verify DKIM signature aligns to `backcountry.com` (not just `b.backcountry.com`) per DMARC alignment requirements.
