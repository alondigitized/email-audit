# Our favorite brands on sale right now 😏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Backcountry's Memorial Day brand-sale blast — a standard multi-brand grid email leaning on "UP TO 30% OFF" and a familiar lineup (Patagonia, The North Face, Osprey, On, Mountain Hardwear). The hero image is strong and the render is clean, but the subject line wastes the opportunity completely. "Our favorite brands on sale right now 😏" is the kind of copy that could come from any outdoor retailer on any weekend of the year — it names nothing, promises nothing specific, and the smirk emoji does no work.
- The deeper problem is cadence. This is Backcountry's **third email today**: the welcome send with 15% off, the Extra 20% clearance blast, and now this. The clearance module at the bottom — which is just a reprise of the earlier send — is doing double duty trying to justify another inbox slot. That's a signal of over-mailing, not cross-promotion.
- The six-brand grid is the structural problem in the body. Every tile has the same generic "SHOP NOW" button, no featured product, no price anchor per brand. It reads like a sitemap, not a sale. This email lands squarely in "decent workhorse" territory — it'll convert existing browsers who were already looking for a reason, but it doesn't create that reason.

## What's working

- Hero image is compelling — outdoor hiker in full kit, strong summer energy, matches Memorial Day timing.
- Brand lineup (Patagonia, TNF, Osprey) is recognizable and trusted; seeing them together signals a real event.
- "Extra 20% off clearance" module at the bottom recycles the ongoing clearance campaign without being intrusive.
- No render bugs — all images loaded, no broken layout, text is legible at a glance.
- The "ENDS SOON!" ticker bar adds a layer of urgency that the hero and subject fail to provide.

## What's weak

- Subject line is completely generic — "Our favorite brands on sale right now 😏" names no brand, no discount, and no deadline.
- Third email of the day from Backcountry — cadence has crossed into fatigue territory; the welcome + clearance + this sends in one day is too much.
- Six brand tiles with identical "SHOP NOW" copy create zero hierarchy — nothing pulls the eye to a leading offer or featured product.
- "UP TO 30% OFF" range language in the hero undercuts the urgency; "up to" always reads as "probably not your item."
- No deadline is visible anywhere in the main body — "ends soon" in a ticker bar isn't a credible deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a brand name and a real number in the subject** — the single highest-leverage change. Something like "Patagonia, TNF & Osprey: Up to 30% off this weekend" would dramatically outperform the current line.
- 2. **Collapse the six-brand grid to one hero product per featured brand** — show a specific jacket or pack with a real price crossed out, not just a logo tile.
- 3. **Add an explicit deadline in the body** — "Sale ends Monday, May 27" is more credible than a scrolling ticker saying "ENDS SOON."
- 4. **Don't send on the same day as the welcome + clearance blasts** — move this to May 17 or 18; it'll get a cleaner inbox slot and better open rate.
- 5. **Drop the clearance module or make it the secondary CTA, not a full reprise** — it dilutes the Memorial Day brand-sale message and reminds subscribers they already got an email about this today.

## Technical Audit — Backcountry Memorial Day Sale

**Sender:** backcountry@b.backcountry.com | **ESP/Platform:** Bluecore (`b.backcountry.com`, `trk.b.backcountry.com`, `partner-fonts.bluecore.com`)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Bluecore-rendered promotional email with a mobile-responsive table layout. Three confirmed technical defects: no plain-text MIME part, missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers at the relay layer, and a CSS selector typo in the fluid grid — plus an untagged tracking pixel.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

All links route through `trk.b.backcountry.com` (Bluecore's redirect/click-tracking domain). No issues with the redirect infrastructure itself, but the HTML source is truncated before destination URLs are visible, so UTM parameter coverage cannot be fully verified here (see §6).

No raw destination URLs are exposed in the visible portion — redirects are opaque Bluecore tokens.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS selector bug — fluid grid col-9:**
```css
/* Visible in <style> block */
.fluid-row .col-9,.md-fluid-row .col-8 { width: 74.25% !important; }
```
`.md-fluid-row .col-8` is a copy-paste error; it should be `.md-fluid-row .col-9`. This means `.md-fluid-row .col-9` gets no width rule on mobile and `.md-fluid-row .col-8` gets double-targeted (overriding the correct 66% rule above it).

**External font stylesheet will be stripped by Gmail:**
```html
<link rel="stylesheet" href="https://partner-fonts.bluecore.com/backcountry/backcountry_fonts_1769208036.css">
```
Gmail removes `<link>` tags entirely. Any font-family declarations in that external file will silently fall through to system fonts. No inline fallback `font-family` stack is visible in the truncated source.

**Missing alt text on tracking pixel:**
```
src: https://trk.b.backcountry.com/.../ho.gif
```
Empty or absent `alt=""` attribute. Screen readers will announce the raw URL or filename. Should be `alt=""` (explicit empty) for decorative/tracking images.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the truncated HTML. Cannot confirm full-document coverage — Bluecore typically renders server-side before send, so broken tokens would show as blank rather than raw syntax.

No issues found in visible portion.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **WARN** | Not captured by AgentMail relay — may be stripped or absent |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Absent — one-click unsubscribe not functional via this path |
| SPF/DKIM | **UNKNOWN** | `Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay |
| Plain-text MIME part | **FAIL** | 0 characters — multipart/alternative missing the text/plain part |

The plain-text failure is the most actionable: ISPs (notably Gmail and Yahoo) use the absence of a text/plain part as a spam signal. It also violates best-practice guidance from both M3AAWG and Yahoo's 2024 bulk sender requirements.

`List-Unsubscribe` headers may exist at the MTA level and simply not be forwarded by the AgentMail relay — confirm with Bluecore send logs before treating as a definitive failure.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Destination URLs are not visible in the truncated HTML (all wrapped in opaque Bluecore redirect tokens at `trk.b.backcountry.com`). UTM parameter presence on landing pages cannot be confirmed from source alone.

Known Bluecore behavior: UTM params are appended to destination URLs at redirect resolution time, configured per-campaign in the Bluecore dashboard. Verify the campaign's UTM template is set — especially `utm_campaign` — since "Memorial Day Sale" is a named initiative and the value should be auditable in GA4.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**
1. **Add plain-text MIME part.** Even a minimal stripped version satisfies ISP requirements and improves deliverability scoring.
2. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` header injection** at the Bluecore/MTA layer. If headers are present at send but stripped by the relay, this is a relay configuration issue, not a template issue.

**P1 — Fix in next template revision:**
3. **Correct CSS selector typo:** Change `.md-fluid-row .col-8` → `.md-fluid-row .col-9` in the `@media` block (affects 9-column fluid layout on mobile).
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the `ho.gif` tracking pixel explicitly (empty string, not omitted).
5. **Add inline `font-family` fallback stack** for Gmail clients where the Bluecore-hosted CSS will be stripped. Minimum: `font-family: [loaded-font], Arial, sans-serif` on key text elements.

**P2 — Investigate:**
6. **Verify SPF/DKIM pass** on `b.backcountry.com` sending domain via direct MTA logs — relay header absence makes this unconfirmable from the email artifact alone.
