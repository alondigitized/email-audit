# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-06-25
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened backcountry.com on my phone and got hit with a bot-check wall before seeing a single product. Instead of the homepage, I'm looking at an orange-and-white interstitial that says "Let's confirm you are human" with a big "Begin" button and a language dropdown. There is zero commerce here — no nav, no hero, no deals, nothing. This reads like Cloudflare or a similar challenge page, not a retail experience.

## What's working

- The "Begin" button is clearly rendered and tappable — at least the interstitial itself isn't broken
- Language selector is visible for non-English users, which is a minor accessibility plus

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked.** A returning customer — someone who DMs their Gearhead rep and shows up on Friends + Family weekend — just got told they look like a bot. That's a gut-punch.
- **No brand identity whatsoever.** Not a logo, not a nav bar, not a color palette I associate with Backcountry. The orange heading could be any Cloudflare challenge on any site.
- **Zero product or category signal.** I came here to check if my Petzl harness is on promo and instead I'm doing a security check like I'm logging into my bank.
- **No explanation of why this is happening.** "Complete the security check before continuing" tells me nothing about whether this is a one-time thing or if I'll keep getting hit.

## Recommendations

- **Only gate bots, not customers.** If I'm a logged-in rewards member or returning visitor with cookies, skip the challenge entirely — challenge anonymous cold traffic, not known customers.
- **Brand the interstitial.** If you must show a challenge, put the Backcountry logo and a line like "Back in a sec — making sure it's you." Right now this page could be a phishing site.
- **Add a direct bypass for authenticated sessions.** A returning shopper who's signed in should never see this.
- **Test on real mobile devices before launch.** Whatever bot-detection rule triggered this is clearly too aggressive and is burning real conversion from high-intent shoppers.
