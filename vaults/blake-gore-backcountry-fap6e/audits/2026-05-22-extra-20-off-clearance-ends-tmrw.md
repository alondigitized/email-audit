# EXTRA 20% off clearance ends TMRW
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is at least the fifth activation of Backcountry's "Extra 20% off clearance" campaign — a deal that was declared over on May 18, resurrected as VIP Early Access on May 20, blasted again on May 21, and now hits May 22 with a hard "ends TMRW" countdown ticker. The urgency is structurally undermined before the subscriber even opens: they've already seen this expiration date come and go twice. The email itself is dense to the point of incoherence — the hero clearance offer competes with three 20% bundle modules, a recycled "SALE SALE SALE" Memorial Day section lifted straight from the May 20 send, and an editorial "Off-Duty Shirts" picks section. Six distinct creative units in one scroll.
- The individual modules are competently executed — clean layout, no render bugs, clear orange brand color throughout — but the kitchen-sink structure kills any single offer from landing. A subscriber who wanted clearance already bought it; a subscriber who hasn't bought yet has now been given five separate "last chances" to reconsider. The credibility of the deadline is the biggest problem this email has, and no amount of creative execution fixes that.
- Clear judgement: this send is calendar-filling noise for an already-fatigued list. The marginal lift from a genuine urgency close is being spent on an audience that no longer believes the clock.

## What's working

- **Countdown ticker at the top** ("ENDS TOMORROW | ENDS TOMORROW…") — immediately visible on render, sets urgency before the hero loads.
- **Clean hero with unambiguous offer** — "EXTRA 20% OFF CLEARANCE" in large type, single SHOP NOW button, no clutter in the hero block.
- **Bundle modules are well-executed** — the three 20% off bundle rows (Hike, Camp, Sun & Swim) are visually consistent with clear category labels and individual CTAs; this is a stronger format than a generic sale grid.
- **Top categories grid** — six category tiles give subscribers a fast lateral navigation path if the hero offer isn't their category.

## What's weak

- **Urgency is dead on arrival.** "Ends TMRW" is the fourth or fifth deadline this exact offer has had since May 16. Subscribers who opened the May 18 "sale ending today" email already know this clock resets.
- **Six competing creative units** — clearance hero, sale-on-sale product grid, category nav, three bundle rows, the recycled Memorial Day "SALE SALE SALE" block, and Off-Duty Shirts editorial. No single offer has room to breathe.
- **"SALE SALE SALE" section is a direct copy-paste from the May 20 send** — identical stacked treatment, same visual. It reads as lazy template reuse rather than a new reason to click.
- **No prices on the product grid** — the four products shown in the "Sale on Sale Ends Soon" section have no visible prices or discount callouts, which removes the most compelling signal in a clearance email.
- **CTA copy is uniformly "SHOP NOW"** — every single button across every module says the same thing. No specificity ("Shop Hike Clearance"), no benefit language.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the "SALE SALE SALE" module entirely.** It's recycled creative from May 20 and splits attention with the clearance close. One offer per close email.
- 2. **Show prices.** Put the before/after price (or at minimum the % off badge) on each clearance product. This is the only data point that actually converts a fence-sitter on a clearance send.
- 3. **Change every CTA button to match its module.** "Shop Hike Clearance" / "Shop Camp Clearance" / "Shop Run Clearance" — the verb+noun specificity is the easiest click-rate lever in the email.
- 4. **Compress to three sections max.** Hero clearance close → one product grid with prices → bundle trio. Cut the category nav and the editorial picks for this send; those belong in a non-sale touchpoint.
- 5. **Acknowledge the cadence in the copy.** A single line like "Last markdown of the season — this one's real" does more work than a fifth countdown ticker, because it names the skepticism the subscriber already has.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 20% off clearance — today's the actual last day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: clearance sale on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Prices drop one more time, then these styles are gone for good.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's waiting — clearance closes tonight at midnight.`
