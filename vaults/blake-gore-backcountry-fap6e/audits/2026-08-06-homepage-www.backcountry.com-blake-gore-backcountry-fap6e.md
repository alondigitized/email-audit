## What's weak

- **The entire page is a CAPTCHA wall** — "Let's confirm you are human" with a "Begin" button is the only content. No logo, no nav, no product.
- **Zero brand presence.** No Backcountry logo, no color/photography identity beyond a generic orange button.
- **No path forward visible** — as a shopper I have to tap "Begin" and presumably clear a puzzle before I even see gear.
- **Language dropdown floating alone at the bottom** feels like a stray settings widget, not part of a considered layout.

## Recommendations

- Get the bot-detection trigger investigated — if a real skier like me is getting flagged on a normal mobile load, that's lost sessions at the door.
- If a challenge is unavoidable for some traffic, brand it — put the Backcountry wordmark/logo above the fold so it's clear I'm still on the right site and not a phishing clone.
- Keep the challenge lightweight (one tap, no puzzle grind) since anything heavier is going to bounce impatient repeat shoppers like me who just want to check if my size is back in stock.
- Log and monitor how often real users see this screen — if it's frequent, that's a conversion leak worth fixing before any merchandising work matters.
