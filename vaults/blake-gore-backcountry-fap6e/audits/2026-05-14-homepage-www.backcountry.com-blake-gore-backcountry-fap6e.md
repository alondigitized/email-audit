# Homepage snapshot · www.backcountry.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I opened Backcountry on my phone and got a bot-check wall — not the homepage, not a sale banner, not even the nav. The entire viewport is a Cloudflare-style interstitial with a single orange "Begin" button asking me to prove I'm human. For a returning customer who's been using this site for years to stalk climbing gear drops, this is a uniquely bad welcome. It tells me nothing about what Backcountry is selling, running, or pushing right now.

## What's working

- The interstitial renders cleanly — correct font, readable orange heading, a functional "Begin" button, and a language selector dropdown. No broken elements.
- The orange color is consistent with Backcountry's brand palette, so at minimum it doesn't look like a phishing page.

## What's weak

- The entire above-the-fold experience is a bot-check wall — no hero, no offer, no nav, no product imagery, nothing that communicates what Backcountry sells.
- Zero personalization or recognition of a returning user — there's no "you're logged in, skip this" or session-aware bypass.
- The copy "Complete the security check before continuing. This step verifies that you are not a bot, which helps to protect your account and prevent spam." is cold and bureaucratic — not the warm, gear-nerd brand voice I'm used to from Telly the Yeti emails.
- A language dropdown (English) is present but serves no purpose for the majority of US users hitting this screen — it's just visual noise during an already frustrating gate.
- No trust scaffolding: no Backcountry logo, no nav, no reassurance that I'm actually on backcountry.com and not some redirect.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Backcountry logo and a one-line brand stamp above the CAPTCHA** — even "backcountry.com | Gear for the human-verified adventurer" would keep the brand alive during the friction moment.
- **Add a session/cookie-aware bypass for logged-in or recently-active users** — someone who checked out last week shouldn't be hitting a full bot wall on a mobile visit.
- **Style the interstitial page in Backcountry voice** — swap the generic "Let's confirm you are human" for something that sounds like the brand: "One quick check before the gear." It's a small lift and it doesn't feel like you landed on a Cloudflare template.
- **Show a minimal sticky promo strip below the interstitial** — even "Free shipping on orders $50+" gives me a reason to push through the CAPTCHA instead of closing the tab.
