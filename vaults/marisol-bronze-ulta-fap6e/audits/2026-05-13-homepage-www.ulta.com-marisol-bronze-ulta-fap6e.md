# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta is really leaning into the wellness-beauty crossover right now — LED masks, AGI supplements, the whole self-care stack. The rewards popup greeting me the second I land is very "we know who you are," which as a Diamond member I actually appreciate. But the overlay is eating the hero alive and I can barely read what the actual offer is before I have to mentally parse two things at once.

## What's working

- **The Rewards popup timing** — meeting me at the door with "Unlock $5* just for joining + Birthday Perks + Points = $ off" is smart for a logged-out Diamond member. It reminds me exactly why I'm loyal here.
- **"Sign in or join for free!" CTA** — clean, low-friction ask, and the checklist format makes the value prop scannable in under two seconds.
- **Wellness by Ulta Beauty branding** — the LED red-light mask + AGI skincare bundle visual is aspirational without being unrelatable. This is the glow-up self-care aesthetic I scroll through on TikTok.
- **"Beauty in 2 hours or less" top bar** — BOPIS / same-day is a genuine differentiator and it's front and center before anything else loads.
- **"Deals for you / 12 items"** section below fold signals personalization, which keeps me scrolling.

## What's weak

- **The rewards overlay covers the hero** — I can only see "Ea..." of what's probably "Early Access," a truncated offer description, and a sliver of the LED mask. The headline I'm supposed to be excited about is buried. The popup should slide in from the side, not sit dead-center on top of the campaign.
- **Right rail is fully cropped** — there's clearly a "70..." deal (probably 70% off) on the right side that I literally cannot read. That's a lost conversion signal.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — as someone who times my orders to hit $35 for free shipping, I want that number somewhere above the fold. It's not here.
- **"Activate" button copy is vague** — activate what, exactly? Without reading the fine print, "Activate" could mean loyalty membership, a specific promo, or an early-access deal. That ambiguity adds friction.
- **No person in the imagery** — the LED mask shot is a product hero, not a lifestyle shot. I can't see myself in it the way I would with a model actually wearing it.

## Recommendations

- **Move the rewards popup to a bottom sheet or side drawer** so it doesn't cannibalize the hero — keep the loyalty hook but let the campaign breathe behind it.
- **Swap "Activate" for offer-specific copy**: "Unlock Early Access" or "Activate 20% Off" tells me what I'm actually getting and reduces the mental tax.
- **Add the $35 free-shipping bar** as a sticky top strip or within the hero — Diamond members still track AOV and that threshold is table stakes for mobile shoppers.
- **Use a lifestyle shot of someone actually wearing the LED mask** — a 28–34 year-old with healthy skin doing her nighttime routine would land harder than a product-on-gradient photo for this audience.
