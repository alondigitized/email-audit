# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta knows exactly what they're doing — the second I land they hit me with the rewards pitch front and center, which honestly works on me because I live for my Diamond perks. The vibe right now is very fragrance gifting (Mother's Day season, makes sense), with colorful perfume bottles plastered across the hero. The problem? That rewards modal is literally swallowing the hero whole, so I can barely read what the actual fragrance promotion is.

## What's working

- **The rewards popup** — as a Diamond member this speaks directly to me. Seeing "Points = $ off a purchase" and "Birthday Perks" called out immediately validates why I shop here. Smart hook.
- **Free same-day delivery at $35** — that threshold is visible right at the top strip, and $35 is easy to hit when I'm buying prestige skincare or fragrance. Practical and aspirational at the same time.
- **The fragrance imagery** — even though it's half-hidden, those jewel-tone bottles (the green one, the purple Valentino-looking one, the pink) are gorgeous and instantly evoke "treat yourself / gift for mom" energy. Very on-point for the season.
- **"Activate" button with contrast** — the black button against the pink background pops immediately, low cognitive load to know where to tap.

## What's weak

- **The popup eating the hero** — this is my biggest gripe. I know a fragrance promo is running — I can see "Ea..." and "20..." peeking out behind the modal — but I literally cannot read it. Blocking the hero with a sign-in gate is a UX own-goal.
- **No visible personalization for an existing Diamond member** — I'm logged in (or trying to be), but there's no "Welcome back, Marisol" or rewards balance visible. The popup treats me like a stranger.
- **"Deals for you" section title is generic** — 12 items visible below fold, showing what looks like lip liners or lipsticks in nude shades. "Deals for you" sounds like clearance, not curated. For a Diamond shopper I'd want "Your member picks" or something that feels earned.
- **Zero urgency on the fragrance promo** — I can't read the deadline because the popup is in the way, and there's no countdown timer or "ends Sunday" copy visible anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the rewards popup by 3 seconds or move it to a side drawer** — don't let it swallow the hero on first paint. Let me see the full fragrance offer first, then slide in the loyalty nudge.
- **Surface my Diamond status and rewards balance in the header** — one small "💎 1,240 pts" indicator next to my account icon would make me feel seen and immediately think about whether I'm close to a reward redemption.
- **Replace "Deals for you" with "Your picks, your perks" or similar** — for a high-value loyalty member, language that signals curation beats language that signals clearance.
- **Add a visible deadline to the fragrance promo** — "Ends Sunday, May 11" above the "Shop now" button would add real urgency and push me to act now rather than come back later.
