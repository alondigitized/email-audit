# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta is doing the absolute most with this Summer Beauty Sale and honestly? As a Diamond member who lives for 21 Days of Beauty and stockpiling during BOGOs, this homepage is practically speaking my language. The "Up to 40% off on 2,000+ products" message hits immediately — and the fact that I can see Redken and Pureology in the hero shot tells me the prestige haircare I actually buy is included, not just drugstore filler. Right now this is clearly a sale-first push, not a discovery or editorial moment, and that's fine by me in early July.

## What's working

- **"Up to 40% off" + "2,000+ products" headline combo** — the volume claim makes the "up to" hedge feel credible. I believe this sale is real and big.
- **"Shop all BOGOs" CTA** — as a Diamond member who stocks up on Redken and Pureology, a dedicated BOGO page is exactly the tap I want. This button earns its real estate.
- **Prestige brand product shots in the hero** — seeing La Roche-Posay, Pureology, and Redken right in the hero tells me my tier of brands is in this sale, not just NYX and e.l.f.
- **Rewards popup content** — Birthday perks and points callout is relevant since I already have Diamond status and I use both of those. The $5 joining bonus is a nice get for anyone who isn't already a member.
- **"Beauty in 2 hours or less" top bar** — BOPIS is something I actually use when I need a product same-day. Love that it's the very first thing on the page.
- **"Ends Jul 11" visible below fold** — urgency is there without being obnoxious about it.

## What's weak

- **Rewards popup blocks the hero for a returning member** — I'm Diamond. I am already in the rewards program. That popup is for acquisition and it's literally sitting on top of the sale content I came here for. Feels like the brand forgot I exist.
- **No personalization or member callout** — not a single "Welcome back, Diamond member" or "Your points balance." For a top-tier loyalty customer, that's a miss. The popup is for strangers; there's nothing for me.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I know it's $35 but if I'm mobile-shopping and new to the brand, there's no anchor for that. The top bar could easily add "Free shipping at $35" next to the BOPIS callout.
- **No person/model in the hero** — it's all product shots floating on a blue background. Feels a little clinical and stock-y compared to what Sephora does with lifestyle imagery.
- **"Shop the sale" CTA is generic** — at my level I'd tap something like "Shop Diamond Early Access" or a category-specific shortcut. Generic "shop the sale" sends me to a wall of 2,000 products with no filtration path.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the rewards acquisition popup for a personalized Diamond member welcome strip** — logged-in Diamond members should see their points balance and a "You're X points from your next reward" nudge, not a join-for-free pitch aimed at strangers.
- **Add "Free shipping at $35" next to the BOPIS banner at the top** — it's one line of text and it closes the loop for anyone building a cart.
- **Add a lifestyle model shot to the hero** — a real millennial with the products in hand (or a flat-lay comparison shot of dupe vs. original, which maps perfectly to my posting habit) would make this feel less like a warehouse sale and more like Ulta.
- **Add a category shortcut bar below the hero** — three or four tappable chips (Haircare, Skincare, Makeup, BOGOs) so I can get to my category in one tap instead of wading through 2,000 products.
