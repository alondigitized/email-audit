# Up to 50% off: Black Friday^ is here! Terms & excl. apply 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday catalog send, and it works as one. A member banner ("5X points") sits above a black hero reading "Black Friday, up to 50% off LIVE online." Below that, a long run of pink-ribboned tiers steps down from "up to 50% off" to 40%, 30% and 25%. Each tier has brand-named tiles and a "Shop now" link. It's easy to scan and the offer is obvious.
- The weakness is cadence and focus. The brand history shows a "Black Friday^ starts NOW online!" send earlier today, so this is a near-duplicate arriving the same day. The top of the email also splits attention between the 5X member offer and the Black Friday hero. At roughly 10,000px tall, most readers will see only the first two blocks. This is a competent, expected send with no surprises.

## What's working

- The black hero with the pink ribbon and "up to 50% off" is unmistakably Black Friday.
- The descending tiers (50%, 40%, 30%, 25%) make a clear price ladder that's easy to skim.
- Named brands (Elemis, Morphe, Gucci, Dyson-style hair tools) and specific tile deals like "$100 off" and "now $15" give shoppers something concrete to click.
- The 5X points member banner gives loyalty members a real reason to act.
- The layout looks intact, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Today's earlier send ("Black Friday^ starts NOW online!") covered the same message, so this one reads as a repeat rather than a new beat.
- The 5X points banner and the Black Friday hero compete at the top. The eye has no single first landing point.
- The email is extremely long. The lower tiers and "plus these can't-miss deals" will rarely be seen.
- "Shop now" under every tile is generic. The black hero also leans on "Ask Ulta AI" rather than a clear shop button.
- The footer is a dense wall of legal fine print, and the subject's "Terms & excl. apply" and caret make the offer feel hedged.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one message. Either put Black Friday first and move the 5X points banner below the hero, or send the member offer as a separate segmented email. This would give the biggest lift.
- 2. Don't send a second Black Friday kickoff within hours. Differentiate it with a time-boxed doorbuster or a specific "top 10 deals" angle.
- 3. Cut the tier sections to the top two or three, and add a sticky "Shop all Black Friday deals" button near the top.
- 4. Replace "Shop now" with specific copy, such as "Shop 40% off palettes."
- 5. Move the fine print into a single collapsed line and trim the subject's disclaimers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday beauty deals: up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5X points for members. Online now, in stores Nov 26.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gucci, Elemis, Morphe and more, with new deals all week.`
