# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Ulta is leading with its loyalty stack hard — the first thing I see is a rewards sign-in modal layered over whatever hero they're actually running, and then immediately below that a three-tier points breakdown (2X / 3X / 4X) anchored by the "DIAMOND EARNS" column that yes, personally, I see myself in. This reads like a brand that knows its members drive the business and is constantly working to close the unregistered gap. The catch: I can't even see the actual campaign or products behind the popup.

## What's working

- **Diamond tier callout (4X POINTS):** As a Diamond member, seeing my tier right there in the top position of the points grid with a sparkle emoji lands. It's validating.
- **Three-tier breakdown (Member / Platinum / Diamond):** Visually shows me I'm at the top — classic gamification that makes me feel like I earned something.
- **"Deals for you — 12 items" section:** Personalized deal rail starting below the fold is exactly what I come to Ulta for. That copy tells me the site knows who I am.
- **BOPIS banner:** "Beauty in 2 hours or less — order online, pick up in store" is dead-on for my shopping habit. I use this constantly.
- **Birthday perks callout in the modal:** The birthday gift is one of my annual Ulta rituals — seeing it called out is a genuine retention signal.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the entire hero:** I have no idea what Ulta is running right now — a collab? 21 Days of Beauty? A clearance event? The modal eats the whole campaign.
- **"Sign in or join for free" CTA is non-member-first:** I'm already a Diamond member — this CTA is speaking past me to the unregistered. There's no "welcome back, sign in" framing.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible:** I know Ulta's $35 minimum by heart, but it's not on screen. For someone new-ish to the site, that's a gap.
- **Zero product imagery in context:** The "Deals for you" rail at the bottom is cropped — I can see "Sale" labels on what look like a foundation and a dark product but nothing is identifiable.
- **$5* asterisk:** Fine print energy on the join offer. What's the asterisk for? If I have to dig to find out, it slightly erodes trust.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal automatically after 3–4 seconds (or on any scroll/tap):** A Diamond member who is already planning to sign in doesn't need a full-screen gate. Let me see the campaign first, then nudge the sign-in.
- **Surface a "Welcome back, Diamond member" variation** for returning devices/cookies — swap the generic join modal for a rewards-balance + points-to-next-reward banner instead.
- **Make the free-shipping threshold ($35) visible in the top bar** alongside the BOPIS message — that's the one piece of info that most reliably shifts my cart behavior.
- **Pin one campaign tile above "Deals for you"** — even a 2-row teaser of what 21 Days of Beauty (or current event) looks like would anchor me to a reason to browse.
