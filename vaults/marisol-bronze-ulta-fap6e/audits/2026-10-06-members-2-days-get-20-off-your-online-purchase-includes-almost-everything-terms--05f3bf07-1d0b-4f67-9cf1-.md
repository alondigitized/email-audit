# Members, 2 DAYS! Get 20% off your online purchase⁺. Includes almost everything! Terms & excl. apply 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud member-only 20% off push, and the hero does its job. A burnt-orange block with "20% off" in giant type and "TWO DAYS | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" above it makes the offer and deadline unmissable. The code (SAVE20) is shown on the hero, so there's no hunting for it. The top banner ("Marisol, earn points that turn into $-off future purchases") confirms the email is aimed at a loyalty member.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. A 12-piece gift-with-purchase module, a "GO ALL IN" stack-your-coupon banner and four near-identical "up to 30% off" app-only tiles (hair, skin, makeup, nails) all compete with the hero. The app-only tiles are the most confusing part. A reader just told "20% off, code SAVE20, online" is then shown "ONLY IN THE APP, Ends Oct 10" at a different depth and with a different deadline. After 7/26's "Lucky you!" SCORE coupon and the 8/1 "ENDS TODAY" repeat, this is a third rotation of the same 20%-off mechanic with a new code. It's competent, but there is no new idea in it.
- Verdict: strong at the top, diluted below. It will convert members who already planned to shop, and it won't create new intent.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a huge "20% off", a "TWO DAYS | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" eyebrow and the code SAVE20 in plain view.
- The loyalty acknowledgement at the top ("Marisol, earn points…") and the "Not a member? Join now!" button handle both members and non-members.
- Free shipping on any $35 purchase sits in the top banner, which lowers friction.
- The "free 12 piece gift" module with a "choose from 2" option adds a tangible, visual incentive. The products are well photographed.
- The 2-day urgency is credible and matches the subject line.

## What's weak

- The hero has no product imagery. It's text on orange, so the email opens without any beauty product in it.
- The offer stack is confusing. There is a 20% off code, a GWP at $80, "up to 30% off" in-app tiles that end Oct 10, and a "GO ALL IN" stacking message, all with different deadlines and conditions.
- The four "up to 30% off" tiles are visually identical and have generic "Shop all" CTAs. The email becomes a long scroll with a lot of repetition.
- The fine print is heavy and exclusion-laden (fragrance, CHANEL, gift cards). "Includes almost everything!" is followed immediately by an exclusion line, which weakens trust.
- The email is very long. The credit-card and app-download modules and the dense legal text at the bottom add noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero product or a small curated strip (a bestseller grid) directly under or beside the "20% off" block, so the offer comes with something to click.
- 2. Split the stacked offers. Either lead with 20% off plus code and drop the in-app 30% tiles, or send the app promo separately. At minimum, label the app-only deadline clearly relative to the code deadline.
- 3. Replace the four repeated tiles with two or three differentiated modules, each with a specific CTA ("Shop hair tools", "Shop skincare") instead of "Shop all".
- 4. Move the exclusions into the hero as one short line ("Excludes fragrance & CHANEL") and drop "almost everything" from the subject.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings or "bestseller" badges, on the product tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Marisol, 20% off ends Thursday — code SAVE20 🤩`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days left: 20% off for Ulta Rewards members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free 12-piece gift with $80 and free shipping at $35.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member code works on skin, hair & makeup. Ends in 2 days.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is a clean, loud member-only 20% off push, and the hero does its job. A burnt-orange block with "20% off" in giant type and "TWO DAYS | MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" above it makes the offer and deadline unmissable. The code (SAVE20) is shown on the hero, so there's no hunting for it. The top banner ("Marisol, earn points that turn into $-off future purchases") confirms the email is aimed at a loyalty member.

Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. Verdict: strong at the top, diluted below. It will convert members who already planned to shop, and it won't create new intent.
