# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta is leading hard with wellness right now — that LED mask hero screams "self-care millennial" and I'm genuinely here for it. The Rewards popup jumping out at me the second I land is very on-brand for a Diamond member who lives for the points game. It's a clean, focused push toward their wellness line with an urgency play at the bottom, though the popup physically blocking half the hero copy is a little chaotic on mobile.

## What's working

- **Rewards popup** — "Ulta Beauty Rewards® are waiting" with the three-point benefit breakdown ($5, Birthday Perks, Points = $ off) is an instant grab for a Diamond member. I already know exactly what I'm getting.
- **LED mask hero** — whoever approved that visual knew their audience. A red LED face mask with AG1 and skincare stacked in front of it is textbook 2026 beauty content. I've literally posted about LED masks.
- **"Beauty in 2 hours or less"** — that top banner matters to me. I use BOPIS constantly. Seeing same-day pickup confirmed immediately reduces any friction about whether I should just drive to the store.
- **"Ends May 16" urgency** — today is May 16. That's not fake urgency, that's a real deadline and it creates actual pressure to tap now.
- **"Today's deals"** section loading into view at the fold break — the 20% off card is visible and I'm already curious what's included.

## What's weak

- **Hero copy is buried** — the popup physically covers the main value prop. I can read "on wellness and self-care brands. Excludes UB Marketplace" but not what percent off or what the actual Earn hook is. I shouldn't have to close a modal to understand the homepage deal.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I know Ulta does free shipping at $35 but it's nowhere on screen. As a mobile shopper building a cart, I want that number front and center.
- **Model is masked** — literally. The face in the hero is completely hidden by the LED mask, so there's zero demographic resonance or aspirational "that could be me" moment. Love the product, miss the person.
- **No new arrivals or prestige-brand spotlight** — as a Diamond member who tracks launches, I want to see what dropped this week. The deals rail is great but it doesn't serve the discovery mindset.
- **$5\* asterisk** — that star is doing a lot of work and there's no fine print visible to explain it. Feels like a bait-and-switch setup even if it isn't one.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the popup to a slim rewards banner** rather than a full modal overlay — let the hero breathe. Keep the "Sign in" CTA but put it in a persistent strip at the top, not blocking the main message.
- **Add the free-shipping threshold** ("Free shipping on $35+") to the utility bar alongside the BOPIS callout — one bar, two perks, zero extra scrolling.
- **Swap the anonymous masked model** for a lifestyle shot where the LED mask is being held or worn by someone whose face and energy are visible — give me someone to project onto.
- **Add a "New at Ulta" horizontal scroll rail** above Today's Deals — even four or five new-arrival tiles with brand names would immediately scratch the discovery itch for high-frequency Diamond shoppers.
