# A little thank you, from us 🫶 Get $10 off your $50 qualifying purchase (Terms & excl. apply)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a Thanksgiving-day thank-you coupon, and it is weaker than the 11/1 tiered send. The hero is a clean "$10 off your qualifying $50" on pink. It carries a barcode, an in-store versus online code split (THANKS), and a deadline of Nov 28 at 11:59pm CT. The single offer is easy to read, and "Shop now" is clear.
- Everything below the hero competes with it. A second Black Friday "up to 50% off" banner, a member-exclusive robe and throw, a free 7-piece bag with a $75 purchase, a sweepstakes, a credit card pitch and an app download all follow. The "thank you" framing is warm, but the ask is a shallow $10 off $50, down from the $10/$15/$20 tiers Ulta ran in early November. It also arrives right after the 11/22 Black Friday sends, so the cadence feels heavy.
- Verdict: a clear hero on a cluttered, long email. It's a decent send that tries to do too much.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. A giant "$10 off" sits on pink with a visible "Shop now" button.
- The deadline is credible. It reads "Ends Saturday, November 28 at 11:59pm CT."
- The online and in-store redemption codes are both shown, with a barcode for store use.
- The free-shipping bar and the Ulta Beauty Rewards banner reinforce member value at the top.
- The "a thank you from Ulta Beauty" script header gives the email a warm, seasonal brand voice.

## What's weak

- The $10 off $50 threshold is a step down from the $10/$15/$20 tiers Ulta ran on 11/1, 11/4 and 11/8, so it feels like a smaller reward.
- The Black Friday 50% off banner mid-email undercuts the coupon. It's unclear which deal to chase or whether they stack.
- There are too many competing modules: the robe and throw, the free bag, the sweepstakes, the credit card, the app and the loyalty promo. No single product is hero-linked.
- The product grid is unlabeled. It has no prices, names or reviews.
- The legal block is very long and dense, and the "^" and "**" caveats sit right next to the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle modules. Keep the coupon, one Black Friday tie-in and one gift-with-purchase. That would lift this email most.
- 2. Label the product tiles with a name, price and "after $10 off" math, so shoppers can see what $50 buys.
- 3. Reintroduce the tiers, or at least add a "$15 off $75" upsell line, so the offer isn't a downgrade.
- 4. Say plainly whether the coupon stacks with Black Friday pricing.
- 5. Add a second "Shop now" button after the product grid.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 off $50 code: THANKS 🫶 ends Nov 28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code THANKS online or scan in store. Stacks with Black Friday deals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: XHTML doctype, `lang="en-US"`, viewport meta, and MSO/VML fallbacks. It has 57% QA pass rate, driven mainly by about 30 missing-alt images and 12 non-HTTPS third-party image requests. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path, so they aren't confirmed defects. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and CTA hrefs were not available to verify.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are **unverified**, not clean.
- **Non-HTTPS image requests (mixed content).** Eleven are plain `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1` through `pub10.php`, which are 10 third-party cookie-sync pixels.
  - `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7Imei…`, which is the Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content call.
  - Clients that block or upgrade HTTP requests may drop these. A dropped `LiveContent.aspx` request could leave a content block empty.
- **The Dotomi pixels are an ad-tech cookie-sync chain** (`cookieredir`) embedded in the email body. It adds 10 external requests per open and extends load time. Many clients and privacy proxies strip them, so they are unreliable as well as heavy.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=165147` is served over HTTPS, but it has no alt attribute.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**. Ulta sends through Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`exct.net`), which typically sets these headers. Check the raw `.eml` before treating it as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown because the relay does not expose it. The sender is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com`, with images on `image.e.ulta.com`. The sending domain is aligned with the brand, which is a good sign, but nothing was verified.
- **Footer and physical address:** the unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated HTML. I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance.
- The subject includes an emoji (🫶) and "(Terms & excl. apply)", which is a disclosure cue. No technical issue was found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing URLs can't be assessed. All 57 tracked links are click-redirect URLs that were not resolved, and no final destination URLs are in the data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is in the tracking pixel's data: `pr_fy26wk43_thursday_broad_10off50_10am_pz_last` (job ID `32648`). It is consistent with the "$10 off $50" subject and suggests a 10am send slot.
- To verify continuity, follow one or two redirects and check that the landing page carries the offer and any promo code.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Treat the header warnings as capture gaps.** Pull the raw `.eml` from the source inbox and check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Fix the capture path so these warnings stop firing on every email.
2. **Resolve a sample of the 57 tracked links**, especially the primary CTA and the unsubscribe link. Record the final URL and UTM parameters to complete the continuity check.
3. **Add alt text** to the eight content image URLs listed in section 3. In the QA check, treat known tracking and pixel domains (`dotomi.com`, `emltrk.com`, `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/`, `exct.net`) as decorative and exclude them from the missing-alt count. That should remove about 17 of roughly 30 alt warnings.
4. **Flag non-HTTPS images by whether they are content or pixels.** The `LiveContent.aspx` one is the most important, because it likely loads visible content.
5. **Fix the truncation.** Capture the full HTML (at minimum the footer and CTA hrefs) so the compliance and link checks are not left unverified.
