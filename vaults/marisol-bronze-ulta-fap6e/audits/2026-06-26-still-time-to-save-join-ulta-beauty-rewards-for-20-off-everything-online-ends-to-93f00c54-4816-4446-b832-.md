# Still time to save 💸️ Join Ulta Beauty Rewards® for 20% off everything online⁺⁺—ends tonight! Use it again (Terms & excl. apply)
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a deadline-reminder send for the same 20%-off-everything promotion Ulta launched on June 23rd. The creative is nearly identical — same pink-gradient hero, same product grid, same Ulta Rewards footer — but the subject punches in last-chance urgency ("ends tonight!") and tacks on a secondary GWP sweetener: a free 7-piece beauty bag buried about two-thirds down the email. That GWP is actually the most interesting thing about this send; it's new value on top of an already-known offer and could move fence-sitters.
- The execution is mechanically clean. Products load, prices render, no obvious layout breaks. But the subject line is a 140-character run-on that will be almost entirely invisible on mobile — the urgency lives in characters 1–20 and everything after "20% off everything online" is dead weight on a phone screen. "Use it again" is genuinely confusing: does it mean returning shoppers can re-use the discount? Or is it just poor phrasing for "reminder"? Either way it muddies what should be a crystal-clear deadline send.
- Bottom line: this is a solid reminder that will convert loyalists who saw the June 23rd email and didn't pull the trigger. It won't win any new converts because nothing here surprises — it's the same offer they already decided to skip once.

## What's working

- **Hero offer is immediately legible** — the 20% off banner reads in under two seconds at the top of the render; no scrolling required to see the deal.
- **GWP sweetener adds genuine new urgency** — the free 7-piece beauty bag is incremental value not present in the June 23rd send; well-placed as a last-push incentive.
- **Product grid with before/after pricing** — sale prices clearly displayed alongside originals; easy to assess value without clicking through.
- **Deadline language is unambiguous in the subject** — "ends tonight!" is the right nudge for a re-engagement send, assuming it renders before truncation.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem-ipsum artifacts visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **Subject is ~140 characters — guaranteed mobile truncation.** Most inboxes cut off around 40–60 chars. The urgency ("ends tonight!") and offer (20% off) are front-loaded but everything from "Use it again" onward is invisible on most phones.
- **"Use it again" is ambiguous and slightly misleading** — it implies the offer can be reused (like a second transaction), but that's not what it means; it reads as a mistake or a bait-and-switch implication.
- **GWP offer is buried** — the free 7-piece beauty bag, which is the genuinely new hook in this send, doesn't appear in the subject line at all. It's treated as an afterthought instead of the lede.
- **No single hero product** — the product grid is a browse experience, not a conversion one. There's nothing to click toward; the email ends with "shop everything" energy rather than "buy this specific thing tonight."
- **Subject and creative are near-identical to the June 23rd send** — subscribers who dismissed that email get almost no reason to reconsider except the urgency timestamp.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the GWP in the subject** — the free 7-piece bag is new information; make it the hook. The 20% off is already known; the gift is the reason to act now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free 7-piece beauty bag + 20% off ends tonight 💸`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 20% off + a free beauty bag if you go tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stacks with 3,000+ sale items — your order ships with a gift`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ulta Rewards perk expires at midnight. One click to claim both.`
- 2. **Kill "Use it again."** If the intent is to encourage a second purchase from existing buyers, say that explicitly ("Bought once? You can use 20% off again tonight."). If it's just an awkward reminder, delete the phrase entirely.
- 3. **Surface the GWP hero above the product grid** — move the 7-piece bag image to immediately below the 20% off banner so the email has two reasons to buy visible in the first scroll stop.
- 4. **Cut the subject to under 60 chars** — front-load offer + deadline, strip the legal line from the subject entirely (move terms to fine print where they belong).
- 5. **Add one curated "tonight only" hero product** — pick a single aspirational item from the sale grid and feature it above the fold as a tap-through anchor. One product beats a wall of SKUs for click conversion on deadline sends.
