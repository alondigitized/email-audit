# Ends tonight: Take 20% off your online purchase⁺ when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards® for free (terms & excl. apply) 🫶
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta's last-call version of the member 20% off that ran in the 10/6 send ("Members, 2 DAYS!"). It adds a free mystery gift with a $10 purchase and a grid of app-only brand deals. The offer is strong and the deadline is credible. But the email has three stacked heroes: the mystery gift, "PEAK SAVINGS", and the big 20% off block. They compete, so the eye doesn't know where to land.
- The body is long and repetitive. Seven or eight orange tiles run down the page: Dyson, Color Wow, Tula, Osea, Peter Thomas Roth, OPI, e.l.f., and Dime. Each is tagged "Only in the app | Ends Oct 10" with its own percentage. That is a useful brand-led browse, but it pushes the 20% promise (labeled "Ends tonight") far from the "Ends Oct 10" tiles. It also gives mixed signals about what ends tonight and what doesn't. The fine print is dense, and the subject itself says "terms & excl. apply".
- Judgement: a solid send for a beauty shopper, undermined by cadence. It's the second 20% off message in two days, and it asks a lot of reading to get to a single clear action.

## What's working

- The "Big time beauty savings / 20% off your online purchase" block is large and unmistakable, with an "Ends tonight | Member exclusive" tag above it.
- The free mystery gift hero at the top is a fresh hook and gives lapsed members a reason to look.
- The brand tiles lead with recognizable names and a bold percentage each, so they are easy to skim.
- Consistent orange and pink blocks keep the brand look intact, and I saw no broken images or overlapping text.
- "Not a member? Join now!" gives non-members a clear way in.

## What's weak

- Three competing hero messages (mystery gift, "Peak Savings", 20% off) dilute the main offer.
- The "Ends tonight" urgency clashes with the "Ends Oct 10" tags on every tile below it.
- The tile section is very long and repetitive, and every tile carries a similar "Shop now" style link instead of a distinct product.
- The fine print and terms are dense and sit directly above the footer.
- This is the second near-identical 20% off message in two days, after 10/6, which makes the "ends tonight" urgency less believable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero, the 20% off member offer with a single "Join & save" button, and move the mystery gift into a smaller secondary strip.
- 2. Align the deadlines. Either say "ends tonight" everywhere, or separate the "today only" coupon from the "through Oct 10" app deals.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4 tiles and show a real product and price on each ("$X after 30% off") instead of just a brand and percentage.
- 4. Give the app deals a single "Get the app" CTA rather than repeating the in-app tag eight times.
- 5. Shorten the subject, drop the parenthetical, and keep one emoji at most.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. Plus 30% off Tula, e.l.f. and more in the app through Oct 10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupon expires at midnight, and the mystery gift is waiting at $10.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Ulta Beauty, "Ends tonight: Take 20% off…"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with dark-mode meta tags and a Movable Ink dynamic-image layer. QA found 0 hard failures. It did find 10 non-HTTPS third-party pixels, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and header checks that were inconclusive because of the relay. The captured HTML is truncated, so the body, footer, and href attributes could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and link health are unverified.
- **10 `http://` pixels from `ads.dotomi.com`.** These are cookie-sync beacons (`/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`).
  - Clients that proxy or block insecure content (Gmail image proxy, Apple Mail with Mail Privacy Protection) will likely drop them.
  - They are a third-party tracking surface, and 10 sequential redirect pixels add load.
  - They may trigger mixed-content or privacy warnings in some clients.
- The ESP open pixel is `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=162237`. The QA output ties it to the campaign label `pr_fy26wk36_wednesday_20off_dedicated_12pm_rem`. It is served over HTTPS and is not an issue by itself.
- The Movable Ink URLs (`mi.ulta.com/p/rp/…?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=162237`) carry a subscriber-level identifier, `mi_u`. This is expected for personalized dynamic content. If the image fails to load, nothing falls back in its place (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 8 image-hosted assets:**
  - Six are on `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/44|45/`: `08964789-…png`, `414d3e76-…jpg`, `4f7c3146-…gif` (flagged twice), `f6048146-…png`, and `70c7f19b-…png`.
  - Two are Movable Ink images on `mi.ulta.com`: `01fa2fab9849e714.png` and `3eb758650b65389e.png`.
  - If any of these carry content such as offer text, CTAs, or product images, then images-off rendering and screen readers lose that information. Decorative or spacer images should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
  - The Movable Ink images are the highest risk. They are dynamic content with no text fallback.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels** (`pub1–10.php` and the `emltrk.com` pixel). This is low impact because they are hidden 1×1 beacons. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would still be cleaner.
- **Markup validity.** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">`, `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" …>`, and the Google Fonts `<link>` are not self-closed. The other `<meta>` tags are self-closed, so the template is inconsistent. This is minor and clients tolerate it.
- **Web font dependency.** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…` is a `<link>` in `<head>`. Gmail and most Outlook versions strip it, so those clients fall back to the CSS font stack. I could not confirm that a fallback stack exists in the truncated source.
- Dark-mode support is declared (`color-scheme` meta plus `:root { color-scheme: light dark }`). I could not check for `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules or image treatments in the truncated source.
- The hamburger-menu CSS is commented out, which is correct. The store-dropdown checkbox hack (`#mobile-label-store`, `#menu-wrapper-store`) is client-limited, and it can't be validated from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject line.
- The truncated HTML means this can't be fully confirmed. Personalization appears to run through Movable Ink (`mi_u=203057427`).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown.
- QA itself says all three may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay stripping headers. They are inconclusive rather than confirmed defects. Because Ulta is a large bulk sender, these headers are very likely present at origin. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from senders at this volume.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the footer is outside the truncated HTML.
- The sender is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain. That is consistent with proper authentication alignment, but it is not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page destinations **could not be assessed**. All 42 links route through tracking redirects that were skipped, and the hrefs are not in the truncated source.
- The offer is gated on joining Rewards. Whether the landing page applies the 20% offer, or shows the same terms and exclusions, needs a manual click-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture the raw `.eml` headers, including `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`, from a direct inbox or seed-list delivery rather than the relay. Then re-run the compliance and authentication checks.
2. Add `alt` text to the content images. Use `alt=""` for decorative images. Give the Movable Ink images a meaningful fallback `alt` and fallback image.
3. Move the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` beacons to HTTPS, or drop them. If the vendor requires HTTP, review whether the beacons are worth the load.
4. Self-close the `<meta>` and `<link>` tags to match the XHTML Strict doctype, and confirm a web-safe fallback font stack for DM Sans.
5. Resolve the 42 tracking links in a follow-up run with redirect-following. This would confirm final destinations, UTM or campaign parameters, and landing-page consistency.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the dark-mode CSS.
