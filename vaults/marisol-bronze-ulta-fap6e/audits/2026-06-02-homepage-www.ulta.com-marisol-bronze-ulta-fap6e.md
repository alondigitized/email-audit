# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Ulta knows exactly who their customer is and isn't shy about it — the second this loaded, a Rewards modal jumped in front of my face like "hey girl, sign in." Behind it, a bright orange summer hero with sun-care products and "switch up your stash" energy tells me it's BOGO season. This feels like a brand that genuinely lives in the beauty obsessive's world, but the modal-over-hero stack makes it a slightly chaotic first impression.

## What's working

- **Rewards modal placement:** As an Ultamate Diamond, seeing "Ulta Beauty Rewards® are waiting" + Birthday Perks + Points = $ off is instant recognition. This is a smart returning-user hook — it assumes I have an account.
- **"Switch up your stash" hero copy:** Casual, beauty-fluent, millennial. It sounds like how I actually talk about my routine. Not corporate.
- **Summer sun-care product selection:** Sun Bum SPF 50, what looks like Sol de Janeiro Body Butter, a tanning lotion — these are the exact products I'd be comparing right now for my summer haul. Very timely.
- **BOGO mix & match framing:** "Mix & match 35+ brands" tells me I can shop across prestige and mass under one deal, which is literally my whole thing.
- **Top utility bar:** "Beauty in 2 hours or less—order online, pick up in store" is genuinely useful as an Ulta loyalist who uses BOPIS constantly.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the hero offer:** I can only read "BO..." of what is probably "BOGO." The full headline is hidden. I'm having to guess at the deal, which shouldn't happen.
- **No model/person visible:** The hero is product-forward only. No one who looks like me — no lifestyle shot, no "real person doing summer things." It feels more like a product catalog page than a campaign.
- **No free-shipping threshold shown:** I know it's $35, but new visitors and lapsed shoppers won't. The top bar mentions BOPIS but skips the shipping hook entirely.
- **No urgency on the BOGO:** No end date, no countdown, no "this weekend only" — for a promotion of this scale, the lack of deadline means I'm not in any rush.
- **Zero trust signals:** No review stars, no "top rated," no "x,xxx sold" — nothing that makes me want to tap into a specific product right now.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the Rewards modal to a sticky banner or bottom sheet** so the hero offer is fully readable; the modal covering "BOGO" is leaving conversion on the table for signed-in users.
- **Add a lifestyle shot to the hero** — someone like me at the beach or doing a shelfie — rather than floating product bottles; it converts "here are products" into "this is your summer."
- **Surface the $35 free-shipping threshold in the top bar** alongside the BOPIS callout; put both benefits in one line: "Free shipping $35+ • Beauty in 2 hours or less."
- **Add an end-date or urgency label to the BOGO banner** ("Ends June 8") — Ulta shoppers respond hard to 21-days-of-beauty-style deadlines, and the absence of one here is a missed lever.
