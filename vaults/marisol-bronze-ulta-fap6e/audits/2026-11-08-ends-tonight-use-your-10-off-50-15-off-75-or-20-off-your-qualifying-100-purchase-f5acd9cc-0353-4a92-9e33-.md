# Ends tonight! Use your $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off your qualifying $100 purchase⁺⁺ with code GIFTS! (terms & excl. apply) 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta's third send of the $10/$15/$20 tiered coupon in eight days, after the 11/1 "Coupon alert!" and 11/4 "bestsellers" versions. This one is the last-call version. The hero is strong. Three huge pink numerals ($10 / $15 / $20) sit on a gift-wrapped backdrop with an "ends today" badge. A barcode, the code GIFTS and the deadline (Nov 8, 11:59pm CT) sit right underneath. The coupon is hard to misread.
- The body below the hero is weaker. "A little beauty matchmaking" promises personalized picks, but the grid repeats the Shark SpeedStyle dryer twice and mixes unrelated categories (hair tools, skincare, fragrance, makeup). It reads as a generic deal grid, not as picks made for me. The free 17-piece bag with a $90 purchase is a good extra, but it sits too far down to help the click.
- Verdict: a competent, urgent send with a clear offer. The fatigue from the repeated coupon and the duplicated product tile cap it at a solid 6-7.

## What's working

- The hero states the three coupon tiers in giant type, so the offer is clear at a glance.
- The "ends today" badge and the "Offer ends November 8" line give a credible deadline.
- The barcode and the online code GIFTS cover both in-store and online redemption.
- The product tiles show visible percent-off badges (30–40%), so the coupon looks stackable with sale prices.
- The "free 17 piece beauty bag" gift-with-purchase adds a second incentive near the bottom.

## What's weak

- The Shark SpeedStyle dryer appears twice, in nearly identical tiles, which looks sloppy and undercuts the "personalized" claim.
- The 11/1, 11/4 and 11/8 sends repeat the same coupon, so the cadence feels heavy and the urgency is diluted.
- The subject runs very long, and the "terms & excl. apply" and ⁺⁺ clutter bury the hook.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "exclusions apply" caveat sits next to a headline promising "prestige brands & fragrance".
- The Rewards join banner at the top competes with the hero offer for attention.

- 2. Move the free-bag gift-with-purchase up beside the hero, since it is the strongest second hook.
- 3. Cut the subject to the offer and the deadline. Drop the ⁺⁺ and the parenthetical terms.
- 4. Vary the creative or the offer across sends within the same week.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: the QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings. It does have widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 35 images flagged) and 10 third-party `http://` pixels. The HTML was truncated and the 41 click-tracking links were not probed, so unsubscribe, footer and link-destination checks are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** all 41 click-tracking links were skipped as tracking/redirect domains. Destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 pixels load over plain HTTP, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`. They may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling. All 10 are also missing `alt`.
- **Open pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=164071` is missing `alt`. It is a tracking pixel, so this is low impact.
- **Empty query parameters:** the `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs carry `multiplier=&memberTier=&filter_type=&filter_v…`. The empty values suggest the dynamic content rendered without populated parameters (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content-bearing images:** about 20 images on `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/...` and `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/...` have no `alt`. Examples:

  - `414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif`, flagged twice, so it is probably used twice
  - `c2df4a7d-…jpg`, `2fdb2996-…jpg`, `b1d74144-…jpg`, `7914ea59-…jpg`
  - `852db034-…png`, also flagged twice
  - `01fa2fab9849e714.png`, `3eb758650b65389e.png`, `a181e331c58479fa.png`, `618b4b9854d3933a.png`, `7f478bafdf9cd3cd.png`

  With images off or a screen reader, any promo content carried only by these images is lost. The code-and-offer details are the main risk.
- **Doctype and markup mismatch:** the doctype is `XHTML 1.0 Strict`, but the head has unclosed void elements (`<meta name="color-scheme" ...>`, `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" ...>`, `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">`). This is invalid XHTML. Most clients are lenient, so impact is low.
- **Web font:** the `fonts.googleapis.com` DM Sans link is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Confirm that a font fallback stack exists in the inline styles. The truncated source didn't show one.
- **No issues found:** viewport meta, `lang="en-US"`, Outlook VML/OfficeDocumentSettings, and dark-mode `color-scheme` declarations are present.
