# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so the first thing I see is a Rewards popup sitting right on top of the hero — Ulta is BEGGING me to sign in before I've even seen what they're selling this week. Underneath it there's a fragrance launch called "Be Her," exclusive to Ulta, and below that a "Deals for you" section that has me curious. This feels like my store — beauty-first, loyalty-obsessed, always dropping something exclusive — but the popup is doing the most right now.

## What's working

- **"Be Her" exclusive launch**: "only at Ulta, get it before it sells out" — the scarcity + exclusivity combo hits. I'm always here for things I can only get at Ulta, and this makes me want to tap "Learn more" once I close the popup.
- **BOPIS strip at the very top**: "Beauty in 2 hours or less. Order online, pick up in store." — I shop both mobile and in-store, and knowing I can order now and grab it today is genuinely useful to me.
- **Rewards popup language**: "Rewards are WAITING" — that phrasing makes me feel like there's money on the table I'm leaving behind. Even as a Diamond, it creates urgency around my account.
- **"Deals for you / 12 items"**: personalized deal language below the fold teases something worth scrolling for.

## What's weak

- **Popup blocks the hero entirely**: I literally cannot see the "Be Her" campaign properly because the Rewards modal sits right on top of it. I'm an existing Diamond — why am I being served a new-member acquisition popup? This is the wrong audience targeting.
- **"$5 just for joining" CTA for an existing member**: as someone who is already Diamond, this offer is irrelevant. It makes the page feel like it doesn't know who I am despite having my data.
- **"Find a store" as the hero CTA**: I'm on my phone shopping online. "Find a store" is not where I'm headed right now. A "Shop the collection" or "Add to cart" CTA would actually convert.
- **No price, no ratings, no reviews visible**: the fragrance launch gives me zero info on cost or what other shoppers think. As someone who compares dupes vs. originals, I need at least a price anchor to decide if "Be Her" is worth the tap.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the Rewards popup for signed-in Diamond members** — replace it with a personalized banner ("Welcome back, Marisol — you have X points") so the hero gets full screen real estate and loyalty-engaged users don't see a new-member pitch.
- **Swap "Find a store" for "Shop Be Her" on the hero CTA** — mobile shoppers need a direct path to the product page, not a store locator.
- **Add a price or "from $XX" badge to the hero product** — even one line anchors the purchase decision and helps the dupe-comparison crowd (me) evaluate immediately.
- **Surface a "21 Days of Beauty" or active event banner** if one is running — that's a signature Ulta moment that drives my visits and is the kind of thing I look for first when I land on the homepage.
