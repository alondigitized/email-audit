# 🤑 Use your $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off your qualifying $100 purchase⁺⁺ on these bestsellers! (terms & excl. apply)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta's tiered-coupon send for the second time in four days. It follows the 11/1 "Coupon alert! $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off $100" email. The hero is clear: three large pink numerals ($10 / $15 / $20) sit in a white card over a gift-wrapped backdrop, with a barcode and a deadline of November 8 at 11:59pm CT. The offer is easy to read. The "It's personal" block and the 8-tile bestseller grid, each with a "+ free gift**" banner, then carry the offer into products.
- The body is where it falls short. The "Top sellers handpicked for you" line has no visible personalization behind it, because the grid is generic bestsellers across foundation, lipstick and mascara. "Marisol" appears only in the Rewards strip. The footer has a large empty white gap with orphaned "ulta.com" and "click here" links floating in it, which looks like a rendering miss. The email is a competent re-run of a coupon the subscriber got three days ago, so it is more repetitive than compelling.
- Judgement: solid and clear, but low on novelty. It is worth about a 6.

## What's working

- The $10 / $15 / $20 tier stack is the largest type in the email and the eye lands on it first.
- The deadline (Nov 8, 11:59pm CT) and the barcode make the coupon feel usable in store.
- The "yep, includes prestige brands & fragrance!" line removes the usual worry that the coupon excludes premium products.
- The product grid is clean, with a consistent card layout, brand name, price and "Shop now" on each tile.
- The "+ free gift**" banners on the product cards add a second incentive on top of the coupon.

## What's weak

- Near-identical to the 11/1 coupon send, so the cadence feels repetitive.
- A large blank white gap near the bottom holds only stray "ulta.com" and "click here" links. It looks broken.
- The "It's personal / handpicked for you" claim isn't backed by anything visible. The only personal touch is the name in the Rewards strip.
- The Rewards join banner sits above the hero and competes with the coupon, and the "free gift**" and "++" asterisks bury the terms.
- There is no star rating or review count on any product, so the grid has no social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the footer. Remove the blank gap and the orphaned links so the email ends cleanly.
- 2. Differentiate from the 11/1 send. Lead with "Your coupon is still live", or change the creative, rather than resending the same hero.
- 3. Make "handpicked" real. Base the tiles on the recipient's browsing or purchase history, or drop the claim.
- 4. Add ratings or "bestseller" badges to the tiles for social proof.
- 5. Spell out what the "free gift" requires, for example "Free gift with $X". Right now it is just a double asterisk.
- 6. Move the Rewards join banner below the coupon hero so the offer comes first.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Prestige and fragrance included. Free gift with select picks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a mostly well-formed XHTML 1.0 Strict, table-based send with `lang`, viewport and dark-mode meta tags. The QA run flagged many images with no alt text, 11 images served over plain `http://`, and three header checks it couldn't confirm (QA pass rate 57%, 0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 42 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`.
  - 10 are Dotomi cookie-sync pixels: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`.
  - 1 is the Salesforce Marketing Cloud pixel `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…`.
  - Mail clients and image proxies may block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, so these pixels may not fire.
- **Third-party calls:** The Dotomi calls are ad-network cookie syncs, not first-party open tracking. They add ten third-party requests per open and expand the privacy surface.
- **Other pixels:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=163808` and `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=163808` are HTTPS, so they are fine on transport.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA run reported 24 warnings (about 22 unique images) with no alt text.
  - **Content images** on `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/…`: `08964789-…png`, `414d3e76-…jpg`, `4f7c3146-…gif` (flagged twice), `a103b506-…jpg`, `d99333f4-…jpg`, `925e52e3-…jpg`, `32415bbe-…jpg`, and `852db034-…png` (flagged twice). Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description for these.
  - **Tracking pixels** (Dotomi, `exct.net`, `emltrk.com`, `mi.ulta.com`): these should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Invalid XHTML:** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but `<meta name="color-scheme" …>` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" …>` are not self-closed, unlike the neighboring `<meta … />` tags. This is minor and clients tolerate it.
- **Font link:** The `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook, so a fallback font stack is needed. The `.helvetica` class suggests one exists, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **No other defects:** The viewport meta, `lang="en-US"`, MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` and Apple data-detector overrides are present. The HTML is truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw message headers from the original sender.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Confirm it in the original delivery headers, not the relay copy.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 42 click links go through tracking redirects, and the probe skipped them. UTM parameters and landing pages can't be confirmed from the truncated source. The only campaign identifier seen is the QA label `pr_fy26wk40_wednesday_broad_coupons_dedicated_12pm_pz_LAS` next to the `emltrk.com` pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all pixels over HTTPS. Switch the `exct.net` `LiveContent.aspx` pixel and the Dotomi `pub1`–`pub10` pixels, or drop the Dotomi cookie-sync calls.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the eight content image sets listed in section 3. Give every tracking pixel `alt=""`.
3. Check the original message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `e.ulta.com`.
4. Resolve a sample of the 42 click links to confirm landing pages and UTM parameters.
5. Self-close the two `color-scheme` meta tags to keep the XHTML valid.
