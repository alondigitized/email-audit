# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Ulta is clearly pushing a wellness moment right now — LED masks, supplements, self-care bundles front and center. For a Diamond member like me who lives for 21 Days of Beauty and posts prestige-vs-dupe comparisons, this is at least speaking my language, but the rewards tooltip slapped right over the hero is doing the most and making everything feel cluttered. It reads like a brand that knows its customer but couldn't quite decide between closing the sale and signing up a new one.

## What's working

- **The rewards overlay** leading with "Ulta Beauty Rewards® are waiting" is smart retention — I'm already a Diamond member so seeing that tooltip with Birthday Perks listed reminds me I have skin in this game
- **The LED face mask visual** is eye-grabbing and timely; that's exactly the kind of thing I'd screenshot for a "worth it or dupe?" reel
- **Dual CTAs** ("Activate" + "Shop now") give me a choice — sign up/activate for the deal vs. browse — without forcing a path
- **"Deals for you" rail** below the fold, with the "12 items" count, tells me there's personalization happening, which as a high-tech shopper I appreciate
- **"Beauty in 2 hours or less. Order online, pick up in store."** in the top strip is a straight-up win — BOPIS is how I shop when I need a product same-day before an event

## What's weak

- **The tooltip/overlay covers the hero** — "In st..." and "Ear..." are truncated; I can't read the actual promo copy, which means the offer that's supposed to drive the Activate CTA is invisible to me
- **No free-shipping callout** — as someone who hits $35 to unlock free shipping constantly, I want to see that threshold surfaced early, especially if I'm evaluating a wellness haul
- **No person/model in the hero** — it's all products and a disembodied face mask; a lifestyle shot with someone who looks like me would make the wellness angle land harder
- **Right-side content is cropped** — I can see "Yo", "70...", "on..." peeking from the right edge; there's apparently a second promo panel I can't read, which is just bad mobile layout
- **Zero review or rating signals** anywhere above the fold — for someone who makes purchase decisions based on star counts, that's a miss

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the rewards overlay on scroll** or move it to a sticky bottom sheet — don't let it eat the hero headline; the offer you're trying to activate should actually be readable
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold ("Free shipping on $35+") in the top strip** next to BOPIS — this is the single fastest conversion lever for a Millennial who's already mid-browse
- **Add a lifestyle model to the wellness hero** — someone in a bathrobe with the LED mask on, not just the mask floating on a gradient; I need to see myself in it
- **Fix the right-panel crop on mobile** — whatever the "70..." promo is, it should stack vertically below the main hero on a 375px viewport, not bleed off-screen
