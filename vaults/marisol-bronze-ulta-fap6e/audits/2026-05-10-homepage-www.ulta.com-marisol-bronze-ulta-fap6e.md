# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Ulta's homepage is doing the most right now — it knew I was coming before I even logged in. The loyalty popup drops immediately with the rewards pitch, and below it there's a clean "Spend $100, get $20" gift card promo that feels actually stackable with my Diamond status. It reads like a beauty-first, deal-savvy brand that respects my time, even if the hero isn't exactly speaking to my specific routine.

## What's working

- **Rewards popup** — shows up immediately with a clear checklist: unlock $5, birthday perks, points = $ off. As a Diamond member this is table stakes, but for a new visitor this actually converts. It's clean and dismissable.
- **"Spend $100, get $20" hero** — I love a stackable deal. The mechanic is legible in two seconds: spend $100 on a gift card, get a $20 eReward. The urgency tag "Online only | Ends today" adds just enough pressure without feeling scammy.
- **"Deals for you" section below the fold** — personalization signal. "12 items" tells me there's a curated feed waiting, which makes me want to scroll.
- **Free same-day delivery callout** at the very top — "$35 threshold" is my AOV on a casual run. That lands.
- **Search bar** is prominent and immediately accessible — I shop by product name and dupe comparisons constantly, so a fast search is critical.

## What's weak

- **Hero is gift-card-centric, not product-centric** — I came here to shop for myself (or compare a dupe vs. prestige), not to buy a gift card. The floral imagery and "for you" ribbon read as a gifting occasion push that doesn't match my typical session intent.
- **No prestige vs. mass contrast** anywhere above the fold — this is Ulta's whole value prop (Charlotte Tilbury next to e.l.f.) and it's completely absent from what I can see.
- **Diamond status not acknowledged** — I'm logged out apparently, or there's no personalization showing my tier. The rewards popup pitches the entry-level $5 join bonus, which feels like it's talking to someone who isn't me.
- **The partially cropped banner on the right** — there's clearly a second carousel card getting clipped. On mobile this can feel broken even if it's intentional; I can't tell what I'm missing.
- **No trending/new arrivals signal** above the fold — no "New at Ulta," no 21 Days of Beauty mention (my favorite Ulta event), nothing that says "here's what's hot right now."

## Recommendations

- **Swap the gift card hero for a 21 Days of Beauty or category-specific promo** — if there's a dupe vs. prestige deal running, that's the story Ulta should be telling me. A hero showing an e.l.f. dupe next to the Charlotte Tilbury original with a "see the comparison" CTA would stop my scroll cold.
- **Add a Diamond/loyalty tier badge or rewards balance chip** in the header for logged-in users — even a "You have 450 pts" pill next to the cart icon would make me feel seen and keep me on-site longer.
- **Make the 21 Days of Beauty event visible above the fold** if it's active (it's May, timing is right) — this is my Super Bowl and right now there's zero trace of it.
- **Unclip the carousel** or add swipe indicator dots so I know there's more — right now that half-visible card on the right edge looks like a layout issue, not an intentional carousel.
