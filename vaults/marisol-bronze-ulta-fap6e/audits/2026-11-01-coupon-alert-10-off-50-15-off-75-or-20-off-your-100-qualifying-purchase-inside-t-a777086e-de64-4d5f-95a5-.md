# Coupon alert! $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off your $100 qualifying purchase⁺⁺ inside! (Terms & excl. apply) 🤑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, tiered-coupon send. Ulta stacks "$10 off $50, $15 off $75, $20 off $100" in giant pink type inside a white card on a pink hero, and the offer is unmissable. The deadline is visible ("Offer ends November 8 at 11:59pm CT"), and the prestige and fragrance carve-in is called out. A single black "Shop now" button sits right under the barcode.
- The product grid undercuts it. Eight tiles all carry a "+ free gift**" banner, but the offer is split between the coupon above and gift-with-purchase below. The result is a foundation-heavy edit: It Cosmetics, Fenty, Clinique twice, Lancôme, NARS, with Benefit and The Ordinary mixed in. Each tile has only its own small "Shop now" link. The coupon tiers and the gifts compete for attention.
- The bottom third is clutter. "Extra, extra" community and AI tiles, in-store events, a credit card promo and an app download all sit below the offer. Near the footer, some small stray blue text links are visible. Overall it's a strong hero with a diffuse body. The coupon is the story. The "Early Black Friday" mention in the fine print is buried.

## What's working

- The tiered coupon ($10/$15/$20) in large type makes the value ladder instantly scannable.
- The deadline (Nov 8) and exclusions are stated right under the barcode, which feels honest.
- The "yep, includes prestige brands & fragrance!" line pre-empts the usual objection to Ulta coupons.
- The "Not to play favorites, but..." headline gives the product grid a playful, on-brand voice.
- The free-shipping bar at the top and the Ulta Beauty Rewards strip add member value without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The grid shows only foundation, serum and brow gel, so it isn't clear how the picks relate to the coupon tiers. No tile shows a price you'd pay after the coupon.
- Eight "+ free gift**" banners look identical and don't say what the gift is. They dilute the coupon message.
- Many secondary modules (community, AI shopping, events, credit card, app download) pile up below the offer.
- Small stray blue text links are visible near the footer, which looks like a minor render glitch.
- Only the hero has a bold CTA. The product tiles use tiny "Shop now" links, so the click targets are weak.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the lower modules to one (app download or events). Let the coupon and the products carry the email.
- 2. Show "with coupon" pricing on the tiles, e.g. "$38 → $28 with $10 off", so the grid proves the offer.
- 3. Name the gift in each tile ("free 5-pc set with $X purchase") instead of repeating the generic banner.
- 4. Add a second coupon CTA mid-way, such as "Shop $20 off $100", so the click is available when the reader reaches the products.
- 5. Fix the stray links near the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Marisol, your coupon: up to $20 off + free gifts 🤑`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $50, $75 or $100. Prestige & fragrance included, through Nov 8.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with a large third-party pixel footprint. Its main problems are about 30 images without alt text and 10 plain-HTTP `ads.dotomi.com` pixels. Compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML source was truncated before the body and footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 46 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure pixels:** 10 cookie-sync pixels load over plain HTTP: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`.
  - They may be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS.
  - They can trigger mixed-content warnings in web-based clients.
  - They add third-party ad-tech requests on open.
- **Insecure Salesforce pixel:** `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...` is also HTTP. It is a Marketing Cloud live-content image, so if it is blocked, dynamic content may fail to render.
- **Other open-tracking pixels:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=163491` and `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=163491` (plus `3eb758650b65389e.png`) are HTTPS and not defects. They add to the tracker count.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 30 `<img>` elements have no `alt`. The list below counts the flagged QA lines, which include duplicates.
  - **Content images (a real accessibility gap):** 3 `.png`/`.gif` under `/m/45/` and `/m/44/` (`08964789-…png`, `414d3e76-…jpg`, `4f7c3146-…gif`, the last flagged twice), and 6 `.jpg`/`.png` under `/m/1/` (`c2df4a7d-…`, `2fdb2996-…`, `b1d74144-…`, `7914ea59-…`, `852db034-…` flagged twice).
  - **Tracking and ad pixels:** the 10 `dotomi` pixels, `LiveContent.aspx`, `ixtw8lub` and the 2 `mi.ulta.com` images. For these, add `alt=""` and a `width`/`height` of 1, so they are treated as decorative.
- **Image-off behavior:** with images blocked, content images without alt text show nothing to screen readers or in image-off views.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans...">` sits in `<head>`. Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip external stylesheets, so those clients fall back to the font stack.
- **Doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Strict is declared, but `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" ...>` are not self-closed. This is harmless in practice.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared in `:root` and the meta tags. The visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules and uses hard-coded colors (`#ffffff`, `#000000`, `#303030`). Because of the truncation, I can't confirm whether those rules exist later in the file.
- **Dead CSS:** the hamburger-menu block is commented out. It is inert, but it adds weight.
- **Title:** `<title>Ulta Beauty </title>` has a trailing space and is generic. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The truncation hides the body, so unresolved tokens (e.g. `%%first_name%%`, `{{...}}`) cannot be ruled out. The tracking URL does show a resolved subscriber ID (`i=203057427`), and the pixel path embeds the campaign name `pr_fy26wk40_sunday_broadcoupon_PZ_10am_last`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing:** the QA check flagged it, but with the caveat that the AgentMail relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing (RFC 8058):** also unconfirmed for the same reason. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify it against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer:** the visible physical address and unsubscribe link can't be checked because the source is truncated.
- **Sender domain:** `U-Mail@e.ulta.com` is a branded subdomain, which is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be verified. All 46 CTAs are click-redirect URLs that weren't probed, so final landing URLs, UTM parameters and coupon-code carry-through are unknown. The only campaign identifier visible is the open-pixel tag `pr_fy26wk40_sunday_broadcoupon_PZ_10am_last`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text to every content image** (the `/m/1/`, `/m/44/` and `/m/45/` assets). Use `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions on all tracking and ad pixels.
2. **Move the HTTP pixels to HTTPS.** That covers `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1–10.php` and `cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx`. If the vendor has no HTTPS endpoint, drop the pixels.
3. **Check the raw message headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and read `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC alignment) directly from the original message.
4. **Resolve a sample of the 46 redirect links.** Record the final URL, status code and UTM parameters, and confirm the coupon offers land on matching pages.
5. **Re-run with the full HTML.** The body, footer, merge tokens and dark-mode CSS can't be assessed from the truncated source.
6. **Fix the QA summary.** It reports "0 issue(s), 3 warning(s)" but lists about 40 warnings. The counter looks like it only counts the compliance and deliverability sections.
