# A legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift with purchase** (Terms & excl. apply) ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Ulta's third gift-with-purchase email in four weeks — Rabanne on 5/7, YSL on 5/8, and now Charlotte Tilbury on 6/3 — and the format fatigue is starting to show. The email runs the familiar Ulta GWP playbook: prestige-brand hero, multiple product spotlights, a loyalty/rewards tail, and a "Beauty Break" seasonal wrapper at the top. Charlotte Tilbury's warm gold palette is consistent and the product photography is good quality, but the email is very long and front-loads a lot of brand story before the actual GWP threshold and CTA become clear.
- The biggest structural problem is that name personalization — which powered the 8/10 scores on the 5/21 tiered-discount and 5/8 mystery offer sends — is entirely absent here. The subject reads generically ("A legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift with purchase") while the 5/21 subject opened with "Marisol," a difference that almost certainly costs open rate. Combined with a subject line over 70 characters and no deadline urgency, this email is likely to get lost on a crowded mobile inbox.
- The Charlotte Tilbury brand alignment is its strongest asset: this is a genuinely desirable prestige brand and the GWP mechanic fits the persona well. But execution left real lift on the table.

## What's working

- Charlotte Tilbury's signature gold-and-warm-brown palette is consistent throughout; the brand identity reads immediately on scroll.
- The "Beauty Break" banner gives the send a seasonal event feel, making it feel slightly less like a routine promotional blast.
- Multiple product spotlights — mascara, face products, complexion — give different Charlotte Tilbury customers a reason to keep scrolling.
- The rewards/RECHARGE loyalty panel at the bottom reinforces membership value for Ulta Rewards members.
- No visible render bugs, broken images, or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- No name personalization in subject or hero — a clear regression from the 5/21 ("Marisol, get $10 off…") and 5/8 mystery offer sends that both used it.
- Subject is well over 50 characters and buries the most exciting word ("legendary") with vague abstraction — what exactly is legendary about the gift?
- The GWP threshold/details (what spend unlocks the gift) are not visible until deep in the scroll; the hero doesn't lead with the number.
- This is the third GWP email in four weeks; the formula is now recognizable enough to feel like template, not campaign.
- No credible deadline or urgency signal visible in the subject, preheader, or hero — unlike the 5/25 send that explicitly said "ends today."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Restore name personalization in the subject** — "Marisol, a legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift is waiting" outperforms the generic version; this is the single highest-lift change given Ulta's data from prior sends.
- 2. **Surface the spend threshold in the subject or preheader** — subscribers need to know immediately whether they're close to qualifying; a number like "$65" is the hook.
- 3. **Add a deadline** — even "this week only" or a specific date lifts urgency; without it, subscribers will defer and forget.
- 4. **Trim the subject to under 50 characters** — cut "(Terms & excl. apply) ✨" from the subject entirely; move legal to preheader or footer where it belongs.
- 5. **Lead the hero copy with the GWP threshold**, not with brand story — "Spend $65, get the Charlotte Tilbury [gift name] free" is a stronger entry than narrative copy.

## 1. Overview
