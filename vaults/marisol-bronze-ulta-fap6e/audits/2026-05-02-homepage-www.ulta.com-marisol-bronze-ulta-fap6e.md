# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Ulta is clearly in promo-mode right now — the second I landed, a rewards popup blocked the hero and a big BOPIS20 deal took up the whole screen. As a Diamond member who already lives for 21 Days of Beauty, this feels like the right energy, but the execution is a little cluttered. It reads like a brand that knows its audience shops both in-store and online, but is trying to serve all of them at once on one crowded first screen.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **Rewards popup with a real value hook** — "Unlock $5* just for joining" + Birthday Perks + Points = $ off is a tight three-line pitch. For someone who is already Diamond-tier, it's a reminder that I'm leaving money on the table if I'm not signed in.
- **BOPIS20 promo headline** — "20% off 160+ brands, pick up in store with code BOPIS20" speaks directly to my hybrid shopping habit. I literally do this — order online, grab in-store same day. The copy nails the use case.
- **Urgency cue** — "Online only | Ends May 7" on a hot pink tag is clean and scannable. I clocked it immediately.
- **Free same day delivery strip** — "$35+ threshold" is the magic number I already know. Seeing it in the top bar is a low-friction reassurance.
- **"Deals for you / 12 items"** — Personalized section below the fold signals the app knows me. Even seeing the label makes me want to scroll.

## What's weak

- **The rewards popup fires on top of the hero** — I haven't even seen the full homepage and I'm already managing a modal. It covers whatever's behind it and creates visual competition with the BOPIS banner below.
- **The carousel is clipped** — There's clearly a second card to the right ("Fr...") that I can't read. On mobile, partial cards work if they're obviously swipeable, but there's no visual affordance here — it just looks cut off.
- **"Shop now" CTA is too generic** — With a specific 20%-off-160-brands deal on the table, the button should say something like "Shop BOPIS20 Deals" to reinforce the offer I'm about to tap into.
- **No product imagery in the hero** — The hero card has ULTA orange bags and some delivery icons but no actual beauty products. For a beauty retailer this feels like a missed shot — show me a serum, a palette, a lip combo.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the rewards popup on scroll or delay it 3 seconds** — Let me see the hero first. Then surface the loyalty nudge once I've engaged with the page. Or fold the rewards pitch into the nav bar ("Hi Marisol, 2,450 pts" style).
- **Add a product thumbnail strip inside the BOPIS hero** — Swap the orange shopping bag illustration for 4–5 actual product images (a YSL lip, a Fenty foundation, a Dyson brush) so I can see what 160+ brands actually means at a glance.
- **Label the carousel dots or add a "2 of 4" pill** — Make it obvious there are more deals to swipe through so the clipped card feels intentional, not broken.
- **Rename the CTA to match the offer** — "Shop 20% Off" or "Apply BOPIS20" is more specific and will convert harder than a plain "Shop now."
