# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta is clearly in full Memorial Day Weekend energy right now — code MDW, prestige + fragrance specifically called out, and a $10/$20 tiered offer that's actually generous. As a Diamond member I'm used to Ulta knowing how to speak to me, but opening the app to a full rewards modal before I can even see the hero is a lot. It reads like a beauty brand that's genuinely invested in its loyalty program — I just wish the modal wasn't the first thing eating my whole screen.

## What's working

- **The $10/$20 tiered offer** is perfectly calibrated for a Diamond member who regularly spends $50–$100 per haul — I'm already doing the math on what to add to cart
- **"Includes prestige brands & fragrance"** is doing a lot of work for me specifically — Ulta's biggest competitor differentiator is that exact combination, and they're leading with it
- **The 5X points BOPUS bar** at the very top hits a real pain point: I shop online but I also run in-store for the BA samples and testers, and getting 5X for doing both is genuinely motivating
- **The rewards modal** is well-written — Birthday Perks, Points = $ off, the "$5 just for joining" — these are real hooks that non-members would respond to, and it reminds me why I'm still Diamond
- **"Deals for you — 12 items"** below the fold signals personalization, which keeps me scrolling

## What's weak

- **The rewards modal blocks the entire hero on mobile** — I can barely read the campaign headline ("Me..." is all I see) and there's another promo card on the right that's completely cut off; the timing of this modal feels aggressive
- **"See coupon*"** — that asterisk on a CTA button is a trust ding; I'm going to wonder what the exclusions are before I tap
- **No urgency signal visible** — there's no "ends Monday" countdown or date on the Memorial Day offer; for a weekend-only deal, that's a miss
- **The right-side card ("Up..." / "Sta...") is completely illegible** due to the modal and mobile crop — if that's a second offer, I'm not getting it
- **No 21 Days of Beauty or birthday-program callout** visible — as someone who literally schedules my calendar around 21 Days, I want that surfaced immediately in May/early June context

## Recommendations

- **Delay the rewards modal by 3–5 seconds or trigger it on second page view** — let me see the hero for one beat before asking me to sign in; I'm already a member and this modal is talking past me
- **Add "Ends Monday 5/26" or a simple countdown chip** on the $10/$20 offer — a tiered Memorial Day deal without a deadline loses half its urgency
- **Surface "21 Days of Beauty" in the top bar or hero** — May is prime season for this and it's one of Ulta's most-talked-about loyalty events; if it's happening, it should be above the fold
- **Make the right-side carousel card fully visible** or stack it below the main offer on mobile — partial cards feel like a render issue even when they're intentional
