# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- OK so I open Ulta and the first thing I get is a rewards recruitment popup blocking the hero — which, as a Diamond member, feels a little "girl I'm already here, chill." Setting that aside, the vibe underneath is very much spring beauty reset: BABO, Sun Bum, warm orange palette, "switch up your stash" energy. This brand knows its audience is beauty-obsessed millennials who want deals and loyalty perks stacked together.

## What's working

- **"Earn 5X points when you buy online, pick up in store" top banner** — as someone who does both, this BOPIS bonus hits immediately and makes me want to add to cart and grab in store
- **"Switch up your stash" hero copy** — this is my exact language for updating my product rotation, not corporate-speak
- **Ulta Beauty Rewards popup** — even though it skews toward recruitment, it does surface Birthday Perks and Points = $ off, which reminds me I have currency to spend
- **Sun Bum + BABE product mix** — the shelf styling shows prestige-adjacent mass brands side by side, exactly the dupe-versus-original comparisons I live for
- **"Deals for you" section label** — personalized framing ("for you," not "sale items") feels algorithmic and curated, which I trust

## What's weak

- **Rewards popup targeting** — I'm a Diamond member, not a prospect. Showing me "Unlock $5 just for joining" when I'm already signed in (or should be auto-recognized) is tone-deaf and blocks the actual hero
- **BOGO copy is partially cut off** — the "BO—" text is truncated behind the popup; I can't see the full offer terms without closing the modal
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — I know Ulta ships free at $35 but it's not on screen; for my mobile-heavy shopping habit that matters before I decide to bundle items
- **Zero lifestyle model imagery** — the hero is all product flat-lay, no one who looks like me using the stuff; that makes it feel more catalog than editorial
- **Second promo at far right is cropped** — I can see "20[%?]" but can't read it; creates curiosity without payoff

## Recommendations

- **Swap the full-screen recruitment modal for a signed-in member banner** — if I'm a Diamond member, show me my points balance and next reward tier right in the top strip, not a "join for $5" pitch
- **Extend the hero to show a lifestyle shot** — swap one product cluster for a 30-something woman with a shelfie or bathroom counter setup; I need to see myself in this
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold ($35) in the top banner or hero subtext** — "free ship at $35" as a persistent nudge increases AOV and reduces cart abandonment for mobile shoppers
- **Make the second promo readable** — if there's a 20% off event running, give it its own banner strip below the hero instead of a cropped peek at the edge of screen
