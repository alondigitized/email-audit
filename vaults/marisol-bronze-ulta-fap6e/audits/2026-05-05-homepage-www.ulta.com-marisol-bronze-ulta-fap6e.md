# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-05

## What's working

- **The Rewards intercept popup** — as a Diamond member, seeing "Unlock $5, Birthday Perks, Points = $ off" with a sign-in CTA before I even see the hero is very targeted. It speaks directly to why I open the Ulta app first thing.
- **Prestige fragrance hero imagery** — the Prada, Dolce & Gabbana, and what looks like a YSL bottle are gorgeous and aspirational. That pink, gold, and blush palette reads luxury.
- **"Free same day delivery on select purchases $35+"** — the threshold is right in the eyeline at the very top. $35 is an AOV I hit before I even get to the fragrance counter, so this feels reachable.
- **"Deals for you"** — personalizing the deals section with "12 items" makes it feel curated rather than generic, which pulls me to scroll.

## What's weak

- **The Rewards popup covers the hero** — I can't read the earn headline or the specific offer number (I can see "Ea..." and "20..." and that's it). The most important real estate on the page is buried under a modal that's supposed to be a loyalty hook, not a blocker.
- **No visible offer specifics on the fragrance deal** — how many points am I earning? What's the multiplier? "Earn... on fragrance purchases. Exclusions apply." tells me almost nothing and the key number is hidden.
- **No human imagery** — there's no model or lifestyle shot, just bottles on a gradient. For a beauty destination that sells the *feeling* of the product, this is a missed opportunity to show someone actually wearing or experiencing the fragrance.
- **"Activate" CTA is confusing** — activate what, exactly? A deal? An offer? An account? Without being able to read the hero copy behind the popup, this button feels like I'm clicking into the void.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the Rewards popup to a dismissible banner at the bottom**, not a full center overlay — keep the hook visible without burying the hero offer. Or auto-dismiss it after 3 seconds.
- **Make the earn multiplier legible above the fold** — "Earn 2X Points on all fragrances this weekend" is a sentence that converts. Don't hide the number.
- **Add a lifestyle shot** — even a half-screen image of someone spritzing in a sunlit bathroom would add warmth and make this feel less like a product catalog and more like a beauty destination.
- **Change "Activate" to "Activate my offer"** — two extra words that turn a vague verb into something I understand without needing to read the buried headline.
