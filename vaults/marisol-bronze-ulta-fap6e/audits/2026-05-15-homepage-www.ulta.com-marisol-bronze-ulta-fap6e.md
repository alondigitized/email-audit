# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- OK so Ulta is immediately going full loyalty-mode on me the second I land — the Rewards popup is literally the first thing I read, which as a Diamond member I actually don't hate. The hero beneath it is pushing a wellness campaign (hello, AG1 and that LED mask!) which is very much my thing right now. Feels like a brand that knows I'm here for both the prestige skincare AND the self-care supplements, just wish the popup wasn't eating half the hero.

## What's working

- **Rewards popup timing**: As a Diamond member, seeing "Unlock $5, Birthday Perks, Points = $ off" immediately tells me this brand knows I'm invested. The "Sign in or join for free!" CTA is clear and low-friction.
- **Wellness hero imagery**: The LED face mask, AG1, and product cluster are genuinely relevant to where beauty is heading in 2026. I recognize that stuff and it makes me want to dig in.
- **"Deals for you" personalization label**: Even glimpsing "12 items" below the fold signals that the page knows who I am and has curated something — that's a scroll magnet.
- **"Beauty in 2 hours or less" top bar**: BOPIS is huge for me. Knowing I can order and pick up same-day removes the hesitation of "do I have time to wait for shipping?"
- **Dual CTAs**: "Activate" and "Shop now" let me choose between claiming the offer and just browsing — not forcing a single path.

## What's weak

- **Popup is eating the hero**: "Earn [X]% on wellness..." is cut off mid-sentence. I literally cannot read what the earn rate is, which is the whole point of the promotion. That's a conversion killer.
- **No human in the hero**: The product flatlay is clean but there's no lifestyle shot — no one wearing the LED mask, no one doing a morning routine. As someone who posts dupe comparisons, I want to see the *moment*, not just the products.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold**: I know Ulta ships free at $35, but I can't see it here. That's a purchase-trigger signal that's missing.
- **"Excludes UB Marketplace"** is buried: Fine print below the main offer copy — not immediately obvious before I tap "Activate."
- **No urgency signal**: Nothing says "ends Sunday" or "limited time." The earn offer floats with no deadline, so there's no reason to act now vs. later.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the overlay-blocking pattern**: Let the rewards callout live in the nav bar or as a slim persistent banner rather than a modal that covers the hero. I want to see the full earn rate on the first load.
- **Swap the product flatlay for a lifestyle moment**: A 30-something doing her nighttime routine with the LED mask on is an instant scroll-stop for me. That's a shareworthy image; the product pile isn't.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold** ($35) in the top utility bar next to the pickup CTA — "Free shipping $35+ · Pick up in 2 hours" in one line is a proven conversion pair.
- **Add a visible deadline to the wellness earn offer**: Even "Offer ends May 18" gives me the push to tap "Activate" today instead of shelving it.
