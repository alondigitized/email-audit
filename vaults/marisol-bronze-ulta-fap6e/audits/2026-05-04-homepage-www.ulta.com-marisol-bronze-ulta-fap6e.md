## What's working

- **5X fragrance points banner** — as a Diamond member who specifically compares prestige originals against dupes, this offer is perfectly timed. I want to hit Activate before I even scroll.
- **Prestige brand imagery** — Prada, YSL, and Dolce & Gabbana bottles in the hero immediately signal "this is my store." No guessing whether this is the beauty section or a drugstore circular.
- **Free same day delivery at $35** — top bar, first thing I see. I know exactly what threshold I need to hit. For me that's basically one fragrance decant.
- **"Ends May 9" deadline** — a real specific date four days from now is motivating in a way that "limited time offer" never is.
- **"Deals for you" personalized rail** — even just the header preview at the bottom of the viewport tells me the site has data on me and is trying to surface relevant items.

## What's weak

- **Rewards popup for a returning Diamond member** — the "sign in or join for free!" prompt loading on top of the hero creates friction before I even engage. If I'm already Ultamate Diamond, this is the wrong hook entirely.
- **Zero lifestyle imagery** — three perfume bottles on a pink gradient is a product shot, not an experience. A millennial who posts dupe comparisons responds to editorial and aspirational context, not flat catalog photography.
- **No visible category navigation** — hamburger and search is all I see. There's no quick-nav strip showing Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance, Hair. If I weren't already interested in the fragrance deal, I'd have no obvious next move.
- **No social proof** — no star ratings, no "bestseller" badges, no "X people favorited this" anywhere on the featured fragrance products.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the sign-in popup for a Diamond-specific welcome module** — if the session is recognized, show "Welcome back Diamond — your points balance: X. Activate your 5X offer below." Don't treat your best customers like acquisition targets.
- **Add a lifestyle vignette to the fragrance hero** — one editorial image of someone at a vanity or spritzing a fragrance lifts this from transactional to aspirational in a way that resonates with the millennial audience.
- **Drop a one-row quick-nav category strip below the hero** — tappable chips for Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance, Hair, Deals so one-tap navigation doesn't require popping the hamburger.
- **Overlay a bestseller badge or aggregate rating on the featured fragrance bottles** — even a "4.7★ (2,841)" badge on the Prada Candy shot would do real conversion work.
