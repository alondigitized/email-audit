# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually like this. Ulta opened straight into a suncare BOGO on brands I know — St. Tropez sitting next to The Ordinary and Sun Bum is basically my entire bathroom shelf — and that rewards popup reminded me I'm leaving points on the table if I don't sign in. The page feels energized and seasonally on-point for late May, though it's doing less work to make me feel like a Diamond member and more work to recruit brand-new signups.

## What's working

- **The BOGO suncare hero** — "BOGO 50% off select suncare / Mix & match 35+ brands" is a killer offer framing for someone like me who buys across price tiers. That "mix & match" language is what makes it click.
- **Brand-mix in the product imagery** — St. Tropez, The Ordinary, Maui Moisture, and Sun Bum in one frame is a prestige-vs-mass visual argument. I can already picture my comparison post.
- **"switch up your stash" copy overlay** — this is the right language register for a 31-year-old beauty obsessive. Not corporate, not too Gen Z, just right.
- **"Ends Jun 6" urgency tag** — specific date, above the fold, credible. No fake countdown clock energy.
- **BOPIS bar** — "Beauty in 2 hours or less. Order online, pick up in store." at the very top signals same-day convenience before I've even scrolled.
- **Rewards popup** — visible immediately, clearly shows the value proposition (Birthday Perks, Points = $), easy to dismiss with the X.

## What's weak

- **Rewards popup is acquisition-focused, not member-focused** — as a Diamond member I want to see my points balance, not "Unlock $5* just for joining." This popup is written for people who don't have an account yet. It's a near-miss.
- **No Diamond-tier acknowledgment anywhere** — not a single callout that recognizes high-spend loyalty. The homepage treats me the same as someone on their first visit.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no one-tap detail** — the subtext mentions exclusions but there's no quick way to see what's excluded. That hesitation is a conversion killer for a deal-seeker.
- **"Deals for you" section is barely visible** — the "12 items" label is below the fold and the product cards are cropped. This section is doing nothing for me at first glance.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I know Ulta ships free at $35 but this page doesn't show it. That's a conversion nudge left on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the rewards popup copy to be member-state-aware** — if I'm cookied as a returning user, show "You have X points — worth $Y off" instead of "Unlock $5* just for joining." That's the difference between retention and acquisition messaging.
- **Add a Diamond-tier badge or personalized header** — even a thin bar that says "Welcome back, Diamond member" or "Your Diamond benefits are active" would make me feel seen without requiring a redesign.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the hero subtext** — one line under the BOGO copy like "Free shipping on $35+" closes the loop and reduces cart abandonment.
- **Tighten the "Exclusions apply" disclosure with a tap-to-expand modal** — a small "See details" link next to exclusions prevents bounce from deal hunters who need to vet the offer before they commit.
