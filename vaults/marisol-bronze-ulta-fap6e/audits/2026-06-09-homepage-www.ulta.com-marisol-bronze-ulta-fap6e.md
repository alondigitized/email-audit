# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta opened with full loyalty mode — Diamond tier savings front and center, a rewards modal that hijacked my whole screen, and a code (MEMBERSAVINGS) for 20% off on almost everything. For me as a Diamond member, this is actually kind of exciting to see, but the popup is doing a lot of heavy lifting over what's clearly a pink-toned member-event hero I can barely read underneath it. This looks like an Ulta Beauty Rewards push, and they are leaning hard into tiered savings right now.

## What's working

- **Diamond 20% off tier circle** — I'm Diamond, this is literally my discount, and it's the biggest, pinkest circle on the page. I clocked it immediately.
- **"Ulta Beauty Rewards® are waiting" popup** — For a new visitor or lapsed member this is smart; for me it confirms my account has perks stacked and ready.
- **BOPIS banner** — "Beauty in 2 hours or less—order online, pick up in store" at the very top is a genuine utility hook. I use BOPIS constantly for last-minute restock runs.
- **Code MEMBERSAVINGS** — Giving me the promo code in plain text on the hero (not just in email) means I can actually use it, not hunt for it in my inbox.
- **"Shop now" CTA** — Clean, high-contrast black button; no ambiguity about what it does.

## What's weak

- **Popup is blocking the entire hero** — The rewards modal is covering the main headline and whatever lifestyle imagery or product is behind it. I'd normally dismiss it immediately, but a less engaged user bounces before they ever see the actual deal.
- **I can't see the full hero headline** — I can see "Me..." and a partial word — not enough to know what story the brand is telling right now.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I'm a $35 free-ship shopper and that anchor is nowhere above the fold.
- **"See coupon*" secondary CTA is vague** — The asterisk and soft copy undercut the urgency. What's the coupon? Different from the 20%?
- **"Deals for you" section below the fold** — I can see "12 items" and "Sale" tags on two products, but they're cut off and I can't identify what categories. Feels like a generic row, not personalized to my Diamond prestige/mass mix.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the rewards modal after 3 seconds or show it as a slide-in banner** — Let the hero breathe. The Diamond offer is compelling enough to live as a sticky strip rather than a full-screen blocker.
- **Swap "See coupon*" for specific copy** like "Exclusions apply — see full list" linked inline — the asterisk ambiguity kills conversion momentum on a deal page.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top utility bar** next to the BOPIS line — "Free shipping at $35 · Pickup in 2 hrs" on one line covers both her shipping and in-store habits.
- **Show one Diamond-specific product recommendation** (prestige skincare, a Dyson, a Charlotte Tilbury collab) directly beneath the tier circles — Diamond members spend; give me something to spend on right there.
