# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Ulta knows exactly who I am before I even tap past the fold — a loyalty rewards modal greets me the second the page loads, reminding me my points are waiting and that prestige brands are in the deal. The Memorial Day Weekend energy is real with tiered spend-and-save offers. But that interstitial is doing a lot of heavy lifting in front of a hero I literally cannot read, which is a miss.

## What's working

- **Rewards modal:** "Ulta Beauty Rewards® are waiting" with three clear bullet checkmarks (unlock $5, birthday perks, points = $ off) is a strong retention hook — this speaks directly to me as an existing Diamond member who already cares about points math.
- **Tiered MDW deal:** $10 off $50 / $20 off $100 is smart because my AOV almost always clears $100 on a prestige haul. These tiers feel made for how I shop.
- **5X points top bar:** "Earn 5X points when you buy online, pick up in store" — that's a BOPIS nudge that speaks to my hybrid shopping habit.
- **"Includes prestige brands & fragrance"** — that three-word qualifier is doing enormous work for me. I need to know my Charlotte Tilbury and Lancôme count before I'll bother applying a code.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks the hero entirely:** I cannot read what the actual campaign is — "Me..." is all I get. Whatever story Ulta is trying to tell for Memorial Day weekend is invisible behind the interstitial. That's a real editorial loss.
- **Two competing CTAs in the modal + the promo bar:** "Sign in or join for free!", "Shop now", and "See coupon*" are all fighting for my attention at once. Decision fatigue on load.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible:** Ulta's $35 free shipping is one of their strongest conversion levers for me, and it's nowhere on screen. That's a missed signal.
- **"Deals for you" rail is barely visible below fold:** I can see "Sale" tags and the top of what look like two products, but there's zero context — no brand names, no product names. Doesn't pull me in.

## Recommendations

- **Delay or shrink the rewards modal.** Let me see the hero for 2 seconds first, then slide in a bottom sheet for the loyalty pitch — or use a persistent banner instead of a blocking overlay. I already know rewards exist; I'm Diamond.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top bar** alongside the 5X points message. "Free shipping at $35" sits right in my consideration zone and it's invisible right now.
- **Add a deadline to the MDW offer.** "Use code MDW" with no end date feels soft. "Ends Monday" would sharpen urgency without feeling manipulative.
- **Show at least one prestige brand logo** in the promo banner — a Fenty, Anastasia, or NARS badge next to "Includes prestige brands" turns vague into credible instantly.
