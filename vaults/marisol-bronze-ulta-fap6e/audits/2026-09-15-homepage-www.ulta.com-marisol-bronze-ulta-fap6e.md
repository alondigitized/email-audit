# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This reads exactly like Ulta on a promo push — clean, deal-forward, and clearly mid-way through their "21+ Days of Beauty" event. Right now it's all about the 50% off Beauty Steals, which is catnip for someone like me who bounces between prestige and mass — Dermalogica sitting next to Kitsch and Benefit in the same rail is basically my whole shopping personality in one screenshot.

## What's working

- **The "50% off Beauty Steals®" hero** — big, bold, unmissable, and the giveaway is 250+ items, which fits my habit of cross-shopping instead of committing to one brand.
- **The product flatlay with the red "50% off" badge** — instantly reads as savings without me having to parse copy.
- **The "Today" rail underneath** — Benefit, Dermalogica, Kitsch, Ellis Brooklyn all in one row is exactly the mass-meets-prestige mix I'd screenshot for a comparison post.
- **The Rewards popup** — "$5* just for joining" plus birthday perks is a genuinely good returning-shopper nudge, not just a generic "sign up" nag.

## What's weak

- **"Diamond & Platinum: Get FREE SHIPPING"** top banner — I'm not sure what tier I'm in, so this reads as "this deal might not be for you," which is a little exclusionary right out of the gate.
- **No people/lifestyle imagery** — it's all product flatlays, so there's nothing here that visually says "this is for a 31-year-old" vs. anyone else.
- **The caret (^) after "21+ Days of Beauty Event"** — signals there's a footnote/exclusion I can't see, which makes me want to double check before I get excited about "over 250" items.
- **"Explore event" CTA is vague** compared to "Today's steals" — one button is action-oriented, the other is generic.

## Recommendations

- Swap the "Diamond & Platinum" free-shipping banner for a universal "$35+ ships free" callout, or clarify tiers right there so non-top-tier shoppers don't feel shut out.
- Add a tiny "see details" link next to the ^ so the fine print is one tap away instead of implied.
- Drop in one lifestyle shot (even a hand swatching a product) in the hero rotation to break up the flatlay-only feel.
- Rename "Explore event" to something concrete like "Shop all 250 steals" to match the energy of "Today's steals."
