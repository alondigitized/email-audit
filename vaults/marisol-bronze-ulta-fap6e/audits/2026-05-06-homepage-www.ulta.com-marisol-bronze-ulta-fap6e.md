# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Ulta knows exactly what it's doing — the second I land, it's pushing Rewards enrollment front and center, and there's fragrance imagery with what looks like a legit points-or-savings offer on prestige bottles (Dolce, Prada). As a Diamond member I'm already in the program, so the sign-in modal is semi-redundant for me, but the fragrance deal peeking out behind it is genuinely tempting. Right now this homepage reads as a loyalty-and-fragrance push, which is very much my world.

## What's working

- **The Rewards modal intercept** — listing Birthday Perks + Points = $ off in plain language is genuinely compelling. Even as an existing Diamond member I'd glance at this to see if there's an "Activate" offer targeted to me.
- **Prestige fragrance imagery** — Dolce & Gabbana, Prada bottles on a pink background is the exact mood-board energy I'm already scrolling for. It signals "this is a beauty moment," not a drugstore run.
- **Free same day delivery at $35** — that threshold is so easy to hit on a single prestige item. It feels like a real perk, not a bait.
- **"Deals for you — 12 items"** rail beginning just below the fold — personalized-sounding label creates immediate pull to scroll.

## What's weak

- **The modal blocks its own hero** — I can see maybe 40% of the fragrance campaign behind the overlay. If the deal is good enough to feature, let me actually see it. The modal is cannibalizing the very offer it's presumably trying to drive me toward.
- **No Diamond-tier acknowledgment** — I'm Ultamate Diamond and the modal is pitching me "Unlock $5\* just for joining" as if I'm a stranger. A logged-in or cookie-recognized returning shopper should see a rewards balance or tier callout, not a new-member pitch.
- **No people in the imagery** — the fragrance shot is all product, no lifestyle. I post dupe comparisons with my own skin in the frame; I respond to seeing real women using this stuff.
- **"Activate" button copy is vague** — activate what exactly? A specific offer? My rewards account? An Ulta card? Ambiguity kills tap confidence on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Make the modal dismissible with one tap and shorter** — or replace it with a slim rewards-balance banner for returning visitors so the hero is fully visible on load.
- **Add a Diamond-tier personalization signal** — swap "Sign in or join for free!" for "Welcome back, Diamond member — your points balance" when a cookie or account is detected.
- **Swap pure product shot for a lifestyle frame** — show a woman in her 30s layering a Prada fragrance, not just the bottle on a pink surface. That's the content I actually screenshot and share.
- **Sharpen the "Activate" CTA** — be specific: "Activate 2× Points on Fragrance" is tappable; "Activate" is not.
