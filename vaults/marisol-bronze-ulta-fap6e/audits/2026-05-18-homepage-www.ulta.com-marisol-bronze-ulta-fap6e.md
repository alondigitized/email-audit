# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Ulta showing up with a BOGO on 35+ brands in May — very "we know you're about to stock up for summer" energy, and honestly they read me correctly. The brand feels like it's leaning hard into value + loyalty right now, which is exactly what keeps me from straying to Sephora. The rewards popup is a little in-my-face but the message is right: they know I'm a Diamond member and they want me to feel that.

## What's working

- **Rewards popup timing** — Showing me that "$5 just for joining" + birthday perks + points-as-cash right when I open the app is smart. As a Diamond member I'd dismiss it fast, but it confirms my status matters here.
- **"Switch up your stash" hero copy** — That's exactly how I talk about my routine. Not "upgrade your regimen," not "discover beauty" — *stash*. That word is me.
- **BOGO on 35+ brands** — This is my literal behavior: buy Sol de Janeiro, grab the Sun Bum dupe alongside it. "Mix & match" is the dupe-test CTA I didn't know I needed.
- **BOPIS strip at the very top** — "Beauty in 2 hours or less" is a real differentiator vs. Sephora. I use this constantly for day-of pickups.
- **Deals for you section** — Personalization signal. 12 items curated for me means they're using my purchase history, which as a Diamond member is extensive.

## What's weak

- **The rewards popup covers the hero** — I literally cannot read the full BOGO offer because the modal sits on top of it. The most compelling thing on the page (a brand-mixing BOGO) is 60% hidden. That's a conversion kill.
- **No prestige brand callout** — I shop MAC, NARS, Urban Decay under the same roof as Sun Bum. Nothing above the fold speaks to that prestige side. The hero skews fully mass.
- **Free shipping threshold buried** — There's a "Fr..." peeking in from the right side that's cut off. If that's the free-shipping banner I need, it's not landing.
- **No model/person in the hero** — Product flatlay is fine but for a beauty brand I want to see the *result*. Who's wearing this? What does the glow look like on a real face?
- **Asterisk with no visible fine print** — "$5* just for joining" — the fine print isn't shown in the popup. Minor but as someone who's been burned by reward exclusions, I notice this.

## Recommendations

- **Layer the popup below the fold or as a sticky drawer** — Let me see the full BOGO hero first. Once I'm engaged, slide up the rewards join prompt from the bottom. Don't bury your best promo behind your acquisition CTA.
- **Add one prestige brand logo to the hero** — Show a MAC or Clinique next to the Sun Bum in the product row. That's Ulta's actual moat vs. Target or Amazon — dual prestige/mass shelf — and it's invisible here.
- **Resolve the right-side cutoff** — Whatever "Fr..." is (free shipping? free gift?) deserves its own visible strip or needs to be promoted into the main hero copy. Right now it's a ghost.
- **Swap "Shop now" for offer-specific copy** — "Shop the BOGO" or "Mix & match now" would convert me faster than the generic "Shop now." I want to know I'm tapping into the specific deal.
