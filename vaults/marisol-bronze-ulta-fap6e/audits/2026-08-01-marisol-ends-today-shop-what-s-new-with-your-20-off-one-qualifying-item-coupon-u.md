# Marisol, ENDS TODAY! Shop what’s new with your 20% off⁺ one qualifying item coupon using code SCORE, including prestige & fragrance. Terms & excl. apply 🤑
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is functionally the same coupon Ulta already sent on 7/26 ("Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off⁺ one qualifying item...") — same code (SCORE), same "one qualifying item" structure, same prestige/fragrance carve-in, just re-skinned with a "Side effects may include doing a happy dance" joke instead of the gamified "landed in your lap" hook. Six days apart with an identical promo code is a rerun, not a new offer, and a subscriber who opened the 7/26 email will clock that instantly.
- The execution itself is competent: a clean red "20% off is calling" hero up top with the barcode and coupon code visible without much scrolling, followed by a genuinely enormous product grid (24+ SKUs spanning drugstore to prestige — Clinique, Marc Jacobs, Versace next to Bath & Body Works, IGK, Kopari) that reads more like a catalog dump than a curated pick. The email closes with a "join Ulta Rewards" pitch aimed at Marisol, which implies she isn't a current member — worth knowing, since it changes how relevant the rest of the loyalty messaging actually is to her.
- Net: solid deliverability mechanics (personalized subject, clear hero, working CTA) undercut by promo-code reuse and subject-line bloat. This lands as a mid-pack send, not the 9/10s Ulta has been pulling on fresher GWP creative.

## What's working

- The hero block ("20% off is calling") is the first thing the eye hits — offer, barcode, and coupon code SCORE are all visible early with no hunting.
- "Shop now" CTA buttons are consistent and repeated on every product tile, so there's no dead-end scrolling.
- The subject line embeds Marisol's name and a hard deadline ("ENDS TODAY!"), which are real personalization/urgency signals, not filler.
- No visible render bugs — images, prices, and coupon tags all display cleanly through the long scroll.
- The rewards CTA at the bottom ("Hi, Marisol... Ready to join?") is a legitimate secondary conversion path if she isn't already a member.

## What's weak

- Same coupon code (SCORE) as the 7/26 send, five business days earlier — this reads as a rerun, not a new promo, to anyone who opened both.
- Subject line is ~154 characters — buries the actual offer under filler ("Marisol, ENDS TODAY! Shop what's new with...") that mobile inboxes will truncate well before "code SCORE" even appears.
- The product grid is enormous and undifferentiated — 24+ SKUs with no merchandising logic (skincare, fragrance, hair tools, and body wash all mixed with no sub-headers), which dilutes rather than sharpens the "one qualifying item" offer.
- Every tile is stamped "with coupon" plus recurring "Terms & excl. apply" language — the fine-print repetition across the whole email makes the 20% off feel more hedged than the hero banner suggests.
- Primary CTA copy is a generic "Shop now" repeated dozens of times rather than anything specific to a hero product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire or meaningfully differentiate the SCORE code — if this is a genuine reissue, say so ("still time" / "last chance") rather than repackaging it as a fresh offer six days later; repeated codes train subscribers to wait for the reminder instead of acting on the first send.
- 2. Cut the subject to lead with the concrete offer and push the legal tail out of the subject line entirely (put "Terms & excl. apply" in the preheader/footer only).
- 3. Break the 24-item grid into 3-4 labeled sections (e.g., "Fragrance," "Skincare," "Tools") so the eye has an entry point beyond "scroll everything."
- 4. Swap "Shop now" for CTA copy tied to what's actually being clicked ("Shop Fragrance," "Redeem Code") to lift click intent from the product tiles.
- 5. If Marisol is a non-member, consider moving the Rewards join pitch higher — right now it's buried after 20+ products, past the point most recipients will scroll.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% off one item (fragrance included) — code SCORE`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Prestige, fragrance, and everything between — pick your one item before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your code SCORE expires tonight. Here's what's worth grabbing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud / ExactTarget infrastructure, per `cl.s11.exct.net`) with mobile-responsive CSS, but fails on personalization (empty greeting token), lacks detectable unsubscribe/authentication headers, and loads several insecure/unlabeled tracking assets.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 60 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect-domain links, not independently verified as functional).
- 10 tracking pixels load over plain **HTTP** instead of HTTPS: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (query param `a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`). Mixed-content HTTP pixels are commonly blocked by mail clients (e.g., Gmail image proxy, Outlook), which can silently break open-tracking/attribution for this send.
- One additional HTTP-loaded asset: `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7...` (SFMC dynamic content/tracking beacon) — same non-HTTPS blocking risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **28 images/pixels missing `alt` text**, spanning both content images (`image.e.ulta.com/lib/.../*.png|jpg|gif`) and tracking pixels (`mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png`, `dotomi.com` pub pixels, `LiveContent.aspx`). Missing alt text on content images degrades accessibility (screen readers) and leaves the email illegible when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
- Source includes a commented-out hamburger menu block (`/* 91925 Commenting out hamburger menu ... */`) — dead CSS left in production markup; not render-breaking but adds unnecessary payload weight.
- Mobile responsive rules (`@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`) and dark-mode meta tags (`color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes`) are correctly present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty greeting slot confirmed by QA**: body renders `"Hi ,"` with a blank name field — the first-name merge token failed to resolve despite the subject line successfully personalizing ("Marisol, ENDS TODAY!"). This indicates the merge field is populated for subject-line personalization but not for the body greeting block, suggesting a template binding mismatch rather than a missing data value.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding it rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm true absence from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available headers.
- **Recommendation:** these three are relay-visibility limitations, not confirmed sender-side failures — verify against raw headers captured outside the AgentMail relay before treating as compliance violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated HTML source provided, and the 60 tracking links were excluded from probing — insufficient evidence to confirm or deny UTM tagging or landing-page alignment. **No issues found** (not verifiable from available data, not confirmed clean).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the body greeting merge-token binding so `Hi ,` resolves to the subscriber's first name (or falls back to a generic greeting) — currently ships broken personalization to production.
2. Migrate the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` pixels and the `cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` beacon to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking loss in clients that block mixed content.
3. Add `alt` text to all content images at minimum (tracking pixels are lower priority but trivial to fix with `alt=""`).
4. Remove dead commented-out hamburger-menu CSS to reduce markup size.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` against raw SMTP headers captured outside the AgentMail relay — current absence may be a capture gap, not a real compliance gap.
