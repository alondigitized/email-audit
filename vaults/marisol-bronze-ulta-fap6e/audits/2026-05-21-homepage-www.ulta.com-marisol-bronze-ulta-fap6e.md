# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Ulta knows exactly what it's doing — the second I land, it's pitching me on rewards, stacking a Memorial Day offer right below, and reminding me why being a Diamond member pays. This is a loyalty-first beauty destination doing a solid job of speaking my language right above the fold. The one thing killing the vibe? A modal that physically buries the hero the moment I arrive, making me close a pop-up before I can even see what the sale is actually about.

## What's working

- **The $10/$20 tiered offer** — Bold, big, orange numbers. Impossible to miss. I'm already doing the mental math: my typical Ulta haul clears $50 without blinking.
- **"Earn 5X points, buy online, pick up in-store"** — This is actually a killer hook for me. I do exactly this: order online, grab in-store to avoid waiting. The banner validates that behavior immediately.
- **The rewards modal content** — "Points = $ off a purchase" and "Birthday Perks" are the exact reasons I stay Diamond. For a new visitor, this is a compelling pitch. For me, it's a reminder my points are waiting.
- **"Includes prestige brands & fragrance"** — Getting prestige + mass in one Memorial Day sale is the Ulta value prop in one line. Nailed it.
- **Code "MDW"** — Explicit, easy to remember, no hunting through emails. That's how promos should work on mobile.

## What's weak

- **The rewards modal blocks the hero** — I literally cannot read the hero headline. I can see "Me..." and then the modal drops. This is a friction-first experience for a returning Ultamate Diamond who does NOT need to be sold on signing in.
- **No personalization** — I'm a Diamond member. This page has zero acknowledgment of that. No "Welcome back, Marisol" or rewards balance. The modal treats me like a stranger.
- **"See details\*\*"** — Two asterisks on a deal. I instinctively start reading fine print in my head. On mobile this is a bounce risk.
- **No visible category navigation** — The hamburger menu is there but no quick-tap beauty categories are visible. I'd have to tap through to find what's on sale in skincare vs. fragrance.
- **No urgency deadline shown** — MDW implies a deadline but I don't see "Ends Monday" or a countdown. The offer feels big but not urgent.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the rewards modal for logged-in Diamond members** — If I'm recognized (cookie or logged in), replace the modal with a personalized reward balance strip: "You have 1,240 points — that's $12 off." That's conversion fuel, not friction.
- **Show a countdown banner beneath the offer strip** — "Ends Monday, May 26" right below the $10/$20 block. One line. Ships in a day. Huge urgency lift.
- **Surface two or three hero category tiles below the fold** — Skincare, Fragrance, Makeup — tappable chips so I can route directly to the department on sale without digging through the hamburger menu.
- **Swap the generic "Shop now" CTA for a category split** — Two buttons: "Shop Prestige" and "Shop All Deals." That maps to exactly the prestige-vs-mass comparison behavior I came here to do.
