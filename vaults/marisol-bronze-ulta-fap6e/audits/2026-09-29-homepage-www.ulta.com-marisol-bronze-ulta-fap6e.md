# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- Ulta is going full "Let's haul!" mode. It's a big promo-first mass-plus-prestige beauty homepage, with orange cards pushing up to 30% off, a BOGO 50% NYX deal and a free 10-piece gift. It feels like my Saturday-in-store energy translated to screen. The rewards pop-over is doing a lot of heavy lifting, though, and it's sitting right on top of the content.

## What's working

- **"BOGO 50% off select NYX Professional Makeup | Ends Oct 3":** It's a specific brand, a specific deal and a real deadline. NYX is my go-to for testing dupes against the prestige stuff.
- **Product-pile imagery on the "up to 30% off" card:** I can see Morphe, Byoma, press-ons and mascara, so I know it's real product I'd buy and not a mood shot.
- **Rewards pop-over:** The three checkmark lines are scannable. "Points = $ off a purchase" is exactly the kind of plain language I like.
- **Top utility bar:** "Beauty in 2 hours or less—order online, pick up in store" speaks directly to my mobile plus in-store habit.
- **Nav:** "Shop / New / Brands / Deals" are all one tap away.

## What's weak

- **Pop-over placement:** It's stuck on the third card and hides part of the "Free 10 piece gift" offer, which is the one I'd probably care about most.
- **Third card cut off:** The carousel peek is fine, but the headline reads "Free 10 piece gift" and then stops.
- **No people anywhere:** It's all product. I don't see anyone who looks like me trying things, and nothing that says "comparison" or "dupe."
- **Free-shipping threshold:** I don't see the $35 free-shipping line. "Free with $22" is a gift threshold and could be mistaken for a shipping one.
- **Floating overlays:** A gradient AI sparkle button and a black "Feedback" tab both float over the content, so there's a lot of clutter on the right edge.
- **"Deals for you" rail:** I can see "Sale" tags and 12 items but no prices, ratings or names above the fold.

## Recommendations

- Auto-collapse the rewards pop-over into a small "Rewards: $5 off" pill in the header after the first view, so it stops covering the third promo card.
- Add a slim strip under "Let's haul!" saying "Free shipping at $35 · Free pickup in 2 hours". That's the number I need to plan my cart around.
- Put star ratings, prices and "was/now" on the first four "Deals for you" tiles. Add a "Best-sellers under $15" or "Dupe-worthy" chip row to speak to people who compare products.
- Swap one product-pile card for a real person swatching NYX next to a prestige shade. That shows the "mass + prestige" idea instead of just saying it.
