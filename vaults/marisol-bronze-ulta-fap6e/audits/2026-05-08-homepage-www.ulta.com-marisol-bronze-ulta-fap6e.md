# Homepage snapshot · www.ulta.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: Ulta knows its loyalty game. The second I landed, a rewards tooltip popped up reminding me that points are literally money, and the entire hero is a 5X points activation for fragrance — which, as an Ultamate Diamond member, is exactly the kind of bonus I chase. The brand is positioning itself as the beauty rewards destination right now, leaning hard into the earn/activate mechanic over product discovery. It's smart, but the above-fold real estate is almost entirely consumed by that one promo card and a popup telling me to *sign in* — not great when I'm already a loyal customer who just wants to see what's new.

## What's working

- **5X points on fragrance + "Ends tomorrow"**: This is the loyalty-activation combo that actually gets me to open the app. Time-boxed earn multipliers are the single most effective lever Ulta pulls on Diamond members, and it's front and center.
- **"Activate" CTA**: Not "Shop now," not "Learn more" — *Activate*. That's the right word for a loyalty mechanic. It tells me there's a step I need to take and that the offer won't just auto-apply.
- **Fragrance bottle imagery**: The product photography is gorgeous — the jeweled purple bottle (very Viktor & Rolf Flowerbomb-coded), the green floral, the hot pink. This is the kind of aspirational yet shoppable imagery that makes me want to tap.
- **Free same day delivery $35+** in the utility bar: I always notice this because my order almost always clears $35 and same-day is clutch for last-minute gifting.
- **"Deals for you — 12 items"** section starting below the fold: personalized deal rails are crack for someone like me who curates both prestige and mass.

## What's weak

- **The rewards tooltip is recruiting, not welcoming**: "Unlock $5\* just for joining" is new-member acquisition copy. If I'm already logged in or returning, this popup is pure noise. There's no balance shown, no "You have 847 points" — which would actually make me feel seen.
- **No category navigation chips visible**: The only nav I can see is the hamburger, search, account, and cart. No quick-tap chips for Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Hair. For a high-frequency mobile shopper like me, one extra tap to browse categories is friction.
- **Only one hero card is visible** (a second card is peeking on the right edge but completely unreadable): The carousel isn't hinted at clearly enough. I almost didn't notice there was more to swipe.
- **Zero editorial/trend content above the fold**: There's no "What's new," no "Trending now," no 21 Days of Beauty callout even though that's one of Ulta's biggest seasonal events. As someone who plans her 21 Days haul in advance, not seeing it immediately is a miss.
- **No model/person imagery**: It's all product bottles. I can't see myself in this page. Even a quick lifestyle shot of someone applying a fragrance would raise the connection.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the generic "sign in or join" tooltip with a personalized member status card** — if I'm cookied/recognized, show my points balance and tier name ("Ultamate Diamond — 1,240 pts") instead of a recruitment pitch.
- **Add horizontal category nav chips below the logo bar** — "Skincare / Makeup / Fragrance / Hair / Deals" as tappable pills. One-tap category access is table stakes for a mobile-first beauty app.
- **Surface the 21 Days of Beauty teaser above the fold** — even a small "21 Days of Beauty starts May 12 →" strip would spike engagement for planners like me who pre-research which categories drop on which days.
- **Hint the hero carousel more clearly** — show at least 20% of the second card or add visible pagination dots so I know there's more and I'm not just seeing a dead-end card.
