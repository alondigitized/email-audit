# Frosted Animal Cookies Are Back with NEW Arrivals!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand product-launch send with no offer. The hero is a pink-and-navy sunburst with a Frosted Animal Cookies group (giraffe, bunny, white lion, elephant). "STEP RIGHT UP!" is a circus-style headline that works and is more playful than the Nov 5 Hello Kitty "Flurries of Fun" frame. The visual hierarchy is clear, and the four stacked product banners are easy to scan.
- The commercial frame is weak. After the 30% OFF Online Exclusives push on Nov 7–8 and the $11.11 flash send on Nov 11, a subscriber who has been trained on discounts gets nothing here. The only urgency is a shipping-deadline banner for Hanukkah (Nov 21) and Christmas (Dec 14). It's useful, but it sits between the hero and the products and fights the hero for attention. The "Back in stock" and "NEW!" tags on two of the banners add some real news.
- Verdict: a competent catalog email that lands at the lower end of Build-A-Bear's recent range. It's pretty and clear, but it gives me no reason to click today rather than next week.

## What's working

- The hero is strong and seasonal. The sunburst, the character group and the "STEP RIGHT UP!" headline form a clear focal point.
- The four stacked banners alternate colors and put the product in focus, so they scan quickly. Each has a specific "SHOP [product]" label.
- "NEW!" on the Pink Elephant Mini Bean and "BACK IN STOCK" on the Giraffe give two honest reasons to click.
- The shipping-deadline banner is credible, specific urgency with real dates.
- The "Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid adds social-proof-style merchandising, and the footer is tidy.

## What's weak

- There's no price or discount anywhere. Following the 30% OFF and $11.11 sends, this reads as a downgrade.
- The "SHOP NOW" button in the hero is small and pale pink on cream, so it has low contrast and low prominence.
- The shipping-deadline banner interrupts the flow between the hero and the product banners and competes with the collection story.
- No prices, ratings or reviews appear on any product tile, so there's no purchase-decision detail.
- The "Best Sellers" tile in "More to Explore" is a plain yellow block with no product imagery. It looks empty next to the Mini Beans tile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Attach an offer to the collection, such as a bundle price or a "free Mini Bean with a giraffe" gift with purchase. Put it in the hero. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Make the hero CTA a high-contrast navy button with specific copy, such as "Shop Frosted Animals".
- 3. Move the shipping-deadline banner below the product blocks, or shrink it to a slim strip.
- 4. Add prices to the banners, for example "Giant Pink Giraffe $XX".
- 5. Replace the empty yellow Best Sellers tile with real product imagery.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure is sound (table layout, MSO conditionals, preheader, responsive media queries). Automated QA flagged a missing plain-text part (0 chars), 9 images without alt text, and 6 HTTP 403 probe failures. The header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be verified through the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 links returned HTTP 403** (4 product "SHOP NOW" links, plus the mini-beans and best-sellers image links):
  - `/snowflake-kitty-stuffed-animal/035464.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/cookiebara-stuffed-animal/032817.html`
  - `/stuffed-animals/build-a-bear-collections/mini-beans`
  - `/stuffed-animals/featured/best-sellers`
  - All six are on `www.buildabear.com` and uniformly 403. This looks like bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe rather than dead pages, so it is **unconfirmed as a real breakage**. Re-verify with a browser-like request. The product IDs in the URLs match the product image filenames (`...035464...`, `...026518...`, `...025567...`, `...032817...`), so the link-to-image mapping is consistent.
- **24 tracking/redirect links were skipped** (not probed), so their resolution and destinations are unverified.
- **UTM hygiene**: every probed link carries the identical `utm_content=Promo`, so clicks on different modules (product tiles, collection banners) can't be told apart. `utm_term` holds the full internal campaign string (`US-111226_US_AnimalCookiesRereleaseLongscroll__Email_Broadcast_...`) instead of a keyword. It is a very long value, and it is a misuse of the parameter. `utm_source=Campaign` and `utm_medium=Email` use non-standard capitalization, which can split reporting from lowercase `email`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **9 images are missing alt text:**
  - 5 long-scroll slices: `Primary_LongScroll_AnimalCookiesReLaunch_26_02.png` through `_26_06.png` (cdn.bfldr.com)
  - 4 product images: `45260801135464~x20135020.jpg`, `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`, `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`, `74240700232817~x20116045.jpg` (buildabear.com/dw/image/...)
- The product images are linked by "SHOP NOW" buttons. With images off or a screen reader, those tiles have no description.
- `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared, but the visible `<style>` block has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules. Clients that honor the meta tag may auto-invert colors unpredictably. This is low severity and based on the truncated head only.
- The visible head shows no rendering defects. The `u+.em_body` Gmail fix, `mso-table-*` resets, and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback missing** (QA FAIL: "Text version is 0 chars"). A multipart/alternative without a text part can raise spam scores and fails in text-only clients.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers really are absent, the email would fail Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown (the relay removes the header). From domain is `ebuildabear.com`. Verify with DNS or a direct-delivery test.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All probed destinations are on `www.buildabear.com` and align with the email's "Animal Cookies" campaign (`utm_campaign=AnimalCookiesRereleaseLongscroll`). Parameters are consistent across links (`utm_id=2805492`).
- Landing-page content could not be verified because of the 403 responses. The only observed continuity issue is the non-differentiated `utm_content` described in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 6 flagged URLs with a browser-like user agent or headless browser to separate WAF blocking from real 404/410s.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. It is currently 0 chars.
3. Add `alt` text to all 9 flagged images. Use empty `alt=""` only for purely decorative slices, and descriptive text for the product tiles and the long-scroll slices that carry content.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message from the sender. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture rather than the email.
5. Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `ebuildabear.com` through DNS or a direct-delivery seed.
6. Use a distinct `utm_content` per module (for example `hero`, `product-1`), move the campaign string out of `utm_term`, and normalize `utm_source` and `utm_medium` to lowercase.
7. Add `prefers-color-scheme` styles, or drop the `color-scheme` meta tags if dark mode isn't supported.
8. Probe the 24 skipped tracking links separately to confirm they resolve to the intended `buildabear.com` destinations.
