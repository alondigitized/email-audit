# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the very first thing I see when I land on buildabear.com is a giant Cookiebot cookie consent wall that takes up essentially the entire viewport — I can barely make out any actual homepage content behind it. What little I *can* see: a partial top announcement bar mentioning same-day delivery, a sticky "Active Offers (4)" bar pinned at the bottom, and a sliver of what looks like colorful imagery peeking out below. Build-A-Bear reads as a fun, kid-centric brand, but right now it's leading with a legal disclaimer wall, which is a rough first impression. Whatever campaign they're running — seasonal, Pokémon, whatever — I can't see it.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4)" sticky bar at the bottom** — even behind the modal I can see there are live offers. That persistent bar is actually smart; it tells me deals are waiting, which is enough to make me want to hit Accept fast.
- **Top announcement bar** — the partial text "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" suggests same-day delivery is being promoted, which as a mom building bears for gifts is genuinely useful info (last-minute birthday panic is real).
- **Clean modal rendering** — the Cookiebot modal itself is legible, well-spaced, and has a clear "Accept" button. If I *have* to see a cookie wall, at least it doesn't look broken.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal walls the entire screen** — I literally cannot see the homepage. Not a peek at the hero, no product imagery, no seasonal Pokémon collaboration callout, nothing. For a returning shopper who knows what they came for, this is friction I don't want.
- **Top bar is cut off** — the announcement reads "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" with the beginning of the text clipped off. I can't tell if it says "Same-Day" or "Next-Day." That detail matters for gifting urgency.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook visible at all** — I'm a Bonus Club member and there's zero signal that being logged in (or signing in) would skip or personalize anything. A cookie wall + no "Welcome back, Bree" moment = feels like a first-visit experience every single time.
- **No seasonal or collab content visible** — if there's a Pokémon drop or Mother's Day promo or spring limited edition, I have absolutely no idea. The modal is eating it.

## Recommendations

- **Show a slim cookie consent bar at the bottom instead of a full-screen takeover** — a bottom-anchored bar with "Accept" lets me see the actual homepage immediately. This is standard practice and GDPR-compliant. The full-modal approach punishes every visitor on every visit.
- **Make the announcement bar copy fully visible** — ensure the left edge of the top strip isn't clipping. "Same-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" (if that's what it says) is a real conversion hook; don't truncate it.
- **Surface a logged-in member state above the fold** — if I'm a Bonus Club member and already cookied, skip the wall or at minimum show my rewards balance in the top bar. Make returning shoppers feel recognized.
- **Pin a collab/seasonal teaser in the top bar** — if there's an active Pokémon or limited-edition drop, that's the one thing that would make me accept the cookie prompt *faster*. A line like "New: Pokémon Spring Collection — Active Offers (4)" in that top strip would have me tapping Accept immediately.
