# Bestsellers & Future Best Friends!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a digest/catalog nudge — a "bestsellers" roundup that leans on products you've almost certainly already seen. The hero copy ("These bestselling picks are totally worth another peek") is a remarkably candid admission that the content is recycled: the Sanrio Hello Kitty Ice Cream Shop section is lifted directly from the 05-26 send (scored 8/10 here), and the Pokémon "Special Event" block echoes the Sylveon send two days ago. - Clean render, recognizable brand, functional CTAs — this email works in the most mechanical sense. But it earns its click entirely on brand loyalty, not on anything it does itself. It's the fifth email in five days from this sender, and it has nothing new to say.

- Clean, bug-free render with consistent blue-and-white Build-A-Bear palette throughout

## What's weak

- "Totally worth another peek" in the hero is passive, self-undermining copy — it concedes the content is old
- No offer whatsoever: no discount, no free shipping, no bundle — zero price motivation to click over browsing the site directly
- The Sanrio Ice Cream Shop block is a near-verbatim re-run from 05-26, four days ago, with nothing added
- Pokémon "SHOP THE SPECIAL EVENT!" is vague — subscribers who opened the Sylveon email don't know why they're being sent back
- Fifth email in five days; cadence fatigue is real and this send does nothing to justify the slot

- 4. **Tighten the Pokémon section** — "SHOP THE SPECIAL EVENT!" tells a subscriber nothing. Name the event or name the character.
- 5. **Reduce cadence or reframe the slot** — a Tuesday bestsellers digest landing after four consecutive campaign sends reads as noise. Consider a weekly "what's trending" cadence instead of daily.
