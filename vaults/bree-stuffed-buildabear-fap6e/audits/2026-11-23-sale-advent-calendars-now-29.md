## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's links are structurally sound, but the UTM values contain unencoded spaces and all 6 probed buildabear.com links failed the QA link check. The plain-text part is empty (0 chars). Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unencoded spaces in query strings (6 of 6 probed links FAIL).** The checker reports "URL can't contain control characters". The cause is literal spaces in `utm_campaign=Advent Calendar Promo` and in `utm_term=US-TARGETED_112326_US_Advent Calendar Promo__Email_Broadcast_...`. Affected URLs:
  - `/snowflake-kitty-stuffed-animal/035464.html`
  - `/nine-tailed-kitsune-fox-stuffed-animal/034916.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/grinch-plush---includes-extra-large-heart/035437.html`
  - `/holiday/sanrio`
  - `/holiday/advent-calendars-and-decor`
- Most clients and browsers percent-encode the spaces, so real-world breakage is likely partial. I can't tell from the truncated source whether the spaces are in the raw `href` or were introduced when the redirect was resolved. If they're in the raw `href`, strict parsers, some link scanners and some analytics pipelines may truncate or reject the URL.
- **21 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped** by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Link text:** the `/holiday/sanrio` link uses an image alt string as its anchor text ("Build-A-Bear exclusive Hello Kitty and Friends Frosted Collection featuring Fros…"). The four product CTAs all read "SHOP NOW", so they're indistinguishable to screen readers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing:** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. This could be a relay capture artifact, but if it's real, it hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Four images have no alt text.** The `src` values are on buildabear.com `/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`, with filenames `45260801135464~x20135020.jpg`, `01250700834916~x20047103.jpg`, `84231211225567x20710014.jpg` and `40260801135437~x20117024.jpg`. - **Positives:** the preheader is present (`div.preheader`, "Save on Advent Calendars in Stores & Online"), with a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` hack to stop body text leaking into the preview. The MSO/VML namespaces and `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present, as are the viewport and `color-scheme` meta tags.
- **Minor:** the viewport content is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 ` with a trailing space. This is harmless but sloppy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for senders at this volume.

- The QA tool itself attributes all three to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so these are unconfirmed rather than confirmed defects. The footer postal address and unsubscribe link weren't visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are also unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM scheme is consistent across links:** `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2807232`. `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized, which GA4 may bucket separately from `email`.
- **`utm_campaign` and `utm_term` contain spaces and `__` double underscores.** This risks split or garbled campaign rows in analytics (see section 2).
- **Possible landing mismatch:** the subject is "Advent Calendars Now $29", but four "SHOP NOW" links go to individual plush PDPs (Snowflake Kitty, Kitsune Fox, Happy Hugs Teddy, Grinch). Only one link goes to `/holiday/advent-calendars-and-decor`. I couldn't confirm that the PDPs show the advertised offer, because the pages weren't probed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Percent-encode UTM values (`Advent%20Calendar%20Promo`), or better, replace spaces with hyphens or underscores in the ESP's campaign-name variables. Re-run the link check.
2. Standardize UTM casing (`utm_medium=email`) and remove the `__` in `utm_term`.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part. If the part exists, fix the relay capture.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, and use unique link text such as "Shop Snowflake Kitty".
5. Check the raw headers at the ESP or sending domain: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Probe the 21 skipped tracking links by following redirects to confirm they resolve with 200 status, and confirm that the PDPs show the $29 advent pricing.
