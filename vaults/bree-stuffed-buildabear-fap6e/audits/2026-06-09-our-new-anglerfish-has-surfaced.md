# Our NEW Anglerfish Has Surfaced!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- The Anglerfish is a genuinely unexpected character for Build-A-Bear — a "toothy deep-sea creature" that leans into the quirky, slightly dark corner of kid/collector culture. The "FROM THE ADORABLE ABYSS" headline is the strongest copy in the email; it nails the character's personality and is one of the better hero lines Build-A-Bear has used this week. The soft aqua/navy deep-sea color palette fits the concept well, and the character plush photography reads clearly.
- The problem is that this character doesn't obviously benefit from the same summer-fun framing that has powered the Chummy Shark (9/10, June 5) and Lightning Bug (8/10, June 8) sends. An anglerfish is a deep-sea, inherently NOT-summery creature, which creates a slight tonal mismatch with the ongoing seasonal campaign. The brand leans into "adorable" but doesn't fully commit to the weirdness that would make this creature genuinely compelling to the collector/alt crowd who'd most want it.
- Structural execution is solid — clean hero, secondary modules for Sprinkles Hello Kitty and Bluey, a "Most Loved This Week" product grid, four CTA navigation links at the bottom. No render bugs. This is a competent, mid-tier character-launch email that undersells its most interesting product.

## What's working

- **"FROM THE ADORABLE ABYSS"** is punchy, memorable, and brand-consistent — easily the best headline of the week.
- Deep-sea color palette (teal/navy/bubble motifs) is thematically coherent with the character.
- Secondary modules are well-separated — Sprinkles Hello Kitty and Bluey Bingo get clear visual real estate without fighting the hero.
- "Most Loved This Week" product grid (Winnie the Pooh, Chococat, Kuromi) adds social proof and cross-sell without cluttering.
- Navigation CTAs at the bottom cover all the key action paths (Build Online, Gifts, Workshop, Personalize).

## What's weak

- Subject line "Our NEW Anglerfish Has Surfaced!" is the weakest part — "Has Surfaced!" is a throwaway pun that squanders the intrigue of the actual concept. Compare to "Our NEW Shark Is a Fin-tastic Friend!" which at least committed to a character name and personality in the subject.
- No price or offer visible — the entire email is discovery/launch with no conversion hook. Every other top-scoring send this week included at least a price point or bundle CTA.
- The hero subhead ("Our cuddly Anglerfish will lure you in for hugs! Smile big with this toothy deep-sea creature.") is competent but safe — it never leans into the genuinely weird/dark charm of an anglerfish, which is the actual differentiator.
- The Bluey module headline is partially cropped and harder to read than the Hello Kitty module above it, creating inconsistent secondary hierarchy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lean harder into the weird.** The anglerfish is not a conventional cute character — that's its competitive edge. The hero subhead should commit to that: something like "Built for the brave, the bold, and the deep-sea obsessed." The current copy plays it safe when weird wins.
- 2. **Add a price anchor.** Even "Starting at $XX" or "Bundle with accessories from $XX" lifts conversion. Every 8+ send this week had a price or offer visible in the hero block.
- 3. **Rewrite the subject line** to match the creative ambition of "ADORABLE ABYSS":
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the Anglerfish: Cute Lives in the Deep`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Something Wonderful Lurks in the Deep 🐟`
- 4. **Rewrite the preheader** to complement rather than echo the subject:
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Toothy. Cuddly. 100% from the abyss — shop your new deep-sea BFF.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most unusual character yet just arrived. Lure yourself in.`
- 5. **Fix the Bluey module crop** — the headline text is visually clipped; either tighten the copy or increase the module's height so it reads cleanly on mobile.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**CSS property case bug** — `<style>` block contains:
```css
p { Margin: 0px !important; Padding: 0px !important; }
```
`Margin` and `Padding` are capitalized. CSS property names are case-insensitive per spec, but some older email client parsers (notably older Lotus Notes and certain webmail renderers) may silently ignore non-lowercase property names. These should be `margin` and `padding`.

**Trailing whitespace in viewport meta:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 " />
```
Trailing space inside the `content` attribute value. Technically harmless in most parsers but is a hygiene defect.

**Redundant duplicate declaration in `.gmailfix`:**
```css
.gmailfix { display: none; display: none !important; }
```
The first `display: none` is overridden by the second. Only the `!important` form is needed.
