# Stuff Your Cub Condo This Wednesday, 6/24!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Save the Date" event invite for a 50%-off Cub Condo stuffing day on Wednesday, June 24th — sent two days out. The format is smart: Build-A-Bear is treating an in-store experience as a ticketed event worth signing up for, not just a walk-in coupon. The 50% OFF dominates the hero visually, and the specific date adds credible urgency that the back-to-back $15 flash-sale resends on June 17 couldn't match — those were discount-first; this is *event-first with a discount as the hook*.
- The creative is clean and on-brand. The Cub Condo structure itself is the hero image — it doubles as product education and brand delight, and the mermaid-bear character in the beach scene continues the summer thread from the Pawlette wardrobe send on June 15 (9/10). Compared to the graduation send on June 13 (5/10) that buried its offer and felt late-season, this one leads with the offer and date without ambiguity.
- The structural weakness: "SIGN UP NOW" is the sole CTA and it's incomplete. You're signing up *for what*, exactly? A reservation? A waitlist? A promo code? That friction will suppress click-through on what is otherwise one of the cleaner event-format sends this brand has deployed in recent cadence.

## What's working

- **50% OFF is unmissable** — the oversized yellow-on-blue treatment dominates the hero and is visible before any scrolling, no hunt required.
- **Specific date badge ("WED Jun 24") anchors the urgency** — it's not "limited time," it's a named Wednesday on a badge, which is far more credible than a countdown with no end-state.
- **Cub Condo hero image is product-accurate and delightful** — the cottage-shaped stuffing station with a mermaid-bear character in a beach setting is unmistakably Build-A-Bear and communicates the in-store experience in one glance.
- **"Save the Date" framing is a format lift** — positions the event as something requiring preparation, not a passive coupon to act on whenever.
- **Clean layout, no render bugs** — hierarchy flows correctly from save-the-date header → offer → CTA → navigation block.

## What's weak

- **"SIGN UP NOW" is ambiguous** — registering for an in-store event is higher friction than redeeming a promo; the CTA doesn't explain what signing up delivers (slot, code, confirmation email).
- **50% off applies to what, exactly?** Sub-copy reads "All the Fun You Can Fit Inside When You Make a Furry Friend" — poetic but not specific. Is it the bear, the outfit, the whole build? A skim reader will discount the offer mentally.
- **No loyalty or member callout** — this brand has member-reward messaging available; there's no "Bears Bucks apply" or "VIP gets early access" signal anywhere in this render.
- **Sub-copy font is small relative to the hero** — the qualifying condition ("when you make a furry friend") sits beneath the dominant 50% OFF in noticeably smaller type; a skim reader will click expecting blanket 50% off anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rename the CTA to explain the commitment** — "SIGN UP NOW" → "Save Your Spot for 6/24" or "Reserve My Cub Condo Time." This eliminates the "sign up for *what*?" hesitation and reinforces the event framing that makes this send distinctive.
- 2. **Spell out exactly what's 50% off in the hero** — Add "50% off your furry friend + outfit" or "50% off one full build" as a direct line under the main offer. Don't make the shopper infer.
- 3. **Add a scarcity layer if slots are real** — "Limited spots — sign up now" converts harder than "SIGN UP NOW" alone. If it's truly capacity-limited, say so.
- 4. **Add a secondary CTA for non-attendees** — A small "Can't make it in-store? Shop online →" below the primary button captures intent from subscribers outside driving distance and prevents a dead end.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off This Wednesday Only — Reserve Your Cub Condo Spot`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stuff Your Bear for 50% Off — This Wed, 6/24`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All the fun you can fit inside — one day only, sign up now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Reserve your spot for Wednesday's in-store event`

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header absent** (QA WARN): Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (enforced Feb 2024) for senders above 5,000 msgs/day. Its absence suggests the header is either being stripped by the AgentMail relay before capture or was never set by the ESP. Either way, this is a deliverability and compliance risk.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent** (QA WARN / RFC 8058): One-click unsubscribe (the Gmail "Unsubscribe" button) requires both `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Missing this header means Gmail's one-click flow won't work, increasing friction for unsubscribes and increasing spam complaint probability.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** (QA WARN): SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable from the captured headers. Could be a relay artifact, but means there's no automated confirmation that authentication is passing on this domain (`ebuildabear.com`). Should be verified directly against the ESP's sending logs.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** and unsubscribe link: Not visible in the truncated HTML, but standard for Build-A-Bear sends. Cannot confirm or deny — full source review required.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters not verifiable** — all 28 click links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Cannot confirm whether destination URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, or `utm_content` parameters.

If this is a US send, it suggests the template may have been recycled from a UK campaign without updating the asset path.
