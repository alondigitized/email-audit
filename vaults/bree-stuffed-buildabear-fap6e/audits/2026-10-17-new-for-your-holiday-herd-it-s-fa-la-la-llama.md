# NEW for Your Holiday Herd: It’s Fa La La Llama!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, on-brand holiday send with a strong hero. The "FLEECE NAVIDAD!" headline sits over a warm shot of the Fa La La Llama with a scarf and cocoa mug. The subject names the product and the hero delivers on it, so the eye lands in the right place. The green-and-red palette and the candy-stripe frame make it read as Christmas at a glance.
- Below the hero the email splits its attention. It carries an ornament block, an advent calendar block, a four-plush grid, a Grinch tile and a repeat of the ornament tile, with eight or more "Shop Now" buttons in all. There is no offer anywhere, only a small "Start Earning Points!" pill at the top. This is the same catalog-style send as the 10-15 "Holiday Fun Starts Now!" and 10-12 advent calendar emails. Today's version is more product-led, but the cadence is getting repetitive.
- Judgement: a good hero on a no-offer catalog email. It's a 5 or 6, like most of this month's sends. The llama is a decent launch moment. The rest of the email dilutes it and gives no reason to click today.

## What's working

- The hero is clear and festive. The llama sits in a cozy holiday scene, and the white-on-green headline is readable.
- The subject names the product, and the hero matches it, so there is no mismatch between inbox and body.
- The "Shop Now" button in the hero sits right under the llama copy, and the candy-stripe border ties it to the frame.
- The seasonal modules (ornament and advent calendar) fit the campaign calendar and the 10-12 advent send.
- The product grid is clean, with a consistent pink button treatment and legible names such as Sanrio Hello Kitty and Maleficent.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer. The "Start Earning Points!" pill is tiny and has no points value or promise behind it.
- The email has too many modules and about eight identical "Shop Now" CTAs, so none stands out after the hero.
- The ornament tile appears twice, once as a hero block and again under "More to Explore." That reads as filler.
- The "Shop Now" copy is generic, and the lower section's links all go to broad categories.
- The "More to Explore" tiles have no headline or label, and the Grinch tile has no copy explaining it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the llama hero plus one secondary module, and drop the repeated ornament tile. A focused email would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Put a member perk in the hero, such as "Earn 2x points on llamas this week." The points pill is currently wasted.
- 3. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific: "Meet the Llama," "Shop Ornaments," "Open Advent Calendars."
- 4. Add price and a "new" or "limited" tag to the llama, plus a hint of the bundle (scarf, mug) so the hero carries a reason to act.
- 5. Label the "More to Explore" tiles, and give the Grinch tile a headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet Fa La La Llama, our new holiday plush 🦙`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fleece Navidad! The Fa La La Llama is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A snuggly new llama in a festive scarf, plus earn points on holiday faves.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fa La La Llama joins the herd. Shop plush, ornaments and advent calendars.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with a hidden preheader and responsive breakpoints at 480px and 639px. The automated checks show 4 product links returning 403 to the probe, 4 images without alt text, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and any remaining body content were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403** to the QA probe, all on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - `/sanrio-starry-sky-hello-kitty-plush/035171.html`
- A 403 (not 404 or 410) on the Demandware product pages most likely means bot or WAF blocking of the probe's user agent. It does not confirm the pages are dead for real users. Re-verify with a browser-like request before treating them as broken.
- **Link text is empty in the QA record** (`Broken link (403):  (link text: "SHOP NOW")`). The reported failures are therefore not distinguishable by text, only by URL.
- **23 tracking and redirect links were skipped** by the probe, so their destinations, redirect chains and status are unverified.
- UTM parameters are consistent across the four probed links: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=HolidayLlamaDuoLaunch`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2800325`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing alt text**, all product images from the Demandware CDN:
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`
  - `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`
  - `98250800435171~x20127120.jpg`

  With images blocked, these linked product tiles have no accessible name beyond the generic "SHOP NOW" text.
- `<title>` is the internal template name, `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. This is not user-facing in most clients, but it is a template-identifier leak that screen readers or some webmail clients may expose.
- The `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags declare `light dark`. No `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules appear in the visible `<style>` block, so dark-mode rendering is left to client auto-inversion. Confirm this in the truncated remainder.
- The preheader and hidden-text padding (`&zwnj;&nbsp;`, `&shy;`) follow the standard pattern, and the preheader has `mso-hide:all`. No issues found.
- The MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block is present for Outlook DPI handling. No issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261017` contains the string "Testing", which may be a segment label or a leaked test-send value. It is worth confirming with the sender that this is the intended production segment name.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed absence.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The sending domain is `ebuildabear.com`, and alignment with the From domain is unverified.
- The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) fell outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM tagging is complete and consistent, with `utm_id` populated. No issues found there.
- **Landing alignment is unverified for the llama products.** The subject and preheader promote "Fa La La Llama in Two Sizes", but the four URLs the QA tool flagged point to other products (Batty Kitty, Maleficent dragon, Sanrio Berry, Hello Kitty). The llama CTAs were not in the supplied data. Check that the primary CTAs resolve to the llama PDP or collection.
- Landing pages could not be fetched because of the 403s, so on-page UTM persistence and product availability are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four 403 URLs with a real browser UA or from a different IP to separate WAF blocking from true failures.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four flagged product images and audit the rest of the email's images in the full source.
3. Capture the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, DKIM-Signature) directly from the original message rather than the relay copy.
4. Replace the `<title>` template name with a meaningful title.
5. Confirm that the `Testing` token in `utm_term` is intended, and that the llama CTAs point to llama products.
6. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to cover the footer, the remaining links, and dark-mode CSS.
