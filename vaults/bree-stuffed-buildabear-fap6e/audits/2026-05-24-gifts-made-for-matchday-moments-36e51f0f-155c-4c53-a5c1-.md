# Gifts Made for Matchday Moments ⚽
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Build-A-Bear leans into the soccer/World Cup energy with a vivid green-and-pink "Summer of Soccer" send that's tightly themed and visually loud — in a good way. The hero ("Celebrate every cheer, chant and GOOOAAALLLL with custom gear for your furry friend") sets up four nation-specific bear blocks (USA "Stars, Stripes and Stitches," Mexico "Kicks With Corazón," Canada "Maple Leaf Magic," plus a GOAT lamb cameo) — each with its own "Shop Now" button. It's a catalog email with a theme wrapper, which is more cohesive than the May 23 "So Many NEW Arrivals" dump.
- Where it stalls is the offer: there isn't one. No %, no $, no free shipping, no time pressure — just "here are themed bears, click to shop." For a marketing email this close to the long weekend (and after last week's stronger Grogu and Lightning Bug launches), the lack of a hook hurts. The "Shop Bestsellers" block at the bottom feels bolted on and breaks the soccer narrative.
- Net: well-designed, on-brand, seasonally smart, but commercially soft. A solid 6 — would be a clean 8 with even a modest matchday offer attached.

## What's working

- Strong theme cohesion — green/pink palette, soccer balls, jersey-wearing bears all read as one campaign.
- Country-specific bears (USA, Mexico, Canada) is smart segmentation bait — feels personal even though it isn't.
- Hero copy "Every cheer, chant and GOOOAAALLLL" has voice and earns the soccer emoji in the subject.
- Clear repeated CTA pattern — every block has its own visible orange "Shop Now" button.
- Footer utility row (Build Your Own, Personalize, Birthday, Workshop) is clean and useful.

## What's weak

- No offer anywhere — no discount, no bundle, no free shipping, no urgency.
- "Shop Bestsellers" block mid-email breaks the soccer theme and dilutes the matchday story.
- Four near-identical bear blocks stacked vertically gets repetitive — feels longer than it needs to.
- GOAT lamb block ("Celebrate the cutest kid on your team") is a weak pun that doesn't quite land visually.
- No tie to an actual match/tournament date — "matchday" is generic when the calendar could anchor it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hook.** Even "Free personalized jersey number with any matchday bear" would convert this from catalog to campaign. The theme is doing all the work right now.
- 2. **Cut the bestsellers block.** It breaks the narrative. If you need it, move it to a P.S. row, not a hero-sized orange banner mid-flow.
- 3. **Anchor to a real date** — World Cup qualifier, MLS weekend, Champions League final — so "matchday" means something.
- 4. **Collapse the country blocks** into a single 3-up grid instead of four stacked full-width units. Cuts scroll length in half.
- 5. Subject + preheader rewrites:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your bear just made the starting lineup ⚽`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: USA, Mexico & Canada matchday bears`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Jerseys, kits and corazón — personalize a bear for your team.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick a side: USA, Mexico, or Canada. Free name on the jersey.`
