## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Of the automated checks, 25% passed. Six product and category links returned HTTP 403 to the QA probe, four images lack alt text, and the HTML has no `lang` attribute. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most of the body were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 links returned 403.** Affected URLs, all on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/sanrio-frosted-hello-kitty-plush/035433.html`
  - `/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-my-sweet-piano-plush/033927.html`
  - `/grinch-plush---includes-extra-large-heart/035437.html`
  - `/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-frosted-pochacco-plush/035435.html`
  - `/new-arrivals`
  - `/stuffed-animals/featured/back-in-stock`
- **The 403s are probably bot protection, not dead pages.** A uniform 403 across product and category pages points to WAF/bot blocking of the probe. A removed page would more likely return 404 or 410. Treat these as unconfirmed, and re-test with a real browser UA or from a whitelisted IP.
- **The probe gave no useful link label.** The "SHOP NOW" entries have an empty label before the URL.
- **18 tracking/redirect links were skipped** (not probed), so their destinations are unverified.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is the same on every link.** The module and position can't be told apart in analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing alt text.** All are product images on `buildabear.com/dw/image/...`:
  - `11260801135433~x20114021.jpg`
  - `91240802233927~x20025114.jpg`
  - `40260801135437~x20117024.jpg`
  - `66260801135435~x20116022.jpg`

  These are the images paired with the "SHOP NOW" links. With images blocked, the link text is the only accessible name, and it is generic.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **The preheader may have an encoding fault.** It renders as `Our NEW Donkey Kong Is a Barrel of Fun! ðŸŒ`, which is mojibake for a 4-byte emoji. The `<meta charset=utf-8>` is present, so this may be introduced by the capture or relay pipeline rather than the sent message. Verify in a raw MIME view, because inbox preview text may show corrupted characters if it is real.

No other rendering issues are confirmed in the visible source. The MSO conditional comments, media queries and hidden preheader padding are standard. Table roles and dark-mode styles couldn't be checked in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens were seen. The `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261120` value suggests a test or seed-send segment name (see section 6).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `ebuildabear.com` differs from the link domain `buildabear.com`. Alignment can't be verified from the available data.
- **The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked** because the footer is beyond the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **The UTM scheme is consistent across links.** All use `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=DonkeyKongLaunchLongscroll&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261120&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2807073`.
- **`utm_term` contains "Testing".** If this went to a live audience, it is a mislabeled or leaked test-segment value that will pollute reporting. If it is a test send, that is expected.
- **`utm_source=Campaign` is not descriptive,** and `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized. Capitalization differences can split rows in case-sensitive reports. The convention is lowercase `email`.
- **The audited links point to Hello Kitty, Grinch and Pochacco plush, plus New Arrivals and Back in Stock.** None of them points to the Donkey Kong product. The campaign is tagged `DonkeyKongLaunchLongscroll`, so those links' sessions will be attributed to a launch they may not relate to. The Donkey Kong CTA was not in the QA results and may be in the truncated portion.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the six 403 URLs with a browser-like client. Fix them only if they fail for real users.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the four product images. Give the "SHOP NOW" links distinct accessible names, for example by product.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Verify the preheader emoji in the raw MIME source. Encode it as an HTML entity if it is corrupted.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the origin send headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
6. Make `utm_content` unique per module or link. Normalize `utm_source` and `utm_medium` to lowercase descriptive values. Confirm the `Testing` term is not on live sends.
7. Increase probe coverage of the 18 skipped tracking links by following the redirects to their final destinations.
