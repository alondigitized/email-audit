- **Surface one offer in the sticky bar before consent** — e.g., "Active Offers (4) — incl. Free Shipping over $35 → View All." Give me a reason to stay while I decide on cookies.
- **Switch to a less intrusive consent pattern** — a bottom sheet or a small corner banner instead of a full modal would let me see the hero while still satisfying GDPR/CCPA. Many brands do this; it dramatically reduces friction for first-time visitors.
- **Geo-target the legal copy** — US visitors shouldn't see "Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited" as the first legal entity named. That's a trust ding for no reason.
