# Act Fast: Pokémon Special Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear doubling down on the Pokémon well it opened on 2026-08-13 with "BACK IN STOCK! Bring Home Espeon & Umbreon" — same characters reappear here, now bundled with Vaporeon and wrapped in "Act Fast" / "Ending Soon" urgency framing tied to a same-day (2026-08-16) deadline per the footer fine print. The creative is clean and on-brand: bold red Pokémon-licensed hero, two clearly product-shot plush modules, then the standard "Most Loved" and "More to Explore" grid.
- The problem is the gap between subject-line urgency and body-copy vagueness. "SPECIAL EVENT! Action-packed savings are waiting" never states a number — the actual mechanic (a 30%-off code) is buried in 6pt fine print at the very bottom, which is a real miss for an email whose entire premise is urgency-driven savings. Combined with three Pokémon-flavored sends in the last four days (Harry Potter 08-05, Espeon/Umbreon 08-13, this one 08-16), the cadence is starting to feel like reheated leftovers rather than a genuine "special event."
- Solid, on-brand execution; middling strategy. It'll get opened on urgency, but the payoff inside doesn't match the promise.

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable without scrolling — big red Pokémon lockup, bold "SPECIAL EVENT!" headline, single obvious "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Espeon/Umbreon and Vaporeon get dedicated, well-lit product modules instead of being crammed into a single grid tile.
- No render bugs — clean layout, images load, buttons are legible, consistent typography throughout.
- Deadline in the fine print (August 16, 11:59pm CST) actually lines up with today's date, so the urgency is technically real, not stale evergreen copy.
- "Most Loved Stuff This Week" and "More to Explore" modules give non-Pokémon shoppers a reason to keep scrolling.

## What's weak

- The actual offer (code POKEMON30, implying 30% off) never appears in the visible hero or body copy — it's buried in bottom fine print, which undercuts the whole "special event" pitch.
- This is Build-A-Bear's third Pokémon-adjacent send in under two weeks (Harry Potter 08-05, Espeon/Umbreon 08-13, this one), and Espeon/Umbreon are literally repeat characters from the 08-13 send — fatigue risk for anyone who opened that one too.
- "Action-packed savings are waiting for you online" is vague filler copy — doesn't say what's on sale or by how much, so the hero promises urgency without substance.
- Exclusions are meaningful (Pikachu, Espeon, Umbreon carry different terms per the fine print) but invisible until the very bottom — that's a bait-and-switch risk if a shopper adds an excluded item expecting the discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount in the hero. "30% OFF POKÉMON FAVORITES" beats "Action-packed savings are waiting" — state the number where the eye already lands.
- 2. Differentiate this send from the 08-13 Espeon/Umbreon email — lead with Vaporeon as the "new" hook since Espeon/Umbreon are repeats, or skip re-featuring them entirely.
- 3. Move the key exclusion (code doesn't apply to Pikachu/Espeon/Umbreon) up near the CTA in plain text, not footer fine print — protects trust and avoids checkout frustration.
- 4. Space out Pokémon sends — three in under two weeks risks training subscribers to skip this franchise's emails specifically.
