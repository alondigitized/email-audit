# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- I open the app and get walled off before I even see the site — a blue "ACTION REQUIRED" birth-year gate stacked right on top of a full cookie consent banner. I can just barely make out a Halloween-costumed bear scene behind them with "Stuff... With..." copy and a "Shop Now" button, but I can't actually get to it. This reads like a compliance-first brand right now, not a "come build a bear" brand — the fun is buried behind two forms I have to fill out first.

## What's working

- The sliver of Halloween hero I can see — costumed bears (a purple cat in a witch hat, a scarecrow-outfit bear) — is genuinely on-brand and seasonally relevant, which matters to me since I track seasonal/collab drops closely.
- "Active Offers (3)" bar pinned at the bottom is a smart pattern in theory — it tells me there's something worth checking without shouting at me, if I could actually reach it.
- The circular category icons peeking out ("Shop All Stuffed Animals," a gift card tile) suggest a clean, scannable nav once you're past the gate.

## What's weak

- The stacked "ACTION REQUIRED" birth-year modal directly on top of the cookie consent modal — I have to clear two full-screen interruptions before I see anything real. That's friction I don't expect from a brand I already have a rewards account with.
- The birth-year modal's language ("Incorrect information may cancel this transaction") is alarming and confusing on a homepage where I haven't started any transaction — it reads like a bug, not a real compliance step.
- I can't read the actual hero headline — "Stuff With..." is truncated behind the modal, so I have no idea what the message or CTA actually says.
- Nav links (Find A Workshop, The Bear Builder, Giftshop, New Arrivals, The Bear Cave) are all grayed out and inert behind the overlay — as a member who wants to jump straight to New Arrivals or check for the Christmas bear restock, I can't do that from here.

## Recommendations

- Don't stack the age-gate and cookie-consent modals — sequence them, or better, don't hard-block the homepage view with an age gate at all until checkout/purchase actually requires it.
- Rewrite the birth-year modal copy — "ACTION REQUIRED" and "transaction may be cancelled" language is scary and out of place on a browsing session, not a checkout.
- Surface the "Active Offers (3)" bar as a visible banner instead of a collapsed strip, so returning members like me see the deal without an extra tap.
- Once past the gate, make sure the "Shop Now" CTA and hero headline are fully visible in the very first frame — right now the real message is a mystery.
