# Happee Birthdae Harry! NEW Arrivals in Stores & Online
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's Harry Potter 25th Anniversary launch, and it's the strongest hero execution in the recent run — better than the Toy Story drop from 2026-07-24. The lead visual (all four House bears lined up on the Platform 9¾ backdrop) is genuinely arresting, the "YOUR MAGICAL JOURNEY STARTS HERE" gold-on-navy treatment feels premium and on-license, and the four House sections (Gryffindor/Slytherin/Ravenclaw/Hufflepuff) are color-coded and organized cleanly with consistent bear-plus-CTA blocks.
- Where it falls down is the same place nearly every recent Build-A-Bear send falls down: there is zero price anchor, zero discount, zero urgency. This is a pure product-intro email riding a big license, and the subject line "Happee Birthdae Harry!" is a fun nod for readers who know the Chamber of Secrets reference, but it buries the actual news (new Harry Potter arrivals) behind a gimmick that won't land for anyone who doesn't immediately parse it.
- Net: gorgeous, on-brand creative that will drive opens on license-recognition alone, but it's another "here's stuff, come buy it" send with no monetary hook — same criticism as 2026-07-26's "Summer Vibes Activated!" (5.00/10), just dressed in much better IP.

## What's working

- Hero shot of all four House bears together with the Hogwarts Express backdrop is a strong, scroll-stopping visual.
- Color-blocked House sections (red/green/blue/yellow) make it easy to scan to "my House" without reading body copy.
- Each House block has its own clear "SHOP NOW" button — no ambiguity about where to click.
- The "25 Years of Magic" Harry Potter anniversary lockup adds legitimacy/occasion to the drop.
- Secondary modules (party booking, Halloween teaser, "Most Loved Stuff This Week") give the email a full, non-empty feel without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- No price, discount, or bundle offer anywhere in the entire email — same gap flagged in 2026-07-26 and only fixed by the one-off 2026-07-27 free-shipping send.
- No urgency/deadline — "new arrivals" never expires, so there's no reason to act today vs. next week.
- Subject line's Chamber-of-Secrets reference ("Happee Birthdae Harry") is cute for superfans but disconnects from the actual content (House-themed plush), costing clarity for casual openers.
- The email is very long (four full-bleed House sections plus three more modules) — mobile readers will scroll a long way past the hero before hitting anything new.
- "Spooky Stuff Coming Soon" teaser mid-email is a jarring seasonal non-sequitur sandwiched between Harry Potter and a Halloween sign-up with no visual cohesion to what's above it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer to the hero band (e.g., "Bundle & Save on House Sets" or a launch-week discount) — this is the single biggest lever, as it's the same missing piece across nearly every recent send in this brand history.
- 2. Tighten the subject line to lead with the news, not just the gimmick — pair the wink with the actual content.
- 3. Trim the four House sections to a single scannable module (logo + 4-across thumbnail grid) above the fold, with "shop your House" as one CTA, and move any one deep-dive House block below.
- 4. Move or cut the "Spooky Stuff Coming Soon" module — it breaks continuity with the Harry Potter theme; place seasonal teasers in a separate send.
- 5. Add a deadline or "while supplies last" cue near the hero CTA to create urgency around the anniversary drop.
