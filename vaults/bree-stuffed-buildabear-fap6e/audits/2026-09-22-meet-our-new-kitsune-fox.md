# Meet Our NEW Kitsune Fox! 💜
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a "NEW Kitsune Fox" and the hero delivers exactly that — a purple, nine-tailed plush with real folklore framing ("inspired by the enigmatic foxes from Japanese folklore, sparkly purple fur and nine plush tails"). That's a genuinely distinct, well-described hero product, which puts this a notch above the generic seasonal catalog sends Build-A-Bear has been running. But the email immediately steps on its own reveal: a top banner leads with "Today Only: 30% OFF Lovable Legends Online," a mid-body block repeats "30% OFF Lovable Legends... Countdown to Fall," and a third full-width module pivots to a completely different character (Beardusa) before the email finally drops into the usual four-tile product grid.
- The structural problem that has dogged every recent Build-A-Bear send is back: too many competing heroes (Kitsune Fox, Lovable Legends discount, Beardusa, then Hello Kitty/Sanrio/Teddy Bear tiles) with no single throughline. The Kitsune Fox is the most interesting creative asset in weeks, but it's fighting for attention with a same-page character (Beardusa) and a promo banner that doesn't even confirm the fox is included in "Lovable Legends."
- Judgement: a strong hero buried in the same stack-of-modules format that's capped every recent send at 5-6/10 — this lands in the same range.

## What's working

- The Kitsune Fox hero image is distinctive and well-lit, with a clear inset showing the nine-tail detail.
- Descriptive copy under the hero ("nine-tailed," "sparkly purple fur," "Japanese folklore") gives real product texture, not just a name.
- "Today Only" urgency appears twice (top banner and mid-body countdown module), reinforcing a credible deadline.
- A specific discount code (LEGENDS) and % off are both visible, not buried in fine print.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The hero product (Kitsune Fox) is never explicitly tied to the "30% OFF Lovable Legends" banner — the recipient has to infer the connection.
- A second character (Beardusa) is introduced immediately after the discount block, diluting the fox as the star of the email.
- The email reverts to the same four-tile "Shop By Product" grid + "New Arrivals/Best Sellers" catalog stack seen in prior sends, with no connection back to the fox.
- "SHOP NOW" is repeated 8+ times with zero variation — none of them say "Shop Kitsune Fox" or similarly specific.
- Footer fine print does the work of clarifying the sale scope ("Lovable Legends Sale valid online only... through 11:59 p.m. PT on September 22, 2026") that should be in the visible copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Merge the top banner and hero: put "30% OFF — Today Only" directly on the Kitsune Fox hero module (not a separate orange bar above it) so the discount and the product read as one offer, not two.
- 2. Cut the Beardusa module entirely from this send, or move it to a separate email — it competes directly with the fox for hero status.
- 3. Change hero CTA copy from generic "SHOP NOW" to "Shop the Kitsune Fox" to reinforce the specific product being promoted.
- 4. Drop or shrink the four-tile "Shop By Product" grid — it has no relationship to the fox/Lovable Legends story and just extends scroll without adding conversion value.
- 5. Move the sale deadline ("through 11:59 p.m. PT on 9/22") out of footer fine print and into the countdown module itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: The Nine-Tailed Kitsune Fox (30% Off Today)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Purple, Sparkly, Nine Tails — Meet Our New Fox 💜`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `30% off Lovable Legends ends tonight — including the new Kitsune Fox.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Inspired by Japanese folklore, sparkly purple fur, nine plush tails.`
