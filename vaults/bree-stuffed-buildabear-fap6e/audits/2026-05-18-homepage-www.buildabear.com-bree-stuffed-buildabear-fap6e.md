# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- The very first thing I see when I open www.buildabear.com on my phone is a full-screen Cookiebot consent modal — the actual homepage is completely buried underneath it. What little I can make out around the edges is a dark navy background and a truncated top banner that says something about "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" I can't see a hero, a product, a Pokémon collab, nothing. Build-A-Bear is leading with legal compliance theater instead of bears.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All"** strip at the very bottom of the screen is a nice persistent bar — the idea that there are 4 active offers is actually compelling if I could get past the modal to see them.
- **Chat bubble** in the bottom-right corner is visible, which signals customer support access — useful if I'm hunting a Pokémon restock and want to ask.
- **Top delivery banner** (even though truncated) hints at same-day delivery, which matters when I'm buying for a kid's birthday tomorrow.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal on mobile**: Cookiebot is blocking 100% of the content. On a phone screen this is an instant vibe-killer — I came to shop bears, not read GDPR copy.
- **Top banner is cut off**: "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" — the beginning of the sentence is literally clipped. I can't read the actual offer.
- **Zero product or seasonal content visible**: I cannot see a single bear, a Pokémon badge, a seasonal exclusive, or a collab callout. That's my entire reason for visiting.
- **No loyalty hook visible**: As a rewards member who watches for restocks, I want to see my account or a member-only badge immediately — nothing.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is at the bottom, below the modal**: It's there, technically, but it's being obscured. Doesn't count as above-the-fold.

## Recommendations

- **Slim down the cookie consent on mobile**: Use a bottom-sheet banner instead of a full-modal takeover — two lines, one "Accept" button, done. Don't wall off the hero on the device that matters most for impulse shopping.
- **Fix the top banner truncation**: The "-Day Delivery" copy is missing its first word(s). Either shorten the copy or widen the marquee ticker so the full message scrolls legibly.
- **Surface the Pokémon or seasonal limited-edition content the moment the cookie modal clears**: If the collab calendar is live, it should be the first thing I land on — not a hero I have to scroll to find.
- **Add a persistent loyalty pill** (rewards points balance or "Member Deals" shortcut) in the sticky top nav so it's always one tap away, modal or not.
