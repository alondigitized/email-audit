# Spooky Season Starts Now: NEW Halloween Friends Are Here!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's Halloween launch, and it's the most product-dense send in the recent run — five hero characters (Enchanting Kitty, Posable Bat, Jumping Spider Duo, Mystical Werewolf Duo, Owloween Cauldron) laid out in a color-blocked stack, followed by a second wave (GIANT Bat, Bag Charms) and a "Shop By Product" grid of four more items. Compared to 2026-08-05's Harry Potter Mini Beans send (8/10, tight and IP-driven) and 2026-08-01's "50% Off GIANT Furry Friends" (7/10, single-minded), this one trades focus for breadth — it's a full seasonal catalog crammed into one email rather than a hero-led story.
- The top-of-funnel hero image is genuinely strong: a moody, well-lit Halloween tableau (picket fence, fog, jack-o-lantern glow) that sells the seasonal mood better than most of BAB's recent product-on-white shots. But after that hero, the email becomes a scroll of undifferentiated colored blocks with no discount, no urgency, and no single focal point — by the fourth or fifth "SHOP X" banner, fatigue sets in.
- No discount, no bundle price, no timer — same pattern as the cuteness-only sends (Silly Goose, Cow Cuddles) rather than the sharper "50% Off" or Harry Potter drops. Verdict: solid seasonal kickoff, over-stuffed execution — this needed to be split into two emails or cut to two hero products.

## What's working

- The hero image (spooky diorama with fence, fog, and lantern glow) is the best mood-setting visual in the recent run — it actually looks like a Halloween scene, not a product photo.
- "Fun-Sized Friends" headline plus subhead clearly explains the Mini Beans pairing mechanic in one line.
- Each product block uses a distinct, high-contrast color (green, orange, navy, red, pink) which makes the stack scannable even if repetitive.
- "NEW" badge on the Bag Charms photo is a clear, visible callout for the newest SKU.
- No render bugs — all text is legible, images loaded, buttons rendered correctly throughout.

## What's weak

- Five nearly-identical "SHOP [PRODUCT]" color blocks in a row with no copy differentiation beyond the product name — it reads as a catalog dump, not a curated pick.
- No discount, price, or urgency anywhere in the email — every plush is presented at full, unstated price.
- The GIANT Bat block interrupts the color-block rhythm with a lifestyle photo, but the sudden style shift makes it feel bolted-on rather than intentional.
- By the time the eye reaches "Shop By Product," a bunny and teddy bear (non-Halloween staples) dilute the seasonal focus the hero image established.
- No visible loyalty/member pricing despite Build-A-Bear having an active Bonus Club program.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the five-product color-block stack down to two or three heroes max (e.g., Enchanting Kitty + Werewolf Duo) and move the rest to a "See the full collection" link — the current version buries strong individual products in volume.
- 2. Add a concrete offer — even a modest "Free Mini Bean with purchase" or a % off would separate this from the last four cuteness-only sends and address the escalating fatigue noted in prior audits.
- 3. Drop the non-Halloween items (Pawlette Bunny, Happy Hugs Teddy) from this specific send — they undercut the seasonal urgency the hero image just built.
- 4. Give the GIANT Bat and Bag Charms sections a "New This Week" or "Just Dropped" microtag to justify their placement after the main collection, since right now they read as an afterthought.

## Technical Audit: Build-A-Bear "Spooky Season" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard SFMC/similar-platform broadcast template with responsive media queries and a hidden preheader; automated QA passed only 30% of checks, driven primarily by malformed tracking URLs and missing compliance/authentication headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken URLs (4 instances, confirmed by QA):** All four "SHOP NOW" product links contain an unencoded literal space in the `utm_term` parameter (`utm_term=US-All Subscribers-20260806`), which the QA harness flags as an illegal control character in a URL. Affected links:
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/pawlette-bunny-plush/033639.html?...&utm_term=US-All Subscribers-20260806&...`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html?...&utm_term=US-All Subscribers-20260806&...`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/glow-in-the-dark-lightning-bug-stuffed-animal/034549.html?...&utm_term=US-All Subscribers-20260806&...`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/red-rose-kitty-stuffed-animal/034272.html?...&utm_term=US-All Subscribers-20260806&...`
  
  Fix: URL-encode the space as `%20` (or `+`), e.g. `utm_term=US-All%20Subscribers-20260806`. This is a template/merge-field encoding bug in the ESP's UTM builder, not a one-off typo, since it's identical across all four links.
- **Tracking/redirect links:** 25 links were click-tracked/redirect-domain URLs and skipped by the HTTP probe — their final destinations were not validated by this QA pass and should be spot-checked separately.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (4 instances, confirmed by QA):** All four product images lack `alt` attributes:
  - `.../dw718e7f58/702517...` (70251742133639~x20352015.jpg)
  - `.../dwd5939e4d/842312...` (84231211225567x20710014.jpg)
  - `.../dw2f28c3c5/032507...` (03250701034549~x20218079.jpg)
  - `.../dw31033006/682507...` (68250700834272~x20058043.jpg)
  
  This is a WCAG failure and also degrades experience for images-off clients (common in Outlook/enterprise mail).
- Head markup itself (MSO conditional comments, `mso-table-lspace`/`rspace`, `.ExternalClass` reset, `.gmailfix`, viewport meta, `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes`) follows standard cross-client boilerplate — no issues found there.
- The visible HTML is truncated after the preheader hack, so body-level rendering (layout tables, CTA buttons, dark-mode handling) could not be fully assessed from the provided source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FirstName%%`, `{{token}}`) or broken personalization blocks are visible in the provided source. Note: the visible HTML is truncated before the main body content, so this assessment is limited to the head/preheader region only.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** Not found (WARN). Could be a relay capture gap rather than true absence, but it should be verified directly against the raw send.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058):** Not found (WARN) — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header:** Not found (WARN) — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this capture.
- **Sender domain:** From address uses `ebuildabear.com`, distinct from the linked destination domain `buildabear.com`. This is common ESP-subdomain practice but is worth confirming has proper SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment given the header gaps above.
- No CAN-SPAM physical-address or footer content was visible in the truncated source to confirm presence/absence — flagged as unverified rather than failing.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters are consistently applied across all four product CTAs: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=HalloweenLaunchV2&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2786927`, with per-send `utm_term` — structurally consistent, aside from the space-encoding bug noted in Section 2.
- Destination paths (`/pawlette-bunny-plush/033639.html`, `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`, `/glow-in-the-dark-lightning-bug-stuffed-animal/034549.html`, `/red-rose-kitty-stuffed-animal/034272.html`) resolve to `www.buildabear.com`, consistent with the brand's primary domain despite the sender using `ebuildabear.com`.
- Because of the unencoded-space defect, these links will fail or behave unpredictably depending on mail client URL handling (some auto-encode client-side, others do not) — this is the primary continuity risk, not domain mismatch.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the UTM-builder template to URL-encode spaces (`%20`) in `utm_term` — highest priority, currently breaks/risks 4 of 4 tracked product links.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all four product images for accessibility and images-off fallback.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers are present on the actual outbound send — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
4. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com` against `buildabear.com`, since `Authentication-Results` could not be confirmed from this capture.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 25 skipped tracking-redirect links to confirm final destinations resolve correctly.
