# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Oh wow — I literally cannot see the homepage. A full-screen Cookiebot consent modal is blocking everything the second I land. The only glimpse I get is a sliver of a top announcement bar that mentions something about "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" and a "Shop Now" / "Active Offers (4)" strip at the very bottom. Build-A-Bear's brand feels friendly and seasonal to me in general, but right now this page is just... a wall of legal copy.

## What's working

- **Top announcement bar** — I can barely make out "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" which hints at a shipping promo I'd want to know about as someone who backs up store visits with online orders.
- **"Active Offers (4) View All"** bottom strip — this peek tells me there ARE offers live right now, which makes me curious enough to hit Accept just to see them.
- **Cookie modal renders cleanly** — no visual glitches in the modal itself; "Accept" and "Manage My Preferences" are both clearly tappable on mobile.
- **Chat widget visible** — I can see a chat bubble in the bottom right, which is useful if I need to ask about a restock.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal blocks 100% of the hero** — I have zero context for why I should even bother accepting cookies. No teaser image, no offer, no collab announcement, nothing.
- **Top banner is cut off** — whatever the delivery promo says is truncated; I can't read it without dismissing the modal first.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook in the visible area** — as a rewards member, I want to see my account or a "Welcome back" signal immediately; there's none.
- **Cookie consent copy is walls of legal text** — the UK-focused language ("Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited") is confusing to me as a US shopper and feels off-brand for a fun, kid-friendly retailer.
- **No product imagery at all above the fold** — a Pokémon drop or holiday bear tease would give me a reason to consent and keep browsing; instead I get legalese.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the full-screen blocking modal with a bottom-bar consent banner** — let me actually see the homepage hero and collab content first; I'll consent from context, not cold legalese.
- **Surface the delivery promo and loyalty status in the announcement bar before the modal fires** — even a "Rewards members: free shipping on orders $35+" strip would give returning shoppers a reason to engage.
- **Add a teaser element visible through or around the modal** — a blurred/dimmed hero with the seasonal headline still readable (like "NEW: Pokémon Collection") would drive cookie acceptance and reduce bounce.
- **Localize the cookie copy for US shoppers** — drop the UK Limited reference on the US domain; it creates confusion and erodes trust.
