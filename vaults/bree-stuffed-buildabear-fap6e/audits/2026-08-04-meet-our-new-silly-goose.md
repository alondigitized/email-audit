# Meet Our NEW Silly Goose! 🪿
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear running the same play it's run at least four times in the past two weeks: introduce a new plush, lean on cuteness, skip the discount. Compared to 2026-07-28's "Sky Puppy's BACK with a New Mini Bean!" (8/10) and 2026-07-30's Pochacco send (7/10), the "Silly Goose" hero image and headline execution here are on par — clean product shot, clear "regular and Mini Beans® sizes" detail, unambiguous SHOP NOW button. But the pattern is starting to show wear: this is the brand's fourth or fifth new-character intro in about ten days, and without a price hook or urgency, it reads as one more entry in a fast-repeating cadence rather than a must-open.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, good hierarchy from hero visual down through the secondary modules (Spooky Stuff signup, Sky Puppy Moth cross-sell, Most Loved Stuff grid). The weakest link is structural, not visual: there's no offer, no urgency, and the subject/preview pairing doesn't do enough to differentiate this send from the last several near-identical "meet our new plush" emails.
- Verdict: solid, on-brand execution, mid-pack for the recent run — it won't embarrass the brand, but it also won't break through a saturated inbox of its own recent lookalikes.

## What's working

- The hero image is well-composed — the goose-and-gosling duo pose reads instantly as "cute," matching the winking "YOU SILLY GOOSE!" headline.
- "Find this Silly Goose in both regular and Mini Beans® sizes" gives a real product detail (size/variant availability) right in the hero copy, not buried below the fold.
- The SHOP NOW CTA button is high-contrast and immediately visible against the purple gradient.
- Secondary modules (Spooky Stuff teaser, Sky Puppy Moth cross-sell, Most Loved Stuff grid with 4 products) give the email depth without feeling cluttered.
- No render bugs — image, type, and button layers all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- No offer of any kind — no %, no $, no free shipping, no BOGO. Every recent high-scoring send from this brand (2026-07-27's 8/10 "FREE Shipping Today Only!") had a concrete offer; this one doesn't.
- CTA copy is generic ("SHOP NOW") rather than specific to the product ("Meet the Goose," "Shop the Silly Goose Duo").
- No urgency or time-bound element anywhere in the hero.
- This is the brand's Nth consecutive "new character reveal" send in under two weeks (Sky Puppy Moth, Pochacco, Harry Potter bears, Berry Cute Duo, now Silly Goose) — the format is becoming predictable and risks fatigue for anyone who's opened the last few.
- The "Spooky Stuff Coming Soon" module has no visual payoff — it's a red panel with cropped bear ears and a SIGN UP button, but no actual product or date, so it can't earn a click on its own.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or bundle anchor to the hero — even "Duo starting at $X" would give the email something concrete to click toward, since the past two months of data show discount-led sends outperform pure character-reveal sends for this brand.
- 2. Swap the CTA from generic "SHOP NOW" to product-specific copy — "Meet the Goose" or "Shop the Duo" — to break the sameness with the last five sends' identical button copy.
- 3. Give "Spooky Stuff Coming Soon" a visual teaser (an actual product silhouette or a date) instead of just cropped ears — right now it can't earn interest on its own.
- 4. Vary the subject-line formula — "Meet Our NEW [Character]!" has now run multiple times in slightly different wording; a benefit- or size-led subject would stand out more in a thread of near-identical past sends.
