# Make Your Own Cat in the Hat for the Movies!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong licensed-movie hero stapled to a catalog email with no offer. "WATCH THE NEW MOVIE TOGETHER!" over a large Cat in the Hat plush with a red "SHOP NOW" button is clear and on-theme. The "ONLY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6!" badge makes the timing credible, since the send date is Nov 6.
- After the hero the email splits into separate pitches. An orange Advent Calendar sale banner sits above the hero. A shipping-deadline band comes next. Then a Grinch block, a Thing 1 / Thing 2 block, a "Most Loved Stuff" grid and a "More to Explore" pair. It has seven or more "SHOP NOW" buttons and no price or percentage anywhere. This matches the pattern in the Nov 1 Snowflake Kitty send and the Nov 3 Grinch send, which also led with a fine hero and then diluted it.
- Judgement: a good subject and hero that the rest of the email undermines. It reads as a competent Seuss tie-in, but I'd have no reason to click today rather than next week.

## What's working

- The hero is big and clean. The Cat in the Hat plush dominates and the headline is high-contrast.
- The "ONLY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6!" badge ties the product to a real date.
- The Dr. Seuss thread continues through the Grinch and Thing 1 / Thing 2 blocks, which makes the middle feel curated.
- The shipping-deadline band (Hanukkah by Nov. 21, Christmas by Dec. 14) gives a useful, credible urgency cue.
- The subject matches the hero, so there is no mismatch after the open.

## What's weak

- The email has no offer. The only discount cue is the "Advent Calendar ON SALE NOW" banner, which has no amount and is unrelated to the subject.
- There are too many equal-weight "SHOP NOW" buttons. The Grinch block, Thing 1 / Thing 2 block, product grid and bottom pair all compete with the hero.
- The Sanrio product names render with a visible encoding glitch ("SanrioÂ® Berry Plush", "SanrioÂ® Cherry Plush").
- The Grinch block (green border) and the Thing 1 / Thing 2 block follow the Cat in the Hat hero without a clear reason to prefer one over another. The generic "Most Loved Stuff" and "More to Explore" sections, with the "holiday stuff you love" ornament tile, dilute the movie theme.
- The advent banner sitting above the hero pulls attention from the movie before the eye reaches the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one job. Keep the hero, a single row of Seuss products, and the shipping band. Drop the "Most Loved" grid and "More to Explore".
- 2. Make the CTA specific. Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Get the Cat in the Hat" and use distinct button copy per block.
- 3. Put real value in the hero, such as a bundle, free ears or accessory, or a price, so the movie date has a reason attached.
- 4. Move the advent banner below the hero, or cut it. Fix the "SanrioÂ®" encoding glitch before the next send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound, with a table layout, a hidden preheader and responsive media queries. The QA run flags 5 links as 403, a 0-character plain-text part, 4 images without alt text, and missing List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers. Several of those flags are probably relay or probe artifacts rather than defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on 5 buildabear.com links.** All 5 are `[FAIL] Broken link (403)`:
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/holiday`
  - The domain is Demandware/SFCC, and a uniform 403 on both product pages and a category page points to bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe, not dead pages. It is not confirmed as broken. Re-test with a browser-like client or a real device before treating it as a defect.
- **22 tracking links were never probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Identical `utm_content=Promo` on every link.** It appears on all four "SHOP NOW" product links and on the `/holiday` banner link. Per-link click attribution in analytics is not possible.
- **`utm_term` carries campaign metadata.** It holds the full send string (`US-110626_US_CatintheHatMovieWeekend__Email_Broadcast_Sustain_Seuss_Omni_NoTier_Q4_MovieTV__Segment-20261106`). That is a misuse of the field, and the string is very long.
- **Link text is empty in the QA data.** The four "SHOP NOW" links report no accessible name beyond the button text, and the `/holiday` link's name is an image alt string, truncated in the QA output.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images:**
  - `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`
  - Each is hosted on `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. With images blocked, these tiles render blank.
- **Plain-text part missing.** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. The relay may have dropped it, but if it is also absent at the source, it is a deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **Probable mojibake in the preheader.** The source shows `Dr. Seussâ€™ The Cat in the Hat Is Here!`, a UTF-8 apostrophe decoded as Windows-1252. The meta charset is `utf-8`, so this may be a capture artifact. If it is real, the inbox preview text is corrupted.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 ">` has a trailing space. `color-scheme` is declared as `light dark`, but the truncated source shows no `prefers-color-scheme` rules, so dark-mode behavior can't be confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**. The QA tool notes these headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Both are unconfirmed, not proven absent. If they are truly missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Check the raw headers at the original recipient.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so neither could be checked. The sending domain `ebuildabear.com` is a subdomain of the brand, so From-domain alignment looks plausible but is untested.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are present and consistent across links.** All five links carry `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=CatintheHatMovieWeekend` and `utm_id=2804426`.
- **Non-standard values.** `utm_source=Campaign` is not a source name, and `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized. Analytics tools are case-sensitive, so this can split `email` and `Email` into separate channels.
- **Landing continuity is unverified.** None of the 5 destinations could be loaded because of the 403s. The two sampled tiles are Sanrio and Maleficent products, but the campaign name and subject refer to Cat in the Hat. Whether those pages match the email's promise needs a manual click-through.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-verify the 5 flagged links with a real browser or an allowlisted probe. If they load, suppress the 403 false positive for `buildabear.com`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images, and make the "SHOP NOW" links name the product (for example via `aria-label`).
3. Give each link a unique `utm_content` (for example `promo_sanrio_berry` or `holiday_banner`). Move the send and segment metadata out of `utm_term`.
4. Normalize UTMs to lowercase (`utm_medium=email`) and use a real source value.
5. Confirm the plain-text MIME part exists in the original send.
6. Inspect the raw headers at the destination inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and address.
7. Check the preheader encoding on the original message. If the mojibake is real, serve the apostrophe as `&rsquo;` or ensure UTF-8 end to end.
