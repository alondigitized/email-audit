# Meet Our NEW Snowflake Kitty! ❄️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand holiday send that has no clear job. The subject sells Snowflake Kitty, a new plush. The orange banner above the hero sells an Advent Calendar sale. The shipping-deadline band, the ornament block, the gift-ideas tile and an eight-product grid then pull in other directions. The icy blue hero is lovely and the "SNOW CUTE" copy is on voice. After that, the email turns into a catalog.
- It also repeats the brand's pattern. After the 10/24 "Up to 50% OFF Online!" send, which scored 7 with a hard number, Build-A-Bear went back to no-offer discovery emails on 10/27, 10/28, 10/29 and 10/30. This one is the fifth in a row. The only offer language is "ON SALE NOW" on the Advent Calendars, with no percentage. The fine print says it's valid only through Nov 30 and "can't be combined", which makes it feel less generous than it sounds.
- Verdict: a competent, low-urgency launch email. The Nov. 21 and Dec. 14 delivery deadlines are the only real urgency, and they sit below the fold. Pick one story and lead with it.

## What's working

- The hero is clear and seasonal. The snowflake plush on a snowy cabin backdrop, with a bold "SNOW CUTE" headline and a white "SHOP NOW" button, makes the eye land there first.
- The subject and hero match. "Meet Our NEW Snowflake Kitty!" is delivered directly by the first image.
- The "order by" dates (Nov. 21 for Hanukkah, Dec. 14 for Christmas) are credible, time-bounded urgency and make a useful holiday service message.
- The color-blocked bands (blue, maroon, light blue) make the email easy to scan and keep the sections distinct.
- Every product tile has its own red "SHOP NOW" button, so the click path is easy to follow.

## What's weak

- The only offer is "Get Your Advent Calendar Yet? They're ON SALE NOW!" It has no percentage or price, and it competes with the Snowflake Kitty hero.
- The Axolotl PJ Sleeper Gift Set appears twice in the product grid, which makes the grid look padded.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW" (hero, ornament, grid, bottom tiles). None names a product, so none tells you where it goes.
- The hero says Snowflake Kitty comes "in two sizes" but gives no price or size names.
- The email has at least six competing themes: Advent, shipping deadlines, ornaments, gift ideas, a product grid and the Grinch/gingerbread tiles. None of them gets the weight a single hero offer would.
- The fine print is tiny, low-contrast blue-on-blue. It carries the sale end date (Nov 30) and the no-combining restriction, which feels buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either make Snowflake Kitty the whole email, with price, both sizes and a "Meet the Kitty" CTA, or make the Advent sale the hero with a real number.
- 2. Put a number on the offer. Replace "ON SALE NOW" with something like "Advent Calendars: Save $X through Nov. 30" and move it into the hero.
- 3. Move the shipping-deadline band up near the top as a slim banner. It is the most credible urgency in the email.
- 4. Cut the grid to three to four distinct products and remove the duplicate Axolotl.
- 5. Use specific CTA copy: "Adopt Snowflake Kitty", "Shop Advent Calendars", "Get the Ornament".
- 6. Make the sale end date visible near the offer, not only in the fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Snowflake Kitty is here ❄️ + Advent sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our frostiest new friend just arrived`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two sizes, one frosty feline. Plus Advent Calendars on sale through Nov. 30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Nov. 21 for Hanukkah and Dec. 14 for Christmas.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly sound (XHTML doctype, viewport meta, preheader with a hidden spacer). Four product CTAs returned 403 during automated probing, four product images have no alt text, and the compliance and authentication headers couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 "SHOP NOW" links returned 403** on buildabear.com:
  - `/axolotl-stuffed-animal-pj-sleeper-gift-set/29486_31016.html`
  - `/cookiebara-stuffed-animal/032817.html`
  - `/axolotl-stuffed-animal-pj-sleeper-gift-set/29486_31016_23681_23681.html`
  - `/pawlette-bunny-plush-party-gift-set/33639_32815_20389.html`
- A 403 on a Demandware storefront usually means bot protection blocked the QA crawler. It does not prove the links are dead for subscribers. Treat them as unverified and re-check them with a browser-grade client.
- **None of the four failing links is the Snowflake Kitty.** The subject and preheader promote the Snowflake Kitty, but the failing URLs point to Axolotl, Cookiebara and Pawlette products. This is not a defect on its own. The kitty's own link isn't in the QA failures, and the visible source doesn't show where it points.
- **25 tracking links were skipped** because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Every probed link has the same `utm_content=Promo`.** The two Axolotl links (`29486_31016` and `29486_31016_23681_23681`) can't be told apart in analytics.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four product images have no alt text.** The files are `16231321229486_31016x20958016.jpg`, `74240700232817~x20116045.jpg`, `16231321229486_31016_23681_23681x20958016.jpg` and `94231331222601_32815_20389~x20010012.jpg`. They are all served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. Since they are probably product links, there is no accessible name when images are blocked, and screen readers will probably read the filename or URL.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The opening tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`.
- **The `<title>` shows an internal template name:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.
- **Minor:** the preheader uses the deprecated `<font class="">` element.
- No other rendering problems are visible. Outlook (MSO) settings, mobile media queries at 480px and 639px, and the `u+.em_body` Gmail fix are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The `utm_term` value `US-US_Testing_Hot-20261101` suggests a test or seed segment. That may be an intentional send label, but confirm it is not a leftover test value.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `buildabear@ebuildabear.com`, a subdomain. Alignment with the DKIM `d=` domain should be checked on the raw message.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked** because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are consistent across the probed links:** `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=SnowflakeKittyDuoLaunch`, `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261101`, `utm_content=Promo` and `utm_id=2802842`.
- **Casing is non-standard.** GA4 reports source and medium case-sensitively, so `Campaign`/`Email` can split from lowercase `email` traffic.
- **`utm_content` is not link-specific,** as noted in section 2.
- **Landing page alignment could not be checked.** The 403s prevented fetching the destinations, so I can't say whether the pages match the creative.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four 403 URLs with a real browser or a UA/IP allowlisted for the QA crawler, and confirm they resolve for subscribers.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the four product images (e.g. the product name).
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and replace the `<title>` with the subject or a human-readable title.
4. Make `utm_content` unique per link (e.g. `hero`, `axolotl_pj`, `cookiebara`) and use lowercase for source and medium.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results on the raw message. The relay may be hiding them.
6. Resolve the 25 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following check.
7. Verify that `Testing` in `utm_term` is not a test value shipped to the live audience.
