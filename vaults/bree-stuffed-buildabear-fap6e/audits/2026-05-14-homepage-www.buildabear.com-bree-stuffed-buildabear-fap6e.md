# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- The very first thing I see when I land on buildabear.com is a full-screen Cookiebot consent modal — not a bear, not a sale, not the Pokémon drop I was hoping to check. The actual homepage is completely wallpapered behind legal text. I can catch a sliver of colorful plush imagery at the bottom and a partial top banner about same-day delivery, but that's it. Whatever the brand is pushing right now, it's being held hostage by a GDPR wall.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All" persistent bottom bar** — I can see there are 4 active promos waiting for me. That little persistent shelf at the bottom survives the modal, which means if I accept cookies fast enough, I know there's something worth looking at.
- **Top announcement strip** — even partially clipped, I can read "Day Delivery Available in Select Locations." That's relevant to me as someone who builds in-store and occasionally panic-orders online to ship to a birthday party.
- **Modal renders cleanly** — Accept and Manage My Preferences buttons are clearly differentiated; no broken layout, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I came here to check the Pokémon collab or Christmas bear restock status and instead I'm reading GDPR legalese. This is the single biggest bounce trigger on the page.
- **Zero brand identity visible** — I cannot see a logo, a bear, a seasonal theme, or any product. A competitor could swap in their cookie wall and I wouldn't know the difference.
- **"Active Offers (4)" badge is tantalizing but unreadable** — I know there are 4 offers but I cannot see a single one. That's a missed moment to hook me before I dismiss the modal.
- **Top announcement bar is clipped** — it starts mid-word ("-Day Delivery") and the right side is cut off with just "Bu" visible. Whatever the full message is, I'm not getting it.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom banner or corner card** — a slim persistent strip instead of a full-screen takeover would let me actually see the homepage. GDPR compliance doesn't require nuking the entire first impression.
- **Surface one offer inside the modal or above it** — even a "🎉 Active Offers (4) — accept to unlock" message on the modal CTA would turn legal friction into a conversion hook.
- **Fix the top announcement bar truncation** — the banner text is clipping on mobile; "Same-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" should wrap or scroll rather than being chopped to "-Day Delivery."
- **Pre-load teaser imagery behind a dimmed modal** — let me see the hero (Pokémon collab? Mother's Day bear?) blurred in the background so I'm motivated to hit Accept instead of back-button.
