# Today Only: ALL Hello Kitty® and Friends Starting at $15 Online!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a flash-sale urgency email built around one clean premise: ALL Hello Kitty & Friends starting at $15, online today only. The hero executes the offer crisply — pink palette, recognizable Sanrio IP art, bold price anchor, and a single "SHOP NOW" CTA above the fold. That's the email doing its job.
- Below the hero, it adds two secondary modules — a "Make Your Own Mocha" cross-sell and a "Shop All Our Sanrio Friends" banner — which smartly expand the purchase basket without muddying the core offer. The "Most Loved Stuff This Week" section at the bottom is generic filler that undercuts the Hello Kitty theme by pivoting to plain teddy bears with no Sanrio branding. It's not broken, just dilutive.
- Overall, this is one of Build-A-Bear's cleaner promotional sends this month. The flash-sale mechanic ("Today Only") gives it more urgency than the 2-for-$30 Bag Charms send from July 13, and the IP appeal is broader than the Moodicorn niche play from July 14. The main drag is the weak bottom half and the absence of any loyalty/member pricing signal.

## What's working

- **Hero is above-the-fold and immediately legible** — "All Hello Kitty & Friends on Sale! Starting at $15" is readable in under two seconds.
- **Price anchor is bold and specific** — the $15 starting price in large type sets a concrete, low-friction entry point.
- **"Today Only" urgency is visible at the top** — creates a credible, time-bounded reason to act now.
- **Secondary Sanrio cross-sells are thematically coherent** — the Mocha and "All Sanrio Friends" modules keep the brand universe tight and give upsell paths without confusion.
- **Clean render, no visual bugs** — layout is crisp, images load, no text overlap or broken elements visible.

## What's weak

- **"Most Loved Stuff This Week" breaks the Sanrio theme** — Timeless Teddy, Happy Hugs Teddy, Miniature Plush, and Longhorn Cow have nothing to do with Hello Kitty; this section feels pasted in from a template.
- **No deadline clock or explicit end time** — "Today Only" is stated but there's no countdown or specific cutoff (e.g., "Ends at Midnight CT"), which weakens the urgency.
- **National Ice Cream Day tie-in is buried and confusing** — the sub-copy "Scoop up the savings for National Ice Cream Day!" adds a thematic wrinkle that isn't visually connected to anything else in the email.
- **No social proof or reviews visible** — given Hello Kitty's cult following, even a single "fan favorite" badge or review count would reinforce the buy signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut or swap the "Most Loved Stuff" module for more Sanrio SKUs** — the generic teddy bears are a basket-abandonment risk; a Hello Kitty bestsellers grid would keep the conversion path clean.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline timestamp** — change "Online Today Only!" to "Online Today Only — Ends 11:59 PM CT" so the urgency is concrete and credible.
- 3. **Make the National Ice Cream Day tie-in earn its space** — either drop it, or build a Sanrio ice cream product moment (a relevant character or product) so it connects visually to the offer.
- 4. **Surface a loyalty/member hook** — even a one-liner ("Bonus points for Bonus Club members today") would add a second reason to click for existing members.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$15 Hello Kitty® Flash Sale — Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `All Sanrio styles on sale — grab your favorites before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Starting at $15 today only — Hello Kitty, My Melody, Cinnamoroll & more.`
