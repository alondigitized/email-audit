# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I literally cannot see the homepage — a full-screen Cookiebot modal is blocking everything the second I land. The only brand signals I'm getting are a truncated blue banner at the top (something about "Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!") and a sticky "Active Offers (5)" bar at the very bottom. Build-A-Bear feels like a fun destination for what I do, but this first impression is a hard stop before the brand even says hello.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (5) View All"** sticky bar at the bottom — at least I know deals exist, even if I can't see them yet.
- **"Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!"** banner peeking through at the top — a useful logistics signal if I'm buying for a birthday this week.
- **Cookie modal itself renders cleanly** — "Accept" and "Manage My Preferences" are both clear, large tap targets. No broken layout on the modal.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie wall** — this is the entire homepage experience above the fold. I came here to see the Pokémon collab or check the spring seasonal lineup, not read GDPR boilerplate.
- **Banner text is cut off** ("Bu…") — even the one piece of brand content visible is truncated and unreadable.
- **No loyalty/rewards surface visible** — I'm a rewards member; there's zero acknowledgment of that before the modal forces a decision.
- **Zero product, character, or seasonal imagery** — the hero, the emotion, the stuffed animal magic: all buried under legalese.

## Recommendations

- **Reduce the cookie modal to a slim bottom banner** — many brands (Gap, Target) do a one-line "We use cookies. Accept / Manage" strip that doesn't nuke the entire homepage experience.
- **Make the top banner fully readable on mobile** — whatever "Day Delivery" promise is there, it's worth showing in full; truncated copy is wasted real estate.
- **Surface the rewards member badge or offer count in the banner** — if I'm logged in, show me my points or a members-only badge above the fold before anything else.
- **Pre-load a blurred/dimmed hero behind the modal** — even a ghosted view of the Pokémon collab or a seasonal bear would tell me I'm in the right place while the cookie prompt resolves.
