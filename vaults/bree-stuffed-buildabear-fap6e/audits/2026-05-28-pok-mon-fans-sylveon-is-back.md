# Pokémon Fans: Sylveon Is Back!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a character-return announcement — Sylveon is back as a limited-availability build. For the target subscriber (a Pokémon fan), the subject line "Pokémon Fans: Sylveon Is Back!" is as precisely aimed as mass-market retail email gets: direct audience address, named character, implied scarcity. The hero earns it — Sylveon is front and center, large, clean, and immediately recognizable in its floral-themed plush form. Adding Eevee as a secondary SKU gives the email a "collection" pull without fracturing focus.
- The email breaks down in the bottom half. A "Most Loved Stuff This Week" module — four unrelated products including Winnie the Pooh and a generic Timeless Teddy Bear — breaks the Pokémon immersion completely. It reads like a boilerplate filler block that nobody thought to swap out. Compared to the May 26 Sanrio send (8/10) which kept its character grid coherent top to bottom, this email lets the Pokémon momentum die on the scroll.
- No pricing anywhere. Every single CTA reads "SHOP NOW." "Limited Time Only" is stated but undated. This email has all the setup for a strong conversion email and then fumbles the specific execution signals that actually close the purchase.

## What's working

- Subject line "Pokémon Fans: Sylveon Is Back!" is textbook self-segmentation — a Pokémon fan opens this on reflex.
- Sylveon hero image is large, polished, and immediately recognizable — above the fold, exactly where it needs to be.
- Eevee section gives a second character SKU and creates a light "collect them both" pull without crowding the hero.
- Preview text adds new information the subject doesn't ("Special Event," "Limited Time Only") rather than repeating it.
- Clean render, consistent blue-and-pink Build-A-Bear brand palette, no visual bugs.

## What's weak

- "Most Loved Stuff This Week" (Pooh, Hello Kitty, Timeless Bear) is off-topic filler that breaks Pokémon immersion mid-email.
- Every CTA is generic "SHOP NOW" — character-specific copy ("Build Your Sylveon," "Make Your Eevee") was the obvious move and wasn't taken.
- Zero pricing anywhere — not even a "from $XX" anchor to give subscribers a purchase signal.
- "Limited Time Only" is unanchored urgency — no end date, no countdown, no credible deadline.
- The standalone "SHOP THE SPECIAL EVENT!" blue block below the hero adds nothing over the hero itself — redundant and visually flat.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Replace the "Most Loved Stuff This Week" module with a Pokémon-only shelf** — show other Pokémon BAB characters available. Keep the IP bubble intact through the footer.
- 2. **Write character-specific CTAs** — "Build Your Sylveon" on the hero, "Make Your Eevee" on the secondary. "SHOP NOW" is a wasted opportunity when the character is the whole point.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline** — "Available through June 2 only" turns vague scarcity into real urgency and gives subscribers a reason to act today rather than later.
- 4. **Add a starting price in the hero** — "from $XX" or a price badge on the product. No price = no purchase signal for fence-sitters.
- 5. **Cut the redundant "SHOP THE SPECIAL EVENT!" block** — the hero already handles this; the repeated block dilutes without adding.
