# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally cannot see the Build-A-Bear homepage right now — the entire screen is eaten by a Cookiebot cookie consent modal the second you land. The only brand signals I can see are a partial promo strip at the very top ("...ed Time Only: Bag Charms 2 for $30...") and an "Active Offers (4)" bar trapped at the bottom. Whatever they're pushing this season — collabs, seasonal bears, anything — it's invisible behind a wall of GDPR legalese. Not a great first impression.

## What's working

- **Partial promo strip:** "Limited Time Only: Bag Charms 2 for $30" is the one brand-relevant signal I can actually see. As someone who buys accessories and limited editions, bag charms are interesting — I'd want to know more.
- **"Active Offers (4)" ticker at the bottom:** at least it signals there's something worth dismissing this modal for. It creates mild pull to keep going.
- **Modal itself is functional:** Accept button is big, blue, and clear. No broken layout, no clipped text inside the modal. It does what it says.

## What's weak

- **The cookie wall is the entire first screen:** Every hero image, CTA, loyalty hook, collab banner — everything I came here for — is buried behind a legal consent form. For a returning customer like me who's been on this site before, this is especially annoying.
- **No homepage content is visible at all:** I can't evaluate seasonal relevance, Pokémon collab placement, or restock callouts because I can't see any of it.
- **"Active Offers (4)" without any preview:** Dangling a count without even hinting at what they are is classic dark-pattern teasing. Just show me the offer types.
- **No loyalty/rewards acknowledgment on landing:** I'm a rewards member — a "Welcome back" or visible points balance would mean something here. Zero sign of it above or around the modal.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie consent for returning visitors** (or at minimum, use a slim banner at the bottom instead of a full-page takeover) — most states/countries allow this for existing logged-in members.
- **Pin the promo strip above the modal** so "Bag Charms 2 for $30" and any collab callouts are always readable, no matter what compliance layer fires.
- **Replace the "Active Offers (4)" dead-end with a mini preview** — even "20% off + Free Shipping + 2 more" gives me a reason to hit Accept faster.
- **Auto-surface a loyalty callout in or near the modal** — something like "Member? Sign in to see your points" keeps the CRM loop alive even during the consent friction.
