## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML structure looks sound (XHTML doctype, MSO conditionals, responsive media queries, dark-mode meta). The QA run flagged 5 product/landing links returning 403, a 0-character plain-text part, 4 images with no alt text, and no unsubscribe or authentication headers captured. Some of these may be probe or relay artifacts, noted below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 links returned HTTP 403** (QA FAIL):
  - `/nine-tailed-kitsune-fox-stuffed-animal/034916.html`
  - `/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-frosted-kuromi-plush/035531.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/holiday`
- All are on `www.buildabear.com`. A uniform 403 across product and category pages points to bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe rather than dead pages. It is unconfirmed that real users are affected. Re-test with a browser-grade client.
- **Empty link text on 4 "SHOP NOW" findings.** The QA output shows the anchor text field blank, with "SHOP NOW" as the label. This is likely image-wrapped or button anchors.
- **20 tracking links skipped** (click-redirect domains), so their destinations were not verified.
- **Inconsistent UTM values:** `utm_content=Promo` is identical on every link, so per-module click attribution isn't possible. `utm_source=Campaign` and `utm_medium=Email` are non-standard generic values (not `email`).
- **`utm_term` is malformed.** It is about 150 characters long and contains a double underscore (`SanrioSwarovskiLaunch__Email_Broadcast`), which suggests an empty template variable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 product images have no alt text** (QA WARN), all from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `01250700834916~x20047103.jpg`
  - `35260802735531~x20052031.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters** (QA FAIL). The email has no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could also be a capture limitation of the relay.
- **Preheader shows mojibake in the captured source:** `Hello KittyÂ® Fans! âœ¨` instead of `Hello Kitty® Fans! ✨`. The document declares `charset=utf-8`, so this may be a capture or decoding artifact. If it is real, the preview text will render garbled in inboxes. Verify against the raw MIME `Content-Type` and transfer encoding.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens were visible in the truncated source. The `utm_term` double underscore noted above is the only hint of an empty variable.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (WARN). The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `ebuildabear.com`, so check alignment with the visible From domain.
- The truncated source does not show the footer, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link could not be verified.
- These three are data gaps, not confirmed violations. Confirm against the raw message headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are present and consistent across links (`utm_campaign=SanrioSwarovskiLaunch`, `utm_id=2806936`).
- Landing-page alignment could not be verified because every destination returned 403 to the probe.
- The campaign is named for the Sanrio/Swarovski launch, but one link goes to a `nine-tailed-kitsune-fox` product and two go to generic Teddy bears. Confirm these are intended companion items.
- Because `utm_content` is the same everywhere, in-email placement can't be tied to site behavior.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 5 flagged URLs with a real-browser UA or headless browser to separate WAF blocking from broken links.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images (`alt=""` only if they are decorative).
3. Add a `text/plain` MIME part.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the final inbox copy.
5. Verify the preheader's UTF-8 encoding in the raw MIME.
6. Use unique `utm_content` values per module (hero, product 1–4, holiday) and lowercase standard `utm_medium=email`.
7. Fix the empty variable in `utm_term` (`__`) and shorten it.
