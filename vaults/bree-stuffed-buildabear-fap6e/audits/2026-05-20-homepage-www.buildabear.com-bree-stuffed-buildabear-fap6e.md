# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Build-A-Bear and immediately hit a full-screen Cookiebot consent wall — the homepage is completely buried. The only things I can actually read are a half-cut announcement bar mentioning "Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" and a tiny "Active Offers (4) View All" strip peeking out at the very bottom. This brand knows its audience is families and collectors, but right now it's leading with a GDPR modal instead of a Pokémon drop or a seasonal bear.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All" bottom strip** — at least I know deals exist. The pill design and count badge are a smart persistent element; I just can't act on them yet.
- **Announcement bar copy** — "Day Delivery Available in Select Locations" is a real hook for me as someone who sometimes needs a last-minute birthday bear. Partial text, but relevant.
- **Chat bubble** — bottom-right support icon is visible and accessible even behind the modal. That's thoughtful for shoppers with questions mid-session.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen Cookiebot modal on first load** — this is the single biggest failure here. I came to see what's new, check if the Christmas bear is back in stock, or find the latest Pokémon collab. Instead I'm reading GDPR legalese. The modal is enormous and UK-flavored (references "Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited"), which feels out of place if I'm shopping US.
- **Announcement bar is cut off** — the right side reads "Bu…" and I can't tell what the full offer is. Half a message is worse than no message.
- **Zero seasonal or collab visibility** — I can't see a single bear, plush image, or campaign hero. For a brand that runs constant IP collabs and seasonal drops, there's no visual reward for opening the app.
- **No rewards/member acknowledgment** — I'm a rewards member. The homepage doesn't greet me with a points balance, a birthday perk, or even a "sign in" nudge that might surface personalized content.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom banner, not a full-page takeover** — a slim "We use cookies. Accept / Manage" bar at the bottom lets the hero breathe and lets me actually see the product. Every second this modal is up is a second I'm not seeing the bear I came for.
- **Surface the Pokémon/collab banner in the announcement bar** — if there's an active IP drop, that strip at the top is premium real estate; swap generic delivery copy for "NEW: [Collab Name] Bears — Shop Now."
- **Add a rewards-member persistent chip** — a small "Hi Bree — 340 pts" pill in the header (or the announcement bar) immediately tells me the site knows me and nudges me toward the next rewards tier.
- **Fix or complete the truncated announcement bar** — ensure the full message fits or carousel properly so I'm not reading a half-sentence value prop.
