# The Grinch and Max Are the Ultimate Festive Duo!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong hero attached to a diffuse email. The Grinch and Max in front of a decorated tree is the best creative Build-A-Bear has sent in this run. "Direct from Who-ville: It's the Grinch!" is a clear headline, and the red SHOP NOW button is easy to find. For a licensed-character launch, the top third works.
- Below the hero, the email loses focus. It carries two more licensed-character bands (Polar Express, Christmas Vacation), a shipping-deadline banner, a four-product grid and a two-tile "More to Explore" block. The orange Advent Calendar sale bar sits above the hero and competes with it, which is the same problem as the 11-01 Snowflake Kitty send. The only real offer is the Advent Calendar sale, and it is a one-line banner with no price, so the email has nothing concrete to act on.
- The email is competent, on brand and seasonal, but it still has no price. It fits the 5-6 range of the last week's sends.

## What's working

- The Grinch and Max hero is warm, festive and instantly recognizable, and the headline pairs with it cleanly.
- The shipping deadlines (Hanukkah Nov. 21, Christmas Dec. 14) are specific and credible for the season.
- The Polar Express and Christmas Vacation bands give strong nostalgia, and the "Merry Clarkmas!" tile has personality.
- The "Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid uses clean product shots, each with its own button.
- The layout is consistent, and each section has its own button.

## What's weak

- The Advent Calendar sale is the only offer, and it is buried in a thin orange bar with no discount amount.
- Every tile uses the same "SHOP NOW" label, so none of the buttons says what it leads to.
- The product names show a text-encoding glitch: "SanrioÃ‚® Cherry Plush."
- The "More to Explore" tiles are vague. The gingerbread tile has no label, and the ornament tile only says "the holiday stuff you love," which repeats the 11-02 send.
- The email has too many competing sections for a Grinch-led send. The classic-story bands and the best-seller grid dilute the hero.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real, priced offer in the hero zone, such as a Grinch and Max bundle price or the Advent Calendar discount. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the email down to the Grinch hero, the shipping deadlines and one secondary block.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific buttons: "Get the Grinch," "Shop Max," "Shop Advent Calendars."
- 4. Fix the Sanrio character encoding, and label the "More to Explore" tiles.
- 5. Move the Advent Calendar sale out of the top bar, or make it a proper secondary offer with its discount stated.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Four product CTAs returned HTTP 403 in the automated probe. The plain-text part is empty, and the four product images have no `alt` attribute. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results checks could not be confirmed because the relay may have stripped those headers. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to the visible head, the preheader, and the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **403 on all four "SHOP NOW" product links.** Each URL is on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/fresno-nightcrawler-posable-plush/034979.html`
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
- A 403 from a retail site like this is often bot or WAF blocking of the probe, so it isn't confirmed as a real break. Re-test with a browser user agent or a real browser before treating the links as dead.
- **Unverified click tracking.** 24 tracking-redirect links were skipped in the HTTP probe. Their redirect targets and final destinations are unverified.
- **Direct product URLs.** The four product links are direct buildabear.com URLs that carry UTMs, so they appear not to go through the click-tracking redirect. The 24 skipped links are presumably the ones that do.
- **Empty link label in the QA output.** The "Broken link" entries show an empty label before the link text. This is likely a QA tool artifact. I can't confirm it from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt on four product images**, all served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
  - `08250701634979~x20230105.jpg`
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
- Each image sits beside a "SHOP NOW" CTA. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no product context.
- **Malformed markup.** A stray `</span>` follows the preheader `<div>` with no matching opening tag: `...</div> </span><!-- AMP -->`. It is invalid HTML and may trigger quirks in some clients.
- **Dark mode declared but not visibly implemented.** The head declares `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes`. The visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules, so clients that honor the meta may auto-invert colors unpredictably. The rest of the source was truncated, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Template name in `<title>`.** The title is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`, a template artifact. Some clients and web-view versions show it.
- **Plain-text part is empty.** The text version is 0 characters. This is a deliverability and accessibility failure, and it is the only FAIL in the deliverability checks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the truncated HTML or the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers at the sending ESP.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown.** The Authentication-Results header was not captured through the relay. Verify against the raw message at the receiving mailbox.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they are not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **A test-segment value is in production UTMs.** All four product links carry `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261103`. The "Testing" segment name suggests a test or seed-list send, or a mislabeled production segment. If it is the latter, analytics will attribute production traffic to a test segment.
- **Inconsistent UTM conventions:**
  - `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value that doesn't identify the sending platform.
  - `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized, while GA4's default channel grouping expects lowercase `email`. Capitalized values can land traffic in "Unassigned" or a separate row.
- **UTMs are consistent across the four checked links.** They share `utm_campaign=GrinchDuo`, `utm_content=Promo`, and `utm_id=2803340`.
- **Same `utm_content` on every link.** `utm_content=Promo` is identical across all four product links, so per-product or per-slot click performance can't be separated.
- **Landing pages not validated.** The 403s block any check that the pages match the promo, such as Grinch/Max product relevance. The four products are Sanrio, Happy Hugs, Fresno Nightcrawler, and Batty Kitty. Whether they align with the "Grinch and Max" subject line is a content question, so I'm only flagging that it wasn't verified.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Re-test the four product URLs with a browser user agent or real browser. If they load, mark the 403s as false positives from bot blocking.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part, since it is currently 0 characters.
3. Add meaningful `alt` text to the four product images.
4. Remove the orphaned `</span>` after the preheader div.
5. Replace the `<title>` with a real subject or brand title instead of the template name.
6. Confirm `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261103` is intended for a production send. If not, correct the segment label.
7. Lowercase `utm_medium` to `email`, and use a descriptive `utm_source`, such as the ESP or list name.
8. Use a distinct `utm_content` per link or slot, for example per product.
9. Check the raw headers at the receiving mailbox for List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, since the relay can't confirm them.
10. Either add `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules or remove the `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags.
11. Follow the redirects on the 24 skipped tracking links to confirm they resolve to valid, correctly parameterized destinations.
