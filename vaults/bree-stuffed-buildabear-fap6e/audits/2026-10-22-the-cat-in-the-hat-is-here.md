# The Cat in the Hat Is Here!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean licensed-collab launch email, and it carries no offer. The hero shows the plush Cat in the Hat with two Thing 1 and Thing 2 bears, and a "Cat in the Hat ONLY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6!" badge sits in the corner. The headline "MAKE A LITTLE MISCHIEF TOGETHER!" has a clear red SHOP NOW button under it. The subject promises the product and the body delivers it. This follows the Lloromannic™ launch on 2026-10-21 with the same format: a collab hero and no discount.
- The body is a clean stack of three tiles: Shop the Cat in the Hat, Shop Thing 1, Shop Thing 2 and Shop the Fish. Each tile links to a specific character, which helps. The email then dilutes itself. After a second SHOP COLLECTION button, it adds a Grinch banner and an "Oh, the Places You'll Go!" bear block, so three franchises compete in one send. The Grinch block repeats the theme of the 2026-10-18 "He's a Mean One" email. After the 2026-10-19 free-shipping send, a subscriber looking for a deal will find none here.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand launch send with a strong hero and good product tiles. It is held back by no offer, no urgency beyond the movie date, and two extra franchises at the bottom.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong character shot, and the Cat in the Hat's striped hat draws the eye first.
- The movie date badge, "ONLY IN THEATERS NOVEMBER 6!", gives a concrete tie-in to the release.
- The red SHOP NOW button is large and clear, and it sits right under the headline.
- The per-character tiles (Cat, Thing 1, Thing 2, Fish) are scannable, with big type and one product each.
- The header's "Start Earning Points!" pill is visible, so the loyalty program is present.

## What's weak

- There is no offer at all, with no price, discount or shipping hook. The email relies purely on novelty.
- The Grinch banner and the "Oh, the Places You'll Go!" block compete with the collab and split attention.
- The body copy under the headline, "Watch the wildly fun romp...", is small and weak. It is about the movie, not the plush.
- The hero's primary CTA says only "SHOP NOW", and there is a second generic "SHOP COLLECTION" button below the tiles. Both are vague.
- There is no price, review or availability signal on any product tile, and no limited-time language.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a reason to buy now, such as an early-access window, a limited-run label, or a Thing 1 and Thing 2 bundle price. That single change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Cut the Grinch and "Oh, the Places You'll Go!" blocks, or move them into a separate send. Keep this email about one collab.
- 3. Rewrite the hero CTA to be specific, for example "Meet the Cat in the Hat".
- 4. Add a price or "from $XX" line, plus a "new" or "limited" label, on each character tile.
- 5. Replace the movie-promo sentence with a plush-focused line.
