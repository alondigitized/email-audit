# NEW Mystical Werewolf in Stores and Online!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's most focused Halloween send in the recent run — one hero bear (Mystical Werewolf in a "Will Howl for Candy" tee, glowing purple smoke backdrop) carrying a single clear theme, unlike the busier "Promise Pets" grid from 2026-08-10 or the licensed-IP chase of the Pokémon sends. The hero shot is genuinely strong: good color contrast, a distinct character, unambiguous "SHOP NOW" right under it.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold. A mystery-egg blind-box block ("MystFURRY Friends") and a "the spooky stuff you love" group shot both compete for the same Halloween real estate the hero already claimed, and neither is the werewolf from the subject line. By the time you hit "Shop By Product," the werewolf is one of four unrelated plush (a mint cat, two pink/blue non-Halloween animals) — the subject's promise gets diluted fast.
- Net: strong hero, weak follow-through, and a subject line that overpromises relative to what's actually being sold. Middle of the brand's recent pack — better than the diffuse Promise Pets email, worse than the tightly-executed Espeon/Umbreon send.

## What's working

- Hero image is on-brand, high-contrast, and directly matches the subject line ("Mystical Werewolf").
- "HAPPY HOWL-OWEN!" headline + one-line subhead + single CTA is a clean, uncluttered top section.
- "Shop By Product" grid gives each item its own price-free but clear CTA button, easy to scan.
- Footer nav (Find a Workshop / Shop Best Sellers / Plan a Party) and social icons are present and tidy, no broken elements.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or offer anywhere in the email — pure browse email, nothing to feel urgency about.
- The "Mystery Plush" egg block and "spooky stuff" collage both crowd out the werewolf after the hero, working against the subject's single-product promise.
- "Shop By Product" mixes Halloween (werewolf) with clearly non-Halloween items (Hello Kitty flavors, Blueberry Highland Cow) with zero explanation — visually disjointed.
- "More to Explore" section (a purse-plush combo, a beach-themed set) has nothing to do with Halloween or the werewolf, further diluting the send's focus in its second half.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the Mystery Egg and "spooky stuff you love" blocks below the "Shop By Product" grid, or drop one entirely — let the werewolf hero flow straight into a Halloween-only product grid so the email stays on the promise made in the subject.
- 2. Replace the mixed "Shop By Product" grid with 4 Halloween-specific plush; hold the Hello Kitty/cow items for a separate send.
- 3. Add a concrete offer or urgency cue (e.g., "in stores & online now" is on-brand but a limited-time framing like "before it's gone" would lift click intent, matching the credible-deadline gap flagged in past reviews).
- 4. Swap "More to Explore" for Halloween accessories (candy pails, costumes) instead of unrelated spring/summer items — the beach photo is especially jarring in an October-themed email.
