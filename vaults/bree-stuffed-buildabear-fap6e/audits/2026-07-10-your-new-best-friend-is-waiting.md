# Your New Best Friend Is Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a Sanrio/Hello Kitty licensed product catalog send — a straightforward collection browse email leaning on the Sanrio IP to drive awareness and shop-through. The hero line "Your Next Best Friend Is Waiting" echoes the subject almost word-for-word, which is a missed opportunity; it adds zero new information. The four-product Sanrio grid below the hero does exactly what it should: show inventory variety. But there's no offer. No discount, no bundle deal, no deadline. For a brand that has spent the last three weeks training subscribers on flash sales (50% off Cub Condo, $15 furry friends), landing in someone's inbox with zero price incentive and zero urgency feels like a creative reset with no payoff.
- The Hello Kitty "Giant Mocha" feature mid-email is the most interesting moment — a specific product callout with a lifestyle photo — but it gets buried below the fold behind four grid items. The "Scoops of All Sizes" section beneath it compounds the clutter: two products, small images, hard to read product names. By the time you reach the four CTA buttons (Build, Personalize, Giftshop, Workshop), the email has lost its thread entirely.
- Verdict: this is a catalog filler between event promos. It will move some Sanrio impulse buyers but won't perform anywhere near the Cub Condo or $15 flash-sale sends.

## What's working

- **Sanrio IP does the heavy lifting** — Kuromi, Hello Kitty, and Cinnamoroll are immediately recognizable to the core audience; no explanation needed.
- **Hello Kitty Giant Mocha callout** is the most specific and visually distinct moment in the email — lifestyle photo breaks the grid monotony.
- **Navigation CTA buttons** at the bottom are cleanly formatted and cover the main shopping actions (build, personalize, gift shop, find workshop).
- **No render bugs** — images loaded, text is legible, layout holds on mobile.

## What's weak

- **Subject line mirrors hero copy exactly** — "Your New Best Friend Is Waiting" (subject) vs. "Your Next Best Friend Is Waiting" (hero). Almost word-for-word, so opening the email teaches you nothing new.
- **Zero offer** — after three consecutive discount-driven emails (50% off, $15 flash, HOURS LEFT), this send gives the subscriber nothing to act on urgently.
- **Product names are unreadably small** in the four-product Sanrio grid — "Sanrio® Hello Kitty® and Friends Wickedly Cute Kuromi Plush" is truncated or microscopic at mobile scale.
- **"Scoops of All Sizes" section** is visually weak — two small product images, minimal copy, no price, no differentiating detail. It reads like an afterthought.
- **Four generic footer CTAs dilute attention** — asking someone to simultaneously build, personalize, visit the gift shop, AND find a workshop is no ask at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an offer.** Even "Free personalization with any Sanrio plush" or "Buy 2 Sanrio friends, save $10" turns a browse email into a transaction email. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Let the hero introduce something the subject didn't.** If the subject is "Your New Best Friend Is Waiting," the hero should name the collection, price, or deadline — not repeat the same phrase with one word swapped.
- 3. **Surface the Hello Kitty Giant Mocha above the grid.** It's the most distinctive product in the email; leading with a hero product and lifestyle shot beats opening with four small grid tiles.
- 4. **Cut the footer CTA count from four to one.** Pick "BUILD YOUR OWN BEAR ONLINE" or "SHOP SANRIO" — one verb, one destination.
- 5. **Increase product name type size** in the grid or drop the names entirely in favor of a single category label ("Shop Sanrio Collection →").
- **Subject Alt A:** `Shop Sanrio: Kuromi, Hello Kitty & More Are Here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hello Kitty Giant Mocha just dropped — shop now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Sanrio plush in every size — build your favorite or grab one ready-made.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kuromi, Cinnamoroll, Hello Kitty — all available now online and in stores.`
