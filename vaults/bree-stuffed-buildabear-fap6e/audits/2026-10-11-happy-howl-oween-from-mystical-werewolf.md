## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has solid responsive scaffolding: an MSO/VML namespace, a preheader with a padding hack, and media queries at 480px and 639px. The QA run found 4 product-image `alt` omissions and 4 "SHOP NOW" links that returned 403 to the probe. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, not because they failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 CTA links returned HTTP 403** to the automated probe. All are on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/sanrio-starry-sky-hello-kitty-plush/035171.html`
- A uniform 403 across every product URL on one host points to bot or WAF filtering of the probe's user agent. It is not confirmed as a dead link. Treat it as **unverified, not broken** until the URLs are checked with a real browser UA.
- **The QA report lists the link text but no `href` text for the failing anchors** (`Broken link (403):  (link text: "SHOP NOW")`). The blank field in the report is a QA-tooling gap, not necessarily an email defect.
- **23 tracking/redirect links were skipped** and are unvalidated. Their destinations, redirect chains and expiry are unknown.
- The product links carry full UTM sets and are not wrapped in a redirect. That is good for transparency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing `alt` text** (confirmed by QA). They are the product images served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `98250800435171~x20127120.jpg`
- With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the product tiles will have no descriptive text. Because the "SHOP NOW" CTAs all share identical link text, the `alt` text is the only thing that distinguishes the products.
- **The `<title>` exposes an internal template name:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. This is cosmetic, but it shows in web-view and some clients' title bars.
- **Dark mode is declared but not implemented.** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes` are present. The `<style>` block has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules, so clients will auto-invert unpredictably.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - The preheader uses `<font class="">` with an empty class.
  - The `<meta name="color-scheme">` tags are not self-closed under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
- **No issues found with:** the preheader implementation (`display:none`, `mso-hide:all`), the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` spacer hack, the viewport meta tag, or the Outlook DPI settings.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `${…}`). The source was truncated, so the footer and lower sections were not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likewise a relay artifact, not evidence of failure.
- The footer was not in the truncated source, so the physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link could not be checked.
- The `From` domain is `ebuildabear.com`, while the links point to `buildabear.com`. This is a normal ESP sending-domain split, and it makes DMARC alignment worth checking.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All four product links share a consistent UTM set: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=HalloweenWerewolf`, `utm_content=Promo` and `utm_id=2798803`.
- **`utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261011` contains "Testing".** It suggests this send went to a test or seed segment, or that a test value leaked into production tracking. If it was a live send, the segment label will pollute analytics.
- **`utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value.** It is not a source such as `email` or `braze`, so it will bucket poorly in analytics.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical across all four product links,** so click attribution can't be split by product or position.
- **Landing-page alignment could not be verified,** because the 403s blocked the probe. Product IDs (035170, 035255, 026518, 035171) match the image filenames, which is consistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four product URLs with a browser-like UA, or whitelist the QA probe. Then confirm they return 200 and that the products are in stock.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, for example "Sanrio Cherry plush". Alternatively, make the CTA link text product-specific.
3. Inspect the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment. Don't rely on the relay-captured headers.
4. Confirm that `Testing` in `utm_term` is intentional. If not, fix the segment naming before the send reaches production.
5. Use unique `utm_content` values per product or slot, such as `promo_sanrio-cherry`.
6. Either add `prefers-color-scheme` styles or remove the `color-scheme` meta tags. Also rename the `<title>` to a customer-facing string.
7. Resolve the 23 skipped tracking links with a HEAD or GET against the ESP's redirect domain, to check that the redirects resolve.
