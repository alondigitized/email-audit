# They’re Here! Introducing Mellow Moodies™
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean launch-follow-up send. It re-announces Mellow Moodies, which Build-A-Bear introduced on 2026-10-08 as "Introducing Mellow Moodies™", and the "They're Here!" subject signals the plush is now on sale. The hero is the same flat-lay: teens playing chess on a pink shag rug, with the plush among them. The hierarchy is good. The eye goes hero, then "Say Hello To Mellow," then the maroon Shop Now button, then three character blocks.
- The three character blocks (Willow, Huck, Mateo) are the strongest part. Each has a distinct color field and a sleepy-eyed plush, so there is a reason to click a specific character. But the email has no offer, no price, no availability detail and no urgency. "They're Here!" promises news, and the body gives a re-skin of last week's launch with the characters named. Compared with the 10-08 launch, which scored 7, this is a near-duplicate with less novelty.
- Judgement: pleasant, on-brand and well laid out, but a catalog send with no commercial hook. It's a mid-pack 5–6.

## What's working

- The three color-blocked character tiles (plum, navy, forest green) are easy to scan and give each plush an identity.
- The hero is warm and lifestyle-led, and it matches the "mindful" positioning.
- The copy is short and on voice: "calm and grounded friends are ready to match your vibe."
- The tile CTAs are character-specific ("Shop Willow," "Shop Huck," "Shop Mateo"), so each one links to a clear product.
- The layout is clean, with a single column and generous whitespace.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, bundle or shipping hook anywhere in the email.
- The hero repeats the 10-08 launch creative, so "They're Here!" feels stale rather than new.
- Two identical "Shop Now" buttons are generic. The first one competes with the character tiles below it.
- Huck's shirt text is cut off ("OWKEY TIRED"), and the mug crops against the tile edge.
- The footer nav (Find a Workshop, Best Sellers, Plan a Party) is unrelated to the launch and dilutes focus.
- There is no price, no "in stores and online" detail and no reviews, so a click has little to go on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to act now. Examples are a price on each character, "Free shipping over $X," or an "in stores today" line. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. Replace the 10-08 hero with a new image, such as a product-only shot of all three characters. Otherwise a repeat subscriber will feel they've already seen this.
- 3. Cut the first generic Shop Now button. Let the hero lead straight into the tiles, and keep one CTA below them.
- 4. Show a price on each tile ("Willow · $XX"), and add a "Meet the trio" line that says what makes each one different.
- 5. Fix the Huck crop so the shirt text reads in full.
- 6. Move the footer nav down or trim it to launch-relevant links.

The automated QA run couldn't confirm unsubscribe headers, authentication, or any link destination. Those gaps are mostly capture and relay limitations rather than confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer and body markup were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 21 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Final landing URLs, redirect chains, and link health are unconfirmed.
- **URL-heavy plain-text part:** 3,950 of 5,495 characters (~72%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. This is most likely long tracking URLs in the text/plain alternative. It is a minor spam-filter risk, not a functional break.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>`. Screen readers can't pick the correct pronunciation profile.
- **Internal template name in `<title>`:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. Some clients and web-view renderers expose this to users.
- **Dark-mode declaration without rules:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes` are set. The visible `<style>` block has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules. Clients will auto-invert colors with no designed fallback. The truncated source may contain these rules further down.
- **Deprecated markup:** the preheader uses `<font class="">`. This is harmless but non-standard.
- **Viewport meta typo:** `initial-scale=1.0 ` has a trailing space. Impact is negligible.
- **Unchecked:** image alt text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and link text were not in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible content (e.g., preheader "Huck, Mateo and Willow: Meet the Mellow Moodies™") is static product copy. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. QA attributes this to the relay.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they couldn't be verified.

The header findings are probably capture artifacts rather than confirmed sender failures. They should be checked against the raw message as originally delivered. The sender is `buildabear@ebuildabear.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be assessed. The probe skipped all 21 tracked links, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment with the Mellow Moodies™ launch are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the tracking links through a redirect-following probe (or resolve them manually) to confirm final URLs, UTM parameters, and landing page relevance.
2. Inspect the raw original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. If they are truly absent, add them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and replace the template-name `<title>` with the campaign title.
4. Either add `prefers-color-scheme` styles or drop the `color-scheme` metas to avoid unpredictable auto-inversion.
5. Shorten the URLs in the text/plain part, or add more plain copy, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
