# NEW Hello Kitty® and Friends Frosted Collection! ❄️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong product-launch email with a weak commercial frame. The hero, a lavender, snowy Hello Kitty and Friends frosted group, is the best creative in the recent run. "Flurries of Fun" with a dark navy "SHOP NOW" button gives the eye one place to land. The four stacked character bands (Hello Kitty, Pochacco, Kuromi, Snowman Mini Beans) are visually clean and each links to its own product, which is better than the generic category links in the Nov 3 Grinch email.
- The problem is that the email argues with itself. An orange Advent Calendar sale banner sits above the hero. A shipping-deadline block, a Mashimals promo and a "Back from the Vault" Bearemy promo sit below. A "Shop by Product" grid with unrelated items (Maleficent, Teddy Bear, Batty Kitty) closes it out. That is five or six jobs in one send, the same diffuse pattern as the Nov 1 Snowflake Kitty email. There is no price and no offer for the new collection. The Hello Kitty fan gets a beautiful launch and no reason to act today.
- Judgement: good creative, no urgency, mediocre commercial structure. It is a 6 again, as with most of the recent sends.

## What's working

- The hero is cohesive and on-season. The frosted palette matches the "Frosted Collection" name, and the Sanrio characters are instantly recognizable.
- "Flurries of Fun" is a clear, branded headline, and the single button under it is easy to find.
- The four character bands alternate alignment and background color, which makes the scroll easy and lets a fan jump straight to their character.
- The shipping-deadline block ("Hanukkah by Nov. 21, Christmas by Dec. 14") gives real, credible urgency further down.
- Product photography is clean, and the plush are shown in their outfits so the details are visible.

## What's weak

- The collection has no price, no offer and no "limited" or "exclusive drop" urgency beyond a small "Build-A-Bear Exclusive" tag in the hero.
- The orange Advent Calendar banner sits above the hero and competes with it. It is the most saturated element on screen, so it pulls the eye first.
- After the collection there are three more promos (Mashimals, Bearemy, Shop by Product). This dilutes the launch, and Mashimals was already the focus of the Oct 29 send.
- The button copy is generic. "SHOP NOW" appears about eight times, and the character bands use "SHOP FROSTED KUROMI" with no price or reason to buy.
- The Shop by Product grid has no connection to the collection. Maleficent and Teddy Bear do not belong in a Hello Kitty send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a price and a scarcity cue in the hero, for example "Frosted Hello Kitty from $XX, while supplies last." One concrete number would do more than any other change.
- 2. Move the Advent Calendar banner below the collection, or drop it from this send. It already got its own beat in the Nov 1 email.
- 3. Cut the Mashimals, Vault and generic product grid. Replace the grid with the matching outfits and accessories for the frosted plush to lift basket size.
- 4. Give each character band its own price and CTA, such as "Get Frosted Kuromi · $XX".
- 5. Move the shipping-deadline block up, directly under the hero button, since it is the only credible urgency in the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Frosted Hello Kitty & Kuromi just landed ❄️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hello Kitty's frosted collection: limited drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kuromi, Pochacco and snowman Mini Beans, all in icy blue. Order by Dec. 14 for Christmas.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusive Sanrio plush in winter outfits. Shop before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup is standard table-based XHTML with a hidden preheader and MSO settings. The QA run flagged 4 product-link 403s, missing alt text on 4 product images, and no capture of the unsubscribe or authentication headers. The 403s and missing headers may be probe or relay artifacts rather than real defects. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 × HTTP 403 on "SHOP NOW" product links.** All are on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/sanrio-starry-sky-hello-kitty-plush/035171.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  
  All four return 403, not 404, so this is most likely bot or WAF blocking of the probe rather than dead product pages. It is unconfirmed and needs a check from a real browser or a whitelisted client.
- **27 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped** and not probed. Redirect resolution and final destinations are unverified for the bulk of the email's links.
- **Repeated generic link text.** All four product CTAs read "SHOP NOW" with no product context (also see section 3).
- **Possible Disney link in a Sanrio campaign.** One link targets a Disney product (`disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush`) but carries `utm_campaign=SanrioFrostedLaunch`. If that link is intentional, the campaign tag is too coarse to attribute it correctly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images.** All are served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `98250800435171~x20127120.jpg`
  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  
  With images blocked or on a screen reader, these show nothing or a filename. Because they pair with a non-descriptive "SHOP NOW" link, the link purpose is lost.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **`<title>` is an internal template name:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. It is shown in some clients and in the browser-view tab.
- **Minor markup nits.**
  - The viewport meta has a trailing space: `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 "`.
  - The preheader has an empty `<font class="">`.
- **Otherwise sound.** The visible source has an MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, a hidden preheader with `mso-hide:all`, a `&zwnj;&nbsp;`/`&shy;` preview-text padding hack, and responsive breakpoints at 480px and 639px. The rest of the body was truncated, so semantic structure (such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables) is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `utm_term` value contains `Testing_Hot` (see section 6).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so presence is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated cause.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address are unverified.** The HTML source was truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm either.
- **Sender domain.** The From address is `buildabear@ebuildabear.com`, a subdomain separate from the click domain `www.buildabear.com`. The source doesn't confirm DKIM or SPF alignment for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistent across all four probed links:** `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=SanrioFrostedLaunch&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261105&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2804161`.
- **`utm_term` includes `Testing_Hot`.** This suggests a test or seed-segment label in a deliverable email, which could pollute analytics segments.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on all four product links.** It can't distinguish which creative or position drove a click.
- **`utm_source=Campaign` is generic.** It doesn't identify the ESP or list. This is a tagging-quality note, not a break.
- **Landing-page validity is unconfirmed.** The 403s above block verification that the PDPs match the promoted items.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 4 product URLs with a browser-like user agent or an allowlisted IP, and confirm the 403s are bot blocking rather than real access errors.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images (for example, product name plus "Shop now"). Make the link text product-specific.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and replace the `<title>` with a customer-facing title.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message at the source ESP, not through the relay. Confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com` the same way.
5. Check that `Testing_Hot` in `utm_term` is intended. Give each link a unique `utm_content` value.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) and the 27 skipped tracking links.
