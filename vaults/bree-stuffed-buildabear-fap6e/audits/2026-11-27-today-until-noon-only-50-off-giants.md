## 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe. Check the raw headers at the origin.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Consistent tagging:** All 6 probed URLs carry the same parameters (`utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=50OffGiants`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2807469`), so attribution should be uniform.
- **`utm_source=Campaign` is non-descriptive:** It identifies neither the platform nor the list, and it is mixed-case. GA4 treats UTM values as case-sensitive, so casing drift can fragment reports.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on every link:** Product tiles, hero and banner links can't be told apart in reporting.
- **`utm_term` carries a long send identifier** (`US-TARGETED_112726_..._Q4_Cyber_-20261127`) instead of a search keyword. That is a nonstandard use, and the string is long.
- **Landing alignment:** The links go to the product pages plus `/sale/black-friday` and `/holiday`. Whether those pages show the 50%-off Giants offer couldn't be verified, because the probe got 403s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images listed in section 3.
3. Re-test the 6 links with a browser-like UA or Playwright to confirm the 403s are bot-blocking. If they are, allowlist the QA probe or exclude those links from the check.
4. Inspect the raw message at the origin for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
5. Make UTMs more granular:
   - Use a lowercase `utm_source` that names the platform (e.g. `esp`).
   - Vary `utm_content` per module (hero, product 1–4, banner).
   - Move the send ID out of `utm_term` into `utm_id` or `utm_campaign`.
6. Change `&` to `&amp;` in the preheader.
