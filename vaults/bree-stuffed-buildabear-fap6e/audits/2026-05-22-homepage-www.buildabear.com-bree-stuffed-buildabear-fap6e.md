# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression? I can't actually see the homepage — a massive Cookiebot consent wall is eating the entire screen the second it loads. The only brand signals I'm getting are a thin blue announcement bar at the top saying "Same-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" and a barely-peeking "Active Offers (4)" strip at the very bottom. Build-A-Bear is currently showing me a legal disclaimer, not bears.

## What's working

- **"Same-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!"** — as a gift buyer who sometimes needs a bear for a birthday *tomorrow*, this blue banner actually registers. It's the one useful brand signal I can see.
- **"Active Offers (4) View All"** strip at the bottom — at least I know deals exist. That "(4)" is doing a tiny bit of curiosity work even buried under the modal.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen Cookiebot modal on first load** — this is the entire experience above the fold on mobile. I'm on my phone, I just want to check if the Pokémon drop is live, and I'm staring at GDPR copy instead.
- **No visible hero, seasonal content, or category navigation** — May is when Build-A-Bear typically runs spring and pre-summer promos; I have zero idea what's happening right now.
- **Cookie modal copy is dense and corporate** — "Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited (collectively 'we', or 'Build-A-Bear') and third party service providers acting on our behalf use cookies..." This is not the warm, playful brand voice I associate with BAB.
- **No rewards/member hint** — I'm a rewards member. I want to see my points or a member exclusive teaser before I even have to dig. Nothing.
- **"Active Offers (4)"** strip is tantalizing but unreachable — you can see it but can't tap it until you clear the modal, which is a dark-pattern-adjacent friction wall.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or reduce the cookie banner to a bottom bar** — a compact "We use cookies. Accept / Manage" strip at the bottom is legally sufficient in most jurisdictions and doesn't nuke the hero. The Cookiebot full-screen treatment is disproportionate for a toy retailer.
- **Put the seasonal or Pokémon collab teaser *in* the announcement bar** — right now that blue bar says "Same-Day Delivery." Swap or add a second slide: "Pokémon Collection — Shop Now" so collectors like me know there's a reason to stay before the modal loads.
- **Surface the rewards balance or a "welcome back" nudge in the sticky bottom bar** — the "Active Offers (4)" bar already exists down there; add a rewards-points chip so returning members feel seen the moment consent is cleared.
- **Compress the cookie modal text** — replace the multi-paragraph legal dump with a one-sentence summary and a "details" accordion. Less friction = faster path to actual shopping.
