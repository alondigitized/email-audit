## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly well-formed, but the QA run flagged four product CTAs that returned 403, four images with no alt text, and a 0-character plain-text part. Compliance headers and authentication results could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 CTAs returned HTTP 403** to the QA probe. All are "SHOP NOW" links on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear-red-satin-pajama-gift-set/26518_33171_23681.html`
  - `/gingerbread-axolotl-stuffed-animal/034239.html`
  - `/cookiebara-stuffed-animal/032817.html`
  - `/pawlette-bunny-plush-party-gift-set/33639_32815_20389.html`
- A 403 (rather than 404) on all four, with a clean product-path structure, points to bot or WAF blocking of the probe. It is probably not a dead page. A real-browser check would confirm this. This is unconfirmed.
- The 22 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so their destinations are unverified.
- The same `utm_content=Promo` appears on all four product links, so clicks can't be attributed per product or placement.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four product images have no alt text** (QA WARN), including `...26518_33171_23681~x20257012.jpg`, `...034239~x20328030.jpg`, `...032817~x20116045.jpg` and `...22601_32815_20389~x20010012.jpg`. All are served from `/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. With images blocked, these slots render blank.
- **Plain-text part is 0 characters** (QA FAIL). This could be an artifact of the AgentMail relay capture. If it is real, the message is HTML-only, which hurts clients that need text and can count against spam scoring.
- **`<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.** This is an internal template name that leaked into production. Some clients and web-view pages show it.
- **Orphan `</span>`** appears right before `<!-- AMP -->`, with no matching opening tag in the visible source. This is malformed markup.
- A `<style>` block sits inside `<body>` (the `div.preheader` rule). Some clients strip it, but the inline `display:none` on the preheader covers that case.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) are visible in the truncated source. The remainder of the HTML was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found** (QA WARN). The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm the sender omitted them. If they are absent at the source, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com` is unknown.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All four links carry the same parameters: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=PenguinPolarBearLaunch&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261028&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2802629`.
- **`utm_term` contains `Testing`** (`US-US_Testing_Hot-20261028`). This suggests a test or seed segment label shipped in a live send. It may also be the intended segment name.
- **`utm_campaign=PenguinPolarBearLaunch` doesn't match the subject** ("Wildly Cute Gift Ideas"). It may be a reused or mis-assigned campaign name, which would mix this send's analytics with another campaign's.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value, and the capitalized values (`Campaign`, `Email`) can split reports in case-sensitive analytics tools.
- Landing-page alignment could not be checked because the pages returned 403 to the probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four product URLs in a real browser or with a browser-like UA. If they load, whitelist the QA probe. If they fail, fix the links before sending.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images (for example, the product name).
3. Confirm a text/plain MIME part exists in the original send, and add one if not.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw source message, not the relay copy. Also check SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the original headers.
5. Replace the `<title>` with a real subject-aligned title, and remove the stray `</span>`.
6. Review the UTM taxonomy. Check whether `Testing` in `utm_term` is intentional. Align `utm_campaign` with this send. Use a distinct `utm_content` per product or slot. Standardize casing.
