# Make a Pair of Chococats™!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**

- The weakness is the rest of the email. Under the Chococat hero, it turns into a generic roundup. ## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup has a sound responsive structure and consistent UTM tagging. It ships with no plain-text part, with the unsubscribe and authentication headers unverified, and with four product CTAs that returned HTTP 403 in the automated probe.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Four "SHOP NOW" links returned 403 to the QA probe. All four are on `www.buildabear.com`, with the same UTM set (`utm_campaign=ChococatDuoResend`, `utm_id=2799217`):
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/fresno-nightcrawler-posable-plush/034979.html`
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
- A 403 on every product URL, with no 404s, points to bot-blocking (WAF or CDN) rather than dead pages. This is inferred, because the probe didn't capture response headers or body. A real client may load these pages fine. The failures are unconfirmed as user-facing breakage and need a browser-based check.
- 22 tracking or redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so their destinations were not validated.
- The link-text column in the QA output is blank for the failing URLs.

  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Plain-text part:** the QA check failed with a text version of 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and is a spam-filter signal.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**

- `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261010` looks like a test-segment label ("Testing") that leaked into the production send. It will pollute analytics segmentation.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value that doesn't identify the ESP or list. Capitalisation is also inconsistent with the usual lowercase convention, which can fragment reports.
- The subject and preheader focus on Chococat. Three of the four verified product links go to unrelated products: Maleficent dragon, Nightcrawler, and Sanrio cherry/berry. I can't tell from the truncated source whether a Chococat CTA exists above them, so landing-page alignment is unconfirmed.
- The campaign name ends in "Resend", so this is a resend. Recipients who already received the original may get a duplicate.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add alt text to the four product images, using the product names.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part. This is the highest-priority fix.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers from the sending ESP, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
4. Re-test the four 403 product URLs with a real browser user agent. If they load, allowlist the QA scanner or ignore the result.
5. Replace the test-segment `utm_term` value before production sends, and standardise `utm_source` casing.
6. Fix the "™" encoding in the preheader at the source, and remove the orphan `</span>`.
7. Rename the `<title>` to the campaign subject.
