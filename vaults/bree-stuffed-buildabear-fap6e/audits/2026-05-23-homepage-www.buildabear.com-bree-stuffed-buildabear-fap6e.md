# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I literally cannot see the homepage — a giant Cookiebot cookie-consent modal has taken over my entire screen the second I landed. The only thing I can read behind it is a partial announcement bar ("...Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!") and a "Active Offers (4)" bar at the very bottom. Build-A-Bear's homepage could be stunning right now for whatever Pokémon or summer collection is live, but I'll never know because Usercentrics is eating my first impression whole.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All" bar** — I can see it peeking at the bottom. That's actually a smart persistent strip that survives the modal, and as someone who hunts promos, I'd tap it the second I dismiss the cookie wall.
- **"Same-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!"** — the top announcement bar is partially visible and that's a hook I care about for last-minute birthday runs.
- **Cookie modal itself is functional** — two-button layout, "Accept" and "Manage My Preferences," no dark-pattern tricks like hiding the decline button.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen modal with no "X" to close** — I have to actively Accept or Manage before I can see anything. For a brand that sells joy and stuffed animals to kids, opening with a UK-flavored GDPR wall (Cookiebot by Usercentrics!) is a jarring tone mismatch.
- **Zero homepage content is visible** — hero, seasonal collection, Pokémon collab, rewards — all invisible. My entire reason for visiting is deferred.
- **No dismiss-and-browse option** — I can't even peek at the sale rail before deciding on cookies. This is a bounce risk for any mobile shopper with low patience.
- **Cookiebot branding is prominent** — "by Usercentrics" logo is front-and-center, which feels very enterprise/B2B for what should be a whimsical kids' brand homepage.

## Recommendations

- **Add an "X / Continue browsing" close button** to the cookie modal — let me start scrolling and surface the full consent prompt after one interaction (click, scroll, tap). GDPR still satisfied, bounce rate drops.
- **Shrink the modal to a bottom sheet** instead of a full-screen takeover — keep the hero visible above it so I can see the current campaign the second I land.
- **Pre-render the "Active Offers (4)" bar above the modal** so I can at least count the deals before I've consented to anything — it's already visible, just make it tappable.
- **If this GDPR wall is UK/EU-only, geo-fence it** — US shoppers (where Build-A-Bear's core traffic lives) shouldn't be hitting a UK consent experience on a US session.
