# Finally, a Porch Goose You Can Cuddle!🪿
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is another no-discount, product-push send from Build-A-Bear, and it's got the same "stack modules and hope something lands" structure as the 2026-09-05 "New Season" email that scored 4.0. The subject promises a "Porch Goose" tying into the viral goose-meme trend, but the hero just reads "Silly Goose on the Loose" with a pumpkin-costume goose plush — cute, on-brand, seasonally correct for Halloween, but there's zero price, zero % off, zero urgency anywhere in the render.
- The creative itself is fine: the goose-in-a-pumpkin-costume image is genuinely charming and probably the strongest visual asset in this campaign compared to recent sends. But after the hero, it's Pumpkin Pal Teddy (vault re-release, already sent 2026-08-20), a Mystery Plush egg, then a 4-item "Shop By Product" grid, then a 2-item "More to Explore" grid. That's five distinct offers/products competing for attention with no pricing on any of them.
- Judgement: a nice hero concept undercut by an unfocused, offer-free body. This reads like a catalog page forwarded as an email, not a single-idea send.

## What's working

- Hero art (goose in a jack-o'-lantern costume) is distinctive, on-theme for Halloween, and matches the subject's "porch goose" hook.
- Clear single CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits right under the hero headline, no scrolling needed.
- Color-blocked module design (purple → green → magenta) makes each section scannable even without reading copy.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, buttons are legible.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or offer anywhere in the email — every module is pure product push, same miss as the 2026-09-05 send.
- Pumpkin Pal Teddy is a repeat from the 2026-08-20 "NEW Vault Release" send, recut into a smaller module here — recycled creative, not new news.
- Four modules plus a 4-item grid plus a 2-item "More to Explore" grid is too much for one email — the goose (the subject's whole hook) gets buried after two more competing offers.
- "Shop By Product" grid mixes unrelated IP (Hello Kitty, Batty Kitty, Silly Goose, Mystical Werewolf) with no organizing logic, diluting the goose focus further.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to a single-product send: goose hero, one supporting shot (goose accessories or the plush alone), one CTA. The subject line already sets up a specific, funny hook — the email should pay that off, not bury it under three other offers.
- 2. Add a real price or offer callout under the hero (even "$25 | Includes sound") — right now there's no reason to click over browsing the site cold.
- 3. Drop the Pumpkin Pal Teddy module — it's a repeat asset from three weeks ago and steals attention from the goose the subject promised.
- 4. If the grids stay, retheme "Shop By Product" to Halloween-only items (drop Hello Kitty/Batty Kitty) so the whole email reads as one seasonal moment instead of a random catalog page.
