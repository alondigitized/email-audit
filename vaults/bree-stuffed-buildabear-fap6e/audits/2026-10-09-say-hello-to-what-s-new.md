## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML is mostly well-formed, with a preheader, dark-mode meta tags and responsive CSS. It has three confirmed defects: no plain-text part, four product CTAs that returned 403 to the QA probe, and missing alt text on the product images. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the AgentMail relay didn't capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Four "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403 to the QA probe.** All four are on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - `/disney-stitch-experiment-626-plush/033677.html`
  - `/fresno-nightcrawler-posable-plush/034979.html`
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
- A 403 is most likely bot-protection (WAF or CDN) blocking the automated probe, not a dead page. The QA data can't tell the two apart. The product IDs are well-formed and the email was sent today (2026-10-09), so confirm by opening the links in a real browser or mail client before treating them as broken.
- **The link text is empty in the QA report.** The "link text" field is blank for all four failures, even though the label is "SHOP NOW". This suggests the CTA may be an image or a VML button whose text isn't in the anchor. That is a minor extraction artifact, and I can't confirm it from the truncated source.
- **22 tracking links were skipped.** They are click-redirect domains, so the probe never checked them. Their destinations are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Four product images have no alt text.** The files and their `src` paths are on `www.buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`
  - `13240702133677~x20105099.jpg`
  - `08250701634979~x20230105.jpg`
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
- If images are blocked, or a screen reader is in use, these products have no text equivalent. Because the "SHOP NOW" anchors above also report empty link text, a screen reader may announce those links with no name.
- The `<title>` is an internal template name: `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. It is harmless in most clients, but it is a hygiene issue.
- **Possible stray `</span>`:** one appears before the `<!-- AMP -->` comment with no visible opening tag in the truncated source. I can't confirm this from the excerpt.
- The `color-scheme` meta tags are present, but I can't check the dark-mode behavior of the images from the source.
- The preheader and `&zwnj;&nbsp;&shy;` padding are correctly hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; overflow:hidden`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`). The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **The plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). That means a single-part HTML message with no `multipart/alternative`. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and accessibility tooling.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` were not found (WARN).** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message from the ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the sender domain `ebuildabear.com` couldn't be checked from this data.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated excerpt. I can't confirm CAN-SPAM compliance either way.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- **UTM tagging is consistent and complete** on all four product links: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=NewArrivals`, `utm_content=Promo`, and `utm_id=2799628`.
- **`utm_term` carries an internal campaign string** that includes a timestamp. Its value is `US-UNION_1791405667136_100926_US_NewArrivals__Email_Broadcast_Roundup_BAB_Omni_NoTier_Q3_Evergreen_-20261009`. This is atypical for `utm_term`, which is normally a keyword. The very long, high-cardinality value can fragment reports in analytics tools that split on the term.
- **Capitalization:** `utm_source=Campaign` and `utm_medium=Email` are capitalized. GA4 treats these case-sensitively, so they can split from lowercase `email` traffic. The source value `Campaign` also identifies nothing about the sender.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be checked.** The probe got 403s, so I couldn't confirm the pages load or that the product matches the CTA.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative so the message is `multipart/alternative`. This is the highest-priority fix.
2. Re-test the four product URLs from a browser or an allowlisted probe. If they load, treat the 403s as probe false positives. If not, fix them before the next send.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, for example the product name. Make sure the "SHOP NOW" anchors have an accessible name.
4. Inspect the raw message at the ESP, not through AgentMail, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
5. Lowercase `utm_source` and `utm_medium`. Move the internal campaign string out of `utm_term`.
6. Replace the template-name `<title>` with the actual subject line.
7. For future audits, capture full headers and the untruncated HTML so the footer and compliance items can be checked.
