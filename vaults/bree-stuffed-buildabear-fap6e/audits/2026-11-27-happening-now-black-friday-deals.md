# Happening NOW: Black Friday Deals!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Build-A-Bear Black Friday send I've seen today, and it mostly repeats the earlier ones. It stacks "Furry Friends starting at $10" (the same offer as this morning's "Black Friday Deals Starting at $10!") on top of "25% off Giant Furry Friends today only." That last one is a weaker version of the 50% off giants offer from "Today Until Noon Only." Nothing here is new, and the subject's "Happening NOW" promises urgency the body doesn't clearly deliver.
- The hierarchy is decent. The cream ribbon panel with "BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" lands first, and each offer panel has a green SHOP NOW button. But the top panel has no number in it, so the first screen is vague. The best offers, $10 and 25% off, sit lower. Below that the email gets busy, with an Advent banner, a delivery-deadline banner, four product tiles, a Grinch tile and a "Shop all" tile, all competing for attention.
- Overall it's a competent but redundant send. It's a reminder email for people who already saw the earlier ones, and it doesn't give them a fresh reason to click.

## What's working

- The panel-per-offer layout lets each offer ("$10" and "25% OFF") carry its own number and its own green SHOP NOW button.
- The "ENDING SOON!" badge and "TODAY ONLY" on the 25% panel give credible urgency.
- The "Order by Monday, Dec. 14" banner is a useful shipping-deadline cue for gift buyers.
- The product tiles (Hello Kitty, Snowflake Kitty, Kitsune Fox) have clear images and names, and the licensed characters give shoppers something to click on.
- The festive ribbon and stripe styling fits the season and the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The hero says "Shop festive deals and exciting markdowns" with no number, so the strongest offers are pushed below it.
- The top orange banner promotes Advent Calendars, a third message above the hero that dilutes it.
- Four sets of fine print at the bottom, and the exclusions in them, undercut the "honest offer" feel.
- The 25% off giants offer is a step down from the 50% off giants offer sent earlier today. A subscriber who got that one will notice the drop.
- The product tiles show no prices, so the Black Friday deal isn't visible at product level. The red "Shop all Black Friday Deals" tile is also text-only.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the $10 offer in the hero with a number, for example "Furry Friends from $10, 25% off Giants today." Right now the hero is generic.
- 2. Consolidate today's sends so the same offer doesn't go out three times with different subject lines.
- 3. Add prices or "was/now" to the product tiles.
- 4. Move the fine print into a collapsed or linked section, or shorten it.
- 5. Drop the Advent banner, or give Advent its own send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: Black Friday Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Giants are 25% off in stores and online today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plush from $10, plus order by Dec. 14 for Christmas delivery.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clean, responsive XHTML/MSO structure with a hidden preheader. Its main confirmed issues are missing `alt` text on 4 product images and a test-segment label in the UTM parameters. The 4 "broken" 403 product links are probably bot-blocking of the QA probe, and the compliance and auth headers couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 product "SHOP NOW" links returned 403** (the QA output shows empty link text before the label):
  - `/sanrio-frosted-hello-kitty-plush/035433.html`
  - `/snowflake-kitty-stuffed-animal/035464.html`
  - `/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-my-sweet-piano-plush/033927.html`
  - `/nine-tailed-kitsune-fox-stuffed-animal/034916.html`
- A 403 (not 404/410) on a Demandware storefront usually means a WAF or bot challenge rejecting the probe. Treat this as **unconfirmed**, not as a dead link. Re-test with a browser-like User-Agent or a real-browser check before calling it broken.
- **25 tracking/redirect links were not probed** (skipped by domain rule). Their destinations and status are unverified.
- All 4 product links carry the identical `utm_content=Promo`, so clicks can't be attributed to a specific product or module.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing `alt` text** (the QA output lists these filenames):
  - `11260801135433~x20114021.jpg`
  - `45260801135464~x20135020.jpg`
  - `91240802233927~x20025114.jpg`
  - `01250700834916~x20047103.jpg`
- With images blocked, or for screen readers, the product tiles have no accessible name. The adjacent "SHOP NOW" text is the only context.
- `<title>` contains the internal template name: `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. This is cosmetic but leaks the template ID.
- The preheader wraps its text in an empty `<font class="">`, which is deprecated markup with no function. Minor.
- No other issues found in the visible source. It has the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, `x-apple`/`format-detection` metas, and breakpoints at ≤480px and 481–639px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible HTML or QA data. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and later modules are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw headers at the sender or in a direct-to-Gmail test.
- **Authentication-Results** was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- The visible HTML excerpt does not show the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Evidence URL: `?utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=EvergreenBlackFriday&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261127&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2808184`
- **`utm_term` contains `Testing`.** The segment/audience label looks like a test or seed value that reached a production send.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value, not a source identifier like the ESP or list name. `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized, and GA4 treats values case-sensitively, so it can split from the standard `email` channel.
- `utm_campaign=EvergreenBlackFriday` doesn't match the subject "Happening NOW: Black Friday Deals!". That's fine if it's intentional, but it means no campaign-level separation for the day.
- Landing pages are product-detail URLs (`/…/035433.html`). Whether they match the email's offer could not be verified because of the 403s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-verify the 4 product URLs with a real browser or allowlisted UA. If they load, add the probe to the WAF allowlist or mark them as false positives.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images (the product names, for example).
3. Replace `utm_term=…Testing…` with the real segment value, and use unique `utm_content` values per module or product.
4. Normalize UTM casing (`email`, lowercase campaign) and use a meaningful `utm_source`.
5. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay. Confirm the footer unsubscribe link and address in the full HTML.
6. Replace the `<title>` with a customer-facing value and remove the empty `<font>` wrapper.
