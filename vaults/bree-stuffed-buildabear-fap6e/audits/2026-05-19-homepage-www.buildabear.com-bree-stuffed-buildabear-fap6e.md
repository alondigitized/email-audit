# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened the site and immediately got wall'd by a full-screen Cookiebot consent modal — the actual homepage is basically invisible behind it. The only real content I can see is a sliver of a top-bar promo that starts with "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" and a sticky bottom strip that shows "Active Offers (4) View All." As a returning shopper who knows exactly what she wants, this is a rough first impression.

## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All" sticky strip** — even with the modal blocking everything, this bottom bar tells me there are live deals. I'd want to tap that the second I can.
- **Same-day delivery top banner** — partially visible, and as someone who sometimes needs a bear last-minute before a birthday party, that's a useful hook if I could read the whole thing.
- **Cookie modal is at least clean and on-brand** — it uses Build-A-Bear's blue and white palette, so it doesn't feel like a third-party intrusion visually.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal swallows 100% of the viewport** — I cannot see the hero, any product imagery, any navigation, or any seasonal content. For a returning shopper who knows the site, this is annoying. For a new visitor, it's a bounce risk.
- **Top banner is cut off** — "-Day Delivery Available in Select Locations!" is truncated and I can't read the full message. Unclear if it's same-day or next-day, what the threshold is, or what locations qualify.
- **Zero loyalty/rewards visibility** — I'm a rewards member and there's absolutely nothing reminding me of that on first load. No "Welcome back" signal, no points balance teaser, nothing.
- **No seasonal or Pokémon collab signal visible** — as someone who tracks the Pokémon collab calendar, I expect to see something that confirms the current drop or teases what's coming. Completely hidden.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom bar or slim top-of-screen strip** — the EU/GDPR-style full-screen modal is overkill for a US-primary toy retailer. A compact "We use cookies — Accept / Manage" bar at the bottom would let me see the actual homepage immediately.
- **Show the rewards balance or "X points to your next reward" in the top nav** before the modal fires — a logged-in user (me) should feel recognized on load.
- **Make the top delivery banner fully readable on mobile** — don't truncate the key value prop in a sticky strip. One line, no overflow.
- **Surface the "Active Offers (4)" count with a teaser of the top deal** — instead of just the count, show the headline offer inline ("Up to 40% off Pokémon bears") so it creates urgency even while the modal is up.
