# Introducing Mellow Moodies™
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean launch email with a clear hierarchy and no offer. The hero is a cozy flat-lay of two teens playing chess on a pink shag rug, with the Mellow Moodies plushes among them. It matches the "mindful" positioning. The copy is "Say Hello To Mellow," then a one-line pitch and a plum Shop Now button. The eye goes hero, headline, button, then down three colour-blocked character tiles for Willow, Huck and Mateo. The layout is simple and on-brand.
- It follows the same pattern as the Oct 4 Kitsune Fox send (5/10) and the Oct 6 Cherry and Berry send (6/10): a pretty product reveal with no price, no deadline and no reason to click today. This is the "10.08.26" drop teased in the Oct 2 email, so the payoff is earned. But this is the third launch-style send in five days, and nothing here sets it apart from the other two. It's a good catalogue page and a weak sales email.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme and shows the plushes in a lifestyle scene with a "Mellow Moodies" lockup.
- The three tiles are split by colour (plum, navy, forest green), and each has a named "SHOP [NAME]" label. That gives three specific click targets.
- The headline and body copy are short, on-voice and easy to scan on mobile.
- The two black or plum Shop Now buttons are high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The footer nav (Find a Workshop, Shop Best Sellers, Plan a Party) gives non-launch shoppers an exit.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price, deadline or loyalty perk. Compare the Sept 28 "FREE Shipping, No Minimum" send (7/10).
- There is no price or "from $" anywhere, so the click is a pure browse.
- Huck's shirt text is cropped to "OWKEY TIRED" by the tile edge. It reads as a minor cut-off.
- "Say Hello To Mellow" and "match your vibe" are soft. Nothing says what a Mellow Moodie does, such as whether it has a scent, weight or accessories.
- The footer is heavy for the amount of content above it. The Foundation, Entertainment and Bear Cave logos and social icons add noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete reason to act now. Examples are a price ("From $XX"), a launch-week free accessory, or a "first 48 hours" perk. That one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Make the two Shop Now buttons specific, for example "Meet the Mellow Moodies" for the top one. Keep the three character tiles as separate deep links.
- 3. Add a one-line product detail under each tile, such as price, outfit included, or sizing. This gives people something to click for.
- 4. Fix the Huck tile crop so the shirt text isn't cut off.
- 5. Space out launch sends. Combine the fox, Cherry and Berry, and Mellow Moodies stories into a weekly digest, or give each its own incentive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Plain-text weight:** the plain-text part is 3665 of 5209 characters URLs (about 70%). That is consistent with long wrapped tracking URLs. It's a minor spam-filter heuristic risk, not a broken link.
- No malformed `href` values are visible in the truncated portion.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` has no `lang` attribute. Screen readers can't pick the correct language, which fails WCAG 3.1.1.
- **Confirmed:** `<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. It exposes the internal template name and is read aloud by assistive tech and shown in "view in browser" tabs.
- **Dark mode:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared. The visible CSS has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block, and the body is hard-coded `bgcolor="#ffffff"`. This is only a possible inconsistency, because the truncated source may define dark styles later.
- **Positive:** the hidden preheader uses `display:none!important`, `mso-hide:all` and `max-height:0`. The `&zwnj;&nbsp;` and `&shy;` padding prevents body text from leaking into the inbox preview. The `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` block is present for Outlook DPI, and there are responsive breakpoints at 639, 480 and 350px.
- **Not verifiable:** image `alt` text, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and CTA tap-target sizes all fall in the truncated section.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear. Only the head and preheader were available, so the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a proven sender defect.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for high-volume senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `buildabear@ebuildabear.com` is unknown because the relay strips or doesn't expose it.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. All 21 links are click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, so there is no evidence of the final URL, UTM parameters or landing page. The preheader promises "in Stores or Online", and I couldn't check whether the CTAs reach a Mellow Moodies page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`) to the `<html>` tag.
2. Replace the `<title>` with the subject or a human-readable string. Strip the `BroadcastTemplate_1A` artifact.
3. Re-run QA against the raw MIME source, or a direct-from-ESP seed. This should surface `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat the compliance warnings as unresolved rather than failures.
4. Resolve the 21 tracking links with a redirect follower to confirm the final destinations, status codes and UTM parameters.
5. Run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to cover `alt` text, the unsubscribe link, the postal address and merge tokens.
6. Either add a `prefers-color-scheme: dark` block or drop the `color-scheme` meta tags if dark mode isn't intentionally supported.
