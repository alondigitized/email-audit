# Our NEW Shark Is a Fin-tastic Friend! 🦈
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a new original character launch — Chummy Shark — framed around a summer/ocean theme. The hero image is strong: the shark character in sunglasses against a tropical backdrop lands cleanly, and the pun in the subject carries through into the body copy with "A Fin-tastic Friend / making waves this summer." The visual identity is tight.
- The problem is structural. Build-A-Bear just sent a dedicated Toy Story email yesterday (June 4), and that same Toy Story module reappears here as the secondary block. For any subscriber who opened yesterday, this reads as recycled creative rather than a meaningful cross-sell. The third module — "A Splash of Fun / Cuddle and collect with your Furry Goods" — is genuinely vague; the plush animal shown isn't clearly identified, and "Furry Goods" as a category label adds no purchase motivation. Below that, a four-product grid further dilutes the shark focus.
- The net read: Chummy Shark is a likable character with a well-executed hero, but the email packs in too many secondary messages and offers zero price incentive to act now. It performs as a brand awareness send, not a conversion driver.

## What's working

- **Hero composition is the strongest in this week's run** — the shark character with sunglasses, tropical backdrop, and "A Fin-tastic Friend" headline is visually cohesive and seasonally sharp.
- **Subject pun lands** — "Fin-tastic" is playful without being cringe; consistent with Build-A-Bear's established brand voice.
- **Character name ("Chummy Shark") is surfaced in the body**, giving the launch a specific identity rather than just "new shark plush."
- **Navigation utility buttons at the bottom** (Build Your Own Bear, Find a Workshop) give subscribers a clear secondary action path.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean across all modules.

## What's weak

- **Toy Story module is recycled creative** — the same characters and framing appeared in yesterday's dedicated send; this cross-sell feels stale for openers.
- **"A Splash of Fun / Furry Goods" module is unexplained** — the plush shown isn't clearly labeled, and "Furry Goods" as category copy communicates nothing.
- **No price hook anywhere** — not a single discount, bundle deal, or loyalty offer in a 10-module email.
- **"Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid buries the lead** — four additional products (Toothless, Teddy Bears, Winnie the Pooh) fragment attention away from the shark launch.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTAs are identical across all five modules** — no differentiation makes every button feel interchangeable.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut or defer the Toy Story module.** Running a cross-promo 24 hours after the dedicated send signals poor campaign sequencing. Let Chummy Shark own this email; bring Toy Story back in a week.
- 2. **Give the secondary module a reason to exist.** "A Splash of Fun / Furry Goods" needs a product name, a price, and a specific CTA. Right now it's decorative filler.
- 3. **Add a launch-week incentive.** Even "Free personalization on Chummy Shark this week only" gives a reason to click today rather than bookmark-and-forget.
- 4. **Collapse the product grid or curate it thematically.** If you keep the "Most Loved" section, filter it to ocean/summer characters only — Bluey beach, Toothless stays if you frame it as adventure/explorer. Winnie the Pooh honey bundle is off-theme.
- 5. **Differentiate CTA copy per module** — "Meet Chummy," "Complete the Collection," "Shop the Bundle" each carry more purchase intent than five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New Drop: Build-A-Bear's Fin-tastic Shark Is Ready to Swim Home`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Say hello to Chummy Shark — our newest summer friend, ready to make waves.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New this summer: Chummy Shark just arrived. Build yours before the wave breaks.`

## Technical Audit — Build-A-Bear "Shark" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-layout email with VML Outlook conditionals and two responsive CSS breakpoints. The HTML source is truncated, limiting full link and compliance verification, but several issues are confirmed in the visible markup.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully audit** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. The following must be verified against the full source:
- Confirm all CTAs have UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`)
- Confirm click-tracking wrapper is applied consistently (not just on primary CTA)
- Sending domain `ebuildabear.com` differs from brand domain `buildabear.com` — verify click-tracking redirects resolve correctly and don't trigger browser security warnings

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`lang` attribute missing** — `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute. Screen readers cannot infer document language. Fix: `<html lang="en" xmlns=...>`.

**CSS property capitalization** — `p { Margin: 0px !important; Padding: 0px !important; }` uses capitalized property names. Per CSS spec these are case-insensitive, but some proprietary email renderers (notably older Samsung Mail) may fail to apply them. Normalize to lowercase.

**Viewport `content` trailing space** — `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 "` has a trailing space before the closing quote. Minor syntax error; most parsers tolerate it but it should be cleaned.

**`&shy;` in preview-padding block** — the filler div uses both `&nbsp;&zwnj;` and bare `&shy;` (soft hyphens). Some clients render soft hyphens as visible `-` characters when reflowing. Prefer `&zwnj;&nbsp;` chains exclusively.

**Alt text** — not verifiable from truncated source; must confirm all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` attributes, particularly product images and CTA buttons rendered as images.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens are visible in the header, preheader, or above-the-fold markup. Cannot audit body personalization from truncated source. Verify:
- No unresolved token literals (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) appear in the rendered output if first-name personalization was intended
- Fallback values are set for any conditional blocks

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)

**Sending domain mismatch** — `From: buildabear@ebuildabear.com`. The envelope domain `ebuildabear.com` must be covered by a DMARC-aligned SPF record and DKIM signature. If DKIM `d=` is `buildabear.com` but the `From:` domain is `ebuildabear.com`, DMARC will fail alignment unless `ebuildabear.com` has its own DMARC policy. This is the single highest-priority infrastructure item to verify.

**Unsubscribe link / physical address** — not verifiable from truncated source. Both are required by CAN-SPAM. The full source footer must be audited.

**List-Unsubscribe header** — not visible in the provided excerpt. Verify the sending platform is injecting `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) headers at the SMTP layer; Gmail and Apple Mail now require these for bulk senders.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Preheader/subject mismatch** — Subject promotes a specific new shark product; preheader reads "New Summer Collection in Stores & Online!" — a generic seasonal message. This is a content-level gap, but it also affects technical continuity: if the landing page targets the shark product and the preheader sets a broader expectation, A/B open-rate attribution will be noisy.

**Landing page alignment** — cannot verify from truncated source. Confirm primary CTA links to the shark product PDP, not the homepage or collection root.
