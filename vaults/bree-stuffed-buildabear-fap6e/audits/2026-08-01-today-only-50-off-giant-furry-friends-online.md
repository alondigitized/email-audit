# Today Only: 50% Off GIANT Furry Friends Online!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's cleanest, most single-minded send in the recent run — a giant percent-off number, one hero product, one CTA, no clutter. Compared to 2026-07-27's "FREE Shipping Today Only!" (8/10, praised for finally pairing urgency with a real discount), this one goes further: 50% off is a much bigger number than free shipping, and the giant longhorn plush held by a laughing model gives instant scale and emotional payoff in a way flat product shots can't.
- Where it loses points is specificity and honesty of the offer. "GIANTS" as a category name is vague — a first-time reader doesn't immediately know if that means the giant teddy bears the brand is known for, or this longhorn (a decidedly off-brand animal for a company called Build-A-Bear). And the fine print doing real work here — "Excludes Disney furry friends," "while supplies last," "online only" — undercuts the boldness of the hero claim, which the 07-27 review already flagged as a recurring brand habit worth fixing.
- Strong hero, single CTA, real urgency (today only, backed by an actual expiring timestamp in the legal copy) — this lands as one of the better sends in the run, just short of great because the product category is fuzzy and the secondary nav block eats a lot of scroll for zero new information.

## What's working

- Massive, unambiguous headline ("50% OFF GIANTS") is the first thing the eye hits — no competing text.
- Single clear CTA button ("SHOP NOW") in high-contrast navy on the light hero background.
- Hero image sells the product's actual selling point (giant scale) via a human model for size reference — genuinely differentiated from a flat product photo.
- Real urgency claim: "today only," and the footer confirms a hard cutoff (11:59 p.m. PT August 1) rather than vague "limited time" language.
- Consistent brand color and voice throughout, no render bugs, no broken images.

## What's weak

- "GIANTS" is a strange, undefined term — a longhorn cow plush isn't what most subscribers picture for a "bear" brand's giant lineup, and no product name or price anchor appears anywhere.
- The four-item nav block (Build Your Own Bear, Personalize Gifts, Giftshop, Find a Workshop) is generic evergreen navigation, not tied to the sale — it's dead weight below a strong hero and dilutes the single-CTA discipline the top half establishes.
- "Excludes Disney furry friends" and "other exclusions may apply" sit right under a headline banner promising 50% off broadly — that's the buried-fine-print pattern that erodes trust on a bold claim.
- No visible price anchor (original vs. sale price) — "50% off" of an unknown number is weaker than showing "$40 → $20."

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the product category in the hero — swap "GIANTS" for something like "50% OFF GIANT PLUSH" or name the actual line, so the offer isn't ambiguous about what qualifies.
- 2. Cut the four-button nav block entirely, or replace it with 2-3 actual giant-plush product thumbnails with prices — turn dead evergreen space into a second conversion opportunity that reinforces the sale.
- 3. Surface the Disney exclusion as a short visible line near the CTA (e.g., "Excludes Disney friends") rather than only in dense footer legal copy — it's honest either way, but hiding it reads worse than stating it plainly.
- 4. Add a price anchor on the hero or in the first nav row ("Now from $20") to make the 50% concrete instead of abstract.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our biggest furry friends, half price — online only through midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet the giant longhorn (and friends) at 50% off, today only.`
