# Sky Puppy’s BACK with a New Mini Bean!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is another straightforward new-character intro — Build-A-Bear's "Sky Puppy Moth" now comes in a plush "Duo" (regular size + a mini companion), staged with cheerful summer art (sun, dragonfly, ladybug, grass). Like the 2026-07-26 "Summer Vibes Activated!" send, there's no discount, no price anchor, no urgency — it's a "here's a cute new thing, buy it" email, the exact pattern that scored weakest in this brand's recent history versus the 2026-07-27 "FREE Shipping Today Only!" send that actually moved the needle.
- Execution-wise it's clean: no render bugs, a clear hero, and a well-organized module stack below (two secondary product callouts, a 4-item "Most Loved" grid, then four footer nav pills). But nothing distinguishes this from the last five "new plush character" emails in a row (Beardusa, Lightning Bug, Toy Story, Beary Goods) — the cadence is starting to blur together.
- Judgment: solid, on-brand, unremarkable. Worth sending, but it's leaving conversion on the table by skipping any incentive.

## What's working

- Hero art is charming and seasonally on-point — sun, dragonfly, ladybug, and trees sell "summer" without saying the word.
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop Now") directly under the hero headline, no scrolling needed on mobile.
- Clear visual hierarchy: hero → two secondary launches → "Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid → footer nav — logically ordered from newest to evergreen.
- Product detail is present in the hero copy ("TWO adorable sizes"), which answers a real buyer question (mini vs. regular).
- No layout bugs — text is legible, images load, buttons are properly sized and centered.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, free-shipping, or bundle offer anywhere in the email — same miss the 2026-07-26 "Summer Vibes Activated!" review flagged, and the opposite of what worked in the 2026-07-27 send.
- Subject promises "a New Mini Bean" but the hero copy never says "Mini Bean" — it just says "TWO adorable sizes," so the actual news hook from the subject line isn't reinforced in the body.
- Every CTA button says the same generic "Shop Now" (six times total) — no product-specific CTA copy anywhere.
- This is at least the fifth new-plush-character launch email in under two weeks per brand history (Toy Story 7/24, Beardusa 7/23, Lightning Bug 7/22, Beary Goods 7/25) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone opening these back-to-back.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive tied to the launch — e.g., "Free mini accessory with Sky Puppy Duo" or a bundle discount for buying both sizes together. This is the single biggest lever; it's the exact gap between this send and the top-performing 2026-07-27 email.
- 2. Make the hero copy match the subject's actual hook — explicitly name "Mini Bean" so the email delivers on what the subject promised.
- 3. Differentiate CTA copy per module (e.g., "Shop Sky Puppy Duo," "Shop Sunshine Bear," "Shop Mashimals") instead of six identical "Shop Now" buttons.
- 4. Space out new-character launches or bundle smaller ones into a single roundup email to reduce lookalike-send fatigue.
