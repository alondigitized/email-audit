# Today Only: $15 Furry Friends Online!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the best hero this brand has produced in the recent run — a real $15 price point, a real code (FALL), a legible "TODAY ONLY" badge, and actual autumn styling that makes it feel seasonal rather than slapped-on. Unlike the last several sends, the offer isn't buried under four modules before you get to it; it's the first thing you see, big, bold, and unambiguous.
- Where it falls apart is the same structural tic that's dogged Build-A-Bear all month: after the strong hero, the email sprawls into "Most Loved Stuff This Week" and "More to Explore" — none of which are the $15 furry friends the subject promised. And there's a hard factual problem: the fine print dates the offer to "September 23, 2025," a year and three days out of step with itself (this send is dated 2026-09-23 based on the campaign), which reads as a copy-paste error a subscriber could actually catch.
- Net: strong top, weak middle, sloppy legal line. Call it a 6 — same ceiling as "Today Only: 20% OFF Furry Friends!" (2026-09-19), for the same reason: good hook, unfocused body.

## What's working

- The hero leads with the offer itself — "$15 SELECT FURRY FRIENDS" in huge type — not a product tease.
- A visible promo code (FALL) and unmistakable "TODAY ONLY" badge give real urgency and a real action.
- Fall leaf/pumpkin illustration ties the offer to the season without feeling generic.
- The two hero product tiles (koala, "Rock and Roll" bear) sit right under the offer and reinforce it with a single "SHOP SALE" CTA.

## What's weak

- The fine print reads "$15 select furry friends valid online only at buildabear.com through 11:59 p.m. CST on September 23, 2025" — the year doesn't match the send date, a visible date error.
- After the hero, the email pivots to unrelated modules ("Most Loved Stuff This Week," "New Arrivals," "Best Sellers") that dilute the one-day offer instead of reinforcing it.
- None of the four "Most Loved" plush (Silly Goose, Sanrio Berry, Fresno Nightcrawler, Sanrio Cherry) are shown at the $15 price, so it's unclear if they even qualify — the offer's scope is muddled by omission.
- Two different CTA button colors (red "SHOP SALE"/"SHOP NOW" vs. navy "SHOP NOW") with no clear hierarchy dilutes which action matters most.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the fine-print date to match the actual send date — a wrong year on the expiration undercuts the "today only" urgency the whole email is built on.
- 2. Cut or shrink "Most Loved Stuff This Week" and "More to Explore" — keep the email to the hero + the two sale tiles, or explicitly relabel the extra plush as "also included in the $15 sale" if they qualify.
- 3. Standardize CTA button color/copy so "SHOP SALE" is visually distinct as the primary action and the catalog CTAs read as secondary.
- 4. Add a countdown timer or explicit end time (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm CT") near the hero itself, not just buried in the footer legal text.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Call: $15 Select Plush, Ends Tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One day only — grab a $15 plush before midnight CST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall savings on select furry friends, online only today.`
