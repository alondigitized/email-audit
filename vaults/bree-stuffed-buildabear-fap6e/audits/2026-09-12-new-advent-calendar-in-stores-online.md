# NEW Advent Calendar in Stores & Online!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a Christmas-in-September "New Arrivals" send anchored on the Advent Calendar, but it's structurally the same problem as the 2026-09-05 "New Season" email that scored 4.0: a hero, then a Harry Potter module, then a 4-item "Shop By Product" grid, then a Best Sellers/New Arrivals split, then a footer nav — five distinct asks stacked with no discount anywhere. There's no offer, no urgency, no price. It's a lookbook, not a marketing email.
- The hero image itself is genuinely good — a warm, authentic-feeling lifestyle shot of two kids opening the calendar by a tree, which is the strongest asset Build-A-Bear has used in this recent run. But the email buries that asset under four more scroll-lengths of unrelated product (Halloween-adjacent Batty Kitty/Werewolf, a Longhorn Cow, a Silly Goose) that has nothing to do with the advent calendar subject line, which is exactly the "category dump" pattern flagged in the 2026-09-05 and 2026-09-06 audits.
- Verdict: on-brand, well-photographed, but no reason to act now. This is a browse email wearing a promotional subject line — score it accordingly, in line with the brand's other no-discount pushes (4-5/10 range).

## What's working

- Hero photography is warm and specific — real kids, real tree, real calendar, not a studio product shot.
- Subject/hero copy alignment is clean: "NEW Advent Calendar in Stores & Online" matches "OUR NEW ADVENT CALENDAR IS HERE!" exactly.
- Clear single CTA directly under the hero ("SHOP NOW" in a well-contrasted dark green button).
- Harry Potter module is visually distinct (navy block, gold-trim branding) and reads as a legitimate secondary promotion, not clutter.
- No render bugs — images load, buttons are legible, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or urgency anywhere in the email — pure product push, same gap flagged in the 2026-09-05 and 2026-09-06 sends.
- The second module (Merry Chip-Mas monkey + Sweet frog) has no headline or context — it's two random plush images with a "SHOP NOW" button and no explanation of what they are.
- Harry Potter and the "Shop By Product" grid (kitty, cow, goose, werewolf) are thematically disconnected from the advent-calendar subject — reads like unrelated modules bolted together, not one cohesive send.
- By the time a reader hits "Shop By Product," they're five scrolls past the subject-line promise with zero new information about the calendar itself (price, days, what's inside).
- "More to Explore" (Best Sellers / New Arrivals) at the bottom adds a fourth distinct CTA path with no urgency to justify inclusion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two modules: hero (Advent Calendar) + one complementary module. Everything after "It's never too early..." should go — the Harry Potter block and product grid dilute a genuinely strong hero.
- 2. Add the price and what's-inside detail for the Advent Calendar directly under the hero — "24 days, $54.50, one surprise per door" beats a vague CTA with no product info.
- 3. Give the second plush module (monkey/frog) a headline — right now it's two unlabeled images with a button.
- 4. If Harry Potter deserves a spot, make it its own follow-up send rather than a mid-scroll module competing with the subject line's promise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Advent Calendar Is Back — 24 Days of Surprises`
- **Subject Alt B:** `NEW: Advent Calendar + Holiday Favorites Just Dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One furry surprise a day, all the way to Christmas Eve.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `In stores and online now — shop before it sells out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard responsive table-based template, but 4 of 4 probed product links return HTTP 403 and both unsubscribe-related headers are absent — the two most significant issues found.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 4 "SHOP NOW" product links fail with **403 Forbidden**:
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  - `/longhorn-cow-stuffed-animal/024867.html`
  - `/silly-goose-stuffed-animal/035140.html`
  - `/mystical-werewolf-stuffed-animal/035021.html`
  All carry consistent UTM params (`utm_campaign=HolidayAdventLaunch`, `utm_id=2793454`), so this isn't a malformed-URL issue — likely bot/WAF blocking on the destination (Akamai/Demandware bot protection commonly returns 403 to non-browser clients) or the products are genuinely gated/unavailable. Recommend verifying manually in a browser before treating as confirmed broken.
- 23 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect domains — not a defect).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 product images missing `alt` text (fails WCAG 1.1.1 / hurts rendering-blocked-image fallback):
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - `61231221224867x20152012.jpg`
  - `04250801635140~x20117113.jpg`
  - `84250801435021~x20058110.jpg`
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `.ExternalClass` and Gmail fixes, and responsive breakpoints at 480px/639px — no structural markup issues found.
- Preheader text implemented correctly with hidden `<span>` + zero-width-joiner padding hack.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization fields present in the visible source (truncated). No issues found in the reviewed portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found — could be a true absence or an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing it; needs source-header verification before treating as a CAN-SPAM gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe unsupported/unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found via relay — authentication status unknown, cannot confirm pass/fail.
- Visible unsubscribe link/footer not present in truncated HTML sample — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe mechanism) from available source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters are consistent and well-formed across all 4 product links: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=HolidayAdventLaunch&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20260912&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2793454`.
- Landing page alignment cannot be confirmed since all 4 destination URLs return 403 rather than a live product page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually verify the 4 product URLs in a real browser/session (not the automated probe) to rule out bot-detection false positives vs. genuinely broken/removed products before escalating to the sender's dev team.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 4 product images for accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
3. Confirm with the ESP whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — re-check via raw headers at the source (not through the AgentMail relay) since relay truncation is a known confound.
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status directly against the sending domain (`ebuildabear.com`) rather than relying on relay-captured `Authentication-Results`.
