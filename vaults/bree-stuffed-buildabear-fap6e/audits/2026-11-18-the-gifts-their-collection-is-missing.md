# The Gifts Their Collection Is Missing
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a licensed-franchise gift guide with no offer, and it's well organized. The red hero, "THE ULTIMATE GIFTS FOR THE ULTIMATE FANS," sets the idea. Four stacked franchise tiles (Hello Kitty and Friends, Pokémon, Harry Potter, Disney) then let a shopper pick their fan's world quickly. The plush photography is strong and recognizable, and the tiles are easy to scan.
- The weakness is that nothing gives a reason to act today. There is no percentage, no bundle and no free shipping. The only urgency is the delivery-deadline banner (Hanukkah Nov. 21, Christmas Dec. 14), and it sits below the fold, after the tiles. The subject promises "the gifts their collection is missing," which implies a collector angle. The body is a generic franchise menu and doesn't speak to completing a collection.
- It's a competent catalog send that will get browsing clicks and little urgency. It repeats the category-navigation format of the Nov 14 animal-lover email, and it has no hook as strong as the Nov 8 "30% OFF" reminder.

## What's working

- The four franchise tiles are clear and recognizable, and each has a distinct shop link, so a fan-gift buyer can jump straight to their category.
- The plush photography is crisp and has character. Pikachu in a Poké Ball hoodie and the Gryffindor and Slytherin bears make the tiles easy to read.
- The hero headline is clear on a red background, with a white SHOP NOW button in a visible position.
- The delivery-deadline banner gives specific dates (Nov. 21 for Hanukkah, Dec. 14 for Christmas), which is credible, useful urgency.
- The Hanukkah bunny tile adds inclusive seasonal coverage.

## What's weak

- No offer is visible anywhere. There is no price, discount, bundle or free-shipping message, so the offer has no hook.
- The deadline banner is buried below the tiles, so the strongest urgency signal appears after the first screen.
- There are too many CTAs. I count SHOP NOW or SHOP buttons on at least nine elements, plus SHOP ALL GIFTS, and none is the single obvious next step.
- The "collection" angle in the subject isn't delivered. Nothing shows accessories or outfits that complete a set.
- The footer area, with the Grinch and the ornament house under "More to Explore," is generic and off-theme from the franchise story. The ornament tile has no label, so it's unclear what the product is.
- The "Start Earning Points!" badge at the top is small and has no benefit attached.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the delivery-deadline banner up beneath the hero, and add one concrete offer. Examples are a gift-with-purchase or a bundle price on outfits and accessories.
- 2. Rewrite the hero around the subject's promise: "Complete their collection" with outfits, accessories and sound add-ons shown beside the plush.
- 3. Tie the loyalty badge to a benefit, for example "Earn 2x points on licensed plush."
- 4. Cut the tile CTAs to one clear label each, and trim the "More to Explore" block or label the products.
- 5. Add price points or "from $XX" to each franchise tile.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Nov. 21 for Hanukkah gifts they'll love`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Outfits, accessories and plush for every fan. Order by Dec. 14 for Christmas.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hello Kitty, Pokémon, Harry Potter and Disney, from $XX.`

- **Link markup can't be checked.** `href` values, `target` attributes and `alt` text fall in the truncated region.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>`, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Template name in the title.** `<title>Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A</title>` exposes an internal template identifier. It is read by assistive tech and shown in browser-view tabs.
- **Deprecated tag in the preheader.** The preheader uses `<font class="">` inside the hidden span, which is obsolete markup. The hiding itself (`display:none!important; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`) is correct.
- **Viewport meta has a trailing space.** The content is `"width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 "`. Parsers should tolerate it, but it's sloppy.
- **Responsive gaps in the media queries.**
  - `.em_h10` is defined only in the ≤480px block, while `.em_h20` exists only in the 481–639px block. Check that the right spacer class is used at each width.
  - The 481–639px block has no rules for the font classes (`.em_font_h1`, `.em_font_p`, `.em_cta`), so type doesn't scale in that range.
- **Checks that couldn't run.** The rest of the markup was not available, so I can't confirm image `alt` text, table `role="presentation"` or the VML button fallbacks. The Outlook conditional block and the `mso-*` rules are present and valid.

## 5. Compliance

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (WARN). Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unsupported. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, subject to the same relay caveat.
