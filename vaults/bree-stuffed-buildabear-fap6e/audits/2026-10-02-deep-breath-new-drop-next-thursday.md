# Deep Breath…New Drop Next Thursday!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a teaser, and a clean one. The hero is a flat-lay of cozy objects: a pink shag rug, coffee, popcorn, a vinyl record, headphones and a planner. The date "10.08.26" sits on top in big plum type. It looks nice and the date is the first thing you see. Still, the email never says what is dropping, and it gives no price and no product.
- The subject, "Deep Breath…New Drop Next Thursday!", promises a calm, mysterious drop, and the hero delivers that mood. The ask is "SIGN UP NOW" for something I can't name, with no incentive and no sign-up context. Compared with this brand's recent sends, the 9/28 "FREE Shipping, No Minimum" email and the 9/23 "$15 Furry Friends" email, this one gives me almost nothing to act on. - My judgement is that it works as a calendar reminder and little else. It would be fine as a short companion to a stronger send, but on its own it spends a send on mood. There is also a real chance the "Sign Up" button leads to a waitlist or SMS capture the email never explains.

## What's working

- The "10.08.26" date is huge and high-contrast. It's the clear focal point.
- The flat-lay photo has a consistent warm palette and fits the "mellow" tone.
- The layout is simple: one hero, one headline, one button. There are no broken images or overlapping text.
- "Thursday, October 8th" backs up the subject's "Next Thursday" with a real date.
- The plum button stands out from the pale background and is easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The email never says what is dropping. "Something Mellow" could be a plush, a collab or a sound-themed line.
- "SIGN UP NOW" has no stated reward. Sign up for what: an alert, early access, a waitlist?
- The hero has no bear or plush in it. For Build-A-Bear, that's a missed brand cue and it reads like a lifestyle brand.
- The three nav links (Find a Workshop, Shop Best Sellers, Plan a Party) are generic and pull attention from the one action. The footer is heavy with logos and fine print for such a short email.
- This is yet another near-daily send. The recent run of 9/23, 9/24, 9/26, 9/28, 9/29 and 10/01 makes a no-offer teaser easier to ignore.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a silhouette or a partial reveal of the product in the hero, such as a plush ear or paw peeking out from the rug. That gives curiosity something to work with, which the current image doesn't.
- 2. Say what the sign-up gets me, for example "Get early access" or "Be first to know", and make the button copy match the benefit.
- 3. Add one concrete hook, such as a quantity limit, a member early-access window, or a countdown to 10.08.
- 4. Drop the generic nav links. Keep one secondary link at most.
- 5. Make the subject say more. The ellipsis and "Deep Breath" are cute but vague.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** the root element is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` with no `lang`. Screen readers can't select the correct pronunciation profile.
- **Dark mode declared but not implemented:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light dark">` are present, but the `<style>` block has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules. Clients that honor the declaration may apply their own inversion, so contrast and image handling are uncontrolled. Either add dark rules or declare `light` only.
- **Template name leaked in `<title>`:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. This is an internal ESP template ID. Clients that show the title, such as web-view and some accessibility tools, will expose it.
- **Minor:** the preheader uses the deprecated `<font class="">` element with an empty class. `Margin`/`Padding` are capitalized in `p {}`, which is harmless because the capitalized `Margin` is an Outlook idiom.
- **Not assessable:** image `alt` text, image-to-text ratio, tap-target sizing and table `role="presentation"` usage.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit against the full HTML (untruncated) and capture raw headers before the relay, so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified directly.
