# Lights, Camera, GIFTS! Our Holiday Gift Guide Is Here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Build-A-Bear send in the recent run with a real offer: "Limited Time Only: 30% OFF Online Exclusives!" in an orange banner. That is a step up from the Nov 6 Cat in the Hat and Nov 5 Hello Kitty emails, which had no price hook. But the offer is not the hero. The red "MUST-SEE GIFTS" block with the movie-reel illustration dominates, and its copy ("Roll credits on your holiday shopping") is a pun with no concrete value. The eye lands on a theme, not the discount.
- The licensed-franchise stack works as a gift guide. Harry Potter, Toy Story, Minions, Star Wars and Dragons are each a clear, tappable category with strong product photography. The shipping-deadline band (Hanukkah by Nov. 21, Christmas by Dec. 14) is useful and credible. The weak spot is the fine print, which says the 30% off ends tonight, Nov 8 at 11:59 p.m. CST, but the body never says so. The deadline is buried at the bottom while the banner just says "Limited Time Only."
- Judgement: a solid, scannable gift guide with the best offer of the week, undercut by a vague headline, a hidden deadline, and six near-identical "SHOP" CTAs. It is a 6 again, in line with the recent run, but with more upside than most.

## What's working

- The orange 30% off banner puts a specific discount at the top, which none of the Nov 1–6 emails did.
- The franchise tiles (Harry Potter, Toy Story, Minions, Star Wars, Dragons) are clean and recognizable, with large product shots and a clear label on each.
- The shipping-deadline band answers a real gift-buyer question with specific dates.
- The layout is a simple single column with consistent tile treatment, so it is easy to scan on mobile.
- The footer gives the full offer terms and the store-versus-online distinction.

## What's weak

- The hero says "MUST-SEE GIFTS" and never mentions 30% off, so the strongest hook is outside the main visual.
- The deadline (ends Nov 8, 11:59 p.m. CST) is only in the footer fine print, and the banner's "Limited Time Only" gives no urgency a subscriber can act on.
- The CTA copy is generic. "SHOP NOW" appears four times plus "SHOP ALL MOVIES & TV" and the tile labels, so no button says what you get.
- The "More to Explore" Grinch and holiday-bears tiles are unlabeled, and both buttons say only "SHOP NOW." The Grinch is a repeat of the Nov 3 creative.
- The 30% off applies only to "Online Exclusives," which is not explained in the body. The fine print is also very small and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the deadline and the discount into the hero: "30% OFF Online Exclusives — Ends Tonight" over the reel graphic. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Replace generic buttons with specific ones: "Shop Harry Potter Bears," "Shop 30% Off Exclusives."
- 3. Label the two bottom tiles ("Grinch Plush," "Holiday Bears") and tie them to the offer.
- 4. Add one line on what counts as an Online Exclusive, plus a price or "from $" anchor on at least one tile.
- 5. Make the shipping-deadline band a sub-hero and keep the dates, but add "Order by Nov 21 for Hanukkah" in plainer language.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% Off Online Exclusives Ends Tonight 🎬`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Harry Potter, Star Wars & more: 30% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online exclusives are 30% off through 11:59 p.m. CST. Gift guide inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Nov 21 for Hanukkah, Dec 14 for Christmas — and save 30% today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible head and preheader are structurally sound for a table-based ESP template. However, the HTML is truncated, so body links, images, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected. The automated QA run reports one FAIL (no plain-text part) and three WARNs, and the header-related ones may be relay artifacts.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 28 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and link health are **unverified**.
- The truncated source shows no anchors, so I can't confirm alt/href pairing, broken links or empty hrefs.
- No other issues are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Stray closing tag:** `</span>` appears immediately after the preheader `<div class="preheader">` with no matching opener. This is invalid markup, and some parsers may mis-nest what follows.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`, so screen readers can't select a language profile.
- **Internal template name in `<title>`:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. This shows in some clients' tab/preview contexts and in assistive-tech announcements.
- **`<style>` inside `<body>`:** `<style type="text/css"> div.preheader { display: none !important; } </style>` sits in the body. Gmail and some webmail clients strip or ignore body-level style blocks. The preheader is also hidden inline (`display: none !important`), so the impact is low.
- **Dark mode declared without visible support:** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `supported-color-schemes` are set, but the visible CSS has no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules. Clients may auto-invert colors unpredictably. This is only a partial finding, since the dark-mode CSS may be further down in the truncated source.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="color-scheme" ...>` isn't self-closed under an XHTML doctype, and the viewport content has a trailing space (`initial-scale=1.0 `). Both are harmless in practice.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports `[FAIL] Plain-text fallback missing or too short: Text version is 0 chars`. A multipart/alternative with an empty text part hurts deliverability and accessibility. This could be a capture artifact, but treat it as a real defect until the raw MIME is checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The body is not visible, so this is unconfirmed beyond the head.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header not found`
- `[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)`
- `[WARN] Authentication-Results header not found`

QA attributes all three to the AgentMail relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are **unknown, not confirmed failures**. The visible/truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, physical address or unsubscribe link, so those can't be assessed. The From domain `ebuildabear.com` is a sending subdomain that should be verified for alignment against the brand domain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. Tracking links were not resolved, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. Nothing confirms a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw MIME and headers directly from the source mailbox, bypassing the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the text/plain part.
2. If the empty text part is real, generate a proper plain-text alternative.
3. Remove the orphaned `</span>` and move the preheader `<style>` rule into `<head>`.
4. Add `lang="en"` (and `xml:lang`) to `<html>`.
5. Replace the `<title>` with a human-readable value, such as the subject line.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, with tracking-redirect resolution enabled, to verify the 28 links, UTM parameters, alt text, footer compliance and any dark-mode CSS.
