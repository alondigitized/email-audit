# NEW Holiday Collection in Stores & Online!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, on-brand holiday catalog email with no real offer. The subject promises a "NEW Holiday Collection," and the hero delivers a cozy plush-by-the-tree image with "the holiday stuff you love." The first thing the eye hits is an orange Advent Calendar banner, though. That banner is a different message from the subject, and it's the same split the 11/01 Snowflake Kitty send had.
- The body is a long scroll of color-blocked "SHOP ___" bands (Happy Hugs Teddy, Pawlette, Longhorn, Timeless Teddy), then Beary Goods, Mini Beans, a four-product grid and a Grinch tile. It's pleasant, but it has no price, no discount and no loyalty hook. The only urgency is the shipping-deadline band (Hanukkah by Nov. 21, Christmas by Dec. 14), and that is soft. Compared with the 10/24 "Up to 50% OFF" send, this is a catalog with no reason to click today.
- Judgement: a solid brand-awareness send, middling for conversion. It has about ten CTAs and no clear primary one.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and seasonal: plush characters under a decorated tree, with a clear "SHOP NOW" button below.
- The color-blocked bands with large "SHOP [character]" copy are scannable and easy to tap.
- The shipping-deadline band gives credible, date-specific gift timing.
- The Mini Beans "Perfect Stocking Stuffers" block is a useful gift-angle segment.
- The footer is clean, with a navigation list and the brand tagline.

## What's weak

- The orange Advent Calendar banner sits above the hero and competes with the subject's "NEW Holiday Collection." The email has two jobs.
- No price, discount or loyalty benefit appears anywhere, so nothing reduces friction to click.
- The email has about ten "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP" CTAs, and the generic "SHOP NOW" buttons on the product grid and the "More to Explore" tiles don't say what they lead to.
- A product name is garbled: "Pawlette„¢ Bunny Plush Party Gift Set" shows a broken trademark glyph.
- The "More to Explore" tiles (an ornament and the Grinch) have no captions. The Grinch tile is a different campaign and feels disconnected.
- The Advent Calendar disclaimer is fine print in the footer, even though the top banner says "ON SALE NOW."

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Either lead with the Advent Calendar sale (state the discount in the banner) or make the new collection the hero and drop the banner.
- 2. Put a concrete hook in the hero, such as a price point or a "gift under $X" line, so the email gives a reason to click now.
- 3. Cut the grid and explore tiles to three or four items, and label the CTAs ("Shop Pawlette," "Shop the Grinch").
- 4. Fix the Pawlette trademark glyph.
- 5. Add a member or Bonus Club line to reward loyalty.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Dec. 14 for Christmas delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gingerbread, Pawlette and more. See what's new for the season.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Advent Calendars are on sale and gifts ship in time for Hanukkah and Christmas.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup and UTM tagging look sound, but six product and category links returned HTTP 403 to the QA probe, eight images have no alt text, and the plain-text part is 0 characters. The compliance and authentication headers could not be inspected, so their status is unknown.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Six links returned 403** on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - 4 × "SHOP NOW" product links:
    - `/gingerbread-axolotl-stuffed-animal/034239.html`
    - `/pawlette-bunny-plush-party-gift-set/33639_32815_20389.html`
    - `/pink-axolotl-stuffed-animal-with-peppermint-dress/29486_34069.html`
    - `/axolotl-stuffed-animal-pj-sleeper-gift-set/29486_31016.html`
  - 2 × image links:
    - `/holiday/advent-calendars-and-decor`
    - `/stuffed-animals/shop-by-character/grinch`
- **These 403s probably come from bot protection, not dead pages.** A 403 (rather than 404 or 410) on every URL, including category pages, points to a WAF or CDN blocking the probe. Re-test with a browser user-agent or a real browser session before calling them broken.
- **The list is incomplete.** 26 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped, so their destinations are unverified.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on every flagged link.** Clicks on the four product links, the advent-calendar link and the Grinch link can't be told apart in analytics. Only the destination URL separates them.
- **Image-link text is long alt text.** Two of the 403 entries show truncated alt strings as their link text ("Decorative ornament hanging on a Christmas tree…", "The Grinch™ graphic featuring…"). This is a side effect of the alt text on those images, not a link defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Eight images have no alt text:**
  - 4 hero images on `cdn.bfldr.com`: `Primary_ClassicChristmas26_CoreLongScroll_US_02.png` through `_05.png`.
  - 4 product images on `www.buildabear.com/dw/image/...`: `96250802134239~x20328030.jpg`, `94231331222601_32815_20389~x20010012.jpg`, `34241421229486_34069~x20546040.jpg` and `16231321229486_31016x20958016.jpg`.
  - With images blocked, these slots render empty and screen readers announce nothing or a filename. The four product images sit next to "SHOP NOW" links, so the product is unnamed.
- **The plain-text part is 0 characters** (QA FAIL). Text-only clients and some spam filters get no content.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - The viewport meta has a trailing space in `content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 "`.
  - A `<style>` block sits inside `<body>` before the preheader. Some clients drop it, though the preheader is also hidden inline.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared as `light dark`. The visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules, but the source is truncated, so I can't confirm any are missing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The source was truncated, so the footer and later modules were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this isn't a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `buildabear@ebuildabear.com`. Verify alignment from the raw headers.
- **The footer was not reviewed.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM tagging is consistent** on every flagged link:
  - `utm_source=Campaign`
  - `utm_medium=Email`
  - `utm_campaign=HolidayLongScroll`
  - `utm_id=2803096`
  - `utm_term=US-110226_US_HolidayLongScroll__Email_Broadcast_Sustain_BAB_Omni_Tier1_Q4_Holiday__Segment-20261102`
- **`utm_term` carries a long internal campaign descriptor** with double underscores. It is not a search term, and it is likely to be truncated or mis-parsed in some reporting tools.
- **Landing pages could not be checked.** The 403s blocked any check that the products match the email, such as in-stock status or the "NEW Holiday Collection" framing.
- **Domain split:** the sender domain is `ebuildabear.com` and the destination is `buildabear.com`. This is normal for ESP sending and needs no change.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the six 403 URLs with a browser-like user-agent or Playwright. Treat them as bot-blocked unless they still fail.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the eight images listed in section 3. Use empty `alt=""` only for purely decorative ones.
3. Add a plain-text MIME part with the main links and the unsubscribe URL.
4. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Don't rely on the AgentMail relay's view.
5. Give each module its own `utm_content` value, such as `hero`, `product_gingerbread_axolotl` or `grinch`.
6. Adjust the QA tool, since both header warnings and the 403s are likely tooling artifacts:
   - Capture raw headers.
   - Mark 403 on known WAF-fronted domains as "unverified" rather than FAIL.
   - Probe skipped tracking links via redirect-follow.
7. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to cover the footer, address and dark-mode CSS.
