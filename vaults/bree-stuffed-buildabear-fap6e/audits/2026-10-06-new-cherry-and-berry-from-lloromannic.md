## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound, with a table layout, MSO conditionals, and a preheader. The automated QA run scored a 27% pass rate. The main findings are 4 product CTAs that returned HTTP 403 to the probe, a 0-character plain-text part, 4 images without alt text, and a template-name `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 "SHOP NOW" links returned 403 to the probe.** All are on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/fresno-nightcrawler-posable-plush/034979.html`
  - `/silly-goose-stuffed-animal/035140.html`
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
- **The 403s are probably bot or WAF blocking, not dead pages.** The URLs are well-formed Demandware product paths, and the QA check can't tell the two apart. A browser-UA or real-client check is needed to confirm. Treat them as unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **The QA output shows an empty anchor-text field for these links.** The visible label is "SHOP NOW" on all four, so the same generic text points to four different products. See §3.
- **23 tracking/redirect links were skipped**, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **No tracking-link issues were visible in the unskipped URLs beyond the 403s.**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on 4 product images**, all served from `.../Sites-buildabear-master/default/...`:
  - `08250701634979~x20230105.jpg`
  - `04250801635140~x20117113.jpg`
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`

  Images-off clients will show blank tiles, and screen readers will read the filenames.
- **The four "SHOP NOW" links share identical text.** A screen-reader links list can't tell them apart, so each needs an `aria-label` or product-specific text.
- **The `<title>` is the template name:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. It's visible in web-view and some clients and should be the subject or campaign name.
- **There's a stray, unmatched `</span>`** right after the preheader `<div>` and before the `<!-- AMP -->` comment. It's invalid markup and may trigger quirks in some parsers.
- **The plain-text part is 0 characters** (see §5).
- **Dark mode is only partly confirmed.** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared as `light dark`. The visible (truncated) CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules, so I can't confirm dark-mode handling beyond that.
- **No other rendering issues are confirmed from the truncated source.**

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`[FAIL]` Plain-text fallback is missing (0 chars).** This is a deliverability and accessibility risk. It could be a capture artifact, but it's flagged as a failure.
- **`[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify it against the raw message at the source.
- **`[WARN]` `Authentication-Results` is not found**, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay. The From domain `ebuildabear.com` is a sending subdomain, and alignment can't be verified here.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address aren't visible in the truncated source.** I can't confirm or deny CAN-SPAM body compliance from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are present and consistent** across the 4 product links:
  - `utm_source=Campaign`
  - `utm_medium=Email`
  - `utm_campaign=SanrioLloromanic`
  - `utm_content=Promo`
  - `utm_id=2798324`
- **`utm_source=Campaign` is a non-descriptive value**, since it names a channel type rather than a source. Capitalization is also non-standard, and analytics tools are case-sensitive, so `Email` and `email` would split into separate rows.
- **`utm_term` is overloaded with an internal campaign string** (`US-UNION_1790888506587_100626_US_SanrioLloromanic__Email_Broadcast_Sustain_Sanrio_...-20261006`). It's about 150 characters and isn't a search term, so it bloats the URLs and misuses the parameter.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on all 4 links.** It can't distinguish placement or product in reporting.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be verified.** The 403 responses blocked the probe, so the destinations weren't checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-test the 4 product URLs with a browser-like client.** If they return 200, whitelist the QA probe. If they still fail, fix the destinations.
2. **Add a plain-text part** (`multipart/alternative`) with the product links and unsubscribe URL.
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the original send, not the relay copy.** Also check SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
4. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the 4 product images** (for example, "Sanrio Cherry plush"). Make the CTA text or `aria-label` product-specific.
5. **Replace the `<title>` with the actual campaign name** and remove the stray `</span>`.
6. **Clean up the UTM scheme.** Use lowercase values and a meaningful `utm_source`. Move the internal ID out of `utm_term` into `utm_id`, and give each link a unique `utm_content`, such as `cherry_shop_now`.
7. **Probe the 23 skipped tracking links manually** for redirect chain length and final-destination status.
