# Online Only for 24 Hours: 2 for $25 Extended!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a direct rerun of the 2026-09-09 "Today Only: 2 for $25 Lil' Cubs" send — same blue circle hero, same confetti backdrop, same offer — just recut as "Online Only for 24 Hours" with an "Extended Today Only!" banner slapped on top. The mechanic is honest and the hero is genuinely legible without scrolling, so on execution alone this holds up. But "extended" from a "today only" offer immediately undercuts the urgency that made yesterday's version work — a deadline that stretches when you don't click on it teaches subscribers to ignore the next one.
- The email also stacks a second unrelated CTA (Give a Dollar, Give a Bear donation module) right under the main offer, which dilutes an otherwise clean single-offer structure. It's the same "one strong module, then noise" pattern the brand keeps falling into (see 2026-09-05, 2026-08-24).
- Solid but repetitive send — it borrows credibility from yesterday's better-structured email while quietly weakening the urgency claim that made it work.

## What's working

- Hero offer (2 for $25) is immediately visible, high-contrast, and readable without scrolling.
- Single clear primary CTA ("Shop Now") sits directly under the hero in a distinct red button.
- Free shipping banner at top adds a second concrete, legible offer for the $100+ shopper.
- No render bugs — clean image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Secondary modules (Mini Beans, Back in Stock) are visually distinct and don't compete with the hero.

## What's weak

- "Extended Today Only" is a contradiction in terms — if it's extended, it wasn't "today only," and that undermines trust in future urgency claims.
- This is the second time in three days subscribers see the identical 2-for-$25 creative (2026-09-09), just recolored copy — high risk of banner-blindness/fatigue.
- The donation module ("Give a Dollar, Give a Bear") competes for attention right after the primary offer instead of living further down the email.
- No product imagery in the hero — it's abstract text-on-circle, so there's no visual cue for what "Select Furry Friends" actually includes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop "Extended" from the urgency language entirely — pick one deadline and hold it, or reframe as "Back by popular demand: One More Day" so it reads as a fresh event rather than a broken promise.
- 2. Move the "Give a Dollar, Give a Bear" module below "More to Explore" so it doesn't split attention from the single paid offer.
- 3. Swap the abstract "2 for $25" circle for a version with 2-3 actual bear/plush thumbnails so shoppers can see what's included before clicking.
- 4. Since this is a rerun of 2026-09-09's creative, differentiate the subject/preview more sharply from that send to avoid subscribers assuming it's a duplicate and skipping the open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `One More Day: 2 for $25 Furry Friends`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Missed It? 2 for $25 Is Back — Online Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $100+ too — shop the expanded lineup`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A bonus day to grab 2 favorites for $25`
