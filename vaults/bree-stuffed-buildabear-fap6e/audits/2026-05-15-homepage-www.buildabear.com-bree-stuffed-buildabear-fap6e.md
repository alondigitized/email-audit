## What's working

- **"Active Offers (4) View All" bottom bar** — I can see there are live promos waiting for me, and the drawer CTA is clear enough that I know something good is there if I dismiss the modal
- **Blue top announcement banner** — partially visible, signals there's a delivery-related benefit; feels timely and logistical which I appreciate as someone who orders online as backup
- **Cookiebot UI itself is clean** — "Accept" and "Manage My Preferences" are clearly labeled; no dark-pattern tricks like hiding the decline option

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal with no X / easy dismiss** — as a returning customer who's been on this site dozens of times, I should not be stopped cold at the door every visit; this is the single biggest friction point visible in the screenshot
- **Top banner is clipped** — the "Bu" text is cut off on the right edge; whatever they're trying to say (Buy One? Bundle?) is unreadable
- **Zero brand/product content above the fold** — no seasonal collab, no Pokémon hint, no new arrivals teaser — everything I care about is buried under GDPR boilerplate
- **Rewards/loyalty hooks: invisible** — I'm a rewards member and there's no "Hi Bree, you have X points" or any member-status callout anywhere I can see

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the cookie modal for returning authenticated members** — if I'm logged in or have already accepted in a previous session, don't make me do this again; at minimum, show an "X" so I can get to the bears immediately
- **Pin a persistent rewards badge to the header** — even behind the modal, a sticky "Member" chip or points counter at the top would let me know the site recognizes me the moment the overlay clears
- **Uncrop that top banner** — the right edge is chopped on mobile; whatever the delivery promise is, I need to read the full sentence; fix the text overflow so the announcement lands
- **Surface the "Active Offers (4)" count in the header or hero** — don't bury it in a bottom drawer; make it the first thing I see after accepting cookies so I'm immediately pulled toward engagement
