# NEW Holiday Mini Beans®!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent product-launch email with a live offer bolted on. The hero is a cozy stocking shot with a Mini Bean tucked in the greenery, and the headline "Stuff Stockings With Mini Beans®" is clear and seasonal. The "Last Chance: 30% OFF Online Exclusives!" banner sits at the very top, so the offer is visible before the hero. That is the right order for a send like this.
- The email pulls in two directions. The subject and hero sell Mini Beans, a small-ticket stocking-stuffer line. The 30% off applies to "select Online Exclusives," and none of the Mini Beans shown is clearly one of them. A shopper can't tell whether the discount applies to what they came for. The body also runs long, with three color-block tiles, a 30% panel, a Snow Cute banner, a four-product grid and two more tiles, and almost every block carries its own "SHOP NOW."
- Compared with the Nov 7 gift guide, which launched the same 30% offer as "Limited Time Only," this send moves to "Last Chance" within a day. That urgency feels thin, and the fine print says the sale ends Nov 8 at 11:59 p.m. CST. The Hanukkah and Christmas order-by dates are useful and credible. Overall it's a solid, on-brand send that dilutes its own hero.

## What's working

- The "Last Chance: 30% OFF Online Exclusives!" orange banner is above the hero, so the offer is seen first.
- The hero is warm and on-season, and the red headline with a single "SHOP NOW" button is clear.
- The three named tiles (Cocoa Koala, Fa La La Llama, Snowflake Kitty) have distinct colors and playful names, and they make the products easy to scan.
- The "Make Sure Gifts Arrive on Time!" block gives real order-by dates for Hanukkah (Nov. 21) and Christmas (Dec. 14), which adds credible urgency.
- The large white "30% Off" on dark green is the strongest typographic moment and can't be missed.

## What's weak

- The offer is only for "select Online Exclusives," and nothing ties it to the Mini Beans in the hero. That is a gap between promise and discount.
- There are roughly 12 "SHOP NOW" buttons, and the same copy repeats on every one. It's a verb with no noun, so no CTA stands out.
- The email is very long. The product grid (Teddy Bear, Sanrio plushes, Batty Kitty) and the Grinch and gingerbread tiles drift away from the Mini Beans theme.
- The offer is stated three times (banner, 30% panel, footer) with different urgency, "Last Chance" versus the generic panel. It reads as clutter.
- The exclusions and the "ends Nov 8" deadline are buried in the small footer print. The body copy never states the deadline.
- The Mini Beans prices aren't shown, so there is no price anchor for a stocking-stuffer pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer and the hero the same product. Say whether the Mini Beans are in the 30% off. If they aren't, headline the hero around the Mini Beans price point, such as "Stocking stuffers under $15."
- 2. Cut the body to one hero, three tiles and one offer panel, and drop the "More to Explore" and product-grid sections or move them to a different send.
- 3. Give each tile a specific CTA, such as "Get the Cocoa Koala" or "Shop Snowflake Kitty," and keep one primary button style.
- 4. Put the deadline in the banner ("Ends tonight, 11:59 p.m. CST") instead of the footer.
- 5. Show prices or a "from $X" on each Mini Bean tile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mini Beans for stockings, plus 30% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Meet the Cocoa Koala and Fa La La Llama 🎄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add a mini tee or Santa hat. 30% off select online exclusives ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Dec. 14 for Christmas. Last day for 30% off online exclusives.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML skeleton is sound: preheader, responsive media queries and Outlook VML settings are all present. The QA run flagged four product links that returned 403, four images without alt text, and a `<title>` that exposes an internal template name. The authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be checked because the relay doesn't expose them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403** to the QA probe:
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  - `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`
- A 403 (not 404) on `buildabear.com` most likely means the site's bot protection blocked the probe, so these pages may load normally for users. Re-test with a browser-like client before treating them as dead.
- All four product IDs match the image filenames (`…5567…`, `…5172…`, `…5255…`, `…5170…`), so the image and link pairing looks correct.
- 29 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so most of the email's links were never checked. The 30% pass rate understates coverage.
- The QA output shows empty link text before "SHOP NOW" in the failure lines. The extractor may not have captured the anchor text correctly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on four product images, all from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/…`:
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
  - `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg`
- These are the product images, so image-blocking clients and screen readers lose the product names.
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **`<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.** That is an internal template ID, not a user-facing title.
- The source was truncated, so table `role="presentation"` and the remaining images could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was missing. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC and one-click unsubscribe status are **unverified, not confirmed absent**. Check them against the raw message in a mailbox that preserves headers.
- The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so the physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTMs are consistent across all four links: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=HolidayMiniBeans&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2804734`.
- `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261108` contains "Testing". If this is a live broadcast, a test-segment label is leaking into production analytics. If it is just a segment name, it is confusing.
- `utm_medium=Email` is capitalized. GA channel grouping expects lowercase `email`, so these sessions may land in "Unassigned" or a separate row.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is generic and doesn't identify the sender or list.
- All four links go to individual product pages rather than a Mini Beans collection page. I can't confirm from the QA data whether that matches the subject line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four 403 product URLs with a real browser user agent. If they load, whitelist the QA probe or mark these results as false positives.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, for example the product name. Also add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Replace the `<title>` with a real title or the subject line, and keep the template name out of it.
4. Confirm `utm_term` isn't a test label. Lowercase `utm_medium` to `email` and use a meaningful `utm_source`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC from raw headers outside the relay. Also check the footer address and unsubscribe link.
6. Expand QA to resolve the 29 skipped tracking redirects so the final destinations are checked.
