# FREE Shipping Today Only!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward free-shipping promo with a real, time-bounded hook — "Today Only!" plus a clear code (SUMMER) and a single primary CTA. Build-A-Bear's blue-and-pink palette is consistent, the hero is unambiguous, and the "today only" urgency is the rare kind that actually feels credible because it's a one-day mechanic, not the perpetual countdown brands tend to lean on.
- Below the hero the email does what almost every Build-A-Bear send does — stacks secondary modules (Class of 2026, Summer Fun in All Sizes, Most Loved Stuff, navigation blocks). It's competent but cluttered, and the offer dilutes once you scroll. The hero alone is an 8; the rest of the email pulls the average down.
- Net: a strong, transactional shipping promo wrapped in the usual catalog dump. The free-shipping bar is the right thing to lead with on a Sunday and it earns the open. The body undersells the urgency by burying it under unrelated graduation/summer modules.

## What's working

- Hero communicates the entire offer in one glance: FREE Shipping, code SUMMER, Today Only.
- "Today Only!" pink starburst breaks the blue field and creates legitimate urgency.
- Single, obvious "SHOP NOW" CTA right under the headline — no competing CTAs at the top.
- Code is short, memorable, and seasonally on-brand (SUMMER).
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, color palette is consistent.

## What's weak

- Subject and preheader are wasted — "FREE Shipping Today Only!" with no preheader signal means the inbox shows just the subject twice or junk.
- Three secondary modules (Class of 2026, Summer Fun in All Sizes, Most Loved Stuff) dilute the "today only" pressure — graduation grad gifts and "all sizes" both have their own CTAs competing with the shipping offer.
- No minimum order, exclusions, or "ends at midnight ET" detail visible — credible urgency would benefit from a deadline timestamp.
- "Most Loved Stuff This Week" thumbnails are small and the SHOP NOW buttons under them repeat the hero CTA four more times — banner blindness sets in fast.
- Nothing in the body re-states the SUMMER code, so a scroller who decides to buy a Pooh plush has to scroll back up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Strip the body to one supporting module max. The whole email should reinforce "free shipping today" — kill Class of 2026 and Summer Fun, keep "Most Loved" as social proof.
- 2. Add a deadline timestamp ("Ends tonight 11:59pm CT") under the hero — credible urgency needs a clock.
- 3. Repeat the SUMMER code at every CTA, not just in the hero. Make the code itself feel like the button.
- 4. State minimum-order/exclusions in tiny text under the hero so the offer feels honest (e.g., "No minimum. Online only.").
- 5. Better subject + preheader pairing:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight — code SUMMER`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: ship your bear free with SUMMER`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No minimum. Code SUMMER at checkout. Ends 11:59pm.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day, no minimum, every order ships free.`
