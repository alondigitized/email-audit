# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the first thing I actually see when I open Build-A-Bear is not a bear — it's a wall of Cookiebot legalese eating the entire screen. There's a Pokémon discount code ("POKEMON30") peeking out at the very top of the page behind the modal, which is genuinely exciting to me, but I can't engage with any of it because a UK cookie consent wall is blocking literally everything. As a brand it reads "we prioritized compliance over customers" the second the page loaded.

## What's working

- **Pokémon promo code in the top banner** — "POKEMON30" is the exact kind of collab-calendar event I track religiously; the fact that it's in a pinned top bar means it *would* be visible to me once the modal clears
- **"Active Offers (5)" bottom strip** — this tells me there's a live deal layer on the site, and the "View All" tap suggests I could surface all five quickly without hunting through nav
- **Top banner appears to mention Uber delivery** — truncated, but if this is same-day fulfillment, that's a real hook for a gifter who needs a bear by Saturday

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks 95% of the viewport** — this is the single biggest failure; nothing can engage me if I can't see the page
- **Cookiebot is showing UK/GDPR copy** ("Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited") on what should be a US shopper's session — if I'm a US customer seeing UK entity language, that's disorienting
- **No visible primary CTA** — completely buried under the modal
- **No imagery whatsoever** — bears are a visual product; showing me a cookie wall instead of a Pokémon Pikachu plush on launch is a wasted first impression
- **No loyalty/rewards signal above the fold** — I'm a rewards member and I have zero confirmation my session recognizes me

## Recommendations

- **Implement a non-blocking cookie notice** — a slim bottom banner with Accept/Decline is compliant and doesn't nuke the entire homepage experience; the current full-screen modal should be a last resort, not a default
- **Audit the geolocation/entity logic** — a US shopper should not be seeing "Build-A-Bear Workshop UK Limited" in the consent text; get the entity language right per region
- **Pin the Pokémon promo code higher and make it tap-to-copy** — right now it's truncated behind a modal; once the page is unblocked, that code should be front and center with a visible discount percentage, not just a raw code string
- **Surface the rewards balance on page load for logged-in members** — even a "Welcome back, Bree — you have 320 points" strip would keep me from bouncing to a competitor
