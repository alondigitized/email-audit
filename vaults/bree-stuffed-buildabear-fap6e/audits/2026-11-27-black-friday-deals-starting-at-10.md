# Black Friday Deals Starting at $10!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, offer-stacked Black Friday send with a clear hero. "FURRY FRIENDS STARTING AT $10" in giant red type beside a pink bear is where the eye lands first, with a green SHOP NOW button right under it. It's a broad catalog email, though. It repeats the $10 hook from the 11/26 "TODAY ONLY: $10 Doorbusters" send, but it drops the "today only" urgency that made that one score 8.
- The three stacked panels (furry friends from $10, clothing and accessories, 25% off giants) compete for attention. The "ENDING SOON" badge on the 25% giants panel is the only real urgency cue. The panel itself says "TODAY ONLY," and the 11/27 noon-deadline send already covered the giants, so this reads as the third Black Friday giants push in one day. Below the panels, the product grid and the "More to Explore" block dilute the message, and the fine print is dense and long.
- Verdict: solid, clear and on-season, but it's more catalog than campaign. The 11/26 and 11/21 sends had sharper single-offer focus.

## What's working

- The "$10" is huge, red and readable at a glance, and the pink bear anchors it.
- Every panel has a clear green SHOP NOW button, so the CTAs are unambiguous.
- The free shipping bar at $50 sits at the top in orange, which is a good, visible incentive.
- The "order by Monday, Dec. 14" banner gives credible, useful holiday-timing information.
- The product grid (Grinch, Hello Kitty, teddy bears) gives clickable, recognizable licensed items.

## What's weak

- Three equally weighted offers (from $10, clothing "save on", 25% off giants) split attention, and the clothing panel has no number at all.
- "SAVE ON CLOTHING & ACCESSORIES!" is vague, with no percentage.
- The "SHOP ALL BLACK FRIDAY DEALS" tile and the product-grid buttons repeat the same message with different button colors (green, pink, blue), which looks inconsistent.
- The fine print runs to five dense blocks, and the "While Supplies Last" note is tiny. This weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The email is very long, and the "Start Earning Points!" pill at the top is cut off and unexplained.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make $10 furry friends the whole hero, add a deadline ("Ends 11/28"), and drop or shrink the other two panels.
- 2. Put a number on the clothing panel (for example "Outfits from $X" or "% off") or cut it.
- 3. Standardize the CTA color and copy to something specific, such as "Shop $10 Plush".
- 4. Trim the product grid to the strongest two items and shorten the fine print into a collapsed "Details" line.
- 5. Make the loyalty pill a real benefit ("Members: extra X% off").
- **Subject Alt A:** `Furry friends from $10 — through Saturday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 plush + 25% off giants today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping over $50, plus Grinch and Hello Kitty favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Dec. 14 and gifts arrive in time for Christmas.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML source is mostly well-formed, with a preheader, hidden spacer text and responsive media queries. The QA data shows four unverifiable product links (HTTP 403), missing alt text on four images, and unconfirmed unsubscribe and authentication headers. A `Testing` segment label in the UTM parameters and an internal template name in the `<title>` suggest production-hygiene gaps.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 links returned 403.** All are "SHOP NOW" PDP links on `www.buildabear.com`, for example `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html?utm_source=Campaign&...&utm_id=2807448`. The others are `/grinch-plush---includes-extra-large-heart/035437.html`, `/sanrio-frosted-hello-kitty-plush/035433.html` and `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`.
  - A 403 from a Demandware/CDN storefront usually means the probe was bot-blocked, not that the page is dead. This is unconfirmed either way, so verify manually in a real browser before calling these broken.
  - The QA output also shows an empty anchor label (`Broken link (403): `), so the link text is not captured cleanly.
- **25 tracking/redirect links were skipped** by the HTTP probe. Their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **UTM casing is inconsistent.** `utm_source=Campaign` and `utm_medium=Email` are capitalised. GA is case-sensitive, so these can split from lowercase `email` traffic.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `40260801135437~x20117024.jpg`
  - `11260801135433~x20114021.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`

  These are the product images served from `/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. They are likely linked, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get no accessible name for the link.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The source shows only `xmlns`, `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o`.
- **`<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.** It exposes the internal template name and is not meaningful to users.
- I can't assess `role="presentation"` on layout tables or the body markup, because the HTML source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, so check the raw message headers. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com` is unknown. It is likely a relay artifact.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the in-body CAN-SPAM elements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **`utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261127`** carries a "Testing" segment label into a live send. This pollutes analytics, and the `US-US` prefix looks duplicated.
- **`utm_campaign=SelectFurryFriends10`** names a plush promo, while the subject is "Black Friday Deals Starting at $10!". Reporting will attribute the send to a different campaign name than the one customers see.
- **UTMs are otherwise consistent across the four PDP links:** `utm_content=Promo` and `utm_id=2807448`. The $10 price claim on the landing pages could not be checked because of the 403s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four PDP URLs with a browser-like user agent or manually. If they are genuinely blocked or removed, fix them before further sends.
2. Add descriptive `alt` attributes to the four product images listed above.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and replace the `<title>` with a user-facing string.
4. Inspect the raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
5. Lowercase the `utm_source` and `utm_medium` values, drop `Testing` from `utm_term`, and align `utm_campaign` with the Black Friday campaign name.
6. Resolve and verify the 25 skipped tracking links, either by following redirects or by allowlisting the probe.
