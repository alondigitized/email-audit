## What's working

- Hero plush (Strawberry Ice Cream Pochacco) is well-photographed, on-brand color palette, and instantly readable as the subject of the email.
- Headline "BERRY SWEET" + supporting copy reinforces the character concept without over-explaining.
- Candy-stripe awning motif repeats across both hero panels, giving the "Ice Cream Shop" sub-collection visual consistency.
- Clean visual hierarchy — no overlapping elements, no broken images, buttons render correctly throughout.
- "Most Loved Stuff This Week" section gives a low-effort browse path for anyone not sold on Pochacco specifically.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or loyalty pricing anywhere in the email — same miss the 7/26 "Summer Vibes Activated!" send got called out for.
- "Shop Now" is repeated verbatim on nearly every CTA (5+ times) — generic and undifferentiated, no product-specific copy.
- No urgency device (limited stock, launch window, event date) despite this being framed as a new-collection drop.
- Zero social proof or product detail (size, price, sound-feature availability) — a purchase decision has to happen entirely off the plush's looks.
- Third straight "new character" email this window with no escalating offer, which risks fatigue against 7/25 and 7/28 sends covering similar ground.

- 1. Add a concrete offer or bundle hook tied to the ice cream sub-line ("Buy the Duo, get a mini scoop free" or a flat % off the Sanrio collab) — this is the single highest-leverage fix given the pattern across recent sends.
- 2. Vary CTA copy per product ("Meet Pochacco," "Adopt Chococat," "Collect the Mini Beans") instead of five identical "Shop Now" buttons.
- 3. Add a light urgency cue under the hero — "New arrival, while supplies last" or a specific end date for the Ice Cream Shop collab.
