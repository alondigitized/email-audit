# Cheer for the Country You Love! ♥️⚽
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's FIFA World Cup 2026 send — and the timing is genuinely on point. The World Cup is hosted across the USA, Canada, and Mexico this summer, so the four-country soccer bear grid (USA, Mexico, Canada, plus what appears to be an Argentina "GOAT" bear) isn't arbitrary nationalism — it's smart event merchandising. "MADE FOR MATCHDAY MOMENTS!" is punchy and the USA soccer bear in sunglasses anchors the hero cleanly.
- The email has two passengers that dilute the soccer message: a Star Wars "Dadalorian" module stuffed in the middle (Father's Day is June 21, so the calendar logic is there, but the execution is a non-sequitur), and a separate "YOU GOAT THIS!" goat plush at the bottom. The GOAT pun is clever in a soccer context but visually reads as a second solo product drop, not a soccer-theme callback. The result is three competing narratives in one scroll.
- No discount, no urgency, no deadline — pure awareness/shop send. For a warm Build-A-Bear audience that follows character drops weekly, that's acceptable. But after a week of high-scoring launches (Shark 9/10, Chococat 8/10), this is a step down in execution clarity.

## What's working

- **Hero timing is legitimate**: FIFA World Cup 2026 is live this summer in North America — the host nation trio (USA/Mexico/Canada) in the product grid is contextually exact, not generic.
- **"MADE FOR MATCHDAY MOMENTS!" headline**: clear, energetic, and immediately category-placing.
- **GOAT pun** ("YOU GOAT THIS!"): earns a smile if you read it as soccer slang — brief moment of brand personality.
- **Star Wars Dadalorian section**: Father's Day timing (June 21) makes calendar sense; carving out a secondary audience in a single send is a reasonable efficiency play.
- **Clean render**: no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds on mobile view.

## What's weak

- **No offer or incentive**: zero discount, no free shipping, no bundle — pure FOMO with no economic hook to close the gap between interest and cart.
- **Three narrative threads competing**: soccer World Cup + Dadalorian Father's Day gift + standalone Goat plush. A subscriber has to decide which story they're in.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTAs are generic**: every button says the same thing — no differentiation between "shop USA bear" vs. "shop Star Wars" vs. "make your own goat."
- **Preview text likely leaking tagline**: "the stuff you love" is the first visible text in the email body, which is what inbox clients pull when no explicit preheader is set — it neither complements nor advances the soccer subject.
- **Cadence concern**: after six consecutive character-launch emails this week, the pivot to a thematic/event email is welcome — but the "YOU GOAT THIS!" section reads like a pulled-forward character launch bolted onto a World Cup send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Dadalorian module or give it its own email.** Father's Day is June 21 — there are 11 days left. Send a dedicated Dadalorian Father's Day email June 16–18 at peak gifting decision time. Keeping it here buries both stories.
- 2. **Add a World Cup countdown offer.** Even "Free shipping on soccer bears through June 14" creates urgency tied to a real-world event deadline.
- 3. **Differentiate the SHOP NOW CTAs.** "Cheer for USA →", "Hoof It for GOAT →" — specificity lifts click rates on multi-product grids.
- 4. **Set an explicit preheader** that advances the subject instead of defaulting to "the stuff you love."
- 5. **Lead the GOAT plush copy with the soccer angle** ("The soccer GOAT you can actually hug") so it reads as thematic, not a separate product drop.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your World Cup Bear Lineup Is Here ⚽🏆`
- **Subject Alt B:** `USA. Mexico. Canada. Pick Your Bear. ⚽`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soccer bears for all three host nations — shop before the tournament heats up.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The ultimate matchday friends are here. Free shipping this week.`
