# Give the Gift of Hello Kitty®!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal Hello Kitty hero with no offer attached. "SAY HELLO TO WINTER FUN" sits over a frosted, pastel plush with a purple SHOP NOW button, and the eye lands there first. The delivery-deadline banner (Hanukkah order by Sat. Nov. 21, Christmas by Mon. Dec. 14) is the most useful element in the send. It gives real urgency without a discount. The Nov 5 Hello Kitty send and the Nov 7 gift guide with 30% OFF set a higher bar. This one has no price hook at all.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. Promise Pets, the reindeer, four Mini Beans-style products, a Grinch tile and a family tile each get a SHOP NOW. The subject promises "the gift of Hello Kitty," but the body spends about 80% of its length on other things. The email is competent and on-brand, but it dilutes its own hook and offers no reason to buy today.
- Judgement: a middling 5. It would improve with a Hello Kitty-only product grid and a single offer.

## What's working

- The hero is clear and seasonal. The Frosted Hello Kitty plush, "Say Hello to Winter Fun" and a single purple button make a clean focal point.
- The shipping-deadline banner gives credible, specific urgency with two dates, which suits holiday gifting.
- The Hello Kitty and Friends Frosted Collection badge signals exclusivity.
- The color-blocked sections (green, maroon) separate the content and are easy to scan.
- Product tiles have short names and individual SHOP NOW buttons.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or free-shipping offer anywhere. After the 30% OFF Online Exclusives sends on Nov 7 and 8, that is a step back.
- The subject says Hello Kitty, but only the hero delivers it. The other modules (Promise Pets, reindeer, Mini Beans, Grinch) pull attention away.
- The product names show visible encoding errors. "Mini BeansÂ®" appears three times in the Stockings section.
- The "Stuff Their Stockings" grid has no prices or reviews, and none of its products are Hello Kitty items.
- There are eight or more SHOP NOW buttons and no hierarchy between them, so the reader has no clear next step after the hero.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- A uniform 403 across well-formed Demandware product URLs is most likely bot protection rejecting the QA probe. It is not confirmed breakage. A re-test with a browser UA or a real browser session would settle it. Treat these as unverified, not as dead links.
- The QA label for these links reads `link text: ""` before "SHOP NOW". The extractor may be capturing an image-only anchor. I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **23 tracking links were skipped** (redirect domains). Their destinations and click-through health are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images have no `alt` attribute.** These are the product images from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`, named `45230700632286~x20029099.jpg`, `36230800432432~x20037117.jpg`, `93230700632285~x20029096.jpg` and `47250702334767Alt1~x20250083.jpg`. With images off, or in a screen reader, the SHOP NOW links have no descriptive context.
- **Probable encoding defect in the preheader:** `See Whatâ€™s New from SanrioÂ® This Holiday Season!` is classic UTF-8 → Windows-1252 double-encoding of `’` and `®`. ## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing or empty (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** If the original message really has no `text/plain` alternative, that hurts deliverability and accessibility. The relay may have dropped it, so check the raw MIME.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA itself notes AgentMail may not capture these headers. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four PDP links with a real browser or a whitelisted UA to rule out bot-protection false positives. Skip any fix until that confirms they are broken.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images.
3. Fix the preheader encoding. Confirm the ESP sends UTF-8 with the correct `Content-Transfer-Encoding`, or use the entities `&rsquo;` and `&reg;`. Remove the orphan `</span>`.
4. Confirm the sent message has a `text/plain` part.
5. Inspect the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. The QA warnings may only reflect relay stripping.
6. Remove `Testing` from the `utm_term` template for live sends, or confirm this was a test deployment.
7. Resolve a sample of the 23 skipped tracking redirects to verify their final destinations and UTM carry-through.
