# Don’t Miss 30% OFF Online Exclusives!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a reminder send for the 30% OFF Online Exclusives offer from the Nov 7 gift guide ("Lights, Camera, GIFTS!"). The hero is a giant white "30% OFF" on forest green with a red SHOP NOW button, so the offer is the first thing you see. For a promo email, that's the right call.
- The body is weaker than the hero. Below it the email turns into a catalog: two promo tiles, a four-product grid and a "More to Explore" pair, with roughly ten near-identical buttons. The hero says "Select Online Exclusives," but nothing in the body tells me which products qualify. It's also the second Build-A-Bear send today after "NEW Holiday Mini Beans®!", and the third in three days to mention 30% off. The "Don't miss" urgency has no deadline, so it reads as filler.
- Judgement: a clear, on-brand offer email with a strong top and a diluted middle. It's fine, but it's a repeat, and the one real urgency hook is buried: the order-by dates for Hanukkah (Nov. 21) and Christmas (Dec. 14).

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer lands in about a second.
- The red SHOP NOW sits directly under the hero copy, above the fold.
- The "Make Sure Gifts Arrive on Time!" banner gives dated shipping cutoffs for Hanukkah and Christmas. These are the most credible deadlines in the email.
- The product grid names specific items (Batty Kitty, Happy Hugs Teddy Bear, Maleficent Dragon, Timeless Teddy Bear) with individual buttons.
- The green and red palette and plush photography are seasonal and consistent with the brand's earlier holiday sends.

## What's weak

- "Don't miss limited-time savings" gives no end date. The only sale terms are in tiny footer fine print, which mentions an offer that is valid until 11:59 p.m. CST on November 8.
- It's unclear whether the featured products are among the "select" exclusives. The grid shows no prices, no sale badges and no "30% off" markers.
- About ten SHOP NOW buttons with identical copy split attention, and the generic wording tells me nothing about where each one goes.
- The hero is text only, with no product, and the gift photos sit below it. The visual payoff comes late.
- It's a rerun of the Nov 7 offer, sent the same day as the Mini Beans launch. That is a lot of Build-A-Bear in one inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Use "Ends tonight, 11:59 p.m. CST" (or a countdown) in place of "Don't miss limited-time savings." This is the highest-impact change because the offer really does end tonight.
- 2. Show that products qualify. Add "30% OFF" badges and sale prices to the four grid products, or replace the grid with confirmed online exclusives.
- 3. Cut the button count. Keep one hero CTA, one product row and one closing CTA, and make the copy specific, such as "Shop 30% Off Exclusives."
- 4. Combine same-day sends, or hold the reminder until tomorrow, so the inbox doesn't get two Build-A-Bear emails in a day.
- 5. Move the shipping cutoff banner directly under the hero as a second urgency layer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — 30% off plush gifts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select online exclusives, today only. Order by Nov. 21 for Hanukkah delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Batty Kitty, Maleficent Dragon and more are on sale until 11:59 p.m. CST.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template with responsive media queries, a preheader and VML/MSO fallbacks. The QA run flagged four product CTAs that returned 403, four images with missing alt text, and several compliance and authentication headers that could not be verified. The 403s and missing headers are probably probe or relay artifacts, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four "SHOP NOW" CTAs returned HTTP 403** when probed, all on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
- A 403 (not 404/410) across all four, with the `utm_*` query strings intact, most likely means bot or WAF blocking of the QA client. Real browsers may load these fine. It is not confirmed broken, but it is unverified. A manual browser check or a request with a realistic UA is needed.
- **24 tracking links were skipped** because they are on click-redirect domains, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- The QA output lists the link text but the anchor text prefix is empty. It is not clear whether the empty field is a QA-tool formatting gap or an anchor issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four product images lack `alt` text**:
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  
  All are served from `/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. With images blocked, these four product tiles have no text equivalent. They are likely the same four products as the "SHOP NOW" links.
- `<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`, which exposes the internal template name. This is minor.
- The preheader uses a deprecated `<font class="">` element inside the hidden `span.preheader`. This is harmless but unnecessary.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 ">` has a trailing space in the value. Parsers generally tolerate it.
- The HTML source was truncated, so alt text on other images, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and `lang` on `<html>` could not be fully assessed. `<html>` has no `lang` attribute in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The truncation limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. If the sender truly omits them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) would not be met.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation. The From domain `ebuildabear.com` should be checked directly via DNS or the original message headers.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are present and consistent on the flagged links: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=LastChance30OffOnlineOnlyStyles`, `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261108`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2804732`.
- **`utm_term` contains `Testing`** (`US-US_Testing_Hot-20261108`). This looks like a test or segment label in a production send, which could pollute reporting.
- **Mixed-case values** (`Campaign`, `Email`) can split channel reporting in analytics tools that treat UTM values as case-sensitive. `utm_source=Campaign` is also a generic value that does not identify the sender or platform.
- Landing page alignment with the 30% offer could not be verified, because the 403 responses prevented content inspection.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four PDP URLs in a real browser or with a browser-like user agent. If they load, whitelist the QA client. If they fail for real users, fix them before more of the send is opened.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images (e.g., product name), and confirm the other images have alt text or `alt=""` where they are decorative.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers at the sender (not the relay). Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
4. Remove `Testing` from the `utm_term` segment naming for production sends. Standardize UTM values to lowercase, and use a meaningful `utm_source`.
5. Replace the template-name `<title>`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and drop the unused `<font>` tag.
6. Rerun the audit with the full HTML (untruncated) to verify the unsubscribe link, footer address, merge tokens and the remaining images.
