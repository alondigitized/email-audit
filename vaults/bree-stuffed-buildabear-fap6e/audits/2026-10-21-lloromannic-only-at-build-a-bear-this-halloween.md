# Lloromannic™ Only at Build-A-Bear This Halloween!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong launch send with no offer. The hero sells a new licensed collab: Lloromannic™ (Sanrio) arriving at Build-A-Bear "for the first time ever." The pink-and-black gothic mirror frame is on-theme and distinctive. The "SPOOKY CUTE" headline and the teal SHOP NOW button read in one glance. The subject names the product and the season, so it delivers what it promises.
- The body is where it loses focus. After the Lloromannic Berry, Cherry and Hello Kitty tiles, it keeps going: a generic "Rock Out in a Huge Way!" tile, a Día de los Muertos block, a four-product grid, and a "More to Explore" pair. That is about a dozen SHOP NOW buttons, and the collab story gets diluted. Unlike the 2026-10-19 "Boo! FREE Shipping TODAY ONLY!" send, nothing here gives a reason to act today. It's the same pattern as the 10-15 and 10-18 catalog sends: good creative, no price or deadline.
- Verdict: a good fit for Sanrio and Hello Kitty fans, and a mediocre email for everyone else. It would perform better if it were about half as long.

## What's working

- The hero frame is distinctive and on-theme. Gothic mirror, bats and a pink-and-black palette make Lloromannic feel like an event.
- "First time ever" is real exclusivity and gives a reason to pay attention.
- The three-tile character stack (Berry, Cherry, Starry Sky Hello Kitty) shows the actual plush clearly. Each tile has a color-matched background and a white button.
- Section headers like "Pick Your Halloween Treat" make the product grid easy to scan.
- There are no visible render bugs. Images load cleanly and the type is legible.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or deadline anywhere. Halloween is 10 days away, but nothing says to order by a date to get it in time.
- The email is too long, with about a dozen near-identical SHOP NOW buttons. The first CTA competes with every tile below it.
- The "Rock Out in a Huge Way!" and Día de los Muertos blocks have nothing to do with Lloromannic. They break the collab story and add clutter.
- The product grid shows the Werewolf twice, once in a hoodie set and once alone. It's repetitive, and none of the items show a price.
- The "SHOP COLLECTION" pill sits oddly between sections. It is the clearest collection CTA and it is buried mid-email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the Lloromannic story. Keep the hero, the three character tiles and one "SHOP THE COLLECTION" button, and move the Día de los Muertos, Halloween grid and "More to Explore" sections to a separate send.
- 2. Add a credible urgency line, such as "Order by [date] for Halloween delivery," or show a price on each tile. "Starting at $XX" would help.
- 3. Make the hero button specific: "Meet Lloromannic" or "Shop Lloromannic" instead of the generic "SHOP NOW."
- 4. Add a trust or social-proof signal, such as "Limited-edition collab" or a stock note, to support the exclusivity.
- 5. If the lower sections stay, give each a unique, specific CTA and one product per row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The HTML also has an encoding artifact, a stray closing tag, and test values in the UTM parameters. The QA pass rate was 27%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403.** The URLs are on `www.buildabear.com`, e.g. `/enchanting-kitty-stuffed-animal/035040.html?...` and `/mystical-werewolf-stuffed-animal/035021.html?...`. A 403 (not 404) from a Demandware storefront usually means bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe, so the pages are probably live. I can't confirm that from the data. A real-browser check is needed before calling them broken.
- **26 tracking links were skipped** because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Some links bypass the click tracker.** The four product links above are direct `buildabear.com` URLs with UTMs, while the other links go through redirect domains. Click attribution is split across two mechanisms.
- **Test value in a live-looking URL:** `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261021` contains "Testing". This is either a test send or a segment label that was never renamed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images.** The filenames are `21261612235021_35107_29377_21512~x20822079.jpg`, `49250802835040~x20053107.jpg`, `84250801435021~x20058110.jpg` and `45231321225539_27647_24630x20555013.jpg`, all on `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. With images off or a screen reader, these product tiles have no text equivalent.
- **Character-encoding artifact:** the preheader reads `Hello KittyÂ® and Friends`. This is the typical result of UTF-8 `®` decoded as Latin-1, and it will show in inbox preview text. It may come from the relay capture, but the `<meta charset=utf-8>` is present, so check the sent MIME `Content-Type`/`Content-Transfer-Encoding`.
