# Creep on in and Visit the Workshop!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent Halloween catalog email that arrives a day too late and offers nothing to act on. The hero is warm: a family in costumes with a Build-A-Bear staffer, under the "Halloween With Heart" headline. The three color-blocked product bands (Enchanting Kitty, Mystical Werewolf, Owlloween Cauldron) are the best part of the layout. The eye moves down the page easily.
- Nothing here gives the reader a reason to click today. The subject is a pun with no offer, and the body has no price, no deadline and no shipping promise. Halloween is tomorrow, so "bring in your whole boo crew" has almost no runway. This is the fourth no-offer send in four days (10/27 Bearemy, 10/28 Wildly Cute Gift Ideas, 10/29 Mashimals, now this). A subscriber who got "Up to 50% OFF Online!" on 10/24 has been trained to expect value, and this delivers none.
- Verdict: a well-built, forgettable send with weak timing. It should have gone out 7 to 10 days earlier, or carried a last-minute offer or in-store hook.

## What's working

- The color-blocked bands (green, orange, pink) are bold and scannable, and each is its own tappable product link.
- The hero shows people in costumes, which sells the in-store experience instead of just plush.
- The "SEE WHAT'S IN STORE" pill is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The "Start Earning Points!" pill at the top surfaces the loyalty program without crowding the creative.
- The costume row has named products with a red SHOP NOW under each, so the reader can see what's behind the click.

## What's weak

- There is no offer anywhere: no %, no $, no shipping or in-store perk. The subject promises a visit, but the email gives no reason to go now.
- The date is wrong for the campaign. With Halloween a day away, an online order won't arrive, and the email never says what's available in-store today.
- The "HARRY POTTERâ„¢" caption has visibly garbled characters, which looks sloppy on a licensed product.
- The "More to Explore" tiles are two unlabeled images over identical "SHOP NOW" buttons. The reader can't tell where either goes.
- The email stacks about six CTAs plus a mystery-plush banner and a partner banner. The "Isloromannie" collab block is dense and small, and it competes with the main story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one time-bound, in-store hook above the fold, such as "Costume your bear tonight, open till 8" or "Last-minute Halloween: in-store pickup today". That changes this from a catalog to a reason to act.
- 2. Fix the "HARRY POTTERâ„¢" text and relabel the two "More to Explore" tiles with real product names.
- 3. Cut the email to one hero, one product trio and one mystery-plush block. Drop the partner banner or move it to a separate send.
- 4. Move this send earlier in the week, or fold it into the 10/28 or 10/29 sends instead of mailing four days in a row.
- 5. Put a loyalty benefit in the hero, such as double points on costumes, instead of a small red pill.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Creep on in and Visit the Workshop!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP template with a hidden preheader, MSO/VML support and responsive media queries. The QA run flagged four product-link 403s, an empty plain-text part and four images without alt text. The 403s are probably bot-blocking rather than dead links. A test-segment value in the UTM parameters is a real issue, and the QA data can't confirm the header and compliance items.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 × 403 on "SHOP NOW" product links:** all are `buildabear.com` PDPs, one for each of the four featured products:
  - `/harry-potter-harry-sweater/035262.html`
  - `/pumpkin-costume-2-pc./025964.html`
  - `/disney-and-pixar-toy-story-jessie-costume/034866.html`
  - `/witch-costume/035002.html`
- **Likely cause:** the 403 is probably bot or WAF filtering of the probe, since the domain is a production Demandware (SFCC) storefront. A real-browser check is needed before calling these broken. The QA output also shows an empty text label in `Broken link (403):`, so the failure detail is incomplete.
- **Unchecked links:** 27 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Test value in production UTMs:** every probed link carries `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261030`. "Testing" suggests a test or seed segment name leaked into the live send, and it will pollute analytics segmentation.
- **Template name leaked:** `<title>Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A</title>` exposes the internal template name.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images:** all four are Demandware `dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...` URLs:
  - `95250801935262~x20101125.jpg`
  - `13231221225964x20432016.jpg`
  - `01250702434866~x20056096.jpg`
  - `84250702335002~x20136107.jpg`
- **Effect:** with images blocked, screen readers and clients will fall back to the filenames or nothing.
- **Plain-text part is empty:** the QA reports `Text version is 0 chars` (FAIL). There is no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts accessibility and spam scoring.
- **Not flagged:** the preheader span, `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack, `mso-hide`, viewport and `format-detection` meta tags all look standard. The HTML was truncated, so the rest of the markup couldn't be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens, such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`. Because the source is truncated, this applies only to the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. That makes this unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay doesn't expose it. Verify against the raw message at `ebuildabear.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** these aren't visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters present:** `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=HalloweenWorkshop`, `utm_term`, `utm_content=Promo` and `utm_id=2802840` appear consistently on all four probed product links.
- **Non-standard values:** `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic placeholder rather than a source identifier such as a list or newsletter name. `Email` is capitalized, and GA4 channel grouping matches `email` or `e-mail` case-sensitively in some configurations. The same `utm_content=Promo` is on all four links, so clicks can't be told apart by position.
- **Landing page continuity:** couldn't be checked, because the PDPs returned 403 to the probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-verify the four PDP URLs in a real browser or with a UA allowlist. If they load, treat the 403s as probe false positives. If not, fix them before resending.
2. Replace `Testing` in `utm_term` with the real segment name, and confirm no test or seed segment is on the live send.
3. Add a plain-text `text/plain` MIME part (currently 0 chars).
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, for example product names.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment from the raw headers at the ESP. These QA warnings are relay limitations.
6. Standardize UTM casing, give `utm_source` a real source value, and use a distinct `utm_content` per link.
7. Rename the `<title>` to something customer-facing rather than the internal template name.
8. Rerun the audit with untruncated HTML to check the footer, the physical address and any merge tokens.
