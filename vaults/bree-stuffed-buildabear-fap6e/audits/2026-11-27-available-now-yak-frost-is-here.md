# Available Now: Yak Frost Is Here!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday send that opens with a product launch and then stacks two promos under it. Yak Frost, a fluffy blue-and-white highland cow, gets the hero panel under "BLACK FRIDAY EXCLUSIVE LAUNCH!". The "while he lasts!" copy adds scarcity. It's a cute, collectible-driven hero, and the subject line matches it.
- The weakness is competition inside the email. Right under the hero sit "Furry Friends Starting at $10" and a huge "50% OFF Giant Furry Friends — until noon today!" panel. The 50% panel is the strongest offer, and it's the one with real urgency. The Yak hero has no price at all. Three green SHOP NOW buttons stack in a row, and the eye splits between launch, $10 and 50%.
- Compared with the 11/26 "$10 Doorbusters" send, which had one giant number and one button, this one is busier and less focused. It's a decent send, but it tries to be a launch email and a doorbuster email at once. Pick one lead.

## What's working

- The Yak Frost hero is charming, large and clear, with "limited supplies" scarcity.
- The "50% OFF" panel is huge and legible, with a credible "until noon TODAY" deadline.
- The orange FREE Shipping banner at $50+ is visible at the top.
- The "order by Monday, Dec. 14" Christmas delivery banner is useful and seasonal.
- The festive red ribbon frame is on-brand and consistent with the earlier Black Friday sends.

## What's weak

- The Yak hero shows no price, so the launch has no concrete value attached.
- Three stacked offers (launch, $10, 50%) compete for attention and dilute the hierarchy.
- The "Furry Friends Starting at $10" panel has tiny "ONLINE ONLY" text and no clear product link.
- The pink "Start Earning Points" pill sits at the top, but no member pricing appears anywhere else.
- The lower sections ("Shop By Product", "More to Explore") add six or more more CTAs, and the dense fine print undercuts the honest-offer feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50% OFF, ends-at-noon offer first and move Yak Frost into a smaller secondary slot. Or keep Yak as the hero and add its price and a "limit 2 per guest" cue, so the launch is concrete.
- 2. Merge the $10 and 50% panels into one offer block so there's a single price story.
- 3. Link the "More to Explore" CTAs to specific destinations instead of two identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 4. Use the preheader to carry the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Yak Frost just landed (limited supplies)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off giant plush ends at noon + meet Yak Frost`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 50% off Giant Furry Friends until noon today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusive Black Friday launch, and plush from $10 online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML layout with responsive media queries and consistent UTM tagging on product links. The QA run flags four product links that returned 403 and four product images with no alt text. Compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers were not captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 product "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403** (the same four links the QA run reports as broken):
  - `buildabear.com/nine-tailed-kitsune-fox-stuffed-animal/034916.html`
  - `buildabear.com/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-frosted-pochacco-plush/035435.html`
  - `buildabear.com/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-my-sweet-piano-plush/033927.html`
  - `buildabear.com/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
- A 403 from a Demandware-hosted storefront usually means bot or WAF blocking of the probe, not a dead page. This is **unconfirmed as a user-facing break**. It should be re-tested with a browser-like client or from a real inbox.
- **25 tracking/redirect links were skipped** (no HTTP probe), so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- The QA output shows an empty link-text field next to the "SHOP NOW" label. The link text itself is present, so this is a reporting quirk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Four product images have no alt text.** All are served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `01250700834916~x20047103.jpg`
  - `66260801135435~x20116022.jpg`
  - `91240802233927~x20025114.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
- With images blocked, those product modules render blank and screen readers read out the filenames.
- **Internal template name in `<title>`:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. Some clients and the "view in browser" page show this string.
- **Minor:** the preheader uses the deprecated `<font class="">` tag. The viewport meta has a trailing space (`initial-scale=1.0 `). Neither is likely to break rendering.
- The preheader (`display:none; mso-hide:all`), the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding hack, and the `u+.em_body` Gmail fix are implemented correctly. The MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are absent from the original message, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com` is unknown. This is a limitation of the relay, not evidence of failure.
- The footer was truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are present and consistent on all four product links: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=YakFrostLaunch`, `utm_term=US-LeadUSClick6M-20261127`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2807429`.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on every link.** Module- or position-level click attribution is not possible.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is generic and capitalized. It will split from a lowercase `campaign` in GA unless normalized.
- The four product URLs checked (Kitsune Fox, Pochacco, Piano, Timeless Teddy) do not match the "Yak Frost" subject by slug. The Yak Frost hero link is not among the checked links, so I can't confirm the subject and landing page line up.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the four 403 product URLs with a real browser user agent. Fix them only if they fail for real users.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images (product name and variant) and any other `<img>` that lacks it.
3. Replace the `<title>` with a campaign-appropriate string and drop the template name `Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.
4. Check the raw `.eml` from the ESP, not the AgentMail copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Give each module a unique `utm_content` (for example `hero`, `product1` through `product4`) and use lowercase `utm_source` values.
6. Probe the 25 skipped tracking links by following their redirects to the final landing page.
7. Confirm the Yak Frost hero CTA exists and points to the Yak Frost collection or PDP.
