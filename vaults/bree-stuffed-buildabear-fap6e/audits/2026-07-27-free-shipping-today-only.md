# FREE Shipping Today Only!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the discount send the last few Build-A-Bear emails have been missing. The 2026-07-26 "Summer Vibes Activated!" review flagged "no discount, no price anchor, no urgency — just here's stuff, buy it," and this one fixes exactly that: a bold, unmissable "FREE Shipping With Code SUMMER at Checkout!" hero with a "Today Only!" badge and a single pink SHOP NOW button, no scroll required.
- Below the fold it reverts to familiar Build-A-Bear catalog mode — the Lightning Bug (recycled from the 2026-07-22 send), a Blueberry Highland Cow scent add-on, a 2x2 "Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid, and a stack of nav-style pill buttons (Build Your Own Bear, Personalize, Giftshop, Workshop). None of that is broken, but none of it is new news either — it's the same modular template this brand runs every week.
- The render is clean: no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent blue/pink branding throughout. The weak spot is the offer's honesty — "FREE Shipping" reads simple in the hero, but the footer fine print reveals it excludes gift cards, e-gift cards, bonus cards, and charitable donations, and can't stack with other offers. That's standard legal boilerplate, but it tempers an otherwise strong, urgency-driven hero. Overall this is one of the stronger recent sends because it finally leads with a real, time-boxed incentive instead of just showing product.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: single offer (free shipping), single code (SUMMER), single CTA (SHOP NOW), single deadline (Today Only) — no competing messages.
- "Today Only!" badge creates real urgency, and the footer confirms a credible, specific cutoff (11:59 p.m. CST, 7/27).
- Clean, bug-free render — no layout breaks, no image gaps, consistent brand palette top to bottom.
- Good visual hierarchy: hero offer first, then two secondary product callouts, then a bestseller grid, then navigation — a logical scroll order.

## What's weak

- The offer feels less generous once you read the fine print: excludes gift cards, e-gift cards, bonus cards, and charitable donations, and can't combine with other offers — that's the kind of exclusionary detail the rubric flags as undercutting an "honest" offer.
- The Lightning Bug module is a straight reuse of the 2026-07-22 "Light Up Summer Nights" hero art — subscribers who opened that email will recognize it as recycled, not new.
- Generic "SHOP NOW" CTA copy repeated six times in the email (hero + 2 product callouts + 4 grid items) — no differentiation in verb choice to signal what's being shopped.
- No loyalty/member pricing or personalization anywhere, despite this being a good spot to reward repeat customers with stacked savings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the CTA copy do more work — swap generic "SHOP NOW" repeated six times for specific verbs tied to each product ("Meet Lightning Bug," "Scent My Cow," "Grab the Minotaur") so the eye differentiates offers instead of pattern-matching the same button.
- 2. Surface the exclusions as a short one-line disclaimer directly under the hero CTA ("Excludes gift cards & donations") rather than only in dense footer legal text — protects trust without killing urgency.
- 3. Swap the Lightning Bug module for a product that hasn't already had its own dedicated send this month — reusing 2026-07-22's hero art two sends later reads as filler.
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit end time in the hero itself ("Ends 11:59pm tonight") instead of just "Today Only" — currently the real deadline only lives in the footer fine print.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Free Shipping Code: SUMMER`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No minimum, no catch — just use SUMMER at checkout today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus new arrivals: Lightning Bug, Blueberry Highland Cow & more.`

The email renders via a standard MSO/Outlook-compatible table layout, but 4 of 4 product CTAs return HTTP 403 errors and the plain-text MIME part is empty, indicating both broken destination links and a deliverability gap.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken product links (403 Forbidden)**, all "SHOP NOW" CTAs:
  - `buildabear.com/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `buildabear.com/minotaur-plush-with-warrior-outfit-and-axe/034407.html`
  - `buildabear.com/sea-angel-plush/034474.html`
  - `buildabear.com/spooky-kitty-stuffed-animal/034053.html`
  
  All four share identical UTM params (`utm_campaign=FreeShip`, `utm_id=2782411`), suggesting a systemic issue with this campaign's product-page URLs or a bot/WAF block on the destination rather than isolated broken links — worth confirming against a non-automated fetch, since 403s can also result from bot-detection on the retailer's WAF rather than a genuinely dead page.
- **22 tracking/redirect links skipped** from HTTP probing (click-tracking domains) — not independently verified as functional; flagged as unverified rather than a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization variables, or dynamic-content placeholders (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{first_name}}`) are present in the visible HTML source. No issues found in the available markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe via mail client (Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement) is not confirmed present.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

Structurally consistent — no tagging issues found.
- However, since the underlying destination URLs return 403, UTM alignment is currently moot — traffic from this campaign cannot reach the landing pages to be attributed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority fix**: Investigate the 403s on all 4 product URLs before send — confirm whether it's a dead SKU/redirect issue or a bot-detection false-positive on the automated checker's request.
2. Add a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative (currently 0 chars) to support text-only clients and improve spam-filter scoring.

4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist at the source ESP (not just at the relay) to ensure compliance with Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements and RFC 8058.
