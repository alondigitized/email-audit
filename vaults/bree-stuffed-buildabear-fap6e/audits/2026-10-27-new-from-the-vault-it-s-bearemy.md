# New from the Vault: It’s Bearemy!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a nostalgia-led brand send with a strong hero and no offer. The "Back From the Vault" banner shows Bearemy, a retro mascot bear in a colorful cap and red varsity jacket. The "FROM 1999" badge and the "30 years" copy sell the heritage story well, and the yellow SHOP NOW button stands out against the navy. It is a clear, fun collector-bait hero.

There is no price, no percentage and no deadline anywhere. ## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has solid structure (preheader, MSO conditionals, responsive classes), but the QA run flagged 6 destination links with 403s, a 0-character plain-text part, and 4 product images with no alt text. Several QA failures (headers, text part) may come from the AgentMail relay rather than the sender. The 403s are probably bot blocking by the probe, not real breakage.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 links returned HTTP 403** to the QA probe. All are on `www.buildabear.com`:
  - 4 "SHOP NOW" product links: `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`, `/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`, `/sanrio-cherry-plush/035170.html`, `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - 2 image-tile links: `/stuffed-animals/featured/back-in-stock` and `/new-arrivals`
  - A uniform 403 across one domain, on both product and category URLs, points to WAF or bot protection on the probe's user agent. It does not point to dead pages. I can't confirm real-user behavior from this data, so verify with a browser-based check.
- **Identical `utm_content=Promo` on every link.** It is the same on product links and category tiles, so link-level click attribution is impossible. Use per-slot values (hero, product-1 through product-4, tile-backinstock, tile-newarrivals).
- **20 tracking/redirect links were skipped** by the probe, so their resolution is unverified.
- **Mixed-case UTM values** (`utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`). GA4's default channel grouping matches `email` case-sensitively, so `Email` may land in "Unassigned". Lowercase values are safer.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images are missing alt text.** These are the product images whose filenames start with `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`, `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`, `43250800435170~x20125127.jpg` and `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`. They are hosted on `www.buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`. With images blocked, the product cards (Maleficent, Batty Kitty, Sanrio Cherry and Berry) have no readable label, and screen readers get nothing useful.
- **No plain-text part.** QA reports a "Text version is 0 chars". Check this against the raw MIME, because the relay may strip it.
- The hidden preheader, `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding and MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` are implemented correctly. The source is truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may not capture them. Check the raw headers of a direct send. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Verify with a seed-list or Gmail "Show original" check. The From domain is `ebuildabear.com`.
- The visible footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTMs are consistent and complete across links: `utm_campaign=BearmeyLaunch`, `utm_id=2802478`, `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261027`.
- **`utm_term` contains `Testing`.** This looks like a test-segment label, and it suggests a test or seed send. If this reached a live audience, the segment label is polluting analytics.
- **No campaign-specific landing page.** The email is named for the "Bearemy" launch, but the visible links go to product pages, `/new-arrivals` and `/back-in-stock`. I can't tell from the truncated HTML whether the Bearemy hero links anywhere. Check that its destination resolves.
- Landing page behavior couldn't be verified because of the 403s.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 6 links with a real browser or a whitelisted user agent. If they work, mark the 403s as a probe false positive. If they don't, fix the URLs.
2. Add alt text to the 4 product images (for example "Disney Villains Maleficent Dragon Plush").
3. Make `utm_content` unique per link slot, and lowercase `utm_source` and `utm_medium`.
4. Inspect raw headers from a direct send for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `ebuildabear.com`.
5. Confirm a multipart/alternative text part exists, and populate it if it doesn't.
6. Confirm the `Testing` value in `utm_term` is intentional, or correct it before the production send.
