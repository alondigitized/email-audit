# NEW Harry Potter™ House Mini Beans®!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear extending the Harry Potter 25th Anniversary push it kicked off with 2026-07-31's "Happee Birthdae Harry" send — same IP, new product tier (Mini Beans instead of full-size bears). The house-crest color-coding (red/green/blue/yellow borders matching Gryffindor/Slytherin/Ravenclaw/Hufflepuff) is a genuinely nice organizing device that makes the four-product wall easy to scan, and it's the strongest structural idea in this email compared to recent sends.
- But there's no discount, no price, no urgency, and no loyalty callout anywhere in the render — this is a pure browse-and-buy IP play, same as "Silly Goose" (2026-08-04) and "Berry Sweetest Cow Cuddles" (2026-08-02), both of which scored 7.00. Given this is the fifth-ish new-plush-no-discount email in under two weeks, and the second Harry Potter send in five days, cadence fatigue is a real risk here even though the execution itself is clean.
- Solid, on-brand, well-organized email that will satisfy existing HP/Build-A-Bear fans but does nothing to convert anyone on the fence — no price anchor, no reason to act today.

## What's working

- House-crest color coding (red/green/blue/gold borders) makes the four-product grid instantly scannable and reinforces the Hogwarts house conceit visually, not just in copy.
- Hero section ("CREATURES COME IN ALL SIZES") uses a warm gradient background and bold typography that clearly draws the eye first.
- Repeated, consistent "Shop Now" CTA styling across every module — no ambiguity about where to click.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the long scroll.
- "Most Loved Stuff This Week" module at the bottom gives a secondary browse path for anyone who scrolled past the HP content without converting.

## What's weak

- Zero price or offer anywhere in the email — no %, no $, no BOGO, no member pricing. Pure product-intro email with no purchase incentive.
- CTA copy is "Shop Now" nine times in a row with no variation — a missed chance to say "Shop Gryffindor Lion" etc. for clarity and CTR tracking.
- No urgency or time-boxing at all — nothing pushes a reader to act today versus next month.
- This is the second Harry Potter email in five days (following 2026-07-31's full-size bear launch) with no acknowledgment of the prior send — reads repetitive to anyone who opened both.
- No size, price, or availability info visible on any product tile, which is exactly the detail a plush shopper needs before clicking through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible price or bundle offer to the hero ("Mini Beans starting at $12" or "Buy 2, Save on the Set") — this is the single biggest lever missing from an otherwise well-organized layout.
- 2. Vary the CTA copy per house tile ("Shop Gryffindor Lion," "Shop Slytherin Snake") instead of four identical "Shop Now" buttons.
- 3. Add a light urgency cue tied to the 25th Anniversary campaign window (e.g., "Available for a limited time") since the anniversary framing already exists in the hero badge.
- 4. Cut or consolidate the two mid-email banner modules (House Pride Bear, Wand Wristie) — they dilute the Mini Beans focus the subject line promised.

- **Subject Alt B:** `NEW: Pocket-Sized Harry Potter™ Plush Have Arrived`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gryffindor, Slytherin, Ravenclaw & Hufflepuff — now in Mini Beans size`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Small enough to collect them all. Shop the new House lineup.`

# Technical Audit: Build-A-Bear "NEW Harry Potter™ House Mini Beans®" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional MSO-compatible template, but all four product CTAs return HTTP 403 on probe, and the message lacks List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers required for modern bulk-sender compliance.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4/4 "SHOP NOW" links return 403 (broken):**
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/chummy-shark-stuffed-animal/034604.html?...&utm_id=2785220`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/longhorn-cow-stuffed-animal/024867.html?...&utm_id=2785220`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/blueberry-highland-cow-stuffed-animal/034599.html?...&utm_id=2785220`
  - `https://www.buildabear.com/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html?...&utm_id=2785220`
  - Note: 403 on automated probe can indicate bot/WAF blocking rather than a truly dead page; recommend manual browser verification before escalating as a hard outage.
- **29 tracking/click-redirect links skipped** during HTTP probing (redirector domains not resolvable via direct request) — these are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- QA pass rate: 30% (4 failures, 3 warnings).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" Aontent="IE=edge" />` — attribute is misspelled (`Aontent` instead of `content`), so this meta tag has no effect in legacy IE/Outlook rendering.
- Malformed MSO conditional block: `<o:PixelsPerInch>96</o:PixelsPerInch></o:PixelsPerInch>` and `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>...</o:OfficeDocumentSettings></o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` both contain duplicated closing tags — invalid XML inside the `<!--[if gte mso 9]>` block, which can cause Outlook to ignore or misapply the DPI/office settings.
- 4 product images missing `alt` text (accessibility/screen-reader issue):
  - `47251351534604~x20612077.jpg`
  - `61231221224867x20152012.jpg`
  - `94250702034599~x20054083.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
- Minor: trailing space in `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0 " />` — harmless but non-standard.
- Preheader hiding technique (zwnj/nbsp spacer hack) and `.ExternalClass`/Gmail fixes are correctly implemented.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization variables, or dynamic content placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) are present in the visible HTML. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a true sender omission, but cannot be confirmed one-click-unsubscribe compliant (RFC 8058) as delivered.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Sender domain (`ebuildabear.com`) differs from the brand's primary domain (`buildabear.com`) — common ESP subdomain pattern, but worth confirming DKIM/SPF alignment for DMARC purposes given the missing Authentication-Results data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters are present and consistent across all 4 CTAs: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=HarryPotterNewMiniBeans&utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20260805&utm_content=Product&utm_id=2785220`.
- `utm_term` value contains `US-US_Testing_Hot-20260805`, which reads as an internal QA/test segment label — verify this isn't a test campaign parameter leaking into production send.
- **Landing page mismatch:** all 4 linked product pages (shark, longhorn cow, highland cow, teddy bear) are generic Build-A-Bear stuffed animals, not Harry Potter™ Mini Beans® products referenced in the subject line and campaign name (`HarryPotterNewMiniBeans`). Links do not route to the promoted product line.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `Aontent`→`content` typo on the X-UA-Compatible meta tag.
2. Clean up the duplicated closing tags in the MSO conditional block (`</o:PixelsPerInch>`, `</o:OfficeDocumentSettings>`).
3. Manually verify the 4 product URLs outside the automated probe (403 may be bot-detection, not a true 404/outage) — and confirm they should point to Harry Potter Mini Beans SKUs, not unrelated products.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 4 product images.
5. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually being sent (check at the ESP/MTA level, since the relay may simply not be capturing them) and validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment given the missing Authentication-Results data.
6. Confirm `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20260805` is intentional and not a test-segment leak into a live send.
