# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Build-A-Bear right now looks like a brand that has *something* exciting going on — I can see "Disney Stitch!" and "Limited Time Only: Bag Charms" teasing at the top — but I genuinely cannot tell what it is because a massive Cookiebot consent modal is plastered over the entire screen the moment I land. Fun brand, killer timing for a Stitch drop, completely buried under legal boilerplate. Not a great first impression when I'm in gift-shopping mode.

## What's working

- **Top marquee ticker** — "Disney Stitch!" and "Limited Time Only: Bag Charms 2..." peeks through above the modal. I immediately clocked "Stitch collab + limited edition" which is exactly my signal to stop scrolling and start reading.
- **Active Offers (5) — View All** bar at the bottom survives the modal takeover. Knowing there are 5 live promos waiting makes me want to dismiss and explore rather than bounce.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen Cookiebot modal** — the entire hero, featured products, and navigation are invisible. For a mobile shopper opening a product page on impulse, this is a hard stop.
- **Truncated ticker copy** — "Bag Charms 2" is cut off mid-sentence. Even the one element I *can* see is broken.
- **Zero rewards/loyalty visibility** — as a Bonus Club member I expect to see my status or a personalized hook; there's nothing here before the wall of cookie text.
- **No collab context** — I know Stitch collaborations exist, but whether this is a new season drop, a restock, or a Bag Charm accessory add-on is completely opaque from this screen.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 3–5 seconds or trigger on first scroll** — let me see the hero and the Stitch drop before hitting me with legal consent; European compliance doesn't require blocking the visual on load.
- **Make the ticker loop full copy** — "Disney Stitch! Limited Time Only: Bag Charms 2-for-$XX — Ends Sunday" should be the complete readable message, not a truncated tease.
- **Surface the rewards balance or a "Welcome back" module** above the fold once the modal is dismissed — I'm a logged-in member; show me my points or a member-exclusive offer immediately.
- **Swap the consent modal Accept button to brand blue with BAB branding** — at minimum, make the friction moment feel on-brand rather than generic SaaS gray.
