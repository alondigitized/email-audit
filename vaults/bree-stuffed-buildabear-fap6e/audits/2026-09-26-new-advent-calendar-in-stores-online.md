# NEW Advent Calendar in Stores & Online!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized holiday-launch email, and the hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: two real kids grinning at a red Advent calendar on a decorated Christmas tree. It's the first genuinely seasonal, on-brand hero I've seen in this run — no reliance on a random plush character to carry the message.
- The problem is what's missing everywhere else: there's no price on the Advent calendar itself, no countdown, no "only X left," nothing that manufactures urgency for what is otherwise a strong seasonal product. The email then sprawls into a Harry Potter cross-promo, four unrelated plush SKUs, and two generic "Back in Stock / New Arrivals" tiles — diluting a genuinely good hero into "yet another catalog blast." Compare to 2026-09-21's "FREE Shipping Today Only" (8/10), which stayed tightly focused on one offer; this one has a strong top and a long, unfocused tail.
- Verdict: good creative, no offer discipline. Ships fine as a launch announcement, but leaves real click-through on the table by not naming a price or a reason to buy today.

## What's working

- Hero image is warm, authentic, and seasonally on-point — real kids, real tree, real product in hand.
- Subject/headline match exactly ("NEW Advent Calendar" ↔ "OUR NEW ADVENT CALENDAR IS HERE!") — no bait-and-switch.
- Clear visual hierarchy: headline → hero image → single green "SHOP NOW" button, easy to scan.
- Secondary plush section ("Chip-mas" gingerbread bear, holiday frog) reinforces the festive theme rather than feeling random.
- No render bugs — clean layout, consistent button styling, footer intact.

## What's weak

- No price, no discount, no "starting at $X" anywhere near the Advent calendar hero — the single most clickable piece of info is absent.
- Harry Potter banner mid-email is a jarring detour that has nothing to do with the advent/holiday theme and competes with the hero for attention.
- Four unrelated "Give a Gift They'll Adore" plush tiles (goose, kitty, Sanrio, teddy) bloat the email into a generic catalog rather than a focused launch.
- "Back in Stock" / "New Arrivals" tiles at the bottom add zero specificity — no products, no offer, just navigation.
- No urgency signal (no "shop before it sells out," no date) despite Advent calendars being an inherently date-sensitive, limited-window product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price and stock/urgency cue directly under the hero CTA (e.g., "$29.99 — while supplies last") — this is the single biggest lever since Advent calendars sell out every year and urgency is completely absent.
- 2. Cut the Harry Potter banner and the "More to Explore" tiles entirely from this send — save them for a separate email; they dilute a genuinely strong hero.
- 3. Trim "Give a Gift They'll Adore" to 2 items max, or drop it — it's not thematically tied to the Advent calendar launch.
- 4. Add a countdown or date cue ("25 days of surprises starts [date]") to reinforce the calendar's daily-reveal mechanic and give a reason to act now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Advent Calendar Is Back — 25 Days of Bear-y Fun`
- **Subject Alt B:** `NEW: $29.99 Advent Calendar, In Stores & Online`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25 days of daily surprises — grab yours before the countdown starts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-requested holiday item is back for 2026.`
