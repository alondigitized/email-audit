# Boo! FREE Shipping on ALL Orders!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest, cleanest offer this brand has put in front of me in the recent run: "FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM" in giant type, a real code (SPOOKY), and a legible deadline in the footer (order by 11:59 p.m. CST 9/29). Compare to the 9/21 send ("FREE Shipping when you spend $20 or more") — this one drops the minimum entirely, which is a genuine upgrade and a real reason to click today rather than skip like I did on the last three plush-launch emails.
- Where it stalls is everything below the hero. The offer is unambiguous, but there's no visible discount tied to any specific product — it's free shipping plus a generic Halloween product grid (skeleton monkey, purple kitty, Toothless, a goose in a pumpkin costume). Nothing in the body reinforces "spooky" beyond the skeleton and witch outfits; the DreamWorks Dragons and goose blocks feel bolted on rather than part of one campaign. Solid top, generic middle — a 6, not higher, because the hero doesn't get reinforced anywhere else in the email.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises: "FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM" with a real, unambiguous code (SPOOKY).
- Halloween theming (spiderweb, red spider, orange/purple palette) is consistent and on-season for a 9/28 send.
- Single clear "SHOP NOW" CTA button in the hero, high contrast, easy to spot.
- Product grid below the fold has clean 2x2 layout with visible product names and repeated CTAs — no render bugs.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- No price or percentage discount anywhere — this is a shipping-only offer, which is real but lower-value than the 9/23 "$15 Furry Friends" or 9/20 "POKEMON30" sends.
- The four Halloween skeleton/witch products are the only items visually tied to the "spooky" hero; the Toothless and goose-in-pumpkin blocks don't reinforce the code or the free-shipping message at all.
- Hero doesn't restate the deadline — "no minimum" is great but a shopper has to scroll to the tiny footer legal text to learn the offer expires tomorrow (9/29), so the urgency isn't felt until it's almost too late.
- "Get Silly This Halloween!" banner has no discount or urgency, just diluting the one offer that's actually good.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 11:59 p.m. 9/29 deadline directly under the "SHOP NOW" button in the hero — right now real urgency is buried in 6pt footer legal copy that most people never read.
- 2. Tie the SPOOKY code visually to at least one of the product tiles (e.g., a "+ free shipping" badge on the skeleton/witch sets) so the offer and the product feel like one campaign, the way POKEMON30 did on 9/20.
- 3. Cut or move the Toothless and goose banners below "More to Explore" — they're unrelated to the free-shipping hook and crowd out the Halloween-specific products that actually match the offer.
- 4. Add a small "Ends tomorrow" ribbon to the hero image itself so urgency survives on mobile even if someone doesn't read past the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Shipping, No Minimum — Ends Tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Boo! Code SPOOKY = Free Shipping, No Catch`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No minimum, no tricks — just use SPOOKY at checkout by 11:59pm CST tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skeleton bears, a witchy kitty, and free shipping on everything.`
