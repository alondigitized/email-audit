# GIANT Chococat™ Back in Stock!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built product-restock email. The subject delivers a specific product and a scarcity cue, and the hero matches it: a smiling teen hugging a Giant Chococat in a blue-sky frame. The hierarchy is good. The eye goes from the hero to "CHOOSE YOUR CHOCOCAT" to the purple SHOP NOW button, then down three stacked size tiles.
- It's a pure product push with no offer, no price and no deadline. Compared with the 9/28 "FREE Shipping, no minimum" send (7/10) and the 9/23 "$15 Furry Friends" send (7/10), there's nothing here to act on today. The "back in stock" urgency is also never backed up in the body. The email doesn't say which sizes are low, and it gives no prices.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand licensed-character drop. It is a fan-service email, not a conversion email, and a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The hero is a real person hugging the plush, with the Giant sized against the mini. This sells the scale that the subject promises.
- The "CHOOSE YOUR CHOCOCAT" headline and logo lockup are bold and legible. The copy explains the three sizes and the "trio" collecting hook in two lines.
- The three stacked tiles ("Shop Giant," "Shop Chococat," the Mini Beans tile) are easy to scan and each has a distinct color block. The Mini Beans tile also adds a cross-brand "Hello Kitty and Friends" hook.
- There are no render bugs. The images load, the text doesn't overlap, and the layout is tidy.
- The footer is tidy: social icons, a clear unsubscribe link, and the fine print is not buried.

## What's weak

- There's no price, offer, or deadline anywhere. The restock framing creates no reason to click now rather than later.
- The subject says "Back in Stock!", but the body never mentions stock or availability. Nothing says "limited" or "selling fast."
- The size tiles don't show prices or dimensions. "Giant," the regular size, and Mini Beans can't be told apart on value, and a buyer can't pick one without clicking through.
- The two purple buttons ("SHOP NOW" and "SHOP COLLECTION") are generic and nearly identical in function. The tile labels are the only specific CTAs.
- The Sanrio licensing line under the first CTA is tiny and almost unreadable. It's a small visual wart.
- This is the sixth send in ten days (the Kitsune Fox, the $15 Furry Friends, the Gargoyle, the Advent Calendar, the SPOOKY free shipping and the Halloween Cuties), so the cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price on the tiles, for example "Giant Chococat — $XX" and "Mini Beans — $XX." A restock should show what the buyer is restocking.
- 2. Back up "back in stock" in the body. Add a line like "Giants sold out fast last time — grab yours" or a live stock cue.
- 3. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, such as "Get the Giant Chococat" and "Shop all three sizes."
- 4. Add one reason to click now: a deadline, free shipping, or an early-access window for Rewards members.
- 5. Make the Sanrio licensing line legible, or move it to the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Chococat™ is back in 3 sizes 🖤`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Giant, classic, or Mini Beans — shop all three sizes before they sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complete your Chococat trio. Prices and sizes inside.`
