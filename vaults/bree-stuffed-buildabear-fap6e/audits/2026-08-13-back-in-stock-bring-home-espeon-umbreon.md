# BACK IN STOCK! Bring Home Espeon & Umbreon
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Build-A-Bear's Pokémon franchise firing again — Espeon and Umbreon (Eevee evolutions) back in stock, riding the same licensed-IP playbook it ran with Harry Potter on 2026-08-05. The hero creative is genuinely strong: two well-rendered plush characters in a moody pink/purple gradient with sparkle effects, clean "EXPAND YOUR EEVEE EVOLUTIONS LINEUP" headline, and an unambiguous yellow SHOP NOW button. It's the best-composed hero of the recent run — better than the Halloween or NFL sends.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email pivots hard into unrelated inventory — Hello Kitty plush, cow stuffed animals, a beach-themed collection — none of which has anything to do with Pokémon or the back-in-stock hook that got someone to open. It reads like two separate emails stapled together: a tight Pokémon promo followed by a generic clearance grid.
- Verdict: strong hook, weak follow-through. The subject and hero would pull a Pokémon collector in; the body loses them by the third scroll.

## What's working

- Hero art is clean and on-brand — Espeon and Umbreon are clearly rendered with no artifacts, on a Pokémon-appropriate gradient background.
- "EXPAND YOUR EEVEE EVOLUTIONS LINEUP" headline directly ties to the subject line's back-in-stock promise.
- Single, high-contrast yellow CTA button ("SHOP NOW") sits right under the offer with no scroll needed.
- A discount code (POKEMON30) is visible in the top banner, giving a concrete reason to click beyond restock news.

## What's weak

- The "Shop By Product" and "More to Explore" sections (Hello Kitty, cow plush, beach set) are completely disconnected from the Pokémon hook — this dilutes the email's focus and buries the actual offer under generic merchandising.
- The banner code (POKEMON30) and the hero CTA aren't tied together visually — it's unclear if "SHOP NOW" in the hero applies the 30% code or just links to the product page.
- No price or specific in-stock/limited-quantity urgency is shown for Espeon/Umbreon themselves — "back for more Pokémon fun" is soft, not concrete.
- Fine print at the bottom notes the event is "online only" and excludes Eevee, Pikachu, Espeon, or Umbreon from the code — meaning the 30%-off banner doesn't actually apply to the products being promoted, which is a bait-and-switch red flag once you read closely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Hello Kitty/cow/beach sections from this send entirely, or replace them with more Pokémon lineup (Pikachu, Jolteon, Vaporeon) to keep the whole email on-theme — right now it dilutes a strong hook.
- 2. Fix the offer clarity: if POKEMON30 excludes Espeon/Umbreon, don't put the code banner directly above their hero image — it implies the discount applies when it doesn't. Either apply the code to these two or replace the top banner with restock urgency copy instead.
- 3. Add a concrete stock cue ("limited restock," "selling fast") near the hero CTA to convert restock urgency into click urgency.
- 4. Tighten "Shop By Product" down to one row max, or move it to a separate send — this email should be a single-offer Pokémon restock alert, not a full catalog blast.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: save 30% on select Pokémon gear with code POKEMON30`
