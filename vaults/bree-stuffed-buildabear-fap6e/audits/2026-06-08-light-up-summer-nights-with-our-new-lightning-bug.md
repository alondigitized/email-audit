# Light Up Summer Nights With Our New Lightning Bug!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- Build-A-Bear is launching another original summer character — a glow-in-the-dark Lightning Bug — using the same new-character playbook that's delivered consistent scores all week. The hero execution is genuinely strong: dark blue "summer nights" backdrop, a black plush bug in shades, the "HEARTS AGLOW" wordplay (emotional warmth + actual glow), and a clean "SHOP NOW" anchor. The dual meaning in the headline is the best copy line in this email.
- The email stumbles below the fold. The "Feel the Sand Between Your Paws / Adopt a Pet" module shows what appear to be real dogs on a beach — not plush — which breaks the brand frame and creates real confusion about what's being sold. The "Most Loved Stuff This Week" grid recirculates Chococat (the June 6 hero) without acknowledging it's already been featured, which is fine cross-sell behavior but is wasted space when there's no discount hook pulling any of it.
- No pricing anywhere. No promo code. No urgency. This email is doing pure character awareness work, and it does it well enough — but it doesn't close. Compared to Chummy Shark (9/10, June 5), which had near-identical structure and also lacked a discount, the Lightning Bug hero lands a notch below because the "glow-in-the-dark" product feature is visually asserted (badge) but never cinematically demonstrated the way Chummy's ocean backdrop sold the character's world.

## What's working

- **"HEARTS AGLOW" headline** — double meaning (emotional + literal glow) is the sharpest copy line in this send and earns the summer-nights frame.
- **Hero staging** — dark backdrop + sunglasses on the bug + "I Glow in the Dark!" badge communicates the product's key differentiator at a glance, no reading required.
- **Chococat in the cross-sell grid** — recirculating last week's launch in "Most Loved Stuff This Week" is smart inventory management and gives the June 6 send longer tail.
- **Subject line** — specific, seasonally relevant, exactly 50 characters, zero spam signals. Does what a new-character subject should do.

## What's weak

- **"Adopt a Pet" module is brand-breaking** — real-looking dogs on a beach with "ADOPT A PET" reads like a SPCA partnership dropped into a plush toy email. The intent may be their animal plush line, but the execution creates genuine confusion about what you're clicking to.
- **No pricing anywhere** — not even a "starting at $XX" anchor. Every "SHOP NOW" is a leap of faith. This email has five separate CTAs and not one tells you what anything costs.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA copy is generic across every module** — the hero, the mystery plush, the Adopt a Pet section all use the same verb. CTA differentiation would help click targeting.
- **Glow feature is claimed, not shown** — the "I Glow in the Dark!" badge tells you, but the hero image doesn't show the product actually glowing. A side-by-side or glow-state image would have sold the feature instead of just labeling it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show the glow.** Replace the static hero with a before/after or split panel — lights on vs. lights off. The entire subject line promises a light-up experience; the hero image should deliver it. This is the single change that lifts the email most.
- 2. **Kill or reframe the "Adopt a Pet" module.** Either relabel it clearly ("Build Your Own Dog Friend" with a plush image front-and-center) or remove it. As rendered, it undermines the email's coherence.
- 3. **Add a starting price anchor to the hero CTA** — "SHOP NOW · from $XX" removes the biggest barrier to clicking.
- 4. **Differentiate CTA copy per module** — "Meet the Bug," "Unwrap the Mystery," "Build a Pup" give each section a distinct identity and reduce CTA blindness.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the Bug That Glows in the Dark`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: Lightning Bug — the one that actually glows`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glow in the dark + summer nights energy — our newest character is here`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hearts Aglow. The Lightning Bug is ready to light up your collection.`
