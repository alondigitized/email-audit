# Boo! FREE Shipping TODAY ONLY!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Build-A-Bear send in the recent run with a real offer, and the hero sells it well. "TODAY ONLY: FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM!" is set in huge navy type on orange, with a spider-web Halloween frame. The code SPOOKY and a SHOP NOW button sit right under it. The eye lands on the offer first, which the Oct 11–18 sends (Werewolf, Advent, Llama, Grinch) never managed.
- The send gets weaker below the hero. Harry Potter, Mellow Moodies, four product tiles and a two-card "More to Explore" block all compete for attention. None of them connects to free shipping. Halloween is only a theme in the hero, since the other blocks are a Harry Potter promo and the Mellow Moodies launch. The "Best Sellers" card is a plain yellow tile with no product in it. Two garbled characters also show in the product names, "Sanrio® Starry Sky Hello Kitty®".
- Judgement: a stronger send than the recent catalog emails because it has a real, time-boxed offer. It is still diluted by catalog sprawl and a few small flaws. I'd score it higher than the 5–6 range of the prior sends.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable. "FREE SHIPPING, NO MINIMUM!" is the biggest text in the email.
- The code SPOOKY is shown in bold, and the CTA button sits directly under it.
- "TODAY ONLY" gives credible urgency, and the footer states a 11:59 p.m. PT deadline.
- The spider-web and red spider art tie the offer to Halloween without hurting legibility.
- "No minimum" removes the usual free-shipping objection.

## What's weak

- Every CTA below the hero says "SHOP NOW" with no noun. Even the Harry Potter button, which is more specific, doesn't mention shipping or the offer.
- The offer isn't repeated anywhere below the hero. A reader who scrolls past the hero sees an ordinary catalog.
- The product names show garbled characters, "Sanrio® Starry Sky Hello Kitty®", which looks sloppy.
- The "Best Sellers" tile is a flat yellow block with no product shown. It is a weak visual next to the Halloween scene beside it.
- The Mellow Moodies and Harry Potter blocks are large and unrelated to the shipping promo. They push the Halloween products down the page.
- The footer fine print is tiny. It notes the code can't be combined with any other offer and excludes gift cards and giant furry friends, which is a lot of exclusions for a "no minimum" headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle blocks. Keep the hero and one row of Halloween products with a "Free shipping ends tonight" banner above the grid. That keeps the email about the offer.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific and repeat the offer, for example "Shop Halloween Plush — Ships Free".
- 3. Fix the encoding bug in the product names.
- 4. Replace the empty "Best Sellers" tile with a product photo.
- 5. Put the key exclusions in one line near the hero instead of leaving them in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight — code SPOOKY`
- **Subject Alt B:** `No minimum: free shipping today only 🎃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SPOOKY at checkout. Ends 11:59 p.m. PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Halloween plush, Harry Potter bears and more ship free today.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML structure is sound (XHTML doctype, MSO conditionals, a preheader, responsive media queries). The QA run found four links that returned 403 to the probe, no plain-text part, and alt text missing on four product images. Several header-based checks could not be confirmed because the relay doesn't capture those headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Four "SHOP NOW" links returned HTTP 403 to the automated probe:
  - `buildabear.com/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `buildabear.com/sanrio-starry-sky-hello-kitty-plush/035171.html`
  - `buildabear.com/fresno-nightcrawler-posable-plush/034979.html`
  - `buildabear.com/batty-kitty-stuffed-animal/035255.html`
- A 403 on a product page usually means bot or WAF blocking of the probe, not a dead page. This is **unconfirmed** as a real defect. Re-test with a browser-like user agent or a real browser before treating it as broken.
- The QA output shows empty link-text fields (`Broken link (403):  (link text: "SHOP NOW")`). This is a QA reporting quirk, not an email defect.
- 23 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped, so their destinations are unverified.
- The product URLs carry a consistent UTM set: `utm_source=Campaign`, `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=FreeShip`, `utm_content=Promo`, `utm_id=2801013`.
- `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261019` contains `Testing`. This looks like a test or QA segment label that was left in a production send. It will pollute analytics segmentation. It's also a duplicated `US-US` prefix, which suggests a template-variable concatenation error.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Four product images have no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA):
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `98250800435171~x20127120.jpg`
  - `08250701634979~x20230105.jpg`
  - `16250801735255~x20139125.jpg`
  - These images are the links' only visible content (each sits next to a "SHOP NOW" link). With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the product is unidentified.
- `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` is declared, but no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules were visible in the truncated source. Without them, clients that apply dark mode will auto-invert colors. This is **unconfirmed** because the source is truncated.
- The `<title>` is the internal template name, "Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A". It is exposed in some web-view and accessibility contexts.
- The `<font class="">` element in the preheader is deprecated markup. It is harmless.
- The `.gmailfix` rule and the `u+.em_body` Gmail hacks are present, which is correct.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) were visible in the truncated source. The rest of the body was not available to check.

**5. Compliance**
- **No plain-text alternative (FAIL):** QA reports the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and it is a spam-filter signal.
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. **Unconfirmed:** the sender may well include them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message headers.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the relay, not necessarily the sender.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so I can't confirm them.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters are present and consistent across the four product links.
- Product URLs go directly to PDPs. The subject says "FREE Shipping TODAY ONLY!" and the preheader says "Use Code SPOOKY at Checkout!". I can't verify from the QA data that the landing pages show the code or the free-shipping banner. That would need a landing-page check.
- The `utm_term` issue described in section 2 also applies here.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add a plain-text MIME part. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images.
3. Replace `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261019` with the production segment value, and fix the duplicated `US-US`.
4. Re-probe the four 403 URLs with a browser user agent or a headless browser to separate bot blocking from real breakage.
5. Check the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Add `prefers-color-scheme` rules, or remove the `color-scheme` meta tag if dark mode isn't supported.
7. Replace the `<title>` with a meaningful subject-aligned string.
8. Confirm the SPOOKY code and the free-shipping offer appear on the landing pages.
