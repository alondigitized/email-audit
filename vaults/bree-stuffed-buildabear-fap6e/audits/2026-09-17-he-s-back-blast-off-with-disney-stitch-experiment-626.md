# He’s Back! Blast Off With Disney Stitch Experiment 626!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the same structural problem that's dogged every recent Build-A-Bear send: a genuinely strong hero — Stitch Experiment 626, a real licensed character with built-in nostalgia and fandom — buried inside a stack of five unrelated modules (Silly Goose ducks, Maleficent Dragon, Hello Kitty, Batty Kitty, Highland Cow, Mini Beans) with zero discount, zero urgency, and zero reason to click today versus next week.
- The hero itself is well-executed: clean copy, a clear plush shot, a visible SHOP NOW button, and the Stitch logo for brand recognition. But "Blast Off" promises event energy that the email doesn't deliver — there's no date, no launch framing, no "new" badge, nothing that says why today matters. It's a product photo with a slogan, not news.
- Compared to 2026-09-09's "2 for $25 Lil' Cubs" (8.0) or 2026-09-07's free shipping send (7.0), this reverts to the catalog-dump pattern that scored 4-5 all last week. Character IP alone isn't enough to lift it out of that band — judgement: mid-pack, roughly a 5.

## What's working

- Stitch Experiment 626 hero is instantly recognizable and well-lit against a clean gradient background.
- Subject line and hero headline are consistent ("Blast Off With Experiment 626") — no mismatch.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is visible without scrolling and uses good button contrast (purple on light background).
- "Shop By Product" grid gives clear individual CTAs per item, each with product name and price-free simplicity.

## What's weak

- No discount, no price, no urgency anywhere in the email — every module is "browse and maybe buy."
- Five+ unrelated modules (Stitch, ducks, Maleficent, Hello Kitty, Batty Kitty, cow, Mini Beans) dilute the one genuinely newsworthy item into just another catalog tile.
- "Silly Goose on the Loose" and "Villainous Side" modules have no connective tissue to the Stitch theme promised in the subject — a reader who opened for Stitch hits unrelated products almost immediately.
- No visible "New" or "Just Dropped" badge on the Stitch hero itself, despite "He's Back!" in the subject implying a re-release event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to Stitch + one complementary "Disney" cross-sell (Villains Maleficent could work if reframed as "more Disney faves") — drop Silly Goose, Hello Kitty, Batty Kitty, cow, and Mini Beans into a separate send. One coherent theme beats six fragments.
- 2. Add urgency to the hero: a release-window callout ("Available for a limited time") or a pre-order/restock framing — "He's Back!" in the subject implies scarcity that the body never pays off.
- 3. Add a "New" or "Back in Stock" badge directly on the Stitch product image so the visual hierarchy matches the subject's promise.
- 4. Give the hero a incentive lever even if small — free shipping threshold or a bundle price with the frog plush shown in the image ("Stitch + Scrump, together").
