# 30% OFF Online: Early Black Friday Starts NOW!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clear Early Black Friday send. A giant red "30% OFF" over a plush penguin in plaid is the first thing the eye lands on, with a green SHOP NOW button right beside it. The offer reads at a glance, and the "online only, limited time" framing gives it real urgency. Compared with the licensed-franchise launches from Nov 12–20 (Donkey Kong, Hello Kitty × Swarovski), this is the brand's first true price-led send of the run, and it is the right kind of escalation for Black Friday week.
- The execution gets muddier below the hero. There are two stacked promos (30% off furry friends, plus a clothing and accessories sale), a free-shipping banner, a delivery-deadline banner, four product tiles and two explore tiles. That is about 11 SHOP NOW buttons in one email. The eye has nowhere to rest, and the "select furry friends" wording hides which plush are actually discounted. The product grid shows no prices or sale markers, so the 30% never connects to a specific item.
- Overall it is a solid, honest promo that wins on hero clarity and loses on focus and specificity. I'd call it a good send that is not yet a great one.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" numeral is huge and unmissable, with "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY" and "ONLINE ONLY FOR A LIMITED TIME" framing it.
- The green SHOP NOW sits right next to the offer, so the CTA is unambiguous.
- The "Make Sure Gifts Arrive on Time!" banner gives credible deadlines (Hanukkah by Nov. 21, Christmas by Dec. 14).
- The product grid features recognizable, giftable items (Hello Kitty, Grinch, Teddy Bear) with clear names.
- The free-shipping bar at the top ($100 or more) adds a second value hook without competing with the hero.
- The "Start Earning Points!" pill in the corner nods to loyalty, though it is small.

## What's weak

- Roughly 11 identical SHOP NOW buttons in three colors (green, pink, blue) dilute the hierarchy and make the email feel like a wall of buttons.
- "Select furry friends" is vague, and none of the four product tiles shows a sale price, a 30%-off badge or the original price.
- The free-shipping banner, the 30% offer and the clothing sale all compete for the top slot.
- The two "More to Explore" tiles are weak. One is a generic Hello Kitty collection card and the other is a lifestyle photo, and neither says what the button leads to.
- The footer fine print is dense and tiny, and it carries different end dates (Nov 25 for the 30% off, Nov 28 for clothing). The body copy gives no deadline for the offer.
- The loyalty pill is easy to miss and gives no member-specific benefit.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name a deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Nov. 25," and badge each product tile with "30% off" and a sale price. This single change would lift the email most, because it connects the offer to products and adds credible urgency.
- 2. Cut the SHOP NOW count to about 4–5. Use one button color, drop the duplicate "Shop By Product" and "Explore" buttons, and put product names or prices on the product buttons.
- 3. Replace "select furry friends" with a specific list or a "shop all 30% off" destination. Show only the discounted plush in the grid.
- 4. Fold the free-shipping bar into one line under the hero, or move it below the offer.
- 5. Make the loyalty hook concrete, for example "Members earn 2x points this week."
- 6. Consolidate the two sale end dates into one clear line above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off plush ends Nov. 25 — Early Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early Black Friday: 30% off Build-A-Bear, online only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hello Kitty, Grinch & more plush are 30% off through Nov. 25 + free shipping on $100.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Dec. 14 for Christmas delivery. Clothing & accessories on sale too.`

# Technical Audit: Build-A-Bear "30% OFF Online: Early Black Friday Starts NOW!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and responsive media queries. The four 403 results are probably bot-blocking by the probe, but the template has real gaps: no `lang` attribute, a leaked internal `<title>`, and four product images with missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 product CTAs returned 403 to the QA probe** (all "SHOP NOW"):
  - `/sanrio-hello-kitty-and-friends-my-sweet-piano-plush/033927.html`
  - `/grinch-plush---includes-extra-large-heart/035437.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
  - `/sanrio-frosted-hello-kitty-plush/035433.html`
- **Likely cause:** a 403 from a Demandware/SFCC storefront to an automated client usually means WAF or bot protection, not a dead page. All four returned the same status on the same host, which fits that pattern. A real 404 or 410 would be more likely to vary. This is unconfirmed, so re-test with a browser-grade client before treating these as broken.
- **24 tracking/redirect links were skipped** (HTTP probe not run). Their destinations are unverified, so the 4 failures above are the only link results.
- **Product-page URLs contain the `---` triple-hyphen slug** (`grinch-plush---includes-extra-large-heart`). It resolves per the markup, but it's a fragile slug if the merchandising team renames the product.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 product images have no alt text:**
  - `91240802233927~x20025114.jpg`
  - `40260801135437~x20117024.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
  - `11260801135433~x20114021.jpg`
  
  Each sits inside a "SHOP NOW" link. With images blocked, a screen reader or text-only client has no description of the product being linked.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`** (`<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>`). Screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **`<title>` is `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.** It is an internal template identifier. It shows in web-view and some client tab titles, and it's unhelpful as an accessible page title.
- **Preheader markup is fine**, with hidden preview text followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` / `&shy;` padding block, both with `display:none`.
- **`role="presentation"` on layout tables could not be verified**, because the HTML source is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the retrievable portion. The rest of the body was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. It is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this needs checking against raw headers from a direct-to-inbox send.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown for the `ebuildabear.com` sender. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address** are in the truncated portion of the source and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs are consistently applied** to all four product links: `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=30OffFurryFriends&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2806932`.
- **`utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261121`** looks like a test-segment label ("Testing", "Hot"). If this is the production send, the term would put traffic into the wrong analytics bucket. If it's a seed or test send, it's expected.
- **`utm_content=Promo` is identical on all four links.** It doesn't separate link position or product, so click-level attribution between the four tiles is lost.
- **Landing-page alignment can't be fully verified**, since the 403s block checking that each product page shows the 30% discount.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the four 403 product URLs** with a real browser or a user-agent allowlisted by the WAF. Confirm they load before treating them as broken.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the four product images (for example, "Timeless Teddy Bear plush").
3. **Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`**, and replace the `<title>` with the subject line or a human-readable title.
4. **Capture raw headers from a direct send** (not through the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. **Vary `utm_content` per link** (for example `tile1-hello-kitty-piano`) to get per-tile attribution.
6. **Check whether `utm_term=…Testing_Hot…`** is a test artifact before relying on this send's analytics.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** The truncated source leaves the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and token checks unverified.
