# Homepage snapshot · www.buildabear.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I see when I land on buildabear.com is a giant cookie consent wall — like, the Cookiebot modal is eating the entire screen. The only actual brand content I can make out is a thin promo strip at the top ("Limited Time Only: Bag Charms 2 for $30") and a sticky bar at the bottom that teases "Active Offers (4)." As a rewards member who came here specifically to check for Pokémon restocks or seasonal exclusives, this is a frustrating first impression — I can't see anything until I click through the legal wall.

## What's working

- **Promo strip at the very top** — "Limited Time Only: Bag Charms 2 for $30" manages to peek above the modal, so even through the cookie wall I can see there's a live offer running. That's something.
- **"Active Offers (4) View All" sticky bar at the bottom** — this is actually smart placement. Even with the modal up, I can see there are four active offers waiting for me. That's a decent enough reason not to immediately bounce once I dismiss the cookie wall.
- **Clean "Accept" button design** — the blue filled button is obvious and doesn't try to dark-pattern me into accepting by hiding a "reject" option (I can see "Manage My Preferences" as an equally visible alternative).

## What's weak

- **The Cookiebot modal is the entire homepage experience right now.** As someone who opens this site on my phone regularly to check for Pokémon drops or Christmas bear restocks, I should not be hitting a wall of legal text the moment the page loads. Returning members shouldn't see this every visit if the preference was already set.
- **Zero brand identity visible above the fold.** No Build-A-Bear logo (the Cookiebot logo is what's most prominent), no seasonal imagery, no hint of whether there's a collab running. If I didn't know the site already, I might wonder if I landed in the right place.
- **No rewards/member recognition.** I'm a Bonus Club member. There's not even a ghost of a "Welcome back" or loyalty hook anywhere visible on this first screen.
- **"Active Offers (4)" is a tease with no payoff yet.** I can see the count but I can't act on it until I dismiss the modal — and on mobile, that bottom bar and the modal compete for my thumb.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for returning visitors who've already consented.** Build-A-Bear should be persisting cookie preferences so repeat visitors — especially logged-in Bonus Club members — aren't greeted with a legal wall on every session.
- **Pin the brand logo above the modal** (or use a lighter-weight cookie notice banner at the bottom instead of a full overlay) so the hero imagery and seasonal campaign content are still partially visible behind a translucent layer. Modal-free browsing = faster path to "oooh, is that a Pikachu?"
- **Promote the "Active Offers (4)" bar more aggressively** — once the modal dismisses, this should expand or auto-scroll into a visible promo rail immediately, not stay as a collapsed sticky strip.
- **Make the first post-modal screen rewards-aware** — if I'm logged in, greet me with my Bonus Club points balance or a "members-only" badge on whatever the current limited drop is. That's the hook that keeps collectors like me returning every week.
