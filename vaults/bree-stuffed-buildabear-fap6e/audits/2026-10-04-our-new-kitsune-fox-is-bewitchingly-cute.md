# Our NEW Kitsune Fox Is Bewitchingly Cute!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean product-launch email with a strong hero and no offer at all. The purple Kitsune Fox sits on a lavender pattern under "NINE TIMES THE MAGIC," with a small inset showing the nine tails. The copy explains the fox's folklore origin, so the product is easy to understand. The hero is the best part of the send.
- Below the hero the email splits its attention. A Día de los Muertos block, a Cinnamoroll tie-in, a four-product grid and two unlabeled lifestyle tiles all compete with the fox. The subject promises one product, and the body turns into a general Halloween catalog. There is no price, no deadline and no member benefit anywhere. For a recipient looking for a deal, nothing says why to click today.
- Against the recent run it's a typical launch send. It matches the 10/02 "New Drop Next Thursday" teaser and the 9/24 Gargoyle launch, which also had no offer. It lacks the concrete hook of the 9/28 "FREE Shipping" send, which scored best in this brand history. I'd call it a solid 5: attractive and trustworthy, but unfocused and offer-free.

## What's working

- The Kitsune Fox hero matches the subject. It has a big product shot, a bold headline and a "SHOP NOW" button right below it.
- The nine-tails inset and the folklore line give the product a story, which suits a collector.
- The Día de los Muertos and Cinnamoroll blocks are visually rich and seasonal, which fits early October.
- The product grid has consistent red "SHOP NOW" buttons and clear product names.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No price, offer, deadline or member perk appears anywhere in the email.
- The fox is the subject, but it gets only one CTA. Four more themes follow it, and the Día de los Muertos and Cinnamoroll blocks carry almost as much weight.
- The "More to Explore" tiles have no labels, so "SHOP NOW" doesn't say what it leads to.
- The Shop By Product grid (Nightcrawler, goose, kitty, ram) has no relation to the fox, so it feels like filler.
- Four different button styles (white, red, teal, navy) make it unclear which is primary.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email about the fox alone. Add a price, availability ("in stores and online"), and a second view or a bundle with an outfit. Cut the grid or move it well below.
- 2. Add a reason to act now, such as a limited run, a sell-out warning or early access for members.
- 3. Change the hero button from "SHOP NOW" to "Meet the Kitsune Fox."
- 4. Label the "More to Explore" tiles, and use one button color and style throughout.
