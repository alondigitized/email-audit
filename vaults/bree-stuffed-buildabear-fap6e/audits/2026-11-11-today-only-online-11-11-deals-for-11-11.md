# Today Only Online: $11.11 Deals for 11/11!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-theme Singles' Day flash send. The red ornament hero reads "$11.11 DEALS FOR 11/11!" with "one-day-only deals online now." The date gimmick is clever, and the urgency is real. It's a better hook than the Nov 8 "Don't Miss 30% OFF" reminder, which was a reminder with no new idea.
- The email never says what the deals are, though. "$11.11 deals" could mean $11.11 prices or a general sale, and nothing below the hero resolves it. The product grid shows four plush items with no prices, no markdown and no savings. The fine print says "Prices as marked," so the reader has to click to find out. The Hanukkah and Christmas shipping banner is useful but pulls attention away from the one-day offer. The last third (More to Explore, the footer links) adds weight without adding a reason to act today.
- Judgement: good concept, thin execution. It's a decent 6 on the strength of the hook and clean render. Putting one real price on the page would take it to 8.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and festive, and the "$11.11 DEALS FOR 11/11!" headline is readable at a glance.
- "Happy Singles' Day… one-day-only" gives a credible, time-bounded reason to act.
- The "Make Sure Gifts Arrive on Time!" banner with specific dates (Hanukkah Nov. 21, Christmas Dec. 14) adds practical seasonal urgency.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images or overlaps, and the product grid is tidy with consistent SHOP NOW buttons.
- The ornament motif carries from the hero into the "holiday stuff you love" tile, which keeps the look consistent.

## What's weak

- No price or discount is visible anywhere. "$11.11 deals" is never tied to a product, so the offer stays abstract.
- The four product tiles have no prices, no "was/now" and no $11.11 badge, so nothing shows they are today's deals.
- There are 7+ SHOP NOW buttons with identical copy. The CTAs compete, and none names a product or deal.
- Two white and blue bear photos sit between the hero and the shipping banner with no caption or link context. They look like filler.
- The Grinch tile in "More to Explore" has no copy and is off-theme for a Singles' Day sale.
- The fine print (online only, exclusions apply, can't be combined) is buried at the bottom, so the exclusions only show up after the hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deal in the hero, for example "Plush from $11.11" or "$11.11 Axolotl Sleeper Set," and put price badges on each product tile.
- 2. Replace the generic bear pair with the real deal items, or remove it. The grid should be the deals.
- 3. Add a countdown or "ends 11:59 PM CST tonight" line directly under the hero. It is currently only in the fine print.
- 4. Give each button product-specific copy ("Get the Axolotl Set — $11.11") and cut the Grinch and ornament tiles.
- 5. Move the shipping-deadline banner below the product grid, so the offer comes first.

- **Subject Alt B:** `11/11 only: $11.11 Build-A-Bear deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Axolotls, bunnies and more at one-day prices. Ends 11:59 PM CST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Singles' Day flash sale ends tonight. See today's $11.11 picks.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound, with the preheader hack, MSO conditionals and responsive media queries all in place. Four product CTAs failed the QA probe with HTTP 403, four product images have no alt text, and a test-segment value appears in the production UTM parameters. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 CTA links returned 403** (all "SHOP NOW", all on `www.buildabear.com`):
  - `/axolotl-stuffed-animal-pj-sleeper-gift-set/29486_31016.html`
  - `/gingerbread-axolotl-stuffed-animal/034239.html`
  - `/pawlette-bunny-plush-party-gift-set/33639_32815_20389.html`
  - `/axolotl-stuffed-animal-pj-sleeper-gift-set/29486_31016_23681_23681.html`
- A 403 (rather than 404) on a Demandware storefront usually means bot or WAF blocking of the QA probe. It does not show that the product pages are dead. Re-test with a browser user-agent before treating these as broken. The 4 failures are the only links the probe actually checked, so the 30% pass rate rests on very few data points.
- **22 tracking/redirect links were skipped** (HTTP probe not run), so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- Two of the four failing URLs share the base SKU `29486_31016`. One is the base SKU and the other is the longer `29486_31016_23681_23681` variant, which repeats `23681`. That may be a duplicated item ID in the SKU string, so check the product feed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 product images**, all served from `buildabear.com/dw/image/v2/BBNG_PRD/...`:
  - `16231321229486_31016x20958016.jpg`
  - `96250802134239~x20328030.jpg`
  - `94231331222601_32815_20389~x20010012.jpg`
  - `16231321229486_31016_23681_23681x20958016.jpg`
- These images are also the product CTAs, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description. The extractor shows the alt as absent, not empty.
- `<html>` has no `lang` attribute in the visible source (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>`).
- `<title>` contains the internal template name: `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`.
- The preheader and the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` / `&shy;` padding block are implemented correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`). The footer is not visible, so tokens there are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found, and `Authentication-Results` was not found either, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- The QA tool itself says these headers may simply not survive the AgentMail relay. These are **unverified, not confirmed defects**. Check the raw message at the sending MTA or in a direct-to-Gmail test before concluding anything.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the footer, which was cut off in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All four product links use `utm_source=Campaign&utm_medium=Email&utm_campaign=SinglesDay1111Promo&utm_content=Promo&utm_id=2804736`, so the parameters are consistent across links.
- **`utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261111`** contains "Testing". This is either a test-segment label leaking into production sends or a mislabeled audience name, and it will pollute segment-level reporting.
- `utm_source=Campaign` is a generic value and not a platform or list identifier. Capitalized values like `Campaign`, `Email` and `Promo` can split from lowercase variants in GA4 reporting.
- `utm_content=Promo` is identical on every CTA, so clicks can't be attributed to a specific product tile or position.
- Destinations are product detail pages (PDPs), which is consistent with the "SHOP NOW" CTAs. The 403s prevent verifying that they load.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four 403 URLs with a real browser user-agent or through a headless browser. If they load, allowlist the QA probe or mark these as false positives.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images (product name and price or offer).
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Replace the template name in `<title>` with the actual subject or campaign name.
5. Fix `utm_term` so it doesn't carry the `Testing` label on production sends, and use unique `utm_content` values per tile.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results from the raw original message, not the relay copy.
7. Resolve the 22 skipped tracking links by following their redirects, to confirm they land on the UTM-tagged PDPs.
8. Check the product feed for the duplicated `23681_23681` segment in the SKU URL.
