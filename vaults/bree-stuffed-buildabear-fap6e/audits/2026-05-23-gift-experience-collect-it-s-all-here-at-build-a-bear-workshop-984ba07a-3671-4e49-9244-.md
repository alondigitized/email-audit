# Gift, Experience, Collect! It's All Here at Build-a-Bear Workshop!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a positioning email — Build-A-Bear is selling the *brand* itself, not a product. The subject "Gift, Experience, Collect!" maps directly to three stacked content blocks ("Gift A Hug," "Experience The Fun," "Collect"), which is rare structural discipline for a promo. As a frame device, it works. The pink-and-teal palette is on-brand and the layout reads cleanly top to bottom on mobile.
- The problem is the email is doing brand-awareness work in a promo slot. There is no offer in the hero — just "A Great Friendship Begins Here" and "Shop Now." The only price-anchored item is a small "Gifts Under $30" tile at the bottom and a "Shop Today's Sale!" strip buried in the lower third. For an existing subscriber who's been hammered with new-arrivals and IP-launch emails all week (Hello Kitty, Grogu, Lightning Bug, Summer Collection, May New Arrivals), this feels like a filler send between campaigns.
- Judgement: structurally tidy, commercially soft. A 6 — would land higher if the "Sale!" strip were the hero.

## What's working

- Subject's three-verb structure ("Gift, Experience, Collect") cleanly mirrors the three body blocks — rare alignment.
- Pink/teal/cream palette is consistent with the brand's recent sends and renders cleanly.
- "Experience The Fun → Find A Store" is a smart inclusion — drives retail foot traffic, not just e-comm.
- Bottom triptych ("Build Your Own Bear Online / Personalize With Sound / Gifts Under $30") gives three distinct entry points for different shopper intents.
- No render bugs — image grid, buttons, and type all hold together.

## What's weak

- Zero offer in the hero. "A Great Friendship Begins Here" is poetry, not commerce.
- "Shop Today's Sale!" is the most commercially loaded element on the page and it's a thin strip near the bottom — buried.
- The three CTAs ("Shop Now," "Find A Store," "See Collections") are generic verbs; none promise a specific outcome.
- "Collect" block is vague — collect *what*? No product line, no IP, no hook.
- After a week of specific campaign sends (Grogu, Hello Kitty, Lightning Bug, Promise Pets), this generic positioning email reads like cadence filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the sale.** Promote "Shop Today's Sale!" to hero. Replace the friendship headline with the actual discount (% off, price floor, category). Right now the strongest commercial signal is the smallest element on the page.
- 2. **Anchor "Collect" to a specific line.** Promise Pets, Hello Kitty Ice Cream Shop, Mini Promise Pets — pick one and make "Collect" mean something. Generic "See Collections" wastes a third of the email.
- 3. **Make CTAs specific.** "Shop Gifts Under $30," "Find Your Nearest Workshop," "Shop the Hello Kitty Collection" — verb + noun + value.
- 4. **Tighten the rhythm.** Three stacked blocks with the same visual weight reads like a directory. Make Gift the lead, Experience secondary, Collect tertiary — or invert based on what's selling this week.
- 5. **Subject + preheader rewrites:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: shop the Build-A-Bear sale →`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $30, in-store experiences, new collections`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online and in stores — find a sale, a store, or your next collectible.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three ways to Build-A-Bear this week: gift, visit, collect.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — Build-A-Bear Welcome Lead Gen 2

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is built on a legacy Salesforce Marketing Cloud template (HTML 4.01 Transitional) with multiple deliverability and accessibility defects: nearly all hosted images load over `http://`, several images lack alt text, and required unsubscribe/auth headers are absent from the captured payload.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 26 tracking links via `click.ebuildabear.com` were skipped by the HTTP probe — unverified but consistent with SFMC click-redirect pattern; no broken-link evidence in source.
- Plain-text alternative is 79% URLs (3410/4321 chars), which spam filters (SpamAssassin `URI_HEX`, Gmail) penalize. Likely a tracking-token expansion in the auto-generated plaintext part — needs a curated text/plain alternative.
- No UTM parameters visible in the captured CTA URL (`click.ebuildabear.com/?qs=...`); UTMs may be appended on redirect but are not inspectable from the email source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 7 images missing `alt` text (e.g., `2a45a281-c71e-4b75-8a61-257e92dec041.jpg`, `dd5f2527-4348-4bc8-99ab-4d5109fe86e8.png`, `9bb8905d-...jpg`, `b3a86953-...jpg`, `ad6ecb02-...jpg`, `3fde8069-...jpg`, `8a3718a3-...jpg`). Fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades preview when images blocked.
- 15+ images loaded over `http://image.ebuildabear.com/...` (content images + Facebook/Twitter/Instagram/TikTok/YouTube social icons + Build-A-Bear Radio). Modern clients (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection proxy, Outlook with strict policies) may block or rewrite mixed-content image requests. Host (`image.ebuildabear.com`) does support HTTPS — these should all be `https://`.
- DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional with XHTML namespace — internally inconsistent but typical for SFMC; acceptable for email clients.
- `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility anti-pattern on mobile.
- `@import url("https://use.typekit.net/gly7dsv.css")` — most mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) strip `@import`; webfont will silently fall back. Not a bug, but the request is wasted.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tags (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`) detected in the captured source.

## 5. Compliance
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not present in capture. Caveat — AgentMail relay may strip headers, so this is unconfirmed at origin.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058): not present. Required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (Feb 2024+) for senders >5K/day. If genuinely missing at origin, this is a deliverability blocker.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not present in capture — SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment cannot be verified. Same AgentMail caveat applies.
- CAN-SPAM physical address / unsubscribe link in body: HTML source was truncated before the footer; cannot confirm from provided excerpt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot evaluate — all CTAs route through `click.ebuildabear.com/?qs=...` tracking redirects that were not followed. UTM/landing alignment is not inspectable without resolving redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Rewrite all image sources from `http://` to `https://`** — single highest-impact fix; affects ~15 image assets and all social icons.
2. **Add `alt` attributes** to the 7 flagged images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable only for purely decorative spacers).
3. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers at the SFMC sending IP** (re-capture without AgentMail relay or check raw headers in a direct inbox) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
4. **Verify `Authentication-Results`** by sending to a mailbox you control and inspecting raw headers; confirm SPF (`ebuildabear.com`), DKIM selector, and DMARC alignment.
5. **Curate the `text/plain` alternative** — strip duplicated tracking URLs to drop URL ratio below ~30%.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1, user-scalable=0` from viewport meta to restore mobile zoom.
7. If Typekit font isn't actually rendering in any client, drop the `@import` to save a request and a CSP flag.
