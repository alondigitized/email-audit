# Just Chillin’ – It’s Our NEW Chinchilla!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean product-launch send that buries its offer. The chinchilla hero is cute and clear: a gray plush in a tan hoodie holding a Mini Bean on a purple-pink gradient, with a "Shop Now" button. But the real money message, "Up to 50% OFF in Our BEARLY Any Left Sale!", sits in a thin orange strip above it. The strip is easy to skim past. The subject sells only the new chinchilla and never mentions the sale.
- The email repeats the same promise lower down with a second "UP TO 50% OFF" block, a green monster plush and a Halloween pet-outfit hero. The result is two competing stories (new chinchilla vs. sale) plus an eight-product grid, and no single lead. Compared with the 2026-10-19 "Boo! FREE Shipping TODAY ONLY!" send, which put one offer front and center, this one is softer and more cluttered. It is competent but not sharp.

## What's working

- The chinchilla hero is a strong, on-brand product shot. The copy names both sizes, "regular and Mini Beans®", which is useful detail.
- The "Up to 50% OFF" banner sits at the very top, so the price hook is visible at the start of the render.
- The "Start Earning Points!" pill gives loyalty members a visible nudge.
- The Halloween pet-outfit block ("Ready for Howl-oween!") fits the season and echoes the "Boo!" send from 2026-10-19.
- Section hierarchy is easy to scan: hero, sale block, shop-by-product grid, "More to Explore", footer links.

## What's weak

- Render bug: the product label reads "SanrioÂ® Berry Plush". That is garbled character encoding, and it looks sloppy on a licensed Sanrio item.
- The sale is stated twice, in the banner and in the "UP TO 50% OFF" block. Both are vague ("up to") and name no hero product at a specific price.
- The deadline appears only in tiny footer fine print (online only, through 11:59 p.m. CST on October 26). The body has no visible urgency.
- Nearly every button says "SHOP NOW", including all eight in the grid and "More to Explore". Nothing tells the reader what they get.
- The email carries too many themes: chinchilla, sale, Halloween pets, Sanrio, Disney Maleficent, teddy bears, owl. Nothing earns the click over the rest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. If the sale is the lead, make "Up to 50% OFF, ends Oct 26" the hero and put the chinchilla in as a featured item. If the chinchilla is the lead, drop the second 50% block entirely.
- 2. Move the deadline into the hero or banner: "Ends Sunday, Oct 26".
- 3. Fix the encoding so "Sanrio®" renders correctly.
- 4. Replace generic "SHOP NOW" buttons with specific copy, such as "Meet the Chinchilla", "Shop Halloween Pet Outfits", or "Shop Sale Plush".
- 5. Cut the product grid to 2–4 items, or show a real sale price on each.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has a sound structure (XHTML table layout, MSO conditionals, responsive media queries), but it has no plain-text part, no `lang` attribute, and four product images without alt text. The 403s on the four product links are probably bot-blocking by the probe, not real breakage. The header checks could not be verified because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four product "SHOP NOW" links returned 403** to the QA probe:
  - `/sanrio-berry-plush/035172.html`
  - `/happy-hugs-teddy-bear/025567.html`
  - `/disney-villains-maleficent-dragon-plush/032609.html`
  - `/timeless-teddy-bear/026518.html`
- All four are direct `buildabear.com` product URLs (Demandware/SFCC), not tracking redirects. A uniform 403 on every direct link, with no 404s, points to bot or WAF filtering of the probe's user agent. It is not evidence that the pages are dead. Confirm with a real browser or mobile user agent before treating these as broken.
- The QA report prints an empty URL after "Broken link (403):". Its link attribution is malformed, but the full URLs are in the detail text.
- **24 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped** by the HTTP probe, so the destinations of most of the email's links are unverified.
- **Tracking parameters are inconsistent in value:** `utm_source=Campaign` is generic and not a source identifier. `utm_term=US-US_Testing_Hot-20261023` contains `Testing`, which suggests a test or seed-segment value reached a live send. That would skew attribution.
- All four links share `utm_content=Promo`, so clicks on different products can't be told apart by content tag.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The plain-text part is missing** (QA: "Text version is 0 chars", FAIL). This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and the email is a multipart/alternative with an empty text part.
- **Four images have no alt text:**
  - `47250800435172~x20128124.jpg`
  - `84231211225567x20710014.jpg`
  - `88240802332609~x20101015.jpg`
  - `69231211226518x20714018.jpg`
- These are the product images tied to the "SHOP NOW" links. With images blocked, the links have no descriptive context.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute** (`<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>`). Screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **`<title>` is a template name:** `Build-A-Bear - Build-A-Bear_BroadcastTemplate_1A`. Some clients and screen readers expose it.
- `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are set to `light dark`, but the visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` rules and the body uses `bgcolor="#ffffff"`. Dark-mode rendering depends on the client's auto-inversion. I can't confirm more because the HTML is truncated.
- The preheader (`Available in Regular and Mini Beans® Sizes`) is correctly hidden and padded with the `&zwnj;&nbsp;` hack. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible, non-truncated HTML contains no unrendered tokens (no `{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`). The footer and body past the truncation point could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. Treat it as **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Check the raw headers at the original receiving mailbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay, so verify at the destination.
- The sender domain `ebuildabear.com` differs from the link domain `buildabear.com`. That is common for ESP sending subdomains, but confirm that SPF/DKIM/DMARC align on the From domain.
- The footer's unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameters are present and well-formed on all four product links:** `utm_medium=Email`, `utm_campaign=ChinchillaDuoLaunch`, `utm_id=2801769`. Capitalization is inconsistent with typical lowercase conventions, which can split GA reports.
- **Landing pages do not match the campaign:** the subject and campaign name concern the new Chinchilla, but all four probed links go to unrelated products (Sanrio berry plush, Happy Hugs Teddy, Maleficent Dragon, Timeless Teddy). Whether the Chinchilla links are among the 24 skipped tracking links is unknown.
- `utm_term=…Testing…` (see §2) is the main continuity risk.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart plain-text alternative. It currently has 0 characters.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four product images, for example "Shop Sanrio Berry Plush".
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and replace the `<title>` with the subject line.
4. Re-test the four product URLs with a browser user agent. If they load, whitelist the QA probe or ignore the 403s. If they fail, escalate to the web team.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and the `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass and alignment) in the original message headers, not the relayed copy.
6. Remove `Testing` from the `utm_term` pattern for production sends. Use a real source value instead of `Campaign`, and give each product a distinct `utm_content`.
7. Resolve and probe the 24 skipped tracking links to confirm that the Chinchilla hero and CTA reach a working landing page.
