# $17 vs $185: There is no difference 💸🍊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN running the "clinical study" comparison play — a $17 serum vs. $185 alternative, dramatized with a head-to-head data table (satisfaction %, purchase intent) rather than just a photo and a claim. It's a sharper hook than the recent run of routine-builder and BOGO sends: "same glow, less dough" plus a literal comparison chart gives the reader a concrete reason to believe, not just a mood board. The twins-testing-it framing (Veronica and Vanessa Merrell) adds a light social-proof layer even though there's no visible follower count or influencer credibility signal beyond the names and arrows.
- Where it falls short of the brand's best sends this week (8/7 Bubble collab, 8/5 Set It Bright) is focus: after the strong hero and comparison table, the email keeps going — a "find your skincare" quiz banner, a "Top Picks for You" carousel, then six flat gray category buttons (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair), then a cruelty-free banner, then a footer mega-block with shipping/rewards/app/social. That's a lot of competing CTAs diluting one genuinely strong offer. The hero and price/satisfaction table alone would carry this email; everything after "Shop Now" is filler that a scrolling reader will bail on.
- Verdict: strong concept, cluttered execution — trim the back half and this is an 8+.

## What's working

- The $17 vs $185 price comparison table is concrete and scannable — satisfaction %, purchase-intent delta, side-by-side pricing.
- Hero copy ("SAME GLOW, LESS DOUGH") is punchy, on-brand, and immediately communicates the value prop without opening the email.
- Product shot with visible ingredient callout (Vitamin C + E + Ferulic Serum) ties the claim directly to a real SKU.
- No render bugs — layout, gradient, buttons, and images all display cleanly.
- Clear single primary CTA in the hero ("Bright This Way") and a second reinforcing CTA ("Shop Now") right after the table.

## What's weak

- Too many competing CTAs after the hero: a skin quiz, a 3-product picks carousel, and six category buttons all fight for the same scroll real estate.
- The six gray category bars (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) are visually dead weight — flat, unbranded, and disconnected from the comparison story above them.
- "Overall Satisfaction 68% vs 68%" is a confusing data point — if satisfaction is identical, it undercuts rather than supports the "no difference, but $17" argument; it should be framed as a strength ("same satisfaction, 91% less cost") not left as a flat tie in a table.
- The influencer names (Veronica/Vanessa) have no context — no @handle, follower count, or "as seen on TikTok" tag, so the social-proof value is muted for anyone who doesn't already recognize them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop Now" — drop the quiz banner, Top Picks carousel, and category button stack, or move them below a hard visual break so the comparison narrative isn't diluted. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Reframe the satisfaction stat so it reads as a win, not a tie: "68% satisfaction — same as the $185 version, at 91% less cost."
- 3. Add handles/context under Veronica and Vanessa's names (e.g., "@merrelltwins, 30M+ followers") to cash in the social-proof value that's currently just implied.
- 4. Tighten the subject — "$17 vs $185" is strong, but pair it with a sharper preheader that names the product category so mobile inbox scanning is faster.
- **Subject Alt A:** `We tested $17 vs. $185. Same glow.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Merrell twins just saved you $168`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Vitamin C serum, dupe-tested and verified.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `68% satisfaction. 91% less money.`
