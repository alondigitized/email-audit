# NEW! Thirst Burst Lip BFFs 👄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN's launch send for the Thirst Burst Lip BFFs duo (scrub + treatment, $12), and it's a clean, well-structured hero-to-product cascade — freckled close-up model shot, pink "new" badge, clear "Only $12" price hook, and a logical two-product breakdown with individual copy for the Smoothie and the Treatment. Compared to the recent run of e.l.f. SKIN sends (2026-09-16's acne-close-up miss at 4.00, 2026-09-10's shimmer-stick cascade at 7.00), this one sits closer to the better end — no render bugs, a real price point up front, and a genuinely on-brand pink/coral gradient system throughout.
- Where it loses points is scope and specificity: "what's inside." then splits into two nearly identical pastel product blocks with soft, generic benefit copy ("helps smooth & visibly plump," "plush, cushiony") and no before/after or swatch proof that either product actually does anything visible. The email also sprawls past the hero into a "find your skincare" quiz block and a three-item "Top Picks for You" cross-sell before finally hitting the nav bar — by the time a reader scrolls that far, the $12 duo is old news. Solid, on-brand, slightly unfocused.

## What's working

- Clear, single, specific offer visible immediately: "Only $12" sits right under the product name in the hero.
- "new" badge + "new + limited edition" copy creates real urgency/scarcity without being spammy.
- Two-product breakdown is logically ordered (scrub first, treatment second) and each gets its own hero shot and benefit line.
- Consistent pink/coral/blue gradient palette ties the whole email together — no jarring color shifts.
- "kit exclusive" callout on the Lip Smoothie adds a credible reason to buy the duo instead of items separately.

## What's weak

- Both product benefit lines are vague filler ("helps smooth & visibly plump," "leaves lips feeling soft, smooth and conditioned") — no ingredient, texture, or before/after visual proof.
- No swatch, shade, or lip-on-model shot for either product — for a lip category, readers can't see actual color/finish payoff.
- Email overstays its welcome: after the $12 offer, it adds a skincare quiz block AND a 3-item unrelated cross-sell ("Top Picks for You") before the nav — this dilutes the single CTA the rubric rewards.
- Hero hook says "duo" but "Buff & Boost" CTA is on the hero, then two separate "Shop Now" buttons follow for each item — unclear if $12 buys the bundle or if that's a per-item price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Clarify pricing: state explicitly whether $12 is for the duo or per item — right now it's genuinely ambiguous and could suppress clicks from price-sensitive shoppers.
- 2. Cut the "find your skincare" quiz block and "Top Picks for You" cross-sell entirely from this send — let the $12 duo be the only ask; move cross-sell to a follow-up email.
- 3. Add one lip-swatch or on-model application shot showing actual color/plump payoff — currently zero proof the product does what the copy claims.
- 4. Swap generic benefit copy for one concrete detail each (e.g., "sugar-based scrub, use 2x/week" / "hyaluronic acid formula, 8-hr hydration") to earn Click-Likelihood's "product detail" signal.
