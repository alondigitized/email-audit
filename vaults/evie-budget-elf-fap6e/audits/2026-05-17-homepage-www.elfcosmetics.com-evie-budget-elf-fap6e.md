# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Got the screenshot. Writing the full review now.
- Okay this is giving exactly what I expect from e.l.f. — clean, fun, and immediately screaming "budget beauty girlies, come HERE." They're pushing a new cream blush-bronzer launch called The Cream Team at $7 each, and honestly the hero model is giving the exact vibe I come here for: dewy, natural, not trying too hard. It reads current, it reads Gen Z, and the price point is front and center where it belongs.

## What's working

- **The $7 price anchor in the hero copy** — "Buttery blush + bronzer melt into skin for a diffused finish. $7 each!" is doing the heavy lifting that Charlotte Tilbury won't do for their Hollywood Flawless Filter. This is the dupe economy and e.l.f. is speaking it fluently.
- **"THE CREAM TEAM" headline** — playful, product-descriptive, and the tone is exactly right for the 18-25 crowd. It doesn't sound like a department store; it sounds like a TikTok creator named it.
- **The hero model** — close-up, curly hair, brown skin, natural flush, gold hoops — this is not a runway face. This is the girl I follow on BeautyTok. Highly relatable for my demographic.
- **The dual hook strip (Beauty Squad + app scan)** — two acquisition plays stacked right below the nav: free shipping and an app reward. Both land above the fold without screaming.
- **Top banner promo specificity** — "$5 off when you buy 2 or more mascaras" with a direct "Shop Now" link. That's not "up to X% off sitewide" nonsense — it's a real deal I can act on right now.

## What's weak

- **"STICK AROUND" CTA is witty but vague** — I love the wordplay but if I'm moving fast I don't immediately know if this takes me to a product page, a collection, or a brand campaign. "Shop The Cream Team" would convert harder.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — no category pills, no quick-tap to Lips or Eyes or Face. One more tap stands between me and the thing I came for. On mobile this is a friction point.
- **Right side of the carousel is cut off** — there's clearly more hero content sliding to the right but there's zero visual affordance telling me to swipe. I might bounce thinking this is all there is.
- **No reviews or product ratings visible anywhere above the fold** — e.l.f. has a cult following and strong review counts; showing even "★★★★★ 12k reviews" on a featured product would close the loop faster.
- **Free shipping threshold is conditional** — it requires joining Beauty Squad, which is fine, but first-time visitors might read "free shipping" and then hit a signup gate and feel misled.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA from "STICK AROUND" to "Shop $7 Shades"** — keeps the price front-of-mind, removes the ambiguity, still on-brand.
- **Add category quick-links below the hero** — pill buttons for Face / Eyes / Lips / Skin would let me jump straight to what I want in one tap instead of opening the hamburger.
- **Add a swipe indicator or second hero dot** — even a subtle pagination dot row below the hero lets me know there's more content to explore without hunting for it.
- **Pull one featured product review count into the hero** — even just "★★★★★ Loved by 30k+" stamped near the product would give social proof before I've scrolled anywhere.
