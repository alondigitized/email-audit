# We're taking care of frizzness 💁‍♀️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. Hair's launch email for the new Humidity Hero Anti-Frizz Styling Spray, and the hero section is genuinely well-built: bold chartreuse background, a confident model shot, a punchy "FROM FRIZZ TO RIZZ" headline, and a clean $9 price point with a single "SHOP NOW" CTA. It's the strongest 600px of the email by far.
- Past that hero, though, the send loses discipline. It stitches in a "meet the mane crew" product lineup shot (fine, on-topic), then abruptly pivots to a leftover National Lipstick Day GWP block with code LIPPIE — the exact promo from the July 27 send in this brand's history, now sitting inside a haircare launch email with zero connective tissue. That's followed by a generic "Top Picks for You" cross-sell (a primer, a face cream, a setting spray — none of them hair products) and six empty-looking category nav bars. The email reads like three different campaigns were pasted into one template.
- Compared to the tightly focused single-SKU sends that scored 8-9 in this brand's recent history (July 24 Cream Blush, July 27 Lipstick Day), this one dilutes a strong hero with recycled, off-category filler. Good top, cluttered middle.

## What's working

- Hero headline "FROM FRIZZ TO RIZZ" is on-trend, punchy, and instantly communicates the product benefit.
- Clear single CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits right under the offer with no ambiguity about the next step.
- Price ($9) and function ("heat-activated... repel moisture for frizz control") are both stated plainly in the hero — no hunting for details.
- "meet the mane crew" section gives the new spray context within the existing hair line, reinforcing it's part of a real system, not a one-off.
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy anywhere in the send.

## What's weak

- The National Lipstick Day GWP block is stale, out-of-category creative bolted into a hair-launch email — it belongs in a lips send, not here, and it's already been used (July 27).
- Subject line has a typo/non-word: "frizzness" isn't a real word (should be "frizziness"), which undercuts an otherwise clever headline pairing.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sells face/primer products with zero connection to the hair story the email opened with.
- Six flat gray category nav bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) add visual dead weight with no imagery or reason to tap.
- No social proof (star ratings, reviews) anywhere near the hero product, despite this being a brand-new SKU that needs trust-building.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Lipstick Day GWP block entirely — it's off-category, recycled, and breaks the hair narrative the email opened with. Replace that real estate with a second hair product (e.g., the Never Thirsty Shampoo/Conditioner from the July 25 haircare launch) or a before/after frizz-control visual.
- 2. Fix the subject line typo and sharpen it toward the product name/benefit rather than vague "taking care of."
- 3. Swap "Top Picks for You" to hair-only cross-sells (brush, heat protectant, scalp care) so the whole email stays in one category — mirrors what made the July 24/25 sends score well.
- 4. Add a one-line review snippet or star rating under the hero product to build trust on a brand-new SKU.
- 5. Give the CTA copy a verb+noun upgrade ("Shop the Spray") instead of the generic "SHOP NOW" repeated four times across the email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze/ESP-templated HTML message with MSO conditional markup and a responsive stack layout; automated QA passed only 27% of checks, with the most significant issues being four broken CTA redirect links, a missing plain-text MIME part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken links (FAIL)** — all four route through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click-tracking redirects but resolve to **relative paths without a domain**, which QA correctly flags as "unknown url type":
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&utm_source=broadcast&lid=1phcyzzughb1&bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&utm_medium=email&is_retargeting=true&` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")

  All four are the two primary product CTAs plus the rewards and app-download links — i.e., the highest-value click targets in the email are broken. Root cause is a missing absolute base URL (e.g., `https://www.elfcosmetics.com`) in the destination the redirector was configured to point to, not a rendering issue in the raw HTML.
- The mobile-app link's underlying path also has an unterminated query string (trailing `&` with no final param), suggesting a template variable failed to interpolate.
- 12 of 41 material links were probed per QA's click-likelihood ranking; 29 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not verified — those remain unconfirmed.
