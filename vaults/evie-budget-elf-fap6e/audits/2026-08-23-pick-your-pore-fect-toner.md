## What's weak

- Bronzing Drops GWP banner is a fourth consecutive repeat (8/13, 8/18, 8/19, now 8/23) — same shade, same $50 threshold, same copy.
- "Top Picks for You" thumbnails are tiny and low-contrast against white — hard to tell products apart at a glance.
