# Choose a full-size gift on us! 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward gift-with-purchase promo. The offer is clear: "Choose a full-size gift *on us* when you spend $50+. No code needed." It sits in a black block under a moody, pink-toned hero. The hero is a clear compact with a brush and an "ONLINE ONLY" badge. The black block gives the offer strong contrast, and the white SHOP NOW pill is hard to miss.
- The weakness is that the email never shows the gift. The hero is a mood shot, and the "choose a full-size gift" copy has no product behind it. The reader is asked to spend $50 for something they can't see. The "Top Picks" row shows $11, $11 and $2 products, which don't help reach $50 and have no tie to the gift. The six gray category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are heavy, and the fine print is dense and tiny. The $50 threshold and the Oct 31 deadline only appear in that tiny print.
- Compared with the Oct 20 kit send and the Oct 14 lip balm send, which each told a tight single story, this is a generic sitewide promo. It works as a reminder but is unlikely to excite. The Oct 19 and Oct 12 roundups were the same kind of catch-all.

## What's working

- The offer is plain and loud: "Choose a full-size gift on us" with "No code needed" removes friction.
- White type on black in the offer block is easy to read, and the CTA pill stands out.
- The "limited time!" line, with the "giving" italic wordplay, keeps the e.l.f. voice.
- The "Top Picks" row shows real prices ($11, $11, $2) with a button under each product.
- The footer mentions free shipping and app and receipt-scanning rewards, which support the purchase.

## What's weak

- No gift is shown, so the central offer is abstract.
- The hero is a decorative compact that says nothing about the offer. Its "gift" wordmark is hard to read.
- The deadline (Oct 31) and the $50 pre-tax threshold are only in the tiny footer disclaimer.
- The six gray category bars take up a lot of vertical space and push the footer far down the scroll.
- "Top Picks for You" doesn't explain why these items were picked, and $2 to $11 items don't build toward $50.
- The only CTA copy is the generic "SHOP NOW", repeated four times.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the gift options in the hero or right under it, as three or four product tiles labeled "Pick 1 free." This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the deadline in the headline block, for example "Ends Oct 31."
- 3. Replace the generic "Top Picks" with a "Build your $50 haul" section of bundles or best sellers that get the reader to the threshold.
- 4. Shrink the category bars to a compact nav row or a 2x3 grid.
- 5. Change the CTA copy to something specific, like "Pick my free gift."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free full-size gift ends Oct 31 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $50, pick a free full-size`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Choose from 5 full-size faves. No code needed, online only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Oct 31 at 11:59 PM PT. Your gift is added at checkout.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found four of the twelve probed tracked links failing, a 0-character plain-text part, and no captured unsubscribe or authentication headers. The failures look like redirect-resolution errors. They may be probe artifacts, but that isn't confirmed, and the truncated HTML limits how much I could check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probed links ended in `unknown url type`.** Each is a click-tracked `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` URL whose redirect resolved to a relative path with no scheme or host:
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Truth+or+Bare+-+Peachy+Pink`
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → `/pages/mobile-app?lid=lp3450u51na1&deep_link_value=...`
- **The cause is unconfirmed.** Either the probe failed to resolve a relative `Location` header, or the redirect really returns a relative path. In the second case a browser would resolve it against `links.elfcosmetics.com` and could 404. Re-test these with a browser-equivalent client.
- **Other probe results:** 12 links were probed and 26 were skipped, so most footer and secondary links are unverified.
- **Tracked-link format:** the links use SendGrid-style `~~` wrappers with opaque payloads, so destination and UTM values can't be read from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Empty `<title>`:** the document has `<title></title>`.
- **Empty font family:** a `mobile_hide` block has `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. It's harmless, since the fallback applies.
- **Missing alt text:** one image has none, `src=https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/3jJHmFDf...`. The `/q/` path on the tracking domain suggests an open-tracking pixel, so impact is probably low. Add `alt=""` to make that explicit.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` appeared in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so this applies only to the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it's unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Sender domain:** `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` are different subdomains. Confirm alignment in the raw headers.
- **Physical address and unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source. The tool says it always probes an unsubscribe link if one is present, but I can't confirm the result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible because the links are wrapped. The only visible parameters are on the redirect targets: `c=email` and `is_retargeting=true` on the app link, and `return_to=%2Faccount` on the login link. Capture the final landing URLs to verify UTMs.
- **Product deep links:** the "Shop Now" links carry variant parameters (`Size=...`, `Color=...`), which is good practice. Verify that the selected variant pre-populates and that the offer applies.
- **Receipt-scanning link:** the image is labeled "Receipt scanning" but points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. It lands on a generic login page rather than a receipt-scanning feature. Logged-out users see a login wall with no context.
- **Gift offer:** the preheader says "When you spend $50+." The probed links go to individual product pages, not a gift-selection page. I can't tell whether the gift-selection destination is linked, because the probe covered only a subset of links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four failing links with a client that handles relative redirects. If they return 404 on `links.elfcosmetics.com`, the ESP's redirect target is misconfigured and needs fixing.
2. Add a `text/plain` multipart alternative, since it's currently 0 characters.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw source, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment there too.
4. Point the receipt-scanning link at a dedicated landing page, or at least a login page that preserves the context.
5. Set a meaningful `<title>`, and add `alt=""` to the pixel image.
6. Capture the final landing URLs for all links, including footer links, to validate UTM consistency.
