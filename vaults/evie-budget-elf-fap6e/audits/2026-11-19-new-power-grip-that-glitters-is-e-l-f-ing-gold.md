## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard tracked-link build (`links.elfcosmetics.com` click wrapper, `braze-images.com` assets). All four QA "FAIL" results come from the probe getting a relative redirect target, which is most likely a QA tooling artifact and not a confirmed broken link. The HTML truncation and relay-stripped headers prevent verification of the unsubscribe link and the authentication results.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 probe failures, same signature:** `unknown url type: '/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Dark+Cocoa+-+Deep+Brown'`, `'/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=So+Petal+-+light+pink'`, `'/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount'` and `'/pages/mobile-app?is_retargeting=true&utm_source=broadcast&onelink_id=wTtZ&c=email&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&bxid=6a0…'`.
  - Each wrapper at `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` returned a path-only `Location`. The probe did not resolve it against the origin, which is the `urllib`/`requests` "unknown url type" failure.
  - A relative `Location` is technically permitted (RFC 7231) and browsers resolve it. **This is not evidence of a broken link for recipients.** The destination host and status code were not captured, so the links are unverified, not failed.
- **Open-tracking pixel without alt:** the `<img>` at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/jydg6T0X-…/jKWHooOjGX8Y7wl7iQ_N_gHzfU5WTCv2gv2sd9W7MeKREXgWqoCg4` has no `alt`. This is a 1×1 pixel, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct form.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 40 links were probed (28 skipped by design), so the 27% pass rate is not representative of the whole email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- Inline style `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` (block-2) contains an empty font-name string. It falls through to Verdana, but it is invalid output from the template.
- The layout is built from `nl-container` tables nested inside `html_block` cells (duplicate `row-1`/`block-1` class names). Every table has `role="presentation"`, and the `lang="en"` attribute and `viewport` meta are present.
- The Open Sans web font is loaded through `<link>` in an `<!--[if !mso]>` wrapper. Several clients, including Gmail, ignore this, so the Arial fallback is what renders there.
- The hero image has descriptive `alt="e.l.f."`. Product-image alt text beyond the truncation point could not be verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The portion after the truncation point was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message in a direct-to-mailbox seed test. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` is not aligned with the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com`, but both are on the same organizational domain, so DMARC relaxed alignment should hold.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link fall after the truncation, so they could not be verified here.
- **Plain-text part:** 11,694 of 13,706 characters (85%) are URLs. That is a real deliverability smell, caused by long tracking URLs in the text alternative.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Account gate:** the "Receipt scanning" CTA lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Unauthenticated recipients hit a login wall, not the rewards content.
- **UTM params:** the app-download link carries `utm_source=broadcast` and `c=email` but no `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`. The probed product URLs show only `?Color=…` variant params. Because the wrapper redirects, UTM tags may be appended at the click-tracker, but this could not be confirmed from the probe.
- **Product deep links:** `Color=Dark+Cocoa+-+Deep+Brown` and `Color=So+Petal+-+light+pink` pass variant selections as query strings. Check that these resolve to the matching variants in-browser.
- **`af_dp` deep link:** `com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome` points to the app home, not a campaign-specific destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the QA probe:** resolve relative `Location` headers with `urljoin(prev_url, location)` and re-run. This will likely clear all 4 FAILs. Until then, don't treat them as defects.
2. **Verify headers on a direct seed inbox,** not the AgentMail relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
3. Capture the full HTML so the footer and unsubscribe link can be audited.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, populate `<title>`, and remove the empty `font-family:''` value.
5. Confirm the `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` tags exist on the final landing URLs.
6. Shorten or trim URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL share below about 70%.
7. Send the receipt-scanning CTA to a public explainer page, or preserve the intent after login.
