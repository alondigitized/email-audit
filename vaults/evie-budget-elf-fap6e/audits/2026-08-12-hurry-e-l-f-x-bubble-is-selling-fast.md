# HURRY! 🏃‍♀️ e.l.f. x Bubble is selling fast 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the third e.l.f. x Bubble send in a week (launch on 8/7, this one on 8/12), and it's back to selling the same 2-in-1 hybrid collab with a "selling fast" urgency hook that the subject line promises but the body never delivers on. The hero banner — pink-to-mint gradient, "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" — is clean and on-brand, and the $10 price point paired with clear product breakouts (Glow Talk lip serum, Camo Slide blush serum) makes the offer easy to parse. But there's a real mismatch: the subject screams "HURRY, selling fast," while the email itself shows zero stock counters, zero countdown, zero "almost gone" badge — just calm product photography and a "NEW! SUPER-LIMITED DROP" tag that's been running since launch.
- Compared to the 8/7 launch send, this is a weaker execution of the same idea, recycling nearly identical hero treatment without adding the scarcity signal the subject line is trading on. It reads as a bait-and-switch: strong urgency copy in the inbox, no reinforcement once opened.

## What's working

- Hero banner is crisp and legible, gradient matches the product colorway, "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" is a fun tagline.
- $10 price called out clearly and repeated per-product (Glow Talk, Camo Slide) — no digging for the offer.
- "3 SHADES" badges on both product shots give a concrete, specific detail (not vague "variety").
- Below-fold "Top Picks for You" adds a personalized cross-sell with prices visible at a glance.
- Category nav bar (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) gives an easy path for browsers not sold on this specific drop.

## What's weak

- No urgency signal anywhere in the render — no stock count, no countdown, no "almost sold out" badge — despite the subject's "selling fast" claim.
- "NEW! SUPER-LIMITED DROP" banner is identical language to the 8/7 launch send, five days later, which undercuts the "limited" claim.
- Three near-identical "SHOP NOW" CTAs in a row (hero, Glow Talk, Camo Slide) create redundancy without escalating urgency.
- This is the third e.l.f. x Bubble email in under a week — cadence risk for fatigue, especially with the offer/hero unchanged.
- No social proof (reviews, ratings, "sold out last time") to back up the urgency claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real urgency element to the body — a stock counter ("Only X left"), a countdown timer, or at minimum a "back by popular demand, limited restock" line — so the email delivers on what the subject promises. This is the single biggest lever; right now open and click intent built by the subject dies on arrival.
- 2. Swap the "3 SHADES" badge for something with numbers-that-move, e.g. "3 SHADES — 2 SOLD OUT" if true, to make scarcity concrete and honest.
- 3. Cut one of the three SHOP NOW CTAs (hero + 2 product sections is redundant) and use the freed space for a proof point — reviews, star rating, or UGC.
- 4. Vary hero creative from the 8/7 launch — same banner five days later signals a lazy resend, not a "selling fast" moment.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Back in stock alert: e.l.f. x Bubble, going fast 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Glow Talk and Camo Slide are almost gone — $10 each`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The 2-in-1 hybrids everyone's talking about. Limited restock.`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics "e.l.f. x Bubble" Email

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a standard Braze-rendered MSO-compatible template with heavy link redirection through `links.elfcosmetics.com`, but ships with a 0-byte plain-text fallback and 4 confirmed broken destination links (33% of probed links failed).

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All hyperlinks route through a Braze click-tracking redirector (`https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), consistent with expected ESP tracking behavior — not itself an issue.
- 4 of 12 probed links resolve to broken/malformed destinations:
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` — flagged "unknown url type"
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` — flagged "unknown url type"
  - "Receipt scanning..." → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` — flagged "unknown url type"
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → `/pages/mobile-app?is_retargeting=true&utm_medium=email&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&lid=f9y3lq23lvre&utm_campa...` — flagged "unknown url type"
  - These are relative paths embedded as the redirect target rather than fully-qualified absolute URLs, which is why the QA resolver reports "unknown url type." This will break for any client/proxy that doesn't correctly resolve the Braze redirect chain to an absolute domain.
- 29 links (footer/utility/social) were deprioritized and not probed — coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

- MSO conditional comments and `@media (max-width:660px)` responsive rules are present and structured correctly; no malformed conditional tags observed.
- Preheader text present and properly hidden via `display:none!important` div.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail), not necessarily absent at origin; flag for header-level verification.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not captured — cannot confirm authentication status from this data.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA probing notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included"), but its exact URL was not included in the truncated source for direct verification.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**

- Product CTAs pass variant selectors (`Size=`, `Color=`) as query params rather than PDP variant slugs/IDs — combined with the "unknown url type" failures, this suggests the redirect target isn't resolving these into valid absolute e-commerce URLs.

**7. Recommendations**
