# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- OK so e.l.f. is literally my brand — I live in Power Grip Primer, I have three backups in my Target haul drawer. The homepage is screaming the right things at me with the Power Grip Collection hero and a real GWP deal in the banner, but a full-page cookie consent modal ate my entire above-the-fold experience before I could even see a product. Like, I know what this brand is, I'm already a fan, and the first thing it showed me was a wall of legal text.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — "Hottest Drops Duo FREE with any $50+ order, code BURST" is exactly how I shop. I'm already doing math in my head about what to add to hit $50.
- **Power Grip Collection as the hero** — e.l.f. knows what they're famous for and they're leaning into it. This is the product that put them on my FYP and I respond to it immediately.
- **"Hottest Drops" copy** — "Hottest" is a word I actually say. The register feels native to how I talk about beauty.
- **Navigation bar** — clean, minimal, elf wordmark is recognizable, icons are obvious. Even through the modal chaos I can see exactly where to go.

## What's weak

- **Full-page cookie consent modal** — This is the #1 issue. I came to shop, not to manage my data preferences. The modal blocks the entire page and the first three buttons I see are about cookies, not products. Total vibe kill.
- **Hero is cut off** — The subtitle "The iconic, makeup-locking lineup has something for every skin to..." just trails off into the modal. I can't read the actual pitch.
- **No loyalty callout** — I'm in the Beauty Squad. Where's my points balance? Where's my member exclusive? This homepage has no idea I'm a repeat customer.
- **No free shipping threshold** — The banner mentions $50 for the GWP but doesn't say a word about free shipping. That's info I need before I start adding to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 5–10 seconds or trigger it on scroll** — Let me see the page first. Glossier does this better; the modal doesn't eat your hero. Implement a bottom-sheet or corner badge instead of a full viewport takeover.
- **Add a Beauty Squad member bar above or below the promo banner** — "Hi Evie — you have 340 points, redeem here" would make me feel seen and increase AOV immediately without a single pixel of new design work.
- **Reveal the full hero subtitle** — The "something for every skin to..." cutoff needs to complete its sentence. Even on a small mobile viewport this should wrap, not disappear into a modal.
- **Stack a free-shipping callout into the promo banner** — "FREE duo with $50+ | Free shipping over $35 | Code BURST" — one strip, three reasons to buy.
