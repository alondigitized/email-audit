# 2 steps to smooth, visibly plump lips 👄 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, product-launch send for the Thirst Burst Lip BFFs Duo ($12) — a Smoothie + Treatment two-step system — and it's a well-built step-by-step tutorial cascade: hero product shot, "lip besties with benefits" headline, then Step 1 / Step 2 broken out with close-up application shots, echoing the same structural discipline as the 2026-09-19 foundation routine-builder send. The close-up lip macro shot for Step 2 is a strong, sensory hero that sells the "visibly plump" claim better than copy could.
- Where it drops off is density and differentiation after the fold: a GWP banner, a full album-drop banner, and a three-item "Top Picks" grid are all crammed in below the core narrative, diluting an otherwise tight single-product story. It's a good top half riding on a busy, unfocused bottom half — solid, not standout.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: product box + single stick + "Only $12" pricing + "SHOP NOW" button, no scrolling needed on mobile.
- Step 1/Step 2 tutorial structure gives a clear reason to use both products, not just one.
- The Step 2 macro shot of lips with the product applied is a genuinely persuasive, on-claim visual (glossy, plump-looking).
- "kit exclusive" callout on the Smoothie sets expectation this isn't sold standalone, adding urgency to buy the duo now.
- No render bugs — clean gradient backgrounds, aligned type, consistent pink/blue color-blocking throughout.

## What's weak

- After Step 2, three unrelated modules (mascara GWP, album drop, Top Picks grid) stack back-to-back with no visual breathing room, burying the core lip offer.
- The album-drop banner ("MIRROR MIX") is a hard style-clash — dark blue/graffiti aesthetic dropped into an otherwise soft pastel email — and has zero connection to lip care.
- Category nav block (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) is plain gray bars with no icons or imagery, a dead zone visually.
- No price/value framing beyond "$12" — no mention of savings vs. buying two full-size products separately, even though this is pitched as a duo/kit.
- No social proof or star ratings anywhere for a brand-new product, which is a missed trust signal for something not yet reviewed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or move the album-drop banner out of this email entirely — it has no lip-care relevance and actively fights the pastel tutorial aesthetic; if it must run, send it standalone or place it last, below Top Picks.
- 2. Add a compact value line under the hero price ("2-step system, $12 — a $9 savings vs. buying separately" or similar) to make the kit exclusivity feel like a deal, not just a bundle.
- 3. Tighten the post-tutorial section to one module (keep GWP, drop or shrink Top Picks) so the lip narrative doesn't get diluted by three competing asks.
- 4. Add a single review star-rating snippet near the hero or Step 2 image once the product has early ratings, to build trust on a new SKU.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails on core deliverability infrastructure — missing plain-text alternative, no unsubscribe headers, and multiple tracked CTA links resolve to unroutable relative product paths instead of absolute URLs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through a Bronto/Cheetah-style click tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), consistent with standard ESP redirect wrapping.
- 4 of 12 probed material links fail resolution with "unknown url type," each decoding to a **relative path with no scheme/host**:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning" link)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&shortlink=email&...` ("Download the e.l.f. app")
  - These are the underlying destination URLs encoded inside the tracking redirect; the redirect wrapper itself resolves, but the final destination is malformed (missing `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` domain prefix), which will break the click-through for any client/proxy that validates the URL before following the redirect.
- 29 of 41 links were not probed (footer/utility/social, deprioritized) — coverage is partial; unscanned links carry unknown risk.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Deeply nested duplicate `<table class="nl-container">`/`<table class="row row-1">` structure (email is wrapped inside itself — the full outer container markup repeats inside `html_block block-1`). This bloats DOM size and risks inconsistent rendering in clients with strict nesting/quirks handling (e.g., Outlook via Word engine).
- One image missing `alt` text: `src="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/ngwvrVz1OIgAP8JfcYpOlw~~/.../dE4b_0JJy7mxelZfwJXaWt1KySWUUY5bZcgUniGISXVDttUj29kOO"` — no `alt` attribute present, fails accessibility (screen readers) and graceful degradation (images-off clients).
- MSO conditional comments and `mobile_hide`/`desktop_hide` classes are present and properly paired with `@media (max-width:660px)` — no structural issue found there.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no merge tag syntax (e.g., `{{`, `%%`, `*|`) present in the visible truncated source; preview text and hero image are static.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in captured headers. Cannot confirm RFC 2369 one-click unsubscribe is present at the transport level (may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence — flagged as WARN, not FAIL).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header** (RFC 8058, one-click POST unsubscribe): not found — same caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found via AgentMail relay — sender authentication status cannot be verified from available data.
- No in-body unsubscribe link was included in the truncated HTML sample provided, so body-level CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) cannot be confirmed or denied from this excerpt; QA notes an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, implying one exists elsewhere in the (truncated) footer.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are visible in either the tracking-wrapper URLs or the decoded destination paths — attribution to landing-page analytics cannot be confirmed from available data.
- Decoded destination paths use Shopify-style query params (`?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml`) for variant selection, consistent with an `elfcosmetics.com` Shopify storefront — but as noted in §2, the paths lack a domain/scheme, so even absent the tracker, these would not resolve as standalone URLs. This is the primary continuity defect: broken relative-path construction upstream of the tracking layer, not a redirect/wrapper problem.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the 4 broken destination URLs by ensuring the ESP template renders fully-qualified URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/products/...`) before wrapping in the click tracker — likely a template variable outputting a relative path instead of an absolute one.
- Add `alt` text to the image at `.../dE4b_0JJy7mxelZfwJXaWt1KySWUUY5bZcgUniGISXVDttUj29kOO`.
- Include a plain-text MIME alternative (`text/plain`) — currently 0 chars, which also increases spam-filter risk.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (check via raw header dump rather than relying on AgentMail relay capture) to confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- Remove the redundant nested full-container markup (duplicate `nl-container`/`row-1` wrapping inside `html_block block-1`) to reduce DOM weight and rendering risk.
- Add UTM parameters to CTA destination URLs for email-to-site analytics continuity.
