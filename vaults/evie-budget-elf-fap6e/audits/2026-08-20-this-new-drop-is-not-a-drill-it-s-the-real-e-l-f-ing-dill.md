# This NEW drop is not a drill... it’s the real e.l.f.ing dill 👀🥒 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s pickle-themed launch email for a 3-piece limited-edition lip balm collection ("Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm" in dill flavors), plus a bonus "Briny Blender" sponge. The pun execution is genuinely fun — "Dill-lulu," "The Real Dill," "Von Dill," "Spicy Lil Dill," "Get Pickled" CTAs, a literal pickle-on-a-fork product shot — and it's the most cohesive, on-brand-personality send in this batch. It's also the most named-product-clear launch in a while: three flavors, each with a shade/finish callout (sheer green, sheer light green with shimmer, red with shimmer), a $9 price on the hero.
- The problem is length and repetition, not concept. The email runs through nearly the same beat five times (green banner → product shot → NEW badge → name → one-line descriptor → button) with almost no visual variance between blocks, so by "Spicy Lil Dill" the eye has already checked out. There's also no urgency or scarcity signal despite "limited-edition new drop" messaging, and the close-up lip/product shots, while stylish, don't clearly show the balm's actual color payoff.
- Compared to 2026-08-19's recycled Bronzing Drops GWP (5/10) and 2026-08-17's Vitamin C comparison send (7/10), this lands closer to the latter — a real launch with real names and a real price — but loses points to redundant structure and a buried, unclear multi-item offer.

## What's working

- Consistent, clever pun theme across subject, hero, and every product name — most personality-forward send in the batch.
- Named products with actual finish/shade detail ("sheer green," "shimmer," "red with shimmer") rather than generic "NEW obsessions" copy.
- Hero clearly states the mechanic and price: "pick(le) of 3... Only $9" is a legible, specific offer.
- No render bugs — clean rendering, no overlapping text or broken images anywhere in the screenshot.
- "Top Picks for You" section at the bottom adds personalization signal with priced, linked products.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical green-banner-plus-product-shot blocks in a row create scroll fatigue; nothing differentiates block 3 from block 4 visually.
- The core offer ("pick 2 of 3 flavors for $9") is stated once at the top and never reinforced near the individual product CTAs, so by the time a reader reaches "Von Dill" they've lost the mechanic.
- "Briny Blender" sponge ($7) is a different product category dropped into the same visual rhythm as the lip balms, muddying the "3 flavors" focus of the hero.
- No urgency/scarcity cue despite "limited-edition" and "back for a limited time" — no countdown, no "while supplies last," nothing time-bound.
- Category nav footer ("NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR") is a wall of gray bars with no visual interest, weak use of prime footer real estate.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the five-block structure to 3: hero + one consolidated "shop all 3 flavors" grid (like the Top Picks module), instead of a full-width repeat for every SKU — this is the single biggest lift, since redundancy is what's killing engagement here.
- 2. Reinforce the $9 multi-buy mechanic at least once more, ideally right above the flavor grid, so it isn't forgotten by the time readers reach the CTAs.
- 3. Move "Briny Blender" into its own short section clearly separated from the lip balm flavors, or cut it from this send entirely to keep the email singularly focused.
- 4. Add a real urgency line ("Only online, only while they last") near the hero CTA to back up "limited-edition" and "back for e.l.f.ing seconds."
- 5. Show one true swatch/before-after shot per flavor instead of moody close-ups — buyers need to see what "sheer green" or "red with shimmer" actually looks like on lips.

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Cosmetics "NEW drop" Campaign

### 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard MJML/BEE-generated table layout with Braze link-tracking redirects; automated QA shows a 27% pass rate driven by broken link resolution, a missing plain-text part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 of 12 probed links fail to resolve** — the Braze redirect wrapper (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) decodes to a **relative path with no host**, not an absolute URL:
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (CTA: "Receipt scanning...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?deep_link_value=...` (CTA: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  - This is a redirect-destination configuration bug, not a client-rendering issue — the wrapper needs an absolute target (e.g., `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/products/...`).
- 29 lower-priority (footer/social/utility) links were deprioritized and not probed per QA scope — not a defect.
- Image asset itself (`braze-images.com/.../original.png`) resolves fine and is separate from the broken CTA redirects.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- One content image is missing `alt` text: `src=".../q/tSo3IDsQrO1s_jLncylozw~~/.../fQfGJm1V2C8bfjkkcimnAQVW0fkNYUqFwguO7VQE5XvkrUj9SPumD"`.
- Header image link (`AAQRxRA~/KVO2Cc...`) does have `alt="e.l.f." title="e.l.f."` — correctly implemented.
- Hidden preheader (`display:none !important` div) is correctly implemented for inbox preview text.
- Responsive `@media (max-width:660px)` breakpoint and `.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes present — standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments included.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found — no merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, Liquid/Handlebars syntax) present in the visible HTML source.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence, but cannot be confirmed from available data; flag for header-level verification at the true SMTP/MIME layer.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) not confirmed present.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from this capture; cannot confirm authentication compliance.
- An in-body unsubscribe link was not visible in the truncated HTML but QA notes "an unsubscribe link is always included" in its footer-link scope — visual/legal footer unsubscribe presence not independently verifiable from the truncated source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Only one broken link shows explicit UTM params (`utm_medium=email` on the app deep-link CTA); the other three broken CTAs show no UTM parameters in their decoded destinations, so campaign attribution cannot be confirmed for those clicks even once the URL resolution bug is fixed.
- Cannot fully assess landing-page alignment since the Shop Now links resolve to relative paths rather than confirmed absolute e.l.f. domains — same root cause as Section 2.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the Braze/link-wrapper redirect config** so all four failing CTAs resolve to absolute URLs (with domain) rather than relative paths — this is blocking "Shop Now," account login, and app-download conversions entirely.
2. **Add a plain-text MIME part** — current plain-text fallback is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and violates multipart best practice.
3. **Add `alt` text** to the image at `fQfGJm1V2C8bfjkkcimnAQVW0fkNYUqFwguO7VQE5XvkrUj9SPumD`.
4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are actually being sent at the SMTP layer (vs. dropped by the AgentMail relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance as of their 2024 policy.
5. **Confirm SPF/DKIM pass** via a direct header capture outside the relay, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observed here.
6. Add consistent UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`) across all CTA links once URLs are fixed, for consistent attribution.
