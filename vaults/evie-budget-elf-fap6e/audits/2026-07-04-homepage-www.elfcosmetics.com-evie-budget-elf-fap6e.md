# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay so the very first thing I see when I open e.l.f.'s site is a cookie consent wall covering the entire screen — like, the brand I literally stan every week at Target, and I can't even see the hero. The top bar is doing some work with the $35 free shipping threshold, and the nav is clean, but the homepage itself is a big fat modal asking me to agree to their data practices before I can shop anything. Not the vibe.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner:** "Free Shipping with all orders $35+! Shop Now" — carousel banner at the very top, clear threshold, that's actually an achievable AOV for me when I stock up on e.l.f. haul day at Target. I see this and I know what I need to spend.
- **Header clarity:** The e.l.f. wordmark, hamburger, search, account, and cart icons are all clean, recognizable, no clutter. On mobile this is exactly what I want — one-tap search if I already know what I need.
- **Cookie modal button hierarchy:** "ACCEPT ALL" is the primary black button, "REJECT NON-ESSENTIAL COOKIES" is outlined — at least they're not hiding the reject option in gray 8px text.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is the entire hero:** The consent wall blocks 100% of the page content below the top banner. I can't see what they're pushing, what's on sale, what the seasonal moment is — nothing. For a brand whose customer is chronically on TikTok and has a three-second attention span, this is a bounce factory.
- **No loyalty hook visible:** I have no idea if I have points, if there's a rewards promo running, if Beautyscape is doing anything — the modal killed that entire section.
- **"Shop By Category" teaser:** I can just barely read those words at the very bottom of the screenshot below the modal — it's doing absolutely nothing for me in this state. It's a ghost.
- **No product imagery above the fold:** I came here because I'm looking for my next e.l.f. Holy Hydration dupe moment, and the page is showing me legal copy. Not one lip, one eye, one concealer swatch.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie consent modal** — let me see the hero for at least one full page scroll before interrupting. Or at minimum use a compact bottom bar instead of a full-screen overlay. Every other cosmetics brand I shop has figured this out.
- **Repeat the free shipping threshold in the hero copy** — since that banner is the only offer visible, double down on it. Make it a pinned strip that persists even after the modal clears so it's always in view while I'm browsing.
- **Surface the loyalty/rewards status in the header** (or just below it) so it's visible even when a modal is up — a small "You have 240 points" pill next to the account icon costs nothing and would make me feel seen.
- **The "Shop By Category" rail below the modal** — if that's the next thing visible after accepting cookies, make those category tiles visually loud (swatches, lifestyle shots, bold label) so the momentum doesn't die after the consent friction.
