# Get Bronzing Drops on us ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a GWP (gift-with-purchase) play — free full-size Bronzing Drops with a $50+ order, "just add shade Pure Gold to your cart." It's a clean, honest offer with a real threshold and no code needed, which reads as trustworthy against this brand's recent bait-y "selling fast" hooks (8/12). But the hero mechanic is buried under a wall of unrelated product pushes (Power Grip Primer, Invisi-Stick SPF, Frizz To Rizz Trio, Lip Oil Stick) that dilute the actual offer into just one more tile in an endless scroll.
- The GWP itself is a smart lever — free product beats a discount for perceived value — and the "GIFT FOR YOU!" badge on the hero is a good touch. The problem is execution: the email reads as a generic weekly catalog with a GWP header slapped on top, not a GWP campaign. Compare to 8/8's "3 steps for stressed skin" (9/10), which stayed tightly scoped to one narrative. This one sprawls across five product categories before recapping "Top Picks" at the bottom.
- Net: a genuinely good offer mechanic wrapped in this brand's usual weekly-digest padding. Solid, not great.

## What's working

- The GWP offer is concrete and low-friction: full-size Bronzing Drops free at $50+, no code, one instruction ("add shade Pure Gold to cart").
- "GIFT FOR YOU!" starburst on the hero product shot draws the eye immediately to the mechanic.
- Clean hero composition — warm gold/bronze tones on black background feel premium and on-brand for a bronzer.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are consistent throughout.
- Second banner ("WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER") reinforces the collab/skincare tie-in without contradicting the GWP message.

## What's weak

- The core offer only gets one screen; five more product sections follow with no thread back to the GWP, so most scrollers never see it as anything but background noise.
- No visible countdown or explicit date for "limited time" — same vague urgency problem flagged in 8/12's send.
- "Top Picks for You" grid at the bottom has tiny, cropped product thumbnails that are hard to identify at a glance.
- Category nav strip (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR) right after Top Picks feels like a homepage footer dropped mid-email, breaking flow before the actual footer.
- Subject promises a specific gift but preview/body give it exactly one screen of real estate — hierarchy doesn't match the subject's promise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the GWP offer plus 2 max complementary product tiles (bronzer application partners like a primer), and move the other 4 unrelated product pushes to a separate send — this is the single highest-impact fix, since right now the offer drowns.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("ends 8/17" or similar) near the CTA to convert "limited time" into credible urgency.
- 3. Repeat the GWP mechanic visually lower in the email (e.g., a reminder banner above Top Picks) so scrollers who skip the hero still see it.
- 4. Enlarge and label the Top Picks thumbnails so they're identifiable without a tap.
- 5. Move or drop the category nav strip — it competes with the footer and adds no send-specific value.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Summary
The email renders via a nested MSO/VML-compatible table layout with Braze/Iterable-style tracked redirect links; several CTA links resolve to unsupported/malformed URL schemes, plain-text fallback and unsubscribe headers are absent, and one image lacks alt text.

## Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 of 12 probed material links fail QA with `unknown url type` errors — the redirect wrapper (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) resolves to relative/malformed destination paths rather than absolute URLs:
  - `Shop Now` → `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`
  - `Shop Now` → `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - `Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.` → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app` → `/pages/mobile-app?is_retargeting=true&lid=f23i6fgaw7c2&utm_source=broadcast&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&deep_link_value` (query string also appears truncated/malformed — missing `=` value and closing)
  - Root cause is consistent with the link-shortener's destination-URL field being populated with a relative path instead of a fully-qualified URL; this will break in any client that doesn't apply the tracking domain's implicit base URL resolution.
- 30 of 42 links were deprioritized/skipped by the prober (footer/utility/social); no data on their validity.
- One open (tracking pixel) redirect confirmed present: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...` used as image `src` for a non-alt-text image (see Rendering section).
