# The e.l.f.ies love NEW e.l.f. POP Tromsø 💖
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a single-theme launch send for e.l.f. POP Tromsø, a limited-edition Spicy Bundt Nice scent. The creative is strong. A dark purple aurora hero with a creator-style portrait sets the mood, and the "come for the… stay for the… fall in love with the…" triptych (spice cake, buttercream frosting, cedarwood) sells a smell through food imagery. That is hard to do in email.
- The send is all story and no deal. There is no discount, no code and no threshold. The only prices are $12–$13 on the products themselves. The black-bar gift offer from the 11/4 send ("Pick your full-size gift on us 💝") is gone, so this reads as a pure launch with less reason to click today. There are also four "Shop e.l.f. POP" or "Shop Now" buttons, which dilutes the focus. The generic "Top Picks for You" row and the empty category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) add length without adding a reason to buy.
- Verdict: a well-art-directed launch email with a weak commercial hook. It will do well with fragrance-curious fans and poorly with anyone who needs an offer. It lands around a 6.

## What's working

- The hero headline "the e.l.f.ies are POP-ping off." is on-brand and clear. The face-first creative gives the eye an obvious place to land.
- The "come for / stay for / fall in love with" triptych makes the scent tangible. The food-textured images and short copy do real work.
- Three product blocks (Silky Body Cream $13, Fragrance Mist $13, Travel Kit $12) each carry a "New & Limited Edition!" badge, a price and a short benefit line.
- Prices at $12–$13 are visible and look easy to say yes to.
- The Tromsø and Norwegian-holiday story fits the season and gives the launch a narrative.

## What's weak

- No promotion is visible: no percent off, no gift with purchase, no free-shipping threshold. The limited-edition label is the only urgency, and it has no date.
- Four near-identical pill CTAs ("Shop e.l.f. POP" twice, "Shop Now" three times) repeat the same action with no variation in copy.
- The "Top Picks for You" row at the bottom is generic: primer, lip liner and lip oil. It has nothing to do with the scent story, and the tiny product images and text are hard to read.
- The six full-width category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) are plain grey and add scroll without value. The email is also very long for what it says.
- The hero creator handle (@riannapepe) is small and carries no review or social proof beyond the portrait.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete offer to the hero, such as a free mini Spicy Bundt Nice mist with a $35 purchase or a launch-week bundle price. That single change would lift clicks the most.
- 2. Cut the repeated CTAs to two, and make them specific: "Smell Spicy Bundt Nice" and "Get the Travel Kit".
- 3. Add a real deadline or scarcity line, such as "limited edition, while it lasts" or an end date.
- 4. Add social proof: a star rating or a review quote on the body cream or mist.
- 5. Remove the category bars and replace "Top Picks for You" with a scent-adjacent set, such as lip balm or a gifting set.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Only 3 of the 12 probed links passed (25% pass rate). The five failures are all relative-path redirect errors from the `links.elfcosmetics.com` click-tracker. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be run because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 probe failures, all `unknown url type`.** Each tracked link on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` resolved to a relative path with no scheme or host:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Baddest+Beige+-+Light+Neutral+Beige` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/glow-reviver-lip-oil?Color=Rose+Envy+-+Rosewood` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?source_caller=ui&pid=Email&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&deep_link_value=…` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause (unconfirmed).** The tracker's redirect probably returns a relative `Location` header, or the probe fails to resolve a relative redirect target. Browsers resolve relative redirects against the tracker host, so recipients may still land correctly. If the tracker host is `links.elfcosmetics.com`, those paths would 404 there. Run a manual `curl -I` on one of these URLs to tell which case applies.
- **Image missing `alt`.** The `<img>` served from `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/MJq_VKUhMn-HXMsNK0wZyA~~/AAQRxRA~/PeQ4fiBs…` has no `alt`. It may be a tracking pixel, in which case use `alt=""`. Otherwise it's a content image that needs descriptive alt text.
- **Not covered by the probe.** 29 links were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This affects web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Empty font name.** There is an invalid `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` declaration on the `mobile_hide` html_block. It falls through to Verdana, so the effect is cosmetic.
- **Missing alt text.** See the image flagged in section 2.
- **Passes.** `lang="en"` is set, layout tables use `role="presentation"`, the viewport meta tag is present, MSO conditional comments are present, and the hidden preheader uses `display:none`/`mso-hide:all`. The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook falls back correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `${}`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Check the raw headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Nothing can be concluded either way.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link or postal address. The probe always includes an unsubscribe link, but its result isn't listed in the output provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** Wrapped tracker URLs hide the destinations, and the resolved paths contain no `utm_*` parameters (`pid=Email` appears only on the app link).
- **Variant deep-links are intact.** The product URLs carry `Size=` and `Color=` selectors, so they should land on the intended variant if they resolve.
- **Account login link.** `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` sends recipients to a login wall, not to a content page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on the five failing tracker URLs to see whether the redirect `Location` is relative. If so, make the ESP/tracker emit absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/…` targets.
2. Fix the QA probe to resolve relative redirects against the previous hop, to avoid false positives on this sender.
3. Add a `<title>` and `alt` text (or `alt=""` for a tracking pixel) to the flagged image.
4. Remove the empty `font-family:''` declaration.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` against the original message headers, not the relay copy.
6. Confirm the destination URLs carry UTM parameters.
7. Plain text is more than 70% URLs (11,814 of 13,082 chars) because of the long tracker links. Shorten the tracker links, or add more descriptive copy to the text part, to reduce spam-filter risk.
