This is a stronger, more focused execution than the sprawling "e.l.f. - Where it loses points: there's no visible price anywhere in the hero or shade cascade, and "CLICK TO SLICK" is a cute pun but a genuinely ambiguous CTA — a reader can't tell if it adds to cart, opens a shade picker, or just goes to a PDP. - Solid, on-brand, visually confident send — but it's withholding the one piece of info (price) that consistently drives clicks in this brand's stronger emails.

## What's working

- The hero shot is genuinely striking: four shimmer sticks fanned on a soft pink gradient with visible sparkle detail on the product tips.
- Shade cascade uses full-bleed close-up swatches with legible name + description for each of the 4 shades — easy to scan on mobile.
- Copy is tight and benefit-led ("Glide on buildable color with addictive slip") without over-explaining.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sell section (Power Grip Primer, Setting Spray, Lip Liner) is relevant and price-tagged, giving at least one place in the email where price appears.
- No render bugs — clean alignment, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere for the hero product — a shopper has to click through just to see the cost.
- "CLICK TO SLICK" is repeated twice but doesn't say what happens next (shop now? pick a shade? add to cart?).
- No urgency or launch-window framing ("new" is stated but there's no limited-time or first-to-try hook).
- No social proof (ratings, review count, "bestseller") anywhere in the hero or shade section.
- Category nav block (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR) is plain gray bars with no imagery — a missed chance for visual interest this far down the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add the price directly under the hero headline or CTA — this is the single biggest lever; every stronger-performing e.l.f. send in this brand history led with a visible price hook ($9, $6, $12).
- 2. Change CTA copy from "CLICK TO SLICK" to something concrete like "SHOP THE SHIMMER STICK" or "SHOP NOW — $X" so intent is unambiguous.
- 3. Add a small "NEW" badge or launch-week callout near the hero to create urgency without needing a discount.
- 4. Consider adding a one-line review snippet or star rating under the hero if available — this brand's sends consistently lack social proof.
