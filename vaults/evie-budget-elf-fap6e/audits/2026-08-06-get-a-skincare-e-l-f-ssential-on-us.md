# Get a skincare e.l.f.ssential on us 😍 
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN's BOGO play — buy 2 skin e.l.f.ssentials, get 1 free with code REFRESH — and it's a fine mechanic buried in a flat execution. The hero banner (bottle + water droplets, "ONLINE ONLY" badge) leads into the offer headline, which is good sequencing, but everything after that collapses into a repetitive scroll of product photo → name → price → black "SHOP NOW" pill, four times in a row with almost no color variation.
- Compared to recent e.l.f. sends in the brand history — the chartreuse "FROM FRIZZ TO RIZZ" hair launch (8/10) or the shade-matched Set It Bright grid (9/10) — this one lacks a distinct visual hook. It reads more like a catalog page than a campaign moment.
- The offer itself is legible and the code is visible, which keeps this out of "confusing" territory, but there's no real urgency (just "for a limited time," no date) and no loyalty-tier callout. Solid, forgettable send — mid-pack for this brand.

## What's working

- BOGO offer and promo code (REFRESH) are stated clearly right under the hero, above the fold.
- Each product gets a dedicated section with name, price, one-line benefit copy, and a clear CTA button.
- "new!" badges flag the two newer SKUs (Hypochlorous Mist, Glass Slick Serum Stick) so repeat subscribers can spot what's unseen.
- "find your skincare" quiz block adds a personalization hook mid-email.
- "Top Picks for You" section with visible pricing gives a quick-scan alternative to the full scroll.

## What's weak

- No color blocking or hero treatment differentiates this from a plain product listing — every section uses the same white background and identical black pill button, unlike prior high-scoring e.l.f. sends that leaned into bold color per section.
- Urgency is vague ("for a limited time") with no actual deadline or date, which undercuts the BOGO's motivational pull.
- No loyalty/Beauty Squad member pricing or points messaging shown despite the brand having a loyalty program footer link.
- Six SHOP NOW buttons in a row create decision fatigue rather than a single clear next action.
- No social proof (ratings, review counts) anywhere in the product cards.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the BOGO offer ("ends Sunday 8/9") — vague urgency is the single biggest miss here and costs both open and click likelihood.
- 2. Break the monotony with alternating color blocks per product section (mirror the chartreuse treatment from the 7/30 hair launch) so the eye has landing points beyond the top.
- 3. Surface loyalty pricing or Beauty Squad points earn on at least one product card — the footer already promotes the app, so tie it into the offer.
- 4. Cut the product count from four to two or three, and make one visually dominant "hero SKU" instead of a flat list.
- 5. Add a star rating or review count under at least the two "new!" products to build trust for unproven SKUs.

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA passed 27% of checks (5 failures, 3 warnings), driven primarily by four unresolved destination links and missing email-authentication/plain-text infrastructure.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken destination links** — all Braze-wrapped (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) links resolve to **relative, schemeless paths** rather than absolute URLs, causing "unknown url type" failures:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` (link text: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` (link text: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (link text: "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_xp=email&utm_campaign=080626_backtoschool_skin_offer&utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&pid=Email&af_dp=com` (link text: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
