# NEW! 🍊 This just peels so bright
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean single-product launch for the Bright Appeel Daily Peel Pads. The hero is a bright orange portrait with a "NEW!" sticker and the headline "WE REINVENTED THE PEEL." The email then walks through "2 steps to peel your best," with Step 1 (resurface) and Step 2 (brighten and soothe), and closes on "30 derm-developed, two-step treatments. $15." The creative is strong and the product story is easy to follow. The orange palette matches the 🍊 in the subject.
- The weakness is the order of information. The $15 price sits about 40% of the way down the email, below two long image-and-copy blocks. Above that point the reader gets ingredients (5% Tri-Acid Complex, Vitamin C, 10% aloe juice) and a small "Peel for smoother feels" button. The Beauty Squad "Surprise Reward Drop" ($10 off with $25+, this weekend only) is the real conversion lever. It is buried under the product story, followed by a skincare quiz, a Black Friday-style gift block, "Top Picks for You" and a category nav that renders as empty gray bars.
- Compared with the 11/08 travel trio email, which led with the headline and price and scored 7, this one is prettier but less direct. It is a good launch email that undersells its own offer. I'd call it a solid 7 on targeting, with a weak inbox presence.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and on-brand, with a clear "NEW!" sticker and a confident headline.
- The "2 steps to peel your best" structure makes the routine easy to scan.
- Ingredient callouts (5% Tri-Acid Complex, Vitamin C, 10% aloe juice) give real product detail.
- The $15 price for 30 treatments is clear and honest once you reach it.
- The purple Beauty Squad reward block is a strong loyalty hook, with "$10 OFF" and "no points needed" visible.
- The orange palette is consistent from the hero through the product shots, and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- The price is not visible at the top. A reader has to scroll past the hero and two steps to find "$15."
- There are many competing blocks below the product story: the reward drop, "find your skincare," a full-size gift block and "Top Picks for You." They dilute the single launch message.
- The CTAs are small and generic ("SHOP NOW" repeated). The hero button, "Peel for smoother feels," is branded but doesn't say what you get.
- The category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) look like blank gray strips, and the Top Picks images are tiny.
- There are no reviews or social proof for a new product, and the footer fine print is dense.
- The brand is sending almost daily (11/05, 11/06, 11/07, 11/08, 11/09, 11/11, now 11/13). Fatigue is a real risk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price and the reward in the hero: "Bright Appeel pads, $15 + $10 off $25 this weekend." Move the purple reward block directly under the hero.
- 2. Cut the email to one idea. Drop the quiz, the gift block and the category nav, or move them below a clear single CTA.
- 3. Make the CTA specific and full-width, such as "Shop Bright Appeel Pads – $15." Use one button style throughout.
- 4. Add a star rating or a short review quote near the price.
- 5. Fix the category nav with real icons or images, and enlarge the Top Picks thumbnails.
- 6. Spread out the send cadence, or segment so frequent openers don't get daily sends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard role="presentation" table layout with a hidden preheader, and 4 of the 12 probed links failed in QA. The failures all share one signature (a relative redirect target), so they may be a probe artifact rather than customer-facing breakage. The HTML is truncated and the auth and unsubscribe headers weren't captured, so compliance and authentication can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The tracked redirects on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` resolved to site-relative paths:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/blemish-breakthrough-triple-acid-resurfacing-serum-1?Size=Regular+0.94+oz+%2F+28+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&af_xp=email&lid=sg4o1wvf1bqh&source_caller=ui&shortlink=email&af_reengage` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause, not confirmed.** The prober can't resolve a relative `Location` header or hop. A browser would resolve it against the final host and probably work, but if the `Location` is returned directly from `links.elfcosmetics.com`, it would resolve against the tracking domain and 404. Re-test with a real client or a manual `curl -sIL` on one link to settle it.
- **The Shop Now URLs carry a `?Size=...` variant parameter.** This is worth checking, because a stale or invalid variant value can land users on a default or out-of-stock state.
- **The app-download link is truncated** at `af_reengage` with no value, which may be a probe or log truncation. The `af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app://home` deep link is also a Shopify `/pages/mobile-app` fallback, so it depends on AppsFlyer handling the redirect.
- **32 links were skipped by the prober**, so coverage is partial.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** The `<title>` element is present but empty.
- **Empty font-family value:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in a `mobile_hide` block. The empty-string family is invalid and falls through to Verdana, so it's harmless but sloppy.
- **Missing alt text** on the image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/ktyshhU-WBX...`. The `/q/` path is most likely the open-tracking pixel, so the real accessibility impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would still be correct.
- **Weak alt on the hero.** The linked banner uses `alt="e.l.f."` (the brand name), which doesn't describe the link destination.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`** is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and falls back to Arial, so it's safe. Many clients strip it anyway.
- **No issues** with `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the `mobile_hide`/`desktop_hide` pattern.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FNAME]` appear. The source is truncated, so the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender is `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com`, a subdomain, so check that its DNS records are aligned.
- **The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link were not visible** in the truncated source. The QA run reports that an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't listed among the failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be verified.** All links are opaque tracked redirects (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<token>`), so UTMs, if any, are added server-side after the redirect.
- **Landing alignment is unverified for the 4 failed links.** The 2 product CTAs (Holy Hydration Thirst Burst Drops, Blemish Breakthrough serum) are the ones that failed. The hero image banner links, which are the primary CTA, were not among the failures listed.
- **The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` landing** sends the "Receipt scanning" link to a login wall. That is expected for a rewards feature, but it adds friction for anyone who isn't logged in.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 4 failed links with a real browser or `curl -sIL` to determine whether the relative `Location` is a probe artifact or a real redirect bug on `links.elfcosmetics.com`. Fix the prober to resolve relative redirects against the previous hop.
2. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers on the raw message, bypassing the AgentMail relay, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
3. Add a `<title>` value and fix the empty `font-family:''`. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and give the hero image descriptive alt text.
4. Validate that the `?Size=` variant parameters on the product links map to live variants.
5. Extend QA to capture the full HTML (not truncated) so the footer, unsubscribe and address can be audited. Make the plain-text URL ratio check treat tracked-link length separately, since 84% URL content (12946/15430 chars) comes from the long wrapped URLs.
