# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay wait, this is *exactly* the energy e.l.f. always has — affordable, Gen Z-coded, a little cheeky. They're pushing "The Cream Team," a new cream blush and bronzer situation for $7 each, and the whole page feels like a TikTok get-ready-with-me thumbnail. It's clearly a beauty brand that knows its customer.

## What's working

- **The $5-off mascara banner** — top of the page, blue strip, zero ambiguity. I see it before I see the logo. That's exactly the kind of thing that makes me stay.
- **"$7 each!" in the hero copy** — e.l.f. knows its brand is the price. Naming the number in the hero is the right call. I don't have to go hunting for it.
- **The model** — young woman, warm skin tone, curly brown hair, gold hoops, applying a cream stick to her cheek. That is so specifically *my* aesthetic coded person. I trust her face.
- **Beauty Squad card above the fold** — free shipping on your first order is a genuine incentive and it's not buried. Smart placement.
- **"e.l.f.ing iconic together"** — the copy register is correct. It's playful without being cringe. I'd say that.

## What's weak

- **"STICK AROUND" CTA** — I get it, it's a stick pun, very cute, but it does not tell me what happens when I tap it. "Shop The Cream Team" would close me faster.
- **Right-side card is clipped** — whatever "Scan... when y... Squad..." says is half off-screen. Even if it's a carousel, I'm not going to swipe to read the rest. That value prop is lost on me.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — no star ratings, no "100K+ sold," no TikTok Made Me Buy It badge. For a brand whose credibility lives on community, this is a miss.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only on mobile** — I can't see any category shortcuts. If I want eye products instead of blush, I have to tap the menu and start hunting.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STICK AROUND" for "Shop The Cream Team — $7 Each"** — keep the pun in the subhead, make the button actually descriptive. You'll capture impulse clickers who don't read body copy.
- **Add a TikTok-style social proof strip** — one line under the hero: "2M+ sold on TikTok" or a star rating count. That's the trust signal that converts my demographic.
- **Surface 2–3 category shortcuts below the loyalty cards** — Eyes / Lips / Skin as tappable chips, so I can reroute without opening the hamburger menu.
- **Reveal the clipped right card** — either full-width it or stack the two cards vertically so the "Scan" offer is readable without a swipe.
