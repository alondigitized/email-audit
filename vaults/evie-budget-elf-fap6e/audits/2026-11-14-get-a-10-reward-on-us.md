# 💜 Get a $10 reward on us!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, offer-first members send. The purple "SURPRISE REWARD DROP!" hero states a concrete $10 off reward with a $25+ minimum, and the offer is readable in about two seconds. The gift graphic, the green "$10 OFF" burst and the black "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" button give a clear hierarchy. It reads as the same "Surprise Reward Drop" beat as the 11/07 lip balm send, with cash off in place of a freebie.
- The body is weaker. A second offer, the "choose a full-size gift on us" block with a $35+ threshold, competes with the reward. The "Top Picks for You" row has three products, and one of them is a repeat of the primer pushed in the 11/11 "Buy a grip" send. Below that, six empty gray category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) look like images that failed to load. The email ends with a long run of legal text.
- Overall it is a solid, honest reward send that stalls in the lower half. A tighter second half would move it from average to good.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Redeem a FREE $10 off reward when making a purchase of $25+. No points needed."
- The green "$10 OFF" burst and the gift graphic make the offer the first thing the eye lands on.
- The weekend deadline is credible. "THIS WEEKEND ONLY" is backed by "Reward expires 11/15."
- The black "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" button has strong contrast against the purple-to-pink gradient.
- "Not a member? Join now – it's free." gives non-members a way in.

## What's weak

- The six gray category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) have no imagery and read like empty placeholders or broken tiles. They add scroll without adding appeal.
- Two offers compete: the $10 reward at a $25 minimum and the free full-size gift at a $35 minimum. The thresholds differ, and the stacking rules are only explained in the fine print.
- The sign-in step adds friction. The email gives no preview of what the reward applies to.
- The "Top Picks for You" row is thin. It has three products, no reviews and no sizing or shade detail.
- The footer is crowded with free shipping, TikTok Shop, receipt scanning, Shop app, app downloads and socials. The long legal block repeats the terms from the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or remove the gray category bars. Replace them with image tiles, or cut them and put the top picks directly under the reward hero.
- 2. Pick one offer per send. Lead with the $10 reward and move the gift-with-purchase to a single line under the picks, or hold it for another send.
- 3. Add one line showing how the reward stacks, for example "$25 cart → $10 off → $15 total." That makes the value concrete.
- 4. Make the top picks more personal. Show real reviews or a "back in stock" cue, and avoid repeating the primer from 11/11.
- 5. Trim the footer to the app download and socials, and move the dense terms below a single link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$10 off $25, no points needed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sign in, add $25 to your bag, and the discount applies at checkout. Ends 11/15.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free full-size gift when you spend $35+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The e.l.f. email is built from nested table markup with tracked redirect links. The QA run failed 5 of 12 probed links, but all five failures share one cause, a relative `Location` header the probe couldn't resolve. That points to a probe limitation more than a confirmed customer-facing break. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so they are unverified rather than confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe failures:** 5 of 12 probed links failed with `unknown url type`. All 5 are `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirects whose target is a **relative path**, not an absolute URL:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Jumbo+2.5+oz+%2F+75+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer-4-niacinamide?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&shortlink=email&utm_medium=email&pid=Email&source_caller=ui&af_xp=email&c=email&af_dp=` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause:** Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header against the current host, so real users probably land correctly. The probe's HTTP client does not. I can't confirm the links work for users, so treat this as a QA tooling gap to verify manually.
- **Possible real issue:** The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` target sends a receipt-scanning CTA to a login wall. That is worth checking for signed-out recipients.
- **App link:** The `/pages/mobile-app` URL ends in a bare `af_dp=` with an empty deep-link value. The QA output is cut off at that point, so I can't tell whether the value is truly empty or just truncated.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 links were probed and 27 skipped. Footer, social and utility links were deprioritized, so their status is unknown.
- **Tracking pixel:** `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/LBcAcUb2...` appears to be the open-tracking pixel. See section 3 for its missing alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** One image has no `alt`: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/LBcAcUb2XubyV54FesTw4A~~/...`. It is probably the tracking pixel, so `alt=""` is the correct fix (with `width="1" height="1"`).
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invalid font-family:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` (in the `mobile_hide` block) has an empty quoted family name. It is harmless because it falls back to Verdana, but it is invalid CSS.
- **Hero image alt:** The linked hero image has `alt="e.l.f."`, which is non-descriptive, though present.
- **Good practices confirmed:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - MSO conditional comments are in place.
  - The preheader is hidden correctly.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML source was truncated, so the rest of the body can't be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The sender domain `t.elfcosmetics.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign, but nothing here confirms alignment.
- **Footer content:** The physical address and unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and no unsubscribe failure was reported. I can't verify the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM visibility:** All links go through an opaque click-tracking redirect, so UTM parameters on the destination can't be verified from the source. Only the app link shows `utm_medium=email` (inside its AppsFlyer-style params), with no visible `utm_source` or `utm_campaign` on it.
- **Landing targets:** The product URLs carry `?Size=...` variant parameters. That is good for landing on the correct variant, but it also depends on the redirect resolving correctly (see section 2).
- **Preheader alignment:** The preheader states "Minimum purchase of $25. This weekend only!". The subject promises a "$10 reward". I can't check landing-page alignment because no landing pages were resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Resolve the 5 relative-redirect failures manually:** Follow each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` URL in a real browser or `curl -L` with relative `Location` handling. Then fix the probe to resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host (`urljoin`).
2. **Check the account link:** Confirm that the `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` receipt-scanning link makes sense for signed-out users.
3. **Check the app link:** Confirm whether `af_dp=` is empty by design.
4. **Verify the compliance headers:** Check the original message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
5. **Fix the markup:** Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, populate `<title>`, and remove the empty `''` font-family.
6. **Consider the plain-text part:** It is 83% URLs (11457 of 13779 chars) because of the long tracked links. Shorter redirect tokens or fewer links in the plain-text part would reduce the deliverability risk.
7. **Cover more links:** Extend probing to the footer and social links, since 27 links went unchecked.
