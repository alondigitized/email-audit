# Grip & spray your own way ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a setting-spray launch that reads as a clear, well-organized product lineup. The hero shows the new Gold Drip Setting Spray bottle on a gold field. Below it is a "PICK YOUR POWER GRIP" ladder of three finishes: luminous, dewy and glassy. Each finish gets a model shot, one line of copy, a $11 price and its own SHOP NOW button. The "choose your finish" framing is a smart way to sell three SKUs at one price.
- The email loses focus in the lower half. A second burgundy promo, the Oct 14 birthday-cake lip balm ("This lippie takes the cake"), interrupts the spray story. A "Top Picks for You" row follows, and then a block of grey category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR). The lip balm promo is a leftover from the Oct 14 send, so a subscriber who got both will see the same creative twice in three days. The hero CTA, "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS," is vague. The copy never says what status you unlock.
- Judgement: a solid product email with good photography and a clear structure. It's let down by a cluttered footer half, a cryptic hero CTA and a repeated lip balm offer. About a 6.

## What's working

- The gold hero with the bottle front and center and a "new!" badge makes the launch obvious at a glance.
- The "PICK YOUR POWER GRIP" structure gives a reason to scroll and lets shoppers self-segment by finish.
- Every product has a clear price ($11), a one-line benefit and its own SHOP NOW button.
- The model photography is bright and varied, and the teal and pink backgrounds make each product feel distinct.
- The buy-3-get-the-4th-free lip balm offer is a concrete incentive, and the deadline appears in the fine print.

## What's weak

- "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS" doesn't say where it leads or what it means. It sits above the real product ladder and may confuse readers.
- The burgundy cake lip balm block repeats the Oct 14 send, "This new lippie flav takes the cake 🎂". It splits attention in a spray email.
- The six grey category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) take up a lot of vertical space and have no imagery. They look like unfinished navigation.
- "Top Picks for You" shows a primer, a lip liner and a spray with no reason given for the picks. The tiny SHOP NOW buttons are hard to tap.
- The offer fine print is tiny and sits at the very bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS" with "Shop Gold Drip Spray – $11". Tie it to the one product shown in the hero.
- 2. Remove the cake lip balm block, or move it into its own send. A clean spray story would convert better.
- 3. Collapse the six category bars into one slim row of text links, or cut them.
- 4. Make "Top Picks for You" spray-related: add a mini-size spray, a primer pairing and a bundle.
- 5. Add a visible bundle or value hook, such as "Buy 2 sprays, get a mini free", to give the $11 price some urgency.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well-structured (table layout with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, preheader). The QA run reports four link failures, a 0-character plain-text part, and several headers it couldn't capture. Several of these are probably relay or probe artifacts rather than defects in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The tracked links on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` resolved to a relative path instead of an absolute URL:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_medium=email&onelink_id=wTtZ&shortlink=email&utm_campaign=101626_pg_settingspray&is_retargeting=true&af_dp=co…` ("Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause:** the click-tracker's redirect returns a relative `Location` value, and the probe didn't resolve it against the site's origin. Browsers resolve relative redirects, so the links probably work for recipients. I can't confirm that from this data, and the final destinations were never fetched.
- **Fix:** have the ESP emit absolute `Location` URLs, or re-probe with relative redirects resolved.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 42 links were probed. The other 30 (footer, social) are unverified.
- **Truncated URL:** the app deep-link is cut off at `af_dp=co`. This is a QA-output truncation, not necessarily a defect in the email.
- **Missing alt text:** one image has no `alt`. Its `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/kDqAjgyhgf9AySPqTZzO_w~~/…`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking pixel, in which case `alt=""` is the correct value. Low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty. Minor, but it affects the web-view and some clients' accessible name.
- **Invalid font-family:** the `mobile_hide` block uses `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty font name is harmless because the fallback applies, but it signals a template-builder default.
- **Web font:** Open Sans loads via a Google Fonts `<link>` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Many clients strip this, and `Arial,Sans-serif` is the declared fallback. This is acceptable.
- **Nested tables:** the layout nests a `.nl-container` table inside an `html_block`, which adds weight and nesting depth. It isn't a defect.
- **Strengths:** `role="presentation"` is used throughout, the MSO conditional comments are valid, and the logo has `alt="e.l.f."`. The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none; mso-hide:all`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `${}`, `%%`).

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part:** `[FAIL] Text version is 0 chars`. This is a multipart gap that can raise spam-filter scores and hurts accessibility. It may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so check the raw send.
- **Missing headers:** `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes the relay may drop them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify on the original message, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is absent, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can't be assessed. The From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain is `links.elfcosmetics.com`, which is aligned in organizational domain.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated portion. I can't confirm either from the data provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM coverage:** the only visible UTM set is on the app link (`utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=101626_pg_settingspray`, `onelink_id`, `af_dp`). UTMs on the product links can't be verified because their final URLs never resolved.
- **Product deep links:** these carry variant parameters (`Size=…`, `Color=…`), which is good practice. Confirm the variants exist and are in stock.
- **Login gate:** the receipt-scanning link goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Logged-out users land on a login wall, not on the feature.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four failed links with relative redirects resolved, and confirm they return 200 on `elfcosmetics.com`.
2. Check the original message (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`.
3. Add a text/plain alternative. A 0-char part is the one deliverability finding that is probably real.
4. Fill in `<title>`, remove the empty `font-family:''`, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Probe more than 12 of the 42 links, including the footer and the unsubscribe URL.
