# Give the gift of e.l.f. & save BIG 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a weekend promo that tries to carry two offers at once. The top half pushes "the more e.l.f., the merrier," a Build Your Own Kit bundle with up to 20% off. Below it, a purple "Surprise Reward Drop!" block offers a free Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm with a $5+ purchase. The pink product hero is strong, with glossy tubes against a hot-pink gradient and an "OMG value!" sticker. But the headline never states the discount, and the subject's "save BIG" is vague. The only real percentage ("up to 20%") is in the fine print at the bottom.
- The free-gift offer is the better deal, and it's buried in the middle. It is also gated: it only applies to Beauty Squad members who sign in, and "Join now – it's free" sits right under the button. Two stacked "SHOP NOW" buttons in the first screens go to a bundle page without telling you what a bundle costs or contains.
- The lower half drifts into a generic template. Top Picks has three products ($11, $6, $2), then six gray category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR), then a values block and an app/footer stack. Next to the 2026-11-04 "Pick your full-size gift on us" send, which had a clear free-gift hook, this one is murkier. I'd call it average.

## What's working

- The hero product image is bold and on-brand, and the "OMG value!" sticker adds energy.
- The free Glow Reviver lip balm with a $5+ purchase is a low-barrier, concrete offer.
- The "Not a member? Join now – it's free" line catches non-members instead of excluding them.
- The Top Picks row shows prices ($11, $6, $2) with individual SHOP NOW buttons, so the products are easy to scan.
- The footer shows free shipping, receipt scanning and app download, which reinforce loyalty.

## What's weak

- The headline "the more e.l.f., the merrier" gives no number, and the 20% discount appears only in the fine print.
- The two offers compete: a bundle discount in the first two blocks and a free gift in the third. Nothing says which one matters.
- Two identical "SHOP NOW" buttons stacked in the first screens add no information.
- Six large gray category bars take up a lot of vertical space and are plain text with no imagery.
- The free-gift terms (members only, sign-in required, $5+ cart minimum, ends 11/10) are in tiny footer text, and "this weekend only" in the block is vague.
- The Top Picks products are cheap items that don't relate to the bundle or the free gift, so "Top Picks for You" doesn't look personalized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one number in the hero: "Build a kit, save 20%," with the free lip balm as a visible second line. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Move the free-gift block above the pink hero, or merge the two, so the strongest offer is seen first.
- 3. Replace the duplicate hero CTA with a specific one, like "Build my kit."
- 4. Swap the gray category bars for a compact row of icons or images, or cut them.
- 5. Add a deadline to the visible copy ("Ends Monday 11/10") rather than only in the fine print.
- 6. Make Top Picks show what's in the bundle or the gift-eligible products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off kits + a free lip balm 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Build a kit, save 20% (ends Monday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm with any $5+ order for Beauty Squad.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Online only: bundle your besties' favorites and unlock your free gift.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-built template (braze-images.com assets) with click-wrapped links on `links.elfcosmetics.com`. Four of the 12 probed material links failed in QA, and the empty `<title>` is a real source defect. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probed links failed with `unknown url type`.** Each tracked URL resolved to a scheme-less, host-less path, which an HTTP client can't follow:
  - `/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=Pink+Positive+-+sheer+pink` ("Shop Now", via `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/xhgeM4j0kvBZU3NO20KFuA~~/...`)
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now", via `.../ulZyJYRjhu1B4WYw7oSRPA~~/...`)
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (receipt-scanning rewards link, via `.../f-SkrGMo-yrlnx7FkxWS5Q~~/...`)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&c=email&onelink_id=wTtZ&is_retargeting=true&pid=Email&utm_source=broadcast&utm_campaig...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app", via `.../xTW3-rp7DbyHmbiLBBS_qA~~/...`)
- **The cause is unconfirmed.** Either the click-tracker returns a relative `Location` header, or the QA probe mishandles relative redirects. The same wrapper domain worked for other links, so the probe is a plausible culprit. Verify by hand with `curl -I` on one of these URLs. If the `Location` header is relative, real clients will usually resolve it against the tracking host (`links.elfcosmetics.com`). That would send users to a wrong or 404 page instead of `elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Pattern in the failures.** The failing links are product, account and app-download deep links. The two product links share the same "Shop Now" text, and they point at different shades (`Pink Positive`, `Mauve Aside`).
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/AXGo0epvYVcLU10Imi5N_Q~~/AAQRxRA~/...` is the open-tracking pixel. It is unremarkable except for the missing `alt` (see section 3).
- **Coverage gap.** 27 links were skipped by design, mostly footer and social, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is confirmed in the source. It gives web-view and browser-tab rendering no title and is poor for accessibility.
- **Missing `alt`** on the tracking-pixel image (`.../q/AXGo0epvYVcLU10Imi5N_Q~~/...`). It is low impact if the image is 1×1, but add `alt=""`.
- **Invalid font-family declaration:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` on an `html_block` div. It falls back to Verdana, so the impact is minimal.
- **Redundant nesting.** The `html_block` wrappers embed full `nl-container` / `row-content` tables inside outer ones. Both layers use the same class names, and the inner table is hard-coded to `width:640px` (`width="640"`). It stacks with `.stack .column`, so this is only a potential issue.
- **Mobile-hidden block.** `row-1 mobile_hide` wraps an empty `html_block` div. No content is lost, but it is dead markup.
- **Positives:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - The header image has `alt="e.l.f."`.
  - A hidden preheader exists.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). The HTML was cut off, so the body past the truncation point was not reviewed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the sender (`makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com`). Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure. Verify from the original message headers or a seed-list test.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** The truncated HTML doesn't show them, so they can't be confirmed. QA always probes an unsubscribe link and reported no unsubscribe failure.
- **Plain text is more than 70% URLs** (11,456 of 13,673 chars, about 84%). The long click-tracking URLs inflate the text part. This is a spam-filter and readability risk for text-only clients.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM on the app link only.** The only visible campaign parameters are on the app-download link: `c=email`, `pid=Email`, `utm_source=broadcast`, `onelink_id=wTtZ`, `is_retargeting=true`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`. `utm_campaig...` is cut off, so the campaign value can't be verified.
- **Product links unconfirmed.** The two product links (`/products/...?Color=...`) carry no UTM parameters in the QA-captured paths. Whether the redirect appends them is unknown because of the relative-URL failure above.
- **Landing pages untested.** Because the redirects failed, the landing pages for the failing links were not tested.
- **Page-name mismatch.** The `/pages/mobile-app` path is a Shopify-style page route. It is worth confirming that it resolves on the final host.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually `curl -IL` the four failing tracked URLs. If the `Location` header is relative, fix the click-tracker or link configuration to emit absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URLs. If the redirects are fine, treat this as a QA-probe artifact and make the probe resolve relative `Location` headers.
2. Fill in `<title>` (for example, with the subject line).
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and fix the empty `font-family:''`.
4. Check the raw sender-side message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Don't treat the relay-induced WARNs as sender defects until verified.
5. Confirm that the product links carry UTM tags after redirect, and keep the app link's `utm_campaign` consistent with the rest of the email.
6. Shorten or reduce the tracked URLs in the plain-text part to bring the URL ratio down.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address, and merge tokens.
