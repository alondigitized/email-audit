# Brightness you can C ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN's strongest value-comparison send of the week: "the $185 alternative" vs. "$17" for a Vitamin C + E + Ferulic Serum, dramatized with an actual balance-scale product shot and two named creators ("Same Glow" vs. "Less Dough"). It's a sharper hook than the "Set It Bright" powder comparison from 2026-08-26 because it uses two competing images (scale, then side-by-side faces) to reinforce the same claim three times before the reader even scrolls to "Shop Now" — that's disciplined creative for this brand.
- Where it drops off is everything after the hero. The GWP banner (free Bronzing Drops with $50+ orders), the "Top Picks for You" row, and six flat gray category bars all feel bolted onto what should have been a single-product spotlight, diluting the strong opening. The nav bars in particular are a visual dead zone — no icons, no imagery, just stacked gray rectangles that add scroll length without adding persuasion.
- Overall this is a well-executed hero with a cluttered back half — a 6.5 send that could be a 7.5-8 if the body were trimmed by half.

## What's working

- The "$185 alternative... but for only $17" comparison is concrete, specific, and instantly graspable — the strongest value hook of this brand's recent run.
- The literal balance-scale product photo visually reinforces the price/value claim rather than just stating it.
- Two real UGC-style faces with named captions ("Veronica," "Vanessa") plus a handwritten-style signature add authenticity and social proof.
- Clear, high-contrast CTA buttons ("BRIGHT THIS WAY," "SHOP NOW") appear twice before any scroll fatigue sets in.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly with consistent orange/cream brand color.

## What's weak

- The email is long: after the strong hero and comparison, it stacks a second offer (Bronzing Drops GWP), a cross-sell grid (Top Picks), and six category nav bars — three distinct asks competing with the single hero message.
- The six gray "NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR" bars are pure text-on-gray with zero visual interest, a big flat stretch that will read as filler on mobile.
- The GWP module ("get Bronzing Drops on us") is a full unrelated product tangent — bronzer has nothing to do with a Vitamin C serum email and splits attention right when the reader should be clicking through on the hero offer.
- "Top Picks for You" isn't personalized in any visible way (same three hydration products anyone would see), so the "for You" framing oversells what's actually a generic cross-sell.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half: end it after "SHOP NOW" following the Same Glow/Less Dough comparison. The GWP banner, Top Picks, and six nav bars can be their own send or a footer link, not stacked here.
- 2. If the GWP offer must stay, move it above the category nav grid and give it a stronger visual tie-in (e.g., "complete your Vitamin C routine" framing) rather than an unrelated bronzer detour.
- 3. Replace the flat gray nav bars with a compact single-row icon or thumbnail strip — same navigation function, far less scroll weight.
- 4. Add a "$168 saved" or "90% less" callout near the price to make the value math even more skimmable at a glance.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Same glow, way less dough — Vitamin C serum, $17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Vitamin C + E + Ferulic — dermstore dupe energy, drugstore price.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email built on standard Braze/e.l.f. redirect infrastructure (`links.elfcosmetics.com`) renders correctly at the markup level, but 4 of 5 probed product/account CTAs resolve to malformed shortlink targets and the plain-text MIME part is missing entirely.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken CTA redirects** confirmed by QA probe — the shortlink targets decode to relative paths instead of absolute URLs, causing "unknown url type" errors:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` (link text: "Shop Now" and "Thirst Burst Drops Regular" — same broken destination reused twice)
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` (link text: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (link text: "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?source_caller=ui&c=email&utm_source=broadcast&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&af_reengagement_window=30d&...` (link text: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app") — this one also has a truncated/malformed query string (trailing `&` with no value, unencoded `://` in `af_dp`)
  - Only 12 of 38 total links were probed (26 skipped per QA scope rules); actual breakage rate across the full link set is unverified.
- All sampled CTAs route through the Braze `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click-tracking redirector, consistent with the platform's standard tracking wrapper — no evidence of tracking-pixel or redirector misconfiguration itself.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text**: image asset `nouu1ZzO0cmlWEcHNBKWf3VYIaqMdQ3GPCFc2D-bT05pagjf52` (served via `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...` tracking-pixel path) has no `alt` attribute.
- Hero banner image (`braze-images.com/.../6537d6e7f775160058956f5a/original.png`) does have `alt="e.l.f."` — acceptable but generic.
- MSO conditional comments, `xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o` namespaces, and `mso-hide` rules are present and correctly structured for Outlook desktop rendering.
- Responsive `@media (max-width:660px)` block and `.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes are present and correctly paired.
- Preheader text uses standard hidden-div technique (`display:none!important` + `mso-hide:all` + zero font-size) — correctly implemented for inbox preview text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization syntax (e.g. `{{}}`, `%%%%`, Liquid tags) observed in the truncated source. No issues found in the visible markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support from this data alone.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected** — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe compliance unverified.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via the relay capture.
- No unsubscribe link was visible in the truncated HTML source, but QA notes an unsubscribe link is always included in its probe set — footer/legal content is outside the truncated portion and not independently verified here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The mobile-app deep link CTA carries `utm_source=broadcast` plus AppsFlyer params (`af_dp`, `af_reengagement_window`) — but the query string is malformed (dangling `&`, unencoded `://`), which will likely break attribution capture on landing, independent of the redirect failure noted in §2.
- Product CTAs pass `Size=` variant selectors as query params (`?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml`), but these are being interpreted as relative paths rather than resolved against a base domain — confirms the redirect destinations were never fully qualified to `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` (or equivalent) before being wrapped in the Braze shortlink.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the shortlink destination URLs for the 4 failing CTAs by ensuring the underlying target is a fully-qualified absolute URL (`https://<domain>/products/...`) before Braze wraps it — the relative-path fragments currently stored are not resolvable.
2. De-duplicate/correct the mobile-app deep link query string — remove the trailing empty `&` param and properly URL-encode the `af_dp` value.
3. Add `alt` text to the `nouu1ZzO...` tracking/content image.
4. Include a plain-text MIME alternative — current plain-text part is 0 chars, which hurts deliverability and violates CAN-SPAM's practical readability expectations for non-HTML clients.
5. Verify directly with the ESP (not just via relay capture) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are being sent, since RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is now a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
