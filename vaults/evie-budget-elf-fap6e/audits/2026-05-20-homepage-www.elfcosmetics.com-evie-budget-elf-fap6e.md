# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay so e.l.f. is literally speaking my language right now — "rosé all day," $11 setting spray, and the Halo Glow Skin Tint that every TikTok girl has been raving about is front and center in the promo banner. This homepage reads as budget-glam spring energy, which is exactly where my brain is at in May. They're clearly pushing the new Power Grip Rosé Setting Spray as the hero launch moment.

## What's working

- **"rosé all day" hero copy** — that's not corporate speak, that's how I actually text my friends about my Friday get-ready-with-me. Lands immediately.
- **The Halo Glow Skin Tint $14 promo banner** — this product is literally everywhere on my FYP. Putting it in the top strip is smart; it's the TikTok proof point that e.l.f. knows what's trending.
- **$11 price point front and center** in the hero body copy — no hunting for the price, no "starting at," just $11. That's my language.
- **Beauty Squad strip** below the nav — the free shipping incentive is a nudge to sign up, visible without any scrolling.
- **"new!" tag on the hero** — tells me this isn't recycled content, something just dropped.

## What's weak

- **No model or person in the viewport** — it's all product and pink background. I want to see a real person rocking this look, ideally someone my age with a relatable face and not a full-glam editorial shoot.
- **Hamburger nav** means I can't see categories without tapping — if I want to browse lips or skincare I have to open a menu, which adds friction.
- **Free shipping is gated behind Beauty Squad sign-up** — the strip says "on your 1st order when you join," which is technically a hook but feels like a condition rather than a clean perk. A "$35 free shipping" threshold would convert me faster.
- **No reviews or ratings anywhere visible** — for a brand that lives on social proof, this is a miss in the above-the-fold zone.
- **Right side of the carousel is cropped** — I can see there's a second slide partially visible, but the cutoff makes it feel broken on first glance.

## Recommendations

- **Add a real person to the hero** — a 20-something doing her everyday makeup, not a studio product shot. Show me the glistening finish on an actual face in natural light; that's the TikTok energy e.l.f. thrives on.
- **Surface a clean free-shipping threshold** ("Free shipping over $35") alongside or instead of the Beauty Squad conditional — it's a faster path to conversion for someone already in my cart browsing mindset.
- **Pin one or two category chips below the hero** (Skin, Lips, Eyes) so I can tap into my aisle in one move without opening the hamburger; shoppable tags are native to how I browse TikTok Shop anyway.
- **Drop a review count or "X sold this week" badge** on the Power Grip Rosé product in the hero — that social-proof nudge is what turns "cute" into "add to cart."
