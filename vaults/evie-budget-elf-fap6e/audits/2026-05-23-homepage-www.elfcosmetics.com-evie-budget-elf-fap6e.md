# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay so e.l.f. clearly did their homework — the gradient, the pun, the glowy close-up skin, it's screaming TikTok-core and I am not mad about it. This looks like the brand I grew up watching blow up on #BeautyTok, and right now they're pushing a new setting product duo. Very "main character makeup" energy, which is exactly what I come here for.

## What's working

- **"lock the e.l.f. in." headline** — the pun lands. "Lock in" is Gen Z slang for committing to something, and tying it to makeup-locking is genuinely clever. I would screenshot this.
- **Hero model** — close-up on a Black woman with genuinely luminous, glassy skin. The product is doing the work visually, not just floating on a white background. This is how you sell a setting spray.
- **Beauty Squad tile** — the loyalty program is the second thing I see after the hero. Free shipping on first order with join is a real hook for a 22-year-old who orders a lot of small-basket hauls.
- **Top banner** — "Subscribe & Save! Unlock free shipping, save and get a free gift on us!" stacks multiple value props in one strip without being cluttered.
- **"DOUBLE SET IT" CTA** — tells me exactly what the product is (a duo), not some vague "Explore Now."

## What's weak

- **No price anchor visible** — I can't see what the duo costs without tapping. For a brand built on affordability, that's a miss. Showing "$XX" right on the hero is a conversion lever they're leaving on the table.
- **Beauty Squad tile is cut off on the right** — the second tile ("Scan") is half off-screen; I'd have to swipe to read it. Feels like a carousel that doesn't signal it's swipeable.
- **No social proof above the fold** — zero star ratings, zero "loved by X people" copy. For a TikTok-adjacent brand whose fans are constantly duetting reviews, not showing even a rating count is a wasted credibility shot.
- **"new!" label is small** — buried in the gradient, easy to miss. If this is a launch moment, make it louder.

## Recommendations

- **Add the price to the hero:** Even just "$XX for the duo" or "under $XX" right under the headline. e.l.f.'s whole identity is the price-to-quality ratio — hide the price and you hide your biggest flex.
- **Swap the truncated carousel for a two-tile grid:** Show both Beauty Squad tiles in full on the same screen so neither gets clipped. The "Scan" tile is invisible to anyone who doesn't know to swipe.
- **Drop a TikTok-style stat under the CTA:** Something like "4.8★ · 12,000+ reviews" would convert hesitant browsers instantly — especially since this is a "new!" product and people want social validation before trying something fresh.
- **Make "new!" a badge or pill:** Give it the same pill-shaped container as the CTA button so it reads as intentional product news, not an afterthought.
