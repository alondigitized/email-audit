# GLOWING FAST! e.l.f. x Bubble is selling out! 🥲 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth e.l.f. x Bubble send in a week, and it's the first one to actually put a specific product front and center with a price: Camo Slide Hydrating Blush Serum, $10. That's the strongest of the batch so far — no more "selling fast" vaguery, just a hero shot, a named product, and a number. The subject line is a mess, though: "GLOWING FAST! e.l.f. x Bubble is selling out! 🥲" opens in all caps with a crying-laughing emoji that reads as more panicked than urgent, and it's the same manufactured scarcity beat this collab has run twice already this week (8/7 launch, "selling fast" on 8/12).
- The body redeems some of the noise — the hero banner is clean, the $10 price point is visible without much scrolling, and the "Top Picks for You" strip adds cross-sell with real prices and buttons. But the giant unlabeled hot-pink swirl block between the hero and the product section is dead space that adds nothing (no copy, no product, no CTA) and just pushes the actual buyable moment further down.
- Judgment: mid-pack for this collab's run — better product specificity than the last two sends, but the subject line is the weakest of the week and the layout wastes prime real estate.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states the offer: "Camo Slide Hydrating Blush Serum. $10" with a single unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button.
- Repeats the CTA and price in a dedicated product section below the fold, reinforcing the offer instead of relying on the hero alone.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sell section shows real prices ($3, $11, $11) and individual shop buttons — concrete, not generic.
- Category nav strip (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) gives browsers an escape hatch if the hero product isn't their thing.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The subject line's "selling out" urgency claim is not backed up anywhere in the visible body — no countdown, no "low stock," no scarcity messaging at all once you open it.
- Large pink/red swirl graphic between hero and product block is pure filler — no text, no product, no CTA, just scroll distance.
- "3 SHADES" badge on the swirl banner is disconnected from any visible shade swatches or names — no idea what the 3 shades actually look like.
- This is the third urgency-styled subject in a week for the same collab (8/12 "selling fast," now 8/14 "selling out"), which reads as cadence fatigue rather than real scarcity.
- All-caps "GLOWING FAST!" plus the 🥲 emoji is a weaker spam-adjacent open than the brand's cleaner recent subjects (e.g. 8/10's "$17 vs $185").

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — if there's no real stock signal, drop "selling out" entirely; if there is, put a stock indicator or countdown in the hero to back it up. This is the single biggest credibility gap in the send.
- 2. Cut or repurpose the giant swirl block — replace it with shade swatches for the "3 SHADES" claim, or remove it and let the product section move up.
- 3. Add a shade name/swatch row directly under the hero so "3 SHADES" is answered visually, not just claimed.
- 4. Ease off urgency language for this collab this week — three "hurry/selling fast/selling out" sends in 7 days is de-sensitizing; save real urgency copy for when inventory is actually low.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buildable, dewy color meets Bubble's dewy skincare.`
