# Get up to 7 gifts on us! ✨​
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a Black Friday gifting email that promises "up to 7 gifts" in the subject and delivers one gift in the body. The black hero reads "YOU ARE SO GIFTED." and offers the e.l.f.ing bag with $35+ and free shipping. The second panel spells out the real deal: spend $35+ and get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Glimmer plus an E.L.F.ssentials pouch. The footer fine print is where the tiered structure ($15, $35, $50 thresholds) actually appears. The email never shows what the other gifts are.
- The hierarchy is good. The big gold-and-white headline, the Black Friday badge and the pill button grab the eye first. The "Top Picks for You" row with prices is a useful add. But the body undersells the subject. A subscriber who opens expecting seven gifts sees one threshold and one pair of freebies, and has to read the fine print to find the tiers. That gap between the subject's promise and the body's delivery is the main weakness.
- Compared with the 11/20 "$5 off Bundles & Sets" send, which led with one concrete, easy-to-grasp offer, this one is murkier. It is still seasonal and on-brand, but it falls short of the strongest e.l.f. sends.

## What's working

- The "YOU ARE SO GIFTED." hero is bold, with strong contrast and a clear Black Friday badge.
- The "spend $35+ for a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Glimmer + E.L.F.ssentials pouch" panel shows the gift concretely, with a checklist look and product shots.
- "Shipping's on us" in the hero adds a second, easy-to-understand benefit.
- The Top Picks row shows real prices ($9, $25, $32) with individual SHOP NOW buttons.
- The footer repeats free shipping, the app, and rewards, which fits the loyalty angle.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "up to 7 gifts," but the render shows only one gift tier ($35+). The $15 and $50 tiers appear only in the tiny fine print.
- Both hero SHOP NOW buttons go to generic destinations, so the CTA copy is vague ("SHOP NOW" appears four times).
- The category navigation (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) renders as plain gray bars. They look like empty placeholders and add little.
- The holiday gift finder quiz competes with the main offer and dilutes focus.
- The terms are buried in the footer: "while supplies last," "No code needed," and the tiered thresholds.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the full gift ladder right in the hero or the second panel: $15 gets X, $35 gets Y, $50 gets Z. That would make the subject's "7 gifts" claim real and clickable.
- 2. Replace the gray category bars with image tiles, or cut them.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy, such as "Get my free pouch" or "Claim my gifts."
- 4. Add a deadline in the hero (the fine print says 11/30 at 11:59pm PST) so the urgency is visible.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday gifts: $15, $35, $50`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $35, get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil + pouch. Free shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three gift tiers, free shipping, ends 11/30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-templated send (a Beefree-style table layout) with every link wrapped in a `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click tracker. The QA run passed 30% of checks. Most failures look like probe-side limits, such as relative redirects and an ASCII-only encoder, rather than confirmed defects in the email. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe block and later rows could not be audited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect target (FAIL, unconfirmed severity).** The "Shop Now" tracker (`.../2-pZYrH5xFolUU__OpGi-Q~~/...`) failed with `unknown url type: '/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo?Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon'`. The tracker appears to return a relative `Location` header with no scheme or host.
  - Browsers resolve this fine, but strict clients and the QA prober do not.
  - It can't be confirmed whether the destination loads, because the probe never followed the redirect.
- **Same pattern on the login link (FAIL).** The "Receipt scanning – Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." tracker returned `unknown url type: '/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount'`.
  - This is the same relative-redirect behavior.
  - It also lands on a login wall, so the destination can't be verified without authentication.
- **ASCII encoding error (FAIL), likely a QA-tool artifact.** The "beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app" link failed with `'ascii' codec can't encode characters in position 119-121`.
  - Positions 119-121 in the redirect target hold non-ASCII characters, probably an unencoded app-store or deep-link URL.
  - If those characters are in the `Location` header itself, that is a minor defect on the sender's tracking domain. If they aren't, it's only a probe bug.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 39 links were probed and 27 were skipped, so the 30% pass rate rests on a small sample.
- **Heavy tracking URLs.** Plain-text is 86.7% URLs (11,364 of 13,101 chars). This comes from the long Braze tracker tokens of 300+ characters.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** One image has no `alt`: the pixel or tracking image `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/93dg-EWCGJ1tIbqpvMUOYA~~/AAQRxRA~/Z7ojvw209jpsf_...`. The `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, which would make this a harmless flag. That isn't confirmed.
- **Keyboard focus removed.** The hero link has `tabindex="-1"` (`<a href="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/i1rMQGC4S8tRuDm6W08hlg~~/..." ... tabindex="-1">`). This takes the primary banner link out of keyboard tab order.
- **Weak hero alt text.** The hero image has `alt="e.l.f."`, which says nothing about the offer. If the image carries the promo text, the alt text hides it from screen readers.
- **9px mobile heading.** `@media (max-width:660px){.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}}` shrinks an `<h1>` to 9px on mobile. That is below the common 11-14px legibility minimum.
- **Empty `<title>`.** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Invisible characters.** A zero-width character (U+200B) trails the subject (`✨​`) and the preheader (`set​.`). It is harmless in most clients.
- **Redundant markup.** Row 1 nests duplicate `nl-container` / `row-1` structures, and `html_block` blocks 2-4 are empty `<div>` shells.
- **Positives.** `role="presentation"` is on the layout tables, `lang="en"` is set, and the MSO/VML fallbacks and `x-apple-data-detectors` handling are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered Liquid or Braze tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`) appear.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so absence isn't confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` aligns with the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer not auditable.** The footer, physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not visible.** All hrefs are opaque tracker tokens, so UTM parameters can't be checked in the source.
- **Destination parameters.** The only resolved destination is `/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo?Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon`. The `%26` encoding for "&" is correct, and the variant is preselected.
- **Relative redirects.** These carry no host, so the landing domain and the persistence of tracking parameters can't be confirmed.
- **Login link.** `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` sends logged-out recipients to a login wall for a rewards promo.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Check the raw headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass from the original message, since the relay may have stripped them.
2. **Make the prober robust.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirecting host with `urljoin`, and use UTF-8 or IDNA-safe handling to fix the `ascii` codec failure. Re-run the 3 failed links and the 27 skipped ones before treating the 30% pass rate as real.
3. **Confirm the redirects.** Check whether `links.elfcosmetics.com` really emits scheme-less `Location` values. If it does, fix them to absolute URLs. Also check the `/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo` landing page.
4. **Fix accessibility.** Remove `tabindex="-1"` from the hero link, add descriptive alt text to the hero image and the open-tracking pixel (or `alt=""` if decorative), and fill `<title>`.
5. **Raise the mobile heading size.** Raise the 9px `<h1>` on mobile, or make it a non-heading element if it's legal copy.
6. **Review the unsubscribe and footer link.** Check the untruncated HTML for the postal address, an unsubscribe link and a preference center.
