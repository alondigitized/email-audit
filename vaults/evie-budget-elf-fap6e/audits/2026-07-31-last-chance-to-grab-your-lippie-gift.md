# Last chance to grab your lippie gift! 💄
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the "last chance" follow-up to e.l.f.'s National Lipstick Day GWP that launched 2026-07-27 ("Don't miss a National Lipstick Day gift on us! 💄," 9/10) — same mechanic, same free Cream Glide Lip Liner with any lippie purchase, same code LIPPIE. The problem: the creative is recycled wholesale. Hero headline still reads "your gift for national lipstick day!" as if this were the launch, not the closer — nothing in the rendered body signals urgency (no countdown, no "ends tonight," no stated deadline). The subject line does all the scarcity work; the email itself doesn't back it up.
- Execution otherwise is clean and on-brand: consistent red/pink palette, three well-built "combo" modules pairing the free liner with a second product, individual CTAs throughout. But for a send whose entire premise is "last chance," the total absence of a visible clock or expiry is a real gap.

## What's working

- Clean, bug-free render — consistent lip-red palette, no overlapping text or broken assets.
- Offer mechanic is stated plainly in the hero with the exact code (LIPPIE) visible.
- Three combo sections each pair the free liner with a different product and its own "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Footer reinforces value with free shipping and receipt-scanning rewards callouts.
- Category nav pills (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) give browsers an off-ramp beyond the single offer.

## What's weak

- No visual urgency anywhere in the body despite "Last chance" in the subject — no countdown, no explicit expiry time.
- Hero copy ("your gift for national lipstick day!") is unchanged from the original 7/27 send — reads like day one, not a farewell reminder.
- "Top Picks for You" surfaces a primer, a face cream, and a setting spray — off-category for a lip-GWP push and dilutes focus right before the footer.
- No price or value context on the combo products — a shopper can't tell what the free liner is actually worth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to match the subject — replace "your gift for national lipstick day!" with something like "ends tonight: your free lippie" and add a visible cutoff time. Urgency needs to be seen, not just implied by the subject line.
- 2. State a real deadline near the primary CTA ("expires 11:59pm tonight") — "last chance" with no stated cutoff reads as filler, not scarcity.
- 3. Swap "Top Picks for You" to lip-adjacent SKUs (other liners, glosses, lip oils) to keep the whole send in-category.
- 4. Show a value cue on at least one CTA (e.g., "$9 value, free with LIPPIE") so the free gift feels concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: your free lip liner 💄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call — LIPPIE gift expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab a free Cream Glide Lip Liner before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code LIPPIE disappears at midnight — add your shade now`
