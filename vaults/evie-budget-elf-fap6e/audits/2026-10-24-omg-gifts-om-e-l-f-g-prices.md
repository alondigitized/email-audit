# OMG gifts. OM-e.l.f.-G prices 🎁

- The deadline (Oct 31) is only in the footer fine print, so the urgency doesn't register above the fold.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally sound (role="presentation" tables, `lang="en"`, preheader, viewport meta). However, 5 of the 12 probed tracked links failed in QA, and the text part, the unsubscribe headers and the authentication results could not be verified. The QA pass rate was 25%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 probe failures, all `unknown url type`.** Each tracked link on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` resolved to a relative path with no scheme or host:
  - `/products/glow-reviver-lip-oil?Color=Jam+Session+-+Black+Cherry` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (receipt scanning / rewards)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?pid=Email&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=...&deep_link_value=com.elfco...` (app download)
- **Cause not confirmed.** This is the probe's HTTP client failing to resolve a relative `Location` header or relative destination. It is consistent with either a prober limitation or a tracker that returns relative redirects.
  - If the tracker really emits relative redirects, browsers would resolve them against `links.elfcosmetics.com` and the clicks would break.
  - The QA data can't tell the two apart. A manual `curl -I` on one of these links would settle it.
- **Wrapped links hide the destinations.** Because every link is wrapped, the final URLs and any appended UTMs can't be seen from the HTML. 31 of 43 links were not probed.
- **Image `alt` is missing** on the image with src `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/bgpN3BwJkJY4f5Z4ZvvtjA~~/AAQRxRA~/fWSYPZTL6Y-...`. Because of the `/q/` path, it may be a tracking pixel, but it should still carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the `mobile_hide` html_block has an empty first font name. It is harmless but malformed.
- The Open Sans Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so the Arial fallback applies in Outlook. This is acceptable.
- The hero image has `alt="e.l.f."` and a fixed `width="640"`. The `.stack .column` and `mobile_hide`/`desktop_hide` patterns are standard.
- The HTML source was truncated, so the rest of the body could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is missing.** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars` (FAIL). If this is real, it hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could also be a capture artifact from the AgentMail relay.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header** (WARN) and **no `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (WARN, RFC 8058). QA notes the relay may strip these. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify this on the raw message.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Verify it in the raw headers at the destination mailbox.
- The visible footer content (unsubscribe link, physical address) was cut off by truncation. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result for it is listed, so it can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only visible UTM set is on the app link: `pid=Email&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=...&af_reengagement_window=30d&deep_link_value=...`.
  - `utm_source` is present, but no `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible.
  - The AppsFlyer-style params are mixed in with it.
- The product links carry only variant params (`Color=`, `Size=`) and no visible UTMs. They may be added at the tracker, but this can't be verified from the source.
- The login link uses `return_to=%2Faccount`, which sends users to a login wall rather than a rewards or receipt-scanning page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually `curl -IL` one of the failing `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` links. If the `Location` header is relative, fix the tracker to emit absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` redirects. If the redirect is absolute, fix the QA prober to join relative redirects against the current URL.
2. Confirm that a multipart/alternative text part exists in the raw message. If it doesn't, add one.
3. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
4. Add a `<title>` and `alt=""` on the tracking image, and remove the empty font-family entry.
5. Standardize `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` on all links, not just the app link.
6. Raise probe coverage beyond 12 of 43 links, or probe the footer unsubscribe link explicitly.
