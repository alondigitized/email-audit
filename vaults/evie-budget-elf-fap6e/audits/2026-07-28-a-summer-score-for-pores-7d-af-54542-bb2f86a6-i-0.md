# A summer score for pores ✨

## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN's single-product spotlight for the PLZ Clarify Facial Oil ($16, 2% salicylic acid) — a much lighter lift than the GWP mechanics and multi-SKU launches that dominate this brand's recent history (Soft Glam Blush, the haircare launch, the Invis-Stick GWP). The creative leans on lifestyle photography (a model applying the oil, a tight close-up of an oil droplet on skin) rather than a clean product shot, and the benefit list under "DROPPIN' BY TO CLARIFY" does the actual selling with emoji-flagged bullets.
- It's competent and on-brand, but it's the thinnest send in this brand's recent run. There's no discount, no urgency, no social proof or star ratings — the things that pushed the National Lipstick Day (9/10) and hypochlorous mist (8/10) sends higher. The subject line is also softer than usual; "a summer score" is vague where past subjects ("Glitz with benefits," "40% off," GWP callouts) gave a concrete reason to open.
- Solid, on-brand, unremarkable — a mid-pack send that would benefit from either a real offer or a testimonial to match this brand's stronger recent work.

## What's working

- Clear single-product focus: one hero SKU, one price, one CTA — no split attention.
- Ingredient/benefit specificity is visible without scrolling: "2% salicylic acid," "non-comedogenic," "helps treat and prevent new blackheads."
- The oil-droplet close-up is a strong, on-theme visual metaphor for a facial oil product.
- Clean visual hierarchy — hero image → bold headline → CTA button, no clutter.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sell block adds a light personalization/discovery layer without derailing the main offer.

## What's weak

- No real offer — "GET IT FOR $16" is just the retail price, not a discount, bundle, or GWP, which is a step down from this brand's recent gift-with-purchase and % -off sends.
- Zero social proof: no star rating, review count, or testimonial, unlike the hypochlorous mist send (7/26) which used two five-star testimonials plus a medical-association seal to build trust for a similarly education-forward product.
- Subject line is vague marketing-speak ("a summer score") rather than naming the product or a concrete benefit/number.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — nothing pushes a reader to act today vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one line of social proof under the hero (star rating + review count, or a single customer quote) — this is the single biggest lever, since the closest comparable send (hypochlorous mist, 8/10) leaned on testimonials to carry a similarly benefit-led pitch.
- 2. Swap "GET IT FOR $16" for CTA copy that either names a real incentive (bundle, GWP, %-off) or, if none exists, sharpens the benefit instead of implying a deal that isn't there.
- 3. Tighten the subject line to name the product or a concrete claim instead of "a summer score."
- 4. Add a short, credible urgency cue (e.g., "back in stock," "while supplies last") if genuinely true — currently nothing time-bounds the read.

# Technical Email Audit — e.l.f. SKIN "A summer score for pores ✨"

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA passed only 27% of checks: 4 CTA links resolve to relative (non-absolute) URLs and fail redirect resolution, the plain-text MIME part is empty, and both unsubscribe-related headers are absent from the captured payload.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 broken redirect targets (FAIL):** All primary links are wrapped in a `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click-tracking redirector (Cheetah/CM Group-style). Four of them decode to **relative paths with no scheme/host**, which the redirect resolver rejects as "unknown url type":
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning — Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?pid=Email&utm_campaign=072826_plz_clarify_summer_break_ooo&bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&c=email&shortlink=email&` ("beauty squad — Download the e.l.f. app")
  
  These need to be fully-qualified (e.g., `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) at the point they're loaded into the ESP's link-wrapping step, or every client that follows them will hit a dead/misrouted redirect rather than the product page.
- The mobile-app link's decoded query string ends in a dangling `&c=email&shortlink=email&` with no trailing key=value — either a truncated param or a template variable that failed to populate.
- 12 of 41 total links were probed (footer/social/utility links deprioritized per QA methodology); an unsubscribe link was confirmed present but not enumerated in the findings above.

4. Populate the hidden preheader with actual campaign preview copy instead of what appears to be an internal placeholder ("PLZ Clarify Facial Oil.").
5. Include a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative (currently 0 chars) — required for CAN-SPAM best practice and spam-filter scoring.
