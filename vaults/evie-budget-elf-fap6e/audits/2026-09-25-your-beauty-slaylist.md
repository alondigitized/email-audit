# Your beauty slaylist 🎶
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s "Mirror Mix" music-tie-in send — a full album drop paired with four product callouts (Power Grip, Soft Glam blush, Main Stain lip stain) and a GWP on the mascara. It's the most content-dense, structurally scattered email in this brand's recent run: nine distinct colored panels stacked with no visual rest, competing for the same eyeball that just landed on a blurry lip close-up hero. Compare to 2026-09-20's Holy Hydration! send (8/10) — two product tubes suspended in water, one message, one CTA — this is the opposite instinct: bury a legitimate GWP offer (full-size mascara free at $50+) six panels deep under a music campaign nobody asked for.
- The hero itself is a problem before you even get to the noise: the "Mirror Mix" graphic is out-of-focus/motion-blurred lips with clashing magenta-on-navy type that reads more like a poster mockup than a finished send. Business-wise this is a brand-awareness/entertainment play, not a commerce email, and it shows — the actual shoppable moments (Power Grip, blush, lip stain, mascara GWP) get equal or lesser visual weight than "listen to our new music album." Judgment: weakest send in the recent run: the GWP deserves to be the hero and isn't.

## What's working

- The mascara GWP panel is genuinely clear: "get a full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara on us... when you spend $50+... no code needed" with a visible product-in-hand shot.
- "Top Picks for You" section is clean, product-forward, with visible pricing ($11, $11, $2) and individual Shop Now buttons per item.
- Color-blocked panels (red/blue/pink) create some visual rhythm and separate each product story.
- Category nav bar (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) gives an easy post-scroll escape hatch.

## What's weak

- Hero image is blurry/out-of-focus lips — looks like an unfinished asset, not a polished launch graphic.
- Message hierarchy is backwards: a music album drop leads, while the actual money offer (free full-size mascara at $50+) is buried near the bottom.
- Nine stacked panels before any real payoff is fatigue-inducing — no single clear "this is the one thing to click" moment.
- Two separate "Mirror Mix" plugs (hero + mid-email "Listen to Mirror Mix" panel) repeat the same non-shopping message twice.
- Subject/preview promise "beauty slaylist" but the email opens with a record label pitch, not a beauty offer — mismatched expectation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the mascara GWP as the hero — it's the only offer with a real dollar hook ($50+ spend, full-size free item). Move Mirror Mix to a single panel further down, not two.
- 2. Cut one of the two Mirror Mix panels entirely — the album doesn't need two placements in a commerce email.
- 3. Replace the blurry lip-close-up hero with a sharp, well-lit product or GWP shot — this asset reads as unfinished.
- 4. Consolidate the three product spotlights (Power Grip / Soft Glam / Main Stain) into one carousel-style panel instead of three full-width color blocks — reduces scroll fatigue.
- 5. Tighten subject/body alignment so "slaylist" pays off with an actual beauty offer up top, not a music campaign.

1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-templated broadcast built on a redirect-tracking domain (`links.elfcosmetics.com`), and the automated QA pipeline flags multiple broken destination links, a missing plain-text MIME part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 of 12 probed links resolve to broken/relative paths after redirect expansion, per QA FAIL findings:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning..." link)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?is_retargeting=true&pid=Email&shortlink=email&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=...` ("Download the e.l.f. app")

- All tracked links route through Braze click-tracking wrappers (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`); this is expected infra and not itself an issue.
- 30 of 42 total links were deprioritized/skipped by the QA probe (footer/social/utility); no data on those.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the Braze redirect/link-shortening configuration so the 4 flagged destination links resolve to absolute URLs instead of bare relative paths — this is blocking "Shop Now," account login, and app-download conversion paths entirely.
- Add `alt=""` (or descriptive alt text) to the tracking pixel/image at `q/J2CbS2kamdMA...`.

- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the raw SMTP level; if absent (not just unreported by the relay), add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from mail server logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
