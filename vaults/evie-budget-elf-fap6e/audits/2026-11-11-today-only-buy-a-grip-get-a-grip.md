# TODAY ONLY! Buy a grip, get a grip 💚
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-idea promo: buy any e.l.f. primer and get one free, riding the 11/11 "wish granted" hook. The pun in the subject ("Buy a grip, get a grip") carries through to the hero headline "GET A GRIP. GET A GIFT." The eye lands on the offer first, and the black "SHOP NOW" buttons are unmissable.
- The problem is that the body loses focus after the hero. It stacks three more products, each with its own button, then a TikTok Shop block with a different "11/11 wish," then a recycled "choose a full-size gift on us" block with a $35+ threshold. That last block is the same offer as the 11/04 "Pick your full-size gift on us 💝" send. The "gift" in the hero is also vague, since the copy says "get one on us" without making clear it's a free primer. The terms are in fine print at the bottom, including at least one exclusion, which weakens the honesty of a big "TODAY ONLY" headline.
- Verdict: strong top third, muddy bottom two-thirds. It's better than the 11/09 catch-all "e.l.f. Edit" and roughly on par with the 11/04 gift send.

## What's working

- The hero headline "GET A GRIP. GET A GIFT." is bold and echoes the subject pun, so the open and the body feel like the same thought.
- The offer is concrete: buy any primer from the e.l.f.ic lineup, get one free.
- Each product block has a photo, a name, a price ($11, $22) and a one-line benefit ("shine-free finish," "dewy finish").
- The hierarchy is clean. Hero, then three large product tiles with consistent black pill buttons, makes the scroll easy.
- The "11/11 wish" framing fits the date and gives the send a seasonal reason to exist.

## What's weak

- The "gift" is never named in the hero. A shopper can't tell if it's a free primer, a sample or a pick-your-own.
- Two competing blocks follow the product tiles: the TikTok "YOUR 11/11 WISH IS GRANTED!" pink grip, and the "choose a full-size gift on us" block. The second is a different offer, and it repeats the 11/04 send. This dilutes the primary CTA.
- Urgency is in the subject only. The body shows no countdown, no "ends tonight" line and no deadline near the buttons.
- Every CTA says "SHOP NOW," so the product buttons are not distinct.
- The nav tiles (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are large empty grey bars with no imagery, so they look like unloaded content.
- The terms and exclusions sit in tiny footer fine print, which is easy to miss for a "free" offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the TikTok block and the "choose a full-size gift" block from this send. Keep one offer and end the body after the product tiles and a "Top Picks" row.
- 2. Name the gift in the hero: "Buy any primer, get a free Power Grip Primer."
- 3. Put the deadline next to the buttons, for example "Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT," and add a small "terms apply" link right under the hero CTA.
- 4. Vary the button copy: "Get my free primer," "Shop the duo," "Shop the spray."
- 5. Replace the grey nav bars with a compact text nav or image tiles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1 primer, get 1 free. Today only 💚`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 11/11 wish: a free Power Grip Primer`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add any primer to your bag and the second is on us. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Matte, dewy or glowy, your grip is free at checkout. 11/11 only.`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics, "TODAY ONLY! Buy a grip, get a grip 💚"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound, with table layout, `role="presentation"` and a preheader. The QA run failed 5 of the 12 links it probed, all with the same relative-URL error, and the header and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 probe failures, one pattern.** All say `unknown url type: '/…'`, meaning the probe received a scheme-less path. The links are wrapped in `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirects.
  - `/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm?Color=Birthday+Cake` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Jumbo+2.5+oz+%2F+75+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Dark+Cocoa+-+Deep+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_campaign=111126_project_mega_offer_0-3&af_xp=email&deep_link_value=...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Cause is unconfirmed.** The error most likely comes from the ESP redirector returning a relative `Location`, or from the probe not resolving a relative redirect. If the redirector really does emit relative redirects, strict clients may fail to follow them. Browsers resolve them correctly. I can't tell from this data whether real recipients are affected. Re-test with a browser-class client before treating it as a bug.
- **Image-wrapped logo link.** It uses `tabindex="-1"` and has no text fallback beyond the `alt="e.l.f."` on the image.
- **Coverage gap.** 30 of 42 links were skipped, so footer, social and unsubscribe link health is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image without alt.** The QA check flags `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/erAe7NxZ4WFbmoanhC7Okg~~/AAQRxRA~/TGmEcY1r...`. The `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, which would make this low severity. Add `alt=""` to make it explicitly decorative.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is minor, but some clients and web views use it.
- **Empty font name.** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the `html_block` div has an empty first family. It is harmless because the fallbacks apply, but it is malformed CSS.
- **Nested tables.** `nl-container` and `row-1` tables are nested inside `html_block` cells, which adds weight and markup duplication. They render fine.
- **Plain text is 85% URLs (13,353 of 15,711 chars).** The long tracked URLs inflate the plain-text part and can contribute to spam scoring.
- **Passes.** `lang="en"`, the viewport meta, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and the MSO conditional blocks are all present. The preheader is hidden correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (such as `{{ }}` or `%%`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Check the raw message at the source or send a seed test to a Gmail or Yahoo inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Verify them on the sending domain `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither appears in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Only one UTM set is visible.** It is on the app link: `utm_campaign=111126_project_mega_offer_0-3`, with AppsFlyer params `af_xp=email` and `deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app://home`. No `utm_source` or `utm_medium` is visible. Other destinations sit behind opaque tracked URLs, so UTM propagation can't be verified.
- **Login-gated destination.** The receipt-scanning link resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Logged-out recipients land on a login page, not a page about the feature.
- **Product variants.** The product links pass variant parameters (`Color=`, `Size=`), which is good for deep-linking. Whether each variant resolves and is in stock was not tested because the probes failed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 5 failed links with a browser-class client that resolves relative redirects. Confirm they reach the intended `elfcosmetics.com` pages before treating them as broken.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. Don't rely on the relay's capture.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, fill in `<title>`, and remove the empty `font-family:''` entry.
4. Confirm `utm_source` and `utm_medium` are applied to all destination links. Resolve the tracked redirects to verify.
5. Extend probe coverage to include the unsubscribe link and the remaining 30 skipped links, and capture the unsubscribe URL result explicitly.
