# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. came to PLAY. I open this and immediately I'm seeing cream blush at $7 — which like, that's my entire personality. The hero is pushing a new product drop ("The Cream Team"), the Beauty Squad loyalty strip is right there, and the whole vibe is warm, dewy, very current. This is the brand that knows exactly who I am and is not wasting my time.

## What's working

- **"$7 each!" in the hero copy** — that price is doing heavy lifting. It removes any friction before I even think about tapping. I already know e.l.f. cream blush is a Charlotte Tilbury Hollywood Flawless Filter dupe situation and this confirms the price point immediately.
- **Beauty Squad free shipping strip** — showing the loyalty perk right below the nav, on the first order, is smart. It catches both new shoppers and reminds me (a returning shopper) that I should be logged in.
- **"Subscribe & Save" top banner** — the purple banner is distinct and leads with "free gift on us" which is genuinely compelling copy. Not just a promo, it's a benefit stack.
- **"THE CREAM TEAM" brand voice** — "e.l.f.ing iconic together" and "Buttery blush + bronzer" is on-brand, punchy, and speaks my language without trying too hard.
- **Product clarity** — I can see both products labeled ("SOFT GLAM CREAM BLUSH" and what appears to be the bronzer stick), which tells me exactly what the launch includes without clicking.

## What's weak

- **No model on skin** — cream blush is all about how it looks on a face. Showing me two product sticks on a peach background doesn't tell me how it wears, what the payoff looks like, or if it works on my skin tone. This is the one thing that would make me go "wait I need that."
- **"STICK AROUND" CTA is punny but vague** — I get it (blush stick → stick around), cute. But for someone landing cold, it's not immediately clear this means "shop the blush." "Shop the Drop" or "Get Yours for $7" would convert harder.
- **Second carousel tile is cut off** — I can see "Scan... when y... Squad." which is partial and gives no information. That real estate is wasted.
- **No reviews or ratings visible** — I buy e.l.f. because I trust the brand, but a first-timer would want to see social proof before tapping anything.

## Recommendations

- **Put a face on the hero** — swap the floating product sticks for a dewy close-up of the cream blush + bronzer on actual skin, ideally multiple skin tones. That's what makes Gen Z stop scrolling. The product shot can live below.
- **Rewrite the CTA to state the value** — "Shop $7 Cream Blush" over "STICK AROUND." The pun is cute in secondary copy; the button needs to convert.
- **Add a star rating or "X sold" counter under the hero** — one line of social proof ("4.8 stars · 12K reviews") under the product names would dramatically increase conversion intent for new visitors.
- **Fix or fully reveal the second carousel tile** — either make it scrollable and indicate that (add dots/arrows) or replace it with a static benefit block that fully renders.
