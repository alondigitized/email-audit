# This $11 lip combo is lit as e.l.f. 😘✨	
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-executed hero-bundle send: "Cream Glide Lip Liner + Glow Reviver Lip Oil Glimmer" for $11, backed by a UGC-style selfie video-still of a model applying the product, and a genuinely useful shade-matrix (16 liner shades on the left, coordinating oil shades on the right). It's a stronger visual than most of this week's e.l.f. sends — the shade swatches do real work that a flat product shot can't.
- Where it loses points is stacking: after the hero and the massive shade chart, there's a second offer (free full-size Bronzing Drops with $50+ orders), then a third module (Top Picks cross-sell), then a six-item nav-bar wall (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) before even hitting the footer. That's three distinct asks competing with one hero, which dilutes the single clear CTA a $11 bundle needs.
- Compared to 2026-08-23's toner send (two hero products, clean spec blocks, 7/10) and 2026-08-24's pickle lip balm restock (7/10), this one has better production value on the hero image but worse restraint on scroll depth — it's trying to do a hero drop, a GWP, and a merchandising catalog in one email.

## What's working

- Hero copy is concrete and singular: two named products, one bundled price ("Only $11"), clear framing ("sculpted, shimmery pout").
- The selfie-in-phone-frame creative feels authentic/UGC rather than studio-polished, which fits the brand's TikTok-native voice.
- The shade matrix is genuinely useful product detail — 8 liner/oil pairings shown side by side lets a shopper self-select before clicking.
- No render bugs: text is crisp, images loaded, buttons aligned, no overlap.
- Cruelty-free/vegan badge row and "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face" reinforce brand trust cheaply.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers in one email (lip combo hero, Bronzing Drops GWP, Top Picks cross-sell) blur the primary CTA.
- The six gray nav-bar blocks (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) are pure filler — they add scroll length without adding persuasion.
- The Bronzing Drops GWP is buried mid-email in a small amber block, easy to miss and disconnected from the lip theme above it.
- Subject line emoji stacking ("😘✨") plus "lit as e.l.f." is a now-familiar pun formula (see 08-24's "not a drill... it's the real e.l.f.ing dill 👀🥒") — the wordplay well is getting shallow this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the shade matrix + one CTA. Move Bronzing Drops GWP and Top Picks to a separate send or the following day — one hero, one offer, one CTA converts better than three stacked asks.
- 2. Drop the six-block nav wall entirely; it's dead weight that only exists in every recent e.l.f. send and adds no persuasion.
- 3. If the Bronzing Drops GWP is time-sensitive, give it its own subject line and email rather than a buried mid-scroll module.
- 4. Tighten the subject — "lit-as-e.l.f." pun is repeating the brand's own recent joke pattern; use the price/value as the hook instead.
