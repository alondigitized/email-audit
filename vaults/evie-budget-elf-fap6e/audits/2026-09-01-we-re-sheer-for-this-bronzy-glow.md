# We’re *sheer* for this bronzy glow 🫰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is "The e.l.f. Edit" — a weekly roundup email, not a single-hero send, and it shows. The Sheer For It Bronzer Tint gets one good banner ("you dropped hints... so we're dropping NEW tints! ... Only $6!") buried after a "HOT AS e.l.f. THIS WEEK" grid and a generic "Bestie, let's catch you up" intro. Compare that to yesterday's 2026-08-31 "NEW! Sheer For It Bronzer Tint" send, which led with the same $6 hook as the actual hero — here it's demoted to slot three in a long scroll.
- The lip combo section, "your new obsessions" grid, and the six-item nav accordion (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) turn this into a browse-the-catalog email rather than a make-a-decision email. It's competently built — no render bugs, consistent brand voice, real product photography — but it dilutes its own best asset (the $6 bronzer tint) by making the reader scroll past a "hot this week" grid and a bestie greeting to find it.
- Net: a serviceable weekly-digest email that buries a genuinely strong hero offer it already proved works. Should have led with the tint.

## What's working

- The bronzer tint banner itself is strong: clear demographic model shot with visible swatch application, bold "$6" price, and an unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button.
- Layout is clean and bug-free — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.
- The "LIP COMBOS HAVE A NEW FIRST STEP" 3-step routine (Stain & Set / Line & Define / Gloss It Up) is a nice merchandising device that shows product-in-use.
- Trust/utility footer signals are present: cruelty-free/vegan badges, free shipping, TikTok Shop, receipt scanning rewards.

## What's weak

- The hero offer (the same $6 bronzer tint that anchored yesterday's dedicated send) is pushed down past a generic "Bestie" greeting and a 4-image "hot this week" grid — weak visual hierarchy for the best asset in the email.
- Subject and hero banner don't match: subject says "sheer for this bronzy glow" but the first fold is a striped tote/bag product shot with no bronzer visible until scrolling.
- "HOT AS e.l.f. THIS WEEK" grid has zero labels or prices on the four products — pure mystery-meat merchandising, no reason to click any single tile.
- The email tries to do too much: bestsellers, new arrivals, hot-this-week, bronzer launch, lip routine, "new obsessions" grid, and a 6-category nav — no single clear CTA path for someone who opened for the bronzer.
- This is the second e.l.f. send in two days pushing the same Sheer For It Bronzer Tint at $6, which starts to feel repetitive rather than escalating.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the bronzer tint as the hero banner instead of burying it third — move the "you dropped hints... NEW tints! Only $6!" creative to the very top, replacing the tote-bag/"THE e.l.f. EDIT" opener.
- 2. Cut the unlabeled "HOT AS e.l.f. THIS WEEK" 4-image grid or add product names + prices — as-is it adds scroll with no purchase signal.
- 3. Trim the email to two sections max (bronzer hero + lip combo routine) and move "your new obsessions" / category nav to a "shop more" footer link instead of a full accordion.
- 4. Since this is back-to-back bronzer-tint promotion (2026-08-31 then 2026-09-01), differentiate the angle — e.g., lean into a UGC/TikTok stat or before/after rather than repeating "you dropped hints."
- **Subject Alt A:** `$6 bronzer tint: the TikTok shade everyone's asking for`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bronzy glow starts at $6 🫰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Sheer For It Bronzer Tint just dropped — shop before it sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: the 3-step lip combo everyone's copying`
