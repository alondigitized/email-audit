# NEW! Sheer For It Bronzer Tint 🤎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s launch send for the Sheer For It Bronzer Tint, and it's a clean, single-hero-product execution with a real price hook: "the biggest flex: only $6!" The TikTok-native framing ("you dropped hints... so we're dropping NEW tints!", "Get the look that's all over your FYP") is a deliberate viral/UGC angle, but it comes at a cost — the primary CTA is "SHOP ON TIKTOK" rather than a direct shop-now link, adding friction for anyone who isn't already on TikTok Shop.
- Visually it's strong: a warm, sun-kissed model shot up top, a genuinely appetizing product-texture close-up (the tint dripping off the applicator), and a "cheeky duo" bundle cross-sell with the blending brush. The "Top Picks for You" module and category nav at the bottom are standard e.l.f. furniture, consistent with recent sends like 2026-08-28's Halo Glow send.
- The weak spot is the CTA routing — three "SHOP ON TIKTOK" buttons and zero standard "SHOP NOW" for the hero product itself (the one that appears is on the bundle, not the tint). For a $6 product this is an impulse buy that should have the lowest-friction path possible, and TikTok Shop is not that for most of the inbox.

## What's working

- Clear single-hero-product focus: NEW Sheer For It Bronzer Tint, priced at $6, stated twice.
- "new!" badge and "you dropped hints..." framing gives a fresh, launch-specific hook rather than generic promo copy.
- The pouring-texture product shot is a strong, appetizing visual mid-email.
- Cross-sell "a cheeky duo" bundle (tint + blending brush) is a logical, well-composed upsell.
- No render bugs — clean layout, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- All three CTAs read "SHOP ON TIKTOK," which routes engaged readers off-email to a different platform instead of direct checkout — high friction for a $6 impulse item.
- No visible price comparison, review count, or star rating for social proof.
- The four bronzy-shade/benefit bullets are copy-only with emoji, no swatches shown, so shade range isn't visually demonstrated.
- "Top Picks for You" feels bolted on and unrelated to the tint launch (primer, setting spray, lip liner) — dilutes the single-hero focus established earlier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap at least the top and bundle CTAs to "SHOP NOW" linking directly to the PDP, reserving "SHOP ON TIKTOK" only for a dedicated TikTok-specific module if that channel is truly the growth driver — don't make it the only path to purchase.
- 2. Add a shade swatch strip (all 4 bronzy shades) near the "4 bronzy shades for lips & cheeks" bullet — this is a $6 makeup item, shade visualization drives conversion.
- 3. Insert a short social-proof line ("4.5★ from early testers" or a review count) near the price to build trust on a brand-new SKU.
- 4. Reconsider "Top Picks for You" placement — either tie picks explicitly to bronzer routine (blush brush, setting spray for longevity) or move it further down/cut it to keep focus on the launch.
