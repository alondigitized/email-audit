# NEW! Sleek Streak hair styling duo 💛💚 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-product launch: the Sleek Streak travel-size hair duo (Gloss Mode Treatment Oil plus mini Humidity Hero Anti-Frizz Styling Spray) for $12. The hero is strong. Chartreuse and purple, with both products in one frame, make it clear within a second what is for sale and at what price. The yellow oil-drip image is the best visual in the email.
- The body then loses momentum. After the product reveal there is a long, repetitive scroll of benefit blocks over a brown hair photo, and a second "Shop Now" button. A free full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara offer at $50+ and a "Top Picks for You" row (primer, lip liner, setting spray) pull attention to makeup, which dilutes a hair launch. - Judgement: a solid 7. The hero and offer are clear, but the email is too long for a $12 product and the mascara GWP competes with the hair story. It follows the brand's usual pattern: the 2026-09-28 "your weekly dose of add to cart" send also pushed the hair launch, and the 10-01 Halo Glow send did the same single-story job more tightly.

- The hero shows both products with "new!" and "Sleek Streak." above "Only $12", so the offer is visible immediately.
- The kit contents ("Gloss Mode Treatment Oil" and "mini Humidity Hero Anti-Frizz Styling Spray") are listed plainly under the oil macro shot.

- The "*Versus control" footnote on the claims is a small honesty signal.
- The palette and voice match the brand's other recent sends.

## What's weak

- The oil macro image is very tall, and the brown hair photo with four benefit lines is a second long block. Together they push the second CTA far down the page.
- The mascara GWP banner competes with the hair launch. Its $50+ threshold and "no code needed" copy add a second decision to a $12 purchase.
- "Top Picks for You" shows primer, liner, and dewy spray. None of them are hair, which undercuts the personalization claim.
- The six gray category bars look empty and do nothing for the story.
- The offer deadline (10/5, 2:59am EST) appears only in the tiny footer, so there is no visible urgency in the body.
- The CTA copy is weak: "Get your sleek on" is vague and "Shop Now" is generic.

- 1. Cut the body to hero, kit contents, and one CTA. Remove one of the two tall image blocks and fold the four benefit lines into a compact row next to the product shot.
- 2. Move the mascara GWP below the main CTA or make it a thin banner, and move its deadline from the footer into the banner.
- 3. Replace "Top Picks for You" with hair-relevant products, such as other styling or smoothing items, or drop it.
- 4. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific: "Get the $12 Sleek Streak duo" instead of "Get your sleek on" and "Shop now".

Header-level checks (auth, List-Unsubscribe) could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 FAIL results, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The affected links are:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (2 links: "Shop Now" and "Power Grip Primer")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&…&bxid=6a013826e53…`
- **Likely cause:** the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` click-tracker returns a relative `Location` header, and the prober doesn't resolve it against the origin. Browsers resolve relative redirects correctly, so these are probably false positives. The data doesn't confirm either way. Re-probe with relative-redirect resolution before treating them as broken.
- **Relative redirect targets:** if the tracker really emits host-less `Location` values, it is non-standard. Some strict clients and scanners may mishandle it.
- **Truncated URL:** the mobile-app URL in the QA output cuts off at `bxid=6a013826e53`. This is likely a display truncation, not a defect.
- **Coverage:** only 12 of 38 links were probed, so 26 are unverified.
- **Missing alt text:** one image has no `alt`. Its src is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/uSUfF_oTHzYAJHqiyOKLgg~~/AAQRxRA~/A_-7gcvOk1cO…`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking or open pixel, which would make this low impact.
