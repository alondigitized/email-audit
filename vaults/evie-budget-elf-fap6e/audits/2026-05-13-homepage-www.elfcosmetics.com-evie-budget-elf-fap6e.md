# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally shop e.l.f. like every other week and this homepage is giving exactly what it should — they're pushing "The Cream Team," their new $7 cream blush and bronzer sticks, and my wallet is already crying in the best way. This feels like a brand that knows its TikTok girlies and isn't pretending to be Fenty. They're leaning into their affordable-and-iconic angle hard, and the Beauty Squad loyalty strip right at the top tells me they want me to sign up before I even see a product.

## What's working

- **"$7 each!" price callout** — I don't need a sale to get excited; that price IS the hook and it's front and center in the hero body copy. The simplicity works.
- **"THE CREAM TEAM" framing** — blush + bronzer together is a whole TikTok trend (the "sunkissed draping" look), so launching them as a duo is smart and culturally timed.
- **"STICK AROUND" button** — the pun (stick = blush stick) is the kind of self-aware corny that e.l.f. does well. It doesn't feel try-hard.
- **"e.l.f.ing iconic" copy** — using their name as a modifier the way we use a certain other F-word is extremely on-brand and I appreciate the commitment.
- **Beauty Squad strip above the fold** — even if I'm not a member yet, seeing "Get Free Shipping on your 1st order" immediately reduces my checkout anxiety before I've even picked a product.
- **Product imagery** — the two sticks shot on a clean pink background is minimal and lets the product colors do the talking. The red blush and bronze bronzer against each other look very giftable and very 'gram-able.

## What's weak

- **No human model in the viewport** — I see sticks on a background, not a person wearing the look. I want to see the blush on a real person (ideally a range of skintones) so I know what "diffused finish" actually means on skin.
- **"STICK AROUND" CTA is punny but not functional** — if someone didn't read the body copy they might not know this button takes them to shop blush + bronzer. Clever ≠ clear.
- **No reviews or social proof above fold** — e.l.f. is HUGE on TikTok, where the products go viral. There's no star rating, no "100K+ reviews" badge, no TikTok shop signal anywhere in this viewport to validate the purchase impulse.
- **Right-edge carousel peek is cut off** — I can see there's another slide or module barely visible on the right, but I can't tell what it is, so there's zero pull to swipe to it.
- **Free shipping threshold unclear** — the Beauty Squad strip says free shipping when you JOIN, not a flat order threshold. For someone not ready to sign up, there's no standard "free shipping over $X" reassurance.

## Recommendations

- **Add a before/after or on-skin lifestyle shot** — swap at least one still-life product image for a close-up of the blush + bronzer applied on a Gen Z face (diverse skintones), even a half-half split. That's the TikTok format that drives e.l.f. purchases.
- **Rewrite the CTA to layer clarity under the pun** — something like `STICK AROUND — Shop $7` so it's both clever AND tells me exactly what I'm clicking into. Don't make me work.
- **Drop a star rating or viral count into the hero** — even a simple "★★★★★ 12,400 reviews" line under the product name costs nothing and doubles credibility for first-time buyers.
- **Add a flat "Free shipping on orders $35+" line** directly in the hero or just below the CTA — most people aren't going to join Beauty Squad just to get free shipping on one order, but a reachable threshold would nudge me to add another $7 item to cart.
