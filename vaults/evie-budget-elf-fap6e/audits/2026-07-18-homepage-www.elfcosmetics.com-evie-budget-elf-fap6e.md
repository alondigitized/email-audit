# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay so e.l.f. is literally my brand and I opened this site and immediately got hit with a cookie consent wall that ate basically the whole screen — like, rude? I can peek at the hero behind it and "SPF that gets glitz done" is giving exactly the chaotic fun energy I expect from e.l.f., but I can't actually *see* the homepage because this GDPR modal is sitting on top of everything. The free shipping banner at the top ($35+) is doing its best, but the first thing I have to do is deal with three cookie buttons before I can even shop.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner** — "$35+ free shipping, Shop Now" in the top bar is pinned above the modal chaos and it's the first thing I read. $35 is very achievable for me (one foundation + a lip liner and I'm there).
- **"back by e.l.f.ing demand" headline** — I can see just enough of the hero copy to know e.l.f. is doing the thing they always do: being unserious in the best way. "e.l.f.ing" as a swear substitute is peak brand voice for this demo.
- **"SPF that gets glitz done"** — this is a product pitch AND a lifestyle pitch in six words. SPF + sparkle is literally the summer 2026 TikTok skincare-makeup fusion moment. They caught it.
- **Yellow/gold hero background** — even peeking around the modal, the warm gold feels intentional and premium-adjacent without trying too hard. It reads "glam" but stays playful.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal eating the entire viewport** — this is the biggest single UX sin I've seen today. The modal covers the hero image, every product CTA, and anything else that might have made me want to stay. On mobile it's especially brutal. First impression: a wall of legal text.
- **No CTA visible above the fold** — "Shop Now" in the free-shipping banner technically counts but it's tiny and in a promo strip, not a real hero button. I can't see the actual hero CTA at all.
- **Hero image is completely hidden** — there's presumably a product or model shot behind that modal but I have zero visibility. A hero image of the SPF product would have sold me immediately; I can't see it.
- **No loyalty hook above the fold** — e.l.f. has a rewards program but there's no sign of it here. Returning shoppers (me, obsessively) don't get any "welcome back" signal.
- **Subheadline gets cut off** — "Sparkling finish meets clear glide with" just… ends. The modal clips the copy mid-sentence. Even the partial hero is broken.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for 3–5 seconds or shrink it to a bottom banner** — a slim "We use cookies — Accept / Manage" bar at the bottom of the screen would preserve the hero and let me see the actual product pitch before I have to make a legal decision. The full-screen takeover on mobile is a bounce risk.
- **Pin a "Shop SPF Now" button above the cookie modal** — if the modal must take over, at least float the primary hero CTA above it in an overlay so I can act before I consent.
- **Show the loyalty balance / rewards CTA in the pinned top bar** — swap "Shop Now" in the shipping banner for a secondary loyalty hook like "Earn points on every order · Join Beauty Squad" so returning shoppers see their in.
- **Make the free shipping threshold a sticky banner, not just a once-seen strip** — as I scroll (once I can scroll), that $35 threshold should follow me so I'm always aware of how close I am.
