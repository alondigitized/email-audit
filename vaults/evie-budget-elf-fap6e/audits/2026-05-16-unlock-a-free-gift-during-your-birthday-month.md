# Unlock a FREE gift during your birthday month 💜
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a birthday-month trigger email built on top of the Beauty Squad loyalty mechanic — and as a concept, it's e.l.f. doing exactly what a loyalty program should do: rewarding members at a moment that feels personal. The purple hero lands with their signature confidence, and "FREE BDAY GIFT? SAY LESS." is the most decisive headline copy e.l.f. has run in the last six sends.
- The problem is execution: the email can't decide what it's selling. Below the hero, three separate "gift" products appear (Holy Hydration! Cleanser Mini, Glow Reviver Melting Face Balm, E.L.F.ING ICONIC BAG), and the bag section has a "GET THE DROPS" CTA that reads like a completely separate product launch bolted onto a birthday email. The hero promises one clear thing; the body delivers a buffet.
- The "with purchase" reveal is the other structural problem. "FREE BDAY GIFT?" sets an expectation; "UNWRAP THIS YEAR'S GIFT WITH PURCHASE" quietly downgrades it. That gap between promise and mechanics is small, but it's the kind of thing a subscriber notices — and it comes immediately after yesterday's Beauty Squad acquisition push ("You're an e.l.f.ing VIP!"), which means the payoff of joining the program felt free and is now asking for a purchase. The sequence would land harder if both the subject and the hero led with "free with any purchase."

## What's working

- **"FREE BDAY GIFT? SAY LESS."** — best hero headline in the last week of e.l.f. sends; confident, colloquial, on-brand, no wasted words.
- **Purple hero palette** — ties directly to the Beauty Squad color system and creates visual continuity with the "You're an e.l.f.ing VIP!" email from yesterday.
- **Birthday month trigger is correctly deployed** — this is the segmented, member-specific payoff that justified yesterday's loyalty-acquisition email; the arc makes sense when both sends are properly segmented.
- **Beauty Squad framing is in the right place** — the program is shown as the unlock mechanism, not buried in the footer.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout breakage, no overlapping text visible in this render.

## What's weak

- **Three gifts, zero commitment** — showing Cleanser Mini, Glow Reviver Face Balm, and the E.L.F.ING ICONIC BAG as birthday gifts splits the decision and dilutes the offer; pick one hero product.
- **"GET THE DROPS" CTA belongs in a different email** — the iconic bag section reads as a separate product-launch email stapled to the bottom; it competes with the birthday message and confuses the conversion path.
- **"FREE" in the hero doesn't survive the "with purchase" reveal** — the subject and hero overpromise; the body corrects the record, but the correction feels like a downgrade, not a clarification.
- **High send cadence hurts this email more than most** — e.l.f. has sent six emails in three days; a birthday trigger should feel special, but at this volume, it reads like another blast with a birthday badge on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero gift and cut the others** — "Your free Holy Hydration! Cleanser Mini with any birthday month purchase" is a concrete, actionable hero. Three options is a UX problem dressed up as generosity.
- 2. **Align the subject, hero, and CTA on the "with purchase" requirement** — don't hide it. Lead with it. "Free cleanser mini with any purchase this month" is more honest and converts better because it sets the right expectation.
- 3. **Pull the E.L.F.ING ICONIC BAG into its own send** — or drop it entirely. A birthday email should have one job.
- 4. **Add a hard date** — "birthday month" has no deadline tension. "Offer expires [date]" or "Valid through May 31" is a real urgency driver.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Your birthday perk is live — free cleanser mini 💜`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Happy birthday month — free gift with any purchase`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Beauty Squad members: grab a free Holy Hydration! mini with your birthday month purchase.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Your free birthday gift is waiting — shop any order this month to unlock it.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CONFIRMED BUG] Empty font-family first value:**
```css
font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif
```
The leading empty string `''` is a malformed value. The Google Font `Jost` is loaded via `<link>` but never referenced in the font stack — it will never render. Should be:
```css
font-family:'Jost',Verdana,sans-serif
```

**[MINOR] Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Several clients (Gmail web, some iOS notifications) surface the `<title>` value. Leaving it blank is a missed opportunity and can surface as untitled in some tab/notification contexts.

**[MINOR] Nested `nl-container` tables:** An `nl-container` table is nested inside a `<div>` inside a `<td>` inside another `nl-container`. This double-nesting is technically valid but adds unnecessary DOM depth that can cause Outlook line-height inheritance bugs.

**Positive findings:**
- `role="presentation"` present on all layout tables — correct
- `alt="e.l.f. beauty squad"` on the hero image — present
- `title="e.l.f. beauty squad"` on the hero image — present
- Proper MSO conditional `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` wrapping the Google Fonts `<link>` — correct; Outlook will not attempt to load it
