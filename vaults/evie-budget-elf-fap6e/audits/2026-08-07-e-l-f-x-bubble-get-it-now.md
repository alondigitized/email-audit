# e.l.f. x Bubble 👯‍♀️ Get it NOW! 
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s launch email for a genuine co-brand collab — e.l.f. x Bubble Skincare — and it's the most differentiated send in this brand's recent run. Where the last several sends (BOGO on 8/6, Set It Bright on 8/5, frizz spray on 8/4, watermelon lip balm on 8/3) were single-SKU or single-mechanic promos, this one is a real new-product story: three 2-in-1 hybrid SKUs (Drop N' Dunk, Glow Talk, Camo Slide) each pairing an e.l.f. shade with a Bubble skincare format, priced individually and laid out with dedicated hero shots.
- The top of funnel is strong — gradient hero, "SUPER-LIMITED DROP" framing, "starting at $10" — and the email does the work of explaining what the collab actually *is* rather than just slapping two logos together. Where it loses points is pacing and CTA repetition: six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons with zero differentiation, and a bottom nav section (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR) that renders as six stacked gray bars taking up a huge amount of scroll real estate for almost no content.
- Overall this is a well-conceived launch email let down by lazy CTA copy and a bloated tail. Strong open, average click follow-through once you're past the hero.

## What's working

- The hero clearly frames this as a co-brand launch ("e.l.f. x Bubble") with a distinct gradient/product-bottle visual that's on-brand and eye-catching.
- Each of the three hybrid products gets its own hero image, name, subhead, price, and shade-count badge ("3 SHADES") — genuinely informative, not just a wall of SKUs.
- "Top Picks for You" adds a personalization layer with three cross-sell products (Power Grip Primer, Cream Glide Lip Liner, Dewy Setting Spray) at accessible price points.
- Free shipping and Beauty Squad receipt-scanning rewards are called out in the footer, reinforcing value beyond this one drop.
- Consistent e.l.f. black/pink/teal visual system carries through the whole email — no tonal breaks.

## What's weak

- All six CTAs say "SHOP NOW" with no product-specific copy — a scanner can't tell which button goes where without reading the block above it first.
- The category nav (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) renders as six full-width stacked gray bars instead of a compact horizontal menu — it eats a large chunk of scroll depth for almost zero visual payoff.
- No discount or promo code anywhere — "starting at $10" is just the entry price point, not an incentive to act now beyond "limited drop" framing.
- No social proof (ratings, review counts, "bestseller" tags) on any of the three hero products, despite one subhead literally calling Bubble's moisturizer "iconic."
- No models/faces anywhere in the email — fine for a product-forward send, but it means there's no demographic or skin-tone representation signal at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate the CTAs: "Shop Drop N' Dunk," "Shop Glow Talk," "Shop Camo Slide" instead of six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons — this is the single highest-leverage fix since it currently forces re-reading to figure out where any click goes.
- 2. Collapse the six stacked nav bars into a single horizontal row or a 2x3 grid — reclaim that scroll depth for something that converts.
- 3. Add a real urgency mechanic to "SUPER-LIMITED DROP" — a restock/sell-out countdown or "while supplies last" line would make the limited-edition claim feel earned rather than decorative.
- 4. Add one social-proof line under the hero ("Bubble's #1 bestseller now in e.l.f. shades") to back up "iconic" and "viral" claims already in the copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `e.l.f. x Bubble: 3 new hybrids just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your skincare + your shade, finally together 👯‍♀️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `2-in-1 hybrids from $10 — shades meet skincare, for real this time`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bubble's bestsellers, now in e.l.f.'s holy grail shades`

## Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics x Bubble Email

**1. Technical Summary**
The email uses a Cheetah/Cordial-style click-tracking redirect layer (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) wrapping all CTAs; automated QA returned a 27% pass rate, driven by 4 broken link destinations, a missing plain-text MIME part, and missing unsubscribe/authentication headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 4 of 12 probed links resolve to relative paths instead of absolute URLs, causing "unknown url type" errors:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&utm_source=broadcast&is_retargeting=true&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&lid=c50ux6y75v` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  - All four resolve behind valid-looking `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect wrappers, but the QA resolver could not derive a scheme+host from the redirect target — the destination is being served as a bare relative path rather than a fully-qualified URL.
- Remaining 8 probed links returned no errors; 29 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed per QA methodology.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Outlook/MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` are present and correctly scoped for Word-engine rendering.
- Preheader text ("We made it e.l.f.ficial with Bubble.") is correctly hidden via `display:none!important;visibility:hidden;mso-hide:all;font-size:1px` pattern.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width:660px` with `.stack`/`.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes is present and structured correctly.
- Hero image (`image_assets/.../original.png`) includes `alt="e.l.f."` and `title="e.l.f."`.
- QA flags one image missing alt text: element id `cFosaEUZGn92Ie3TwuIiIK0cowapnwdccVZ7tlsubLHOBRaMRG`, `src="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/17kYQoxHuwuKKYCjdH52Mw~~/AAQRxRA~/cFosaEUZGn92Ie3TwuIiIK0cowapnwdccVZ7tlsubLHOBRaMRG65V"` — the `/q/` path suggests this is an open-tracking beacon rather than content, but it should still carry `alt=""` to avoid screen readers announcing the raw filename.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%field%%`) appear in the provided HTML. No issues found — note the source is truncated, so this cannot be fully confirmed for the untruncated remainder.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected (WARN) — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a confirmed absence at origin.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe under 2024 bulk-sender rules) not detected (WARN).
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM) not detected — status unknown via this relay.
- A body-level unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA probing methodology, but this doesn't substitute for the header-level mechanism required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Only 1 of the 4 flagged CTA destinations carries explicit tracking params (`c=email&utm_source=broadcast&is_retargeting=true`, mobile app link); the other 3 destination paths (product pages, account login) show no UTM parameters in the resolved path.
- The relative-path resolution issue in Section 2 directly breaks email→site continuity: without a resolvable host, these 3 product/account links cannot be confirmed to land on a valid page.

**7. Recommendations**
- Fix redirect-target URL generation so all 4 flagged links resolve to fully-qualified absolute URLs (missing scheme/host).
- Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- Generate a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative (currently 0 chars).
- Add `alt=""` (or descriptive alt) to the flagged tracking-beacon image.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM pass status at the sending ESP, independent of relay header visibility.
- Standardize UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) across all CTAs, not just the mobile app link.
