# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay e.l.f. knows exactly what they're doing — this is peak budget-beauty-girlie territory and I felt that immediately. They're launching "The Cream Team," a new cream blush and bronzer duo at $7 each, and honestly that price point combined with the TikTok-coded "e.l.f.ing iconic" copy made me stop scrolling. This looks like a brand that does their homework on what's trending.

## What's working

- **"$7 each!" in the hero copy** — that price point does literal heavy lifting for a budget shopper. I don't have to click anything to know if this is for me or not.
- **"THE CREAM TEAM" headline** — cream products are everywhere on my FYP right now; leading with that positions e.l.f. as trend-aware, not trend-chasing.
- **Beauty Squad strip above the fold** — visible loyalty hook before I've even scrolled once. Tells me the brand remembers I exist.
- **"e.l.f.ing iconic" subhead** — that playful brand voice is so distinctly theirs. Instantly recognizable, not trying too hard.
- **Subscribe & Save banner at the very top** — free gift + free shipping for subscribers? That's the first thing I read. Sets a generous tone immediately.
- **The product shot** — two sleek cream sticks, warm peachy tones, very editorial for $7. Looks more expensive than it is.

## What's weak

- **"STICK AROUND" CTA is cute but ambiguous** — I get the pun (stick = cream stick), but it doesn't tell me what happens when I tap it. "Shop The Cream Team" or even "Shop $7 Sticks" would convert harder.
- **Beauty Squad strip is partially cut off on the right** — there's a second panel ("Scan… when you… Squad") that gets clipped. Whatever that offer is, I can't read it.
- **No model or skin-tone context in the visible crop** — I can see the product sticks but no one wearing them. For a blush/bronzer, I want to see it on skin before I commit.
- **No urgency signal** — $7 is compelling but there's no "limited launch" or "while supplies last" to push me from "cute" to "add to cart now."
- **Free shipping tied to joining Beauty Squad** — feels like a conditional, not a universal perk. Could feel like a small bait if you're not already a member.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STICK AROUND" for "Shop The Cream Team — $7 Each"** — keep the playful energy in the subhead, but make the button copy do conversion work.
- **Add a before/after or on-skin swatch to the hero** — even a small secondary image of the blush on two or three different skin tones would do it. TikTok trained us to expect shade range proof.
- **Reveal the clipped Beauty Squad "Scan" panel** — if that's an app scan-to-earn feature, that's a strong Gen Z hook and it shouldn't be cut off on load.
- **Add a soft scarcity line** — something like "New drop — stock goes fast" or a low-key "limited shades" call in the hero subtext would create just enough urgency without feeling manipulative.
