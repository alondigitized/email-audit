# Find your shade & Set It Bright! 🤩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s launch email for the new Set It Bright powder quad, built as a shade-finder: five skin-tone ranges (Fair, Light, Light/Medium, Medium/Tan, Deep/Rich) each get a matched model photo and a 2x2 grid of correcting/brightening callouts, plus a bonus universal "Lilac Haze" shade. It's a genuinely useful, inclusive format — more functional than the 8/3 watermelon lip balm single-SKU send, closer in spirit to the 7/30 hair-spray tutorial email but applied to shade-matching instead of usage steps.
- The catch is that this is a full-price launch with zero discount mechanic, a real departure from the cadence this brand has been running — 7/29's Glow Reviver BOGO and 7/31's "last chance" lippie GWP both scored 8-9 and both leaned on a concrete incentive. Here there's no %/$ off, no GWP, just "$12" and curiosity. Combined with a long shade-comparison module that only has SHOP NOW buttons at the very top and very bottom, this is a well-designed but under-monetized send — good top-of-funnel education, weaker at converting the moment of recognition into a click.
- **Judgement:** solid creative execution, right feature for a new SKU, but it's leaving clicks on the table by not putting a CTA next to each shade match and by skipping any incentive.

## What's working

- The five-tone-plus-Lilac-Haze structure with matched model photography makes shade-finding genuinely usable, not just decorative.
- Hero is clean and fast: headline ("set it bright in 6 shades"), price ($12), and a SHOP NOW button all land above the fold with no clutter.
- The "new!" badge correctly signals this is a fresh launch, not a recycled promo — consistent with actual campaign timing.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sell (Power Grip Primer, Mini Holy Hydration cream, Setting Spray) adds relevant prep/finish products at accessible price points without derailing the main story.
- No render bugs — clean gradient background, no overlapping text, no broken images across a long, image-heavy layout.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, BOGO, or GWP mechanic — breaks from the brand's recent higher-performing sends (7/29 BOGO, 7/31 GWP last-chance) that both scored 8-9.
- Only two SHOP NOW buttons bracket the entire five-section shade module — a reader who identifies their tone in the middle has to scroll away from the CTA to act.
- CTA copy never varies from generic "SHOP NOW," missing a chance to reinforce the shade-match hook from the subject.
- No social proof (star ratings, review counts) anywhere near a product whose main purchase barrier is shade-match uncertainty.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "SHOP NOW" (or shade-specific) button under every individual tone section, not just top and bottom — this is the single biggest fix, since it closes the gap between "that's my shade" recognition and the click.
- 2. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for hook-matched copy like "Shop My Shade" to tie the CTA back to the subject line's promise.
- 3. Add a lightweight trust signal (star rating or reviewer count) near the hero or the Lilac Haze block to offset shade-match anxiety.
- 4. Surface "free shipping" (currently buried in the footer) closer to the CTA to compensate for the lack of a price incentive.

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics — "Find your shade & Set It Bright!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-templated, table-based HTML build with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup, but 4 of 12 probed CTAs resolve to malformed destination URLs and the message ships with no plain-text part and no detectable unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through a Braze click-tracking redirector (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`). Four resolve to destinations the QA prober flags as **"unknown url type"** — the decoded final destination is a bare relative path with no scheme/host, e.g.:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (link text: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` (link text: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (link text: "Receipt scanning...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_campaign=080526_set_it_bright_shaderange&utm_medium=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.a` (link text: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  
  This pattern (relative path, no `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` host) indicates the underlying destination URL was not fully qualified when the redirect map was built — a genuine send-side link-configuration bug, not a prober limitation, since all four share the same malformed shape.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/_KQtZrD04-Z35eCQl_QX8g~~/AAQRxRA~/NbDcQb6wTk0dHNazPm4dNvR98HVPOp8l93a8mmS4Am6ho39LGc8f2` — functioning as expected, but see §3 for missing alt text.
- QA probed 12 of 45 total links (material/high-click-likelihood only); footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not verified — those 33 are unconfirmed, not passing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Missing `alt` attribute on tracking pixel image `NbDcQb6wTk0dHNazPm4dNvR98HVPOp8l93a8mmS4Am6ho39LGc` (src above). Low visual impact (1x1-style tracking asset) but fails automated alt-text checks.
- Hero image (`https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/6532bc91543698004cb67f6e/original.png?1697823889`) correctly has `alt="e.l.f."` and `title="e.l.f."`.
- Preheader text is correctly hidden (`display:none!important;mso-hide:all;...max-height:0px`) with fallback text "Blur, brighten, correct & set."
- Markup contains a redundant nested structure: `block-1` (an `html_block`) contains a full second copy of `<table class="nl-container">…<table class="row row-1">…</table>` wrapping the hero image, duplicating the outer document's own `nl-container`/`row-1` scaffold. This is consistent with an HTML snippet being pasted with its own table wrapper into another table cell rather than as a bare fragment — adds markup weight and nested-table depth that can cause inconsistent spacing/rendering in Outlook's Word engine.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and the 660px mobile breakpoint media query are correctly structured for responsive/Outlook fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, Liquid/Handlebars syntax) are present in the available HTML source. No issues found in the visible markup — note the source is truncated, so personalization blocks later in the template are not covered by this review.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected. `[WARN]` — may be a header-capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than absence at origin; cannot confirm either way from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Plain-text MIME part**: missing/0 chars — `[FAIL]`. CAN-SPAM doesn't mandate a text part, but its absence removes a fallback unsubscribe/compliance path for clients that don't render HTML and commonly hurts inbox placement.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM)**: not detected via the relay — status unknown, not necessarily failing.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA (footer link always included in probe set), but its exact URL/text was not captured in the excerpt provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Only one probed link carries UTM parameters: `/pages/mobile-app?utm_campaign=080526_set_it_bright_shaderange&utm_medium=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.a` — and that link is itself one of the four broken/malformed destinations (§2).
- The two product CTAs (`/products/power-grip-primer?Size=...`, `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=...`) carry only a `Size` variant param — **no `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`** — breaking campaign attribution continuity between email click and site analytics even if the link were otherwise valid.
- Because the four flagged destinations lack a resolvable host, landing-page alignment cannot be verified at all for those CTAs — they cannot be confirmed to land on any real e.l.f. page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect map for the 4 broken CTAs (power-grip-primer, holy-hydration-face-cream, account/login, mobile-app deep link) — re-qualify each with the full `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` host before the Braze link is generated.
2. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` (and ideally `utm_content`) to the two product PDP links to restore attribution continuity.
3. Add `alt=""` (decorative) or a descriptive `alt` to the tracking pixel image, or confirm it's intentionally non-semantic.
4. Generate and attach a plain-text MIME part matching the HTML content.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are set at the ESP/ISP-relay layer and verify they survive AgentMail relay transit — retest with direct header capture at the sending MTA if the current WARN is a relay artifact.
6. Remove the duplicated nested `nl-container`/`row-1` table wrapper inside `block-1` to reduce markup depth and Outlook rendering risk.
