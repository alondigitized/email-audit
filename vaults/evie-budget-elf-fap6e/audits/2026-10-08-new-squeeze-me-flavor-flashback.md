# NEW! Squeeze Me Flavor Flashback 🫦
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, playful, single-story launch for the Squeeze Me lip balm "Flavor Flashback" collection. The hero is strong: a model biting a pretzel while holding a pretzel-flavored tube makes the concept clear in one glance. "Only $5" sits right under the headline, so the offer is easy to find. The flip-phone, charm-bracelet and pink-fuzz block sells the nostalgia theme well.
- It loses ground after the hero. Four stacked emoji benefit lines add little. Then a color-swatch strip names the flavors (fruity o's, milk carton, mall pretzel, fruit punch pouch, banana candy) with no product shots and no prices. The three "Top Picks for You" items are a primer, a lip liner and a setting spray, none of them lip balm. The $11 primer and spray dilute a $5 impulse-buy message. There are three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons and no urgency beyond "limited edition."
- Judgement: solid, on-brand creative with a good concept, but the middle of the email is repetitive and the bottom has weak merchandising. It is a typical e.l.f. 7 for a subscriber who cares about lips.

## What's working

- The hero image shows the product in use with a snack tie-in, which makes the "throwback snack" idea instant.
- "Only $5" is placed right beside the "new & limited edition!" label in the headline block.
- The pink, nostalgic flip-phone block fits the Flavor Flashback theme.
- The flavor color strip is fun and fits the "which flavs do you crave?" hook.
- The footer has useful loyalty and shipping items: free shipping, receipt scanning and the Beauty Squad app.

## What's weak

- The "Top Picks for You" items (primer, liner, setting spray at $11 and $2) are unrelated to the lip story and conflict with the $5 message.
- The flavor strip has no product images, no individual links and no names on the actual tubes, so I can't tell which product is which.
- The emoji benefit list (nourishing, pocket-sized, 5 flavors, charms) is generic, and the small black "SHOP NOW" buttons are repeated three times with no variation.
- The category nav rows (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are big grey blocks that take up a lot of space and add friction.
- There is no deadline or stock cue, so "limited edition" carries all the urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the swatch strip with the five actual tubes, labeled by flavor and priced at $5, each linking to its own page. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Swap "Top Picks" for lip-focused items such as a lip oil, a liner and a lip bundle, or add a "build your set" offer.
- 3. Cut the emoji list to one line and move the product grid up to just after the hero.
- 4. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Shop $5 lip balms", and add a real deadline or "while supplies last" cue.
- 5. Shrink the category nav into a compact text row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5 snack-flavored lip balms. $5 each. 🥨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pretzel, banana candy, milk carton… your lips will crave these`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition Squeeze Me flavors, only $5 while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Which throwback snack is your lip balm? Pick your flav.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**

The visible HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables and a hidden preheader. The 4 FAIL link results look like artifacts of the QA probe, not defects in the email. The missing plain-text part and missing headers can't be confirmed from this data.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All four "Link error: unknown url type" failures have the same cause. The probe followed each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` tracking redirect and got a **relative** destination it couldn't resolve:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_medium=email&onelink_id=wTtZ&...`
- A relative `Location` header is legal under RFC 7231. The tracked redirects returned destinations, so they likely work in a real client. This is probably a probe or redirect-resolution gap, so treat it as unconfirmed, not a broken experience.
- The probe never validated the final destinations, so the product and account pages are untested.
- The `/pages/mobile-app?...` URL is cut off at `lid=ydaobyf`. This is probably truncation in the QA output, since the redirect token itself is complete.
- Only 12 of 40 links were probed. The other 28, mostly footer and social links, are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the mobile-hidden `html_block` is an empty font name. It's harmless because the fallback works, but it's sloppy.
- The logo link has `tabindex="-1"`, which removes it from keyboard navigation. Its image alt is `e.l.f.`, which is fine.
- One image has no alt text. Its src is `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/WbT7CIco...`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking pixel, which makes this low severity. The image wasn't fetched, so that's unconfirmed.
- Nested duplicate `nl-container` and `row-1` classes inside `html_block` wrappers add markup weight. This is a Braze/Bee export pattern and not a functional problem.
- The Open Sans webfont link is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the fallback stack is `Arial,Sans-serif`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered Liquid or merge tokens (`{{`, `${`, `%%`) appear in the visible source. Only part of the source was available, so the remainder is unchecked.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** the checker did not find them. The report says AgentMail may strip them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not captured, so the status is unknown. The From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Plain-text part:** the text version is 0 chars, which was flagged as a FAIL. This may be an ingestion artifact. If the real message is HTML-only, spam filters can penalize it and text-only clients will show nothing.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer was truncated out of the source. The probe says an unsubscribe link was always included, but I can't confirm the address or the link text.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- The product links deep-link to variants (`?Size=...`, `?Color=...`), which is good practice.
- The only visible UTM set is on the app link: `utm_medium=email&utm_source=broadcast&pid=Email&c=email`. It has no `utm_campaign` or `utm_content`, so campaign-level attribution relies on the Braze params (`bxid`, `lid`).
- The "Receipt scanning" CTA lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. That is a login wall, not content, and the UTMs may not survive the return redirect.
- I couldn't check whether the subject ("Squeeze Me Flavor Flashback") matches the destinations, because the probe didn't load them.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-probe the four redirects with relative-`Location` resolution against the destination origin to confirm the pages return 200. Don't treat the current FAILs as defects until then.
2. Confirm the plain-text MIME part in the raw message. If it's really empty, add a multipart/alternative text version.
3. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The AgentMail relay may be dropping them.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add alt text to the tracked image (`alt=""` if it's a pixel), and drop `tabindex="-1"` from the logo link.
5. Add `utm_campaign` and `utm_content` to the landing URLs.
6. Capture the full HTML including the footer, so the address and unsubscribe link can be verified.
