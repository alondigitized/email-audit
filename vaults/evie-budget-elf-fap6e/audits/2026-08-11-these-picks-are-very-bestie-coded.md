# These picks are very Bestie-coded 😍 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s weekly roundup/digest send, and it undercuts itself right out of the gate: the hero banner is a gorgeous, glossy shot of the e.l.f. x Bubble lip products with a "3 SHADES" badge — genuinely eye-catching — but the headline underneath it reads "YOUR WEEKLY HIGHLIGHT REEL / Peep our latest picks for you," a generic catch-all that has nothing to do with the product in the hero. The visual promises a collab launch; the copy delivers a filler roundup.
- After the header, the email becomes a pure catalog scroll: two rows of unlabeled product thumbnails ("YOUR NEW OBSESSIONS"), then four more products under "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK," then six flat gray category bars (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair). There's no price, no product name, no offer anywhere in the body — the only monetary hook is "free shipping, limited time only" buried in the footer icon row. Compare this to the brand's last several sends — the Bubble collab launch (8/7), the BOGO push (8/6), the Set It Bright shade-finder (8/5) — which all had one clear hero product and a specific mechanic. This one has neither; it's a low-effort holding-pattern email between real campaigns.
- Verdict: clean and on-brand visually, but it's the weakest send in this brand's recent run because it has no offer, no named hero product, and a headline that ignores its own hero image.

## What's working

- Hero image (e.l.f. x Bubble lip oils/glosses with "3 SHADES" badge) is well-lit, dynamic, and on-brand.
- Bold black-and-white typography and consistent e.l.f. voice carry through the whole email.
- Two clear, high-contrast primary CTAs ("SHOP BEST SELLERS" / "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS") sit right under the headline.
- Category navigation bar (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) gives an easy path to browse if the shopper isn't sold by the roundup.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and footer all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The hero image (Bubble collab) and the headline/CTAs underneath are completely disconnected — nothing links the gorgeous hero product to a "shop this" action.
- Every product tile in "YOUR NEW OBSESSIONS" and "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK" is unlabeled — no product names, no prices, no shade/size info.
- No concrete offer anywhere in the visible body; the only savings mention ("free shipping, limited time") is a small footer icon, not a headline hook.
- The email reads as a generic weekly digest with no urgency, no theme, and no single hero product to click through to.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero do its job: put the Bubble collab name and a "SHOP THE COLLAB" button directly under/on the hero image instead of a disconnected generic headline — this is the single biggest lift available since the visual is already strong.
- 2. Add product names and prices under every thumbnail in "YOUR NEW OBSESSIONS" and "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK" — unlabeled tiles ask the reader to guess what they're clicking.
- 3. Surface the free-shipping offer higher, ideally as a banner line near the top, not buried in the footer icon row.
- 4. Give the email an actual theme or mechanic (like the recent BOGO or shade-finder sends) rather than a generic "highlight reel" — even a light framing ("bestie-approved picks") would tie the subject line to the body.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email uses Braze/Iterable-style redirect tracking with a non-functional link-shortening layer: 9 of 12 probed CTAs resolve to relative store paths instead of absolute URLs, breaking every major product and navigation link. Plain-text fallback and unsubscribe compliance headers are also absent from the payload captured.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 9 of 12 probed links fail with "unknown url type" — the tracking redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) is decoding to a relative path (e.g. `/products/blemish-control-face-primer?Size=Large+1+oz+%2F+30+ml`) rather than a fully-qualified `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URL. Affected CTAs: "Shop Now" (×3 product links), "Receipt scanning" account link, "SHOP BEST SELLERS" (×2), "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS", "beauty squad" app deep link.
- Query strings are otherwise well-formed (UTM params, `lid`, `bxid` present) — the defect is isolated to the missing scheme/host, not param construction.
- One tracked image pixel lacks alt text: asset id `Cg5WiBQ-ikkHNkLkaEi5ynTkuBXUCYQvVUb53YyXkhLdoFVZu-` (src `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/JJxOxKVno4FcfSoU4LgUBQ~~/...`).
- 31 lower-priority (footer/social/utility) links were not probed; unsubscribe link was included in probing per QA policy but no failure reported for it specifically.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Preheader uses standard hidden-div + zero-width-joiner padding technique — correctly implemented (two `display:none !important` blocks, one visible preview text "Peep our top picks for you!", one whitespace padding block).
- MSO conditional comments present for Outlook (`<!--[if mso]>` blocks for Word rendering settings and `sup`/`sub` sizing) — standard defensive markup, no issues found.
- Responsive breakpoint at 660px with `.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes implemented via media query — no issues found.
- Header hero image (`image_assets/.../original.png`) has `alt="e.l.f."` — acceptable but generic; not flagged as it's present.
- One image missing alt text (see #2 above) — flag under accessibility as well: screen readers will announce nothing for this asset.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%TOKEN%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a capture gap in the AgentMail relay rather than absence at the sending MTA; cannot confirm compliance status either way from this data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected via AgentMail relay — authentication status unknown, not confirmed failing.
- Recommend verifying these three headers directly against the source MTA rather than the relay-captured copy before treating as a compliance failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters are consistently applied where visible: `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=081126_weekly_edit`, plus `lid`/`bxid`/`pid` tracking params — structurally consistent across CTAs.
- Continuity cannot be verified end-to-end because the redirect targets are malformed relative paths (see #2) — this blocks any landing-page-alignment check downstream of the click, not just the click itself.
- App deep link (`com.elfcosmetics.app://home`) includes fallback params (`shortlink=email`, `pid=Email`) — structurally correct, same relative-path resolution issue applies before the deep link fallback fires.

7. Recommendations
1. **Priority — fix redirect resolution**: The link-shortening/redirect service is stripping or failing to prepend the scheme+host on 9 of 12 CTAs, sending users to `unknown url type: '/products/...'` errors. Audit the Braze (or equivalent ESP) link-tracking template for a missing base-URL variable in the destination-URL field.
2. Add `alt` text to the image asset `Cg5WiBQ-ikkHNkLkaEi5ynTkuBXUCYQvVUb53YyXkhLdoFVZu-`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are present at the source MTA — current absence is only confirmed at the relay capture point, not the origin.
4. Verify a plain-text MIME alternative is generated and non-empty; current capture shows 0 chars, which risks spam-filter penalties and breaks plain-text-preferring clients.
