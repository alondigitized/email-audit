# GIVEAWAY 💜 Win a year of Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s Indy 500 tie-in giveaway — "FUEL YOUR e.l.f." with a year's supply of Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick up for grabs alongside Katherine Legge's #20 IndyCar livery. It's a smart cultural moment for the brand: they're sponsoring a female IndyCar driver, and the racing photography is genuinely arresting — the purple #20 car, the driver in a varsity jacket, real motorsport energy. That's a creative swing most beauty brands wouldn't attempt.
- Then the email keeps going. Below the giveaway is a stacked second hero ("Empyting [sic] Legendary Items!" — that's a visible typo), then a "6 hours to score" flash callout on Glow Reviver Lip Oils, then a Walgreens free-shipping module, then Top Picks, then category nav, then footer. Four competing CTAs in one send. The giveaway hero is strong enough to carry the email alone; instead it's buried in a kitchen-sink stack.
- Net read: top half is an 8. Bottom three-quarters drags it to a 6. And that "Empyting" misspell in headline type is a real miss for a brand that usually nails its puns.

## What's working

- Hero photography is genuinely on-brand and bold — purple IndyCar, helmet shot, driver portrait all read editorial.
- "FUEL YOUR e.l.f." pun ties product (lip oil) to the racing context cleanly.
- "A year of free Glow Reviver" is a concrete, memorable prize stakes.
- Time-bounded "6 hours to score" callout creates real urgency for the secondary offer.
- Walgreens free-shipping module signals omni-channel reach for non-members.

## What's weak

- Visible typo in a headline: "Empyting Legendary Items!" — should be "Emptying." Inexcusable in display type.
- Four stacked offers (giveaway, restock, flash sale, Walgreens shipping) compete and dilute the hero.
- Subject says "GIVEAWAY" but the email pivots to a flash sale and restock — bait-then-merchandise feel.
- Top Picks grid below the fold feels like template filler when the giveaway should be the single ask.
- No visible entry mechanic / deadline for the giveaway in the hero area — what's the action? "Shop Now" doesn't fit a sweepstakes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email in half.** The giveaway deserves its own send. Pull the restock and the 6-hour flash into a separate email tomorrow. One ask per send.
- 2. **Fix the typo immediately** — "Empyting" → "Emptying." Resend or correct ASAP; a misspelled hero in display type damages trust.
- 3. **Make the giveaway mechanic explicit in the hero** — "Enter by [date]" + a button that says "Enter to Win," not "Shop Now." Right now I don't know how to enter.
- 4. **Lead the subject with the prize, not the format.** "Win a year of Glow Reviver" pulls more than "GIVEAWAY 💜 Win a year of…" which front-loads a generic format word.
- 5. **Use the Katherine Legge sponsorship harder** — name her, link her story. "Beauty fuels speed" or similar. Right now the IndyCar imagery is decorative; it could be a brand moment.
