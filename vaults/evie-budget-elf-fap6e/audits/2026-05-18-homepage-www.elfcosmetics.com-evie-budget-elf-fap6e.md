# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay e.l.f. is literally *me* so I'm biased going in, but even setting that aside — this homepage knows exactly who it's talking to. The Cream Team hero is very "TikTok blush moment" energy, the $7 price point is right there in the copy, and the Beauty Squad free-shipping hook hits the second you open the app. It's pushing a newness story with a cheeky pun and I respect the hustle.

## What's working

- **The $7 price callout in the hero body copy** — this is the whole brand value prop in three characters and it's doing exactly what it should
- **"e.l.f.ing iconic"** — that phrasing is so on-brand Gen Z I genuinely laughed; feels like it came from someone who actually uses TikTok
- **The mascara bundle deal in the top banner** — "$5 off when you buy 2" is a concrete, useful offer for someone stocking up (me, always me)
- **Beauty Squad placement** — right below the nav, above the fold, immediate free-shipping incentive before I've even seen the hero
- **The model** — curly hair, warm skin, gold hoops, natural blush application — this is not a runway shot, it's a "my friend is doing my makeup" shot and it works

## What's weak

- **"STICK AROUND" as a CTA** — love the pun, but it doesn't tell me I'm going to a product page; someone less familiar with the brand might genuinely not know what it does
- **No product images in the hero** — I can see the model applying *something* but I can't actually see the blush or bronzer product itself; I want to see the packaging and the shade range
- **No category nav visible** — the hamburger menu means my lipstick or eyeshadow categories are buried one extra tap away; I might bounce to Target app instead
- **No trust signals at all** — no review count, no "100,000+ 5-star reviews," nothing; e.l.f. has incredible ratings and they're leaving that leverage on the table
- **Right-side carousel panel is cut off** — there's something getting sliced on the right edge that I can't read; if it's an offer or product, it's invisible

## Recommendations

- **Swap "STICK AROUND" for "Shop The Cream Team — $7 Each"** — keep the pun in the body copy, make the button actually explain what happens when you tap it
- **Add a shade grid or product flat-lay under the hero copy** — show me the actual blush + bronzer SKUs so I can see if my shade is in there before I tap through
- **Surface review count as a trust strip** — one line: "⭐ 4.7 · 2M+ reviews" between the loyalty banner and the hero converts skeptics
- **Reveal the right-side carousel panel** — whatever is being cut off on that right edge should either be surfaced or removed; partial content is friction
