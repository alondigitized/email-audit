# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I literally can't see the homepage because a full-screen cookie consent wall is eating 80% of my screen the second I land. What I *can* peek at behind it looks fun — colorful skincare packaging in lime, blue, and purple, what looks like a real-person UGC moment ("Anna uses Never Ti[red]…"), and the brand yellow on the hero. But I can't engage with any of it because I'm being interrogated about my data choices before I've even seen a product.

## What's working

- **Free shipping banner** — "Free Shipping with all orders $35+!" is right at the very top in that bold blue strip and that threshold is genuinely reachable for me (I regularly hit $40+ in a single Target run). No ambiguity, no asterisk visible.
- **Nav bar is clean and fast** — hamburger, search, profile, cart all visible and tappable. I know where to go if I can just get past the modal.
- **Hero color palette peeking through** — the lime green + periwinkle + lavender on what looks like a skincare lineup is very on-trend Gen Z. If I could see it properly it would hook me.
- **"Anna uses Never Ti[red]…" UGC framing** — calling out a real person using a product by name feels TikTok-coded, like a creator haul or "this is my everyday." That's my language.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is a wall, not a banner** — it takes up the full viewport on mobile. I can't see the hero, I can't see a CTA, I can't see a product. This is the very first thing I see and it's a legal form.
- **Three-button cookie UI is a soft dark pattern** — "ACCEPT ALL" is black and dominant, "REJECT NON-ESSENTIAL" is white/outline, "CUSTOMIZE MY CHOICES" is ghost. The visual weight pushes me toward accept. I notice this.
- **No loyalty hook at all** — e.l.f. has a rewards program. Zero mention of it above the fold, even in the promo strip.
- **"Shop Now" in the top banner links to… what?** — "Free Shipping with all orders $35+! Shop Now" — Shop what? Gives me nothing to click toward.
- **Hero is wasted** — whatever campaign is running (looks skincare, looks fun) is completely invisible until I clear the modal.

## Recommendations

- **Switch the cookie consent to a bottom sheet or slim persistent banner** — the full-screen wall is killing first impressions. A 72px bottom strip with "We use cookies — Accept / Manage" would let me see the homepage immediately.
- **Add the e.l.f. rewards/points callout to the promo strip** — something like "Free Shipping $35+ | Earn points on every order →" turns one strip into two reasons to engage.
- **Make the "Shop Now" in the promo banner category-specific** — "Shop Skincare," "Shop Lip," whatever the hero is pushing. Generic "Shop Now" wastes the click.
- **Let the UGC hero breathe** — if "Anna uses Never Tired" is the campaign, put that text bigger so it's visible even partially behind a slim consent bar. That hook is doing real work if I could actually read it.
