## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics, "Bestie,, your fall lineup needs these 🍁"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based Braze-style template with a branded click-tracking domain. It has one probe failure on the "Receipt scanning" CTA, a visible punctuation bug in the subject line, and a few markup defects. SPF/DKIM and the List-Unsubscribe headers could not be verified because of relay capture limits. The HTML was also truncated, so the footer was not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Failed probe (confirmed):** The "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." link (`https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/QRxqDaave-wq1N4f…`) failed with `unknown url type: '/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount'`.
  - The redirect chain ends in a **relative** path, `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`.
  - Either the tracker returns a relative `Location` header, which is non-compliant and handled inconsistently, or the probe failed to resolve it against the base host.
  - Either way, the final destination is a login wall (`/account/login`) rather than a content page. A logged-out recipient lands on a sign-in form.
  - The failure is not confirmed to be a real user-facing break. Re-test with a client that resolves relative redirects.
- **Probe coverage gap:** Only 12 of 36 links were probed and 24 were skipped, so link health is unverified for about two-thirds of the email. This includes the footer and social links.
- **Tracking pixel:** The `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/Dz3ABNEN…` URL matches the open-pixel pattern (`/q/`). The wrapper domain is first-party and aligned with the sender's `m.elfcosmetics.com`, which is good.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty font-family (confirmed):** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` appears on the `html_block` wrapper div. The empty quoted family is invalid and is probably an unset template variable. It falls back to Verdana, so the impact is low.
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` gives no document title for screen readers or web-view rendering.
- **Missing alt text:** The image `src=https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/Dz3ABNEN…` has no alt attribute. This is probably the tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so it is explicitly decorative.
- **`@import` for web font in body:** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost…')` sits in a `<style>` inside the body block, and a `<link>` to Google Fonts is also in `<head>`.
  - Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore both, so Jost will not load there and the fallback stack applies.
  - The font is loaded twice (`<link>` and `@import`) for no benefit.
- **Nested duplicate structure:** The `html_block` embeds a second `nl-container`/`row-1`/`row-content` table tree inside the first. This duplicates class names and adds nesting depth with no purpose.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,793 of 11,552 characters (85%) are URLs, because of the long tracking links. This is a low-severity deliverability signal and an unreadable text alternative.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Subject line punctuation bug (confirmed):** `Bestie,, your fall lineup…` has a doubled comma.
  - "Bestie" looks like a fallback value for an empty first-name token.
  - The template likely appends a comma after a fallback that already includes one, as in `{{first_name | default: 'Bestie,'}},`.
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) were found in the visible HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified rather than confirmed missing.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
  - Verify against raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer (physical address, unsubscribe link):** Not visible in the truncated HTML, so this could not be assessed. The QA report says an unsubscribe link was probed, but the result isn't listed as a failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are opaque tracking tokens (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…`), so UTM parameters and landing alignment could not be verified from the source. Resolved final URLs for the probed links are not in the QA data.
- The one resolved destination, the receipt-scanning link, ends at `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. It has no campaign context in `return_to` and lands on a generic account page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the subject-line template so the fallback and punctuation don't both emit a comma, and add a test for the empty-first-name case.
2. Re-probe the receipt-scanning link and make the tracker return an absolute `Location` header. Consider pointing it at a logged-out-friendly landing page instead of `/account/login`.
3. Remove the empty `font-family:''` and set `<title>` to the subject or brand name.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and drop the duplicate Jost `@import` and `<link>`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results from raw headers, since the relay hides them.
6. Run the QA probe at full coverage, or at least on all CTA and footer links, and log the resolved final URLs with their UTM parameters.
7. Shorten the plain-text part, or trim its link wrappers, to lower the URL ratio.
