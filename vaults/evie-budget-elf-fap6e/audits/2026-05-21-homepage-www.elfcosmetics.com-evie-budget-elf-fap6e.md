# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay wait, this is literally my site. e.l.f. knows exactly what they're doing — the whole "rosé all day" hero is giving summer, it's giving soft-glam, and that $11 price tag on a NEW product is right in my comfort zone. This homepage is pushing a fresh launch with a strong Beauty Squad loyalty hook front and center, which honestly got me before I even scrolled.

## What's working

- **"rosé all day" hero headline** — that's a TikTok caption, not just a tagline. It grabbed me instantly. It sounds like something I'd literally type under a GRWM video.
- **$11 price shown in hero copy** — e.l.f. doesn't make you hunt for the price. It's right there: "NEW Power Grip Rosé Setting Spray, $11." That's confident budget-beauty energy.
- **Top banner $4 off promo** — the Halo Glow Skin Tint is a legit TikTok cult product and offering it at $14 after $4 off is smart. That's my impulse-buy range.
- **Beauty Squad free-shipping badge** — positioned right under the nav, immediately after I land. It's the first reason to create an account and it makes sense financially.
- **Model aesthetic** — the woman in the hero is dewy, glowy, realistic-looking. Not runway-glamour, not GRWM-mess. It's the exact look I'm trying to recreate.

## What's weak

- **No returning-member hook visible** — I'm already a Beauty Squad member. Where's my points balance? Where's member-exclusive pricing? This page treats me like a new visitor and that's a miss.
- **Top banner is text-heavy for mobile** — "Get $4 off! Try e.l.f.ie fave Halo Glow Skin Tint SPF 50 for only $14. Shop Now" — that's a lot to parse in a thin banner strip on a small screen.
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — I can't see any category links without tapping the menu. On mobile, that's a friction point when I want to jump straight to skincare or eye.
- **Partially visible right-panel carousel** — there's clearly a second slide barely peeking in at the right edge (blue/purple section) but there's no swipe affordance or dot indicator visible. I might not even know there's more.
- **Zero trust signals in viewport** — no review counts, no star ratings, no "4.8 stars, 50K reviews" badge anywhere above the fold. For a new product launch, that's leaving social proof off the table.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Your Squad Points: [X]" chip to the nav bar** for logged-in users — one small element that instantly signals this page knows who I am and rewards me for being loyal.
- **Surface one or two micro-review stats on the hero** — something like "⭐ 4.7 (12K reviews)" near the "SHOP NOW" button would close conversions way faster on a new product launch.
- **Add a swipe indicator (dots or arrow) to the carousel** — I can see a second card peeking from the right. Give me a visual cue that there's more to explore or I'll just tap the CTA and miss the rest of the story.
- **Swap the top banner to a shorter punch** — "Halo Glow Skin Tint SPF 50: $14 (save $4) →" is scannable in one glance on mobile; the current version requires two reads.
