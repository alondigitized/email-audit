# Don’t miss a National Lipstick Day gift on us!  💄
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s National Lipstick Day send, and the structure is a gift-with-purchase play: a free Cream Glide Lip Liner with any lip product purchase, unlocked with code LIPPIE. It's the same GWP mechanic that powered the July 23 "Glitz with benefits" send (10/10) — free item + purchase requirement + a code — but here the payoff sits directly under the hero instead of buried, so the execution is tighter than the July 22 free-shipping flash sale (6/10) that hid its hook.
- Where it's weaker is urgency and differentiation. "National Lipstick Day" implies a one-day event but nothing in the render actually says "today only" or gives a cutoff time, so the holiday tie-in reads more like flavor text than a deadline. The three "lip combo" modules (glossy color, buildable tint, sheer stain) are a nice bit of use-case education, but every section ends in the same unstyled "SHOP NOW" button, so nothing in the CTA layer differentiates one offer from the next.
- Judgment: solid, on-brand, bug-free execution of a proven mechanic, dragged down by unclaimed urgency and generic CTA copy — a good-not-great send from a brand that's shown it can do gift-with-purchase better.

## What's working

- The gift offer (free liner + code LIPPIE) is stated in plain language directly beneath the hero image — no digging required.
- Three color-coded "combo" modules (pink/purple/red) visually teach specific product pairings rather than just listing SKUs.
- Render is clean throughout — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum across a fairly long email.
- Footer reinforces free shipping, the Beauty Squad receipt-scanning rewards program, and app download — familiar trust signals for repeat subscribers.

## What's weak

- No explicit deadline or "today only" language despite the subject's "don't miss" framing — the urgency is implied, not delivered.
- "SHOP NOW" is reused as the CTA on every single module (4+ times) with zero copy variation.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the email, unlike some of this brand's stronger recent sends.
- This is roughly the sixth e.l.f. email in about a week per the brand history (July 21–26) — cadence risk is real even though this one is well-made.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hard deadline to the hero copy ("today only — ends 11:59pm") — the biggest lever, since the subject promises urgency the body never pays off.
- 2. Vary the CTA copy per module ("Grab My Free Liner," "Shop the Glossy Duo," "Shop the Stain Combo") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW."
- 3. Add a one-line proof point near the hero (star rating or UGC callout) — this brand has leaned on testimonials effectively before (e.g. the July 25 haircare launch).
- 4. Write real preheader copy — none was detected, which wastes prime inbox real estate right next to the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free lip liner is waiting 💄 (today only)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `National Lipstick Day: pick your free liner shade`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add any lippie + code LIPPIE = free Cream Glide Liner`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3 lip combos, 1 free gift — ends tonight`
