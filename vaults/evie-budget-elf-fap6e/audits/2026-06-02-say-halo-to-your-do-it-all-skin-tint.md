# Say *halo* to your do-it-all skin tint 🙋‍♀️ 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- e.l.f. is running back the Halo Glow Skin Tint three days after the May 30 Allure send scored 8/10 on this same SKU. The email itself is competent — clean hero, ingredient-level benefit copy, a well-executed 18-shade grid — but there's nothing here a subscriber who opened May 30 hasn't already seen. The pun-forward subject recycles the "halo" wordplay without attaching any new offer, urgency, or product angle.
- The harder problem is a visible SPF number conflict: the product name in the hero reads "SPF 50," the benefit grid reads "mineral SPF 30 sunscreen." That's not a nitpick — a shopper paying attention to formulation claims will notice, and it undermines the ingredient credibility the email spends three bullet points building.
- Net verdict: solid creative execution on a story already told. Without a new hook, this send is paying full creative cost for a fraction of the May 30 lift.

## What's working

- Hero layout lands cleanly above the fold on mobile — product, name, and "do-it-all" promise are all visible at a glance
- Benefit ladder (buildable coverage → SPF → niacinamide/hyaluronic acid → reviews) is specific and logically sequenced
- 18-shade grid with diverse model thumbnails is the strongest inclusivity signal e.l.f. has deployed this week
- "Rated 500 5-star reviews" social proof is positioned directly above the SHOP NOW CTA, where it earns its keep

## What's weak

- SPF number conflict is visible in the render: hero says "SPF 50," benefit grid says "mineral SPF 30 sunscreen" — a careful reader will notice
- No promotional hook to differentiate from May 30: no discount, no new shade launch, no limited window
- "TRY THIS" CTA is the weakest phrasing in the email — sounds hesitant when the product story surrounding it is confident
- Subject pun ("say *halo*") echoes the brand name without adding information, especially hollow for the subscriber who already opened the Allure send three days ago
- Beauty Squad loyalty callout is buried in footer micro-copy; it adds zero weight to the offer

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the SPF conflict first.** One number everywhere. If the formula is SPF 30, the hero image must say SPF 30. This is the single change that restores purchase confidence.
- 2. **Give this send a new hook.** A member price ("Beauty Squad: $12"), a free-shipping window, or even a shade count spotlight ("find yours in 18 shades") gives subscribers a concrete reason to engage rather than mentally filing this as "that email again."
- 3. **Rewrite "TRY THIS" to carry the price.** "Shop $14 Skin Tint" or "Get Yours — $14" puts the offer into the button, where CTR is highest.
- 4. **Hardcode the review count into the subject.** "500 five-star reviews" is a more compelling inbox signal than a wordplay pun — and it's already in the email body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `500 five-star reviews. $14 skin tint. Yours.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `18 shades, SPF 30, niacinamide — $14`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buildable coverage + halo finish. See why 500 shoppers gave it five stars.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your shade is in here — 18 options, one formula, $14.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` is empty**
```html
<title></title>
```
When the email is opened in a browser (View in Browser link) or previewed in certain clients, the tab/window title is blank. Should be set to the subject line or a descriptive string.

**Focus indicator suppressed on the only visible link**
```html
<a ... style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">
```
`outline:none` removes the visible focus ring. `tabindex="-1"` removes the element from keyboard tab order entirely. For a logo link that also serves as a navigation anchor, this fails WCAG 2.1 SC 2.4.7 (Focus Visible). Acceptable only if the link is purely decorative and the same destination is reachable elsewhere in the email.

**Three empty HTML blocks add dead markup**
```html
<table class="html_block block-2" ...><div>...</div>  <!-- empty -->
<table class="html_block block-3" ...>                 <!-- empty -->
<table class="html_block block-4" ...>                 <!-- empty -->
```
These generate real bytes and HTTP overhead with no content. Strip or consolidate.

**No `srcset` or retina image variant**
The hero image is a single raster at `width="640"`. On 2× displays it will render at half the native pixel density. Braze supports `<picture>` or double-density variants via template blocks.

**Preheader correctly implemented** — hidden span with `display:none !important` and MSO `mso-hide:all` is present and populated: *"Help protect, hydrate & brighten skin."*

### 7. Recommendations
