# Ghoulishly good looks for the weekend 🕯️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is another "The e.l.f. Edit" catch-all roundup. It wears a Halloween costume: a dark, witchy palette and a "which e.l.f.ing magical sister are you?" quiz concept. The three character panels (Riley, Jade, Maya) have strong, distinctive styling and are the best creative in the email. The hook is fun, but the email never pays it off. Each panel is a pretty portrait with no product name, price or link I can see.
- The commercial message is weak. The hero offers two generic buttons, "Shop Best-Sellers" and "Shop New Arrivals", with no price or deadline. The only concrete offer is the full-size gift with a $50+ spend. It sits near the bottom, below the product grid, even though it was the lead on 10/21 ("Choose a full-size gift on us!"). Four days after the 10/24 gift guide, the cadence feels repetitive.
- Verdict: strong mood and character, weak conversion. It reads as a brand beat, not a sales driver. It's about the same tier as the 10/19 "Bestie, your fall lineup" roundup.

## What's working

- The Riley, Jade and Maya panels are distinctive. They use bold typography and styled props, and they carry the Halloween theme without a cliché ghost graphic.
- The dark, moody palette is cohesive and seasonal, with candle-lit product shots.
- "It's not witchful thinking. It's soft glam." is a clever line, and the single "Shop Now" button under it is clear.
- The "Too good to ghost" 2x2 product grid is clean, with a "Shop Now" link under each item.
- The category nav (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) is easy to scan at the bottom.

## What's weak

- The quiz concept has no payoff. The three personas have no product names, prices or visible CTAs, so there is no reason to choose one.
- The hero has no offer. "Shop Best-Sellers" and "Shop New Arrivals" are two competing, generic buttons.
- The full-size gift offer ($50+ spend, no code) is buried below the product grid and sits in a small block.
- The product grid shows no prices or names, so there's no reason to click one item over another.
- The email is long and has no clear priority. The nav list is a stack of large grey bars that adds scroll without value.
- The subject says "for the weekend", but it's Monday 10/26. The "late-night plan" copy in the hero doesn't tie to that either.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the one concrete offer. Put the "full-size gift on us at $50+" at the top of the hero, and make it the single CTA.
- 2. Make each persona panel shoppable. Add a product name, a price and a "Shop Riley's look" button, so the quiz drives clicks.
- 3. Add prices and product names to the 2x2 grid, with one star item.
- 4. Collapse the category nav into a compact row of links.
- 5. Fix the timing language. Use a real deadline instead of "weekend", or send it on Thursday or Friday.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's structure is mostly sound: table layout with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, and a hidden preheader. But 3 of the 12 probed links failed in the QA crawl, and the compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The probe errors may be a crawler artifact rather than a broken link (see section 2).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 probe failures (30% pass rate).** All three go through the Braze click-tracker at `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` and ended with `unknown url type` on a *relative* path:
  - `/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm?Color=Birthday+Cake` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_medium=email&lid=np8l1vfno9yj&...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause:** the tracker's redirect returned a relative `Location`, or the prober didn't resolve it against the host. That means the failures are **not confirmed as end-user breakage**. Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so these links probably work. Verify by hand with `curl -sI <tracker URL>` and check the `Location` header. If it really is relative, it's non-standard for a click-tracker and some clients may mishandle it.
- **Long tracking URLs:** each tracked URL is about 600+ characters. This causes the QA warning that 13,051 of 15,038 plain-text characters (87%) are URLs, which hurts the text/HTML ratio.
- **Coverage:** only 12 links were probed and 30 were skipped. The unprobed footer, social and utility links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Header banner link has `tabindex="-1"`** (`<a ... style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">`). This removes the link from keyboard focus order, and `outline:none` removes the focus indicator.
- **One image has no alt text:** `src=https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/Xh17q_UDEF0d_F1LXXQR0A~~/...`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking or open pixel. If so, it needs `alt=""` (decorative), not a descriptive alt.
- **Empty font stack:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the first html_block falls back to Verdana, which is probably harmless.
- **Web font loading:** the `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost...')` sits in a `<style>` inside `<body>`, which many clients ignore (Gmail strips it). The `<link>` in `<head>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Fallback to `Verdana, Sans-serif` is declared, so this only degrades typography.
- **Hidden preheader and mobile CSS:** the preheader uses `display:none; mso-hide:all`, and the `@media (max-width:660px)` stacking rules look correct. No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source was truncated, so this only covers what was shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message in a direct-delivery inbox. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` differ, so check that DKIM `d=` and the DMARC alignment are correct.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither was in the truncated source or the probed set, so they are unverified. The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none was reported failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM present on at least one link:** the app link carries `utm_medium=email`, `utm_source=broadcast` and `utm_campaign=102626_weekly_edit_0-3`. The campaign name encodes the date (10/26/26), and `source_caller=ui` and `lid=` are Braze-style parameters.
- **UTMs not visible on the other two failed links** (`/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm?Color=Birthday+Cake`, `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`). The probe output may have truncated them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are absent, product-page traffic would be attributed weakly.
- **Login link target:** the receipt-scanning link points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Logged-out users land on a login wall rather than a page explaining the feature.
- **Deep link:** `?Color=Birthday+Cake` preselects a variant. Confirm the variant exists and is in stock on the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on the three failing tracker URLs and confirm the final `Location` resolves. If the redirect is relative, fix it in Braze link templating; otherwise treat this as a QA-tool bug and make the prober resolve relative redirects against the current host.
2. Remove `tabindex="-1"` and `outline:none` from the header link, add a title (the subject line is a good choice), and set `alt=""` on the `/q/` image.
3. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct send for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. Confirm UTM parameters on all CTA links, not just the app link.
5. Move the font `@import` into `<head>` or accept the fallback stack, and fix the empty `font-family:''`.
