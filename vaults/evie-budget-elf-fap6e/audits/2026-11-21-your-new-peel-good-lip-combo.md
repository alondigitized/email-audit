# Your NEW peel-good lip combo 💖👄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-built tutorial email: three lip products in a 1-2-3 routine ("buff it," "peel it," "slick it"). The black "your new peel-good lip combo." hero works with the subject, and the stacked photo and product blocks are easy to scan. The $6, $6 and $9 prices are shown up front, which helps a buyer who's already interested.
- The email has no promotional hook. The only offers are the Beauty Squad "$5 off bundles and sets" block and the "choose a full-size gift on us" block, and both sit below the lip routine. On a Black Friday weekend (the 11/20 send was a $5-off-bundles reward drop), a send with no offer above the fold is easy to ignore. The $5 reward is also for bundles and sets, so it doesn't connect to the lip combo. The nine-product grid and category bars below dilute the message.
- Judgement: a strong creative concept that's solid but not urgent. It's a good send for lip fans and a weak one for anyone who needs a discount to act.

## What's working

- The "buff, peel & slick" three-step structure is clear, and each step has its own photo, price and button.
- The black hero banner with the pink-lip model is eye-catching, and the headline matches the subject.
- The "new!" sticker on the Peel of Approval Lip Liner Stain marks a real launch.
- Each step has usage copy, such as "let it set for 5 minutes… slowly peel it off." That makes the peel-off stain easy to understand.
- The purple Beauty Squad block ("$5 off bundles and sets," "sign in to redeem") is a clear loyalty callout, and the render looks clean.

## What's weak

- No discount is visible until you scroll about 60% of the way down, so the lip combo itself has no reason to buy today.
- All three step CTAs say "Shop now," which is generic and doesn't name the product.
- The lower half is cluttered: the full-size gift block, the "Top Picks for You" row, five category bars, then footer badges. The routine's momentum is lost.
- The "Top Picks" products (a Toasted Tomato palette, a drops duo, a skincare kit) are off-topic for a lip-focused email.
- The legal and fine-print text at the bottom is dense and tiny, and it hides the reward's conditions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the hero, such as "Get the full lip combo for $X" or a bundle price, with a single "Shop the combo" button. This is the change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Add a "Shop all 3" bundle CTA after step 3, since the routine is the product story.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Lip Exfoliator," "Shop Lip Liner Stain," "Shop Glow Reviver."
- 4. Move the Beauty Squad reward up under the routine, and tie it to the lip bundle if one exists.
- 5. Cut "Top Picks" or replace it with lip products, and drop the category bars on single-theme sends like this one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is a conventional table-based layout with `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"` and a preheader. The three automated link FAILs are most likely a QA-probe limitation, not broken links. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 link probes failed, probably a false positive.** The probe error was `unknown url type: '/account?...'`, and the same error appeared for `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` and `/pages/mobile-app?pid=Email&...`. The tracked links on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` appear to answer with a relative `Location` header, which is valid under RFC 7231. The probe client didn't resolve it against the origin, so no destination status was captured. Treat these as unverified, not broken. The three affected links are:
  - "Not a member? Join now - it's free."
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f."
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app"
- **Tracking redirector:** all CTAs go through the first-party `links.elfcosmetics.com` domain, which keeps the sender and link domains aligned.
- **Plain-text bloat:** 12,438 of 15,192 plain-text characters (about 82%) are URLs, which is above the 70% threshold. The cause is the long wrapped tracking tokens. It is a minor spam-score risk.
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 44 links were probed and 32 were skipped (footer and social links were deprioritized), so those 32 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Missing alt text:** the image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/og-l2UW53SFTUa4J7xqn-w~~/...` has no alt text. The `/q/` path suggests it is an open-tracking pixel, so this is low impact and a non-issue for screen readers if it is `display:none` or 1×1. Adding `alt=""` would satisfy the checker.
- **Logo link out of tab order:** the logo link has `tabindex="-1"` (`<a ... style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">`). It also sets `outline:none`, so keyboard users can neither reach the link nor see focus on it.
- **Tiny mobile heading:** the CSS rule `.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}` shrinks an `<h1>` to 9px on mobile. That is below legible size, and if the heading carries real content it is an accessibility problem. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm what that block contains.
- **Empty font name:** one inline style uses `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty first family is harmless but sloppy.
- **Nested wrapper tables:** the `html_block` wrappers contain nested `nl-container` tables. This is verbose, and the redundant nesting adds weight and some Outlook fragility.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible portion of the source (truncated) shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{...}}` or `%%...%%`. The Braze-style `bxid=` and `lid=` parameters appear populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are flagged as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay.
- **Sender alignment:** the From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain is `links.elfcosmetics.com`. Both are subdomains of the same organizational domain.
- **Footer content:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source, and no unsubscribe-link failure was reported by QA. I can't verify them from the data provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs on the join link:** the link carries `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=112126_peelofapproval_3_step_lip_routine`. The parameters are well-formed, and the campaign name encodes the send date (11/21/26).
- **App link:** `/pages/mobile-app` uses AppsFlyer-style parameters (`pid=Email`, `onelink_id`, `is_retargeting=true`) and no `utm_*`. That is probably intentional.
- **Receipt-scanning link:** it goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, with no UTMs and no receipt-scanning page. The link text promises one feature but the destination is a generic login page. Attribution is lost and the landing page doesn't match the promise.
- **Truncated redirect values:** the probe output shows the `bxid` value cut off, so I can't check it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the three FAIL links with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the redirect origin. Re-test before treating them as broken.
2. Capture raw headers from the original message, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
3. Remove `tabindex="-1"` and `outline:none` from the logo link, or at least restore a visible focus style.
4. Fill in `<title>`, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Check what the mobile `h1` at 9px contains. If it holds real content, raise it to 14px or more.
6. Point the receipt-scanning CTA at a page for that feature, and add `utm_*` parameters to it.
7. Probe the 32 skipped links, or at least the unsubscribe and preference links, in a later pass.
