# Pick your full-size gift on us 💝
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a "free full-size gift with $35+" send, and the offer is clear: a black block under the hero reads "choose a full-size gift on us" with "up to a $9 value" and "no promo code needed." The creative is strong, with big product close-ups and a consistent black and white frame. That block works better as the hero than the glossy bottle shot above it.
- The email then loses focus. Three near-identical product blocks (Glow Reviver Lip Oil Glimmer, Pout Clout Lip Plumping Pen, Glow Reviver Plumping Lip Oil) each get a "Shop Now" button, so it reads as a lip catalog and not a gift pick. The TikTok Super Brand Day banner, the Top Picks row and the category bars add a second and third message. The gift is "up to a $9 value" behind a $35 spend, and the "pick your gift" promise never shows a choice of gifts. The three lip products are the gifts, but nothing says so.
- Verdict: a solid, on-brand promo that would convert better if it were tighter. It sits in the middle of the pack against the recent run, which includes the Peel of Approval Lip Liner Stain launch on 11/01 (8/10) and the Halloween lip-price send on 10/28 (8/10).

## What's working

- The offer is concrete and unambiguous: free full-size gift, $35+ spend, no promo code.
- The "ONLINE EXCLUSIVE" and "FREE gift!" badges on the hero grab the eye.
- Each product block has a large image, a shade callout ("Crystal Baller or Chocoloev," "Wicked Cherry or Just Peachy") and one short benefit line, so the gift choices are tangible.
- The black and white typography with big white CTA buttons is clean and consistent with e.l.f.'s brand voice.
- I see no render bugs: no broken images, overlaps or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- Three identical "Shop Now" buttons give no clear primary action, and none says which gift you're choosing.
- The email never shows the full set of gifts side by side, so "pick your gift" has no real picking moment.
- The "up to a $9 value" wording and the spend requirement are small. The dates and exclusions are only in the fine print, which weakens the "limited time" urgency.
- The TikTok Shop Super Brand Day banner, Top Picks and category bars after the gifts compete with the main offer and make the email very long.
- The email is almost entirely lip-focused, so it will feel narrow to anyone who doesn't buy lip products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the gift section as one compact row of the three gifts with prices, a single "Choose your gift" CTA, and the spend threshold in the headline ("Free full-size gift with $35+").
- 2. Put the real end date in the offer block ("Ends 11/15") instead of only "limited time only."
- 3. Cut the TikTok banner or move it to a separate send, and trim the category bars.
- 4. Add a quick qualifying-spend cue, such as a progress line or "add $X more," near the top.
- 5. Use specific CTA copy like "Claim my free gift" in place of repeated "Shop Now."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free full-size lip gift with $35+ 💋`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free gift ends 11/15 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Choose a lip oil, plumping pen or gloss. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add to cart, spend $35, and your pick ships free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
5 of 12 probed links failed with "unknown url type", and every failure is a relative path (`/products/...`, `/account/login...`, `/pages/mobile-app...`). The cause is probably a redirect-resolution gap in the QA prober, not a broken email. The auth and unsubscribe-header warnings come from the relay and are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect targets (5 FAIL).** All five tracked links on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` ended with `unknown url type` on a relative path:
  - `/products/glow-reviver-lip-oil?Color=Jam+Session+-+Black+Cherry`
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Baddest+Beige+-+Light+Neutral+Beige`
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `/pages/mobile-app?lid=0wesrzk74tei&onelink_id=wTtZ&shortlink=email&is_retargeting=true&pid=Email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=1104…`
- **Likely cause.** The tracker's 302 `Location` header returned a host-less path, or the prober failed to resolve it against the redirect host. This pattern points to the probe, since a relative `Location` is valid per RFC 7231 and browsers resolve it. It is unconfirmed, and the final status of these links is unknown. Re-test with a client that resolves relative redirects.
- **Truncated link.** The mobile-app URL is cut off at `utm_campaign=1104`, and the "Receipt scanning" link is cut off mid-token. This is probably display truncation, not an email defect.
- **Skipped links.** 29 of 41 links were not probed. Footer, social and most secondary links are unverified.
- **Plain-text part.** 11,794 of 13,769 characters (about 86%) are URLs, because of the long tracking wrappers. This is a minor spam-filter heuristic risk. The tracking tokens are around 300 characters each.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty, which hurts accessibility and the web-view tab label.
- **Invalid font stack.** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in `html_block block-2` has an empty family name. It falls back to Verdana, so it is harmless but sloppy.
- **Missing alt text.** One image has no `alt`. Its `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/R0ne3G4Zxnl8…`. The `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, which makes this low severity. If it is a content image, it needs `alt`.
- **Nested layouts.** The source nests full `nl-container`/`row-content` table layouts inside `html_block` cells. That is more fragile in Outlook and adds weight, but I found no confirmed rendering break.
- **What is correct.** `lang="en"` is set, tables use `role="presentation"`, MSO conditionals are present, a viewport meta is present, and a hidden preheader is present. The header image has `alt="e.l.f."`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible (truncated) source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** The QA tool did not find either header. It notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected via the relay. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` are aligned subdomains of the same organizational domain.
- **Body checks.** The QA output lists no result for the in-body unsubscribe link. The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Mismatched destination.** The "Receipt scanning – Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." link resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. That is a generic login page, not a receipt-scanning feature page. The `return_to=%2Faccount` also drops the user on the account page after login, not on the promised feature.
- **UTMs on the app link.** The mobile-app link carries `utm_medium=email` and `utm_campaign=1104` (truncated) plus `pid=Email` and `shortlink=email`. It does not show `utm_source` before truncation.
- **UTMs on the product links.** The three product links show only variant params (`Color=…`, `Size=…`) and no visible `utm_*`. UTMs may be appended by the tracker or sit in the truncated portion, so this is unverified.
- **Landing-page alignment.** The product deep links carry variant selections (Color, Size), which is good. The final landing status is unknown given the probe failures.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the probe with relative-`Location` resolution. If the five links still fail in a real client, report them to the sender.
2. Pull the original headers to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, rather than relying on the relay copy.
3. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, or a descriptive `alt` if it is a content image.
4. Fill in `<title>`, and replace the empty font family in the second `html_block`.
5. Point the receipt-scanning link at the feature page, not `/account/login`.
6. Confirm that every product link carries `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`, and that UTM naming is consistent with the app link.
7. Re-run with the full HTML, since the source was truncated, to check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
