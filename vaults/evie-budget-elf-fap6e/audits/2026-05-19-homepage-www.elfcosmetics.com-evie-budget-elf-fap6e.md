# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. immediately feels like home — the "rosé all day" hero is SO on my FYP energy, and the $11 setting spray price tag makes me want to tap before I even finish reading. This is very much a brand that knows it's talking to me, a broke girl who still wants to look glazed and dewy. Right now they're pushing newness in the setting spray category and dangling that Beauty Squad loyalty perk front and center.

## What's working

- **"rosé all day." headline** — playful, Gen Z, rhymey, product-relevant. I would genuinely screenshot this to send to my group chat.
- **$11 price point called out in body copy** — I don't have to dig for the price. It's right there. That's the whole selling point for e.l.f. and they lead with it.
- **Top banner with specific $4 off + $14 price** — two numbers, one tap. I know exactly what I'm getting before I even commit.
- **Beauty Squad strip** — free shipping on first order is a real incentive. I'd join right now if I haven't already.
- **Pink spray-mist hero image** — the aesthetic is very "cloud skin" TikTok. It reads as aspirational but still attainable.

## What's weak

- **No category navigation visible** — the hamburger is there but I can't see what categories exist without tapping through. If I want to browse lip or blush I have no shortcut.
- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — no star ratings, no "10K+ reviews," nothing. e.l.f. has massive fan reviews; hiding them is leaving credibility on the table.
- **Beauty Squad strip is acquisition-only** — if I'm already a member, where's my rewards balance or member pricing badge? It ignores the loyal shopper.
- **No urgency or scarcity signal** — the $4 off feels like it could last forever. No "this week only" or "limited launch" creates no reason to act now vs. later.
- **The partially-visible carousel panel on the right** — the "N" peeking through suggests more content but there's no visual affordance to swipe. Easy to miss on a quick scroll.

## Recommendations

- **Add a star rating count to the hero** — e.g. "★★★★★ 47,000 reviews" under the product name. One line. Zero redesign needed. Massive credibility boost for a $11 purchase.
- **Surface a "Members Only" price or early-access label** for Beauty Squad members on the hero product — reward the already-loyal shopper instead of treating everyone like a stranger.
- **Add a swipe indicator or dot pagination** to the hero carousel — the partially visible right panel is wasted signal on mobile. A dot nav makes it obvious there's more to see.
- **Drop a deadline on the $4 off banner** — "through Sunday" or "this week" gives me a real reason to shop now instead of adding to cart and forgetting it.
