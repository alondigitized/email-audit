## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Braze/Bazaarvoice-style template with tracked redirect links, but two CTA links resolve to relative app-internal paths instead of absolute URLs, plain-text fallback is missing, and unsubscribe/authentication headers could not be confirmed.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Two probed links fail to resolve to valid absolute URLs:
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." → redirect target resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, a relative path with no scheme/host. This will break in most email clients, which require absolute URLs.
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → redirect target resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&shortlink=email&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F...`, also a relative path (likely intended as an AppsFlyer deep link, but missing the absolute domain).
- All sampled links route through the `links.elfcosmetics.com` Braze click-tracking redirector (`/f/a/{hash}/AAQRxRA~/{payload}`), consistent with standard redirect-and-track wrapping.
- 26 of 38 material links were not probed (deprioritized footer/utility/social links per QA policy); no claim is made about their validity.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- One tracked pixel/image (`https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...`) is missing `alt` text — affects screen readers and clients with images blocked.
- Preheader text is duplicated: a visible preheader ("Plus, new arrivals you can't miss") is followed by a separate hidden block of zero-width joiner/space characters used as preheader padding — standard technique, not a defect.
- MSO conditional comments and `@media (max-width:660px)` responsive rules are present and correctly structured for Outlook/mobile fallback.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%...%%`) found in the truncated HTML source.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance from available data; QA notes this may be a relay capture gap rather than a sender-side omission.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from this data.
- An in-body unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA (always included per probing policy), satisfying the CAN-SPAM visible-unsubscribe requirement at the content level; header-level one-click compliance remains unverified.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The app-download CTA target string includes `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email`, consistent UTM tagging for channel attribution — but the underlying URL is broken (relative path, see §2), so the tagging never reaches a live landing page.
- The receipt-scanning CTA has no visible UTM parameters and is also broken (relative path), so email-to-site continuity cannot be assessed for that link.
- Main hero image links to a fully-qualified tracked redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), which appears structurally sound but wasn't included in the QA pass/fail probe results provided.

7. Recommendations
1. Fix the two broken CTA targets by prefixing the destination with the correct absolute domain (e.g., `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/account/login?...` and the intended app deep-link domain/scheme, likely an AppsFlyer OneLink host) before the Braze redirect wraps them.
2. Add a plain-text MIME alternative — currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and breaks plain-text clients.
3. Add `alt` text to the tracking/content image currently missing it.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the ESP level (may just be lost in the AgentMail relay capture) and verify one-click unsubscribe support.
5. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against send logs, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
