# Thankful for carbs and a solid beauty lineup 🥧
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the "e.l.f. Edit" catch-all again, wrapped in a Thanksgiving-week theme. The subject promises "carbs and a solid beauty lineup." The body delivers a travel-kit hero, a November Fall Favorites grid, a six-card "What's Your Long Weekend Personality?" quiz, a gift-with-purchase banner, a "consider your next beauty obsession" product row, a gift finder quiz and a category nav. It is charming and on-voice, but it is far too long and spreads attention across a dozen CTAs.
- The one real offer is a full-size gift on us. It's a black band in the lower third: "up to a $9 value! just spend $35+ and add to cart. no promo code needed!" It sits well below the fold, and the hero's only message is a small pink bar about "Long days, full tables." The hero shows a passport and travel kit, with no price or reward. - Judgement: a solid brand-voice send that wastes its best asset. Compared with the 11/20 "$5 off Bundles & Sets" send (the strongest in this run), the offer here is buried and the layout is a scroll marathon. Expect middling engagement.

## What's working

- The subject and body copy share a playful Thanksgiving voice ("Bestie, if you're anything like us, you're extra thankful for two things this week").
- The quiz-style "Long Weekend Personality" cards (The 5-Step Houseguest, The Dessert Table MVP) are on-brand and give each product set a reason to exist.
- The travel hero with the pink pouch and passport fits the holiday-travel moment.
- The full-size gift banner has a clear threshold ($35+) and no promo code.
- The sender and brand identity are instantly recognizable.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. The gift-with-purchase sits roughly 60% down the email, and the hero has no discount or reward.
- There are too many CTAs (SHOP NEW ARRIVALS, SHOP BEST SELLERS, many SHOP THE SET and SHOP NOW buttons, TAKE THE QUIZ), so no single path stands out.
- The "consider your next beauty obsession" row shows tiny, hard-to-read product images with no visible names or prices.
- The category nav (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) is a large empty-looking grey block that adds scroll with little value.
- The offer text and the "Hey friend" hero strip are small and low-contrast. The top banner copy is hard to read.

- 2. Cut the email by about a third. Keep the hero, one product grid and the gift band, and drop the category nav and one of the two quizzes.
- 3. Reduce to one primary CTA style and one primary action, such as "Claim my free gift."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a Braze/SendGrid-style click-tracking wrapper (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) with table-based, `role="presentation"` markup. The three QA failures all come from relative redirect targets (`/products/...`, `/account/login...`), which are probably checker artifacts. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay not capturing headers, so they can't be confirmed. The HTML was truncated, so footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 [FAIL] link probes, all `unknown url type: '/...'`:**
  - `/products/hydrating-camo-cc-cream?Color=Fair+100+W+%E2%80%93+fair+with+warm+undertones` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/bright-appeel-daily-peel-pads?Size=30+Day+Pack` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
- **Likely cause:** each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect returned a relative `Location` header, and the probe failed to resolve it against the redirect host. Browsers and mail clients resolve relative `Location` values per RFC 7231, so these are probably not broken for recipients. The check is unconfirmed, though, because the final destinations were never fetched. Fix the probe by resolving with `urljoin(response_url, location)`. Separately, emit absolute `Location` URLs from the redirector.
- **Login link:** the receipt-scanning CTA lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. That is a login wall for any recipient who isn't signed in. It is a functional landing, not a break.
- **Color parameter:** the CC-cream URL contains an encoded en dash (`%E2%80%93`) and spaces (`+`) in the `Color` value. It resolves, but it is fragile if the storefront matches the variant string exactly.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 51 links were probed and 39 were skipped. Footer, social and utility links are unverified.
- **Tracked image URL:** `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/ZVOX-iIQJTAm_SU1AA0t2w~~/AAQRxRA~/cqZ5IMLg...` is routed through the tracking domain. It is likely an open-tracking pixel or a proxied image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** one image is missing `alt`. It is the tracked `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...cqZ5IMLg...` image. If it is a tracking pixel, add `alt=""` explicitly. If it is content, add descriptive alt text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this is minor, but some clients and accessibility tools use it.
- **Empty font-family:** the first `html_block` has `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. It falls back to Verdana, so it is harmless but sloppy.
- **Unsupported `@import`:** `<style>@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost...')</style>` sits in the `<body>` inside an `html_block`, with a separate `<link>` to Google Fonts in the head. Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore both. Sans-serif fallbacks cover this, but the web font will not render in those clients.
- **Redundant nesting:** the `html_block` contains a second `nl-container` / `row-1` structure nested inside the outer `row-1`. Duplicate class names can cause CSS collisions, but there is no confirmed rendering failure.
- **Good practices confirmed:** `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, an MSO conditional block, and a viewport meta tag. The hidden preheader uses `display:none; mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%` were visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against raw headers from the origin or a direct-to-mailbox test. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown through the relay. This cannot be assessed here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not in the truncated source, so they were not verified. QA states an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it was reported.
- **Sending domain alignment:** the From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com` and links use `links.elfcosmetics.com`. Both are on the brand's registered domain, so this looks consistent.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** all hrefs are opaque tracked URLs. UTM parameters can't be confirmed unless the final redirect targets are resolved. The visible destination parameters (`Color=`, `Size=`) show product variant deep-linking.
- **Plain-text bloat:** [WARN] 16,755 of 19,673 characters (about 85%) of the plain-text part are URLs. The long tracking tokens inflate the text part. Shorten the tokens, or reduce the number of links in the text version.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the link-probe resolver to handle relative `Location` headers, then re-run the 3 failed links. Don't treat them as broken until then.
2. Resolve the final URLs for all 51 links, including the 39 skipped, to confirm UTMs and status codes.
3. Fetch raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` at the origin.
4. Add `alt` to the `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...cqZ5IMLg...` image. Use `alt=""` if it is a pixel.
5. Fill in `<title>`, correct the empty `font-family:''`, and move font loading out of `<body>`, relying on web-safe fallbacks.
6. Check that the receipt-scanning CTA works for non-logged-in users, for example by using a public explainer page.
7. Reduce plain-text URL density (see section 6).
