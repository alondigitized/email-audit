- The hero price burst ("$153 → $95") puts a concrete value claim in the first screen.
- "just dropped!" ties back to the Oct 6 waitlist teaser and signals that the wait is over.
- A black SHOP NOW button appears twice, with high contrast on both the purple and white backgrounds.
- The calendar photo is distinctive and on-brand, and the open doors show how it is meant to be used.
- The "sleigh the holidays" copy keeps the e.l.f. voice and gives a quick list of what you get: 24 items, full and mini sizes, exclusive faves.

## What's weak

- The email never shows what is inside the calendar. There are no product shots or door reveals, so you can't judge the value.
- The "Top Picks for You" row (primer, liner, setting spray) is off-topic and competes with the calendar CTA.
- The grey category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) look like placeholders and take up a lot of scroll for little.
- The emoji-and-line stack is long and repeats the hero. The $95 / $153 claim appears twice.

- 2. Add credible scarcity: "limited quantities, last year's sold out." Put it in the hero.
- 3. Cut the middle copy stack to two lines and fold the price into one callout.
- 4. Collapse the grey nav bars into a compact text or pill nav, or remove them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well-formed table-based markup with a preheader, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` tables. Four tracked links returned redirect targets the QA probe couldn't follow, and the message has no plain-text part. The QA pass rate is 27%, but most of that comes from probe limits and relay-stripped headers, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The click-tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…~~`) answered with a relative `Location` value, so the probe had no host to request:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=…&lid=…&source_caller=ui&pid=Email&onelink_id=wTtZ&deep_link_value=com.elf…` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
- A relative `Location` is permitted by RFC 7231, and browsers resolve it against the tracker host. That would send users to `links.elfcosmetics.com/products/…`, which is probably wrong. If the tracker really emits host-less paths, these four links 404. If it only looked that way in the probe, they work. I can't tell which from the data, so treat them as **unverified, not confirmed broken**.
- The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` link is a login wall, not a promotional landing page.
- 27 of 39 links were skipped by the prober, so the remaining links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- One image has no `alt`. Its `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/MHITwaJpGpVcj8R2z5UM1A~~/…`, and the `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel. If so, add `alt=""` explicitly.
- A `<div style="font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif">` carries an empty font name. It's harmless but sloppy.
- The hero image has `alt="e.l.f."`, which is a brand name and not descriptive.
- The Open Sans `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct.
- No other issues found in the visible source. It was truncated, so the full body wasn't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars [FAIL].** There is no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify it on the raw original.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay artifact and says nothing about the sender.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated portion. I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters can't be checked because every probed link ends in a tracking redirect and the final URLs weren't resolved. Only the mobile-app link shows params (`pid=Email`, `source_caller=ui`, `onelink_id`), and those are app-attribution params, not UTMs.
- Product links carry variant selectors (`Size=…`, `Color=…`), which is good for landing alignment if they resolve.
- The login-wall destination is covered in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve the four failing links with a probe that handles relative `Location` headers, using the tracker host as base. Then confirm the real destinations and that the tracker emits absolute URLs.
2. Add a `text/plain` alternative.
3. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't rely on the relay copy.
4. Fill in `<title>`, add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and remove the empty `font-family:''`.
5. Send the login-wall link to a public rewards or receipt-scanning page instead, or at least a page that doesn't need sign-in first.
6. Confirm UTM params on the final landing URLs once the redirects resolve.
