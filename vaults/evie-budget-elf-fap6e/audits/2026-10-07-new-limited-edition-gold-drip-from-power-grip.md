# NEW & LIMITED EDITION! Gold Drip from Power Grip ⭐️🏆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a tight, single-theme launch for the Power Grip Gold Drip limited edition. The gold-on-gold palette carries from the hero through every block. The "new!" badge, the "limited edition!" label and the "Only $11 each!" line all appear in the first scroll. The headline is bold, and the black "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS" button is easy to find.
- The email then repeats itself. The primer and the setting spray each get a full-bleed image block with its own "SHOP NOW" button. There is also a shimmer-texture block and a model photo, so the real content is two products at $11 each stretched over about six screens. The "Top Picks for You" row shows non-gold items (the Power Grip Primer, a $2.00 lip liner and the Dewy Setting Spray). That works against the limited-edition story, and nothing else in the email puts a deadline on the offer.
- Overall it's a solid launch email, on par with e.l.f.'s recent 7/10 sends. The visuals are strong, but the copy is thin on proof and urgency. The footer category buttons (NEW, EYES, LIPS and so on) are bulky and add nothing.

## What's working

- The gold theme is consistent and eye-catching, with the hero product shot landing the "limited edition" idea immediately.
- The offer is concrete and repeated: "Only $11 each!" and "$11" in both product headlines.
- Each product gets its own headline, one-line benefit and black "SHOP NOW" button.
- The model close-up shows the primer in use, which adds a human, aspirational touch.
- The footer carries the usual e.l.f. trust signals: cruelty-free and vegan, free shipping, receipt scanning and the app.

## What's weak

- The "infused with micro-shimmer flecks" block is a full screen of texture with one line of copy, which adds scroll without adding value.
- "Top Picks for You" shows non-gold products, including the $2.00 lip liner. It dilutes the limited-edition focus and reads as generic rather than personal.
- The hero button says "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS", which is cute but unclear about where it goes and doesn't say "shop".
- No reviews, ratings or social proof appear for either product.
- "Limited edition" has no end date or stock cue, so the urgency isn't credible.
- The grey category buttons (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) take up a lot of space and compete with the gold story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tighten the body to the hero, one combined "shop the duo" block and a bundle or pairing hook. Cut the shimmer texture block and merge the primer and spray into one side-by-side module.
- 2. Add real urgency, such as "while supplies last" or a specific stock or date cue.
- 3. Replace "UNLOCK GOLD STATUS" with a verb-plus-noun button like "Shop Gold Drip".
- 4. Add a star rating or a short review quote under each product.
- 5. Replace "Top Picks for You" with Gold Drip items or a related Power Grip lineup, and shrink the category nav into a compact text row.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics "Gold Drip from Power Grip"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is mostly sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a preheader, and a responsive media query. However, 4 of the 12 probed links failed in QA, and the plain-text part is empty. The header-based compliance checks could not be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures share one signature.** Each tracked link on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` ends in a redirect whose target is a relative path, which the probe's HTTP client could not follow (`unknown url type`):
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_xp=email&shortlink=email&source_caller=ui&lid=xykro1hblt7x&onelink_id=wTtZ&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2F` ("Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Probable cause is the probe, not a dead link.** A relative `Location` header is valid under RFC 7231, and browsers resolve it against the tracker host. Still, the redirect resolves to a relative path on the tracking domain rather than an absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URL, so final landing destinations are unverified. A real browser or mail-client click may work, or it may land on `links.elfcosmetics.com/products/...`, which would be a 404. Manual verification is needed.
- **Login redirect:** `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` sends recipients to a login wall rather than a content page.
- **Coverage gap:** 28 of 40 links were skipped, so link health is only partially confirmed.
- **Tracked image:** `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/h5VrpDaM...` is a tracked image URL, probably the open pixel, and is fine.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on an image whose `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/h5VrpDaM78SW1WxZnyHhTg~~/...`. This is likely a tracking pixel and low impact. If it is a content image, it needs alt text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Invalid font stack:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in `html_block block-1`. The empty-string family is harmless, since Verdana is the fallback, but it indicates a template defect.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`** is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, which is correct, and the web font falls back to `Arial,Sans-serif`.
- **Nested `nl-container` tables:** the same layout table is repeated inside `html_block` wrappers. This is valid but adds weight and complexity.
- **Gmail clipping risk:** the source is truncated, so total size can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback is empty** (`Text version is 0 chars`). This is a deliverability and accessibility defect and can raise spam scoring.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it should be verified in raw headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (relay limitation).
- **Footer unverified:** the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion, so they can't be assessed.
- **Sender alignment:** `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com` and the `links.elfcosmetics.com` tracking domain are both on the brand's own domain, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be verified.** Every link is wrapped in an opaque tracker, so the destination query strings are hidden in the visible source. The one exception is the app link, which carries AppsFlyer OneLink params (`af_xp=email`, `shortlink=email`, `onelink_id=wTtZ`, `af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app://`).
- **App deep link:** `af_dp` is a custom URI scheme, which is expected for OneLink. Behavior without the app installed wasn't tested.
- **Variant-specific URLs:** the product links carry variant params (`Size=...`, `Color=...`). Confirm the landing pages preselect that variant.
- **Subject vs. preheader:** the preheader ("Only $11 each.") matches the product focus, but that can't be checked against landing prices from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four failing links with relative-redirect resolution, or click-test them manually. Confirm the final URLs are absolute on `www.elfcosmetics.com`. If the redirects really resolve relatively on the tracking host, fix the ESP redirect config.
2. Add a plain-text part. A 0-character text version is the one confirmed deliverability defect.
3. Check the raw message headers at the source for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
4. Populate `<title>` and add `alt=""` (or descriptive alt) to the tracked image.
5. Remove the empty `''` font-family from the `html_block` template.
6. Replace the `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` destination with a public landing page for the receipt-scanning promo.
7. Capture the full HTML, including the footer, so the unsubscribe and postal-address checks can run.
