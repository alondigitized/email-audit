# We’re *sheer* for NEW shades❣️😚
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a shade-expansion email for the Sheer For It Blush Tints — four new shades bringing the line to nine total. The email executes e.l.f.'s standard new-shade playbook well: hero model, clean headline, a named swatch grid, and a price anchor ("Only $8!"). Nothing is broken and the visual hierarchy holds.
- The problem is context, not execution. Three days ago, e.l.f. ran a 6-hour flash sale on this exact product at 2-for-$8. Any subscriber who acted on that deal is now looking at new shades priced at $8 each with no discount. The send reads like a routine catalog drop rather than a reason to come back to a product the list just bought. The urgency and deal-orientation that made the 5/24 flash email punch above its weight are completely absent here.
- The Target rewards module mid-email is an odd structural choice — it interrupts the product story with a loyalty pitch for a different brand's loyalty program. This email does what it needs to, but it doesn't earn a revisit from the engaged subscriber who already bought on the 5/24 sale.

## What's working

- **Shade swatch grid is excellent** — all 9 shades shown with names, giving browsers a real reason to scroll.
- **Price is stated in the hero copy** — "Only $8!" does honest work contextualizing the product tier.
- **Hero model close-up is on-brand** — warm, approachable, skin-focused in a way that suits a blush/lip product.
- **Clean layout, no render issues** — no broken images, overlapping text, or missing sections.

## What's weak

- **Sheer For It flash-sale fatigue** — this is the second Sheer For It email in four days; the 5/24 "2 for $8" deal makes $8-each feel like a step backward, and no copy addresses that tension.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is a missed opportunity** — "Shop 4 New Shades" or "Find Your Shade" would be more specific and click-worthy.
- **Target rewards module breaks the product story** — a mid-email pivot to Target Circle points dilutes focus and adds visual complexity with no direct lift for e.l.f.
- **Zero urgency** — no limited-quantity language, no deadline, no "just dropped" framing that would justify opening the email over the 5/24 one.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Acknowledge the flash-sale moment** — Lead with "Four new shades just dropped — and yes, they're still $8." That one line reframes price as a feature, not a change in terms.
- 2. **Make the CTA shade-specific** — "Shop Cherry Cola & 3 More" gives browsers a reason to click over "SHOP NOW."
- 3. **Move or cut the Target rewards module** — either make it a footer note or remove it entirely; it kills the shade-discovery momentum right before the "Top Picks" section.
- 4. **Add a scarcity or recency signal** — "Just launched" with a timestamp, or "Shades this popular sell out fast" anchors why now.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cherry Cola just dropped (and 3 others)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Still $8. Still long-lasting. Brand new shades for cheeks & lips.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `9 shades now. Find the one you've been missing.`

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Cosmetics "Sheer For It" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on the BEE/Beefree editor framework, delivered via Braze (image CDN) and tracked through Sailthru (`links.elfcosmetics.com`). The truncated source reveals several structural and accessibility defects; footer/compliance section is not visible in the provided excerpt.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Sailthru redirect chain — UTM visibility unknown**
The only confirmed link in the excerpt is the logo:
```
https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/7B8f1vRRmyoA-W2L7ehRvg~~/...
```
This is a Sailthru encoded redirect. UTM parameters (if any) are encoded inside the payload and cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect. If UTMs are absent or mis-keyed, all clicks will land in GA4 as `(direct)`.

**`tabindex="-1"` on logo anchor**
```html
<a href="..." target="_blank" style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">
```
The logo CTA is explicitly excluded from keyboard tab order. If the same pattern is applied to promotional CTAs lower in the email (not visible in this excerpt), those buttons will also be unreachable by keyboard.

**`outline:none` on all links**
Same selector above removes the focus ring. Paired with `tabindex="-1"` this is a double accessibility suppression.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers and some email clients (iOS Mail, Gmail accessibility mode) announce the title. Empty title degrades the experience for AT users.

**`<style>` block placed inside a `<div>` (invalid)**
Block-3 injects a `<style type="text/css">` element directly inside a `<td><div>` wrapper — not in `<head>`. Many email clients strip or ignore `<style>` outside `<head>`, meaning the `.responsive-text` responsive font-size rules will silently fail on those clients.

**Nested `nl-container` — template copy error**
The outer `nl-container` table contains an inner `nl-container` with its own full `row > row-content > column` stack for the logo block. This is a Beefree copy-paste artifact. It adds ~400 bytes of redundant markup and can confuse MSO rendering.

**Empty `html_block block-2`**
```html
<div style="font-family:Open Sans,Arial,Sans-serif;text-align:center" align="center"> </div>
```
A completely empty block between the logo and the offer bar. Harmless but indicative of a template cleanup gap.

**Google Fonts via `<link>` tag**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Open+Sans" rel="stylesheet">
```
Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>` so it is intentionally skipped in Outlook. However, it will also fail in privacy-hardened clients (Apple Mail with "Block Remote Content" enabled) and some Android clients. The inline font-stack fallbacks (`Arial, Sans-serif`) handle this correctly — confirm they are used consistently throughout, not just in the nav bar.

**Image hosted on `braze-images.com`**
```
https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/6532bc91.../original.png
```
This is the Braze legacy CDN domain (`appboy`). Braze has been migrating assets to `cdn.braze.com`. If this asset was uploaded pre-migration it should still resolve, but worth verifying it hasn't been orphaned.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM / Landing Page)

**UTMs unconfirmable without redirect resolution**
All clicks route through `links.elfcosmetics.com` Sailthru redirects. The actual destination URL and UTM parameters (campaign, source, medium, content) are encoded in the redirect hash and cannot be inspected from static source. Must be verified by following each redirect:
- Confirm `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`
- Confirm `utm_campaign` matches the campaign slug in Sailthru
- Confirm `utm_content` differentiates individual CTAs (logo vs. product vs. hero vs. nav links)

Without per-link `utm_content` differentiation, click attribution is aggregated and click-to-conversion mapping is impossible.
