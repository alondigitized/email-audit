- The two strongest offers — BOGO (buy 2 get 1 free) and the e.l.f. x Bubble collab — are buried below four full product cards instead of leading the email.

- Compared to 8/6's BOGO send and 8/7's Bubble launch, this reads like a lower-effort catalog dump that recycles both of those campaigns' assets as afterthoughts.

- 2. Cut two of the four product cards (or shrink them to a compact grid) and use the reclaimed space to surface the Bubble collab higher — a genuine collab beats a generic supplies list for open-to-click conversion.
