# National Watermelon Day just got juicier 🍉💋 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a tight, single-SKU launch email, and it's e.l.f. doing what it does best: a themed "holiday" (National Watermelon Day) wrapped around a genuinely new product, the Squeeze Me More Lip Balm Duo. The hero shot is the strongest visual in this whole recent run — mango and watermelon flesh flanking the two lip balm tubes on a sunset-gradient background is a legitimately appetizing, on-brand still life, and the price ($9, struck through from $8 — wait, that's backwards, see below) sits right under the headline.
- Compared to the last few sends — the Glow Reviver BOGO (2026-07-29, 8/10), the Humidity Hero launch (2026-07-30, 8/10), the National Lipstick Day GWP arc (2026-07-27 → 2026-07-31, 9/9/8/10) — this is another new-product spotlight rather than a gift-with-purchase mechanic, which is a nice change of pace and keeps the "holiday tie-in" formula from going stale. The one real flaw is a pricing display that reads as broken: "$9 $8" with the strikethrough on the lower number implies the price went up, not down, which will cost clicks from anyone who actually looks at it.
- Solid seasonal hook, best hero image in weeks, one fixable pricing glitch. Call it a 7 — good, not great.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely appetizing — ripe mango and watermelon flesh framing two candy-colored tubes reads as summer/fun without looking generic.
- "happy watermelon day to those who celebrate!" is a distinctive, on-brand voice moment that stands out from generic promo copy.
- The ingredient/benefit callouts (lightweight hydrating formula, ultra sheer wash of color, squeeze tube application, lip-loving ingredients) with matching emoji icons give real product education in a scannable format.
- "Top Picks for You" cross-sell block is clean and gives a clear secondary path to purchase for shoppers not sold on the lip balm.
- Category nav bar (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) and footer perks (free shipping, receipt scanning, Beauty Squad app) are consistent, trustworthy brand furniture.

## What's weak

- The price display "$9 $8" has the strikethrough on the wrong number — as rendered, it looks like the price increased from $8 to $9, which undercuts the entire offer.
- Two CTAs ("SHOP NOW" and "SQUEEZE ME MORE") do the same job with no differentiation — one should point at the product, the other could point at the full lip category.
- No urgency or time-boundedness anywhere in this email — "National Watermelon Day" is a nice hook but nothing signals whether this is a today-only price or a standing SKU launch.
- No social proof (star rating, review count) anywhere near the hero product, even though e.l.f. typically has strong review volume to lean on.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the price display first** — show it as "$8 $9" (sale price first, struck-through original second) or simply "$8, was $9" so it unambiguously reads as a discount, not a price hike. This is the single highest-impact fix; a confusing price kills the CTA on your hero product.
- 2. Add a light urgency cue tied to the holiday framing — e.g., "today only" or "through Sunday" — since National Watermelon Day is inherently a one-day hook and the email doesn't use that.
- 3. Differentiate the two CTAs: keep "SHOP NOW" under the hero linking straight to the Duo's PDP, and change "SQUEEZE ME MORE" to something like "SHOP ALL LIP BALMS" so it serves a distinct purpose instead of duplicating the first button.
- 4. Add a rating snippet ("4.5★ | 2,300 reviews") under the benefit icons — e.l.f.'s lip products typically carry strong ratings and this email has room for it above "Top Picks for You."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Watermelon Day just dropped a new lip balm 🍉`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lips called. It's watermelon season.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Squeeze Me More Duo — mango & watermelon, $8 today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hydrating, sheer, squeezable — meet your new everyday balm`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics — "National Watermelon Day" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses a Braze-hosted click-tracking redirector (`links.elfcosmetics.com`) with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup, but 4 of 12 probed CTAs resolve to malformed/relative destination URLs and the message ships with no plain-text part, a missing `List-Unsubscribe` header pair, and one untagged image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 of 12 probed links fail resolution with `unknown url type` errors — the tracking redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) unwraps to a **relative path with no scheme/host**, not an absolute URL:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (CTA: "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=...&utm_campaign=080326_national_watermelon_day&lid=...&source_caller=ui&af_dp` (CTA: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")

  This indicates the redirect target was saved without a base domain (likely a template merge/export error at the ESP), not just a QA-tool limitation — all four are high-intent CTAs (product PDP and account/app links), so this is a functional breakage risk, not cosmetic.
- All sampled links route through the Braze click-tracking domain (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` for clicks, `/q/...` for the tracking pixel) — tracking wrapper itself is present and consistent.
- 26 of 38 total links were not probed (footer/utility/social, deprioritized by design) — no data on those.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Responsive breakpoint at 660px with `.stack`/`.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes is implemented correctly for mobile stacking.
- Preheader text ("Celebrate with the Squeeze Me More Lip Balm Duo.") is present and hidden via the standard `display:none!important` technique.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, Liquid/Handlebars syntax, or unresolved personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%field%%`) found in the visible HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — either missing at send time or not captured by the relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support without header data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- Note: these are flagged as **WARN**, not confirmed failures, since the relay may simply not be capturing/forwarding these headers. Recommend verifying directly against raw SMTP headers at the sending MTA rather than relying on the relay capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Only one probed link carries explicit UTM parameters: the app-download CTA includes `utm_campaign=080326_national_watermelon_day` plus Branch/AppsFlyer params (`bxid`, `lid`, `af_dp`, `source_caller=ui`) — consistent with a deep-link/app-install flow.
- The two product CTAs ("Shop Now" ×2) and the account/rewards CTA carry **no UTM parameters** in their resolved destination — none of `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` present. This breaks campaign attribution continuity for those clicks even setting aside the relative-URL resolution issue above.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (PDP content matching promoted products, correct size/variant preselection via `Size=` query param) since destinations don't resolve to absolute URLs in current QA data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect target generation for the 4 failing links so they unwrap to absolute URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) instead of relative paths — verify at the ESP template/export step before next send.
2. Add UTM parameters to the two product CTAs and the account/rewards CTA for attribution parity with the app-download link.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the untagged tracking/content image, or confirm it's a pure tracking pixel (in which case `alt=""` is acceptable but should be explicit).
4. Generate and include a plain-text MIME part — currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring and accessibility for text-only clients.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by checking raw headers at the MTA (not just the relay capture), given Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
