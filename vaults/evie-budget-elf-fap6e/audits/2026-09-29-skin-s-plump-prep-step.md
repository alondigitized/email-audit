# Skin’s plump + prep step 🪩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-hero launch send for the Glass Slick Serum Stick ($12). It's the second Glass Slick push in a week, after the 2026-09-23 "Get five-star bouncy skin ⭐️" send. The blue hero with the pearlescent stick is striking, and "the plump + prep step." is a sharp headline. The before/after split gives the email its proof point.
- The email loses focus after that. A mascara GWP banner (spend $50+), a three-product "Top Picks" row that has nothing to do with the hero, and six empty gray category bars all compete with the serum stick. The Top Picks are a Thirst Burst serum and two Holy Hydration cleansers, so a reader who came for the stick gets no path to related products. The category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are plain gray blocks with no imagery, so they look unfinished.
- Verdict: a strong top third followed by a generic template tail. It's better than the 2026-09-28 category-dump send, but it's not as tight as the 2026-09-27 "under $8" bundle. It's a solid mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. The oversized product shot on a saturated blue background gives an instant focal point.
- "the plump + prep step." is short and benefit-led, and the "Only $12." price sits right in the subhead.
- The before/after split (before vs. e.l.f.ter) is credible, close-cropped and shows visible texture and glow.
- The black "SHOP NOW" pills are high-contrast and repeated after both the hero and the proof section.
- The GWP banner is easy to read. It says "get a full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara on us" and gives the $50+ threshold and "no code needed."

## What's weak

- Top Picks don't include the hero product, so the email dilutes its own launch.
- The six gray nav bars have no imagery and no offer, so they read as placeholder blocks and take up a lot of scroll.
- There are two identical "SHOP NOW" buttons in the hero area, and no button copy names the product.
- The GWP threshold ($50+) is out of step with a $12 hero, and the offer conditions sit in small footer print.
- The pink cruelty-free/vegan block has a generic tagline, "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face," that doesn't relate to the skincare launch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace Top Picks with products that pair with the stick: primer, foundation, or a Halo Glow item. Show the stick in a "prep + apply" routine and add a bundle price.
- 2. Remove the gray category bars or replace them with image tiles. If they stay, cut them to two or three.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, like "Get the Glass Slick Stick – $12". Use one button after the hero and drop the second.
- 4. Show the GWP threshold in the banner headline (e.g., "spend $50, get a free full-size mascara").
- 5. Add a short line of social proof, such as a star rating or review count, under the before/after.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Only 27% of the automated QA checks passed. Four of the 12 probed material links failed with `unknown url type` on relative destination paths, and the email has no plain-text part (0 chars). The header and authentication warnings are probably relay-capture artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
All four failures come from the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click-tracking wrapper. Each resolves to a relative path with no scheme or host:

| Link text | Resolved target |
|---|---|
| "Shop Now" | `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` |
| "Shop Now" | `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` |
| "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." | `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` |
| "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" | `/pages/mobile-app?shortlink=email&c=email&source_caller=ui&lid=xpf7net258pg&af_reengagement_window=30d&utm_campaign=092926_glasssli…` |

- **Cause:** the redirect `Location` is a relative path, so the probe couldn't follow it. This is consistent with the source `href`s being authored as relative paths and then wrapped by the ESP.
- **Possible user impact (unverified):** a browser resolves a relative `Location` against the tracking host. That would send clicks to `links.elfcosmetics.com/products/...` instead of `elfcosmetics.com`. It could also be a probe-side limitation. Confirm with `curl -sI <tracking URL>` and read the `Location` header.
- **Scope:** 12 links were probed and 28 skipped. The 4 failures are 33% of the probed set, and they include CTA-styled "Shop Now" buttons. The failure of any skipped link is unknown.
- **Truncated data:** the app-link URL is cut off in the QA output, so the full `utm_campaign` value can't be verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback:** QA reports 0 chars (`[FAIL]`). This may be a relay-capture artifact, but if the message really has no `text/plain` part, it is a deliverability and accessibility gap.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty.
- **Missing alt:** one image has no alt text: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/DG2dYFeNO-zkbLuuG227RA~~/...`. The `/q/` path looks like a tracking pixel, in which case the impact is minor.
- **Hero alt text:** the hero image has `alt="e.l.f."`, which is only the brand name. If that image carries offer copy, the offer isn't available to screen readers or with images blocked. This can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Invalid attribute:** `<img ... height="auto">` is invalid, because the `height` attribute expects an integer. Outlook may misrender it. The inline `height:auto` is fine.
- **Malformed CSS:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the `mobile_hide` block has an empty first family. It is harmless because the fallbacks apply.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` (Open Sans) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Many clients strip it, but the Arial/Sans-serif fallback is declared, so it degrades safely.
- **Already correct:** layout tables use `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"` is set, and a hidden preheader is present.
