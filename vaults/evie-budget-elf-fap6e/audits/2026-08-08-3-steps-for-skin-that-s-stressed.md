# 3 steps for skin that’s stressed 
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. SKIN's routine-builder play: a 3-step skincare regimen (Hypochlorous Mist → Pimple Patches → Clarify Facial Oil) framed around "stressed" skin. The individual step cards are clean and well-photographed, but the email doesn't stop there — it tacks on the Bubble collab teaser from yesterday's send (8/7, "e.l.f. x Bubble") and the BOGO REFRESH offer from 8/6 ("Get a skincare e.l.f.ssential on us"), turning what could be a tight single-purpose send into a four-campaign scroll.
- The result is a lot of "SHOP NOW" buttons and no single dominant CTA. A subscriber who's seen the last five days of e.l.f. emails (8/2 through 8/7 in the brand history) will recognize both bolt-on promos as repeats, which flattens the "new" feeling this send is trying to create with its own headline offer.
- Solid execution, muddled priority. This lands as a mid-pack send — good creative craft dragged down by campaign-stacking and a soft, discount-free hero.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent product photography for all three routine steps — each on its own colored background, easy to scan.
- The "3 steps" structure gives the email a clear narrative arc (soothe → treat → clarify) rather than a flat product grid.
- Ingredient/benefit specificity is visible per step (e.g., "2% salicylic acid + calm" on the Facial Oil, hypochlorous callout on the mist).
- No render bugs — layout, images, and buttons all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- "Top Picks for You" section adds a personalization cue even though it's generic cross-sell.

## What's weak

- No single primary CTA — five-plus identical "SHOP NOW" buttons across steps, the Bubble teaser, the BOGO banner, and Top Picks dilute the eye instead of directing it.
- The hero has no discount or urgency of its own; the actual offer (buy 2, get 1 free, code REFRESH) is buried two scrolls down and is a repeat of the 8/6 send.
- The Bubble collab banner ("we just glow together") is also a repeat beat from 8/7 — three of the last three sends now share overlapping promo content, which reads as campaign fatigue to a daily subscriber.
- CTA copy is generic ("SHOP NOW" everywhere) rather than specific to each product (e.g., "Shop the Mist").
- No social proof (ratings, review counts) anywhere in the body despite three product callouts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Bubble and BOGO REFRESH sections entirely — this send already has a complete narrative (3-step routine); stacking two unrelated repeat campaigns on top just adds noise and cadence fatigue.
- 2. Put an actual offer in the hero (e.g., "20% off the routine" or bundle pricing) so there's something concrete to open for, not just a regimen pitch.
- 3. Vary the CTA copy per step ("Soothe Now," "Treat Now," "Clarify Now") so the buttons reinforce the routine story instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" five times.
- 4. Add a single line of social proof under at least the hero product (e.g., star rating or "10K+ reviews") to build trust before the click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Redness, breakouts, stress — sorted in 3 steps`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soothe, spot-treat, and clarify — shop the full routine`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New routine drop: calm stressed skin in 3 easy steps`
