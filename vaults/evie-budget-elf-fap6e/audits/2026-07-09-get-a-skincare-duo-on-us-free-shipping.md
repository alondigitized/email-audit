# Get a skincare duo on us + FREE shipping
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a GWP (gift-with-purchase) send from e.l.f. SKIN: add the "Hottest Drops Duo" to cart, use code BURST, get it free — plus free shipping on orders $50+. The hero is clean, the price-anchoring on Top Picks ($6–$10) is smart, and the "BURST" code cleverly echoes the "Dew Burst Drops" product line visible in the body. The creative is competent and on-brand.
- The fundamental problem is a subject-line contract the body breaks. "Get a skincare duo on us + FREE shipping" reads like you're getting both things free with any order. The body reveals the free shipping has a $50 minimum. That's a bait-and-switch — minor in absolute terms, but it trains subscribers to distrust the subject line over time. It's the kind of thing that quietly erodes open rates quarter over quarter.
- Cadence is the other flag. This is the seventh e.l.f./e.l.f. SKIN email in nine days. Even if SKIN and makeup segments are separated, they share an inbox. Today's send is solid but would benefit more from a one-day rest than from any copy tweak.

## What's working

- **Code-to-product echo**: promo code BURST maps visually to "Dew Burst Drops" in the Top Picks row — subtle but coherent.
- **Warm hero visual**: caramel/terracotta background paired with gold/neutral product packaging is seasonally appropriate and on-trend for summer skin.
- **Price ladder is clear**: Top Picks rows $6 / $8 / $10 — low stakes, easy to add to cart to hit the $50 threshold.
- **"FREE DUO GIFT!" badge** in the hero image anchors the value prop before you even read the headline.
- **e.l.f. SKIN sender identity** distinguishes this from the makeup-line sends that dominated the prior six days — smarter segmentation than blasting the full list.

## What's weak

- **Subject overpromises on shipping**: "FREE shipping" in the subject; "with all orders $50+" in the body. The qualifier should live in the subject or at minimum the preheader.
- **SHOP NOW is a dead-end CTA**: compared to the July 5 Soft Glam email ("build the routine" with four numbered steps), this CTA gives you nowhere specific to land — just a generic shop page.
- **No urgency anchor**: "online only" and "limited time only" appear but neither has a date or countdown. The July 2 "Lock the e.l.f. in" email had the same gap — this brand repeatedly skips the deadline.
- **Code friction at checkout**: BURST must be applied manually. For a GWP, "auto-added at cart" converts better than a code. At minimum, surface this earlier — it's buried in sub-copy.
- **Top Picks at $6–$10 work against hitting $50**: showing $6 and $8 items right below the $50 free-shipping line makes the math feel hard. The anchor products should be in the $15–$25 range or the email should explicitly say "add 3–4 favorites."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject-line contract first.** Move the $50 minimum into the subject or preheader so openers aren't surprised. "Free duo + free shipping on $50" is 37 chars and honest.
- 2. **Replace SHOP NOW with a destination-specific CTA**: "Get the Duo Free" or "Claim Your Free Drops Duo" — a verb + specific noun that matches the hero offer.
- 3. **Add a deadline.** Even "this week only" converts better than "limited time." The July 4 SPF send's summer-urgency framing was the right model; borrow it.
- 4. **Anchor Top Picks to bigger baskets**: swap one $6 item for a $15–$18 set so the path to $50 feels achievable in 2–3 items, not 6–7.
- 5. **Add a BURST code auto-copy or inline explanation**: "Tap to copy: BURST" reduces checkout abandonment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free skincare duo when you spend $50 (code: BURST)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Drops Duo is waiting — code BURST inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on every order this week. Here's how to claim it.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Add 3 favorites, use BURST at checkout, grab the duo free.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **Empty `<title>` tag** (`<title></title>`): Some preview panes (Apple Mail, Samsung Mail) display the document title; leaving it blank produces "Untitled" in those contexts. Should be set to match the subject line or sender name.
- **Mobile h1 font-size of 9px:** The media query rule `.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1 { font-size: 9px !important }` reduces a heading to 9px on mobile — well below the WCAG 2.1 minimum legible threshold. Evidence: `@media (max-width:660px)` block in `<style>`.
- **Google Fonts `<link>` tags:** `fonts.googleapis.com` stylesheets are stripped by Gmail, Outlook, and most major clients. Open Sans and Jost will not load. Fallback stack (`Arial, Sans-serif`) is present in inline styles, so this is a degraded-experience issue rather than a breakage — but the Jost font has **no inline fallback** in the preheader div (`font-family:Open Sans,Arial,Sans-serif`; Jost is referenced in the stylesheet only).
- **`#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none }`:** This Gmail-targeting rule suppresses all link color and underlines inside the message body. If body text is dark and no explicit link color is set inline, linked text will be visually indistinguishable from surrounding copy.

**No issues found:** `lang="en"` is set on `<html>`. All `<table>` elements carry `role="presentation"`. The logo `alt` attribute is present (`alt="e.l.f."`).
