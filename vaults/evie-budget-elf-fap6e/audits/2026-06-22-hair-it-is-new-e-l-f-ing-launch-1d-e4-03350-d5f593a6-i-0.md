# Hair it is! 💁‍♀️ NEW e.l.f.ing launch
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. Hair's third bundle push in seven days — and the creative fatigue is visible in the structure. The hero delivers a clean TikTok Exclusive banner with "your bundles, bundled." copy that is essentially a reprise of the June 18 "Bundle your haircare 💁‍♀️" send. The subject line promises a *new e.l.f.ing launch*, but the email body delivers a Weekly Highlight Reel digest with hair relegated to the hero slot. That mismatch is the email's core problem: the subject sets a focused launch expectation; the body pivots immediately into a catch-all digest.
- The hair hero itself has no dedicated CTA button. Click intent built by the hero evaporates as the eye travels down into "YOUR WEEKLY HIGHLIGHT REEL," "YOUR NEW OBSESSIONS," and four generic product tiles with "SHOP NOW" links that have nothing to do with hair bundles. The weekly digest format made sense on June 15 as a standalone roundup; grafted onto a declared product launch, it reads like two emails fighting for the same body.
- The verdict: this is a June 18 bundle email wearing a different subject line. Sending the same concept four days later without a new offer, a price anchor, or any scarcity mechanism is a cadence mistake. A subscriber who opened June 18 is looking at the same hero creative and has no new reason to click.

## What's working

- **TikTok Exclusive badge** on the hero is a real differentiator — exclusivity signals create native platform urgency that the email doesn't otherwise provide.
- **"your bundles, bundled." headline** is concise, witty, on-brand voice; no ambiguity about what category this is promoting.
- **No render bugs**: images load, layout holds, no broken sections, no truncated text.
- **Free shipping threshold (spend $15+) visible in footer** — this is the one concrete offer in the email; it should be in the hero.
- **Beauty Squad app download** in the footer extends the loyalty CTA naturally for existing members.

## What's weak

- **Hair hero has no CTA button** — the most important action in a launch email is absent from the hero itself; you have to scroll past the entire Weekly Highlight Reel section before hitting any buy-path.
- **Digest body cannibalizes the launch message** — "YOUR WEEKLY HIGHLIGHT REEL" and six generic product grid tiles immediately below the hair hero dilute the declared campaign focus to near zero.
- **Zero new offer vs. June 18** — "your bundles, bundled" with no price, no savings callout, and no deadline is the same bundle pitch from four days ago without a reason to act this time.
- **Cadence is at its limit**: this is e.l.f. Hair email #4 in seven days (June 16, 18, 20, 22); the June 21 skin pivot was a smart rest that this send immediately undercuts.
- **"NEW e.l.f.ing launch" in subject overpromises** — there is no new product visible; the hero shows the same bundle collection that has been promoted since June 16.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the digest section entirely.** If the declared purpose is a hair launch, commit to it. One hero, one CTA, one product story. The Weekly Highlight Reel is a separate email type; mixing them here dilutes both.
- 2. **Add a CTA button directly inside the hair hero** — "SHOP THE BUNDLES" placed immediately below the "your bundles, bundled." headline would capture intent before the eye ever reaches the digest.
- 3. **Introduce a concrete incentive** to separate this from the June 18 send. A limited-time bundle price ("$XX for 3"), a free-with-bundle gift, or a member-exclusive discount gives a subscriber who saw June 18 a new reason to click.
- 4. **Rest e.l.f. Hair for at least 48 hours** and let the June 21 SKIN momentum land before returning to the same hair bundle concept. If cadence pressure demands a send, rotate to a different sub-brand.
- 5. **Surface the TikTok Exclusive mechanic in the subject.** "TikTok Shop exclusive" is an actual scarcity signal — it belongs in the subject, not buried in a hero badge.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TikTok Exclusive only: e.l.f. Hair bundles drop now 💁‍♀️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bundle up your hair routine — TikTok Shop exclusive`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match & save — new e.l.f. Hair combos only on TikTok Shop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New hair bundles just landed. Grab yours before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Hair Launch Email

**From:** makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com | **ESP detected:** Sailthru (link pattern) + Braze (image CDN)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for Outlook/mobile compatibility but contains a cross-platform asset split (Sailthru link tracking, Braze image CDN), a broken `font-family` declaration, and duplicate conflicting Google Fonts imports that add unnecessary HTTP requests.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cross-platform ESP split (high risk)**
- Click links route through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` — the `/f/a/` path is Sailthru's redirect format
- Header image is served from `braze-images.com` (Braze asset CDN)
- This combination indicates either an active platform migration or a misconfigured send — analytics will be split across two platforms with no unified attribution

**Encoded destination unverifiable**
- The only link in the provided source is a Sailthru-wrapped redirect. The final destination URL is base64-encoded and cannot be confirmed without decoding. UTM parameter carriage through the redirect chain is unauditable from source alone.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the title in some clients (e.g. Apple Mail). An empty title degrades accessibility; should be `<title>e.l.f. Hair — New Launch</title>` or similar.

**Broken `font-family` declaration**
```html
font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif
```
The first value is an empty string literal — not a valid font name. The web font (Jost) never gets applied; the browser falls through to Verdana immediately. Should be `font-family:'Jost',Verdana,sans-serif`.

**Duplicate, conflicting Google Fonts imports**
- `<head>` contains: `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Jost">` (v1 API, no `display=swap`, no weight variants)
- Body `<style>` block contains: `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost:ital,wght@0,100..900;1,100..900&display=swap')` (v2 API, full variants)

Two separate HTTP requests for the same font family, different API versions, the `<link>` version lacks `display=swap` (causes FOIT). The `@import` inside body is also ignored by several clients (e.g. Gmail strips `<style>` blocks in body). Net result: font load behavior is undefined across clients.

**Nested `nl-container` structure**
The outer table has `class="nl-container"` and the inner `<div>` in row-1 contains a second complete `nl-container` structure with its own `row row-1`. This nested duplication is likely a template assembly error and may cause double-rendering of the header block in some clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Preheader is hardcoded**
```html
<div style="display:none !important;…">Peep our top picks for you!</div>
```
No merge token — the same preheader text goes to all recipients regardless of segment or behavioral data. No issues flagged beyond this observation (the structure itself is technically correct).

**Preheader padding uses mixed invisible characters**
The filler div uses a mix of `͏` (U+034F combining grapheme joiner) and `‌` (U+200C zero-width non-joiner). Mixed character classes in hidden content can contribute to spam score in conservative filters (SpamAssassin RAZOR/BAYES).

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — Cannot assess from the truncated source. The footer containing the physical address, unsubscribe link, and preference center is not present in the provided HTML. Must be verified in the full source before send.

**Sender domain:** `m.elfcosmetics.com` (subdomain mailer). DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be confirmed from HTML source alone — requires header inspection of a delivered copy. Given Sailthru+Braze split, confirm DKIM signing is on `m.elfcosmetics.com` and not a Braze or Sailthru shared domain.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable** — All links are Sailthru-wrapped. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are injected by the Sailthru redirect config cannot be confirmed from source. If UTM injection is not configured in Sailthru's link management settings, GA4/site analytics will attribute all traffic as direct.

**Landing page alignment** — The logo link destination is the only confirmed link in the source fragment; product/CTA links are in the truncated portion and could not be audited.
