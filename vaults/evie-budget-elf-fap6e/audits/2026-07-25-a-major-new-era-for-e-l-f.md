# A major new era for e.l.f. 💁‍♀️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. entering the haircare category for real — six SKUs, seven price points, and a hero visual built around a customer testimonial ("Anna uses Never Thirsty Shampoo & Conditioner") rather than a single product push. Compared to the tight, single-SKU launches in this brand's recent history (Soft Glam Cream Blush, Invis-Stick SPF), this is a heavier lift: it has to introduce a whole new shelf, not just add to an existing one. The wordplay copy ("e.l.f.ective, e.l.f.ordable, e.l.f.onic") carries the brand voice well and the product grid is legible.
- The subject line undersells the news, though. "A major new era for e.l.f." is vague enough that it could be about anything — a rebrand, a store opening, a CEO change — and doesn't earn its curiosity with a payoff until you open. And the email has one real production flaw: the category navigation bar (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR) renders as blank grey bars with no imagery, which reads as broken rather than intentional.
- Strong brand execution on a genuinely big launch, undercut by a soft subject line and one visible layout bug.

## What's working

- The hero directly names the news — "LOOK WHO'S E.L.F.ING HAIR" — with a real product in hand (Never Thirsty Shampoo), not a vague teaser.
- The six-product "meet the mane crew" grid uses consistent color-blocked cards with price, one-line benefit copy, and a legible name/subname hierarchy for every SKU.
- Brand wordplay ("e.l.f.ective, e.l.f.ordable, e.l.f.onic") ties the whole email together and is distinctly on-voice.
- Two "CLICK HAIR" CTAs (top and bottom of the product block) give the reader a shot at converting without excessive scrolling.
- Loyalty/utility footer elements (Beauty Squad app, receipt scanning, TikTok Shop) are present and consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- The category nav bar (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) renders as unstyled grey bars with no icons or images — looks broken, not like a designed menu.
- No actual offer anchors the send — no %/$ off, no bundle, no intro-price; it's a straight catalog listing at full price.
- "Top Picks for You" surfaces a face primer, a face cream, and a setting spray in a hair-launch email — the personalization module contradicts the email's own focus.
- The vegan/cruelty-free tagline ("e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face") doesn't mention hair at all, a missed chance to close the loop on the category story.
- Subject line gives no indication this is a haircare launch, so the "aha" only lands after opening.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to name the category — the biggest lever here is telling people what the "major new era" actually is before they open, not after.
- 2. Fix or remove the blank nav bar — as rendered it looks like a bug, not a feature; either populate it with icons/thumbnails or drop it from this send.
- 3. Swap "Top Picks for You" to hair-adjacent cross-sells (or omit it) so the email doesn't undercut its own launch narrative with face-category products.
- 4. Add one concrete hook — an intro bundle price, a "buy the shampoo + conditioner duo" pairing, or a limited-time bundle discount — to give the CTA something beyond curiosity to click on.
- 5. Extend the closing tagline to include hair ("e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, face, and now hair") to close the category-launch loop visually.
