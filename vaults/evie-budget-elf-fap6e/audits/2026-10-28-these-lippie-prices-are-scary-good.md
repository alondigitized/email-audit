# These lippie prices are scary good 👻
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-theme Halloween lip send. The pun subject ("scary good") carries into a black-and-white headline over a tight close-up of glossy red lips. The offer is easy to find: lip combos for $15 or less, with two $11 bundles below it. Each bundle gets a full-bleed pink or plum product shot, a black copy block and a white "SHOP NOW" pill, so the structure is easy to scan.
- The email doesn't commit to a single message after the hero. The $15 headline sits above $11 combos, so the price story is muddled. A gift-with-purchase banner ("choose a full-size gift on us" at $50+) then competes with the lip offer. Below that, "Top Picks for You" shows a primer, a lip liner and a hydrating product. The lip liner is the only item that fits the theme. The empty grey category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) look like stacked placeholders and add dead scroll.
- Compared with the 10-27 Gloss Mode oil send (7/10) and the 10-24 gift guide (7/10), this one has a sharper theme and a clearer offer, but the lower half is generic. It's a solid, slightly above-average promo.

## What's working

- The subject and hero headline match, so the pun pays off the moment the email opens.
- The close-up of lips with a doe-foot applicator is a strong, on-category hero.
- Two product-pair modules with a named bundle and a flat $11 price make the value concrete.
- Each module repeats the same pattern: image, bold headline, one line of copy, white pill CTA. The eye moves down without effort.
- The pink and plum product photography is rich and fits the season without a costume gimmick.

## What's weak

- "$15 or less" in the hero conflicts with the $11 bundles below it. A reader can't tell which price applies.
- The gift-with-purchase banner mid-email competes with the lip offer. It's also the same "gift on us" promo from the 10-21 send.
- "Top Picks for You" is off-theme. The primer and the hydrating item aren't lip products, and the prices shown ($11, $2, $13) don't line up with the bundle story.
- The grey category bars read as empty placeholder blocks with no imagery, which looks unfinished.
- The legal fine print is tiny and dense, and nothing in the email says when the offer ends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lock the offer to one number. Either "lip combos, $11 each" in the hero, or keep "$15 or less" and state each bundle's saving against its separate prices.
- 2. Put a visible deadline in the hero, such as "ends Halloween", since the fine print says October 31.
- 3. Move the gift-with-purchase block to the bottom, or cut it from lip-focused sends.
- 4. Make "Top Picks" lip-only, with product names, so the module supports the theme.
- 5. Replace the grey category bars with image tiles, or remove them.
- 6. Rename the CTAs from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop the $11 lip duo" and similar.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, viewport meta, MSO fallbacks, hidden preheader). The QA run flags 5 of 12 probed links as failing, an empty plain-text part, and missing compliance and authentication headers. Several of these may be artifacts of the relay or the probe, and I note that below.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 click-tracked links failed the probe** with `unknown url type`. All five go through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` and resolve to a relative path with no scheme or host:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=That+Merlot+Tho+-+Soft+Burgundy` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&utm_medium=email&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&utm_source=broad...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **I can't tell whether this is a real defect or a probe artifact.** Either the tracker returns a relative `Location` header, which a client that doesn't resolve it against the host would reject, or the redirect target is malformed. The probe couldn't confirm a 200 for any of them. Manually follow one redirect chain (`curl -sIL`) to check.
- **The app link URL is cut off** mid-parameter at `utm_source=broad` in the QA output. That is probably display truncation, but check that the full URL isn't actually truncated.
- Of the 40 links, 28 were skipped by design (footer and social links). Their health is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Image missing alt text:** `src=https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/bwuJcMOJe5PkuiD99aEUYA~~/...`. The `/q/` path looks like an open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` to satisfy validators.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Empty font-family name:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in an HTML block. It falls through to Verdana, so it is harmless but sloppy.
- **Nested duplicate `nl-container`/`row` table structures** inside `html_block` wrappers add DOM depth. This is a template-export artifact with no confirmed rendering failure.
- **No plain-text part** (see Compliance).
- **The header image alt `e.l.f.`** is minimal, but this is a style point and not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appeared in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] The plain-text alternative is 0 characters.** It is a multipart deliverability and accessibility gap. The relay may not have captured it, so check the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Verify against the raw message, because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com`, and the click-tracking domain is `links.elfcosmetics.com`.
- **The physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be confirmed** because the source is truncated before the footer. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs on the product links are unverified.** The `/products/...` destinations show only variant parameters (`Size=`, `Color=`) and no `utm_*` after resolution. Either the tracker appends them on redirect or they are missing, and campaign attribution in analytics depends on which.
- **Only the app link carries UTMs** (`utm_medium=email`, `utm_source=broad...`, plus `bxid`). Check that the truncated tail also has `utm_campaign`.
- **The "Receipt scanning" CTA goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`.** Logged-out recipients land on a login wall instead of a page that explains the feature. The `return_to` target is `/account` and not a receipts page.
- **The product deep links preselect variants** (the Size and Color parameters). This is good for alignment, but it depends on the redirects resolving, which they didn't in the probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually trace the 5 failing `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirects to see whether the `Location` header is relative or the links are broken. If they are broken, fix them with the ESP/tracking-domain owner.
2. Inspect the raw MIME for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the `text/plain` part, to separate relay-stripping from real omissions. If a part is really absent, add a text part and one-click unsubscribe headers.
3. Confirm the `utm_*` parameters survive the redirect on the product links, and that the app link is complete.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and a real `<title>`, and remove the empty font-family.
5. Point the receipt-scanning CTA at a public page for the feature instead of the login route.
