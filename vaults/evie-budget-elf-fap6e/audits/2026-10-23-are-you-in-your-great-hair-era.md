# Are you in your great hair era? 💁‍♀️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide send with a hair theme, and it's the cleanest creative e.l.f. has sent this week. The hero is a purple-and-lime block that says "gifts for a great hair era." Below it are four full-bleed lifestyle panels, one per product. Each has a "for their ___ era" tagline, a product name, a price, and a yellow SHOP NOW button. The subject's "hair era" framing carries straight through the body, so it feels like one idea.
- The weakness is the offer. There's no discount, bundle deal, or deadline anywhere. The only concrete signals are the prices ($10, $15, $9, $18), and those are small. The "Top Picks for You" row and the category list (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) then pull the reader toward makeup, away from the hair story. It's a good product-led email, but it doesn't give anyone a reason to buy today.
- Compared with the rest of the week, it sits near the top. It's less pushy than the 10/21 "full-size gift on us" promo and more focused than the 10/19 catch-all "Edit." It's about on par with the 10/22 Halo Glow launch, but without that email's "limited edition" urgency.

## What's working

- The "for their ___ era" copy repeats across all four panels and ties back to the subject line.
- Each panel pairs a product name with a price, so a reader can judge it quickly.
- The lifestyle photos are bright, saturated, and varied. The lime and purple palette is distinctly e.l.f.
- The SHOP NOW buttons are consistent, high-contrast yellow on purple, and easy to find.
- The layout is a clear vertical scroll, with no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There's no promo, discount, or deadline. "Gifts" with no gifting date or free-shipping threshold gives nobody a reason to act now.
- All four buttons say the same "SHOP NOW" and don't name the product, so none of them is specific.
- The hero is a product shot with no model, and the hero text is small relative to the panel.
- The "Top Picks for You" row shows makeup (blush, lip liner, primer), which dilutes the hair theme. The row is small and its price text is hard to read.
- The grey category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are a lot of dead space at the bottom, and they don't reinforce the gift idea.
- The email is very long, and the real value is in the first half.

- 2. Make each CTA specific: "Shop Gloss Mode Oil," "Shop the Blowout Duo," and so on.
- 3. Put the prices in larger type, or add a "Bundle & save" line, since the prices are the strongest hook.
- 4. Replace the "Top Picks for You" row with hair products, or a hair-focused set, to keep the story consistent.
- 5. Cut the grey category bars, or collapse them into one slim nav row.
- 6. Make the hero text larger, and show a person in the hero to match the panels below.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a clean, table-based structure with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` tables and a preheader. Five of the 12 probed links failed the automated check, and there is no plain-text part. The QA data can't confirm the unsubscribe headers or authentication results.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 of 12 probed links errored** with `unknown url type: '/…'`, a relative path with no scheme or host. All five are wrapped `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirects:
  - `/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=Left+on+Red+-+sheer+red` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Espresso+Martini+-+Cool+Taupe+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (receipt scanning / rewards)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?is_retargeting=true&af_reengagement_window=30d&bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause (not confirmed):** the tracker's redirect returns a relative `Location` header, and the QA client doesn't resolve it against the host. This could be a probe artifact rather than a broken link. Real mail clients resolve relative redirects, so the failures are unverified rather than confirmed breaks. A manual check of each wrapped URL would settle it.
- **Printed error for the app link:** the `deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app` string appears to be cut off in the QA output, so I can't check the full URL.
- **Click tracking:** all links go through the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/…` redirect domain. That is normal ESP click tracking, and the first-party domain is good for deliverability.
- **Coverage gap:** 28 links were skipped. Footer, social and utility links were not verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** one image has no `alt`. Its src is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/r8wLea8pGNlyNdw5YUbI-g~~/AAQRxRA~/uGw1nUoR6G3rBSUVId9WKzs-…`. The `/q/` path looks like an open-tracking pixel, which should carry `alt=""`. This is low severity.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this hurts accessibility and the "view in browser" tab label.
- **Empty font name:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in an `html_block`. It is harmless because the fallback applies, but it is a template artifact.
- **Nested `nl-container` / `row-1` structure:** each `html_block` embeds a full table layout inside another table. The same class names repeat (`row-1`, `block-1`) and the nesting is deep. This is valid but fragile in Outlook.
- **Web font:** Open Sans is loaded via Google Fonts inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, with an Arial fallback. No issue.
- The hero image has `alt="e.l.f."` (brand name only), and the viewport meta tag and `mso` conditionals are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing:** `Text version is 0 chars` is a FAIL. HTML-only mail is a spam-scoring and accessibility risk, and it hurts clients that render text only.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. I can't confirm whether the sender omitted them or the relay dropped them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It is expected to be missing through the relay, so don't treat it as a defect.
- The From address uses `m.elfcosmetics.com`, a dedicated mail subdomain, with tracking on `links.elfcosmetics.com`. This is good practice. The QA data says an unsubscribe link was always probed, but no result is shown. The physical address is not in the truncated source, so I can't evaluate it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination paths revealed by the failed probes (`/products/...?Color=…`, `/products/...?Size=…`) carry **no `utm_*` parameters**. Attribution may be handled by the ESP redirect or appended server-side, but nothing in the QA data confirms it.
- The product links deep-link to specific variants (`Color=`, `Size=`), which aligns the landing page with the creative.
- The app link carries `bxid`, `af_reengagement_window` and `deep_link_value`, so attribution there is handled through the AppsFlyer/Braze parameters.
- The "Receipt scanning" link points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, a login wall. Logged-out recipients land on a sign-in page rather than the rewards feature. This is a continuity concern, not a break.
- I can't verify landing-page content, because the probes errored before reaching the pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow the five failing wrapped links to confirm they resolve. If the relative `Location` header is real, have the ESP return absolute URLs.
2. Add a plain-text alternative part. It is currently 0 chars.
3. Inspect the raw `.eml` or the ESP's send config for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay hides these from the QA tool.
4. Add `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` to the destination URLs, or confirm the ESP appends them.
5. Fill in `<title>` and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Remove the empty `font-family:''` declaration in the template.
7. Probe the 28 skipped links, especially the footer, to verify the unsubscribe flow.
