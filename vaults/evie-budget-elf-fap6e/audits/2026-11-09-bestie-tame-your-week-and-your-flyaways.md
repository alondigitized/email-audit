## Executive summary

- The subject promises a hair-care email ("tame your week and your flyaways 💇‍♀️"), and the body is a catch-all "The e.l.f. Edit." The hero is a black bag with headphones and a lip product, and the intro copy mentions "smoothing out your hair," but that is the only hair reference. Nothing in the render shows a hair product. This is the same mismatch as the 2026-11-03 "Bestie, your daily pick-me-up is served" send, which also promised something the body didn't deliver.

Each one competes with the others. The "free full-size gift on us" block is the strongest offer. It sits mid-email under a dark hero, though, and the header shows no deadline. The bottom half is also weak, with empty gray category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) that look like unfinished placeholders.
- Verdict: a competent brand template with a subject line that doesn't match the content. It would work better as a hair-focused send, or with a subject that matches what's actually in the body.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 of 12 probed links failed.** All five are `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` tracked redirects. The probe error is `unknown url type: '/products/...'`, which means the redirect resolved to a path with no scheme or host:
  - `/products/peel-of-approval-lip-liner-stain?Color=Honey+Maker+-+Warm+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/fine-as-fleck-glitter-eyeshadow?Color=Pop+Off+Pink` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/micro-fine-brow-pencil?Color=Neutral+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/glow-reviver-lip-oil-glimmer?Color=Crystal+Baller+-+Champagne+%2B+Crystal+Pearl` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
- **Likely cause.** Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header, so these may work for real users. A strict HTTP client does not, and the probe is an example. Treat this as unconfirmed for end users until someone clicks the links. If the tracker really emits relative redirects, any client that doesn't resolve them (some security scanners, link-preview bots) will break.
- **Login-wall link.** The receipt-scanning link points at `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. It sends all recipients to a login page rather than to a feature page.
- **Coverage gap.** 35 links were skipped, so link health for the rest is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
