# NEW kits for your skincare fix 💙
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, gift-framed skincare kit launch from e.l.f. SKIN. The hero, "come & kit it.", is a pun that lands, and the "Starting at just $12" line puts a concrete price up top. Three new-badged products follow, each with a price and a black SHOP NOW button: the Holy Hydration! e.l.f.ssentials Travel Trio at $15, the Thirst Burst Lip BFFs at $12, and the e.l.f.conic Skin Routine at $25. The structure is easy to scan, and the pastel blue and pink visuals are on brand.
- The email carries too many ideas for one send. After the three kits it adds a limited-time gift-with-purchase ("Choose a full-size gift *on us* when you spend $50+"), a skincare quiz, a Top Picks row of three products, a category nav, and a loyalty and app footer. The gift offer is a strong hook but sits mid-email in a small black block. The Top Picks row is thin. The category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) render as big empty gray blocks, which looks unfinished.
- Compared with the Oct 14 lip balm send and the Oct 16 setting spray send, which were tight single-story launches that scored 7, this one is broader. It's better than the Oct 19 "Bestie" roundup because it has a clear theme and prices. Verdict: solid and promotional, but it buries its best offer.

## What's working

- The "come & kit it." headline is a playful pun and ties to the 💙 in the subject.
- Starting-at-$12 pricing in the hero gives a concrete price anchor.
- Each kit has a bold price in its headline and a one-line benefit, such as "3 hydrating holy grails in one carry on-friendly kit."
- Every block has a clear black SHOP NOW button, so the CTAs are unambiguous.
- The "new!" badges and the saturated blue and pink product photography make the hierarchy easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The gift-with-purchase ($50+, no code needed) is the strongest offer but sits mid-email in a small block, well below the hero.
- The category navigation (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) renders as large empty gray bars. It looks like missing images and wastes vertical space.
- The email is very long. It has three kits, a gift offer, a quiz, Top Picks, a category nav, and a footer with four feature blocks. The focus is diluted.
- The Top Picks products are small and low-contrast. Two of them, the Daily Cleanser and the Makeup Remover, aren't kits, so they drift from the theme.
- The lip balm duo is a makeup item inside a skincare-branded send, and the "Holy Hydration!" headline is repeated for two different kits, which is confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift-with-purchase up directly under the hero, or fold it into the hero line: "Kits from $12 + a free full-size gift at $50."
- 2. Fix or remove the empty gray category bars. Use real tiles or a simple text nav.
- 3. Cut the Top Picks row and the quiz block to shorten the email, or make Top Picks show kit-related items only.
- 4. Give each kit a distinct name. Don't use "Holy Hydration!" twice, and label the kits by who they're for, such as "travel," "lip," or "gift."
- 5. Add social proof, such as a star rating or review count, on each kit.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. SKIN, "NEW kits for your skincare fix 💙"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, preheader, MSO conditionals). The delivered message has no plain-text part, and the QA probe flagged four tracked CTAs. The probe's "unknown url type" errors look like a relative-redirect artifact rather than confirmed breakage (see §2). The source was truncated, so the footer and the rest of the body could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** This is the error a client throws when a redirect `Location` is relative and was not resolved against the host. The failing wrapped links are:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?lid=…&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&pid=Email&af…` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
- **What this means.** Browsers resolve relative `Location` headers, so real users probably land fine. Treat these as unverified, not confirmed broken. The final destinations were never fetched, so landing-page status is unknown.
- **Possible tracker issue.** If the redirect host (`links.elfcosmetics.com`) really emits relative `Location` values, that is non-standard behavior worth confirming with the ESP. It is a low-severity risk for strict clients.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 41 links were probed (29 skipped by ranking), so the other 29 are unverified.
- **Link wrapping.** All observed links use the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` click-tracking wrapper. That is expected, and the hero image link is wrapped consistently.
- **Open-pixel URL.** The `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/…` URL flagged for missing alt looks like a tracking pixel. That is likely benign, but the `<img>` lacks `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>` (confirmed in source).
- **Missing alt.** The `/q/un4O8phm…` image has no `alt`. If it is a pixel, add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Generic alt.** The hero banner's `alt="e.l.f."` / `title="e.l.f."` doesn't describe the linked content. The image is a link, so its alt should convey the destination or action.
- **Invalid font-family.** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` appears in a `mobile_hide` html_block. The empty font name is harmless but sloppy.
- **Nested wrappers.** Repeated `html_block` blocks each contain a full `nl-container`/`row` table stack. This is bloat and risks duplicate class names, but it is not a rendering failure from what was visible.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is unsupported in some clients, and the Arial/Sans-serif fallback is present.
- **Hidden preheader.** It is implemented correctly with `display:none`, `mso-hide:all`, and `max-height:0`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) were observed. The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part missing.** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars` ([FAIL]). This is a multipart/alternative gap that hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, that is a gap for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results** was not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is unknown. The sender is `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com`, a subdomain, so DMARC alignment is worth verifying separately.
- **Footer.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible because the source was truncated, so they could not be confirmed or refuted. The QA probe says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and no failure was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Receipt-scanning CTA.** It points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, a generic login/account page rather than a receipt-scanning feature page. The link text and destination don't align, and logged-out users hit a login wall.
- **App-download CTA.** The destination carries `utm_medium=email`, `pid=Email`, and `deep_link_value=…app://home`. The `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` values are cut off in the QA output, so completeness is unverified. The `pid=Email` and `deep_link_value` params suggest AppsFlyer attribution, which looks correct.
- **Product CTAs.** They carry only `?Size=…` in the visible destination. UTMs may be appended by the redirector, but this couldn't be confirmed because the probes failed.
- **Variant param.** `Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` is a plain variant pre-selection, so confirm the value matches the current Shopify option labels (for example `3.7 oz / 110 mL` has mixed-case `mL`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain alternative part.** This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Re-probe the four failing links** with relative-`Location` resolution. If they still fail, check the redirect configuration in the tracker.
3. **Confirm the headers at the source** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) from the raw message rather than the AgentMail relay copy.
4. **Fix the empty `<title>`.** Set it to the subject or campaign name.
5. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel** and a descriptive alt to the hero image.
6. **Repoint the "Receipt scanning" CTA** to the feature or rewards landing page, not `/account/login`.
7. **Verify UTM completeness** (source/medium/campaign) on the final landing URLs, and run the QA over the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer and the skipped links.
