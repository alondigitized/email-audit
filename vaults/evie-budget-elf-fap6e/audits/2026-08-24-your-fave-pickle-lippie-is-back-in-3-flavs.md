# Your fave pickle lippie is BACK in 3 flavs! 👀🥒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same pickle-lip-balm collection e.l.f. teased on 2026-08-20 ("This NEW drop is not a drill... it's the real e.l.f.ing dill 👀🥒"), now reframed as a "BACK in 3 flavs" restock/return email. The hero is clean and on-brand — model biting into a lip balm, "Speak for a Limited Time!" badge, "Totally Dill-Lulu" headline, $9 price, one clear CTA — but it's immediately followed by the same grab-bag "weekly highlight reel" structure the brand has leaned on repeatedly (unnamed "New Obsessions" grid, unnamed "Hot as e.l.f." grid, recycled Briny Blender sponge tie-in, recycled Bronzing Drops GWP banner).
- The subject line is genuinely strong for this persona — "back," "3 flavs," and the pickle emoji create real curiosity and continuity with a product I may have missed the first time. But the body undercuts it: the pickle collection gets one hero block and then the email pivots into the same undifferentiated roundup format seen on 2026-08-18, complete with the identical Bronzing Drops banner from 2026-08-13/2026-08-19. This reads as reused inventory dressed up with a fresh subject line, not a dedicated restock send.
- Verdict: strong hook, thin follow-through — good enough to open, mediocre once inside.

## What's working

- Hero product shot is sharp, on-model, and legible with no render bugs.
- "Speak for a Limited Time!" badge plus "NEW!" and "Only $9" create immediate urgency and a concrete offer.
- Single unambiguous CTA ("Get Pickled") sits directly under the offer copy with clear visual hierarchy.
- Briny Blender cross-sell reuses the same pickle theme coherently ($6, "dill-ightfully bouncy sponge") rather than feeling bolted on.

## What's weak

- The "New Obsessions" and "Hot as e.l.f. This Week" grids show six + four products with zero names, prices, or benefit copy — pure filler, same pattern flagged on 2026-08-18.
- Bronzing Drops GWP banner is the identical creative/copy/threshold ($50+, shade Pure Gold) from 2026-08-13 and 2026-08-19 — third appearance, now visibly stale.
- Subject promises a focused "pickle lippie is back" story but the body only gives it one hero block before pivoting to an unrelated generic roundup.
- No social proof, review stars, or shade/flavor callouts for the 3 flavors themselves — "3 flavs" is promised in the subject but never named in the visible hero copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the 3 flavors in the hero block (e.g., "Sour Apple, Spicy Pickle, Original Dill") — the subject sets up curiosity the body never resolves, which is the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Cut or replace the recycled Bronzing Drops banner — third identical send in under two weeks is cadence fatigue, not urgency.
- 3. Add product names + prices to the "New Obsessions" / "Hot as e.l.f." grids, or drop those sections entirely for a restock-focused send — right now they dilute the pickle story the subject promised.
- 4. Move Briny Blender up next to the hero (both are pickle-themed) and push the generic roundup below it, so the whole top half stays on-theme.

# Technical Audit — e.l.f. Cosmetics "Pickle Lippie" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 23% pass rate (7 failures, 3 warnings), driven primarily by broken product/account links, a missing plain-text MIME part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 broken destination links (FAIL)** — all routed through the `links.elfcosmetics.com` (Braze) click-tracking redirector, but resolving to **relative paths with no host**, which QA flags as "unknown url type":
  - `/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm?Color=new+shade%21+The+Real+Dill+-+Green` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/instant-lift-waterproof-brow-pencil?Color=Grey` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning" link)
  - `/collections/best-sellers?lid=...&utm_source=broadcast...` ("SHOP BEST SELLERS")
  - `/collections/whats-new?lid=...&utm_source=broadcast...` ("SHOP NEW ARRIVALS")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=...&lid=...` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
  - This is a Braze link-template configuration error — the destination URL was saved as a relative path instead of an absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URL, so the redirector cannot resolve where to send the click.
- **Coverage caveat**: QA probed 12 of 46 material links (34 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); remaining links (including footer/social/unsubscribe) were not verified and could carry the same relative-path defect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Product-specific links (`/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm`, `/products/instant-lift-waterproof-brow-pencil`) and the account/app links show **no UTM parameters** in the destination path, only `Color=`/`return_to=` query args — inconsistent with the campaign-tagged collection links, and continuity can't be verified regardless since all six are currently broken (Section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Braze link templates for the 6 failing destinations — set absolute URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) instead of relative paths.
2. Add `alt` text to the two flagged images.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers directly on raw sent headers (relay may be dropping them, not the sender).
4. Populate the plain-text MIME part — currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring independent of any CAN-SPAM requirement.
5. Standardize UTM parameters across all CTA links, including product and account links, to preserve email-to-site attribution.
6. Expand link probing beyond the 12 material links to cover footer/unsubscribe links, since compliance-critical links were deprioritized in this pass.
