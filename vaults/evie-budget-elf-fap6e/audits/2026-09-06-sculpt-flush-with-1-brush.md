# Sculpt & flush with 1 brush 👩‍🎨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s launch send for the Cream Blush & Bronzer Duo Brush ($9), and it's a genuinely well-built hero-cascade email — model shot with visible product application, one clean price hook ("$9"), and two logical bundle-adjacent products (Soft Glam Cream Blush $7, Soft Glam Cream Bronzer $7) that pair with the tool being sold. The "Pairs Perfectly With" lip-product banner is a smart bridge between hero and cross-sell, and the layout keeps a single CTA style ("SHOP NOW" white pill) consistent throughout, which reads clean on mobile.
- Where it loses points against the brand's recent best (2026-08-30's Hypochlorous Mist at 8/10, 2026-08-28's Halo Glow Skin Tint at 7/10) is depth: no clinical/consumer-study hook, no award badge, no swatch-on-skin comparison — just a straightforward "new tool + new formulas" stack. The Beauty Squad $5 Reward banner is a nice loyalty touch but is visually disconnected (different color block, different type treatment) from the rest of the email, and the long stack of empty category-nav bars ("NEW," "EYES," "LIPS," etc.) at the bottom adds dead scroll weight. Solid, on-brand, sellable — but not a standout.

## What's working

- Hero image clearly demonstrates the brush's use case (blush application on a model's cheek) — product benefit is visible, not just described.
- Price is stated immediately in the hero copy ("$9") — no hunting required.
- Cross-sell logic is sound: brush → lip products → cream blush → cream bronzer, all thematically tied to "sculpt & flush."
- Consistent CTA button style and copy ("SHOP NOW") throughout — no ambiguity about what to click.
- Beauty Squad $5 Reward banner adds a loyalty incentive layered on top of the product push.

## What's weak

- The Beauty Squad reward banner's purple/blue gradient and confetti badge clash visually with the soft pink palette used everywhere else — it reads like a bolted-on module, not part of the same email.
- Bottom third of the email is mostly empty gray category-nav bars (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) with no imagery or copy — pure scroll filler.
- "Top Picks for You" section feels generic/algorithmic (Power Grip Primer, Setting Spray, Lip Liner) and dilutes the single-hero focus the top of the email established.
- No social proof, star ratings, or award badge anywhere — unlike the brand's stronger recent sends (Halo Glow Skin Tint's Allure badge, the Hypochlorous Mist's stat callout).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or visually reskin the Beauty Squad reward banner to match the pink palette — right now it looks like a different email got spliced in.
- 2. Trim the six empty category-nav bars at the bottom to a single horizontal nav row — reclaims significant scroll real estate.
- 3. Add one concrete proof point to the hero (e.g., "94% said blends like a $30 brush" or a shade/finish comparison) — the brand has used stat hooks well before (Hypochlorous Mist send) and it's missing here.
- 4. Move or trim "Top Picks for You" — it competes with the duo-brush hero rather than reinforcing it.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
Email renders via a redirect-tracking layer (links.elfcosmetics.com) but the underlying merge/personalization tokens were not resolved before send, breaking the majority of probed CTAs; plain-text fallback and unsubscribe headers are also absent.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 6 of 12 probed material links fail with "unknown url type," all resolving through Branch/Iterable-style click tracker `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` to raw, unrendered destination paths — e.g. `/products/cream-blush-bronzer-duo-brush?lid=1dqg42ag2i51&utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=090626_sg_brush_pairing` (missing base domain — should resolve to `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/products/...`).
- Same defect on: `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`, `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`, `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, second `cream-blush-bronzer-duo-brush` CTA, and `/pages/mobile-app?...&af_dp=com.elfcos` (deep-link param also truncated — `af_dp` value cut off at `com.elfcos`, likely should be a full app scheme/bundle ID).
- Pattern indicates the destination host was stripped or never templated into the tracking redirect at send time — this affects primary product CTAs, the account/rewards link, and the app deep link, i.e. most of the email's conversion paths.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- One image lacks alt text: `src="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/RxEzzsRvJLDSPwrF2wRlcg~~/.../62yKlF__kLuBpsYjms4llE6UDIfo98rUFK0LfMwVU8kt2CQZCNxJp"` — no `alt` attribute present.
- Hero image block does include `alt="e.l.f." title="e.l.f."` — no issue there.
- MSO/Outlook conditionals, VML namespace, and mobile `@media (max-width:660px)` stacking rules are present and structured correctly; no markup issues found in the visible source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No literal unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`) found in the visible source, but the broken link paths above (raw relative URLs embedded inside the click-tracker) strongly suggest a template/merge failure in link construction — the destination-URL variable appears to have not been substituted with a full absolute URL before the tracking wrapper was generated.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found — may be a relay capture limitation, but if genuinely absent this is a CAN-SPAM/Gmail-Yahoo bulk sender requirement gap.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click): not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status unverifiable from this capture.
- No unsubscribe link content was included in the truncated HTML source provided, so footer compliance text/mechanism can't be independently verified from source; QA notes an unsubscribe link is always included in probing scope.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params)**
- Where UTM parameters are present, they're consistent: `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=090626_sg_brush_pairing` on both brush-product CTAs and the app-download link — campaign naming is coherent across links.
- However, since the underlying destination paths are broken (see §2), UTM-tagged traffic cannot currently reach the intended landing pages at all — this is a continuity failure, not just a tracking cosmetic issue.
- `power-grip-primer` and `cream-glide-lip-liner` links carry variant params (`Size=`, `Color=`) but no UTM tagging — inconsistent with the brush CTAs' tagging pattern.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the link-templating step feeding the click-tracker so destination URLs are absolute (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) before wrapping — this is the highest-priority fix, as it currently breaks 6 of 12 primary CTAs including product, account, and app-download links.
2. Verify and complete the `af_dp` deep-link parameter for the mobile app CTA; it appears truncated (`com.elfcos`).
3. Add `alt` text to the image asset served from `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/RxEzzsRvJLDSPwrF2wRlcg~~/...`.
4. Add a plain-text MIME alternative (current version is 0 chars) to support text-only clients and improve deliverability/spam scoring.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the originating ESP (not just dropped by the relay) and validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly against ESP send logs.
6. Standardize UTM tagging across all product CTAs, including the variant-parameterized links (`power-grip-primer`, `cream-glide-lip-liner`), for consistent attribution.
