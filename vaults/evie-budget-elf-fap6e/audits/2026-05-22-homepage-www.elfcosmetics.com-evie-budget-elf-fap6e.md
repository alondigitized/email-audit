# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I opened this and immediately felt like e.l.f. *knows* me — "jelly pop glow stick on us" is literally TikTok-core language and the Memorial Day freebie-with-purchase is the exact kind of deal I screenshot and send to my group chat. This is a budget beauty brand doing budget beauty correctly: loud offer, clean hero, loyalty pitch right at the top. It's not reinventing itself, it's just staying in its lane and executing.

## What's working

- **The "jelly pop glow stick on us" headline** — "glow stick" in the BeautyTok sense hits immediately. I know what this product is, I've seen it on my FYP, and the word "us" makes it feel like they're giving it to me personally.
- **$8 VALUE! badge on the product** — price-anchoring the freebie is smart. It makes me feel like I'm getting something real, not a sample I'll throw away.
- **"Ends 5/25. Use code GLOW."** — both the deadline and the code are right there in the hero copy. No hunting, no fine print hunt.
- **Beauty Squad free-shipping pill above the fold** — I see the loyalty program before I even hit the hero. Low friction to understand what I'm signing up for.
- **Top banner "Subscribe & Save! Unlock free shipping, save and get a free gift on us!"** — stacks an additional hook before the hero even loads.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — for a deal this specific, the button should say "Get My Free Glow Stick" or "Claim GLOW." "SHOP NOW" could be any website.
- **No model or lifestyle imagery** — it's a product close-up on a dark background. I can't see what this looks like on a real person, which matters for a glow product.
- **Beauty Squad pitch is acquisition-only** — "1st order" framing means if I'm already a member, that pill says nothing useful to me. No "check your points" or rewards balance.
- **Right-panel content is cut off** — there's clearly a carousel slide bleeding in from the right (pink background, partial text). It looks like the hero didn't load fully, even if it's technically a slider.
- **No reviews or ratings on the featured product** — e.l.f. has a ton of 5-star reviews. Showing even a star count on the glow stick image would make me more likely to tap.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Claim My Free Glow Stick"** — specificity converts. It reinforces the offer at the exact moment I'm deciding to tap.
- **Add a lifestyle inset or UGC thumbnail in the hero** — even a small TikTok-style swatch image showing the glow effect on a real face would close the "does this actually look good" gap without redesigning the hero.
- **Add a "X,XXX ⭐ reviews" badge on the product image** — right next to the $8 VALUE badge. e.l.f. earns this, they should flex it.
- **Personalize the Beauty Squad pill for returning members** — "Welcome back! You have X points" for logged-in users vs. "Get Free Shipping" for new visitors. Literally two states, big impact for retention.
