# Get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick on us! 💋
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f.'s Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick BOGO — buy one, get one free with code LIPS2, gated as an "app exclusive, 6 hours only" flash offer. The hero does its job: a bold magenta banner with oversized type puts the offer front and center, followed immediately by a ten-shade swatch strip that turns an abstract "lip oil stick" into something concrete and shoppable.
- Where it stumbles is the second half of the email. Right after the BOGO CTA, it stacks in a second, unrelated gift-with-purchase — the National Lipstick Day Cream Glide Lip Liner offer (code LIPPIE) — which was already the entire subject line of the July 27 send ("Don't miss a National Lipstick Day gift on us! 💄", 9.00/10). Bolting a second, previously-standalone campaign onto this one dilutes the single clear ask this email opened with, and readers who already redeemed LIPPIE three days ago get served it again. The "Top Picks for You" block compounds this by surfacing primer, moisturizer, and setting spray — none of them lips — in an email whose entire premise is a lip product giveaway.
- Net: strong hero mechanics (offer clarity, shade variety, clean render) undercut by a cluttered, unfocused body that dilutes a genuinely good single offer. Solid but not top-tier for this brand.

## What's working

- Hero banner offer is unmissable — large type, high-contrast magenta block, one clear CTA directly beneath the copy.
- The ten-shade swatch strip (pink-me energy, trufflemaker, cherry on top, etc.) gives the product real texture and choice without needing a product photo grid.
- "Limited time only!" badge on the hero image and "app exclusive – 6 hours only!" banner both create credible, specific urgency.
- Clean, consistent e.l.f. black/pink/white branding throughout with no render bugs — swatches, buttons, and footer icons all display correctly.
- Footer clearly reinforces cruelty-free/vegan positioning and functional perks (free shipping, receipt scanning, Beauty Squad app).

## What's weak

- Bundling the LIPPIE (National Lipstick Day) GWP offer into this email recycles a promo that already had its own dedicated send on July 27 — it reads as leftover inventory, not a fresh beat.
- "Top Picks for You" recommends a primer, face cream, and setting spray — zero lip products — in an email built entirely around a lip oil giveaway; it breaks the category focus right when the reader is primed to buy lips.
- Two competing offers (LIPS2 BOGO vs. LIPPIE gift) with two different codes force the reader to figure out which promo applies to their cart, adding decision friction.
- "SHOP NOW" (used three times) is generic — it doesn't tell the reader what they're shopping for at each tap.
- Dense fine print/exclusion language at the bottom ("cannot be combined," "while supplies last," minimum-purchase caveats) sits below an otherwise very simple offer, which cuts against the "honest, no buried fine print" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the LIPPIE/National Lipstick Day block entirely — it's already been the hero of a separate, well-performing send. Let this email be a single-minded BOGO push; a cleaner ask converts better than two competing codes.
- 2. Replace "Top Picks for You" with three lip products (liner, gloss, or another lip oil shade) so the whole email stays in-category — right now it actively distracts from the ask.
- 3. Swap generic "SHOP NOW" buttons for offer-specific copy, e.g. "Claim My Free Stick" or "Shop the BOGO."
- 4. Pull the "6 hours only" urgency line up into the subject or preheader — it's a strong, credible deadline that's currently buried in the hero image instead of driving the open.
- 5. Move the exclusion/fine-print language into a single condensed line rather than a full paragraph block, so the offer reads clean through to the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO alert: Glow Reviver Lip Oil, free for 6 hours ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lip oil stick is on us (app only, today)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 1, get 1 free — 10 shades, code LIPS2, gone in 6 hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `App-exclusive BOGO on Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick ends tonight.`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics — "Get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick on us!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based MSO-compatible layout with Braze-hosted images, but 7 of 12 probed CTA/link destinations resolve to malformed relative-path URLs, and the message lacks both a plain-text MIME part and RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
7 of 12 probed links fail with "unknown url type" — the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect wrapper is resolving to a **relative path with no scheme/host**, not a valid absolute URL. Affected CTAs:
- "get a glow reviver lip oil stick on us!" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&lid=ql1snl3l2otv&shortlink=yvg5pc9f&...`
- "Shop now" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fproducts%2Fdetail%2F8684572016728&utm_source=broadcast&...`
- "shop now" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?utm_source=broadcast&utm_campaign=072926_buy1bgslipstick_get1onus&af_xp=custom&...`
- "Shop Now" (power-grip primer) → resolves to `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
- "Shop Now" (holy hydration cream) → resolves to `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g`
- "Receipt scanning..." → resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`

This is a redirect-chain bug, not a QA-tool artifact: the final hop is missing the `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` (or AppsFlyer OneLink) domain, so any client resolving this the same way will hit a broken/relative URL.
