# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I open e.l.f. and the first thing that slaps me in the face is a full-screen cookie consent modal — like, I can barely even see the homepage behind it. What I *can* see looks cute though: warm, glowy tones and a "SOFT GLAM CREAM BLUSH" drop that is very much speaking my language. The announcement bar is doing the heavy lifting here with a National Best Friends Day promo and a $9 entry price, which is peak e.l.f. behavior — but none of that matters if I'm stuck staring at a legal wall.

## What's working

- **Announcement bar copy** — "Celebrate National Best Friends Day with e.l.f.conic duos, starting at $9" is so on-brand it's almost annoying how well it works. BFF gifting content at an e.l.f. price? That's literally a Target run I would make.
- **"NEW! SOFT GLAM CREAM BLUSH" hero text** — what little I can see of the hero behind the modal is the right product for me. Soft glam is a trending aesthetic and a cream blush launch signals they're paying attention to the format wars (cream vs. powder is a whole TikTok discourse).
- **Warm, skin-tone aesthetic peeking through** — the nude/peach palette visible behind the modal matches the soft glam moment. It doesn't feel clinical or department-store-cold.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is a full-screen hostage situation** — on mobile this modal covers roughly 80% of the homepage. The hero image, main CTA, and any product content are completely invisible. This is the worst possible first impression.
- **No loyalty/rewards hook above fold** — e.l.f. has an e.l.f.UP! rewards program and I see zero trace of it. For a returning customer like me who stockpiles points, this is a missed re-engagement moment.
- **"Shop Now" in the announcement bar is doing double duty** — it's the only real CTA I can interact with, and it's tucked in a tiny banner above the modal. That's not a CTA hierarchy, that's a fire escape.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold** — e.l.f.'s free-ship floor is a known loyalty trigger for budget shoppers and it's nowhere to be found.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or shrink the cookie modal on mobile** — swap the full-page takeover for a slim bottom banner with a one-tap "Accept All" that doesn't bury the hero. Cookie compliance shouldn't cost you the first impression.
- **Move the "starting at $9" price into a larger, styled badge on the hero** — right now it's buried in a thin top banner in small text. A "DUOS FROM $9" callout on the hero image itself would land harder and faster.
- **Add an e.l.f.UP! rewards nudge in the announcement bar** — something like "e.l.f.UP! members get early access" would hook returning shoppers and drive signups in one line of copy.
- **Surface a "Shop Soft Glam Blush" CTA button** — even a partial button peeking below the modal fold would give my thumb somewhere to go the millisecond I dismiss the cookie wall.
