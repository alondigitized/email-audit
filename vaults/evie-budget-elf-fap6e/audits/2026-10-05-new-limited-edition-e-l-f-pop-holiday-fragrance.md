# NEW & Limited Edition! e.l.f. POP holiday fragrance ✈️❄️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong, single-story launch for the e.l.f. POP "Spicy Bundt Nice" holiday fragrance collection. The Tromsø/Norwegian-holiday theme runs from the hero through the product blocks and the Skaar music tie-in. The "transport your e.l.f. to Tromsø" hero, with the boarding-pass-style luggage tag, is a distinctive concept. The "come for / stay for / fall in love with" scent-note triptych makes a fragrance, which can't be smelled, easy to picture.
- The email runs long. After three well-built product blocks ($13 body cream, $13 fragrance mist, $12 travel kit), it adds a Skaar video block, a generic "Top Picks" row of Power Grip items, and a six-item category nav. Those pull attention away from the fragrance. The "Top Picks" row also looks like a leftover from the 2026-09-28 "weekly dose of add to cart" send, which scored 5/10 for the same category-dump problem. The top two-thirds is a 7.
- My judgement: a well-themed, on-brand fragrance send that loses a little focus in its back third. It fits a persona interested in new beauty launches, but not one who is specifically hunting for fragrance.

## What's working

- The Tromsø hero is a single, memorable concept, and the NEW! badge makes the launch obvious.
- The scent-note triptych (holiday spice cake, cardamom buttercream frosting, cedarwood and warm musk) gives the fragrance a taste-and-smell story.
- Each product has its own block with a price in the headline ($13, $13, $12), a one-line benefit, and a "SHOP NOW" button.
- The "NEW & LIMITED EDITION!" badges add credible scarcity to each product.
- The Skaar "PRESS PLAY" block adds a fun, shareable touch and fits the Norwegian theme.

## What's weak

- The "Top Picks for You" row (Power Grip Primer, Cream Glide Lip Liner, Power Grip Dewy Setting Spray) has nothing to do with the fragrance story and splits attention.
- The six-item category nav (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) takes up a lot of vertical space and leaves a long, empty-looking tail.
- There is no visible offer beyond product prices. No bundle, free-shipping threshold or member perk appears near the hero.
- Both the hero and the first product block say "SHOP E.L.F. POP" or "SHOP NOW" without naming the item.
- Two separate "SHOP E.L.F. POP" buttons in the first two sections send readers to the same place.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "Top Picks" row and shrink the nav. End the email on the Skaar block or a bundle CTA so the fragrance remains the only story.
- 2. Add a visible incentive near the hero, such as free shipping over a threshold, a gift-with-purchase or a "get all three for $X" bundle.
- 3. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "Shop Spicy Bundt Nice" and "Get the Travel Kit", in place of the generic "SHOP NOW".
- 4. Add a deadline or stock hint ("while supplies last") to back up the limited-edition claim.
- 5. Merge the two early "SHOP E.L.F. POP" buttons into one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Holiday spice cake, cardamom frosting + cedarwood musk. Body cream $13, mist $13.`
