# Homepage snapshot · www.elfcosmetics.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so e.l.f. is clearly still doing the "affordable but it girl" thing — lipstick fan flat lay, diverse models, a buy-2-get-1 skincare deal in the top banner. But the second I land on the page I get hit with a giant "score 15% off" popup AND a cookie banner stacked right on top of each other before I can even see what's on sale. It reads like a brand that knows its audience (me) but hasn't QA'd its own homepage in a minute.

## What's working

- **The banner deal:** "Buy 2 skin e.l.f.ssentials, get 1 on us! Use code REFRESH at checkout" — that's exactly the kind of stack-up-at-Target math I live for.
- **The lipstick flat lay:** rows of e.l.f. lippies in every shade is *chef's kiss* for someone who already knows this brand is the everyday workhorse next to my Glossier splurges.
- **"Beauty Squad" in the nav:** instant loyalty-program recognition without having to hunt for it.
- **"NEW DROP" badges** peeking through at the bottom of the product grid — tells me there's fresh stuff without me having to dig.

## What's weak

- **Two popups stacked on each other:** the "score 15% off" email modal and the cookie consent banner are both fighting for my thumb at the same time — I can't even read the hero copy behind them.
- **Hero text is basically unreadable:** all I can make out is a fragment like "...and ...e together" and a red "$10." — whatever that promo actually says, I can't tell.
- **No visible reviews, ratings, or "girl-tested" social proof** above the fold, which is wild for a brand that lives and dies by TikTok reviews.
- **Cookie banner has a weirdly small "English" language toggle** crammed above three big buttons — feels cluttered for something that should be a one-tap dismiss.

## Recommendations

- Kill the double-modal stack — pick ONE overlay (the email capture) and push cookie consent to a slim bottom bar that doesn't block the popup itself.
- Let me see the hero before you ask for my email — delay the "score 15% off" popup by a few seconds of scroll or a exit-intent trigger instead of slamming it on page load.
- Put a review-count or star rating badge on the hero product so I know it's TikTok-approved before I even scroll.
- Make "Beauty Squad" show a teaser of points/rewards right in the nav dropdown instead of just being a plain link.
