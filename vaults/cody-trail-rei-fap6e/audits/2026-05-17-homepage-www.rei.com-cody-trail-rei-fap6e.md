# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is REI doing exactly what REI should be doing — leading with the Anniversary Sale at the top of the year, right when I'm gearing up for summer trip planning. The hero is a direct hit: a dude in an orange puffy with trekking poles on a ridge, which is... basically my Saturday. The member 20% off banner is the first real content I see, and as a co-op member who tracks these promotions on the calendar, it's speaking my language immediately.

## What's working

- **Member savings banner** — "20% on a full-price item & one REI Outlet item" is exactly the kind of math I do as a co-op member. I know this sale. I've been waiting for it. Seeing it the moment the page loads keeps me from bouncing.
- **Trade-In tab in the nav** — it's right there in the top strip. REI re/Supply and Trade-In are part of how I shop (buy once, trade in, repeat) and surfacing it this high signals REI gets me.
- **Hero model and lifestyle shot** — not a product flat-lay, not a studio white-background sneaker. A real human on a real ridge in outerwear. That's REI's brand at its best and it's calibrated for my lifestyle.
- **Anniversary Sale framing** — "Our Biggest Sale of the year" is a claim I believe from REI. It's not discount-store noise; it carries credibility from years of member expectations.
- **Time bound is visible and credible** — "Now thru May 25" in the hero means I know the clock. Eight days. That's real urgency without feeling manufactured.

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button visible** — "Shop now!" is buried as a text link in the banner copy. The hero is gorgeous but there's no button driving me into the sale. Whatever CTA lives below the fold might as well not exist on a mobile first scroll.
- **Classes & Events nav is truncated** — I take REI co-op classes. That tab gets clipped to "Classes & Eve..." which is sloppy on mobile and makes me wonder what else is hidden.
- **Zero conversion trust signals** — no free-shipping threshold, no return policy reminder, no review counts visible. For a "Biggest Sale of the year" push, the homepage should be doing more conversion work from the first scroll.
- **REI Outlet mention in the banner is undersold** — I actively shop the Outlet for deals. Saying "& one REI Outlet item" is great, but there's no Outlet imagery or secondary visual hook to reinforce it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a clear hero CTA button** — "Shop the Anniversary Sale" as a white or outlined button overlaid on the hero, above the fold. The hero is doing all the creative lifting but there's no button asking me to move.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — "Free shipping on orders over $X" in the banner or below it. I'm a dividend-focused shopper who stacks orders to hit thresholds; just showing the number influences cart behavior immediately.
- **Fix the nav clip on mobile** — "Classes & Events" should either fit or the tab copy should be shortened to "Classes" — truncating it to "Classes & Eve..." reads as a bug, not a design choice.
- **Add a secondary Outlet-specific module** — swap one hero sub-unit for an Outlet strip with 2-3 product images and prices. The banner mentions Outlet but nothing visual follows through.
