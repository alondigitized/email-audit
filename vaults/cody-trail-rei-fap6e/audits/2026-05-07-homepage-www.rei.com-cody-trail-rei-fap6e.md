# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- REI's homepage is leading with a camp kitchen push — YETI, Zempire, OXO Outdoor cooking gear front and center — which honestly isn't a bad call for early May when I'm already mapping out summer trips. The co-op DNA is right there in the nav (Trade-In, Classes, Impact Report banner), which immediately signals this is my brand. But there's zero member pricing or dividend language above the fold, and no concrete offer to act on right now.

## What's working

- **"Trade-In" in the top nav** — it's literally the first utility link I see after "Shop REI." That's co-op culture made visible. I notice it immediately.
- **Impact Report banner** — "See how we made our mark on our planet, communities & lives outside" is exactly the language that separates REI from a generic gear retailer. Hits the co-op member identity hard.
- **Camp kitchen hero timing** — YETI + Zempire + OXO Outdoor in one shot feels like a curated gear recommendation, not a random category dump. May is exactly when I'm upgrading my stove kit for summer weekenders.
- **"Cooking, covered." headline** — short, confident, no fluff. The subhead "Elevated camp meals start with better gear" is practical framing I respect.
- **Search bar placement** — right below the logo, large and labeled "Search for gear & clothing." I use search constantly; this placement is correct.

## What's weak

- **No member pricing or dividend callout** — I'm a co-op member. My patronage dividend is a real number and a real reason I buy here over Backcountry. None of that is above the fold. Huge miss.
- **No concrete offer** — "Shop camp kitchen" is a category browse, not a deal. I can't see a %, a dollar off, or even a free-shipping threshold. Nothing pulls my wallet out.
- **No people in the hero** — it's a flat lay of pots and a camp stove. Lifestyle imagery with someone actually cooking at a trailhead campsite would hit harder for me.
- **Partial second carousel panel** — I can see a pink shoe and cut-off text on the right edge. That half-panel is visual noise. It doesn't tell me anything useful and doesn't invite me to swipe.
- **Classes & Events is truncated** — "Classes & Eve..." gets cut off in the top nav. That's one of my favorite REI features (I've done two intro backpacking classes) and it's barely legible.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a member pricing badge or dividend reminder** in the hero or directly above the CTA — something like "Member prices inside" or "Earn toward your 2025 dividend" makes the co-op advantage visceral.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip** just below the search bar or above the hero — "$50 ships free, always" requires zero design budget and gives me a concrete reason to fill a cart.
- **Swap the flat-lay camp hero for a lifestyle shot** of someone actually cooking at a backcountry site — show the use case, not just the product lineup.
- **Fix the nav truncation on mobile** — either abbreviate "Classes & Events" to "Classes" or bump it to the hamburger menu so it reads cleanly without cutting off.
