# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- REI's homepage feels like home base — co-op branding front and center, a Made by REI Co-op sun-protection layer as the hero, and a Outlet sale strip in the top banner. The brand is leaning into summer adventure prep and past-season clearance simultaneously, which sends slightly mixed signals, but the core identity is unmistakably on-point for me.

## What's working

- **"MADE BY REI CO-OP" label on the hero product** — instant signal that this is exclusive gear, not white-label stuff. As a co-op member I take that seriously.
- **"Up to 50% off past-season styles" promo banner** — exactly how a dividend-maximizer like me thinks about REI Outlet; I'd tap that immediately to see what's in my size.
- **Sahara Shade Hoodie product story** — "adventure-grade sun protection, only at REI" is tight functional copy. No lifestyle fluff, just the value prop.
- **Trade-In link in the top nav** — I've used Trade-In before and the fact that it's surfaced top-level, not buried, tells me REI still values that program. Good trust signal.
- **Clean mobile layout** — search bar where I expect it, hamburger for deep nav, cart always accessible. Zero friction to get to the next step.

## What's weak

- **No member/dividend signal above the fold** — I'm a co-op member. My dividend balance, a "Member pricing" badge on the hero, or even a "Members save an extra X%" strip would make me feel seen. Right now the page reads the same whether I'm signed in or not.
- **Hero is a flat lay on rock, not a person wearing it** — I can't gauge fit, coverage, or whether this is a boxy or trim cut. For a sun-protection layer that's a meaningful gap.
- **50% off is for outlet/past-season, but the hero showcases new co-op gear** — the page is trying to do two things at once (sell current season, clear old stock) and those audiences have different intent. The mismatch is subtle but real.
- **Partially visible second carousel card** — the "Peanut... Project" collaboration card is cropped off-screen on the right. It creates curiosity but also mild layout jank on mobile.
- **No price or member price shown on the hero product** — as someone who compares co-op price vs. Backcountry before buying, I want to see a number before tapping through.

## Recommendations

- **Add a logged-in member strip below the promo banner** — "Your 2026 dividend: $XX | Expires [date]" with a Shop Now link. Takes one data point and turns a generic visit into a personalized reason to spend.
- **Swap the flat-lay hero for a lifestyle shot of someone wearing the Sahara Shade on trail** — even a mid-hike shot would immediately communicate fit and use-case, especially for mobile shoppers who can't zoom and inspect fabric.
- **Surface member pricing on the hero CTA** — even a small "Co-op Members save 10%" badge next to the button would close the loop for dividend-focused members and separate the co-op buy from browsing Backcountry.
- **Anchor the outlet banner copy to the co-op identity** — change "from your favorite brands" to "co-op brands + past-season exclusives" to keep the outlet strip feeling like an REI-specific benefit, not a generic flash sale.
