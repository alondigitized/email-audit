# Starts now: REI Outlet coupon 👉⏳ (Ends 9/21)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet's coupon-code play — a flat "SAVE $30 WHEN YOU SPEND $150 OR MORE" spend-threshold offer, distinct from the percentage-off mega-sale template that's dominated the brand's sends all Labor Day cycle (2026-08-30, 2026-09-01, 2026-09-05). The hero is clean and legible — huge "$30" dollar sign, clear threshold, visible code (OUTSEPT26), clear deadline (thru 9/21) — genuinely the strongest single visual moment in this brand's recent history.
- Below the hero it falls apart into a generic category grid (women's, men's, hike, running) with no discounts, no urgency, no product specificity — just navigation dressed up as content. That's a lot of scroll for zero incremental offer value. Compared to the 2026-09-05 "coupon expires SOON!" send (20% off one item, member-gated), this is a broader, less exclusive, more mass-market offer — no membership requirement visible, which is honest but also less differentiated for anyone who already is a member.
- Net: strong hero, weak body, solid subject urgency. A member skimming their inbox during the Labor Day/fall gear cycle would likely open, but nothing below the fold gives them a reason to click deeper than "Shop Now."

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmissable — oversized "$30" numeral with the threshold spelled out immediately beneath it.
- Deadline is stated twice (hero copy "thru 9/21" and fine print "Ends 9/21") — consistent, no ambiguity.
- Coupon code is displayed prominently and is copy-pasteable in appearance (OUTSEPT26).
- Primary CTA "SHOP NOW" is a clear white button against the teal hero, easy to spot.
- No render bugs — clean rendering, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- The category grid (Women's/Men's/Hike/Running) shows zero products with prices or additional offers — pure navigation, no incremental reason to click.
- Only one of four grid tiles has a visible garment (the dress); the men's/hike/running tiles pair a plain photo against a blank teal CTA block, which reads as template filler rather than curated merchandising.
- No mention of the $30 member bonus-card teaser at the very top ("Earn a $30 bonus card when you become a member...") being tied thematically to the $30-off hero — two different "$30" offers stacked with no connective copy, which is mildly confusing.
- No loyalty/member-specific benefit surfaced in the hero itself (co-op card mention is buried in the footer trust badges).
- No social proof, sizing/fit info, or product-specific detail anywhere in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the four generic category tiles with actual discounted hero products (e.g., a $150+ bundle idea — boots + pack) so the $30-off/$150-spend math is demonstrated, not just stated.
- 2. Resolve the two competing "$30" mentions (bonus card vs. dollar-off coupon) — either cut the top banner or clearly differentiate it visually from the hero.
- 3. Add a second CTA lower in the email tied to a specific category with pricing, not just "SHOP WOMEN'S CLOTHING" as a flat color block.
- 4. Surface the Co-op Mastercard 5% rewards benefit closer to the hero for members — right now it's buried in the footer trust-badge row.

- 9 `mlinks.rei.com` links (ESP click-tracking redirects) all failed with "read operation timed out," including the primary CTA "SHOP NOW," all category CTAs, the header/footer logo link, and the "Unsubscribe" link itself.
- 7 `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` deep-link URLs carry a doubled-domain merge error: the `af_r` param and `deep_link_value` param each re-embed a full `https://www.rei.com/...` URL with duplicated `cm_mmc` query strings appended twice in sequence (e.g. `.../f/scd-outlet?cm_mmc=...C-_-...-_-26_1_403_09964_TC_CTA1&deep_link_value=/f/scd-outlet?cm_mmc=...` — same `cm_mmc` value repeated as both the primary URL param and the `deep_link_value` param), consistent with a template merge/escaping defect in the AppsFlyer (`af_r`) deep-link generation.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text: two pixel/tracking images (`mi.rei.com/.../o.gif`, `mlinks.rei.com/e/eo`) plus 4 content images served via `sp.rei.com` open-tracking redirects.
