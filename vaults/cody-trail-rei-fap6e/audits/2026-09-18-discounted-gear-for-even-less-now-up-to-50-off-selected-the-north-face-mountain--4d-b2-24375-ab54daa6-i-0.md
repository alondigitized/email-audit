# Discounted gear for even less—now up to 50% off selected The North Face, Mountain Hardwear & more
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is REI stacking five different "up to 50% off" blocks into one scroll — Patagonia/North Face/Mountain Hardwear/REI Co-op, then On/Beyond Yoga/Nike, then a Garmin fenix 8 dollar-off, then an REI Outlet $30 coupon, then a member sweepstakes. Individually each block is clean and on-brand; together it reads like a catalog dump rather than a single offer worth opening for.
- The subject line at 96 characters is going to truncate hard on mobile, and it front-loads three brand names instead of the number that actually sells the click. Given the brand history — this is at least the sixth "up to X% off" or coupon send from REI since 9/1 — this lands as more of the same rather than a fresh reason to click.
- Visually it's competent: consistent red/white REI system, no broken images, clear buttons throughout. But "past-season styles" clearance framing combined with five separate discount mechanics (percentage off, dollar off, coupon code, sweepstakes) undercuts the "one honest offer" read a subscriber wants from a quick scan.

## What's working

- Hero block is unmissable — big red "UP TO 50% OFF" with a single clear "SHOP THE DEALS" button above the fold.
- Consistent branding and grid layout make each section easy to scan even though there are many.
- Member sweepstakes and the loyalty card recap at the bottom give REI Co-op members a reason to feel the email is "for them."
- Garmin fenix 8 and REI Outlet blocks carry actual specific numbers ($250, $30, code OUTSEPT26) rather than vague percentages.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Five stacked "up to 50% off" claims across different brand groupings dilute any single offer into noise — nothing reads as THE deal.
- The email is extremely long for what is essentially a "browse everything" digest, which mirrors the pattern flagged in the 9/1 "best deals" roundup send.
- No single hero product image — every block is a brand logo tile, so there's nothing tangible to click toward.
- No urgency in the subject or hero; "while supplies last" is generic filler, while the Garmin and Outlet deadlines (9/20, 9/21) are buried deep in the scroll.
- Subject line is too long (96 chars) and will truncate on most mobile inboxes before the "50% off" payoff even lands.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and lead with it exclusively — either the North Face/Mountain Hardwear clearance or the Garmin fenix 8 deal, not both plus three others in the same send. Move the rest to a "more deals" footer strip.
- 2. Shorten the subject to front-load the number and cut brand-name stuffing.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the subject/hero — "ends 9/21" is sitting in the Outlet block but never surfaces where it would drive urgency.
- 4. Replace at least one brand-logo tile with an actual product shot (a jacket, a boot) so there's something concrete to click toward.
- 5. Trim the email length — five offer blocks plus a sweepstakes plus a loyalty recap is too much; cut to 2-3 sections max.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails automated QA at a 6% pass rate: nearly every CTA/product link resolves through a mobile deep-link redirector that produces malformed, doubled-domain URLs, and standard email authentication/unsubscribe headers are missing.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed CTA links (SHOP THE DEALS, SHOP NOW ×4, SHOP ALL DEALS, SHOP THE DEAL, SHOP REI OUTLET, Garmin deal link, Unsubscribe) failed with "read operation timed out" against `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/...` — the ESP click-tracking redirector is unresponsive for every material link, including the unsubscribe link.
- 12 material links show a doubled-domain merge error: URLs of the form `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/...&deep_link_value=/...` embed the full destination as a query param on top of an AppsFlyer deep-link host, but the `deep_link_value` param is passed as a relative path with its own `?` inside another URL's query string, producing invalid/ambiguous nesting rather than proper URL-encoding. Example: `af_r=https://www.rei.com/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...` immediately followed by `&deep_link_value=/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...` — the second `?` inside `deep_link_value` breaks query-string parsing since it's not percent-encoded.
- This pattern repeats across 12 links (product category, product detail, and outlet CTAs), affecting `/f/scd-deals`, `/b/patagonia`, `/b/the-north-face`, `/b/mountain-hardwear`, `/b/rei-co-op`, `/c/fitness`, `/s/garmin-fenix-8-deals`, `/f/scd-outlet` destinations.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 images missing `alt` text, including two tracking/functional images (`o.gif` open-pixel, `eo` event pixel) and two content images referenced only by opaque asset IDs (`cBfFWckaATcDLPGh8jmPKMr23wxYzlPtWQmLjk5RAT4QfxvWP1`, `enI09fUGApGnED3IM5UTXOebu4QKJB6jebOUvtoQ-rxR_WTYjS`).
- 2 images served over plain `http://` from `sp.rei.com` (same asset IDs as above) — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook) and will render broken or stripped.
- HTML uses extensive MSO/Outlook conditional comments and mobile-responsive media queries (`@media (max-width:739px)`), consistent with standard MJML-generated output; no structural rendering errors detected in the visible markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible HTML source.
- The `deep_link_value` and `af_r` query parameters function as pass-through personalization/routing tokens for the AppsFlyer mobile redirector; see doubled-domain issue in Section 2 — this is a merge/templating defect, not a missing-token defect.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`) but timed out during automated probing — functional status unconfirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe (POST) not confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from available data.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking params (`cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260918_CLR_FW37_NeMa_z_z-_-...`) are consistently applied across CTAs and appear well-formed on their own.
- However, because these params are embedded inside the malformed doubled-domain `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` wrapper (Section 2), successful arrival at the intended `rei.com` landing pages (e.g. `/f/scd-deals`, `/b/patagonia`, `/s/garmin-fenix-8-deals`) is not confirmed — the redirector may fail to parse `deep_link_value` correctly, breaking attribution continuity between email click and landing page.
- Plain-text version is 70%+ URL characters (10,456/14,917 chars), consistent with the same long tracking/deep-link URLs bloating the text alternative.

7. Recommendations
- Fix URL-encoding of the `deep_link_value` (and nested `af_r`) parameters in the AppsFlyer `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` links so the embedded destination URL's own query string doesn't collide with the wrapper's query string (percent-encode the entire nested URL).
- Investigate the `mlinks.rei.com` click-tracking redirector for availability — every single probed link timed out, including unsubscribe, which is a deliverability/compliance risk regardless of the URL-encoding bug.
- Add `alt` text to all five flagged images, prioritizing the two functional/tracking pixels for consistency with accessibility tooling.
- Serve the two `sp.rei.com` images over HTTPS.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the ESP (this may be a monitoring/relay artifact rather than a true gap — recommend checking raw headers at the source MTA).
- Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against the sending MTA rather than relying on the relay-captured `Authentication-Results` header.
