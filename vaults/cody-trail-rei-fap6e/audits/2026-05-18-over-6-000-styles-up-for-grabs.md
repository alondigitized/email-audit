# Over 6,000+ styles up for grabs
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the third wave of REI's Anniversary Sale sequence — the mid-sale "you still have time" digest that goes out after launch euphoria fades. The body is strong: multiple tiered offers (25%, up to 50%, 20%), a clean visual grid of must-see deals, a member badge, and a lifestyle hero that earns the "big adventures" copy. REI clearly knows how to execute a sale email in the render.
- The problem is the front door. "Over 6,000+ styles up for grabs" is the weakest subject in the entire Anniversary Sale run. After a sharp "Anniversary Sale starts NOW 🎉" on May 15 and a punchy "You could be getting 20% off 1 REI Outlet item" on May 17, this one retreats to vague volume language with no discount hook and a grammatical redundancy ("Over" and "+" both mean "more than"). It reads like a clearance rack, not a celebration.
- This is a competent bulk email fighting against its own inbox position. Subscribers who've already seen three REI sends in five days need a reason to re-engage — and "6,000 styles" isn't it.

## What's working

- **Multi-tier offer structure is clear**: 25% Off camping gear, Up to 50% Deals, and 20% Off Co-op Collection are all distinctly blocked out — a subscriber can scan and find their category fast.
- **Member badge is prominent** at the top, reinforcing co-op identity during the sale's peak window.
- **"Must-see deals" product grid** shows specific category discounts (20%, Save $50) with real product imagery — this is the most scannable section in the email.
- **Visual hierarchy holds**: hero → category offer → product grid → broader sale banner flows logically without crowding.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout gaps visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject line wastes the slot**: "Over 6,000+ styles up for grabs" is volume-only with no discount hook, no urgency, and a redundant "over X+" construction. The May 15 and May 17 subjects were sharper.
- **"Up to 50% DEALS" banner is bait-y**: The orange block dominates visually but "up to" is the weakest discount framing — it promises nothing specific. The 25% and 20% sections are more honest.
- **CTA copy is generic throughout**: Every button reads "SHOP NOW" or equivalent — no category specificity (e.g., "Shop Camping Gear" vs. "Shop Co-op Picks").
- **No visible deadline or countdown**: After the May 13 teaser email used a countdown timer effectively, this email has no urgency mechanism. Subscribers don't know how many days are left in the sale.
- **Cadence fatigue risk**: This is the 4th REI email in 5 days. Nothing in this send signals "last chance" or differentiates it from the May 15 launch send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline to the subject and hero**: The Anniversary Sale must have an end date — show it. "3 days left: 25% off camping gear + more" would outperform the volume claim on every metric.
- 2. **Replace "Up to 50% DEALS" with the single best deal**: Pick the deepest specific discount and lead with it — "Packs, tents, and bikes: up to 50% off select styles" is more honest and more clickable.
- 3. **Make CTA copy category-specific**: "Shop Camping Deals" or "See Bike Offers" beats "Shop Now" for click intent. The email already has the category structure — use it in the buttons.
- 4. **Add a "sale ends" line** in the hero subhead: One short line ("Sale ends May 21 — members save more") would restore the urgency the countdown timer provided on May 13.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 days left: 25% off camping gear + more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off — Anniversary Sale ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tents, packs, bikes, and Co-op picks — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save more. Shop before the sale ends.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `lang="und"` on `<html>` tag:**
```html
<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>
```
`"und"` (undetermined) is not a valid BCP 47 language tag for an English-language email. This will cause screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) to use incorrect pronunciation rules and may suppress language-based rendering in some clients. Should be `lang="en"` or `lang="en-US"`.

**Issue — Mobile scrollbar suppression applies to all content, not just a container:**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width:320px){
  body::-webkit-scrollbar{display:none!important}
}
```
This hides the native scroll indicator on all WebKit-based mobile clients (iOS Mail, Chrome/Android) at ≥320px — effectively every mobile device. If the email has horizontally overflowing content, recipients will have no visual affordance that it's scrollable. This is a usability risk, not just cosmetic.

**Note — `p,a{margin:0!important}` in 320px+ media query:**
```css
p,a{margin:0!important}
```
Overrides all paragraph and anchor margins globally at mobile widths. Legitimate for tight layout control but will collapse intended whitespace if any dynamic content (personalization blocks, live text) is inserted that relies on default paragraph spacing.
