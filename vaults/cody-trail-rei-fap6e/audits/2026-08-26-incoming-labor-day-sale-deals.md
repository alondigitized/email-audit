# Incoming: Labor Day Sale deals 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a real change of pace from the "SAVE UP TO 70%" mega-percentage sends REI Outlet has run all summer — this is a preview/teaser email, not a discount blast, and the countdown timer plus "Incoming" framing gives it a genuine reason to exist in the inbox. The catch: there's no actual offer here. "Discount based on original prices. Selection varies by location" is about as vague as sale copy gets, and the email spends most of its real estate on a gift card promo, a co-op membership pitch, and a YETI new-colors block that have nothing to do with Labor Day.
- The hero is clean and legible — big countdown clock, clear "PREVIEW THE DEALS" CTA, no render bugs — which is a real improvement over the cluttered, three-hero-block chaos of the 8/25 send. But by the time you scroll past gift cards, co-op membership, YETI, and a four-brand grid, the Labor Day thread is basically abandoned. This reads like REI stitched together whatever modules were sitting in the queue and called it a Labor Day teaser.
- Verdict: good format experiment, weak execution — it teases urgency it can't back up with specifics, and it doesn't stay on-message.

## What's working

- Countdown timer (1:14:42 to sale start) creates real, credible urgency without shouting.
- Hero is uncluttered — one headline, one image, one CTA, easy to scan on mobile.
- "PREVIEW THE DEALS" is a clear, specific, low-friction CTA for a teaser email.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.
- The QR-code sale poster graphic is a nice visual anchor that reinforces "Labor Day Sale."

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer — no %, no $ off, just "discount based on original prices." This is the vaguest teaser REI has sent in this whole tracked run.
- Email sprawls into unrelated modules (gift card bonus, co-op membership, YETI colors, brand grid) that dilute the Labor Day focus after the fold.
- "Selection varies by location. While supplies last" fine print undercuts the urgency the countdown just built.
- Preview/teaser emails work best short — this one is nearly as long as the full sale emails it's supposedly previewing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the hero + one supporting module. A teaser doesn't need gift cards, co-op membership, and YETI — those compete with and bury the Labor Day message.
- 2. Replace vague "discount based on original prices" with a concrete range if REI has one locked (even "up to 50%") — a teaser with no number isn't a teaser, it's an ad for an ad.
- 3. Move the countdown timer or a smaller version of it further down so it's still visible when readers scroll past the hero — right now urgency dies after paragraph one.
- 4. If the other modules must stay (revenue reasons), retitle sections so it's clear they're separate from Labor Day, e.g. "While you wait, here's what's new" — right now it reads like scope creep.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is an MJML-generated, table-based HTML build with standard Outlook/mobile media-query scaffolding; automated QA returned a 6% pass rate driven primarily by link-probe timeouts on the `mlinks.rei.com` redirector domain and missing alt text on multiple images, not by structural HTML defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed material links timed out** ("The read operation timed out") on `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/z/c/...` — including primary CTAs (`SHOP GIFT CARDS`, `SHOP NOW`, brand tiles, `SHOP ALL NEW ARRIVALS`) and the `Unsubscribe` link. This indicates the click-tracking redirector was unresponsive at probe time; cannot confirm final destinations resolve correctly.
- **13 AppsFlyer OneLink URLs flagged for "doubled-domain" pattern**, e.g.:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/...&deep_link_value=/...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=20260826_OFP_LDS_Pre_z_z_C&is_retargeting=true&af_reengagement_window=30d`
  This is the expected shape for AppsFlyer smart-link deep-link wrapping (`af_r`/`deep_link_value` legitimately embed the destination as a nested query value), so the "repeated domain" heuristic is likely a false positive rather than a broken merge token — but it should be manually spot-checked since two entries (`gift-card/purchase` and `full-price-yeti-products`) appear as **exact duplicates**, suggesting the same tracked link/asset was placed twice in the template.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
