# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-06-01
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- REI opens exactly how I want it to — clean co-op branding up top, a camping hero with NEMO and YETI badges that signal "we stock gear you'll actually research," and a membership banner that leads with a real dollar number. The "Camp, solved." headline is the right tone: practical, confident, not trying too hard. This is a brand that knows its customer, and it's pushing summer camp season hard right now.

## What's working

- **NEMO + YETI brand badges on the hero**: Those logos do a lot of credibility work fast. NEMO tents are a considered purchase; seeing them front-and-center says REI is stocking the real stuff, not just entry-level gear.
- **"Camp, solved." headline**: Utilitarian and confident. That's exactly the register I want from an outdoor retailer — solve the problem, get out of the way.
- **Membership banner placement and specificity**: "$30 single-use promo card — plus a lifetime of benefits" leads with money and follows with philosophy. The "thru 9/7" date is a real deadline that gives it urgency.
- **"Trade-In" in the top nav**: Small but huge for me. It's right there without scrolling. That's the re/Supply pathway surfaced at the entry point — exactly where it should be for someone who treats gear as an asset.
- **"Classes & Eve..." nav link (truncated)**: Even truncated, seeing REI Co-op classes in the nav immediately signals co-op culture. That's loyalty reinforcement before I've even looked at the hero.

## What's weak

- **Family camping imagery ≠ my use case**: The hero shows adults and kids around what looks like a picnic table and a car-camp-style NEMO tent. That's not a backpacking setup. I do weekend trail trips with a pack — this imagery is adjacent but not precise.
- **No existing-member acknowledgment**: The membership banner is pure acquisition copy. For someone who's been a member for years and tracks their dividend, there's nothing here that says "hey, you're already in — here's what's unlocked for you." A simple "Member Pricing" badge or "Your dividend: $X" would flip this.
- **$30 promo is new-member-only**: If I'm already a member, that offer doesn't apply to me. There's no visible promotion that rewards my existing loyalty — no member-only sale callout, no dividend drop date.
- **"Get from setup to downtime faster..." subhead is generic**: Fine copy, but it could be about any brand's camping section. It doesn't leverage the Co-op angle or anything that makes REI different from an Amazon camping category.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the family picnic table hero for a trail-side campsite shot**: Show a solo packer or a couple at a backcountry site with a backpacking tent pitched on a ridge. Keep NEMO in frame. Cody is the buyer — not the dad setting up car camp for the kids.
- **Add a returning-member ribbon or banner state**: "Member? Shop today's exclusive deals →" or surface a dividend-balance nudge for logged-in users. The join-now banner wastes real estate on someone who's already a Co-op member.
- **Surface a member-only sale or REI Outlet teaser above the fold**: "REI Outlet" is in the nav but not promoted in the hero area. A strip that says "Member pricing now live on summer packs — ends 9/7" would land harder than the new-member recruitment pitch for existing members.
- **Pull the Trade-In CTA into the hero or a banner strip**: Right now it lives in a text nav link. A "Got old gear? Trade it in for store credit" strip between the membership banner and the hero would immediately activate the re/Supply mindset for members like me.
