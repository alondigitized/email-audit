# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- REI's homepage is sending me straight into gear mode — camp kitchen collab with YETI and Zempire front and center, which tells me they know their audience is planning summer trips. The promo banner is loud and the top nav is packed with exactly the shortcuts I use (Trade-In, Classes, REI Outlet). It feels like a co-op, not a department store, and that matters to me.
- **8/10**

## What's working

- **Trade-In nav link** — visible in the top bar immediately, no hunting required. That's the first thing I check before buying anything new.
- **YETI x Zempire hero** — a collab featuring two brands I trust, tied to a real use case (camp cooking). Not vague "adventure awaits" copy — it's specific.
- **"Cooking, covered." headline** — short, confident, product-led. Speaks to someone who actually camps, not someone who just buys the aesthetic.
- **"Shop camp kitchen" CTA** — green pill button, easy tap target, category-specific rather than a generic "shop now."
- **REI Outlet in the top nav** — dividend shoppers live in the Outlet. Surfacing it above the fold is smart.
- **Promo banner placement** — "Up to 50% off past-season styles" is above the hero, impossible to miss.

## What's weak

- **Sign In not personalized** — I'm a member. The nav says "SIGN IN" but doesn't acknowledge my co-op status or remind me of my dividend. A cold visit and a member visit look identical, which is a missed hook.
- **Second hero panel is cropped** — the right side shows a pink shoe with text cut off ("The ne..."). A mobile carousel that shows half a card with no visual affordance to swipe is just confusion.
- **No loyalty/dividend callout above the fold** — the banner leads with a discount but says nothing about member pricing or the 10% dividend. That's the thing that differentiates REI from every other outdoor retailer.
- **Classes & Events nav is truncated** — "Classes & Eve..." gets cut off on mobile. That's a core REI differentiator and it looks broken.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member badge or dividend reminder below the logo** — even a one-line "Welcome back, Co-op Member — your dividend is building" would reinforce loyalty and separate the experience from a generic retailer.
- **Fix the half-visible right panel** — either give the carousel a visible swipe indicator/arrow or go full-width single card. Half a shoe and truncated copy is worse than no second panel.
- **Swap the promo banner copy to lead with member exclusivity** — "Members get up to 50% off past-season styles" converts better for co-op members than a generic discount line.
- **Expand the top nav label** — "Classes & Eve..." should be "Classes" on mobile to avoid truncation; losing that word kills the category clarity.
