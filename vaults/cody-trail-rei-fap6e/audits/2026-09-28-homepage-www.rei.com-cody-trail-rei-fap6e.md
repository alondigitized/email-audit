# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This reads exactly like the REI I know — co-op branding front and center, merino base layers for fall, and a card-rewards pitch instead of a generic "SALE" banner. Right now it's pushing membership sign-in, the $30 promo card for new members, and 2x rewards on the Co-op Mastercard, with new base layers and a Garmin GPS watch as the hero products. Feels honest to the brand, just a little cluttered by that sign-in popup elbowing its way into frame before I've scrolled an inch.

## What's working

- **"New base layers" tile** — merino wool, antimicrobial, wicking, quick-drying, tagged "New Design." That's textbook shoulder-season backpacking gear, and I'd genuinely click "Shop base layers."
- **2x Co-op Mastercard rewards banner** — as a dividend-driven member, a card that stacks rewards on top of my annual patronage dividend is exactly the kind of value stack that keeps me loyal instead of price-shopping REI vs. competitors.
- **Membership banner up top** — "$30 single-use promo card when you spend $50+" is a real, specific number, not vague "join and save" fluff.
- **Garmin GPS watch teaser** — "NEW ARRIVAL," premium GPS, peeking in from the right edge. That's a category I actually browse (trail tech), and it's smart to tease it rather than bury it in Electronics.

## What's weak

- **The sign-in modal fires immediately and covers the second hero tile** — I can't even read "Double rewards, Double ad—" because the popup is sitting on top of it. That's friction before I've done anything.
- **Three competing CTAs above the fold** (Shop base layers, Learn more, Shop now) plus two more in the modal (Create account, Sign in) — five buttons in one screen is a lot to parse before my eye lands anywhere.
- **No people in any of the imagery** — flat-lay clothing and an illustrated van, no one actually wearing the gear on trail. For a co-op that sells the outdoor lifestyle, a lifestyle shot would sell harder than a product flat-lay.
- **No visible free-shipping threshold or return policy** — as someone who buys gear once and expects to repair it twice, REI's famous return policy is a trust signal that should be doing work on this screen and isn't.

## Recommendations

- Delay or soft-dismiss the sign-in modal until after a scroll or a few seconds, so it doesn't stomp on the "Double rewards" banner underneath it.
- Swap the base-layer flat-lay for a quick lifestyle shot — someone layering up at a trailhead campsite — to match the "Double rewards" van illustration's outdoorsy energy.
- Surface REI's 1-year satisfaction guarantee or return policy as a small trust badge near the hero, not buried deeper in the page.
- Pick one hero CTA to visually dominate (bigger button, more whitespace) instead of three same-weight buttons across the carousel.
