# REI Outlet coupon expires SOON!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- REI Outlet is running a classic coupon-expiry urgency email — 20% off one full-price item, with a countdown timer anchoring the hero. The mechanic is proven and the creative execution is competent: countdown + coupon code + "SHOP NOW" is a clean conversion play. The problem is everything below the fold. What should be a focused, single-offer send turns into a five-section catalog scroll — 25% Off GORE gear, ASICS footwear, Altra footwear, and then a "Biggest Sale of the Year" banner at the bottom — each with their own CTAs. The urgency built in the hero evaporates the moment your eye drifts downward.
- The bigger issue: the "Biggest Sale of the Year" module at the bottom appears to reference the REI Anniversary Sale, which — per this subscriber's inbox — wrapped up yesterday (May 22). Sending a "Biggest Sale of the Year" block the day after that sale ended is either stale content or a confusing overlap between REI and REI Outlet promotional calendars. Either way, a real subscriber notices.
- This is REI Outlet's second countdown-timer send in three days (the May 21 Outlet finale scored a 10/10 here, largely because the entire email was laser-focused on one message). This one tries to do too much and pays the price in diluted urgency.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer in the hero** immediately communicates that time is running out — the strongest single signal in the email.
- **Coupon code visible in the render** (appears personalized or at least distinctive) — makes the offer feel concrete and actionable.
- **"Picks for your REI Outlet coupon" section** smartly bridges the coupon to product inspiration, which is a good merchandising move.
- **Brand presentation is clean** — REI Outlet logo, green CTA buttons, and REI OptOutside footer are all on-brand with no visible render bugs.

## What's weak

- **Five distinct sections, five different offers** — the email cannot decide whether it's a coupon expiry send or a catalog browse. The urgency built by the countdown is immediately undermined by the scrollable content below.
- **"Biggest Sale of the Year" block at the bottom appears to be stale or misaligned** — the REI Anniversary Sale ended May 22; this send lands May 23. Sending customers toward a sale that concluded yesterday is a real credibility hit.
- **"SHOP NOW" appears on every single section** — with no variation in CTA copy across five separate blocks, the email trains the eye to ignore buttons rather than respond to them.
- **The hero's 20% off is one full-price item** — not immediately clear from a quick read whether the discount applies broadly or has significant exclusions; the visual balance of the hero buries that context.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut to one CTA.** This email should have exactly two elements: the countdown + coupon hero, and one product grid tied directly to the coupon. Everything else (GORE 25%, ASICS, Altra, Biggest Sale) belongs in a separate send or a secondary link. The May 21 Outlet finale worked because it did one thing.
- 2. **Pull or replace the "Biggest Sale of the Year" module.** If the Anniversary Sale is over, this block needs to be swapped for in-season Outlet inventory. Sending stale sale content is worse than sending nothing.
- 3. **Make the coupon code the subject line's hero.** Subscribers who see a personalized or distinctive coupon code in the subject line open at higher rates.
- 4. **Vary the CTA copy.** First CTA: "Use ANNIE Now" or "Apply My 20% Off". Downstream CTAs: "Shop GORE Deals" / "Find My Size in ASICS". Generic "SHOP NOW" everywhere wastes the specificity opportunity.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off code expires today — use ANNIE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `ANNIE: 20% off expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One full-price item at 20% off. Countdown is live.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your coupon is burning — here's what to use it on.`
