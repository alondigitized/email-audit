# Turn these deals into gear before they disappear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- REI is sending their second email of May 22 — the guilt-trip "Anniversary Sale is winding down 😭" send already went out today (scored 8/10 here). This one pivots to a merchandised approach: personalized product picks, layered offer tiers (20% member coupon + 50% outlet + 30% on a specific product), and softer urgency copy. The structure is legitimate and the "Picked for you" module is a real differentiator. But after seven emails in eight days — two today — fatigue is compounding fast, and "before they disappear" lands nowhere near as hard as the May 21 countdown timer still ticking 14 hours ago.
- This email earns its spot as a fence-sitter converter. Subscribers who ignored the urgency send might respond to a curated product pitch. But REI is doing the thing that burns lists: sending two different creative angles on the same day to the same segment, hoping one sticks.
- Solid merchandising, too many emails, too little deadline discipline.

## What's working

- "Picked for you & coupon ready" personalized grid is the strongest conversion module — product-level relevance makes this feel curated, not blasted
- 20% member coupon is well-placed and explicitly reinforces the Co-op loyalty identity
- Multiple offer tiers (20%, 50%, 30%) give different entry points for different buyer intents and cart sizes
- "Free Standard Shipping" header banner removes a common checkout friction point before the subscriber even reads the offer
- Visual hierarchy holds — hero leads, offers layer cleanly below the fold

## What's weak

- "Deals, soon to slip away" is soft urgency with no anchor — no date, no timer, no countdown; the May 21 send had a literal 14:54:34 ticking in the body
- Second REI email in a single day compounds list fatigue even for engaged subscribers
- The 50% off block shows multiple competing brand logos that create visual noise and dilute a claim that should be a punch
- Personalized picks have no urgency signal alongside them — no "low stock," no "expires tonight," no stock count
- CTA copy is generic throughout: "SHOP THE SALE," "SHOP NOW," "SHOP REI OUTLET" — no specificity about what you're clicking into

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero.** "Sale ends Monday at midnight PT" is ten times stronger than "before they disappear." The May 21 timer email proved deadline specificity converts — even plain text beats vague language.
- 2. **Consolidate CTAs in the first half.** Four distinct actions (SHOP THE SALE, SHOP REI OUTLET, FIND A STORE, SHOP ONLINE) scatter intent. Pick one primary verb for this creative.
- 3. **Add scarcity copy to the personalized grid.** "3 left in your size" or "low stock" next to picked items would dramatically lift the module that's already doing the most work.
- 4. **Clean up the 50% off block to one hero brand.** Three logos competing for a single offer claim dilutes all three.
- 5. **Replace the subject** with something that anchors to the member benefit or a concrete deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your member picks + 20% off — sale ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 picks we saved for you (+ up to 50% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off ends soon — your curated gear is already waiting`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member coupon is loaded. Here's what to use it on.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issue — language attribute:**
```html
<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>
```
`lang="und"` means "undetermined." Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver) use this attribute to select pronunciation and text-to-speech language. For an English email this must be `lang="en"`. `dir="auto"` is acceptable but `dir="ltr"` is more explicit for a left-to-right English layout.

**Confirmed issue — scrollbar suppression:**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width:320px) {
  body::-webkit-scrollbar { display:none!important }
}
```
Hiding the scrollbar on the email body affects webmail clients rendered in a browser (Gmail web, Outlook.com). This can mask overflow content in preview panes and is non-standard in email contexts. Remove unless there is a specific render bug this was introduced to fix.

**Positive:** MSO OfficeDocumentSettings block is present and correct (`<o:AllowPNG/>`, `96 PixelsPerInch`). Responsive breakpoints at 480px and 768px are both present. `img` reset CSS (`border:0; height:auto; outline:0`) is correctly applied globally.

**Cannot assess:** Alt text on product images, color contrast ratios, button tap-target sizes — body is truncated.
