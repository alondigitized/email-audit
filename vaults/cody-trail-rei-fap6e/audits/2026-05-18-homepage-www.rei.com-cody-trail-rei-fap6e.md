# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is REI in full anniversary-sale mode, and they know exactly who I am. The first thing I see is a red banner telling me — specifically, as a member — to save 20% with a code, followed by a hero of a guy who looks like he just got off the same trail I was on last weekend. It reads as confident, co-op-proud, and seasonally sharp.

## What's working

- **Member-specific promo banner** — the red strip leading with "Members, save 20%" is the first thing I read. As a dividend-chaser, this is the hook I come for.
- **Hero model match** — 30s bearded guy, hooded puffer, trekking poles, mountain backdrop. I don't feel like I wandered into the wrong store.
- **Code + deadline combo** — ANNIV26 with "thru May 25" is exactly the kind of concrete, calendar-able offer that makes me add a tab to my phone.
- **Trade-In link in the top nav** — it's right in the nav strip above the logo. As someone who repairs twice and trades in when a jacket finally dies, having it surfaced at the very top feels intentional and on-point.
- **REI Co-op Members badge on the hero** — low-key reinforces that this sale is for insiders, not the public. Feels like a club, not a clearance rack.

## What's weak

- **No direct backpacking/outerwear category shortcut** — the nav shows "Shop REI | REI Outlet | Trade-In | Travel | Classes & Eve…" but there's no quick-tap to Backpacking, Hiking, or Outerwear above the fold. I have to go through the hamburger or "Shop REI" drop.
- **"Shop now!" is a text link, not a button** — on mobile, that's a small tap target buried in a text block. A distinct CTA button on the hero would be cleaner.
- **No free-shipping callout above fold** — REI typically does free shipping on orders over $50, but it's nowhere visible here. For a sale this big, surfacing that would remove a friction point.
- **No product imagery** — the hero is lifestyle-only. I can't see a jacket, a boot, or a tent that makes me want to tap through immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Add a split CTA below the hero headline** — two buttons: "Shop Member Deals" and "Explore Outerwear." The current single "Shop now!" text link doesn't give me a category shortcut.
- **Pin a free-shipping badge to the promo banner** — something like "Free shipping on orders $50+" next to the 20% offer. It's one line and closes a conversion gap.
- **Swap one hero frame with a gear close-up** — a lifestyle shot of a puffer, a boot, or a tent alongside the model gives me something to want, not just someone to identify with.
- **Surface the Garage Sale or re/Supply teaser** — even a small banner strip below the hero. "Looking for used gear? Check re/Supply →" would capture the Cody-specific behavior that REI Outlet alone doesn't satisfy.
