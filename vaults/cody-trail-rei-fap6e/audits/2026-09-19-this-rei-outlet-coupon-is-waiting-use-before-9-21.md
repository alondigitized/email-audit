# This REI Outlet coupon is waiting, use before 9/21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet running the exact same $30-off-$150 coupon it already sent on 2026-09-17 ("Starts now: REI Outlet coupon 👉⏳ (Ends 9/21)") — same code (OUTSEPT26), same threshold, same 9/21 deadline — just repackaged as a "last chance" reminder. The creative is clean and legible: a huge teal hero block states the offer with zero ambiguity, followed by a "personalized finds" grid and a sweepstakes module. Nothing is broken, but nothing is new either.
- The problem is cadence and honesty of urgency: a subscriber who opened the 9/17 email already has this coupon code. Sending an identical offer two days later with only the subject line changed reads as filler, not strategy — and worse, it may actually depress urgency, since receiving the "same" deal twice in one week signals it isn't scarce. The personalized product grid is the only genuinely fresh element in the send.
- Verdict: solid execution, weak strategy — this is a repeat-send that adds inbox clutter rather than incremental value.

## What's working

- Hero offer ($30 off $150, code OUTSEPT26) is unmissable — huge type, high contrast, above the fold.
- Clear single primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the offer with no scroll needed.
- "Your personalized REI Outlet finds" grid shows six real product photos (headlamp, Hoka shoe, boots, multi-tool, pants, carabiners) — feels tailored rather than generic.
- Fine print (code, expiration, terms) is visible directly under the CTA, not buried at the bottom.
- Marquee "LIMITED TIME OFFER" ticker text reinforces urgency without being obnoxious.

## What's weak

- This is a near-exact repeat of the 2026-09-17 send — same code, same $30/$150 threshold, same 9/21 deadline — with only the subject reworded as a "reminder." No new value added.
- No countdown timer this time, despite the brand having used one just two sends ago (9/5, 9/6) for similar deadline-driven offers — makes the "waiting" urgency feel softer than it could.
- The sweepstakes module ("Win an Epic Hiking Trip") is visually strong but competes with the coupon for attention and isn't tied to the $30 offer at all — feels bolted on.
- "Your personalized REI Outlet finds" products aren't linked to specific discounts — no price or % off shown on any of the six items, so it's unclear if they're on sale at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend the identical coupon two days after the first send — if it's genuinely the last chance before 9/21, say so explicitly and add a live countdown timer (REI has already proven this works, e.g. 9/6 and 9/3 sends) rather than reusing static "waiting" language.
- 2. Cut or move the sweepstakes block below "Shop All REI Outlet" — right now it dilutes focus on the actual deadline-driven offer.
- 3. Add prices or "%-off" badges to the personalized product grid so browsing converts into clicks instead of curiosity.
- 4. Tie the personalized grid to the $30/$150 threshold explicitly (e.g., "add 2 of these to hit $150").

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $30 coupon expires in 48 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code OUTSEPT26 — stock up on jackets, footwear & gear before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours: $30 off orders of $150+ at REI Outlet`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

The email uses REI's mLinks click-tracking redirector and an AppsFlyer deep-link wrapper; QA probing found link timeouts on nearly every material CTA and a URL-construction defect (repeated domain) in the app deep-link, alongside missing unsubscribe/authentication headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 12 material links probed via `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/z/c/...` — all 12 returned `[FAIL] The read operation timed out`, including the hero CTA, all "SHOP NOW" buttons, "SHOP ALL REI OUTLET", "Download our app", "Get details", and the "Unsubscribe" link (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?_t=...`). This indicates the redirector endpoint was unresponsive at probe time — cannot confirm final destinations resolve correctly.
- 4 instances of a doubled-domain merge error in the app deep-link:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/f/scd-outlet?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/f/scd-outlet?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...&is_retargeting=true&af_reengagement_window=30d`
  The `af_r` and `deep_link_value` params both embed the full target URL with its own querystring, but those nested querystrings are not URL-encoded — the `?` inside `af_r=https://www.rei.com/f/scd-outlet?cm_mmc=...` terminates the AppsFlyer param parsing early and the second `?deep_link_value=...` is read as a literal sibling param rather than encoded value. This is a template/merge encoding bug, not just a stylistic doubled-domain — it will likely break deep-link parsing on both iOS/Android AppsFlyer SDK resolution.
- Tracking pixels present: `mi.rei.com/p/cp/.../o.gif` (open tracking) and `mlinks.rei.com/e/eo` (engagement pixel) — both structurally fine, only flagged for missing `alt`.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Two images served over `http://` instead of `https://` (`sp.rei.com/q/QyrdoGFbQbRYNlWDJ0DuTA~~/...` and `sp.rei.com/q/A39H8nWFbMgU9oW3phHLow~~/...`), which mixed-content-blocking clients may fail to load.
- 4 images missing `alt` text: the two `sp.rei.com` images above, the `mlinks.rei.com/e/eo` pixel, and `mi.rei.com/p/cp/.../o.gif`. Tracking pixels are low-priority, but the two `sp.rei.com` content images should have descriptive `alt`.
- Heavy MJML-generated CSS with extensive Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and `.mj-column-per-*` breakpoint classes — standard responsive-table pattern, no structural issues observed in the truncated markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No visible unresolved merge tags (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) in the truncated source.
- The `af_r`/`deep_link_value` doubled-domain issue (Section 2) is effectively a merge-token encoding failure — the destination URL was concatenated into the tracking template without encoding, corrupting the parameter boundary.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- Unsubscribe link is present in-body (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?_t=...`) but QA could not confirm it resolves (timed out).
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found — one-click unsubscribe via mail client UI is unavailable, and this header is effectively required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules for high-volume senders.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): not found — even if `List-Unsubscribe` were added, one-click POST unsubscribe isn't supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from this relay.
- Note: header absences are flagged by QA as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding these headers rather than proof the sending infrastructure omits them — worth confirming against raw SMTP headers at the source before treating as a confirmed compliance gap.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Campaign tracking params are consistent across CTAs: `cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260919_OUT_XoX_Eng_z_Sep_C-_-...` and `c=20260919_OUT_XoX_Eng_z_Sep_C`, `pid=email_com_gm_it` — internally consistent naming convention.
- Landing target for the app-deep-link CTAs is `www.rei.com/f/scd-outlet`, consistent with the "REI Outlet" subject/offer.
- Cannot verify actual landing page load/alignment because the underlying `mlinks.rei.com` redirects timed out during probing — final resolved URL and page content are unconfirmed.

7. Recommendations

- Priority 1: Fix the AppsFlyer deep-link template — URL-encode the `af_r` and `deep_link_value` values (or their nested querystrings) so `cm_mmc` and other params don't prematurely terminate parsing. Affects at least 4 CTA links in this send.
- Priority 1: Re-probe all 12 timed-out `mlinks.rei.com` links from a clean network path to confirm whether the timeout is transient (relay/monitoring issue) or an actual outage on REI's redirect service — an outage would break every CTA and the unsubscribe link platform-wide.
- Priority 2: Confirm at the sending MTA whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM) are actually present but stripped by the AgentMail relay, versus genuinely absent — the latter is a deliverability risk under current Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
- Priority 3: Serve the two `sp.rei.com` images over `https://` and add descriptive `alt` text to non-tracking images.
