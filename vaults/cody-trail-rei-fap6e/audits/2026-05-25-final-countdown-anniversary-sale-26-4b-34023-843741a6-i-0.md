# Final countdown: Anniversary Sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- This is REI's "Final Countdown" closer on May 25 — at minimum the fifth urgency-flavored Anniversary Sale email in the past three days. The top half earns its keep: "Last chance for your best chance to save" is the best hero copy in this entire campaign wave, two 20% off coupon codes (ANN25 visible) give a tangible reason to open, and the "MEMBERS SAVE MORE" badge reinforces loyalty without a separate send. For a wrap-up email, the intent is right.
- Then the countdown hits: `0 : 00 : 00` — the exact same broken timer that appeared in the May 24 "Deals end tomorrow!!!" send scored here at 6/10. One broken timer is a rendering accident. Two in a row is a systems failure, and subscribers who saw both are now wondering whether the sale is actually over. The trust damage compounds because there's no copy backup explaining the deadline — just a zeroed clock floating in the hero.
- The bottom half of the email tries to be three emails at once — must-have deals, REI Outlet, women's clothing — which dilutes the "final chance" urgency into a merchandise browse. By the fifth "last chance" send, the subscriber who was going to buy has bought. This email needed to be shorter, sharper, and timer-free.

## What's working

- "Last chance for your best chance to save" is alliterative and confident — a clear upgrade over the panicked "You ALMOST missed these deals" and "Deals end tomorrow!!!" sends.
- Two distinct 20% off coupon codes (ANN25 visible) give the email specific utility — a real reason to open and act.
- "MEMBERS SAVE MORE" badge in the hero reinforces loyalty value without a dedicated loyalty send.
- Subject line drops the triple exclamations and emoji panic of the May 24 send — "Final countdown: Anniversary Sale" is cleaner and more credible.
- Must-have deals section shows concrete percentage-off figures (25%, 30%) with product imagery rather than vague "savings."

## What's weak

- Countdown timer reads `0 : 00 : 00` — the same render bug from the May 24 "Deals end tomorrow!!!" send; two consecutive broken timers signals a pipeline problem, not just a one-off glitch.
- Five-plus "this is almost over" emails in a week has completely exhausted the word "final" — by now it registers as noise.
- Three distinct deal sections (must-haves, outlet, women's clothing) fragment the email into a catalog browse rather than a closing urgency push.
- No personalization or product tie-back to what the subscriber has viewed or bought — same generic grid as every prior send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or fix the timer.** Two broken timers in two days means this widget cannot be trusted. Replace it with a hard deadline line in bold body copy: *"Sale ends tonight at midnight PT."* A working clock is a conversion driver; a zeroed clock is a conversion killer.
- 2. **Collapse the email to one deal section, not three.** Pick the single strongest category (must-haves or outlet), cut women's clothing, and add a "last chance, shop all deals" CTA at the end. Shorter = higher click-through at end-of-campaign.
- 3. **Add one social-proof line** in the hero — e.g., *"Over 2M members saved this week"* — to validate the urgency rather than assert it.
- 4. **Surface a specific dollar save** next to the 20% code ("Save $60 on a $300 jacket") so the coupon feels concrete.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your ANN25 code expires tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 20% off + must-have deals under $100`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code ANN25 at checkout — Members save even more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25–30% off gear, one day left — no timer required`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`lang="und"` on `<html>` element — confirmed defect.**
```html
<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>
```
`und` (undetermined) is not a valid BCP 47 language tag for an English-language email. Screen readers (NVDA, VoiceOver, JAWS) use `lang` to select the correct pronunciation engine. Should be `lang="en"` or `lang="en-US"`.

**Global scrollbar suppression — potential webmail regression.**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width:320px){
  body::-webkit-scrollbar{display:none!important}
```
Hiding the scrollbar on `body` at 320 px+ affects every webkit-rendered webmail context (Gmail Chrome, Apple Mail). Intended for an app shell, not an email.

**Global margin zero on `<p>` and `<a>` — may cause text compression.**
```css
p,a{margin:0!important}
```
Applied in the same 320 px+ block. Overrides browser defaults for all paragraph spacing across the entire email in mobile-width webmail clients.

**`<title>` is bare "REI".** Acceptable for email; no issue.

**`dir="auto"` with no explicit LTR declaration.** Low-risk for an English-only send, but `dir="ltr"` is safer and unambiguous.
