# Lower-priced deals on selected favorite brands 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is REI running the exact "SAVE UP TO 50%" mega-percentage hero it's used all summer, but stitched together with an unusually generous number of distinct offer blocks — HOKA/clearance grid, Mountain Hardwear tents at 40% off, Altra footwear at 25% off, and the LABORDAY26 member 20%-off code all crammed into one long scroll. It reads less like a curated send and more like five separate emails glued together, which dilutes any single offer's impact.
- The good news: it's clean, legible, and bug-free — clear product photography, consistent orange/red REI branding, unambiguous buttons throughout. The bad news: after 6+ "up to X%" sends this summer (2026-08-25, 2026-08-14, 2026-08-07), this hero is indistinguishable from the last three, and the subject line doesn't even try to differentiate — "lower-priced deals" is vague where the 2026-08-29 "extra 20% off" member-benefit subject actually stood out.
- Verdict: solid execution, weak differentiation. This is a mid-pack REI send that a fatigued subscriber skims past.

## What's working

- Clean, high-contrast hero with a legible "UP TO 50% OFF Deals" headline and immediate CTA.
- Product grid (HOKA shoes, jackets) uses real photography with clear per-item "Shop Now" links, not just a generic homepage dump.
- The Mountain Hardwear tent spotlight (40% off) and Altra footwear (25% off) give two concrete, specific sub-offers rather than one vague blanket discount.
- Member benefit block (LABORDAY26, 20% off one Outlet item thru 9/7) is placed near the bottom with a distinct visual treatment, reinforcing loyalty value.
- No render bugs — fonts, spacing, and buttons all display correctly end to end.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Lower-priced deals on selected favorite brands" is generic and low-signal compared to REI's own recent "extra 20% off" and "Labor Day Sale" subjects — no number, no urgency, no brand name in the subject itself.
- Five distinct offers (clearance, tents, Altra, member 20%, plus implicit Labor Day framing) stacked in one email with no clear hierarchy — nothing tells the eye which offer matters most.
- "Up to 50% off" is the same tired mega-percentage template REI has run nearly every week since July; zero novelty for a repeat subscriber.
- Fine print ("Discount based on original prices. While supplies last. Selection varies by location.") repeats after almost every block, undercutting the sense of a clean, honest deal.
- No date-anchoring in the hero itself (unlike the 2026-08-28 "AUG 28 – SEP 7" send) — feels stale relative to REI's better recent Labor Day creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member 20%-off + LABORDAY26 code — it's the most differentiated, highest-value offer in the email and REI already proved (2026-08-29, 6.0/10) that this angle works better than generic mega-percentage. Move it above the fold.
- 2. Cut the email to 2-3 offers max instead of 5; split Mountain Hardwear tents and Altra footwear into their own future sends rather than burying them mid-scroll.
- 3. Stamp the Labor Day date range ("thru 9/7") directly into the hero headline, not just in small print under each block.
- 4. Tighten the fine print to one instance near the footer instead of repeating after every section.
- 5. Rewrite the subject to surface a specific number or the member code, not "lower-priced deals."

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email is a heavily-templated MJML/Outlook-conditional build with severe link infrastructure failures — all 12 probed CTAs timed out and 20 tracking redirects contain malformed doubled-domain parameters.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 material links probed (SHOP THE DEALS, Shop Now ×6, SHOP CLEARANCE, SHOP THE DEAL, SHOP NOW, the Outlet promo link, and Unsubscribe) failed with "read operation timed out" against `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...` — the click-tracking redirector is unresponsive or blocking automated probes.
- 20 `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` deep-link redirects contain a doubled-domain merge error: the `af_r` param embeds a full `https://www.rei.com/...` URL that itself contains a nested `deep_link_value=` repeating the same path plus duplicated `cm_mmc` query strings (appears twice in each URL, e.g. `...nav_logo&deep_link_value=/nav?view=home&cm_mmc=...nav_logo`). This is consistent with an AppsFlyer deep-link template where the query string was appended to `af_r` before URL-encoding, rather than after — producing malformed nested URLs that may break attribution parsing or fail to deep-link correctly into the app/site.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images missing `alt` text (tracking pixel `o.gif`, tracking pixel `eo`, and two content images `FXstCYsYk3...` and `43LKNz4f...`).
- 2 images served over `http://` instead of `https://` (same `FXstCYsYk3...` and `43LKNz4f...` assets, both via `sp.rei.com`), which may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- Heavy Outlook conditional-comment and MJML responsive scaffolding present; no rendering errors detected in the truncated source beyond the above.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge-token or personalization-tag issues found in the visible source (no unresolved `{{...}}`/`*|...|*` tokens observed).

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present but its underlying URL (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`) is among the timed-out links — functional unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — required for one-click unsubscribe support in Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe via POST is not supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking parameters use REI's own `cm_mmc` scheme (e.g. `cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260830_CLR_LDS_Eng_z_1-_-202608300955-_-nav_logo`), consistently applied across nav, image, and CTA links — naming convention itself is internally consistent.
- However, as noted in §2, the `cm_mmc` and destination path are duplicated within each `af_r`/`deep_link_value` pair in all 20 mobile deep-link URLs, meaning the landing-page target embedded in tracking cannot be cleanly resolved without de-duplication logic on the receiving end.

7. Recommendations
