# Up to 50% off: deeper markdowns on selected gear & clothing
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet running the same kitchen-sink structure it's used all summer — a "SAVE UP TO X%" hero, stacked with a second "New Markdowns" brand-logo block, a generic product grid, and a third "UP TO 50% OFF" run/fitness callout. What's different this time is the layering: instead of one hero offer, this email stacks three separate percentage-off claims (50%, 30%, 50% again) in a single scroll, which actually muddies the read rather than reinforcing it. Compared to the July 30 send (8/10, "Just in! Up to 70% off"), this one has a weaker headline number (50% vs 70%) and no hard deadline anywhere in the visible copy — no "ends tomorrow," no countdown.
- The execution is clean and on-brand — bold typography, consistent orange/white palette, real product photography, no render bugs — but there's no urgency, no single clear focus, and the top four product tiles have no visible prices, so the tiles do nothing to convert. This is a mid-pack send: competent template execution, undifferentiated offer, no reason to act today.

## What's working

- Clean, no render bugs — logos, product photos, and CTAs all display correctly with good contrast.
- Clear top-of-email hierarchy: eye lands on "UP TO 50% OFF Deals" immediately on open.
- Named-brand social proof (Patagonia, Arc'teryx, Columbia, Wild Rye / Nike, Beyond Yoga, Salomon) signals real inventory, not vague "select styles."
- Membership hook up top ($30 bonus card at $50 spend) gives loyalty members a concrete reason beyond the discount.
- Multiple clear CTA buttons ("SHOP THE DEALS," "SHOP NOW," "SHOP ALL THE DEALS") reduce friction to click.

## What's weak

- Three separate "up to X%" offers (50%, 30%, 50%) in one email dilute rather than build urgency — no clear single hero offer.
- Zero urgency signal anywhere in the visible render — no deadline, no countdown, no "ends tomorrow" (unlike the 7/5 and 8/2 sends).
- Product grid tiles ("Save on gear like this") show no prices, sizes, or discount %, so they don't function as real merchandising — just generic stock shots.
- Repetitive creative fatigue: this is essentially the same hero-then-brands-then-grid formula REI Outlet has run since early July, just with a lower headline number (50% vs the 70% used in three of the last four sends).
- "Deals" as a hero subhead is generic filler — doesn't tell me what kind of gear or clothing is on sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and cut the other two down to a single supporting line — three stacked "up to X%" blocks compete with each other; consolidate into one "up to 50%" hero plus a single "plus extra markdowns on outdoor & run brands" subline.
- 2. Add a real deadline back in — every high-scoring REI send in this history (7/30, 8/2) had a visible "ends tomorrow" or date; this one has none, which likely explains the lack of urgency.
- 3. Put prices or discount tags on the four product tiles — "SHOP NOW" under an unpriced photo of a green vest gives no reason to click that specific item over the hero CTA.
- 4. Vary the template — five sends running the identical hero/logo-row/grid layout risks banner blindness; test a single-hero, single-CTA version for this cadence.

# Technical Audit: REI "Up to 50% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This email uses an MJML-generated responsive template with ESP link-tracking (`mlinks.rei.com`) and an AppsFlyer/OneLink mobile deep-link redirector (`mobile.rei.com`). Automated QA returned a **7% pass rate (23 issues, 4 warnings)**, driven primarily by link-probe timeouts and a systemic URL-encoding defect in the mobile deep-link redirector.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed material links timed out** ("The read operation timed out"), including every CTA (`SHOP THE DEALS`, `SHOP NOW`, `SHOP ALL DEALS`) and the `Unsubscribe` link, all routed through `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`. This affects the redirector domain broadly enough (across distinct link IDs) to suggest an infrastructure/availability issue on `mlinks.rei.com` at send/check time rather than one bad link — worth re-verifying, since a systemic redirector outage would break every CTA and the unsubscribe path simultaneously.
- **Unencoded nested query parameters causing "doubled-domain" merge errors** (9 instances) on all `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` deep links, e.g.:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...`
  The `af_r` and `deep_link_value` values are raw URLs containing their own unencoded `?`/`&`, so `cm_mmc`, `is_retargeting`, and `af_reengagement_window` parse as top-level params of `mobile.rei.com` instead of as part of the fallback/deep-link value. This will corrupt the AppsFlyer fallback URL and/or drop campaign attribution on the deep-linked destination.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>` — `lang="und"` (undetermined) is not a valid BCP47 language tag; screen readers and translation tools cannot reliably determine content language.
- 5 images missing `alt` text (image IDs `UDi7VYYo...`, `eo`, `o.gif`, `xRGUHuIu...`), reducing accessibility for screen-reader users and hurting fallback rendering with images disabled.
- 2 images served over `http://` (`sp.rei.com/q/l1q2YSlmOh5J8yLiFzbBiA...`, `sp.rei.com/q/z3RBH2YJ3SiXqzHlzVqAqg...`) — non-HTTPS assets are commonly blocked or flagged as mixed content by mail clients (Outlook/Gmail proxy image loaders).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) were found in the provided HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — one-click unsubscribe visibility to mailbox providers cannot be confirmed via headers (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a true absence).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — if truly absent, this disqualifies the message from Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from available data.
- The in-body `Unsubscribe` link (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`) is present (satisfying the visible-mechanism requirement) but was one of the links that timed out during probing — functional reachability is unconfirmed.
- Physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requirement) is not visible in the truncated HTML provided; cannot confirm.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking uses REI's proprietary `cm_mmc` parameter (e.g. `email_com_gm_it-_-20260807_CLR_FW31_NeMa_Pat_MAP_CTRL-_-202608070848-_-26_1_401_09780_z_CTA1`) rather than standard `utm_*` params — fine if REI's analytics stack (Adobe/CM360-style) consumes `cm_mmc` on-site, but not interoperable with GA4-style UTM attribution if that's also in use.
- Deep-link paths (`/f/scd-deals`, `/c/fitness/f/scd-deals`) match the CTA copy (deals, fitness category) — nominal alignment, but this cannot be fully verified because the doubled-domain encoding bug (Section 2) means the actual landing destination and attribution params delivered to `rei.com` may differ from what's intended.
- `pid=email_com_gm_it` and `c=20260807_CLR_FW31_NeMa_Pat_MAP_CTRL` are consistent across all CTA instances, indicating uniform campaign tagging where the encoding is otherwise intact.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link probes against `mlinks.rei.com` to confirm whether the timeout is transient or a live outage affecting all CTAs and the unsubscribe link.
2. Fix the AppsFlyer OneLink construction: URL-encode the `af_r` and `deep_link_value` parameter values (`encodeURIComponent`) so nested `cm_mmc`/tracking params don't leak into the outer query string.
3. Set a valid `lang` attribute (e.g. `lang="en"`) instead of `lang="und"`.
4. Add descriptive (or explicitly empty `alt=""` for decorative/pixel) alt attributes to the 5 flagged images.
5. Serve the `sp.rei.com` tracking/personalization images over `https://`.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (verify at the MTA/ESP level, independent of relay capture) to ensure one-click unsubscribe compliance.
7. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) via a direct header inspection rather than through the relay, given deliverability risk from an unauthenticated result.
