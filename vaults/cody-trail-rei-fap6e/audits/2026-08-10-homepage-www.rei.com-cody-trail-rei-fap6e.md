# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This reads like classic REI: outdoorsy, brand-forward, co-op-first. Right now they're pushing trail running shoes — HOKA, Topo, Salomon — with a past-season clearance angle layered underneath. It's on-brand and well-shot, but the sign-in popup jumping in front of the hero the second the page loads is annoying, and it's literally cutting off headline copy on two of the three carousel panels.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% off past-season styles from top-rated brands" top banner — that's exactly the re/Supply, deal-hunting instinct REI co-op members like me have.
- Brand logo strip (HOKA, Topo, Salomon) under each hero panel — quick credibility check before I even tap in.
- "Shop trail running" / "Shop HOKA" buttons are unambiguous — I know exactly where I land.
- REI Co-op / Outdoor Research / The North Face lockup at the bottom with "UP TO 50%" — reinforces this is a real co-op deal, not just one brand's push.

## What's weak

- The sign-in modal popped up unprompted and is sitting directly over the middle and third hero panels — I can't even read "It's a roller coast[er]" or the third shoe's full pitch.
- Hero is 100% footwear/trail running. Nothing here nods to outerwear, backpacking gear, or Trade-In — the categories I actually shop.
- No visible member pricing or dividend callout in the hero itself — the rewards pitch is buried inside the interruption modal instead of being a standing badge.
- No new-arrivals or gear rail visible in this fold — just the running carousel and a sale lockup.

## Recommendations

- Kill the auto-triggered sign-in modal on first load — let me scroll the hero before asking me to create an account.
- Swap one of the three hero carousel slots for a backpacking or Trade-In moment — right now it's all trail-running, all the time.
- Put the "Up to 50%" dividend/member-pricing detail as a persistent badge near the nav instead of hiding it inside the popup.
- Add a visible "shop by activity" rail (Camp & Hike, Climb, Trade-In) right under the hero so co-op members like me don't have to hunt the top nav.
