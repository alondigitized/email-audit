# Selected outdoor brands up to 70% off—ends tomorrow
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet running the exact same "SAVE UP TO 70%" hero it's used essentially every send since early July — the same giant type treatment, the same "ENDS TOMORROW" urgency banner, the same four-brand list format (this time KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering), the same "Don't miss these top picks for you" grid, the same "SHOP ALL THE DEALS" button. Compared to the July 30 and July 31 sends, this is a near-identical template refresh with a swapped brand lineup and a tighter deadline (8/3 vs. a rolling window).
- The "top picks for you" grid is the recurring weak point: none of the four tiles show a price, a brand name, or a discount percentage — just a legging, a t-shirt, a climbing shoe, and a boot with generic "SHOP NOW" links. There's no visible tie to KEEN, Columbia, prAna, or ALPS Mountaineering anywhere in the product grid, so the hero's brand promise and the body's product picks feel disconnected.
- At this point the cadence itself is the story: four nearly identical "up to 70%" blanket sends in five days (7/30, 7/31, and now 8/2) is fatigue territory. The execution is clean and on-brand, but there's nothing new here to earn a click from someone who's already seen this exact layout twice this week.

## What's working

- Clean, legible hero with unambiguous urgency ("ENDS TOMORROW") and a concrete offer ceiling ("70%").
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") repeated consistently through the hero and grid.
- Member benefit banner up top ($30 bonus card) gives a secondary, more personalized hook beyond the blanket discount.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, layout is consistent, footer/legal/social blocks all render correctly.
- Loyalty perks (100% guarantee, REI Co-op Mastercard 5% rewards) are visible below the fold for engaged/member readers.

## What's weak

- Fourth near-identical "up to X% off" email in about a week — same hero, same layout, same "SHOP ALL THE DEALS" close as 7/30 and 7/31.
- Product grid has zero prices, zero brand labels, and no visible connection to the four brands named in the hero copy (KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering).
- "Don't miss these top picks for you" implies personalization, but the four items (leggings, tee, climbing shoe, boot) span unrelated categories with no obvious logic tying them together.
- Subject says "outdoor brands" but the hero and grid don't visually reinforce which brands beyond small body copy — no logo row this time, unlike the July 30 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the generic four-tile grid for branded tiles — put the KEEN/Columbia/prAna/ALPS logo or product name and a price/discount on each tile so the hero's brand promise pays off in the body. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Break the template rotation — after three straight "SAVE UP TO X%" hero sends in a week, this needs a different creative frame (e.g., lead with the member bonus card, or a single hero product) to avoid being auto-filtered as noise.
- 3. Add a price or "from $X" anchor under at least the hero CTA so the offer feels concrete rather than a bare percentage.
- 4. Tighten the subject to match the shrinking urgency window more specifically.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard MJML-generated, table-based HTML build with Outlook/MSO conditional comments and mobile breakpoint styles. Automated QA could not verify 12/12 probed material links (all timed out) and flagged 2 unique tracking URLs with malformed nested query parameters; several images lack alt text and two load over plain HTTP.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **All 12 probed material links timed out** ("The read operation timed out") when resolving through the `mlinks.rei.com` redirector (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style tracking domain), including the hero CTA ("SHOP NOW"), footer nav, and the Unsubscribe link. This is a verification failure, not confirmed breakage — but it means link destinations (including the unsubscribe target) are **unconfirmed** and should be manually re-tested.
- **Malformed nested-URL parameters (2 unique instances, "doubled-domain" flag):**
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260802_OUT_T70_EnTom_z_Jul_CTRL-_-202608020816-_-26_1_403_09713_z_CTA1&deep_link_value=/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...`
  Both `af_r` and `deep_link_value` (AppsFlyer deep-link params) embed a full nested URL *with its own unescaped `?cm_mmc=...` query string*. Because the nested `?` and `&` are not percent-encoded, the outer parser sees `cm_mmc`, `deep_link_value`, `pid`, `c`, `is_retargeting`, `af_reengagement_window` as flat siblings of `af_r` rather than as part of the nested destination URL. This risks AppsFlyer dropping/mis-parsing the deep link and losing campaign attribution on click. A second, distinct instance carries the same defect for the hero image link (`...09714_z_Image1`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **5 images missing `alt` text**, including two content images served from `sp.rei.com` and the two tracking pixels (`eo`, `o.gif` — pixels don't strictly need alt text, but the two `sp.rei.com` content images do for screen-reader/image-blocked fallback).
- **2 images use `http://` instead of `https://`:**
  `http://sp.rei.com/q/nNLIRgoCEr5qLvd-Q-OCrA~~/AARGbBA~/oe39FIKRBzVla7jduPcybqAbImzrUL8SVZJjVQ7coBzkrMEY7mUIqwwdyAAUMzFwhD`
  `http://sp.rei.com/q/GUn7CWmazEyOTzXoqqF1_A~~/AARGbBA~/jq6Q2Fd8xpNZXXLom8VSbLHcrxoLeH6_0Oqe210unSQcADBtdd8R9kglYse1yFTjL3`
  Mixed-content images over plain HTTP may be blocked or flagged by mail clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading (e.g., Outlook, Gmail proxy in strict mode).
- Standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`), VML `xmlns:o`/`xmlns:v` declarations, and `mj-column-per-*` responsive breakpoints are present — no structural rendering defects found in the visible markup.

No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{token}}`, `[[field]]`) are visible in the truncated HTML source provided. Full personalization audit is inconclusive given the source truncation.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- A functional unsubscribe link is present in-body: `https://mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?_t=...&_m=...&_e=...` (link text "Unsubscribe") — satisfies CAN-SPAM's visible opt-out requirement, though its resolution timed out during probing (see §2) and could not be confirmed live.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — per QA, may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent; cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance from this data.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support is unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not detected** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown via this relay; not evidence of an actual auth failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Consistent campaign tagging scheme across CTAs: `cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260802_OUT_T70_EnTom_z_Jul_CTRL-_-202608020816-_-26_<slot>_403_<id>_z_<placement>` (e.g. `z_CTA1`, `z_Image1`), giving distinct attribution per content block.
- Redirect chain structure: `mlinks.rei.com` (ESP tracking) → `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` (AppsFlyer deep link) → `www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals` (landing page), which aligns with the subject line/CTA copy (outlet deals promotion).
- Final landing-page rendering/alignment could not be verified — all probes timed out before reaching `rei.com`, and the malformed deep-link params in §2 add risk that the destination URL doesn't resolve as intended on mobile app-install/deep-link flows.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Re-run link verification against `mlinks.rei.com` and `mobile.rei.com` with a longer timeout/retry policy to distinguish genuine breakage from redirector latency — do not treat current timeouts as confirmed dead links.
2. Percent-encode the nested destination URL inside the `af_r` and `deep_link_value` AppsFlyer parameters (i.e., encode the embedded `?`, `&`, `=` in `https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...`) to prevent query-param collision and attribution loss.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two `sp.rei.com` content images.
4. Serve the two `sp.rei.com` images over `https://` instead of `http://`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM `Authentication-Results` directly from raw SMTP headers at the source (not via the AgentMail relay) to close out the compliance/deliverability unknowns.
