# EXTRA savings for members on 3 brands thru 7/19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, member-exclusive brand-stacking play — REI Outlet leaning into the "JUST FOR MEMBERS / EXTRA 25% OFF" hook with The North Face, Obōz, and Free Country as the three featured brands. The structure is logical: hero coupon → brand blocks in order → footer trust signals. The teal header color and the "JULOUTLET26" code make this feel like a genuine member reward rather than a mass promo.
- The problem is familiarity. REI's brand history shows they've run the "$20 off for members" and "JUST FOR MEMBERS" creative at least once already (July 3 coupon drop, scored 8/10), and the general "extra % off selected brands" formula has appeared multiple times. This send hits the same structural beats: member gate, coupon code, shop-by-brand layout. The July 3 email earned its 8/10 because it was a pivot; this one lands in the "more of the same" bucket.
- The three brands are a mixed bag for a serious outdoor buyer. The North Face is a natural anchor. Obōz footwear is a solid, loyal-buyer niche. Free Country is discount-tier apparel and will feel random to a core REI demographic. That brand juxtaposition undercuts the premium feel the teal hero builds.

## What's working

- **Member exclusivity is loud and early.** "JUST FOR MEMBERS / EXTRA 25% OFF" in the hero is unambiguous — the offer and the gate are both visible above the fold.
- **Coupon code is displayed prominently.** "JULOUTLET26" is in a styled box in the hero, easy to read and copy.
- **Three-brand architecture gives the email clear structure.** The North Face, Obōz, and Free Country each get their own product grid with "SHOP ALL," so it's scannable rather than a wall of product.
- **Deadline is visible.** "Ends 7/19" appears in the hero and is restated clearly.
- **Footer trust signals are solid.** 100-day return badge, REI Co-op Mastercard earn rates, and app download links are all present.

## What's weak

- **Free Country is a brand mismatch.** Paired with The North Face and Obōz, it reads oddly — it's a mid-tier value brand, not an outdoor specialist, and its presence here dilutes the "premium member deal" framing.
- **No visible hero product image.** The hero is pure type and CTA on a teal background — there's no aspirational product shot to make the offer feel tangible before the brand grids begin.
- **Product images are small and largely identical in scale.** Across all three brand blocks, the shots are thumbnail-sized; there's no featured hero product to anchor interest within each section.
- **"Become a Member" CTA in the hero is noise for existing members.** Including a member-acquisition CTA inside a member-exclusive email creates a mixed message — this reads like template bleed from a dual-audience send.
- **No model lifestyle photography.** All product images appear to be flat/studio shots; no on-person or in-context imagery, which matters especially for apparel and footwear.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop Free Country or replace it with a more credible outdoor brand.** Swap in Smartwool, Darn Tough, or Black Diamond — anything that reads "REI Outlet" rather than "T.J. Maxx." Free Country's inclusion actively harms the brand-stack signal.
- 2. **Add a hero lifestyle image behind or alongside the text block.** Even a single aspirational shot (mountain trail, coastal trail running) would give the email an emotional hook. Right now it's entirely utilitarian.
- 3. **Kill the "Become a Member" button in the hero.** This is a members-only email. The acquisition CTA here creates confusion and wastes prime real estate. Replace it with a second "Shop Now" or a specific brand callout.
- 4. **Make the coupon code the subject line.** The current subject buries the punchline. Leads with the code in the subject and you give a concrete, copy-pasteable reason to open.
- 5. **Add a per-brand offer callout.** Instead of just "25% off," each brand block could note something like "TNF: select jackets from $89 after discount" — specificity converts.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member code for extra 25% off The North Face, Obōz & Free Country`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The North Face, Obōz & Free Country — already on sale, now 25% more off. Ends Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off selected brands — use code JULOUTLET26 at checkout. Members only, thru 7/19.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issue — invalid language tag:**
```html
<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>
```
`lang="und"` (BCP 47 "undetermined") tells screen readers (JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver) the language is unknown, causing incorrect pronunciation rules for English content. Should be `lang="en"` or `lang="en-US"`. This also violates WCAG 2.1 SC 3.1.1.

**Minor — `dir="auto"` instead of `dir="ltr"`:** Acceptable but unnecessarily ambiguous for a fixed-English email. `dir="ltr"` is more explicit and avoids any right-to-left inference in bidirectional-aware clients.
