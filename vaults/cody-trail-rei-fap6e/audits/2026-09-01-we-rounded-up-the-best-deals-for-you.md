# We rounded up the best deals for you 😎
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet's "best deals" digest email, and it's basically a greatest-hits reel of every offer already running this Labor Day cycle — the "ENDS TODAY: UP TO 60% OFF" flash-deal countdown, the 25% off footwear grid, the 20% off member-loyalty push, and a rain jacket callout, all stacked in one long scroll. Compared to 2026-08-29's cleaner member-benefit-only send, this one reverts to the kitchen-sink format REI has leaned on all summer, just with a literal countdown timer added for urgency.
- The countdown timer ("20:31:19... ENDS TODAY") is the standout new element and it's genuinely well-placed right under the flash-deal hero — that's the strongest single signal in the email. But burying it under a full-bleed lifestyle hero, then following it with three more separate offer blocks before the countdown's own CTA even resolves, dilutes the urgency it's trying to create. Net: recognizable brand, real discounts, functioning countdown — but it's a cluttered, "here's everything we've got" send rather than a sharp single offer.

## What's working

- The countdown timer ("20:31:19 HOURS MINUTES SECONDS") under "ENDS TODAY" is a concrete, credible urgency device rendered cleanly in bold red-on-white.
- The footwear grid is well laid out — six brand blocks (Altra, KEEN, La Sportiva, Danner) with clear product photography and legible "25% Off" numerals.
- Member benefit is called out with a real code (LABORDAY26) and specific 20% figure, consistent with the 8/29 member-first send.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- Footer trust signals (100-day returns, co-op Mastercard rewards) reinforce loyalty positioning.

## What's weak

- Four distinct offer campaigns (flash deal, footwear 25%, outlet 20% member, rain jackets) are stacked in one email with no single hero — this is exactly the "kitchen sink" pattern flagged in the 8/25 and 8/30 sends, not an improvement.
- Subject "We rounded up the best deals for you 😎" is generic aggregator language — it doesn't mention the flash sale, the 60% off, the ends-today urgency, or Labor Day at all, so the strongest asset in the email (the countdown) isn't teased in the inbox.
- "Popular brands everyone's loving" hero at the top adds a full screen of scroll before any offer or number appears — on mobile this pushes the actual deal below the fold.
- The rain jacket section ("Ready for (rain)fall") has no discount attached at all, which flattens the otherwise offer-dense email and reads as filler.
- This is the fourth Labor Day-branded send in eight days (8/26 teaser, 8/28 launch, 8/29 member extra, now this) — cadence fatigue risk for a daily opener.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject and hero with the flash-deal urgency, not a vague roundup — the countdown timer is the only truly time-bound, high-stakes element here and it's currently buried under a brand-logo hero.
- 2. Cut the email to one primary offer (flash 60% off + countdown) with the footwear and member-outlet deals demoted to a single secondary block, rather than four co-equal campaigns.
- 3. Move the countdown timer above the "Popular brands" hero, or replace that hero entirely, so urgency is the first thing seen without scrolling.
- 4. Either attach a discount to the rain jacket section or cut it — an undiscounted product callout in a deals-roundup email undercuts the "best deals" premise.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⏱ Ends today: 60% off + extra 20% for members`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack an extra 20% off with code LABORDAY26 — but the clock's ticking`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Footwear, outerwear, and outlet deals — all before midnight`

1. Technical Summary

The email fails automated QA at a 6% pass rate (29 issues), driven by widespread click-tracking link timeouts and a systemic doubled-domain merge/tracking error affecting most product CTAs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 8 `mlinks.rei.com` redirect links (including "SHOP THE SALE", "SHOP THE DEALS", "SHOP FOOTWEAR DEALS", "SHOP REI OUTLET", "SHOP NOW", "Download our app", "REI Co-op", "Popular brands everyone's loving", "FPO") all failed with read timeouts, indicating the redirector service is unresponsive or the links are broken.
- 17 CTA links using the pattern `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/...` contain a doubled-domain merge error — the `af_r` deep-link parameter embeds a second full `https://www.rei.com/...` URL, and QA flagged this as "URL contains a repeated domain." Example: `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/c/mens-clothing/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/c/mens-clothing/f/scd-deals?cm_mmc=...`. This affects nav links and most product-category CTAs (men's/women's clothing, footwear, outlet, brand pages).
- Two CTAs ("SHOP FOOTWEAR DEALS" outlet section and "outlet-coupon-eligible-items") appear as exact duplicate URLs in the QA output, suggesting the same tracked link is repeated across two separate rendered elements.
