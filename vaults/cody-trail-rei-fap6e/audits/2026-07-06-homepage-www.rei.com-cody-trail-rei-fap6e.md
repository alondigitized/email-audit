# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- REI is clearly running a 4th of July sale push and they're not being subtle about it — the whole above-the-fold is "deals" energy with an Osprey pack and climbing helmet front and center. As a co-op member this is my brand, and the gear imagery lands, but I'm a little disappointed that signing in doesn't seem to surface any dividend or member-exclusive angle — it reads more like a generic sale than a "hey member, here's your early access" moment.

## What's working

- **Osprey pack + helmet hero imagery** — I recognized that backpack immediately. Showing actual aspirational gear instead of a lifestyle model is my preferred visual language; it lets me self-project.
- **"4th of July Deals" headline + 50% OFF badge** — Clean, specific, seasonal. The badge placement to the right of the headline creates a natural left-to-right reading path ending on the number.
- **"Shop the deals" CTA button** — Red on beige, good contrast, unambiguous copy. I know exactly what tap does.
- **Top nav "Trade-In" link** — Visible in the utility bar. That's a feature I actively use and the fact it's surfaced at the top without hunting shows REI knows their member base.
- **Promotional banner auto-play with pause control** — The (II) pause button on the ticker banner is a small UX kindness I notice and appreciate.

## What's weak

- **Zero co-op member framing** — I'm a dividend member and there's no "Member Early Access," "Earn on this purchase," or "Your member price" angle anywhere above the fold. REI's biggest differentiator from REI Outlet and Amazon is the co-op structure, and it's invisible here.
- **"Up to 50% off" on sale + clearance + Outlet** — Conflating three different inventory types (in-season sale, clearance, outlet) under one umbrella discount is vague. I'm trying to figure out if I'm buying current-year Osprey or last-season's colorway.
- **No specific product or category shortcut** — "Shop the deals" drops me into a sea of stuff. One tap to "Backpacking" or "Tents & Shelters" would get me to product faster than a browse session.
- **No human in the imagery** — Fine for me, but the hero is pure product-on-white. A quick shot of someone on trail with that Osprey frame would add warmth without losing the gear focus.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member badge to the hero** — Swap "Save on sale, clearance and REI Outlet items" for "Members earn dividend on every deal" and surface a "Sign in for member price" micro-CTA below the main button. This costs one line of copy and turns the sale into a co-op advantage.
- **Split the deal CTAs by category** — Replace the single "Shop the deals" button with two smaller secondary links: "Backpacking & Camping" and "Apparel & Footwear." One additional tap saved = measurably higher conversion for high-intent visitors.
- **Call out Trade-In / Re/Supply explicitly in the hero context** — A small text link "or trade in old gear to fund new gear →" below the CTA would be pure Cody bait and reinforces the co-op values loop.
- **Tighten the "up to" language** — "Up to 50% off select gear" or "50% off + more on marked items" is still promotionally sound but feels less like a bait headline.
