# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- REI is leading with a camp kitchen push — YETI, Zempire, OXO Outdoor, cast-iron pans over a two-burner camp stove — which reads "car camping" more than "weekend backpacking trip." As a Co-op member I immediately clock the Trade-In tab in the nav (nice) and the 2025 Impact Report banner, which actually matters to me, but the hero has zero dividend hooks, zero member pricing, and zero above-fold indication that I'm being rewarded for my loyalty. Feels like a well-executed brand homepage aimed at a broad outdoor audience, not a returning Co-op shareholder.

## What's working

- **Trade-In tab in top nav** — it's right there at the top level. For me, that's a reminder that I can bring in old gear and offset my next purchase. One of the best things REI does and they surface it prominently.
- **"Shop camp kitchen" CTA button** — dark green, clear copy, doesn't try to be clever. I know exactly what I'm tapping.
- **2025 Impact Report banner** — as a Co-op member who chose REI *because* of its values, this banner lands. I'd actually tap "Learn more." It's rare that a sustainability callout feels this earned.
- **Brand logos in hero (YETI, OXO Outdoor)** — signals quality curation. If REI is stocking it alongside YETI, I trust the rest of the camp kitchen shelf.
- **Clean mobile layout** — search bar, nav, hero, CTA — zero clutter. Loads looking complete.

## What's weak

- **No Co-op member hook above the fold** — I have a dividend sitting there. Show me my balance, show me a "Members save X%" badge, *something*. Treating me like a guest when I've been a member for years is a miss.
- **Hero is car-camping, not backpacking** — That's a big camp stove, a spread of fresh food, OXO kitchen tools. This is a front-country setup. I'm planning a three-day Cascades trip. The pivot to camp kitchen doesn't reach me where I am.
- **Zero offers above the fold** — No sale price, no member-exclusive discount, no free-shipping call-out. If I landed here mid-week without a specific intent, there's no hook to push me from browsing to buying.
- **No urgency or time-bound signal** — The partially visible second panel might have something (Garmin pre-order?) but nothing on the primary hero anchors me with a deadline.
- **No imagery of a person** — The hero is all food and gear. I can't picture myself in this scene.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the dividend or member pricing in the hero or banner** — A simple "Your Co-op dividend: [X]. Ready to use." strip just below the nav would immediately separate me from a first-time visitor and reinforce why I shop REI first.
- **Swap or alternate the hero to a backpacking/trail lifestyle shot** — Camp kitchen is a valid category push, but a trail-ready image (person cresting a ridge, ultralight kit) would catch me before I even read the copy. A/B test it against the current car-camping spread.
- **Add a concrete offer to the hero sub-copy** — "Members save 20% on camp kitchen, now through May 25" is all it takes. Right now "Elevated camp meals start with better gear" is aspirational but soft.
- **Expose re/Supply or Outlet as a tap target above fold** — Garage Sale, re/Supply, and Outlet are how I find value at REI. One chip or badge in the hero ("Also in re/Supply") would pull me from browse to buy without cannibalizing full-price.
