# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a co-op that sells to people who actually go outside. The top bar has Trade-In, Classes & Events, Expert Advice and Membership, so it feels like my kind of place. Right now it's pushing snow gear and a Gift Guide, with a "Up to 50% off past-season styles" strip. A sign-in popup is sitting on top of the header before I've done anything.

## What's working

- **Top utility bar** (Trade-In, Classes & Events, Expert Advice, Uncommon Path, Membership): it matches how I shop. Trade-In and classes are the reasons I'm a member.
- **Category nav** (Camp & Hike, Climb, Cycle, Water, Run, Snow, Travel): one tap to Camp & Hike, which is my weekend. It's plain, with no gimmicks.
- **Orange promo strip**: it's high-contrast and says what the discount covers. I'd click "Shop now!" for past-season shells.
- **Gift Guide panel**: the campfire crowd in fleeces and puffies looks like my friends. It feels like community, not a catalog shoot.
- **Third panel (puffy jacket, Patagonia / North Face logos)**: this is the one that pulls me. I buy outerwear once and repair it twice, so brand names plus "Jackets th…" is my lane.
- **Store locator** ("Near you · Closed. Opens 10am · Arcadia"): a nice local touch for a co-op.

## What's weak

- **Auto-opened sign-in popup**: it pops over the header and clips the "Deals" nav item. I haven't looked at anything yet, so I'd close it right away.
- **Popup rewards copy**: it says "Total REI Rewards" but gives no number. As a dividend-driven member, I want to see "10% back" or member pricing, not a generic "easier for members".
- **Hero skews to skiing**: it's fine, but my weekends are backpacking and trail. Nothing above the fold says Camp & Hike.
- **Three equal panels with no priority**: I don't know where I'm meant to land first.
- **Right panel cut off** ("Jackets th…", "Shop jackets"): the panel that's most relevant to me is the one that's clipped.
- **No trust or social proof**: no star ratings, return policy or free-shipping line in view.
- **No Garage Sale or re/Supply callout**: that's the member-only deal I'd actually hunt for.

## Recommendations

- Don't auto-open the sign-in popup. Show it as a slim "Members: see your dividend" chip beside the Sign in link, so the "Deals" nav item and the hero stay clear.
- Put a quantified member benefit in the orange strip. For example: "Up to 50% off past-season styles. Members get an extra [X]% back."
- Add a Camp & Hike / backpacking panel next to the ski and gift panels. Use a pack-on-trail shot with a "Shop packs & shelters" button.
- Add a small "Garage Sale + Used Gear (re/Supply)" tile with a date. Put a free-returns / member-shipping line under the hero so the trust signals show up without scrolling.
