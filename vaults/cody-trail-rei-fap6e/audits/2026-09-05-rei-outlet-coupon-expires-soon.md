# REI Outlet coupon expires SOON!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the members-only "20% off one item" angle again — same as the 2026-08-29 send ("It's yours: an EXTRA 20% off 1 item at REI Outlet thru 9/7") — but with a countdown timer stapled on top and a "coupon expires SOON!" subject line borrowed from the flash-deal playbook. The problem is the coupon isn't actually urgent: the countdown reads 2 days 15 hours, but the code LABORDAY26 and the Outdoor Research/tent deals below it are all explicitly "thru 9/7," so the "SOON" framing in the subject oversells a deadline that's really still two-plus days out.
- Visually it's clean and well-organized — clear hero, legible countdown, a tidy 2x3 handpicked-deals grid, then three distinct sub-promos (Outdoor Research, sitewide 50%, Labor Day sale, tent spotlight). But that's also the issue: this is five emails stacked into one, and the actual coupon (the thing the subject promises) gets buried after a grid of unrelated products before you even see the "USE CODE" block reinforced again.
- Net: solid production, recycled offer, subject line urgency that isn't well-supported by the countdown shown. Middling send, consistent with this brand's recent 5-6/10 run.

## What's working

- Countdown timer (2:15:11) gives the subject's urgency a concrete visual anchor.
- Coupon code (LABORDAY26) and "20% OFF one REI Outlet item" are both bold and easy to spot in the hero.
- "Handpicked deals for you" grid gives six concrete, browsable products above the fold-adjacent area.
- Clear tiered CTA structure (Shop REI Outlet / Become a Member) lets both members and non-members self-select.
- No render bugs — clean grid alignment, no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Subject says "expires SOON" but the visible countdown is over 2 days out — mismatched urgency.
- Single email crams four separate promos (member coupon, Outdoor Research, sitewide 50%, tent spotlight) — no single clear hero after the fold.
- "Members only" gate is stated but the reward for non-members (Become a Member CTA) has no visible incentive/benefit copy beyond the button.
- This is the third distinct "thru 9/7" deadline framing this week (Labor Day teaser → sale launch → member coupon → now "expires soon") — cadence fatigue risk.
- Fine print ("Discount based on original prices. Selection varies by location.") repeats after nearly every block, diluting the sense of a clean, honest single offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the actual countdown — if it's really 2+ days, don't say "SOON." Either tighten to a true last-day send or say "2 days left," not artificial urgency.
- 2. Cut this to one dominant offer per send — split Outdoor Research and the tent spotlight into their own emails; this one should be 100% about the member coupon.
- 3. Move the "USE CODE LABORDAY26" instruction directly under the countdown, not as a separate line beneath it, so scanning mobile users see code + urgency in one glance.
- 4. Add a one-line member benefit under "Become a Member" (e.g., "get an extra 20% off, free shipping, and $10 off your first purchase") — right now it's a button with no value prop.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off code disappears in 2 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `REI Outlet members: this coupon ends 9/7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use LABORDAY26 before it's gone — one item, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members-only: 20% off any Outlet item, ends Monday`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Automated QA reports a 6% pass rate driven by 12/12 probed CTA/tracking links timing out and 20 destination URLs containing a doubled-domain merge error, alongside missing alt text, mixed HTTP/HTTPS image sources, and absent authentication/compliance headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 12 probed material links (mlinks.rei.com redirect/tracking wrappers) failed with "read operation timed out," including primary CTAs "SHOP REI OUTLET," "SHOP OUTDOOR RESEARCH DEALS," multiple "Shop Now" buttons, and the "Unsubscribe" link itself. Redirect infrastructure at mlinks.rei.com appears unresponsive or unreachable at test time — this blocks verification of final destinations and should be re-tested against the live send.
- 20 destination URLs behind the mobile.rei.com/AkCd/ deep-link wrapper contain a doubled-domain merge error: the `cm_mmc` query parameter is appended to the inner destination path before being re-embedded in `af_r`, causing `cm_mmc` (and its value) to appear twice — once inside `af_r=...` and once as a top-level param, and the `deep_link_value` mirrors the same duplication. Example: `af_r=https://www.rei.com/outlet?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/outlet?cm_mmc=...&...&cm_mmc=...`. This is a merge-tag/templating defect in the deep-link generator, not a rendering issue — likely results in malformed query strings on the landing page or in the mobile app deep-link handler.
- Tracking pixels present: mi.rei.com/p/cp/*/o.gif and mi.rei.com/p/rp/*.png (open/render tracking) — functioning as expected, no errors reported.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
