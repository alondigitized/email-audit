# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is REI doing what REI does best — leading with a member-first offer right when it counts. The Anniversary Sale banner hits immediately above the hero, the guy in the orange down jacket holding trekking poles on a ridgeline is basically my weekend, and the co-op framing puts the membership front and center. It's the annual tent-pole moment and the page knows it.

## What's working

- **Orange members banner** — "Members, it's the **final weekend**" is the first thing I read. As a dividend member, that framing makes me feel like I'm in on something. The urgency ("final weekend") and the exclusivity ("Members") are both doing work.
- **Hero model** — That guy in the orange hooded jacket with trekking poles is me on a Saturday in the Cascades. Age, gear, vibe — nailed. I don't have to squint and imagine myself in this picture.
- **Code ANNIV26 spelled out** — I don't have to hunt for the deal. It's right there in the banner. One screenshot to my group chat and I'm the hero.
- **"Anniversary Sale 2026 — Our Biggest Sale of the year"** — clear, confident, no hedging. Big-tent promise.
- **Trade-In tab in top nav** — that's in the header navigation and it's exactly my loop: bring the old jacket in, put money toward a new one.

## What's weak

- **No product rails above the fold** — I'm a member here to shop outerwear and packs; showing me lifestyle copy is great but I'm already sold on REI. Where's the "Member Deals" or "Outerwear" shelf I can tap into immediately?
- **"Shop now!" is a text link, not a button** — on mobile, a small underlined hyperlink is harder to hit than a full-width CTA button. The offer is strong; the tap target doesn't match the urgency.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I'm about to spend $180 on a jacket. Telling me shipping is free (or what the threshold is) right here would remove a checkout hesitation I'll hit later.
- **Classes & Events nav is truncated** — "Classes & Eve..." is cut off in the top bar. As someone who checks REI classes regularly, that feels sloppy on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Add a full-width "Shop Anniversary Sale" button** below the hero headline — replace or augment the "Shop now!" text link with a pill/button at native tap size so the CTA is actually easy to hit on a 6-inch screen.
- **Drop a two-tile quick-shop row below the hero** — "Men's Outerwear" and "Packs & Bags" tiles with member pricing badges would let me skip straight to my categories without opening the hamburger.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the banner** — even "Free shipping on orders $50+" next to the promo code would eliminate a friction point before I get to checkout.
- **Fix the truncated "Classes & Eve..." nav label** — either abbreviate to "Classes" or widen the nav rail; it reads as a layout bug to a regular REI shopper who uses that tab.
