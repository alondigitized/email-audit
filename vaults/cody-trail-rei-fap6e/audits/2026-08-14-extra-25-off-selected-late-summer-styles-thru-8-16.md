# EXTRA 25% off selected late-summer styles thru 8/16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest, most legible REI Outlet send of the run I've been tracking since early July — and a real departure from the format. Instead of the "SAVE UP TO 70%" mega-percentage hero stacked with a second brand-logo block (the July 30/31, Aug 2, Aug 7 template), this one leads with a single, specific "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero, a real promo code (OUTAUG26), a clear end date (8/16), and a category-relevant product grid (sunglasses, sandals, swimwear, watersports gear) instead of generic unlabeled tiles.
- The catch: this is a summer-clearance/outdoor-gear email with zero apparent targeting toward a fashion/home-goods persona like Walker — sunglasses, boardshorts, flip-flops, and a bikini top don't map to any category this reader shops. It's well-built creative wasted on a mismatched send list, which is a segmentation problem, not a creative one.
- Judgement: strong execution, weak fit. Compared to the July 30 send (8/10, the high-water mark for this brand), this trades brand-name social proof (KEEN, Columbia) for a tighter, single-offer hero — a net creative improvement — but still lands mid-pack because of persona mismatch and a code (not automatic) discount.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous hero offer — "EXTRA 25% OFF" with a visible, correctly-cased promo code (OUTAUG26) sitting right below the copy.
- Clear urgency with a real deadline ("Ends 8/16") repeated twice (hero subtext and fine print) without feeling shouty.
- Product grid is thematically coherent — sunglasses, sandals, boardshorts, swimwear all read as one "late-summer" category, unlike prior sends' generic mixed tiles.
- No render bugs — clean teal/white contrast, images loaded, buttons rendered correctly, consistent typography throughout.
- Membership/loyalty content is present and specific (5% card rewards, 1-year member returns, $30 bonus card for new members).

## What's weak

- No prices or percentage-off shown on individual product tiles — every one just says "SHOP NOW," so the shopper can't tell which items actually qualify or what they'd pay.
- Not a Walker-relevant category at all — this is outdoor/beach gear (boardshorts, flip-flops, swim top), a persona mismatch regardless of execution quality.
- Discount requires manually applying a code rather than being automatic, adding friction versus recent sends that led with "up to X% off" applied at checkout.
- Product grid has no alt text/labels visible under the thumbnails beyond "Shop Now" — a shopper can't identify brand or product name without hovering/clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or "% off" badge directly on each product tile — the single biggest lever here, since urgency + code are already strong but the grid gives no reason to click one tile over another.
- 2. Auto-apply the discount instead of requiring code entry, or at minimum show "code auto-applied at checkout" to reduce friction perception.
- 3. Add one line of brand/product name under each tile (e.g., "Sunski Polarized Sunglasses") so the grid functions as product discovery, not just imagery.
- 4. Segment this send away from non-outdoor personas — swimwear/watersports creative should route to subscribers with prior beach/outdoor purchase history.

# Technical Audit — REI Outlet "EXTRA 25% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Built on MJML, the email passes only 7% of automated checks: all 12 probed links timed out on click-tracking redirects, and 7 CTA/image links contain a doubled-domain merge error that will send users to a broken URL.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **All 12 probed links failed with read timeouts** through the `mlinks.rei.com` click-tracking domain (e.g. `https://mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/c5d2f76db64c49879f7e2eed33b43dea/397065b0bc954f77a4ebbc32afd6c47c` for "SHOP NOW"). This affects every SHOP NOW CTA, "SHOP ALL DEALS," "Download our app," the "REI Outlet" logo link, the preheader link, and the Unsubscribe link. A 100% timeout rate across a single tracking domain points to an infrastructure/redirector issue on the `mlinks.rei.com` host rather than 12 independent link failures.
- **Doubled-domain merge error (7 instances)**: URLs of the form `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...` embed a full second URL inside the `af_r` (AppsFlyer deep-link redirect) parameter with an unencoded query string. Because `af_r`'s value contains an un-encoded `?cm_mmc=...`, the query-string parser will treat everything after the first `?` in the outer URL as literal — the `deep_link_value` param will also break for the same reason. This affects the top CTA (`z_Aug_DEF...z_403_09746_TC_CTA1`) and the Image1 link (`z_403_09748_z_Image1`), each duplicated across desktop/mobile/AMP variants.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 `<img>` tags are missing `alt` text: two content/tracking images (`ORS-Hi01HAJV93nYsB87H3m1dJwGItehYLRaOcRS8gaqtM1Lzw`, `ovqqmSGRRZ8E1qceYPrD9erjMvVnZlJtKyaAn46PODlpEsf5Fi`) and two 1x1 open-tracking pixels (`eo`, `o.gif`). The pixels are low-priority, but the two content images should carry descriptive `alt` per WCAG/CAN-SPAM-adjacent accessibility norms.
- 2 images load over plain `http://` (`sp.rei.com/q/mvzxHDnaAIkY8fw-TupqqA~~/...` and `sp.rei.com/q/-Lf3Ht-RZAELZVut3PCT1w~~/...`). Non-HTTPS image sources are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy over HTTPS and may drop or warn on mixed content).
