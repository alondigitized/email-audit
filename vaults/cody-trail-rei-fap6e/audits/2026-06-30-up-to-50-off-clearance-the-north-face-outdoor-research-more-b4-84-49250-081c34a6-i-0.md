# Up to 50% off clearance: The North Face, Outdoor Research & more
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance kitchen-sink email — and it knows it. The 50%-off hero is clean and on-brand for a post-4th-of-July clearance push, with The North Face and Outdoor Research named explicitly in the subject. But by the time you scroll through the YETI brand spotlight, the Columbia 25% section, and the REI Outlet 60%-off tease, the email has made four separate asks with four separate discount values. For an outdoor persona every section is technically relevant — nothing is wasted — but the email is trying to close every possible door simultaneously instead of driving hard on one offer.
- Both the YETI member-20%-off spotlight and the REI Outlet 60%-off block are recycled directly from the June 27 and June 29 sends. A loyal subscriber has seen both of those standalone. The post-season clearance hero is the genuinely fresh element here, and it's the strongest one — but it shares real estate with content that already had its moment.
- The execution is professional: photography renders cleanly, product grid shows real merchandise, no broken images. The strategic failure is pacing. This is the sixth REI/REI Outlet email in five days, and the creative is showing the fatigue.

## What's working

- Hero "UP TO 50% OFF Post-season styles" is visually dominant with a clean brand-logo strip (TNF, Patagonia, Columbia, Outdoor Research) anchoring the credibility immediately above it
- Subject line names specific brands rather than hiding behind vague sale language — "The North Face, Outdoor Research" earns opens from outdoor-focused subscribers
- Product grid shows actual merchandise (teal jacket, footwear, rain shell) rather than aspirational lifestyle shots with no product visible
- YETI member benefit is cleanly isolated in its own section — "Members get 20% off any full-price YETI purchase" rewards the subscriber relationship clearly
- REI "Opt Outside" tagline + centennial badge in footer maintains brand consistency even at the very bottom

## What's weak

- Four competing offer sections (50% clearance hero → YETI 20% → Columbia 25% → REI Outlet 60%) dilute focus; the highest-discount offer is buried at the very bottom
- YETI and REI Outlet sections are reprise content — subscribers who opened the June 27 and June 29 sends have already seen these offers in larger, dedicated sends
- "SHOP NOW" appears six or more times across sections with no differentiation — by the third one it reads as wallpaper
- No deadline is visible anywhere; "LIMITED TIME" appears in the REI Outlet block with zero specificity, which is weaker than nothing
- Subject line at 64 characters will truncate on most mobile inboxes before reaching "& more"

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 60% off REI Outlet offer** — it's the sharpest discount in this email and it's buried last; swapping the hierarchy would make the hero feel more urgent
- 2. **Add a hard deadline** — "Ends July 4 at midnight" or "Sale closes Tuesday" anywhere above the fold would convert fence-sitters who otherwise assume this runs all week
- 3. **Differentiate CTA copy per section** — "Shop TNF Clearance," "Unlock YETI 20%," "Shop Columbia Rain Gear" instead of six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons
- 4. **Collapse YETI and Columbia into a single "Brand Deals" row** — the email is too long, and two prior sends already gave each brand its moment
- 5. **Hold one day** — this is send six in five days; one fewer touchpoint this week protects list health more than this incremental coverage adds

`"und"` (undetermined) is not a valid BCP 47 language tag for English content. Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver) use this attribute to select the correct speech synthesis language. Should be `lang="en"` or `lang="en-US"`.
