# We need your help! Vote for how to spend $1M on trails
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine departure from REI's summer promo cycle — no discount, no countdown timer, no urgency. It's a values/community campaign tied to National Public Lands Day (Sep 26), asking recipients to vote on where a $1M trail-fund donation goes, then upselling a limited "Protect Public Lands" merch collection. Compared to the last six sends — all mega-percentage-off or flash-deal countdowns — this reads as a genuine cadence break, and that's a strength for brand trust even though it has zero purchase incentive.
- The design is clean and on-brand: bold hero typography, a US map graphic, and clear "VOTE NOW" / "SHOP THE COLLECTION" CTAs. But there's no discount, no loyalty tie-in, and the ask (vote) competes with the sell (shop merch) in the same email, diluting both. For a shopper conditioned by REI's last 10 emails to expect a percentage off, this will likely get a courtesy open and a shrug-click at best — no offer means no urgency, and the "vote" CTA isn't really shoppable at all.
- Judgement: strong brand-building content, weak revenue engine. Fine as a once-in-a-while goodwill send, but it won't move the needle on clicks-to-purchase the way the flash-deal emails have.

## What's working

- Clear, bold hero statement ("Help us spend $1M on trails") with a strong visual (US trail map) that's instantly legible.
- Concrete, dated hook — National Public Lands Day, Sep 26 — gives the ask a real deadline.
- Two unambiguous buttons ("VOTE NOW" and "SHOP THE COLLECTION") are easy to spot and high-contrast.
- Merch product shot is well-composed — tees, tote, hat, and bottles clearly laid out against an outdoor backdrop.
- No render bugs — layout, images, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Zero purchase incentive — no %/$ off, no member benefit, no free shipping in the entire hero/body.
- Two competing CTAs (vote vs. shop) with no clear primary action — the eye doesn't know which to prioritize.
- "Limited-edition collection" has no price shown anywhere, so there's no way to gauge value before clicking.
- Coming off five straight percentage-off or flash-deal sends, this pivot may register as "not a deal" and get skipped by deal-conditioned openers.
- No loyalty/member callout despite REI Co-op membership being central to the brand (unlike the 2026-08-29 "members get extra 20%" send).

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero action. Either lead with the vote (civic/brand story) and demote merch to a secondary block, or lead with the shoppable collection and frame the $1M donation as supporting context — not two co-equal CTAs.
- 2. Add a price or "from $X" on the merch collection teaser so the shop CTA has some concrete stake before the click.
- 3. Tie the vote to a small member incentive (e.g., "vote and get an extra 10% off the collection") to bridge the goodwill ask into a purchase motive.
- 4. Show the vote options in-email (even 2-3 category icons: "trail restoration," "access," "conservation education") so recipients feel informed before clicking out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vote now: where should REI's $1M trail fund go?`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your vote decides how we spend $1M on trails`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `National Public Lands Day is Sep 26 — cast your vote and shop the limited collection.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Help direct $1M toward trails you care about most.`

## Technical Audit — REI "Vote for how to spend $1M on trails"

### 1. Technical Summary
Email uses MJML-generated responsive markup with Outlook/mso conditional fallbacks; automated QA reports an 11% pass rate driven by systemic link-prober timeouts and a broken deep-link redirect, plus missing authentication/unsubscribe headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 10 of 12 probed material links (SHOP THE COLLECTION, VOTE NOW, Download our app, REI Co-op, Learn more, 100% Satisfaction Guarantee, REI Co-op Mastercard, Sign up for texts, Instagram, Exclusions apply) all returned "The read operation timed out" against `mlinks.rei.com/z/c/24132/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/...` — this is the Cheetah Digital/Marigold click-tracking redirector, and a 100% timeout rate across nearly all links points to the tracking domain being unreachable/slow at check time rather than 10 independent link failures.
- The Unsubscribe link (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?_t=...`) also timed out under the same redirector, which is a compliance-relevant failure (see §5).
- Two instances of a doubled-domain merge error in the "Protect Our Public Lands" CTA deep link:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/rei-x-public-lands?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/s/rei-x-public-lands?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...&is_retargeting=true&af_reengagement_window=30d`
  This is an AppsFlyer OneLink smart-redirect wrapping a full `rei.com` URL inside `af_r`/`deep_link_value` params, each of which independently carries the full `cm_mmc` query string — the QA tool is flagging the nested domain, but on inspection the structure (OneLink → af_r/deep_link_value → target) is a standard deep-link pattern, not necessarily broken. Worth confirming the `?` after `af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/rei-x-public-lands` is correctly encoded, since an unencoded `?` there starts a second query string that gets merged with the outer one and could cause the receiving app/browser to mis-parse `cm_mmc`.

- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` params observed; REI's tracking relies on the proprietary `cm_mmc` scheme instead — internally consistent, but note if any downstream analytics expect standard UTM parameters they won't be populated by these links.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link prober against `mlinks.rei.com` — a 10/12 timeout rate strongly suggests a transient network/redirector issue rather than 10 simultaneously broken links; confirm with a manual curl/browser check before treating each as a discrete bug.
2. Verify the Unsubscribe link resolves independently of the general prober outage — this is compliance-critical and should be checked with retries/longer timeout.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` presence directly at the source ESP (Marigold/Salesforce Marketing Cloud) rather than relying on the AgentMail relay capture, since the relay may simply be stripping these headers.
4. Add `alt` text to the two flagged content images and migrate their `sp.rei.com` sources from `http://` to `https://`.
5. Double-check URL-encoding of the nested `?` in the `af_r`/`deep_link_value` params of the AppsFlyer OneLink CTA to ensure the inner `cm_mmc` query string isn't merged into the outer one.
