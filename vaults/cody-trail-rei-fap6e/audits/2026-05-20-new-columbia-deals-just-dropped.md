# NEW Columbia deals just dropped 😮
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is REI's sixth email in eight days — a Columbia brand spotlight dropped mid-Anniversary Sale. The creative pivot from generic sale messaging ("50% off everything") to a brand-curated lane ("Just in: handpicked Columbia deals") is the right call at this stage of the cadence; it gives subscribers who are Columbia loyalists a reason to open that yesterday's blanket outlet email didn't provide.
- The body is a structured brand drop: Columbia hero at the top, a "must-see deals" product grid, then three secondary offer blocks stacked below (footwear up to 50%, 40% off REI Co-op, a 20%+20% member section, and an "Ends Tomorrow" REI Outlet urgency banner). That's a lot of sections for one email. The Columbia hero earns its position, but everything below it competes equally for attention with no clear hierarchy winner.
- Overall verdict: a smart subject-line pivot on a fatigued cadence. The hero executes cleanly; the lower half bloats the email and risks scroll abandonment before the member-pricing section — which is the most compelling offer for a co-op member.

## What's working

- **Columbia co-branding in the hero** is the strongest creative decision — it segments the audience more precisely than the prior generic Anniversary Sale sends.
- **"Just in: handpicked deals"** framing creates a discovery/curation signal that contrasts well with the volume messaging of May 18 ("6,000+ styles").
- **"Ends Tomorrow" REI Outlet banner** near the bottom injects genuine deadline urgency that the subject line doesn't carry.
- **Up to 50% off footwear** is rendered in a bold, high-contrast block that stops the scroll clearly.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping elements, no lorem ipsum visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject urgency is zero** — "just dropped" is casual discovery language, not a deadline; there's no "ends Sunday" or "48 hours only" hook at the inbox level.
- **Five competing offer sections below the hero** dilute the Columbia story — by the time you hit the footwear block, the brand spotlight has collapsed into another omnibus promo email.
- **"Buy Now" CTAs throughout are generic** — not one button names a category, benefit, or Columbia product type (e.g., "Shop Columbia Rain Gear" would convert better).
- **Yesterday's "Up to 50% off REI Outlet" (scored 9/10) and today's email both feature "Up to 50% off" language** — the offer is no longer surprising, and the urgency of the Ends Tomorrow banner is undercut by sending it two days in a row.
- **Member pricing/20%+20% section is buried below three other blocks** — for co-op members, that's the highest-value offer in the email and it's the fourth section down.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with member pricing or fold it into the Columbia hero** — "Members: 20% off all Columbia gear this weekend" is a stronger personal hook than a generic brand-drop headline. Move it up.
- 2. **Replace all "Buy Now" CTAs with category-specific copy** — "Shop Columbia Jackets," "Find Your Trail Shoe," "Explore Footwear Deals." Specific verb + noun always outperforms bare "Buy Now."
- 3. **Cut the email to three sections max** — Columbia hero + footwear block + Ends Tomorrow urgency. Remove the 40% Co-op and 20%+20% sections into a separate follow-up or the footer.
- 4. **Inject a deadline into the subject line** — "Columbia deals just dropped" reads evergreen; "Columbia deals — ends Sunday" reads like an event.
- 5. **Promote the member-pricing section to the hero subhead** — "REI Members: stack your 20% on top of sale pricing" used as a hero subtitle would make co-op members feel targeted rather than browsed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Columbia picks: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: stack 20% on Columbia this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handpicked Columbia gear at Anniversary Sale pricing — select styles, limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Co-op members get an extra 20% on top of Columbia's sale prices. See what's in.`
