# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is REI at its best — I opened the app and the first thing I see is a guy who looks exactly like me on a ridgeline, holding trekking poles, in a jacket I probably covet. The Anniversary Sale banner is right there, screaming 20% off for members, with a code. REI knows who I am, and this homepage is not shy about it.

## What's working

- **The member savings banner** — "20% off + one Outlet item with code ANNIV26" is exactly the kind of dividend-adjacent perk I joined the co-op for. The specificity (two-item coverage, explicit code, May 25 deadline) makes it feel real, not vague.
- **The hero image** — man with trekking poles, orange hardshell, ridgeline. That's not a runway shot. That's a weekend trip. It speaks directly to the use case I care about.
- **Anniversary Sale 2026 framing** — calling it "Our Biggest Sale of the year" is bold but REI earns it. I know this sale. I plan around this sale. Seeing it confirmed before I even scroll feels like validation.
- **Trade-In in the top nav** — it's right there in the secondary navigation strip, above the logo. That's a signal that REI knows its members trade gear, not just buy it.
- **"Classes & Eve..." in the top nav** — truncated but visible. That's a hook for me since I've done REI navigation classes. Quick access to that is a plus.

## What's weak

- **"Shop now!" is a text link, not a button** — the banner could convert harder with a pill-shaped CTA button. The text link feels like 2012 web design on an otherwise clean mobile page.
- **No re/Supply or Garage Sale callout above the fold** — I actively use the Used Gear / re/Supply program. There's zero mention of it here, even though it fits the thrift-minded, dividend-driven co-op member perfectly. The banner could easily say "or save even more in re/Supply."
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I need to know if my order qualifies. If I'm buying one full-price item to use the 20% code, I want to know if I clear the shipping minimum without adding a filler item.
- **Hero CTA button not visible** — the hero image text says "Our Biggest Sale of the year" but there's no button overlaid on it above the fold. On mobile I'd have to scroll or tap the banner link — a missed tap target.

## Recommendations

- **Add a CTA button to the hero overlay** — "Shop the Anniversary Sale" in REI's signature dark green, positioned just below "Now thru May 25." Don't make me hunt for where to tap.
- **Add a re/Supply mention to the promo banner** — something like "...& browse used gear in re/Supply" right after the Outlet item. It costs one line of copy and captures the thrift-first member segment directly.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold** — add a thin marquee or sub-line under the promo banner: "Free shipping on orders over $X." Remove one decision I have to make at checkout.
- **Swap "Shop now!" text link for a styled pill button** — even a small outlined button in white on the red banner would meaningfully increase tap area and perceived urgency.
