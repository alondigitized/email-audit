# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**

- REI's first screen reads like a brand that gets my outdoor DNA — camp cooking hero with YETI and Zempire, a 50%-off past-season strip right at the top, and Trade-In literally in the first navigation bar. Right now it's pushing spring camp-kitchen season hard, and the co-op value loop (buy quality, trade in, repeat) is at least implied in the nav even if it's not front-and-center. My one gripe: this is car-camping energy, not trail energy — and I'm a backpacker.

## What's working

- **Trade-In in the top nav bar** — I see it immediately and it's the core reason I keep coming back to REI. No other retailer leads with this.
- **"Up to 50% off past-season styles" banner** — As a dividend-driven member who shops off-cycle, this is the headline that makes me actually read the page.
- **YETI × Zempire co-branded hero** — Real brands I recognize, not a vague lifestyle stock photo. "Cooking, covered." is punchy and earns its period.
- **`Shop camp kitchen` CTA** — Unambiguous copy, REI-green button color, correct category placement.
- **Full-width search bar** — "Search for gear & clothing" means I'm one tap from finding exactly what I came for even if the hero doesn't match my niche.

## What's weak

- **No co-op member hook above the fold** — My dividend, member exclusives, or even a "members earn X% back" badge — none of it is visible without scrolling or signing in. For a membership-forward brand, this is a real miss on the first screen.
- **Camp kitchen skews car-camping, not backpacking** — A YETI cooler and a Zempire base-camp stove setup signals "car overlander," not "ultralight JMT prep." The hero doesn't speak to my actual use case.
- **Second panel is horizontally truncated** — The pink running shoe tile on the right is partially cut off; I can see about 40% of it but can't read the copy or tap the CTA without sideways scrolling. Wasted real estate.
- **Zero trust signals in viewport** — No review counts, no free-returns callout, no free-shipping threshold. A member who's comparison-shopping needs at least one of these before tapping into a category.

## Recommendations

- **Add a one-line co-op member badge to the promo banner** — something like "Members earn 10% back" next to the "Up to 50% off" copy. One edit, immediately differentiates REI from every other outdoor retailer.
- **Swap the YETI/Zempire base-camp scene for a trail-kitchen scene with ultralight cookware** — JetBoil or MSR on a granite slab vs. a car-camping spread signals "this is for people who carry their kitchen." That's me.
- **Stack the two hero panels vertically on mobile** — Full-width stacked cards instead of side-by-side tiles so the second panel's copy and CTA are fully legible without horizontal scroll.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the top strip** — Replace the pause-button icon on the promo banner with "Free shipping on $50+" to give value-conscious shoppers the info they're looking for without scrolling to the footer.
