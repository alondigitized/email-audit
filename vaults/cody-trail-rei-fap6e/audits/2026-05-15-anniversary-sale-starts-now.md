# Anniversary Sale starts NOW 🎉
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the payoff to the May 13 teaser ("Anniversary Sale launches May 15 👀") — and REI delivers on the promise. The hero executes the brief cleanly: "Our Biggest Sale of the year" over a lifestyle outdoor shot, flanked by the 20% Co-op member badge. Free shipping is called out in the top banner before a single scroll. The email then merchandises aggressively across sleeping bags (40% off), apparel, and footwear (25% off), closing with a $10-off loyalty kicker.
- The cost of that breadth is focus. By the time you've scrolled past the sleeping bag hero, the Opening Weekend deals grid, the footwear section, the Kith x Orvis collab, and the $10-off incentive, you've seen five distinct calls to action — none sharper than "Shop Now." A subscriber who came for one thing is forced to hunt. The 40% off sleeping bag buried mid-email is the single most compelling hook in the whole send; it should have been the hero.
- Clear judgment: this is a competent, on-brand sale launch — but REI left conversion on the table by not betting on their best deal.

## What's working

- The May 13 teaser set up anticipation; "starts NOW" in the subject delivers the payoff with genuine momentum.
- Free Standard Shipping in the top banner removes the most common abandonment trigger before the reader even sees the offer.
- The Co-op 20% off badge is repeated across the hero, which reinforces member value without requiring a scroll.
- The 40% off sleeping bag is a standout deal that substantiates the "Biggest Sale" claim — specific product, specific discount.
- Visual hierarchy in the hero is clean: headline → badge → CTA, no competing elements.

## What's weak

- Five distinct offer sections (20% hero → 40% bag → Opening Weekend grid → 25% footwear → $10 loyalty) fragment attention rather than driving one decisive click.
- Every CTA reads "Shop Now" — generic across all sections, no category signal to help scanners self-select.
- No explicit sale end date is visible anywhere in the email; "Opening Weekend" is evocative but doesn't create a hard deadline.
- The 40% off sleeping bag — the strongest deal in the email — is mid-scroll, not the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 40% off sleeping bag to the hero.** Lead with the sharpest deal to anchor "Biggest Sale" with a specific proof point. The 20% member discount becomes the supporting claim, not the headline.
- 2. **Add an explicit countdown or end date.** "Ends Monday" or a timer widget converts "Opening Weekend" from flavor copy into actual urgency.
- 3. **Differentiate CTA copy by section.** "Shop Sleeping Bags," "Shop Footwear," "Shop Deals" — each gives scanners a reason to click the section relevant to them.
- 4. **Collapse the email to three sections max.** Hero deal → top-category grid → loyalty kicker. Cut the Kith x Orvis collab or defer it to a follow-up send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping + deals on gear, footwear, and apparel — this weekend only`
