# Save up to 30% on new REI Outlet arrivals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident new-arrivals push from REI Outlet — teal hero, massive "30% OFF" lockup, Patagonia and HOKA brand logos front-and-center, six product images below. The bones are right. REI Outlet knows how to build a promotional email, and this one has no technical failures.
- The strategic problem is math. Subscribers who got the June 5 "up to 50% off" email and the June 12 "EXTRA 20% off 3+ clothing items" member deal are now being asked to get excited about 30% off new stock. "New arrivals" is a legitimate content type, but REI hasn't done the work to reframe the value — the hero copy just drops 30% in a context where the last two emails used 50% as the headline number. That perception gap is the main drag on click intent.
- The email is competent and won't embarrass anyone, but it's doing the minimum required for a new-arrivals send. Nothing here would make a subscriber stop scrolling for long.

## What's working

- **Hero lockup is strong**: dark teal background, "30% OFF" in oversized type, Patagonia + HOKA logos pull recognizable brand equity into the first pixel.
- **Clean product grid**: six product images, consistent photography style, individual "SHOP NOW >" links — well-executed editorial grid.
- **Subject line is accurate and clean**: 42 characters, no ALL CAPS, no emoji spam, reads at a glance.
- **Footer trust layer**: 1-year member returns and 5% card rewards are visible credibility anchors for subscribers who are REI Co-op members.
- **No render failures**: no broken images, no overlapping elements, no placeholder text — this went out clean.

## What's weak

- **30% after 50%**: the June 5 and June 12 sends used deeper or member-stacked discounts; arriving at 30% without reframing the "new arrivals" benefit makes this read as a smaller deal, not a fresh drop.
- **All CTAs are "SHOP NOW"**: eight instances of "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP NOW >" with no noun attached. Nothing tells you whether you're shopping boots, jackets, or poles.
- **No urgency mechanism**: "While supplies last" in fine-print gray does no lift. No deadline, no inventory signal, no countdown.
- **Product grid lacks focus**: six items spanning footwear, trekking poles, and apparel — no through-line category makes it harder to click with intent.
- **No visible preheader**: the email opens directly with navigation links. Any inbox preview text is likely leaking nav copy ("Men | Women | Footwear..."), not campaign copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reframe "new arrivals" as scarcity**: the hero copy already says "check 'em out now for first dibs" — that's the right instinct. Promote it to the headline. "First Dibs: Just Landed at REI Outlet" is a stronger hook than "30% Off New Arrivals" when 50% has been the recent baseline.
- 2. **Fix the preheader**: set an explicit preheader that extends the subject rather than defaulting to nav links. This is a single-line HTML change with measurable open-rate impact.
- 3. **Give CTAs a noun**: "Shop Trail Shoes," "Shop New Jackets" — any noun anchors the click and reduces friction in a multi-category grid.
- 4. **Consolidate the product grid by category**: if the intent is to showcase new footwear arrivals, show only footwear. If apparel, show apparel. Six random items fragment the message.
- 5. **Add a soft deadline**: even "This week only" or "New stock — sell-through is fast" converts "while supplies last" from fine print into a real reason to act now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Just landed at REI Outlet: Patagonia, HOKA & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New outdoor gear just dropped—up to 30% off while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `First dibs on Patagonia, HOKA, Osprey—new arrivals sell fast`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh stock just hit REI Outlet. Shop before it sells out.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue (High): `lang="und"` on root `<html>` element**

```html
<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>
```

`lang="und"` (ISO 639-3 "undetermined") disables screen reader language detection and suppresses some client-side spell-check and hyphenation rendering. For an English-language email this must be `lang="en"`. This is a template-level defect that will affect every send using this template.

**Issue (Medium): Scrollbar suppression at 320px+**

```css
@media only screen and (min-width:320px) {
  body::-webkit-scrollbar { display:none!important }
}
```

Hiding the OS scrollbar in webview-based email clients (Gmail Android, Samsung Mail) can break scroll affordance when content overflows the viewport. This affects usability, not rendering correctness, but is worth flagging.

**Issue (Low): Aggressive `p, a` margin reset on mobile**

```css
@media only screen and (min-width:320px) {
  p, a { margin:0!important }
}
```

Zeroing `<p>` margins globally at 320px+ removes vertical rhythm for all paragraph elements, overriding any component-level spacing. Inline styles or per-component overrides below this breakpoint will be suppressed.

**No issues found:** charset declaration (`utf-8`), viewport meta, MSO conditionals, and `X-UA-Compatible` are all correctly implemented.
