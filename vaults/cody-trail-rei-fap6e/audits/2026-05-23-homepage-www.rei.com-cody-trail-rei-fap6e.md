# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- REI knows exactly who they're talking to right now — a co-op member who's been waiting all year for the Anniversary Sale. The homepage leads with a member-exclusive 20% off code, a legit outdoor lifestyle hero (dude in a shell with trekking poles), and a deadline that actually means something. This feels less like a retailer and more like the co-op sending me a personal heads-up.

## What's working

- **Member-first promo banner** — leading with "Members, it's the **final weekend**" before I even see the hero image signals I'm in the right place. As a member-only-sale shopper this is everything.
- **Trade-In link in the top nav** — I shop re/Supply and trade-in gear; seeing it in the primary nav (not buried in a footer) tells me REI still takes that seriously.
- **Hero model is casting gold** — male, 30s, full technical shell, trekking poles, alpine backdrop. I could swap faces with that dude without a second thought.
- **"Our Biggest Sale of the year" copy** — not overwritten, not cute. Direct. The Anniversary Sale is the one I plan around every year and this confirms it.
- **Urgency is honest** — "Final weekend" on the Anniversary Sale is credible because it's a real REI event I've participated in before. I believe it.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button on the hero** — "Final weekend to save!" is copy, not a tap target. I have to hunt for "Shop now!" in the banner. On mobile, a floating button or a clear hero button would remove friction.
- **No category shortcuts visible** — I can't see any quick-tap tiles for Backpacking, Hiking, Outerwear, or REI Outlet. Everything above the fold funnels me through a single sale gateway, which feels broad.
- **No dividend / reward balance callout** — I'm literally dividend-checking before every Anniversary Sale. If my balance were visible (even a teaser like "You have $XX in dividends"), that would seal the deal.
- **Search bar prominence vs. browse pattern** — search is front and center but sale-mode shoppers browse categories, not search. The layout bets on intent-driven search when the real opportunity is browse-and-discover.

## Recommendations

- **Add a hero CTA button** — replace or supplement "Final weekend to save!" copy with a high-contrast button ("Shop the Anniversary Sale") that taps directly to the sale landing page.
- **Surface dividend balance for signed-in members** — a one-line "You have $42 in dividends — use them now" line in the promo banner would supercharge conversion for people like me who stack dividends on the Anniversary Sale.
- **Drop 3-4 category quick-links below the hero** — Hiking, Backpacking, Camping, REI Outlet — let me self-select instead of making me open the hamburger menu.
- **Swap "Terms apply" to a short inline clarifier** — something like "Members only, one-time use, valid through Sunday" right in the banner. Reduces click-to-understand friction.
