# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is REI firing on all cylinders for me — Anniversary Sale front and center, member code in the banner, and a hero that looks like it was shot on the PCT. As a co-op member, I see my people on this page immediately. They know exactly who pays their dividends.

## What's working

- **Member-exclusive promo banner** — the red "Members, save 20%" strip with code ANNIV26 is the first thing after the logo. As someone who checks his dividend balance quarterly, this tells me showing up here pays off.
- **Hero image casting** — a bearded guy in his 30s, mid-mountain, trekking poles in hand, genuine smile. Not a model in a studio. That's the actual demographic that drives co-op revenue.
- **"Trade-In" in the top nav** — visible in the utility strip alongside Shop REI, REI Outlet, Travel, Classes. I appreciate that re/Supply and the circular economy are baked into navigation, not buried in a footer.
- **Anniversary Sale deadline** — "Now thru May 25" creates real urgency without fake countdown gimmicks. Five days is enough to act, short enough to mean it.
- **REI CO-OP MEMBERS badge** — the co-op identity chip visible at the bottom of the viewport reinforces that this is a member space, not just a retail site.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I'd love to know the free-ship floor before I start adding to cart, especially if I'm pairing a Trade-In credit with an Anniversary purchase.
- **Hero CTA button missing above the fold** — the banner has a text "Shop now!" but the hero itself has no visible CTA button in this viewport. Given I'm on mobile, that's a scroll-to-convert gap.
- **No category shortcuts above the fold** — I'm a backpacker who came to shop Apparel or Packs. There's no quick-tap to "Men's," "Backpacking," or "Outerwear" in the visible area — just the hamburger menu.
- **Classes & Eve[nts] is truncated in top nav** — as someone who signs up for REI Co-op classes, the nav item is cut off and I can't even read what it says without tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Add a hero CTA button** — overlay a "Shop Anniversary Sale" button on the hero image with a subtle dark scrim. Text links get ignored; a button gets tapped.
- **Surface a horizontal category shortcut row** immediately below the hero — four chips: Men's, Women's, Packs & Gear, Re/Supply. One tap to my category instead of hunting the hamburger.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the promo banner** — even a small "| Free shipping $50+" appended to the ANNIV26 line would reduce cart abandonment for me.
- **Run the top nav at full width or stack it** — "Classes & Eve..." truncating on mobile looks like a layout oversight. Scroll horizontally or collapse to icons.
