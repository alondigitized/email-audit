# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-07-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- REI is leaning all the way into "the runners' co-op" right now — big hero, HOKA/Altra/ASICS logos, road-running CTA. As a co-op member who's here to check gear before a weekend trip and maybe cash in my dividend, I'm immediately registering the brand correctly but feeling like I wandered into someone else's aisle. The membership banner up top is doing real work for me, though — that's the one piece that speaks directly to why I'm here.

## What's working

- **Co-op membership banner** — "Become an REI Co-op Member thru 9/7 and earn a $30 single-use promo card" with a real deadline is exactly the kind of signal I scan for. Even as an existing member I'm reading this to see if there's a renewal or referral angle.
- **Trade-In in the top nav** — I spotted it instantly. REI putting Trade-In at the same level as "REI Outlet" tells me the brand gets that I value the gear lifecycle, not just the transaction.
- **"Classes & Eve..." in top nav** — truncated but I know what it is. I've taken REI classes and this being nav-level (not buried) is a trust signal that they're still investing in that.
- **HOKA / Altra / ASICS brand logos in the hero** — even if the campaign isn't for me, I know these are serious performance brands. Signals product credibility.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — my eye moves from the green banner to the hero headline to the CTA without fighting the layout.

## What's weak

- **Hero is the wrong campaign for me** — "Welcome to the runners' co-op" is targeting a different customer. I came here thinking about trail footwear and a new sleeping pad, and the first thing I see is road-running group photos. I'm not bouncing, but I'm also not excited.
- **$30 promo is for new members, not returning ones** — "Join now" is the CTA on that banner. I'm already a member. There's nothing here showing my dividend balance, my current member discount tier, or any "member exclusives this weekend" hook. Huge miss for the dividend-driven co-op crowd.
- **No backpacking or hiking signal anywhere above the fold** — not a single pack, trail, tent, or trekking pole. For a brand whose co-op identity is built on the backcountry, the mobile above-the-fold is 100% road running.
- **Free shipping threshold not visible** — I always want to know if I'm close to free shipping before I commit to browsing. Nothing here.
- **No trust signals visible** — no star ratings, no review counts, no returns callout. If I were evaluating a specific product I'd want to know the return policy immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Member Perks This Week" module directly below the hero** — show my dividend balance or at minimum a banner saying "Member? Here's your exclusive pricing this weekend." That converts existing members like me far better than a "Join now" pitch.
- **Rotate or A/B the hero** — keep the running campaign as one card in the carousel but make the default homepage hero something trail/adventure-adjacent (backpacking launch, re/Supply promo, Garage Sale callout). Road running is one channel; outdoor adventure is the REI identity.
- **Pin a "Trade-In & re/Supply" shortcut above the fold** — it's in the nav but it deserves a visual callout or strip. Members like me are worth more per-visit when that funnel is obvious.
- **Add a free-shipping strip** — a simple "Free shipping on orders over $X for members" line beneath the search bar would increase AOV and reduce checkout abandonment.
