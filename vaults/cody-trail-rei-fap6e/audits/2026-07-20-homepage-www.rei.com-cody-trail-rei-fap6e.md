# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- REI is leaning hard into running right now — HOKA, ALTRA, ASICS logos plastered across the hero, "Welcome to the runners' co-op" as the headline. As a Co-op member who spends weekends with a pack on my back, not road shoes on my feet, this homepage feels like I walked into the wrong department. The Co-op identity language is right, the 50%-off past-season banner is the kind of thing that makes my dividend-driven brain perk up, but the experience is mis-targeted for me.

## What's working

- **"Up to 50% off past-season styles" banner** — this is exactly how I shop. Past-season gear at a discount is how I built out my kit. Taps straight into my deal-first behavior.
- **"Trade-In" in the top nav** — I noticed it immediately. That's a trust signal that REI speaks my language around circular gear ownership.
- **"Welcome to the runners' co-op" headline copy** — the co-op framing is smart brand language even if it's pointed at runners. The word "co-op" always registers with me.
- **Brand logos (HOKA, ALTRA, ASICS) visible** — useful credibility anchors. I know these brands are serious, even if I'm not buying road shoes today.
- **Clean mobile layout** — search bar up top, hamburger menu, sign-in icon, cart — all where I expect them. Zero friction getting oriented.

## What's weak

- **Running hero entirely misses my category** — I'm here for backpacking, outerwear, maybe re/Supply. "Shop road running" as the above-fold CTA is irrelevant to why I opened REI.com.
- **No Co-op member recognition** — I'm a member. I have dividends. There's no "Hey, you have $X to spend" or member-pricing badge anywhere visible. REI knows me; the homepage doesn't act like it.
- **No backpacking, hiking, or camp imagery above the fold** — the partial right-panel shows what might be The North Face but it's cropped and unreadable. My category is invisible.
- **No urgency or deadline on the promo** — "Up to 50% off past-season styles" is compelling but floaty. No end date, no "while supplies last" — doesn't move me to act now.
- **re/Supply / Garage Sale not mentioned** — those are major differentiators for me vs. buying new. A small badge or nav link would go a long way.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or A/B the hero for a backpacking/outdoor adventure scene** — even a carousel that rotates between running and trail/camp would capture me on the first frame without losing the runner segment.
- **Add a member dashboard strip** — a slim banner reading "Your 2025 dividend: $XX — Redeem now" is one of the highest-conversion moves REI could make for returning members. Put it between the promo banner and the hero.
- **Add re/Supply or Garage Sale to the promotional banner** — "Up to 50% off past-season styles + shop re/Supply" doubles down on the circular economy angle that makes REI different from REI Outlet clones.
- **Give the past-season promo a deadline** — even "Ends Sunday" on the red banner creates urgency without feeling manipulative. Right now it reads like a permanent markdown.
