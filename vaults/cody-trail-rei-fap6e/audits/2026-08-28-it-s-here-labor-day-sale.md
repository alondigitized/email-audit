# IT’S HERE: Labor Day Sale ☀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the first genuinely date-anchored Labor Day send in the run — "AUG 28 – SEP 7" is stamped right into the hero, and it finally breaks from the interchangeable "SAVE UP TO 70%" mega-percentage template REI Outlet has leaned on since July. But it immediately reverts to the kitchen-sink structure the brand always falls back on: a hero, then a 25% Off block, then an Outlet-member 20% off block with a promo code, then a product grid, then ANOTHER 25% off block ("Layer up on clothing deals"), then a 40% off tent spotlight. That's four separate percentage-off callouts stacked in one email, which dilutes any single offer rather than reinforcing it.
- The visual is strong at the top — silhouetted hikers at a lake at dusk is on-brand and seasonally appropriate — but by the time a reader scrolls past the third "X% Off" red banner, it reads as noise rather than urgency. The REI Co-op Mastercard footer block (5% back, 1 year to make returns) is a nice loyalty touch that's easy to miss given how far down it sits.
- Net: this is a mid-pack REI Outlet send. It's better than the generic July sends because it has a real campaign hook (Labor Day) and named products, but it repeats the redundant multi-offer stacking problem flagged in the 8/25 and 8/7 audits. Compare to 8/14's "EXTRA 25% off" send, which scored higher precisely because it was cleaner and less repetitive — this one regresses toward the cluttered format.

## What's working

- Clear campaign anchor: "Labor Day Sale AUG 28 – SEP 7" gives a concrete, credible date range not seen in the generic summer sends.
- Hero photography (hikers, lake, dusk light) is seasonally appropriate and higher production value than the flat product-grid summer sends.
- The promo code (LABORDAY26) for the member 20% off is explicit and visible, not buried.
- Named, recognizable brand product callouts (Darn Tough, KUHL, prAna) add specificity vs. generic "outdoor brands."
- Loyalty footer (REI Co-op Mastercard, member benefits) is present and relevant to members.

## What's weak

- Four distinct percentage-off blocks (25%, 20%, 25% again, 40%) in one email — no single hero offer, so nothing anchors as *the* deal.
- "Free Standard Shipping" top banner and "Late Summer Deals" corner badge compete visually with the Labor Day hero for the top-of-email real estate.
- The repeated red "25% Off" banner appears twice with near-identical copy ("Selected REI Co-op Brand Clothing & Gear") — visually and thematically redundant.
- Product grid ("Handpicked deals for you") has flat product-only shots with tiny "SHOP NOW" links — no pricing shown, weakest visual hierarchy in the email.
- Cadence concern: this is yet another "up to X% off" structure days after the 8/26 teaser explicitly promised something different was "Incoming."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut to one hero offer. Pick either the 25% co-op brand discount or the Outlet 20%-off-plus-code and lead with it alone; move the rest into a single secondary block instead of three additional banners.
- 2. Add prices or "was/now" on the "Handpicked deals for you" grid — right now those four products have no visible discount, just a photo and a link.
- 3. Resolve the top-of-email clutter: pick one badge (Labor Day sale) instead of stacking free-shipping banner + hero + "Late Summer Deals" corner tag.
- 4. Move the loyalty/Mastercard block higher — it's a real differentiator for a Co-op member and it's currently buried below six other content blocks.
- 5. Follow through on the 8/26 teaser's promise of something different — this format is nearly identical to the 8/7 and 8/25 sends it was supposedly previewing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day Sale is live: save on gear thru 9/7`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day gear deals start now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25% off co-op brands, plus an extra 20% for members`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tents, socks, layers — all marked down thru Labor Day`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
MJML-generated REI HTML renders structurally sound, but the automated QA run shows a 6% pass rate driven almost entirely by link-probe timeouts and merge-tag/deep-link doubling across nearly every tracked CTA.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 tracked links (mlinks.rei.com redirector) timed out on probe**, including primary CTAs `"SHOP THE SALE"`, `"SHOP NOW"`, `"SHOP REI OUTLET"`, five `"Shop Now"` instances, and the `"Unsubscribe"` link itself. Timeouts on the redirect layer (`mlinks.rei.com/z/c/...` and `/a/zc/...`) mean these could not be confirmed to resolve — evidence is inconclusive (timeout ≠ confirmed broken) but warrants re-probe given the volume (12/12 material links failed).
- **13 "doubled-domain" merge errors** in `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` deep-link URLs: the `af_r` param embeds a full `https://www.rei.com/...` URL, and `cm_mmc` is duplicated verbatim between the outer query string and the `deep_link_value`/`af_r` nested params, e.g.:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/?cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-...&deep_link_value=/nav?view=home&cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...`
  This is a templating/merge-tag defect in the AppsFlyer deep-link generator — `cm_mmc` should appear once, not duplicated between the outer and nested query strings.
  Affected CTAs: nav_logo, nav_mnsdls, nav_wmnsdls, nav_dls, and multiple product CTAs (`CTA1`–`CTA5` across several blocks, including two exact duplicate URLs for `26_2_402_09849_z_CTA1`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images missing `alt` text (tracking pixel/spacer images: `cJfLhcr3PO...`, `o.gif`, `eo`, `YdJlQ-KMbO...`). The `o.gif` and `eo` pixels are tracking beacons and low-priority, but the two `sp.rei.com` images should have `alt=""` at minimum for screen-reader compliance.
- 2 images served over **`http://`** instead of `https://` (`sp.rei.com/q/PiNUw_L65-...` and `sp.rei.com/q/KrzKYSWpnm...`) — non-HTTPS resources risk being blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- MJML boilerplate (Outlook conditionals, MSO fallbacks, responsive `@media` blocks) is present and structured correctly; no malformed table/tag issues observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No first-name or account-level merge tokens observed in the truncated source. The doubled `cm_mmc` parameter (Section 2) is technically a merge-tag/templating defect rather than a personalization-token defect — flagged there.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Unsubscribe link is present (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`) but **timed out on probe**, so it cannot be confirmed functional.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing headers rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found** — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via this relay; cannot verify authentication.
- Given the header gaps are flagged as relay-side limitations by the QA tool itself, treat these as **unconfirmed**, not as confirmed compliance failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking params (`cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260828_OFP_LDS_KO_z_AM_C-_-...`, `pid=email_com_gm_it`, `c=20260828_OFP_LDS_KO_z_AM_C`) are consistently applied across CTAs — naming convention is coherent.
- The doubled-domain/doubled-param defect (Section 2) directly threatens landing-page continuity: if `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` mis-parses the duplicated `cm_mmc` or the embedded `af_r` URL, users could land on `rei.com` without correct attribution or deep-link routing (e.g., landing on homepage instead of `/c/mens-clothing/f/scd-deals`).
- Plain-text version is **>70% URLs** (12,404/16,056 chars), which may trigger spam filters and adds no continuity risk directly but is worth flagging in deliverability.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 12 timed-out `mlinks.rei.com` links (including Unsubscribe) with a longer timeout/retry to distinguish true breakage from transient redirector latency.
2. Fix the AppsFlyer/`AkCd` deep-link template to emit `cm_mmc` once — either in the outer query string or nested in `af_r`/`deep_link_value`, not both — across all 13 affected CTAs.
3. Deduplicate the two identical `26_2_402_09849_z_CTA1` URLs — likely a copy-paste error in the template mapping.
4. Change the two `sp.rei.com` image sources from `http://` to `https://`.
5. Add empty `alt=""` attributes to the two non-tracking-pixel images missing alt text.
6. Confirm with the sending platform (or a direct SMTP capture) whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present — the AgentMail relay may be stripping them rather than them being absent at origin.
7. Reduce URL density in the plain-text alternative to lower spam-filter risk.
