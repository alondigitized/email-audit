# It’s yours: an EXTRA 20% off 1 item at REI Outlet thru 9/7
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the members-only member-benefit angle finally showing up — "MEMBERS GET an extra 20% off one item" with the LABORDAY26 code is genuinely different from the mega-percentage kitchen-sink hero REI Outlet has run all summer, including the 2026-08-28 Labor Day send. But it doesn't stay disciplined: right below the exclusive-feeling hero and product grid, it drops into the exact same "UP TO 50% OFF" Patagonia/TNF/REI/Beyond Yoga block, then a Yakima/Thule rack push, then a footwear brand block, then a second full Labor Day banner. Five distinct offers stacked in one email is fatigue, not variety.
- The hero itself is legible and the offer is concrete (20% off one item, code visible, expiry date visible), which is more than most of this brand's recent sends manage. But burying a single-item 20%-off deal under four other unrelated promos undercuts the "yours" personalization framing in the subject line — nothing after the hero reinforces exclusivity or the code.
- Judgment: a good hero concept dragged down by the same bloated, undifferentiated body this brand runs every time. Worth the open, not worth trusting as "personalized."

## What's working

- Clear, singular hero offer: "An extra 20% off one item" with a bold red 20% badge — easy to parse at a glance.
- Promo code (LABORDAY26) is visible in the hero, not buried below the fold.
- "Members only. Online only." fine print is present right under the CTA, not hidden in the footer.
- Product grid below the hero is clean — 6 items, consistent thumbnail sizing, individual "Shop Now" links.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean end-to-end.

## What's weak

- Five separate promo blocks (member 20% off, 50% off past-season, Yakima/Thule racks, fall footwear, second Labor Day banner) turn one email into a flyer dump — no single dominant CTA survives.
- The second "Labor Day Sale AUG 28–SEP 7" banner near the bottom directly duplicates the top hero's dates/offer with a different visual treatment — confusing about which is the "real" deal.
- "One item" restriction isn't reinforced anywhere in the product grid — none of the 6 grid products are flagged as eligible, so it's unclear if this code applies site-wide or just to featured items.
- The member benefit framing (20% off) gets diluted by a non-member "up to 50% off" block one scroll down — a member reading this may wonder why the generic sale offer looks bigger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two sections max: the member 20%-off hero + product grid, and one secondary block (pick either footwear or racks, not both) — this is the single biggest lift, since offer-stacking is this brand's most consistent problem across every send I've reviewed.
- 2. Tag at least 2-3 grid products as "eligible for your 20% off" so the code has an obvious landing spot.
- 3. Delete the duplicate bottom Labor Day banner — it competes with the hero for the same real estate/date range.
- 4. Move "Members only" language into the subject or make it bolder in-hero (e.g., a small membership badge) to justify the "It's yours" personalization framing.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via MJML-generated tables with standard Outlook/MSO fallbacks, but nearly every tracked link times out on probe and several CTAs route through a broken deep-link redirector that double-embeds the destination URL as a query parameter.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed material links timed out** ("The read operation timed out") across both `mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/...` and `mlinks.rei.com/z/c/...` redirector paths, including the primary CTAs "SHOP REI OUTLET" and "Shop Now" (e.g. `https://mlinks.rei.com/a/zc/24132/7d9ff52392a74cbb9b843386e1aca60a/1ff279ab49614e4887b16b6f321441fa`). This indicates the ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud click-tracking redirector (`mlinks.rei.com`) is unresponsive or blocking automated probing — cannot confirm final destinations resolve.
- **Doubled-domain merge error on 13 links**: URLs built on `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` (an AppsFlyer OneLink smart-redirect) pass `af_r` and `deep_link_value` params that both contain a full `https://www.rei.com/...` URL with its own querystring appended raw, e.g.:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-coupon-eligible-items?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/s/outlet-coupon-eligible-items?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...`
  The unescaped `?` inside `af_r` and `deep_link_value` truncates the intended nested query string and merges it with the OneLink parent params (`pid`, `c`, `is_retargeting`, etc.), corrupting both the deep-link target and the `cm_mmc` attribution string. These values should be URL-encoded (`encodeURIComponent`) before concatenation.
- The Unsubscribe link is also routed through the same `mlinks.rei.com` redirector and also timed out during probing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images missing `alt` text: two `sp.rei.com` tracking/content pixels (`HpyO6bOe9...`, `xiWuk0I3eL...`), the `mi.rei.com` open-tracking pixel (`o.gif`), and the `mlinks.rei.com/e/eo` tracking pixel. Tracking pixels typically warrant `alt=""` rather than a missing attribute for stricter HTML validators.
- 2 images served over plain `http://` instead of `https://` (`sp.rei.com/q/N-ybn_RNzQmb6az3Az-9Rg~~/...` and `sp.rei.com/q/PZwGIfks3TxQqqMMgJ99lA~~/...`) — likely to be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS image loading.
- MJML/Outlook conditional boilerplate (`mso-hide`, VML namespaces, `.mj-*` responsive classes) is present and structured normally; no malformed conditional comments observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge-token placeholders (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) or unresolved personalization fields were present in the visible source or QA output. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — one-click unsubscribe header absent (or not captured by the relay); an in-body Unsubscribe link exists but it routes through `mlinks.rei.com/e/eh` (also timed out on probe).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe via POST is not confirmed supported.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay.
- Note: the missing-header findings carry a caveat that the AgentMail relay may simply not be capturing/forwarding these headers — treat as unconfirmed rather than definitive absence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking uses `cm_mmc` (e.g. `email_com_gm_it-_-20260829_OUT_LDS_Cou_KO_z-_-202608290911-_-26_2_403_09874_z_CTA1`) rather than standard `utm_*` params — internal REI convention, consistent across all CTAs, no mismatch detected.
- As noted in §2, the `cm_mmc` value is at risk of truncation/corruption on the 13 `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` OneLink CTAs due to unencoded nested query strings, which would break landing-page attribution matching even if the link itself resolves.
- Underlying destination paths (`/s/outlet-coupon-eligible-items`, `/f/scd-deals`, `/s/yakima-thule-car-rack-deals`, `/b/yakima/f/scd-deals`, `/b/thule/f/scd-deals`, `/s/footwear-new-arrivals`, `/outlet`) appear topically aligned with the email's outlet/Labor Day sale content — no mismatch identified, but cannot confirm actual page load since links were not resolvable during probing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. URL-encode the `af_r` and `deep_link_value` parameter values on all `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` OneLink CTAs so the nested `https://www.rei.com/...?cm_mmc=...` targets aren't truncated/merged with the parent querystring (13 instances flagged).
2. Investigate `mlinks.rei.com` redirector availability — 100% of probed links (12 material + unsubscribe) timed out, which blocks both automated QA and potentially real subscriber clicks if the issue is server-side rather than a probe artifact.
3. Add `alt=""` to the 4 tracking-pixel/decorative images currently missing `alt` attributes.
4. Serve the two `sp.rei.com` images over `https://` instead of `http://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in mail clients.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and DKIM/SPF authentication results are actually present on the raw sent message (independent of this relay's capture) to verify CAN-SPAM and deliverability compliance.
