# Last day: Up to 50% off at REI Outlet
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the finale send of the three-day flash sale REI Outlet launched May 19 (scored 9/10 here). The mission is simple: convert fence-sitters with a hard deadline. The countdown timer — 14:54:34 — is the strongest urgency asset in the entire Anniversary Sale sequence so far, and it earns its place above the fold. Against that, the rest of the email is largely recycled: same headline, same offer depth, same product grid structure as two days ago. If you opened the May 19 send and didn't buy, this one gives you no new argument — just a louder clock.
- The membership savings block ("25% Off Selected Women's/Men's Clothing") is the one move that pushes beyond the May 19 template. Framing last-day urgency around a member-exclusive benefit is the right instinct — it turns a generic "sale ends soon" into a "don't leave your co-op benefit on the table" moment. That angle deserved to be higher on the page.
- The bottom cross-promo ("Biggest Sale of the Year / 20% / 20% / 20%") is clutter that fragments the conversion path at the worst possible moment. You're a subscriber with a countdown in your face — don't give them an exit ramp to a different sale.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer + "ENDS TODAY" banner** is the sharpest single urgency signal in the sequence — more concrete than the soft "3 days only" from May 19.
- **Membership savings section** activates co-op identity at the right moment — member guilt is a real conversion lever on deadline sends.
- **"25% Off All $225 Footwear"** is a specific sub-offer with a real price filter; easier to act on than the blanket "up to 50% off."
- **Visual hierarchy holds** — hero countdown first, product grid second, member benefits third; the eye doesn't wander.
- **No render bugs** — all product images load cleanly, the CTA button has solid color contrast.

## What's weak

- **Identical offer, identical framing to May 19.** Subscribers who resisted twice haven't been given a new reason to say yes.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a wasted CTA** on a deadline email — the clock is the asset; the button copy should name it.
- **Anniversary Sale cross-promo at the bottom** hands the reader an escape hatch to a different funnel at exactly the wrong moment.
- **No featured "last chance" hero product** — a countdown is a natural home for one specific item at a sharp price; instead it's another anonymous product grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Insert a single "last chance" hero product** — one item (boot, jacket, tent) with original price, sale price, and a credible "only X left" stock signal. Converts urgency into specificity.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to "Claim Your 50% Off" or "Shop Before Midnight" — the deadline is the pitch; put it on the button.
- 3. **Surface the membership block above the product grid.** Member-exclusive pricing is REI's strongest differentiator; it should be the second thing you see after the countdown, not the fifth.
- 4. **Cut the Anniversary Sale cross-promo entirely.** If it must appear, move it below the footer rule or into a post-purchase flow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Member deals + 50% off end tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends at midnight — 50% off Outlet + your member bonus`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member-exclusive 25% off clothing disappears at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 50% off window closes today — here's what's still in stock`
