# Save up to 70% selected styles. Yep, that’s 7-0.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet running the identical template it's used all July — massive "SAVE UP TO 70%" hero, four named brand logos, a "Deals picked just for you" grid, closing on "SHOP ALL THE DEALS." Compared to the July 30 send ("Just in! Up to 70% off... thru 8/3," 8.00/10), this is the same offer, the same end date (8/3), and nearly the same brand roster (KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering swapped in for one of last time's names) — this reads like a same-campaign resend two days later, not a new push.
- Execution is clean — no render bugs, clear CTA, decent product variety across the four tiles (leggings, base layer, tee, climbing shoe) — but the "picked just for you" tiles have no prices and no product names, so the personalization claim is undercut by generic imagery. The $30 bonus-card banner at top is a nice member hook but it's boilerplate REI has run repeatedly.
- Judgment: solid execution, weak novelty. A subscriber who opened the July 30 email will recognize this immediately and may skip it as a duplicate.

## What's working

- The 70% hero number is huge, high-contrast, and impossible to miss — clear visual anchor.
- Four recognizable brand logos (KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering) build immediate credibility for the outdoor persona.
- Clean 2x2 product grid with real, distinct product photography (leggings, top, tee, climbing shoe) and no broken images or layout issues.
- Deadline is explicit and repeated twice ("Ends 8/3") — both in hero copy and fine print.
- Member benefits (returns policy, Co-op Mastercard 5% rewards, $30 bonus card) are all visible without scrolling far.

## What's weak

- No prices or discount percentages on any of the four "picked for you" tiles — just "SHOP NOW" under bare product shots, so there's no actual click hook per item.
- This is a near-copy of the July 30 email (same 70%, same 8/3 deadline, same layout) sent just two days later — cadence fatigue risk is real.
- Subject's "Yep, that's 7-0" gimmick doesn't appear anywhere in the rendered body — the joke is subject-only and adds nothing visually.
- "Deals picked just for you" claims personalization but the products look like generic catalog fill (a climbing shoe next to leggings and a base layer isn't a coherent "for you" set).
- Model diversity is thin — only one product tile shows a person, limiting sizing/fit context for the other three items.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the near-duplicate cadence.** If this is the same 70%/8-3 promo as July 30, either escalate the offer (an added coupon, extra %, or new brand) or hold it — sending twins two days apart trains subscribers to skip.
- 2. **Add price or % off directly on each product tile.** "SHOP NOW" with no number is the single biggest missed click opportunity in the body — even "$29.93 (60% off)" under each image would double the tile's pull.
- 3. **Make "picked just for you" actually feel curated** — group by category (all climbing, or all apparel) rather than mixing legwear, base layer, tee, and climbing shoe randomly.
- 4. **Pay off the subject line's joke in the hero.** If "Yep, that's 7-0" is the hook, echo it visually near the 70% — right now the subject over-promises personality the email doesn't deliver.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Climbing shoes, base layers, and leggings — all marked down.`

# Technical Audit: REI Outlet — "Save up to 70%" (2026-07-31)

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 10% pass rate with 15 failures, driven by systemic link-probe timeouts across all material CTAs and a URL-encoding defect in the mobile deep-link redirector; compliance headers could not be confirmed via the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12/12 probed material links timed out** ("The read operation timed out") on the `mlinks.rei.com` redirector (Cheetah/Salesforce Marketing Cloud link-wrapping domain), including the primary CTAs `SHOP NOW`, `SHOP ALL THE DEALS`, and the header logo link. This affects both `/a/zc/` and `/z/c/` path variants, suggesting the redirector endpoint itself was unreachable or rate-limiting probes at check time rather than an issue isolated to one link.
- **Malformed deep-link URLs (3 occurrences)** on `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&pid=...&c=...`. The `af_r` and `deep_link_value` parameter values are raw, unencoded URLs that themselves contain `?` and `&`. This causes the nested query string (`cm_mmc`, a second `cm_mmc`, `pid`, `c`, `is_retargeting`, `af_reengagement_window`) to be parsed as top-level parameters of `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` instead of being scoped inside `af_r`/`deep_link_value`. Flagged by QA as "repeated domain" (`mobile.rei.com` ... `rei.com` appearing twice) — root cause is missing percent-encoding of the nested URL values, not a template duplication bug.
- Unsubscribe link resolves through the same `mlinks.rei.com/e/eh` redirector pattern as the timed-out links — same underlying availability risk applies to opt-out.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 4 images missing `alt` text: two are 1x1/tracking pixels (`eo`, `o.gif` — acceptable to omit alt on pure trackers), but two are content images (`yWFbyTVj...`, `D-Tuy8X6X...`) served from `sp.rei.com` with no `alt` attribute — screen readers and image-blocked clients will render nothing for these.
- Same two content images are served over **`http://`** (`http://sp.rei.com/q/...`), not `https://`. Mail clients that enforce mixed-content/TLS policies (Outlook, Gmail proxy) may block or fail to render these.
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and Outlook-specific fixes (`mso-table-lspace`, `.mj-outlook-group-fix`) are present — indicates MJML-generated markup with standard Outlook fallbacks in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `<html dir="auto" lang="und" ...>` — `lang="und"` (ISO 639-2 "undetermined") indicates the locale/language merge token was not resolved to an actual language code (e.g., `en-US`) at send time. This is a template/personalization defect, not a valid production value, and will cause screen readers/translation tools to mis-handle the document language.
- No other unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FIELD%%`, `{{token}}`) were visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- Visible unsubscribe link is present in-body (`Unsubscribe` → `mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`), satisfying CAN-SPAM's visible opt-out requirement.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found. `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click): not found. QA notes both may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay — **inconclusive, not confirmed absent**.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — same relay caveat applies; sender authentication status is unconfirmed rather than failing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking (`cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260731_OUT_T70_Eng_z_Jul-_-...`) is consistently applied across CTA and image-link variants, with distinct placement tags (`z_CTA1`, `z_Image1`), suggesting correct per-placement attribution design.
- The mobile deep-link encoding defect (Section 2) directly threatens continuity: because `af_r`'s nested query string breaks out into the parent URL, the landing destination (`/s/outlet-handpicked-deals`) and its `cm_mmc` campaign tag may not survive the AppsFlyer/OneLink redirect intact, risking loss of attribution or a broken landing experience for app-deep-link clicks specifically (desktop/web CTA links use the separate `mlinks.rei.com` wrapper and aren't affected by this encoding issue).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the `mlinks.rei.com` links from a second network path/time window to confirm whether the timeout is transient infra flakiness vs. a genuine outage before treating all 12 CTAs as broken.
2. Percent-encode the `af_r` and `deep_link_value` values in the `mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` deep links so the nested URL's query string doesn't merge with the parent's.
3. Add `alt` text to the two non-pixel content images from `sp.rei.com`, and serve them over `https://`.
4. Set `lang` to a real locale (e.g., `en-US`) instead of `und` at template render time.
5. Independently verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` via raw headers (outside the AgentMail relay) before concluding they're absent from the actual send.
