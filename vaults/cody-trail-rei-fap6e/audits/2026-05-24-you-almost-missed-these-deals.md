# You ALMOST missed these deals 😮‍💨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- REI is in deep fourth-quarter mode on the Anniversary Sale. This is now the third "almost over" send in a week, and the creative is showing fatigue — same red-on-red hero, same "So many deals, so little time" copy, and once again a countdown timer that has rendered out to `0:00:00` in the hero. That's the second zeroed-out timer I've audited from REI in three days. If a real subscriber sees this, the urgency mechanic doesn't just fail — it actively undermines trust ("the sale already ended, why is this in my inbox?").
- Below the broken hero, the merchandising is actually decent: 20% off Anniversary Sale, 40% off select HOKA, 25% off other HOKA styles, plus discrete product tiles with real percentages. Solid information density for someone deep in the funnel. But the lead asset is the one people see first, and it's broken.
- Net: the offer stack is fine, the execution is tired and the timer bug is now a pattern. Mid-pack.

## What's working

- Multiple concrete offers stacked: 20% off sale, 40% off HOKA, 25% off select styles.
- HOKA-specific blocks with branded product shots — strong category signal for runners/walkers.
- Co-op Member badge visible on offers, reinforcing loyalty value.
- "Run on SALE to them while you can" tile gives a clear category to dive into.

## What's weak

- Countdown timer in the hero reads `0:00:00` — a real, visible render bug for the second time this week.
- Hero copy ("So many deals, so little time") is verbatim recycled creative from earlier sends.
- Subject leans on the same emoji-driven panic pattern ("😮‍💨") that's been used three times in five days — fatigue is real.
- Visual hierarchy is muddy below the hero — three discount tiers compete instead of leading with the strongest.
- No specific deadline date/time anywhere visible in copy — only the broken timer was carrying that weight.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the timer.** Either ship a working countdown or replace with a static "Ends Monday 5/26 at 11:59pm PT" date stamp. A zeroed timer is worse than no timer.
- 2. **Lead with HOKA, not the generic red hero.** The HOKA 40% off block is the most concrete offer in the email — promote it to the hero and let the sale umbrella sit below.
- 3. **Drop the emoji panic in subject.** Three "😮‍💨/😰/😭" sends in a week trains subscribers to mute.
- 4. **Add a real deadline in copy.** "Sale ends Monday night" beats a broken timer every time.
