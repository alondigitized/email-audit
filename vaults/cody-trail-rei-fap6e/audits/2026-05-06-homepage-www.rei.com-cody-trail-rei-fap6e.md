# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- REI is leading with a camp kitchen push — YETI, Zempire, OXO Outdoor, and a solid lifestyle shot of food actually being cooked outside. As a co-op member who spends weekends car camping and backpacking, this speaks to me more than a shoe wall. That said, the homepage is playing informational not transactional: there's no member pricing, no dividend reminder, no offer — just a "come look at gear" vibe.

## What's working

- **Camp kitchen hero**: "Cooking, covered." + YETI/Zempire/OXO logos does exactly what a co-op-member weekend camper wants to see in May. Spring camping season, relevant brands, lifestyle shot that isn't staged to death.
- **"Shop camp kitchen" CTA**: Dark green, high-contrast, unambiguous. Doesn't say "Explore" or "Discover" — it says shop. I'd tap it.
- **Top nav "Trade-In" link**: Visible without scrolling. As someone who buys outerwear once and repairs twice, Trade-In is one of REI's actual differentiators. Having it in the top rail without hunting is a win.
- **"Classes & Eve..." in top nav**: Truncated but visible — co-op classes are a real hook for me. The navigation at least gestures toward it.
- **2025 Impact Report banner**: I actually care about this. Co-op member, dividend-driven — seeing that REI published an impact report above the fold signals they still take the mission seriously.

## What's weak

- **No membership/dividend callout above the fold**: For a co-op, this is the whole point. I don't see my rewards balance, a "member-exclusive pricing" badge, or any hook reminding me why I'm shopping here instead of Backcountry or REI Outlet.
- **Second carousel card is half-cropped**: I can see a pink running shoe and "The ne..." — probably "The new [something]" — but it's cut off. That's dead inventory on screen. Either show it or don't.
- **No offer or price signal**: Not a single dollar amount or percentage off anywhere above the fold. REI Member Sales are one of the main reasons I shop here. If one is running, I can't tell.
- **Sign In is icon-only**: The person icon says "SIGN IN" in tiny text beneath it. For a returning member, a more prominent "Sign In to see member pricing" hook would immediately separate REI from a generic gear retailer.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member-pricing strip above the hero**: One line — "Co-op members save an extra 20% on camp kitchen" or "Member Dividend: $X waiting for you" — turns this from a browse into a mission.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold**: "$50 ships free" or "Free shipping for members" in the sub-nav or hero caption. I'm calculating whether to add to my cart right now and I don't have the info I need.
- **Fully reveal the second carousel card or cut it**: A half-visible pink shoe next to a camp kitchen hero creates visual confusion. Either let it breathe as a full card or push it below the fold.
- **Move "Trade-In" into the hero copy cadence**: Something like "Gear up. Trade in what you've got." below "Cooking, covered." would tie REI's unique circular-gear value prop into the main story instead of burying it in nav.
