# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is REI firing on all cylinders for me specifically — the Anniversary Sale banner is the first thing I read, it calls out my member discount by name, and the hero model is basically me in five years with better poles. The brand is pushing its biggest annual sale hard and making zero apologies about it being a member-first event.

## What's working

- **The member-specific promo banner** — "Members, save 20%..." leading with my status before I even hit the hero? That's good CRM. It reads like REI knows I'm already in the co-op and is rewarding me for it.
- **The hero model** — bearded guy in an orange hooded puff, poles up, ear-to-ear grin at altitude. That's aspirational for me without being a gear bro flexfest. Relatable and appealing.
- **"Trade-In" in the top nav** — I spotted it immediately. That's my re/Supply reflex. One tap to unload old gear for credit is exactly how I shop REI.
- **"Anniversary Sale 2026 — Our Biggest Sale of the year"** — zero ambiguity. No lifestyle poetry, just a clean statement of what's happening and when.
- **"Now thru May 25"** — specific deadline, no dark-pattern fake urgency, just a real calendar date.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always check whether I'm near free-ship before adding to cart. It's not here, which means I'd have to hunt for it mid-checkout.
- **No product or category shown** — the entire above-the-fold is a sale wrapper with no specific product hint. I don't know if this means tents, shoes, or base layers are on sale. I want a teaser.
- **"Classes & Eve..." is truncated** in the top nav — I take REI classes and that's meaningful to me, but it's cut off and feels like an afterthought on mobile.
- **No dividend / annual member reward callout** — the 20% coupon is great, but no mention of my actual co-op dividend balance or what this sale means for my year-end equity.
- **Hero has no scroll indicator or secondary CTA** — there's nothing nudging me to swipe or explore beyond the sale announcement.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop by Activity" strip just below the fold** — Backpacking, Camping, Climbing, Cycling. One row of icon tiles. I can find my category in a single tap instead of opening the hamburger nav.
- **Surface a specific deal teaser in the hero** — even "Up to 40% off trekking poles + packs" would give me a reason to tap immediately rather than just noting the sale and wandering.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in the banner** — something like "Members save 20% · Free shipping over $50." One line, zero extra real estate, huge reduction in checkout abandon.
- **Replace the truncated "Classes & Eve..." nav label with a visible "Classes" chip** — or surface the REI Experiences callout as a module earlier in the scroll for members. That's a differentiator no one else has.
