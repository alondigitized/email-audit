# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is REI firing on all cylinders for the Anniversary Sale — and honestly, as a co-op member, this is exactly the email-that-becomes-a-bookmark I was waiting for. The hero is a bearded dude with trekking poles and an orange puffer on a mountain, the member-exclusive promo code is plastered above the fold, and the sale end date is four days out. REI knows who its customer is, and this page looks like it was built for me.

## What's working

- **Member promo banner** — the red strip with ANNIV26 and "Members, save 20%" is above the search bar. I see it the moment the page loads. That's a co-op member's first dopamine hit.
- **Hero casting** — a bearded outdoorsman in an orange puffer with trekking poles on a ridge is not accidental. That's me on a weekend. REI knows who's spending money at this sale.
- **Sale urgency** — "Now thru May 25" with today being May 21 creates real scarcity without a gimmicky countdown timer. Clean and credible.
- **Trade-In in the top nav** — "Trade-In" is literally visible in the tab bar. That's a core behavior for a dividend-and-reuse shopper like me, and it's top-level real estate.
- **REI Co-op Members seal** — the bottom-left badge reinforces that this sale has a membership tier that matters. It signals that being a member is a different experience, not just a loyalty points grind.
- **"REI Outlet" in the nav AND in the promo** — double exposure for the channel where I actually shop for deals.

## What's weak

- **No hero CTA button** — the "Shop now!" is a tiny inline text link inside the red banner. The big hero image has no overlaid button. I have to hunt the tap target.
- **Category navigation buried** — the top nav shows "Shop REI | REI Outlet | Trade-In | Travel | Classes & Eve…" — but "Backpacking," "Outerwear," or "Footwear" aren't one-tap accessible. I have to open the hamburger to find my department.
- **"Classes & Eve..." is truncated** — I can't actually read the full nav item. As someone who shows up for REI Co-op classes, that truncation is a miss on mobile.
- **No free-shipping callout** — for a "Biggest Sale of the year" push, no mention of free shipping or order threshold. That would close the loop on why to buy online vs. in-store.
- **No product tiles** — the screenshot is pure hero + banner. I can't see any product imagery or categories to click into. It's a great billboard but it doesn't show me what's on sale.

## Recommendations

- **Add a full-bleed CTA button on the hero** — "Shop the Anniversary Sale" in REI's dark green, overlaid at the bottom of the hero image. The "Shop now!" text link is too easy to miss on a phone thumb.
- **Untruncate or restructure the top nav** — either shorten "Classes & Events" to "Classes" or move it to the hamburger, so "Shop REI | Outlet | Trade-In | Travel | Classes" all render readable on a 375px viewport.
- **Surface two or three category tiles below the hero** — Backpacking, Outerwear, Footwear — so the sale click path is one more tap, not a nav excavation.
- **Drop a free-shipping threshold line in the promo banner** — "Free shipping on orders $50+" takes 15 characters and eliminates a checkout hesitation for an online-first sale push.
