# Homepage snapshot · www.rei.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- REI's homepage is doing what REI does best — leaning into the outdoor lifestyle with a clean, confident brand voice. Right now they're pushing camp kitchen with a YETI/Zempire/OXO Outdoor co-branded hero, which honestly lands well for me. It reads like a brand that knows its member base, even if it's not explicitly waving my dividend in my face above the fold.

## What's working

- **"Cooking, covered." hero:** Pithy, confident, Millennial-coded copy. Doesn't over-explain. I felt it immediately.
- **YETI + Zempire + OXO brand logos:** Three brands I actually respect stacked in one frame — that's a shortcut to trust for a gear nerd like me.
- **"Shop camp kitchen" CTA:** Unambiguous. Green against dark. Tells me exactly where I'm going.
- **Top nav Trade-In link:** Visible in the utility bar — exactly the kind of co-op ecosystem feature that keeps me loyal. Not buried.
- **Impact Report banner:** As a co-op member who votes in the annual meeting, I actually care that REI shipped a 2025 Impact Report. This lands differently for me than it would for a casual shopper.

## What's weak

- **No dividend or member pricing signal above the fold:** As a dividend-driven co-op member, I want to see something that reminds me why I'm a member — even "member price" badges on featured products would do it.
- **Camp cooking skews car-camping, not backpacking:** The camp stove / cookware scene reads glamping or car camping. As someone who backpacks on weekends, I'm slightly adjacent but not fully targeted — a trail meal or ultralight setup shot would've been sharper.
- **Impact Report banner eats prime real estate:** The red bar is the first thing my eye hits below the logo. I appreciate the mission content, but it's not a shopping hook — it delays my path to product.
- **No people in the hero:** Nobody using the gear. A shot with someone actually cooking at a site would make it feel aspirational rather than editorial.
- **Partial second card (Garmin Forerunner pre-order):** Barely visible, cropped — this fragment creates noise without payoff. Either show it or hide it.

## Recommendations

- **Add a member-price badge or dividend callout to the hero block:** Even a small "Members save more — sign in" chip would remind me why I'm a co-op member and not just a REI.com visitor.
- **Swap the overhead food-spread shot for a backpacker-at-camp scene:** Show someone on a rocky ridgeline cooking with a JetBoil or Zempire setup — that pulls me in harder than a glamping kitchen aesthetic.
- **Promote the Garage Sale or Re/Supply module above the fold:** I am literally their target for trade-in and used gear. A "Garage Sale near you" or "Shop Re/Supply" chip in the hero row costs nothing and would spike my engagement.
- **Convert the Impact Report banner to a thinner, collapsible chip:** Keep the mission signal — it matters to members — but don't let it own the viewport. A dismissible pill in the nav saves space for something that moves me toward a cart.
