# Just in! Up to 70% off selected KEEN, Columbia & more thru 8/3
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is REI Outlet running the exact same play it's run for a month straight: giant "SAVE UP TO X%" hero, a row of named brand logos, six generic product tiles with no prices, ending in a "SHOP ALL THE DEALS" button. Compared to June 30's "Up to 50% off clearance" (9.00) and July 1's "Up to 60% off" (8.00), this is the same template escalated to 70% — which is smart discount-laddering for a subscriber who's seen the last three sends, but it also means the creative has zero surprise left.
- The hero itself is genuinely strong: the number is enormous, unmissable, and the four brand logos (KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering) do real targeting work for anyone into hiking/outdoor gear. Where it falls apart is the product grid — six shoppable tiles, zero product names, zero prices, just "SHOP NOW ›" repeated six times. That's a missed opportunity given the render clearly has room for it.
- Net: a competent, on-brand outlet blast that will get opened on subject strength alone but won't out-click its predecessors because the body gives the reader less concrete reason to pick a specific item than June 30's send did.

## What's working

- The "SAVE UP TO 70%" hero is the largest, cleanest type treatment in this whole brand history — impossible to miss even skimming.
- Four recognizable brand logos (KEEN, Columbia, prAna, ALPS Mountaineering) sit right under the hero, doing instant category qualification for an outdoor-gear shopper.
- The member bonus-card banner ($30 for spend $50 before 9/7) is a real, specific loyalty hook placed above the fold.
- Product imagery is varied and relevant — sandals, a sleeping bag, a pack, trail runners, a hiking boot, activewear — covering multiple outdoor use cases in one scroll.
- Render is clean: no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- Every one of the six product tiles is nameless and priceless — just an image and "SHOP NOW ›," so there's no way to judge fit, price, or whether it's actually 70% off or 10%.
- "Discount based on comparative value" is the kind of soft disclosure that undercuts the headline number — sharp-eyed shoppers will read that as "the 70% isn't really 70% off REI's own price."
- This is the third "Up to X% off" mega-hero in five weeks per brand history (50% on 6/30, 60% on 7/1, now 70% on 7/30) — the format is getting stale even as the number climbs.
- No single hero product or bestseller called out — six equal-weight tiles with no "start here" signal beyond the generic "Big savings start here" header.
- Subject line at 62 characters will truncate on most mobile inboxes, likely cutting off "thru 8/3" — the one piece of real urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a name and a price (or "was $X, now $Y") on each of the six tiles — this is the single biggest lever; right now there's no reason to click one tile over another.
- 2. Trim the subject to fit mobile without truncation and move the deadline earlier in the string, since it's the strongest urgency signal.
- 3. Cut or reframe "Discount based on comparative value" — if it must stay for legal reasons, don't let it sit directly under the hero's promise.
- 4. Break the pattern from the last three sends — swap the giant-percentage hero for a specific hero product (e.g., a featured KEEN boot at its outlet price) so this doesn't read as send #4 of the same template.
- 5. Add preview text — none was rendered/supplied here, which wastes free inbox real estate that could reinforce the KEEN/Columbia angle before the open.

- **Subject Alt B:** `70% off hiking gear just landed at REI Outlet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on boots, packs & sleep gear — thru Sunday only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `KEEN, Columbia, prAna & ALPS Mountaineering, all in one outlet drop.`

# Technical Audit: REI Outlet — "Up to 70% off KEEN, Columbia & more" (2026-07-30)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an MJML-generated (`mj-column-per-*` classes) responsive HTML email using REI's `mlinks.rei.com` click-tracking redirector and AppsFlyer (`mobile.rei.com/AkCd/`) deep links; automated QA returned a 7% pass rate driven primarily by link-probe timeouts and a malformed AppsFlyer redirect parameter structure.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 `mlinks.rei.com` redirects timed out during probing** ("The read operation timed out"), including all 8 `SHOP NOW` CTAs, the hero banner link, the footer nav links, and the `Unsubscribe` link itself. A single click-tracking domain failing across every probed link suggests either a rate-limit/bot-block on `mlinks.rei.com` (common for automated QA probes hitting ESP redirectors) rather than genuinely dead destinations, or a redirector outage — this cannot be distinguished from the HTML alone and should be verified with a manual click-through.
- **AppsFlyer OneLink duplication**: 7 links to `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/` were flagged for "repeated domain," e.g.:
  `https://mobile.rei.com/AkCd/?af_r=https://www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&deep_link_value=/s/outlet-handpicked-deals?cm_mmc=...&pid=email_com_gm_it&c=...`
  The `af_r` (web fallback) and `deep_link_value` (in-app deep link target) parameters both carry the full `cm_mmc` campaign string independently. This is standard AppsFlyer OneLink structure, not a broken link — the QA heuristic likely false-positives on any URL embedding a second full URL. However, the duplicated `cm_mmc` string across both parameters is worth a sanity check to confirm both copies stay in sync if campaign codes are ever hand-edited.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text, including two 1×1 tracking pixels (`mi.rei.com/p/cp/.../o.gif`, one with an **empty `mi_u` path segment**: `mi.rei.com/p/cp//o.gif?mi_u=...`) and content images (`sp.rei.com/q/.../nh10Bv...`, `sp.rei.com/q/.../0P3SZu...`).
- 2 images load over **plain `http://`** (both `sp.rei.com/q/...` assets) rather than `https://` — likely to be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- MSO conditional comments and Outlook-specific `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` block are present, indicating standard multi-client fallback support is in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization placeholders are visible in the provided source or QA output. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- A visible `Unsubscribe` link is present in the footer (`mlinks.rei.com/e/eh?...`), satisfying the baseline CAN-SPAM visible opt-out requirement.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — QA notes it may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay; cannot confirm absence vs. capture gap from this data alone.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — if genuinely absent, the message doesn't support one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail/Yahoo require as of their 2024 bulk-sender policies for mail at this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay; flagged as unknown rather than failing.
- Physical postal address (required by CAN-SPAM) is not visible in the truncated HTML provided — cannot confirm compliance either way from this excerpt.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking uses REI's proprietary `cm_mmc` scheme rather than standard `utm_*` params: `cm_mmc=email_com_gm_it-_-20260730_OUT_T70_KO_z_Jul-_-202607300835-_-26_1_403_09707_z_CTA1`, encoding channel (`email_com_gm_it`), date (`20260730`), campaign (`OUT_T70_KO_z_Jul`), and placement (`z_CTA1`/`z_Image1`) — internally consistent and distinguishes CTA vs. image click positions.
- Landing destination `www.rei.com/s/outlet-handpicked-deals` is domain-consistent with the sender (`rei.com`) and subject line's Outlet promotion. No cross-domain mismatch.
- Cannot confirm the landing page itself renders/aligns with the promotion since it sits behind the timed-out `mlinks.rei.com` redirect layer (see §2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually verify 2–3 `mlinks.rei.com` CTA links resolve correctly outside the automated probe — the 100% timeout rate across all tracked links points to a probe-side block, not necessarily 12 dead links.
2. Add `alt` text to all images, including tracking pixels (`alt=""` is sufficient for decorative/pixel images to satisfy accessibility scanners).
3. Fix the empty path segment in `https://mi.rei.com/p/cp//o.gif` (double slash, missing ID) — likely a broken tracking pixel losing open-tracking data for this send.
4. Serve the two `sp.rei.com/q/...` images over `https://` instead of `http://` to avoid mixed-content blocking in Outlook/webmail.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (check raw headers outside the AgentMail relay) — required for one-click unsubscribe compliance with major mailbox providers.
6. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via a direct header capture, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observable through this relay.
