# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Home screen is almost entirely swallowed by a GDPR cookie-consent modal — the actual product is a blurred ghost behind it. What does peek through confirms this is Kayak's flight-search tool: a pre-filled search form, a rust-colored Search button, and a predictive pill that reads "Flights to London next weekend." For a meta-search power user like me, the bones are right. The timing of that modal, shoved in my face before I see a single useful pixel, is deeply annoying.

## What's working

- **"I understand" CTA on the cookie modal** — dark background button, high contrast, single tap to clear the blocker. At least they made dismissal obvious instead of burying it.
- **Search form fragments visible behind the modal** — "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" and the Search button peek through. I can already see what I'm about to get, which sets correct expectations.
- **"Flights to London next weekend" suggestion pill** — that contextual shortcut at the bottom is exactly what a power user wants. One tap, real query, no extra friction. That's Kayak knowing its audience.
- **Honest cookie copy** — states "essential, functional, analytical, and advertising purposes" without pretending they're not tracking me. I hate retargeting but I at least respect transparency about it.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as first content** — I opened Kayak to search flights, not to process a privacy disclosure. This modal blocks 90%+ of the above-the-fold experience. The product might be excellent and I'd never know.
- **"Advertising purposes" in the cookie copy** — as someone allergic to retargeting, this is a yellow flag. I'll tap "I understand" to proceed but I'm already a little annoyed.
- **Zero brand differentiation visible** — "Welcome to KAYAK" is the headline. No value prop, no hook, no reason why Kayak over Google Flights. That real estate is gone to consent management.
- **No returning-user acknowledgment** — I'm a Price Alert subscriber. There's no "Hey, you're back" or rewards balance or alert-status callout anywhere visible. I'm treated like a cold stranger.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie modal to a bottom sheet or banner** — don't let consent management own the full viewport. Show the search form and at least one value-prop line above it; let me see the product while I decide whether to accept cookies.
- **Surface a returning-user chip immediately post-dismiss** — "Your London alert: $487 ↓ from $531" is the kind of hook that makes a Price Alert subscriber feel seen. One line, above the search form.
- **Add a "book direct" nudge near the search results CTA** — something like "We'll show you the price. Book straight on the airline." That directly addresses the one thing that makes me use Kayak over Expedia.
- **Replace the generic "Flights to London next weekend" pill with personalized alert shortcut** — if I'm a logged-in user with active alerts, that pill should surface my actual alert destination, not a generic example.
