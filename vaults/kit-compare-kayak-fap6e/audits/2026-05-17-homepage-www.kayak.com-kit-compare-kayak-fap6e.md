# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I open Kayak — my go-to price radar — and the first thing I see is a cookie consent modal that literally blocks the entire search experience. Ironic for a site that I specifically use *because* I don't want to be retargeted. The little I can see through the modal tells me this is a flight-search landing with a "Flights to London next weekend" AI prompt chip — which is a nice touch — but I can't interact with anything until I click through the legal wall. Classic.

## What's working

- **"Flights to London next weekend" prompt chip** — the AI-generated suggestion pill at the bottom is exactly the kind of shortcut I'd tap; it anticipates intent without me typing anything, and it's genuinely useful
- **"Search" button** — orange, high contrast, legible even through the modal blur; the core action is still reachable
- **"1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" pre-filled defaults** — the form behind the modal already has sensible defaults for a solo traveler; no friction there once I dismiss the wall

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is literally the entire first impression** — for a privacy-skeptical millennial who hates retargeting, being greeted with "we use cookies for advertising purposes" is the worst possible hello. I came here *specifically* because I don't trust OTAs; now you're leading with ad-tech disclosure
- **Zero search context visible** — I can't tell what vertical I'm in (flights? hotels? cars?) because the modal obscures the nav and search tabs
- **No price alert or deal hook** — I subscribe to Kayak Price Alerts; there's no "set an alert" nudge, no "prices are low right now" signal, nothing that makes me feel like the platform is working for me
- **No returning user recognition** — I've used Kayak dozens of times and there's zero "welcome back" or recent search history visible; it feels like a cold start every time

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss-on-scroll or banner-style consent** — move the cookie notice to a bottom banner instead of a full-screen modal; let me see the search form immediately so the value proposition isn't blocked before I've even engaged
- **Surface a Price Alert CTA above the fold** — "Get alerted when prices drop" with a simple email input would speak directly to users like me who are in research mode, not buy-now mode
- **Add "recent searches" persistence** — even a single chip like "Your last search: JFK → LHR" would signal that Kayak remembers me and is on my side
- **Lean into the AI prompt chips harder** — the "Flights to London next weekend" chip is the most interesting thing on screen; show 2-3 of these personalized suggestions so the intent-matching feels proactive, not accidental
