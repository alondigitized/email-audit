# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- The first thing Kayak served me — on my own phone, a tool I actively use and have subscribed to Price Alerts on — is a full-screen cookie modal disclosing that they use tracking for "advertising purposes." I'm here specifically because I don't want to be retargeted, and they opened with the one thing I despise most. Behind the consent gate I can just barely see the flight-search form and a Search button, plus a smart-suggestion chip reading "Flights to London next weekend." The actual product is hostage to a legal disclosure wall.

## What's working

- **Smart suggestion chip** — "Flights to London next weekend" with a sparkle/AI icon is visible below the fold gate; that kind of intent-prediction is exactly what a meta-search power user appreciates and it's the most interesting signal on screen
- **Search button placement** — the coral "Search" CTA is visible enough through the modal that I know the product is there waiting; form labels ("Select dates," "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags") confirm this is flight-search-first, not hotels or packages
- **No layout chaos** — the parts of the UI visible behind the modal look clean; no broken images or overlapping elements

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as first impression** — explicitly states cookies are used for "advertising purposes," which is the single fastest way to erode trust with a user who is, by habit, allergic to retargeting; I know GDPR compliance is real, but leading with it is a branding choice, and it's a bad one for this audience
- **Zero above-fold product value** — the homepage's actual value prop — "compare prices across every source so you can book direct" — is completely invisible; I'm looking at a legal disclaimer, not a search tool
- **No returning-user recognition** — I have Price Alerts set. Kayak knows this. There is no "Welcome back, you have 3 active alerts" hook, no recently viewed routes, nothing that signals this is a tool that knows me
- **No trust differentiation from OTAs** — Kayak's entire moat over Expedia/Google Flights is that it's neutral meta-search, not a booking merchant. That message is nowhere in what I can see; for a returning power user, reinforcing "we show you results, we don't sell you a ticket" matters

## Recommendations

- **Delay or soften the cookie consent gate** — at minimum, move it to a slim bottom banner rather than a full-screen takeover so the search form is usable on landing; or use a "manage preferences" pattern that doesn't block the whole viewport
- **Surface returning-user state immediately** — if I'm logged in or have cookies from Price Alerts, swap the generic form for a personalized widget ("Your LAX→LHR alert: $612, down 8% this week") — that's the hook that makes me stay
- **Add a one-liner differentiator in the hero area** — something like "We search everywhere. You book direct." costs zero dev time and is the core reason I use Kayak over Google Flights
- **Make the smart suggestion chip more prominent** — the "Flights to London next weekend" AI chip is buried below the fold-adjacent area; test surfacing two or three of these intent shortcuts above the search form
