# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see on kayak.com isn't kayak.com at all — it's a full-screen "Welcome to KAYAK" cookie wall telling me the site tracks me for "essential, functional, analytical, and advertising purposes." For a guy who exists specifically to dodge retargeting, that's not a great opening line. Everything else — the actual search tool I came here for — is grayed out and blocked behind an "I understand" button.

## What's working

- The modal itself is legible and well-formatted — clear heading, real link text ("Terms & Conditions," "Privacy Policy"), one obvious button.
- Behind the scrim I can make out the actual flight search form is already populated with sane defaults ("1 adult, Economy, 0 bags") — that's the meta-search utility I'm here for, at least in skeleton form.
- There's a "Flights to London next weekend" suggestion pill with a sparkle icon peeking out below the search box — an AI-generated shortcut, which is a nice touch for someone who just wants to jump straight to results.

## What's weak

- The cookie modal explicitly says data is collected for "advertising purposes" — for a Kayak Price Alert subscriber who books direct specifically to dodge retargeting, that's the worst possible first sentence to read.
- The entire homepage is inaccessible until I dismiss the wall — no way to glance at the search form or scan for a deal without acting first.
- No hero copy, no visible promo, no loyalty/price-alert callout anywhere in frame — the modal is eating 100% of the above-the-fold real estate.
- Search button and date field are rendered but non-interactive (grayed/dimmed), so even the "working" part isn't usable yet.

## Recommendations

- Rewrite the consent copy to lead with function, not ad-tech — "We use cookies to save your searches and price alerts" reads very differently than "advertising purposes" to a privacy-wary meta-search user.
- Shrink the modal to a bottom banner or slim strip instead of a full-screen blocker, so the search form and any hero content stay visible and interactive underneath.
- Surface a Price Alert / "track this fare" callout right on the search form, above the fold — that's the exact hook this persona already uses Kayak for.
- Keep the "Flights to London next weekend" AI-suggestion pill — that's genuinely useful — but let it be visible without needing to clear a modal first.
