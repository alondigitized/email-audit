# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see is a full-screen cookie consent gate that's upfront — almost admirably so — about the fact that Kayak is tracking me for advertising. Very on-brand for a company that knows I'm about to go shop its competitors anyway. Behind the modal the flight search widget is exactly what I came for, and there's a "Flights to London next weekend" quick-pick pill at the bottom that shows they at least know their user is here to search, not browse. Utilitarian meta-search energy, but the gate taxes you before you get anything useful.

## What's working

- **The flight search form** is the right skeleton — "Select dates / 1 adult, Economy, 0 bags / Search" is exactly how I orient on load. No clutter, no upsells, just the query box.
- **"Flights to London next weekend" suggestion pill** — this quick-search chip at the bottom is smart. I'd actually tap that. It's the right mental model: show me a useful shortcut, not a promo.
- **The modal text is honest** — it says "advertising purposes" in plain English. Most sites bury that. I still don't love it, but I respect the transparency.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as first impression** — a native user like me dismisses it in two seconds, but it's still a friction tax on load. Worse: it explicitly says "advertising" which lights up my retargeting antenna immediately.
- **No Price Alert hook anywhere visible** — I'm a Kayak Price Alert subscriber. That's my primary relationship with this product. It's not surfaced anywhere in the viewport.
- **Zero personalization or returning-user recognition** — I've been using this site for years and it serves me a generic onboarding modal. No "Welcome back, here are your saved searches" — nothing.
- **The "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" pre-fill** is a generic default, not a remembered preference. Native users notice that.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the full-screen consent gate with a slim banner** — keep the honesty, lose the wall. A sticky strip at the bottom frees the hero for actual content and preserves that first-load energy.
- **Surface a Price Alert hook above the fold** — something like "Tracking a fare? Check your alerts →" for returning users who are logged in or have cookies. That's the retention play Kayak is leaving on the table.
- **Swap the generic "1 adult, Economy" prefill for remembered preferences** — if I always search Business or always do 2 passengers, pre-populate it. Native users feel the gap immediately.
- **Make the quick-search chips more prominent** — "Flights to London next weekend" is buried below the search button. Move two or three of those chips up, above the fold, so I can one-tap into a search without filling anything out.
