# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Above the fold, Kayak is literally wallpapered by a cookie consent modal — so my first interaction is a privacy negotiation, not a price check. What little bleeds through behind it is exactly what I came for: a flight search widget and a "Flights to London next weekend" suggestion chip. The brand knows who it is, but the cookie wall is doing real damage to first impressions.

## What's working

- **"Flights to London next weekend" suggestion chip** — that AI-assist shortcut is exactly my vibe. I love meta-search that anticipates intent without requiring me to fill out five dropdowns.
- **Search widget visible behind the modal** — even obscured, I can see "Select dates" and "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags," which confirms this is a search-first tool, not a deal-push editorial page.
- **Orange "Search" CTA** — high contrast, unambiguous verb, placed where I'd expect it. No "Discover your journey" nonsense.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no jank, no overlapping elements, fonts are legible.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal eating the entire above-the-fold** — I came to search flights, not negotiate my data rights on a cold open. This is friction before I've seen a single price.
- **"Advertising purposes" language in the cookie notice** — I'm allergic to retargeting by persona definition. Reading "advertising purposes" before I've even seen a search bar puts me on edge about whether Kayak is going to stalk me across the web after this.
- **Zero personalization hooks visible** — I'm a Price Alert subscriber, I've used this site. There's no "Welcome back" signal, no saved search, no alert status in the viewport. The site treats me like a stranger.
- **No offer or price signal above the fold** — a meta-search tool's superpower is showing me that fares exist at a compelling price point. None of that is visible.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom sheet or slim bar** — a full-overlay modal that blocks 90% of the screen is a conversion killer on mobile. Put consent in a persistent bottom strip; let me see the search widget immediately.
- **Surface a Price Alert status chip for returning users** — even a passive "Your LAX→JFK alert: $312 — last seen $340" would signal that Kayak is working for me in the background, which is exactly why I subscribed.
- **Add a "Book direct" or "View airline site" callout near the search widget** — I book direct, not through Kayak's OTA flow. If I see "We'll take you to the airline's site," I trust this tool more. That's differentiation against Expedia et al.
- **Lead with the suggestion chip above the form, not below it** — the "Flights to London next weekend" chip is smart; promoting it above the date selectors would make the interface feel more like a smart assistant and less like a form.
