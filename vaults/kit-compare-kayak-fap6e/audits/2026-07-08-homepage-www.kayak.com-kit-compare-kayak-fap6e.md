# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- I opened Kayak and the first thing I see is a full-screen cookie consent wall — the actual search tool is completely buried behind it. Once I mentally subtract the modal, I can just barely make out a search form with "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" and an orange "Search" button, plus what looks like a "Flights to London next weekend" quick-search chip. This is a utility-first product that's being held hostage by its own GDPR friction before I've even seen what I came for.

## What's working

- **The orange "Search" CTA** bleeds through the modal — even half-obscured, the color-coded button reads as "primary action" and I know where to tap once I clear the wall
- **"Flights to London next weekend" quick-suggest chip** — that AI/smart-suggest chip at the bottom is exactly what a meta-search power user wants; it surfaces a real intent without me typing anything
- **Clean form pre-fill ("1 adult, Economy, 0 bags")** — Kayak defaulted to the most common search params without asking me a five-step wizard; that's respectful of my time

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as the welcome experience** — this is the entire viewport. There is no homepage. I am being interrogated about tracking before I've seen a single flight result. For someone who is explicitly *allergic to retargeting*, this modal is the least welcoming possible greeting.
- **"I understand" as the only action** — there's no "Reject" or "Manage preferences" visible as an equally weighted option; it's a dark-pattern nudge toward full consent
- **Zero Price Alert / returning user hook** — I'm a Kayak Price Alert subscriber. There's nothing here acknowledging that. No "Your alerts," no "Prices moved on your routes." That's a wasted relationship.
- **No value prop copy anywhere** — behind the modal there's no headline telling me *why* Kayak vs. Google Flights. The brand is just assuming I already know.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie notice to a bottom banner strip**, not a full modal — let the search form breathe above the fold on first load; the consent ask doesn't need to own the entire screen
- **Surface a "Your Price Alerts" card for returning users** — if I'm logged in or cookied, show me "BOS→LAX: Price dropped $38 since Thursday" in a top rail; that's the product's killer feature and it's invisible here
- **Add a "Reject non-essential" button at the same visual weight as "I understand"** — removes the dark-pattern feel and actually builds trust with the privacy-conscious travelers who are Kayak's core demo
- **Inject the quick-suggest chip copy into the search form as placeholder text** ("Try: Flights to London next weekend") rather than a separate floating pill — fewer UI elements, same discoverability
