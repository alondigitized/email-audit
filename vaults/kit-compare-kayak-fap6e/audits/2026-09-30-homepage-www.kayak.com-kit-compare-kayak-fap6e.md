# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a clean, utilitarian meta-search tool, which is what I want from Kayak. The headline is "Find the right flig…" and, past the modal, "…sites." I can only read half of it because a cookie modal covers the middle. The page pushes the search box first and social proof second: "Save when you compare," "41,000,000+ searches this week," five stars. There's no deal hype and no retargeting-style banner, but the first thing I hit is a consent wall that mentions "advertising purposes."

## What's working

- **"Save when you compare" card with the JetBlue, Alaska, Delta, United and American logos:** it shows Kayak as the price-finder that points me to airlines, which is how I use it. It doesn't try to sell me the ticket.
- **Search bar with LAX already filled in:** the origin is set, and I only need "To?", dates, and Search. That's the fewest taps possible.
- **"41,000,000+ searches this week" and "Travelers love us / 1M+ ratings on our app":** these are fast credibility checks. I don't need anyone telling me Kayak is legit, but the numbers are concrete.
- **Product tabs (Flights, Stays, Cars, Packages):** the Flights icon is highlighted in orange, so I know where I am right away.
- **"Flights to London next weekend" pill:** a natural-language search prompt near the bottom. It's handy if I'm feeling lazy, and it's a nice extra.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal covers the headline and most of the tab row.** "Find the right flig…" is cut off mid-word, and the last tab reads "Cru…". I can't read the value prop or reach Cruises until I dismiss it.
- **The modal has a single "I understand" button and no "Reject" or "Manage" option.** It says the site uses tracking for "advertising purposes." I'm allergic to retargeting, so this is the one thing that makes me want to close the tab.
- **No price-alert hook.** I'm a Kayak Price Alert subscriber, but from the screen I can't tell there's a way to set alerts. The heart and the profile icon are the only signs of anything saved.
- **No sample fares, trending routes, or deals visible.** Everything above the fold is about Kayak, not about a flight I could book.
- **The three trust cards are big and low-information.** They take up a lot of space to say what one line could.
- **"1 adult, Economy" and "0 bags" are small and low-contrast** next to the Search button.

## Recommendations

- **Split the consent modal into "Accept" and "Reject non-essential."** Make it a slim bottom sheet so the headline and search form stay visible on first load.
- **Add a price-tracking row under the search bar.** For example: "Track a route: get an alert when LAX → JFK drops." It would speak directly to signed-in and alert-minded users.
- **Replace one of the three trust cards with a live "Trending fares from LAX" strip.** Show three routes with a "from $XXX" price so I can see a concrete number above the fold.
- **Make the trip-type and bag selectors bigger and bolder,** so the whole search flow reads at a glance on a phone.
