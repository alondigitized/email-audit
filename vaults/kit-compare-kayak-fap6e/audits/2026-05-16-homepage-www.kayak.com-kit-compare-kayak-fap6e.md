# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- First screen and Kayak is already ratting itself out — the cookie banner straight-up says "advertising purposes" before I can even see the search box. As a tool I use specifically to avoid being retargeted into oblivion, that disclosure sitting in my face before I've done anything is a bad omen. Behind the modal I can see there's a clean search form and an AI suggestion chip, so the product is probably fine — but this opener is rough.

## What's working

- **"Flights to London next weekend" AI suggestion chip** — that little peeking chip at the bottom of the modal is clever; it signals "we know what you want before you ask," which is exactly the energy I come here for
- **"Search" button is visible through the modal** — even half-buried, the orange CTA anchors the eye and tells me the tool is right there
- **"1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" pre-fill** — sensible defaults mean the form is already close to usable; I'm not starting from scratch

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocking 100% of the homepage** — I cannot see any content, offers, or navigation until I tap "I understand"; this is a forced gate before I get any value
- **"advertising purposes" disclosure** — right in the opening text; as someone who books direct specifically to escape ad retargeting, reading this before I've even searched is alienating
- **No meta-search credibility signals visible** — Kayak's whole pitch is "we compare everything"; none of that is visible; the modal erases it
- **No returning-user hook** — I use Kayak regularly; there's no "your saved alert" or "pick up where you left off" — just a wall
- **"I understand" button copy** — dark, legal-ese phrasing that feels coercive, not welcoming

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom bar, not a full-screen modal** — let me see the search form and a teaser of results before demanding consent; dozens of sites do this without violating GDPR
- **Lead with "we compare 900+ airlines and never sell your data to retargeters"** — lean into the positioning that actually speaks to people like me; right now the ad-tracking disclosure undercuts the brand promise
- **Surface the Price Alert / saved searches hook above the fold** — that's the product feature that turns me from a visitor into a habitual user; it should be front and center, not buried
- **Change "I understand" to "Let me search"** — single copy swap that shifts the modal from compliance theater to forward momentum
