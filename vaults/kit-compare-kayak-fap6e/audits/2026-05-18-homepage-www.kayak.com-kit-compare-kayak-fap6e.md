# Homepage snapshot · www.kayak.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Kayak is my whole deal — I live in this app for Price Alerts — and the first thing I get is a cookie consent wall blocking the actual search UI. I can barely see the search form underneath. The "advertising purposes" language in the consent copy is exactly the kind of thing that makes me go full private-tab mode. At least the shell of a flight search form is peeking through.

## What's working

- **The "Search" button** — orange, readable through the modal blur, immediately legible as the action I want to take once the modal is gone
- **"Flights to London next weekend" contextual suggestion chip** — at the bottom of the viewport, this reads like Kayak knows I'm a repeat user who searches specific routes; it's the one personalization signal that cuts through

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal as first experience** — this is the entire above-the-fold story. "This site uses cookies... for advertising purposes" is the opening line to someone who obsessively blocks retargeting pixels. Bad first impression.
- **"Welcome to KAYAK"** — that headline on the modal is doing zero work. It's not a welcome; it's a legal CYA. Calling it a welcome is a small insult.
- **No Price Alert callout visible** — I'm a subscriber. There's no hook here for me as a returning user. The page treats me like a stranger.
- **1 adult, Economy, 0 bags default** — I can see this behind the modal but it doesn't feel personalized; it's just the form defaults. No memory of my last search.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the consent modal trigger to a bottom sheet or banner** — let the search UI breathe above the fold. The modal is the entire first-screen experience right now, which is wasted real estate.
- **Resurface Price Alert status for signed-in users** — a "You have 3 active Price Alerts" chip near the search bar would make me feel like a power user, not a first-time visitor.
- **Reframe the consent copy away from "advertising purposes" as the second line** — bury that in the detail view; lead with "essential and functional" to reduce bounce from privacy-conscious users.
- **Pre-fill the search form with my last route** — I've searched this before. Show me BOS → SFO or wherever I last looked, not a blank "1 adult, Economy, 0 bags" shell.
