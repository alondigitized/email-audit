# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Sephora immediately reads as a gift-mode homepage right now — everything above the fold is pointing me toward Mother's Day, which makes sense for early May, but as someone shopping for myself it's a bit of a detour. The loyalty angle (4X points, Beauty Insider callouts) is doing real work and the fragrance sample promo up top is a nice hook. It feels premium but not cold — very much the Sephora I know.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **Free fragrance sample set banner** at the very top — "$50 min. spend, code SCENTSYAY" — that's exactly the kind of low-friction perk that gets me to add one more thing to my cart
- **Sign In for FREE Shipping CTA** right under the nav — smart placement, and free shipping is a real reason I'd log in immediately
- **4X points on fragrance gifts (code BESTSCENTS)** — Beauty Insider loyalty language is speaking directly to me; I track my points obsessively and "4X" is a genuine motivator
- **"Beauty Insiders only" fine print on the hero** — I appreciate the in-group signal; it makes me feel like a VIP even if the hero itself is gift-focused
- **Category nav (New, Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance)** — quick escape hatch if I want to self-shop instead of gift-shop

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% Mother's Day gift sets** — I'm a self-purchaser coming to check new drops; there's zero above-the-fold content aimed at me
- **No "New Arrivals" or launch teaser visible** — as someone who tracks the launch calendar, the absence of any new-product energy in the hero is a miss
- **Hero image feels cluttered** — multiple product groupings (Prada, Jo Malone-adjacent, pink gift boxes) are competing; no single product is getting its moment
- **"BEAUTY INSIDER" badge in the hero feels like an afterthought** — it's a circle stamp on a busy image, easy to scroll past
- **Partially cropped second module** ("Ha..." visible on the right) — whatever that card is, it's getting cut off on mobile; I can't tell what it is or tap it confidently

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New This Week" or launch-highlight strip directly below the hero** — even a thin horizontal scroller with 3-4 new mascara/lip drops would catch me before I bounce to the New tab
- **Swap the cluttered multi-product hero for a single hero featuring one hero fragrance gift set** with a clean lifestyle shot (someone opening it, not just the boxes floating in space)
- **Make the Beauty Insider badge interactive** — a tappable element linking to my points balance or exclusive member deals would make the loyalty layer feel useful, not decorative
- **Surface the "AI Beauty" tab in the hero** — there's a "NEW" badge on it in the bottom nav; a one-line callout ("Try AI Beauty — find your perfect shade match") above the fold would drive trial
