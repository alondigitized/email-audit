# These hot sets + you = ⚡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a holiday gift-set edit, but it opens as something else. The top third is a Healthy Hair & Scalp Kit hero (a charity tie-in), a free Apple Love scent mini, and a 30% Savings Event badge. The "sets" the subject promises don't start until the "Meet Must-Have Sets" banner, well below the fold. A reader who opened for the "hot sets" has to scroll past three competing messages to reach them.
- Once you get there, the grid is solid. It has 10 sets with brand, name, price, and "$XX value" framing, plus "Only at Sephora" and "Clean" badges. It is closer to the 10/13 "MAKEUP SETS: HERE & NOWHERE ELSE" send, which scored 7, than to the 10/17 catch-all. But it carries the same clutter as the 10/18 clean-beauty send, which stacked three offers at the top. This is the fourth or fifth Sephora send in nine days.
- Judgement: good product merchandising in the middle, a muddled top and a weak subject. It's a mid-6 email that could be a 7 or 8 if the sets led.

## What's working

- The set cards are well built. Each has a clear product shot, brand, name, price, and value comparison, for example "$55 ($80 value)".
- The "New • Only at Sephora • Limited Edition" labels add exclusivity without needing a discount.
- The Spot It. Shop It. tiles (Korean haircare, Prada) and the Haus Labs lip liner give the email a second, editorial-feeling section.
- The Beauty Insider block and Rewards Bazaar link near the bottom keep loyalty visible.
- The Clean at Sephora badges help shoppers who filter by that value.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject promises sets, but the hero is a hair and scalp kit. The sets are pushed below the fold.
- The top has three competing messages: the Savings Event 30% badge, the Apple Love scent mini, and the hair kit. None clearly wins.
- The scent mini sits above the hero and takes the primary position. Its "Shop to redeem" CTA is tiny and its terms are unclear.
- The charity copy under the hair kit hero is dense and small. It reads like fine print wedged into the hero.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop Now" and "Shop to redeem". No set tile has its own button, and the sets have no reviews or ratings.
- The footer fine print is a long wall of text. The "30% off" badge links to terms that are hard to find.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sets. Move "Meet Must-Have Sets" into the hero and demote the hair kit to a single tile in the grid. That one move would match the email to its subject and likely lift click-through the most.
- 2. Pick one top offer, either the scent mini or the Savings Event. Make it a single strip with a specific verb CTA, such as "Get your free mini".
- 3. Name the offer in the subject. Say what's free or discounted, or give a price anchor like "$98 value".
- 4. Move the charity copy into a small footnote and keep the hero clean.
- 5. Add per-tile CTAs ("Shop the set") and a rating or "bestseller" tag on 2–3 sets.
- 6. Space sends out. After 10/17 and 10/18, another near-identical promo reads as noise.

  - Two separate tracking systems fire per open. This is not a defect, but both pixels lack alt text (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 3 images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `AQEAAZcF2g…` (`app.sephora.com/O/…`)
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/…/2021_Navs_Header_06`)
  - `o.gif` (`mi.sephora.com/p/up/…/o.gif`)

  The two pixels should have `alt=""`. The nav header image needs descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`** uses an invalid value. The valid directive is `noindex`. It has no effect on inbox rendering, and it is minor.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4 concern).
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`** is applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This blocks user text scaling on iOS.
- **`<title>Sephora</title>`** is generic and doesn't match the subject line. Impact is minor.
- **Plain-text part is empty** (QA FAIL, 0 chars). This is the one hard failure. It hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks. AgentMail may have dropped the part during capture, so check the raw MIME before treating it as a sender defect.
