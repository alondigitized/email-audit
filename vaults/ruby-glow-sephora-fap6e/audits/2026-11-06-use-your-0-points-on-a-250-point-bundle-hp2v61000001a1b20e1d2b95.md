# Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- The subject, "Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle," is the weakest part of this send. The email is a Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar, and the render does support the "250-point" half of the subject. A banner reads "NEW REWARD OPTION: Now you can get THREE 100-point rewards for just 250 points." But the "0 points" in the subject reads like an unfilled personalization field. The recipient is told they have nothing to spend, and the whole point-redemption pitch is undercut before it opens. That is the most damaging thing in the send.
- The body does its job. The "November means new rewards!" hero is clear, there is a single "SHOP NOW" button, and the 100-point and 250-point grids make the catalog easy to scan. The problem is stacking. The header carries a 10% Insider sale medallion (11/3–11/9, code ALONSALE). Below it sit a "WANT UP TO 500 POINTS?" challenge, a free-delivery line, a GWP promo ("Use code UNREADY"), Kérastase, a holiday gift guide, and a long legal block. A reader who opened for points is sent three ways.
- Judgement: a clear rewards-catalog email with a subject that undercuts it. The tone sits close to your 11/2–11/3 sends, which also led with a 10% medallion. Here the headline offer is points, not discount, and that mismatch with the header is the main body-level problem.

## What's working

- The hero "November means new rewards!" with one "SHOP NOW" button gives a clear primary action.
- The "THREE 100-point rewards for just 250 points" banner states the new mechanic in one line.
- The 100-point and 250-point sections use the same product-card layout, with the point value and "ONLINE ONLY" labelled under each item, so the catalog is easy to scan.
- The 10% Insider medallion with dates (11/3–11/9) in the header gives a concrete, time-bound offer.
- Real products from recognizable brands (Saie, Huda Beauty, Dior, Fenty, Moroccanoil) make the rewards feel worth having.

## What's weak

- The "0 points" in the subject looks like a broken merge or a bad data pull, and it tells the recipient they can't use the offer.
- The header's 10% sale medallion competes with the points message. The email has two different offers at the top, and the points story is not the first thing the eye lands on.
- The lower half piles on a GWP code promo, a Kérastase feature, a holiday gift guide and four utility strips, which dilutes the rewards message.
- The legal and fine-print block runs long, and the barcodes at the bottom take up a lot of height.
- The 100-point and 250-point cards have no individual CTAs visible, so the only clear click path is the single hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject so it never shows "0 points." Either suppress the merge when the balance is zero, or move to a segmented subject that doesn't depend on the number. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Pick one lead offer. If the send is a rewards send, move the 10% medallion down to a thin strip, or lead with the discount and use points as a secondary section.
- 3. Make each product card a link with a "Redeem 100 pts" style button, so the click path isn't only the hero.
- 4. Cut the lower promo stack to one secondary module, such as the GWP code, and drop the Kérastase and gift guide blocks from this send.
- 5. Shorten the legal block, or collapse it behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 rewards for 250 points, now live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in the Rewards Bazaar: 3 picks for 250 pts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dior, Fenty, Moroccanoil and more, pick yours before 11/9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insider points go further this month. See the new bundle.`

It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. This is the source of the QA "Image missing alt text" warning. It is hidden, so the impact is low, but it will be flagged by validators.
- **Pixel placement:** the pixel sits outside the preheader `div`s, directly in the `td`. It is harmless but not validated beyond that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width vs. mobile:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`, while the responsive rules only apply at `max-width:480px`. Unless an inner override exists in the truncated portion, small viewports will scroll horizontally.
- **Malformed MSO conditional inside `<style>`:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media` block. Conditional comments don't belong in CSS. Browsers and most clients will treat `<!--[if mso]>` as part of the selector and drop the `img.mso-size` rule. The Outlook-specific sizing for `.mobileHeaderLogo` will not apply as intended.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is likely ignored in email.
- **JSON-LD:** the block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` (empty value) and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"` with no properties. These are non-functional, and Gmail will ignore or reject them.
- **Missing alt:** confirmed for the tracking pixel only. Alt text on visible images could not be checked in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

A recipient with 0 points cannot redeem a 250-point bundle.
