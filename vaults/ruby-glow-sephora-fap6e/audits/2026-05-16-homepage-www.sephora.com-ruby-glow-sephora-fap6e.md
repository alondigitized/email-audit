# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora knows their audience — this is clearly a beauty destination for someone like me, clean product photography, new launch front and center, and a skincare sample incentive right at the top. But for a Rouge member who's been buying here for years? The homepage feels weirdly generic — no rewards balance, no "welcome back," no hint that I'm a VIP. It's pitching me like I just discovered Sephora for the first time.

## What's working

- **The skincare sample set banner** at the very top with the GETSAMPLES code is exactly the kind of stacking-value offer I live for — I'm already calculating whether I need to top up my cart to hit $45.
- **"New Sincerely Yours Lip Balm"** hero is doing the right job — new launch, clean product photography, pastel-heavy which reads very skincare-girly-Millennial.
- **"CLEAN at SEPHORA" badge** on the hero image is a real trust signal for me — I do filter by clean beauty and seeing it front-and-center validates the product before I even tap.
- **The mobile nav tabs** — New, Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance — are exactly one tap from where I shop. That's smart information architecture for a mobile-first girl.
- **The "AI Beauty" tab** in the bottom nav with the "NEW" badge genuinely catches my eye — high-tech comfort is my thing and I'd want to explore that.

## What's weak

- **Zero Rouge acknowledgment**: I have years of purchase history, a points balance, and pending birthday perks — none of that is surfaced. The homepage treats me like a stranger. That "Sign In for FREE Shipping" messaging is insulting when I'm already a Rouge loyalist.
- **No reviews or ratings** anywhere above the fold — for a new product like this lip balm, I need at least a star rating or "X reviews" to feel confident clicking through.
- **Urgency is missing**: No "ends Sunday," no countdown, no "limited quantities" — the sample set offer floats with no deadline, which actually makes me less likely to act on it immediately.
- **The hero carousel** has dots suggesting more slides, but there's no visual invitation to swipe — on mobile I might miss whatever else is being promoted.
- **No model/person in the imagery** — it's all flat-lay product shots, which I love, but I have zero idea who this product is for or how it looks in use.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Hi Ruby, your Rouge points: X" strip** directly below the sign-in bar for logged-in users — or at minimum surface "Check your rewards" as a tappable CTA, because that's what brings Rouge members back weekly.
- **Drop a star-rating count under the hero headline** — even "4.8 ★ (2,341 reviews)" next to "New Sincerely Yours Lip Balm" would dramatically lift my confidence and conversion likelihood without redesigning anything.
- **Put an expiry date on the GETSAMPLES offer** — "Offer ends May 19" would trigger instant FOMO and stop me from thinking "I'll add more to my cart later" (which often means never).
- **Animate or label the carousel dots** with a category hint — a small "New Arrivals | Top Rated | Skincare Sets" label under each dot gives me a reason to swipe instead of bouncing straight to the nav.
