# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora is doing what Sephora does — clean, confident, very on-brand in black and white with that pop of color from the products. Right now they're pushing skincare hard, specifically a 4X Beauty Insider points event tied to some kind of "Next-Gen Essentials" campaign, and there's a free sample set offer front and center. For me, as a Rouge member who plans her VIB purchase windows like it's a sport, this landing feels targeted and relevant.

## What's working

- **The Beauty Insider 4X points badge on the hero** — I literally shop around BI events, so this is the signal that makes me stop scrolling Instagram and open the app. It's prominent and immediately legible.
- **"Ends 5/20" urgency on the SKINWINS card** — I respond to real deadlines. Six days is enough to create genuine urgency without feeling fake.
- **The GETSAMPLES banner at the very top** — free gift with $45 min. spend is a low lift for me. I spend $45 in skincare without blinking.
- **Navigation tabs with "Skincare" and "Makeup" one tap away** — mobile nav is actually good here. I'm not hunting.
- **"AI Beauty" tab in bottom nav with a NEW badge** — I'm a high-tech comfort person and this caught my eye immediately. That's a rabbit hole I'd tap.
- **The hero model** — she looks like someone I'd follow on TikTok. Genuinely relatable, not overly styled.

## What's weak

- **"Sign In for FREE Shipping" is wasted on me** — As a Rouge, I already get free shipping. This hook reads like a pitch to non-members, not a reward for loyalty. Should say something like "Rouge members: your perks are waiting" or show my points balance if I'm signed in.
- **"Gentle routines for developing skin"** — I'm 29, not 15. "Developing skin" reads like a teen skincare line. The copy undershoots my sophistication level and makes me wonder if this campaign is actually for me.
- **No product names or brand callouts visible** — The hero is gorgeous but vague. I can see products on a surface but I can't tell what they are. As someone who knows the launch calendar, I want to immediately recognize what's being spotlighted.
- **No review counts or ratings visible** — Sephora reviews are one of the reasons I shop there. Zero evidence of that trust signal in this viewport.
- **The partially visible card on the right** — I can see there's something there but it's cut off. On mobile this creates anxiety that I'm missing something. The horizontal scroll isn't obvious.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Sign In for FREE Shipping" for a loyalty-tier-aware hook**: If I'm a known Rouge user (via cookie or app state), show "Welcome back, Rouge — 4X points are live." The current copy is acquisition-focused when the session behavior suggests retention.
- **Name the products in the hero** — Add a small product name overlay or "Shop the routine" link so I can identify what I'm looking at without an extra tap. I should be able to tap directly into a product PDP, not just a category page.
- **Rewrite "developing skin" copy** — "Next-Gen Essentials" is a great line. The sub-copy should match that energy. Try "Boundary-pushing routines. 4X points on every drop." That keeps the millennial-to-Gen-Z tone without accidentally aiming at high school freshmen.
- **Make the horizontal scroll indicator obvious** — Dots or a visible edge peek would signal that more content exists. Right now the cropped right card just looks like a layout error.
