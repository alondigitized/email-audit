# 👍🏿 👍🏼 👍🏾 Feel-good ingredients are here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a "clean beauty" edit with three offers stacked above it. At the top is a 4X Insider points banner, then a FREE Kosas lip trial size with a $30+ purchase and code TRYKOSAS, then a big "NEW AND CLEAN AT SEPHORA" hero. The clean-beauty edit is a coherent theme with a clear hero and a tidy grid of eight products with checkmark badges. But the first screen asks the reader to process the points deal, the freebie and the clean theme at once.
- The subject doesn't match the body. "Feel-good ingredients are here" is vague, and the email never explains what a feel-good ingredient is. The three thumbs-up emojis in different skin tones are a clear inclusivity nod. They read as odd clutter next to a clean-beauty message and add nothing about the offer. The 4X points deal ending 10/21 and the free Kosas trial are the real hooks, and neither is in the subject.
- Compared with the 10/13 "MAKEUP SETS: HERE & NOWHERE ELSE" send (7/10), which stuck to one theme, this one is cluttered again. It repeats the stacked-message pattern of 10/17 ("Beauty this good moves fast 👀") and 10/9. It's decent, but the strongest offers are buried in the creative and absent from the subject.

## What's working

- The "NEW AND CLEAN AT SEPHORA" hero is one clear theme, with a Kosas product shot and a single "SHOP NOW" button.
- The 4X points deal has a hard deadline (ends 10/21) and a code (4XHYDRATE), which gives it credible urgency.
- The FREE Kosas trial-size block has a visible product shot, a code and a "SHOP TO REDEEM" button.
- The product grid is consistent. Each tile has a product image, a clean-at-Sephora badge, a "NEW" or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag, a brand and name, and a price.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is a nice loyalty touch.
- Nothing is broken. Images load, and there is no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete in the first screen: 4X points, the free Kosas trial and the clean hero.
- The 4X points banner is a small strip that is easy to skim past. It is not the hero, even though it is the strongest time-bound offer.
- Most CTAs are a generic "SHOP NOW". The grid products carry no individual buttons, and there are no ratings or reviews.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." block mixes two unrelated messages: an auto-replenish deal (15% off) and Black-owned brands. The rhode block and the "ONLY AT SEPHORA" strip add to the sprawl.
- The footer carries long fine print, several offer exclusions and a barcode, so the offers feel less straightforward.
- The subject emojis and phrase say nothing about the offers inside.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead offer and make it the subject. Either the 4X points deal with its 10/21 deadline, or the free Kosas trial. Move the other below the fold.
- 2. Make the 4X points banner a full hero if it stays the lead, or cut it to a one-line strip.
- 3. Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with specific button copy, such as "Shop clean makeup" or "Get my free Kosas gloss".
- 4. Add star ratings or "best seller" cues to the grid tiles and link each product.
- 5. Cut the "Spot it. Shop it." block, or split it into its own send. Move the long fine print into a collapsible or smaller footer.

- **Text resizing disabled.** The `<style>` block sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. That blocks user text scaling.
