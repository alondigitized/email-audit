# Today only ‼️ 10% off your $100+ purchase.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Sephora's July 16 send opens with a subject that promises a clean transactional win — "Today only ‼️ 10% off your $100+ purchase" — but delivers an email whose visual hero is a "Free Hair Sample Set." That disconnect is the central problem: the 10% threshold-discount is a solid conversion mechanic, but it never gets its moment. Instead, the creative splits attention between the GWP hair sample campaign, a bundle offer, and a loyalty section — turning what should be a focused conversion email into a multi-message broadcast.
- Coming one day after the July 15 "Just Your Type" fragrance email, this is Sephora's fifth send in nine days. Cadence fatigue is a real risk. Worse, the 10% off $100+ (max value: a $10 discount) lands nine days after Stacking Weekend's 30% + 2X points. Any subscriber who bought during that arc will benchmark this offer against it and find it wanting.
- The Beauty INSIDER loyalty section at the bottom is smart — it reminds members their relationship has more than transactional value — but it's buried below multiple competing sections. This email earns the open on the strength of the subject alone, then loses focus before the click.

## What's working

- **Subject line is concrete and time-gated:** states the offer, the minimum spend, and the deadline in under 45 characters — no mystery needed.
- **"Sephora Insider" sender name carries weight:** opens without hesitation from a loyal subscriber.
- **Free Hair Sample Set is a genuine GWP hook:** layering a gift on top of a discount is smart mechanics, even if the execution buries it.
- **Product grid range:** the "Save it. Shop it." section covers hair, fragrance, and skincare — something to browse for different subscriber profiles.
- **Beauty INSIDER callout at bottom:** reinforces membership value and long-term relationship, not just the one transaction.

## What's weak

- **Hero and subject are misaligned:** you open expecting a 10% off confirmation and instead see "Free Hair Sample Set" as the lead creative — the discount doesn't anchor the email visually.
- **"Get 8% Off\*" app banner conflicts with the 10% subject:** a quick skim creates instant arithmetic confusion before the subscriber even gets to the hero.
- **Four competing offers, zero hierarchy:** 10% off, free hair sample, bundle deal, and 100-point rewards all fight for attention. None owns the email.
- **CTA copy is too vague:** "HOW TO REDEEM" — redeem which offer? The fragmentation makes this feel like a dead end.
- **Step-down fatigue:** 10% off $100+ is objectively weaker than the July 7 Stacking Weekend deal. Sephora trained subscribers to expect more; this send will feel thin by comparison.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the 10% off the visual hero, full stop.** "Free Hair Sample Set" belongs as a secondary line — "Plus a free hair sample set on any $100+ order" — not the lead frame.
- 2. **Kill the "Get 8% Off\*" banner on this send or align it.** It contradicts the subject before the subscriber even reaches the body. Rewrite it to "10% off — even better in app" or drop it entirely.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA.** Replace "HOW TO REDEEM" with "Shop & Save 10%" or "Get My 10% Off." Verb + specific outcome.
- 4. **Anchor the mechanic in the hero copy.** "$100+ = 10% off + a free hair gift" is simple and honest — it should appear in the hero text, not buried in fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 10% off when you spend $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10% off + a free hair gift — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, save 10% — and grab a free hair sample set. Expires tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Beauty Insider reward: 10% off any $100+ order, one day only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users from zooming on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Remove `maximum-scale=1` or add `user-scalable=yes`.

**Broad `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` suppresses user text scaling**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }
```
Applied to `body` and block-level elements, this overrides OS-level text size preferences. Accepted as a legacy email hack for iOS font inflation, but WCAG 1.4.4 non-conformant. Scope should be narrowed to specific cells where inflation is actually a problem, not applied globally.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>
```
No `lang="en"` (or locale equivalent). Violates WCAG 3.1.1. Screen readers will guess the language. Fix: `<html lang="en" ...>`.

**`robots` meta value is malformed**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`no index` (with space) is not a valid robots directive token. The correct value is `noindex`. Low impact in email clients, but if this email is web-hosted or mirrored, the directive is silently ignored and the page may be indexed. Fix: `content="noindex"`.
