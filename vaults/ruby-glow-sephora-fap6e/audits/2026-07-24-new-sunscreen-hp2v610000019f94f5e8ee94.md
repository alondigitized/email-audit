# NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's sunscreen new-arrivals send — the brand's first SPF-focused product launch email after a heavy run of hair care promotions (three in the last week, including back-to-back 4X points offers on July 19 and July 22). The category pivot is genuinely welcome: July 24 is peak sunscreen season, and the yellow hero ("SOMETHING NEW IS HERE") sets a warm summer tone without overselling it. The product grid is the real asset — six-plus SPF products visible with brand names and prices, serving multiple price points and skin needs.
- The execution stumble is impossible to miss: a "For Dry Hair" module appears near the bottom, clearly a remnant of last week's hair campaign that wasn't stripped before send. It breaks editorial focus and reads like a content calendar collision. Everywhere else, the CTA copy stays at "SHOP NOW" — four-plus instances with zero variation — which flattens what could have been a sharper editorial voice for a launch email.
- Overall: a well-timed, clean-rendering send that earns its open on seasonal relevance alone, with one unforced error and a CTA layer that needed one more pass.

## What's working

- **Seasonal precision**: A sunscreen launch email on July 24 with beach-themed emojis needs no explanation — the timing speaks for itself, zero friction to relevance.
- **Yellow hero aesthetic**: Warm golden background with product photography creates a cohesive summer visual identity without feeling forced.
- **Product breadth with visible detail**: Six-plus SPF products shown with brand names, SPF values (SPF 30, SPF 40), and prices ($34 for WHIP Daily Whip Sunscreen) — enough for a subscriber to comparison-shop without leaving the email.
- **"Get a Mini" banner at top**: A clear GWP anchor gives this launch email a purchase hook beyond "just look at new things" — real conversion incentive.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, overlapping text, or empty placeholders visible anywhere in the layout.

## What's weak

- **"For Dry Hair" module at bottom**: Completely off-theme for a sunscreen send — a stale hair care block that survived the template swap. Breaks editorial focus and makes the email feel unedited.
- **All-caps subject line**: "NEW SUNSCREEN" reads like a shout — zero curiosity, mild inbox spam-signal, and no benefit over a more conversational framing.
- **Generic CTA copy throughout**: "SHOP NOW" appears at least four times with no variation; missed the chance to say "Shop SPF," "Find Your Sunscreen," or "See All New Arrivals" to reinforce the editorial story.
- **No loyalty mechanic**: After two July emails leading with 4X points, this send offers no member incentive — the contrast makes it feel like a lesser-value touch.
- **No social proof**: No star ratings, "Best Seller" callouts, or "#1 at Sephora" badges visible anywhere in the product grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the "For Dry Hair" section entirely.** Replace with a complementary SPF module ("How to Layer SPF in Your Routine") or a second editorial sunscreen callout. Single biggest lift.
- 2. **Rewrite CTA copy to be category-specific.** "Shop SPF →" or "Find Your SPF Match →" — anything that reinforces the editorial angle beats four identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 3. **Add a loyalty hook near the product grid.** "Earn points on every purchase" or a small 2X/3X SPF bonus would tap the member behavior Sephora conditioned in July's hair care run.
- 4. **Add one social proof signal.** A "Best Seller" badge on the WHIP product or a "#1 SPF at Sephora" editorial callout would meaningfully increase click confidence.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject to lower the shout and raise the pull:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `New SPF drops just landed 💦☀️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your new summer sunscreen is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop new arrivals from Summer Fridays, WHIP & more — now at Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New sunscreen launches from the most popular names — find yours`
