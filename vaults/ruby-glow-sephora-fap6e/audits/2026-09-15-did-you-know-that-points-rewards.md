# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a Rewards Bazaar redemption catalog, not a shopping promo — and it's honest about that from the top: "Grab September rewards while you can" over a maroon hero with a curated tray of products (cosmetic bag, skincare, makeup palette, mirror). That's a clean, on-brand hero for a Beauty Insider member. But the subject "Did you know that points = rewards?" is generic and slightly condescending — any loyalty member already knows this, so it reads as filler rather than a hook.
- Below the hero it turns into a long, scrollable point-tier list (1,000–15,000 points) with real product photography and clear "X AVAILABLE NOW" scarcity flags, which is a nice touch for urgency. The problem is this email only works for Rouge/VIB members sitting on a large point balance — for anyone else it's a wall of unattainable rewards with no personal points balance shown ("You have 0 points" sits limply at the very top, undercutting the whole pitch).
- Judgement: solid execution for a niche loyalty-redemption email, but weak subject/preview and a tone-deaf pairing with a 0-points recipient bring it down from what could be a 7.

## What's working

- Hero copy ("Grab September rewards while you can") ties directly to the loyalty program, matching the Beauty Insider persona.
- "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" badge plus scarcity counts ("30 AVAILABLE NOW", "3 AVAILABLE NOW") create credible urgency.
- Clean, unbroken product photography for all seven reward tiers — no render bugs or broken images.
- Clear single CTA ("SHOP NOW") in the hero, reinforced at the bottom nav ("SHOP NOW" / "REWARDS BAZAAR").
- Tier/segment bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) gives quick visual context for loyalty status.

## What's weak

- Top strap literally says "You have 0 points" — directly undercuts an email whose entire premise is "you have points to spend."
- Subject line is bland trivia phrasing, not a concrete offer or product tease.
- No personalization of the points balance is visible anywhere below the top strap — recipient can't tell which rewards are actually in reach.
- All rewards start at 1,000 points and run up to 15,000 — for a low/no-point member this is aspirational at best, alienating at worst.
- Long single-column list with no visual hierarchy differentiating "attainable" vs. "aspirational" rewards.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress this send (or dynamically rewrite the hero) for 0-point members.** If Sephora can show "You have 0 points" in the header, it can also segment the send — the highest-leverage fix is not sending a 1,000+ point redemption catalog to someone with zero points.
- 2. Replace the subject with something concrete tied to the reward catalog itself, e.g. a specific low-tier item, so it's relevant even to lighter-point members.
- 3. Add a "starting at 1,000 points" framing early in the hero so lower-balance members aren't misled into thinking everything is out of reach.
- 4. Sort or badge the list by point tier ("Under 2,000 points" first) so scrolling readers hit attainable rewards before 15,000-point aspirational items.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 750+ point rewards are almost gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Rewards Bazaar drops: FUGAZZI, MILK & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From 1,000 to 15,000 points — see what's still in stock`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited quantities on this month's top redemptions`
