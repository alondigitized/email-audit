# 🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals catalog send from Sephora, and the "THIS JUST IN" subject describes it accurately. The Carolina Herrera La Bomba Intensa hero (a red butterfly-winged bottle) is striking. Below it, a clean 2x4 "New Must-Haves" grid shows each product with a "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" or "LIMITED EDITION" tag, a name and a price. Gift-set and body-care tiles and a Holiday Gift Hits block close out the body.
- The top third of the email works against it, though. Three messages stack above the hero: a shipping and pickup strip, a "Savings Event 30% off / COMING SOON" banner, and a free-fragrance-mini offer. Only then does "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" appear. The reader gets three competing offers and a hero with nothing to buy except a "Shop Now" button. The subject also promises news but doesn't say what, and it's the seventh near-identical beauty send in seven days. Compare 2026-10-25's "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" and 2026-10-22's "🎉 Open for new items", which was also a new-arrivals grid.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand catalog email with a good hero image. The cluttered top and the generic subject hold it back, and the subject gives no reason to open this one over yesterday's.

## What's working

- The Carolina Herrera butterfly bottle is a strong, distinctive hero image that draws the eye.
- The "New Must-Haves" grid is easy to scan, with consistent tags, names and prices on every tile.
- Product-level pricing is visible without clicking ($24 to $198).
- The "Spot it. Shop it." and "Holiday Gift Hits" blocks give the email a seasonal angle.
- The Beauty Insider tier strip (Insider / VIP / Rouge) and the 30% savings-event teaser nod to loyalty members.

## What's weak

- Three promotional banners stack above the hero, so the eye doesn't land on a single offer.
- The savings event is marked "COMING SOON" and has no date, so the top banner can't prompt action today.
- The hero says "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" and "Find out more about these beauty picks," but gives no offer or reason to click now.
- The hero's "SHOP NOW" is generic, and several other "SHOP NOW" buttons compete with it.
- The free mini sits behind a $40 purchase and exclusions, and a long block of dense fine print fills the footer.
- Sephora has sent a similar beauty email every day this week, and nothing here sets this one apart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top stack to one message. Lead with the hero and move shipping and the savings-event teaser below it or into the footer. If the savings event is the real story, give it a date and make it the hero.
- 2. Put the offer in the hero. "Free Carolina Herrera mini with $40" is the only concrete incentive, and it's currently the second banner.
- 3. Name a product in the subject and replace the generic "SHOP NOW" with copy like "Shop the Carolina Herrera set."
- 4. Cut the number of tiles and give the best two a "why now" line, such as "Only at Sephora" or "Limited edition, while it lasts."
- 5. Segment by category so a fragrance-first reader sees fragrance in the hero.
