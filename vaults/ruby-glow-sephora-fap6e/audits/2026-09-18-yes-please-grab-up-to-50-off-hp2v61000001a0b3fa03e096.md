# Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- The subject "Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off" lines up with the hero — a coral "SURPRISE! 50% OFF Select Beauty" banner with "An EXTRA day, just for you" — which is a genuine improvement over this brand's usual bait-and-switch pattern (see 2026-09-16's "Beauty's most wanted" that buried the same 50% banner under new-arrival copy). But once you scroll past the fold, this is another Sephora catalog dump: a Chris McMillan shampoo launch, eight new-product tiles, a Rhode try-it kit, a Marc Jacobs concealer, and a loyalty footer, none of which reinforces the 50% hook — it just moves on.
- The "EXTRA day" framing is the one piece of real urgency and it's honest-feeling, tucked with a visible exclusions disclaimer rather than buried in tiny print. Past that, this reads like the median Sephora send in this brand history — competent hero, then an unfocused product wall.
- Verdict: solid subject/hero match earns this above the brand's recent floor, but the endless scroll of unrelated new-arrivals dilutes the offer instead of building on it.

## What's working

- Subject and hero actually match — "50% OFF Select Beauty" is the first thing you see, unlike the 09-16 and 09-14 sends that buried their subject's promise.
- "SURPRISE! ... An EXTRA day" gives a credible, specific urgency reason instead of generic "today only" language.
- Clear green "SHOP NOW" CTA button directly under the offer, high contrast, easy to spot.
- Loyalty tiers (Insider/VIB/Rouge) and Beauty Insider branding are visible near the footer for member context.
- No render bugs — clean grid, product photography loads, text is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- The offer never reappears after the hero — eight+ unrelated new-product tiles (shampoo, mascara, hair gift set, perfume, concealer) crowd out the 50% message.
- "Select Beauty" is vague — no product category or brand names are shown to tell you what's actually included in the deal.
- The email is extremely long or scroll-heavy for a single offer, which buries the CTA is dead weight by the time you reach the fragrance/points section.
- Secondary promos (4X points, Rhode trial kit, Marc Jacobs launch) compete with the headline discount rather than supporting it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a second "50% off" CTA or reminder banner halfway down the email — right now the discount is a one-and-done at the very top and gets lost in the scroll.
- 2. Name at least 2-3 "Select Beauty" categories or brands included in the 50% off directly in the hero subtext, so the offer feels concrete, not vague.
- 3. Cut the email length — move the Chris McMillan launch and the eight-tile new-arrivals grid to a separate send so this one stays focused on the discount.
- 4. Move the loyalty points/4X fragrance callout closer to the top if it's meant to drive incremental urgency, rather than after a long unrelated scroll.
