# ENDS TODAY‼️Up to 50% off select beauty.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's second email blast on May 26 closing out the Stacking Weekend campaign — the fourth send in the series dating back to May 22. The creative is clean, the dual-incentive mechanic (50% off + 500 bonus points) is Sephora's best loyalty weapon, and the addition of a personalized point balance ("Use 374 Beauty Insider Points") and a store-locator module are genuine improvements over the morning "LAST CHANCE" send.
- The problem is pure cadence. A subscriber who opened this morning's LAST CHANCE email and didn't convert is now receiving an afternoon "ENDS TODAY" version of the same email with barely evolved creative — same offer, same layout, just a harder subject line. That's not urgency layering, that's list fatigue in real time.
- Taken in isolation this is a well-executed promo. In context, it's an anxious repeat that trades tomorrow's open rate for today's incremental conversions.

## What's working

- **Dual-incentive hero is still sharp**: 50% off and 500 bonus points side-by-side create an instant stacking calculation that rewards loyal shoppers.
- **Personalized point balance ("374 Beauty Insider Points")**: CRM-level personalization that makes the email feel targeted rather than blasted — the strongest differentiator vs. the morning send.
- **"ENDS TODAY" banner is high-urgency and credible**: Paired with the actual campaign close date, it doesn't feel manufactured.
- **Store locator with "closest store" data**: Adding brick-and-mortar intent capture in a promotional email is smart differentiation — notable that the earlier same-day send didn't have it.
- **Clean render throughout**: No broken images, no layout breakage, product grid in the "Must-Haves" section is well-formatted.

## What's weak

- **Second same-day send on an identical offer**: Subscribers who opened the morning LAST CHANCE email get this as a near-resend; a meaningful percentage will unsubscribe or filter.
- **ALL CAPS + ‼️ in subject**: "ENDS TODAY‼️" reads as shouting and trips spam-adjacent visual signals — a poor brand fit for a premium beauty retailer.
- **"Select beauty" qualifier remains vague after four sends**: Subscribers still can't tell what's in vs. out without clicking; this ambiguity adds pre-click friction.
- **Product thumbnails in "Must-Haves" are too small to be actionable**: At mobile scale they're decorative, not click-driving.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse the LAST CHANCE and ENDS TODAY sends into a single end-of-day email** — both are doing the same urgency job; one of them is pure list erosion.
- 2. **Lead the subject with the loyalty personalization** — surfacing "374 points" in the subject line targets the high-value subscriber who has points to spend, not the entire list.
- 3. **Clarify the "select beauty" scope** — adding a category list ("skincare, makeup, haircare") to either the subject or the hero copy removes doubt and reduces click-to-conversion drop-off.
- 4. **Make the "Must-Haves" thumbnails larger and link to specific PDPs** — this grid should be the click engine; at current thumbnail size it's wallpaper.
- 5. **Downgrade ALL CAPS + ‼️ to title case + single emoji** — "Ends tonight: 50% off + 500 pts" lands the urgency without the shout.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 374 points + 50% off beauty — last hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend ends tonight: 50% off + 500 bonus pts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skincare, makeup, haircare — shop before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use your Insider points before the sale closes tonight`
