# Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Cyber Week Deal" send with a clear hero: "Massive Haul Incoming," then two big 30% off blocks (Ole Henriksen and Milk Makeup) on purple sequins. The eye lands on the offer immediately. It is a stronger and more coherent hero than the 11/19 "Select Dyson" send or the 11/17 hair-gift send, which led with something unrelated to the subject.
- The body gets busy below the hero. It stacks a price-drop product grid, a Rhode launch, 50% off hair tools and 30% off MAC, a Black Friday app preview, a store locator and a gift guide. Most of that is fine for a Cyber Week blast, but the top-of-email promo ("Gift fragrance, get 4x points," code CYBERSCENT) competes with the 30% hero. That is the second offer a reader has to process before reaching the hero.
- The subject, "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️", delivers on the body, but it is generic. It is the third Cyber Week subject in four days, after 11/21 "daily deals just dropped" and 11/22 "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!". The repeated creative and the "deals are here" claim feel like cadence fatigue. The email is competent and honest, but unremarkable.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible: "30% OFF" on purple, with SAVE NOW buttons under each brand.
- The Cyber Week framing is current and matches the subject.
- The product grid shows crossed-out prices ($7.00 vs $10.00, $24.00 vs $24.00 struck, $16.00 vs $32.00), which makes the savings concrete.
- The Black Friday app preview ("50% Off," 11/26 at 6pm PT) gives a credible deadline and pushes app downloads.
- The store locator with hours and the barcode for in-store 4x points bridge online and in-store.

## What's weak

- The top banner offers fragrance points, a different offer from the 30% hero, so the first message is split.
- The email is very long, about 6,200px, with a store block, a gift guide and a stack of fine print. Primary CTAs get diluted.
- Several stacked deals use different mechanics (30% off, up to 30% off, 50% off, 4x points). There is no single "best deal" to anchor on.
- The product grid has no names or CTA buttons, only prices, so it is hard to tell what the items are.
- The fine print is dense, with asterisks and exclusions ("in-stock merchandise only," "Exclusions/terms apply").

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Move the fragrance points banner below the fold or into the footer so the 30% hero is the first thing read.
- 2. Cut the body to about three sections: the hero deals, one product grid with named products and a "Shop" CTA, and the app/Black Friday teaser. Move the store locator and gift guide to a separate send.
- 3. Name the products in the grid, and make each item clickable with a "Shop [brand]" label.
- 4. Vary the subject from the last two Cyber Week sends by leading with a specific brand or number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Ole Henriksen & Milk Makeup, 24 hrs only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cyber Week: 30% off top brands, today only ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus MAC 30% off and hair tools up to 50% off through 11/30.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Black Friday 50% off hits the app 11/26. Get ready now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Two QA findings are confirmed in the source: the plain-text part is empty (0 chars, FAIL), and image alt-text handling is inconsistent. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) came back "not found", but the report attributes that to the AgentMail relay, so I can't treat it as a Sephora defect. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and CTA hrefs weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 36. It reported no broken or redirecting links, so none are confirmed.
- Tracking pixels are present and well-formed:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1` is a 1x1 open pixel.
  - A second hidden pixel at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` has `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
- The `mi_ecmp` campaign ID is consistent across the `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs, so the campaign attribution is coherent.
- Because 36 links were skipped, I can't say whether any of them have tracking problems.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in `<style>`:** an MSO conditional comment sits inside the `@media` block: `.mobileHeaderLogo{…}<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge{…}`.
  - `<!--` and `-->` are only ignored at the top level of CSS. Inside an `@media` block, some parsers will drop the following rule(s). That includes `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, and possibly the other mobile rules after it.
  - The `img.mso-size` rule also never reaches Outlook, because the `[if mso]` comment isn't in the HTML body.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed layout width:** the wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. It may cause horizontal scroll on narrow screens unless a mobile override exists. I couldn't see one in the truncated source.
- **Missing alt text (QA):**
  - `o.gif`: the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel. This QA warning is a false positive.
  - The hidden `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt`, but it is `display:none`, so it has no practical impact.
  - Six `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` images are flagged: `df9af91c42339c07`, `da0148084c4c66f6`, `20e4e51340afcef6`, `727459dc432025a7`, `731160b4c3175e23` and `8e8fdee6cedcdc03`. Their markup is outside the truncated source, so I couldn't confirm the flag. If they are content images (the `/rp/` path suggests rendered content), the missing alt text is a real problem. With images blocked, the email would show nothing.
- **Plain-text part missing:** QA reports 0 chars (FAIL). This is a multipart/alternative gap that affects deliverability and screen-reader or text-only clients.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`.
  - `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source.
- The JSON-LD block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and an empty `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. These are empty template fields, so the Gmail promo annotation will have no data. That is low severity and not user-visible. It also uses `http://schema.org/` rather than `https`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified and not a confirmed defect. Check the raw `.eml` before concluding anything.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data.
- The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the footer, which was truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but I can't verify it from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible source shows no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- The only campaign identifier I can confirm is `mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1` on image and pixel URLs. No `utm_*` parameters are visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part.** The multipart/alternative text part is empty, which is the only FAIL in the QA run.
2. **Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block** in `<style>`. Put it in a separate `<!--[if mso]><style>…</style><![endif]-->` block.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the six `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` images, after confirming their markup.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify headers from the raw message**, not the relay copy. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so the CTA hrefs, UTM parameters and footer compliance elements can be checked.
7. **Minor cleanup:**
   - Fix `robots` to `noindex`.
   - Populate or remove the empty `discountCode` and `PromotionCard` JSON-LD.
   - Treat the `o.gif` alt warning as a QA false positive.
