# These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a gift-guide send. A "MERRY MUST-HAVES" hero leads into a K18 gift-set block, then five "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" limited-edition sets (Eadem, M·A·C, Glow Recipe, L'Oréal Professionnel, Prada) with set prices and "$ value" callouts. A Beauty Insider 4X points code sits mid-email. The product tiles are clean and the pricing is honest and specific. The email fights itself at the top and bottom, though.
- Above the "Merry Must-Haves" hero is a "Get a Free* K18 Trial Size" banner, and below the gifts are two more offers: 15% off moisturizers and Same-Day Unlimited. That makes four competing offers in one send. The subject promises "These gifts," but the first thing you see is a free sample, not a gift. The same thing happened on 10/24 and 10/23, when a trial-size banner pushed the on-subject hero down the page.
- My judgement is a solid gift-season send that doesn't commit. The gift-set tiles are the strongest part, and they sit below a mixed top and above a cluttered bottom. The render is heavily shrunk, so I'm reading layout and large type, not the fine print.

## What's working

- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags with a set price and a "$ value" anchor (e.g. "$38 ($44 value)") make the gift sets feel concrete and worth it.
- Each of the five gift tiles has the same structure: a big product photo, a brand name, a tag line and a price. That makes it easy to scan.
- The 4X Beauty Insider points offer with a code and a 10/28 deadline gives loyal members a credible reason to act.
- The seasonal framing ("Merry Must-Haves," "Hot picks guaranteed to top lists") fits late October gift shopping.
- The "SHOP NOW" button under the hero is clearly visible.

## What's weak

- The free K18 trial-size banner pushes the gift hero down, so the top of the email doesn't match the subject.
- The hero is more headline than visual. "MERRY MUST-HAVES" sits on a flat gradient with no product or gift shown.
- The K18 block carries two offers at once, 4X points and gift sets, so the eye bounces between them.
- The bottom is cluttered. "Spot it. Shop it.", the 15% off tile, Same-Day Unlimited, a Beauty Insider credit-card panel and a large legal block all stack up after the gifts.
- Only the gift sets carry prices. The 15% off tile and the "New and Only at Sephora" tile name no product or price, so they're weak CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift hero to the top and drop the K18 trial-size banner. If a free sample has to run, make it one slim bar, not a full module.
- 2. Pick one offer. For example, lead with the 4X points code and make it the single CTA, with the gift sets as proof.
- 3. Replace the flat "MERRY MUST-HAVES" hero with a collage of the actual gift sets, so the image matches the subject.
- 4. Cut the bottom modules to one. Keep the moisturizer 15% off or Same-Day Unlimited, not both, and name a product and price.
- 5. Rename "SHOP NOW" to something like "Shop gift sets under $50".
