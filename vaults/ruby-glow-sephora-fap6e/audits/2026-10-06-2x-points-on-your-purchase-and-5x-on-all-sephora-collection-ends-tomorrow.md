# 🚨2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells urgency on a points promo: 2X on your purchase, 5X on all Sephora Collection, ends tomorrow. The body mostly sells something else. The points banner sits at the top with the code SAYPOINTS and a 10/7 deadline, but the "Holiday Vaults Are Here" gift sets take over from there. That block runs six tall product cards: the Sephora Collection advent calendar at $99, Laneige at $149, a vanity case at $155, a sheet mask set at $50, the Tom Ford discovery set at $100, and Crown Affair at $272. The points story comes back near the bottom as a 2X/3X/4X/5X ladder.
- The result is two campaigns in one send, and the one the subject promised is the weaker one visually. This is the 10/1 "STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points" email re-sent with a deadline, so it's the second ask for the same mechanic this week. The 10/5 "APP EARLY ACCESS" email's app hook returns in a small tile near the bottom. The email is very long, about 9,800px at full size, and the final stretch is a coupon code, a Kohl's barcode and legal copy. It's polished and on-brand, but the focus is split and the length buries the deadline.
- Judgement: a solid, professional seasonal send. The subject's urgency is only partly paid off by the layout.

## What's working

- The top banner puts "2X" and "5X" badges, the code SAYPOINTS and "Ends 10/7" above the fold, so the subject's promise is confirmed immediately.
- The vault cards show a price plus a "$X value" anchor, such as $99 for a $277 value. That is a concrete, easy-to-compare offer.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags add a scarcity reason that fits the season.
- The layout is consistent, with gold-framed cards and a single product per card. It reads cleanly with no visible broken images or overlapping text.
- The Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal and Venmo logos under the hero reduce price friction for gift-sized purchases.

## What's weak

- The deadline-driven points offer is a thin strip, while the vaults take roughly 60% of the email. The eye lands on gift sets, not the thing that ends tomorrow.
- The email is too long. The points ladder, app early access, auto-replenish, SAVETEN and the Kohl's barcode all compete for the same click.
- The CTAs are inconsistent: "GET GIFTING," "TRY IT," "SHOP NOW," "USE CODE SAVETEN" and "USE CODE SAYPOINTS." There is no single primary action.
- The points offer appears twice, at the top and again mid-lower, with different graphics. That feels repetitive after the 10/1 send.
- The bottom section is mostly dense legal copy and a barcode block. It makes the email feel cluttered at the end.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the points deadline the hero and cut the vault grid to 2–3 products. One red "ENDS TOMORROW" banner with one "Earn 5X now" button would match the subject and lift clicks the most.
- 2. Pick one primary CTA and reuse it. The points code should be one tap or auto-applied, not a code to copy.
- 3. Move the app early-access and auto-replenish tiles into a separate send. Mixing them in dilutes the urgency.
- 4. Make the SAYPOINTS code and the exact deadline time visible in the first screen, such as "Ends 10/7 at 11:59pm PT."
- 5. Cut the repeated 2X/3X/4X/5X ladder, or make it a simple table showing which tier applies to which purchase.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 5X points on Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tomorrow: double points + 5X on Sephora Collection`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS before 10/7. Advent calendars from $50 count too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on holiday vaults and earn up to 5X Insider points.`
