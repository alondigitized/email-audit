# STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This email is clear on the offer and cluttered everywhere else. The header block does the work. "STARTS TOMORROW" sits over two medallions, Insider 10% off (11/3–11/9) and Sephora Collection 30% off (10/30–11/9), with the code ALONSALE and a "BUILD YOUR BASKET" button. The medallions put the offer above the fold. The 11/3 start date creates real urgency, since this is the Insider tier that the 10/29 "Coming soon" strip previewed.
- The rest of the email is a long scroll with competing content. It has a category grid, a "Beauty on Demand" strip, a "Save on these faves" product row, a Spot it. Shop it. block, a gift-guide block, and a legal footer. Two barcodes close it out. There is no single hero product, so the eye has nowhere to land after the medallions.
- The subject says "10% off EVERYTHING," but the render shows a 10% tier for Insiders and a separate 30% tier on Sephora Collection, with "limitations/terms apply" footnotes. This is the fifth near-identical promo since 10/30, and a sixth send in the 30% Sephora Collection thread. It is a competent send, but it is fatigued.

## What's working

- The two side-by-side medallions state both discounts and both date ranges at the top.
- "STARTS TOMORROW" is a credible deadline, and it follows the 10/30 "STARTS TODAY" send.
- Sephora is a recognizable sender, and the black, gold and red styling keeps the brand look consistent.
- The "BUILD YOUR BASKET" button is a clear, visible CTA.
- The barcodes at the bottom make in-store redemption simple, including the Kohl's one.
- The product row shows prices, for example the Armani My Way at $174.

## What's weak

- The subject's "EVERYTHING" overstates the offer. The render shows Insider-tier 10% with fine-print exclusions.
- The 10/30 "STARTS TODAY" send and the 11/1 send already pushed the same 30% Sephora Collection offer. Repeating it here dilutes this email's own news.
- After the header there are about eight modules with no clear hierarchy, so the page is a very long scroll.
- Several product images in the "Save on these faves" row are small and the copy is tiny. The prices are hard to read on mobile.
- The legal fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "limitations/terms apply" footnotes are small.
- The "Free gift with purchase drops 11/3 at 11AM PT" strip is easy to miss, even though it is the most interesting hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one audience. Make the Insider 10% the hero, and move the Sephora Collection 30% to a smaller secondary strip.
- 2. Cut the body to a hero, one product row and a gift-guide link. Drop "Spot it. Shop it." and the duplicate category modules.
- 3. Promote the free-gift-with-purchase drop time (11/3, 11AM PT) into the hero, because it gives a reason to come back.
- 4. Make the subject honest about the tier, for example "Insiders: 10% off starts tomorrow".
- 5. Enlarge the price and product copy in the faves row so it reads on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Insiders: 10% off starts tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow 11AM: free gift + 10% off 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 30% off Sephora Collection. Use code ALONSALE at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Insider savings and a free gift with purchase, 11/3–11/9.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has no hard breakage in the visible portion, but the email ships with no plain-text part (0 chars, the one QA FAIL). Compliance and authentication can't be verified from the captured data. The HTML is truncated, so footer, UTM and personalization checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Visible links use redirect wrappers, for example `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a19cca0369ad11356e9666b528/939fd687…?__dU__=…`. The domain is first-party and consistent with the open pixel.
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 links and skipped 40. No probe failures are reported, but the 40 skipped links are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…' style="display:none; …mso-hide: all"/>` has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. This is the source of the QA "missing alt" warning. It is hidden, so the screen-reader impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Pixel placement:** The pixel sits after the preheader inside the same `<td>`, so it fires on any HTML render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS (inferred, not rendered):** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS rules. In standards-based parsers the `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` selectors are likely invalid. That would drop the `.mso-size` rule and the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows it.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media` rules don't override it. If the truncated remainder doesn't either, mobile clients will scroll horizontally. The inner tables use `max-width:700px`, which is correct.
- **Zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it (accessibility).
- **Language:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` uses an invalid token. The valid value is `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD block has `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` (empty value) and `"@type":"PromotionCard"`, which isn't a valid Gmail promotion type. Gmail will ignore or reject the markup, so the block is dead weight.
- **Preheader:** Nested `display:none` divs followed by `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding are implemented correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `__dU__` and `/O/` tokens are resolved tracking IDs. The truncated body can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The message is HTML-only, which hurts spam scoring and accessibility. This is a real defect in the sent message.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. It can't be called a sender defect without the raw headers. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is absent,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. This is a relay artifact, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer:** The visible source doesn't reach the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address can't be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Links go through the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirector, so UTMs and the final destination aren't visible in the source. No probe results (final URL, status, redirect chain) were provided. Landing page alignment with the "10% off EVERYTHING" offer can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **High:** Add a `text/plain` alternative. The 0-char text part is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **High:** Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't treat the warnings as defects until then.
3. **Medium:** Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule and into its own `<!--[if mso]><style>` block, so the following mobile rules aren't invalidated.
4. **Medium:** Confirm a mobile override for the 700px `min-width` table. Otherwise switch to a fluid `width:100%; max-width:700px` pattern.
5. **Low:** Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixel, `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and remove `maximum-scale=1`.
6. **Low:** Remove or fix the JSON-LD (empty `discountCode`, invalid `PromotionCard`) and the invalid `robots` meta.
7. **Process:** Capture the full HTML and raw headers, and raise the link-probe limit (40 of 52 skipped), so UTM, footer and unsubscribe checks can be completed.
