# Get 10% off when you stock up on new palettes 💖
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- The strongest read is that this is a clear, on-brand palette push. The header does the work. A "LIMITED TIME ONLY: FREE SAME-DAY DELIVERY" banner sits above two medallions: Sephora Collection 30% off (10/30–11/9) and Insider 10% off (11/3–11/9). A "Hot New Palettes" hero follows with a SHOP NOW button. The subject promises "10% off when you stock up on new palettes," and the render supports that with the Insider medallion and a palette-led body.
- The image is heavily downscaled, so I can read the layout and headlines but not the fine print or every product price. The body is long. It runs through Huda Beauty, Patrick Ta and Danessa Myricks, then a product grid, a gift-with-purchase block, a hair gift sets block, a Kohl's store locator and a footer. The palette story is strong in the first third. After that it dilutes into generic Sephora modules.
- This is the sixth Insider-event send in six days, after 11/6, 11/5, 11/4 and 11/3 (twice) and 11/2. The creative is solid but not differentiated from "10% off our most-searched beauty" on 11/5. Judgement: competent and relevant, but lost in a flood of near-identical promos.

## What's working

- The header stacks the free same-day delivery banner and the two dated medallions (30% and 10%), so the offer is clear immediately.
- The "Hot New Palettes" hero with a black SHOP NOW button has a clear primary CTA and visible hierarchy.
- The subject and hero match, since both lead with palettes.
- Brand features (Huda Beauty, Patrick Ta, Danessa Myricks) give named, recognizable hooks.
- The product grid labels items "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" and shows prices (for example $55, $72, $64), which supports a purchase decision.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with a gift-with-purchase block, hair gift sets, a Kohl's locator and gift guide links all competing with the palette story.
- The 10% off applies to Insiders only, and the code line in the header is small. The subject doesn't signal the member condition.
- "Stock up" in the subject implies a bulk discount, but the body shows plain 10% off with no bundle mechanic.
- The Kohl's store map and hours block is irrelevant to a palette shopper and pushes the CTAs down.
- Several competing urgency messages (same-day delivery, 30% off ending 11/9, GWP) dilute the focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the product grid. Drop the Kohl's locator, hair gift sets and one of the two GWP and gift modules, so the palette story ends on a final CTA.
- 2. Put one specific product CTA on each brand feature (for example "Shop Huda's Bronzer and Contour Palette") instead of relying on one generic SHOP NOW.
- 3. Make the Insider condition and code visible in the hero, for example "Insiders: 10% off with code ALLONSALE".
- 4. Add reviews or ratings to the product grid.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a conventional XHTML table layout (700px fixed width) with two tracking pixels and hidden preheader text. The automated QA flags one hard failure, a missing plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication warnings may be relay artifacts. The truncated HTML shows a few markup defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** QA probed 12 material links and reported no failures. 46 links were skipped, mostly footer, utility and social links, so their status is unverified.
- **Two tracking pixels are present and working as designed:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a hidden open-tracking image (`display:none; mso-hide:all`).
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider` is a 1x1 pixel carrying a user ID and a campaign ID.
- The `_Inside` value in the QA output is a truncation of the `_Insider` value in the source. It is not a tracking defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS placement:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. The conditional comment is not valid there. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it, including `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` may force horizontal scroll in clients that ignore media queries. The truncated source doesn't show a mobile override for this table, so confirm against the full HTML.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is harmless in email.
- **Alt text, one real finding and one false positive:**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` open pixel has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is minimal, but add `alt=""`.
  - The `o.gif` image was flagged by QA, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That is correct for a decorative image, so this is a false positive.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` carries no campaign context.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!-- US shell snippet -->` exposes internal template naming.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and an empty `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. This is incomplete structured data, probably an unpopulated template variable. Most clients ignore it, but Gmail may not render promo annotations.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part missing:** the QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars". This hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a capture limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- The footer, physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are beyond the truncated HTML, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The truncated source contains no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider`, which is consistent with the subject ("new palettes") and the 2026-11-07 send date.
- QA's link probe reported no redirect or landing failures for the 12 links it checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify the raw message headers** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, and whether a `text/plain` part exists). This will show whether the QA failures come from the relay or from the sender.

5. Add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com/O/…` open pixel and change `no index` to `noindex`.
6. Populate or remove the empty `discountCode` and `PromotionCard` JSON-LD.
