# 4X points on fragrance (yes way!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a rerun of the same 4X points/fragrance offer from 2026-09-19 ("Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS"), and it's the more elegant of the two executions. The subject "4X points on fragrance (yes way!)" is direct and lines up immediately with the hero banner — "4X POINTS¹ ON ALL FRAGRANCE" with a clear "SHOP NOW" — so there's no bait-and-switch on open, unlike several sends in this brand's recent history.
- Where it earns real credit is structure: instead of a flat product grid, the email organizes fragrance into themed "combo" moments — Dessert-Core Duo, Soft Girl Combo, Date-Night Stack — each pairing two products with prices and a clear "clean" badge where relevant. That's a genuinely different creative approach from the "50% off Select Beauty" pink-banner send this brand has leaned on repeatedly (09-16, 09-17, 09-18). It reads more like an editorial fragrance guide than a blanket discount blast.
- The miss is depth of urgency and offer clarity. "4X points" is a loyalty mechanic, not a price cut, and the deadline (9/23) has already passed relative to today's date — this is a rerun of stale campaign copy being re-audited. There's also a LOT of scrolling required (7+ combo sections) before hitting the Beauty Insider tier block, which dilutes the single clear CTA a busy inbox scanner needs.

## What's working

- Hero banner directly matches the subject line — 4X points, code NEWSCENTS, shop now button all visible in one glance.
- Thematically grouped "combo" sections (Dessert-Core, Soft Girl, Date-Night) give product pairs a narrative hook rather than a plain grid.
- "Only at Sephora" and "New" badges are used consistently to signal exclusivity per product.
- Clean/vegan callouts (green checkmark badges) are visible on relevant SKUs for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- Prices are shown for every product pair, so there's no click-to-discover-price friction.

## What's weak

- The offer is a points multiplier, not a discount — for a shopper motivated by savings, "4X points" is a weaker hook than the 50%-off sends this brand has run all week.
- Deadline "through 9/23" creates false urgency once this email is being reviewed after that date — a rerun of dated copy.
- The email is extremely long (7+ distinct sections plus a loyalty tier block) before reaching Beauty Insider status — most subscribers won't scroll that far.
- No single dominant hero product — attention is split across 6+ fragrance pairings with no clear "start here."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to 3 combo sections max and move the rest to a "see more pairings" link — length is diluting the primary 4X offer.
- 2. Add a points-value translator near the hero (e.g., "that's up to 400 points on a $100 bottle") since raw multiplier language is abstract for less-engaged loyalty members.
- 3. Move the countdown/deadline to the very top in bold, since "through 9/23" is buried in small print under the hero.
- 4. Consolidate the "New to fragrance layering?" AI chat CTA and the "Spot it. Shop it." editorial block — they compete with the main offer for clicks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your fragrance haul just got 4X the points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack scents, stack points — ends 9/23`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mix, match, and multiply your rewards on every fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer your signature scent, earn 4X points doing it`
