# Earn 4X points on hair care for your healthiest hair yet ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third hair-focused email in four days — and the second 4X hair-care-points offer in that window (July 19 scored 8/10 for the same deal). The mechanics are solid: clean render, products segmented by hair type, an unambiguous SHOP NOW CTA, and a Kerastase gift-with-purchase hook in the top banner. But the editorial hero ("Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science") pivots away from the loyalty offer the subject promises, and the 4X points badge doesn't surface until roughly 60% of the way down the email. The subject–hero contract breaks before the first scroll.
- The bigger problem is cadence. If I opened the July 19 "4X points on all hair care" send and ignored it, Sephora sending the identical promotion three days later with shinier creative doesn't fix the underlying reason I didn't act — it just erodes the perceived scarcity of what should feel like a member-only moment.
- The email is competent and clean. It is not special. A subscriber who noticed the July 19 send will feel this as filler, not an event.

## What's working

- **Kerastase GWP in the banner** — gift-with-purchase hook is immediately visible above the hero; follows the strong pattern from July 20's Charlotte Tilbury send.
- **Product segmentation by hair type** — Dry, Frizzy, Fine, Thick sections reduce decision fatigue and signal editorial curation.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no layout breaks, no overlapping elements across the full scroll.
- **SHOP NOW CTA** — large, visible, above the editorial midpoint; no friction to entry.
- **Loyalty badge present** — the 4X circle callout appears in the lower third, anchoring the subject's promise even if it arrives late.

## What's weak

- **Hero/subject disconnect** — subject promises a loyalty win (4X points); hero delivers a science lecture ("Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science"). The offer doesn't reappear until deep scroll.
- **4X badge buried** — the core incentive lives ~60% down the email; any mobile reader who skims the hero and doesn't scroll never sees the offer the subject line sold them on.
- **No visible deadline** — 4X points events feel special when time-bounded; nothing in the render communicates expiry, undercutting urgency.
- **Cadence self-sabotage** — the July 19 4X hair care send was the same promotion. Three days and one new concept doesn't justify a repeat hit; it confirms the offer isn't actually scarce.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface the 4X badge in the hero** — swap "It's Science" editorial for a dual message: science-forward headline + inline 4X callout. The subject promised the deal; the hero should honor it within the first viewport.
- 2. **Add a visible expiration** — even "ends Sunday" in the hero banner converts passive interest into action and makes the cadence repetition feel like a reminder, not a re-send.
- 3. **Kill the repeat** — if the July 19 event is still live, send a "last chance" variant, not a fresh creative with the same promo. If it closed and opened again, say so explicitly ("New window: 4X is back for 48 hours").
- 4. **Shorten and sharpen the subject** — 58 chars with emoji is at the mobile truncation edge; "your healthiest hair yet" is filler after the offer is already stated.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on hair care — this weekend only ✨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 4X points event is open (ends Sunday)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Kerastase gift with $65+ — shop shampoo, serums, scalp treatments and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complimentary Kerastase + 4X Insider points through Sunday. Shop by hair type.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` tag**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` present. Screen readers default to system language; WCAG 3.1.1 requires language declaration. Fix: `<html lang="en" ...>`.

**Issue — `text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
This suppresses OS-level font scaling for users who rely on it (low-vision users on mobile). The property should be `100%` rather than `none`, or scoped only to elements where it's structurally necessary (e.g., buttons).

**Non-issue — `robots` meta typo**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
"no index" with a space is technically malformed (`noindex` is the valid token), but email clients don't crawl via robots meta, so this has no functional impact.

**Non-issue — XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Old but functionally correct for email. No action needed.
