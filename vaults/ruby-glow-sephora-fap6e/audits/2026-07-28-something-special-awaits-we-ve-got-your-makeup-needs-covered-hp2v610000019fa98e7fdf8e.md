# Something special awaits. We’ve got your makeup needs covered.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the second time in four days Sephora has opened with the exact phrase "Something special awaits" — the July 25 send paired it with "This is what your hair wants," this one pairs it with "We've got your makeup needs covered." Both are teaser-plus-promise subject constructions, and both mislead: the July 25 email led with a lip sale despite promising hair content, and this one promises "makeup needs" but the body is dominated by fragrance (Prada, ViktorRolf), hair care (Kérastase, Fable & Mane, Pureology), and skincare (Farmacy, Shiseido) — only one or two items in the entire "Trending Picks" grid (Armani Beauty skin tint) are actually color cosmetics.
- The one genuinely strong piece of this email is the top: a pink hero banner with "Get 20% off Select LANEIGE," "Celebrate National Lip Day," and "Today only!" — concrete, time-bound, and visible without scrolling. Everything below that hero, though, is a generic multi-category product dump dressed up as "Beauty for You," which dilutes the sharp opening offer into an unfocused catalog scroll.
- Judgment: strong top third, weak framing and follow-through. The hero mechanics (offer, urgency, CTA) are executed well, but the subject line over-promises a makeup focus the body doesn't deliver, and the recycled "Something special awaits" opener is becoming a brand tic that a repeat subscriber will start to tune out.

## What's working

- The pink hero banner leads with a specific, time-bound offer ("20% off Select LANEIGE," "Today only!") right at the top, visible with zero scrolling.
- "SHOP NOW" CTA buttons are bold, high-contrast, and repeated at every section break, so there's never ambiguity about the next action.
- Specific products are named with prices ("Lip Sleeping Mask in Acai Mango Smoothie, $25," "Lip Glowy Balm in Acai Mango Smoothie, $19") rather than just linking to a category page.
- Insider status and points balance ("You have 2 points") are visible at the very top, reinforcing loyalty relevance.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text, no broken layout across the long scroll.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "your makeup needs covered," but the body is overwhelmingly fragrance, hair care, and skincare — real makeup (color cosmetics) is barely represented, echoing the same subject/content mismatch flagged in the July 25 and July 26 sends.
- "Something special awaits" is a reused opener from the July 25 email just three days prior — a subscriber who opened both will notice the recycled hook.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the render, despite eight products being pitched in the "Trending Picks" grid.
- The email sprawls into an unfocused multi-category catalog after the strong Laneige hero, diluting the one clear offer into generic browsing.
- Subject line itself carries no urgency or specificity — "Today only!" urgency only appears once you're already in the body, so it does nothing to drive the open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the actual hero offer instead of a vague teaser — put "20% off Laneige lip picks, today only" in the subject line itself so urgency and specificity drive the open, not just the click.
- 2. Retire "Something special awaits" as an opener for at least a few sends — it was just used July 25 and is starting to read as filler.
- 3. Either rename the "Trending Picks for You" section to reflect its actual mixed-category contents (fragrance/hair/skin) or curate it to genuinely makeup-only items if the subject is going to promise "makeup needs."
- 4. Add a review count or star rating to at least the hero Laneige products — with zero social proof anywhere, there's no trust signal beyond the Sephora name itself.
- 5. Trim the email — eight unrelated "Trending Picks" plus three more promo blocks below the fold is a lot of unfocused real estate competing with the one sharp offer at the top.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on Lip Sleeping Mask + Lip Glowy Balm, plus new arrivals across hair and skin.`
