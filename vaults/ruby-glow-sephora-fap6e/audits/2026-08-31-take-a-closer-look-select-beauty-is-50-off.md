# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Sephora runs the bait-and-switch play again. The subject line — "Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀" — puts the discount front and center, but the actual email opens with a red "Beauty Insider Celebration" banner ($5 off 250 points, 4X points) and then a full "THE NEXT BIG THING" new-arrivals hero (Natasha Denona, Violette_FR) with a whole product grid underneath before the 50% off Clinique block finally shows up, roughly two-thirds down the page. This is the same structural move seen in the 8/29 "Select brands are 50% off" send (5.00/10) — promise the discount in the subject, bury it under a prestige/new-arrivals wall.
- Once you do reach the offer, it's actually decent: a real 50% off Clinique moisture surge product with a "TODAY ONLY" urgency stamp. But by that point the reader has already scrolled past two unrelated hero sections and an eight-item product grid. Compared to the 8/28 send ("50% off select beauty starts today," 6.00/10) which led with the discount, this is a regression back to the catalog-first structure the brand keeps defaulting to.
- Judgement: this is a subject-line-driven click that the layout actively undermines. Fix the hierarchy and this is a 7+; as rendered, it's a middling catalog email wearing a deal-drop subject line.

## What's working

- No render bugs — layout is clean, images load, text is legible throughout.
- The 50% off block, once you reach it, is a real single-product deal (Clinique) with clear "TODAY ONLY" urgency and honest fine-print disclosure ("Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last.").
- Beauty Insider point multiplier (4X) is visible up top for loyalty members.
- Product grid has clear "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA," and "CLEAN" badges that aid quick scanning.
- Clear, unmissable "SHOP NOW" CTAs repeated at every section.

## What's weak

- The subject's headline offer (50% off) is not the hero — "THE NEXT BIG THING" new-arrivals section occupies the top of the email instead.
- Visual hierarchy misleads: the eye lands on Beauty Insider points and new launches first, not the promised discount.
- The 50% off section is small relative to its subject-line billing — one modest banner sandwiched between an 8-item grid and a "Spot it. Shop it." section.
- Cadence concern: this is at least the third email in ten days leading with a "50% off select" framing (8/28, 8/29, now 8/31), and only one of the three actually delivered the offer up top.
- Too many competing CTAs (new arrivals, app download, Black-owned beauty, new Tower 28 launch) dilute the single offer promised in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 50% off Clinique banner to the very top**, above "THE NEXT BIG THING." If the subject sells the discount, the discount should be the first thing rendered — this is the single highest-impact fix, and it's the same fix this brand still hasn't made since the 8/29 send.
- 2. Cut or shrink the new-arrivals hero to a smaller secondary module rather than the first full-width section.
- 3. Expand the offer beyond one Clinique SKU, or make clear in-hero that "select beauty" covers more brands — right now the visual promises one product, not a category-wide sale.
- 4. Trim the mid-email clutter (App Early Access, Need to Know Beauty, Tower 28 swipe serum) into a single "more from Sephora" strip so the deal isn't competing for attention.
- 5. Vary the subject template — three near-identical "50% off select" subjects in 10 days risks fatigue; alternate wording/emoji more.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clinique moisture surge: 50% off today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off beauty pick is waiting 👀`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One day only: half off select Clinique favorites, no code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 4X points for Beauty Insiders — ends tonight.`
