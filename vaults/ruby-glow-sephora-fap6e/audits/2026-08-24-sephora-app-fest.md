# ✨ Sephora App Fest ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora running back the App Fest concept exactly one week after the 8/17 send ("💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫," 7.00/10) — same day-by-day calendar structure (Tuesday 25% off Tarte/Dyson/Sephora Collection, Wednesday 25% off Anastasia/MUFE/Amika, Thursday 10% off + Afterpay, Friday Charlotte Tilbury early access), just re-skinned and re-dated. Structurally it's still the strongest format this brand history has produced — a real calendar with real % discounts and named brands beats the flat catalog grids — but running it twice inside eight days with the identical layout and mechanic reads as reused creative, not a fresh event.
- The top of the email is doing too much before the reader ever gets to App Fest: a "Choose a Trial Size" GWP module sits above the fold, ahead of the actual campaign hero, which buries the thing the subject line promised. Once you scroll past that, the calendar itself is clean and legible, and the 500-points "Beauty Challenge" CTA is a nice loyalty layer. But this is a long, scroll-heavy email with a lot of competing CTAs (Shop to Redeem, Shop Now, Join Now, three more Shop Now buttons at the bottom) and no single dominant offer — everything is "25% off select brands" or "10% off," qualified by exclusions, which caps how strong the pull feels.
- Judgement: solid structural repeat of a good idea, dragged down by a buried hero, cadence fatigue, and a wall of asterisked exclusions. Call it a 6/10 — better than the flat catalog sends, weaker than the 8/17 original because it's the same trick twice.

## What's working

- The day-by-day deal calendar (Tue–Fri) is genuinely scannable with bold percentage badges, dates, and named brands (Tarte, Dyson, Anastasia Beverly Hills, MUFE, Charlotte Tilbury).
- "+500 Bonus Points" Beauty Challenge module gives Beauty Insider members a concrete, visible loyalty incentive tied to a specific action.
- Bottom modules ("Hair Faves Made Fancy," "All Over Your Feed," "Beauty Under $25") add merchandising variety without cluttering the main calendar.
- No render bugs — text, badges, and product photography all display cleanly with no overlap or broken images.

## What's weak

- The "Choose a Trial Size" GWP banner sits above the App Fest hero, so the subject line's promise isn't the first thing you see when you open.
- This is the second App Fest send in eight days (8/17 was the launch) with an identical mechanic and layout — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who opened last week's.
- Every offer is qualified ("Stacks with other offers," "Exclusions/terms apply," asterisks on nearly every module) — none of it reads as a clean, honest headline number.
- Too many competing CTAs (Shop to Redeem, Shop Now ×4, Join Now) with no single visual anchor — the eye has nowhere obvious to land first.
- Friday's Charlotte Tilbury slot has no discount at all, just "24-hour early access" — noticeably weaker than the three days before it and dilutes the calendar's momentum.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the App Fest hero (the "APP FEST — YES, THERE ARE MORE DEALS!" banner) to the very top, above the trial-size GWP module — the subject line sells App Fest, the email should open on App Fest.
- 2. Differentiate this send from 8/17's launch email explicitly — e.g., "Week 2" framing or "New deals just dropped" — so repeat openers don't feel like they're rereading last week's email.
- 3. Pick one day's offer (likely Thursday's 10% + Afterpay, since it's dual-mechanic) and make it the single hero CTA above the fold instead of giving all four days equal visual weight.
- 4. Cut the exclusions/asterisk density in the visible copy — push "Exclusions/terms apply" into one footer line instead of repeating it under nearly every module.
- 5. Give Friday's Charlotte Tilbury slot an actual discount or GWP to match the momentum of Tue–Thu, or cut it if there's nothing to offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New App Fest deals just dropped ✨`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25% off Tarte, Dyson & Sephora Collection — today only in the app`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: earn up to 500 Beauty Insider points this week`
