# Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23 with code NEWSCENTS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the most coherent Sephora send I've seen in this run — subject, hero, and offer actually line up. "Get 4X points on all fragrance until 9/23" opens directly into a top banner reading "4X POINTS¹ ON ALL FRAGRANCE / Earn more points on purchases with code NEWSCENTS through 9/23" followed by a Prada hero bottle stamped "4X BEAUTY INSIDER POINT PER DOLLAR." No bait-and-switch, no buried subject — a genuine break from the "50% off Beauty" banner this brand keeps recycling regardless of subject line (2026-09-16, 2026-09-15).
- That said, it's a loyalty-points offer, not a price cut, and it's stacked with unrelated cross-sell almost immediately: a "Get a Mini" GWP teaser, then a full "BEAUTY FOR ALL" generic shop-now section, before the fragrance content even loads. The email tries to be a fragrance promo and a general new-arrivals catalog at once, which dilutes the one clean hook it opens with.
- Solid open-likelihood email, middling click email — the reward (points, not dollars off) is a softer motivator, and the layout buries a large "His New Scent Lineup" grid of gifting products under a wall of unrelated new-arrival cards before you ever get to a single clear CTA tied to the subject's promise.

## What's working

- Subject and hero genuinely match — 4X points banner and code (NEWSCENTS) appear immediately at the top.
- Clear deadline stated twice ("through 9/23"), which supports credible urgency.
- Prada hero image is clean, well-lit, on-brand, no render bugs visible.
- "Beauty Insider" 4X badge reinforces loyalty-member relevance for a program member.
- Legal/footer/redemption instructions are unusually clear (repeats the code and window at the very bottom near the barcode).

## What's weak

- The fragrance offer is immediately diluted by an unrelated "Get a Mini" GWP call-out and a generic "BEAUTY FOR ALL / These picks will on repeat" section before any fragrance product appears.
- After the Prada hero, the "His New Scent Lineup" section pivots to a large grid of non-fragrance new arrivals (Benefit, Peter Thomas Roth, Olaplex, Glossier, Fresh, DAE, Beautyblender) — none of which are fragrance, undercutting the "4X on fragrance" premise.
- Offer is points-based, not a price discount — weaker purchase trigger than the brand's usual "50% off" sends.
- No specific fragrance products/prices are shown beyond the single Prada hero — no product grid of eligible scents to click into.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled: three distinct promo concepts (mini GWP, general new arrivals, fragrance points) compete before the eye settles anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "BEAUTY FOR ALL" generic section entirely (or move it below the fold) and replace it with a fragrance-specific product grid (3-6 eligible scents with prices) directly under the Prada hero — right now there's no fragrance CTA to click beyond one image.
- 2. Move "Get a Mini" GWP teaser down or drop it — it competes with the fragrance hook in the first screen.
- 3. Add a visible dollar-equivalent or "earn up to $X in rewards" framing near the 4X badge — points are abstract, a dollar anchor converts better.
- 4. Rename "His New Scent Lineup" section copy to match its actual (non-fragrance) content, or replace the section with real fragrance SKUs so subject/body stay aligned all the way down.
- 5. Tighten subject to lead with urgency plus code visibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on fragrance — ends 9/23`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fragrance haul earns 4X points thru 9/23`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop new arrivals from YSL, Prada, Rabanne and more before it ends`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code NEWSCENTS at checkout — in stores and online`
