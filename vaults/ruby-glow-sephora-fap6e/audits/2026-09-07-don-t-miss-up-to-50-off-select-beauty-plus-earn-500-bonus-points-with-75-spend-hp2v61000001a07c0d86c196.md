# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Same bait-and-switch Sephora has run all week: subject promises "up to 50% off select beauty," the top banner delivers a "Stacking Weekend" 50%-off box with bonus points — then the entire rest of the email, from "ELEVATE WITH EADEM" all the way down, is a single-brand product showcase for Eadem, a skincare line most subscribers have never heard of. This is functionally the same structure as 9/4's "Stacking Weekend" send and 9/2/8/31/8/29's "50% off then unrelated story" pattern, just with Eadem subbed in for whatever brand ran last time.
- The Eadem section itself is well produced — clean product photography, consistent "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badging, clear per-item pricing — but it's mismatched with the subject line's promise and buries the actual discount at the very bottom of the email in a small "50% Off Select Beauty" banner that most readers will never scroll to.
- Judgement: this is a repeat-offender structural problem, not a one-off. Score reflects a decently executed brand spotlight wrapped in a subject line that doesn't describe the email.

## What's working

- The Stacking Weekend hero banner at the very top is clean, on-brand, and states both the % off and the 500-point bonus clearly.
- Eadem product shots are consistent, well-lit, and each has a visible price and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" exclusivity badge.
- No render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is clean top to bottom.
- Second CTA block ("50% Off Select Beauty") at the bottom reinforces the sale with a product thumbnail and price.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "up to 50% off select beauty" but ~80% of the email body is a single-brand (Eadem) skincare showcase with zero connection to that offer.
- The 50%-off deal is only reiterated once, in a small banner near the very bottom — anyone who doesn't scroll the full email misses it entirely.
- No urgency cue anywhere in the body despite the subject's hourglass emoji (⏳) implying time pressure.
- This is the same "50% off banner → unrelated brand story" structure seen on 8/29, 8/31, 9/2, 9/3, 9/5 — the cadence is repetitive and the pattern is now predictable/skippable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Either make this genuinely an Eadem spotlight email (drop the 50%-off subject line entirely) or make it a Stacking Weekend email (drop most of the Eadem block) — stop conflating the two, since the mismatch is now a recurring credibility problem across five-plus sends.
- 2. Move the bottom "50% Off Select Beauty" banner up directly under the hero, so the offer the subject promises is visible without scrolling.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends tonight at midnight") near the hero banner to justify the ⏳ emoji in the subject.
- 4. Rotate the "brand of the day" showcase concept only when it's clearly labeled as such in the subject line, so open rate isn't earned under false pretenses.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend: 50% off select beauty ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, meet Eadem — our newest skincare exclusive`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `500 bonus points on $75+ — offer ends soon`
