# So hot right now 💯
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora running the same play as the July 31 "Hot on social for a reason 🌶️" send — a trending-picks roundup instead of another lip-discount blast — and it mostly works for the same reasons. The product grid (ViktorRolf, Kérastase, Farmacy, MAC, NEST, Sephora Collection, Prada, Laneige) is varied, mostly exclusive-to-Sephora, and gives a beauty shopper real browsing material rather than a single flat percentage-off claim.
- Where it loses points versus the 7/31 send is focus. That email opened clean with one trending-products idea; this one stacks a 4X-points foundation/concealer promo, a free GWP mini offer, a "BEAUTY for YOU" hero, a Shark Beauty device callout, an 8-item grid, a mystery-reward game, a K18 app-exclusive plug, and a Laneige skincare block — all before the footer. It's not broken, just crowded, and the subject line "So hot right now 💯" is the vaguest hook Sephora has used in this batch of audits; it promises nothing specific the way "up to 50% off lip" or "free foundation sample" did.
- Net: a solid, well-merchandised email dragged down by a generic subject and too many competing top-of-funnel offers. Good content, undersold framing.

## What's working

- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder copy anywhere in the scroll.
- Genuine loyalty and GWP mechanics are visible (4X points on foundation/concealer, free mini with $50 spend, Beauty Insider tier bar, mystery reward game) rather than a flat discount claim.
- Product grid shows real price points and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" / "NEW" tags, giving the reader concrete reasons to click through.
- The K18 scalp serum block clearly signals a specific benefit (24-hour early access via app) rather than vague "check it out" copy.

## What's weak

- Subject line "So hot right now 💯" carries zero specificity — no product, no offer, no category — a step back from sharper subjects like "FREE foundation and concealer sample set" (7/30).
- Too many competing "top" offers stacked above the fold (4X points band, Get a Mini box, Beauty for You hero) dilute where the eye should land first.
- Preview text is boilerplate shipping/pickup copy, not a teaser for what's actually inside (trending picks, GWP, mystery reward) — it wastes the second line of inbox real estate.
- No social proof (ratings, review counts, "as seen on") anywhere in the trending section, despite the whole premise being "these are trending."

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to name the actual hook — trending picks or the GWP mechanic — instead of a vague hype phrase. This is the single biggest lever since the current subject undersells genuinely good content underneath it.
- 2. Cut the email down to one clear lead offer above the fold; move either the 4X-points band or the Get-a-Mini box below the trending grid instead of stacking three offers before the hero.
- 3. Rewrite the preview text to preview the trending-picks angle instead of reusing the generic shipping/pickup utility line.
- 4. Add a light social-proof cue to the trending grid (e.g., "#1 in Fragrance" or a star rating) since the entire section's premise is "hot right now" — right now it's just an assertion.
