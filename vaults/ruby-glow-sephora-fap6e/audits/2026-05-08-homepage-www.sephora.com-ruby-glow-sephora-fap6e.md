# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Opening Sephora's homepage feels like walking into the store — clean, editorial, immediately beauty-coded. They're pushing sunscreen hard right now, which is the right spring move, and the Mother's Day urgency banner has me clocking the clock. But as a Rouge member who lives for launch-week energy, nothing above the fold is telling me what's *new* — and Sephora knows that's literally why I open this app.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day urgency banner** — "Ends 5/10 at 1pm PT. CODE FREESAME" is specific, timed, and genuinely useful. Two days left, I know exactly what I'm working with.
- **Youth to the People Youthscreen in the hero flatlay** — That brand is in my actual routine. Seeing it front-center immediately reads as editorial curation, not just a random product dump.
- **Category nav strip** — Makeup, Skincare, Fragrance all one tap away. No hunting required.
- **Bottom nav bar** — "Me" and "Offers" tabs mean my Rouge account and deals are always one tap from anywhere on the page. Smart persistent architecture for mobile.
- **Sign-in prompt placement** — It's right below the header, above the fold. Hard to miss for returning members who haven't logged in yet.

## What's weak

- **Zero new-launch visibility** — I track the launch calendar obsessively. The first thing I want above the fold is what dropped this week or what's trending in the app. A sunscreen editorial hero is seasonal but it's not *news*.
- **No Rouge/VIB recognition** — "Sign In for FREE Shipping" is acquisition copy, not loyalty copy. If I'm already a Rouge member, I want to see "Your points are ready" or "Rouge Early Access," not the same prompt a first-timer gets.
- **"FIND YOUR MATCH ▶" goes… where?** — That reads like a quiz entry point, not a category page. I'd rather land on the SPF shelf or a curated edit than answer five questions before I can browse.
- **No skin or lifestyle imagery** — The hero is a product flatlay. Beautiful, but I can't see how these sunscreens look on actual skin, different undertones, finish types — nothing to help me imagine *myself* in the product.

## Recommendations

- **Replace "FIND YOUR MATCH" with a direct shelf link** — "Shop New SPF Arrivals" or "Shop Sunscreens" lands me on product immediately and skips the quiz friction that kills mobile conversions.
- **Personalize the sign-in bar for returning Rouge members** — Even just: "Welcome back — your [X] points are waiting" is one line of copy that signals you know who I am and validates my loyalty tier.
- **Add a "New This Week" product rail above the fold** — Three or four thumbnail cards (new mascara drop, trending serum, recent Sephora Collection launch) would give me the launch-calendar dopamine hit that keeps me opening this app daily.
- **Add a lifestyle or model shot to the sunscreen carousel** — Show the product *in use* on diverse skin tones and finishes. That's what converts a scroll-past into a "wait, I need that."
