# Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a catch-all Sephora send that tries to carry five messages at once: a free-sample spend offer, Hot Sellers, a Huda Beauty 4X points promo, a personalized product edit, and a holiday and new-brand grid. The subject, "Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this," promises personalization. The body only partly delivers it. The "Your Personalized Beauty Edit" row of Chanel, amika and Tom Ford sits well below the fold.
- The strongest asset is the top banner: "Spend More, Get More" with six free trial sizes at $105 min. spend and code MORE4U. It is concrete and sits at the top. The big pink "Hot Sellers" hero and the Huda 4X points block then take over, so the eye lands on a generic headline instead of an offer. This is the fourth points or incentive send in about a week. It follows the 10/8 Dr. Groot trial-size email, the 10/7 and 10/6 2X/5X points emails, and the 10/6 loyalty catalog email.
- Verdict: a competent but diffuse module stack. It is a mid-tier send that is easy to ignore in an inbox already full of Sephora promos.

## What's working

- The MORE4U banner at the top states a clear offer: six free trial sizes at $105 min. spend, app only.
- The Huda Beauty block pairs a credible 4X points badge with a code (HUDA4X) and a date (until 10/14).
- The "Personalized Beauty Edit" shows product, brand, price and image on a clean white grid.
- The Holiday Drops tile has a "24 HOURS ONLY" badge, which gives it believable urgency.
- The brand is instantly recognizable, and the rendering is clean with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The "Hot Sellers / You'll want them all" hero is the largest element but has no offer, so it is generic.
- The email carries about six competing CTAs: Shop Now (many), Hot Sellers, Huda, Holiday Drops, New & Clean, and Dieux.
- The first free-sample offer is limited to the app and has "Exclusions/terms apply" in tiny text, so the honesty signal is muddy.
- The "personalized" edit is buried midway down and shows only five products. Chanel at $154 and Tom Ford at $405 feel like a luxury-fragrance skew rather than a real personal match.
- The footer, the legal text and the "INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE" tier bar are dense and push a very long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero and move the personalized edit up under the banner. Either lead with MORE4U or lead with the personalization the subject promised.
- 2. Replace the "Hot Sellers" headline with the offer. For example, "6 free minis when you spend $105."
- 3. Cut the lower modules (New & Clean, Dieux) or move them to a separate send, so cadence and message stay focused.
- 4. Add social proof such as star ratings and review counts under the product picks.
- 5. Show Insider points status ("You have 0 points") in the hero so the loyalty hook is personal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `6 free minis + 4X points on Huda, Insider`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Insider picks + free trial sizes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $105 in the app, pick up to 6 free samples. Code MORE4U.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Huda Beauty 4X points through 10/14, plus fragrance picks chosen for you.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML namespaces and a responsive media query. QA found one hard failure (no plain-text alternative) and three warnings: missing alt text on two images, plus unverifiable List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers. The source was truncated, so body links and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 of 55 material links and skipped 43, ranked by click likelihood, so about 78% of links are unverified.
- An open-tracking pixel is served from `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. It has no alt attribute. This is low-impact, but it is flagged by QA.
- Images are hosted on the ESP CDN, `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/...`. That is normal for Epsilon sends, with no defect evidenced.
- Click-wrapper and redirect behavior could not be assessed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (Epsilon CDN) has no alt. This is a visible header or nav image, so with images blocked the header navigation disappears for screen readers and image-off clients.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` restricts pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The space makes the directive invalid. It has little effect in email but signals template sloppiness.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. Minor.
- **Positive:** The Outlook/MSO resets, `x-apple-data-detectors` handling and `#outlook a` fixes are present. The DOCTYPE and VML namespaces are correct for Outlook.
- **Markup style:** Mobile rules use attribute selectors such as `*[class=hide]`. Some clients, such as Gmail on non-Google accounts, strip or ignore these. The class-selector fallback (`.hide`) is present, so this is not an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source.
- The subject "Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this." uses a segment label rather than a name token. There is no defect, and no first-name personalization.
- The truncated body could not be checked for tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The message appears to be HTML-only (no `text/plain` alternative). This increases spam-filter risk and degrades accessibility for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header** was captured. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender defect.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** was captured, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify at the source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, and alignment cannot be confirmed.
- **Footer:** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters or landing URLs were visible in the truncated source or QA output.
- None of the 12 probed links reported redirect-chain or landing-page mismatches.
- Continuity could not be assessed. This is not evidence of a problem.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part** with real content (not a stub). This fixes the only FAIL.
2. **Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** against the raw message at the receiving mailbox, not the relay copy, before treating them as sender defects. If they are truly absent, add both (RFC 8058).
3. **Capture Authentication-Results** from the original delivery to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
4. **Add descriptive alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
6. **Fix `content="no index"`** to `noindex`, or drop the tag.
7. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML**, with the probe budget raised or targeted at the footer and CTA links. That would cover unsubscribe, UTM and landing-page checks, plus token scanning past the truncation point.
