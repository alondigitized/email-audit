# Hot drops coming through
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals roundup with a clear job: "Hot Drops" showcases fresh launches from Haus Labs, Summer Fridays, OUAI, YSL, Charlotte Tilbury, Moroccanoil, Ultra Violette and LoveShackFancy. The mint hero with a SHOP NOW button is clean. The one-brand-per-block layout is easy to scan, and the "NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA / LIMITED EDITION" tags give each product a reason to click.
- It also buries its best commercial hooks. The points promo (2X/5X, code SAYPOINTS, ends 10/7) is a thin strip at the very top, and the "30% off Hair Faves, today only" banner sits near the bottom. A reader who scrolls the long middle of full-price launches may never reach either. This is a discovery email for people already browsing, with little to push someone to act today. Compared with the 10/2 sends ("50% OFF select brow products", 8/10), which were a single offer with a single CTA, this one is weaker as a conversion email. It fits better as a brand-building send.
- The email is very long and ends with a dense legal block and a barcode. The barcode is a store-use item, and on a phone it adds a lot of scroll after the content ends. Verdict: a pretty, well-organized launch showcase that makes its discount hooks too easy to miss.

## What's working

- The "HOT DROPS / The latest has entered the chat" hero is bold and on-voice, with an obvious SHOP NOW button.
- Each product block follows the same pattern: image, brand name, tag line, product and price. That makes the long scroll easy to scan.
- The "NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA / LIMITED EDITION" labels and the Clean at Sephora badges give real reasons to care about each product.
- The brand lineup is strong and varied: Haus Labs, Summer Fridays, YSL, Charlotte Tilbury, Rhode. It is credible for a Sephora Insider reader.
- The Holiday Exclusives "Launch Soon! Build your basket" tease sets up the next send.

## What's weak

- The points offer (2X/5X, code SAYPOINTS) is crammed into a thin top banner and easy to miss. It is arguably the most actionable item in the email.
- The 30% off Hair Faves banner, marked "Today only", sits at the bottom of a very long scroll, so its urgency is wasted.
- It has no single dominant offer. The hero, the points strip, the holiday tease, the auto-replenish block, Rhode and the 30% off banner all compete.
- Most product blocks are full price and give only a brand name as the click target, so there is little urgency or savings pull.
- The footer is very long, with a dense legal block and a barcode, and it adds a lot of dead scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the points offer and the 30% off Hair Faves banner up directly under the hero, or fold the points mechanic into the hero itself. Lead with a reason to buy today, then show the drops.
- 2. Cut the body to 4-5 products and make the CTAs specific per product, such as "Shop Haus Labs liner", instead of relying on the brand name as the link.
- 3. Trim the lower modules (auto-replenish, Rhode, holiday tease) to one secondary block.
- 4. Shorten or collapse the legal footer, and keep the barcode out of the main flow if possible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Epsilon-hosted images. QA flags an empty plain-text part (0 chars) and several images with missing or filename-style alt text. The missing unsubscribe and authentication headers are most likely relay-capture gaps, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 71 links were probed (59 skipped, mostly footer, utility and social). No broken links were reported among the probed set, so none are flagged.
- Coverage of the rest is unverified, including the footer legal and preference links.
- The source is truncated, so I can't confirm the tracking-parameter scheme or redirect behavior.
- `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is an image `src` on a different host from the Epsilon image CDN. It is likely a tracking or open pixel and needs `alt=""` and explicit 1x1 dimensions. This is an inference from the URL pattern, not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text:** Five images are missing meaningful alt text (QA WARN):
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (the alt is the filename or is absent).
  - Three `26-10-03_haus-labs-the-edge-lip-shaping-pencil_ema…` images (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../de0a3226-...`).
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...` image.

  With images blocked, these render empty or as filenames. The header nav image is the most impactful, since it likely carries navigation content.
- **Zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` are applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This also blocks user text resizing.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The correct value is `noindex`. This has low impact in email.
- **Plain-text part:** QA reports it as 0 chars (FAIL). A missing text/plain alternative hurts accessibility and spam scoring. It could be a capture artifact, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **Boilerplate:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic, and a leftover `<!-- US shell snippet -->` comment ships in production.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible portion of the source. Because the HTML is truncated, the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found (WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Check the original message headers before treating it as a defect. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` could not be validated from the data provided.
- **Footer:** The unsubscribe link and postal address weren't visible in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters, destination URLs and landing-page resolution can't be assessed from the truncated source or QA output. No issues are confirmed.
- No probe status or redirect-chain data was provided for the primary CTAs, such as the Haus Labs lip pencil links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Treat the WARNs as relay artifacts until verified.
2. Verify the plain-text part in the raw MIME. If it is truly empty, add a text/plain alternative that includes the unsubscribe URL.
3. Add descriptive alt text to the five flagged images (the product name for the Haus Labs images, the nav labels for the header). Use `alt=""` for decorative images and tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and replace `text-size-adjust: none` with `100%`.
5. Fix the robots meta to `noindex`, or remove it, and strip the leftover template comment.
6. Re-run link probing with a higher cap, or specifically on the footer, preference and legal links. Capture the full HTML, since it was truncated, to audit UTMs and merge tokens.
