# LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves 👀
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing, a last-call hair sale, and the body delivers a different email. The top banner does match it: "Up to 40% off Hair Faves," a TODAY ONLY badge, "Save big on PATTERN by Tracee Ellis Ross, Vegamour, and more." Everything below it is a Minis campaign ("Minis Make It Easy… under $60"), then a points-boost block, a skincare-sets and glossy-hair promo, and a Same-Day Unlimited ad.
- The urgency is real, since TODAY ONLY and "Ends 10/7" are both stated. But the sale gets about 10% of the vertical space. The same pattern showed up on 2026-09-29 ("From your feed to your cart") and 2026-09-28 ("Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof"), where a hair-sale subject sat on top of a general catalog send. This is the third time in a week.
- Verdict: a competent Sephora digest with a decent sale banner, but the "LAST CALL" subject sets up a single-purpose urgency email that this isn't. It is also the second hair-sale send in two days, after 2026-10-02's "Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves."

## What's working

- The top banner states the offer plainly: "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" with a TODAY ONLY badge and a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The Minis grid shows named products with prices, such as the Cyklar and Gisou sets at $40 and $35, so the email is concrete.
- Insider points are surfaced twice: the 2X/5X block ("Ends 10/7, use code SAYPOINTS") and the P.S. line about the DELIVERED code.
- The render is clean. I can see no broken images or overlaps, and the section rhythm is easy to scan.
- The NEW, ONLY AT SEPHORA and LIMITED EDITION labels give shoppers reasons to pick products.

## What's weak

- The subject's offer is only a small banner at the top. The hero is a large Minis creative, so the eye goes to Minis, not the sale.
- Too many competing messages: hair sale, Minis, points, skincare sets, glossy hair, Same-Day Unlimited and Beauty Insider. There is no single focus.
- The sale banner has no product imagery at the scale the Minis grid gets, and "select" and "and more" make the 40% off vague.
- The CTAs are mostly generic "SHOP NOW" and "TRY IT."
- The legal and fine print at the bottom is very long, and the barcode block adds length without helping.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match the subject. For a LAST CALL send, put a full-width hero with "Last call: up to 40% off hair" and a countdown or "ends tonight." Follow it with a 2×4 grid of discounted hair products with prices. Move Minis to a separate send.
- 2. If Minis stays, change the subject to lead with it, and leave the hair sale as a secondary banner.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs, such as "Shop hair deals" or "Shop PATTERN."
- 4. Trim the points block and the Same-Day Unlimited ad from this send. They dilute the urgency.
- 5. Space out the hair-sale sends so they don't repeat on consecutive days.
