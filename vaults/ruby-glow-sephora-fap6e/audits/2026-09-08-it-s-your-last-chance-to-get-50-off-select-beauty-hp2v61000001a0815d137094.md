# ⚠️ It’s your LAST CHANCE to get 50% off select beauty ⚠️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Stacking Weekend" send Sephora has been running since 9/4, now dressed up with LAST CHANCE urgency in the subject and a "TODAY ONLY" badge on the mid-page 50% box. The problem is the same one this brand has repeated all week: the top-of-email hero is "UP TO 50% OFF" Select Beauty in a big red circle, but the actual named products directly below (Make Up For Ever, Nest New York, Sephora Collection, Glow Recipe, Shiseido, Huda Beauty) are shown at regular prices with no discount tags — the "50% off" and "shop these products" sections are visually stapled together but structurally disconnected.
- The urgency framing this time is more aggressive than prior sends — "⚠️ LAST CHANCE ⚠️" in the subject plus "Hurry — it's the last day" copy — which at least is a step toward matching subject to content versus the 9/3 "Just dropped: point of view" mismatch. But the email still stacks four distinct offers (50% off beauty, 500 bonus points at $75, $10 Beauty Insider Cash, free samples) into one wall of red boxes, and the actual 50% off callout with product images doesn't appear until roughly a third of the way down the page, well past the fold.
- Judgement: functional but fatigued. The urgency escalation is a reasonable tactic, but this is at least the sixth near-identical Stacking Weekend/50%-off structure this week, and the offer stacking still buries rather than clarifies the deal.

## What's working

- Subject urgency ("LAST CHANCE," warning emojis) is backed up by matching on-page copy ("Hurry — it's the last day to save"), which is more consistent than the 9/3 send.
- The red "UP TO 50% OFF" circle badge is a clear, high-contrast focal point right at the top.
- "500 Bonus Points" and "$10 Beauty Insider Cash" callouts speak directly to loyalty members with concrete numeric value.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- The "Save On Must Haves Like These" product grid (Make Up For Ever, Nest, Shiseido, etc.) shows only regular prices — no visible tie-in to the 50% off headline directly above it.
- Four separate offers (50% off, bonus points, Beauty Cash, free samples) compete for attention in the first screen, diluting the single "LAST CHANCE" urgency the subject promised.
- The actual "50% off Select Beauty" product callout with linked items doesn't appear until deep in the email, well past several unrelated CTAs (App Early Access, Holiday Exclusives).
- "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon" mid-email is a jarring detour that undercuts the "last chance today" urgency — it tells the reader to wait for something else right when they're being told to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the actual discounted, linked product set (the one under "50% off Select Beauty / TODAY ONLY") to the very top, replacing the generic red circle badge — show the deal with real products immediately, not three scrolls later.
- 2. Cut to one offer above the fold. Pick either the 50% off or the bonus points stack, not both — the multi-offer wall is the same complaint from 9/7 and 9/6.
- 3. Remove or relocate "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon" — it directly contradicts the "last day" urgency and belongs in a separate send.
- 4. Add strikethrough/sale pricing to the "Save On Must Haves" grid so the 50% off claim is visibly true against real products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 50% off select beauty ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — your 50% off Beauty Insider deal expires today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 500 bonus points when you spend $75 — offer ends 9/10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free samples, $10 Beauty Cash, and today's last chance at 50% off.`
