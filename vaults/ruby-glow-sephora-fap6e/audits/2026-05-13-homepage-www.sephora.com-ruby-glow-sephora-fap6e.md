# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora is doing their thing — clean, mobile-optimized, straight into new-arrivals energy. The "Meet the Latest" hero speaks directly to my brain because I genuinely do check for new drops on the reg. But honestly? For a Rouge member who practically lives in this app, I expect more personalization than a generic sign-in nudge and a vague "so many deals" banner with no actual numbers.

## What's working

- **"Meet the Latest" hero copy** — "Brand-new beauty just dropped. What are you waiting for?" hits the FOMO nerve perfectly for someone like me who tracks every launch date. It's urgent without being obnoxious.
- **Category tab strip** — "New | Makeup | Skincare | Fragrance" is exactly the one-tap access I want. I can get to new arrivals in literally one tap.
- **Product hero imagery** — The assortment of mascaras, lip products, a teal tube, and the Sephora Collection gift set is visually dense and immediately says "beauty haul" to me. Recognizable product shapes trigger the scroll reflex.
- **Top promo banner** — "trial sizes, sample sets and more" — yes, I am absolutely the person who stockpiles trial sizes before a VIB sale. That landed.
- **Bottom nav bar** — The "Offers" tab with a dedicated icon at the bottom is great for mobile; I don't have to hunt.

## What's weak

- **Zero Rouge/Beauty Insider personalization** — I'm a Rouge member and this homepage has no idea. No points balance, no tier badge, no "Welcome back, Ruby" energy. The sign-in strip is cold and functional.
- **"So many deals" is empty copy** — The blue banner promises deals but gives me nothing specific — no "20% off," no category, no threshold. It's a tease with no payoff above the fold.
- **No model or person in the hero** — The hero is all product flatlay. I want to see someone wearing that mascara or holding that lip gloss so I can visualize it on me.
- **Partially visible second hero slide** — I can see the edge of another slide with what looks like "G... Co... FIN..." — this feels half-rendered and makes me wonder what I'm missing. It's a mild visual distraction.
- **No urgency mechanism** — Nothing tells me these new drops are limited or time-sensitive. "What are you waiting for?" tries, but there's no countdown, no "drops end Sunday" — missed opportunity.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the vague banner copy with a specific offer** — "Shop 20% off new arrivals through Sunday" or "Earn 3× points on new drops this week" would convert my attention into a tap immediately.
- **Surface Beauty Insider status in the sign-in strip** — Even when logged out, show "Sign in to see your Rouge perks" or the tier benefits. That's what makes me sign in fast vs. skipping it.
- **Swap the pure product flatlay for a lifestyle hero** — Keep the products but anchor them on a person (real skin, real age range, not polished model) actively using one of the featured items. A shot of someone applying the mascara or doing a skincare routine would pull me in more.
- **Add a "Just Dropped This Week" pill or rail** — Even if it's just three product tiles below the hero, it converts the "new arrivals" promise into something I can actually tap. Right now "Meet the Latest" is a headline that goes to a generic shop page.
