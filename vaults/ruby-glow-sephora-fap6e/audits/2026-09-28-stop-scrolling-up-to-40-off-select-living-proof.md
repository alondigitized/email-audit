# Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof 🤍
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight-up "digest" send masquerading as a hair-sale email — the subject promises "up to 40% off select Living Proof," but the actual hero content is a Kérastase GWP banner and a black-and-white "Beauty's Most Wanted" model shot, with the Living Proof offer buried roughly two-thirds of the way down the scroll. It's a long, kitchen-sink layout stacking eight-plus separate brand blocks (Kérastase, YSL, NARS, Sincerely Yours, MAC, Gisou, Sol de Janeiro, REFY) before the promised hair deal even appears.
- Compare this to 2026-09-23's "Hair Daily Deals" send, which led with a burgundy hair-care banner that matched its subject exactly — that's the format this email should have used and didn't. Here the subject-to-hero mismatch is the single biggest problem, and it repeats the "email doesn't deliver what the subject promised" pattern seen across several recent sends in this run (09-21, 09-20).
- Judgement: functional but poorly targeted — a subscriber who opens for "40% off Living Proof" scrolls past a wall of unrelated fragrance, makeup, and skincare before finding it, which is a real click-through tax.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent block structure throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breakage anywhere in the render.
- "CLEAN at Sephora" badges are clearly visible on the Sincerely Yours and Gisou blocks, useful trust signal for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- The Living Proof section itself, once reached, is well-formed: clear "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" banner, "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag, and a legible "SHOP NOW" button.
- Pricing is specific throughout (e.g., YSL $37/$130, NARS $36/$55) rather than vague "shop the sale" language.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Living Proof," hero says Kérastase / "Beauty's Most Wanted" — the promised offer is nowhere near the top of the email.
- No preview text visible beyond the "free shipping" utility bar — a wasted opportunity to reinforce the Living Proof hook right under the subject line.
- The email is a marathon scroll (roughly 8+ distinct brand sections) with no visual anchor telling the reader where the subject's offer lives.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency on Living Proof is a strong lever that's completely undercut by how far down the page it sits.
- Generic black-and-white model banner ("Beauty's Most Wanted") has no offer, no product, no CTA context — pure filler before the real content starts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Living Proof block to the hero position, matching subject to lead creative — this is the single biggest lever, exactly the lesson from the well-executed 09-23 Hair Daily Deals send.
- 2. Add real preview text that reinforces the hair offer instead of leaving the free-shipping bar as the only preview signal.
- 3. Trim or collapse the middle brand grid (YSL/NARS/MAC/Gisou/Sol de Janeiro) into a smaller "also new" strip so the email isn't an 8-section scroll before secondary content.
- 4. Give "TODAY ONLY" more visual weight (color block, countdown-style treatment) since urgency this far down loses impact.
