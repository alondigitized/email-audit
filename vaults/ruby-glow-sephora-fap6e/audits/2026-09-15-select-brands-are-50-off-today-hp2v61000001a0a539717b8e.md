# Select brands are 50% off today 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- For once the subject and the hero actually line up: "Select brands are 50% off today 🤑" opens directly into a "50% off Select Beauty" badge with "TODAY ONLY" stamped on a WW fragrance bottle, exactly what this brand history has flagged as the winning formula on 2026-09-12 and 2026-09-11. But the discount claim is a bait-and-switch the second you scroll — "Select Beauty" isn't defined anywhere near the hero, and every single product below (Tower 28, Kenra, Kiehl's, Rare Beauty, Drunk Elephant, M.PH) is tagged "NEW," "LIMITED EDITION," or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" with full price and an "ADD TO CART" button, not a discounted one.
- The email essentially runs two unrelated campaigns stitched together: a 50% off banner up top, then eight full-price "new arrivals" product tiles, then three more unrelated modules (body moisturizers, Cult Gaia fragrance, fragrance minis). There's no single hero product tied to the 50% claim, so a shopper who opens for the deal has to hunt for it and won't find it before hitting six other pitches.
- This is a repeat of the "TRENDING NOW" bait problem flagged in the 2026-09-14 K18 audit — a strong, specific subject promise buried under unrelated filler. Net: decent open bait, weak follow-through.

## What's working

- Subject and hero visually match — "50% off Select Beauty" badge is the first thing seen, no scroll needed.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag is clear and legible.
- Product grid is clean — consistent tile layout, pricing, and "ADD TO CART" CTAs throughout.
- "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" seal appears consistently on qualifying products, useful for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- No render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- "Select Beauty" is never explained — no brand names, no category, no link to what's actually discounted.
- The 50% off hero has no product tied to it; the WW bottle shown isn't confirmed as part of the deal.
- Every visible product below the hero is full price, undermining the subject's promise entirely.
- The email crams four unrelated campaigns (50% off, "Beauty's Latest," body moisturizers, Cult Gaia, fragrance minis) with no hierarchy — the eye has nowhere confident to land after the hero.
- "*Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last." sits in tiny gray text directly under the hero — a fine-print flag right at the point of highest attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the actual discounted brands/categories directly under the "50% off" badge (as the 2026-09-12 and 2026-09-11 sends did with Clarins/Estée Lauder/Kiehl's) — the single biggest lever here, since the deal is currently unverifiable.
- 2. Link the hero "SHOP NOW" straight to a filtered "Select Beauty" landing page, not the homepage.
- 3. Cut the email to one campaign per send, or clearly separate "50% off" from "New Arrivals" with a divider and its own mini-headline so shoppers don't assume new-arrival tiles are discounted.
- 4. Move the exclusions disclaimer to the footer instead of directly under the hero offer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your favorite brands: 50% off, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Clarins, Kiehl's & more before the deal's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus new drops from Rare Beauty and Tower 28`
