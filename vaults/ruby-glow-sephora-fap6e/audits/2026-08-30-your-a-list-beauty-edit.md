# Your A-list-beauty edit
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's "new arrivals catalog" template again — the same one that ran on 8/23 ("Psst, something new just arrived") — just retitled "Iconic Beauty" and populated with a fresh crop of new launches (Prada fragrance, Prada makeup, Living Proof, Sol de Janeiro, Miu Miu, Charlotte Tilbury, Crown Affair, Glow Recipe). It's a long, undifferentiated vertical scroll of product tiles with almost no narrative or hierarchy beyond "new item, new item, new item." The one genuine offer — "50% off Select Beauty, Today Only" — is buried at the very top as a thin banner and never mentioned again; the subject line doesn't even reference it.
- The subject "Your A-list-beauty edit" is vague and curatorial-sounding but promises nothing concrete, which undercuts the one real deal in the email. This is a brand-catalog send wearing a discount email's clothes — subscribers scanning the inbox get no signal that there's an actual 50%-off deal inside.

## What's working

- The "50% off Select Beauty / Today Only" banner at the top is a real, time-bound, concrete offer with a clear CTA button ("SHOP NOW").
- Clean tile-based product photography throughout, no broken images or overlapping text.
- "Clean at Sephora" and "Planet Aware" badges give quick trust signals for shoppers who care about ingredients/sustainability.
- Each product block has a distinct colored background, which at least visually separates one brand from the next in a long scroll.

## What's weak

- The subject line and hero ("Iconic Beauty") completely bury the one concrete offer in the email — a subscriber who opens for "50% off" has to scroll past a full brand catalog to find it and it's never revisited.
- No hierarchy: eight-plus products get equal visual weight, so the eye has no obvious "start here" beyond the very top banner.
- Repeats the exact structural pattern of the 8/23 "new arrivals" send — same format, different SKUs, three brand-catalog sends within about a week (8/23, 8/26, now 8/30 flavor).
- "Spot it. Shop it." skincare/fragrance mini-grids feel like a second email tacked onto the bottom, disconnected from "Iconic Beauty" framing above.
- No pricing shown for most items except a couple of range prices ($16-$103) — most shoppers care about price and it's mostly absent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the whole email with the 50%-off offer as the actual subject and hero — right now the strongest asset in the email is hidden under generic curatorial copy. Rebuild the top third around the deal, then use the new-arrivals grid as a secondary "also new this week" section below it.
- 2. Cut the catalog down to 3-4 hero launches with a one-line "why it's worth it" instead of 8+ equal-weight tiles — pick a hierarchy (biggest launch first).
- 3. Add prices to every tile — price-sensitive members will bounce off tiles with no visible cost.
- 4. Give "Spot it. Shop it." its own visual identity or fold it into the top offer section — right now it reads like a bolted-on afterthought.
- 5. Vary the format from the 8/23 arrivals send — same structure two sends running risks subscriber fatigue; try a "editor's top 3 picks" carousel instead of a full vertical scroll.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Prada, Miu Miu & more just landed (50% off today)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One day only: 50% off select beauty, plus Prada, Sol de Janeiro & Charlotte Tilbury are new.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your deal window closes tonight — shop 50% off before new arrivals sell out.`
