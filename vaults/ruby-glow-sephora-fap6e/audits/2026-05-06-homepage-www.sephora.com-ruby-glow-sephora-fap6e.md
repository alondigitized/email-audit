# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the second I opened this I got hit with a very pink, very gift-y Mother's Day moment — which honestly makes sense because it's literally May 6th and Sephora is never sleeping on a gifting moment. This homepage is screaming "buy something for your mom," but the 4X Points fragrance banner at the top has me personally activated because I was already thinking about stocking up on my Maison Margiela. It feels seasonal and polished, but it's optimized for the gift-buyer, not for me impulse-hunting a new launch.

## What's working

- **The 4X Points fragrance banner** — timed through 5/10, specific promo code (BESTSCENTS), and fragrance is exactly the category I'll impulse buy in. This is the thing that got me.
- **"Mother's Day Gifts $50 & Under" hero** — the $50 ceiling is smart for mobile. I immediately know I can do this budget. The gift set imagery (perfumes, palettes, mini collections) is pure Sephora fantasy and I want everything in that flat lay.
- **The "New" tab in the navigation** — one tap to new arrivals. That's my first instinct every time I open this app.
- **"AI Beauty" tab in the bottom nav with a NEW badge** — that's going to get my finger immediately, because I want to know what that is.
- **Sign In for FREE Shipping prompt** — gives me a reason to log in that's practical, not just "personalize your experience" fluff.

## What's weak

- **Hero speaks to gift-givers, not self-shoppers** — "Value sets for the ones who guide, support, and nurture us" is lovely, but I'm here for the new Charlotte Tilbury drop, not to feel grateful about my mom right now. This hero doesn't catch the self-purchase mode I'm almost always in.
- **No model or human in the hero** — it's all product flat lay on a pink background. Beautiful, but I can't see anyone wearing or using anything, which makes it harder to connect emotionally when I'm not already in gift mode.
- **Sign-in friction before I can see personalized content** — my Rouge status, my wishlist, my recently viewed — none of that is surfaced because I'm treated as a guest. That's a miss for a returning customer like me.
- **Free shipping threshold is vague** — "Sign In for FREE Shipping" tells me nothing about what I need to spend. What's the threshold? That matters before I tap anything.
- **The right-side panel is cut off** — there's clearly another carousel panel (I can see "Ce..." and "Su..." text) but it's not obvious this is swipeable. No dot indicators, no swipe affordance visible.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "New This Week" or "Just Dropped" rail below the hero** — I scroll past this Mother's Day moment fast; give me a new-arrivals hook before I lose interest and go to r/MakeupAddiction instead.
- **Surface Rouge tier + points balance in the header** — even just "Hi Ruby, you have 4,280 points" would stop me cold. That's the hook that makes me feel seen as a loyalty member, not just a generic visitor.
- **Make the carousel swipeable with visible indicators** — dot navigation or an arrow on the right panel so I know there's more. Right now that cut-off right edge just looks like a render issue.
- **Add free-shipping threshold in plain text** — "Free shipping when you spend $50+" next to the sign-in prompt removes one mental barrier before checkout.
