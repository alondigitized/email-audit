# Target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This subject promises "target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum" but the email you open buries that content under a 50% off select beauty banner and a "TRENDING NOW / Thanks later" filler block before you ever see hair. By the time the before/after scalp image and the K18 pitch actually appear, a subscriber who opened specifically for gray/thinning hair has already scrolled past two unrelated promos.
- Once you get there, the K18 section itself is genuinely strong: a real before/after scalp photo with "AFTER 3 months of nightly use" and callouts for "LESS GREY" / "MORE HAIR," plus concrete clinical claims ("up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys") and clear pricing ($120 / $75). That's exactly the kind of specific, credible product proof this rubric rewards. But it's followed immediately by an unrelated 10-product grid (fragrance, lip liner, dry shampoo) that has nothing to do with hair thinning, diluting the single-product focus the subject set up.
- This is a classic Sephora bait email — the subject is written to convert a very specific persona (someone worried about graying/thinning hair), but the actual send is a generic multi-category promo with the hair story wedged into the middle third. Compare to the brand's recent 50%-off sends (9/8–9/12), which at least delivered their subject's offer immediately at the top; this one delivers a *different* offer (50% off beauty) at the top than what the subject promised (K18 serum).

## What's working

- Real, specific before/after scalp photography with labeled callouts ("LESS GREY," "MORE HAIR") — strong, credible visual proof.
- Concrete clinical stat ("up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys") tied to a named study duration (12-week clinical study).
- Clear pricing and exclusivity ("ONLY AT SEPHORA," $120 / $75) with a direct "Buy online, pick up in store or curbside" option.
- No render bugs — images, text, and grid all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The K18 hero content the subject promises doesn't appear until well past two unrelated modules (50% off beauty, "TRENDING NOW").
- The top-of-email 50% off Beauty banner competes with and dilutes the subject's single-product focus — different message than what got the open.
- Immediately after the K18 pitch, a 10-item unrelated product grid (fragrance, lip liner, dry shampoo) breaks the narrative thread the subject set up.
- No visible CTA button directly under the K18 section itself in the "SHOP NOW" hierarchy sense — the strongest CTA button is attached to the unrelated 50% off banner and "TRENDING NOW," not to K18.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with K18: move the before/after scalp image and "NEW AND ICONIC K18" block to the very top of the email, directly under the header, so the hero matches the subject immediately.
- 2. Add a dedicated "SHOP K18" button right under the hair-count/grey-reduction stat — right now the strongest proof point has no adjacent CTA.
- 3. Cut or relocate the generic "TRENDING NOW / Thanks later" block — it adds zero information and pushes relevant content further down.
- 4. If the beauty product grid must stay, retitle it something hair-adjacent or move it out of this send entirely — a 10-product cross-category grid right after a hair-specific pitch reads as bait-and-switch.
