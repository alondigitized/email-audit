# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- For once, the hero actually matches the subject: "Save 50% on select beauty ❤️" opens with a "50% off* Select Beauty" badge naming Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, and Azzaro, plus a "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag and a clear "SHOP NOW" button. That's a real improvement over the string of Sephora sends this week (9/5 through 9/10) that promised a percentage-off deal in the subject and delivered something else entirely up top.
- But the email doesn't stay honest for long. One scroll down, the entire rest of the message — "Rewards Bazaar," "September means new rewards," a full points catalog (100-point and 250-point reward tiers), a Prada collection teaser, and a holiday-drops countdown — has nothing to do with the 50%-off subject line. The offer that got you to open is a single screen's worth of real estate in a six-panel email.
- This is a marginal improvement on brand history (the hero finally lines up with the subject), but the bait-and-switch structure this brand has run all week is still fully intact underneath it. Net: slightly better than the 4.00-5.00/10 run of the past six days, still not a well-targeted send.

## What's working

- Hero badge and subject are aligned for once — "50% off* Select Beauty" with named brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro) matches "Save 50% on select beauty" in the subject.
- "TODAY ONLY" creates credible, visible urgency right on the hero image.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is large, high-contrast, and impossible to miss above the fold.
- Product grid for point rewards is clean — clear product shots, names, and point costs with no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- The email pivots immediately from a 50%-off sale to a loyalty points catalog ("Rewards Bazaar," 100/250-point rewards) that has zero connection to the subject's promise.
- Fine print ("Exclusions/terms apply") sits directly under the offer, which undercuts the "50% off" headline before the reader even scrolls.
- The email is trying to do three unrelated jobs at once (flash sale, points redemption, new Prada launch, holiday-drop teaser) with no visual through-line tying them together.
- "New reward are here!" ribbon repeats redundantly along both edges of the Rewards Bazaar banner — visual clutter with no added information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job. If the subject sells 50% off, the entire body should be about the 50%-off sale — move the Rewards Bazaar, Prada teaser, and holiday-drops block into their own dedicated sends. This is the single biggest lever; everything below the first screen currently works against the subject's promise.
- 2. Move "Exclusions/terms apply" out of the hero's direct sightline — put it in the footer instead of directly under the offer, so it doesn't read as a hedge on the headline.
- 3. If Rewards Bazaar must stay in the same send, at minimum add a transition line ("Plus, your points go further this weekend") so it doesn't feel like a second, unrelated email got pasted in.
- 4. Drop the duplicate "New reward are here!" ribbon — one mention is enough; repeating it on both edges adds visual noise without new information.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown & Azzaro before midnight — exclusions apply`
