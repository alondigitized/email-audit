# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression — Sephora knows what it's doing. The 30% off Sephora Collection banner hits immediately, and then it drops me right into a personalized "Chosen For You" grid with Rhode, Chanel, and YSL. This feels like a brand that's both running a real sale and knows what I've been browsing. The vibe is polished and loyalty-forward, though some of the loading UI is a little janky on my end right now.

## What's working

- **"Ends Today: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection" banner** — urgency + discount combo is exactly what makes me open the app mid-commute. I already know the Sephora Collection dupes I want.
- **"Chosen For You" personalization rail** — Rhode Pocket Bronze and Chanel GABRIELLE ESSENCE are things I've literally had open in other tabs. This feels like it knows me.
- **"NEW" + "CLEAN" badges on the Rhode product** — as someone who tracks launch calendars, a "NEW" flag in a personalized section is a trust signal AND a discovery tool simultaneously.
- **Star ratings + review counts visible on every product card** — 257 reviews on Rhode, 134 on Chanel, I can make a snap judgment without tapping in.
- **"Summer Sets Under $35" category pill** — gifting season and summer travel are real use cases for me, and that pill is exactly the kind of shortcut I tap.

## What's weak

- **Skeleton loader still visible** — there's an obvious gray circle (profile avatar?) and two loading bars that haven't resolved above the category chips. On a flagship retail app in 2026, that's embarrassing.
- **"JOIN BEAUTY INSIDER" CTA targets the wrong user** — I'm Rouge. Seeing a join prompt on my logged-in homepage feels like Sephora doesn't know I exist, which undercuts the "Chosen For You" personalization right below it.
- **No shade-match or try-on entry point above the fold** — I browse mobile but convert in-store for shade matching. A "Try It On" or "Shade Finder" CTA somewhere visible would be a perfect bridge for me.
- **Product names truncate hard** — "Pocket Bronze Long-Wearing Cream…" and "GABRIELLE CHANEL ESSENCE Eau de…" both cut off mid-product name. I can't tell if it's the cream or a powder without tapping.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — that number matters when I'm deciding whether to add one more thing to hit the threshold.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the "JOIN BEAUTY INSIDER" banner CTA with a tier-aware message** — for Rouge members, show "You're earning 1.5× points today" or "Rouge exclusive: early access to new drops." The join hook should only show to non-members.
- **Resolve the skeleton loader or add a fallback state** — the gray blobs above the category pills are a visible polish failure. Ship a static fallback for slow connections.
- **Add a "Shade Finder" or "Try It On" shortcut chip** in the horizontal category pill row — something like "Find Your Shade" next to "New" would be a direct path to the feature that bridges my mobile-browse / in-store-convert habit.
- **Show free-shipping threshold in the promo banner** — "30% off + Free shipping on $35+" on one strip would eliminate a friction point I always check manually.
