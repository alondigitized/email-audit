**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**

- This is a loyalty-incentive send riding a hair care / styling moment — "4X Points on ALL Hair Care" anchored by "MAJOR BEAUTY MOMENT / Be Your Own Stylist." The top banner communicates the offer cleanly: code HAIRCARE, deadline 7/20, shop-to-redeem CTA. ## What's working

- **Offer is clear and top-of-funnel**: The 4X Points banner with code HAIRCARE and the 7/20 deadline is the first thing you see — no hunting required.
- **Subject-to-hero alignment**: "4X points on all hair care" in the subject matches "MAJOR BEAUTY MOMENT / Be Your Own Stylist" in the hero — a clean through-line, unlike the July 16 mismatch.
- **Genuine urgency**: A deadline of 7/20 (tomorrow from today's date) is a real reason to click today rather than "later."
- **Product breadth in the grid**: Showing Dyson alongside smaller brands signals the offer is comprehensive, not cherry-picked.
- **Clean render**: No broken images, overlapping text, or layout issues visible.

## What's weak

- **Double ‼️ in the subject fractures the sentence**: "hair care ‼️ and styling tools ‼️" reads as two separate exclamations interrupting a product list — grammatically awkward and edging toward spam signal.
- **Below-fold pivot kills focus**: Makeup lineup and Clean at Sephora blocks are full campaigns unrelated to the 4X hair offer — the email doesn't know what it is.
- **No social proof**: Reviews, bestseller badges, or "top-rated" callouts are absent from the product grid.
- **"SHOP TO REDEEM" is generic**: The CTA button copy doesn't name a category or product — "Shop Hair Care" or "Shop Styling Tools" would carry more pull.
- **Three emails in two days**: After the Ariana Grande launch and the World Cup editorial, this lands as the third send in 48 hours — cadence fatigue is real even when the offer is strong.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the off-topic below-fold sections**: Remove or replace the Makeup Latest Lineup and Clean at Sephora blocks with additional hair care product picks or a "top-rated tools" spotlight. If those campaigns need to run, give them their own send.
- 2. **Fix the CTA copy**: Replace "SHOP TO REDEEM" with "Shop Hair Care & Styling" — gives the click a destination and earns the tap.
- 3. **Rewrite the subject to remove the double ‼️ and surface the deadline**: The 7/20 expiry is the single strongest open driver and it's buried in body copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on hair care & tools — ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 4X hair care points expire July 20`
- 4. **Use the preheader to name a brand or product**: Dyson, Olaplex, or a trending tool would add specificity the subject can't hold.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dyson, Olaplex, T3 and more — use code HAIRCARE`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code HAIRCARE • Ends 7/20 • All tools included`
- 5. **Add one "bestseller" or review badge to the product grid**: Even a star-rating callout on one product lifts credibility and can drive the click over the threshold.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`robots` meta value has an invalid space:**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
Correct value is `noindex` (no space). RFC-compliant parsers will reject `no index` as an unknown directive and may index the page. Should be:
```html
<meta name="robots" content="noindex" />
```

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta suppresses pinch-zoom:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
This is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 — Resize Text). It prevents low-vision users from zooming on mobile. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `maximum-scale=5`.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied globally:**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Disabling text-size-adjust on all elements blocks OS-level font scaling for visually impaired users. Should be applied only to specific layout-critical elements, not the full selector list.

**Duplicate `.appear` rule block in mobile media query:**
The `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)` block contains two identical rule sets for `*[class=appear], .appear`:
```css
*[class=appear], .appear { display: block!important; display: table!important; }
/* ... other rules ... */
*[class=appear], .appear { display: block!important; display: table!important; }
```
The duplicate is dead code — no functional impact but increases payload size unnecessarily.

**Mobile breakpoint is 480px**, not the more common 600px used by major clients (Outlook, Apple Mail). Content between 480px–600px viewport widths won't trigger mobile styles. If any layout breaks at tablet-width viewports, this is the cause.
