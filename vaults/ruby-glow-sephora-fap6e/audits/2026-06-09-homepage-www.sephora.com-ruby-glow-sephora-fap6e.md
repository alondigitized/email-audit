# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Sephora's mobile homepage is clean, fast, and screams "Beauty Authority" — but right now it's pushing hard on Father's Day gifting and a "Coming Soon" tease for summer deals, which means the above-the-fold real estate is doing almost nothing for me personally. I came here to check new drops, maybe browse a mascara or stalk my Insider points, and instead I'm looking at a flat-lay of men's cologne sets and toiletry bags. The bones are great — the nav is exactly where I need it — but the hero is a total miss for anyone who isn't shopping for a dude.

## What's working

- **Top announcement banner** — "Coming Soon: Major Summer Deals! Up to 50% off* select beauty" in that bright pink is exactly the kind of signal that keeps me checking back. I see it immediately and it primes me to stay.
- **Horizontal category nav** — "New," "Makeup," "Skincare," "Fragrance" are right there in one swipe. No hunting. For mobile, this is exactly how it should be.
- **"AI Beauty" tab** (bottom nav, with NEW badge) — as someone with high tech comfort who tracks launches obsessively, that badge is genuinely eye-catching. I want to know what that does.
- **"Skip the clichés" copy** — the tone is self-aware and a little cheeky, which lands for my generation. It doesn't feel corporate.
- **Clean mobile layout** — search bar centered, cart and wishlist in the top right, bottom nav pinned. The muscle memory works.

## What's weak

- **The entire hero is about men** — "Win at Gifting Him" with cologne bottles and blue toiletry bags tells me Sephora isn't thinking about me right now. I'm the one who spends here 365 days a year, and I'm being handed a shopping list for someone else.
- **No Insider / Rouge callout** — I'm Rouge. I have a balance. I go to exclusive early-access events. None of that is surfaced anywhere I can see. That's a wasted personalization opportunity on a logged-in (or returning) shopper.
- **"Coming Soon" is a frustration** — a deal I can't act on today is just FOMO fuel. There's no "notify me," no countdown, no incentive to do anything right now except feel vaguely annoyed.
- **No new launches or hot-right-now rail visible** — I live for the new drop. If there's a new mascara or a limited-edition palette, I can't see it at all above the fold.
- **No social proof** — zero star ratings, zero review counts, no "bestseller" badges visible.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a "New Arrivals This Week" spotlight** — show me the three newest makeup drops with a direct-to-PDP tap. Even a simple editorial-style grid beats a flat-lay of men's gift sets for a Rouge member landing on the homepage.
- **Add a Rouge-tier banner or personalized header** — even something like "Welcome back, Rouge member — your points: [X]" above the hero converts a transactional visit into a relationship touchpoint. Sephora has the data; use it.
- **Turn the "Coming Soon" banner into a countdown + waitlist** — "Deals drop in 3 days — add to calendar" or an email/push opt-in would make that banner actionable instead of just a teaser.
- **Pin a "New" category badge on the nav rail** — put a red dot or "3 new drops" indicator on the Makeup tab so I know there's fresh content waiting before I even tap.
