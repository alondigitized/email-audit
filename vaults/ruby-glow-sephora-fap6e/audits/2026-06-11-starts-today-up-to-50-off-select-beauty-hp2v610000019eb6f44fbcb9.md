# STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🎀
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's summer sale launch — a structurally heavier, more complex email than the May "Stacking Weekend" series. The hero reads clearly: a "STARTS TODAY" urgency banner, "Major Summer Deals" framing, a 500-bonus-points loyalty hook, and three stacked discount tiers (50% / 20% / 25% off). That three-tier structure is the email's core tension. Leading with 50% grabs attention, but the immediately following 20% and 25% rows diffuse it into "which thing am I shopping?" — a classic cascade that trades clarity for coverage.
- The bottom half of the email is a full product catalog: a "SAVE ON MUST-HAVES" grid, a new-launch callout, a hot-deal feature, a 500-point redemption module, and a "We Belong to Something Beautiful" brand values section. That's five separate content blocks below the fold. For a sale launch email, that's too much — it reads like two or three emails mashed together.
- Verdict: a competent sale launch that covers the bases but never fully commits to one killer offer. The May 22 Stacking Weekend send was sharper because it had a single, stackable mechanic. This one spreads too thin.

## What's working

- **"STARTS TODAY" urgency** is front-and-center in both the subject and the email banner — immediately signals a new campaign window.
- **500 bonus points** loyalty hook is prominently badged near the hero, rewarding the subscriber base Sephora has been cultivating across previous sends.
- **"Beauty Insiders Get More"** section cleanly differentiates loyalty tiers with icons — member-exclusive early access and free shipping are visible without hunting.
- **No render bugs** — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact throughout the long scroll.
- **New 500-Point Rewards module** with "REDEEM NOW" CTA gives loyalty members a second, specific reason to click beyond the sale discount.

## What's weak

- **Three discount tiers (50% / 20% / 25%) undercut each other** — the eye never settles on "the deal" because three different percentage frames compete in the same scroll.
- **No sale end date visible** — "STARTS TODAY" tells me when it opens, not when it closes. Without a deadline, urgency stalls at the first click.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the primary CTA button copy** — a generic verb with no noun. "BUILD YOUR BASKET" is sharper but appears lower, after the deal tiers.
- **Product grid images are too small** — the "SAVE ON MUST-HAVES" section shows ~6 products but they're thumbnail-sized; no individual product detail (shade, size, formula) is legible.
- **Email is five content sections too long** — new launch + hot deal + point redemption + brand values all below a dense sale header dilutes every one of those messages individually.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one hero offer** — pick either the 50% tier OR the 500-bonus-points mechanic as the single lead, not both. The stacking weekend worked because the two incentives compounded each other (spend $75, get both). This email lists three separate discount bands and a points badge simultaneously — that's confusion, not stacking.
- 2. **Add an end date** — "Sale ends Sunday" (or whatever the actual close date is) below the hero would complete the urgency loop that "STARTS TODAY" opens.
- 3. **Replace "SHOP NOW" with a category-specific CTA** — "Shop 50% Off Skincare" or "Claim 500 Points" forces the decision that generic shop-now defers.
- 4. **Cut to three content sections max** — keep the hero deal, the loyalty member perks, and one product spotlight. Drop or move the new launch + hot deal + brand values to separate sends this week.
- 5. **Increase product grid image size** or cut half the products and show fewer with legible names and prices.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Major Summer Sale starts now — 50% off select beauty 🎀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 500 bonus points + up to 50% off starts today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Beauty Insiders get early access and free shipping — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three ways to save: 50%, 20%, and 25% off select beauty — sale ends Sunday.`
