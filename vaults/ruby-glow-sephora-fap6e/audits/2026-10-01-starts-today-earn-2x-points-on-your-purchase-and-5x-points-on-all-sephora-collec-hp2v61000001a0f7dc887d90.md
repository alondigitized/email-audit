# STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-points mechanic sent as a full catalog email. The hero is a giant "2X INSIDER" with "Earn 2X points on your entire purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection". It's the clearest thing in the email, and the subject matches it. The tier ladder ("Samples 100 pts → $5 off 250 → $10 off 500 → Beauty Experiences 750+") turns abstract points into rewards, which is the best thinking here.
- The email then buries its own offer. Below the hero it stacks a Free Same-Day Delivery P.S., a cardmember promo, six "Top Picks" product tiles, Buy Now Pay Later logos, a **"Up to 50% off Hair Faves – TODAY ONLY"** banner, a founders video, a "New and Under $50" block, a gifting block, a Beauty Insider footer and a wall of fine print. That is five or six competing messages. The offer is also gated behind a code, SAYPOINTS, and the button reads "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" instead of telling me where I'm going.
- This follows the 9/22 "4X points on fragrance (yes way!)" send, so I'm now getting the third points-multiplier promo in about ten days. The 9/29 "From your feed to your cart" send also had a mismatched hair-sale banner at the top. This one repeats that habit by putting a hair-sale banner mid-email. Solid and on-brand, but long and unfocused.

## What's working

- The "2X INSIDER" hero is huge and unambiguous, and the subject promise matches it.
- The "See What Your Points Can Do" ladder (100 / 250 / 500 / 750+) makes the value tangible.
- Each Top Pick shows the points you'd earn next to the price, e.g. Tom Ford "810 points" at $405 and Summer Fridays "48 points" at $24.
- The end date, 10/7, is stated, and the free-delivery P.S. repeats it.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email runs very long, with at least five distinct promos competing: points, delivery, cardmember, hair 50% off and founders content.
- The only CTA copy is "USE CODE SAYPOINTS". It gives no verb or destination and makes me copy a code.
- The Top Picks lead with a $405 Tom Ford fragrance and $24 to $48 items. The 810 points reads as a big number but isn't a deal.
- The "Up to 50% off Hair Faves – TODAY ONLY" banner sits below the fold and cuts against the "earn points" focus. Its urgency also conflicts with the 10/7 deadline above it.
- Several screens of fine print and a footer barcode sit at the bottom and add clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job. Keep the hero, the tier ladder, 3-4 products and one shop button. Move the hair banner, founders video and gifting to separate sends.
- 2. Replace "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" with a button like "Shop 2X points" and auto-apply the code. Show the code as secondary text.
- 3. Swap the product mix. Lead with hair and skincare items under $30 that match the audience, and add the Sephora Collection 5X items since the subject promises them. The grid has no Sephora Collection product.
- 4. Put the end date in the hero ("Ends 10/7") so urgency isn't only in the P.S.
- 5. Shorten the subject to the offer and drop the all-caps lead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2X points starts today (5X on Sephora Collection)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your points just doubled — through 10/7 😍`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code SAYPOINTS. Samples at 100 pts, $10 off at 500.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free Same-Day Delivery with code DELIVERED. Ends 10/7.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** only 12 of 55 links were probed and 43 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). Probe results weren't provided, so no broken links are confirmed.
- **Click tracking:** the first flagged image is served from `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`. That path looks like an ESP open or redirect wrapper rather than a direct asset URL. Link destinations couldn't be inspected in the truncated source.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261001_PME_Stacking_Starts_Today_BI` carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`). This is expected, but the image has no `alt` attribute. A tracking pixel should use `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] No plain-text alternative:** the text part is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and spam scoring, and leaves text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks with nothing.
- **Missing alt text (3 images):**
  - the `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` image
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/…/2021_Navs_Header_06`), which looks like a navigation header image, so missing alt likely hides navigation text when images are blocked
  - `o.gif` (the tracking pixel, see above)
- **Zoom restrictions:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom.
  - The CSS rule `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; … }` disables text-size scaling.
  - Both are accessibility issues (WCAG 1.4.4 / 1.4.10).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` isn't a valid directive. It should be `noindex`. This is low impact in email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the email, which matters for the web-view and accessibility title.
