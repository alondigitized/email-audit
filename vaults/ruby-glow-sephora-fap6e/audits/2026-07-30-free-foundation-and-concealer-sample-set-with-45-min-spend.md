# FREE foundation and concealer sample set with $45 min. spend 🤩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-organized send built around a genuine gift-with-purchase mechanic rather than another vague "up to X% off" promise — a welcome break from the run of lip-discount emails that plagued this brand's last two weeks (7/25–7/29), several of which got dinged for bait-and-switch math. The hero is legible at a glance: free sample set, $45 minimum spend, dated points bonus (4X/5X through 8/5), single "SHOP NOW" CTA. Loyalty tie-ins (points bar, tier badges, SHADEMATCH code) reinforce that this is written for an enrolled Beauty Insider, not a cold prospect.
- Where it loses points is density and specificity of the ask. The offer stack (free sample + 4X points on all foundation/concealer + 5X points on Sephora Collection only + SHADEMATCH code + $45 min spend) requires real parsing, and three separate "exclusions/terms apply" footnotes for one promo undercuts the "simple, honest GWP" read. The long scrolling product carousel below the fold (fragrance, hair serum, cleansing balm, lipsticks) is generic merchandising that has nothing to do with the foundation/concealer hook and dilutes the single offer this email opened with.
- Net: a competent, on-brand send that fixes the credibility problem from recent lip promos but still asks the reader to do too much math for a "free" offer.

## What's working

- Hero headline ("Free* Foundation and Concealer Sample Set") is legible and lands first — clear visual hierarchy on a lavender background that pops against the white masthead.
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the offer stack, not buried.
- Loyalty context is front and loaded — points balance ("You have 0 points"), tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge), and a dated point multiplier (4X/5X through 8/5) all visible without scrolling far.
- Named-brand sample lineup (MERIT, tarte, PATRICK TA) gives the "free set" concrete weight instead of a vague "free gift" claim.
- Format break from the brand's last six sends, which were nearly all repetitive lip-discount subject lines — this reads as a genuinely different offer type.

## What's weak

- Three separate "exclusions/terms apply" asterisks stacked across one promo (sample set, points bonus, SHADEMATCH) signal more fine print than a single free-sample offer should need.
- The 4X vs. 5X points split (all foundation/concealer vs. Sephora Collection only) plus a required promo code is a lot of conditional logic to parse in a skimmed email.
- The lower two-thirds is a generic product carousel (fragrance, hair serum, cleansing balm, multiple lipsticks) that's disconnected from the foundation/concealer hook — it competes with, rather than reinforces, the hero offer.
- CTA copy is the generic "SHOP NOW" repeated four times through the email instead of anything category-specific.
- No single hero product image for the sample set itself — the small collage of samples is busy and doesn't clearly show what's actually included.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the carousel section (ViktorRolf, Kérastase, Farmacy, MAC, NEST, Laneige, Miu Miu) or move it to a separate send — it's unrelated to the free-sample hook and pulls focus from the one offer worth clicking.
- 2. Collapse the 4X/5X points explanation into one line ("4X–5X points on foundation & concealer, code GETMORENOW") instead of two separate bullet-style claims — reduce cognitive load.
- 3. Swap "SHOP NOW" for a category-specific CTA like "Claim My Sample Set" or "Shop Foundation & Concealer" to tie the button directly to the offer.
- 4. Add urgency to the subject line itself — the 7/30–8/5 window is buried in the footer and never surfaces in the subject or hero.
- 5. Replace the busy sample-image collage with one clean hero shot of the actual sample kit packaging so recipients know exactly what they're getting.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $45 spend = free foundation & concealer set`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `10 must-try shades from MERIT, tarte, PATRICK TA + up to 5X points`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free samples, real points: shop foundation & concealer before 8/5`
