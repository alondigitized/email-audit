## Executive summary

- This is a content newsletter, not a promo. "The Chat" gives four makeup tips from Mario, Mary Phillips, Violette and Patrick Starrr, then a Breast Cancer Awareness Month block. The editorial design is strong: big "Experts Know Best" headline, one portrait per tip, handwritten signatures. It reads like a magazine page.

## What's working

- The "Experts Know Best" hero is clear, and the visual hierarchy is clean from top to bottom.
- Each tip has a face, a short how-to and a signature. The format repeats consistently and is easy to scan.
- The tips are practical, such as the contour-shade lip trick and color correcting before concealing, so the content has real value.
- The Breast Cancer Awareness Month block fits the season. It states the donation terms in the copy rather than hiding them.
- The Beauty Insider and Rewards Bazaar footer keeps the loyalty program present without crowding the content.

## What's weak

- No offer is visible. There is no percentage off, no points multiplier and no free sample, which is a break from the promo-heavy sends earlier this week.
- No CTA button is visible. The four tips don't link to named products or "shop this look" actions.
- The subject has no connection to the content. "I HAVE PATRICK" with phone emojis only pays off at the fourth tip, and the all-caps styling reads as shouty.
- The awareness block is a long wall of small text, and its small-print donation disclosure is hard to read.
- Mid-email, the tips give technique but never name the products used, so interest has nowhere to go.

- 2. Rewrite the subject so it says what's inside, and keep the personality.
- 3. Put a visible Beauty Insider hook, such as a points multiplier on featured products, in the top third.
- 4. Condense the Breast Cancer Awareness section into a short pull-quote with a "Learn more" button, and move the donation disclosure to a smaller footer line.
