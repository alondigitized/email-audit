# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I see Sephora's homepage and they are *clearly* in SPF season mode — the whole above-the-fold push is a 5X Points deal on sun care, featuring those sleek, minimalist Korean sunscreen tubes I've been seeing everywhere on TikTok. This feels very curated for someone who already knows the Insider program, knows K-beauty is having a moment, and is ready to stock up before summer. Classic Sephora — they know their girl.

## What's working

- **"5X Points on SPF" hero copy** — the loyalty multiplier framing is instant dopamine for a Rouge member. I know exactly what this means for my Insider account.
- **"Including weightless Korean sunscreens"** — this single line signals they know exactly what I've been researching. It's a search-term-to-hero pipeline that feels earned.
- **"Ends 5/20" deadline** — credible, specific, five days from today. That's real urgency, not manufactured.
- **Top banner promo (code GETSAMPLES)** — stacked offer on top of the hero deal. Two reasons to spend $45 today before I even tap anything.
- **Bottom nav includes "AI Beauty" tagged NEW** — as a high-tech comfort shopper I am immediately curious about that tab.
- **Sign In for FREE Shipping row** — keeps the member benefit front and center even for signed-out users.

## What's weak

- **No lifestyle model/person visible** — it's all product-only photography. At 29, I want to see skin. Show me the glow, not just the tube.
- **"USE CODE SKINWINS" is a text link, not a button** — on a mobile thumb, a proper tappable button would convert better. The CTA needs more visual weight.
- **No review stars or social proof on the featured products** — I cannot tell if either sunscreen has 4.7 stars or 3.1 stars. That changes whether I tap it.
- **The sign-in interstitial row breaks the scroll flow** — it reads like a generic e-commerce nag and creates visual noise right before the hero.
- **Free-shipping threshold is vague** — "Sign In for FREE Shipping" tells me nothing about the minimum. I need a number.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the product-only hero with a lifestyle shot** — a real person (late 20s, diverse skin tone) holding or applying one of those Korean sunscreens would add warmth and aspiration without losing the product focus.
- **Make "USE CODE SKINWINS" a tappable pill button** with a black or gradient background — same copy, double the tap surface area, much clearer affordance on mobile.
- **Add review count + star rating directly below each hero product name** — even a single line "4.8 ★ (2,340 reviews)" changes the conversion math dramatically.
- **Collapse the sign-in row into the existing search bar area** — a small "Sign in for free shipping" ghost text next to the cart icon keeps the benefit visible without adding a full-height interstitial row.
