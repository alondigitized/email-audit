# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first swipe and I'm already spotting the Emi Jay Lychee Baby Collection front and center — if you know, you know. Sephora is fully in launch-drop mode right now, leaning into that exclusive, "only here" energy that keeps Beauty Insiders like me glued to the app. This feels like a brand that knows exactly who it's talking to, even if the loyalty perks aren't doing enough heavy lifting above the fold.

## What's working

- **"ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge on the Emi Jay hero** — the exclusivity callout is catnip for a Beauty Insider. I *know* I can't get this at Ulta.
- **Free Concealer Sample Set with $45 min. spend + code FINDMYSHADE** — the offer is specific, the bar is reachable, and the code is printed in full. No vague "see site for details."
- **"notes of lychee, cherry & vanilla" copy on the image** — ingredient-forward, sensory language. That's how I talk about products. It makes me want to smell it.
- **The Emi Jay hero itself** — limited-edition claw clips alongside a new mist scent? That's a bundle I'd buy without a second thought. Whoever chose this hero knew their audience.
- **Fragrance + Makeup tabs immediately visible in the category nav** — my two biggest spend categories are one tap away without digging into a hamburger menu.

## What's weak

- **No Rouge tier acknowledgment anywhere** — I'm a Beauty Insider Rouge and the homepage treats me like a stranger. No "Welcome back," no points balance, no Rouge exclusive teaser. That's a miss for retention.
- **The "Sign In" bar competes with the hero** — the black "Sign In" button on the left immediately above the hero creates a visual interrupt. My eye doesn't know whether to go left (account) or down (shop).
- **No urgency signal on the free concealer offer** — the promo strip doesn't say when the offer ends. Without a date, I'll clip it mentally and never come back to it.
- **No model/person in the hero** — it's a beautiful product flatlay but I want to *see* the mist on someone's skin or the claw clip in someone's hair. Aspirational lifestyle context is missing.
- **The second hero slide ("Hy..." — partially cut off)** — the carousel pagination dots hint at more content but give me no reason to swipe. Mystery isn't a hook.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Rouge/VIB status chip in the sign-in bar** — instead of generic "Sign In for FREE Shipping," show "Rouge member? Early access starts now ›" or display a points balance for logged-in users. One line of personalization makes this feel like it was built for me.
- **Put a deadline on the free concealer promo** — add "ends June 7" or "this weekend only" to the promo strip. Same words, three extra characters, massive urgency lift.
- **Swap the product flatlay hero for a lifestyle shot** — show someone with the Emi Jay mist mid-spritz, claw clips in an undone bun. Product flatlays are catalog; lifestyle sells the *feeling*.
- **Tease the next carousel slide** — if there are 4+ hero slides, show a sliver of the next card peeking in from the right edge. Implicit scroll invitation instead of passive pagination dots.
