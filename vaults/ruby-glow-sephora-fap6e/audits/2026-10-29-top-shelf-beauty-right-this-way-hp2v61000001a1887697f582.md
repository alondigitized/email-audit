# Top-shelf beauty, right this way 👉🏽
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- The strongest read is the offer, which sits in a thin banner above the hero. The "Savings Event" strip has a 30% off Rouge/VIB tier and a 10% off Insider tier, with dates of 11/3–11/9. It's labelled "Coming soon" and "Starts tomorrow." The actual hero is a "TOP SHELF" navy block with a Shop Now button, then a Shark Beauty "Limited-Edition" photo. Those compete for attention, and the subject line promises the second one, not the first.
- The back half is a standard Sephora product grid: eight "New / Only at Sephora" items with prices, then a Seth Rogen gift-guide teaser. It's tidy and shoppable, but it's a catalog, not a story. The subject "Top-shelf beauty, right this way 👉🏽" could be on any send in this run, and so could the content. Compared with 10/28 ("🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟") and 10/26 ("These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️"), this is the third new-arrivals or gift grid in four days.
- Judgement: competent and on-season, but it buries the best news (a member discount event starting tomorrow) and leads with vague "top shelf" copy. The Insider discount should have been the subject.

## What's working

- The Savings Event banner shows concrete percentages and dates (30% off, 10% Insider, 11/3–11/9) and has its own "Build Your Basket" button.
- The Insider 10% tier makes the member benefit visible without scrolling.
- The product grid is clean, with a consistent layout. Each item has a brand, product name, price, and a "New / Only at Sephora / Limited Edition" tag.
- Product tags like "Clean at Sephora" and set values ("$82 value") give some price justification.
- The footer perks strip (free shipping, credit card, Sephora Texts, app) is useful and consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- Two heroes compete. The savings banner and the "TOP SHELF" block each have their own CTA, so the eye doesn't know where to land.
- "Coming soon" on the discount banner reads as deferred. Nothing in the email says what to do today, so there is no urgency.
- Shop Now / See How Easy It Is / Build Your Basket are generic CTAs with little product specificity.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of text with multiple tier-specific dates, which weakens the "honest offer" read.
- The Seth Rogen "Anyone can gift beauty" tile has no clear tie to the rest of the email and splits focus again at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Savings Event the single hero. Put the percentage and the 11/3 start date in the subject and the headline, and demote "Top Shelf" to a secondary module.
- 2. Rewrite the CTAs to be specific, for example "Shop Shark Beauty sets" and "Save 10% as an Insider."
- 3. Cut the grid from eight items to four, led by the Shark Beauty hero product, and add ratings for social proof.
- 4. Break the fine print into short per-tier lines (Rouge, VIB, Insider) instead of one paragraph.
- 5. Drop the Seth Rogen tile, or move it into a dedicated gift-guide send.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus limited-edition Shark Beauty sets, only at Sephora.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a 700px fixed-width XHTML table layout with ESP click-tracking redirects. The automated QA run has 1 FAIL (no plain-text part) and 3 WARNs, and several deliverability and compliance items can't be verified from the data supplied. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and most link content were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Links are wrapped in ESP redirects, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1887697f582fec2369fea4b34/c652418d…?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gvf-…`. The final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Link probing:** 12 links were probed and 44 skipped. No per-link status codes were provided, so no broken links are confirmed. The 44 skipped links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>` is hidden. Clients that block images won't register opens, so open data is unreliable. This is expected behavior, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. A multipart/alternative with no text body hurts deliverability and breaks text-only clients. This could be a relay capture artifact, but it can't be ruled out.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** The tracking pixel `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…'>` has no `alt`. It is non-visual, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` anyway.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only resize a few classes (`.mobileHeaderLogo`, `.mobileIcon`, etc.). Without `max-width:100%` or stacking behavior on this table, it likely forces horizontal scroll on narrow clients that ignore the media query. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm the rest of the layout.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments aren't valid there, and some parsers will drop the rule that follows it.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Unsupported JSON-LD:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block is stripped by most clients. It also contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is the same for every send.
- **Preheader:** The `display:none` preheader is `Worth showing off.`, followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. Nested `div`s are well-formed.

No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The subject emoji `👉🏽` (with skin-tone modifier) is valid UTF-8, and the charset is declared as `utf-8`. Tokens in the truncated body can't be checked.

- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** They fall in the truncated section of the HTML, so they can't be verified. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result was provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **Landing-page alignment:** It can't be assessed without the redirect targets or probe results.

## 7. Recommendations

2. **Headers:** Capture the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run QA against the original message rather than the relayed copy.

5. **Layout:** Replace the fixed `width:700px; min-width:700px` with `width:100%; max-width:700px`.
6. **Alt text:** Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
7. **Coverage:** Re-run with the full HTML and per-link probe results. That lets you verify the footer, the unsubscribe link, final redirect destinations and UTMs, and the 44 skipped links.
