# Oooh, it’s 50% off select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "50% off select beauty," but the email that opens is "The Hair Styling Awards" — a 12-product hair-care review roundup with star ratings and reviewer quotes for shampoo, dry shampoo, mousse, and volumizing spray. The 50% off beauty box doesn't show up until deep in the footer stack, buried beneath a "15% off shampoo & conditioner" offer and a bonus-points call to action. This is the same subject-vs-hero mismatch this brand has run all week (9/4–9/8), but this send is the most extreme version yet: the entire primary content block has nothing to do with the subject line's offer.
- A subscriber who opens for the 50% off deal will scroll past nearly ten screens of hair-product award copy before finding it, and by then two other competing offers (bonus points, 15% off) have already been presented. That's not a hierarchy problem, it's a content-mismatch problem — the subject line and the email body appear to have been assembled independently.
- Judgement: weak send. The "Hair Styling Awards" content itself is well-produced (star ratings, real product names, clear visual grid), but pairing it with a beauty-discount subject line that isn't delivered until the bottom of the email is a clear bait-and-switch, consistent with this brand's recurring pattern.

## What's working

- Hair product roundup is well-organized with clear category headers (Sleekness and Shine, Definition, Volume) and star-rating badges.
- Product cards include reviewer quotes, price, and "Staff Pick"/"Clean at Sephora" callouts, giving real social proof.
- Visual design is clean — consistent product photography, no overlapping text or broken images visible.
- Rewards/points integration ("500 Bonus Points" badges) is visible and tied to spend thresholds.

## What's weak

- The subject's "50% off select beauty" offer doesn't appear until nearly the bottom of the email, after 12 unrelated hair-product reviews.
- No single clear hero/CTA at the top — the free hair trial-size banner, then an awards roundup, dilutes the promised discount message.
- Multiple competing offers stacked in the footer (15% off shampoo/conditioner, bonus points, 50% off beauty) create decision fatigue instead of one clear action.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency badge on the 50% box contradicts this offer's actual run since 9/4 per brand history — same false-urgency pattern flagged in prior audits.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 50% off select beauty box to the top of the email if that's the subject-line offer — don't make subscribers scroll through unrelated content to find what they opened for.
- 2. If the "Hair Styling Awards" content is the real intent of this send, change the subject line to reflect that instead of leading with a beauty discount most of the email doesn't deliver.
- 3. Cut the offer stack in the footer down to one primary CTA — currently three offers compete (bonus points, 15% off, 50% off).
- 4. Drop or substantiate the "TODAY ONLY" badge — this offer has run for six straight days per brand history, so the urgency reads as false.
