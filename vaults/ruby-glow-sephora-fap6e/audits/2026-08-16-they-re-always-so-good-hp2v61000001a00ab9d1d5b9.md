## What's working

- Trial-size offer at the very top has a real, specific incentive: free Medik8/AESTOP/Ultra Violette items with a $30 purchase and visible promo code (PERSKIN).
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the grid.
- Product grid has clear pricing on every item (Dior $65–$231, Shiseido $67, etc.) — no vague "shop now" without context.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "CLEAN" badges on Prada, Farmacy, and Kérastase add differentiation signals within an otherwise flat grid.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the footer reinforces loyalty segmentation visually.

## What's weak

- Subject "They're always SO good 👑" promises a specific hero story but the body is an eight-item generic grid — classic bait-and-switch on curation.
- No single hero product gets a "why this" callout; it's the same flat catalog pattern already dinged on 8/12 and 8/13.
- The trial-size offer at top and the $5-off-250-points offer at bottom compete for attention with zero connective tissue between them — two disconnected promos bookending one big undifferentiated grid.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section (Makeup's Latest Lineup / Beauty Insider Celebration events) is a second grid-within-a-grid, adding to scroll fatigue rather than focusing the eye.
- Crown emoji in subject doesn't pay off anywhere visually in the email — no "iconic"/"best of" framing device ties back to the 👑.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero product from the grid (ideally the Dior duo, since it's featured largest) and build the top of the email around it with a real "why it's a cult-fave" line — reviews count, a specific ingredient/scent note, anything — instead of a flat 8-SKU list with no differentiation.
- 2. Move the trial-size offer's promo code and $30 threshold into the subject/preheader — it's the only real dollar-value hook in the send and it's buried above the fold with no mention in the subject line.
- 3. Cut the grid from 8 items to 4-5 and add one line of "why" copy per product (a review snippet, a bestseller rank, "sold every 10 seconds") to justify "always SO good."
- 4. Consolidate the two bottom promo modules (Spot It/Shop It grid + $5 Beauty Insider offer) — pick one, since stacking unrelated offers dilutes both.
