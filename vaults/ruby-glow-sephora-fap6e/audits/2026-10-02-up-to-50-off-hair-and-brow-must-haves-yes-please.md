# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Sephora send of the day built around the same sale. The subject sells "up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves." The top banner does match it: "National Brow Day, Save up to 50% off select brow products," with a SHOP NOW button. Then the email pivots hard into an "October means new rewards!" Beauty Insider Rewards Bazaar. That section takes up most of the first half: a "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" border, a 100-point reward grid and a 250-point reward grid. The hair offer, "Up to 50% off Hair Faves, TODAY ONLY," doesn't show up until near the bottom.
- The subject promises a sale and the body is mostly a points catalog. A reader who opens for 50% off brow and hair has to scroll past a loyalty redemption menu to get to half of what was promised. The brow offer is well placed, but everything beneath it competes: rewards, 2X/5X points, a first-access holiday drop, 20% off Value-Size Hair Care, and the hair banner.
- I saw this on 2026-10-02, the same day as "50% OFF select brow products 🤨", so the brow sale is going out twice in one day. This one is the muddier of the two. It's a long, many-offer digest with a promise that's right at the top and a payoff that's buried.

## What's working

- The "National Brow Day" banner with "Save up to 50%" and a SHOP NOW button is visible at the very top, so the headline offer isn't hidden.
- The Beauty Insider rewards content (100 and 250 point tiers, "NEW REWARD OPTION") gives members a reason to engage beyond price.
- The "TODAY ONLY" tag on the hair banner and the "LIMITED TIME ONLY" tag at the top add credible urgency.
- The rewards grid is orderly: product shots with brand, name, point cost and an "ONLINE ONLY" tag.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- Too many offers compete: brow 50% off, rewards, 2X/5X points, holiday drops, 20% off value sizes, and hair faves. No single hero wins.
- The subject leads with hair and brow, but the hair deal is near the bottom, behind the rewards catalog.
- The rewards catalog (100 and 250 point items) takes up the middle of the email and doesn't connect to the subject's sale.
- The email is very long, and the same offers repeat in the banners and the "Don't miss out" section, so it reads as a digest.
- Most CTAs are a generic "SHOP NOW" or "Shop now". The fine print is long, with several promo codes (SAYPOINTS, DELIVERED) in small type.
- It's a near repeat of the earlier brow send the same day. A subscriber would see the cadence as noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Either run the brow-and-hair sale as the whole send, or send rewards separately. This would lift it the most.
- 2. If hair and brow are the subject, put both sale banners at the top, side by side, with the rewards catalog moved below.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, like "Shop brow deals" and "Shop hair deals", instead of a generic "Shop now".
- 4. Merge or skip a brow-only send on days when a combined brow-and-hair send is going out.
- 5. Show one or two named hero products with a price or percent, not just categories.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brow Day: up to 50% off, today only 💗`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hair + brow faves, up to 50% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new Rewards Bazaar picks from 100 points. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Big Olio, UNITE and more, markdown prices for one day only.`

## Technical Audit: Sephora Insider, "Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗"

### 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with VML/Outlook handling and media queries. The automated QA flags one hard failure, an empty plain-text part, plus several header and alt-text warnings. The HTML source is truncated, so body-level checks (links, footer, tokens) rest mostly on the QA data.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed only 12 of 58 links (46 skipped, mostly footer, utility and social), so coverage of the remaining links is unverified.
- Tracking and image hosts are `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (an opaque encoded path) and `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/...`. Both are third-party or ESP-controlled. No defect is evident, but the unlabeled `/O/` image is probably an open-tracking pixel.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images** (QA):
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` appears to be a navigation header image. Without alt text, the nav is unreadable with images blocked, and screen readers announce the filename.
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is likely a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so it isn't announced.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` has a space. The valid token is `noindex`, so the directive is likely ignored. This is minor, since it only matters for web-view copies.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Sephora` doesn't match the subject or content. This is low impact for email.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. HTML-only mail is an accessibility and deliverability weakness: spam filters penalize it, and text-only clients and some assistive setups get nothing.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these in the relay, so this is **unverified, not confirmed absent**. If they really are missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (≥5k/day) require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` can't be assessed from this relay. Verify against a direct-delivery copy.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reported no failure, so no violation is confirmed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no CTA `href` values or UTM parameters, and QA reported no landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** This is the only hard failure.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (the nav labels). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
4. **Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`**, or drop it.
5. **Re-audit with raw headers** from a direct-delivery copy to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** to check the CTA UTM parameters, the footer unsubscribe link and address, and any unrendered tokens.
