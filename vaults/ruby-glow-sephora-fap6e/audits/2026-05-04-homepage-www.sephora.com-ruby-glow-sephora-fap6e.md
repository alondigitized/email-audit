## What's working

- **The SCENTSYAY top banner** — a free fragrance sample set with a $50 spend and a clear promo code is the exact kind of reward that makes me add one more thing to my cart. Concrete, easy to redeem, relevant to me.
- **"Mother's Day Gifts $50 & Under" headline** — the price cap does the work. It removes decision fatigue and makes the click feel low-risk.
- **The product flat-lay aesthetic** — warm pinks, Sephora Favorites-style packaging, mini-sized product assortment scattered beautifully. As someone who buys sets twice a year during VIB sale, this imagery reads like candy.
- **Navigation bar with "New," "Makeup," "Skincare," "Fragrance"** — I can reach every category I care about in one tap, no hunting required.
- **AI Beauty tab with "NEW" badge** in the bottom nav — I'm immediately curious what that does.

## What's weak

- **Zero Beauty Insider / Rouge personalization above the fold** — I'm a Rouge member and there's no points balance, no tier badge, no "Welcome back, you have X points" moment. That's a missed VIP signal.
- **Hero speaks to gift-givers, not self-buyers** — "Value sets for the ones who guide, support, and nurture us" is nice sentiment, but I'm here to discover what just dropped, not write a hallmark card. There's no "New Arrivals" or "Just Launched" hook competing for attention.
- **No urgency signals** — Mother's Day has a real deadline but there's no "Ships by May 11" or countdown. That free-shipping / delivery cutoff is the most powerful conversion lever they're not using.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no ratings, no "10K reviews," no "community favorite" badge on any featured product. For a platform built on peer reviews, this is a wasted opportunity.
- **Sign-in strip feels transactional, not welcoming** — "Sign In for FREE Shipping" treats loyalty like a logistics perk instead of a status flex. Rouge members want to feel seen, not just processed.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic sign-in strip for a personalized Rouge moment** — show "Hi Ruby — you have [X] points. Rouge-exclusive early access to Mother's Day sets →" for logged-in members. Non-logged users can still see the sign-in CTA.
- **Add a Mother's Day shipping deadline badge to the hero** — "Order by May 10 for delivery in time" under the "SHOP NOW" CTA. One line, immediate conversion pressure, no dark patterns.
- **Surface a "New This Week" product chip or badge** on at least one product in the flat-lay so launch-obsessed shoppers like me have a reason to tap in beyond gifting.
- **Pull one hero slot for self-gifting** — something like "Treat Yourself Too" with a featured new mascara or limited-edition palette. Let the carousel earn its keep.
