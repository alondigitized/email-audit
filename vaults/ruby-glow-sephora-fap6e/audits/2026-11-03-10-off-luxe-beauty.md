# 💎 10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY 💎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a price-tier email with a subject line that promises something the body never shows. The subject says "10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY." In the render, the 10% only appears at the bottom, in the barcode fine print for Insiders (Nov 3–9). The hero is "Treat Yourself to Luxe Makeup" with three tiers: Luxe Under $40, $60 and $70. Each tier is a clean product grid with brand, item and price (Armani $39, Dior $55, Gucci $65). That is a good, shoppable layout, but it is a different offer from the one in the subject.
- The top third also works against the hero. An "in-store event" block ("Come hang with us… Find an event near you") sits above a Free Same-Day Delivery banner (code SPEEDY, $75 minimum, ends 11/9). The hero doesn't start until about a fifth of the way down. Below it come Holiday Hits, Hair Gift Sets, a $50-and-under gift strip and a gift guide, then dense legal copy and two barcodes for Kohl's. This is the fifth Sephora send in five days. It follows the 11/3 "FREE gift with purchase, Insider?" email and the 11/2 "STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING" email. A subscriber has seen the 10% message twice already, so this one adds little.
- Judgement: a competent catalog, but it is a mismatch between subject and body, and it is cluttered. The luxe grid is the good part, and the subject and header should have been built around it.

## What's working

- The three price-tier bands ("Luxe Under $40 / $60 / $70") make a clear, scannable frame. A shopper can go straight to their budget.
- Each product tile names the brand, the item and the exact price (e.g. "Eye Tint Long-Lasting Liquid Eyeshadow, $39"). That is useful detail.
- The imagery is rich and premium. The star-shaped eyeshadow and the YSL and Dior tiles read as luxe.
- The Free Same-Day Delivery banner has a credible deadline (11/9, 1pm PT) and a code, which gives it real urgency.
- The Insider barcode with the 11/3–11/9 window gives loyalty members a clear way to redeem in store.

## What's weak

- The subject promises 10% off, and the body never states it near the top. The reader has to reach the barcode footer to find it.
- The header stacks an event invite, a stacking note and a delivery promo before the hero. That is three competing messages above the main one.
- "SPLURGE NOW" is a vague CTA with no category or product, and it is one small button for a very long email.
- The lower half, with Holiday Hits, Hair Gift Sets, $50 gifts and a gift guide, dilutes the luxe message. The email scrolls to roughly 6,900px, and the fine print runs very long.
- The creative gives no sign of product reviews or ratings. The cadence also feels repetitive, with a fifth promo in five days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and the hero match. Put the 10% Insider offer in a badge above "Treat Yourself to Luxe Makeup" and cut the event block, or move it to the bottom.
- 2. Move the delivery banner below the first product tier, or fold it into a thin strip. One offer should lead.
- 3. Replace "SPLURGE NOW" with a specific CTA such as "Shop Luxe Under $40," and add a button under each tier.
- 4. Cut the lower modules (hair gifts, $50 gifts, gift guide) or push them to a separate send.
- 5. Add star ratings to the product tiles.
- 6. Space out the next send, or give it a new angle, since the same 10% offer has been in several sends this week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Luxe makeup under $40 (plus 10% off for Insiders)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dior, YSL, Armani from $39 — 10% off this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Insiders save 10% through 11/9. Free same-day delivery over $75 with code SPEEDY.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop luxe under $40, $60 and $70, then use your Insider barcode in store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a fixed-width 700px table layout with hidden tracking pixels. QA reports a 50% pass rate, with one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several header checks that can't be confirmed. The source was truncated, so links, footer and merge tokens could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 46. It reports no broken or redirecting links, so none are flagged. Coverage is limited to the highest-ranked links, and the skipped ones are unverified.
- Two tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a hidden open-tracking pixel (`display:none; mso-hide:all`).
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261103_Oct_Luxe_MBC_Lite_Dedicated_BI` is a 1x1 pixel carrying a user ID and campaign code. Two separate pixels means two tracking systems. That is not a defect, but a client that blocks images will suppress both.
- The `mi_ecmp` value says `Oct` while its date prefix is `20261103`. This is a naming inconsistency in the campaign code and doesn't break anything.
- The JSON-LD block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`, so the code is empty. `PromotionCard` is not a recognized schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>`, so the practical impact is nil. The markup is still invalid.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part is missing (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** Without a `text/plain` alternative, spam filters may penalize the message and text-only clients get nothing. The failure could be an AgentMail relay capture issue, so check the raw MIME.
- **The `alt` warnings are mostly false positives.** The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is the correct treatment for a decorative image. The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt`, but it is `display:none`, so screen readers shouldn't reach it. Adding `alt=""` to it would silence the warning.
- **The `<style>` block has a malformed MSO conditional.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block. In non-Outlook parsers, `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` becomes an invalid selector. The next rule, `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge`, will likely be dropped as well. That would leave the Rouge tier-badge mobile sizing unapplied.
- **Fixed layout:** the wrapper table has `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The media query only changes font sizes and a few image widths, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally.
- **Pinch zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks it in clients that honor the tag. This is an accessibility issue.
- `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. There are no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The user-specific values are in the tracking pixel URLs, and they are populated. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both. QA notes the relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Verify it against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`. Check the raw headers.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set and reports no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No evidence either way. The truncated HTML shows no CTA URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be evaluated. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp` in the pixel URL.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. Pull the raw MIME to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. If the headers are really absent, add them.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule, and check that `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` is applied after the fix.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Delete the JSON-LD block, or populate `discountCode` and use valid types.
6. Add `alt=""` to the hidden `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel. Treat the `o.gif` alt warning as a false positive.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the CTA URLs, UTM parameters, footer compliance and the 46 skipped links.
