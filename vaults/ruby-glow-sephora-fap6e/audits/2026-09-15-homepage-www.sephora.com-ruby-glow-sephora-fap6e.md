# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened Sephora on my phone and got... a wall of grey loading boxes. There's a top banner about deals and a nav bar that looks like Sephora, but the entire hero and product carousel area never rendered — just skeleton placeholders. As a Rouge member who checks this app compulsively for new drops, this is exactly the kind of glitch that makes me close the app and check Ulta's site instead.

## What's working

- The top banner strip ("Don't miss out! So many deals, trial sizes, sample sets and more. Shop Beauty Offers") at least gives me a reason to tap, even if generic.
- The nav bar is clean and legible — "50% Off Deals," "Makeup," "Sale & Offers" are all one tap away, which matters when I'm hunting a specific category fast.
- "AI Beauty Chat NEW" badge catches my eye as a Rouge member who likes trying new features first.
- "Chosen For You — Personalized based on your preferences" module at the bottom signals personalization, which is the kind of thing that keeps me scrolling on a normal day.

## What's weak

- The entire hero and carousel section is just three grey skeleton boxes with grey placeholder text bars underneath — nothing rendered, no product, no imagery, no copy.
- Because nothing loaded, I have zero sense of what's on sale, what's new, or what launch is being pushed this week — which is the whole reason I open this app.
- No visible rewards/points balance or Rouge-tier callout, which is a returning-shopper hook I'd expect front and center for someone like me.
- "Chosen For You" text appears but with no actual product images to back it up yet.

## Recommendations

- Fix the render/load issue so hero banners and the "Chosen For You" carousel actually populate before the page is considered "loaded" — or show a proper fallback image instead of raw skeleton state.
- Put my Rouge points balance or tier status near the top nav, not buried — I want to see "247 points to your next reward" the second I open the app.
- Replace the vague "So many deals, trial sizes, sample sets and more" banner copy with a specific offer (e.g., "Free deluxe sample with $35+" or a launch countdown).
- Once images load, lead the hero carousel with an actual new-launch mascara or a VIB/Rouge sale callout — that's the content that gets me to stop scrolling.
