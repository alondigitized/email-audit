# All over your feed 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a social-proof email wrapped around a free-trial-size offer. The subject, "All over your feed 🤩", sells TikTok-style hype. The strongest offer is at the top: a FREE Emi Jay Angelstick trial size with a minimum spend. The "TRENDING ON SOCIAL… And actually *that* good" block, the Emi Jay x Dedcool collab, and the "Spot it. Shop it." section all follow the subject's theme.
- Execution is the usual Sephora shelf, though. The render is about 7,000px tall. It runs from the hero through a three-item "We Think You'll Love These" row and an eight-card "Trending Picks" grid, then Auto-Replenish 15% off, a Venmo tile, "Florals for Fall", and a Beauty Insider tier bar. Beyond the hero, nothing is a clear second-priority message. The savings-event strip (30% / 10% / "Coming soon") is a nice member touch. It is small and its dates and terms are hard to read.
- Judgement: a decent, on-brand send that is let down by length and a vague subject. It's a close cousin of 10/8's Dr. Groot trial-size email and 10/11's "Beauty for literally everybody", which also stacked a trial-size offer on a catch-all grid. A subscriber who saw both would read this as the same send with new products.

## What's working

- The hero puts a concrete, free offer (an Emi Jay trial size) above everything else, with a visible "SHOP NOW" button.
- "TRENDING ON SOCIAL" is large, high-contrast and instantly readable. It carries through the subject's theme.
- The Emi Jay x Dedcool collab gets its own section with "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags. That signals exclusivity and creates urgency.
- Product cards show names and prices, with some showing sale prices in parentheses, so a reader can judge value without clicking.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar and savings-event strip keep the loyalty benefits visible.
- The render is clean. I can see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- At about 7,000px the email is far too long. The eight-card "Trending Picks" grid and the lower modules (Auto-Replenish, Venmo, florals) dilute the hero.
- The subject "All over your feed 🤩" says nothing about the offer. It doesn't mention the free trial size, a discount or a category.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons are generic. "SHOP NOW" appears on the hero, the trending block, Auto-Replenish, the Venmo tile and the florals block. None says what the click leads to.
- The key terms are buried. "*Exclusions/terms apply" and the free-gift qualifying spend sit in tiny type beside the hero. The savings-event strip's 30% / 10% tiers and dates are also tiny and hard to read.
- The late modules compete with the lead. "You can Venmo us!" and the Auto-Replenish "15% off" deal are separate offers that each ask for attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by half. Keep the hero, the Emi Jay collab, and one product row of 3-4 items. Move Auto-Replenish, Venmo and florals to other sends. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put the offer in the subject. The hook is the trial size, so say so.
- 3. Make the hero CTA specific, for example "Get my free Emi Jay mini". Replace the repeated "SHOP NOW" buttons with product- or category-specific verbs.
- 4. Move the qualifying spend and deadline into readable type next to the offer, for example "Free with $X, ends [date]". Don't leave them only in the footnote.
- 5. Make the 30% / 10% / Coming soon savings strip legible, with the dates and the tier it applies to, or drop it.
- 6. Collapse the "We Think You'll Love These" and "Trending Picks" sections into one, and rank the items by what's actually selling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Emi Jay Angelstick, as seen on TikTok`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Emi Jay x Dedcool drop is here 🤩`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free trial size with your min. spend, plus the limited-edition clip set only at Sephora.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Emi Jay's viral hair tools, Insider savings, and what's trending right now.`

# Technical Audit: "All over your feed 🤩" (Sephora Insider, shop@beauty.sephora.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email sent through Epsilon (`images.harmony.epsilon.com`). The one hard QA failure is a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed from the captured data. The source is truncated, so findings below are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 55 skipped. The QA data reports no broken or redirecting links among the probed set.
- The 55 skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not verified.
- One image is served from the tracking-style path `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...`. It is likely the open-tracking pixel, which QA flagged for missing alt (see section 3).
- No other tracking issues are confirmable from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA WARN):**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...` is probably the tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-.../2021_Navs_Header_06...` is the nav header image. If it is a real content image, it needs descriptive alt text, because a missing alt attribute makes screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`, so the space makes the directive invalid. It has little practical effect in email, but it is a code defect.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. It matters mainly for the web-view version.
- **Text-size adjustment:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This disables user text-size scaling on iOS and similar clients.
- **Media queries:** the `*[class=x], .x` attribute-selector pattern is verbose and redundant. It is not an error.
- No further rendering problems are confirmable in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The truncated remainder was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback: 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and spam scoring, and it is an accessibility regression for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, the email does not meet Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown from this capture. Sephora's `beauty.sephora.com` subdomain sent via Epsilon is likely aligned, but that cannot be verified here.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical address were not visible in the truncated HTML. QA reports an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result is not given.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. The truncated source shows no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified. The QA data lists no redirect or landing failures for the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** This is the only confirmed failure.
2. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers** at the sending side, since the relay may have dropped them.
3. **Check SPF, DKIM and DMARC** from the original message headers, not the relay copy.
4. **Add alt text.** Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel and a descriptive alt on `2021_Navs_Header_06`.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`.
6. **Give the `<title>` a descriptive value.**
7. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML.** That would verify the UTMs, the footer address, the unsubscribe href, and the 55 skipped links.
