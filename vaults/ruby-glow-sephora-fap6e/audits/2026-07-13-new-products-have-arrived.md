# 🎉 New products have arrived
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's first explicit "new arrivals" send since the Stacking Weekend campaign closed — a logical pivot after the July 11–12 editorial double ("These picks are everywhere" + "Obsessed? Same."). The format is clean: one hero offer in the banner, a pick-your-favorite hero, a mid-scroll gift conversion moment, and a new-product grid. The bones are solid.
- But the subject line — "🎉 New products have arrived" — is the weakest possible version of a new-arrivals subject. Compare it to July 7's "ENDS TODAY: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, 2X points on all products, and FREE Same-Day Delivery," which stacked every offer into the subject explicitly and scored a 9. This subject gives a subscriber who's received seven emails in 13 days absolutely no reason to prioritize the open. No product name, no category, no offer hook, no urgency — just an emoji and the vaguest possible promise.
- Once you're inside, the email delivers. The Rare Beauty free gift is a legitimate mid-body conversion lever, the minis offer is real, and the product grid gives you names and prices to evaluate. The execution gap is entirely at the inbox layer: the subject squanders the open rate, and "SHOP NOW" undersells what's actually a well-curated arrival drop.

## What's working

- Top banner "Buy 2 Minis, Get Both 50% Off" leads with a concrete price-reduction hook before the hero even loads
- "Take your PICK" hero is clean — large editorial type, warm palette, visible SHOP NOW button, no visual clutter above the fold
- FREE Rare Beauty Gift section creates a second conversion event mid-scroll without requiring a separate promo promise
- Product grid shows specific product names and prices — gives the reader something real to react to, not just brand imagery
- App Early Access column adds channel diversification without bloating the main message

## What's weak

- "🎉 New products have arrived" is maximally generic — no product name, no category, no offer, no reason for a cadence-fatigued subscriber to open now
- No urgency anywhere in the body: neither the minis offer nor the Rare Beauty gift has a deadline or supply signal, making it trivially easy to close and forget
- "SHOP NOW" CTA is a missed opportunity — for a new-arrivals email, "Shop New In" or "Explore New Drops" takes two seconds to write and adds directional signal
- Seven emails in 13 days means this send is competing against Sephora's own recent memory in the subscriber's inbox — "Obsessed? Same." two days ago covered editorial discovery; this email retreads the same territory without escalating the hook
- The free Rare Beauty gift CTA ("SHOP TO REDEEM >") offers no visible minimum, condition, or deadline — the mechanism is opaque and reduces motivation rather than building it

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject to name the offer or a product.** "Your free Rare Beauty gift is here" or "New from Rare Beauty, Benefit + more" would outperform the current line with almost no effort. This is the single change with the highest open-rate upside.
- 2. **Add a soft deadline or supply signal to the Rare Beauty gift.** "Free gift — while supplies last" or a concrete end date converts that mid-body section from informational to actionable.
- 3. **Pull the minis offer into the preheader.** "Buy 2 minis, get both 50% off" is a genuine value proposition that's currently buried in the banner. It belongs in the inbox preview.
- 4. **Sharpen the SHOP NOW CTA.** Replace with "Explore New Arrivals" or "Shop What's New" — same button, meaningfully more signal.
- 5. **Tighten the email length.** The Benefit Pocket Pal section and App Early Access panel dilute the new-arrivals narrative; on a heavy-cadence week, a focused email outperforms a comprehensive one.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2 minis, get both 50% off. Shop the freshest Sephora arrivals.`
