# Choose one of two FREE sample bags filled with fan faves 💫 Min. spend required.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear free-gift send, with one caveat: the render is very small, so I read the layout and large type more reliably than the fine print. A "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag" banner leads, with a "Shop to Redeem" button, a 30% Savings Event badge and an Insider free-shipping strip. The rest is a holiday gift-set edit: six "New • Only at Sephora • Limited Edition" sets, each with a price and value claim, such as $98 ($164 value).
- The subject promises one thing, the sample bag. The body delivers that at the top, then pivots to gift sets, which is the same pattern as the 10/19 "hot sets" send. The free-bag offer is the strongest lever. It is buried under a long product scroll, and the asterisk and "Min. spend required" qualifier make it feel conditional. Sephora has sent six emails in the last ten days (10/12 to 10/20), so this one competes with near-identical beauty promos.
- Judgement: a solid, on-brand 6 to 7. The free-gift hook is good, but the email splits attention between the gift, the savings event, free shipping and a long gift-set list.

## What's working

- The free sample bag is the first thing you see, and "Shop to Redeem" is a clear action.
- Each gift set has a large product shot, a price and a "$XX value" line, which makes the savings concrete.
- The gold-framed cards repeat one layout, so the scroll is easy to follow.
- Insider perks (free shipping and points) sit near the top for members.
- The 30% Savings Event badge and the holiday gift framing match the current season.

## What's weak

- The subject ends with "Min. spend required." and the body has an asterisk, so the offer feels conditional. The spend threshold isn't visible at a glance.
- The top stacks three competing messages: the sample bag, the 30% badge and the free shipping.
- After the sample-bag hero, the email becomes a generic gift-set grid. The sample bags themselves are barely shown, so you can't see what is inside.
- There is no clear deadline or "ends" date near the offer, so the urgency is weak.
- Six sends in ten days with the same "Only at Sephora" and limited-edition language makes this feel like cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the sample bag the whole story. Show both bags side by side with their contents, the spend threshold in the headline (for example "with $35 purchase"), and one deadline.
- 2. Cut the 30% badge and the free-shipping strip from the top fold, or move them below the hero.
- 3. Trim the gift sets to three or four. Each should link to its product page and tie back to the free bag.
- 4. Replace the "Shop Now" and "Shop to Redeem" buttons with one specific button, such as "Choose Your Free Bag".
- 5. Add a visible end date to the offer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Choose Team Glow or Team Glam, only while supplies last. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

It also found three images without alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay may have stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
No issues found. 12 material links were probed and 43 were skipped by ranking, and QA reported no broken, redirecting or erroring links. The 43 skipped links (mostly footer, utility and social) were not verified, so coverage is partial.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 3 images (QA WARN):**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2021_Navs_Header_06`) looks like a navigation header image. With images blocked or a screen reader, any nav links in it have no text equivalent.
  - `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../US_4XHYDRATE_Barcod…`) is a barcode or code image. The redemption information is unavailable when images are off.
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx…` is the image on `app.sephora.com/O/`, which looks like an open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on some clients. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This blocks user font scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Non-descriptive title:** `<title>Sephora</title>`. It is minor, but it gives no context to assistive tech or to web-view rendering.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients and only matters if the web-view version is indexed.
- **Stale asset name:** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` suggests a reused 2021 header. This is informational and does not break anything.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The rest of the body was not available, so this is only confirmed for the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **QA FAIL, plain-text part missing:** the text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and spam scoring, and it also hurts accessibility for text-only clients.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN):** neither header was found, so RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. QA notes that the relay may have dropped them. Sephora is a large sender that usually sets them, so verify this against the raw message headers before treating it as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA expected this from the relay, so it is a monitoring gap rather than a confirmed failure.
- The body-level unsubscribe link is always included in probing, and QA reported no failure for it. Physical address presence was not verifiable in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. The visible source gives no evidence of missing or inconsistent UTM parameters or landing-page mismatch. The probed links raised no continuity errors, and the truncated source doesn't show the CTA hrefs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part** with the offer, the min-spend condition, CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Add alt text.** Use the real nav labels for `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` and the code or offer text for `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg`. Use `alt=""` on the `app.sephora.com/O/…` tracking pixel.
3. **Fix the viewport meta** by removing `maximum-scale=1`. Also scope or remove the `text-size-adjust: none` rule so it only targets the iOS auto-inflation quirk.
4. **Verify the headers on the raw message** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`), outside the relay. Treat missing headers as a defect only if they are also absent there.
5. **Minor cleanup:** change `content="no index"` to `noindex`, and give the `<title>` a descriptive value.
