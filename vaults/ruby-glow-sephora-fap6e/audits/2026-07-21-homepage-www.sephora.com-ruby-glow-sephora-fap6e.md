# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened Sephora and... it's barely there? The hero is literally a giant grey box — the page is mid-load and nothing actually rendered. I can see the promo banner, the nav pills, and the bottom tab bar, but the main hero image, the headline, the CTA — all skeleton placeholders. As a Beauty Insider Rouge who's on this app constantly, I know what Sephora *can* do, but right now this screenshot shows me a brand that did not deliver its homepage before the capture happened.

## What's working

- **The promo banner** — "Get a FREE\* hair sample set with $45 min. spend. Use code GETSAMPLE" is right at the very top, high contrast yellow background, and I can act on that immediately. $45 is totally reachable for me in one order.
- **The quick-nav pill row** — "Shop My Store," "New," "Summer Sets Under $35," and a partially visible "Makeup" pill are *exactly* the kind of zero-friction shortcuts I want on mobile. "New" and "Summer Sets Under $35" both speak to my browse behavior.
- **The bottom tab bar** — Home, Shop, Offers, My Store, and a "NEW AI Beauty" tab (with a sparkle badge) tells me Sephora is leaning into personalization tech. As a high-tech-comfort millennial, that AI Beauty tab intrigues me.
- **Search bar prominence** — big, centered, impossible to miss. I know exactly where to go when I have a specific product in mind.

## What's weak

- **The hero is a blank grey box** — the entire focal point of the page failed to load. No image, no headline, no CTA button. This is the worst possible first impression.
- **Skeleton text blocks below the hero** — grey pill-shaped placeholders where product name, description, and CTA button should be. Nothing I can read or tap.
- **No Beauty Insider hook above the fold** — I'm Rouge tier and there's zero acknowledgment of that. No "Welcome back, Ruby" moment, no points balance, no member-exclusive offer.
- **The partially cut-off nav pill** — "Makeup" is sliced at the right edge, hinting there's more but I can't see it. On a first load, that friction could make me miss a category entirely.
- **No product imagery whatsoever** — between the blank hero and the skeleton cards beside it, there is literally zero merchandise on screen.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the page load before anything else** — the hero skeleton is a critical render issue. Prioritize lazy-loading the banner image so *something* visual appears within the first 1–2 seconds; even a brand color + headline-only fallback beats a grey box.
- **Inject a logged-in loyalty hook into the top banner or just below nav** — swap the generic promo banner for a personalized one for Rouge members: "Ruby, you have 1,240 points — redeem now" would stop me cold.
- **Make the "Summer Sets Under $35" pill lead to a curated makeup/skincare landing, not just a generic sale page** — the pill copy is already good; the destination needs to match the energy with editorial photography and Rouge early-access framing.
- **Surface the "AI Beauty" tab's value prop in the hero** — if there's a new AI feature, tease it there with a specific hook ("Find your perfect foundation shade in 30 seconds") so it doesn't just look like a mystery badge.
