# Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend ❣️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's "Stacking Weekend" send — a legitimate structural improvement over the brand's usual bait-and-switch pattern seen in prior audits (8/29, 8/31, 9/2, 9/3 all opened with a "50% off" banner that gave way to unrelated new-arrivals content). Here the "50% off Select Beauty" and "500 bonus points with $75 spend" offers are stacked together explicitly, both are named in the subject, and the top third of the email actually delivers on that promise with a clear circular badge layout before pivoting to product carousels.
- That said, this is still a maximalist, offer-soup email — five distinct promotions (free same-day delivery, 50% off select beauty, 500 bonus points, free hair sample bag, $10 beauty cash redemption) compete in the first two screens before you even reach a single product. The "50% off Select Beauty ends 9/8" banner and the separate later "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" banner mid-email create the same internal contradiction (9/8 vs "today only") that's dogged this brand's recent sends.
- Verdict: better-executed than the last several Sephora sends because the offers are consolidated and visually anchored up top, but it repeats the "today only" urgency claim inconsistently and still buries the actual limited-time deal under a wall of secondary promos.

## What's working

- The hero band clearly stacks two concrete offers (50% off select beauty, 500 bonus points at $75 spend) with a plus sign visually tying them together — this directly matches the subject line's promise, unlike recent sends that decoupled subject and hero.
- Bold red/white color-blocking creates strong visual hierarchy at the top of the email — the eye lands on "Stacking Weekend" then the two offer circles immediately.
- "Beauty Insider" tier tabs (Insider/VIB/Rouge) are visible near the footer, giving loyalty members a visible reference point.
- Product grid ("Save on Must Haves Like These") uses real, legible product images and pricing (Make Up For Ever $29.40, NEST $108, etc.) with no broken images or lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The "50% off Select Beauty" badge in the hero says "Ends 9/8," but a near-identical "50% off Select Beauty" banner further down says "TODAY ONLY" — same offer, two conflicting deadlines in one email.
- Five separate offers (delivery, 50% off, bonus points, free hair sample bag, $10 cash redemption) stacked in the first two screens dilutes focus; nothing signals which is the priority.
- After the strong offer block, the email reverts to the brand's default new-arrivals catalog scroll (Spot it Shop it, RHODE, "Come Hang with Us"), the same padding pattern flagged in the 8/29 and 8/31 audits.
- The barcode/in-store redemption block at the very bottom is generic and disconnected visually from the rest of the email's red branding — feels bolted on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and one deadline. Don't run "50% off, ends 9/8" and "50% off, TODAY ONLY" in the same email — this is the exact credibility problem flagged in the 8/31 and 8/29 audits and it undermines urgency copy entirely.
- 2. Cut the offer count in the top two screens from five to two (keep 50% off + bonus points; move free delivery, hair sample bag, and points-redemption info into a secondary "more ways to save" section below the fold).
- 3. Move at least 2-3 real products above the fold alongside the offer badges so the click path is shorter — right now it's a long scroll of pure promo copy before any shoppable item appears.
- 4. Shorten and de-density the footer legal/terms block visually — it's currently a wall of small gray text that hurts scannability but doesn't need to be cut for content reasons.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stacking Weekend: 50% off + 500 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your biggest beauty savings weekend starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Combine 50% off select beauty with bonus points — ends 9/8`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free samples, bonus points, and 50% off — all weekend`
