# Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest Sephora send in the recent run. The hero is a huge "50% OFF" on a black sparkle background, with "Black Friday Starts Online Now" above it and a single SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first. It is a real step up from the 30% Cyber Week blocks of 11/21–11/26 (e.g. "Stop scrolling: Today's deal is here 😲"). It also fits the calendar, since the offer runs 11/27–11/29.
- The weak points are further down. The 50% claim is vague, because the hero says "select gifts" and shows no specific product or price. Below it, the email turns into a long, busy scroll: a "Want more Cyber Week deals?" grid, "You Might Also Like", a 30% tools banner, a store locator, a gift guide and a barcode. The "Cyber Week" language is also a little stale next to a "Black Friday" hero. Overall it is a good hero attached to a bloated body.

## What's working

- The hero "50% OFF" is enormous, high contrast and clearly the focal point.
- The single SHOP NOW button sits directly under the offer, with dates (11/27 through 11/29) in the supporting line.
- The product images are cleanly arranged under the hero (skincare, fragrance, palette) and set the gifting theme.
- The top banner offers 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT, which gives members a benefit.
- Product cards show real prices and strikethroughs (Dr. Jart $52 → $36.40, LANEIGE $24 → $18), which are concrete.
- The store locator and hours are a useful in-store tie-in, and the scannable barcode lets someone redeem in store.

## What's weak

- "50% OFF" with a footnote symbol and "select gifts" is vague, and the exclusions are buried in the fine print.
- The purple 4X-points banner competes with the hero as a second offer.
- The body repeats "Cyber Week" messaging, which clashes with the Black Friday hero. It also adds a third offer ("Up to 30% off select skin and hair tools") that dilutes the 50%.
- The "Want more Cyber Week deals?" and "You Might Also Like" sections are repetitive. UNOVE appears in both, and the sections are visually busy.
- The email is very long, and the footer and legal text are dense. The tiny gift guide buttons are hard to tap.
- This is the sixth or seventh send in about a week, so cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name what is 50% off in the hero, for example "50% off gift sets" with one hero product and its price. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Cut the body to one product grid and drop "You Might Also Like", the duplicate UNOVE card and the extra 30% banner.
- 3. Retire the "Cyber Week" language in a Black Friday send, or make it consistent.
- 4. Move the 4X points banner below the hero, or merge it into the offer line.
- 5. Make the gift guide buttons larger and move them up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off gifts starts now at Sephora`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: 50% off gift sets, 3 days only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select gifts for them and beauty faves for you, 11/27–11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. This hurts spam scoring and text-only clients. It may be a capture artifact, but the QA check failed as reported.
- **Missing alt text (QA flagged 3 images):**
  - The Epsilon-hosted image `20261126_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Faves_Thursda…` has no `alt`. If it is content or a CTA, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. I can't see its markup in the truncated source.

  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` at all. It is hidden, but it should still get `alt=""`.

- **Layout is wider than a phone screen.** The wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. The only media query is `@media (max-width:480px)`. Unless the truncated remainder overrides this, narrow viewports will scroll horizontally.
- **Malformed CSS.** An MSO conditional comment sits inside the `@media` block: `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->`. Non-MSO parsers may treat `<!--` as a CDO token. That can invalidate the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, in some clients.
