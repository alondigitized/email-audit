# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm immediately hit with the Minis Sale banner — red, top of screen, 50% off, ends *tomorrow*, and I already know this because I've had it circled on my mental calendar for weeks. The rest of the screen is all "Chosen For You" which is giving me personalized fragrance and rhode picks, and honestly? The algorithm knows me. This is Sephora doing what Sephora does best: making a Rouge member feel like the app was built for her specifically.

## What's working

- **"Chosen For You" personalization rail** — rhode and Chanel in the same carousel is genuinely my basket. The algorithm pulled through and the layout puts this front and center instead of burying it.
- **Minis Sale urgency banner** — Red background, 50% off, ends 7/15. As someone who has her VIB sale stockpile strategy planned out months in advance, this banner is a reflex tap for me.
- **Star ratings + review counts on product cards** — Valentino at 2.9K reviews and Chanel at 723 reviews right there in the "Chosen For You" grid. I don't need to open the PDP to know these are legit picks.
- **"AI Beauty" tab in bottom nav with NEW badge** — I clocked it immediately. As a high-tech-comfort beauty insider, I *will* be tapping that.
- **Category nav tabs** — "New," "Minis Sale," "Makeup," "Skincare" all surfaced in one row. I can get anywhere in one tap.

## What's weak

- **Skeleton/loading element visible between nav and content** — There's a gray circle and bar just sitting there unrendered. Looks broken. For a first impression on mobile, that's sloppy and chips at the premium feel Sephora is supposed to project.
- **No lifestyle imagery whatsoever** — It's all product photography on white. I love a product shot but I also want to see the *vibe* — a model, an editorial scene, something aspirational. For a beauty brand this visually rich, the first screen feels flat.
- **No loyalty balance or Rouge callout** — I'm clearly logged in (they're showing me personalized picks), so why isn't my points balance or Rouge status visible? That's a massive missed hook for someone like me who checks her point balance like a bank account.
- **Free-shipping threshold not mentioned** — I know Sephora ships free at $35 for Rouge but nothing on screen confirms it. That's a conversion nudge they left on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the unrendered skeleton element immediately** — Whatever profile/loyalty module is failing to load above "Chosen For You" needs a timeout fallback, not a floating gray placeholder. Ship a fix or suppress the element.
- **Add a loyalty balance chip next to the search bar** — One small "15,230 pts | Rouge" badge near my avatar would turn this from a generic personalized homepage into a loyalty dashboard that pulls me deeper every visit.
- **Inject one lifestyle/editorial hero tile** — Swap the gap above "Chosen For You" for a full-bleed editorial image tied to the Minis Sale. Show someone like me with a beauty haul, not just bottles on white.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** — Add "Free shipping for Rouge members" or "Free at $35" after the 50% off copy. One line. Removes a friction point at the bottom of the funnel.
