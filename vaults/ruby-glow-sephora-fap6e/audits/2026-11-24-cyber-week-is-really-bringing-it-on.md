# Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but crowded Cyber Week send. The hero, "It's Good to Be You" on purple sequins, leads into three stacked 30% off blocks (Kiehl's, Laura Mercier, Youth to the People), each with a SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offers, and the repeated 30% makes the deal easy to understand. This is the same purple sequin template as the 11/21, 11/22 and 11/23 sends, so it's the fourth near-identical Cyber Week email in four days.
- The weakness is everything around the hero. A "FREE* Sample Bag" banner with a GRABMYBAG code and a "Cyber Week is here! Save up to 50%" banner sit above the actual hero. That makes three competing offers before the first 30% block. Below the hero are a product grid, a 25% off Sol de Janeiro block, a Korean beauty block, a holiday gift guide and a loyalty strip, so the email never settles on one message. The 30% figure also carries a footnote mark, and the grey "Available on in-stock merchandise only" line is small.
- Judgment: solid execution, no sharp focus. It's fine for a promo-heavy week, but a persona would struggle to tell what's new compared with yesterday's send.

## What's working

- Three large "30% OFF" blocks with brand logos and product shots are scannable and unambiguous.
- Every block has its own SHOP NOW button, so the CTAs are clear.
- Purple sequins with white numerals stay consistent with the Cyber Week campaign and the earlier sends.
- The "24 hours–only at Sephora. In store and online." line adds credible urgency.
- The footer carries loyalty and utility elements: free shipping, a Sephora Credit Card offer, a loves list and app download.

## What's weak

- Three offers stack above the hero (50% banner, free sample bag, then the 30% hero), which dilutes the top of the email.
- The 30% repeats across three brands with no differentiated product or reason to pick one.
- The "Treat Yourself for Less" grid gives no deadline and uses small price text, so it reads like filler.
- The email is very long and has many sections, and the late blocks (Sol de Janeiro, Korean beauty, gift guide) pull attention away from the main offer.
- The footer fine print and the footnoted offers are small and dense, so conditions are easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the top stack to a single banner and lead with one hero offer. The free sample bag deserves its own send.
- 2. Put a different hook in the hero, such as a "today only" brand of the day or a Beauty Insider tier perk, so the email feels distinct from the 11/21–11/23 sends.
- 3. Trim to three sections: the hero deals, one curated product row with a deadline, and the loyalty strip.
- 4. State the exclusions plainly under the offers, for example "30% off select items, in-stock only", instead of using footnote marks.
- 5. Name the deadline in the subject and the first line: "through 11/30".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based 700px layout with Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The main confirmed defect is a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Authentication and unsubscribe-header gaps are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what's visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and 40 footer, utility and social links were skipped, so the skipped links are unverified.
- **Redirect tracking:** links go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a20df…?__dU__=…`, a per-recipient redirect. Destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source, so they can't be audited here.
- **Tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all"/>` is a hidden open pixel. It has no width or height attributes, which is minor.
- **Schema markup:** the JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a defined schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>` from email, so this is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The only media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)`, and it contains no rule that collapses this wrapper. On narrow viewports this likely causes horizontal scroll or scaling, unless the inner tables are fluid. The inner tables use `max-width:700px; width:100%`, so this needs a real-device check.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS rules, and some parsers may drop the rules that follow. The `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc` rules come right after it.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The impact is negligible in email.
- **Alt text (QA warning):** the only image flagged is the hidden 1x1 tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`). It is hidden with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`, so this is a benign false positive. No other alt-text failures were reported in the visible portion.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The `__dU__=` parameter on links is a resolved per-recipient identifier.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these. This is unconfirmed, so verify against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, so alignment needs checking on the raw message.
- **Footer not visible:** the physical address and in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was included in the probed links, but this can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

A full redirect-chain capture is needed to check UTM persistence and landing alignment with the "Cyber Week" / "STACK. SAVE. ENJOY." offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text MIME part** (this is the only confirmed failure).
2. **Verify headers on the raw message** (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) before treating the compliance warnings as real defects. Capture them from a non-relay inbox, or fix the relay's header passthrough.

4. **Make the 700px wrapper fluid** (`width:100%; max-width:700px`, and drop `min-width:700px`).
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
6. **Add `width="1" height="1" alt=""` to the tracking pixel.**
7. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML** and the redirect chain, so the footer, UTM parameters and the 40 skipped links can be checked.
