# 50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the second lip-discount subject line from Sephora in two days, and it repeats the exact same structural problem flagged in the July 26 send ("Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves"): the subject promises "lip faves," the hero banner delivers a "50% off Select Sephora Collection" National Lip Day markdown — and then the email immediately abandons lip products entirely for a "Just Landed at Sephora" fragrance launch (RUHVEDA, INFLUIXIOUS) and a New Arrivals grid that's mostly blush, body butter, hair mist, BB cream, and a $105 eau de parfum. Only two of the eight grid items (Urban Decay lip liner, Fenty gloss stick) are actually lip products.
- The top banner itself is clean and well-executed — bold offer, "Today only" urgency, product thumbnails, one clear CTA — so anyone who acts in the first three seconds gets a good experience. But the email as a whole is really a fragrance-and-new-arrivals send wearing a lip-deal subject line to drive opens, which is the same bait-and-switch pattern this brand has now run twice in two days.
- Judgement: solid top-of-funnel execution undercut by a subject line that doesn't match 80% of the body. Functional, not great — cadence fatigue and honesty issues cap it below the brand's best sends (e.g. the 7/24 sunscreen launch or 7/25 save-the-date, both 9/10).

## What's working

- Hero banner leads with a specific, high-contrast offer (50% off, "Today only") and a single unambiguous "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- National Lip Day framing gives the discount a credible occasion rather than a generic markdown.
- Loyalty mechanics are visible and layered — Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar plus a separate "4X points on Patrick Ta" callout with a promo code.
- Render is clean throughout: no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent Sephora black/white/pink branding.
- Product grid includes useful shorthand tags ("NEW," "Online Only," "Clean") that aid quick scanning.

## What's weak

- Subject says "lip faves" (plural, broad); the actual lip content is one banner plus two products in an eight-item mixed grid — a repeat of the exact bait-and-switch called out in the 7/26 send.
- No human models anywhere — every image is a product flat-lay, so there's no visible signal this is targeted to any particular demographic.
- The "SHOP NOW" CTA is reused four separate times with identical generic copy — no CTA is specific to what it's actually linking to (lips vs. fragrance vs. new arrivals).
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the render.
- This is the second near-identical "lip discount" subject in two consecutive days, which reads as promo fatigue rather than a fresh event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the dominant content — either lead with the fragrance launch (which gets more real estate) or restructure the body to actually be lip-heavy if "lip faves" is the hook. Right now the subject buys the open and the body doesn't pay it off.
- 2. Differentiate the CTA copy per section ("Shop Lip Deals," "Shop New Fragrance," "Shop New Arrivals") instead of four identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 3. Space out lip-discount sends — two lip-led subjects in 48 hours trains subscribers to skim past the next one.
- 4. Add at least one specific hero product image with a price/size callout near the top banner rather than only a collection-level graphic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New fragrance just landed + 50% off lip faves 🪩`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 50% off Sephora Collection lip picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: meet RUHVEDA and INFLUIXIOUS, our newest Black-owned fragrance brands.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `National Lip Day markdowns end tonight — shop before they're gone.`
