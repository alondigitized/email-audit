# 10% off our most-searched beauty 🛍️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "10% off our most-searched beauty." The body leads with "NEW AND ONLY AT SEPHORA," a launch feature for Kayali Yum Vanilla Cupcake. The 10% Insider medallion (11/3–11/9) is visible in the header, but the hero is a new-arrivals story. The subject says "most-searched" and the body says "new and only," so the two don't match.
- The header is busy. A Parachute gift-with-purchase banner, a "DON'T MISS OUT" bar, the Insider 10% medallion, a Same-Day Delivery P.S. and the codes ALONSALE and UNREADY all sit above the hero. A reader has to work out which offer applies. The product grid, "Spot it. Shop it." tiles, a Savings Event hair-tools banner and a gift guide follow. The email is long and reads as a catalog with a discount attached.
- Verdict: the Kayali hero is strong, the offer is real, and the brand is trusted. The offer stacking and the subject/body mismatch keep it at an average send. This is the fifth near-identical 10% send in a week, after 11/3 and 11/4, so the cadence is wearing thin.

## What's working

- The Kayali hero is the clearest moment: a big bottle photo, a price range ($29–$150) and a "SHOP NOW" button.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" labels give each grid item a reason to exist beyond the discount.
- The product grid shows individual items with names and prices, so the shopper can see specifics.
- Clean-badge callouts add useful product detail.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." tiles for bestselling perfume and top-rated foundations add some social-proof language.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "most-searched" beauty, but the body is about new and exclusive launches. The expectation doesn't match.
- The header stacks several offers: the Parachute GWP, 10% off, two codes and a same-day-delivery P.S. They compete for attention.
- The 10% off medallion is small and sits under other banners. The hero doesn't carry the discount.
- The email is very long, with many products and sections. The Savings Event hair-tools banner and the gift guide dilute focus.
- The fine print is dense, and the in-store barcode takes up space at the bottom. The offer feels more complicated than "10% off."

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero agree. Either lead with the discount on the most-searched items, or lead with the launch and change the subject.
- 2. Cut the header to one offer. Pick 10% off or the Parachute GWP, and move the other lower.
- 3. Put the Insider 10% and the code in a single bold bar above the Kayali hero.
- 4. Trim the grid to 4–6 products and drop one of the secondary banners. The 11/3, 11/4 and 11/5 sends are near-identical, so vary the creative.
- 5. Pull the fine print into a short summary with a link, and keep the barcode but make it smaller.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 700px fixed-width XHTML layout with redirect-wrapped tracking links on `app.sephora.com`. The one hard failure is the missing plain-text part (0 chars). The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay probably strips those headers. The supplied source is truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect-wrapped links.** The visible links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ac4a4224ae4fbc369fb45280/…?__dU__=…`, which is a first-party click-tracking redirect. QA probed 12 of 52 links and skipped 40, so the final destination of most links is unverified.
- **Open pixel.** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It uses single-quoted attributes and has no `width`, `height` or `alt`. This is functional but fragile, and it is the first of the QA "missing alt" warnings.
- **Empty offer data.** The JSON-LD block contains `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type. Most clients ignore or strip JSON-LD, so this has no practical effect, but the markup is incomplete.
- No broken-link failures were reported among the probed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - the tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…`), which is harmless because it is hidden
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_14.jpg`
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_15.jpg`

  The last two are `images.harmony.epsilon.com` content images. If they carry text or links, images-off clients and screen readers lose that content.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed width.** The wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The 480px media query only resizes specific classes, so clients that ignore `@media` may scroll horizontally.
- **Malformed CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid in that position, so the rule may be dropped or may break the parsing of the rules after it.
- **Invalid meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is minor.
