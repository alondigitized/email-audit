# ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🫣
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third "closing urgency" beat in the Major Summer Deals campaign — the June 22 email already said the "chapter is closing," and now June 23 says "ENDS TODAY." In isolation the subject line is clean and effective. Against the cadence backdrop, the urgency is spent before the email opens. Subscribers who read yesterday's closing-urgency send have already processed this deadline once; the second final buzzer trains them to ignore the third.
- The email itself is technically competent at the hero level. The 50% and 25% tiers are both legible immediately, and the BeautyINSIDER bonus-points callout (the "500" points stack) is a genuine incremental value add that wasn't in the June 16 version of this same offer. The breakdown is scope: below the sale hero Sephora has loaded a Just Arrived section, a Merit brand feature, an "If You Love It" cross-sell, a BeautyINSIDER loyalty pitch, and a brand story footer. That's the same digest-bloat problem that tanked the June 20 email to a 5/10. This send avoids that floor because the primary message IS the sale and it reads clearly above the fold — but the execution muddies progressively as you scroll.
- Clear judgment: effective hero, undermined by urgency fatigue and digest sprawl.

## What's working

- **Dual-tier discount is immediately legible**: "50%" and "25%" are rendered in large, distinct type with paired SHOP NOW buttons — no ambiguity about what the offer is.
- **BeautyINSIDER 500-point stack**: The bonus points callout adds genuine incremental value for loyalty members beyond the base sale price, differentiating this from the June 16 send.
- **Beauty Insiders Get More section**: Bonus points AND same-day delivery are called out explicitly, giving loyalty-tier subscribers a concrete reason to act now rather than wait.
- **Product grid with visible ratings**: "Save on Must-Haves Like These" grounds the abstract discount in browsable products with star ratings visible, which aids click intent.

## What's weak

- **Urgency is spent**: June 22 already said the chapter was "closing ⏲️" — a same-discount "ENDS TODAY" follow-up the very next day is a second final buzzer and erodes credibility for future closing campaigns.
- **Digest bloat undercuts the sale focus**: Just Arrived, Merit brand spotlight, "If You Love It" cross-sell, BeautyINSIDER loyalty pitch, and a brand story footer all stack below the hero. Five emails in one send.
- **Every CTA reads "SHOP NOW"**: No category-specific copy anywhere — a subscriber who only wants skincare deals has no directional shortcut and must browse from a catch-all sale landing page.
- **ALL CAPS "ENDS TODAY"** in the subject is a mild spam-signal flag and tonally jarring compared to the softer "chapter is closing" framing Sephora used yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the digest sections for this one send**: Drop Just Arrived, Merit feature, and the brand story. This is an ENDS TODAY urgency email — every section below the sale hero competes with the call to action and bleeds urgency.
- 2. **Add a countdown timer under the 50% badge**: "ENDS TODAY" as static text is weak. Hours:minutes remaining converts the abstract deadline into a felt, ticking constraint.
- 3. **Make CTA copy category-specific**: Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Skincare Deals," "Shop Makeup Deals," "Shop Hair Deals" — subscribers should be able to enter their preferred category without a browse-everything landing page.
- 4. **Don't repeat the closing urgency beat**: The June 22 "chapter is closing" send should be the last urgency email in this campaign run. Running it again June 23 devalues future deadline language.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last hours: 50% off select beauty ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your sale + 500 bonus points — both end today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your BeautyINSIDER points before this closes at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `50% off skincare, makeup, hair — shop before it's gone.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** blocks pinch-to-zoom on iOS. This prevents visually impaired users from magnifying content and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). The constraint is unnecessary for modern responsive email layouts.
  ```html
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally** via the catch-all selector `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This suppresses OS-level font scaling for users who rely on system accessibility text size settings.

- **`robots` meta typo** — `content="no index"` (space) is non-standard; the correct value is `noindex`. Irrelevant to deliverability but indicates template hygiene issue.
  ```html
  <meta name="robots" content="no index" />
  ```

- **Duplicate `display` declarations** in `.show` and `.appear` rules — `display: block!important` followed immediately by `display: table!important` in the same ruleset. The block declaration is dead code and signals a copy-paste artifact.

- **No `lang` attribute** visible on `<html>` tag (XHTML namespace only). Screen readers use `lang` for correct pronunciation. Cannot confirm absence vs. truncation.

- **`alt` text on images** — cannot audit from truncated source.
