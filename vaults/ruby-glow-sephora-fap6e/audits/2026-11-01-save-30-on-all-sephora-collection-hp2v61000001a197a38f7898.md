# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises one thing and the body delivers another. "Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection" is the clear offer, and it sits in a thin banner at the top: a gold "30% OFF" medallion, dates 10/30–11/9, and the code ALONSALE. Directly under it, a giant "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" hero takes over and pulls the eye away from the sale. From there the email becomes a long holiday catalog covering Jo Malone, Merit, Dae, Gisou, L'Occitane, Byredo and Tower 28, with no Sephora Collection product shown at all.
- This is the third 30% Sephora Collection send in as many days, after "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection" (10/30, the strongest of the run) and "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍" (10/31). The 10/30 send led with one clear offer. This one buries the offer under a gift guide, much like the 10/31 "personal holiday shopper" send. A subscriber would likely read it as the same sale with more clutter.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand holiday catalog, but it is a weaker version of the 10/30 send. The offer has no product to hang on, so the 30% banner gets ignored.

## What's working

- The 30% banner is unmistakable at the very top, with a code and a credible date window (10/30–11/9).
- Product tiles show names, prices and strike-through values, such as "$68 ($92 value)", which makes the deals concrete.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" labels add exclusivity and give the grid some scannability.
- The scannable barcodes at the bottom (30% for Rouge/VIB, 10% for Insider from 11/3–11/9) turn the email into a usable in-store coupon.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" hero outshouts the actual offer, so the visual hierarchy contradicts the subject line.
- No Sephora Collection product appears anywhere, so the "ALL Sephora Collection" promise has nothing to click.
- The email is very long, with at least six distinct modules (Jo Malone, product grid, Byredo/lip sets, skincare sets, Holiday Gift Guide, service strip). The eye has no single destination.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, and the 30% vs 10% tier split is only explained at the very bottom.
- This is the third near-identical 30% send in three days, which makes the urgency feel stale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the 30% offer. Show a Sephora Collection product shot with the medallion and one button, "Shop Sephora Collection". Move the gift guide below it.
- 2. Cut the body to one hero plus one grid of 6–8 Sephora Collection items, with the sale price shown on each tile.
- 3. State the tiers up top, for example "30% Rouge/VIB · 10% Insider", instead of leaving them in the footer.
- 4. Space out the sends. Make the next one a last-days message with a real deadline rather than a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 30% off Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 30% code ALONSALE ends 11/9`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lip, face & brush bestsellers, all 30% off through 11/9. Use code ALONSALE.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus gift sets from Jo Malone, Merit & Gisou to start your holiday list.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block has no override for `.tbl` or `.tbl_cntr`. The truncated source may contain one later, but if not, this forces horizontal scroll on mobile.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Invalid CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments there are not valid and may break the following rules in some parsers. The rules affected are `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and the ones after it.
- **Alt text (QA warnings):** Both images missing `alt` are tracking pixels (`/O/AQEAAZcF…` and `o.gif`). The first is `display:none`, so the practical impact is low. Add `alt=""` to both.
- **Minor markup:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
  - The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip it, and it adds no value.
