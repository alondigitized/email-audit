# From your feed to your cart 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- The subject and the body don't match. "From your feed to your cart 🤩" promises a trending, social-media-driven edit. The strongest content, the hair offer with the same-day delivery code, is what the subject leaves out.
- This is the same pattern as 2026-09-28 ("Stop scrolling: up to 40% off select Living Proof"), where the hero didn't match the subject. The cleanest recent send was 2026-09-23 ("Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care", 8/10). The sender name here is "Sephora Daily Deals" and the top banner is a hair deal. Yet the subject doesn't mention the deal. It reads as a generic brand line on a deals-branded send.
- Judgement: a competent Sephora digest with a real offer and good product density, but a soft subject and no single story. It's mid-pack, better than the Living Proof mismatch and worse than the Hair Daily Deals send.

## What's working

- The top banner is a concrete offer: "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" with "TODAY ONLY" and a SHOP NOW button, plus a same-day delivery code (DELIVERED, ends 10/7).
- The Cult Gaia "NOW AT SEPHORA" block is a real launch, with named products and prices ($28–$48).
- The product grid shows price, brand, and badges ("Only at Sephora", "Clean", "Limited Edition", "Online only"). That helps skimming and decisions.
- Beauty Insider is visible near the top ("You have 0 points") and again near the bottom, with a Rewards Bazaar link.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlaps.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a "feed to cart" social edit and doesn't mention the hair deal or the 50% off, which are the strongest hooks.
- There are too many competing SHOP NOW buttons: hair, Social Stars, Pay Later, value-size hair care, Korean skincare. The eye has no single path.
- The large "SOCIAL STARS" and Cult Gaia hero takes a lot of vertical space with no price or button until well below it. The second banner also has no offer.
- The offers overlap and conflict. "Up to 50% off" and "20% off value-size" are two hair deals with different footnotes. "Today only" sits beside an "ends 10/7" delivery code.
- Heavy footnote markers (†, §) and a very long legal block make the deal feel conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to lead with the hair deal, since that's what the top of the email actually sells and it matches the "Daily Deals" sender name.
- 2. Pick one hero. Put the hair offer and CTA first. Move the Cult Gaia and social edit into a smaller module further down.
- 3. Cut the section count to three: hair deal, one launch, one product grid. Drop the Pay Later and Korean skincare blocks, or send them separately.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop hair deals" or "Shop Cult Gaia" instead of a string of SHOP NOWs.
- 5. Make the deadline consistent. Use "today only" for the deal and put the delivery code in one place, so the two clocks don't conflict.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...`. This is most likely the tracking pixel, so it is low severity. It should still carry `alt=""`.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, an Epsilon-hosted navigation header image.
  - `20260929_Social_stars_hed_07.jpg`, a heading image. With images blocked, the heading disappears or screen readers read out the filename.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`, so iOS text scaling is off.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients and is harmless. It is a code-hygiene issue, and the same tag would be ignored if the email were hosted as a web view.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message. This matters little in email but shows up in web-view and archive contexts.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars, so there is no multipart/alternative fallback. This affects text-only clients, some accessibility tooling and spam scoring. It may be a relay capture artifact, but treat it as a real gap until a raw-source check says otherwise.
