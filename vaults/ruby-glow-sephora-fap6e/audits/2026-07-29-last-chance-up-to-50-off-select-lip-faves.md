# LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "up to 50% off lip" send from Sephora in eight days, and it still hasn't fixed the math problem flagged on 7/26, 7/27, and 7/29. The hero banner promises "Get up to 50% off Select Lip Picks," but scanning all eight itemized products, the best actual discount is the MAC lipstick at 40% off ($25→$15) — nothing in the grid touches 50%. The "LAST CHANCE ‼️" framing adds false urgency to a discount that was never really on the table.
- Visually the email is clean: consistent pink-toned hero, a tidy 2x4 product grid with clear strikethrough pricing, and legible CTAs throughout. The problem isn't execution, it's honesty — this brand has now run the identical "up to 50%" headline against a sub-40% actual sale four times in a row, and a subscriber who opened even one of the prior sends will recognize the pattern immediately.
- Judgement: solid production, dishonest headline, and now a credibility problem from repetition. This should score well on craft but the bait-and-switch and stale "last chance" urgency drag it down hard on trust.

## What's working

- Clean 2-column product grid with product photo, name, and clear strikethrough pricing (original vs. sale) for all 8 items.
- "CLEAN" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays give ingredient-conscious shoppers a fast visual filter.
- Secondary modules ("Just In From Dae," "$35 and Under," "Buy More, Save More") add legitimate additional value beyond the lip sale, giving multiple entry points.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is visible and reinforces loyalty-program relevance.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, layout is not broken.

## What's weak

- Headline promises "up to 50% off" but the highest actual discount in the grid is 40% (MAC), most items are 20-30% off — the same bait-and-switch structure called out in the 7/26, 7/27, and 7/29 sends.
- Subject line uses "LAST CHANCE ‼️" urgency on a sale that, per brand history, has just been re-run under near-identical "up to 50% lip" framing multiple times this week — the urgency reads as recycled, not credible.
- No visible end date/time or countdown anywhere in the hero or product grid to back up "last chance" — the only date shown ("STARTS 7/31") is for an unrelated Buy More Save More promo.
- Double exclamation-adjacent punctuation ("‼️") plus "LAST CHANCE" in caps is a mild spam signal in the subject line.
- This is the brand's fourth near-identical lip-discount send in eight days — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who's seen the 7/26, 7/27, or 7/29 versions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline math: either genuinely discount one hero item to 50% and badge it "up to 50% off" honestly, or change the headline to "up to 40% off" to match the MAC lipstick, which is the actual top discount.
- 2. Add a real deadline element (date + time, or a countdown bar) directly under the hero if "LAST CHANCE" is going to be used — right now nothing in the render substantiates urgency.
- 3. Since this is the fourth lip-sale send this week, differentiate the creative — lead with a different category or bundle offer to avoid subscriber fatigue from repetitive "up to 50%" headlines.
- 4. Move the MAC lipstick (the actual best deal) into the hero image/copy so the promise and the proof are in the same glance.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: MAC lipstick 40% off + more lip deals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last shot at lip sale prices — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Prices go back up tomorrow — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `8 lip faves still marked down, from $15.`
