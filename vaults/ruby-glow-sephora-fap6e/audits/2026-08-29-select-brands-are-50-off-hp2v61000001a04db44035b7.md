# Select brands are 50% off 🤑
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a bait-and-switch email. The subject promises "50% off select brands" and the hero banner even says "TODAY ONLY" — but scroll past that top banner and the entire rest of the email is a "Real Luxury" prestige catalog (YSL, Caudalie, Jo Malone, Kérastase) with zero discount pricing attached to any of it. The 50% off applies narrowly to "Too Faced, Drunk Elephant and MAC Cosmetics" per the fine print, none of which appear anywhere in the body.
- That disconnect is the whole story here. A subscriber clicks in expecting a deal-drop on brands they can browse, and instead gets a moodboard of full-price luxury SKUs with prices like $195 and $199 sitting right under a "50% off" banner — that's a trust problem, not just a layout one. Compare this to the 8/28 "50% off select beauty" send (6.00/10) which actually led with the discounted category throughout; this one only nods to the deal in the top banner and then abandons it completely.
- Judgement: skip. The subject line will pull opens, but the body actively undersells its own promo and risks feeling deceptive once a shopper realizes the products shown aren't discounted.

## What's working

- Hero banner is bold and legible — pink circle badge with "50% off Select Beauty" and a lipstick product shot is a clean scroll-stopper.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency badge is visually distinct (white circle, black border) and sits right in the hero.
- Section structure (Makeup → Skincare → Fragrance → Hair Care) is easy to scan with consistent color-blocked backgrounds per category.
- "NEW," "CLEAN + PLANET AWARE," and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges give each product a specific reason to care, not just a name.
- No render bugs — images, type, and color blocks all render cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.

## What's weak

- The 50% off deal referenced in the subject and hero never reappears — every product below shows full price ($46, $99–$119, $195, $32–$62, $78).
- Brands actually included in the deal (Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, MAC) are never shown in the body — a shopper has to hunt or guess.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency is undercut by fine print saying "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" — classic buried caveat.
- The email pivots into an unrelated "Buy Now Pay Later" (Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal) block and then a rhode cross-promo, diluting the one call-to-action a 50%-off subject should be driving toward.
- Primary CTA is diffuse — six different "Shop Now"/"Shop the Icons" buttons across sections instead of one clear path to the actual discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the discounted brands (Too Faced, Drunk Elephant, MAC) immediately below the hero with before/after pricing — the subject sold a deal, the first fold after the hero should deliver it, not "Real Luxury."
- 2. Cut the luxury catalog scroll from this send entirely, or move it to a separate non-deal email — mixing a "50% off today only" hero with $195 full-price fragrance in the same body actively contradicts the offer.
- 3. Replace the vague fine print with a visible, honest exclusion line near the hero itself (e.g., "Valid on Too Faced, Drunk Elephant & MAC only") so shoppers aren't misled after clicking.
- 4. Consolidate to one primary CTA button tied to the actual sale category; keep BNPL/rhode content, if kept at all, below a clear divider so it doesn't compete with the deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Too Faced, Drunk Elephant & MAC`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick starts now 🤑`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Too Faced, Drunk Elephant & MAC — half off, today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before this one's gone tonight.`
