# SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day 💄
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's third "up to 50% off lip" send in five days, and the pattern from the July 26 and July 27 emails repeats almost exactly: the headline promises "up to 50% off," but the actual itemized deals top out at 40% (MAC lipstick, $25→$15). Everything else in the eight-product grid clusters between 20-30% off, and there's a tiny superscript "a" hanging off the "50%" in the hero — a visual tell that a footnote is doing damage control the headline copy won't do itself.
- To Sephora's credit, this send is more transparent than the July 26 one-item bait-and-switch — it shows eight real products with real strikethrough pricing instead of hiding the math behind a single vague claim. But the cadence problem is now the bigger issue: three near-identical "lip discount" subject lines in less than a week (7/26 "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves," 7/27 "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves," 7/29 "up to 50% off this National Lip Day") reads as one campaign wearing three outfits, and a subscriber who opened the last two will recognize this instantly.
- Clean render, clear hierarchy, loyalty tier visible — the production quality is solid. The offer honesty and promo fatigue are what drag this down from a top-tier send.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: pink gradient, large "Get up to 50% off Select Lip Picks" headline, single "SHOP NOW" button — nothing competes for attention above the fold.
- The product grid shows real, itemized before/after pricing on eight SKUs instead of one representative item, which is more transparent than recent sends from this brand.
- No render bugs — all ten product/promo images loaded cleanly, no overlapping text or broken tiles.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is visible near the footer, reinforcing loyalty relevance.
- The secondary "Buy More, Save More" module has a real, credible date range (starts 7/31, ends 8/2), which is more disciplined than the vague urgency in the hero.

## What's weak

- Headline math doesn't check out: nothing in the grid hits 50% off — the best deal is 40% (MAC, $25→$15), most items land at 20-30%. That's the same bait-and-switch shape flagged in the 7/26 send.
- Third "up to 50% off lip" subject line in five days (7/26, 7/27, now 7/29) — repetitive cadence a subscriber will notice and start tuning out.
- "SHOP NOW" is used as the CTA four separate times (hero, two Spot It blocks, Buy More module) with zero differentiation — none of them tell you what you're shopping for.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." block cross-sells Dae haircare, which has nothing to do with National Lip Day and dilutes an otherwise focused send.
- No visible deadline on the primary lip sale itself — only the unrelated Buy More promo has a hard end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline math — cap the claim at what's actually true ("up to 40% off") or restructure the grid so at least one hero item genuinely hits 50%. This is the single highest-impact fix; repeated inflated "up to X%" claims erode trust with a subscriber who's now seen it three times in a week.
- 2. Break the cadence — after three lip-discount sends in five days, the next send should rotate to a different category entirely, or consolidate into one definitive "Lip Day" event email instead of three near-duplicates.
- 3. Differentiate the CTAs — swap generic "SHOP NOW" for "Shop Lip Deals," "Shop Dae," "Build Your Basket" so each block's action is self-evident without reading the surrounding copy.
- 4. Cut or relocate the Dae haircare cross-sell — it breaks the lip-day narrative; if it must stay, push it further down past a stronger lip-specific block (e.g., a "bestsellers" or "reviews" module).
- 5. Add a visible deadline to the hero offer itself, not just the secondary Buy More promo.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Lip Day deals: MAC, Charlotte Tilbury & more, up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `8 lip must-haves marked down — shop before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From matte to gloss: today's best lip deals, all in one place`
