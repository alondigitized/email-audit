# Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands 💸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Same Sephora "Stacking Weekend" chassis this week's brand history has flagged five times running, now wearing a new subject line: "Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands." The email opens with "SEPHORA Stacking Weekend — Hurry! Use code SEPTBONUS to earn 500 bonus points with $75+ spend," then pivots immediately into "App Fun Starts Now," a Hourglass product drop, six "Discover the Deals" tiles, and only *then*, deep past the fold, a "50% off Select Beauty" box marked "TODAY ONLY." The subject says "50% off select brands" but the thing that actually gets top billing is points-stacking and an app-download push — the same bait-and-switch structure this brand has run all week (9/5 through 9/9).
- The layout itself renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text — and the Hourglass hero shot and product grid are well-produced. But the offer hierarchy is backwards: a subscriber opening for "50% off" has to scroll past a bonus-points banner, an app ad, and a six-item new-arrivals grid before finding the thing they were promised.
- Judgement: competently built, poorly prioritized. This is a 5/10 send that would be a 7-8 if the 50% off box were the hero instead of buried mid-page.

## What's working

- Hourglass "Ambient Lighting Edit" hero image is high-quality and gives a clear single-product focus with an "24 HOURS — APP EARLY ACCESS" urgency hook.
- The Beauty Insider points bar (500 bonus points, "TODAY ONLY") is visually distinct and gives a concrete, honest number.
- Product grid ("Discover the Deals and Drops Coming Soon") uses clear NEW/LIMITED EDITION/ONLINE ONLY badges and legible pricing.
- No visual rendering defects — colors, fonts, and images all display correctly.
- Footer barcode/in-store redemption code (SEPTBONUS) is a nice cross-channel touch for in-store shoppers.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "50% off select brands" but the 50% off box is roughly two-thirds of the way down the email, after Stacking Weekend, the app push, and the six-item product grid.
- This is the same Stacking Weekend / points-plus-percentage structure scored 5-7/10 across 9/5 through 9/9 — no creative evolution, just a new subject line on a recycled layout.
- "App Fun Starts Now" and "24 HOURS — APP EARLY ACCESS" compete with the 50% offer for primary attention, diluting the CTA the subject actually sold.
- The 50% off box itself is small relative to its promised importance — it's a modest banner sandwiched between "Spot it. Shop it." and the Korean Hair Care section, not a hero.
- Below "New Sephora Collection" the email sprawls into four more distinct offers (point of view devices, gift ideas, Same-Day Unlimited) — cadence fatigue for a subscriber who's seen a near-identical promo stack daily this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 50% off Select Beauty box to the very top, above Stacking Weekend — if the subject leads with the discount, the email must open with it.
- 2. Cut or shrink the app-download and Hourglass sections to a single secondary block below the main offer, not competing above it.
- 3. Reduce the number of distinct offers in one send (Stacking Weekend, App Fun, Hourglass, 50% off, Korean Hair Care, point of view, Gift Ideas, Same-Day Unlimited is at least seven asks) — pick one hero offer per send.
- 4. Enlarge and visually differentiate the 50% off box so it reads as the headline creative, not a mid-page banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off select beauty — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick is waiting (ends tonight)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the exact brands included before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: new Hourglass drop, 24-hour app early access.`
