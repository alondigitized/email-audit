# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This one actually does what the subject says: "50% off select faves" opens straight into a "50% off Select Beauty" hero box naming Clarins, Estée Lauder, and OUAI, with "TODAY ONLY" stamped right on it. It's the same pink/orange gradient chassis this brand has run all week, but at least the top-of-email promise and the visible content line up for once.
- The problem is everything below the fold. After the 50% hero, the email pivots hard into "Hot and Trending" — a full-bleed Kilian Paris fragrance launch (Cocktails Collection, $37–$150) that has nothing to do with the 50%-off deal, followed by a long new-arrivals grid (Saie, Laneige, Gisou, Nars, Josie Maran, Armani, Touchland) at full price. The 50% off narrative that got the open never returns — no reminder banner, no "don't forget your 50% off" nudge before the email closes out with kits and holiday teasers.
- Net: strong open-to-hero match, but the email abandons its own offer two screens in and turns into an unrelated product catalog. Middling send — better than most of this week's bait-and-switch versions, but still not a coherent single-offer email.

## What's working

- Hero banner directly matches the subject: "50% off Select Beauty" with brand names (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) visible.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency badge is clearly legible and placed right in the hero.
- Clear "SHOP NOW" CTA button is present immediately below the hero, no scrolling needed.
- Product grid has real prices, "NEW" and "Only At Sephora" tags, and clean square product photography — no render bugs or broken images.
- Sender/brand is instantly recognizable (Sephora logo top-left, Beauty Insider branding throughout).

## What's weak

- The 50% off offer disappears after the hero — the entire rest of the email (Kilian launch, product grid, kits, holiday drops) is full-price content with no offer reinforcement.
- "Hot and Trending" Kilian Paris section dominates the most visual real estate but is a $37-$150 new fragrance launch, unrelated to the deal that earned the open.
- No visible list of which "select beauty" products are actually included in the 50% off — the hero names three brands but the grid below doesn't tag any item as discounted.
- Email runs very long (7+ distinct sections: hero, Kilian launch, 8-item grid, kits, curls, holiday drops, footer) with the actual offer only living in the first screen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Reinforce the 50% off offer at least once more mid-email (e.g., a repeated banner before or after the Kilian section) so it's not abandoned after one screen — this is the single biggest lift available since the hero-to-body mismatch is what's suppressing this brand's scores all week.
- 2. Tag which grid items qualify for the 50% off, or add a "Shop the sale" filter link near the product grid instead of leaving it as an untagged new-arrivals catalog.
- 3. Move or trim the Kilian Paris launch — it's beautiful creative but works against the "today only" urgency of the actual subject line.
- 4. Add specific product photography or a name inside the hero itself (currently just a bottle silhouette) to make the 50% off feel concrete rather than generic.
