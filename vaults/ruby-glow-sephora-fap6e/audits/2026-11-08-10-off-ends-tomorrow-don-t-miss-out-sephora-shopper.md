# 10% off ends TOMORROW ⚠️Don’t miss out, Sephora Shopper!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "10% off ends TOMORROW," but the body opens on a free same-day delivery banner. Below it sits a "FINAL HOURS!" countdown (41 hours, 3 minutes, 31 seconds) and two medallions: 30% off (10/30–11/9) and 10% off for Insider members (11/3–11/9). So the deadline claim is true, and it is backed by a live timer. The email still buries it under three competing offers: delivery, 30%, and 10%.
- The hero then switches to "Holiday Value Sets" and a grid of new limited-edition items (Summer Fridays, Charlotte Tilbury, LANEIGE, Makeup by Mario). The urgency in the header and the merchandising below it don't line up. A free-gift module (Parachute headband and washcloth set at $100) adds a fourth offer. The email is long and busy, and the one message the subject set up, "10% ends tomorrow," never gets its own moment.
- Judgement: a credible urgency send with a strong countdown, weakened by offer clutter and a hero that doesn't match the subject. Average execution.

## What's working

- The "FINAL HOURS!" countdown timer makes the deadline concrete and believable.
- The subject's urgency matches the 11/9 end date shown on the medallions.
- The Sephora wordmark and Savings Event header are instantly recognizable.
- Promo codes (SPEEDY, ALLONSALE, UNREAD) are shown plainly in the header and gift module.
- The product grid shows named brands with prices ($18.20, $22, $24, $25), which gives the shopper concrete items to consider.

## What's weak

- Four offers compete at the top (delivery, 30%, 10%, free gift), so no single offer leads.
- The hero, "Holiday Value Sets," ignores the "10% off ends tomorrow" message from the subject.
- The 30% and 10% medallions are small, and the fine print under them is hard to read, so it's unclear who qualifies for which.
- The email runs very long, with many sections (Spot It. Shop It., Fragrance, Gift Guide), which dilutes the urgency.
- The product grid is a lot of small tiles with only one "SHOP NOW" button in the hero, so there is no clear per-product action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 10% Insider deadline the hero. Put the countdown directly beside a single large "10% off ends tomorrow" panel, and move the delivery and free-gift offers lower down.
- 2. Cut the body to one product module of 6 to 8 items plus a gift guide, and drop the Spot It. Shop It. and Fragrance blocks.
- 3. Replace "SHOP NOW" with a specific CTA such as "Shop value sets before 10% ends."
- 4. Spell out eligibility for the 10% and 30% medallions in plain text next to each.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 10% Insider savings ends tomorrow ⏰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 10% off + free same-day delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Holiday value sets and new limited editions, still included. Use code ALLONSALE.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a missing plain-text alternative (0 chars, the one QA FAIL) and a malformed `@media` block that may drop some mobile rules. Header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay. The visible HTML shows no unrendered merge tokens.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Links use the ESP redirect wrapper `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1bba7d5bb96a4c8f4bbc782e8/...?__dU__=...`. Only 12 of 47 links were probed and 35 were skipped (QA WARN). The QA output gives no per-link status, so I can't confirm the destinations resolve.
- There are two tracking pixels:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider` appears to be a second pixel. Both are standard, but two separate tracking domains on one send is worth knowing about.
- The `mi.sephora.com` pixel URL carries a user identifier (`mi_u=4970293024993920`) in the query string. That is normal for tracking, but it means the URL is per-recipient.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** both tracking pixels (`app.sephora.com/O/...` and `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif`) have no `alt`. They are hidden, so screen-reader impact is low. The first is hidden inline. The second's hiding wasn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Malformed `@media` block:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` sits inside `@media only screen and (max-width:480px){...}`. Browsers parse the `<!--[if mso]>` and `<![endif]-->` tokens as part of the next selectors. That likely invalidates the `img.mso-size` rule and the following `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule in non-Outlook clients. Conditional comments belong outside the media query.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper table is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. On viewports under 700px this forces horizontal scroll unless the client rescales. The `max-width:480px` media rules don't override `min-width` on the table.
- **Missing text fallback:** no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, and `<title>` is only "Sephora".
- **Minor:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email.
- **Minor:** the JSON-LD block includes `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard`. The empty code is not valid structured data, and most clients strip `<script>` anyway.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source.
- The subject ends "Sephora Shopper!". That reads like a fallback value for a missing first name, not a real name. The send may be using a default for this recipient. I can't confirm this from the source.
- `"discountCode": ""` in the JSON-LD is an empty dynamic field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** QA notes this may be a relay capture artifact. This is unconfirmed, not a verified sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN):** same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this should be verified against raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `beauty.sephora.com` and the link domain `app.sephora.com` differ in subdomain only.
- **Footer (unsubscribe link, physical address):** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify them. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set but reports no result for it.
- **Plain-text version (FAIL):** 0 chars. A multipart/alternative text part is missing or wasn't captured. Some spam filters score HTML-only mail negatively.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are hidden behind the redirect wrapper and truncated, so I can't verify UTM parameters or the landing page.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider` on the pixel, which is dated today and includes "Value_Sets_Insider".
- The preheader says "30% off ALL Sephora Collection", while the subject says "10% off". The landing page for each offer could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a text/plain part. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query, or wrap it in its own `<style>`. Then verify the mobile rules apply.

4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and add `lang` to `<html>`.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment), as the relay hid them.
6. Raise the link-probe limit to cover the 35 skipped links, or at least all `app.sephora.com/T/` CTAs, and check UTMs on the final landing URLs.
7. Check the name fallback behavior for recipients without a first name.
