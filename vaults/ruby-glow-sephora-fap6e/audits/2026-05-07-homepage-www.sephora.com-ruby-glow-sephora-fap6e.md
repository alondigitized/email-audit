# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora is not playing around right now — the second I landed on this page, I was greeted with trendy product shots I actually recognize (REFY, Dior, m.ph), and copy that speaks my language. This feels like a beauty insider's home base pushed into Mother's Day gifting mode, which honestly still works for me because I'm absolutely buying myself something.

## What's working

- **"The internet's obsessed—and you're next."** — that line is doing heavy lifting. It's social-proof baked into hero copy, and it sounds like something a beauty editor would text me. I immediately want to see what the internet is actually buying.
- **Mother's Day banner with CODE FREESAME + specific deadline** — "Ends 5/10 at 1pm PT" is the kind of urgency I actually respect. Not a vague "limited time," a real clock.
- **Product selection in the hero carousel** — REFY, Dior, m.ph are legitimately buzzy right now. Whoever curated this knows the launch calendar.
- **"Makeup" tab is immediately visible in the category nav** — I can get to my category in one tap without hunting through a hamburger menu.
- **"AI Beauty" tab (NEW badge) in the bottom nav** — this is the kind of feature I would absolutely tap as a high-tech-comfort shopper.

## What's weak

- **No Beauty Insider Rouge recognition above the fold** — I'm a top-tier loyalty member and there's zero personalized signal for me. No points balance, no "Welcome back, Rouge," nothing. This landing should feel like VIP, not like I'm a first-time visitor.
- **No lifestyle imagery — just product stills on a lavender/white background** — the products look good but there's no aspirational human moment. A 29-year-old Millennial responds to a real skin look, not a product lineup on a pedestal.
- **Zero pricing or price-anchor anywhere above the fold** — I don't know if these foundations are $20 or $80 until I tap through. That friction matters when I'm in browse mode.
- **No ratings or "bestseller" badge on the hero products** — REFY and m.ph are still niche enough that a social proof signal (even "5,000+ reviews") would close the loop on the "internet is obsessed" claim.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Rouge/Beauty Insider balance chip to the header** — even a small "You have 3,200 points" callout would immediately make logged-in Rouge members feel seen and drive engagement with the rewards flow.
- **Swap one product still for a skin-on lifestyle shot** — show the foundation on an actual face (diverse shade range, dewy finish) so the "Buzzy Foundations" headline lands emotionally, not just informationally.
- **Surface a price anchor or member price in the hero** — something like "Starting at $42 · Rouge members get early access" would activate my loyalty status and give me a reason to tap vs. just scroll.
- **Add a "★ 4.8 / 12K reviews" badge to at least one featured product** — the copy claims the internet is obsessed; prove it with a number right on the card.
