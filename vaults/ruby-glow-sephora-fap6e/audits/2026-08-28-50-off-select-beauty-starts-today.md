# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's actual "50% off Today Only" deal-drop send, and it's the most direct, offer-forward email in this brand history — a real hero discount up top instead of another App Fest banner or catalog scroll. The "GET 50% OFF" hero with a black CTA button is unmissable, and the "TODAY ONLY" module immediately backs it up with three specific products and real strikethrough pricing (Clinique lipstick $25→$12.50, Lawless foundation $47→$23.50, Skinfix serum). That's a legitimate step up in clarity from the 8/24 App Fest send (6/10) and the 8/23 new-arrivals catalog (5/10).
- Where it falls apart is the "Deals Drop Daily" section right under it — Week 1/2/3 grids of unlabeled products with no names, prices, or discounts visible. It reads like an editorial teaser but sits directly beneath a hard 50%-off hero, which muddies what's actually on sale today versus what's coming "daily." Then the email pivots hard into unrelated modules (hair tools, Afterpay 10%, Olive Young K-beauty) that dilute the 50%-off narrative before it even lands.
- Net: strong hero, real numbers, but a bloated body that undercuts its own headline offer. **6/10** — better than this brand's recent catalog dumps, but still a scattergun after the first screen.

## What's working

- Hero band ("GET 50% OFF") is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss on open.
- "TODAY ONLY" module shows three actual products with names, brands, and real before/after pricing — first time in this brand history a deal send has this much concrete specificity up top.
- Primary "SHOP NOW" CTA button is clearly styled and repeated twice (hero + bottom of deals module).
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is present, giving loyalty-member context.
- Clean section breaks (hairline divider) separate the deal content from the secondary promos, so it doesn't fully blur into one wall.

## What's weak

- The Week 1/2/3 "Deals Drop Daily" grid shows product photos with zero names or prices — pure visual filler that adds scroll without adding information.
- Three unrelated promos (Dyson/BondiBoost hair tools, Afterpay 10% off, Olive Young K-beauty edit) crowd the bottom half, diluting the single 50%-off message the subject line promised.
- No visible urgency/countdown on the hero itself beyond "Today Only" text — feels static rather than time-pressured for a flash-style deal.
- The purple-to-blue gradient background across the deals section is busy and makes the product tiles harder to scan against.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or radically shrink the "Deals Drop Daily" Week 1/2/3 grid — either label each with product name + price, or drop it entirely and let the "TODAY ONLY" module carry the email. Unlabeled product photos are dead weight in a deal-focused send.
- 2. Move the Afterpay 10%/App Fest stack and Olive Young launch to a secondary "also happening" email or footer strip — they compete with, rather than support, the 50%-off headline.
- 3. Add a real countdown or explicit end-time ("Ends tonight at 11:59pm PT") to the hero to manufacture urgency that matches "Today Only."
- 4. Simplify the gradient background in the deals section to a flat white/cream so the product cards pop more.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off pick of the day is waiting`
