# Happening now: up to 50% off select beauty and 500 points with $75 spend
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is "Sephora Stacking Weekend" again — same combined offer structure as the 9/4 send I already scored 7/10 ("Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend"), just recut with a different hero. The top banner leads honestly with both parts of the deal (50% off + 500 bonus points), which keeps it in line with the brand's recent, better-behaved pattern rather than the bait-and-switch openers seen 8/28 through 9/3.
- Where this send falls down is focus: after the top banner, it pivots hard into a "Free Hair Sample Set" GWP block, then a long unrelated new-arrivals product grid (Bobbi Brown, Jo Malone, Oribe, Dermalogica, etc.) before finally re-surfacing the "50% off Select Beauty" offer as its own standalone banner much further down. That's two competing hero offers stacked on top of each other with a wall of product tiles in between — a subscriber has to scroll past a lot of unrelated merchandising to get back to the headline deal.
- It's a middling, cluttered execution of a genuinely decent offer. Net: functional but unfocused.

## What's working

- Top banner states both halves of the offer clearly: "50% off" plus "500 bonus points on $75 spend," matching the subject line's promise.
- No render bugs — all product tiles, images, and text blocks display cleanly.
- "NEW," "ONLY AT SEPHORA," and "NEW AND ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges give scannable differentiation across the product grid.
- Loyalty tiers (Insider / VIB / Rouge) are visible near the footer, reinforcing membership relevance.
- Multiple concrete secondary offers are visible (Free Hair Sample Set with code, 15% off shampoo/conditioner subscribe offer), giving several viable paths to a click.

## What's weak

- The 50% off Select Beauty banner is buried a second time — it doesn't appear again as its own callout until after a large, unrelated product grid, splitting attention across the email.
- Two hero-caliber offers (Stacking Weekend banner at top, "50% off Select Beauty / TODAY ONLY" banner mid-email) compete for the same "main offer" slot, diluting hierarchy.
- The middle third is a generic new-arrivals grid (8+ SKUs across categories) with no thematic tie to the discount — pure filler between two better offers.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency on the second banner contradicts the subject's "Happening now" framing and isn't reinforced anywhere else, making the deadline feel like an afterthought rather than a real constraint.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the mid-email new-arrivals grid or move it below the fold entirely — it's the single biggest thing diluting focus between the two beauty offers.
- 2. Merge the two "50% off" banners into one hero treatment at the top with the points stack, TODAY ONLY urgency, and a single CTA — don't make subscribers scroll to find the restatement.
- 3. Move the Free Hair Sample Set GWP either above the 50% banner (if it's meant to be the lead hook) or well below it — right now it interrupts the primary offer's momentum.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: 50% off Select Beauty + 500 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stack it: 50% off beauty, 500 points at $75`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free hair sample set with $45 spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Benefit, Rare Beauty, Supergoop — all 50% off today.`
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit end-time to "TODAY ONLY" so the urgency reads as credible rather than boilerplate.
