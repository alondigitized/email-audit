# Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest recent Sephora send I've audited. The hero is "CYBER WEEK STARTS NOW, 30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics," a huge numeral on purple with a clear SHOP NOW button. The subject promises "daily deals just dropped," and the "Great Deals Comin' In Hot" calendar (11/22 through 11/30) backs that up. It is the first send in the recent history where the offer and the subject line match.
- The top of the email undercuts the hero. A "FREE* Sample Bag" banner with the BAGITFIRST code sits above everything. It is app-only, needs a $95 minimum spend, and has fine print. That is the same habit as the Dior mini on 11/17 and the Dior lip trial on 11/14. The body then adds four more promos: gift cards, "up to 30% off," Kérastase and a holiday gift guide. By the bottom it reads as a catalog again.
- Judgement: a good, targeted-feeling Cyber Week send, with a real offer and a credible deadline. Two competing offers at the top and a long tail dilute it.

## What's working

- The 30% OFF Benefit Cosmetics hero is huge, specific and unmissable, with one clear SHOP NOW button.
- The "APP EXCLUSIVE" and "Today only!" framing gives urgency that reads as credible.
- The 11/22–11/30 deal calendar rewards return visits and shows brand logos and products for each day.
- The purple sequin Cyber Week design is distinct and seasonal, with no visible render bugs.
- The subject and the hero line up: Cyber Week deals, delivered as a daily-deals grid.

## What's weak

- The "FREE* Sample Bag" banner above the hero competes with the 30% offer. It carries a $95 minimum and an asterisk, and it is a Beauty Insider offer.
- Below the calendar there are five more promos: gift cards, "up to 30% off," Kérastase, the gift guide and the footer perks. The email loses focus.
- "Exclusively on the app" in the subject, plus app-only offers in the body, excludes anyone who doesn't have the app.
- The calendar thumbnails and brand names are tiny and hard to read on mobile, and the "Great Deals" list has no discount depth for each day.
- This is another Sephora send landing on 11/21, and the brand's cadence is heavy: 11/12, 11/13, 11/14, 11/17, 11/19 and now this.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% Benefit hero to the top and drop the sample-bag banner to a slim strip, or cut it from this send entirely.
- 2. Trim everything below the deal calendar. Keep the calendar and one gift-guide CTA.
- 3. State the discount for each day in the calendar ("11/23: up to 30% off Milk Makeup") so it works as a reason to return.
- 4. Make the app CTA a button that says "Get the app" and sits next to the hero, so non-app users know what to do.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Cyber Week: a new Sephora deal every day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Today only: 30% off Benefit, plus a sneak peek at 11/22–11/30 deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kenra, Milk, Fenty and more: see what drops each day.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a Harmony/Epsilon-style table layout, redirect-wrapped click tracking and a hidden open pixel. The only hard QA failure is an empty plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication warnings look like relay capture gaps, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and most body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** The visible links are wrapped redirects on `app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ff7b42938dd2e7369ff0b33c/...?__dU__=...`. The final destinations are not visible in the source.
- **Probe coverage:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 26. It reported no broken links among the 12, so nothing is confirmed broken. The 26 skipped links, which are mostly footer and social, are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...'>` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. Clients that block images or strip hidden elements will under-count opens. This is a measurement caveat, not a defect.
- **Wrapper depth:** The pixel and links both use long opaque tokens, so destinations can't be audited without following the redirects.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[Real] Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **[Real] Fixed width:** The wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. Only a `max-width:480px` media query is defined, and it touches font sizes and a few image classes. The wrapper is not made fluid, so narrow viewports may scroll horizontally unless the clients' own scaling kicks in.
- **[Real] Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. MSO conditional comments are not valid there, so the Outlook override likely won't apply.
- **[Minor] Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">`. The valid token is `noindex`. This has no effect in email.
- **[Minor] Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` does not describe the message.
- **JSON-LD:** The block contains `"discountCode": ""` and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a schema.org type. Gmail annotations won't use it, and an empty discount code is invalid.
- **Alt text (QA WARN):** The flagged image is the hidden tracking pixel (`/O/AQEAAZcF...`), so alt text is not meaningful there. Alt coverage on visible images could not be checked in the truncated source.
- **Plain text (QA FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. This may be an AgentMail capture artifact. If it is real, the message is HTML-only, which hurts accessibility for text-only clients and adds a spam-filter signal.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The personalization elsewhere in the email is not visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN):** Both are missing, but QA notes the relay may strip them. This is **unverified**, not confirmed non-compliant. Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, and its alignment is unverified.
- **Physical address and body unsubscribe link:** These are expected in the footer, which was truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is listed. Confirm manually.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Attribution appears to rely on the `__dU__` token and the redirect path, which is a platform-level approach.
- Landing-page alignment could not be verified because final destinations are hidden behind redirects. The 12 probed links returned no flagged errors.
- The `app.sephora.com` host suggests app and universal-link routing. Behavior for desktop users and users without the app was not tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run QA against the original delivery before logging these as defects.
2. Confirm whether the text/plain part is really empty, for example from the MIME structure. If so, add a meaningful one.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block into a proper `<!--[if mso]><style>` section.
5. Add a fluid fallback for the 700px wrapper, such as `width:100%; max-width:700px`, instead of a hard `min-width`.
6. Follow the redirects on the skipped 26 links, especially the footer unsubscribe and preference links. Record the final URLs and any UTM parameters.
7. Clean the JSON-LD by removing the empty `discountCode` and the invalid `PromotionCard` type, or drop the block.
