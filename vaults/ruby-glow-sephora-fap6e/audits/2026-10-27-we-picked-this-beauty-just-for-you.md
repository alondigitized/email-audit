# We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This send promises personalization it doesn't deliver. "We picked this beauty just for you!" and "Chosen Just for YOU" lead into a Gisou lip oil and honey lip mask, then a generic grid of Marc Jacobs, Juliette Has a Gun, Rare Beauty, Clarins, Versace and EADEM. Nothing visible says why these were chosen. The render is clean and the product-price grid is easy to scan, but "just for you" is a header on a standard catalog send.
- The strongest concrete offer is buried above the hero. A Topicals trial size is free with code HISKINLOVE on a $30 purchase. It sits in the top banner and competes with the hero. The hero itself has no offer, only a "Shop Now" button and Gisou "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" product copy with $28 and $26 prices. It follows the same pattern as the 10/24 and 10/23 sends, where a free-trial-size banner sat above the on-subject story.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand Sephora send that scores a 7 and isn't a standout. The cherry subject emoji has no visible tie to the creative, since the Gisou lip oil is "Cherry Chocolate" and the cherry theme isn't repeated anywhere in the email body.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and clean: orange gradient, large "Chosen Just for YOU" type, and two glossy Gisou products with names and prices ($28, $26).
- The Topicals free trial size is a concrete, specific offer with a visible code and a "Shop to Redeem" button.
- The product grids show names, brands and prices ($168, $150, $25, $142, $138, $24), so there's no mystery on cost.
- Beauty Insider tier messaging (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE, "Rewards Bazaar") is visible for members.
- "Only at Sephora" and "Limited Edition" tags add scarcity and exclusivity without shouting.

## What's weak

- The "just for you" claim is unsupported. Nothing shows my skincare or lip history, and the grid is generic bestsellers.
- The free-gift banner above the hero sets up a two-offer hierarchy, and the hero has no offer of its own.
- The email is long. The credit card block, AI Beauty Chat, Bestselling Vanillas and the Insider bar all dilute focus after the hero.
- There's no deadline on the Topicals offer ("online only" is the only qualifier), so nothing is time-bounded.
- Fine print is long and dense. Exclusions, the $30 minimum and a code that works only online appear in tiny type at the bottom and the top banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the personalization real. Add a one-line reason under the hero, such as "Because you shopped lip care" or "Based on your Beauty Insider picks", or drop the "just for you" language.
- 2. Put one offer in the hero. Either move the Topicals free gift into the hero, or add a Gisou-specific incentive such as points or a bonus gift.
- 3. Add an end date to the Topicals code, such as "through Sunday".
- 4. Cut the lower modules (credit card, AI chat, vanillas) or move the credit card block into a separate send.
- 5. Tie the cherry subject emoji to the creative, for example by naming the Cherry Chocolate lip oil.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gisou lip oil, $28, plus a free Topicals trial size with $30.`

## Technical Audit: "We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with VML/Outlook support and Epsilon/Sephora tracking infrastructure. The automated QA flagged one hard failure (no plain-text part). The remaining warnings are mostly relay-capture limits rather than confirmed defects. The HTML source is truncated, so body links, footer and merge tokens could not be inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 47, and it reported no probe failures. Footer, utility and social links were deprioritized, so they are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels are in place:**
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261027_CS_Curated_For_You`
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`
  - The `mi_u` parameter carries a numeric user ID in the URL.
- **Image hosting is split across domains:** `images.harmony.epsilon.com`, `app.sephora.com` and `mi.sephora.com`. This isn't a defect, but a block on any one domain affects only its own assets.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. Relay capture may contribute, but treat it as a real gap until a raw MIME check shows a `text/plain` part.
- **Three images lack `alt` text:**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` at `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06`. This is a content image, so the missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap.
  - `o.gif` and the `app.sephora.com/O/…` image look like open-tracking pixels. For these, an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom. The CSS also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`, which disables text resizing in iOS and WebKit clients.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The impact is negligible for email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` carries no email-specific context.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the truncated portion. The rest of the body was not available to check.

### 5. Compliance
- **Unconfirmed, not defects:**
  - `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
  - The QA output attributes all three to AgentMail relay stripping. They need verification against the original message headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result was reported.
- **Sender:** `shop@beauty.sephora.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice for reputation isolation.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign tag:** `mi_ecmp=20261027_CS_Curated_For_You` appears only on the pixel URL. It matches today's send date and the "curated for you" theme.
- **UTM parameters:** none could be verified because the CTA hrefs are in the truncated portion. Landing-page alignment is unverified.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` MIME part.** This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Add `alt` text.** Write descriptive `alt` for `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` and set `alt=""` on both tracking pixels.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and reconsider `text-size-adjust: none` so users can zoom and resize text.
4. **Check the raw headers.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass results on the original message.
5. **Fix the robots meta** to `noindex`, or drop it.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** That would let it check CTA UTMs, the footer and unsubscribe link, and merge tokens.
