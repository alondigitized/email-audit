## What's weak

- The subject says "50% OFF", but only three of the eight products are at 50%. The others are roughly 30–40%.
- There is a single generic "SHOP NOW" button in the hero. The product tiles don't show any visible CTA.
- There are no ratings, reviews, shade ranges or "bestseller" cues anywhere in the grid.
