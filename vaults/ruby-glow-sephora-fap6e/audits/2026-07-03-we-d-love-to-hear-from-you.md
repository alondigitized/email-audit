# We’d love to hear from you!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a post-purchase or CRM survey request from a separate "Sephora Opinions" sender address, not the main promotional Sephora pipeline. The incentive — 100 Beauty Insider points — is surfaced immediately in the preheader, which is the smartest thing this email does. The body is plain, functional text: no images, no product shots, no visual drama. That is probably intentional (survey emails get flagged as promotional less often when they look transactional), but it also makes the email feel cold and form-letter-ish.
- The timing is worth noting: this hits the inbox on the same day as the "Ends 7/7" Stacking Weekend continuation send (audited today, 8/10). Sephora is running two emails simultaneously on July 3rd — one pure promotion, one pure utility. The survey email is functionally separate, but subscribers who got both today face engagement fatigue.
- The legal terms block at the bottom is jarring and outsized. Bolded NDA-adjacent language ("you agree to not discuss nor disclose any information or questions asked therein with anyone into perpetuity") creates friction and mild alarm for a casual shopper. It's the last thing you read and it leaves a bad taste.

## What's working

- **Preheader does the heavy lifting.** "Earn 100 Beauty Insider reward points for your time" is concrete and loyalty-relevant — it's the reason to open, and it's the first thing visible.
- **Hard deadline is explicit and precise.** "Bold Wednesday, July 07, 11:59am Pacific Time" — no vagueness, credible urgency.
- **Time estimate is honest.** "10 minutes" sets expectation correctly; it respects the reader's time.
- **CTA is unambiguous.** "CLICK HERE TO GET STARTED ▶" — clear button, clear verb, no confusion about what happens next.
- **Frequency cap is disclosed.** Footer states "contacted 2 times at most within a calendar year," which builds trust for an opt-in survey program.

## What's weak

- **Generic salutation.** "Hi Sephora Shopper" signals zero personalization — first-name merge is table stakes for loyalty program email.
- **No visual hierarchy.** Plain text wall with no imagery, color, or scannable structure. The eye has nowhere to land except the single CTA button.
- **The NDA terms block is disproportionate and alarming.** Bold legalese about "perpetuity" and "all forms of communication, written, verbal, digital" is wildly overbuilt for a shopping-habits survey and will suppress completion rates.
- **Sender domain mismatch.** `opinions@beauty.sephora.com` is unfamiliar versus the usual `@sephora.com` — subscribers who recognize the normal sender will hesitate.
- **Points-credit lag buried in parenthetical.** "Typically takes up to 30 days" is easy to miss and undercuts the reward incentive.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Personalize the greeting** — swap "Hi Sephora Shopper" for first name. Every Beauty Insider account has it; there is no excuse not to use it here.
- 2. **Move the 100-point incentive into the body hero, not just the preheader** — make it the opening sentence, bold, not buried in paragraph two. "Complete a 10-minute survey by July 7 → earn 100 bonus points" should be line one.
- 3. **Cut or radically shrink the terms block** — a single sentence like "Survey responses are confidential" is enough. The "into perpetuity / written, verbal, digital" language reads like a legal threat and will hurt completion rates.
- 4. **Add one lightweight visual** — even the Beauty Insider logo or a star-points icon breaks the text wall and signals legitimacy.
- 5. **Subject is generic; rework it around the points hook:**
- **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 100 points in 10 minutes — quick survey inside`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your opinion = 100 Beauty Insider points (expires 7/7)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Takes 10 minutes. Points post within 30 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tell us how you shop beauty — survey closes July 7 at midnight.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Epsilon/Harmony ESP infrastructure with functional redirect-based click tracking and open-pixel tracking. Two hard blockers exist: zero-character plain-text fallback (deliverability FAIL) and missing List-Unsubscribe headers (compliance risk), both likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay capture rather than the upstream send.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues with link structure itself**, but notable observations:

- All clicks route through Epsilon's redirect system: `beauty.sephora.com/T/{campaignId}/{recipientHash}/{messageId}?__dU__={token}` — click tracking is active.
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://beauty.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` with base64-encoded recipient identifier in the URL path.
- "View in browser" link uses a `/H/` path variant: `https://beauty.sephora.com/H/2/v610000019f292425be8a785df4bbe5be50/...` — consistent with Epsilon's web-view handler.
- The `__dU__` query parameter on the logo CTA (`?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gvf-dtDhFgyx2PTfuJRsWdK`) is an Epsilon device/user token not present on the web-view link — minor inconsistency but not a bug.
- **Survey destination URL not visible in truncated source** — cannot confirm the landing page or UTM passthrough for the primary CTA. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **[WARN x5] Spacer GIFs missing alt text** — `http://epidm.edgesuite.net/CMS/Coding/Sephora/misc/spacerGif10X10.gif` has no `alt` attribute on any of its 5 instances. Decorative spacers should carry `alt=""` (empty string) so screen readers skip them. Missing `alt` causes some clients to render broken-image icons when images are blocked.
- **[WARN x5] Spacer GIFs served over HTTP** — same `http://epidm.edgesuite.net/...` URL. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block mixed-content HTTP images. These spacers control layout spacing; if blocked, vertical rhythm collapses.
- **[WARN] Open-pixel missing alt text** — `https://beauty.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute.
- **Deprecated `<font>` tags** — body copy uses nested `<font style="...">` tags (two levels deep). These are HTML 4 relics; inline `<span>` is the current approach, though practically harmless in most email clients.
- **DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional** — acceptable for email but worth noting; it opts the email into quirks-mode rendering in some clients.
- **Preview text div is empty** — `<div class="set_outlook_hidden" ...></div>` contains no text. The subject line alone fills the inbox preview snippet; a populated preheader would extend messaging real estate. (Out of scope for technical audit — flagged for completeness.)

No issues with: `lang="en"` attribute, viewport meta, `format-detection` meta, or mobile media query breakpoint (600px).

### 5. Compliance

**Issues found:**

- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent** — QA reports header not captured. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the native "Unsubscribe" button; its absence degrades one-click unsubscribe UX and increases spam complaint risk. Likely a relay-capture gap, but should be verified against raw headers from a direct SMTP capture.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058)** — one-click POST-based unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policies (>5k sends/day). Absence can suppress inbox placement for high-volume sends.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not captured** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown for this capture. `opinions@beauty.sephora.com` sending through Epsilon's infrastructure should produce a valid DKIM signature; this needs confirmation via direct header inspection.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 characters** — QA confirms text/plain MIME part is empty. This is a CAN-SPAM/CASL best-practice gap and a deliverability risk; some spam filters penalize missing plain-text parts. Epsilon's platform supports auto-generating plain text from HTML — this should be enabled.
- **Cannot confirm** physical mailing address or footer unsubscribe link from truncated HTML. These are CAN-SPAM requirements; verification requires full source.

**Cannot fully assess** — the primary survey CTA URL is not visible in the truncated HTML source. The following is observable:
