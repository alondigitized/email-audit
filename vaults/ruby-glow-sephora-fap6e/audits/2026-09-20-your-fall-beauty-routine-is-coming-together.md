# Your fall beauty routine is coming together 🍂
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-arrivals showcase dressed up as a "fall routine" story, and the seams show immediately. The subject promises a fall beauty *routine coming together*, but the hero banner just says "FALL INTO NEW BEAUTY" over a generic model close-up — no routine, no step-by-step, no fall-specific product callout. What follows is a long, undifferentiated scroll of "NEW" and "NEW + EXCLUSIVE" product tiles (Salt & Stone, Fenty, Emi Jay, Kayali, Huda, Josie Maran, Caudalie, Loveshackfancy) with zero narrative thread tying them into a "routine."
- The one genuinely concrete offer — "Get a Trial Size... on the Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez Positive Light Liquid Luminizer" — is buried at the very top in a tiny gray strip above the fold, easy to skim past, and it's not even the hero. The real hero is a skincare-focused "YOUR DIEUX RITUAL" block, which is closer to "routine" territory than the header banner is — meaning the email's own top-to-bottom hierarchy contradicts itself.
- Compared to this brand's recent cadence (2026-09-19 "4X points on fragrance," 2026-09-15 "50% off Select Beauty") this is a weaker, offer-light send — it reads like a product catalog rather than a promo, and the subject overpromises a curated "routine" experience the layout doesn't deliver.

## What's working

- The "YOUR DIEUX RITUAL" section is the strongest visual moment — clean before/after-style model shot, clear product callouts (firming cream, moisturizer), and it's the only part that gestures at an actual "routine."
- Clear "NEW" and "NEW + EXCLUSIVE" badges make new-arrival status scannable at a glance.
- "Clean at Sephora" and "Cruelty-Free" badges are visible on relevant product blocks, useful trust signals for the ingredient-conscious.
- No render bugs — images, text, and product tiles are all rendering cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- The "4X Points on Fragrance" and "New and Under $50" mini-banners near the footer add secondary offer variety.

## What's weak

- Subject says "fall beauty routine" but the hero banner ("FALL INTO NEW BEAUTY") and the endless single-item product list never assemble into a routine — no step 1/2/3, no "your fall edit" curation.
- The only real dollar-value offer (free trial-size Rare Beauty luminizer) is crammed into a tiny top strip, visually subordinate to everything below it.
- The email is a long undifferentiated wall of ~8 near-identical product blocks (photo + name + price) with no visual hierarchy telling the eye where to land first.
- No urgency anywhere in the main body — no "ends," no "limited," nothing time-bound outside the small 4X points footer banner.
- Repetitive structure (image, brand name, one-line description, price, repeat) gets monotonous well before the fold ends, risking fatigue/scroll-abandonment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to actually deliver on the subject: pick 3-4 products and frame them as "Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3" of a fall routine, not a flat new-arrivals dump.
- 2. Promote the Rare Beauty free trial-size offer into a real hero banner — it's the only concrete, price-relevant offer in the email and it's currently invisible.
- 3. Cut the product list roughly in half and group remaining items into 2-3 themed sub-sections (e.g., "Skin Prep," "Fragrance Layering," "Hair for Fall") to restore hierarchy.
- 4. Add a visible deadline or urgency cue to at least one offer in the main body, not just the footer fragrance banner.
- 5. Vary the tile layout (alternate large hero shots with smaller grid clusters) to break up the monotony of the repeating single-column blocks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your fall skin routine, step by step`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New fall arrivals worth building a routine around`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Start with skin, finish with scent — this week's new arrivals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A free trial size, plus everything new for fall`
