# Save on your faves: up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🌟
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Sephora shampoo/conditioner send in 72 hours. The June 19 "Woo-hoo 🎉 Up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🫧" email carried the exact same offer; this one reframes it with "Save on your faves" warmth and swaps the foam emoji for a star, but the discount is identical. For a subscriber who ignored the June 19 send, this is a clean non-intrusive retarget. For one who converted, it's noise. For one who opens and expects a hair-care email, the body quickly throws off-topic curveballs — a "#71 Sephora Collection Pro Brushes" feature and a full "Best of Skincare" module that have nothing to do with shampoo.
- The hero executes cleanly: discount stated, CTA prominent, free same-day delivery bar adds a value layer. But Sephora's template engine injects evergreen modules regardless of the stated category, and this email is a textbook example of that dilution. The lack of any expiration date on the 25%-off offer is a real missed opportunity — without a deadline, there's no reason to click today versus next week.
- Judgment: Competent retarget with clean bones, dragged down by content injection, a subject line that blows past mobile character limits, and zero urgency mechanics.

## What's working

- Hero is immediately legible: "Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners" lands first, SHOP NOW button is visible and unambiguous.
- "Save on your faves" framing is warmer and more relational than June 19's "Woo-hoo" open — it plays to subscriber familiarity.
- Free same-day delivery call-out in the header bar adds a purchase incentive without eating body copy space.
- BeautyINSIDER and points-earning section gives loyalty members a second click motivation beyond the discount.
- Kerastase "New From" module introduces a premium editorial anchor that elevates the editorial feel.

## What's weak

- This is the second 25%-off-shampoo send in three days — a subscriber who saw and skipped June 19 gets no new reason to act.
- Subject line is ~67 characters, well past the 50-char mobile cutoff; "select shampoos and conditioners" truncates on most phones.
- The "Everyday Icons #71 Pro Brushes" section mid-email has no connection to hair care and breaks the email's stated theme.
- "Best of Skincare" module near the bottom is pure template injection — it signals to recipients that this email wasn't curated for them specifically.
- No expiration date anywhere — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, no urgency mechanic to drive same-session conversion.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an expiration date to the hero** — "Ends Sunday, June 22" or "48 hours left" converts this from a passive offer into an action prompt. This is the highest-leverage single change.
- 2. **Trim the subject below 50 chars** and make the deadline the hook — see alts below.
- 3. **Strip the Pro Brushes and Best of Skincare modules** from this send; they dilute the hair care story and signal generic batch-and-blast.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to "Shop Hair Deals" — verb + noun + category keeps the click destination clear.
- 5. **Suppress to June 19 clickers** — anyone who already converted on shampoos should receive a "you might also love" cross-category send instead.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your faves: 25% off shampoos — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 25% off hair faves — 48 hrs left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day delivery on your next hair haul.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your go-to shampoos and conditioners, finally on sale.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **User zoom blocked**: `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` — `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

- **Text-size-adjust suppressed globally**: The rule `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; ... }` disables OS-level text scaling across virtually all elements. This overrides accessibility settings for users who have increased system font size. Scope this rule narrowly (e.g., to `td` or `.sup` only, where it's legitimately needed for Outlook control).

- **Generic `<title>`**: `<title>Sephora</title>` — some email clients (e.g., Apple Mail, Outlook on Mac) surface the `<title>` in tab/window previews. A descriptive value like `Sephora — Up to 25% Off Shampoos & Conditioners` is more useful, though impact is minor.

- **`robots` meta value has a space**: `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` — should be `noindex` (no space). Negligible in email context since crawlers rarely index email HTML, but technically malformed.

No issues found with DOCTYPE selection (XHTML 1.0 Transitional is correct for broad email client support), MSO VML namespace inclusion, or the Outlook/Gmail data-detector suppression rules (both are properly implemented).
