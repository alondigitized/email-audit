# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the most honest version of Sephora's "50% off Select Beauty" play I've seen this week — the subject says "Score 50% off select beauty," and the hero banner delivers exactly that: a "TODAY ONLY" box with three named products (Benefit brow pencil, Supergoop sunscreen, Rare Beauty blush) each showing a real strikethrough price and sale price. That's a structural improvement over 8/28, 8/29, 8/31, and 9/2, where the same "50% off" banner gave way to an unrelated new-arrivals scroll with no discount shown anywhere.
- That said, the second half of the email — "Get 50% Off These Picks" — undercuts the headline again. None of those eight products (Pureology, Tarte, Kiehl's, Charlotte Tilbury, Anastasia, Shiseido, Milk Makeup) show a discounted price or strikethrough; they're listed at full price under a "50% Off" section header, which is the same bait-and-switch pattern as prior sends, just demoted to a sub-section instead of the whole email.
- Net: better than the 9/2 and 8/31 sends because the top of the email actually proves the discount, but the second section still promises 50% off and shows full-price tags. Call it a 6/10 — real progress, still not fully honest.

## What's working

- Hero banner shows three named products with genuine strikethrough pricing (Benefit $28→$14, Supergoop $33→$19, Rare Beauty $27→$13.50) — this is the first "50% off" send this week to actually prove the discount above the fold.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency framing is credible and paired with visible proof, not just a claim.
- Clean single-column product grid with a bug-free product photo for every item.
- FREE shipping for Beauty Insiders is called out twice (top banner + hero subhead), reinforcing loyalty value.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is present for member segmentation, consistent with the brand's loyalty focus.

## What's weak

- "Get 50% Off These Picks" section shows eight products at full price with no strikethrough or sale tag — same bait-and-switch structure flagged on 8/31, 9/2, and 9/3.
- No indication of which tier of picks (Insider/VIB/Rouge) qualifies for the second-section discount, unlike the top hero which is clear.
- Preview/preheader text is not visible in the render — can't confirm it complements the subject.
- Superscript footnote markers (ᵃ, †) on "GET 50% OFF" and "FREE shipping" point to fine print buried at the very bottom in tiny gray text — exclusion-heavy pattern the rubric penalizes.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the 6th "50%" beauty email this week: 8/28, 8/29, 8/31, 9/2, 9/3, now 9/5.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add real strikethrough pricing to every item in "Get 50% Off These Picks," matching the hero section's format — right now the section header promises a discount the product cards don't show, which is the single biggest credibility gap in this email.
- 2. Move the exclusions footnote closer to the "GET 50% OFF" banner (even a one-line "select items, while supplies last" under the CTA) instead of relegating it to bottom-of-email gray text.
- 3. Differentiate this send from 9/2 and 9/3 — same banner treatment three sends running trains subscribers to skip it; rotate hero art or lead with a single hero product instead.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or end-time to "TODAY ONLY" to make the urgency measurable, not just asserted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 50% off Benefit, Supergoop & Rare Beauty`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off picks are live — shop before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `3 cult favorites, half off — plus free shipping for Insiders`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Benefit, Supergoop, Rare Beauty: today's 50% off lineup`
