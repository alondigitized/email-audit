# Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear offer wrapped in a confused email. The subject promises "30% off ALL Sephora Collection," and the top banner delivers it: a gold "30% OFF" medallion, dates 10/30–11/9, and the code ALONSALE. But the giant "BEAUTY for YOU" headline and the Eadem and Yise moisturizer hero take over the page. Neither product is Sephora Collection, so the first thing you see contradicts the subject.
- This is also a near-repeat of yesterday's "STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊" (10/30). That send had one big medallion and nothing competing with it. Today's version adds the same offer to a general catalog email. It buries the offer under a "We Think You'll Love These" grid, a Valentino ad, K18 gift sets, a holiday gift guide, and a credit card pitch.
- Verdict: a competent Sephora catalog send, but it dilutes its own best message. Repeating the offer a day later, with the offer pushed down the hierarchy, will read as noise.

## What's working

- The 30% medallion, the dates and the promo code sit together in a compact banner at the very top.
- The subject names one specific offer and a specific product line.
- The product grid shows names and prices (e.g. Rare Beauty blush $25, Torriden $27), so the recipient can judge value without clicking.
- The barcode block at the bottom makes in-store redemption easy.
- The holiday gift guide and category buttons (Skincare, Makeup, Fragrance, Hair) give relevant ways in.

## What's weak

- The hero is a "Beauty for You" headline and two non-Collection moisturizers. It buries the Sephora Collection offer and doesn't match the subject.
- The medallion banner is thin and cramped beside product swatches, and the product images are small. The "Savings Event" label is tiny.
- There are no Sephora Collection products anywhere I can see. The one offer in the subject is never shown on a product.
- The email is very long. After the offer there's a grid, a Valentino ad, K18, a gift guide, a credit card promo, an app download and four fine-print blocks.
- The fine print is dense, and the 30% tiers (30% off for Rouge/VIB, 10% off for Insider, per the 10/29 send) aren't explained in the render. That makes the offer feel less honest.
- The CTA is a generic "SHOP NOW" and it points at the wrong products.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero around Sephora Collection. Put 4 to 6 real Collection products under the 30% medallion, with the code and end date in the headline. This one change would lift the email most.
- 2. Cut the lower modules (Valentino, K18, gift guide, credit card) or move them below a short Collection grid. The email should have one job.
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, like "Shop Sephora Collection 30% Off." Add a second CTA by category.
- 4. Say who gets 30% and who gets 10% right beside the offer, in plain text, so it doesn't look like fine print.
- 5. Don't send the same offer two days running without new creative. Use a "ends Sunday" or "last chance" angle for later sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your code: ALONSALE (30% off Sephora Collection)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lip, skin and brush bestsellers, plus use code ALONSALE at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge and VIB get 30% off, Insider 10%. Ends 11/9.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with redirect-wrapped tracking links. The QA run flagged one hard failure, a 0-character plain-text part, and several unverifiable header checks. Most header findings are probably relay artifacts and not confirmed defects. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a…?__dU__=…`, a first-party click-tracking redirect. The QA report says 12 material links were probed and 38 were skipped. No per-link status codes were provided, so I can't confirm any broken links or redirect chains.
- **Hidden open pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>` is the open-tracking pixel. It sits inside the preheader `<td>` and uses single-quoted attributes. It works, but it has no `width`/`height` attributes.
- **Second tracker:** `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261031_CS_Curated_For_You_BI` is a separate third-party-style open pixel. The two trackers measure opens independently, so their counts may not match.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, confirmed):** Both tracking pixels have no `alt`: `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` and `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif`. Both are non-content images, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Plain-text part is empty (QA FAIL):** "Text version is 0 chars". If this is a real defect and not a relay artifact, it hurts deliverability and leaves text-only clients with nothing. It should be verified against the raw MIME.
- **Malformed MSO conditional inside `@media`:** The source has `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. Inside an at-rule block, the parser treats `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` as selectors. Both are invalid, so the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule is likely dropped on mobile clients. The conditional should sit outside the media query.
- **Fixed-width layout:** The wrapper has `min-width:700px; width:700px`, and the body has `min-width:100%`. On viewports under 700px, the fixed table width will force horizontal scrolling or scaling. The only mobile overrides are font sizes and a few image sizes.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` is not a valid value; it should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients.
  - The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Scripts are stripped by most clients, and this markup is incomplete and not useful.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear. Identifiers such as `__dU__=` and `mi_u=` are resolved. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN):** The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers. This is unconfirmed, so don't treat it as a sender defect. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. Nothing can be concluded about authentication.
- **Footer:** The footer, physical address and body unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source. The QA report says an unsubscribe link was probed, but gave no result. CAN-SPAM body compliance is unverified, not failed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs are hidden behind `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be verified from the source or the QA data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261031_CS_Curated_For_You_BI` on the tracking pixel. Its label, "Curated For You", doesn't obviously match this send (subject: "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection"). This may be a segment or template label, so check that campaign naming matches the intended send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part. If it is really empty, add a proper plain-text alternative.
2. Capture the original headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay, so the compliance checks aren't blind.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query so `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and later rules aren't dropped.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, or switch the layout to fluid or hybrid widths.
6. Raise the probe cap (currently 12 of 50 links) and resolve the final redirect URLs, so UTM and landing-page continuity can be checked.
7. Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML to cover the footer, address and unsubscribe link.
