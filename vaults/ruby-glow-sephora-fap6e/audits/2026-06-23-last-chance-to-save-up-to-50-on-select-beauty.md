# Last chance to save up to 50% on select beauty 🫨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Sephora's second "it's ending" email on June 23 — the "ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🫣" was already logged for the same date, and now this "Last chance" send lands with nearly identical offer language. That cadence call is the single biggest problem here: when subscribers already got the "ENDS TODAY" email this morning, they stop believing the urgency signals by the afternoon. The credibility that took the whole Major Summer Deals campaign to build gets burned in one doubled-up send.
- Inside the email the value stack is real — 50% off, 25% off, and a 500 bonus points stacker is a legitimately strong combination for Beauty Insiders. The same-day delivery deadline (ends 6/25 at 11:59 PM ET) is the most specific and credible urgency signal in the entire email. The problem is Sephora buried it in the middle of what is easily a 12-section email — "Just Arrived" new products, "FANCY FROM MERIT" editorial, and "IF YOU LOVE IT" features have no business being in a closing-urgency send. The email doesn't know if it's a sale close or a browse experience.
- The creative holds together visually and renders cleanly. But it's trying to do twelve jobs in a send that should do one: drive the last stack before the sale ends.

## What's working

- **Three-layer offer stack in the hero** — 50% off + 25% off + 500 bonus points gives members a compound reason to click that non-members don't have, which rewards the loyal subscriber base.
- **"STACK NOW!" CTA** in the hero is a differentiated verb among a sea of "SHOP NOW" buttons — it signals a member-specific mechanic and will pull Beauty Insiders to action.
- **Same-day delivery deadline with a hard timestamp** (ends 6/25 at 11:59 PM ET) is the most credible urgency signal in the email — far stronger than "last chance."
- **No render bugs** — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout holds across the long scroll.

## What's weak

- **Duplicate urgent send on the same day** — "ENDS TODAY" went out June 23; this "Last chance" follows hours later with the same 50% off headline, training subscribers to ignore both.
- **Twelve competing content sections** — "Just Arrived" new products contradict the sale-close framing entirely; "FANCY FROM MERIT" and "IF YOU LOVE IT" editorial belong in a different email.
- **Every sub-hero CTA says "SHOP NOW"** — no directional signal about which offer, category, or product tier to act on.
- **"Select beauty" appears in both subject and hero with zero specificity** — no product count, no category callout, no exclusion list; the qualifier makes the offer feel smaller than it is.
- **Zero social proof** — no reviews, no "selling fast" copy, nothing to signal that other people are acting on this offer right now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't send two closing-urgency emails on the same day.** This send should have replaced the "ENDS TODAY" email, not stacked on it. If there's a true "last push" moment, schedule it for 6–8 PM when the credible deadline is hours away.
- 2. **Cut to three sections**: hero (50% off + 500 points stack) → Beauty Insiders block with the exact 6/25 11:59 PM ET deadline → one product grid of 4-6 top-discounted SKUs. Delete everything else.
- 3. **Surface the credible deadline in the hero**, not buried in the Beauty Insiders section. "Stack ends 6/25 at 11:59 PM ET" in the hero CTA row is worth more than five editorial sections combined.
- 4. **Replace "SHOP NOW" CTAs with category-specific labels** — "Shop Skincare," "Shop Makeup," "Shop Hair" — so click intent is directional.
- 5. **Add a product count** — "300+ picks up to 50% off" converts "select" from a weasel word into a proof point.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off + 500 points stack — ends tonight 🫨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 300+ picks up to 50% off at Sephora`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day delivery ends 6/25 at 11:59 PM — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 500 bonus points before midnight tonight`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Locking zoom prevents low-vision users from enlarging content. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `user-scalable=yes`.

**Issue — `text-size-adjust: none` applied to broad selector set**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applying `text-size-adjust: none` to `body` and block elements globally suppresses OS-level font scaling for visually impaired users. The intent (prevent Outlook/iOS auto-resize) is legitimate, but scope should be narrowed to `table, td` only.

**Issue — `robots` meta value has a space**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
The correct token is `noindex` (no space). Most crawlers are lenient, but the malformed value is technically non-compliant. Correct to `content="noindex, nofollow"`.

**Observation — XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype**
Expected for broad email-client compatibility (Outlook 2016 and earlier). Not an issue.
