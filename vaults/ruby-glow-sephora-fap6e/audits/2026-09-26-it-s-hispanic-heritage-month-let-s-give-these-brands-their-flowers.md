# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This one earns its premise. "Hispanic Heritage Month—let's give these brands their flowers" promises a founder-spotlight showcase, and the body delivers exactly that: AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, and Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez each get a photo, a founder quote, and a shop link. The "Founders Face to Face" video module with Dez Perkins and Denise Vasi is a genuinely nice touch — it makes the cultural framing feel earned rather than tacked-on.
- Where it falls apart is focus. By the time you scroll past four brand spotlights, a founders video, hair-care and skin-care upsells, a 4X points K-beauty banner, a 20% value-size haircare banner, and a credit-card pitch, the "flowers for these brands" narrative has been buried under Sephora's usual grab-bag of unrelated offers. This is the classic Sephora pattern across this run (see 2026-09-20's "fall routine" send) — a good hook up top, diluted by an undifferentiated coupon dump below.
- Net: strong concept, poor editorial discipline. Cut it to two brands and the video, and it's an 8. As shipped, it's a 6.

## What's working

- Hero banner and headline ("Unidos, Somos Más") is on-theme, colorful, and legible with no render issues.
- Founder quotes paired with real photos (Aora Mexico founders, Rare Beauty/Selena Gomez) add authenticity that a generic product grid wouldn't.
- "Founders Face to Face" video block is a distinct, clickable content format that breaks up the product-grid monotony.
- "Shop and Support" CTA button copy is consistent and reinforces the cause-marketing angle across every brand block.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, text aligned, layout consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Message dilutes fast: four brand spotlights plus a video plus three unrelated promos (K-beauty points, haircare sale, credit card) crowd out the Hispanic Heritage Month story.
- No single hero offer — this reads as a content/PR email, not a deal email, so there's nothing for a deal-motivated shopper to lock onto.
- The credit-card and 4X-points banners at the bottom feel bolted on and visually clash with the warm, editorial tone set at the top.
- Very long scroll (the image is over 9,000px) for what is essentially eight small modules — mobile readers will bail before reaching the video or the bottom offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — keep 2 brand spotlights + the founders video, move the other 2 brands to a follow-up send. Focus wins opens and clicks more than coverage does.
- 2. Move the "Shop and Support" CTAs into full-width buttons under each product pair instead of small text links, so the eye has one obvious next action per section.
- 3. Drop the credit-card and generic K-beauty/haircare banners from this specific send — they contradict the heritage-month narrative and read as filler.
- 4. Add a single sitewide callout (e.g., "Shop the full Hispanic Heritage Month collection") near the top so browsers who won't read the whole scroll still get one clear CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Meet the founders behind Hispanic Heritage Month's best beauty`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 Latina-founded brands you need to know 🌺`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia & Rare Beauty — hear from the founders`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Founders Dez Perkins & Denise Vasi talk beauty, culture, and community`
