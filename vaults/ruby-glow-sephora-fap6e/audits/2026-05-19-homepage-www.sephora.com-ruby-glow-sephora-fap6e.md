# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora is really doing the absolute MOST right now with the Summer Fridays drop, and honestly? The homepage knows exactly who it's talking to. It's leaning heavily into the skincare-meets-makeup hybrid trend — bronzer with skincare ingredients is *very* 2025 — and the color story on that compact flat lay is chef's kiss. But as a Rouge member, I'm a little annoyed that I open the app and my points balance and Beauty Insider perks are completely invisible. Like, hi, I spent thousands of dollars here.

## What's working

- **Summer Fridays hero feature** — launching a hero-position feature around a buzzy brand's new bronzer balm is exactly the kind of content I'd pause my scroll for. I've been watching this drop.
- **"available in six shades!" overlay** — showing product range directly on the flat-lay image removes a click. I immediately want to see which shade is mine.
- **The promo bar copy** — "Get a Skincare Sample Set! Free* with $45 min. spend. Use code GETSAMPLES" is concrete. I know the threshold, I know the mechanic.
- **Category nav strip** — Makeup, Skincare, New, Fragrance are all one tap. I can bail to "New" instantly if the hero doesn't hook me.
- **Model application shot** — seeing the product actually on skin (warm bronzed glow, real brush technique) is more convincing than a pack shot alone.

## What's weak

- **No Beauty Insider / Rouge benefits visible** — as a Rouge member, my relationship with Sephora starts with my points. Not showing my tier, my balance, or upcoming VIB sale access is a real missed hook for returning members.
- **"Sign In for FREE Shipping" is pushy, not warm** — this reads like friction, not a welcome-back moment. It leads with what I don't have instead of recognizing what I do have.
- **No urgency on the Summer Fridays module** — if this is a new launch, tell me it just dropped. "Just In" is soft. "Launched today" or a "limited shades" flag would sharpen it.
- **Zero review/rating signals** — I can't see any star ratings or review counts anywhere on the featured product. For $40+ makeup I'm buying blind here.
- **No price visible** — the hero feature has no price point. I have to tap before I know if this is a $20 or $58 balm.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Beauty Insider tier and points in the header or sign-in prompt** — swap the cold "Sign In for FREE Shipping" for "Welcome back, Rouge member — 1,840 pts" for logged-in users. That's retention, not acquisition messaging.
- **Add star count + review number to the product module** — even "4.7 ★ (312)" underneath the product copy would push me significantly closer to tapping SHOP NOW with confidence.
- **Add a "Launched today" or "Just dropped — limited shades" badge** — the "Just In" headline is doing quiet work; make it urgent. A small badge on the hero image corner would do it.
- **Show the price in the feature module** — "The Bronzer Butter Balm — $38" right next to the CTA removes a discovery friction point and lets me know if it pairs well with my $45 promo threshold.
