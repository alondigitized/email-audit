# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- OK so Sephora is clearly leaning hard into Mother's Day gifting right now — the hero is gift cards, full stop, with that very pretty floral treatment. As a Beauty Insider Rouge who is here for the launches and the VIB stacking, this feels like the brand talking *past* me to a gifter, not to me as the obsessed shopper I am. The one thing that actually grabs me? That 4X Points on Fragrance banner at the very top with code BESTSCENTS — and it expires today, May 10th. That's the real hook for someone like me.

## What's working

- **4X Points fragrance banner** — as a Rouge member this is legitimately exciting, and "through 5/10" with today being May 10 creates real urgency without being sleazy about it
- **BESTSCENTS promo code** — specific, memorable, copyable; I'm already thinking about what I'd buy in fragrance
- **Top navigation bar** — "New," "Makeup," "Skincare," "Fragrance" are all immediately tappable, one move from where I want to be
- **"Sign In for FREE Shipping"** — a clean, low-friction nudge to authenticate that ties a real benefit to account access
- **AI Beauty tab (NEW badge)** in the bottom nav — I noticed that immediately; as someone who's tech-comfortable and always wants to find the next thing, that badge is a magnet
- **Seasonal timing** — Mother's Day gift cards on May 10 is exactly right; Sephora knows its calendar

## What's weak

- **Gift card hero for a loyalty obsessive** — I came here to shop for myself, not to buy my mom a digital gift card; the hero is completely off-target for the core repeat buyer
- **No product imagery above the fold** — not a single SKU, tube, palette, or bottle in the hero; for someone who launches her app to scout new drops, this is a scroll-past
- **No people / no beauty-in-use imagery** — it's floral and nice but there's no model, no skin, no lashes — nothing that connects to the actual product experience
- **Free shipping threshold is vague** — "Sign In for FREE Shipping" doesn't tell me the order minimum; I don't know if I'm $10 away or $50 away
- **New arrivals not visible above the fold** — I have to dig into the nav to find "New"; for someone who checks the launch calendar weekly, this should be front and center
- **No Rouge-tier recognition** — if I'm already signed in (implied by returning), I'd want to see my points balance or a "Rouge exclusive" badge; nothing here rewards my loyalty tier

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a new-launch or "What's hot this week" module** — gift card gifting is one slot in a carousel, not the lead; a mascara launch or Mother's Day "treat yourself" angle would serve the core loyalist audience better
- **Surface a Rouge member benefit badge in the banner** — "Rouge members earn 4X today" lands harder than the generic version when I'm signed in; personalize that strip
- **Add a "New Arrivals" quick-shop rail below the hero** — five to six product tiles with star ratings, even just thumbnails, would double my scroll depth instantly
- **Specify the free-shipping threshold** — "Free shipping on orders $35+" on the sign-in prompt removes friction and gives me a target to hit
