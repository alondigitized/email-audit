# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a loyalty-catalog email. The subject, "Did you know that points = rewards?", sells a concept. The body sells two things: a "Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points" Beauty Insider Challenge banner, and the October Rewards Bazaar ("October rewards are here!") with a Laura Mercier spotlight. The subject promises neither.
- The body is solid for an Insider member. Each reward shows a point cost and a live stock count, for example "750 points / 350 available now" and "30,000 points / 1 available now". That scarcity is the most credible urgency in the email. But two competing heroes, a long scroll, and a subject that sounds like a PSA keep this a mid-tier send.
- It's also the second points-themed Sephora email today, after "2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow", and it follows the 10/5 app early-access send. A subscriber sees points messaging stacked back to back. The subject needs a concrete hook to stand apart.

## What's working

- Every reward shows its point price and "available now" count, so a member can judge value immediately.
- The green "up to 500 points" banner has a clear "JOIN THE CHALLENGE >" button and a "+500" badge, and it sits above the fold.
- The Laura Mercier spotlight gives the catalog a brand anchor, and the tiered cost ladder (750 to 30,000 points) is easy to scan.
- The experience reward, "An Evening in New York City", is aspirational and breaks up the product grid.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the Beauty Insider tier bar and app download link in the footer are tidy.

## What's weak

- The challenge banner and the Rewards Bazaar hero each ask for a different action, "Join the Challenge" versus "Shop Now", so neither clearly wins.
- The subject names neither the 500-point challenge nor the Bazaar. "Did you know" is a weak opener.
- The preheader area leads with shipping and pickup boilerplate ("Enjoy Same-Day Delivery, FREE shipping*..."), not points copy.
- No deadline is visible for the Bazaar or the challenge, so urgency rests only on stock counts.
- The email is very long, and the second tier, "750+ Point Rewards", repeats the same grid pattern. The fine print at the bottom is dense.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Put the 500-point challenge first with a clear end date, and move the Bazaar below it as supporting content. Alternatively, send the Bazaar alone.
- 2. Put the actual hook in the subject: the 500 points, or the stock-limited rewards.
- 3. Replace the shipping boilerplate in the preheader with real points copy.
- 4. Add a visible deadline to the challenge, such as "Ends Oct 31".
- 5. Cut the second product tier, or collapse it into a "See all 750+ point rewards" link.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join the Insider Challenge, then spend points on Laura Mercier kits while they last.`
