# Homepage snapshot · www.sephora.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sephora walked in dressed for the occasion — this feels like peak VIB sale energy and it landed right in my face the second the page loaded. The brand is going full Beauty Insider mode: bold discount, stacked rewards, and a hard deadline. As a Rouge member who literally circled May on her calendar for exactly this kind of event, I felt seen.

## What's working

- **The "STACKPOINTS" code in the top banner** — I immediately recognize this as a combinable promo mechanic. As a Rouge member who knows the stacking rules, this is like a bat signal.
- **"Ends 5/26" deadline** — three days out, feels credible and urgent without being fake-countdown aggressive.
- **The BI 500 bonus points + 50% off dual callout** — this is exactly how I think about VIB events, two levers at once, and Sephora showed both upfront.
- **Sign In for FREE Shipping** — frictionless incentive. I'm already logged in on my phone but this tells me the session benefit is real.
- **Bottom nav with "Offers" tab** — I can tap straight to promotions in one move. That's mobile-first thinking done right.
- **"AI Beauty" tab with NEW badge** — I see that and I'm immediately curious. They know I'll tap a newness signal.

## What's weak

- **In-store only caveat buried in micro-print** — the 50% off is stacked on *in-store merchandise only*, but the hero doesn't make that clear until you squint at the footnote. As a mobile shopper who might drive to the store, I need that front and center, not hidden below the hero text.
- **No product imagery in the hero** — the graphic is stylized (silhouette + typography), which is fine aesthetically but I don't see a mascara, a palette, or anything tactile that makes me want to tap and *buy*. Give me something to covet.
- **No new arrivals or trending rail visible** — above the fold it's all sale energy. I love a deal but I also came here to discover what dropped this week.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — for a sale event this specific, "SHOP THE SALE" or "STACK MY REWARDS" would convert me faster and feel more on-brand.

## Recommendations

- **Add a product thumbnail strip below the hero** — even three teaser swatches ("Top picks in the sale: [mascara] [serum] [palette]") gives me something to click toward instead of a generic category dump.
- **Move the in-store-only restriction into the hero body copy**, not a footnote — "In-store + select online styles" is enough. Hiding it feels manipulative and I will tweet about it.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" CTA copy to "STACK MY SAVINGS"** — mirrors the headline, rewards-fluent language, and it's one of those micro-copy wins you can ship in a day.
- **Surface my current Insider points balance** once I'm signed in — a line like "You have 2,340 points — redeem now" would close the loop and push me straight to cart.
