# Want a FREE gift with purchase, Insider? 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a free gift with purchase. The body leads with a percent-off sale instead. The screenshot reaches me heavily downscaled, so I can read headlines and layout but not the fine print or every code. The top banner says "Insider, everything is on sale! STARTS TODAY" over two medallions: 10% off for Insider (11/3–11/9) and 30% off Sephora Collection (10/30–11/9). The free gift, a Parachute headband and washcloth at a $100 spend, sits in the "Stack Your Savings" block below. That is the wrong order for a subject line built on the gift.
- The email is clear about the sale and cluttered after that. Below the hero it stacks perks, a points challenge, a credit card promo, a product grid, a store locator, gift-guide links and a long legal block. It is also the fifth or so send since "STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍" on 11/2 and the 30% Sephora Collection pushes on 10/30–11/1. A subscriber would see this as the same sale again, with the gift as the new hook.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand sale email. The subject sets up a gift that the body buries, and the length dilutes it. It is a 6–7 send.

## What's working

- The "STARTS TODAY" header and the two medallions make the sale legible at a glance, with dates attached.
- The Insider-first framing ("Insider, everything is on sale!") speaks to loyalty members.
- The "Stack Your Savings" perks block shows the gift, the 500-point challenge and the credit card benefit in one place.
- The "Save On These Faves" product grid shows real products with sale prices, so there is something concrete to click.
- Sephora branding is consistent and trusted, and the render looks clean with no obvious broken images.

## What's weak

- The subject's hook is the free gift, but the gift is a small text block well below the hero.
- The email is very long. Perks, points, credit card, grid, Spot It, scents, store finder, gift guide and legal compete with each other.
- The hero has no single dominant offer. It carries two discounts with different dates and codes, plus a same-day delivery strip.
- The primary button is a generic "Shop Now", and the gift block's link is a small code callout rather than a strong button.
- Cadence is heavy. This repeats the 30% / 10% message from the last five days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free gift into the hero, with the product image and one button such as "Claim your free gift". The subject promised the gift, so the first screen should show it.
- 2. Cut the body to hero, gift, three or four products and the legal footer. Move the points, credit card and store locator blocks to a separate send.
- 3. Add a minimum-spend and "while supplies last" line right beside the gift, so the fine print doesn't read as buried.
- 4. Give each section a specific button. Use "Shop Sephora Collection 30% off" and "Get my gift" in place of repeated "Shop Now" and "Learn more".
- 5. Slow the cadence or vary the creative. Five near-identical sale emails in a week trains people to skip the next one.
