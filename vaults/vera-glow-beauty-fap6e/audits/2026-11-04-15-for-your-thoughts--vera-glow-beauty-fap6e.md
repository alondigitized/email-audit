# $15 for your thoughts
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a survey email dressed up as a catch-up. $15 for five minutes is a fair trade, and it's the first ILIA send in a while that isn't a tutorial or a shade lesson. I did the math right away: $15 is about 25% off a $60 skin tint, and ILIA's free shipping only kicks in at $50. So the credit alone doesn't get me free shipping.
- The hero is lovely. Two faces pressed together, and "Let's Catch Up" is big and warm. But there's no product, no launch, and nothing from the Black Friday calendar. After the 11/3 "1, 2, or 3 sweeps?" blush send and the 11/2 "[YOUR SHADE HERE]" email, I'd have liked a reason to shop. Instead I get homework.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because $15 is $15 and I'm an Ulta Diamond who loves a credit. But I'd want to see terms first, and the only terms are a tiny "*Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details" link at the bottom. I'd mark it read and save the survey for when I'm bored. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The offer is plain: "5-minute survey, $15 site credit." No code and no tiers.
- The "GET STARTED →" button is big, dusty rose, and impossible to miss.
- The survey promise ("deliver more of what you're into") hints at shade and skin-type personalization, which is what I want from a brand.
- The VIP, Personalized Shade Match, and Auto-Replenish icons at the bottom are useful loyalty cues.
- The hero is clean and has no layout bugs.

## What's weak

- "Get Started" is a vague CTA. "Take the 5-min survey" or "Claim my $15" would tell me what happens next.
- Nothing is shoppable. Sephora's 10% + free gift email on the same day at least gave me a lip lookbook to browse.
- The credit's terms are hidden: minimum spend, expiry, and whether it stacks with VIP aren't stated. I can't do my points math.
- The second block (ILIA "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better") is a big empty rose box that repeats the logo. It adds nothing, and it pushes the footer further down.
- "Let's Catch Up" is generic. It doesn't mention I'm a subscriber or a past customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the money in the hero: "Take a 5-min survey, get $15." Right now the offer sits below the fold of the image.
- 2. State the credit's terms next to the button: expiry date, minimum, and VIP stacking.
- 3. Replace the empty logo block with 3-4 bestsellers, like Super Serum Skin Tint or the blush from 11/3, so the credit has somewhere to go.
- 4. Change the CTA to something specific.
- 5. Add urgency, such as "credit valid through Black Friday," so it ties to the event calendar.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tell us your shade, get $15`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A quick survey, a $15 site credit, and better shade picks for you.`
