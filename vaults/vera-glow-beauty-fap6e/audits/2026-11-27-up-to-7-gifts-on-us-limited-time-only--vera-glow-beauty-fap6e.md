# Up to 7 gifts on us 🎁 Limited time only!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the twelfth e.l.f. email in about eleven days, and I'm doing the cadence math again. The 11/24 "Get up to 7 gifts on us!" send promised seven gifts and delivered one. This one has nearly the same subject and the same "YOU ARE SO GIFTED" hero. It is a recycled send with a TikTok live block added.
- The offer is clear once I read it. Spend $35+ and get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Glimmer plus an e.l.f.ssentials pouch, with free shipping. The Beauty Squad "100 points off every product reward" block is the part I actually care about. I'd click that one before the hero. I'd scroll it, do my points math, and probably not buy today, because I'm saving my Rouge and Diamond budget for Sephora's 50% off.
- Would I forward it? No. Would I click? Maybe, but only the Beauty Squad tile.

## What's working

- The Beauty Squad tile ("100 points off every product reward") is the only loyalty content I saw. It's the one place my points-math brain gets something to chew on.
- The $35+ threshold with a named gift is specific, and the handwritten-note styling over the pine branches feels festive.
- The "Top Picks for You" row has real prices ($32, $10, $15), so I can tell what I'm buying without clicking.
- The black, pink and gold palette is on brand and easy to scan. The hero has one big headline and one white button.
- The TikTok live block gives a real time window (11/27, 2-8PM PT) and a surprise Lip Kit Drop. That's a launch-day hook.

## What's weak

- "Up to 7 gifts" in the subject is misleading when the body shows one gift. That's the same bait-and-switch I flagged on 11/24.
- There are too many competing blocks: the hero, a second "SHOP NOW" gift block, Beauty Squad, TikTok and the quiz. That's five CTAs before any product. The quiz also repeats the Holiday Gift Finder from 11/23.
- The "NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR" category bars are giant grey slabs. They eat scroll space and say nothing.
- The "Top Picks for You" row doesn't look personalized to me. It's a generic Lip Oil Kit, a dewy kit and a travel trio, with no shade or skin-tone signal.
- The fine print is tiny, and the offer's real conditions ("while supplies last," a minimum purchase for the points redemption) are buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the body. Name the one real gift and drop "up to 7."
- 2. Cut the email to the hero, the Beauty Squad points tile, one product row and the TikTok drop. Remove the quiz and the grey category bars.
- 3. Put the Beauty Squad math in the hero: "100 points = $X off" with my current balance. Sephora puts Beauty Insider tier status up top, and e.l.f. should too.
- 4. Personalize the product row by shade or by what I've browsed.
- 5. Move the real conditions up next to the CTA: minimum spend, supplies, and the 11/29 end date.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard table-based template and has no visible structural breakage. The QA run had a 33% pass rate, and both failures are link probes that errored. Neither failure is confirmed as a real defect. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe error 1, relative redirect target.** The link "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/G8iQ2lgQ…`) failed with `unknown url type: '/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount'`.
  - The redirect resolved to a relative `Location` (`/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`), which the probe couldn't follow.
  - The destination is a login-gated account page. A recipient who isn't signed in lands on a login wall, not on the rewards feature.
  - I can't tell from the data whether a browser would resolve this correctly. This needs a manual click-through.
- **Probe error 2, non-ASCII in redirect.** The link "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/yIffVMqs…`) failed with `'ascii' codec can't encode characters in position 125-127`.
  - The three-character span suggests an unencoded multi-byte character (for example ™, ®, or an emoji) in the redirect target.
  - This is most likely a limitation of the probe's HTTP client. If the `Location` header really contains raw non-ASCII, it is technically malformed, though browsers usually tolerate it. The data doesn't show which.
- **Click wrapping.** All sampled links go through the opaque tracker `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/…`. The final destinations can't be read from the HTML.
- **Probe coverage.** Only 12 of 41 links were probed, so 29 are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title></title>`, which is a minor accessibility and clipping issue.
- **Missing alt text.** One image has no `alt`: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/FKtnO_iruo7pI5P8NGNVHQ~~/AAQRxRA~/F8TPUaiR…`. The `/q/` path suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` to it.
- **Tiny mobile heading.** The `@media (max-width:660px)` rule `.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}` forces an `h1` to 9px on mobile. That is below readable size and is a real accessibility concern, whether intentional or a template error.
- **Plain-text part.** The plain-text part is 11,910 of 14,124 characters URLs (84%). The cause is the long tracking tokens, but it adds spam-filter risk.
- **No issues found** for `lang="en"`, the viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, MSO conditional comments, or the hidden preheader.
- Each image in the visible source has `alt`, and the one logo checked is `alt="e.l.f."`. The rest of the markup is truncated and can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The source is truncated, so this covers only the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** Neither header was found, but the QA output says they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA output attributes this to the relay.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** The unsubscribe link is probed, but the truncated source doesn't show the footer. The physical-address requirement can't be verified here.
- The sender domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the click-tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` are different subdomains of the same brand domain, which is fine.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters.** They can't be verified because tracker wrapping hides the destination query strings, and probe results for resolved URLs weren't provided.
- **Landing-page alignment.** The "Receipt scanning" CTA leads to a login-gated page, as in section 2. That is the one confirmed continuity concern.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Click through the "Receipt scanning" link in a real browser. If it lands on a login wall, point it to a logged-out landing page or a deep link that handles authentication.
2. Make redirect `Location` headers absolute and percent-encode any non-ASCII characters (for example ™ or emoji) in the destination URLs. Also make the probe tolerate non-ASCII (IRI-to-URI conversion) and relative redirects, so these two failures stop reporting as link errors.
3. Check the raw message at the ESP for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. These are required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Fix or remove the 9px mobile `h1` rule on `.row-6`.
5. Add a meaningful `<title>` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. Re-run the probe on the 29 skipped links, and capture resolved destination URLs so UTM parameters can be audited.
