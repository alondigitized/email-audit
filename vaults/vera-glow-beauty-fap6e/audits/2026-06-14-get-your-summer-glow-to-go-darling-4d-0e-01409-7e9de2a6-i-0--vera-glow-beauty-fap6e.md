# Get Your Summer Glow To Go, Darling! 💗
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury is back in my inbox on June 14, and I've genuinely lost count. After the June 13 "A Free Gift, NEW! Bridal Boxes + So Much More!" send — which was already a kitchen-sink email trying to do everything at once — this one shows up the very next day with a nearly identical cocktail: a summer glow story, another Free Mini Magic Gift offer, and the ongoing UNREAL competition that's been cycling through their sends since at least June 9. That's back-to-back "free gift" energy with no cooldown, and I notice.
- The email itself is pretty. Warm rose and gold palette, a clean product-in-hand hero, a grid of genuinely summery skincare and glow items — Charlotte Tilbury's aesthetic is consistently beautiful and they don't slip on execution here. But "pretty" is table stakes at this send frequency. I need a reason to click, not just a reason to linger.
- The Free Mini Magic Gift offer is buried mid-scroll behind the product grid and the competition module. If CT had put that GWP in the hero — with a clear spend threshold front and center — this is a different email. As-is, I'm marking read and moving on. Not forwarding to a friend because I literally already texted her about the free gift offer yesterday.

## What's working

- The hero visual is clean and aspirational — product-in-hand against that warm blush palette reads "summer travel kit" without screaming it
- "Get Your Summer Glow To Go" gives the email an actual POV (the on-the-go travel angle is specific enough to feel like a real editorial theme)
- The Free Mini Magic Gift offer is present and visible, even if it's lower than it should be
- Charlotte Tilbury's brand voice stays consistent — "Darling" is their signature and it fits the overall warmth of the layout
- SHOP NOW button is visible and tappable; no render issues anywhere in the email

## What's weak

- Back-to-back free gift offers: yesterday was "A Free Gift, NEW! Bridal Boxes + So Much More," today is another GWP — the offer loses urgency when it's a standing Tuesday special
- The UNREAL competition module has now appeared in at least three sends this week; at this point it's wallpaper, not a hook
- "SHOP NOW" is the CTA — that's Ulta's generic button language, not where Charlotte Tilbury should be living
- Six products in a grid means CT wants me to do the work of deciding what this email is about; Glossier's "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" this week required zero cognitive overhead because they picked one story
- Zero time-bound urgency visible anywhere — nothing in the hero, nothing under the products — so there is no reason to act today versus next week

## Recommendations

- 1. **Promote the GWP to the hero.** "Free Mini Magic Gift when you spend $X — today only" as the headline is a stronger open than "Get Your Summer Glow To Go." That's the offer; everything else is context.
- 2. **Add a deadline, any deadline.** Even "this weekend only" reframes the whole email from browse-whenever to act-now.
- 3. **Give the UNREAL competition its own send or cut it from the rotation.** It has appeared enough times this week that it no longer creates excitement — either make it the hero of its own email or retire it from the sidebar.
- 4. **Rewrite the CTA.** "Get The Glow" or "Build My Summer Kit" lives in CT's world. "SHOP NOW" is what Ulta puts on emails they didn't think hard about.
- 5. **Cut to one hero product.** Lead with whatever single product defines the summer glow story — Charlotte's Magic Cream travel size, a highlighter, something — and let the grid be the secondary layer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free mini gift is still here, Darling ✨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer glow, on the go — free gift inside 💗`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $X, get a free mini Magic Gift. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Travel-ready glow, one tap away — plus your free gift`
