# Early Access: Up To 45% Off Beauty Kits In The App! 🤩💫
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay. Nine. This is Charlotte Tilbury email number nine (at minimum) since June 11, and I say that with receipts because I've been tracking every single "Unreal" and "Glaze It" and "Longest Day" send this month. So context matters here: going into this one, my thumb was already hovering over the unsubscribe. And then I see 45% off Iconic Kits — same number as the June 22 "Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW!" send — and my first reaction is... wait, didn't I already get this?
- The twist is the App Early Access framing. CT is dangling the offer as an app-exclusive, which is genuinely smart loyalty mechanic on paper. The 3 Free Full-Size Gifts section is a nice add I didn't expect. But the moment I register that the primary CTA is **Download the App** — not "Shop Now," not "Claim Your 45% Off," but *download an app I may or may not already have* — I feel the friction wall go up. Sephora does early access on both app and web simultaneously. Glossier's "Final Hours" send on June 20 took me directly to the sale in one tap. CT is asking me to gate-check before I can even see the offer.
- Mark as read, probably. Won't forward. If I'm being honest, I'd only click if I genuinely needed a kit and was already at a rebuy moment — which today I'm not.

## What's working

- **45% off is the biggest number CT has put in a hero this month** — the June 22 send used the same percentage but buried it in a launch-sale hybrid; here it's the entire point of the email, and that focus helps.
- **"3 Free Full-Size Gifts" section lands as a genuine value stacker** — this is a loyalty reward on top of a discount and it's visually separated so I actually registered it.
- **Warm coral-pink color palette is immediately recognizable** — no guessing what brand this is, CT's visual identity is airtight.
- **Product grid shows actual kit imagery** — I can see what I'm buying rather than vague "summer essentials" language.
- **"Early Access" framing rewards the subscriber relationship**, at least in theory — it reads like a perk, not just another mass blast.

## What's weak

- **"Download the App" as the primary CTA is a conversion killer** — I might already have the app. I might be on desktop. Either way, this adds a step between me and the 45% off, and that friction is unearned.
- **This is email nine in roughly two weeks** — cadence fatigue is real, and "Early Access" doesn't feel exclusive when I've already opened eight CT emails this month.
- **The app-exclusive framing subtly reads as punishing non-app users** — which includes a meaningful chunk of their subscriber list and feels a little bait-and-switch for email recipients who aren't going to pivot to mobile just for this.
- **Fine print at the bottom is dense** — the deal exclusions are buried in a wall of tiny text, and after nine CT emails I'm conditioned to check the asterisks.
- **No specific kit hero** — the product grid shows several items but none of them is *the* featured kit, so there's nothing to get excited about specifically. ILIA's "Skincare Mode: Summer" at least gave me one anchor product to focus on.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the "Download the App" primary CTA or at minimum add a secondary "Shop on Web" link** — gating the deal behind an app install is the single biggest conversion drag in this email. Let me claim my 45% off wherever I am.
- 2. **Feature one hero kit at the top with its name and price** — give me something specific to covet. Right now the 45% off is a number without an object.
- 3. **Lean into the Early Access exclusivity with a countdown timer** — show me exactly how long I have before this goes public. That's the one lever that would actually make me move fast.
- 4. **Consolidate the 3 Free Gifts callout closer to the hero offer** — it's buried in the lower third and this is legitimately strong; it should be paired with the 45% headline.
- 5. **Acknowledge the cadence by making this feel like a moment** — something like "We saved this one for app members first" gives the fatigue-weary subscriber a reason to stay engaged instead of feeling drowned.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Early Access to 45% Off Iconic Kits Starts Now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 3 free full-size gifts with your order — app members get first pick`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `45% off kits + 3 free gifts, before it opens to everyone`
