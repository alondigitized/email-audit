# Up to 50% off Early Black Friday^ deals starts NOW! (Terms & excl. apply) 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the pink "Early Black Friday: up to 50% off with new deals each week" hero is clear, and the weekly-drop framing is smart. Then I scroll and it turns into a 14-tile brand grid, each with its own "Ends Oct 31" badge. It's a catalog, not an email. I'm an Ulta Diamond, and the very top still says "join for free" under "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards®." That's the same stranger-targeted line that annoyed me on 10/7 ("Ends tonight: Take 20% off… when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards for free"). The "Rae," greeting at the top is nice. It doesn't make up for being pitched a membership I already have.
- I'd click, because I'm already cross-shopping BF. But I'd click a brand tile, not the hero. I wouldn't forward it. Ulta Diamond points math isn't anywhere in here, and I'd want that before I stock up.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read. "Up to 50% off" in big pink type with a "Shop now" button sits right where my eye lands.
- "This week's deals end Oct 31" gives me a real deadline and a reason to come back weekly.
- Each tile shows the discount and brand together: Tarte 30%, Clinique 25%, OPI bogo 50%, Benefit 30%, Tree Hut now $9.99 ea. That's scannable.
- The mass-prestige mix (Tarte, Milani, Kopari, Squishmallows) matches how I use Ulta's branded mass-prestige.
- The in-store events block with "Rae, you're invited!" and the TikTok Shop LIVE promo are fresh touches.

## What's weak

- "Join for free" and the "when you join" line are aimed at non-members, and I'm Diamond. It feels like I'm a stranger.
- Fourteen "Ends Oct 31" badges are repetitive and the page is very long. Every offer is equal weight, so nothing is the star.
- The tiles lack real prices, so I can't do my points math. There's also no Diamond multiplier or bonus-points callout anywhere.
- It's the same discount-grid format as Sephora's catalog sends. Sephora at least tucks in a single clear promo strip. Here the "up to" ranges hide what I'd actually save.
- The credit-card "free gift" section and the "Double the love" and app blocks pile on at the bottom, after the offer.
- Small type and "(Terms & excl. apply)" fine print make me suspicious about what's excluded.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment members away from the join copy. Replace it with a points-forward line like "Diamond: your points are worth more this week."
- 2. Cut the grid to 6 hero tiles and add one "top 3 deals" row, so the eye isn't spread over 14 brands.
- 3. Show a sample price or a points-earned callout on key tiles (e.g., "Clinique $X, earn Y pts").
- 4. Put a "new this week" label on tiles so the weekly-drop story is actually visible. A day-by-day teaser would build a cadence, the way Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty does.
- 5. Collapse the credit-card, app and event blocks into a single footer row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style send (`image.e.ulta.com`, `emltrk.com`) with Movable Ink dynamic images and 10 third-party Dotomi pixels. Automated QA found no hard failures, but header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. Many images have no alt text, and 10 pixels load over plain `http://`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The checker skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. The truncated HTML shows none of the anchor `href`s, so I can't confirm them either.
- **10 Dotomi pixels use `http://`.** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1` are cookie-sync calls to a third-party ad network.
  - Most clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS images (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection), so these likely never fire.
  - Where they do load, they add 10 extra third-party requests.
- **Some images appear twice.** `852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png` and `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` are each flagged twice. This may be intended reuse (such as a repeated spacer or divider), but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - Content-hosted images: `08964789-…png`, `414d3e76-…jpg`, `4f7c3146-…gif` (twice) and `852db034-…png` (twice), all under `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/…`.
  - Movable Ink images: `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png` and `3eb758650b65389e.png`. If these carry meaningful content, they are invisible to screen readers and to image-blocked clients.
  - Pixels: the 10 Dotomi pixels and the open pixel `ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=163148`. These should have `alt=""` plus zero dimensions so screen readers skip them.
- **XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype with unclosed void tags.** `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">`, `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" …>` and the Google Fonts `<link … rel="stylesheet">` are not self-closed, unlike the neighboring `<meta … />` tags. Clients tolerate this, so it's low severity.
- **Dead CSS.** The `<style>` block ships a large commented-out hamburger-menu section (`/* 91925 Commenting out hamburger menu … */`) to every recipient. Total HTML size isn't known from the truncated source, so I can't say whether this pushes it toward Gmail's ~102 KB clipping limit.

No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the available source or subject. The per-recipient identifiers in the Movable Ink and `emltrk` URLs (`mi_u=202740837`, `i=202740837`) are populated.
