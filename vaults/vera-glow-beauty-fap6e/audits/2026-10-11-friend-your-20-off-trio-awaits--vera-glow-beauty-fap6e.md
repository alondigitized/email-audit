# Friend, your 20% off trio awaits
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in three days for the same offer. There was "Add 3, subtract 20%" on 10/9, "Buy 3, save 20%—limited time only" on 10/10, and now this. It's also plain text. There's no hero, no image, no button, just a "Hi Friend" note from "Marissa (from ILIA)". The founder-note idea is a fine pattern change after two glossy sends. But the "Friend" greeting reads like a fallback for a missing first name, and it makes the warmth feel fake.
- The offer is still the cleanest part. BUNDLEUP gets me 20% off any three full-size products, and I can do that math in two seconds. But "it ends soon" is the only deadline. Ulta's "Ends tonight" 20% send at least gave me a clock. Drunk Elephant's "Kamo plus one" also gave me a visual to anchor on. Here I'm reading a wall of text with underlined links that don't look like buttons.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I already know the code from the last two emails, and I'd only click if I wanted the Skin Tint. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- BUNDLEUP is right there in the first few lines, and "any three full-size products" is easy to understand.
- The three picks are useful. Skin Tint SPF 40 with light-to-medium coverage and a mineral SPF, plus the dual-sided mascara wand, gave me real product detail, unlike Glossier's all-vibes sends.
- The plain founder-note format is a change of pace after the last three glossy emails, and it loads with no broken images.
- The "Easy as 1, 2, 3" close ties back to the "rule of threes" theme from 10/10.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is a button. The CTA is an underlined sentence, "Ready to save 20% on your own trio?", and the tap target is tiny on a phone.
- "Hi Friend" feels like a missing-name fallback. I'm a known subscriber, and it treats me like a stranger.
- "It ends soon" gives me no date. I can't plan a haul around a deadline I can't see.
- The asterisk and "See site for full promotional details" bury the exclusions. I can't tell if sets, minis or already-discounted items count.
- It's a third nudge on a 10/9 offer. Sephora's three-day catalog dump was annoying, and this is the same fatigue.
- There's no loyalty or tier angle and no price on any of the three products. I can't work out what a trio costs after 20%.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real button, "Shop your trio," and put a hard end date next to the code.
- 2. Show the three products as thumbnails with prices and a combined total before and after 20%. That's the math I'd do myself.
- 3. Fix the greeting. Use the first name when you have it, or drop the greeting.
- 4. Say what's excluded in one line instead of "see site."
- 5. Stop re-sending the same offer every day. Send one reminder in the last 24 hours with a real deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: 3 ILIA faves, 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Skin Tint + Gloss + Mascara = 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BUNDLEUP takes 20% off any 3 full-size picks. Ends Sunday at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our team's three most-reordered favorites, with the math done for you.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
Plain-text-style Klaviyo email with five tracked links and a tracking pixel. The QA run found no hard failures, but the captured data can't confirm unsubscribe headers, a physical address, or authentication. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so those items are unverified, not confirmed missing.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- All links use the redirect domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M4P4Y50H7VT17T5PCGGWRJH7_0` through `_4`. The final destinations can't be checked from the source.
- Links `_1` and `_3` both wrap the BUNDLEUP code text, and `_0` and `_2` both wrap the promo CTA. This is probably the same destination, so the click data will be split across them.
- Link `_4` ("See site") is the only path to the promo terms. The "*" asterisk disclaimer in the body has no matching footnote in the visible source.
- All anchors use `target="_blank"` with `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"`. No issue.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- QA WARN: the tracking image `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4P4Y50H7VT17T5PCGGWRJH7` has no alt text. It is an open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. An `alt=""` would be the correct form.
- The `<head>` contains only a Klaviyo `@import` of custom fonts, wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. There is no `<title>`, no `lang` attribute on `<html>`, and no viewport meta in the visible source. These could be real gaps or truncation artifacts.
- The font stack is `Karla, sans-serif` at 15px, with `font-size: 11pt` on the `<li>` elements. The unit mix is inconsistent. Karla loads only through `@import`, so clients that strip it fall back to `sans-serif`.
- The preheader text is "Buy 3 + save", followed by a long run of `&nbsp;` padding. The padding is correct.
- The layout is a plain `<p>` and `<ul>` with no `role` attributes, which is fine for accessibility. The footer table uses `align="left"` with an inline `background:#fff`, and the source is cut off there.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No issues found. "Hi Friend," renders as a fallback and the subject reads "Friend, your 20% off trio awaits". No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`) are visible.
- "Friend" in both places likely means the first-name field was empty for this subscriber and the default was used.

**5. Compliance**
- QA WARN: `List-Unsubscribe` header not found. The report says AgentMail may not capture it.
- QA WARN: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found, so one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- QA WARN: no physical mailing address detected (CAN-SPAM). The footer table is truncated in the source, so this needs checking against the full HTML.
- QA WARN: `Authentication-Results` header not found, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown. The sender `info@iliabeauty.com` and the tracking domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` are on the same organizational domain, which supports alignment.
- None of these four warnings is a confirmed violation. All depend on headers the relay may have dropped.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters can't be assessed. Every link goes through the Klaviyo-style redirect, and the destination URLs aren't exposed.
- The promo code BUNDLEUP appears as text only. It is not passed in a URL, so the customer has to enter it manually at checkout.
- Landing page alignment can't be verified without following the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Re-run the QA against the raw message, not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Check the full footer for the unsubscribe link and the physical address. Raise a compliance issue only if they're missing there too.
3. Resolve the five `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/...` redirects and record the final URLs and their UTM parameters.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Add `lang`, `<title>`, and a viewport meta to the template, if the full source lacks them.
6. Consider auto-applying BUNDLEUP through a URL parameter, so the code isn't a manual step.
