# Psst, something new just arrived 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the full body of the exact "Psst, something new just arrived" send I already flagged this morning at 7/10, and the full scroll doesn't change my read at all — Tower 28 Beauty Foundation with that before/after close-up is still the strongest hero on the page, and it's still buried under an App Fest banner and a 25% off Pureology/Too Faced/CLINIQUE strip that has nothing to do with "new arrivals." Scrolling further just confirms what I suspected from the top: this is a straight product-dump — Drunk Elephant, Chris McMillan, One/Size, Aestura, Dr. Groot, Dolce&Gabbana — six-plus brands stacked with zero curation logic beyond "new this week."
- What actually works for me down here that I couldn't see in the earlier crop: the "Earn Points Faster" section with 4X/5X on my Sephora Credit Card and Ulta Target Card cross-shop callout, plus the free Point of View sample GWP. That's real Beauty Insider math, and it's the first personalization signal in the whole email. But it's below eight other product blocks, which is way too much scrolling for a Rouge member who just wants to know what's new and what I'm earning.
- Compare this to Sephora x Olive Young from 8/20, which I scored a 9 — that one had a clear single-concept hero (K-beauty edit) all the way through. This one splinters across too many unrelated launches with the rewards math as an afterthought instead of up top where a Rouge member would actually stop scrolling. I'd skim to Tower 28, maybe screenshot the points multiplier, and move on — not a forward-to-a-friend, more of a "I'll check the app later."

## What's working

- Tower 28 before/after skin close-up is still the strongest visual on the page and it's genuinely new, non-comedogenic, sensitive-skin relevant to how I shop.
- "Only At Sephora" and green "Clean at Sephora" badges are stamped consistently across Drunk Elephant, Chris McMillan, One/Size, and Aestura — that's real curation/exclusivity signal.
- The rewards section at the bottom (4X points on Sephora Credit Card, 5X on Ulta Target Card, free Point of View sample with purchase) is exactly the tier-math content I look for as a Rouge/Diamond cross-shopper.
- Prices are visible on every single product tile — no "starting at" vagueness, I know exactly what Drunk Elephant Kaimo drops cost ($42) before I click.

## What's weak

- Eight-plus product sections in one scroll with no clear priority order — Dr. Groot scalp spray and D&G perfume next to skincare next to makeup, it reads like a catalog dump, not a curated "new arrivals" story.
- The 25% off Pureology/Too Faced/CLINIQUE banner up top is a completely different promo mechanic competing with the "new arrivals" narrative — pick one story.
- Rewards/points math is the best content in the email and it's stuck at the very bottom after nine product blocks — total buried lede.
- No sense of urgency or exclusivity window on any of these "new" drops — nothing tells me if Tower 28 sells out or restocks, so no reason to act today vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Earn Points Faster / free sample GWP block up near the top, right under the hero — that's the section most likely to make a Rouge member actually click, and it's currently invisible unless you scroll past nine other sections.
- 2. Cut this to 3-4 hero launches max (Tower 28, one skincare, one fragrance) instead of nine brand blocks — Sephora x Olive Young worked because it stayed on one story; this one dilutes itself.
- 3. Drop or relocate the 25% off Pureology/Too Faced/CLINIQUE banner — it's a different offer type and it's competing with "new arrivals" messaging in the first scroll.
- 4. Add a restock/limited-quantity cue to at least the Tower 28 hero so there's a real reason to act now instead of "whenever."
