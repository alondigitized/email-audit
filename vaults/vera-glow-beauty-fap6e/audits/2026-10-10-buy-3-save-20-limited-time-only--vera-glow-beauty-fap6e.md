# Buy 3, save 20%—limited time only
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same offer as the 10/9 "Add 3, subtract 20%" send, resent less than 24 hours later with new creative. "Rule of threes" and "beauty math" are cute and the orange "buy 3 get 20% off" burst is clear. But I already did this math yesterday, and nothing has changed except the hero photo and a code swap. Yesterday's code was in the hero. Today's code, BUNDLEUP, is in the banner, the body and the bottom button.
- The body also does the thing I always ding: no prices anywhere. I get a skin tint, a mascara, a shadow stick and a lip crayon, and I have no idea what a three-item cart costs. Sephora would at least show me the Rouge math. Ulta would put a price on every tile, and honestly that's the one thing it does right.
- Would I click? Maybe, because the skin tint at 20% off in a bundle is the one thing that pulls me. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it as read and keep yesterday's email as my reference.

## What's working

- The hero offer is huge and readable. "Rule of threes" plus the "buy 3 get 20% off" burst tells me the deal in two seconds.
- The code is repeated in the top banner, the body and the bottom CTA, so I won't lose it at checkout.
- The "prime picks" and "multiples" split is smart. Singles for people who want to build a set, and pre-built trios and sets for people who don't want to think.
- The footer trust cues are real: Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Personalized Shade Match, and Free US shipping over $50.
- The product photos are clean and the layout is easy to scan, with a clear hierarchy from hero to CTA to grid.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere. Drunk Elephant finally added price bubbles on 10/9 ($89/$135), and ILIA still makes me guess whether three items are $90 or $130.
- This is a repeat. The 10/9 send had the same 20% offer, and the 10/7 skin tint send was only three days ago. By the 10/5 "Need a new coat?" email I was already counting ILIA as a frequent sender, and this is the fifth in nine days.
- The "multiples" tiles don't tell me whether the trios and the Hero Set count as one product or three. If a trio counts as one item, the deal is much worse. That's the buried-fine-print feeling, and the footer's "see site for full promotional details" doesn't help.
- There's no loyalty angle at all. "Join ILIA VIP" sits in the footer, but I get nothing for being a member. Sephora Insider at least had Huda 4X points, and I can do points math on that.
- The "shop now" buttons on the grid are thin outlined boxes that feel weak next to the maroon CTA. The 20%-off "ends 10/12" deadline is only in the tiny footer text, so the urgency is hidden.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices or a sample bundle to the hero or right under it, for example "Skin tint + mascara + shadow stick = $X, you pay $Y." That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Put the deadline (ends 10/12) in the hero next to the burst instead of the footer.
- 3. Say plainly whether sets and trios count toward the three items.
- 4. Give VIP members something extra, like earlier access or stacked points.
- 5. Don't resend the same offer within 24 hours. Space it out or change the angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 ILIA faves, 20% off — ends 10/12`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Build your bundle: skin tint + 2 more, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code BUNDLEUP works on any 3, including the skin tint. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your trio, see your total, and save 20% at checkout.`
