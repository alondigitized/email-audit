### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Klaviyo-sent XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with Outlook MSO conditionals and a mobile-responsive media query block. One non-standard tracking element in the body and two accessibility concerns in the preheader; the truncated source prevents full compliance and UTM verification.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Non-standard open-track element.**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KXHGKMY91JFT0GW963NMKAF2_0">
```
`rel="stylesheet"` is invalid on a `<span>` and will not load CSS in any email client. This appears to be Klaviyo's open-tracking request disguised as a stylesheet call — but the mechanism is non-standard and may be stripped or blocked by clients (notably Apple Mail's proxy, which pre-fetches links and would record a false open regardless).

**Correct approach:** use a 1×1 `<img>` tracking pixel, which is the ESP-standard open-tracking mechanism and passes through Klaviyo's normal engagement attribution.

**Click tracking domain:** `ctrk.klclick.com` — expected Klaviyo infrastructure. No issue.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Preheader spacer characters may be announced by screen readers.**
The hidden preheader span uses two distinct invisible characters as padding:

- `͏` — U+034F COMBINING GRAPHEME JOINER (repeated ~230×)
- `­` — U+00AD SOFT HYPHEN (repeated ~40×)

NVDA and JAWS have known edge-case behavior where soft hyphens (U+00AD) are read aloud as "hyphen." The CGJ (U+034F) is generally safe. Standard recommendation is to use `&zwnj;` (U+200C ZERO WIDTH NON-JOINER) instead of soft hyphens for inbox preview padding.

**Everything else looks correct:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, MSO conditionals for Outlook 2007–2019, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and `format-detection=telephone=no` are all present and properly formed.
