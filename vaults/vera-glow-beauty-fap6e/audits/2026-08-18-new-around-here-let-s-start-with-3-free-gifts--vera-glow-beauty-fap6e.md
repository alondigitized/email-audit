# New around here? Let’s start with 3 free gifts.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA's done this to me before — "A back to school special for educators + students" was plain black-text-on-white with zero art direction, and this is the same email again, just with a broken name token this time. "New around here, ? Welcome to the world of ILIA" — that comma-question-mark gap is a merge field that flat-out failed to fire, and for a brand I've watched nail actual personalization ("For your Green eyes" on 8/14 was genuinely great), seeing them botch the *most basic* first-name insert is embarrassing.
- The offer itself isn't bad — a free 3-piece Beauty of Clean set with a $50 order is a real welcome incentive, and I clocked "Sephora's #1 mascara" as a legit trust cue since I'm Rouge tier and that means something to me. But there's no hero image, no logo, no product photography, no button — just a wall of text with one blue hyperlink. Sally Beauty's "Buy 2 Get 1" grid I've seen nine times this month is repetitive, but at least it *looks* like an email. This looks like it got stuck in dark-mode purgatory again.
- I wouldn't click past the one link out of curiosity more than urgency, and I definitely wouldn't forward this to a friend — there's nothing forward-worthy here, it's not even confirmed to be for me since the name token broke. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The gift-with-purchase mechanic (3 free minis with $50+ order) is a concrete, specific offer I can do the points/value math on.
- Calling out "Sephora's #1 mascara" is a smart trust signal for someone like me who cross-shops both retailers.
- Ingredient/formula framing ("skincare-powered formulas made for sensitive skin") speaks to a real purchase consideration, not just hype.
- Fine print is upfront and honest ("While supplies last. US only. New customers only.") rather than buried.

## What's weak

- The personalization token straight-up broke — "New around here, ? Welcome" — worse than a generic greeting, it looks glitchy and unfinished.
- Zero visual hierarchy: no hero image, no logo, no product shots of the actual gift set, just paragraphs of black text.
- No button — the only CTA is one small blue hyperlink buried mid-sentence, easy to miss scrolling on mobile.
- This is a new-customer-only offer sent to what should be my known inbox — if I'm already a subscriber, this reads like it wasn't even meant for me.
- Compared to ILIA's own 8/14 "For your Green eyes" send, which had real visual personalization, this is a huge step backward in execution.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken merge token immediately — a visibly failed personalization field is worse than no personalization at all; test the "New around here" fallback for missing first names before this goes out again.
- 2. Add an actual hero image of the 3-piece gift set — I have no idea what "Beauty of Clean Makeup Set" looks like, and product photography is table stakes.
- 3. Turn the hyperlink into a real CTA button ("Claim My 3 Free Gifts") so there's something to visually anchor and tap on mobile.
- 4. Segment this so existing subscribers don't get a "new around here" welcome email — it undercuts trust when the brand doesn't seem to know who I am.

- **Subject Alt B:** `We're gifting you a Beauty of Clean set (new here?)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Multi-Stick, Lip Sketch, and our #1 mascara — yours free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A clean beauty starter kit, on us, for a couple days only.`
