# Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the Daily Deals calendar dropped into hair — up to 50% off hair faves, free same-day delivery on top, today-only urgency — and I'll admit the mechanic itself is solid Sephora inventory clearance, but visually this is one of the busiest, most cluttered sends I've logged from them, and that's saying something after the "Only At Sephora" hero banners from 9/21. There's a full markdown calendar grid (9/23 through 10/4) crammed into the middle of this email showing me what's on sale every single day this week, which is genuinely useful for someone who does the math like I do, but it's presented like a spreadsheet, not a marketing email.
- The tier program is completely absent here — no Rouge callout, no points multiplier, nothing that acknowledges I'm Rouge and I check for this every time. Compare that to their own 9/22 "4X points on fragrance" send, which at least had a mechanic built around loyalty status — this one's just a generic markdown wall.
- I'd click through on the K18 or Olaplex value-size cards since those are real, named, in-demand products at legit discounts, but I'm not forwarding this — it's too cluttered to hand to a friend as "look at this deal," and the calendar grid needs a screenshot crop just to be legible.

## What's working

- "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" plus free same-day delivery stacked as two offers is a genuine value combo, not just filler.
- Named product markdowns (Vegamour serum $64→$44.80, Shark Beauty Flexstyle $299→$209.30, Amika Kure mask $44→$22) give me real numbers to do math against.
- The value-size hair care section (Olaplex No.4, K18, Ouai, Amika) with visible strikethrough pricing and "value" call-outs is exactly the kind of stock-up section I shop.
- "Today Only" badge on the hero creates credible urgency since the deal window is dated (ends 10/7).

## What's weak

- Zero Rouge/tier acknowledgment anywhere — no points multiplier, no "as a Rouge member" language, nothing that says they know I'm their top-tier shopper.
- The 12-day markdown calendar grid is visually overwhelming — tiny product thumbnails in a dense table is not mobile-skimmable, I'd have to zoom to read any of it.
- The email just keeps going — hair deals, then value-size hair, then "Spot It Shop It" fragrance/holiday teasers, then value sets, then Beauty Insider tier bar — it's four emails stapled together.
- "§§2" and superscript symbols next to the 20% off value-size header with no visible footnote nearby reads like buried fine print, which the rubric specifically dings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the giant markdown calendar grid or move it to a landing page — link "See All Deals" instead of cramming 12 days of thumbnails into the email body. This is the single biggest fix; it's actively hurting scannability right now.
- 2. Add a Rouge-specific line — even "Rouge members, these markdowns stack with your birthday gift" — since Sephora has all this tier data and isn't using it here.
- 3. Trim the email to one focused theme (hair deals) and cut the fragrance/holiday/value-set modules to a separate send — this feels like three campaigns merged into one.
- 4. Clarify or remove the "§§2" footnote marker near the 20% off value-size header — floating symbols with no near-by asterisk explanation read like bait-and-switch.
