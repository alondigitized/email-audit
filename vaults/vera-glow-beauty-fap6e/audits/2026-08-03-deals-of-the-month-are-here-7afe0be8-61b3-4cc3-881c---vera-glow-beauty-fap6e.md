# Deals of the Month Are Here ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Sally's back in my inbox for the fifth time in about a week, and this time COLORfest has actually launched — "Welcome to your best color era" with real products and real discounts instead of the confetti teaser they sent me on 7/30. That's progress, but I'm still standing here asking why I'm getting hit with $5-off-$20 hair-color math, a Wella BOGO, and a Sally Rewards pitch on the same day I'm tracking my Sephora Rouge points and deciding whether my Ulta 21 Days haul justifies itself. This isn't my lane — I'm buying serums and setting sprays, not developer and toner shades.
- The execution is honestly fine — clean grid, a pretty model shot, a legible "Shop COLORfest" button up top — but it's Sally running the exact same three-offers-stacked-on-one-email pattern (percentage off, BOGO, rewards teaser) I've seen from them every single send this cycle. Compare that to Sephora's actual Beauty Insider Celebration email (8/1) that led with one big gold badge and one clear campaign — Sally is throwing three different math problems at me before I've even scrolled past the hero.
- I'm not clicking. This reads as mass-hair-supply noise next to my Sephora/Ulta rotation, and it's not going to a friend — I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The COLORfest hero is legible at a glance — "Welcome to your best color era" plus one clean "Shop COLORfest" button, no clutter up top.
- The model shot is genuinely pretty and on-brand for a color-focused campaign, not some stock-photo throwaway.
- Sally Rewards' "Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September" is at least concrete math, which I always appreciate — I do this exact calculation for Sephora and Ulta constantly.
- Klarna financing badge is a nice touch for the bigger hair-color hauls, even if it's not relevant to my basket size.

## What's weak

- Three different offer mechanics crammed into one email — $5 off $20, buy-two-get-one-free Wella, up to 40% off styling tools, plus the rewards pitch — is the same offer-stacking confusion Sally ran on 7/29's rewards email, and it makes me trust none of them fully.
- This is hair color and hair tools, not skin/makeup/fragrance — nothing here touches the prestige categories I actually stock up on at Sephora's Beauty Insider events.
- Zero personalization — no name, no "based on your last purchase," nothing tying this to me specifically, unlike Ulta's "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" subject lines that at least attempt segmentation.
- No urgency or end date anywhere — "Deals of the Month" just floats there with no deadline, so there's zero reason to act today versus next week.
- This is the fifth Sally send in about a week (7/27 curls, 7/29 rewards, 7/30 COLORfest teaser, 7/31 delivery, now this) — the cadence is genuinely fatiguing at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one offer and lead with it.** Sephora's Celebration email worked because it was one badge, one campaign, one clear ask. Sally should headline the strongest deal (probably the Wella BOGO — real savings) and demote the rest to a footer strip.
- 2. **Add urgency with a real date.** "Deals of the Month" needs an actual end date in the hero, not buried in the fine print at the bottom.
- 3. **Segment this to actual hair-color shoppers.** If I've never bought hair dye or tools from Sally, don't lead with COLORfest — show me something from my actual purchase history, the way ILIA's ingredient-deep-dive at least ties back to product category.
- 4. **Slow the cadence.** Five emails in a week is Skechers-retargeting territory, not "trusted beauty brand" territory — space these out or consolidate the rewards/COLORfest/deals threads into one weekly send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $5 hair-care reward is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `COLORfest deals + your Sally Rewards balance, inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save on Wella, styling tools, and more through 8/31`
