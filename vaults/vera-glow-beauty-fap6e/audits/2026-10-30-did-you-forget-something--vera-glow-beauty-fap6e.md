# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the eleventh "forgot something?" email I've gotten from Skechers, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. Yesterday's 10/29 send used this exact subject line, "Did you forget something?", so now they're repeating themselves as well as faking the cart. The gold "Ready to check out?" hero is pretty, and the Día de los Muertos skull UNO sneaker is a fun seasonal pick. But the black "Complete your order" button promises an order I never started.
- The real news is buried at the bottom: 20% off for Skechers Plus members, valid through 11/2. Sephora put a giant "30% OFF" medallion on Sephora Collection today, dated 10/30–11/19. Ulta at least leads with a number, even if it's the rewards pitch. Here I scroll past about six shoes to find the discount. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and move on.

## What's working

- The "Ready to check out?" hero is bold and readable in gold foil, and the orange "Complete your order" button is easy to tap.
- The skull UNO sneaker is a seasonal, fun hero product, and the "UNO - Alma De Guerrero" caption names it.
- The 20% off Plus member banner is clear and high-contrast once I reach it, and it gives a real date (valid thru 11/2).
- The six women's styles in glitter, pink and blue look current. They're clean, and the names and "Leather" callouts are readable.
- The gift-card-free layout is simple: nav rows for Women, Men and Kids, plus app, curbside and Afterpay/Klarna icons. Nothing is broken.

## What's weak

- The cart premise is false. "Complete your order" with nothing in my cart feels like a trick, and "No tricks, just treats!" is ironic given that.
- The offer sits below about two screens of product. The hero doesn't mention 20% at all.
- The discount is only for Skechers Plus members. The fine print says I must be logged in, and it excludes sale items, new releases and accessories. That undercuts the "just treats" framing.
- This is the same subject as 10/29 and the same fake-cart beat as 10/23, 10/26 and 10/28. Glossier at least rotated its gift offers, and this doesn't.
- There's nothing for me as a beauty shopper. No personalization and no points math, so I can't tell what Plus earns me or what my tier is worth.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with the real offer: "20% off for Plus members, through 11/2". Drop the fake cart framing unless a cart actually exists. Sephora's 30% medallion is the model here.
- 2. Put a single, specific CTA on the hero, such as "Shop the Day of the Dead UNO", and cut the duplicate "Complete your order" buttons.
- 3. Tell me my Plus status or give me a join prompt, with the points or savings math. That's what keeps me engaged as a loyalty shopper.
- 4. Move the fine print summary up to a short line under the banner: "Login required, select styles."
- 5. Cap the cart-reminder cadence and vary the creative. Eleven sends in two weeks is training me to ignore them.
