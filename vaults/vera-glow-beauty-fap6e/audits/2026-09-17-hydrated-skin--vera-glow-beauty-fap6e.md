- Okay, this is the actual product email that "Hydrated skin" — 5/10 baseline in my history — should've been all along, and it's a nice change of pace from the DRUNKVIP20 fatigue I've been logging all month. No fake urgency, no "secret sale" bit — just B-Hydra Intensive Hydration Serum, laid out with ingredient call-outs and a legit before/after stat block ("97% agreed skin felt smoother"). The neon-yellow "welcome to the club / 15% off" banner up top feels bolted on, like two different emails got mail-merged together, but once you scroll past it this is genuinely one of the more competent skincare product breakdowns I've gotten this week.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the ingredient trio (Pro-Vitamin B5, Sodium Hyaluronate, Pineapple Extract) with the little swatch photos is exactly the kind of "what am I actually paying for" info I want before I add a $48 serum to cart. ## What's working

- The "PLEASE HYDRATE RESPONSIBLY" headline is a decent wink without being try-hard, and it's paired with a clean product shot on a two-tone background.
- Ingredient breakdown with macro swatch photos (the oil droplet, the powder, the pineapple gel) is the most informative thing Drunk Elephant's sent me in weeks — way more useful than another DRUNKVIP20 banner.
- The stat block (97%/93%/93%) with the sample-size disclosure at the bottom is honest enough that I don't feel like I'm being sold junk science.
- "B-Hydra plays well with others" regimen-stacking section respects that I already have other DE products and want to know what to layer, not just what to buy standalone.
- The $100+ spend threshold for deluxe samples at the bottom is a real loyalty-shopper mechanic, not just another blanket discount code.

## What's weak

- That top yellow "15% off club" banner feels like a leftover from a different campaign — no explanation of what "the club" even is, no tier language, and it visually clashes hard with the clean pastel product photography below it.
- Zero Beauty Insider / Rouge tier math anywhere — I'm cross-shopping Sephora and Ulta events specifically for points multipliers, and a DTC brand like Drunk Elephant giving me nothing on loyalty stacking is a missed beat, especially compared to Sephora's "4X Points" framing I saw yesterday.
- No shade/skin-tone or skin-type personalization — this is a blanket "everyone is dehydrated" pitch, no "for your skin type" segmentation.
- The hero model shot only shows up mid-email, way after the stat block, so the eye has nowhere warm to land early on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut or completely reskin the yellow "15% off club" banner — right now it reads like a template leftover bolted onto a serum launch email; either integrate it into the hydration story or drop it entirely.
- 2. Move the demographic/model photo with the stat callouts higher, right under the hero, so there's a face and a number above the fold instead of just product-on-seamless.
- 3. Add one line connecting this to any loyalty mechanic — even something simple like "Rewards members get 2x points on B-Hydra this week" would close the gap with what Sephora and Ulta are doing in my inbox.
- 4. Give the $100+ deluxe sample offer its own subject-line moment in a future send — it's buried at the very bottom and it's honestly a stronger hook than "hydrated skin."
