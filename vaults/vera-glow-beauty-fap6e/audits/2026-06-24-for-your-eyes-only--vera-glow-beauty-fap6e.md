# For your eyes only
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- So. The sale that was supposed to end June 19 — the one Glossier sent three urgency emails about in a single day — is apparently back? Or never actually ended? Because the offer I'm looking at right now on June 24 is 20% off sitewide or 25% off $100+, which is verbatim what they were calling "Final Hours" five days ago. The "NOT FOR YOU" headline twist is genuinely clever copy — spy-drop energy, implying this is too exclusive for the masses — but the offer underneath is the exact discount I could have gotten before the fake deadline expired. I've been tracking this sale like I track my Rouge points, and that kind of manufactured exclusivity doesn't land when you have receipts.
- That said, I'll give credit where it's due: the subject line is actually working. "For your eyes only" is short, intriguing, no spam noise, and it made me open. The personalized code angle in the body is smart — it adds a real segmentation signal, even if the deal itself isn't truly unique. The product selection is strong Glossier DNA: Gloss Shot, Lash Slick, Glossier You, Boy Brow — this isn't filler, this is their actual portfolio hitting the right notes for someone like me.
- Would I click? Probably — but only because I've been debating the Gloss Shot restock anyway and a code in hand is a code in hand. Would I forward it to a friend? No, because I'd have to explain why the sale that ended June 19 is still going and that conversation is exhausting.

## What's working

- **The subject is the best thing in this email.** "For your eyes only" is 18 characters, zero spam signals, high intrigue — it punched through a crowded inbox without screaming.
- **"NOT FOR YOU" hook copy is clever** — the spy-exclusive framing sets up the personalized discount code in a way that actually makes sense thematically.
- **Product grid is genuine Glossier.** Gloss Shot, Lash Slick, Boy Brow, Glossier You — these are the hero SKUs that people actually buy, not random clearance filler.
- **Clean render, no bugs.** The two-column grid is crisp, the lifestyle shots are on-brand, the close-up Lash Slick model is gorgeous.
- **Personalized discount code.** A code specific to this send is a real touch — Charlotte Tilbury did something similar with their App early access on June 14, but at least here the code makes the "exclusive" promise feel less hollow.

## What's weak

- **The "exclusive" frame collapses if you've been paying attention.** This is the same 20/25% discount from the sale that had three urgent "ends tonight" emails on June 19. The exclusivity is performed, not real, and any engaged subscriber knows it.
- **No deadline visible.** For a "special just-for-you" offer, there's no clear expiration — which weirdly undercuts urgency more than a fake deadline would.
- **"Shop now" CTA is doing no work.** Six instances of "Shop now" — no product-level specificity, no "Get Gloss Shot," no category anchor. Drunk Elephant's "Summer to go" email at least tied CTAs to a destination.
- **No loyalty signal anywhere.** I'm a repeat Glossier customer and there's zero acknowledgment of that. Sephora manages to mention Rouge tier status even in generic sends — Glossier just treats me like a stranger with a secret code.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the exclusivity real.** If this is a "for your eyes only" offer, the discount should be different — 30% off, early access to a product, a bonus gift. Right now the code is the only differentiator, and the underlying % is public history.
- 2. **Give the code a deadline.** "Valid through [DATE]" in the hero section, not buried in footer asterisks. The urgency that worked on June 19 ("ends tonight!") is completely absent here.
- 3. **Tie the CTAs to the products.** "Shop Gloss Shot" and "Shop Lash Slick" instead of six identical "Shop now" links — especially on mobile, a specific CTA converts better.
- 4. **Add a loyalty nod.** Even a single line — "For our most loyal customers" — turns this from a mass discount email into something that actually respects the relationship.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Just for you: 25% off your next Glossier order`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your secret: 25% off, yours until [DATE]`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A little thank-you for being a loyal customer — code inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `This offer isn't going to the masses. Just you.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid tracking beacon element**
The Klaviyo open-tracking beacon is injected as:
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KVVEWQ7X3ZP1A3A8MFMYAXN5_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This is a Klaviyo-generated workaround to load the beacon outside Outlook conditional blocks (`<!--<![endif]-->`). Browsers and email clients ignore the attributes, so no functional breakage occurs — but it is malformed HTML per XHTML 1.0 Transitional (the declared DOCTYPE), and may trigger strict HTML validators or spam-filter heuristics.

**Click-tracking domain:** `ctrk.klclick.com` — Klaviyo shared domain. No custom tracking domain (e.g., `click.glossier.com`) is in use, which reduces sender brand trust signals and makes DMARC alignment dependent on Klaviyo's infrastructure rather than Glossier's own domain.
