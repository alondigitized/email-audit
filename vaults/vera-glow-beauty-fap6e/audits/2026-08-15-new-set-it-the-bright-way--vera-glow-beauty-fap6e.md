# NEW! Set it the ✨bright✨ way
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "new" powder quad + velvet puff for $12 is an actual new launch — a nice break from e.l.f. recycling the Bubble hero shot four sends running (8/7, 8/11, 8/12, 8/13). The color-correct-and-brighten pitch is genuinely useful info, and I like that they show the shade wheel with little callout arrows explaining what each half does instead of just a product shot. But nothing here is speaking to ME specifically — no points balance, no "you're a Beauty Insider" nod, no shade-match to my undertone. It's a clean product launch email, not a loyalty email, and at $12 the price point tells me e.l.f. isn't even trying to compete on my Rouge/Diamond math, it's just impulse-add territory.
- I'd skim this, maybe screenshot the powder quad for myself since setting powder is a real category I shop, but I wouldn't forward it — there's no urgency or exclusivity angle that makes it share-worthy. Ulta's "20% off one item, ends tonight" send at least gives me a reason to act today; this just says "shop now" with no clock on it. Mark as read, maybe circle back if I'm restocking setting powder.

## What's working

- The "NEW" badge top-left is unmissable and the hero model's dewy, brightened skin actually sells the "brighten like a pro" claim visually.
- Shade-wheel diagram with "color-correcting shades" / "brightening shades" arrows is genuinely educational — better product storytelling than most of these mass brands bother with.
- $12 price is stated right in the hero copy, no hunting for it.
- The bonus GWP block ("Bronzing Drops on us," $50+ spend, no code) is a clear, honest mechanic — same clean structure e.l.f. ran on 8/13's Bronzing Drops send, so at least they're consistent on offer clarity.
- "Top Picks for You" section attempts personalization with a lip liner, primer, and setting spray grid.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty signal — no points balance, no tier status, nothing that says e.l.f. knows I'm a repeat shopper, unlike ILIA's "For your Green eyes" send which actually used my data.
- "Top Picks for You" is almost certainly not actually personalized to me — it's a generic lip liner/primer/setting spray trio with no explanation of why these were picked, which feels like a label slapped on a static grid.
- No urgency anywhere — no deadline on the launch, no countdown on the Bronzing Drops GWP beyond vague "limited time."
- The category nav (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) is just gray bars — visually flat and skippable, doesn't earn its real estate.
- Model diversity is one shade range (Halimotu wears Deep/Rich, Jhoemy wears Medium/Tan) but there's no shade-match tool or "find your match" CTA, so it's demographic signal without functional personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real loyalty hook — "e.l.f. Beauty Squad members get X" or points-per-dollar math on the $12 quad. This is the single biggest lift; right now it reads like a stranger's newsletter, not a repeat-customer email.
- 2. Put a deadline on the Bronzing Drops GWP — "ends Sunday" turns a nice-to-have into a reason to act now.
- 3. Make "Top Picks for You" actually feel personalized — reference past purchase category or browsing, not just a static 3-item grid.
- 4. Tighten the CTA stack — three "SHOP NOW" buttons before you even hit the GWP block is repetitive; vary the copy ("Shop the Quad," "Get the Glow").
- 5. Give the category nav bars visual weight (icons or thumbnails) so they don't read as a wall of gray filler before the footer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free full-size Bronzing Drops on $50+`
