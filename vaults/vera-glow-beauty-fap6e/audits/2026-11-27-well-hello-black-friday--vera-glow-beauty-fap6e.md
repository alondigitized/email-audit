# Well, hello, Black Friday!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Drunk Elephant email in about a week, and the first one that feels like the actual Black Friday send. "It's on! BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" is right at the top, so I get the offer in half a second. I'd already seen the same 30% on 11/25 and 11/26 under the "Cyber Week" label. This is the same sale in new wrapping, and I'm tired of it.
- The email stacks four offers: 30% off sitewide, a free full-size B-Goldi with $110+, a 20% code on Trunk 7.1, and "up to 40% off" last chance. The Trunk's "$340 / $733 value" is the loudest thing on the page, but it's the one I'd least want to do math on. The pile of fine print at the bottom reads like a legal exam. The kits grid at the end is the cleanest part, since it shows real prices and review stars.
- I'd click, mainly for the B-Goldi gift, which I'd count as about $39 of value. I wouldn't forward it. The Black Friday email I'd actually text a friend about is Sephora's.

## What's working

- The "BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" headline is huge and unmissable. It's clearer than the 11/25 "Up to 40% off" subject, which didn't match the hero.
- The free full-size B-Goldi at $110+ is a real gift-with-purchase I'd build a cart around. It's much better than Glossier's merch sale, where nothing earns me anything.
- The kit tiles show crossed-out prices, sale prices, star ratings and "738 people are checking this out." That gives me price and social proof together.
- The mint and neon-yellow blocks break up the scroll, and the pink gift box hero looks gifty and seasonal.
- "Free ground shipping with all orders" in the top banner removes a common reason to abandon a cart.

## What's weak

- The offers contradict each other. 30% off sitewide, 20% off with code DRUNK20, and up to 40% off all appear, and the footnotes use four asterisk levels to separate them. I can't tell what my actual price is without a spreadsheet.
- The Trunk 7.1 section says "Save $393 on 10 full-size favorites" next to a "$340 ($733 value)" badge. The 20% math doesn't obviously match, and the green duffel image dominates a section where the actual products are small.
- This is the same 30% I've gotten all week. There's no new reason to act now, and no deadline countdown beyond the buried 11/29 fine print.
- There's nothing for me as a tier member. Sephora and Ulta give me points or tier multipliers, and Drunk Elephant gives me nothing for being loyal.
- The links are tiny and plain ("SHOP NOW" in small underlined text), which is a tap-target problem on a phone. Nothing is personalized to my skin type or past orders.

## Recommendations

- 1. Collapse the offers into one headline and one code. Show the final price on each product, not "30% off everything, including our kits" next to stacked footnotes.
- 2. Put the real deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday 11/29, 11:59 PM ET," instead of burying it in the fine print.
- 3. Make the B-Goldi gift the lead for return buyers, and make its threshold clear: "Free $39 B-Goldi when you spend $110."
- 4. Add a loyalty or points line for members. Sephora's tier-style callouts are the benchmark.
- 5. Make the CTAs full-width buttons, and show more of the bestsellers (Protini, Lala Retro) with prices instead of the duffel.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off + free full-size B-Goldi (through Sunday)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday cart: 30% off, plus a $39 gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $110, get a free B-Goldi. Ends 11/29 at 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kits from $58, free ground shipping, and a gift with $110+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with third-party webfonts and 19 click-tracked links. Confirmed issues are limited to the recipient address in a tracking-pixel URL, non-HTTPS social icons, two images with no alt text, an invalid `@import` placement, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, and the QA run scored 57% with 0 failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **19 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Visible tracking domains:**
  - `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`
  - `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif`
- **Three tracking vendors are in play:** the sender domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, `everestengagement.com`, and `mi.drunkelephant.com`. This adds third-party dependencies, but no broken links are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif` is a tracking pixel.
  - `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…` is also a tracking pixel.
  - Both are likely 1×1 pixels, so the impact is low. They should still carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Non-HTTPS images (confirmed by QA):** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and the icons may fail to render.
- **Invalid `@import` placement (from source):**
  - The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule in the same `<style>` block.
  - CSS requires `@import` to precede all other rules, so clients that honor it will ignore these lines.
  - The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` are stripped by Gmail and other clients.
  - Brown Bold, Light and Thin will fall back to system fonts in many clients. The fallback stacks fall outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.
- **Zoom blocked (from source):** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **iOS text sizing (from source):** the `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule overrides the earlier `body` setting of `100%`. It stops text resizing in clients that honor it.
- **Stray HTML comments inside `<style>` (from source):** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->` sits inside the media query. It is harmless in most clients but is malformed CSS.
- **Font origins:** the webfonts come from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/...`. Both are third-party hosts, so a failed fetch means a fallback font.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email in a URL (confirmed by QA):** the tracking pixel URL contains `?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`. The email is resolved, with no unrendered tokens.
- **Why it matters:** a plaintext email address in a query string leaks to proxies, image caches and server logs. It is not a rendering defect.
- **`mi_u=98161986`:** this is a numeric subscriber ID on the `mi.drunkelephant.com` pixel. The subject line has no tokens.
- **Unrendered tokens:** none are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both were not found, per QA. The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them in the raw message on direct delivery.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing. The tool attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` should be checked in a direct-to-mailbox test.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the footer is outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign code visible:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfstarts_produc…`. It is truncated and follows the vendor's own `mi_` convention.
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the available data. Links route through `everestengagement.com`, and the QA tool skipped all 19 tracked links. I can't confirm whether `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` reach the destination, or whether the landing pages match the offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Change the three social icon `src` values from `http://` to `https://` on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Stop putting the raw email address in the pixel query string (`e=`). Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them in favor of `@font-face` and a robust fallback stack. Remove the `<!-- -->` comments inside the CSS.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Revisit `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
6. Rerun the QA against a raw message received directly, without the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment to `cheers.drunkelephant.com`.
7. Follow the 19 tracked links by hand or with a redirect-following probe. Verify final URLs, status codes and UTM carry-through.
