# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- I clocked the subject line before I even clicked — "This is perfect for you!" — and the first thing I thought was: *wait, didn't I already open this one?* I did. May 26th, same subject, same sender. I scored that send an 8. This one lands on June 29th, which puts it at email number nine or ten from Skechers in roughly six weeks, and the creative team has recycled the exact same subject line. That's not a test — that's a copy-paste.
- Inside the email it's the classic browse-abandon formula: one hero sneaker (the SKX Nexus in teal, cute actually), a "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" headline, a "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" CTA, ten rows of algorithmic shoe recs, and a 20% off banner buried below all of it. The bones are fine. The problem is I've seen this exact skeleton from Skechers at least four times in the last month. The cadence has fully eroded my ability to feel urgency.
- I would not click. I would not forward to a friend. I would mark it read and mentally log Skechers as "the brand that sends too many emails" — which, at this point, is the loudest thing they're communicating.

## What's working

- **The teal SKX Nexus hero is genuinely appealing.** Clean product shot, summer-ready colorway, and it stands out against the white background — gives me Lush's clean product-first aesthetic without the chaos.
- **20% off is a real offer.** It's concrete, it's legible, and "A SINGLE PAIR" keeps the fine print manageable. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off everything online⁺⁺ — Terms & excl. apply" hedging — this is actually more honest.
- **Afterpay and Klarna in the footer.** For a $65–$100 sneaker this matters, and it's smart to surface it without making it the centerpiece.
- **The algorithmic rec grid is well-formatted.** Two clean rows of five, product names labeled, no broken images, reasonable whitespace.

## What's weak

- **The subject line is a rerun.** "This is perfect for you!" was already sent on May 26th — I literally have it in my inbox above this one. At minimum test a different line. At worst this is a sign that no one is watching the sends calendar.
- **The 20% offer is buried below the fold.** On mobile, I'd have to scroll past the hero shoe AND the ten-product rec grid before I even know there's a discount. ILIA opens with its value prop in the hero; e.l.f. put "18 flexible shades" in the subject line. Skechers hides its best card in row three.
- **"STILL THINKING ABOUT IT?" + "YOU'VE GOT GREAT TASTE!" is very mid.** The compliment feels hollow by send number nine. Sally Beauty's "Last Chance: BOGO 50% Off Hair Care" names the offer in the headline — this email makes me guess.
- **Zero personalization visible beyond the implied browse data.** No name, no tier, no "you last looked at X." For a brand sending this volume, the least they can do is put my name on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Never recycle a subject line.** Pull the email calendar and confirm no duplicate subjects in any 90-day window. This single fix would have prevented the biggest credibility hit this email takes.
- 2. **Bring the 20% offer into the hero block.** "STILL THINKING ABOUT IT? Take 20% off before it's gone" — same abandon trigger energy, now with a reason to act. The discount should be visible without a single scroll on mobile.
- 3. **Reduce send frequency or segment harder.** Nine emails in six weeks has crossed into noise. Cap the cadence at 2 sends/week max or split by purchase recency — reward active buyers, give the lapsed ones a rest.
- 4. **Name the product in the hero CTA.** "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK AT THE SKX NEXUS" is more compelling than a generic button.
- 5. **Surface Skechers Plus tier or exclusive member status** above the footer fine print if it's a member send — Sephora never buries the Insider perks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `SKX Nexus: 20% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `That teal sneaker. Still here. Now 20% off.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your browse, your discount — 20% off a single pair, no minimum.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The SKX Nexus isn't going to wait forever. Take 20% off now.`
