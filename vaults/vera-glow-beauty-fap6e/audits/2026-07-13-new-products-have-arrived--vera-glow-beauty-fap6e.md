# 🎉 New products have arrived
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay so "New products have arrived" is possibly the most vanilla subject line Sephora has sent me all month. After two back-to-back "Obsessed? Same." sends on the 12th — which actually had editorial personality — and "These picks are everywhere" on the 11th, this feels like someone hit the default template button. I almost didn't open it.
- But here's the thing: the body earned it back. "Take your PICK" as the hero concept is charming, the 50% off minis deal at the very top is genuinely useful to me (that's how I trial launches before committing full-size), and the FREE Rare Beauty Gift section with a clear "SHOP TO REDEEM" CTA is exactly the kind of loyalty math I'm always running as a Rouge member. The new product tiles with visible names and prices let me actually make a buying decision without clicking through blind.
- This is email eleven from Sephora in roughly twelve days. Eleven. The content is solid enough that I'd click — specifically on the Rare Beauty GWP and the minis deal — but I'd probably not forward it. Mark as read after clicking? Yes. Forward to a friend? Only if I could spot something in the product grid that my friend was specifically waiting for.

## What's working

- **50% off minis at the very top** — real savings, zero ambiguity, and it's how I actually shop new launches before going full-size. This is Rouge behavior and Sephora knows it.
- **FREE Rare Beauty Gift with "SHOP TO REDEEM"** — the CTA copy is specific enough that I know exactly what action to take, and Rare Beauty as the GWP brand is a credible draw. Not a random mascara from a brand I've never heard of.
- **Product tiles with visible names and prices** — I can do the math without clicking. That's rare. Compare to e.l.f.'s "Juicy NEW drip from Power Grip" email, which made me open and then hunt for pricing — Sephora surfaces it here.
- **APP EARLY ACCESS callout** — as someone who tracks launches, the early-access hook signals my tier actually matters. That's a loyalty signal I respond to.
- **Visual hierarchy holds** — eye goes: minis deal → hero → product grid → GWP. Clean top-to-bottom read.

## What's weak

- **"New products have arrived" is embarrassingly generic** — after the editorial streak this brand has been on, this is a step backward. Sally Beauty sent "Summer hair starts with a freebie" and earned an 8. Sephora has the budget and the brand equity to name the brand. Drop Rhode, Fenty, Rare Beauty in the subject and I'm opening faster.
- **No deadline visible anywhere** — the minis deal has no end date I can see. Urgency is zero. ILIA's editorial emails skip offers entirely and own it; Sephora is trying to run a promo without committing to one.
- **Eleven emails in twelve days is too many** — I'm not churning yet but I'm aware of the cadence, and a generic subject line on email eleven reads as fatigue.
- **"Take your PICK" hero is cute but unanchored** — without copy calling out specific new brands or categories, it's a concept without a reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a brand name in the subject** — the product grid clearly has recognizable names. Lead with one. The subject is the only thing standing between delete and open.
- 2. **Add an end date to the minis deal** — even "Ends Sunday" would convert me faster. No deadline = no reason to act today vs. next week.
- 3. **Lean harder into the Rare Beauty GWP in the subject** — that's the email's strongest hook and it's buried below the fold.
- 4. **Reduce send cadence or segment harder** — eleven emails in twelve days is where Rouge members start going to "promotions" tab settings and muting.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rare Beauty gift unlocked — plus 50% off minis`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 new drops worth your Rouge points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy 2 minis, get both half off — plus a free gift when you spend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your new launch radar: Fenty, Benefit, and more just arrived.`
