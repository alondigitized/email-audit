# Plot twist: there’s more to gift!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- I'll give Glossier this much: the lip-product lineup is cute. But it's the fifth Glossier send in eight days with zero prices, zero offer and zero Rewards mention, and I can't do any points math on it. Between the 10/6 "Our 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is here!" and the 10/8 Mini Wardrobe Kit, "Drop Two" of the same gift guide feels like the same email in a new outfit.
- I did like the three new lip products: Just Baked, Toasty Nuts and Black Cherry. The flavor-of-the-season styling is fun, and the Black Cherry duo is the one I'd actually want for fall. But the right-hand copy in two of the modules gets cut off mid-sentence ("fresh-from-the-oven delici…", "anything bu…"), and that looks sloppy. Sephora would have put a price bubble and a Rouge early-access line on every one of these.
- Would I click? Maybe on Black Cherry, just to see the price. Would I forward it? No. It's a mark-as-read for me. I'd rather wait for a Sephora Savings Event or an Ulta 21 Days drop to buy beauty gifts.

## What's working

- The "Drop Two" label tells me this is a staggered holiday launch, so I know there's a cadence to follow.
- The product photography is strong. The Toasty Nuts tube on candied almonds and the Black Cherry duo with the cherry in the cream coupe both stopped my scroll.
- The "NEW!" tags make the launches clear at a glance.
- The layout is a clean zig-zag with one button per module, so the hierarchy is easy to follow.
- The gift-set hero with the pink tissue and ribbon fits the season and matches the 10/6 gift guide creative.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere. Drunk Elephant finally added price bubbles ($89/$135) and e.l.f. showed "$95 for a $153 value" on its advent calendar, so Glossier is behind both.
- There's no offer, code, deadline or Rewards mention. That's the third Glossier send in a row with none of those.
- The copy is clipped on the right side of the Just Baked and Black Cherry modules, so the sentences end mid-word.
- "Get gifting" shows up twice and links to the same guide, which I already got on 10/6. It's a repeat, not a new reason to click.
- Every CTA is a generic outlined "Shop now" or "Get gifting". The buttons are small and low-contrast, and I'd have to hunt for them on my phone.
- The shades and skin-tone personalization I care about are missing. Everything is one-size flavor lip balm.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on every product, and add the gift-set price in the hero. That's the single biggest lift. I do the math before I click, and right now there's nothing to calculate.
- 2. Add a Glossier Rewards line, like "Earn 2x points on gift sets through 10/20". Ulta and Sephora both lead with member math.
- 3. Fix the text clipping in the side-by-side modules.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific and filled, like "Shop Black Cherry Duo" instead of "Shop now".
- 5. Make "Drop Two" newsworthy. Say what was in Drop One, or lead with the one product that's actually new instead of repeating the gift guide link at both ends.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results warnings are probably relay artifacts, so they are unconfirmed rather than failures. The provided source is truncated, so links, footer and tokens could only be partly assessed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked URL on a non-link element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M4K4YADQF54MWAVK4JT7GKQF_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `href` and `rel` are invalid on `<span>`. It looks like a `<link rel="stylesheet">` that Klaviyo's link rewriter converted. It is inert, so it is not a functional break, but it adds a pointless tracked URL and fails HTML validation.
- **Third-party tracking domain:** Click and open tracking use `ctrk.klclick.com`, while the sender is `eml.glossier.com`. A tracking domain that doesn't match the sender can slightly hurt reputation and link-rewrite trust. This is a low-severity observation.
- **Link health:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 5 (footer, utility and social). It reported no failures, but nothing is confirmed about the 5 skipped links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 5 content images):** `heroimage_1_5.jpg`, `2colimageright_1.jpg`, `2colimageright_1_1.jpg`, `2colimageright_1_2.jpg` and `group_3971_6.jpg`, all on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_13942e29…`. With images blocked or a screen reader, the hero and 2-column content may render as nothing or as filenames. I can't tell from the QA output whether the `alt` attribute is absent or just empty.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M4K4YADQF54MWAVK4JT7GKQF` should carry `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions. This is minor.
- **Unbalanced conditional comment:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, but its closer `<!--<![endif]-->` appears after `<body>` begins. In Outlook (MSO), the intervening `</head><body>` is hidden inside the comment. Non-MSO clients are unaffected, and Word's parser is usually forgiving, so the real impact in Outlook is unverified.
