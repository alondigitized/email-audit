# Re: Your four Black Friday gifts
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth ILIA email in about five days, and it's a plain-text "personal note" from "Marissa." I did the points math anyway. It's a decent offer, but nothing here makes me stop scrolling. There's no hero image, no button and no product shot, only blue underlined links in a wall of text. The "Re:" subject and the "Hey there" opener are a fake-personal trick. Compared with the pretty two-model hero in "All set for Black Friday?" (11/27), this is a big step down visually.
- The content is useful, though. The gift ladder is clear: a free Complexion Stick on every order, a free mini over $100, and a Color Block lipstick plus a tote over $175. That's the tiered math I like. But the numbers are scattered through paragraphs. The "20% off, 25% with VIP" line is buried in the first sentence, and the "$175 for 4 gifts" payoff is at the bottom. Sephora's giant serif "50% OFF" opens with the number. Ulta leads with a $10 coupon and a barcode. I wouldn't forward this. I'd skim it, maybe tap the VIP link to get the 25%, and mark it read.

## What's working

- The gift tiers are spelled out by dollar threshold ($0, $100, $175). I can do the cart math in my head in about two seconds.
- "Join our FREE VIP program and unlock 25% off" is a real stackable perk. That's the same math I do with Sephora Rouge.
- The new-launch callout (Sun Serum Mineral Sunscreen SPF 50, Thru Line Waterproof Eyeliner) tells me what's fresh, not just what's discounted.
- The "$50 value" on the Complexion Stick makes the free gift feel concrete.
- The text is readable on a phone without zooming, and nothing is broken.

## What's weak

- There are no visuals and no button. Every CTA is an inline text link, which are tiny tap targets on mobile. Glossier and Drunk Elephant both put a big block button in the hero.
- The best offer, 25% off through VIP, sits in a "*Hint" aside. A sentence-level footnote shouldn't be the real headline.
- The "Re:" subject plus "Hey there" and a "Marissa" signature feels manufactured. I'm not fooled, and I've seen ILIA's team-note format before ("A quick note from team ILIA," 11/26).
- The fine print, "See site for full promotional details," is the only thing at the bottom. There's no deadline, so the urgency is gone.
- Cadence: nine emails in five days, with a gift-offer subject similar to "Your free Complexion Stick is waiting" (11/27) and "New. Gifts. Added." (11/26). e.l.f. is doing the same thing, and it's tiring.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero block or a stacked offer graphic. It should show "20% off, 25% with VIP" and the three gift tiers as a small table, with one big button: "Shop & pick my gifts."
- 2. Put the VIP join link in a button of its own, not in a parenthetical. That's how brands like Sephora get the loyalty math in front of me.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "ends Cyber Monday 11:59 PT," plus a stock warning on the gifts.
- 4. Add one product image row with the Skin Tint, Lash Mascara and Lip Sketch.
- 5. If the personal-note format stays, drop the "Re:" and let the sender name carry it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a simple, Klaviyo-authored, near-plain-text HTML email. Every link goes through the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` click-tracking domain. The QA data can't confirm authentication or unsubscribe headers, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer, so footer compliance can't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 6 body links are wrapped redirects, `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M8FXATA87ERWHJ34HTMQPG9V_0` through `_5`. The destination URLs and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source. See section 6.
- Link `_0` ("20% off the entire site") and link `_1` ("Join our FREE VIP program") are separate offers, but the destinations can't be checked from the HTML.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M8FXATA87ERWHJ34HTMQPG9V` has no `alt` (QA WARN). This is low severity because it's a tracking pixel. It should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, so screen readers skip it and it doesn't render as a broken-image placeholder.
- All links use `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"` and `target="_blank"`, which is fine.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The source has no `<meta charset>`, `<meta name="viewport">`, `<title>` or `lang` attribute on `<html>`. The `<head>` contains only the `@import`. Without a charset declaration, the curly apostrophes (`We’re`, `that’s`) and the em dash risk mojibake in some clients.
- The web font loads through `@import url("https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css")`. It is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded. Many clients ignore `@import`, including Gmail, so Karla falls back to Arial. This is a graceful degradation and not a bug.
- The layout uses `<p>`, `<ul>` and `<br><br>` with no table scaffolding or max-width wrapper. It should reflow acceptably, but the line length is unconstrained on wide viewports.
- The body is text-only with inline styles, so alt-text risk is limited to the pixel above.
- The footer `<table align="left">` uses the deprecated `align` attribute. The rest of the table is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The greeting is a generic "Hey there," with no first-name merge, so nothing can render broken.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058) are not found. The QA note says AgentMail may strip these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. The Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements make both headers mandatory for this sender volume, so verify them on the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results** is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `iliabeauty.com` differs from the tracking and return-path domain `send.iliabeauty.com`. That is a normal subdomain setup, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Subject line:** `Re: Your four Black Friday gifts` is a fabricated reply prefix on a bulk promotion. CAN-SPAM prohibits deceptive subject lines, and a fake "Re:" implying a prior conversation is a recognized risk pattern. Fake "Re:" prefixes can also hurt spam-filter scoring.
- **Footer:** the truncated source shows the footer table opening (`color:#888; font-size:12px`), but the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are cut off. They can't be verified from the supplied data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed because the wrapped `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/…` URLs hide the destinations. The following need checking by resolving the redirects:
- Whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) survive the redirect.
- Whether the `_0` and `_1` landing pages show the advertised 20% site-wide and 25% VIP offers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve each `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/…_0` to `_5` redirect chain. Confirm HTTP 200, the final landing page and the UTM parameters, and that the 20% and 25% VIP messaging matches the landing page.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, bypassing the AgentMail relay. Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original too.
3. Remove the fake `Re:` prefix from the subject line.
4. Add `<meta charset="utf-8">`, a viewport meta, `<title>` and `<html lang="en">`. Set `alt=""` and explicit dimensions on the tracking pixel.
