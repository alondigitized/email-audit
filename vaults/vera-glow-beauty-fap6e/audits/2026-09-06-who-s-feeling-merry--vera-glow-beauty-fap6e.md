# Who’s feeling merry?!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, at least it's not "secret sale" fatigue this time — wait, no, it IS: the top banner says "SECRET SALE! 20% off with code DRUNKVIP20," and that's the exact same DRUNKVIP20 code I've now seen twice in two days (9/4's "Well damn, 20% off" and 9/5's "Lookitchoo, 20% off!"). Below that though, they actually pivoted to something new — a "2026 Holiday Collection" gift-set launch called "The Drunk Elephant Atelier," which is a legit reason to open an email in September, if a little early even for me and I stock up on everything.
- The bundles look genuinely pretty — the yellow gingham packaging with the pink ribbon accents is very "fashion house does skincare," and I like that each set has a little theme (Gala Glow, Couture Shows, Maison Moisture, Finishing Touches). But there's zero pricing on any of these gift sets, and the 20% off code up top explicitly excludes "bundles" in the fine print at the bottom, so the whole hero offer doesn't even apply to the thing they spent the whole email selling me. That's a bigger miss than anything Sephora or Ulta pulled this week.
- Would I click? Maybe out of curiosity on "Shop Holiday" just to see set pricing, but I wouldn't forward this — Drunk Elephant needs new material, not a new season slapped on the same discount code.

## What's working

- The holiday gift-set visual is genuinely well-styled — gingham + ribbon + swan-neck logo reads premium, not generic
- Four distinct bundle callouts (Gala Glow, Couture Shows, Maison Moisture, Finishing Touches) give me actual browsing structure instead of one flat grid
- Clean single-column layout, easy to scroll on my phone with no cramped text

## What's weak

- The DRUNKVIP20 code is the third identical instance in three days (9/4, 9/5, now 9/6) — I called this out yesterday and it's still the headline
- Fine print says the 20% off excludes bundles, gift cards, and Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops — so the "Secret Sale" banner doesn't even apply to the holiday collection it's sitting on top of, which is straight-up bait-and-switch energy
- No prices anywhere on the gift sets — I can't do my points math or decide if a set is worth it without clicking through
- Zero mention of Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond points multipliers on a holiday gifting email, which is exactly when I'd want to know if this stacks with a points event

## Recommendations

- 1. Either apply the 20% to the holiday bundles or drop the "Secret Sale" banner from this send entirely — running an excluded discount above a bundle-only email reads dishonest, and it's the single biggest trust hit here.
- 2. Add prices under each bundle name so I'm not clicking blind — ILIA's "Build a look around blush" send at least showed a clear routine with visible product steps; this needs the same clarity.
- 3. Retire DRUNKVIP20 for a few sends — three days running is starting to feel like Sephora's "You have 0 points" problem, stale and recycled.
- 4. Call out points/Rouge or Diamond eligibility on holiday gift sets — high basket-size gifting is exactly when tier shoppers like me decide where to spend.
