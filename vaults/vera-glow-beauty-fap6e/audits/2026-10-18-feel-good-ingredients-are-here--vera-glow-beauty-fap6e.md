## What's working

- The 4X points on all moisturizers banner is at the very top with a clear end date of 10/21. It's the first thing I do math on.
- The free Kosas trial size with a $30+ purchase gives me a threshold I can plan a cart around.
- The green "Clean at Sephora" badges on every product make the grid scannable. It's a cleaner organizing idea than Ulta's usual "everything on sale" layout.
- NEW and ONLY AT SEPHORA labels plus visible prices (Yepoda $39, 7 Virtues $94, OGEE $99 with the $126 value) tell me what's a launch and what it costs, with no tap needed.
- The Insider, VIB and Rouge status bar near the bottom reminds me where I stand on points, and the barcode makes the in-store redemption easy.

## What's weak

- The subject says "feel-good ingredients," but the hero is "NEW AND CLEAN AT SEPHORA" and the money is in a moisturizer points banner. Nothing in the subject tells me about the 4X points.
- The top has three competing blocks (4X points, the Kosas freebie and the Clean hero) with three different calls to action, and the "Shop to redeem" button is tiny.
- The skin-tone thumbs-up emojis feel like a mixed signal. They're inclusive, but they don't say what's inside. Glossier's "STILL ON! Get 20% off best-sellers" states its offer in the subject line.
- Nothing in the Clean hero is a skincare or moisturizer product, so the "hydrate" code isn't tied to anything I can see. The only moisturizer-adjacent item is a $39 Briogeo hair mask, which doesn't qualify.
- The fine print under the footer is a wall of tiny type, and the exclusions are hard to read on a phone.
- It's the same Sephora catalog format as 10/17 and 10/13, with no tier-specific message for Rouge and no mention of my points balance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the points deal in the subject line and lead the email with moisturizers. If the offer is 4X on moisturizers, show 4 to 6 moisturizers with a "= 240 pts on a $60 jar" line.
- 2. Make the free Kosas gift and the 4X points one combined banner with a single CTA, instead of two stacked offers.
- 3. Cut the grid to a smaller, tighter set and tie each product to a skincare benefit, or keep Clean as a clearly separate second email.
- 4. Add a Rouge-aware line, like "Rouge: you're X points from your next reward."
- 5. Make the code auto-apply with the CTA link so I don't have to type 4XHYDRATE.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on functional content.** `US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../US_4XHYDRATE_Barcode`) has no `alt`. A barcode or offer code with no text equivalent is unreadable to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Missing alt on a header image.** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` has no `alt`. The same applies if it carries nav text.
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel.** `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` has no `alt`. This is low severity, but an empty `alt=""` is the correct pattern for a pixel.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The `<style>` block sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. That blocks user text scaling.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The space makes the value invalid. Impact is minimal in email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. Without a `text/plain` alternative, there is no fallback for text-only clients, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only messages. This could be a relay capture artifact, but it is the only FAIL.
