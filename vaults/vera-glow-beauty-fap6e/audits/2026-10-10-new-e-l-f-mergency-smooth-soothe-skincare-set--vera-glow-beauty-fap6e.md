- I also noticed the copy glitches. The Hypochlorous Mist blurb says "hydrocolloid pimple patches," which is the Zits patch description pasted twice. ## What's working

- The $25 price for three items sits right in the hero with a green "SHOP THE SET" button. I can find the offer immediately.
- The numbered 1-2-3 structure makes the set easy to scan. Each product gets its own problem and solution line ("Breakout e.l.f.mergency?").
- The National Eczema Association "Accepted" badges on the mist and cream are real trust cues, and they matter to me for barrier-repair skincare.
- The "find your skincare" quiz block is a personalization nudge, which is something I always want. Ulta's skin quiz is buried deeper than this.
- The teal and orange palette is bold. The product shots are clean, so the visual hierarchy is clear.

## What's weak

- There's no "value" math. $25 for three products means nothing without a stated savings or per-item prices. Glossier has the same problem, with no prices at all in its last send.
- The mist copy repeats "hydrocolloid pimple patches," which is the wrong product description. That's a visible copy error and it hurts the trust cues.
- "Top Picks for You" shows $13, $7 and $8 items that have nothing to do with the set. It feels like a generic dump rather than real personalization.
- The category tabs (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are big gray bars. They take up a lot of the scroll and add nothing.
- There's no Beauty Squad or points tie-in in the main body. The footer mentions "Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.," but I can't tell what this purchase earns me. Ulta shows points on almost everything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add the value. Something like "$25 · a $41 value" next to the hero price, plus a line for what this earns with Beauty Squad. That single change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Fix the mist description so it describes the mist (soothe, calm, hypochlorous).
- 3. Replace the generic "Top Picks" with items that pair with the set, such as a SPF or a moisturizer.
- 4. Shrink or cut the gray category bars.
- 5. Put the Eczema Association badge in the hero. It's the strongest credibility signal in the email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based with `role="presentation"` and a preheader, and its links go through a Braze/SendGrid-style click tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`). The QA run flags four link-probe failures, a missing plain-text part, and a missing alt attribute. Several of the compliance and authentication warnings look like relay capture artifacts rather than confirmed sender defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures share one signature.** Each reads `unknown url type: '/…'`, which is the error a Python HTTP client raises when a redirect `Location` is relative and isn't resolved against the host. The affected links:
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml`
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL`
  - "Receipt scanning…" → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → `/pages/mobile-app?utm_campaign=101026_project_soothe_launch&utm_source=broadcast&lid=un7cdob804dq&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%…`
- **Likely cause is the probe, not the email.** The tracker resolved to a valid-looking e.l.f. path in each case. This is probably a probe limitation, so I'd treat the four failures as unverified. I can't confirm from this data that the links are broken for users, or that they work.
- **Hero link uses a long opaque token.** The `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/aqdSOI…` href is a single encoded redirect. That is normal for click tracking, but the destination can't be audited from the source.
- **Alt text is missing on one image.** The image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/Y5DUcuSOxcFqmnvmCYBOmQ~~/AAQRxRA~/0wwjAb-NHWJC3mjhu3OJhix7vHpoKiMO2gr6ujcrMGe39XjS3M84R` has no `alt`. The `/q/` path suggests it may be the open-tracking pixel, which is harmless but should still carry `alt=""`.
- **Coverage is partial.** QA probed 12 of 42 links and skipped 30. Footer and social links were deprioritized, so their health is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on one image** (above). The hero image has `alt="e.l.f."`, which is present but not descriptive. The visible content is unknown because the source is truncated.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is a minor issue and affects only web-view and client tab labels.
- **No `<meta name="color-scheme">`.** The head has no dark-mode declaration.
- **Structure is sound where visible.** Tables use `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"` is set, the MSO conditional blocks are present, and the viewport meta is present. The mobile breakpoint is `max-width:660px`.
- **Web font may be ignored.** The Google Fonts `<link>` sits in the `<head>` inside a `!mso` conditional. Many clients (Gmail among them) strip it, and the stack falls back to Arial/Sans-serif, which is acceptable.
- **Empty font-family value.** One block declares `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty string is harmless but sloppy.
- **Inline `*{box-sizing:border-box}`.** This is unsupported in some clients and has no practical impact.
- **Hidden preheader is correct.** "Your skin's emergency contacts" uses `display:none`, `mso-hide:all` and `opacity:0`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear, and I can't rule them out in the truncated portion. The `lid=un7cdob804dq` value in the app link is a rendered per-send or per-link ID, not a leaked token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing: [FAIL].** The text version is 0 chars. This could be a real multipart omission or an AgentMail capture artifact. If it is real, it hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found: [WARN].** QA itself notes the relay may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, the email would not meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (RFC 8058 one-click).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found: [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated cause.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and physical address are unverified.** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the report doesn't list its result. The truncated source doesn't show the footer address.
- **Sending domain.** `m.elfcosmetics.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and the click domain is `links.elfcosmetics.com`, so alignment looks plausible but can't be verified without headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs sit inside the redirect.** Only the app link exposes UTMs: `utm_campaign=101026_project_soothe_launch&utm_source=broadcast`. It has no `utm_medium`, so the `utm_source=broadcast` value alone gives analytics little to separate channels with.
- **Product links.** The Holy Hydration "Shop Now" links go to `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops` and `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser`, with a size variant parameter. These are not the "e.l.f.mergency Smooth & Soothe Skincare Set" named in the subject line. The product links may be secondary placements, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Login redirect.** The receipt-scanning link points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, which sends users to a login wall rather than a feature page.
- **Truncated app deep-link.** The `af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%…` value is cut off in the QA output, so it can't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the probe.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host, then re-run the four failed links before treating them as defects.
2. **Confirm the plain-text part.** Inspect the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part. If it is absent, add one.
3. **Check the headers.** Read `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` from the original message (e.g. Gmail "Show original") rather than the relay copy.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to the `/q/` tracking image, or a real description if it's a visible graphic.
5. **Fill in the `<title>`** and consider a `color-scheme` meta tag.
6. **Add `utm_medium=email`** (and `utm_content` per placement) so the sessions can be separated from other channels.
7. **Re-run QA on the full HTML.** The source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address need a separate check.
