# Add 3, subtract 20%
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is the cleanest ILIA offer I've seen all month. "buy 3 = 20% off" is huge in the hero, and there's a code, so I can do the math in two seconds. After the 10/7 skin tint and palette duo send, where I had no idea what anything cost, I wanted a number. This one gives me one.
- It also doesn't feel like a Sephora or Ulta carbon copy. Ulta's "20% off when you join Rewards" email from earlier this week was aimed at a stranger and recycled its own 10/6 coupon. ILIA's version is a straight bundle mechanic with no join-first hoop. My complaint is that it's the fifth ILIA email in about a week, and the product grid is generic. No model, no shade guidance, and I'm left to guess which three I'd pick.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the Eye Stylus Trio or the tint, because I'd happily stack a lash mascara, a stick and a crayon to hit three. I'd forward it only to the friend who's been eyeing the skin tint. Otherwise I'd save it for a restock.

## What's working

- "buy 3 = 20% off" in giant type with the "beauty math" tag is the offer, and it matches how I think. It's the clearest hero ILIA has sent since the 10/2 "It's Back" email.
- The code BUNDLEUP appears in the top banner, the body and the footer button. I never have to hunt for it.
- The "Add 3 to cart" button is a burgundy block with a verb and a noun. It's easy to tap on mobile.
- "multiples" gives me pre-built trios: Eye Stylus Trio, Art of the Eye Trio, Hero Set. That solves the "which three?" problem, which Sephora's Savings Event pages usually leave to me.
- The product shots are clean and consistent, so the brand voice feels trusted.

## What's weak

- The deadline, "Sale ends at 11:59pm 10/12/26," is buried in the tiny footer text. The body only says "a limited time only," which is weak urgency.
- There are no prices anywhere, not even on the sets. I can't tell whether the Hero Set beats buying three separate items. Glossier's Mini Wardrobe Kit send had the same problem.
- No models and no shade-range cues. "Personalized Shade Match" is down in the icon strip, but I'd want it near the tint.
- "ILIA VIP" is a one-word footer link. I'm a points-math person, so I wanted to know whether VIP stacks with the 20%. Sephora Insider at least tells me what the tier does.
- The "prime picks" grid has four products with no reason to pick them, and the outline "shop now" buttons are visually weak next to the hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the end date ("Ends Sunday 10/12") into the hero and the banner. That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add prices or "from $XX" under the sets and mark a savings number, like "Save $14 on the Eye Stylus Trio." Cite the math.
- 3. Put a model or shade-swatch strip beside the skin tint and say whether VIP points stack.
- 4. Make the secondary "shop now" buttons filled instead of outlined, and add a sub-line under each pick: why it's in the grid.
- 5. Space out the cadence. ILIA hit me 10/2, 10/5, 10/7 and now 10/9.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 3, save 20% through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 3-piece ILIA math (code inside)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Eye trios, skin tint, lash mascara. Ends 10/12 at 11:59pm. Code BUNDLEUP.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick any 3 and the 20% comes off at checkout. Pre-built trios inside.`
