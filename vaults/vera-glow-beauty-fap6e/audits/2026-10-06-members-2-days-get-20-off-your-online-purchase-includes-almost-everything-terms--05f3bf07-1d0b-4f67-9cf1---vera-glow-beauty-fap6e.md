# Members, 2 DAYS! Get 20% off your online purchase⁺. Includes almost everything! Terms & excl. apply 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Marisol, earn points that turn into $ off future purchases" is a nice nod to the Rewards math, and the big orange "20% off" hero is impossible to miss. Compared to the 8/1 "ENDS TODAY" sends with the barcode-on-book-stack hero, this one is cleaner and more direct. It's a "Members, 2 DAYS" blast, and as a Diamond I read it as "yes, my coupon is live, go cart something."
- Then I scrolled, and it turned into a 6,500-pixel wall: 100+ free gifts, a free 12-piece gift, "GO ALL IN," and three near-identical "up to 30% off" app-only tiles. The code SAVE20 and the exclusions (fragrance, CHANEL, gift cards) are small, and the app tiles compete with the hero. Sephora's 2X/5X points send was cleaner about what to do and when.
- Would I click? Yes, but only to stack SAVE20 on a restock. I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read-after-shopping email, not a share-with-a-friend one.

## What's working

- The "20% off" hero sits in giant white type on orange and is readable instantly, with "Two Days | Member Exclusive" above it for urgency.
- The Rewards strip at the top, with my name and the points-to-dollars line, makes me feel recognized.
- The "free 12 piece gift with select $80 online purchase, $127 value" tile gives me real math to work with. That kind of threshold is what I stack against.
- The product tiles are clean, with clear categories (hair, skin, makeup, nails).
- "Ends Oct 10" on the app tiles gives a clear deadline.

## What's weak

- The ask is muddy. The main CTA is "Online use code SAVE20" plus "Not a member? Join now!", so there's no big "Shop now" button. I have to remember the code and go find the site.
- The hero says "includes almost everything!", but fragrance, CHANEL and gift cards are excluded, and that's in tiny type under the headline. I have to read a pile of fine print to know what I can actually buy.
- Three identical "up to 30% off" app-only tiles (hair, skin, makeup, nails) read as filler. Ulta is copying Sephora's wall-of-banners approach without the points hook that makes Sephora's worth scrolling.
- It's a mixed message. The 20% coupon is online, the gifts are online-only, the 30% is app-only, and "GO ALL IN" tells me to stack them. Nothing tells me which one to pick or how they combine.
- There are no models or personalization beyond my name. I get no skin tone or type cues, no launches, and no brand-curation signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the primary CTA a real button, "Shop 20% off now," with the code auto-applied by link. Keep the join CTA secondary.
- 2. State the exclusions in one line right under the hero, not in the 6pt block at the bottom.
- 3. Replace the three duplicate app tiles with one app tile plus one personalized pick, based on what I actually buy.
- 4. Add a points-multiplier hook for Diamond members. Sephora's 2X/5X and Sally's BIGDEALS20 both make the math obvious in one glance.
- 5. Put the deadline in the hero ("Ends Oct 8" or similar). I can't read it at this size.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rae, your 20% off + a free 12-pc gift ✨`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 12-piece gift with $80, plus up to 30% off in the app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fragrance and CHANEL excluded, everything else is fair game.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (XHTML doctype, MSO/VML support, `lang="en-US"`, responsive media queries). The confirmed defects are 10 non-HTTPS, alt-less third-party pixels and about 17 content images with no alt text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 35 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so link destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`.
  - Clients that block mixed content or proxy images (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop them.
  - They are an unencrypted third-party cookie-sync call from an email.
  - They carry no alt text.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=162044 + pr_fy26wk36_tuesday_20off_6am_rem + 31211` has no alt text. The URL appears to include literal ` + ` concatenation with campaign identifiers, which suggests a malformed or oddly built URL. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.
- **Possible duplicate asset:** `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` is flagged twice, so it may be inserted twice.

1. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS (or drop them). Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to all pixels, including `emltrk.com`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six `image.e.ulta.com` content images and the two `mi.ulta.com` dynamic images. This matters most for any image that carries the 20% offer or a CTA.
3. Check the `emltrk.com` URL for the literal ` + pr_fy26wk36… + 31211` string and fix the template concatenation if it is real.
4. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` exist in the raw headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC, by testing against a direct inbox instead of the AgentMail relay.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, and resolve the tracking redirects, to check UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
