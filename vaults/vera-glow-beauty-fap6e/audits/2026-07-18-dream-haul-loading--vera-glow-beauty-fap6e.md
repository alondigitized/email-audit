## Executive summary

After the double "JUST YOUR TYPE" on the 15th and the double "10% off" on the 16th where I genuinely thought my Gmail was glitching, I'm relieved to open something with new creative today.

## What's weak

- Five distinct sections competing for one click is too many — trial sizes, the Ariana launch, a product grid, the "Spot It. Shop It." QR camera feature, AND hair points is genuinely exhausting.
- The "Spot It. Shop It." QR section sandwiched mid-email is jarring — it's a whole interactive feature drop inside a launch email, and it competes directly with the Ariana Grande moment instead of supporting it.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`lang` attribute absent on `<html>` element**
The opening tag declares three XML namespaces but omits `lang="en"`. Screen readers cannot determine content language, and some email clients use `lang` for font selection.
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"
      xmlns:v="urn:schemas-microsoft-com:vml"
      xmlns:o="urn:schemas-microsoft-com:office:office">
<!-- ↑ missing lang="en" -->
```

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta blocks pinch-to-zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents accessible zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) requires content reflow without assistive technology at 200% scale; `maximum-scale=1` blocks this at the browser level. Dropping `maximum-scale=1` is the fix.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied too broadly**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
This selector list includes `body` and `span`, meaning it overrides the OS-level "Large Text" accessibility setting on iOS for all text in the email. Applying only to `td` (the minimum needed for Outlook kerning) removes the side effect on body copy.

**Robots meta value malformed**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
The value has a space: `no index` instead of `noindex`. Inconsequential in email clients (none crawl), but worth noting if the same template is used in a web-rendered version.
