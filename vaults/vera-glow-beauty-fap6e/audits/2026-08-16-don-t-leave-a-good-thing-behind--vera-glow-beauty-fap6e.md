- "SHOP THE SALE" is a clear, unambiguous CTA with a visible underline treatment.

## What's weak

- The hero product — a men's navy workout walker — has nothing to do with a 29-year-old woman doing Sephora/Ulta points math; there's zero personalization here, just a recycled retarget.
- The "more options" grid mixes kids' Care Bears sneakers with a men's dress shoe with zero logic — it reads like a random assortment dump, not curation.
