# Just dropped: point of view.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "point of view" landed twice this week and I stand by what I said this morning — it's a legit new brand drop, not a reskin of the same 50%-off template. What's different this time is I actually scrolled the whole thing: prep it / snatch it / wear it is a clean three-act structure with product callouts and little testimonial quotes ("my skin looks visibly smoother, firmer, and way more snatched") that make it read like an actual editorial launch, not a discount blast bolted onto a hero banner.
- But that 50% off Select Beauty banner up top is doing this email dirty — it's the exact same PHLUR/Urban Decay/INKEY List "Today Only" strip I've now seen strapped to unrelated hero content multiple times this week, and it steals the first three seconds of attention from what's actually new. Ulta's Marisol 20% coupon banner has the same problem — a static offer glued onto whatever hero happens to be running that day — so at least Sephora isn't alone in this habit, but it's still lazy.
- I'd click through to "point of view" out of curiosity since I haven't seen it anywhere else, but I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's solid, not "you need to see this" material. This is a keep-scrolling-and-tap-shop-it email, not a screenshot-and-send one.

## What's working

- The "point of view" brand intro has real visual identity — black-and-white product-in-hand photography that feels distinct from Sephora's usual pastel grid.
- Prep it / snatch it / wear it structure with founder-pov quotes gives this actual editorial weight, unlike Ulta's flat "20% off calling" single-offer sends.
- Points/tier footer (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is present and I can see my tier band, which Ulta's Marisol email skips entirely in favor of just blasting the coupon code.
- Cross-sell modules at the bottom (Olive Young "It's Here," New Kilian scent) are genuinely relevant to how I actually shop — I do jump between Korean beauty drops and niche fragrance.

## What's weak

- That "50% off Select Beauty, Today Only" banner at the very top has nothing to do with "point of view" — it's the same disconnected offer strip I flagged in yesterday's "hottest drops" send, just recycled again.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere in the prep/snatch/wear grid — everyone gets the same six products regardless of what Sephora already knows I've bought.
- Founder's POV section is text-heavy black-and-white blocks stacked five deep — on my phone that's a lot of vertical scroll before I hit a real "shop the collection" moment.
- No Rouge-specific perk tied to this actual launch (early access, sample, points multiplier) — just the generic tier bar at the footer, same as every other send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the 50%-off banner out of the top slot entirely or replace it with a launch-specific hook ("Rouge gets early access" / "Free sample with POV purchase") — right now it's cannibalizing the one genuinely new thing in this email.
- 2. Add one skin-tone/skin-type filter or "pick your finish" interactive element into the prep/snatch/wear grid so this doesn't feel like six products blasted at everyone.
- 3. Move "shop the collection" CTA higher — right now I have to scroll through prep/snatch/wear before there's a clear buy-everything button.
- 4. Tie a concrete Rouge perk to this specific launch, not just the generic footer tier bar.
