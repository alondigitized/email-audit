# Stop scrolling: Today’s deal is here 😲
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Sixth Sephora email in six days, and my first reaction was "okay, purple sequins, I get it." The subject says "Stop scrolling," but the body is a long scroll. It's a Sunday Riley / Fresh / Skinfix 30% off trio, then a product grid, then a fragrance points promo, a lip gift guide and a footer. I did stay for it. The FREE Sample Bag strip at the top (GRABMYBAG, $65 minimum, online only) is the real hook for me, because I'm always stacking Rouge perks on top of a sale.
- It's a better build than yesterday's 11/25 "Wait, you're going to want in on these Cyber Week deals 😅". That one stacked offers with no clear order. Today the 30% off blocks are stacked in a clear order. It's still a Cyber Week recap with the same creative for the sixth time. I'd click, probably the 4X points on fragrance, but I wouldn't forward it. Mark it read, then come back on the 30th.

## What's working

- The free sample bag banner is the first thing I see. Free gift with a code is my love language, and it makes my points math easier.
- Three big "30% OFF" blocks, each with a brand and a Save Now button, make the offer impossible to miss. Sunday Riley, Fresh and Skinfix are brands I actually buy.
- The "24 hour deal" line gives real urgency without feeling fake.
- The Shop More Must-Have Deals grid shows prices ($36, $27, $19), so I can compare without clicking. The "Only at Sephora" tags help too.
- The 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT (through 11/30) is the loyalty tie-in I want. It's a clear reason to buy fragrance now.

## What's weak

- Sephora's usual overstuffing. There's the sample bag, three brand blocks, two product grids, the complexion makeup promo, the fragrance points, the Kissable Lips feature, a holiday gift guide and a credit card pitch. Nothing wins.
- The subject promises "Today's deal" (singular), but I count four or five offers. The mismatch is a little annoying.
- The sample bag fine print is tiny: "$65 minimum, online only, exclusions apply." The "30% off" blocks have an asterisk too. I can't read either without zooming, and a 30% hero that's really "select brands" feels a bit sneaky.
- It's the sixth email in six days with the same sequin background. Ulta's 11/26 Black Friday send at least gave me a tier ladder (50/40/30%). Here I can't tell which 30% off is best.
- The Shop All Deals and Save Now buttons are vague. None of them says what I'd be saving on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to three modules: the sample bag, one 24-hour 30% off block, and 4X points. Move everything else to a "more deals" link. Ulta's Black Friday send ran one tier ladder and stayed clear.
- 2. Put the minimum spend and the code in readable type next to the free sample bag. I'd rather see "$65" than hunt for it.
- 3. Tell me in one line which Cyber Week email this replaces. Fifth and sixth sends feel like a repeat, so a "what's new today" line would make it feel different. Also vary the creative. Drunk Elephant and e.l.f. gave me product-led heroes this week and I didn't tune them out.
- 4. Make buttons specific: "Shop 30% off Sunday Riley" instead of "Save Now."
- 5. Use my Rouge status. "Rouge: 4X points on fragrance, plus your free bag" would feel personal.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout at a fixed 700px, with links routed through the `app.sephora.com` redirect domain. The only hard QA failure is the empty plain-text part. The missing headers (`List-Unsubscribe` and `Authentication-Results`) are probably relay artifacts and need checking against the original message. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and most link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 material links and skipped 42, and the footer, utility and social links were deprioritized, so most of the 54 links are unverified.
- Click tracking uses opaque redirects of the form `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a…?__dU__=…`. Destination URLs can't be audited from the source.
- The open-tracking pixel is `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>`. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, so it fails to register opens in clients that block images or don't load `display:none` images.
- The pixel `<img>` has no `alt` attribute, which is the flagged QA warning. It is a hidden 1×1 tracker, so screen-reader impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` fixes it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- The wrapper `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` forces a 700px minimum width. Together with `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta, mobile clients that honor it will scroll horizontally. That meta also disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- The `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block contains an MSO conditional comment (`<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->`) inside the CSS rule. Some parsers will drop the rules after it, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- The `<html>` element has no `lang` attribute, and `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- The JSON-LD block uses `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a valid schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>` tags, so the impact is low.
- The preheader is `DO. NOT. MISS.` followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding run. The nested hidden `<div>` structure is valid.
- **Plain-text part missing.** QA reports 0 chars, a FAIL. This hurts deliverability scoring and any client or user that needs text-only. If the relay stripped the text part, treat it as a capture artifact, but it should be checked against the raw MIME.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source.
- The JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` has `"discountCode": ""`, an empty field. It is probably a template default, and it is low severity because the block is invisible to most recipients.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN), and neither was `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / RFC 8058 (WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may not preserve them. Without them, the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirement for one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` could not be assessed.
- The footer postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result for it was reported. I can't confirm or flag this item.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible, because links are wrapped in the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirects and the final destinations are not in the supplied data.
- Landing-page alignment can't be assessed from this source. No issues confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text MIME part.** This is the one confirmed FAIL.
2. **Verify the raw message headers.** Check for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass) outside the AgentMail relay before treating them as real defects.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.** Replace the fixed `min-width:700px` with a fluid or hybrid layout, `width:100%; max-width:700px`.
4. **Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block** so the mobile rules that follow it aren't dropped.
5. **Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.** Fix `content="noindex"`, and drop or correct the JSON-LD (`PromotionCard`, empty `discountCode`).
6. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML.** That would cover the 42 skipped links, the footer and unsubscribe markup, the UTM parameters and any merge tokens.
