# Your difference is lowkey your gift 🎁 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a gift guide with no gift-guide math in it. The top is a periwinkle block with "e.l.f. made to stand out" and two small buttons, "Take the gift quiz" and "Shop all gifts." Then come three stop-motion doll heroes at $9, $13 and $11. The dolls are cute and on brand. But I scrolled the whole thing hunting for a deal and found one: a "choose a full-size gift on us" strip, buried halfway down on a black band.
- The hero product shot has no price, no offer and no deadline. The best hook is in the middle, and it's wedged between a TikTok Shop Super Brand Day block and a "Join us for Mega Lives" block. That's three promos fighting for one slot. After the 11/1 Peel of Approval launch email, which had one product, one price and one button, this feels like a step backward. I'd skim it and probably not click. I might forward the Glow Reviver Lip Oil to a friend who likes lip oil, but I wouldn't forward the email itself.

## What's working

- The three doll heroes are charming and have their own color blocks (pink, blue, green). Each has one product, one price and one "Shop now" button, so I can scan them fast.
- Prices are visible in the headlines ($9, $13, $11). That's the e.l.f. price-point math I can do in my head.
- The "Top Picks for You" row at the bottom has real product shots with prices ($11, $8, $12). It reads as personalized.
- The "choose a full-size gift on us" band is a strong hook. It says "up to $9 value," "just spend $35+" and "no promo code needed!", so it's honest and specific.

## What's weak

- The hero block says nothing concrete. "Give the gift of (s)e.l.f.-expression" is a slogan, not an offer, and the two buttons under it are tiny.
- The gift-with-purchase is buried below three product blocks. Sephora would have led with it, and Ulta would have put the $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder first.
- The TikTok Shop Super Brand Day and Mega Lives blocks pull attention away from the gift. They read like a second email stapled on.
- The empty gray category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) take up a lot of space. They look like collapsed tabs, and they're tall for tap targets.
- There's no loyalty or points angle. Sephora's Savings Event email had Insider 10% off front and center, and this has nothing for a member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "choose a full-size gift on us" offer to the top as the hero. Put the dolls underneath as proof.
- 2. Cut the TikTok Shop and Mega Lives blocks, or move them to a footer strip. One promo per send.
- 3. Replace the hero slogan with a real offer line plus a deadline, such as the last day for gift orders.
- 4. Collapse the gray category bars into a compact row of text links.
- 5. Add a line for e.l.f. Squad or loyalty members, so I feel like a known customer.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built from a Braze/Iterable-style template wrapped in nested tables, and every link goes through `links.elfcosmetics.com` click tracking. The QA probe failed 4 of the 12 material links with relative-redirect errors. Compliance and authentication headers couldn't be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **4 probed links failed with `unknown url type`.** The redirect target came back as a relative path with no host:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/hydrating-camo-concealer?Color=Light+Beige+-+light+with+golden+beige+undertones` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&shortlink=email&c=email&utm_source=broadcast&source_caller=ui&bxid=6a0138…` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Cause not confirmed.** Either the probe lost the base host on a multi-hop redirect, or the tracker really emits a relative `Location` header. In the second case, clients would resolve these against `links.elfcosmetics.com` and break. A manual `curl -I -L` on these four URLs would settle it.
- **Pass rate is only 27%.** 33 of 45 links were not probed, so the true failure rate is unknown.
- **One image has no `alt`.** Its `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/DLHSJlnKlDsoGsJMx0Ylig~~/…`. The `/q/` path looks like the open-tracking pixel, so this is probably harmless. Confirm that it is a 1x1 pixel and not a content image.
- **Tracking URLs are very long.** Plain text is 86% URLs (12,934 of 14,999 chars), which can hurt text-part spam scoring. Each tracked URL is about 400 characters or more.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Invalid font stack:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in `html_block block-1`. It still falls back, but the empty `''` entry is an authoring defect.
- **Duplicated nested layout.** Each `html_block` embeds a full `nl-container` > `row` > `row-content` table set inside the outer one, which adds size and nesting depth. Several `html_block` containers are empty (the `mobile_hide` row contains only whitespace).
- **Header image:** it has `alt="e.l.f."` and `title="e.l.f."`, which is acceptable. Tables use `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"` is set, and the preheader is hidden correctly.
- The Google Fonts `<link>` is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`).

**5. Compliance**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA note says this may be a relay capture gap, so it is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. `From: makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com` and the click domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` share the organizational domain `elfcosmetics.com`, which is good for alignment.
- **The footer isn't visible in the truncated source.** I can't confirm the physical postal address or an unsubscribe link in the HTML.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- **App-download link:** it carries `utm_source=broadcast`, `c=email`, `shortlink=email` and `bxid=…`. The truncated part may hold `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign`, but I can't confirm that. `utm_source=broadcast` is a generic ESP value, not a stable source name.
- **Product links:** the paths carry variant parameters (`Size=`, `Color=`). I see no UTM parameters on them in the QA output, but the final URLs weren't resolved, so UTM presence can't be verified.
- **The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` link** lands on a login wall. That is fine for a rewards CTA, but it can't be tested unauthenticated.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Manually resolve the 4 failing tracked links with `curl -sIL`. If the `Location` header is relative, fix the ESP's redirect or base-URL config. If the chain is fine, fix the probe's relative-URL joining.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. Don't treat them as defects until then.
3. Fill in `<title>` and drop the empty `''` font-family entry.
4. Confirm the alt-less `/q/` image is a tracking pixel. If it isn't, add `alt=""` or descriptive alt text.
5. Raise the probe budget above 12 links so the footer and secondary CTAs get checked.
6. Make sure all landing URLs carry full `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` values.
