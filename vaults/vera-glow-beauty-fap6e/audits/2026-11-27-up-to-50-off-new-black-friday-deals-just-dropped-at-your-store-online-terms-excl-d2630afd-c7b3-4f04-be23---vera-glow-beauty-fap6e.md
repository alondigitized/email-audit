# Up to 50% off! NEW Black Friday deals just dropped at your store & online (Terms & excl. apply) 👏👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Ulta email since 11/4, and it feels like the Black Friday machine with new product tiles. The $10-off coupon with a barcode sits right under the header, then a free 7-piece beauty bag, then a purple "Over 200 new deals just joined the party" block. The 11/26 "A little thank you" send had the same $10 coupon, so I'm looking at it for the third time. The 11/22 "Up to 50% off" send at least laid out the whole tier ladder. This one only shows 30% and 20% tiles, and the "up to 50%" in the subject never shows up as a hero number I can find.
- The product tiles are the good part: Tower 28 style complexion, Charlotte Tilbury, Live Tinted, Bubble and Bubble, Living Proof, Supergoop, and Dolce & Gabbana and Juicy Couture fragrance. The orange, pink and purple blocks with gift-box graphics do break up a very long scroll. It's still a catalog on a page that's roughly 11,000px tall.
- I'd skim it, screenshot the Charlotte Tilbury 30% tile, and not forward it. The points math is where it loses me. The top banner looks like it's pitching me on joining Ulta Beauty Rewards, and I'm already Diamond. Sephora's "50% OFF starts now" hero landed in one second. Here I have to scroll past a coupon, a GWP and a banner before I reach the actual new deals.

## What's working

- The "30% off" labels are big and consistent, so I can scan the tiles fast.
- The "Just added" tags make the new deals feel new, which is the whole angle of this send.
- Beauty category chips (makeup, skin care, hair, fragrance, bath and wellness) sit above the product tiles. It's easy to jump to my category.
- The brand mix has prestige names like Charlotte Tilbury and Dolce & Gabbana next to mass names like Bubble and Bubble. That's the Ulta mass-prestige mix I shop.
- The free 7-piece bag with a $15 online purchase is a concrete add-on, and it's a clear reason to check out.

## What's weak

- The $10-off-$50 coupon is the third time I've seen it (11/4, 11/26, now). It looks like a recycled slot.
- "Up to 50%" in the subject isn't backed by a 50% hero. The tiles I can see are 30% and 20%, which is a bait-and-switch feeling.
- The top loyalty banner reads as "join Rewards" at a Diamond member. It doesn't acknowledge my tier.
- No points math anywhere: no 2x or 5x points event, no Diamond perk, no Ulta 21 Days tie-in. Compared with Sephora's Beauty Insider event, nothing here earns me more.
- The sections at the bottom (plush robe, TikTok Shop, in-store events, app download) feel like filler after about ten product tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero deal at the top, such as "Up to 50% off" with a visible number and a single "Shop Black Friday" button. Move the coupon below it, or drop it, since I've seen it twice.
- 2. Segment by tier. Diamond members should see a points multiplier instead of a "join Rewards" pitch.
- 3. Show a real 50% tile or change the subject to match the highest tier actually in the email.
- 4. Cut the bottom third (robe, TikTok Shop, events, app) or move it to a footer strip.
- 5. Add a time stamp or countdown on the "just added" deals so the urgency feels credible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `200+ new Black Friday deals, up to 50% off 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New today: 30% off Charlotte Tilbury & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Just added: 30% off complexion, hair care and fragrance. Diamond members earn bonus points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free 7-piece bag with $15 online, plus 200+ new deals. Ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found no hard failures, only warnings. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay. The main confirmed code-level issues are 24 images with missing alt text and 11 images served over plain `http://`, mostly third-party cookie-sync pixels. The HTML source was truncated, so only the QA data covers the body.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 55 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped for HTTP probing, so link health, redirect chains and destinations are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`
  - `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNzMs...`
- **Likely blocking:** Clients that block mixed or insecure content will probably drop these.
  - The `dotomi` pixels are 10 third-party cookie-redirect calls, a heavy ad-sync payload for one email.
  - `LiveContent.aspx` is Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content, so it may fail to render when blocked.
- **Third-party pixels:** The `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=165131` images and the `ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=165131` pixel are tracking beacons. They are HTTPS and not defects, but they are more third-party requests.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA tool says the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both (RFC 8058 one-click) for senders of this volume. Check against the raw original message, not the relay copy.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` is missing for the same relay reason, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The From domain is `e.ulta.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** I can't confirm them because the source is truncated and the QA run has no result for them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 55 click-tracked links were skipped, so destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked. The only campaign identifier visible is in the pixel parameters (`t=165131`, and the label `pr_fy26wk43_friday_bf_6am_Last`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to the 10 content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels and tracking beacons.
3. Move the `dotomi` pixels and `LiveContent.aspx` to HTTPS, or remove them. If `LiveContent` is dynamic content, verify it renders when `http://` is blocked.
4. Cut the 10 `dotomi` cookie-sync pixels to what is needed.
