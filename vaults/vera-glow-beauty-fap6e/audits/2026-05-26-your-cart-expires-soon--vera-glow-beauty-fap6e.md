# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers email number six in under three weeks, and the thing I have to say before anything else: I got "This is perfect for you!" from Skechers earlier today. Same day. Two cart-trigger emails in a single inbox session is not a retention strategy — it's a spam complaint waiting to happen. By comparison, even e.l.f. at fourteen emails since May 15th managed to space them enough that each one felt like a distinct beat. Two Skechers hits before dinner? That's just ambient noise.
- The email itself is actually better-executed than the "This is perfect for you!" send. "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." is a genuinely charming cart-abandon hook — conversational, a little self-aware. The three specific products are cleanly rendered with full names, the "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" CTA is impossible to miss, and the 20% off offer is real and visible without digging. That's better mechanics than most of the cart-abandons I've tracked this month.
- I still wouldn't click. I'm a beauty person deep in Ulta 21 Days mental math and the afterglow of Sephora's Stacking Weekend. Shoes are a secondary activity on a good day, and I got this same brand's email four hours ago. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG." is the best headline Skechers has written in this entire six-email run — it's disarming instead of desperate
- Three specific product names and images render cleanly — Glide-Step Pro, BOBS Sport Skids, Mommy & Me Glide-Step Pro — I know exactly what was in the cart
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" blue button is unambiguous: verb-noun, centered, impossible to miss
- 20% off a single pair is a concrete offer, visible without scrolling past a lifestyle photo wall like Ulta tends to do
- Clean layout — no broken images, no overlapping text, none of the countdown-timer-stuck-at-zero chaos I clocked on Charlotte Tilbury's "20% OFF Ends Tonight" send

## What's weak

- Two Skechers emails in one day completely neutralizes the urgency mechanic — if I didn't open the first send, the second one trains me to ignore both
- "Mommy & Me: Glide-Step Pro - Rosy Glow" is listed as a cart item but reads like a recommendation that wasn't labeled — either I added a matching kids shoe (unlikely) or the cart attribution is off, and that confusion creates friction right at the hero
- "Expires soon" is vague to the point of being performative — Drunk Elephant's "Last day for 25% off!" had a hard deadline I could actually plan around; "soon" is just vibes
- No loyalty signal anywhere — Sephora at this tier would show my Rouge balance or a bonus-point opportunity; Skechers just assumes I'll complete the order on 20% alone
- The 20% off feels evergreen because it's appeared in some form in every Skechers send I've seen — by now it doesn't read as urgency, it reads as the floor

## Recommendations

- 1. **Enforce a one-email-per-day send cap with suppression logic** — if any Skechers email hit this address in the last 12 hours, hold "Your cart expires soon!" until tomorrow. No creative work compensates for double-hitting someone's inbox.
- 2. **Replace "expires soon" with a real number** — "Your cart expires in 24 hours" or a countdown element. Sally Beauty's "Okay, Last Chance 😉" email actually worked because the deadline was embedded in the campaign arc; Skechers needs to commit to a timestamp.
- 3. **Move the 20% offer into the hero block**, above the fold — right now it lives below a product grid and a "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" divider. The offer is the close; lead with it.
- 4. **Label the Mommy & Me product** as a "Complete the Look" or "You Might Also Like" rail if it's upsell — don't let it sit in the cart-abandon slot looking like a misfire.
- 5. **Add one loyalty anchor** — even a "Skechers Elite members get an extra 5%" line would make this feel like it knows who I am.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your cart expires in 24 hours — 20% off, right now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Still thinking about the Glide-Step Pro? Here's 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The BOBS Sport Skids and Glide-Step Pro are still yours — but only until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off a single pair. Your cart holds until tomorrow. That's the deal.`
