# Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands 💸
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's a mess — subject says "50% off select brands" and I open it to what is basically FIVE emails duct-taped into one: a Stacking Weekend banner, an App Fun teaser, an Hourglass launch, a "Discover the Deals" gift-set carousel, THEN finally the 50% off banner buried past the midpoint scroll, followed by Korean Hair Care, a "point of view" razor thing, and a Beauty Chat gift plug. My thumb would be tired before I ever hit the actual offer.
- The Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit palette is genuinely a "new-only-at-Sephora" launch I'd stop for, and the Rare Beauty Selena Gomez set and K18 Biomistoric set are on-brand for my cross-shop habit — but by the time I scroll to the actual discount, I've already been sold four other things. This is the same complaint I logged yesterday on "Oooh, it's 50% off select beauty ❤️" — Sephora keeps writing the subject line as if the sale IS the email, then structuring the email like the sale is an afterthought.
- I'd mark this as read without clicking. If anything, I'd screenshot the Hourglass launch section and send it to my group chat, not the whole email — that's the only piece worth forwarding.

## What's working

- The Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit hero shot is clean and the "24 HOURS — App Early Access" urgency actually lands as credible, limited-window FOMO.
- "NEW · LIMITED EDITION," "ONLINE ONLY," "APP ONLY" tags on the gift-set grid are genuinely useful shorthand for how I decide what's worth chasing.
- Beauty Insider points math is visible up top (500 bonus points w/ SEPTBONUS) which is exactly the kind of tier-program content I'm scanning for.
- The bottom Beauty Insider ribbon with Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar is a nice touch of "we see your tier" — though it's sixth-scroll-screen-deep, so I almost never get there.

## What's weak

- The 50% off banner — the entire reason for the subject line — is stranded near the bottom of a very long scroll, after Stacking Weekend, App Fun, Hourglass, and a six-item gift-set grid. That's worse hierarchy than even Ulta's "20% off is calling" email, which at least puts the coupon code near the top.
- "Select Beauty" with no visible brand callouts on the 50% banner itself — I have to keep scrolling/clicking to find out what's actually included, and after yesterday's Hair Styling Awards bait-and-switch I don't trust "select" anymore from this sender.
- This email tries to be a launch email, a gift-guide email, a hair-care email, AND a sale email all at once — it's the Sephora version of Glossier's fifth-Labor-Day-email problem, except crammed into one send instead of five.
- Zero personalization — no "Hi [name]," no mention of my Rouge tier by name, no product recs based on what I've bought. Ulta at least drops my first name ("Marisol, ENDS TODAY!") even in a cluttered send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: one pure "50% off Select Beauty" email with the brands/categories included stated in the first screen, and a separate Hourglass launch email. Trying to do both in one is why the offer reads as buried.
- 2. Move the 50% off banner to directly under the header ribbon — right now Stacking Weekend AND Hourglass both outrank the subject-line offer in visual priority.
- 3. Name the "select brands" in the hero — even three logos (Rare Beauty, Tatcha, K18) would make the offer feel concrete instead of vague.
- 4. Add my name and tier status near the top, not just at the bottom Insider ribbon — Ulta's doing this already and it reads more personal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rare Beauty, Tatcha & more — 50% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge perk: 50% off select brands, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See which brands are included before they sell out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: Hourglass just launched, 24-hour early access`
