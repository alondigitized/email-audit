# ✨ Sephora App Fest ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, wow, this is the full-scroll version of the App Fest email I already flagged this morning at a 7/10, and honestly seeing the whole thing top to bottom actually bumps my opinion up a little — the day-by-day calendar is genuinely useful and I can see myself screenshotting the Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday grid to plan my week around it. "0 points" jumping out right at the top before I even get to the content is a little rough on the ego but at least it's honest math I can act on.
- The trial-size PERFSKIN offer up top with Medik8, AESTURA, and Ulta Violette is a nice hook, but it's buried under the fold psychologically because App Fest is screaming for attention right below it — and then there's SO much scroll after that before I hit anything that feels personalized to me specifically. Where's my Rouge tier? I'm not seeing a single "you're Rouge, here's your early window" callout anywhere in this whole scroll, which after a full read genuinely surprises me for their biggest calendar event of the season.
- Bottom line: I'd screenshot the deals calendar for reference and probably tap through on Thursday for the Afterpay stacking, but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — it's dense and useful, not delightful, and the tier-blindness is a real miss on their marquee event.

## What's working

- The Tuesday/Wednesday/Thursday/Friday calendar with brand logos (tarte, Dyson, Anastasia, MUFE, amika, Charlotte Tilbury) is scannable and I know exactly what to shop and when — way better organized than Ulta's single "20% off one item, ends tonight" mechanic they've been running since 7/26.
- The 500 Beauty Insider bonus points challenge is a real, concrete gamification hook that Ulta's generic couponing doesn't even attempt.
- Afterpay stacking 10% off on Thursday is a smart layer for anyone splitting a big Sephora haul, and the "stacks with other offers" badge on each day removes the guesswork.
- "Spot it. Shop it." trend section (hair accessories, makeup-feed dupes, under-$25 picks) gives me a scroll-worthy reason to keep going instead of bouncing after the calendar.

## What's weak

- Zero Rouge-tier personalization anywhere in this entire scroll — no "your early access window," no tier badge, nothing that acknowledges I'm not a VIB. For their biggest event of the year that's a real gap next to how Sephora usually flexes tier status.
- "0 points" sitting right at the top feels like an accidental gut-punch before any offer even loads — not a great emotional entry point.
- This is a LOT of scroll for one email — five distinct day-blocks plus a points challenge plus three more merchandising modules before the footer. My thumb is tired by the time I hit Beauty Under $25.
- The hero PERFSKIN trial-size offer at the very top gets completely orphaned once APP FEST kicks in — I basically forgot it existed by the time I finished reading.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a Rouge-specific line right under the APP FEST banner — even just "Rouge: your early access starts now" would make this feel like it was sent to me and not the whole file.
- 2. Trim the scroll — the trend modules (Hair Faves, All Over Your Feed, Beauty Under $25) could be a separate "shop the vibe" send later in the week instead of tacked onto an already-long calendar email.
- 3. Move the 500-point Beauty Insider Challenge higher, right after the calendar — it's the single best differentiator versus Ulta's generic 20%-off code and it's currently buried two-thirds down.
- 4. Kill or reposition the "0 points" top banner — lead with something like "Enjoy Same-Day Delivery" only, save the points balance for lower in the email.
