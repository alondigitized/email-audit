# FREE Limited-Edition Stickers When You Shop Unreal Icons! 🧡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Charlotte Tilbury, send number six (at least) since June 11. I want that to be the first thing on record, because it's impossible to read this email like a fresh subscriber when my inbox is basically a CT wall right now. And honestly? This one is competent — clean layout, recognizable hero, clear CTA — but "competent" is doing a lot of heavy lifting here when the previous send ("Get Your Summer Glow To Go, Darling!") was genuinely aspirational and this one is... stickers.
- Free limited-edition stickers. With qualifying purchase. On the app. The offer is charming in theory — sticker culture is real, I own sticker sheets — but the moment I clocked the app requirement buried in the body copy, I felt the same way I feel when a free shipping offer turns out to have a $200 minimum. The subject promised me a gift. The fine print made it a download incentive. That gap is the whole problem.
- The single thing keeping me from archiving immediately is that I'm already in the Unreal line — I have the Gloss and I've been eyeing the new Highlighter — so the GWP math might still pencil out. But I'd mark this read for now and come back if the sticker sheet is actually cute enough to screenshot. I'd forward this to exactly zero friends.

## What's working

- **"GET YOUR FREE GIFT" CTA is unmissable** — orange button, prominent placement, copy that actually says what you get. No "Discover" or "Explore" vagueness here.
- **The warm orange palette holds the brand** — six emails in and CT's visual identity is still immediately recognizable; this doesn't look cheap or off-brand despite being a lower-stakes send.
- **The product name-check in body copy is useful** — "Unreal Highlighter Fresh, Healthy Glow Stick" and "Unreal Skin Hydrating Foundation Stick" give me a quick mental map of what qualifies; I don't have to click just to find out.
- **The hero-to-app-module separation works structurally** — the GWP section and the "New! Unreal Highlighter — Shop Now On The App" module are visually distinct enough that the email isn't a total jumble.

## What's weak

- **App requirement is buried bait-and-switch energy** — "Unlock Free Stickers!" is the hero promise; "on the App" is the condition, and it's in the body copy, not the button. If Glossier pulled this they'd get ratio'd on Twitter.
- **Stickers as GWP feel undersized for the spend** — I'm a Sephora Rouge tier shopper. When I'm spending $80–$110 on an Unreal product, the free gift should at minimum be a deluxe mini. A sticker sheet reads like it belongs with a Ulta $15 threshold offer, not a CT purchase event.
- **Zero deadline visible** — no "while supplies last," no expiry date, nothing. Compare this to the June 13 send that at least had an event hook. This one floats in time like an evergreen drip, which kills every scrap of urgency the "free gift" framing built.
- **No human model** — the June 14 "Summer Glow To Go" email had aspirational skin and vibes. This one is all graphics and product shots. The demo drop in energy is real.
- **The "Magical savings: Up to magical 30% off" block is meaningless by now** — it's been in the footer of every CT email this month. Off what? For whom? When? I scroll past it on autopilot.

## Recommendations

- 1. **State the qualifying threshold and the sticker contents in the hero** — "Spend $50 on Unreal Icons, get this sticker kit free" with a close-up of the actual sheet. Treat the sticker as a mini collab drop, not a bonus line item.
- 2. **Add a deadline in the subject or hero** — "This weekend only" or "Limited quantities" — anything to create a reason to click now vs. later (which is never).
- 3. **Be upfront about the app requirement** — either add "App-exclusive" to the subject line so I know before I open, or remove the app gate entirely for a purchase-anywhere redemption. Don't let me get to the button and then discover the condition.
- 4. **Retire the "Magical savings" footer module** — it's doing reputational damage at this point; it reads as filler, not benefit.
- 5. **Lean into the sticker aesthetic if you're going there** — show me the full sticker sheet prominently. If they're actually cute or collectible, that's the hero image, not an orange abstract background.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Unreal Icons + a free sticker kit — this weekend only 🧡`
- **Subject Alt B:** `App-exclusive: free stickers with any Unreal Icon purchase`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the Highlighter, Foundation Stick, or Gloss — sticker kit is yours`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited quantities — your free sticker kit ships with your Unreal order`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on Braze (ESP), using standard table-based layout with MSO conditionals and a 600px fixed container. The HTML is structurally sound but has a font-loading failure vector in Gmail/webmail and several unverifiable items due to the truncated source.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click tracking:** All links route through `clicks.charlottetilbury.com` (Braze redirect). This is expected, but the final destination URLs and whether UTM parameters survive the redirect cannot be confirmed from the source alone — see §6.

**Image CDN:** Images are served from `cdn.braze.eu` (Braze EU data center). The cache-buster parameter `?1780997459` is a Unix timestamp consistent with a recent asset upload date — no issue.

**No issues found** with tracking domain configuration itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Font loading will silently fail in Gmail (web):**
```html
<!--[if !mso]><!-->
<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css" rel="stylesheet" type="text/css">
<!--<![endif]-->
```
Gmail and many webmail clients strip `<link>` tags from `<head>`. The MSO conditional correctly excludes Outlook, but Gmail/Yahoo will not load this font. Without seeing the `font-family` fallback stack in the CSS, it's unknown whether a system-font fallback is specified. If not, the browser default (typically Times New Roman or serif) will render instead.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Several email clients (notably some mobile clients) surface the `<title>` value in thread lists or notification previews. Leaving it blank is a minor regression — it should match the subject line.

**Accessibility positives confirmed:**
- `role="presentation"` on all layout tables ✓
- `alt` text present on visible hero image: `alt="NEW! UNREAL HIGHLIGHTER FRESH, HEALTHY GLOW STICK"` ✓
- `lang="en"` on `<html>` ✓
- `display:block` on images (prevents inline spacing gaps) ✓

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All destination URLs are encoded inside Braze tracking redirects (`clicks.charlottetilbury.com/f/a/...`). The decoded destination and its UTM parameters cannot be inspected from the HTML source.

**Risk:** If Braze is not configured to append UTM parameters before redirect, attribution will be lost. Recommend verifying in the Braze campaign link settings that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are appended to all destination URLs.
