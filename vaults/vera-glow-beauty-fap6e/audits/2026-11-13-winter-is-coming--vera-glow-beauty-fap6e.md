# Winter is coming
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Drunk Elephant email in two weeks that isn't another Singles Day retread, and I'm relieved. The 11/5 "Singles Day steals" through 11/11 "Last day: FREE $60 gift" run was the same $100 sample gift four times. This one has a new story: Maison Moisture refill kits, a holiday collection, and a "samples up to 6!" ladder. The yellow-and-aqua hero is loud and clean, and I know it's a holiday launch within a second.
- My problem is the math. Three bundles are priced at $89, $83 and $89 against values of $135, $123 and $139, but nothing tells me which products I'm actually getting. "One Protini + a refill" doesn't say what size. The sample ladder ($65+, $95+, $125+) is hidden in the black banner, and the fine print is a wall of gray text. I'd have to do real work to figure out my best cart, so I'd probably only click if I already wanted a moisturizer.
- I'd click once, read, and not forward it. It's decent, but it isn't a "text a friend" email.

## What's working

- The black top banner puts the sample ladder up front: "FREE SAMPLE DUOS WITH ORDERS $65+, $95+, AND $125+." I can run tier thresholds on that immediately.
- The "REBUILD, RESTORE, REFILL. AND RENEW." hero is bold and seasonal. The three tubs on pedestals read as a collection.
- The $89, $83 and $89 circle badges with the "VALUE" crossed against the real price make the savings obvious without a calculator. About 34% off on all three.
- The color-coded labels (Strengthen + Moisturize in teal, Rescue + Recover in purple, Rebuild + Restore in coral) match each bundle's packaging. It's a quick, skimmable way to pick a skin concern.
- The "Bundle up, everyone!" Kamo block gives a real second offer: 20% off Kamo plus a moisturizer.

## What's weak

- There's no member or loyalty line anywhere. Sephora Insider emails always tell me my tier perks, and this one treats me like a stranger on a first order.
- The fine print sits in tiny gray text. "Limit one per order," "while supplies last," and "excludes gift cards" all matter for the sample ladder, and I can barely read them.
- The "SHOP NOW" links beside each bundle are small underlined text, not buttons. On my phone they're tough tap targets.
- It's not clear what a "refill kit" is or why I need one. There's no ingredient or skin-type detail and no reviews. I'd have to click through to learn what's in the box.
- I can't tell whether the "up to 6" samples stacks with the bundle prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sample ladder in the hero as a three-step graphic ($65 → 2, $95 → 4, $125 → 6) instead of a banner. That's the strongest math hook in the send.
- 2. Make each bundle's "SHOP NOW" a full-width button on mobile, and add a short line on what's in the box (sizes, refill count).
- 3. Add a skin-type or concern selector so the segmentation feels personal.
- 4. Lift the end date into the body. The fine print says 11/13 to 11/19, but a bold "Ends 11/19" near the hero would be a real deadline.
- 5. Add one review line or star rating under each bundle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 6 free samples + refill kits`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 moisturizer bundles, 34% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $65, $95 or $125 for sample duos. Ends 11/19.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Protini, Lala, Bora + refills from $83 (up to $139 value).`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email. It has three `http://` social icons, a recipient email address in a tracking URL, and a CSS block with parsing problems. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers. The source was truncated, so findings are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient email in a URL query string.** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the subscriber's address in plaintext. URLs are logged by proxies, link scanners and the browser, so this exposes PII.
- **Tracking pixels without an `alt` attribute.**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holrefillkitsre`
  - The `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image above.
  - Both need `alt=""`, and ideally `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"`.
- **15 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the 15 destinations are confirmed to resolve.
- **Two pixels from two vendors.** The `mi.` pixel and the Everest pixel both fire on open. That is redundant tracking weight, not an error.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Three social icons use `http://`.** All are on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/`:
  - Facebook: `cc02d07d-…png`
  - Instagram: `9b452915-…png`
  - TikTok: `29f975e1-…png`
  
  Clients may block or rewrite mixed-content images, so these icons can fail to render. They do have alt text.
- **`@import` is misplaced.** Three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` rules appear after the `body {…}` rule in the `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so browsers ignore them. The same fonts are also loaded by the `<link>` tags, so the imports are redundant.
- **`<!-- … -->` comments sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block.** The comments wrap `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=mobile]`. Inside a CSS block, the `<!--` and `-->` tokens become part of the next selector. That likely invalidates the `*[class=mobile1]` rule that follows the `-->`. I inferred this from the CSS and did not render it.
- **`maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Third-party font hosting.** Brown Bold, Light and Thin load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most email clients ignore web fonts, so the fallback stack matters. I could not see it in the truncated source.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** `lang` on `<html>`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the full stack of fallback fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible HTML. The only personalization seen is the `e=` parameter described in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cheers.drunkelephant.com` are unknown. This is a gap in the QA setup, not a confirmed failure.
- **Not verifiable:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the visible portion of the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign identity travels in vendor parameters: `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holrefillkitsre` on the `mi.` pixel, and `c=us_de_broad…` on the Everest URL. No standard `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated source.
- The final landing URLs are unverified because all 15 tracking links were skipped. UTM propagation and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Stop putting the raw email address in the `e=` query string. Use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID.
2. Change the three social icon URLs to `https://`. The same host should serve them over TLS.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the stylesheet, or drop them in favor of the `<link>` tags. Remove the `<!-- -->` comments from inside the `@media` block and re-test the `mobile1` rule.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the original message, not the relay copy. Add a direct-SMTP capture or a header-preserving path to the QA setup.
7. Add a probe that follows redirect chains for the skipped tracking links, so landing URLs and UTM parameters can be checked.
