# Ends tonight: Take 20% off your online purchase⁺ when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards® for free (terms & excl. apply) 🫶
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm literally an Ulta Diamond, so "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards for free" in the subject is talking to someone who isn't me. Then the body opens with "Rae, earn points that turn into $ off future purchases," and that isn't my name. The big orange "20% off your online purchase" block with code SAVE20 is the part I actually care about. It sits under a free mystery gift hero and a "PEAK SAVINGS" banner, so I scroll past two other pitches to reach it.
- This is the second 20%-off-everything send in two days. On 10/6 it was "Members, 2 DAYS!" and now it's "Ends tonight." The 8/1 "ENDS TODAY" with code SCORE wore me out the same way. The brand tiles at the bottom are the best part: Dyson, Tula, OPI, R.E.M. Beauty and Dime with 20-30% off. But they're all tagged "ONLY IN THE APP | Ends Oct 10," while the hero code is for online. Now I'm doing stacking math across two different channels and two different deadlines.
- Would I click? Yes, probably to the Dyson or Tula tile, since I'd stack a 30% brand deal with SAVE20 if the app lets me. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read after I've screenshotted the code.

## What's working

- The orange "20% off your online purchase" block with the SAVE20 code is high-contrast and impossible to miss once I reach it.
- The brand tiles show the discount on each product ("Tula 30% off," "OPI 25% off," "Entire brand"), so I can compare deals without clicking.
- The "Ends tonight | Member exclusive" tag adds credible urgency. It gives me a real deadline, and the 10/6 send said 2 days.
- The free mystery gift with a $55 purchase (choose from 3) is a real perk. It beats Glossier's holiday gift guide, which had no offer at all.
- The prestige brand mix (Dyson, Tula, Peter Thomas Roth, Dime) matches how I use Ulta 21 Days and prestige splurges.

## What's weak

- The subject and the "Not a member? Join now" button are aimed at non-members. I'm Diamond and should get a member-first message.
- The greeting says "Rae," which isn't my name, so the personalization feels off.
- There are three competing heroes before the real offer: free mystery gift, "PEAK SAVINGS," and the 20% block. The eye doesn't land in one place.
- The tiles say "ONLY IN THE APP" while the code is for online. It's confusing, and I can't tell if the code works on the app-only brands.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text, and "Includes almost everything!" still comes with a long exclusions list. I can't read it without zooming.
- The Rewards points math is missing. There's no tier status, no points balance, and no 2X or bonus-points moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split by segment. Send members a hero with their tier and points balance, and send non-members the join pitch. A Diamond should never get "when you join."
- 2. Make the 20% / SAVE20 block the hero. Move the mystery gift below it as a secondary add-on.
- 3. Resolve the app vs. online confusion. Give the tiles one clear label, or put "Works with SAVE20" on them.
- 4. Fix the name token and add points math, like "You're X points from your next $ off."
- 5. Trim the subject to about 45 characters and cut the ⁺ and the "terms & excl. apply" clutter. Move the legal text to the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 20% off + a free mystery gift 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Vera, your 20% off ends tonight (code SAVE20)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it on Dyson, Tula & more. Free gift with $55 inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn points toward your next $ off. Online code SAVE20.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed blocking defects. Its QA pass rate is 57% (0 issues, 3 warnings), and the findings are mostly missing alt text, 10 insecure `http://` third-party pixels, and unverifiable compliance headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and most `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **42 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **10 pixels load over plaintext HTTP.** These are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`.
  - Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them, and the cookie-sync calls will silently fail.
  - Each also lacks alt text.
  - They are third-party ad-network cookie-sync pixels, which adds extra third-party requests per open and a privacy and deliverability footprint.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=162237`. It is HTTPS and fine. The QA output shows the campaign string `pr_fy26wk36_wednesday_20off_dedicated_12pm_rem` and `31278` attached to it, which suggests internal campaign metadata is exposed in a rendered attribute (likely `alt` or `title`). This is a minor information leak, and I can't confirm it without the full source.
- **Secondary tracker:** `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=202740837&mi_ign=162237` and `3eb758650b65389e.png`. Both lack alt text and are likely tracking or personalization images.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 8 content-style images, all on `image.e.ulta.com`:**
  - `.../m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `.../m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`

  - `.../m/44/f6048146-d0e9-4590-9040-5734168c9dbb.png`
  - `.../m/44/70c7f19b-7d40-41be-a24e-f04cc770ef04.png`
  - If any of these carry offer or CTA content, the message is lost with images off or in screen readers. Alt text is mandatory for accessibility whenever an image conveys information.
- **Tracking and pixel images:** For the 10 dotomi pixels, `emltrk` and `mi.ulta.com`, an empty `alt=""` is acceptable. The QA reports the attribute as missing rather than empty, so they should carry `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.

- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared in both a meta tag and `:root`. Dark-mode overrides could not be verified in the truncated source.
- **Hamburger menu CSS:** It is commented out, but the store-dropdown rules (`#mobile-label-store`, `#menu-wrapper-store`) still use the checkbox hack, which is unsupported in several clients. Whether a fallback exists is unverifiable from the truncated source.
