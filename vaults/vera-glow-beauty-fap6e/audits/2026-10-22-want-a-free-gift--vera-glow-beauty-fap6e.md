# Want a free gift?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is relief, because Glossier finally sent something that isn't another "20% off, STILL ON!" Between 10/15 "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐" and 10/20 "20% off best-sellers ends tonight!" I'd had four near-identical sale emails in six days. A free Milky Jelly Cleanser with a $75+ order is a new angle, and a gift-with-purchase is something I can run the math on.
- The math is where it stalls. It's a $75 threshold and the email never says what the cleanser is worth, so I can't tell if I'm getting a $10 or a $25 value. It also just says "very limited time" with no date. The e.l.f. "Choose a full-size gift on us!" send put its threshold at $50, and Sephora's sample-bag emails at least tell me to redeem with Insider points. Glossier gives me neither a value nor a deadline.
- I'd open it out of curiosity, and I might click through. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, check whether my cart gets to $75, and move on.

## What's working

- "Something good, on us!" plus the $75+ threshold is a real, concrete offer. It's much clearer than the pretty-pictures-and-no-prices era before 10/15.
- The hero is clean. The headline, one line of copy and a "Shop now" button sit next to a big cleanser bottle, so my eye lands on the gift first.
- The "A super gentle cleansing experience!" block gives me a real reason to care: "dissolves dirt, oil and makeup… never stripped." The model with glowing deep-toned skin is a nice change from the usual pale-skin default.
- The four-product grid (Boy Brow, Ultralip, Cloud Paint Plush Blush, Futuredew) is easy to tap. Each one has its own button, so I can build a $75 cart from it without hunting.

## What's weak

- The copy in the second block is cut off on the right edge. It reads "Formulated with gentle, conditioning ingredi…" and "this cleanser dissolves dirt, oil and makeup" runs off the page. That's a visible bug, and it's the copy that sells the product.
- "For a very limited time" is empty urgency. Ulta puts a date on its "Ends tonight" emails, and I can't plan around "very limited."
- There's no cleanser price or value and no member tie-in. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, so loyalty math is how I shop, and Glossier gives me nothing to compare against.
- Every button just says "Shop now," six times. The gift offer is in the hero, but nothing tells me how to claim it, such as a code or "added at checkout." The asterisk points to a tiny "Terms apply" link at the very bottom.
- It's another Glossier send in a week that already had four. The brand cadence is wearing me out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline and the claim mechanic in the hero. Something like "Free Milky Jelly Cleanser with $75+. Ends Sunday. Auto-added at checkout." would lift this the most.
- 2. Fix the clipped text block so the full sentence about ingredients and "never stripped" shows.
- 3. Show the cleanser's value ("$24 value") so I can do the math against my usual Sephora and Ulta gifts.
- 4. Vary the button copy: "Claim my free cleanser," "Shop Boy Brow," "Shop Ultralip." Don't use six identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 5. Add social proof, like a star rating or "best-selling cleanser" under the product story.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Milky Jelly Cleanser with $75+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cleanser ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get our gentle gel-to-milk cleanser on us. Auto-added at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The cleanser that never strips, free with any $75 order. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-sent template (`stripocdn.email` images, `ctrk.klclick.com` tracking) with sound structure (`lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). The confirmed defects are missing image alt text, a few CSS quirks, and a hidden-span oddity. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture relay, not from the email itself. The source is truncated, so footer and link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unverified.** QA probed 0 of 12 material links ("12 probed, 12 skipped"), so no status codes, redirect chains or landing-page checks exist. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Invalid markup in the body.** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M5J18D5GZ6E06GRHY7JP7MZW_0"></span>` is a `<span>` with `rel`/`href` attributes, which are not valid on a span. It looks like a Klaviyo link-tracking artifact and is harmless in practice. It does mean the tracked URL sits in a non-anchor element that clients may strip.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M5J18D5GZ6E06GRHY7JP7MZW` is the open-tracking pixel and has no alt text. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Six content images have no alt text (confirmed by QA):** `heroimage_1_73u.jpg`, `group_3971_Vsc.jpg`, `group_3986_VDp.jpg`, `group_3987_AUC.jpg`, `group_3986_1_kue.jpg`, `group_3987_1_jxI.jpg`. With images blocked, the hero and the following blocks render blank. Screen-reader users get no content, or just the filename.
- **The Gmail fix selector can't match.** The CSS contains `u + .body img ~ div div { display: none }`, but the tag is a bare `<body>` with no `class="body"`. That hack never applies.
- **Mobile `h2` is small.** The `@media (max-width: 600px)` rule sets `h2 { font-size: 12px !important }`, below the 14px used for `p`/`a` (`.bo p, .bo a`). If `h2` carries real content, it will be hard to read on mobile.
- **No other issues confirmed.** Preheader hiding (`display:none; mso-hide:all`), the dark-mode image rule and the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` block are standard. The markup after the preheader is truncated, so table structure, `role="presentation"` and button fallbacks can't be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them. That makes it a capture limitation, not a confirmed sender non-compliance. Both should be checked against the raw message from the original delivery.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sending domain is `eml.glossier.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **The footer is not in the truncated source.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link can't be verified here. QA notes an unsubscribe link was included in link ranking, but its URL isn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible, because the links are Klaviyo-wrapped and the source is truncated. The probes that would show the final landing URL were all skipped. Nothing here is confirmed as broken.

2. Add `class="body"` to `<body>`, or remove the dead `u + .body …` selector.
3. Check whether `h2` content at 12px on mobile is readable. If it is body or offer copy, raise it to 14px or more.
4. Re-run link probing against the top CTAs, and capture the final URLs and UTM parameters after Klaviyo redirects.
