# Another round of Protini!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: I got a Protini email yesterday, one the day before, and one on 10/11. Now it's "another round" of the same jar. The kit hero is pretty, with the mint refill pods on a pale blue background and the yellow band, but it's the fifth Drunk Elephant send in a week, and I'm doing the math on whether I even need to read it. The "Made-to-Measure" kit with an "$89 value $135" bubble is the same kit I saw on 10/9 ("Before the fall…"), just re-shot.
- The one thing I actually care about is buried in the bottom block, in tiny gray type. It's the Kamo + full-size moisturizer purchase that gets a free pouch and a B-Hydra deluxe sample, and it ends 10/17 at 2:59 AM ET, which is basically tonight. That's the real hook, and it's sitting under the fold in 8-point text. I'd screenshot it for my Ulta 21 Days / Sephora cross-shop list, but I wouldn't click straight away. I'd probably leave it unread, not forward it, and wait for the real holiday set drop.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "ALL THE FILLS" with the three kits lined up reads fast, and the refill-pod concept is a real product story.
- The "$89 value $135" bubble gives me something to do math on. Ulta and Sephora would never show a value-versus-price this clearly on a skincare set.
- The "Pick Your Own Pouch Day" block has a concrete GWP and a clear mechanic: buy Kamo plus a moisturizer, pick a pouch.
- Three short benefit lines (gel-cream, peptides, firms) are scannable on a phone.
- The "cuckoo bananas crazytown" copy has personality. It's still a recognizable Drunk Elephant voice.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is new. It's the same kit, the same peptide copy, and the same GWP I've seen since 10/9–10/11. I can't tell why this send exists.
- The deadline and GWP terms are in tiny gray fine print at the bottom. A real offer ending tonight should be in the hero.
- "$89 value $135" doesn't tell me what I pay. Is it $89 for $135 of product? The bubble looks like a price but reads like a value claim, so I can't do my math cleanly.
- There are no points, no Rouge or Diamond-style member perk, no reviews, and no model or skin-tone signal. Sephora's recent "Makeup Sets: Here & Nowhere Else" send at least had a per-block structure with prices.
- Both main CTAs are the same "SHOP NOW" box, and neither names a product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Pick-Your-Own-Pouch deadline into the hero: "Free pouch + B-Hydra sample ends tonight." That's the only urgency in the email and it's currently hidden.
- 2. Give the kit an actual price: "$89 (a $135 value)". Add a "save $46" tag so I don't have to calculate it.
- 3. Stop sending one SKU five days in a row. Rotate in a different product or a customer story, like the Karen piece on 10/15, which I liked more.
- 4. Replace "SHOP NOW" with specific CTAs, e.g. "Get the Protini refill kit" and "Claim my free pouch".
- 5. Add a social-proof line, such as a review count or "best-seller", under the kit.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free pouch ends tonight 🕑`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Protini refill kit: $89 ($135 value)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy Kamo + a moisturizer, pick a free pouch. Ends 2:59 AM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One Protini + one refill, $46 less than buying separately.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The confirmed problems are missing alt attributes on two tracking images, three `http://` social icons, a recipient email address exposed in a tracking URL, and a few CSS and viewport patterns that hurt robustness. Header-based checks (unsubscribe, authentication) could not be verified from the data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. One example is `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`.
- **Two tracking image beacons are in use.**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holprotinirefil` is an open pixel.

  - Two separate tracking vendors add load and a second place for the subscriber identifier to leak.
- **The HTML source was truncated.** I can't confirm the full link inventory, the `href` values on the CTAs, or whether any are broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two images.** Both are tracking images, so `alt=""` is the correct fix.
  - `o.gif` (`mi.drunkelephant.com`)
  - the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` beacon
- **Three social icons load over plain `http://`.** The sources are `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`, for Facebook (`cc02d07d-…`), Instagram (`9b452915-…`) and TikTok (`29f975e1-…`). Clients and proxies may block or warn on mixed content. Other images on the same host may use `https`, so the mixed usage looks inconsistent.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Web fonts depend on third parties.** The `<link>` and `@import` entries load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and `@font-face` loads from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…`. Most email clients ignore these, so fallback stacks matter, but I can't see them in the truncated source.
- **`@import url(...)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule.** Per CSS, `@import` must precede other rules, so these are invalid and will be ignored. The `<link>` tags do the same job in the few clients that support them.
- **HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->`) sit inside the `@media` block.** Some parsers will mishandle the rule after them.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute** in the visible portion. This hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **The `<title>` is only "Drunk Elephant".** It is generic and does not match the subject.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) in the visible source.
- **The recipient's email is in plaintext in a URL.** The beacon contains `?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`, so the address appears in the query string of a third-party request. That exposes PII in logs, referrers and proxies. A hashed or opaque ID would avoid this.
- **A numeric subscriber ID is also exposed.** `mi_u=98161986` is in the open-pixel URL.

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. If the sender really omits it, they would fall short of Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com` cannot be assessed from this data.
- **The visible footer HTML (unsubscribe link, physical address) is not in the truncated source.** I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements.
