# This lip combo is e.l.f.ing sweet 🫶🍭 
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Email nineteen from e.l.f. since May 15th. I'm not exaggerating — I have the count. And yet "e.l.f.ing sweet" with candy emojis did make me tap open because the playful wordplay is genuinely one of the few things this brand does consistently well. More to the point: "$12 lip combo" is points math I can run in my sleep. I'm a Ulta Diamond member actively building my 21 Days haul list, and this just made the shortlist.
- The "YOUR GLOW-TOS" pun earned a real smile — first genuinely clever moment in at least a week of their marathon. Four shade names that actually tell me the vibe before I click (Cotton Candy, Fuchsia Forward, Peach Ring, Spill the Tea) is the kind of product detail that helps me decide without a trip to the landing page. Then the Target receipts module showed up in the middle of the email and the mood died. It reads like a different campaign got spliced in.
- Would I click? Yes — on the shade swatches, as research for my next Ulta run. Would I forward it? No. The cadence has trained me to assume whatever e.l.f. sends today will still exist when I actually decide to buy.

## What's working

- **$12 is in the hero, above the fold, zero ambiguity** — I don't have to hunt for the price. That's the single number that earns a second of real attention.
- **"GLOW-TOS" pun** — first copy moment in this month's e.l.f. marathon that felt written by a human rather than a campaign scheduler.
- **Shade names do the selection work** — Cotton Candy, Fuchsia Forward, Peach Ring, Spill the Tea tell me tone family instantly; this is the product detail I need to decide between a quick "add to cart" now versus waiting.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — hero offer → shade picks → secondary modules, in that order. Nothing fights the $12 message at the top.
- **Target rewards cross-promo is at least trying to differentiate** — Charlotte Tilbury and ILIA aren't offering me a receipt-upload loyalty angle; it's the only thing in this email that doesn't exist in the last eighteen sends.

## What's weak

- **Email nineteen with no deadline = no urgency whatsoever** — ILIA sent me "Coming 06.02—our softest launch yet" this week and I set a calendar reminder. This email gives me zero reason not to wait until Ulta 21 Days opens.
- **Target receipts module breaks the lippy flow** — it drops mid-email right after the shade grid and feels like a completely different ask; Sephora weaves loyalty messaging into the offer instead of bolting on a separate module.
- **"Top Picks for You" is anonymous personalization** — the label says it's curated for me but I can't tell if those three products are actually based on my history or just bestsellers wearing my name like a costume.
- **"SHOP NOW" is doing minimum wage work** — Glossier at least writes "Shop the Pink Capsule" or "Grab yours"; this is the laziest CTA possible on what is otherwise a tighter-than-average e.l.f. email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline.** Even "This weekend only" or "Through Sunday" converts the shade-interest into a same-session decision instead of a future haul note.
- 2. **Move the Target receipts module to the footer** — one line, a small logo, done. Don't let it kill the lip-combo momentum right when the shade names are working.
- 3. **Replace "SHOP NOW" with offer-specific CTA copy** — "Get the $12 Combo" or "Pick Your Shade" ties the button to the specific decision I'm already making.
- 4. **Make "Top Picks for You" earn the label** — show me something tied to my last purchase or browse history, not just bestsellers. Right now it reads like the personalization header is lying.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Custom redirect domain is properly configured** — all links route through `links.elfcosmetics.com` (not a generic ESP subdomain).

**UTM parameters unverifiable from source.** All destination URLs are Braze-encoded opaque blobs:
```
https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/5zcfUnOkhsex9c6S3KW9yA~~/AAQRxRA~/6uqyaSYn...
```
UTM params may be appended post-redirect, but cannot be confirmed without decoding or a live click-trace. This is a standard Braze pattern but worth flagging for QA validation.

**`tabindex="-1"` on every linked image** — the hero image anchor uses `tabindex="-1"`:
```html
<a href="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/..." tabindex="-1">
```
This removes image-wrapped CTAs from keyboard tab order. If this pattern repeats across all image links, keyboard-only users cannot activate any image-based CTA.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag.**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers and some preview panes use `<title>` as document label. Should be set to the subject line or a short description.

**Double-wrapped `nl-container` inside `html_block > div`.** The outer layout wraps inner `html_block` cells in a `<div>`, then re-opens a full `nl-container` table tree inside it. This nested-table-in-div pattern can cause Outlook width calculation failures.

**`<style>` blocks embedded inside `<td>` elements.** Gmail and several webmail clients strip `<style>` tags that appear outside `<head>`. The responsive `.responsive-text` `@media` query is declared inside a `<td>`-nested `<div>` — it will silently fail in Gmail.

**Google Fonts load will fail universally for email clients.**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Open+Sans" rel="stylesheet">
```
Gmail, Outlook (all versions), Yahoo, and Apple Mail block external `<link>` stylesheets. The fallback `Arial, Sans-serif` will render in all clients; the `Open+Sans` load is wasted overhead.

**`Futura Std` / `Jost` declared but never loaded.**
```css
font-family: 'Futura Std', Futura, 'Jost', Verdana, sans-serif;
```
Neither `Futura Std` nor `Jost` is included via any `@font-face` or external `<link>`. Rendering falls through to `Verdana` in all clients.
