## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails on core deliverability infrastructure — missing plain-text alternative, no unsubscribe headers, and multiple tracked CTA links resolve to unroutable relative product paths instead of absolute URLs.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All CTAs route through a Bronto/Cheetah-style click tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), consistent with standard ESP redirect wrapping.
- 4 of 12 probed material links fail resolution with "unknown url type," each decoding to a **relative path with no scheme/host**:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-daily-cleanser?Size=Regular+3.7+oz+%2F+110+mL` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning" link)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&shortlink=email&...` ("Download the e.l.f. app")
  - These are the underlying destination URLs encoded inside the tracking redirect; the redirect wrapper itself resolves, but the final destination is malformed (missing `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` domain prefix), which will break the click-through for any client/proxy that validates the URL before following the redirect.
- 29 of 41 links were not probed (footer/utility/social, deprioritized) — coverage is partial; unscanned links carry unknown risk.
