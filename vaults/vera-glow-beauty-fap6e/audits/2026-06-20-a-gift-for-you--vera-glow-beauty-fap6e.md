# A gift for you
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- Six. This is number six from ILIA in roughly nine days, and I'm keeping count because the June 18 send — "This lip color lasts. This gift won't." — already played the gift card. Walking up to my inbox two days later with "A gift for you" feels like ILIA is hoping I have the memory of a goldfish. The subject line is so generic it could have come from literally anyone: Lush, a Sephora editorial blast, a birthday email from my dentist. It nearly got a swipe-archive based on the preview alone.
- Inside the email, ILIA earns it back. "Stay sharp" is a genuinely clever pun for a sharpener GWP, the shade callouts — PAPAYA, BLUE NOTE — are specific enough to make me reach for my mental swatch drawer, and the 12-hour hold plus Sea Succulent ingredient callout is exactly the language that gets me to add to cart. The production quality is doing heavy lifting the subject line refuses to do. I'd click — lip products are my category and the free sharpener math makes sense — but I wouldn't forward it, because sending someone "A gift for you" from ILIA feels embarrassing, and the June 18 version was a better emotional entry point for the same campaign.
- This reads like a reminder email that forgot to say it's a reminder.

## What's working

- **"Stay sharp" hero headline** — the pun is earned and lands cleanly over the lifestyle shot; best single copy moment across ILIA's entire June lip arc
- **Shade specificity** — naming PAPAYA and BLUE NOTE with actual copy ("a poppy red that's all treat no trick") does the work swatches alone can't, and it makes me want to buy the specific shade, not just the product
- **Benefit stack is focused and credible** — 12-hour hold, anti-bleed, hydration, Sea Succulent; this is the ingredient-adjacent copywriting that builds trust with someone who reads the back of the tube
- **$14 price anchor is visible** — makes the GWP feel like genuine value rather than a clearance tactic
- **Clean render, clean layout** — no broken images, legible at mobile size, hierarchy flows from hero to product to CTA without visual clutter

## What's weak

- **"A gift for you" is the laziest subject line in the batch** — no product name, no offer amount, no brand signal; it would lose a split test against almost anything with a noun in it
- **Same gift angle two days later** — "This gift won't" on June 18 vs. "A gift for you" on June 20 is the exact same emotional territory; even Charlotte Tilbury, who has been hammering "Unreal" for two solid weeks, rotates her framing more than this
- **No expiration on the GWP** — Glossier's "Final Hours: Up to 25% off" moved me because it had a clock; ILIA gives me zero reason to act today versus next week
- **No loyalty or tier acknowledgment** — I'm doing the math on every Sephora Rouge event and Ulta 21 Days of Beauty window, and this email treats me like a cold prospect who just wandered in from a Google ad

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject line to lead with the actual offer** — "A gift for you" is a placeholder, not a sell; naming the product and the benefit earns the open
- 2. **Add a deadline to the GWP** — even a "while supplies last" banner converts browsing intent into same-session purchases; the cart-and-ghost is real
- 3. **Differentiate the June 20 send from June 18** — if this is the urgency follow-up, say that: "last chance" or "this week only" signals that this is a different beat in the same campaign, not a copy-paste
- 4. **Acknowledge the customer relationship** — even a single line differentiating a known subscriber from a cold email shows you've been paying attention; Drunk Elephant does this better with their "oh, my stars" intimacy, even when the copy is oblique
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stay sharp: free sharpener with every Lip Sketch`
- **Subject Alt B:** `PAPAYA, BLUE NOTE + a free gift — shop Lip Sketch`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `12-hour hold, no feathering, and the best pencil sharpener you didn't know you needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your new lip routine is one shade and one free tool away.`
