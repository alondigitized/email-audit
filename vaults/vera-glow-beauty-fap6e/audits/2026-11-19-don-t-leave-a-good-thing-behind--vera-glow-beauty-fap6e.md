# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again, and this is the same browse-abandonment template I've gotten a dozen times, with a Black Friday banner stuck underneath. "Selling fast… add to cart before they're gone!" is manufactured urgency, and I've heard it on 11/13 ("This is perfect for you!") and 11/14 ("This style has limited availability!"). The Britto S-1992 hero is a fun, colorful sneaker, but I never looked at it. Nothing here tells me why it's mine.
- The real offer, 30% off sitewide for Skechers Plus members, is buried below two rows of product thumbnails. It's the same deal as yesterday's "Early access is open!" email, so I'm getting the offer for the second time in two days. I'd have done the points math in half a second if it had been at the top.
- Would I click? No, I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either. If I were shopping for sneakers, I might scroll to the red banner, but I'm not.

## What's working

- The red "30% OFF / SITEWIDE FOR MEMBERS" banner is unmistakable, and the white-on-red contrast reads instantly.
- The Britto hero is a clean product shot on white, and the single navy "SHOP NOW" button is obvious.
- The six-shoe grid is tidy, and every shoe is labeled as a women's style.
- The fine print states the dates (11/18–11/22), the online-only limit and the exclusions, so the offer doesn't feel like a bait-and-switch.

## What's weak

- The 30% off sits below the fold, under the hero and six thumbnails. Sephora would put the Beauty Insider event number in the first screen, and Ulta would put it in a banner.
- The hero has no price, no offer and no member callout. "Selling fast" with no number is just filler.
- The "Shop Now" in the red banner is small text with tiny gray fine print, so it doesn't look like a button.
- The offer repeats yesterday's send, and the "great options we think you'll love" grid carries no reviews or prices. Drunk Elephant's "$158 of skincare for $69?!" shows how a concrete number makes a send click.
- The footer is a long stack of generic modules (texting, app, curbside, Afterpay, Klarna). It's a lot of clutter for an email that's supposed to be urgent.
- The red banner ends with "See below for details*", which sends me down to the footer to read the terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off banner to the top and make the hero the offer. Ilia and e.l.f. Cosmetics both lead with the number. Pair it with the hero shoe and a real "Shop 30% off" button.
- 2. Drop the fake-scarcity line unless there's real inventory data behind it.
- 3. Show the member price on each shoe (for example, "$89 → $62 with Plus") so the points math is done for me.
- 4. Cut the footer modules to one row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off ends Sunday: Plus members only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Britto sneakers + 30% member price`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Auto-applied at cart through 11/22. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members save 30% sitewide. Sale ends Sunday.`
