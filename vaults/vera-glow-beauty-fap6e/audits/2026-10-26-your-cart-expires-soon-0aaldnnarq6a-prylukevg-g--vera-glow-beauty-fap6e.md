# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh fake-cart email from Skechers in about ten days, and I still haven't put a single thing in a cart. "You liked these and you weren't wrong" is a cute line, but the button underneath says "Complete your order." I have no order. Back on 10/22 ("Did you forget something?") and 10/23 ("Your Items Are Waiting!") I called out this same beat. Nothing has changed except the subject line now adds a ticking clock.
- The hero is one lilac GO WALK Workout Walker on a lot of white space. It's a clean shoe and I'd consider it for a gym day. The real offer, "Members take 20% off and earn a $5 reward on $75+", sits about two-thirds of the way down. I have to scroll past six more sneakers to find it. As a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, I read points math first, and the $5 reward on $75 is only about 6.7% back. That's weak next to Ulta's 21 Days or a Sephora Savings Event.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it, since there's nothing to share and "cart expires" doesn't apply to me.

## What's working

- The "You liked these" headline is warm and confident, and it's a better voice than the usual "Don't leave these behind."
- The hero shoe is a soft lilac slip-in with no kids' toy-truck sneaker. That fits me far better than the 10/19 Skechers Garage send.
- The 20% off band is high-contrast navy with huge type, so the offer is easy to read once I reach it.
- The grid of six styles has readable captions, and the Care Bears and floral BOBS give me some personality.
- The women/men/kids nav stack has big tap targets that are easy to hit on a phone.

## What's weak

- "Complete your order" with no cart is a stranger pretending to be a friend. It's the same fake-cart beat as the 10/22 "Don't leave these styles behind."
- The 20% off and $5 reward are buried below the hero and the product grid. Sephora puts the offer first, and it would never make me scroll for it.
- The "Members take 20% off" offer shows an end date of 10/26 in tiny gray type, the same day this email lands. The "expires soon" urgency is real, but nothing in the email states it.
- The fine print is a wall of legalese: "select regular priced items", "excludes sale items", "must be logged in." It reads like an exclusion list, not an honest offer.
- The footer is overstuffed with texting, app, curbside, Afterpay, Klarna, find-a-store and social. That's seven asks after the main one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Send this only to people who actually have a cart, and move the 20% off and $5 reward band to the top, under the hero.
- 2. Replace "Complete your order" with a specific button like "Shop the GO WALK Workout Walker." If there's no cart, drop the cart framing altogether.
- 3. Put the 10/26 deadline in the headline of the offer band as "Ends tonight," not in gray fine print.
- 4. Cut the footer clutter down to one secondary ask.
- 5. Show the loyalty math on the page, such as "$75 = $5 back," so I can compare it to Ulta and Sephora.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders structurally sound, with table layout, a viewport meta tag, `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` and mobile breakpoints. It has three confirmed technical problems: no plain-text part, 13 images served over `http://`, and an unsubscribe link that failed the probe with a 400. The QA pass rate was 38%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returned 400 [FAIL].** The probe of `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…` got a 400. The token is per-recipient, so a probe from another client or IP may be rejected. This may be a probe artifact, but it should be verified manually because it is a compliance-critical link.
- **Primary CTAs returned 429, not confirmed broken.** "Complete Your Order" and "You liked these and you weren't wrong." both go through `skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/…`. A 429 is rate-limiting by the tracking host, so I can't confirm the destination resolves.
- **Opaque click wrapping.** Every probed link, including "web version", goes through the Attentive redirector with long opaque tokens. The final landing URLs can't be checked from the source.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 45 links were probed and 33 were skipped, so the footer and social links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content: 13 images use `http://`.** All are on `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/{1,5,11}/…`. Affected images include:
  - the logo ("Skechers")
  - the hero CTA image ("Complete Your Order")
  - the app-store badges
  - Afterpay
  - the social icons

  Clients and proxies may block or rewrite them. The fix is to serve the same host over HTTPS.
- **Missing alt text.**
  - `a6ec5198-5437-4eaa-8613-eacc775726f9.jpg` is a content image with no alt.
  - The open-tracking pixel (`skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=…`) has none. That one is low severity, but it should have `alt=""`.
- **Weak alt text.** The image `49468f73-…png` has the alt "Text", which is a placeholder rather than a description.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head. This is minor.
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars [FAIL].** There is no `text/plain` alternative. That hurts accessibility and deliverability, and clients that prefer plain text show a blank message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Because the source is truncated, the lower body wasn't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link failed the probe (400).** See section 2. It is present in the body, labelled "Unsubscribe".
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may be stripping these headers. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements would not be met. This needs verification against the raw headers at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `msgs.skechers.com`.
- **Physical postal address** isn't visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified because every link is wrapped in an Attentive redirect (`/e/c/<token>`). No destination URLs were resolved, because the probes returned 429 or were skipped.
- The preheader says "Join Skechers Plus today for free shipping!" while the subject and CTA concern the cart ("Complete Your Order"). I couldn't confirm that any link lands on a cart-restore page or a Plus signup page. This is a risk, not a confirmed defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative. This is the only QA FAIL on the deliverability side.
2. Switch all 13 `image.emails.skechers.com` image URLs to `https://`.
3. Manually click the unsubscribe link from the real inbox copy to see if the 400 reproduces. If it does, fix the Attentive unsubscribe route.
4. Check the raw message headers at the origin (before the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
5. Add an alt to `a6ec5198-…jpg`, replace "Text" with a real description, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
6. Fill in the empty `<title>`.
7. Re-probe the "Complete Your Order" CTA with throttling or retry to get the final landing URL. Then check that its UTM parameters and the cart-restore behavior work.
