## Executive summary

- Okay, an eighth send from Sally Beauty in my logs, and this one's actually a nice reset — no "Appiversary" spend-ladder, no press-on nail BOGO, just a straightforward L'Oréal Technique hair color launch. The braided red hair hero shot is genuinely arresting — great texture, gorgeous saturated color — but here's the thing: I don't dye my own hair at home, I'm getting balayage touch-ups at a salon my colorist controls, so a box-dye wall (even a "salon-inspired" one) just isn't speaking to a Rouge/Diamond tier shopper like me. It's the same beat as their 9/17 "Fall color, with a glossy finish" send, which I scored higher because at least that one felt like a fresher category move — this one's back to the six-color swatch grid I've seen from every drugstore hair brand.
- Nothing here mentions points, tier status, or loyalty — and I don't even have a Sally Beauty account, so this brand keeps missing me on the exact thing that would actually hook me. ## What's working

- That braided red hair hero image is a scroll-stopper — rich color, good lighting, feels editorial not stock.
- "Keep Color Fresh All Season" is a real seasonal hook, not stale copy — feels tied to actual fall.
- Clean single-CTA hierarchy up top: headline → subhead → one black "Shop now" button, no clutter.
- Six product tiles are organized and easy to scan, each with its own "Shop now" link.
- Klarna 4-payments messaging is a nice touch for anyone stocking up on multiple boxes.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty/points language anywhere — as a Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond shopper who lives by the points math, an email with nothing for me to earn or redeem just reads as generic.
- At-home box dye doesn't match how I actually shop color — I get professional color, so this whole category is a miss for my persona regardless of execution.
- No shade-match or skin-tone personalization despite this being literally a hair-color-selection email — Drunk Elephant's Kamo shade-finder tool from their recent sends does this so much better.
- Repetitive product-grid layout (six near-identical boxes) gets visually monotonous by the fourth tile.
- No urgency or time-bound offer at all — no discount, no deadline, just "shop now," which won't move we-need-a-reason shoppers like me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a shade-finder or quiz CTA instead of (or alongside) the static grid — something like Drunk Elephant's Kamo tool — so this feels personalized instead of a wall of boxes.
- 2. Layer in a loyalty hook — even "Earn Sally Rewards points on every box" would give tier-math shoppers like me a reason to engage.
- 3. Add a real discount or bundle deal (e.g., "Buy 2, get free gloss treatment") — right now there's no offer at all, just a product showcase.
- 4. Trim the grid to 3-4 shades max with slightly more visual variety (texture close-ups, not just box front) to avoid the repetitive feel.
