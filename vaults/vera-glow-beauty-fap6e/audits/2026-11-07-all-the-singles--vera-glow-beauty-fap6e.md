# All the singles!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is basically the same Singles Day gift-with-purchase email Drunk Elephant sent me on 11/5 ("Singles Day steals"). It has a new subject line, a puffer tote added, and a new Atelier block at the bottom. I do like that it's a tiered GWP and not another Subscribe & Save ladder. Spend $100 and I get four samples. Spend $150 and I get a tote. I did the math right away. A $100 cart is easy for me, and $150 is where I'd consider padding the cart.
- The "four-piece sample gift" is the weak part. Two of the four are single-use packettes and the other two are 5mL minis. That's a lot of hero space for samples. The tote gets the biggest image in the email, and it looks good, but it's a bag. It doesn't tell me anything about skincare. The fine print at the bottom says the window runs through 11/12, and the hero never says so. I have to scroll to the bottom to find the deadline, which is annoying.
- Would I click? Probably, but only because I already know I want C-Firma. I wouldn't forward this to a friend. I'd mark it as read and keep the tier math in my head.

## What's working

- The $100 and $150 tiers are stated plainly and sit right under the headline. That's easy for me to do points math on.
- The hero product shot shows the actual gift: two packettes, a C-Firma mini and a Protini mini. I can see what I'm getting.
- The lilac-and-silver puffer tote is cute and photographs well. It reads as a gift I'd actually use, and it fits my age group.
- The layout is clean, with a single column and big type. It's easy to read on a phone without zooming.
- The Atelier block gives me a holiday-set teaser, so I'm not left with only the GWP.

## What's weak

- The deadline of 11/12 at 2:59 a.m. ET is only in the fine print. The hero has no urgency cue, and a real countdown would have helped.
- The "Celebrate 11.11" copy repeats the 11/5 send. Same offer, same angle, just two days later.
- The tote's "perfect in every way" copy is filler. It doesn't say what size the bag is or whether it's a real gift.
- There's no loyalty or member angle anywhere. Sephora's Savings Event shows me my Rouge number up top, but there's none of that here.
- Both "SHOP NOW" buttons go to a generic destination. Neither CTA names a hero product.
- The Atelier section's "SHOP NOW" is a tiny text link, which is a poor tap target. It's also the weakest part of the email.

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero, for example "Ends 11/11". Right now it's buried at the bottom.
- 2. Show the tiers as two steps, "$100 → samples, $150 → tote", with a progress-style graphic.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific. "Shop C-Firma" or "Build my $100 cart" would beat two generic "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 4. Make the Atelier "SHOP NOW" a real button.
- 5. Skip the third send of the same GWP. Either escalate it or send something new.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with third-party tracking pixels and web fonts. The source is truncated, so the body, CTAs, footer and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked. Confirmed problems are the recipient email address in a tracking-pixel URL, `http://` image sources, and an invalid `@import` placement.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links not probed:** 12 links were skipped because they are on tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chain and final HTTP status are unverified.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the address in plaintext in a query string. URLs get logged by proxies and CDNs and can leak through referrers.
- **Second identifier in a pixel URL:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayresen` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`) plus a campaign tag.
- **Two open-tracking pixels:** `o.gif` (mi.drunkelephant.com) and the everestengagement.com image. Two vendors fire per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** both tracking images above have no `alt` attribute. If they are 1x1 pixels, they should have `alt=""`.
- **Insecure image sources:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png` (`cc02d07d-…`, `9b452915-…`, `29f975e1-…`). Clients may block or rewrite them, which gives mixed-content warnings or broken icons.
- **`@import` after a rule:** in the `<style>` block, `body {…}` comes before the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` statements. Per CSS spec, `@import` after any rule is ignored. The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` still load the fonts, so the `@import` lines are redundant dead code.
- **Third-party font dependency:** the Brown Bold, Light and Thin fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most clients (Gmail, Outlook) ignore web fonts, so fallbacks are what actually render. I couldn't see the font stacks because of the truncation.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern on clients that honor it.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`/`xml:lang`. The `<title>` is the generic "Drunk Elephant".
- **HTML comments in CSS:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->` sits inside the `@media` block. Browsers and clients usually tolerate it, but it can break parsing in stricter clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The email and user-ID values in the tracking URLs are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
These findings are unconfirmed. The QA tool says the relay may be stripping the headers.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header:** the QA check flagged it as missing.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** one-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely an artifact of the AgentMail relay.
- **Body-level checks not possible:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source.

The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverifiable:** all click URLs go through redirect domains that were not probed, so I can't confirm UTM preservation or landing-page alignment.
- **Campaign tag:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayresen` is a vendor tag, not a UTM parameter.
- **Subject vs. campaign:** the subject "All the singles!" and the campaign slug "singlesdayresen" are consistent.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Move PII out of URLs:** replace the plaintext `e=<email>` parameter in the everestengagement.com pixel with a hashed or opaque ID.
2. **Switch images to HTTPS:** change the three social icons on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com` from `http://` to `https://`.
3. **Add alt text:** set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels, with explicit `width="1" height="1"`. Make sure the social icons keep their descriptive alt text ("Facebook", etc.).
4. **Fix the CSS:** delete the redundant `@import` lines or move them above `body {}`. Remove the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside the `@media` block.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
6. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and a more descriptive `<title>`.
7. **Verify headers at the source:** check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on a direct-to-mailbox capture, not the relay copy.
8. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** to cover the unsubscribe link, postal address and CTA/UTM checks. Probe the redirect chains with a redirect-following check, not an HTTP HEAD.
