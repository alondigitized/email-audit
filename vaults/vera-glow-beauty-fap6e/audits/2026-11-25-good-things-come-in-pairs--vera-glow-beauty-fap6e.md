# Good things come in pairs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Glossier again, and I'm doing the math before I even scroll. The hero stacks three offers: 30% off sets, 25% off everything, and 30% off $100+ with a free Crème de You. That's a lot of numbers for one block, and the "25% off everything" is the same sale I've now seen on 11/22, 11/23, 11/24 and earlier today in "Thoughtful gifts under $25 ✨". It's the same promo in a new outfit, and I'm numb to it.
- The sets angle is the new part, and the gifting logic is decent. But the email undersells it. It's four product tiles with no prices, no "was/now" and no reviews. I can't tell what a Fragrance Trio costs after 30% off. Sephora's Cyber Week email led with the deals and made the savings obvious. Here I'd have to click through and calculate it myself.
- I'd probably open it, since I'm a Glossier subscriber and the sale is real. I'd skim it, maybe tap "Shop sets", and forward it to nobody. It reads as inventory clearance, not a moment.

## What's working

- The hero headline "Two is better than one" is clear, and the pairing theme gives the send a hook instead of just another percent-off banner.
- The free Crème de You at $100+ gives me a threshold to hit. I like a gift-with-purchase target when I'm doing basket math.
- The product photography is clean. The fragrance duo and the coffee-and-earbuds flat lay look like actual gift-set content.
- The "Shop sets" button is bold and obvious, and each tile has its own "Shop now" button, so the tap targets are big on a phone.
- The Black Friday sale banner at the bottom reinforces the season, so there's no stale promo language.

## What's weak

- Three stacked offers in the hero is too much. "25% off everything" and "30% off sets" together make me stop and work out which one applies to what.
- There are no prices, savings amounts or reviews on any tile. Compare Ulta's tier ladder (50%, 40%, 30%) from its Black Friday send, which laid the whole sale out in one view.
- The top banner is cut off at the right edge ("spend $100"), and the "30% OFF" badge on the hero image is also clipped. Both read as sloppy.
- Three of the four tiles are fragrance. This follows 11/24's "Try something new with 25% off fragrances!", so the creative feels repeated, and the boy brow tile is the only non-fragrance product.
- There's nothing for me as a member: no points, no Glossier-side perk and no early access. Sephora's Cyber Week email at least nods to Beauty Insider tiers.
- The tiles' subcopy ("Choose your scents", "Grooming pomade") is generic and doesn't help me pick.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the hero to one offer, "30% off sets", and move the 25% sitewide and $100+ gift into a thin secondary line.
- 2. Add the actual set prices with the discount applied, for example "Fragrance Duo $__ → $__", and a star rating on each tile.
- 3. Fix the clipped top banner and the cropped "30% OFF" badge so the offer text is fully readable.
- 4. Vary the tiles. Add a skincare or makeup set instead of three fragrance sets.
- 5. Add a deadline. "Ends Cyber Monday" would make the urgency credible.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Crème de You at $100+. Ends Cyber Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. The QA run found no hard failures, but 6 images lack alt text and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and most link markup were not available for inspection.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links go through Klaviyo click tracking (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM_0`). The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 13 (footer, utility, social). It reported no broken or redirecting links among the probed set. The 13 skipped links are unverified.
- Invalid markup in `<body>`: `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` have no meaning on a `<span>`. It looks like a tracking-injection artifact and is harmless but non-conformant.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM`) has no alt attribute, and QA flags it. It should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images, flagged by QA):**
  - `heroimage_1_8.jpg`
  - `group_3986_15.jpg`
  - `group_3986_13.jpg`
  - `group_3987_16.jpg`
  - `group_3986_14.jpg`
  - the tracking pixel `01M89G4A4NP4RK5TVSX4E2R3HM`

  The five `stripocdn.email` content images need descriptive alt text. If any of them are product or CTA images, the links around them will be unlabeled for screen readers and for image-blocked clients.
- **Doctype/markup mismatch:** The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is declared, but the head uses unclosed HTML-style tags such as `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1" name="viewport">`. Most clients tolerate this.
- **Conditional comments:** `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opens in `<head>`, and its closing `<!--<![endif]-->` sits in `<body>` after the span. Because the comment straddles head and body, some parsers may handle it inconsistently. I couldn't confirm any rendering failure from the source.
- **Mobile CSS:** The mobile rules sit in a standard `@media only screen and (max-width: 600px)` block, and I found nothing broken in the visible CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. Because the source was truncated, the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The sender is `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment couldn't be verified.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** Neither could be checked because they fall in the truncated section. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but it reported no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

I can't confirm whether UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present or whether they survive the redirect. The QA data reports no landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.
