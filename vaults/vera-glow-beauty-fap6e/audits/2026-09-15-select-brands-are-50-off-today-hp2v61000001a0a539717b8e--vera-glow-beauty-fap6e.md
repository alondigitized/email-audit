# Select brands are 50% off today 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, credit where it's due — for the first time in what feels like forever, I don't see "you have 0 points" plastered under the header the second I open a Sephora email. That merge-field glitch has been on literally every Sephora send I've logged this week (both K18 blasts, and now this one previously flagged it too), so seeing it actually resolved is a genuine relief.
- That said, the offer itself is doing a lot of hand-waving. "50% off Select Beauty" with "Today Only" urgency sounds great until I remember Sephora runs some flavor of "select brands" discount practically every week — this is basically the same beat as their earlier "select brands are 50% off" send, just recycled. No brand names in the hero, no indication of which of my Rouge-tier faves are actually included, just a lone WW-branded bottle floating there that I don't even recognize. Compare that to Glossier's "Brow down" email, which just showed me Boy Brow with real customer faces and let the product speak — no guessing games.
- Below the fold it gets more interesting with the INNBEAUTY Project launch and the "Don't Miss Out" grid (Tower 28, Rare Beauty, Drunk Elephant refill, Kiehl's), which is genuinely well-curated for me. I'd scroll past the vague top banner, but I'd linger on the product grid. Would I click? Maybe on Rare Beauty or the Drunk Elephant refill. Would I forward this? No — nothing here is exciting enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- The "you have 0 points" merge bug that's dogged every Sephora send I've logged this week is finally gone — clean header this time.
- The product grid ("Don't Miss Out On These Faves") is legit curated for a Rouge shopper — Rare Beauty, Tower 28, Drunk Elephant, Kiehl's, all with clear "Add to Cart" buttons right on the card.
- "Clean at Sephora" badges are visible on multiple products, which matters to me when I'm comparing ingredient callouts across brands.
- "New + Only at Sephora" and "Limited Edition" tags are doing real curation-signal work — that's the brand-exclusivity beat I actually shop for.

## What's weak

- The hero offer — "50% off Select Beauty" — never tells me which brands. I have to click through blind, which after a week of "select brands" promos from this exact sender feels like a shell game.
- The featured hero product (that WW bottle) has zero context — no name I can read, no price, no "why this." It's the weakest visual anchor in the whole email.
- "Today Only" urgency is undercut by the fact that I've seen near-identical 50%-off-select-brands language from Sephora before — it doesn't feel like a real one-day event, it feels like the standing playbook.
- No Rouge-tier point math anywhere (multiplier events, bonus points on this purchase) despite me being exactly the shopper who tracks that obsessively.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the brands in the hero. "50% off Fenty, Tatcha, Drunk Elephant" converts way better than "Select Beauty" — Ulta's barcode-hero approach at least ties the offer to a visual; Sephora's is emptier than that.
- 2. Put the product grid brands (Rare Beauty, Tower 28, Drunk Elephant) into the hero banner itself instead of burying them three scrolls down.
- 3. Add Rouge-tier point context — "earn 2x points today" would make this feel built for me instead of generic.
- 4. Swap the anonymous WW hero bottle for one of the actually-recognizable "New + Only at Sephora" launches already in the email.
