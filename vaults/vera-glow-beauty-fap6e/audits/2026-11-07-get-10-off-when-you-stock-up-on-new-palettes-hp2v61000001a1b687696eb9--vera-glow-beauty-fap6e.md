# Get 10% off when you stock up on new palettes 💖
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Savings Event palette push again, and the subject undersells me. I'm Rouge, so my number is 30% off, not 10%. The top banner shows both tiers, and I do the math before I look at a single palette. Rouge is 30% (10/30–11/9) and Insider is 10% (11/3–11/9). I'd open this, but the subject makes it sound like the lowest tier.
- The hero is strong: "Hot New Palettes" in a gold script on black, with a big SHOP NOW button. The Huda, Patrick Ta and Danessa Myricks creator panels are gorgeous. Below that it's a long scroll of product tiles, then store info, a gift guide and a second barcode. That's a lot of email for one offer. I'd click the hero, but I wouldn't forward it.
- This is also the second near-identical Savings Event send today, after the 11/7 "stock up" emails and the 11/5 "most-searched beauty" wall. Ulta would have led with a coupon ladder. Sephora's tier math is better, but it's the same message again.

## What's working

- The 30% Rouge / 10% Insider banner lets me do my points math at a glance, with the "Use code ALLONSALE" line right there.
- The "Hot New Palettes" hero is clear: one gold headline and one SHOP NOW button, and my eye lands there first.
- The Huda, Patrick Ta and Danessa Myricks creator panels give it a real launch feel, not just a discount wall.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags on the product tiles (Dior, Charlotte Tilbury, Natasha Denona, ELF) tell me what's actually a launch.
- Prices are shown on each tile ($55, $72, $64), so I can estimate my discount without clicking.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with 10%, which is the lowest tier, so it undersells Rouge and Ulta Diamond me. Rouge members were told 30%.
- The email is very long. The palettes, gift guide, Kohl's store locator and the "Meet the Latest" brand block all compete for attention.
- There's a free-delivery banner, a free gift with purchase, and two barcodes at the bottom. That's too many offers, and none stands out.
- The Kohl's store map and hours are irrelevant when I'm shopping for a palette on my phone.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense block that I can't read without zooming.
- There's no personalization. Nothing speaks to my skin tone or my past palette purchases.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the subject by tier. Rouge should see 30%, Insider 10%, and the email shouldn't open with the lowest number.
- 2. Cut the Kohl's map, the second barcode and the brand tile so the palettes stay the story.
- 3. Show the tier price on each tile, like "$55 → $38.50 for Rouge." That would be the points math I'm already doing.
- 4. Keep one secondary offer, either the gift with purchase or free delivery, not both.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge: 30% off new palettes ends 11/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Huda, Patrick Ta, Dior palettes, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last weekend: your tier discount applies to every launch. Code ALLONSALE.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New and only-at-Sephora palettes, priced for your tier.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a conventional XHTML table layout (700px fixed width) with two tracking pixels and hidden preheader text. The automated QA flags one hard failure, a missing plain-text part (0 chars). The compliance and authentication warnings may be relay artifacts. The truncated HTML shows a few markup defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.** QA probed 12 material links and reported no failures. 46 links were skipped, mostly footer, utility and social links, so their status is unverified.
- **Two tracking pixels are present and working as designed:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a hidden open-tracking image (`display:none; mso-hide:all`).
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261107_Holiday_Makeup_Palettes_Insider` is a 1x1 pixel carrying a user ID and a campaign ID.
- The `_Inside` value in the QA output is a truncation of the `_Insider` value in the source. It is not a tracking defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Invalid CSS placement:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. The conditional comment is not valid there. Some parsers may drop the rules that follow it, including `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` may force horizontal scroll in clients that ignore media queries. The truncated source doesn't show a mobile override for this table, so confirm against the full HTML.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is harmless in email.
- **Alt text, one real finding and one false positive:**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` open pixel has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is minimal, but add `alt=""`.
  - The `o.gif` image was flagged by QA, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That is correct for a decorative image, so this is a false positive.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` carries no campaign context.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!-- US shell snippet -->` exposes internal template naming.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and an empty `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. This is incomplete structured data, probably an unpopulated template variable. Most clients ignore it, but Gmail may not render promo annotations.
