# NEW! Sheer For It Bronzer Tint 🤎
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, $6 for a bronzer tint is a genuinely fun impulse buy, and the freckled model with the dripping tint applicator is a decent scroll-stopper — but the "SHOP ON TIKTOK" CTA immediately kills my momentum because I'm reading this in Gmail, not TikTok Shop, and I'm not about to open a whole other app mid-scroll. This is the same beat e.l.f. hit on 8/28 with Halo Glow ("skincare or makeup? e.l.f. yes.") — genuinely good hooky copy, cheap price point, product that actually looks new — but they keep routing me off-platform instead of just letting me check out where I already am.
- Compare this to ILIA's "Last chance — Lip Sketch 4 Thru Line" send I logged, which tied urgency to an actual in-cart GWP mechanic — that's a real reason to click through to checkout. This one's just "go watch a TikTok, then maybe buy something." I'd probably swipe past without opening TikTok, but the $6 price and the "cheeky duo" bundle at the bottom (tint + blending brush) might get me to at least screenshot it for later.
- Not forwarding this one — it's cute but not shareable-worthy the way the vitamin C balance-scale send from 8/27 was. Marking as read, maybe circling back if I remember the TikTok hashtag.

## What's working

- The dripping tint applicator shot is genuinely appetizing product photography — makes the texture and shade payoff obvious without me reading a word.
- "You dropped hints... so we're dropping NEW tints!" is a fun, on-brand hook that nods to TikTok/FYP culture without being try-hard.
- $6 price called out twice (hero + "the biggest flex: only $6!") — that's impulse-buy math I don't have to do myself.
- The "Top Picks for You" rail at the bottom with Power Grip Primer, Setting Spray, and a $2 lip liner is smart cross-sell at prices that don't require thought.
- Model demographic (young, freckled, natural skin texture) reads current and Gen-Z/Millennial-adjacent, consistent with e.l.f.'s TikTok-native brand voice.

## What's weak

- Every single CTA — three of them — says "SHOP ON TIKTOK," not "shop now" or "add to cart." That's real friction; I have to leave my inbox and open a different app to buy anything.
- No loyalty/Beauty Squad points messaging anywhere, even though e.l.f. absolutely has a rewards program — as a Rouge/Diamond points-math person, that's a missed beat compared to how hard Sephora and Ulta lean into tier math in nearly every send.
- Shade range is vague — "4 bronzy shades for lips & cheeks" with no swatch strip or shade-match guidance for skin tone, which matters a lot for a bronzer/tint product.
- This is basically the same "new $X-something product + TikTok trend + emoji benefit list" template e.l.f. ran on 8/29 (lip combo) and 8/28 (Halo Glow) — the cadence is starting to feel repetitive three sends in four days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap "SHOP ON TIKTOK" for an actual "Shop Now" / "Add to Bag" CTA that goes to elfcosmetics.com — TikTok Shop should be a secondary link, not the only path to purchase, or I just won't convert.
- 2. Add a shade swatch strip for the 4 bronzy shades directly under the applicator shot — I need to see how this reads on different skin tones before I trust a $6 tint to not go orange.
- 3. Work in a Beauty Squad points callout ("earn X points on this") — e.l.f. is leaving easy personalization on the table for tier-math shoppers like me.
- 4. Vary the send template — three near-identical "NEW drop + FYP callout + emoji list" structures in one week is starting to blur together; a different layout or format (video thumbnail, UGC carousel) would help this specific launch stand out.
