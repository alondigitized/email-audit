- The BOGO mechanic is dead simple to parse — "buy two, get one free select hair color, mix & match" is clearer than anything Ulta's sent me this month with their "20% off one qualifying item" gymnastics.

- Nothing here for me personally — no skin tone/hair type personalization, no "your last purchase was X" signal, no tier status shown even though I scrolled all the way to the rewards banner.
- "Earn 2x points on all purchases" is teased as "Starting tomorrow" with a full-width banner and zero explanation of what a point is worth or what tier unlocks what — Sephora's Beauty Insider math is always visible to me; this isn't.
- The DIY box-color premise itself is a mismatch for someone who cross-shops Sephora launches and gets salon color — this reads like it's aimed at a much younger, DIY-first shopper.
- Long scroll before any actual sale content — six nav categories, a delivery banner, then a full COLORfest hero before I even see the offer.
- The bottom third is a wall of legal/loyalty fine print stacked three banners deep (rewards, "shop twice earn $5", Klarna) that dilutes the one offer that actually matters.
