# Just dropped: point of view.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "point of view" as a brand name is new to me and that's actually the hook — I don't think I've seen this launch teased anywhere else, so credit where due, Sephora's actually doing a real new-brand-drop this time instead of recycling the same "50% off Select Beauty" banner I've now logged five times since 8/28. The "founder's pov" storytelling with prep it / snatch it / wear it as a routine builder, plus the actual testimonial quotes under each product ("my skin looks visibly smoother, firmer, and way more snatched") — that's Glow Recipe-tier launch copy, not filler.
- But then it just... keeps going. Skincare sets, a new Kilian scent, Olive Young K-beauty, a Beauty Insider tier bar — by the time I hit the tier chart I've forgotten what "point of view" even was three screens up. This is the exact scroll fatigue I called out in e.l.f.'s "sheer for this bronzy glow" send — a real launch buried under a full digest.
- I'd open this one, I'd scroll through the launch section because the product story is genuinely good, and I might click "shop it" on prep it — but I wouldn't forward it. It's a launch email pretending to be a newsletter, and that dilutes the thing that actually earned my attention.

## What's working

- The "founder's pov" narrative structure with prep it / snatch it / wear it actually tells me how to use the products, not just what they are.
- Real customer-voice testimonials under each step ("every time I use it, my skin looks visibly smoother, firmer") reads like actual social proof, not marketing copy.
- Clean black-and-white hero for the "introducing point of view" launch — feels premium and different from Sephora's usual color-block promo tiles.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" flag under "own your POV" is exactly the exclusivity signal that gets me to click before Ulta even has it.

## What's weak

- The top banner is still "50% off Select Beauty, Today Only" — the same mechanic I've now seen in every Sephora send since 8/28, stapled onto an otherwise fresh launch.
- After the point of view section ends, it turns into a full digest: skincare value sets, Kilian Paris fragrance, Olive Young K-beauty — three unrelated promos before I even reach my Beauty Insider tier bar.
- No tier-specific offer or points math anywhere — as a Rouge member I'm scanning for "double points" or a Rouge-exclusive perk and there's nothing, just the generic INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE bar at the very bottom with zero personalization.
- Nowhere does it say what skin type or tone "point of view" is formulated for — for a routine-builder line that's the first thing I want to know before I add four products to cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "wear it" — the skincare sets, Kilian scent, and Olive Young block should be separate sends. This is one strong launch story diluted into a Tuesday digest.
- 2. Move the Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar up near the top with an actual member perk tied to this launch (early access, double points on POV) instead of a generic footer graphic.
- 3. Add skin type/tone callouts to the "prep it" and "snatch it" product tiles — routine builders live or die on "is this for me."
- 4. Drop or de-emphasize the "50% off Select Beauty" banner up top — it's competing with, not supporting, the actual news.
