- Let me be upfront about where I'm standing when this one lands: the brand history on Sephora has me watching the cadence like a hawk after May 26's three-in-one-afternoon "ENDS TODAY" pile-up. So when "STARTS TODAY" hits my inbox on June 11 — and I can already see from the record that this subject line has a prior audit at this exact date — my first instinct isn't excitement, it's pattern recognition. *Is this a second send?*
- The email itself is actually well-constructed. The three-tier discount stack (50%/20%/25%) reads in about two seconds, the 500-point bonus is planted right in the hero so I can do my Rouge math immediately, and the Laneige Glow Mode callout mid-scroll is the kind of newness hook that turns a sale email into a destination. ## What's working

- Three-tier offer structure (50%/20%/25%) is scannable in one pass — Rouge math is instant: $75 spend + 500 bonus points is ~$6.25 back minimum, and that mental calculation happens before I've scrolled at all
- 500 bonus points callout earns hero-level real estate and it deserves it — this is the detail that separates a Sephora sale email from a Charlotte Tilbury one, which barely acknowledges its loyalty layer
- Laneige Glow Mode launch slot mid-email makes this feel like a curated drop, not just a clearance push — that kind of newness anchor is why I open Sephora sale sends even when I've already seen the discount tiers
- "Beauty Insiders Get More" section reinforces the tier hierarchy clearly, and the INSIDER callout block at the bottom reminds me what status I'm defending — Sephora does loyalty identity better than almost anyone in my inbox right now
- "SAVE ON MUST-HAVES LIKE THESE" product grid reads as curated rather than a generic inventory dump, which Drunk Elephant's recent emails keep defaulting to

## What's weak

- "STARTS TODAY" with no end date is urgency theater — I need the deadline, not the start signal; without it I'll tab-switch and come back tomorrow, which is how I never come back
- No personalization anywhere visible — e.l.f.'s last four sends have had "for you" product logic surfacing SKUs in my category; Sephora is still broadcasting to the list like I'm a stranger
- The 50%/20%/25% tiers are visually distinct but not *categorically* explained — I can't tell from the hero which brands or categories land in each bucket, and that ambiguity slows my click decision
- Dense legal footer is visible even at thumbnail scale and looks like it belongs to a different brand — it doesn't kill the email but it's a jarring contrast to the polished hero

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard end date in the hero** — "Ends June 14" or "3 days only" right under the 50% headline; this is the single change that would most directly lift click urgency
- 2. **Differentiate tier buckets with brand callouts** — even showing 3 brand logos per tier in the hero would answer "50% off — but of what?" before the scroll
- 3. **Personalize the product grid** — pull my top repurchase SKUs into "must-haves"; Sally Beauty has been doing basic browse-history surfacing and it works harder than a generic grid
- 4. **If this is a second same-day send**, swap the hero to a "Rouge Early Access" exclusive or a single new-launch spotlight to earn the second touch rather than read as a resend error
- 5. **Tighten the footer** — break up the legal wall visually; even alternating font weight or adding breathing room would help
