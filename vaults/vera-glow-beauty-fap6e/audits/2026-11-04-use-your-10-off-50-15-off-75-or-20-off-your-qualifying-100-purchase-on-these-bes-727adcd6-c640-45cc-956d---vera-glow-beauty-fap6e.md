- The $10 / $15 / $20 stack dominates the top. The math is instant (20% at each tier), and "includes prestige brands & fragrance" is the line that matters to me.
- The "Offer ends November 8 at 11:59pm CT" deadline is specific and credible.
- Prices are on every tile, and "+ free gift" badges add real value. Fenty at $48 and the Morphe palette at $16 are easy to evaluate.

- The "join Ulta Beauty Rewards" banner pitches me as a stranger. I'm Diamond, and Sephora never makes me feel like a newcomer.

- Near the bottom there's a big empty gap with stray "ulta.com." and "click here" text floating in it. It looks broken.

- "Shop now" under every tile is the same weak CTA. There's no single main button.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud send (`exct.net`, `emltrk.com`) with an XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype, `lang="en-US"` and dark-mode meta tags. The automated QA run (57% pass rate, 0 errors, 3 header warnings) found many images with no `alt`, a cluster of non-HTTPS pixel and dynamic-content images, and no header data. The HTML was truncated, so the body links, footer and any merge tokens could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links unverified.** 42 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and expired links were not checked.
- **Plain-HTTP images.** These are flagged as likely to be blocked:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1` (10 cookie-sync pixels). Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will fetch or block them, so these syncs are unreliable. Ten third-party ad-tech calls in one email also add load.
  - `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQi…`. This looks like an SFMC dynamic-content image. If it is blocked, that content slot likely renders blank. It should be served over `https://`.
- **Tracking pixels.** Open tracking is `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=163808`. The `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png` and `3eb758650b65389e.png` images carry `mi_u=202740837`, which looks like a recipient ID.
- **Duplicate asset.** `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` and `852db034-…png` each appear twice. This is likely intentional, such as a repeated spacer or icon, but it is worth confirming.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing `alt`.** The QA run flagged these `image.e.ulta.com` images:
  - `08964789-…png`
  - `414d3e76-…jpg`
  - `4f7c3146-…gif`
  - `a103b506-…jpg`
  - `d99333f4-…jpg`
  - `925e52e3-…jpg`
  - `32415bbe-…jpg`
  - `852db034-…png`

  The check cannot tell a missing attribute from an empty `alt=""`. If these are product or offer images, they have no text equivalent when images are blocked, and screen readers will read the filenames. Decorative images should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Pixels missing `alt`.** The tracking and sync pixels (`emltrk.com`, `mi.ulta.com`, `dotomi`, `LiveContent.aspx`) should have `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` where possible.
- **Web font dependency.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…">` is ignored by Gmail and most Outlook clients. The truncated source did not show whether the inline `font-family` stacks fall back to a web-safe font. Verify that they do.
- **Positive checks.** `<meta name="viewport">`, `translate="no"`, VML/Office namespaces, the MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings` block, and the `x-apple-data-detectors` and `#MessageViewBody` overrides are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and the subject (`🤑 Use your $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off…`) show no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens. The truncated body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers captured.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click support for senders at Ulta's volume. Check the raw message in the original inbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown, and the QA tool attributes this to the relay. Sender `U-Mail@e.ulta.com` is on a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 42 click links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked. The only campaign identifier visible is in the QA label: `pr_fy26wk40_wednesday_broad_coupons_dedicated_12pm_pz_LAS`. It is not confirmed as a UTM value on the links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve `LiveContent.aspx` and all pixels over HTTPS. Drop or consolidate the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` http pixels, or move them to server-side or HTTPS endpoints.

3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass, since the relay hides them.
4. Re-run the link check with the click domain allow-listed or resolved. Then verify the final landing URLs and their UTM parameters.
