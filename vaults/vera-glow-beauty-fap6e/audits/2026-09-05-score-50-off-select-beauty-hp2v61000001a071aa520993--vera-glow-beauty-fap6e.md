# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, my first reaction opening this is "here we go again" — 50% off select beauty is the exact same mechanic Sephora ran yesterday in the "Stacking Weekend" email I scored a 9 and 8 on, just stripped of the bonus-points layer that made that one feel special. The hero is still clean and impossible to miss — big red "GET 50% OFF" front and center, "SHOP NOW" button right under it, no scrolling needed — so on pure legibility this beats Ulta's cluttered "book stack" hero they've been recycling since July.
- The product grid underneath is actually solid for me — Charlotte Tilbury, Kiehl's, Anastasia Beverly Hills, Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez are all brands I actually buy, and the prices are struck through so I can do my points-per-dollar math without opening a calculator app. That's more than I can say for Glossier's vague "fall favorites" sitewide 30% I logged this week.
- But I clocked something annoying immediately: it says "you have 0 points" right at the top, and my tier bar at the bottom shows Rouge — so either this is a generic send that isn't actually reading my account, or my points genuinely reset, and either way it undercuts the "you're a known VIP" feeling. I'd click through for the Charlotte Tilbury foundation and the Kiehl's clay mask, but I wouldn't forward this — it's Tuesday's sale in a Friday coat.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero is unmissable — bold, high-contrast, no squinting required.
- "Today Only" + free shipping for Beauty Insiders gives me two reasons to act now instead of bookmarking it.
- Product picks are genuinely in my wheelhouse — CT, Kiehl's, Rare Beauty, ABH — not filler.
- The Tarte mascara has an actual "Best of Beauty" Allure badge, which is real social proof, not just marketing copy.
- Rouge is highlighted in my loyalty tier bar at the bottom, which is a nice touch of recognition.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" directly under the header is either a personalization bug or a slap in the face to a Rouge member who's definitely not sitting at zero — this is the kind of thing that makes me trust the brand less, not more.
- This is basically a reskin of yesterday's "Stacking Weekend" email minus the bonus-points hook, so the cadence is starting to feel like Sephora emailing me the same sale on a loop, same complaint I had about Drunk Elephant's "secret VIP" sale reappearing.
- "SHOP NOW" as the CTA is generic — Sephora knows exactly which categories I shop, so it could say "Shop Skincare Deals" or similar.
- No actual urgency countdown or end date beyond "Today Only" in the hero — feels like it could be reused verbatim tomorrow.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points display — if it's showing 0 for a Rouge member, that's either a broken feed or a scary signal my account merged wrong; either way, surface my real points balance or drop the line entirely rather than showing a number that reads as an insult.
- 2. Make the CTA specific to my shopping history — "Shop Your Skincare Picks" instead of "Shop Now" would immediately separate this from the templated feel.
- 3. Differentiate this from yesterday's Stacking Weekend send — if it's the same 50%-off mechanic, at least reference the stacking bonus points again so loyal openers don't feel like they're rereading an email.
- 4. Add a hard countdown clock or explicit end time instead of just "Today Only" — Ulta's "ENDS TODAY" framing, while repetitive elsewhere, at least creates real urgency.
