# Something special awaits. We’ve got your makeup needs covered.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I need to flag something up front — this is the exact same email I already opened today. Same subject, "Something special awaits. We've got your makeup needs covered," same "0 points" banner glaring at me under the INSIDER strip even though I'm Rouge, same everything. I already wrote about the points-balance issue on this exact send, so getting it a second time in the same day just confirms my complaint wasn't a fluke — Sephora's system genuinely isn't pulling my real balance into these blasts.
- Setting the dupe issue aside and judging the creative itself: the 20% off Laneige hero is fine, National Lip Day is a real occasion (though buried in body copy, not the subject — e.l.f. did this better with "National Lipstick Day gift on us" right in the subject line), and the "BEAUTY FOR YOU" purple hero into the Lip Sleeping Mask/Glow Balm duo is clean, on-brand Sephora styling. But a 20%-off-one-brand offer feels small next to Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item, any brand" send that scored a 9 with me last week — Ulta's felt like a genuine surprise-and-delight, this feels like a Laneige co-op ad wearing a Sephora wrapper.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Lip Sleeping Mask restock since I'm due. Would I forward it? No — nothing here is friend-worthy, it's just catalog scroll. And getting the identical email twice in one day is the kind of thing that makes me numb to Sephora's "urgency" language going forward.

## What's working

- The Laneige duo (Lip Sleeping Mask + Glow Balm, Acai Mango Smoothie) gets real hero treatment with prices right under the products — I don't have to hunt.
- Rouge is highlighted in red on the tier bar at the very bottom, so at least somewhere in this email I'm visually acknowledged as top-tier, even if the points count above says otherwise.
- "Trending Picks for You" mixing Prada, Kérastase, Farmacy, Armani Beauty is a decent curated spread — feels like Sephora's multi-brand strength, which Ulta's more mass-heavy assortment doesn't always match.
- Layout is clean, no broken images, no overlapping text — visually it renders exactly the way I'd expect from Sephora.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" at the top — same broken personalization I called out on this exact email earlier today. Two years of Rouge status and the header still can't find my balance.
- I'm getting this literally identical email twice in one day. That's worse than e.l.f.'s daily cadence because at least e.l.f. varies the hook each time — this is a straight duplicate.
- Subject line is generic filler ("makeup needs covered") when the body actually has a real hook (National Lip Day, 20% off Laneige) that never made it into the subject.
- "SHOP NOW" as the only CTA copy everywhere is lazy — Ulta and ILIA both give me more specific verbs tied to the actual product.
- The 20% off is Laneige-only, not sitewide — feels narrow compared to Ulta's broader qualifying-item discount that scored higher with me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points display before anything else — a Rouge member seeing "0 points" twice in one day is a trust problem, not a copy problem.
- 2. Stop sending the identical creative twice in the same day — vary the subject or hold the second send for a genuinely new angle.
- 3. Pull the real hook into the subject line instead of generic boilerplate.
- 4. Swap "Shop Now" for product-specific CTA copy ("Shop the Lip Sleeping Mask," "Shop Lip Day Deals").
- 5. Consider a sitewide or multi-brand lip offer instead of single-brand Laneige to compete with Ulta's broader qualifying-item discount.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge perk: 20% off Laneige lip favorites`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lip Sleeping Mask, Glow Balm & more — 20% off today`
