# A cool kiss of color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a pretty lip edit, and the best thing in it is buried at the bottom. The hero is a gorgeous red gloss wand and "Color's in Season," with four winter lip products stacked underneath. Then, past the footer-adjacent fold, there's a 30% off Swipe + Go block that the whole email should have led with. I almost missed it.
- After ILIA's 10/25 "On everyone's lips" and 10/30 "Just a few recs," this is a third lip-forward send in about a week. It's a good one, since the product story is real: Blue Note, Wanderlust, Studio, Marsala, each with a shade name and a wear claim. But the actual deal is a 30% off cheeks + lips stick that has nothing to do with the lip story above it. The only offer up top is a free mini eye stylus with $75+, a tiny banner that reads like fine print.
- Would I click? Maybe the Overglaze gloss in Studio. Would I forward it? No. If it were 30% off the lip lineup, I'd be texting friends. Sephora is doing 30% off all Sephora Collection, and I can do the points math on that in two seconds. Here I have to hunt for the offer and then guess whether it covers anything I just read about.

## What's working

- The shade-named product blocks (Blue Note, Wanderlust, Studio, Marsala) give me the specificity I want from a prestige-clean brand.
- The model casting is genuinely varied in skin tone, and each lip color is shown on a face. That does the shade-matching work Ulta's grid emails never do.
- The hero copy is seasonal and sensory: "bold berries, cherry reds, a cool kiss of color." It sounds like fall lips, not a template.
- The wear claims (12 hours, 24 hours) are concrete and useful.
- The footer trust cues are tidy: free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, and ILIA VIP.

## What's weak

- The 30% off is buried below the lip section and has nothing to do with the hero story. The offer and the narrative don't match.
- The top banner, "FREE MINI EYE STYLUS W/ $75+*", is small and asterisked, and a stylus is an eye product in a lip email.
- Two stacked offers, the stylus gift and 30% off, don't connect and make me do math.
- No prices anywhere on the lip products, so I can't tell if I'm in "worth it" territory.
- The ILIA VIP callout is a tiny footer icon. I'm a points person, and there's no member pricing or earn rate to get excited about.
- Two "SHOP" buttons in the lip section both link generically. Nothing says which shade I'd land on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 30% off Swipe + Go up to the hero, or put an offer line in the hero banner. If the discount covers lip products, say so loudly; if not, don't build a lip story above it.
- 2. Show prices on each product block so I can judge value instantly.
- 3. Make each product block its own link to that shade, not one catch-all button.
- 4. Add a VIP line with the points-earn rate or member early access. That's the loyalty hook Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond emails lean on.
- 5. Drop the eye stylus gift from the top banner or tie it to the lip theme.
