# Oooh, it’s 50% off select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so subject says "50% off select beauty" but this is actually a Hair Styling Awards email — twelve-category product roundup dressed up as a sale send, with the 50% off banner buried at the very bottom next to "You have 0 points" (again). That's a bait-and-switch subject and I clocked it immediately, because I've now seen "50% off select beauty" from Sephora at least twice this week alone (9/8 twice, now 9/9) and every single time the actual hero content is something completely different.
- The Hair Styling Awards concept itself is genuinely well done — I like the "Editor's Pick," "Clean," and star-rating badges, the category structure (Sleekness, Definition, Volume) is clear, and Virtue, Amika, Oribe, Living Proof are all brands I actually reach for. But it's a hair-focused editorial digest hijacked by a beauty-sale subject line, and by the time I scroll to the actual 50% off banner I've already forgotten that's supposedly why I opened this.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the quiz ("What's Your Hairstyling Goal?") out of curiosity, but not because of anything the subject promised. I wouldn't forward this — it's fine, not exciting — and the disconnect between subject and content bugs me enough that I'd probably scroll past next time I see "50% off" from Sephora since it's clearly become their all-purpose clickbait subject.

## What's working

- The Hair Styling Awards format with star ratings, "Editor's Pick" and "Clean at Sephora" badges gives real curation signal — feels like an actual buying guide, not just a shelf dump.
- Recognizable brands throughout (Virtue, Amika, Oribe, Kérastase, Living Proof, Ouai) — this is squarely my Rouge-tier shopping list.
- "Spend $75+ use code 500SEPH for 500 bonus points" ribbon shows up twice, which at least keeps points math visible for someone like me who's always doing that calculation.
- The hairstyling goal quiz CTA is a nice interactive touch that Ulta rarely bothers with.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "50% off select beauty" but that offer doesn't appear until the literal bottom of a hair-awards email — that's misleading and it's the second+ time this week I've caught Sephora doing this exact subject-line switcheroo.
- "You have 0 points" ribbon at the very top, again — this is now showing up in basically every Sephora send I've logged this week (9/8 twice, and now this one) and it still stings as Rouge member with real spend.
- The email is enormously long — twelve product categories before you even reach the actual discount — way more scroll than a phone read deserves.
- Model imagery skews mature/editorial rather than reflecting me as a 29-year-old shopper; feels more "hair magazine" than "Sephora Beauty Insider."
- Two competing bonus-points offers (15% off shampoo/conditioner category vs. 500 bonus points vs. the 50% off banner) stacked at the bottom is confusing — I can't tell which deal actually applies to what I'd buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: one true Hair Styling Awards editorial email, one true "50% off select beauty" sale email with the discount as the actual hero. Stop conflating subject lines with unrelated content — that's the single biggest fix here.
- 2. Move the 50% off banner (or whatever the real subject-matching offer is) above the fold if it's going in the subject line at all.
- 3. Drop or reposition the "You have 0 points" ribbon — it's demoralizing and now a running joke across five-plus sends this week.
- 4. Trim the awards list to top 4-5 picks with a "see all 12 winners" link instead of forcing a full scroll.
- 5. Clarify which discount code applies where — one clean offer block at the bottom, not three stacked mechanics.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Hair Styling Awards: 12 winners, shop the edit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge picks: award-winning hair, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sleekness, definition, volume — the products that won`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `12 categories, one winner each — see who took the crown`
