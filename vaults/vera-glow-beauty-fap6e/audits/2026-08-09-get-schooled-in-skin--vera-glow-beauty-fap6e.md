# Get schooled in skin 🤓 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "get schooled in skin" — e.l.f. is now on day whatever of this streak, and this one's basically a "supplies list" gimmick wrapped around a four-product skincare/lip lineup: Glass Slick Serum Stick ($12), Hypochlorous Mist ($12), Invisi-Stick SPF 50 ($13), Thirst Burst Lip Treatment ($8). It's clean, it's readable, prices are all sitting right under the name where I want them — but it's also the same "here's four things, tap through them" format e.l.f. ran on 8/6 with the BOGO skincare send and 8/8 with the three-step routine. Nothing here screams "buy now" the way the 8/7 Bubble collab did.
- The one thing that'd actually get me to open my wallet is buried mid-scroll: "buy 2 skin e.l.f.ssentials, get 1 on us" with code REFRESH — that's a real BOGO, same mechanic as Tuesday's send, just reskinned with new hero art. And then right under it there's ANOTHER promo, the Bubble collab teaser again ("WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER") — so now I'm getting the same collab pitched to me for the third time this week inside a different email. That's the kind of redundancy Sally Beauty gets dinged for when they run six sends in two weeks with no new hook.
- Would I click? Maybe the BOGO banner, since REFRESH is an actual code and not just teaser copy. Forward to a friend? No — this is a routine restock email, not a "you need to see this" moment. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The "back to school" supplies-list framing is a cute seasonal hook and at least differentiates this from the streak of skincare-routine emails e.l.f. keeps sending.
- Every product card has price, a real 2-3 line ingredient/benefit call-out (Hypochlorous Acid, Peptide Complex, non-comedogenic SPF), and its own SHOP NOW — no hunting for what does what.
- The BOGO banner ("buy 2 skin e.l.f.ssentials, get 1 on us," code REFRESH) is a genuine offer with a real mechanic, same standard e.l.f. hit on 8/6 — at least it's consistent, not bait-and-switch.
- Model shot up top is diverse in a low-key way and skin-focused (dewy, close-up), which fits an actual skincare send rather than a color-cosmetics one.

## What's weak

- Two competing promos stacked back to back — the REFRESH BOGO banner immediately followed by the Bubble collab banner — dilutes both. I don't know which one I'm supposed to act on.
- The Bubble collab teaser is the third time this week I've seen "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" from e.l.f.; by now it reads like filler, not news, the same way Ulta's 20%-off-one-item code has shown up three sends running.
- Zero personalization or loyalty math anywhere — no Beauty Squad points, no "you're 40 points from a reward," nothing. Sephora's K18 send at least shows a points bar (even if it's broken); e.l.f. doesn't even try.
- "Top Picks for You" is a lie — it's just three of the same SKUs already shown above (Holy Hydration! Toner, Thirst Burst Lip Treatment, Holy Hydration! Face Cream), not anything algorithmic or new.
- No urgency anywhere except "for a limited time" on the BOGO, with no actual date/deadline — compare to Ulta's "ENDS TODAY" framing, which at least forces a decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and lead with it. Put the REFRESH BOGO at the very top instead of burying it two-thirds down — right now the supplies-list product grid eats all my scroll before I even see the deal.
- 2. Cut the Bubble collab banner from this send entirely, or make it the whole email — running it as a secondary banner for the third time this week just trains me to skip it.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the BOGO ("ends Sunday 8/11") instead of vague "for a limited time" — steal Ulta's urgency playbook here.
- 4. Fix "Top Picks for You" — either wire in actual purchase-history logic or rename it "Bestsellers" so it's not overpromising personalization it doesn't deliver.
- 5. Add a loyalty line (Beauty Squad points balance or "X points on this order") — I'm doing points math on every beauty email I open, and e.l.f. is one of the only brands in my inbox giving me nothing to calculate.
