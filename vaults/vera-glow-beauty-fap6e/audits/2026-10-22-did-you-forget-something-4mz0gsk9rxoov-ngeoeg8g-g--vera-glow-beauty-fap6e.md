# Did you forget something?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the fourth cart-abandon email in a week for a cart I never filled. "Ready to check out?" with a "Complete your order" button is the same fake-cart beat as 10/17 "Don't leave these styles behind!", 10/18 "Pick up where you left off!" and 10/18 "This is perfect for you!". I haven't put anything from Skechers in a cart. Gold-foil hero, "complete your order" twice, and then a bestseller grid that has nothing to do with whatever "my cart" was supposed to hold.
- The real news is buried halfway down. It's a members-only 20% off plus a $5 reward on $75+, valid through 10/26. I do points math for a living, and that's a decent stack. But the email leads with guilt instead of the offer. Ulta would have put "20% off" in the hero and called it a day.
- Would I click? Probably not. I might open the sale page if I needed sneakers, but I don't, and the fake-cart move has trained me to ignore these. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The 20% off with the $5 reward on $75+ is a clear, specific offer. The navy banner has good contrast and I can read it on my phone.
- The single hero shoe, the pale mauve GO WALK Workout Walker, is clean and pretty. It's a women's product, which fits me.
- The bestseller grid is all women's styles, and the pink Zinger Evo is cute.
- The orange "Complete your order" button pops against the white and is easy to tap.
- The category links (Women, Men, Kids, and so on) are big tap targets, so I can skip straight to Women.

## What's weak

- "Ready to check out?" is a fake cart-abandon claim. I have no cart, so it reads as untrustworthy, and it's the same beat as 10/17 and 10/18.
- The 20% off sits under the hero, the shoe and the six-shoe grid. It's a long scroll before I hit the only reason to click.
- The two "Complete your order" buttons and "Shop all best sellers" all compete. The "Shop the sale" link is small, and it's the one I actually care about.
- The offer is "Members take 20% off," and the fine print says Skechers Plus only, online only, with lots of exclusions. The banner never says what a member is or how to join. That's not very honest or clear.
- The email is long and cluttered. After the offer there's texting, app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna and social, with no loyalty status or tier math anywhere for a points person like me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and drop the fake cart. Put "Plus members: 20% off through 10/26" in the hero, and keep abandon framing only for people who actually have a cart.
- 2. Make one CTA ("Shop 20% off") and remove the repeated "Complete your order."
- 3. Show my Plus status or points balance, and show how the $5 reward works. That's the math I care about.
- 4. Tie the grid to something real, like "Women's best sellers under $80," with prices and the sale price visible.
- 5. Cut the footer clutter (texting, app, curbside, socials) down to one row.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off + a $5 reward, thru Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 20% on women's walkers online. Plus earn $5 back on $75+.`
