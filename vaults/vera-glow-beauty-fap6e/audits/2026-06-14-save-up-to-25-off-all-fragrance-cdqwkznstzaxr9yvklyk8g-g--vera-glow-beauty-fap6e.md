# Save up to 25% off all fragrance
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- Day four. I have to lead with that because I cannot pretend I opened this with the same energy I had on June 11 when the big orange ANNUAL SALE hero actually made my thumb stop. That send felt like a Glossier event. This one — the fourth consecutive "save up to 25% off" email — feels like a clearance rack employee tapping me on the shoulder while I'm already in the store.
- That said, Glossier did the one smart thing they could have done on day four: they narrowed. Fragrance only. That is genuinely a different email from June 12's "Sale is LIVE" and June 13's everything-goes grid, and as someone who has been mentally eyeballing Glossier You since the sale dropped, a dedicated fragrance send actually re-engages me. The ocean imagery is doing real sensory work — it reads "skin-warm, salty air, one spritz before you leave the house" — and the subscription lock-in at the bottom is a hook I haven't seen in the previous three sends. That is new information, and I notice new information.
- But there is no deadline visible anywhere, the subject line is the June 13 email copy-pasted with "all" inserted, and "Buy now" on every product tile is a Glossier voice crime. I'd click — fragrance is my weak spot — but I would not forward this because I already texted my group chat about the Friends sale on Tuesday.

## What's working

- Narrowing to fragrance is the right strategic call on day four — it finally feels like a sub-campaign, not a rebadged repeat.
- The ocean hero imagery is genuinely evocative for a scent email; it earns the scroll without being on-the-nose about what it's selling.
- Pricing and star ratings are both visible in the product grid without scrolling — the math is right there.
- The "Glossier You Size" section addresses a real buying decision I face (solid vs. spray, size vs. travel), which feels like actual merchandising rather than filler.
- The subscription call-out at the bottom is the first genuinely new hook across four sends — locks in a discount permanently, which is a better long-term offer than "sale ends whenever."

## What's weak

- The subject line is a word-for-word echo of June 13's send with "all" appended — if Glossier expects me to treat this as a fresh email, they needed fresh copy.
- No visible sale deadline anywhere in the email — after four days of the same offer, I genuinely don't know if this ends tonight or next Tuesday, and that ambiguity kills urgency.
- "Buy now" on every tile is lazy CTA copy for a brand that writes better than this — ILIA's "Get ready w/ HAIM" emails score 9/10 partly because even the microcopy sounds like the brand. Glossier sounds like Shopify default here.
- The product grid runs small on what renders as a mobile frame — product names require zooming, which is a real tap-target problem.
- No segmentation signal — if I already purchased Glossier You on June 11 during the sale, I should not be receiving this email unchanged.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a deadline in the subject and the hero.** "Ends Sunday" — or whatever the real date is — is the single highest-leverage change. Drunk Elephant sent five emails in eight days (scored 8/10) and I called out deadline visibility as the fix there too; Glossier is making the same mistake.
- 2. **Lead with a single hero product at the sale price.** Glossier You Eau de Parfum, 1.7 oz, $47 with buy — then let the grid be browse inventory. Charlotte Tilbury buried me in tiles in their last send and scored 7/10; don't be Charlotte Tilbury.
- 3. **Replace "Buy now" with brand-voice CTAs.** "Grab your size," "Shop Glossier You," "Find your formula" — pick one that sounds like the brand.
- 4. **Add a Sephora points callout.** Glossier is available through Sephora; I am Rouge. One line — "earn Rouge points when you shop via Sephora" — would actually change where I click.
- 5. **Suppress or swap creative for buyers.** If I converted on June 11, show me the subscription pitch full-screen, not the sale I already acted on.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Glossier You is 25% off — your size is still in stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fragrance sale: ends Sunday. Glossier You starts at $47.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Solid, spray, or travel — lock in the discount before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Subscribe and keep 20% off forever. The Friends Sale won't come back until next year.`
