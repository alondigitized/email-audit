# Spotlight on: Scrubee
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Spotlight on: Scrubee" — Lush is doing a full single-product deep-dive on their honey body butter today, ingredient breakdown and all, and this is a nice change of pace after "Less is more" (7/26) went full brand-manifesto with zero product focus and "What's the scent of the summer?" (7/28) was a whole fruit grid with no hero. This one actually picks a lane: one product, one review quote, one "what's inside" ingredient list. I like that they're not asking me to pre-order Halloween candles or Advent calendars in the same week for once.
- That said, there's genuinely nothing here for me tier-wise — no Lush Lovers points math, no member pricing, nothing that acknowledges I've bought from them before even though the review quote literally says "one I always repurchase." The banner up top dangles "$5 off with the Lush app" and then the entire rest of the email ignores that thread completely. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item just landed in your lap" (9/10) — a specific number, a specific mechanism, a code. Lush just gives me vibes and a honeycomb.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity about the reviews section, but there's no discount attached so there's zero urgency. Wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's a nice product story, not a deal worth sharing.

## What's working

- The honey/coconut hero shot at top is genuinely gorgeous — moodier and more premium-feeling than Lush's usual bright collage grids.
- Star rating + real customer quote ("gorgeous honey scent... leaves my skin creamy & soft") gives me actual social proof, something Ulta and Drunk Elephant rarely bother with.
- "What's Inside Counts" ingredient breakdown (cocoa butter, coconut shell, honey) speaks directly to the "ingredients" data point I actually care about before buying body care.
- "Ethical Bee Keeping in Brazil" section is a nice trust/sourcing story — feels more substantive than Lush's usual "naked packaging, 30 years" refrain from 7/26.

## What's weak

- "$5 off with the Lush app" banner at the very top is the only offer in the whole email and it's never explained, tied to a code, or referenced again — total bait-and-switch energy compared to Sephora's clean "$45 min spend" threshold or e.l.f.'s "6 hours only" urgency.
- No Lush Lovers point value anywhere — I'm doing points math in my head for every Sephora/Ulta send and Lush just leaves me guessing.
- CTA copy is soft — "Join The Hive" and "Read More" are cute but not "Shop Scrubee" or "Add to Bag," so I'm never actually pushed toward checkout.
- The "More Sweet Bestsellers" cross-sell (spray + dusting powder) dilutes what was supposed to be a single-product spotlight — pick a lane and stay in it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Turn that top banner into an actual offer tied to Scrubee — "$5 off Scrubee with the app" — instead of a disconnected generic app plug that never resolves.
- 2. Swap "Join The Hive" for a direct "Shop Scrubee" button right under the hero — that's the CTA that actually converts curiosity into a cart add.
- 3. Add a Lush Lovers points callout near the price ("Earn X points on this purchase") — I am tracking this across every brand and Lush is the only one leaving it blank.
- 4. Cut the bestsellers cross-sell block or move it below the "Ethical Bee Keeping" story — let Scrubee own the top half uninterrupted.
