- This is the fourth Singles Day send from Drunk Elephant in a week, and the fourth time I've seen the same $100 four-piece sample gift. It was the same on 11/5 "Singles Day steals", 11/7 "All the singles!" and 11/9 "Singles Day, right this way". The only new thing is the countdown timer, which shows 15 hours left. That is a real deadline, so "Last day" is honest this time. It's also the reason I'd actually look at the email.
- The offer is clear. Spend $100 and get C-Firma, Protini, Amino Rain and D-Bronzi minis. Spend $150 and get the puffer tote. But I've read this email three times already, and the tote has been there since 11/7. If I haven't bought by now, a timer isn't going to change that. I'd click only if my cart was already near $100, and I'd forward it to nobody.

## What's working

- The countdown banner at the top sets the deadline before anything else, and "Last day" in the subject matches it.
- The hero says "spend $100 and it's yours" in plain words. I can do that math in two seconds.
- The two-tier ladder is easy to follow: $100 gets the samples, $150 gets the tote. It gives me a reason to add one more item.
- The product shot of the minis on the pedestal shows exactly what I'd get.
- The 20% off a Kamo plus moisturizer bundle line in the banner adds a second, concrete offer.

## What's weak

- It's the same creative as 11/5, 11/7 and 11/9. I've seen it so many times that I skim it.
- The offer details are buried in grey fine print at the bottom. That includes the 11/12 2:59 a.m. ET cutoff and the exclusions. The bundle terms (one Kamo plus one moisturizer, no stacking with codes) sit down there too.
- The Kamo bundle is a strip of caps text in the banner. There's no image and no button, so it's easy to miss, and it's the only price cut in the email.
- The Atelier block at the bottom is vague. "Limited-edition holiday collection" has no price and no product named.
- There's no loyalty or member angle. As an Ulta Diamond and Sephora Rouge, I'd expect points or tier perks somewhere.

- 2. Put the real deadline in the preheader, like "Ends 2:59 a.m. ET". Don't make me hunt for it in the footer.
- 3. Show one price and one product in the Atelier block, or drop it.
- 4. Stop re-sending the same creative. A last-day email could be a simple "you left it in your cart" or a "your gift is still unclaimed" send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is typical table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with no unrendered merge tokens visible. The automated checks flag three problems: non-HTTPS image sources, missing alt attributes on two tracking pixels, and the recipient's email address in a pixel URL. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the 14 tracked links were never resolved.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified.** 14 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so landing destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in a pixel URL.** The image `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the address in a plaintext query string. It will appear in proxy, CDN and server logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images: `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` pixel. Both look like tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""`, and they should be `width="1" height="1"` if they aren't already.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png` (`cc02d07d-…`, `9b452915-…`, `29f975e1-…`). Clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule inside `<style>`. CSS requires `@import` to come first, so they are ignored. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` and the `@font-face` rules carry the font loading instead.
- **Third-party font dependency.** The `Brown Bold`, `Light` and `Thin` fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. Most clients strip `<link>` and `@font-face`, and the font host adds an external dependency and a tracking surface.
- **Malformed CSS.** The HTML comments `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media` block. They are harmless in most clients but can break parsing in some.
- **Mobile sizing.** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` and `max-width:12px` under the media queries. That looks very restrictive and may clip text on mobile. I can't confirm this without a render, and the HTML is truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`) are visible in the supplied source. The only personalization seen is the resolved address in the pixel URL (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` should be checked against the raw headers.
- **Body footer not verifiable.** The HTML was truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters unverified.** The 14 click links were not probed, and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the pixel URLs.
- **Campaign naming.** The visible tags use `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdayends_`. The campaign name refers to Singles' Day ends, while the subject says "Last day: FREE $60 gift". I can't confirm a mismatch without seeing the landing pages.

4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of `<style>`, or drop them in favor of the `@font-face` rules. Add web-safe font fallbacks.
5. Remove the HTML comments from inside the `@media` block.
