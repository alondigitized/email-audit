# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Stacking Weekend is now on send FOUR from Sephora that I've logged (9am at 9/10, follow-up at 8/10, a THIRD at 8/10, and now this) — and this time it's buried under an entire EADEM brand takeover before I even get back to the 50%-off mechanic I actually care about. The top banner is the exact same 50%/500-points combo I've scored twice already this morning, so nothing new there, but then it's eight — I counted — product modules for one skincare line before "50% off Select Beauty" resurfaces near the bottom.
- EADEM's a solid clean-girl skincare brand and I don't hate seeing it, but this reads like Sephora forgot which email they were sending. Compare this to ILIA's "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" send I logged at 7/10 — that one had ONE clear campaign idea and stuck to it. This is two emails Frankensteined together: a Stacking Weekend promo email and a single-brand spotlight, with none of my Rouge tier math anywhere in either half.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I scroll all the way to the "Spot It. Shop It." real vs. dupe module near the bottom, that's actually kind of fun. But I'm not forwarding this — it's too scattered, and I've already screenshotted the Stacking Weekend banner three times this week for my own tracking.

## What's working

- The 50% off + 500 bonus points banner up top is instantly legible — I know the deal in two seconds, same as the earlier sends today.
- EADEM's product photography is clean and the "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag under each product is a real exclusivity flex I respect.
- The "Spot It. Shop It." dupe-comparison module near the bottom is genuinely engaging content, not just another banner.
- "Beauty Insider" branding and the points bar are present, so at least the loyalty tie-in exists somewhere in the email.

## What's weak

- Eight straight EADEM product blocks (Spray and Soothe, Boost Radiance, Capsule Faves, Patch It Up, Glow On, Get Sun-Soaked, Mini Essentials) before the actual sale resurfaces — that's a LOT of scroll for someone who opened for the discount, not a brand story.
- Zero points balance or Rouge-tier math anywhere — I'm Rouge, I want to know what 500 points actually gets me or where I'm sitting toward my next reward, and it's just not here.
- No skin-type or shade personalization on any of these EADEM picks — I don't know if the Dark Spot Serum or the sunscreen tint matches me at all.
- This is the fourth near-identical Stacking Weekend top banner I've seen — the escalating-urgency device is wearing thin, and burying it under a mid-scroll skincare wall makes the fatigue worse, not better.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the EADEM spotlight to 2-3 hero products max and move the 50% off Select Beauty banner back up near the top instead of making me scroll past a whole separate campaign to find it — right now this feels like two emails mashed into one.
- 2. Add my actual points balance and progress toward Rouge rewards next to the "500 bonus points" callout — a real number does more work than a generic incentive.
- 3. Tag skin type/concern on the EADEM products (e.g., "best for oily skin," "brightening") so I'm not guessing whether Patch It Up or Glow On actually fits me.
- 4. Lead with "Spot It. Shop It." or a similar interactive module higher up — it's the most engaging thing in the whole email and it's stuck near the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `EADEM is here — plus still 50% off select beauty`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New: EADEM skincare, only at Sephora. Sale ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See your points balance + this weekend's top picks.`
