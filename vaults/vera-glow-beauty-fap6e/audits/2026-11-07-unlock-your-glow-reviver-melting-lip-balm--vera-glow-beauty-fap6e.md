# Unlock your Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm! 💋
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the first e.l.f. send in a week where I can do my points math in two seconds. A free full-size Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm with a $5+ purchase is a real mechanic. It's also a better gift-with-purchase than the "save BIG" email from 11/6, which never put a number on anything. "SURPRISE REWARD DROP!" on that purple block is loud, and I like that. The offer is the first thing my eye lands on after the lip balm hero.
- The "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" button is a little annoying. I'm sure it's tied to Beauty Squad, but it reads like a chore, not a reward. The free lip balm is also competing with a second promo block right underneath it, "the more e.l.f., the merrier," which is the exact "save big on custom gifts" message from 11/6. That's the second send in two days with the same bundle block. The bottom half is a wall of grey category bars with no imagery, and it looks half-loaded.
- Would I click? Yes, because a $5 minimum for a free full-size lip balm is basically free. Would I forward it? No. I'd just add the balm to cart next time I'm replenishing a Power Grip Primer. It's a good email for a low-effort buyer. It's not a launch, and I don't get any tier math beyond "Beauty Squad."

## What's working

- The free Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm for a $5+ purchase is a concrete offer, and the "no code needed" line removes the usual promo-code scavenger hunt.
- "Reward expires 11/8" gives me a real deadline, and it's tomorrow. That's credible urgency.
- The hero is a saturated pink flat-lay of lip balms in every shade. It sells color range at a glance, and the "Beauty Squad Exclusive" sticker tells me it's a member perk.
- The purple reward block has strong contrast. The headline, offer and button read fine on my phone without zooming.
- "Top Picks for You" has real prices ($6 and $11), so I can price-check against Ulta and Sephora while I'm still in the email.

## What's weak

- "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" makes me feel like I have to do homework. A "Shop the free lip balm" button would be better.
- The "the more e.l.f., the merrier" block repeats 11/6's "Bundle up and save big on custom gifts" with no number. It dilutes the one good offer.
- The category block (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) is six grey bars with no imagery. It looks like a broken module, and it uses up a lot of scroll.
- "Top Picks for You" shows a lip tint, a primer and a setting spray. None of them is the Glow Reviver, and none has a shade, rating or review. Where's the social proof?
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. The $5+ minimum, the shade rules and "can't be combined with" caveats are all buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rename the CTA to "Add my free lip balm" and link it straight to the Glow Reviver page. Drop the sign-in framing, or put the sign-in note under the button.
- 2. Cut the "merrier" bundle block, or give it a real number. One offer per email would lift this more than any design tweak.
- 3. Replace the grey category bars with product tiles, and make the picks Glow Reviver shades with star ratings.
- 4. Move the key terms ($5+ minimum, 11/8 expiry, one per customer) up under the button in a readable size.
- 5. Add a Beauty Squad points-balance line, like Sephora's tier banner. Then I could do my math.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound (table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, hidden preheader). Four of 12 probed links failed, but the evidence points to the QA probe failing on relative redirect targets rather than a confirmed broken email. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/...'`.** The redirect on each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` link resolved to a host-less relative path:
  - `/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=Plums+Up+-+sheer+plum` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_xp=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&source_caller=ui&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=…` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Probable cause:** the probe's HTTP client could not resolve the `Location` header. Relative `Location` values are valid under RFC 7231, and browsers resolve them. This is likely a probe limitation, not a user-facing break, but it is unverified. Re-probe with relative-redirect resolution before treating these as defects.
- **Redirect through an opaque click tracker:** all CTAs go through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<base64>~~`. That is expected for the ESP, but the final destinations can't be verified from the HTML alone.
- 27 of 39 links were skipped by the probe, so coverage is partial. The "27% pass rate" is computed over a small probed set and shouldn't be read as an email-wide figure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (1 image):** the `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/5R7F-jdL5Q9APoBKKBNwYw~~/AAQRxRA~/LrCbpbRK…`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking pixel. If so, it needs `alt=""` so screen readers skip it, and it is low severity. If it is content, it needs real alt text.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** Minor, but some clients and accessibility tools use it.
- **Empty font-family name:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the block-2 wrapper. It falls back to Verdana, so it's harmless but sloppy markup.
- **9px heading on mobile:** `.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}` in the `@media (max-width:660px)` block. Text this small is hard to read. If it is legal or fine-print copy, consider 11px or larger.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and has Arial and sans-serif fallbacks. No issue.
- **Duplicated structure:** `nl-container` and `row-1` classes are reused inside nested `html_block` wrappers. This is common with builder output, and I found no rendering failure from it.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I could not check the rest of the body for further alt-text or structural issues.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML or the subject. The source was truncated, so the later body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, so it can't be treated as a sender defect. Check the raw message headers at the receiving mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` are aligned subdomains of `elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and none of the 4 failures is one. I can't confirm the postal address from the truncated source.
- **Plain-text part is 83% URLs** (11336 of 13641 chars). The long click-tracking URLs inflate this and could slightly raise spam-filter scoring.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Click-tracked URLs are opaque, so UTM presence can't be verified from the HTML. The one resolved destination that shows tracking is the app link: `utm_source=broadcast`, `af_xp=email`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`, `bxid=…`.
- The two resolved product destinations (`/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=…`, `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=…`) show no `utm_*` parameters in the probe output. The tracker may add them downstream, or attribution may rely on the ESP's click ID.
- The probed product CTAs go to the Sheer For It Blush Tint and Power Grip Primer, while the subject names the Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm. The lip-balm link was not among the failures, and I couldn't tell from the data whether it was probed.
- The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` destination for "Receipt scanning" sends users to a login wall, so logged-out recipients land on a sign-in page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the probe with relative-`Location` resolution against `https://www.elfcosmetics.com`. This will likely clear the 4 failures. If any still fail, treat that link as broken.
2. Inspect the raw message in a real inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass), rather than through the relay.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `/q/…` tracking pixel, or real alt text if it is a content image.
4. Fill in `<title>` and remove the empty `font-family:''`.
5. Review the 9px mobile `h1` override at `.row-6` for legibility.
6. Confirm that UTM parameters survive the redirect on the product links.
7. Consider shortening tracked URLs in the plain-text part to cut the URL-to-text ratio.
