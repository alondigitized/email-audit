# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the FIFTH "50% off Select Beauty" send I've logged in eight days now — 8/28 twice, 8/29 twice, 8/30, and now this one on 8/31 — and Sephora has officially blown past "recurring promo" into "are you even trying" territory. The hero real estate this time goes to "THE NEXT BICG THING" (Natasha Denona, Violette_FR) before the 50% off banner even shows up, which is actually a NEW beat compared to the last four sends where the discount was slammed at the top — so credit where due, they buried the lede on purpose to sell me new-launch energy first.
- The points math is right there at the very top ("You have 6 points," "Get $5 off when you redeem 250 points," "4X points on top brands") which is catnip for me as Rouge — I'm always doing this arithmetic — but the offer itself, when it finally shows up mid-scroll, is the same "50% off ONE/SIZE by Patrick Starrr, Living Proof, and Clinique, TODAY ONLY" mechanic I've now seen at 6-7/10 four times this week. Compare that to Drunk Elephant's "The world is your moisture" send, which I gave a 7/10 for actually organizing product by skin need — Sephora's just recycling the same pink banner with a rotating brand cast.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Tower 28 swipe serum foundation with the before/after — that's the one genuinely new thing here. Would I forward to a friend? No — this is background noise at this point, not a moment.

## What's working

- The points/Beauty Insider banner up top (6 points, $5 off at 250, 4X on top brands) speaks directly to my Rouge brain — nobody does loyalty-math bait like Sephora.
- "THE NEXT BIG THING" section leads with real exclusivity signal — "Only At Sephora" tags on Violette_FR and several other SKUs give me a genuine reason to open Sephora over Ulta.
- The Tower 28 "New Swipe Serum Foundation" with a before/after face image at the bottom is the one section that made me actually pause and read ingredient/finish claims.
- Clean product grid layout with NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA / CLEAN tags — easy to scan on mobile without zooming.

## What's weak

- The 50% off banner is stapled dead center of the email, disconnected from everything above and below it — same "comp slapped in" feeling I flagged on 8/30 and 8/29.
- Five sends in eight days with the identical "50% off Select Beauty, TODAY ONLY" mechanic is fatigue, not urgency — I stopped feeling the deadline pressure around send #3.
- The brand list this time (ONE/SIZE, Living Proof, Clinique) is buried in tiny fine print under the banner instead of being named in the headline, unlike 8/29's cleaner build that put Too Faced/MAC right in the banner.
- Subject line "Take a closer look" is vague filler — it doesn't tell me what's new versus what's the same recycled discount, so I can't triage it from the inbox.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere despite Sephora having my Beauty Insider profile — this reads like a mass blast, not a "known customer" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop running the same 50% off Select Beauty banner five times in eight days — rotate in a genuinely different offer or fold it into one clean weekly digest instead of daily repeats.
- 2. Put the actual brand names (ONE/SIZE, Living Proof, Clinique) in the subject line or hero copy instead of hiding them under the banner — that's what made 8/29's version work better.
- 3. Move the points/loyalty banner math to actually connect to the featured products (e.g., "4X points on ONE/SIZE today") instead of floating disconnected at the top.
- 4. Personalize with my skin tone/type for at least one section — I have that data on file as Rouge, use it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New from Natasha Denona + Violette_FR (plus 50% off, ends tonight)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your 6 points + today's 50% off picks, matched to your Rouge profile`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New launches first, deals second — here's what's actually new`
