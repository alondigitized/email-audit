# Meet the popular girls
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Meet the popular girls" — and then I open it and it's... the starter kit. Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom, You Travel Spray, the Pink Mini Bag — Glossier's greatest hits, bundled up and pitched to me like I've never heard of them. Except I have Boy Brow in my bag right now. This is email number fourteen-plus since July 7th, and after the "I ❤️ NY" campaign and the GWP mini bag drama, they've pivoted to onboarding copy — "New to us? Start with this set" — aimed at someone who is very much not me.
- The production is clean, as always — that clear beaded tray under the Boy Brow, the pink bubble-wrap Balm Dotcom flat lay, the close-up freckled selfie for the Travel Spray — Glossier's photography is still doing more work than half their competitors' entire campaigns. But there's no actual offer anywhere. "Shop + save" on every button with zero percentage, zero dollar amount, zero loyalty tie-in. Sephora's Daily Deals emails are annoying and repetitive but at least they show me my points balance; Glossier just assumes I'll click because the products are pretty.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's a re-skin of the site's PDP grid with no reason for me, a Rouge/Diamond member who already owns three of these four items, to act today. Marking as read, not forwarding — there's nothing here a friend hasn't already seen from Glossier eleven times this month.

## What's working

- The bag flat-lay hero is genuinely pretty — the beaded tray and bubble-wrap backdrop for Boy Brow and Balm Dotcom are on-brand and scroll-stopping.
- Each product gets its own mini-hero shot and dedicated CTA, so if I did want just one item, the path is short.
- "Even more sets" footer cross-sells The Face Kit and Fragrance Two Ways without cluttering the main flow — smart use of real estate.
- The freckled close-up for You Travel Spray nails the "soft, warm, familiar, ultra-personal" copy — matches the demographic they're going for.

## What's weak

- Zero offer. "Shop + save" appears five times and never once says how much I'd save — Ulta's "20% off, just landed in your lap" email from this week buries nothing, it leads with the number.
- This is explicitly a new-customer onboarding email ("New to us?") sent to someone who's been on their list long enough to get thirteen-plus emails since July 7th — I'm not new, I'm fatigued.
- Text is clipped on the Boy Brow description — "It visi..." cuts off mid-word against the image column, which reads sloppy for a brand this polished.
- No loyalty or rewards mention at all, which stings more coming right after ILIA managed a full shade chart and Ulta managed a points-forward hero in the same week.
- Cadence is the real issue — this is the fourth-ish "here's our classics" reset in three weeks with no new offer mechanic, just repackaged existing sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number on it. Even "20% off your first Icons Set" would beat five unlabeled "Shop + save" buttons — this is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Fix the audience mismatch — either genuinely gate this to new subscribers or drop the "New to us?" framing for existing customers and reposition as a restock/refill nudge instead.
- 3. Fix the clipped copy under Boy Brow before the next send — "It visi..." is a visible bug, not a style choice.
- 4. Add a loyalty signal for repeat buyers — a "you've already got Boy Brow, complete the set" callout would feel personalized instead of generic.
- 5. Slow the cadence or vary the mechanic — after thirteen-plus emails, repackaging the same four hero products without a new hook (GWP, bundle discount, refill reminder) reads as filler.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom, You Spray, Mini Bag — bundle and save`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Already a fan? Here's your refill shortcut.`
