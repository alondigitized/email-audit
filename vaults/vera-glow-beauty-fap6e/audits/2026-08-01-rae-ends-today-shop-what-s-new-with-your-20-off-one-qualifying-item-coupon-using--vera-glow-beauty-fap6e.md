# Rae, ENDS TODAY! Shop what’s new with your 20% off⁺ one qualifying item coupon using code SCORE, including prestige & fragrance. Terms & excl. apply 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "20% off is calling" — with a barcode over a book stack for that same vaca-brain aesthetic they've been running since July 26. I've now gotten this exact 20%-off-one-item mechanic from Ulta three sends in a row (7/26 twice, and now this), and the only thing that's changed is the flavor text went from "landed in your lap" to "is calling" to "ends tonight." I'm Rouge at Sephora and Diamond at Ulta, doing the points math on every send, and this one gives me zero reason to pick Ulta over Sephora today because the offer itself hasn't moved an inch.
- What actually gets my attention is the product grid — Burberry, Versace, Ole Henriksen, Bath & Body Works, Live Tinted, Charlotte Tilbury dupes-adjacent stuff, Theraface, GHD — that's a real cross-section of prestige and mass-prestige and it does scratch the itch since I use Ulta specifically for that branded mass-prestige mix. But it's buried under a "20% off ONE qualifying item" banner that doesn't tell me which of these 24 products actually qualify until I click through, which is friction I don't love compared to how Sephora ran the actual Beauty Insider Celebration this week with a clean "STARTS 8/1-8/31" badge and no guessing games.
- Would I click? Probably, because Theraface and the GHD styler catch my eye and I have a returns-worthy reason to browse. Would I forward it? No — this is the third near-identical version of this exact email and I'm not going to forward my friend the same coupon code I've already told her about.

## What's working

- The barcode-on-book-stack hero is still visually the strongest thing Ulta's shipped lately — genuinely better than the flat product-waterfall Drunk Elephant and ILIA have been running.
- Product grid spans real prestige (Burberry, Versace, Charlotte Tilbury dupes energy via Rare Beauty, Live Tinted) alongside device buys (Theraface, GHD) — that's the actual mass-prestige mix I shop Ulta for.
- "With coupon" price tags are shown directly under each product, so I don't have to do math at checkout — small win for someone as points/price-obsessed as me.
- Ulta Rewards module at the bottom reminds me to redeem points before checkout, which is at least a nod to loyalty even if it's boilerplate.

## What's weak

- This is the same 20%-off-one-item mechanic Ulta ran on 7/26, twice. Nothing escalated, nothing new except "ends tonight" urgency bolted on — Sally Beauty at least pivoted subject matter entirely between sends this week; Ulta is just recycling.
- "ENDS TODAY" urgency is buried in the subject line but the hero banner doesn't visually shout deadline — no countdown clock, no bold "TODAY ONLY" treatment the way Sephora's "STARTS 8/1-8/31" badge does it.
- 24 products with zero curation logic I can see — no "for you" or "new arrivals" framing, just a long scroll of everything, which reads more like inventory clearance than personalization.
- My name "Rae" only shows up in the subject line and again at "Hi, Rae" way down by the Rewards module — nothing in the hero or product selection feels tailored to my actual purchase history.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the identical 20%-off-one-item offer three sends running — even Ulta's own "Still time to save" 20%-off-everything from 6/26 felt fresher because at least it applied storewide. Rotate the mechanic or escalate the discount.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or bold "TODAY ONLY" stamp in the hero itself — "ends tonight" is doing no urgency work sitting quietly in the subject line while the banner just says "20% off is calling."
- 3. Curate the grid — group by category (fragrance / skincare / tools) or flag "bestsellers" so 24 flat product tiles don't read as a dump.
- 4. Surface my Diamond-tier points balance or a "you're X points from your next reward" line near the top, not buried at the bottom Rewards block — that's the personalization signal that actually gets tier-obsessed shoppers like me to click.

# Technical Audit: Ulta Beauty — "20% off⁺ SCORE" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders on a legacy XHTML 1.0 Strict / MSO-conditional template with mobile media queries, but ships with a broken name-merge field, no verifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers, and multiple insecure (HTTP) tracking pixels — automated QA recorded a 43% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 10 tracking/redirect pixels load over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (all sharing param `afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`). Non-HTTPS resources are frequently blocked by mail clients (mixed-content), which would silently drop this tracking/attribution data.
- One additional HTTP-only asset: `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABB7...` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content/tracking beacon) — same mixed-content risk.
- 60 additional tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (by design, not a confirmed failure) — destination validity is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 24 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` attributes, including primary content images (e.g. `08964789-...png`, `414d3e76-...jpg`, `d46f473b-...jpg`) and 1x1 tracking pixels (`mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png`, `dotomi` pixels). Screen readers will announce nothing or fall back to filenames/URLs for these.
- Document uses the deprecated XHTML 1.0 Strict DTD with heavy MSO conditional comments — standard/expected for legacy ESP (looks like Salesforce Marketing Cloud/ExactTarget) templates, not itself a defect.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` meta tags and mobile media queries (`@media max-width: 480px`) are present, indicating dark-mode and responsive handling was considered — no issues found here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed defect:** Greeting merge field renders empty — QA flagged `"Hi ,"` with no name inserted, indicating a failed/unmapped personalization token in the body.
- Note: the subject line personalization did resolve correctly ("Rae, ENDS TODAY!"), so the failure is isolated to the body greeting token/data-binding, not the personalization pipeline as a whole.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not present in the captured headers — may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not present — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.
- Recommend re-checking these three items against raw SMTP headers (not the relay-processed copy) before treating as confirmed failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs were visible in the truncated HTML source provided; link destinations were not resolvable (60 tracking links skipped by QA). Cannot assess campaign-tracking or landing-page alignment with the data available — flagging as a gap in this audit rather than a confirmed issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the body greeting merge token so it degrades gracefully (e.g., "Hi there," fallback) when the name field is null — currently emits literal `"Hi ,"`.
2. Migrate the 10 `dotomi.com` tracking pixels and the `cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx` beacon from `http://` to `https://` to prevent mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-enforcing mail clients.
3. Add `alt` text to content images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable for pure tracking pixels, but content images need descriptive text for accessibility).
4. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` against raw source headers (not the AgentMail-relayed copy) to confirm true compliance status rather than a capture artifact.
5. Obtain full (non-truncated) HTML and resolved tracking-link destinations to complete UTM/landing-page continuity checks.
