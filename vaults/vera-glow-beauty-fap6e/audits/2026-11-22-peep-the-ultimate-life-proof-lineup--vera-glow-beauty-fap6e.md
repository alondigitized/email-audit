# Peep *the* ultimate life-proof lineup 💋✨

## Executive summary

But it's a catalog of three products with nothing to make me act today. Compare the 11/19 gold Power Grip send, which had one hero and a launch moment. - The useful stuff sits halfway down. The Beauty Squad "Surprise Reward Drop" with $5 off bundles and sets repeats the 11/20 send, which already had the sign-in catch. - The $5 Main Stain Lip Marker and $6 Sheer For It Blush Tint show prices in the headline, so I can do the math in half a second.
- The row of lip markers in the pink "NEW!" block is a good product shot, and shade range is visible.
- Each product has its own black "SHOP NOW" button, large enough to tap on a phone.
- The "4 NEW SHADES!" callout on the blush tint tells me what's actually new.
- The Beauty Squad block shows the member benefit and an expiry date of 11/22.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. "Keep your look on lock" is vibes, not a deal.
- The $5 off bundles and sets reward is a rerun of 11/20's "THIS WEEKEND ONLY!" send. It's also gated behind sign-in and a $10+ cart.
- The "full-size gift on us" block is the only thing that feels like a deal, yet it's below three product sections. The "up to $9 value" language is vague.
- The category nav (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) renders as grey empty bars, so it looks like broken content.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and I can't read the reward and gift terms without zooming.

- 2. Make the grey nav bars readable buttons, or remove them.
- 3. Space out the sends. Seven in six days makes every email feel like a repeat.
- 4. Show the Beauty Squad points or reward math in plain numbers, like Sephora does for Rouge tier.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is built from table layouts with `role="presentation"`, a viewport meta tag and a preheader. All 3 probed link failures come from relative-path redirect targets that the QA probe could not resolve, so they are probably probe artifacts rather than confirmed broken links. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be evaluated because the relay stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **3 probe failures (30% pass rate).** Each is `unknown url type: '/...'`, which means the redirect from `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` resolved to a relative `Location` that the probe did not join to a base host:
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo?Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon`
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → `/pages/mobile-app?pid=Email&is_retargeting=true&shortlink=email&utm_campaign=112226_lifeproof_lineup&af_reengagement_window=30d&af_...`
- This is **not confirmed as a user-facing break**. A browser would resolve these against the redirect host. It does show that the tracker redirects to host-relative paths, and the probe's final destination was never verified.
- **Login wall:** the receipts link lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Unauthenticated recipients are sent to a login page instead of the receipt-scanning feature.
- All links are opaque Iterable-style wrappers (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...`). Final destinations are not visible in the source.
- 30 of the 42 links were not probed, because the probe ranks by click likelihood. Coverage is partial.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- An inline style declares `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` (an empty font name). It falls through to Verdana, so it is harmless but sloppy markup.
- Nested duplicate structures: `class="nl-container"` and `row-1` tables are repeated inside `html_block` wrappers. This is redundant nesting from a block-builder export and increases the risk of Outlook table quirks.
- **Missing alt:** the image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/9SkbPQWlC2bMOyOUbpqfvA~~/AAQRxRA~/qag7PN...` has no `alt`. The `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` to make the intent explicit.
- The header logo has `alt="e.l.f."`, and `lang="en"` and the viewport meta are present.
- The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is stripped by many clients, and the Arial/sans-serif fallback is declared.
- Only the first part of the source was available, so the rest of the body was not audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. e.l.f. is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender, so these headers are required for it. Check the raw message headers from a direct send.
- **Authentication-Results** is missing (SPF/DKIM/DMARC status unknown). This is expected via the relay, so no conclusion is possible.
- A physical address and in-body unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result is not listed among the failures.
- Plain-text part: 12,200 of 14,755 characters (83%) are URLs, because of the long tracking tokens. This is a spam-filter heuristic risk. Shorter tokens or a leaner text part would help.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The app-download link carries UTMs (`utm_campaign=112226_lifeproof_lineup`, plus `pid=Email`, `shortlink=email`, `is_retargeting=true`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`). It uses AppsFlyer-style attribution on a web page `/pages/mobile-app`.
- The "Shop Now" product link shows only the variant param `Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon`. No UTM is visible in the resolved target, though the tracker may append them server-side. This is unverified.
- The receipts link has only `return_to`, with no campaign attribution.
- Product-link parameters are inconsistent across the email.
- The campaign naming `112226_lifeproof_lineup` matches today's date (2026-11-22).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the three links with a redirect resolver that joins relative `Location` headers to the redirect host (`elfcosmetics.com`). Confirm that the final status is 200 and that the Mango & Watermelon variant is in stock.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on a direct (non-relay) copy, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
3. Confirm UTM presence on product and account links after the redirect. Add consistent `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` to all CTAs.
4. Point the receipt-scanning CTA at a public landing page, not a login redirect.
5. Fill in `<title>`, remove the empty `font-family:''`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking image.
6. Shorten the tracked URLs or the plain-text part to reduce the URL-to-text ratio.
