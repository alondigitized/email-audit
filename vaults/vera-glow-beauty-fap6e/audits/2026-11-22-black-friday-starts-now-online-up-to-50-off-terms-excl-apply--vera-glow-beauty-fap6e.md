# Black Friday^ starts NOW online! Up to 50% off. Terms & excl. apply 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was relief. After four weeks of the $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder (11/4, 11/8 and the rest), this one finally has a different shape. It's Black Friday, up to 50% off, plus 5X points. I'm Ulta Diamond, so that member block near the top is what I read first. The "up to 50%" hero is clear, and the five category circles under it (makeup, skincare, hair, fragrance, bath and body) let me jump to my lane.
- Then I hit the scroll. It's a very long stack of brand tiles, and the 5X points offer is competing with a dozen other numbers. The tiles say 30%, 40%, $100 off, $150 off, "now $15 ea" and 50%. I did the points math on a few of them and then got tired. Sephora's Cyber Week email put one big "30% OFF Benefit" on sequins and I got it in half a second. This one makes me work for it.
- Would I click? Yes, for the Shark Beauty $100 off and the free gift-with-purchase tiles. Would I forward it? Only to my friend who's holding out for a Dyson. Otherwise I'd save it for the 5X points window and move on.
- One caveat on this review: the screenshot is heavily downsampled, so I can read the big offer numbers and layout but not the small fine print.

## What's working

- The 5X points member block sits right under the shipping banner. As a Diamond member I notice it immediately, and it's real loyalty value.
- The hero is clear: "Black Friday, up to 50% off" in big white type on black, with a Shop now button.
- The category circles under the hero are a good shortcut. I don't have to scroll through haircare to reach skincare.
- Each tile has one brand, one number and one button. The numbers are concrete: "$100 off" Shark Beauty, "$150 off" the styling tool, "now $15 ea" shampoo and conditioner.
- The free gift tiles at the bottom (9-piece and 8-piece) are a good closer. Gift-with-purchase is how I decide a haul is worth it.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, and the offers aren't ranked. "Buy one get one free" at the top has the same weight as 25% off, so I can't tell the best deal.
- The "up to" language on the hero and several tiles hides the real discount. I want to know which brands hit 50%.
- Every tile has the same "Shop now" button. It's a lot of identical CTAs, and none says what it's for.
- The 5X points block competes with the hero for attention. Both are big offers, and I don't know which one the email wants me to act on.
- There's a blank gap near the bottom, above the footer, that looks like an image that didn't render.
- It's the same black-and-pink Ulta look as every other send, and it feels like an Ulta-Sephora carbon copy again. Nothing says "Ulta", and Sephora's Cyber Week email at least had a daily-drops calendar.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the single best deal in the hero. Say which brands get 50%, not just "up to 50%".
- 2. Merge the 5X points block and the hero into one headline offer, so there's one hierarchy instead of two.
- 3. Cut the tiles from about twelve to six. Rank them by discount, with the biggest first.
- 4. Replace the identical "Shop now" buttons with specific copy like "Shop Shark Beauty $100 off".
- 5. Add a "when it ends" line. Online starts NOW is good, but I want the end date and when the in-store deals begin, the way Sephora's Cyber Week calendar gives me the dates.
- 6. Fix the blank gap above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off + 5X points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 5X points start now + up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shark Beauty $100 off, BOGO makeup, and free gifts. Online now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Diamond members earn 5X on everything. See the top deals first.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict email with VML/MSO fallbacks, and all 52 links go through click-tracking redirects. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The real findings are missing `alt` attributes on content images and ten non-HTTPS third-party ad-sync pixels. The header and compliance warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unconfirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Ten `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` to `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1` pixels are embedded. These are cookie-sync calls to a third-party ad network (Dotomi), not first-party tracking.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=164886` and `.../3eb758650b65389e.png`, plus `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=164886`, are open/analytics beacons. The `emltrk.com` beacon URL carries a campaign tag, `pr_fy26wk42_saturday_bf_launch_8pm_Last`, and a numeric ID, `32516`.
- **Link rot:** None of the links that were probed returned errors. The report lists no broken links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** Six `image.e.ulta.com` assets have no `alt` attribute (the `4f7c3146…gif` is listed twice, and the `852db034…png` is listed twice):
  - `.../m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `.../m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `.../m/45/4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif`
  - `.../m/3/95387140-e203-4c97-b6ee-adf6232acec9.jpg`
  - `.../m/1/852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png`

  If these carry content or links, they will render as blank or as a raw filename when images are blocked, and screen readers get nothing useful. If they are decorative, they should have `alt=""` instead of no attribute.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The `mi.ulta.com`, `emltrk.com` and Dotomi pixels also lack `alt`. These are most likely 1×1 beacons, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is still the correct markup.
- **Mixed content:** All ten Dotomi pixels use `http://`. Gmail and Apple Mail proxy images, but other clients and webviews may block or warn on non-HTTPS content. A blocked pixel means the cookie sync fails silently.
- **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans...">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook desktop. Rendering there depends on the fallback stack, which was not visible in the truncated source.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` meta tags and the `:root` declaration are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncation means the rest of the body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. The QA output itself says this may be a relay capture problem, so it is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. The Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements need both headers to be present on the original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also expected through the relay. The From domain `e.ulta.com` is a subdomain used for sending, but alignment could not be verified.
- **Body-level compliance:** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion and could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 52 click-tracking links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are unknown. The only campaign identifier visible is the `emltrk.com` tag `pr_fy26wk42_saturday_bf_launch_8pm_Last`. No evidence of a mismatch was found.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six `image.e.ulta.com` content images, or `alt=""` if they are decorative. Add `alt=""` to all pixels.
2. Move the ten Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. Ten third-party sync calls add weight and a privacy surface for little email value.
3. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against raw `.eml` headers rather than the relay copy. That will settle whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are actually missing.
4. Resolve the 52 tracking links with a redirect-following probe. Capture the final URLs and check UTM parameters and landing-page status.
5. Capture the full HTML rather than the truncated copy so the footer, unsubscribe link, address and font fallback stack can be audited.
