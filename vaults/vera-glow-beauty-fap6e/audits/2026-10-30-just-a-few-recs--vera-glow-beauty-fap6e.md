# Just a few recs
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- "Just a few recs" is a gift guide with a free-mascara offer tucked in the top banner. It's pretty, but it doesn't say anything urgent. After 10/27 "How to find your shade" and 10/28 "Freshly picked California color", I was glad ILIA had gone back to product. Now it's back to gifting. The "Our Gift Recs" hero is cute and the three mini sets are easy to scan. But the real offer, a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $75+, sits in a thin red strip at the top and again near the bottom, with an asterisk.
- Compared with Sephora's giant "30% OFF" medallion dated 10/30–11/19, this has no number and no date. Drunk Elephant's "All the gifts." at least committed to a gift-guide mood. ILIA is mixing a gift guide, a bestseller grid and a GWP all in one email. I'd skim it and maybe click through to the lip sketch set. I wouldn't forward it.
- I'm doing the math anyway: $75 for a mini mascara is a high bar when the sets alone are likely under that. I can't tell if the sets qualify without clicking.

## What's working

- The "Our Gift Recs" hero shows the lip and eye sticks clearly against the blue, and the red "Shop the collection" button is easy to see.
- The three mini sets each get a clear name and a one-line description ("Three shimmery waterproof shadow sticks with 12-hour wear"). That's the product detail I actually want.
- The red banner and CTA buttons are consistent, so the eye knows where to tap.
- The "More Holiday Hits" grid mixes skincare (Super Serum Skin Tint, Sun Serum SPF) with makeup, so it works if I'm shopping for skin rather than lips.
- The free-shipping-over-$50 line and the trust icons (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet) at the bottom help build credibility.

## What's weak

- The free-gift offer is easy to miss. It's a tiny top banner and a late-email section, and the asterisks send me to "see site for details".
- There are no prices on any product, so I can't judge whether the sets get me to $75.
- The gift threshold is $75, but the email never shows a basket-building path to it.
- Four separate "Shop" buttons (collection, all sets, bestsellers, shop now) all lead to what looks like the same place. "Shop the collection" is vague.
- ILIA VIP appears only as a tiny footer icon, even though I'm a loyalty-tier person. The 10/16, 10/17 and 10/19 sends pushed VIP hard, so hiding it here feels inconsistent.
- There are no people or shade-diverse imagery, so nothing shows me the products on skin. I don't get any shade-matching guidance, which was the strength of the 10/27 email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the gift offer the hero: "Free mini mascara with $75+" with an end date, plus a progress-to-threshold cue.
- 2. Add prices to the three sets, and say whether they count toward the $75.
- 3. Cut the four near-identical CTAs down to two. Each should say what's behind it, e.g. "Shop gift sets" and "Get my free mascara."
- 4. Show VIP perks and points in the body, not just the footer.
- 5. Add one swatch or shade-range row, like the 10/27 shade guide, so I can see the colors on skin.
