Minis." on 10/23, the two "Beauty in high demand 📈" sends on 10/24, and "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" twice on 10/25, I'm numb to the format. The 🌶️🌶️🌶️ subject did make me curious, though. It tells me nothing concrete.
- The one real money thing is the "Get a Free* K18 Trial Size" strip at the top. Under it is a 4X Beauty Insider points banner on K18 with code 4XK18, which is where my points math kicks in. Then it's a long scroll of gift sets. The "MERRY MUST-HAVES" hero is generic, and the savings-event strip carries no dollar amount I can see.
- Would I click? Probably the K18 4X points, since I'd stock up on that anyway. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The K18 free trial-size strip is up top and reads as a concrete freebie.
- The 4X points on K18 with code 4XK18 is exactly the tier math I want. Rouge me notices that right away.
- The "Only at Sephora / New / Limited Edition" tags on each set help me see what's exclusive. That is how I decide whether to cross-shop at Ulta.
- Every gift set shows its price and its "value" (e.g. $38 for a $44 value). I can check the savings myself.
- The "Same-Day Unlimited" and Beauty Insider modules at the bottom are useful reminders.

## What's weak

- The "MERRY MUST-HAVES" hero is vague, and the 🌶️ subject doesn't tie to anything visible in the body.
- Most of the gift sets are $38–$65 with no discount, just "value" framing. Compare that to Ulta's "Up to 50% off Early Black Friday" email, where the deal is the headline.
- The email is very long, with a single-column set of big images and little text. I'd have to scroll a lot on my phone to find the points offer.
- The top strips are tiny and the fine print is dense. The asterisks on the free trial size and the savings event make it feel like the offer is buried.
- Sixth send in six days. The cadence is fatigue, and it feels like a catalog copy of itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer, either the K18 4X points or the free trial size, in the hero instead of the generic "Merry Must-Haves." Put the code in a button.
- 2. Cut the gift-set list to three or four items and add a clear "Shop all gift sets" CTA.
- 3. Make the Savings Event strip show the actual discount and dates, because I plan my stock-ups around the event.
- 4. Space the sends out. Sephora should stop mailing the same catalog every day.
