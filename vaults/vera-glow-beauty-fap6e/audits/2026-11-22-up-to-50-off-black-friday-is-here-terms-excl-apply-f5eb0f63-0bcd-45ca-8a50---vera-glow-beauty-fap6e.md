# Up to 50% off: Black Friday^ is here! Terms & excl. apply 🖤
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Ulta Black Friday send I've seen today, and it's the one that finally lays out the whole sale. After the 11/4 and 11/8 $10/$15/$20 coupon sends, I like the tier ladder. It runs 50%, then 40%, then 30%, then 25%, and each tier gets its own block with product tiles. I can scan it and know where my money goes. The "Member 5X POINTS" banner at the top is what makes me, as a Diamond member, pay attention.
- It's very long, though. The scroll is endless, and the point of the email gets lost in the middle. I'd click, probably the 5X points banner or the 50% tier. I wouldn't forward it, because it's the same Black Friday structure Sephora and every other retailer is running. I'd keep it in my inbox as a reference for the sale until Friday.

## What's working

- The "5X POINTS" member banner sits right at the top. Points math is my thing, and I saw it immediately.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY: Up to 50% is LIVE online" hero is clear and dominates the top of the email.
- The tiered layout (50% / 40% / 30% / 25%) lets me skim for my price tier, and big pink headers separate each one.
- The category chips (Makeup & nails, Skin care, Hair, Fragrance, Bath & body) let me jump straight to what I want.
- The free 5-piece Ulta Beauty Collection gift offer is a concrete add-on, and a Diamond-tier shopper like me would pick up on it.

## What's weak

- The email is endless. By the 25% tier I'd stopped reading, and the "plus these can't-miss deals" block at the bottom is easy to miss.
- Each tile has a small "Shop now" link that is hard to tap on a phone.
- The brand tiles don't show which specific products are discounted. "Select Fragrance" and "select makeup" don't tell me what I'm buying.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, with long "Terms & excl. apply" paragraphs at the bottom. I'd want that kind of exclusion language more visible up top, because Ulta has a history of hiding exclusions.
- I got the same Black Friday email earlier today. That's the third send in one day, which feels like spam.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half. Lead with 5X points plus the 50% tier, then link out to a landing page for 40/30/25.
- 2. Make the tiles show specific products with sale prices, not "select" lines. Sephora's Cyber Week email names products and shows real prices.
- 3. Make the CTAs full-width buttons that are easy to tap.
- 4. Add a personalized strip, such as "Diamond: you earn 5X on these brands."
- 5. Stop sending several near-identical Black Friday emails on the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Diamond: 5X points + up to 50% off, live now 🖤`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: 50% off Ulta Beauty brands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 5X points on everything, plus a free 5-piece gift. Online now.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fragrance, makeup, hair tools: every tier of savings in one place.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated HTML source and automated QA show a 57% pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings). The confirmed issues are missing `alt` attributes on multiple images and 10 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed from this capture, and the link, UTM and footer checks are blocked by the skipped links and truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were skipped** because the QA treated them as click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **10 third-party pixels use plain `http://`**: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`, then `pub2.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - Gmail, Apple Mail and others proxy images, but non-HTTPS sources can be blocked as mixed content in some clients and webmail.
  - Ten cookie-redirect/sync calls to an ad-tech domain add load time and a privacy/deliverability footprint.
- Other tracking pixels:
  - `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=164895 + pr_fy26wk43_sunday_bf_6am_last + 32490`
  - `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=164895`
  - `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/3eb758650b65389e.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=164895`
- The `emltrk.com` URL contains literal spaces and `+` characters (` + pr_fy26wk43_sunday_bf_6am_last + 32490`). That is either a QA-report formatting artifact or an unencoded URL. Verify against the raw source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images** (all warnings from the QA report):
  - `https://image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `.../m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`

  - `.../m/1/852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png` (flagged twice)
  - Where these carry promotional content, images-off rendering and screen readers get nothing.
- **Missing `alt` on pixels** (`dotomi` ×10, `mi.ulta.com` ×2, `emltrk.com` ×1). For tracking pixels the fix is explicit `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them. I can't tell from the QA output whether the attribute is absent or merely empty.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`** (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans...`) is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The font-family fallback stack isn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it is adequate.
- Present and fine:

  - `color-scheme` meta tags
  - MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`
  - Apple data-detector overrides
- The `<title>` is `Ulta Beauty ` (generic, with trailing whitespace). This is minor.
- The remainder of the HTML is truncated, so table structure and `role="presentation"` usage are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens were visible in the captured source or the QA output. Subscriber-identifying parameters appear in the pixel URLs (`mi_u=203057427`, `i=203057427`). They are resolved and expected for tracking, but they are a per-recipient identifier passed to third parties.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as **unverified, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it is worth checking against a direct-delivery copy.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com` (a subdomain of `ulta.com`). The header is expected to be absent via the relay.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML and can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 68 click-tracked links were skipped and none were resolved, so UTM parameters and landing page destinations are unknown. The only visible campaign identifier is the `emltrk.com` pixel label `pr_fy26wk43_sunday_bf_6am_last`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five content images listed in section 3.
2. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to all 13 tracking pixels.
3. Move the 10 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or consolidate them into a server-side sync. The `http://` sources are the only hard failures that clients can act on.
4. Check the `emltrk.com` pixel URL in the raw source for unencoded spaces or `+` characters.
5. Re-run the compliance check against a direct-delivery copy with raw headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Update the QA tooling so it:
