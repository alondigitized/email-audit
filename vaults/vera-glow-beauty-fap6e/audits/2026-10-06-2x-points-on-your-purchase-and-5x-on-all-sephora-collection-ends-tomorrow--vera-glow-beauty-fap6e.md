# 🚨2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This email gets my attention, though the subject and the body aren't selling the same thing. The subject is all points math: 2X on my purchase, 5X on all Sephora Collection, ends tomorrow. The body opens with the points banner, then pivots to a long scroll of Holiday Vaults, advent calendars and gift sets. I wanted the points math and got a gift guide. I'm Rouge, so I'd open it. But it reads like three emails stacked together: points, vaults and AI gift chat, then app early access, auto-replenish and a $100 coupon.
- The Sephora Collection Premium Advent Calendar is $99 with a $277 value, and I can price that against 5X points. The Laneige 12 Day set at $149 and the Crown Affair Hot Hair Vault at $272 are Sephora-exclusive and limited, so they have real scarcity. The screenshot is tiny, so the fine print and code instructions are hard to read. That's a problem for a mobile reader. I'd click the advent calendar and nothing else. I wouldn't forward it, but I'd screenshot the 5X Sephora Collection line.

## What's working

- The points banner is first and ends 10/7. It's the only email from this week that leads with Rouge-relevant math, which is the main reason I'd open it.
- The "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA + LIMITED EDITION" tags on every vault tell me what's exclusive.
- The price and "$277 value" labels sit next to each product. That lets me do value math in about two seconds, and Ulta's emails usually don't give me this.
- The points tier strip (2X, 3X, 4X, 5X) near the bottom shows how my spend maps to points. That's the Beauty Insider tier info I care about.
- Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal and Venmo logos up top reassure me on payment, which matters on a $272 vault.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth Sephora send since 10/4 and the third in two days. After the 10/5 "APP EARLY ACCESS" emails, the cadence feels heavy.
- The body is too long. Six vaults, an AI gift chat tool, a points strip, app early access, auto-replenish and a $100 coupon are all competing for one click.
- Two codes, SAVEPOINTS and SAVETEN, plus the points tiers and the in-store barcode, make the offer hard to follow. I'm good at points math and I still had to work to find the actual 2X/5X rule.
- The points banner is small and the copy is dense, and the 2X/5X condition isn't explained in a way I can read without zooming.
- There are no people anywhere, so nothing in the email speaks to my age or skin tone. It's all product on gold foil.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the points offer plus the three strongest vaults. Move app early access, auto-replenish and the $100 coupon to another send.
- 2. Put a single-line points rule at the top: "2X on your order, 5X on Sephora Collection, code SAVEPOINTS, ends 10/7." Make the code a tap-to-copy button.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific. "Shop the $99 Advent Calendar" beats "GET SHOPPING."
- 4. Show my tier and point balance, for example "Rouge: you'd earn X points on this vault." Ulta's 21 Days and Ulta's tier banners do this better.
- 5. Space out sends. Combine the app early access and points messages into one weekly send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `5X points on Sephora Collection ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge: 2X points + $99 advent calendar, ends 10/7`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVEPOINTS: 2X on your order, 5X on all Sephora Collection. Holiday Vaults inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our limited-edition Holiday Vaults are here, and your points just got faster.`
