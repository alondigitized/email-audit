# Smell rich with Glossier You Doux
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, finally — after five Labor Day sale emails in a row from this brand, Glossier sends me something that's not about a discount code. "You smell rich" is a good hook, the hero image is genuinely striking (the model's skin, the bracelet, the way she's cradling the bottle), and the fragrance notes breakdown with top/middle/base is the kind of detail I actually want before I drop $$ on a new scent. No banner screaming "30% OFF ENDS TONIGHT" for once.
- But there's zero price anywhere in this email. Not on the hero, not in the size tiers at the bottom (100ml/50ml/8ml), nothing. I'm Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond — I do the points math on every single purchase, and this email gives me nothing to math with. I don't even know if this is a $70 rollerball or a $150 bottle until I click through, which is friction I don't love from a brand I already have to actively decide is worth full price versus chasing a Sephora Beauty Insider event discount instead.
- Would I click? Probably, out of genuine curiosity about the scent — Palo Santo, frankincense, myrrh sounds cozy. Would I forward it to a friend? Only if she's already a Glossier girl; there's nothing here compelling enough to convert someone. I'd read it, maybe tap through on mobile, not urgently.

## What's working

- The hero visual is elevated — moody, warm-toned, on-brand for a "fall fragrance" launch, and doesn't feel like a repeat of anything in my Glossier history.
- Actual product education (top/middle/base notes) instead of just another sale banner — a nice change of pace after "Save 30% off fragrance" on 9/7.
- Multiple CTAs ("Shop now," "Shop Doux") are clearly placed with visible buttons throughout.
- Sizing options (100ml, 50ml, 8ml) shown at the bottom gives me a real choice, unlike a lot of single-SKU pushes.

## What's weak

- No price anywhere — not even a "starting at $X." Ulta's 20%-off emails at least show me a mechanic; this gives me nothing to weigh against my Sephora Rouge points balance.
- No loyalty or rewards mention at all — I don't know if this qualifies for Glossier Rewards points, which matters to me since I'm always cross-shopping loyalty math between Sephora, Ulta, and brand-direct.
- Layout seems to be running wide/cut off on the right side in a few sections (the "Fit for fall" copy and note list both look clipped) — reads like a rendering issue on my end.
- No social proof or reviews — for a new scent launch, I'd want to see "4.8 stars" or a customer quote before committing to a full-size purchase sight-unseen (well, smell-unseen).

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or starting-at price near the top CTA — this is the single biggest miss. I shouldn't have to click through to do my math.
- 2. Mention Glossier Rewards / points value if this purchase earns them — give loyalty people like me a reason to buy direct instead of at Sephora.
- 3. Add a star rating or one-line review snippet near the notes breakdown to build trust on a launch.
- 4. Fix whatever's causing the right-edge text clipping in the "Fit for fall" and notes sections.
