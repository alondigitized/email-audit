# A little thank you, from us 🫶 Get $10 off your $50 qualifying purchase (Terms & excl. apply)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Ulta email in about three weeks, and I'm doing the same math again. This is the same $10-off-$50 coupon from the 11/4 and 11/8 sends, now with a "thank you" bow on it. The coupon comes first, with the barcode and the code THANKS. The "up to 50% off" Black Friday block sits much lower, even though that's the better deal. It feels like three emails stacked into one long scroll.
- Pink-and-orange gifting energy is on-brand for Ulta, and the "includes prestige brands and fragrance too!" line did catch me. The fine print runs a mile long, though, and the sweepstakes block at the bottom is noise. I'd open it because it's Ulta and I'm Diamond. I'd click once, to grab the coupon code, then screenshot it and move on. I wouldn't forward it. There's nothing here a friend couldn't get from the 11/22 "Black Friday is here" send, which laid out the sale ladder better.

## What's working

- The $10 off hero is huge and unmistakable, with the barcode and the online code THANKS right in the same card. I don't have to hunt for it.
- "Yep, includes prestige brands and fragrance too!" answers the exact question I'd have. I cross-shop Sephora, so prestige eligibility is the thing I check first.
- The member-exclusive plush robe / luxe throw block with a $75 fragrance purchase is a real perk, and it gives Ulta Rewards members something to do.
- The free 7-piece beauty bag with a $75 gift purchase is concrete and visual. I can tell what I'd get.
- The deadline is clear: ends Saturday, November 28 at 11:59 PM CT.

## What's weak

- It's the fourth "same coupon, new wrapper" send. After the 11/4 "Use your $10 off $50" and 11/8 "Ends tonight!" emails, a $10 off $50 hero doesn't feel like a thank-you. It feels like a repeat.
- The "Black Friday up to 50% off" banner sits mid-email, below the coupon and the robe block. The bigger offer is buried under the smaller one. Sephora would lead with the biggest number.
- The product grid in the middle has no prices, no names, and no reviews. It's pretty, but I can't tell what any of it is or what it costs.
- The Rewards points math is missing. There's no points multiplier or tier perk, only a "Join for free" bar on top, which is odd for someone already in the program.
- The Magic Starts with You sweepstakes block and the wall of legal text drag the bottom half. It reads like clutter, and the fine print is tiny enough that I'd have to zoom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 50%-off Black Friday headline above the $10 coupon, or merge them into one clear "stack the coupon on top" message. Right now the two offers compete.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes and trim the legal block, or move them out of the main scroll.
- 3. Add the product names and prices under the product grid so it earns the scroll.
- 4. Add personalization. Show me my points balance or my tier perks, not a "Join for free" bar I've already used.
- 5. Space out the coupon sends. Four near-identical emails in a row trains me to skip them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: XHTML doctype, `lang="en-US"`, viewport meta, and MSO/VML fallbacks. It has 57% QA pass rate, driven mainly by about 30 missing-alt images and 12 non-HTTPS third-party image requests. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path, so they aren't confirmed defects. The HTML is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and CTA hrefs were not available to verify.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **57 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are **unverified**, not clean.
- **Non-HTTPS image requests (mixed content).** Eleven are plain `http://`:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1` through `pub10.php`, which are 10 third-party cookie-sync pixels.
  - `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7Imei…`, which is the Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content call.
  - Clients that block or upgrade HTTP requests may drop these. A dropped `LiveContent.aspx` request could leave a content block empty.
- **The Dotomi pixels are an ad-tech cookie-sync chain** (`cookieredir`) embedded in the email body. It adds 10 external requests per open and extends load time. Many clients and privacy proxies strip them, so they are unreliable as well as heavy.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=165147` is served over HTTPS, but it has no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Tracking and pixel images missing alt:** `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png`, `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/3eb758650b65389e.png`, the 10 `pub*.php` pixels, `LiveContent.aspx`, and `emltrk.com`. - **Web font:** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. The CSS fallback stack is outside the truncated portion, so I could not check it.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared, but I saw no `prefers-color-scheme` rules in the visible CSS. The `#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit !important }` override forces link colors in some clients. Verify contrast in dark mode.
- **Positive findings:** `lang` is set, MSO document settings are present, and Apple data-detector overrides are included.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject.
- `mi_u=203057427` is a subscriber or member ID in the `mi.ulta.com` image URLs. It is an identifier embedded in a URL and is not itself a defect.
- Anything below the truncation point is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**. Ulta sends through Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`exct.net`), which typically sets these headers. Check the raw `.eml` before treating it as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown because the relay does not expose it. The sender is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com`, with images on `image.e.ulta.com`. The sending domain is aligned with the brand, which is a good sign, but nothing was verified.
- **Footer and physical address:** the unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated HTML. I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance.
- The subject includes an emoji (🫶) and "(Terms & excl. apply)", which is a disclosure cue. No technical issue was found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing URLs can't be assessed. All 57 tracked links are click-redirect URLs that were not resolved, and no final destination URLs are in the data.
- The only campaign identifier visible is in the tracking pixel's data: `pr_fy26wk43_thursday_broad_10off50_10am_pz_last` (job ID `32648`). It is consistent with the "$10 off $50" subject and suggests a 10am send slot.
- To verify continuity, follow one or two redirects and check that the landing page carries the offer and any promo code.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Treat the header warnings as capture gaps.** Pull the raw `.eml` from the source inbox and check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Fix the capture path so these warnings stop firing on every email.
2. **Resolve a sample of the 57 tracked links**, especially the primary CTA and the unsubscribe link. Record the final URL and UTM parameters to complete the continuity check.
3. **Add alt text** to the eight content image URLs listed in section 3. In the QA check, treat known tracking and pixel domains (`dotomi.com`, `emltrk.com`, `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/`, `exct.net`) as decorative and exclude them from the missing-alt count. That should remove about 17 of roughly 30 alt warnings.
4. **Flag non-HTTPS images by whether they are content or pixels.** The `LiveContent.aspx` one is the most important, because it likely loads visible content.
5. **Fix the truncation.** Capture the full HTML (at minimum the footer and CTA hrefs) so the compliance and link checks are not left unverified.
