# Wait, you’re going to want in on these Cyber Week deals 😅
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Sephora email in five days, and it's the purple sequins again. But this one is the first where I'd actually stay. The 11/24 "Cyber Week is really bringing it ON" was mostly hype. Today the offers are stacked. There's a 4X points gift-fragrance banner, a free sample bag, four "30% off" brand tiles, a product grid and a fragrance block. I'm doing the points math before I scroll.
- The brand tiles are the draw. Eilish, Lancôme, a skincare tile and Viktor&Rolf each get a giant "30% off", and they're easy to scan. The problem is the volume. It's a very long email, with at least five CTAs, and the offers fight for attention. The 4X points banner and the "GRABMYBAG" code at the top are exactly what a Rouge member wants, but the code is tiny and the fine print is tinier.
- Would I click? Yes, probably on Lancôme or the fragrance block. Would I forward it? Only to my one friend who is also a points nerd. Otherwise I'd keep it open until the weekend.

## What's working

- The top banner leads with "Gift fragrance, get 4X points" and "Use code CYBERSCENT". That's loyalty math I can act on.
- The 30% off tiles are huge and scannable, with a clear "Save Now" button on each.
- The free sample bag with code GRABMYBAG is a cute extra, and the "choose 1 of 4" framing makes it feel curated.
- The "Snag These Can't-Miss Deals" grid shows real prices, like Summer Fridays $19 and Laneige $24. I can judge the value without clicking.
- The deal window ("24 hours only", "through 11/30") is clear and credible.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth send in five days with the same sequin creative. Ulta would at least rotate the hero.
- Too many competing offers: 4X points, a free bag, 30% off, up to 30% off fragrance, up to 50% off gifts. Nothing is the single hero.
- The GRABMYBAG code is tiny, and the fine print is a wall of text I'd never read.
- The product grid is small and the type is hard to read on a phone without zooming.
- I got nothing personalized. There was no Rouge tier callout and no points balance, so it felt like a stranger email.
- The 30% brand tiles are in-stock only, and the footnote is easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero, either the 30% brand haul or 4X points, and cut the rest of the stacked offers. Ulta's 11/25 Black Friday email put the full tier ladder (50/40/30) in one block and it read cleaner.
- 2. Add Rouge-specific content, like "Rouge: your 4X points on fragrance ends 11/30."
- 3. Make the sample bag code a big button that copies the code.
- 4. Shorten the email and cut the footer fine print down.
- 5. Vary the creative from day to day. e.l.f. also overloaded me, but at least its hero changed.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge: 4X points on fragrance ends 11/30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free sample bag with code GRABMYBAG`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Cyber Week picks: 30% off top brands`

# Technical Audit: Sephora Cyber Week (2026-11-25)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a hidden preheader and two tracking pixels. It has a fixed 700px layout and a missing plain-text part, which is the one QA FAIL. Most compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed, because the headers weren't captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is incomplete.** QA probed 12 of 53 links and skipped 41. No probe results (status codes, redirects) were provided, so link health is unverified. Only the 12 probed links were checked, and none of them is reported as broken.
- **Open-tracking pixels, observed:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a hidden `<img>` with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261125_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_3` is a 1x1 image.
  - Two separate pixel hosts is normal for ESP plus analytics. The campaign ID (`mi_ecmp`) is clearly labeled, which is good.
- **Per-recipient ID in the pixel URL.** `mi_u=4970293024993920` is a per-recipient identifier. This is expected, but it will resolve for anyone who receives a forwarded copy.
- **Pixel placement.** The `app.sephora.com` pixel sits inside the preheader `<td>` after the hidden div. That is functional, but it is the first element a client will preview.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout.** The wrapper uses `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"` (`class="tbl tbl_cntr"`). The `@media (max-width:480px)` block only adjusts fonts and icon sizes. It doesn't override the 700px minimum, so mobile clients will likely scale down or scroll horizontally. I can't confirm inner tables are fluid because the source is truncated.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Malformed conditional comment.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block of a `<style>` tag. Outlook conditionals don't apply there, and the stray `<!--` may cause some parsers to drop the rule.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no practical effect in email.
- **Unsupported JSON-LD.** The `application/ld+json` block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip or ignore it, and the empty values are meaningless if parsed.
- **Missing alt text, from QA.**
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/...` pixel has no `alt` attribute at all. It is hidden, so the impact is low.
  - The `o.gif` pixel is flagged, but the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`. That is correct for a decorative image, so this is a false positive.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't describe the message. This is minor and only affects "view in browser" and some assistive tools.
- **Plain-text part missing.** QA FAIL: the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility. The AgentMail relay may have dropped it, so check the raw MIME to confirm.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`, etc.) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the body wasn't fully checked. The only personalization found is the `mi_u` ID in the pixel URL.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe is not found (WARN).** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unverified. If the raw message also lacks it, that is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results is absent (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` can't be validated from this data.
- **In-body footer not verified.** The visible source doesn't show the unsubscribe link or physical address because it is truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set, but no result was given. This is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page URLs are not visible in the truncated source, and no probe results were provided, so continuity can't be assessed. Only the tracking-pixel campaign tag (`mi_ecmp=20261125_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_3`) is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as real defects.
2. **Confirm the text/plain part.** If it's genuinely empty, add one, since the 0-char FAIL is the only hard failure.
3. **Make the layout fluid.** Change the `min-width:700px; width:700px` wrapper to `width:100%; max-width:700px`. Also remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. **Fix the style block.** Move the Outlook `<!--[if mso]>` rule out of the `@media` block. Correct `no index` to `noindex`, or drop the tag.
5. **Remove the JSON-LD block**, or populate it with a real `discountCode`.
6. **Add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com` pixel.** This clears the legitimate QA warning. Treat the `o.gif` warning as a false positive.
7. **Re-run QA on the full HTML and a larger link sample.** Probe past the top 12 so UTM and landing-page alignment can be audited.
