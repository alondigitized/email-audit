# It’s not over yet
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "you have 0 VIP points to spend" as the top banner — thanks for reminding me I'm not even in the club yet, ILIA, real warm welcome. This is at least a step up from the plain-text Marissa cosplay they ran on 9/17 and 9/20 — there's an actual hero shot here, the pencil/mascara/eyeliner fanned out on that rust velvet backdrop with the little "20% off" gift tag hanging over it is genuinely pretty, on par with the styled flat-lay from their 9/19 "Safe for sensitive eyes" send. But underneath the nice photography this is the same VIP-gate mechanic they've run three sends running now (9/18, 9/17, 9/14) — join VIP to get 20% off, still gatekeeping a customer who's clearly already on their list since they're emailing me directly.
- The "two days left" urgency is doing real work here — that's more credible than "This only happens once a year" from 9/20 — but I've now seen this VIP-or-nothing structure so many times it's white noise. Sephora's Beauty Insider tier program tells me exactly where I stand (points balance, next reward); ILIA is asking me to join a loyalty program from scratch every single email, which as a Rouge/Diamond member is honestly a demotion in how I'm being treated.
- I'd scroll past this, not click. Not forwarding it — my group chat is not asking me about the fifth "join VIP" email this month.

## What's working

- The velvet hero shot with the pencil, mascara, and eyeliner fanned out plus the hanging "20% off" tag is genuinely well-styled — best production value ILIA's put out since the 9/19 flat-lay.
- "Two days left" is a real, credible deadline, not vague "ends soon" language.
- The free gift ladder (free multi-stick, free velvet bag on $100+) gives me a concrete reason to add to cart beyond just the discount.
- Clear tier-benefit list at the bottom (free shipping, points, early access) — at least it spells out what VIP actually gets me.

## What's weak

- Leading with "You have 0 VIP points to spend" is borderline insulting to someone they're emailing directly — either treat me like a stranger or a member, not a scolding hybrid.
- This is the fourth "join VIP for 20%" gate I've logged in two weeks (9/14, 9/17, 9/18, now this) — the mechanic hasn't moved at all, just the hero image.
- No shade-match or skin-type personalization anywhere, and I've told ILIA what I wear — Drunk Elephant's "15% off looks good on you" at least varied its mechanic (two-or-more gets a bump), this is just a rerun.
- Six separate CTAs (VIP, gift shop, cart extras, bundle up, join VIP again) with no single clear hero offer — Sephora's Clean beauty send on 9/20 had one clean hero + one CTA and it worked better.
- The "sale's on sale" ribbon section is a mood image with zero information — dead weight before I even get to the actual offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the VIP gate entirely for known subscribers — if I'm getting this email, show me my actual points balance and apply the 20% directly. That single fix would do more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Cut the email down to one hero offer + one CTA above the fold instead of six competing sections (gift shop, sale's on sale, cart extras, bundle up, VIP join) — I'm not going to scroll through five asks.
- 3. Add a real personalization hook — my past purchases or shade match — the way Sephora's before/after hair shot at least implied "this is relevant to you."
- 4. Replace "sale's on sale" ribbon with actual product or copy — it's just a stock photo of fabric right now.
