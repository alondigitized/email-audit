# Love your curls all summer long
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first honest reaction — Sally is not really talking to me. "Enjoy hydrated, healthy-looking curls all season" with a warm-weather frizz-control angle is fine copy, but I'm not the curl-focused Sally shopper; this reads like it's aimed at someone actively managing textured hair as their whole routine, not someone cross-shopping Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond for color and skincare drops. The hero model is genuinely pretty and the curl definition looks real (not overly retouched), so points for that, but the whole email is hair color and hair care skus stacked on top of each other with zero mention of the loyalty math I actually track.
- The one thing that did stop my scroll was the pink Sally Rewards banner — "Shop July 24-31 and receive a $5 Reward to use August 10-31" — because I'm always doing the points-and-reward-window math in my head, and this is at least a real mechanic with real dates. But it's buried two-thirds down the page after four separate product tiles I have to scroll past first. Compare that to Ulta's "Lucky you! 20% off one qualifying item" email that scored a 9 with me — Ulta puts the reward front and center with a barcode you can act on immediately. Sally makes me work for it.
- Would I click? Not really — nothing here is a curl person's must-have for me specifically, and there's no clear single CTA I care about. I wouldn't forward this to a friend either; it's competent hair-color merchandising, not something worth sharing. I'd mark as read and move on, same as I do with most of Sally's sends lately.

## What's working

- The hero curl photo is warm, realistic, and well-lit — no obvious retouching overkill, feels aspirational without being fake.
- "NEW AT SALLY" label up top gives launch-day clarity, which I always want to see before I decide if something's worth my attention.
- The Sally Rewards $5 offer has actual, credible dates (earn July 24-31, redeem Aug 10-31) — that's real mechanic, not vague "member exclusive" fluff.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" line is a nice touch for anyone stacking a bigger hair-color haul.
- Utility bar up top (BOPIS, 2-hour delivery) is genuinely useful and something neither Sephora nor Ulta emphasize as hard.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization signal — no name, no "based on your last order," nothing that tells me Sally knows I'm a color-and-skincare shopper, not a curl-care one.
- The product grid (ion, L'Oreal Technique, ion again, Wella) is all hair-color-adjacent, which undercuts the "curls all summer" headline — the trending products don't actually match the hero story.
- Reward banner is buried below four product tiles; Ulta's comparable offer email leads with the reward instead of hiding it.
- No urgency anywhere in the hero — "all season" is the opposite of a deadline, so nothing pushes me to act today.
- This is Sally's fourth-ish send in a short window (per my own history: BOGO on the 25th, It's a 10 on the 24th, L'Oreal spotlight on the 22nd) and none of them have felt tailored to me — it's starting to feel like generic rotation, not curation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Rewards $5 offer, not curl copy — move the pink banner to the top or fold its offer into the hero itself. That's the one mechanic with real dates and real value; don't make me scroll for it.
- 2. Segment the "trending favorites" grid to match the hero story or my purchase history — if the headline is curls, show curl creams and diffusers, not permanent hair color kits.
- 3. Add urgency to the reward window in the subject/preheader itself, since "August 10-31" redemption is a real deadline worth surfacing early.
- 4. Personalize the greeting or subject line with tier/points context — Ulta and Sephora both do this better; even a simple "Rouge/Diamond member" nod would make this feel less like a mass blast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your $5 Sally Reward is loading — here's how to unlock it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Curl season starts now (+ a reward waiting for you)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn by July 31, redeem August 10-31 — details inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New curl care, plus a $5 reward window you don't want to miss`
