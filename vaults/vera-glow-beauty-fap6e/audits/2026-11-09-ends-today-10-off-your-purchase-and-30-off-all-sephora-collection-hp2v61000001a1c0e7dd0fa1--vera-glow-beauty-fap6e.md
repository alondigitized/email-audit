# ENDS TODAY 🚨 10% off your purchase and 30% off ALL Sephora Collection
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- Fourth Savings Event email in a row (11/6, 11/7, 11/8, now 11/9). This is the first one where "ENDS TODAY" is actually true, and the hero is clean. Two big badges sit side by side: Insider 10% and Sephora Collection 30%, both "Ends 11/9." I'm Rouge, so my number is 30%, and I did the math before scrolling. Still, I've seen this hero four days running. The "Last Chance" energy is earned today, but I'm fatigued.
- The body is a long scroll. It has the 500-point challenge, Beauty on Demand, eight product tiles, a free-gift tease, a Kohl's store locator and a holiday gift guide. The promo codes are in the hero, which helps. By the Kohl's block and the barcode at the bottom, it feels like a catalog.
- Would I click? Yes, but only to shop Sephora Collection before midnight, and I'd probably go straight to the app. I wouldn't forward it. Ulta ran the same $10/$15/$20 ladder for four weeks and I tuned out. Sephora is heading the same way.

## What's working

- The two-badge hero (Insider 10%, Sephora Collection 30%) shows both tiers at a glance. Ulta never gives me that kind of side-by-side.
- "ENDS TODAY" with "Ends 11/9" on both badges is a real deadline. It's more credible than the 11/8 "ends TOMORROW" send.
- The online and in-store codes are right under the hero (ALLONSALE and SCSAVE). I don't have to hunt for them.
- The 500-point Beauty Insider challenge is a real loyalty hook, and it gives me points math to play with.
- The product tiles show sale prices with the old price struck through. EADEM, Summer Fridays, Rare Beauty and Dr. Jart+ are brands I actually shop.

## What's weak

- Four days of the same hero. Without the "ends today" line, this is a carbon copy of 11/6.
- The Rouge-specific 30% isn't called out for me. The "10%" badge is the biggest thing on screen, so it reads like I'm a basic Insider.
- The Sephora Collection 30% badge is a hero, but the product grid below shows almost no Sephora Collection items. Mostly it's prestige brands at 10%.
- Too many modules: gift with purchase, Kohl's store finder, gift guide, Beauty on Demand and a skincare block. The fine print runs very long.
- There's no skin-tone or skin-type personalization. Everyone gets the same blast.
- The "Free Same-Day Delivery" strip is tiny and gets lost under the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it down to the countdown, the two badges and one product row. Drop the Kohl's block from a final-day send.
- 2. Show Sephora Collection product tiles right under the 30% badge, so the offer I'm racing the clock for is the one I see.
- 3. Label the tiers: "Rouge: 20% off." Show members the number that applies to them.
- 4. Make the same-day delivery cutoff ("order by 1 PM") bigger and put it higher.
- 5. Add a skin-type-personalized product row for known members.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rouge, Insider and VIB all end 11/9 — code ALLONSALE`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with a fixed 700px layout and first-party tracking domains. The automated QA run failed on the missing plain-text part. The HTML source is truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 40 skipped. No broken links or redirect failures were reported for the probed set. The unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it.
- **Tracking pixels are first-party.**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261109_HSE_Insider_Ends_Today` is a 1x1 image.
- **Image-blocking clients lose open tracking.** Both pixels are images, so they won't register opens in clients that block images by default.
- **Pixel in a hidden preheader.** The first pixel sits right after the hidden preheader `div`. Some clients strip hidden content, which could drop it.
- **No UTM parameters are visible.** None appear in the visible source, but links past the truncation point weren't inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **QA false positive on `o.gif`.** The source has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel. That warning can be ignored.
- **Real missing `alt` on the `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel.** The `<img>` has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is minimal, but adding `alt=""` would clear the QA warning.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has none, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Layout tables lack `role="presentation"`.** None of the visible layout tables, such as `table.full-wrap` and `table.tbl`, carry it.
- **Fixed-width layout.** The wrapper is `min-width:700px; width:700px`. Mobile rendering depends on the `@media (max-width:480px)` overrides, and the wrapper has no fluid fallback.
- **Malformed CSS inside the media query.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Non-MSO parsers will likely drop `img.mso-size` and the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, because the stray tokens corrupt their selectors. This is probable from the CSS grammar, not confirmed by rendering.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `ld+json` block has `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` and a bare `"PromotionCard"`. The empty discount code is invalid markup, and most clients strip or ignore the block.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The pixel carries a per-recipient identifier, `mi_u=4958433775694848`, and it is populated. The rest of the body was truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is missing.** The text version is 0 chars, so there is no `multipart/alternative` text part. This affects deliverability scoring and accessibility. It could be an AgentMail capture artifact, but that is unconfirmed.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the relay may not preserve these headers, so this is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` can't be assessed from this capture.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part** (the only confirmed FAIL), or verify whether the relay is dropping it.
2. **Confirm the headers** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in the raw message at the origin MTA, not through the relay.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Add `lang="en"`** to `<html>` and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
5. **Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block** out of the `@media` rule, into its own conditional `<style>` block, so `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` isn't dropped.
6. **Add `alt=""`** to the `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel.
7. **Fix or remove the JSON-LD.** Drop the empty `discountCode` and the bare `PromotionCard`, or populate them.
