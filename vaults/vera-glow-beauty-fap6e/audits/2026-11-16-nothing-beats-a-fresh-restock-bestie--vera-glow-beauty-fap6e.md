# Nothing beats a fresh restock, Bestie🧡
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- My gut says this is a pleasant "newsletter" send with no real reason to open it right now. I've gotten four e.l.f. emails in a week, and the 11/14 "$10 reward on us" and 11/13 "This just peels so bright" emails each had a clear hook. This one doesn't. "The elf EDIT" is a curated roundup: new arrivals, best sellers, fall favorites, a gift with purchase, the Bright Appeel pads again, and a quiz.
- The one real offer is the free full-size gift on $35+ ("up to a $9 value"), and it sits in the middle of the email. The subject promises a restock, but I can't find a single "back in stock" item in the body. Sephora would have opened with the offer. Ulta would have put a bold %-off banner at the very top. Here I have to scroll through a hero photo and two black "shop" buttons before I see anything with a dollar sign.
- Would I click? Maybe the gift banner, because I'd do the points math on a $35 threshold. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The "choose a full-size gift on us" banner is clear: $35+ spend, no promo code needed, and "limited time only!" That is a real, mathable offer.
- The Bright Appeel "two-step glow-up" section ties back to the 11/13 launch, so the sequence feels coherent for someone who saw that email.
- Footer trust cues are strong: cruelty-free and vegan badges, free shipping, and the app download. They remind me why I cross-shop e.l.f.
- The "Bestie" greeting fits the brand voice and feels friendly, not corporate.
- The product grids are clean, and the Bright Appeel and lip products are easy to see.

## What's weak

- The subject says "fresh restock," but nothing in the body shows a restock or back-in-stock label. It's a bait-and-switch on the hook.
- The hero is a stylized photo with "THE elf EDIT" in a heavy, tight font, and the top two buttons ("Shop New Arrivals," "Shop Best Sellers") don't tell me anything specific.
- The "November Fall Favorites" tiles are four tiny "Shop Now" cards with no product names, prices, or shades. I can't tell what I'd be buying.
- No Rewards or tier math. After the 11/14 reward-drop email, I expected my balance or points status to show somewhere.
- Too many sections: the quiz, the category nav, the TikTok Shop and receipt scanning blocks, and the footer. The email is very long and the offer gets lost in it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free full-size gift into the hero with a number, such as "Free full-size gift at $35." Then make the subject match the content.
- 2. If there's a real restock, show it with a "Back in stock" tag, product names and prices. If not, don't promise one.
- 3. Add names and prices to the Fall Favorites tiles, plus a rating or review count.
- 4. Add a personalized Rewards line, for example "You have $X to use."
- 5. Cut the quiz, the category nav and the TikTok block to shorten the email.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is table-based with `role="presentation"` and a preheader, but 4 of the 12 probed links failed in QA (27% pass rate). Those failures look like a probe or redirect-handling problem rather than confirmed breakage. Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 probe failures, all `unknown url type`.** The error shows the redirect chain ended in a relative `Location` value that the probe couldn't resolve:
  - `/products/camo-liquid-bronzer-contour?Color=1+Fair`
  - `/products/halo-glow-highlight-beauty-wand?Color=Champagne+Campaign+-+Champagne+Pearl+for+Fair+to+Tan`
  - `/products/putty-blush?Color=Tahiti`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (the "Receipt scanning" link)
- **Not confirmed as recipient-facing breakage.** Browsers resolve relative redirects, so this may only be a probe limitation.
- **Resolution base is unknown.** The relative path would resolve against whichever host issued the redirect. If that host is the tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com`, the result would be a 404. If it is `elfcosmetics.com`, it is fine. Verify with `curl -sIL` on each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` URL.
- **Wrapping is consistent.** The links go through the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...~~/AAQRxRA~/` tracker, and the `?Color=` variant parameters survive in the destinations.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 47 material links were probed, so the 35 skipped links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- `<title></title>` is empty.
- The `html_block` wrapper uses `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty first family is invalid and is ignored in most clients.
- Google Fonts is loaded via `<link>` and a body-level `<style>@import url(...Jost...)</style>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore the `@import`, and an `@import` placed inside the body is non-standard. The fallback stack (Verdana) applies.
- One image has no alt text: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/-V4AGN...`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking pixel, so the impact is minor, but add `alt=""`.
- The logo link has `tabindex="-1"` and `alt="e.l.f."`. This is acceptable, but it removes the link from keyboard navigation.
- The `<html lang="en">` attribute is present.
- The source is truncated, so the rest of the body could not be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered Liquid or merge tokens in the visible source. The subject's "Bestie" is a fixed salutation and may be a first-name fallback. The truncated HTML doesn't show how it is generated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers, so it is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Check `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com` against the raw headers or a seed-list tool.
- An unsubscribe link was probed according to QA, but its result was not listed. The footer and physical-address block fall in the truncated portion, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be verified. The tracker URLs are opaque, and the probe never reached a final URL on any of the 4 failed links.
- The relative destinations suggest product-detail pages with variant preselected, which is good alignment. The "Receipt scanning" link goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Logged-out recipients land on a login wall rather than a rewards or receipt-scanning page, so the destination may not match the link text.
- The plain-text part is 85% URLs (14833 of 17423 chars), because the tracking URLs are very long (about 500 characters each). This is a minor spam-score risk, not a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Run `curl -sIL` on the 4 failed links. Confirm the `Location` headers resolve to `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` and not to the tracking host. If they are relative, ask the ESP to emit absolute URLs.
2. Fix the probe to resolve relative redirects with `urljoin(current_url, location)`, so the 4 failures don't recur as false positives.
3. Pull the raw headers. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Set a real `<title>` and remove the empty `font-family:''`.
5. Add `alt=""` to the `/q/` tracking pixel.
6. Point the "Receipt scanning" link at a public landing page instead of the login redirect.
7. Capture the UTM parameters from the final URL, which the probe could not reach, to complete the continuity check.
