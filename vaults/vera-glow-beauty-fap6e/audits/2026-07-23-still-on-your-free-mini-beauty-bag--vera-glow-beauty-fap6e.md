- "Shop now" CTAs are doing the bare minimum — compare to how ILIA used "coming thru 07.28" to create a specific, verb-forward deadline beat; "Shop now" tells me nothing about *why* right now
- "Limited time" appears in the hero copy but no date is given — Glossier has been running this offer for at least two days by this point, and vague urgency is starting to feel like a shrug
- I have no idea what's actually inside the Mini Beauty Bag — is it a plain pouch? Lined? What's the approximate size? At the $75+ ask, that gap in product detail bugs me
- Twelve emails in sixteen days is a lot; this cadence is burning through my goodwill fast

- 1. **Add a hard deadline.** Drunk Elephant's "Feeling sticky?" from a few days ago committed harder to summer urgency than this does. Even "Offer ends Sunday" in the hero copy would do real lift. "Limited time" in 2026 means nothing.
- 2. **Tell me what's in the bag.** One line — "fits 6+ full-size products" or "includes zip closure, wipe-clean lining" — answers the hesitation and earns the click.
- 3. **Rewrite the primary CTA.** "Claim your free bag" or "Shop $75+ · Get the bag" beats "Shop now" by a mile. Sally Beauty's recent L'Oreal spotlight ran stronger action language than this.
- 4. **Pull back cadence.** Sephora sends frequently but varies the value prop enough that I don't feel stalked. This is the fourth straight email in the same I ❤️ NY bag-adjacent orbit.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Issue — Semantically invalid tracking span:**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KY8N2VQWA3BJCTCSEYKK7DVN_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This is Klaviyo's open-tracking pixel disguised as a stylesheet call, but `<span>` cannot fetch external resources; the open track fires only if the email client renders the `href` as a load (some do via attribute sniffing, most don't). A proper 1×1 `<img>` tracking pixel is the reliable standard and should be used instead. If Klaviyo is generating this automatically, verify the Klaviyo template setting for open tracking — this pattern is a non-standard variant.

**Cannot confirm:** Full click-link inventory is not visible in truncated source. All CTA links should route through `ctrk.klclick.com` click-tracking URLs.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Preheader padding uses rendering-risky Unicode:**
The preheader span uses two distinct invisible-character techniques interleaved:
- U+034F `͏` (Combining Grapheme Joiner) — broadly safe
- U+00AD `­` (Soft Hyphen) — **risk**: some clients (notably older Android Gmail) render soft hyphens as visible hyphens, producing garbage characters in the preheader

Recommend replacing all U+00AD soft hyphens with U+200C (Zero Width Non-Joiner) or U+FEFF (Zero Width No-Break Space), which have better cross-client invisibility.

**Positive:** `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `format-detection=telephone=no`, and full MSO conditional stack are all present and correctly structured.

**Cannot confirm:** `alt` attributes on images — source is truncated before `<body>` image content.
