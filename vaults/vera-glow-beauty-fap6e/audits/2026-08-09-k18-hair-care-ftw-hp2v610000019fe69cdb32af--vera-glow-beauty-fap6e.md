## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" showing right up top under my Beauty Insider bar tells me they haven't refreshed my status since my last order — mildly annoying but not a dealbreaker. But this is a wall of K18 SKUs stacked on top of each other (FutureIQ serum, then the Molecular Repair Mask again even though I already got a free trial-size of it at the top) with zero mention of my actual tier math until I'm four screens deep.
- The free trial-size mask with code K18SERUM at $40+ is the one thing I'd actually act on — free is free, and I'm a Rouge member who spends there anyway. The 4x points on Clarins and the Mystery Reward bracelet redemption are both things I'd screenshot for myself, but they're crammed into one tiny "Spot it. - Would I click? Yes, for the free trial-size mask, since it's a real $0 add and I'm Rouge. Would I forward it? No — this is a single-brand vendor push dressed as a Sephora send, not something my friends need to see. Compare to Sephora's own 8/6 "Discover the art of body care" email, which I scored an 8 for that same free-trial-with-merch-order hook up top — this one buries the equivalent offer under a giant hero product wall instead of leading with it.

## What's working

- The free K18 trial-size mask with code K18SERUM at $40+ is a concrete, honest offer right at the top — no fine print I had to hunt for.
- Before/after hair photos (gray regrowth counts, platinum damage repair) are real clinical-feeling proof, not just product-on-white shots.
- "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badges are a trust cue I actually look for when I'm buying prestige haircare.
- 10% off subscribe-and-save on the serum is loyalty-adjacent pricing that speaks my language.

## What's weak

- My points balance literally reads "0 points" — as a Rouge member that reads as broken personalization, not "you have zero," and it's the first thing I see.
- 4x points on Clarins and the Mystery Reward bracelet — the actual tier-program content I care about most — are squeezed into a tiny two-tile block near the very bottom instead of leading.
- This is basically three separate K18 hero pushes stacked back to back (trial mask, FutureIQ serum, then the molecular repair mask again) — repetitive for one email.
- "Beauty Must-Haves: NARS, LYS Beauty, and REFY" at the end feels like a bolted-on afterthought with zero context, unlike ILIA's recent "Thru Line Eye Essentials" send which at least built a real bundle story around its cross-sell.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or hide the "0 points" display — showing a wrong or stale balance to a Rouge member undermines the whole personalization pitch before I've even scrolled.
- 2. Move the 4x Clarins points and Mystery Reward bracelet block up near the top, above the second K18 product — that's the loyalty math I open Sephora emails for.
- 3. Cut one of the three K18 hero moments — trial mask + serum + repair mask in one send is Skechers-level repetition ("Don't leave a good thing behind," 4/10) for a brand I actually like.
- 4. Give the "Beauty Must-Haves" NARS/LYS/REFY block an actual reason to exist (new-in? trending with K18 shoppers?) instead of a random tack-on.
