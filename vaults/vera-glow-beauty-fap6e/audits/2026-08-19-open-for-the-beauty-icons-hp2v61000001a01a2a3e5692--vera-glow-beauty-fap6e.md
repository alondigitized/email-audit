- This is the second Chanel-hero Sephora email in one day for me — Sephora's now repeating creative within the same 24 hours, which reads as cadence fatigue, not a fresh campaign.
- The eight-product grid has no thread — shampoo next to hand sanitizer next to blush isn't a curated "icons" story, it undercuts the "Beauty's Most Wanted" premise entirely.
- My Rouge points balance isn't shown anywhere near the top — I have to scroll to the bottom bar to even see the tier structure, and there's no personalized point count like Ulta at least attempts with my name in subject lines ("Marisol, ENDS TODAY!").
- App Fest and the Beauty Insider Celebration are crammed in as afterthought tiles at the bottom, when App Fest deserved its own send (which it already got, better, on 8/17) — this feels like a leftover-inventory email, not a designed one.
- No skin-tone or shade personalization anywhere, despite Sephora having my Color IQ on file — this could be any Rouge member's inbox, not mine specifically.
