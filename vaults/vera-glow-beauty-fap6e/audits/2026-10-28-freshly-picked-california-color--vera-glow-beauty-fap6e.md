# Freshly picked California color
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a pretty email and a mostly useful one. After the 10/16, 10/17 and 10/19 VIP-gift pile-on, ILIA is back on product. A limited-edition baked face palette with three shade collections is a launch I'd actually look at. The pink-and-red tie-dye hero and the palette render are strong, and the model's blush shot made me want the Ethereal Baked Face Palette.
- The offer is the weak part. A free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $75+ is a tiny gift on a high threshold, and it's tucked below the fold. The top strip says "FREE MINI LIMITLESS LASH MASCARA W/ $75+*" and the mascara section repeats it. A palette likely costs well under $75, so I'd have to add another item to get the gift. That's the points math I'd do, and it doesn't come out in ILIA's favor. There's also no price on the palette anywhere, and no Rouge-style tier perk for members.
- Would I click? Probably yes on "Pick your palette," mostly out of curiosity about the shades. I wouldn't forward it. Sephora would put the price and a "Add to Bag" shortcut right in the hero. This is a nice look-book, not a buying email.

## What's working

- The hero is gorgeous. "California Color" in red serif over the tie-dye art, with the palette beside it, lands the limited-edition story fast.
- The annotated model shot (Highlighter, Bronzer, Blush, Blush) shows how the palette is used, which beats a flat product photo.
- The three named collections (Botanical Blur, Poppy Fields, Coastal Bloom) side by side let me pick a shade story at a glance.
- The spec list is quick to scan: 8-hour wear, weightless blurring powder, smoothing, buildable.
- The "Reuse or Recycle" block is a nice touch for a clean-beauty shopper, and the two how-to photos make it feel real.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere for the palette, and the only offer is a $75+ gift with a threshold I'd have to calculate.
- The primary CTA is "Pick your palette," which is vague, and "Shop the palettes" and "Shop now" repeat the same destination. Three buttons, one job.
- The "Mini Mascara Gift" badge on the mascara image is small and tilted, and the offer is buried under the recycling section.
- There's no shade-match help in the body, such as skin tone or undertone guidance, even though the footer mentions "Personalized Shade Match." For a baked blush palette I want to know which collection suits my skin.
- The recycling copy and fine print are small. The "*Curbside recycling programs vary" text and the footer links were hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the price and a clear offer in the hero. For example: "$XX palette. Free mini mascara at $75+." Then I'd know if it's worth a cart add-on.
- 2. Move the gift offer right under the first CTA instead of after the recycling block. The offer is the reason to click today.
- 3. Make the three shade collections clickable with individual product links and a "best for" line, like "Best for fair-to-light, cool undertones." That gives me the shade guidance I'm missing.
- 4. Use one CTA label across the email with a specific verb and noun, like "Shop Ethereal Palettes."
- 5. Add a VIP line near the gift, like "VIP: earn 2x points on palettes," so members get a visible perk.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Botanical Blur, Poppy Fields, or Coastal Bloom: pick your shade story.`
