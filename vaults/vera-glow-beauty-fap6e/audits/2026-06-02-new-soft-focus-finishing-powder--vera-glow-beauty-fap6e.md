# NEW Soft Focus Finishing Powder

## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the email I've been waiting for since May 29 when ILIA sent that beautifully-timed teaser — "Coming 06.02—our softest launch yet" — which I genuinely appreciated after surviving the F+F sale gauntlet. And now it's June 2, launch day, and... the payoff is a product education scroll. No price. No "Shop Now" button I can click above the fold. No "available now at Sephora" line for those of us who are going to be doing the Rouge point calculation in our heads before we even close the email.
- The photography is stunning, the formulation story is right for ILIA's lane, and the model representation genuinely reflects someone who looks like she uses this product — that matters to me. The three-shade callout (light, medium, deep) is appreciated but feels thin. I have literally more shades of blush in my drawer than this powder has options.
- I'd mark this as read, open a new tab to Sephora, and search for it there — because this email built no bridge between "here's the product" and "here's how you buy it right now." Compare that to Charlotte Tilbury's "Did You Miss This Magic?" which told me what was in the kit, what it cost, and had a visible button — this feels like ILIA handed me a brochure when I asked for a receipt.

## What's working

- **The teaser payoff lands on the right date.** Arriving exactly on June 2 as promised from the May 29 tease earns real trust — most brands miss their own announced dates.
- **Hero visual and model are A-tier.** The close-up skin photography is gorgeous and the model read is genuinely aspirational without feeling unattainable — exactly the ILIA aesthetic I subscribe for.
- **Formulation callout section is well-organized.** "Featherweight, Blurring, Hydrating, 12-hour shine control" is a clean benefit stack; the checkmark badges (no talc, no fragrance, vegan, cruelty-free) work as fast trust signals.
- **"Reusable Mirror + Recyclable Compact" is the kind of sustainability detail that makes me feel better about spending.** Drunk Elephant buries this stuff in the footer — ILIA made it a visual block.
- **Soft Focus Complex ingredient callout adds credibility.** Naming the complex rather than just listing benefits reads like a real skincare brand, not a makeup brand playing dress-up.

## What's weak

- **No price and no visible CTA button.** This is the biggest miss. I'm in launch mode, I have my Sephora app open, and you gave me a lookbook. Sally Beauty managed to attach a BOGO mechanic to National Nail Week — ILIA can't put a "Shop Now — $XX" button on a product launch email?
- **Three shades called "light, medium, and deep" is not a shade range story in 2026.** Three translucent options feels like a 2015 rollout. Even a quick "which shade is yours?" CTA would have softened this.
- **The VIP section at the bottom is illegible at this render size.** I can see the badge but I can't read what I'm getting. If there's a member early-access moment here, it's completely buried — which defeats the purpose of having a loyalty tier.
- **"Hi there" energy all over again.** After 21 emails this month, I'm still not being greeted by name. e.l.f. called me "Bestie" (annoying in its own way), but at least it tried.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price + "Shop Now" button immediately after the hero.** One line: "Soft Focus Finishing Powder — $XX — Shop at Sephora →". That's the whole ask. Do it before the ingredient breakdown.
- 2. **Move the VIP benefit to above the fold.** If Rouge/Diamond members get early access, early shipping, or a GWP, that should be the *second* thing I read, not the last.
- 3. **Give the three shades names, not just descriptors.** "Light, Medium, Deep" reads like a placeholder. Even something minimal like ILIA's own naming system makes this feel like a finished product rather than a mockup.
- 4. **Add one real-person review or skin-type callout.** "Works on sensitive, oily skin" or a single quote from a tester. Glossier does this in even their laziest emails; it converts.
- 5. **Trim the ingredient/benefit copy.** The Soft Focus Complex section plus the checkmark badges plus the formulation claims is three separate trust-building moments that slightly undercut each other. Pick two.
