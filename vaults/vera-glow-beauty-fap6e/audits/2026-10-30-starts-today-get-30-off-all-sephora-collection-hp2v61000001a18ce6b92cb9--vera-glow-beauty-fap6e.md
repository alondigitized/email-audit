# STARTS TODAY: Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😊
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Collection event, and it earns the open more than the last week of sends did. Compared with the "THIS JUST IN" send on 10/28 and the "Top-shelf beauty" send on 10/29, this one has a real offer and a real date. 30% off all Sephora Collection runs 10/30 to 11/9, and the "everything else starts 11/3" line tells me when the bigger event hits. As someone who stocks up during Beauty Insider events, that 11/3 teaser is what I'd screenshot.
- The body is the problem. It's a very long scroll with a lot stacked in it: the 500-point challenge, the credit card pitch, the delivery options strip, a product grid, gift sets, a Kohl's store locator and a barcode. The hero does its job and then the email drifts into everything-Sephora. There's also no Rouge-specific math, so no "your 30% on 11/3" and no points total, even though I've been getting "You have 0 points" strips all week.
- I'd click, mostly to check which Sephora Collection pieces are worth the discount. I wouldn't forward it. It's a sale email, not a find.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer clearly: a big "30% OFF" medallion with the 10/30–11/9 dates right on it.
- "STARTS TODAY" is real urgency with a visible end date, which is more credible than a vague "last call."
- The "everything else starts 11/3" line sets up the next beat and gives me a reason to come back.
- The free shipping and returns strip with no minimum removes a common objection.
- The Kohl's barcode and the online and in-store codes are practical for anyone who shops both ways.

## What's weak

- The codes (ALLONSALE, SCSAVE) are small text under the hero. I want that offer to be one tap, with the code auto-applied.
- The 500-point challenge and the credit card block compete with the sale. Sephora is stacking three asks in one email.
- Nothing is tier-aware. As a Rouge I'd expect to see my own number, not generic "Insider" language.
- The product grid is thin and the images are small on mobile. A "Sephora Collection" tag on every tile doesn't tell me which lip, liner or palette is the hero.
- The legal text and the Kohl's store block make the scroll very long. By the barcode I've lost the thread.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job: the hero, 3 to 4 best-selling Sephora Collection items with prices, then a single "Shop now" CTA. Move the points challenge and credit card pitch to a separate send.
- 2. Personalize by tier. Show "Rouge: your 30% on everything starts 11/3" and my current points.
- 3. Auto-apply the code through the link so I don't have to copy ALLONSALE.
- 4. Put social proof on the products, such as star ratings or "best seller" tags.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off all Sephora Collection, today through 11/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sephora Collection is 30% off (and 11/3 is coming)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping, no minimum. Rouge savings on everything start 11/3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Lip, liner, palettes and advent calendars — the full line, 11 days only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional layout with two open-tracking pixels. The one hard failure is the missing plain-text part (0 chars). The header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay capture gaps, not confirmed defects. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported: 12 material links were probed and 36 were skipped by ranking. The skipped links are unverified, not passing.
- The truncated source shows two tracking images:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261030_SC_30_off_Starts_Today_BI`. This is a 1x1 pixel with a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign code (`mi_ecmp`).
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`. This is a second open pixel from a different tracking domain, hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`.
- Two open pixels on different subdomains is redundant, but it is not a defect.
- The QA "Image missing alt text" warnings are false positives for deliverability or accessibility purposes:
  - `o.gif` already has `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` attribute at all. It is `display:none`, so there is no practical impact. Adding `alt=""` would clear the warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom restriction.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The value is invalid as written and has no effect in mail clients.
- **Malformed CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` conditional is nested inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px) { … }` block in `<style>`. It is not valid CSS and may cause the following rules to be dropped by some parsers.
- **Fixed width.** The wrapper is `width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. This forces horizontal overflow on narrow viewports unless the inner `max-width:700px` tables and the 480px media query fully override it. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor, since it matters mainly for web-view rendering.
- **Structured data.** The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip `<script>`, and Gmail only honors specific schemas, so this is dead markup with no effect.
- **Preheader.** The `Save big now through 11/9.` text is followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding run, which is correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` value is populated in the pixel URL. The truncated body could not be checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] No plain-text part.** The text version is 0 chars, so this is a multipart/alternative gap. It hurts deliverability and accessibility, since text-only and screen-reader-preferred clients get nothing. This is the only confirmed failure.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** The QA tool itself notes AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Sephora is a large sender, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, so it is likely present at the origin. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results absent.** This is also an expected relay artifact, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, not failed. The envelope domain `beauty.sephora.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign, but alignment is unverified.
