# One, two, free
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- "New customers only" — right there in the fine print — and I'm out before I even finish reading the hero. I'm Rouge and Diamond, I've probably already got a Sephora order history with ILIA in it, so a $65 welcome trio locked to first-time buyers is just not for me. It's a gorgeous shot, I'll give them that — close-up face, lip pencil drawn right on the mouth, "One, Two, Free" is a cute headline — but the second I hit "New customers only" my brain moves on.
- Compare this to Drunk Elephant's 8/11 "Introducing Kamo Drops" — that was an actual new-product launch anyone could buy into. This is ILIA gating a gift behind an acquisition funnel and dressing it up as a launch. It's also the same "GWP as headline" trick Glossier tried with their 20%-off blush send, except at least Glossier's discount applied to existing customers too.
- I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding. Filed as "not me," archived.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely striking — clean skin, no filter overload, the lip-pencil-on-mouth visual ties directly to product #1 in the kit.
- "$65 VALUE" badge on the hero gives me a real number before I even scroll — that's honest anchoring.
- The numbered gift breakdown (1, 2, 3 with product swatches) is clear and easy to scan on mobile.
- Loyalty/values footer icons (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact) are consistent ILIA brand equity — feels earned, not bolted on.

## What's weak

- "New customers only" kills it instantly for a Rouge/Diamond member like me — there's zero acknowledgment I might already be a customer.
- No ILIA VIP tie-in or loyalty point math anywhere despite "Join ILIA VIP" sitting right there in the footer icon row — massive missed cross-sell for someone whose whole personality is tier math.
- Two CTAs stacked ("Build My First Order" then "Claim Your Free Trio") saying almost the same thing is redundant and a little confusing on first scroll.
- "*While supplies last... See site for details" is the second layer of fine print after the new-customer gate — feels like a two-step bait-and-switch by the time I hit it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this send — existing customers should get a completely different email (loyalty points, reorder nudge, or a $50+ gift-with-purchase that isn't acquisition-gated). Sending me a new-customer-only offer wastes the send entirely.
- 2. Move the "new customers only" disclosure up near the hero, not buried under the CTA — if I'm going to bounce, let me bounce in one second, not after reading three sections.
- 3. Cut one of the two CTAs — "Build My First Order" and "Claim Your Free Trio" are doing the same job.
- 4. Add a VIP points callout for members, since the footer already teases "Join ILIA VIP" — connect that thread.
