## What's weak

- Not a single price appears on 12 products. I can't tell what a gift set costs or how it gets me to $75.
- The free mini eye stylus is the only real offer, and it's at the bottom after a very long scroll.
- The hero says "gift ideas loading…" but has no CTA beyond a modest red "Shop Gifts" button below the fold.
- No deadline, shipping cutoff or holiday-order date. Sephora's Savings Event email ("ENDS TODAY") at least had real urgency.
- Every tile has the same "shop now" label, so none of them says what I'd actually be buying.

- 3. Add a real deadline, like a ship-by date for holiday orders. Sally Beauty's "Finish your gift list today" had the same problem: urgency with no reason behind it.
- 4. Cut the 12 tiles to 6 and use specific CTAs, like "Build my trio."

The "57% pass rate" mostly reflects skipped and unprobed checks, not defects.
