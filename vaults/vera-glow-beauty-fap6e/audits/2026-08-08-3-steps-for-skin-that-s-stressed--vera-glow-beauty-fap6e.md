## What's weak

- The BOGO "buy 2 skin e.l.f.ssentials, get 1 on us" with code REFRESH is the exact same mechanic I already got on 8/6 — no new deal, just recycled into a different wrapper.
- The e.l.f. x Bubble "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" strip is also a repeat plug from 8/7's email, so now I've got three campaigns fighting for one send.
