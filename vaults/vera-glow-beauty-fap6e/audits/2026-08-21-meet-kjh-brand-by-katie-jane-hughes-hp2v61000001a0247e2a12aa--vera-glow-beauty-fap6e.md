- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" framing under the hero gives clear exclusivity signal, which matters to me as Rouge — I know this isn't sitting on an Ulta shelf too.
- The diverse model casting on Lotta Lip and Bat Those Lashes actually looks like people I'd see wearing this, not just one demographic.

## What's weak

- I'm seeing this exact "Play Makes Perfect" launch creative for the second time today — zero new info, so it reads like a resend, not a fresh beat.
- The "Get a Mini" banner up top has no visible offer mechanics — no minimum spend, no code, just "*Exclusions/terms apply" fine print energy before I even get to the launch.
- No Rouge points multiplier or Beauty Insider tier callout anywhere near the KJH products themselves — the loyalty math I always do is completely absent until the footer's generic Insider banner.
- Tacking Bombs Points and Best of Hair promos onto the bottom of a brand launch email dilutes the "this is a real moment" feeling — Sephora x Olive Young kept its focus tight and scored better with me because of it.

- 2. Put a real, specific "Get a Mini" offer at the top — dollar threshold and product name, not vague banner copy — or drop it entirely so it doesn't steal hero real estate from KJH.
- 3. Add a Rouge/Insider points callout directly on the KJH launch (e.g., "Earn 2X points on KJH.brand through [date]") — that's the kind of tier-specific hook that actually gets me to click over just browsing.
