# Up to 25% off just for you ends tomorrow...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- Day fourteen. Or fifteen. I genuinely stopped counting after Glossier sent "ends tonight!" and "ends tomorrow" in the same morning on June 19, then resurrected the whole thing on June 24 as a "for your eyes only" exclusive. And here we are on June 28 with — surprise — "ends tomorrow" again.
- Here's the thing though: the email itself is actually cleaner than most of what's hit my inbox this week. The layout is textbook Glossier — hero shot, tidy best-sellers grid, clear offer math. And the personal code (SAVE80YO) is a genuine hook. If I hadn't watched this sale zombie its way through half of June, that code would have me clicking fast.
- But I have watched it. At this point "ends tomorrow" is background noise, like Sally Beauty's "Up to 40% off" landing in my inbox for twenty-five consecutive days. I'm marking this read. If the same email lands June 30 with a fresh "ends tomorrow," I'm unsubscribing — and I say that as someone who has recommended Boy Brow to literally everyone I know.

## What's working

- The personal code SAVE80YO is front and center in the hero copy — that's the one thing in this email that feels actually differentiated from the prior blast
- Offer math is crystal clear at the top: 20% sitewide or 25% off $100+, no hunting required
- Best-sellers grid hits the right products — Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint — these are genuine Glossier icons that move, not filler
- Layout is render-clean and mobile-legible with zero broken images or text overlap
- "ENDING TOMORROW" placement above the fold is decisive — at least they committed to the hierarchy

## What's weak

- The credibility of "ends tomorrow" is fully exhausted — I've seen this claim fail four times in two weeks from this exact sender
- "Shop now" is the laziest CTA possible; compare to e.l.f.'s product-specific copy or even Sephora's "Shop Summer Deals" — there's zero pull here
- No social proof, no community signal, no UGC — Glossier literally built its brand on that and this email reads like a Charlotte Tilbury blast instead
- The hero lifestyle image is art-directed but doesn't show the product clearly enough to trigger desire — I can barely tell what's in the shot
- No Glossier loyalty or member angle anywhere; compare to Sephora who makes Rouge feel seen even in their most generic sale sends

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the unused code as the actual hook** — if SAVE80YO is genuinely personalized and unused, "your code expires at midnight" beats "ends tomorrow" every single time; that's a different email
- 2. **Retire "ends tomorrow" until it means something again** — the credibility math is upside down at this point; the urgency is actively working against conversion for anyone paying attention
- 3. **Swap "Shop now" for something code-specific** — "Redeem 25% off" or "Use SAVE80YO before midnight" does real work
- 4. **Hero one product with emotional context** — Cloud Paint with a shade story or a Balm Dotcom scent-preference call-out would do more than four tiles of "Shop now"
- 5. **Add one social signal** — even a single "fan favorite" or community pick label would reconnect this to the brand identity they've been eroding across this sale run
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your code SAVE80YO expires tonight — 25% off Glossier`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You haven't used your Glossier code yet`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `20% off everything, 25% off $100+ — one code, one real deadline.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boy Brow, Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint — all at your lowest price.`

## Technical Audit — Glossier "Up to 25% off" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Email is Klaviyo-sent (ESP: `eml.glossier.com`, click tracker: `ctrk.klclick.com`) and built with Stripo-generated markup. One confirmed invalid HTML construct in the open-tracking implementation; remaining sections are truncated and several compliance items cannot be fully verified.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[CONFIRMED] Invalid open-tracking element**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KW8413GX68SRXPS7YAA0J3Q8_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. The standard implementation is a 1×1 `<img>` pixel. Some clients (notably Apple Mail with MPP, and strict HTML parsers) may silently drop this element, causing open-tracking undercounting. Klaviyo's standard open pixel should be `<img width="1" height="1" src="..." alt="">`.
