# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is back with the exact same Skechers Garage kids' toy sneaker — die-cast truck bolted to the side and all — that's now shown up in four sends I've logged: 7/28 "This is perfect for you!", 8/11 "This style has limited availability!", 8/16 "Don't leave a good thing behind," and 8/18/8/19's near-identical toy-sneaker duo. This is literally the same product photo recycled with a new urgency wrapper, this time "SELLING FAST... ADD TO CART BEFORE THEY'RE GONE!" I'm a 29-year-old Rouge/Diamond beauty shopper — I have never once clicked on a boys' light-up sneaker, and Skechers' retargeting logic seems completely broken.
- The one genuinely useful thing buried below the fold is the 20% off Skechers Plus sitewide banner — that's a real loyalty mechanic and if I actually shopped Skechers footwear it'd be worth a look. But it's stapled onto six women's shoe thumbnails that only exist to paper over the fact the hero product is wrong for me, then a sitewide code that undercuts the "selling fast" urgency on the one item up top.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — there's nothing beauty-adjacent here at all, this isn't even the right category for my inbox. Marking as read and mentally docking Skechers again for the same broken-record retargeting.

## What's working

- The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is a legible, honest loyalty offer with a real code (SKXPLUS) and a visible deadline (8/25).
- Layout is clean and scannable — no overlapping text, no broken images, everything renders fine.
- The "here's more options" grid at least tries to pivot to women's styles after the hero miss.

## What's weak

- The hero product is, again, a boys' Skechers Garage toy sneaker with a plastic truck — completely wrong demographic, wrong category for me, and the fourth time I've seen this exact photo.
- Zero personalization signal — no name, no size, no "picked for you," nothing that suggests Skechers knows anything about who's opening this.
- "SELLING FAST" urgency undercuts itself once you scroll to a sitewide 20% off code valid through 8/25 — if it's really selling fast, why is everything also on sale?
- Not remotely my category. Sephora's App Fest emails at least stay in-lane with day-by-day beauty calendars; this is shoes and toys landing in a beauty shopper's inbox with no relevance filter at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the retargeting engine — stop showing kids' toy sneakers to accounts that have never browsed kids' or toys. This is the single biggest lever; four sends running with the wrong hero product is a targeting failure, not a creative one.
- 2. Lead with the 20% off Skechers Plus sitewide offer instead of burying it — that's the actual news here, not a random toy shoe.
- 3. Drop or soften "selling fast" language when a sitewide discount code runs in the same email — the two messages contradict each other.
- 4. Add basic personalization (name, last-viewed category) so the email reads as targeted rather than a blast.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off code is almost gone`
