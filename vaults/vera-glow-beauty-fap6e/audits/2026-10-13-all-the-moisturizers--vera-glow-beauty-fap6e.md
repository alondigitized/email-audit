- There isn't a single price, size or $/oz figure on the page. I can't do my points math, and Sephora would at least show a price next to every jar.
- The only offer is the pouch GWP, at the very bottom. The "Shop Moisturizers" button sits in the hero above it, before any offer shows up.
- The fine print is tiny gray text and hard to read on my phone. The qualifying-product list is long, the Kamo purchase requirement is easy to miss, and the dates (10/10 to 10/17) only show up there.
- It's the same Kamo-plus-gift story as 10/11, plus the two kit emails before it (10/7, 10/9). That's four sends in a week on overlapping creative.
- No reviews, no ratings and no "best seller" cue. No Rewards or member perk either, even though I'm Rouge and Diamond elsewhere.
- Both "Shop Moisturizers" buttons are gray and plain, and they go to a category rather than a specific product.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style image hosting and click-wrapped links, and renders from a table layout with VML/Office namespaces. The confirmed problems are a plaintext recipient email address in a tracking URL, three `http://` images, invalid CSS patterns, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient email in a URL query string.** The Everest pixel/link is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The `e=` parameter carries the address unhashed, so it can end up in third-party logs, proxies and referrer data.
- **Unprobed links.** 15 tracking links were skipped by the QA check because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect behavior are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels.** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_chooseyourmoist` is a Movable Ink-style pixel with a user ID (`mi_u`). It is informational, but it adds a second tracking domain to the Everest one.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on two tracking images.** These are `o.gif` (mi.drunkelephant.com) and the Everest `/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image. Both are likely 1×1 pixels, so the fix is `alt=""` rather than descriptive text. Without it, some screen readers read out the filename or URL.
- **Insecure image sources.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that block mixed content may drop them. The sender's own domain very likely serves HTTPS.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule inside the `<style>` block. Per CSS spec they are ignored there. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` do the same job, so the imports are redundant either way.
- **HTML comments inside `@media`.** The block contains `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->`. Inside a CSS block, `<!--` and `-->` are not ignored. They become part of the next selector, which can invalidate the adjacent `*[class=mobile1]` rule. I haven't tested this in a client, so treat the effect as likely rather than confirmed.
- **Fragile font hosting.** Fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` (third party) and from a versioned Demandware path, `…/v1719565389232/fonts/BrownRegular.*`. A site deploy that changes the version path would break the font. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, so check that the fallback stack is defined.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. The only personalization evidence is the resolved recipient address in the Everest URL, and no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` tokens appear in the truncated source. The unseen remainder of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post.** The QA check did not find either header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers really are absent, the email lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown because the `Authentication-Results` header was not captured. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, and alignment can't be checked from this data.
- **Footer.** The source is truncated, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters unverifiable.** Links go through tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be inspected. Campaign identifiers are visible in the pixels (`us_de_broadcast_promotion_chooseyourmoist`, `c=us_de_broad…`), and the naming is consistent across vendors.
- **Landing-page alignment unchecked.** The 15 redirect links were not followed, so the match between the "All the moisturizers" subject and the landing pages is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the plaintext address in the Everest `e=` parameter with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
2. Change the three social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://` on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Delete the post-rule `@import` statements, or move them to the top of the `<style>` block. Remove the `<!-- … -->` wrappers inside `@media`.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the raw message at the origin, not through the relay.
7. Capture `Authentication-Results`, or check SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `cheers.drunkelephant.com` directly.
8. Resolve the 15 redirect chains with a followed-redirect probe to validate final URLs and UTM parameters.
9. Host fonts on a first-party domain, and make sure the fallback stack doesn't depend on `onlinewebfonts.com`.
