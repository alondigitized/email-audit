# The Appiversary celebration starts now 🎉
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty's not even trying to hide that this is a corporate-hair-color aisle, not a Sephora/Ulta swap — but I'm reviewing it anyway because it landed in my inbox, and honestly, it's a nice break from the COLORfest confetti-dot spam I've been getting from them daily since mid-July. "Appiversary" is a genuinely new concept I haven't seen from Sally before, and 15% off BOPIS with a Klarna callout is at least a coherent offer instead of another sitewide-%-off retread.
- That said, this brand just isn't built for me — Sally is a professional hair-color/salon-supplies retailer, and I'm a Sephora Rouge / Ulta Diamond prestige-skincare-and-makeup shopper. Toners, boosters, and tint brushes with brand names like "ion" and "Wella" mean nothing to my routine, no matter how clean the layout is.
- I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to a friend — this is a mark-as-read-and-move-on email, though I'll give it credit for at least varying the creative from the confetti-dot wall I called out on 8/27 and 8/31.

## What's working

- The "Appiversary" hook is a genuinely new campaign beat — first time I've seen this angle from Sally, unlike the tenth COLORfest rerun I flagged on 8/31.
- 15% off BOPIS with code APPBOPIS and a hard end date (Sept 3) is a real, specific, time-bound offer — no buried fine print up top.
- Product grid is clean with visible price-free "Shop now" links under each SKU, so browsing feels frictionless even if the products aren't for me.
- Klarna 4-interest-free-payments strip is a nice trust/flexibility cue for a bigger basket.

## What's weak

- Zero demographic or category match for me — this is professional hair color and salon tools, not the prestige skincare/makeup mix I actually buy.
- No mention of any loyalty tier math (Sally Rewards is pushed generically — "Not a member? Join now" — with no points-per-dollar specifics), which is exactly the kind of thing that gets my attention and it's missing.
- The hero model photo (phone selfie holding Sally products) feels dated and low-budget next to how ILIA or Sephora shoot their launch heroes — this reads more like a 2019 retail email.
- "2x Sally Rewards on in-app purchases in September" is buried in a small text block below the fold instead of being the headline — that's a real miss if app engagement is the actual KPI here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the loyalty math, not the app download — if Sephora can get me to open with "Free Marc Jacobs Beauty Gift" GWP math (scored 7/10 in my inbox this week), Sally should lead with "2x points this week" as the hero banner, not bury it two-thirds down.
- 2. Cut the generic "Your Beauty, Your Way" product grid — professional stylist SKUs (toners, boosters, tint brushes) read as random when there's no context for who needs them.
- 3. Refresh the hero image — the phone-selfie mockup is stale next to what ILIA and Glossier are shooting right now.
- 4. Tighten the offer stack — leading with both a 15% BOPIS deal AND 2x app points AND free 2-hr delivery in one email dilutes all three; pick one hero offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Appiversary Week 1: 15% off pickup, ends Tuesday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2x Rewards points all September (app only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals drop every week through September — first one's 15% off BOPIS`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code APPBOPIS: 15% off buy-online-pickup-in-store, ends 9/3`
