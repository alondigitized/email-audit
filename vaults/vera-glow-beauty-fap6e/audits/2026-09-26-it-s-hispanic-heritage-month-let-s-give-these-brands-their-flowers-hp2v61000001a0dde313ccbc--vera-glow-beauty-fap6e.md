# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Unidos, Somos Más" up top actually stopped my scroll — Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez, Touchland, Ceremonia, AORA Mexico, all with founder quotes and "Shop & Support" framing. That's a real content investment, not a banner slapped on a template, and it lands differently than the generic new-launch dumps I've been getting from Sephora all week (9/25's "New beauty to obsess over," twice). But by the time I hit the "Founders Face to Face" video CTA I'd already scrolled past six brand blocks, and then it just... reverts to the standard promo furniture — 4x points on K-beauty, 20% off hair care, credit card pitch. The heritage content and the sales content don't feel like the same email.
- Would I click? Maybe the Rare Beauty block, because I already own two of their blushes and trust the brand. Would I forward it? Actually yes — to my friend who's Mexican-American and always calls out brands for doing performative heritage-month stuff. This one has actual founder names and quotes, which is more than I can say for e.l.f.'s "Mirror Mix" music tie-in gimmick from last week. But I'd mark it read, not act on it — nothing here is time-bound or discounted, so there's no urgency pulling me to buy today.
- Compared to Ulta's evergreen "20% off one item" barcode hero that's been recycled since July 26th, this is a bigger creative swing. But it's also longer than it needs to be — ten+ brand blocks before the fold-worthy stuff (points multiplier, hair care %) shows up.

## What's working

- The founder quotes (Rodrigo Peñafiel, Andrea Liduvina, Bakuchiol founder, Selena Gomez) give this actual substance over a generic "we love diversity" banner.
- Rare Beauty block is genuinely relevant to me — I'm Rouge tier and have bought from that line before.
- "Shop & Support" framing on every block is a clean, repeatable CTA that ties commerce to the theme without feeling forced.
- The closing "4x points on K-Beauty" and "20% off Value-Size Hair Care" are concrete offers I actually track for my points math.

## What's weak

- No discount or GWP tied to the heritage spotlight itself — every other Sephora send this month (Hair Daily Deals 9/23) leads with a hard number; this one makes me scroll ten screens before any % appears.
- Ten-plus brand blocks in a row with identical layout (photo, quote, two products) gets repetitive fast — my thumb was on autopilot by AORA Mexico's second product.
- The "Founders Face to Face" video CTA is buried mid-scroll where fatigue has already set in, not up top where it'd actually get watched.
- Zero segmentation signal — nothing here suggests Sephora used my Rouge status or my Rare Beauty purchase history to prioritize what I see first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject/hero with the concrete offer (4x points or 20% off hair) and use the heritage spotlight as the emotional mid-body hook, not the whole top half — right now the reader has to earn their way to the discount.
- 2. Cut the brand blocks from six to three — pick the ones most relevant to the recipient (for me: Rare Beauty, Touchland, Ceremonia) instead of a uniform wall.
- 3. Move "Founders Face to Face" video CTA up near the top, right after the "Unidos, Somos Más" intro, while attention is still fresh.
- 4. Add a small "as a Rouge member" or points-multiplier callout inside at least one brand block to tie loyalty status to the cultural content, not just the footer promo.
