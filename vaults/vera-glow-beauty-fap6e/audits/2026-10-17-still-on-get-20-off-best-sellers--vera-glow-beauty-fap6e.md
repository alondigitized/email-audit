# STILL ON! Get 20% off best-sellers
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the same 20% sale as Thursday's "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐", resent with "STILL ON!" in front. It's the second Glossier 20% email in three days. The offer is clear, and the banner has a free Futuredew Solid at $75+. Glossier has finally given me numbers after weeks of pretty pictures, so I'm not mad about it. I just wish it were a new send and not a reminder.
- I like the review-driven layout. Real quotes with five stars (Alejandra on You Doux, Charmaine on Boy Brow, Ada on Cloud Paint) make it feel less like a catalog dump. But there's no sale end date anywhere, no Glossier Rewards mention, and no prices. I can't do points math on a "20% off" with no deadline. I'd click Shop fall blush and maybe grab Cloud Paint, but I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Take 20% off all best-sellers" headline and blue Shop now button sit right at the top. I got the offer in two seconds.
- The star-rated customer quotes are social proof I actually trust, and they beat generic product blurbs.
- The "Forever Favorites" row has blue "20% OFF" tags on each product (Balm Dotcom, Glossier You, Lash Slick), so the discount is visible exactly where I'd pick something.
- The free Futuredew Solid at $75+ shows up in the top banner and again near the bottom, which gives me a basket-building target.
- Each block links to a specific product (fragrance, brows, blush), not just the homepage.

## What's weak

- "STILL ON!" with no end date gives me zero urgency. Ulta at least says "Ends tonight," and I can plan around that.
- There's no Rewards or member-tier mention anywhere. As a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, I want to know if the 20% stacks with anything.
- There are no prices on any product. "20% off" of what? Sephora puts the price on every block, and so did e.l.f. on its $11 setting sprays.
- The top-right hero image is cropped at the edge, so the "20% OFF SURPRISE SALE" ticker text and the banner text get cut off. It looks slightly broken.
- The "Terms apply" fine print is tiny and sits at the very bottom, after the free-gift offer. That's buried fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, like "Ends Sunday," and put it in the subject.
- 2. Put prices on the Forever Favorites row, with the sale price next to the original.
- 3. Add a Rewards line, like "Rewards members: points on top." Even a "no stacking" note would help.
- 4. Fix the hero crop so the ticker text and banner copy aren't cut off.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built email (`eml.glossier.com`) is structurally sound: it has a doctype, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, a `<title>`, MSO conditionals and a 600px breakpoint. The confirmed defects are 11 images without `alt` attributes and a malformed tracking `<span>`. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probing:** 12 material links were probed and none were reported as failing (0 issues). 23 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- **Click tracking:** Links are wrapped by Klaviyo (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/...`). Final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Malformed tracking element:** The body opens with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M53J0YD1APZETG3K2RDKTP7C_0"></span>`.
  - `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`, so it does nothing functional.
  - It sits right before the `<!--<![endif]-->` close of the non-MSO conditional. That placement is likely a Stripo/Klaviyo template artifact.
  - Low severity, but it is invalid markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 10 content images:** All are hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_9687a5.../`: `frame_3986.jpg`, `frame_3992.jpg`, `reviewimageleft_1.jpg`, `reviewimageleftlarge_1.jpg` (×2), `group_3973.jpg`, `group_3972_2.jpg`, `group_3974_1.jpg` (×2) and `group_3971_2.jpg`.
  - The filenames suggest product, review and layout imagery.

- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M53J0YD1APZETG3K2RDKTP7C` should have `alt=""`. Impact is minimal.
