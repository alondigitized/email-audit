# Open for our bag edit
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- I'm going to say this upfront: I already reviewed "Open for our bag edit" from Sephora earlier today. Same subject, same Charlotte Tilbury hero, same SHOP NOW buttons. After the back-to-back "4X points on all hair care" doubles yesterday, and the World Cup lip email déjà vu the week before, Sephora has now officially made duplicate sends a pattern, not a fluke. Someone needs to look at the send queue.
- Setting that aside and judging the email on its own merits — there's something genuinely worth saving here. The Charlotte Tilbury anchor, the product curation pulling from Chanel, Maison Margiela, Tower 28, and L'Occitane in the same edit, the free mascara GWP up top — this is the Sephora I subscribed to. That mix of prestige-to-accessible across brands is something Ulta genuinely can't replicate as cleanly; their "TATCHA gift with $60 purchase" email last week felt stitched-together by comparison. But then Sephora undercuts the whole "bag edit" editorial promise by cramming in seven separate content modules below the fold. A curated bag edit should not also be trying to sell me oil-balancing hydration AND the Best of Lip roundup AND a Spot It Shop It feature all in one scroll.
- I am not clicking this. I saw it once already. I'd archive and go back to the first send if I actually wanted to shop the CT launch. Would not forward — my friends already got this email, probably twice.

## What's working

- The Charlotte Tilbury anchor is a real reason to open — "JUST IN FROM CHARLOTTE TILBURY" above a full editorial hero is the kind of prestige signal Sephora does better than anyone
- Free CT mascara GWP at the top stops the scroll; it's specific, it's aspirational, it has a hook that doesn't feel like "20% off everything" laziness
- Model casting is right for me — young, glowy, editorial-beauty coded, not the stock-photo-sunscreen-hat energy Ulta has been defaulting to
- The brand mix in the product grid (Chanel + Tower 28 + Maison Margiela) actually reflects how I shop — prestige and cult-indie in the same cart
- The INSIDER loyalty block at the bottom acknowledges I'm a known customer, not a cold list hit

## What's weak

- Duplicate send — same subject, same creative, same day; the cadence here is actively eroding trust across the whole sender relationship
- The "bag edit" premise promises curation, but the layout has at least six competing sections: CT feature, product grid, Spot It, Try It Love It, Best of Lip, Oil-Balancing Hydration — that's not an edit, that's a dump
- "SHOP NOW" is generic and fights the editorial premise; e.l.f.'s "mist opportunity" send this week had more personality in its CTA than anything Sephora uses
- The free CT mascara GWP threshold is buried in fine print next to the SHOP TO REDEEM button — I can see there are conditions, but I can't read them at normal scroll speed

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress the second send.** Sephora has open-rate data. Anyone who already opened this email today should be excluded from the resend — this is the single highest-leverage fix and it doesn't touch a single pixel of creative.
- 2. **Collapse the modules to three.** Lead with CT (keep it), pick one product highlight, pick one category play (Best of Lip OR Oil-Balancing — not both). The current seven-module stack doesn't say "bag edit," it says "we had content to fill."
- 3. **Put the GWP threshold in big type.** "Free CT mascara with $X purchase" should be readable above the fold without squinting — the offer is strong enough, stop hiding its mechanics.
- 4. **Swap "SHOP NOW" for editorial CTA copy.** "Shop the Bag Edit" or "See What's In Our Bag" ties the click to the concept and doesn't read like a leftover template.
