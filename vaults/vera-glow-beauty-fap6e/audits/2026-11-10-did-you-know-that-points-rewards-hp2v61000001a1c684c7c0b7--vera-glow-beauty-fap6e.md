## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with every link wrapped in `app.sephora.com/T/…` click-tracking redirects. The QA run passed 25% of checks, and the main confirmed technical issues are the missing plain-text part, the fixed 700px layout, and a few markup defects. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and most links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 of 12 probed links failed.** These are the "Rouge", "November Rewards Are Here", "Summer Fridays Hybrid-Makeup Essentials", "Summer Fridays On-the-Go Faves" and "Unsubscribe" links.
  - Four failed with `_ssl.c:1112: The handshake operation timed out`. One failed with `The read operation timed out`.
  - All five point to `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1c684c7c0b731176e96638918/940e9e6a…/940e9e6a-01f8-4c43-b019-b0c28bb8a638?__dU__=…`.
  - These are connection-level timeouts, not HTTP 4xx/5xx responses. They could be rate limiting or bot filtering by the redirect host, or a probe-side problem. They do not prove the links are dead.
- **Wrapped links hide the destinations.** Every link goes through the redirect domain, so destination URLs cannot be verified from the source.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt text.** The hidden `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…' style="display:none; …mso-hide: all"/>` is flagged by QA. This is low severity because it is a hidden open pixel. An empty `alt=""` would silence the warning.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 32 links were skipped, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed-width layout.** The wrapper uses `<table width="700" … style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The viewport meta is set, but `min-width:700px` forces horizontal scrolling on screens narrower than 700px. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only adjust font sizes and icon sizes.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The value as written is not a valid directive.
- **Malformed CSS.** An HTML conditional comment `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. This is not valid CSS there, and parsers may drop the rules that follow it, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`.
- **JSON-LD is ineffective.** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip scripts, and the empty values are not usable markup.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It is just "Sephora".
- **Plain-text part is missing.** QA reports `Text version is 0 chars`. See section 5.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated portion. The `__dU__` parameter is a per-recipient tracking token, not a merge field.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text alternative missing (QA FAIL).** A 0-character text part hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could also be an artifact of how the relay captures the message.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers not found (WARN).** Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders. QA notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. QA expects this from the relay, so it is not a defect of the email itself.
- **In-body unsubscribe link exists but could not be probed.** The "Unsubscribe" link at `…/940e9e6a01f84c430000021ef3a0bce8/…` timed out. Its function is unverified.
- **Postal address could not be checked.** It is not in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All links are redirect-wrapped (`/T/…?__dU__=…`), no UTM parameters are visible in the source, and the failed probes returned no landing pages. Whether UTMs are appended after the redirect is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Re-probe the 5 failed links** with a longer timeout, retries and a browser-like User-Agent. Treat them as broken only if they fail again with an HTTP error. Prioritise the Unsubscribe link.
2. **Add a multipart/alternative text/plain part** with the content and the unsubscribe URL.
3. **Capture the raw headers** from the original send, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
4. **Make the layout fluid.** Use `max-width:700px; width:100%` instead of `min-width:700px`. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. **Fix the markup.** Move the MSO conditional out of the `@media` block, change `no index` to `noindex`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
6. **Follow redirects on the probed links** to confirm the final URLs carry the intended UTM parameters.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML.** The truncated source left the footer and the address unchecked.
