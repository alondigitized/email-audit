- This is the third Glossier 20% email in four days, after 10/15's "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐" and 10/17's "STILL ON!". The hero even says "STILL ON!" again. I already know the sale exists, so this one only matters if I want perfume. The fragrance angle is a real change from best-sellers, though. I'm a points-math girl, and "20% off scents" on three named Eau de parfums is something I can actually price out.
- The layout is clean and I can find the offer fast. The Doux scent-notes block is half-broken, though. The TOP row has "Sweet Violet" and the MIDDLE and BASE rows have notes, but the rows don't line up, so it looks like the table slipped. There's also no price anywhere, and no Glossier Rewards or points mention. The free Futuredew Solid with $75+ is the only threshold I get, and it's in the top banner, which is cut off on the right ("$75+ orde").
- Would I click? Probably, for You Rêve, if I were in the market for fragrance. Would I forward it? No. It's the same sale for the third time, and I'd skip it until the week of a real Sephora Beauty Insider event.

## What's working

- The "Save 20% off the scent of the season" headline is clear, and the blue "Shop now" button is hard to miss.
- Three bottles, each with a blue "20% OFF" tag and its own "Save on You / Fleur / Rêve" button, let me shop by scent. That's how I pick fragrance.
- The scent-notes block (Palo Santo, Frankincense, Myrrh, Ambrette) gives me what I'd want before blind-buying a perfume.
- The free Futuredew Solid with $75+ is a clear spend threshold I can plan around. It's a decent gift-with-purchase hook.
- The models have deep skin tones and the imagery feels current and inclusive.

## What's weak

- It's the same 20% for the third time in four days. "STILL ON!" is the label, so the scarcity is gone and I have no reason to act today.
- The notes table looks broken. The TOP row shows only "Sweet Violet", and the MIDDLE and BASE rows sit offset from their labels.
- No dollar prices and no deadline. I can't do my math on "20% off" with nothing to apply it to, and I can't tell when it ends.
- The top banner is cut off ("$75+ orde"), and the "*Terms apply" in the footer is tiny. The free-gift fine print isn't visible where the offer is.
- There's no Glossier Rewards or member benefit. Sephora and Ulta would at least show my tier or points here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an end date in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", and drop "STILL ON!". That alone would bring back some urgency.
- 2. Show the price and the savings on each bottle, for example "$78 → $62". I need the number to decide.
- 3. Fix the scent-notes table so TOP, MIDDLE and BASE each line up with their notes.
- 4. Add a Rewards line, such as "Members earn 2x points on fragrance". Ulta and Sephora both put tier math in their sends.
- 5. Make the free-gift banner fit on mobile and put the terms right under it.
