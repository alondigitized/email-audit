# This new lippie flav takes the cake 🎂
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- I'll say it: the cake-candle hero is cute, and the lip balm is $9. But this is the third "NEW!" drop from e.l.f. in five days. I got the Advent Calendar on 10/9, the Smooth & Soothe set on 10/10, the "Bestie" roundup on 10/12, and Spicy Bundt Nice yesterday on 10/13. That's a lot of cake and spice. Yesterday's send was already a dessert-flavored vibe email, so this one feels like the same party with a different candle.
- The offer is the best part, and it's buried. Buy 3 Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balms and the 4th is free. That sits in the fourth block, half way down. The hero only says "$9." My points math brain wants the deal up top. Four balms at $9 is $27 for four, which is $6.75 each. That's a real number, and the email never says it.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because it's e.l.f. and $9 lip balm is an easy add. Would I forward it? No. It's a reasonable product email, but nothing in it is worth a text to a friend.

## What's working

- The hero is a tight lip close-up with a pink balm tip and a bold "new!" sticker. I get the product in under a second.
- "THIS LIPPIE TAKES THE CAKE." in white on burgundy is loud and readable. The pun matches the subject line.
- The cake-and-candles photo with the girl holding the balm sells the flavor story. It feels like a birthday, not a catalog.
- The emoji checklist (birthday cake, shimmer, buttery formula, silicone tip, $9) scans fast. It's the clearest part of the email.
- "Buy any 3, get the 4th on us" is a real, easy-to-read deal, and no code is needed.

## What's weak

- The buy-3-get-1 deal doesn't show up until the fourth block. Sephora would have put the value in the hero. Ulta would have buried it too, but at least its threshold math is usually plain.
- Three "SHOP NOW" buttons in a row, all with the same copy. It's repetitive, and none of them says "Get the Cake Balm" or anything specific.
- The "Top Picks for You" row is a Power Grip Primer, a Lip Liner and a Dewy Setting Spray. That's generic. It doesn't feel personal, and the liner is the only lip item.
- The grey category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) are a lot of dead space. They look like a nav menu stacked vertically, and they don't add anything.
- There's no Beauty Squad or rewards points tease on the offer itself. As someone doing points math, I see the rewards line only in the footer.
- The bottom fine print is tiny. The deal ends 10/18, but that's not visible in the main body, so there's no urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Say "Buy 3, get the 4th free" next to "$9," and put the end date there too.
- 2. Cut the second and third "SHOP NOW" blocks into one. The hero, the checklist and the promo block all say the same thing.
- 3. Make the CTA specific: "Get the Birthday Cake Balm" instead of "SHOP NOW."
- 4. Replace the generic Top Picks with other lip products, or other Glow Reviver shades, so the cross-sell makes sense.
- 5. Add a Beauty Squad points line, like "earn 2x points on lip," since I'm already counting.
- 6. Drop the grey category bars, or turn them into a compact nav.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Braze-sent HTML template using presentation tables, a preheader and `lang="en"`. Four probed links ended in relative redirect targets, which is probably a checker artifact. There is no plain-text part, and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four "Link error: unknown url type" failures.** All four are Braze-wrapped `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` links. They resolve to relative paths, not absolute URLs:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?shortlink=email&pid=Email&utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&af_dp=...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **The relative targets are probably a probe artifact.** The error text is Python/`requests`-style `unknown url type`. Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header against the redirecting host, so these links most likely work for subscribers. The QA tool didn't resolve them, so the destination HTTP status is unverified. I'd treat this as unconfirmed, not a confirmed break.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 29 skipped. The footer, social and most secondary links weren't verified.
- **Tracking pixel has no alt text.** The flagged `<img>` is at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/YTemWZ6ZvdKKvGSFH39zlA~~/...`, which looks like the Braze open-tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Missing `alt` on the tracking pixel.** It should be `alt=""` so screen readers skip it.
- **Malformed `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`** in a `mobile_hide` html_block. The value is harmless because it falls back to Verdana, but it points to an empty template variable. That block has no visible content in the truncated source.
- **No plain-text alternative.** See section 5.
- **Google Fonts `<link>`** (Open Sans) is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. It is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back to Arial. This is expected behavior, not a defect.
- **Nothing else flagged.** The layout tables use `role="presentation"`, and the viewport meta tag and `lang="en"` are present. The source was truncated, so the remaining content blocks weren't reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered Liquid or merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `{%...%}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part is missing.** The QA check reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). This hurts deliverability and accessibility. It could be a relay capture issue, but it is flagged as a failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this needs to be checked against the raw message from the ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical address can't be verified.** They fall in the truncated portion of the source. The QA notes say an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe, and no failure was reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters exist only on the app link.** It carries `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email` plus `pid=Email` and an AppsFlyer `af_dp` deep link. UTMs on the product links are hidden inside the Braze-encoded redirect payloads, so they can't be confirmed from the source.
- **The receipt-scanning CTA lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`.** That sends subscribers to a login wall, not to program information. It is a continuity risk.
- **The product CTAs deep-link with variant parameters** (`?Size=...`, `?Color=...`). Those landing pages weren't fetched, so variant preselection and stock are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with relative `Location` headers resolved against the redirecting host (`urljoin`). Then re-check the four flagged links to confirm 200 responses.
2. Add a plain-text MIME part in Braze, since the current text version is 0 characters.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw ESP message. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `m.elfcosmetics.com`, because the relay hides these.
4. Set a meaningful `<title>` and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
5. Fix the empty font-family variable in the `mobile_hide` block, or remove the block.
6. Point the receipt-scanning CTA to a public program page, or add a post-login redirect that lands on the feature.
7. Confirm that UTM parameters are consistently applied to the product links in the Braze link template.
