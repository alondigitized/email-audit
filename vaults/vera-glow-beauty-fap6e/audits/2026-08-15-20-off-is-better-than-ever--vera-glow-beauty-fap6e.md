# 20% off is better than ever!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "20% off is better than ever" — and then I open it and the 20% off is gated behind SheerID verification for teachers/students/military/medical/first responders. DE, we did this exact bit on 8/9's "The secret weapon" send too, except this time it's not even a launch tie-in, it's just recycled the SheerID gate again as the whole email. I don't qualify for any of those five categories, so this entire hero section — headline, image, CTA — is dead to me before I even finish reading it.
- The one thing that's actually for me is buried at the bottom: the Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops sample card with the cute green quilted travel bag, free on $110+. That's a real product tease with a real (if steep) threshold, and it's honestly a better hook than the top of this email. Compare that to Sephora's ILIA send ("This is your shade") which nailed personalization by eye color — DE is sitting on a legit new-launch story with Kamo and buried it under yet another gatekeeping stunt.
- I'm not clicking GET VERIFIED, I don't forward this, and I'm scrolling straight to archive after glancing at the bag.

## What's working

- The Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops callout with the 6-shade sample card and the green quilted bag is a legit, appealing visual — I want that bag.
- Clean, on-brand minimalist layout — no render bugs, text is crisp, hierarchy reads top to bottom fine.
- The free deluxe sample banner up top is a real, unconditional perk tied to subscription orders $50+, no verification hoops for that one.
- Fine print is thorough (dates, exclusions) even if it's a lot — at least they're not hiding the restrictions.

## What's weak

- The headline offer — 20% off — is entirely locked behind SheerID verification I don't qualify for, so the whole top half of the email is irrelevant to me, same move as 8/9's "Retinol Is Our Superhero" gatekeeping.
- Five stacked "click here if you're..." buttons (teacher, student, medical, military, first responder) eat massive vertical real estate for a demographic I'm not in — Ulta would never bury the actual best offer this deep for this much of their base.
- No RougeVIB-style loyalty math anywhere — DE doesn't run a points program, but compared to Sephora/Ulta emails I get, there's zero "here's what you've earned" personalization, just generic verification bait.
- The Kamo launch — the one thing I'd actually click — gets a tiny thumbnail-sized section at the very bottom after all that SheerID noise, when it should've been the hero.
- $110 threshold for the free sample/bag is steep and only mentioned in small text under the product blurb, easy to miss on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Flip the hierarchy — lead with Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops as the hero, since it's the one story here that's actually new and relevant to more than a verification subset.
- 2. Cut the five stacked buttons down to a single "Get Verified" CTA with a dropdown or short list — don't make non-qualifying subscribers scroll past a wall of buttons that aren't for them.
- 3. Segment this send — people who've already verified or clearly don't qualify shouldn't get the SheerID-first version at all; give them the Kamo-led version instead.
- 4. Move the $110 free-gift threshold up near the Kamo image itself, not just in the fine print.
- 5. Give repeat SheerID subscribers something new — this is the second time this month the gate's been the whole pitch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Kamo Cover Drops + a free bag on us`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Teachers, medics, military: your 20% is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Find your shade, snag the travel bag, free on $110+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 15-25% off for verified essential workers`
