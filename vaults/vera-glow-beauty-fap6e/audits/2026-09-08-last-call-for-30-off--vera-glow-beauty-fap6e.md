# Last call for 30% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "ends tonight" — this is Glossier's FIFTH Labor Day email in under a week, and I mean that literally: I logged "Save 30% off select favorites" on 9/3 (6/10), "Save big on fall favorites 🍂" on 9/5 (5/10), "Want a free gift?" on 9/6 (5/10), and "Save 30% off fragrance" yesterday on 9/7 (6/10) — and now it's back to the exact same 30%-off-sitewide mechanic that started this whole run five days ago, just with an "ends tonight" clock bolted on. The grid is clean, the products are recognizable Glossier hits (You Rêve, Lip Glaze, Generation G), and the 50%-off concealer/foundation section at the bottom is genuinely the freshest thing I've seen from them all week. But I've now seen "30% off select favorites" phrasing twice from this exact brand, and the urgency ("a few hours!") is starting to feel like the boy who cried wolf.
- Compare that to Drunk Elephant, who I've now caught running DRUNKVIP20 five days straight (9/4 through 9/8) without even bothering to change the subject line format — at least Glossier is rotating creative and burying a real 50%-off deeper-discount tier at the bottom instead of just recycling one code forever. But Sephora's "LAST CHANCE 50% off" ribbon has shown up in five of my last five logged sends too, so the whole category is drowning in fake urgency this week.
- I'm not clicking this one. I already shopped the 30% sale from the 9/3 email, and "ends tonight" on a promo I've now seen four times doesn't move me — it just confirms Glossier thinks I'm not paying attention to my own inbox. Not forwarding, not opening the next one unless the subject tells me something new.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF concealer and foundation callout at the bottom is the one genuinely new offer in five days of Glossier Labor Day emails — that's worth surfacing higher.
- Clean two-column product grid with real shade/finish descriptors (medium coverage + dewy, sheer matte + fragrance-free) — that's the kind of formula detail I actually want.
- "Ends tonight" and the bold LABOR DAY SALE hero at least make the deadline visually unmissable if I hadn't opened any of the four prior sends.
- Recognizable Glossier product photography — the You Rêve/Body Spritz stack and the Lip Glaze macro shot are on-brand and shoppable-looking.

## What's weak

- This is my FIFTH Glossier Labor Day email since 9/3 — the fatigue is real, and "select favorites" is now word-for-word identical to the subject I saw five days ago.
- No loyalty math anywhere — I'm cross-shopping Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond points against every offer, and Glossier gives me zero reason to care about tier status or points here.
- The 50% off items — the actual news in this email — are buried at the very bottom below three other product rows.
- "A few hours" urgency lands weak on the fourth-plus rerun of the same 30% mechanic; it reads as recycled copy, not a real countdown.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the 50% off concealer/foundation deal, not the generic 30% sitewide — that's the actual differentiator this send has over the last four, so put it above the fold.
- 2. Stop reusing "select favorites" language — rotate subject copy every send the way you're (mostly) rotating hero art, or the fatigue compounds faster than the sale converts.
- 3. Add a real countdown timer or specific end time (e.g., "ends 11:59pm ET") instead of vague "a few hours" — specificity is the only thing that makes urgency credible on send #5.
- 4. Drop in a loyalty nod — even a line like "plus free samples for members" would give Rouge/Diamond types like me a reason to open over the competing Sephora/Ulta emails hitting my inbox the same week.
