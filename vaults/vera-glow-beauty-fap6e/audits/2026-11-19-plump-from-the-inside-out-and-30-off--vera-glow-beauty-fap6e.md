# Plump from the inside out, and 30% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: pretty, but it's burying the lede. The subject promises 30% off, and I had to scroll past a huge pink hero and a yellow copy block to find "30% off" in a lavender box at the bottom. The only number up top is the "$62.30 VALUE: $139" burst. That one works. I did the math right away: $62.30 against $139 is about 55% off, which is better than the 30% in the subject. So the subject and the email don't match, and that bugs me.
- This is the third DE kit push in a week. On 11/17 it was "$158 of skincare for $69?!" with that $69.30 burst, and on 11/15 it was the sample-duo ladder. Today is another kit-at-a-discount, now a Bora refill. I like that it's a different product from the 11/13 "Winter is coming" send. But Sephora would put "30% off" in the hero, and Ulta would at least add a points multiplier. Neither shows up here.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because I use Bora and a refill is a smart buy. I'd forward it to a friend only if she's a barrier-repair person. I'd skim it, check the price on Sephora, and probably buy there for Rouge points.

## What's working

- The "$62.30 VALUE: $139" burst on the kit photo gave me a real price and a value anchor.
- The product shot is clean. You can see the Bora jar and the refill pod together, so I understood the kit right away.
- "Repairs skin barrier and reduces redness" is a benefit line I care about, more useful than just "moisturizer."
- The "procrastinators" box has the right tone and adds light urgency ("selling fast", "all month long").
- The fine print is readable and sits right under the offer: no minimum, discount applied at checkout. That's honest.

## What's weak

- The subject says 30% off, but the hero says "Rejoice, Refill, Repair" with no offer anywhere in the first screen.
- The discount math is unclear. 30% off and "$62.30, value $139" are two different stories. If $139 is the bundled value, I can't tell what the 30% applies to.
- There are three "SHOP NOW" CTAs, one of them a tiny text link. None name the product or the savings.
- The "Cannot be combined with other discounts" note kills any stacking. There's no loyalty angle, no points, no member pricing.
- The other two kits in the bottom photo have no names or prices, so I don't know what they are.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "30% OFF HOLIDAY KITS" in the hero banner above the product image. It's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Reconcile the numbers. Show "$139 value, now $62.30" once and explain what the 30% applies to.
- 3. Name the product and the savings in the CTA ("Shop the Bora Kit", "Get 30% off").
- 4. Label the other kits with names and prices, or cut that block.
- 5. Add a member or points hook, or give me a reason to buy at DE directly instead of Sephora.

## Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant, "Plump from the inside out, and 30% off!"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has no confirmed critical defects, but it has several real code-level issues. These are tracking pixels without alt text, `http://` social icons, a recipient email address in a tracking URL, and zoom-blocking viewport and text-size settings. Compliance and continuity can't be fully verified. The HTML is truncated and the QA tooling couldn't capture headers or probe the tracking links.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **12 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination resolution, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The recipient's email is exposed in a tracking URL query string:**
  `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`
  This is plaintext PII in a URL. It can leak through logs, proxies and referrers, and a forwarded email would expose it.
- The Marketing Cloud-style pixel `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holborarefill_p…` carries a numeric subscriber ID (`mi_u`). This is lower risk, since the ID is not directly identifying.
- There are two separate tracking and analytics vendors, `mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`. This is not a defect, but it adds third-party requests.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, confirmed):** `o.gif` (mi.drunkelephant.com) and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image. Both are tracking pixels, so the fix is `alt=""`, which marks them as decorative to screen readers.
- **Non-HTTPS images (QA, confirmed):** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that don't proxy images may block or warn on them.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The CSS rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` also disables user text scaling.
- **Invalid `@import` placement:** the `<style>` block starts with `body {…}` and puts `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` after it. Per CSS rules, `@import` must come first, so browsers ignore these three imports. The `<link>` tags and `@font-face` blocks may still work. The fallback font stack isn't visible in the truncated source.
- **Probable CSS parse hazard:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` comments sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. HTML comment tokens inside a CSS block are not valid comments. They can invalidate the next rule, which would likely be `*[class=vouchertext4]`. Its `.vouchertext4` class twin is separate and not affected. This is likely but not verified in a client.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=… xmlns:w=…>`. The `<title>` is a generic "Drunk Elephant".
- **Third-party fonts** (`db.onlinewebfonts.com`, plus the `drunkelephant.com` Demandware CDN) are unsupported in Gmail and Outlook. Fallback behavior can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. Recipient personalization in the Everest URL resolved correctly. The source is truncated, so the body and footer are not fully covered.

### 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (QA WARN).** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) is also not found. The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed absence**. Check the raw `.eml` at the origin or in a seed inbox.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com` and the image host is `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`, which suggests aligned subdomain infrastructure.
- The unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be verified.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated source, and the 12 tracking links were not resolved. UTM parameter presence, destination alignment with the "30% off" promise, and any promo-code carry-through can't be verified.
- The campaign identifiers (`mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holborarefill_p…`, `c=us_de_broad…`) show an internal naming convention. They are truncated and give no landing-page evidence.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the plaintext email from the Everest tracking URL** (`?e=…@etell.app`). Use an opaque or hashed recipient ID.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and the Everest pixel.
3. Change the three social icon URLs to `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/...`.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
5. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them in favor of the `<link>` tags. Delete the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside the `@media` block.
6. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a more descriptive `<title>`, such as the subject line.
7. Verify headers from a raw message (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`). Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe link, postal address, UTMs and the resolved link destinations.
