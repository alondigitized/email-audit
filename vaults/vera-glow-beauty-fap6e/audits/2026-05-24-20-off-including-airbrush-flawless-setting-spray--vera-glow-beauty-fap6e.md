# 20% Off! Including AIRbrush Flawless Setting Spray 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay so this is Charlotte Tilbury email number — let me count — *seven* in nine days. Seven. May 16 was the 15% with the exclusion-clause math, May 18 was the mini gifts send I actually liked, May 21 was "20% Off Is HERE," May 22 was a triple-stack (scratch card, "20% Off Icons," and the "Still Deciding, Darling?" nag), and now Saturday morning of Memorial Day weekend they're back with "20% off including AIRbrush Setting Spray." The cadence is genuinely past the point of charm.
- That said — credit where it's due — leading with Setting Spray is actually a smart pivot. They've been running the same Pillow Talk / Magic Cream loop for a week, and pivoting the hero to AIRbrush (with the "clinically proven waterproof to last up to 16 hours" line) at least gives me a *reason* this email isn't a carbon copy of the last one. The Loyalty-Members "Unlock A Free Collagen Lip Bath in Pillow Talk Rare when you spend $115" block is also a real lever — that's a tier-program flex, and I'm a Magic Rewards member so it lands.
- Would I click? Probably not — I already opened the Memorial Day sends from Sephora and Glossier this week and I've done my points math elsewhere. Would I forward it? No. Would I mark it as read? Yeah, after rolling my eyes at the cadence.

## What's working

- The AIRbrush hero is a smart re-angle — they finally stopped recycling the Pillow Talk image and gave me a different product to consider.
- The "16 hours" waterproof claim is the kind of concrete product detail I actually care about going into summer humidity.
- The Loyalty Members GWP block ($115 spend → free Pillow Talk Rare Collagen Lip Bath, $30 value) is a clear tier-program reward and it's segmented for me.
- The CT velvet-burgundy palette still does its job — visual hierarchy lands on the offer first.
- Two clear CTAs ("Shop 20% Setting Spray" / "Shop 20% off Everything") give me an entry point either way.

## What's weak

- This is the *seventh* 20%-off email in nine days. Drunk Elephant ran their F&F for five emails and I wasn't mad — CT is running the same offer past the fatigue line.
- The $115 spend gate to unlock the free Lip Bath is steep for a GWP; Sephora's "$75 spend = 500 bonus points" from yesterday is more honest about the math.
- The fine print block at the bottom is *massive* — "Excludes gift cards, consultations, subscriptions, beauty kits and already-discounted items..." This is the exclusion-clause gymnastics I clocked back on the May 16 send.
- No specific Memorial Day language — this is the long weekend, every other brand is owning it, and CT is just running "20% off" again without naming the moment.
- The "discover the magic of charlottetilbury.com + app" block in the middle feels like filler — I don't need a generic app-download pitch in the middle of a sale email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cool the cadence.** Two sends on the same day (May 22) and now another on May 24 is too much. Glossier dropped one launch email Friday and one reminder Sunday and got a 9/10 from me — restraint scores.
- 2. **Lead with Memorial Day, not just "20% off."** Every other brand in my inbox is owning the long weekend — Sally, Ulta, Sephora, e.l.f. — and CT is the only one pretending it's a random Saturday.
- 3. **Surface the GWP threshold above the fold.** The Loyalty Members $115-spend Lip Bath unlock is the single most interesting thing in this email and it's buried below two CTAs.
- 4. **Shrink the exclusion fine print or restructure as a quick bulleted list.** The wall of grey text undermines trust.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Memorial Day: 20% off AIRbrush Setting Spray (16hr wear)`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge members: free Pillow Talk Lip Bath at $115`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Collagen Lip Bath when you spend $115.`
