- "Your beauty favorites" promises personalization, but the grid is four hair dyes. It's not tailored to me, and it's nothing like my skincare and makeup habits.
- The "Holiday Edition" circle text is clipped at the edges, and it's holiday framing on October 5th. It feels early and a bit cluttered.
- "Not combinable. Excludes clearance." sits in tiny type right under the CTA, so the "sitewide" claim feels softened.
- The three stacked red and pink banners above the hero fight the hero for attention.
- The legal block is a wall of text, and the footer ends with an internal tag, "CRM-14551_SBHA_USA_FullFile_BigDeals_Concord_Test". That looks like a test send.

- 2. Move the 3x app points up into the hero, next to the code. Make the points multiplier a headline, the way Sephora Insider does with Rouge 2X/5X.
- 3. Cut the stacked banners down to one, and drop the holiday ring.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with responsive CSS and an `@media (max-width:499px)` block. The source was truncated and the relay stripped authentication and unsubscribe headers, so compliance and UTM findings can't be confirmed. The confirmed defects are missing alt text on 8 images, an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, and one non-HTTPS tracking pixel.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` was sent with a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in `r=`. The cache-busting value was never substituted, so clients and proxies can cache the pixel and under-count impressions.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over HTTP. Clients that block non-HTTPS images will drop it, and the pixel will fail to fire.
- **Link probe skipped:** 54 tracking and redirect links were not probed, so their destinations and status codes are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel stack:** the email loads at least four third-party beacons: Evergage, `pxl.mon-trk.com`, MyVisualIQ and Dotomi. Each adds a failure point and a privacy-proxy block risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (8 images):**
  - Four Evergage images: `summary.png` at `.../campaign/xNP1l/mzy21/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png`. These look like dynamic content blocks, so screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing for them.
  - One image with an empty `src`, which is invalid markup and may trigger a broken-image icon or a request to the page URL.
  - Three tracking pixels (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`). These need `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so they are treated as decorative.
- **Fonts:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica, which is safe.
- **Boilerplate present:** viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` overrides, and VML/Office namespaces. No issues found with these.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the MyVisualIQ URL (see Section 2).
- **Email address in image URLs:** the Evergage image URLs carry `?userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email address appears in plaintext in image request query strings. This exposes PII to the image host, proxies and logs.
- **Contact ID in pixel path:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../2321445/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` embeds what looks like a CRM contact ID (a Salesforce-style 18-character ID).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` were not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers. Absence of the headers is not evidence that the sender omitted them.
- **Not assessable:** the body footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) falls outside the truncated HTML, so it couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 54 links were skipped and the visible HTML doesn't show the destination URLs or UTM parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the sender's ESP substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time, or remove the MyVisualIQ pixel.
2. Move the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or drop it.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four Evergage dynamic blocks. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixels. Remove or fix the empty-`src` image.
4. Stop putting the raw email address in the Evergage `userId` parameter. Use a hashed or opaque ID.
5. Fix the relay so it captures the original headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`). Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the unsubscribe footer, physical address and UTMs.
6. Probe a sample of the 54 skipped tracking links by following the redirect chain to the final landing page.
