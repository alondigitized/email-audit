# Hair Daily Deals starts today 🎉 20–50% off select hair care.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a lot of email — I basically got the entire Sephora hair aisle dumped on me in one send. "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" plus free same-day delivery with code DELIVERED is a real one-two punch, and the calendar strip with a full week of category-by-category daily deals (fragrance Monday, skincare Tuesday, hair Wednesday, etc.) is genuinely useful info for someone like me who plans around this stuff. But by the time I scroll past the "Today Only" hero, the markdown callouts, the calendar grid, AND a whole second "20% Off Value-Size Hair Care" section with four more products, I'm fatigued — this feels like two emails stapled together.
- I already flagged on 9/22 that Sephora's "4X points on fragrance" was repeating within hours of itself, and this Hair Daily Deals send has the same problem in a different flavor: it's not repetitive across days, it's just doing too much in one scroll. Ulta's "20% off one item" mechanic (which I've now seen three times since 7/26) is boring but at least it's ONE clear ask. This one buries a genuinely strong markdown (GRO Hair Serum $44.80 vs $64, that's 30% off real product) under a wall of merchandising.
- Verdict: I'd click, because the Vegamour and K18 markdowns are real prices I recognize and want, but I wouldn't forward this — it's not clever or exclusive enough to share, it's just a big sale email.

## What's working

- "Get up to 50% Off Hair Faves" + the "TODAY ONLY" badge gives me real urgency without being screamy about it.
- Actual strikethrough pricing on the hero products (GRO Serum $64→$44.80, Amika mask $54→$44) — I can do the math myself, no guessing.
- The 7-day deal calendar is smart CRM — I now know Thursday 10/1 is Whitening & Bronzer and can plan around it like I do with Sephora's Beauty Insider event.
- "LIMITED EDITION — ONLINE ONLY" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags on Olaplex/K18 give me a real reason this isn't just an Ulta rerun.
- Clean at Sephora badges on K18 and Amika are a nice callback to the 9/21 "Clean at Sephora" send — consistent brand language.

## What's weak

- Two full promotions crammed into one email (Today Only hair deals + 20% off value-size hair care) — pick a lane.
- The daily deals calendar grid is tiny text on what's clearly a mobile screenshot — I'd have to pinch-zoom to read Wednesday's actual deal list.
- No Rouge or points-multiplier callout anywhere — for a Rouge member this is a missed beat, especially with Ulta's 21 Days always reminding ME of my tier status.
- "Spot it. Shop it." and the fragrance/holiday teaser blocks at the bottom feel bolted on and dilute the hair focus the subject line promised me.
- No skin-type or hair-type personalization — I've got fine hair and color-treated hair concerns, and Vegamour thinning-hair serum next to Olaplex bond repair next to Ouai clarifying shampoo tells me nothing was filtered for me specifically.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — Today Only Hair Faves as its own tight email, Value-Size 20% off as a separate day's send. Don't make me do the cognitive sorting.
- 2. Add a Rouge-specific line near the hero ("Rouge members: stack today's markdown with your birthday gift" or similar) — right now this reads generic-INSIDER, not tier-aware.
- 3. Make the calendar grid tap-friendly — bigger text or a "See Wednesday's Deals" button instead of a full 7-day image grid I can't read on my phone.
- 4. Lead the hero with the single best markdown (GRO Serum at 30% off) instead of a generic "up to 50%" claim, since only that item hits the top number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 30% off Vegamour GRO + free same-day hair haul`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge hair drop: up to 50% off, ends at 1pm PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Wednesday hair markdowns + free same-day delivery, gone by 1pm.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Olaplex, K18, Amika — today's the day these drop.`
