# PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- I'll be honest: this is the third Sephora app push in 48 hours, and I'm starting to feel it. After the 11/21 "Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀" send, I didn't need a "PSA" telling me Cyber Week starts early on the app. The 30% Color Wow hero is loud and clear, though. It's a real number in giant white type on purple sequins. Color Wow is a brand I'd restock on sale, so I did a little mental fist pump.
- The email stacks four things on top of each other: a free sample bag with code BAGITFIRST, 30% Color Wow, an 11/23–11/30 calendar, and then Sol de Janeiro, Merit and minis. It reads like a catalog, not a single message. My points math also stalls. The sample bag needs a $95 minimum spend, and the Color Wow deal is "app only, today only" with "in-stock merchandise only" fine print. I can't tell whether the two stack or whether I should hold my cart for Black Friday.
- Judgment: I'd click, but only for Color Wow, and only if I hadn't already seen Sephora's 11/21 send. I'd screenshot the calendar and not forward it. It's the same purple-sequin template I've already seen, and it's worse than that Benefit-led version, because this one has less of a single story.

## What's working

- "30% OFF Color Wow" is huge, white on purple, and I read it in half a second.
- "Today only" and "app only" are credible urgency, and the SHOP NOW button is big and obvious.
- The 11/23–11/30 calendar grid tells me exactly which brands drop each day (Fenty and Tarte on 11/27 and 11/28, for example). It's the best event-planning content in the email.
- Showing three actual Color Wow bottles, including Money Mist, makes this a product offer and not just a brand banner.
- Sol de Janeiro "25% OFF through 11/30" is a second concrete number in a category I actually buy.

## What's weak

- Subject and sender don't match: "PSA" is vague, and the sender says "Sephora Cyber Week". Ulta's "Black Friday^ starts NOW online! Up to 50% off" at least put the number in the subject.
- The free sample bag banner competes with the hero. It's stuck behind a $95 minimum, "1 of 4" bags and tiny exclusions text I can barely read on my phone.
- The email is very long, with seven or more sections, and the bottom half (Merit, minis, gift guide, credit card, Texts) is generic filler.
- Nothing about my Rouge tier shows up, so I got no member perks or points multiplier. Ulta at least talks Diamond benefits in its sends.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, including the "Available on in-stock merchandise only" notes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with Color Wow 30% and move the sample bag below it. Right now two offers fight for the top spot.
- 2. Put the number in the subject and show a clear deadline.
- 3. Add a Rouge line such as "Rouge: extra points on app orders" so I can do my points math.
- 4. Cut everything below the calendar to one product row, or drop it entirely.
- 5. State on the sample bag whether it stacks with the Color Wow deal.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The QA run scored 50%. One check failed (empty plain-text part), and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML was truncated, so only the head, preheader and the first link were visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Links go through a click-tracking redirect, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a203a6cd7fc50e7d969125cd18/fd137967a20e4a7d0000021ef3a0bcc2/...?__dU__=...`. This is expected behavior.
- QA probed 12 of 38 material links and skipped 26. The QA output lists no broken links among the probed set. The skipped links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel (`https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAn...`) is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It is an expected tracking pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on the tracking pixel (QA WARN).** The `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...'>` has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` would be cleaner. Screen readers may announce the filename in some clients.
- **Invalid robots meta.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` uses an invalid value. The valid token is `noindex`, so the directive is ignored. This is inert in email clients but still a code error.
- **Malformed CSS.** The `<style>` block has `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS. Parsers may drop `.mobileHeaderLogo`, the rules after it, or the whole media block. The rules that follow (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner`, `.mobileDisc`) are at risk of not applying.
- **Fixed-width layout.** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. If the mobile media block is dropped (see above) or the client ignores it, mobile viewers get horizontal scroll or scaling. The viewport meta also sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom (an accessibility concern).
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` does not match the subject.
- **Inert structured data.** The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients ignore it, and Gmail's schema support does not apply to this markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the truncated source, and the QA run flagged none.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** There is no evidence of RFC 8058 one-click support, with the same caveat as above.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `beauty.sephora.com` and the relay strips the header, so authentication cannot be assessed.
- **Footer/unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither was visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them. QA reports that an unsubscribe link was included in the link probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be confirmed. Links are wrapped in redirects (`app.sephora.com/T/...?__dU__=...`), and the final landing URLs were not captured in the source or QA output.
- The preheader promo code `BAGITFIRST` could not be checked against landing-page handling because no landing page data was available.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part (QA FAIL, 0 chars).** Add a `text/plain` alternative. Before treating this as a sender defect, check whether the AgentMail relay drops the part. The result may be a capture artifact.
2. **Fix the CSS.** Move the `<!--[if mso]>...<![endif]-->` block out of the `@media` rule so the mobile styles apply reliably.
3. **Add `alt=""`** to the hidden tracking pixel.
4. **Change `content="no index"` to `noindex`**, or remove the meta tag.
5. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
6. **Drop or populate the JSON-LD block.** The empty `discountCode` and `PromotionCard` add nothing.
7. **Re-run QA against the raw message.** Capture the full headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) and resolve the 26 skipped links and their final UTMs, so the compliance and continuity sections can be confirmed.
