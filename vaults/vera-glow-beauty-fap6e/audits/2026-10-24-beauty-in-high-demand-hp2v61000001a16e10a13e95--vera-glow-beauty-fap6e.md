- The Paula's Choice trial-size strip is concrete: a free gift, a code, and a $45+ threshold. I can do the math on it.
- The "Clean at Sephora" badges on the Summer Fridays products make quick sense to me.
- The product grid is easy to scan, with name, one descriptor, and price all in one tidy line. The "Only at Sephora" tags help.

- "Spot it. Shop it." with the Emi Jay x Dedcool and Anastasia/Lancôme minis adds some variety.

- The hero eats a third of the screen and has no offer. "Beauty for You" plus one "Shop Now" button is vague and wastes my first scroll.
- The Paula's offer is stuck in a thin strip at the top, with small "Exclusions/terms apply" text and "Online only." That reads as fine print.
- "You have 0 points" in the header is wrong for a Rouge. It makes the email feel generic.
- The Insider tier bar (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is tiny and static. There's no points math, no Bazaar tie-in, and no event date.
- Florals for Fall and Makeup Minis feel like filler. I got "Oh. My. Minis." on 10/23, so Makeup Minis again is a repeat.

- 4. Stop recycling minis and new-arrivals content across 10/22, 10/23 and 10/24. Give each send one story.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 47. It reported no broken links or redirect failures among the probed set. The unprobed links are unverified, not confirmed good.
- The tracking images use two hosts:
  - `https://www.sephora.com/gway/v1/evtEmail?apikey=…&context=…&timestamp=…` (two instances, including one with `p13n=con…`)
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx…` (open or click tracker)

- The QA output shows `×tamp=` in the `evtEmail` URL, which suggests `&timestamp` was decoded as the `&times` entity. This is probably a QA-display artifact. Inside an HTML attribute, `&times=` followed by `=` is not decoded, so the URL should work. Confirm that the raw `src` uses `&amp;`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 4 images):**
  - Two `evtEmail` pixels.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…` tracker.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` from `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…`.
