**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Skechers found a new toy to hallucinate about — literally. "How about a second look? Seen this before? We thought so" and the hero product is a kids' Skechers Garage light-up sneaker with a die-cast truck bolted to the side. I've never looked at this. Not once. This is the same brand that retargeted me on a navy men's Workout Walker three separate times (8/10, 8/11, 8/16) claiming it was "perfect for me," and now they've pivoted to a boys' toy shoe with the same "we know you've been eyeing this" copy. It's not funny anymore, it's just broken personalization.
- Below the fold there's an actual "here's some more options" grid mixing Care Bears kids' sneakers, BOBS slip-ins, and one pair of white sneakers that could plausibly be mine — buried among five items that clearly aren't. Then a real 40% off summer styles banner shows up, which is the only thing here I'd remotely click, and even that's generic sitewide messaging with zero curation.
- Verdict: not opening this again on faith, definitely not forwarding — I'd only send this to a friend as a "look how bad retargeting can get" joke. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- The 40% off summer styles banner is clean, high-contrast, and the one legit offer in the whole email.
- Layout and brand chrome (logo, footer, loyalty-adjacent utility icons like Afterpay/Klarna, store locator) are tidy and on-brand.
- One item in the "more options" grid — the white slip-in BOBS sneaker — is at least adult womens' footwear, so not a total swing and a miss.
- No render bugs — images loaded, buttons are tappable, text is legible without zooming.

## What's weak

- The hero product is a boys' light-up toy sneaker with a truck attached. I am a 29-year-old woman. This isn't a near-miss, it's a different customer entirely.
- "Seen this before? We thought so" is a lie — I've never viewed this product, and Skechers already burned this exact self-aware retargeting line on the Workout Walker in the 8/11 send.
- The "more options" grid is a grab-bag of Care Bears kids' sneakers and random BOBS styles with no thread connecting them to me or to each other.
- Zero tier/loyalty math, zero size or fit info, no mention of my past purchases — I'm being treated like an anonymous cookie, not a customer with history.
- This is now the fifth Skechers send in two weeks retargeting me on products I've never engaged with — Workout Walker x3, grey slip-in, now a kids' toy shoe.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the retargeting engine before sending another "seen this before" email — it's actively damaging trust by targeting the wrong age/gender segment entirely.
- 2. Lead with the 40% off sale as the hero, not a randomly-selected product I never viewed — Ulta's "20% off is calling" send at least ties the offer to something concrete even if it's repetitive.
- 3. If doing a "more like this" module, filter by gender/category actually inferred from browsing — mixing kids' and womens' product in one grid reads as spam-grade personalization, something even Sally Beauty's mass-market fall email didn't botch this badly.
- 4. Add loyalty math or a Sole Rewards point callout — I'm doing points math across Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond constantly, and Skechers gives me nothing to calculate here.
- 5. Drop the "we thought so" framing entirely until retargeting accuracy improves — Glossier's repetitive "Cheek Week" sends are at least emailing me about something I'd plausibly buy.
