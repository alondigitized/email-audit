# Black Friday just in 🔥: Merch on sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Glossier again, and this time it's merch, not skincare. The tumbler and playing cards are cute, but I'm a points-math girl and nothing here earns me anything. The hero says "All merch is on sale," then the circles stack 25% off everything and 30% off $100+. I can't tell which one applies to a $38 tumbler. The banner cutting off at "free You Candle when you spend $100" doesn't help either.
- I'd also seen the free-candle-at-$100 mechanic in this morning's "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle" send, and the 25% has run all week. "We never do this" is a nice touch for a first-ever merch sale, but it's the seventh Glossier send in about five days. I'd skim it and likely not click. I'd forward it to a friend who collects Glossier merch, but not to anyone shopping for beauty.

## What's working

- "Just in" plus "For the first time ever" gives the merch sale a real reason to exist, which beats a plain "more sale" send.
- The blue and pink palette is clean, and the tumbler hero image is charming and recognizable.
- The 2x2 product grid with "Limited edition" labels adds a little scarcity, and the product names are specific (Hot Take Tumbler, Balm Dotcom Keychain).
- The "Save now" button is big and easy to tap, and every product has its own full-width "Shop now" bar.

## What's weak

- The offers are muddled. The 25% off circle and the 30% off $100+ circle sit side by side, and I can't tell which one covers merch.
- The top banner is cut off at "when you spend $100" in the render, so the threshold reads as incomplete.
- No prices or sale prices appear on any product, so I can't do my usual math. Sephora's 50% email at least gave me a number I could apply to something.
- It's all merch with zero skincare or makeup. As someone who stocks up on beauty, that's irrelevant to me.
- Four identical "Shop now" buttons, and "Save now" in the hero, give no product-specific CTA copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the hero, "25% off all merch," and move the $100 candle deal to its own block. Drunk Elephant's "30% off sitewide" send was far clearer.
- 2. Show original and sale prices under each product, for example "$38 → $28.50," so I can do the math.
- 3. Fix the banner so it fully shows the free candle threshold and doesn't truncate.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, like "Get the tumbler" or "Shop keychain," instead of repeating "Shop now."
- 5. Add a skincare or makeup row so a beauty buyer has a reason to click, the way e.l.f.'s gift sets stayed on-category.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built (stripocdn.email) Klaviyo send (ctrk.klclick.com) with a responsive structure and no visible rendering faults. Its real defects are missing alt text on 5 content images plus the tracking pixel, and a leftover tracking `<span>`. The header and link checks are mostly inconclusive because the data wasn't captured or probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Only 1 of 12 material links was probed.** The other 11 were skipped by the QA ranking. Per the QA output, only the unsubscribe link was guaranteed a probe. Status codes and redirect chains for the CTAs are unverified.
- **Stray tracking element.** Right after `<body>` is `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M8EGZK22GYPHM4G6DHPKN0W7_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`, and the element is empty. It looks like a Klaviyo click-tracking artifact, not a working link. It has no user impact but is invalid markup.
- **Open pixel.** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M8EGZK22GYPHM4G6DHPKN0W7` is present as an image with no alt (see section 3).
- All links go through the `ctrk.klclick.com` wrapper, so final destinations can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `heroimage_1_16.jpg`
  - `group_3986.jpg`
  - `group_3987_1.jpg`
  - `group_3986_1.jpg`
  - `group_3987.jpg`
  - the tracking pixel `01M8EGZK22GYPHM4G6DHPKN0W7`
- The first five are content images hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, they render as nothing or as filenames. The pixel should carry `alt=""`.
- **Title mismatch.** `<title>` reads "Black Friday just in: 🔥 Merch on sale", while the subject reads "Black Friday just in 🔥: Merch on sale". The emoji and colon are in different positions, which is minor.
- Present and correct: `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, and MSO conditional comments with `OfficeDocumentSettings` for PNG and 96 DPI.
- **Hover behavior.** The `.rollover:hover` and `.bg:hover` rules are inside the `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` block. They are hidden from Outlook, which is intentional and fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|`, `%%`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers. **Treat as unverified, not a confirmed violation.** Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results** is missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `eml.glossier.com` are unknown. This is likely the same relay limitation.
- The unsubscribe link was probed per the QA note, but the result isn't shown in the excerpt.
- The physical postal address and footer unsubscribe markup are not visible in the truncated source and can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Every link is wrapped in a Klaviyo redirect, and 11 of 12 links were not probed. The final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page match can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the five Stripo images, and `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
2. Remove or replace the invalid `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` after `<body>` if the ESP template allows it.
3. Align the `<title>` with the subject line.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` from the original receiving inbox, not the AgentMail relay. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
5. Expand link probing to follow the Klaviyo redirects for the top CTAs. This would verify final status codes, UTM parameters and landing-page continuity.
