## Executive summary

- "This one's just for you" and then it opens with a free trial-size hair spray and a "Found It for You" hero for Prada Paradoxe — a fragrance, not skincare or makeup tailored to anything I've actually bought. So no, this isn't "just for me," it's Sephora's standard mid-August merch wall with a personalization subject line slapped on top. The 0-points glitch is sitting right under INSIDER again — same bug I flagged on yesterday's "winning" send and the K18 email before that — at this point it's basically Sephora's signature typo.
- That said, once I get past the subject-line fib, the body is genuinely well-curated: the "Found It for You" hero into a tidy 8-item discovery grid (Farmacy, VICTOR&ROLF, Kérastase, Sulwhasoo, MAC, Touchland, Maison Margiela, Armani) reads like an actual edit, not just a dump. ## What's working

- ROUGE is correctly highlighted in my tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) — so at least the tier logic works even when the points counter doesn't.
- The "Found It for You" hero with the Prada Paradoxe bottle is a clean, single-product moment with a clear NEW badge and price — no clutter.
- The 8-product discovery grid mixes prestige (Maison Margiela, Armani Beauty) with clean-beauty signaling (Farmacy's "Clean" checkmark) and "Only at Sephora" flags on three items — that's real curation, not just a random SKU dump.
- Same-Day Delivery / free shipping / in-store pickup banner up top is useful and on-brand for how I actually shop cross-channel.

## What's weak

- "0 points" under INSIDER, again — third time I've seen this exact sync bug across sends this week, and it undercuts the whole "just for you" personalization premise before I even scroll.
- Subject promises something tailored to me; the body is a generic new-arrivals wall with a free trial-size upsell as the actual hero offer, not a real % or $ discount.
- "Get a Trial Size" is buried at the very top above the fold with no visual weight — small type, no color pop — so the one "free" thing in this email barely registers.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere despite Sephora having all my Beauty Insider profile data — this could've gone to literally any Rouge member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points display before shipping another "just for you" email — a wrong "0 points" directly under a personalization-themed subject line is the single biggest credibility killer here, worse than anything Ulta or e.l.f. did in their recent sends.
- 2. Make the trial-size offer visually match its value — bigger callout, a "$0 with $40" badge, not buried in body copy next to Same-Day Delivery text.
- 3. If you're going to say "just for you," actually gate the grid by purchase history or skin profile — right now it's the same "new arrivals" format I called out on 8/8's "Ready, set, shop" and 8/10's "Time for something new."
- 4. Give the Rouge Rewards Bazaar mystery reward module ("GET A MYSTERY REWARD") more prominence — that's the one thing here that's actually tier-specific and interesting, and it's stuck two-thirds down the page.
