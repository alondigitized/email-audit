# Shop 20% off sitewide
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Twelve. This is ILIA email number twelve since May 16, and at this point I'm not even mad anymore, I'm just numb. "Shop 20% off sitewide" — the subject is the most stripped-down version of this campaign yet, like even ILIA's copywriter ran out of poetic ways to say the same thing. Last weekend it was "The art of 20% off forever," then "The time is now—20% off," and now we're just at "Shop 20% off sitewide" — descending order of effort, ascending order of fatigue.
- That said, the email itself is *fine*. The hero pencil-on-blue with "20% off bestsellers" plus the "25% off for VIPs" callout is the cleanest creative I've seen from them this whole F&F run. The bestseller carousel — Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Limitless Lash, Multi-Stick, The Base Face Milk — is actually useful, because those are the four products I'd recommend if a friend asked "what should I buy from ILIA," so they nailed the merchandising even if the subject line gave up.
- Would I click? Honestly, probably yes, because I've been sitting on Super Serum Skin Tint in my cart for a week waiting for VIP math to make sense and 25% finally does. Forward? No. Mark as read? Not until I check out.

## What's working

- The bestseller grid is exactly the right four products — Super Serum, Limitless Lash, Multi-Stick, Face Milk. That's a curated greatest-hits, not a dump.
- "25% off for VIPs" called out next to the 20% — finally, *finally* tier math I can do without a calculator. That's what Sephora Rouge emails get right and what Ulta Diamond emails usually bury.
- The "Cart Deco" callout for a free gift over $100 is a nice loyalty-adjacent nudge — feels like a perk, not bait.
- Hero pencil close-up is genuinely beautiful product photography. ILIA's art direction this whole campaign has been miles ahead of Sally Beauty's "20% Off Is Calling ✨" which I just scored a 4.
- Star ratings ("★★★★★") visible on every product tile — social proof without screaming.

## What's weak

- TWELVE emails in nine days. I've audited Drunk Elephant's whole F&F run at six emails and that already felt heavy. ILIA has doubled it. At some point the math stops mattering because I assume the next email will have it too.
- Subject line is the laziest one in the campaign. "Shop 20% off sitewide" is what you write when you've already sent eleven versions of the same thing and the team is out of ideas. Glossier's "30% is still on!" did more with fewer words because it acknowledged the previous send.
- No deadline in the hero. The whole campaign keeps saying "now" but never says *until when* — that's the same complaint I had on email #11. Drunk Elephant gives a date. Sephora gives a date. ILIA gives vibes.
- "Cart Deco" is cute branding but the "free gift over $100" threshold isn't visible in the hero — I had to scroll past four products to find it. Bury the lede much?
- "Makeup That Makes You Look Better" tagline at the bottom — slightly off compared to ILIA's usual "skin first" voice, feels generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. **STOP. SENDING.** Twelve in nine days is suppression-list territory. One more "20% off" subject this week and I'm hitting unsubscribe, and I'm a paying customer who literally has product in cart.
- 2. Move the deadline to the hero. "20% off ends Monday 11:59 PT" — that's all I need.
- 3. Lead with the VIP 25% tier — that's the only thing differentiating this send from the previous eleven. Make it the hero, not a subhead.
- 4. Pull the "free gift over $100" callout above the product grid. That's the unlock for cart math.
- 5. Subject line needs to evolve with the campaign — Glossier's reminder did this well by acknowledging "still on."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 25% for VIPs, 20% for everyone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Cart Deco gift at $100. Ends 5/27.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Super Serum, Limitless Lash, Multi-Stick — all 25% for you.`
