# Firmer skin?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "We've Got You Covered" — this is Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops, and it's the same launch DE teased 8/11 with "Introducing Kamo Drops!" (7/10, my highest score from them all week). This one leans into the "clinically proven to firm skin" claim instead of the intro-launch energy, and honestly the product itself is legit interesting — full-coverage foundation/concealer/tint that's also skincare is exactly the multi-tasking claim I'd actually research before buying, not just click past.
- The three-model hero shot is doing real work — three distinct skin tones lined up cheek to cheek, which reads as an actual shade-range flex, not a token diversity add. That's more than Sally Beauty managed with their Zotos/AGEbeautiful subject-body mismatch, and it beats Skechers phoning in the same navy walker photo for the third time this month.
- Where it loses me: there's no price anywhere, no points math for anything Sephora/Ulta-adjacent (this is a direct-to-consumer subscription play, not omnichannel), and the "SHOP KAMO" CTA just goes to a product page, not a shade-finder or quiz — which for a 25-shade foundation is the one interactive tool I'd actually want. I'd open this on my phone, skim the shade swatches at the bottom, and probably not click today — but I'd screenshot it for my "wait for a sale" mental list. Not forwarding to a friend, this isn't news-worthy the way "Bundle Up, People!" (8/7, 8/10) was.

## What's working

- Three-model hero immediately signals real shade range, not an afterthought swatch strip.
- "Clinically proven to firm skin" plus the "What you make it / Ready for action / Clinically proven to" breakdown is genuinely clear product education, not vague claims.
- Swatch trio at the bottom of the info block gives me a real sense of the shade spread before I even click.
- Consistent DE green-and-cream brand system, feels like the same house as the 8/11 Kamo intro and 8/9 retinol sends.
- The "TRY THIS AT HOME" sample-card + travel bag GWP is a smart secondary offer for someone shade-shy about a new foundation.

## What's weak

- Zero price shown anywhere for Kamo itself — I have no idea if this is a $38 tint or a $58 foundation, and that's the first thing I check before clicking.
- The subscription free-sample banner at top ($50+) and the $110+ GWP fine print at the bottom are two different thresholds for two different offers — I had to reread it to untangle which unlocks what.
- No shade-finder quiz or "find your match" CTA despite this being a 25-shade product — the copy literally says "we couldn't fit all 25 shades," so send me to a tool, not a static grid.
- Nothing here acknowledges me as a repeat customer or points member — no "welcome back," no loyalty nod, just a cold product pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price (or "starting at $X") right under the "SHOP KAMO" button — this is the single biggest missing signal for a considered purchase like foundation.
- 2. Add a shade-finder/quiz CTA near the swatch trio instead of routing everyone to the same generic product page.
- 3. Collapse the two GWP offers (subscription $50+ deluxe sample vs. $110+ sample card + bag) into one clear comparison table instead of two separate fine-print blocks.
- 4. Acknowledge existing customers — even a small "already own Kamo? here's what's new" line would beat the cold-open framing.
