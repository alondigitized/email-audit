# NEW & Limited Edition! e.l.f. POP holiday fragrance ✈️❄️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the eighth e.l.f. send I've logged since 9/28, and it's the first one in a while that made me stop scrolling. The "transport your e.l.f. to Tromsø" hero has a boarding-pass-meets-fragrance concept, and the Northern Lights purple-and-copper palette looks more prestige than the glitter-stick chaos from earlier in the week. The "come for the spice cake, stay for the cardamom frosting, fall in love with the cedarwood" scent-note block is the best copywriting I've seen from them. Sephora and Ulta both do notes breakdowns for fragrance, and this one actually makes me want to smell it.
- The email is long, and the offer is thin. Prices are $13 body cream, $13 mist and $12 travel kit, but there's no points angle, no bundle, no "buy two" and no free-shipping threshold. I do the points math on everything, and nothing here gives me any. Also, three separate "Shop e.l.f. POP" buttons plus three product buttons is a lot of the same ask. After that, the Skaar "Press Play" song block and the generic Top Picks row (primer, lip liner, dewy spray) feel like filler from a different email.
- Would I click? Probably yes on the Travel Kit, since $12 for a body cream plus a mist minis duo is a cheap way to test a limited-edition scent before committing. Forward to a friend? Maybe to my fragrance-obsessed group chat, only because of the Tromsø concept. Otherwise I'd read it and move on.

## What's working

- The "Tromsø" travel concept is a real idea. It feels like a launch, not a restock, and it beats the plain "NEW!" energy of the 10/2 Sleek Streak send.
- The "come for / stay for / fall in love with" scent-note block tells me what it smells like (spice cake, orange zest, cardamom buttercream, cedarwood, warm musk). Drunk Elephant's birthday email gave me nothing this concrete.
- Prices are right in the product titles ($13, $13, $12). Clean anchors, and all under $15.
- The model shots with body cream on a shoulder and mist at the neck show how the products are used, which Glossier does well too.
- "NEW & LIMITED EDITION" badges on every product create credible scarcity without all-caps shouting.

## What's weak

- No loyalty or points hook anywhere. Sephora Insider led its recent send with Rouge 2X/5X points, and I still can't tell how e.l.f. rewards me.
- Too many CTAs. Three "Shop e.l.f. POP" buttons plus three product buttons dilutes the click, and there's no single hero offer.
- The Skaar "Press Play" block is off-topic for a fragrance email, and the song gets more real estate than the travel kit does.
- The "Top Picks for You" row is generic (primer, lip liner, dewy spray) and doesn't match the fragrance theme. "For You" implies personalization I don't see.
- The category nav (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) is a big gray list that eats a lot of scroll at the bottom.
- There's no deadline. "Limited edition" never says how limited or until when.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real offer or bundle. Something like "Body cream + mist for $22" or "Free shipping on POP over $25" gives me math to do. Ulta would have put a gift-with-purchase here.
- 2. Cut the CTAs to one primary "Shop the Tromsø Collection" button, with product buttons secondary.
- 3. Lead the products with the Travel Kit. It's the best value and the best hero image, and it's currently third.
- 4. Drop the Skaar block or move it below the products. Replace Top Picks with a "Complete the glow" row of the fragrance-adjacent lip oil and body products.
- 5. Add a stock or date cue, such as "While supplies last" or "Ends 11/30", plus a rewards line.
