# Use your 0 points on a 250-point bundle
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- "Zero points" in my own inbox again. This is the same subject Sephora sent earlier today, and it still reads like Sephora is calling me broke. I'm Rouge, so I'm not at zero. I'm also mid-Savings Event math, so I opened it to see whether the bundle was actually worth redeeming.
- The body is a real Rewards Bazaar: 100-point and 250-point tiles with Saie, Huda, Moroccanoil, Dior, Fenty and Byredo. The "three 100-point rewards for just 250 points" line is the math hook I wanted, but it sits mid-page under a hero that says "November means new rewards!" The subject promises one bundle and the body shows a catalog. The ROUGE 10% code, ALLONSALE, also pulls attention away from the points story.
- I'd click, but only to check what the 250-point bundle contains. I wouldn't forward it. Ulta would have put the points value up top in one clean tile. This is the seventh Sephora send since 11/2, and it's starting to blur into the 11/5 "10% off our most-searched beauty" email.

## What's working

- The "Now you can get THREE 100-point rewards for just 250 points" line is the points math I care about.
- The 100 and 250 point tiers are clearly labeled, with a brand, product and "ONLINE ONLY" under each.
- The prestige names (Dior, Fenty, Saie, Huda, Byredo) feel curated, and the tiles are clean.
- The 10% off ALLONSALE code and the 11/1–11/9 date are visible near the top, so the urgency is credible.
- The barcodes at the bottom make the savings event usable in store.

## What's weak

- The subject says "0 points," but the body never mentions it. The hero says "November means new rewards!" with a generic SHOP NOW.
- The 250-point bundle is never shown as a single bundle. I have to read the small line under the hero and then scan the tiles to figure out what I'm choosing.
- Too many competing offers: the code, the points bazaar, a GWP headband, Kérastase, the holiday gift guide, a $15 credit-card offer and the free shipping code. It's a long scroll with no single winner.
- The tiles show no value cues like full size versus mini, retail price or cents-per-point, and no shade or skin-tone personalization.
- The Kérastase and holiday gift sections look like a different email stitched on, and the footer terms run long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero a bundle visual with the headline "3 rewards, 250 points" and a button that says "Pick your 3 rewards." That one change would help most, because it matches the subject and puts the points math first.
- 2. Say what the bundle includes. Show three product thumbnails and the dollar value, for example "$X of rewards for 250 points."
- 3. Cut the page to the points offer plus the sitewide code, and move the Kérastase and gift-guide blocks to a separate send.
- 4. Personalize by tier. Show Rouge members their actual balance and how many bundles they can redeem.
- 5. Add a value line to each tile (full size, retail value) so I can do the points math.

- **Pixel placement:** the pixel sits outside the preheader `div`s, directly in the `td`. It is harmless but not validated beyond that.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width vs. mobile:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`, while the responsive rules only apply at `max-width:480px`. Unless an inner override exists in the truncated portion, small viewports will scroll horizontally.
- **Malformed MSO conditional inside `<style>`:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media` block. Conditional comments don't belong in CSS. Browsers and most clients will treat `<!--[if mso]>` as part of the selector and drop the `img.mso-size` rule. The Outlook-specific sizing for `.mobileHeaderLogo` will not apply as intended.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It is likely ignored in email.
- **JSON-LD:** the block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` (empty value) and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"` with no properties. These are non-functional, and Gmail will ignore or reject them.
- **Missing alt:** confirmed for the tracking pixel only. Alt text on visible images could not be checked in the truncated source.
