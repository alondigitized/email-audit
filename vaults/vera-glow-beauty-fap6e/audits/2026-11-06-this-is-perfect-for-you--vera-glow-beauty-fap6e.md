# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh fake "you left something behind" email from Skechers in about two weeks. "Still thinking about it?" is the same cart nudge again, and I still haven't put anything in a Skechers cart. The 11/5 and 11/4 "Your cart is one click away!" sends did this too. The subject, "This is perfect for you!", promises personalization. The body is one Arcade Cozy Fit sneaker and six random women's runners and boots. Nothing here knows who I am.
- The one real thing is the navy banner: Skechers Plus members take 20% off regular-priced styles, valid through 11/10. That's the only reason I'd stay past the hero. It sits under a lot of scrolling, though. The hero spends about a third of the screen on a headline and a single shoe, and the offer comes after the product grid.
- I'd skim it, screenshot nothing, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, and this doesn't feel like a brand courting me. It feels like an automation firing.

## What's working

- The 20% off banner is high contrast and easy to read. "Skechers Plus members take 20%" is a clear member perk.
- The "valid thru 11/10" deadline is specific, and the fine print is spelled out at the bottom.
- The six-up grid shows women's styles with clear names, so I get a quick read on the range.
- The nav list (Women / Men / Kids / New Arrivals / Sale) has big tap targets that are easy on a phone.

## What's weak

- The cart-reminder framing ("Still thinking about it?", "Take another look") is false for me, since I never browsed or carted anything. It's the same trick as 11/2 through 11/5.
- The subject says "perfect for you," but the pick is a cozy-fit slip-in and the grid is a mix of runners and a fall boot. There's no signal of my taste.
- The offer is buried below the grid. I had to scroll past the hero and six shoes to find the 20%.
- The grid shows no prices and no sale prices. I can't do any points math.
- The footer is a pile of app badges, Afterpay/Klarna and social icons. It's noise on a promo email.
- There are no reviews and no loyalty point value. "Skechers Plus" never says what it costs or what it earns.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus banner above the hero, or make it the hero. Lead with the offer and let the product support it.
- 2. Drop the cart language unless there's a real cart. Frame it honestly as a pick for the person, e.g. "Picked from what you browsed."
- 3. Show a sale price under each shoe and add star ratings.
- 4. Cut the footer clutter and keep one CTA ("Shop Plus 20% off").
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off through 11/10`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Slip-ins pick + 20% off for Plus`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Regular-priced styles only. Ends 11/10. Log in to save.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Arch Fit Arcade and 5 more women's picks, 20% off with Plus.`
- Compared with the others in my inbox:
- Ulta's coupon ladder ($10 off $50, $15 off $75, $20 off $100) puts the savings first. Skechers makes me hunt for it.
- Sephora's Savings Event sends are noisy, but the 10% is in the subject. Skechers' "perfect for you" is vague.
- Drunk Elephant's "Singles Day steals" actually delivered a gift-with-purchase. This one delivers a reminder I didn't ask for.
