# ⚠️ It’s your LAST CHANCE to get 50% off select beauty ⚠️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "You have 0 points" is up top AGAIN — that's the same ribbon I flagged yesterday morning on the "Autumn Edition" digest, and now it's sitting over yet another Stacking Weekend send, my FIFTH logged from Sephora on this event alone. The "50% off ENDS TODAY" banner up top does at least give me real urgency this time instead of the vague "last chance" energy of prior sends, and I'll admit the giant red "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT BEAUTY" circle is doing its job visually. But then it just... keeps going. SEPTBONUS 500 points, then $10 Beauty Insider Cash, then Get Samples, then a "Save On Must Haves" product grid, then a SECOND "50% off Select Beauty" module with different products, then App Early Access, then a THIRD "Get 500 Bonus Points" banner, then Holiday Exclusives. That's not an email, that's four emails welded together.
- I'm not clicking through all that scroll on my phone before I lose the thread. The one thing that would get my thumb moving — the actual 50%-off product list — is nowhere to be found; it's all "select beauty," "exclusions apply," "see details below." Compare that to ILIA's "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" send I logged — that one told me exactly what I was buying before I tapped anything. This one wants me to shop blind on a coupon code buried in module three.
- Would I forward this to a friend? No. Would I click? Maybe the top button out of muscle memory, but I'm not scrolling this whole thing. Mark as read and move on — I've got the code, I don't need the novel.

## What's working

- The red "UP TO 50% OFF ENDS TODAY" strip up top gives real same-day urgency, better than the softer "last chance" language Sephora ran yesterday.
- The 50%-off red circle graphic is the clearest visual anchor in the whole email — my eye lands there first, no competition.
- SEPTBONUS 500-point callout ties into tier math I actually track, and the $75 threshold is specific.
- Rouge/Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider / VIB / Rouge, Rouge highlighted) is a nice touch of recognition, even buried that low.

## What's weak

- FIVE Stacking Weekend sends logged from Sephora now — this is fatigue, not urgency, and it's starting to read as spam-adjacent even without the visual signals of spam.
- The email is at least six stacked modules deep (Stacking Weekend hero, SEPTBONUS, Beauty Insider Cash/Rewards/Samples, product grid, second 50%-off module, App Early Access, third bonus-points banner, Holiday Exclusives) — total layout chaos, no single hierarchy.
- "Select Beauty" is never defined — no product list, no brand names, no category call-out for the actual 50% off, just "exclusions apply, see details below."
- The "0 points" ribbon is the same broken personalization I called out on yesterday's send — Rouge member, zero points shown, still not fixed.
- Redundant CTAs: three separate "Shop Now" buttons for three different offers before I even hit the product grid.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE offer per send. Lead with the 50% off, put actual category/brand names in the hero (mascara? skincare sets? fragrance?), and move SEPTBONUS, samples, and Holiday Exclusives to separate sends spaced out — not all in one scroll.
- 2. Fix the "0 points" ribbon — if I'm Rouge tier, show my real balance or drop the ribbon. This is the second send in two days with this bug.
- 3. Name actual products or categories under "select beauty" instead of forcing me to tap through to find out what's included.
- 4. Kill the duplicate 50%-off module lower in the email — it's confusing to see the same offer restated with different products and no clear relationship to the first one.
- 5. Space out Stacking Weekend cadence — five sends and counting is Glossier-level overkill (I flagged Glossier for FOUR sends this week), and it's actively training me to skim past Sephora subject lines.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 50% off skincare, makeup & fragrance — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge perks + 50% off, all ending today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: $10 Beauty Insider Cash and 500 bonus points with $75`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend's last day — see what's still 50% off`

# Technical Email Audit — Sephora "Last Chance 50%" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard Sephora ESP template (Epsilon/Harmony infrastructure) with heavy legacy Outlook/mobile CSS hacks; automated QA passed 50% of checks, with 1 failure and 3 warnings concentrated in deliverability headers and accessibility.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **3 images missing `alt` text** (QA FAIL-adjacent warning):
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYxMDAwMDAxYS0wODEzLTBjZjAtOWM1Ny0wY2...` (tracking/beacon image)
  - `o.gif` (open-tracking pixel, `mi.sephora.com`)
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (nav header image, `images.harmony.epsilon.com`)
  - Missing alt text on the nav header image specifically degrades screen-reader experience; the two tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty, decorative) rather than none at all.
- `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` — the value `"no index"` (with a space) is not a valid robots directive token; if this meta tag is ever crawled in a hosted/web version of the email, it will fail to suppress indexing as intended (correct token is `noindex`).
- Extensive legacy Outlook/VML and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` hacks present — consistent with intentional multi-client (Outlook/Gmail/Apple Mail) support, no defects observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization syntax found in the truncated HTML.
- No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — one-click/list-level unsubscribe is not exposed at the header level; mailbox providers (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) increasingly require this. May be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence — recommend verifying against raw source at the sending MTA.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — without this, one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed compliant with Google/Yahoo 2024+ bulk sender requirements.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from the captured headers; recommend checking at the origin MTA since Gmail/Yahoo enforce authentication for bulk senders.
- In-body unsubscribe link presence was not flagged as missing by the automated probe (footer links deprioritized/skipped, not confirmed absent).

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking beacon uses Epsilon/Harmony-style params (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=20260908_LWS_Ends_Today`) rather than standard `utm_source/utm_medium/utm_campaign` — this is expected for Epsilon-hosted campaigns but means standard web-analytics UTM attribution will not apply unless a separate UTM layer is appended to click-through CTAs.
- Insufficient data in the truncated source to confirm CTA destination URLs carry consistent campaign parameters or that landing pages match the `LWS_Ends_Today` campaign context — recommend reviewing the 12 probed links directly for UTM/param consistency.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the two tracking-pixel images and a descriptive `alt` to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`.
2. Fix `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` → `content="noindex"` if this template is ever rendered as a hosted web page.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are present at the originating MTA — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance; confirm this isn't purely a relay-capture gap.
4. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) at the origin, since it wasn't captured via the relay.
5. **Fix missing plain-text MIME part** (0 chars) — this is a hard compliance/deliverability risk (CAN-SPAM requires readable content in whatever format is sent, and a missing text/plain part is a common spam-filter signal).
6. Confirm probed CTA links carry campaign-consistent tracking params matching the landing page's expected attribution.
