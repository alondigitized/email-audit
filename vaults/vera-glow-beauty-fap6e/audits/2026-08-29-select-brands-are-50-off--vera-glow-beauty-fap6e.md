# Select brands are 50% off 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "50% off Select Beauty" TODAY ONLY as the hero is a legit stop-scroll — a real deadline, a real percentage, and it names Too Faced, Elizabeth Arden, and MAC right in the banner instead of hiding the brand list behind a link. That's more concrete than the "50% off select beauty" send Sephora ran yesterday (8/28), which also had this exact headline and turned out to be the one where "You have 0 points" was slapped under the header — a genuine loyalty bug. This one, on first glance, skips that landmine.
- But then the email pivots hard into "Real Luxury" editorial mode — Crème de la Crème Makeup, Next-Level Skincare, Feel-Good Fragrance, Hype-Worthy Hair Care — and it's basically four separate mini-catalogs stacked under one 50%-off banner that never gets referenced again below the fold. It's gorgeous, moodboard-y photography, but I'm Rouge tier doing points math in my head and none of it is here. No "you're this many points from your next reward," no birthday gift reminder, nothing that says "Vera, we know you." It reads like a magazine spread got bolted onto a flash-sale subject line.
- I'd click through on the 50%-off CTA specifically because "Today Only" pressures me, but I wouldn't scroll the whole email or forward it to a friend — it's pretty, not personal, and I've now seen this brand run the identical "50% off select beauty" hook twice in two days.

## What's working

- The "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" banner up top is genuinely clear: specific %, specific brands (Too Faced, Elizabeth Arden, MAC), visible urgency badge, and a bold "SHOP NOW" button.
- No render bugs this time — everything loads clean, no overlapping text, no broken images, unlike yesterday's "0 points" glitch.
- New-launch flags (YSL Make Me Blush, Jo Malone Sea Salt & Bergamot, Kérastase 8H Serum) are clearly tagged "NEW," which matters to me since I track launches.
- Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal payment block is a nice touch for anyone stacking a big Sephora Beauty Insider Event haul.
- Rewards footer bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at the bottom at least acknowledges the tier system exists, even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no points balance, no tier-specific perk, no "as a Rouge member you get X." Ulta's "20% off" sends at least put my name (Marisol, in that recent one) right in the subject; Sephora doesn't even try here.
- The 50%-off hero and the "Real Luxury" editorial section feel like two different emails glued together — the offer never resurfaces once you scroll past the banner, so the hierarchy collapses.
- This is the second "50% off select beauty" subject line in as many days from Sephora — if I got both, that's fatigue, not a fresh drop.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" fine print sits right under the hero in tiny gray text — not hidden exactly, but it dampens the 50% claim before I even scroll.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere, despite this being a skincare-heavy send (Caudalie, U Beauty) — competitors like e.l.f.'s Halo Glow send at least leaned into a specific skin concern with a swatch shot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Repeat the 50%-off mechanic at least once more — either as a sticky reminder banner mid-scroll or by tagging which of the featured products actually qualify for the discount. Right now the luxury catalog and the sale feel unrelated.
- 2. Put a points/tier line directly under the hero: "Rouge members, redeem X points toward this event" — something Ulta and even e.l.f. do better with segment-specific hooks.
- 3. Cut or shrink the fine print's visual weight, or move it to the very bottom — right now it undercuts the 50% headline before the reader even gets excited.
- 4. Since this is the second consecutive day running near-identical "50% off select beauty" copy, rotate creative or at minimum change the subject emoji/structure so it doesn't read as a repeat send.
- 5. Add a swatch or texture shot to the skincare/makeup blocks — flat product-on-table shots don't show me shade or finish the way a model swatch would.
