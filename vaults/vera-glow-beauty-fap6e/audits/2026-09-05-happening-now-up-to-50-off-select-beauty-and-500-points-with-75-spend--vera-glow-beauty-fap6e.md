# Happening now: up to 50% off select beauty and 500 points with $75 spend
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I already flagged the "Stacking Weekend" concept twice this morning and yesterday — this is that same email again, just now stacked with a Free Hair Sample Set banner up top and about eleven scrolls of product grid before I hit anything new. The hero is still doing the job — big "50%" wheel, "500 BONUS POINTS," "Stack your Beauty Insider rewards to get the max" — that copy is legitimately good and on-brand for how I actually shop Sephora. But then it just... keeps going. Free hair samples, then a random nine-product grid with zero organizing logic (Bobbi Brown next to Jo Malone next to a hand sanitizer?), then a SECOND 50% off panel restating the same offer with Selena Gomez's name slapped on it, then shampoo, then fragrance, then lips.
- This is the same skeleton I scored 9 and 8 on this week, but the execution here is bloated in a way those weren't — it feels like three separate emails got merged into one send. Sephora's own Selena Gomez Rare Beauty tie-in gets buried as filler item #11 instead of being the differentiator it should be. I'd scroll past this on my phone within three seconds of opening, not because the offer's bad, but because I already know what's in it. Not forwarding this one — I'd forward the actual "Stacking Weekend" hero if my friend hadn't seen it already, but this version is too cluttered to bother screenshotting for her.

## What's working

- The top banner nails the stacking math — 50% off + 500 points is exactly the kind of layered Beauty Insider value that gets me to actually do mental math before I check out.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the second 50% panel creates real urgency, not fake urgency — I know Sephora sales like this expire fast.
- Free Hair Sample Set with a real $43 min-spend threshold and named brands (Kérastase, Fromlabs, Crown Affair) is a legit GWP hook I haven't seen from them this week.
- Rewards Bazaar footer and the dual barcode (Sephora + Kohl's) reminds me this is a real loyalty ecosystem, not just a coupon blast — that's a trust cue Ulta can't match with its single-retailer footprint.

## What's weak

- This is functionally the same "Stacking Weekend" email I scored 8-9/10 on twice already this week (9/4 and earlier today) — the repetition is starting to read as inbox fatigue, not consistency.
- The product grid in the middle (Jo Malone, Dermalogica, Merit, Touchland hand sanitizer) has no visual hierarchy or category logic — it just reads like a random assortment dump, unlike ILIA's tight 4-step routine build I scored 7/10 on.
- Selena Gomez / Rare Beauty gets buried as a small panel deep in the scroll instead of leading — that's a wasted celebrity-brand hook.
- Way too long for mobile — I counted at least 6-7 full screens before reaching the fine print, which is more scrolling than Glossier's whole email that I dinged for being repetitive at 5/10.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE clear offer per send — either lead with Stacking Weekend or lead with the Hair Sample GWP, don't merge both into one marathon scroll.
- 2. Move the Selena Gomez/Rare Beauty panel up near the hero — that's a bigger draw than a random hand sanitizer in the middle grid.
- 3. Kill or drastically shrink the unorganized nine-product grid; group by category (skincare / fragrance / hair) like ILIA does with routine builds.
- 4. Stop resending the identical "Stacking Weekend" hero mechanic within the same 24-48 hours — I've now logged it three times.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Selena's pick + 50% off — Beauty Insider Stacking Weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free hair samples with $43+, plus your Rouge/VIB perks stack all weekend`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rare Beauty, 50% off, and 500 bonus points — see what's new only at Sephora`
