# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" is the first thing I see under my Rouge status bar, which is a rough way to open — and this is Sephora's fifth send in my feed this week, so the pattern's getting obvious: hard offer at the top, then a mile of unrelated new-launch merchandising. "50% off Select Beauty" up top is real and specific (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI, "Today Only"), but the second I scroll past that hero band it pivots into a full Kilian Paris cocktails collection launch, then a twelve-item new-arrivals grid, then Favorites Kits, then Holiday Drops. That's not one email, that's three campaigns duct-taped together.
- Compared to the 9/12 send I logged that hit 7/10 with a tight three-product "Today Only" grid, this one buries its actual discount under a wall of full-price new launches — Kilian's $37-$150 fragrance trio, Armani Beauty, Josie Maran — none of which are on sale. ILIA's VIP sale email (8/10) kept it to one offer and one clear CTA the whole way down; this reads like Sephora couldn't decide what today's email was actually about.
- I'd click the 50% off band since I'm always doing the points math and a real percentage off Clarins/Estée Lauder is worth two seconds of my attention, but I'm not scrolling through the rest, and I'm not forwarding this — nothing here is exciting enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- The hero band leads with a real number — "50% off" in huge type is impossible to miss.
- "TODAY ONLY" with a countdown-style badge creates actual urgency I trust from Sephora.
- Brand names are named upfront (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) so I know if it's worth clicking before I do.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is right there, so at least the loyalty program is visible even if my points count reads oddly.

## What's weak

- The 0-points display at the very top is either a render bug or looks like one — either way it reads wrong for a Rouge member like me.
- The offer basically ends after one screen; everything after Kilian is full-price new-launch merchandising with zero relation to the "50% off" subject line.
- Twelve new products crammed into one grid (Saie, Laneige, Gisou, Obart, Nars, Josie Maran, Armani, Touchland) is overwhelming with no personalization — I don't have combination skin or curly hair flagged anywhere, it's just everything.
- "Spot it. Shop it." plus "First Access: Holiday Drops" plus Favorites Kits stacked at the bottom is the same sprawl I flagged in the 9/11 sends two days running — Sephora keeps escalating the digest instead of trimming it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the actual sale — if it's "50% off select beauty," show me the sale products immediately after the hero, not a full-price fragrance launch and a 12-item new-arrivals wall.
- 2. Fix or clarify the points display at the top — a Rouge member seeing "0 points" undermines the whole loyalty pitch.
- 3. Move Kilian, the new-arrivals grid, Favorites Kits, and Holiday Drops into a separate send — this is three campaigns' worth of content in one email.
- 4. Add a Rouge-specific callout near the offer (early access, bonus points multiplier) since I'm tier-eligible and there's no reason to treat me like a base Insider.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge exclusive: 50% off Clarins, Estée Lauder & more — today only`

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI — up to 50% off, today only`
