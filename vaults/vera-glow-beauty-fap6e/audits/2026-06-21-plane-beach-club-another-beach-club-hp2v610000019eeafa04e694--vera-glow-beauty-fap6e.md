### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`** — malformed directive. The correct token is `noindex` (no space). Browsers and crawlers that parse this strictly will ignore it. Low severity in email context since most clients strip head meta, but technically incorrect.
