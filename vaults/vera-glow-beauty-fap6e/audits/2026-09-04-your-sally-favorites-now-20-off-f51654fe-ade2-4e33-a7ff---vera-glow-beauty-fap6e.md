# Your Sally favorites, now 20% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Sally's back a day after "September deals are here!" and this one's actually a Labor Day sitewide sale, so at least there's a reason for the send. But it's still hair color, toners, and tint brushes — I flagged this exact mismatch on 9/3 and 9/1, and nothing's changed: no Sephora Rouge points math, no Ulta Diamond multiplier talk, this is a different loyalty universe entirely with its own "Sally Rewards" tiered points banner.
- The two friends with Sally shopping bags is a decent lifestyle shot, and 20% off sitewide with a clean code is honestly a stronger, clearer offer than the confetti-dot COLORfest sends I've been logging since July. But the product grid — perm booster, color sealer packette, blonde toner, tint brush — is professional hair-color-aisle stuff, not anything a Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond shopper like me is browsing for.
- I'm not clicking, not forwarding. This is a scroll-past-and-archive email, same as every other Sally send in my history.

## What's working

- 20% off sitewide with a real code (LABORDAY20) and a hard end date (September 7) is more concrete than the vague "deals are here" language from 9/3.
- The rewards points multiplier banner (2x/3x/4x by spend tier) is a legible, well-laid-out mechanic even if it's not my loyalty program.
- Klarna 4-interest-free-payments strip is a nice trust/convenience cue for anyone actually shopping this catalog.
- Photo up top feels current and seasonal (Labor Day, warm-weather styling) rather than stale.

## What's weak

- Every single product tile is hair color/toner/tint tools — nothing in my Sephora-Ulta cross-shop lane (skincare, prestige makeup, fragrance).
- No skin tone, skin type, or any personalization signal at all — this reads like a mass blast, not a "known customer" email.
- Zero mention of a beauty event calendar moment or launch-day anything, which is the thing that actually gets me to open a beauty email.
- Sephora's "Stacking Weekend" send I logged today scored 9/10 because it stacked points AND percent-off AND felt like it knew I was Rouge tier — this Sally email has a rewards program right there in the creative and doesn't even try to make it feel earned or personal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment sends by purchase history — if I've never bought hair color from Sally, don't lead every email with it; even a "Beauty From Head to Toe" section could pull in skincare/tools I'd actually browse.
- 2. Cut the redundant top bar — "Beauty in a Dash" and "Free 2 Hour Delivery" compete with the 20% off hero for the first screen; pick one CTA.
- 3. Tie the rewards banner to an actual account state ("You're at 1,200 points, spend $50 more for 3x") the way Sephora and Ulta do — right now it's generic tier math with no "this is you" moment.
