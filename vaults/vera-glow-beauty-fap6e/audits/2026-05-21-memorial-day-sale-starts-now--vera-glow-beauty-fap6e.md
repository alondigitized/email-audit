# ⭐ Memorial Day Sale starts NOW ⭐
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so after four straight Glossier emails of pure editorial vibes — the graduation Q&A, "Eau de main character," the full pet-UGC chaos from earlier today — they finally dropped an actual sale email. And honestly, the tonal whiplash is noticeable. The typographic SALE treatment in the hero is very Glossier — playful, a little chaotic in a good way — but everything underneath it feels like it was assembled quickly because they had to ship a Memorial Day send like everyone else in my inbox today.
- Here's the thing though: Glossier doesn't discount often, which means when they do, I actually pay attention. I saw the 50% OFF badges on those product tiles and my points-math brain turned on immediately. Drunk Elephant just ran a 25% Friends & Family event last week and Charlotte Tilbury did 20% off, so 50% off specific Glossier SKUs is legitimately the strongest deal signal I've gotten from a prestige brand this cycle. That part cuts through.
- Would I click? Yes, specifically to the 50%-off products. Would I forward to a friend? No — this isn't a "you have to see this" email, it's a "useful if you already know and like Glossier" email. Tap the tile, add to cart, done.

## What's working

- **50% OFF product badge callouts are doing heavy lifting** — product-level discount signals beat a sitewide percentage every time; my eye went straight to those tiles
- **The SALE typographic treatment in the hero is distinctly on-brand** — stacked creative letters instead of a generic "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" banner earns points for not looking like the Charlotte Tilbury "20% Off Is HERE!" boilerplate I got two days ago
- **Clean two-column product grid with visible CTAs** — scrollable, no broken columns, no layout chaos; tap targets felt reasonable on mobile
- **"Get a free Lip Gloss on us" GWP at the bottom** — free product offers convert me, especially when it's a real SKU not a mini sample

## What's weak

- **No end date anywhere in the email body** — "starts NOW" in the subject tells me nothing; is this a 48-hour window or does it run through next Wednesday? Drunk Elephant put "ends Sunday" right in the hero and that alone created real urgency
- **The actual discount copy is tiny under the hero graphic** — "Enjoy 30% off select favorites" in small text below that big SALE art is not how a deal-brain shops; I had to hunt for the percentage
- **"SHOP NOW" on every single CTA button** — six identical buttons read as a template, not a curated recommendation; after the Sally Beauty and e.l.f. volume I've been getting, lazy button copy is a skip signal
- **No loyalty or "insider" framing** — ILIA just sent me a VIP-gated offer; Charlotte Tilbury surfaces insider perks; Glossier treats me the same as someone who signed up yesterday and that gap shows

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an end date to the hero — full stop.** A single line like "Sale ends Monday, May 26" below the offer copy converts browsers into buyers. This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. **Swap every "SHOP NOW" for product-specific CTAs** — "Shop Lip Gloss," "Shop Eye Cream," "Shop Colorslide" — it tells me where I'm landing and makes each tile feel curated instead of templated.
- 3. **Move the 50% OFF product tiles above the 30% off tier** — lead with your strongest signal; right now the hero says 30% and the better deals are buried mid-scroll.
- 4. **Tie the free Lip Gloss GWP to a threshold** — "free with $50+ orders" feels more premium and prevents the offer from reading like a clearance move.

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off some of our faves this weekend only`
