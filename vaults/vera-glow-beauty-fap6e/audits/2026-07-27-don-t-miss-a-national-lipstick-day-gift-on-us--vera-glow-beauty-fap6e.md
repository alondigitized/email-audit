# Don’t miss a National Lipstick Day gift on us!  💄
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. AGAIN — this is now day nine or ten of basically nonstop inbox presence, and today's hook is "National Lipstick Day gift on us," which at least has a real occasion attached to it, unlike some of the wordplay-only sends they've been running. The offer itself is genuinely simple: free Cream Glide Lip Liner with any lippie purchase, code LIPPIE at checkout. I like a free-gift-with-purchase, I always do the math on whether GWP beats a percentage-off, and this one's clean — no minimum spend threshold buried anywhere I can see.
- The close-up lip shot at the top is gorgeous and on-brand, and then it's three back-to-back "build your own lip combo" modules (liner + oil, liner + balm, liner + stain) that all reuse the same free liner as the connective tissue — smart, actually, because it's cross-selling three price points off one gift hook instead of just dumping a product grid on me.
- Would I click? Probably, yeah — I'm a lip-liner person and free is free, and the code is right there in the hero so I don't even need to hunt for it. Would I forward to a friend? Not really, this isn't event-worthy enough to text someone about, but I'd screenshot the code for myself before it expires.

## What's working

- The hero copy names the actual holiday (National Lipstick Day) and the actual mechanic (free gift with purchase) in one breath — no vague "special surprise" nonsense.
- Code LIPPIE is visible in the hero module itself, not buried three scrolls down — Sephora's "50% off Sephora Collection lip faves" send I audited on 7/27 at least also led with a clear number, but e.l.f. beats it on giving me an actual code to type versus an auto-applied discount I have to trust is working.
- The three lip-combo modules each pair the free liner with a different finish (glossy, buttery, matte stain) — genuinely useful "which one am I" decision-support instead of one flat product wall.
- "Top Picks for You" section at the bottom feels like it's trying at personalization even if the products (primer, face cream, setting spray) have nothing to do with lips.
- Free shipping banner and the crossed-out AR/vegan/cruelty-free icon row near the bottom are trust cues I actually register as a Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond shopper who cares about that stuff.

## What's weak

- Nine-plus emails in nine-plus days and this is yet another promo — the cadence fatigue from my last several e.l.f. reviews hasn't let up, and "gift on us" doesn't feel special when it's this frequent.
- "Online Only" badge in the top corner is small and easy to miss on a phone — if I'm near a Target endcap later today expecting to redeem this, that's a letdown moment e.l.f. isn't preventing.
- No end date or countdown anywhere visible — Drunk Elephant's "SMS SOS" send at least implied urgency even when it was really just an SMS signup ask; here I have zero sense of whether LIPPIE dies tomorrow or next month, so there's no reason to act today versus whenever.
- "Top Picks for You" is not actually for me — a primer, a face cream, and a setting spray on a lipstick-day email is generic merchandising dressed up as personalization, and I can tell.
- Zero loyalty tie-in — no mention of e.l.f. Beauty Squad points multiplier on this purchase, which is a miss given how hard I track Sephora and Ulta points math in my head.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible deadline/countdown to the hero module — "gift on us" with no expiration is the single biggest lever here; even "ends Sunday" would create real urgency instead of implied urgency.
- 2. Make "ONLINE ONLY" bigger or move it into the body copy near the CTA, not just a tiny corner badge, so I don't get burned trying to redeem in-store.
- 3. Swap "Top Picks for You" for actual lip category adjacents (lip scrub, lip mask, a second liner shade) — right now it undercuts the personalization illusion by being obviously generic.
- 4. Call out Beauty Squad points on this purchase if there's a multiplier — give tier shoppers like me a reason to stack this with a bigger cart.
- 5. Trim send frequency — I said this on 7/26 and 7/25 too; even a great single email loses impact as the tenth one this month.
