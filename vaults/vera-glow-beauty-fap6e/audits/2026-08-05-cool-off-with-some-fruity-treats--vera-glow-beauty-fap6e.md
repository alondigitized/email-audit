# Cool off with some fruity treats
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Glossier's Balm Dotcom bench getting a flavor refresh — Black Cherry, Mango, Coconut — dressed up as a "suited for summer" fruit drop, and honestly? Cute, but slight. This isn't the NYC tube launch from 8/1 with the yellow-cab hero that actually felt like an event; it's closer to the 7/30 "Our go-to summer lip?" send, just with the GWP swapped out for three new tint flavors. I like Balm Dotcom, I own four of them, but "three new flavors of a $14 lip balm" isn't pulling me off my phone.
- The bubble-wrap hero shot is genuinely pretty — that pink cellophane texture against the tube colors is very "unbox me" — and the mango-with-real-mango-cubes shot is the standout of the three. But then I hit a real problem: on both the Black Cherry and Mango product blocks, the headline text is getting clipped off the right edge of the screen. "Black Cherry Balm Dotcom" and the mango description are literally running off-frame. That's not me being picky, that's a QA miss.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I needed a lip balm restock — Coconut sounds genuinely appealing as a gloss topper. Would I forward it? No. This is a "glance and archive" email, not a "send to the group chat" moment like the 8/1 NYC drop was.

## What's working

- The bubble-wrap hero photography is the best-looking asset in this email — glossy, tactile, on-brand for a "cool off" heat-relief hook.
- Mango Balm Dotcom's shot with real mango and strawberry chunks actually sells the flavor/scent story better than a plain product-on-white shot would.
- "Shop now" CTA is consistent and repeated after every single product — no ambiguity about what to do next.
- Coconut's "perfect as a topper" line is a smart use tip that speaks to how I actually layer lip products, not just generic copy.

## What's weak

- The right-edge text clipping on Black Cherry and Mango is a real render bug — I'm reading "Black Cherry Balm Dotcom" cut off mid-word and the mango description trailing into nothing. That reads broken, not intentional.
- Zero mention of price, and zero mention of a bundle deal for buying all three — if this is a flavor drop, give me a "get all 3" bundle CTA, don't make me add each one separately.
- No Rouge or Insider tie-in whatsoever — Glossier doesn't run a tier program the way Sephora/Ulta do, but there's also no "new drop, first dibs" urgency, no GWP mention at all this time, which is a change of pace from literally every recent send (8/3 keychain reminder, 7/30 mini bag).
- This is Glossier's fourth Balm Dotcom-centric email in about a week (7/29 starter kit, 7/30 lip tutorial, 8/1 NYC flavor, now this) — I'm starting to feel like the whole catalog is one product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped headline/body text on the Black Cherry and Mango blocks immediately — that's a basic rendering QA fail that makes the email look unfinished on whatever client/width rendered this.
- 2. Add a "Shop the trio" or bundle CTA above the three individual products — I don't want to tap "Shop now" three separate times when the whole pitch is "three flavors."
- 3. Give me a price or a bundle discount — Sally Beauty and ILIA both lead with a number in their sends this week; Glossier's leading with zero pricing signal here.
- 4. Vary the Balm Dotcom cadence — three lip-balm-centric sends in eight days is Skechers-level repetition (see their 7/29→8/2 repeat slip-in), and Glossier's better than that.
