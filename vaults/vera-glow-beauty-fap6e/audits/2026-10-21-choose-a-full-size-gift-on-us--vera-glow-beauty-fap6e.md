# Choose a full-size gift on us! 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the offer is good and the email buries it. A free full-size gift at $50 with no code is a real hook. But the hero image is a clear compact with a brush in pink shimmer, and I can't tell what the gift is. "Choose a full-size gift" with no gift visible is a tease with nothing behind it. This is the fifth e.l.f. send since 10/14, after the 10/20 skincare kits and the 10/19 "Bestie" roundup, so I'm already numb to the cadence.
- The black block with "these gifts are giving" and the white SHOP NOW pill is clean. Then it falls apart. "Top Picks for You" shows a $11 setting mist, an $11 primer and a $2 lip liner, and none of them is the gift. The nav is six gray slabs (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR), which is filler. I'd skim it, maybe click if I needed to restock the setting mist, and not forward it. It's a mark-as-read for me.

## What's working

- "Choose a full-size gift on us when you spend $50+. No code needed." is one clear threshold with no code to hunt for. I like that.
- The black block with the white SHOP NOW pill gives a clear first read, and the button is large enough to tap.
- The $11 / $11 / $2 prices sit right under each product. Ulta's "buy more, get more" emails make me scroll for prices, so this is easier.
- "ONLINE ONLY" and "limited time!" are stated up top, so the exclusivity isn't a surprise.

## What's weak

- The gift itself never appears. The hero is a mystery compact, and I'm being asked to spend $50 for something I can't see. Sephora's Beauty Insider sample-bag email (10/21) showed the bag and had a "Shop to Redeem" button, and it opened cleaner than this.
- The deadline sits in the 6-point fine print at the bottom (October 31, 11:59 PM PT). The body only says "limited time." Ulta's "Ends tonight" emails put the date in the subject.
- "Top Picks for You" is not personalized. Three random items at $11 and $2 won't get me anywhere near $50, and there's no threshold math like "add 2 more to unlock."
- The gray category slabs are dead weight. There's no imagery, no product, and no reason to tap.
- There's no member or Beauty Squad perk in the body. As an Ulta Diamond I want to see points or tier value. There's a "receipt scanning" tile at the very bottom, and that's it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the gift options in the hero. Put 3-4 full-size gift tiles with "Pick 1" instead of the mystery compact. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero: "Ends 10/31," not just "limited time!"
- 3. Replace the generic "Top Picks" with a "build your $50" row, like a $11 mist + $11 primer + bundle math, so I can see how to reach the threshold.
- 4. Replace the gray slabs with image tiles, or cut them.
- 5. Add one line for Beauty Squad members, such as "Earn double points on gift orders."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run found four of the twelve probed tracked links failing, a 0-character plain-text part, and no captured unsubscribe or authentication headers. The failures look like redirect-resolution errors. They may be probe artifacts, but that isn't confirmed, and the truncated HTML limits how much I could check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probed links ended in `unknown url type`.** Each is a click-tracked `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` URL whose redirect resolved to a relative path with no scheme or host:
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - "Shop Now" → `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Truth+or+Bare+-+Peachy+Pink`
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." → `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" → `/pages/mobile-app?lid=lp3450u51na1&deep_link_value=...`
- **The cause is unconfirmed.** Either the probe failed to resolve a relative `Location` header, or the redirect really returns a relative path. In the second case a browser would resolve it against `links.elfcosmetics.com` and could 404. Re-test these with a browser-equivalent client.
- **Other probe results:** 12 links were probed and 26 were skipped, so most footer and secondary links are unverified.
- **Tracked-link format:** the links use SendGrid-style `~~` wrappers with opaque payloads, so destination and UTM values can't be read from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible because the links are wrapped. The only visible parameters are on the redirect targets: `c=email` and `is_retargeting=true` on the app link, and `return_to=%2Faccount` on the login link. Capture the final landing URLs to verify UTMs.

6. Capture the final landing URLs for all links, including footer links, to validate UTM consistency.
