# 💘JUST YOUR TYPE 💘
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- I need to say this upfront: I already reviewed a "💘JUST YOUR TYPE 💘" from Sephora today. So when this hit my inbox again — same subject, same sender, same date — my immediate reaction was to check my email client for a glitch. It wasn't a glitch. That says something. After the double "New products have arrived" on the 13th (where I literally refreshed my inbox twice), Sephora is developing a habit of making me wonder if their email ops is as polished as their in-store experience.
- That said, I opened it — because Jo Malone Sea Salt & Bergamot in the hero and 50% off minis in the banner is a combination that works on me as a Rouge member who's always doing the points math. The top half of this email is doing real work. The bottom half is where it all falls apart: fragrance launch → editorial block → 3X POINTS → leave-in conditioners → Patrick Starr → Beauty Insider footer. That's not a curated send, that's Sephora's entire content calendar shoved into one scroll. Compare this to ILIA's "brown mascara, made mini" that I flagged recently — one product, one idea, immediate click. Sephora is doing the opposite.
- I'd scroll through and probably click the minis deal. I would not forward it. The Jo Malone hero deserves its own moment and the haircare pivot mid-email kills the fragrance mood completely.

## What's working

- **The minis deal is clear and prominent** — "Buy 2 Minis, Get Both 50% Off" right at the top banner, price reduction instant-readable, no math required.
- **Jo Malone as the featured launch feels editorial** — specific product, real price ($110.00), named fragrance. This is not "new arrivals"; it's a story.
- **"This is for YOU" hero copy** is simple and aspirational without feeling like a mass-blast. It carries.
- **3X POINTS is visible mid-email** — as Rouge, I always notice this and run the calculation. It's there even if it's undersold.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum. Everything loads.

## What's weak

- **The fragrance mood breaks the second I hit haircare.** Sephora knows my Rouge purchase history. Leave-in conditioners have no business showing up after a Jo Malone launch hero. That's a segmentation miss, not a content strategy.
- **Two competing offers, two competing buying occasions, zero clarity.** The 50% off minis and a $110 fragrance launch are different shopping intents. Stacking them at the top creates friction before I've even decided what this email is about.
- **No deadline on the minis deal.** e.l.f. Hair ran "2nd restock, act fast!" and I clicked immediately. Sephora says "50% off" with no end date and I'll tab it and forget it.
- **"SHOP NOW" on every CTA.** Generic. If Jo Malone gets "Shop Now," Patrick Starr gets "Shop Now," and the conditioner section gets "Shop Now," the phrase becomes visual noise.
- **Same subject twice in one day.** Even if this is a segmented resend to a different cohort, there's no world where sending identical subject lines to the same inbox on the same date feels intentional rather than broken.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one story per send.** This should be the Jo Malone fragrance launch email or the minis event email — not both. The two-hero structure is killing the editorial credibility Sephora spends its brand equity building. ILIA never does this; each send is one idea.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the minis deal.** "Ends Sunday" in the banner. That's all. Without it, the offer floats and I feel zero urgency to click now versus next week.
- 3. **Cut haircare from this send entirely.** If Sephora's data shows I buy fragrance, this is not where the leave-in conditioner belongs. Send that separately or to a different segment.
- 4. **Elevate the 3X POINTS tie-in.** If you're running a points multiplier during a prestige launch, lead with the math: "Earn 3X points on Jo Malone today." That's a Rouge-specific reason to click right now, and it's buried in the lower third.
- 5. **If this is a resend, change the subject.** Even "Another look: your type" signals intent rather than looking like a technical error.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Rouge bonus: 3X points on Jo Malone today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Jo Malone drop + minis at 50% off — through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sea Salt & Bergamot just landed. Plus: buy 2 minis, get both half off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge members earn 3X points on the new Jo Malone launch — today only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issue — viewport `maximum-scale=1`:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
This disables user-initiated zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 — Resize Text). Users with low vision cannot enlarge content. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**No dark mode CSS detected:** No `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block is present in the embedded stylesheet. On iOS Mail and macOS Mail with dark mode enabled, black text on dark backgrounds or inverted image colors is possible. Increasingly standard to include.

**`robots` meta value has a space:**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
Correct value is `noindex` (no space). While most crawlers handle this gracefully, it is technically incorrect per the spec.
