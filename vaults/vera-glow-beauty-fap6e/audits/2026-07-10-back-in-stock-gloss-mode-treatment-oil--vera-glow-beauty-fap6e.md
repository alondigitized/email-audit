# Back in stock! ✨💛 Gloss Mode Treatment Oil
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I genuinely did a small double take at the sender — "e.l.f. Hair" as a distinct sub-brand email is new for me. After four consecutive makeup-forward e.l.f. sends (the $20 lip combos on July 1, Soft Glam for under $30, blurreal, and the skincare duo yesterday), this is a real pivot, and I appreciate the category shift. The hero image is fun — curly-haired girl, yellow energy, "DRIP. DROP. SHINE WON'T STOP." actually made me smirk — and $15 for a treatment oil is the kind of price point where I don't even need a discount code to impulse grab it.
- But here's my issue: the subject says "back in stock" and the email body says "new!" Those are two completely different consumer states. Back in stock means I probably missed it, there's scarcity, I should move fast. New means I'm discovering something for the first time. You cannot run both messages simultaneously without confusing me. And then the footer closes with "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face." — in a hair email. No mention of hair. That's a copy-paste template error that fully undercuts the entire sub-brand moment they're trying to build.
- I'd mark this as read, probably. I might go look up the Treatment Oil myself out of curiosity but I'm not clicking from this specific email — the mixed signals killed my momentum.

## What's working

- **"DRIP. DROP. SHINE WON'T STOP."** is genuinely good copy — rhythmic, product-accurate, feels like a TikTok hook in email form
- **$15 price in the hero body copy** — at that price I don't need a sale, I need a vibe check, and the email delivers one
- **"Meet the mane crew"** lineup section is smart brand-building for a new sub-brand; shows the range without overwhelming me
- **"Top Picks for You"** personalization block with actual hair products ($8, $11, $9) — feels like they used my purchase history, not a random grid
- **"Online only" tag** on the Hottest Drops Duo section is the right call — creates channel urgency without a fake countdown

## What's weak

- **"New!" in the hero vs. "Back in stock!" in the subject** — these are mutually exclusive messages and running both simultaneously makes the whole send feel unedited
- **Footer tagline: "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face."** — there is no "hair" in that sentence. In a hair sub-brand email. This is a template ghost and it completely undermines the e.l.f. Hair identity they're trying to build; Lush's "The root of good hair" send this week felt more intentionally owning its hair moment than this does
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — for a $15 hero product with a specific name, "Grab the $15 Oil" or "Snag It Before It's Gone" would have converted me faster
- **The "Hottest Drops Duo on us" offer is unreadable** at email width — the fine print text about buy-5-get conditions is too small to parse, and I actually want to know what that deal is

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one message — scarcity OR new launch — not both.** If this is a back-in-stock trigger (which the subject claims), lean all the way in: "It sold out. It's back. Here's why." The hero copy should mirror the subject.
- 2. **Fix the footer tagline** before this sub-brand does another send. "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, face — and now your hair." takes 10 seconds to update and makes the sub-brand moment feel intentional.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA** from "SHOP NOW" to something product-specific — "Grab the $15 Treatment Oil" or "Get It Before It Sells Out Again."
- 4. **Make the Hottest Drops Duo offer legible** — bump the font, pull the core condition ("spend $35, get the duo free") into a single scannable headline.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `It sold out once. The $15 Treatment Oil is back. ✨`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `The glossy hair oil everyone grabbed — back in stock 💛`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Strengthens + shines for $15. Online only, while it lasts.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `The Gloss Mode Treatment Oil that sold out is yours again — for $15.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built in Braze with standard MSO/table layout. Two confirmed rendering defects: a double-nested container structure (builder artifact) and a 9px mobile heading — both are code-level bugs, not design choices.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-tracking domain:** All hrefs route through `links.elfcosmetics.com` (Braze click-tracking). Expected, but the redirect chain should be validated end-to-end for HTTPS-only hops and correct final destination.

**Image asset age:** The hero logo is served from:
```
https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/6532bc91543698004cb67f6e/original.png?1697823889
```
The query-string timestamp `1697823889` decodes to **October 2023** — a recycled asset. Verify this is intentional for a "back in stock" email that may have product-specific imagery requirements.

**UTM parameters:** Not visible in the pre-redirect href strings in the provided source. Confirm UTMs are appended post-redirect or injected by Braze; they are not present in the raw source links.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — 9px mobile heading:**
```css
@media (max-width:660px) {
  .row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1 { font-size: 9px !important }
}
```
9px is below the WCAG minimum (~14px) and practically unreadable on any mobile device. This appears to be an accidental override, not intentional sizing.

**Double-nested container (builder artifact):** The `html_block block-1` div contains a complete duplicate `nl-container → row-1 → row-content stack` structure, wrapping the logo inside a second full email skeleton. This redundant nesting can cause box-model issues in Outlook and Gmail:
```html
<table class="html_block block-1" ...>
  <tr><td class="pad"><div ...>
    <!-- Entire nl-container/row-1/row-content structure re-declared here -->
    <table class="nl-container" ...>  ← duplicate root
```

**`tabindex="-1"` on logo link:**
```html
<a href="..." tabindex="-1">
```
Removes the logo link from keyboard tab order entirely. Screen-reader and keyboard-only users cannot navigate to it.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Yahoo Mail and some webmail clients display this in tab labels. Should be set to the subject line or brand name.

**Google Fonts reliability:** `Jost` and `Open Sans` are loaded via `<link>` tags (inside `<!--[if !mso]>` conditional). Gmail strips external `<link>` tags — Jost has no declared fallback in the visible source (only `Open Sans, Arial, Sans-serif` appears in the div styles). If Jost is used for body copy elsewhere in the template, those elements will fall through to Arial in Gmail without a matching stack.

No merge tokens (e.g., `{{${first_name}}}` or `*|FNAME|*`) visible in the provided source. Cannot confirm personalization state from truncated HTML — verify in Braze that any intended first-name insertion is present and has a valid default fallback.

DMARC alignment requires `m.elfcosmetics.com` to be covered by the `elfcosmetics.com` DMARC record (`adkim=r` relaxed mode covers subdomains).
