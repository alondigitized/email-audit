# 20% off all lip + cheek favorites
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "color, unlocked" — pretty visual, I'll give ILIA that, the tassel-and-lipstick flatlay actually looks like a nice styled desk shot instead of another sale banner. But the offer is gated behind "JOIN VIP" and I already have to squint at that "want the key to 20% off... plus a free Multi-Stick?" copy to even understand what's being asked of me — is this a new signup or am I already in? After the 9/12 "VIP Sale starts now" (8/10, the actual lever-pull), then 9/13's "VIPs get the best + 20% off" (7/10) and 9/14's "makeup that makes your skin better" (7/10), this is now the FOURTH VIP-flavored send from ILIA in four days and I'm fatigued. It's the same 20% number recycled with a different hero shot.
- This is a scroll-and-forget for me — I'm Rouge tier at Sephora and Diamond at Ulta, I don't need to "join" anything for a lip gloss discount, that's table stakes at my regular two. Not forwarding this, not clicking past the flatlay.

## What's working

- The hero flatlay (tassel bag charm, lip gloss, blush) is genuinely pretty — better production value than the plain-text ILIA sends I've gotten this week.
- Product grid is clean — Overglaze Lip Gloss, Lip Sketch Crayon, Soft Focus Blush, Color Haze all have their own shot + one-line benefit + Shop Now, so I know exactly what's in "lip + cheek."
- The complimentary-upgrades section (free Multi-Stick on all orders, free velvet bag on $100+) adds real incremental value on top of the 20%, which none of ILIA's other VIP sends this week called out.
- "very. important. perks." section lists points, free shipping, early access — that's the loyalty-math stuff I actually care about, even if it's generic.

## What's weak

- Fourth VIP-branded send in four days — I already flagged escalating VIP fatigue on 9/13 and 9/14, and this is the same well, just re-skinned as a lip+cheek bundle.
- The lede buries whether I'm already VIP or need to join — "want the key to 20% off... plus a free Multi-Stick" reads like bait copy, not a clear member perk.
- No shade-matching or skin-tone personalization anywhere, which matters a lot for lip/cheek color product — Glossier's "Brow down" send actually leaned into real faces and reviews, this one is all product-on-white/product-on-fabric with zero human skin tone reference.
- No urgency or end date visible — compare to Ulta's "ENDS TODAY!" 20%-off send (6/10) which at least forces a decision; this just floats.
- Category framing ("lip + cheek favorites") doesn't distinguish this from the 9/12 sitewide VIP sale — feels like the same event getting re-cut four different ways.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the VIP-gate ambiguity** — lead with "Members: your 20% is live" or "Not a member? Join free + get 20% today" as two distinct paths, not one blended sentence.
- 2. **Add a real deadline** — Sephora and Ulta both use event windows I can plan around; ILIA's sale has now run four days with no visible end, which kills urgency entirely.
- 3. **Show it on skin** — at least one swatch-on-skin or diverse-tone shot for the blush/lip color grid; right now it's catalog photography with zero color-payoff cues.
- 4. **Differentiate this send from 9/12-9/14** — if it's the same sale, say "last call" or "new: lip + cheek edit," don't just reskin the hero.
- 5. **Pull the free Multi-Stick/bag perk above the fold** — that's the actual incremental hook that's new this week, and it's currently buried two-thirds down.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free velvet bag on orders $100+`
