# 20% off makeup + skincare + SPF
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Nine. Nine ILIA emails since May 16, and this is the second one today — "Feat. the latest + greatest" already landed earlier this morning, scored an 8, and now here comes "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" before I've even finished my coffee. I have been counting obsessively, the way you count something that started charming and is now wearing on you.
- Here's the honest thing: I still opened it. "20% off makeup + skincare + SPF" reroutes my brain every time. Yesterday's "base layer: 20% off skincare" email — which I scored a 9 — had me tab over to my ILIA cart. Today they've opened the gates to full-face: makeup, skincare, and SPF. For a Rouge member who stocks up on Super Serum Skin Tint whenever there's a real percent off, that category expansion is genuinely meaningful. The hero shot is beautiful — glowing close-crop skin, aspirational without being airbrushed to oblivion — and the editorial sections that follow ("For All Types" with shade swatches, "Sensitive at best," "Blonde Pores") do actual targeting work. The "Earn 1 Million" section near the bottom stopped my scroll because anything hinting at points multiplication is my kryptonite.
- But the 20% off is not the visual hero. The brand tagline is. After nine emails selling me this event, I need the offer in the first blink. I'd click, I'd probably spend, and then I'd mute this list for a week. I'd forward it — but only to my one friend who's Rouge and has already stress-texted me about the skin tint twice this week.

## What's working

- "makeup that makes your skin better™" as a hero line is ILIA's best brand asset — clean, on-skin close-up, zero hard-sell energy, earns the editorial trust before asking for money
- "For All Types" with the circular shade swatch grid gives me instant inclusivity read — I'm checking for my undertone instinctively
- Skin-type editorial sections ("Sensitive at best," "Blonde Pores") are legitimately useful targeting; Charlotte Tilbury blasted three emails today — *three* — with zero of this texture-level persona work
- "Earn 1 Million" section stopped my scroll — any loyalty-milestone language reads like a reward cue to a Rouge member, even before I know the mechanic
- Renders clean: no broken images, no phantom gaps, no overlapping text — Drunk Elephant's F&F email this week held the same standard, and it matters for perceived brand premium

## What's weak

- The 20% off is not the visual hero — the tagline is. After nine emails in this sale push, I need the offer front-and-center in the first 300px, not after the brand story
- This is email two today, and it rhymes too closely with yesterday's skincare-only 20% off — subscribers who already bought yesterday now feel like they missed the full-category deal, which breeds quiet resentment
- No visible deadline anywhere in the scroll — Sephora's sale email this week told me exactly when the window closed; ILIA's open-endedness gives me permission to tab it for later indefinitely, which means I probably don't buy today
- "Cart Deco" product-rec grid at the bottom undercuts the editorial premium built above it — Glossier doesn't pollute their editorial-to-sale emails with generic recommendation filler
- "SHOP NOW" is the laziest verb in beauty email; "Get 20% off Skin Tint" or "Save on SPF" gives me a reason to click *now* instead of *later*

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero hierarchy** — put the 20% off offer as a bold text block or colored ribbon ABOVE the brand tagline in the hero zone; the tagline can live below and support, but the offer has to own the first blink
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline** — "Offer ends Sunday at midnight" or a visible countdown changes a "maybe later" into a decision for a loyalty-math brain like mine
- 3. **Acknowledge yesterday's buyers** — a one-line module: "Bought skincare yesterday? Here's what to add at 20% off" with 2-3 makeup SKUs rewards the prior-day purchase instead of ignoring it
- 4. **Retire "Cart Deco"** — replace with a curated "complete your routine" unit that bridges the skincare-focused May 21 send to today's full-face offer
- 5. **Name the "Earn 1 Million" mechanic** — I can see it but I can't tell if it's a Sephora Points event, a milestone, or just aspirational copy; give it a verb and a number I can act on
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off everything: skin tint, mascara, SPF`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Full-face 20% off — makeup + skincare + SPF today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Yesterday was skincare. Today it's your whole routine — 20% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skin tint, setting spray, AND sunscreen — all 20% off. Offer ends soon.`
