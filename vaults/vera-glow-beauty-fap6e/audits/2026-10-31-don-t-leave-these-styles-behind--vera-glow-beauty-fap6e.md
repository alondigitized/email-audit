# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Skechers is back in my inbox for what feels like the twelfth "you left something behind" nudge. I still haven't put a single thing in a cart. "WE SAVED THESE STYLES FOR YOU" is a polite lie. Nothing was saved, and the six shoes are generic women's sneakers I never looked at. The 20% off Plus-member band sits below the fold and is dressed up as a Halloween treat. It's the real offer, and it's buried under a blue hero that sells nothing.
- Compared to the 10/30 "Did you forget something?" and "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends, this one is at least a product grid and not another empty cart. Still, it's the same cadence and the same fake-personalization trick. I'd skim it, do the points math (20% off, members only, online only, ends 11/2, so nothing stacks), and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd click only if I needed a white sneaker today.

## What's working

- The six-shoe grid is real product, not a blank cart, and each shoe has a name and a material (Leather) I can read.
- The 20% OFF block is big, high-contrast, and has a "Valid thru 11/2" date. That's the only credible urgency in the email.
- The dark navy band is the one place my eye lands first on scroll, so the offer hierarchy works once I get to it.
- The footer is tidy: app, curbside pickup, Afterpay/Klarna. I know where to go next.

## What's weak

- "We saved these styles for you" implies personalization that isn't there. After eleven fake-cart emails, I notice.
- The offer is below the hero, the grid, and a lot of white space. Sephora puts its 30% right in the top banner. Ulta leads with "Up to 50% off" in the subject line itself. Here I have to scroll past six shoes to find the discount.
- The offer is Skechers Plus members only, and it only shows up in the band and the fine print. If I'm not a member, I'm unsure whether it applies to me. That feels like exclusionary language, not an honest offer.
- The product links are small, underlined text with no price, no sale tag, and no rating. I can't judge value, so I have nothing to click on.
- There's a fat nav list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) plus a texting block, app badges, and BNPL logos. It reads like a template. The email has no real point of view.
- The images aren't broken, but they're low-res and uneven, with mismatched crops and spacing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% off into the hero, above the shoes. Lead with the offer and the deadline, and put the shoes underneath.
- 2. Stop claiming "we saved these for you" unless it's true. Use "Plus members: 20% off ends 11/2," or show items I actually browsed.
- 3. Add the original price, the sale price, and a star rating under each shoe. Make each product an actual button or tap-sized card.
- 4. Say what Skechers Plus is and whether I'm in it. A line like "You're a Plus member: your 20% is applied at checkout" would do it.
- 5. Cut the footer clutter. Keep one app link and one store finder, and drop the other utility blocks.
- 6. Space the sends out. The last four days have been near-identical.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 leather sneakers, 20% off through 11/2`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members-only price on slip-ins and Arch Fit. Online only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pink Zingers, Cozy Fit leather, and more. Ends 11/2.`
