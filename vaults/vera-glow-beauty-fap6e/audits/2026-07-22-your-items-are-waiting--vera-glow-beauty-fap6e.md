# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- I have already seen "Your Items Are Waiting!" from Skechers. Six days ago. July 16th. Exact same subject line, sitting in my inbox again like Skechers thinks I have amnesia. This isn't a drip sequence — it's copy-paste, and by the time a subject line repeats verbatim inside a week, my brain has already filed it under "ignore this sender."
- That said, the email itself is doing competent cart-abandonment work. "We Noticed You Noticing Us" is cheeky copy that works once, and surfacing the actual Cate Bears and Star Line shoes I left in my cart — front and center, with a real 25% off code for Plus members — is exactly how a recover email should be built. The blue "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is unmissable. The hero hierarchy is clean.
- Then I hit the recommendations section and Skechers serves me a grid of **Men's** styles. Men's Arch Boot Sneaker. Men's Flex Advantage. Men's Skecho National. I have women's shoes in my cart. This is a data targeting fail, not a design problem, and it bleeds credibility out of every personalized signal the hero just built. I'd mark this read and walk away — same as I did six days ago.

## What's working

- "WE NOTICED YOU NOTICING US..." is genuinely playful copy — it earns a smirk the first time you read it
- All three hero cart items (Star Line, Cate Bears Party, Brighton This Day) are front and center with real product names and clean photography — no guessing which shoes they want me to buy
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" CTA is bright blue against white and impossible to scroll past
- The Skechers Plus 25% off banner is visible and the code (SAVINGS) is printed right on it — no buried-link hunting required
- Cate Bears colorway — the pink floral playfulness — reads fun and age-appropriate, demographic-adjacent to me even if it's shoes not skincare

## What's weak

- This is the same subject line from July 16. That's not a nurture sequence — that's a file-find-replace failure, and the second time I see it I have zero reason to believe urgency is real
- The cross-sell recommendation grid is almost entirely Men's styles — I have women's shoes in my cart; surfacing "Men's Arch Boot Sneaker" as something I'll love is a glaring data mis-fire
- The 25% off is gated by Plus membership **and** $100+ spend — the banner makes it look like a broad offer until you hit the fine print, which is the exact bait-and-switch feeling that erodes trust
- The footer is a five-module jumble: texting opt-in, app download, AfterPay, curbside pickup, store locator — all competing for a tap after I've already been asked to complete my order

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop recycling the subject line** — "Your Items Are Waiting!" already aired on July 16. This single change is the highest-impact fix. Even personalizing to the hero product (Cate Bears) would break the pattern.
- 2. **Fix the gender targeting in cross-sell** — if my cart has women's shoes, my "you'll love" grid should surface women's styles. This is a segmentation/data issue, not creative, and it actively undercuts the personalization story the hero is trying to tell.
- 3. **Reframe the 25% off clearly** — lead with the threshold: "Spend $100, save 25% — code SAVINGS." Don't let the banner read as a blanket offer and then hide the $100+ qualifier in the fine print.
- 4. **Collapse the footer to two utility modules max** — app download and store locator, pick two. The current layout is a scroll graveyard after the primary CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still saving your Cate Bears — 25% off for Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cart + 25% off = complete it today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAVINGS. Spend $100, save 25%. Your Star Line is waiting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus member perk inside — here's your actual reason to come back.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-tracking domain** — All links are routed through `skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=…`. This is Attentive's standard redirect. No issues with the mechanism itself, but:

- The `web version` link (`https://skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=u001.LNc6Vor…`) resolves through the redirect chain. Destination URL is not inspectable from source alone; UTM parameters cannot be confirmed present on the landing URL without following the redirect.

**Potential issue:** If the UPN tokens embed the destination URL without UTM params appended by Attentive's redirect, sessions will arrive untagged. Requires redirect-follow verification.
