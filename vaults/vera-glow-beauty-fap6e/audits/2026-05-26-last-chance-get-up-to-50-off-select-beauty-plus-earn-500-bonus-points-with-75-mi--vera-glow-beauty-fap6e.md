# LAST CHANCE 🥺 Get up to 50% off select beauty—plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 min. spend.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Stacking Weekend is my Super Bowl, and I've had this sale on my calendar since Sephora's first "Save up to 50% + 500 bonus points" email hit my inbox on May 22 — which actually landed *twice* that day. So by the time this "LAST CHANCE" send arrives on May 26, I'm already mid-checkout. As a Rouge member who runs the points math obsessively, the combination of half-off pricing stacked with bonus points stacked with Beauty Insider Cash is the one event I use to liquidate my wishlist. This email knows that. It opens with the full offer stack in the hero and surfaces BI Cash earn AND redeem details before I scroll a single pixel.
- Where it loses me is in the inbox, before I even open. A 94-character subject line that wraps into gibberish on mobile, and this being the third nearly-identical send in four days — that cadence fatigue is real. I'm also done with the store locator block mid-sale-email; I didn't open a flash sale push to look at a map.
- I'm clicking regardless because I planned to. But the email is coasting on the event's own equity more than it's earning the conversion. I'd forward the Rhode limited-edition section to a friend who's been eyeing it — that product feature is doing quiet heavy lifting at the bottom.

## What's working

- **Offer stack is immediately legible** — 50% off + 500 bonus points + $75 minimum spend are all parsed in the hero without decoding layered copy
- **"ENDS TODAY" badge earns its urgency** — this IS the close-out send, so the deadline feels honest rather than manufactured
- **Beauty Insider Cash called out explicitly** — both earn and redeem visible above the fold, which signals they know they're talking to a loyalty member, not a cold prospect
- **Rhode limited-edition feature** — not on sale, but dropping it into a "last chance" event email is a smart editorial move that gives me a gifting reason to click past the discount section
- **"LAST CHANCE to Save on These Must-Haves" product grid** gives the email real browsability and something to anchor intent below the hero

## What's weak

- **"LAST CHANCE" all-caps in a 94-character subject** — after two identical sends on May 22, this escalation reads like Charlotte Tilbury's "20% OFF!!" eight-email death spiral, and it erodes trust faster than it builds urgency
- **Zero personalization on the loyalty hook** — three Stacking Weekend sends and I still don't see my points balance or where I sit on the Rouge threshold; Ulta at least labels my Diamond tier even when the email is otherwise lazy
- **Store locator block breaks the sale flow completely** — a map and store hours dropped mid-scroll on an online flash sale email is an unconverted exit waiting to happen
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is completely generic** — after the most personalized loyalty event in the Sephora calendar, sending me to a button that says the same thing as every Lush email feels like a missed segmentation shot

## Recommendations

- 1. **Surface my Rouge points balance and tier gap** in the hero subtext — "You have 847 points. $75 today = 1,347 points." That one line converts Rouges better than any percentage badge.
- 2. **Cut the subject to under 50 characters** and move the offer specifics to the preheader — the full offer stack in the subject is getting clipped on mobile where it matters most.
- 3. **Kill the store locator block** — this is an online-purchase email during a limited-time event; the map section adds scroll depth and zero conversion value.
- 4. **Swap "SHOP NOW" for a category-split CTA** — "Shop Skincare Sale" and "Shop Makeup Sale" as side-by-side buttons, or at minimum "Shop the Sale" to distinguish from the dozen generic CTAs in everyone else's send this week.
- 5. **Add a CTA to the Rhode limited-edition feature** — the section has a product, a name, a beautiful shot, and no button. That's a click left on the table at a high-intent moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 50% off + 500 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend ends tonight — don't leave points on the table`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Rouge perks stack with the sale. Here's your window.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off select beauty + earn 500 bonus points with $75.`
