# Top-shelf beauty, right this way 👉🏽
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- The banner is the best part of this email again. The "Sephora Savings Event: Coming Soon" strip with 30% off for Rouge and 10% off for Insider is the only thing that makes me do math. Everything below it is a product catalog that could have gone to anyone. It's the same banner I got on 10/28 under "🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟", just with a different subject and a shark-hair-tool hero. That's the seventh-plus Sephora send in about a week, so my inbox is a Sephora wall.
- The "Top Shelf" hero and the Shark Beauty limited-edition holiday collection are pretty and clear enough. The product grid under "Discover Your Next Indulgence" (Sol de Janeiro, Paula's Choice, Fenty Beauty, Givenchy) has real names and real prices, which I like. But nothing in it says "you, Rouge, in this shade, at this tier." Nothing tells me how many points I'd bank or whether my 30% applies to any of it. Ulta would have put my Diamond status and points balance up top. e.l.f. made two lip products for $11 easy to understand in one glance. Here I'm decoding fine print.
- Would I click? Probably, but for the event, not for this email. I'd screenshot the banner, put the dates in my calendar, and start a wishlist. I'd forward it to a friend only if she's also a Rouge who stocks up, and even then I'd text the dates, not the email.

## What's working

- The Rouge 30% / Insider 10% split in the top banner is exactly the tier math I care about, and "Build Your Basket" is a good pre-sale nudge.
- Real product names with prices ($24 lip liner, $49 Sol de Janeiro set, $134 Givenchy, $48 Fenty foundation) let me compare value instantly.
- "New," "Limited Edition" and "Only at Sephora" tags on each product are launch-day signals I actually track.
- The Clean at Sephora badges on a couple of products are a nice trust cue for ingredient-conscious shoppers.
- The holiday gift guide strip, with skincare, makeup, hair and fragrance buttons, fits the season and is easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The top banner says "Coming Soon" and "Starts Tomorrow" in tiny text. The actual dates are small and the exclusions are tiny, so I can't tell at a glance when my 30% kicks in.
- The email never acknowledges that I'm Rouge. There's no points balance, no tier progress and no "your 30% starts X" line. It treats me like a generic Insider.
- The hero ("Top Shelf: the lineup you'll keep coming back to") is vague. I can't tell what the offer is or why today.
- The Seth Rogen "Anyone can gift beauty" block feels off-brand for me. It's a big visual that pulls attention from the products and the event.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of text. It's honest, but it makes the offer feel buried and conditional.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put my tier in the banner. For example: "Rouge: 30% off starts tomorrow. You'll earn X points on a $Y basket." That one change would make this feel like it was sent to me.
- 2. Pick one hero with one CTA. Either the event countdown or the Shark Beauty launch, not both, and put the dates in large type.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4 products and tie each to a tier price, such as "Rouge price after 30%: $X."
- 4. Move the Seth Rogen gifting block below the gift guide, or drop it from the send.
- 5. Space out sends. A week of near-identical Sephora emails is why I skim them.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a 700px fixed-width XHTML table layout with ESP click-tracking redirects. The automated QA run has 1 FAIL (no plain-text part) and 3 WARNs, and several deliverability and compliance items can't be verified from the data supplied. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and most link content were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Links are wrapped in ESP redirects, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1887697f582fec2369fea4b34/c652418d…?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gvf-…`. The final destinations can't be read from the source.
- **Link probing:** 12 links were probed and 44 skipped. No per-link status codes were provided, so no broken links are confirmed. The 44 skipped links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all"/>` is hidden. Clients that block images won't register opens, so open data is unreliable. This is expected behavior, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** The text version is 0 chars. A multipart/alternative with no text body hurts deliverability and breaks text-only clients. This could be a relay capture artifact, but it can't be ruled out.
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** The tracking pixel `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…'>` has no `alt`. It is non-visual, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` anyway.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The `@media (max-width:480px)` rules only resize a few classes (`.mobileHeaderLogo`, `.mobileIcon`, etc.). Without `max-width:100%` or stacking behavior on this table, it likely forces horizontal scroll on narrow clients that ignore the media query. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm the rest of the layout.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments aren't valid there, and some parsers will drop the rule that follows it.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Unsupported JSON-LD:** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block is stripped by most clients. It also contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is the same for every send.
- **Preheader:** The `display:none` preheader is `Worth showing off.`, followed by a `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. Nested `div`s are well-formed.
