# What's the scent of the summer?
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Pick of the Bunch" — Lush is doing a full fruit-scented lineup today, and visually this is genuinely one of the more fun grids they've sent me. Orange, lime, mango, coconut, rose, apple, toffee apple — the color-blocked photography with hands actually squeezing fruit and product is tactile in a way that stands out in my inbox. But I'm scrolling through four full scent categories — Very Zest, Berry Tropical Vibes, Juicy Jams, plus a random "We Like Big Bags" packaging callout — and there is not one single offer, discount, or code anywhere. Compare that to Ulta's "Lucky you! 20% off" send from earlier this week, which had a real code, a real deadline, and still managed a cute hero shot — Lush gave me zero of that.
- This is also just... a catalog. No new-launch framing (this reads like existing SKUs re-photographed for summer), no member perk, no personalization to my skin type or scent history despite me being a repeat opener. After Lush's own "Less is more" no-offer manifesto email on 7/26 scoring a flat 5, and the Moomin collab riding high on novelty at 7, this feels like a step back into pure aesthetic-no-substance territory. I'd scroll through it because the visuals are genuinely pretty, but I'm not clicking anything specific — there's no one product I'm being pointed to, just vibes. Not forwarding this to a friend, not opening my wallet.

## What's working

- The photography is legitimately eye-catching — hands physically squeezing an orange, cracking a coconut, holding a peach — it's tactile and fresh in a way a lot of flat product-grid emails aren't.
- Four distinct scent-family sections (Very Zest, Berry Tropical, Juicy Jams) gives some structure and lets me self-sort by mood, which beats Glossier's flat-lay tote email from earlier in July that had zero organizing principle.
- Product names are specific and paired with format (Orange Body Scrub, Toffee Apple Bath Bomb) so I know exactly what I'd be buying, not just a vague lifestyle shot.
- The "$5 off with the Lush app" banner up top is at least one small hook, even if it's buried in a thin black strip I almost skipped past.

## What's weak

- Zero sitewide offer or launch framing on any of these — this is a re-skinned existing catalog with a seasonal bow on it, and after "Less is more" two days ago also skipping the discount, that's twice in a row Lush has asked me to just admire the brand instead of buy something.
- No member/rewards tie-in at all — I track my Sephora Rouge points and Ulta Diamond tier without even trying, and Lush doesn't even nod at its own loyalty program here.
- The CTA "Squeeze The Day" only lives up top under the hero — once I scroll into the actual product grids there's no per-item "shop now," just static images and text, so there's real friction getting from "I like this peach massage bar" to an actual product page.
- No skin type, fragrance-profile, or ingredient callouts — for a brand built on "naked packaging" and fresh ingredients messaging, I'd expect at least a scent-note or ingredient blurb, and there's none.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in the hero — even 15% off the summer scent edit would turn this from a lookbook into a reason to buy. Right now Ulta and e.l.f. are both giving me codes this week and Lush is giving me mood boards.
- 2. Make each product image clickable to its own PDP with a mini "shop" tag, not just a static grid — right now the only functional CTA is at the very top.
- 3. Add one line of scent-note or ingredient detail per hero product (e.g., "Orange Body Scrub — cold-pressed orange oil + sea salt") to tie back into Lush's actual naked-ingredients story instead of just color and vibes.
- 4. Tie this to the Lush Rewards program visibly in the body, not just as a footer icon — give repeat customers like me a reason to feel this is "for me" and not a mass blast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fruit-scented favorites, 15% off this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer scent lineup just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Orange, coconut, peach + more — shop the fresh fruit edit`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 scent families, one juicy sale (ends Sunday)`
