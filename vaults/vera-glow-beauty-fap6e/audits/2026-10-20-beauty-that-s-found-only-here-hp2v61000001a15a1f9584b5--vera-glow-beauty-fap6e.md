## What's weak

- Three competing heroes stack at the top: the 4X banner, the sample bag and the "Only at Sephora" block. The eye doesn't know where to land.
- The sample bag has a lot of asterisks ("FREE*", "$35+ purchase"). It's a free bag with strings, and it's buried in exclusions.

- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. I can't read it on a phone without zooming.
- Subject and body mismatch: "found only here" doesn't mention the 4X points, which is the only urgent thing in the email.
