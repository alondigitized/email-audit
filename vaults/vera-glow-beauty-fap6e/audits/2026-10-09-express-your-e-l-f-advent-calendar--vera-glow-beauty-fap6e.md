# Express Your e.l.f. Advent Calendar 🎁🎄🎨 
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the waitlist from 10/6 paid off. The advent calendar is out and the hero is clean: purple, a big "just dropped!", and the box with its numbered doors. I can do the math at a glance, $95 for a "$153 value", which works out to about 38% off and roughly $4 a door. That's the kind of thing I screenshot into my group chat.
- Where it loses me is everything under the hero. After the price, I get a long stack of stickers and emoji lines ("sleigh the holidays", "full & mini sizes"). It never tells me what's actually behind the doors. Sephora and Ulta both show you the lineup, and I'm not paying $95 for "new exclusive faves." I'd click to see the contents, but I wouldn't forward it yet. This is also roughly the tenth e.l.f. send I've logged since 9/28, and the one after the 10/7 Gold Drip email. The cadence is wearing on me.

## What's working

- The price is huge and unmissable. "OMG Price: $95" with $153 struck through sits on the product photo and again mid-email, so I never have to hunt for it.
- The hero is dense but readable. The purple background, big white lowercase headline, and black "Shop Now" pill make a clear first read.
- The calendar photo makes it feel like a real gift, with makeup, skincare, and brushes scattered around the box. It's very giftable and very Millennial.
- "Just dropped!" is real launch-day language. It follows up the 10/6 waitlist email, so the payoff feels earned.
- The "Top Picks for You" row shows actual prices ($11, $2, $11), which is more than Glossier's Mini Wardrobe Kit send gave me.

## What's weak

- There's no list of what's inside. "24 makeup & skincare e.l.f.ssentials" is vague. Ulta's 21 Days and Sephora's calendars let me see the lineup, and I do points math on contents, not slogans.
- The middle section is a lot of decorative emoji and doodles for very little information. It's six lines of copy and a repeat of the price I already saw.
- The "Top Picks for You" products (a primer, a lip liner, setting spray) have nothing to do with the advent calendar. They read as generic bestsellers, not personalized to me.
- There's no urgency or stock signal. Advent calendars sell out, and "limited stock" or a ship-by date would move me. Nothing in the render has a deadline.
- The grey category bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) are big, empty-looking blocks. They push the footer far down and add nothing.
- There's no tier or points math at all. Sephora Insider's recent send at least had codes like MORE4U and HUDA4X, and ILIA's "Add 3, subtract 20%" was a one-line offer with a code. Here I get a "value" number and nothing that rewards me for being loyal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the contents. Put a 3x4 grid of "doors" with the hero items, or a "see what's inside" reveal, directly under the price, and cut the doodle section.
- 2. Add a real deadline or scarcity cue near the hero, such as "while supplies last" or "ships by Dec 1."
- 3. Show the per-door price or the savings ("just $3.96 a door" or "save $58") so the math is done for me.
- 4. Swap the generic Top Picks for calendar-adjacent items, or for stocking-stuffer gift sets.
- 5. Link CTAs to the calendar's product page and label them "Shop the calendar" instead of "Shop Now."
- 6. Add a member perk, or at least an e.l.f. rewards mention, near the offer. That's how Ulta and Sephora hook me.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is well-formed table-based markup with a preheader, `lang="en"` and `role="presentation"` tables. Four tracked links returned redirect targets the QA probe couldn't follow, and the message has no plain-text part. The QA pass rate is 27%, but most of that comes from probe limits and relay-stripped headers, not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probe failures, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The click-tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…~~`) answered with a relative `Location` value, so the probe had no host to request:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=…&lid=…&source_caller=ui&pid=Email&onelink_id=wTtZ&deep_link_value=com.elf…` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
- A relative `Location` is permitted by RFC 7231, and browsers resolve it against the tracker host. That would send users to `links.elfcosmetics.com/products/…`, which is probably wrong. If the tracker really emits host-less paths, these four links 404. If it only looked that way in the probe, they work. I can't tell which from the data, so treat them as **unverified, not confirmed broken**.
- The `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` link is a login wall, not a promotional landing page.
- 27 of 39 links were skipped by the prober, so the remaining links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars [FAIL].** There is no `text/plain` alternative, which hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify it on the raw original.

- Product links carry variant selectors (`Size=…`, `Color=…`), which is good for landing alignment if they resolve.
- The login-wall destination is covered in section 2.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Resolve the four failing links with a probe that handles relative `Location` headers, using the tracker host as base. Then confirm the real destinations and that the tracker emits absolute URLs.
2. Add a `text/plain` alternative.
3. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't rely on the relay copy.

5. Send the login-wall link to a public rewards or receipt-scanning page instead, or at least a page that doesn't need sign-in first.
6. Confirm UTM params on the final landing URLs once the redirects resolve.
