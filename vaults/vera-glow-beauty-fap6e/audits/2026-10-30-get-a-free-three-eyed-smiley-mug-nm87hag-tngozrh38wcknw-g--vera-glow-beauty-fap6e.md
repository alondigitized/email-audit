# Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that Glossier is on its fifth gift email in nine days. The 10/22 "Want a free gift?" was the first. Then came 10/24 "Get Milky Jelly Cleanser, free!" and 10/26 "Last call for your free gift!". Today's 10/30 morning send was the Balm Keychain. Now the gift is a mug. The mug is cute, but a mug with a $75 order doesn't make me do points math the way a Sephora Rouge 30% off event does.
- The offer is clear and the hero is clean. "Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug with your $75+ order*" sits right under "Limited time", with a Shop now button. The asterisk and the "Terms apply" line at the very bottom are the only fine print, and it's buried. There's no end date, so "limited time" doesn't give me a deadline. There's also nothing about Glossier Rewards or my status. I can't tell if my points stack or if the mug counts toward them.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already building a cart. I'd skim it, not forward it, and then go cross-shop. I'd check whether Sephora or Ulta has a better GWP threshold, because a $75 spend for a mug doesn't beat Ulta's 21 Days. It feels like a home-goods promo for a skincare brand.

## What's working

- The offer is in the first headline with a dollar threshold: "$75+ order". I don't have to hunt for the spend.
- The pink mug with the red smile is on-brand and photographs well. The marshmallow hot chocolate shot makes it feel like a cozy-season gift.
- The "Get ahead of gifting" grid shows the Mini Wardrobe Kit, Balm Dotcom Keychain, Just Baked Balm Dotcom and You Duo. Each has a "Limited edition" or shade-descriptor line and its own Shop now button, and the buttons are full-width and easy to tap.
- The layout is clean and readable with no broken images, which is better than Skechers' cart-nag sends.

## What's weak

- Five gift emails in nine days is fatigue. It's the same "free gift" beat as 10/22, 10/24 and 10/26, with only the object swapped.
- There's no deadline. "Limited time" and "claim your gift today" give me no date, and Sephora's "Coming Soon" banner at least gives me a clock.
- The asterisk terms are only at the very bottom, in tiny text. I can't tell if the mug is while-supplies-last or if it's one per order.
- The second block's body copy is cut off at the right edge: "holding yo…". That's a visible render bug.
- Every CTA says "Shop now", and nine of them in a row is just "Shop now" repeated. The Balm Keychain also appeared in this morning's send, so the recap feels recycled.
- No price shows on any product, and no Rewards or points info. As a Rouge/Diamond person I notice that.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an end date in the hero and move the key terms up: "Free mug with $75+, ends Sunday, while supplies last". Right now the urgency is generic.
- 2. Cut the cadence. Bundle the mug into one weekly gift email instead of a new object every two days.
- 3. Fix the clipped text in the "Treat yourself or gift" block.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy: "Claim my mug" in the hero, "Get the kit" and "Add the keychain" in the grid.
- 5. Add prices and a points line, like Ulta does, so I can do the math.
