# The Art of the Eye—20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "for your eyes only" is a cute Bond nod and I'll admit the hero shot — mascara wands fanned out on a mirrored tray with jewelry-ad lighting — is the best visual ILIA's sent me all week. But I've now logged four ILIA emails in three days (9/13's "VIPs get the best + 20% off," 9/14's VIP Sale, 9/14's bare-bones "moment, friend" text email, and now this), and it's the same 20% off VIP-gated mechanic wearing a new outfit each time. The "art of the eye" tool-kit framing is genuinely nice editorial work, but I already know the punchline before I scroll: join VIP, get 20%, done.
- This one I'd actually click through on the visual alone — the eyeliner-on-green-background shot for the "NEW" Thru Line liner caught my eye more than anything Sephora or Ulta sent me this week. But forward to a friend? No. This is going straight to "read, mentally filed under ILIA's still doing VIP sales," not screenshotted for anyone.

## What's working

- That hero tray shot is legitimately styled like a jewelry ad, not a sale banner — best ILIA visual this week.
- The "art of the eye" 3-step kit (shadow stick, liner, mascara) gives me an actual reason to look at three products instead of just "shop the sale."
- Gift-at-checkout section (free multi-stick, free velvet bag on $100+) is a real incentive stacked on top of the discount, and it's laid out clearly with a polaroid-tag visual.
- VIP perks are spelled out concretely — free shipping, points, early access — so I know exactly what I'm joining for, unlike Skechers' vague "miss you" plays.

## What's weak

- Fourth "VIP" push from ILIA since 9/13 — the framing keeps shuffling (VIP Sale → best+20% → plain-text moment → now art-of-the-eye) but the mechanic hasn't moved an inch, and I'm gated behind "JOIN VIP" again just like 9/15's lip+cheek email.
- "20% off" isn't in a big number anywhere near the top the way Sephora does it — I have to hunt the corner badge on the hero image for the actual digit.
- No sense of who this is for beyond "anyone with eyes" — no skin-tone or eye-shape personalization, no "matches what you bought last time" signal.
- Fine print at the bottom flags exclusions (bundles, gift cards, auto-replenish) which knocks down how "sitewide" the offer actually feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the number. Sephora's 9/15 "Beauty's Most Wanted" send put 50% off in giant type above the fold — ILIA buries 20% in a small corner badge. Make it the headline, not a badge.
- 2. Stop resetting the VIP hook every 24-48 hours. I've paid it forward three times this week already (9/13, 9/14 x2) — either commit to one big VIP push per week or vary the discount depth, not just the creative wrapper.
- 3. Tie the "art of the eye" kit to something personal — my past purchases, my eye shape, my shade match — the way Drunk Elephant's "name an issue" flow at least attempts segmentation.
- 4. Move "gifts at checkout" higher — free bag/multi-stick is a stronger click driver than another liner shot, and it's currently past the mid-scroll fatigue point.
