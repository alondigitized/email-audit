## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based, 700px fixed-width XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with Epsilon-wrapped tracking links. The only hard QA failure is the empty plain-text part (0 chars). The rest is minor markup and accessibility debt, plus header checks that can't be confirmed because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link failures confirmed.** QA probed 12 of 52 links (40 skipped, deprioritized footer/social). It reported no broken links, but the other 40 are unverified.
- **Redirect-wrapped links.** Links go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1a2021dc…?__dU__=…`. The final destination isn't visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify where they resolve.
- **Two tracking pixels with no `alt`:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`, hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261103_HSE_Insider_Starts_Today`.
  - Both are invisible, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct marking.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA fails this check ("Text version is 0 chars"). The cause may be the AgentMail relay or the sender, which I can't tell from the data. It hurts deliverability scoring and text-only or screen-reader clients.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed 700px layout.** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't override it. The CSS is truncated, so it may be handled further down.
- **Malformed CSS inside the media query.** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block of a plain `<style>`, not in a conditional-comment wrapper. Non-Outlook parsers will probably treat `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` as invalid selectors. That drops the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` mobile override, which affects Rouge-tier headers on mobile.
- **Missing structure and metadata:**
  - `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
  - Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.
  - `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) in the visible source.
- **Inconsistent salutation.** The subject says "Insider" but the preheader says "Sephora Shopper" ("It goes well with your 10% off, Sephora Shopper."). That looks like a name fallback firing in one field and a tier label in the other. The rest of the source is truncated, so I can't check the body.
- **Empty structured-data fields.** The JSON-LD has `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `{"@type": "PromotionCard"}` with no offer data. The Gmail promotion annotation is therefore non-functional, and `DiscountOffer` is not a standard schema.org type.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post are not found.** QA itself flags this as possibly a relay capture artifact. I can't confirm either way. If the headers are genuinely absent, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results is missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and QA attributes this to the relay. This is not a confirmed defect.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are past the truncation point, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Can't be assessed from the data.** All links are Epsilon redirects, and the truncated source and QA output show no resolved landing URLs or UTM values.
- **Campaign ID is only in the pixel.** The one visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261103_HSE_Insider_Starts_Today`. Whether the same campaign ID reaches the landing pages is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` part. This is the only hard QA failure.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, bypassing the AgentMail relay. That will separate relay artifacts from real gaps.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule into a proper conditional `<style>`. Then the Rouge-tier mobile rule survives.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
6. Populate or drop the empty `discountCode` / `PromotionCard` JSON-LD.
7. Align the salutation fallback between subject and preheader.
8. Re-run link probing on the 40 skipped links, and resolve the redirect chains to confirm landing pages and UTM or campaign parameters.
