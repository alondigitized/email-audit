## What's working

- The "Experts Know Best" hero is clean and editorial. The eye lands on it first, and nothing is broken or overlapping.
- The tip format is useful. "Color correct first, then conceal. Peach cancels out blue and purple tones" is something I'd actually use.
- The creators are recognizable and the lineup is varied: Mario, Mary Phillips, Violette, Patrick Starrr. That gives it real beauty-community credibility.
- The October Breast Cancer Awareness section is plainly written. It says $25 of each kit goes to Memorial Sloan Kettering and gives a $350,000 minimum. I appreciate the transparency.
- It feels more premium than Glossier's lip-lineup send, which had no copy depth at all.

## What's weak

- There are no CTAs. I see no "Shop Mario's picks" button and no product names or prices under the tips. Ulta would have put a shoppable carousel under every tip.
- The "INSIDER, You have 0 points" header ignores my Rouge tier. A points-obsessed Rouge/Diamond sees that and thinks I'm a stranger.
- The subject line doesn't tell me it's a tips email. "I HAVE PATRICK 📞📞📞" is all caps with stacked emojis, and it promises a drop or a story.
- The tip cards have no products attached. I don't know which contour shade Mary is using or which One/Size by Patrick Starrr product creates the peach corrector.
- The breast cancer kit at the bottom is the only thing for sale, and it's small and buried. A bundle with a donation could be the reason to click, and it's the weakest part of the layout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a product link under every tip, like "Shop Mario's blend brush" or "Shop the One/Size corrector," with price and Rouge-tier point or perk info.
- 2. Pull the breast cancer kit up and give it a real button. A cause-linked set is a real reason to click.
- 3. Use my tier in the header. "Rouge, you have X points" would signal a known customer, and a Rouge-only perk would help even more.
- 4. Make the subject match the content, and keep it to one emoji.
- 5. Space out the sends. I got a catalog dump yesterday, and this tips email would hit harder alone.
