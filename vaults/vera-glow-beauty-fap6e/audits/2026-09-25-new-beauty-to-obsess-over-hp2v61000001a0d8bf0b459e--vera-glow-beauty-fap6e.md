# New beauty to obsess over 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Sephora's entire new-launch wall dumped into one email again — I flagged this exact pattern on 9/25 with the near-identical "New beauty to obsess over 😍" send, and this looks like the same template, maybe even the same day's batch. Libre Vaniile and Santal Couture up top is genuinely exciting (I've been eyeing the Libre line), but then it's Obart, Dior, Innisfree, M∙A∙C, Oribe, LYS Beauty, Sol de Janeiro, Unite Hair, EADEM, NuFace, Innbeauty Project — that's eleven brands in one scroll with zero curation logic I can find.
- The "Up to 50% off Hair Faves, Today Only" banner up top is the only real urgency/offer in the whole email, and it's crammed above the fold before I even get to the new-launch content, which feels like two different emails stapled together. I'm not clicking through eleven product tiles on my phone — I'll maybe tap Libre Vaniile since it's genuinely new-to-me, but this isn't getting forwarded to anyone.
- Compared to Ulta's "20% off one item" sends, at least Sephora's showing me real product instead of a barcode-on-book-stack hero, but Ulta's mechanic is scannable in two seconds and this isn't. Drunk Elephant's Kamo shade-finder email earned my click by doing ONE thing well; this does eleven things adequately.

## What's working

- Libre Vaniile and Santal Couture hero up top is a legit "new fragrance drop" hook for someone like me who chases launches.
- "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badges on Obart and Innisfree are exactly the ingredient-transparency signal I look for before I click into a product page.
- The Beauty Insider footer band ("We Belong to Something Beautiful") reinforces the loyalty program is still front and center, which matters since I'm Rouge tier.
- Oribe's "Gold Leaf Collection Hair Gift Set ($120/$157 value)" gives me actual price-math to do, which is catnip for a points-obsessed shopper like me.

## What's weak

- Eleven brands in one scroll with no clear organizing principle — is this "new," "trending," or "random warehouse picks"? I can't tell, and neither could a skimming subscriber.
- The "Up to 50% off Hair Faves Today Only" banner competing with the "new launches" content means I don't know if this is a sale email or a launch email — Sephora's hair Daily Deals send on 9/23 did this same "today only" hair mechanic already, so it feels recycled into a different template four days later.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere despite Sephora having my Beauty Insider profile data — Dior's tinted moisturizer and MAC's tint stick are exactly the products where "matched to you" would have been easy and persuasive.
- Zero social proof or star ratings on any of the eleven products, which is a miss when this many new items are competing for one tap.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 3-4 curated picks max with an actual "why we picked these for you" angle — right now it reads like inventory dump, not curation, and that's the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Separate the "50% off Hair Faves Today Only" sale banner into its own send — it's stepping on the new-launch story and diluting both messages.
- 3. Add shade/skin-tone matching on at least the Dior and MAC products using my Beauty Insider profile — Ulta doesn't do this either, so this would actually be a real differentiator for Sephora.
- 4. Add star ratings or "X,XXX loves" social proof under at least the hero products to build trust before the tap.
