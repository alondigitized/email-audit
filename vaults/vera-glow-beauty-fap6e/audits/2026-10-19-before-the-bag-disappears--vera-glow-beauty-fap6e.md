# Before the bag disappears
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same two-gifts VIP offer in four days. I had "The (2) gifts of VIP" on 10/16 and "Friend, these gifts have your name on them" on 10/17. Now it's "Before the bag disappears" with the same velvet bag and mini mascara hero. The "LAST CHANCE" sticker is the only new thing, and I've seen that urgency from ILIA all month.
- The email also contradicts itself. The top banner says mini mascara with any $75+ order. The body says free mini mascara plus velvet bag with any $100+ VIP order. The lower block says mascara with $75+ again. I do points math for a living, so I'm not going to guess which threshold is right. The asterisks say "see site for details," which doesn't help.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I already know the gift, and I'm not buying $100 of makeup to get a mini mascara and a pouch. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read, and maybe come back if the deadline were stated.

## What's working

- The hero is still gorgeous. Rust velvet bag against blue sky and red blossoms stops my scroll.
- "From: us. To: you." is a cute, on-brand opener.
- The red CTA buttons are big, full-width and easy to tap.
- The six-product grid has clear names. The eye stylus and the Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 are what I'd actually shop.
- The trust row at the bottom (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Shade Match, free shipping over $50) is reassuring.

## What's weak

- The $75 versus $100 threshold conflict is the biggest problem. I can't do the math on a gift if the rules change between sections.
- There's no deadline anywhere. "Last chance" and "before the bag disappears" with no date or time means the urgency isn't credible.
- The mini mascara gift is repeated three times (banner, hero text, bottom block). The bottom orange block feels like padding.
- The six "shop now" buttons are thin outlined boxes. They're weak next to the red CTAs and small for a thumb.
- The product grid has no prices, no shades and no reviews. I'd have to click through just to see what $100 buys.
- The VIP join pitch ignores that I may already be a member. "Join VIP + claim gifts" reads like I'm a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one threshold ($75 or $100) and use it in the banner, body and footer. Add a real end time, like "ends tonight at midnight PT."
- 2. Cut the repeated mascara block, or replace it with something new. A shade-matched tint or a customer review would be more useful than a third gift pitch.
- 3. Show prices and star ratings under each product, and make the "shop now" buttons solid.
- 4. Segment by member status. Members should see "Your gifts are waiting" and non-members should see "Join free."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: free mini mascara + velvet bag`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your VIP gifts expire at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get both gifts. Last day to claim.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The velvet bag + mini mascara are gone after tonight.`
