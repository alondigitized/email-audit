# Ends Tonight
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Day five. I've been tracking ILIA's Buy 3, Save 20% run all week — the dice on day two, the rock-paper-scissors on day three, the lip-shade breakdown on day four — and this is what the finale looks like. "Ends Tonight." That's the whole subject line. No discount. No product tease. Just vibes and a clock.
- Inside, the creative energy has officially left the building. "The power of *** sale" in the hero is genuinely confusing on a quick mobile scroll — three asterisks read like a render glitch before context kicks in. The body copy does the work ("Power of Three Sale ends tonight, 20% off any trio") but the hero should've already closed that. I had to read twice to get it, and on my commute, the delete reflex is faster than that.
- The good news: the "LAST DAY" badge, the top banner with the code, and the big "Buy 3, Save 20%" type are all doing their jobs. I'm probably clicking because I was already primed by the previous four sends. But as a standalone email? This is a reminder memo, not a closer. I'd mark it read and maybe come back later — which, in sale-finale terms, is a loss.

## What's working

- **"LAST DAY" badge** in the corner is the hardest-working element in this email — red, immediate, no reading required.
- **Top banner** leads with offer + code before the hero even loads: "BUY 3, SAVE 20% WITH CODE BUNDLEUP" is exactly what I need above the fold.
- **"Buy 3, Save 20%"** type hierarchy in the hero is clean — once my eye finds it, the offer is clear.
- **1VIP callout** in the lower section reminds me there's a loyalty layer here, which I like knowing even if I'm not deep in the program.
- **Trust badges** (Planet Partner, cruelty-free certification, Pact Collective) still land — this is the clean beauty signal that separates ILIA from a random Ulta house brand.

## What's weak

- **"The power of *** sale"** — three asterisks in the hero read as censored or broken copy on first pass. It's day five and the creative peaked on day three; this is not the send to get experimental with the headline typography.
- **"Ends Tonight" subject** is four words of urgency with zero payoff — no offer amount, no product hook. Sally Beauty opened this week's holiday send with "25% off sitewide" in the subject and scored a 10; ILIA is leaving points on the table every single morning by refusing to put the number in the line.
- **Code-as-CTA is friction** — "USE CODE: BUNDLEUP" on the button means I have to remember something or copy-paste it. Drunk Elephant's Fourth email linked directly to the promo; no mental overhead required.
- **No product hero** — the imagery shows some aesthetic bowls and a tube, but at day five of a sale, showing me the three most-shopped trios would do more for my cart than a brand-mood arrangement.
- **Fifth-email fatigue is real** — the novelty budget ran out. After two genuinely clever game-mechanic emails, "Ends Tonight" feels like the intern drafted it at 11pm.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the offer in the subject** — "Ends Tonight" is anonymous to anyone who doesn't already have the sale memorized. "20% off ends at midnight — grab your trio" tells me the stakes before I open.
- 2. **Spell out the hero copy** — "The Power of Three Sale ends tonight" or lean into the countdown: "3, 2, 1... Last chance." Don't make me decode asterisks on my phone.
- 3. **Hero the top three trios with product names** — on the last day, a "Most-shopped trio" module with images, names, and prices will drive real add-to-cart intent. This is what Sephora does for Beauty Insider Event closers and it works.
- 4. **Auto-apply the code at checkout** — cut the code friction entirely and change the CTA to "SHOP THE TRIO — 20% OFF." Nobody should be manually pasting BUNDLEUP in 2026.
- 5. **Add a specific deadline time** — "Expires 11:59 PM PT" beats "ends tonight" when I've been half-watching this sale for five days and don't fully trust the deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 3, save 20% — final hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: Power of Three ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop your trio before midnight — code auto-applies at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off any three. Ends at midnight PT. No extensions.`

## Technical Audit — ILIA "Ends Tonight" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on MJML via Klaviyo with MSO/VML conditional comments for Outlook and standard mobile breakpoints at 480px. The truncated source prevents full link and compliance auditing, but several infrastructure-level issues are confirmed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully audit** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. From what is visible:

- All anchor colors are overridden by five stacked `a:` rules (`a:not([name])`, `:link`, `:visited`, `:active`, `:hover` all set to `#5f4b3c`). The `a:not([name])` selector excludes named anchors but is parsed inconsistently in older Outlook WebView clients — verify CTA buttons use inline `style` color overrides, not class-only styling.
- UTM parameter presence on CTA links cannot be confirmed from the truncated source. Flag for manual check.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`@import` for web fonts — confirmed broken in Gmail and Outlook:**

```css
@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```

Gmail strips `@import` entirely; Outlook ignores it. Fallback chain `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` on `p, h1, h2, h3` is correct, so rendering degrades gracefully — but the Klaviyo custom font will never render in Gmail (estimated ~40%+ of commercial email opens).

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title> </title>
```
Renders as a blank tab title in webmail preview. Low severity but fix to brand name or subject line.

**`img` rule declared twice** — `img { border: 0; line-height: 100%; ... }` appears in two separate style blocks. Duplicate declarations are harmless but indicate template hygiene issues.

**Alt text on images** — cannot audit from truncated source. Confirm all `<img>` tags carry `alt` attributes; ILIA's product-forward imagery will render as broken boxes in image-off clients if omitted.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens visible in the truncated source. Preheader text is hardcoded:

```html
<div style="display:none;..."> 20% off is over in 3, 2, 1... ͏ ͏ ͏ ...
```

The padding characters are U+034F (Combining Grapheme Joiner), a standard preheader-padding technique. No broken `{{ }}` or `{% %}` tokens confirmed in visible portion.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Cannot confirm from truncated source:**
- Physical mailing address (required by CAN-SPAM §5)
- Unsubscribe link and honor-within-10-days mechanism

**Authentication** (DKIM/SPF/DMARC) — not auditable from HTML source; must be verified against received message headers. Klaviyo handles DKIM signing for `iliabeauty.com` if the domain is properly authenticated in the Klaviyo account — confirm `d=iliabeauty.com` in the DKIM signature.
