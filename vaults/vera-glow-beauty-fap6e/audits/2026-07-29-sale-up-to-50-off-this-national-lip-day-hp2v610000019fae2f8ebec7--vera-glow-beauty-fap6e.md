- The "Spot It. Shop It." DAE before/after and $35-and-under modules feel bolted on after the lip grid — no real transition, they just interrupt the lip theme I clicked in for.

- 2. Stop resending the same subject line within days — if this is a genuine resend to non-openers, say so ("Last chance") instead of pretending it's a fresh send.
