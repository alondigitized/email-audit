# Ready to claim it all?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is back with the 20/25/40 number shuffle, and now it's a plain-text email with no images. It reads like a note from "Marissa," but it's the same Black Friday promo I've seen all week. My first reaction was relief that I don't have to decode another hero image. My second was irritation, because the points math still doesn't work out. I get 20% off everything, 25% if I join VIPs, and up to 40% off select lip and cheek items. Every line has an asterisk, and I can't tell which discount stacks with which.
- I would not click this one. There's no product, no shade and no hero image, so nothing makes me want anything. The only links are "20% off everything" and "join free to shop." I'd skim it, notice the free Complexion Stick with any order, and move on. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.
- Compared with the 11/22 "20% off—all. the. time." send and the 11/21 "Choose your shade of 30% off" send, this one at least lists the stack in one place. That's the only upside, and it doesn't make up for the missing visuals.

## What's working

- The bulleted stack puts the perks in one scannable list: 25% VIP, free Complexion Stick, free mini over $100, and up to 40% off lip and cheek.
- The "free Complexion Stick with any order" gift has no spend threshold. I like that more than a percentage, and it's the one perk I'd act on.
- The plain-text, personal-note format is a nice break from the sequin-heavy Sephora and Ulta sends.
- "Join free to shop" tells me how to get the 25%, and a free signup is easy for me to take.

## What's weak

- Three discount tiers sit in the first few lines (20%, 25%, up to 40%), and every one has an asterisk with no terms visible. I can't tell whether the VIP 25% beats the 40% lip and cheek deal.
- There's no product imagery at all. I don't know which Complexion Stick or which mini I'd get, and I can't see a shade.
- Only two text links are visible, and no button. There's no obvious "Shop now" that gets me to the sale.
- The copy is generic: "What's in your cart? There are no wrong answers." I have nothing in my cart, and it doesn't speak to my skin tone or type.
- I got the 25%, 20% and 30% messaging three times in three days, so this reads as the fourth near-identical promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero image and a real CTA button, "Shop 25% off + free stick." Plain text can work, but it needs one dominant action, which this email lacks.
- 2. Collapse the stack into one clear ladder, for example "Guest 20% / VIP 25% / Lip + cheek up to 40%," with the terms visible in one line. I'd also show the end date. Ulta does this well with its 50/40/30 tiers and a deadline.
- 3. Name the gift. "Free Skin Rewind Complexion Stick" with a swatch would give me a reason to act.
- 4. Segment by the shade I browsed or my past purchases, and drop the "what's in your cart" line for anyone with an empty cart.
- 5. Slow the cadence. Glossier and e.l.f. are also sending near-daily, and I'm tired of all of it.
