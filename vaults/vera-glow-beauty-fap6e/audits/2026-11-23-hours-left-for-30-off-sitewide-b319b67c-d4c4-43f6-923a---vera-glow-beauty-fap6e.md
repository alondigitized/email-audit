# Hours left for 30% off sitewide
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again, and this is the sixth member-30% send in a week. The offer is clear, but I've seen it so many times that it barely registers. "LAST CHANCE!" over a giant white-on-red "30% OFF SITEWIDE" gets me the number in half a second. Then the 11/22 send already told me "30% off early access is almost up ⏱️," so "hours left" is the same deadline in a new outfit. The fine print says the promo ends 11/22, which makes it feel like a recycled message.
- I'm not their customer, so the shoe grid (Slip-ins, Cozy Fit, Court & Classics, Performance) doesn't speak to my routine. I'd click only to check whether "sitewide" covers a gift. I wouldn't forward it. Ulta's 50/40/30 ladder gives me a better deal to chase, and Sephora's app push gives me a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is huge and high-contrast, and I get the offer instantly.
- "Discount auto-applied at cart" under the button means no code to hunt for. That's friction removed.
- The Doorbuster block with "starting at $49.99" gives me a second concrete number.
- The size grids, women's 5–10+ and men's under 7.5–12+, are a thoughtful touch for shoppers who know their size.
- The Skechers Plus loyalty header and "Join and earn 1000 bonus points ($5 back)" give me the points math I always do.

## What's weak

- There's no real deadline. "Hours left" has no clock or timestamp, and the fine print says "thru 11/22" while the send is dated 11/23, so the urgency isn't credible.
- Four category tiles have no products, prices or reviews. Glossier at least showed me Boy Brow and Lash Slick with social proof.
- The legal copy is a dense wall of tiny text, and it repeats itself.
- The "Join Skechers Plus" header contradicts the "Member Exclusive" banner. I can't tell whether I'm already a member or being sold on joining.
- The nav list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, Gift Guide, Sale) is generic. Nothing is personalized to me or to what I browsed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the urgency real. Put a live countdown or an "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT" line in the hero, and send it only when the date is accurate.
- 2. Replace the four category tiles with one or two hero products showing the sale price, a strike-through of the original price and a star rating.
- 3. Cap the cadence. Six near-identical 30% sends in a week is e.l.f.-level fatigue (seven in six days), so suppress people who already clicked.
- 4. Reconcile the member messaging. Show one clear state, either "You're in" or "Join for access."
- 5. Shorten the fine print and link out to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 30% off sitewide, auto-applied`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Skechers Plus 30% off expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed. Slip-ins and $49.99 doorbusters included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call for members: ends 11:59 PM PT tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup has no blocking defects: it is table-based, has `role="presentation"` and a preheader, and its UTMs are consistent. The confirmed issues are two plain-HTTP images without alt text (one is a tracking pixel carrying a subscriber ID), accessibility-hostile viewport and CSS settings, and a deliverability and compliance picture that can't be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking pixel over HTTP:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM`
  - It is not HTTPS, so clients may block it and open-rate data will be undercounted.
  - The `mi_u=8108776175` identifier travels in cleartext.
  - Its host (`ink1000.com`) is separate from the sending domain.
- **Mixed subdomains:** the favicon uses `image.emails.nl.skechers.com` and content images use `image.emails.skechers.com`. Both are served over `http://`.
- **Unprobed links:** 45 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains and final destinations are unverified.
- **No issues found** in the one decoded link, `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BFEARLYACCESS&utm_...` (HTTPS, well-formed).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (the pixel) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The second is a content image, not a pixel.
- **HTTP image source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` may be blocked or rewritten by clients.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has none.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes keyboard focus indication.
- **Text size adjust disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` doesn't describe the message.
- **Breakpoints:** the CSS uses 375px, 480px, 640px and 680px media queries, plus a 768px query for `.mobile-content` / `.desktop-content`. This is inconsistent, though no failure is confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.
- The HTML is truncated, so the rest is unverified.
- The only recipient identifier seen is `mi_u=8108776175` in the HTTP pixel (see §2).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a verified defect. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require them.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body elements are unverified.
- **Sender:** `no-reply@emails.skechers.com` is a no-reply address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign ID matches:** `PRO_US_NA_U_SITESELECT_FULL_EN_11222026_PM` appears in both the pixel (`mi_ecmp`) and the `utm_campaign` value.
- **Annotation link:** it carries `utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1` plus `src=2026Q4BFEARLYACCESS`.
- **Possible source mislabel:** `utm_source=trig` suggests a triggered send, but the campaign name says `FULL` (a full-list send). This is an inference and worth checking.
- **Offer end time:** JSON-LD `availabilityEnds` is `2026-11-22T23:11:59Z`. The `:11:59` looks like a typo for `23:59:59`. The preheader says only "ends tonight", so the expiry time is unconfirmed.
- **Landing page:** the page was not probed, so the offer match is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all images, including the pixel and the favicon, over HTTPS. Move the tracking pixel to a first-party HTTPS domain.
2. Add `alt=""` to the pixel and a descriptive `alt` to `dde00662-…png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and restore a focus style on `a`.
4. Check the sent message's raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't rely on the relay capture.
5. Confirm `availabilityEnds` and the `utm_source=trig` value on a full-list campaign.
6. Re-run the QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and the merge tokens.
