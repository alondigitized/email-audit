# Save big on fall favorites 🍂
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second Labor Day sale email from Glossier in three days — I logged "Save 30% off select favorites" yesterday at 6/10, and this is basically the exact same 30%-off sitewide mechanic just re-skinned with a new "SALE / LABOR DAY" collage hero and a fresh product lineup pulled through. So my first reaction is déjà vu, not excitement. The good news is the scroll itself is genuinely nice to look at — Generation G in Jam, the Cloud Paint duo, Lilyhaze — this is Glossier doing what it does best, a clean product-forward catalog with real skin-tone diversity in the lip swatches.
- But there's zero personalization, no points, no tier callout, nothing that tells me they know I'm a repeat shopper, and the free-gift threshold buried at the very bottom ($75 Glossier You Solid) is honestly the most interesting offer in the whole email and it's getting no real estate. Compare that to Sephora's "Stacking Weekend" send I scored a 9 on this morning — bonus points AND a percentage discount AND urgency stacked right at the top. This just reads as "shop the sale," dressed up nicely. I'd scroll it on my phone out of habit, maybe tap on Cloud Paint in Eve since I've been eyeing a fall blush, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — it's not distinct enough from yesterday's send to be worth sharing.

## What's working

- The lip swatches on two different skin tones (deep and fair) actually feel like Glossier is thinking about who's looking at this, not just running one model.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — Cloud Paint texture shot, the Lilyhaze candle styling — this is on-brand and doesn't feel thrown together.
- The 30% off is stated plainly in the hero with no scavenger hunt for the number.
- Each product card has its own "Shop now" CTA so I don't have to scroll back up to act on something I like.

## What's weak

- This is the same 30% Labor Day sale I already got yesterday — same offer, new wrapper, and that's lazy cadence two days in a row.
- The free-gift-at-$75 offer is the actual differentiator here and it's shoved to the very bottom below four product modules — I almost didn't scroll far enough to see it.
- No loyalty or rewards mention anywhere — Glossier doesn't really have a points program like Sephora/Ulta, but that also means there's nothing here rewarding me for being a repeat customer specifically.
- The hero "SALE" collage is visually loud but tells me nothing concrete — no end date, no category exclusions, no urgency beyond "Labor Day."

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the $75 free-gift offer up near the hero — that's a real incentive that could push average order value, and Sephora/Ulta lead with their best stacked offer, not bury it.
- 2. Add an explicit sale end-date/time in the hero — "ends tonight" language is what got Ulta's 20%-off email a bump in my urgency scoring; Glossier has none of that here.
- 3. Don't run the same 30%-off mechanic two sends in a row — differentiate day two with a category focus (e.g., "last call on fall lips") instead of repeating the identical sitewide language.
- 4. Add a line acknowledging repeat customers or purchase history — even something simple like "since you loved Cloud Paint in [shade]..." would beat the generic catalog scroll.
