# This $11 lip combo is lit as e.l.f. 😘✨	
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, $11 for a two-piece lip combo is squarely in my "add to cart without thinking" zone, and the phone-selfie hero of a girl applying gloss in a mirror actually feels current — that's the kind of UGC-adjacent shot I'd screenshot for a friend, not some overlit studio close-up. But this is basically a rerun of the same beat e.l.f. has been running all week — 8/28 was Halo Glow, 8/27 was the vitamin C serum, 8/26 was the setting powder — always "prestige dupe energy at $11-17," and I'm starting to feel like I'm reading the same ad with a different product bolted on.
- The lip liner/gloss shade wall is genuinely useful — I can actually see "mauve aside" vs "truth or bare" vs "candy coded" lined up, which matters way more to me than generic swatches since shade-matching is the whole reason I hesitate on lip products online. That said, the email then sprawls into a Bronzing Drops GWP, three unrelated "Top Picks," and six empty nav accordion bars (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) that look broken — they're just gray bars with no thumbnails, which reads like a render bug even if it's intentional collapsed nav.
- I'd click the shade wall to check pinky swear or plum & get it, but I wouldn't forward this — it's a nice execution of a mechanic I've already seen four times this week from this exact brand. Compare that to Sephora's Daily Deals problem (running the literal same 50%-off send three times in two days) — e.l.f. at least varies the hero product, but the "prestige dupe at $11-17" formula is wearing thin the same way.

## What's working

- The phone-selfie application shot feels like real content, not a studio push — matches how I'd actually see this product on TikTok.
- $11 price point stated right in the hero copy — no math required, no scrolling to find it.
- The shade wall for both the lip liner and lip oil is laid out clearly enough to actually shade-match, which is rare for a lip email.
- "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face" plus the cruelty-free/vegan badges are a nice trust cue without being preachy about it.

## What's weak

- Five sends in a row now (8/25 through 8/29) all lean on "cheap dupe, big claim" — this one's no exception, it's just lips instead of skin or face.
- Those six gray NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR bars look like broken/unloaded content blocks, not intentional nav — that's a real visual miss on a phone screen.
- The Bronzing Drops GWP is a completely different product category shoved in the middle of a lip email — no thematic tie-in, just noise between the shade wall and "Top Picks."
- Zero mention of any loyalty points math (Beauty Squad), which is exactly the kind of thing I'm always calculating — an $11 item earning squad points would've been an easy add.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Bronzing Drops GWP block entirely or move it to its own send — it breaks the lip narrative and dilutes the one offer I actually care about.
- 2. Fix or delete those six empty gray nav bars — either populate them with thumbnails or drop them, because right now they read as a rendering failure.
- 3. Add Beauty Squad points value next to the $11 price ("earn X points") — that's the one lever that would make me feel like a recognized member instead of a random inbox recipient.
- 4. Vary the "cheap dupe" framing that's now run five sends straight — even a shade-launch or bundle-savings angle would break the pattern fatigue.
