# Get 10% off must-haves AND a FREE gift with purchase ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- The subject sold me 10% off plus a free gift, and the body is a lip-balm lookbook. "Mistletoe Must-Haves" is a stack of lip products: Summer Fridays, Laneige, Eadem, Olehenriksen, Kayali, Gisou and Experiment. The 10% Insider event and the gift with purchase are only in the thin header strip, and I'd have missed them if I weren't hunting. This is the fifth Sephora send in three days. After 11/2's "STARTS TOMORROW" and 11/3's "FREE gift with purchase, Insider?" and "10% OFF LUXE BEAUTY", the offer is old news and the creative has gone soft.
- The product curation is cute. Gingerbread Laneige, Kayali's marshmallow-scented lip balm and Gisou's cherry-chocolate mask all feel like holiday launches I'd screenshot. But it's six lip products in a row at $24–$34 with no hero offer. The 250-point reward option and the barcode at the bottom are the real money, and they're buried.
- Would I click? Probably, but only the Laneige gingerbread, and only because I'm already in the Savings Event mindset. I'd forward the Laneige block to a friend. I wouldn't forward the email.

## What's working

- The "Stacks with other offers" and Same-Day Delivery strip at the top does the points math for me. I just wish it were the hero.
- Limited-edition flavors are labeled "NEW FLAVORS + LIMITED EDITION + ONLY AT SEPHORA". That's launch-day clarity, and I stock up on exactly that.
- Product shots are appetizing. The churros-and-Eadem pairing and the chocolate-drip Gisou image are scroll-stoppers.
- Every product has a name, a price and a one-line benefit, so I don't have to click to learn what it is.
- The 250-point Reward Option and the barcode at the bottom make it easy to redeem in-store.

## What's weak

- The subject promised the offer, but the hero says "Mistletoe Must-Haves" with a SHOP NOW button. 10% off and the gift aren't in the hero.
- Six lip products stacked vertically is a long scroll with no variety. It feels like a catalog, not an event email.
- Zero shade or skin-tone personalization. For a lip email, I want to see swatches.
- Cadence fatigue. Same event, five sends in three days. Ulta's coupon-ladder emails at least rotate the offer.
- The tiny legal copy and the second barcode block are unreadable on my phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 10% Insider and the free gift on the hero, with a tier line like "Rouge: 15% off" if the tier discount applies. Today the offer is just a banner.
- 2. Cut the lip lookbook to 3 products and add a gift-with-purchase module. Ulta would put the coupon ladder above the fold. Sephora's own 11/3 "Insider everything is on sale" hero was much clearer.
- 3. Add a per-product CTA instead of only a top SHOP NOW.
- 4. Move the 250-point reward redeem to the top as a second hook.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, 700px-fixed-width ESP template (Epsilon/Harmony) with redirect-wrapped tracking links. The main confirmed defect is a missing plain-text alternative (0 chars). Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, and the QA run reports a 50% pass rate (1 fail, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** CTAs go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1a7.../<id>?__dU__=...`. The final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Low link coverage:** Only 12 of 53 links were probed and 41 were skipped (QA INFO). Status for the skipped links is unverified.
- **Campaign ID mismatch (low confidence):** The second tracking pixel is `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?...mi_ecmp=20261104_Holiday_Limited_Edition_Lip_Ba...`. That campaign ID refers to a lip balm campaign, but the subject is a "Savings Event" offer. This could be template reuse or a mis-tagged campaign, and the truncated source can't settle it. Confirm in the ESP.
- **Duplicate open tracking:** Two open-tracking mechanisms are present, `app.sephora.com/O/...` and `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif`. This is intentional, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] No plain-text part:** Text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability scoring and text-only and accessibility clients.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper table has `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't reset it, so horizontal scroll is possible on mobile. The truncated source may hide an override.
- **Malformed CSS (likely):** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block of a `<style>` tag. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS rules. Browsers and clients parse `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` as an invalid selector and drop that rule. They will probably also drop the adjacent `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows `<![endif]-->`. The `mso` fix never applies as written.
- **Missing alt text (2):** Both are hidden tracking pixels.
  - `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...` has `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `mi.sephora.com/.../o.gif` is not hidden in the visible source.
  - Impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would clear the warning.
- **Invalid markup (minor):** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The JSON-LD declares `"@type":"PromotionCard"`, which isn't a schema.org type, and an empty `"discountCode": ""`. Gmail ignores this block, so it is dead markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The empty `discountCode` in the JSON-LD is the only blank-valued field, and it's metadata rather than rendered content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** Both WARNs say the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. That makes this an unverified gap, not a confirmed violation. Check the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for `shop@beauty.sephora.com`. This is a relay limitation.
- **Body footer:** The QA run reports that an unsubscribe link was included in probing. The truncated HTML doesn't show it, the physical address, or the link's probe result, so none of these are confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be confirmed. Links are redirect-wrapped (`app.sephora.com/T/...`), and the campaign parameters live in the `__dU__` token.
- **Landing page alignment:** It is unverifiable from the given data, because 41 of 53 links weren't probed and the promo-code state isn't exposed.
- **Next step:** Resolve the redirect chain for the primary CTA, including the 10% off / free gift link. Check that it lands on a Sephora page that carries the offer and that the `mi_ecmp` campaign value matches.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain MIME part** (the only hard FAIL).
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
3. **Move the mso conditional** out of the `@media` block and use a separate `<!--[if mso]><style>...</style><![endif]-->`. This restores the dropped `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule.
4. **Make the 700px wrapper fluid** with `max-width:700px; width:100%` and a mobile override.
5. **Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions** to both tracking pixels.
6. **Verify at source, not through the relay:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw message.
7. **Re-run probing at full coverage** (53 links), and confirm the `mi_ecmp=...Holiday_Limited_Edition_Lip_Ba...` campaign tag is correct for this send.
8. **Clean up the markup:** use `noindex` and drop or fix the invalid JSON-LD `PromotionCard` and empty `discountCode`.
