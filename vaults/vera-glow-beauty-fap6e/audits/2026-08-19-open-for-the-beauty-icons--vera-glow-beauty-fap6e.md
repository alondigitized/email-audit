- The Chanel hero image is legitimately scroll-stopping — best visual asset in a Sephora send I've seen this week, better than the countdown graphics from App Fest.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges on the Touchland and Juliette Has A Gun items are a real curation signal — reminds me why I don't just buy this stuff at Ulta.

- The trial-size Fenty/NARS offer at the very top is a concrete, specific perk I'd actually use next order.

## What's weak

- No Rouge-tier badge or personalized point balance anywhere — just "you have [X] points" placeholder energy, generic INSIDER strip at the bottom with no math for me specifically.
- Ten-plus products crammed into one grid with no clear hierarchy — feels like Ulta's "20% off one item" sends where everything competes for attention instead of one hero offer.
- App Fest — the actual live event I already got a dedicated, better-designed email for on 8/17 — is squeezed into a small teal tile near the bottom instead of leading.
- Single hero CTA is just "Shop Now" with no destination specificity — compare to the Chanel fragrance line right below it which at least names the product.

- 2. Put a Rouge-specific badge and real points balance at the very top, not generic "you have [X] points" filler — I want to see MY number.
- 3. Cut the ten-item grid to 4-5 "editor's picks" max, or split it into a second send — nobody's making it through all ten before losing interest.
