# Don't Miss 50% Off It's a 10 💖
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- Twenty. I've been counting, and this is email twenty from Sally in roughly three weeks. The only reason I didn't swipe-archive it on sight is that the subject line actually did something: it named a brand. "50% Off It's a 10" is specific in a way that email nineteen — "Ready, Set, Getaway ☀️" — was embarrassingly not. It's a 10 is a real brand, I have the Miracle Leave-In in my bathroom right now, and 50% off clears my Ulta Diamond discount by a mile.
- But I'm doing the math the second I open it: can I stack a coupon at Ulta during 21 Days of Beauty and beat this? Probably not on It's a 10 specifically, so Sally has a window. What they didn't do is tell me the window closes. "Don't Miss" is not a deadline. "Don't Miss" is what you say when you have nothing better. Compare that to Glossier's "Final Hours: Up to 25% off" from earlier this month — that email had countdown energy and a real end date. This one just floats there hoping my urgency reflex kicks in on instinct.
- I'd click. Reluctantly, the way I click on anything after the sixteenth email from the same sender. I would not forward this — I'd text my curly-haired friend the specific product names, which I can actually see in the email, and that's the best compliment I can give.

## What's working

- **The offer names a brand I recognize.** "50% off select It's a 10 leave-in treatments" is miles ahead of June 12's "Fresh Finds for Summer" which offered absolutely no number and no brand.
- **Summer timing is perfect.** "Smooth hair, eliminate frizz & hold up in humidity" is the exact brief for late June. I needed this email today, not in a vague seasonal way — in a "my hair did something embarrassing in the grocery store parking lot" way.
- **The model is doing work.** She has rich, dark, voluminous hair that's exactly the "what this product promises you" visual. Demographic match is real.
- **Four specific products shown with names.** I can see Miracle Leave-In, Plus Keratin, Silk Express, and Lite — I can make a product decision without clicking. That's good email.
- **Sally Rewards is visible.** Bare minimum, but it's there.

## What's weak

- **Twenty emails in three weeks and "Last Call" is already a joke.** I've seen "Last Call" from Sally so many times it's lost all meaning. It's like Charlotte Tilbury calling everything "Unreal" — seven sends in, the word stops landing.
- **"Select" is carrying the weight of an asterisk I can't see.** Which It's a 10 products are excluded? The fine print block at the bottom is a full paragraph. That's a trust tax I didn't agree to when I opened.
- **No end date anywhere.** "Don't Miss" is not a deadline. I'll assume the sale runs until the next Sally email, which will be tomorrow.
- **The Pride Month section is an afterthought.** A little logo, generic diverse imagery, disconnected from the It's a 10 hero story. Either build a Pride-relevant campaign or don't run the module. This reads like a legal checkbox.
- **"Shop Now" is the laziest CTA in marketing.** Every single competing email — e.l.f., Glossier, ILIA — is doing more specific button copy than this.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an expiration date or time.** "50% Off Ends Sunday at Midnight" would transform this. Glossier's countdown energy drove me to actually schedule around the sale. "Don't Miss" drove me to scroll past.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA button.** "Get 50% Off It's a 10" or even "Shop It's a 10 Deals" — anything with the brand name in it. "Shop Now" is a placeholder, not a conversion tool.
- 3. **Clarify "select" above the fold.** One line: "All Miracle Leave-Ins included — excludes kits." Takes the sting out of the fine print and makes the offer feel more honest.
- 4. **Integrate or cut the Pride module.** If you're going to celebrate Pride, tie It's a 10 into a "be your most unapologetically you, frizz and all" story. If you can't, pull the module — it's doing reputational work without adding value.
- 5. **Cool the send cadence.** Twenty emails in three weeks means even a legitimately good offer like this one lands with my guard up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Call: 50% Off It's a 10 Ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `It's a 10 at half the price — your summer frizz fix`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Miracle Leave-In, Plus Keratin, Lite — all 50% off. Offer ends [date].`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before it's gone — every It's a 10 leave-in, 50% off now.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`Founders Grotesk` undeclared — confirmed defect.**
The global anchor rule specifies:
```css
a { font-family: 'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif; }
```
No `@font-face` block for `Founders Grotesk` exists anywhere in the `<style>` block. Every link in the email renders in the fallback (Helvetica / system sans-serif) in all clients. If this is intentional, the font name should be removed to avoid the lookup cost and the misleading declaration.

**Open Sans pinned to `v17` — outdated but functional.**
Six `@font-face` rules point to `https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/opensans/v17/...`. Open Sans is currently at v29+ on Google Fonts. Google continues to serve old version paths, so no rendering breakage today, but these URLs will stop working if Google cleans up legacy paths. The version-pinned URL pattern is a maintenance liability.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`.**
```html
<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` (or equivalent) is present. Screen readers (NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver Mail) use this to select the correct pronunciation engine. Required for WCAG 2.1 SC 3.1.1.

**`img { white-space: pre }` is a no-op.**
The `white-space` property does not apply to replaced elements (`<img>`). It's harmless but is template cruft that should be removed.

**Outlook VML namespaces present** — `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o` are declared, indicating the template has Outlook background/shape support. This is correct practice.

**`a[x-apple-data-detectors]` suppressor present** — correctly prevents iOS from auto-linking phone numbers and addresses inside anchor text.

**`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta present** — correct.
