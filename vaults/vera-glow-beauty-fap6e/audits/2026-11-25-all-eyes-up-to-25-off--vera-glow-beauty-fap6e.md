- The "very important perks" block shows the VIP benefits: free shipping on every order, early access, and redeemable points.

## What's weak

- The 25% banner, the 20% circle and "20% off" in the body copy are three number signals. I can't tell whether eye products are 20% and everything else 25%, or whether VIP unlocks 25%.

- The footer "SEE SITE FOR DETAILS" is tiny and low-contrast. It sits right under the free-gift CTA, which makes the offer feel less honest.
- It's the third ILIA full-template send in three days with the same offer, so the cadence is wearing thin.

- 2. Show sale prices on the three products, e.g. "$30 → $24."
- 3. Add a "free with $100+" progress cue, or spell out the stacking: does the gift stack with the discount?
- 4. Move the VIP perks nearer the top, since the offer depends on membership.
- 5. Put the fine print in readable type.

## Technical Audit: ILIA, "All eyes: up to 25% off"

The HTML is truncated, so the footer, body links and CTA hrefs were not available. Findings below are limited to what the source and QA data show. Items I couldn't verify are marked as such.

### 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email (`static-forms.klaviyo.com` fonts, `kl-*` classes, MJML-style `mj-column-per-100`) that sends links and the open pixel through a first-party tracking domain (`trk.send.iliabeauty.com`). The QA run found no hard failures. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts, and the real defects are minor markup and accessibility gaps.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link coverage is thin.** QA probed 1 of 12 material links and skipped 11, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified for nearly every CTA.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M89P9DA22PTMG4KB2QYSMMAN` is an open-tracking image. It has no alt attribute (see §3).
- No broken or malformed links are confirmed in the visible source.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on two images:
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so it needs descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
  - The tracking pixel above. It should have `alt=""`, and ideally `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:block"`, but it is low severity.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Empty `<title>`.** It contains only whitespace (`<title> </title>`).
- **Web fonts load via `@import`.** The email imports `fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat` and a Klaviyo custom font CSS. Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`. The `"Helvetica", sans-serif` fallback exists, but headings are declared in `Arial` while `p, h1, h2, h3` are overridden to Montserrat, so the font stack is inconsistent.
- **Preheader** is correctly hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`, and the `͏` spacer padding is standard. No issue.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|FNAME|*`) appear in the visible source. The footer and body below the truncation point weren't checked.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from relay data. The `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` subdomain suggests a configured custom sending domain, but that is inference only.
- **Physical address and visible unsubscribe link:** not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't shown.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unverified. CTA hrefs were not in the truncated source and 11 of 12 links were not probed, so UTM parameters, redirect targets and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked. Links that go through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` will redirect, and the final destination parameters should be captured.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the cloudfront `.jpeg` image. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and put a real value in `<title>`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers at the origin. Don't treat the relay's missing headers as a confirmed defect.
4. Re-run link probing with a higher cap (11 of 12 skipped) and record the final URL and UTM parameters for each CTA.
5. Don't rely on `@import` for Montserrat. Keep a consistent fallback stack, and use the same family for headings and body text.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, including the footer, so the address, unsubscribe and merge-token checks are complete.
