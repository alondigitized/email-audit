## Executive summary

- Okay, I need to say this up front: this is the *third* "Your fall beauty routine is coming together 🍂" send I've gotten logged for today, 9/20. Same subject, same structure — new-drops-in-a-row format with Salt & Stone, Fenty, Emi Jay, Kayali, Huda, Josie Maran, Caudalie, Loveshackfancy all stacked in a scroll. I already reviewed this exact creative once today at 6/10 and now I'm getting near-duplicates with different tracking IDs. That's not a subscriber experience, that's a send-log bug, and it's the kind of thing that makes me want to open Gmail filters instead of my Beauty Insider account.
- Setting the dedupe issue aside for a second, the actual content is fine — genuinely fine, even good. "Your Dieux Ritual" hero with the retinol pairing, then a clean run of new-and-only-at-Sephora drops with the Leaping Bunny/clean badges doing real trust work. Kayali and Huda are exactly my lane. But I've now seen this exact wall of product three times in one day, so my read isn't "ooh, new," it's "why do I have this again."
- I'd scroll it once, not click, and definitely wouldn't forward — the only thing worth forwarding to a friend would be a note complaining about my inbox getting spammed with the same email.

## What's working

- The "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" tagging on Salt & Stone, Fenty, Kayali, Huda is real exclusivity signal — I shop Sephora specifically for that kind of first-access.
- Clean/Leaping Bunny badges stacked next to Salt & Stone, Josie Maran, and Caudalie hit my ingredient-conscious side without me having to dig for it.
- Fenty Beauty by Rihanna gets full pricing and shade-adjacent copy ("Fluid Face Foundation Brush 165, $36") right in the block — that's the kind of concrete detail that gets me to actually read instead of skim.
- Points-relevant footer ("4X Points on Fragrance") is at least present for people like me who are always doing the math.

## What's weak

- I already reviewed this literal subject line and layout today — this is send #3 with the same hero. Sephora, I see you, and it's not flattering.
- Nine back-to-back product blocks before I hit any real offer is a lot of scroll for zero urgency — no deadline anywhere in the body.
- "SHOP NOW" repeated block after block is the least specific CTA copy possible — doesn't tell me if I'm buying skincare, fragrance, or hair.
- The trial-size Selena Gomez Rare Beauty banner up top and the "4X Points" banner at the bottom feel bolted on, not sequenced with the rest of the email — no narrative thread tying trial size → new drops → points.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the send-dedupe issue first — three copies of the same subject in one day is a deliverability and trust problem bigger than any copy tweak.
- 2. Pull one hero offer to the top with a real deadline — Ulta's "ENDS TODAY" mechanic is annoying by volume but at least it's unambiguous; this email has zero urgency anywhere.
- 3. Vary CTA copy per block ("Shop Kayali" / "Shop Fenty Shades") instead of nine identical "SHOP NOW"s — cheap fix, real lift to scanability.
- 4. Move the points/4X banner up near the top — that's the one thing that'd actually make me stop and calculate value, and it's buried at the very bottom under the barcode.
