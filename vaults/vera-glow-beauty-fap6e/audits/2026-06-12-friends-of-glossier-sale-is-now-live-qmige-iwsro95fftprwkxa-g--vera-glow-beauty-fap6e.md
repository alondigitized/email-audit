# Friends of Glossier Sale is now LIVE!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I opened this the morning after the June 11 "Sale is here" email, and my first reaction was honestly just... didn't we already do this? Glossier sent me essentially the same news two days in a row — yesterday was "the Friends sale is HERE," today is "the sale is NOW LIVE." If it was live yesterday, what changed? That subject line distinction doesn't hold up under any scrutiny, and it makes the cadence feel padded rather than purposeful.
- Inside, the email leads with a clean "Don't miss up to 25% off!" hero and then drops straight into a product grid — six items in two rows with individual "Shop now" buttons. Which, fine, practical. But it's a big step down from the editorial energy Glossier usually brings. Compare this to the June 11 send with the big orange ANNUAL SALE typography that actually made my thumb pause, or the Next Top Dog reunion email that I shared with two people unprompted. This one reads like an automated catalog refresh, not a curated send.
- The subscription lock-in pitch at the bottom is smart in theory, but burying it after a generic product grid means most people won't reach it. I'd probably scroll to see if anything specific in that product grid jumps out, click through to one product if it did — but I wouldn't forward this. It doesn't earn that.

## What's working

- **The 25% off number lands clearly** — "Don't miss up to 25% off!" is visible without scrolling, which is the only way sale emails should work in 2026.
- **Individual product "Shop now" buttons** under each grid item are actually useful — I can go straight to Lash Slick without hunting the homepage.
- **Clean Glossier aesthetic, no render bugs** — the layout holds, nothing overlaps, the red CTAs pop cleanly against the white background.
- **The subscribe-and-save cross-sell is strategically timed** — sale is live, so anchoring "lock in this rate forever" to the discount is at least logical.

## What's weak

- **Two consecutive sale announcement emails with no meaningful differentiation** — "Sale is here" (June 11) followed by "Sale is NOW LIVE" (June 12) feels like a sequencing error, not a planned drip. Charlotte Tilbury at least gives each send in a sequence a distinct job.
- **Recycled hero badge** — the circular sale graphic appears to be the same asset from yesterday's email. No new visual hook means I've already mentally processed this before I start reading.
- **Six-product grid with thumbnail-sized images** — I can barely evaluate these products at this scale. ILIA leads with a single hero product and nails it; Glossier is trying to show me everything and I end up choosing nothing.
- **"Up to 25% off" is hedged** — Sephora's "up to 50% off select beauty" at least feels like a range. "Up to 25%" on a brand with relatively tight pricing feels like it might actually mean 10% off on half the things I want.
- **No sale end date visible** — zero urgency mechanic beyond "now LIVE." If I close this email, I have no reason to come back by a specific date.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give this send a distinct angle from yesterday's** — if June 11 was the announcement, June 12 should be curated picks ("Our 5 bestsellers at 25% off") or a stat ("3,000 orders in the first 12 hours"). Make each send earn its place.
- 2. **Surface the sale end date in the hero** — "Sale ends Sunday" turns a casual scroll into a calendar moment. No deadline = no panic = no click.
- 3. **Replace the 6-product grid with 2-3 hero products** — full-width or half-width cards with actual product copy (one line about why you'd want it) convert better than thumbnails. Even Ulta — not known for editorial restraint — manages to elevate a hero product above the fold.
- 4. **Kill the hedged language** — pick a bolder claim: "25% off sitewide" or "everything in your wishlist, 25% off." "Up to" kills confidence.
- 5. **Move the subscription pitch into the hero as a secondary CTA** — "Buy once at 25% off, or lock in 25% forever with Subscribe & Save" is a genuinely compelling fork that could live right under the headline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Glossier sale is live — 25% off ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off window is open (closes soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Lash Slick, Glossier You, and every bestseller — 25% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The full site is 25% off. Here's where to start.`
