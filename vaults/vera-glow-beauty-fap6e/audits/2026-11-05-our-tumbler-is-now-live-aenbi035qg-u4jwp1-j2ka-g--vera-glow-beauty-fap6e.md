## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked XHTML 1.0 Transitional template. The QA run passed 57% of checks with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The findings that hold up are missing alt text on 7 images and link coverage that is too thin to call the links verified. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link verification is mostly incomplete.** Only 1 of 12 material links was probed, and 4 of those returned 429.
  - The four "Shop now" links that returned 429 were `ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M6P4RHB4QFWEZZBYKBCN8V0H_8`, `_10`, `_11` and `_12`. A 429 is rate limiting by the click tracker, not a broken link, but it means none of them was resolved to a destination.
  - Six links were never checked because the 70s time budget ran out: `_14`, `_16`, `_17`, `_18`, `_1…` (truncated in the QA output). They count as neither pass nor fail.
- **Every link goes through the Klaviyo click-tracking redirect** (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/…`). This is normal for the platform, but it hides the final destinations from static analysis.
- **Invalid markup near the top of `<body>`:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M6P4RHB4QFWEZZBYKBCN8V0H_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` aren't valid attributes on `<span>`. It looks like an ESP artifact. It is harmless in most clients, but a strict parser or link scanner may treat it as a link.
