# 🚨 ENDS TODAY: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, 2X points on all products, and FREE Same-Day Delivery
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Eight. We are on email **eight** of the Sephora Stacking Weekend campaign — and this one shares its subject line *verbatim* with the send I already scored a 9 earlier today. I want to be clear about what that means: this is not a re-send catch-up or a segmented follow-up. This is a near-identical deployment hitting the same inbox the same day with the same creative. The email body itself is solid — the hero communicates 30% off + 2X points cleanly, the LAST CHANCE product tiles give me something specific to click into, and the Fragrance Minis section is actually a nice secondary editorial choice for end-of-event. But the moment I saw the subject in my inbox, I thought I was looking at a ghost — or worse, a deployment error.
- The barcodes at the very bottom of the render are also a genuine head-scratcher. They look like in-store loyalty scan codes that either leaked into the email template or rendered when they weren't supposed to. Not a broken layout exactly, but deeply weird. That kind of artifact makes me trust the email slightly less.
- Net verdict: I'd click from muscle memory — it's the last day, I have a cart, I know the offer — but I would not forward this to a friend. I'd feel embarrassed sending a duplicate-subject email like it's a deliberate strategy.

## What's working

- **The hero does its job.** 30% badge on the left, 2X points circle on the right — the two key offers land without squinting or scrolling, even on mobile.
- **"LAST CHANCE" section with specific product tiles and point values** is the clearest editorial win in the body. Showing me concrete products with actual numbers (360, 50, 79, 188 point values) gives me a shopping reason to scroll, not just a vague promise.
- **Fragrance Minis section is a smart add.** End-of-event fragrance discovery angle skews exactly toward my usage pattern — I trial minis before committing to full size, and I do it on sale days.
- **Brand consistency.** Sephora's color palette, type treatment, and "We Belong to Something Beautiful" footer are all where they should be. No identity confusion, no off-brand imagery.

## What's weak

- **Exact duplicate subject line on the same calendar day.** The 9 AM send already fired with this headline. Sending it again reads as a deployment mistake or a list segmentation blunder that wasn't caught before it hit inboxes.
- **Barcodes at the bottom of the render** should not be there. Whatever they're meant to do — in-store scan codes, tracking pixels gone wrong — they visually signal that something broke in the template before it went out.
- **Subject line is 100+ characters.** The rubric flags anything over ~50 for mobile. "🚨 ENDS TODAY: 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, 2X points on all products, and FREE Same-Day Delivery" truncates hard on every phone in my contacts list — and the truncation cuts the most differentiating part (Free Same-Day) entirely.
- **"ENDS TODAY" in all caps** stacks on top of the 🚨 emoji. Compare this to ILIA's "PSA—mini totes are back" from earlier this summer — that send scored a 9 using almost zero urgency screaming. Sephora is leaning on alarm-signal stacking when the offer itself is strong enough to close without it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the duplicate send.** Full stop. If you must send twice, change the subject line — even a minor variation like personalizing by tier ("Rouge member: today's your last day") transforms it from a deployment error into a deliberate segmentation.
- 2. **Strip the barcodes.** QA the template footer before deployment. This is one pre-send checklist item.
- 3. **Tighten the subject to mobile-first length.** The offer can still be specific without being exhaustive — pick the single highest-value benefit and let the preheader carry the second.
- 4. **Dial back the ALL CAPS.** "Ends today" lowercase is still urgent. "ENDS TODAY" with 🚨 is inbox screaming.
- **Subject Alt A:** `🚨 Last hours: 30% off Sephora Collection + 2X points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only, Rouge: 30% off + double points end at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free same-day delivery — your cart is waiting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your points before the window closes tonight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] User zoom disabled — WCAG 2.1 §1.4.4 failure**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-to-zoom on iOS Safari and Android Chrome. This is a confirmed WCAG 2.1 Level AA failure (Resize Text, criterion 1.4.4). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**[MEDIUM] Text scaling suppressed globally**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to nearly every element type. Blocks OS-level font size overrides for users with low vision. Standard practice is `-webkit-text-size-adjust: 100%` (prevents iOS auto-inflate) rather than `none` (blocks all scaling).

**[LOW] Non-descriptive `<title>`**
```html
<title>Sephora</title>
```
Screen readers announce the title when the email opens in a browser/web view. A descriptive title (e.g., `Ends Today: 30% Off Sephora Collection`) improves accessibility for AT users.

**[LOW] `robots: no index` present** — correct for email web-view pages, no action needed.
