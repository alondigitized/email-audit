## What's weak

- No loyalty signal at all — I'm Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond and even a small "+2x Beauty Squad points" line would've made this feel like they know I'm a repeat buyer, not a cold subscriber.
- The email nosedives into a "find your skincare" quiz module and an unrelated "Top Picks for You" grid (eye cream, cleanser, makeup remover) that has nothing to do with lips — it kills the launch-day focus this send started with.
- The category nav bar at the bottom (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR) is just gray text blocks — looks like a placeholder wireframe, not finished creative.
- No before/after or swatch shot showing the actual lip color payoff — for a "plump lips" claim I want to see the pigment or shine, not just packaging on a marbled pink background.

- 2. Add a Beauty Squad points callout near the $12 price ("earn 2x points on this kit") — costs nothing to include and instantly makes tier-shoppers like me feel seen.
- 3. Show a swatch or on-lip payoff shot instead of just product-on-marble — I need to see the "thirst burst" plumping effect to believe it.
