# Cheek Week ends tonight!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "ends tonight" — this is the third beat this week I've watched Glossier hammer on the same 20% off blush promo, and by now I've got the whole campaign memorized: the sitewide launch on 8/11, the "Cheek Week" category rebrand on 8/13, the diary-caption editorial on 8/15, and now the countdown close. Credit where due — closing a campaign with actual urgency instead of just fading it out is the right move, and I do like that they organized the grid by finish (Soft + Natural, Summery + Bright, Deep + Vampy) instead of just dumping six products at me.
- But I've already shopped this if I was going to — I saw the 20% at launch, I saw the looks on 8/15, there's nothing new here except a ticking clock and a $75 free-gift threshold that's honestly the most interesting thing in the whole email. e.l.f. SKIN's "Same Glow, Less Dough" dupe email this week did more with a single hero than Glossier's doing across six product tiles.
- Marking as read, not clicking. If I were going to act on anything it'd be math-checking whether I'm already over $75 for the free Cheek Brush, not the 20% off itself — I've seen that number four times now.

## What's working

- The hero image is genuinely pretty — soft pink-on-pink blush application shot that's on-brand and easy on the eyes at 7am scrolling.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" + "Save 20% off all blushes" up top is clear and unambiguous, no scavenger hunt for the offer.
- Organizing shades by finish (Soft + Natural / Summery + Bright / Deep + Vampy) is a smart way to make six near-identical Cloud Paint SKUs feel like a curated edit instead of a catalog dump.
- The $75+ free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush gift is a real incentive I hadn't seen in the earlier sends — that's the one line that could actually move me.

## What's weak

- This is the exact same 20% blush offer I've now seen on 8/11, 8/13, and 8/15 — there's zero new information here besides the deadline, and I'm fatigued on it.
- No mention of Rouge/Beauty Insider or any loyalty math anywhere — Sephora would never let a members-only week like this run without pointing to points multipliers.
- Six nearly-identical "Shop now" gray buttons with no single hero CTA — my eye doesn't know where to land first, unlike Sally Beauty's "Buy 2 Get 1" grid which at least anchors on one clear mechanic.
- The free-gift threshold ($75+) is buried at the very bottom below six product tiles — that's the actual differentiator and it's the last thing I'd scroll to see.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the $75+ free Cheek Brush gift up near the hero — that's the freshest hook in this send and it's currently buried under six product blocks.
- 2. Add a Rouge/Beauty Insider callout or points note — I'm tracking points math across every brand I shop, and Glossier leaving that on the table while Sephora and Ulta lean on it hard is a miss.
- 3. Cut the grid from six shades to a "top 3" pick with one dominant CTA — right now no single button reads as THE button.
- 4. Since this is campaign-close, add a scarcity cue beyond just "tonight" — units left, or "last chance to get the gift" — something with teeth, the way Sephora App Fest does with countdown clocks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off blushes + free brush at $75`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cheek Week closes tonight — don't miss the free gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance at 20% off every Cloud Paint shade`
