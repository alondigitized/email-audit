# New I ♥️ NY merch just arrived!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Six emails in nine days. Six. And this morning's subject — "New I ❤️ NY merch just arrived!" — lands right after yesterday's "NEW! I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom" send, which I already noted felt redundant against the July 14 "Meet I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom." I opened this one with genuine confusion about what exactly was new. Turns out: it's not a beauty product. It's a t-shirt and a keychain.
- The I ❤️ NY campaign earned real credit from me. The four-part Balm Dotcom build — scent tease on the 12th, countdown on the 13th, launch on the 14th, a redundant "NEW!" on the 15th — was one of the tighter sequences Glossier has run this year. But now we've hard-pivoted to lifestyle merch with zero transition. There's a Big Apple Tee, an Applebaby Keychain, and the only thing that actually stopped my scroll: a free Big Apple Sticker Pack GWP offer. As someone who buys Glossier for Balm Dotcoms and Boy Brow, I did not open this email to shop branded keychains.
- I will click "Claim yours" on the sticker pack because it's free and I'm a completionist. That's it. I'm not buying the tee. I'm definitely not forwarding this — Drunk Elephant's "Help your skin. Save the planet." last week gave me a clearer reason to click in one hero image than this entire email does. Marking it read and moving on.

## What's working

- **GWP hook is genuinely smart.** The free Big Apple Sticker Pack section breaks the product-grid monotony and gives me an action that isn't "spend money on a t-shirt."
- **Visual brand consistency.** The red-apple color story, the Glossier wordmark at top, the clean white backgrounds — it still looks unmistakably like them, which is table stakes but executed well.
- **The Big Apple Tee photography is cute.** The model shot is young-Millennial-coded, the styling fits the NYC campaign, and it's not a basic product flat. I see myself in the aesthetic even if I won't buy.
- **No render bugs.** All images loaded, no broken layout, text is legible at inbox scale. After the Sephora double-send situation I audited, a clean render is worth noting.

## What's weak

- **Zero beauty product in the lead.** I subscribed to Glossier for skincare and lip products. An entire email of apparel and accessories after five days of Balm Dotcom hype is a momentum killer.
- **Campaign fatigue is real.** This is send six of the I ❤️ NY sequence. ILIA managed to intrigue me with a single well-shot blush email ("That flushed feeling") — Glossier is now testing my patience with merch as an act-five chaser.
- **"Merch in the city" hero copy does no work.** It's decorative, not directional. Compare to e.l.f.'s "Toner 101" — at least that subject told me exactly what the email was before I opened it.
- **"Claim yours" on the keychain is confusing.** That CTA reads like a free item, but it appears to be a for-purchase product. Bait-and-switch energy, even if unintentional.
- **No pricing visible at a glance.** If I'm going to buy a tee, I need to see the number without clicking through. Ulta would've shown the price right on the tile — Glossier makes me do homework.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the sticker pack GWP, not the tee.** The free offer is the click hook — bury it fourth and you've already lost half your audience. Flip the hierarchy: GWP up top, merch below.
- 2. **Add a beauty bridge.** One sentence — "Balm Dotcom, meet your merch moment" — connects the product launch sequence to the lifestyle pivot and earns the context shift.
- 3. **Kill the "Claim yours" CTA on the paid keychain.** It reads as free. Either change copy to "Shop now" or make it actually free.
- 4. **Show prices inline.** Even just "$40" under the tee thumbnail removes the friction of a click just to find out if it's reasonable.
- 5. **Rest the cadence.** Give me 48 hours after a launch before the merch upsell. Six emails in nine days is pushing toward the unsubscribe threshold.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The tee, the keychain, the free stickers — I ❤️ NY merch drop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a Big Apple Tee and Applebaby Keychain to match your new Balm Dotcom.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Claim your free sticker pack with any I ❤️ NY purchase.`
