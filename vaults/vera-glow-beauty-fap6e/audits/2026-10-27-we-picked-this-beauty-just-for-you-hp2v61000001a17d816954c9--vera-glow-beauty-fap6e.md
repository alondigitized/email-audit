# We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- "Chosen Just for YOU" is the biggest thing on the page, and nothing in it feels chosen for me. The top strip says "You have 0 points." I'm Rouge, and the tier bar near the bottom has its marker sitting on Insider. Same problem as the 10/27 send before this one. It's also the seventh Sephora email in a week (after "These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️" on 10/26 and "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" on 10/25). The real offer is the little Topicals trial size at the top, and it's tucked above the hero.
- The Gisou lip oil and honey lip mask in the hero are cute, and "Only at Sephora, limited edition" gets my attention. But it's a product catalog, not a launch with a date, a points multiplier, or a reason to buy today. Below the hero it turns into Marc Jacobs, Versace, Clarins and EADEM, which is the usual grab bag. Ulta would at least put a dollar-off or points event on top. I'd skim it, maybe screenshot the Gisou lip set, and not click.
- I wouldn't forward it. I might text a friend "Gisou lip oil is $28" if I wanted a reason to talk about it. Otherwise it's mark as read.

## What's working

- The Gisou hero is gorgeous. The glossy lip oil and the honey mask are visually appetizing, and the price shows right in the product lines ($28, $26).
- "New • Only at Sephora • Limited edition" is the launch language I care about, and the Clean at Sephora badge adds to it.
- The Topicals trial-size freebie with code HISKINLOVE is a concrete offer at the top, with a SHOP TO REDEEM button.
- Value callouts like "$51 value" and "$101 value" on the Gisou sets let me do the points and savings math quickly.
- The Beauty Scan module is a real personalization hook. It's the only block that speaks to skin type.

## What's weak

- "Chosen Just for YOU" over a generic catalog feels fake. Nothing reflects my Rouge tier, my history, or my skin.
- The top strip says "You have 0 points." For a Rouge member, that reads like the email doesn't know me.
- The Topicals freebie is small, and the code requires a $30 purchase with exclusions. It's buried above the hero and the fine print is easy to miss.
- Below the hero it's five or six unrelated products (Marc Jacobs, Juliette, Rare Beauty, Clarins, Versace, EADEM) with no theme or deadline.
- The Sephora Credit Card "Get 25% off Your First Purchase" block is a stranger pitch to someone who has been a loyal member for years. The tier bar lands on Insider, not Rouge, so no tier math is possible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Lead with the Gisou launch plus a real point multiplier or a date, and drop "Chosen just for you" unless the picks are actually based on my purchases.
- 2. Fix the tier and points data so the header and bar show Rouge and my real balance. Add a line like "X points to your next reward."
- 3. Move the Topicals freebie into the hero zone with the minimum spend and deadline in plain sight, not a footnote.
- 4. Cut the below-hero grid from 6 products to 3, grouped by theme (lip, fragrance, skincare).
- 5. Skip the credit card block for people who already hold the card or are Rouge.
