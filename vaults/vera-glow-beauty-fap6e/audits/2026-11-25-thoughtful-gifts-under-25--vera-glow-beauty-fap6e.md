The under-$25 gift angle is the one new thing here, and it's a smart framing. It's an easy price anchor for stocking-stuffer math, which is my whole December.
- I'd probably open it, because Boy Brow and Lash Slick are in my cart rotation anyway. But the render looks clipped on the right edge. ## What's working

- "Under $25" is a clear, shoppable gifting frame. I can do the points math in my head.
- The four-product grid (Boy Brow, Lash Slick, Balm Dotcom, Ultralip) is full of hero SKUs I recognize, and each has a name and a descriptor.
- The close-up lip and brow photography is gorgeous and on-brand. It looks more premium than Ulta's usual busy grids.
- The free Crème de You with a $100+ order gives me a spend threshold to build a cart around.
- "It's the little things" has a warm, in-voice tone and doesn't read like a generic discount blast.

## What's weak

- The right side of the email is visibly cut off. The "30% OFF" circle, the top banner, the second column's buttons and the Crème de You headline all run off the edge. This is the biggest problem.
- There are two competing offers, 25% off everything and 30% off $100+, with no clear winner. The hero bubbles stack them confusingly.
- Every product button just says "Shop now". That's four identical CTAs, and none names the product.
- There's no deadline anywhere. "Black Friday Sale" is a banner, not a countdown. Drunk Elephant's VIP countdown sold me urgency better this week.
- There are no reviews or shade info, and the Glossier rewards/points angle is missing. Sephora and Ulta always show tier perks, and here I'm treated like a stranger.
- It's the seventh or eighth Glossier email in a week, and the offer hasn't changed since 11/22's "Loved by you, now 25% off".

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the right-edge clipping so the full offer bubble, banner and both product columns are visible. Test it on mobile.
- 2. Pick one offer for the hero (25% off, or the free Crème de You) and demote the other to a single line.
- 3. Replace "Shop now" with product-specific CTAs, like "Get Boy Brow" or "Get Lash Slick".
- 4. Add a real deadline, like "Ends Monday 11:59pm ET", and a price on each product card ("$18 → $13.50").
- 5. Add review stars or a "best seller" tag to the four grid items. Sephora does this in almost every send.
- 6. Cut the cadence, or vary the creative, so day eight doesn't repeat day six.
