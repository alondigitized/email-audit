# ENDS TODAY 🚨 10% off your purchase and 30% off ALL Sephora Collection
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Savings Event send in a row (11/6, 11/7, 11/8, now 11/9), and "ENDS TODAY" is the first one where the urgency is actually true. The hero is clear: Insider 10% and Sephora Collection 30%, both marked "Ends 11/9", with a SHOP NOW button right under them. The subject only leads with the 10% number again. As Rouge, my number is 30%, and I did that math the second I saw the badge.
- The body is long. After the hero there's a same-day delivery strip, a points challenge (earn up to 500 points), a credit card perk, a Beauty on Demand banner, a 6-product grid, a gift-with-purchase block, a store locator and a holiday gift guide. The product grid is good, with prices shown (EADEM at $21.60, Dr. Jart+ at $46.80, Summer Fridays at $22.50). The bottom half is a lot of scroll for a one-day deadline. I'd open it and probably click the Sephora Collection offer. I wouldn't forward it, because it's the same email I've seen all weekend.

## What's working

- The 10% and 30% badges, each with "Ends 11/9", sit above the fold. I can see the deadline and the tier split without scrolling.
- "ENDS TODAY" is credible. The Sephora Collection 30% is the most concrete offer I've seen in this run of emails.
- The code lines (ALLONSALE and SCSAVE) make the in-store and online mechanics clear, so I can do my math before checkout.
- The product grid has real sale prices with the original struck through, plus brand names I recognize. That's what I want to see.
- The points challenge (up to 500 points) is tier-math content that's useful to me.

## What's weak

- Four days of the same event means fatigue. On 11/8 the countdown timer was the new thing, and this one has no timer at all.
- The subject leads with 10%, which is the Insider number. Rouge gets more, and the 30% only appears second.
- Too many competing blocks. The points challenge, credit card offer, GWP, Beauty on Demand and the gift guide all pull attention away from the one deadline.
- The email is very long. The store locator and a map for "Kohl's" are filler for a last-day push.
- The fine print and exclusions text is tiny. I can't read it without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle: keep the hero, the Sephora Collection 30%, the product grid and one perk. Everything else should go in a different send.
- 2. Put a live countdown in the hero, like the one on 11/8.
- 3. Use a tier-aware subject so Rouge members see their number first.
- 4. Move the Sephora Collection products higher, since that's the new news.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `10% off for everyone, 30% off Sephora Collection. Codes inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus same-day delivery by 1pm PT. Last call.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is largely standard table-based ESP output, but the QA run found one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several header/authentication items it couldn't verify. The HTML is truncated, so nothing past the logo/tier block could be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial:** 12 links were probed and 40 skipped. No broken or redirecting links were reported, but 77% of the links are unverified, including footer and utility links. The unsubscribe link was probed, but its result isn't in the findings.
- **Open-tracking pixels (2):**
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261109_HSE_Insider_Ends_Today` (1x1, `alt=""`, `aria-hidden="true"`)
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` (hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`)
- **Two different tracking hosts:** the pixels use `mi.sephora.com` and `app.sephora.com`, which is expected for a split ESP and analytics setup. Both are first-party subdomains, so this is not a defect.
- **Recipient-level ID in a URL:** `mi_u=4970293024993920` is a recipient-level identifier in a query string. It is fine for tracking but will leak if the message is forwarded.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback missing (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. The HTML-only message hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility. This could be a relay capture artifact, so confirm against the raw MIME.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid conditional comment in CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media (max-width:480px)` block. The comment tokens are likely to corrupt selector parsing in non-Outlook clients. The `img.mso-size` rule and the immediately following `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule are probably dropped on mobile.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper has `min-width:700px; width:700px` inline, and no override of that wrapper is visible in the mobile `@media` block. That may force horizontal scrolling on narrow clients. This is unconfirmed because the source is truncated.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email, so it's cosmetic.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.
- **Alt-text warnings are mostly false positives:**
  - `o.gif` has `alt=""` plus `aria-hidden="true"` in the source, which is correct for a decorative pixel.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt` attribute. It is hidden, so the impact is negligible. Adding `alt=""` would clear the warning.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` value is rendered, not templated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** this is likely a relay artifact, but unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require the header.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click is unconfirmed for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` could not be verified.
- **Footer not inspected:** the body-level unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated portion of the HTML. CAN-SPAM compliance there is unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No CTA hrefs are visible in the truncated source, and no probe results for destination URLs were provided. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261109_HSE_Insider_Ends_Today` on the pixel. Verify that the CTA links carry matching campaign tags.

**Structured-data issue:** the JSON-LD block has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. The empty `discountCode` is a data defect. Most clients strip `<script>` anyway, so it has no effect there.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart `text/plain` alternative, and check the raw MIME to confirm it's missing at the source and not just at the relay.
2. Capture raw headers, bypassing the relay, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule, or use a separate `<style>` inside a conditional comment.
5. Add a mobile override for the 700px fixed-width wrapper (`min-width`/`width`), or switch it to `max-width:700px; width:100%`.
6. Add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com` pixel. Fix `content="noindex"`, or drop the tag.
7. Populate or remove the empty `discountCode` in the JSON-LD.
8. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML and a larger probe budget, so the footer, unsubscribe link and CTA/UTM checks can be completed.
