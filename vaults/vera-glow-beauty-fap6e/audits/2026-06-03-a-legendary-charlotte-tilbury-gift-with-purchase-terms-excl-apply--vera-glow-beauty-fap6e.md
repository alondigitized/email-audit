# A legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift with purchase** (Terms & excl. apply) ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, *finally* — Ulta breaks out of the tiered spend-and-save death march with something that actually catches my attention. Four emails of "$10 off $50 / $20 off $100" in five days, and now we pivot to a Charlotte Tilbury GWP. I will not pretend I didn't perk up. CT is absolutely in my rotation — I've been tracking their Pillow Talk launches and I audited CT's own email just recently (the "FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades for FREE" one), so I'm already primed for the brand.
- That said, the "Beauty Break" Ulta wrapper at the very top is doing a lot of heavy lifting against the CT brand experience. Charlotte Tilbury has a very specific visual language — that deep burgundy/gold signature — and Ulta shoves it below a generic pink-gradient hero section. The prestige signal gets diluted immediately. The GWP offer itself is also frustratingly vague from what I can see; I want to know the exact spend threshold before I'm clicking anything.
- I'd mark this as "read and click to verify the GWP terms" — not a hard pass, but definitely not a one-tap, no-brainer moment. I'd forward this to my group chat only if the GWP turns out to be something actually worth stocking up on.

## What's working

- **Charlotte Tilbury gets genuine visual real estate.** The brand's warm burgundy/gold aesthetic carries through the mid-section and I can tell this isn't just a logo slap — CT products are front and center with multiple hero shots.
- **Multiple CT products featured.** Mascara, eyeshadow, and what looks like the Flawless Filter family — exactly the kind of haul moment that makes a GWP email worth opening.
- **Loyalty bar with barcode at the bottom.** As a Diamond member, seeing that section tells me this email is aware I'm a rewards customer, not just a cold prospecting send.
- **Subject pivots from the spend-and-save rut.** After four identical tiered-discount emails, "gift with purchase" reads as a genuine event change, not just another cadence blast — I notice it in the inbox.
- **No render bugs visible.** Everything loads cleanly, images are sharp, layout doesn't break.

## What's weak

- **"Beauty Break" hero at the top buries the lede.** The Ulta brand wrapper uses the top scroll position — prime real estate — for a generic SHOP NOW instead of leading with the Charlotte Tilbury GWP value prop. CT's own recent sends go straight to the offer hero; Ulta is padding with a house banner first.
- **GWP threshold is not scannable.** I can't immediately read *what I need to spend* to unlock the gift. If I have to scroll and hunt for that number, I'm already losing interest. ILIA and even the Sally Beauty "Summer Hair Survival Kit" both put the qualifying mechanic in the hero zone — Ulta is making me work for it.
- **"Legendary" in the subject line.** CT's own marketing loves that word, but inside a Ulta send it reads like borrowed brand equity rather than a genuine signal. It's hypey in a way that slightly triggers my spam-adjacent radar.
- **Subject line is too long.** "A legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift with purchase** (Terms & excl. apply) ✨" is well over 50 characters — the asterisks and fine-print signal get truncated on mobile, which makes the subject feel like a legal notice more than a beauty event.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the GWP spend threshold into the hero, above the fold.** "Spend $X, get [specific gift] FREE" needs to be the first thing I read — not buried mid-scroll. Drunk Elephant and Glossier both lead with the value mechanic; Ulta should match that here.
- 2. **Let Charlotte Tilbury open the email.** Kill or shrink the generic "Beauty Break" banner and start with CT's signature dark-richness hero. The prestige signal gets set in the first 200px or it doesn't get set at all.
- 3. **Name the specific gift item in the subject.** "Free [Pillow Talk lip] with $X CT purchase" outperforms the vague "legendary gift" every single time — specificity is what drives the tap.
- 4. **Drop one of the two asterisks from the subject.** The `**` reads as a Reddit markdown glitch, not a footnote convention. It undercuts the prestige tone immediately.
- 5. **Add a countdown or end-date to create credible urgency.** Right now there's no "ends Sunday" or "while supplies last" tension that would push me to click today vs. tomorrow.

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard commercial email build using XHTML 1.0 Strict + VML Outlook conditionals. Several CSS errors and a global link suppression rule are the primary technical risks; the Google Fonts dependency will silently fail in most major clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully assess** — HTML is truncated before body links appear. The following should be verified against the full source:
- Confirm all CTA hrefs pass through `e.ulta.com` click-tracking with UTM params appended
- Verify no raw `ulta.com` links bypass the ESP tracker (common in footer/unsubscribe blocks)

Sender domain `e.ulta.com` is consistent with ESP subdomain routing — no anomaly there.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Google Fonts blocked by major clients**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans:ital,opsz,wght@0,9..40,100..1000;1,9..40,100..1000&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
External `<link>` stylesheets are stripped by Gmail, Outlook (all versions), Yahoo, and Apple Mail on some configurations. DM Sans will not load; the client falls through to whatever fallback is declared inline. No visible fallback stack is declared in the truncated CSS — confirm `font-family` fallbacks exist in body/cell rules.

**[HIGH] Global link underline/color suppression**
```css
#MessageViewBody a {
  color: inherit !important;
  text-decoration: none !important;
}
```
This rule removes all visual link affordances for every anchor in the email body. Combined with image-based CTAs this may be acceptable, but any text links will be invisible as interactive elements — WCAG 2.1 failure (Success Criterion 1.4.1, use of color).

**[MEDIUM] Invalid `rgba` alpha value**
```css
background: rgba(255,255,255, 255);
```
CSS `rgba()` alpha must be `0`–`1` (or `0%`–`100%`). The value `255` is invalid. Behavior is browser/client-defined — most will clamp to `1.0` (opaque white), but this should be corrected to `rgba(255,255,255,1)` or `#ffffff`.

**[LOW] Missing unit on `top` value**
```css
#menu-wrapper { top: 50 !important; }
```
`top: 50` without a unit is invalid CSS. This is dead code (hamburger menu is commented out) but leaves an invalid declaration in the live stylesheet.

**[LOW] Trailing whitespace in `<title>`**
```html
<title>Ulta Beauty </title>
```
Signals template/merge artifact. Low severity but indicates the subject-line personalization token may not be wired to the `<title>`.
