# New to rewards? Get $5 off $25
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Sally "join rewards" pitch I've gotten in about a week. The subject says "New to rewards?" and I'm not, so it reads like it was sent to a stranger. It's the same $5 off $25 as "Join today, get $5 off $25" on 10/17, with a new headline on top. The two models with the SALLY tote bags are cute and the pink is on brand. Nothing in it is aimed at me, though.
- The body is a loyalty explainer that repeats itself, then a product grid of hair color and developer. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond who buys nails, skincare and makeup, so a 32 oz developer bottle does nothing for me. Sephora's Beauty Insider math is clear and mine. Here I have to work out the value of 1.25 points per $1 on my own.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim the tier boxes, decide $5 off $25 is worth less than my Ulta 21 Days points, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero offer is clear and sits high: "Get $5 off $25+ in October." The black "Join Sally.Rewards" button is easy to find.
- The two-tier comparison (Preferred is free, Elite is $200+ a year for 1.25 pts per $1, free shipping and early access) gives me the math I like. It's easy to scan.
- The hero photo is bright and diverse. It's more fun than the plain "Holiday Gift Edit" send from 10/15.
- The utility strip at the bottom (In-Store, Online, free shipping over $40, 2 Hour Delivery, Buy Online Pick Up In-Store) is useful and tappable.

## What's weak

- "New to rewards?" is the wrong message for a lot of the list. The 10/12 send ("Don't leave 15% off behind") was at least a clean offer. This one is just the signup bait again.
- The product grid is hair color, developer and lightener. It has nothing to do with the loyalty pitch and nothing to do with how I shop.
- "Use Your Reward on These Favorites" makes no sense, since I haven't earned a reward yet. The products have no prices, no stars and no reviews.
- The CTA is "Join Rewards" twice and then four "Shop now" links. That's generic copy with no verb-plus-noun that would tell me what I'm getting.
- The legal block is a long wall of tiny type. It says the offer ends 10/31, the reward expires within 30 days, and it can't be stacked, but the hero never mentions any of that. The email also doesn't show me a deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress current members from this send and send them a points-balance or tier-progress email instead. Right now it's the fourth rewards pitch in a week.
- 2. Make the product grid match the hero. If you want me to use the $5 off $25, show a nail, skincare or best-seller edit that gets a basket past $25, with prices.
- 3. Put the end date on the hero: "Ends 10/31." The only date is in the fine print.
- 4. Show the reward math with an example. For instance: "Spend $25, pay $20."
- 5. Cut the second "Join Rewards" block. Two copies of the same pitch on one page is too many.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with mobile media queries, but it carries a literal unreplaced `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a plaintext email address in an image URL, and an insecure `http://` tracking pixel. Compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed because the relay did not capture headers, and the HTML provided is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` contains the literal placeholder `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the cache-busting and impression-dedup value is lost. This is a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` uses plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail may block or rewrite it, so this Dotomi (Epsilon) sync pixel may not fire. It may also trigger mixed-content warnings.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** There are at least four tracking beacons besides the ESP's own links: Evergage, `pxl.mon-trk.com`, myvisualiq and Dotomi. Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.
- **Unverified links:** 50 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the probe (`[WARN] 50 tracking link(s) skipped`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified, not confirmed broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** The four Evergage dynamic images (`.../campaign/pLpNA/HxBq1/1/summary.png` through `/4/summary.png`) have no alt attribute. These look like product recommendation tiles, so images-off clients and screen readers get no content. This is the most significant accessibility issue.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (mon-trk), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` have no alt. These are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but means the intended font never loads.
- **Dark mode:** The visible CSS has no `prefers-color-scheme` handling. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm this.
- **No issues found** with the viewport meta, MSO conditionals or VML namespaces in the visible portion. The `.mobileHide`/`.stack` classes are present at `max-width:499px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **PII in a URL:** The Evergage image URLs include `userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output), which is a plaintext recipient email address in a query string. It leaks to the Evergage/Salesforce image host and any proxy or log that sees the request, and it may be forwarded with the email. Hashing the ID would avoid this.
- **Unreplaced token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is covered in section 2.
- No other unreplaced `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`-style tokens were visible in the truncated source.
