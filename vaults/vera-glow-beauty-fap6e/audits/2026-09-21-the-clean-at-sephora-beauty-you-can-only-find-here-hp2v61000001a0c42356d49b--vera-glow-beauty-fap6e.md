## What's weak

- Zero mention of my Rouge tier or points balance anywhere near the top — Ulta's "20% off" sends at least put my name (Marisol, in their case) in the subject; this doesn't personalize for me as a top-tier member at all.

- "Spot it. Shop it." as a section header does nothing for me — vague filler copy between the product grid and the actual deals.

- 2. Cut the product grid from eight items to a curated three or four "editor's picks" — I don't need every single new launch, I need the ones worth my points.

- 4. Give the before/after hair mask its own clear CTA button instead of relying on the generic "Shop Now" up top — that image is the strongest asset in the email and deserves its own action.
