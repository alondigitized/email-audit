# Learn your lip line(r)s 💋
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a clean lip-liner launch email with a reward drop and a gift offer stacked underneath it. The hero is simple: "learn your lip line(r)s" is a cute pun, and the three-product stack is easy to scan. The prices make it, too. $6 for the new Peel of Approval stain, $5 for the Main Stain marker and $2 for the Cream Glide liner. At those prices I'd add a liner to a cart on impulse.
- But it's the 5th e.l.f. email in a week. I got 11/9 (flyaways), 11/11 (Power Grip BOGO), 11/13 (Bright Appeel) and 11/14 ("Get a $10 reward on us!"). The "Surprise Reward Drop" block in the middle is the exact same $10-off-$25+ offer from yesterday, just moved down the page. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, so I notice when a brand re-sends the same loyalty hook. I'd click the Peel of Approval stain, and I might forward the $2 liner to a friend. The reward block mostly makes me feel like I've already seen this.

## What's working

- Each product gets one block, one price and one "Shop Now" button, so I know what I'm tapping.
- The prices ($6, $5, $2) are in the headline, so I don't have to hunt for the cost. Sephora would have put "from $22" somewhere in the fine print.
- The "new!" badges on the Peel of Approval stain and Main Stain marker mark the launches clearly. That is launch-day clarity I care about.
- The close-up lip photography is strong, and the models look like me.
- The Beauty Squad $10 reward callout includes "Not a member? Join now," so there's a loyalty hook for non-members and I can do the points math fast.
- The "Top Picks for You" row at the bottom has real prices and a "Shop Now" button on each product.

## What's weak

- The $10 reward is a repeat of yesterday's send, with only "this weekend only" added. It's not news, and I can't tell if the 11/14 reward is the same one or a new one.
- The full-size gift offer ("choose a full-size gift on us," $35+) lands right after the reward. Two offers on top of three products dilutes everything, and neither offer is tied to lips.
- Every button says "Shop Now." There's no verb plus noun, like "Get the $2 liner."
- There's no shade, skin-tone or "which liner pairs with which stain" help. As a shopper I'd want shade-matching info, because six liner shades is a lot to pick from. The email only shows pencils, not the full range.
- The category nav (New / Eyes / Lips / Face / Skincare / Hair) is a stack of big grey bars with a lot of dead space. The fine print is also tiny, and I'd have to zoom to read the reward conditions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer and the product in the same hero. Something like "$2 liner + $10 reward." Right now the offer is buried below three product blocks, while Ulta puts its ladder offer up top.
- 2. Cut the repeat. If the reward ran yesterday, don't give it a full block today. Put in a slim banner, or swap it for something new, like a lip bundle.
- 3. Make the product blocks do more. Add shade swatches, a "pair it" tip, and a star rating on the Peel of Approval stain.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop the $6 stain" and "Get the $2 liner."
- 5. Space out the sends. Five in a week is a lot, even at these prices.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New $6 peel-off lip stain 💋`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 lip liners, $2 to $6 💋`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free $10 reward this weekend when you spend $25+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Peel-off stain, lip marker and a $2 liner. Which one's yours?`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is sound: it uses table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, an MSO/VML shim and a hidden preheader. The 4 "FAIL" link results come from the probe failing to resolve relative redirect targets, so they probably aren't real breakage. The header-related warnings are caused by the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed either way. The headline "27% pass rate" should not be taken at face value.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probable false positives (4 links).** Every failure has the same error, `unknown url type: '/products/...'`. Python's `urllib`/`requests` raises that when a URL has no scheme or host. The wrapper links on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…~~` appear to return a relative `Location` header, which browsers and mail clients resolve without trouble. The failing destinations are:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Jumbo+2.5+oz+%2F+75+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Dark+Cocoa+-+Deep+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning…")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&bxid=…&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&af_xp=email&sour…` ("Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Not confirmed as real defects.** The probe never reached a final HTTP status for these four, and the truncated source doesn't show their `href` values. I can't say whether they work.
- **Tracking.** The ESP click-tracking wrapper is applied consistently. The logo link `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/UdFqiqEo…` follows the same pattern.
- **Coverage.** Only 12 of 42 links were probed and 30 were skipped. The 27% figure covers only the probed links, so it isn't a rate for the whole email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (1 image).** The image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/nJGDrtFnQi6M2RBxCZQwVg~~/AAQRxRA~/ueFwiYjxKBaDPrgZadT4ZYrBlRu1lW…` has no `alt`. The `/q/` path suggests it is a tracking pixel or a content image served through the tracker. If it is content, it needs `alt`. If it is a pixel, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`.** This is a minor accessibility gap in clients that expose the document title.
- **Empty font name.** A `mobile_hide` block has `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty first family is harmless but looks like a template bug.
- **Web font.** The Google Fonts `<link>` for Open Sans is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Many clients strip it, but `Arial,Sans-serif` fallbacks are declared, so this is not a defect.
- **Fine as written:**
  - `viewport` meta
  - `lang="en"`
  - `role="presentation"` on layout tables
  - `mso-table-lspace`/`mso-table-rspace`
  - an image `alt` on the logo (`alt="e.l.f."`)
  - a preheader hidden with `display:none` plus `mso-hide:all`

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The app link carries a `bxid=` parameter with a resolved ID, so personalization was applied.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. Nothing here is evidence of failure.
- **Unsubscribe link and postal address.** The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for one is shown. The footer is outside the truncated source, so the physical address can't be verified.
- **Sender domain.** `m.elfcosmetics.com` sends the mail and `links.elfcosmetics.com` handles tracking. These are consistent first-party subdomains.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters can't be assessed.** The `href`s are opaque tracker tokens, and the probe didn't resolve the final destinations.
- **Product deep links look correct.** The variant parameters match the products (`Size=Jumbo 2.5 oz` on `power-grip-primer`, `Color=Dark Cocoa - Deep Brown` on `cream-glide-lip-liner`).
- **The app link is built correctly.** It carries AppsFlyer parameters (`af_dp`, `af_xp=email`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`). The value was cut off at `sour…`, so I can't tell whether the `source` parameter is intact.
- **Login redirect.** The "Receipt scanning" link lands on `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. That is a login wall, so a logged-out user doesn't reach the promised rewards content directly.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the QA prober.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the previous response URL with `urllib.parse.urljoin`, then re-run. This should clear the 4 FAILs and make the pass rate meaningful.
2. **Check raw headers.** Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the original message, not the relay copy.
3. **Alt text.** Add `alt` to the `/q/nJGDrtFn…` image (`alt=""` if it's a pixel). Also fill in `<title>` and remove the empty `font-family:''`.
4. **Tracking-URL length.** Plain-text URLs make up 12,072 of 14,530 chars, or 83%. If the plain-text part uses the full tracker URLs, shorten them or drop links from the text part.
5. **Probe coverage.** Capture the footer unsubscribe link result and the final URLs, including UTMs, for the full link set, not only the top 12.
