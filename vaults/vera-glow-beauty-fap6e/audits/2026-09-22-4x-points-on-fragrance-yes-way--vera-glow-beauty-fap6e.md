# 4X points on fragrance (yes way!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, 4X points on fragrance is a real hook for me — I'm Rouge, I'm always doing the points math, and 4X isn't the usual "2X on a random category" filler, so my eye actually stopped. Then they back it with "Scent Stacking" and a legit sampler-to-full-size layering mechanic — Kayali, YSL, Dolce&Gabbana, Prada, Phlur, Kilian all stacked in themed pairs (Dessert-Core Duo, Soft Girl Combo, Date-Night Stack). That's actual curation, not just a wall of product tiles, and it reads like someone who knows fragrance TikTok is a thing.
- Where it loses me: this is an absolute scroll marathon. I counted like nine distinct sections before I even hit the Beauty Insider tier bar, and the promo code (NEWSCENTS) is buried three or four screens down when it should've been under the hero. Compare this to Glossier's "Boy Brow just won an award" send I logged — that one had one clear beat and got out. This is Sephora doing the opposite: throw everything at the wall.
- I'd click — the layering combos are genuinely up my alley and I'd want to check if my Kilian or Kayali picks qualify for 4X. Would I forward to a friend? Maybe the Scent Stacking graphic specifically, since my friends and I already do this. But this isn't a "screenshot and text the group chat" email overall, it's a "come back to this later" email, and honestly with fragrance emails I usually just act fast or forget.

## What's working

- 4X points is a real number, not vague "earn more" language — that's the kind of math I actually do in my head against Ulta's 21 Days.
- The layering combos (Dessert-Core Duo, Soft Girl Combo, Date-Night Stack) feel like they were built by someone who fragrance-layers, not just a merchandising template.
- "Only At Sephora" and "NEW" badges are all over the product cards — brand-curation signal is strong, same beat as their 9/21 "Clean at Sephora" send, but this time it's actually backed by exclusivity language on real SKUs.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) near the bottom at least acknowledges the loyalty structure exists.

## What's weak

- This email is way too long — nine-plus sections before the footer, no clear single hero. Ulta's "20% off is calling" send at least kept its offer up top even when reused.
- The 4X points code (NEWSCENTS) doesn't show up clearly until near the very bottom fine print — for a points-math person like me, that's the whole offer, it shouldn't require a full scroll.
- "First Access: Holiday Drops" and "New Chris McMillan" wedged mid-fragrance-email feels like three emails duct-taped together, not one coherent send.
- No mention of my actual Rouge status or points balance — they know I'm Rouge, so "you're 4X-ing at the top tier" would've hit harder than a generic tier chart everyone sees.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the NEWSCENTS code and expiration date directly under the hero banner — right now the offer mechanics are orphaned in the footer, and that's the one thing I actually came here to calculate.
- 2. Cut this down to hero + one layering combo + CTA. Save the holiday drops and Chris McMillan content for separate sends — Glossier's restraint (one beat, done) is the model here, not this kitchen-sink approach.
- 3. Personalize the tier bar — swap the generic Insider/VIB/Rouge graphic for "You're Rouge — here's what 4X looks like on your usual spend."
- 4. Lead the layering section with the priciest combo's total value math (e.g. "$140 value for $98") since that's catnip for a points/value shopper like me.
