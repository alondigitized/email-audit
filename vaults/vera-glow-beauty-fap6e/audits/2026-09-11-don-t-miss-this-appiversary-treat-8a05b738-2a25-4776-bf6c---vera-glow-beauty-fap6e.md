# Don’t miss this Appiversary treat 💅
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Sally's back for the fifth time this week, and it's the exact same 30NAILS Appiversary offer I flagged on 9/7 — just now stamped "Last Day for This Deal" and pushed to app-download instead of straight shop. This isn't my aisle to begin with — no Sephora Rouge points, no Ulta Diamond math, nothing that acknowledges I'm cross-shopping two loyalty programs — but at least this send has a real deadline (ends 9/11) instead of vague "Appiversary" filler, and the four-product grid at least shows something instead of another lifestyle-only hero.
- The "download the app" push up top is where this loses me — I'm not installing an app for a nail supply brand I open twice a year, and burying the actual dollars-off offer below a giant lifestyle tote-bag shot is backwards. Sephora and Ulta would never lead with "download our app" over the offer; they lead with the number.
- This is a mark-as-read, not a click, not a forward. I've now seen this same 30NAILS/Appiversary combo three sends running (9/7, and now 9/11 as "last day"), and there's nothing here that escalates or personalizes for me.

## What's working

- The red "Last Day for This Deal" banner with 30% off + code 30NAILS + explicit "Ends September 11" is a legible, honest, time-bound offer — better than the vague "September deals" framing from 9/3.
- Four real products gridded out (OPI kit, nail glue, wireless drill, press-ons) with names and "Shop now" links — more substance than the pure lifestyle shot on 9/7.
- Fulfillment icons (free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, BOPIS) are clear and scannable.
- Rewards module at least explains the 2x points mechanic for September, even if it's not tiered like my actual loyalty programs.

## What's weak

- Opening real estate goes to "download the app for exclusives" instead of the offer — that's a big ask for a low-frequency shopper like me, and it delays the one thing I actually care about.
- Zero acknowledgment of loyalty tier, purchase history, or anything that signals Sally knows I'm a repeat opener — just a generic "Not a member? Join now" pitch.
- Same 30NAILS code, same Appiversary framing I already got on 9/7 and referenced on 9/4 — no new creative, just a countdown slapped on top.
- The giant tote-bag glamour shot eats a full screen of scroll before I hit any product or offer — Ulta's worst emails do this too, but Glossier and ILIA at least get to the point faster.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the 30%-off deadline banner, not the app-download pitch — that's the actual reason to open, and Sephora's flash-sale emails always put the number first.
- 2. Cut the tote-bag hero photo or shrink it — it's pure scroll-tax with no product or offer info.
- 3. Add any loyalty-tier acknowledgment (even just "Sally Rewards members get 2x points on this order") near the top, not buried after the product grid.
- 4. Rotate creative — this is the third variation of the same Appiversary/30NAILS beat in a week; give it a different angle or retire the promo.
