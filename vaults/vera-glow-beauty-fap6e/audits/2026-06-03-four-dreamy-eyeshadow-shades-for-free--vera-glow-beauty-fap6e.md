# FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades... For FREE! 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Email fourteen from Charlotte Tilbury — and I say that with full awareness that the brand has completely lost track. After eleven days of Exagger-Eyes countdown clocks and 20% off hammers, this one lands differently: it's loyalty-gated, it's product-specific, and the offer is genuinely something. A free Pillow Talk Dreams palette is not a "free sample with purchase" — that's a real unit with real value, and they're leading with it. The "EXCLUSIVE TO LOYALTY MEMBERS!" banner at the top actually made me feel seen for two seconds.
- What knocks it down is the CTA copy. "JOIN + SHOP NOW" — join *what*, exactly? I'm already in the loyalty program. If this is an app-join mechanic, that's a buried ask I didn't consent to when I opened an email about free product. That friction is real and annoying. Also: after thirteen previous emails, I have zero sense of how long this offer runs. No end date, no timer, nothing.
- I'd click — but I'd go in skeptical that the fine print is going to complicate the word "free." I would not forward it. The Glossier squad doesn't need to be pulled into Charlotte Tilbury's email cadence trauma.

## What's working

- **Loyalty-exclusive framing lands well** — the gold "EXCLUSIVE TO LOYALTY MEMBERS!" banner is the first thing my eyes hit and it actually felt like a reward, not a blast
- **The product shot is stunning** — Pillow Talk Dreams palette open, warm rosy tones, immediately recognizable; this is the aesthetic I signed up for
- **Model casting is on point** — rich warm rose-gold eye look on a dark-skinned model is exactly the kind of inclusive beauty signal CT does better than most in the prestige space
- **Free palette is a real offer** — not a deluxe sample, not a GWP mini; a full luxury palette with visible value makes the "free" land as genuine
- **Visual hierarchy works** — eye goes palette → headline → CTA without hunting

## What's weak

- **"JOIN + SHOP NOW" is confusing** — I'm a loyalty member already; the "JOIN" implies a hoop I didn't know about, and that's friction right before conversion
- **No urgency signal anywhere** — ILIA's June 2 launch email had a clear "available now" + countdown; this has no date, no deadline, no "while supplies last" — the offer floats in a void
- **Fourteen emails in, zero acknowledgment of cadence** — Drunk Elephant just ended a two-week hostage situation and came back with a single intentional hero email; CT is still sending like they're behind on quota
- **The app section below the fold feels bolted on** — "Discover the Magic of charlottetilbury.com + App" with three small icons reads as a template footer, not a CTA worth my attention
- **Subject line ALL-CAPS on "FOUR" and "FREE!"** — this is textbook spam-trigger styling and it cheapens a prestige brand; Sephora's best sends never shout in caps

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the CTA copy** — "Unlock My Free Palette" beats "JOIN + SHOP NOW" every time; be specific about what the tap does
- 2. **Add an offer deadline** — even "Offer ends Sunday" is enough; the Sally Beauty BOGO I reviewed recently worked because the deadline was impossible to miss
- 3. **Drop the CAPS from the subject** — prestige brands whisper, they don't shout; reframe the number as exclusivity, not volume
- 4. **Consolidate the app section or cut it** — if the loyalty perk is app-exclusive, say so once, clearly, in the offer block; don't bury it in a three-icon template row
- 5. **Add a single shade/product callout** — Charlotte Tilbury knows my purchase history; even "your most-purchased finish, now in Pillow Talk Dreams" would feel personal

- **Subject Alt B:** `Loyalty exclusive: free Pillow Talk Dreams palette`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Four dreamy shades — unlocked for members only. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The palette you've been eyeing. Yours free this week only.`
