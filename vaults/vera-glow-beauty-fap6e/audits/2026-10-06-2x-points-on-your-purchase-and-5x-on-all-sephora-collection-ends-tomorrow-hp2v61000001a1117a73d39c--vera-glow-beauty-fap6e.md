# 🚨2X points on your purchase and 5X on ALL Sephora Collection ends tomorrow 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- I'm Rouge, so a 2X/5X points subject gets me to open. The email itself is a 9,800-pixel scroll, though, and I only counted about five distinct things in it. The top banner says "Earn Points Faster on Top Brands, Ends 10/7, use code SAYPOINTS." That matches the subject. Then the email turns into "Holiday Vaults Are Here," a stack of advent calendars and gift sets, and the points math is pushed aside. I got the same bait-and-switch yesterday on the 10/5 "APP EARLY ACCESS: Glossier, LANEIGE" send. The subject promised one thing and the body led with something else.
- The points are repeated mid-email with a 2X/3X/4X/5X ladder and again at the bottom with the tier boxes, so I never lose the thread. The $99 Sephora Collection calendar with "$277 value" is the one thing here I'd actually price out. At 5X it's about 495 points on a $99 spend, since Sephora Collection is the 5X brand. I'd click, but only to check that stack. I wouldn't forward it. This is the third near-identical points push in three days, and the 10/6 send is basically a rerun of 10/5.
- I'm reading this from a very reduced-size render, so I can't verify every line of fine print.

## What's working

- The points banner is first, with big 2X and 5X badges and a deadline. A Rouge member sees the math immediately.
- Each vault tile has a price and a "value" figure, like $99 against $277 and $149 against $225. That makes the deal easy to judge.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags create real scarcity on the LANEIGE, Saie and Crown Affair kits.
- The tier boxes at the bottom (Insider / VIB / Rouge) feel like Sephora knows who I am.
- The big barcode gives me an in-store path. Ulta's emails rarely do this cleanly.

## What's weak

- The subject says points, but the email is a long advent-calendar catalog. The offer and the body don't match.
- Points are stated three separate times, and the codes differ: SAYPOINTS, then SAVETEN for "10% off your $100 purchase." Two codes in one email is friction, and I wasn't sure which one stacks.
- "Get Gift Ideas Instantly / AI Beauty Chat" in the middle of the scroll interrupts the sales flow and feels bolted on.
- The "First Access: Holiday Drops" and "Auto-Replenish Hot Deal 15% off" tiles at the bottom are tiny, and I'd miss them. That's weak hierarchy.
- The fine print at the bottom is a dense wall of text. It's too small for me to read on my phone without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one hero. Either "2X points + 5X Sephora Collection" with a single code, or the vaults, not both competing. I'd keep the points hero since that's what the subject sells.
- 2. Collapse the three points blocks into one, and put the Sephora Collection calendar directly under it, since that's where the 5X applies.
- 3. Pick one code per email. Right now SAYPOINTS and SAVETEN compete for attention.
- 4. Add real points math on the calendars, for example "$99 = 495 points at Rouge 5X."
- 5. Move the AI Beauty Chat tile to the footer.
- 6. Make CTAs specific. "Get Gifting" is vague, so use "Shop Advent Calendars."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 5X on Sephora Collection advent calendars`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code SAYPOINTS, plus holiday vaults from LANEIGE, Saie and Tom Ford.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email sent through Epsilon, with no plain-text part. The automated QA run is 50% pass: one FAIL (empty text alternative) and three WARNs. Two WARNs are relay artifacts (the missing headers). The HTML itself is truncated, so link and UTM findings can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 40 skipped (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). QA reported no failures among the probed links, so none are confirmed broken.
- The unprobed 40 links, including footer links, have no coverage.
- The tracking pixel at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a long opaque token. This is normal for open tracking, but it has no `alt`, so QA flags it (see section 3).
- No other tracking issues are confirmed. The truncated source doesn't show the link `href`s.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing plain-text part (FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and affects text-only clients and screen-reader fallbacks.
- **Missing `alt` on 2 images (WARN):**
  - The tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`). It should have `alt=""` and a 1×1 size.
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-...`). This looks like a navigation header, so it likely carries link text, which is lost when images are blocked.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This overrides user text scaling.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html xmlns=...>` tag has none.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't match the subject line.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It's harmless in email but invalid.
- **Leftover comment:** `<!-- US shell snippet -->` remains in `<head>`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]`, and QA flagged none. The body past the `<style>` block wasn't available, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe (WARN) and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) (WARN):** both are missing. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders over 5k/day. Check the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** not captured by the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Verify the From domain `beauty.sephora.com` independently.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but it reports no result for it. Confirm it resolves.
- **Physical address:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source and QA output show no CTA `href`s, UTM parameters or landing page probe results. The only URLs visible are the tracking pixel and the Epsilon image host.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part that mirrors the offer, CTAs and unsubscribe link. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. Add descriptive `alt` text to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop the `text-size-adjust: none` rules (or limit them to Apple data-detector fixes).
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Set a meaningful `<title>`. Fix `no index` to `noindex`.
5. Pull the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC). Then decide whether the warnings come from the relay or the sender.
