# Try our fragrances for free!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "free" in the subject line got me to open, but the actual mechanic is a gift-with-purchase, not a sample program — "Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit with your $50+ order" is GWP, not "try for free," and I clocked that gap immediately. No points math anywhere, no Rouge or Diamond acknowledgment, nothing that tells me Glossier knows I'm a beauty-event person who tracks this stuff across two loyalty programs. That said, the fragrance family lineup itself is genuinely well shot — four distinct scent stories with real notes listed (Iris/Pink Pepper/Ambrox, Palo Santo/Frankincense/Myrrh, Osmanthus/Ylang Ylang, Plum Butter/Toasted Almond) is more scent education than I get from most of these sends.
- This is a keep-scrolling, not a click. I'd open it, skim the discovery kit line, maybe bookmark it mentally as "check if my cart hits $50," but I'm not adding four line extensions of the same base note structure to cart off one email. Not forwarding — this reads more like a catalog refresh than a moment worth sharing.

## What's working

- The "You smell good / rich / pretty / yummy" naming convention is a clean, recognizable Glossier voice thread through the whole email — consistent brand personality.
- Actual fragrance notes are listed for every scent (Iris, Pink Pepper, Ambrox on the original; Buttercream, Plum Butter, Toasted Almond on Rêve) — that's the ingredient-level detail I actually want before I spend on fragrance.
- Product photography is moody and premium — the dark wood pedestal for You Doux, the floral bed for Fleur — this doesn't look like a mass discount blast.
- The GWP hero offer is at least stated with a concrete threshold ($50+) rather than vague "shop now" language.

## What's weak

- Subject says "try our fragrances for free" but the body offer is a GWP tied to a $50 spend — that's a bait-and-switch gap between promise and delivery, and I notice it every time a brand does this.
- Zero mention of Rouge, Diamond, or any loyalty/points angle — compare to Sephora's "4X points on fragrance" send I just logged on 9/22; Glossier isn't even trying to compete on the tier-math axis I actually shop by.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in this email — no "ends tonight," no "while supplies last" on the GWP, so there's zero reason to act today versus next week.
- Four back-to-back "Shop now" CTAs with near-identical copy and layout gets monotonous by the third scroll — the eye stops differentiating them.
- No skin-type or scent-preference personalization — this went out as one static grid to everyone, no "based on what you've bought" signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-offer mismatch first — either actually give me a free sample (mini vial, no purchase threshold) or change the subject to "Free Discovery Kit with $50+" so I'm not primed for something the email doesn't deliver.
- 2. Add a deadline to the GWP — "ends Sunday" or similar — because right now there's no reason not to close the tab and forget it.
- 3. Work in a loyalty angle — even a line like "Rouge & Diamond members: this stacks with your point multiplier" would put Glossier in the same conversation as Sephora and Ulta instead of ignoring the mechanic entirely.
- 4. Break up the four repetitive "Shop now" blocks — maybe a quiz CTA ("Find your scent") up top so I'm not just reading four almost-identical modules in a row.
