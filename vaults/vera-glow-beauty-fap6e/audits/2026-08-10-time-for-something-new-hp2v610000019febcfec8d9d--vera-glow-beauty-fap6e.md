# Time for something new 🛍️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "something new is here" — this is basically Sephora's Tuesday drop email again, the same "new arrivals wall" format I flagged on 8/8 for "Ready, set, shop." Except this time it actually earns its keep a little more: there's a $10 bonus card GWP on a $75 spend, a trial-size mascara offer with real brand names (Charlotte Tilbury, DIOR, Lancôme), and a genuinely stacked new-arrivals grid — Fenty Beauty by Rihanna lip duo, Josie Maran body butter, NEST fragrance, a Too Faced palette. That's a lot of covetable product in one scroll.
- But my Rouge tier bar at the bottom shows the loyalty tier toggle sitting on "INSIDER" with the slider nowhere near VIB or Rouge — I can't tell if that's just decorative nav or actually reflecting my status, and if it's not pulling my real tier that's the same sync gap I keep hitting with K18 (7/9, two days running on "0 points"). Compared to Ulta's stale 20%-off-one-item loop I've now seen three times, this at least feels like a fresh, high-effort merchandising push rather than the same coupon reheated.
- I'd open this, I'd scroll the whole thing because the new-arrivals wall is genuinely well curated for me, and I'd probably tap through to the Fenty duo or the NEST fragrance. Not forwarding it though — nothing here is a "you need to see this" moment for a friend, it's just a solid drop day.

## What's working

- The GWP card up top ($10 bonus card on $75+) is a clean, concrete incentive with a real dollar threshold.
- The trial-size mascara offer names actual prestige brands (Charlotte Tilbury, DIOR, Lancôme) instead of vague "sample" language — that's the kind of specificity Drunk Elephant's "choose your fighter" send lacked.
- The new-arrivals grid is genuinely curated to my taste: Fenty Beauty by Rihanna, Josie Maran, NEST, Too Faced — recognizable, buzzy, prestige-adjacent names, not filler.
- "NEW · ONLINE ONLY" and "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges give real exclusivity signal, something Sally Beauty's generic "beauty deals" roundup never bothers with.
- Clean layout, easy to scan on mobile, no overlapping text or broken images that I can see.

## What's weak

- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom shows "INSIDER" highlighted, not Rouge — if that's meant to reflect my actual status, it's wrong, and it's the same personalization miss I called out on K18 twice this week.
- Subject "Time for something new" is the exact same energy as 8/8's "Ready, set, shop — new beauty has landed" — this is now back-to-back Tuesday-style drop emails with near-identical framing, and the cadence is starting to blur together.
- Twelve individual product tiles is a lot of scrolling before I hit anything beyond "look at stuff" — no single hero product gets the spotlight the way ILIA's Thru Line Eye kit did.
- "Fragrance Worth the Hype" banner at the bottom is vague catch-all copy, feels tacked on compared to how specific the rest of the email is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix or hide the Beauty Insider tier display if it's not pulling my real Rouge status — a wrong tier badge undercuts every other personalization signal in the email.
- 2. Differentiate this from the 8/8 send — either lead with a single hero product (Fenty duo would be my pick) instead of another full "wall of new arrivals," or explicitly tag it as a distinct weekly beat.
- 3. Move the $10 GWP and mascara trial-size offers higher or repeat them near the bottom CTA — they're the most concrete value props here and shouldn't only live above the fold.
- 4. Give the Fenty Beauty by Rihanna item its own callout module — it's the most exciting SKU in this grid and it's buried as a 1-of-12 tile.
