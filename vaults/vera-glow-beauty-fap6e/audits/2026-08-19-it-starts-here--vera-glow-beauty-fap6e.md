# It starts here
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "It starts here" — cute, but this is just Drunk Elephant's cleanser lineup, full stop. No mystery box element, no new launch, just Beste No. 9, Mello Marula, Slaai, E-Rase, and the Juju Bar all lined up like a catalog page. It's clean, it's on-brand, it's very DE — clinical white space, the little elephant sketch, that signature squiggle packaging — but there's genuinely nothing here that couldn't have landed any week this summer. Compare that to ILIA's "1 free mini, 3 ways to wear" (6/10) which at least tied its GWP to a real product story and a gorgeous macro shot; this is just five products in a row with "SHOP NOW" under each one.
- The one thing that actually caught my eye is the top banner: free deluxe sample with new subscription orders $50+. That's a real mechanic, and paired with the bottom "Send in the Cleaner" GWP (spend $75+, pick a deluxe sample + a Protini packette), there's an actual incentive structure here — just buried under generic "wash away summer grime" copy that could be for literally any cleanser brand doing a summer push.
- I'm not clicking off this one. I already own Juju Bar and Beste No. 9, there's no new drop, no bundle math for me to do, and the whole email reads like a reorder reminder dressed up as a lineup showcase. Not forwarding it — nothing here is worth telling a friend about.

## What's working

- The GWP mechanics are real and specific — free deluxe sample on $50+ subscriptions, plus the $75+ "Send in the Cleaner" deluxe + Protini packette offer at the bottom.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with consistent product shots on white — very on-brand DE aesthetic, no render bugs.
- Each product gets its own line with a one-sentence descriptor and its own "SHOP NOW," so if I already know I want Slaai, I can jump straight to it.
- The dark banner up top puts the actual offer (free sample) front and center before I even scroll.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no nod to my purchase history, no "you loved Beste No. 9, try this next," nothing that says DE knows I'm Rouge/Diamond tier anywhere else.
- This is a full-catalog dump of the cleanser line, not a launch or a story — compare to DE's own "Introducing Kamo Drops!" (7/10, their best of the week) which felt like an actual event.
- "Summer grime" is stale seasonal copy in mid-August when fall sets are already dropping at Sephora and Ulta — feels a beat behind.
- The GWP fine print requires real math (new subscriptions only, $75+ after discounts before tax/shipping, one per order) that's buried in tiny gray text at the very bottom, not surfaced near the offer itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the GWP offer as the hero, not buried in a gray banner and a footer callout — if $75+ gets me a deluxe sample AND a Protini packette, that's the actual hook, put it above the fold with real visual weight.
- 2. Cut the catalog dump down to 2-3 hero products max, or reframe as "build your routine" with a bundle discount — five near-identical "SHOP NOW" rows kills urgency.
- 3. Swap "summer grime" for something that acknowledges it's late August — even a soft "reset before fall" angle would feel more current.
- 4. Add a line of member/loyalty framing somewhere — even "Rouge members, your sample ships free" would make this feel less like a stranger blast.
