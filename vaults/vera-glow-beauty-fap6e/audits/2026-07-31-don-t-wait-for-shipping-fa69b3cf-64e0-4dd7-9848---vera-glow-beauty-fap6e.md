# Don't wait for shipping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so my gut reaction to "Don't wait for shipping" is: this isn't really for me. Sally Beauty pivoted from COLORfest confetti mode (7/30) straight into a logistics email — 2-hour delivery, BOPIS discount, Klarna financing — and there's not a single product shot, shade swatch, or model in the whole thing. It's clean, it's on-brand red, and the offer is genuinely easy to find, but it's selling me on *speed* and *payment plans*, not on anything I'd actually want to buy.
- The 15% off BOPIS with no minimum is a real, honest offer and I appreciate that the exclusions note sits right under the button instead of buried three screens down in the fine print — that's more than I can say for some of these sends. But compare this to Ulta's "Lucky you" email that scored a 9 with me: barcode-and-book-stack hero, clear 20% off, felt like it was styled for someone with taste. This Sally send feels like it was built by the ops team, not the creative team.
- I'm not clicking this one. It's not a category I shop from Sally in the first place (I'm here for hair color touch-ups occasionally, not nails or textured hair care), and there's zero reason given why *today* matters to me specifically. Not forwarding, not saving — straight to read and archive.

## What's working

- The hero circle is genuinely easy to read — "15% off your next Buy Online, Pick Up in Store order," no minimum, ends 08/02, all in one glance without scrolling much.
- Exclusions are disclosed right under the CTA button instead of hidden in a wall of legal text at the bottom — small thing, but I notice when brands hide it versus flag it.
- The icon row (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2 Hour Delivery, BOPIS) is a tidy way to lay out fulfillment options without a paragraph of copy.
- Brand voice is consistent — same bold red/black Sally look I've seen in every send since COLORfest, so at least it's recognizable in a crowded inbox.

## What's weak

- Zero product photography, zero models — I can't tell what I'd even be buying with this discount. Glossier's lip tutorial send (9/10 with me) at least shows me the product on skin; this shows me nothing.
- No loyalty math anywhere in the visible email — the footer legal text mentions "Rewards points and reward balance based on account data" but doesn't show me my actual balance or tier. I do this math obsessively for Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond; Sally just name-drops the concept and moves on.
- This is a hair/nails-only brand nav (Hair Color, Hair Care, Textured Hair Care, Nails) — I'm a prestige skincare/makeup cross-shopper first, so the category itself is already a soft miss for me most days.
- The Klarna callout for "4 interest-free payments" reads like it's targeting someone stretching a $35 basket, not someone comparing Sephora Beauty Insider event stacking — mismatched shopper profile.
- No personalization at all — I'm addressed as a generic subscriber ("You are currently subscribed at [email]"), not by name or purchase history, even though Sally clearly has my account data per the footer disclaimer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add my actual Sally Rewards points balance or tier progress in the hero or right under it — if you're going to reference "account data" in the footer, surface it where I can act on it. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Put a real product in the hero — even a flat-lay of a hair color box or a top nail polish shade — so the 15% off has something to attach to visually.
- 3. Swap "Shop now" for a specific CTA tied to the offer, like "Reserve my pickup order" — it's more concrete and matches the BOPIS mechanic being promoted.
- 4. Segment this by category history — if I've only ever bought hair color from Sally, don't lead with a generic storewide BOPIS discount; tell me it applies to my usual aisle.
- 5. Move the urgency into the subject line itself instead of just "don't wait" — tell me the deadline up front.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off BOPIS, no minimum — ends 8/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Sally Rewards points + a flash BOPIS deal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skip the line — order online, grab it in store today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Flash sale ends Sunday: 15% off, no minimum spend`
