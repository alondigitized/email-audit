## Executive summary

- Okay, "You have 0 points" — again, top right, tiny type next to INSIDER, but I clocked it in about two seconds because I'm Rouge and that number should never read zero. At some point this stops being a glitch and starts being a trust problem.
- Past the points bug, though, this is actually a decent send. "JUST DROPPED" into "Only At Sephora" rhode Spotwear patches is a real exclusive with a real price ($16) and a real mechanic — that's more concrete than most of what's landed in my inbox this week. The GWP up top (free Highlight Milk deluxe sample, code RHODETREAT, $30+ purchase) is a second legible offer stacked cleanly above the fold, which honestly beats Sephora's own 8/13 "Beauty you can take from day to night" send where the offer felt like an afterthought.
- I'd click through on the rhode Spotwear tile specifically — patches in a strawberry shape is a genuinely fun product story and I haven't seen it from Ulta or anyone else. I would NOT forward this to a friend though; it's a grid dump after the hero, and the 0-points thing makes me want to open my account and check my balance more than it makes me want to shop.

## What's working

- The GWP banner up top has a real mechanic: size, product name, code, spend threshold — no guessing required.
- "JUST DROPPED" + "ONLY AT SEPHORA" on the rhode Spotwear patches is a legit exclusive-launch beat, and $16 is a visible, specific price.
- The product grid below the fold is well-organized with NEW/LIMITED/ONLINE-ONLY tags doing real filtering work for someone like me who cross-shops constantly.
- INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE tier bar at the footer at least visually acknowledges the loyalty structure exists, even if the number above it is wrong.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" — Rouge tier, and Sephora's sync has now been broken across five-plus consecutive sends (8/11 through today). This isn't a cosmetic bug anymore, it's a pattern I've flagged every single time.
- The hero "JUST DROPPED" section is all product-and-props photography with zero on-model shot — for a strawberry-shaped patch that's a fun visual story, showing it on skin would've sold it harder.
- The mid-email grid is a straight 8-item wall with no ranking — Miss Dior EDP at $115-225 sitting next to a $16 patch and a $24 body mist is a scattershot merch strategy, not a curated one.
- No beauty-event or points-multiplier callout anywhere, which is exactly the kind of content that would make a Rouge member actually engage instead of just scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points sync before anything else — five-plus sends this week with "0 points" for a Rouge member is a data problem, not a copy problem, and it's actively eroding trust every time I open one of these.
- 2. Give the rhode Spotwear launch an on-model or in-use shot instead of just flat-lay props — right now it reads more like a product page than a hero.
- 3. Cut the mid-grid from 8 items to 4-5 and cluster by price tier or occasion so it doesn't whiplash from $16 to $225.
- 4. Add a Rouge-specific line (event access, bonus points multiplier, early access window) somewhere above the fold — right now nothing here treats me differently than a Beauty Insider newbie.
