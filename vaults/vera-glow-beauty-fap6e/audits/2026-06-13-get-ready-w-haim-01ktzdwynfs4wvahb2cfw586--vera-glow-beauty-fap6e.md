# Get ready w/ HAIM
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-five. I'm at twenty-five ILIA emails counted in June, and "Get ready w/ HAIM" arrives as the third installment of the collab arc in five days — following the June 9 launch and the June 11 Este "godsend" quote send. The format is smart: a clean GRWM triptych with a section for each sister — Este's routine, Alana's, Danielle's — capped by a "Win A Signed Vinyl + $500" giveaway at the bottom. Structurally, this is exactly how I consume beauty content on TikTok and YouTube. Conceptually, I get it.
- But here's the honest problem: I already know ILIA is doing a HAIM collab. This is the third time they've told me. This email doesn't give me new information, a new product reveal, or a reason why today is different from June 11. There's no discount, no loyalty hook, no Sephora Insider event tie-in — and for someone like me who is always running points math before tapping "add to cart," the content is nice but the buy signal is missing. The giveaway is the only concrete call to action and it lives below the fold after three full GRWM scrolls.
- I'd mark this read. I'm not clicking through to shop, I'm not forwarding it. Maybe I enter the giveaway if it takes under ten seconds, but that's engagement theater, not purchase intent.

## What's working

- **GRWM triptych is the right format for a three-sister collab** — giving Este, Alana, and Danielle individual sections respects them as distinct humans with different aesthetics rather than collapsing them into one "HAIM x ILIA" monolith. That's a meaningful creative call.
- **Clean render with zero bugs** — three-panel layout holds, images load, no overlapping text. Glossier's Annual Sale emails have been equally clean lately but this has more editorial warmth.
- **Demographic signal is precise** — the styling, photography, and music-meets-beauty intersection is aimed squarely at late-20s women. It reads like it was made for me, not at me.
- **"Start with ILIA" hero copy is punchy** — it sets the participation frame without overselling.
- **The giveaway prize is specific and real** — signed vinyl + $500 cash is a prize that fans of the band would actually want. Not a vague "exclusive bundle."

## What's weak

- **Zero purchase trigger** — no discount, no point multiplier, no loyalty perk, no bundle price. As a Sephora Rouge member, I need a reason to open my app. "Three celebrities I like use ILIA" doesn't move me when I already own ILIA products.
- **Third HAIM email in five days is hitting fatigue threshold** — Ulta pulled the same move with the D&G boat-emoji send this week (twice, same creative) and I clocked it immediately. ILIA is dangerously close to that same wall here.
- **Individual product CTAs are too small to read or tap at mobile scale** — I can make out "daily skin tint" under Este's section, but I can't read what I'm supposed to tap per sister. The CTA per routine needs to be a full-width tappable button.
- **Giveaway buried after three content sections** — the $500 hook is the strongest reason to click anything in this email and I have to scroll past Este, Alana, and Danielle to find it. Charlotte Tilbury at least leads with the theatrical reveal; ILIA leads with content and saves the offer for last.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the giveaway mechanic** — open with "Each HAIM sister picked one hero product. Vote for your fav. Win signed vinyl + $500." Give me a reason to scroll AND a reason to click in the first viewport.
- 2. **Full-width tappable "Shop Este's picks" / "Shop Alana's picks" / "Shop Danielle's picks" buttons per section** — one button each, linking to a pre-filtered PDP or shoppable routine landing page. Make the tap obvious on mobile.
- 3. **Add a loyalty hook if there's any deal to surface** — even "Rouge members earn double points on HAIM collab products this week" as a single line would stop me mid-scroll and make this email feel like it was sent to me, not a list.
- 4. **Either consolidate the HAIM arc or skip this send** — the June 9 launch and June 11 quote email covered the collab. This email should wait until the giveaway close announcement or a product restock, not arrive mid-arc with no new news.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Font loading via `@import` in `<style>` block:**
```css
@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```
Gmail (webmail) strips `<style>` blocks that contain `@import`. Montserrat and the custom Klaviyo font will silently fall back to `"Helvetica", sans-serif` in Gmail. This is a known Klaviyo limitation; fallback stack is defined, so rendering degrades gracefully rather than breaking — but verify the fallback renders acceptably at the brand level.

**No hover differentiation on links:**
```css
a:hover { color:#5f4b3c; text-decoration:none }
```
Hover color is identical to default. In desktop clients that render hover states, there is no visual affordance that the element is interactive. Not a blocker, but an accessibility note.

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title> </title>
```
Some clients (notably older Outlook versions) display the title. Should contain the subject line or brand name rather than a single space.

**Alt text:** Cannot confirm — image tags are not visible in truncated source. Verify all `<img>` tags carry non-empty `alt` attributes for screen reader support and plain-text image-blocking scenarios.
