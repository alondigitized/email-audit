# Build a look around blush
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually a genuinely nice ILIA send — a real 4-step routine build (base, blush, lashes, bonus blur) instead of the flat product-recap format I dinged them for on 8/29 with "Three weeks til fall, two new shades, and one cover story" (3/10, no hero, no button). The flatlay hero is gorgeous, the step-by-step is legible and actually teaches me something, and the close-up model shot at the bottom sells the blush payoff beautifully — soft flush, dewy skin, not overdone.
- That said, I've now seen Overglaze get two dedicated sends in the last week (9/1 new shades, 9/3 "even when you take it off"), and now it's Soft Focus Blurring Blush getting the hero treatment — ILIA's cadence is starting to feel like a rotating spotlight on whatever's newest rather than anything tied to my basket or VIP status. There's no mention of my VIP tier anywhere, no personalized shade match callout despite that literally being a footer icon.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the routine format pulls me in and "Shop the Look" is a real CTA. Would I forward it? Not to a friend, this isn't shareworthy content, it's just a nicely built product email. Mark as read after a click-through.

## What's working

- The numbered routine (Base → Blush → Lashes → Bonus Blur) gives me an actual reason to buy four things, not just one — smarter than a flat grid.
- The Allure 2025 Readers' Choice badge on the mascara is real trust signal, not filler.
- The final close-up model shot shows the blush actually blended into skin — I can picture how it'd look on me, which most brand emails skip.
- "Free U.S. shipping over $50" and the VIP/shade-match/auto-replenish icon row at the bottom are useful, even if buried.

## What's weak

- No VIP tier callout or personalized shade recommendation up top — for a brand that has a shade-match program, that's a miss for someone like me who cross-shops Sephora Rouge and knows exactly how good personalization can look.
- Third Overglaze/blush-adjacent hero push from ILIA in under a week — cadence is getting repetitive even if the creative is good each time.
- No specific % or $ off — just "free mini eye stylus w/ $75+," which is a nice GWP but requires me to do napkin math on whether I'm even close to that threshold.
- Model in the final shot is stunning but it's a single skin tone/type shown — no diversity signal for shade range despite this being a blush/complexion push.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a VIP or shade-match callout right under the "Blushed + Ready" headline — ILIA has the program, just isn't using it in the send.
- 2. Show the GWP threshold math more concretely ("spend $75, get a free mini") instead of the small banner strip — right now it's easy to miss on mobile.
- 3. Space out Overglaze/blush pushes — three related sends in under two weeks risks fatigue even with good creative, unlike e.l.f.'s cleaner single-product 9/1 Halo Glow send I logged at 7/10.
- 4. Add a second skin-tone swatch/model shot near the "Shop the Look" CTA so shade range feels visible before I click through.
