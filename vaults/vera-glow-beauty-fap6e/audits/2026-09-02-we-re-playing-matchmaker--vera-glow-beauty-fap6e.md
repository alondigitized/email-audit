## What's weak

- Two offers competing for my attention — shade finder up top, then a hard-sell free gift with $110+ purchase at the bottom — dilutes the focus. Sephora's 8/29 launch scroll for BYREDO/Dior Backstage stayed on-message the whole way through; this doesn't.

- Zero urgency or dates on the shade-finder push itself — the gift has a hard window (9/1–9/9) but that's mentioned only in the legal fine print, not called out visually.
