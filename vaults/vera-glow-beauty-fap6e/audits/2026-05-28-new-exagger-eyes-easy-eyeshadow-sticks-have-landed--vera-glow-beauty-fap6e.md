# NEW! Exagger-Eyes Easy Eyeshadow Sticks Have Landed 🌟
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Eleven. That's where we are with Charlotte Tilbury. But here's the thing about email eleven versus emails two through nine: this one is doing something completely different, and I clocked that instantly. No countdown timer. No "20% OFF ENDS TONIGHT." No zeroed-out clock like email nine dropped on me. Just a clean product launch — a three-step tutorial for the new Exagger-Eyes Easy Eyeshadow Sticks, with a "Transfer-Proof!" badge right in the hero answering the exact question I'd have about an eyeshadow stick before committing.
- The sequencing is actually smart. Yesterday's send used the Met Gala as a teaser hook for the same product. Today is the drop. That's editorial storytelling, and after nine consecutive discount hammers it genuinely lands differently. The scribble-smudge-swipe tutorial is the kind of content I actually want from a prestige eye brand — not another countdown. The shade swatches do the work of answering "okay but does this come in my undertone?" without me having to click anywhere.
- Would I click? Yes — not because of the "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts" at the bottom (though I'm definitely doing the math), but because this is a new eyeshadow stick launch I haven't seen in-store yet and I need to know if any of these shades are worth adding to my Sephora cart before they sell out. Would I forward it? Only to my one friend who's already a Tilbury stan. Too brand-specific for a general send.

## What's working

- **Transfer-proof callout in the hero.** That's the question every eyeshadow stick buyer has before purchasing — does it crease by noon? Answering it above the fold saves me a Google.
- **Three-step tutorial format.** Scribble, smudge, swipe — this makes the product feel accessible, not intimidating. It's actual product education, not just glamour shots.
- **Visible shade swatches.** I can see the actual color range before I click, which is how I decide whether a launch is relevant to me at all. e.l.f. does this well on their new-shade sends; Tilbury matches it here.
- **Clean editorial reset after the sale barrage.** After nine "20% off" emails, this reads as a genuine product moment rather than another discount lever being pulled. The cadence shift is noticeable and earns some goodwill back.
- **Charlotte's signature quote anchors the aspiration.** "Everyone Can Be An Icon With Exagger-Eyes" with her signature is consistent brand voice — doesn't feel like filler, feels like the brand's actual point of view.

## What's weak

- **Eleven emails in eleven days.** Even a genuinely good email takes a credibility hit at this frequency. I'm reading this through a layer of low-grade Tilbury fatigue.
- **Yesterday's subject and today's both open with "NEW! Exagger-Eyes."** Two back-to-back sends with near-identical subject structures collapse the novelty signal on the second one — both start to blur together in the inbox.
- **"CHARLOTTETILBURY.COM + APP" is not a CTA.** Compare this to how e.l.f. sends me directly to the new-shade landing page — this is a URL, not a button. I want "SHOP THE SHADES" or "FIND YOUR SHADE," not the homepage address.
- **No hero shade called out by name.** If one colorway is the breakout, name it in the copy. Give me a specific thing to want before I've even scrolled to the swatches.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name a hero shade in the CTA or hero copy.** "Get [SHADE NAME] Before It Sells Out" beats a generic site URL every time. Specificity is what converts a browser into a buyer.
- 2. **Differentiate today's subject from yesterday's.** "The Drop Is Here" vs. "Spotted At The Met Gala" would read as two distinct chapters of a story. Two "NEW! Exagger-Eyes..." sends in a row trains me to treat them as one block of noise.
- 3. **Move the "2 Free Mini Beauty Gifts" offer higher.** It's buried near the footer. That offer is Tilbury's strongest argument for buying on their own site instead of through Sephora for Rouge points — if you want me to skip the points math, you have to surface that reason above the scroll.
- 4. **Add a secondary path to Sephora.** Tilbury knows I cross-shop. If this is available at Sephora with my Rouge tier, give me that lane — or at least acknowledge you know I'm going to check.
