# Ready, set, SAVE 🎉 Black Friday is here with 50% OFF ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Ninth Sephora email in about a week, and my first reaction was "okay, this is the 11/27 'Black Friday just got real' send again, just reshuffled." The giant serif "50% OFF" on black sequins is still a great hero. I get the number in half a second, and "in store and online through 11/29" gives me a real deadline. The trouble is that I'd already seen this hero earlier today. That makes this a resend, not a new reason to open.
- The body is where it earns its keep. Two purple "TODAY ONLY" 30% tiles (Paula's Choice and Fenty Beauty, then Farmacy) give me something to act on right now. The 4X points on fragrance sits in the top banner and again on the barcode at the bottom, and I like that. I do the points math on everything, and 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT is the line that makes me care. It's buried in a thin purple strip at the top and a barcode at the very bottom, though.
- I'd click, probably the Paula's Choice or Fenty tile, and I'd screenshot the CYBERSCENT code for my sister. I wouldn't forward the whole thing, because it's a very long scroll and half of it is store hours.

## What's working

- "50% OFF" fills the top of the screen in huge white type. The offer lands instantly, and the through-11/29 deadline is right under it.
- The "TODAY ONLY" 30% tiles for Paula's Choice, Fenty and Farmacy give me real urgency and brands I actually shop.
- The 4X points on fragrance with code CYBERSCENT is the kind of Beauty Insider math I live for. The barcode at the bottom means I can use it in store.
- The "Only at Sephora" tags on Dr. Jart, Summer Fridays, EADEM and Laneige product tiles show prices. I can judge value without clicking.
- The Sephora at Kohl's store finder with hours is useful if I'm shopping in person this weekend.

## What's weak

- This is a resend of the earlier 11/27 "Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊" send, and the "Ready, set, SAVE" subject adds nothing new. Eight sends in a week is a lot, even for me.
- The 4X points offer is crammed into a skinny banner at the top and a tiny barcode block at the bottom. For a points person it's the best deal here, and it's treated like fine print.
- The 50% OFF has a footnote symbol and "available on in-store merchandise only, excludes fragrance" in tiny text. Fragrance gets 4X points but no 50% off, and that mixed message stings.
- The page is very long. After the two 30% tiles I hit a "Cyber Week deals don't stop here" product grid, a "We think you'll love these" grid, a green Olaplex / Touchland / Too Faced tile, a store map and a gift guide. It feels like four emails stacked together.
- The product grids are small and the text is tiny on a phone. The price lines are hard to read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 4X points offer into the hero area as its own visible tile. Points-motivated shoppers are the ones most likely to stock up, and right now that offer is hiding.
- 2. Cut the length. Drop the store map and hours block, or send it only to people near a store, and stop at the gift guide.
- 3. Make the hero different from the earlier 11/27 send. Add a countdown or a "today only" stack so a second email the same day has a reason to exist.
- 4. Spell out the 50% exclusions near the hero in readable text. A clean "50% off select, excludes fragrance" is more honest than a tiny footnote.
- 5. Enlarge the product tiles and put the member price on them. Show which items are 30% off and which are 50% off.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 30% off Fenty + Paula's Choice`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off ends 11/29 (+ 4X points on fragrance)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fenty, Paula's Choice and Farmacy drop to 30% off today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code CYBERSCENT for 4X points on fragrance through 11/30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a fixed-width 700px table layout and wraps every link in `app.sephora.com` click-tracking redirects. The main confirmed defects are the missing plain-text part (0 chars), mobile-hostile viewport and width rules, and malformed CSS and metadata in `<head>`. The HTML source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and address checks rely on QA data only.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Opaque redirects:** links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a21d5302189982d0369fb45280/…?__dU__=…`. Final destinations can't be read from source.
- **Probe coverage:** QA probed 12 links and skipped 36. It reported no broken or failing links among those probed, so no link failures are confirmed.
- **Open pixel 1:** `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` is a hidden `<img>` with no `alt` attribute (QA WARN).
- **Open pixel 2:** `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa…` is a second tracking pixel with no `alt` (QA WARN). Two separate open-tracking vendors fire per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part missing (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars. This can hurt spam scoring and leaves text-only clients with nothing. The AgentMail relay may have dropped it, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **Not responsive:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The only `@media (max-width:480px)` rules change font size and image dimensions, and none override the table width. Mobile clients will scroll horizontally or shrink the whole layout.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments aren't valid in CSS there. Per CSS parsing rules, this likely invalidates the `img.mso-size` rule and the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows it, so the Outlook size override and that mobile rule probably never apply.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. It is harmless in email but malformed.
- **Pixel `alt` attributes:** both pixels need `alt=""` to satisfy the alt-text checks. The first is also hidden with `display:none`, so it isn't a screen-reader issue in practice.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN):** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers. That is unconfirmed. If the sender omits them, the email fails the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirement (RFC 8058).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` can't be verified from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the footer is cut off in the provided source. Neither can be confirmed or flagged.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs not verifiable:** links use the `app.sephora.com/T/…` redirect with an opaque `__dU__` token, so no UTM parameters are visible in the source.
- **Campaign ID:** the only readable one is `mi_ecmp=20261127_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Fa…`, which is truncated.
- **Landing pages:** QA did not report landing-page status or content alignment. A redirect-chain trace of the primary CTAs is needed to confirm UTMs and destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a text/plain MIME part** with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
2. **Fix mobile layout:** replace the fixed `min-width:700px` with `max-width:700px; width:100%`, or add a media-query override. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. **Move the mso fix:** put the `<!--[if mso]>` block outside the `@media` rule, and validate the `<style>` block so the dropped rules apply.
4. **Add `alt=""`** to both tracking pixels, and consider consolidating to one open-tracking vendor.
5. **Check the raw headers:** confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass on the original message rather than the relay copy.
6. **Fix the JSON-LD:** the `DiscountOffer` has an empty `"discountCode": ""`. Populate it or remove the block so Gmail doesn't process an invalid annotation.
7. **Trace the redirect chains** for the top CTAs to confirm final URLs and UTMs.
8. **Correct the robots meta value** to `noindex`.
