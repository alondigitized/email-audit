# Want a free gift?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Glossier's THIRD email in four days about the same Labor Day sale — I logged "Save big on fall favorites 🍂" yesterday at 5/10 and "Save 30% off select favorites" on 9/3 at 6/10, and now here's "Want a free gift?" which is just... the same 30%-off mechanic with a free-gift-with-purchase bolted on top. Same hero product grid (You Rêve, Body Spritz, Stretch Balm, Lip Glaze), same orange buttons, same airplane-window solid perfume lifestyle shot I've seen before. The only new beat is the $75+ free You Solid gift, buried two-thirds down the page after four other CTAs.
- There's zero tier or points math here, which is my whole thing — no Rouge, no Diamond, nothing. This is a mass sitewide sale email, not a "we know you're a VIP" email, and after three of these in one week it's starting to feel like Glossier is just spraying the same sale copy at everyone and hoping something sticks.
- I wouldn't click from this one specifically — I already clicked (or at least considered clicking) on the version from two days ago. Not forwarding, not deleting either, just letting it sit. Ulta's stacking-weekend email did this so much better — offer front and center, points attached, done.

## What's working

- The free-gift-with-$75 hook is a genuinely different angle than the last two sends — that's the one new piece of info here.
- The 5-star review block with a real customer quote ("Adison") adds some trust/social proof I don't usually get from Glossier.
- Product grid is clean — two products per row, price context via the "50% OFF" flag badge on the Stretch Balm.
- Brand voice and visual style (soft pink, orange CTA, clean type) stays consistent with everything else I've gotten from them.

## What's weak

- Third near-identical 30%-off email this week — I'm fatigued on the "sale favorites" grid at this point.
- No tier or loyalty math anywhere — no points, no Rouge/Diamond-equivalent status callout, nothing that treats me like a repeat customer.
- The free-gift offer, which is the actual headline hook ("Want a free gift?"), doesn't show up until after FOUR unrelated product CTAs — that's real hierarchy failure for a subject-line-driving offer.
- The gift itself (a You Solid) is only shown as an unlabeled product photo near the bottom — I had to scroll to even confirm what the "free gift" was.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free-gift block to the top, right under or replacing the 30%-off hero — it's the subject line's whole promise, so lead with it, don't bury it after four unrelated shop-now buttons.
- 2. Add any loyalty signal — even a generic "double points this weekend" line would separate this from Sephora and Ulta's tier-driven sends and give me a reason to care beyond price.
- 3. Consolidate — stop sending three sale emails in one week with the same grid; if the mechanic hasn't changed, don't re-send, just bump urgency on the existing thread.
- 4. Label the gift product photo directly ("Your free You Solid ↑") so I don't have to guess what I'm getting.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free gift is waiting (spend $75, it's yours)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off + a free Glossier You Solid, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a full-size fragrance on us — plus 30% off sale favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Labor Day sale + a free gift. Ends soon.`
