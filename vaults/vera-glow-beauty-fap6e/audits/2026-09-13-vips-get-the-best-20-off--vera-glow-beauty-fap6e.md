# VIPs get the best + 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "nothing but the best" — ILIA's leaning hard into that VIP framing today, and I get it, but here's the catch: this is ILIA's second "VIP" push in as many days after the 9/12 "The ILIA VIP Sale starts now" send (8/10), and this one is actually a step down. Yesterday's email led with a hard 20% sitewide number and stacked free gifts front and center. Today's is the same 20% sale but gated behind "JOIN VIP for free" as the primary CTA — so now I have to sign up for a program before I even see if I qualify for the discount I already know exists. That's friction I didn't have yesterday.
- The bestseller polaroid grid is genuinely well done — Super Serum, Limitless Lash, Lip Sketch, the Base Face Milk — real product shots with award badges (Allure, 14,000+ reviews) doing actual trust-building work, not just vibes. That part reads more premium than Sephora's Daily Deals emails, which just slap a pepper emoji on a percentage and move on.
- Would I click? Probably, because I already have ILIA in my cart-rotation and the bestsellers list is legit tempting (that multi-stick freebie especially). Would I forward it? No — this is a "join our loyalty tier" email dressed up as a sale email, and my friends who aren't already ILIA people would bounce off the VIP-gate confusion before they got to the products.

## What's working

- The bestseller polaroid layout with award badges (Allure, star counts, "12-hour wear") gives me real reasons to buy, not just "new drop, trust us."
- Free gifts section (velvet bag on $100+, multi-stick on any order) is clearly itemized — no buried fine print.
- "VIP TREATMENT" benefits box spells out free shipping, points, and early access in plain language — that's the loyalty math I'm always doing anyway.
- Clean minimal palette (blush/cream) feels on-brand and calmer than Sally Beauty's neon-badge chaos I flagged this week.

## What's weak

- The 20% off is sitewide-for-VIPs, but I have to join a program to unlock it — that's a bait-and-switch beat, not the straightforward "20% off sitewide" yesterday's send promised.
- Two "VIP sale" emails back-to-back in 24 hours (9/12 then 9/13) is starting to feel like the Sally Beauty Appiversary rerun I dinged this week — same offer, different headline dressing.
- No mention of my actual Rouge/Diamond tier status or points balance anywhere — Sephora's Daily Deals at least references my points (even if it botches the number), ILIA treats me like a cold lead here.
- "shop 20% off bestsellers" CTA appears mid-email but the top CTA is "join VIP + shop the sale" — two competing asks stacked, muddies the hierarchy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the discount, not the club — swap "JOIN VIP + SHOP THE SALE" for a direct "SHOP 20% OFF NOW" as the primary button; let VIP enrollment happen at checkout, not as a gate.
- 2. Cut the redundancy with 9/12's send — if it's the same 20% sale, say what's new today (extra gift tier? fewer hours left?) instead of just reskinning the same offer with new hero copy.
- 3. Add a personalization line acknowledging existing customers/points — even a simple "as a returning ILIA shopper" would beat the blank-slate treatment.
- 4. Trim the two competing CTAs into one clear path — sale button up top, VIP-benefits button lower where the benefits box already lives.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off is here — no membership required`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bestsellers, 20% off, plus a free gift (ends soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Serum, mascara, lip crayon — all 20% off. Free multi-stick with any order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the wait: shop 20% off bestsellers before the VIP sale ends.`
