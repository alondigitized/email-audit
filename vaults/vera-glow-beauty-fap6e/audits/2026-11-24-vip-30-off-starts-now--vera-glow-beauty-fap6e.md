# VIP 30% off starts now!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: finally, a Drunk Elephant email that says what's happening today. The countdown banner, "Hey, VIP!" and a flat 30% off sitewide with the code DRUNKVIP in a cyan block all land fast. After the 11/22 "PSA: 30% off starts soon!" tease, this is the payoff. I just wish the 11/22 send had been this clear.
- It's a clean, loud Cyber Week email. The code is easy to copy and the $69.30 Protini price is right there. It reads as a real VIP early-access send, not a Sephora or Ulta copy. Sephora's Cyber Week email gave me a 24-hour brand list, and this one gives me one brand and one number.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the Protini. Would I forward it? Maybe to a friend who's been eyeing the Bespoke kit. It's another 30% send in a week of 30% sends, though, so I'd mostly just do the math and move on.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (00 days, 14 hours, 52 minutes) gives me urgency I can believe, and it sits above the hero.
- "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE" is huge and unmissable, so I don't have to hunt for the number like I did in the 11/19 "Plump from the inside out" email.
- The code DRUNKVIP is written out in the body, so I don't need to wonder whether the discount applies automatically.
- The product grid shows the math: Protini at $69.30 struck from $99.00, and Kamo at $29.40 from $42.00. Star ratings and "881 people are checking this out" add social proof.
- The free gift with a $100 order (Beste No. 9 cleanser plus Protini) gives me a spend threshold to plan around.

## What's weak

- There's no loyalty or tier math. As a Sephora Rouge, I want to know whether this stacks with Beauty Insider points, and nothing says so.
- The "up to 40% off" banner contradicts the "everything is 30% off" message. The fine print says the 40% applies only to select last-chance items, and kits are excluded.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, and it matters. It says the 30% can't be combined, excludes gift cards, and the free gift needs $100 after discounts but before tax and shipping. I had to read it twice.
- "You lucky duck!" feels a bit forced to me. There's also no personalization beyond "VIP", so no skin type and no category.
- Only four products are shown, and they're all skincare. There's nothing for my makeup-heavy cart, and no shade info for the Bronzing Drops.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the offer hierarchy. Commit to either 30% sitewide or "up to 40%" in the banner. Mixed numbers make me doubt the math.
- 2. Put the free-gift threshold in plain text next to the 30% code, something like "Spend $100, get the Beste + Protini kit free." Don't leave it in 8pt fine print.
- 3. Add a one-line stacking note. Even a simple "Can't be combined with other offers" up top would beat burying it in the footer.
- 4. Add a "Bestsellers by concern" row, or a "Gift sets" link, so I'm not stuck with four SKUs.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML email with CSS-parsing problems and three non-HTTPS image URLs. The recipient's email address is also exposed in a tracking-pixel URL. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay didn't capture the headers. The source was truncated, so findings below are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 19 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains and final status codes are unverified.
- **Plain-text email in a URL:** The Everest pixel is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The recipient's email is unhashed in the query string. It will show up in proxy logs, image-proxy caches and referrer data.
- **Two tracking pixels:** `mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmallvipstart` and the Everest URL above. Both are open or engagement pixels. They give no signal when images are blocked, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection inflates them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (mi.drunkelephant.com) and the Everest pixel have no `alt` attribute. Tracking pixels should have `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` where appropriate.
- **Mixed content:** Three social icons load over `http://`: Facebook `cc02d07d-…png`, Instagram `9b452915-…png` and TikTok `29f975e1-…png`. All are on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`. Some clients and proxies will block or rewrite them. These images do have alt text.
- **Invalid `@import`:** `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` comes after `body{…}` in the `<style>` block. `@import` must precede all other rules, so browsers ignore these three imports. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` still load the fonts.
- **Malformed CSS inside `@media (max-width:600px)`:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` are HTML comments, not CSS comments. Inside an at-rule block, the `<!--` and `-->` tokens become part of the selector prelude. Those rules are dropped, and the following `*[class=vouchertext4]` rule is likely dropped too, because the `-->` prefixes it.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** The `<html>` tag has none, only `xmlns`.
- **Font dependency:** The custom fonts depend on `db.onlinewebfonts.com` and the drunkelephant.com Demandware static path, which is a versioned path (`v1719565389232`). No fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were visible in the subject or the truncated source. The only personalization is the email in the Everest URL, covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so presence is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, and image hosting is on the same subdomain, which is consistent.
- **Footer not visible:** The in-body unsubscribe link and postal address were in the truncated portion, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters in the visible source:** Campaign identifiers appear only as vendor parameters: `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmallvipstart` and `c=us_de_broad…`.
- **Landing pages unverified:** Links resolve through redirectors that were not probed. The VIP 30% offer, and whether the destinations are the Demandware `drunkelephant.com` storefront, can't be confirmed from the data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the plaintext email from the Everest pixel URL, or hash or tokenize it.
2. Switch the three social icons to `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`.
3. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
4. Move `@import` to the top of `<style>`, or drop it, since `<link>` already loads the fonts. Replace the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside `@media` with `/* */` or remove them.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Also resolve the 19 redirect links to confirm destinations and `utm_*` handling. The QA pass can't determine these through the relay.
