# Save up to 25% off before it’s too late
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- Day nine, if I'm keeping score right. Glossier has been hammering some version of "25% off" into my inbox since June 15 — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance," then "Save up to 25% off Glossier favorites" later the same afternoon, then "What to buy with up to 25% off," then yesterday's actually-clever "Friends don't let friends pay full price." So when this "before it's too late" lands, my first instinct isn't urgency, it's eye-roll. The deadline has been expiring since Wednesday.
- That said — and I want to be fair here — the creative inside is doing something the previous sends didn't. The "Recommendations From You" angle, with actual community quotes next to Glossier You and Balm Dotcom, reads more like peer curation than a blast template. That is legitimately different from the past week of interchangeable sale grids, and it's the one thing that nudges me toward clicking rather than archiving.
- I'd click, but narrowly — and only for Glossier You because I've been considering a full size forever and the community pull sealed it. I would not forward this. The campaign has exhausted my "text a friend about this deal" impulse entirely.

## What's working

- **Community sourcing angle** — "Recommendations From You" reframes the product grid as peer curation instead of a markdown dump. It's the first creative hook this sale has had since day one.
- **Tiered offer copy** — "25% off sitewide OR 20% off $100+" is more useful than a flat-percent headline because it helps me instantly math out which threshold works for my cart size.
- **Hero products with real quotes** — Glossier You and Balm Dotcom are the brand's anchor SKUs, and framing them through shopper voice adds the lightweight social proof that a straight % headline can't.
- **Subscription positioning** — anchoring the subscription pitch to the sale ("lock in 25% off permanently") is clever bottom-of-funnel logic. If I'm already buying, the upsell is low-friction.
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, no overlapping copy, three-column product layout reads clearly on mobile.

## What's weak

- **"Before it's too late" is a cry-wolf situation** — Glossier has been telling me the sale is "almost over" since June 15. The urgency language reads hollow after a week of identical cues, and "ENDS SOON" without a specific timestamp lands like wallpaper at this point.
- **Three identical "Shop now" CTAs** — every product card uses the same button copy. Drunk Elephant ran cleaner differentiation on their June 17 send, where secondary links carried the action context. Three generic Shop Nows in a row is a click-fatigue trap.
- **No hard deadline anywhere visible** — "ENDS SOON" without a date or countdown means my brain defers. If I see "Sunday midnight," I plan around it. Vague urgency reads as soft bait.
- **Subscription module creates cognitive split** — "Start a subscription and lock in 25% off" is a completely different offer from the sale. Burying it below three product cards means neither message lands clean.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stamp a hard deadline front and center** — "Ends Sunday at midnight" is six words that would outperform every piece of urgency copy currently in this email. ILIA's June 16 send had a specific event window and it made the action feel real; Glossier's vague ENDS SOON feels like a holdover.
- 2. **Differentiate the CTAs by product** — "Shop Glossier You," "Get Balm Dotcom," not three "Shop now" buttons that blur into each other. Charlotte Tilbury does this well even when rotating the same offer, and it materially improves the feel of curation vs. blast.
- 3. **Spin the subscription module out to its own post-sale send** — the day the sale closes, hit me with "Missed 25% off? Lock it in permanently." Competing with the sale CTA in one scroll-session dilutes both.
- 4. **Give the hero a power SKU** — "community favorites" is still a category, not a product. Lead with Glossier You by name in the hero headline and let the recommendations section reinforce it. Specificity converts.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Community's top picks: 25% off — sale ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Glossier You, Balm Dotcom + more before the sale closes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Real shoppers picked these. Real deadline this time — midnight Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The community's most-loved Glossier products, 25% off until Sunday.`

## Technical Email Audit — Glossier "Save up to 25% off before it's too late"

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on a Klaviyo-delivered XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with standard MSO conditionals. One structurally invalid element introduces a silent failure risk; everything else in the visible source is well-formed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Issue: `<span>` used as stylesheet/resource loader with `href` and `rel` attributes**

```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KVB5CK3DBKCRPKT1P0VV07VX_0"></span>
```

`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This element will be silently ignored by all conforming parsers — it will not load a stylesheet, fire a request, or register as a click. If this is intended as a stylesheet injection or a Klaviyo open-tracking hook, it is broken. The correct element for a stylesheet load is `<link rel="stylesheet" href="...">` in `<head>`. If it is intended as an open-track pixel, it should be a 1×1 `<img>` with `src`.

- **Domain confirmed:** `ctrk.klclick.com` is Klaviyo's click/tracking CDN — sending domain alignment is correct.
- CTA links in the body are not visible in the truncated source; cannot audit UTM passthrough on those.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Preheader padding uses mixed zero-width characters**

The preheader filler block mixes U+034F COMBINING GRAPHEME JOINER (`͏`) and HTML soft-hyphen entities (`­` / U+00AD):

```html
<span style="display:none ...">Explore product picks from our community ͏ ͏ ͏ ... ­ ­ ­ ...</span>
```

Soft hyphens (`­`) can render as a visible hyphen glyph in Outlook 2016+ and some Samsung Mail versions. The U+034F block alone is sufficient to suppress preview text leakage; soft hyphens should be removed.

**Preheader/subject mismatch**

Hidden preheader: *"Explore product picks from our community"*
Subject: *"Save up to 25% off before it's too late"*

These are thematically unrelated. Email clients that surface the preheader alongside the subject (Gmail, Apple Mail, Outlook mobile) will show a confusing secondary message. This is a functional rendering concern, not just a content one.

**DOCTYPE**

XHTML 1.0 Transitional is in use. Functionally acceptable, but `<!DOCTYPE html>` (HTML5) is now the safer default — it prevents Outlook's quirks-mode box model on newer builds and is what Klaviyo, Litmus, and MJML all emit by default.

**No image `alt` text confirmable** — source is truncated before `<img>` tags appear. Flag for QA pass on full source.
