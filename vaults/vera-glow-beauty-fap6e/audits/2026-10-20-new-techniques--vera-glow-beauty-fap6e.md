# New techniques
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the first ILIA email in a week that isn't a sale or a gift push. It's a tutorial, and I like it for that. After the 20% sends, "The (2) gifts of VIP" on 10/16, the repeat on 10/17 and "Before the bag disappears" yesterday, a step-by-step eye look is a real change of pace. The illustrated eyes and the 01/02/03 steps feel like an editorial, not a catalog.
- But I'm a points-math girl, and there's nothing here for me to calculate. The only offer is a tiny "Mini mascara with any $75+ order" strip at the very top. It's the same gift that was the hero three days running, now demoted to a banner. The three "Shop the look" buttons all say the same thing and don't tell me which products I'm buying or what they cost. I'd read it on the train and probably not click.
- I wouldn't forward it. If ILIA's stuff were in my Sephora cart already, I might screenshot the smoke look for a friend. Sephora's tutorial-style sends at least put a price and a "add all 3" button next to the steps. I'd mark this as read and save it.

## What's working

- The hero ("the art of the eye") is a close-up with a clean, editorial feel. It's the opposite of the promo-banner sameness I've seen from Glossier all week.
- The 01/02/03 steps with product swatches are easy to scan. I can see Eye Stylus, Thru Line and Limitless Lash at a glance.
- Three looks (nuanced neutrals, color theory, smoke effect) give me something to pick from. "Based on your eye color or mood" is a nice personalization nod, even though it's generic.
- The shade callouts under each illustration ("Eye Stylus in FAWN, Thru Line in ABSTRACT") tell me exactly what to buy.
- The footer has "Personalized Shade Match" and "Join ILIA VIP", which are trust cues. Free U.S. shipping over $50 is also stated clearly.

## What's weak

- The offer is a thin banner at the top, easy to miss, and it's the same mini mascara I've already been pitched three times this week. The $75+ threshold also isn't tied to anything in the body.
- All three CTAs read "Shop the look →". I can't tell what they link to, and there are no prices anywhere.
- There are no individual product links or "add to bag" buttons. The steps list products but don't link them.
- VIP is only mentioned in the footer. I'd want to know the points or tier angle after the gift emails that pushed it so hard.
- The subject is vague. "New techniques" doesn't say anything about eyes, a tutorial or an offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make each step's product tappable, with a price. Add a "Shop all 3 looks, $XX" bundle button under each look.
- 2. Move the mini mascara gift offer into the first screen below the hero, as a real message, not a top strip. Tie it to these looks, for example "Build your look, get the mini mascara at $75."
- 3. Make the three CTAs specific: "Shop the neutral look," "Shop the smoky look," and so on.
- 4. Add a line on VIP points for these products. It would give a returning shopper a reason to buy here instead of at Sephora.
- 5. Add a social proof or review quote under at least one look.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 eye looks, 3 pencils, 1 mascara`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your eye color, 3 easy looks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Step-by-step neutrals, color and smoke, plus a free mini mascara at $75.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Match your look to your eye color. Shades, steps and where to start.`
