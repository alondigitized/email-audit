# Stop scrolling: Today’s deal is here 😲
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Sixth Sephora email in six days, and the purple sequins are back. The subject says "Stop scrolling," but this is a very long scroll. The 30% off Sunday Riley / Fresh / Skinfix trio is a decent hook, and it's the only reason I'd stay past the top. After 11/25's "Wait, you're going to want in on these Cyber Week deals," this one feels like leftover creative with a new daily deal slotted in.
- The 24-hour clock is credible, and I like that it's brand-specific. The free sample bag banner at the top and the 4X points on fragrance (code CYBERSCENT) at the bottom are the two things I'd actually do math on. They sit at opposite ends of the email, though, and neither one is in the subject. Below the deal block it turns into a catalog: "Shop More Must-Have Deals," "Your Next Beauty Obsession," gift guides. I'd click the Skinfix or Sunday Riley tile and mark the rest as read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The three stacked "30% OFF" tiles are huge, and I couldn't miss the offer. Each has its own SAVE NOW button.
- "24 hour deal—get 30% off these top brands" gives me a deadline that feels real, not manufactured.
- The 4X points on fragrance module (11/23–11/30) is Rouge-tier math I actually care about. I'd stack it with a gift purchase.
- The product rows show real prices with sale and original side by side (Dr. Jart, Sol de Janeiro, Summer Fridays), so I can judge the discount without clicking.
- The "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "NEW" tags help me spot exclusives, which is how I cross-shop against Ulta.

## What's weak

- The subject promises one deal and the body delivers three brand tiles plus a sample bag, a points promo, a lip gift and a gift guide. It's too much.
- The sample bag banner at the top competes with the 30% hero. Two offers fight for the first screen.
- Nothing speaks to me as a Rouge member. There's no tier perk, no points balance and no "your Rouge benefits" line.
- The purple sequin background has been the same since 11/22, and it's fatiguing at this point.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text at the bottom, and the exclusions (in-stock only, "Excl. terms apply") are easy to miss.
- It's the sixth send in six days, with nothing new in the structure.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Put the 30% tiles first and move the sample bag and points promos lower, or run them as separate sends.
- 2. Add a tier line: "Rouge: your 4X points stack on top." That's the math I'm doing in my head anyway.
- 3. Refresh the creative. After six days the sequins feel stale. Even a color swap would signal that something changed.
- 4. Cut the "Shop More" and "Next Obsession" rows. The email is about the deal.
- 5. Put the countdown in the preheader so urgency shows before the open.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Sunday Riley, Fresh & Skinfix — 24 hrs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: 30% off 3 skincare brands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends tonight. Plus 4X points on fragrance with CYBERSCENT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part (QA FAIL, 0 chars), and the source has a few markup and accessibility defects (zoom-blocking viewport, a malformed conditional comment in the CSS, and a fixed 700px wrapper). The missing List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers are probably relay-capture artifacts, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 42 skipped. The QA data reports no failures among the probed links, but the 42 skipped (footer, utility and social) are unverified.
- All visible links go through opaque Epsilon/Sephora redirects (`https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a2186…/…?__dU__=…`). Final destinations can't be checked from the truncated source.
- The open-tracking pixel is `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` with inline `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It is correctly hidden, but it lacks `alt`, which is what QA flagged. It is also self-closed in an XHTML doctype and sits in the same `<td>` as the preheader.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing plain-text alternative** (QA FAIL, "Text version is 0 chars"). This hurts deliverability scoring and users of text-only clients.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed-width wrapper:** the main table is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The only visible media query (`max-width:480px`) targets `.mobile*` and `.footerBanner` classes, not this wrapper. In the visible source, nothing overrides it, so mobile may scroll horizontally. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm this.
- **Malformed conditional comment:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. This is not valid CSS, and some parsers may drop the rules after it (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and others).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The impact is minimal.
- **Missing alt text:** the tracking pixel has none (flagged by QA). If alt text matters for the pixel, use `alt=""`.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` are visible in the truncated source.
- The JSON-LD block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""`, an empty value that looks like an unpopulated template field. `PromotionCard` is not a valid schema.org type, and the context uses `http://schema.org/`. Gmail may ignore or reject this markup.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN):** the QA tool itself says the relay may not capture it, so this is unverified.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN):** RFC 8058 one-click support is unconfirmed. It is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` can't be validated from this data.
- The QA note says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported. The footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use redirect tokens (`__dU__=…`) and no visible `utm_*` parameters. Campaign attribution is presumably handled server-side in the redirect.
- Landing-page alignment can't be assessed because the probe results and final URLs are not provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a multipart plain-text alternative (fixes the QA FAIL).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` query, and make the 700px wrapper fluid on mobile (`width:100%; max-width:700px`).
4. Verify the raw headers on the original message, outside the relay: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel. Fix `content="noindex"` and the empty `discountCode` in the JSON-LD.
