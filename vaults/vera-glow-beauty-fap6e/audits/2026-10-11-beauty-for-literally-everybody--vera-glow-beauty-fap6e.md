# Beauty for literally everybody
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the third Sephora catalog dump in three days, and the Insider header never uses my Rouge status. The "Beauty Your Way" hero with the Kayali Yum Body Collection looks pretty. It's a body-care launch, though, and I can't tell if there's any math in it. Then there's a six-tile "New + Only at Sephora" grid with prices and "value" amounts. Under that sits a Huda 4X points tile, which is the same Huda promo I got on 10/9. The MASCARA trial-size strip at the top is the only real offer, and it's tiny.
- I'd open it, because the sender name is Sephora and I'm Rouge. I'd skim the product grid for launches, glance at the 4X tile, and probably not click. I'd forward the Huda 4X tile to a friend, but only if she's buying Huda anyway.

## What's working

- The Kayali launch is clearly labeled "New" and "Only at Sephora," so I get launch-day clarity at a glance.
- The grid tiles show price and value, like Kiehl's $72 for a $110 value and Josie Maran $36 for a $48 value. That's math I can do on the spot.
- The "Clean at Sephora" and "Planet Aware" badges give me a quick filter without reading any copy.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar near the bottom (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is a nice loyalty cue, and the 4X Huda block is bold and readable.

## What's weak

- The trial-size offer is jammed into a thin strip at the top, with a promo code in a small badge. It's the same MASCARA / code-gated structure as the Dr. Groot send on 10/8, which I also found buried.
- The 4X Huda points tile is a repeat of the 10/9 "Hey, Insider" send, and it needs the code HUDA4X and the app. That's friction for something I've already seen.
- "Beauty Your Way" with "Love this for you" claims personalization, but nothing in it tells me it's about me. There's no skin tone, no Rouge perk and no points balance.
- The six-tile grid has no CTA buttons and no reviews, so I don't know which launch to tap. Ulta would at least slap a "Shop now" under each one.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and the points terms are buried down there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer the hero. Put the 4X points or the free trial size up top with a real button, and push the Kayali launch to second.
- 2. Use my tier. Show "Rouge: your 4X is live" or a points balance, since I'm Rouge and Sephora already has the data.
- 3. Dedupe the cadence. After the 10/7 2X/5X send, the 10/8 Dr. Groot send and the 10/9 Huda send, don't resend Huda a third time. Sephora is out-repeating even Glossier right now.
- 4. Add a "Shop" button or "Add to bag" under each grid tile, and show star ratings.
