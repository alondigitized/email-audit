# Choose your own fragrance adventure!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so yesterday I got the hoodie origin story — a full nostalgia trip about the original Glossier Hoodie — and today I open my inbox to fragrance customization. I appreciate the topic pivot (Pink Capsule → hoodie → fragrance is a real editorial rotation, not just the same hero repackaged three times), but the tonal whiplash is real. No throughline, no "and speaking of feeling good in your Glossier…" moment. Just: new concept, go.
- The "choose your own adventure" framing is genuinely smart for a fragrance launch. Glossier is selling a configurable set concept — Travel Spray, Trio, Duo, You+Balm — each anchored to a different "travel style" scenario. The alternating layout is clean and readable. The "Build your own" CTA in the hero is clear. What kills it for me is the complete absence of pricing. I am Sephora Rouge. I do the math. I cannot do the math here because there is no math to do. Charlotte Tilbury landed in my inbox this week screaming FOUR eyeshadow shades for FREE. ILIA followed a drop with a full ingredient breakdown. Glossier gives me vibes and four "Customize yours" buttons and expects me to leap.
- I'd click the "Build your own" out of curiosity, not intent. I wouldn't forward this — there's nothing urgent or exciting enough to trigger a "omg have you seen this" text.

## What's working

- The "choose your own adventure" framing is a genuinely fresh concept for a fragrance email — the scenario-matching (Trio for multitaskers, Duo for staycations, You+Balm for road trips) is editorial in a way that feels like actual copywriting.
- "Build your own" in the hero is unambiguous — one clear action before you even hit the product breakdown.
- Alternating image-left / text-right layout is easy to scan and doesn't collapse at mobile widths.
- The summer travel hook lands seasonally — this feels like a June email, not a recycled asset.
- Clean render, no broken images, no layout collisions — Glossier's production quality is consistently solid.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing anywhere — not even a "starting at $X" anchor. For someone who mentally calculates cost-per-use and points-per-dollar, this is a wall. Ulta would have put the offer price in the hero.
- No Sephora loyalty callout — Glossier's DTC model means I can't earn Rouge points, and they're not doing anything to compensate for that (no exclusive, no GWP, nothing).
- Four separate "Customize yours" CTAs creates decision fatigue before I've even committed to the category — no hierarchy between products, no "most popular" signal.
- The day-after pivot from hoodie nostalgia to fragrance configuration feels editorially disconnected — no bridge copy to carry me from one world to the next.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price anchor in the hero.** Even "Sets from $X" would let me start the mental math. Right now I have to click into a configurator just to find out if this is in my budget.
- 2. **Pick one hero product and go deep.** The four-scenario grid is clever in theory, but a single "Travel Spray — try our bestseller You" with real copy depth (notes, size, what it layers with) would convert better than four shallow vignettes.
- 3. **Add a "most popular" or "community favorite" badge** to one of the four styles — social proof without testimonial blocks.
- 4. **Build a storyline from the hoodie email.** Yesterday was comfort and origins. Today could be "that same feeling, in a bottle." The throughline is already there — Glossier just didn't use it.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid tracking element — confirmed defect.**

```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KT7YXC0MQWSAHYWEKER6GYBE_0"></span>
```

`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on a `<span>` element. This construct does nothing in compliant renderers. If the intent is a CSS file load, it must be `<link rel="stylesheet" href="…">` in `<head>`. If the intent is a tracking ping, a 1×1 `<img>` or a Klaviyo open-pixel tag is the correct mechanism. As written, this call is silently dropped by all major email clients — any open-tracking or style-loading tied to this URL will not fire.

**Tracking domain:** `ctrk.klclick.com` — Klaviyo's standard click-redirect subdomain. No anomaly there beyond the broken element above.

**Full link audit blocked** by truncated source. All CTA hrefs and image src values beyond the first screen are unverifiable from the supplied excerpt.
