# Your A-list-beauty edit
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I've now logged FOUR of these "50% off Select Beauty" banners in six days — 8/28 twice, 8/29 twice, and now this one on 8/30 — and it's the exact same pink "TODAY ONLY" pill stapled on top of what's actually a completely different email underneath. Once you scroll past that banner, though, this is genuinely one of the better "Iconic Beauty" launch drops I've seen from Sephora: Prada Paradoxe, Charlotte Tilbury Airbrush Setting Powder, Miu Miu, Sol de Janeiro — that's a real new-launch lineup, not filler.
- The problem is the banner is doing all the SEO/preview work and it's stale. I've seen "50% off Select Beauty" so many times this week it's basically wallpaper now — Ulta's "20% off is calling" barcode hero has run three times since 7/26 and I dinged them for it, and Sephora's doing the identical thing to itself. The new-launch content is the actual story here and it's buried under a promo I've already seen four times.
- Would I click? Maybe, to check the Charlotte Tilbury powder since it's Sephora-exclusive. Would I forward it? No — nothing here feels urgent or unique enough to text a friend about, and I'd rather just check the app when I'm ready to shop.

## What's working

- The "Iconic Beauty" launch wall is legit — Prada Paradoxe, Miu Miu fragrance, Charlotte Tilbury, Sol de Janeiro all in one scroll is a stronger new-launch cadence than Glossier's single-fragrance-family rehash from 8/27 or ILIA's zero-image bullet newsletter I scored a 3.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags show up repeatedly (Prada, Charlotte Tilbury, Crown Affair) — that's real curation signal, the thing that actually makes me choose Sephora over Ulta for a launch.
- Clean Beauty and Planet Aware badges are visible on Living Proof and Crown Affair — I do filter by that, so it's nice it's flagged inline instead of me hunting for it.
- The layout is scannable — each product gets its own color block, name, one-line description, and price range, so I'm not squinting at a wall of text.

## What's weak

- The hero banner is the fourth near-identical "50% off Select Beauty TODAY ONLY" send this week — I'm numb to it at this point, and it's cannibalizing attention from the actual new-launch content below.
- Zero Beauty Insider points math anywhere — no "you have X points," no tier callout, nothing showing my Rouge status. Ulta at least tries to force a name and coupon code into the subject even when it's repetitive.
- No pricing specificity on most items — "Touch Cream-To-Powder Blush, $42" is fine, but half these tiles just say "NEW" or "ONLY AT SEPHORA" with no price, so I can't do my usual mental math before I even click in.
- The bottom Insider signup block reads like it doesn't know I'm already a member — asking me to pick "Insider / VIB / Rouge" like a stranger instead of just showing my current tier and points balance.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the recycled 50%-off banner or at least vary the creative — I've seen this pink pill four times in six days and it's actively training me to skip the top of the email.
- 2. Lead with "Iconic Beauty" and the new launches — that's the actual news this week and it's getting buried under stale promo real estate.
- 3. Add my points balance and Rouge tier status somewhere near the top — even Ulta manages to shove a name and code into the subject line; Sephora showing zero personalization here is a miss for a top-tier member.
- 4. Put prices on every tile, not just some — I'm doing points math in my head before I click, and half these products give me nothing to work with.
- 5. Fix the bottom tier-signup module to recognize existing members instead of pitching "Insider vs VIB vs Rouge" like I haven't already picked one.
