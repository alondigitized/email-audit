# Open for our bag edit
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- After back-to-back duplicate sends — "4X points on ALL hair care" appearing twice yesterday, and the World Cup lip emails stacking up the day before — "Open for our bag edit" at least arrives in a different creative lane. I'll give Sephora that. The Charlotte Tilbury lead is genuinely smart: CT as a "Now Only at Sephora" exclusive is the kind of thing that makes being Rouge feel like it means something beyond just accumulating points. And the GWP (free CT mascara, right at the top) is a real offer, not a tease.
- But the email then scatters. Below the Charlotte Tilbury section I get a product grid with Caudalie, what I think is Dr. Jart, L'Occitane, Peace Out — with no through-line, no discount hook, no reason to browse. Then "Try It, Love It," then "Best of Lip," then oil-balancing hydration. It reads like three separate emails stitched together and labeled "bag edit." Drunk Elephant's "Golden hour every hour" got called out for being wordplay-over-substance, and this subject does less work than even that — "bag edit" doesn't tell me Charlotte Tilbury is inside, doesn't tell me there's a GWP, doesn't tell me anything. The email earns more than the subject deserves.
- I'm clicking the Charlotte Tilbury section. I'm ignoring the rest. Mark as read after that.

## What's working

- Charlotte Tilbury "Just In" + "Now Only at Sephora" is the right exclusivity signal — that phrasing makes the Rouge tier feel like access, not just math
- GWP (free CT mascara) is above the fold with a clear "SHOP TO REDEEM" CTA — no confusing points thresholds, just spend and earn
- The Mini Pillow Talk at $9 is a smart low-commitment entry point; gives me a reason to add to cart even if I don't need mascara right now
- Warm peachy editorial tones and the model read as clearly directed at me — this doesn't feel like a gender-neutral blast or a 45-year-old's inbox
- This is at least not the 4X hair care template again; the editorial "bag" framing signals a creative reset even if the execution is scattered

## What's weak

- "Open for our bag edit" as a subject is the single biggest miss — after five emails in three days, I need a reason to override Sephora fatigue, and this subject gives me nothing
- The product grid below the Charlotte Tilbury section completely loses the narrative — Caudalie and L'Occitane are fine brands but they have no hook here, no offer, no ingredient callout; ILIA at least makes you feel their editorial POV in a single section
- Zero urgency anywhere — no end date, no "while supplies last," not even a vague "this weekend only"; Glossier threads their editorial emails with a quiet time pressure that this completely lacks
- "Try It, Love It" and "Best of Lip" feel copy-pasted from a different campaign; there's no editorial voice tying it to "what's in our bag"
- The GWP fine print is real (points threshold, full-size purchase condition visible at top) — for a non-Rouge shopper this offer might feel exclusionary once they scroll

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put Charlotte Tilbury in the subject.** "Free CT mascara inside" or even "CT Just In — and there's a gift" outperforms "bag edit" with zero downside.
- 2. **Kill three sections.** The email should end after Charlotte Tilbury + the GWP. Caudalie, L'Occitane, "Try It, Love It," "Best of Lip," and oil-balancing hydration each deserve their own send — or at minimum a single connecting narrative voice, like Glossier uses when they stack product rows under a campaign concept.
- 3. **Add any time signal.** "Available this week only" costs nothing to write and lifts urgency from zero.
- 4. **For Rouge: show the multiplier if one exists.** If there's a 3X or 4X on Charlotte Tilbury right now (which there sometimes is during multiplier events), that is the email. That's what made the July 19 hair care sends worth opening even as duplicates.
- 5. **Let the bag metaphor earn its keep.** If you're going to say "what's in our bag," write each section as if someone actually curated it — a quick editorial line per product, a reason it made the bag. Glossier does this better than anyone; Sephora has the editorial credibility to pull it off and keeps choosing not to.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Charlotte Tilbury Mascara — yours with any CT purchase`
- **Subject Alt B:** `CT Just In: bag edit, bonus gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Charlotte Tilbury arrivals, exclusive to Sephora. Plus a free mascara when you shop.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `What's in our bag? Charlotte Tilbury, Caudalie, Peace Out — and a free gift for you.`
