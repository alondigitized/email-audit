# Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a "create an account" ad dressed up as a Black Friday email. I've been on Glossier's list for weeks. I got three balm sends in a row (11/12, 11/15 and "Final Hours" on 11/17), so I expected a date, a number and a reason to act. This one tells me the sale starts Wednesday only in the subject line. Inside, the CTA is "Create account," which I clearly don't need.
- The hero also contradicts itself. A giant "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" sits next to a "30% OFF $100+" burst in the corner, and the asterisk gives me no terms. I can't do my points math when I can't tell whether it's 25% or 30%, or what the threshold is. Sephora and Ulta both lead with a start date and the tier-by-tier timing. This one makes me guess.
- I wouldn't click. If I'm already a member, nothing here speaks to me. I'd read it as a half-finished teaser and wait for Wednesday's send. I wouldn't forward it either, because I'd have to explain "25 or 30?" to a friend.

## What's working

- The 25% and 30% numbers are huge and instantly readable. The royal-blue hero is unmistakably Glossier and stops the scroll.
- Early access for members is a real perk. It's the same mechanic as Sephora's Beauty Insider early window, so I get the idea immediately.
- The "Why join?" list is scannable: early access, birthday gifts and event invites. It's clean and fast to read.
- The model photo with the pink bottle in the bubble bath fits the brand. It also reads as someone around my age with real skin texture.

## What's weak

- There are two competing discounts, 25% off everything and 30% off $100+, and the corner burst is cut off. I can't tell which is the real offer.
- "Create account" is the wrong CTA for me. I've gotten Glossier emails for weeks, so I'm already in the database. I don't know whether the "member" language means a paid tier or just a free account.
- The asterisks on "EVERYTHING*" and "member today.*" lead to a tiny "Membership terms here" footnote. Nowhere does the email give me the Wednesday start date, the end date or any exclusions. That feels like buried fine print.
- There's no product, no hero SKU and no shade or skin-tone cue. After the Balm Dotcom push, I'd want to see what's in the sale. The "SALE SALE SALE" ticker strip adds nothing.
- The footer's giant smiley and hand art is cute but takes up space that should hold a deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the date and the single offer up front: "Members get 25% off Wednesday." Drop one of the two discount numbers, or show 30% off $100+ as a clearly labeled second tier.
- 2. Segment the send. Existing customers should get "Shop early access" or "Set your reminder." Only non-members should see "Create account."
- 3. State the terms in the body: start and end dates, exclusions and the member threshold. Put them in plain text, not behind an asterisk.
- 4. Add two or three hero products with prices, such as Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint and You Perfume. Show what 25% off actually saves me.
- 5. Swap the "SALE" ticker for a countdown to Wednesday.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything starts Wednesday, members first`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early access to Black Friday opens Wednesday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members shop 25% off everything first. Here's what to add to your cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access opens Wednesday: Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint and more.`

- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 2. The skipped links aren't identified, and the source shows no broken ones.
- **Stray tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7P27Z15ZB379CVZN7HR5KC2_0"></span>` is the first element in `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid attributes on a `<span>`. It looks like a Klaviyo injection artifact. It is harmless but not valid markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `frame_4042.jpg` (`qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_877f…`)

  - The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7P27Z15ZB379CVZN7HR5KC2`. It should carry `alt=""`.
