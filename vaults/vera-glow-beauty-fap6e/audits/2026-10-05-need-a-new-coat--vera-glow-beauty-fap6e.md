# Need a new coat?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- I read "Need a new coat?" and thought outerwear. Then I opened it and it was a skincare layering routine. That's a cute pun, but it cost the subject line its honesty. ILIA is also in my inbox for the fourth time in nine days, after 9/27, 9/30 and 10/2, so the novelty is gone.
- Inside, the "a new fall coat" hero is pretty. The close-up skin shot and the four-step "layer it" routine are easy to scan. But the only offer is the red bar at the top: a mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $100+. On 9/30 the same bar offered a mini eye stylus at $75+. The threshold went up $25, and I noticed.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the Barrier Base Skin Protectant Cream, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. I'd forward Sephora's Rouge 2X/5X points banner because it hands me math I can use. This one just says "claim gift."

## What's working

- The "a new fall coat" hero with the close-up dewy skin is clean and on-season, and the headline reads in about two seconds.
- The "layer it" Step 1–4 zigzag is easy to scan. Each product has its own Shop Now, so I can jump straight to the one I care about.
- "Bright Start Retinol Alternative Eye Cream" tells me what it does. That's the ingredient-level detail I look for.
- The mascara section, orange and blue sky with a "Mini Mascara Gift" badge, is editorial and eye-catching, like the 9/27 seasonal-sets send.
- The footer trust row is nice: free shipping over $50, ILIA VIP, auto-replenish, and shade match.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the email is about a coat. The subject promises something the body doesn't deliver.
- The only offer is a GWP behind a $100+ threshold with an asterisk. There's no discount and no points angle. Compare Sally Beauty's "20% off, code BIGDEALS20" in a big circle, which is a clear price offer.
- The mascara gift is buried near the bottom. The same gift is in the top bar, but it's small, low-contrast text.
- There's no price on any of the four products, no reviews, and no sizing or shade information. Drunk Elephant at least put its $90 / $143 value burst on the kit.
- The footer VIP and shade-match items are tiny icons. I'm an ILIA subscriber, but nothing here treats me like a member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-body mismatch, or lean into it with a hero that says what's actually in the email. Put the GWP in the hero, not a thin top bar.
- 2. Add one real value line. For example, "Free mini mascara ($XX value) with $100+" gives me something to do math with, the way Drunk Elephant's value burst does.
- 3. Put prices and a star rating under each step, and add a "build the full routine for $X" bundle CTA.
- 4. Surface VIP or loyalty pricing near the top, so I have a reason to compare it to my Sephora Rouge math.
- 5. Use a more specific CTA than "Shop Now" on the product tiles, such as "Add Barrier Cream."

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Klaviyo-built, MJML-derived HTML message with tracked links on `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. Two images lack alt text, and the QA run couldn't verify the unsubscribe headers or SPF/DKIM. Those header gaps are probably capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender defects.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The QA probed 12 material links and skipped 13. It reported 0 broken links among the probed set. The 13 skipped links, mostly footer, utility and social, were not verified.
- The tracking-pixel image `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4691J7KGMZ6C325N37Y7A5B` is served from a tracking subdomain. That is normal for an open pixel.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't check individual `href` values beyond what the QA probed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. If this image carries content or a link, a screen reader or an image-blocked client will show nothing or the filename.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M4691J7KGMZ6C325N37Y7A5B` has no `alt`. This is low impact for a 1×1 pixel, but adding `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Empty `<title>`:** The source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor for email, but some clients and web-view versions use it.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** The source imports `https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css` and `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat`.
