# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Sephora send in about 72 hours pushing the same Sephora Collection 30% event. The 10/30 "STARTS TODAY" sends, the 10/31 "Get 30% off ALL Sephora Collection 😍" and the "personal holiday shopper" email all carried it too. The top banner does its job: "Happening now," a big 30% medallion, 10/30–11/9, and "Use code ALLONSALE." But right under it the email forgets the sale. The next screen is "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS," then a Jo Malone London holiday block, then a long run of Merit, Dieux, Dae, Gisou, L'Occitane and Make Up For Ever. None of that is Sephora Collection, and none of it shows 30% off.
- So the email pitches a house-brand sale and then shows me a full-price prestige gift guide. I'm a Rouge and Diamond cross-shopper, and I do the points math on every send. I can't tell what the 30% applies to once I've scrolled past the banner. I'd open it from habit, tap "Shop Now" once, and not come back. I wouldn't forward it. A friend would have already seen the same banner five times this week.

## What's working

- The top banner is clear. "Happening now," the 30% medallion, the dates and the code are all stacked together, and I can read the whole offer in two seconds.
- The "code ALLONSALE" line gives me something to act on. It's better than Ulta's "Up to 50% off Early Black Friday^ (Terms & excl. apply)" send, which hid the real terms behind a caret.
- The product cards show prices with the value next to them, like the Merit set at $68 ($92 value) and the L'Occitane advent calendar at $110 ($186 value). That's the "what am I actually saving" math I want.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" labels are good launch-day curation signals. They sell exclusivity better than a generic gift grid.
- The barcode at the bottom for in-store use is a smart touch for someone who shops Sephora in person. I just wish it were higher up.

## What's weak

- The hero offer and the body don't match. The 30% is on Sephora Collection, but nothing from Sephora Collection shows up in the product grid. Glossier at least kept its free-mug hero consistent all the way down.
- The email is very long, and there's no Beauty Insider or Rouge points angle anywhere. I can't see tier points, a points multiplier or a member perk. Sephora is the brand I stock up with at the Beauty Insider event, so this reads like I'm a stranger.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop Now," "Shop Now," "Shop Now." Compare Sally Beauty's "Create your look today" with 2-hour delivery, which gave me a reason to click.
- The fine print is a dense wall at the bottom, and the useful part, the barcode for 30% off Sephora Collection with 10% off the rest of the purchase from 11/3, is buried under it.
- The cadence is exhausting. The 11/1 send is the same banner as 10/30, 10/31 and the AI-shopper email. At this point I'm skimming the banner and moving on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put Sephora Collection products directly under the banner: 3–4 hero items with the sale price shown. Make the gift guide section two.
- 2. Add a Rouge or Beauty Insider line, such as "Rouge: earn X points on top." That's the only reason I'd pick this over Ulta's 21 Days.
- 3. Move the barcode or in-store code up under the hero, and shrink the legal block.
- 4. Change the CTAs to something specific, like "Shop Sephora Collection 30% off."
- 5. Cut the send frequency, or vary the creative so day 4 doesn't look like day 1.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge: 30% Sephora Collection ends 11/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Sephora Collection picks worth the 30%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code ALLONSALE + 10% off the rest from 11/3. Barcode inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Lip sets, palettes and skincare gift sets, all 30% off through 11/9.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` block has no override for `.tbl` or `.tbl_cntr`. The truncated source may contain one later, but if not, this forces horizontal scroll on mobile.
- **Viewport:** `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Invalid CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments there are not valid and may break the following rules in some parsers. The rules affected are `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` and the ones after it.
- **Alt text (QA warnings):** Both images missing `alt` are tracking pixels (`/O/AQEAAZcF…` and `o.gif`). The first is `display:none`, so the practical impact is low. Add `alt=""` to both.
- **Minor markup:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
  - The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip it, and it adds no value.
  - `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
