# Take a closer look—select beauty is 50% off 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a genuinely different build from the last five "50% off Select Beauty" sends I've logged since 8/28 — Sephora actually led with "THE NEXT BIG THING," a real new-launch rail (Natasha Denona eyeshadow palette, Violette_FR lip treatment, plus a whole scroll of NEW and ONLY AT SEPHORA tags), before it eventually gets to the same tired 50% off TODAY ONLY banner I've now seen practically daily this week. That's a smarter order of operations than what I've been getting — new-launch discovery up top, deal buried where it belongs for someone like me who already knows the promo exists.
- But my Beauty Insider points math is nowhere. I see "4X points per dollar" in the top banner and a Rewards Bazaar teaser at the very bottom, but no "you have X points" number tied to me specifically, and the 50% off banner doesn't say if it stacks with points multiplier or redemption. I'd probably click through on the new-launch section out of genuine curiosity — Natasha Denona palettes are a real want for me — but I'm not forwarding this to a friend. It's solid, not exciting, and it's the sixth near-identical deal banner from this brand in nine days.

## What's working

- The "NEXT BIG THING" launch rail up top is a real point of differentiation from the last several Sephora sends I logged — it's not just another 50% off wrapper.
- ONLY AT SEPHORA and CLEAN tags are doing real curation work — that's the brand-exclusivity signal I actually shop for versus Ulta's mass-prestige mix.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." app-early-access module is a smart mid-funnel CTA that Ulta rarely bothers with.
- Before/after on the Tower 28 Swipe Serum Foundation gives me a real product-detail signal I care about before I click.

## What's weak

- The 50% off Select Beauty banner is, again, the exact same red "TODAY ONLY" pill I logged on 8/28 (twice), 8/29 (twice), and 8/30 (twice) — this is now the sixth time this exact mechanic has hit my inbox in nine days, and it's fatigue at this point, not urgency.
- Zero visible points balance or personalized Rouge-tier callout — just a generic "4X points" banner, so it doesn't feel like they know I'm Rouge specifically.
- The email is enormously long with a lot of near-duplicate product tiles (perfume mist, hair mask, body spray, foundation) stacked with no clear priority order — my eye doesn't land on one hero, it just scrolls forever.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the sixth consecutive "50% off Select Beauty" banner or at minimum change the creative — Glossier and Drunk Elephant both varied their formats this week while Sephora just restaples the same pink pill.
- 2. Put my actual points balance and Rouge tier badge next to the 4X banner — that's the single thing that would make this feel personalized instead of blasted.
- 3. Trim the mid-body product grid to 3-4 hero items max instead of 8+ near-identical tiles — right now nothing outranks anything else visually.
- 4. Move "New Swipe Serum Foundation" with its before/after higher up — that's a stronger hook than half the filler tiles above it.
