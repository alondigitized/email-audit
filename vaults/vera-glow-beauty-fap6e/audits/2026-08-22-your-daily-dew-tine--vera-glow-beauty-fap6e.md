# Your daily dew-tine 💧
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. SKIN doing "Summer Skin Must-Dews" and "Your Daily Dew-Tine" is cute copywriting, but this is straight-up skincare — toner, gel serum, lip treatment, cleanser — not the color cosmetics/GWP hijinks e.l.f. usually runs, so my brain has to context-switch. Everything's under $13 which is the whole e.l.f. value prop working as intended, and the "get Bronzing Drops on us" $50-threshold GWP banner mid-scroll is a real, honest offer I recognize from their playbook (same beat as 8/19's "Get Bronzing Drops on us" send, just recycled here as a secondary CTA instead of the headline).
- The problem is this isn't really "for someone like me" in any Sephora Rouge / Ulta Diamond sense — there's zero tier-program math, no loyalty callout, no skin-type personalization, it's just a straight product catalog scroll. Compare that to ILIA's "wore it to the swimming pool and it didn't run" send I gave a 7 this week — that one had a specific, provable claim. This is just "dew-tine, dew-tine, dew-tine" wordplay stacked over four product blocks with identical pink pastel gradients, so by the third bottle my eyes glaze.
- I wouldn't click past a maybe-tap on the Thirst Burst Drops (I actually do need a hydrating serum), but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — it's an e.l.f. drugstore-aisle scroll, not an event. Marking it read, possibly circling back for the $13 serum only because it's genuinely cheap.

## What's working

- The hero shot — model with the pink Thirst Burst Lip Treatment mid-swatch — is a strong, on-brand open, good lighting, feels current.
- Prices are printed right under every product name ($12, $13, $8 each) so I don't have to hunt or click through to know what I'm committing to.
- The Bronzing Drops GWP banner is a legit, specific offer (full-size on $50+, add Pure Gold to cart) — no vague "free gift" language.
- "NEW!" badge on the Lip Treatment trio is clear launch signaling, which e.l.f. has been doing consistently across recent sends (the pickle-drop launch on 8/20 had similar badge treatment).

## What's weak

- No loyalty/rewards language anywhere — no beauty squad points math, nothing tying this to e.l.f.'s Beauty Squad program, which is a miss for a persona like me who's always doing the tier math.
- The whole email is one repeating template — product photo, name, price, one-line benefit, pink pill CTA — four times in a row with almost identical pastel-splash backgrounds. By product three it reads as filler, not curation.
- "Top Picks for You" is not personalized at all — it's just three more skincare SKUs in a row, no "based on your purchases" signal, no name mention anywhere in the email.
- The category nav bar (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) at the bottom is just gray blocks stacked vertically — reads more like a broken table of contents than a real navigation module.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Bronzing Drops GWP as the hero, not buried two-thirds down — that's the one offer with real urgency/value, and it's currently competing with three individually-priced products for attention. Ulta's "20% off ends tonight" send at least puts its one offer up top; this one makes me scroll for it.
- 2. Add a loyalty tie-in — even one line like "Beauty Squad members: earn 2x points on Thirst Burst" would make this feel like it knows I'm a repeat shopper, not a cold-list send.
- 3. Cut the product count from four full blocks to two, and use the space for a real skin-type callout (dry/oily/combo) — right now "quenching," "hydrating," "dewy" is used on every single product, so nothing differentiates who each one is actually for.
- 4. Fix "Top Picks for You" to actually reflect purchase history or browsing, or rename it — as-is it's misleading personalization.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Thirst Burst Lip Treatment, $8`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free full-size Bronzing Drops (ends soon)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: 3 new drip-glaze lip shades, $8 each`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beauty Squad members, your dew-tine starts here`
