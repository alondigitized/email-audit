# Hot drops coming through
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a decent launch roundup, but it's the fourth Sephora email I've logged in about two days, and the subject gives me no reason to open this one over the others. After the 10/2 "50% OFF select brow products 🤨" and "Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves" sends, I'm in full points-math mode. This email did give me something to calculate: a 2X/5X points banner with "Ends 10/7" and a code at the top. That's what actually got my attention, and the "Hot Drops" hero is second.
- The body is a clean one-product-per-block launch feed, with Haus Labs, Summer Fridays, OUAI, YSL, Charlotte Tilbury, Moroccanoil, Ultra Violette and LoveShackFancy. I like seeing the prices and the "NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags. The bottom is a mess, though: Holiday Exclusives teaser, auto-replenish, Rhode, a 30% off Fenty hair block and a wall of fine print. It feels like a newsletter stapled to a promo.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the YSL Black Opium Pink Glaze or the Charlotte Tilbury powder, after checking whether the 5X applies to those brands. Would I forward it? No, because there's nothing here a friend couldn't find in the app.
- Compared with the others: Ulta would have put the 20% coupon in the subject line and made me hunt for the new launches. ILIA's "It's Back (with NEW shades)" sold one product in two seconds, where Sephora shows eight and leaves me to pick. Glossier's "Your fall face awaits" had no offer at all, so Sephora wins on giving me something to calculate.

## What's working

- The "Earn Points Faster on Top Brands / Ends 10/7 / SAXPOINTS" banner gives me a deadline, a code and a multiplier above the fold. That's exactly the math I want.
- Every launch block has the brand, "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA · LIMITED EDITION" and a price. I can triage without clicking.
- The Haus Labs hero with the model close-up is the strongest image, and the Summer Fridays and LoveShackFancy blocks add a few Clean at Sephora badges.
- The "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon! Build your basket" teaser makes me want to wait before spending, which fits how I shop the Beauty Insider events.
- The barcode at the bottom means the in-store points event works straight from my phone.

## What's weak

- The subject "Hot drops coming through" is vague. I can't tell it's a launch roundup or a points event from the inbox.
- The points banner at the top competes with the Hot Drops hero. Two headlines fight for the first screen, and there's only one SHOP NOW button for a long scroll.
- I can't tell which of the 2X and 5X brands are in the product blocks, so I can't work out my points without clicking through.
- The last third is a pile of unrelated modules, including the Fenty 30% hair block marked "TODAY ONLY", which repeats Friday's hair and brow send. The fine print at the bottom is tiny and unreadable.
- The product blocks are all large and similar, so the email is very long on mobile and I'd stop scrolling before the holiday teaser.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the subject and put a "2X/5X" badge on each eligible product tile. That one change would lift the email most, because it ties the launches to the points math.
- 2. Split launches and promos into two sends, or cut the bottom modules (auto-replenish, Rhode, Fenty) so Hot Drops owns the email.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy on each block ("Shop Haus Labs liner", not just a global SHOP NOW).
- 4. Show Rouge-tier perks, such as early access to Holiday Exclusives, instead of treating me like a generic Insider.

  - The `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF...` image.

  With images blocked, these render empty or as filenames. The header nav image is the most impactful, since it likely carries navigation content.
