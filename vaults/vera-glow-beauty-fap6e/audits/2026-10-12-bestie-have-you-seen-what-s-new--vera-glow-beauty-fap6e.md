# Bestie,, have you seen what's new? 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- "Bestie" got me to open this, and then it handed me a roundup. I've now seen the Advent Calendar on 10/9 and Squeeze Me Flavor Flashback on 10/8 as their own sends. This email is both of them stapled together with a free-mascara gift on top. It's the "greatest hits" digest, so nothing in it feels new to me. The Fruity O's, Milk Carton and Mall Pretzel flavors with those pastel pink-phone models are cute, but I've already seen them.
- The free full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara with $50+ is the part I'd actually do math on. It's a real gift-with-purchase, and the "no code needed" line is good. I'd still want the offer in the hero. It's the second block down, and the top of the email is a donut photo with "THE elf EDIT" in a sliced-off banner. Sephora would lead with the GWP and tell me my Rouge points on it. Ulta would at least put a member price up front.
- Verdict: I'd click once on the mascara gift and skim the rest. I wouldn't forward it, because it's the tenth-ish e.l.f. send this month and there are no member perks anywhere. It's a mark-as-read for me unless I'm already building a $50 cart.

## What's working

- The free full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara with $50+ and "no code needed" is a clear, honest offer. It's the one thing I'd do math on.
- The flavor swatch labels (Fruity O's - Green, Milk Carton - White) tell me the shade names at a glance.
- The "OG VALUE: $153 / OMG PRICE: $95!" badge on the Advent Calendar makes the value easy to see. That's the same $95 / $153 math I did on 10/9.
- The diverse models in the Flavor Flashback grid feel current and relatable. It's a good Millennial nostalgia hook.
- The sticky category nav (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) is a handy way to browse by category.

## What's weak

- The hero is a donut and fork photo with a cropped "THE elf EDIT" banner. It says nothing about makeup, an offer or a launch, and the top of the email is wasted.
- The email recycles things I've already seen: the 10/9 Advent Calendar and the 10/8 Squeeze Me launch. "What's new" is a stretch.
- "Squeeze Me flavor flashback" shows "just for $51" with a tiny "$5" price reading that I can't verify. There's also no per-product CTA under the swatches, so I can't tap straight to a shade.
- The category buttons are empty gray bars. They're big, but they say nothing and have no images or featured items.
- There's no loyalty or points mention, and nothing for my skin tone or type. The sender is treating me like a stranger.
- The fine print at the bottom is tiny, and the mascara terms are buried there. That includes the $50 threshold and the end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the free mascara GWP in the hero: "Free full-size mascara with $50+". Drop the donut shot.
- 2. Pick one story per send. A weekly roundup is fine, but keep it to a single new item. Three offers in one email dilutes all of them.
- 3. Add a points or rewards line for members, the way Sephora does for Beauty Insider events.
- 4. Link each flavor swatch to its product page, and replace the empty category bars with product thumbnails.
- 5. Move the mascara terms and end date above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free full-size mascara with $50+ 👀`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bestie, your free mascara is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $50, get Clear Brow & Lash Mascara on us. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus Advent Calendar and Squeeze Me flavors, all in one place.`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics, "Bestie,, have you seen what's new? 👀"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, mostly clean Braze-tracked build, but the subject line has a likely merge-token defect. Two probed CTA links failed, one of them because the destination is login-gated. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the relay and the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Both failed probes share the same error, `unknown url type: '/…'`.** Each tracked URL on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` resolved to a relative `Location` target and the probe didn't resolve it against the host. This looks like a probe limitation rather than a confirmed 404, but the final landing state is unverified.
  - "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. The CTA lands on a login wall, not on a feature page.
  - "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?lid=b02kcdy2x9l1&af_xp=email&onelink_id=wTtZ&c=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&pid=Email&utm_source=broadcast&af…`. This is an AppsFlyer OneLink and probably works in a real client. The failure is likely a probe artifact.
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 23 skipped. The 33% pass rate rests on a small sample, and 2 of the 4 non-info flags are these link failures.
- **The header link carries `tabindex="-1"`** (`<a href="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/VmEho43i…" … tabindex="-1">`). Click wrapping is otherwise consistent, with all observed hrefs going through Braze `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…~~/AAQRxRA~/…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Link removed from keyboard navigation.** `tabindex="-1"` on the header logo link takes it out of the tab order (keyboard and screen-reader focus).
- **Missing alt text on one image.** It is `src=https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/e1SyntzyG8WnMwh9yMMTHA~~/…`. The `/q/` path is likely the Braze open-tracking pixel, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` would clear the flag.
- **Empty font-family name.** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the html_block wrapper falls through to Verdana. The Jost web font is never actually applied there.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in the head.
- **Web font loading.** `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost…')` is inside a `<style>` in the body. Many clients, including Gmail, strip or ignore this, so it degrades to the fallback stack.
- **Duplicate structure.** A nested `nl-container` / `row-1` / `row-content` is repeated inside the `html_block`. This is harmless but adds weight and duplicates class names.
- **Done correctly.** `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, MSO conditional blocks, a hidden preheader div, and a 660px mobile breakpoint.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Double comma in the subject:** `Bestie,, have you seen what's new?`. This is likely a fallback value ("Bestie,") combined with a template comma, or a punctuation-bearing token, so the fallback path renders a typo.
- No unresolved `{{ }}` or `${ }` tokens appear in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so the full body isn't confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a violation. Check the raw message at the source ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Sender domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither is visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for it.
- **Plain-text part:** 9,504 of 11,676 characters (81%) are URLs, which exceeds the 70% warning threshold. The cause is long Braze tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only visible parameters are on the app link: `utm_source=broadcast`, `c=email`, `pid=Email`, `af_xp=email`, `onelink_id=wTtZ`. There is no `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` in the visible portion (the URL is truncated), so completeness is unconfirmed.
- Other CTAs hide their final URLs behind Braze redirects, so UTM propagation can't be verified from source.
- The receipt-scanning CTA ends at a generic login page (`return_to=%2Faccount`) with no campaign context carried through, which breaks message-to-landing alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the subject token fallback** so it can't render `,,`. Strip trailing punctuation from the fallback or remove the hard-coded comma.
2. **Retest the two failing links** with a probe that resolves relative `Location` headers. Then fix the receipt-scanning CTA to land on a public explainer page rather than a login wall, or deep-link post-login to the feature.
3. **Remove `tabindex="-1"`** from the header logo link.
4. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.** Replace the generic `e.l.f.` header alt text if that image carries a message.
5. **Clean the empty font-family** (`''`), populate `<title>`, and move the Jost import to the head with a safe fallback stack.
6. **Verify the headers at the ESP.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in the raw message, not through the relay, and confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. **Trim the plain-text part** by shortening tracking URLs or adding descriptive text, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
8. **Rerun probing with a higher link budget** so the 23 skipped links are covered.
