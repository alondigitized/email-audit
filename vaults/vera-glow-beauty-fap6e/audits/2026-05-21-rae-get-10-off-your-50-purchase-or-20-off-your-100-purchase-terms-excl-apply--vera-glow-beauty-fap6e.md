# Rae, get $10 off your $50 purchase⁺ or $20 off your $100 purchase⁺! Terms & excl. apply
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Ulta comes out of Memorial Day weekend with a tiered spend-and-save I genuinely want to do the math on — $10 off a $50 basket or $20 off a $100 basket. That second tier is a 20% effective discount, and my Diamond brain clocked it immediately. The summer "ON" framing is clean, the barcode is baked right into the email for in-store use, and the free insulated tote is a cute seasonal add-on that doesn't feel like a clearance dump.
- But Ulta always has a tell, and it's right there in the subject line: "Terms & excl. apply" — unnamed, unspecified, doing unlimited damage to my trust before I even open. I've clicked through on Ulta offers enough times to find that my entire NARS or Urban Decay basket is excluded, and that experience has trained me to hesitate. The Saturday-only 5X points is a nice loyalty hook in theory, but it's asking me to schedule my shopping life around Ulta's retention calendar.
- I'd mark this as read unless I already had a $100 basket mentally assembled. It's a utility email, not a delight email. Not forwarding to a friend — not because it's bad, but because it's transactional in a way that only matters if you're already in motion.

## What's working

- The $10/$50 vs. $20/$100 tier structure is instantly scannable — I did the math in under two seconds and knew exactly which cart I'd build
- Barcode embedded in the email for in-store use is something Charlotte Tilbury never does and is honestly one of Ulta's strongest UX advantages for brick-and-mortar shoppers
- "Summer savings: ON" headline is clean, reads fast on mobile, and earns its seasonal placement in late May
- 5X points on Saturday is a real loyalty signal — it tells me Ulta knows I'm tracking my Diamond tier, even if the day-specific execution is clunky
- The free insulated tote GWP is relevant to summer and adds perceived value without feeling like surplus inventory getting cleared

## What's weak

- "Terms & excl. apply" with zero preview of what's excluded is a trust killer — if even one bullet of top-line exclusions were shown, I'd feel like a known customer instead of a click target
- Subject line is ~88 characters and gets truncated brutally on mobile; I never even see the second tier or the terms caveat before deciding whether to open
- The email is addressed to "Rae" — my name is Vera, and that broken merge tag breaks the personalization illusion entirely and makes it read like a mass blast
- The credit card upsell module mid-email splits attention with the primary savings CTA; pick a lane — either sell me on the offer or sell me on the card, not both in one scroll
- Saturday-only 5X points is a great retention idea buried under unnecessary day-specific friction with no "add to calendar" or reminder mechanic to make it actionable

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pull the credit card module** to the footer or a dedicated send — it's competing with the primary savings message and diluting both asks
- 2. **Show two lines of top-line exclusions** inline ("Excl. prestige brands, 21 Days of Beauty items") — ILIA had the same blank-trust-language problem in their loyalty email last week and it cost them clicks for the same reason
- 3. **Anchor the offer to a hard deadline** — "Through Monday" or "Memorial Day weekend only" converts a passive promo into urgency; this is exactly what Charlotte Tilbury did in their 20% off send that scored a 9 for me, and the deadline was the single biggest reason I clicked
- 4. **Make the Saturday 5X points at minimum a "this weekend" window** — doubling the redemption window costs Ulta almost nothing and removes the scheduling friction
- 5. **Drop the terms disclaimer from the subject entirely** — that belongs in fine print, not in the first 15 characters of a subject line competing for inbox real estate
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vera, stack up to $20 off your Memorial Day haul`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$20 off $100 + 5X points this weekend — summer starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store or online · see exclusions inside · ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your summer beauty budget just got a boost — here's what's excluded`
