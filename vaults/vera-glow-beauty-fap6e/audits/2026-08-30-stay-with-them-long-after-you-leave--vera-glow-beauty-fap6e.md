# Stay with them long after you leave
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the Glossier You fragrance family again — same lineup that showed up in the "friendship pairing" quiz on 8/27 — but this time it's every single scent stacked into one scroll with a quote-testimonial format ("That's the coolest person I've ever met," "I feel like I've known this person forever"). It's a genuinely pretty build — moody, editorial beauty photography, one model per scent, consistent typography — but there is zero offer anywhere in this email. Not a percentage, not a bundle price, not even a "new" flag. It's a six-scent product parade dressed up as a campaign moment.
- My gut reaction: I scroll past this in about four seconds. It's nice to look at, but I already know these scents exist — Glossier's been pushing this family relentlessly all month — and there's nothing here that moves me from "aware" to "buying." I'm not clicking, I'm not forwarding. If anything I'd screenshot the Rêve girl's lip gloss and ping a friend about that, not the fragrance push. This reads like a brand-awareness send that landed in a sales inbox slot.

## What's working

- The photography is genuinely good — moody, diverse, feels like an actual campaign shoot rather than a stock studio set.
- Each scent gets its own personality-quote hook, which is a clever structural device for six products that could otherwise feel repetitive.
- The hero "Make the right first impression" framing ties cleanly into the fragrance-as-signature idea — coherent concept, well executed visually.
- Model diversity across the six scents (freckled redhead, sun-kissed brunette, braided model, deep skin tone) actually reads current, unlike some of Glossier's back-to-school sends.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price, no bundle deal — as a Rouge/Diamond points-math shopper, I have literally nothing to calculate here, so this email doesn't even register as a "shop" moment.
- Six near-identical CTA blocks ("Shop now" x6) with no differentiation in urgency or hierarchy — my eye doesn't know which one to land on first.
- This is the same You fragrance family Glossier already used for the 8/27 "Types of best friends" quiz — two sends on the same product line inside 72 hours starts to feel repetitive, not campaign-building.
- No loyalty tie-in at all — Sephora's Rouge event emails at least gesture at "your tier," Ulta's 21 Days emails lead with the coupon code. Glossier gives me nothing to feel like a recognized customer here.
- Long scroll (six full-bleed sections) with no anchor nav or "which scent is right for me" quiz link up top — if I'm not already sold on fragrance, there's no on-ramp.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add an actual offer — even a modest one. Compare to Sephora Daily Deals' "50% off Select Beauty" (6/10, mainly because there IS a number) or Ulta's 20%-off-one-item mechanic — those at least give me points math to do. This email has nothing to calculate, so it reads as pure content, not a sales push.
- 2. Cut this to 2-3 hero scents instead of all six — the full lineup already ran on 8/27's friendship quiz; recycling the identical product set this fast reads as low-effort rotation, not a new campaign beat.
- 3. Add a "find your scent" quiz CTA at the top, since Glossier clearly has the personality-matching concept built — but here it's decorative copy, not an actual interactive tool.
- 4. Give Rouge/Diamond-tier readers a reason to care — even a "members get free samples with fragrance orders" line would beat silence.
- 5. Subject and preheader need to actually reflect the fragrance framing instead of vague sentimentality:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Find your signature scent (new + fan favorites)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `6 Glossier You scents, one quiz`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `From soft and familiar to lush and gourmand — take the quiz.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on Glossier You this week only.`
