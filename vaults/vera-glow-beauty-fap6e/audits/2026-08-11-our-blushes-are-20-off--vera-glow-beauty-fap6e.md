# Our blushes are 20% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, an actual 20%-off blush sitewide-ish promo — that's a real number, and I'll give Glossier credit for that after four straight sends this month that were basically content marketing wearing a subject line (horoscopes, keychains, a lip liner meme bit). But the execution here is sloppy in a way that pulls me right back out: the body copy is clipped on the right edge of nearly every module — "achieving a gl—", "Cloud Paint Plush Blush in Puf—" — like the template broke on render. That's not a nitpick, that's the kind of thing that makes me think twice about tapping a CTA on a brand I already trust.
- The offer itself is fine but thin for me specifically — I'm Rouge and Diamond, I do the points math in my head before I even open an email, and there is zero mention of what this means for me as a repeat customer. No "you've earned," no bonus points multiplier, nothing. Compare that to Sephora Insider actually surfacing points balance up top (even when it's broken — 0 points, again, per my last two audits) — at least they're trying to speak my loyalty language. Glossier just assumes I'll click because pink.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the "Shop now" under Cloud Paint Plush Blush specifically, since I don't own a shade yet. Would I forward it? No — this isn't interesting enough to send a friend, it's a straightforward percentage-off blush email with a text rendering bug.

## What's working

- 20% off is a real, specific, visible number right under the hero headline — no digging required.
- "Cloud Paint" and "Cloud Paint Plush Blush" are both clearly named products with distinct shade call-outs (Wisp, Storm, Spark, Bloom, Puff), which helps me picture exactly what I'm buying.
- The "OUR FAVORITES" and "WE RECOMMEND" model grids show a genuinely diverse range of skin tones — that's a demographic signal that lands better than most of the Skechers or Ulta sends I've seen lately.
- Free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush GWP at $75+ gives me a second reason to hit threshold, and "Cheek Week" framing at least tries to build a mini-event out of it.

## What's weak

- Body copy is visibly cut off on the right side in multiple blocks ("achieving a gl—", "Puf—") — that reads as a broken email, not a polished launch, and it's the first thing my eye catches after the hero.
- No loyalty tie-in anywhere — I'm Rouge/Diamond and this email doesn't acknowledge tier, points, or an event multiplier at all, unlike Sephora Insider which at least attempts a points display.
- The CTA buttons are pale pink-on-white or gray-on-white with thin borders — low contrast, easy to miss scrolling fast on my phone.
- "For a limited time" with no actual date or countdown is the same soft, non-urgent language I flagged on the Applebaby keychain reminder — there's no real deadline pressure to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the copy truncation first — that's the single biggest credibility hit in this email; a broken layout undercuts every other decision the design team made.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 8/16") instead of "limited time" — Ulta's been running actual "ENDS TODAY" urgency on its 20%-off sends and it works on me.
- 3. Surface my Rouge tier or points balance somewhere near the offer — even a line like "as a Rouge member, stack this with your birthday reward" would make me feel seen instead of blasted.
- 4. Darken the "Shop now" button treatment so it doesn't wash out against the white background on mobile.
