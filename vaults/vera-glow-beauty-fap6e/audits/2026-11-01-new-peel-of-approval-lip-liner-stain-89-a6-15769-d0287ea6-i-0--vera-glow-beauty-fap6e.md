## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Braze-style template with nested tables and click-wrapped links. 4 of the 12 probed links failed, and each failure is a redirect that resolved to a relative path. The unsubscribe and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
All four failures share one signature, `unknown url type: '/…'`. The tracking redirector at `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` appears to return a relative `Location` value, or the probe mishandled the hop. The source gives no way to tell which.

| Link text | Resolved target |
|---|---|
| "Shop Now" | `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Baddest+Beige+-+Light+Neutral+Beige` |
| "Shop Now" | `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` |
| "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f." | `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` |
| "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" | `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&lid=82iukdvlkbej&is_retargeting=true&af_xp=email&utm_medium=email&deep_link_value=com.…` |

- **Relative redirect targets:** RFC 7231 allows relative `Location` headers, and browsers resolve them. A strict client such as the probe, or some security scanners and link-protection gateways, may fail on them. If the redirector really emits host-less paths, the failure also affects those clients.
- **Not confirmed as a user-facing break:** the QA data doesn't show the host the paths would resolve against. Re-probe with relative-redirect handling before treating these as dead links.
- **Unprobed links:** 31 of 43 links were skipped as footer, utility or social. Their health is unknown.
- **Opaque wrapping:** every visible `href` is a `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/<token>~~` wrapper. The final destinations and UTM values can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** one image has none, `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/aL6d2Rrgv2ds76sEk856Dg~~/…`. The `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel. If so, add `alt=""` to keep it out of the accessibility tree.
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty, which hurts web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Invalid font stack:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in a `mobile_hide` block has an empty family name. It's harmless because it falls through, but it's malformed.
- **Redundant nesting:** the `nl-container` and `row-1` tables are repeated inside `html_block` wrappers. Everything uses `role="presentation"` and the MSO conditionals are present, so this only adds weight and fragility.
- **Fonts:** the Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Clients that strip `<link>`, such as Gmail, fall back to Arial.
- **Dark mode:** no `color-scheme` or `prefers-color-scheme` handling in the visible source, and the body is hard-coded `#fff`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible, truncated HTML. Tokens in the remainder can't be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA tool itself notes AgentMail may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Verify on the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com` with a `links.elfcosmetics.com` tracking domain, which is aligned. Alignment can't be proven from this data.
- **Unsubscribe and postal address:** the unsubscribe link was reportedly included in probing, but its result isn't shown. The postal address is in the truncated footer and can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM coverage:** only the app link shows email attribution in its resolved target (`utm_medium=email`, `af_xp=email`). The `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` values are not visible there, so attribution coverage can't be confirmed for the other links.
- **Product deep links:** the two "Shop Now" targets carry variant parameters (`Color=…`, `Size=…`), which is good deep-linking. They show no UTMs in the probe output, but the tokens may add them downstream.
- **Login link:** the receipt-scanning link goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Logged-out users land on a login wall rather than the receipt feature.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four failing links with relative-`Location` resolution against `www.elfcosmetics.com`. If they resolve, record the failures as a QA tooling artifact. If the redirector emits relative paths to real clients, switch it to absolute `Location` URLs.
2. Add `alt=""` to the `/q/` tracking image and fill in the `<title>`.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and the `Authentication-Results` headers against the raw message, not the relay copy.
4. Remove the empty font-family name and flatten the duplicate nested `nl-container` tables.
5. Check that `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` are set on all CTAs after redirect resolution.
6. Raise the probe budget, or add a sample of footer links, so the 31 skipped links get some coverage.
