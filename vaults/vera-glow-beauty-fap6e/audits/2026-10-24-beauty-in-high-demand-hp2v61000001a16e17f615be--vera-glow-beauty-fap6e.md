## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's tracked-link and image infrastructure is Epsilon/Harmony and Sephora gateway based. The automated QA pass rate was 50%, with one hard failure (an empty plain-text part) and several header and alt-text warnings. Most compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay did not capture the headers, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage was partial.** QA probed 12 of 59 material links and skipped 47 (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). No broken or redirecting links were reported for the probed set. The skipped links are unverified.
- **API key in tracking-pixel URLs.** The `evtEmail` pixels use `https://www.sephora.com/gway/v1/evtEmail?apikey=nQc7BFt78yJBvfYDKtle9APd5RrX984i&context=…`. This looks like a client-side event key, which is normal for pixels. It is still a credential-style parameter in every recipient's HTML, so confirm it is scoped write-only.
- **Possible unescaped ampersand (low confidence).** The QA output shows `context=6rMyANpz7hjTknEn1dS2ML×tamp=202…`. That is consistent with a raw `&timestamp` being decoded as the `&times` entity. It may just be an artifact of the QA extraction. Check the raw source and use `&amp;timestamp` if it is unescaped.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 4 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` is a content image, probably the nav header, so this is a real accessibility gap.
  - Two `evtEmail` pixels and the `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` open pixel also lack alt. These are tracking images, so the impact is low. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom is disabled.** The viewport meta is `width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The impact is negligible.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is the same for every send. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source and QA data show no unrendered merge tokens or placeholder text.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is missing (0 chars).** The email is HTML-only, which hurts deliverability and spam scoring. It also leaves no fallback for text-only clients. Confirm this isn't a relay artifact, then add a `text/plain` alternative.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unverified. Sephora is a large sender and likely sets both. Check the raw message headers before treating it as a defect, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and that is expected through the relay. Verify against the original message.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result was not reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs appear in the truncated source or QA output. Links use redirect and tracking domains (`app.sephora.com/O/…`, `images.harmony.epsilon.com`), so the final destinations and campaign parameters need to be checked after redirect resolution.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a plain-text MIME part. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Capture the raw headers to settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, instead of relying on the relay-stripped copy.
3. Add meaningful alt text to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Escape the `&` in the pixel URL query strings (`&amp;`), if the raw source shows it unescaped.
6. Fix `content="noindex"`, and set a per-campaign `<title>`.
7. Re-run link probing with the footer and unsubscribe links in scope, and capture the redirect chains to audit UTM and landing-page alignment.
