# 50% OFF select brow products 🤨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- A brow sale on a Friday is a solid Sephora Daily Deals send, and I got the point in two seconds. The giant "UP TO 50% OFF SELECT BROW PICKS / TODAY ONLY" sits on a peach-to-yellow gradient with one white SHOP NOW button. This is cleaner than the 9/29 "From your feed to your cart" hair send, which tried to be a TikTok roundup and a sale at once. It's also much calmer than the 10/1 "STARTS TODAY: 2X points" email, which at least had a points hook for me to plan around.
- My points-math brain went straight to the grid, and "up to 50%" is doing a lot of work. Only 3 of the 8 tiles actually hit 50%: Benefit's Brow Setter at $28 to $14, and both Sephora Collection pieces at $16 to $8. Kosas is about 40%, and Anastasia, Glossier, Grande and MUFE are all about 30%. Still, $8 for a Sephora Collection brow wax or pencil is an easy add-to-cart.
- Would I click? Yes, probably for the Benefit gel and the $8 brow wax. Would I forward it? Only to my brow-obsessed friend, and I'd mark it as a "today only" screenshot, not a link. It's good, but nothing here rewards me for being Rouge.

## What's working

- The hero is big and unmissable. "TODAY ONLY" is on its own line, so I know the clock without reading fine print.
- Every tile shows the strikethrough price and the red sale price. I can do the discount math in my head, which Ulta's "20% off one item" coupon emails never let me do.
- It's a good mix of prestige and budget: Benefit, Anastasia, Kosas and Glossier next to $8 Sephora Collection. That's my Sephora-plus-Ulta cross-shopping habit.
- The "Clean at Sephora" badge on Kosas and the "Sephora Quality / Great Value" badges are fast trust cues.
- The white product cards with pink borders pop against the yellow, so the grid is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50%" oversells a grid where most items are 30%. The asterisked exclusions line under the headline feels like a hedge.
- I'm Rouge, but the top bar says "You have 0 points." The tier bar's marker sits at INSIDER, which feels wrong for me. There's no points math or Rouge perk, and I just came off the 2X points event on 10/1.
- Nothing helps me choose a shade. Several of these are pencils and gels, but no shade count or "available in X shades" is visible. There are no ratings or reviews either, even though Drunk Elephant's shade-clock email gave me something to react to.
- The "introducing point of view" banner is an off-topic detour right below the sale. It's a different brand story with a tiny SHOP IT.
- This is the second Sephora send in two days with an almost identical Daily Deals format. It's starting to feel like a daily template.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add loyalty to the hero.** Something like "Rouge: free shipping + earn points on sale items" would tie this to my tier. Right now the member bar is decoration.
- 2. **Sort the grid by discount.** Lead with the true 50% items (Benefit, Sephora Collection) and put the 30% items lower. Add a "50% off" pill on those tiles so the headline is honest.
- 3. **Add star ratings and a shade count** on each tile. Pull in social proof like "#1 brow gel" for the Benefit gel.
- 4. **Make the CTA specific.** Use "Shop brow deals" instead of "Shop Now". Consider a second CTA lower in the grid.
- 5. **Move the "point of view" banner** below the sale, or cut it from deal sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Benefit, Kosas, Glossier brows up to 50% off 🤨`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Benefit 24-HR Brow Setter $14 + $8 Sephora Collection pencils. Ends 11:59pm PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge perk: free shipping on every brow pick. Sale ends tonight.`
