# Get the full Soft Glam fam for < $30! 🤑
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## What's working

- **Hero price lands immediately** — "FOR ONLY $27" in the hero copy is bold and legible; the subject says `<$30` and the email says `$27`, which is exactly the no-bait-and-switch alignment I wish Ulta's GWP threshold emails would learn.
- **Routine sequencing is intuitive** — cover, conceal, blush, highlight is the right order for application and makes scanning the email feel productive, not chaotic.
- **Diverse casting without trying too hard** — the hero model and the curly-hair model in the blush section look like real people across real skin tones. e.l.f. has always been better at this than most, and it shows here.
- **Clean render, no layout breakage** — after Ulta's "Beauty Break / this event is over" disaster (which I audited this week and still cannot explain), a crisp no-bug mobile render is worth crediting.
- **The "fam" framing is brand-right** — calling it a product family, not a kit, fits how e.l.f. talks. It doesn't feel grafted on from a different brand's playbook.

## What's weak

- **Four "SHOP NOW" buttons, no single anchor CTA** — ILIA's "Ends Tonight" finale this week had one button, one decision, maximum momentum. Here I'm being asked to make four separate purchase micro-decisions. My attention scatters, and scattered attention means I close the email.
- **Zero urgency** — the July 1st lip combo email carried limited-quantity energy. This feels like a permanent shelf price, which means "I'll come back to it," which means I won't. Drunk Elephant said "BEACH, PLEASE" on the Fourth and still managed to stack a time-bounded splash; e.l.f. could learn that move.
- **"Top Picks for You" is obviously a generic module** — those category pills at the bottom (Blur, Eyes, Face, Skincare) are dressed up as personalization but are clearly not. Sephora's Stacking Weekend July 1st email showed me my actual Rouge tier discount. This comparison is not flattering for e.l.f.
- **No loyalty or incentive layer** — e.l.f. doesn't run a points program the way Ulta Diamond does, but they could reference a first-order perk, a referral nudge, anything. For someone doing points math, the $27 is interesting but "what else do I get" is the question this email never answers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a deadline on it** — "Through Sunday" or "This week only" would move this from "interesting" to "cart now." ILIA proved this week that a real deadline changes behavior even when the offer itself isn't better.
- 2. **Consolidate to one hero CTA** — "Shop the Full Soft Glam Routine — $27" linking to a bundle page, with individual product breakdowns below for mix-and-match shoppers. Let the four sections be content, not four competing conversion points.
- 3. **Speak to existing fans, not first-timers** — "Complete your e.l.f. routine" or "Already have the primer? Here's what pairs with it" reframes this as a loyalist upsell, not a tutorial. I've been in this inbox for years.
- 4. **Upgrade the preheader** — if it's defaulting to link junk or a repeat of the subject, that's a missed second hook on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your full Soft Glam routine — all $27 of it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 steps. 4 products. Under $30. ⏰`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cover. Conceal. Blush. Highlight. Four products, one ridiculously good price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The complete Soft Glam fam for $27 — all four steps, one cart.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is rendered via Braze (ESP), using standard table-based layout with MSO/VML conditionals for Outlook. The HTML source is truncated, limiting full compliance verification; findings below are scoped to what is observable.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Opaque redirect URL — UTM parameters unverifiable**
The only visible CTA link routes through `links.elfcosmetics.com` using Braze's encoded redirect format:
```
https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/TcUnjva7CVNp__t4c7FTNQ~~/AAQRxRA~/8SmXJf...
```
No raw UTM parameters appear in the `href`. They may be injected by the redirect target, but this is unconfirmable without resolving the URL. If UTM params are missing on the destination page, all GA4/analytics attribution for this send is lost.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Empty `<title>` tag** | `<title></title>` — screen readers in some clients announce the title on open; empty title degrades accessibility |
| **Logo link removed from keyboard tab order** | `<a href="..." tabindex="-1">` on the header image link — sighted keyboard users cannot reach this link |
| **Gmail link styling wiped** | `#MessageViewBody a{color:inherit;text-decoration:none}` — this selector targets Gmail webmail's wrapper and strips all visual indicators that text links are clickable |
| **Google Fonts missing `display=swap`** | `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Open+Sans` — no `&display=swap` parameter; risks FOIT (Flash of Invisible Text) in clients that load web fonts |

Logo `alt="e.l.f."` is present — no issue there.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. Cannot fully audit without the complete HTML.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Cannot confirm from truncated source:**
- Physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5))
- One-click or clearly labeled unsubscribe mechanism
- Any required "Advertisement" disclosure

**Sending domain:** `m.elfcosmetics.com` (subdomain). SPF/DKIM/DMARC cannot be assessed from HTML — verify at DNS level that the `m.` subdomain is covered by DKIM alignment with the `From:` domain.

**Subject line risk:** `< $30` contains a raw `<` character. While safe at SMTP level, certain older or aggressive HTML-parsing clients may misinterpret it. Encoding as `&lt;` is safer.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

The Braze redirect pattern (`~/AAQRxRA~/`) is standard and will pass through to the destination URL — but only if UTM parameters were configured in the Braze campaign settings. Without resolving the redirect URL, destination alignment (correct landing page, UTM source/medium/campaign values) cannot be confirmed. This is the highest-risk gap in this audit.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Resolve the Braze redirect URL** and confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on the destination — this is the most impactful item.
2. **Add a meaningful `<title>`** (e.g., the subject line) for accessibility.
3. **Remove `tabindex="-1"`** from the logo link, or document why it's intentional.
4. **Audit `#MessageViewBody a` rule** — if text CTAs exist elsewhere in the email (not visible in truncated source), this rule will make them invisible as links in Gmail webmail.
5. **Append `&display=swap`** to the Google Fonts URL.
6. **Encode `<` as `&lt;`** in the subject line to eliminate any parsing edge cases.
7. **Verify the footer** (not in truncated source) contains: physical address, unsubscribe link, and any required legal copy.
