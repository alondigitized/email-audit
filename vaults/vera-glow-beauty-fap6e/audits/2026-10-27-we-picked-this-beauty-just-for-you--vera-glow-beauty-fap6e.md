# We picked this beauty just for you! 🍒
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- "Chosen Just for YOU" is the biggest thing on the page, and nothing in it feels chosen for me. I'm Rouge. The tier bar near the bottom has its marker sitting on Insider, and the top strip says "You have 0 points." Sephora knows exactly who I am, so I read that as a stranger treatment. This is the seventh Sephora send in about a week, after "Oh. My. Minis." on 10/23, the two "Beauty in high demand 📈" sends on 10/24, and "This beauty is the talk of the town 📞" on 10/25. The 10/26 "These gifts = 🌶️🌶️🌶️" email used the same catalog layout.
- The real hook is at the very top: a free Topicals trial size with code HISKINLOVE on a $30 purchase. It's a decent perk, but it reads like a GWP and the "ONLINE ONLY" circle badge is small. Then the Gisou lip oil ($28) and lip mask ($26) hero pulls my eye, and it's pretty. After that it turns into a product wall. Marc Jacobs at $168, Clarins at $142, Versace at $138 and a Rare Beauty blush at $25 aren't a curated set for one person.
- Would I click? Maybe for the Topicals freebie, if I was already buying a $30 Gisou item. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it and mark it read.

## What's working

- The Topicals trial size with a code and a $30 threshold is a concrete offer, and it sits at the top where I see it first.
- The Gisou hero shows two limited-edition, Sephora-exclusive items with prices ($28 and $26). The "ONLY AT SEPHORA" flags give me launch-day urgency without a countdown.
- The Clean at Sephora badges on the Gisou row give me a quick filter for the ingredient stuff I care about.
- The Insider / VIB / Rouge bar and the "Rewards Bazaar" link are at least visible, so points math is possible.
- Prices are on almost every product tile, so I don't have to tap to find out whether something is $25 or $168.

## What's weak

- "Chosen Just for YOU" promises personalization, and the content is generic. I'm Rouge and the email treats me like a new Insider with 0 points.
- There's no event cue. As someone who stocks up at the Beauty Insider event, I want to know whether this is a full-price moment or something to wait out. This email is silent about it.
- The "Your Next Beauty Obsessions" grid mixes a $24 balm with a $168 perfume and has no skin-type or shade cues.
- The credit card block, the AI Beauty Chat tile and the fragrance tile make it a long scroll. The fine print is huge and dense.
- It's the seventh near-identical catalog in a week, so the Gisou hero doesn't feel special.

## Recommendations

- 1. Use the loyalty data you already have. Show my real tier and points, and open with "Rouge perk" or "X points to your next reward" instead of "0 points."
- 2. Pick one lead offer and drop the other three promos (credit card, AI chat, vanillas). Ulta's Early Black Friday email led with one big "Up to 50% off" banner, and the offer was clear in half a second. Sephora's top strip is quieter than that.
- 3. Tie the product grid to something I did, such as "Because you bought Rare Beauty" or "Back in stock in your shade," rather than a generic obsession list.
- 4. Put the Topicals deadline in the main hero copy, not a small badge.
- 5. Compare ILIA's "On everyone's lips." It was a tight, single-theme lip story that I wanted to read. Sephora has a lip theme with Gisou, but it dilutes it with perfume and serum.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing (QA FAIL).** The text version is 0 chars. Relay capture may contribute, but treat it as a real gap until a raw MIME check shows a `text/plain` part.
- **Three images lack `alt` text:**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` at `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06`. This is a content image, so the missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap.
