# This only happens once a year
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Marissa's back, and it's the exact same plain-text personal-email cosplay ILIA ran on 9/17 "Browsing the VIP sale?" and 9/14 "This could be your moment, friend" — no hero image, no product shot, no logo, just a wall of bullet points signed off with a first name like it's a text from a friend. At this point that's three sends in under two weeks running this identical template, and it's starting to read less like a "personal note" and more like ILIA doesn't want to spend design budget on their own VIP sale.
- The offer itself is actually decent — 20% off sitewide, free full-size Multi-Stick, free Velvet Makeup Bag at $100+ — but I have to squint to find it buried under a "join ILIA VIP (it's free)" gate before I even get to shop. Sephora doesn't make me sign up for a new program to get my Rouge discount, and Ulta's "20% off one item" mechanic (which I've now seen three times this month) at least doesn't require account creation mid-email. I'd mark this as read, not click, and definitely not forward — there's nothing here worth screenshotting for the group chat.

## What's working

- The 20% off + free Multi-Stick + free Velvet Makeup Bag stack is a genuinely strong three-tier offer if you actually read the bullets.
- "This sale only happens once a year" gives real urgency, unlike some of ILIA's vaguer past sends.
- The "ends 09.22.26 at 11:59pm PT" fine print at the bottom at least gives me a hard deadline instead of vague "ending soon" language.
- Year-round perks list (points, early access, birthday gifts, free shipping) is a nice reminder of what VIP actually gets me.

## What's weak

- Zero visuals — no hero shot, no product photography, no logo, nothing to stop my thumb while scrolling. Glossier's "Boy Brow just won an award!" send had actual restraint but still gave me a product image; this gives me nothing.
- I have to "join ILIA VIP" before I can even use the 20% — that's an extra step Sephora and Ulta don't make me take for a comparable discount as an already-known customer.
- This is the third near-identical plain-text "Marissa" email in two weeks (9/14, 9/17, now 9/20) — the novelty of "personal note" wore off after the first one.
- No mention of my existing points balance or VIP status if I already have one — pure stranger treatment, not "welcome back."
- The products name-dropped (Super Serum Skin Tint, Limitless Lash Mascara) aren't shown, just mentioned in passing text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Ditch the plain-text template for this specific send — a once-a-year sale deserves an actual hero image, not the third copy-paste "friend" email in a row. This is the single biggest lever here.
- 2. Move the offer above the fold and lead with it, not "join VIP" as a precondition — show me the 20% first, gate the checkout step, not the reading.
- 3. If I'm already a VIP member, suppress the "join VIP" CTA entirely and swap in "shop your 20% off" — segmentation ILIA clearly has the data for but isn't using.
- 4. Show the two named products (Super Serum Skin Tint, Limitless Lash Mascara) as actual thumbnails, not text.
- 5. Vary sender persona cadence — three "Marissa" sends in six days is a pattern I've now flagged twice.
