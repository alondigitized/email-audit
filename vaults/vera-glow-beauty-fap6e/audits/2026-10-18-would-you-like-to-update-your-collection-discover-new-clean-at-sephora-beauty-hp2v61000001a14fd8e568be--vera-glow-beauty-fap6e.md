# Would you like to update your collection? Discover new Clean at Sephora beauty.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Sephora send in about 24 hours. The first two were Friday's "Beauty this good moves fast 👀" and this morning's "Feel-good ingredients are here," and I'm tired of them. The subject asks if I want to "update my collection" and promises Clean at Sephora, but the top of the email leads with a 4X points banner on moisturizers. Under that is a free Kosas trial size, and only then the Clean hero. That's three offers fighting for the first screen, and the Clean hero is the one the subject sold me.
- I'm a Rouge who does points math for a living, so the 4X on ALL moisturizers through 10/21 with code 4XHYDRATE got my attention. It's in-store and online, and it's a real deadline. But it's the same 4X moisturizer deal that was buried in this morning's email. Now it's on top, with a barcode at the bottom. Would I click? Probably the Kosas freebie or the 4X banner, not the Clean grid. Would I forward it? No. I'd save it for the Beauty Insider event stock-up and move on.

## What's working

- The 4X points banner is the first thing I see, with a clear code (4XHYDRATE) and a credible 10/21 end date.
- The free Kosas Lip Pulse Glossy Lip Plumper has its own code (TRYKOSAS) and a "$30+ purchase" threshold, which makes the math easy.
- The green "Clean at Sephora" badge on every product is a consistent, scannable trust cue.
- Prices are shown on every product, with value amounts like "($52 value)" and "($126 value)," so I don't have to tap to find out what things cost.
- The "NEW" and "ONLINE ONLY" tags help me spot launches, and the Kosas hero shows new shades.
- The Insider / VIB / Rouge tier strip is a nice nod to members, and the barcode for Kohl's stores is useful if I'm there.

## What's weak

- The subject promises Clean beauty, but the hero is a 4X moisturizer banner plus a free-gift module. The Clean hero is third.
- It's a long catalog scroll with no clear priority. Eight products, then Auto-Replenish, Black-owned brands and rhode, which is too many ideas for one email.
- The Kosas hero image is a flat-lay of unlabeled pots. I can't tell which new shades are new or what they cost without scrolling to the copy.
- The Auto-Replenish "15% off" and the Black-owned brands module feel tacked on and have nothing to do with Clean.
- The fine print is long and small, and it runs right into the barcode, which hurts readability on a phone.
- I'm already a Rouge, and the "Shop now" in the tier strip treats me like a generic member. There's no personalization and no points total.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with Clean and the Kosas shades, and move the 4X moisturizer deal into a slim bar. Or flip it and lead with 4X, then change the subject to match. Don't run both.
- 2. Cut the catalog to four products and add a one-line "why it's clean" for each.
- 3. Use a segmented skincare module for people who bought moisturizer recently, and show a points total for Rouge members.
- 4. Run the Auto-Replenish, Black-owned brands and rhode modules in their own sends.
- 5. Shorten the fine print and put the codes in a tappable block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on moisturizers ends 10/21`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New Kosas shades + a free lip plumper 💚`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code 4XHYDRATE gets you 4X points on every moisturizer through 10/21.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Kosas Lip Pulse with $30+ and new Clean at Sephora launches.`
