# How to find your shade
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a complexion guide, and I like it more than the last few ILIA sends. After the 10/16, 10/17 and 10/19 VIP-gift pile-on, "On everyone's lips" on 10/25 and "Re: sensitive skin, eyes, or lips" on 10/22 were a relief. This one keeps that educational tone. The hero says "The Match Game" and promises to help me find "The One." That's a shade-matching pitch, and I do have foundation opinions.
- It's long, though. There are four product blocks, each with its own "SHOP NOW" button, then a Step 2 shade-finder module, then the mascara gift. The free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $75+ shows up in the top strip and again near the bottom, so the gift is back after all. It's a much softer push than the VIP bag.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on "Take the Quiz" or "Send a Selfie," since those are the useful parts. I wouldn't forward it. I'd skim it, maybe save it for my next complexion restock, and move on.

## What's working

- The "Step 1: Your Coverage Type" and "Step 2: Find Your Shade" structure is clear. I can see the logic without reading much.
- Each product has the same "First Impressions / Best Qualities / Long Term" breakdown. It's scannable and lets me compare Super Serum Skin Tint, Skin Blur, True Skin and Skin Rewind quickly.
- The quiz and selfie options sit in the peach block with the Shade Guide link below them. Sephora's shade finder is buried in the app, so this is a good use of the email.
- The mini mascara gift is clearly stated: "with any $75+ order."
- Swatches next to the bottles are a nice touch. I can see undertone and finish before I click.

## What's weak

- Four "SHOP NOW" buttons look identical and don't name the product. Each one is the same outlined box, which is a lot of repetition.
- There's no price, shade count or review proof on any product. As a Rouge/Diamond shopper I do the points math, and there's nothing here to calculate.
- I see no Insider/VIP or member pricing mention beyond the footer "Join ILIA VIP". Ulta would have put points and tier perks up high.
- The gift offer is small and sits in the very top strip, so it's easy to miss. It repeats near the bottom, but with fine print ("See site for details").
- The "Skin Rewind Complexion Stick" block has less of the product photo's details visible than the others, and the page is a long scroll for one real action.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the shade quiz and selfie tool above the product blocks, or at least link them in the hero. Finding my shade is the actual job, and it's currently at the bottom.
- 2. Replace the four "SHOP NOW" labels with specific copy such as "Shop Skin Tint" or "Shop Skin Blur," and add a price and shade count under each.
- 3. Add social proof like a star rating or "# shades" line to each product.
- 4. Show the gift threshold once, clearly, near the first CTA.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Take the quiz or send a selfie, plus a free mini mascara with $75+.`

# Technical Audit: ILIA, "How to find your shade"

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 16 were skipped, as deprioritized footer and social links. The QA output reports no failed probes, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I could not inspect individual `href` values. I am not flagging redirect chains or malformed URLs.
- Tracking uses the first-party domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`, which is consistent with Klaviyo click and open tracking. No issues found.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`, which screen readers need to choose pronunciation.
- **Empty title:** `<title> </title>` contains only whitespace.
- **Missing alt text, content image:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. If the image carries content, it needs descriptive `alt`. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M5YX7HF2Q4RRMNWHWF9T7H96` is the open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` so it isn't announced.
- **`@import` web fonts:** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat...)` are not supported in Gmail and several other clients. The declared fallbacks are `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif`, so degradation is graceful. This is low severity.
- The viewport meta tag, MSO conditional comments, and the `#outlook a` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets are present and correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}`, or `*|...|*`. The truncation limits how far this can be confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** the QA check says the relay may not capture it. This is unverified, not confirmed missing. Check the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found:** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it, so confirm it at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the AgentMail relay stripping the header, so this is a tooling gap rather than a sender defect.
- An unsubscribe link was included in the link probing. The QA output reports no failure for it, but I could not confirm the footer postal address from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The truncated HTML exposes no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and no landing-page probe results were reported. I am not flagging an issue.

2. Set descriptive `alt` text on the CloudFront image, or `alt=""` if it is decorative. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
