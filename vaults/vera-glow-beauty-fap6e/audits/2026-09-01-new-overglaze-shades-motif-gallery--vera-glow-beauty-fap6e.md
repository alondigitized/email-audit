# NEW Overglaze shades—Motif + Gallery
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, two brand-new Overglaze shades — Motif and Gallery — and this actually caught me because ILIA's Overglaze line has been genuinely good since launch, so a new-shade drop is worth a second look, unlike the Crown Affair collab or the plain-text recap newsletter I got from them this week. The lip swipe macro shot is gorgeous, the "100% said it locks in moisture" stat feels earned rather than clinical-flex the way Drunk Elephant's ingredient stats always do, and the GWP (free mini Eye Stylus in Beam at $75+) is a real, specific mechanic — not a vague "shop now."
- That said, there's zero segmentation here. No VIP tier callout even though there's a literal "Join ILIA VIP" badge sitting in the footer icons doing nothing, no shade-match personalization despite "Personalized Shade Match" also being a footer icon rather than a headline feature. Compare that to Sephora's Marc Jacobs send — Rouge status and stacking language up top — and ILIA is leaving loyalty math on the table while I'm sitting here doing exactly that kind of math in my head for every other brand in my inbox.
- I'd click through on "Shop Overglaze," this is genuinely one of ILIA's stronger sends lately — better than 8/29's newsletter dump, on par with 8/26's lip close-up. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, but I'd bookmark the shades to check swatches on darker skin tones before buying.

## What's working

- The two-tube shade lineup shot with clean names (Motif, Gallery) up top is easy to scan in three seconds.
- The lip macro with the applicator mid-swipe is the strongest visual asset in the email — it's what makes me trust the "24-hour hydration" claim.
- "100% said it locks in moisture + quenches dry, chapped lips" is a specific, useful proof point without being over-clinical like Drunk Elephant's SheerID split-face stat sends.
- The customer quote ("like buttering your lips up so they will love you") adds a real voice instead of generic ad copy — nice touch for social proof.
- GWP mechanic (free mini Eye Stylus at $75+) is concrete and gives me a reason to hit the spend threshold, similar to how Sephora's Marc Jacobs GWP worked on me.

## What's weak

- Only one skin tone shown in the hero lip shot — ILIA's 8/26 "On everyone's lips" send did a dual-tone close-up that was noticeably better for shade-match confidence, and this one steps backward.
- No loyalty/VIP callout in the body even though "Join ILIA VIP" sits as a tiny footer icon — as a Rouge/Diamond points-chaser I want that front and center, not buried.
- The two shade swatches (Motif, Gallery) are small circular crops with no name-visible-at-a-glance skin-tone range — can't tell how Gallery (a berry-red) looks against different depths.
- Fine print on the "100%" stat and the GWP ("*See site for details") is the kind of soft-buried disclaimer that dings trust a little, even if minor.
- Two back-to-back CTAs ("Shop Overglaze" then "Claim Your Gift") dilute the single clear action — I have to decide which one matters more.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a VIP/loyalty line directly under the hero or near the CTA ("ILIA VIP members earn 2x points on Overglaze this week") — footer badges don't move Rouge-tier shoppers like me.
- 2. Swap the single-tone lip macro for a two-or-three-skin-tone swatch grid so I can actually judge Motif vs. Gallery against my own tone before clicking.
- 3. Consolidate to one primary CTA — merge the GWP into the "Shop Overglaze" button copy ("Shop Overglaze + Get a Free Mini") instead of running two competing buttons.
- 4. Move the "*See site for details" fine print inline near the offer instead of small-type at the bottom of that block.
