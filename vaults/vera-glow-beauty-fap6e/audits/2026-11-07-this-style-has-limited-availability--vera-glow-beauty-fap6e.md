# This style has limited availability!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a Skechers sneaker email in my beauty inbox again. The subject says "limited availability," but the hero says "How about a second look? Seen this before? We thought so." So the subject promises scarcity and the body delivers a retargeting nudge. Nothing in the email says a style is limited. It's the same fake-cart energy as the 11/4 to 11/7 sends, just with a softer headline. I still haven't put a Skechers anything in a cart.
- The one real thing is the navy block: Skechers Plus members take 20% off regular-priced styles, valid through 11/10. That's a number I can work with, but it sits way down the page below two product grids. Sephora puts the Rouge 30% in the top banner before I see a single palette. Ulta puts the $10/$15/$20 ladder up top. Skechers makes me scroll through seven sneakers first.
- Judgment: I'd skim it, and I wouldn't click or forward it. It's the ninth nudge in two weeks and I have no shoe intent.

## What's working

- The 20% off Plus members block is a clean, readable number with a visible "Valid thru 11/10" deadline.
- The big navy "Shop Now" button under the hero shoe is unmistakable.
- The hero shoe is large and clear, and the white-sole sneaker photo is crisp.
- The nav list (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals, Sale) is big enough to tap on my phone.
- The fine print is complete. It states the dates, online-only, and the Plus login requirement, so the offer feels honest.

## What's weak

- The subject's "limited availability" isn't supported anywhere in the email. I see no low-stock cue and no "only a few left" copy.
- The 20% off is buried below about 1,000px of products. Compare e.l.f.'s free Glow Reviver Melting Lip Balm with a $5+ purchase, where the mechanic is clear in two seconds.
- The six-shoe grid has tiny captions and no prices, so no product has a reason to be clicked. ILIA's one free mini eye stylus with $75+ is a far more specific hook.
- It's the same retargeting creative as 11/7's "Don't leave these styles behind!" and 11/6's "This is perfect for you!". The cadence feels automated, not personal.
- The footer has a big pile of utility blocks (app, curbside, Klarna, texting) that dilute the ending.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% Plus banner to the top, above the hero, and make it the headline. Put "ends 11/10" in the headline too.
- 2. Either show real scarcity on the hero shoe (a "Low stock in your size" cue) or drop "limited availability" from the subject.
- 3. Add prices or a "was/now" to the grid so I can do the math, and cut it to three shoes.
- 4. Cap the cart-reminder cadence and stop sending near-identical sends on consecutive days.
- 5. Add a Plus-member callout for existing members, like Sephora's tier math, so the email treats me as a known customer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off ends 11/10`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Arch Fit Arcade is still here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ins, Arch Fit and more at 20% off for Plus members through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take 20% off regular-priced styles before 11/10. Online only.`
