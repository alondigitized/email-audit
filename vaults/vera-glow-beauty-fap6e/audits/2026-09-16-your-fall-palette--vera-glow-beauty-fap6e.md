# Your fall palette
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Glossier finally doing what I've been begging for since that fifth Labor Day discount email — a real seasonal color-story drop with zero "% off" anywhere on the page. "Sultry Reds" and "Cozy Browns" is a genuinely good hook for fall, and the close-up lip and eye macro shots do the actual selling-the-product work that Sephora's Daily Deals banner-of-everything approach never bothers with. It reminds me a little of the "Brow down" email from 9/13 — no discount, just product-forward styling with real faces — which is the version of Glossier I actually want in my inbox.
- That said, my Rouge/Diamond brain immediately clocked what's missing: not one mention of a shade-match tool, no "your undertone" personalization, nothing that says this brand knows I've been buying Ultralip in three shades already. I'd click into this — probably straight to Ultralip in Cranberry since I already own it and "crimson-berry red" for fall sounds like a repurchase, not a new experiment. Would I forward it? Maybe to my one friend who's still deciding between warm and cool reds this season — the walnut vs. espresso brown comparison is legitimately useful for her.

## What's working

- Zero stacked discount code fatigue — a relief after four Labor Day emails in a row logged this month.
- The two-column macro shots (lips, under-eye, brows) make texture and finish genuinely readable — better product truth-in-advertising than ILIA's mirrored-tray mascara shot from earlier this week.
- Clear thematic split (Sultry Reds / Cozy Browns) gives me an actual shopping framework instead of a random grid.
- Demographic range across the models feels authentic to Glossier's usual cast, not one narrow beauty-ideal face repeated six times.

## What's weak

- No loyalty signal at all — I'm Rouge and Diamond, and there's nothing here about points, a GWP threshold, or member-exclusive early access, which is table stakes for me at this point.
- "Get them all" hero CTA is vague — doesn't tell me if that means one bundle purchase or just "browse the collection."
- No price anywhere on the page, on any of the six products — for someone doing points math in my head, I need at least a ballpark to decide if this is a $18 lip liner or a $34 splurge.
- Product grid buries "Shop now" under generic gray-outline buttons that don't pop against all that white space — Sephora's Daily Deals banner had way more visual urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a shade-finder or "your matches" personalization strip right under the hero — Glossier has the data on what I've bought before, use it.
- 2. Put a price or starting-at price on every product tile — I'm not clicking six times just to find out what a Balm Dotcom costs.
- 3. Swap "Get them all" for something that actually names the collection, like "Shop the Fall Palette."
- 4. Add even a soft loyalty nod — "Rewards members get early access" — costs nothing and would make me feel less like a stranger.
