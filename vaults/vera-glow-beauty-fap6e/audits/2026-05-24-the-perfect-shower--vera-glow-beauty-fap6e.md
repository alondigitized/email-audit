## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Lush email number SIX in twelve days — May 12 was the vibe mood board, May 14 was the fragrance parade, May 16 was the summer catalog, May 20 was Dream Cream, May 22 was Karma, and now Saturday of Memorial Day weekend they're back with… shower products. No sale, no Memorial Day hook, no loyalty math — just a teal block that says "Shower Thoughts" over a couple in a shower and then a parade of Hsuan Wen Hua, Banana, Buffy, Good Karma, and Olive. On the Saturday of MDW. When every other brand in my inbox is screaming 25–50% off.
- I want to be charitable because the creative is genuinely cute — the photography is on-brand, the "Shower Thoughts" pun lands, and the couple-in-the-shower hero is a Millennial-coded image in a way that, say, the Lush Karma send wasn't. But as a Sephora Rouge / Ulta Diamond who is doing points math this weekend, this email has zero hooks for me. No tier, no offer, no urgency. Sephora's "Save up to 50% + 500 bonus points" send today is doing actual work. Glossier's 30% reminder is doing work. Lush is… vibing.
- I'd scroll, maybe save Buffy to a mental list for next time I'm at a Lush in an airport, and archive. No click, no forward.

## What's working

- The "Shower Thoughts" wordplay is the kind of cheeky Lush voice I actually like — better than the generic "Have a Lush life 🏖️" framing from May 16.
- The hero image — couple in the shower — finally reads Millennial/age-appropriate, where the Karma send felt more Gen Z.
- The product grid layout is clean and scannable; I can tell what's a shower gel vs. a scrub vs. a body butter from thumbnails alone.
- Buffy and Olive callouts are the *right* products to feature for a shower email — they're the actual hero SKUs, not deep-cut nonsense.
- The "Meet Jannie" creator/influencer block at the bottom is a nice trust beat that competitors like Drunk Elephant don't bother with.

## What's weak

- No offer. None. It is Memorial Day weekend Saturday and there is not a single discount, points multiplier, free-shipping threshold, or gift-with-purchase. Sephora and Ulta are wallpapering my inbox with tiered offers; Lush is showing me a mood board.
- Six emails in twelve days with zero loyalty math means I'm being trained to ignore the sender name entirely.
- "Shop Now" on a teal hero with no price, no product, no urgency — that's a click into the void.
- No size, no price, no review count on any of the product tiles. I can't even comparison-shop Buffy without leaving the email.
- Lush still has no visible loyalty/tier program in any of these sends. Sephora Beauty Insider event, Ulta 21 Days — those send me running. This sends me to archive.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put an actual offer on the hero.** Even a modest one — "Free shower miniature with $40" or "10% off shower for the long weekend" — would change everything. Right now this email is wallpaper on the busiest beauty shopping weekend of Q2.
- 2. **Tie it to Memorial Day.** "Long weekend showers, sorted" with a 3-day window. Sally Beauty is on email eight of this beat for a reason — it works.
- 3. **Show price and size on each product tile.** Buffy is $19 for the small — say so. Reduce friction between hero and PDP.
- 4. **Cut cadence.** Six emails in twelve days with no offer is how you become Drunk Elephant Friends & Family fatigue without even getting the sale credit.
- 5. **Anchor one hero product, not five.** The Dream Cream send on May 20 did this well — the May 24 grid dilutes attention.
