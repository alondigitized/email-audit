# PSA 📣 Get 4X points on go-to foundations & concealers.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the same 4X points send I already flagged as my favorite Sephora email this week, and re-reading it doesn't change my mind — "Your Foundation & Concealer Offers" with a hard "4X" badge is exactly the kind of concrete, points-math content that gets me to actually stop scrolling. I don't need a mood board or a model shot to sell me on foundation shopping; I need to know what multiplier I'm getting and on what, and this delivers that in the first screen.
- Where it starts to lose me is the sheer length of the scroll — twelve-plus products each repeating "EARN X points / USE CODE: GETMORENOW" in an identical red-tag format starts to blur together by the time I hit Charlotte Tilbury and Makeup By Mario. Compare that to Ulta's approach lately, which is one flat 20% code slapped everywhere with zero curation — Sephora's at least organizing this around a real category and real point values, which is more useful to me even if the layout gets repetitive.
- I'd click — the "STACK 'EM UP" CTA is right up top and the points math is too good to ignore (I'm eyeing that Charlotte Tilbury 208-point line specifically). I probably wouldn't forward this to a friend the way I did with the identical send earlier today, just because scrolling through a dozen near-identical product tiles isn't screenshot-worthy — it's a "save for later" email, not a "look at this" one.

## What's working

- The "4X" badge and "STACK 'EM UP" hero copy up top means I don't have to hunt for the offer — it's the first thing my eye hits.
- One code (GETMORENOW) across the whole list keeps checkout simple — I'm not juggling five different promo codes like some Sally Beauty sends make me do.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) near the bottom reminds me this is tiered to my status, which is the loyalty signal I actually track.
- The $5 Beauty Insider Cash redemption (250 points at checkout) is a nice second offer layered under the main one — genuinely useful, not filler.
- "Clean at Sephora" and shade-swatch dots on individual products give me the ingredient/shade context I actually care about before I click through.

## What's weak

- Twelve-plus product tiles in an identical red-tag format is a lot of scroll for one email — by product eight or nine my eyes are glazing over "EARN 152 points" for the fifth time.
- No skin-tone or model imagery anywhere, unlike e.l.f.'s "Set It Bright" shade-quad send, which actually showed the shade range on real faces — this is purely product-bottle-on-white-background, so it doesn't feel personalized to me specifically.
- The offer window ("July 30-August 5, 2026") is buried in the tiny legal text at the bottom, not in the hero — I had to hunt for when this actually ends.
- The "So hot right now" waterfall Sephora ran twice this week already trained me to expect repetition from this sender, and while this send is thematically different, the product-tile-after-product-tile structure still feels like the same visual pattern recycled.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product list to the top 5-6 highest point-earners and let me "see all 12" via CTA — right now the scroll fatigue undercuts the strong opening.
- 2. Move the "Ends 8/5" deadline up into the hero banner next to the 4X badge — urgency shouldn't be buried in footer legal text.
- 3. Add at least one shade-diverse model or swatch strip near the top so this doesn't read as pure product photography — even a small "shades for every tone" banner would help.
- 4. Give the $5 Beauty Insider Cash redemption its own visual weight higher up — it's a genuinely good hook and it's currently sandwiched below two unrelated promo banners (Summer Fridays, hair accessories).
- **Subject Alt A:** `4X points on your go-to foundation, ends 8/5`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your foundation & concealer just got 4X the points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the code + your tier for max points before 8/5.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `12 foundations, 4X points, one code: GETMORENOW.`
