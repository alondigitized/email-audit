# Black Friday: 25% off everything
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Glossier again, and I'm doing the math before I scroll. The hero is a blue block with a giant "25% OFF EVERYTHING*," and a smaller "30% OFF $100+" circle that's cropped at the top right edge. That crop is the first thing I noticed. It's the same stacked-offer problem as the 11/25 "Good things come in pairs" send, only messier here. Two percentages fight each other, and a thin banner on top adds a free candle at $100.
- The rest is a clean four-product grid: Glossier You, Balm Dotcom, After Baume and Boy Brow. It's pretty but generic, and nothing in it shows a sale price or a points hook. The candle block at the bottom is the real hook, and it's the same free-gift-at-$100 mechanic as this morning's "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle" (8/10). I'd click through to the candle and then check out. I wouldn't forward it, because it's a repeat of a repeat.

## What's working

- The big white "25% OFF EVERYTHING" on electric blue is impossible to miss, and the "Shop Black Friday" button sits right beside it.
- The "$45 value, completely free" candle copy lets me price it out in two seconds.
- The "Community Favorites" grid has a short descriptor and a "Shop now" button under every product, so the tap targets are easy on a phone.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" ticker strip breaks up the scroll and keeps the season clear.
- The brand voice is consistent and the product photography is clean.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks 25% off, 30% off $100+ and a free candle banner. I can't tell which deal wins without doing the math myself.
- The "30% OFF" circle and the "25% OFF" text are visibly clipped at the right edge, so the hero looks broken.
- The product tiles show no prices, no sale prices and no ratings, so the grid is pretty but doesn't help me decide.
- There's nothing for me as a points-math girl. No Glossier rewards mention, and no stacking info. Sephora's 50% OFF send at least has a Beauty Insider angle.
- The "Boy Brow: Grooming pomade" tile is a close-up of an eye with no shade or skin-tone range. The "Shop now" buttons also look cut off at the right edge.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline offer. Lead with "25% off everything" and move the $100 threshold into the candle block only. Make the hero circle say one thing.
- 2. Fix the hero crop so the full "30% OFF" and "25% OFF" are visible.
- 3. Add sale prices or "from $X" under each product tile, and a star rating, to cut down on friction.
- 4. Add a deadline, like "Ends Cyber Monday," so the urgency feels credible. Right now it's only "available now just a few days."
- 5. Add a skin-tone or shade-range cue on the Boy Brow and balm tiles.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free $45 Glossier You Candle on $100+. Ends Monday.`
