# Give your hair stellar shine 💫
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- The astronaut hero made me smile. "WHAT THE H.E.L.F.?" is classic e.l.f. wordplay, and the moon-surface stuff is fun. But the first full screen of my phone is a giant photo of someone in a space helmet. The actual offer is a small line of text underneath: Gloss Mode Treatment Oil, "Only $10." That's a hair oil I'd never heard of until now, and I had to scroll to learn what it is.
- This is also the 10/23 "great hair era" send again, with a second gift offer stapled to the bottom. Two days ago I got "OMG gifts. OM-e.l.f.-G prices" and yesterday the "Ghoulishly good looks" quiz email. Four sends in five days is a lot, even for e.l.f.
- Would I click? Maybe, because $10 is an easy impulse buy. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the Sephora Beauty Insider event to stock up.

## What's working

- The space theme carries through every block, so the email feels like one idea and not a pile of modules.
- "88% reduction in breakage*" is a real claim with a number, and the glowing gold bottle on the moon is the best visual in the email.
- "Only $10" is a clear price. It's easy to say yes to for a treatment oil.
- The "boosts shine, strengthens hair, softens & smooths, great for all hair types" stack is skimmable, and the emoji bullets keep it playful.
- The gift-with-purchase block is a decent sweetener. "Choose a full-size gift when you spend $50+. No code needed" is clearer than most GWP fine print.

## What's weak

- The hero photo is huge and doesn't show the product. The offer and the "SHOP NOW" button sit well below it, so I have to scroll before I know what's being sold.
- There are three identical neon "SHOP NOW" buttons stacked for one product. That's repetitive, and the generic copy doesn't say what I'd be shopping for.
- The "Top Picks for You" row (Power Grip Primer, Cream Glide Lip Liner, Blemish Breakthrough spot gel) has nothing to do with hair, and nothing in it feels picked for me. It also contradicts the "for you" label.
- There's no member or tier hook. The footer mentions receipt scanning and the Beauty Squad app, but nothing about my points or what I'd earn. Sephora and Ulta at least pretend to know my tier.
- The asterisks ("versus control," "temporarily," "after a single application on dry hair") are tiny. The fine print also puts the gift deadline at November 1, but the email body never says so.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the Gloss Mode bottle in the hero, with "$10 treatment oil" in the headline, and cut the helmet-only shot. The offer should be visible on the first screen.
- 2. Use one CTA for Gloss Mode, like "Shop the $10 Oil," and drop the other two buttons.
- 3. Replace the unrelated Top Picks with hair products (a shampoo, a mask, a heat protectant), or add a shade/skin/hair-type quiz hook.
- 4. Put the GWP deadline in the headline, like "Gift ends 11/1." Ulta's Early Black Friday email did that better.
- 5. Space the sends out. A gift email, a quiz email and a hair email in five days feels like the same promo on repeat.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Braze-sent email with tracked links through `links.elfcosmetics.com`. The QA run shows 27% pass, driven by four link-probe errors, an empty plain-text part, and missing unsubscribe and authentication headers. Several of these may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay and probe tool rather than defects in the sent email (details below). The HTML source was truncated, so only the first two blocks were inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probes failed with `unknown url type`.** The failing targets were:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Baddest+Beige+-+Light+Neutral+Beige` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&af_xp=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=102726_hair_astronaut_support&shortlink=emai…` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause:** each `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect returned a root-relative `Location` header (path only, no scheme or host). Relative `Location` values are valid under RFC 7231 and browsers resolve them, so this is probably a probe limitation, not a user-facing break. I can't confirm that from the data, and the final landing pages were never verified. The four CTAs remain unvalidated rather than proven broken.
- **Probe coverage:** only 12 of 39 links were probed (27 skipped, mostly footer and social). The footer and legal links are unverified.
- **Wrapped tracking links:** all links are wrapped (`/f/a/<token>~~/AAQRxRA~/...`), so the destination can't be read from the HTML. The logo link is one example, and it was not probed.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/awBpm1SbeMxhrY5VW4Mr7A~~/AAQRxRA~/b9sVCPC4...` is the image flagged for missing alt (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title:** `<title></title>` is empty in `<head>`. Some clients and webmail views use it for the tab or window title, and it affects accessibility.
- **Missing alt on the pixel:** the `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/...` image has no `alt`. If it is the tracking pixel, it should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip it and clients that block images don't show a placeholder.
- **Invalid font-family:** a `mobile_hide` block uses `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif`. The empty quoted family is a templating leftover and is harmless but invalid.
- **Nested wrappers:** the layout has duplicated `nl-container` and `row-1` structures inside `html_block` wrappers. This is not a defect, but it adds weight and makes the HTML more fragile to edit.
- **What's in place:** `role="presentation"` is set on layout tables, `lang="en"` is set, the logo has `alt="e.l.f."`, and there are MSO conditionals and a viewport meta tag.
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to Arial and Sans-serif.
- **Preheader:** the hidden preheader div is present with `display:none;mso-hide:all`. Nothing pads it, so inbox previews may append body text after "Gloss Mode Treatment Oil".

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The inspected portion of the source contains no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `${}`, or `%%`. Later content was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. Without them there is no RFC 8058 one-click support, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `m.elfcosmetics.com` is unknown. This is flagged as a relay limitation, not a confirmed failure.
- **Plain-text part is 0 chars (FAIL).** There is no `text/plain` alternative. If the ESP sent one and the relay dropped it, this is a false positive. Otherwise it is a deliverability risk and an accessibility gap.
- **Footer unverified:** the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source. The unsubscribe link was not among the probed links, even though the QA note says one is always included.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM on the app link:** the `/pages/mobile-app` destination carries `utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=102726_hair_astronaut_support` plus AppsFlyer params (`af_reengagement_window=30d`, `af_xp=email`). Campaign naming is consistent.
- **UTM on product links:** the `/products/...` URLs carry only variant params (`Size=`, `Color=`) and no UTMs. The login link has only `return_to=%2Faccount`. Attribution for those clicks relies on the Braze click tracking rather than UTMs. The probe may have truncated the query strings.
- **Variant deep links:** these use human-readable values (`Color=Baddest+Beige+-+Light+Neutral+Beige`), which break if a variant is renamed. Landing-page match cannot be confirmed because the probes failed before reaching the page.
- **Content alignment:** the preheader is "Gloss Mode Treatment Oil", but the probed CTAs point to a primer and a lip liner. I can't judge relevance from the data, but the landing pages should be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the four failed links with a client that resolves relative `Location` headers. Confirm the final URLs return 200 and match the intended products.
2. Check the raw message at the ESP for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the `text/plain` part. Then decide which QA failures are relay artifacts and which are real.
3. If the plain-text part is truly absent, add a `text/plain` alternative to the Braze template.
4. Fill in `<title>` and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Remove the empty `font-family:''` declaration from the template.
6. Add UTM parameters to the product and login links, or confirm that click-tracking attribution covers them.
7. Probe the footer and unsubscribe links, and confirm the physical address is present in the full HTML.
