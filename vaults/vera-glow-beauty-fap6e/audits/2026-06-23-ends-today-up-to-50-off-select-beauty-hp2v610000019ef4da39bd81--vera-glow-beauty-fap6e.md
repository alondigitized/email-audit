# ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🫣
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- I've now seen this email twice in one day from Sephora — literally same subject line, "ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty," and I reviewed it this morning. That context colors everything. The email itself is a competent sale-close send: bold "Major Summer Deals" hero, 50% off leading the fold, loyalty perks tucked right underneath, even a free same-day delivery banner at the top. For a Rouge member like me who's been tracking this event all week, that opener hits the right notes — except I already mentally closed this chapter when I got the first send of this exact email earlier today.
- What's frustrating is that the creative underneath that subject is actually well-assembled. Beauty Insiders bonus points section, product grid with specific items, a "Just Arrived" tray, and a Merit spotlight section that's doing real editorial work. This is not a lazy email. Sephora clearly put work into the content. But by duplicating the send on the same day, they've turned a solid sale-close email into wallpaper.
- I would not forward this to a friend — I already forwarded the first one. I probably would click through if something in the product grid caught my eye, but my urgency meter is officially broken from cadence fatigue.

## What's working

- **50% off hero leads the fold immediately** — the red section with "up to 50% off" is the first thing the eye lands on, no hunting required, which is cleaner than how Ulta typically buries the hero offer below a busy nav.
- **"Beauty Insiders Get More" section is doing loyalty lifting** — bonus points and same-day delivery highlighted separately from the main offer is smart; it layers the value prop for members like me without cluttering the hero.
- **Free same-day delivery promo code in the header banner** is a genuinely useful perk that differentiates from competitors; Charlotte Tilbury's app-exclusive offers from earlier this month created a similar VIP-feels-to-me signal.
- **"Just Arrived" tray at the bottom** keeps the email feeling current, not just clearance-dump — that editorial balance is something ILIA consistently nails and Sephora is matching here.
- **Two distinct offer tiers visible (50% and 25%)** serve different purchase thresholds — smart for a cross-category beauty audience.

## What's weak

- **Exact-duplicate send on the same calendar day** — I audited this morning's version of "ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty 🫣" and I'm staring at it again. This is the Sephora duplicate problem I flagged on June 21 (PLANE 👏🏽 BEACH CLUB) and again June 22 (Major Summer Deals closing, twice). It's now a pattern, not an incident.
- **Too many competing sections below the fold** — 50% off, 25% off, 500 bonus points, same-day delivery, product grid, Spot It Shop It, Merit spotlight, Just Arrived, BeautyINSIDER newsletter. The email is trying to do six different things and none of them get to breathe.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is as generic as it gets** — after a strong urgency hook in the subject, the button copy does nothing. "Shop 50% Off Picks" would convert harder. Sally Beauty has the same problem — "Shop Now" on everything — and I ding them for it too.
- **No single hero product anchor** — the product grid is a scattershot of thumbnails. Drunk Elephant's "Summer to go" email from last week led with a tight, editorial hero product that made me stop scrolling. This email expects me to do all the curation work myself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Deduplicate the sends.** Before anything else. One "ENDS TODAY" email per day, per list segment. This is suppression logic, not creative — but it's the highest-impact single fix. Every duplicate erodes the trust equity Sephora has built as a sender.
- 2. **Pick ONE hero product or collection and build the fold around it.** Even a curated "Top 5 picks under $30 from the sale" thumbnail would outperform the scatter grid.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA button** from "SHOP NOW" to something tied to the hero offer — "Grab 50% Off Beauty" or "See All Sale Picks."
- 4. **Trim the below-fold sections by at least two.** BeautyINSIDER newsletter and "We Belong to Something Beautiful" brand story content have zero business living inside a sale-urgency email. Save them for a non-sale content send.
- 5. **Leverage the Rouge/member segmentation** — even a single line like "Your Rouge early access ends tonight" would make this feel like a send written for me, not a broadcast blast to 40 million people.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — Major Summer Deals wrap up today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Beauty Insider bonus points window closes too — don't lose them.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free same-day delivery + up to 50% off: today only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** blocks pinch-to-zoom on iOS. This prevents visually impaired users from magnifying content and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). The constraint is unnecessary for modern responsive email layouts.
  ```html
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally** via the catch-all selector `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This suppresses OS-level font scaling for users who rely on system accessibility text size settings.

- **`robots` meta typo** — `content="no index"` (space) is non-standard; the correct value is `noindex`. Irrelevant to deliverability but indicates template hygiene issue.
  ```html
  <meta name="robots" content="no index" />
  ```

- **Duplicate `display` declarations** in `.show` and `.appear` rules — `display: block!important` followed immediately by `display: table!important` in the same ruleset. The block declaration is dead code and signals a copy-paste artifact.

- **No `lang` attribute** visible on `<html>` tag (XHTML namespace only). Screen readers use `lang` for correct pronunciation. Cannot confirm absence vs. truncation.

- **`alt` text on images** — cannot audit from truncated source.
