# What’s in a bag’s balm?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Glossier just sent me a flat lay of what's allegedly in three different tote bags, organized by lip balm flavor, and there's genuinely no offer anywhere in this email. Email thirteen-plus since July 7th (I'm now doing the math they clearly aren't doing with me), and this is the first one in the whole "I ❤️ NY" run that doesn't even pretend to have a deal — no GWP threshold, no discount, just vibes and a "Shop now" button four times. I do spot the applebaby charm sitting right there in the I ❤️ NY balm dotcom flat lay, so at least it's tying back to the July 25th "Get a free Applebaby Keychain" send instead of introducing yet another disconnected concept — I'll give them continuity credit.
- But compare this to what's actually in my inbox from everyone else this week: Sephora Daily Deals is telling me "save up to 50% on select lip must-haves," Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item" landed with an actual code, Sally's running BOGO again. Glossier is asking me to admire someone's hair clip and film camera arrangement with zero dollar signs in sight. Cute concept, zero conversion mechanism.
- This is a mark-as-read, not a click. I'm not forwarding a bag flat lay to a friend — I forwarded the free keychain email because there was something to actually get. No offer, no urgency, no points math for me to do (and I always do the points math), so this one just sits there looking pretty.

## What's working

- The "Bag check" concept is a clean creative swerve after twelve nearly-identical GWP reminder emails — at least the format is new even if the campaign thread isn't.
- Applebaby charm showing up in the flat lay ties this straight back to the July 25th keychain GWP, so regular readers like me aren't lost.
- The red/white Glossier aesthetic is instantly recognizable — I know exactly whose inbox I'm in within half a second.
- "Shop now" buttons are placed consistently under every product grouping, so wherever my eye lands there's an obvious next step.

## What's weak

- There is no offer. Not a discount, not a GWP threshold, not free shipping — just object photography. After thirteen emails in three weeks I need a reason to actually buy, not just look.
- Zero mention of Rouge or Diamond points, zero urgency, zero "ends tonight" — nothing for me to weigh against my Sephora/Ulta calendar.
- No models, no skin tone or shade cues anywhere — for a beauty brand that's a missed chance to signal "this shade is for you."
- The product lists ("_little red book and pen," "_film camera") read more like a mood board caption than shoppable copy — I can't tell what I'm actually buying until I click.
- Cadence is genuinely fatiguing at this point; Ulta and Sephora both lead with a concrete number every single send, and Glossier's gone a full email without one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in this send — even a soft one ("20% off balm dotcom trio") would do more than another flat lay. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Add shade/flavor swatches or a quick scent note next to each balm — I need product detail, not just "black cherry" as a vibe.
- 3. Tighten "Shop now" into flavor-specific CTAs ("Shop Black Cherry") so the click feels like it's going somewhere specific, not a generic PDP.
- 4. Slow the cadence or vary the format more — this is at least the 13th Glossier email since July 7th, and back-to-back concept sends without an offer risk me tuning out entirely.
