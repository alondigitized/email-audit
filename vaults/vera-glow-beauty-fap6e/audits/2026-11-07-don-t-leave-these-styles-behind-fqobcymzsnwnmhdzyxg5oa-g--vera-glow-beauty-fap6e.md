# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth "you left something behind" email from Skechers in about two weeks, and I still haven't put anything in a Skechers cart. "Don't leave these styles behind!" is the same fake-cart nudge as 11/6's "This is perfect for you!" and 11/5's "Your cart is one click away!". The only new thing is that the body now says "We saved these styles for you."
- The one real thing in here is the 20% off Skechers Plus offer, valid through 11/10. I did the math: 20% off regular-price only, online only, member-gated, no stacking, and sale styles are excluded. Six women's sneakers and a boot are fine, but nothing tells me why I'd want them over any other pair. I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. It gets marked as read.
- Ulta would have put the offer in the subject and made me feel the clock. Sephora would have shown my points balance. This email does neither.

## What's working

- The "20% OFF" band is big and high-contrast, so my eye lands on it right after the hero.
- There's a clear deadline, "Valid thru 11/10", which gives it credible urgency, unlike the empty cart-expiry lines.
- The six-product grid renders cleanly with no broken images, and the pastel Slip-ins and the UNO boot look current for the season.
- The Skechers Plus tie-in shows a member benefit up front, which is more tier-program thinking than the last seven emails had.

## What's weak

- The hero says "We saved these styles for you," but I never saved anything. Compare that with Glossier's merch email, which at least admitted it was a launch.
- The best part, the 20% off, sits below the fold and below six product links. The headline and the "Shop Now" button come first.
- The member discount is gated behind a Plus account and the asterisk fine print, with a long list of exclusions. It's online only, regular-price only, and excludes sale, premieres and select accessories.
- The product names are tiny underlined links with no prices and no sale price shown, so I can't do points math or compare against what Skechers Plus would save me.
- The bottom half is generic. The Women/Men/Kids/Clothing nav list, the app badges, the Afterpay and Klarna logos and the social icons all feel like boilerplate.
- Nothing here is aimed at a beauty-first shopper. I can't tell that anyone looked at my skin tone, my category or my cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer in the subject and hero. Stop faking cart abandonment. Put "20% off" and the 11/10 deadline up top, and tell me it's for Plus members.
- 2. Show prices on the products, with the member price next to the regular price, and put one specific product in the hero instead of six equal tiles.
- 3. Cut the fake-cart language entirely or only send it to real abandoners. After seven of these in two weeks, it reads as spam.
- 4. Trim the footer clutter so the offer and a single CTA get all the attention.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off women's sneakers through 11/10`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus members save 20% on regular-priced styles. Online only, ends 11/10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slip-ins, Max Cushioning and fall boots, all 20% off for members.`
