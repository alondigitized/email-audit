# Your fall beauty routine is coming together 🍂
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the "new drops all in a row" format Sephora does, and honestly it's a decent one for me — Salt & Stone, Fenty, Emi Jay, Kayali, Huda, Josie Maran, Caudalie, Loveshackfancy all stacked with clean product shots and "NEW" or "NEW + LIMITED EDITION" tags is exactly the launch-radar scan I actually want from Sephora. The Rare Beauty trial-size teaser up top with Selena Gomez is a smart hook too — free-with-purchase trial sizes are a real driver for me trying something I've been on the fence about.
- But then it just keeps scrolling. And scrolling. By the time I hit "Spot it. Shop it." I've lost the plot, and then it pivots hard into "4X Points on Fragrance" with code NEWSCENTS — which I already got as its own standalone email yesterday (9/19), so seeing it bolted onto the bottom of this one feels like Sephora reusing the same asset twice in two days instead of giving it real estate of its own. No Rouge-tier callout anywhere despite this being exactly the kind of email where "as a Rouge member, shop new arrivals 2 days early" would've made me feel like a VIP instead of one of the masses. I'd scroll it once, maybe screenshot the Kayali and Josie Maran gift sets for my Sephora wishlist group chat, then archive — not a click-through session.

## What's working

- The "NEW" launch grid format is legible and scannable — Salt & Stone, Fenty, Emi Jay, Kayali all get clean product-forward shots with pricing visible.
- Josie Maran and Caudalie gift sets show both retail price and the discounted set price ($36 vs $48, $49 vs $85) — that's real points-math fuel for me.
- "Clean at Sephora" and "Planet Aware" badges are visible on several products, which matters to how I filter my scrolling.
- The hero "Fall Into New Beauty" banner with the model is a clean seasonal reset, unlike Ulta's stale barcode-on-book-stack hero I've seen since July.

## What's weak

- Zero Rouge-tier language anywhere — no early access, no points multiplier tied to my tier, nothing that treats me like the top-spend customer I am.
- The 4X fragrance points + NEWSCENTS code at the bottom is a straight repeat of yesterday's 9/19 standalone send — same code, same offer, just tacked onto a different email instead of new content.
- This is a full-catalog dump disguised as a "routine" email — eight+ brands in one scroll is closer to what I flagged in the 9/18 "Yes, please! 50% off" send that just kept going.
- No single clear primary CTA — "Shop Now" appears multiple times pointing at different sections, so my eye doesn't land anywhere specific.
- Subject promises "your fall beauty routine is coming together" but nothing in the body is personalized to my actual purchase history — it's generic new-arrivals, not "based on what you bought."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 3-4 hero launches max and split the rest into a separate "new arrivals" digest — Glossier's restraint (7/10 on Boy Brow) is the model here, not the everything-bagel approach.
- 2. Add Rouge-tier early access messaging up top — "Rouge members shop new arrivals 48 hours early" would immediately lift relevance for me.
- 3. Drop the recycled NEWSCENTS fragrance block or give it its own send — reusing yesterday's exact offer here just reads as filler.
- 4. Pick ONE hero product (Kayali or the Josie Maran set, since gift-set math is the most clickable thing here) and build the whole CTA hierarchy around it.
- 5. Add a "you were browsing X" or purchase-history hook to actually earn the "your routine is coming together" subject line promise.
