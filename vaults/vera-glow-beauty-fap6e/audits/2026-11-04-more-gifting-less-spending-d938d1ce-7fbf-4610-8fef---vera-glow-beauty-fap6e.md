- 2. Make the CTA a full-width button with specific copy, like "Shop gifts under $15."

# Technical Audit: "More gifting, less spending 🎁" (Sally Beauty)

The supplied HTML is truncated, so body links, the footer and the unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected. Findings below rest only on the visible `<head>` and the QA data.

## 1. Technical Summary
The `<head>` and template CSS look sound: XHTML Transitional, a mobile breakpoint at 499px, and an Outlook VML namespace. The QA data shows third-party tracking pixels with a broken macro and an insecure `http://` source, plus an exposed Salesforce-style ID. Compliance and authentication couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The vendor's template substitution didn't run, so the pixel is cacheable and impression counts may be wrong.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over HTTP. Clients may block it or show a mixed-content warning, so the Dotomi/Epsilon sync probably doesn't fire.
- **Unverified links:** 56 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect behavior are unconfirmed.
- **Pixel stack:** At least three third-party pixels are present: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com`. Each adds a remote request and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Two images have empty `alt` and empty `src` in the QA output, which suggests spacer or placeholder images. Confirm they carry `alt=""` (decorative) rather than no attribute.
- **Pixels without alt:** The `impression_pixel`, `UCMController` and `pxl.mon-trk.com` images have no usable alt. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, so they aren't announced to screen readers.
- **Malformed meta tag:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` isn't self-closed, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It is harmless in practice.
- **Font fallback:** The `a{}` rule sets `font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif`, but no `@font-face` is defined for Founders Grotesk (only Open Sans). Links render in the Helvetica fallback in most clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Exposed identifier:** `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` appears as the image alt text and as the final path segment of `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2331538/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`. The `003…` prefix matches a Salesforce Contact record ID. It's a per-recipient identifier in a URL and could be read aloud by screen readers.
- **Merge tokens:** No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens were visible in the truncated source. The only unresolved placeholder found is `[CACHEBUSTER]`, covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header wasn't found, and neither was `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing headers, so I'm not treating it as a confirmed sender defect.
- **Authentication:** `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain is `em.sallybeauty.com`.
- **Footer:** The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Unable to assess: no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source. All 56 tracking links were skipped by the probe, so landing pages and UTM persistence through the redirects are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the MyVisualIQ pixel template so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is replaced with a real value at send time.
2. Change the Dotomi pixel to `https://login.dotomi.com/...`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support HTTPS.
3. Move the Contact ID out of the pixel URL path and alt text. Use an opaque, non-reversible token, and set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
4. Give the two empty-alt images an explicit `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive text if not.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and raw headers (bypassing the relay if possible) to verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the UTM parameters on the 56 tracked links.
6. Self-close the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta tag, and either add an `@font-face` for Founders Grotesk or remove it from the stack.
