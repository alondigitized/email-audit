## What's working

- **Beach bag timing is locked in.** June 7 is exactly when I'm thinking about what goes in my tote for the beach weekend — this editorial premise earns its send date.
- **Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray in a summer context is smart placement.** I hadn't framed setting spray as a beach bag essential before this email, and the seasonal reframing actually works as discovery.
- **The warm sandy palette holds through the entire scroll.** Zero render issues, no broken images, consistent art direction — the email looks expensive, which is the right signal for a prestige brand.
- **Loyalty members section shows up.** It's buried below the fold, but it's there — Charlotte Tilbury at least acknowledges I'm a member rather than blasting me like a stranger.

## What's weak

- **Format fatigue is real.** This is the same multi-product digest wrapper from May 30 and June 6. Three of these in eight days reads as a content drought, not a content strategy — and I clock it immediately.
- **No offer anywhere in the hero.** No percent off, no GWP, no early-access framing — and yet Ulta is running CT GWPs on the same day. If price or value is on my mind, CT's own send gave me nothing to work with.
- **No single hero product to want.** ILIA's recent proof-point send for the Soft Focus Powder scored a 9 because it built an entire email around one product and made me feel specifically like I was missing something. This grid of seven items diffuses all of that desire — I'm not moved to buy any one thing.
- **The "UNLOCK HEALTHY GLOW" mid-email CTA felt orphaned.** It's a layout moment that doesn't clearly tie to one product or one action — it reads like a section break that never got filled in.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero product and build the whole email around it.** The setting spray summer argument is strong — price it, show the size, tell me why it's the move for humid outdoor days, and give me one CTA to the PDP. One product with conviction beats seven products with none.
- 2. **Surface the loyalty offer into the hero.** If there's a member exclusive somewhere below the fold, that's the headline. Especially on a day when Ulta is outcompeting CT's own email with a CT GWP.
- 3. **Break the digest cadence.** One high-intention single-product send resets the "just browsing" perception that three consecutive digests have created.
- 4. **Replace grid SHOP NOW with a single curated CTA.** "Build My Beach Bag" or "See the Full Summer Edit" — something that acts like a destination, not a scattered storefront.
