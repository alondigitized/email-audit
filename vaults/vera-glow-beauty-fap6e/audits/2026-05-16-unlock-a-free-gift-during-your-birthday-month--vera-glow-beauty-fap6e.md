# Unlock a FREE gift during your birthday month 💜
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- After yesterday's "You're an e.l.f.ing VIP!" loyalty push — which felt like e.l.f. was just shouting their rewards program at me for sport — this birthday email actually earns its place in my inbox. A birthday gift unlock is one of the few moments where a brand has permission to interrupt my day, and the purple hero with "FREE BDAY GIFT? SAY LESS." lands instantly. That's the e.l.f. tone at its best: cocky but self-aware.
- Here's my issue: "UNWRAP THIS YEAR'S GIFT WITH PURCHASE." hits you right below the hero. That "with purchase" qualifier after a "FREE" headline is the exact bait-and-switch move I've trained myself to catch, and when I catch it I feel a little played. Drunk Elephant had a full gift moment last week with zero fine print gymnastics — they just told me what I was getting. e.l.f. has the products to be straight about it too.
- I'd click — a birthday gift from a brand I genuinely shop is worth the tap. Would I forward it? I'd screenshot the iconic bag reveal and drop it in my group chat. Would I mark it as read and move on? Not today.

## What's working

- "FREE BDAY GIFT? SAY LESS." — genuinely punchy, no wasted words, hits immediately before any scroll
- Two named minis called out (Holy Hydration! Cleanser Mini and Glow Reviver Melting Balm Mini in Birthday Cake) — actual products, not "a surprise awaits" vagueness
- The e.l.f. Iconic Bag as the hero gift is a specific, tangible reveal — leagues better than a mystery coupon like Ulta was running last week with the "Mystery Offer ends soon" email
- Beauty Squad tie-in is natural here because birthday perks are the whole point of the program
- The purple visual identity is completely locked in — this reads e.l.f. before the logo even registers

## What's weak

- "UNWRAP THIS YEAR'S GIFT WITH PURCHASE." sits right below the hero and walks back the "FREE" framing — that hierarchy problem undercuts trust before I even scroll
- This is e.l.f. email number five in under three days; the birthday send breaks the noise but the cadence fatigue is real and accumulating
- "Bring your birthday to your Beauty Squad profile to unlock" implies I haven't done this yet — if they know to send me this email, they should know my birthday status; that friction shouldn't exist
- Zero urgency on the window closing — "during your birthday month" tells me nothing about whether I have two days or three weeks left

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead honest in the hero** — "Your birthday gift, free with your next order" is still compelling and doesn't feel like a gotcha. The current layout makes the "with purchase" feel like it's trying to hide.
- 2. **Add a hard close date** — "Offer valid through May 31" immediately makes this more actionable; vague month windows don't create urgency.
- 3. **Auto-verify birthday if Beauty Squad profile exists** — if I'm in the loyalty system, skip the "bring your birthday to your profile" copy and go straight to "your gift is ready."
- 4. **Show the bag's retail value** — Ulta always anchors the birthday gift with a dollar value on their landing pages; knowing it's a $30 value vs. a $10 value changes whether I rush to claim it.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[CONFIRMED BUG] Empty font-family first value:**
```css
font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif
```
The leading empty string `''` is a malformed value. The Google Font `Jost` is loaded via `<link>` but never referenced in the font stack — it will never render. Should be:
```css
font-family:'Jost',Verdana,sans-serif
```

**[MINOR] Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Several clients (Gmail web, some iOS notifications) surface the `<title>` value. Leaving it blank is a missed opportunity and can surface as untitled in some tab/notification contexts.

**[MINOR] Nested `nl-container` tables:** An `nl-container` table is nested inside a `<div>` inside a `<td>` inside another `nl-container`. This double-nesting is technically valid but adds unnecessary DOM depth that can cause Outlook line-height inheritance bugs.

**Positive findings:**
- `role="presentation"` present on all layout tables — correct
- `alt="e.l.f. beauty squad"` on the hero image — present
- `title="e.l.f. beauty squad"` on the hero image — present
- Proper MSO conditional `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` wrapping the Google Fonts `<link>` — correct; Outlook will not attempt to load it
