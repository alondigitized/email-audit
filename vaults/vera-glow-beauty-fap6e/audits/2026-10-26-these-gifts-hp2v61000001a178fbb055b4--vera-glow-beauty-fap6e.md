- The K18 block (4X points, code 4XK18, ends 10/28) is a real points play. I can do that math in two seconds.

- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags on each tile give the launch-day exclusivity I look for.
- Each gift tile shows a price and a "$ value" number, so the savings are clear without clicking.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (VIB / Rouge) near the bottom is a nice nod to members. I wish it were higher up.

## What's weak

- It's the sixth catalog-style send in six days, so it's fatiguing. Gift sets are not what I stock up on between events.
- The Savings Event banner is a thin strip. It doesn't say what the actual savings are or how they stack with the Rouge discount.
- The 15% off moisturizers and cleansers deal is at the very bottom, so most people will never see it.
- Several tiles (Prada, L'Oréal Pro hair rescue, MAC) pull me away from the K18 hero. There are too many competing CTAs.
- The text under each tile is small. The product name and price are hard to read on a phone without zooming.

- 2. Cut the gift grid to 3 tiles and move the 15% off Subscribe & Save offer up next to the K18 hero.
