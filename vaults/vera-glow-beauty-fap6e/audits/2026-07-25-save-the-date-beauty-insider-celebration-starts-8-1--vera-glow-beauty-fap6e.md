# Save the date: Beauty Insider Celebration starts 8/1 🗓️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Save the date" is the whole strategy here and honestly? I respect it. Sephora telling me the Beauty Insider Celebration runs 8/1–8/31 a week out means I can actually plan my points math instead of scrambling — that's the kind of advance notice Ulta never bothers with (their last send to me literally announced an event was already over). The "250 points = $5 off" banner and the credit-card 500-points bonus during 8/6–8/9 are exactly the mechanics I track every single event.
- But — "You have 0 points"?? At the very top of the header, above the fold, on an email addressed to a Rouge member? That's either a broken personalization token or Sephora genuinely doesn't know how many points I have, and either way it undercuts the entire "just for our members" pitch two lines later. It's the kind of glitch that makes me trust the rest of the offer math a little less.
- I'd open this on the strength of the subject line alone, and I'll screenshot the calendar dates for myself — but I'm not clicking "Get a Sneak Peek" today since the real offers don't kick in until 8/1. I'd probably forward this to my group chat just to say "mark your calendars," not because there's anything to buy right now.

## What's working

- The 8/1–8/31 date range front and center means I can actually plan around it, unlike Ulta's "event is over" fumble I called out on their last send.
- "250 Points = $5 Off" is dead-simple points math — no mental gymnastics required, which is more than I can say for half these loyalty programs.
- The tiered mystery reward (BaubleBar bracelets) and the Rewards Bazaar callout give me a reason to actually check back mid-event instead of just once.
- Product grid is clean and on-brand — VIKTORROLF, Kérastase, NEST are legit "only at Sephora" exclusives, which is the curation signal that keeps me loyal to this banner over Ulta's mass-prestige mix.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is visible at the bottom, so I can at least confirm I'm still tracked as Rouge even with the points glitch up top.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" at the header is either a broken merge field or a genuinely alarming account issue — for a Rouge member this reads as a real trust hit, not a nitpick.
- This is a save-the-date with zero live, redeemable offer today — the CTA "Add to Calendar" is fine, but "Get a Sneak Peek" for the credit card promo feels like a tease with nothing behind it yet.
- The product grid at the bottom has no visible pricing tier callouts tied to the event itself — it's just a generic new-arrivals wall bolted onto a loyalty announcement, which dilutes the actual Beauty Insider message.
- Legal fine print block at the very bottom is a wall of gray text that nobody's reading — not a dealbreaker, just noise after an otherwise tight layout.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the "0 points" bug first — that's the single biggest trust-killer in this send, and it needs to show my actual balance before it goes out again.
- 2. Give me my *actual* points balance in the hero, not a generic "1 point = 5¢" reminder — Sephora has the data, use it like DAE and Glossier's GWP mechanics do when they personalize the ask.
- 3. Tie the product grid to points value ("182 points" instead of just "$182") so the whole email reinforces the math instead of switching modes halfway down.
- 4. Move the credit-card 8/6–8/9 bonus higher or make it clearer it's a *preview* window, not the main event — right now it reads like competing offers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge points are about to go further`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A month of bonus points, mystery rewards, and brand-only drops — starting Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: earn 500 points with your Sephora Credit Card, 8/6–8/9 only.`
