# 25% off for VIPs starts now
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA finally put a number on it, and it's 25% off sitewide. That's the first thing I do points math on. The hero is big, red and confident. But the "25% off" is clipped by the pencil image, so the bottom of the numbers is cut off. It looks half-designed. Then the body copy says "VIPs now have early access," and the banner says "Join free." So this is a sign-up gate dressed up as a sale. Glossier did the same "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members" move, and I called that an account-creation ad too.
- Compared with the 11/16 "48 hours from now…" teaser, this is the payoff. The offer is clearer, with 25%, 2x points and two free gifts. But the asterisk and the "Click HERE for promotion details" link in the footer tell me the fine print matters. I'd tap through, but I'd be checking whether the free gifts stack with 25% before I put anything in the cart. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The red banner and hero tell me the number right away: 25% off, early access, join free.
- The free complexion stick with any order and the free mini of choice with $100+ are easy to math out. I like the mini at the $100 threshold.
- The 2x VIP points line is the loyalty hook I look for. It's the Sephora Insider-style stacking I'd actually plan around.
- Best-sellers are shown with Allure badges: Limitless Lash Mascara, Super Serum Skin Tint, Eye Stylus. That's real social proof.
- Personalized Shade Match in the footer icons is a nice nod to skin-tone shopping.

## What's weak

- The "25% off" hero text is cut off by the pencil image, so the bottom edge of the numerals is clipped.
- The "Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40" shows up twice in the 2x2 grid. It looks like a copy-paste error, and it wastes a slot on a different hero.
- The "shop now" buttons are small, thin-outlined and low-contrast, and they don't name the product. At phone size they're tricky to tap.
- I can't tell what's actually early about the access, or when the sale starts or ends. There's no deadline, so no urgency.
- The "very important perks" block repeats the VIP pitch a third time. Free shipping on every order also conflicts with "Free U.S. shipping over $50" in the footer, which is confusing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero so the full "25% off" is readable, and put the join-free action in the same frame as a clear date: "Early access ends Nov 21."
- 2. Replace the duplicate skin tint with a different product, such as a lip or brow product. Make each button say "Shop mascara" or "Shop skin tint," and add prices or sale prices.
- 3. Show the offer math: "25% off + 2x points + free stick." Put the asterisk terms right under the hero, not in a footer link.
- 4. Cut the perks block, or fix the free shipping contradiction.
- **Subject Alt A:** `ILIA VIP early access: 25% off + 2x points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your early 25% off ends Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join free, get a free complexion stick with any order, plus a mini at $100.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Lash Mascara and Super Serum are in the sale. 2x points while it lasts.`
