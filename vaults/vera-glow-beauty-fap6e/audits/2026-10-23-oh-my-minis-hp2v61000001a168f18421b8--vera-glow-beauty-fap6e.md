# 😍 Oh. My. Minis. 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is a catalog with a cute subject line. Sephora sent me this exact "Oh. My. Minis." email earlier today, so this is a repeat, and the brand history shows the same pattern with "Open for new items" on 10/22 and the sample bag on 10/21. The Laneige hero is pretty, but the offer I'd actually act on sits in a thin banner above it. That's the "Choose a Trial Size" strip: free Korean-skincare mini with code KBEAUTY and a $40 purchase.
- I'm Rouge, so I did the points math right away. The email shows me a tier bar near the bottom, but it doesn't tell me what my status earns on these minis. Nothing says "Rouge gets X." The gift sets are $20 to $73, and the "value" prices ($51 value, $106 value) do the persuading. That's a fine merchandising grid, but it's a lot of scrolling with no member hook.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the Laneige lip set or the free trial size. I wouldn't forward it. It's a holiday-minis catalog, and Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty would have put a dated deal up top.

## What's working

- The "Choose a Trial Size" banner is a real offer: a free K-beauty mini with a $40 spend. It's the best thing in the email.
- Every product tile shows the set price next to the value (e.g. "$26 ($36 value)"), so I can do the math fast.
- The "NEW • LIMITED EDITION" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags tell me what's exclusive. I like that signal as someone who cross-shops Ulta.
- The Clean and Planet Aware badges on the SAIE, Tower 28 and Josie Maran tiles fit how I shop.
- The Halloween Beauty Services and Free Samples tiles are timely and seasonal.

## What's weak

- The best offer is squeezed into a thin white strip above the hero. The hero's "Mini Sets for the Holidays" with a "Shop Now" button gets all the visual weight and carries no offer.
- It's the second send of this subject today. Sephora is repeating itself the way it did with the 10/22 new-arrivals email and the 10/21 sample bag. That's a cadence problem.
- It's very long, and there's no deadline anywhere. Nothing tells me when the minis or the free trial size end.
- My tier isn't acknowledged. The Beauty Insider bar and "Rewards Bazaar" are there, but there's no "Rouge members get..." line.
- The Halloween services and sample tiles are tiny and hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free trial size (KBEAUTY) into the hero, and add a deadline.
- 2. Add a tier line like "Rouge: free shipping + early access on minis," with points shown on the sets.
- 3. Cut the grid to 4 hero sets, and add a "Under $25" filter since minis are impulse buys.
- 4. Don't send the same creative twice in a day. Re-send only to non-openers, with a new subject.
