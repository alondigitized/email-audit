# Early Access To SALE Ends Soon! 🧡✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- Ten. I want that number on record before I say anything else — this is Charlotte Tilbury email number ten since June 11, and I have been logging them because somewhere around number seven it became necessary for my sanity. And yet, despite everything, I opened this one and I'm not even mad about it, because "Golden Goddess Glow" plus "3 FREE FULL-SIZE GIFTS" is actually a different kind of pitch than the Unreal Summer percentage-off parade that preceded it. The gift-with-purchase tier mechanic is a genuine creative pivot, and CT knows how to make a GWP offer hit — $118, $198, $270, stacked cleanly so I can see exactly what I'm committing to. I was already running the math before I finished reading the first tier.
- Here's where it falls apart: "Ends Soon!" without a date is not urgency, it's vibes. After watching Glossier run an "ending soon" sale for a full uninterrupted week in June, I am immune to this construction. If the early access window closes June 26 at midnight, say that. I can't plan a $198 CT run around "soon." Also, the preheader is essentially the subject line reworded — "Exclusive Early Access Ends Soon!" — which is a wasted slot that Sephora consistently fills better by teasing a specific product or category instead of echoing the hero copy.
- I would not forward this. I would screenshot the GWP tiers and paste them into my beauty group chat with "you're buying CT anyway, might as well hit $198 now." That's a recommendation but it's not the email doing the work — that's me doing the work for the email.

## What's working

- **The GWP pivot genuinely breaks the pattern.** Nine emails of "X% off" and then suddenly "here are three tiers of free full-size gifts" — that's the first creative refresh in this entire June run.
- **Tier clarity is excellent.** $118 / $198 / $270 with individual "GET THIS GIFT" CTAs per tier is exactly how you communicate a spend ladder without making me decode anything.
- **"SPOIL ME NOW!" is real CTA copy.** It's aspirational, it's CT's brand voice, and it's not "Shop Now" or — god help us — "Discover."
- **The warm golden palette is cohesive and on-season.** Dewy skin close-up, bronzer aesthetic, peachy-gold tones — this reads as summer beauty editorial, not a stock-photo sale email.
- **45% off kits as a secondary offer is additive.** If I can't stretch to the GWP thresholds, there's still a reason to click. Good fallback architecture.

## What's weak

- **"Ends Soon!" is not a deadline.** No date, no time, no clock — just a claim. After Glossier's week-long "ending soon" in June, I don't click on vague urgency anymore.
- **The preheader is a reworded subject line.** "Exclusive Early Access Ends Soon!" against "Early Access To SALE Ends Soon!" is nearly identical — Sephora uses this space to tease offer specifics and it shows; CT left money on the table here.
- **No tier acknowledgment.** CT has my purchase history. "As an early access member, your gifts are waiting" would make $270 feel like a VIP reward, not just a price tag. Drunk Elephant's personalization angle works because it feels like it knows you; this doesn't.
- **Cadence is unsustainable.** I'm not scoring for it, but I'm naming it: the brand trust that got me to open email ten is the same trust ten emails in two weeks is burning through.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a hard date in the subject and CTA.** "Ends June 26" is one phrase that turns "soon" into a reason to click tonight instead of tomorrow, which means never.
- 2. **Rewrite the preheader** to tease the GWP mechanic directly — "Spend $118, get a free full-size gift" is a completely different inbox story than repeating the subject.
- 3. **Name my tier.** Any loyalty callout — "early access member," even just "you're in" — closes the psychological gap between seeing $270 and actually building a cart.
- 4. **Surface one hero product by name** in the subject line to give beauty-obsessed openers something specific to seek out.
- **Subject Alt A:** `3 Free CT Gifts — Early Access Ends June 26 🧡`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Charlotte Tilbury gifts expire tonight ✨`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $118+, choose a free full-size gift. $198 gets you two.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Golden Goddess GWP: three tiers, one deadline. See yours.`
