# September deals are here!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Sally Beauty, again — and I said the same thing on 9/1 with the Appiversary email: this is objectively not my aisle. No Sephora Rouge points, no Ulta Diamond math, no brand-curation moment, just a straightforward hair-color-and-clippers circular dressed up as a "September deals" reset. The model's a nice, warm fall-red-lip visual, but that's about the extent of anything that reads "beauty event" to me.
- Functionally it's clean — BOGO 50% hair care, 30% off BaByliss clippers, BOGO jewelry, a $15.99 color bundle, all with clear end dates and Shop Now buttons stacked in a legible red/cream block layout. No render bugs, no broken images, nothing overlapping. But there's zero reason for someone who cross-shops Sephora launches and Ulta 21 Days to click through, because there's no prestige, no points, no "your tier" language anywhere.
- I'm marking this read, not clicking, not forwarding. If anything I'd forward it to my mom who still box-dyes her own hair, not to my beauty-swap group chat.

## What's working

- The fall-red-lip hero photo is genuinely a nice seasonal visual, feels current for September.
- Every offer block has a clear end date (9/30, 9/14) — no vague "for a limited time" fluff.
- Shop Now CTAs are consistent and easy to spot against the red background.
- Klarna 4-interest-free-payments strip and the five-icon service row (in-store, online, free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, BOPIS) are useful trust/logistics cues even if not persona-relevant.

## What's weak

- Nothing here maps to how I actually shop — no Beauty Insider or Ulta Rewards equivalent, no points multiplier, no prestige/skincare/makeup category at all.
- It's hair color, clippers, and jewelry — categorically the wrong aisle for someone tracking Sephora launches and Ulta events.
- Compared to Sephora's "point of view" drop I logged this week (7/10, a real new-brand moment) or even ILIA's Overglaze shade launch (7/10), this has zero discovery or newness pull — just repackaged category basics.
- Cadence-wise this is Sally's fourth-plus distinct send I've logged in weeks (Appiversary 9/1, COLORfest last-chance 8/31, $35/15% 8/29, BOPIS 15% 8/28) — the brand is prolific but none of it builds toward anything for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment me out entirely, or if Sally insists on emailing me, lead with something cross-category like fragrance or nail (their "Nails" nav link exists but never gets promoted) instead of hair color/clippers every time.
- 2. Cut the offer count — four separate BOGO/percent-off mechanics in one email dilutes urgency; Glossier's Labor Day "30% off select favorites" (6/10 from me) at least kept it to one clean ask.
- 3. If loyalty program exists, surface it — even a small "Rewards members get X" line would give this some stickiness for someone like me who's always doing the points math.
