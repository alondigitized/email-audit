# Your picks, up to 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is back with the 20/25/40 number shuffle again, and I'm doing the points math again. The top banner says 25% off sitewide, the hero circle says 20% off, and the red block near the bottom says up to 40%. Three numbers in one email. It's the fourth ILIA send in three days, after yesterday's "20% off—all. the. time." and this morning's plain-text "Ready to claim it all?".
- The pie-tin of Multi-Sticks is a cute hero, and "Your picks" implies browse-based personalization. But the four products below are the bestsellers (Super Serum Skin Tint twice, Multi-Stick, mascara), with no prices, no shades and no reviews. It doesn't feel like it knows what I looked at. It feels like a generic grid.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the Multi-Stick, but I'd check the code at checkout first. Forward it? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The pie-tin Multi-Stick hero is charming and on-season. It stands out from the red-and-glitter Black Friday look at Sephora and Ulta.
- The "SHOP YOUR PICKS →" button is big, dark and unmistakable.
- The Allure Best of Beauty badges on the products are real trust cues for a prestige-curious shopper like me.
- The "very important perks" block spells out free shipping, points on every purchase, and early access. That's the loyalty math I want to see.
- The Personalized Shade Match footer icon speaks to my skin-tone needs.

## What's weak

- 25%, 20% and up to 40% all appear with asterisks, and the fine print is a link at the very bottom. Sephora's Cyber Week email said "30% off these top brands for 24 hours" and I knew exactly what I was getting. Here I have to decode it.
- The Super Serum Skin Tint appears twice in the grid, once in the first row and again in the last. It looks like filler.
- No prices, no shade names, no star ratings. Glossier at least put reviews in front of me. Here the product cards are just a name and a "shop now" box.
- There's no deadline anywhere. Ulta's 50/40/30 ladder told me what ends when. ILIA says "Black Friday sale" and nothing about when it stops.
- The "free gift with all orders*" line is buried in the body copy, and I don't know what the gift is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline number and let the others be tiers. Something like "20% off everything, 25% for VIP, up to 40% on select items," laid out as a ladder the way Ulta does it.
- 2. If it says "Your picks," show what I actually browsed, with shade names, prices and the discounted price.
- 3. Add a deadline, such as "ends Monday midnight."
- 4. Show the free gift as an image, not a footnote.
- 5. Replace the repeated skin tint with a different product.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Multi-Stick you viewed is 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ILIA picks: 20% off ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free gift with every order and 25% for VIP members.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shade-matched favorites, up to 40% off select items.`
