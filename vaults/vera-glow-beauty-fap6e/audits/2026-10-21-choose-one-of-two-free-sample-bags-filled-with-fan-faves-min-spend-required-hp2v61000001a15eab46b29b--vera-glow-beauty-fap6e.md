# Choose one of two FREE sample bags filled with fan faves 💫 Min. spend required.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened this for the free sample bag, and it's a repeat. The same subject hit my inbox earlier today, so this is the second send of it. The top does its job: "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag," a "Shop to Redeem" button, and a Savings Event teaser in the strip above it. Then the email turns into a long gift-set catalog that I had to scroll through to get anywhere.
- I did the points math and the asterisk bothers me. "Min. spend required" is in the subject, but the threshold isn't in the subject or the hero as far as I can read. Sephora ran "Beauty that's found only here" yesterday and "These hot sets + you = ⚡" the day before, and this is the same gift-set scroll again. The 30% Savings Event is still only "coming soon," so I'm in hold-my-cart mode. I'd click once, to check the bag contents and the spend threshold. I wouldn't forward it, and it's one more in a week of Sephora sends that feel the same.
- Ulta would have put the 21 Days-style deadline up top, and e.l.f. SKIN's "NEW kits for your skincare fix" stated its prices in the hero. This email stacks eight gift sets, so the freebie ends up buried.

## What's working

- The "Pick Your FREE* Sample Bag" hero is the first thing I see, and it's a real perk.
- "Choose one of two" tells me there's a decision to make, and I like that it's framed as a choice.
- The Savings Event teaser (30%) at the top tells me when to hold my big haul.
- Each gift set shows a price and a "$X value" burst (like $26 on the Laneige lip minis and $49 on the Fenty set). That's easy math for me, and it's better than Drunk Elephant's vague tiers.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags add some launch-day clarity.
- The Insider free-shipping banner and the Insider tier bar near the bottom tell me I was recognized as a member.

## What's weak

- The sample bag is the draw, but the contents and the minimum spend are nowhere clear. That's the information I need to decide.
- The 30% Savings Event is only "coming soon," so it's a tease with no date I can plan around.
- The gift-set scroll is very long. Eight stacked sets with no priority makes the hero compete with everything below it.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is tiny and dense. The barcode and coupon section is cramped.
- It's the same subject as earlier today, and the rest of the week's sends were also gift-set heavy. That's cadence fatigue.
- The email gives me nothing to personalize it to me. I'm a Rouge member, and nothing shows my points balance or my progress toward the next perk.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the spend threshold, the bag contents, and the redemption deadline in the hero. Something like "Spend $X, get your pick of 2 bags, ends [date]."
- 2. Cut the gift-set list to 3 sets, or move it to a separate gifting send.
- 3. Show my tier and points. For example: "Rouge: you're X points from your next reward."
- 4. Give the Savings Event a date and a Rouge early-access angle. Rouge members already get early access at Sephora, so say it.
- 5. Don't resend the same subject on the same day. Send the second one to non-openers only, with a new subject.
