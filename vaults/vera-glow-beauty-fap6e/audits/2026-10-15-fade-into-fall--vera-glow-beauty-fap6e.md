# Fade into fall
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- I'm glad ILIA finally dropped the 20% broadcast. It's a calmer, prettier email than the last four. But I opened this expecting a "fall edit" and I got a catalog with a gift-with-purchase buried in the banner. The warm-toned montage up top is gorgeous and the copper CTA is clear. Then it turns into five product rows with five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- The real offer is a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara with $75+. It's in a thin top banner and again near the bottom, and nothing in the hero says it. That's a weak spot for me. Sephora would've put the freebie in the hero. Ulta would've put a points multiplier next to it. I'm doing GWP math here: a mini mascara at $75+ is a fine threshold, but the email never tells me the mini's value, so I can't tell if it's worth padding my cart.
- Would I click? Probably one tap on "Shop fall shades," since I've been eyeing Motif/Resin gloss. I wouldn't forward it. It's a nice reset after four back-to-back 20%-off sends, but it doesn't push me to buy today.

## What's working

- The hero montage nails the autumn mood. Warm skin tones and golden foliage actually show me the shades.
- The copper "Shop Fall Shades" button is easy to see and easy to tap.
- The alternating image/text product rows are clean, and the italic shade names (Motif + Resin, Gilded + Relic) read like a real shade story.
- The lash mascara GWP is a refreshing change from the 10/9–10/13 20% fatigue. Glossier's "Surprise Sale" at least gave me a number, and this gives me a freebie.
- The footer trust cues are tidy: free shipping over $50, Leaping Bunny, Personalized Shade Match, and ILIA VIP.

## What's weak

- The GWP is tiny in the top banner and has no value attached ("mini" of what size?). It should be the hero.
- There are no prices on any product. I can't build a $75 cart without clicking each one.
- Every row has the same ghost-style "SHOP NOW" with no hierarchy. Five identical buttons make it a catalog.
- There's no end date. "Claim a free lift" has no urgency, and the fine print is hidden behind "see site for details."
- I'm an ILIA VIP-adjacent shopper, but the VIP tier only shows up as a footer badge. There's no points or tier math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the free mini mascara in the hero as a big "free gift with $75" with an end date, and drop the montage below it.
- 2. Add prices and a "build to $75" helper, like a $75 bundle of gloss + eyeliner + blush.
- 3. Make the product CTAs specific ("Shop Motif + Resin gloss") and give one row a filled button.
- 4. Show the gift value and a VIP earning line ("VIPs earn double points on this order").
- 5. Add a visible deadline and spell out the terms in the email body.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free mini mascara with $75 + fall shades`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New fall shades: Motif, Relic, Magnetic`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, get a free mini Limitless Lash Mascara. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Candlelit blush, honey browns, and a free mascara gift. VIPs earn extra points.`
