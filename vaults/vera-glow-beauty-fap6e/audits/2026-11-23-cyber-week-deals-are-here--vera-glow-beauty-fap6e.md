- Okay, this is the first Sephora send this week that gave me an actual in-store, today-only deal instead of another "download the app" nag. - It's also a long email. After the 11/21 "Cyber Week daily deals just dropped" send and the 11/22 "PSA" send, I'm now reading a third version of the same purple sequin template. The Black Friday Preview, the store finder, the gift guide and the Kohl's barcode all pile onto one scroll. Ulta's 50/40/30 ladder email at least put the whole sale in one place.
- I'd open it and tap the Ole Henriksen hero. I'd forward the CYBERSCENT code to a friend who shops fragrance. I wouldn't forward the email itself.

## What's working

- "Gift fragrance, get 4X points" with code CYBERSCENT sits right at the top. That's a real, math-able perk for a Rouge.
- The 30% OFF hero for Ole Henriksen and Milk Makeup is huge and readable in half a second. It beats the vague "Gift Edit" banner Sally Beauty sent.
- The "24 hours only" line gives me a deadline I believe, since it's tied to one day.
- The product grid shows crossed-out prices ($7 vs $10, $16 vs $32), so I can see the savings without clicking.
- The "Black Friday Preview: 50% off" teaser, with 11/26 at 6pm PT, tells me when to plan my haul. That's the beauty-event calendar content I want.
- The store hours and nearest-location block is useful if I'm picking up in person.

## What's weak

- It's the third purple sequin Sephora email in about 48 hours, after the 11/21 and 11/22 sends. The creative isn't fresh.
- Too many offers compete: 30% brands, 30% MAC, 50% off preview, 4X points, Kohl's, a gift guide and a credit card pitch. The eye has nowhere to settle after the hero.
- The footer legal copy is tiny and dense. The "in-stock merchandise only" caveats are buried.
- The Limited-Edition rhode block is a nice surprise, but it's a grey block with a small "Shop now" and no price. It feels stuck on.
- The Cyber Week Deals grid has no product names or brands I can read, so I can't tell what I'm buying.
- The subject's "‼️" is a bit shouty compared with Ulta's cleaner "Black Friday is here! Terms & excl. apply."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the 4X points / CYBERSCENT code as the hero, because that's the most loyalty-relevant element. Cut the store finder, gift guide and credit card pitch from a send that's already about today's deals.
- 2. Make the 24-hour deal the single hero, then give the Black Friday 50% preview its own send so it isn't diluted.
- 3. Add brand and product names plus a points-earned line to each grid item, like "$24 · earns X pts."
- 4. Refresh the creative between sends so it doesn't read as the same email three days running.
- 5. Move the "in-stock merchandise only" language closer to the offer instead of the fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Two QA findings are confirmed in the source: the plain-text part is empty (0 chars, FAIL), and image alt-text handling is inconsistent. The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) came back "not found", but the report attributes that to the AgentMail relay, so I can't treat it as a Sephora defect. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and CTA hrefs weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 36. It reported no broken or redirecting links, so none are confirmed.
- Tracking pixels are present and well-formed:
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1` is a 1x1 open pixel.
  - A second hidden pixel at `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…` has `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
- The `mi_ecmp` campaign ID is consistent across the `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs, so the campaign attribution is coherent.
- Because 36 links were skipped, I can't say whether any of them have tracking problems.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in `<style>`:** an MSO conditional comment sits inside the `@media` block: `.mobileHeaderLogo{…}<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge{…}`.
  - `<!--` and `-->` are only ignored at the top level of CSS. Inside an `@media` block, some parsers will drop the following rule(s). That includes `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, and possibly the other mobile rules after it.
  - The `img.mso-size` rule also never reaches Outlook, because the `[if mso]` comment isn't in the HTML body.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed layout width:** the wrapper table has `min-width:700px; width:700px`. It may cause horizontal scroll on narrow screens unless a mobile override exists. I couldn't see one in the truncated source.
- **Missing alt text (QA):**
  - `o.gif`: the source shows `alt="" aria-hidden="true"`, which is valid for a decorative pixel. This QA warning is a false positive.
  - The hidden `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt`, but it is `display:none`, so it has no practical impact.
  - Six `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` images are flagged: `df9af91c42339c07`, `da0148084c4c66f6`, `20e4e51340afcef6`, `727459dc432025a7`, `731160b4c3175e23` and `8e8fdee6cedcdc03`. Their markup is outside the truncated source, so I couldn't confirm the flag. If they are content images (the `/rp/` path suggests rendered content), the missing alt text is a real problem. With images blocked, the email would show nothing.
- **Plain-text part missing:** QA reports 0 chars (FAIL). This is a multipart/alternative gap that affects deliverability and screen-reader or text-only clients.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The value should be `noindex`.
  - `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source.
- The JSON-LD block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and an empty `"@type": "PromotionCard"`. These are empty template fields, so the Gmail promo annotation will have no data. That is low severity and not user-visible. It also uses `http://schema.org/` rather than `https`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified and not a confirmed defect. Check the raw `.eml` before concluding anything.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data.
- The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the footer, which was truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but I can't verify it from the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- The only campaign identifier I can confirm is `mi_ecmp=20261123_Cyber_Week_Daily_Brand_Deals_Day_1` on image and pixel URLs. No `utm_*` parameters are visible.

3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the six `mi.sephora.com/p/rp/*.png` images, after confirming their markup.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify headers from the raw message**, not the relay copy. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC in `Authentication-Results`.
