**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "New and Only Here" is Sephora doing what Sephora does best — this is a straight-up new-arrivals drop, and my eye goes right to that before/after skin close-up for Tower 28 Beauty Foundation. That's the strongest hero this brand's run in my recent history — actual skin transformation proof, not another Coco Mademoiselle bottle or barcode-on-book-stack filler. But then it just... keeps scrolling. Drunk Elephant, Chris McMillan, Patrick Starrr, Aestura, Dr. Groot, Dolce&Gabbana fragrance — nine different "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" product blocks stacked vertically with zero curation narrative tying them together.
- This is basically a category dump dressed up as a launch email, and it's the opposite problem from what I flagged with Sephora's Olive Young edit (9/10, 8/20) — that one had an actual thesis. This has none. It's also mid-scroll where they bury "Earn Points Faster" 4X-5X on The Ordinary and a free point of view GWP — that's Rouge-relevant content that should never be this far down.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably on Tower 28 since the before/after actually sold me. Would I forward it? No — there's no single hook compelling enough to share, it's just a laundry list.

## What's working

- The Tower 28 before/after is genuinely persuasive — real skin, real claim ("natural-looking finish," "medium buildable coverage"), best visual proof I've seen from Sephora lately.
- "Clean at Sephora" seal on Drunk Elephant and Chris McMillan is a real trust cue I actually look for.
- Prices are visible right on the product blocks ($34, $70, $28, $190) — no hide-the-price games.
- The Ordinary 4X/5X points multiplier is genuinely good Rouge-tier content, exactly the kind of "points math" I'm always doing.

## What's weak

- Nine near-identical "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" blocks with no editorial thread — feels like they just queued the whole new-arrivals feed into one email.
- The points multiplier and free GWP are Rouge's actual bread and butter and they're buried past eight product blocks — I'd have quit scrolling before I got there.
- No sizing/skin-tone/shade guidance on Tower 28 foundation despite it being the hero — for a foundation launch that's the first thing I want.
- "Foundation & Concealer Faces" top banner (25% off Pureology, Too Faced, CLINIQUE) is a completely different offer/category competing with the "new arrivals" theme right at the top — confusing opener.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to 3-4 heroes max and move The Ordinary points multiplier + free GWP up to position two, right after Tower 28 — that's the actual Rouge hook, not product #8.
- 2. Add shade range or skin-tone match language to the Tower 28 foundation block since the before/after implies a match story it never finishes.
- 3. Split the top banner (25% off brands) into its own send or clearly separate it visually — right now it fights the "New and Only Here" framing before you even scroll.
- 4. Give this a real curatorial angle like the Olive Young edit did — "this week's clean beauty drops" or similar — instead of an undifferentiated product feed.
