- My gut reaction is that this is a vibe email, not a shopping email. The hero is a woman biting into a slice of spice cake, then a moody Northern Lights banner. - The Tromsø and holiday-spice-cake concept is a real hook, and "cardamom buttercream frosting" and "cedarwood and warm musk" tell me what it smells like.

- The "NEW!" badge on the hero makes the drop clear right away.

## What's weak

- No price on the hero product. Sephora always puts the price on the fragrance, and I can't decide anything without one.
- The two "SHOP E.L.F. POP" buttons are identical and sit around a wall of mood copy. The text blocks are small and low-contrast, tan on mauve, and I had to squint.
- Top Picks is a random mix (primer, lip liner, setting spray) and doesn't tie to the fragrance. The "SHOP NOW" buttons under each are tiny tap targets.
- The category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are huge gray blocks with no imagery. It reads as filler and makes the email very long.
- Nothing on points. e.l.f. Rewards is mentioned, but I can't see what this earns, and I can't do the points math I do at Sephora and Ulta.

- 2. Cut the three mood blocks to one and give the space to a real offer or a bundle.
- 3. Make Top Picks about the fragrance (a layering set or a mini) instead of random bestsellers.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (table layout, `lang="en"`, preheader, `role="presentation"`), but the automated pass rate is 27%. Four material links failed probing, there is no plain-text part, and an image is missing alt text. The authentication and unsubscribe-header results can't be confirmed because of relay limits.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 of 12 probed links failed** with `unknown url type`. The probe got a relative path after following the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_xp=email&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&bxid=...&onelink_id=wTtZ&pid=Email&c=ema...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely a probe limitation, not confirmed breakage.** The final destination was a relative `Location` that the QA probe didn't resolve against `elfcosmetics.com`. A browser would resolve it against the redirecting host (`links.elfcosmetics.com`), so this is worth checking manually. If that host doesn't serve `/products/...`, these links 404 for real recipients. The probe didn't capture the redirect's `Location` header, so I can't tell which case applies.
- The variant parameters (`Size=`, `Color=`) are preserved in the destinations, which is correct.
- All links go through a Braze click-tracking redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...~~`). That is expected, but the tracking tokens are long and wrap into the probe output.
- 29 links were not probed, including footer and social links, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **One image has no alt text.** Its `src` is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/f_FRpP6s2GKCmL-Z6Ntmhg~~/AAQRxRA~/q4GDlxQsbkPUoVA9iw-pogz4clRoQ1JkAgDn7AmsVltSJYCyIijLn…`. This is probably the tracking pixel or a content image, and the truncated source doesn't say which. If it's a content image, it needs an `alt`; if it's the pixel, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Empty font name:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in `html_block block-1`. It falls back harmlessly, but it points to a template variable that didn't render.
- **Nested layouts:** each `html_block` embeds a complete `nl-container` / `row` / `row-content` table tree (640px). This adds markup weight and raises the risk of Outlook/MSO width quirks. It isn't a confirmed failure.
- **Webfont:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Open+Sans` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and the stack falls back to Arial.
- Positives: the logo image has `alt="e.l.f."`, the layout tables use `role="presentation"`, and the mobile stacking media query is at `max-width:660px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `*|…|*`. Only the first part of the HTML was available, so the rest is unchecked. The empty `font-family:''` above is the only possible template artifact.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No plain-text alternative.** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). Multipart emails without a text part can raise spam-filter scores. This may be an AgentMail relay limitation, but it is flagged as a failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com`.
- The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address aren't visible in the truncated source, so I couldn't verify either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The final URLs are hidden behind Braze redirects. UTM parameters can't be verified from the source, and the probe failures prevented checking them at the destination.
- The app-download link carries attribution parameters (`af_xp=email`, `pid=Email`, `onelink_id=wTtZ`, `bxid=…`, `c=ema…`, truncated), so that link is tracked.
- The product links resolve to `/products/<handle>?Size=…` / `?Color=…`, so they deep-link to the correct variants. No `utm_*` parameters appear in the captured paths.
- Landing alignment can't be confirmed for the four failed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Manually follow the four `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` links (primer, lip liner, receipt scanning, app). Check that each `Location` header is absolute and returns 200 from `elfcosmetics.com`. Fix the probe to resolve relative redirects against the final host. If real, fix the Braze link domain.
2. Add a plain-text part to the multipart message.
3. Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from a direct mailbox rather than the relay, then re-test.
4. Add an `alt` to the flagged image, using `alt=""` if it is the tracking pixel.
5. Fill in `<title>`, and remove or fix the empty `font-family:''`.
6. Confirm that `utm_*` parameters are appended by Braze at click time, and that the unsubscribe link and postal address are in the full HTML.
