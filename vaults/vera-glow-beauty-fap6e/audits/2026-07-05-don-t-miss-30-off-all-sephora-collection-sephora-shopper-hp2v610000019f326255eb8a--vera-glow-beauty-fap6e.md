# 🔔 Don’t miss 30% off ALL Sephora Collection, Sephora Shopper
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- Five. That's how many Sephora Stacking Weekend emails have hit my inbox since July 1st, and this is the *second* one today with this exact subject line. The July 1st launch was a genuine 9 — I had a cart pre-loaded before I finished reading it, had the stacking mechanics laid out in my notes, and loved it. This feels like Sephora sent the campaign creative team home at 4pm and put the "send again" button on a timer.
- That said, the email itself is not bad. The content pivot away from "here are the mechanics" to "here's what's trending / new from Paula's Choice" is actually smart for a day-five send — it gives me a reason to click beyond just "you still haven't bought." The "All Over Your Feed" social block and the Paula's Choice launch tease are the two things that would actually pull me in. If this had been send number two, I'd have clicked immediately.
- But it's send number five. My muscle memory is already auto-archiving this before I've finished the preview text. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it — my friends who care about Stacking Weekend already know, and I already sent them the July 1st email.

## What's working

- **Paula's Choice new launch section** — new launches always make me tap. Even on the fifth send I'm scanning for something I haven't seen before, and this delivers.
- **"All Over Your Feed" social block** — this is how I actually find products. Seeing it in an email feels native to how I shop, not like a brand forcing themselves into my TikTok brain.
- **Stacking Weekend banner at top** — Sephora keeps the offer prominent rather than burying it below editorial scroll. Smart template discipline, unlike Ulta which would have put a "Beauty Break — this event is over" banner up (*yes, I saw that Ulta send, that was a disaster*).
- **Paula's Choice features specifically** — compared to a vague "shop skincare" link, this feels curated. That's the difference between e.l.f. SKIN's Fourth of July SPF focus (which earned an 8 from me for specificity) and a generic brand dump.
- **No render bugs** — clean render, images loading, hierarchy intact. Drunk Elephant's beach email had the same cleanliness; this meets that bar.

## What's weak

- **Duplicate send on the same day** — I literally already reviewed this exact subject line from Sephora earlier today. That's an operations failure, not a creative one, and it nukes trust in the brand's list hygiene.
- **"BEAUTY WITH A FOLLOWING" hero copy** — this is editorial fluff on what should be an urgency-close email. If you're trying to get the stragglers to convert before 7/7, lead with the offer, not a vibe.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — ILIA's day-four "3 lip shades, 20% off" email gave me a specific product entry point; clicking felt purposeful. "SHOP NOW" sends me to a homepage I can navigate myself. Give me a "Shop Paula's Choice" or "Grab Sephora Collection" button.
- **No stacking mechanic reminder** — the reason the July 1st email was a 9 was that it showed me *how to stack*: 30% + 2X points + free same-day. Day-five stragglers need that reminder front and center, not buried. This email assumes I already know, which I do, but a new opener doesn't.
- **"Sephora Shopper" label feels generic** — I'm Rouge. My Beauty Insider points balance is a personality trait at this point. Calling me "Sephora Shopper" in the subject is like Sally Beauty calling their Prestige-tier member a "Hair Enthusiast."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Merge the two July 5th sends into one** — the operations team sent this same subject twice today. One email, half the list damage.
- 2. **Swap hero copy to offer-led for the final days push** — instead of "BEAUTY WITH A FOLLOWING," try: "Ends Monday: Your Sephora Collection 30% off window." Use the hero real estate to close, not to editorialize.
- 3. **Swap "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTAs** — "Shop Paula's Choice New Arrivals" and "Complete Your Sephora Collection Haul" as separate buttons. Two conversion paths, both specific.
- 4. **Add the stacking mechanic reminder** — one line: "Stack it: 30% off + 2X points + FREE same-day delivery, ends 7/7." Rouge members do the math; make it easy.
- 5. **Personalize to tier** — "Rouge member" instead of "Sephora Shopper." Acknowledge I'm already in the program.
- **Subject Alt A:** `🔔 Ends Monday: Paula's Choice is 30% off right now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge exclusive: Stacking Weekend closes 7/7 — your cart's waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack 30% off + 2X points + free same-day. This is the last weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New from Paula's Choice + 30% off all Sephora Collection — ends Monday.`

| **User zoom disabled** | `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` — `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom | High (WCAG 1.4.4) |
