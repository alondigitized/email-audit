# You might be a foundation person
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was "damn" too, but not for the reason they wanted. "RESULTS LIKE: DAMN" is a fun headline, and the 25-shade clock of swatches is the best-looking asset Drunk Elephant has sent me all week. But the actual deal is a dark gray strip at the very top, and it reads like a legal banner. "TWO IS BETTER THAN... NOT TWO!" took me a second to decode. It's 20% off one Kamo plus one full-size moisturizer, same transaction. Once I did the math, I realized I'd have to buy the moisturizer to get the discount.
- This is the fifth Drunk Elephant send since 9/23, and it's the Kamo story again. The 9/23 "It's OK to throw shade" email already walked me through the shade finder. The 9/29 "$158 for $99?" email was the holiday kit one, and I called out that it never mentioned the numbers in the subject. Today's offer is at least in the email, but it's buried. Sephora Insider did a better job today: a giant red "2X" is the first thing you see, and I know my points math instantly. Here I'd have to scroll to the fine print to find out what I'm actually buying.
- I'd open it, scroll to the clock, and probably click Shop Kamo, because I'm already curious about the 12-hour wear. I wouldn't forward it. A friend who's shade-matching wants a swatch she can find, not "MEDIUM 15W" under a single blob.

## What's working

- The swatch clock is gorgeous. It shows the full 25-shade range at a glance, from very fair to very deep, and it's the kind of visual Rare Beauty and Tower 28 do well.
- "SHOP KAMO" shows up twice in clear green buttons, so I never have to hunt for the CTA.
- The mix of models, a fair-skinned woman, a deep-skinned woman, and an older woman in the bare-face vs. after shot, makes the shade range feel real rather than just claimed.
- The clinical stats (100% immediate radiance, 91% texture) are big, bold, and easy to scan, and the footnotes are there.
- "Use-it-as-you-see-fit foundation/concealer/skin tint" explains the product category fast.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried in a dark gray bar at the top, and the headline is a joke instead of the deal. Ulta would have put "20% off" front and center, and Sephora would have led with the points bonus.
- "TWO IS BETTER THAN... NOT TWO!" is confusing. The 20% only applies if I buy a Kamo and a moisturizer together.
- The fine print has a visible typo: "checkout.Offer valid while supplies last." It also says it can't be combined with other promos, and it excludes subscription orders. I'd rather see that up front.
- There's no loyalty angle at all. I'm Rouge at Sephora and Diamond at Ulta, and nothing here tells me whether Drunk Elephant's own program does anything.
- The shade finder is thin. I get one swatch ("MEDIUM 15W") and an underlined link, when I wanted an actual quiz or at least a few more shades.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "20% off Kamo + a full-size moisturizer" right under the headline, with the "TWO IS BETTER THAN… NOT TWO!" joke as the sub-line instead.
- 2. Put the shade finder where the clock is. Make the swatches clickable, or add a "Find your shade in 60 seconds" button right under the image.
- 3. Show a real review or star rating. 34 people in a clinical study isn't the same as social proof, and I'd trust "4.6 stars from 1,200 reviews" more.
- 4. Fix the typo and pull the fine print up so the "must buy both" rule is visible before I click.
- 5. Add a real deadline. "While supplies last" isn't urgent. "Ends Sunday" would be.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: the automated QA found 0 issues and 3 warnings, with a 57% pass rate. The confirmed problems are three `http://` social icons, two images with no alt text, the recipient's email address in a tracking URL, and CSS that some clients will handle badly. Header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, authentication) couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so I can't rule out issues further down.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracked links not probed:** 14 links were skipped because they go through click-redirect domains (`mi.drunkelephant.com`, `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com`). Their final destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the pixel URL `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the address in plaintext. That is PII in a query string, so it can show up in proxy, CDN and referrer logs.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoclinicals_p…` is a standard open pixel. It also carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u=98161986`).
- **Mixed content:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS, or block mixed content, may drop them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt` attribute. Both look like tracking pixels. They should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, so screen readers skip them and some clients don't show placeholder text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `<style>` block declares `body {…}` first and then three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules. `@import` is only valid before other rules, so these are ignored. The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags in `<head>`, which is redundant.
- **HTML comments inside CSS:** the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block contains `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->`. In CSS these are legacy CDO/CDC tokens. Some parsers will drop the adjacent rule, which could affect `*[class=mobile1]` in that block.
- **Third-party font hosts:** `BrownBold`, `BrownLight` and `BrownThin` come from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and `BrownRegular` comes from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`. Most email clients ignore web fonts, so the font stack needs a safe fallback. I couldn't see whether one is declared, because the source is truncated.
- **Unusual mobile sizing:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:12–15px` and `width:17px !important` at ≤418px and ≤600px. This is likely to clip text on small screens.
