# We’re *sheer* for this bronzy glow 🫰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "The e.l.f. Edit" is basically a beauty digest disguised as a newsletter, and it's a LOT — best sellers, new arrivals, a lip-combo tutorial, a six-category grid, all before I even get to a real price point. The bronzer tint gets its own hero moment ("you dropped hints... so we're dropping NEW tints!") and $6 is still genuinely fun impulse-buy territory, same as when I logged this exact product yesterday (8/31, "NEW! Sheer For It Bronzer Tint 🤎," 6.00/10). Problem is, I already saw this. Same tint, same "sheer" wordplay, one day later — that's not a cadence, that's a rerun.
- This isn't a targeted send, it's a catalog dump — "Bestie" greeting, two generic CTAs up top (Shop Best Sellers / Shop New Arrivals), then an avalanche of category tiles. I'm not opening this on my phone and finding anything faster than I would just going to the site. Sephora's Marc Jacobs GWP email from a few days back at least led with one clean offer I could act on in five seconds; this makes me scroll past a lip-liner tutorial and a full nav menu before I hit anything shoppable.
- Verdict: skim, maybe tap the bronzer tint since it's cheap and I liked it yesterday too, but this isn't a forward. It's not broken, it's just noise — a bigger version of the same tint email with more stuff bolted on.

## What's working

- The bronzer tint hero shot with the swatched cheek and "Only $6!" price tag is legible and the offer is instantly clear — no fine print hunting.
- "HOT AS e.l.f. THIS WEEK" product grid photography feels current and UGC-ish (the striped-towel flatlays, the mirror selfie with the mist), consistent with what's been working for e.l.f. all week.
- Free shipping, "Beauty Squad" rewards, and TikTok Shop are all visible in the footer band — e.l.f. knows I'm price- and points-motivated even if it's buried down there.
- Cruelty-free/vegan badges are front and center right before the sign-off, which matters to me as a values checkbox even if it's not my #1 driver.

## What's weak

- This is the same Sheer For It Bronzer Tint I already got pitched on 8/31 — one day, zero new information, just wrapped in a bigger catalog. That's lazy sequencing, not a fresh beat.
- Zero loyalty math anywhere near the top — no Beauty Squad points callout on the bronzer, no "member price," nothing tying this to tier status the way Sephora's Rouge program constantly nudges me. I'm doing points math in my head for every purchase and e.l.f. gave me nothing to calculate.
- The six gray category tiles (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) are just a site nav pasted into an email — pure filler that pushes the one real product further down.
- "Bestie" as the opener is doing a lot of work to feel personal but there's no actual personalization — no skin tone match, no "you bought X, try Y," nothing that says e.l.f. knows me specifically.
- This is basically an Ulta-style "everything at once" send — Ulta's 20%-off-one-item emails at least anchor on a single coupon code even when they're cluttered; this doesn't even have that anchor, it's scattered across four sections before you land on the one live offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the repeat — if the bronzer tint ran 8/31, either give it a genuinely new angle (a shade match quiz, a UGC video embed) or swap the hero to something I haven't seen, because back-to-back identical heroes read as autopilot.
- 2. Cut the send in half — the lip-combo tutorial and the six-tile category nav should be their own emails or moved to a "shop more" footer link, not stacked into the same scroll as the actual offer.
- 3. Add a Beauty Squad points line directly under the $6 price ("+X points with Beauty Squad") — I'm doing this math anyway, do it for me and I'll trust the email more.
- 4. Move "free shipping, limited time only!" up near the hero CTA instead of burying it in the footer icon row — that's a real lever for a $6 impulse buy.
