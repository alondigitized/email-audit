# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same fake-cart email again, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. "We noticed you noticing us… your cart is still waiting" is almost word for word the 10/23 and 10/30 "Your Items Are Waiting!" sends, and the 10/31 "Don't leave these styles behind!" email landed the same day. That's at least twelve of these in about two weeks. The Day of the Dead UNO skull sneaker is a fun seasonal hero, but it's one random shoe I never looked at. The 20% off Skechers Plus banner is the only real offer, and it's buried below six sneakers.
- I'm Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, so I do loyalty math constantly. Skechers Plus at 20% is a decent member perk. The email never says what Plus costs or what it earns, and the fine print says I have to be logged in to qualify. Sephora would open with a points multiplier or a clear event tier. Ulta would have put the dollar-off math above the fold. I'd mark this as read and move on. I'd forward it to nobody.

## What's working

- The skull UNO hero is a bold seasonal image. It's the only thing in the email with personality.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is a clear navy button directly under the hero shoe.
- The 20% off block is high-contrast, and the "Valid thru 11/2" deadline feels credible.
- The six-shoe grid is clean and labeled, with a mix of slip-ins and lifestyle sneakers.
- Afterpay and Klarna are visible, and that matters when I'm weighing a pair.

## What's weak

- "Your cart is still waiting" is a lie, and I've said so for two weeks. It makes me trust the brand less.
- The 20% off is members only (Skechers Plus), hidden under the product grid and gated by fine print.
- No price appears anywhere in the email. The skull shoe, the grid and the 20% math all have no dollar figure.
- The footer is long: texting, app, curbside, BNPL and a store finder. It reads like a drugstore circular.
- Every send is the same layout with a swapped hero shoe. It doesn't feel like a brand that knows me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the fake-cart framing unless there's a real cart. Lead with the 20% Plus offer and an honest reason, like the Halloween "No Tricks, Just Treats" theme.
- 2. Move the 20% banner above the product grid and add a "Join Skechers Plus" CTA for non-members, with the cost shown.
- 3. Show prices and a "was/now" figure on the hero shoe so I can do the math.
- 4. Pause these sends for subscribers who haven't added to cart. Cap them at one a week.
- 5. Trim the footer to two or three modules.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends Sunday for Skechers Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The Alma De Guerrero UNO: spooky good, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% online through 11/2. Not a member yet? Join free.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Day of the Dead UNO plus 6 best sellers, all eligible.`

The automated QA run found a failing unsubscribe link probe, no plain-text part, and 12 images served over `http://`. Authentication and list-unsubscribe headers could not be checked because the AgentMail relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

A 400 may be an artifact of the probe, since the URL carries a per-recipient token and an `attn_sid`, but it can't be confirmed from this data. Verify it manually.
- **Two CTAs returned 429 (WARN), so they are unverified rather than broken:**

  - "We noticed you noticing us... Your cart is still waiting..." (`skechers.e.attentivemail.com/e/c/Q1urwf-q…`)
- **Wrapped links hide the destinations.** All links are Attentive click-tracking redirects (`/e/c/<opaque token>`), including the "web version" link. Destination URLs can't be read from the source.
- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 32 skipped, so most links are untested.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
