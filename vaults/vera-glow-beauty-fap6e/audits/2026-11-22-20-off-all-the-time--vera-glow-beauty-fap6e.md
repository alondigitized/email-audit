# 20% off—all. the. time.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is on the 25/20/30 number shuffle again, and I'm doing the points math again. The banner says "unlock 25% off sitewide* | join VIP for free." The hero says "20% Off now + forever." The copy under it says 20% only applies if I start a subscription. So the headline number is 20%, the banner number is 25%, and the real deal is a subscription. I've now seen this same confusion on 11/20, and twice on 11/21 ("All the complexion + up to 25% off" and "Choose your shade of 30% off"). It's a tired loop.
- The skincare is pretty, and the sensitive-skin lineup is a good edit. But I came in for Black Friday math, not a subscription pitch. Sephora's Cyber Week email at least tells me what I'm getting today. Ulta's 50/40/30 ladder was clearer too. I'd skim this, maybe screenshot the free complexion stick for my cart, and not forward it.
- I would click, but only to check whether the free gift stacks with the VIP 25%. The email doesn't tell me, so I'd have to leave the inbox to find out.

## What's working

- The hero is huge, the 20% reads in half a second, and the Base Face Milk bottle is beautiful.
- The "free gift edit" is the strongest part. A free complexion stick with any order and a free mini of choice at $100+ are real threshold math I can plan around.
- The models have a range of skin tones, which suits shade-match shoppers like me.
- The footer trust cues are good: Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Personalized Shade Match, and Join ILIA VIP.
- The "Shop to claim" CTA in a dark red block is easy to spot and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The banner says 25%, the hero says 20%, and the body says 20% only with a subscription. I can't tell which number applies to me.
- The asterisk on the offer plus "*See site for details" buries the conditions. It doesn't feel fully honest.
- Subscribe + save forever is a bigger commitment than a one-off Black Friday haul. The subscription is the CTA, but I came for the sale.
- The product blocks have no prices, reviews, or shade info. "Shop now" on every block is generic.
- The VIP tier gets one banner mention and one footer icon. As a points person, I want to see the benefit and the math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one number and one condition, like "20% off every subscription order" or "25% for VIP," not both at the same time.
- 2. Say whether the free gifts stack with the VIP discount.
- 3. Add prices, star ratings, and a "best seller" tag to each product block. Make the CTAs specific, like "Shop Base Face Milk."
- 4. Show VIP benefits in a real block with a tier-style breakdown. Points people want to see it.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-built HTML is structurally sound, with no QA errors and no broken links reported. Three warnings remain: two missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable unsubscribe and authentication headers. The source I received was truncated, so body-level checks (footer, UTMs, merge tokens) are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were flagged. 12 material links were probed and 13 were skipped. The skipped ones are footer, utility and social links, which are deprioritized by design.
- Click and open tracking runs through the brand-owned domain `trk.send.iliabeauty.com`. The open pixel is `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M81ZQY85EV5M4JYBW6J9EZS1`. This is normal.
- The 13 skipped links were not verified, so their status is unknown.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so screen readers get nothing and image-blocking clients show a blank slot. It needs descriptive alt text.
  - The open-tracking pixel at `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/...`. It should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so it is treated as decorative.
- **Empty `<title>`:** the source has `<title> </title>`. This is minor, but a few clients and web-view versions use it.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Web fonts via `@import`:** the email imports `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css` and the Google Fonts Montserrat stylesheet. Gmail and several other clients ignore or strip `@import`. The stack `"Montserrat","Helvetica",sans-serif` falls back acceptably, so this is low severity. Headings are hard-coded to `Arial`, which is inconsistent with the body font stack but harmless.
- Outlook handling is present (`o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, `mj-outlook-group-fix`, `mso-table-lspace`). The preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`). No issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`) appear in the visible source. The remainder was truncated, so this is not exhaustive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact. This is unconfirmed, not a proven sender defect. Check the raw message headers from an original delivery before treating it as a finding. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this volume.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The tool attributes this to the relay stripping it, so it can't be assessed from this capture.
- The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) fell outside the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but its result isn't shown. I can't confirm the CAN-SPAM footer elements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. No destination URLs or query strings are visible in the truncated source, and the QA output reports no landing-page results. Links appear to be wrapped by Klaviyo's tracking domain, so UTMs would be applied at redirect time. A resolved-URL capture would be needed to verify them.

1. Add descriptive `alt` to the `c9cefca7-…jpeg` image and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
2. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and a meaningful `<title>`.
3. Re-run header QA on a direct-delivery copy, not the AgentMail relay. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment).
4. Capture the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and the 13 skipped links. Follow the redirects to verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
