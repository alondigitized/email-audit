# Your free Complexion Stick is waiting 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh ILIA email in about four days, and I'm doing the math again. The subject gives me a free $50-value Complexion Stick with any order, but the top banner says "25% off sitewide, join VIP for free" and the body says "save 20% sitewide." Two sitewide numbers in one email is the same problem I flagged on "20% off Skin Tint SPF 40" (11/25), and it still isn't fixed. After "New. Gifts. Added. (Up to $120 value)" yesterday, this feels like a re-skin of the same stack.
- The email itself is well built. The shade-range close-up, the "42 shades / buildable medium coverage / weightless 12-hour wear" checklist, and the real reviews sit above the sale. That's the product-first order I like, and it beats Ulta's recycled $10-off-$50 coupon. It's still a lot of scroll for "free with any order."
- I'd click, because a free $50 stick with no threshold is a better hook than Glossier's free candle at $100+. I wouldn't forward it. It's good, but it's the fourth version of the same offer.

## What's working

- "Free w/ any order" has no spend threshold. Glossier's You Candle needs $100+, and that's a real edge for me.
- The "$50 value" badge on the stick makes the value easy to price out.
- The "the look + feel" block lists 42 shades, medium coverage, natural-matte finish and 12-hour wear. That's the shade and finish detail I actually shop on.
- Two real reviews with a swatch beside them give me proof without leaving the email. Sephora's trio emails rarely do this.
- The VIP perks block lists free shipping, points on every purchase, and early access to launches and sales. It's useful tier-math content for a Rouge/Diamond shopper like me.

## What's weak

- The 25% in the banner and the 20% in the body copy contradict each other. The 25% is probably the VIP rate, but nothing explains that.
- Three different discount stories compete: free stick, 20/25% sitewide, and "up to 40% off" on the red tile. Sephora leads with one deal. e.l.f. leads with one gift.
- There's no deadline anywhere. For a Black Friday send, "waiting" is soft urgency, and there's no end date.
- The hero says "The Lineup," not the free stick. The offer is in a tiny pink circle and a thin banner, and I have to squint at the asterisks.
- "Sale's on sale" is a cutesy CTA that tells me nothing about what I'm clicking. The up-to-40% tile also doesn't say which items are included.
- The footer text is tiny, and the asterisk fine print is nearly unreadable on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline offer, the free Complexion Stick. Fold the VIP 25% into one line: "25% for VIP, 20% for everyone."
- 2. Move the "free w/ any order" badge into the hero headline and add an end date. Drop "The Lineup."
- 3. Add a points line for members, like "Earn 2x points on top of the freebie." Sephora does this and I always notice it.
- 4. Rename "Sale's on sale" to something like "Shop up to 40% off," and name the categories in the tile.
- 5. Show a shade-finder or skin-tone selector near the top. Personalization is the real way to lift this send.
