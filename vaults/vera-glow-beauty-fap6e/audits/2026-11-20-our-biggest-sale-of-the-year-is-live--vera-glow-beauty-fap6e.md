# Our biggest sale of the year is live!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was "wait, didn't I just get this?" Yesterday's "Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING" got a 7 from me because it finally had a real number. Today's send is the same 25% sale with a fragrance skin on it. It's the fourth Glossier email in four days, after 11/17's balm send, 11/18's member push and 11/19's early-access email. The subject promises a "biggest sale of the year," and I'm doing the points math on a brand that has no points. I can see the discount, but nothing in it rewards me for being loyal.
- The email is also mostly a fragrance catalog. Four Glossier You flavors get big photos, then the 100ml sizes, then a body butter gift-with-purchase. If I'm a fragrance person, it's decent. If I came for the Balm Dotcom or skincare staples I stock up on, there's nothing here. I'd click once to check the fragrance price, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Enjoy 25% off everything*" in big black type next to a blue Shop now button reads in half a second.
- The product photography is gorgeous, especially the blue Glossier You Soie shot in water. It feels like Glossier and not a promo template.
- Each fragrance has a scent line like "Soft, warm + familiar" or "Creamy, sensual + beachy." That works as a mini product description and lets me pick without clicking.
- The free Crème de You with a $100 spend gives me a threshold to build toward. That's the same basket-building math I do with Sephora's Beauty Insider events.
- The models and bottle styling look diverse and current, and the vibe fits a 29-year-old.

## What's weak

- Render problems are visible all over this email:
- The top banner is cut off ("Get a free Crème de You when you spend $100").
- The hero badge is clipped to "EVERYTHIN."
- The "Black Friday Sale" marquee runs off the edge.
- The bottom headline is truncated to "Get a free Crème de You w…"
- On a $100+ gift-with-purchase offer, that's sloppy.
- The offer is contradictory. The headline says 25%, one hero badge says 30% off, and a section header says "Extra Savings — 30% Off." The asterisks and a tiny "Terms apply" make me wonder what the real number is.
- Every product card has a full-width blue Shop now button. With eight near-identical buttons, no single one stands out.
- There's no deadline anywhere. "Biggest sale of the year is live" with no end date kills urgency.
- Nothing is personalized. It doesn't say whether I'm a member or what I've browsed, and there's no skincare or balm even though I just got three balm sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped text and make the offer a single number. Pick 25% or 30%, say what the 30% applies to, and put a real end date next to it.
- 2. Make the first screen show the offer and the gift together: "25% off + free Crème de You at $100, ends Sunday."
- 3. Replace the repeated Shop now buttons with specific ones, like "Shop Glossier You" or "Shop body butter." Cut the grid to one hero product per row.
- 4. Segment by what I've bought. I'm a balm buyer, so lead with Balm Dotcom and skincare, and keep the fragrance grid for people who've browsed it.
- 5. Show members something. Glossier's account-creation push on 11/18 was about member perks, so this email should show early access or extra savings for members.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Glossier gift is waiting (+25% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Crème de You at $100, plus 30% off select fragrance. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up on Balm Dotcom and Glossier You before it's gone.`
- Compared with competitors:
- e.l.f.'s "$10 reward" email put its hook in the hero and got an 8 from me. Glossier makes me read two banners to piece together its gift.
- Drunk Elephant buried "30% off" below the fold, and Glossier at least leads with the number. Then it undercuts that by showing a second number in a badge.
- ILIA's plain-text Black Friday note had fewer render problems and a clearer ask.
- Sephora would have put Rouge-tier early access up top.
