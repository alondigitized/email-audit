## What's working

- The split-face hero with two different skin tones under one spotlight is genuinely well art-directed — better than anything Sally Beauty or e.l.f. SKIN has put in front of me this week.

- The ingredient callouts (hyaluronic acid, allantoin, niacinamide, squalane) map directly to "comforts sensitive skin" — that's the personalization-by-skin-type signal I'm always looking for and rarely get.
- "1% for the Planet" tree-planting tie-in is a differentiated trust cue — Glossier's Virgo Season send had zero substance by comparison.
- Clean layout, generous whitespace, easy to read without zooming on mobile.

## What's weak

- Zero offer, discount, or urgency anywhere — for a Rouge/Diamond shopper who's always doing the points math, this is a pure content play with no reason to buy today.
- No mention of my ILIA VIP status or any loyalty perk, even though the footer literally has a "Join ILIA VIP" icon sitting right there unused in the body.
- Shade-matching is nowhere despite "Personalized Shade Match" being listed as a footer icon — a huge miss for a tinted product where fit/shade is my #1 purchase blocker.
- Multiple CTAs ("Shop Skin Tint," "Learn More," "Shop Now") all funnel to generic product/brand pages, not a specific shade finder or my actual shade.

- 2. Surface the shade-match tool inline, not buried as a tiny footer icon — put a "find your shade" CTA right under the clinical stats block.
- 3. Call out my VIP tier status by name in the body ("Rouge members get early access...") — right now this treats me like a stranger, not a repeat Sephora/Ulta cross-shopper.
