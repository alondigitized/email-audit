# Safe for sensitive eyes
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "easy on the eyes" is a genuinely good pun and the flat-lay hero — mascara, eyeliner, and shadow stick fanned out on that burgundy plate with the little gold charm — is easily the best styled shot ILIA's sent me this week, better than the "Art of the Eye" mirrored-tray shot from 9/16. But then I hit "JOIN VIP + SHOP" and I'm annoyed all over again, because this is the fourth email in five days gating the 20% off behind a VIP signup I probably already have, same as 9/18's "latest + greatest + 20% off." The eye-safety/ophthalmologist-tested angle is legitimately good copy for someone who wears contacts like me, and I do like that they showed actual awards (Allure) on two products instead of just claiming things. I'd scroll for the gifts-at-checkout section, but I'm marking this read, not clicking — I've seen this VIP ask too many times this week to bite again.

## What's working

- The flat-lay hero shot with the plate, charm, and three products fanned out is the most editorial visual ILIA's sent me all month — it actually stopped my scroll.
- "Safe for sensitive eyes + ophthalmologist-tested" is real, relevant copy for a contact-lens wearer like me — not just vague clean-beauty language.
- Allure badges next to the mascara and shadow stick are actual third-party proof, not just ILIA patting itself on the back.
- Gifts-at-checkout section (free multi-stick on all orders, free velvet bag on $100+) is a concrete, additive offer stacked on top of the 20%.

## What's weak

- The 20% off is locked behind "JOIN VIP" again — this is the fourth VIP gate in five sends (9/14, 9/17, 9/18, now this), and I'm Rouge/Diamond tier everywhere else, so being treated like a stranger who needs to "join" is grating.
- No shade-match or skin-tone personalization anywhere, despite this being a whole "clinically tested" product story — Sephora's fragrance push at least segments by category interest.
- Three product call-outs (mascara, shadow stick, eyeliner) but only one shared CTA button — no per-product "shop now" links, so I can't jump straight to the eyeliner I actually want.
- "Gifts at checkout" graphics look like sticky-note Polaroids, which is a fun style choice but the free-multi-stick photo is small enough I have to squint on my phone to tell what it even is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the VIP gate on the headline offer — I'm already enrolled everywhere, and gating the same 20% for the fourth time this week reads as ILIA not tracking who's already a member, which is worse than Glossier's restraint-driven "Boy Brow just won an award" approach that skips gimmicks entirely.
- 2. Add individual "Shop [product]" links under each of the three eye products instead of one generic CTA — right now the click path forces me to the whole eye collection instead of the specific eyeliner I want.
- 3. Make the Allure badge do double duty — call it out in the subject or preheader since award-backed proof is rarer than generic VIP pushes and performed well for ILIA on 9/17's "Win-win-win."
- 4. Bump the gift-at-checkout images bigger or crop tighter — right now the velvet bag and multi-stick photos are too small to register as an incentive at a glance.
