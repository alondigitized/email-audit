# National Watermelon Day just got juicier 🍉💋 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- Fifteen-plus days into this streak and e.l.f. found a National Watermelon Day tie-in for... a mango-and-watermelon-scented lip balm duo. I'll give them credit, it's a genuinely cute hero — the yellow and pink Squeeze Me More tubes nestled against real watermelon and mango slices is the best food-styling shot they've run all month, and it actually smells relevant to the holiday instead of being a stretch. But c'mon, this is a $9 lip balm markdown to $8, not an event, and I'm now numb to the "why you'll e.l.f.ing love it" template because I've scrolled past this exact bullet-point-with-emoji layout for Glow Reviver, for the bronzers, for basically everything since July.
- Nothing here talks tier math, nothing references Beauty Squad points, and the "Top Picks for You" carousel is doing the personalization work while the rest of the email is just seasonal wallpaper. Compare that to Sephora's actual Beauty Insider Celebration send from 8/1 with the gold "STARTS 8/1-8/31" badge — that felt like a real event I'm invited to. This is e.l.f. finding a national day to hang a $1-off promo on.
- I'm not clicking. I've already got two other e.l.f. lip products from this same streak sitting unused, and $1 off a $9 balm isn't urgency, it's noise. Not forwarding this — my friends would just ask why e.l.f. is still in my inbox every single day.

## What's working

- The hero product shot against real mango and watermelon is genuinely well-styled — best food tie-in visual they've run this stretch.
- The offer is stated cleanly right under the headline: "$9 $8" with a strikethrough, no hunting for the discount.
- "Top Picks for You" carrying three cross-sell SKUs with prices and Shop Now buttons is a nice bit of segmentation, more than most of their recent sends bothered with.
- Cruelty-free/vegan badges near the bottom are a trust cue I actually register as a Sephora/Ulta shopper who cares about that stuff.

## What's weak

- A $1 discount dressed up as a "National Watermelon Day" event is thin — Sephora's actual Beauty Insider Celebration this week makes this look like a rounding error by comparison.
- Zero loyalty language — no Beauty Squad points call-out, nothing tying this to my account, unlike Ulta which at least personalizes with "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" style copy.
- This is day fifteen-plus of e.l.f. in my inbox and the "WHY YOU'LL E.L.F.ING LOVE IT" bullet template is now recycled wallpaper — I've seen this exact structure for the Glow Reviver and bronzer sends.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — "limited time" free shipping in the footer is the only time-bound language in the whole email, and it's buried below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Attach an actual loyalty hook — "Beauty Squad members earn 2x points today" would do more for me than the watermelon framing ever could.
- 2. Cut the discount claim or make it real — $1 off reads as an afterthought; either bundle it with a GWP or drop the "juicier" framing since the offer doesn't back it up.
- 3. Retire the "WHY YOU'LL E.L.F.ING LOVE IT" bullet block for a few sends — I've seen it enough times this month that it's starting to blur every product together.
- 4. Give this a deadline. A "today only" or "through Sunday" badge would create the urgency the holiday tie-in is trying to borrow.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Beauty Squad watermelon treat is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mango + watermelon lip balm, now $8 — Squad members earn double points`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics — "National Watermelon Day" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses a Braze-hosted click-tracking redirector (`links.elfcosmetics.com`) with standard MSO/Outlook conditional markup, but 4 of 12 probed CTAs resolve to malformed/relative destination URLs and the message ships with no plain-text part, a missing `List-Unsubscribe` header pair, and one untagged image.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 4 of 12 probed links fail resolution with `unknown url type` errors — the tracking redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) unwraps to a **relative path with no scheme/host**, not an absolute URL:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (CTA: "Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=...&utm_campaign=080326_national_watermelon_day&lid=...&source_caller=ui&af_dp` (CTA: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")

  This indicates the redirect target was saved without a base domain (likely a template merge/export error at the ESP), not just a QA-tool limitation — all four are high-intent CTAs (product PDP and account/app links), so this is a functional breakage risk, not cosmetic.
- All sampled links route through the Braze click-tracking domain (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` for clicks, `/q/...` for the tracking pixel) — tracking wrapper itself is present and consistent.
- 26 of 38 total links were not probed (footer/utility/social, deprioritized by design) — no data on those.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — either missing at send time or not captured by the relay; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support without header data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.

- Note: these are flagged as **WARN**, not confirmed failures, since the relay may simply not be capturing/forwarding these headers. Recommend verifying directly against raw SMTP headers at the sending MTA rather than relying on the relay capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Only one probed link carries explicit UTM parameters: the app-download CTA includes `utm_campaign=080326_national_watermelon_day` plus Branch/AppsFlyer params (`bxid`, `lid`, `af_dp`, `source_caller=ui`) — consistent with a deep-link/app-install flow.
- The two product CTAs ("Shop Now" ×2) and the account/rewards CTA carry **no UTM parameters** in their resolved destination — none of `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` present. This breaks campaign attribution continuity for those clicks even setting aside the relative-URL resolution issue above.
- Cannot confirm landing-page alignment (PDP content matching promoted products, correct size/variant preselection via `Size=` query param) since destinations don't resolve to absolute URLs in current QA data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect target generation for the 4 failing links so they unwrap to absolute URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) instead of relative paths — verify at the ESP template/export step before next send.
2. Add UTM parameters to the two product CTAs and the account/rewards CTA for attribution parity with the app-download link.
