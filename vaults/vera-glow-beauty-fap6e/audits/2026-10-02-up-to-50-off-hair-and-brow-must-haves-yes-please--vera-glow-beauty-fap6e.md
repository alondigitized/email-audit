# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: this is the third Sephora send I've gotten in two days, and it's trying to be four emails at once. The top is National Brow Day, up to 50% off select brow. Then it flips to "October means new rewards!" and a Rewards Bazaar. Then a 2X/5X points code. Then 20% off value-size hair care and "Up to 50% off Hair Faves." I opened it because of the 50%, but I scrolled a long way to find the hair part the subject promised.
- The brow deal is basically this morning's "50% OFF select brow products 🤨" send with a new coat of paint. The Rewards Bazaar is the part I care about. Three 100-point rewards for 250 points is real points math, and I'll do it. Still, it sits in the middle of a sale email and fights the sale for attention. Drunk Elephant gave me one idea in its foundation send, and Glossier at least stuck to one GWP, even if it ran it three times. Sephora gave me five.
- Would I click? Yes, the Rewards Bazaar or the brow deal, probably brow. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the 2X Insider event math to line up.

## What's working

- The 50% off brow banner is at the very top, so the offer shows before any scrolling. Ulta would have buried it under a coupon-code header.
- The Rewards Bazaar shows exact point costs (100 and 250) next to named products: Rhode, Dieux, Anastasia Beverly Hills, Jo Malone, Dior, Gisou, Color Wow. That's the points math I want.
- "Today only" and "Limited time only" tags on the sale blocks make the urgency credible.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIP / Rouge) near the bottom reminds me I'm Rouge. Ulta rarely does that so cleanly.
- It's a recognizable Sephora layout with clear product shots. It looks trustworthy, not chaotic.

## What's weak

- The subject says hair and brow, but the email opens with brow, then rewards, then points, then hair. The hair block with "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" is near the bottom.
- There are too many SHOP NOW buttons (brow, Rewards Bazaar, holiday drops, hair, value-size). No single hero.
- The repeated "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" marquee strips around the rewards banner are loud and repetitive.
- The 2X / 5X points block is small, with a code (SAYPOINTS) and a deadline of 10/7. After the 2X "STARTS TODAY" send yesterday, it feels like an afterthought.
- Fine print is long and dense at the bottom, and the offers are "select" and code-dependent. Honest, but not friendly.
- It's the third near-identical Sephora promo in two days, after the two 10/2 brow sends and the 10/1 2X points send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either do the brow and hair sale as the hero, or send the Rewards Bazaar on its own. Mixing them dilutes both.
- 2. Move the 2X / 5X points block up near the top, with a code and a deadline, and cut the rewards marquee strips.
- 3. Lead with hair if the subject says hair. Split the layout 50/50, brow and hair, above the fold.
- 4. Use CTAs that name the category: "Shop brow deals" and "Shop hair deals" instead of five SHOP NOWs.
- 5. Put the Rouge-tier benefit line up top, for example "Rouge: earn 2X with SAYPOINTS."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brow Day: up to 50% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hair + brow deals up to 50% off 💗`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 3 new 100-point rewards for just 250 points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code SAYPOINTS = 2X points through 10/7.`

The HTML source is truncated, so body-level checks (links, footer, tokens) rest mostly on the QA data.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed only 12 of 58 links (46 skipped, mostly footer, utility and social), so coverage of the remaining links is unverified.
- Tracking and image hosts are `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (an opaque encoded path) and `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/...`. Both are third-party or ESP-controlled. No defect is evident, but the unlabeled `/O/` image is probably an open-tracking pixel.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images** (QA):
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` appears to be a navigation header image. Without alt text, the nav is unreadable with images blocked, and screen readers announce the filename.
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is likely a tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so it isn't announced.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients. This conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` has a space. The valid token is `noindex`, so the directive is likely ignored. This is minor, since it only matters for web-view copies.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Sephora` doesn't match the subject or content. This is low impact for email.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 0 chars, so there is no `text/plain` alternative. HTML-only mail is an accessibility and deliverability weakness: spam filters penalize it, and text-only clients and some assistive setups get nothing.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The truncated source can't rule out tokens further down.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes AgentMail may strip these in the relay, so this is **unverified, not confirmed absent**. If they really are missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (≥5k/day) require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` can't be assessed from this relay. Verify against a direct-delivery copy.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reported no failure, so no violation is confirmed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no CTA `href` values or UTM parameters, and QA reported no landing-page mismatches among the 12 probed links.

1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** This is the only hard failure.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (the nav labels). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.

4. **Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`**, or drop it.
5. **Re-audit with raw headers** from a direct-delivery copy to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
