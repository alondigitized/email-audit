# It’s Hispanic Heritage Month—let’s give these brands their flowers 🌺🌼🌸
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a genuinely different beat from the usual Sephora new-launch dump — "Unidos, Somos Más" leading into a founder-by-founder Hispanic Heritage Month spotlight (AORA Mexico, Touchland, Ceremonia, Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez) is a real curation angle, not just inventory dressed up in a headline. Compare that to the 9/25 "New beauty to obsess over 😍" send that was Byredo/Dior/Innisfree/M∙A∙C all crammed into one scroll with zero narrative thread — this one at least has a reason to exist beyond "buy stuff."
- That said, it's buried under a hair-care banner at the very top ("Up to 30% off Hair Faves") that has nothing to do with the theme, and the heritage-month content doesn't kick in until I've scrolled past that. And by the time I hit "Founders Face to Face," a $32 lip balm, and the credit card pitch, the focus has splintered into the same everything-bagel Sephora always sends — hair deals, a random new launch (Deji Skin), a store card offer, Beauty Insider tier bars. It's Sephora doing the thing Ulta does with "20% off one item, plus fragrance, plus terms apply" — cramming five campaigns into one email until nothing is the hero.
- I'd scroll through this one instead of instant-archiving, mostly because Rare Beauty is an actual purchase trigger for me and the founder quotes add some texture I don't usually get from this list. Would I click? Probably on the Rare Beauty block. Would I forward it? Not to a friend — the Hispanic Heritage Month framing is nice but doesn't feel personalized to me as a Rouge member, and it's diluted by the bolt-on offers at top and bottom.

## What's working

- The "Unidos, Somos Más" hero with founder photos and direct quotes ("Rodrigo Peñafiel and Nara Tayares, Founders") feels like actual brand storytelling, not just a product wall.
- Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez appearing twice (Eau de Parfum + Beauty Eau de Parfum) is a real draw for me — I stock up on Rare Beauty at Sephora events specifically.
- "Founders Face to Face" video block with Dez Perkins and Denise Vasi is a nice touch — social proof from real founders beats generic UGC.
- Clean section dividers (pink/teal/orange color blocking) made each brand easy to visually separate while scrolling.

## What's weak

- The hair-care banner at the very top has nothing to do with the Hispanic Heritage Month theme — it undercuts the message before I even get to it, unlike Sephora's Hair Daily Deals send from 9/23 which at least committed fully to one mechanic.
- No mention of my Rouge tier or points anywhere — not even in the "We Belong to Something Beautiful" Beauty Insider footer bar, which just shows generic tier icons with no personalization to my status.
- Bottom of the email pivots hard into unrelated stuff — Deji Skin launch, a Sephora Credit Card pitch for 25% off — that has nothing to do with the heritage theme and just feels like Sephora tacking on every active campaign.
- No concrete percentage or dollar offer tied to the heritage-month brands themselves — it's "shop and support" with no incentive, while the hair banner up top gets the only real discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a dedicated Hispanic Heritage Month spotlight and a separate hair-care deals email. Right now the theme gets diluted by unrelated offers stacked above and below it, and that's the single biggest thing dragging this down.
- 2. Add a small "for you" personalization line near the top — even just "As a Rouge member, here's early access to shop these founders" — since none of the tier math I obsess over shows up anywhere.
- 3. Pair each featured brand with an actual incentive (bonus points, GWP, or % off) instead of just "shop and support" — Ulta's 20%-off mechanic is tired but it at least gives me a reason to click today.
- 4. Move "Founders Face to Face" video higher, right under the hero — it's the most differentiated content in the email and it's currently buried past four brand blocks.
- 5. Cut the Deji Skin and credit-card blocks entirely from this send, or move them to a separate email — they break the emotional thread the first two-thirds built.
