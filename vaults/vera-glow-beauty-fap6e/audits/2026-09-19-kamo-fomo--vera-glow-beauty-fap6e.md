# Kamo FOMO?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, no DRUNKVIP20 again — that's a real streak now, and instead they're actually launching something: Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops, a concealer/foundation/tinted-moisturizer/firming-drop hybrid in 25 shades. That's a legit hero — one product, one clear job, explained without a wall of jargon, and the swatch-on-under-eye demo shot does more work than any subscription banner could. My eye landed on the green bottle first, which is exactly where it should land.
- But then I hit the top banner — "sign up for two or more subscriptions and save 15%" — before I even get to see what the product is, and that's such a backwards way to open. And the whole email is basically the same "SHOP KAMO" CTA five times in a row with different backdrops, which starts to feel like padding once you scroll past the third one. No points, no Rouge/Diamond callout, nothing that says "hey Vera, we know you." Compare that to ILIA's "Safe for sensitive eyes" flat-lay, which felt genuinely styled for someone like me — this is fine product photography stretched across way too much scroll.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — new-launch concealer-foundation hybrid with 25 shades is a real category I care about, and it beats the sixth "secret sale" rerun hands down. Forward to a friend? Maybe the second image (the swatch demo) if I'm debating the shade range with someone. Overall: open, skim to the shade/coverage claim, click once, move on.

## What's working

- "TRIED KAMO YET?" is a real launch hook, not a discount wink — first genuinely new SKU energy I've seen from this brand in weeks.
- 25-shade, 12-hour, "undetectable" coverage claim is specific enough to make me want to check my shade before I even click through.
- The under-eye swatch photo on the model is the strongest visual in the email — it shows exactly how sheer/buildable it looks on skin, not just in the bottle.
- "DRUNK TIP: PLAY AROUND!" mixing instructions (with moisturizer, oil, serum) is useful product education, not filler copy.

## What's weak

- Leading with a subscription-discount banner above the actual product reveal buries the hero — I have to scroll past sales mechanics to find out what I'm even being sold.
- Five identical "SHOP KAMO" buttons in a row with no variation in messaging makes the back half of the email feel like a scroll-tax, not new information.
- Zero loyalty/tier signal — no Rouge or Diamond callout, no "as a member you get X," which stings given how hard I track points math everywhere else.
- The "Fall Skin, Sorted" $100+ deluxe-sample block at the bottom is a totally different offer competing for attention right as I'm losing steam — should've been its own send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the subscription banner from the top entirely (or move it to the bottom near the $100+ gift block) so "TRIED KAMO YET?" is the very first thing I see — that's the actual scroll-stopper.
- 2. Consolidate the five "SHOP KAMO" CTAs down to two — one after the hero, one after the swatch demo — so the email doesn't feel like it's padding for length.
- 3. Add a small "Kamo just dropped — be first" or early-access line for repeat customers/subscribers, since Drunk Elephant clearly has my purchase history and isn't using it.
- 4. Split "Fall Skin, Sorted" into its own follow-up email instead of tacking it onto a launch send — it dilutes the Kamo focus.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Email delivers as third-party template code (external font provider, redirect-heavy tracking) with several unresolved compliance/deliverability header gaps and three non-HTTPS image assets.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 15 tracking/click-redirect links present, routed through `everestengagement.com` redirect domain (e.g. `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad...`) — these were skipped by automated HTTP probing, so final destination and redirect health are unverified.
- Open/tracking pixel present: `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoevergreen_p`.
- Recipient email address appears embedded in plaintext within a tracking URL query param (`e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`) — PII exposed in a clickable/loggable URL, a common flag in link-hygiene audits (increases exposure risk if link is forwarded/cached/logged by proxies).

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Three social icon images served over `http://` instead of `https://`: Facebook, Instagram, TikTok (`http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/...png`). Mixed-content images are frequently blocked/flagged by mail clients on HTTPS-rendered pages.
- Two images missing `alt` text: the `o.gif` tracking pixel and the `everestengagement.com` redirect image — the latter is a functional/clickable image, so a missing alt degrades accessibility for screen-reader and images-off users.
- Custom web fonts (`Brown Bold`, `Brown-Light`, `Brown-Thin`) loaded via `@import`/`<link>` from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` plus self-hosted `BrownRegular` from a Salesforce Commerce Cloud (`demandware.static`) path — most email clients strip `@font-face`/external `@import`, so this is a fallback-dependent, non-functional-in-most-clients pattern rather than a hard error.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- Recipient identifier is being passed as a literal email string in a tracking URL parameter (see Link & Tracking Issues) rather than an opaque token/hash — worth flagging as a personalization-implementation concern, not a merge-token failure.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot confirm compliance status from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) header not detected — same relay-capture caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured data — authentication status is unknown, not confirmed failing.
- No visible in-body unsubscribe link/physical address in the truncated HTML segment provided — indeterminate due to truncation, not a confirmed CAN-SPAM violation.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Tracking pixel query params show `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoevergreen_p` — campaign labeling present, but no standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters observed on the sampled links; continuity to landing-page analytics cannot be confirmed from this data.
- All sampled destination links route through the `everestengagement.com` redirector before (presumably) reaching `drunkelephant.com` — final landing URL and parameter passthrough were not probed (skipped per QA), so UTM/landing alignment is unverified.

7. **Recommendations**
- Serve the three social icons over `https://` to prevent client-side blocking/mixed-content warnings.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the tracking pixel (can be empty `alt=""` since it's non-content) and to the `everestengagement.com` linked image (should describe the click target, e.g. "Shop Now").
