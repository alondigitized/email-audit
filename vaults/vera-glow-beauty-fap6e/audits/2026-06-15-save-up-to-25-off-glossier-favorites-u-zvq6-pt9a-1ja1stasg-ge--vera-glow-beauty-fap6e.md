# Save up to 25% off Glossier favorites
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-15**
## Executive summary

- Day six. Or maybe day five-and-a-half, because if I'm counting correctly, Glossier already sent me a sale email **earlier today** — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" — and now here's a second one landing the same afternoon with a nearly identical subject. I genuinely had to double-check my inbox to make sure I wasn't losing my mind. The orange ANNUAL SALE tiles are still doing their job visually — that coral brick pattern is unmistakable brand equity — and the tiered offer (20% sitewide, 25% on orders $100+) is legitimately a good deal. But the emotional goodwill I had on June 11 when I first clicked through? Spent. Glossier has burned through it like a beauty advent calendar on day three.
- The structure is fine: fragrance section, forever favorites, a subscription upsell. Boy Brow at $18 is always going to get my attention. But I've seen this exact layout, these exact products, in these exact positions for five days running. The DON'T MISS OUT header is especially hollow when I've been told not to miss out every single day this week.
- I'd mark this as read. I would not click. I would absolutely not forward it — I already sent a friend the June 11 email and that's the one she needed to know about.

## What's working

- **Tiered offer is smart and visible** — "20% off sitewide or 25% off $100+" is laid out clearly in the hero text, no fine print hunting required.
- **Boy Brow at $18 with a price callout** stops me mid-scroll; it's the kind of specific, low-friction staple that converts.
- **The orange ANNUAL SALE hero is still legible** — the tiled repeating text is chaotic but the brand is unmistakable; no render bugs, no broken images.
- **Subscription upsell is a genuinely differentiated ask** — "lock in 25% off forever" is a smarter CTA than "shop now" and should've been the hero.
- **Fragrance section featuring Glossier You is well-matched** to someone who already buys that SKU and watches for fragrance sale windows.

## What's weak

- **Second email in a single day with almost identical subject lines** — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance" this morning, "Save up to 25% off Glossier favorites" this afternoon. That's not a segmentation strategy, that's a scheduling error that makes me distrust both sends.
- **"DON'T MISS OUT" as the hero headline is pure filler** — after five days of urgent-sounding copy, this reads as brand static.
- **No deadline anywhere in the visible email** — "this offer won't last forever" is not a deadline; Charlotte Tilbury managed to put an actual end date in her June 14 send and it felt more credible than this.
- **"Shop now" CTAs across every section** — four or five of them, zero differentiation. ILIA put a specific product name in their CTA and it made me feel like they knew what I'd click; this feels like a homepage redirect farm.
- **No loyalty signal for a known Glossier customer** — I've spent real money here. The email treats me like a cold prospect.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the double-send on the same calendar day.** One email, one message. Whatever QA process let two near-identical subjects go out June 15 needs to be fixed before next send — this is the single highest-impact fix.
- 2. **Put the subscription upsell front and center as the hero.** "Lock in 25% forever" is a differentiated ask that none of the competitors are running. e.l.f. is sending me BOGO triggers and Sally Beauty is hammering loyalty points — Glossier has a subscription angle that neither of them has. Lead with it.
- 3. **Add a specific deadline.** "Sale ends Sunday, June 16 at 11:59 PM" would do more work than a week's worth of "don't miss out" copy.
- 4. **Consolidate the fragrance and favorites sections into one curated edit.** Five "Shop now" buttons is not hierarchy, it's noise. Pick three products and give each one a reason to exist in this specific email.
- 5. **Acknowledge the cadence fatigue.** Even a single line like "last day to save" would signal self-awareness and make me feel less like I'm in a drip sequence that forgot to stop.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: 25% off Glossier (ends tonight)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Subscribe now, save 25% forever — sale ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25% off $100+ orders — and a reason to subscribe you haven't heard yet.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at sitewide savings before this one's gone.`
