# Loved by you, now 25% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Glossier send in five days, and the first one in a while that put real product in front of me. Reviews for Glossier You, Boy Brow and Lash Slick are my kind of content. But the hero is a red stocking and a heel, which has nothing to do with beauty. Then there's the sticker problem. One circle says "30% OFF $100+" and another says "25% OFF EVERYTHING," both on the same image. I did the points math twice and still couldn't tell which discount I'm getting.
- The 11/21 "A gift for you: Crème de You" email was the same free-gift offer. The 11/20 "Our biggest sale of the year is live!" email was the same 25% sale. Today's copy even says "still on," so the email admits it's a repeat. I'd skim it, enjoy the Boy Brow review, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Believe the Love" review block is the best thing here. Five stars, a real quote from Amber about Glossier You, and the Boy Brow "8 years" line all feel like social proof from an actual person.
- "Get 25% off everything!*" is in plain black type with a blue Shop now button. I get the offer in about a second.
- The blue Black Friday marquee band breaks up the page, and the brand voice is consistent and clearly Glossier.
- The free Crème de You block explains the gift in a clear sentence, with the $100+ threshold stated.

## What's weak

- Two discounts compete. The "30% OFF $100+" and "25% OFF EVERYTHING" stickers sit on the same hero image, and the Crème de You gift is offered on top of them. I can't tell if they stack.
- The hero image is hosiery and heels. It doesn't show a product or my skin, so nothing tells me this is for a beauty shopper.
- The right edge looks cropped in my render. The top banner reads "spend $10…", the "25% OFF EVERYTHING" sticker is clipped, and the Lash Slick quote and the gift headline run off the page. That makes it look broken.
- Both Shop now buttons go to a generic destination. None of the review products has its own link, and I want to tap Boy Brow straight to the product page.
- This is the same 25% sale and the same free gift I've seen all week. There's no new reason to act today, and "very limited time" has no deadline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer for the hero. Make it "25% off everything" and give the gift its own clearly separate section. Remove the 30% sticker or explain how it works.
- 2. Replace the stocking image with a product shot, ideally a best-seller from the review block.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm ET".
- 4. Make each review product a tappable link to its page, with "Shop Boy Brow" style button copy.
- 5. Fix the cropping on the right edge so the banner, stickers and quotes are fully visible.
- 6. Add Glossier Rewards or points info. Sephora and Ulta both show me tier math, and Glossier shows me nothing.

The confirmed issues are six images without `alt` attributes and some invalid markup. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the relay, and all 12 link probes were skipped.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation is incomplete.** The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped all 12, so no link has a confirmed HTTP status or redirect chain. Nothing is confirmed broken, and nothing is confirmed working.
- **Non-standard tracking markup.** The body contains `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M82Q5580B0ZMS728DFQAZSEP_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. It sits ahead of the `<!--<![endif]-->` closer, which also looks misplaced. It is probably a Klaviyo injection artifact. It is harmless in most clients but fails HTML validation.
- **Unbranded tracking domain.** Click and open tracking go through `ctrk.klclick.com` (Klaviyo's shared domain), while the sender is `eml.glossier.com`. A custom branded tracking domain would align link and sender domains. This is an observation from the source, not a confirmed defect.
