# Two gifts are better than one
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Four days ago, ILIA's brown mascara mini landed in my inbox and it genuinely earned a 9/10 from me — differentiated pitch, tight copy, one product to care about. Today's "Two gifts are better than one" feels like ILIA trying to monetize that goodwill with a GWP follow-up, and honestly? The *concept* is smart. "Swipe Twice" as the hero for a mascara duo is clever wordplay and the visual direction stays on-brand: clean, editorial, confident. The problem is that the email immediately undercuts itself with a **"BUILD YOUR FIRST ORDER"** CTA plastered below the hero. I am a repeat ILIA customer. That button copy is acquisition language — it's what you put in a prospecting flow, not in the inbox of someone who's bought from you before. It makes me feel like ILIA doesn't know me at all.
- The second problem is that ILIA is telling three different stories here: the mascara duo GWP, a "Latest + Greatest" multi-product grid, and a separate "The Mini Tote is Back" promo. None of them gets enough room to breathe. Drunk Elephant did "Hot takes" last week with a completely stripped-down email and it *hit* precisely because of what it didn't try to do. This email tries to do too much.
- I'd mark it read, maybe circle back for the mascara duo later — but I wouldn't click through right now, and I'd definitely not forward it.

## What's working

- **"Swipe Twice" hero copy is genuinely good** — layering mascara is a real technique and the dual-swipe framing makes the duo concept land before you read a word of body copy.
- **Following the July 14 mascara launch with a GWP angle is the right cadence move** — give someone a reason to buy after you introduced the product. The sequencing is smart even if execution wobbles.
- **The email renders cleanly** — no broken images, no overlapping text, product photography is crisp. ILIA's visual identity stays coherent from hero to footer.
- **"Mascara Is a Mood" section editorial energy is peak ILIA** — the lash close-up imagery does what an esthetician packing list or a travel flat-lay does: makes me *feel* something before I do math.

## What's weak

- **"BUILD YOUR FIRST ORDER" is the single worst thing in this email** — acquisition CTA copy sent to an active subscriber signals a broken segmentation setup. I felt like I accidentally opened someone else's email.
- **Three competing stories kill the hierarchy** — mascara duo vs. Latest + Greatest catalogue vs. Mini Tote is back. Sephora's GWP emails at least commit to one hero offer and keep the grid below the fold. Here everything is trying to be the hero.
- **No visible offer mechanic** — the subject promises two gifts but I can't tell from the hero what I need to spend, what the threshold is, or how long this runs. The GWP is implied, not stated.
- **Cadence is accumulating mascara fatigue** — July 14 was brown mascara mini (fantastic launch), July 17 was "Beauty pre-check" (still mascara-adjacent travel edit), and now this. e.l.f. just sent five emails in five days and I flagged it — ILIA is heading that direction on this one product category.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "BUILD YOUR FIRST ORDER" for any subscriber who has a purchase history** — replace with "Add to Your Routine" or "Complete the Duo." The single highest-lift change in this email, full stop.
- 2. **Surface the GWP mechanic in the hero** — a one-liner like "Spend $65, get two free gifts" under "Swipe Twice" closes the loop the subject opened. The rule in beauty email is: the inbox subject opens the loop, the hero closes it. Right now that loop stays open.
- 3. **Cut the Latest + Greatest grid or move it below the tote promo** — this email should be a mascara email. The multi-product grid is a catalogue move; it dilutes the "Swipe Twice" story and competes with the tote section for attention.
- 4. **Add a deadline** — "offer ends Sunday" or "while supplies last" does real work. The click likelihood rubric penalizes missing urgency for good reason.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Get two gifts with your mascara duo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Swipe twice, earn twice — your duo GWP is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $65, choose two gifts — offer ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Mascara Duo is back. Two free gifts when you layer up.`

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
```html

```
Missing `lang="en"` — required for screen reader language detection and WCAG 2.1 §3.1.1.

**Google Fonts loaded via `@import`**
```css

@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
```

Gmail, Yahoo Mail, and Outlook strip `@import` rules. Montserrat will silently fall back to `Helvetica` in those clients. Use `<link>` tags in `<head>` instead of `@import` for broader font support (still Gmail-limited, but works in Apple Mail, Outlook iOS, Samsung Mail).
