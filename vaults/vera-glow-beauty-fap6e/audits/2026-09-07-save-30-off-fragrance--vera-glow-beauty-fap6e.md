# Save 30% off fragrance
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, at least this one's actually a fragrance email and not another vague sitewide banner — Labor Day Sale, 30% off select scents, and it's leaning hard into Glossier You Reve. But I've now logged FOUR Glossier emails in five days (9/3 "30% off select favorites" at 6/10, 9/5 "fall favorites" at 5/10, 9/6 "free gift?" at 5/10, and now this) and it's the same sale mechanic wearing a different scent's face each time. This is the Glossier You family AGAIN — the exact fragrance line I flagged on 8/30 in "Stay with them long after you leave." At this point I know their entire fragrance wardrobe better than my own.
- There's genuinely nice stuff in here — the Reve bottle shot is gorgeous, there's a real review with a name attached, and the body spritz cross-sell section is decently organized. But nothing here talks to me as a Rouge/Diamond member. No points math, no "you're this close to a reward," nothing that says they know I'm not a first-time Glossier shopper. I'd scroll it on my commute, maybe tap through to Reve since I do actually like gourmand scents, but I'm not forwarding this — it's not a groundbreaking that would make my group chat.

## What's working

- The hero shot of the magenta Reve bottle against that soft fabric backdrop is genuinely one of the better product photos I've seen from them lately — stops the scroll.
- "Our dreamy, yummy gourmand fragrance" — that's specific scent-profile language, not generic "smells amazing" filler, and it tells me exactly what I'm getting before I click.
- Real customer review with a name (Isabel) and a 5-star rating gives it a trust cue most of these Glossier sends skip.
- The "More Scent Savings" grid with 30% OFF badges on three body spritzes is clean and scannable — I can compare products without hunting.

## What's weak

- Fourth near-identical Glossier promo in five days — I said this about 9/5's "fall favorites" already being a re-skin of 9/3's sale, and this is a re-skin of a re-skin. The fatigue is real.
- Zero loyalty/member signal anywhere — no points balance, no tier perk, nothing. Sephora's "Autumn Edition" digest at least led with "You have 0 points" (even if it was buried under too many modules, per my 9/7 read) — Glossier doesn't even try.
- The free gift section ("Get a Glossier You Solid on us with your $75+ order") is stuck at the very bottom below three product tiles — that's a real incentive buried where most people never scroll to.
- Asterisks on both the headline offer ("30% off select scents*") and the free gift ("on us with your $75+ order.*") with the fine print only resolved in tiny italic "*Terms apply." at the footer — feels like it's hiding exclusions the way Ulta buries "excl. apply" in their subject lines.
- No skin-tone or personalization cues at all, and for a fragrance email there's no note on longevity, sillage, or how it wears — the kind of product detail that would actually move me on a scent purchase.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the $75+ free gift teaser up near the hero — a free Glossier You Solid is a stronger hook than "30% off select scents" and shouldn't be buried under three body spritzes.
- 2. Kill the vague asterisks or resolve them inline ("30% off, ends Monday" beats "30% off select scents*" with fine print three screens down).
- 3. Add ANY loyalty signal — even a simple "as a Glossier Rewards member, you also earn 2x points on fragrance this week" would separate this from the generic sitewide clone.
- 4. Since this is Glossier You Reve specifically, add one line on how it wears (longevity/sillage) — I care about that more than the tasting-notes copy.
- 5. Space these sends out — four fragrance/sale emails in five days is Sephora-digest levels of noise without the payoff.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall fragrance refresh: 30% off Glossier You`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The gourmand scent everyone's asking about, now on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free Glossier You Solid with orders $75+, ends soon.`
