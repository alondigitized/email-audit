# NEW! Unreal Icon! If You've Got It... Glaze It!  💫
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay. Deep breath. This is Charlotte Tilbury number seven (at least) since June 11, and every single one of them has been some variation of "Unreal" this, "Unreal" that. I pulled up my inbox this morning and genuinely could not tell you off the top of my head whether I had already opened this specific email or if it was the June 19 "Meet Your UNREAL Summer Skin Line-Up" send wearing a different subject line. That cadence context is load-bearing for this review — because the email itself? Actually gorgeous. Warm golden editorial, two stunning models across different skin tones, a clear product story about a new glazed-skin product ("Hydrated Glaze" seems to be the hero). The copy leans into the glazed-skin trend hard and the visuals back it up. But there's zero offer — no price point, no discount, no loyalty incentive — which means this is a pure awareness send asking me to click to discover a product I've now been teased about for a solid week and a half.
- Would I click? Probably, because I'm already a CT buyer and the glazed skin trend is my whole summer vibe. But I resent that I'm clicking on autopilot rather than because this email earned it. Would I forward to a friend? Honestly yes — the visuals are that good — but I'd preface it with "warning: CT will then send her seven more of these."

## What's working

- **The hero section lands immediately.** "Hydrated Glaze" with the warm-toned, dewy-skin model is a clean, trend-forward opener — the glazed skin moment is everywhere right now and CT is correctly planting its flag.
- **Diverse skin tone representation across the email.** I can see at least two models — a lighter golden complexion up top and a deeper skin tone in the "Revive Skin With A Glazed Glow" section. That's the correct choice for a product called "Tailored Golden Shade."
- **The product ingredient story is present.** "10% Shiner Ingredients, 80% UNREAL Glow" — that specificity does real work. It's not just vibes, it's something I can repeat to a friend or post in a Reddit thread.
- **Visual hierarchy is warm and editorial.** The color palette is cohesive throughout — burnt gold, bronzed skin, amber tones. Charlotte Tilbury's visual brand is doing its job.
- **CTA buttons are visible and clearly actionable.** I can see orange/gold buttons at multiple points — I never have to hunt for where to click.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever.** This is a pure awareness send with zero price anchor, no loyalty incentive, no Rouge/Insider parallel. Compare this to ILIA's "This lip color lasts. This gift won't." — which at least built urgency — or Glossier's final-hours sale architecture. CT is asking me to click on discovery alone, which is harder by email seven.
- **The "Supremely Flattering / Tailored Golden Shade" section felt limiting.** One shade implied, or at least one shade hero'd — for a glazed product that ostensibly reads universally, I wanted shade range signaling, not just one golden reference. Glossier's You-dew or Futuredew launches always show the formula across multiple skin tones *with the product on skin*.
- **No loyalty or member call-out.** I'm a Charlotte Tilbury customer. There's no mention of any member-exclusive access, early launch, or points logic. Sephora or Ulta would at least acknowledge my tier in a send this deep into a campaign.
- **Cadence fatigue is doing real damage here.** By send seven on the same campaign, this email needs to give me a NEW reason to engage — a price reveal, a shade expansion, a bundle, anything. Instead it's a reframe of the same product story with different model shots.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a price or limited-early-access hook.** Even "available now, £X / $X" or "launching to CT members first" gives me a reason to click that "shop" isn't carrying alone on day ten of the campaign.
- 2. **Show the shade range on diverse skin tones simultaneously.** A side-by-side of the glaze formula across three skin tones in one panel would answer the fit question that "Tailored Golden Shade" currently raises more than it answers.
- 3. **Acknowledge the campaign run-up.** A one-liner like "You've seen the tease. It's here." would reward long-time openers and acknowledge the journey without being self-deprecating.
- 4. **Consolidate the section count.** Four distinct editorial sections for one product is one too many — "Game-Changing Glow from Nature" and "Revive Skin With A Glazed Glow" could be merged into a single panel.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — Charlotte Tilbury "Unreal Icon" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built with a standard table-based layout delivered via Braze (EU region), with VML Outlook shims and a Beefree-style multi-row structure. Several structural HTML violations and a truncated dark-mode CSS block introduce parsing risk across clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Braze redirect domain confirmed:** All links route through `clicks.charlottetilbury.com` (Braze click-tracking). This is expected, but:

- **Destination URL unverifiable** — the `href` in the visible CTA is a Braze-encoded opaque token. UTM parameters on the *final* landing page cannot be confirmed from the source (see §6).
- **Image CDN:** `cdn.braze.eu` (EU Braze CDN) — consistent with EU data residency; no issue on its own, but image blocking will hit users with images disabled since no `alt` is visible in the truncated `<img>` tag.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| Empty `<title>` | `<title></title>` in `<head>` | Low — some clients surface this in tab/notification text |
| `<meta>` tags inside `<div>` | `<meta name="color-scheme" ...>` placed in a `<td>` HTML block, not `<head>` | Medium — these are silently ignored; dark mode color-scheme hint never applies |
| Unclosed dark-mode `@media` block | `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark) { ... }` in inline `<style>` inside `<div>` is missing closing `}` in source | High — malformed CSS can cause downstream rule bleed in WebKit-based clients |
| Raw `<tr><td>` inside `<div>` | HTML block wraps table rows in a `<div>` (invalid HTML) | Medium — Outlook and older clients may mis-render |
| External font stylesheet | `<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css">` | Medium — stripped by Gmail, Yahoo, and most webmail; fallbacks (`Helvetica, Arial, Sans-serif`) kick in but may affect visual fidelity |
| No visible `alt` text | `<img src="https://cdn.braze.eu/...` truncated with no `alt=` in scope | High — accessibility failure; images-off rendering shows blank |

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Preheader is fully static:** `"Unlock Limited-Edition Stickers When You Shop Charlotte's Unreal Icons!"` — no recipient-level token (e.g., `{{first_name}}` or Braze `{{${first_name}}}`).
- No merge tokens are visible anywhere in the truncated source. If the full HTML contains none, the send is fully un-personalized, which is a missed Braze capability.
- No conditional content blocks (Braze `{% if %}` or Connected Content) detected.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **`noreply@mail.charlottetilbury.com` sender address** — CAN-SPAM and GDPR best practice requires a functional reply-to or visible contact path. A `noreply` From with no Reply-To header set is technically non-blocking for CAN-SPAM but degrades trust signals and can trigger spam filters.
- **Footer not visible** in truncated source — CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe link cannot be confirmed. Must be verified in the full render. Row `row-41` exists in the CSS (social block), suggesting a footer is present but unconfirmed.
- **Authentication headers** not available in the HTML source — DKIM/SPF/DMARC must be verified at the MX/header level on delivery.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All CTAs pass through Braze's opaque redirect (`clicks.charlottetilbury.com`). Braze appends UTM parameters at redirect time if configured in the campaign settings — **this cannot be confirmed from the HTML alone**. Verify in Braze campaign settings that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are set on the link-tracking configuration.
- If UTMs are not configured at the Braze level, all traffic lands on `charlottetilbury.com` as direct, breaking attribution.

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Fix the unclosed dark-mode `@media` block** — add the missing `}` before this ships; CSS bleed in Apple Mail is a real risk.
2. **Move `<meta name="color-scheme">` to `<head>`** — it does nothing inside a `<td>`.
3. **Add `alt` text to all images** — required for accessibility compliance and images-off rendering.
4. **Audit Braze campaign link settings** to confirm UTM parameters are injected at redirect time, not missing entirely.
5. **Remove raw `<tr><td>` from inside the HTML `<div>` block** or restructure as a proper nested table — the current structure is invalid HTML.
6. **Set a Reply-To header** to a monitored inbox if the From must remain `noreply@`.
7. **Confirm footer** contains physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe — verify in full HTML render, not just the truncated source.
