# Bestie, tame your week (and your flyaways) 💇‍♀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a lot of email wrapped around one real offer. The hero is a handbag flat-lay with "THE e.l.f. EDIT" and nothing about hair, even though the subject promised flyaways. The email never delivers on the subject. It's a catch-all: fall favorites, a zodiac skin quiz, a "newness" row, a bundle banner and a nav list. The actual offer is the free full-size gift, up to a $9 value with $35+ spent, no code. It sits third from the top and I almost missed it.
- Honestly, the math is fine. $35 for a $9 gift is roughly 25% back, and I can do that in two seconds. But I got this same "come see everything" energy on 11/5, 11/6 and 11/8, and that's five e.l.f. sends in five days. The 11/7 lip balm email was better because the gift was the whole point. This one buries it.
- Would I click? Maybe once, for the gift, if I were already building a $35 cart. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read? Yes.

## What's working

- The "Choose a full-size gift on us" block is clear: a $35+ threshold, "no promo code needed" and a Shop Now button. That's an easy mechanic.
- The zodiac skin grid, with product names like Vitamin C + E Ferulic Serum and Holy Hydration! Face Cream, is fun. It's the only part where I got ingredient-level detail.
- The footer has receipt scanning, rewards, free shipping and the TikTok Shop. That is the only loyalty signal I could see.
- It's on-season: fall favorites and early gift language for 11/9.

## What's weak

- The subject says flyaways and hair, but the hero and body are generic. Hair only appears as a blank gray bar in the nav list.
- The hierarchy is flat. Two top buttons, a fall grid, the gift, a quiz, a bundle and more all compete, and the gift should be the hero.
- The gray bars (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) render as empty slabs. They look like missing content, not navigation.
- "Bestie" is not personalization. Nothing here acknowledges my tier or purchase history. Sephora's Savings Event email led with two clear badges, Insider 10% and Collection 30%, and I knew in a second what was in it for me.
- The fine print is a dense wall at the bottom, and "up to $9 value" feels vague.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free gift the hero. Put "Free full-size gift with $35+" above the fold with the gift product photographed. Ulta's "Ends tonight" ladder is repetitive, but at least the offer is on top.
- 2. Cut the body to one offer plus one product row, not seven modules. Drunk Elephant ran the same gift three times in five days, but it was at least one message.
- 3. If the subject says hair, the hero should be hair, or change the subject.
- 4. Replace the blank gray category bars with real tiles or remove them.
- 5. Show the end date near the gift CTA, not only in the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 of 12 probed links failed.** All five are `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` tracked redirects. The probe error is `unknown url type: '/products/...'`, which means the redirect resolved to a path with no scheme or host:

- **Likely cause.** Browsers resolve a relative `Location` header, so these may work for real users. A strict HTTP client does not, and the probe is an example. Treat this as unconfirmed for end users until someone clicks the links. If the tracker really emits relative redirects, any client that doesn't resolve them (some security scanners, link-preview bots) will break.
