# Our newest Tumbler is almost ready to sip… ☕
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the first Glossier email in two weeks that isn't a free gift, and I'm relieved. After the 10/22 "Want a free gift?" send, the Milky Jelly email on 10/24, and the mug on 10/30, 11/1 and 11/2, a plain launch teaser feels almost classy. A pink tumbler on a tile floor with spilled coffee is a cute, on-brand shot, and "coming Thursday 11/5" gives me a real date.
- But it's a waitlist email with no price, no size, no color range and no perk. "Join the List" asks me to hand over a signup for a product I know nothing about. "Sold out in under 24 hours last time" is the only hook, and that's the mug again. Glossier is selling me the same drinkware drop with a different object.
- I'd open it out of curiosity, because I'm tired of the mug emails. I probably wouldn't click until I see a price. I wouldn't forward it. It's a reminder, not a gift.

## What's working

- The 11/5 date is specific and gives me a real launch day, which I rarely get from Glossier.
- The pink tumbler photo is the hero and it loads cleanly. The coffee ring and notes make it feel lived-in.
- It's a clean layout with one button, so there's no promo clutter. It's a nice break from the gift pile-on.
- "Sold out in under 24 hours last time" is credible scarcity, and I remember the mug selling out.

## What's weak

- There's no price, capacity or color, and I can't do any math on a tumbler I can't size. Sephora and Ulta would at least show a price.
- There's no Glossier points or member perk, such as early access for members. I'm in the loyalty program and the email treats me like a stranger.
- "Join the List" is a generic, low-energy button. It's also not a verb plus a noun.
- The white space on the left looks empty next to the big photo, and the footer's giant smiley and hand take up as much room as the hero.
- It's a fifth drinkware email in two weeks. It's the same mug energy with a different vessel.

- 1. Add the price, size and color on the hero. Even "$28, 20 oz, Balm Dotcom Pink" would give me something to act on.
- 2. Give members early access. Ulta does this well with Diamond tier early drops, and Sephora uses it for Rouge. Glossier should too.
- 3. Rename the button to "Get Thursday's Tumbler Alert" or "Remind Me 11/5."
- 4. Cut the footer art, or shrink it so the hero stays the focus.
