- The 25% off sitewide offer is dead simple — no spend-ladder gymnastics like their last three sends.
- Code (FALL25) and deadline (Sept 30) are both visible without hunting.

## What's weak

- Product grid is 100% professional hair-color/developer SKUs — zero relevance to someone like me who isn't doing color at home.
- No loyalty/rewards messaging anywhere, despite Sally having a rewards program — I'm Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, and I notice immediately when a brand doesn't even try to speak points.
