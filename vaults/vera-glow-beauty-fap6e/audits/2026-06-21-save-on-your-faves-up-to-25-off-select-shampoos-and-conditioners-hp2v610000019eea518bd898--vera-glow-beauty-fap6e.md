# Save on your faves: up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🌟
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- Four. This is the fourth time this exact offer — "up to 25% off shampoos and conditioners" — has crossed my inbox since June 19. And before I even get into the email itself, I need to name that: the June 21 version of this already landed this morning (I reviewed it), and now here's a second delivery ID for the same subject line on the same day. Whatever segmentation logic triggered this resend, it absolutely should not have. My inbox is not a testing ground.
- The email itself, taken cold, is fine. The shampoo hero is cleanly placed at the top, "SHOP NOW" is visible, Sephora's production value is always solid. But then the email goes rogue on me — pivoting immediately into "Everyday Icons: #71 Sephora Collection Pro Brushes." Brushes. I opened this for shampoo math and now I'm looking at a brush product showcase. That's a real editorial miss. It reads less like a curated send and more like a digest template that got repurposed with a new hero.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I barely registered it before archiving, which is exactly what happens when a brand burns your trust on cadence.

## What's working

- **Hero is on-brand and clean** — the yellow-gold gradient, "Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners," and a clear "SHOP NOW" button are legible and render without obvious bugs.
- **Free same-day delivery mention** is a real perk — that's a win tucked into the promo bar that could push someone on the fence toward acting today.
- **Kerastase section earns its place** — a "New from Kerastase" block in a hair-care-focused send is genuinely relevant; that's the kind of editorial pairing that justifies the scroll.
- **No broken images or render failures** — the layout holds, text is readable, and nothing looks visually corrupted on the render.

## What's weak

- **Same email, same day, different delivery ID** — this is the thing that matters most. The June 21 send already went out with this identical subject line. I didn't ask for a resend. This is a list-management error that reads as either an A/B test gone wrong or a trigger misfire, and it kills any goodwill the actual content might earn.
- **The brush pivot is editorial whiplash** — "Everyday Icons: #71 Sephora Collection Pro Brushes" immediately follows the shampoo hero. I came in for hair care and I'm now reading product copy about a brush kit. The two categories share nothing.
- **No deadline anywhere visible** — 25% off is a great hook, but with no "ends Sunday" or "today only" language visible in the hero, I have zero reason to click now versus never.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** — compared to Sally Beauty naming "It's a 10" in their subject line and linking directly to the brand, Sephora's CTA lands nowhere specific. Sally's June recent send beat this on specificity by a mile; even ILIA's "A gift for you" had a tighter redemption path despite being vaguer in subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the resend, or personalize the resend trigger** — if this is a non-opener resend, change the subject line. Hitting my inbox twice in one day with the same copy tells me the ESP is just firing blindly.
- 2. **Pull the brush section or put it after the hair care block** — the editorial sequence should be: shampoo/conditioner offer → Kerastase newness → brushes (if at all). Don't break the hair-care journey mid-email.
- 3. **Add a deadline to the hero** — even "Offer ends June 23" stamped on the hero graphic would lift click urgency meaningfully.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA** — "Shop Shampoo Deals" beats "SHOP NOW" every time. Charlotte Tilbury's solstice send at least anchored the CTA to an event; this floats.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off hair faves — today only ✂️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shampoo deal is about to expire 🫧`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kerastase, Olaplex, and more — ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge members: stack your points before this closes`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` blocks user zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 violation):**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents users from pinching to zoom on mobile. This violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `user-scalable=yes`.

**Issue — `robots` meta value has a space:**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
The correct value is `noindex` (no space). `no index` is non-standard and may not be honored by all crawlers. Low risk for email but indicates a template defect.
