# Last call for your free gift!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Glossier send in five days that's about the Milky Jelly gift. First was 10/22 "Want a free gift?", then 10/24 "Get Milky Jelly Cleanser, free!", and now this. At least "FINAL DAY" gives me a real reason to act, which the old "STILL ON!" 20% run never did. Still, I've seen the offer twice, so this only matters if I haven't bought yet.
- The math is simple: $75 gets a $14 cleanser. That's a decent threshold for stocking up on Boy Brow and Balm Dotcom, but it's not a haul. Nothing here tells me my points, my tier, or whether the gift stacks with anything. I'd glance at it, maybe tap if I had a cart half-built, and not forward it.

## What's working

- "FINAL DAY" and "Today is your LAST DAY" make the urgency credible, and it's the one real deadline in their last few sends.
- The offer is one clear line: free Milky Jelly Cleanser with a $75+ order. No percent ladder to decode, unlike Drunk Elephant's Subscribe & Save tiers.
- The close-up bottle shot is clean and puts the gift front and center.
- The star ratings and customer quotes under each product give me social proof. Sephora's gift-catalog sends skip that.
- The layout is simple and uncluttered. The "Shop now" buttons are big enough to tap.

## What's weak

- The "Today is your LAST DAY" copy and "Don't miss out!" are generic. Nothing about why this cleanser is worth my $75.
- Every product card says the same "Shop now," so there's no verb and noun to tell me what I'm buying.
- The gift is the star, but the cards below sell perfume, brow and lip balm. The cleanser has no ratings or reviews.
- The third card is cut off on the right ("I dont need..."), and the footer smiley and hand graphics bleed off the edge. It looks like a render glitch.
- There's no loyalty angle at all. I'm Rouge at Sephora and Diamond at Ulta, so a stranger-style send with no points or member perk feels flat. The asterisk and "Terms apply" sit at the very bottom, so I have to scroll to learn the exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a countdown or "ends tonight at 11:59pm ET" in the hero, and the gift's size and value ("full-size, $14 value"). That's the biggest lift, since the deadline is the only new information.
- 2. Replace the three unrelated product cards with products that help me reach $75, such as the best-selling Boy Brow plus Balm Dotcom, with a running total. Ulta would show the "spend $X more" math, and I do that math anyway.
- 3. Add a Milky Jelly review or two in the hero, since the gift is the actual draw.
- 4. Make the buttons specific: "Add Boy Brow," "Add Balm Dotcom."
- 5. Move the terms line up under the offer so I don't have to hunt for it.
- 6. Fix the cut-off third card and the oversized footer graphics.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: free Milky Jelly with $75`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free cleanser expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Add Boy Brow + Balm Dotcom to hit $75 and the $14 cleanser is yours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last chance, gift ends tonight. Spend $75, get full-size Milky Jelly.`

## Technical Audit

The confirmed issues are missing `alt` attributes on 4 content images and the tracking pixel. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay not exposing headers, and the truncated HTML hides everything below the preheader. They aren't confirmed defects.

- Only 12 material links were probed and 7 were skipped. The QA tool deprioritizes footer and social links, so the other links are unverified.
- The QA data reports no broken links, redirect loops or non-200 responses.
- The odd `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/…_0"></span>` just before `<!--<![endif]-->` looks like a Klaviyo tracking artifact. It is empty and invisible, so it is harmless.
