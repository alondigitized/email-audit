# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- It's Skechers again, and I clocked it before I even finished scrolling — that same grey men's slip-in from the "This style has limited availability!" send on 7/29 is back, now dressed up as "Selling Fast... add to cart before they're gone!" I don't have a men's grey Arch Fit Arcade in any cart, browsing history, or fantasy life, so the urgency is aimed at a customer who does not exist. This is at least the fifth Skechers email in two weeks doing some flavor of "your items are waiting" — 7/16, 7/22, 7/25, 7/28, 7/29 — and now this one, just with the panic dial turned up one more notch.
- The "2 FOR $100" footwear/apparel/accessories bar is the only thing here that isn't recycled cart-nagging, and it's buried two-thirds down the email under a wall of men's sneakers I'll never click. Compare that to Sephora's actual Beauty Insider Celebration send (8/1-8/31) that led with a gold badge and real member value up top — Skechers still doesn't know I exist as a person, let alone a customer, six emails deep.
- Not opening this one for real, and definitely not forwarding — unless it's to a friend as a "look how broken this brand's targeting is" joke, which, fine, that's still a forward I guess. Straight to archived-without-reading.

## What's working

- The "2 FOR $100" banner is a real, legible, specific offer with a visible deadline (thru 8/3) — the cleanest thing in the email.
- Layout renders clean — no overlapping text, no broken images, nav bar and footer icons all load fine.
- Afterpay/Klarna and curbside pickup icons are at least consistent with how a value-shopper (not me, but someone) actually transacts.

## What's weak

- Hero product is a men's grey slip-in — same shoe, same demographic miss as the 7/29 and 7/28 sends. I'm a 29-year-old woman who tracks Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond points, not restocking men's Arch Fit Arcades.
- "Selling Fast" urgency on a shoe I never viewed is the same fabricated-scarcity move as "Your cart expires soon!" (7/25) and "Your Items Are Waiting!" (7/22, then repeated 7/16) — I've now seen this exact retargeting failure mode four times.
- Six product recs below the fold are all men's sneakers and a men's hoodie — zero attempt at gender-matching, let alone anything beauty-adjacent I'd actually filter for.
- The real offer (2 for $100) is stranded below a giant irrelevant hero instead of leading — Ulta's 20%-off sends at least put the coupon code near the top even when the creative repeats.
- No loyalty tie-in, no personalization token, nothing that says Skechers knows my name or purchase history — just generic mass blast dressed as urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the retargeting logic before touching copy — I've now gotten this same grey men's shoe pushed at me with "limited availability," "selling fast," and implied cart urgency across three separate sends. That's a broken pixel/audience match, not a creative problem.
- 2. Lead with the 2-FOR-$100 deal, not a phantom abandoned-cart shoe — it's the only offer here with real teeth, put it above the fold.
- 3. Gender-segment the "more great options" grid — if I've never clicked a men's product, stop recommending six of them in a row.
- 4. Drop manufactured urgency language ("selling fast," "before they're gone") when there's no real inventory signal behind it — it reads as bait after this many repeats.

# Technical Email Audit: "Don't leave a good thing behind." (SKECHERS)

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an Attentive-served (`msgs.skechers.com` / `attentivemail.com`) promotional email with a broken unsubscribe link, no plain-text MIME part, and missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers — combining to create both a compliance risk and a deliverability risk.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link returns HTTP 400** — QA FAIL. The link (`https://skechers.attentivemail.com/ls/click?upn=u001.LNc6...BFWD_...`) is broken, meaning recipients clicking "Unsubscribe" cannot opt out via that link.
- All links use Attentive's `ls/click?upn=` redirect wrapper, consistent with standard click-tracking; no malformed `href` syntax observed in the sampled markup.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://skechers.attentivemail.com/wf/open?upn=...` (expected for an ESP-served campaign).
- QA probing coverage: 12 of 38 material links were actively probed (ranked by click likelihood); 26 lower-priority footer/social/utility links were skipped by design, so link-health beyond the unsubscribe failure is not fully verified.

- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — QA WARN. SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available headers (relay capture gap noted by QA, not a confirmed auth failure).

- No issues found in the directly observable markup (link structure is consistent with standard ESP redirect tracking).
