# Up to 50% off Early Black Friday^ deals starts NOW! (Terms & excl. apply) 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Marisol, earn points that turn into $ off future purchases... when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards" is the very first line I see. I've been Diamond for years, so it's a stranger pitch again. The pink "Early Black Friday: up to 50% off with new deals each week" hero does catch my eye. The email then falls apart into about 14 brand tiles, each with its own "Ends Oct 31" badge. I can't tell which deal is the real one.
- This is the same email as the 10/25 "Early Black Friday" send in my brand history, and it's the same fragmented grid. I'd open it because of Tarte, Clinique, and the Milani and Benefit tiles. I'd skim, maybe tap Tarte at 30% off, and then go compare against Sephora. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent deal roundup, but it isn't built for me as a member.

## What's working

- The pink hero says "up to 50% off with new deals each week", so the Black Friday cadence is clear.
- The brand tiles name prestige-adjacent brands I actually buy: Tarte at 30%, Clinique at 25%, Benefit at 30%, Milani, and OPI at "bogo 50% off".
- Each tile shows its own percentage and end date, so I can do the points math quickly.
- The free-gift credit card block and the Ulta Beauty Rewards banner are visible near the bottom, so member benefits aren't completely missing.
- The visuals are on-brand and bright. The product photography looks consistent from tile to tile.

## What's weak

- The top loyalty banner says "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards". I'm already a Diamond, so it's talking to a stranger, same as the 10/7 "join for free" send.
- 14 or so tiles with identical "Ends Oct 31" badges bury any hero. Nothing tells me which is the best deal.
- Several tiles only say "select products" or "select brands". "Up to 50%" with "terms & excl. apply" in the subject feels like fine print doing a lot of work.
- It's very long. I have to scroll through three or four screens before I reach the in-store events and TikTok Shop content.
- I see no tier math. There are no Diamond bonus points, no 2x or 3x events, and nothing personalized beyond my name.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the top banner. For members, replace "join Rewards" with "Diamond, your 20% coupon is live" or a points-multiplier teaser. Non-members can keep the join pitch.
- 2. Cut the 14-tile grid to 4–6 hero deals, and lead with the best one: Tarte, Clinique, or the OPI bogo.
- 3. Vary the badges. Put the "Ends Oct 31" deadline once in the header instead of on every tile.
- 4. Add a Rewards-points callout, such as "Earn 2x points on these brands this week".
- 5. Link to specific hero products, not just "Shop now" on every tile.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Marisol, Black Friday deals drop weekly — week 1 is live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tarte 30% off, bogo OPI, Clinique 25% — plus Diamond bonus points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New deals every week through Black Friday. This week's end Oct 31.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with dark-mode meta tags, Outlook VML settings and heavy third-party tracking. The QA run found no hard failures. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, and the image markup has accessibility and mixed-content problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **47 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **10 insecure pixels (confirmed).** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1` all load over plain HTTP. Clients that enforce HTTPS or proxy images (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may block them, and the pixels may then not fire.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** These ten Dotomi calls add 10 extra requests to the render path, and they are a privacy-disclosure concern.
- **Multiple tracking pixels** are also present:
  - `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=163148`
  - `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=163148`
  - `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/3eb758650b65389e.png?mi_u=203057427&mi_ign=163148`
- **Subscriber-identifying parameters.** `mi_u=203057427` and `i=203057427` appear in the pixel URLs. These are normal for ESP tracking but worth noting for privacy review.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing `alt` (confirmed).** These are the accessibility-relevant cases:
  - `.../m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `.../m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `.../m/45/4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` (flagged twice, so it appears twice in the body)
  - `.../m/1/852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png` (flagged twice)
- **Alt-less images with no assigned role.** A missing `alt` makes screen readers announce the filename and gives no fallback when images are blocked. If any of these are purely decorative, they should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** These are `mi.ulta.com`, `emltrk.com` and the Dotomi pixels. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether they're hidden 1x1 images. If they are, the impact is minimal, but adding `alt=""` is still best practice.
- **Doctype and markup mismatch.** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" ...>` aren't self-closed. Email clients tolerate this, so it's a low-severity validity issue.
- **Web font dependency.** The `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans...">` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Rendering there depends on the fallback font stack, which is cut off in the truncated source.
- **Dead CSS.** The hamburger-menu block is commented out (the "91925" note) but still ships in the `<style>`, adding payload for no effect. This is minor.
- **Hardening is present.** `lang="en-US"`, the viewport meta, `-webkit-text-size-adjust`, the Apple data-detector overrides and `o:OfficeDocumentSettings` for Outlook are all in place.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (unverified).** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may not preserve this header, so this is likely a capture limitation. Confirm against the raw message from the sender.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (unverified).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so this should be confirmed on the original.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `e.ulta.com` can't be validated here.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** Neither could be checked in the truncated source. I'm not flagging them as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable.** All 47 tracked links went through click-redirect domains and weren't resolved, so destination URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign identifier.** The emltrk pixel references `pr_fy26wk39_sunday_ebf_6am_last`. That is the ESP-side campaign tag, but whether it propagates to the landing-page UTMs is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images listed in section 3. Use `alt=""` on decorative images and pixels.
2. Move the ten `ads.dotomi.com` pixels to HTTPS, or remove or consolidate them. This fixes the mixed-content blocking and cuts the request count.
3. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks on the raw sender message (not the relay copy). Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.ulta.com`.
4. Resolve the redirect chains on a sample of the 47 tracked links (follow to the final URL) to verify UTMs, status codes and landing-page match.
5. Self-close the `<meta>` tags, or switch to an HTML5 doctype. Strip the commented-out hamburger CSS to reduce weight.
6. Verify the web-font fallback stack renders acceptably in Gmail and Outlook.
