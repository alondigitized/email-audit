## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the ninth Sally Beauty send I've logged, and it's the same "deals end soon" pitch I got on 9/28 with the "Appiversary deals end soon!" email. That one also had 25% off sitewide. This time the code is FALL25 instead of the Appiversary framing, and the headline is "Don't miss these fall deals." The copy is generic. The offers stack: BOGO 50% off hair care, 30% off clippers, BOGO 50% off jewelry, and 25% off sitewide. Nothing tells me which one to care about.
- The hero is a gorgeous redhead with wavy hair, and the red and cream palette is bold. But the biggest offer, 25% sitewide with a code, sits at the very bottom, under three product blocks. Sephora would put the code and the Rouge/VIB tier angle at the top. Ulta would at least show the 21 Days-style "ends today" countdown near the hero. I've been counting Sally's cadence all week (9/21, 9/24, 9/25, 9/28, and now today), so "last chance" from a brand that keeps saying "last chance" has lost credibility.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I actually needed hair color or clippers. I'd probably screenshot the FALL25 code, since I'm always doing the points math, and skip the rest. I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read for me.

## What's working

- The "Ending soon" marquee banner and "Ends September 30" on every block make the deadline clear.
- The 25% off sitewide block has a visible code (FALL25) and a big black "Shop now" button. The offer itself is easy to grasp.
- The BOGO 50% hair care block shows real products. I can see the Sexy Hair, It's a 10 and Biolage-style bottles, so it feels concrete.
- The Klarna strip and the "2 Hour Delivery / Pick Up In-Store" icons are useful. That's the "beauty in a dash" convenience I actually use.
- The redhead hero photo is striking and feels on-season for fall hair color, which fits the 9/25 L'Oréal launch send.

## What's weak

- The strongest offer, 25% off sitewide, is buried at the bottom after three secondary deals. I'd have led with it.
- Four different offers with no hero deal makes the hierarchy muddy.
- The hero says "special savings" and gives no number. It's a big empty-calorie block before I see a single price.
- There's no member or loyalty content at all. No Sally Beauty Rewards points math, no tier callout. Sephora and Ulta both make me feel known. Here I'm a stranger.
- The jewelry BOGO and the clippers block have nothing to do with my beauty focus. The jewelry photo is a tiny, unclear product shot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the code. Put "25% off sitewide, code FALL25" in the hero, and drop the empty "special savings" line.
- 2. Cut to one hero deal and one supporting deal. Fold the clippers and jewelry into a single "more deals" row.
- 3. Add a loyalty line, like "Earn 2x Rewards points today," so I have some points math to do.
- 4. Stop reusing "last chance" and "ending soon" every few days. Space the sends or vary the offers.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional/VML template with mobile media queries. The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels: an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a non-HTTPS pixel, and several pixels with no alt text. The header-level compliance checks could not be completed because the relay did not capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad platform never substituted it, so the cache-buster is not working. Clients or proxies that cache the pixel may under-count impressions, and the unencoded brackets can make the URL invalid.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id...` loads over HTTP. It can be blocked as mixed content or by image proxies, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2320396/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq.net` and `dotomi.com` are three separate ad-tech trackers. The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). If so, it is a subscriber identifier passed to a third party in a URL.
- **Link validity not verified:** 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. I can't confirm they resolve, and they are neither passes nor failures.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (6 images):**
  - Two `<img>` tags have an empty name and empty `src` in the QA output. These may be spacer or placeholder images, or an extraction artifact.
  - The tracking pixels `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` also have no alt text. Pixels should carry `alt=""` (with `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip them. As written, some clients read out the filename or URL.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` refers to a font that has no `@font-face` in the visible source. Only Open Sans is declared, so links fall back to Helvetica. This is minor, and the source is truncated, so I can't rule out a declaration further down.
- **Markup:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, although the doctype is XHTML 1.0. This is harmless in practice.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed:** the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL (see section 2). It is an ad-server macro that was not resolved.
- **Not confirmed:** no other unresolved tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` appear in the visible portion. The source is truncated, so I can't check the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain is `em.sallybeauty.com`. This is a relay limitation and not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 51 click-tracking links were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show the destination URLs or UTM parameters. No landing-page mismatch is confirmed.
