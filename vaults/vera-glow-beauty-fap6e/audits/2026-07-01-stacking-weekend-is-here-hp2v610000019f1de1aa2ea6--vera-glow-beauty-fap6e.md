# ⭐️ STACKING WEEKEND IS HERE ⭐️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the email. As a Sephora Rouge member, "Stacking Weekend" is genuinely on my beauty calendar the way other people have dentist appointments — I know it's coming, I plan around it, and when I see that 30% off + 2X points combo sitting above the fold, my first instinct is to open Sephora's app before I've even finished reading. The June 29th "We're treating you to something new" send felt like a palate cleanser after surviving five straight Major Summer Deals emails — and in hindsight it was clearly the warm-up act for this. Smart sequencing.
- That said, the email is doing six or seven jobs at once: the stacking offer, the credit card layer, the free sample bag, a Dolce&Gabbana launch, app-only exclusives, a points redemption breakdown, and a "Happy 4th!" wave at the end. By the time I hit the holiday section, I'd already clicked away in my head. The points breakdown is genuinely the content I want and I screenshot it for my group chat. The D&G launch buried mid-email is actually a killer detail for a Rouge member — new prestige at 30% off is why we showed up — but it's fighting for space with too many other messages.
- I would click this. I'd probably forward the points breakdown screenshot to my beauty group chat rather than the email itself. Mark as read without reading every block? Honestly yes for the bottom half.

## What's working

- **30% off + 2X points in the hero** — value prop lands instantly, no decoding required, it's the first thing my eye hits.
- **"Ends This Sunday" deadline** — clean, credible urgency that I believe because Sephora actually ends these events; it doesn't feel manufactured the way Glossier's rotating "ends tonight / ends tomorrow" cycle does.
- **Points redemption breakdown** — the "What Your Points Can Do" section is exactly the math I do after every Sephora purchase; this is differentiated content that Ulta doesn't serve me as well.
- **Dolce&Gabbana launch drop mid-email** — folding prestige newness into a sale event is smart for a Rouge shopper; it answers the implicit question "is there anything new I should be spending my 30% on?"
- **Cardholder section layering** — even though I don't have the Sephora Visa, seeing a real stacked benefit for cardholders reads as genuine loyalty depth rather than one-size-fits-all spam.

## What's weak

- **ALL CAPS subject line** — "⭐️ STACKING WEEKEND IS HERE ⭐️" shouts at me in my inbox; Lush's Sanrio collab email, which I scored a 9, never once raised its voice and felt more like a friend's tip-off than a broadcast announcement.
- **Email length — too many blocks** — six-plus content sections makes this read like a retail circular; Drunk Elephant's "The It Kits" email kept one hero, one offer, and it felt premium; this one loses its own urgency by diluting the hero offer.
- **"Shop Now, Get Later" GWP framing** — the free sample bag mechanic is confusing at a glance; Ulta spells out "spend $35, get the bag" clearly; I had to read this twice to understand the trigger.
- **No Rouge tier callout** — I'm Rouge, I earn more points, I get the highest discount tier, and the email treats me identically to a Insider. One personalized line — "Your Rouge 30% is ready" — would have felt like Sephora knowing who I am.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with tier in the subject** — being explicit about "Rouge" or "Beauty Insider" in the subject line is the single highest-lift change; it earns the open before I even see the offer.
- 2. **Collapse to three content blocks**: (1) stacking offer + deadline + GWP trigger spelled out, (2) what to buy — product picks including the D&G drop, (3) points math. Cut the app push; that's for a separate triggered send.
- 3. **Spell out the GWP mechanic in the hero area** — "Spend $X, get the sample bag free" as a line below the 30% headline rather than a separate "Shop Now, Get Later" module.
- 4. **Soften the subject caps** — title case or even just standard case signals "curated" rather than "clearance shelf."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge: your 30% off Stacking Weekend starts now ⭐️`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stacking Weekend is live — 30% off + double points, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your Rouge discount + 2X points on new launches including D&G. Through Sunday only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30% off + a free sample bag with qualifying purchase. Here's how to maximize your points this weekend.`
