- Zero personalization — no "Vera," no Beauty Insider or Diamond-tier language, nothing that treats me like a repeat Rouge shopper versus a cold subscriber.

- 2. Swap the generic "Top Picks for You" grid for actual halo-glow accessories (shimmer highlighter, glow-boosting serum) so the whole email stays on-theme instead of breaking into a random face-product ad.
