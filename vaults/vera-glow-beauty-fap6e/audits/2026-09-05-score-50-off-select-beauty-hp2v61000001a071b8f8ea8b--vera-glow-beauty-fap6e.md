# Score 50% off select beauty 😎
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "GET 50% OFF" in giant type at the top, "Today Only" panel, free shipping for Beauty Insiders — I've seen this exact skeleton from Sephora multiple times this week already, most recently the "Stacking Weekend" send I scored 8-9/10 on 9/4 and the reskin of this same offer I already logged this morning under this identical subject line. The "Today Only" trio (Benefit brow pencil, Supergoop SPF, Rare Beauty blush) is a decent hook and specific enough to skim fast, and the tiered price strikethroughs ($28→$14, $38→$19, $27→$13.50) do the points-math work for me instantly, which I appreciate. But there's zero mention of bonus points this time — Sephora had me at 500 bonus points with $75 spend just yesterday, and dropping that lever makes this feel like the stripped-down version of a better email they already sent.
- The tier bar at the bottom (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is a nice touch of "we see you" but it's static, not telling me what MY Rouge-specific perk is today — Ulta's "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" personalization from 9/3, while otherwise a mediocre email, at least used my actual name. This is a fine, scannable sale email but it's not one I'm opening a tab for — I'd skim it in the app, maybe tap through if I need sunscreen, and mark it read. Not forwarding this one; it's not different enough from what I already got from Sephora this week to be worth a friend's time.

## What's working

- The "Today Only" curated 3-pack (Benefit, Supergoop, Rare Beauty) gives me a fast decision set instead of a wall of products.
- Strikethrough pricing on every single item does my points/value math for me before I even click — I don't have to open a calculator like I do for some Ulta coupon-code sends.
- Grid of 8 additional 50%-off picks spans skincare, makeup, and haircare, so there's something in my actual rotation (Kiehl's Rare Earth mask, Charlotte Tilbury foundation).
- Beauty Insider tier bar at the footer at least visually acknowledges the loyalty program exists.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badge on the Milk Makeup primer is a decent exclusivity flex that Ulta can't match on that SKU.

## What's weak

- No bonus points, no stacking mechanic — flat 50% off is a downgrade in structure from the "Stacking Weekend" email two days ago, and I feel the difference immediately.
- Zero personalization — no name, no "picked for you," nothing tied to my Rouge status beyond the generic tier chart at the bottom.
- "Today Only" urgency undercut by the main grid below it having no deadline at all — is that also today only, or does it run all week? Genuinely unclear.
- This is now the second nearly identical "Score 50% off select beauty" send I've logged today — the cadence is starting to feel spammy rather than urgent.
- Superscript "ᵃ" and dagger footnotes on the hero with terms buried at the very bottom in tiny gray legal text feels like the exact "exclusions I have to hunt for" pattern I dinged Ulta for on 9/3.

## Recommendations

- 1. Bring back the bonus-points stacking mechanic from the 9/4 email — that's the single biggest lever Sephora has that Ulta doesn't, and dropping it makes this feel like a lesser rerun.
- 2. Put a real countdown timer or explicit end-time under "Today Only" instead of vague urgency — tell me if the grid below expires too.
- 3. Add light personalization — even just "Rouge Member" stamped near the hero would beat the generic tier bar.
- 4. Differentiate this from this morning's near-identical send — if it's genuinely a new day's rotation, say "New today" clearly so I don't dismiss it as a duplicate.
- 5. Move exclusions closer to the offer in slightly larger type — not asking for a wall of legal text, just enough that "50% off" doesn't feel like a bait grab.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New picks just dropped — plus free shipping for Insiders`
