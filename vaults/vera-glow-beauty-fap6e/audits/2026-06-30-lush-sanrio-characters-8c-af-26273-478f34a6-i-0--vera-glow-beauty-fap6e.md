# Lush × Sanrio Characters
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- Email twenty-two. I know it's twenty-two because I've been counting Lush sends the same way I track how many times Ulta resends the same "ends tonight" email — with mild exasperation and a spreadsheet I started ironically and now maintain earnestly. And yet: "Lush × Sanrio Characters" still made me stop. I grew up with a Hello Kitty alarm clock and a Cinnamoroll pencil case. This subject line is a nostalgia ambush, and it worked.
- Inside, the email delivers on the promise. The floating character-shaped products against the pastel sprinkle-border layout are genuinely cute — it looks like Lush put actual creative effort into this collab, not just slapped a Sanrio logo on their usual product grid. The "Choose Your Character!" framing turns product browsing into a personality quiz, which is exactly the kind of thing that makes me screenshot and send to three different group chats.
- The killer is there's no offer. Not a member price, not a launch discount, not even "free shipping on the collab." I'm always doing the math — is this cheaper with my Sephora Rouge points, is there a Ulta 21 Days window coming up? Lush gives me nothing to math against. I'd click out of Sanrio loyalty. I would not forward this as "must-shop" until I knew what my Lush Club membership does for me here. Mark as read with a tab open in the background.

## What's working

- **The collab name in the subject is enough.** Four words, zero fluff — for a Millennial who still owns Cinnamoroll merch, "Lush × Sanrio Characters" is the whole pitch.
- **Floating product hero is delightful.** Character-shaped soaps or bath bombs in the air against a pastel background reads immediately as "limited edition, get it before it's gone."
- **"Choose Your Character!" is interactive energy.** Six polaroid-framed character products — it reframes shopping as self-expression, which is exactly how Sanrio has always marketed itself.
- **Sprinkle-border design system is cohesive.** The Sanrio aesthetic is all the way through — header, borders, color palette — it doesn't feel like a lazy licensed slap-on.
- **No render bugs.** Everything lands clean; layout is solid top to bottom.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer.** No launch discount, no member price, no free shipping. Compared to the Sally Beauty BOGO I saw this week — where the offer was in the subject AND the hero — this feels like it wants full retail without earning it.
- **"Explore Now" CTA is generic.** "Shop Your Character" or "Find Your Sanrio Match" would land so much harder with what this email is doing creatively.
- **Lush Club is an afterthought.** The Club badge is buried in the footer. If loyalty members get early access or points bonuses on this collab, that needs to be in the hero — not footnoted.
- **No price anchors anywhere.** I can't evaluate whether this is an impulse buy or a budget conversation. ILIA shows me what I'm walking into before I click; Lush doesn't.
- **Cadence fatigue is real.** Twenty-two emails in June. The creative here is strong enough to survive it, but just barely.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the CTA to work.** Change "Explore Now" to "Find Your Character" or "Shop the Sanrio Drop" — the creative is doing all the work and the button is phoning it in.
- 2. **Surface a Lush Club perk in the hero.** Even "Members earn 2× points on the collab" would give me the math hook I need before I click through.
- 3. **Add a price or starting-at anchor.** "Bath bombs from $X" or "Starting at $Y" somewhere in the hero or just below the CTA keeps me from abandoning because I can't budget-plan.
- 4. **Lean into the character quiz mechanic.** A "Which Sanrio character is your bath personality?" hook above the grid would make this genuinely shareable — and Lush needs more reasons to go viral, not just reasons to email.
- 5. **Cadence flag:** Twenty-two sends in a month is not sustainable. The Sanrio drop is strong enough to be the marquee send of the week — protect it by cutting the sends around it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Six characters. One is definitely you. Shop the Lush × Sanrio drop.`
