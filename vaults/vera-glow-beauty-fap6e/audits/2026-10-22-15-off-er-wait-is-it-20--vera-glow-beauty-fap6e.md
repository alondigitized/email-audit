# 15% off! Er, wait. Is it 20%?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Drunk Elephant send in four days that pushes the same subscription ladder. The 10/19 "15% off, you say?" email introduced it. 10/21's "Do I hear 15% off?" repeated it. Now the subject is a wink at its own confusion, "15% off! Er, wait. Is it 20%?", and the body is the same 15/20/10 ladder again. The joke is cute. But I did the points math on day one, and nothing here is new. The offer ends Oct 27, so there's no fresh urgency either.
- The hero is clean and the product lineup looks good. I see Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra and T.L.C. on pedestals with a clear SHOP NOW button. The holiday gift guide block below it is the only new beat. Still, I'd skim it and move on, because I've already seen this ladder.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd forward it to nobody. I'd mark it read and wait for the Sephora Beauty Insider event, where I can stack Rouge perks on top of Drunk Elephant instead of locking into a subscription.

## What's working

- The hero headline "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN" is big and readable on a phone. The 15/20/10 numbers are bolded in the body, so I can scan them quickly.
- The product shot is gorgeous. The colour-coded bottles (teal, orange, blue, pink) are instantly recognisable as Drunk Elephant, and I can tell which products I'd be subscribing to.
- The SHOP NOW button sits directly under the offer copy, so there's no hunting for the CTA.
- The gift guide block has a different mood: pale blue background, kits stacked up. It gives me something new to look at that isn't the subscription pitch.
- The fine print states the dates and exclusions plainly: starts Oct 19, ends Oct 27, new subscription orders only, not stackable. I'd rather have that than a surprise at checkout.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a 15%-or-20% reveal, but the body is the same ladder from 10/19 and 10/21. The "wait, is it 20%?" hook doesn't resolve into anything new.
- The deadline is only in the tiny grey fine print. There's no "ends Oct 27" in the hero, so no urgency where my eye lands.
- The SHOP NOW button is a thin outlined box with small text on a grey background. It's easy to miss compared with the huge headline.
- The grey hero background feels flat and a bit dull for a brand this colourful.
- No loyalty or tier hook. As a Rouge who counts points, I see no mention of Drunk Elephant's rewards and no Sephora or Ulta angle. A free-shipping note is buried in the fine print.
- The gift guide link "GIFT GUIDE ME" is a small underlined text link. It competes with the main CTA and is a weak tap target on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the same ladder. If it has to run again, change the creative: a before/after of what a year of auto-delivery costs, or a specific routine bundle with a real dollar figure on a named product.
- 2. Put the end date in the hero ("Ends Oct 27") and make the SHOP NOW button solid and larger.
- 3. Cut the cadence. Four sends in four days of one offer trains me to ignore the brand. Sephora and Ulta would space this out around a calendar moment.
- 4. Name a hero product with a price. "Protini, $68, now $57.80 on your first order" is math I can act on.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Selligent-style send (`cheers.drunkelephant.com`) built on an XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table-based layout. QA found no hard failures, only 3 warnings: missing alt text on two tracking images, three `http://` social icons, and absent compliance and authentication headers (probably a relay artifact). The HTML source was truncated, so footer, body links and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so I can't confirm they resolve or that destinations match the offer.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekresend_p`. Its campaign ID (`subweekresend`) indicates this is a resend of an earlier send.
- **Second tracking image:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. It is a second third-party tracker alongside the `mi.` pixel.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the `e=` parameter puts the recipient's plaintext email address in the query string. It will show up in proxy, CDN and server logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt` attribute. Both look like tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but they should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Insecure image sources:** the Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load over `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`:
  - `cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)

  These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and have no HTTPS fallback.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick a language. `<title>Drunk Elephant</title>` is generic.
- **Fragile web fonts:**
  - Fonts load through render-blocking `<link>` tags and `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` inside `<style>`. `@import` is unsupported or stripped in most email clients.
  - `BrownRegular` is pulled from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`, a versioned asset path that can break when the site redeploys.
  - A third-party font host adds an extra request and a privacy leak, and the fallback behavior depends on the font stacks in the truncated part of the source.
- **Questionable CSS:**
  - `<!-- … -->` HTML comments sit inside the `@media` block (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->`), which can break parsing in some clients.
  - `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:12–15px` and `max-height` with `!important`, which risks clipping text on mobile.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens found:** none such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` in the visible source.
- **Seed address in the tracker URL:** `e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app` shows the recipient merge resolved correctly (see the PII note in section 2).

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `info@cheers.drunkelephant.com`, a subdomain, so alignment can't be checked from the data given.
- **Body compliance elements unverified:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable:** no `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source, and the 11 click-tracking links were not resolved. Campaign attribution rests on the ESP IDs (`mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekresend_p`, `c=us_de_broad…`).
- **Landing pages not checked:** destinations were not followed, so I can't confirm the landing page matches the promo.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three social icons to `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com` pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Host fonts on a first-party HTTPS domain, drop the `@import` calls, and define web-safe fallback stacks. Move the `<!-- -->` comments out of the `@media` block.
5. Stop putting the recipient email in tracker query strings (`e=`), or use an opaque ID.
