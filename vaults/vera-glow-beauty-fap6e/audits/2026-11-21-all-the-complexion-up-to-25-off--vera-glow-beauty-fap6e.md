# All the complexion + up to 25% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is still running the 25/20/30 number shuffle, and I'm doing the points math again. The top banner says "unlock 25% off sitewide* | join VIP for free." The "20% off*" blush circle sits on the hero. The body copy says "20% off sensitive-skin friendly coverage + a free full-size Complexion Stick with any order." The red VIP block at the bottom goes back to "unlock 25% off." This is the same mismatch I flagged on 11/19 and again on 11/21 with "Choose your shade of 30% off."
- Underneath that, the email is a good complexion lineup. Each of the five products has its shade count, coverage, finish and benefits listed, and the shade-match quiz is a real personalization hook. I'd click through to the Skin Rewind Complexion Stick, because a free full-size with any order is a decent gift. I wouldn't forward it. It's the fourth ILIA send in about three days, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The spec lines on each product (shades, coverage, finish, benefits) tell me what I need without a click. Sephora's own foundation emails rarely do that.
- "Your shade here" with the quiz CTA is the personalization I want. Shade matching is the main thing stopping me from buying complexion online.
- The free full-size Complexion Stick with any order is a clear gift with no spend threshold. It beats Drunk Elephant burying their 30% in a lavender box at the bottom.
- The VIP block spells out the points: 2x points, free shipping, 25% off. That's the math I care about.
- The product grid zig-zags the image left and right, and the single dark-maroon CTA color keeps it easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The offer is stated three different ways: "25% sitewide*", "20% off*" and "up to 25%" in the subject. Ulta would put one number up top and keep it consistent.
- The "25% off sitewide" in the banner needs VIP, but the hero's 20% doesn't. It's not clear which one applies to me without signing up.
- The tiny "free" and "NEW" badges and the fine print under the gift edit are hard to read on a phone.
- Four product CTAs say "Shop Now," while the "Find My Shade" one is the only specific one. I'd like a verb plus the product.
- The send feels repetitive. After "Choose your shade of 30% off" the same day, I'm getting Glossier-style fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline number and use it everywhere: banner, hero, subject and VIP block. Show the member price versus the non-member price side by side.
- 2. Make the subject and the hero say the same thing. Lead with the free full-size, since it's the strongest hook.
- 3. Cut the send cadence, or give each email a different angle (complexion on one day, gifts on another) instead of the same offer with a new shade.
- 4. Make the product CTAs specific, like "Shop Serum Tint" or "Match my shade," and make the badges bigger.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free full-size Complexion Stick + 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Find your shade: 20% off complexion`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 2x points when you join VIP free. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Take the 1-min shade quiz, then claim your free stick.`
