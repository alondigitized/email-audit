# Hot drops coming through
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Sephora send I've logged in about two days. Three days of Daily Deals and brow sales, and now a launch roundup. The subject gave me no reason to open this one over the others. Inside, it's a solid new-arrivals scroll. The best part is a 2X/5X points banner sitting above the fold, with the code SAYPOINTS+ and a 10/7 end date. That's the only part of this email that makes me do points math.
- Honestly, I'd open it because I'm a Rouge who stocks up around the Insider event, not because of "Hot drops coming through." The scroll itself is good: Haus Labs, Summer Fridays, OUAI, YSL, Charlotte Tilbury, Ultra Violette and LoveShackFancy, with a price under almost every product. But the points banner is tiny and the launches have no real hierarchy. Would I click? Yes, probably the Charlotte Tilbury powder or the Haus Labs liner. Would I forward it? No. I'd screenshot the SAYPOINTS+ code for my group chat and move on.

## What's working

- The 2X/5X points banner is above the hero and has a clear end date (10/7). That's the one thing here I'd actually act on.
- Every product has a price and a "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" tag, like Haus Labs lip liner, Summer Fridays and Charlotte Tilbury powder at $30. I can decide without tapping.
- The launch mix fits me: prestige makeup, fragrance (YSL Black Opium Pink, OUAI St. Barts, LoveShackFancy) and hair (Moroccanoil mask), plus the Clean at Sephora badges.
- "Holiday Exclusives Launch Soon! Build your basket" teases the beauty-event calendar. That's what I plan around.
- The barcode with SAYPOINTS+ at the bottom is useful if I'm shopping in-store or at Sephora at Kohl's.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero ("Hot drops… the latest has entered the chat") are vague. After the 10/2 "50% OFF select brow products" email, I had a concrete reason to open that one. This one gave me none.
- The 30% off hair faves ("today only") is buried near the bottom, below the Rhode and auto-replenish tiles. If that's the real deal, it should be up top.
- Too many modules for one email: launch grid, points banner, holiday teaser, auto-replenish, Rhode, hair sale, Beauty Insider footer. It's the same "four emails in one" problem as the 10/2 send.
- The fine print under the points code is tiny and dense. I can't see which tiers or brands are excluded without zooming.
- Only a small "SHOP NOW" under the hero. The product tiles have no real buttons, so tap targets on my phone feel like guesswork.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the points offer into the subject and hero. Something like "2X points on new launches" beats a vibe line. Lead the body with the points banner at full size, with the end date and my tier benefit.
- 2. Pick one launch hero (Haus Labs liner) and group the rest under a "more new" row. Right now eight products have equal weight.
- 3. Move "30% off hair faves, today only" up, or cut it. The mixed signals dilute the points message.
- 4. Give each product a "Shop [brand]" button, not just an image link.
- 5. Space out sends. A launch roundup right after a brow sale and a rewards email makes me skim.
