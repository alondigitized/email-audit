# It’s OK to throw shade.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "It's OK to throw shade" got me in for the pun, but this is actually the Kamo shade-finder email I was half-expecting after the 9/19 "Kamo FOMO?" launch tease — Drunk Elephant is now doing the work of walking me through the 25-shade range with an actual diverse arm swatch shot, five skin tones from fair to deep, and that's a real personalization signal I don't get from most of my inbox. The top banner offer — 20% off Kamo + a full-size moisturizer bundle — is buried under a headline about shade-matching instead of leading with it, which feels backwards for someone like me who's already doing points-and-percent math in my head before I even open the email.
- This is a nice step up from the plain product-drop energy of "Hydrated skin" (9/17, 6/10) because there's an actual interactive tool (shade finder) plus a GWP mechanic (free Lala Retro deluxe + travel bag on $120+) stacked on top of the 20% bundle discount — that's three offers in one send, which honestly out-does e.l.f.'s single clean GWP mechanic ("Clear Brow & Lash Mascara on us") from earlier this week. But it's a LOT of scrolling to get there, and unlike Sephora's Hair Daily Deals email that hits me with "up to 50%" and a code in the first screen, I have to scroll past two "FIND YOUR SHADE" CTAs before the actual value prop shows up.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — I've got Kamo curiosity from the FOMO email already, and "medium 14W, warm neutral undertones" as a sample result is the kind of shade-specificity that actually gets me to trust a foundation launch. Not forwarding this one though, it's a nice-to-open not a must-share.

## What's working

- The arm swatch hero with five distinct skin tones (fair to deep) is genuine demographic breadth — better than the single-model close-up most brands lean on.
- Naming a specific example match ("MEDIUM 14W, Medium with warm neutral undertones") makes the shade finder feel real, not just a gimmick.
- Stacking three mechanics — 20% bundle discount, free deluxe sample, free travel bag on $120+ — gives me actual math to do, which is my whole thing.
- Clean, on-brand green palette and product shot consistent with the Kamo launch I've been tracking since 9/19.

## What's weak

- The 20% bundle offer is stuck in a gray top banner in tiny caps — I nearly skimmed past it before "WHAT SHADE ARE YOU? (SURE?)" pulled my eye instead.
- Two "FIND YOUR SHADE" CTAs before you ever see pricing or the free-gift details — that's a lot of scroll for someone deciding whether to click.
- No loyalty or points language anywhere — I'm Rouge and Diamond, and Drunk Elephant emails never once acknowledge tier status, unlike Sephora which at least nods to Beauty Insider event cadence.
- The asterisked fine print at the bottom (min spend, date windows, exclusions) undercuts the "clean offer" feel — free gift is only valid 9/23–9/29, and that window isn't flagged anywhere near the actual CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% + free gift offer out of the gray banner and into the hero — lead with the deal, not the quiz, especially since I already know the shade-finder exists from the FOMO teaser.
- 2. Add the 9/23–9/29 deadline directly under the GWP mention in the body, not buried in the footer asterisk — urgency only works if I see it before I scroll past.
- 3. Cut one of the two "FIND YOUR SHADE" CTAs — one above the swatch, one below the demo photo is redundant.
- 4. Any nod to Rouge/Diamond status or "as a VIP" would close the loyalty gap every DE send has had since I started tracking this brand.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shade match is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25 shades, 5 undertone families — plus 20% off your Kamo bundle`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free deluxe sample + travel bag with $120+, through Tuesday`
