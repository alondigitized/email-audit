# Beauty’s most wanted just landed 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, no "you have 0 points" glitch this time — that's officially two sends in a row where Sephora's fixed the merge-field bug that's been haunting every single one of their emails I've logged this week (both K18s, the two 50%-off-select-brands sends, and the Rewards Bazaar one). Progress. But "Beauty's Biggest Hits" as the hero headline is doing a lot of nothing — no specific launch name, no specific category, just vague superlative copy sitting over a Charlotte Tilbury powder that doesn't get named until three scrolls down.
- What actually caught my eye: the Rouge tier bar (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE, with ROUGE blacked out to show I'm in it) sitting right above the footer, and the 5X points banner with code SEPHORAXP good "until 9/16" — that's today, so at least the urgency is real and not recycled. Compare that to the Rewards Bazaar email from 9/15 where I was still doing manual points math with zero help from the layout — this one at least does some of that work for me.
- Would I click? Probably, but only because I already have Sephora open in my head from three other emails this week — not because this one earned it. I wouldn't forward it to a friend. It's a solid, clean, on-brand send that's coasting on recognition rather than giving me a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar near the footer is a small but real personalization touch — I can see exactly where I sit at a glance, something Ulta's "20% off one item" emails never bother with.
- The 50% off Select Beauty banner up top is unmissable and paired with a "TODAY ONLY" tag, so the eye lands on the offer first, no scrolling needed.
- No render bugs — clean grid, no overlapping text, no broken images, and mercifully no repeat of the "0 points" bug from 9/15.
- The Charlotte Tilbury Airbrush Flawless Blur Loose Setting Powder gets a real "ONLY AT SEPHORA" exclusivity callout with price ($50) — that's the kind of brand-curation signal that makes me feel like I'm getting first dibs, not a mass blast.
- 5X points on Sephora Collection through today is a legit loyalty lever, more concrete than the vague "points = rewards" framing from yesterday's Rewards Bazaar send.

## What's weak

- "Beauty's Biggest Hits" as hero copy tells me nothing — no category, no name-check of what's actually new. Glossier's "You smell rich" hook beat this by miles for specificity.
- Zero demographic signal — no models, no skin-tone range, nothing that says "this is for you" versus any other Sephora shopper. Just product-on-white-background after product-on-white-background.
- It's a wall of unrelated products (lip balm, cologne, body butter, foundation, lipstick, eyeshadow palette) with no organizing logic — feels like an inventory dump, not a curated edit.
- This is my fifth or sixth Sephora email logged this week between the two K18s, the two 50%-off sends, and yesterday's Rewards Bazaar — the cadence is genuinely wearing on me at this point, Sally Beauty levels of repetition.
- The 15% off shampoo/conditioner subscribe offer and the Kérastase Holiday Sets banner are crammed into the bottom half with almost as much visual weight as the hero — nothing tells me which of these three offers I should actually care about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "Beauty's Biggest Hits" with something that names the actual headline product or category — lead with Charlotte Tilbury by name in the hero, not four screens later. That's your strongest asset here.
- 2. Cut the product grid down to a curated 4-6 items tied to a theme (new launches, or "your Rouge picks") instead of nine random SKUs with no connective tissue.
- 3. Move the loyalty tier bar and 5X points banner higher — up near the hero, not buried above the footer where most people have already stopped scrolling.
- 4. Pick one offer to be the star of the bottom section (Kérastase 24-hour window has real urgency) and downgrade the other two to a smaller secondary strip.
- 5. Personalize the subject line with something that reflects my Rouge status or a specific launch, not generic "most wanted."
