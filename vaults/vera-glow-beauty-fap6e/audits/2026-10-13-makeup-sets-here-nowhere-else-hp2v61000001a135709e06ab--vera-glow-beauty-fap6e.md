- "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA + LIMITED EDITION" repeats on every tile, so the exclusivity pitch is consistent.
- Brand names are visible on each tile (Makeup by Mario, Dior, NARS, YSL Beauty, Charlotte Tilbury, ONE/SIZE by Patrick Starrr). That's curation I can scan in seconds.
- The "$32 / $47 value" style callouts let me judge the deal without clicking.
- The gold-ribbon gift styling and the "Get Gift Ideas Instantly" and "$35 and Under" modules fit mid-October holiday shopping.

## What's weak

- Nothing shows my Rouge status or what these buy in points. Ulta would at least tease Ultamate Rewards points on a set like this.
- The Fragrance Mini block at the top muddies the hero. My eye lands on a perfume before it reaches "Makeup They'll Love."
- It's about eight stacked tiles with the same layout. After the third, I'm just scrolling.
- There's no real deadline. "Limited edition" isn't urgency, and I can't tell when the free shipping or the sets run out.
- The "value" figures are the only savings shown. I can't tell if the $32 price is a discount or just a bundle.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
