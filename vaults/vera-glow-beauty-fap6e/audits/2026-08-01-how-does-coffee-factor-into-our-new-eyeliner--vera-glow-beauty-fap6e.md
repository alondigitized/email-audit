- Okay, ILIA is on a real content streak now — first the launch page (7/28), then the "last day" urgency send (7/26)... wait, chronologically this one and the "waterproof" ingredient deep-dive (7/30) are basically twins, and this is the more interesting of the two because it's told through an actual Q&A with the Packaging Development Manager instead of generic copy. Coffee chaff repurposed into the pencil barrel is a genuinely cool sustainability story and I like that they let Kristin HooSang explain it in her own words instead of slapping "SUSTAINABLE" in a banner.
- That said, this is squarely a brand-story email, not a shopping email, and I already know that about ILIA by now — they've sent me an origin story, a launch page, a vacation-photo email, and a "last day" push all in the same two-week window. The "4X VIP points" flag at the very top is doing the real commercial work here, buried above a hero that's 90% coffee-bean aesthetic.
- I'd read this on my commute because the coffee-chaff hook is genuinely interesting trivia, but I'm not clicking through to buy today — I already saw the full launch page two days ago. This is a "keep the tab open, forward the coffee-bean fact to my friend who's obsessed with clean beauty ingredients" email, not a "shop now" email.

## What's working

- The coffee-bean-to-eyeliner Q&A format is a fresh beat ILIA hasn't used yet this stretch — it's not another flat-lay or GWP countdown.
- "4X VIP points" at the very top is the one concrete loyalty hook I actually care about as a Rouge/Diamond member, even if it's small.
- Real employee headshot and name (Kristin HooSang) builds more trust than stock copy — feels like an actual person, not a marketing template.
- The waterline/sensitive-eyes/24-hour-wear benefit list near the bottom is clean and skimmable, and it's the part that actually sells me on wearing this.
- Product-in-hand macro shot near the end shows pigment and application, which is more useful to me than the earlier coffee-bean stock photo.

## What's weak

- The hero image is just an artsy swirl of packaging with "grounds for innovation" — cute pun, but it takes real scrolling before I even see the product clearly.
- This is basically a rehash of the "waterproof eyeliner" ingredient story from 7/30 — different angle (packaging vs. formula) but same "let an ILIA employee explain the science" format two sends in a row. It's starting to feel like a content strategy rather than four distinct reasons to open.
- Two identical "SHOP THRU LINE" CTAs and nothing else — no swatch chart, no "shop in your shade" like the 7/28 launch email had.
- Zero personalization to me specifically — no shade recommendation, no "you bought X, this pairs with it," despite ILIA presumably having my purchase history.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the product benefit, not the pun — put "24-hour wear, waterproof, safe for the waterline" copy block near the TOP, then let the coffee story be the "why," not the whole email.
- 2. Cut this to one Q&A exchange instead of three — the sustainability story is interesting but three back-to-back interview blocks is a lot of vertical scroll for a promotional email.
- 3. Add a shade/swatch strip like the 7/28 launch send had — I still don't know what shades exist or which one matches me.
- 4. Make the 4X VIP points banner louder — right now it's tiny top-of-email text; Sephora's Beauty Insider Celebration send (8/1, scored 8/10) proves a big bold loyalty badge is what actually gets me to open.
