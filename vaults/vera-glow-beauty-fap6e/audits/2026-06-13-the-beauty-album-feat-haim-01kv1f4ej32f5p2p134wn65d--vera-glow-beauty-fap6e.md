# The Beauty Album feat. HAIM
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- Four. This is send number four in the HAIM x ILIA arc in five days, and I want to be transparent about where my thumb is when this one hits: I'm fatigued. Not annoyed exactly — ILIA executes too well to get annoyed — but I've already done the June 9 launch, the June 11 Este quote drop, the June 13 GRWM walkthrough, and now this "beauty album" frame. Each one has been visually cohesive and on-brand, but the arc is officially running out of new things to tell me.
- That said: the editorial concept here is genuinely distinctive. Three-panel magazine spread, each HAIM member gets her own quote and photo moment, the "the beauty album" headline in serif italic is gorgeous. ILIA is treating this like a Rolling Stone spread, not a promotional blast — and I respect the creative commitment. The giveaway hook ("Win A Signed Vinyl + $500") at the bottom is the one piece of new value I haven't seen in the prior sends, which gives me at least one reason to scroll.
- Would I click? Maybe — to enter the giveaway. Would I click to shop? Probably not, because there's no price anchor or loyalty hook anywhere in this email. Compare that to the Glossier fragrance sale I got recently — that email landed with "save up to 25% off" plastered in the hero, and I had a reason to convert. This one is beautiful editorial content asking me to feel something, not buy something. Mark as read unless I want that vinyl.

## What's working

- **The magazine format is genuinely ownable.** "The beauty album" with three sisters getting individual close-up portraits and quotes reads like a curated editorial, not a promo blast — nobody else in my inbox this week is doing this.
- **HAIM's faces are a demo signal match.** Young millennial women, natural skin, the kind of makeup that reads effortless-but-ILIA — I feel addressed, not ignored.
- **The giveaway ($500 + signed vinyl) is the right move at this arc stage.** Four emails in, adding a sweepstakes gives me a fresh engagement hook. Smart pivot from "here's the product" to "here's a reason to interact even if you already own it."
- **Clean layout, zero render issues.** White background, serif type, the photo sections are all intact. After the Charlotte Tilbury "everything at once" chaos or Drunk Elephant's dense grids, this scrolls like breathing.
- **Three-member structure is smartly egalitarian.** Alana, Este, Danielle each get a quote and visual real estate — it avoids making this feel like another Este feature, which the June 11 email already was.

## What's weak

- **No purchase offer anywhere.** Not a single dollar off, not a free-with-purchase, not a "Rouge members get early access" hook. I do the points math every time I open an email — when there's nothing to calculate, I move on faster. Glossier gave me 25% off fragrance in their last send. Sally Beauty had a BOGO lined up. ILIA is asking me to buy on vibes alone by email four.
- **This is the fourth send on the same creative concept in five days.** The arc was brilliant at launch and peaked with the Este godsend quote subject. Arriving here, the diminishing-returns wall is real — even a great magazine issue doesn't publish four covers of the same shoot in one week.
- **No loyalty or tier recognition.** As a Sephora Rouge customer who has bought ILIA at Sephora, there's nothing here that signals ILIA knows who I am. Compare to the Sephora "STARTS TODAY" email that surfaced Rouge-tier early access right in the hero — ILIA has the relationship data via Sephora and isn't using it.
- **The giveaway is buried below three full content sections.** By the time I scroll to the vinyl prize, I'm already past the purchase CTAs. If the giveaway is the scroll-stopper, it should be closer to the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one concrete offer to reactivate the arc.** By send four, ILIA should introduce a limited-edition bundle, a GWP tied to the collab products, or a loyalty member perk. Something I can act on with my Rouge points. The editorial alone can't carry four consecutive sends without a transaction layer.
- 2. **Elevate the giveaway to the first CTA below the hero.** Right now I have to scroll past all three sisters to find the vinyl. The giveaway is the freshest element in this email — let it breathe above the fold or immediately below the hero.
- 3. **Tighten the cadence to three sends max for any collab arc.** The June 9 launch + June 11 quote + one consolidated "the beauty album" finale would have been a perfect three-act structure. The GRWM email and this one are two sends competing for the same territory.
- 4. **Surface a "for members" signal.** Even a small "ILIA at Sephora — apply your points" line or a "Rouge exclusive" label on one SKU would have made this feel personalized rather than broadcast.
