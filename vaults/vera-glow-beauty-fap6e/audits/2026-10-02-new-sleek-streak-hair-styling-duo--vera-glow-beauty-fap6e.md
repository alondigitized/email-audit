# NEW! Sleek Streak hair styling duo 💛💚 
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh e.l.f. send I've logged since 9/28, and the hero is a good one. The purple background with the lime-green kit looks more prestige than the glitter-stick chaos from earlier in the week. "Only $12" for a travel-size oil and anti-frizz spray is a clean anchor, and it's the same price-point play as the 9/29 Glass Slick Serum Stick email. But the email is long. The hero is strong, then I scroll through a giant oil-dropper image and a hair-texture photo with emoji bullets before I hit a second CTA.
- Then the email pivots to a free full-size Clear Brow & Lash Mascara with a $50+ spend. That's a different offer from the hero, and it's buried below the first fold. My points brain lights up here. I can't tell whether the mascara gift stacks with the $12 duo, and the fine print is tiny. The "Top Picks for You" row is a primer, a lip liner and a setting spray. None of those are hair, so the personalization misses the launch entirely.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already building a cart. I'd save it for when I'm adding to an e.l.f. order, not forward it to a friend. It reads as a decent launch email that doesn't give me a reason to act today.

## What's working

- The purple hero with the lime kit pops. "new!" is stamped on it, and the product is the first thing I see.
- "Only $12" in the headline gives me a price without scrolling.
- The kit-includes block names both products, so I know what I'm getting without clicking.
- The free-mascara-on-$50+ gift is a real hook, and "no code needed" removes friction.
- The cruelty-free, vegan and certified badges near the bottom are a trust cue I notice.

## What's weak

- The oil-dropper image and the hair-texture photo take up almost a full screen each. The "spray repels humidity" and "oil strengthens hair" text on the photo is pretty but light on information.
- There are two "Shop now" CTAs for the duo and a third for the mascara, with nothing to say which one matters most.
- "Top Picks for You" shows a primer, a liner and a setting spray. That contradicts "for you" when the whole email is about hair.
- The category nav (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) is just blank gray bars on my render. It looks broken.
- There's no member or points tie-in beyond a small "earn rewards" line at the bottom. A Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond shopper expects some points math in the email, and I can't do any here.
- The claims are footnoted with an asterisk ("*Versus control"), and the fine print for the mascara offer is microscopic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the middle section. Keep the hero, the kit-includes block and one CTA, then go straight to the mascara gift. The email is about three times longer than the offer needs.
- 2. Make the personalization real. If someone is clicking a hair launch, "Top Picks for You" should show hair products or at least a hair-adjacent bundle.
- 3. Fix the category bars so they render with labels or icons, not blank gray blocks.
- 4. Say plainly whether the $12 duo counts toward the $50 mascara threshold. That's the math I'm doing in my head.
- 5. Add one social proof element, such as a star rating or "X sold" on the kit. Sephora and Ulta always give me reviews on a launch.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Structurally sound Braze/Bee-style email (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, preheader, mobile stack styles). The QA run flagged 5 link failures, a 0-char plain-text part and a missing alt attribute. All 5 link failures share one cause that looks like probe behavior, not a confirmed broken link. Header-level checks (auth, List-Unsubscribe) could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 FAIL results, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The affected links are:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (2 links: "Shop Now" and "Power Grip Primer")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&…&bxid=6a013826e53…`
- **Likely cause:** the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` click-tracker returns a relative `Location` header, and the prober doesn't resolve it against the origin. Browsers resolve relative redirects correctly, so these are probably false positives. The data doesn't confirm either way. Re-probe with relative-redirect resolution before treating them as broken.
- **Relative redirect targets:** if the tracker really emits host-less `Location` values, it is non-standard. Some strict clients and scanners may mishandle it.
- **Truncated URL:** the mobile-app URL in the QA output cuts off at `bxid=6a013826e53`. This is likely a display truncation, not a defect.
- **Coverage:** only 12 of 38 links were probed, so 26 are unverified.
- **Missing alt text:** one image has no `alt`. Its src is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/uSUfF_oTHzYAJHqiyOKLgg~~/AAQRxRA~/A_-7gcvOk1cO…`. The `/q/` path suggests a tracking or open pixel, which would make this low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** this affects accessibility and web-view rendering.
- **Invalid font-family:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the mobile_hide block has an empty font name. It falls through to Verdana and is harmless, but it is an invalid declaration.
- **Pixel alt text:** the alt-less image above should carry `alt=""` if it is a pixel.
- **Plain-text part:** the QA reports 0 chars (see section 5).
- **Fine:** the primary logo image has `alt="e.l.f."`, the viewport meta is present, and tables use `role="presentation"`.
- **Nested tables:** `nl-container` and `row-1` are duplicated across nested tables. The source is truncated, so this can't be assessed further.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain text:** `[FAIL]` text version is 0 chars. A missing text/plain part hurts deliverability and accessibility. It may also be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** `[WARN]` neither header was found. The QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they really are absent, Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules (RFC 8058 one-click) are not met.
- **Authentication-Results:** `[WARN]` not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters are not visible on any link.** All hrefs are opaque tracker URLs. The only UTM data seen is in the resolved mobile-app target: `utm_medium=email`, `c=email`, `af_xp=email`, `shortlink=email`, `bxid=…`. It has no `utm_source` or `utm_campaign` in the captured portion.
- **Primary CTA target:** the "Shop Now" links resolve to `/products/power-grip-primer` and `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner`. These are not the Sleek Streak hair duo named in the subject and preheader. Whether the email's featured product is linked correctly can't be confirmed from the probed links.
- **Login link:** the receipt-scanning link goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`, a login wall rather than a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run link QA with relative `Location` resolution. Confirm the 5 "unknown url type" results are probe artifacts before changing anything.
2. Probe the remaining 26 links, especially the Sleek Streak hero/CTA links, which are absent from the probed set.
3. Inspect the raw MIME for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the text/plain part. If any are truly missing, fix them at the ESP.
4. Populate `<title>` and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
5. Remove the empty `font-family:''` declaration.
6. Add `utm_source` and `utm_campaign` to destination URLs if analytics attribution is expected.
