# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Skechers again, and my first reaction opening this is just "why am I still getting these." I track Sephora Rouge points and Ulta Diamond tier in my head without even trying, and this email has literally nothing for me — no beauty content, no launch-day anything, just three pairs of sneakers I apparently "liked," one of which is a toddler-pink Care Bears shoe with a heart on it. I don't shop Skechers with any regularity, so a subject line screaming "Your cart expires soon!" over items I never actually put in a cart feels like a swing and a miss before I even open the picture.
- The 25% off Skechers Plus code is the only thing here that's remotely a real offer, and it's buried two-thirds down the page under three rows of "best seller" thumbnails I don't care about. Compare that to how Sephora handled their Beauty Insider Celebration send on 7/22 — clean, dated, told me exactly what to expect and when. This one just dumps product tiles at me and hopes something sticks.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding, straight to archive. This is the fourth or fifth near-identical Skechers cart/browse-abandon email I've seen in three weeks (the July 16th "Your Items Are Waiting!" and July 21st "Did you forget something?" both hit the same beat), and it's not landing any better this time.

## What's working

- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is at least unambiguous — I know exactly what happens if I tap it.
- The Skechers Plus 25% off $100+ callout is a real, dated offer (valid 7/22–7/27), not vague filler.
- Render is clean — no overlapping text, no broken images, product shots are crisp.
- Loyalty math is visible if you're a member (code SAVINGS, threshold clearly stated) — I appreciate when a brand shows its work like Ulta usually does with vault cash.

## What's weak

- Subject says "cart expires soon" but the hero says "YOU LIKED THESE" — there's no actual cart shown anywhere. That's the same disconnect I flagged in Skechers' July 21st "Did you forget something?" send: the subject promises urgency the body doesn't deliver.
- Zero relevance to me. I'm not a Skechers shopper and there's nothing here — no beauty tie-in, no lifestyle framing, nothing — to make this feel like it was sent to a real person versus a list.
- The Care Bears kids' pink sneaker in the product carousel is a jarring demographic miss for a 29-year-old browsing grown-up sneakers, let alone beauty products.
- This is at least the fourth Skechers "you forgot something" flavored email I've gotten this month — Glossier ran twelve emails since July 7th but at least kept evolving the mechanic each time; Skechers is just recycling the same cart-abandon shell.
- No product detail (sizing, fit, restock) visible for anything shown, so even if I were interested there's real friction before I could act.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — either show an actual cart with items and a countdown, or drop the "expires soon" framing entirely and lean into "restock alert" or "back in stock," which is honest to what's actually being shown.
- 2. Move the 25% off Skechers Plus offer up top, above the product carousel — right now it's the strongest asset on the page and it's buried.
- 3. Cut the Care Bears kids' shoe from an adult browse-recovery send — segment kids' product recs separately so the demographic match doesn't feel random.
- 4. Add basic product detail (available sizes, "back in stock") under at least the hero shoe — nobody re-engages with a product they can't confirm is available in their size.
- 5. Slow the cadence — three near-identical "you left something" emails in three weeks trains people to stop opening, the same trap Glossier is dancing around but managing better with mechanic variety.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still deciding on these? Sizes are moving`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off for Skechers Plus — ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Slip-ins and Arch Fit picks, plus a members-only discount`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Complete your order before these sell out`
