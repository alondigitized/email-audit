# There’s a new Balm Keychain in town
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a cute product drop. It's the first Glossier send in a while that isn't about the free-gift pile-on. After 10/22 "Want a free gift?", 10/24 "Get Milky Jelly Cleanser, free!" and 10/26 "Last call for your free gift!", I'd braced for a fourth Milky Jelly reminder. A balm keychain is a different beat, and the layout is clean. But it's a $-less email. I don't see a price on the keychain, no points angle and no reason to buy today.
- The real offer, the free Three Eyed Smiley Mug at $75+, is buried at the bottom with an asterisk. That's the actual hook, since I'm already doing math on whether my cart clears $75. Sephora would put that threshold up top. I'd click the keychain out of curiosity, maybe, but I wouldn't forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a keychain with a balm screwed in, on a clean background. I get the product in two seconds.
- The "So, how does it work?" FAQ block is a smart, honest way to explain a gadget-type product.
- The Holiday Favorites tiles name the shade and finish ("Subtle copper shimmer", "Mauve tint"). That's the product detail I actually care about.
- The brand voice is consistent and playful. The smiley and wave sign-off feel like Glossier.
- "STILL ON" on the mug is a clear signal that it's a continuing offer.

## What's weak

- There's no price anywhere on the keychain, so I can't tell if it's a $12 add-on or a $30 collectible.
- Every CTA says "Shop now", five times. It's not specific, and four of them don't say what they link to.
- The mug offer is the only thing that moves my cart total, and it sits in the last block with "*Terms apply" in tiny print at the very bottom.
- There's no member or loyalty angle. Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond emails at least acknowledge my tier. This treats me like a stranger.
- The Holiday Favorites tiles are two unrelated products. The lip close-up is pretty but has no clear link to Just Baked beyond the caption.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the money. Put the mug and the $75 threshold in a banner above the hero, and put a price on the keychain.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Get the keychain", "Shop Toasty Nuts", "Claim my free mug".
- 3. Move the "*Terms apply" detail closer to the mug offer so the deal feels honest.
- 4. Add a "keychain + balm" bundle price. That gives me a reason to hit $75 and unlock the mug.
- 5. Add something for members, even a points multiplier.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Balm Keychain is here + a free mug at $75`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clip your balm on. New Keychain.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: spend $75, get a free Smiley Mug. Ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Screw it on, clip it, go. Keychain + balm from $__.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Stray tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M65176PWRZXQ6P7CB0Q6GRGG_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid attributes on a `<span>`. It's inert and has no user impact, but it looks like a Klaviyo/Stripo injection artifact.
