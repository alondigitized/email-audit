# $10 off your $50 purchase⁺ or $20 off your $100 purchase⁺ in store or online (Terms & excl. apply) ends today!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- OK so I've been watching this exact offer drop three times in four days — the $10/$20 tiered spend-and-save that first showed up on May 21st as the Memorial Day kickoff (twice, in fact, once for "Marisol" and once for "Rae" in back-to-back sends I clocked immediately). Now here it is again on Sunday evening as a "friendly reminder," and the notable regression is that Ulta stripped the personalized name from the subject entirely. The 5/21 sends had names right up front; this one opens cold, as if I've never shopped there in my life, let alone spent enough to be Diamond. That's a miss when the whole point of "friendly reminder" framing is supposed to feel like they know me.
- That said — my Diamond brain is still doing the math. $20 off a $100 basket is a clean 20%, the barcode is front and center and renders correctly, and the free mystery gift add-on is a genuinely smart threshold sweetener. If I've been sitting on a cart this week, this closes me. The email works mechanically. It just doesn't make me feel anything.
- Would I click? Possibly — if I already had items in my cart. Would I forward it to a friend? No. Nothing here is curated or discoverable. Compare this to ILIA's 14-email "Layers of free gifts" campaign, which by email five was actually making me *want* to learn what was in each layer. This is a coupon blast, start to finish. Mark as read unless I'm actively shopping.

## What's working

- The $10 and $20 offer tiles are immediately parseable at a glance — the tiered math is clear and I don't have to scroll or read fine print to understand the deal
- The barcode is prominent and renders cleanly, which matters for in-store Diamond pickups — this is genuinely useful and Ulta does this better than Sephora's in-app experience
- "Free mystery gift" is a nice add sitting right below the coupon — creates incremental urgency without feeling aggressive
- "Ends today" wrapped in "friendly reminder" framing is softer than Charlotte Tilbury's "20% OFF!! Now Is The Time!" energy (eight sends in ten days, for the record) — Ulta is being warmer here
- Product grid renders without broken images or overlapping text

## What's weak

- No name personalization anywhere — the May 21 versions led with "Marisol," and "Rae," in the subject itself; dropping it for the closing send is a counterintuitive regression, especially on a "reminder" tone email
- The subject line is a full sentence, not a subject line — over 100 characters with "(Terms & excl. apply)" buried inside it kills mobile scanability
- The product grid is a warehouse dump, not a curated edit — e.l.f. gave me a single hero product (Jelly Pop Glow Stick, free, on a Sunday) and I knew exactly what I was getting; Ulta shows me everything and therefore nothing
- Third send of the same offer in four days — the cadence fatigue is real, and I say this as someone who *likes* Ulta promo math
- Zero Diamond-tier specificity in the body — no "your points stack on top of this," no tier callout, nothing that signals they know I'm a high-value customer vs. a first-time opener

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the name back in the subject** — the 5/21 send had it, the closer send dropping it is a step backward that undercuts the "reminder" conceit entirely
- 2. **Cut the subject to under 50 chars** — strip "in store or online" and lose the parenthetical; let the "ends today" do the urgency work alone
- 3. **Add a single Diamond-specific line in the hero section** — even "Diamond members: this stacks with your points" turns a generic blast into a retention signal
- 4. **Pick three hero products** — curate a tight "use it on these" edit instead of the warehouse grid; give me a reason to land somewhere specific rather than just the homepage
- 5. **Move the mystery gift above the fold** — it's a conversion lever that's doing most of the incremental work; it deserves top-half placement
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vera, your $20 off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: $10 or $20 off your haul`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free mystery gift when you spend $30 — use in-store or online`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Diamond members: your spend-and-save coupon expires today`
