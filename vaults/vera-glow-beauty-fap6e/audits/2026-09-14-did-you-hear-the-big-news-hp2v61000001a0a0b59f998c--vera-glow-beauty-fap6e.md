## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" is STILL wrong under my Rouge status — third-plus send this week I'm flagging this exact bug, and it's actively insulting when I'm sitting on real Rouge points.
- Zero shoppable products — every card is a category/ingredient, not an item I can tap and buy, so there's no clear path from "interested" to "cart."
- No pricing, no member perks, no launch-day urgency — this is pure content marketing with no commerce hook, which is a missed opportunity for a database this size.
- The "Heart Journey" CSR block at the bottom (store team photo + annual report link) feels like a random tonal shift after all the ingredient science — I don't need Sephora's impact report in the same email as spicules.

- 3. Cut the "Heart Journey" CSR section entirely or move it to its own dedicated send — it clashes with the K-beauty editorial tone and reads like two different emails stapled together.
