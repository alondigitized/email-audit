# Stacking Weekend ends tomorrow 😳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- Seven. That is how many Stacking Weekend emails have hit my inbox since July 1st — and Sephora just sent the same subject line *twice today*. I already reviewed "Stacking Weekend ends tomorrow 😳" this morning and scored it a 7. Now here's the identical sender name, identical emoji, different creative underneath. That's not a strategy, that's a suppression failure.
- Inside, the bones are actually fine: the hero leads with the sale offer, the product grid surfaces multiple categories including what looks like a "get up close and personal" eye section and a grab-a-deal block, and the Sephora aesthetic is clean and consistent — no broken images, no layout chaos. If this were email two or three in the series, I'd scroll through and probably add the Drunk Elephant Protini to my cart for the fourth time.
- But it's not email two. And the open only happened because I auditing inboxes professionally right now. Real-inbox Vera sees "Sephora Sale" + "ends tomorrow 😳" *again* and leaves it unread until she genuinely needs to confirm the sale is still live. I'm not clicking. I'm not forwarding. I'm marking this as "Sephora sent the same subject twice on Day 6" and closing the tab.

## What's working

- **Hero-first layout** — the offer hits above the fold; no digging required to find the sale hook
- **Multiple product categories in the scroll** — the "Get Up Close and Personal" section and the grab-a-deal grid show editorial range, not just a homepage redirect
- **Clean execution** — no render bugs, palette is consistent, product imagery is sharp; same quality bar as the July 5th sends
- **Urgency is actually real this time** — unlike Glossier's "ends tonight" that quietly lived nine extra days, Sephora's deadline is genuine, which is worth something

## What's weak

- **Duplicate subject on the same day** — this is the same subject line as the earlier July 6 send, already reviewed and scored a 7. This is a suppression or segmentation miss, not a strategic resend
- **Zero Rouge personalization** — seven emails deep and I still see nothing that tells me what the stacking math looks like *for a Rouge member*. ILIA made their "Buy 3 Save 20%" feel like it was written for their loyalists; Sephora is mass-broadcasting to a customer they know extremely well
- **Product grid is still catalog mode on a deadline day** — Drunk Elephant and ILIA both understood the "final send = single hero + single CTA" assignment. Dense grids work on launch day (July 1st earned its 9); by day six the scroll is just friction
- **The 😳 emoji has no juice left** — it read as playful in the first email this morning; appearing twice in one day from the same brand on the same subject makes it look like an accident

## Recommendations

- 1. **Suppress this send for anyone who already received the same subject today** — this is the single biggest fix and it's purely operational. Sending Rouge members the same headline twice in one day trains them to tune out, and these are $500+/year customers
- 2. **Inject tier-specific hero copy for the final day** — "Rouge: here's your stacking math before midnight" would have me opening this immediately. Sephora literally knows my points balance. Use it
- 3. **Collapse to a single editorial hero for the close** — the launch email gets the full catalog, the closer gets one hero product and one CTA. Pick something scarce, lean in, let me feel the deadline
- 4. **Retire the 😳 after first use today** — second use reads as a copy-paste error, not urgency

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `robots` meta value malformed:**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
`no index` with a space is not a valid directive. Correct value is `noindex`. Most crawlers will ignore the malformed directive and **may index the email web-view** if one exists.

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` on viewport:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents OS-level pinch-zoom in mobile browsers. Fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Standard email practice, but flags as an accessibility risk.

**Issue — `text-size-adjust: none` applied globally:**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to `body` and nearly every element selector. Suppresses the browser's automatic text scaling for small-screen readers. Acceptable on narrow selectors; applying to `body` is overly broad.

**No issues found:** DOCTYPE, charset declaration (`utf-8`), VML namespace for Outlook, IE Edge compatibility meta, and media-query breakpoint structure are all standard and correct.
