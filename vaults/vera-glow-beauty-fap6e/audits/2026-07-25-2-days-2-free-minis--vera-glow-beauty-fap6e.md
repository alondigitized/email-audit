# 2 days. 2 free minis.
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA just sent me what looks like a plain-text email that forgot it was allowed to have a design. No hero image, no product photography, no button — just a wall of black text on white with some bolded phrases and blue hyperlinks. For a brand whose entire aesthetic is "clean girl minimalism," I get that pared-back can be intentional, but this reads like the HTML failed to load, not like a considered choice.
- The offer itself is actually decent — free Overglaze Hydrating Lip Gloss mini with any order, plus the cult-favorite ILIA Mini Tote back with a $75+ spend — and "back by popular demand" on the tote is a nice touch since I remember people going feral for that thing last time. But there's zero visual reason to stop scrolling. Compare this to e.l.f.'s Soft Glam Cream Blush email (10/10 in my inbox this month) which had actual product shots and color swatches, or Drunk Elephant's "Feeling sticky?" send with its ocean-spray hero — those emails look like a brand invested in the moment. This one looks like a fallback plain-text version that got sent as the primary.
- I wouldn't forward this to a friend — there's nothing visually shareable here. I'd probably still click through because the GWP math works for me (free gloss + free tote at $75 is an easy add-to-cart nudge), but I'd mark it read fast and this is the first ILIA send in this whole eleven-day sprint that actually made me wonder if something broke on their end.

## What's working

- The two-tier GWP structure (free gloss on any order, free tote at $75+) gives me a concrete reason to hit a spend threshold, which is exactly how I shop anyway.
- "Back by popular demand" on the Mini Tote is a real signal — I remember that item being a hype piece, so the callback lands.
- The bulleted product list (Crown Affair x ILIA collab, Skin Tint + Finishing Powder Duo, Eye Stylus Trio) at least gives me multiple entry points to click if the gloss/tote isn't enough on its own.
- "2 days" urgency in the subject is honest and matches the "for the next 48 hours only" copy in the body — no bait and switch there.

## What's weak

- There is no image anywhere in this email — no product shot of the lip gloss, the tote, or any of the four linked launches. For a color-cosmetics brand this is a dealbreaker; I have no idea what "Enamel" pink looks like.
- Zero visual hierarchy — everything is the same font, same size, with bold used almost randomly. My eye doesn't land anywhere near an "offer."
- No CTA button. It's just inline blue links buried in paragraphs, which on mobile means I'm tap-targeting a few words of text, not a thumb-friendly button.
- No branding at all — no logo, no color, nothing that visually says "ILIA" until I hit "The ILIA Team" sign-off at the very bottom.
- Compares badly to this brand's own recent work — the 07/24 "goes well with free gloss" email scored 8/10 in my read specifically because it showed me products styled with the gift. This is the same gift, minus every visual asset that made that one work.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add actual product photography — the gloss swatch, the tote, and at least a flat-lay of the four new launches. This is the single biggest lift available; right now this email could be mistaken for a broken render.
- 2. Build a real CTA button ("Shop Now" or "Claim Your Minis") above the fold instead of making me tap inline text links.
- 3. Lead with the free gloss visual before the copy — "picture the perfect pink" needs an actual picture.
- 4. Tighten the product list into a 2x2 image grid with prices, not a plain bulleted list — I want to see the Crown Affair towel, not just read about it.
- 5. Add a countdown or explicit end-date/time instead of just "next 48 hours" — Sally Beauty's flash sale format did this well with a clear deadline stamp.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free pink mini disappears in 48 hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free gloss + the tote is back (2 days only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: the ILIA Mini Tote returns at $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New summer launches to fill your cart with`

# Technical Audit: ILIA "2 days. 2 free minis." Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Email is a standard Klaviyo-generated campaign (list ID `01KYCW9E1Z1Q0ZAG5XKQWSGTDA`) with correct preheader/MSO handling, but automated QA flags 4 broken-link failures and 2 missing compliance headers that warrant verification.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All 5 content links (`_0`–`_3` product links, `_4` unsubscribe) route through Klaviyo's click-tracking redirector `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01KYCW9E1Z1Q0ZAG5XKQWSGTDA_N` — expected behavior, not a defect.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01KYCW9E1Z1Q0ZAG5XKQWSGTDA` (1x1, correctly hidden via CSS).
- **QA-reported "broken links" are almost certainly a tooling artifact, not real breakage.** All 4 FAIL entries end in a bare `=` (e.g. `https://iliabeauty.co=`, `.../product=`, `.../products/the-=`, `...unsubscribe?a=3DSguDsz&=`), and one contains `=3D` — the quoted-printable encoding for a literal `=`. This is the signature of a QA parser reading raw MIME source **without decoding quoted-printable soft line breaks (`=\n`)**, truncating any URL that happened to wrap at 76 characters. The `iliabeauty.co` DNS failure and the `kmail-lists.com` 404 are both explained by this truncation. **Recommend re-running the link checker against QP-decoded HTML before treating these as real 404s/DNS failures.**
- All links carry `rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"` — `nofollow` is Klaviyo's default for tracked links; not a defect, just informational (no SEO equity passed through, irrelevant for email delivery).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- Web font `@import` is correctly scoped inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->...<!--<![endif]-->`, preventing Outlook from attempting to load it — no issue.
- No `alt` text on the 4 product links (they're text links, not images) — N/A.

- Footer content uses a layout `<table>` without `role="presentation"` — minor a11y nitpick; screen readers may announce it as a data table.

- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): **not found** — status unknown. Per QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping/not capturing the header rather than an actual authentication failure; cannot confirm pass/fail from this data alone.
