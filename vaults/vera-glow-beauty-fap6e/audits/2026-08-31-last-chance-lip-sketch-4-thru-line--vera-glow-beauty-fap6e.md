# Last chance—Lip Sketch 4 Thru Line
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "last chance" energy actually lands here because it's tied to a real GWP mechanic — free mini Lip Sketch with any Thru Line waterproof eyeliner order — not some vague sitewide discount. The eye close-up hero is gorgeous and on-brand for ILIA's whole "clean beauty, good skin" aesthetic, and I like that they immediately show me the actual product lineup (10 new eyeliner shades) instead of making me hunt. But this is also the third eyeliner/lip-combo GWP beat I've seen from ILIA this week — "On everyone's lips" (8/26) had the dual lip macro, "A gift—line for line" (8/24) had basically this same "line for line" GWP mechanic, and now this is round three of "buy a liner, get a lip thing free." I'm starting to feel the cadence fatigue.
- I'd click through on this one, mostly because the "10 new shades" hook is genuinely new information and I do actually need a waterproof liner for fall. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, though — nothing here is surprising enough to text someone about, and my group chat already got the Crown Affair one from 8/28.

## What's working

- The last-chance badge plus "tonight" deadline in the body copy actually gives me a credible reason to act now, unlike Sally Beauty's COLORfest badge I've now seen slapped on the same layout for a month straight.
- Showing me the full 10-shade liner rack as a product shot immediately after the hero is smart — I can actually see what I'm buying before I click.
- The ingredient/benefit callouts (24-hour wear, waterproof, safe for waterline / sensitive lips) speak directly to how I actually shop — I read claims before I buy, every time.
- "In safe hands" clean-beauty closer with the cruelty-free/Leaping Bunny/1% for the Planet badges is a legit trust cue that neither Sephora nor Sally bother to include in their sends.
- ILIA VIP mention at the very bottom is a nice touch, though buried — more on that below.

## What's weak

- This is the third "buy a liner/lip product, get a mini free" GWP send in about a week — 8/24's "line for line," 8/26's dual-lip macro, and now this. It's the same trick with a new coat of paint.
- Zero mention of my ILIA VIP status or points anywhere near the offer — it's tucked as a tiny icon in the footer strip, not integrated into the actual CTA. Compare that to Ulta, which at least puts "Marisol" and the coupon math right in the subject line — heavy-handed, sure, but it feels personalized in a way this doesn't.
- The "More That Draws the Eye" cross-sell grid (six extra products) dilutes the actual last-chance urgency — by the time I scroll past mascara, brow pencil, concealer, and eye cream, I've forgotten this was supposed to be a deadline email.
- No visible price or specific %/$ savings anywhere — I know I get a "free mini" but I don't know what dollar value that actually is, so the offer feels a little soft next to Sephora's flat 50%-off framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a dollar value on the free gift right in the hero ("$15 value, free") — vague "free gift" undersells it next to Sephora's explicit 50%-off math.
- 2. Cut the cross-sell grid or move it below a hard fold — it's stepping on the urgency of the actual promo.
- 3. Surface my VIP tier/points math near the "Shop to Claim" button instead of burying it in the footer — I am always doing the points math, and this email gave me nothing to calculate.
- 4. Vary the GWP mechanic itself next send — three "free lip mini with liner" emails in a week is starting to blur together in my mind the way Sally Beauty's COLORfest confetti dots have.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: free Lip Sketch ($15 value) with any liner`
- **Subject Alt B:** `10 new liner shades + your free mini, gone at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `ILIA VIP members: your free mini ships automatically.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Waterproof, 24-hour wear, safe for sensitive eyes — shop before it's gone.`
