- Okay, "keep it between us... our secret sale is for you" — I audited this exact "secret VIP sale" bit from Drunk Elephant yesterday (9/4, "Well damn, 20% off," DRUNKVIP20) and here it is again with the identical code and identical 20%-off mechanic, just a different headline dressed on top. - The one genuinely new thing here is the "Summer Ending? Not on our watch" panel — a $36-value five-piece gift with orders $110+, which is a real added incentive on top of the 20% off, and I'll give them credit for that layering. But between this and 8/26's "Best time for 20% off" SheerID gate, and 8/31's countdown-timer version of the same 20%, I'm now three-plus sends deep on Drunk Elephant just running the same discount number in different wrapping paper.
- I'd click through for the gift-with-purchase since I'm already sitting on points math for a $110 order, but I'm not forwarding this one — nothing here is exciting enough to text a friend about, and the "between us" bit reads a little worn out at this point.

## What's working

- The gift-with-purchase panel is a legit incremental offer stacked on the 20% off, not just a re-skin — that's more than Drunk Elephant usually gives me.
- Product grid at the bottom shows star ratings and "X people are checking this out" social proof, which is a trust cue I actually register.
- Clean single-column layout, no overlapping text or broken images, easy to scan on my phone without zooming.
- "SHOP NOW" button is unambiguous and sits right under the offer — no hunting for the CTA.

## What's weak

- The core 20% off / DRUNKVIP20 mechanic is a straight repeat of 9/4's "Well damn, 20% off" — same code, same math, just reheated with new copy.
- "Keep it between us" secret-VIP framing has now shown up enough times that it doesn't land as exclusive — it reads as a template.
- Four-item "you're gonna like these guys" grid is generic upsell, not curated to anything I've bought or browsed — no personalization signal at all.
- No mention of Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond points stacking, which is the first thing I'm calculating before I buy — Drunk Elephant never plays in that lane and it's a missed opportunity given how much of my spend logic is loyalty math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing DRUNKVIP20 back-to-back — rotate codes or at minimum change up the framing beat (I've now seen "secret," "well damn," and a countdown timer all attached to the same 20%).
- 2. Move the $110+ gift-with-purchase panel above the fold — that's the actual news in this email and it's buried under a repeat offer.
- 3. Add urgency with a real date/time, not just implied "sale ends" — I can't tell from this render when DRUNKVIP20 expires.
