# From LA to London and back
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- Twenty. I'm at twenty ILIA emails in May, and this one — arriving two days after they already teased "our *softest* launch yet" on June 2 — is a brand travelogue with no offer, no CTA button, and "Hi there" as my greeting. Hi *there*. I've spent hundreds on ILIA at Sephora Beauty Insider events alone. My name is in their system. Charlotte Tilbury manages thirteen emails and still finds ways to personalize the greeting; ILIA can't manage it at twenty.
- To be fair to them, there's a real content angle here — an Allure 2026 Readers' Choice Awards double win is legitimately worth an email. Limitless Lash for Best Clean Mascara and Multi-Stick for Best Clean Blush are signals I track. That's the part I would have opened for. But instead of leading with the award win in any visual way, they buried it in bullet point two of a text memo, underneath a travel recap about cities I've now been told about three times this month. And the June 2 launch mention? Yesterday's send already did that. I'm not getting new information. I'm getting a remix.
- I mark this as read. I do not click through. I do not forward it to a friend. The Allure angle is the one thing that could have made me DM a friend a screenshot, but not like this.

## What's working

- The Allure 2026 Readers' Choice Awards callout is real social proof — award season matters in my world and two wins in clean beauty is credible, not manufactured
- Limitless Lash and Multi-Stick are named specifically, which is more than Glossier's "For your summer plans" gave me in terms of actual product intel
- The lifestyle photo grid at the bottom has warm, diverse energy — the model imagery reads as actually representative of who buys this brand
- Pulling back from promotional content after sixteen straight sale days is the right instinct — the execution just didn't deliver on it

## What's weak

- "Hi there" from a brand where I have purchase history is a miss — this is Sephora-tier spend and I'm being treated like a cold prospect
- The June 2 launch was already teased in yesterday's send; repeating it without any new detail (no product name, no shade, no ingredient hint) doesn't build anticipation, it just adds to the frequency fatigue
- No CTA button anywhere — the hyperlinks in body text are not the same thing, and a text-link-only email after nineteen promo sends feels like an accidental draft that got deployed
- "From LA to London and back" tells me nothing about beauty, products, or why I should care today — e.l.f. gets away with vague wordplay because their voice earns it; ILIA's subject here is just... geography
- The Allure win is buried in bullet two below a travel recap — that should be the hero, with an image, above the fold

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Allure win** — hero image of the award badge or the winning products, not a three-paragraph travel summary; awards are the one genuinely shareable moment in this email
- 2. **Personalize the greeting** — "Hi Vera" is table stakes in 2026; segment at minimum by purchase tier or category
- 3. **Add one specific CTA button** — "Shop Allure-Winning Limitless Lash" with a direct product link, not just inline hypertext
- 4. **Either give me a real June 2 tease or drop it** — a product name, a shade range, an ingredient — anything I can't already find from yesterday's email; re-teasing the same mystery a day later just burns goodwill
- 5. **Connect the travel narrative to the customer** — "We went to London and came back with something you told us you wanted" is a story; "we were in cities" is a press release

## Technical Audit — ILIA "From LA to London and back"

### 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-delivered email using `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` redirect tracking and a Klaviyo-hosted custom font via `@import`. The truncated source cuts off before the footer, so compliance elements (unsubscribe, address) cannot be fully confirmed.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Inconsistent `text-decoration` on anchors** — Several links carry inline `style="text-decoration: underline;"` (e.g., `_2`, `_3`, `_5`, `_6`, `_7`, `_8`) while others do not (`_0`, `_1`, `_4`, `_9`). This is a rendering inconsistency, not a stylesheet rule — likely a copy/paste artifact from the template editor.

**`rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow"` on all links** — `nofollow` is semantically meaningless in email (crawlers don't follow email links), but it also means inbox preview renderers that do fetch links won't pass link equity. Not harmful, but it's noise and can interfere with some link-prefetch behaviors in iOS Mail.

**UTM parameters not confirmed** — All hrefs resolve through `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/...` redirects. UTM injection at redirect time cannot be verified from HTML source alone. If Klaviyo's UTM append setting is off or misconfigured, clicks will land untagged.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Zero-width space spacers** — `<div><span>​</span></div>` (U+200B, decimal `8203`) is used as a vertical spacer between paragraphs. Screen readers may announce these as blank elements or pause unexpectedly. Replace with `margin-bottom` on the preceding element or a proper `<div style="height: 12px;">` spacer.

**No `lang` attribute on `<html>`** — Missing from the visible source. Required for screen reader language detection and WCAG 2.1 SC 3.1.1.

**No `<meta charset>` visible in `<head>`** — The truncated `<head>` shows only the `@import` style block. If `<meta charset="UTF-8">` is absent, non-ASCII characters (e.g., `—`, `​`, `é`) are at risk of garbling in some clients.

**Custom font via `@import`** — Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` MSO conditional, which is correct Outlook handling. Fallback stack `Karla, Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` is well-formed.

**No `alt` attributes confirmed** — HTML is truncated before image tags; cannot audit. Flag for QA verification.

This is either intentional for a newsletter format or a dropped merge tag. Either way, the profile database has first names — leaving value on the table or masking a template error.

No broken/unfired merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%7B%7B`) visible in the truncated source.
