# The Sephora Major Summer Deals chapter is closing ⏲️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the second time this subject line has landed in my inbox today. I noted it in my last review — "The Sephora Major Summer Deals chapter is closing ⏲️" — I already gave that send an 8. And now here it is again, different message ID, same creative, same date. Sephora just pulled a Beach Club. We've been here before, and it wasn't cute then either.
- That said, *this* version has a distinct hook the earlier one didn't lead with as loudly: "App Early Access." The sender display name is literally "Sephora App Early Access," which means Sephora is positioning this as a segmented, exclusive send for app users. Except — I have the app. I'm Rouge. I've had the app since approximately the Obama administration. So when the primary CTA is "DOWNLOAD NOW," I am not the intended recipient of that button. That's a segmentation miss that undercuts what is otherwise a solid sale-end email.
- The 300 bonus points callout and the 50% off hero salvage this for me as a Rouge member doing the math. Marc Jacobs Beauty being back is genuinely news worth scrolling for. But I'm not clicking "Download Now," and I'm not forwarding this to a friend who already has the app either. Mark as read, note the sale deadline, open the app I already have.

## What's working

- **50% off hero is unmissable** — it's the first thing the eye lands on, big and clear, before any scrolling.
- **"Earn 300 Bonus Points" is the right language for me** — I'm doing the math in real time, this pings my Rouge-member brain immediately.
- **Marc Jacobs Beauty section is actual news** — a brand re-launch buried in the lower third is more compelling than generic sale copy; I'd scroll to it.
- **"Chapter is closing" urgency reads as credible narrative, not panic** — I called this out in the earlier send today and I'll stand by it; it's better than a countdown timer graphic.
- **Beauty Insider branding visible** — reinforces that this is a member-targeted send, even if the CTA says otherwise.

## What's weak

- **"DOWNLOAD NOW" is the primary CTA and I already have the app** — Sephora has my Rouge data; this button should not exist for me. It's the most tone-deaf possible ask for a loyal customer.
- **This is the second delivery of this exact email today** — same subject, same creative. The Beach Club precedent from earlier this week should have been a warning; it wasn't.
- **Product grid is too small to read on this render** — I can't identify specific products or prices without pinching in; there's no one hero product pulling focus.
- **No name personalization anywhere visible** — they know I'm Rouge, they know what I buy, and I'm getting a generic "Select Beauty" offer.
- **"Select Beauty" with the asterisk is doing quiet damage** — the exclusions aren't spelled out anywhere I can see, which makes the 50% feel less trustworthy than it should.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill "DOWNLOAD NOW" for existing app users** — replace with "Shop the App" or "Shop Early Access" deep-linked into the sale. Glossier's "Final Hours" email last week managed a clear, specific CTA with no app-store misdirect; Sephora can do the same.
- 2. **Lead with the points math, not the download** — "Rouge member? Earn 300 points before midnight" as the sub-hero copy immediately segments me correctly.
- 3. **Surface one hero product from the sale** — even one specific "this moisturizer is 50% off until midnight" item anchors the scroll better than a grid of thumbnails I can't read.
- 4. **Don't send the same subject line twice in one day** — if this is an app-segment resend, change the subject to signal it's distinct.
- 5. **Spell out a few notable exclusions** — or at minimum don't hide them in tiny asterisk copy. Sally Beauty's "50% Off It's a 10" email was precise on the brand name; Sephora's "Select Beauty" is frustratingly vague by comparison.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge members get 24-hr early access — sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last hours: 50% off + 300 bonus points in the app`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your app exclusive is live. Tap to stack points before midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 300 points while the Major Summer Deals window closes.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue 1 — User zoom disabled (WCAG 1.4.4 violation)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents users from pinching to zoom on iOS/Android. This is a Level AA WCAG failure. Fix: remove `maximum-scale=1` or set it to `5`.

**Issue 2 — Text scaling suppressed globally**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
`text-size-adjust: none` is applied to 10 element types including `body`. This overrides OS-level accessibility text scaling for low-vision users. The selector set is broader than necessary — limit `text-size-adjust` to layout containers only, not `body` or `span`.

**Issue 3 — Image alt text unverifiable**
No `<img>` tags are visible in the truncated source. Alt text coverage cannot be confirmed. Requires full-source review.

**Non-issue (by design):**
- XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE is correct for email; not a defect.
