# Set your sights here 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay. So Glossier sent three "ends tonight / ends tomorrow / for your eyes only" urgency emails in a four-day stretch, and then the sale quietly lived another nine days before finally dying — and *this* is what comes next. No offer. No percentage. Just "shop + save" four times in a row and a pun in the subject line. I get what they're doing: product reset after a promo cycle that overstayed its welcome. That instinct is right. The execution has some real problems.
- There's actually good stuff in here — the Face Kit section with the model shot is the strongest creative Glossier has sent me since the sale madness started, and the NYC teaser at the bottom stopped my scroll cold. A city-specific drop tease with I ♥ NY branding? That's the kind of exclusive-feeling signal I actually care about. But they buried it below three full product sections and a fragrance tile row like it's a footnote, when it should be running the whole email.
- I'd open this — Glossier is an instinct-open for me at this point — but I'm not clicking through. "Shop + save... how much exactly?" is not a conversion email for someone who just survived their fake-deadline saga. I wouldn't forward it. I'd file it under "noted" and wait for the NYC drop to actually land.

## What's working

- The "NEW" badge on each set section immediately signals product launch, not repackaged inventory — I trust the label
- The Face Kit model shot is doing real work: young woman, natural skin, holding product in a real-life grip — that's styling context, not a flatlay, and it reads as genuinely aspirational for me
- "We coupled up our Perfecting Skin Tint with Stretch Balm Concealer" is specific product composition copy — I know exactly what's in the kit without clicking
- The NYC teaser creative breaks the scroll with a bold red background and I ♥ NY art — distinctive, exclusive-feeling, completely different energy from the rest of the email
- Brand voice holds throughout ("put-together face," "we made it simple") — very Glossier, never corporate

## What's weak

- "Shop + save" appears four times and never once tells me *how much* I'm saving versus buying separately — I cannot do the bundle math they're implying exists, which is the one thing that would convert me
- After the 25%-off-that-never-ended arc, dropping to zero concrete offer with no transition framing feels abrupt — there's no "thank you for your patience, here's what's new" bridge
- The NYC teaser is buried at the absolute bottom of a long scroll — their most interesting content is treated like a footer
- No loyalty acknowledgment anywhere — I've been in the Glossier ecosystem through all of this, and this email addresses me like a stranger
- The "Even More Sets" two-tile row (Travel Spray + Fragrance) feels like overflow that didn't make the cut for a hero slot — weakens the editorial confidence

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the NYC tease up** — make it the second hero, right after the main sets intro. "Something New York this way comes — be the first to know" above the fold generates far more sign-ups than burying it in the scroll basement. ILIA has been running their buy-three game with progressively stronger creative day after day; Glossier has a genuinely exciting exclusive-drop hook and isn't using it.
- 2. **Show the bundle savings math inline** — "Boy Brow Duo: save $X vs. buying separately" is one line of copy per section. Sally Beauty ran their 25% sitewide math clean and visible and it hit a 10. Glossier saying "save" without the number is asking me to trust them after a month of fake deadlines.
- 3. **Add a loyalty nod** — even a single line ("as a Glossier member, you're seeing this first") reframes me as a known customer. e.l.f. did this better on their July 2nd switch-up, and they're a fraction of Glossier's brand equity.
- 4. **Cut the "Even More Sets" overflow tile row** — end after the three hero sets. The fragrance and travel tiles dilute the edit; the NYC teaser should be the fourth section, not a product afterthought.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Malformed tracking injection:**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KWN6D5H0G3GDFE2WBCCDKAWR_0"></span>
```
A `<span>` element does not support `rel` or `href` attributes — this is invalid HTML. This is a Klaviyo open-tracking mechanism injected as a stylesheet-mimicking span. While common in Klaviyo sends, it can trigger spam-filter heuristics on stricter MTA rules and will fail HTML validation. A standard 1×1 tracking pixel (`<img>`) is the correct vehicle for open tracking.

**Tracking domain confirmed:** `ctrk.klclick.com` — Klaviyo's CDN. No issues with the domain itself.

**Remaining links not auditable** — HTML is truncated before body links appear.
