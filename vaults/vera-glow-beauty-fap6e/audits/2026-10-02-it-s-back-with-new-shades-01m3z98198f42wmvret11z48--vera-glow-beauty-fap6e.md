# It’s back (with NEW shades)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the hero lands. A close-up model, a peach compact, and "It's Back" in plain type make the product obvious in about two seconds. Glossier's "something festive" teaser made me guess what the product was. ILIA just shows me the Ethereal Baked Face Palette and tells me it's returning with new shades. I'm a blush-and-highlighter person, so I stayed.
- The body is where it earns the score. The three shade stories (Botanical Blur, Coastal Bloom, Poppy Fields) each show a different skin tone holding the compact, with the shade names, finish and "(new)" tags listed beside them. That's the swatch info I screenshot before a restock. Drunk Elephant's 25-shade clock last week was gorgeous but made me work to find what was actually for sale. ILIA is easier. Two red "Shop the Palette" buttons keep it clear.
- My complaint is the offer, because I'm always doing the points math. The free mini Limitless Lash Mascara needs $100+. A palette at that price won't get me there alone, so I'd have to add a $30 mascara I don't need. Sephora's Rewards Bazaar and Ulta's 21 Days never make me do that. I'd click and I'd add the palette to my cart. I might forward it to my cousin, who wears the deeper shades. I wouldn't rush to hit the threshold.

## What's working

- The hero puts the product in my face. "It's Back" plus the model holding the compact gets to the point faster than Ulta's recycled barcode-and-books hero.
- The shade stories show real range across skin tones, with a model for each collection. That's the personalization cue I want from a complexion brand.
- Each collection lists names, finish tags and "(new)" markers (AKOYA champagne pearl/highlighter, SURGE poppy red/blush). I can plan my cart without leaving the email.
- The "#safe for sensitive skin, #non-comedogenic, #formulated without talc" strip is a quiet trust signal that fits ILIA.
- The CTAs are clearly buttons, repeated mid-email and again at the end, and they stay readable on mobile. I never scrolled looking for the link.

## What's weak

- The free mini mascara at $100+ is a GWP with a high threshold. It doesn't help me on the palette price, and the "*See site for details" fine print is tiny.
- There's no deadline. "(But not for long)" is vague, and I've seen nothing tying this to a sell-out date. e.l.f. at least has "Only $12" energy, and Sephora gives me a date.
- There's no loyalty tie-in. ILIA VIP only shows in the footer icons, so I can't tell whether I'd earn points or get early access to a limited edition.
- This is the 4th ILIA email since 9/26. The Poppy Fields story echoes the 9/30 "Painting poppies" send, so it feels a bit recycled.
- The mascara section at the bottom, "a free lift," has a big image with a lot of empty space. After the palette story it feels tacked on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real reason to act now in the hero. Add "Limited edition, while supplies last" or an early-access line for VIP members, and tie the mascara GWP to a lower threshold such as $60+.
- 2. Add loyalty math. Something like "VIP members earn 2x points on the palette" gives me a reason to buy now.
- 3. Cut the mascara block to one line near the top. The top bar already has the gift, so the long bottom section repeats it.
- 4. Add one social-proof element, such as a review count or "sold out last year," to reinforce the scarcity.
- 5. Space out the sends. Four in a week dilutes the "back in stock" moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The sold-out ILIA palette is back`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 new shade stories, limited run`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ethereal Baked Face Palette returns with new blush & bronzer shades. Limited edition.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sold out last year. Shop all 3 collections + a free mini mascara.`

## Technical Audit

The QA run could not verify links or authentication because probing was skipped and the relay dropped headers. The only confirmed code-level defects are missing `alt` text on one content image, a missing `lang` attribute, and an empty `<title>`.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link status unverified.** QA reports "12 material link(s) probed, 12 skipped", so no HTTP status, redirect chain or destination was checked. Treat link health as unknown, not as passing.
- **Click and open tracking.** The tracking pixel is served from `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M3Z9KWKZEJJYWW7BXZHHEV62`, a first-party branded tracking domain. That is fine, but the pixel could not be validated because probing was skipped.
- The HTML is truncated, so I can't confirm the CTA hrefs or whether any are empty, `#`, or malformed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content image (real issue).** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. If it is meaningful, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. If it is decorative, it should have `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel (low severity).** `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M3Z9KWKZEJJYWW7BXZHHEV62` has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and a 1x1 size so it is treated as decorative.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=… xmlns:v=…>` with no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Empty `<title>`.** It is `<title> </title>`, which is a minor accessibility issue in web-view and browser contexts.
- **Web fonts via `@import`.** `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css)` and `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` are ignored by Gmail and several other clients. The fallback is `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif`, so degradation is acceptable. This is informational only.
- The preheader is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0; overflow:hidden`) and padded with `͏` characters to block body-text bleed-through.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}`, `{% %}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The truncated HTML means the rest of the body was not checked.

Verify against the raw message from the sending ESP, which is likely Klaviyo given the `SguDsz` company ID.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is flagged as an expected relay artifact, not a confirmed failure.
- **Footer.** The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be verified here. QA states an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set but was skipped, so its status is unknown.
