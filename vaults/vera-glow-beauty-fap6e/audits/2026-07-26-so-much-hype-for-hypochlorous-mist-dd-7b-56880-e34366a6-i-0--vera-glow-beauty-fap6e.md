# Technical Audit: e.l.f. SKIN — "So much hype for hypochlorous mist" 🤩

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses standard Oracle Responsys click-tracking redirects (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), but 5 of 12 probed links resolve to malformed/relative destination URLs, plain-text fallback is completely missing, and unsubscribe compliance headers are unconfirmed — QA reports a 25% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 5 of 12 probed links fail with `unknown url type` because the redirect resolves to a **relative path instead of an absolute URL** (missing scheme/host), which breaks link validation and likely breaks rendering in any client that doesn't silently prepend the base domain:
  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/holy-hydration-thirst-burst-drops?Size=Regular+1+oz+%2F+30+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning..." link)
  - `/pages/mobile-app?pid=Email&af_dp=...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  - `/pages/skincare-finder?lid=dxv4vyd79u1m&utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=072626_saveyourelf_hypochlorous_reviews&` ("oily skin")
- All tracked links route through the Responsys-pattern redirector (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/{hash}~~/AAQRxRA~/{hash}`), consistent across the email — no mixed/foreign redirect domains observed.
- 27 non-material (footer/social/utility) links were deprioritized and not probed; an unsubscribe link was confirmed present in that set per the QA tool's own logic, but its target was not validated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- One tracked pixel/image is missing `alt` text: `src="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/XUIGlTDhNlIoLeSOPScNWA~~/AAQRxRA~/vG0MMeoGnlRDHMpm4m_jHvf5jy5VEiMxLiKhnNDzlYMKMv99h_fI9"`.
- The hero banner `<img>` (`alt="e.l.f."`) has alt text; other images in the truncated source were not evaluated beyond this.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `mobile_hide`/`desktop_hide` classes, and a preheader `div` (`"NEW Save Your e.l.f. Hypochlorous Mist"`) are present and structured correctly — no markup issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The mobile-app deep link ends with `bxid=` (empty value) — an unresolved merge token for the broadcast/recipient ID, truncated with no closing value.
- The skincare-finder link ends with a dangling `&` after `utm_campaign=...`, indicating a merge token (likely `utm_content` or a recipient token) that resolved to an empty string, leaving orphaned query syntax.
- No `{{merge_tag}}`-style unresolved placeholders were found in the visible HTML/body copy.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found in QA capture (may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true absence — cannot confirm from HTML alone).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found — if genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of their 2024 policies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — status unknown, same relay-capture caveat applies.
- An unsubscribe link is present in-body per QA's link inventory, but its destination was not independently validated (deprioritized/unprobed).
- **Recommend verifying headers at the raw SMTP/relay level** rather than relying solely on this capture, since AgentMail relay may strip or fail to surface these headers even when present at origin.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Only one probed link carries visible UTM parameters: `utm_source=broadcast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=072626_saveyourelf_hypochlorous_reviews` (skincare-finder link) — and that link is also one of the broken/malformed ones (see §2, §4).
- The other 4 broken links show no UTM parameters at all in their resolved destination, meaning campaign attribution would be lost even if the URL-resolution bug were fixed.
- Destination paths (`/products/...`, `/account/login`, `/pages/mobile-app`, `/pages/skincare-finder`) are consistent with elfcosmetics.com site structure, but since none resolved to an absolute URL, landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed end-to-end.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the redirect/link-shortener configuration so destination URLs resolve to fully-qualified absolute URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) instead of relative paths — this is the root cause of all 5 link failures.
2. Investigate the empty `bxid=` and dangling `&` in the mobile-app and skincare-finder links — both indicate a merge-token/personalization field that failed to populate at send time.
3. Add or restore the plain-text MIME part (currently 0 chars) for deliverability and accessibility compliance.
4. Add `alt` text to the tracked image referenced in §3.
5. Independently verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` at the raw email/header level (outside the AgentMail relay) to confirm whether these are true compliance gaps or capture artifacts.
6. Add UTM parameters consistently across all tracked CTAs, not just the skincare-finder link, to preserve campaign attribution once the link-resolution bug is fixed.
