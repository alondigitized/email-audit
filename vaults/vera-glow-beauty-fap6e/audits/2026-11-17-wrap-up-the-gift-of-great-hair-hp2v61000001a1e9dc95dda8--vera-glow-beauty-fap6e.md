# Wrap up the gift of great hair 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- Another Sephora email, and the Dior banner is the first thing I see again. I'm not mad at "Get a Dior Mini" with the sample of Sauvage EDP. But I've now had six Sephora sends in about five days, and I can already tell which tab this one belongs in: gift guide, holiday sets, scroll, close. The hair-gift angle at least gives it a reason to exist. The "Hot Hair Gifts" green hero is clean, the pink Dae bundle shot is pretty, and the product grid is easy to browse.
- The problem is that this email wants three things at once: the Dior freebie, hair sets, and a Cyber Week tease at the bottom. I'd click the K18 or Fenty by Rihanna set because the "$47.20 ($85 value)" price is the only place the points math feels alive. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save the Cyber Week tease for later and mark the rest as read. The sets are nice, but nothing here beats waiting for the Beauty Insider event.

## What's working

- The "Hot Hair Gifts" hero is simple, with a single "Shop Now" button. The eye knows where to go.
- The product grid labels are useful: "New · Limited Edition," "Only at Sephora," and the value in parentheses, like "$53 value" on K18. I can do the savings math right there.
- Fenty by Rihanna's "$47.20 ($85 value)" has a visible sale price. It's the only concrete discount I could find in the grid.
- The "Cyber Week Is Coming!" block with free sample bags is a credible reason to hold my cart, and it reads as a calendar beat I care about.
- Brands like K18, Living Proof, Dr. Groot, Davines and Ouai look curated and are all names I'd actually buy.

## What's weak

- The Dior banner sits above the hair hero, so the email opens with a fragrance sample and then pivots to hair. That's a split message.
- Nothing speaks to my Rouge tier. There's no points multiplier, no tier-specific perk, and no "Insider" content, even though the sender is "Sephora Insider."
- The fine print at the bottom is a giant wall of tiny text. I can't read it without zooming, and it makes the Dior offer feel buried under terms.
- It's the same holiday-set story as 11/13's "Meet the best of the best🏆" and 11/14's "’Tis the season for luxe beauty gifts 💎." Gift-guide fatigue is real.
- The lower half stacks too many modules: Spot it. Shop it., Holiday Gift Guide twice, Jo Malone, Cyber Week, and four category buttons. It dilutes the hair hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either lead with the Dior mini or lead with hair gifts, not both. If the Dior freebie is the hook, put the spend threshold in the banner so I don't have to open the terms.
- 2. Add a tier line like "Rouge: earn 2x points on hair sets" so the Insider sender name pays off.
- 3. Put the sale price on every product card, not just Fenty. Show "$X ($Y value)" consistently.
- 4. Cut the repeated Holiday Gift Guide modules and keep one.
- 5. Shrink or collapse the legal block behind a "Terms" link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional, sent through Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The automated QA found one hard failure (no plain-text part) and three warnings, and the source has some CSS and markup defects. The HTML was truncated, so footer, CTA and most link markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Every visible link is wrapped in an opaque redirect, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1e9dc95dda88f73369fe94bd8/.../?__dU__=...`. The final destinations can't be seen from the source.
- QA probed 12 of 55 links and skipped 43. No probe results (status codes, redirect chains) were supplied, so **no broken links are confirmed**. Coverage of the unprobed links is unknown.
- Two tracking pixels are present:
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261117_Hot_Hair_Gifts` is a second, separate tracker.
- Having two trackers on different hosts (`app.` and `mi.`) adds third-party requests and image-blocking exposure. This is not a defect, but note it.
- The `mi_u=4970293024993920` parameter is a per-recipient identifier in a plain GET URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** the `app.sephora.com/O/...` pixel and `o.gif` both have no `alt`. These are tracking pixels, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct markup.
- **Malformed CSS inside the media query:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside `@media only screen and (max-width:480px) {...}` in the `<style>` block. Conditional comments aren't valid CSS there, and CDO/CDC tokens are not ignored inside a block. Non-Outlook parsers will likely drop `img.mso-size` and the adjacent `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule, because its selector is prefixed with `<![endif]-->`. Move the MSO override out of the media block.
- **No responsive override for the outer wrapper:** the table has `width="700"`, `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"` and `class="tbl tbl_cntr"`. The visible `@media` block has no rule for `.tbl` or `.tbl_cntr`. That likely forces horizontal scroll or scaling on mobile, unless rules later in the truncated source handle it.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang`, and `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Invalid JSON-LD:** the `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block has `"discountCode": ""` and a `PromotionCard` type that is not a schema.org type. Most clients strip it, but where it is honored it is empty or invalid data.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear. Only the truncated portion is covered. The empty `discountCode` in the JSON-LD is an empty value, not a visible merge failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The message is HTML-only, which hurts deliverability and accessibility. This may reflect what was captured rather than what was sent, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the original message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. Alignment can't be confirmed, although the From domain `beauty.sephora.com` and the link domain `app.sephora.com` share the same parent.
- The footer is not in the truncated HTML. The visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be verified. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in link probing, but no result was provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Landing-page alignment can't be assessed because no probe results or final URLs were provided. No issues can be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify the raw MIME for a `text/plain` part and the `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` headers. Treat these as real defects only if they're absent from the original.
2. Move `img.mso-size` out of the `@media` block and into a separate MSO-only `<style>` block, so the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule isn't dropped.
3. Add a mobile override for `.tbl` (`min-width:0; width:100% !important`) so the wrapper doesn't force 700px.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Fix `content="noindex"`. Drop the JSON-LD block or populate it with valid schema.org types.
6. Extend link probing to cover the 43 skipped links, or at least the footer unsubscribe and preference links, and capture the final landing URLs and UTM state.
