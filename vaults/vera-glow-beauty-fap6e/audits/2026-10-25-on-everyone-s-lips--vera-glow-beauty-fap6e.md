# On everyone’s lips
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the first ILIA email in a while that I actually want to read. After the 10/16, 10/17 and 10/19 VIP-gift pile-on and the pro-program invite on 10/21, a lip-combo guide feels like a palate cleanser. The hero says "Holiday Hues" over a pile of crayons, lipsticks and glosses. That's pretty, and it's my category.
- The "Line + Fill + Gloss" formula is the best thing here. It's a routine I can copy, and it doubles as a shopping list. But the only offer is a thin strip at the top: "FREE MINI LIMITLESS LASH MASCARA W/ $75+*". The asterisk and the $75 threshold make it a points-math email, and the gift is a lash product in a lip email. The lip sets are priced nowhere I can see.
- I'd open it and scroll. I'd click on one of the Looks, probably Cool Berries. I'd probably screenshot it for my wishlist and not forward it, because there's no deal worth texting a friend about.

## What's working

- The Line + Fill + Gloss formula is easy to follow. I could shop a whole look in one pass.
- The three Looks are named and shaded: Cool Berries, Bold Reds, Effortless Neutrals. They get specific, with Rhyme, Wild Aster, Gallery, Case Study, Tango and Flambé. That's the shade-level detail I want.
- The limited-edition lip sets are a real gifting angle. The artwork packaging looks giftable.
- It's a calm change from the sale pushes of the last two weeks. "Clean, cruelty-free, sensitive skin-friendly" is a consistent trust message.
- The footer shows the VIP program and Personalized Shade Match, so there are some member cues.

## What's weak

- The only offer is the tiny "$75+" lash mascara banner. It has no tie to the lip theme and is easy to miss. The asterisk is also unexplained in the email body.
- None of the products show a price. I have to click through to do my math, and I do the math every time.
- The three Looks have no individual links I can see. Only the Lip Sketch and Gloss Set and the Trio get SHOP NOW buttons.
- The lip-set copy gives no price or value. It also doesn't say whether the sets are cheaper than buying the pieces separately.
- "Join ILIA VIP" is buried in the footer after 10/16–10/19 pushed VIP so hard. I get no tier perk or points info here.
- The email is very long and the middle sections look alike. The model photo near the end feels disconnected from the lip story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a lip-set price and a "save $X vs. buying separately" line right under the hero. It would also help to put the lash-mascara gift in the lip context ("Add a lip set, unlock a free mini mascara at $75").
- 2. Link each Look to a bundled cart ("Shop Cool Berries – $__"). Give me a one-tap way to buy the trio.
- 3. Say what the asterisk means, and give the offer end date.
- 4. Show the VIP perk next to the lip sets. For example, "VIP members get early access to the holiday sets."
- 5. Cut the sensitive-skin model block, or move it up. It currently interrupts the shopping flow.
