# ▶ We’re treating you to something new
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so after surviving the gauntlet of five Sephora "ENDS TODAY" and "LAST CHANCE" emails about the Major Summer Deals sale inside roughly two days — I genuinely needed a palate cleanser. And in that sense, this lands at the right moment. The tone shift from pure urgency to "hey, come see what's new" is the email equivalent of exhaling. I didn't jump for my phone the way I would for a Rouge flash offer, but I didn't groan either, which after that sale cadence is honestly a win.
- The problem is that once I opened it, I couldn't figure out what Sephora actually wanted me to do. There's a trial size mechanic at the top, then new arrivals with bonus sizes, then a basket-building promo, then a Stacking Weekend loyalty offer at 30% off, then a "Try It Love It" section, then Just Arrived. That's six distinct narratives competing in one scroll. The Stacking Weekend piece is the most interesting thing in here for me — that's the exact points math I run — but I had to dig mid-email to find it.
- Net verdict: I'd mark it as "come back to this" and open it later when I can actually think through the Stacking Weekend. I would not forward it to a friend — there's nothing specific enough to share. Compare that to e.l.f.'s "1 e.l.f.conic skin tint. 18 flexible shades" send, which I immediately screenshotted for my group chat. Focus wins every time.

## What's working

- The Stacking Weekend + 30% off for members section is exactly what I'm here for — that's the loyalty math that changes my purchase timing.
- "NEW + BONUS SIZE" framing on product tiles is a value signal I respond to; it reads as more for my spend.
- Tone shift away from urgency is genuinely smart after five consecutive sale emails — this doesn't feel desperate or fatigued.
- "Choose a Luxe Trial Size" aligns with how I actually shop prestige skincare — I sample before I commit full price.
- Clean render, no broken images, readable on mobile.

## What's weak

- "Something new" as the hero message is frustratingly vague — for someone who tracks launches and the beauty calendar, I need to know *what* is new before I decide to care.
- Five or six competing content sections means there's no visual hierarchy — the eye doesn't know where to land after the hero.
- "SHOP NOW >" is the laziest possible CTA copy; Charlotte Tilbury's "NEW To Sale" at least told me what I was walking into.
- No visible deadline or date window on the Stacking Weekend — urgency without parameters is noise.
- Multiple CTAs (SHOP NOW, BUILD YOUR BASKET, and whatever follows) dilute each other rather than funnel me anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Stacking Weekend** — put the loyalty 30% off with an explicit date range front and center in the hero. That's the headline offer for Rouge members and it's buried mid-email.
- 2. **Name the new launch in the subject** — "something new" is a missed launch moment. e.l.f. named their Skin Tint right in the subject line; Sephora should name whatever this is. A vague tease works for a brand with genuine mystique, not a weekly mailer.
- 3. **Cut to two or three sections max** — pick a story (launch OR loyalty event OR new arrivals) and commit. This email is trying to be a weekly newsletter and a launch moment and a rewards event all at once.
- 4. **Add a date to Stacking Weekend in the hero or CTA** — "Stacking Weekend ends Sunday" is a real, credible urgency lever.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge perk: stack your points this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `[Product name] just dropped — plus stack 30% this weekend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `30% off for members + new launches you haven't seen yet`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Stacking Weekend starts now — here's what to grab first`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`robots` meta typo** — `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` contains a space. The valid value is `noindex` (no space). Some crawlers may ignore the malformed directive, risking inadvertent indexation of the web-view version.

- **Zoom prevention** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, AA) requires content to be resizable up to 200% without loss of functionality.

- **Broad `text-size-adjust: none`** — Applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td` in the stylesheet. This suppresses the OS-level text scaling that users with low vision depend on. Should be scoped to specific fix targets (e.g., `.sup`) rather than applied globally.

- **Missing `lang` attribute** — The `<html>` opening tag has no `lang="en"` (not visible in source; could be omitted entirely). Screen readers use this to select the correct voice/language profile.

- **Generic `<title>`** — `<title>Sephora</title>` provides no context if the email is opened in a browser tab or web view. Should match or summarize the subject line.
