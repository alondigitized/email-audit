# What makes it waterproof?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is ILIA finally doing what I've been asking for since the 4th-of-July GWP treadmill — an actual ingredient-education send with real macro shots of the formula on skin, not another "free mini" flat-lay. "4X VIP points on Thru Line eyeliner" in that top banner caught my eye before anything else because points math is literally how I decide what to add to cart, and pairing a loyalty multiplier with a launch product is the kind of stacking Ulta trains me to look for every 21 Days cycle. The "here to stay / smooth operators / a fine line" ingredient breakdown — film formers, avocado oil pigments, omega fatty acids — is genuinely well done and reads like a mini derm deck, which for a "clean-adjacent" prestige brand like ILIA is exactly the trust signal I need before I put a pencil on my waterline.
- Where it loses me: there's no actual discount, no price, and no shade range shown for a product with FOUR shades on that swatch grid — I have zero idea what those look like against my own skin tone since I only get a top-down grid, not a lip/eye-tone match visual. Compare that to the 7/28 waterproof eyeliner send, which at least gave me a full shade/finish chart; this one regresses to a teaser without committing to the payoff.
- Verdict: I'd open it (good subject), I'd scroll through it because it's genuinely pretty, but I would not click today — no price, no clear single CTA I'm excited about, and I already know this SKU from three sends ago. Not forwarding it either; it's a nice-to-look-at brand deck, not a "you need this now" moment.

## What's working

- The VIP points banner up top is the first thing that grabbed me — 4X points is a real, quantifiable reason to buy today, not just "new drop" noise.
- The ingredient-story format (color lock film formers → avocado oil pigments → omega fatty acids) is a step up in production value from ILIA's usual GWP spam and actually builds category authority.
- "Safe for sensitive eyes / Safe for the waterline" directly answers the exact question I'd have before trying a new eyeliner on my waterline — that's real product-fit info, not fluff.
- Clean, consistent brand voice and moody macro photography that feels premium, on-brand, and distinct from the flat commerce grids Sephora and e.l.f. keep sending me.

## What's weak

- Zero price and zero specific discount anywhere in the body — the only "offer" is the points multiplier, and I can't do points math without knowing what I'm even buying at what cost.
- Four shades swatched on paper but never shown worn, so I still don't know which one matches me — a huge miss for a personalization-hungry shopper like me.
- Two identical "SHOP THRU LINE →" buttons with no differentiation — same generic CTA copy both times, no urgency, no "shop now and get your bonus points" tie-back to that banner.
- This is a single-SKU pencil getting its third or fourth email in under two weeks (following the 7/26 "Over(glaze)" and 7/28 waterproof sends) — the cadence is starting to feel like ILIA doesn't have anything else in the pipeline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the subject or hero — even "4X points, ends Sunday" beats an open-ended ingredient question with no urgency or value attached.
- 2. Show the four shades worn on a diverse set of eyes/skin tones directly under the swatch grid — this is the single biggest miss for a personalization-obsessed shopper like me.
- 3. Differentiate the two CTA buttons — one could say "Shop Thru Line" and the second "Find Your Shade" to give me a lower-commitment click option.
- 4. Add a real price or bundle-and-save line near the first CTA — right now there's no reason for me to act today versus bookmarking it for later.
- 5. Retire this SKU from the rotation for at least two weeks — three ILIA sends this month have now centered the same eyeliner; even Sephora Insider got called out for repeating themselves twice in one day, don't join that list.
