# Lush × Moomin has landed
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Lush × Moomin — I actually clocked this before I even opened it because "has landed" is a smart move after whatever chaos that Advent calendar email was doing on July 21st. Two days after asking me to pre-order a holiday box, they dropped an IP collab with a beloved Finnish character from the 1940s. Points for range, I guess? The illustrated hero is genuinely lovely — soft blues, hand-drawn Moomin characters in a nature scene, very much Lush's aesthetic dialed up through a storybook lens. The vibe is immaculate.
- But here's the thing: this email is a vibe and almost nothing else. There's no product. No price. No "this bath bomb is the Moomin one" with a close-up and ingredients I can evaluate. The CTA is "See More Moomins," which — charming, sure, but I'm a Sephora Rouge who does points math before she commits to a bath product. "See More Moomins" is not doing points math with me. It's a mood board with a teal button.
- I'd click because the collab sounds cool and limited-edition Lush always moves fast — but I'm going in blind. I would not forward this to a friend. I'd DM them the product page once I found something worth sharing.

## What's working

- The illustrated hero is genuinely editorial — the Moomin-in-a-forest scene is cohesive, brand-right, and feels intentional rather than slapped together
- "A FRESH COLLAB" in that large serif font hits cleanly — the eye goes straight to it, no visual clutter competing
- The Moomin quote from *Comet in Moominland* (1946) is a delightful credibility move — it tells me Lush actually knows the IP they're licensing
- "Limited-edition" in body copy creates real scarcity signal for me; Lush collabs do sell out and I know it
- Clean render, no broken images, no stray code — compared to some of the Ulta emails I've seen recently (looking at you, "this event is over" banner), this is flawless technically

## What's weak

- Zero product specificity — not a single product name, SKU, or hero item visible; ILIA's launch email "something new arrives 07.28" actually built more tension with *less* content because at least there was a countdown anchor
- "See More Moomins" is whimsical CTA copy that prioritizes the character over the commerce — I want "Shop the Collab" or "Get Yours Before It's Gone"
- No price, no loyalty/Lush rewards framing, no "members get early access" — after 31 emails I still don't know if Lush has a loyalty program or if I should even care about being a subscriber
- No demographic signal beyond "likes cute things" — the illustration skews young-ish but Moomin is niche enough that this could land for a 45-year-old Scandinavian nostalgia shopper just as easily as it lands for me

## Recommendations

- The single highest-impact change: show one product. One bath bomb, one soap, one *something* with a name and an ingredient call-out. e.l.f. SKIN's "Glitz with benefits" email worked because the offer was immediately legible — you saw what you were getting. This email makes me do a click of faith.
- 1. **Add a hero product module** — one featured item (the most iconic piece of the collab), with product name + one key ingredient/scent and the price. Even "from $X" is better than nothing.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA** from "See More Moomins" to "Shop the Limited Collection" — keep the Moomin love in the body copy, but the button needs to convert, not charm.
- 3. **Add urgency framing** — "limited run, while supplies last" or a soft inventory signal. Lush collabs historically sell out; make me feel that.
- 4. **Surface loyalty value** — even one line: "Rouge members, early access drops [date]" or equivalent. After 31 emails I still don't know if subscribing to Lush gets me anything.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lush × Moomin is here — get it before it's gone`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New drop: Lush × Moomin limited collab`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dreamy scents, fresh ingredients, and a whole lot of Moomin love — shop before it sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited-edition bath & body with everyone's favorite Moomintroll — new today`
