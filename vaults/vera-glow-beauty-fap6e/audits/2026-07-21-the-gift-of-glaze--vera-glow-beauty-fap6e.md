# The gift of glaze
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- Seven days. Seven ILIA emails. And here we are with "The Gift of Glaze" — one day after "Your gift, gone in a swipe." ILIA has discovered the word "gift" converts and they are wringing every last drop out of it.
- Here's the thing: the email itself is actually beautiful. The hero model with those glossy lips is doing exactly what it needs to do, the "Goes with Gloss" product grid is smartly organized, and the mini tote coming back is a genuinely compelling hook — that bag sold out last time. If this were email two of the week I would have clicked immediately.
- But it's not. It's email seven, the second consecutive "gift" subject, and I have Sephora Rouge benefits sitting unused while ILIA is selling me direct with no points play in sight. I'll mark this as read and check if the tote sells out — I'm not forwarding it to my group chat because they're as fatigued as I am.

## What's working

- **Hero visual lands instantly** — the close-up glossy lip shot communicates the product before I read a single word of copy.
- **"GET YOUR GLAZE" CTA is specific and fun** — verb plus product noun, matches the hero energy, easy tap target.
- **"Goes with Gloss" product grid is well-organized** — six products with individual SHOP NOW buttons means I can skip straight to the one I want without hunting.
- **The mini tote return is a real hook** — its own dedicated section at the bottom gives returning fans a genuine reason to act, not just browse.
- **No render bugs anywhere** — clean layout, images loaded, visual hierarchy holds all the way down the scroll.

## What's weak

- **Second consecutive "gift" subject in two days** — July 20 was "Your gift, gone in a swipe," today is "The gift of glaze." ILIA is telegraphing that they found a word that converts and are now wearing it into the ground.
- **Zero loyalty or points angle** — when I buy ILIA, I buy it at Sephora to stack Rouge points. This email doesn't acknowledge that my path to purchase goes through a third-party channel. Drunk Elephant at least plays that dual-channel game; ILIA is asking me to leave points on the table.
- **Mini tote purchase threshold is too small to read** — I can see the body copy references a minimum spend but the number is tiny. If you're dangling a fan-favorite freebie, make that threshold a headline.
- **Day seven of a daily send streak is doing damage** — Glossier drove me crazy at nine emails in thirteen days; ILIA is at seven consecutive. The fatigue tax eats whatever goodwill the creative earns.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the back-to-back "gift" framing** — two gift subjects in 24 hours signals creative exhaustion. Lead with the actual product: name the glaze, name the formula, make it feel like a launch not a GWP hook.
- 2. **Make the mini tote threshold a hero callout, not footnote** — "Free mini tote with $65+" in large type converts; buried small print doesn't.
- 3. **Add a channel acknowledgment for Sephora/Ulta shoppers** — one line ("earn your Beauty Insider points") would land for every Rouge and Diamond who routes clean beauty through their points account, which is a lot of ILIA's audience.
- 4. **Cut to 3x/week max** — each email after day five is depressing the open rate on the one that follows. They'd get more mileage from the mini tote if it weren't competing with six predecessors.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New Balmy Glaze launch + free mini tote with $65+. Limited supply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The tote sold out last drop. Yours with any $65 purchase, today only.`
