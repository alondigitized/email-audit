# Black Friday Savings Came Early!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Sally email in about a week that says "Black Friday" without a real Black Friday offer. The hero is a big gold-on-black "Early Black Friday Deals" banner with one "Shop now" button and no number in it. I had to scroll past it to find the deals. It's a stacked version of the 11/6 and 11/8 sends. The 40% off styling tools is the same offer from "Black Friday came early 👀" on 11/8. The Wella BOGO from 11/12's "Save on your next color refresh" is now a generic "Buy one, get one 50% off Big Bottle Sale" on ION and Macadamia conditioner.
- The math is doable, which is more than I can say for Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder. BOGO 50% is 25% off a pair. $18.99 for a color, developer and bond-repair bundle is a clear price. 40% off a hair dryer is fine. But the hero buries all of it, and there's nothing for me as a points person. I see no Rewards callout, no tier math, and no member price. I wouldn't forward it. I'd click one thing, probably the color bundle, and only if I were already restocking. I'd mark it as read.

## What's working

- The $18.99 One 'N Only bundle is the best block here. It's a real price, and it names what's in the box: color, developer and bond repair.
- The three-block structure (BOGO, bundle, 40% off) is easy to scan. Each block has a big bold offer line and a "Shop now" link under it.
- The red strip at the top is useful: free 2-hour delivery on $35+, and 15% off when you buy online and pick up in store. That's the one place I saw a real benefit.
- The Klarna row and the icon row (in-store, free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery) are trust cues. They're clean and not noisy.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer, only "Early Black Friday Deals." Glossier's "Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!" put the math in the subject. Here I have to scroll to find it.
- It's a repeat. The 40% off styling tools and the BOGO bottle sale both show up in the 11/8 and 11/12 sends. Drunk Elephant did the same thing with its Singles Day gift, four sends in a week.
- Nothing is shown for Sally Beauty Rewards members. I'm a points-math person and there's no Rewards callout in the body, and the footer only says points are "based on account data at time of email send."
- The glitter backgrounds make the hair dryer and iron look cheap. Only the One 'N Only block gives me a product detail. The conditioners have no size, no price, no sale price and no reviews.
- There's a model photo of a woman with straight dark hair in the middle of the color block. It has no link and no label, so it doesn't tell me what shade I'd get.
- The fine print is dense and long. The "buy online, pick up in store" 15% off is in the red strip, but the conditions are only in the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the best offer in the hero: "BOGO 50% off big bottles + $18.99 color bundles" instead of "Early Black Friday Deals." This is the biggest change.
- 2. Add a Rewards line, such as "2x points on color through 11/17." That beats Ulta's repeated ladder for people who track points.
- 3. Put prices on every product tile, and add the bottle size to the BOGO tiles. Show the original price next to the sale price.
- 4. Replace the unlabeled model photo with a shade-and-link tile, or cut it.
- 5. Show the real end date for each offer. "Early" doesn't tell me when it ends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off big bottles + $18.99 color kits`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Color kit for $18.99 ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off styling tools and free 2-hour delivery on $35+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Wella-level color, bond repair included. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional, Open Sans webfont, mobile-media-query template. QA shows no hard failures, but a 57% pass rate with 3 warnings. The two most concrete problems are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a third-party pixel and an insecure `http://` tracking image. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed. The HTML source was truncated, so body, footer and link-level findings are limited to what QA surfaced.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations, status codes or redirect chains were verified.
- **Insecure tracking image:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…` uses plain HTTP. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop it, which silently loses that tracking signal.
- **Heavy third-party pixel stack.** The QA output shows pixels from `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net` and `login.dotomi.com`. I can't confirm from the data whether these are all intended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five images flagged for missing alt text:**
  - Two have an empty label and empty `src`, so the QA output can't identify them. Check for `<img>` tags with no `src`.
  - Three are tracking pixels (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`).
- For tracking pixels, `alt=""` is acceptable. Confirm they are 1x1 and not exposed to screen readers, for example with `width="1" height="1"` or `aria-hidden`. The QA data doesn't show this.
- The two empty-`src` images can't be assessed from the data, but they are likely rendering defects or broken placeholders.
- **No other issues confirmed.** The visible head has a correct charset meta, a viewport meta, a `<title>`, the Apple data-detector reset and a `max-width:499px` mobile breakpoint.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&…&advt=-` contains a literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro wasn't substituted at send time, so every recipient gets an identical `r=` value and the pixel can be cached or deduplicated, which undercounts impressions. Several parameters are also `-11`, which looks like a placeholder or unset default: `si`, `ci`, `pi`, `ad` and `aca`.
- **Identifier in pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2334197/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` has a path segment that looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). That is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL, which is a privacy and data-sharing consideration. It is not a broken token.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** [WARN]. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture the header, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** [WARN]. One-click unsubscribe is therefore unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** [WARN]. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips or omits the header. The sender is `em.sallybeauty.com`, a subdomain, so check alignment directly with the original message headers.
- **Not assessable:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment **could not be assessed.** All 55 tracking links were skipped and the visible HTML is truncated.
- No evidence of UTM presence or absence. Because the links go through click-redirect domains, any UTMs would be applied at the redirect destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the `t.myvisualiq.net` pixel so it is replaced with a unique value at send time. Replace the `-11` placeholder IDs with real values or remove the pixel.
2. Switch `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController…` to HTTPS, or confirm Dotomi supports it.
3. Locate and fix the two `<img>` tags with empty `src`. Add `alt=""` plus 1x1 sizing to the tracking pixels.
4. Check the raw `.eml` from the original send, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment).
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolve a sample of the 55 tracking links to confirm destinations, redirect chains and UTM carry-through.
6. Review whether the Salesforce-style ID in the `mon-trk.com` pixel path is intended to be exposed to a third party.
