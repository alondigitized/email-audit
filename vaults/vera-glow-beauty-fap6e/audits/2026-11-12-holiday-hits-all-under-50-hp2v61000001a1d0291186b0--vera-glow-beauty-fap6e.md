# Holiday hits, all under $50 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the subject and the email don't match. The subject promises "all under $50," but the first thing I see is a purple "Spend More, Get More Fragrance" banner about free trial sizes at $50, $80 and $110. Gifts Under $50 only starts underneath it. I'm a points-math person, so a spend-tier gift with purchase should be a clean hook for me. But it sits above a gift guide, and the two ideas fight for the top spot.
- The gift guide itself is good. Every tile has a price and a value, like the Dolce&Gabbana sampler at $50 ($77 value) and the Tarte set at $32 ($70 value). That lets me do the math without clicking. It's also the best-organized Sephora send I've had since the 11/10 "points = rewards" email, which at least gave me something to calculate. This one gives me value math but nothing on Rouge points or Insider multipliers.
- Would I click? Yes, probably the Tarte or Laneige tile, because I like seeing the value spread. Would I forward it? No. It's a solid catalog and nothing I'd text a friend about.

## What's working

- The prices and values are right on the tiles ($26 for $36 value, $32 for $70 value). That's the kind of thing I screenshot.
- Labels like "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tell me what's a launch and what's exclusive. That's the curation signal I want.
- The green CLEAN badges on Salt & Stone, Phlur and Lys Beauty are easy to spot, and I like the ingredient-conscious angle.
- The red holiday background with one product per tile is easy to scroll on a phone.
- The Klarna, Afterpay and PayPal block and the "Spot it. Shop it." section give me real next steps.

## What's weak

- The fragrance banner and the "Gifts Under $50" hero are two different campaigns stacked together. The banner can read as a price-tier promo, so "under $50" gets muddied.
- The fragrance banner's fine print is tiny, and it says online only. I'd need to scroll or squint to learn what the sample tiers are tied to.
- There's no Rouge or Insider points content in the body, even though this was sent from "Sephora Insider." I'd expect tier-specific math from that sender name.
- The email is very long, and everything below Lys Beauty (Shark Beauty, rhode, the gift guide links, the footer) reads like filler.
- Ulta would have led with the gift-guide hook alone. Sephora's top banner dilutes it, and it feels like the same tiered-spend mechanic Ulta runs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hook for the top. Either lead with "Gifts Under $50" or lead with the fragrance gift with purchase, and move the other below the fold.
- 2. Put points in the tiles. Show something like "$32 = 32 points, 64 on Rouge Week" so the Insider sender name earns its place.
- 3. Make the banner's tier ladder legible without zooming. "$50 / $80 / $110 = 1 / 2 / 3 free trial sizes" in one line would do it.
- 4. Cut the email from 7,800px to the first six or seven product tiles plus one CTA. The long tail adds nothing.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Gifts under $50 + free fragrance trial sizes`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to $70 in value per set, plus free trial sizes at $50, $80 and $110.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New and Sephora-exclusive sets from Tarte, Laneige and Phlur. Online only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a 700px fixed-width table layout with ESP-tracked links (`app.sephora.com/T/...`). The QA run has one hard failure, a 0-character plain-text part. Several header checks are inconclusive, probably because the message came through the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** 12 of 49 links were probed and 37 were skipped by design. No per-link status codes were supplied, so I can't confirm or rule out broken links.
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links are wrapped as `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1d0.../?__dU__=...`. Every click needs the redirect to resolve, and its health is unverified.
- **Tracking pixels:** There are two. One is `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (hidden, `display:none`). The other is `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched`. Both lack `alt`, which is the source of the QA "missing alt" warnings. They are non-content images, so the impact is minimal, but `alt=""` is the correct markup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in the media query:** The `<style>` block has `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` inside `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)`. Conditional comments are not valid CSS. In non-MSO clients the `<!--[if mso]>` text becomes part of the selector prelude for `img.mso-size`. The `<![endif]-->` text then prefixes `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`. Both rules are likely dropped, so the Rouge-tier mobile header sizing probably doesn't apply.
- **No mobile reflow for the wrapper:** The wrapper table uses `min-width:700px; width:700px`, and the only media query shown doesn't override it. This risks horizontal scrolling on narrow viewports. The truncated source may contain further overrides.
- **Zoom disabled:** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` has a space. The valid token is `noindex`. This is low impact.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a schema.org type. Gmail may ignore it, but it is invalid markup.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. This is minor.
- **Plain-text part:** `[FAIL]` Text version is 0 chars (see Recommendations).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `__dU__=` link parameter and `mi_u=` pixel parameter are populated per-recipient. The `discountCode: ""` in the JSON-LD is empty, but it is metadata and not visible content.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe** and **List-Unsubscribe-Post** (RFC 8058): both not found. The QA note says the relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders, so verify against raw headers from a direct-to-inbox test.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (relay artifact). The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` should be checked for alignment in a direct-delivery test.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed. The QA harness reportedly always probes an unsubscribe link, but no result was provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The destination URLs are opaque ESP redirect tokens (`/T/v61000001a1d0.../`), so UTM parameters and final landing pages can't be seen in the source or the probe data supplied.
- The campaign identifier `20261112_Gifts_Under_50_Stitched` appears in the open pixel, which implies a consistent campaign naming convention. I could not verify that the redirect targets preserve it.
- **Gap:** There is no evidence either way on landing-page alignment. Resolve the redirects and check the final URLs and parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain-text part (FAIL):** Add a `multipart/alternative` text part. If the 0 chars is a capture artifact, confirm the raw MIME.
2. **Header checks:** Re-test with a direct-delivery seed inbox to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't treat the current warnings as confirmed defects.
3. **Media query:** Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule, or put it in a separate MSO-conditional `<style>`. This restores `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` sizing.
4. **Mobile layout:** Replace `min-width:700px` with `max-width:700px; width:100%`, or override it in the media query.
5. **Accessibility:** Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
6. **Metadata:** Fix `"no index"` → `noindex`, populate or remove the empty `discountCode`, and drop the invalid `PromotionCard` type.
7. **Link coverage:** Extend probing, or follow redirects for the top CTAs, to capture final URLs and UTM parameters for the continuity check.
