# The (2) gifts of VIP
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA is finally giving me something other than 20% off. After four sends of the same trio offer, two gifts is a real change of pace. The hero is pretty, with the velvet rust makeup bag and the mini mascara against that blue sky. But I got to the "$100+ VIP order" line and my points math brain kicked in. There are two thresholds here, a $75+ mini mascara and a $100+ bag. The banner says one thing, the body says another, and the second block repeats the $75 gift. I had to read it twice to work out which gift is at which spend.
- It's also "Join VIP + claim gifts," and I'm not sure what VIP costs or whether it's free. Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond have taught me to want the tier math upfront. I'd probably click through just to see the VIP terms and whether the bag is worth bumping a cart to $100. I wouldn't forward it. It's a decent email, but it's not a "text my friend" email.

## What's working

- The hero has a real gift visual. The velvet bag is cute and I can see exactly what I'd get, which is better than ILIA's catalog-only "Fade into fall" send on 10/15.
- The red banner at the very top gives me the mini mascara offer in one line before I scroll.
- "From: us. To: you." is a charming, on-brand line. It's a lot warmer than the plain-text "Friend, your 20% off trio awaits" from 10/11.
- The 2x3 product grid is clean, with a clear label and a shop-now button on every product.
- The bottom trust row has Leaping Bunny, 1% for the planet, shade match and auto-replenish. That's useful for a clean-beauty shopper.

## What's weak

- Two thresholds ($75 and $100) in one email muddy the offer. I had to hunt for which gift unlocks at which spend.
- The "Join VIP" CTA doesn't say what VIP costs or what I get beyond these gifts. The fine print is buried behind "*See site for details."
- The product grid has no prices, no reviews and no hero product. Sephora at least shows me the price and the star rating.
- "Join VIP + Shop" and "Join VIP + Claim Gifts" are near-duplicate CTAs. Two giant red buttons plus the mascara block is a lot of repetition. The mascara shows up three times.
- The subject says "(2) gifts" but the email doesn't clearly number them. The mascara gift is stated three times and the bag only once.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the two gifts side by side in the hero with their spend tiers in a simple table: "$75 = mini mascara. $100 = mini mascara + velvet bag." That's the biggest fix, because it removes the math.
- 2. Say upfront what VIP is: free or paid, and what it earns. Ulta puts the Rewards pitch in the first line.
- 3. Add prices and star ratings to the product grid. Cut the grid to 4 products and keep one CTA.
- 4. Cut the duplicate mascara block lower in the email, or replace it with a "what's in the bag" shot.
- 5. Put a real deadline on the gift offer. There's no urgency now.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free mini mascara + velvet bag, your VIP gifts`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $75, get a mini mascara. Spend $100, get the bag too`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two free gifts with your ILIA VIP order. Here's the spend math.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mini Limitless Lash Mascara at $75, velvet bag at $100. Ends Sunday.`
