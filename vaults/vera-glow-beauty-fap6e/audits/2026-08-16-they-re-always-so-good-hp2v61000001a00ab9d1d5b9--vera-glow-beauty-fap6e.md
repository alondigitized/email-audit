# They’re always SO good 👑
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "The Drop-Everything Edit" — that's a genuinely different hook from the last four Sephora sends I've opened this week, which were all "beauty forecast" chili peppers or that spinning VIBES/AURAS/ERAS wheel. This one calms down: hero fragrance duo (Miss Dior, Sauvage), then a clean cult-fave grid — Shiseido, Kérastase, Prada, Oribe, Farmacy, Sulwhasoo. It reads like an actual edit, not a gimmick, and I like that.
- But the top banner is doing a bait-and-switch I need to call out: "Choose a Trial Size... FREE ONLINE ONLY WITH $30 PURCHASE" is stacked directly above "The Drop-Everything Edit" hero, so my eye genuinely doesn't know which offer is the email. And down at the bottom, the INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE bar is back — no visible progress indicator or point count this time, which is actually an improvement over yesterday's "0 points" bug, but there's still nothing telling me where I sit as Rouge. Compare that to Ulta's tier math, which at least shows a number even when it's wrong.
- Would I click? Probably yes, on the Miss Dior or Kérastase serum specifically — those are actual products I'd buy. Would I forward it? No, it's not funny or unhinged enough to send to a friend the way ILIA's Kelsey Lu profile or Glossier's blush-diary send were. I'd open, scroll, maybe tap one product, and move on.

## What's working

- "The Drop-Everything Edit" is a real creative concept, not another spinning-wheel bit — nice break from this week's Sephora cadence.
- Grid of cult favorites (Kérastase, Oribe, Farmacy, Sulwhasoo) is genuinely my category — this is where I actually spend.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "CLEAN" badges on Kérastase and Farmacy are trust cues that matter to me as a Rouge shopper doing brand-exclusivity math.
- No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum — clean render top to bottom.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) is present without the visibly-wrong "0 points" bug from the last two sends.

## What's weak

- The trial-size banner up top competes directly with the hero — two offers fighting for my eye before I even know what the email is about.
- Prices are shown as ranges ("$65–$231," "$95–$232") with zero indication of size, so I can't tell what I'm actually clicking into.
- Tier bar shows no arrow, no point count, nothing personalized to tell me I'm Rouge specifically — just three static labels.
- "Beauty Celebration" and "MAKEUP'S LATEST LINEUP" tiles at the bottom are crammed in with no real offer detail, feels like filler after a strong top half.
- $5 off for 250 points is buried near the bottom, past ten product tiles — that's the one concrete offer in the whole email and it's nearly invisible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the trial-size promo and the Drop-Everything Edit hero into two distinct visual zones (or send trial-size as its own email) — right now they cannibalize each other for attention.
- 2. Move the $5-off-250-points offer up near the top — that's my actual redeemable Rouge benefit and it's currently the tenth thing I see.
- 3. Add real point balance to the INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE bar — even a static "Rouge · 2,340 pts" beats an unlabeled three-way toggle.
- 4. Add a size/volume label under each price range (e.g., "50mL, $65") so I'm not guessing what I'm clicking into.
