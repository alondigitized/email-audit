# Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Sephora is in my inbox for the fourth day in a row. It's the same purple sequin Cyber Week creative as 11/22 "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!" and 11/23 "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️". The difference today is that this send shows the whole sale, so it's the most complete of the three. There are three 30% off brand tiles (Kiehl's, Laura Mercier, Youth to the People), a free sample bag with the code GRABYBAG, a "Treat Yourself for Less" product grid, and a Korean Beauty block.
- The problem is that it's a lot, and none of it is personalized. Nothing here knows I'm Rouge. There's no points math, no Rouge perk and no "your tier gets X." I also wonder why I'd care about Kiehl's again after yesterday's Ole Henriksen push. I'd open it, scroll, and probably click Laura Mercier. I wouldn't forward it, because it's a recycled hero with new brand names.

## What's working

- The "FREE* Sample Bag" strip at the top is the first thing I see. Pick 1 of 4 bags with a GRABYBAG code is a concrete freebie that I'd actually grab.
- The three 30% off brand tiles each have a big number and a "SAVE NOW" button. They're easy to scan on a phone.
- "Treat Yourself for Less" shows struck-through prices, like Touchland $10 → $7 and Laneige Lip Sleeping Mask $24 → $18. That gives me the math I like.
- Laura Mercier is the one prestige name here that I'd stock up on, and Laneige is the one I'd add to a cart.
- The "through 11/30" deadline is credible, and the hero says "24 hours–only," which gives me a deadline for today.

## What's weak

- It's the same sequin hero for the fourth day. Ulta would at least rotate the creative with its 21 Days drops.
- There's no Rouge or Beauty Insider points content anywhere. The sample bag is the only member-ish perk, and it has a $35 minimum and a code. I do the points math, and Sephora gave me none.
- The sample bag banner and the "Cyber Week is here! Save up to 50%" banner stack at the top, so the hero competes with two other offers before I reach it.
- The footer fine print is huge and hard to read. "On in-stock merchandise only" repeated on each tile feels like a gotcha.
- Drunk Elephant's "VIP 30% off starts now!" with one code in one block was cleaner. This email has too many CTAs and no single clear winner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make either the sample bag or the 30% off brands the hero, not both plus a 50% banner.
- 2. Add tier-aware content. A "Rouge: your extra 10% + points" strip would make me feel known, which Ulta does well with Diamond.
- 3. Rotate the brands daily, with a "new today" badge so each send feels fresh.
- 4. Cut the footer fine print and put the exclusions next to the offers they apply to.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 30% off Laura Mercier + free sample bag`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge, your Cyber Week picks are 30% off 🤩`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kiehl's, Laura Mercier & Youth to the People — 24 hours, in store and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code GRABYBAG for a free sample bag, plus 30% off top brands through 11/30.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based 700px layout with Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The main confirmed defect is a missing plain-text part (0 chars). Authentication and unsubscribe-header gaps are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what's visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed and 40 footer, utility and social links were skipped, so the skipped links are unverified.
- **Redirect tracking:** links go through `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a20df…?__dU__=…`, a per-recipient redirect. Destination URLs and UTM parameters aren't visible in the truncated source, so they can't be audited here.
- **Tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…' style="display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all"/>` is a hidden open pixel. It has no width or height attributes, which is minor.
- **Schema markup:** the JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a defined schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>` from email, so this is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper is `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. The only media query is `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)`, and it contains no rule that collapses this wrapper. On narrow viewports this likely causes horizontal scroll or scaling, unless the inner tables are fluid. The inner tables use `max-width:700px; width:100%`, so this needs a real-device check.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS rules, and some parsers may drop the rules that follow. The `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc` rules come right after it.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The impact is negligible in email.
- **Alt text (QA warning):** the only image flagged is the hidden 1x1 tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`). It is hidden with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`, so this is a benign false positive. No other alt-text failures were reported in the visible portion.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The `__dU__=` parameter on links is a resolved per-recipient identifier.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability and accessibility, and some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these. This is unconfirmed, so verify against the raw original message. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
