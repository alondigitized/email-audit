# Going back to school?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- "Going back to school?" — I'm 29, no kids, no PE periods in my life anymore, so this one just doesn't have my name on it. It's a fine execution of a very tired seasonal trope: Boy Brow, Lash Slick, Stretch Balm Concealer, three products Glossier has been running as "essentials" since basically forever, now repackaged under a school-supply framing that reads like it was written for a 22-year-old going back to campus, not someone Rouge-tier deep into prestige beauty rotation.
- What actually caught my eye was the macro concealer shot — that swipe of Stretch Balm under-eye is genuinely nice product photography, the kind of clean, skin-real imagery Glossier does well and that e.l.f. SKIN's Bronzing Drops shot (5.00/10, the one I read this morning) also nailed. But nothing here is new, nothing is discounted, and there's zero "Cheek Week"-style campaign momentum — I've now sat through four Glossier sends in a week building an actual narrative around blush, and this just... drops that thread entirely for a random essentials bundle with a "back to school" wrapper slapped on.
- I'm not clicking. It's a hard scroll-and-archive. I wouldn't forward it — there's nothing here worth telling a friend about, no launch, no deal, no exclusivity.

## What's working

- The hero shot of the model applying Boy Brow is clean and on-brand — Glossier's signature soft, real-skin aesthetic comes through.
- The Stretch Balm Concealer macro close-up is the strongest visual in the email — tactile, honest, no over-retouching.
- Layout is easy to scan on mobile — alternating image/copy blocks with clear CTA buttons under each product.
- "Even more sets" cross-sell at the bottom (Skincare Icons, Full Body Routine) at least surfaces a real 20% off, buried as it is.

## What's weak

- "Going back to school" is a demographic miss for me — I'm not shopping for PE-period-proof makeup, and there's no flex to signal this could also read as "new season, new routine" for someone my age.
- Zero mention of my Rouge or Diamond status, no points multiplier, no member pricing anywhere — Sephora and Ulta both work tier math into nearly every send I open, and Glossier just skips it entirely.
- All three "essentials" are existing SKUs with full price and no urgency — no launch-day energy, no bundle discount on the hero offer, just "Choose your shade" buttons.
- The actual 20% off (Skincare Icons, Full Body Routine) is buried at the very bottom under three full product blocks — Ulta would never bury a live percentage that far down.
- Zero continuity with the Cheek Week campaign I've watched all week (8/11 launch through 8/18 "ends tonight") — feels like a completely different marketing team forgot what shipped yesterday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% off Skincare Icons / Full Body Routine bundles up top as the hero offer — that's the only real discount in this email and it's currently invisible unless you scroll past four screens.
- 2. Ditch "back to school" entirely for my segment or reframe it as a general "new season, new routine" angle that doesn't skew so young.
- 3. Add tier context — even a simple "Rouge members, these ship free" line would make this feel less like a stranger blast.
- 4. Tie back to the Cheek Week thread that just wrapped — a "loved Cheek Week? here's your next essential" bridge keeps campaign continuity instead of feeling like whiplash.
- 5. Give at least one of the three essentials an actual bundle price instead of three separate full-price CTAs.
