# SALE: up to 50% off this National Lip Day 💄
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, National Lip Day — sure, I'll bite, because at least this is a real occasion and not just another "spot it, shop it" filler day. Eight lip products in a clean grid with actual struck-through pricing (MAC $25→$15, Charlotte Tilbury $28→$19.60) is exactly the kind of scannable layout that gets me to actually read prices instead of skimming past. But "0 points" is sitting right up top again, and I flagged this exact same issue on both of yesterday's 7/28 sends — either sync my Beauty Insider account correctly or don't show the number at all, because a Rouge member with two years of history does not have zero points and seeing it every single email is starting to feel like a glitch nobody's fixing.
- The grid itself is solid — good brand mix (MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, Gisou, Summer Fridays, Laneige, Make Up For Ever, Clinique, Wonderskin), real discounts, "CLEAN" badges on the ones that qualify. But then it sprawls into a DAE mousse launch, a $35-and-under fragrance/skincare bundle, and a "Buy More, Save More" teaser for something that doesn't even start until 7/31 — three different offers competing with the lip sale that's supposedly the headline. Sephora, compare this to your own 7/26 "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves" send that scored an 8 for me — that one stayed on-message. This one dilutes a good idea with unrelated cross-sells before I even get to the loyalty tier bar.
- I'd click into the lip grid, specifically Charlotte Tilbury or Summer Fridays since I've been eyeing both. I wouldn't forward this — it's fine, not exciting — and I'm marking it read after I check if the $15 MAC price is actually cheaper at Ulta this week, because that's just how my brain works now.

## What's working

- The 8-product lip grid with visible strikethrough pricing is genuinely the best layout element here — I can price-compare in two seconds without clicking anything.
- "CLEAN" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays give me the ingredient signal I actually care about without extra clicks.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar down at the bottom highlights ROUGE in red — small thing, but it's nice to see my actual tier called out instead of a generic "member" badge.
- "Enjoy Same-Day Delivery, FREE shipping†, in-store pickup & more" up top hits real Rouge-tier logistics perks, not fluff.

## What's weak

- "0 points" — same broken-looking balance I called out in both 7/28 emails ("Something special awaits"). This is now three sends in a row with the same glitch and it's starting to actively erode trust in the loyalty math.
- The email can't stay on-topic — National Lip Day headline gets buried under a DAE mousse launch, a $35-and-under grab bag, and a Buy More Save More promo that doesn't even start for two more days.
- No shade or skin-tone personalization on the lip products, which is a miss for a lip-focused send — Sephora has the data on my Rouge purchase history, use it.
- The "Buy More, Save More" banner starting 7/31 has zero business being in a National Lip Day email dated 7/29 — it's just noise pulling focus off the actual sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to just the lip grid plus the tier bar — kill the DAE launch and the $35-and-under module entirely. One offer, one message, higher conversion.
- 2. Fix the points display or hide it. This is the third consecutive email showing "0 points" for a two-year Rouge member — it's a data bug, not a design choice, and it's actively making me distrust the whole loyalty program messaging.
- 3. Add a "Recommended for your skin tone" row using my Rouge purchase history — even three suggested shades would beat a flat product grid.
- 4. Move "Buy More, Save More" to its own send closer to 7/31 instead of teasing it three days early inside an unrelated sale email.
