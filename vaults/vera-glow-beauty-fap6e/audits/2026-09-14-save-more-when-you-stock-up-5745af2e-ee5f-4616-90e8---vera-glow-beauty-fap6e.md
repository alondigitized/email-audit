# Save More When You Stock Up 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Sally, again — sixth time I've logged this brand and it's STILL the Appiversary well they're drawing from, just now with an actual tiered spend ladder attached: $10 off $40, $20 off $80, $30 off $100 with code SAVEMORE, ends 9/21. That's a real step up from the flat 30NAILS and 20%-off sends I flagged on 9/4, 9/7, 9/10, and 9/11 — at least there's math I can do here. But the whole thing is once again gated behind "Shop in app," and it's hair color developer and a bondbar kit, not one single thing in my actual cart categories.
- I'm not opening the app for this. If I were deep into a hair color box dye situation I might screenshot the tier math for myself, but forward to a friend? No — this reads like Sally emailing their core stylist-adjacent customer, not a Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond prestige shopper like me. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The $10/$20/$30 spend-tier math is genuinely clear and I can do it in my head without a calculator, unlike Sephora's buried "up to 19,000 more hairs" K18 framing this week.
- The hero model with the color-care haul actually looks like she's mid-routine, not a generic stock smile — feels more real than Skechers' recycled die-cast-truck creative I keep flagging.
- Free 2-hour delivery and buy-online-pickup-in-store banners up top are legit convenience signals if I actually needed this stuff today.
- Klarna 4-interest-free-payments strip is a nice touch for a bigger bondbar-kit purchase, even if $14.99 barely needs financing.

## What's weak

- Every meaningful offer is locked behind "Shop in app" — three separate CTAs push me to download an app I don't have, which is more friction than Glossier's straight-to-product Boy Brow send or e.l.f.'s single clean lip-oil drop this week.
- Zero personalization — no name, no nod to what I've bought before, no tier/loyalty math for me specifically, just generic Rewards program mentions in the fine print.
- The product grid is 100% hair color/developer plus a bondbar kit — nothing for someone who's cross-shopping Sephora Beauty Insider events and Ulta 21 Days, so this doesn't feel targeted at me at all.
- This is the same Appiversary campaign wrapper I've now seen five times in ten days — the escalating urgency ("ends September 21" this time vs "Last Day" on 9/11) is starting to feel manufactured rather than credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Let me shop the tiered discount on the website, not just in-app — that single change would unlock the whole email for people like me who aren't installing a hair-supply app.
- 2. Segment this: if I've never bought hair color, don't lead with developer 4-packs — show me something adjacent (styling tools, nail, skincare) that's actually in my basket history.
- 3. Stop reusing "Appiversary" as the umbrella for every September send — differentiate the beat like ILIA at least varies its VIP-sale framing, or I'll tune out the whole month.
- 4. Put the $10/$20/$30 tier math in the subject line itself — it's the strongest asset in this email and it's currently buried mid-scroll.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- One tracking pixel loads over plain HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&dtm_cmagic=f6b5f8&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook mixed-content policies), risking the pixel silently failing to fire.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 images flagged with missing/empty `alt` text, including all 4 Evergage `summary.png` pixels, the `mon-trk.com` pixel, the `myvisualiq.net` impression pixel, the `dotomi.com` UCM pixel, and the image with an empty `src`.
