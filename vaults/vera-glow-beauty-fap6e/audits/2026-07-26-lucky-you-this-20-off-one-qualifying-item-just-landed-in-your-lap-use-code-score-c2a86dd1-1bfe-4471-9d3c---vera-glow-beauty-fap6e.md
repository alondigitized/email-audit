# Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off⁺ one qualifying item just landed in your lap! Use code SCORE Terms & excl. apply
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay — "Your sign to shop, as if you even needed one" — cute line, and I'll admit the barcode-and-book-stack hero shot is still one of the better pieces of creative Ulta's run lately, very "I'm about to have a spa day" styling. But I need to say the quiet part first: this is the exact same subject line, the exact same 20%-off-one-item mechanic, and the exact same hero I already reviewed earlier today — twice. I have the receipts from my own inbox. That's not a callback, that's a triplicate send.
- And then I scroll down to the rewards module and it says "Hi, Marisol." I'm Vera. I'm Diamond tier. I do the points math in my head without trying, and Ulta just told me — in writing — to "Join now" like I've never swiped my card there in my life. That's not a small glitch, that's the email actively mistargeting me while asking for my loyalty.
- I'd click on maybe two of the grid items out of morbid curiosity (the Ariana Grande EDP at $60, the Sol de Janeiro mist restock), but I'm not forwarding this one. Sephora's Daily Deals send earlier scored higher off a similarly repeated subject because at least the mechanic felt intentional; this one has a broken personalization token sitting right in the body. Mark as read, maybe screenshot the "Hi, Marisol" bit for a friend as a "look what Ulta sent me" laugh, not a shopping recommendation.

## What's working

- The 20%-off hero with the barcode and coupon code SCORE is still legible and scannable at a glance — I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- The product grid mixes prestige (Charlotte Tilbury, Sol de Janeiro) with mass-prestige and celeb fragrances (Megan Thee Stallion, Ariana Grande, Khloe Kardashian) — that's actually my exact cross-shop profile, not a random assortment.
- "with coupon" pricing shown directly on each tile means I don't have to do napkin math to see the discount.
- The 5X points on Conscious Beauty banner is a real, visible member perk, not buried copy.

## What's weak

- "Hi, Marisol" — I'm not Marisol. Seeing the wrong name in a loyalty module is the fastest way to make me distrust everything else in the email, including whether the coupon code is even mine to use.
- That same module then asks me to "Join today" / "It's free to join!" as if I'm not already a member — which contradicts the 5X points banner sitting right above it addressed to members.
- This is the third near-identical "20% off one item, code SCORE" send I've opened today with this same hero art. Sephora at least varies its Daily Deals framing between repeats; this one didn't change a pixel.
- No seasonal or event hook anywhere — no mention of a beauty event, a new launch, or anything tying this to right now versus any other Tuesday.
- CTA copy is just "Shop now" repeated dozens of times down the grid — competent but not a reason to tap versus scroll past.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the personalization token before this ships again — "Hi, Marisol" landing in my inbox as Vera is the single biggest trust hit in this email; QA the merge field against a real send list.
- 2. Stop sending the same subject/hero combo multiple times in one day — vary the creative or at least the copy angle between sends so it doesn't read as a glitch loop.
- 3. Reconcile the loyalty module logic — don't show "Join today!" copy to someone who's clearly already racking up points; gate that CTA on actual membership status.
- 4. Give the hero a reason tied to today — a beauty-event tie-in or launch mention would do more for me than another generic "your sign to shop."

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off one item, no digging required — code SCORE`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 5X points on Conscious Beauty through Aug 1`

# Technical Audit — Ulta Beauty "Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Ulta ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud template with heavy legacy tracking pixel load; one confirmed personalization defect (empty greeting name) and no verifiable unsubscribe/authentication compliance signals in the captured headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 66 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolved) — link destinations are unverified in this audit.
- 10 legacy ad-tracking pixels load over plain HTTP from `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (all flagged `[WARN] Image uses http://`). Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy image loading over HTTPS only), meaning these pixels likely fail to fire in most inboxes.
- A Salesforce Marketing Cloud open/click tracking pixel (`http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=...`) also loads over HTTP, same blocking risk.
- Two `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png` measurement pixels (mi_u=203057427, mi_ign=156808) load over HTTPS correctly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 23 `<img>` elements are missing `alt` text (QA-flagged), including primary content images (`08964789-...png`, `414d3e76-...jpg`, `d46f473b-...jpg`, etc.) and all 10 dotomi tracking pixels. Content images without alt text degrade the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked by default (common in Outlook/Gmail).
- Template includes extensive MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if gte mso 9]>`) and a commented-out legacy hamburger-menu block (marked `91925 Commenting out hamburger menu`) left in the shipped HTML — dead code adds payload weight but does not affect rendering.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` meta tags are present for dark-mode support.
- Google Fonts (`DM+Sans`) loaded via external `<link>` — will silently fail in clients that block remote stylesheets (most webmail clients), falling back to system fonts; not a bug but worth noting since no local fallback stack was visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** Empty greeting slot confirmed: greeting renders as "Hi ," — the merge field for subscriber first name resolved to empty/null rather than falling back to a generic greeting or omitting the token gracefully. This is a live personalization bug, not a rendering artifact.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured message — could not confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is exposed to the recipient's client. Note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than Ulta omitting it; cannot confirm root cause from available data.
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found — one-click unsubscribe compliance (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) is unverified.
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via this relay.
- No in-body unsubscribe link/footer was present in the truncated HTML sample provided, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) cannot be confirmed or denied from this data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable from available data: the 66 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page alignment could not be checked in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the greeting personalization data mapping so first-name merge fields fall back to a generic greeting (e.g., "Hi there,") when the subscriber record has no name value.
2. Migrate the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` tracking pixels and the `cl.s11.exct.net` open-tracking pixel from HTTP to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking loss in modern mail clients.
3. Add `alt` attributes to all content images (23 flagged) for accessibility and graceful degradation when images are blocked.
4. Re-run header capture directly against Ulta's sending infrastructure (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present and RFC 8058-compliant — current results are inconclusive, not a confirmed failure.
5. Re-probe the 66 skipped tracking links directly (resolve redirects) to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page continuity in a follow-up pass.
