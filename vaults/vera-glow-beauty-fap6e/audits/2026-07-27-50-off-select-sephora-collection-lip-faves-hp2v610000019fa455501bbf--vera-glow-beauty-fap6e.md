# 50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Sephora hitting me with a lip sale again, and my first thought is "didn't I just read this?" Because I basically did — my 7/26 read of "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves ⭐" scored an 8, and now two days later I'm getting "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩" tied to National Lip Day. Same percentage, same category, different emoji. That's not a new offer, that's a rerun with a disco ball slapped on it.
- The top banner itself is actually clean — pink hero, "Today Only!" urgency, clear SHOP NOW button, I know exactly what I'm being asked to do in the first three inches. But then the email just... keeps going. Gold "Just Landed at Sephora" banner, a whole Black-Owned fragrance launch section for RUHVEDA and INFLUXIOUS, a nine-item new-product grid, an app download push, a 4X points promo on Patrick Ta, "The Latest Lineup," and finally the loyalty tier footer. That's six separate campaigns stapled into one email under a subject line that only covers the first one.
- Would I click? Probably, because 50% off Sephora Collection is genuinely a good deal and I do stock up on my own products at these markdowns. Would I forward it to a friend? No — there's nothing here worth sharing, it's just Sephora's usual everything-bagel send. I'd tap Shop Now on the hero and ignore the rest, then archive.

## What's working

- The hero banner nails the basics — 50% off, specific collection, "Today Only" urgency, one clear SHOP NOW button, no scrolling required to see it.
- The 4X points on Patrick Ta with a promo code through 7/29 is exactly the kind of thing I actually do math on — that's real Rouge-brain bait.
- Footer shows my tier ladder (Insider/VIB/Rouge) plainly, so I'm not guessing where I stand.
- New fragrance section (RUHVEDA, INFLUXIOUS) with the Black-Owned badge is a nice discovery beat if I were in browsing mode, not sale mode.

## What's weak

- The subject and hero promise a lip sale; by section two I'm looking at brand-new fragrance launches that have nothing to do with lips. Bait-and-switch energy.
- This is the second near-identical "up to 50% on lip stuff" send from Sephora in three days — I flagged the exact same repetition complaint on the 7/26 send, and it's still not fixed.
- Six unrelated campaigns crammed into one email kills the hierarchy Ulta nails when it isolates a single offer (their "Lucky you 20% off" send scored a 9 from me for exactly that restraint).
- No single hero product or shade linked for the lip sale itself — it's a vague "shop the collection" CTA, not "shop this gloss."
- Zero reviews or social proof anywhere in a grid of nine new products, which is a missed trust cue Sephora usually has room for.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two emails.** Lip sale gets its own send with one hero product linked. Fragrance launch gets its own send later in the week. Stop merging urgency-driven sale copy with slow-browse discovery copy — ILIA's single hero-shot emails outperform this kind of grab-bag every time.
- 2. **Stop reusing "up to 50% off lip" as a subject line every 48 hours.** Rotate the angle — lead with National Lip Day as the hook instead of the discount, since I've now seen the percentage twice this week.
- 3. **Link the hero banner to a specific bestseller**, not the general collection page — give me one thing to want, not a category to dig through.
- 4. **Move the 4X points/Patrick Ta block up**, right under the hero — that's genuinely persona-relevant and it's currently buried past two other sections.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lip Day is today — 50% off Sephora Collection`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge lip stash just got 50% cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: 4X points on Patrick Ta through 7/29`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only — new fragrance drops too`
