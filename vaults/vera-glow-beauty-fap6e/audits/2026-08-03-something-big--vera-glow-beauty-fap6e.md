- Zero loyalty tie-in — no Rouge/Diamond callout, no points multiplier, nothing that acknowledges I'm a tier member. Sephora's own Beauty Insider Celebration email is running RIGHT NOW and DE doesn't reference it at all.
- The offer (free samples on $85+ orders) has nothing to do with the actual teaser — it's just DE's standing GWP mechanic slapped on top of a launch tease, which feels like two emails mashed into one.

- No product name, no ingredient hint beyond "clinical color," no shade range shown beyond two swatches — as someone who does the points-and-ingredients math before I buy, I need more than a guessing game to plan a cart.

ILIA already ran the full teaser→launch→ingredient-deep-dive arc in late July and by the third email it felt redundant even to me; don't make me sit through three DE emails to get basic info.

# Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant — "Something big..."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard MSO/XHTML transitional table-based layout with custom web fonts, but ships with unencrypted image assets, missing unsubscribe/authentication headers, and duplicate font-loading requests.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 tracking/redirect links (`mi.drunkelephant.com`, `everestengagement.com` domains) were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing since they are click-tracking/redirect endpoints — link destinations were not independently verified as live.
- Open-tracking pixel confirmed: `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoteaser_prod`.
- No broken/dead links identified in the reachable (non-tracking) portion of the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Duplicate font loading**: the three custom fonts (Brown Bold, Brown-Light, Brown-Thin, IDs `4150d76a...`, `6145ed70...`, `68d0f984...`) are each loaded twice — once via `<link rel="stylesheet">` in `<head>` and again via `@import` inside the `<style>` block for the same font IDs. This doubles font-fetch requests on clients that render both.
- **Missing `alt` text** on two images:
  - `o.gif` (open-tracking pixel) — acceptable for a 1x1 pixel, but flagged by QA.
  - `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image (this one is a rendered `<img>`, not a pixel — missing alt is a real accessibility gap for screen readers).
- **Non-HTTPS image sources** (4 social icons): Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, TikTok icons all load via `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/...`. Mixed-content/non-HTTPS assets are commonly blocked or flagged by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), risking broken social icons for recipients.
- Doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional with `xmlns:v`/`xmlns:o`/`xmlns:w` MSO namespaces present, indicating intentional Outlook/Word conditional support — no issue there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- One tracking URL embeds the recipient's email address in plaintext query string: `...everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad...`. Passing raw PII (email address) as a URL parameter is a technical exposure risk — it can leak via `Referer` headers to third-party domains (social icon links, embedded fonts) if the recipient clicks through, and persists in browser history/link-scanning logs.
- No other merge-tag placeholders (e.g., unresolved `{{first_name}}`-style tokens) were observed in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers (may be a relay artifact rather than sender omission — flagged as WARN, not confirmed absent at origin).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — authentication status of this message is unverified via the captured headers.
- Unsubscribe link presence/placement in the visible HTML body could not be confirmed from the truncated source provided.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking uses ESP-native parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoteaser_prod`) rather than standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query params — no standard UTM tagging was observed on the reachable tracking pixel.
- Destination/landing-page URLs are wrapped behind `everestengagement.com` click-redirects, so final landing page and UTM alignment could not be verified (redirects were skipped per the QA probe).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the redundant `@import` font declarations in `<style>` since the same fonts are already loaded via `<link rel="stylesheet">` — cuts 3 duplicate network requests.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `everestengagement.com` rendered image (non-pixel).
3. Serve the four social icons (`image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`) over `https://` instead of `http://`.
4. Avoid placing raw recipient email addresses in outbound tracking-link query strings; use an opaque token/ID instead to prevent PII leakage via referrer headers.
5. Confirm at the ESP/relay level that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe), and that SPF/DKIM/DMARC results are present — current data only shows they weren't captured by the relay, not that they're absent at origin.
