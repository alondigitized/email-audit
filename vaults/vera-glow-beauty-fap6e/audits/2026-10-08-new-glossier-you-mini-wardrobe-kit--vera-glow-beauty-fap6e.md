- The gifting angle fits the season, and the cookie-tray gift guide module at the bottom is a nice soft landing.

## What's weak

- No price on the kit or the full-size bottles. For a gift set I can't tell if it's $30 or $80.
- No Glossier Rewards or points mention. There's no earn rate, no member angle and nothing to calculate.
- The body copy looks clipped at the right edge in the Doux and Soie blocks ("makes…", "this bright, bea…"). I can't read the full sentences.
- Five identical "Shop now" buttons tell me nothing about where each one goes.
- There's no urgency or reason to buy this week, like a ship-by date, a limited run or a GWP. "NEW!" is the only hook.
- It's the second gift-oriented Glossier send in three days, and the gift guide it points to still has no offer.

- 1. Put the price in the hero, with a Rewards line under it. For example: "$XX · earn points on every order" or a member early-access window. That one change would move this from a 6 to an 8 for me.
- 2. Fix the text clipping in the two-column blocks so every scent description reads in full.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop the Mini Kit," "Shop Doux," "Shop Soie." Drop the repeated "Shop now."
- 4. Add a real reason to act now, like a holiday ship-by date, a gift-with-purchase threshold or limited-run language.
- 5. Don't send the gift guide again with nothing new in it. If the guide is the destination, put a price or a bundle in the email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
