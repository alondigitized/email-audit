# 1, 2, or 3 sweeps?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a pretty blush tutorial with the real money buried in the bottom third. The "the build-up" hero and the one-, two-, three-layer "blushed / blushier / blushiest" graphic are a smart way to explain buildable color. But I opened it expecting a sale, and the 30% off Multi-Stick is a tiny badge on a stick photo, then a line of body copy after two screens of scrolling. The only offer at the top is a thin coral bar, "Free mini eye stylus w/ $75+*", and I'd skim right past it.
- This is better than the 11/2 "[YOUR SHADE HERE]" send in one way: it has a clear product story. Sephora would put "30% off" in the hero and Ulta would put it in the subject line. ILIA makes me hunt for it. I'd click Soft Focus Blush out of curiosity. I wouldn't forward it, because there's no points math to share. I'd skim it and move on.

## What's working

- The blushed / blushier / blushiest graphic over a deep-skin model teaches buildable color in one glance.
- The "Do you like your blush barely there or bright + bold?" copy is conversational and clearly tied to the single "Shop Soft Focus Blush" button.
- The Precision Powder Brush pairing is a believable add-on to the blush.
- The footer trust row has Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Pact and "Personalized Shade Match". It's a useful reminder, and it fits my clean-beauty habits.
- It's one hero product with one secondary offer, so the email doesn't feel like a clutter dump.

## What's weak

- The 30% off is buried. It's a small "30% OFF" circle on the Multi-Stick photo, and the body copy only mentions it below the fold.
- The free gift bar at the top is tiny, and the $75+ threshold is wrong for me. After the 10/30 free-mascara send and the 10/16-10/19 VIP gift pile-on, I'm tired of ILIA's gift-with-purchase asks.
- Offer clarity is thin. I can't tell whether the 30% applies to the blush, only the Multi-Stick, or both, and the asterisk says "see site for details."
- The email never mentions ILIA VIP perks. There's an "ILIA VIP" icon in the footer and nothing about points or tier benefits. Sephora's Insider emails state the tier math right up front.
- The subject "1, 2, or 3 sweeps?" gives me no hint of an offer. It reads like a quiz, and the build-up graphic never says "sweeps."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "30% off Multi-Stick" in the hero and subject, then keep the blush tutorial as the secondary beat. Ulta's $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder works because the savings is the first thing you see.
- 2. Make the offer clear. Say which products are 30% off and the end date, like "ends Sunday." Drop the "see site for details" asterisk.
- 3. Add a VIP line near the top, such as "VIP: earn 2x on blush." I'm always doing points math, and ILIA gives me nothing to calculate.
- 4. Add shade names under the model, such as "Shown in Soft Focus Blush in [shade]," plus a swatch row. Last week's "[YOUR SHADE HERE]" at least did shade-matching.
- 5. Cut the free-eye-stylus bar or turn it into a clear mini banner. Glossier at least runs one hook per send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Multi-Stick + the blush build-up`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Blushed, blushier, blushiest (30% off sticks)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Soft Focus Blush in 1, 2 or 3 layers, plus 30% off all Multi-Stick shades.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer your flush, save 30% on Multi-Stick for a limited time.`
