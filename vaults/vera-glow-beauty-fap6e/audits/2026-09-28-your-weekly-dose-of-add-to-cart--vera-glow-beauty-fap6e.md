# your weekly dose of “add to cart” 💅
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- "Bestie" — okay, e.l.f. is really leaning into the TikTok-little-sister voice again, and a Shimmer Glimmer Glitter Hair Stick that "brings buildable pink-to-blue shimmer" is giving me flashbacks to Claire's, not a Rouge-tier makeup routine. I'm not the hair-glitter customer. This is a big step down in sophistication from last week's "Halo Glow Goddess Balm" send (9/24, 7.00/10) where the model shots actually sold me on the product.
- Where this email redeems itself is the mascara GWP — full-size clear brow & lash mascara free at $50, no code, just add to cart. That's a real, honest offer with zero friction, and it's buried mid-scroll after a TikTok pitch and a random pumpkin-latte-coded "goodbye summer" tile grid that means nothing to me. Sephora's Living Proof send buried its deal behind a free-gift banner too (5.00/10) — e.l.f. is doing the same trick, just with a hair-glitter stick instead.
- Would I click? Maybe scroll straight to "shop new arrivals" out of habit, but I'm not opening TikTok Shop for this, and I'm not forwarding it — nothing here screams "you need to see this" to a friend.

## What's working

- The free full-size mascara at $50 spend with no code is a genuinely clean, no-friction GWP — better than Ulta's "20% off one item, excludes prestige" mechanic I keep seeing (6.00/10, three sends running).
- "SHOP BEST SELLERS" / "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" buttons are clear, high-contrast, thumb-friendly.
- The vegan/cruelty-free banner and "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face" reinforce brand values I already associate with them — consistent with their voice.
- Loyalty touchpoints (Beauty Squad app, receipt scanning, free shipping) are all present in the footer, which at least acknowledges I might be a member.

## What's weak

- The hero product — a hair glitter stick sold exclusively via TikTok Shop — has nothing to do with me as a 29-year-old Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond shopper; this reads like it was written for a 19-year-old.
- "Goodbye summer, hello fall 🍂" with a pumpkin-latte photo and a bag of what looks like brown sugar is such a non-sequitur grid — no product names, no prices, I have no idea what I'm looking at until I tap.
- Zero personalization — no name, no skin tone/type callout, no tier math, nothing that treats me like the loyalty member I actually am.
- The best offer (free mascara) is the fourth scroll stop, not the hero — Sephora's Daily Deals send got dinged for this exact bait-and-switch pattern on 9/28 already, and e.l.f. is repeating it in the same inbox day.
- Category nav (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) is just flat gray bars with no product previews — visually dead weight at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the mascara GWP as the hero, not buried after TikTok and a fall mood board — that's the actual offer with a real dollar threshold, put it above the fold.
- 2. Cut or replace the hair-glitter-stick hero entirely for a Rouge/Diamond-tier audience — swap in a bestseller or the fall skincare/makeup pairing that's teased in the second block, with actual product names and prices.
- 3. Add basic segmentation — even "since you love [X category]" would beat the generic blast, especially after four e.l.f. sends I've now logged this month with no personalization.
- 4. Give the "goodbye summer, hello fall" grid actual labels — right now it's four unlabeled lifestyle photos I have to tap blind to understand.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall refresh + a mascara on us`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — just add to cart and save`
