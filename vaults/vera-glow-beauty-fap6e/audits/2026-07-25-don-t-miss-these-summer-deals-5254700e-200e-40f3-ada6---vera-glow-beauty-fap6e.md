# Don't miss these summer deals 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Sally rotating back to the BOGO well — "Buy two, get one free" is almost word-for-word the structure from their July 20th send ("More hair care, more savings"), just with different SKUs stapled on. I clocked that instantly and it took some of the wind out of my sails before I even got to the wax bundle. That said, I'll give credit where it's due: the flash sale banner up top — $10 off $50, $20 off $75, code SUMMERSAVINGS — is the clearest, most stackable offer structure I've seen from this brand in weeks, and it's sitting right at the top where I don't have to hunt for it.
- The thing this email is missing that keeps nagging at me is any loyalty math. I'm Sephora Rouge, Ulta Diamond, and I am ALWAYS doing points math in my head — Sally has zero rewards tier language anywhere in this send, just straight percentage-off codes. Compare that to how Ulta at least gestures at member pricing (even when they botch it, like that TATCHA GWP email that told me an event had already ended). Sally isn't even trying to make me feel like a known customer with a point balance — I'm just a coupon code recipient here.
- Would I click? Probably yes, actually — the wax bundle at $29.99 is genuinely useful for summer and the deal math is dead simple. Would I forward it? No. This isn't a "look at this" email, it's a "yeah I'll swing by the app later" email. Marking it read, maybe tapping through on my next scroll.

## What's working

- The flash sale banner ($10/$50, $20/$75, code visible, hard end date July 26) is the cleanest offer stack Sally's sent me all month — no digging required.
- "Last call for summer savings" hero copy is doing actual seasonal work, not just vibes, and pairs a real deadline (July 29) with the visual.
- The Sliick Wax Bundle callout ($29.99, itemized with what's included) is the one moment in this email that feels like a real product pitch instead of a coupon dump — that's category-relevant for me since I do wax between salon visits.
- Klarna financing strip is a nice touch for a $30-plus cart, even if I don't personally need it.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty or rewards-points language anywhere — for a brand chasing my dollars in a category I'm this deep into (Rouge, Diamond), that's a miss. Even Ulta at least name-drops member pricing, badly as they sometimes execute it.
- The "ENDS SOON" scrolling ticker under the flash sale banner renders as a cropped, cut-off row of text on both edges — reads like a layout bug, not a design choice.
- This is functionally a rerun of the July 20th "buy two get one free" structure with new product photography slapped on — I've seen this exact mechanic from Sally twice in five days now.
- Subject line "Don't miss these summer deals" tells me nothing — no percentage, no product, no urgency I can act on before opening. Compare to e.l.f.'s "A major new era for e.l.f." which at least signaled something new was happening.
- The fine-print block at the very bottom (price variance, exclusions, rewards calc disclaimers) is dense enough that I'm not scrolling to read it — it just sits there as legal cover, not clarity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the flash sale offer ($10/$20 off) INTO the subject line — that's the strongest asset in this email and it's currently buried behind a generic subject. This is the single biggest lift available here.
- 2. Fix the cropped "ENDS SOON" ticker — either make it a clean static badge or drop it; right now it looks broken, not urgent.
- 3. Add any loyalty signal — even "Rewards members earn double points on wax" would close the gap with how Ulta/Sephora talk to tier shoppers like me.
- 4. Stop repeating the BOGO mechanic two sends in a row — rotate to a different structure (tiered spend threshold, gift-with-purchase) so it doesn't read as copy-paste like Skechers' "Your Items Are Waiting!" rerun.
- 5. Swap generic "Shop now" CTAs for category-specific copy on at least the hero button — "Shop Summer Hair Deals" pulls harder than a fourth identical "Shop now."
