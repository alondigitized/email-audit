# How about a great skin day?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Merry and Bright, For Starters" — Drunk Elephant is doing holiday bundles now, and honestly this is the cleanest send I've gotten from them in weeks. The $99-value-$158 math is right there in a price tag graphic, no digging required, and the kit itself (Protini, C-Luma, Beste No. 9) is a genuinely coherent brighten-and-moisturize routine, not a random grab-bag. But it's also September 27th and this is "2026 HOLIDAY COLLECTION" — that's early even for the beauty-industry holiday creep, and it made me do a double take before I clicked past the yellow banner.
- The math checks out and the product knowledge is real, but there's zero acknowledgment I'm a Rouge/Diamond member anywhere in this email — no "as a VIP" nod, no bonus points language, nothing. Compare that to Sephora's Hispanic Heritage Month send, which at least does the work of contextualizing why I should care beyond "here's a box." This is just a box.
- Would I click? Probably yes, actually — I'm a Protini loyalist and $99 for a full routine kit is a legit value play I'd run through my Sephora Beauty Insider points math before buying direct. Would I forward to a friend? Only if she's already an Elephant girl — this isn't a "look what I found" share, it's a "yeah I already knew this was coming" scroll-and-close.

## What's working

- The price/value callout ($99, value $158) is impossible to miss — it's sitting right on the hero image, no scrolling or squinting required.
- Product education is doing real work here — each of the three items gets a one-line "why this matters" instead of just a name and a shop button, which is more than Sally Beauty's typical send bothers with.
- The "Try This At Home" section teasing Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops with a 25-shade sample card is a smart secondary hook — it's new-launch energy layered onto a holiday-kit email instead of competing with it.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — light blue plaid backgrounds, consistent product photography, no visual bugs or overlapping text anywhere I can see.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty-tier acknowledgment — no VIP language, no member pricing, no "you've earned this" framing, which stings when I'm Rouge AND Diamond and I know for a fact Ulta's 21 Days emails at least gesture at "member exclusive."
- "2026 HOLIDAY COLLECTION" on September 27th is genuinely jarring — that's Halloween candy still on shelves energy, and it undercuts the "urgency" of anything else in the email.
- No shade/skin-type personalization anywhere — this is a one-size-fits-all kit pitch for a brand that literally sells a 25-shade concealer two sections down; the disconnect is a little funny.
- The GWP fine print at the bottom (free gift with $120+ orders, 9/23–9/29 window) is buried below the fold in tiny gray text — that's a real incentive to spend more and it's basically hidden, which is the opposite of what Glossier does when it wraps a GWP in urgency framing up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the GWP offer (free Kamo sample + Lala Retro deluxe sample on $120+) up into the hero or a dedicated banner — that's a genuine upsell lever buried in 8pt gray copy at the very bottom nobody scrolls to.
- 2. Add one line of loyalty acknowledgment — even "Rewards members, this kit earns double points" would close the gap with how Ulta and Sephora treat their tier shoppers.
- 3. Either commit to holiday framing hard (countdown, ship-by-Christmas date) or soften "2026 HOLIDAY COLLECTION" to "early access" language — right now it just reads premature.
- 4. Tie the Kamo teaser more tightly to the kit — a "complete your routine" cross-sell CTA instead of a separate section would lift click-through from people who came for the kit but stay for the concealer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `A full brightening routine, bundled and $59 off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: free samples on orders $120+, through 9/29.`
