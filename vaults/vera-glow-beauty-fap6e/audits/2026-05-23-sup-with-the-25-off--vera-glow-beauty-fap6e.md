# Sup with the 25% off?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so we're on email five of the Drunk Elephant Friends & Family campaign — I counted, and I'm not mad about it, but I am clocking it. The subject line "Sup with the 25% off?" actually made me do a small smile because DE is basically winking at the fact that yes, this is the fifth time they've told me about this deal since Tuesday. The banner copy — "WHY ARE YOU STILL READING THIS? GO SHOP!" — is the self-aware cherry on top. Points for reading the room.
- That said: I already bought. I went in on launch day when the F&F invite hit (scored it a 9) and again on the "Stock up" send on Wednesday. So at this point DE is preaching to the converted. The email isn't changing my mind — I'm already in — but I'm also not annoyed the way I am with, say, ILIA, who sent me eleven emails since May 16 with a plain-text "Marissa" voice that wore out its welcome by send four. DE is at least doing something different with the tone each time.

Balm, $100+ adds the Hydra and Bright set — because it genuinely changes the math on whether to push your cart over a threshold. For me? Already done. ## What's working

- **Meta-banner is genuinely charming.** "WHY ARE YOU STILL READING THIS? GO SHOP!" is the rare brand move that acknowledges over-sending without apologizing for it — it's confident.
- **GWP spend tiers are clearly structured.** "$75+ = full-size F. Balm" and "$100+ = Hydra + Bright set" are immediately scannable, which is exactly how I make cart decisions.
- **Product grid with visible prices is useful.** Seeing Protini, Babyfacial, Lala Retro, and Virgin Marula with their price points helps me do the math on whether a friend should build a qualifying cart.
- **Subject line is fresh.** 21 characters, no spam, no ALL CAPS — "Sup with the 25% off?" feels distinct from every other email in my inbox today (looking at you, Sally Beauty's third Memorial Day iteration).
- **Clean render, zero bugs.** No broken images, no lorem ipsum, layout holds on mobile — which Charlotte Tilbury cannot consistently say after her third send of the day yesterday.

## What's weak

- **No end date anywhere visible.** "Don't wait!" is not a deadline. Glossier's reminder send two days ago explicitly said the sale was still live with a specific close, which actually pulled me back to check my cart. DE is leaving that urgency on the table.
- **Fifth email, same core offer.** The meta-humor covers some of the fatigue but the underlying message is identical to the prior four. By now, if you haven't bought, you're probably a no regardless of which DE product is in the grid.
- **No personalization to my history.** Ulta sent me a tiered spend-and-save today that had my name in the subject. DE treating me like a stranger on email five is a miss — a "you last bought X, here's what pairs well at 25% off" would have actually moved me.
- **Fine print wall at the bottom.** Dense asterisk legalese in tiny type — I can't read it without pinching, and after enough of it I start wondering what the catch is even when there isn't one.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard end date to the hero.** "Sale ends Sunday" or a countdown changes the calculus for fence-sitters. Glossier did this cleanly on their reminder; DE can too.
- 2. **Personalize one touch.** Even "You've been with us since [year]" or "Based on your routine, these are the F&F picks for you" would make email five feel like a targeted note, not a blast.
- 3. **Cut the product grid or make it editorial.** Right now it's four random SKUs with "SHOP NOW" under each. Lean into a "complete the routine" story or "top five F&F picks for skincare obsessives" — give me a reason to care about the curation.
- 4. **Tighten the GWP messaging into the hero.** The spend tiers are buried in section two. Lead with "Spend $100, get the Bright set FREE" — that's a more compelling hook than "25% off sitewide" on its own.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully confirm from truncated source.** No UTM-decorated links are visible in the provided excerpt. Font asset URLs reference `drunkelephant.com` without UTM params, which is expected for assets — but CTA links must be verified against the full source.

**Third-party font CDN risk:** All `BrownBold`, `BrownLight`, and `BrownThin` weights are loaded from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. This is a licensing-gray-area font hosting service. If that CDN goes down or rate-limits, fallback rendering kicks in mid-send with no retry. Not a link tracking issue per se, but a load-time and legal exposure point.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Critical — broken CSS inside media queries:**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width: 600px) {
  <!-- *[class=contenttable] { width:414px !important;} -->
  <!-- *[class=mobile] { width:414px !important;height: auto !important;} -->
```
HTML comment syntax (`<!-- -->`) inside a `<style>` block is invalid. These two rules are dead — they will never apply. Any layout behavior that depended on `.contenttable` or `.mobile` resizing on mobile is silently broken.

**Triple font loading — redundant and slow:**
The same three Brown font variants are loaded three ways simultaneously:
1. `<link>` tags (top of `<head>`)
2. `@import` inside `<style>` (same URLs)
3. `@font-face` blocks (different source URLs for two of the three)

This fires up to 3× the font requests per weight. Most email clients will block external fonts regardless; this multiplies wasted load attempts.

**Universal overrides with side effects:**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none; max-height:1000000px; mso-line-height-rule:exactly}
*{line-height: 100%}
```
`line-height: 100%` applied via the universal selector will collapse line spacing on any element that does not explicitly override it — including multi-line alt text, legal footer copy, and unsubscribe text. This is a known legibility trap in Outlook and older Android clients.

**Missing `lang` attribute:**
`<html>` element has no `lang` attribute. Screen readers cannot infer language; some clients will mis-hyphenate or mis-render text.

**No `role="presentation"` confirmation:** Cannot confirm from truncated source whether layout tables carry `role="presentation"` — required for VoiceOver/NVDA to skip them.
