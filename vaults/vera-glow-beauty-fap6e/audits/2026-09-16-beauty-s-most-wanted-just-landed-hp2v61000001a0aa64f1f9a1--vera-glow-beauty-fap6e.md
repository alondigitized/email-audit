# Beauty’s most wanted just landed 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Beauty's Biggest Hits" is a decent hook and the 50% off Fenty/First Aid Beauty/Sephora Collection banner up top is the strongest offer Sephora's sent me all week — TODAY ONLY urgency actually reads as credible instead of the usual evergreen filler. But then I scroll two inches and there it is again: "You have 0 points" sitting right under the INSIDER header, same merge-field glitch I flagged on the 9/15 "points = rewards" send and the 9/15 "50% off select faves" send. I'm Rouge tier. I don't have zero points. Seeing that on a "beauty's most wanted" email is almost funny at this point.
- The Charlotte Tilbury setting powder callout and the eight-product grid (Jo Malone, YSL, Hourglass's new limited-edition palette, Estée Lauder) is genuinely well-curated — that's Sephora doing what Sephora does best, multi-brand prestige discovery in one scroll. - I'd click through on the 50% off banner or the Hourglass palette specifically. I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's a solid but not surprising send, and the points glitch makes me trust the "5X points" and "shampoo subscribe & save" callouts a little less than I should.

## What's working

- The 50% off First Aid Beauty/Sephora Collection/Fenty banner is the clearest, most specific offer Sephora's run this week, and "TODAY ONLY" actually feels time-bound instead of copy-pasted urgency.
- The product grid is real curation — Charlotte Tilbury, YSL Libre, Jo Malone, the new limited-edition Hourglass Ambient Lighting palette — this is exactly the cross-brand prestige mix that makes me open Sephora over Ulta.
- "GET 5X POINTS on all Sephora Collection" is a genuine points-math hook for someone like me who tracks Rouge tier status.
- Layout is clean — no overlapping text, no broken images, everything renders crisp from hero banner down to the footer.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" is STILL there under the INSIDER header — third time I've logged this exact glitch this week alone (both 9/15 sends). For Rouge tier, that's not a nitpick, that's the brand telling me it doesn't know who I am.
- Subject line "Beauty's most wanted just landed" is vague — no product, no % off, no category. The 50% off banner is the real headline and it's buried in the subject line entirely.
- No preview/preheader text rendered at all in my inbox view — that's wasted real estate when there's a 50% off deal that could've been teased there instead.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section header is filler copy that doesn't tell me anything about what I'm looking at.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points merge-field bug before anything else — this is the fourth-plus time I've seen it this week and it actively undercuts every loyalty CTA in the email (5X points, tier bar, etc.).
- 2. Pull the 50% off offer into the subject line — it's buried under vague "biggest hits" copy right now.
- 3. Add a real preheader that teases the Charlotte Tilbury launch or the Hourglass limited-edition palette instead of leaving it blank.
- 4. Give the product grid at least one line of specificity per item (shade range, skin type fit) — right now it's just name + price, no differentiation from a catalog page.
