# Still Deciding, Darling?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Third Charlotte Tilbury email today. *Third.* I already opened the scratch-card "Peel to Reveal" this morning — that one was actually clever — and then the "20% Off Icons" send hit right after. And now here's "Still Deciding, Darling?" landing in my inbox like CT doesn't trust me to function without a nudge every four hours. I am fatigued in a very specific, well-moisturized way.
- What I'm looking at is basically a nurture carousel: there's a product discovery section, a "complete your beauty profile" prompt, a virtual try-on module, a 1-2-1 consultation booking section, a returns explainer, and a shipping code. That's five distinct asks in one email. If this is supposed to be cart abandonment copy, it completely forgot to show me what I was supposedly "deciding" on. There's no cart. No product. No "you were looking at this" hook. It's just CT's onboarding greatest-hits deck dressed up with a "darling" to soften the confusion.
- I'd mark it as read. I wouldn't click, and I definitely wouldn't forward — nothing here is shareable or exciting. The morning's scratch-card was a gift link moment; this is a flowchart.

## What's working

- **15% off code in the top banner** — "UNLOCK A MAGICAL 15% OFF YOUR FIRST ORDER WITH CODE DARLING" is immediately visible and at least gives me a reason to scroll.
- **Brand voice is intact** — "Darling" reads as CT, not generic retail-bot. That counts for something after seven emails.
- **No render bugs** — clean layout, images loaded, text is legible at every section.
- **Loyalty tier icons in the footer** — the K-tier visual reinforces that this is a program with depth, even if the email doesn't lean into it.

## What's weak

- **"Complete your beauty profile" is an onboarding ask sent to a repeat customer** — I've been buying CT products for years. This reads like CT has zero memory of me, and compared to how Glossier segments their flows or how ILIA's nurture emails feel earned and personal, this is embarrassing.
- **Five competing CTAs with no clear hierarchy** — "See Icons," "Start Now," "Try Now," "Book Now," "Learn More," plus a shipping code. I don't know what CT actually wants me to do, so I'll do nothing.
- **"Still Deciding" subject implies cart abandonment, but there's no cart content** — If you're going to trigger a cart-recovery email, show me the lipstick I left behind. Ulta's personalized spend-and-save emails at least know my name and tier. This just knows I exist.
- **No urgency, no deadline** — the 20% off send this morning had temporal energy. This one floats in the void with a code and a vague hope.
- **Cadence is genuinely damaging trust at this point** — Three emails in one day. Drunk Elephant runs F&F sales with two sends max and it feels *curated*. This feels like CT hit "send all segments" and called it a day.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Show the actual product she was browsing** — If this is cart/browse abandonment, inject the SKU. One product image, the name, the price, a "Pick up where you left off" CTA. That's the entire email. Everything else is noise.
- 2. **Kill four of the five CTAs** — Choose one ask per email. The consultation booking and the returns section belong in a welcome series, not a re-engagement send.
- 3. **Add a deadline to the 15% code** — "Code expires Sunday" converts; "code exists somewhere" does not.
- 4. **Move the 15% code front and center into the hero**, not just the top banner — make it impossible to miss on mobile.
- 5. **Suppress this send if the subscriber already opened CT email today** — Simple cadence logic. Lush managed five sends in ten days without feeling spammy; CT sent three today and I'm filing mental unsubscribes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your cart's getting cold, Darling`
- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off — but only if you're ready`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The icons you were eyeing are still waiting. Code inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grab 15% off before the weekend — your call, darling.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Braze-sent table-based email with MSO VML conditionals and a responsive breakpoint at 620px. No catastrophic rendering failures apparent; several accessibility and tracking hygiene issues warrant attention.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Confirmed from source:**
- All links route through `clicks.charlottetilbury.com` (Braze click-tracking proxy). This is expected, but the logo anchor uses a long opaque Braze redirect URL — no evidence of UTM parameters being appended downstream without inspecting the final redirect destination.
- Logo `<a>` has `tabindex="-1"` applied: `<a href="https://clicks.charlottetilbury.com/..." target="_blank" style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">`. This removes the link from keyboard tab order entirely, breaking keyboard navigation.

**Requires verification (not determinable from truncated source):**
- Whether destination URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` after Braze redirect resolves.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Issue | Evidence |
|---|---|
| `<title>` is empty | `<title></title>` — screen readers announce tab/window title; blank is a miss |
| Logo anchor has `outline:none` + `tabindex="-1"` | `style="outline:none" tabindex="-1"` — removes focus ring and keyboard reachability |
| Web font loaded only for non-MSO clients | `<!--[if !mso]><!--><link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css"><!--<![endif]-->` — Outlook renders system fallback font; verify fallback stack is declared in inline CSS |

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- Image assets served from `cdn.braze.eu` — EU-origin CDN. Latency acceptable for EU recipients; US/APAC recipients may see slower image load. Not a blocker but worth noting if open rates are geographically skewed.
- UTM continuity unverifiable without resolving the Braze redirect chain. Must confirm `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` are present on landing page URLs, especially for the primary CTA buttons (rows 5–8 per CSS selectors `.row-5 .column-1 .block-5.button_block`).

---

### 7. Recommendations

1. **Add `alt` text to all images** — confirm logo `img` has descriptive alt; product images should have product name as alt text.
2. **Remove `tabindex="-1"` from the logo anchor** — there is no technical justification for pulling the primary brand link out of tab order.
3. **Replace `outline:none` with a visible focus style** — or remove the property entirely and let clients apply default focus rings.
4. **Add `<title>` content** — e.g., `<title>Charlotte Tilbury — Still Deciding, Darling?</title>`.
5. **Verify UTM params survive Braze redirect** — spot-check at least the primary CTA URL by clicking from a test send and inspecting the final URL in browser devtools.
6. **Confirm List-Unsubscribe-Post header** — required for Gmail bulk sender compliance (>5k/day threshold).
7. **Audit DKIM alignment** — ensure signing domain matches envelope `From` subdomain to avoid deliverability penalties.
