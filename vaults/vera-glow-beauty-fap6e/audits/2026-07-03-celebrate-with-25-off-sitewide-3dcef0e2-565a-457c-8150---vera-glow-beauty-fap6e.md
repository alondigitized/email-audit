# Celebrate with 25% off sitewide
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-eight. That's where I am with Sally, and the holiday gave them permission to reset the creative. After "Flash deals worth celebrating" landed in my inbox yesterday with the same Fourth of July energy, I expected today to feel like a stale sequel — but the patriotic nail art hero stopped me mid-scroll. Red, white, blue acrylics with stars? That's actually a *look* I'd screenshot for inspo, which means the hero is pulling double duty as content and commerce. Sitewide 25% off with a visible code (AMERICA25) makes the math instant: no stacking confusion, no floor, no "select items." That's the clarity I wish Ulta had shown when it inexplicably sent me an email with a hero banner reading "this event is over" back on June 30.
- What I can't forgive is the total absence of any loyalty acknowledgment. I've been opening Sally emails since at least June 24. I'm a rewards member. Sephora Stacking Weekend — which is literally on my Google Calendar — opened with member points front and center. Sally sent me this email like I'm a cold prospect who stumbled in from a Google ad.
- Would I click? Yes — I need developer and I'm not waiting for an Ulta GWP to justify the haul. Would I forward it? To my DIY color friend, absolutely.

## What's working

- **Nail art hero is doing real creative work** — the red/white/blue patriotic mani is seasonally sharp and genuinely inspirational; it reads as beauty content, not just a sale banner.
- **AMERICA25 code is front-of-hero, not buried** — I can see it without scrolling, I can type it from memory, and "sitewide" means I don't have to audit my cart for exclusions.
- **2-hour delivery callout** — this is a genuine differentiator from Sephora and Ulta who can't promise same-day on July 3rd; for a holiday weekend stock-up, this matters.
- **Product grid is curated correctly** — Color Brilliance, 20-volume developer, Wella toner, AGEbeautiful — these are the Sally-specific SKUs I actually come here for, not random fill.
- **Klarna integration is surfaced** — smart to show 4-payment on a stock-up send where someone might buy 3-4 SKUs at once.

## What's weak

- **No expiration on AMERICA25** — does this code die at midnight July 4th or does it run through the weekend? No deadline = no urgency = I bookmark this tab and forget it exists by Sunday.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment** — not one mention of Sally Beauty Rewards points on a sitewide 25% off send. This is the exact email where the points-math crowd (me) would stack rewards on top of the discount and it would feel like a VIP moment. Instead I'm treated like a stranger.
- **"Shop now" repeated four times** — the product grid CTAs are all identical. After ILIA's "Third One's the Charm" creative on their Buy 3 mechanic, this feels like a template default rather than a considered CTA.
- **Back-to-back July 4th emails** — yesterday was flash deals with holiday framing, today is sitewide with holiday framing. Two coded-holiday sends in 48 hours from the same brand tests my patience even when the offer is legitimately better.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard expiration date in the hero** — "25% off sitewide. Ends July 5 at 11:59 PM." This is the single highest-lift change; it converts "I'll look later" into "I need to shop now."
- 2. **Put loyalty points in the subhead** — "Plus earn Sally Rewards points on every dollar — stack it on top of 25% off." That one line turns this from a sale email into a member moment.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs** — "Shop Hair Color," "Shop Tools," "Shop Nails" in the product grid instead of four "Shop Now" defaults. The grid is already doing the category work; the buttons should close the loop.
- 4. **Space the holiday cadence** — yesterday's send was good; today's send is good. But let the first one breathe. A "last chance — AMERICA25 expires tonight" follow-up on July 4th morning would land harder than this feeling like Part 2 of a series.
- **Subject Alt A:** `AMERICA25: 25% off sitewide — ends July 4`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stock up before the holiday: 25% off everything at Sally`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hair color, developer, tools — 2-hour delivery available today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn Rewards points on top of 25% off — shop before it ends`
