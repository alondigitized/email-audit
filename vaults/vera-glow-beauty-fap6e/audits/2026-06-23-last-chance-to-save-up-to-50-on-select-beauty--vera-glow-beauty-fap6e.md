# Last chance to save up to 50% on select beauty 🫨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay I'm going to be real with you: I have reviewed four Sephora emails about this exact sale in the last two days. "The Major Summer Deals chapter is closing" landed twice on June 22. "ENDS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty" landed twice on June 23 — I gave both of those 8s. And now here is "Last chance to save up to 50% on select beauty" with a different shaking emoji and I genuinely cannot tell you if this is a fifth send or if Sephora's ESP has achieved sentience and is just panic-firing at my inbox until I capitulate. The 50%/25% tier structure is front and center, the "Beauty Insiders Get More" section with bonus points and same-day delivery is exactly the kind of loyalty math I care about as Rouge, and the MERIT "Just In" placement at the bottom is a nice signal that this isn't purely a clearance dump. - But here's the thing: I already clicked the 9am version of this same creative. I already did the points math. I already either bought or I didn't. "Last chance" from a brand that said "ENDS TODAY" six hours ago is just crying wolf with a different emoji. I'd archive this without clicking. I would not forward — my group chat already has the link from when I shared the earlier send.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth Sephora email about this exact sale in 48 hours — "Last chance" has lost all credibility when I got "ENDS TODAY" from the same address earlier today
- The email is extremely long and multi-module — by "Just Arrived" I've completely forgotten why I opened and what the actual end time of the sale is
- No timestamp anywhere — "last chance" without a specific hour means nothing; Charlotte Tilbury's "Early Access: Up To 45% Off Beauty Kits In The App!" at least gave me a concrete access window
- Zero Rouge-specific personalization — same "Beauty Insiders Get More" block I'd see as a basic BI member; as Rouge I want stacked point math, not the generic tier callout
- "Select beauty" continues to do no work — I still don't know if my replenishment items are included without clicking through

## Recommendations

- **#1 most impactful:** If you're re-sending to non-openers on the same day, change the creative — different hero product, different product grid, at minimum add the sale end time (e.g., "ends at midnight PT"). Sending a synonym subject with the same image to openers is a cadence mistake that burns list trust.
- **#2:** Pin a countdown timer or at least a specific end time in the hero. "Last chance" is meaningless. e.l.f. does "ships FREE" with a deadline date; Glossier's final-hours email on June 19 gave me an actual cutoff. Sephora can do this.
- **#3:** Personalize the Beauty Insiders block by tier. Show me Rouge math: "You earn 1.25x points this event — here's what 500 bonus points + your current balance gets you toward the next reward." That's the email I forward to my friends.
- **#4:** Trim the email to 3 sections max during a finale send — hero offer, 3-4 must-have SKUs, loyalty callout. The "Spot It. Shop It." and "Just Arrived" modules are noise at the bottom of a last-chance email.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Locking zoom prevents low-vision users from enlarging content. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `user-scalable=yes`.

**Issue — `text-size-adjust: none` applied to broad selector set**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applying `text-size-adjust: none` to `body` and block elements globally suppresses OS-level font scaling for visually impaired users. The intent (prevent Outlook/iOS auto-resize) is legitimate, but scope should be narrowed to `table, td` only.

**Issue — `robots` meta value has a space**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
The correct token is `noindex` (no space). Most crawlers are lenient, but the malformed value is technically non-compliant. Correct to `content="noindex, nofollow"`.

**Observation — XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype**
Expected for broad email-client compatibility (Outlook 2016 and earlier). Not an issue.
