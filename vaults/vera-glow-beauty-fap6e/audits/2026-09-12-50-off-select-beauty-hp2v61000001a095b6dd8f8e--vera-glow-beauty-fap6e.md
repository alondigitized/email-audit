## What's weak

- Zero Rouge-specific benefit — no points multiplier, no early access, no "as a Rouge member" language; my tier badge only shows up in the generic INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE nav bar at the bottom.

- Rewards Bazaar link at the bottom is just tacked onto the nav bar again, not integrated as actual content this time.

- 2. Stop recycling "TODAY ONLY" three sends in a row — if it's really daily, say "New drops daily" instead of implying urgency that isn't real.
- 3. Cut the grid from 8 unrelated SKUs to a curated 4 tied to something I've browsed or bought — Sephora has the data, this feels like it wasn't used.
