# Shipping's on us! 📦 last chance!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- Day 17. I know it's 17 because yesterday's "So Blurreal Lip & Cheek Whip" send was 16 and I gave it a 9 — the best in the streak — so I noticed immediately when this dropped the next morning. And here's the thing: e.l.f. already ran free shipping three days ago. June 23. "e.l.f.rything ships FREE! 📦" — scored it an 8, noted the offer, moved on. Now we're back with the exact same offer dressed in "last chance" urgency, no deadline date anywhere in the hero copy. I've seen this exact move from Ulta — they ran their 20%-off email, then sent "ends tonight!" as a follow-up and I called it out directly. e.l.f. just borrowed the same playbook.
- That said, the email itself is clean. The product lineup has real merit: Haze Liner at $13, the Lip & Cheek Whip at $8 (yesterday's hero, now demoted to second slot in a roundup), Lip Butter at $5, Cream Bronzer at $7. These prices are peak e.l.f. and I can do the math in three seconds. The hierarchy is logical — shipping offer up top, products below, small picks grid at the bottom. No layout chaos, no broken images, nothing offensive.
- Would I click? Probably on the $5 Lip Butter or the Whip because the prices are genuinely good — but the email didn't earn that click. The email just happened to contain products I already wanted. Would I forward it? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- **Free shipping hero is above the fold and unambiguous** — clear font, clean layout, locked in before I scroll a pixel.
- **Product grid with visible prices** — $5 to $13 with names attached lets me do points math instantly; this is e.l.f.'s strongest habit.
- **The So Blurreal Lip & Cheek Whip still lands** — even as a secondary product, the lip close-up is visually strong and it's already on my radar from yesterday's launch.
- **Model in the bronzer section reads like me** — the curly-haired woman is young, feminine, relatable; demographic match is solid.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, no broken images, no overlapping copy.

## What's weak

- **"Last chance" is a claim they cannot back up** — same free shipping ran June 23; calling today the deadline without a specific time or end date reads as manufactured urgency, exactly like the Ulta "ends tonight" cycle I've been tracking in this category.
- **Yesterday's hero got buried** — the Lip & Cheek Whip was the singular focus of a 9/10 send 24 hours ago; dropping it to slot 2 in a shipping roundup cheapens both emails and makes the launch moment feel like a price setup.
- **Five products, five identical "Shop Now" CTAs** — Sally Beauty varies its action copy within a single send; e.l.f. copy-pastes and it makes everything feel like a template, not a recommendation.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment** — I'm doing points math on every beauty email I open; a single line about rewards or bonus points would have made this feel personal instead of broadcast.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put an actual deadline in the hero** — "Free shipping ends tonight at midnight" or don't use "last chance" at all. Charlotte Tilbury runs countdown language and backs it up; e.l.f. needs to too or stop using the phrase.
- 2. **Give the Lip & Cheek Whip one more day of hero status** — it earned a 9/10 launch send; let it breathe for 48 hours before folding it into a roundup. The demotion makes the June 25 email look like a teaser for a sale, not a genuine launch.
- 3. **Vary the CTA copy per product** — "Snag the $5 Butter," "Shop the Whip ($8)," "Try the Liner" — costs nothing to write and makes the email feel like someone picked these for me.
- 4. **Add a one-liner loyalty hook** — "Earn 2× points on every item, through midnight" is ten words and would make a Rouge/Diamond-tier shopper like me lean forward.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight — Haze Liner + 4 new picks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$5 Lip Butter. $8 Lip Whip. Free shipping till midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cart is waiting — shipping disappears after today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack the new launches before the free shipping window closes`
