# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "you have 0 points" is STILL sitting right there under the header — that makes it, what, five sends running now that I've logged this from Sephora this week between the two K18s, "50% off select faves" (twice), "DID YOU HEAR THE BIG NEWS?," and now this one. I'm Rouge. I've spent enough to be Rouge. Sephora, please fix your merge field, I'm begging.
- Past that glitch, though, the actual concept here is smart — a points-redemption showcase timed to a "grab your rewards before they're gone" urgency hook is exactly the kind of content a Rouge member wants, way more than another blanket 20%-off code. The reward tiers are laid out cleanly, the "X available now" scarcity counters are a nice touch, and the Dr. Dennis Gross LED mask at 15,000 points with only 3 left creates real FOMO for someone like me who's actually sitting on a point balance.
- But then the tier bar at the bottom of the hero has the red arrow pointing at INSIDER, not ROUGE — so even while the copy is trying to talk to a big spender, the visual is telling me I'm base-tier. That's a bigger miss than the points glitch, honestly, because it undercuts the entire premise of the email. I'd still click — the reward grid is genuinely compelling to me — but I'm not forwarding this to a friend until Sephora sorts out who I actually am to them.

## What's working

- The "Grab September rewards while you can!" hero copy nails urgency for a points-hoarder like me.
- The reward grid itself is well curated — Makeup by Mario, Milk, Haus Labs, Crown Affair, Dr. Dennis Gross — that's a legit mix of cult and prestige.
- "X available now" scarcity counters (3 available, 7 available) make we want to act before the good stuff is gone.
- Product shots are clean and styled together on that vanity tray, which reads more editorial than a plain product-on-white grid.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" under the header, again — five sends in a row now that I've logged this week from Sephora, and it directly contradicts the entire "you've earned points" premise of this email.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar highlights INSIDER, not ROUGE, even though I'm a Rouge member — glaring mismatch on an email that's supposed to be about MY rewards.
- No skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere in the reward picks, even though several of these (Makeup by Mario eyes kit, Haus Labs lip liner) are shade-dependent products.
- The subject line "Did you know that points = rewards?" is condescending for a Rouge member who does the points math obsessively — I know what points are for.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the merge fields — both the "0 points" banner and the tier-bar highlight. This is the fifth Sephora send I've flagged with a broken points display; it's actively undermining a loyalty email.
- 2. Swap the generic subject for something that leads with the scarcity/urgency that's actually in the hero — that's the strongest hook in the whole email and it's buried.
- 3. Add my actual point balance and tier into the hero copy dynamically ("You have 3,200 points, Rouge member — here's what's about to sell out") instead of generic "you shopped and earned points" copy.
- 4. Reorder the grid so the lowest-point-cost, highest-scarcity items (Dr. Dennis Gross, 3 left) sit near the top instead of the bottom — I almost didn't scroll that far.
