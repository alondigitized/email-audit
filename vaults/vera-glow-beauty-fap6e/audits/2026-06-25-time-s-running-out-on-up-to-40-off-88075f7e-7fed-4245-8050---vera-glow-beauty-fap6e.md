# Time's running out on up to 40% off ⏰
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- The count I've been keeping puts this at twenty-three, maybe twenty-four. Two days ago Sally sent "Shop deals up to 40% off" — email #22 — and today the subject swapped "Shop deals" for "Time's running out on" and added a clock emoji. Same headline discount, same product categories, same hero structure. This is a resend with urgency paint, and I clocked it the moment the subject hit my inbox.
- Here's where it gets complicated: the email itself is genuinely well-assembled. The hero reads immediately — "Up to 40% Off Online & In Store" lands in the first scroll, the "free 2-hour delivery" callout is a real differentiator I don't see Sally push, and there's a $59.99 infrared styler in the Online Only section that could have made me actually pause and math out whether it's cheaper than Ulta's 21 Days pricing. The bones are solid.
- But I'm not clicking. I'm barely reading. The creative did not fail this email — the cadence did. I'm archiving it on autopilot and the $59.99 styler is dying in the process.

## What's working

- **Free 2-hour delivery** is specific and actually useful — it separates Sally on speed in a way neither Sephora nor Ulta routinely beats to my inbox
- **$59.99 infrared styler** in the Online Only section gives me a real price anchor to comparison-shop; named price + professional product category is the right move
- **Hero reads immediately** — the offer is the first thing my eye lands on, no hunting required, no lifestyle fluff in the way
- **BOGO offer structure** is clearly labeled and requires zero mental math, which is all I ask of a sale grid
- **40% off styling tools is above the fold** — right category for me, and I don't have to scroll past editorial copy to find it

## What's weak

- **This is the June 23 email with an urgency wrapper.** "Shop deals up to 40% off" → "Time's running out on up to 40% off" is not a new send; it's a resend and I recognized it in three seconds
- **"Time's running out" with zero deadline date.** Sephora's "Last chance" sends always anchor to a specific date — this one gestures at urgency with a ⏰ and expects me to panic-buy on vibes
- **The hero product has no name.** That tall blue bottle front-and-center could be anything — a conditioner, a toner, a mask. Charlotte Tilbury would never put an anonymous product in a hero position; if it's worth the real estate, name it
- **The product grid tiles are fragment-sized.** "40% off select ion—" and "Buy one, get one 50% too—" are cut off mid-copy on mobile; the tiles read as placeholders, not offers
- **Zero persona signal.** Nothing in this email tells me Sally knows I'm 29, that I do loyalty math, or that I've opened the previous twenty-two emails. Compare that to Sephora's Insider event sends — even mass sends at least acknowledge my tier

## Recommendations

- 1. **Name the deadline in the hero and the subject.** "Time's running out" without a date is noise. "Ends Sunday midnight" is a reason to act. One date, three placements: subject, hero banner, CTA button.
- 2. **Hero the $59.99 infrared styler, not a generic "up to 40% off."** Specific product + specific price converts better than a range discount on a nameless bottle. CT and e.l.f. both know naming the product in the subject is what earns the tap.
- 3. **Stop the resend pattern.** If this is the final-hours push on the June 23 sale, write it that way — "Final 48 hours: 40% off styling tools ends Sunday." Don't send the same email twice in two days and call it urgency.
- 4. **Move free 2-hour delivery into the hero hierarchy.** Right now it's subtext beneath the headline. Speed-to-door as a real differentiator deserves its own callout line at hero level — that's a win over Ulta standard shipping and worth a line.
- 5. **Tier-personalize the loyalty section.** "Don't miss out on Big Deals rewards & savings" is generic mass copy. If I'm a specific tier, show me what the sale means for my points multiplier or bonus rewards. Sephora does tier previews even on mass sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `$59.99 infrared styler — sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 2-hour delivery + 40% off tools through midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Styling tools, fragrances, BOGO — all counting down to Sunday`
