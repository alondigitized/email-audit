# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the same fake-cart beat again. "Don't leave these styles behind" and "We saved these styles for you" imply I abandoned something, and I never added a thing. It's the fifth one in about a week: "Did you forget something?" and "Pick up where you left off!" came earlier, plus an earlier send under the same "Don't leave these styles behind" subject (10/17). Whatever personalization this is meant to signal reads as a mass blast of six random women's sneakers.
- The real news is buried. The 20% off plus a $5 reward on $75+ sits under six product tiles. Ulta would lead with that, as in "Ends tonight: 20% off". I do the points math on everything, and this one is thin. $5 back on $75 is about 6.7%, only on online purchases and only once. It excludes sale items and prior purchases, and I have to be logged in to Skechers Plus. The fine print is longer than the hero copy.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'm a beauty shopper cross-shopping Sephora and Ulta, and this is footwear. I'd skim it, see the member 20%, and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "SHOP NOW" button is big, blue, and impossible to miss, and the headline is readable at a glance.
- The 20% OFF band is high-contrast, with a clear "Valid thru 10/26" deadline and the $5 reward math stated.
- The six product tiles carry names and materials like "Leather", so I can see what's behind each tap. That's better than a vague hero.
- The layout is clean and renders with no broken images. The text-to-shop menu rows (Women, Men, Kids…) are big, easy tap targets.

## What's weak

- The offer is below six products, so I have to scroll to the only reason to click. Sephora's "Open for new items" at least put the product first, but it had no offer. This has an offer and hides it.
- "We saved these styles for you" is a fake-cart framing. I never saved anything, and after four of these in a week it feels manipulative.
- There are no prices, no sizes, no reviews, and no "new" or "low stock" tags on the products. Nothing tells me why these six.
- The legal block at the bottom is longer than the email's actual message. "Excludes sale items" next to a "Shop the Sale" button is contradictory.
- A faint, blurry blue strip sits right under the logo. It looks like a placeholder, though it doesn't break anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% OFF + $5 reward band above the product grid, and make it the hero. Right now the headline is a feeling and the offer is an afterthought.
- 2. Drop the fake-cart framing unless it's real browsing data. If it is, show the one product I actually viewed, big, with price and size.
- 3. Put prices and a reason on each tile, like "Slip-ins, $X" or "Just in". Glossier's "Want a free gift?" and e.l.f.'s "$12" hero both put the number in my face immediately.
- 4. Make the CTA specific, like "Take 20% off sneakers" instead of "SHOP NOW".
- 5. Cut the cadence. If I got "Did you forget something?" yesterday, today's send shouldn't be the same beat. Drunk Elephant did the same four-in-four-days thing and it burned goodwill.
