# NEW! Black Cherry Line + Balm Duo 🎀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the black cherry lip duo is cute, but the email's real job was to tell me what it costs, and it doesn't. The hero shows a burgundy Lip Line and a Balm Dotcom with a cherry sitting in a coupe of cream. It's pretty, and a burgundy lip for fall is exactly my lane. But I scrolled the whole thing and found no price, no bundle savings, no Glossier Rewards mention and no deadline. I can't do points math on a "gift for always looking put together."
- It also feels like a rerun. The Wardrobe Kit was the whole point of the 10/8 "NEW! Glossier You Mini Wardrobe Kit 🎀" send. It's back here as a "little extra," along with the You Duo, and the 10/6 gift guide and 10/10 "Plot twist: there's more to gift!" both led with the same pink-tissue gifting imagery. The lips are the only new thing, and they get two blocks while the repeats get a whole row.
- Would I click? Maybe the hero out of curiosity about the shade, but I'd probably check Sephora first to see if it's there for Rouge-tier points. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. The lip liner and balm sit next to a "NEW!" tag, so the launch reads in about two seconds.
- The "A touch of burgundy" lip close-up shows me the actual shade on skin, which is the only swatch I get. It beats a flat product shot.
- The shade names and descriptors under the Balm Dotcoms ("Toasty Nuts: subtle copper shimmer," "Just Baked: mauve tint") give me a real product detail to decide on.
- The layout is clean and scannable. Each product has its own full-width Shop now button, so the tap targets are easy on a phone.
- The brand voice is consistent ("zero overthinking!", the smiley in the footer). It feels like Glossier.

## What's weak

- There is no price anywhere. No "$", no "%", no bundle math and no Rewards mention. This is the same gap I flagged on 10/6, 10/8 and 10/10.
- The "A touch of burgundy" copy is cut off at the right edge. It reads "paired our beloved Balm Dotcom with o…" and the sentence is clipped. That looks sloppy on the exact block that explains the duo.
- Almost every button says "Shop now," so none of them tells me where I'm going. "Shop the look" and "Shop holiday" are slightly better, but there's no verb plus a specific noun.
- The Wardrobe Kit and You Duo are repeats from earlier sends, and they're labeled only "Limited edition." I don't know the scarcity or the date.
- The "But wait! The gifting doesn't stop there" block is a pink paper photo with a generic "Shop holiday" button. It's filler after an already long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a price and the math in the hero.** For example, "Black Cherry Lip Line + Balm Duo, $XX, saves $X vs. buying separately." That's the one change that would lift this the most. Ulta puts "20% off" in the subject, and ILIA put "Buy 3, save 20%" in theirs. Sephora's "Makeup Sets: Here & Nowhere Else" at least tells me why to buy now.
- 2. **Add a Rewards line.** Something like "Earn 2x points on the duo this week" would let me do the math.
- 3. **Fix the clipped copy** in the burgundy block so the sentence ends properly.
- 4. **Cut the repeats.** Drop the Wardrobe Kit and You Duo row, or put it in a separate send, and add a shade-range or "available on" detail for the Lip Line.
- 5. **Write specific CTAs.** Use "Shop the Black Cherry duo," "Get Toasty Nuts" and so on, instead of repeating "Shop now."
- 6. **Add a deadline.** If the holiday set is limited, say "Limited edition, ships by [date]."
