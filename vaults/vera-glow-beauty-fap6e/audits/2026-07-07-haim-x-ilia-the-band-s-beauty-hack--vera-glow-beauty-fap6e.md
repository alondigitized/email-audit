# HAIM x ILIA—the band’s beauty hack
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- OK so ILIA came in with HAIM and I will be honest — I was not expecting this pivot. We literally just closed out their Buy 3, Save 20% run on July 5 (the dice, the rock-paper-scissors, the "Ends Tonight" finale I tracked all week), and now two days later they're dropping a full celebrity editorial. The creative team clearly had this queued.
- Here's where I land: HAIM is a legit name drop. These are not micro-influencers, not TikTok faces, not the rotating "brand partner" faces I see in every Ulta Beauty email. Grammy-nominated musicians who've been using Skin Tint long enough to have a genuine routine opinion? That lands differently. Este talking about covering up without breaking out, Alana confessing she never understood sunscreen until Skin Tint made it effortless — those feel like real testimonials, not ad copy a PR agency wrote for them.
- But I am fully aware I could have bought this at 20% off 48 hours ago. There is no offer here. No loyalty callout, no points nudge, no reason to click now versus bookmark for the next Sephora Beauty Insider event. Would I click? Probably — to screenshot for my next event haul list. Would I purchase today? No. Would I forward to a friend who's been sleeping on clean SPF? Yes, actually — especially the Alana quote. This is the best ILIA has executed in a non-sale email all summer.

## What's working

- **HAIM is a credible, non-obvious celebrity choice** — three Grammy-nominated sisters who aren't known as "beauty girls" makes the endorsement feel earned rather than bought; this reads nothing like the Ulta revolving influencer wall
- **Three-panel testimonial structure is skimmable and specific** — each sister gets a use-case headline ("safe for sensitive skin," "makes spf seamless," "keeps things simple") so I can immediately identify whose story is mine
- **Este's sensitive skin angle is a genuine differentiator** — she's talking about covering up without a breakout, which is the exact Skin Tint proof point that separates it from a tinted moisturizer
- **Visual hierarchy flows cleanly from top to CTA** — HAIM x ILIA header → benefit hero copy → testimonials → single CTA, nothing competing or bleeding
- **Summer SPF timing is sharp** — Alana's confession about finally "getting" sunscreen because Skin Tint did it for her is peak July energy and the right seasonal hook

## What's weak

- **Zero offer, zero loyalty callout, the morning after their sale ended** — I tracked five days of Buy 3 Save 20% emails and now I'm being asked to go full price with zero points incentive; that's a real friction point for anyone doing the beauty math
- **"GRAB YOUR SKIN TINT →" breaks the editorial tone** — this email builds a sophisticated, quiet mood over three testimonials and then hits a generic DTC verb at the close; Glossier would never let "GRAB" near a collab this premium
- **No shade range moment anywhere** — Skin Tint runs 18+ shades and this collab is the perfect place to show that depth; as it reads now it looks like a single-SKU product, which undersells the whole positioning
- **"GRAMMY-nominated trio" is buried in body copy** — that is the strongest trust signal in the email and it's sitting in paragraph two of the hero; it should be in the header or subhead where it earns the open before the scroll

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a loyalty or "earn points" nudge somewhere in the footer** — even a single line like "Earn Sephora Beauty Insider points with every purchase" gives me a reason to buy now instead of waiting; right now there is no hook for the points-math crowd, and that's most of ILIA's customer base
- 2. **Swap "GRAB YOUR SKIN TINT →" for "Find Your Shade →"** — that one word shift moves the CTA from generic to the actual decision I need to make; shade selection is the real barrier to purchase for a product like this
- 3. **Surface the shade range** — a row of five or six bottle swatches below the testimonials or above the CTA does the work without adding copy; Fenty taught us all that the shade range IS the product story
- 4. **Move "GRAMMY-nominated trio" to the hero subhead** — it should live directly under "hits every note," not buried in body copy two sections down; that credential is what separates this from a random collab announcement
- 5. **Frame this as a "set-bag essential" or "on-tour pick"** — even without a discount, story-pegging it to HAIM's summer tour or their actual routine adds a time hook that makes the email feel current rather than evergreen

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**External resource loading at open time:**
- `@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css)` — loads on open; expected for Klaviyo but counts as a network request before click.
- `@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap)` — Google Fonts import fires on render in clients that allow external CSS. Gmail strips `@import`, so Montserrat silently falls back to Helvetica there. No functional breakage, but the render differs across clients.

**Links not assessable** — HTML is truncated before any `<a href>` elements. CTA URLs and tracking parameters cannot be verified.

| Issue | Evidence |
