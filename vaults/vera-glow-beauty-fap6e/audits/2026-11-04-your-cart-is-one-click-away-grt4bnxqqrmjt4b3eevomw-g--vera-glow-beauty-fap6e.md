# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth fake-cart email in under two weeks, and I still haven't put anything in a Skechers cart. The 10/31 "Your Items Are Waiting!", 11/1 "Pick up where you left off!" and the last two "Your cart expires soon!" sends all made the same claim. The hero is a skull-painted UNO sneaker with a "Day of the Dead" look. It's a seasonal novelty shoe, and nothing about me says I'd have left it behind. "Let's make it official" is a weirdly romantic headline for a nag.
- The one thing that's new is the 25% off banner, 2 days only, for Skechers Plus members. That's the first real reason to care in this whole run, but it sits about two-thirds of the way down, under a six-shoe grid. Sephora puts the Insider event math right in the subject line. Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder owns the top of the email. Here I have to scroll past a "cart" I never made to find the deal.
- I'm not clicking. I'd mark it read. If I were a Plus member and actually shopping for sneakers, the 25% would make me open the site, but I'd get there from the banner, not the hero. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The 25% off for Plus members is a clear number with a clear deadline (valid thru 11/4), and it's the only real offer.
- The navy and white offer block stands out from the rest of the email, so it's easy to spot once I scroll to it.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is large, high-contrast and unambiguous.
- Skechers is a recognizable sender with consistent branding.
- The Afterpay and Klarna icons, the curbside pickup and the app badges are useful practical extras.

## What's weak

- I have no cart, so "Your cart is ready when you are" and "Complete your order" are false. After two weeks of these, it reads as spam, not a reminder.
- The member offer is buried below the product grid, not in the hero or subject, so the subject and the hero both undersell the best thing in the email.
- The hero shoe is a one-off skull graphic with no price, so I can't tell what I supposedly picked.
- The six-shoe grid has no prices, no ratings and no sale tags. Nothing is tied to the 25% off.
- The footer fine print is tiny and dense. It also says "select regular-priced items", which makes me doubt how far the 25% goes.
- The same "cart" nudge has hit me every day or two for two weeks, so the cadence feels wrong.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer. Put "25% off for Plus members, ends tonight" in the subject and the hero. Cart language only goes to people who actually have a cart.
- 2. Show prices, or the price after 25% off, under each shoe, and add a "Plus price" tag so I can do the math.
- 3. Add a countdown or an "ends tonight" line near the top, since the offer expires 11/4.
- 4. Suppress the abandoned-cart flow for people with no browse or cart activity, and cap the cadence at one reminder.
- 5. Replace the six-item "we think you'll love" grid with items I actually viewed, or the best-sellers for my category.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 25% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days only: 25% off sneakers for Plus`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 25% on select styles online. Ends 11/4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Plus discount applies automatically at checkout. Ends tonight.`
