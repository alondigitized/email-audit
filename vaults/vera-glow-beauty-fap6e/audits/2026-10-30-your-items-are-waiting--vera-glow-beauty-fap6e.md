# Your Items Are Waiting!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth fake-cart email from Skechers in about two weeks, and I still haven't put anything in a cart. "We noticed you noticing us… your cart is still waiting" is the same line as the 10/23 "Your Items Are Waiting!" send. It's the same beat as 10/26 "Your cart expires soon!" and 10/29 "Did you forget something?". The olive GO WALK Workout Walker is a men's-looking dad sneaker. I have never browsed one.
- The only thing that makes me do math is the navy band: "20% OFF for Skechers Plus members, valid thru 11/2." It sits halfway down, under a black "Shop all best sellers" bar. Sephora puts the Rouge 20% right at the top. Skechers buries the one real offer under a product I didn't pick.
- I wouldn't click. I'd skim for the discount and then archive it. I wouldn't forward it, because there's nothing here for a friend. I'm not a Plus member, and I don't know what the membership costs or how it earns, so I can't judge the 20%.

## What's working

- The 20% off band for Plus members is a clear, specific number with a deadline (11/2). It's the only thing I'd act on.
- The "Complete your order" button is big, navy and unambiguous.
- The sender name is instantly recognizable, and the layout is clean and readable on a phone.
- The footer fine print is honest: online only, must be logged in, exclusions listed.
- The Afterpay/Klarna row and the Text SKECHERS to 83828 prompt are useful, practical touches.

## What's weak

- The cart premise is fake. I never added anything, and this is send number ten, so it reads as spam rather than service.
- The hero shoe is a men's walker, and the recommended grid is mixed, with men's boots and John Deere styles. Nothing signals that it knows me.
- The 20% offer is buried below the fold, under the "Shop all best sellers" bar. I have to scroll past a shoe I don't want to find the one reason to buy.
- The product names are long and cluttered ("Slip-ins: Contour Foam - Cozy Fit Dazzling Stride Sneaker | Leather"). There are no prices, no reviews and no sale markers, so I can't compare.
- The footer is a wall of links and badges (app, curbside, find a store, social). The Plus terms are tiny and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cap abandoned-cart sends at one or two, and only for people who actually have a cart. If it's a browse email, call it "Still looking?" and don't claim a cart.
- 2. Move the 20% Plus offer to the hero, above the shoe, and show the sale price on the product.
- 3. Use the viewed category, or women's styles, in the hero instead of a men's walker.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the six-product grid, and shorten the names.
- 5. Say how Plus works: the cost, the tier and the benefits.
