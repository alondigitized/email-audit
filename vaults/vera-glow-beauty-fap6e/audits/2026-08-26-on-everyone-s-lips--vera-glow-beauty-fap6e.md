## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually stopped scrolling on this one — that dual close-up lip shot with two different skin tones is genuinely one of the better hero images ILIA's run this month, and "line for line" as a GWP mechanic (free mini Lip Sketch with a Thru Line Waterproof Eyeliner purchase) is a real, specific offer, not vague "shop now" filler. It's basically a full lip-category catalog dressed up as one email — crayon, balm, lipstick, gloss, overnight mask — which is smart because it's a legit "everything lip" moment, not another single-SKU push.
- That said, my brain immediately does the points math and comes up empty: no VIP tier callout in the hero, no mention of whether this GWP or any SKU here earns bonus points toward my ILIA rewards, nothing about a beauty-event tie-in. - Verdict: I'd click through on the Balmy Tint (that "trending on TikTok" badge is doing real work on me), but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — it's a solid catalog email, not a can't-miss moment.

## What's working

- The dual-tone lip macro hero is genuinely striking and gives real skin-tone/shade-range signal without a word of copy.
- "Trending on TikTok" badge on the Balmy Tint Lip Balm is a real social-proof cue that pulls my eye and my click.
- The GWP mechanic ("line for line") is concrete — free mini with a specific SKU purchase — not a vague "treat yourself."
- Clean grid layout with consistent product shots, price-free copy that's easy to scan on a scroll.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty/points language anywhere — no ILIA Beauty Insider or VIP tier math, which is the first thing I look for as a points person.
- No urgency or deadline on the GWP offer beyond "*See site for details" — feels soft compared to Sally's or Ulta's ticking-clock sends.
- Six products stacked is a lot of scroll before you hit the actual GWP hook near the bottom — buries the best offer.
- No pricing visible anywhere, which as a Rouge/Diamond cross-shopper I actually want to comparison-shop against Sephora/Ulta cart price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "line for line" free mini GWP offer up near the hero — it's the strongest, most specific offer in the email and it's currently buried after six product tiles.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("ends Sunday") to the GWP claim — right now it reads open-ended and low-urgency.
- 3. Layer in any VIP/rewards angle — even "double points on Lip Wrap this week" would close the loyalty gap that Sephora/Ulta emails at least attempt.
- 4. Add price points to the product grid so cross-shoppers like me can do the math without leaving the email.
