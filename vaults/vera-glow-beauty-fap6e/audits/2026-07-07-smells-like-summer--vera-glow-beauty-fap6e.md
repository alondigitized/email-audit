# 🌴 Smells like summer
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-five. I've lost count of the exact number but the July 4 send was twenty-four and this lands three days later, so here we are at twenty-five Lush emails. And I'm going to say something that I did not expect to say: the creative is genuinely pretty. The citrus-yellow hero, the "Sweet Summer Things" headline, the teal mint chocolate block below it — this is actually a visually coherent editorial. It's the kind of thing you'd screen-cap and send to your group chat under "summer mood."
- But here's the problem: it's a product showcase with zero offer. No discount. No event. No "you're Rouge, here's your early access." Just... vibes. And vibes don't make me click Shop Now when I've already audited twenty-four other Lush emails and can feel the frequency fatigue setting in. Compare this to what ILIA did with their Buy 3, Save 20% run — five days of escalating creative with actual math you could act on. Lush keeps sending me beautiful emails with nowhere to land.
- I would skim this, save the Sanrio collab note for when I'm actually near a Lush store, and mark it read. Would not click. Would not forward — though the "Zest Is Best" section is dangerously close to shareable energy.

## What's working

- **Color-blocked editorial structure is strong.** Yellow → teal → yellow → Sanrio pastel pink is a coherent seasonal palette that feels intentional, not templated.
- **"Cooling Mint Chocolate" section earns its real estate.** That's a distinctive product name and the teal backdrop makes it pop — I actually want to know what that is.
- **"Zest Is Best" headline is fun.** Happy Hippo, Lime Bounty — this is playful, in-brand naming that feels distinctly Lush and not Sephora-Collection generic.
- **Sanrio collab callback works.** I audited the full Lush × Sanrio send on June 30 and it was a 9. Keeping that thread alive at the bottom of a summer editorial is smart retention — it's something I'd actually look for.
- **No render bugs.** Clean layout, readable text blocks, no broken images.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer.** This is a pure editorial with no discount, no event, no loyalty hook. Sephora's "Stacking Weekend" sends were stacking actual percentage points and points multipliers — this is just product photography with a Shop Now button.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is doing too much lifting.** Multiple sections all funneling to the same generic button feels lazy. Drunk Elephant at least puts a product name in the link context — here every CTA is interchangeable.
- **Frequency is catching up.** Three sends in three days (July 2, July 4, July 7) with no escalating offer logic. Sally Beauty hit twenty-nine sends and managed to make me care because they were building toward something. This just feels like Lush filling the calendar.
- **No loyalty signal whatsoever.** I'm a returning subscriber who has opened twenty-five of their emails — there's nothing here that acknowledges that. Even a "fan favorite" tag or a "back by request" note would land differently.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one anchoring offer, even a soft one.** A GWP threshold, a free sample with orders over $50, a loyalty points bonus — anything that gives "Shop Now" a reason to exist beyond "we made pretty pictures." The editorial can stay; just give it stakes.
- 2. **Split the CTAs by product family.** "Shop Mint" and "Shop Citrus" buttons instead of two identical "Shop Now" labels would let me self-select and would give Lush actual click-through data worth having.
- 3. **Call out the Sanrio collab more aggressively.** It's buried at the bottom after three product sections. That collab scored a 9 for me — it's your biggest recent hook, and you're treating it like a footnote. Put it second, after the hero.
- 4. **Give the subject line a product tether.** "Smells like summer" is vibe copy; it tells me nothing about what's inside and doesn't differentiate from fifty other summer beauty sends this week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Citrus, mint, and the Sanrio collab you've been waiting on.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Zest Is Best — shop summer's freshest drops before they're gone.`
