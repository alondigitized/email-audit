# 💘JUST YOUR TYPE 💘
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- "JUST YOUR TYPE" with the double hearts — okay, I opened. After the double "New products have arrived" on the 13th (I genuinely refreshed my inbox because I thought it was a duplicate) and the two back-to-back "Obsessed? Same." sends on the 12th, this subject line actually has personality again. But the second my eyes land on the body, the personalization promise disappears completely. "This is for YOU" over a general product grid is the email equivalent of a barista calling you by the wrong name and handing you someone else's order anyway.
- The minis deal and the Jo Malone hero are doing real work here — those are the things that have me hovering over a CTA. But by the time I've scrolled through fragrance → product grid → 2X points → leave-in conditioners → One/Size by Patrick Starr, I've lost the thread. This email doesn't know what it wants me to buy, so I have to decide for myself.
- I'd click — but I'd text the minis deal to my friend separately because the email itself doesn't make the case cleanly enough to forward.

## What's working

- **The minis deal is right at the top and I can do the math instantly.** "Buy 2 Minis, Get Both 50% Off" is the kind of concrete stacking opportunity I show up for — no scrolling required to find the hook.
- **Jo Malone as the hero is a credible prestige move.** Sea Salt & Bergamot in July is actually seasonally correct, unlike some of the stale promo energy Sephora has sent this month. The size ($175, 3.4 oz) is visible — that's a real product detail.
- **The 2X points callout would absolutely pull me into a cart session.** As a Rouge member running annual point math, a multiplier is found money. I just wish it wasn't buried mid-scroll.
- **"JUST YOUR TYPE" is the best Sephora subject in three days.** It breaks the streak of duplicate or generic subject lines and actually made me curious. That's earned — relative to the recent cadence, this felt like a reset.
- **Beauty Insider badge at the bottom.** It's a small thing but it signals member content, not a cold blast.

## What's weak

- **The personalization is completely hollow.** "JUST YOUR TYPE" + "This is for YOU" + a general multi-category grid is the same playbook Glossier ran when they sent "Meet I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom" and then immediately sent "NEW! I ❤️ NY Balm Dotcom" the next day — the tease promises something the body doesn't deliver.
- **Leave-in conditioners mid-scroll in a fragrance-forward email is a category whiplash.** I'm emotionally in a Jo Malone headspace and suddenly there's haircare. That's Ulta-energy — not Sephora.
- **Six different "SHOP NOW" CTAs dilute intent.** Each section has its own button and I have no idea what Sephora actually needs me to do. ILIA's "brown mascara, made mini" email had one clear lane and one click destination; this email has at least five.
- **No deadline on anything.** The minis offer, the 2X points — no expiry visible. Without urgency I bookmark it and the cart sits abandoned for four days.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to one hero.** Jo Malone summer fragrance story or minis event — not both simultaneously plus haircare plus One/Size. A tight single-concept email like ILIA's mini mascara launch converts harder than a departmental catalog.
- 2. **Put a deadline on the minis deal.** Even "ends Sunday" above the fold is enough to trigger the act-now reflex instead of the save-for-later scroll.
- 3. **Deliver on the personalization promise.** If the subject says "JUST YOUR TYPE," the body should at minimum bucket by finish or vibe — floral, clean, bold — so it feels like a quiz result rather than a homepage visit.
- 4. **Elevate the 2X points to secondary CTA position.** It's the best loyalty hook in the email and it's sitting below the fold next to conditioner bottles.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your summer signature scent (+ 50% off minis)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sea Salt & Bergamot is calling your name ☀️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `2X points on fragrances this week — stack it with the mini deal`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Jo Malone dropped something for summer. Ends Sunday.`

```html

```
This fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Users with low vision cannot zoom the email on mobile. `maximum-scale` should be removed or raised to `5`.

**Broad `text-size-adjust: none` suppression**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to virtually every element. This compounds the zoom issue above and can prevent OS-level accessibility font scaling from working in some clients.

**No `lang` attribute visible on `<html>` tag** — the element has `xmlns` and VML namespace attributes but no `lang="en"`. Screen readers use this to select the correct pronunciation engine. If omitted in the full source, add `lang="en"`.


Correct value is `noindex` (no space). Inconsequential for email clients (they ignore robots meta), but indicates a templating quality gap.
