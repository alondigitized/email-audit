# This is perfect for you!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so first thing — this isn't even beauty, it's Skechers again, and the subject "This is perfect for you!" made me actually laugh a little because no, a grey men's slip-in is not "perfect for me." I'm a 29-year-old Sephora Rouge/Ulta Diamond shopper who does points math in her head for fun, and Skechers has sent me "Your cart expires soon!" (7/25), "Your Items Are Waiting!" (7/22 — a repeat of 7/16), and "Did you forget something?" three separate times this month. Now it's "Still thinking about it?" — a fourth flavor of the exact same cart-abandonment nudge, and I genuinely can't tell these apart anymore in my head.
- The one thing that's actually new and actually bad: every single recommended product below the hero shoe is labeled "Men's." Men's Aero Burst, Men's Track, Men's GO WALK, Men's Hotshot, Men's GO Walk 8 — six for six. If a brand is going to retarget me off a browsed item, at least match the gender data you clearly have from my browsing session. Ulta's recent "Lucky you! ✨ 20% off" send at least felt like it knew who was opening it — barcode-and-book-stack hero, clean single offer, no gender whiplash. This one just feels like Skechers fired off a generic template and didn't check who was on the other end.
- Verdict: not clicking, not forwarding — this is getting archived without a second look, same as the last three from this brand.

## What's working

- The "2 FOR $100" banner is genuinely legible — big number, clear scope (footwear/apparel/accessories), dated through 8/3, no digging required.
- "TAKE ANOTHER LOOK" is a real CTA button, not a vague hyperlink — I know exactly what tapping it does.
- Layout is clean and renders fine — no overlapping text, no broken images, easy to scroll on a phone.
- Footer utility (Afterpay/Klarna, curbside, text club) is at least organized instead of dumped in one line.

## What's weak

- Every recommended product is a men's shoe — Men's Aero Burst, Men's Track, Men's GO WALK, Men's Hotshot, Men's GO Walk 8. That's a hard demographic miss, not a style preference thing.
- The subject line "This is perfect for you!" tells me nothing — no product, no offer, no urgency. Compare that to Ulta's "Lucky you! This 20% off just landed in your lap" — at least that names the mechanic.
- This is the fourth near-identical cart-abandonment subject from Skechers this month (cart expires, items waiting x2, forgot something x3, now this). Even Glossier, who I've called out for thirteen-plus sends since July 7th with zero offer, at least varies the creative. Skechers is just cycling the same three nudge templates.
- No preview text populated at all — just dead space in the inbox where a real hook should be.
- No loyalty tier or points messaging anywhere, which is exactly the thing I'm scanning for as a Rouge/Diamond shopper — Skechers never even attempts this the way e.l.f.'s "National Lipstick Day gift" at least ties to an occasion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the gender match on recommendations — if I browsed a women's or unisex silhouette, don't backfill the grid with six men's sneakers. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Retire this cart-nudge subject line rotation — I've now seen four variants in under two weeks. Pick one and stop, or actually escalate the offer each time instead of repeating the beat.
- 3. Name the actual browsed product in the subject line instead of "perfect for you" — specificity is what got Ulta's 20% off email opened.
- 4. Add real preview text instead of leaving it blank — free real estate, currently wasted.
- 5. If Skechers has any rewards/loyalty program, surface it — I'm tracking points across two other brands and this email gives me zero reason to think Skechers even has a program.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still your size? The Arch Fit Arcade is holding.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2-for-$100 ends 8/3 — grab the pair you left behind`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Arch Fit Arcade slip-in, plus women's picks we think you'll actually like.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mix and match footwear, apparel, and accessories — 2 for $100 thru Sunday.`
