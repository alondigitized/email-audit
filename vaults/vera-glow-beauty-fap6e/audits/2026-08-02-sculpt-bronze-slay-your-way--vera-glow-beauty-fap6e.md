# Sculpt, bronze & slay your way 🤎

## Executive summary

- Day fourteen. e.l.f. again — and this time it's a legit new-product story, three different bronzer formulas (cream, liquid, powder) matched to three different skin tones, which is honestly the most product-education this brand has done all month. I actually like the "find your finish" framing, it's a real decision tree instead of another lip oil BOGO.
- But my eye keeps snagging on the price: $7 across all three. Sephora's Beauty Insider Celebration send I logged on 8/1 made me feel like a VIP with that gold "STARTS 8/1-8/31" badge; this is just... $7, shop now, $7, shop now, three times in a row. No tier math, no points, nothing that acknowledges I've had this brand in my inbox for two weeks straight. I'd scroll, maybe screenshot the cream bronzer shade match for myself, and move on — not forwarding this, not feeling urgency to click today.

## What's working

- The three-model, three-shade-range lineup (deep, fair, tan) actually reads as inclusive without being a token gesture — each face is shot in the same soft glam lighting so the bronzer looks consistent across skin tones.
- "sculpt, bronze & slay your way" is a fun, on-brand headline that doesn't feel like a rehash of last week's Hair or SKIN sends.
- Clear finish/pigment differentiation per product (buttery satin vs. high-pigment dewy vs. medium powder) — that's the kind of product detail I actually want before I click.
- $7 flat price is visible under every product, no hunting for it.

## What's weak

- Zero loyalty signal — no e.l.f. Beauty Squad points, no "you're a member" callout, nothing. Sephora's Insider Celebration went all-in on tier status same week; this brand just doesn't play that card at all.
- This is my third e.l.f. bronzer/complexion-adjacent send since 7/28 (skin, hair, now bronzer) — cadence fatigue is real, and nothing here acknowledges I've already seen five e.l.f. emails this week.
- "Top Picks for You" is doing zero personalization work — a primer, a face cream, and a setting spray dumped under a bronzer email isn't "for me," it's just merchandising filler.
- No urgency or time-bound anything — compare to the "6 hours only" Lip Oil BOGO from 7/29; this one just sits there at $7 with no reason to act today vs. next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a Beauty Squad points callout under the $7 price ("Earn X points" or a tier-exclusive early access window) — the loyalty gap is the single biggest miss given how hard Sephora/Ulta lean on this in every send I've logged this week.
- 2. Cut the send cadence or bundle this into one "complexion week" email instead of three separate skin/hair/bronzer sends in six days — I'm starting to autopilot past the sender name.
- 3. Replace the generic "Top Picks for You" grid with an actual bronzer-adjacent bundle (setting spray + bronzer + brush) so it reads as a curated routine, not leftover inventory.
- 4. Give the $7 price a comparison anchor ("normally $9" or "member price $7") — flat pricing with no frame of reference doesn't feel like a deal, just a price.
