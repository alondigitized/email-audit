# 💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, App Fest — this is Sephora's big one, the actual calendar event, not another "beauty forecast" filler send, and the countdown-style day-by-day breakdown (Tuesday deluxe samples, Wednesday new Swipe Serum Foundation, Thursday the credit card push, Friday YSL early access, Saturday/Sunday Point of View) is genuinely useful because it tells me exactly which day to actually open my app. That's more planning-friendly than anything Ulta's sent me lately — Ulta's "20% off one item, ends tonight" mechanic (the "SCORE" coupon send) doesn't require me to plan my week, it just nags me before a deadline.
- That said, the loyalty tier tracker at the bottom is doing the same thing it's done in every Sephora email I've opened this week — INSIDER is highlighted, VIB and ROUGE are not, and I am Rouge. I flagged this on 8/15's VIBES/AURAS/ERAS send twice already and it's still broken today. For a program this obsessed with tier gating (25% off is explicitly "fan-tier brands," the 4X points is Westman Atelier only, the 500 bonus points needs a "Challenge"), not knowing my own tier on sight is a real miss.
- I'd click into the app for this one — App Fest is worth tracking — but I wouldn't forward it. It's dense enough that I'd screenshot the day-by-day grid for myself instead.

## What's working

- The day-by-day countdown structure (Tue–Sun) is the clearest calendar-style layout I've gotten from Sephora all month — way more scannable than the spinning VIBES/AURAS/ERAS wheel from 8/15.
- 10% off + a free gift bundle stacked right under "STARTS TODAY" gives me an immediate, unambiguous hook without scrolling.
- Brand-specific days (YSL on Friday, Westman Atelier 4X points on Friday, Pureology/Too Faced/Clinique 25% on Sunday) are real curation signal — feels like an actual event calendar, not a generic "shop now."
- The Sephora Credit Card 25% + 500 bonus points block on Thursday is a genuinely relevant offer for someone doing points math like me.
- "5 More Days of App Fest" recap toward the bottom is a smart mid-email reset for anyone who scrolled past the early days.

## What's weak

- Tier tracker at the bottom still shows INSIDER lit up and VIB/ROUGE dark — same broken sync I called out on 8/15's send, now a third time. If you're going to gate offers by tier, at least show me my correct one.
- The whole email is a wall of near-identical black-and-pink day blocks — by Sunday my eye has glazed over and I'm skimming headers, not offers.
- "Beauty Insider Challenge" for the +500 bonus points is vague — complete "buying bath and body care," no specifics on spend threshold or which products qualify.
- Multiple asterisked "exclusions apply" tags with zero visible detail — I have to tap through to find out if my actual cart qualifies, which is the same buried-fine-print complaint I had with Drunk Elephant's SheerID-gated 20% off on 8/9.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the tier tracker — this is the third send in a row where my Rouge status doesn't register. It undercuts every "member benefit" claim in the email.
- 2. Break the day-by-day grid visually — alternate background colors or add product-category icons per day so Tuesday doesn't blur into Wednesday on a fast scroll.
- 3. Put real numbers on the Beauty Insider Challenge — "spend $75 on bath & body, get 500 points" beats a vague "complete tasks."
- 4. Move the Yves Saint Laurent and Westman Atelier days higher, or tease them in the subject — those are the actual reasons I'd block out my calendar, and right now they're buried mid-scroll.
