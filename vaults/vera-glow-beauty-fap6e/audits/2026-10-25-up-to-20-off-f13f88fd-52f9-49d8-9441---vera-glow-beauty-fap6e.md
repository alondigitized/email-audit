# Up to 20% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Drunk Elephant send since 10/19 for the same Subscribe & Save ladder, and I'm tired of it. After "20% off?" yesterday and "Health Education Week (and 15% off)" on 10/23, "Up to 20% off" tells me nothing new. The one new thing is the countdown timer, which shows about 20 hours left. That's a real deadline, and the fine print confirms the offer ends Oct 27 at 2:59 am EST.
- The layout is clean and the product shot is pretty. The hero headline, "AND YOU'LL NEVER RUN OUT AGAIN," starts mid-thought and doesn't say what the offer is. I have to read the paragraph to learn it's a subscription discount and not a sitewide sale. Sephora's Savings Event banner at least puts the percent right up top, and Ulta's "Up to 50% off" tells me the number before I scroll.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'm Rouge and Diamond, so I do points math on everything, and a 15% first order that drops to 10% forever doesn't beat a Beauty Insider event stack. I'd forward it to nobody. I'd mark it read and wait for the holiday gift sets.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (00 days, 20 hours, 27 minutes) gives me a real deadline, and it matches the Oct 27 end date in the fine print.
- The 15% / 20% / 10% ladder is spelled out in bold, so the math is easy to do once I'm reading.
- The product pedestal shot with the Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra and T.L.C. is on-brand and shows the bestsellers I'd recognize.
- The gift guide block with the Bespoke and Haute Hydration sets is a smart secondary pitch that fits the holiday calendar.
- Free shipping and "adjust or cancel anytime" are stated in the fine print, which builds some trust.

## What's weak

- The headline doesn't say the offer. "Never run out again" is cute, but the discount is buried in the paragraph below it.
- The hero is a large block of grey text, and the single "SHOP NOW" button is thin and low-contrast. It's easy to miss.
- Nothing says "subscription" until the second line of the paragraph, so the subject "Up to 20% off" reads like a sitewide sale. That's a mismatch.
- This is the sixth send of the same offer in about a week. The countdown is the only new element, and it feels like pressure to buy.
- There's no loyalty or member angle, no skin-type personalization, and no hero product. "Shop now" goes to who knows where.
- The fine print is a block of small grey text. It restricts the offer to new subscription orders only and says it can't be combined with other promotions, which makes me doubt the "up to" headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the headline, for example "15% off your first Drunk Elephant subscription," and make the CTA a solid, high-contrast button.
- 2. Cut the frequency. Send one email at the start, one mid-event, and one for the final 24 hours, instead of six near-identical ones.
- 3. Link each product to its own subscription page, with a price and a "subscribe" price shown side by side, so I can do the math.
- 4. Add a skin-type or routine quiz, or a "build your subscription" module, so the email feels personalized.
- 5. Move the key terms (new subscriptions only, no stacking) closer to the offer so the headline doesn't feel misleading.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 15% off your first subscription`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: save 15% on Protini & C-Firma`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Then 20% off your second order, 10% every time after. Free shipping.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Subscribe & Save ends Oct 27 at 2:59 am EST. Stock your shelf.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source and QA data show no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). Confirmed problems are three `http://` social icons, two unlabeled tracking images, and a recipient email address in a tracking-pixel URL. The List-Unsubscribe and authentication checks could not be verified, and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 12 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains, and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Unlabeled tracking images:** two tracking images have no `alt` attribute:
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekendssoon`
  - `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…&c=us_de_broad…`
  - They are probably open pixels. Without `alt=""` some clients and screen readers announce the URL or filename.
- **Two tracking vendors:** the email loads pixels from two separate domains, `mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`. This is not an error, but it adds third-party requests.
- **Email address in a URL:** the everestengagement pixel carries the recipient address in plaintext as `e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`. If these URLs are logged or forwarded, the address leaks through query strings.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Mixed content:** three images use `http://` and may be blocked or rewritten by clients:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `.../9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `.../29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
  - These carry alt text, but if they are blocked the social icons won't render.
- **Third-party web fonts:**
  - The `<link>` tags and `@import` rules load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. This is an external dependency, and most clients ignore it anyway.
  - The `@import` rules appear after the `body{…}` rule in the `<style>` block. `@import` is only valid before other rules, so those three imports are likely ignored by parsers that enforce this. The `<link>` tags still apply where supported.
- **HTML comments in CSS:** `<!-- … -->` markers sit inside the `@media` block. Some clients may mis-parse them. Only a truncated part of the source was visible, so I can't confirm the full impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The recipient email is merged correctly into the pixel URL, and the campaign identifiers in `mi_ecmp` and `c=` also resolved. The source is truncated, so I can't check the body.
