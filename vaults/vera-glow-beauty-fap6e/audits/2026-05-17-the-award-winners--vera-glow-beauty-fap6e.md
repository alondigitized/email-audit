# The award winners
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA is back in my inbox less than 24 hours after the Welcome send — and the concept this time is genuinely smart: "Critical Acclaim" leading with editorial press quotes from ELLE, Bustle, Vogue, and Allure for each award-winning product. For a brand trying to build credibility with a new subscriber, letting the press do the persuading is a smart move. The warm neutrals and clean typographic layout are fully ILIA — nothing feels off-brand.
- But here's the tension: the 20% Friends + Family offer I know is still live gets stacked at the bottom, *below* six product editorial sections. The email is trying to be two things at once — a credibility play and a promotional send — and it doesn't fully commit to either. The editorial format earns my attention; the buried discount is what converts me. Running them in the same email without clear hierarchy means each one dilutes the other.
- Would I click? Probably yes, and specifically on the Super Dewy Skin Tint callout — that's the one I've been mentally adding and removing from my Sephora basket for months. Would I forward to a friend? Only to my one friend who actually cares when something gets an ELLE co-sign. Everyone else gets me talking in person, not forwarding cold emails.

## What's working

- The "Critical Acclaim" hero is a confident brand move — it signals editorial credibility before product pitch, which feels less pushy than a straight promo.
- Pull quotes with named publication sources (ELLE, Vogue, Allure, Bustle) per product is genuinely compelling social proof — this is a format I actually pause to read.
- "SHOP AWARD WINNERS" is specific and action-oriented — no vague "Discover" copy in sight, which puts this on par with how Drunk Elephant handles CTAs.
- The product mix covers multiple entry points — skin tint, mascara, eye cream, lip balm — so there's something regardless of which step of my routine I'm actively restocking.
- The layout renders cleanly, warm neutrals hold, and the typography doesn't fight itself.

## What's weak

- "The award winners" as a subject is the same low-effort energy as yesterday's "Welcome to ILIA" — zero specificity. e.l.f. put a product name AND a price point in their subject on May 15; ILIA gave me a generic category noun.
- The 20% off is buried at the absolute bottom of a scroll-heavy editorial email — if you're in an active Friends + Family window, that offer should be the headline, not the footnote.
- Second email in two days as a new subscriber. Lush did three sends in four days on me this month and I'm already on autopilot-scroll; don't start that pattern with me this early.
- No urgency signal anywhere in the visible email — no end date, no "offer expires," nothing. Charlotte Tilbury's "Just For You" framing creates perceived scarcity even when the deadline is soft; ILIA gives me no reason to act now over Tuesday.
- Six products presented at roughly equal visual weight means I'm not pushed toward any single decision — a hero product with a dominant pull quote would convert better.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the offer, validate with the editorial.** Flip the architecture: "20% off our most-awarded lineup — here's why the press agrees." Let the ELLE quote earn the sale, not bury it.
- 2. **Crown one hero product.** Super Dewy Skin Tint is ILIA's most recognizable franchise — make the ELLE quote anchor the email above the fold and let the others support below. One hero drives a click; six equal options create decision paralysis.
- 3. **Add a visible deadline to the 20%.** Even something like "through Sunday" is enough. Charlotte Tilbury closes with urgency framing on every send — ILIA leaves money on the table here by not doing the same.
- 4. **Throttle the cadence.** Two sends in 24 hours on a Welcome-stage subscriber is a churn accelerant. If Friends + Family is the urgent story, own that send fully rather than splitting across two emails.
- 5. **Rewrite the subject and preheader.**
- **Subject Alt A:** `ILIA's most-awarded products — 20% off now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `ELLE called it. Vogue called it. Here's what won.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Super Dewy Skin Tint, Limitless Lash, and more — sale ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The clean beauty products every editor keeps recommending. 20% off right now.`
