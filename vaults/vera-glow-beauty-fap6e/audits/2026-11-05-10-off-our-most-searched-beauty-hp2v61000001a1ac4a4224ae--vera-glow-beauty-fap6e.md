# 10% off our most-searched beauty 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Sephora send since 11/2, and the subject and body don't match again. The subject promises "most-searched beauty." The body is a "New and Only at Sephora" launch wall with a pastel-rainbow gradient. The top is strong: a free Sephora x Parachute gift at $100, Use code ALONSALE, 10% off Insider, and "Don't miss out." But the hero below it is a Kayali Yum Vanilla Cupcake perfume at $29–$150. That is a launch, not a sale, so I'm looking at two emails stacked together.
- I did the points math. The 10% off with code ALONSALE ends 11/9, the free gift needs $100, and it stacks with other offers. On a Rouge-tier basket that's easy to justify. It's the same 10% I've seen on 11/3, 11/4 and the earlier 11/5 send, so I'm not rushed. The new-launch wall is the part I'd actually scroll for: Sol de Janeiro, Point of View, Crown Affair, Dezi Skin, Haus Labs, Dr. Dennis Gross, SAIE and Phlur. The clean badges help too.
- I'd click, but only on the Kayali and Haus Labs tiles. I wouldn't forward it. Ulta has been pushing coupon ladders at me all week, and Sephora is repeating its own creative.

## What's working

- The top banner puts the offer first: 10% off, a code and an 11/3–11/9 window. I don't have to hunt for it.
- The free Sephora x Parachute gift with $100 gives me an easy threshold for the points math.
- The Kayali Yum Vanilla Cupcake hero is a real launch. It has one product, a price range and a clear "SHOP NOW" button.
- The "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags on each tile give me a reason to shop here instead of Ulta.
- The clean badges and price points on each tile (e.g. $18, $32, $92) let me judge value without clicking.
- The barcode at the bottom is a nice touch for store shoppers.

## What's weak

- The subject says "most-searched beauty," but the body is a launch wall. Nothing in the email is labelled most-searched.
- Most of the tiles are limited-edition gift sets with no discount shown. I'm left guessing what the 10% actually applies to.
- The bottom half is a generic mix: bestselling perfume, top-rated foundations, hair tools and a holiday gift guide. It dilutes the launch story and makes the email very long.
- No Rouge-tier benefits show up. There's no points multiplier, no Rouge perk and no Beauty Insider event date, so I get nothing for my tier.
- Foundations get a "Meet your match" line, but there's no shade-range or skin-tone personalization.
- The footer fine print is dense. It's the usual legal block, but the exclusions are hard to read on a phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match the body to the subject. If the hook is "most-searched," lead with a tight grid of best-sellers that qualify for the 10%. Move the launch wall to its own send.
- 2. Show what's excluded. A single "Not included: Dyson, Chanel..." line near the offer would cut the fine-print hunt.
- 3. Add tier math: "Rouge: extra points on launches" or "Insiders, your code is ALONSALE, ends 11/9."
- 4. Trim the length by cutting the bottom half of the email and keeping one gift CTA.
- 5. Segment by category. If I've bought perfume, lead with Kayali and Phlur. If I've bought skincare, lead with Dr. Dennis Gross and Dezi.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Redirect-wrapped links.** The visible links use `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1ac4a4224ae4fbc369fb45280/…?__dU__=…`, which is a first-party click-tracking redirect. QA probed 12 of 52 links and skipped 40, so the final destination of most links is unverified.
- **Open pixel.** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…'>` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; font-size:0; mso-hide:all`. It uses single-quoted attributes and has no `width`, `height` or `alt`. This is functional but fragile, and it is the first of the QA "missing alt" warnings.
- **Empty offer data.** The JSON-LD block contains `"discountCode": ""` and a bare `PromotionCard` type. Most clients ignore or strip JSON-LD, so this has no practical effect, but the markup is incomplete.
- No broken-link failures were reported among the probed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (3 images, from QA):**
  - the tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…`), which is harmless because it is hidden
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_14.jpg`
  - `20261105_New_and_Exclusive_Stitched_15.jpg`

  The last two are `images.harmony.epsilon.com` content images. If they carry text or links, images-off clients and screen readers lose that content.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed width.** The wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The 480px media query only resizes specific classes, so clients that ignore `@media` may scroll horizontally.
- **Malformed CSS.** The `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid in that position, so the rule may be dropped or may break the parsing of the rules after it.
- **Invalid meta value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no effect in email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is minor.
