# Black Friday^ starts NOW online! Up to 50% off. Terms & excl. apply 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was relief. After weeks of the $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder (the 11/4 "🤑 Use your $10 off $50…" send and the 11/8 "Ends tonight!" send), Ulta finally sent a different shape of email. The top reads Black Friday, up to 50% off, online starting now, and 5X points on everything for members. As an Ulta Diamond I did the math immediately: 5X on a haul is the real headline for me, and it's stacked right above the hero.
- Then the email keeps going. It's a very long scroll of brand tiles, each with a percent or dollar number: BOGO mix and match, $100 off a hair tool, 25%, 30%, 40%, and 50% tiles, and two free gift-with-purchase bundles at the bottom. The number-per-tile approach is clear, but it reads like a catalog and not a curated edit. I'd click, mainly to check the 5X points and the BOGO. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good utility email, but it isn't exciting.

## What's working

- "5X POINTS on all purchases in the Ulta Beauty app" sits in its own block near the top. That's the member perk I actually care about, and it's easy to find.
- The Black Friday hero is black with a bold "up to 50% off" and "online starts NOW." Both the offer and the timing are clear.
- Each tile has one big number in bold type (30%, 40%, 50%, $100 off, now $15 each). I can skim the whole email and know what's discounted without reading the fine print.
- The category pills under the hero (makeup, skin care, hair care, fragrance, bath and body) let me jump to my category, which suits how I shop.
- The gift-with-purchase blocks at the bottom ("Free 9 piece Ulta Beauty Collection gift," "free 8 piece gift") are a real reason to wait and bundle a cart.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long. By the middle I'm scrolling past repeated tiles, and the best deals (BOGO, the gift bundles) are either far up or all the way at the bottom.
- Every tile has the same layout and a generic "Shop now" button. Nothing tells me which deal is the doorbuster.
- The "^" and "Terms & excl. apply" language shows up in the subject, and the in-store date is tucked into small type. I can't tell how deep the 50% really goes or on which brands.
- The very bottom looks like a big empty gap before the footer. It feels unfinished after such a dense email.
- There's no personalization. Nothing tells me my Diamond tier or my current points balance, so I'm still doing the tier math myself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the top 6–8 tiles and put the BOGO and the gift-with-purchase offers right under the hero. Link the rest through the category pills.
- 2. Show a points example like "Spend $100, earn 500 points" and name my tier so I don't have to calculate it.
- 3. Rename the buttons. "Shop BOGO," "Shop Shark deals," and "Claim your gift" would beat identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 4. Put the in-store timing and the exclusion link above the fold. Don't bury them in small type.
- 5. Fix the empty block at the bottom.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off + 5X points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5X points starts now (+ BOGO and free gifts) 🖤`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online deals are live now. BOGO, $100 off hair tools, and a free 9-piece gift.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members earn 5X in the app. Shop the 50% off brands before they sell out.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate comes from three header warnings and about 20 image warnings. The headers are likely a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed defects. The confirmed code-level issues are 10 plain-HTTP third-party pixels and 9 content-image `<img>` tags without alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe link could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 52 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none were checked for status, redirect chain or destination. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 `<img>` tags load over `http://` from `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. All use the query string `?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`.
  - These look like ad-platform cookie-sync pixels.
  - Non-HTTPS sources can be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
  - They add 10 extra third-party requests and expose recipient data to an ad network.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=164886` is HTTPS and works as intended. It has no alt text (see section 3).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. The per-recipient identifier `mi_u=202740837` / `i=202740837` is rendered, not a raw token. The source is truncated, so the body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. QA notes it "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is unconfirmed rather than a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, same caveat. One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `U-Mail@e.ulta.com` is unknown. This is an expected limitation of the relay.
- **Footer:** the visible source doesn't show the unsubscribe link or physical postal address because of truncation. They could not be verified.
- **Sender identity:** the From domain `e.ulta.com` matches the image CDN `image.e.ulta.com`. No mismatch found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 52 click links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations weren't captured. The only campaign identifier visible is the tracking-pixel label `pr_fy26wk42_saturday_bf_launch_8pm_Last`, which isn't evidence of link-level UTM tagging.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Probe the tracking links.** Resolve the 52 redirect links with a GET/HEAD follow-through and record the final URLs, status codes and UTM params, so section 6 can be assessed.
2. **Capture raw headers.** Check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results from the original message rather than the relay copy. Until then, treat compliance as unconfirmed, not failed.
3. **Add alt text.** Give the 7 content images meaningful `alt` values (including the Movable Ink images) and `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
4. **Move the pixels to HTTPS.** Serve the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` cookie-redirect pixels over HTTPS, or remove them. Consider whether 10 third-party sync pixels are warranted in an email at all.
5. **Re-run on the full HTML.** The audit used truncated source. Re-run on the complete document to check the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, fallback fonts and any remaining tokens.
