- Okay, this is Sephora actually giving me something to do with my points math for once — "Free Fragrance Sample Set" plus stacking 4X Points on all fragrance through 9/23 is a real one-two punch, and as a Rouge member who cross-shops every beauty event on the calendar, a 10-pick sample bar from KAYALI, Miu Miu, and D&G is genuinely up my alley. ## What's working

- "Free Fragrance Sample Set" with up to 10 picks from KAYALI, Miu Miu, D&G&G is a genuinely differentiated mechanic — not another blanket %-off, and it plays right into my beauty-event brain.
- Stacking 4X Points on all fragrance (code NEWSCENTS, through 9/23) gives me an actual points-math reason to act before month-end.
- The tier bar (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) is visible near the top — at least the loyalty structure is present even if the point count under it is broken.
- Fragrance bestseller grid has real prices and named products (Jo Malone $175, KILIAN $305, TOM FORD $405) — no placeholder junk, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" is STILL broken — third or fourth time I've caught this exact bug from Sephora this week alone, and it's sitting in the header where I'd actually check my Rouge balance.
- Five distinct offers (50% off beauty, free fragrance samples, fragrance bestsellers, trending picks, tools, 100-point rewards) crammed into one send with no visual hierarchy telling me which one is the actual headline — my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- The "50% off Select Beauty — Today Only" banner up top competes directly with the fragrance offer for primary real estate, which dilutes both.
- "Trending Picks for You" claims personalization but Bobbi Brown/whip it/Farmacy reads like a generic bestseller shelf, not anything tailored to what I actually buy.

- 1. Fix the points-balance merge field — this is the fourth time logged this week and it undercuts every other personalization claim in the email. Ship this before touching anything else.
- 2. Pick ONE hero: either the fragrance sample set or the 50% off flash sale, not both competing above the fold. The sample set is the more novel offer — lead with it exclusively.
- 3. Cut the email after the fragrance bestseller grid — Trending Picks, Styling Tools, and the 100-point teaser can be their own send; this one's trying to be four emails at once.
