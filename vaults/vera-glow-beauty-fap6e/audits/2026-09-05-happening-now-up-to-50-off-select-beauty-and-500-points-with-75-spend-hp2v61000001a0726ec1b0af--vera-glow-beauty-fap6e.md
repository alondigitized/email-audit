# Happening now: up to 50% off select beauty and 500 points with $75 spend
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is officially the third "Stacking Weekend" send I've logged today — I scored the 9am version 9/10 and the follow-up 8/10, and now here's a THIRD one at basically the same skeleton: 50% off select beauty top banner, Rouge tier bar, points math, but this time it's stapled to a "Free Hair Sample Set" promo that's fighting the Stacking Weekend hero for my attention right in the first scroll. That's a lot of email for one day from one sender.
- The good news is the bones are still solid — my Rouge tier is visible, the $75-spend-for-500-points math is spelled out, and there's an actual curated new-launch grid (Bobbi Brown, Jo Malone, Merit, Dermalogica) that feels like Sephora doing what only Sephora does. But cramming a whole separate hair-sample-kit campaign above the fold of a flash-sale email is confusing hierarchy, and by the time I hit the eleventh scroll of "15% off shampoo," "perfume's latest lineup," "lip's latest lineup," my eyes have glazed. I'd scroll past this one — I already acted on the identical offer this morning, so this is just noise at this point, not forward-worthy.

## What's working

- The Stacking Weekend hero banner at top is still legible and on-brand pink/red, with the 50% off badge and 500-points callout both visible without much scrolling.
- Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar near the bottom shows me exactly where I sit — that's the loyalty-math signal I always want.
- The new-launch product grid (Bobbi Brown, Jo Malone, drip iv, Oribe, Merit Flush Balm Blush) is genuinely well-curated and feels like it's speaking to a beauty-obsessed Rouge shopper, not a mass audience.
- "Perfume's Latest Lineup" and "NEW AND ONLY AT SEPHORA" flags give me the exclusivity signal I look for before I'll click into fragrance.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-identical "Stacking Weekend" email in my inbox today — the Free Hair Sample Set banner bolted onto the top just adds visual competition instead of feeling like a bonus.
- The page is enormous — hair samples, then new launches, then 50% off, then "Spot it. Shop it.," then shampoo, then perfume, then lip — that's six distinct campaigns stacked in one send with no clear single CTA to land on.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the 50% off panel contradicts a weekend-long "Stacking Weekend" framing I've now seen running across multiple sends today — which deadline is real?
- Compare this to ILIA's "Build a look around blush" send I logged — one routine, one clear narrative. Sephora's just throwing everything at the wall here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the redundant send entirely — I've already gotten this exact 50%-off-plus-500-points mechanic twice today. If Sephora wants a third touch, differentiate it hard (e.g., make it purely about the Free Hair Sample Set) instead of re-running the same hero.
- 2. Cut the email in half — pick ONE lead offer (either Stacking Weekend or Hair Sample Set, not both) and move the rest to a "shop more" digest send tomorrow.
- 3. Fix the urgency contradiction — "TODAY ONLY" vs. "Stacking Weekend" needs one consistent deadline across all three sends today.
- 4. Take a page from ILIA's playbook and build one of these product blocks into an actual routine/story instead of a flat grid — it'd stand out from Ulta's near-identical layouts.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Hair Sample Set + your 50% off is still live`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge: 500 points AND a free hair kit today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New launches from Bobbi Brown, Jo Malone & Merit just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Redeem SAMPLEHAIR before your 50% off expires`
