- Sally Beauty again — this is the seventh send I've logged from this brand, and I've genuinely lost count of how many times they've run the "Appiversary" spend-ladder deal versus this new BOGO 50% off hair care mechanic. At least this one has some visual polish to it — the fall-toned model with the curly hair and red lip is pretty, on-brand for a "refresh your routine for fall" hero. But I don't shop Sally Beauty at all, I don't have their app or their loyalty account, and this whole email is built around Macadamia, It's a 10, TRESemmé, and Sexy Hair — all fine mass brands, none of which touch my actual routine of Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond prestige buys.
- Compare this to the Drunk Elephant "It's OK to throw shade" send I logged on 9/25 — that one built a whole shade-finder journey off a launch tease, personalized to my skin. - The hero image is genuinely nice — warm fall lighting, curly-hair texture, red lip, feels seasonally appropriate and not stock-photo-stiff.
- "Buy one, get one 50% off select hair care" is a clear, specific, unambiguous offer with a hard end date (Sept 30) — no vague "save more" language this time.
- The four-product grid is clean and readable — brand name, product name, "Shop now" link all visible without clutter.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" banner is a small but real trust/conversion cue for anyone hesitating on a bigger cart.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is for me — Macadamia, It's a 10, TRESemmé, Sexy Hair are drugstore/mass names, zero overlap with my Sephora Rouge / Ulta Diamond prestige rotation.
- No loyalty tie-in at all — I don't have a Sally account, and there's zero incentive shown for signing up (no "new member" hook, no points math I can even attempt).
- The "It's a 10 — celebrate Hispanic culture" banner feels bolted on and disconnected from the rest of the email's fall theme — reads like a separate campaign mashed into this send.
- This is at least the seventh Sally email I've logged and the offer structure keeps rotating (BOGO here, spend ladder before) but the brand roster never changes — no sense of discovery or newness.

- 1. **Segment or suppress** — if I've never purchased or opened a Sally email in this many sends, stop sending at this cadence; it's just burning inbox real estate at this point.
- 2. **Lead with something that builds identity, not just a grid** — Drunk Elephant's shade-finder or Sephora's Hair Daily Deals calendar (which I scored 7/10) both gave me a reason to engage beyond "here's a discount." Sally could bundle this into a "fall hair type quiz" instead of a flat product grid.
- 3. **Cut or relocate the Hispanic Heritage banner** — it deserves its own dedicated send with real curation, not a tacked-on strip between a BOGO promo and a Klarna ad.
- 4. **Add a loyalty hook** if Sally has any rewards program — even "Beauty Insiders" mass-tier framing would give infrequent shoppers like me a reason to open an account.
