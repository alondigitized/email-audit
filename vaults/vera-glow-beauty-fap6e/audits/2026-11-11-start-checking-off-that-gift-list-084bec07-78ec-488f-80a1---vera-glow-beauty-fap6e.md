# Start checking off that gift list 🎁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is the same gift-guide email Sally sent me on 11/4 ("More gifting, less spending 🎁"), reshuffled into price tiers. It's pretty and festive, and "Check off your list early" is a nice nudge. But I scrolled the whole thing and found no discount, no code, and no deadline. Under all those "Gifts under $5/$10/$15" blocks there's only a "valid online, order by 4pm" line in the fine print.
- I do like the price-tier structure. It's easy to scan, and it's a useful idea for stocking-stuffer math. But Sephora and Ulta tell me why I should buy now, and Sally didn't. This is the third gift-list send in about a week, after 11/4 and 11/9 ("Finish your gift list today"), and it carries less urgency than either.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were already browsing for stocking stuffers. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the real Black Friday email.

## What's working

- The price-tier layout (under $5, $10, $15) is a clear, shoppable way to organize gifts, and I can budget at a glance.
- The red hero with ribbons and product flat-lays looks festive, and it's consistent with Sally's 11/4 gift look.
- The service strip at the top ("2-hour delivery, no minimum purchase") is a genuinely useful perk.
- Products carry brand names (Bondbar, Sabrina Carpenter fragrance), which gives some curation signal.
- The Klarna and free shipping at $40+ icons answer the cost question near the bottom.

## What's weak

- There's no offer. Nothing says % off, BOGO, or a code. A "Budget-friendly finds" headline isn't a promotion.
- There are no prices on the product tiles. The section header says "under $5," but I can't see the exact price or deal on each item.
- The hero CTA is a small underlined "Shop now" link, not a button. It's a weak tap target on a phone.
- There's no Sally Beauty Rewards or points-math content at all. I'm a points person, and nothing says I earn anything here.
- The subject says "Start checking off," but nothing in the email creates urgency. Compare Drunk Elephant's "Last day: FREE $60 gift" or Ulta's "Ends tonight!" countdown, which both have real deadlines.
- The product tiles lack reviews, ratings, or shade/size details, and the email has no skin-tone or hair-type personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one real, dated offer to the hero, such as "Buy 2, get 1 free on all gifts under $15 through Sunday." Glossier's free Discovery Kit with any order shows how one concrete offer carries an email.
- 2. Put prices and a "shop" link on each tile, and make the hero CTA a real button.
- 3. Show Sally Beauty Rewards earn math, for example "Earn 2x points on gifts this week."
- 4. Add star ratings to the products to build trust, the way Sephora's emails do.
- 5. Cut the duplicated shipping and 2-hour-delivery content, since that's already in the top banner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `12 gifts under $15, ready in 2 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hand creams from $5. Order by 4pm for same-day pickup.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn double Rewards points on every gift under $15 this week.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive `@media (max-width: 499px)` rules. The QA run found 0 errors and 3 warnings. The real defects are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, an `http://` pixel, and missing alt attributes on the tracking pixels. The header and link-probe results are unverified because of the relay and the skipped probes.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 56 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations were checked. Link validity and landing status are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&…` ships with the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so every open requests the same URL. Mail clients and proxies can cache that, which undercounts impressions.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` is a non-HTTPS image source. It can be blocked or rewritten by clients and proxies, and it triggers mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Third-party pixels:** the email loads three third-party tracking images (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com`). Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 5 images.
  - Two have an empty src and no alt. An empty `src` on an `<img>` can trigger a broken-image icon or a redundant request in some clients.
  - The three tracking pixels (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`) have no alt. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Markup:** the doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed. This is minor and unlikely to affect rendering.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was truncated, so the body structure, `role="presentation"` usage, and `lang` attribute are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is a literal unresolved placeholder (see §2).
- **Recipient ID in the URL:** the pixel path `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2333614/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` contains what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID. The `003` key prefix matches that object type, though this is inferred from the format. It is an expected tracking mechanism, but it is a recipient-level identifier in a third-party URL.
- No other unresolved tokens were observed in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed warnings:** the QA tool flags `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and `Authentication-Results` as not found. The tool itself says these may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so these are **not confirmed sender defects**. SPF, DKIM, and one-click unsubscribe status are unknown.
- **Sender domain:** the From address is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with proper sending setup.
- **Footer:** the visible footer, physical address, and unsubscribe link were not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed. All 56 tracked links were skipped, so the redirect targets and any UTM parameters were never resolved. No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Make the ad server substitute `[CACHEBUSTER]` at send time, or remove the pixel.
2. Change the `login.dotomi.com` pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support TLS.
3. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels. Give the two empty-src images a valid src or remove them.
4. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers (or a seed inbox that preserves them) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
5. Resolve a sample of the 56 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. Review the privacy implications of embedding the Contact ID in the third-party pixel path.
