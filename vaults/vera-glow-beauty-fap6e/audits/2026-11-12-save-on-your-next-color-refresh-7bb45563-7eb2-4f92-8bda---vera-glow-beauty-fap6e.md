"Holiday hair color, for less" with BOGO 50% off Wella and buy-two-get-one-free AGEbeautiful is real math I can do. It's still the 11/6 and 11/8 "Early Black Friday Deals" template, though. Same black hero, same "Shop now," and a color-only cut of it. The subject also promises a "refresh" and the email delivers a discount ladder, and a sale that's been running since 11/1 isn't news.
- I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, so Sally is where I go for the hair color I'm already loyal to. A 50% off second box is useful if I'm stocking up. ## What's working

- BOGO 50% off Wella and buy-two-get-one-free AGEbeautiful are concrete offers. I can compare them to Ulta's color deals in two seconds.
- The "Early Black Friday Deals" kicker with gold-on-black reads festive and seasonal.
- The two-up product grids with names make it clear which shades are on sale.
- The Klarna and 2-hour delivery strips are real conveniences for last-minute stocking.

## What's weak

- The "Shop now" buttons under both offers go nowhere specific, and the hero "Shop now" is generic.
- The product shots are boxes on glittery gold squares. I can't see a shade, and there are no reviews, ratings or prices, so I don't know the actual savings.
- There's no Sally Beauty Rewards messaging. Sephora Insider and Ulta both lead with tier perks.
- The two red banners at the top (free 2-hour delivery, 15% off online pickup) compete with the hero offer.
- This is the same Black Friday template I've seen on 11/1, 11/6 and 11/8. The fine print is also long, and "select" hair color is vague.
- The gift edit by price is a third idea at the bottom, and it echoes the 11/11 "Start checking off that gift list" send.

- 2. Add a rewards line, like "Earn 2x points on color this week," so members see a benefit.
- 3. Name the end date. "Early" with no deadline gives me no reason to act today.
- 4. Use shade swatches and ratings instead of glitter boxes.
- 5. Cut the gift edit from color sends, or send it separately.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with mobile media queries and Outlook/VML namespaces. The QA data shows one confirmed defect, an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a tracking pixel, and one confirmed insecure `http://` pixel. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated because the relay stripped the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so body links, footer and UTMs were not available for review.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro (confirmed):** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…` still has the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder in `r=`. The vendor macro was never substituted, so the pixel URL is cacheable and impression counts may be under-reported.
- **Insecure pixel (confirmed):** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients that proxy or block mixed content (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop it, and the request exposes `dtm_*` parameters in cleartext. The `dtm_cmagic` and `cli_promo_id` values are visible in the URL.
- **Subscriber ID in a pixel URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-f108-4c0f-99ff-4b5d2e0c19ef/2333878/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`. The trailing `003aZ…` segment looks like a CRM contact ID (Salesforce `003` prefix). This is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL, which is a privacy consideration.
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect health, final destinations and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Three separate third-party trackers (mon-trk, VisualIQ, Dotomi) add load and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (flagged):** Two images have an empty name and empty `src`, so they are probably malformed `<img>` tags. The `mon-trk`, `impression_pixel` and `UCMController` images also have no `alt`. For tracking pixels, add `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"`) so screen readers skip them. The first two images couldn't be identified from the data, so check whether they are content images.
- **Minor markup issues in the visible head:**
  - `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, which is invalid in XHTML 1.0 but harmless in practice.
  - `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face`, so it always falls back to Helvetica.
- No other rendering defects are confirmable from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `[CACHEBUSTER]` token in the VisualIQ pixel is an unreplaced merge/macro value (see §2).
- No other unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are evident in the supplied excerpt or QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **unconfirmed** and not proof the sender omitted them. Verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause. The From domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with a configured ESP, but no alignment evidence was captured.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 54 links were not probed and none appear in the truncated source, so UTM coverage and landing-page alignment with "Save on your next color refresh" are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Capture full raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results, DKIM-Signature) before drawing compliance conclusions. This is the main gap in the QA pipeline, not necessarily in the email.
2. Fix the VisualIQ pixel so `[CACHEBUSTER]` is substituted at send time or replaced with a random value.
