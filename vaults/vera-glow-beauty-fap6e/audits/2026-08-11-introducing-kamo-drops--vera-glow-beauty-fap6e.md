# Introducing Kamo Drops!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is DE actually launching something, and it shows — "Introducing Kamo Drops" is a genuine new-product email, not another retinol-superhero bit or a SheerID gate, and it's a nice change of pace after "The secret weapon" (8/9, 5/10) and the discount gymnastics of "Well, hi, 20% off." (8/5, 4/10). A concealer/foundation/tinted-moisturizer/firming-drop 4-in-1 with 25 shades is exactly the kind of multi-tasker I want explained clearly, and the model swatch shot actually shows the product on skin instead of just texture porn.
- That said, this reads like a straight product-launch deck, not a Rouge/Insider send — there's zero mention of points, early access, or any acknowledgment that some of us have been buying DE since the marula oil days. It's giving generic-DLC-brand energy, the same flatness I dinged Ilia's "Do you line, smudge, or wing it?" for (5/10) — solid product story, no relationship signal.
- I'd click through on "SHOP KAMO" out of genuine curiosity about the shade range and the firming claim, but I wouldn't forward this to a friend — there's no urgency, no bundle, nothing gifting-worthy yet. Compare to DE's own "Something big..." teaser from 8/3 (9/10) — that one made me want to screenshot it and text someone "mark your calendars." This one's competent, informative, low-drama. I'll click, I won't share.

## What's working

- "INTRODUCING KAMO DROPS!" is unmistakably launch-day language — no vague teaser fatigue like the 8/3 email, they're finally delivering on it.
- The top banner locks in the mechanic immediately: free deluxe sample on new subscription orders $50+, so I know the ask before I even scroll.
- The cheek swatch photo does real work — showing the drop blended into skin answers "does this look natural" before I have to ask.
- Product education is genuinely useful: concealer/foundation/tinted-moisturizer/firming-drop explained in plain, skimmable language instead of buried in ingredient jargon.
- Green-on-cream palette is consistent DE branding, no Sephora-Ulta carbon-copy vibes here — this actually feels like it could only be Drunk Elephant.

## What's weak

- 25 shades and not one shade-range swatch strip — I have zero idea if my shade is even in the lineup, which matters more to me than almost anything else in a foundation-adjacent launch.
- No Rouge/Insider callout, no points multiplier, nothing that says DE knows I'm a repeat, tiered shopper — compare to how Ulta at least tries with "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" personalization (6/10), DE here reads like a cold blast.
- Triple CTA repetition ("SHOP KAMO" x3, "SHOP NOW" once) with no variation in urgency or offer — it's the same ask four times with no escalation.
- The $110+ free travel bag offer is buried at the very bottom under a wall of small print — that's actually a decent secondary hook and they're wasting it.
- No price shown anywhere for Kamo Drops itself — for a new product I'm about to evaluate against my Charlotte Tilbury and Ilia stash, I want the number up front, not a mystery.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a 6-shade (or better, full 25-shade) swatch strip near the hero — shade-matching anxiety is the #1 thing killing conversion on a product like this, more than any other single fix.
- 2. Surface the $110+ free travel bag + sample card offer higher, right under the hero, not after two more scroll-screens of product education — that's a real incentive getting buried.
- 3. Add loyalty language — even a line like "Rouge members, this counts toward your points" would make me feel seen instead of blasted.
- 4. Put a price on the product somewhere in the main body, not forcing me to click through just to find out what I'm signing up for.
- 5. Vary the CTA copy across sections ("SHOP KAMO" → "FIND YOUR SHADE" → "CLAIM YOUR BAG") so each block earns its own click instead of repeating the same ask.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: Kamo Drops, 25 shades, one bottle`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Concealer. Foundation. Firming. One drop.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free deluxe sample on new $50+ subscriptions, today only-ish`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25 shades, 12-hour wear, and a free bag at $110+`
