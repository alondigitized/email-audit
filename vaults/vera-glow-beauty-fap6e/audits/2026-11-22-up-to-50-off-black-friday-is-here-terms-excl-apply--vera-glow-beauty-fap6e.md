# Up to 50% off: Black Friday^ is here! Terms & excl. apply 🖤
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction: finally, the real Black Friday email, and it's the second one from Ulta today. After the 11/4 "Use your $10 off $50…" and 11/8 "Ends tonight!" coupon-ladder sends, a tiered "up to 50% / 40% / 30% / 25%" layout is a relief. I scrolled the whole thing, and the structure is easy to follow. I'm Ulta Diamond, so the 5X points block near the top is where my eye went first.
- It's also a very long email. The screenshot is a tall, repeating stack of category bands. I can read the big percentages (50, 40, 30, 25) from the headers. The product-level text and fine print are too small to read at this size. The top line reads like I'm not a known customer: it asks me to join Ulta Beauty Rewards to earn points. I'm Diamond. That's a miss.
- Would I click? Yes, but only to check the fragrance and Shark deals against what Sephora is doing. I wouldn't forward it, because it's a calendar-driven blast and not something I'd text a friend about.

## What's working

- The "member 5X POINTS" band sits right under the header. It speaks to me as a points-math person.
- The big Black Friday hero says it's live online, with stores opening at 10am local time. That's a clear launch-day signal.
- The tiered bands (up to 50%, 40%, 30%, 25%) are huge and scannable. I can find my discount level fast.
- The "plus these can't-miss deals" section calls out specific items: a $100 off Shark deal, a "now $15" shampoo and conditioner deal, and a free gift with purchase. Those are the concrete hooks.
- The free 9-piece Ulta Beauty Collection gift near the top is a real gift-with-purchase teaser.

## What's weak

- The top copy invites me to "join Ulta Beauty Rewards" when I'm already a Diamond member. It treats me like a stranger.
- It's an extremely long scroll with many near-identical bands. The 50% band is followed by 40%, 30% and 25%, and the hero offer gets diluted.
- The fine print and terms are tiny and dense at the bottom. The subject already says "Terms & excl. apply", so I know the "up to 50%" is carrying a lot.
- Most of the "Shop now" links look small and text-like. They're tiny tap targets on a phone.
- This is the second Black Friday send today (see 11/22 "Black Friday^ starts NOW online!"). The cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress the "join Rewards" copy for existing members and show tier-specific points math instead. For example: "Diamond: 5X points = X points on a $100 cart."
- 2. Cut the page by half. Lead with the two or three biggest doorbusters and link the rest from one "see all deals" button.
- 3. Make the CTAs full-width buttons with specific copy, like "Shop fragrance 50% off" instead of "Shop now".
- 4. Add one real deadline, such as "Online deals end Monday 11:59 PM PT". "Live now" has no end date, so there's no urgency to act.
- 5. Don't send two near-identical Black Friday emails on the same day. Make one the app or points email and the other the deals email.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 5X points start now, Rae 🖤`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with 68 click-tracked links. Its main confirmed issues are about 15 images with no `alt` attribute and 10 third-party pixels loaded over plain `http://`. The compliance and authentication checks came back empty, but that is a capture limitation and not proof of a defect. The HTML source was also truncated, so the body, footer and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **10 third-party cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`. This is mixed content that clients may block or rewrite, and it adds 10 third-party requests per open.
- **Subscriber ID in pixel URLs.** The ID `202740837` appears in `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png?mi_u=202740837&mi_ign=164895` and in `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=164895`. This is a persistent identifier in image URLs, so it is a privacy note. It does not break anything.
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt`.** `emltrk.com` and `mi.ulta.com` are 1x1 trackers. Use `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images with no `alt` attribute (16 QA warnings, about 11 distinct images).** The count includes 4 duplicates: `852db034-…png` is flagged twice and `4f7c3146-…gif` twice.
  - Content images on `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/…`:
    - `08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
    - `414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
    - `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` (also flagged a second time)
    - `852db034-b7e7-4f49-bcce-3b881d164154.png` (flagged twice)
  - Tracking and pixel images:
    - `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/…` (2 images)
    - `ixtw8lub.emltrk.com`
    - `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Why this matters.** If the content images carry text, such as the offer, screen readers and image-blocked clients show nothing for them. That is a real gap if they are offer or CTA graphics. I could not tell which they are from the data given.
- **Web font.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans…">` in `<head>` is ignored by Gmail and Outlook, so those clients fall back to the stack. Confirm the stack has fallbacks.
- **Dark mode.** `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared as `light dark`. No dark-mode rules were visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm it renders correctly.
- **Minor.** `<title>Ulta Beauty </title>` has a trailing space. The `xmlns:v` and `xmlns:o` VML declarations are present, so Outlook handling exists.
- **Pass rate.** The QA run reports 57% passing, which mostly reflects the alt and `http://` warnings above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source and QA data show no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`. The body was truncated, so this applies only to the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN, RFC 8058 one-click).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- **These are likely capture gaps.** The QA tool itself notes the headers "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". Do not treat them as confirmed sender defects. The sender is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com`, and a large retailer would normally have them in place. Verify against the raw `.eml` headers.
- **Footer not verified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unconfirmed.
- **Subject line.** The subject has a `^` footnote marker and `🖤`. These are not technical defects, and the UTF-8 charset is declared in `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" content="text/html; charset=UTF-8" />`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 68 click links went through redirect domains and were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing destinations were not captured. The only campaign identifier visible is the tracker's `pr_fy26wk43_sunday_bf_6am_last + 32490` string. That is a tracker label, not a UTM.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the content images (`08964789…png`, `414d3e76…jpg`, `4f7c3146…gif`, `852db034…png`). Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels (`emltrk`, `mi.ulta.com`, `dotomi`).
2. Move the 10 `ads.dotomi.com` pixels to HTTPS or drop them. Ten cookie-sync pixels per open add latency and a mixed-content risk.
3. Re-run the audit with the full raw message. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, the footer unsubscribe link and postal address, and the resolved UTMs after following redirects.
4. Fix the QA tooling so it distinguishes relay-stripped headers from absent ones. As it stands, it produces warnings that can't be acted on.
5. Confirm the web-font stack has fallbacks for Gmail and Outlook, and test dark-mode rendering.
