# Score 20% off all blushes
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Cheek Week" — this is Glossier actually building a mini-campaign around a category instead of one horoscope post or one flavor drop, and I respect the structure even if the offer itself is the same 20% I already saw yesterday in "Our blushes are 20% off." The shade grid with Soar, Wisp, Storm, Dawn is genuinely useful — that's the kind of "which one is actually for my skin tone" info I need before I click, and it's rare a brand shows four different skin tones on the SAME product instead of one token diverse model buried at the bottom.
- But two sends in a row with the identical 20%-off-blush mechanic is exactly the repetition I called out with e.l.f.'s "Bestie-coded" recycled Bubble teaser — if this is literally the same discount as yesterday just re-skinned as a "week," that's not a new offer, that's a content calendar wearing a new hat. And nowhere do I see my Rouge or points status, which after Sephora Insider showing me "0 points" four days running, I'm now actively checking for and dinging brands who skip it.
- I'd click through to the shade grid because that's genuinely decision-useful, but I'm not forwarding this — it's a rerun with better photography, not new news.

## What's working

- The shade swatch grid (Soar/Wisp/Storm/Dawn) across real skin tones is the single most useful thing in this email — that's actual shade-matching info, something Ulta and Sephora rarely bother to include in a promo blast.
- "Cheek Week" gives the send a campaign frame instead of feeling like a random product email — cleaner than Glossier's own "Leo season" filler from 8/7.
- The free-gift tier ($75+ gets a free Cloud Paint Cheek Brush) is a real, specific loyalty-adjacent incentive, not vague "surprise gift" language.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — one clear hero, then modular product blocks — no render bugs, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- 20% off blush is literally what I got yesterday (8/12, "Our blushes are 20% off") — this reads like the same promo repackaged with a campaign name slapped on, and that's lazy cadence.
- Zero mention of Rouge/Insider points math anywhere — I'm Diamond and Rouge, and after Sephora's own app forgetting my tier four days running, I notice when brands don't even try.
- No urgency or end date visible anywhere in the email — "Cheek Week" implies a window but there's no countdown or expiry, so there's zero reason to act today vs. next week.
- The hero photo is striking but doesn't show swatches or shade info — I have to scroll five sections down to get the actual decision-useful content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't run the same 20%-off-blush deal twice in 48 hours without new framing — either escalate the offer (tiered discount, bundle) or hold it back a few days so it doesn't read as a rerun.
- 2. Move the shade grid up near the hero — that's the strongest content in the email and it's currently buried below three redundant "shop now" product blocks.
- 3. Add a hard end date/countdown to "Cheek Week" — right now there's no urgency signal at all.
- 4. Surface loyalty status or points-toward-reward messaging somewhere, even a small line — competitors are already being judged on this and Glossier can differentiate by doing it well instead of skipping it.
