## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it uses role="presentation" tables, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, an MSO fallback and a preheader. All 3 FAILs are link-probe errors. They look like probe or redirect-resolution artifacts rather than confirmed defects in the email. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay stripped the headers, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative redirect targets (2 FAILs, probe-side):**
  - "Shop Now" resolves to `/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo?Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon`.
  - "Receipt scanning…" resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`.
  - Both `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` wrappers returned a relative Location, and the probe couldn't resolve it (`unknown url type`). Browsers resolve relative redirects correctly, so this is probably a probe limitation. It could be a real bug if the click-tracker emits a relative Location to some clients. I can't confirm that from the data.
- **Non-ASCII redirect (1 FAIL, probe-side):** The "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app" link failed with `'ascii' codec can't encode characters in position 213-215`. The redirect chain contains non-ASCII characters in an unencoded URL. Per RFC 3986 the redirect target should be percent-encoded. Verify the raw Location header. The probe's ASCII-only handling may also be at fault.
- **Account-gated destination:** The receipt-scanning link lands on `/account/login`, so logged-out users see a login wall. This is an intentional pattern, not a bug.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 40 links were probed and 28 were skipped, so the remaining links are unverified.
- **Image without alt (1 WARN):** The image at `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/9Ue7XLygfPu2mZWrvJm5tQ~~/...S5EWCrr6...` has no alt text. The `/q/` path is the click-tracker's open-pixel pattern, so this is probably a 1×1 tracking pixel and low severity. Confirm it is `width="1" height="1"`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title>`:** `<title></title>` is empty. This is minor, but some web-view clients show it.
- **Mobile h1 size:** The `@media (max-width:660px)` rule `.row-6 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}` shrinks that heading to 9px on mobile. That is below readable size. If the heading carries real content such as legal or offer terms, it is an accessibility problem. If it is a decorative or SEO-only heading, it is not.
- **Empty blocks:** Nested `nl-container` tables and empty `html_block` blocks (`block-2` through `block-4`) add DOM weight and nested-table depth without content.
- **Fonts:** The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and `Arial,Sans-serif` is declared as a fallback. No issue.
- **Hidden preheader:** The preheader div uses `display:none`, `mso-hide:all` and `max-height:0`. No issue.
- The source was truncated, so alt text and markup beyond row-2 couldn't be audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `%%` appear in the visible source. Coverage is limited to the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both headers are not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so absence is unconfirmed rather than a violation. Large senders such as Braze/e.l.f. normally include them. Check the original `.eml`.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the stated cause.
- **Footer checks:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified in the truncated HTML. The QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result for it appears in the output.
- **Sender domain:** The `From` domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com`, a subdomain that matches the brand's tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com`. That alignment is good.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Every link is wrapped in an opaque click-tracking redirect (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`), so UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.
- The two resolved destinations (`/products/squeeze-me-more-lip-balm-duo?Color=Mango+%26+Watermelon` and `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`) show no visible UTM parameters. The tracker may append them downstream. Verify with a full redirect trace.

- The preheader says "get that e.l.f.ing bag with every purchase of $35+" and "shipping's on us". Check that the landing pages state the same threshold.
