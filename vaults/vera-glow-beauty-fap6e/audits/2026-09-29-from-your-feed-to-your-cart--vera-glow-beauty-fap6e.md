- "Up to 50% off Hair Faves" with the TODAY ONLY badge is clear and urgent, and it's above the fold.
- The free Same-Day Delivery code (DELIVERED, ends 10/7) is a useful sweetener.
- The Cult Gaia feature lists prices for each size ($48–$228), so I can do my own math without clicking.
- The "Only at Sephora" and "Clean" badges on the products help me spot exclusives.
- The Beauty Insider strip near the bottom shows the tier ladder (Insider, VIB, Rouge), so I know where I stand.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "from your feed to your cart" but the hero is a hair sale. The subject and the content don't match.
- "Social Stars" is a huge banner with no offer. It's a full scroll of vibes before any product.
- The email is very long, with too many modules competing: hair sale, Cult Gaia, product grid, Klarna/Afterpay, 20% value-size hair care, Korean skincare. There are two separate hair promos with different discounts.
- The Rouge/points benefit is tiny. As a Rouge and Diamond member, I want to see points multipliers or my perks up top. Sephora's Savings Event emails do this better.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is a huge wall of text, and the exclusions asterisks on the hero are tiny.
- Some grid items (Versace, Tom Ford at $405) are prestige items with no deal attached, so they feel like filler.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the TikTok-viral products, matching the subject, or lead with the hair sale and rename the subject.
- 2. Cut the second hair promo, or merge the two into one clear hair event.
- 3. Put member-specific value near the top: "Rouge: 2x points today" or "Your points balance."

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
