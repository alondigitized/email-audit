# 💖 Spend $35, Save 15%
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- It's Sally, again, and it's a genuinely different offer for once — 15% off any $35+ purchase, but this time the whole point of the email is "download the app in August to save," which is a new wrinkle I haven't seen in my Sally history. That's something. But the confetti-dot border is the exact same asset they've now run at least ten times since mid-July (COLORfest, BOPIS 15%, AUGUST20), so the "new mechanic, same wallpaper" combo makes it feel like a reskin rather than a real campaign refresh.
- The offer itself is fine on paper — 15% off $35 is roughly in line with what Ulta and Sephora run monthly — but Sally buries the actual redemption mechanic (download the app first) under a hero image of a woman just... holding her phone and smiling, which tells me nothing about hair color, hair care, or nails, the categories in the header nav. There's zero product shown. Compare that to e.l.f.'s $11 lip combo send, which I clicked because it showed me the exact product; this shows me a lifestyle stock photo and asks me to trust the process.
- I wouldn't click past the "Download now" button since I'm not installing an app for a beauty-supply chain I use maybe twice a year, and I definitely wouldn't forward this — it's competent but not exciting enough to share.

## What's working

- The top two banner strips (15% off BOPIS, free 2-hour delivery) give me two concrete, time-bound offers before I even scroll — that's efficient.
- The rewards teaser ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is actual loyalty math, which is the one thing I always scan for, and it's rare Sally leads with member benefit language this clearly.
- Klarna financing callout is a nice, current touch — Drunk Elephant's moisturizer breakdown didn't bother with payment flexibility, so credit to Sally here.
- The five-icon utility row (in-store, online, free shipping $40+, 2-hour delivery, BOPIS) is genuinely useful at-a-glance info I don't usually get from Ulta or Sephora sends.

## What's weak

- The main 15% off offer is gated behind downloading the app — that's a bait-and-switch feel; the hero says "Get 15% off," not "Get 15% off if you install our app," and I had to read the fine print to catch it.
- No product is shown anywhere in this email — no hair color, no nail polish, nothing from the header's own category nav. It's a lifestyle photo of a woman on her phone, which could be an ad for literally any app.
- This is the same red-teal-purple confetti-dot border I've now logged in nine-plus Sally sends this month — at this point I could draw it from memory, and it's starting to blur every one of their emails together.
- "Disclaimer: Offer expires in 30 days" under the hero photo is vague — a real deadline (a date) would feel more urgent and credible, the way ILIA at least dated its content even without an offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the actual redemption path in the hero: "15% off $35+ — in the app" should be the headline, not a caveat under a lifestyle shot, so I'm not misled into thinking it's a no-strings sitewide discount.
- 2. Swap the generic phone-selfie hero for an actual product shot from one of the three nav categories (hair color, textured hair care, nails) — give me something to want, the way e.l.f.'s lip-combo hero did.
- 3. Retire the confetti-dot border for at least one send. Even a plain black-and-white treatment would read as a palate cleanser against nine straight COLORfest-style sends.
- 4. Put a real expiration date in the disclaimer instead of "expires in 30 days" — dates create urgency, vague windows don't.
- 5. Move the $5 reward-in-September teaser higher — that's the strongest loyalty hook in the email and it's buried two banners deep.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Download the app, save 15% on $35+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in the app: 15% off + a $5 reward coming`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free 2-hour delivery and a $5 reward when you shop twice this month.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `App-exclusive: 15% off $35, BOPIS savings, and Klarna on orders $35+.`
