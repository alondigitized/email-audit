# New. Gifts. Added. (Up to $120 value)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth ILIA email in about three days, and this time there's actually something new to do math on. After "A quick note from team ILIA" (11/26) had nothing to calculate, I'm glad a gift tier showed up. It's a real reason to open, and I like the free gift stack. It still makes me squint at the numbers.
- The banner says 25% off sitewide for VIP. The body says 20% off. Then three gifts sit on three different spend thresholds: $175+, $100+, and "any order." The "up to $120" in the subject only works if I stack all three, and I'd need to spend $175 to get there. That's not hard for a Sephora Rouge, but I'm checking the fine print before I commit. Sephora would show me one tier and one number. This makes me build my own spreadsheet.
- Would I click? Yes, because the free Complexion Stick with any order is a good hook for a Skin Tint shopper. Would I forward it? No. It's a Black Friday gift-with-purchase email, and my friends have seen the same thing from Ulta and Sephora all week.

## What's working

- The "new. gifts. added." hero is clean. The tote bag in the model's hand is shelf-appeal, and the "up to $120 in gifts" circle sells the value in one glance.
- Each gift has its own card with a red value tag ($51, $19, $50), so I can see what I'm getting without reading the fine print.
- The free Complexion Stick with any order has no spend gate. That's the one I'd actually use.
- There's a single maroon "Get your gifts →" button right under the hero copy, and it's unmissable.
- The perks block names 2x VIP points and free shipping, which is the loyalty math I look for.

## What's weak

- The top banner says 25% off for VIP. The body says 20% off sitewide. I don't know which one I get, and ILIA has done this to me on every send this week, including 11/25's "Skin Tint SPF 40."
- The gift tiers are confusing. Free Mini of Choice needs $100+, the Tote and Lipstick need $175+, and the Stick needs nothing. The headline "up to $120" hides that. Ulta's "$10 off $50" is one line and I get it.
- Every gift card uses the same beige texture, so the cards blur together. Only the red value tag separates them.
- The "very important perks" block is low-contrast small text on maroon, and "free shipping" is repeated again below it. I'd need to zoom to read it.
- The fine print is a tiny "Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details" link at the bottom. That's where the exclusions are, and it feels buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one discount number and put it in both the banner and the hero. Show a "VIP 25%" vs "Everyone 20%" line once, not in two places.
- 2. Replace the three separate cards with a simple tier ladder: "Any order → Complexion Stick, $100 → Mini of Choice, $175 → Tote + Lipstick." That gives me the points-math moment in one glance.
- 3. Make the perks block larger and higher contrast, and cut the duplicate shipping line.
- 4. Put the key fine print (exclusions, dates) in the footer text, not behind a link.
- 5. Add a deadline. There's no urgency cue, which is odd for Black Friday week. Sephora and Drunk Elephant both lead with "ends tonight/Cyber Week."
