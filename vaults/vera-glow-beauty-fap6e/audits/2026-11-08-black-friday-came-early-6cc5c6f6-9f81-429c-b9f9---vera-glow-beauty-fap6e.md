# Black Friday came early 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the 11/6 Sally email again, cut down to its styling-tools half. The subject says "Black Friday came early 👀," but the hero says "Early Black Friday Deals," and the offer is only 40% off select styling tools. That's the same 40% tools line Sally added on 11/6 ("Shop Early Black Friday Deals!"). Sally has now sent me three Black Friday-framed emails in eight days (11/1, 11/6, now 11/8), and the Big Bottle Sale BOGO from the first two is missing.
- I did like the hero. Black background, huge "40% off," one white Shop now button, and I got it in one second. Then it falls apart. Four hair tools, no prices, no "was/now," and "select" tools with no list of which ones. I can't do points math without a price, and nothing mentions Sally Beauty Rewards in the body. The only loyalty text is in the fine print. I'm Rouge and Diamond and I buy mass-prestige, so a blow dryer is a once-a-year purchase for me. I wouldn't click this. I'd mark it read and wait for the real Black Friday send.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and legible. "40% off" is the biggest thing on screen, with one clear white Shop now button.
- The four-product grid is clean. Brand names (ION, Tool Science, GVP, ION Luxe) are labeled in small caps above each product.
- The Klarna strip gives me "4 interest-free payments above $35," so I know the price floor for splitting a purchase.
- The 2-hour delivery and buy-online-pick-up-in-store banners at the top are a real convenience, with no minimum purchase.

## What's weak

- None of the products has a price, a sale price, or a "% off" tag, so "40% off" is unverifiable.
- "Select styling tools" doesn't tell me which ones. The four hero products could be on sale or just pretty.
- The subject says "Black Friday came early," but the email is a single 40% category. It feels like a rerun of the 11/6 email.
- Rewards are missing from the body. Sephora's 11/8 send put both tiers in the header, and Sally gave me a points balance disclaimer only in the fine print.
- The "Offer valid online only... when you order by 4pm" line is buried at the bottom. A deadline like that should be in the hero.
- The four "Shop now" links are generic text links, and the buttons are tiny on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices and savings on every product, such as "$89.99 → $53.99," and name the exact tools in the hero. Sephora's countdown email gave me a real deadline and both tiers up top, and this one needs the same level of specificity.
- 2. Add a Sally Beauty Rewards line near the hero, such as "Members earn 2x points on tools." Ulta's coupon ladder at least shows the math.
- 3. Move the 4pm cutoff into the hero as the urgency element.
- 4. Stop sending the same Black Friday email every few days. Make this one the tools drop and save "Black Friday" for the real event.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off ION & Tool Science dryers, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dryers & flat irons: 40% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 4pm for 2-hour delivery. Rewards members earn extra points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ionic dryers from $__ and cordless flat irons, all 40% off.`
