- The hero is about 540px of pink ribbon and "CYBER WEEK IS COMING!" with no offer in it. The 30% is buried below the fold on mobile, much like the 11/19 "Plump from the inside out" send I called out for hiding its number.
- It's a pre-sale tease. Sephora's Cyber Week app deals are live now, and Ulta's Black Friday ladder is already running, so I'm shopping them first.
- The "exclusive gifts" are never shown or named. I can't tell if they're worth waiting for.
- The prices on the grid are full price, with no "sale price Nov 25" shown. It feels like a catalog, not a deal.
- There's no member or loyalty angle. Nothing says early access, so I'm treated like a stranger, not a repeat buyer.
- This is the third Drunk Elephant send in about a week (11/17, 11/19, 11/21), so the cadence feels heavy.

- 2. Show the gift with the sale. Name the free gift and the spend threshold, for example "Free mini Protini at $75."

## Technical Audit: "PSA: 30% off starts soon!" (Drunk Elephant)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with VML/Office namespaces. The QA run found no hard failures, but there are three confirmed code-level problems: insecure `http://` image sources, a recipient email address embedded in a tracking URL, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The header-level compliance checks could not be verified.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **16 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Recipient email in a tracking URL.** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the plaintext address in the query string. It will appear in third-party server logs and any proxy or referrer logs.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmteaser_prod` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). This is normal open tracking, but note the ID.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images.** Three social icons load over `http://`: Facebook (`cc02d07d-…png`), Instagram (`9b452915-…png`) and TikTok (`29f975e1-…png`), all on `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`. They may be blocked or proxied, and they trigger mixed-content warnings. The same host already serves other images over HTTPS, so the change is trivial.
- **Missing alt text** on `48678b99-d4cb-4760-bd6c-f8ac15250b4c.jpg`, a content image. The `o.gif` pixel and the Everest image also lack alt, but `alt=""` is acceptable for a pixel.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid CSS ordering.** The `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule, so they are ignored by spec-compliant parsers. The fonts are also loaded via `<link>`, and most email clients strip both.
- **HTML comment tokens inside `@media (max-width:600px)`.** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` appears inside the block. Inside a CSS block, `<!--` and `-->` are not ignored, so the adjacent rule (`*[class=vouchertext4]`) is likely dropped. The duplicate `.vouchertext4` class rule still applies, so the impact is low.
- The source is truncated, so the rest of the body could not be checked.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) in the visible source.
- The address in the `e=` parameter resolved correctly, but it is a privacy concern (see section 2).

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified failure. It needs to be checked against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a pipeline limitation, not evidence of a sender problem.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign IDs are present: `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmteaser_prod` and `c=us_de_broad…`.
- The links are wrapped in click-redirect domains, so UTM parameters and landing pages cannot be audited from source. Final-destination parameters and alignment with the "30% off" messaging are **unverified**.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three social icons (`http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`) to `https://`.
2. Remove the plaintext recipient email from the Everest URL (`?e=`) and use an opaque ID instead.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive alt text to `48678b99-…jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking images.
5. Move `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them, and remove the `<!-- -->` tokens inside `@media`.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the authentication results against the raw message, bypassing the relay.
7. Resolve the redirect chains out-of-band to confirm landing URLs and UTMs.
