# Beauty you can take from day to night 💕
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" again — top right this time, next to INSIDER, instead of top left under it. Cosmetic shuffle, same broken sync problem I flagged on 8/11 and 8/12. Four sends running now and Sephora still can't get my Rouge status to render correctly. Worse: scroll to the tier bar at the bottom and it's highlighting "INSIDER" — the entry-level tier — not Rouge. I've been Rouge for years. This isn't a sync glitch anymore, it's an email system that has genuinely never been told who I am.
- That said, the actual merchandising is decent. "Makeup by Mario" cream eyeshadow launch is a real new-product beat, the shade grid (Shiseido, Prada, Farmacy, Kérastase, Maison Margelia, ViktorRolf, Touchland, Sulwhasoo) is a legit "just landed" curation moment, not a recycled hero. Compare that to yesterday's Prada Paradoxe "Found It for You" — at least this send isn't pretending a random fragrance is personalized to me.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Makeup by Mario shadow — that's a real "new" I care about. Would I forward it? No. Would I mark as read and move on unbothered by the broken points bar for the fourth straight day? Also no — I'm getting genuinely annoyed, and I said as much on the last two.

## What's working

- The "Just In From Makeup By Mario" section is an actual new-launch beat with a price ($25) and a real product callout, not vague "new arrivals" copy.
- The shade/product grid is dense and curated — feels like an editor pulled together the week's best drops across skincare, fragrance, and hair, which is the Sephora-multi-brand-discovery thing I actually shop for.
- "Only At Sephora" tags on Farmacy, Kérastase, and ViktorRolf are a real exclusivity signal — that's the kind of thing that actually pulls me away from Ulta.
- The trial-size Rare Beauty offer up top with a $30 purchase threshold is a concrete, specific mechanic (not vague "free gift with purchase" mush).

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" — fourth day in a row I've opened a Sephora email with broken points sync. This is now a trust problem, not a bug.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom highlights "INSIDER," not Rouge — that's actively wrong, not just missing. I've spent enough to be Rouge; showing me the entry tier is worse than showing nothing.
- "All Day. All Night." hero is pure mood copy with a product-less eyeshadow palette photo — no product name, no price, no "shop this." It's a wasted hero slot between the trial-size offer and the actual Mario launch.
- No Rouge-specific perk anywhere (early access hours, birthday gift reminder, points multiplier) despite this being exactly the kind of "beauty event calendar" moment I'd want called out.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Rouge tier detection before anything else — this is the fourth consecutive send with broken loyalty data, and it's eroding trust in every other personalization claim in the email.
- 2. Swap the "All Day. All Night." mood hero for a real hero product with a price and CTA — right now it's dead weight between two stronger sections.
- 3. Add a Rouge-specific line near the top (points balance that's actually correct, or an early-access perk) — Ulta's 21 Days push at least tries member-tier messaging even when clunky; Sephora's own Rouge program deserves better than a wrong tier badge.
- 4. Lead with the Makeup by Mario launch instead of burying it third — that's the most compelling, specific "new" in this email and it's currently competing with a content-free hero.
