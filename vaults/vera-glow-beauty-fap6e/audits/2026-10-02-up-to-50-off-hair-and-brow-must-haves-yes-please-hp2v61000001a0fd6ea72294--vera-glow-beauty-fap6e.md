# Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves? Yes, please 💗
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Sephora send in two days, and it's trying to be four emails at once. It opens on National Brow Day ("up to 50% off select brow products"). Then it switches to a Rewards Bazaar hero ("October means new rewards!"). Then come a 100-point and 250-point reward ladder, a 2X/5X points block, a First Access Holiday Drops tile, a 20% off value hair care tile, and a hair faves banner. I got the point of this morning's "50% OFF select brow products 🤨" in two seconds. This one took me a full scroll to untangle.
- The Rewards Bazaar is the part I care about as a Rouge member who does the points math. The new 100-point tier includes Rhode Pineapple Refresh and the Dior Homme Intense, and the 250-point tier has Color Wow and Josie Maran. That's worth my attention. But the tiers are buried under a brow headline I've already seen twice, so it's a mess.
- I'd open it because I always open Sephora, and I'd click the Rewards Bazaar. I wouldn't forward it. It's mark-as-read once I've pulled the point catalog. The 10/1 "STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points" email sold a single idea much better.

## What's working

- The Rewards Bazaar catalog shows real products with point costs (100 and 250), so I can do my points math right away.
- The Rhode, Dior and Color Wow picks feel curated. They're brands I'd actually spend points on.
- "Up to 50% off" brow in the top banner is a clear, specific offer.
- The SAYPOINTS 2X / 5X Sephora Collection block ("Ends 10/7") gives me a code and a credible deadline.
- The Insider tier bar (Insider / VIP / Rouge) reminds me I'm a known member.

## What's weak

- Four competing heroes (brow, rewards, 2X/5X points, hair) mean there's no single focal point, and the eye doesn't know where to land.
- The brow banner is basically this morning's "50% OFF select brow products" email again, so it's the third brow beat in a day.
- The "24 HOURS ONLY" holiday tile and the "TODAY ONLY" hair tile stack urgency on urgency, and it all reads like noise.
- The body is very long, with dense fine print and a barcode footer. The product grid has small text and lots of dead space.
- The "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" ticker strips repeat the same message, which adds clutter without information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: a brow/hair sale and a Rewards Bazaar drop. Lead with a single hero, since Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty puts one offer in each send.
- 2. Put the point-tier catalog right under the first hero. It's the best content and it's buried.
- 3. Cut the repeating "NEW REWARDS" strips and the stacked urgency badges. Keep one deadline.
- 4. Show my current points balance and what I could redeem, so the math is done for me.
- 5. Shorten the legal block and move it behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Rewards Bazaar: 100 points gets you Rhode`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your points just got more fun, plus 50% off brow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rhode, Dior and Color Wow are now redeemable. See what your points buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Brow Day deals end tonight. New rewards are live too.`

The missing List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers are probably relay-capture gaps, not confirmed defects. The HTML was truncated and 46 of 58 links were not probed, so coverage is partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken or redirecting links were reported among the 12 probed material links.
- The 46 skipped links are mostly footer, utility and social links, so their status is unverified.
- `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a long opaque token path on the ESP's tracking host. It is probably an open-tracking pixel, and it is one of the two images flagged for missing alt.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is missing:** QA reports "Text version is 0 chars" (FAIL). This may be an AgentMail capture artifact, but if the message was sent as HTML-only it hurts deliverability and accessibility.
- **Two images have no alt text.**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (`images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-...`) is a nav header image. If it is linked or carries text, it is unreadable to screen readers and with images blocked.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/...` image is likely the tracking pixel, so the impact is low. It should still use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). The CSS also sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`, which disables text resizing on iOS.
- **Malformed meta value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. It has no practical effect in email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` carries no campaign context. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The truncated portion could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found** (WARN). QA notes the relay may strip them. This is unconfirmed, but Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so the headers should be checked on the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results was not found** (WARN), so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` could not be assessed.
- The unsubscribe link is reported as always included in probing, but no result was surfaced. The physical postal address was not visible in the truncated source. Both are unverified, not failures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. Link-level UTM parameters and landing-page results were not included in the QA data or the truncated source, so continuity could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify a text/plain part exists in the raw MIME. If it does not, add one that mirrors the offer and CTAs.
2. Add a descriptive `alt` to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and set `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and reconsider `text-size-adjust: none`.
