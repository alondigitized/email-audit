# Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a catalog dump with two offers on top. The "Spend More, Get More" banner offers free trial sizes with code MORE4U. Right under it is Huda Beauty 4X points with code HUDA4X. Both need a code, both have fine print, and neither is the hero. The Chanel, Tom Ford, Jo Malone and amika grid says "Personalized Beauty Edit" but looks like a bestseller list. The 4X Huda block is what I'd act on, since I'm Rouge and I do the points math on everything.
- It's the third promo email in a few days. On 10/8 the Dr. Groot trial size was the big offer. On 10/7 and 10/6 it was the 2X/5X points send. This one feels like a recycled mix of those. Ulta would have put one clear coupon at the top and skipped the second code. Sephora usually opens cleaner than this, and here I had to work to find the deal.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the Huda block, and not forward it. It's a mark-as-read unless I'm already shopping for powder.

## What's working

- The Sephora and Insider header is instantly recognizable, and the "You have X points" strip tells me they know I'm a member.
- The Huda 4X block is the clearest offer. The big "4X" badge, the code and the 10/14 end date let me do the math in seconds.
- The Huda copy names real shades, prices ($42 and $40) and "only at Sephora," which is the launch-day detail I care about.
- The "First Access: Holiday Drops, 24 hours only" tile gives credible urgency. It also fits the holiday gift stocking I do at the Beauty Insider event.
- The Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar near the bottom is a nice loyalty touch.

## What's weak

- Two codes (MORE4U and HUDA4X) are stacked with a "combinable with other offers" note, and the fine print is a huge wall of text. I can't tell which one matters more.
- The free-trial-size offer is app-only with a $105 minimum. The top banner is cluttered and hard to read on a phone.
- "Your Personalized Beauty Edit" doesn't feel personal. Tom Ford shows up twice, and the Chanel and Jo Malone fragrances are $154 to $405 with no points tie-in, so it looks like a generic grid.
- "Hot Sellers" has a big blank pink area and a lone "Shop Now" that goes to who knows where. It's wasted space for a deal email.
- Skin tone, skin type and shade-matching personalization is missing, apart from the m.ph by Mary Phillips tile. That's too many tiles for the email's length.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. The Huda 4X is the strongest, so make it the hero and push the trial-size banner below it or into a separate send.
- 2. Make the grid truly personalized: show items I've viewed or loved, or my shade, and show the points I'd earn on each.
- 3. Cut the tile count from about 8 to 4, and use specific CTA copy like "Shop Huda 4X points" instead of a generic "Shop Now."
- 4. Put "ends 10/14" in the hero. The deadline is currently buried in small text.
