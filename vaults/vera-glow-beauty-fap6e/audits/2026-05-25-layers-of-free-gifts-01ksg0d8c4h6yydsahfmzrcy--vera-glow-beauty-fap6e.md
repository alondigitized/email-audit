# Layers of free gifts
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Fourteen. I've been a loyal ILIA subscriber documenting this Friends & Family campaign since May 16, and I want to give this email real credit before I say anything else — "Layers of free gifts" is the first creative pivot in the entire sequence. The tiered GWP mechanic (mini gift with any order, full-size at $125+, Barrier Build at $175+) is genuinely useful intel. It changes how I think about my cart. That's different from every other send in this campaign, which has been a straight loop on "20% off" until yesterday's "All eyes on 20% off" threw a pun at me and called it a concept.
- The problem is that even a good email can't fully land when you've been desensitized across 13 prior sends. The e.l.f. free Jelly Pop Glow Stick drop converted me in seconds because it was one surprising move in a context where I wasn't already exhausted. This ILIA email would've been genuinely strong as email three or four in the campaign. Arriving now, it reads like "oh, also there are gifts" rather than a lead story.
- I'd click — the tier structure makes me want to confirm the thresholds before I check out. I would not forward it. My friend already has thirteen other ILIA emails and this one won't be the reason she opens her inbox.

## What's working

- The LAYER 1 / LAYER 2 / LAYER 3 scaffold is the clearest, most scannable hierarchy in this entire campaign — I instantly know what I get at each spend threshold
- Specific named products (Limitless Lash Mascara, Lip Conditioner, Sunray Cream Bronzer, Barrier Build) make the gift tiers feel real rather than a vague GWP placeholder
- Stacking 20% off + tiered GWP is genuinely additive — unlike the Drunk Elephant "Well, hi, 25% off!" send from a few days ago, which just kept reprinting the same discount without layering value
- The VIP 25% off mention at the bottom is the first time this campaign has surfaced the subscription upsell in a way that feels like a reward rather than an asterisk
- No render bugs — clean layout, images loaded, text is readable

## What's weak

- "DISCOVER YOUR CART >" is a baffling CTA for a gift-selection email — I want "CHOOSE YOUR FREE GIFT →" or "SHOP + CLAIM," not a verb that sounds like a treasure hunt
- No visible end date anywhere in the body — Charlotte Tilbury stamps a countdown even when their emails are junk, and at minimum I need to know whether the sale closes tonight or next Tuesday before I decide to act now vs. wait
- The VIP 25% off feels buried in the bottom lifestyle block — this is the highest-value hook in the email (free VIP, extra 5% off) and it's effectively footnote treatment
- Fourteenth email in ten days is the silent killer — the cadence rubric exists for a reason, and this send is paying the penalty for every email before it
- No personalization signal — not my name, not a category nod to what I've actually bought from ILIA, nothing to suggest they know I'm a skincare person or a lip color person

## Recommendations

- 1. **Launch the GWP mechanic earlier in the campaign** — this email deserves to be the concept email, not the cleanup email; send this as email #3 with 11 days of sale left and it lands differently
- 2. **Rewrite the primary CTA** — "CHOOSE YOUR FREE GIFT →" is specific, actionable, and maps directly to what the email is asking me to do
- 3. **Add a visible end date in the hero block** — one line: "Sale ends May 27 at midnight" creates real urgency without fabricating anything
- 4. **Promote the VIP upgrade to its own module** — a side-by-side of 20% vs. 25% with a single "JOIN VIP FREE" button would make this the conversion moment it deserves to be; right now it's an afterthought
- 5. **Lead with the Barrier Build as the aspirational anchor** — showing the $175 reward first creates desire-then-justify-the-spend framing, instead of starting at the floor (any order)
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pick your free gift — mini, full-size, or both?`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 tiers of free. Here's how to stack them.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $125, choose a full-size bronzer or highlighter. Spend $175, add the Barrier Build too.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Every order gets a free mini. Big orders unlock more. Sale ends May 27.`
