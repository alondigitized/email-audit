- 2. Put the deadline in a visible badge or banner, not the footnote.

# Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant, "Great, glow, and gorgeous"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no broken markup or unresolved merge tokens in the visible source. It does have a PII leak (the recipient's email address in a tracking pixel URL), invalid font `@import` placement, and `http://` image sources. The HTML is truncated and 13 tracking links were not probed, so link destinations, the unsubscribe link, and UTM parameters could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **PII in a tracking URL.** The Everest beacon is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. It carries the plaintext recipient email in the `e=` parameter. Anyone who sees the URL can read it, including image proxies, corporate gateways, and logs.
- **Subscriber ID in the open pixel.** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holcolordrop_pr` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). This is lower risk than the email address but still identifies the subscriber.
- **Two tracking vendors.** The email loads two separate pixels (`mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`), which adds third-party requests and privacy surface.
- **Unverified links.** 13 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so redirect chains and landing status codes are unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** `o.gif` and the Everest beacon image have no alt. Both are tracking pixels, so this is low severity. An empty `alt=""` would be correct for them.
- **Non-HTTPS images.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that don't proxy images may block them as mixed content. The sibling hosts already serve HTTPS, so this is easy to fix.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** The `<style>` block starts with `body {…}` and only then has `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)`. CSS requires `@import` before all other rules, so these three imports are ignored by compliant parsers.
- **Third-party webfonts.** The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` for `db.onlinewebfonts.com` are stripped by Gmail and several other clients. The `@font-face` rules for BrownBold, BrownLight and BrownThin depend on that host. Clients that don't load them fall back to the system font.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Dead code.** The `*[class=contenttable]` and `*[class=mobile]` rules inside the 600px media query are commented out with `<!-- -->`, which is not valid CSS comment syntax. That is a code hygiene issue, not a confirmed rendering failure.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]`. The recipient address is resolved in the `e=` parameter (see §2 for the PII concern).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not captured.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm they are missing at the sender. Treat this as unverified, not as a defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer not checked.** The visible source is truncated before the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holcolordrop_pr` (and `c=us_de_broad…` on the Everest beacon). No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source.
- The 13 CTA and tracking links were not resolved, so UTM pass-through to `drunkelephant.com` and landing alignment cannot be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Remove the plaintext email from the Everest beacon URL.** Use an opaque or hashed identifier in `e=`. This is the highest-priority fix.
2. **Switch the three social icons to `https://`.** The host `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com` should already support it.
3. **Move the `@import` statements to the top of the `<style>` block.** Better, drop them and rely on the `@font-face` rules with web-safe fallbacks (for example Arial or Helvetica) for clients that block remote fonts.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.**
6. **Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or the sender's ESP preview.** That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, and let the 13 skipped links be resolved to check for `utm_*` parameters.
