# Zero packaging. Double points.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-seven Lush emails and counting — and "Zero packaging. Double points." is actually one I stopped at, because finally, they gave me something to *do the math on*. I'm a spreadsheet-energy loyalty girlie and this email is speaking my language. Double points on packaging-free products from July 11–13 is a concrete event with a real deadline, and the five naked product callouts give me enough to map my haul.
- That said, I'm side-eyeing the hero CTA hard: "Download The App." I already have the app. I'm a Lush Club member. If you know me well enough to be emailing me about double points rewards, why are you treating me like I'm a fresh convert? It should say "Earn Double Points Now" or "Shop Naked Products" — which does appear as the secondary CTA, and is doing all the actual lifting. The primary button is a miss.
- I'd mark this as read with a cart open. I wouldn't forward it — if my friends don't already know about the double points window, that's more Intergalactic Bath Bombs for me.

## What's working

- **"More Points, Less Plastic" hero copy** — combining the eco message with the loyalty hook is smart. Lush always leads with values, but tying it to *double points* makes me care more than a pure sustainability pitch would.
- **Five named naked products in the body** — Intergalactic, Honey I Washed the Kids, Seanik, Scrubee, Coconut Drench. These are cult items I recognize instantly. They didn't send me to a generic category page without giving me somewhere to land.
- **Three-day window clearly stated** — July 11–13 is visible in the body copy. Credible, specific, and today is day one. That's real urgency.
- **No render bugs; clean visual hierarchy** — hero → offer → products → join-the-club ladder. My eye goes in the right order. The illustrated Lush characters are on-brand and the layout holds up.
- **Lush Club benefits section** — the point icon, the member prizes icon, first access to launches — they reminded me why I signed up in the first place.

## What's weak

- **"Download The App" as the hero CTA is absurd for an existing member** — Drunk Elephant's "Time to come clean" had a clean "Shop Now" button; Sephora's post-Stacking Weekend send linked directly to the offer. Lush buried the actual call to action ("Shop Naked Products") below the first fold.
- **Preheader is a wasted asset** — "🥕 Free Same-day delivery $100+" is their standing shipping-threshold banner. When the subject is a loyalty event, the preheader should be adding urgency, not reminding me they offer free shipping (which every brand does). Sally Beauty at least writes a preheader that's in the same conversation as the subject.
- **Zero product detail** — I see names and category labels (Bath Bomb, Shampoo Bar) but nothing about skin/hair type fit, key ingredients, or who should reach for each. For someone shopping naked-product shampoo bars specifically, I want to know if Seanik is for my hair type.
- **No demographic signals** — the illustrated blob character and cracker are pure Lush whimsy but they're not signaling 29-year-old Millennial. No human, no texture, no lifestyle moment. ILIA's Wimbledon email put a person in the frame and it elevated the whole send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero CTA to "Earn Double Points Today" or "Shop the Double Points Event"** — "Download The App" is an acquisition CTA in a retention email. It signals that Lush doesn't know whether I'm a member or not, which undermines the whole loyalty premise.
- 2. **Use the preheader as urgency copy** — "3 days only. Earn 2× points on every naked product." would complement the subject instead of competing with it.
- 3. **Add a hair/skin fit note under each product** — even one line ("for all hair types" under Seanik, "dry skin dream" under Scrubee) converts browsers into buyers faster. Drunk Elephant's cleanser breakdown earlier this month reminded me what ingredient-forward beauty copy looks like and how much I respond to it.
- 4. **Personalize based on purchase history** — if I've bought Intergalactic three times, lead with it. If I've never bought a shampoo bar, maybe don't front-load Seanik in position two.
- 5. **Consider a points-math callout** — something like "a $35 haul = X points = $Y off your next order." I'm doing that math in my head anyway; make it easy and the conversion goes up.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double points on naked faves — 3 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn 2× rewards + help the planet (Jul 11–13)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `3 days only: double Club Rewards on every packaging-free pick`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your points on Intergalactic, Seanik + more — ends Sunday`
