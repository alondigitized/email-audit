# Get up to 50% off select beauty today 💖
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one fast. "50% off select beauty today" after weeks of watching these offers cycle through my inbox — I was already calculating what I could stack against my Rouge points before the email loaded. Then I got hit with Father's Day. Three full sections of "For the Non-Nonsense Dad" and "For the Well-Groomed Guy" before I could find a single 50%-off skincare SKU. That's not a gift guide with a sale attached — that's a bait-and-switch, and I want to call it that directly.
- The sale section is in there — "Spot It. Shop It." with what looks like discounted beauty items — but it sits buried below multiple men's gifting grids that have nothing to do with why I opened this email. I'm Sephora Rouge because I shop for myself, obsessively, on my own schedule. Sephora knows this. They have the purchase data. Sending me a Father's Day gift guide under a 50%-off sale subject line is a misuse of that relationship.
- And I can't ignore the pattern here: May 26 Sephora sent "ENDS TODAY" up to 50% off beauty three times in one afternoon — I'm still recovering from that chaos. June 11 it came back as "STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off select beauty." Now June 16 it's "Get up to 50% off select beauty today." Same offer. Third campaign window. My threshold for clicking has dropped every single time. I'd scroll down to the sale section, probably. I would not forward this. I would not hit "SHOP GIFTS."

## What's working

- The subject line still converted me to an open — "today" plus a concrete percentage works even when I'm fatigued.
- Free Same-Day Delivery is visible in the hero; that's a real perk for a last-minute Father's Day shopper (even if that's not me).
- The Father's Day gift sections have prices visible in the product grids — at least it's not vague browse-and-discover content.
- The INSIDERS section at the bottom signals that Sephora knows I'm a member, which is something.
- Email renders cleanly with no broken images or layout bugs across the full scroll.

## What's weak

- Subject promises 50% off beauty; hero delivers a men's gift guide — the most important first-impression real estate in the email is aimed at the wrong person.
- The actual sale content ("Spot It. Shop It.") is buried after three Father's Day sections, which means on mobile I'm scrolling through content I don't care about to reach the reason I opened.
- "For the Non-Nonsense Dad" and "For the Well-Groomed Guy" are not demographic signals for a 29-year-old woman shopping for herself.
- This is the third "up to 50% off select beauty" campaign pattern since May 26 — the urgency word "today" has lost meaning when "today" has been running for three weeks.
- Ulta sent almost the identical playbook on June 15 — YSL Father's Day gifts, gift-guide structure, buried sale — and I called that out as a carbon copy. Sephora just confirmed it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Resequence the email**: The "Spot It. Shop It." 50% off beauty section should be the hero. Full stop. The subject line earned an open on a sale promise — the first thing I see should honor that promise. Relegate Father's Day gifting to a secondary module.
- 2. **Segment the list**: People with gifting purchase history get the Father's Day guide. People who exclusively self-purchase (me, my entire Rouge history) get the straight-up sale email. This is two emails in one, and it's hurting both.
- 3. **Break the headline pattern**: "Up to 50% off select beauty" has appeared in the subject line of at least three separate campaign windows since May 26. Write a new hook — specific brand names, specific categories, something that doesn't read like a resend.
- 4. **Surface Rouge benefits up front**: If Rouge members get early access to this sale, stack that into the hero. "Rouge access: your 50% off sale" beats "Get up to 50% off" every time because it treats me like a known customer.
- 5. **Sharpen the sale section**: "Spot It. Shop It." is vague browse bait. Give me three specific SKUs, the before/after price, and an add-to-bag CTA while I'm in purchase mode.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rouge exclusive: 50% off the sale drop — shop it today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off list: skincare, fragrance + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The sale you've been waiting for — top picks below`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours on 50% off — your Rouge picks are in here`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** blocks pinch-to-zoom on iOS/Android. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) requires zoom up to 200% without loss of content. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
  ```
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied to 12 element types** including `body`, `div`, `td`, `span`. This prevents OS-level text scaling for low-vision users. The rule is applied too broadly:
  ```css
  div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
      -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  }
  ```
  Limit to `table, td` (the standard email reset target) or omit on `body`.

- **`robots` meta value is malformed**: `content="no index"` has a space; the valid token is `noindex`. Functionally harmless in email (crawlers don't index email HTML), but indicates a copy-paste error in the shell snippet comment (`<!-- US shell snippet -->`).

- **Alt text**: Unverifiable in truncated source. Flag for manual check on all `<img>` tags.
