## What's working

- The discount code is shown in the very first visual band — no hunting, no "scroll to find the fine print," it's right there.
- "Keys, wallet... what are you forgetting?" is clever preheader copy — playful, distinctive, and actually DE's tone.
- Product descriptions drop ingredient language ("ceramide-rich," "skin barrier") which signals DE trusts that their customer already has context.
- The birthday capture at the bottom is a smart loyalty play — I always appreciate when brands want to celebrate me.
- No render issues — the email is clean, product imagery loads, hierarchy is readable on mobile.

## What's weak

- "Discover Drunk" is a stranger's headline. If I'm in the customer file, this framing is a demographic miss — I'm not discovering anything.
- One "SHOP" CTA for an email featuring three named products is a wasted opportunity. I want "Build Your Barrier Set" or at minimum a product-specific link.
- Zero urgency — no deadline, no "code valid through Sunday," nothing to move me now vs. next week when I'll have forgotten.
- The "we saw you coming" promise feels undelivered; those four product tiles look like bestsellers, not a browsing-history serve.
- Birthday and product offer as two conversion goals in one email means neither gets my full attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reframe for existing customers.** If I'm in the file, the hero headline should be "Your barrier routine, 15% off" — not "join the family." Split acquisition vs. retention sends.
- 2. **Replace "SHOP" with a specific CTA.** "Build the Barrier Set" or "Shop Bora Barrier + Friends" — anything that tells me what I'm clicking toward.
- 3. **Add a hard deadline.** "Offer ends Sunday" or even "this week only" changes my click calculus entirely.
- 4. **Make the "we saw you coming" section earn its copy.** If you're going to invoke personalization language, the product tiles need to reflect my actual browse behavior, not your top sellers.
- 5. **Move birthday capture to its own triggered send.** A post-purchase or re-engagement email dedicated solely to birthday perk enrollment will convert better and not dilute the discount message.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Mobile zoom blocked:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` prevents user zoom on mobile. This violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Remove `maximum-scale=1` or replace with `maximum-scale=5`.

**Issue — `font-display:swap` is a no-op in email:**
All six `@font-face` declarations include `font-display:swap`. No major email client (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) honors this property. It adds dead weight but causes no rendering breakage. Remove for hygiene.

**No issue — Dark mode meta tags:**
```html
<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">
<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light dark only">
```
Correctly implemented. Verify the `<style>` block (not visible in truncated source) also includes `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` rules — meta tags alone don't apply dark styles.
