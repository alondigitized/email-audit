# Don't miss 15% off your order
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a Sally email with a number in the hero. "15% off" in huge italic white on black is impossible to miss. The catch is that it's buy online, pick up in-store, and "Ends today!" is the whole pitch. After the 11/23 "glow" email and the 11/19 gift edit, both with no number up top, this is the first time Sally led with the offer. A 15% BOPIS deal that expires the day before Thanksgiving is a thin reason to leave the house, though.
- Below the hero it falls apart. The "Shop Your Favorites" block is four random professional-line items: an ion booster, a sealer packet, a tint brush and a nail glue. Nothing in it is a launch, a deal or a skin-tone-matched shade. I do skin and makeup, so none of it is mine. I'd glance and archive it. I wouldn't forward it, and I'd click only if I were already planning a color touch-up.

## What's working

- The "15% off" hero is the clearest Sally offer I've seen this month. The eye lands on it first.
- "Ends today!" is credible urgency and gives me a deadline.
- The Sally Rewards block gives me points math to do. A $5 bonus reward on a $50 gift card is a ~10% return, which I can reason about.
- The footer's 2-hour delivery and Klarna strip are useful trust cues.

## What's weak

- 15% BOPIS-only is a weak "Black Friday" deal. Ulta's tier ladder of 50/40/30% makes this look like a coupon.
- The product grid has no prices, no sale prices and no reviews, so there's nothing to make me click. A tint brush isn't a deal.
- The "Black Friday Deals" label doesn't match what's inside, which is one online-to-store discount. It's the same pattern as the 11/13 "Black Friday Savings Came Early!" send.
- The closure banner and free-delivery bar sit above the hero and compete with it.
- The fine print is long and dense. It says the offer is in-store only and can't be combined, and that's buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put hero products on sale with real prices. Show "15% off" with 3 best sellers and the price after discount.
- 2. Make the BOPIS limitation clear in the hero, not just in the fine print.
- 3. Merge the rewards block with the deal. Something like "15% off + $5 reward on a $50 gift card" is one clear stack.
- 4. Move the closure and delivery bars below the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off, pick up today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 15% off before Thanksgiving`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order online, pick up in-store by close. Plus a $5 reward on gift cards.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stores close tomorrow. Grab your color essentials today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is table-based XHTML with an MSO/VML fallback. Its main confirmed defects are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` token in a third-party pixel, an insecure `http://` pixel, and missing alt text on the dynamic images. The header-based compliance checks couldn't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 53 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Unresolved macro:** the impression pixel `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&…` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never replaced, so the cache-buster is a constant. Clients and proxies that cache images (Gmail, Apple MPP) can then dedupe or miss the impression.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&…` loads over plain HTTP. Clients can block it as mixed content, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Pixel count:** at least four third-party tracking pixels are visible: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `t.myvisualiq.net`, `login.dotomi.com` and `sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com`. The truncated source may hide more.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** QA flagged 10 images without alt text.
  - Four are the Evergage dynamic content images: `…/campaign/Vwn6U/je1h1/{1,2,3,4}/summary.png`. These look like content (product or recommendation blocks), so they need real alt text.
  - Two have an empty `src` (`Image missing alt text: : src:`). Empty-src images can trigger a broken-image icon or a request to the page URL in some clients.
  - Three are tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi`). They should have `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
- **Body CSS:** the visible `<style>` block has a mobile breakpoint (`max-width:499px`) and `u ~ div .pattern` Gmail handling. The source is truncated, so I can't assess the rest of the markup.
- **Font:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` falls back to Helvetica. No issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the `myvisualiq` pixel (see section 2).
- **PII in URL:** the Evergage image URLs carry `userId=rae-l@ete…` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email address appears in a query string in plaintext. It can leak through proxy logs, image-proxy caches and referrer data. Use a hashed or opaque ID instead.
- **Dynamic images:** the four Evergage `summary.png` images are rendered server-side per recipient. A failed or blocked request would leave empty slots, and without alt text there is no fallback.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, an aligned subdomain, but alignment can't be confirmed from the data.
- **Footer content:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address fall outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Unverified:** UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed. All 53 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't expose the final URLs.
- **Likely mismatch:** the subject promises "15% off your order". Whether that code or offer carries through to the landing page is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the `myvisualiq` pixel so it resolves to a per-send or per-open value, or remove the pixel.
2. Change the `login.dotomi.com` pixel to `https://`. If Dotomi doesn't support it, drop the pixel.
3. Replace the email address in the Evergage `userId=` parameter with a hashed or opaque ID.
4. Add descriptive alt text to the four Evergage `summary.png` images. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixels. Remove or fix the two empty-`src` images.
5. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results` before treating the compliance warnings as real.
