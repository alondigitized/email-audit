# NEW! Be so blurreal ☁️💗 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I genuinely paused on this one — "blurreal" is a *cute* portmanteau and a lip-and-cheek whip that blurs color is exactly the kind of dual-use formula I'll impulse-grab at Target. The pink cloud visual, the rosy model, the "2 Ways to Whip It" tutorial structure — this is a clean, on-brand new-launch email and e.l.f. did the right things. They tied it back to the Soft Glam family from the July 5 send ("Color source: Soft Glam"), which is a nice continuity breadcrumb for anyone who was just looking at that palette email three days ago.
- But I have to be honest with myself: this is the **fourth lip-forward email** in eight days. July 1 was "leveled-up lip combos," July 5 was the full Soft Glam fam, July 7 was $15 lip combos, and now today is a new lip-and-cheek launch. The subject wordplay is doing its job but my inbox is running a lip-fatigue premium right now. The lack of any loyalty hook — no "Rouge members get early access," no points reminder — also stings, especially when Sephora's "Summer's hottest beauty" send at least gestured toward Insider tiers. e.l.f. keeps treating me like a new visitor.
- I'd click through to the Target page because $8–$11 and I'm already picking up groceries, but I wouldn't forward this. There's nothing exclusive or urgent enough to send to a friend.

## What's working

- **"Blurreal Lip & Cheek Whip" name is immediately clear** — the hero copy tells me exactly what this product is and what the effect is without making me decode marketing speak.
- **"Only at elfcosmetics.com & Target" exclusivity flag** is prominent — as someone who cross-shops launches, knowing the exact retail door matters to me.
- **"2 Ways to Whip It" how-to structure** earns its real estate; showing lip application AND cheek blending communicates versatility without needing a paragraph of text.
- **"Color source: Soft Glam" callout** is a clever breadcrumb back to the July 5 email — it rewards people who opened that one and creates a natural product ecosystem.
- **Clean visual hierarchy, no render bugs** — eye goes pink hero → product CTA → tutorial → Top Picks. Nothing broken, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- **No loyalty hook whatsoever** — I'm a habitual beauty-event-math person and there's zero points, Rouge, or member angle here. ILIA ran "Buy 3, Save 20%" with tier-coded value; e.l.f. is leaving that lever entirely untouched.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTAs are generic across all three sections** — at minimum the second one could say "Shop Cheek Look" or "Try the Cheek Hack" to differentiate and signal what clicking actually gets me.
- **No urgency, no launch window** — if this is a new product, tell me when it dropped and whether it's limited. Glossier's "Something new is coming" sent earlier this month created more launch tension from teaser alone.
- **Top Picks grid at the bottom feels algorithmic, not curated** — three product tiles with prices but no context for why they go with the new launch dilutes the focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a loyalty tier hook to the hero** — even one line: "Rouge members: earn 2× points on launch day" or a countdown to an e.l.f. Beauty Squad reward event would change how I prioritize this.
- 2. **Make the mid-email CTAs specific** — replace the second and third "SHOP NOW" with something like "Get the Cheek Blur" so each section has a distinct destination signal.
- 3. **Add launch scarcity or window** — "Just launched — 7/8 only at Target & elfcosmetics.com" tells me to act today instead of saving it for my next Target run and forgetting.
- 4. **Trim or collapse the Top Picks grid** — one anchor product recommendation ("Pair it with: Halo Glow Blush Beam") would convert better than three generic tiles.
- 5. **Rest the lip-first email cadence for 48 hours** — the brand history context is doing real work here: four lip-forward sends in eight days blunts this launch's impact.

### 1. Technical Summary

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Braze click-wrapper present but UTM params unverifiable pre-redirect.**
All links route through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` (Braze encoded redirect). UTM parameters, if any, are embedded inside the encoded payload — they cannot be inspected or validated without following the redirect chain. No raw UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, etc.) are visible in the href before encoding.

**Image asset hosted on Braze CDN with timestamp token:**
```
https://braze-images.com/appboy/communication/assets/.../original.png?1697823889
```
The `?1697823889` cache-buster timestamp dates to October 2023. This is cosmetically stale but functionally fine as long as the asset remains hosted.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` element:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Several email clients (notably Outlook on Windows) render the `<title>` value as the window/tab label. Empty title degrades the experience in those clients.

**`tabindex="-1"` on primary logo link:**
```html
<a href="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/..." tabindex="-1">
```
This explicitly removes the element from keyboard tab order. Keyboard-only users cannot navigate to or activate the logo link. This fails WCAG 2.1 SC 2.1.1 (Keyboard).

**Global link style override strips all link affordances:**
```css
#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none }
```
This rule, targeting Gmail's message wrapper, removes underlines and color from all links in Gmail. Any inline-styled text links (not buttons) in the body will be visually indistinguishable from body text in Gmail for iOS/Android.

**Nested `nl-container` tables (double-wrap):**
The outer `nl-container` contains an `html_block` which injects a *second* `nl-container` with its own full `row-1` structure. This creates unnecessary DOM nesting depth and could cause unexpected width calculation in older Outlook versions that interpret nested table widths differently.

**Image `alt` text is minimal:**
```html
alt="e.l.f."
```
The logo image is the primary content block. Alt text of `"e.l.f."` is technically present (not empty), so it passes the hard fail case, but it does not convey the email's offer ("So Blurreal Lip & Cheek Whip") for image-off rendering.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No Liquid/Braze merge tags are visible in the truncated section. The preheader is hardcoded:

No subscriber-name personalization, segment-conditional blocks, or dynamic content tokens are observable. The Braze link URL does contain what appears to be a per-recipient encoded payload (the `~~/AAQRxRA~/` segment is characteristic of Braze's per-user link tracking), so click attribution is individualized even without explicit merge tags in visible markup.

**Truncation caveat:** Product recommendation blocks or dynamic content lower in the email cannot be assessed.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**What can be verified from headers/domain:**

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters are not inspectable pre-redirect (see §2). The risk is that if Braze's redirect appends UTMs, they are present; if not configured, all clicks will appear as direct traffic in analytics rather than email-attributed.

**To verify:** Follow the encoded link and inspect the final destination URL for `utm_source=email&utm_medium=...` or equivalent. If absent, all click attribution will be lost in GA4/site analytics.
