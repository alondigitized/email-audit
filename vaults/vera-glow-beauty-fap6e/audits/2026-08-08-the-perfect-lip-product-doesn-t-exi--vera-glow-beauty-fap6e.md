# The perfect lip product doesn’t exi-
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "the perfect lip product doesn't exi-" made me click into this expecting some Gen-Z meme bait, and instead I got... Lip Line, Glossier's lip liner, dressed up with a genuinely funny/gross "your natural lip shade is the same color as your nipple" line. That's a bold, weird, very-on-brand Glossier hook and it actually made me pause for a second — I don't think Sephora Insider or Ulta would ever run copy like that. But past the shock line, this is a straightforward liner relaunch: swatches, shade names (Hold, Grip, Graze, Buff, Bit, Nip, Flush, Bare), a bundle upsell. No price, no member perks, no urgency anywhere.
- This is basically the same energy as the 8/1 "New York in a tube" Balm Dotcom send — nice product photography, one clever line, minimal hard sell — except that one had a real launch story (new flavor, NYC hero) and this one is closer to filler like the 8/5 fruity Balm Dotcom refresh or the 8/3 GWP reminder. It's cute, it's well shot, but there's zero reason for me to act today versus next week.
- I wouldn't click past the initial "huh, nipple?" curiosity — I'd scroll through for the swatches since I do care about shade matching, then close it. Not forwarding this one; the nipple line is memorable but not "send to my group chat" memorable. Marking as read.

## What's working

- The "nipple-matched" line is genuinely disruptive copy — it's the one thing in this email I'll actually remember by tonight.
- Multi-arm-tone swatch shot with 8 named shades (Hold, Grip, Graze, Buff, Bit, Nip, Flush, Bare) is useful — I can actually see how Bare or Buff reads on different skin tones, which most brands skip.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — white space, one CTA per section, no Ulta-style banner clutter or Sally-style five-emails-a-week chaos.
- The "In A New York Minute Set" bundle at the bottom at least gives me a reason to add more than one item if I do click through.

## What's weak

- No price anywhere — not on the liner, not on the bundle. I have to leave the email to even know if this is a $10 or $22 product before deciding to click.
- Zero loyalty signal. I'm Rouge and Diamond tier elsewhere and this email doesn't even nod at Glossier's own rewards program, let alone give me a reason tied to points.
- No urgency, no launch-day framing, no "new" badge — is this a new product or an old SKU getting a copy refresh? Compare to Glossier's own 8/1 NYC tube send, which felt like an actual event; this reads like restocked inventory.
- Extreme macro lip close-up (the "smudge, transfer, water-resistant" shot) is a little uncanny/cropped tight — reads more clinical-dermatology than beauty-glam, and the model's teeth/gumline being that visible up top is a bit jarring on first scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "from $X" under the hero CTA — I shouldn't have to click "Shop now" just to find out cost, especially when Drunk Elephant's "Less money!" send this week led with dollar savings and scored an 8 from me for exactly that clarity.
- 2. Add one Rewards line — even "Earn double points on Lip Line this week" would turn this into a targeted send instead of a generic product page email.
- 3. Give it a launch hook or a deadline. If Lip Line is new, say "NEW" in the hero; if it's not, cut the "History" section and lead with the swatch/shade-match angle instead, which is the actual selling point for someone like me.
- 4. Swap the extreme lip macro for a fuller face shot — it breaks the otherwise polished, minimal Glossier aesthetic the rest of the email nails.
