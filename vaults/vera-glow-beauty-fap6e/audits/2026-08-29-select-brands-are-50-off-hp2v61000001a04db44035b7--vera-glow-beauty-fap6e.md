# Select brands are 50% off 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a THIRD version of this exact "50% off Select Beauty" send in my inbox in two days — I logged 8/28 twice already at 5/10 each with a "0 points" bug, and now here's 8/29 with a cleaner build. The good news: no glitchy points banner this time. But the rest of this email is Sephora doing what Sephora does when it's not actually trying — a wall of "Real Luxury" editorial copy (Crème de la Crème Makeup, Next-Level Skincare, Feel-Good Fragrance, Hype-Worthy Hair Care) that reads like a catalog spread, not a targeted offer.
- The hero is still doing the work — "50% off Select Beauty," Too Faced/Drunk Elephant/MAC named right in the banner, "TODAY ONLY" pill — so I'm not mad at the top three inches. But then it just... keeps scrolling into $195 YSL parfum and $138 U Beauty serum with zero mention of whether ANY of that is part of the 50% off. That's the core problem: is Kérastase 8H Magic Serum part of the sale or not? I genuinely can't tell from the layout.
- Compared to Ulta's "20% off one item, code SCORE, ends tonight" — annoying but at least clear about mechanics — this Sephora send has a better headline offer but muddier execution once you scroll. e.l.f.'s Halo Glow send this week beat both of them by just showing me the product doing the thing. I'd click the hero banner, skim past everything else, and not forward this — it's the same brand catalog I've already half-memorized from three prior sends this week.

## What's working

- "50% off Select Beauty" hero banner is legible and specific, with named brands (Too Faced, Elizabeth Arden, MAC) right there — no digging required.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge gives real urgency without feeling fake.
- No repeat of the "0 points" bug from the two 8/28 sends — glad that got fixed.
- Product photography is genuinely pretty — the Crème de la Crème blush shot and the Feel-Good Fragrance flat-lay look premium, on-brand for Rouge tier.

## What's weak

- Zero tie-back to the 50% off after the hero — I have no idea if YSL, Caudalie, U Beauty, or Kérastase are included in the sale or just regular full-price "New" merchandising.
- No Rouge/Beauty Insider points math anywhere in this email — for a loyalty-obsessed shopper like me that's a missed layup, especially with a real % off on the table.
- This is the third near-identical "50% off Select Beauty" send I've gotten in 48 hours (8/28 x2, now 8/29) — the cadence is starting to feel spammy rather than exciting.
- Editorial section headers ("Real Luxury," "Hype-Worthy Hair Care") push the actual sale mechanics so far down that by the time I hit Klarna/Afterpay and the rhode plug, I've forgotten what the email was even about.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a "Sale" or "50% Off" flag directly on any product tile that qualifies — right now the whole mid-section reads as unrelated new-arrival merchandising layered on top of a sale banner.
- 2. Add points math under the hero — "Earn 2x points on Select Beauty today" would speak directly to my Rouge-tier brain and differentiate this from Ulta's flat %-off code.
- 3. Cut send frequency — three "50% off" emails in two days trains me to stop opening any of them, which is exactly what's happening.
- 4. Move the Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) up near the hero instead of burying it in the footer — that's a trust cue I actually look for.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ends tonight — plus 2x points, Rouge`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Too Faced, MAC, Elizabeth Arden: 50% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your Rouge perks stack with today's 50% off Select Beauty`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the sale before it's gone — new arrivals inside too`
