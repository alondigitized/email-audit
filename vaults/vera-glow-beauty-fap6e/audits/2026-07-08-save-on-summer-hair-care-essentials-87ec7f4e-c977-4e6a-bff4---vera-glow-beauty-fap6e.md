# Save on summer hair care essentials 🔆
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- Thirty. I'm at thirty Sally emails now, and after the whiplash of that July 3 "25% off sitewide" high and then the July 5 "Last Call" hangover, I had lowered my expectations walking into this one — and Sally mostly delivered what I expected: competent, but not exciting. The red circle flash-sale hero is clean, the 20% off is immediate, the "Ends July 10" deadline is right there in the hero copy. No mystery about what they're asking me to do. But here's my thing: we went from 25% off sitewide three days ago to 20% off (almost) everything today. That's a step backward on the discount. I noticed. My Rouge-trained brain did the math instantly.
- The "Picked For You" module is doing real work here — I can actually see product cards that feel curated rather than just a category dump. And the Sally Rewards "Buy More, Earn More" tiers section is relevant to me as someone who always does the points math before checking out. That said, this email is squarely in professional-salon territory. Nothing about this says "Sephora Rouge girlie who impulse-buys the new ILIA launch" — it says "woman who buys Wella in bulk."
- I'd probably click into the flash sale to check what's actually included before the July 10 cutoff — but I wouldn't forward this. It's a solid stock-up email, not a "you have to see this" email.

## What's working

- **Hero is instant-read** — the red circle with "20% off (almost) everything" and the July 10 cutoff date loads first, no scrolling needed, no buried offer.
- **Exclusions disclosed upfront** — "Excludes hair color & clearance" is right there in the hero text, not hiding in 6pt footer type. That's honest and I respect it.
- **"Picked For You" module** — actual product cards with names and shop-now links feels personalized, even if the algo clearly knows I'm a hair care buyer.
- **Sally Rewards tiers visible** — the Buy More, Earn More section with the point multipliers is exactly the kind of loyalty math I want to see before I decide how much to spend.
- **Clean layout, no visual bugs** — grid renders well, models are diverse and include women who look like me, no broken images.

## What's weak

- **Offer regression** — we literally just did 25% off sitewide on July 3. Now we're at 20% off (almost) everything. That "almost" is doing a lot of damage when I'm comparing it to the better offer from five days ago.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is lazy** — e.l.f. wrote "$15 lip combos on main" and I knew exactly what I was clicking into. "Shop Now" tells me nothing about where I'm landing or what's featured.
- **Subject line buries the discount** — "Save on summer hair care essentials" doesn't say 20% off. ILIA puts the actual hook in the subject; Sally makes me open to find the number.
- **The loyalty tiers look stacked in confusing order** — the 2x/3x/4x multipliers appear to go from higher spend to lower spend visually, which made me do a double-take to figure out the actual tier math.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the 20% in the subject** — "20% off hair care — flash sale ends July 10" beats "save on summer hair care essentials" in every A/B test imaginable. Sephora consistently leads with the number.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA to the category** — "Shop Hair Care Flash Sale" or "Shop 20% Off Tools" instead of "Shop Now." Specificity = click.
- 3. **Lean into the loyalty play harder** — if a Sally Rewards member spends $50, lead with what their effective price is after the multiplier. That's the math Ulta Diamond brain does automatically; give me the shortcut.
- 4. **Swap the offer framing** — "(almost) everything" erodes confidence. "20% off tools, brushes, styling & more" is more specific and doesn't trigger my "what's excluded?" anxiety the same way.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off hair care — Flash Sale ends July 10 🔆`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale: 20% off tools, brushes & more thru 7/10`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up on your summer staples before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sally Rewards members: stack your points before July 10.`
