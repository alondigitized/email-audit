# Pick up where you left off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "did you forget something?" email I've been getting for two weeks, with a new subject line on top. The subject says "pick up where you left off," but the headline says "DID YOU FORGET SOMETHING?" That's the exact subject of the 10/29 and 10/30 sends. I still haven't put anything in a cart, so I'm not forgetting anything. The six shoes aren't mine either. They're a generic grid, not a saved cart.
- The one real thing here is the 20% off for Skechers Plus members, and it sits in a navy band halfway down the page. The ask is tucked under a Halloween "No tricks, just treats!" line, even though it's Nov 1 and the offer ends 11/2. Ulta's early Black Friday blast leads with the percent and the deadline. Sephora's Collection 30% event leads with a clear event name. Skechers makes me scroll past a hero to find the discount.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read. It's a women's sneaker email in a beauty-first inbox. If I needed slip-ins, I'd already know Skechers has 20% off thanks to the last ten emails.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF band is high-contrast white on navy, and the "Valid thru 11/2" deadline is real.
- The big blue "SHOP NOW" button is unmissable and easy to tap on a phone.
- The six women's styles have product names, and each is linked. I at least see what's on offer, unlike a generic homepage dump.
- The layout renders cleanly. There are no broken images, and the text is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a saved session ("pick up where you left off"), but the body is a generic grid. That's the same fake-cart move as 10/23, 10/30 and 10/31.
- The discount isn't in the hero. I only get "Go on, take another look" and a Shop Now button before I see any reason to shop.
- "No tricks, just treats!" is Halloween copy on Nov 1, so it reads stale. The offer is also gated to Skechers Plus members, and the headline never says that.
- The fine print is a dense wall: online only, "select regular priced items," and a long exclusion list. It undercuts "20% off."
- There are no prices, ratings or sizes on any shoe. Sephora and Ulta show the sale price or the points math. Here I can't do any math.
- The bottom third is filler (app download, Afterpay/Klarna, Find a Store, text-to-join). It all competes with the one offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer in the hero, e.g. "20% off for Plus members — ends 11/2." Drop the "forget something?" framing unless there's a real cart.
- 2. If it's a browse-abandon email, show the one item I viewed, with its price and a "Your size is in stock" line. Otherwise stop sending abandonment copy to people who haven't abandoned anything.
- 3. Swap the Halloween line for a Nov 2 deadline, or drop it, and say plainly who the 20% is for.
- 4. Add prices and star ratings under each shoe, and cut the grid to three.
- 5. Move the app, Afterpay and text-signup blocks below a single "Shop additional styles" row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 20% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 20% off ends 11/2 — 6 styles to try`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Slip-ins, Arch Fit and boots, 20% off online for Plus members through 11/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last day to use your Skechers Plus 20%. Free to join if you're not in yet.`
