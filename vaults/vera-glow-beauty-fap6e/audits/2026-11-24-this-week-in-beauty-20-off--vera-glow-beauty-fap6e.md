- ILIA sent me another plain-text "note from Marissa," and it's the second one in two days. Yesterday's "Ready to claim it all?" was the same format. I can do the points math without hunting. Two free gifts is a real hook, but they're buried in bullets. Sephora's Cyber Week email gave me a purple sequin hero and "30% off for 24 hours," and I knew in two seconds what was happening. Here I have to read like it's homework.

## What's working

- The offer ladder is finally in one place: 20%, 25% with VIP, 30% on Multi-Stick, up to 40% on select items. I can compare tiers at a glance.
- The "TWO free gifts (over $60 value)" line is bold and concrete. A full-size Complexion Stick with any order is a good gift-with-purchase.
- "Join VIP (it's free) to unlock 25% off sitewide + 2x reward points" gives me loyalty math, which is what I want.
- The product blurbs are specific. "Safe for sensitive eyes," "#1 bestseller" and "start a subscription to lock in 20% off forever" are real details, not filler.
- The plain, personal voice is readable on a phone with no zooming.

## What's weak

- There are no images at all. I can't see a shade, a finish or a swatch, and for makeup that's a dealbreaker.
- There's no button. "Start sifting through our sale here" is an easy-to-miss text link at the bottom, and "Happy shopping!" appears twice in a row.
- The number shuffle is still here. 20%, 25%, 30% and 40% show up in one email, and the asterisks mean I have to go check the fine print.
- No sale end date, so there's no urgency. "Almost halfway through" is vague next to Ulta's 50/40/30 ladder.
- This is the same promo I've seen all week, in a new format. The 11/23 "Your picks, up to 25% off" email had a visual hero, and this is a downgrade from it.
- Tap targets are tiny, inline underlined links packed tightly together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero image and a real CTA button, something like "Shop 20% off + free gift." Most of the lift comes from this. Glossier and e.l.f. make me see product, and ILIA gives me text.
- 2. Collapse the ladder into a clean table: "Everyone 20% / VIP 25% / Multi-Stick 30%." Cut the "up to 40%" line, or put it in its own block.
- 3. Put the free gifts up top with a threshold line like "Spend $100, get a free mini."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a simple Klaviyo-style text email. Every link goes through the `trk.send.iliabeauty.com` click tracker and there are no unresolved merge tokens. The QA run couldn't verify header-level compliance or authentication (4 warnings, 0 errors), and the source is truncated, so the footer wasn't available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links use the pattern `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/01M85CSJDHTEYGP2T7YMVWY48J_{0..7}`. They are well-formed, HTTPS, and share one campaign ID, with sequential per-link indices.
- The link destinations are hidden behind the redirector. The QA data doesn't include a resolved destination, redirect chain, or HTTP status for any link, so I can't confirm any link is broken.
- The `*` disclaimers appear in the "20% off" line and both free-gift bullets. No link or footnote text for them is visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the terms are present.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M85CSJDHTEYGP2T7YMVWY48J`. It is on the same first-party tracking domain as the links, which is good for deliverability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pixel `<img>` has no alt text** (QA WARN). This is a tracking pixel, so the practical impact is minimal. Adding `alt=""` would stop it being flagged and keep screen readers from announcing a filename.
- **Custom font import:** `@import url("https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css")` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Gmail and several other clients strip `@import`, so Karla will fall back to the declared `Arial, "Helvetica Neue", Helvetica, sans-serif` stack. The fallback is acceptable.
- **Document head:** the visible `<head>` contains only the style block. There is no `<meta charset>`, `<meta name="viewport">`, `<title>`, or `lang` attribute. The body contains non-ASCII characters (`’`, `—`), so the charset has to come from the MIME `Content-Type` header, which the QA data doesn't show.
- **Layout:** the body is flat `<p>`/`<ul>` markup with inline styles and no table or container width. It should reflow fine on mobile, but line length on desktop is uncontrolled.
- Link color `rgb(17,62,244)` on a white background has sufficient contrast.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The greeting is a static "Hey there," and no unrendered `{{ }}`, `{%`, `*|`, or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) headers not found** (QA WARN). The report notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. If the sender really omits them, this is a deliverability risk, since Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders. Check the raw message headers to confirm.
- **No physical address detected** (QA WARN). The visible source ends before any footer, so the address and unsubscribe link may be in the truncated portion. The check is inconclusive, not confirmed as a violation.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sending domain `iliabeauty.com` and the tracking subdomain `send.iliabeauty.com` align, which is a good sign, but it isn't proof.
- No in-body unsubscribe link is visible in the retrieved source, which is again likely a truncation effect.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be evaluated. The `href` values are opaque tracker URLs, and the QA data has no resolved destinations.
- Anchor text is specific ("Join VIP", "up to 40% off", "Multi-Stick", "Thru Line Waterproof Eyeliner"). Whether each lands on a matching page needs a click-through test.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Inspect the full raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present. If the relay is stripping them, fix the capture so the audit isn't blind to this.
2. Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass) from the original headers, not the relay copy.
3. Re-run the footer checks (physical address, unsubscribe link, `*` terms) on the untruncated HTML.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel `<img>`.
5. Add `<meta charset="utf-8">`, a viewport meta tag, a `<title>`, and `lang="en"` to the head.
6. Follow each `trk.send.iliabeauty.com/l/…_0` to `_7` redirect and record the final URL, status code, and UTM parameters.
