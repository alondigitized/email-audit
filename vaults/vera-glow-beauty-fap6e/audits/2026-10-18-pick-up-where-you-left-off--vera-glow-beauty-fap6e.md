Ulta puts the percentage in the subject line, and Sephora leads with the event and the Rouge tier perk. ## What's working

- The women's shoe grid (Mark Nason jogger, BOBS Kitten Kicks, Cozy Kiss slipper) finally matches me. It's a real step up from the kids' truck sneaker in the 8/24 send.
- The navy "MEMBERS TAKE 20% OFF" band is the clearest block in the email. It has a big number and a "Valid thru 10/21" deadline.
- The fine print states the dates (10/14–10/21), says it's online only, and says you must be logged into a Skechers Plus account. Nothing about the terms is hidden.
- The hero is clean: big blue headline and a solid "SHOP NOW" button. There are no render bugs that I can see.

## What's weak

- "Did you forget something?" is a false cart-abandon line. I never added anything, so it reads as a lie, and it's the same trick as "We saved these styles for you" on 10/17.
- The 20% is below the fold of the first screen. The hero says "Go on, take another look" with no offer, so there's no reason to tap yet.
- None of the six tiles has a price, a sale price, a rating or a review. They're only underlined text links, and the tap targets are small on a phone.
- The bottom half is a generic footer pile: Women/Men/Kids list, text signup, app download, curbside pickup, Afterpay and Klarna, and social icons. It buries the one thing I came for.
- It's the fourth Skechers "come back" send I've logged. Sally Beauty's "join our rewards" email wore me out the same way.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 20% member offer into the hero. Headline "20% off, members only, through 10/21" with the button right under it. Then the grid is proof, not the pitch.
- 2. Drop the fake cart-abandon framing unless the email shows items I actually viewed. Otherwise say it's a member sale.
- 3. Add a sale price and a star rating to each tile, and make the whole tile tappable. e.l.f. puts the $11 price on every block, and that's why I clicked.

It also loads 10 images over plain `http://`, has no plain-text part, and has an empty `<title>`. Several header-level checks could not be completed through the AgentMail relay.

  - Token-based unsubscribe endpoints often reject non-browser probes, so this may be a probe artifact. I can't confirm it is a real break from this data.

- **Plain-text part.** The text version is 0 chars (FAIL). The relay may not capture it, but if it is truly absent, that hurts deliverability and accessibility. Multipart/alternative should include a text part.
- **Physical address.** I can't see a postal address in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

- **Landing page alignment.** The abandon-PLP product and CTA links weren't in the probed or visible data, so landing-page continuity is unverified.
