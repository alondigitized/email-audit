## Executive summary

- Okay, this one actually made me stop scrolling — three "looks" broken out by model with a little diary-entry caption ("I just got done riding my bike," "is it obvious I have a crush on you?") is a genuinely cute editorial device, and pairing each with the exact shade swatch underneath is the kind of merchandising I wish more brands did. It's still blush, again, for like the fifth Glossier send this month, but at least this time it's dressed as a lookbook instead of a straight sale blast.
- The 20% off Cloud Paint + Cloud Paint Plush Blush is buried in tiny subhead text under the hero though, and there's zero urgency on it — no end date, no "today only," nothing. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off⁺ ENDS TODAY" energy or even Sally's endless COLORfest countdown — Glossier just sort of... mentions the discount and moves on to vibes. I'd probably scroll through this one for the swatches (genuinely good product photography) but I'm not opening my wallet off this send specifically.
- Would I click? Maybe, to browse Storm or Soar since I'm due for a warm-weather blush anyway. Would I forward it? To a friend who's into the "no-makeup makeup" aesthetic, sure — the model diversity and shade range (Dusk to Bloom) is legitimately well done. Mark as read and move on, not urgent.

## What's working

- The three-model, three-mood structure (tropical vacation / bike ride / crush) gives the blush categories actual personality instead of a flat product grid.
- Shade swatches under each look (Storm, Burst, Dusk, Puff, Soar, Bloom) let me actually judge undertone before clicking — Glossier rarely gives me this much product detail up front.
- Model diversity across skin tones is solid — I can actually picture how Soar or Dusk would look on me versus on Gabriella or Lily.
- Clean single-column layout, no clutter, easy to skim on my phone without zooming.

## What's weak

- The 20% off offer is one small line under the H1 — no banner, no color pop, no countdown. It reads like an afterthought next to how hard Ulta and Sally push their percentage-off headers.
- No mention of my Glossier points or any loyalty status — I don't even know if Glossier has a real tier program compared to my Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond math, and this send does nothing to remind me if I should care.
- "Get the look" and "Shop now" are repeated like six times with zero variation — after the third one it stops being a CTA and starts being wallpaper.
- No urgency/deadline on the discount at all, so there's no reason for me to act today versus next week when the next blush email inevitably lands.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% off in the hero as an actual visual element — bigger type, a color block, maybe a code — not a throwaway subhead line. This is the single biggest lever; right now the offer is invisible until you're already reading closely.
- 2. Add a deadline. "20% off through Sunday" turns this into something I act on today instead of file away.
- 3. Vary the CTA copy per section — "Shop Storm," "Shop Puff" — instead of six identical gray "Shop now" buttons that blur together on scroll.
- 4. If Glossier has any kind of loyalty or repeat-purchase signal, surface it — even "You've bought Cloud Paint before, get 20% off a refill" would beat generic messaging.
