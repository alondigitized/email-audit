# 50% off select beauty⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'll give Sephora this — of the four sends I've logged from them this week, this is the cleanest by a mile. "GET 50% OFF" is huge and unmissable, there's an actual "TODAY ONLY" grid up top with three real products and real crossed-out prices (Clarins Double Serum $48, Estée Lauder Double Wear $13, OUAI Hair Mist $19), and then a second "Get 50% Off These Picks" grid that's genuinely scannable. No Rewards Bazaar sprawl, no brand-spotlight tangent, no Holiday Drops teaser bolted on — just the sale.
- That said, my Rouge tier is showing as "you have 0 points" right in the top banner, which is either a data glitch or the laziest kind of non-personalization, and it stings more because the tier bar below flags me correctly as Rouge. And "select beauty" is still doing a LOT of lifting for a grid that spans skincare, hair mist, fragrance, mascara, eye cream, lip gloss, and sunscreen — that's not a curated edit, that's "everything we could hit 50% on today."
- I'd click into the Clarins Double Serum since that's a genuine holy-grail item at a real discount, but I'm not forwarding this — my friends are as sick of daily Sephora sale spam as I am, and this is the fourth one this week.

## What's working

- "GET 50% OFF" hero is bold, immediate, no scrolling needed to find the offer — actually beats yesterday's version on hierarchy.
- The "TODAY ONLY" trio (Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI) has real before/after pricing, not vague percentages — that's the specificity ILIA's "Recipe" email nailed and most Sephora sends this week didn't bother with.
- Second grid is a clean 4x2 layout with visible strikethrough pricing on every item — way tighter than the Frankenstein scroll I flagged on 9/10.
- Free shipping for Beauty Insiders is called out right under the hero, which is a real loyalty perk I actually use.

## What's weak

- The insider bar literally says "you have 0 points" — I'm Rouge, I have points, this is either broken personalization or it's showing me as a stranger, and either way it undercuts every other Rouge signal on the page.
- "Select beauty" spans nine unrelated categories (skincare, hair mist, mascara, eye cream, lip gloss, sunscreen, fragrance) — this isn't a curated drop, it's an inventory clearance dressed up as an event.
- Zero skin-tone or skin-type personalization anywhere — the Tarte lip gloss and Hung Vanngo lip product swatches are generic, no "picked for you" logic visible.
- This is my fourth near-identical 50%-off Sephora send this week (9/10, 9/11 x2, now 9/12) — the cadence is genuinely fatiguing even though today's execution is better.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points display — showing "0 points" to a Rouge member is a trust-breaker; if it can't render my real balance, drop the line entirely rather than showing a wrong number.
- 2. Tighten "select beauty" into an actual edit — pick one theme (e.g., "skincare refresh" or "fragrance layering") instead of nine random categories, so it reads curated instead of clearance.
- 3. Add a skin-type or skin-tone filter tag on product cards (like "for dry skin" or shade-matched swatches) — this is the one lever Sephora has that a mass-market clearance email can't fake.
- 4. Space out the cadence — four sends in a week with the same "50% off select beauty" mechanic trains me to stop opening, not to shop today.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clarins Double Serum, half off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge perks + 50% off, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping included. New deals drop tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3 cult favorites, 50% off while they last.`
