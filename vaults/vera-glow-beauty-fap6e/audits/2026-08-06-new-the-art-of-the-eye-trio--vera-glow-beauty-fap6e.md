# NEW The Art of the Eye Trio
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually a nice one — "The Art of the Eye" launch, three products (shadow stick, eyeliner, mascara) framed as "paint / sketch / frame," and a genuinely useful "Eye Color Theory" matrix at the bottom matching eyeliner/mascara shade to your eye color. That's the kind of personalization-adjacent content I actually want from a beauty brand — it's not "hey Vera," but it's treating me like someone who thinks about color theory when picking a liner, which is more than most sends manage.
- The top banner — "4X VIP POINTS — NEW THRU LINE EYELINER" — is exactly the kind of tier-math hook that gets me to actually read past the subject line, and I'll take a launch-day 4X multiplier over another vague "member perks" mention any day. Compare that to Ulta's last send to me, which was the third rerun of the same "20% off one item, ends tonight" mechanic since 7/26 — ILIA's at least giving me something new to look at instead of recycling urgency copy.
- Where it loses me a bit: this is a lot of scroll for three products, and there's no actual discount on two of the three items — just "SAVE 15%" tucked under the mascara. I'd open it, I'd probably click through to the eye color chart out of curiosity, but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — it's a nice trio-launch email, not a "you need to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The "paint / sketch / frame" concept ties three separate products into one coherent story instead of feeling like three bolted-together promos.
- 4X VIP points banner up top is real tier-program value, not vague "member benefits" language — this is the strongest hook in the email.
- The Eye Color Theory grid (Brown/Blue/Green/Hazel × shade combos) is genuinely useful personalization-by-proxy — better than anything Glossier or Sally Beauty have sent me this week.
- Clean, minimal layout — desaturated sage/cream palette feels premium and on-brand, no clutter.
- "Free U.S. shipping over $50" and trust badges (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact recycle) are visible without digging.

## What's weak

- Only one of the three products (mascara) has a visible discount — "SAVE 15%" — the shadow stick and eyeliner get zero pricing signal, so it reads more like a catalog page than a promo.
- It's a long scroll for three products; by the time I hit the color chart I've forgotten the "A Daily Practice" CTA from the top.
- No urgency or expiration on that 15% — no deadline means no reason to act today versus next week.
- "A Daily Practice" as a CTA is vague — doesn't tell me if I'm shopping the trio, one product, or a routine guide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real, consistent offer across all three products (or bundle pricing on the trio) instead of burying 15% under just the mascara — right now it looks like ILIA forgot to discount two-thirds of the launch.
- 2. Move the Eye Color Theory chart up higher, or tease it right after the hero — it's the most differentiated content in the email and it's currently below three full product blocks.
- 3. Swap "A Daily Practice →" for something concrete like "Shop the Trio" so the CTA matches what I'm actually clicking into.
- 4. Add a deadline to the 15% mascara offer — even "this week only" gives me a reason to act now instead of bookmarking it.
