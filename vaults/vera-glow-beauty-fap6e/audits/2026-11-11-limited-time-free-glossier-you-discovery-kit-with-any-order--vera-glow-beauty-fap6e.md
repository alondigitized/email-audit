# LIMITED TIME: Free Glossier You Discovery Kit with ANY order
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this one is finally a real offer. A free Discovery Kit with any order, through Thursday, is the first Glossier email in a while that gives me something concrete. After the merch run on 11/3, 11/5, 11/6 and 11/9, with no price and no deadline in any of them, this is a relief. The "NOW THROUGH THURSDAY" eyebrow and the kit photo at the top tell me what the deal is in about two seconds.
- The rest of the email is a fragrance catalog: four scents, each with its own "You smell ___" line and a Shop now button. That's cute and on brand, but I'm here for the free kit. Every button goes to a $-priced bottle, and none of them tell me what I need to spend to get the kit. "Any order" plus an asterisk makes me assume there's a catch. I'd click Get yours to see the real minimum. I wouldn't forward it, because there's no points math and nothing to brag about to a friend.
- Compared with Sephora Insider's "points = rewards" send, this is simpler but thinner. Sephora gave me math to do. Glossier gives me a sample set and the word "free." Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder at least states the spend thresholds up front. Glossier hides its threshold behind the asterisk.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "Free Glossier You Discovery Kit with any order*" sits next to a photo of the red kit box with the four vials, so I see the offer and the product together.
- "NOW THROUGH THURSDAY" gives a real deadline, which is better than the invented urgency in Sally Beauty's "Finish your gift list today."
- "Offer automatically applied at checkout" means no code to copy, which is easier than Ulta's GIFTS code.
- The four scent blocks use short note lists (Iris, Pink Pepper, Ambrox; Buttercream, Plum Butter) and alternating image placement, so they scan quickly.
- The "You smell good / rich / pretty / yummy" voice is fun and consistent with the brand.

## What's weak

- "Any order*" gives me no minimum. The terms are in tiny footer text at the bottom, so the fine print is buried and I can't tell whether it's honest.
- All four Shop now buttons look identical to the hero button, and none of them lead back to the kit. The email has one offer and four buttons for something else.
- The right-column copy is cut off at the edge on "best-sell…" and "Glossier Y…". It looks like a render bug and a little sloppy.
- There's no loyalty or points angle at all, and no pricing on any scent. I can't tell whether the kit is worth it compared with buying a Sephora sample set with Rouge points.
- This is the fifth Glossier send in about a week and a half that leads with a freebie (the 10/22 free gift, the free mug on 10/30, 11/1 and 11/2). The "LIMITED TIME" subject feels like more of the same.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the spend threshold in the hero, for example "Free Discovery Kit with any $XX order." If the minimum really is nothing, say "no minimum" in plain text. A hidden catch costs more clicks than a threshold would.
- 2. Make the scent blocks lead back to the offer, with each button reading something like "Add to bag, kit free."
- 3. Show prices and a value line on the kit, such as "$XX value, yours free."
- 4. Fix the clipped text in the right-hand columns.
- 5. Add a member or loyalty line, and one social proof element such as a rating or review count.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free scent kit with any order, ends Thursday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 Glossier You scents, yours free through Thursday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Iris, Doux, Fleur, Rêve: sample all four. Applied automatically at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No code needed. Add anything to your bag and the kit's included.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built email has no automated errors, only 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The confirmed defects are 5 content images with no `alt` text and a malformed `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` tag. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links go through Klaviyo click tracking (`ctrk.klclick.com/l/...`), so the final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.
- 12 material links were probed and 6 were skipped, because footer, utility and social links are deprioritized. The QA output reports no failures, but it also doesn't list status codes, so it confirms nothing about link health. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown.
- The body opens with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M742GHW100KQVYSXM6TSR1ZQ_0"></span>`. A `<span>` has no valid `rel` or `href` attributes, so this is invalid markup. It looks like a Klaviyo or ESP injection that is meant to be a tracked link.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M742GHW100KQVYSXM6TSR1ZQ` has no `alt`. This is minor, and a pixel should have `alt=""`.
