As a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, I'd still do the math: 30% off plus a $32-value Hydra and the Bright kit at $100+. That's decent. - The lime free-shipping bar at the top is a nice extra with no minimum.
- The pastel products against the red ornaments look festive and on brand.
- The fine print includes the actual dates (11/25 to 11/26, ET), so the deadline is credible.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "up to 40%" but the body leads with 30%. The 40% is only on Last Chance items, buried in the asterisk. That's a bait-and-switch feel.
- There are three different "Shop Now" links and no product-specific CTAs. Ulta would at least link the hero products.
- The cyan block is copy-heavy filler ("You don't want to miss out on the gifts") with a small outlined button that's easy to miss.
- There's no mention of loyalty or VIP status, even though I got "Hey, VIP!" on 11/24. I'm not being treated like a known customer.
- There are no prices, no reviews and no hero product. "Stock up on everything" doesn't tell me what to buy.
- The deadline is only in the fine print. A "ends tomorrow 11:59 ET" line up top would add real urgency.

- 2. Make the subject match the hero, or lead with the 40% Last Chance items and show them in the body.
- 3. Link to individual products with prices, the way Sephora did on 11/25 with its stacked offers.
- 4. Add VIP or member recognition and a short best-sellers row with ratings.

# Technical Audit: Drunk Elephant "Up to 40% off."

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with several code-level problems. Subscriber email and ID are exposed in tracking URLs, three images use `http://`, and the viewport meta blocks zoom. Compliance headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source is truncated, so findings below cover only the visible portion.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **13 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmreminderpm_` is an open-tracking pixel with no `alt`. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Second tracking image:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…&c=us_de_broad…` is a tracking image from a second vendor (Everest). It also has no `alt`.
- **Two tracking vendors:** The email loads two separate tracking domains, `mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images (3):** The social icons load over `http://`, so clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings.
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-…png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-…png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-…png` (TikTok)
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it. This is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Missing language:** `<html xmlns=… >` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language.
- **Third-party font dependency:** Three `<link>` tags and `@import` rules pull from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. The `@import` rules sit after the `body{}` rule inside `<style>`, which makes them invalid per the CSS spec, so they are ignored. The `<link>` tags are stripped by most clients (Gmail), so the fonts will fall back. The fonts also depend on a third-party host and on `drunkelephant.com` Demandware asset paths with version hashes (`v1719565389232`) that can break.
- **Malformed CSS:** HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->`) sit inside the `@media` block. Some parsers may mishandle the rules around them.
- **Overly narrow mobile rules:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px/12px !important` with `max-height` limits. This risks clipped or collapsed text on mobile.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Drunk Elephant</title>` is generic. Low severity.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **PII in URL:** The Everest tracking image URL carries the recipient's email in plaintext: `?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app`. Email addresses in query strings leak through logs, referrers and proxies. A hashed identifier would avoid this.
- **Subscriber ID in the pixel:** `mi_u=98161986` is a raw numeric subscriber ID in the open-tracking URL.
- **Unresolved tokens:** None found in the visible HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip it. It is **unverified, not confirmed absent**. Re-check against the raw original message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, and this sender is clearly bulk.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is good practice, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source. Verify in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Campaign tag:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmreminderpm_` in the pixel suggests a "BFCM reminder PM" send, while the sender name says "Cyber Week" and the subject says "Up to 40% off." This is a tracking-parameter inconsistency, not necessarily a defect.

- **Landing-page alignment:** Not testable. Destination links were skipped as tracking redirects.

4. **Add `alt=""` to both tracking images** (`o.gif` and the Everest pixel).
5. **Verify the headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) before treating these as defects. The relay may be dropping them.
6. **Fix the CSS.** Move `@import` rules to the top of `<style>` or drop them in favor of inline fallback font stacks. Remove the HTML comments inside `@media`. Re-check the `max-width:15px` mobile rules.
