# Last Chance: BOGO 50% Off Hair Care ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-six. I'm calling this email twenty-six because that's roughly where I am in Sally's cadence right now, and my inbox has the marks to show it. Email twenty-five was "Up to 40% Off: Shop Before It Ends" — and I noted at the time the creative was running on fumes. So here's the thing: email twenty-six actually pivots. The offer changed. BOGO 50% off hair care is a structural shift from the flat discount they've been dragging through the calendar for a week, and I noticed. But the math-brain kicked in immediately — BOGO 50% is effectively 25% effective savings on a two-item pair. Email twenty-five was 40% off those same products. If I need one thing, the old deal was better. If I'm stacking two Ion dye kits for back-to-back touch-ups, maybe BOGO wins. The fact that I ran that calculation while looking at the hero means the subject earned the open. The body didn't fully close.
- I'd click through to see which brands qualify — Ion and L'Oreal Technique in the product grid are salon-grade staples I legitimately reach for at home. I would not forward this. It's a solo purchase calculation email, not a "you need to see this" moment. Mark as read after clicking, assuming the brands I want are in-deal.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% is a concrete, anchored offer with a hard June 30 deadline surfaced right in the hero** — I didn't have to hunt for the expiry or dig through a modal
- **The model is doing real work:** vivid auburn-red hair that reads as clearly colored, connects instantly to the hair color products below, and looks like someone who actually uses the SKUs Sally is selling
- **Ion and L'Oreal Technique in the trending grid are the right call** — these are brands Sally customers actually buy, not aspirational padding, and they belong under a hair color BOGO
- **"Keep your color looking fresh" carries the single-theme momentum** from hero through mid-email without a jarring category pivot (until the nail glue, which I'll get to)

## What's weak

- **BOGO 50% is worse math than the 40% off Sally was running two days ago,** and no one acknowledged the change — I clocked it immediately, which creates friction instead of excitement
- **Beauty Secrets nail glue is sitting in "Don't Miss What's Trending"** while the BOGO is explicitly for select hair care — confusing inclusion that makes me wonder what else doesn't qualify for the deal I came for
- **Zero loyalty mention.** Sephora would have worked in my Rouge cashback at this offer tier; Ulta bakes Ultamate Rewards math into their sale CTAs. Sally treats me like an anonymous visitor every single send
- **The Pride Month block has no product hook** — it's two women smiling next to a logo. Lush's recent "I've got chills" send found a way to make seasonal moments feel brand-genuine and actually tied it to product. This reads like a banner they're contractually obligated to include
- **"Last Chance" has been fully diluted.** At email twenty-six in a single stretch, urgency language is just scheduled calendar noise

## Recommendations

- 1. **Do the math in the hero.** "Grab two, pay 75%" or "Buy two, split the second 50%" — I'm calculating it anyway, and a brand that shows its work earns the click faster than one that makes me do algebra
- 2. **Audit the trending grid for BOGO eligibility.** Every product below the hero should be in the deal. Nail glue in a hair care BOGO email is a trust tax
- 3. **Add a loyalty entry point.** Even "New to Sally? Beauty Club members save more" starts to close the gap Sephora and Ulta have over Sally on making me feel like a recognized customer, not a cold lead
- 4. **Anchor the Pride block to product or cut it.** A collab shade, a donation tie-in, a curated Pride look — anything that makes it feel intentional rather than compliance. Charlotte Tilbury's recent sale emails aren't doing this any better, but they at least stay on-message
- 5. **Retire "Last Chance" in the subject.** Name a brand instead — specificity will outperform vague urgency at this point in the cadence
