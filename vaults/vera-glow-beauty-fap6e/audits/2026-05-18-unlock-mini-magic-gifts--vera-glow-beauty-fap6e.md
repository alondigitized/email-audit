# Unlock Mini Magic Gifts ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Two days after the "A Magical 15% Off Just For You" CT send — which, per my last audit, was all velvet and candlelight but made me do exclusion-clause mental gymnastics — they're back with a GWP. And honestly, this one lands cleaner. Free Pillow Talk mini lip plus mini Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray when I spend $95 is a mechanic I know by heart. I've done this math before. The question isn't whether I *want* the minis — I do — it's whether I'm ready to spend $95 specifically this week, with no expiry date pushing me off the fence.
- The structural problem is that this email has three separate briefs stapled together: the GWP hero, the Exagger-Eyes waitlist module, and an app feature grid. ILIA sent their "award winners" email with one clean idea and it felt like it respected my time. CT is asking me to process three different actions in one send, and the hero energy bleeds out by the time I hit that app download section.
- I'd click the GWP CTA if I was already planning a CT purchase this week. I would not forward this — every CT subscriber got the same email, so it's not a tip-off worth sharing. I'd mark it as acted-on and move on.

## What's working

- The hero is immediately readable — "Unlock Free Mini Gifts!" is large, clear, and tells me the offer before I scroll anywhere.
- Naming *exactly* which minis (Pillow Talk Matte Revolution + Airbrush Flawless Setting Spray) instead of a vague "free gift" is earned credibility — I know what I'm unlocking and I can decide if those are products I actually want.
- "GET YOUR GIFTS" is one of CT's better CTAs — specific verb, specific noun, not hiding behind "Discover the Magic."
- The model, color palette, and luxe warmth read as totally on-brand for CT's aspirational demographic, which aligns with where I sit as a buyer.
- The Exagger-Eyes module is bold enough that if I'm tracking that launch, I'd spot the waitlist button without hunting for it.

## What's weak

- The word "magical" appears in this email *three separate times* — the subject line, the "Magical savings" tile header, and inside the tile copy — two days after CT used "magical" in the 15% off subject line. The signature language is becoming a verbal tic.
- Three competing CTAs (GET YOUR GIFTS → JOIN THE WAITLIST → implied app download) fracture the email's intent. Pick one primary action per send.
- The "up to a magical 30% off beauty today!" callout is buried in a small feature grid halfway down. If there's a live sitewide sale, that should be the hero, not a footnote.
- No expiry date visible anywhere — the GWP has no "through Sunday" or "while supplies last" signal, which removes urgency and gives me permission to sleep on it.
- No CT Beauty Rewards mention. Sephora would never let a GWP email go without a Rouge tier callout. CT leaving loyalty benefits invisible is a miss — I'm doing points math constantly and I need to know this spend counts.

## Recommendations

- 1. **One CTA per email.** Split the Exagger-Eyes waitlist into a dedicated send timed closer to launch day. Let the GWP offer own this email entirely.
- 2. **Add a hard expiry to the GWP.** "Through Sunday, May 19" or even "while minis last" gives me a reason to click now instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
- 3. **Surface the 30% off if it's real.** If there's a live sale running alongside the GWP, lead with the bigger discount and position the minis as the bonus.
- 4. **Retire "magical" as a modifier for at least one send cycle.** Three uses in one email, two sends in a row — it's CT's most overworked word right now.
- 5. **Add one line of Beauty Rewards acknowledgment.** Even "This spend counts toward your Beauty Rewards tier" at the bottom of the GWP section treats me like a known customer instead of a cold prospect.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your two free minis are waiting (spend $95 to unlock)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pillow Talk lip + Airbrush Flawless, both added automatically to your bag.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two CT minis, no code. Just hit $95 at checkout.`
