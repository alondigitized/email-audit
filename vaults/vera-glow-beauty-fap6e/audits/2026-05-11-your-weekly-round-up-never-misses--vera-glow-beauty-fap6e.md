# Your weekly round-up never misses 💯
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-11**
## Executive summary

- OK so I do have e.l.f. on my radar — Power Grip Primer alone got them a permanent spot in my bag — but this email landed in my inbox feeling like it was drafted for a new subscriber who's never bought beauty online before. The "weekly highlight reel" concept is actually cute positioning but zero follow-through: nameless product thumbnails, a promo code banner with no visible offer value, and a loyalty section buried so deep at the bottom I almost missed it. There's no points math, no "launched today" signal, no indication of what's new vs. what's been sitting on the shelf for six weeks.
- The model is gorgeous and the skin is giving exactly the right glow reference, so the brand vision is there. But the execution reads like a content calendar checkbox, not a send that knows I already own half of what's in that grid. I'd open it for the welcome code, click once to see if the offer is worth anything, and close the tab. I would not forward this.

## What's working

- The hero model's skin tone and glow-finish look genuinely aspirational — right age demographic, not stock-photo energy
- "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK" creates a real editorial break and signals there's a curated weekly angle, not just homepage recycling
- Two distinct CTAs at hero (Best Sellers vs. New Arrivals) let me self-sort by shopping mode — that's smart
- Welcome offer code is right at the top where I can see it before I decide to read the rest
- Beauty Squad + Afterpay callouts in the footer show they know their customer is budget-stacking, which I respect

## What's weak

- Every single product thumbnail is anonymous — no name, no price, no shade — I cannot tell a concealer from a bronzer at that size
- The promo code banner says "your offer is waiting" but never says what the offer *is* — that's bait, and I notice
- No points-per-dollar or Beauty Squad tier mention anywhere in the body copy, which is the one loyalty math signal that would actually move me
- "Weekly round-up" subject gives me zero reason to open faster than next week's round-up — there's no urgency or launch hook
- The category nav accordion (NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE) at the bottom feels like a website sitemap dropped into email — adds no value here

## Recommendations

- 1. **Disclose the welcome offer value in the banner.** "15% off your first order — use code [X]" outperforms a mystery code every time. Let me do the math before I click.
- 2. **Name the products.** At minimum one line of product name under each thumbnail in "YOUR NEW OBSESSIONS." If I can't identify it, I'm not clicking it.
- 3. **Swap the hero CTA for a specific hero product.** "Shop Power Grip Primer" or whatever the #1 weekly seller is beats "Shop Best Sellers" — tell me what to buy.
- 4. **Add a "New This Week" header** that explicitly flags what's a new launch vs. a restocked favorite. Vera lives for drop day.
- 5. **Put Beauty Squad points math in the email body** — "Earn 1 point per $1, redeem for free product" takes one line and converts loyalty-aware shoppers.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `15% off your first order — plus the launches worth adding to cart right now`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. SKIN "Weekly Round-Up" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on Braze with standard MSO/VML compatibility shims. Two structural defects are present: a malformed `font-family` declaration and duplicate Google Fonts loading. The HTML truncation prevents full compliance verification, but the visible footer region is absent from the sample.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **All CTAs route through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`** — Braze click-tracking redirects. This is expected; however, UTM parameters are **not visible in the raw `href` attributes**. If Braze is not appending UTMs server-side at redirect time, downstream analytics will attribute all traffic as direct. Example CTA href: `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/KJ6eQewsKFWgbGfvkMdI8g~~…` — no `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` in the encoded payload visible in the source.
- **Logo link also uses the Braze redirect domain**, so even brand/nav clicks are tracked — intentional but worth confirming the destination resolves to `elfcosmetics.com` homepage.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Empty `font-family` first value** (`font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` on the outer wrapper `<div>`): the empty string is ignored by all clients, causing Verdana fallback instead of Jost. Should be `font-family:'Jost',Verdana,sans-serif`.
- **Duplicate Google Fonts load**:
  - `<head>`: `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Jost">` — loads weight 400 only, wrapped in MSO conditional (`<!--[if !mso]>`).
  - Inside HTML block body: `@import url('...family=Jost:ital,wght@0,100..900...')` — loads full variable range.
  - Two separate HTTP requests to Google Fonts per render; inconsistent weight availability between the two.
- **`tabindex="-1"` on logo `<a>` tag**: removes the logo link from keyboard tab order entirely. Keyboard-only users cannot navigate to it. Selector: `row-1 .image_block a[tabindex="-1"]`.
- **Empty `<title></title>`**: some clients surface this in preview or accessibility trees. Should match subject line or a sanitized version of it.
- **Nested duplicate `nl-container` / `row-1` table structure**: the outer `row-1` wrapper contains an HTML block whose inner content re-declares `class="nl-container"` and `class="row row-1"`. This is a template nesting error (likely an ESP drag-and-drop HTML block inserted inside a row). Does not break rendering but inflates DOM depth and complicates client-side CSS targeting.
- **`#MessageViewBody a { color:inherit; text-decoration:none }`**: Gmail webmail-scoped reset strips all visual link affordance from inline text links. Intentional for design but flagged for accessibility — linked text is indistinguishable from static text without hover state.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **No merge tokens present** in the visible HTML. Subject line ("Your weekly round-up never misses 💯") and preheader ("Peep our top picks for you!") are static strings — no first-name or behavioral personalization tokens.
- No unrendered/broken Liquid or Braze templating syntax (e.g., `{{first_name}}` fallback failure) visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

- **Footer not present in truncated HTML** — cannot verify physical mailing address or unsubscribe link. CAN-SPAM requires both. This must be confirmed against the full source.
- **Sending domain**: `makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com` — subdomain ESP send. DKIM, SPF, and DMARC alignment cannot be verified from HTML; recommend checking mail headers (`Authentication-Results`) on a received copy.
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not verifiable from HTML source alone. Gmail and Yahoo now require `List-Unsubscribe: <mailto:...>` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` at the SMTP level for bulk senders.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

- **UTM attribution is unconfirmed**: Braze redirect URLs encode destination + tracking data in a Base64-like payload. If the Braze campaign is not configured to append UTM parameters to outbound URLs, GA4/analytics will log sessions as `(direct) / (none)`. Verify in Braze campaign settings under "Link Aliasing" → UTM parameters.
- **Cross-device landing page**: email subject references "weekly round-up" implying a curated page; the logo CTA destination is unknown without following the redirect. Confirm destination matches email theme to avoid continuity drop-off.

---
