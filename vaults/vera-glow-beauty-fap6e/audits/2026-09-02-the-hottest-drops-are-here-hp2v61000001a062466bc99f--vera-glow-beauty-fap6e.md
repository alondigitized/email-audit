# The hottest drops are here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "50% off Select Beauty, Today Only" at the top made me pause for half a second — but then I scrolled and realized this is actually a different animal than the FIVE identical "50% off Select Beauty" sends I logged between 8/28 and 8/31. This one pivots hard into "JUST DROPPED" — Byredo, Dior, rhode, OUAI, Jung Saem Mool, Kayali, Touchland, amika — that's an actual new-launch rail, not another recycled sale banner. As a Rouge member who tracks launch cadence like it's my job, I respect that Sephora finally gave me something to look at besides the same discount code on repeat.
- That said, this email is LONG. Nine-plus product blocks stacked vertically with almost no differentiation in layout — it's a wall of "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags that starts to blur together by the time I hit Jung Saem Mool. Compare that to the ILIA "Overglaze" send I read — two shades, one clean hook, done in one scroll. Sephora's playing "everybody gets a shelf" instead of picking a hero and running with it.
- Verdict: I'd open it (the "Today Only" 50% off banner earns that), I'd skim past most of the middle, but I'd actually screenshot the BYREDO section to send to my friend who's obsessed with Alto Astral — that's a real forward-worthy moment buried in an otherwise bloated send.

## What's working

- The top banner leads with a concrete, time-boxed offer — "50% off Select Beauty... TODAY ONLY" is exactly the urgency math I'm always doing.
- BYREDO getting its own dedicated "HAS ARRIVED" section with the Alto Astral hero image is a genuine launch-day beat, not filler — that's the kind of prestige-brand drop that makes me open Sephora over Ulta.
- Multiple "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags are doing real exclusivity signaling — I clock those instantly as a cross-shopper deciding where to spend.
- The Charlotte Tilbury before/after skin tint tile at the bottom is a smart use of social-proof-adjacent visual, even tucked away that low.
- Rouge/Insider tier bar at the very bottom at least acknowledges the loyalty program exists, even if it's an afterthought.

## What's weak

- This is WAY too long for one send — nine product blocks with near-identical layout (image, name tag, one-liner, arrow CTA) makes my eye stop working by block five. Ulta would never let a single email run this deep without breaking rhythm.
- No prices anywhere except the Kayali and CT sections — for a "just dropped" email I want to know if I'm looking at a $28 lip oil or a $95 fragrance before I tap through.
- The "15% off skincare" and "Best of Skincare" tiles at the very bottom feel bolted on, like two separate email templates got smushed together — it dilutes the "JUST DROPPED" focus the whole email built up.
- Zero personalization — no "Marisol" name-drop like the recent Ulta send, no skin tone/type callout despite showing a shade-heavy Charlotte Tilbury tint. For a Rouge member with purchase history, that's a miss.
- I have to scroll past eight brands before I even get points math or tier language — my top priority as a Rouge/Diamond stacker is buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product rail from nine brands to three or four — lead with BYREDO and one or two others, and move the rest to a "see more new arrivals" link. Length is killing engagement here.
- 2. Add prices to every product tile — I'm not clicking blind on unknown price points, especially prestige brands like Byredo and Dior.
- 3. Move loyalty/points messaging up near the top banner, not buried below the fold — that's my actual decision driver as a Rouge member.
- 4. Merge or cut the bottom "15% off skincare" + "Best of Skincare" blocks — pick one skincare beat, not two competing CTAs after an already-long scroll.
- 5. Personalize the subject/preview and hero with name or category history, the way Ulta's "Marisol" send did — even a generic "New drops in your favorites" beats zero personalization.
