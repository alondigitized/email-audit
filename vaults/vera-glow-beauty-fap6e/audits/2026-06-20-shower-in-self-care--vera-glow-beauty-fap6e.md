# Shower in self-care 🚿
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- Nineteen. I'm at nineteen Lush emails this month and I'm keeping count because the Sanrio collab two days ago — email eighteen — genuinely earned its place in my inbox. This one? It's fine. "Stand-Up Self-Care" is a solid pun, the routine breakdown (Lather → Scrub → Soften → Freshen → Glow) is actually useful editorial content, and the warm pink-to-orange color palette looks cohesive on mobile. Lush knows how to make a visually clean send.
- But here's the thing: there's no reason to click *today*. No offer. No discount. No limited-edition drop. No "only 3 left." Just "Discover More," which is the CTA equivalent of a shoulder shrug. After the Sanrio launch two days ago set a high bar with a real collab story, a routine-guide email with zero urgency lands flat. It's a content piece masquerading as a marketing email.
- I'd mark it as read and maybe come back if I was actively in the market for a shower scrub. I wouldn't forward it — there's nothing to say. "Hey, look at this... shower routine?" Charlotte Tilbury sent me a full lip launch this week with product close-ups and a real reason to shop. This doesn't compete on that energy.

## What's working

- **"Stand-Up Self-Care" hero copy is genuinely clever** — the shower pun lands, it's ownable, and it made me pause for half a second.
- **The routine architecture (Lather → Scrub → Soften → Freshen → Glow) is the right editorial frame** — it actually teaches me something and organizes the product grid in a way that feels curated, not dumped.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — eye goes hero → section headers → product cards without fighting for attention. No junk at the top.
- **Warm pink-coral-orange palette reads cohesive** — models look young and feel demographically close to me, the styling is fun without being try-hard.
- **No render bugs** — the email looks clean, images load, nothing overlaps.

## What's weak

- **Zero offer — this is the core miss.** No percentage off, no GWP, no member exclusive. After nineteen emails from this brand, "Discover More" doesn't justify an open, let alone a click.
- **"Discover More" CTA is meaningless** — that button could be on literally any email from any brand. ILIA ran "This lip color lasts. This gift won't." the same week — specific, urgent, named product. This is the opposite.
- **No loyalty hook** — I'm a Lush rewards member and there's nothing here for me specifically. Sephora would have threaded Rouge pricing or a points reminder into this template without thinking twice.
- **No urgency signal anywhere** — the Glossier sale email I just tracked had countdown energy across every section. This feels like a shelf display, not a reason to shop right now.
- **Routine structure is underlinked** — "Scrubee" and "Magic Crystals" are tiny cards with minimal detail. If you're going to do an editorial routine, commit: show a price, show a key ingredient, give me one reason each product is the one to buy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one concrete reason to shop now** — even a "free shipping this weekend" or "Rewards members earn 3x points today" transforms this from content to commerce. The routine format is actually great scaffolding for a members-only event.
- 2. **Kill "Discover More" and write a product-specific CTA** — "Shop the Shower Routine," "Get Rose Jam," or even "Build My Routine" are all better. Anything with a noun.
- 3. **Anchor to the Sanrio moment** — you just sent a massive collab email two days ago. At least one of these routine products should connect to that energy: "Can't stop thinking about the Rose Jam from the Sanrio drop? Here's how it fits your full shower routine."
- 4. **Price or savings signal on at least the hero product** — one visible price point or member discount anchors the editorial as shoppable, not just inspirational.
- 5. **Tighten the grid** — five sections × two products is ten SKUs. Cut to three hero products total and give each room to breathe with a short ingredient or scent callout.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your shower routine, upgraded 🚿`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stand-up self-care: Rose Jam + 4 more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Lather → Scrub → Soften → Glow routine, built for you`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards members: earn 3x points on your full shower haul`
