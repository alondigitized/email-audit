# We’ve got your routine right this way.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a palate cleanser — no DRUNKVIP20 in sight for once. Instead of the fifth "secret sale" rerun, Drunk Elephant's pushing a skincare-quiz tool ("Build Your Routine") plus a genuine $100+ deluxe-sample gift-with-purchase. I actually like the concept — it's the kind of personalization play I wish more of these brands attempted instead of just slapping my name into a subject line like Ulta does.
- But execution-wise it's flat. There's no real price off, no loyalty math for me to do (and Drunk Elephant doesn't run a points program the way Sephora Beauty Insider or Ulta Diamond does, so I'm not accruing anything here regardless). And that fourth product tile in the routine row is just... an empty outline. No product rendered. On a brand that prices itself as prestige-clean-girl-science, a broken image is a bad look.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity for the quiz — I'm the type who'll always take a "build your routine" tool for a spin. Would I forward it? No. This isn't gift-worthy or urgent enough to share. Compare to ILIA's VIP sale (8/10 on my scorecard) — 20% off sitewide plus stacked free gifts is an instant click; this is a "maybe later, if I remember" open.

## What's working

- The "Skincare for You (and Only You)" framing is a nice break from the discount-code treadmill this brand's been running all week.
- The routine visual (Cleanser → Serum → Cream → Finish) is genuinely useful — I can see the actual step order, which nobody else in my inbox bothers to show.
- The $100+ GWP is a real, specific offer with clear deluxe-sample callouts (Protini, Beste No. 9, B-Hydra, T.L.C. Framboos, Ceramighty) — not vague "free gift" copy.
- Dates are spelled out precisely (9/12–9/21), so I know exactly how long I have.

## What's weak

- That fourth product tile is flat-out broken — just a blank outline where "Finish" should be. If I'm expected to buy a 4-step routine, show me all 4 steps.
- Zero loyalty/tier acknowledgment — no VIB/Rouge-style nod, nothing recognizing me as a repeat Drunk Elephant buyer, which stings a little after Ulta at least tries with "Marisol, ends today" personalization.
- No percentage or dollar discount anywhere — just a spend threshold for samples. After a week of 20%-off DRUNKVIP20 spam, pivoting to "spend $100 for free samples" with zero markdown feels like a step back for someone doing points-and-percent math like me.
- Top banner ("Kamo Triple-Peptide Cover Drops is making headlines") and the body content don't connect — it's advertising a totally different product than what's featured below, which muddies the hierarchy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken fourth product image immediately — a blank box on a "clean skincare" brand reads like a QA failure, not confidence.
- 2. Pick one hero: either the quiz tool or the GWP threshold, not both fighting for top billing — right now my eye doesn't know where to land first.
- 3. Give tier/repeat-customer some acknowledgment, even just "as one of our regulars" — Ulta's doing this better with name-personalized subject lines.
- 4. Consider stacking an actual percentage discount with the GWP threshold — after a week of 20% codes, silence on price feels like a bait-and-switch, even if it isn't one.
- 5. Resolve the banner/body mismatch — either feature Cover Drops in the body or drop it from the top banner.
