# Singles Day steals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a Drunk Elephant send that is a real gift-with-purchase and not the Subscribe & Save ladder. That's a relief after 10/24 "20% off?", 10/25 "Up to 20% off" and 10/26 "15% off is leaving us...". The hero says spend $100 and get a four-piece sample set, then spend $150 and get a puffer tote. I can do that math in two seconds. The catch is that Singles Day isn't a Beauty Insider moment. It's a GWP with no discount, and I'd only act on it if I already needed a restock.
- The layout is clean, but the tote section is wasteful. A big silver-and-lilac bag fills the middle of the email, and it's a bag I'd get only after spending $150. Then the Atelier banner shows a holiday collection with no price or set names. The "you're gonna like these guys" grid is the only part with actual products. I'd forward this to nobody, but I'd open it and probably click through to the $100 tier. A C-Firma and Protini sample set is real value, though I'd want to see exactly what's in it.
- Compared with Sephora's "10% off our most-searched beauty" send, where the subject and body didn't match, this one at least delivers on its subject. "Singles Day steals" is a bit of a stretch for a GWP, though. Nothing here is a steal.

## What's working

- The "HAPPY SINGLES DAY!" hero states the mechanic at the top: spend $100, get a four-piece sample gift.
- The sample products are shown right in the hero, so I can see what I'm getting.
- There are two clear spend tiers, $100 and $150. That's easy for me to math out against my cart.
- The product grid has star ratings and "578 PEOPLE ARE CHECKING THIS OUT" social-proof counts. This is better than Ulta's coupon-ladder emails, which have no social proof.
- The fine print at the bottom gives exact dates and times, 11/5 to 11/12, so the deadline feels credible.

## What's weak

- Singles Day with no discount is weak. The "steals" in the subject promise savings, but the email offers only gifts.
- The fine print names C-Firma and Protini 5mL, Amino Rain and D-Bronzi packettes. The hero doesn't make clear which are the four pieces.
- The Atelier holiday collection section has no prices, no set names and no CTA beyond a small "SHOP NOW" link. It feels tacked on.
- The tote takes up a huge block of the email, and it's tied to a $150 spend. It's a bag, not skincare, so it doesn't do much for me.
- There's no Rouge or Diamond-style loyalty or member benefit anywhere. Sephora and Ulta would both put points math or a tier perk up front.
- The product grid has no prices, so I can't tell if I'm reaching $100 with a single moisturizer.
- The "SHOP NOW" buttons in the hero and tote are small, outlined and low-contrast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices to the product grid and a "$X away from your gift" line or a bundle that hits $100. This would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Show the four-piece sample set as a labeled list in the hero, with names and sizes.
- 3. Cut the tote block by half, or put it below the Atelier section, and make the $150 tier a smaller banner.
- 4. Give the Atelier collection real product names, prices and a stronger CTA.
- 5. Make the CTA buttons solid and larger for thumb taps.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a legacy XHTML table layout with several head-level CSS and accessibility defects. The QA run could not verify the unsubscribe headers, authentication results, or click destinations, because the AgentMail relay stripped the headers and 17 tracking links were skipped. The pass rate is 57% (0 issues, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 17 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so none were checked for status codes, redirect chains or final landing URLs.
- **Personal data in a tracking URL:** `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the recipient's email address in plain text in the query string. It can leak through logs, referrers and forwarded mail. This is the ESP's design, but it is a real exposure.
- **Open pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdaystart` is a standard tracking pixel and not a defect. It has no `alt` attribute, so it is flagged below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** both `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` to both, since they are tracking pixels.
- **Non-HTTPS images:** three social icons load over `http://`: Facebook (`cc02d07d-…png`), Instagram (`9b452915-…png`) and TikTok (`29f975e1-…png`), all from `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/`. Clients that block or rewrite mixed content may drop them. Switch them to `https://`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` overrides the earlier `100%` on `body`, so users cannot scale text.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule in the `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so these are ignored. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` do the same job.
- **Third-party font host:** the `BrownBold`, `BrownLight` and `BrownThin` fonts depend on `db.onlinewebfonts.com`. `BrownRegular` is hot-linked from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`. That versioned path breaks whenever the site redeploys. Fallback stacks were not visible in the truncated source.
- **HTML comments in CSS:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] … -->` sits inside the `@media` block. It can invalidate the rules that follow in some parsers.
- **Mobile sizing:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` / `12px` and `max-height`, which will clip text if the font size changes.
- **Not verifiable:** the source was truncated, so I could not check the `lang` attribute, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible source or the QA data. The recipient address is merged into the `e=` parameter (see section 2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** the QA note says the relay may not capture it. This is unconfirmed and not a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, and the tracking domains differ, which is normal for an ESP.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** both fall outside the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** none appear in the visible URLs. Campaign attribution uses vendor-specific `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_singlesdaystart` and `c=us_de_broad…`. If the landing pages are expected to carry `utm_*`, that cannot be confirmed.
- **Landing page alignment:** it cannot be assessed, because every probe was skipped and the final URLs are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA against the original message, or with raw headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Resolve the 17 tracking redirects through a follow-redirects probe to capture the final landing URLs, status codes and any `utm_*` parameters.
3. Change the three social icon URLs from `http://` to `https://`.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` override.
6. Move the `@import` rules above `body{…}` or delete them. Self-host the Brown fonts and add web-safe fallbacks.
7. Remove the `<!-- -->` comments inside the `@media` block.
8. Report to the ESP that the recipient email address appears in the `e=` query parameter.
