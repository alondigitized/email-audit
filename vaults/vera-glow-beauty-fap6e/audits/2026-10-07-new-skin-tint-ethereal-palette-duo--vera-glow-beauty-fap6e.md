# NEW Skin Tint + Ethereal Palette Duo
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is pretty, but I have no idea what it costs. The hero has a skin tint bottle next to a blush palette over a moody ocean shot, and "The Set With a Purpose" is nice type. It's a holiday bundle with zero price, zero savings and zero points math. I do the math, so that stung. Sephora would have put "Rouge: 2X points" on this. Ulta would have put a big orange "20% off" on it.
- It's also the fifth ILIA email I've logged since 9/30, after "Need a new coat?" on 10/5 and "It's Back" on 10/2. This one at least has an actual launch and a product-forward hero, which beats the 9/30 poppies artist profile where I scrolled twice to find a product. But half the email is a reforestation story. That's a lovely story. It isn't a reason to buy a $60-plus set this week.
- Would I click? Probably yes, out of curiosity about the bundle price and shade options. I wouldn't forward it. Nothing here makes it a "you need to see this" send. I'd skim it and move on.

## What's working

- The hero shows both products right away. I know it's a skin tint and a palette in about two seconds, which is clearer than Glossier's no-product holiday guide.
- The "Shop the Set" button is red, full-width and repeated twice. It's easy to find and easy to tap on my phone.
- The two product blocks are clean: Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 and the Ethereal Baked Face Palette. The bullets are useful: "1 bronzer, 1 highlighter, 2 shades of blush" and "lasts up to eight hours."
- The shade-collection flat-lay of palettes is the best image in the email. It makes me want to choose a colorway.
- The trust row at the bottom (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Pact recycling, shade match) is on-brand and reassuring.

## What's weak

- There's no price, no bundle savings and no deadline anywhere. "Limited edition" is the only urgency, and I can't evaluate a set I can't price.
- Nothing speaks to me as a points person. "Join ILIA VIP" is buried in the footer, with no perks or earn rate. Sephora Insider and Ulta Rewards put the math up front.
- The reforestation block is a full-screen-length tangent. The "*Up to $100,000" footnote sits tiny on the palm leaf and is hard to read, so it feels like fine print.
- "Choose your shade of" doesn't show me any shades. A skin tint lives or dies on shade range, and I get three bottles in different browns and no swatches.
- No people. The only skin in the email is one hand holding a bottle. I can't see how the tint looks on a face like mine.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer and the price in the hero. Something like "Set price + free shipping" or "Save $X vs. buying separately" would do it. It's the single biggest lift. Then add a line for VIP members, like "VIP: earn 2X."
- 2. Cut the reforestation block down to one line near the top, and put a shade picker or swatch strip where it was.
- 3. Add one or two faces wearing the tint, plus one rating or review snippet ("4.7★ from X reviews").
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as "Holiday set, while it lasts" or an end date.
- 5. Make the second CTA specific: "Pick your tint shade" is stronger than a second "Shop the Set."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited edition, 3 palette collections, free shipping over $50`
