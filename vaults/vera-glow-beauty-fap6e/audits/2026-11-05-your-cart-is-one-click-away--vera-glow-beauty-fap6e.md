# Your cart is one click away!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth fake-cart email from Skechers in about two weeks, and I still haven't put anything in a Skechers cart. The 11/4 send had the same subject, and before that came "Your cart expires soon!" on 11/2 and 11/3. "Let's make it official" is a wedding line on a shoe email. The hero is a black leather Uno with a blue Day of the Dead skull painted on it. That is a very specific item for a cart I never built, so it reads as fake. It's the right season for it, but it's wasted on a nudge about a cart I don't have.
- The one real thing here is the Skechers Plus 20% off, regular-priced styles, valid 11/5 to 11/10. It's buried in a navy box below six John Deere boots and slip-ins, and it's gated behind a Plus login I never signed up for. Ulta would have put that offer in the subject line. Sephora would have told me my Rouge tier math up top. This email gives me nothing to calculate, because the rate is flat and I don't know if I qualify.
- I'd mark it read. I wouldn't click and I wouldn't forward it. If the 20% were the headline instead of a fake cart, I might look.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF block is big, high-contrast and easy to read. The "valid thru 11/10" line gives it a real deadline.
- The hero shoe is clean on white, and the Alma De Guerrero skull sneaker is a striking product shot.
- The "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" button is clear and sits right under the hero.
- The footer gives me Afterpay and Klarna, curbside pickup and app downloads. Those are useful trust cues.

## What's weak

- The cart claim is false for me, and it's the fifth or sixth time. The 11/4 "Your cart is one click away!" scored 4/10 for this exact reason.
- The best offer is third in the visual order. It sits below a hero and a six-shoe grid that's mostly men's John Deere work boots, which don't fit a 29-year-old beauty shopper.
- The 20% is for Skechers Plus members only, and the fine print says I must be logged in. I'm not a member, so "take 20% off" feels exclusionary. It also excludes sale styles, prior purchases and accessories.
- The "SHOP ALL BEST SELLERS" button is a heavy black bar. It competes with the actual offer and sends me to the wrong place.
- Product captions are tiny and I can't read them without zooming. There are no prices and no reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending cart-abandonment emails to people who never carted. Segment on real cart events, and send everyone else a straight promo.
- 2. Move the 20% Plus offer to the hero and subject line, and add one line on how to join. The email would still be mostly the same.
- 3. Cut the grid to three women's styles and show prices. Make the CTA say "Shop 20% off women's sneakers."
- 4. Replace "Let's make it official" with copy that matches the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off Skechers through 11/10 (Plus members)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 days: 20% off regular-priced sneakers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join Skechers Plus free and the discount applies at checkout. Ends 11/10.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Women's slip-ins and D'Lites, all 20% off until Monday.`
