# What are you being for Halloween?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one's cute. It's a Halloween costume-by-makeup guide, and it's the first Glossier send in a week that gives me a reason to scroll. Compare it to the 10/2 "Something festive's right around the corner…" teaser, where I had to guess what the launch was. This one just shows me three looks and the products for each. It's editorial, not promotional. There's no offer, no code, no Glossier Rewards mention and no deadline, so I have no points math to do.
- The "shop the look" format is good, but the email is low-stakes. Sephora would have stacked a Beauty Insider bonus or a bundle on this, and Ulta would have hung a "20% off one item" coupon on every costume. Glossier just says "Shop now" ten times. I'd click through to see Cloud Paint in Haze, and maybe screenshot Elizabeth Bennet for a friend. I wouldn't forward it, and I wouldn't buy that day without a reason.

## What's working

- The three-costume structure (Elizabeth Bennet, Porcelain Doll, Goddess Athena) is a clear, scannable concept.
- The "THE PRODUCTS" rows, with a three-up grid, a shade name and a one-line descriptor for each, tell me what I'd be buying. "Cloud Paint in Haze: a deep fuchsia" is the kind of detail I want.
- The Porcelain Doll model shows a deeper skin tone wearing Cloud Paint Plush Blush in Burst. That's real shade inclusivity, and I noticed it.
- The Boy Brow close-up shows the product on skin, which makes it feel wearable.
- The type is clean and readable on a phone, and nothing is broken or overlapping.

## What's weak

- There's no offer, no free shipping, no Rewards tie-in and no deadline, so there's no reason to buy today. Ulta and Sephora would never send an event email this bare.
- "Shop now" appears 10 times, and every button is identical. Nothing says "Shop the Elizabeth Bennet look" or "Add all 3."
- The hero is mostly a crop of a mouth with a giant open grin. It's a bit startling, and the headline "Halloween's creeping up" sits in white space and doesn't connect to it.
- Halloween is under four weeks away, but there's no shipping-by date and no bundle for a full look. That makes it feel less urgent than Drunk Elephant's "$90 / $143 value" kit, which at least showed me value.
- The looks are all soft and natural. For a costume email, I wanted one bolder look.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a "Get the whole look" bundle button for each costume with a visible price or bundle savings. That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Put a Glossier Rewards or free-shipping line under the hero, and make the buttons specific.
- 3. Swap the hero crop for a full-face costume shot, and add one dramatic look.
- 4. Add a "order by 10/24 for Halloween" cue.
