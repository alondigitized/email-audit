# Time to tie up 20% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Fifteen. That's where we are. And I say that not as a complaint preamble but as actual context for how I'm processing everything about this email, including the fact that "Time to tie up 20% off" is genuinely one of the better subject lines in this entire run. The "tie up" framing — like finishing a look, wrapping a bow — is the kind of considered copywriting I expect from ILIA, and it's been largely absent since the Friends & Family campaign devolved into daily "20% off sitewide" blasts around email nine or ten. The hero is clean: navy-on-white, the offer is front and center, "FINAL HOURS TO SAVE →" is a real button with real copy. The "inspired by you" product grid with the arm swatches is the strongest creative element in the email — diverse skin tones, specific product names (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Limitless Lash), and it actually feels like someone thought about me rather than just blasting the entire subscriber list.
- But here's the thing: "ENDS TOMORROW" has appeared in this campaign so many times that it now reads as background noise. I could not tell you whether this is the third or fifth time ILIA has told me tomorrow is the last day. Drunk Elephant pulled off something similar with "We extended the sale…but this is it!" — at least they acknowledged the extension with self-awareness. ILIA just keeps saying tomorrow with a completely straight face, and at this point the urgency is functionally broken for anyone who's been paying attention. The Cart Deco gift mechanic — "choose a free mini with every order" — was interesting the first time I saw it; now I want to know which minis without having to click.
- Would I click? If I had an abandoned cart, probably yes, just to close the loop. Would I forward to a friend? No — I would have sent them the email from day one of the sale when the launches were fresh and the offer felt like news.

## What's working

- The "inspired by you" product grid is the creative standout — arm swatches showing at least four different skin tones make the Skin Tint recommendation feel targeted rather than generic
- "FINAL HOURS TO SAVE →" is a real verb-forward CTA button, not "Discover" or "Shop Now" — this is above average for ILIA's campaign CTAs
- Hero hierarchy is bulletproof: ILIA logo → sale label → offer → deadline → button, no ambiguity on where the eye lands first
- The "Cart Deco" framing for the GWP section is playful and brand-consistent — better than Charlotte Tilbury's clinical "add to bag for your gift" copy
- Body copy "Almost ready to frame and finish" picks up the subject line's "tie up" metaphor and actually creates a coherent narrative moment

## What's weak

- "ENDS TOMORROW" has been deployed so many times across this campaign that I've stopped believing it — the urgency mechanism is burned out
- The VIP 25% upgrade offer is buried in small body copy above the CTA; that's a better deal than the primary offer and it's invisible to anyone who skims
- "SHOP TO CLAIM →" for the Cart Deco section tells me nothing about what I'm claiming — e.l.f. links directly to the gift-selector page; this drops me somewhere unspecified
- No prices visible in the "inspired by you" product grid — showing the original vs. sale price on even one product would make the 20% math immediate and tactile
- Email fifteen. I don't have anything new to say. The cadence itself is the bug.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the VIP angle in the subject** — "25% off if you join VIP (it's free)" is actually the better offer buried in body text. Make it the hero or at least the subject.
- 2. **Give the Cart Deco section a peek** — show two or three mini product images so I know I want them before I click; the blush compact image is decorative, not informative.
- 3. **Add one price reveal** — show "$38 → $30.40" on even one hero product; the 20% math done for me converts better than "20% off" abstract.
- 4. **Retire "ENDS TOMORROW"** — swap to a specific time ("ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT") or acknowledge the campaign arc the way Sally Beauty's "Okay, Last Chance 😉" did — the wink of self-awareness goes a long way at campaign close.
- 5. **One fewer email** — seriously.
- **Subject Alt A:** `VIP upgrade: 25% off (free to join) — closes tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Skin Tint picks have 12 hours left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Super Serum, Limitless Lash, Multi-Stick — 20% off until midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the 20% — join VIP free and get 25% on everything. Ends tonight.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>`**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o="..." xmlns:v="...">
```
No `lang="en"` (or locale equivalent). Screen readers cannot infer language; some spam filters penalize this. Fix: `<html lang="en" xmlns=...>`.

**Issue — `@import` font declarations will be stripped by Gmail**
```css
@import url(https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css);
@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```
Gmail (web) strips `@import` rules entirely. Montserrat and the custom Klaviyo font will silently fall back to the declared stack (`"Helvetica", sans-serif`) for Gmail users — which is the majority of consumer inboxes. This is functionally acceptable only if the fallback stack is intentional and visually tested. No `<link>` tag alternative is present.

**Issue — Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title> </title>
```
A single space is the standard Klaviyo stub, but some accessibility auditing tools flag it. Low severity.

**Cannot assess — Image alt text** — Body images are in the truncated portion. Confirm all `<img>` tags carry non-empty `alt` attributes, especially for CTA buttons rendered as images.

---

No unresolved `{{ }}` or `{%  %}` tokens visible in the header/preheader region. Full body is truncated — verify no broken merge tags exist in product blocks or discount code fields before send.
