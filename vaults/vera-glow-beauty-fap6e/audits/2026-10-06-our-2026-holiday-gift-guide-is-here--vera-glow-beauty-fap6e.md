# Our 2026 Holiday Gift Guide is here!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Pretty, but there's no math in it, and I do math. This is a holiday gift guide with two products, no prices, no offer, no deadline and no Rewards mention. The photography is lovely: pink tissue, ribbon, the mosaic gift boxes. But I opened it at the "first holiday drop" and left knowing nothing I couldn't get from the product page. The 10/5 Halloween makeup guide at least gave me something to do. This one gives me two boxes to look at.
- The "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe" is also the same kit Glossier pushed as a free $50+ GWP on 9/28 ("Last call! Free Mini Glossier You Wardrobe"). Now it's a gift to buy, with no hint of whether the free-gift deal is still live. That's confusing for someone who tracks every GWP. Would I click? Maybe once, to see the price of the Duo. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the real holiday offers.

## What's working

- The hero image is gorgeous. Ribbon, tissue paper and the bottles in red, lilac and teal read as holiday without being cheesy.
- The "GIFT 01 / GIFT 02" labels make it easy to scan, and the zigzag image/text alternation keeps the scroll moving.
- The headline "The 2026 Holiday Gift Guide" is clear. I know what this email is in about a second.
- The cookie photo and the "Keep your eyes peeled..." tease make me feel more holiday drops are coming. That's good for cadence.
- The packaging shots show the Duo's gold mosaic box clearly, which makes it feel giftable.

## What's weak

- No prices, no offer, no free shipping threshold and no Rewards mention. Sephora's holiday gift sets always lead with value, like "$X value for $Y". This one doesn't.
- The Duo copy is cut off on the right edge: "signatur… T…". I can see the text running off the screen, and it looks sloppy.
- The CTAs are all a generic "Shop now" in small grey boxes. That's three identical buttons, and none says which product it opens.
- The Mini Wardrobe overlaps the 9/28 free-gift campaign. I can't tell if I should buy it or wait for another freebie.
- The last button says "Sign up". I'm already on this list, same as the 10/2 teaser. It's asking me for something I've already done.
- Only two products for a "gift guide" is thin. Ulta's holiday guides come with price tiers and a countdown, and Sephora has the Beauty Insider event dates.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put prices and the value on every product, like "Glossier You Duo, $XX" or "$YY value". Without them I can't compare to Sephora's holiday sets.
- 2. Add a clear offer or Rewards angle: "Rewards members earn double points on gift sets through 10/XX." Even a free-shipping threshold would help.
- 3. Fix the right-edge text clipping in the Duo block.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific ("Shop the Duo", "Shop the Wardrobe") and style them as real buttons.
- 5. Replace "Sign up" with "Get early access" or "Remind me", since I'm already a subscriber.
- 6. Add more gifts with price bands, such as under $25 and under $50, so it works as a real guide.

- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 3 (footer/utility/social, deprioritized by design). The data reports no broken or erroring links among the probed set.
