# Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Glossier again, and I'm doing the math before I scroll. A free Glossier You Candle with a $100+ order is a real hook, and the "$45 value" tag makes it easy to price out. But it's the same free-gift-at-$100 mechanic as the 11/26 "LAST CHANCE: free Crème de You" and 11/25 "Good things come in pairs." Only the freebie changed. That Crème de You offer was "last chance" less than a day ago, so the urgency doesn't feel credible.
- The hero reads "25% off everything or 30% off your $100+ order." The candle is only the lead, and I have to parse two discounts plus a gift threshold. The red candle photo is gorgeous and the layout is clean, but the "*" is doing a lot of work, and the terms are a tiny underlined link at the very bottom.
- Would I click? Yes, because I'm already shopping Black Friday and 30% off plus a $45 candle clears my threshold easily. Would I forward it? No. It's a reminder, not a discovery.

## What's working

- The candle hero is instantly clear, and the red glass pops against the black-and-white checkerboard.
- "A $45 VALUE!" lets me do the points and value math in one second. Sephora's gift-with-purchase emails often skip a dollar value.
- The reuse angle ("store makeup, pencils or whatever") makes the freebie feel useful rather than filler.
- The product grid (Glossier You, Cloud Paint, Boy Brow) has big blue SALE tags and a "Shop now" button under every item. The tap targets are generous.
- The model casting is diverse, with a close-up eye and brow and a blush shot, and it feels peer-aged.

## What's weak

- The hero stacks "25% off everything or 30% off $100+" with a gift on top. That's three offers, the same overload I called out on 11/25.
- There's no end date anywhere. After "LAST CHANCE" yesterday, a bare "Black Friday Sale" has no deadline.
- The hero "Shop now" and the second "Get your gift" buttons go to unspecified destinations, and I can't tell whether the gift auto-adds.
- The body paragraph and button on the second block are cut off at the right edge ("Once... the glass vess"). That's a visible render bug.
- There's no loyalty angle and no personalization at all. I'm treated like a stranger, and this is my eighth Glossier email in about a week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer: the free candle at $100. Move the 25%/30% stuff to a single sub-line, and put the end date right in the hero.
- 2. Fix the clipped text in the second block, and say outright that the gift auto-adds at checkout.
- 3. Put the terms in plain text under the hero instead of a tiny footer link.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Bundle the free-gift swap into one send rather than a new email per freebie.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free $45 candle with $100+, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free Glossier You Candle is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get the candle free, plus 30% off. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The $45 Glossier You Candle is on us. Here's how to claim it.`

## Technical Audit: Glossier, "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with click tracking through `ctrk.klclick.com` and images hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). Confirmed issues are limited to missing image alt text and one malformed element after `<body>`. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No issues found with link health.** 12 material links were probed and none were reported broken. 14 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M8D891K3TX5E61ZXW2R87VC9` has no alt attribute. See Section 3.
- The HTML was truncated, so I could not inspect individual CTA `href` values.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 7 images (confirmed by QA):**
  - `frame_4053.jpg`
  - `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1_`
  - `group_3986_16.jpg`
  - `group_3987_17.jpg`
  - `group_3986_17.jpg`
  - `group_3987_18.jpg`
  - the tracking pixel `01M8D891K3TX5E61ZXW2R87VC9`

  Six are on `stripocdn.email` and one is the Klaviyo pixel. If the six content images are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If they carry content, they need descriptive alt text. The pixel should have `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:block"` if those are not already set. A missing alt attribute lets screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Invalid markup immediately after `<body>`:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klcl…">`. `rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This looks like a stray stylesheet link or tracking artifact, and it may trigger a fetch or be stripped by some clients. The truncation hides what it points to.
- **Doctype and charset mismatch:** the document declares XHTML 1.0 Transitional but uses the HTML5 form `<meta charset="utf-8">`, unclosed. This is common in Stripo output and harmless in practice.
- **`<title>` does not match the subject:** `<title>` is "Free Candle? You've got it" and the subject is "Your New Gift: Glossier You Candle". This is low severity, but the title is used by some web-view and archive renderings.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `{% %}`, `*|…|*`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion is unchecked.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unconfirmed, probably a capture artifact:** QA did not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers, and did not find `Authentication-Results`. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these, so none of the three can be treated as a sender defect. Check the raw original message for them.
- The sender domain `eml.glossier.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with proper SPF/DKIM alignment, but this is not verified.
- The QA tool says it always includes an unsubscribe link in its probing. I saw no unsubscribe link failure, but the footer and physical address are outside the truncated source, so I cannot confirm them.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The source is truncated before the CTA hrefs. Klaviyo click-wrapping (`ctrk.klclick.com`) hides final destination URLs, so UTM presence and landing page alignment would need redirect-chain resolution. The QA output does not report any.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` attributes to the 6 Stripo-hosted images. Use `alt=""` for decorative ones and descriptive text for content. Add `alt=""` to the Klaviyo pixel.
2. Remove or fix the `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` element after `<body>`.
