- The crumpled pink paper with the red tape is on-brand and looks like unwrapping. It's clean and uncluttered.
- The headline is big and readable on a phone, and the hierarchy is easy to follow: headline, one line, one button.
- The footer is tidy, with an easy unsubscribe link and the Glossier smiley and hand doodles as a branded sign-off.

## What's weak

- There's no product, date, or offer, so I have no reason to care yet. Sephora announces Beauty Insider and Rouge dates and Ulta announces 21 Days, and both tell me when to show up. This tells me nothing.
- The "Sign up" button doesn't say what I'm signing up for. It also looks like a plain grey box, not a real CTA.
- I'm a current subscriber, so "Be the first to know" reads like the email thinks I'm a stranger. I should get early-access or loyalty framing, not a signup ask.
- Drunk Elephant's shade-clock email and e.l.f.'s Halo Glow body launch both showed the actual product. Glossier showed a piece of paper.
- After three GWP emails in a week, a fourth send with no offer feels like cadence filler.

- 2. Make the CTA a real button with a verb and a noun, and make it look like a button.
- 3. Add loyalty or early-access language for people already on the list. That's the Rouge-style perk I'd respond to.
- 4. Skip the teaser if there's no date. Fold it into the next real launch email so my inbox doesn't get a fifth Glossier send.
