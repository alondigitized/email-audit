# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I already flagged this exact send this morning at a 5/10 — "50% off select beauty" as the header, "You have 0 points" bug front and center — and now that I'm looking at the full scroll, my opinion isn't moving much. The 50% OFF hero still pulls my eye first, I'll give it that, but everything under it just keeps confirming this was a rushed send: three weeks of "Today Only" / "Deals Drop Daily" callouts stacked on top of each other with tiny product thumbnails I can't actually read prices on past week 1, then a random Afterpay 10% detour, then an Olive Young K-beauty cross-promo shoved in with zero connection to the 50% off message, then finally a Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom that highlights "INSIDER" — the lowest tier — when I'm Rouge.
- That tier bar is the real tell. Sephora knows I'm Rouge. They've sent me Rouge-specific event access before. Putting a generic INSIDER-highlighted tier strip at the bottom of a mass blast tells me this wasn't segmented for me at all — it's the same email going to a first-year Beauty Insider and a five-year Rouge member. Compare that to Ulta's "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" send from 7/26 — clunky and coupon-code-heavy, sure, but at least it used my name and felt like it knew who I was. This Sephora send has more content but less personalization.
- Verdict: I'd scroll past this one. The 0-points bug alone kills trust, and by the time I hit "Deals Drop Daily," three near-identical purple modules in a row, I'm not shopping — I'm just scrolling to see how long it goes. Not forwarding, not clicking. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The 50% OFF hero headline still does its job — pink gradient, big type, stops the thumb.
- "Deals Drop Daily" as a mechanic is smart in theory — gives me a reason to check back all week instead of just today.
- Week 1's product row is legible — Clinique lipstick, Farmacy-looking jar, and eyeliner with clear strike-through pricing ($25→$12.50 on the Clinique).
- The Afterpay 10% callout is a genuine incremental offer, not just filler — stacks with other App Fest promos per the badge.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" is still sitting right under the header — for a Rouge member with real spend history, that's either a broken feed or an insult, and I already called this out this morning.
- The tier bar at the bottom highlights INSIDER, not ROUGE — as a Rouge member this reads as "we don't actually know your tier," which is worse than not showing a tier bar at all.
- Week 2 and Week 3 product rows have zero visible pricing or product names — I have no idea what I'm looking at or whether it's relevant to me, just thumbnails.
- The Olive Young K-beauty block is a total non-sequitur dropped into a 50%-off promo email — feels like two campaigns got merged into one send.
- Three "Today Only" / weekly-deal modules in a row is visual fatigue — by week 3 I've stopped reading, I'm just scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points bug before anything else ships — a wrong point balance is a data-trust problem, not a copy problem, and it's now shown up in two of my logged Sephora sends today.
- 2. Swap the tier bar to actually highlight ROUGE for Rouge members — if the personalization engine can't do that reliably, pull the tier bar entirely rather than show the wrong one.
- 3. Cut Week 2 and Week 3 down to one hero item each with visible pricing, or link out to a dedicated "this week's deals" page instead of cramming three weeks into one email.
- 4. Move or cut the Olive Young block — it doesn't belong in a 50%-off flash-deal email; save it for its own send.
- 5. Lead the Afterpay 10% earlier or fold it into the hero — right now it's buried two-thirds down after fatigue has already set in.
