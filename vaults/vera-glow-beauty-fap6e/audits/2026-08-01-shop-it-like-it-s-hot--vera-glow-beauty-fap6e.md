# Shop it like it’s hot
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Keep Your Cool" — cute seasonal hook, and I'll give Lush credit for a genuinely pretty teal-and-cream layout, but this is another Lush send where the subject promises a shop-the-sale energy ("Shop it like it's hot") and the body just... doesn't deliver one. It's a numbered tips listicle — cool your shower jelly, stash a toner in your bag, try the foot lotion, layer on a face mask — dressed up as a routine guide, not a promotion. That's basically the same move as "Less is more" on 7/26 and "Pick of the Bunch" on 7/28: nice creative, zero actual deal.
- The one dollar sign in this whole email is a thin "$5 off with the Lush app" bar wedged above the logo, easy to scroll right past, and it's not even tied to anything I'm looking at below it. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item just landed in your lap" email that scored a 9 with me, or Sephora finally going all-in on the actual Beauty Insider Celebration with a big gold badge — those brands lead with the deal. Lush leads with vibes.
- Would I click? Maybe on "Hit Refresh" out of curiosity since the visuals are nice, but I'm not forwarding this to a friend and I'm not stopping my scroll for it. Skim, maybe tap once, move on.

## What's working

- The teal hero with "Keep Your Cool" is genuinely eye-catching and on-brand for August heat — good seasonal read.
- Clean, bug-free render — numbered sections, product shots, and the wavy section breaks all display correctly, no overlapping text or broken images.
- The "Too Hot to Sleep?" purple section pivoting to the Sleepy lavender line is a smart secondary hook — gives me a second reason to keep scrolling.
- Lush Club loyalty banner is visible near the bottom ("Scoop up points on eligible purchases") — at least they're reminding me the program exists.
- Product-level detail is there if you look (mint and arnica in the foot lotion, lavender in Sleepy) — more ingredient specificity than a lot of brands bother with in a tips email.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Shop it like it's hot" but there's no actual sale, discount tier, or urgency anywhere in the body — it's bait for a deal that isn't there.
- "Hit Refresh" as the primary CTA is a cute pun but tells me nothing about where I'm going — no "Shop Cooling Picks," just vibes.
- The $5 app offer at the very top is disconnected from everything below it and easy to miss on mobile scroll.
- No skin-type or skin-tone personalization, no points math, no "you're this close to your next reward" — nothing that says Lush actually knows I'm Rouge/Diamond-tier obsessive about tracking my spend elsewhere.
- Models are mixed gender with no clear lean toward a millennial-female audience, so it reads more like a general house list send than something segmented for me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick a lane: if the subject says "shop," put an actual offer in the hero — % off, a bundle price, something — instead of tips with a buried app-only $5 credit.
- 2. Make "Hit Refresh" concrete: swap for "Shop Cooling Picks" so the CTA tells me what I'm clicking into.
- 3. Link each numbered tip's arrow icon straight to that specific product page, not a general collection — right now it reads like four teaser cards with no clear destination.
- 4. Surface Lush Club higher and tie it to this email specifically ("earn 2x points on Cooling Kit this week") instead of a generic evergreen banner at the bottom.
- 5. Add one social-proof line under a hero product (star rating, "our #1 summer pick") — nothing in this send tells me anyone else likes these products.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your summer heat survival kit is here`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4 ways to beat the sweat (ft. your new fridge toner)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shower jelly, cooling toner, minty foot lotion — plus double Lush Club points this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The heat isn't backing down. Neither are we.`
