# These go well with free gloss…
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- The "goes with gloss" pairing format is genuinely the smartest merchandising move ILIA has pulled in this entire eleven-day sprint — showing me exactly which products complement the free Overgllam gloss, not just waving the freebie into the void. The lip close-up hero is pure ILIA editorial, the "GET YOUR BLUSH →" button is the most specific CTA copy they've written all month, and the three-category pairing layout (blush, lip crayon, eye pencil) actually helps me think through a cart. That part? Works.
- But I am on my tenth ILIA email in eleven days, and the sequencing is jarring me. Day before yesterday they dropped the 07.28 launch tease — black-and-white lash close-up, building real anticipation — and today that momentum just evaporates into a GWP push with a tote bag section bolted on the bottom like two separate briefs got stapled together at 5pm. Glossier's GWP reminder last week had me feeling like I was about to miss something I actually wanted. This one hands me a free gloss and walks away with no deadline. I'd click the blush CTA today, but not with any urgency, and I'm not forwarding it — if anything I'd tell a friend "the free gloss thing is live" flatly, not with hype.

## What's working

- **Product pairing layout is real merchandising**: blush + gloss, lip crayon + gloss, eye pencil + gloss — telling me what to actually buy to maximize the GWP is so much stronger than a generic gift banner.
- **Hero holds**: the terracotta lip close-up is editorial and instantly signals ILIA quality, no face needed.
- **CTA button copy is the best they've written this streak**: "GET YOUR BLUSH →" is specific, directional, category-named — miles better than Drunk Elephant's "Shop Now" or Sephora's generic "Explore."
- **Visual hierarchy is clean**: eyes land hero → offer copy → product pairings → CTA, no confusion.
- **Mini Tote "best not to linger" line** does create some scarcity signal, even if the section feels disconnected.

## What's weak

- **Cadence is actively damaging this**: ten emails in eleven days — I'm not counting out of fondness, I'm counting because my thumb is hovering over unsubscribe.
- **07.28 launch momentum completely abandoned**: they built something with that tease and then dropped it with no callback whatsoever.
- **No deadline on the free gloss**: "yours free with any order.\*" — until when? This week? Forever? Glossier put urgency on their GWP reminder and I felt it. ILIA just left the door open and walked away.
- **Two-CTA problem**: "GET YOUR BLUSH →" and "BAG IT" are competing for the same click, diluting both.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment**: I'm a known ILIA buyer through Sephora. Not one mention of points, Rouge, or anything that treats me like a returning customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Thread the 07.28 launch into this GWP mechanic** — "Something new drops 07.28. Order now and it ships with a free Overgllam gloss." That turns the GWP into a pre-launch incentive and keeps the narrative alive instead of breaking it.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the free gloss** — "Ends Sunday" anywhere in the first scroll converts this from passive to urgent.
- 3. **Cut the Mini Tote section or give it its own send** — one email, one action; the tote deserves its own moment anyway.
- 4. **Add one star rating or review pull-quote under the Soft Focus Blurring Blush** — it's the featured hero product, and social proof would push the cart-builder over the line.
- 5. **Drop a loyalty line in the footer** — even "This order earns Sephora Beauty Insider points" would make me feel seen.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free gloss drops with your order — but not for long`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pair with free gloss: blush, liner, or eye pencil`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Overgllam in Caramel with any order. Pick a pink to pair it with.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Which pink goes with your free gloss? Three options waiting.`
