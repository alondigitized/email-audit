# This one’s just for you 😘
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- "You have 0 points" — top left, right under INSIDER, again. That's the fourth day running I've had to open a Sephora email and see my Rouge status reading zero, and at this point I genuinely don't trust their sync more than I trust the actual offer. Past that glitch, this is a "Found It for You" hero built entirely around Prada Paradoxe — a $380 fragrance I have never bought, never browsed, and don't wear — followed by a generic "what's popular" grid of skincare and makeup with zero personalization to my actual purchase history.
- The trial-size Kerastase spray banner up top is honestly the most relevant thing in the whole email, and it's buried above a fold I almost scrolled past. Compare this to Glossier's actual 20%-off blush send — a real number tied to a real category — versus this, which is "look what's trending" dressed up as "just for you." It's not for me, it's for anyone with a Sephora account.
- I'm not clicking. Definitely not forwarding — my friends would clock the fake personalization in about two seconds, same as I did.

## What's working

- The Kerastase trial-size-with-$40-purchase offer at the very top is a concrete, actionable deal — free is free.
- Clean grid layout for the "popular" products — easy to scan eight items without feeling cluttered.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "Clean" badges are legible trust/curation signals I actually look for.
- Fragrance minis strip at the bottom is seasonally relevant for a summer travel-size push.

## What's weak

- "0 points" under INSIDER at the top — same broken loyalty sync I flagged on the K18 send (8/9) and both "winning" emails from 8/11. It's a pattern now, not a glitch, and it undercuts every other CTA in the email.
- Subject says "This one's just for you" but the hero is a $380 fragrance nobody's history-matched me on — that's a bait-and-switch on the personalization promise.
- No headline discount anywhere in the body — the only real "offer" is the trial-size spray, and it requires a $40 minimum spend I have to hunt for.
- The "popular products" grid is a scattershot of skincare, makeup, hair, and fragrance with no thread tying it to what I've bought before — feels like a default merchandising block, not curation.
- "Get 1 of 5" mystery reward for 250 points reads like filler when I supposedly have zero points to redeem with anyway.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points display before anything else ships — I've now seen "0 points" across four separate Sephora sends this week, and it's actively eroding trust in every offer they send me.
- 2. Replace the Prada hero with something tied to my actual purchase category — if I'm Rouge tier with real spend history, show me it.
- 3. Put an actual percentage or dollar offer in the hero, not just "Shop Now" — Glossier's straightforward 20%-off blush email beat this exact "content marketing wearing a subject line" problem.
- 4. Move the Kerastase trial offer higher or make it the hero — it's the only genuinely actionable thing in this email.
- 5. Cut the grid down to 3-4 items curated from my history instead of 8 generic bestsellers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Rouge picks, restocked and ready`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in your favorites: skincare + fragrance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Kerastase spray with $40 — plus what's trending in your categories`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your points are waiting. Here's what to grab first.`
