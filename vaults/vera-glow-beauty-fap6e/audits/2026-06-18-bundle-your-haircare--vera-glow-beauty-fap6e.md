# Bundle your haircare 💁‍♀️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Day nine. I know because I've been doing the mental math every time a new e.l.f. entity hits my inbox — and now there are three of them. Main e.l.f., e.l.f. SKIN (which dropped June 14 and was, honestly, the best send of the streak), and now e.l.f. Hair twice in forty-eight hours. The June 16 TikTok Shop launch email introduced the brand; this one is already bundling before I've emptied a single bottle from the first drop. The bundle mechanic itself is smart — I am the exact person who will do math in my head to figure out if a trio is cheaper than buying solo — but this email doesn't do the math for me. It just prints prices. "$27" is a number. "Save $12 versus buying separately" is a deal. There's a difference, and this email doesn't know it.
- ILIA has been doing the same thing lately: sub-two-day follow-ups that feel like a brand panicking rather than marketing. At least ILIA has the SKU depth to justify the cadence. e.l.f. Hair has been live for forty-eight hours.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I would mark it read and go back to watching Glossier's NYC party sell out.

## What's working

- Four bundle tiers with actual dollar amounts ($18, $27, $10, $22) — I appreciate a brand that shows its price before I have to click anywhere
- The yellow-purple-cyan palette is visually distinct from the complexion-heavy main e.l.f. sends; it reads "hair brand," not "the same email with a new hat"
- "Top Picks for You" at the bottom is a real personalization signal — algorithmic or not, it acknowledges I'm a known customer, not a cold lead
- No render bugs anywhere — images loaded, text is clean, layout held together on what is clearly a mobile-optimized build
- The "your bundles, bundled." hero copy has some wordplay personality; it's the one line that sounds like a copywriter was in the room

## What's weak

- Two e.l.f. Hair emails in forty-eight hours, layered on top of what was already a nine-day family send streak — there is no version of this cadence that doesn't erode my willingness to open
- Four bundles at the same visual weight with no hero recommendation: I'm not being sold anything, I'm being handed a menu and told to figure it out myself
- Zero urgency anywhere — no end date, no "introductory pricing," no limited-quantity signal — nothing that makes June 18 the day to act versus next Wednesday
- For a brand that literally launched on TikTok Shop two days ago, there is no UGC, no viral clip, no "this is going crazy on TikTok" social proof hook — the obvious layup, completely missed
- No savings math: Glossier's NYC party email (9/10) made the value proposition undeniable by spelling out exactly what you'd pay full price versus sale; this email makes me do homework to feel the deal

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero bundle and commit to it** — lead hard with the Never Thirsty Duo at $18 (low barrier to entry, skeptic-friendly price), then show the rest as "while you're here." Four equal-weight options is a product page, not a send.
- 2. **Do the savings math out loud** — "Never Thirsty Duo: $18 (save $12 vs. buying separately)" converts browsers into buyers; "$18" does not.
- 3. **Lock in launch-window urgency** — "Introductory bundle pricing, through June 22" costs nothing to write and creates a reason to act today.
- 4. **Pull one TikTok moment** — you launched on TikTok Shop; someone has already gone viral. One embed or screenshot from a creator showing the product in the wild would do more work than any copy in this email.
- 5. **Pace the follow-up** — one launch email, then wait until there's genuine product news (sell-out, restock, a new SKU) before another send. The June 16 intro was good; the June 18 bundle cheapens it.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Hair "Bundle your haircare" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based layout with MSO conditional comments, sent via Braze infrastructure (`braze-images.com`, `links.elfcosmetics.com`). HTML source is truncated, so the footer/compliance block and all CTAs beyond the header logo could not be fully audited — flagged findings below reflect only what is visible.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Sender domain / local-part mismatch**
`From: "e.l.f. Hair" <makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com>` — the local-part `makeup` was likely inherited from the parent brand's sending config and does not match the Hair sub-brand. Not a deliverability blocker, but can surface in phishing-detection heuristics and confuses inbox preview.

**Click-tracking domain**
All links route through `links.elfcosmetics.com` (Braze click wrapper). Correct and expected, but the encoded URL for the logo CTA is exceptionally long (~700 chars). If any ESP relay truncates query strings at 2 KB, this is at risk. Verify the redirect resolves cleanly end-to-end.

**Final destination URLs unverifiable**
Because all hrefs are Braze-encoded redirects, the actual landing page URLs and their UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from the HTML source alone. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers and some email clients (notably VoiceOver on iOS Mail) announce the document title. An empty title produces silence or a fallback string. Should be set to a descriptive value matching the subject line, e.g. `<title>Bundle your haircare — e.l.f. Hair</title>`.

**Logo link removed from tab order**
```html
<a href="..." tabindex="-1">
```
Keyboard and assistive-technology users cannot navigate to the logo/header link. If this is intentional (decorative), the image `alt` should be `alt=""` rather than `alt="e.l.f."`. As written, it's contradictory: the image has meaningful alt text but the link is unfocusable.

**Global link decoration reset**
```css
#MessageViewBody a { color: inherit; text-decoration: none }
```
This strips underlines and color from all links in Samsung Mail and some Outlook WebApp versions. CTAs relying only on text (no button background) will be invisible as links in those clients.

**Nested `nl-container` tables**
The outer `nl-container` wraps an inner identical `nl-container` inside an `html_block`. This double-nesting is redundant and can cause Outlook column width miscalculations at 640 px. No functional breakage confirmed from the truncated source, but worth a targeted Outlook rendering test.

**`html_block` blocks 2–4 are empty**
Three consecutive `<div>` blocks (`block-2`, `block-3`, `block-4`) contain only whitespace. These render as phantom spacing rows and add unnecessary DOM weight. Remove or consolidate.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens visible in the auditable portion of the HTML. The preheader is fully hardcoded:

```
"Get the most out of your haircare for better styling and mane-tenance."
```

No first-name insertion, product recommendation token, or loyalty-tier variable detected. This is not a defect, but confirms the send is untargeted/batch for this section.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**HTML truncated — footer not auditable**
CAN-SPAM requires: (a) physical postal address, (b) clear unsubscribe mechanism, (c) honest subject line. The subject line ("Bundle your haircare 💁‍♀️") is non-deceptive. Physical address and unsubscribe link could not be confirmed because the source was cut off before the footer. **These must be verified against the full render.**

**Authentication headers not provided**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed from HTML alone. Given the sending domain is `m.elfcosmetics.com` (a dedicated marketing subdomain), DKIM signing should be scoped to that subdomain. Request header dump from a received copy to verify `dkim=pass` and `dmarc=pass`.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

**UTM parameters unconfirmable from HTML**
All click URLs are Braze redirect tokens. The encoded destination and any UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are not readable in the raw HTML. Confirm via Braze link audit or by resolving the redirect chain manually:

```
https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/8RqRqO7AMIk0uM9Yc0impw~~/...
```

Expected minimum: `utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=<campaign_id>`.

**Logo CTA destination unknown**
The header logo is the only confirmed link in the visible HTML. Its landing page alignment (homepage vs. haircare landing page) cannot be verified. Best practice: logo → brand homepage; body CTAs → campaign-specific landing page.
