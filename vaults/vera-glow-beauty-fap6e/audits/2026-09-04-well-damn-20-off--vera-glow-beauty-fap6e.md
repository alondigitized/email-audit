## Executive summary

- Okay, "keep it between us" — cute, but I've had this "secret VIP sale" energy from Drunk Elephant before, so it doesn't feel that secret anymore. The GWP free-with-$110 "Summer Ending" set is a nice added hook I haven't seen from them this month, and the "you're gonna like these guys" product grid with review stars and live "X people are checking this out" counters is doing real work to get me scrolling.
- Visually this is clean and on-brand — the neon green circles pop against all that white space, and the hero shot with the products fanned out looks expensive. But it's also generic mass-market "SALE" copy dressed up in pink script; nothing here says skin type, nothing says "for you specifically," and Sephora's Stacking Weekend email I scored 9/10 this morning did tiered bonus points plus a real dollar threshold — this is just flat 20% off, no loyalty math for me to do at all.
- I'd click through to poke at the GWP set since $36 free is a real number, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — it's a solid single-code promo email, not a moment.

## What's working

- The $36 value free gift with $110+ orders gives me an actual number to chase, unlike a vague "free gift" tease.
- Dropping the SheerID verification step from 8/26 and 8/31 removes friction — I can just shop, no email-gate dance.
- The product grid with star ratings and "1,216 people are checking this out" social proof nudges me toward Protini and the new Kamo serum foundation specifically.
- Hero product shot (dropper, Protini jar, C-Firma) is genuinely pretty and reads premium, not cluttered.

## What's weak

- Zero personalization — no skin type, no "you bought Protini, refill time," nothing that treats me like Rouge/Diamond tier, unlike what I'd expect from a brand tracking my purchase history.
- "Keep it between us" / "secret sale" framing is worn out — this is my third 20%-off Drunk Elephant email in about a week (8/26, 8/31, now this), so "secret" rings hollow.
- Kamo Triple-Peptide Serum Foundation shows up here too, same week I've now seen Kamo pushed in three separate sends (8/11, 8/13, 8/21) — feels like the brand is leaning on one hero product across every campaign.
- No expiration date visible up top for the 20% code itself — I have to dig into the fine print at the bottom to find the 9/9 cutoff, which Sephora puts front and center in hero copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer deadline (9/9) directly under the code in the hero, not buried in fine print — Ulta's "ENDS TODAY" urgency in the hero is more persuasive even if it's overused by them.
- 2. Swap "secret sale" framing for something that acknowledges cadence fatigue — I've gotten this exact discount twice already this month.
- 3. Add one line of segmentation — "since you loved Protini" or similar — to make the product grid feel curated, not generic bestsellers.
- 4. Lead with the GWP set higher up, before the discount code — $36 free is more differentiated than another 20% code.
