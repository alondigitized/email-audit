# 🎉 New products have arrived
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- Ten emails in twelve days, and this is the second one today with "New products have arrived" in the subject line. I actually opened my Sephora folder twice because I thought my email client was glitching. It wasn't — they literally sent the same subject on the same day. That's the kind of inbox-management failure that gets you muted before the end of the month.
- Once I'm inside, though? There IS money here. The 50% off minis banner leads the email before I see anything else — that's exactly the right call, put the deal where I can't miss it. And the Rare Beauty free gift early access is Sephora using their loyalty tier the way they're supposed to: making Rouge feel like a VIP lane, not just a number. But "Take your PICK" as the big hero headline is doing absolutely nothing. Yesterday's "Obsessed? Same." had editorial personality. This feels like a completely different team picked up the account and defaulted to filler.
- The email tries to be a minis sale, a new arrivals roundup, a free gift promotion, an app launch teaser, AND a Benefit relaunch all at once. I would click for the minis math. I would not forward this — I'd just text my friend the offer verbally.

## What's working

- **50% off minis banner is front-and-center immediately** — I see the deal before the logo fully loads, which is exactly right; I don't have to scroll to know why I'm here
- **Rare Beauty free gift with early access is legitimately exciting** — this is Sephora's loyalty program earning its keep; the exclusivity signal is real
- **Brand curation is calibrated for me** — Merit, Benefit, Rare Beauty are brands I actually buy; no drugstore filler padding the grid
- **Prices are in the product tiles** — I can start doing the threshold math without clicking out, which for a Rouge member doing points calculations is genuinely useful

## What's weak

- **"Take your PICK" is the most generic hero headline they've sent me in two weeks** — after "Obsessed? Same." actually had voice, this is a hard step backward and doesn't tell me what I'm picking or why today
- **Second "New products have arrived" today, same subject, different email** — Sephora broke the cadence; this is the kind of thing that turns a loyal subscriber into someone who tabs them to "promotions" and checks in monthly
- **Six sections compete for one click** — minis, free gift, app early access, just arrived, Benefit relaunch, Beauty Insider footer; none of them get the spotlight they deserve and ILIA runs a single-concept editorial that consistently punches harder with less content
- **No deadline on the free Rare Beauty gift** — "Shop to Redeem" floating without an end date is anxiety-neutral, which means it doesn't convert; Ulta always anchors their GWP with "through Sunday" and it works

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one lead and commit** — the Rare Beauty free gift early access with a clear threshold ($75+?) should be the entire email, not section five of six; build everything around it
- 2. **Add a deadline to the gift** — "Through Sunday" or "First 500 members" turns a nice perk into a reason to move today
- 3. **Rewrite the hero** — "Take your PICK" could be "Your Rare Beauty Gift Is Ready — Rouge First" and immediately tell me this email is for me, not a blast to everyone
- 4. **Fix the cadence before the creative** — two identical subjects in one day erodes every trust signal a good email builds

- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off minis + a free gift — pick your move`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Early access for Beauty Insiders — grab your Rare Beauty bonus with $75+ before it opens to everyone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two minis, both at 50% off. Plus a free gift if you hit $75. Run the math.`
