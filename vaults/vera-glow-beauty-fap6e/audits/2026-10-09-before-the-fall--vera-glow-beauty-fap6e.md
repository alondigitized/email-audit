# Before the fall…
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: the math finally showed up, and I'm still not sold. Three Made-to-Measure Moisture kits now have price bubbles ($89/$135, $83/$123, $89/$139). That's the thing I begged for on the 9/29 send, so credit where it's due. But I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, and I did the points math in my head right away. "Value" is Drunk Elephant's own number, and I have no idea what a Protini or a Lala costs on its own, so I can't tell if the savings are real. This is also the same Maison Moisture kit family from "A flood of hydration" (10/4) and "Great, glow, and gorgeous" (10/7), only recolored. It's the fourth kit send in nine days.
- The layout is clean, and the alternating image/text rows are easy to scan on my phone. The yellow-and-sky-blue hero is pretty and on-brand. There's no deadline, no code, no gift-with-purchase and no mention of Sephora Rouge or any other loyalty angle. "Before the fall…" promises urgency and then delivers a "get your provisions ready" brochure. I'd skim it, maybe tap one kit, and move on. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The price bubbles with "$89 / VALUE: $135" are the first thing my eye lands on in each row. I can see the discount math without reading any copy.
- Each kit has its own pastel color (mint, lilac, pink) and a name that says what it does: Strengthen + Moisturize, Rescue + Recover, Rebuild + Restore. I can pick by skin concern.
- "One Protini + a refill" explains what's in the box in a few words. It's clearer than the 9/29 "$158 for $99?" send.
- The hero is eye-catching, and the big "SHOP NOW" button is hard to miss.
- The Atelier banner at the bottom hints at more of the holiday collection, which sets up a reason to come back.

## What's weak

- No deadline and no code. Sephora's Beauty Insider events and Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty both run on a clock. This runs on "fall," which isn't a date.
- The "Value" prices aren't backed up. I can't see what I'd pay buying the jar and refill separately, so the savings are just a claim.
- It's the third near-identical kit hero in nine days (10/4, 10/7, now 10/9). It feels like inbox wallpaper, not a new launch.
- Nothing for members. There's no points multiplier, no early access and no birthday tie-in, even though Drunk Elephant just asked for my birthday on 10/5.
- The three "SHOP NOW" links are small, thin underlined text on the kit rows. They're a tiny tap target on a phone.
- The Atelier block has a lot of small boxes crammed together and no price or product to focus on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline and a reason to act now, like "Kits ship by [date]" or "Free refill through Sunday." That makes the "before the fall" subject line pay off.
- 2. Show what the kit saves against the jar-plus-refill price, e.g. "$89 vs $135 separately, save $46," so I can compare it to Sephora's gift sets.
- 3. Make the row CTAs real buttons with specific copy like "Get the Protini kit" instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" links.
- 4. Add a member line, something like "Rouge and Insider members get early access." Right now it reads as if I'm a stranger.
- 5. Vary the creative. If the kits are the story, change the lead each send (a review, a before/after, a bundle comparison) so I'm not seeing the same three tubs again.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has several code-level problems: the recipient's address sits in plaintext in a tracking URL, three images use `http://`, and two images have no alt text. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay may not have captured them. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, body links and landing-page URLs were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Recipient email exposed in a URL.** The Everest tracking URL is `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. It puts the subscriber's address in a query string in cleartext. That leaves it open to logging in proxies, referrers and analytics, and to link forwarding.
- **Tracking pixel.** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holrefillkits_p…` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). This is normal for an open pixel, but it is persistent per-user data.
- **Unprobed links.** 14 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so none of their final destinations were checked.
- **Mixed tracking vendors.** The email uses both `mi.drunkelephant.com` and `everestengagement.com`. Both are third-party redirect or pixel hosts and both add a dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images).** The `o.gif` pixel and the Everest image (`…everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`) have no alt text. The pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Insecure image sources (3).** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that proxy or block non-HTTPS content may drop them.
- **Invalid `@import` placement.** In the `<style>` block, three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule. CSS requires `@import` to come first, so parsers ignore them.
  - The `<link>` tags to the same fonts partly cover this, but many clients strip `<link>` in `<head>`.
- **Malformed CSS.** HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->`) sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. Some parsers may drop the rules around them.
- **Font hosting.** The email depends on `db.onlinewebfonts.com` for Brown Bold, Light and Thin. It also depends on a versioned Demandware path for `BrownRegular` (`…/v1719565389232/fonts/…`), which may break when the site is redeployed.
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, and `<title>` is only "Drunk Elephant". Both affect screen-reader behavior.
- **Mobile sizing.** At ≤418px and ≤600px, `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` are forced to `max-width:15px` / `12px` with `!important`, which risks clipping or wrapping text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) are visible in the available source. The recipient address is resolved, but it is exposed as described in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header** was found (QA WARN). The QA check notes AgentMail may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header** (RFC 8058) was found, so one-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, and no alignment can be confirmed here.
- **Footer not auditable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID only.** The one visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holrefillkits_p…`, which is a vendor tag rather than UTM parameters.
- **No UTMs visible.** None of `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` appear in the available source.
- **Destinations unchecked.** Because 14 links were skipped, final landing URLs and whether the destination matches the "holiday refill kits" campaign could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove the plaintext `e=<email>` parameter from tracking URLs, or replace it with an opaque token.
2. Switch the three social icons to `https://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/…`.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel and meaningful alt text to the Everest image.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of `<style>`, or drop them in favor of `<link>` with web-safe fallback stacks. Remove the HTML comments from inside the `@media` block.
5. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>` and use a more descriptive `<title>`.
6. Confirm that `List-Unsubscribe` (with `mailto:` and HTTPS) and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present in the raw message. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cheers.drunkelephant.com`. Re-run the audit against the full raw `.eml`, since the QA data suggests the relay dropped the headers.
7. Re-audit with untruncated HTML, then resolve the redirect chains to verify landing pages and UTMs.
