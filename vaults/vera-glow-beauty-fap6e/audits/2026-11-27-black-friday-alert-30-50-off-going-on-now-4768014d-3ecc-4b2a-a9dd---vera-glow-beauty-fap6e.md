# BLACK FRIDAY ALERT: 30-50% off going on now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again. This is the 11/27 "it's actually Black Friday" send, and it's the first one in a while that has something new in it. "BLACK FRIDAY: WAKE UP & SAVE!" sits over a giant red "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" on black, so I get the offer in half a second. Below that is a dashed red Plus-member box: "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING, CODE: BF2026." That stacking is the points math I actually care about, and it's more than the "Fan favorites, now for less" send on 11/26 gave me.
- It's still a footwear brand in my beauty inbox. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, so nothing here touches my routine. The email is clean, though. Four category tiles carry specific percentages and the doorbuster block is clear. It's the best-organized Skechers send I've audited this week. I'd skim it, screenshot the stacking code for my mom's sneaker order, and not click for myself. I wouldn't forward it to a friend either, unless she's on a feet-all-day job.
- Sephora's 50% OFF hero on sequins and Ulta's $10 barcode coupon both feel more like events than this does. Skechers does win on tier clarity. The Plus ladder is easier to read than ILIA's "25% off, join VIP" banner.

## What's working

- The "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" hero is huge and high-contrast, and I read it instantly. It's as clear as Drunk Elephant's "30% OFF SITEWIDE" opener.
- The Plus box shows "EXTRA 15% OFF" with the code BF2026 right under it. That's the stacking math I want to see.
- The tiles label each category with its own number: Shoes 30%, Boots 40%, Apparel 50%, Sale up to 50%. That gives me a ladder, not just one blanket figure.
- "Doorbuster Deals starting at $49.99" gives me a concrete price point. Sally Beauty only gave me a percentage.
- The page has no broken images or overlapping text, and the shop buttons are easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The offer has nothing to do with beauty. Sephora and Ulta would at least give me points or a free gift.
- The tile hierarchy gets muddy. Every tile has the same "SHOP NOW" button, and the product names are wrapped in overlay text.
- The Plus bar at the top is easy to miss. "Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points (That's a $5 reward!)" is tiny gray text. It's a tier hook buried above the hero.
- The fine print is a wall. The "Valid online only thru 11/29" deadline is small and gray under the red button, and the footer carries two dense blocks of exclusions.
- This is the sixth or seventh "30% sitewide" message this week, going back to "Hours left for 30% off sitewide" on 11/23. The cadence is fatiguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Plus stack up next to the hero as one combined "30-50% + extra 15%" line, and make "Join Plus" a button instead of gray text.
- 2. Make the deadline bold and put it beside the hero: "Ends 11/29, 11:59 PT."
- 3. Give each tile one named hero product with a price, not just a category label.
- 4. Cut the cadence. Send one "Black Friday is live" message and one "last chance" message.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is mostly sound, with a table layout, `role="presentation"`, a preheader, and responsive CSS. The confirmed problems are two non-HTTPS images (one a third-party tracking pixel carrying a recipient ID), two images with no alt text, and accessibility settings that block zoom and text scaling. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the headers were not captured. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and the body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 41 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains). Their destinations and status are unverified, so no link validity claim can be made.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11272026_AM` loads over plain HTTP.
  - It is a third-party open-tracking pixel, on a different domain from the ESP image host.
  - The `mi_u=8108776175` parameter looks like a per-recipient identifier, sent in cleartext.
  - Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop the pixel, so opens will be under-counted.
- **Insecure image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` also uses `http://`, and may be blocked or flagged. The same host serves the JSON-LD images over `https://`, so an `https://` version should be available.
- **Favicon:** `<link rel="icon">` points to `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`. This is a different subdomain (`emails.nl.`) from the main image host, and it is also on `http://`. It has no rendering impact in email clients, but it is inconsistent.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** two images have no `alt` attribute: `o.gif` (the pixel) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. The pixel should have `alt=""`. The PNG needs descriptive alt text if it is content, or `alt=""` if it is decorative.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS from scaling text.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline: none; }` removes the keyboard focus indicator.
- **No language set:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers cannot pick the right language.
- **Positives:** the visible layout table has `role="presentation"`, and the preheader is hidden correctly (`display:none !important`, `font-size:1px`). Because of the truncation, I cannot confirm the remaining tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The only per-recipient value seen is `mi_u=` in the tracking pixel, noted above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, same caveat.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `no-reply@emails.skechers.com`.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical address were not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.
- **Reply address:** the `no-reply@` sender is a common deliverability and CAN-SPAM discussion point, but there is no evidence of a violation here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM structure in the JSON-LD annotation URL:** `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BLACKFRIDAY&utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11272026_AM&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1`.
  - The campaign ID matches the `mi_ecmp` value in the pixel URL, so the two are consistent.
  - `utm_source=trig` is unusual, since it looks like a trigger or ESP code rather than a source name.
- **Landing page:** `/black-friday-cyber-deals/` is plausible for the "30-50% off" offer. I could not check the live page because the tracking links were skipped, so I cannot say whether the offer, the 15% member stack, or the end date match.
- **Offer window:** the DiscountOffer schema runs `2026-11-23T00:11:00Z` to `2026-11-29T23:11:59Z`. These are odd timestamps, and they should be confirmed as intentional.
- **Other links:** the UTM parameters on the other 40 or so body links are not visible in the truncated source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the `ink1000.com` pixel and the `dde00662-…png` image over HTTPS, and ask the vendor whether `mi_u` can be sent in a less exposed way.
2. Add `alt=""` to `o.gif`, and meaningful alt text to `dde00662-…png`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1`. Consider relaxing `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`, and restore a focus style on `a`.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw message at the original recipient inbox. The relay results are inconclusive.
6. Resolve a sample of the 41 tracking links to check that destinations and UTMs reach `/black-friday-cyber-deals/`, and verify the offer end date.
7. Re-run the audit on the full HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links can be checked.
