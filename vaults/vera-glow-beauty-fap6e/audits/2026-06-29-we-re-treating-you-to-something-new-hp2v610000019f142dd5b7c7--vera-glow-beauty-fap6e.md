# ▶ We’re treating you to something new
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-29**
## Executive summary

- I've now seen this subject line twice. "▶ We're treating you to something new" already hit my inbox once on June 29 — I scored that version a 7 — and here it is again, same sender, same date, different email ID. Whether that's a resend to an overlapping segment or an A/B test I landed in twice, the word "new" in the subject is already doing less work the second time I see it. That's not a great start.
- Inside, the email is genuinely doing a lot — and that's the problem. I counted at least seven distinct content blocks: free shipping bar, Luxe Trial Size offer, "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero, a product launch grid, a "PARIS DON'T" editorial, a Stacking Weekend loyalty event callout, a 30% Spot It section, and a "Just Arrived" carousel. Each one is visually clean on its own. Together they feel like a weekly digest, not an email with a point. The 30% offer and the Stacking Weekend loyalty event are the most actionable things in here — and both of them are buried below the fold, which is the opposite of how they should be prioritized for a Rouge member.
- I'd scroll, I might click into the Stacking Weekend section if I caught it, but I wouldn't forward this. The palate-cleanser-after-the-sale-blitz effect I appreciated in the first version of this email has worn off by the time it's hitting me twice.

## What's working

- **Free shipping bar at the very top** — clean, functional, I see it before anything else
- **"SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero** is on-brand and visually distinct; the purple palette signals Sephora without screaming sale
- **Luxe Trial Size near the top** is smart loyalty-program placement — feels like a reward, not a pitch, and it's close to the fold
- **Stacking Weekend loyalty event** is the kind of points-math content I actually want from Sephora — something Charlotte Tilbury would never think to surface
- **No sale-urgency language** — after the June 22-23 "ENDS TODAY / LAST CHANCE" gauntlet I logged, this tonal shift is genuinely appreciated

## What's weak

- **Duplicate subject line** — I already reviewed this exact subject in this same batch; "something new" loses its hook on the second pass from the same sender on the same day
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is doing the absolute minimum** — with seven launches potentially in this email, a verb-and-noun CTA is a missed opportunity on every single one
- **30% offer is buried in block five or six** — the most concrete price signal in the entire email is where people have already stopped scrolling
- **Content block count is out of control** — PARIS DON'T, Beauty PIERRE, Just Arrived, Spot It. Shop It. — pick a lane or link to a hub page; this email needs an edit, not another section
- **No demographic signal visible** — at any scale in this screenshot I cannot tell if this is curated for my skin tone, age, or concerns; it reads as a broadcast, not a send to a known customer

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the Stacking Weekend loyalty event** — if there's a points-multiplier or threshold event live right now, that IS the email for a Rouge or Diamond member; everything else is supporting content
- 2. **Put the 30% signal above the fold** — even a headline like "30% picks + what just dropped" would reorder my attention correctly
- 3. **Name a specific new launch in the subject** — e.l.f. did this with their skin tint email and it was the strongest subject I saw all month; "something new" is a missed opportunity to name-drop a prestige launch
- 4. **Cut to three sections max** — hero (loyalty event or launch) + new arrivals grid + one editorial pick; drop PARIS DON'T and Beauty PIERRE to a footer text link

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`robots` meta typo** — `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` contains a space. Valid value is `noindex` (no space). Some crawlers may ignore the malformed directive, risking inadvertent indexation of the web-view version.
- **Zoom prevention** — `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta disables pinch-to-zoom. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, AA) requires content to be resizable up to 200% without loss of functionality.
- **Broad `text-size-adjust: none`** — Applied globally to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. Suppresses OS-level text scaling that users with low vision depend on. Should be scoped to `.sup` only.
- **Missing `lang` attribute** — No `lang="en"` visible on the `<html>` tag. Screen readers use this to select the correct voice/language profile.
- **Generic `<title>`** — `<title>Sephora</title>` provides no context in browser tab or web-view. Should reflect the subject line or offer.
