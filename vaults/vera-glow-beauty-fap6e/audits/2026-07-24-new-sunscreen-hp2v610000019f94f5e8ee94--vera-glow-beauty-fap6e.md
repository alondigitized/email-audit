# NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Hold on — I literally just reviewed a "NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️" from Sephora earlier today and gave it a 7/10. Same subject line. Different message ID, different layout, some different products. I don't know if Sephora's ESP misfired, if this is an A/B test they forgot to flag, or if the send schedule is genuinely that broken, but landing the same sunscreen subject in my inbox twice in one day tanks whatever credibility the first send earned. I already did my sunscreen research off the first email. This one is just noise.
- Inside the email itself — the yellow "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" hero is breezy and seasonal, and I appreciate that sunscreen products are actually front and center. The product grid delivers: Biore, Supergoop!, some newer brands I recognize. And the "Auto-Replenish Hot Deal" buried mid-scroll is actually interesting to me as someone who goes through SPF constantly in summer. But then there's a full "For Dry Hair" module at the bottom. We started at sunscreen and ended at shampoo. The subject line made me a promise; the email broke it before I even got to the fold.
- I would not click. I already half-processed this sunscreen content earlier today. If the first send hadn't landed, this one might have gotten a tap. The double-send killed the urgency.

## What's working

- The yellow gradient hero with "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" is visually clean and season-appropriate — genuinely cheerful for a July email.
- Sunscreen products are actually featured with brand names visible (Supergoop!, Biore) and SPF specs called out, which is what I need to make a decision.
- "Auto-Replenish Hot Deal" with a LIMITED TIME badge is the most action-driving copy in the whole email — smart hook for consumables like SPF.
- Product prices are visible in the grid, which saves me a click just to find out if something is in my range.
- INSIDER badge at the bottom at least nods to the loyalty program, even if it's just a logo.

## What's weak

- This is literally the second "NEW SUNSCREEN 💦 ☀️ 🏖️" Sephora has sent me today. No subject line variation, no acknowledgment — just a second send into my inbox like I have amnesia. Glossier, when they resend a GWP reminder, at least tweaks the framing ("still on: your free mini beauty bag"). Sephora sent me the identical subject line.
- "For Dry Hair" at the bottom has no relationship to the sunscreen promise in the subject. I came for SPF, not a hair care module. This is the same unfocused-everything problem from Sephora's recent hair care email (July 22nd, 4X points), just inverted.
- "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" is a generic headline that doesn't commit to anything — I'd expect that placeholder energy from a smaller brand, not Sephora. The subject line was more specific than the hero.
- No points math anywhere. As a Rouge member who is actively running numbers on what to buy before the next Insider event, the complete absence of any "earn X points on SPF" callout is a miss. Sally Beauty managed to run a flash sale and still surface discount logic cleanly.
- The "Snap. Shop. It." UGC block feels like filler — I can't see enough detail in the lifestyle images to tell what products are being featured.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop the double-send.** This is a suppression/deduplication problem. If this is genuinely an A/B test, label it internally and don't let both variants hit the same subscriber in the same day. Urgent fix.
- 2. **Commit to one category in the body.** If the subject says sunscreen, cut the hair care module. Save it for the 4X points hair email (which was great). One category, one email = one click.
- 3. **Activate the "Auto-Replenish Hot Deal" in the hero.** Move that LIMITED TIME framing up above the fold. That's the most compelling hook in the email and it's buried.
- 4. **Add Insider points math to the SPF products.** Even a "Rouge members earn X points" badge on the hero product would convert me faster. e.l.f. SKIN does this with product-level benefit callouts and it's always cleaner than Sephora's approach.
- 5. **Make the hero headline specific.** "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" could be on any Sephora email any week of the year. Try "Your Summer SPF Shortlist Is Here" or "The Sunscreens Sephora Just Dropped."
