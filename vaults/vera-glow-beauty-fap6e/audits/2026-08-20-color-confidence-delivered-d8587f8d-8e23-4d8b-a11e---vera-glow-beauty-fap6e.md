## Executive summary

- Okay, it's Sally again — I feel like I say that every single time this brand lands in my inbox. This is COLORfest beat number... honestly I've lost count, but my brand history says nine-plus and this is clearly another one, this time pivoting to "Buy two, get one free Select Wella hair color." The confetti dots are back, the pink banner is back, and the "20% off sitewide" flash sale bar up top is stacked on top of the BOGO offer below it, which is a little confusing — which deal am I actually getting?
- That said, credit where it's due: this is a genuinely clean product grid. ColorCharm, ShineLuxe, the powder lightener — real box shots, real product names, real "Shop now" links under each one. It's DIY box color territory though, and I'm Rouge/Diamond tier — I get my color done at the chair or I buy prestige at Sephora's Beauty Insider event. This just isn't my category. Compare that to the Sephora x Olive Young send I scored a 9 on — that one felt like a genuine curated collab drop with K-beauty specificity. This is a drugstore color aisle in email form.
- Would I click? No. Would I forward it? Only to a friend who does her own box color, and even then I'd caption it "Sally's still running COLORfest, apparently forever."

## What's working

- The hero model has warm, glossy brunette hair that actually sells "color with confidence" — decent visual match to the product story.
- Individual product shots with names and benefits (nourishes, neutralizes brassiness, rebuilds bonds) give this more substance than Sally's usual grid dumps.
- The Sally Rewards banner at the bottom ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is at least a real loyalty mechanic, even if $5 is tiny beer next to my Sephora points math.
- Klarna financing callout is a nice touch for the price-conscious shopper, even if it's irrelevant to me.

## What's weak

- Three offers competing at once — 20% off sitewide code AUGUST20, free 2-hour delivery on $35+, and BOGO Wella color — with zero visual hierarchy telling me which one actually applies to what I'd buy. That's classic Sally clutter I've flagged before.
- This is DIY box hair color and toner — not remotely my price point or my shopping mode. I don't touch box dye; I'm getting balayage from a colorist and buying prestige haircare from Oribe or Olaplex at Sephora.
- No personalization anywhere — no name, no tier callout, nothing that says "we know you." Ulta at least drops my name and a specific coupon code in subject lines even when the offer's repetitive.
- "COLORfest" as a campaign name means nothing to me at this point — it's just wallpaper after seeing it run non-stop since late July.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and lead with it — either the BOGO Wella deal or the sitewide 20%, not both stacked with a delivery banner on top. Right now the eye doesn't know where to land.
- 2. Segment this send away from prestige/high-tier loyalty members like me — if Sally has purchase history data, box color buyers should get this, not everyone on the list.
- 3. Retire "COLORfest" as a campaign name for a while — nine-plus sends under one banner in a month is fatigue, not a campaign.
- 4. Add a loyalty-tier or points callout that's actually worth doing math on — $5 for two purchases doesn't move we-do-the-math shoppers like me.
