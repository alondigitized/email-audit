## What's working

- The top banner is clear. "Happening now," the 30% medallion, the dates and the code all sit together, and the dates are visible.
- The gold-glitter holiday look is on-season and polished. It reads like Sephora and doesn't look broken.
- "$35 and Under Gift Sets" is the most useful block for me. It has a price ceiling, a "SHOP NOW" button and "LIMITED EDITION" urgency.
- The footer perks are neatly laid out: free shipping, Sephora Texts, 25% off with the Credit Card, and a wish-list prompt.

## What's weak

- The 30% offer drops out of the email after the first screen. The chatbot, "Beauty on Demand," payment logos and gift sets never tie back to the discount.
- The chatbot mockup is huge. Its recommendations (a Dyson toothbrush, a $128 Kayali set) aren't Sephora Collection, so they don't match the offer in the banner.
- The "Shop Gifts Now" block is just Klarna, Afterpay, PayPal and Venmo logos. That's filler. It tells me how to pay but not what to buy.
- Nothing is personalized for me as a Rouge member. No tier, no points, no "earn X on this."
- The 30% disclaimer is tiny in the footer. The barcodes are also at the very bottom, so I'd have to scroll the whole email to use the deal in store.

- 1. Open with the 30% Sephora Collection deal and three or four actual products. Cut the chatbot demo down to one short module lower in the email.
- 2. Add a Rouge-specific line, such as "Rouge: your 30% stacks with your points," so tier members feel recognized.
- 3. Put the in-store barcode near the top, or add an "Add to Wallet" button. Ulta's Early Black Friday email at least puts its offer first, even though it greets me like a stranger.
- 4. Replace the payment-logo block with Sephora Collection gift picks, since that's the brand the sale is on. Sally Beauty's Halloween email was more focused, with one hero look and a 2-hour delivery claim.
- 5. Space out the cadence. This is a fourth or fifth send for the same event, and I need a reason to open each one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no plain-text part (0 chars, the one QA FAIL). Its markup also has a few fixable problems: a malformed MSO conditional inside a media query, a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and an open-tracking pixel without `alt`. The HTML was truncated and the QA data is limited, so link results, UTMs, footer content and header-based compliance can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links go through the ESP redirector, for example `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a192bcd656c5f5c2f4bbe5c7c0/c72011c4.../c72011c4-fa56-47ff-8ae1-75ac6bc9916f?__dU__=...`. This is expected click tracking, not a defect.
- QA probed 12 of 38 links and skipped 26. No probe failures were reported, but the 26 skipped links (footer and social) are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel, `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...'>`, has no `alt`. It is hidden (`display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`), so the impact is low, but it is what QA flagged. Adding `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` would clear the warning.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **No plain-text alternative [FAIL].** QA reports the text version as 0 chars. This hurts deliverability and accessibility for text-only and screen-reader clients, and is a common spam-filter signal for HTML-only mail.
- **Malformed CSS in the media query.** An `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` block sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px) {...}` rule in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't belong in CSS. Parsers' error recovery may drop the rules that follow it, namely `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `.mobileHeaderTierBI`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileBelong`, `.footerBanner` and `.mobileDisc`. The MSO override also never reaches Outlook this way, so it should sit in its own `<!--[if mso]><style>` block.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width layout.** The outer wrapper is `width="700"` with `min-width:700px`. Unless the truncated body overrides it, this forces horizontal scroll in clients that ignore or strip media queries, such as Gmail with non-Google accounts.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email.
- **Generic title.** `<title>Sephora</title>` is a minor issue.
- **Incomplete JSON-LD.** The block has `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` and a bare `"PromotionCard"`. Empty or invalid markup is ignored at best and may fail Gmail schema validation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder is truncated, so this is unverified for the body and footer.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for one-click unsubscribe aren't met.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, so check alignment on the raw message.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address.** QA states an unsubscribe link was probed, but the truncated source doesn't show it or the postal address, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The visible hrefs carry only the ESP's `__dU__` token. The truncated source shows no `utm_source`, `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign`, and attribution may be applied at the redirect.
- Landing destinations aren't visible because the redirector hides them, and the QA data doesn't list final URLs. Continuity with the "AI Beauty Chat" preheader is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` MIME part that mirrors the CTAs and includes the unsubscribe URL. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` rule out of the `@media` block into a dedicated conditional `<style>` block, and re-test the mobile classes that follow it.
3. Drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `alt=""` and 1×1 dimensions to the tracking pixel.
5. On the raw message, verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Treat the QA warnings as capture limitations until then.
6. Remove or complete the JSON-LD (empty `discountCode`, bare `PromotionCard`).
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML, and resolve the redirect chains for the 26 skipped links to check UTMs and landing pages.
