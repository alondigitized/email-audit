# Last chance—enter to win $2,000 of ILIA
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same sweepstakes in a week, so my gut reaction is "okay, I get it." It ran as the thin banner on 11/9 ("Gift ideas loading…") and as the hero on 11/10 ("One match. $2,000 of ILIA."). Now it's back with a "last chance" sticker. The deadline is the one new thing, and I'll give it that. But it's a quiz-for-entry mechanic, not a discount, and I've already seen it twice.
- The free mini Eye Stylus in Beam with a $75+ order is the part I'd do math on. It's the only real purchase incentive, and it's in the second half of the email. A $75 threshold for a mini shadow stick isn't thrilling. At Sephora I'd rather spend that $75 toward Rouge points.
- I'd open this one, since it's quick and the sender is familiar. I'd probably skip the quiz and might mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. If I clicked anything, it would be "Shop to claim," and only if I was already planning a $75 cart.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "$2,000 of ILIA" in big type with the pink bags makes the prize obvious in one glance.
- The "last chance" sticker adds urgency without shouting, and it looks like part of the design.
- The "Get shade matched" button is dark and unmissable, and the CTA copy is clear.
- The free mini Eye Stylus has a visual badge, and the close-up eye shot sells the product well.
- The footer trust row (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, free shipping over $50) is tidy, and I like seeing the clean-beauty cues.

## What's weak

- I've now seen this same sweepstakes on 11/9, 11/10 and today. That's fatigue, and it makes the "last chance" feel more like cadence than a real deadline.
- No end date or time appears anywhere. "Last chance" with no deadline isn't credible, and my 11/13 survey email at least said "midnight."
- The $75 threshold for a free mini feels steep. Nothing says what a mini is worth.
- The asterisks send me to "see site for details" with no inline terms. That's a bit murky for a sweepstakes.
- There's no loyalty or points angle. ILIA VIP is only a tiny footer icon, so I can't do any points math.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deadline in the hero (e.g., "Ends tonight at midnight PT") so "last chance" feels real.
- 2. Make the gift-with-purchase more prominent, and lower the threshold or state the value of the mini.
- 3. Add one line of sweepstakes terms inline (no purchase necessary, eligibility) rather than only linking out.
- 4. Surface ILIA VIP perks near the offer, such as double points on the order that earns the mini.
- 5. Stop re-sending the identical creative. A fresh angle, such as a shade-match result teaser, would do more than a third repeat.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on a content image.** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt`. If it carries meaning, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Empty `<title>`.** The source has `<title> </title>`, which is whitespace only. This is minor, since it matters mainly for web-view rendering.
- **No `lang` attribute on `<html>`.** The tag is `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>`. Screen readers rely on `lang` to pick pronunciation.
- **External font loading via `@import`.** The email imports `https://static-forms.klaviyo.com/fonts/api/v1/SguDsz/custom_fonts.css` and `https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat`. Gmail and Outlook ignore `@import`. The fallback stack `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` is declared, so degradation is graceful and this is informational only.
