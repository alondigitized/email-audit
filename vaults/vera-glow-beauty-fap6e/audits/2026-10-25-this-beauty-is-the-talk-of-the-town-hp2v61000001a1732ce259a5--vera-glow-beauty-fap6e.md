# This beauty is the talk of the town 📞
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-25**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a nicely built catalog, and I'd still scroll past it. It's the fourth Sephora send in four days, after "Oh. My. Minis." on 10/23 and "Beauty in high demand 📈" twice on 10/24. The "talk of the town" line is the same "everyone's buzzing" energy in a new outfit. The Phlur Heavy Cream hero is pretty and the "Clean at Sephora" badge is a nice touch. But the only real money-saver is the thin Trial Size strip with code GLAMPICKS, which is free with a $30 purchase, online only. That's the same buried-offer problem I called out on 10/24.
- The loyalty bar is what really stung. The header says "You have 0 points," and the tier tracker parks me at Insider. I'm Rouge, so the one thing Sephora should get right is wrong. Ulta at least misses me the other way, with "join Ulta Beauty Rewards" to a Diamond. This one isn't even personalized. "Your Personalized Edit" shows Marc Jacobs Perfect twice (in that row and again under "Trending for a Reason"), plus a Versace Bright Crystal that reads like a 2012 mall fragrance. Would I click? Maybe the Rare Beauty blush, since it's $25 and I already know the shade. Forward it? No. Mark it read and wait for the Savings Event details.

## What's working

- The Phlur hero is clean. "NEW" plus the prices ($99 and $38) are right there, so launch clarity is good.
- Product tiles show brand, product, and price together, so I can judge value without tapping. That's more than Glossier's single-product emails give me.
- The Allure Best of Beauty and "Only at Sephora" badges add real trust and exclusivity.
- The "SEPHORA SAVINGS EVENT, Step 1" banner tells me an event is coming. I'm stocking up for it anyway, so that's useful.
- Type is large and readable on mobile, and the layout has no visible bugs.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried. The free trial size is a small strip under the nav, and the actual savings event is just a black banner with no dates or percentages. I can't do points math without a deadline or a tier discount.
- The loyalty bar contradicts my status. It shows "0 points" and puts me at Insider, so I can't trust any tier-based pricing here.
- The "Personalized Edit" isn't personal. Marc Jacobs Perfect repeats in two sections, and nothing reflects my skin tone, my shade history, or anything I've bought.
- The email is long and crowded: hero, two product grids, two promo tiles, Saie, and a tier bar. Nothing tells me which section matters most.
- There's no end date or urgency anywhere. The Savings Event "Step 1" has no dates, so it gives me no reason to act now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero carry the offer. Put the Savings Event dates and discount ladder (Insider/VIB/Rouge percentages) at the top, then the Phlur launch below. Ulta leads with "Up to 50% off" and a clear pink CTA, and Sephora should do the same.
- 2. Fix the loyalty module so it shows my real tier and points, with a line like "You're 340 points from a Rouge reward."
- 3. Remove the duplicated Marc Jacobs tile and make the personalized row actually personalized, using past purchases and shade matches.
- 4. Cut the email to one hero, one product grid, and one promo tile. Cutting the other sections would trim at least a third of the length.
- 5. Make the CTAs specific. Use "Shop Phlur" or "Redeem trial size" instead of "Shop now."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code GLAMPICKS: free trial size with $30+, online only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a 0-character plain-text part, which is the one hard QA failure. Two images lack alt text, and the viewport meta tag disables pinch-zoom. Authentication and unsubscribe-header status could not be verified because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links, footer and merge tokens were not available to audit.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 of 60 links and skipped 48. No broken links were reported among the probed set, but the skipped links are unverified.
- The first image without alt text has `src="https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx…"`. The long opaque path on the `/O/` route looks like an open-tracking pixel. If so, it is a tracking beacon and not a content image.
- Link-level evidence such as redirects and UTM parameters was not in the truncated source or QA output. No other link issues are confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN, 2 images):**
  - `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`, likely the tracking pixel. Use `alt=""` if it is decorative.
  - `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-…/2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, a nav header image. Without alt text, the nav labels are unavailable when images are blocked or read by a screen reader. This one needs real alt text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Text-size adjust:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This blocks user text-size scaling in iOS Mail and some WebKit clients.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The space makes the value invalid. Impact is minimal for email.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. It is only a minor issue for email.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the truncated portion. The remainder was not available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability, since some spam filters penalize HTML-only mail, and it hurts accessibility for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is also expected from the relay. It is a verification gap, not a confirmed failure.
- The footer's physical address and unsubscribe link were not in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but its result was not shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The truncated source and QA output show no destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page checks. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a plain-text part** (multipart/alternative). This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag. Review the `text-size-adjust: none` rules.
3. **Add alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. **Fix the robots meta** to `noindex`, or remove it.
5. **Re-check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`** against the raw message, for example in Gmail's "Show original", to rule out relay stripping.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so the footer, merge tokens and UTM parameters can be checked. Probe the 48 skipped links as well.
