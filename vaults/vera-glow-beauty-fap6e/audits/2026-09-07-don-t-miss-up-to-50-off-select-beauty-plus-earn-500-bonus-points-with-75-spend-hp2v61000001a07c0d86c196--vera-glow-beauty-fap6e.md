# Don’t miss up to 50% off select beauty! Plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 spend ⏳
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is send number FOUR I've logged from Sephora today for Stacking Weekend — 9am at 9/10, follow-up at 8/10, a third at 8/10 — and this time they've buried it under a full-scale EADEM brand takeover before I even get to my points. "Elevate with Eadem" with SEVEN product modules (spray, boost, capsule, patch, glow, sun-soak, mini essentials) is a lot of real estate for a single niche skincare brand I only sort of recognize, and I have to scroll past all of it to hit the actual Stacking Weekend banner again near the bottom.
- The top strip does have my 50% off and 500-point math sitting right there, which I appreciate on autopilot at this point — I've had it memorized since 9am. But burying a repeat of my own loyalty offer under a single-brand skincare catalog is a weird sequencing choice, and it makes this feel less like "here's your event" and more like "here's an ad we sold space for, oh and also stacking weekend still exists." Compare this to ILIA's "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" send — that one committed to ONE clear campaign idea and executed it. This is Sephora trying to do two campaigns in one email and neither wins.
- I'm not clicking this one. I've already acted on Stacking Weekend from the 9am send, and I have zero reason to scroll through seven Eadem SKUs I didn't ask about. Marking as read.

## What's working

- The Stacking Weekend ribbon up top still has my numbers dialed in — 50% off, 500 bonus points at $75, clean and legible.
- The EADEM section itself is visually cohesive — consistent product photography, consistent typography, feels like an actual brand story rather than a slapped-together banner.
- The 50% Off Select Beauty callout near the bottom (pink/blue gradient) at least reinforces the offer a second time before the footer, so if I scrolled fast I'd still catch it.

## What's weak

- Seven — SEVEN — EADEM modules stacked vertically before any other content is exhausting on mobile scroll, and EADEM isn't a brand I associate with "event" energy the way Charlotte Tilbury or Rare Beauty drops usually are.
- This is my fourth nearly-identical Stacking Weekend touch today and the offer hasn't escalated or changed at all — just re-skinned with a brand wrapper.
- No Rouge-tier callout anywhere in this send — Ulta at least attaches Diamond math ("$20 off" mechanics) to my name in their subject lines; here I'm just "a customer who gets 500 points."
- The "REAL LUXE" and "HABIT'S LATEST LINEUP" tiles near the bottom are crammed into a two-column grid that reads cluttered after the long EADEM scroll — feels like leftover inventory dumped at the end.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the EADEM takeover down to 2-3 hero products max, not seven — nobody needs the full catalog in an inbox email.
- 2. Lead with Stacking Weekend as the ONLY headline of this send, or don't send it as a fourth touch today at all — let EADEM have its own separate email another day.
- 3. Add my actual Rouge status/points balance instead of generic "500 bonus points" — Ulta's doing better on this personalization beat lately.
- 4. Cut the REAL LUXE / Habit's Latest Lineup grid entirely from this send — it's noise competing with two other stories already crammed in.
