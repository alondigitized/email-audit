# FYI! 3 for $30 balms is still on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 3-for-$30 offer I got on 11/12 ("Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!"), now with an "FYI!" subject and a "still on" label. I don't mind the reminder, because the math is still good. $30 for three is $10 a tube against $16 list, and the email says 38%, which is the same 37.5% rounded up. But it's a repeat. Nothing new here makes me act today instead of next week.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan, and the flavor grid works. It does a lot of the "which one do I pick?" work for me. But there's no deadline, so "still on" gives me no reason to hurry. Sephora would at least tell me when it ends. I'd click through to the grid, and I'd forward it to a friend who's a Balm Dotcom person. I wouldn't forward it as a deal alert.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "Get three balms for just $30!" and "save 38%". I can do the math in two seconds.
- The 3x3 flavor grid, sorted into Classics, Romantics and Fresh + Fruity, makes picking three easy. Birthday, Rose and Mango each look different.
- The models are varied, with different skin tones and close-up lip shots. It feels real and not stocky.
- The "clinically proven to moisturize for up to 12 hours" block gives me a reason to buy beyond the price.
- The layout is clean and uncluttered, with an obvious hierarchy.

## What's weak

- There's no end date anywhere. "Still on" says the deal exists, but I can't tell when it stops. That's weak next to Ulta's "Ends tonight!" and its code.
- It's a near-repeat of 11/12, so it feels like a resend and not a new beat.
- There are ten "Shop now" buttons. The copy is generic, and none says "Add to bag" or names the flavor.
- No loyalty angle, and no mention of whether the $30 is a bundle price or applies automatically. I can't tell if I need to build the bundle or if it's pre-set.
- The small gray flavor names and "Nourishing lip balm" captions are hard to read on a phone. The product images are big but the labels are tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday", to the hero and the subject. Urgency is the one thing missing.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, like "Build my 3 for $30", and explain the mechanics in one line (pick any 3, discount applies at checkout).
- 3. Make the grid buttons say "Add [flavor]" so each one is a direct add-to-bag.
- 4. Don't resend the same hero three days later. Lead with something new, like a "most-picked trio", a gift angle or a new flavor.
- 5. Add a line on gifting or stocking stuffers. It's mid-November and I'm already building my holiday list.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo/Klaviyo-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional template, sent from `eml.glossier.com` with Klaviyo click tracking (`ctrk.klclick.com`). The only confirmed defect is missing alt text on 15 images. The header warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, and the truncated HTML doesn't let me verify the body links or footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 links were probed and 29 were skipped, and the QA output lists no failures.
- The source is truncated before any `<a href>`, so I can't check redirect chains or tracking-wrapper consistency.
- `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7EAYEQ4FA4S7YG0YJGNXKVY_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` are invalid on a `<span>`. This looks like a Klaviyo-injected tracking artifact and is harmless in practice, but it is non-conformant markup.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7EAYEQ4FA4S7YG0YJGNXKVY`. It was flagged for missing alt text, which is trivial for a pixel but easy to fix with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 15 images.** All are `stripocdn.email/.../CABINET_*` assets except the tracking pixel:
  - `glossiersuperpackcarousel01_1_1.jpg`, `group_3972_3.jpg`, `group_3973_4.jpg` and `group_3987.jpg`
  - `glossiermjcfuturedewcarousel01_1_3_rGY.jpg` (twice), `glossierbdctriocarousel01_1_4_33Y.jpg` (twice) and `glossiermjcfuturedewcarousel01_1_2_sh6.jpg` (twice)
  - the remaining carousel images and `95339270_2980034135406876_2064437603473555456_n_1_`
- The repeated filenames suggest the same image appears in more than one `<img>` tag, probably the `.rollover-first` / `.rollover-second` hover pair. If the images carry product or offer information, the email loses it when images are blocked or read by a screen reader.
- The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but the head uses unclosed HTML5-style `<meta charset="utf-8">` tags. This is non-conformant but tolerated by mail clients.
- Good practice is present: `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a `<title>`, viewport meta, MSO conditional comments and a hidden preheader with padding characters.
