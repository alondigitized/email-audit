# We *never* do this...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that Glossier's "we never do this" is a hoodie email. It's the fourth Glossier send in four days, and I'm a beauty girl, so merch is the opposite of what I came for. After 11/21's "A gift for you: Crème de You" and 11/20's "Our biggest sale of the year is live!", I'd already clocked the 25% sale. This one wraps the same number around a sweatshirt.
- The hero is clean, and the pink hoodie with the 25% and 30% stickers is cute. But I'm doing the points math again and the stickers don't agree. "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" sits next to "30% OFF $100+*", and the header banner is cut off at "Get a free Crème de You when you spend $100". Sephora Insider would give me one clear number plus my tier perk. This gives me three offers and no member math.
- Would I click? Probably not, because I'm not buying a hoodie. I'd mark it read. I might forward it to a friend who loves merch, but I wouldn't send it as a beauty deal.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read. "Glossier Hoodies are 25% off!" lands in about a second, and the blue "Shop now" button is hard to miss.
- The pink hoodie against the blue stickers is on-brand and photographs well.
- The customer review ("lasts longer through wear and washes") gives real social proof and talks about quality.
- The "Permanent Collection" and "Limited edition" labels on the three-hoodie row give me a little urgency and tell me what's scarce.
- The Black Friday banner confirms this is part of the sale season, so the timing makes sense.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the email is skincare, makeup or fragrance, and I came for beauty. The subject's "We *never* do this..." sets up a big reveal that turns out to be a 25% hoodie sale. That's a bait-and-switch.
- The three offers fight each other: 25% off everything, 30% off $100+, and a free Crème de You at $100. There's no clear order or threshold math, and all of it carries asterisks.
- The top banner is cut off mid-sentence ("spend $100…"). That's the one concrete gift-with-purchase and I can't read the whole thing.
- Five "Shop now" buttons in a row is repetitive. "Shop now" on every button isn't specific, and none of them name a product.
- There's no loyalty or member benefit, no deadline and no end date. After four sends this week I've got no reason to act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with beauty. Put the gift with purchase and the 25% in the hero, and move the hoodies below as an add-on.
- 2. Fix the stacked offers. Pick one headline number and put the threshold in the button copy, like "Get 30% off $100+".
- 3. Unclip the top banner so the full Crème de You offer shows.
- 4. Name the products on the buttons ("Shop the Pink Hoodie") instead of repeating "Shop now".
- 5. Add a real end date, and space out the sends. Four in four days is too many.

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off hoodies + a free Crème de You`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100 and get a free Crème de You. Ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our pink embroidered hoodie is back at 25% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked send with sound baseline markup (`lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals, preheader). The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes on 6 images and a malformed tracking `<span>` in the body. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML was truncated before the footer and links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tracking element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7Z2R2VVEHDTKCR9BNNF2D4B_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`.
  - `href` and `rel="stylesheet"` are not valid on a `<span>`, so the element does nothing and is invalid markup.
  - It looks like a Klaviyo click-tracking rewrite hitting a stray attribute. It is likely harmless, but it is a template defect.
- **Link probing:** 12 material links were probed and 14 skipped. The QA output reports no broken, redirected or erroring links, so I'm not flagging any. The 14 skipped footer and social links were not verified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7Z2R2VVEHDTKCR9BNNF2D4B` is flagged for missing `alt`. For a tracking pixel the fix is `alt=""`, which is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 6 content images** (QA-confirmed, all on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`):
  - `frame_3985_1.jpg`
  - `reviewimageleftlarge_1.jpg`
  - `group_3973.jpg`
  - `group_3972.jpg`
  - `group_3974.jpg` (flagged twice, so it appears twice)
