# Up to 25% off, two gifts, and 2x points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction: the subject says "up to 25%" and the hero says "20% Off." I do points math for a living, so that mismatch bugs me right away. The banner at the top says "unlock 25% off sitewide* | join VIP for free." The big number is 20%, and 25% only shows up if I join VIP. The headline is also clipped by the model's head. "20% Off" runs straight through her hair and forehead. It's the same clipping problem I called out on 11/18's "25% off for VIPs starts now," and they haven't fixed it.
- The structure is good. Two gifts, a free complexion stick with any order and a free mini of choice at $100+, give me real things to calculate. The red VIP block spells out the member upside: 2x points and free shipping. The four product tiles near the bottom are weak, though. Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 shows up twice, in the left slot of row two as well as the right slot of row one. That looks like a template mistake.
- Would I click? Yes, probably "Shop the Sale," since I already know I'm stocking up on tints in the ILIA Black Friday window. Would I forward it? No. It's a solid promo, but nothing about it is surprising.

## What's working

- The two free gifts are clear. "Free complexion stick with any order" is a low-bar gift, and "free mini of choice w/ $100+" gives me a spend threshold to plan around.
- The red VIP block explains the member benefits plainly: 2x redeemable points, free shipping, and 25% off. That's the tier-program math I look for.
- "Shop the Sale" is a wide dark button that's easy to hit with a thumb.
- The proof badges on the product tiles (Allure Best of Beauty, 1% For the Planet) add credibility for the Limitless Lash Mascara and Eye Stylus Shadow Stick.
- Compared with ILIA's 11/16 "48 hours from now…" tease, this one gives me a number and a start date. That's a real improvement.

## What's weak

- Subject says "up to 25%," hero says "20% Off," and the top banner says "25% off sitewide*." Three different framings of the offer, and the asterisks make it feel like fine print.
- The "20% Off" headline is cropped by the model's head and hair. I can still read it, but it looks careless.
- Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40 appears in two of the four product slots. That looks like a bug, and it wastes a slot that could show another SKU.
- The product grid has no prices, no shades, and no reviews. Sephora's Black Friday sends show sale prices on tiles, and e.l.f. puts a "$10 OFF" burst right on the hero.
- The footer link "Click HERE for ILIA Promotion Details" is tiny, so the exclusions are hidden. For a sale with asterisks, that feels like buried terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the headline one offer. Either lead with "25% off for VIPs, 20% for everyone," or put the VIP number first and the guest number second. Don't let the subject, hero and banner each say something different.
- 2. Move the headline so the model's face doesn't cover it. A clean text band above the photo would fix the clipping.
- 3. Fix the duplicate Super Serum Skin Tint tile and replace it with a bestseller such as a lip or brow product. Add sale prices on the tiles.
- 4. Put the points math next to the VIP block, for example "VIP = 25% + 2x points + free shipping." Then I don't have to calculate it.
- 5. Put the key exclusions in plain text under the CTA instead of behind a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ILIA today, 25% with VIP + 2 free gifts`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: free complexion stick with any order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free VIP = 25% off, 2x points, free shipping. Mini of choice at $100+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skin Tint, Lash Mascara and more, all on sale. Sale ends soon.`
