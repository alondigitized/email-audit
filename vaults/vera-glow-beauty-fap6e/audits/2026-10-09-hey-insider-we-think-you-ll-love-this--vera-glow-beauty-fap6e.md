- "You have 0 points" in the header is wrong for me and undercuts the "personalized" claim.

- The email is long, with four or five different themes (Dieux, m.ph, holiday). The hierarchy gets flat after the Huda hero.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML namespaces and a responsive media query. QA found one hard failure (no plain-text alternative) and three warnings: missing alt text on two images, plus unverifiable List-Unsubscribe and authentication headers. The source was truncated, so body links and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. QA probed 12 of 55 material links and skipped 43, ranked by click likelihood, so about 78% of links are unverified.
- An open-tracking pixel is served from `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...`. It has no alt attribute. This is low-impact, but it is flagged by QA.
- Images are hosted on the ESP CDN, `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/...`. That is normal for Epsilon sends, with no defect evidenced.
- Click-wrapper and redirect behavior could not be assessed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` (Epsilon CDN) has no alt. This is a visible header or nav image, so with images blocked the header navigation disappears for screen readers and image-off clients.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` restricts pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. The space makes the directive invalid. It has little effect in email but signals template sloppiness.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive. Minor.
- **Positive:** The Outlook/MSO resets, `x-apple-data-detectors` handling and `#outlook a` fixes are present. The DOCTYPE and VML namespaces are correct for Outlook.
- **Markup style:** Mobile rules use attribute selectors such as `*[class=hide]`. Some clients, such as Gmail on non-Google accounts, strip or ignore these. The class-selector fallback (`.hide`) is present, so this is not an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source.
- The subject "Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this." uses a segment label rather than a name token. There is no defect, and no first-name personalization.
- The truncated body could not be checked for tokens further down.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The message appears to be HTML-only (no `text/plain` alternative). This increases spam-filter risk and degrades accessibility for text-only clients.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header** was captured. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven sender defect.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** was captured, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify at the source.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, and alignment cannot be confirmed.
- **Footer:** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link were not visible in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters or landing URLs were visible in the truncated source or QA output.
- None of the 12 probed links reported redirect-chain or landing-page mismatches.
- Continuity could not be assessed. This is not evidence of a problem.

2. **Verify List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** against the raw message at the receiving mailbox, not the relay copy, before treating them as sender defects. If they are truly absent, add both (RFC 8058).
3. **Capture Authentication-Results** from the original delivery to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
4. **Add descriptive alt text** to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
