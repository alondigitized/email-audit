# These picks are everywhere
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Nine emails in eleven days. Nine. I've been counting since the Stacking Weekend marathon kicked off, and this is the first send post-blitz that drops the percentage sign entirely — which, okay, I respect the editorial pivot. "Hot on Social" as a theme after eight countdown emails is Sephora waving their hands like *okay but look at the products though.* I see the play.
- The email is genuinely dense — Patrick Starrr ONE SIZE is getting a feature (inclusive shade range, appreciated), Rhode has new drops (my thumb hovered the second I spotted it), and there's a 4X Points offer buried mid-scroll. That last part is the one that should have led the email. I calendar Sephora Insider events. Points math is my religion. They hid the altar behind three product grids and a "Bundled Beauty" module and expected me to find it.
- I'd click on Rhode. I'd scroll to the 4X Points section to do the math. I would not forward this to anyone — it doesn't have a sharp-enough angle to share. Mark as read after the Rhode tap.

## What's working

- **Rhode new launches surfaced** — Hailey Bieber's brand is in its cultural moment right now and Sephora put it in the email; that's the right read on what my age bracket is watching
- **4X Points offer visible mid-email** — it's buried, but it's there, and for a Rouge member that's enough signal to keep scrolling
- **Patrick Starrr ONE SIZE shade-range language** — calling out inclusive coverage explicitly is the kind of product detail that makes me trust the edit
- **Clean render, cohesive visual** — the pink gradient hero is very Sephora: nothing broken, no overlapping text, aesthetically tight
- **"Hot on Social" framing is current** — it's not stale promo language; it tracks with how the algorithm-aware beauty consumer actually discovers products

## What's weak

- **4X Points buried deep** — as someone who does the points math obsessively, this needed to be the second thing I see, not something I find by accident after three product grids
- **"Get a Mini" top banner is vague** — what mini, for what purchase threshold? "SHOP TO REDEEM" with no qualifying amount visible makes me hesitate before clicking; Drunk Elephant's "Time to come clean" (9/10) never made me guess the hook
- **Email is too long, too many stories** — Patrick Starrr launch, social picks, Rhode drops, 4X Points, Bundled Beauty, Spot It Shop It — this is five separate emails wearing a trench coat. ILIA's Wimbledon send (8/10) held a single thread start to finish; this one frays before the fold
- **Zero urgency signals** — after eight consecutive countdown emails, pivoting to no deadline whatsoever is whiplash. Even "4X Points ends Sunday" would give me a reason to act now instead of tab-and-forget

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move 4X Points above the fold** — this is the offer that actually moves Rouge and VIB members to action; hiding it mid-scroll is a direct conversion miss
- 2. **Clarify the mini offer inline** — "Free Mini with $75+ purchase" right there in the banner, no click required to understand the mechanic; Sally Beauty's "Last day for 20% off" (9/10) never made me decode the offer
- 3. **Pick one editorial spine** — Patrick Starrr OR Rhode OR the points event; all three dilutes all three
- 4. **Add a credible deadline to the 4X Points module** — "Ends [date]" does real work for loyalty-obsessed members who'd otherwise assume it runs all month
- 5. **Make product prices legible at thumbnail size** — I cannot read most of the pricing in the product grid at this render scale, which makes the points math impossible before I click

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** — confirmed in source:
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
This blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4). Should be removed or replaced with `maximum-scale=5`.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied to every element** — confirmed:
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
This overrides the OS/browser text-size accessibility setting for all elements. Acceptable on `body` to prevent iOS auto-inflate, but applying it globally suppresses user-initiated zoom adjustments throughout the email.

**`<title>Sephora</title>`** — non-descriptive. Screen readers read the `<title>` when the email opens in a browser; a subject-aligned value (e.g., "These picks are everywhere — Sephora") improves accessibility.

**`meta name="robots" content="no index"`** — `no index` (two words) is not a recognized directive; correct syntax is `noindex`. Harmless in email context but indicates copy-paste error in the shell template.
