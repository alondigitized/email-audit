# Complete Your Exagger-Eyes Look With FREE Minis 🪄
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Email twelve. I'm keeping count, and Charlotte Tilbury is keeping up. But here's what this one does that the previous eleven mostly didn't: it shifts the narrative from "give us money, save money" to "give us money, *get something*." The 20% off era is clearly over, and the brand is doing the smart beauty pivot — GWP. Mini Magic Cream, mini mascara, the three-step routine laid out like a lesson. I genuinely appreciate the structure here. It feels like an email from a brand that has an actual editorial point of view, not just a CMS template someone hit "schedule" on.
- That said — I'm twelve emails deep in twelve days. I did the math the second I opened it: $130 to unlock the free minis. That threshold is Charlotte Tilbury standard operating procedure, and as a Rouge-tier spender I know the game. But burying "$130" in a small body copy paragraph while the hero screams "2 Free Mini Gifts!" is the kind of move that makes me side-eye a brand. Sephora usually puts the qualifying spend *in* the hero text. CT is hiding it below the fold.
- Would I click? Yes — specifically for the Step 2 "NEW!" eyeshadow sticks, not the GWP minis. Would I forward? Actually yes — the 3-step layout is screenshot-friendly, easy to text to a friend who's been eyeing the CT lineup. Mark as read? Not today.

## What's working

- The 3-step routine framework (Prime + Moisturize → Sculpt + Highlight → Volumize + Maximize) is genuinely clever — it's selling a system, not just a product
- The FREE!/NEW! badge placement is smart: FREE on steps 1 and 3, NEW on step 2, so the paid product is sandwiched between the freebies
- Dual CTAs give me two entry points depending on my motivation — "Shop Eyeshadow Sticks" if I'm launch-curious, "Get Your Free Gifts" if I'm deal-motivated
- Clean layout with zero render bugs — no broken images, no overlapping text, everything crisp
- The GWP pivot itself is strategically sound after nine consecutive discount emails — maintains price integrity better than a tenth "20% off" blast

## What's weak

- "$130" spend threshold is buried in body paragraph copy, not the hero — after the big "2 Free Mini Gifts!" it feels like fine print bait
- No deadline or urgency signal anywhere in the email — coming off nine "ENDS TONIGHT" sends, the sudden absence of any time constraint makes this feel low-stakes; Drunk Elephant's GWP emails at least run "while supplies last"
- Twelve emails in twelve days is a lot; no segmentation visible here — there's zero acknowledgment that I might be a customer who already spent during the 20% off period and doesn't need a GWP nudge
- The bottom "Discover the Magic of CharlotteTilbury.com + App" block feels like it belongs to a different email entirely — the app promo breaks the eye-routine narrative and lands with a thud
- No personalization whatsoever — ILIA's Friends & Family finale (May 28) at least *felt* like it was addressed to a known customer; this reads like a cold broadcast

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the $130 threshold into the hero** — the hero should read "Spend $130 and get 2 Free Minis" not just "2 Free Mini Gifts!" The threshold is the whole mechanic; hiding it erodes trust
- 2. **Add a supply or time qualifier** — "While minis last" or "Offer ends June 2" costs nothing and adds conversion urgency without going back to the countdown-timer era
- 3. **Remove or delay the app promo section** — it kills the routine narrative; save it for a standalone send or relegate to the footer fine print
- 4. **Suppress this send for recent purchasers** — if I just spent $130+ during the 20% off sale, emailing me about a $130 GWP threshold feels tone-deaf; segment it out
- 5. **Introduce tier recognition** — even one line like "As a Charlotte Tilbury member, your minis are reserved" would make this feel targeted instead of blasted
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $130, unlock mini Magic Cream + Lash Mascara`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Exagger-Eyes kit is almost complete — 2 free minis inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Mini Magic Cream and mini mascara, free when you spend $130. While minis last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The full Exagger-Eyes routine — now with 2 gifted minis at $130+.`
