## What's working

- The hero is finally unmissable: "20% off sitewide" with the code spelled out. Sephora and Ulta usually bury this kind of thing, and Sally didn't.
- The red banner at the top says stores are closed Thanksgiving and shop online 24/7. That's useful, honest, and timely.
- The 20% full-size fragrance tile and the "Buy one, get one 50% off" tile are readable and have clear offers.
- The "Shop holiday gifts from $5" tile gives me an easy low-cost stocking-stuffer entry point.
- The Klarna strip and the shipping and pickup icons make the payment and delivery options obvious.

## What's weak

- I see "Ends 11/29" only in the tiny red strip, not in the hero. There's no urgency where my eye lands.
- The product grid is mostly hair color and tools. Nothing is skincare or makeup, and nothing is aimed at someone my age shopping beauty prestige.
- The Sally Beauty Rewards points aren't mentioned at all. Sephora Beauty Insider and Ulta emails always remind me of tier perks, and Sally sends zero member math.
- The legal block is dense, and the exclusions (Happy Birthday Co., clearance, no combining) are tiny. I'd feel a little nervous the code won't apply to my cart.
- The model hero photo is pretty, but it doesn't show a product, so it's decoration instead of a reason to buy.

## Recommendations

- 2. Swap the model photo for a hero product, so the picture sells something.
- 3. Lead the grid with the beauty categories that compete with Ulta 21 Days: skincare, makeup, and fragrance.
- 4. Clarify the exclusions near the code, in plain language.
- 5. Don't undercut your own 30% by sending a lower offer with no explanation. Say why this is the best code to use right now.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Sally Beauty's "Get 20% off sitewide" email is an XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with mobile media queries at `max-width: 499px`. The QA run passed 57% of checks, with 0 hard failures. The confirmed issues are an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in a tracking pixel, a non-HTTPS pixel, and a subscriber email address embedded in image URLs. The compliance headers couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the cache-busting parameter is missing or invalid. This is a confirmed defect in the template or ESP.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is loaded over plain HTTP. Clients and proxies may block it, and it exposes the request in transit.
- **Link probing:** 56 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect chains and breakage are unverified, so this is a QA coverage gap and not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixel load:** the email loads pixels from several third-party vendors:
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com`
  - `t.myvisualiq.net`
  - `login.dotomi.com`
  - `sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com`

  Each is an extra request and a possible blocking point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes:** QA flagged these images:
  - Four Evergage dynamic-content images: `.../campaign/syxLN/4jLS1/{1..4}/summary.png`. These look like real content modules, not pixels, and would be blank for screen readers and with images off.
  - Two images with an empty `src` and no alt.
  - Tracking pixels (`003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `impression_pixel`, `UCMController`). A missing alt matters less on these, but `alt=""` is still the correct markup.
- **Empty `src`:** two flagged images have `src:` empty (a blank string). These are broken image references, and some clients show a broken-image icon.
- **Font fallback:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It will fall back to Helvetica everywhere. That is harmless, but the declared font never loads.
- **Markup nit:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` isn't self-closed under an XHTML doctype. This is cosmetic and renders fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Subscriber email in image URLs:** the Evergage image URLs carry `userId=rae-l@ete...` (truncated in the QA output). The recipient's email address is in a query string, so it ends up in proxy logs, referrer data and third-party vendor logs.
- **Contact ID in a pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../2338540/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` contains what looks like a Salesforce contact ID. This is normal for tracking, but it is a persistent identifier.
- **Unresolved token:** `[CACHEBUSTER]` is the only unresolved placeholder visible in the captured data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both are reported missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. It needs a check against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note again attributes this to the relay. The sender domain `em.sallybeauty.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** the supplied HTML is truncated before the footer, so I can't confirm either. I'm not flagging them as missing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data. The 56 tracking links were skipped, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs weren't resolved. The "20% off sitewide" offer's landing page and promo-code handling are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the MyVisualIQ pixel, or remove the pixel.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixel to `https://`, or drop it if the vendor doesn't support TLS.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four Evergage `summary.png` content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the tracking pixels.
4. Fix or remove the two empty-`src` images.
5. Pass an opaque hashed ID to Evergage in place of the raw email in `userId=`.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`. The relay-stripping explanation is only a hypothesis.
7. Resolve the 56 skipped tracking links with a redirect-following probe to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
8. Check the untruncated footer for the unsubscribe link and physical address.
