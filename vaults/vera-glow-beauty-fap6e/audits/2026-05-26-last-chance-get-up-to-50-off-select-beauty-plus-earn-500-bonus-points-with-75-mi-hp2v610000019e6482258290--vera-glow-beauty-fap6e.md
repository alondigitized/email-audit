# LAST CHANCE 🥺 Get up to 50% off select beauty—plus earn 500 bonus points with $75 min. spend.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Full transparency: I've been tracking every single Sephora Stacking Weekend email since the May 22 double-send, so walking into this "LAST CHANCE" email on day five of a campaign I already have memorized is a very different experience than a cold open. That said, I opened it anyway — because the 50%-plus-500-bonus-points stack on a $75 minimum is the kind of math I run in my head even in my sleep as a Rouge member. The hero section does exactly what a final-day email needs to do: the offer is undeniable, the deadline is earned, and the visual hierarchy above the fold is crisp.
- Below the fold, though, Sephora just... kept going. Color Wow launch content. "Just in from Dye." A Limited-Edition Rhode drop. A store locator. A Beauty Insider section. A barcode. This is four different email briefs crammed into one last-chance send, and by the time I scrolled to the bottom I had to remind myself I was here for a sale.
- I'd click — I had this in my calendar — but I would not forward this to a friend. If someone texted me "did you see the Sephora email?" I'd link them straight to the sale page and save them the scroll.

## What's working

- **Hero math is instant**: 50% off + 500 bonus points for a $75 spend, surfaced visually without having to read a sentence — perfect for a last-day push when I'm deciding in two seconds
- **"ENDS TODAY!" deadline feels earned**: After four Stacking Weekend emails, this urgency is 100% credible — not manufactured panic like Charlotte Tilbury's all-zeros countdown timer debacle
- **Rhode Limited-Edition callout**: Smart pull for my age cohort — Hailey Bieber's brand has real millennial cultural weight, and it signals Sephora knows who they're emailing
- **Beauty Insider barcode in the footer**: Genuinely useful if I'm doing a last-day in-store run; functional loyalty integration done right
- **Clean render**: No broken images, no overlapping text — simple bar but it clears it, especially compared to CT's broken timer

## What's weak

- **Below-fold chaos**: New launches, store locator, and a Rhode section all competing with "LAST CHANCE sale" in a single email — too many messages, zero priority ranking
- **Product thumbnails are microscopic**: The "Must-Haves" grid tiles are too small to identify products on a phone; Ulta's last-day tiered spend-and-save emails surface two or three hero products at full legibility — this is a tappable mess
- **Subject line is nearly 100 characters**: Gets eviscerated on mobile; "LAST CHANCE 🥺" is doing all the work and everything after the em dash is probably cut off in most inboxes
- **Zero personalization after five emails**: ILIA's Friends & Family sends were layering personalized gift recommendations by this point in their campaign; Sephora is still mass-blasting an anonymous product grid after four sends to me specifically
- **New arrivals undercut the urgency**: Mixing "just launched" creative into a sale-deadline email sends conflicting signals about what action I'm supposed to take

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut below-fold sections by half**: One sale block, one set of hero products with legible pricing, one loyalty CTA. The Rhode drop and Color Wow launch are separate emails — Sephora has the sends to spare.
- 2. **Bigger product cards in the Must-Haves grid**: Four products clearly shown with price + percent-off overlay beats eight unreadable thumbnails every time.
- 3. **Trim the subject** — it's doing three jobs:
- **Subject Alt A:** `LAST CHANCE: 50% off + 500 bonus points ends today 🥺`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: stack 50% off AND 500 bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, earn 500 points on top of 50% off. Stacking Weekend ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's never been this cheap. Ends today — no extensions.`
- 4. **Surface one personalized product**: One "we think you'd love this at 50% off" above the sale grid would make this feel like Sephora knows me — e.l.f.'s Jelly Pop free-gift send nailed this; specific product, specific moment, the click felt obvious.
- 5. **Rhode gets its own email**: A Limited-Edition collab deserves full creative, not a tertiary section inside a last-chance clearance send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta — confirmed issue**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents user-initiated pinch-zoom. WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 failure. Replace with `initial-scale=1` only.

**Blanket `text-size-adjust: none` — confirmed issue**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applied to virtually every element including `body`. Overrides OS-level text scaling for low-vision users. Should be scoped to specific components (e.g., heading cells) or removed entirely in favor of `100%`.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` — confirmed issue**
```html
<html xmlns="..." xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` present. Screen readers cannot determine the document language. WCAG 2.5 SC 3.1.1 failure.

**Malformed robots meta value**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
Correct value is `noindex` (no space). Inconsequential in email rendering contexts, but technically invalid.

**Generic `<title>`**
```html
<title>Sephora</title>
```
VoiceOver and NVDA announce document title on load. A descriptive title (e.g., "Last Chance Sale — Up to 50% Off | Sephora") is preferred.

**`.hide` / `.hide_img` mobile suppression — needs full source**
```css
*[class=hide], .hide { display: none !important; ... }
*[class=hide_img], .hide_img { display: none !important; }
```
Pattern is standard, but if applied to non-decorative content without a visible alternative, it removes content from screen readers on mobile. Verify which elements carry these classes.
