# So hot right now 💯
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- Ugh, we're doing this again. I open expecting "hot right now" to mean an actual trend story and instead I get the exact same nine-product waterfall I already side-eyed on 7/31 for "Hot On Social" — LED mask, hair tool, then a wall of eight fragrance/skincare/makeup tiles with no thread connecting them. I scrolled through Shark Beauty, ViktorRolf, Kérastase, Farmacy, MAC, NEST, Sephora Collection, Prada, and Laneige and came away with zero point of view on what Sephora actually wants me to buy.
- The one thing that stopped my thumb was the 4X points on foundation/concealer banner up top with a real code and a real deadline (8/5) — that's the kind of math I actually do. The Jo Malone mini-with-$50-purchase banner is cute too. But then it's three stacked banners before I even hit the "BEAUTY FOR YOU" hero, and the hero itself is just... a product grid with no story.
- Compare this to Sephora's own 8/1 "Beauty Insider Celebration" send, which I gave an 8 — that one had one clear hero, one clear campaign badge, one clear reason to open. This is the opposite: no hierarchy, no curation point of view, just merchandising sprawl. I'd mark this read and move on. Not forwarding it — there's nothing here worth telling a friend about.

## What's working

- The 4X points on foundation/concealer banner is a real, dated offer (ends 8/5) with an actual code — that's the loyalty math I care about.
- The Jo Malone Sea Salt & Bergamot mini-with-purchase is a genuinely nice touch, and it's clearly labeled "COMPLIMENTARY" with a code.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at least reminds me where I stand, even if it's buried at the footer.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, everything loads and lines up.

## What's weak

- This is the same nine-to-ten-product waterfall structure as the 7/31 "Hot On Social" send — same format, different SKUs, same fatigue.
- No hierarchy: three separate banner offers (points, delivery, GWP) stacked before the actual hero, so my eye doesn't know where to land.
- Zero curation logic connecting a $349 LED mask to a $16 lip stain to a Prada fragrance — it reads like a warehouse dump, not a "trending picks" edit.
- No models, no faces, no demographic signal anywhere — I can't tell if this was built for me or for anyone.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section buries a genuinely interesting K18 scalp serum early-access drop next to a vague "mystery reward" — both deserved their own send, not a two-up tile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero and build the whole email around it — either the Shark Beauty device launch or the K18 early access, not both plus eight unrelated SKUs. Drunk Elephant's "Something big..." teaser proved restraint reads as premium; this is the opposite instinct.
- 2. Cut the product grid to 3-4 SKUs max with an actual editorial thread ("your trending picks this week"), the way ILIA's "Clean Beauty 101" send stayed tightly themed around back-to-school.
- 3. Move the points offer and Beauty Insider tier bar up near the hero — don't make me scroll to the footer to see my own tier status.
- 4. Give the "mystery reward" its own moment with real stakes (what are the possible rewards?) instead of a vague "GET 1 OF 5" tile competing with K18 for attention.
- 5. Stop reusing the waterfall format two sends in a row — even Ulta's repetitive 20%-off code (three sends running since 7/26) is more consistent than Sephora flip-flopping between "curated hero" (8/1) and "sprawl" (7/31, now this one).
