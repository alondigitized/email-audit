# Now you see it: 25% off
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is email number SIX in the Drunk Elephant Friends & Family campaign and I am officially keeping a tally in a spreadsheet at this point. Subject line "Now you see it: 25% off" — cute, I see the wink, the "now you see it" thing is doing the magician bit because the sale is *visibly* still happening and they're reminding me before it disappears. Tuesday F&F launch got a 9 from me, the Friday "Sup with the 25% off?" got an 8, and now Saturday we're back with another reminder. I'm not opening this with fresh eyes anymore.
- That said — the email itself is *competent*. The hero is clean, "25% off SITEWIDE" is huge, the product photography is doing its job, and the "TIME TO STOCK UP. Everything is 25% off!" middle section is finally giving me a clean grid of specific SKUs with prices, which is what I actually wanted on send #2. Bright Drops, B-Hydra, Lala, T.L.C. Sukari — these are the heroes I'd cart.
- Would I click? Honestly, I already bought on launch day, so no. Would I forward? No, my group chat is *done* with Drunk Elephant for this week. Mark as read? Yeah. If I hadn't pulled the trigger Tuesday this would be the one that finally cracked me — but the brand has earned diminishing returns on volume the same way Charlotte Tilbury did at email seven and ILIA did at eleven.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — "25% OFF SITEWIDE" in display type, no asterisks visible above the fold, the offer is the offer.
- "This way to the friend zone" tagline is on-brand DE silliness and lands better than yesterday's enchilada.
- The "TIME TO STOCK UP" product grid with visible prices ($38, $19.50, $35, $32) is the move I wished they'd led with on email #2 — Sephora's "Save up to 50%" grid does this exact thing and it's effective.
- "Spend $100+ and we'll throw in a free Hydro and the Bright Kit" — the GWT threshold is concrete and the reward is a kit, which is the right currency for a stock-up email.
- Visual hierarchy actually breathes — pastel pink, mint green, peach color blocks separating the routine modules (Future's Looking Bright / Mask Me Anything / Stock Up) makes this scannable.

## What's weak

- This is the SIXTH email in the F&F campaign and there's zero acknowledgment that I might already be a buyer — no "thanks for shopping" segmentation, no "did you forget something?" cart-recovery tone. Sephora's loyalty-aware sends are doing this better right now.
- The "Now you see it" subject is cute but doesn't add new information — F&F is in week one, nothing is "disappearing" with urgency I can feel; no countdown, no end-date visible above the fold.
- No Sephora-versus-direct math anywhere — as a Rouge, I'm sitting here doing the silent calculation of "25% off here vs 4% back + free 2-day at Sephora" and DE never addresses it.
- Three different routine modules stacked (Bright, Mask, Stock Up) makes the email *long* — I'm scrolling past content I already saw in the Tuesday and Wednesday sends.
- The "FULL SIZE GIFT" sticker on the mask module is doing a lot of visual work but I can't tell from the layout if that's tied to the $100 GWT or a separate offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment the sixth send.** If I already bought, this should be a "thanks + here's what you missed" email, not the same evergreen grid. Glossier's reminder send respected the previous opener; this doesn't.
- 2. **Put the end date in the hero.** "Ends Tuesday" or a countdown next to "25% OFF SITEWIDE" — by send six, urgency has to be earned, not implied by the subject pun.
- 3. **Lead with the GWT, not the discount.** By now I know it's 25% off. "Spend $100, get a free Bright Kit + Hydro" is the *new* information — make that the hero on the late-cycle sends.
- 4. **Cut the email in half.** Pick ONE module (the stock-up grid) and ship it. Three routine blocks plus the grid is asking for a scroll I won't give you on send six.
- 5. **Add the Sephora math.** "Same 25% you'd wait all year for at Rouge — but direct, today" — name the competitor in your head and disarm it.
