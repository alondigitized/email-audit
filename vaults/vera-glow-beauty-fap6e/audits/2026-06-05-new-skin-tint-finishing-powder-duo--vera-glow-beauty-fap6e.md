# NEW Skin Tint + Finishing Powder Duo
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Twenty-two. That's where I'm at with ILIA this month. And yet — okay, I have to be honest — this one actually made me stop. The "soften shine—**not** your summer glow" hero line is clever in a way that feels earned after sitting through the June 3rd ingredient breakdown for the Finishing Powder and the June 2nd launch email. Bundling the Super Serum Skin Tint and the Soft Focus Finishing Powder as a duo at 15% off is the kind of points-math move I appreciate: two products I've already been warmed up on, now with a bundle incentive. The VIP points banner at the top is doing exactly what it should.
- That said — the asterisks are multiplying. There's a `*` on the VIP points banner AND a `*` on the 15% off button, and I can't read the fine print from here. Two asterisks on two offers in a single email is the kind of thing that makes me hover before clicking. Compare this to e.l.f.'s June 5th "$7 Soft Glam Cream Bronzer" send, which had zero asterisk friction — the price was the price, period. ILIA's offer is good; the fine print hedging undercuts it.
- Still: the two-step product education layout is clean, the model photography is editorial-prestige and matches the brand, and the ingredient callouts (SPF 40, non-comedogenic, fragrance-free) speak directly to what I check before buying a complexion product. I'd click. I wouldn't forward — this is very "for me specifically," not a shareable find.

## What's working

- **Hero copy lands**: "soften shine—not your summer glow" is a clean subversion that's actually on-topic in June, and it plays perfectly off the Soft Focus Finishing Powder they've been building up since June 2nd.
- **VIP points banner is front-and-center**: +200 points with any complexion order is at the very top — as a Rouge/Diamond cross-shopper I'm always doing this math, and putting it above the fold is correct.
- **15% off as bundle incentive is smart**: Two products I'm already warmed up on; this feels like a logical step-up from the launch and ingredient emails.
- **Ingredient/claim callouts in Step 2 are exactly right**: "Safe for sensitive skin," "non-comedogenic," "fragrance or talc free" — that's my actual purchase checklist for a finishing powder.
- **Editorial model photography is prestige-tier**: Close-up skin textures on what reads as a WOC model reinforce the shade-range and skin-quality positioning ILIA keeps hitting.

## What's weak

- **Double asterisk problem**: Both the VIP points and the 15% off carry `*` — two asterisks, two sets of conditions, no visible explanation. Charlotte Tilbury's June email had fine print too but at least the headline offer was unqualified.
- **No urgency anywhere**: No "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing. The 15% off floats in a time vacuum. Sally Beauty's "Brighten Up for Summer" at least had date-specific event framing.
- **No personalization**: "Hi there" energy. After 22 emails, they should know me by name and probably my shade.
- **Cadence fatigue is real**: By the third complexion-launch-related email in four days, the signal-to-noise is degrading. Glossier's Next Top Dog reunion felt *special* because it broke pattern. This doesn't.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill one asterisk** — either the VIP points OR the 15% off needs to lose the `*`. If both have conditions, consolidate them into one clearly visible line under the CTA. Asterisk stacking signals "this isn't as good as it sounds."
- 2. **Add a real deadline** — "15% off through Sunday" or "offer ends June 8" costs nothing and converts more. Time-bounded urgency is the single biggest missing signal here.
- 3. **Personalize the opener** — "Your summer do-list, [First Name]:" is one merge tag away from feeling curated vs. broadcast.
- 4. **Anchor the bundle price** — "Skin Tint ($58) + Finishing Powder ($44) — now $91 together" makes the 15% tangible without a calculator.
- 5. **Give the hero image more product drama** — the compact photo is nice but the Skin Tint bottle doesn't really appear prominently; show both products side by side.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your duo, 15% off: Skin Tint + Finishing Powder`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Glow now, shine never — the duo is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bundle the launch week favorites + earn 200 VIP points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `SPF, glow, shine control — 15% off through Sunday only.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**External font imports will fail silently in most clients:**
```css

@import url(https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Montserrat&display=swap);
```

Fallback chain `"Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif` is appropriate, but the visual divergence may be significant if the layout relies on Montserrat's metrics.
