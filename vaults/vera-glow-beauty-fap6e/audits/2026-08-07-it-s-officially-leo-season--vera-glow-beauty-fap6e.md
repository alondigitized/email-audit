- There is no offer, no percentage off, no GWP, no new launch — nothing that requires me to act today. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off, ENDS TODAY" energy or even Glossier's own GWP reminder from 8/3 — at least that had a mechanic.

- 2. Swap at least one staff testimonial for a real customer review or UGC — right now it reads as internal culture content, not proof I'd trust to buy on.
