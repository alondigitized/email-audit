# Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off⁺ one qualifying item just landed in your lap! Use code SCORE Terms & excl. apply
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "landed in your lap" — cute, but let's not pretend Ulta didn't send me this exact 20%-off-one-item mechanic before, just with different flavor text on it. The barcode-in-book hero is genuinely well-shot (very "vacation reading, but make it a coupon"), and stacking a scroll of celebrity-fronted product — Megan Thee Stallion, Ariana Grande, Khloe Kardashian, Rare Beauty — under one 20%-off umbrella is smart because it lets me self-select into whatever I'm actually shopping for that week. But then I hit the Rewards footer block and it greets me with "Hi, Rae." My name is not Rae. That's not a nitpick, that's Ulta telling me they don't actually know who's reading this.
- Compare this to Sephora's Daily Deals send, which I scored a 9 because the offer was dead simple and up top with zero fine-print friction. This one buries "one qualifying item" and "excl. apply" language right into the hero itself, which means the 20% off is doing a lot less than it looks like once you actually try to use it on, say, the Charlotte Tilbury highlighter and the Kayali fragrance in the same cart. And after three near-identical "20% off everything online" Rewards-join emails from Ulta in the same week back in June, this reads like the same lever pulled again with a different coupon code.
- Verdict: I'd scroll it on the train, screenshot the barcode for later, and not click through right now. I would not forward this — there's no single hero product interesting enough to text a friend about.

## What's working

- The barcode-in-a-book hero is a genuinely nice visual, feels more editorial than the usual pink banner blast.
- 20% off applying to prestige brands *and* fragrance is a real headline — Ulta usually excludes fragrance, so calling that out matters to someone like me who buys Kayali and Khloe Kardashian scents.
- The product grid is dense with names I actually track — Rare Beauty, Fenty Skin, Charlotte Tilbury — so it reads like it knows my brand roster even if it doesn't know my name.
- "5X points on select Conscious Beauty Clean ingredient brands" is a real loyalty hook for me since I'm doing the points math on every Diamond-tier purchase anyway.

## What's weak

- "Hi, Rae" in the Rewards footer — that's flatly the wrong name, and it undercuts every "you" and "your lap" in the hero copy above it.
- "One qualifying item" is a real limiter dressed up as a big splashy 20% — I have to pick ONE thing out of forty products shown, which feels like bait after the visual generosity of the grid.
- No urgency or expiration anywhere I can see — no "ends tonight," no date — so there's zero reason to act today versus next week.
- This is the same 20%-off-with-a-code play I've now seen from Ulta multiple times this summer (June 23, twice on the 26th) — just a new code (SCORE) swapped in for the old one.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the name token — "Hi, Rae" going out to anyone who isn't Rae is a trust-breaker, not a nice-to-have fix.
- 2. Lead with the exclusion clearly in-hero ("one item, your choice") instead of letting me assume the whole cart gets 20% off — honesty up front beats disappointment at checkout.
- 3. Add a real deadline. Ulta's own June sends proved urgency works for me ("ends tonight" got an 8); this one has none.
- 4. Cut the grid down or organize it by category (skincare / makeup / fragrance) — forty unlabeled products is a lot of scroll for one usable code.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 20% off code (pick your favorite prestige pick) ✨`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fragrance is IN on this 20% off code — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One item, 20% off — Charlotte Tilbury to Rare Beauty, it's your call.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Code SCORE unlocks 20% off, fragrance included — through Sunday.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
