# Holiday glow, without the gray
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a gray-coverage hair color email, and I'm 29 with no grays to cover. "100% gray coverage" plus a model in her 40s or 50s told me within two seconds that it isn't for me. It's also the sixth Sally send I've audited in about two weeks. After "A gift for the guys" (10/20), the Rewards pitches on 10/17 and 10/19, and the Halloween nails email on 10/16, this one is at least the first hair-color email.
- The execution is clean. The hero is a big readable headline, there's one dark Shop now button, and the Buy one, get one 50% off Inspired by Nature by ion™ block is a real, specific deal. But the pink banner at the top still asks me to "sign up" for Sally Rewards ($5 off $25), which I've been pitched on four times already. The red bar above the hero is a different offer ($10 off $50 or $20 off $75, code SPOOKY, ends 10/27). So there are three offers stacked above and below one hero, and the BOGO has a different deadline (10/24). I wouldn't click, I wouldn't forward it, and I'd mark it read.

## What's working

- The hero headline is big and legible on a phone, and the single dark "Shop now" button is unmissable.
- The BOGO 50% block is concrete. It names the product line and the deadline (Ends October 24), so I know exactly what I'd get. That's clearer than the vague percent-off sends I get from Ulta.
- The Klarna strip, free shipping at $40+ and 2 Hour Delivery icons are useful trust and convenience cues.
- The product shot of the two ion tubes is clean, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The "gray coverage" message and the mature model don't match me at all. Sephora would segment me out of this, and Sally clearly didn't.
- The Sally Rewards banner at the top says "sign up" again. I've already seen that $5 off $25 pitch on 10/17 and 10/19, so it reads as a stranger email.
- I count three competing offers with three deadlines: SPOOKY ends 10/27, the BOGO ends 10/24, and the legal text says 10/31. The SPOOKY code is also stale Halloween language in an email about holiday hair.
- The legal fine print is long, and the Rewards terms (5-business-day delay, 30-day expiry) are what I'd have to do points math on. None of it is visible up top.
- There's no loyalty tie-in for existing members, and no points or tier math at all. Ulta's "21 Days of Beauty" and Sephora Insider at least reward me for being a member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this. Send it to people who've bought hair color, and don't send it to a Rewards member who's browsing nails and makeup.
- 2. Pick one offer. Lead with the BOGO 50% and drop the SPOOKY bar and the Rewards banner. For existing members, swap the Rewards banner for a points-multiplier line.
- 3. Put the deadline on the hero button and make the CTA specific, like "Shop BOGO hair color".
- 4. Show a few shades or before/after results under the hero instead of a single product block.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, a mobile `@media (max-width: 499px)` block and Open Sans web fonts. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings). The confirmed defects are in the third-party tracking pixels, and the header warnings are unverified because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so body links, UTM parameters and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **48 tracking links were not probed.** The check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Unsubstituted macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&ao=883…`. The literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` was never replaced, so the cache-buster is ineffective. Clients may cache the request and under-count impressions. Some clients or proxies may also reject the unencoded brackets.
- **Mixed content:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990…` is an HTTP pixel and may be blocked or rewritten by image proxies. Where it is blocked, the Dotomi/Epsilon sync will not fire.
- **Stacked third-party pixels:** `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-…/2327825/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `myvisualiq` and `dotomi` all load in one message. Each adds an external request, and a slow third party can delay rendering.
- The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`003` prefix). It is embedded in a third-party pixel URL, which exposes a CRM identifier. This is inferred from the ID format, not confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Images missing alt text (4 flagged):**
  - one `<img>` with an empty `src` and no alt
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `myvisualiq …/impression_pixel`
  - `dotomi …/UCMController`
- The three tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"` and `style="display:none"`. Without these, some clients show broken-image icons or expose the URL to screen readers.
- **Empty `src` image:** an `<img>` with no source can trigger a request to the page URL in some clients and renders as a broken image.
- **Font stack:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. Only Open Sans is declared. It will fall back to Helvetica. The CSS was truncated, so this could be defined later.
- `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other `<meta/>` tags, in an XHTML doctype. This is cosmetic and has no rendering impact.
- No other rendering defects are confirmed. The MSO/VML namespaces, the `u ~ div .pattern` Gmail fix and the `x-apple-data-detectors` reset are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Failed token:** the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the myvisualiq URL is an unresolved macro (see section 2).
- No other unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) were found in the visible source.

- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.
