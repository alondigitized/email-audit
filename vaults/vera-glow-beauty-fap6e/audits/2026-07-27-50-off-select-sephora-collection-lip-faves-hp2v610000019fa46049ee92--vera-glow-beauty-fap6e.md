# 50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I need to say this up front — this is the exact same subject line, exact same date, as an email already sitting in my inbox history: "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves 🪩" from 7/27, which I already opened and scored an 8. So either Sephora resent this to me, or their send cadence has officially blurred into one continuous lip-sale fog. Either way, my gut reaction is déjà vu, not excitement.
- Once I get past that, the email itself is a mess of mixed signals. The top banner promises "50% off Select Sephora Collection" for National Lip Day, but then I scroll and the very next thing I see is a giant script "Just Landed at Sephora" hero that has nothing to do with the sale, followed by a Black-Owned fragrance brand launch (RUHVEDA, INFLUIXIOUS), and then a product grid that's mostly NOT lip — blush, body butter, hair mist, BB cream, scalp toner. Two lip products (Urban Decay liner, Fenty gloss stick) buried in a grid of eight. This is not a "lip faves" email, subject line be damned.
- Would I click? Probably, because the 4X points on Patrick Ta with code PT4XPTS is genuinely good loyalty math and I do that calculation in my head automatically. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is a "mark as read and maybe tap the points offer" email, not a shareable one. Compare this to Ulta's "Lucky you! 20% off" send, which scored a 9 with me because the hero shot and offer were one cohesive thing — Sephora's hero and offer are fighting each other here.

## What's working

- The 50% off banner up top is legible and the offer is stated plainly, no hunting required.
- Rouge is visibly highlighted in red on the Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar near the footer — small thing, but it's nice to be seen as a Rouge member and not just "a customer."
- 4X points on Patrick Ta with a clear code and a 7/29 deadline is exactly the kind of loyalty-math offer that gets me to actually do something.
- Product grid has real prices and "CLEAN" / "NEW · ONLINE ONLY" tags, which at least gives me shopping-relevant detail at a glance.

## What's weak

- The subject says lip faves, the banner says Sephora Collection, the hero says "Just Landed" — three different messages in the first three inches of this email, and none of them agree.
- This is a rerun of the exact 7/27 "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves" send I already scored — either a duplicate send or Sephora is repeating creative and subject lines within the same week, on top of the 7/26 lip email that also hit twice.
- National Lip Day is being milked for the third time in my recent history (7/26, 7/26 again, now this) — I said it before and I'll say it again, I've already celebrated this "holiday" enough.
- The product grid drifts way off the "lip faves" promise into fragrance, hair, skin, and scalp — it reads like a generic new-arrivals dump slapped under a lip-sale subject line.
- "SHOP NOW" is the CTA on every single button — no product-specific copy anywhere to differentiate what I'm actually clicking into.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject line match the body — if the hero and grid are mostly new arrivals across categories, don't call it "lip faves." Pick one story and commit to it top to bottom.
- 2. Kill the duplicate send. If this genuinely re-hit my inbox with the same subject as 7/27, that's a segmentation bug, not a campaign — Ulta doesn't do this to me.
- 3. Lead with the 4X Patrick Ta points offer higher up — that's the strongest, most specific hook in this whole email and it's buried past the fold, under the fragrance section.
- 4. Swap "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTA copy ("Shop Lip Faves," "Shop 4X Points") so the buttons actually tell me what I'm clicking into.
- 5. Retire "National Lip Day" language until it's actually a fresh beat — three sends deep, it reads as recycled urgency.
