# Don't leave a good thing behind.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Skechers in my beauty inbox again. The subject is "Don't leave a good thing behind" and the hero is "SELLING FAST… add to cart before they're gone!" That's the same fake-cart nudge I've gotten about eight times in two weeks, from "Your cart expires soon!" on 11/2 through "Don't leave these styles behind!" on 11/7. I still haven't put anything in a Skechers cart. The hero shoe is a black quilted Slip-ins sneaker, and nothing in the email says it's actually selling fast.
- The real news is buried halfway down: Black Friday countdown, BOGO 50% off or 20% off a single pair, valid 11/12–11/17. That's the first concrete offer Skechers has given me in this run of sends, and it should have been the hero. I did the math anyway: BOGO 50% on two $70 pairs is 25% off the total, so the single-pair 20% is the weaker deal. It's a decent offer, but it's below the fold and under a scarcity line I don't believe.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. If I were in the market for sneakers, the offer might get me there, but this is a mark-as-read. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper, and nothing here speaks to how I shop.

## What's working

- The BOGO 50% / 20% off banner is a navy block with big white type. It's the clearest thing in the email.
- Offer dates are stated (11/12–11/17), so there's a real deadline.
- The single hero shoe with one "Shop Now" button is easy to read, and the button is large enough to tap.
- The six-product grid is clean and the women's styles are labeled. The colorful Britto sneakers are the only thing with any personality.
- The footer has Afterpay and Klarna, which helps for pricing math.

## What's weak

- "Selling fast… before they're gone!" has no proof behind it. Compare Sephora's "Seth Rogen did your holiday shopping," which at least has a real hero and a joke.
- The offer sits about 60% of the way down. Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder gets old, but it's at least in the top fold. Skechers makes me scroll past six shoes to find the deal.
- The subject ("good thing behind") and the hero ("selling fast") are two different messages. Neither mentions Black Friday or a discount.
- There's no loyalty or member benefit anywhere. Glossier gave me a clear 3 for $30 and Sephora has Beauty Insider tiers. There's nothing here I can stack points on.
- The offer disclaimer is tiny and dense, with exclusions for sale styles, premieres, accessories and more. "Discount auto-applied" is fine, but the fine print undercuts the headline.
- The category list (Women / Men / Kids / Clothing) is a big stack of gray bars that adds length without adding any pull.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer. Make "Buy one, get one 50% off" the hero, with the countdown and end date, and move the shoe grid below it.
- 2. Drop the fake scarcity unless there's real low-stock data. If a style really is low, say which size or color.
- 3. Make the subject and hero tell the same story.
- 4. Add something for a repeat subscriber, like a member perk or an early-access line. Right now I'm treated like a stranger.
- 5. Cut the category stack and shorten the footer.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy one, get one 50% off, or 20% off a single pair. Auto-applied at cart.`
