# Last day for 20% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Drunk Elephant did the SheerID thing to me again — this is the same "verify to get 20% off" mechanic I already logged on 8/26 ("Best time for 20% off"), just now dressed up in a countdown timer and "back to school" copy about sharpened pencils and warm sweaters. I'm not a teacher, student, first responder, military member, or healthcare worker, so the entire hero — the countdown clock, the big "GET VERIFIED" button, the five verification tiles — is dead weight to me before I even scroll past the fold.
- The redeeming part is buried at "you're gonna like these guys" — the actual Kamo Triple-Peptide launch tie-in with the Protini/Lala GWP mechanic is genuinely interesting to me since I've got Rouge points to burn and I like a good "mix your own tint" gimmick. But Drunk Elephant made me scroll past an entire screen of irrelevant verification gates to get there, which is backwards. Compare that to Sephora's 8/29 "Take a closer look" send, which led with an actual new-launch rail (Natasha Denona palette) up top — no gatekeeping, no "prove you qualify" friction.
- I'm not clicking the hero CTA, I'm not forwarding this to a friend, and I'd probably scroll straight to the product grid at the bottom, price-check Kamo against what I already know from Sephora Beauty Insider, then close the email.

## What's working

- The countdown timer creates real urgency — "00 DAYS 14 HOURS 50 MINUTES" reads as legit last-day pressure, better than Sally Beauty's tenth recycled COLORfest countdown I've seen since mid-July.
- The Kamo Triple-Peptide "mix your own tint" GWP with Protini or Lala samples is a smart cross-sell that actually fits my "always doing the points math + stocks up at events" habit.
- The product grid at the bottom has real social proof ("1,407 PEOPLE ARE CHECKING THIS OUT") and star ratings, which builds trust the way ILIA's Lip Sketch GWP send did for me on 8/27.
- Clean, on-brand minimalist layout — no render bugs, the model's skin-focus close-up is sharp and matches my age/demographic.

## What's weak

- The entire top half of this email — hero, five verification buttons — is a SheerID-only offer that excludes me completely, same dead-end I hit on 8/26's "Best time for 20% off."
- Subject says "20% off," but that discount only applies if I'm verified as teacher/student/military/first responder/medical — that's a bait-and-switch feel for a Rouge/Diamond shopper like me with no relevant credential.
- No mention of my actual loyalty tier or points anywhere — Sephora and Ulta both do a better job surfacing member-specific value even in generic sends.
- The real news (Kamo launch, GWP mechanic) is stranded below three separate screens of irrelevant verification content — burying the lede badly.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Kamo launch and GWP mechanic, not the SheerID gate — that's the universally relevant offer and it's currently invisible until deep scroll.
- 2. Segment sends: if I'm not SheerID-eligible based on past behavior, don't send me the verification-first version at all — send me the Kamo-first version instead.
- 3. Add a Rouge/loyalty-tier callout somewhere, even a small "as a Beauty Insider, here's what's new" line, so it doesn't read like a stranger blast.
- 4. Move the countdown timer to sit against the Kamo sellout risk ("selling out fast") instead of a discount I can't use.
