# Don’t leave these styles behind!
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- It's Skechers in my beauty inbox again. This is roughly the ninth "you left something behind" email in two weeks, and I still haven't put a single Skechers shoe in a cart. "We saved these styles for you" is the same fake-cart nudge as 11/7's "Don't leave these styles behind!" and 11/6's "This is perfect for you!". The tone is a little more honest this time, because there's an actual Black Friday offer. It's sitting in a navy band under six shoes, though, not in the hero.
- The offer itself is fine. BOGO 50% off, or 20% off a single pair, auto-applied at cart, runs 11/12–11/17. That's about 25% off a two-pair order, which I can math out. But there's no loyalty angle, and nothing here is for someone who shops Sephora Beauty Insider events and Ulta 21 Days. Would I click? No. I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it either, other than as a joke about the cadence.

## What's working

- The offer band is high-contrast and readable: "BUY ONE, GET ONE 50% OFF or 20% OFF A SINGLE PAIR." Two clear mechanics, and "auto-applied at cart" means no code to hunt for.
- The 11/12–11/17 window in the fine print is a real, bounded deadline, and "Black Friday Countdown" frames it credibly.
- The six-product grid is clean, with consistent photography and descriptive names like "Women's Max Cushioning Endeavor Sneaker". It's all women's styles, which at least matches my gender.
- The big blue "SHOP NOW" button is unmistakable, and the headline hierarchy is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero promises "we saved these styles for you," but I never saved anything. It's the same cart-abandonment trick as the last eight sends, and it reads as manipulative.
- The deal is below the fold. The hero CTA sells nothing but a vague "Shop Now," and the actual offer comes after the product grid.
- None of the six products show a price, a sale price or a review. The links are also underlined text, which makes poor tap targets on a phone.
- The bottom half is a long, generic grey menu (Women, Men, Kids, Clothing…), the app download, Afterpay/Klarna, and a texting prompt. It's filler, and it buries the offer.
- There's no member or loyalty benefit, and no nod to me as a known customer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero, e.g. "BOGO 50% off through 11/17", and drop the fake "we saved these for you" framing unless a real cart exists.
- 2. Add prices and savings under each shoe, e.g. "$85 → 2 for $63.75 each", so the deal is visible without clicking.
- 3. Make each product a tappable card, with a star rating and a "BOGO eligible" tag.
- 4. Cut the nav and utility blocks (app, Klarna, store finder) down to one footer row.
- 5. Cap cart-nudge frequency. Nine in two weeks is spam, and ILIA and Sephora don't do this to me.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Six women's styles to pair up, or take 20% off a single pair.`

- **Unsubscribe link returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/dvm?token=…&attn_macro=dW5zdWJzY3JpYmVMaW5r…`. A 400 on a token-bearing Attentive link may come from the probe (missing UA or method, or a single-use token). It is not confirmed as a real user-facing failure. Verify it manually, because a broken unsubscribe is a compliance risk.
- **"LET'S GET TEXTING" returned 400.** The URL is `https://skechers.attn.tv/p/pUK/joinsms/?utm_content=footer&utm_term=text-signup…`. The same probe caveat applies.

- **Probe coverage was partial.** 12 links were probed and 22 skipped, so the status of the skipped links is unknown.
