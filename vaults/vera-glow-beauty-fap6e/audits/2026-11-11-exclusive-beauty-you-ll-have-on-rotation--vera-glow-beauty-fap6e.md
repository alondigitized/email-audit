- My gut reaction: finally, an offer I can do math on. After the 11/6–11/9 "ENDS TODAY" grind and two low-effort sends on 11/10 ("The best NEW holiday sets go fast" and "Did you know that points = rewards?"), this one leads with a free full-size-ish Gisou Honey Infused Hair Oil trial set with code GIFTGISOU. - Past the hero it turns into a catalog: Gisou sets, then 8 "Great Gifts, Great Value" sets, then a Makeup Gifts banner and a holiday gift guide. It's pretty, but I got lost after the first two screens. I'd click the Gisou hero and maybe the Ordinary set. I wouldn't forward it, because nothing here is a "text your bestie" moment.

## What's working

- The top banner has a concrete offer: a complimentary Gisou hair oil with code GIFTGISOU at $30+. That is real points-math fuel.
- The "ONLY AT SEPHORA" hero with the "$45 ($54 VALUE)" lip mask and oil set gives me a value calculation up front.

- The "Clean at Sephora" badges on the Milk Makeup, Briogeo and Dedcool tiles are a good curation signal.
- The Ordinary, Dr. Idriss, Make Up For Ever and Milk are the right prestige-brand mix for my cart.

## What's weak

- The hero is cluttered. A banner, a second "ONLY AT SEPHORA" headline and a gift-set block all compete before I hit a real product grid.
- The right edge looks clipped in several places: the "Insider" badge, the "*Exclusions/terms apply" line and "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" all run off the edge.
- There's no deadline, so no reason to act today. Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder at least had its "ends tonight" badge.
- Beauty Insider tier math is missing. As a Rouge I'd expect a points multiplier or tier perk here, and there's only a credit card pitch at the bottom.
- The free gift needs a code, $30+ and online only, and it's followed by fine print. That's exactly the fine print I'm tired of.
- The grid has 8+ gift sets, plus a "Spot it. Shop it." block, a Makeup Gifts banner and a gift guide. Glossier's free Discovery Kit email had one offer. This is a catalog.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the GIFTGISOU offer and a deadline in the subject and make it the single hero. Cut the second "Only at Sephora" block.
- 2. Add a Rouge or Insider-tier line, like "Rouge: 2x points on Gisou sets", so I can do the points math.
- 3. Trim the grid to 4 products and fix the right-edge clipping.
- 4. Move the exclusions out of the fine print and next to the code, as one plain sentence.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email (700px fixed width) sent through Epsilon/Harmony with `app.sephora.com` click and open tracking. The only hard QA failure is the missing plain-text part (0 chars). The header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is low.** 12 of 59 links were probed and 47 were skipped, so link health for about 80% of the links is unverified. The QA output reports no broken links among the 12 probed.
- **Destinations are opaque.** Links are wrapped in redirectors, e.g. `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1cafb62d7c684a5f4bbcfb648/...?__dU__=v0G4RBKT...`. The final landing URLs can't be seen in the source.
- **Two separate open-tracking pixels are present.**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is a hidden `<img style="display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all">`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261111_Exclusive_Gifts` is a second pixel. It carries a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on both tracking pixels.** QA flagged both. They are hidden, so the impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct markup.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Malformed CSS in the media query.** An MSO conditional comment sits inside the `@media` block: `@media only screen and (max-width:480px){... <!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]--> ...}`. Conditional comments aren't valid inside CSS. Some parsers will drop the rules that follow it, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`.
- **Fixed-width layout.** `min-width:700px; width:700px` on the wrapper table is wider than the 480px breakpoint. Only the rules in the media query are mobile-adjusted, so any that are dropped leave the layout at 700px.
- **No `lang` attribute** on `<html>`. The `<title>` is the generic "Sephora".
- **JSON-LD markup is empty or invalid.** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` block contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which isn't a valid schema.org type. Most clients strip it, but Gmail may reject invalid email markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. Per-recipient values appear only as encoded tracking parameters (`mi_u`, `__dU__`). The empty `discountCode: ""` in the JSON-LD is the only blank dynamic field. Coverage is limited by the truncation.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is missing.** The text part is 0 chars. A multipart/alternative message without a `text/plain` part is a spam-filter negative and breaks text-only clients.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool says the relay may have stripped it. This is unverified, not confirmed missing.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain `beauty.sephora.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) are in the truncated portion. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible. Destinations are hidden behind `app.sephora.com/T/...` redirects, so landing-page alignment and UTM passthrough can't be verified from the source.
- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261111_Exclusive_Gifts` on the `mi.sephora.com` pixel. It follows a date-plus-name convention. Whether it matches the redirect-side campaign naming can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part. This is the only confirmed failure.
2. Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message headers or a direct-to-inbox seed. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC there, since the AgentMail relay hides them.
3. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` rule out of the `@media` block into its own `<style>` or a conditional comment outside the query.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, fix `content="noindex"`, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
6. Fix or drop the JSON-LD block (empty `discountCode`, invalid `PromotionCard`).
7. Re-run the audit with an untruncated HTML source and a higher probe limit. That would cover the footer, the unsubscribe and address check, and the 47 skipped links. Resolving the redirect chains would also let the UTMs and landing pages be checked.
