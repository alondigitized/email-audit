# Problem, meet solution.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so no DRUNKVIP20 for the sixth-plus time in a row — this is Drunk Elephant actually building a product-finder concept, "Name an issue. Well, a skin issue," walking through Dull/Dry/Uneven/Puffy with a bestseller Polaroid-taped-to-a-wall aesthetic for each. It's clean, it's on-brand, and after logging the "secret sale" wink for the fifth day straight on 9/8, I'll take literally any format that isn't a duplicate discount code.
- But here's the thing — there's no offer anywhere near the top. It's pure problem/solution copy until I scroll deep into a "Spend $100+, get deluxe samples" GWP buried below the fold, and even that's soft — no percentage off, no dollar amount, just samples. Compare that to Ulta's 9/12 "20% off⁺ one qualifying item" or Sally Beauty's tiered SAVEMORE ladder — those are concrete numbers I can do math on. This is vibes.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity for Protini since I've heard about it, but I'm not opening my wallet off this. Wouldn't forward it — nothing here is shareable, it's just tidy skincare-quiz-adjacent merchandising. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- The problem/solution structure (Dull → B-Goldi, Dry → Protini, Uneven → C-Firma, Puffy → Ceramighty) is genuinely useful shorthand — it's basically the skincare-quiz energy from their 9/12 "routine right this way" send, minus the actual quiz tool.
- Taped-Polaroid product photography feels tactile and premium, not another flat product-on-white grid like the "you're gonna like these guys" section below it.
- The bottom module has real social proof — star ratings and "1,235 people are checking this out" — that's more trust signal than most of what I've seen from ILIA or e.l.f. Hair this cycle.
- Fine print is upfront and unhidden (dates, exclusions, what's included) rather than buried — better than Ulta's dense excl.-apply legalese jammed into the subject line itself.

## What's weak

- Zero discrete offer above the fold — I have to scroll past four product call-outs before I hit the Spend-$100 GWP, and even Sephora's Rewards Bazaar on 9/10 led with the mechanic faster than this.
- The GWP threshold is steep — $100+ for samples, not even a percentage off — feels like a weak ask next to Ulta's $20-off-one-item or Sally's $10-off-$40 entry tier.
- No Rouge/points-tier callout anywhere. I'm dropping real money here across brands and there's not a single "as a VIP" nod — Ulta at least personalized with "Marisol" in-subject on 9/12.
- The "you're gonna like these guys" recs feel like a generic cross-sell bolt-on, disconnected from the problem/solution narrative above it — tonal whiplash after the styled Polaroid section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the GWP offer (or a real percentage-off) into the hero, right under "Our bestsellers can help" — don't make me scroll four product blocks to find the ask.
- 2. Add a Rouge/loyalty acknowledgment somewhere — even a small "as a VIP, here's early access" line would close the gap with what Ulta's doing with named personalization.
- 3. Lower or tier the GWP threshold ($50+/$100+ ladder) so it's not all-or-nothing at $100.
- 4. Cut or reskin the bottom "you're gonna like these guys" grid to match the Polaroid treatment — right now it looks bolted on from a different email.
