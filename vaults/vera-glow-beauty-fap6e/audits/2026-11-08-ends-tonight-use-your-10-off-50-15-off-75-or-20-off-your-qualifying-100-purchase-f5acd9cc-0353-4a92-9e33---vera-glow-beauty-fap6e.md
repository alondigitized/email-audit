The SpeedStyle dryer shows up twice at 32% off, once as a Shark Beauty card and again further down with a different photo. For a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond who tracks launches, that looks sloppy. ## What's working

- The $10/$15/$20 stack is huge and reads in two seconds. The 20%-three-ways math is easy.
- The "ends today" badge and "Offer ends November 8 at 11:59pm CT" give me a real deadline.
- The barcode plus the online code GIFTS covers both in-store and online. That's useful for a points-math person.
- The free 17-piece bag with a $90 order, with "choose 4 from 4" options, is a gift-with-purchase I'd actually plan around.
- The Ulta Beauty Rewards credit card block at the bottom shows the member angle: 2 points per $1 spent.

## What's weak

- The dryer repeats. The Shark SpeedStyle at 32% off appears twice with the same copy, and it's a different Shark product in the last row. That's filler, not matchmaking.
- The "personalized picks we know you'll love" claim doesn't match what I see: hair tools and a pore pad. Nothing skews to makeup or skincare, my actual lane.
- Several 30-40% discounts are on "ULTA Beauty Collection" or Shark, not prestige names, so it doesn't feel like a curated event.
- My Diamond status is invisible. The top banner pushes me to "join" Ulta Beauty Rewards, which reads like I'm a stranger.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text, and the "⁺⁺" exclusions are buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the duplicated Shark cards with products from my actual purchase history, such as skincare, makeup or fragrance. Show a tier perk if I'm Diamond.
- 2. Show the points I'd earn on a $100 purchase next to the $20 off. That's the math I'm doing anyway.
- 3. Drop the "join Ulta Beauty Rewards" banner for known members and show a points balance instead.
- 4. Cut the product grid to 4 unique picks and make the free bag the second hero.
- 5. Pull the exclusions up near the hero so "yep, includes prestige brands & fragrance!" doesn't feel like fine print bait.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has no hard failures: the QA run reports 0 issues and 3 warnings. It does have widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 35 images flagged) and 10 third-party `http://` pixels. The HTML was truncated and the 41 click-tracking links were not probed, so unsubscribe, footer and link-destination checks are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** all 41 click-tracking links were skipped as tracking/redirect domains. Destinations and redirect health are unknown.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 10 pixels load over plain HTTP, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?a848b7436c89b41e828aeec7260d6e42d=1`. They may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling. All 10 are also missing `alt`.
- **Open pixel:** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=203057427&t=164071` is missing `alt`. It is a tracking pixel, so this is low impact.
- **Empty query parameters:** the `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs carry `multiplier=&memberTier=&filter_type=&filter_v…`. The empty values suggest the dynamic content rendered without populated parameters (see section 4).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content-bearing images:** about 20 images on `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/...` and `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/...` have no `alt`. Examples:
  - `08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif`, flagged twice, so it is probably used twice
  - `c2df4a7d-…jpg`, `2fdb2996-…jpg`, `b1d74144-…jpg`, `7914ea59-…jpg`
  - `852db034-…png`, also flagged twice
  - `01fa2fab9849e714.png`, `3eb758650b65389e.png`, `a181e331c58479fa.png`, `618b4b9854d3933a.png`, `7f478bafdf9cd3cd.png`

  With images off or a screen reader, any promo content carried only by these images is lost. The code-and-offer details are the main risk.
- **Doctype and markup mismatch:** the doctype is `XHTML 1.0 Strict`, but the head has unclosed void elements (`<meta name="color-scheme" ...>`, `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" ...>`, `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/...">`). This is invalid XHTML. Most clients are lenient, so impact is low.
- **Web font:** the `fonts.googleapis.com` DM Sans link is ignored by Gmail and Outlook. Confirm that a font fallback stack exists in the inline styles. The truncated source didn't show one.
- **No issues found:** viewport meta, `lang="en-US"`, Outlook VML/OfficeDocumentSettings, and dark-mode `color-scheme` declarations are present.
