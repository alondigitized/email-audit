# NEW! The Halo Glow fam just went full body ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this one is actually pretty. The peach-and-pink hero with "give skin a halo-to-toe glow" looks like a prestige launch, closer to Rare Beauty or Tower 28 than the glitter-stick chaos I've been getting from e.l.f. all week. Three NEW products, three clean prices ($12, $14, $15), and one "Shop Now" button under each. That's easy to scan.
- It's still the seventh e.l.f. send I've logged, though. After 9/30's blush + bronzer sticks, 9/29's Glass Slick Serum Stick, and 9/28's "add to cart" email, I'm getting a new "NEW!" product every day. Nothing here tells me why the body cream is worth buying today. There's no deadline, no sale and no points math. The only urgency is "limited time only" on free shipping, down in the footer.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Liquid Filter at $15, since I already own the face version. I'd forward it to a friend only if she's a body-shimmer person. I wouldn't do it for the offer. Mark as read, and keep it in mind for my next Ulta 21 Days.

## What's working

- The hero is cohesive. The pink-to-peach gradient and glowing product shots feel more grown-up than the "bestie" voice of the 9/28 send.
- Each product has its own block, a price in the headline, and a one-line benefit. "$12" and "$14" are the same clean anchor I liked in the 9/30 and 9/29 emails.
- The "new!" badges on the first two products make the launch obvious in about two seconds. Sephora's launch emails do the same thing.
- The body cream copy mentions a vanilla and magnolia scent and a hint of shimmer. That's an actual sensory detail.
- The "Top Picks for You" row with $11, $2 and $11 items gives me a cheap add-on option.

## What's weak

- There's no offer. No code, no bundle, no "buy 2" and no rewards multiplier. Sephora Insider's "STARTS TODAY: 2X points" email gave me a reason to act today. This one gives me nothing to do the math on.
- Every button just says "SHOP NOW". It's three identical buttons. "Get the Body Cream" would at least tell me what I'm tapping.
- No shade or skin-tone information. The Liquid Filter is a tinted product, but I only see one bottle and one shade. Ulta and Sephora would show me a range.
- The "Top Picks for You" row is a Power Grip Primer, a lip liner and a setting spray. None of it ties to the glow theme, and I've never bought a lip liner from e.l.f. It doesn't feel personal.
- The text-only category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) take up a lot of scroll for no payoff. The free shipping and rewards perks are buried in the dark footer. I can't tell if I earn anything on this order.
- The "Hit the high points of your body" copy on the balm is vague. It doesn't say if it's stick or balm, or what shade it comes in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real hook in the top fold, such as "free shipping at $X" or "NEW Halo Glow Body: 2X rewards points this week". Ulta runs this kind of mechanic every other week.
- 2. Make the buttons specific and different: "Get the Body Cream", "Grab the Goddess Balm", "Shop Liquid Filter shades".
- 3. Show a shade strip or "available in 12 shades" for the Liquid Filter so I can tell if it works for my skin tone.
- 4. Replace the generic top-picks row with products that are actually related to the glow theme, such as a Halo Glow Setting Powder or a bundle.
- 5. Cut the category bars, or turn them into a small icon row. Move the free-shipping and rewards line above the footer.
- 6. Give the send a reason to exist on a day other than the previous six, such as a launch-day-only perk or an early-access window.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: it uses `role="presentation"` tables, `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and an MSO fallback. The 27% QA pass rate comes mostly from link-probe failures on relative redirect targets, which look like probe artifacts, plus missing-header and empty-plain-text findings that need confirming against the raw message. The source is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and physical address could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 probe FAILs, all `unknown url type: '/…'`.** The targets are:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve`
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=…&shortlink=email&…&utm_medium…`
- **Likely cause.** Each is a `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` click-tracker whose redirect returned a relative `Location`. The prober failed to resolve it against the destination host. Mail clients and browsers resolve relative redirects, so this is probably a probe limitation, not a user-facing break. It does mean the final destination hosts were never verified.
- **Unverified.** Only 12 of 40 links were probed (28 skipped), so the rest are not covered.
- **Truncated URL.** The mobile-app link is cut off at `utm_medium`, so its UTM values can't be checked. The evidence is the QA log's cutoff, not necessarily the source.
- **Redirect hygiene (minor).** The tracker emits a scheme-less relative `Location` header. A spec-compliant but unusual redirect like this can trip strict link scanners and security gateways.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty title.** `<title></title>` is empty. Some clients and archive views use it, so it should be populated.
- **Missing alt on a tracking image.** The `<img>` sourced from `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/I_8tqJyGvj3MnT5EtAZgmw~~/…` has no `alt`. This looks like an open pixel and should carry `alt=""`.
- **Duplicate table ids and classes.** `nl-container` and `row-1` repeat inside nested `html_block` wrappers (blocks 1 and 2). This is harmless for rendering but adds nesting weight, and the HTML is truncated so size/clipping risk can't be assessed.
- **Font fallback.** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` has an empty first family, so it falls through to Verdana. The Open Sans `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` and is ignored by most webmail, and the fallback is Arial. Both are low impact.
- **Good.** There is a hidden preheader (`Glow all out with hot drops + a holy grail fave.`) and a mobile stack breakpoint at 660px. Images use `alt` and `title` on the logo (`alt="e.l.f."`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The `bxid` parameter in the app link is a resolved Braze ID, not a token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **FAIL: plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This affects deliverability and accessibility and should be fixed at the sender, since e.l.f. sends via Braze/SparkPost-style tracking.
- **WARN: `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the origin before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **WARN: `Authentication-Results` absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and tracking domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` are aligned subdomains of the same organizational domain, which is favorable but not proof.
- **Not assessable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section. Neither can be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** The app link shows `utm_medium` but is cut off, and the product links resolved to paths whose query strings contain only variant selectors (`Size=…`, `Color=…`), with no UTM visible. Attribution may be carried by the Braze/SparkPost tracker instead of by UTMs. Confirm the final landing URLs.
- **Variant deep links are specific:** `Size=Regular+0.8+oz+/+24+ml` and `Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` preselect a variant. If a SKU is out of stock, the landing page could fall back to a generic PDP, so check these two destinations.
- **Login-gated link.** The "Receipt scanning" link goes to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. Recipients who aren't logged in land on a login wall rather than the rewards feature.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the prober.** Resolve relative `Location` headers against the redirect host, then re-run the four failed links to get real status codes and final URLs.
2. **Confirm the headers at the origin.** Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, to separate real gaps from relay stripping.
3. **Add a plain-text part.** Make it a real multipart alternative, not an empty one.
4. **Populate `<title>`.** Add `alt=""` to the `/q/…` tracking pixel.
5. **Extend probe coverage.** Verify the unsubscribe link and the footer address, which sit in the truncated portion.
6. **Verify UTM and attribution on the final URLs.** Check whether the two variant PDP links and the login-gated rewards link land where intended.
