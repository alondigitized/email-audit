## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the second time this exact email — "FREE foundation and concealer sample set with $45 min. spend" — has landed in front of me today, and it's the same purple hero, same product grid, same tiny badge burying the actual catch. I already caught this the first time: "Free" isn't free, it's gated behind a $45 minimum spend, and that condition is crammed into a barely-legible badge under the SHOP NOW button instead of anywhere near the headline. That's the kind of thing that makes me trust Sephora emails less every time I open one.
- The 4X/5X points on foundation and concealer thru 8/5 is genuinely useful to me as a Rouge member doing the points math, and the SHADEMATCH callout is a real personalization signal I care about — that's more than Ulta usually bothers with in a promo blast. But "0 points" sitting right up top in the header is either a broken personalization token or Sephora forgot I've spent actual money with them, and either way it reads sloppy.
- I'm not clicking this one. I already opened it once today, the offer terms haven't changed, and the fine-print bait-and-switch is the same complaint I logged a few hours ago. Not forwarding it — if anything I'd screenshot it for a friend as an example of what NOT to do.

## What's working

- The 4X/5X points multiplier on foundation and concealer thru 8/5 is a concrete, dated loyalty offer I can actually act on — better than Sally Beauty's rewards pitch, which didn't even show me my own point balance.
- SHADEMATCH badge is a real nod to product-fit personalization, something ILIA's recent eyeliner launch also nailed with its shade chart — Sephora at least gestures at it here.
- Clean product grid, no broken images, no overlapping text — visually tidy even if busy.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the footer keeps my tier status top of mind, which Ulta also does well in their "Lucky you" 20%-off send.

## What's weak

- The "Free" in the headline is not free — it's $45-minimum-spend free, and that condition is stuffed into a small badge instead of stated plainly up top. This is the exact bait-and-switch I flagged on this same email earlier today.
- "You have 0 points" in the header feels like a broken personalization token, not a real balance — that's worse than no personalization at all.
- Product grid ($182 ViktorRolf, $172 Miu Miu, random hair oils) has nothing to do with the "foundation and concealer" hero — feels like a generic cross-sell dump, not curated for someone shopping the actual promo.
- I'm getting this exact subject and creative for the second time today — same problem I called out with the "LAST CHANCE" lip faves emails hitting me three times in one week.
- Two barcodes at the bottom (Sephora stores + Kohl's) with zero explanation of which one applies to me is just confusing clutter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the $45 minimum spend out of the fine-print badge and into the hero copy itself — "Free samples with $45+" as the headline, not buried underneath. Trust is the whole issue here.
- 2. Fix or remove the "0 points" display — either show my real balance or drop it; a wrong number is worse than none.
- 3. Swap the generic $172-$182 fragrance grid for products that actually tie to the foundation/concealer promo, or at minimum shade-match picks based on past purchases.
- 4. Suppress duplicate sends — this creative already hit my inbox once today under the identical subject line.
- 5. Clarify the two barcodes — label which is for Sephora vs. Kohl's so I'm not guessing at checkout.
