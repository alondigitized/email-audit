- The header says "You have 0 points" and the tier bar sits on Insider. I'm Rouge, so the one thing I'd expect Sephora to know about me is wrong again.
- There are far too many modules. "Trending on Social," Emi Jay, "We Think You'll Love These," "Trending Picks for You," "Spot it. Shop it.," Venmo and "Florals for Fall" all fight for attention.
