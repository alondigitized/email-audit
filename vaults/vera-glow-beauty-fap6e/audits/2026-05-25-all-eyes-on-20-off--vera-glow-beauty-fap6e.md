# All eyes on 20% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Thirteen. This is ILIA email number THIRTEEN since May 16, and at this point I'm just documenting the descent. "All eyes on 20% off" — okay, cute, finally a subject line with a *concept* attached, an actual eye-products angle hinted at by the close-up of someone's gorgeous winged liner in the hero. That eye photo is genuinely beautiful and it's the first time in this whole F&F barrage that ILIA has bothered to give the creative an editorial point of view instead of just slapping "20% off sitewide" on another flat lay.
- But then I scroll and… it's just the same Prep / Color / Frame product grid I've now seen four or five times in this campaign, with Cart Deco bolted to the bottom as the perma-CTA. The eye hook is purely a header device — it doesn't actually anchor the email around eye products. The Limitless Lash and Eye Stylus are buried in the middle, not pulled forward as "all eyes on these."
- Would I click? Honestly no, I already cashed in my F&F on Glossier's 30% (which got a 9 from me) and Sephora's bonus points event. ILIA at 20% with a 24-hour countdown banner doesn't beat Sephora Rouge math. Forward? Absolutely not. I'd mark as read and move on — though I'll admit the hero photo earned me one extra second of attention before I bounced.

## What's working

- The hero close-up eye shot is genuinely the strongest creative ILIA has run all campaign — editorial, intimate, makes you *want* to wear eye makeup.
- "Eyeing these?" as hero copy is the first time the subject and visual actually do a callback to each other.
- The Prep / Color / Frame routine framework is a smart way to merchandise a sitewide sale without it feeling like a dump.
- "24 HR EXTENSION" countdown at the top finally adds credible time-bound urgency this campaign has been missing.
- Cart Deco shoutout at the bottom is a nice loyalty/value-add detail — free gifting feels like a real perk.

## What's weak

- The eye-makeup concept is abandoned by row two — if "all eyes on 20% off," show me eye products first, not Skin Blur Serum Concealer.
- Thirteen emails in ten days. The fatigue is real and the creative is just being recycled with new headers.
- The product grid is identical to the May 22 and May 23 sends — same routine framework, same hero shots, same "Shop Now" buttons.
- 20% off doesn't compete. Drunk Elephant gave me 25%, Glossier 30%, Charlotte Tilbury 20% with gifts, and Sephora Rouge stacks points on top.
- No personalization, no nod to past purchases, no "Vera, your favorite Liquid Powder is included" — Ulta would at least have called me by name like the Marisol email did.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Commit to the eye concept.** If the subject says "all eyes," the first product row should be Limitless Lash, Liquid Powder Eye Tint, Eye Stylus — then expand to the routine. The hero promise has to match the merchandising.
- 2. **Cool the cadence.** This is send 13. Pause for 48 hours and re-enter with a "final hours" send Monday night, not another mid-campaign reminder.
- 3. **Stack the offer.** Add a gift-with-purchase or free shipping threshold at $50 — 20% alone is the floor of this category right now.
- 4. **Personalize one row.** "Reorder your Liquid Powder" or "based on your last order" — Sephora and Ulta both do this; ILIA treats me like a stranger every send.
- 5. **Shorten.** Cut the email to hero + three eye products + Cart Deco. The Prep/Color/Frame grid is exhausted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Eye products, 20% off — 24 hours left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Vera, your lash routine is 20% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limitless Lash, Liquid Powder, Eye Stylus — F&F ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The eye edit + 24-hr extension. Cart Deco free with $50+.`
