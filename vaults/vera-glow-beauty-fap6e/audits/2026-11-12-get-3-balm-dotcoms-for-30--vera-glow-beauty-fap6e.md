# Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Glossier email in a while where I can do the math. Three Balm Dotcoms for $30 works out to $10 each, against $16 list. That's a 37.5% discount on a product I'd actually use. It's a better offer than the 11/11 Discovery Kit email ("Free Glossier You Discovery Kit with ANY order") because there's no cart threshold to work around. It also ends the merch run from 11/3 to 11/9, which had no price, no offer and no deadline.
- The execution is cleaner than I expected. The hero is a split layout: copy on the left, three tubes with a blue "$30" burst on the right. The blue-box "$16 → $10" tags on every tile make the savings obvious without reading any copy. My issue is that the hero burst is cropped at the right edge, so the old price is cut off. I'd click, but I'd want to know whether the $30 deal is a pre-built set or mix-and-match, and the email doesn't say. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good deal, not a news story.
- Compared with Sephora Insider's "Holiday hits, all under $50," this one matches its subject better. Sephora opened with a purple fragrance-gift banner that had nothing to do with its subject line. Ulta has run the same $10/$15/$20 ladder for four weeks, and this is fresher than that.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer in the headline: "Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!" I can see the price and the product in two seconds.
- The blue "$16 → $10" tags on all six tiles are scannable. They work like Ulta's price callouts, but with more personality.
- The "If you're looking for sultry tints / subtle favs" split is smart curation. It's the closest thing to personalization here, and it helps me pick a shade family.
- Each tile has a flavor line ("Ripe cherries + burgundy tint," "Fragrance-free + untinted"). That's the product detail I care about.
- Having the Holiday Shop as a secondary module at the bottom keeps the balm offer as the hero.

## What's weak

- The "set" mechanic is unclear. The hero says "two balm sets," but all six tiles show $10 each. I can't tell if I pick any three or buy fixed sets, and the tiles all say plain "Shop now."
- The hero burst is clipped at the top right, so the original price is cut off. It looks like a layout mistake.
- Nothing mentions a deadline. "Limited time" and "available now for a limited time" are vague. Ulta at least gave me "Ends tonight."
- There's no loyalty angle at all. Sephora would have shown Rouge multipliers, and Ulta would have shown Diamond points. There's no member pricing and no points to calculate.
- Every CTA is a gray "Shop now" button. Seven identical buttons are weak copy and low contrast, and the tap targets look thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday," with the mechanic spelled out: "Pick any 3, $30, applied at checkout."
- 2. Fix the clipped hero badge so "$16 → $10" or "Save $18" shows in full.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop now" with named CTAs, such as "Add Black Cherry."
- 4. Add a line on stacking with the free Discovery Kit offer from 11/11, or whether the two can't be combined.
- 5. Add a social-proof line such as a star rating or "best-selling balm."
