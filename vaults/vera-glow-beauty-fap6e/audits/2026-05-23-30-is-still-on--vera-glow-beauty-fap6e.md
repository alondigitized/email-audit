# 30% is still on!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Two days after Glossier dropped the "Memorial Day Sale starts NOW" email — the one that got me, genuinely — here comes the reminder. My first instinct was a small eye-roll, because the launch email did the job on Friday and I'd already mentally bookmarked my cart. But then I actually read the body and realized they didn't just resend the same layout. They curated it: Body form, Monochromes, No. 1 Pencils — three specific product zones with real UGC reviews attached. That's not lazy. That's the "here's what to shop" version of a sale nudge, and it works on me more than a blank SALE SALE SALE hero.
- What pulled me in is the free Lip Glaze GWP at the bottom. I'd already seen the 30% — the GWP at $75 is the closer that makes me do the math. As someone who is currently doing the Drunk Elephant F&F math ($75 threshold there too) I'm comparing baskets, and the free Lip Glaze tips the scale toward spending the $75 at Glossier specifically.
- Would I click? Yes — probably the Monochromes or the free gift section. Would I forward to a friend? No. The May 21 launch email was the one to share. This is a personal nudge, not a "you have to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The repeating "MEMORIAL DAY SALE" salmon-on-white hero background is loud and seasonal without being tacky — it's still identifiably Glossier, not a generic mass-market blast.
- Three specific product lines highlighted with UGC reviews is smart curation — instead of a link to the whole sale page, they're doing the shopping for me.
- Bianca's Body form review mentioning the sandstone scent? That's exactly the kind of detail that makes me add something to my cart I wasn't already thinking about.
- The free Lip Glaze GWP at the bottom is well-placed — it's a reward for reading to the end, and it reframes the $75 threshold as "you're already spending it, might as well get this."
- No render bugs anywhere — images load, text is clean, every "Shop now" button is tappable.

## What's weak

- "Plus, 50% off saving even products" is confusing copy. I had to re-read it twice — "saving even" doesn't register cleanly. Is this "savings event" products? A specific Glossier SKU category? That ambiguity is copy friction on the hero, which is the worst place for it.
- No end date for the sale anywhere. "Still on" implies it's ending soon but gives me zero urgency anchor. Charlotte Tilbury at least tells me "ends Sunday" — Glossier just says "still on" and hopes I panic.
- "Shop now" on every single CTA. Four buttons, all identical. The Lip Glaze button should say "Claim your free glaze" and the product CTAs should name the product. Generic verbs waste real estate.
- No loyalty mention at all — no Glossier You Rewards note, no tier benefit, nothing. Sephora's Memorial Day email called out the 500 bonus points right in the hero. Glossier is leaving that lever unpulled.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the confusing subhead copy.** Rewrite "Plus, 50% off saving even products" to something scannable — "Plus, select styles at 50% off" or "Plus, shop our sale-on-sale picks." One confusing line on the hero undoes a lot of goodwill.
- 2. **Add a sale end date.** Even "Ends Monday night" is enough. "Still on" without a deadline is a missed urgency lever — ILIA does this on every send and it works.
- 3. **Differentiate the CTAs.** Make the Lip Glaze button "Claim your free glaze →" and the product buttons "Shop Body form" / "Shop Monochromes" / "Shop No. 1 Pencils." Specific verbs convert better than "Shop now" stacked four times.
- 4. **Add a loyalty rewards callout.** Even one line — "Glossier You members earn points on every purchase" — gives me a reason to buy here vs. waiting for Sephora points day.
- 5. **Surface the sale deadline in the hero.** A small "Offer ends May 26" line under the 30% headline would do it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Glossier's 30% is ending — here's what to grab`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off + a free Lip Glaze (yours at $75)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Body form, Monochromes & No. 1 Pencils before the sale closes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends soon: 30% off almost everything + a free Lip Glaze with your $75 order.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Invalid element used as tracking hook**
```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KS92561ACNHCRD3J53F7XD81_0"></span>
```
`rel` and `href` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This sits inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` so Outlook never sees it, but strict-parsing clients (some webmail sandboxes, aggressive spam filters) may strip the `href` silently, causing Klaviyo's link rewrite/open tracking to fail for those recipients. If this is an open-tracking pixel, it should be an `<img>` with a 1×1 transparent src; if it is a click-tracked URL, it must be an `<a>`.

**Scope limitation:** The HTML is truncated before the body links appear. UTM parameter coverage and destination URL integrity cannot be verified from the provided source.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Preheader padding with ZWNJ characters**
```
Shop the Memorial Day Sale ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏…
```
U+200C (zero-width non-joiner) characters are used to pad the preheader. This is a widely-used technique and renders correctly in major clients, but it is invisible to screen readers and will be read aloud as whitespace bursts by some AT implementations. Low severity, noted for completeness.

**MSO conditional structure:** Properly formed `<!--[if gte mso 9]>`, `<!--[if mso]>`, and `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` guards are all in place. `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` and `<w:WordDocument>` blocks are correctly wrapped in `<noscript>` and `<xml>` respectively.
