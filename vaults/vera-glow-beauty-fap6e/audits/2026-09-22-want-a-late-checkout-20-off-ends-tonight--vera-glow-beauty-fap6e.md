# Want a late checkout? 20% off ends tonight.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "want a late checkout" as the subject and then the actual email doesn't even mention checkout — no hotel visual, no cheeky vacation framing, just... a shopping bag on a hanger with a red-gloved arm. I get it, "checkout" as in cart checkout, but that's a swing-and-a-miss pun that made me squint at my phone for a second. The offer itself — 20% off sitewide, VIP sale ends tonight — is fine, but the entire email gates it behind "JOIN VIP (it's free)," and I already have an ILIA account. This is the same move ILIA pulled on 9/18 with "The latest + greatest + 20% off," and the 9/21 send literally opened with "you have 0 VIP points to spend" — so at this point I'm fairly sure ILIA's VIP program either doesn't know who I am or is designed to make me feel like a stranger every single time.
- The freebies (velvet makeup bag on $100+, free multi-stick on all orders) are a nice touch and genuinely more generous than what I've seen from Drunk Elephant's recent 15%-off-for-subscribers send. But between the bag-on-a-hanger hero, the illustrated arrows pointing at products like a scrapbook, and the "come for the sale, stay for the perks" copy, this feels scattered rather than urgent for a "sale ends tonight" email.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the free multi-stick with any order — that's genuinely a good hook. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read and move on.

## What's working

- "20% off sitewide" and "ends tonight" are both legible in the hero without scrolling — that's honest urgency, unlike Sally Beauty's endlessly recycled Appiversary ladder.
- Free multi-stick on all orders + free velvet bag on $100+ are concrete, tiered incentives — better mechanic than a flat percent-off.
- The "come for the sale, stay for the perks" section actually lists real member benefits (free shipping every order, points, early access) — more substance than Ulta's barcode-hero email that just says "20% off" and moves on.
- Product grid is clean, well-lit, consistent white-background shots — brand voice feels controlled and premium, similar quality bar to e.l.f. SKIN's splashy hero.

## What's weak

- The "late checkout" subject line has zero visual payoff in the email — no hotel, no travel cue, just a shopping bag, so the wordplay lands flat once you open it.
- Gated behind "JOIN VIP" when I'm already a customer — same friction ILIA ran on 9/18, and it still reads like they don't know I've bought their concealer for two years.
- No skin tone, shade, or personalization signal anywhere — just generic product shots, nothing tailored to me specifically.
- Visual hierarchy sags in the middle: the illustrated-arrow section is cute but breaks the "sale ends tonight" urgency established in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/hero mismatch — either make "late checkout" a real visual bit (a suitcase, a clock, a "your cart is checking out" gag) or scrap the pun for something that matches the bag-on-hanger image.
- 2. Stop gating existing customers behind "JOIN VIP" — recognize logged-in/returning shoppers and show the CTA as "Shop your 20% off" instead of "Become a VIP."
- 3. Add a shade-match or "recommended for you" module — ILIA has a Personalized Shade Match feature per their own footer icons, use it here.
- 4. Move the free-gift tiers ($100+ bag, free multi-stick) higher, above the VIP CTA, since that's the strongest hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off sitewide — plus a free multi-stick, tonight only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your VIP sale closes at midnight (20% off + free gifts)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free velvet bag on $100+, free multi-stick on every order — sale ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last hours: 20% off sitewide, no VIP signup required.`
