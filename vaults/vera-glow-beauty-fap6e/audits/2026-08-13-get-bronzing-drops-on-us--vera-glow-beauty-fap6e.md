# Get Bronzing Drops on us ✨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a GWP that's actually a real GWP — "get Bronzing Drops on us" with "orders $50+, add shade Pure Gold to your cart, no code necessary" is the clearest offer mechanic e.l.f. has run all week, and I like that it's spelled out in one breath instead of buried. But then it just... keeps going. Power Grip Primer, Invisi-Stick SPF, Frizz to Rizz Trio, Glow Reviver Lip Oil, then a whole separate "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" Bubble callout again — this is basically day six of Bubble showing up somewhere in an e.l.f. send, and by the time I hit "Top Picks for You" I've forgotten the free Bronzing Drops hook that got me to open in the first place.
- Compared to yesterday's actual "e.l.f. x Bubble is selling fast" launch (6/10, my highest from them this week) and the "$17 vs $185" dupe-test send from Sally— sorry, from e.l.f. on 8/10 (7/10, their best), this one has a real threshold offer up top but then dilutes it into a full catalog scroll instead of staying focused. Ulta's 20%-off-one-item mechanic is repetitive too, but at least it doesn't make me scroll through six unrelated products to find it again.
- I'd click "shop now" under the Bronzing Drops banner specifically to hit the $50 threshold, but I wouldn't forward this — it's not exciting enough, just efficient bargain math, which is honestly my favorite kind of email math.

## What's working

- The GWP mechanic is dead simple: spend $50, add the shade, done — no code to dig up, which is more than I can say for half my Sephora Insider emails lately.
- "Gift for you!" ribbon on the hero product photo is a clean, immediate visual cue before I even read copy.
- Genuine model diversity in the Frizz To Rizz and Lip Oil sections — the lip closeup with the deep red-brown shade actually looks like a real bronzing-season pick, not a generic beauty stock shot.
- "New" flags on the SPF stick and the Frizz to Rizz Trio give me fast scan-ability for what's actually launch-worthy vs. evergreen.

## What's weak

- Way too much scroll — GWP hero, then four more full product sections, then a second Bubble hero, then a nav grid, before I even get to footer. My eye has nowhere to land twice.
- The Bubble "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" banner recycles the exact pink/teal palette and shade lineup from yesterday's actual launch send and the day before's "Bestie-coded" recap — that's three placements of the same creative in three days.
- Zero mention of Beauty Squad points or tier status anywhere, and this is exactly the kind of "spend $50+" threshold where I'm doing points math in my head — e.l.f.'s program isn't Rouge/Diamond-tier robust, but they could at least nod to it.
- No urgency/deadline on the Bronzing Drops GWP itself ("limited time" is vague) — Sally Beauty's "final days" framing, weak as that email was, at least gave me a reason to act today instead of next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — GWP hero, one or two "shop the $50 threshold" product picks, done. Move Bubble, Frizz to Rizz, and Lip Oil to their own sends; this is currently three campaigns duct-taped together.
- 2. Put an actual date on the GWP ("ends 8/16" or similar) — "for a limited time" with no date is the laziest urgency lever in the book.
- 3. Add a Beauty Squad points nudge near the $50 threshold — "you're this close to unlocking [tier]" would make the spend math feel rewarding instead of just transactional.
- 4. Retire the Bubble banner for at least a week — I've now seen this pink lip-oil trio in four consecutive sends.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $50 cart just earned a free gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code, no gimmick — full-size Bronzing Drops, automatically added.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hit $50 and Pure Gold Bronzing Drops are yours.`
