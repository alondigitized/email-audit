# Up to 50% off! NEW Black Friday deals just dropped at your store & online (Terms & excl. apply) 👏👏
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Ulta email I've gotten since 11/4, and the second today after the 11/26 "A little thank you" send, which already had the $10-off-$50 coupon. It's the same Black Friday machine with new product tiles. The screenshot was shrunk to a thin strip, so I can read the big headings, prices and brand names but not the fine print. At that size the layout reads as a long stack of pink, orange and magenta blocks with gift-box art in the margins.
- The "Over 200 new deals just joined the party" idea is the best part, because it gives me a reason for a fresh send. But the first thing I hit is the coupon again, not the new deals. I've now seen that $10 off $50 barcode on 11/4, 11/8, 11/26 and today, so it feels like filler. I'd click, but only to scan the 30% tiles, not for the coupon. Forward to a friend? No. Mark as read after the scroll.

## What's working

- "Over 200 new deals just joined the party" is a concrete, news-y hero that justifies another Black Friday email.
- The 30% off "just added" tiles for Tarte, Charlotte Tilbury, Living Proof and Juicy Couture are easy to scan. Each has a big number, a brand name and its own button.
- The $16.99 price tile and the "$133 value" callout give me real math to do.
- The Ulta Beauty Rewards line at the very top, about points turning into dollars off, is exactly my language.
- The prestige and mass mix (Supergoop, Dolce & Gabbana, Bumble and bumble, e.l.f.-style value tiles) matches how I shop Ulta's 21 Days.

## What's weak

- The $10 off $50 coupon with the barcode is the first big block, and I've seen it four times already. It buries the "new deals" story.
- The "up to 50%" in the subject is hard to find. Nearly every tile I can see is 30%, with one 20%.
- Every tile has the same "Shop now" button. Nothing says which brand is the hero.
- The email is extremely long, and the gift-wrap borders add clutter. The fragrance, hair and skincare tiles all blur together.
- The subject is about 98 characters with "Terms & excl. apply" and two clapping emojis, so it gets cut off on my phone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the "200+ new deals" hero and put the coupon below it, or drop it on days it's already been sent.
- 2. Show the real top deal up front. If something is actually 50% off, give it the first tile, or lower the "up to 50%" claim.
- 3. Add Rewards math to the product tiles, for example "30% off + 2x points", the way Sephora does with its Beauty Insider tiers.
- 4. Cut the tile count by about half and group them by category with specific button copy such as "Shop Tarte".
- 5. Move "Terms & excl. apply" out of the subject and into the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `200+ new Black Friday deals, 30% off Tarte & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: 30% off Charlotte Tilbury, Living Proof 🖤`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns in stores and online today, plus $16.99 finds.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn points on every new deal. Ends soon, so I'd start with the 30% off picks.`
- Compared with the others in my inbox:
- Sephora's "50% OFF starts now" fills the top of the screen with one number. Ulta makes me scroll for its biggest percentage.
- Drunk Elephant's "30% OFF SITEWIDE" gives a single clear rule, while Ulta gives me tile-by-tile rules.
- Sally Beauty's one white "Shop now" under "20% off sitewide" shows what an unambiguous CTA looks like. Ulta has about fifteen.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict email with a dark-mode meta tag and MSO conditional comments. QA flagged 3 warnings and a 57% pass rate. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and 11 non-HTTPS image sources. Several compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations not verified.** 55 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were probed for status, redirect chain or final landing URL.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** Ten cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1`. They are 1x1 tracking requests with no `alt`, and clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop them.
- **Insecure dynamic content image.** `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=…` is also non-HTTPS. If it carries visible content rather than a pixel, it may render as a blank or broken block.
- **Third-party cookie syncing in the email body.** The 10 Dotomi redirect pixels are third-party tracking calls. Most clients proxy or block them, so they add weight and failure points.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=165131 …` has no `alt`. It is a normal beacon, so the missing `alt` here is low impact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA flagged these `image.e.ulta.com` assets, which look like content rather than pixels:
  - `…/m/45/08964789-66f1-406b-84da-7fe1840efb8d.png`
  - `…/m/44/414d3e76-c584-4831-ac2f-067f82b68465.jpg`
  - `…/m/45/4f7c3146-d437-4161-b9bd-a1e8ce719ffd.gif` (flagged twice)
  - `…/m/1/f4520f7d-…jpg`, `…/m/1/5528bb52-…jpg`, `…/m/1/4fc3aa75-…jpg`, `…/m/1/8c9f688d-…jpg`
  - `…/m/1/852db034-…png` (flagged twice)
- **Dynamic images without alt.** `https://mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png` and `…/3eb758650b65389e.png` (Movable Ink) have no alt either. If images are blocked, these slots show nothing.
- **Caveat on alt.** The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or an intentional `alt=""`. Decorative images with `alt=""` are valid. Check that any image carrying promo or offer text has real alt text.
- **Invalid markup for the doctype.** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Strict, but `<meta name="color-scheme" content="light dark">`, `<meta name="supported-color-schemes" …>` and the Google Fonts `<link …>` are not self-closed. The other `<meta>` tags are self-closed. Most clients tolerate this, but strict XML parsers would not.
- **Web font dependency.** DM Sans loads from `fonts.googleapis.com`. Many clients strip `<link>` in `<head>`. The truncated source doesn't show a fallback stack for it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The source is truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (unverified).** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is not confirmed as absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (unverified).** The same capture caveat applies. If it is truly absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The From address is `U-Mail@e.ulta.com`, a subdomain consistent with the `e.ulta.com` image host.
- **Body compliance elements not verified.** The truncated source doesn't show the visible unsubscribe link or the postal address, so those can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Because the 55 links were skipped, no UTM parameters or landing pages could be checked against the subject's offer. The only campaign identifier visible is in the open pixel: `pr_fy26wk43_friday_bf_6am_Last`, which implies a 6 AM Friday Black Friday send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images on `image.e.ulta.com` and to the two `mi.ulta.com` images. Use `alt=""` explicitly on decorative ones.
2. Move the 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` pixels and `LiveContent.aspx` to HTTPS, or drop the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels from the email body.
3. Re-run the audit against raw message headers, not the AgentMail relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Resolve the 55 tracking links to their final URLs to check landing pages and UTM parameters.
5. Close the unclosed `<meta>` and `<link>` tags in the head, or switch to an HTML5 doctype.
6. Capture the full HTML so the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) and merge tokens can be checked.
