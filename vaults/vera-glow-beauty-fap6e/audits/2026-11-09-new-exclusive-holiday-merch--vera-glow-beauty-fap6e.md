# NEW: exclusive holiday merch!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a pretty merch catalog with no price, no offer and no deadline. Glossier has now sent me merch emails on 11/3, 11/5, 11/6 and again today, and the 11/6 "Say hello to our new holiday merch!" send was nearly the same pitch. The subject has "NEW:" and "exclusive" in it, but the body says "finally here" and has no price on any item. I can't do the points math on a tumbler when I can't see what it costs.
- The layout is clean and the product photography is cute. The pink bat, the metallic beauty bag and the keychain are all very Glossier. Compare that to Sephora's Savings Event send, which at least gave me a countdown and a real 10% deadline. Even Ulta's tired $10/$15/$20 ladder gives me something to calculate. This one just says "Shop now" four times.
- Would I click? Maybe the beauty bag, out of curiosity. Would I forward it? Only the baseball bat, as a joke, since it's SoHo/LA/London only. For me this is mark as read, and I'd be more likely to click if the price were on the page.

## What's working

- The "Our holiday merch is finally here!" hero is clear about what the email is, and the pink bat flat-lay is eye-catching.
- The 2x2 product grid with "Limited edition" labels is easy to scan. Each item gets its own button.
- The "Retail exclusive" bat block is a fun, on-brand touch with real scarcity: SoHo, LA and London only.
- The brand voice ("for decorative purposes only (mostly) 😉") is consistent and trusted.
- The text is readable on a phone, and the buttons are big enough to tap.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere, which is a big miss when the hero says "cross everyone off your list." I can't tell whether the tumbler is $25 or $45.
- There's no offer, no free shipping, no Glossier loyalty or points mention and no gift-with-purchase. Compare ILIA, which led with a free mini eye stylus at $75+.
- "Limited edition" is the only urgency. There's no deadline, no stock count, and nothing like the "sold out in under 24 hours last year" line from the 11/5 tumbler email.
- This is the fifth or so merch email in two weeks, and it repeats the 11/6 send. The tumbler was already the hero on 11/5, so this reads as a rerun.
- Every "Shop now" button looks identical and is generic. None names the item.
- The bat is a retail-only item I can only buy in three cities, so it's a dead end for most subscribers, and it takes up the whole bottom third.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price on every product tile. Do the same for the hero, and add a "from $X" line.
- 2. Add one real offer or deadline, such as free shipping over a threshold or a gift-with-purchase on merch. Gift season is when I'm stocking up, the way I do at Sephora Beauty Insider events.
- 3. Cut the send cadence, or make each merch email feature something genuinely new instead of re-showing the tumbler.
- 4. Make the buttons item-specific ("Get the tumbler", "Get the bag") and move the bat lower or shrink it.
- 5. Add a points or rewards line if Glossier has a member program, so I have a reason to buy here rather than at Sephora.

- **Subject Alt B:** `The Hot Take Tumbler is back (limited run)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited-edition drops from $__. Pink bag, tumbler, playing cards, and a bat you can only get in-store.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last year's tumbler sold out in 24 hours. Grab your holiday gifts before they're gone.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Every link is routed through Klaviyo click tracking** (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M6YZVT12JDZZW3NGD6643BWY_N`). That is expected, but the destination URLs can't be seen in the source.
- **3 "Shop now" links returned HTTP 429** (`_3`, `_6`, `_8`). The probe was rate-limited, so these are inconclusive and not broken.
- **8 links were never probed** because the 70s time budget ran out (`_10`, `_12`, `_14`, `_15`, `_16` and others). Their status is unknown. Only 12 of the material links were probed, and 11 of those were skipped.

It looks like a Klaviyo/Stripo artifact of the `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` conditional that opens in `<head>` and closes inside `<body>`. It is harmless to rendering but is a dead tracked URL.
