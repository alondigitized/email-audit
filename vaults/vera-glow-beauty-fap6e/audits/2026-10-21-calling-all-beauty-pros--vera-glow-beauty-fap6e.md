# Calling all beauty pros
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this one isn't for me. "Calling all beauty pros" is a pro-program invite, and I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond who buys at retail. I'm not a licensed esthetician or a working MUA. Still, I read all of it, because ILIA's inbox has been all VIP gift sends for a week. "The (2) gifts of VIP" went out 10/16, the repeat on 10/17, then "Before the bag disappears" on 10/19. After the 10/20 "New techniques" tutorial, this is a second non-promo send in a row. I give them credit for that.
- The execution is clean. The Artist Collective hero is pretty, and the three tiers (40% MUAs, 30% skin experts, 20% students) are easy to scan. The "know a pro?" forward block is the only hook that gives me something to do. I'd forward it to the MUA friend who is always asking me where to get ILIA wholesale. I wouldn't click Apply for myself, and I'd mark it read. My points math says there's nothing here for me. There's no VIP tie-in and no way for a regular member to earn.

## What's working

- The three-tier breakdown (MUAs 40%, skin experts 30%, students 20%) is the clearest offer structure I've seen from ILIA all month. I could do that math instantly.
- The "Join the Program →" button near the top and "Apply Now →" after the steps are dark, high-contrast, and impossible to miss.
- The 3-step "How to Apply" is short, with proof of eligibility stated up front. There are no surprises.
- Real editorial photography (backstage MUA at work, close-up winged liner) gives it credibility. It reads like a brand talking to its craft community, not a discount blast.
- The "know a pro?" forward line is a smart referral hook, and it gave me an actual reason to share.

## What's weak

- Nothing in it is for me as a consumer. Pros get 40% and I get nothing, and there is no consumer tie-in or VIP mention anywhere above the footer.
- The collapsible-looking "Who's It For?" rows have little circle icons that look interactive. The text is small, and I'd have to squint on my phone.
- There's no urgency or deadline. Compare Ulta's "Ends tonight: 20% off" and Glossier's "ends tonight" sends. This could sit in my inbox forever.
- "Perks: early product launches, and events" is vague. I'd want to know which launches, how early, and what events.
- The big pink "Makeup That Makes Your Skin Better" block is a repeat of the generic brand sign-off and adds nothing here.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Send this to pro and student lists, and give regular VIPs a different message. Sephora wouldn't send a Pro Program invite to its Rouge consumer base.
- 2. Put the 40% in the first line of the hero copy. Right now it's buried mid-scroll.
- 3. Add one concrete example of a perk, such as a named launch or an event date. That would beat "early launches and events."
- 4. Add a soft consumer track, such as a "Not a pro? Join VIP" link, so non-pros don't dead-end.
- 5. Make the tier text bigger. The accordion rows are tiny on mobile.
- **Subject Alt A:** `MUAs & estheticians: 40% off ILIA, year-round`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The ILIA Artist Collective is open`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pros get 40% off, students 20%. Apply in 3 steps.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Proof of eligibility + a few minutes = early access to every launch.`
