# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the exact same brand I flagged yesterday and the day before, and today's version somehow escalates the sprawl further. "50% off Select Beauty" gets me one screen — Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro, "TODAY ONLY" — and then it's an immediate hard cut into Rewards Bazaar, a full 100-point and 250-point redemption catalog, a "Spot it. Shop it." brand spotlight for Prada and some black-owned-brands push, AND a Holiday Drops teaser with a 24-hour countdown. That's four separate campaigns fighting for one subject line's promise.
- I did the points math instinctively the second I saw "100 POINT REWARDS" — that's the one section that's genuinely built for me as Rouge, and I'll give credit where it's due, the redemption tiles are clean and specific (Kérastase oil, K18, Versace EDT). But burying my loyalty perk under a flash sale banner that expires "today only" is bad sequencing — I don't know if I'm supposed to shop the 50% off first or cash in points first, and the email doesn't tell me.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Rewards Bazaar section specifically, since I've got points sitting there. Would I forward to a friend? No — this reads like three interns' campaigns got stapled together, and Ulta's "20% off one item" mechanic, repetitive as it is, is at least legible in one glance. I'm marking this read and moving to the points section only.

## What's working

- The "50% off Select Beauty" hero at least names actual brands (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro) instead of vague "select beauty" mush — that's more honest than Sephora's own 9/9 send.
- The 100-point reward grid is genuinely well curated for someone at my tier — Kérastase, K18, Versace, Viktor&Rolf all read like real prestige, not filler.
- "ONLINE ONLY" tags on redemption items are a small but real piece of information I need before I try to redeem in-store.
- The Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar in the footer nav is a nice touch — reminds me where I sit without me having to dig.

## What's weak

- Four unrelated campaigns (flash sale, Rewards Bazaar, brand spotlight, Holiday Drops) crammed into one email — this is worse than the "Frankenstein" Stacking Weekend send from 9/10, not better.
- The 50% off hero has zero specificity on the discount mechanic — no code, no minimum spend, no exclusions spelled out beyond "Exclusions/terms apply" in tiny print.
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency on the hero gets diluted by a "24 HOURS ONLY" callout later for Holiday Drops — two competing countdowns in one email kills the urgency of both.
- The repeating pink ribbon text "NEW REWARDS ARE HERE!" tiled across the banner reads like a layout glitch at first glance, not an intentional design element.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section (Prada, "black-owned brands") has no connective tissue to anything else in the email — feels bolted on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: one flash-sale email for the 50%-off Kiehl's/Bobbi Brown/Azzaro deal, one loyalty email for Rewards Bazaar. Trying to do both kills urgency on the sale and buries the points redemption I actually came for.
- 2. Move the points balance ("You have X points") that's sitting in the tiny top utility bar down into the Rewards Bazaar section itself, next to the tiles — I shouldn't have to scroll back up to remember my balance while deciding what to redeem.
- 3. Cut "Spot it. Shop it." and Holiday Drops entirely from this send — they dilute the flash-sale urgency and read like campaign leftovers, not a coordinated push.
- 4. Add an actual discount code or minimum-spend line under the hero — "Exclusions/terms apply" alone reads like Ulta's buried fine print, not a clean 50%-off callout.
- 5. Fix the repeating pink ribbon copy — either make it one clear banner ("NEW REWARDS JUST DROPPED") or remove the tiling, it currently looks broken.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `100 new redemptions just dropped, Rouge — see what's yours`
