# Comfort for every budget
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again, and this is the first send in a week that gives me a clear number. "30% OFF sitewide for members" is white on red at the top, and I read it in half a second. The price-tier tags ($50, $75 and $100 and under) are a smart budget-shopper structure. It's the same 30% member offer from 11/18's "Early access is open!", so it feels like a rerun.
- The email gets a little confusing past the banner. It runs three big lifestyle photos with three identical "Shop Now" buttons, then a best-sellers row with no products I can see, just a gap. I'm a points-math girl, and the "Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points (That's a $5 reward!)" line is the most interesting thing here. It's tiny, though, and it sits above the offer.
- I'd open this, skim it, and probably not click. It's a footwear sale in a beauty inbox. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF sitewide for members" banner is big and instantly readable. It does better than Ilia's number shuffle.
- The $50/$75/$100 and-under tags make the offer easy to scan by budget. Sephora's Savings Event emails do something similar with price tiers.
- The "$5 reward" translation on the Skechers Plus bonus points is the kind of points math I actually want.
- The gift-tag graphics make it clearly holiday and clearly Black Friday. It's seasonal, not stale.
- The footer links (Women/Men/Kids/Gift Guide/Sale) are clean and easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The "Best-Selling Styles" section shows large empty white space where the shoes should be, with only a few small shoe thumbnails far down. It looks like a broken-image gap, and it's the weakest part of the email.
- Three identical "Shop Now" buttons give me no product-specific reason to click. A button that says "Shop slippers $50 & under" would do more.
- The member requirement, "must be logged into a Skechers Plus account," is only in the fine print at the bottom. The banner says "for members" but never says what that means or how to join.
- "AI-generated model" labels show on the photos. It reads as less trustworthy, and I'm not the target for these shots anyway.
- This is the 30% offer again from 11/18 and 11/19, so it's the third beat on the same promo in four days. Ulta's 21 Days and Sephora's Savings Event at least rotate the hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the best-sellers module. Show real products with prices, and make each one a link to its own product page.
- 2. Move the Skechers Plus join prompt and the "$5 reward" line into the hero, next to the 30% banner. State the requirement up front.
- 3. Replace the three identical CTAs with tier-specific ones, such as "Shop $50 & under".
- 4. Add a clear end date and time near the banner, not only in the fine print.
- 5. Vary the hero between Black Friday sends so the promo doesn't feel like a rerun.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your member 30% off ends 11/26`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gifts under $50, $75 and $100, plus 1000 bonus points when you join Plus.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Slippers, sneakers and loafers at member pricing. Shop before it ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not probed:** 39 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped (e.g. `https://click.emails.skechers.com/ope…`). Redirect resolution and destination status are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_PRICE_ENGAGEDL90D_EN_11212026`
  - It uses `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and open tracking may under-report.
  - It sends `mi_u=8108776175`, a numeric recipient ID, in cleartext.
  - It is hosted on a third-party domain (`ink1000.com`), not the sender's own domain.
- **Insecure favicon:** `<link rel="icon" href="http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/lib/fe3515707564057f721c77/...">` is `http://` and on a different subdomain (`emails.nl.`) than the other image host (`image.emails.skechers.com`). Email clients ignore favicons, so this is low impact, but it is a stray insecure reference.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, `http://` source:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`. If it is content, it is invisible with images off or to screen readers. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixel:** `o.gif` has no `alt`. A pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=...>` has no `lang` attribute, which screen readers use to pick pronunciation.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Skechers&reg;</title>` does not describe the message.
- **Text size adjust:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` stops iOS users from enlarging text.
- **Layout wrapper:** the visible outer wrapper has `role="presentation"`, which is good. I can't confirm this for the truncated remainder.
