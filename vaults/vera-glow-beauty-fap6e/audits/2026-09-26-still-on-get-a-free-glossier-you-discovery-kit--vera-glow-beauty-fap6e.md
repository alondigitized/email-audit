# Still on! Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I already flagged this exact GWP last week — this is the same "Get a free Glossier You Discovery Kit" send from 9/22, just now wearing a "still on!" urgency wrapper. That's not a new campaign, that's Glossier realizing the first send didn't convert enough people and running it back with a bolder subject. The hero shot is genuinely pretty — those little pink perfume vials in the tray catch my eye every time — and the "BELIEVE THE HYPE" review block with real star ratings and named customers is the strongest trust signal in the whole email.
- But I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this. It's a GWP dressed as generosity ("free"), the threshold is $50 which is basically a given for me anyway, and there's zero mention of Rouge or Diamond points math — which is exactly the kind of thing that gets me to open a beauty email. This reads like it's talking to a first-time Glossier browser, not someone who's cross-shopping Sephora Beauty Insider events against Ulta 21 Days.

## What's working

- The perfume-tray hero photo is clean, on-brand, and immediately readable as "fragrance," so I know the category before I even read copy.
- "BELIEVE THE HYPE" with three named reviewers and star ratings gives real social proof — better than the plain-text Marissa-cosplay ILIA keeps sending me.
- The offer mechanic line ("Offer automatically applied at checkout") removes at least one layer of friction — no code to remember.
- Layout is calm and uncluttered, a relief after e.l.f.'s "Mirror Mix" album-release chaos email.

## What's weak

- This is a repeat send of the 9/22 GWP with a new subject slapped on — I already docked that one for calling a gift-with-purchase "free," and doing it twice in five days makes it feel like Glossier is just A/B testing urgency language on the same tired offer.
- No loyalty/points signal at all — as a Rouge/Diamond member who's always doing the math, an email with zero mention of point multipliers or event tie-in just doesn't feel built for me.
- $50 threshold isn't "still time"-urgent, it's just... Tuesday. There's no real countdown, no date, nothing that creates credible urgency.
- The CTA buttons ("Get yours," "Shop now") are generic — none of them name the actual product I'd be getting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lose the recycled GWP entirely for a beat, or at minimum change the visual — running the identical tray photo and offer as 9/22 with just a new headline is going to train me to skip these.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("ends Sunday") if this is genuinely a last-chance send — "still on" implies urgency but shows no clock.
- 3. Work in a loyalty angle — even one line like "counts toward your rewards" would make this feel like it's talking to a member instead of a stranger.
- 4. Make CTA copy specific: "Shop You Eau de Parfum" beats a bare "Shop now" three times in a row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: free discovery kit ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $50 order = a free fragrance kit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `4 minis, 0 extra steps — applied at checkout`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The kit everyone's been asking to bring back`
