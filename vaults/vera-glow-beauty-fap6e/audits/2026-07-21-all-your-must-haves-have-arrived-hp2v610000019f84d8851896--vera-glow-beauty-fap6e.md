# All your must-haves have arrived
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- I need to lead with the obvious: this is the second Sephora email today with the subject "All your must-haves have arrived." Different barcode in the footer, same subject line. After the twin "Open for our bag edit" sends on July 20th and the double "4X points on all hair care" before that, Sephora is now three-for-three on same-day duplicates this week. I have a whole folder of these. At some point the brand starts training me to ignore the first send because the second one will inevitably follow.
- What's actually inside though? Legitimately interesting. "Just Dropped & Only Here" as the hero positions this as an exclusives launch roundup — which is precisely my catnip. I track new Sephora arrivals the way I track Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty drops. The Dyson Supersonic Multi-Styler and Dryer as the hero product is a high-conviction call, and the 4X points badge shows up early enough for me to immediately start doing the math on a $600 hair tool. Below the Dyson there's a multi-brand grid — Jo Malone, Crown Affair, Touche.Land, Hung Vangio Beauty, and a few others — all new or newish, all exclusive-feeling.
- But the grid is where the email loses me. It's a catalog scroll with no curation logic, no second hero that earns it, and no clarity on whether 4X applies to all of it or just the Dyson. I'd screenshot the Dyson section, probably click through out of muscle memory, and never forward this. Mark as read.

## What's working

- "Just Dropped & Only Here" is exactly the launch energy I respond to — exclusive newness at Sephora is their actual edge over Ulta, and this headline says that
- Dyson Supersonic Multi-Styler as the featured hero is smart placement; it's high-intent, high-ticket, and a genuine Sephora exclusive drop
- 4X points badge is visible before you even hit the product grid — as Rouge tier I'm immediately calculating what 4X on a Dyson means in point dollars
- "Shop | Read Reviews" dual CTA on the Dyson respects that this is a considered purchase, not an impulse buy; giving me the review path is actually useful
- Crown Affair and Jo Malone appearing together signals curation, not a bulk dump — these are brands I actually have opinions about

## What's weak

- Duplicate send, full stop — second "All your must-haves have arrived" today, which continues a pattern of same-day duplicates that started eroding my trust in Sephora's send list this week
- The multi-brand grid below the Dyson becomes a catalog with no hierarchy; Sincere Yours, Touche.Land, and Hung Vangio Beauty all compete equally and I can't identify which one is the "next" hero
- 4X scope is completely ambiguous — is it on new arrivals only? On the Dyson only? Sephora has been running 4X on everything lately so the badge has started to feel decorative rather than informative
- Zero urgency — no end date on the 4X, no "just landed" timestamp, nothing that tells me whether Touche.Land is available now or pre-order

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into two sends** — a dedicated Dyson launch email and a "new brands just arrived" roundup; cramming both into one email cuts click depth on the high-ticket item and buries the brand grid
- 2. **Clarify the 4X window explicitly in the hero**: "Earn 4X on new launches through Sunday" gives me something to calendar and act on — compare to how e.l.f. ran their Jumbo Power Grip Primer send on July 17, which told me exactly when the 3X window closed
- 3. **Badge the actual Sephora exclusives** in the grid tiles — if Jo Malone and Crown Affair are Only Here, say it at the tile level; that's Sephora's moat against Ulta and they're leaving it implicit
- 4. **Audit the send list before pressing send** — the duplicate pattern is now a trust problem, not a design problem; a spam-folder complaint is a worse outcome than a delayed send

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 violation)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users from zooming on mobile. Fails WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Should be removed or set to `maximum-scale=5`.

**`text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Disabling text-size-adjust on all these elements overrides user accessibility settings. Acceptable for `body` as a reset; applying to every element is excessive and may break OS-level font scaling in some clients.

**Malformed robots meta value**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
Value should be `noindex` (no space). Gmail and some webmail clients do cache/index email content; the space renders this directive ineffective.

**`ExternalClass` line-height declared twice**
```css
.ExternalClass * { line-height: 112%; }
.ExternalClass p, .ExternalClass span, .ExternalClass font, .ExternalClass td { line-height: 112%; }
```
Redundant — the wildcard selector already covers all descendants. No rendering defect, but dead CSS.
