# Time for something new 🛍️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay — this is the exact same "Something new is here" email I already opened today, so no surprise here, but let's actually dig into it since I skimmed it fast the first time. The hero is clean: a nice product-still-life of the MAC Lustreglass tube and the Too Faced Sweet Peach palette, "SHOP NOW" front and center. Where it loses me is at the very bottom — the Beauty Insider tier bar shows the marker sitting all the way at "INSIDER," the base tier, when I've been Rouge for ages. That's the second time this week I've caught Sephora's system not reflecting my actual status (I flagged the K18 "0 points" bug on 8/9), and for a program that's supposed to be my main loyalty flex, this is a real miss.
- The trial-size mascara banner up top is genuinely the most compelling thing in this email — free trial size with a $75 purchase is a classic Sephora move and I'd actually consider adding it to a cart I'm already building. But everything below the "NEW arrivals" wall (ten products in a grid) is just noise scrolling — no curation, no "for you" logic, just an alphabet soup of new launches across categories I don't all care about (acne moisturizer next to a $98 fragrance next to a hair mousse).
- I'd scroll past most of it, tap the trial-size banner if I were already shopping, and not forward this — it's the same "new arrivals wall" format I called out on 8/8 for "Ready, set, shop," just with a stronger hook up top.

## What's working

- The "$10 Bonus Card with $75 Digital Gift Card" banner up top is a real, concrete offer I can do the math on instantly.
- Free trial-size mascara from Tilbury/Dior/Lancôme with any $30 purchase is a genuinely covetable perk that feels earned, not generic.
- The hero product photography (perfume bottle + Too Faced palette) is warm and on-brand — feels like Sephora, not a stock photo dump.
- Rihanna's Fenty Beauty lip duo tagged "LIMITED EDITION" is a smart scarcity flag that'll catch my eye specifically.

## What's weak

- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom shows my marker at "INSIDER" — I'm Rouge, and this is the same personalization failure I called out on the K18 sends yesterday, just a different flavor of it.
- Ten new-arrival tiles in a row with zero curation logic — mascara serum next to $98 EDP next to acne moisturizer — reads like a warehouse dump, not "picks from the most famous names" as promised.
- "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" is the exact same "new arrivals" beat as "Ready, set, shop—new beauty has landed" from 8/8, just reworded — Sephora's repeating this format at a pace I'm starting to notice.
- No skin-tone or category personalization anywhere — I get the same undifferentiated grid a brand-new subscriber would get.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the Rouge status sync** — this is the second loyalty-tier display bug this week (K18 "0 points," now the tier bar showing Insider). If the backend can't reflect real status, don't show the bar at all; showing it wrong is worse than omitting it.
- 2. **Curate the "new arrivals" grid by my purchase history** — instead of ten random new items, show 3-4 in categories I actually buy (I skew skincare/makeup, not fragrance-heavy).
- 3. **Move the Fenty Beauty limited-edition duo higher** — that's the actual scarcity hook, not a generic "something new" headline that's now a repeat format.
- 4. **Vary the hero language across sends** — three "new arrivals" emails in under a week (8/8, 8/9, 8/10) with near-identical structure is fatiguing the format itself.
