# The latest + greatest + 20% off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, another day, another "20% off" gate for me to join VIP — I'm already logged into my ILIA account, they know it, and they're still making me click "JOIN VIP" like I haven't been buying their concealer for two years. This is the fourth ILIA VIP-gated send I've logged this week (9/15 lip+cheek, 9/16 "Art of the Eye," 9/17 "Win-win-win"), and the "20% off" mechanic is now so recycled it's basically wallpaper. The product grid is genuinely nice — real hero shots of the Thru Line Eyeliner, Sun Serum SPF, Skin Blur Concealer, Overglaze Lip Gloss — and the "gifts at checkout" tier is decent bait, but I've seen this exact flatlay-collage-into-VIP-CTA structure so many times now that my thumb is on autopilot to archive.
- I'm not clicking this one. I might glance at the Skin Blur Concealer shot since I'm between holy-grails right now, but the "sign up to get the discount you're being shown" trick is the same move Ulta's been pulling with "20% off one qualifying item" all month, and Sephora at least fronts the VIP tier math instead of dangling a fake headline discount. Not forwarding this — it's not bad enough to roast with a friend, just tired enough to skip.

## What's working

- The top collage hero (eyeliner, powder compact, serum bottle, gloss) is genuinely well-styled — moody, cohesive, feels like ILIA's brand, not stock photography.
- Real product photography under "latest + greatest" — Thru Line Eyeliner, Sun Serum SPF 50, Skin Blur Concealer, Overglaze Lip Gloss all have their own clean shot + one-line benefit copy, which is more than Sally Beauty or e.l.f. usually bother with.
- "Gifts at checkout" section with the free multi-stick and velvet bag is a real, visible loyalty perk — not buried fine print.
- "VIP treatment" box spells out free shipping, points, and early access in plain bullets, so at least the value prop isn't hidden if I'd bother reading it.

## What's weak

- The 20% off is gated behind "JOIN VIP" — I'm already a customer, this should've been "here's your 20% off" not "join to unlock it," same bait-and-switch energy as ILIA's own 9/15 "20% off all lip + cheek favorites" send.
- Zero personalization — no name, no skin-tone match callout despite "Personalized Shade Match" being listed as a brand perk in the footer, no acknowledgment I'm already logged in.
- This is the fourth near-identical VIP/discount push from ILIA this week — the offer mechanic hasn't moved even though the hero products rotate.
- No urgency anywhere — no deadline on the 20% or the checkout gifts, so there's zero reason to act today versus next week.
- The "latest + greatest" framing implies new launches, but Thru Line Eyeliner and Overglaze Lip Gloss have both shown up in my inbox in the last few days under different subject lines — this isn't actually new to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop the VIP-join gate for known members and just show the discount applied — that single fix would stop this from feeling like a bait-and-switch every single send.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the 20% off and the checkout gifts — "ends Sunday" turns this from skippable to something I'd actually open before it lapses.
- 3. Personalize the hero row — lead with my shade match or my last purchase category instead of a generic 9-square collage everyone gets.
- 4. Stop reusing "latest + greatest" language for products I've already seen twice this week — either make it genuinely new or call it what it is (a restock/sale reminder).
- 5. Move "vip treatment" perks (free shipping, points, early access) above the fold — that's the actual differentiator for someone like me who's tracking points math across Sephora and Ulta too, and it's currently buried two-thirds down.
