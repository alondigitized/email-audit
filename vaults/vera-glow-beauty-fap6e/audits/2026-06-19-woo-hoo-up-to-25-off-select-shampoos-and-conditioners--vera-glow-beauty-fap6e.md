# Woo-hoo 🎉 Up to 25% off select shampoos and conditioners 🫧
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I have context walking into this one. The last two Sephora sends I clocked — both June 16, both "Get up to 50% off select beauty today 💖" — were a cadence mess that left me genuinely suspicious of whatever comes next. And now, June 19, Sephora lands in my inbox with "Woo-hoo 🎉" and a haircare-specific 25% off. My first reaction is: wait, did the discount just get cut in half? 50% last week, 25% this week. That math lands weird even before I open it.
- Once I'm in, the email is doing a lot. Haircare hero at the top, then a "Beauty For All" catch-all section, then a product grid, then a 500 bonus points section, then same-day delivery, then Father's Day gifts. It's like three separate campaigns stapled together. I'm a Rouge member — I actually care about the 500 bonus points mechanic — but I have to scroll and actively hunt for it instead of having it front and center where it belongs.
- The "Father's Day Gifts, Handled" block at the bottom on June 19 — two days before Father's Day — isn't exactly misplaced timingwise, but it reads as a total non-sequitur when the hero is my shampoo routine. I'm not marking this as spam, but I'm also not forwarding it. This is a "quickly scroll to see if my Olaplex is in the sale and close" email.

## What's working

- **The haircare category hook is differentiated.** After weeks of generic "50% off select beauty," narrowing to shampoos and conditioners actually stands out — this is more like the e.l.f. Hair bundled haircare send that scored a 9, where a specific category focus does real work.
- **"Save and Earn 500 Extra Points" is the sleeper star.** As a Rouge member doing points math constantly, seeing a bonus points mechanic is a signal to stop scrolling. It's there — I just wish it were higher.
- **"Save through Sun, 6/21" is a real deadline.** Credible, dated, not "while supplies last" vagueness. That's good.
- **Same-day delivery callout is useful.** "FREE Same-Day Delivery" with a promo code is a concrete perk that closes the "should I just go in-store" loop for me.
- **The layout is clean — no render bugs, no broken images.**

## What's weak

- **25% feels like a step down after 50%.** The June 11 "STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off" trained me to expect a bigger number. 25% off shampoos doesn't register as a major event — it reads as a quiet clearance, not a reason to reload my cart.
- **"Woo-hoo" in the subject is doing a lot of emotional lifting the offer can't back up.** If the offer were stronger, the energy would work. At 25% off select haircare, it just reads as trying too hard.
- **The email is three campaigns stacked.** Haircare + Beauty For All essentials + Father's Day is not a coherent shopping moment. Compare this to the ILIA sends that stay single-SKU, single-offer focused — this feels like a content calendar collision.
- **Father's Day block reads as an afterthought for me specifically.** I'm 29F shopping for myself. A "Shop Men's Gifts" block below my conditioner sale is a tonal detour.
- **500 bonus points buried mid-scroll.** This is the offer that would actually move me. Charlotte Tilbury buries its gifts with purchase too — but at least CT ties it to a product story. Here the points mechanic feels like a footnote.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the loyalty mechanic.** "Up to 25% off haircare + earn 500 bonus points" in the hero — not buried mid-email. For Rouge members, that double benefit is the whole reason to click today vs. tomorrow.
- 2. **Pull the deadline into the subject line.** "Save through Sun" is in the email body, but it's not in the subject. That's leaving urgency on the table.
- 3. **Cut the Father's Day block or silo it.** Either send a dedicated Father's Day email (which presumably went out already) or put it in a footer footnote — don't make me scroll past a men's gifting section to find my shampoo deals.
- 4. **Feature 2-3 specific products in the hero.** Glossier's recent "Up to 25% off ends tonight!" email — even though they had the cadence problem of sending it twice in one day — at least made the product the hero. Show me the Olaplex or the Kérastase. Give me a reason to trust the "select" curation.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** (`<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`) — prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 Resize text, level AA). Should be removed or set to `maximum-scale=5`.

- **`text-size-adjust: none` applied globally** — the CSS rule `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; ... }` suppresses OS-level font scaling for visually impaired users. This is applied to `body`-level elements, making it impossible for accessibility tools to override.

- **`robots` meta value has an invalid space**: `content="no index"` — the correct value is `noindex` (no space). Clients and crawlers that process email web-view URLs may fail to honor this. Low severity for email, but indicates a template defect.

- **`<title>Sephora</title>`** — generic, non-descriptive title provides no context in browser preview or screen reader tab announcements. Should reflect the campaign subject (`Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners`).
