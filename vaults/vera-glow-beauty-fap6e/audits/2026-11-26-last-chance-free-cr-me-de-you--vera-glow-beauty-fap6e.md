The confirmed problems are missing alt text on 5 images and a malformed tracking `<span>`. The header-based compliance warnings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

It is likely a Klaviyo or Stripo artifact, and it carries a click-tracking URL that nothing can follow.
