# 🎉 Open for new items
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is "okay, a catalog, fine." This is the same "New Arrivals" email Sephora sent me earlier today under this subject, and it has no offer in it. The rum-cake-looking Wild Cousin perfume hero is pretty. I still can't tell what I'm supposed to do except browse. The "You have 0 points" line in the header also stings a little, because I'm Rouge and I do points math on everything.
- Sephora beat itself on 10/20 ("Beauty that's found only here") with 4X points and a free sample bag. Both gave me a reason to act today. This one gives me a reason to scroll. Ulta would have led with a 21 Days deal or a points multiplier on the new launches. Glossier at least sent "Want a free gift?" on 10/20 and gave me one hook.
- Would I click? Maybe on the Saie vault or the Benefit gift set, because "$76 value" is at least a number. Forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for the Savings Event.

## What's working

- The "Only at Sephora" badges and "Limited Edition" tags on Saie, Benefit, Kate McLeod and Make Up For Ever tell me what's exclusive, so I know what I can't get at Ulta.
- Value callouts like "$155 ($225 value)" and "$44 ($76 value)" give me something to do math on.
- The product grid is clean and easy to scan, with clear price tags and "NEW" labels.
- The app-exclusive "First Access: Holiday Drops, 24 hours only" block is the one real urgency cue in the email.
- Names like Paula's Choice, PHLUR and Saie are brands I'd actually shop.

## What's weak

- There's no offer anywhere. Nothing about points, a promo or free shipping. Ulta's 20% coupon emails at least give me something to act on.
- The "You have 0 points" line makes me feel like a stranger. I'm Rouge, and the email isn't treating me like one.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar near the bottom has no points info, and "Rewards Bazaar" is buried.
- The hero is a perfume with no name, price or product link. "Already obsessed" is filler, and the CTA says "Shop Now" for the whole site.
- The email is long, and the "Spot it. Shop it." section has three competing mini-heroes (the app drop, the "Bigger is Better" value sizes and Kayali). It reads as a catalog dump.
- The hero rum cake with whipped cream looks like dessert, not perfume. I got confused for a second.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a points hook on the hero, such as "2X points on new arrivals this week", personalized to tier. That's the single biggest lift.
- 2. Link the hero to the Wild Cousin product and put a price on it. Right now it's a mood board.
- 3. Swap "You have 0 points" for something that makes sense for a Rouge member, or drop it.
- 4. Cut the grid from 6 products to 4 and make the app-exclusive block the single secondary CTA.
- 5. Don't send this under the same subject twice in a day.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Limited editions that won't be at Ulta. Rouge members earn 2X.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA on 3 images:**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAn…`, which looks like another open-tracking pixel.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, the tracking pixel above.
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`, the nav header image. This is a content image, so its missing alt is the one that affects users. When images are blocked or read by a screen reader, the nav labels are lost.
  - The two pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling:** the CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust: none` on `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This disables user text-size scaling on iOS and Windows.
- **Language:** the visible `<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid. The correct value is `noindex`, so parsers may ignore it. The impact is low.
- **Title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic. This is low severity.
