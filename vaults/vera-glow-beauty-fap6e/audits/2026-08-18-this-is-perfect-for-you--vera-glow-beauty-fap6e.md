## What's weak

- The hero product is a kids' light-up "Skechers Garage" toy sneaker with a truck graphic — I don't have kids, I've never shopped kids' shoes, this is nowhere near my world.
- The "more options" grid is all Care Bears sneakers for girls/kids — again, not remotely me.

- 1. Fix the retargeting logic entirely — I've now been sent a men's walker three times and now a kids' toy sneaker; whatever "last viewed" signal is feeding this is broken and burning trust with every send.
- 2. If this is meant to be a browse-abandonment email, show the actual item I looked at, not a randomly assigned SKU from a totally different shopper segment.
- 3. Lead with the 40% off sitewide offer instead of burying it below a product I never asked about — that's the one universally relevant hook in this whole email.
- 4. Segment sends by actual account activity/purchase category before firing a "perfect for you" subject line — it's not perfect for anyone if the product doesn't match the person.
