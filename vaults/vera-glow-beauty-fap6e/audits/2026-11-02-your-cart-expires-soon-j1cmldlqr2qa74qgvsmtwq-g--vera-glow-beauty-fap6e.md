# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- I haven't put anything in a Skechers cart, so "your cart expires soon" is the same fake nudge I've been getting for two weeks. The 10/30 "Did you forget something?" and 11/1 "Pick up where you left off!" emails said the same thing. The headline then pivots to "YOU LIKED THESE / AND YOU WEREN'T WRONG," a skull-painted Day of the Dead UNO sneaker I never looked at. The subject, hero and offer don't line up.
- The real offer is a navy banner halfway down: 20% off for Skechers Plus members, valid through 11/2, which is the day this landed. That's the same 20% Ulta's $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder gave me, but Ulta put the math at the top. Here I had to scroll past six shoes and a black "Shop all best sellers" bar to find it, and then read fine print saying I must be logged in and it's online only.
- I wouldn't click, and I wouldn't forward it. It's a mark-as-read. If I were a Skechers person, the 20% would have me open the app, but I'd resent the cart-expiry pressure to get there.

## What's working

- The 20% OFF banner is bold, white on navy, and readable in a second. When I finally reach it, the offer is clear.
- "COMPLETE YOUR ORDER" is an unambiguous button, with a single hero shoe above it.
- The "Plus members" language ties the offer to a loyalty tier, which I always want to do math on.
- The six-shoe grid is clean, with consistent product tiles and readable names.
- The footer utilities (app, curbside, Afterpay/Klarna, store finder) are tidy and scannable.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a cart expiry, but the email never shows a cart. I get a hero shoe and a "More great options" grid, so it feels bait-and-switch.
- The 20% is buried below the fold, behind the hero and the grid. Sephora's Collection 30% emails and Ulta's coupon ladder lead with the number.
- "NO TRICKS, JUST TREATS" Halloween copy and a Day of the Dead skull sneaker are stale on Nov 2. The offer expires the same day it lands, so there's no real urgency.
- The 20% is Plus-members-only, online-only, login-required, and excludes premieres, sale styles and select accessories. That's a lot of exclusions for a banner that reads as an open promo.
- Nothing here is beauty or anything I buy. It's women's sneakers with no skin, shade or event relevance, so I feel like a stranger on a list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the 20% Plus offer in the hero and make the expiry date the headline. Move the product grid below it.
- 2. Only send "cart" language when there's an actual cart, and show the real item with its price, size and stock status.
- 3. Refresh the seasonal creative the day the calendar flips. Nov 2 shouldn't still be Halloween.
- 4. Cut the cadence. This is roughly the twelfth abandoned-cart-style send, and a single well-timed reminder would beat six near-duplicates.
- 5. State the exclusions in one line near the offer rather than only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off ends tonight for Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your UNO Alma De Guerrero + 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus members save 20% online through 11/2. Log in to apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your pick is still in stock. Check out before the deal ends.`
