# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is a genuinely different beat from Sephora — not another 50%-off flash sale, it's the Rewards Bazaar, a points-redemption catalog. And I'm into that concept in theory, I'm Rouge tier, I do the points math obsessively. But the second thing I see under the header is "You have 0 points" — again. That's now five sends running I've logged this week (both K18s, "50% off select faves" twice, "DID YOU HEAR THE BIG NEWS") where that same broken merge field shows up, and this time it's actively undermining the entire premise of the email, which is "look at all these rewards you've earned to spend." If I actually have 0 points, why are you showing me a 15,000-point Dr. Dennis Gross mask?
- The reward grid itself is nicely laid out — real product shots, point costs, and scarcity counts ("3 AVAILABLE NOW") that create real urgency instead of fake urgency. Haus Labs at 4,500 points, Milk Makeup Sticks Vault at 2,000 — that's useful info I'd actually scroll for if the header math weren't broken.
- Would I click? Only to go check my actual points balance on the app, not from this email's CTA. I wouldn't forward it — this is Sephora talking to itself about a bug, not to me.

## What's working

- The Rewards Bazaar concept is a nice change of pace from the endless "50% off select faves" sale emails I've been getting all week — it's segmented content, not another discount blast.
- My Rouge tier tab is correctly highlighted red in the tier nav at the bottom — that's a real personalization signal, unlike the broken points count.
- Scarcity counts ("16 AVAILABLE NOW," "3 AVAILABLE NOW") on each reward feel honest and specific, not manufactured urgency.
- Product photography is clean and on-brand — the Fugazzi bag, the Crown Affair silk set, all shot consistently.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" is STILL sitting under the header — fifth send I've logged this week with this exact bug, and it's the worst possible email for it to appear in since the whole point is "spend your points."
- No indication of how many points I actually have or need to reach the next reward — for a points-math person like me, that's the one thing I actually want front and center.
- The hero says "shopped and earned points" but gives zero specifics — vague personalization, not real personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points merge field before anything else ships — this is bug #5 this week and it's now actively contradicting the email's core CTA.
- 2. Add my real point balance and progress toward the nearest reward tier ("You're 320 points from the Milk Makeup Sticks Vault") — that's the hook that gets Rouge members like me to actually click.
- 3. Move the highest-scarcity items ("3 AVAILABLE NOW") higher — that urgency is more compelling than the generic "grab rewards while you can" hero copy.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `You're closer to a free reward than you think — see what's available now`
