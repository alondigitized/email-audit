# Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend ❣️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I already scored today's "Stacking Weekend" email 8/10 this morning off the subject line and the concept, and seeing the actual render confirms it — this thing is stacked with legit hooks and it doesn't feel like padding. The "+" between the 50% off wheel and the 500 bonus points wheel is a genuinely clean visual explainer of the math, which is exactly the kind of thing I'm doing in my head anyway with Rouge points. Then it keeps going: free hair sample bag with $45 spend, the $10 Beauty Insider Cash redemption block, a "Save on Must Haves" product grid with actual SKUs (NEST Madagascar Vanilla, Grande Cosmetics lash serum, The INKEY List patches), a second 50% off Select Beauty banner, RHODE's new Spotivape shape, and a Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom. That's a lot of email.
- Where it loses me a little compared to the 9/3 "point of view" launch drop I logged — that one felt like a curated editorial moment, this one feels like Sephora threw every lever they have into one send because it's a big promo weekend. It's effective, not elegant. Compare to Ulta's 9/4 "20% off, ends today" email — Ulta's still running the same barcode-on-book-stack hero they've had since 7/26, one flat mechanic, no stacking logic, no product grid. Sephora is doing something structurally smarter here even if it's visually busier.
- I'd click. The 50% off Select Beauty banner is the one I'd tap first since Sol de Janeiro and Stila are actually in my rotation. I'd forward this to my group chat — the stacking logic is worth explaining to my friend who never remembers to redeem her Beauty Insider Cash.

## What's working

- The "50% off + 500 bonus points = the best deals" math is spelled out visually with the "+" between two circles — I don't have to do mental gymnastics like I do with Ulta's coupon stacking rules.
- Beauty Insider Cash reminder ($10 back at 500 points) is placed right where I'm already thinking about points, which is smart sequencing.
- The "Must Haves" grid names real products at real prices (Grande Cosmetics $68, NEST $108) — no vague "shop now" mystery box.
- RHODE's Spotivape teaser under "New from RHODE" is a legit launch signal, not recycled creative — feels current, unlike Skechers' fourth rerun of the same die-cast truck toy I've flagged three times now.
- Tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) at the footer is a nice touch that acknowledges the loyalty structure even if it's just decorative here.

## What's weak

- This is a genuinely long scroll — by the time I hit RHODE and the "Come Hang with Us" event block, I've forgotten the top offer was time-bound to 9/8.
- Multiple asterisked "exclusions/terms apply" callouts stacked across different offers (hair sample bag, 50% off, free samples) start to feel like a shell game — which exclusion applies to which deal isn't clear at a glance.
- "TODAY ONLY" on the second 50% off banner contradicts the "Ends 9/8" on the first 50% off wheel above it — is it one deal or two overlapping ones? That's confusing, not urgent.
- No skin tone/type personalization anywhere despite Sephora clearly having my purchase history — this is a mass blast, not a "known customer" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the "Ends 9/8" vs. "TODAY ONLY" conflict on the two 50% off banners — pick one deadline or clearly differentiate the two offers so I'm not doing detective work.
- 2. Move the Beauty Insider Cash + points math higher, right under the hero, since that's the actual differentiator versus Ulta's flat 20% mechanic — right now it's competing with six other blocks below the fold.
- 3. Add one personalized product rec (e.g., "based on your last order") into the Must Haves grid — even one swapped tile would beat Sally Beauty and Skechers-style generic blasts I've logged this week.
- 4. Trim the bottom third — the RHODE teaser and "Come Hang with Us" event block dilute the stacking-weekend focus; split them into a separate send.
