# Oooh, it’s 50% off select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I already flagged this one yesterday morning and it hasn't changed a single beat — subject says "50% off select beauty ❤️" and I open it to a full Hair Styling Awards digest, twelve categories deep, "Sleekness and Shine," "Definition," "Volume," each with a five-star-rated product and a "SEPHORA APPROVED" badge. The actual 50% off beauty banner I was baited with doesn't show up until I've scrolled through basically the entire email, wedged between a 15% shampoo/conditioner offer and a "Get 500 bonus points" tile.
- The Hair Awards content itself is fine — I actually like a curated best-of list, and Amika, Olaplex, Kérastase, Living Proof are all brands I trust — but this is false advertising on the subject line, full stop. Sephora's done this exact bait-and-switch on me twice now in 24 hours (this and the near-identical "LAST CHANCE" send from yesterday), and my "You have 0 points" ribbon problem from earlier in the week is thankfully gone at least, so that's one thing improved.
- Compared to Glossier's fifth Labor Day rehash this week or Sally Beauty's third Appiversary repeat, this isn't fatigue from repetition — it's worse, it's a mislabeled email. I'm not clicking through twelve hair product tiles to find a sale that should've been the hero. Mark as read, no forward.

## What's working

- The hair awards format is genuinely useful — five-star ratings and "Sephora Approved" badges give me real trust signals I don't get from most brands.
- Product roster is on-brand and premium: Amika, Olaplex, Kérastase, Living Proof, Moroccanoil — names I actually buy.
- Beauty Insider tier bar and points math ("Get 500 bonus points") are visible near the bottom, which is the loyalty math I'm always checking for.
- No render bugs — clean product photography, badges display correctly, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- The subject line promises 50% off beauty; the email delivers an awards showcase with the actual offer buried after 10+ screens of scroll.
- Free hair trial size banner up top, Hair Awards hero next, THEN three full award categories before any discount appears — zero visual hierarchy toward the promised offer.
- This is the second consecutive Sephora send in less than a day using "50% off select beauty" language while the real content is something else entirely (yesterday: LAST CHANCE ribbon fatigue; today: mislabeled awards digest).
- No fine print visible on the 50% off till I scroll deep, so I can't even tell what's excluded without hunting.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails — Hair Awards is a strong standalone send; don't bolt a mismatched sale banner onto its subject line to juice opens.
- 2. If sending as one email, put the 50% off banner in the hero position, above the awards content, since that's what the subject promised.
- 3. Add category/product-type filtering copy near the top ("shop by hair goal") so I know if this applies to me before I scroll 10 screens.
- 4. Tighten the subject to match content, or split the offer into its own dedicated send like Ulta does with its single-mechanic 20%-off pushes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The Hair Styling Awards: this year's winners`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off select beauty — shop now, no scrolling required`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `12 categories, one winner each — plus your 50% off pick`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shampoo to styling cream, all award-winning, all in one place`
