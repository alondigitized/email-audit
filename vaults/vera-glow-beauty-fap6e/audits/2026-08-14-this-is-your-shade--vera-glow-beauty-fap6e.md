# This is your shade
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "For your Green eyes" as a subject/hero — be still my heart, this is the first personalization I've seen from ILIA all week that isn't just my first name slapped on a generic template. It's clocking my eye color and building the entire "Color Theory" module around it, and even though I know they probably don't *actually* know my eye color and this is templated by segment or just... every color shown, the framing feels smart. It's the most editorial, most "we thought about this" email ILIA has sent me since the Thru Line Eye Essentials kit on 8/8 that I gave a 6.
- That said, once I get past the pretty eye close-up, this turns into a shade-matching chart I have to actually study — four eye colors, three products each, tiny shade name labels — and there's no button that says "shop for Green eyes," just one generic "SHOP THRU LINE" way at the bottom of all four charts. So the concept is stronger than the execution. I'd probably scroll through this one on the toilet out of genuine curiosity (rare for ILIA lately), but I'm not one-tap buying anything — I'd need to zoom in on the Green Eyes row specifically, and by the time I do that the moment's kind of passed. Not forwarding this, but not archiving unread either — this earns a real scroll, unlike "This is your shade" siblings from other brands that are just fragrance-note astrology.

## What's working

- The hero eye close-up with "for your Green eyes" in that pretty script font is genuinely arresting — best visual open ILIA's had all week, way better than the flat black-text ILIA email from 8/13 that scored a 3.
- Segmenting by eye color instead of just dumping every product is a real personalization attempt, more than Sephora's generic "0 points" nudge I called out on 8/12's rhode email.
- The free mini Eye Stylus GWP banner at the very top ("$75+") is a clean, specific offer mechanic — no vague "treat yourself" language.
- The "just the essentials" section with the Thru Line Eyeliner + brush + sharpener trio flat-lay is genuinely covetable, on-brand with the 8/8 kit send I liked.
- Ingredient/format language is minimal but the Limitless Lash Mascara callout ("lengthens, lifts, separates... 12 hours, two shades—black and brown") gives me real product info I can act on.

## What's weak

- No direct CTA under "for Green Eyes" specifically — I have to scroll past Blue and Brown to get to my section, then there's no button there either, just a "SHOP THRU LINE" way at the bottom that dumps me into everything, not my shade.
- Zero mention of my VIP tier or points — ILIA doesn't run a Rouge/Diamond-style program so I get it, but after weeks of Sephora reminding me I'm sitting at "0 points," any beauty email that skips loyalty math feels like it's talking to a stranger, not a repeat Rouge/Diamond shopper.
- The shade swatch labels ("Nectar," "Filigree," "Regal," "Brocade") are cute but genuinely hard to read at this size on mobile — no visible link from swatch name to an actual product page.
- "SAVE 15%" appears with zero context on what triggers it or when it expires — no code, no deadline, so it reads more like wallpaper than an offer I can act on now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give me a real deep-link: put a "SHOP FOR GREEN EYES →" button directly under my eye-color section instead of forcing me to scroll to one catch-all CTA at the bottom — this is the single biggest miss given how strong the personalization concept is.
- 2. Attach an actual deadline and code to "SAVE 15%" — right now it's the most naked, unbacked offer in the email.
- 3. Add real ingredient/derm claims near the mascara or eyeliner section (waterproof duration, sensitive-eye safe, etc.) — ILIA's clean-beauty positioning is their whole differentiator and it's barely showing up here.
- 4. Lead with my actual name plus eye color if you have it on file ("Vera, for your Green eyes") — right now it reads templated, not personalized, the same trap Skechers fell into with "Hey, we miss you" that scored a 5 from me.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your eye color, decoded — shop Green`
