# 😍 Starts now! Drunk 30% off Black Friday
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Drunk Elephant email in about a week, and my first reaction was "okay, it's actually Black Friday now." The hero finally says "It's on! BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE," which is the same 30% I've been getting since the 11/25 "Biggest sale of the year: 30% off." The "Starts now!" subject is honest, but the number hasn't moved in days. The only new thing is the free full-size B-Goldi serum at $110+ and the Trunk 7.1 bundle at 20% off with DRUNK20.
- I did the math, because I always do. 30% off is solid, and a $39 B-Goldi on top of a $110 spend is a real sweetener. But then I hit three different discounts in one email: 30% off sitewide, 20% off the Trunk, and up to 40% off last-chance items. It reads like a coupon-stacking puzzle, and the fine print says none of it combines. Sephora's 50% off send says one number and sticks to it.
- Would I click? Yes, but only because I'm already eyeing the B-Goldi threshold. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for a Sephora Beauty Insider event to compare against.

## What's working

- "BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" sits above a clean pink gift-box hero, so the offer lands in half a second.
- The free full-size B-Goldi with $110+ is the only genuinely new hook, and "a value of $39!" makes it easy to price out.
- The Trunk 7.1 block does the savings math for me: "$340, $733 value" and "Save $393." I like that.
- The kit grid shows struck-through prices, star ratings and "674 people are checking this out," which gives me proof and a little FOMO.
- Free ground shipping with all orders is in the top banner, so there's no minimum to hunt for.

## What's weak

- Too many numbers: 30%, 20% with a code, up to 40%, plus the B-Goldi gift. The fine print says offers can't be combined, so I can't tell which one wins for my cart.
- Same creative and the same 30% as the 11/25 "Biggest sale of the year" and 11/26 "Up to 40% off" sends. This is cadence fatigue, not news.
- "Up to 40% off, last chance, while supplies last" has no product and no hero, just a pink ribbon. It's vague and it clashes with the 30% banner above it.
- The fine print is four asterisked paragraphs at the bottom, and I only see the $110+ threshold and the dates there. Nothing tells me the free gift needs $110 after discounts but before tax and shipping.
- There's no loyalty or member angle at all, and nothing personalized for my skin type. Sephora and Ulta both make my tier matter.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with 30% off plus the free B-Goldi at $110+, and drop the "up to 40%" tile unless it names specific products.
- 2. Put the $110+ threshold and the end date (11/29 at 11:59 pm ET) next to the Goldi block, not only in the footer.
- 3. Show the Trunk as an either/or choice: "Trunk 7.1: 20% off with DRUNK20, or 30% off individual items. Here's which saves more."
- 4. Add a real "last day" send for 11/29 instead of a sixth near-identical send.
- 5. Add a member or personalization layer, like picks by skin type or early access for rewards members.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: 30% off, free serum at $110`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $110, get a full-size B-Goldi free. Ends 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Trunk 7.1 saves you $393 with code DRUNK20.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with several markup and CSS defects: three `http://` images, two tracking images with no `alt`, an `@import` after other rules, and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay did not capture them. The HTML was truncated and 19 links were not probed, so several checks below are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 19 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown. No broken links are confirmed.
- **Insecure images:** three social icons use `http://`, which proxies and some clients block or flag as mixed content:

  - `.../29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…` are open or tracking beacons. Two separate tracking vendors load per open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt:** the two tracking images above have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` rules come after the `body{...}` rule in the same `<style>` block. `@import` must come first, so browsers ignore them. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` do the same job and are the only font loading that works.
- **Font support:** the fonts come from third-party hosts (`db.onlinewebfonts.com`, `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/...`) via `@font-face`. Gmail and Outlook drop these, so the rendering depends on a fallback stack. I can't tell whether one exists in the truncated source.
- **CSS comments:** HTML comments (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] ... -->`) sit inside the `@media` block. They are invalid there and are dropped by the CSS parser. This is harmless here but is fragile.
- **Fixed mobile sizes:** `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` set `max-width:15px` and `max-height:12px` under 418px and 600px. This risks clipping text, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Email in URL:** the recipient address is exposed in plaintext in the tracking URL: `?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. It will appear in referrer data, proxy logs and forwarded copies. A hashed or opaque ID would be safer.
- **Merge tokens:** no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. QA notes the relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so the status is unknown. The From domain is `cheers.drunkelephant.com`, which should be checked against the original headers.
- **Footer:** the visible footer address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM footer compliance can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Destination URLs:** they are wrapped in click-redirects and weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID:** the pixel's `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfstartsmidnigh…` suggests the campaign is tagged. The tag is cut off here.
