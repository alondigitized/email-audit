# 🌟 THIS JUST IN 🌟
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the seventh Sephora send in about a week, and the subject is two stars and "THIS JUST IN." That tells me nothing. The top strip says "You have 0 points," and I'm Rouge. The tier bar near the bottom also has the marker on Insider, so Sephora still doesn't know who I am. Rouge gets 10% off and a different Savings Event window than Insider, and the 30% / 10% badges up top don't tell me which one is mine.
- Past that, the email is decent. The free Carolina Herrera Good Girl Jasmine mini is the best thing here. Code CHASMINE with a $40 purchase is a real reason to add a fragrance to a cart, and I'll do that math. Then it turns into the usual new-arrivals catalog: KJH, Peter Thomas Roth, OUAI, Manucurist, Sol de Janeiro, Velour, Menokin, Valentino. Everything is tagged "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" or "LIMITED EDITION," and none of it has a deadline.
- I'd open it, click the fragrance mini, and probably not forward it. Ulta's 21 Days sends at least give me one date to plan around, and this one doesn't.

## What's working

- The free Good Girl Jasmine mini is a concrete perk, and the code CHASMINE and $40 threshold make the points math easy.
- The "Shop to redeem" button on the mini is clear and sits right at the top.
- Prices are on every product (Sol de Janeiro $34, OUAI $45, Manucurist $19, Valentino $198). I can judge value without tapping.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags make the exclusives easy to spot.
- The holiday sections ("Gift-worthy hair sets," "Must-have body care," "Holiday Gift Hits") match the season.

## What's weak

- The subject is two stars and "THIS JUST IN." It names no product, no offer and no date.
- The "You have 0 points" strip and the tier bar marker on Insider are wrong for a Rouge member. The whole email is written to a stranger.
- The Savings Event badges (30% and 10%) have no readable tier mapping, and "COMING SOON learn more" gives me no date to plan around.
- The page has two competing heroes, the fragrance mini and "SOMETHING NEW IS HERE" with a giant red bow. The second one sells no specific offer.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The mini needs a purchase and the sale has exclusions, and I can't tell which rules apply to me.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the member data first. If I'm Rouge, show my tier, my points, my discount and my Savings Event window.
- 2. Put one offer in the subject and preheader. The free fragrance mini is the strongest asset in the email, so lead with it.
- 3. Cut the "Something new is here" block, or turn it into a second offer with a deadline.
- 4. Give the "coming soon" tease a real date.
- 5. Trim the product grid to four items, ranked by what I've browsed.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code CHASMINE gets a free Carolina Herrera mini. Plus 8 new exclusives.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with VML/Outlook support and a mobile media-query block. QA found one hard failure (no plain-text part) and several alt-text gaps. The source I received is truncated, so body links, footer and UTM parameters could not be checked directly.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 of 66 links, skipping 54. It reported no broken links among the probed set, so the other 54 are unverified.
- The first image missing alt text is `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`. This looks like an open-tracking pixel served from the sender's own click/open domain, with images on `images.harmony.epsilon.com` (Epsilon's content handler). A missing alt on a 1x1 pixel is low risk, but it should have `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.
- No link-level defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 4 images):**
  - `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`: nav header image, so the navigation is unreadable with images blocked or in a screen reader.
  - `20261028_CS_Just_Arrived_hed_05.jpg`: headline image.
  - `20261028_CS_Just_Arrived_fix_06.jpg`: fixed content image.
  - The tracking pixel above.

  If the headline image carries the message text, the email has no readable message when images are off. The filenames suggest the header images carry meaningful content, so they need descriptive alt text.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index" />`:** the standard value is `noindex`, with no space. This is harmless in email but is invalid markup.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1 />`:** this blocks pinch-zoom on some clients and is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`<title>Sephora</title>`:** it is generic and does not match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!-- US shell snippet -->` is harmless but shows an unscrubbed template.
- **Mobile CSS:** it relies on attribute selectors like `*[class=hide]` alongside class selectors. This is a legacy pattern that can be stripped by some clients, but nothing here is confirmed broken.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The body and footer were not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility, and some filters penalize HTML-only mail.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If it is real, it conflicts with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for `shop@beauty.sephora.com`. This reflects the relay, not necessarily a sender defect.
- **Not verifiable:** the physical postal address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source. QA says an unsubscribe link was always probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. No destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated HTML, and QA reported no landing-page results. Tracking appears to go through the `app.sephora.com` redirect domain, so UTMs may be applied server-side and be invisible in the source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` part.** This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the three content images,** and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel (`app.sephora.com/O/…`).
3. **Fix the meta tags:** change `no index` to `noindex` and remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport.
4. **Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** against the raw message at the source, bypassing the relay, before treating them as defects. Do the same for SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. **Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML** and probe the 54 skipped links. That would cover UTM consistency, the footer and unsubscribe link, and the postal address.
