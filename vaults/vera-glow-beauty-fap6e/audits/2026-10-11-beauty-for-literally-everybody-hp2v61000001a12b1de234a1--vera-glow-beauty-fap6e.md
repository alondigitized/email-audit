# Beauty for literally everybody
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Sephora catalog dump in three days, and it still doesn't know I'm Rouge. The header says "INSIDER | You have 0 points," and the tier bar near the bottom has the arrow parked on Insider. That's a stranger greeting. Sephora has my purchase history. I'd expect the Rouge rewards-bazaar angle up top, not a zero.
- The Kayali Yum Body Collection hero is pretty and "Beauty Your Way" is a fine headline. The two offers that matter to my points math are the free mascara trial size with $40 and 4X points on Huda Beauty. One sits in a thin strip at the very top and the other is a half-width tile near the bottom, and the Huda one is app-only with a code. The hero sells a body-care collection with no price and no offer. I'd skim it, maybe screenshot the Huda tile, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The Kayali hero is clean. It has one product story, a clear "SHOP NOW" button, and a "Same-Day Delivery" line.
- The product grid shows real prices with value callouts, like Kiehl's $72 ($110 value) and Dyson $649. That's the math I want.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags give me launch-day clarity and a reason to act on the drops.
- The 4X Huda tile gives a date window (10/8–10/14), and the code is spelled out.
- The mascara trial-size strip names YSL and NARS, so the brands aren't a mystery.

## What's weak

- Rouge me gets a "0 points" Insider header and a tier bar highlighting Insider. There's no tier math and no personalization at all.
- The trial size is buried in a thin strip above the hero. It needs a $40 purchase, online only, and the code MASCARA.
- The Huda 4X is app-only and needs a code. The fine print then stacks on top (points caps, exclusions), so the offer doesn't feel honest or simple.
- The Dyson, Rare Beauty and One/Size grid has no CTA buttons, only text links. Tap targets look small on a phone.
- It's the third Sephora send in three days: 10/9 "Hey, Insider...We think you'll love this" and now this one. It's the same layout and the same stacked codes, and nothing feels fresh or earned.

## Recommendations

- 1. Personalize to tier. Put "Rouge: you're X points from your next Bazaar pick" in the header instead of "0 points." That alone would lift this email.
- 2. Lead with one offer, either the 4X points or the free trial size, in the hero, not above it.
- 3. Drop the app-only and code stacking. Auto-apply the offer or show one code.
- 4. Add "Add to Basket" buttons to the grid and cut it to 4–6 items.
- 5. Space the sends out. Three catalog dumps in a row is how Sephora trains me to ignore it. Ulta Diamond emails at least lean on one coupon deadline, and Sephora can do better.

Authentication and unsubscribe-header status could not be verified from the relay capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 47 skipped. The QA data reports no broken or redirect-failing links among the probed set, so no link failures are confirmed.
- The 47 unprobed links (mostly footer, utility and social) are unverified, not known-good.
- `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` is an `<img>` with no alt attribute. Its path pattern suggests an open-tracking pixel. It should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers don't announce a filename.

- The CSS uses mobile-class selectors (`.hide`, `.show`, `.tbl`, `.padcN`), Outlook MSO resets and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` handling. No rendering defects were confirmed from the visible portion.
