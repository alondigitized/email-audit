# Save up to 25% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Glossier sale email in three days, and the opening energy has officially left the building. The big orange ANNUAL SALE tiles were a genuine dopamine hit on June 11 when the sale first dropped — I did the math, I mapped my cart, I was genuinely excited. But seeing essentially the same repeating diagonal type on June 13 with a subject line that could have come from a random flash-sale site ("Save up to 25% off" — no brand name, no anchor at all) is the beauty email equivalent of someone sending the same text three days in a row. I clocked the sender name before I clocked the subject, and that's not how Glossier usually plays it.
- What actually made me stop scrolling was the subscription section at the bottom: "Start a subscription and lock in 25% off*." That's genuinely new content in this sale window — and for a replenishable like Boy Brow at $22, I'm doing the math. That section earns a click. The "Summer Scouts" and "Community Favorites" blocks are solid editorial framing but feel copy-pasted from Tuesday's send.
- The weird copy choice that kept pulling me out: "Let all friends pay full price." I read that four times. Is that a sharing hook? Is it telling me non-Friends members are now locked out? Is it passive-aggressively suggesting I not share the sale link? It communicates nothing clearly and made me trust the offer slightly less. I'd click through for the subscription terms and to grab Boy Brow, but I'm not forwarding this — my friends already know the sale is on because Glossier told them twice before today.

## What's working

- **Boy Brow at $22 with "COMMUNITY FAVORITES" framing** — naming it as a community pick on top of a sale price is the double-tap that makes me want to buy three of them.
- **Subscription 25% lock-in is genuinely new content** — the first two sale emails didn't surface this angle; being able to permanently save 25% on replenishables by subscribing is real ROI math I'll actually do.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — hero → Summer Scouts → Community Favorites → Subscription, all the way down without a single visual interrupt; Glossier's minimal aesthetic does the heavy lifting.
- **No render issues** — clean layout, images loaded, no broken blocks or overlapping text anywhere in the email.

## What's weak

- **"Let all friends pay full price" is the most confusing hero copy I've seen this week** — I cannot tell if this is a share-with-friends hook, a jab at non-Friends members, or just an accident. Charlotte Tilbury runs theatrical copy all the time and at least she's committed to a bit; this reads like a copywriter's first draft that nobody questioned.
- **Third sale email, nearly identical creative** — Summer Scouts product shots and the diagonal ANNUAL SALE tiles are carbon copies of June 11 and June 12. By this logic, Sephora's "STARTS TODAY: up to 50% off" worked on day one because it signaled a *new* moment; this one signals nothing has changed.
- **"Save up to 25% off" subject has zero brand equity** — ILIA just got me to open with "It felt like a godsend." —Este Haim and scored a 9. Glossier dropping a generic percent-off subject on day 3 of a sale window is a waste of the trust they built in the Friends Sale launch.
- **"LIMITED TIME" with no date** — the tag is doing no behavioral work. If this sale ends Sunday, tell me it ends Sunday. Otherwise it's decoration.
- **Every CTA says "Shop now"** — four sections, four identical buttons. At minimum, "Subscribe & Save 25%" on the subscription block would match the actual offer and signal I'd land somewhere useful.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the subscription angle in the subject** — the 25% lock-in is the only differentiated content in this send vs. the prior two. Make it the hook, not the footer.
- 2. **Replace "Let all friends pay full price" immediately** — if the intent is a share/referral moment, say "Send your friends the link." If it's now sitewide, say "Now open to everyone." This line needs a rewrite before the next send.
- 3. **Add the sale end date visibly** — "Ends Sunday, June 15" in the hero or on the CTA transforms "LIMITED TIME" from wallpaper into a reason to act today.
- 4. **Make CTAs product-specific at minimum in the subscription block** — "Subscribe & Save 25%" over "Shop now" is the single-word swap that would earn more clicks on that module.
- 5. **Differentiate the creative from June 11 and June 12** — introduce a new product not in Tuesday's email, run a countdown timer, or add a "Low stock on these" callout to signal this is a *closing window*, not a repeat broadcast.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Subscribe to Boy Brow and never pay full price again. Sale ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off sitewide. 25% off $150+. Or subscribe and save permanently.`
