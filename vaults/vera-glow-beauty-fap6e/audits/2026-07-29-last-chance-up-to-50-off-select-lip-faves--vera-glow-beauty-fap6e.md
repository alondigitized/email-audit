# LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, third time's the charm — I've now got THREE emails in my inbox with the exact subject "LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves," and I already reviewed this same creative twice on 7/29. At this point I'm convinced Sephora's ESP is stuck in a loop, because the grid, the pricing, even the "Buy More, Save More" module underneath are all identical to what I saw before. The lip grid itself is still genuinely strong — MAC, Charlotte Tilbury, Gisou, Summer Fridays, all with real struck-through pricing — so if this were the first time I'd click without hesitation.
- But it's not the first time, and that's the whole story here. The "You have 0 points" banner at the top is still glaring at me as a two-year Rouge member, which is the same miss I called out on the "Something special awaits" email from 7/28 — Sephora clearly isn't pulling my loyalty data into these blasts. Compare that to Ulta's "20% off just landed in your lap" send, which at least felt like it knew I was mid-shop even without real point balances shown.
- Verdict: I'd mark this one read without clicking, purely because I already shopped this exact grid off an earlier copy. If I hadn't already opened the near-duplicates, I'd forward the MAC lipstick markdown to my group chat — that's a real deal. But three sends of the same thing in the same week is exactly the fatigue Sephora needs to watch for.

## What's working

- The lip grid is clean and scannable — eight products, real before/after pricing (MAC $25→$15, Wonderskin $22→$17.60), no lorem-ipsum, no broken images.
- "Celebrate National Lip Day with major markdowns" gives the sale an actual occasion instead of a generic "spot it, shop it" excuse — same beat Drunk Elephant's "Lippe Lock" hit, and honestly Sephora's grid execution beats DE's tighter, smaller send on sheer product breadth.
- The CLEAN badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays are a nice trust cue for ingredient-conscious shoppers without cluttering the layout.
- "Buy More, Save More" stacked underneath (up to 20% off Sephora Collection, no code needed) gives me a second reason to add to cart if the lip stuff alone doesn't close it.

## What's weak

- This is the same exact send I've now seen twice before under this identical subject line — that's not urgency, that's an ESP loop, and it kills the "last chance" framing dead.
- "You have 0 points" at the very top, unchanged from the last two sends — I've been Rouge for two years and this brand still can't surface my real balance.
- The "‼️" in the subject is a spam-adjacent signal I don't love from a brand I already trust; Sephora doesn't need double exclamation emoji to get me to open.
- No skin-tone or lip-tone personalization anywhere in the grid — every shade shown is generic product-forward photography, not "matched to you."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the send logic first — if I've already received and reviewed this exact creative twice, a third identical "LAST CHANCE" blast trains me to stop opening. Cap repeat sends of the same promo to once, maybe a day-2 reminder at most.
- 2. Swap "0 points" for my actual Rouge balance or at least tier status — even Ulta's vaguer "sign to shop" send felt more personal than a hardcoded zero.
- 3. Drop the second "‼️" in the subject — one urgency signal is enough, two reads as spammy from a trusted sender.
- 4. Add one shade-matched or "picked for you" lip product up top based on my purchase history — the grid is generic enough that it could be any Sephora customer's inbox.
