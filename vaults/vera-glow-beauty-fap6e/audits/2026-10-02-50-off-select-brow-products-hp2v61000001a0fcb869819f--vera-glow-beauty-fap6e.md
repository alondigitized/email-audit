## Executive summary

- Okay, a brow sale is a solid Sephora Daily Deals send, and I opened it quickly. "Up to 50% off select brow picks, TODAY ONLY" sits on a peach-to-yellow gradient, and I get it in about two seconds. It's cleaner than the 10/1 "STARTS TODAY" 2X points blast, which had one huge hero and nothing else. This is a more useful shopping email because there are eight real products with real sale prices.
- The "up to 50%" is carrying more than it should. Only the two SEPHORA COLLECTION items ($16 to $8) are actually 50% off. Everything else is 30% or less. Grande's serum is $73 to $51.10, and Benefit is $28 to $14, which is a real 50%, so some of it is good. I did the math and the Benefit gel is the best deal for me. The strip at the top says "You have 0 points," and that bugs me. I'm Rouge, and the email treats me like a stranger. If it showed my balance, I'd know whether to stack this with Rewards Bazaar.
- I'd click, mostly for the Benefit gel at $14. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good, ordinary deal email.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Up to 50% off" plus "TODAY ONLY" gives me the offer and a credible deadline right away.
- Every product shows the strike-through price next to the red sale price, so I can do the points math without clicking.
- The eight-item, two-column grid scans well on a phone and mixes prestige (Anastasia, Benefit, Glossier) with Sephora Collection value picks.
- The "Clean at Sephora" and "Sephora Quality" badges are useful trust cues for someone who shops ingredients.
- The footer's Beauty Insider tier bar and "Rewards Bazaar" link remind me that member perks exist.

## What's weak

- "0 points" in the Insider bar is wrong for a Rouge member. It feels like they don't know who I am, and Ulta at least puts my name in subject lines.
- The hero's "up to 50%" is misleading, because only the two SEPHORA COLLECTION items hit 50%. The Grande serum at about 30% off is the weak spot.
- The "introducing point of view" banner is a different brand story entirely. It sits right under the products and distracts from the sale.
- The brow-gel and pencil swatches are tiny and mostly white tubes, so there's nothing about shade range. Brow shades vary a lot by skin and hair tone, and none of that shows here.
- The "Shop Now" button is small and buried in the gradient. The product tiles don't look tappable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Personalize the Insider bar with my real points balance and tier, plus a line like "Rouge: stack your 10% off." That would lift this more than any other change.
- 2. Put the deepest-discount items first in the grid, with "50% OFF" badges on them. The hero should say "Up to 50% off" only if the top row proves it.
- 3. Add shade swatches or "6 shades" labels on the pencil items.
- 4. Move the "point of view" banner below the loyalty block, or cut it from a one-day sale.
- 5. Make the CTA a full-width button: "Shop brow deals."
