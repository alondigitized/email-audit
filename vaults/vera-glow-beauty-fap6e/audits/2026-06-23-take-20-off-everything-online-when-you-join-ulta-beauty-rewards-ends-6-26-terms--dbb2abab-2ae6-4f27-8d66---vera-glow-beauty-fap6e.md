# Take 20% OFF everything online⁺⁺ when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards® (ends 6.26) Terms & excl. apply
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-23**
## Executive summary

- Same email. I'm saying it right out of the gate: this is the exact send from June 23 that already landed in my inbox, and I already had feelings about it. We're now three days later, it's ending today (6/26), and Ulta is dropping it again like I didn't clock it the first time. The "Summer Deal Days / 20% off everything online" hero is clean enough — big pink block, clear percentage, product grid underneath. But the entire premise collapses the moment the subject line says "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards." I'm Diamond. I have spreadsheets about my Diamond status. I do not need to join anything.
- The product grid itself is genuinely useful — I can see pricing with discounts, a good range of products across categories, and the layout doesn't break. If this email had landed in a non-member's inbox, or even a base-tier Rewards member who needed the nudge, I'd call it competent. But I'm not that person, and Ulta knows I'm not that person, and they sent this anyway. That's a segmentation failure that undercuts everything else the design got right.
- I would not click. I would not forward. I would mentally note that Ulta has now sent me the same "join our loyalty program" email twice in four days and that's not a great use of anyone's June.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is solid** — "20% off" reads in under a second, the pink background pops, visual flow lands on the offer first before product grid.
- **Ends 6/26 urgency is real** — it's today, which means the deadline is genuine and the email actually has credible time pressure right now.
- **Product grid with before/after pricing** — I can see the actual sale prices next to originals; that's the math I want to do before I click.
- **"Summer Deal Days" branding is seasonally appropriate** — doesn't feel stale or off-calendar.
- **No visible render issues** — images loaded, layout held, no overlapping elements from what I can see.

## What's weak

- **"When you join Ulta Beauty Rewards" in the subject line** — I'm Diamond. This is the same miss I flagged on the 6/23 send and it's still here. Ulta could segment this in their sleep.
- **No member tier acknowledgment anywhere** — Charlotte Tilbury's early access emails at least signal "you unlocked this." This email treats me identically to someone who's never heard of Ulta.
- **Preview text is fine-print junk** — "Terms & excl. apply" is not a preheader, it's a disclaimer. Sephora doesn't blow their preview slot on legal copy; Ulta keeps doing this.
- **The "++" on "everything"** — visible in the subject, probably in the body too. Ulta's exclusions list is long. "Everything" reads like a promise and the double-plus walks it back quietly.
- **It's a resend of a June 23 email** — no creative refresh, no new angle, nothing to signal that this is the "LAST CHANCE" moment rather than just another pass.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment out existing loyalty members and rewrite the offer framing** — Diamond/Platinum subscribers should see "Your member discount is live" not "join to save." This is the single highest-lift change.
- 2. **Make the preheader do work** — "Ends tonight, June 26 — your Summer Deal Days discount is still active" beats "Terms & excl. apply" by every metric.
- 3. **Add a "ends today" badge or banner to the hero** — the 6/26 deadline is in the subject but if I skip the subject (which happens on a resend), I lose the urgency entirely.
- 4. **Feature one hero product with a name** — the grid is fine but Glossier's "Final Hours" email (reviewed June 19) always surfaces one icon product above the grid to anchor the click. A named hero item outperforms a wall of tiles.
- 5. **Drop the "++" or explain it inline** — if prestige brands are excluded, say "prestige excluded" plainly. Buried fine print reads as bait-and-switch to a Diamond member who knows Ulta's carve-outs.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Deal Days end tonight — 20% off online (Diamond members, this is for you)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 20% off ends 6.26 — Summer Deal Days closing out`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends tonight. Shop the sale before it's gone — your member pricing is already applied.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today only: 20% off almost everything. Exclusions listed, but your faves are in.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Google Fonts `<link>` will be suppressed in Gmail and Outlook:**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=DM+Sans:..." rel="stylesheet">
```
Gmail strips `<link>` tags from `<head>`. Outlook ignores web fonts entirely. The `.helvetica` fallback (`font-family: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif`) is present and will activate — rendering is not broken, but DM Sans will never load in the two highest-volume email clients.

**Issue — Dead CSS bloating payload (~3–4 KB):**
The hamburger menu block is explicitly commented out (`/* 91925 Commenting out hamburger menu ... END Hamburger Menu */`) but the full ruleset for `#mobile-label`, `#menu-wrapper`, `#mobile-checkbox`, and associated selectors remains in the `<style>` block. This inflates email size unnecessarily and can push Gmail into clipping (102 KB clip threshold).

**Issue — Invalid CSS values in commented block (low risk, hygiene):**
Even in commented-out code, these indicate stale maintenance:
- `background: rgba(255,255,255, 255)` — alpha channel must be 0–1; `255` is invalid.

**Issue — `<title>` has trailing whitespace:**
