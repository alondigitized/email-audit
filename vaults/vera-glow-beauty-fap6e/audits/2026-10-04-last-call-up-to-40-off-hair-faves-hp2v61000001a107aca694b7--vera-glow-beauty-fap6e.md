# LAST CALL: up to 40% off hair faves 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the subject promised hair and the email gave me minis. The hair offer is a thin strip at the very top: "Up to 40% off† Hair Faves," with a TODAY ONLY tag and a PATTERN by Tracee Ellis Ross callout. Then the whole middle is "Minis Make It Easy," a long grid of travel-size products. This is the same bait-and-switch as the 10/4 send I logged earlier today, where "LAST CALL" on hair led into a pink minis takeover. Sephora is repeating its own creative within hours.
- The deal hunter in me still got a few things out of it. The Point Multiplier Event is live: 2X on top brands and 5X on Sephora Collection, ending 10/7, with code SAYPOINTS. That matters for my Rouge math, and I'd stock up on minis during it. But that's buried halfway down, and the "P.S." with the DELIVERED code is just a thin strip. The barcode at the bottom is a nice touch for in-store Kohl's use, but it makes the footer very long.
- Would I click? Probably, but for the points event, not the hair sale. I wouldn't forward it. It's a lot of content, and it competes with the three Sephora emails I got in the last two days.

## What's working

- The "Up to 40% off† Hair Faves / TODAY ONLY" banner puts a real discount and a real deadline right at the top.
- The P.S. same-day delivery code (DELIVERED, ends 10/7) is an easy savings add-on. I'd actually use it.
- The Point Multiplier block (2X / 5X Sephora Collection, code SAYPOINTS) speaks directly to how I shop. The points math is what I care about.
- The mini product grid shows names and prices ($20 to $85), so I can judge value quickly. The Clean at Sephora and "Only at Sephora" badges help.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at the bottom acknowledges my status, and the in-store barcode is useful.

## What's weak

- The subject says hair, but about 80% of the email is minis. The hair sale gets a thin strip and a tiny PATTERN image. That's a bait-and-switch feel.
- The hero is the MIU MIU mini perfume spray at $35, which has nothing to do with the hair offer.
- The points event is buried below the long product grid. It should be near the top.
- The footer is very long, with dense fine print and a double barcode. The offer terms on the 40% off are hard to find.
- The tier bar is generic. It doesn't show my Rouge points balance or what I'd earn.
- Too many competing blocks: hair, minis, Glossy Hair, skincare sets, Same-Day Unlimited, Rewards Bazaar. There's no single clear CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails, or make the hero match the subject. If hair is the "LAST CALL," lead with hair products, prices, and the PATTERN collab, and move minis to a separate send.
- 2. Move the Point Multiplier event up to the top and show the points I'd earn on a typical mini.
- 3. Personalize the tier bar with my actual Rouge points balance and a "you're X points from a reward" line.
- 4. Cut the product grid in half and add one clear CTA per section.
- 5. Shorten the legal fine print or collapse it behind a link.
- Compared with the others: Ulta's "ends tonight" 20% coupon email is cleaner, with one mechanic and one code. ILIA's "It's Back" hero shows the product in about two seconds, and Sephora's hero here took me longer to parse. Drunk Elephant's 25-shade swatch grid was more visual and more specific. Sephora's six-block layout is the opposite.

## Technical Audit

Other findings are two images with no alt text and a few header and markup items. Several categories can't be assessed because the HTML is truncated and the relay dropped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** 12 material links were probed and none were reported as failing.
- **Coverage gap:** 51 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so those are unverified. The unsubscribe link is always probed, and no failure was reported for it.
- **Tracking pixel without alt:** `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx...` has a long opaque token path and no alt text. It is most likely the open-tracking beacon, though that is an inference from the URL pattern. Pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 2 images:**
  - `app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (likely the tracking pixel, see above).
  - `images.harmony.epsilon.com/ContentHandler/images/de0a3226-.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`. The file name is exposed as the fallback, and the image appears to be the nav header. With images blocked, the navigation is unreadable.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` has a space. The valid token is `noindex`. This is low impact in email but is invalid markup.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` does not match the subject. It is minor, but it affects the browser-view page title and the accessible name.
- **No issues found** with the doctype, VML/Office namespaces or the `mso-*` and Outlook resets. The 480px media-query hooks look intact in the visible CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. Body content past the `<style>` block was not available, so this check is incomplete.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** The message appears to be HTML-only, with no `text/plain` alternative. This hurts spam scoring, and text-only and accessibility clients get nothing. Confirm the raw MIME on the real send, because the relay may have stripped it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the original message headers from the direct delivery path.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. From `shop@beauty.sephora.com`, alignment can't be confirmed or denied.
- **Not verifiable:** the physical postal address and in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated footer. The probe does include an unsubscribe link, but the QA data doesn't say what it returned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. The visible HTML doesn't include CTA `href`s, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment are unverified. The only observed tracking infrastructure is the Epsilon image host and the `app.sephora.com/O/` beacon. No landing-page mismatch was reported by the probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a `text/plain` alternative.** This is the only confirmed failure. Check the raw MIME first to rule out a relay artifact.
2. **Add `alt` text to both flagged images.** Use `alt=""` for the tracking pixel and a meaningful alt for the nav header.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Fix `content="no index"` to `noindex`**, and set `<title>` to match the subject or preheader.
5. **Re-run QA against the direct-delivery headers** to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
