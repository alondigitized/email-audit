# NEW Thru Line Waterproof Eyeliner
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, ILIA actually built something this time — a real launch page with a hero product shot, a technique tutorial, and a full shade/finish chart, which is a level of production I haven't seen from them in this eleven-day stretch of GWP spam and plain-text panic emails. "New Thru Line Waterproof Eyeliner" is a legitimate new-launch email and it reads like one: swatches, application technique broken into three steps, a toolkit cross-sell for the brush and sharpener. This is closer to what Drunk Elephant's "Our top shelf" catalog-wall energy was going for, except ILIA actually gives me a reason to care — 24-hour wear, waterproof, safe for sensitive eyes.
- But here's my problem: there is no price anywhere, no VIB/Rouge point multiplier, no member pricing, nothing that acknowledges I'm dropping serious money at Sephora and Ulta and tracking every dollar of it. Compare this to Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item" send that scored a 9 with me — that email respected that I'm doing math in my head. ILIA just assumes I'll click through and find out the price myself. I'd click, because a new eyeliner launch with a legit tutorial is genuinely interesting to me, but I'm not forwarding this to a friend — there's nothing shareable or urgent enough here, it's just a solid product page dressed as an email.

## What's working

- The hero shot of stacked eyeliner pencils in warm neutral tones is clean and immediately reads "new launch," not another GWP promo.
- The three-step technique breakdown (smudge+soften, line+define, wing it) with close-up eye photos is genuinely useful — ILIA rarely gives me this much real content.
- Ten shades laid out in a clear swatch table with names (Trace, Portrait, Abstract, etc.) is the kind of shade transparency I actually need before I click "shop."
- The toolkit cross-sell (angled brush, dual sharpener) makes sense as an upsell without feeling like a separate email got bolted on.

## What's weak

- Zero pricing anywhere in the email — not on the hero, not near the CTA, not in the shade chart. I have to click through blind, and at my price-conscious, points-tracking level that's a real barrier.
- No mention of Sephora or Ulta at all — as a Rouge/Diamond shopper I check every ILIA email for where I can actually redeem this, and this one leaves me guessing whether it's Sephora-exclusive at launch or not.
- The model shot is a single close-up with minimal demographic signal — fine, but it doesn't tell me skin tone range or how this looks on deeper skin tones, which matters when a shade chart has 10 options.
- "Lock in your signature look" is a cute CTA but it's vague — doesn't tell me if I'm buying one liner or exploring the whole line.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price under the hero image immediately — even "$28" next to the CTA would kill the biggest friction point in this email.
- 2. Add a line about where to redeem points — "Available now at Sephora" or "Redeem your Rouge points" would make this feel built for me instead of a generic launch blast.
- 3. Swap "Lock in your signature look" for something that signals it's a single hero product, e.g., "Shop the eyeliner that stays put."
- 4. Add a swatch-on-skin strip showing a couple of the 10 shades on different skin tones — the chart alone is flat and doesn't sell the pigment.
