# Cyber Week is really bringing it ON 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Fourth day in a row from Sephora, and my first thought was "okay, the purple sequins again." It's the same Cyber Week creative as 11/22 "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!" and 11/23 "Cyber Week deals are HERE ‼️". Today's twist is a 24-hour-only "It's Good to Be You" block and three stacked 30% off brand tiles: Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People. Kiehl's and Laura Mercier are my kind of prestige, so that part got my attention.
- The offer is clear, but the email is long and busy. There's a banner at the top, then a free sample bag, then the 24-hour block, then three brand tiles, then two product grids, then more promos. It feels like three emails stapled together. I'd click, but only on the Kiehl's tile. I'd forward it to my group chat only if the sample bag code is useful to them.

## What's working

- The "30% OFF" numerals are huge, so the math is instant. I can see the discount on Kiehl's, Laura Mercier and Youth to the People at a glance.
- The free sample bag with code GRABMYBAG is a good perk. Choosing 1 of 4 bags with top brands is how Sephora gets me to the app and the store.
- Kiehl's and Laura Mercier are strong prestige names and feel like the right curation for me.
- The "Treat Yourself for Less" grid shows real prices with strike-through, like the Summer Fridays and Laneige lip masks. It gives me concrete numbers to compare.
- "Only at Sephora" labels on UNOVE and EADEM are a good brand-curation signal.

## What's weak

- The top banner promises "Save up to 50% on select beauty through 11/30," but the hero shows 30% and the Korean Beauty block says "up to 30%." That mismatch makes me suspicious that the 50% is buried in fine print.
- The 24-hour window is stated in small copy under the headline. Because Sephora has used "today only" all week, I don't feel urgency anymore.
- Nothing here mentions Rouge. There's no tier benefit and no points math, and I stock up around Beauty Insider events. That's a big miss for a Rouge member.
- Fine print like "Available on in-stock merchandise only" and "Exclusions/terms apply" shows up on almost every tile. It reads as exclusionary.
- The footer is long: gift guide, Sephora Texts, credit card, wish list, app download. By then I'm just scrolling.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to one hero offer. Lead with the 24-hour 30% brand event and move the sample bag and the secondary promos below it.
- 2. Make the headline numbers consistent. Either explain how the 50% works or drop it from the banner.
- 3. Add a Rouge line, for example "Rouge: extra points on today's 30% brands," so it feels like it knows me.
- 4. Put a countdown or "ends tonight" tag on the 24-hour block, like Drunk Elephant's countdown banner on its VIP 30% off send. That would make the urgency credible.
- 5. Show product shots with prices inside the 30% tiles, as Ulta does in its tier-ladder Black Friday email, instead of only a logo and a button.

- **Subject Alt B:** `24 hours: 30% off prestige faves (+ free sample bag)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kiehl's, Laura Mercier, Youth to the People. Ends tonight, in store and online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Use code GRABMYBAG for a free sample bag, plus Rouge points on today's drops.`
