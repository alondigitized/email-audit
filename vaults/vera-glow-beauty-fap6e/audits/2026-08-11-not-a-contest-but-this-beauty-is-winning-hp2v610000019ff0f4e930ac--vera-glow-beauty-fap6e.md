# Not a contest, but this beauty is winning 🏅
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" again — top left, right under INSIDER, same broken sync I called out on the K18 email (8/9) and yesterday's "winning" send too. At this point it's not a glitch, it's a pattern, and it's the first thing my eye hits every single time. Once I get past that annoyance, this one's actually got some decent bones — Vanilla Canyon as the "Hot Right Now" hero is a real, specific, currently-buzzy launch, not filler copy, and the "Frequently Chosen Together" + "Trending Now" grid is doing legitimate merchandising work instead of just dumping products at me.
- But it's a wall. I'm scrolling forever — Sofie Pavitt Face, then Hot Right Now, then Frequently Chosen Together, then an eleven-product Trending Now grid, then two more promo blocks, then Free Foundation Samples, then the footer. Compare that to Sephora's own "Something new is here" sends from 8/10 which I already flagged twice as the same new-arrivals-wall format — this is basically that format again, just with a Vanilla Canyon skin on top. Ulta wouldn't bury an offer this deep; their "20% off is calling" send at least keeps one mechanic front and center.
- I'd skim the hero, maybe screenshot Vanilla Canyon for my wishlist, and archive. Not forwarding this to a friend — nothing here is surprising enough to share, it's just a very long scroll.

## What's working

- Vanilla Canyon as "Hot Right Now" is a legit, specific, in-the-culture launch — not generic "new arrivals" copy.
- "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" badge on the perfume gives me a real filter signal I actually use when shopping.
- "Frequently Chosen Together" and "Trending Now" are functional, curated merchandising, not just a random product dump — Marc Jacobs Daffodil & Musk next to NEST Madagascar Vanilla is a smart complementary pairing.
- Free Foundation and Concealer Samples with "$25+ spend" and "find your perfect shade" speaks directly to my shade-matching habit, which most brands skip entirely.

## What's weak

- "0 points" under INSIDER, again — same sync failure I've now flagged three sends running (8/9, 8/10, 8/11). It reads like Sephora doesn't know I'm Rouge.
- No Rouge-tier callout or points multiplier anywhere despite me being their top tier — Ulta at least name-drops me directly ("Marisol, ENDS TODAY!") even when the offer is repetitive.
- This is an extremely long, low-hierarchy scroll — by the time I hit Trending Now's eleven products I've lost the thread of what the email actually wants me to do.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — no event, no expiring code, nothing time-bound to make me act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points sync before anything else — showing "0 points" to a Rouge member three sends in a row is a trust problem, not a cosmetic one.
- 2. Cut the product wall — Frequently Chosen Together + the full Trending Now grid is at least 14 products; pick 4-5 and let Vanilla Canyon actually breathe as the hero.
- 3. Add a Rouge-specific line near the top — even a simple "Rouge members earn 2x on Vanilla Canyon" would justify my tier and beat what Ulta's doing with generic coupon codes.
- 4. Give the sample offer a deadline — "Pick 3 with $25+ spend" has no urgency; a "through Sunday" tag would push me to act now instead of bookmarking and forgetting.
