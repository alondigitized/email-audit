# Our Friends of Glossier Sale is here...
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- The big orange ANNUAL SALE typography in the hero actually made my thumb stop. Glossier rarely goes full discount mode, so a Friends sale with 25% off is a real number — meaningful enough that I started doing math immediately. My excitement hit a wall the second I saw "Create an account" as the hero CTA. I am *on your email list*, Glossier. I have bought Glossier You twice. This email clearly escaped from an acquisition nurture flow into my existing-subscriber inbox, which is a classic segmentation fumble that makes me feel like an afterthought rather than the loyal customer you're supposedly rewarding. Below that friction, the product cards do their job — Glossier You at sale price, Boy Brow at $18, Glossier You Dew — but the fragrance-heavy lineup ignores all the skincare reasons I ever started shopping here.
- Coming off six content emails in roughly two weeks (the Next Top Dog reunion on June 5 was genuinely charming; the hoodie origin on June 3 actually earned its send), a sale is exactly the right next beat. The cadence isn't the problem. I'd scroll to the Shop Now buttons for Glossier You — $66 at 25% off lands around $49.50, which clears my mental "worth buying" threshold. But I wouldn't forward this one, because a friend who got it would probably just be confused about whether she even qualifies for the sale.

## What's working

- The orange ANNUAL SALE hero is a legitimate pattern interrupt — after weeks of soft lifestyle and brand-story sends, a bold typographic sale announcement immediately signals "this one is different."
- "Up to 25% off" is a specific, real number. Glossier discounts are rare enough that 25% feels meaningful rather than the 10%-coupon background noise I ignore from Ulta every other day.
- Individual product cards with price badges let me do the math inline without clicking anywhere — Glossier You ~$49.50, Boy Brow ~$13.50, You Dew ~$48. That math-legibility is exactly what I need to decide whether to open my wallet.
- Brand voice stays clean throughout — no stacked exclamation points, no ALL CAPS body copy, no fake urgency theater. Glossier knows not to yell at me the way, say, Charlotte Tilbury's "content too hot to load" gimmick did last week.
- The subscription 20% pitch is visually separated at the bottom and doesn't muddy the primary 25% sale message — good hierarchy call even if the logic of two competing discounts is confusing.

## What's weak

- "Create an account" as the primary hero CTA for someone already on the subscriber list is a targeting miss that makes me feel redirected to the wrong door entirely — Sephora would never surface a "join Rouge" CTA to me mid-Rouge-event.
- The product curation is fragrance-heavy: Glossier You, Glossier You Dew, Boy Brow. No Balm Dotcom, no Futuredew, no skincare. That's a narrow slice of the line to anchor a Friends-of-Glossier *Annual* Sale on.
- Zero visible sale end date anywhere in the email — ILIA's HAIM collab email last week and Sephora's "STARTS TODAY" flash event both showed clear deadlines. Without one, "is here..." reads as indefinite and I'll mentally table it until later, which often means never.
- The 20% subscription offer alongside the 25% Friends sale creates a confusing dual-discount landscape. Am I better off subscribing? Do they stack? I shouldn't have to think this hard mid-promotional email.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the segmentation first, everything else second** — existing account holders should skip "Create an account" entirely and land on "Shop the sale now." This is the single highest-impact fix; it transforms the email from one that feels like a mistake into one that feels like a reward.
- 2. **Add a hard end date to the hero** — "Ends Sunday, June 15" in the orange banner would tighten urgency more than any copy tweak. The Sephora Flash Event emails always show the clock. This should too.
- 3. **Lead the product grid with a skincare hero** — open with Balm Dotcom or Futuredew before the fragrance section. The Friends sale spans the whole catalog; the email shouldn't make it look like a You Dew clearance.
- 4. **Show was/now pricing on cards** — "Was $66 → $49.50" is more motivating than a "$66" badge with an implied discount. Price anchoring closes the click.
- 5. **Separate the subscription pitch** — the 20% sub offer actively competes with the 25% Friends offer in the same send. Kill it here, follow up in a separate email a day later for anyone who didn't buy at 25%.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off access just unlocked`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop Glossier You, Boy Brow + more — ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Sunday — your Friends of Glossier discount is live`
