# Want a FREE gift with purchase, Insider? 😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Savings Event email I was waiting for, but the subject undersells it. "Free gift with purchase" is the third-string perk. The real news is stacked below: Insider 10% off, Sephora Collection 30% off, and Rouge-friendly extras. The two side-by-side badges up top are clear and I got the math in two seconds. 10% off 11/3 to 11/9 stacks on 30% off Sephora Collection through 11/9.
- Still, the 11/2 "STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍" send did the same job with a stronger hook, and this one is about the seventh Sephora email in a week. The gift (parachute headband and washcloth at $100, "drops today") is cute and time-gated, but it's not worth the Rouge-tier email fatigue. I'd click for the Sephora Collection restock and the points challenge, and skip forwarding it. The 11/2 one was the forward.

## What's working

- The 10% Insider and 30% Sephora Collection badges are the hero, and the "STARTS TODAY" banner kills any date confusion.
- The extra perks are stacked, not buried: a $100 gift with purchase, up to 500 points from the Beauty Insider challenge, and the credit card savings block. That's points-math content.
- The "Save On Your Faves" grid shows real prices, so I can do my own discount math without clicking.
- The same-day delivery strip and the "Beauty on Demand" icons (pickup, delivery, shipping) cover the logistics at a glance.
- The Kohl's store locator and the barcode at the bottom are handy for in-store stacking.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with the weakest perk and never says "10%" or "30%". The 11/2 subject was stronger.
- The email is very long. The product grid, "Spot it. Shop it.", the holiday gift guide and the Kohl's block all stack up. The only buttons I could make out near the top were "Shop Now," "Join Challenge" and "Learn More," and none of them is a verb plus a product.
- Promo codes appear in several places (the ones I could read looked like ALLONSALE and SCSAVE, plus a gift code). It's easy to wonder which one applies to what.
- The fine print and the Kohl's barcodes take up the bottom third, which is a lot of clutter for a send that already feels like a copy of an Ulta tiered-coupon email.
- I couldn't spot a shade or skin-type personalization anywhere. Nothing tells me this is for me beyond "Insider."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the headline offer in the subject, and keep the gift as the sweetener.
- 2. Cut the second half of the email, or collapse it into one "Shop the event" module. Fewer exits would mean more clicks.
- 3. Add a points-math line for Rouge, like "Rouge: 10% off + 500 bonus points."
- 4. Collapse the promo codes into one clear "Use code" box per offer.
- 5. Personalize the product grid by shade or skin type, which Glossier and ILIA already do better.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge, your 10% is live + a free gift at $100`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free headband and washcloth at $100, drops 9am PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack 10% off with 500 bonus points. Ends 11/9.`
