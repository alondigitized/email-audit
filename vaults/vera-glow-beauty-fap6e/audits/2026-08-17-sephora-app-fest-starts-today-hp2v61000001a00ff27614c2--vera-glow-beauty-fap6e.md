# 💫 Sephora App Fest starts today 💫
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is App Fest, and I already covered this exact send earlier today for the countdown breakdown, but now I'm looking at the full body and it's genuinely a lot — in a good way, mostly. This is the calendar-anchored, tent-pole event I actually plan my Sephora spend around, and stacking 10% off + a free 5-piece set on top of the day-by-day drops (Tuesday SCOREMORE trial minis, Wednesday Swipe Serum Foundation, Thursday the credit card push, Friday 4X points on Westman Atelier, Saturday YSL early access, Sunday POINT.OF.VIEW) is the right structure for someone who's doing points math across nine days, not one flash sale.
- What pulls it down from a great send to a good one: the bottom third gets messy. I've got the same tier bar (INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) sitting stacked awkwardly above a totally separate "New Roz Milk Mousse" / "Beauty Must-Haves" module and a Sephora x Olive Young cross-promo, and by the time I hit the actual Insider tier bar footer it feels like three emails got taped together. My tier status isn't even highlighted here the way it usually is with that little red arrow I've flagged before — I had to hunt for it.
- Would I click? Yes — "Stack Now" at the top, immediately, because the day-by-day format means I don't want to miss Thursday's credit card 25%+500pt combo or Friday's Westman Atelier 4X. Would I forward? To my Sephora-obsessed group chat, absolutely, this is the one email all year worth forwarding. Ulta's never going to build a 9-day retention machine like this — their "20% off one item, ends tonight" mechanic is one-and-done by comparison.

## What's working

- The day-by-day calendar layout (Tue→Sun) is genuinely useful for planning which day I log in, way better than Sally Beauty's scattered nine-beat COLORfest rollout with no structure.
- Thursday's Sephora Credit Card callout stacking 25% off + 500 bonus points is the kind of concrete, stackable math I live for.
- Friday's "4X points on ALL Westman Atelier" with a specific promo code (4XWESTMAN) is a real, actionable offer, not vague "earn more" language.
- The "+500 Bonus Points" beauty challenge CTA near the bottom gives me another reason to open the app daily during the event window.
- App-exclusive framing (24-hour early access, app-only deals) matches how I actually shop Sephora — I'm always in the app first.

## What's weak

- The tier bar (INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE) is buried at the very bottom with no arrow marking my status — same sync issue I've now called out four times this week, and this time it's just missing entirely rather than misplaced.
- The last third of the email (New Roz, Beauty Must-Haves, Sephora x Olive Young) feels bolted on — it dilutes the App Fest urgency instead of closing strong.
- "FIVE More Days of App Fest" section duplicates info I already got from the day-by-day list above it — redundant, not additive.
- Small text on the day-specific offers (percentages, exclusions) is genuinely hard to read on mobile without pinching.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the bottom third — New Roz Mousse, Beauty Must-Haves, and the Olive Young teaser don't belong in an App Fest email. Save them for a separate send; let this one end on the beauty challenge/points CTA.
- 2. Fix the tier bar — show MY tier (Rouge) highlighted with the arrow pointing at it, right under the hero, not buried in the footer with no marker.
- 3. Cut "FIVE More Days of App Fest" — it repeats the calendar already shown; replace with a single "add to calendar" CTA instead.
- 4. Bump font size on the day-specific fine print so I'm not squinting to see what's excluded.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App Fest is live: 10% off + a free 5-pc gift 💫`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 9 days of App Fest deals start now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rouge members: today's drop is 3 free SCOREMORE minis`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10% off sitewide, plus a new deal every day through 8/28`
