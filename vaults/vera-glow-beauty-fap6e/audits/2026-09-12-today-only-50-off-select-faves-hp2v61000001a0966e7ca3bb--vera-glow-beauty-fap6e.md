# Today only: 50% off select faves 🌶️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" under my Rouge status bar is the exact gut-punch I flagged on this same subject line's other variant this week — Sephora, you know I'm Rouge, you know my points balance, and leading with "you have 0 points" is either lazy segmentation or a broken merge field, and either way it reads bad. Past that though, this is actually one of the better-organized sends I've gotten from them lately: real 50% off callout, a legit "TODAY ONLY" badge, then the Kilian Paris Cocktails Collection launch gets its own full hero section with actual notes and price tiers, which is the launch-day clarity I'm always asking for.
- The problem is length and repetition fatigue — this is a full eight-product new-arrivals grid (Saie, Laneige, Gisou, Obart, Nars, Josie Maran, Armani, Touchland) stacked under the Kilian drop, then MORE sections after that (Favorites Kits, Defined Curls, Holiday Drops). By the time I hit "First Access: Holiday Drops" I've mentally checked out — compare that to e.l.f.'s "Glow Reviver Lip Oil" send (6/10) that did ONE product and got out, or ILIA's VIP sale (8/10) that led hard with one offer and didn't bury it under six more asks.
- I'd click through to the Kilian collection because new fragrance drops actually get me, but I'm not scrolling this whole thing on my phone, and I wouldn't forward it — it's too much of a kitchen-sink digest to send a friend anything specific.

## What's working

- The Kilian Paris Cocktails Collection gets real editorial treatment — three SKUs, price ranges, and actual scent notes (coffee, pineapple rum, champagne), which is the kind of launch-day specificity I want.
- "TODAY ONLY" badge on the 50% off banner is unmissable and gives credible urgency, unlike the vaguer sends I've logged from them earlier this week.
- "Clean" and "New · Only at Sephora" badges are scattered through the product grid — genuinely useful filtering signal for someone like me who cares about exclusivity and ingredient claims.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is visible at the bottom, at least acknowledging the loyalty structure even if the top banner botches my actual point count.

## What's weak

- "0 points" is either wrong or a broken personalization token — I'm Rouge tier, this should never show zero, and it's the first thing my eye hits.
- This is way too long — 50% off banner, Kilian hero, 8-product new grid, Favorites Kits, Defined Curls, Holiday Drops. That's six distinct asks in one email with no clear hierarchy on where I should actually go.
- The "50% off Select Beauty" hero doesn't show me which products are actually included — no grid of discounted items like the cleaner Sephora send I logged two days ago that had three real products up top.
- Cadence problem: this is at least the fifth near-identical "50% off" Sephora email I've logged this week (9/11 "Save 50%", two on 9/12 alone), and my inbox is starting to tune it out the way I already tune out Glossier's fifth Labor Day email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the loyalty points bug first — showing "0 points" to a Rouge member either kills trust or looks broken; either way it's the single biggest thing dragging this down.
- 2. Cut the email in half — lead with 50% off (with actual included products shown, like the 9/12 "cleanest by a mile" version did), then Kilian launch, then stop. Push Favorites Kits/Defined Curls/Holiday Drops to a separate send.
- 3. Show 2-3 actual discounted product thumbnails under the 50% off banner instead of one bottle shot — I want to see what's on sale before I click.
- 4. Slow the cadence — five "50% off" emails in a week is ILIA-VIP-sale energy without ILIA's discipline of making one offer feel special.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Kilian Paris fragrances + 50% off, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge status: 50% off select faves ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cocktails Collection just dropped — plus half off Clarins, Estée Lauder, OUAI`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop today's 50% off before it's gone (exclusions apply)`
