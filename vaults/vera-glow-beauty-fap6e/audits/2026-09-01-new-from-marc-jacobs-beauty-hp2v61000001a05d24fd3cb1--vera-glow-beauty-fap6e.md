# New from Marc Jacobs Beauty 💌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "New from Marc Jacobs Beauty" plus a free GWP pouch actually got me to open, and this one's a genuinely different animal from the five back-to-back "50% off Select Beauty" sends I've logged since 8/28 — this is a real brand-launch drop, not the same pink TODAY ONLY pill reskinned again. The GWP banner up top (free puffy makeup bag, $100+ MJ purchase, code MJGIFT) is exactly the kind of tier-adjacent perk I check for, and the "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" concealer callout scratches my exclusivity itch — that's a real differentiator versus Ulta, who can't claim Sephora-exclusive brands like Marc Jacobs Beauty.
- That said, this is a LONG scroll of nearly identical product-tile pairs (name, one-line description, no prices except the concealer) before it dead-ends into yet another "50% off Select Beauty TODAY ONLY" banner near the bottom — the same banner I've now seen at least six times in five days. It's like Sephora bolted their evergreen recurring promo onto an otherwise solid new-launch email out of habit.
- I'd click through on the concealer or the eyeshadow, mostly out of curiosity about swatches and shade range, but I wouldn't forward this to a friend — nothing here is a "you need to see this" moment, it's just a competent catalog drop. Marking as read after I check the concealer page.

## What's working

- The GWP hero (free pouch with $100+ MJ purchase, code MJGIFT) gives me a concrete reason to buy now, not just browse.
- "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" on the concealer is a real exclusivity signal — Ulta genuinely can't compete with MJ Beauty here.
- Product photography is clean and consistent — every tile has a real product shot, no broken images or placeholder gaps.
- The model's skin-forward "born with it" glow ties visually into the concealer's "skin-like coverage" pitch — cohesive art direction.
- Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is visible near the footer, so I can at least confirm my Rouge status is acknowledged somewhere.

## What's weak

- No shade range or price shown for most products (eyeshadow, lipstick, bronzer, blush stick, eyeliner, mascara) — as someone who's quick to do the points math, I want dollar amounts and Rouge points value up front, not just for one item.
- The "50% off Select Beauty TODAY ONLY" banner at the bottom feels bolted-on and undercuts the new-launch story — I've now logged this exact banner on 8/28 (x2), 8/29 (x2), 8/30, and 8/31, so seeing it again here just tells me it's not actually urgent.
- Nine near-identical product tiles in a row with no hierarchy — nothing tells me which ONE thing to care about beyond the concealer at the very top.
- Zero personalization — no "Rouge member" callout tied to the actual products, no skin-tone/type filtering, just generic copy that could go to any subscriber.
- The app-early-access and Chanel fragrance tiles at the bottom feel like unrelated inventory dumped in to fill space, diluting the Marc Jacobs focus.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the product list from nine tiles to three hero picks (concealer, eyeshadow, lipstick) with prices and Rouge points value attached — I want the math, not a full catalog scroll.
- 2. Drop the "50% off Select Beauty TODAY ONLY" banner from this email entirely — it's been sent standalone at least six times this week already; stapling it here just cheapens the MJ launch.
- 3. Add shade-range or "shop by skin tone" module under the concealer — MJ Beauty's whole thing is inclusive shade ranges, and this email doesn't show a single swatch.
- 4. Move the GWP mechanic (code MJGIFT) into the subject/preheader instead of burying the "how to redeem" logic only in the top banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Marc Jacobs pouch + new concealer, Rouge`
- **Subject Alt B:** `MJ Beauty just landed: shop new, get a free gift`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free puffy pouch with $100+ Marc Jacobs Beauty — code MJGIFT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New skin-like concealer, only at Sephora`
