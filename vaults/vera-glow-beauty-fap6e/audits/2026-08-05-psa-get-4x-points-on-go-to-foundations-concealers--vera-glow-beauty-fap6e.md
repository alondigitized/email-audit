# PSA 📣 Get 4X points on go-to foundations & concealers.
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the Sephora email I actually forward to my group chat. "4X points on go-to foundations & concealers" is speaking my exact language — not a vague "shop now," a hard number on the currency I actually track. Eleven products, each with its own point value stacked right next to the price (Charlotte Tilbury Airbrush Flawless at 208 points, the Rare Beauty Liquid Touch at 152, Merit's Minimalist at 152) — I can do the math in my head before I even click, which is exactly how I decide what goes in cart during a multiplier window.
- What pulls this above the usual noise is that it's a real category push, not another influencer-bait waterfall. Compare this to Sephora's own "Hot On Social" sends from 7/31 and 8/4 — those were the same nine-product LED-mask-and-hair-tool grab bag dressed up twice, and I side-eyed both. This is the opposite: tight category, real brands I actually rotate (Tower 28, Haus Labs, Saie, Merit), Clean badges where relevant, and a deadline that's TODAY. That's the kind of specificity the 8/1 Beauty Insider Celebration nailed too, and this send is cut from the same smarter cloth.
- Would I click? Yes — I'm going straight for the Charlotte Tilbury and the Rare Beauty since those are my dupes-of-the-month anyway. Would I forward it? Also yes, to my Sephora-Rouge group chat, specifically because the points-per-product breakdown does the math for them too. My only hesitation is the filler blocks halfway down (Summer Fridays app download, hair accessories) that have nothing to do with the headline offer and make we wonder if this could've been trimmed tighter.

## What's working

- The hero literally says "Your Foundation & Concealer Offers" — zero guessing what this email is for, straight into my category.
- Per-product point values next to price is the single best mechanic I've seen from Sephora all week — I don't have to open a calculator to know Dior's Forever Skin Perfect Stick at 232 points on a $58 item beats Too Faced at 144 points on $36.
- Loyalty tier bar (Insider/VIB/Rouge) is visible near the bottom, so I know exactly where I sit and what unlocks — a signal Ulta's "20% off one item" send from 7/26-8/1 never bothers to give me.
- "Ends 8/5" is today, and unlike the vague urgency language other brands lean on, this is a real, checkable deadline against my calendar.
- No render bugs — clean grid, product shots load, Clean badges are crisp, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- The "New Summer Fridays" app-download block and "Hot Hair Accessories" block bury the foundation/concealer focus under two completely unrelated categories right when I'm mid-scroll doing my points math.
- "STACK 'EM UP" as the primary CTA is cute but vague — it doesn't tell me if I'm stacking points, products, or codes until I actually read further down.
- There's a second competing offer ("$5 off, redeem 250 points") crammed in below the product grid — two calls to action diluting one email's job.
- No reviews or star ratings on any of the eleven products, which matters when I'm choosing between, say, Haus Labs and Make Up For Ever on foundation coverage.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Summer Fridays and hair accessories blocks entirely (or move to a separate send) — this email earns its keep by being hyper-focused on one category, don't dilute it.
- 2. Sharpen the CTA from "STACK 'EM UP" to something like "Shop 4X Points" so it's unambiguous on first glance.
- 3. Move the deadline ("Ends 8/5") up into the subject line or preheader — it's the strongest urgency lever in the whole email and it's currently buried in a small banner.
- 4. Add a one-line "as a Rouge member you also get X" callout near the tier bar — I'm Rouge, tell me what that actually buys me on this offer.
- 5. Drop star ratings or "X,XXX loves" under at least the hero products (Charlotte Tilbury, Rare Beauty) — social proof would push me from "considering" to "adding to cart."
