# Your cart expires soon!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same fake cart nudge again, and this time it's dressed up as "YOU LIKED THESE." I never added anything to a Skechers cart. I got "Your Items Are Waiting!" on 10/31, "Pick up where you left off!" on 11/1, and "Your cart expires soon!" on 11/2. Now the identical subject is back for 11/3 with a slightly new hero. The Day of the Dead skull UNO is cute, but "Complete your order" for an order that doesn't exist is a stretch.
- The real offer is buried halfway down: 25% off for Skechers Plus members, 2 days only, through 11/4. That's the only reason to click, and it sits below six random shoes and a black "Shop all best sellers" bar. I'd do the points math instantly if the discount were up top. Sephora's 10% off Luxe Beauty email this week led with the offer, and this one made me scroll to find it.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap the 25% block, and move on. I wouldn't forward it. If I needed shoes, the discount would pull me in, but nothing here is aimed at me.

## What's working

- The 25% off Plus members banner is clear: big number, "2 days only," and a "Valid thru 11/4" deadline.
- The skull UNO hero is a distinctive product shot and is the only thing with any personality.
- The member framing gets a perk in front of me, like a tier benefit at Ulta or Sephora.
- The "Shop additional styles" list of Women, Men, Kids, Clothing, New Arrivals and Sale is easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- "Your cart expires soon" is a lie for me. There was no cart, and it's the fourth or fifth version of this nudge in a week.
- The "Complete your order" CTA points at a cart that doesn't exist, so it reads as bait.
- The 25% off offer is buried under the hero and a six-shoe grid. Ulta puts its $10/$15/$20 coupon ladder at the very top.
- The product grid mixes men's John Deere boots with women's sneakers, so it isn't personalized. It looks like generic best sellers.
- The fine print says "Holiday warm-up" and excludes sale styles and prior purchases. The banner says "25% off" with no exclusions in sight, which feels a little bait-and-switch.
- There's no price on any shoe, no reviews, and no tier math for the Plus program, such as what it costs to join or what I'd save.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the offer. Put "25% off for Plus members, ends 11/4" in the hero and put the skull shoe under it.
- 2. Only send the cart framing if there's a real cart. Otherwise send an honest "Plus members: 25% off for 2 days" email.
- 3. Show the product I actually browsed, with a price and the discounted price. Drop the men's boots from a women's grid.
- 4. Say what Plus is and what it saves me, the way Sephora spells out Insider math, and put the exclusions near the banner.
- 5. Stop sending near-identical emails daily. e.l.f. and Glossier at least rotate their creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Plus members: 25% off ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days only: 25% off your Skechers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Skechers Plus perk: 25% off online through 11/4. Exclusions apply.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Day of the Dead UNO and 5 other picks, all 25% off with Plus.`
