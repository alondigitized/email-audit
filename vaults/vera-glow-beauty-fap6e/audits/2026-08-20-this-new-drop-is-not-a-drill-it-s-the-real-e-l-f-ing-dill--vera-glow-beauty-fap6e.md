## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. is fully committed to the pickle bit now — "Totally Dill-lulu," "The Real Dill," "E.L.F. Von Dill," "Briny Blender" — and honestly? I'm charmed against my will. This is the fun, weird, Gen-Z-coded e.l.f. energy that makes them stand out from Sephora and Ulta's endless "20% off one item" copy-paste sends.
- That said, this is a $9-$11 mass-prestige lip balm drop, not a Rouge or Diamond-tier event, so there's zero points math for me to do here — no beauty insider callout, no loyalty tie-in, nothing that acknowledges I'm a repeat e.l.f. shopper versus someone who just wandered in. - Verdict: I'd open it (the subject made me laugh), I'd scroll through for the pun value, and I might actually tap "Get Pickled" for the lip balm trio because $9 is nothing — but I'm forwarding this to my group chat for the jokes, not because I think my friends need dill-flavored lip balm.

## What's working

- The pickle/dill pun theme is fully committed and consistent start to finish — "Dill-lulu," "The Real Dill," "Von Dill," "Get Pickled" — it's silly but it's *on-brand* silly, unlike Sally Beauty's ninth identical COLORfest rerun.
- $9 price point for limited-edition flavors is a real, low-friction offer — I don't need to think twice about that spend.
- The tongs-holding-a-cornichon-shaped-lip-balm shot is genuinely eye-catching food-prop styling, way more creative than a flat product-on-white grid.
- "NEW" and "NEW & LIMITED EDITION" badges give me quick urgency cues without shouting.
- "Top Picks for You" section at the bottom at least gestures at personalization, even if the products themselves feel generic.

## What's weak

- Five near-identical green hero blocks in a row (Dill-lulu, Real Dill, Von Dill, Spicy Lil Dill, Briny Blender) all with the same "Shop Now" pill button — by block four I've stopped reading and I'm just scrolling past.
- Zero loyalty/points language anywhere — as a Beauty Squad-adjacent shopper who does the math on every purchase, this reads like e.l.f. doesn't know or care that I'm a repeat customer.
- "Briny Blender" is a $7 makeup sponge shoehorned into a lip balm launch just to keep the pickle joke going — it dilutes the actual news (three new lip balm flavors) into a generic "everything is pickle-themed today" grab bag.
- No skin tone or shade-range signal on any of these lip products — for tinted balms that's a real miss when Ilia and Drunk Elephant at least show diverse lip swatches.
- The close-up lip shots (green shimmer balm, red spicy balm) are pretty but interchangeable with e.l.f.'s last five sends — nothing here screams "new drop" visually beyond copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the repeated green hero blocks down to one consolidated "3 new dill flavors, $9 each" carousel instead of five stacked full-width sections — that's the single biggest drag on scroll and attention right now.
- 2. Add a loyalty hook — even one line like "Beauty Squad members get early access" — since e.l.f. has a loyalty program and this send makes zero use of it.
- 3. Move "Top Picks for You" personalization higher, or make it feel less like a generic afterthought grid at the bottom.
- 4. Show shade/finish swatches on skin, not just isolated lip close-ups, so I know what "sheer green with shimmer" actually looks like on.
- 5. Drop "Briny Blender" from this specific send or give it its own email — it dilutes the lip balm launch focus.

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Cosmetics "NEW drop" Campaign

### 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard MJML/BEE-generated table layout with Braze link-tracking redirects; automated QA shows a 27% pass rate driven by broken link resolution, a missing plain-text part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **4 of 12 probed links fail to resolve** — the Braze redirect wrapper (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) decodes to a **relative path with no host**, not an absolute URL:
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` (CTA: "Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` (CTA: "Receipt scanning...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?deep_link_value=...` (CTA: "beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  - This is a redirect-destination configuration bug, not a client-rendering issue — the wrapper needs an absolute target (e.g., `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/products/...`).
- 29 lower-priority (footer/social/utility) links were deprioritized and not probed per QA scope — not a defect.
- Image asset itself (`braze-images.com/.../original.png`) resolves fine and is separate from the broken CTA redirects.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the Braze/link-wrapper redirect config** so all four failing CTAs resolve to absolute URLs (with domain) rather than relative paths — this is blocking "Shop Now," account login, and app-download conversions entirely.

4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers** are actually being sent at the SMTP layer (vs. dropped by the AgentMail relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance as of their 2024 policy.
5. **Confirm SPF/DKIM pass** via a direct header capture outside the relay, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't observed here.
6. Add consistent UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`) across all CTA links once URLs are fixed, for consistent attribution.
