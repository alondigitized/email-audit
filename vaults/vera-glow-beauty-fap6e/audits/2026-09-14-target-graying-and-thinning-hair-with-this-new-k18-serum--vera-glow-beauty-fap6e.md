# Target graying and thinning hair with this NEW K18 serum
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "you have 0 points" is staring at me again right under the header — same gut-punch I flagged on the last two Sephora sends this week, and I'm Rouge, so either fix the merge field or stop showing it. But once I get past that, this is genuinely the most interesting Sephora email I've seen in days: a real "before/after 3 months" hair scalp photo for the new K18 scalp serum, with actual clinical stats ("up to 19,000 more hairs and 70% fewer greys") instead of the usual "50% off select faves" grab-bag. That's a launch story, not a discount dump.
- The problem is it buries that story under a "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" banner at the very top that has nothing to do with K18, so my eye doesn't even know what this email is about until I scroll past the "TRENDING NOW / Thanks later" filler section. I'd open this because K18 is a brand I actually track, but I'd scroll straight past the top banner and the generic 8-product grid to get to the one thing I came for.
- Would I click? Yes, on the K18 tile specifically. Would I forward it? Maybe to my friend who's been asking about the K18 scalp serum since Sephora's Beauty Insider event teased it — the clinical photo is genuinely shareable. Everything else in this email is noise I'd ignore.

## What's working

- The before/after scalp photo with "LESS GREY / MORE HAIR" callouts is the strongest visual hook I've seen from Sephora in weeks — it's specific, it's science-y, it's not another flat-lay product shot.
- "Only at Sephora" tagging on the K18 serum plus the FutureIQ Biomimetic Hair Longevity Serum name gives me real launch-exclusivity signal, which is exactly what gets a Beauty Insider event hoarder like me to act.
- The product grid has real badges I actually use to filter — "CLEAN," "NEW," "Online Only" — so I can skim by what matters to me instead of guessing.
- Prices are visible on every tile ($120, $75, $32, $180...) with no need to click through, which saves me a step.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" under my Rouge status bar — again. This is now three sends running where Sephora shows me a blank points balance instead of my real Rouge number, and it undercuts every "member" gesture in the email.
- The "50% off Select Beauty — TODAY ONLY" hero at the top is a completely different offer than the K18 story below it, so the email opens feeling like two unrelated campaigns stapled together — Glossier's "Brow down" send this week proved you don't need a banner fighting the hero for attention.
- "TRENDING NOW / Thanks later / SHOP NOW" is a full section that says nothing — no product, no category, just filler between the discount banner and the actual K18 content. That's dead scroll space on mobile.
- The 8-item grid below K18 (Rhode, Charlotte Tilbury, Guerlain, Dr. Idriss, Living Proof, Haus Labs, Valentino, Cyklar) has zero connective tissue to hair care or to each other — it reads like a leftover "new arrivals" dump tacked on, which is the same complaint I had about Ulta's barcode-hero send running the same generic mechanic three times since 7/26.
- No age/skin-type/hair-type personalization anywhere — I've told Sephora my hair concerns before, and this reads like it went to every Rouge member regardless of profile.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with K18, not the 50% banner.** Cut the disconnected "50% off Select Beauty" hero and "TRENDING NOW" filler entirely — open on the before/after scalp photo. That's the actual news and it deserves the first screen.
- 2. **Fix my points balance.** Third send in a row showing "0 points" for a Rouge member — this is now a pattern worth escalating internally, not a one-off glitch.
- 3. **Tighten the product grid to a hair-care capsule.** If you're going to follow K18 with more products, make them shampoo/scalp/hair-adjacent, not a random mix of fragrance and lip liner — give me a reason to keep scrolling in the same mindset.
- 4. **Add a "based on your hair goals" line** near the K18 block — even a generic "since you shopped hair care last visit" would beat the current blank-slate treatment.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `12-week clinical study, only at Sephora — see the before/after`
