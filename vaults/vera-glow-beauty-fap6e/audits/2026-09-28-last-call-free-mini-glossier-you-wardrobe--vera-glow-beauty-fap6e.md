# Last call! Free Mini Glossier You Wardrobe
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third Glossier You Discovery Kit send in a week. 9/22 was "Try our fragrances for free!", 9/26 was "Still on!", and now it's "Last call!" It's the same $50+ GWP in a new urgency outfit. The subject says "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe" and the hero says "Discovery Kit," so even the product name changed between the inbox and the body. My points-math brain wants a deadline, and there isn't one. "Last call" with no date is just a vibe.
- The layout is clean, and the hero offer is the first thing I see. Then it goes soft. Below the fragrance blocks there's a "Start Here" grid of Cloud Paint, Boy Brow, Lip Line and Lash Slick. Those are four evergreen products with no prices, no shades named, and no reviews. Sephora would have put a threshold nudge next to that grid, like "add Cloud Paint to hit $50." Glossier leaves me to do that math alone.
- Would I click? Maybe once, if I were already building a $50 cart. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait to see whether "last call" actually ends.

## What's working

- The hero puts the offer first. "Free Glossier You Discovery Kit with your $50+ order" sits top-left next to a strong red product shot.
- The "LAST CALL" eyebrow is easy to spot and sets the urgency tone fast.
- The kit photo shows all four vials, so I can see what "free" means.
- The Cloud Paint and Boy Brow tiles give me a way to reach $50 with products I'd actually buy.
- The layout is uncluttered and reads fine on a phone. The "Shop now" buttons are big enough to tap.

## What's weak

- "Last call" has no date or time. I can't plan around it, so the urgency feels fake.
- The subject calls it a "Mini Glossier You Wardrobe" and the body calls it a "Discovery Kit." That mismatch makes me doubt it's the same offer.
- This is the same GWP as 9/22 and 9/26. That's three sends in seven days, and the "last call" has lost its credibility.
- The four "Start Here" tiles have no prices, shade names or reviews. "Shop now" appears seven times and never says what I'd be shopping for.
- Nothing speaks to Rouge or Diamond members. There's no points multiplier or early access.
- The asterisk terms are tiny and sit at the very bottom, so I can't see exclusions without hunting for them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, like "Ends Sunday 11:59pm PT," ideally with a countdown. That does more for this email than any other fix.
- 2. Use the same name for the gift in the subject and the hero.
- 3. Tie the product tiles to the $50 threshold. Show prices and add a line like "Cloud Paint + Boy Brow = $50, gift unlocked."
- 4. Give each button its own copy, like "Shop Cloud Paint" or "Shop Lash Slick," instead of seven identical "Shop now" buttons.
- 5. Move the key terms up next to the CTA.
- 6. Stop resending this creative. If the offer is really ending, send it once, with a date.
