## What's working

- The hero is a real scroll-stopper. The orange eye and red curls feel current and closer to editorial than the usual drugstore hero.
- "Get it today with 2-hour delivery" is the right Halloween-day utility, and the four-icon strip repeats it.
- The product modules are clean and scannable. Lashes, nails and hair color each get a headline with two products, and the "new at Sally" badges flag newness.
- The Klarna line is useful. It tells me about 4 interest-free payments on orders over $35.
- The model's age and style fit me. This is the first Sally hero in a while that isn't a 50-year-old gray-coverage model, like 10/23 "Holiday glow, without the gray."

## What's weak

- The BOGO 50% lash deal is the only product-level offer, and it's a small caption I could easily miss. I had to hunt for it.
- Two stacked banners compete at the top. The $5 off $25 rewards signup is in pink and the 15% off next buy is in red. I'm already a Rewards-style member everywhere, so "sign up" reads as stranger copy. Ulta does this to me too.
- Every product link is a bare "Shop now," with no prices. My points math has nothing to work with. I can't tell the lash set's price or what the BOGO saves.
- The subject, "Last minute details," is vague and says nothing about Halloween, lashes or an offer. The body has no countdown or "ends tonight" cue either, even though the fine print says the offer ends 10/31 at 11:59 p.m. EST.
- The fine print is a wall of text. The $5 reward comes in 5 business days and excludes tax, gift cards and shipping, which makes the top banner feel less honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the BOGO 50% lashes offer into the hero, with a badge and an "ends tonight" line. Lead with the offer instead of the mood.
- 2. Put prices and a points or rewards callout on each product. Show "$X, earn Y" so I can do the math.
- 3. Collapse the two banners into one clear member message, and recognize existing members instead of pitching signup again.
- 4. Put a countdown or a "Order by 6 pm for 2-hour delivery" cutoff next to the CTA, so the urgency is credible.
- 5. Make the CTA specific, like "Shop Halloween lashes," instead of repeating "Shop now."

# Technical Audit: Sally Beauty, "Last minute details"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML support and responsive CSS. QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The real defects are third-party tracking pixels (an unresolved macro, one plain-HTTP image, missing alt text). Header-based checks could not be verified. The HTML source is truncated, so findings are limited to what the QA data and the visible head show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro in a pixel URL:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The ad-server macro was never substituted, so the cache-busting and impression attribution for this pixel are likely broken. Mail clients may also cache or dedupe the request.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` loads over plain HTTP. Clients such as Gmail proxy it, but others may block it or show a mixed-content warning. This is an Epsilon/Conversant tracker, and an HTTPS endpoint should be requested.
- **Unprobed links:** 53 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Pixel volume:** At least three third-party pixels are present (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`). Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on four images:**
  - An image with empty `src` and no alt, which is a broken or empty `<img>` element.
  - `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel`
  - `dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`

  Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`. Without them, some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Font stack mismatch:** `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless but inconsistent with the declared Open Sans.
- **Meta tag:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other meta tags in an XHTML doctype. This is a minor validity issue with no client impact.
- **Unverifiable:** The truncated source prevents checking the main-content images' alt text, lang attribute, and role="presentation" on layout tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- `[CACHEBUSTER]` is an unresolved macro (see section 2).
- `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` path looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier exposed in a third-party pixel URL, which is a privacy consideration. The token itself is populated.
- No other unresolved tokens are visible in the available source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. The sender is a major retailer on a dedicated subdomain (`em.sallybeauty.com`) and very likely sends them. To confirm, check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, and the QA note attributes this to the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Do not treat this as a sender failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and destination URLs cannot be assessed. All 53 tracking links go through click-redirect domains and were not resolved.
- The visible source gives no evidence of landing page alignment issues.

2. Ask for or switch to an HTTPS endpoint for the dotomi/Conversant pixel.
