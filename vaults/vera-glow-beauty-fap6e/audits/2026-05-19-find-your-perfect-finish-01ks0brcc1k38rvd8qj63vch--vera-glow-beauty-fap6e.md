# Find your perfect finish
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Four emails in four days. I'm counting: May 16 welcome, May 17 awards editorial, May 18 brand overview, and now this — a finish-type quiz with a Friends + Family sale buried inside it. The first two emails earned their opens. This one is starting to feel like ILIA forgot I've already been welcomed.
- That said, the "What kind of finish do you like?" structure is genuinely the smartest thing ILIA has done in this run — organizing the lineup by Natural Matte, Dewy, and Natural is exactly how I actually think when I'm shopping base. I have opinions about finish. So I'd have been into this editorial... if it weren't also hiding two different discount percentages in the same scroll. Top promo bar says 20% off sitewide. The big pink sale section further down says 30% off. I do the math on every Sephora Beauty Insider event and every Ulta 21 Days window. Two different numbers in the same email without an explanation is the kind of thing that makes me stall and close the tab.
- Mark as read, maybe come back later on desktop to figure out which discount is real. Would not forward to a friend until the offer math is settled.

## What's working

- The finish-type quiz framework (Natural Matte / Dewy / Natural) is genuinely useful segmentation — it tells me what to buy before I have to do the product research myself.
- Model diversity across the three finish categories earns trust; if I'm trying to match the Super Serum Skin Tint to my skin tone, I need to see that shade range actually represented.
- "3X POINTS THIS WEEK" in the top promo bar is exactly the signal I watch for — I'm always calculating multiplier windows, and ILIA surfacing this at the very top is correct priority.
- Specific product callouts (Super Serum Skin Tint SPF 40, Free Skin Serum Foundation) beat a vague "shop foundations" link — I can evaluate whether these are already in my routine.
- "Shade. Set. Match." is clean brand voice that delivers a function without sounding clinical.

## What's weak

- **20% at the top, 30% in the body** — that discrepancy is a trust-breaker for anyone doing the math. No explanation of whether it's a tier difference, a time gate, or a typo. Drunk Elephant's "Let's get something clear" run was confusing on concept but at least the offer was clean.
- Friends + Family sale with **no visible end date** means I'm buying on vibes. Charlotte Tilbury's "Unlock Mini Magic Gifts" send two days ago had a clearer urgency signal than this, and it wasn't even a sitewide sale.
- **Fourth email in four days** is pushing unsubscribe territory. Sephora Insider sends daily because their offer volume justifies it. ILIA is a focused brand and this cadence is flattening the specialness.
- "Thanks. A Million" section is a visual mystery — I genuinely could not tell if that was customer reviews, a loyalty milestone, or UGC at a single scroll. Ambiguous creative costs a click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reconcile the discount** — 20% and 30% cannot coexist without explanation. If VIP/early access gets 30% and standard gets 20%, say so in one line. The confusion is killing conversion confidence.
- 2. **Add a deadline** — "ends Sunday" or even just "3 days left" is all I need. CT does this in every promotional send; it works on me every time.
- 3. **Make the subject do sale duty** — "Find your perfect finish" is fine editorial copy but fails completely at communicating that a major sale is live right now. The subject is leaving money on the table.
- 4. **Pull back the cadence** — 48 hours between sends minimum, or segment me into a "sale alerts only" track that feels like a value-add rather than a drip.
- 5. **Label "Thanks. A Million"** clearly — "Over 1M sold" or "What our customers say" converts that section into social proof instead of a layout puzzle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends + Family is live — find your finish, 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off sitewide + 3X points — which finish are you?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Natural matte, dewy, or just good skin — all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The F+F event is live. SPF tints, foundations, the whole ILIA lineup.`
