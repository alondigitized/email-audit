## Executive summary

- So I have context walking in here: this is the second time this specific June 19 shampoo email has crossed my inbox — same subject, different delivery ID. ## What's working

- **Hero offer is immediate and readable.** "Up to 25% off Shampoos & Conditioners" with "Save through Tue, 6/24 — real-time savings" requires zero effort to parse; the end date is right there.
- **500 Bonus Points layer is the right loyalty hook.** The $75+ spend to earn 500 points with a code is exactly the math I run as a Rouge member — this section earns its placement.

## What's weak

- **"Woo-hoo" tone doesn't match Rouge membership.** I've spent years at Sephora and thousands of dollars. "Woo-hoo 🎉" feels like they're surprised I'm here, not like they know who I am. Compare Sephora's own June 11 "STARTS TODAY" send — more confident, more direct, scored a 9.
- **Father's Day section is a hard mismatch.** I opened for haircare. Suddenly I'm being asked to "shop men's gifts." That's a completely different purchase intent dropped into the middle of my shampoo email.
- **"Beauty for All — Essentials for every routine" is filler.** That section is a generic link that lives in every Sephora email. It dilutes the haircare focus without adding anything I'd act on.
- **"Select" with no brand callouts is a trust flag.** After two June 16 sends where the "50% off select beauty" turned out to be narrow, seeing "select shampoos" without any brand names visible makes me hesitant to click before I can verify my preferred brands are actually included.
- **FREE Same-Day Delivery dates may already be stale.** The delivery promo banner appears to reference 6/18–6/20 — if this email is hitting non-openers as a resend on 6/19 or later, the 6/18 window is gone and the 6/20 window is same-day only.

- 1. **Name the brands in the hero.** "25% off Kérastase, Briogeo, and more through Tue" tells me immediately whether I'm buying. "Select shampoos and conditioners" without brand visibility sends me to the site to find out the discount doesn't cover what I want — that's a conversion killer.
- 2. **Cut the Father's Day section from haircare emails entirely.** Or run it as a separate send. e.l.f. Hair kept its "Bundle your haircare" email single-purpose and landed a 9; Sephora's June 11 "STARTS TODAY" did the same. One campaign per email is just better execution.
- 3. **Surface the 500 Bonus Points code in the hero.** The points accelerator is the strongest click driver for a Rouge member. Don't bury it three sections down — pull the "spend $75, earn 500 points" callout directly under the price-off hero.
- 4. **Drop "Woo-hoo" for a subject line that respects tenure.** Something that signals this is for members, not for everyone.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 failure)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Locking `maximum-scale=1` prevents users with low vision from zooming. Remove `maximum-scale=1` or change to `maximum-scale=5`.

**Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` (WCAG 3.1.1 failure)**
The `<html>` tag declares XHTML namespaces but no `lang`:
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
Should be `<html lang="en" ...>`. Screen readers use this to select the correct speech engine.

**Overly broad `text-size-adjust: none`**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
Applying this to `body` and block elements suppresses OS-level text scaling for users with accessibility needs. Scope this to `td` only (standard practice) and remove from `div`, `a`, `span`, `strong`.

**`<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` — non-standard value**
`no index` (with a space) is not a recognized robots directive; the correct token is `noindex`. Most crawlers will ignore the malformed value rather than respect it, meaning web-viewable versions of this email could be indexed.
Correct to: `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />`

**Generic `<title>`**
```html
<title>Sephora</title>
```
Screen readers announce the title when the email opens in a browser/webview. A descriptive title (`Up to 25% off Select Shampoos & Conditioners — Sephora`) improves accessibility for web-view recipients.
