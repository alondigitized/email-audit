# We’d love to hear from you!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Survey email dropping right in the middle of Stacking Weekend follow-up traffic — which, honestly, is the most Sephora thing ever. The preheader does the heavy lifting here: "Earn 100 Beauty Insider reward points for your time" is the only reason I'm not immediately archiving this. I do the points math in my sleep, and 100 points as a Rouge member is basically a dollar of reward value for ten minutes of my life. The math is fine. The execution is not.
- What actually made me pause — and not in a good way — is the terms block at the bottom. "By starting the survey, you agree to not discuss nor disclose any information or questions asked therein with anyone into perpetuity." Into *perpetuity*. That means I can't verbally tell my friend what questions Sephora asked me. I can't tweet about it. Forever. For a survey about how I shop for moisturizer. That's a weird, aggressive legal posture that completely undercuts the "xoxo Sephora" sign-off two paragraphs above it.
- I'd mark this as read and maybe come back to it — the points are real — but I'd be annoyed the whole time I was taking the survey. Definitely not forwarding this to anyone. That NDA clause would send my group chat into chaos.

## What's working

- **Preheader is doing all the work.** "Earn 100 Beauty Insider reward points for your time" is specific, loyalty-coded, and correctly leads with the value exchange before you even open. Best line in the whole email.
- **Deadline is credible and visible.** Tuesday July 7, 11:59am Pacific — that's the same deadline as Stacking Weekend. Good alignment with the campaign calendar I'm already tracking.
- **Clean render, no breakage.** Text-heavy but everything displayed correctly. No broken images, no overlapping elements. Points for that given how often survey emails feel like afterthoughts.
- **"You will only be contacted 2 times at most within a calendar year" in the footer** is a nice trust signal — shows they're not about to spam me with survey requests.
- **Separate sender address** (opinions@beauty.sephora.com) is smart segmentation. Keeps survey traffic out of my promo inbox cadence.

## What's weak

- **"Hi Sephora Shopper" is unacceptable at Rouge tier.** Sephora uses my name in every promo email. This survey team apparently had zero access to personalization. I'm a known customer — treat me like one.
- **The NDA clause at the bottom is alarming.** "Not discuss nor disclose any information or questions asked therein with anyone into perpetuity" — for a shopping survey? That's the kind of language that makes you screenshot and text your friend immediately, which I guess is now legally prohibited? Massive trust-eroding move.
- **CTA copy is lazy.** "CLICK HERE TO GET STARTED" is generic corporate-speak. Compare to how e.l.f. writes their CTAs — direct, specific, personality-forward. This reads like it came from a different team entirely.
- **100 points buried in paragraph two.** The whole email leads with "Sephora looks for opinions" before getting to the reward. The points should be the hero, not the disclaimer.
- **No visual warmth at all.** It's a wall of text on white. The Stacking Weekend launch email on July 1st (a 9 in my recent history) had visual energy — this looks like a terms-of-service update.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Personalize the salutation, full stop.** "Hi Vera" should be table stakes at this point. The promo team does it; the survey team needs to match.
- 2. **Lead with the 100 points, not the ask.** Flip the opening paragraph: "As a thank you for being a valued Rouge member, we're giving you 100 Beauty Insider points for 10 minutes of your time." THEN make the ask.
- 3. **Rewrite that NDA clause or pull it entirely.** At minimum, soften it and explain *why* — is it because the survey contains proprietary competitive research? If yes, say that. "The questions inside are confidential" lands very differently than "perpetuity" language that sounds like litigation prep.
- 4. **Give the CTA copy a pulse.** Something like "Share My Feedback →" or "Take the Survey (10 min)" — anything that sounds like a human wrote it.
- 5. **Tie it visually to the Stacking Weekend campaign.** A single branded banner showing the Sephora Collection sale context would remind me why my opinion matters right now — connect the survey to the moment.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Vera, earn 100 points — quick survey (ends 7/7)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Got 10 min? 100 Beauty Insider points are yours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your feedback shapes what's next at Sephora — takes 10 min, points post within 30 days`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `100 bonus points for 10 minutes. Survey closes July 7 at midnight PT.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is sent via Epsilon/Harmony ESP infrastructure with functional redirect-based click tracking and open-pixel tracking. Two hard blockers exist: zero-character plain-text fallback (deliverability FAIL) and missing List-Unsubscribe headers (compliance risk), both likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay capture rather than the upstream send.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No issues with link structure itself**, but notable observations:

- All clicks route through Epsilon's redirect system: `beauty.sephora.com/T/{campaignId}/{recipientHash}/{messageId}?__dU__={token}` — click tracking is active.
- Open tracking pixel present: `https://beauty.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` with base64-encoded recipient identifier in the URL path.
- "View in browser" link uses a `/H/` path variant: `https://beauty.sephora.com/H/2/v610000019f292425be8a785df4bbe5be50/...` — consistent with Epsilon's web-view handler.
- The `__dU__` query parameter on the logo CTA (`?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gvf-dtDhFgyx2PTfuJRsWdK`) is an Epsilon device/user token not present on the web-view link — minor inconsistency but not a bug.
- **Survey destination URL not visible in truncated source** — cannot confirm the landing page or UTM passthrough for the primary CTA. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issues found:**

- **[WARN x5] Spacer GIFs missing alt text** — `http://epidm.edgesuite.net/CMS/Coding/Sephora/misc/spacerGif10X10.gif` has no `alt` attribute on any of its 5 instances. Decorative spacers should carry `alt=""` (empty string) so screen readers skip them. Missing `alt` causes some clients to render broken-image icons when images are blocked.
- **[WARN x5] Spacer GIFs served over HTTP** — same `http://epidm.edgesuite.net/...` URL. Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail proxy/block mixed-content HTTP images. These spacers control layout spacing; if blocked, vertical rhythm collapses.
- **[WARN] Open-pixel missing alt text** — `https://beauty.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` tracking pixel has no `alt` attribute.
- **Deprecated `<font>` tags** — body copy uses nested `<font style="...">` tags (two levels deep). These are HTML 4 relics; inline `<span>` is the current approach, though practically harmless in most email clients.
- **DOCTYPE is HTML 4.01 Transitional** — acceptable for email but worth noting; it opts the email into quirks-mode rendering in some clients.
- **Preview text div is empty** — `<div class="set_outlook_hidden" ...></div>` contains no text. The subject line alone fills the inbox preview snippet; a populated preheader would extend messaging real estate. (Out of scope for technical audit — flagged for completeness.)

No issues with: `lang="en"` attribute, viewport meta, `format-detection` meta, or mobile media query breakpoint (600px).

### 5. Compliance

**Issues found:**

- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent** — QA reports header not captured. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the native "Unsubscribe" button; its absence degrades one-click unsubscribe UX and increases spam complaint risk. Likely a relay-capture gap, but should be verified against raw headers from a direct SMTP capture.
- **[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058)** — one-click POST-based unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policies (>5k sends/day). Absence can suppress inbox placement for high-volume sends.
- **[WARN] Authentication-Results header not captured** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown for this capture. `opinions@beauty.sephora.com` sending through Epsilon's infrastructure should produce a valid DKIM signature; this needs confirmation via direct header inspection.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 characters** — QA confirms text/plain MIME part is empty. This is a CAN-SPAM/CASL best-practice gap and a deliverability risk; some spam filters penalize missing plain-text parts. Epsilon's platform supports auto-generating plain text from HTML — this should be enabled.
- **Cannot confirm** physical mailing address or footer unsubscribe link from truncated HTML. These are CAN-SPAM requirements; verification requires full source.

**Cannot fully assess** — the primary survey CTA URL is not visible in the truncated HTML source. The following is observable:
