# NEW Soft Glam Cream Blush in 7 shades 💖 
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, eight e.l.f. emails in eight days and I am starting to feel it. I opened this one mostly out of reflex — e.l.f. is basically a permanent fixture in my inbox at this point — but the subject actually earned it. A cream blush launch with seven named shades at $7 each is genuinely news. That's $49 for the full range, which is still less than one NARS Blush. My completionist brain was clocked before I even scrolled.
- The email itself is doing exactly what a launch email should: one product, one price, all the shades laid out, nothing competing for attention. The model is stunning — young, curly hair, warm brown skin, blush worn naturally rather than baked-on — and the color story reads even on a small screen. "Plum intended" and "cinna-mood" are doing everything I expect from e.l.f. shade naming, and I genuinely smiled at "STICK AROUND" as the first CTA. They know what they're doing.
- I'm clicking. I'm not forwarding it though — the thing that would make this shareable is side-by-side swatches on different skin tones, and that's missing. I'll mark it read, open the link, and start doing the shade math for what works on me.

## What's working

- **$7 EACH is the loudest thing in the email** — it's the dominant type size and I registered it before I finished reading the hero; genuinely impressive pricing for a launch
- **All seven shades named and listed** — "gumball," "plum intended," "heat check," "muted mauve," "pink on it," "melon-drama," "cinna-mood" — the wordplay is consistent and fun, and listing every shade signals confidence in the full range
- **"STICK AROUND" CTA is earned cleverness** — it's a product pun that works, unlike a lot of e.l.f. wordplay that can veer into try-hard territory
- **Model is a strong match for me** — natural makeup application, curly hair, warm brown skin; this looks like something I'd actually wear, not an editorial fantasy
- **Single-product focus, no clutter** — unlike the July 21 summer checklist email which was sprawling and unfocused, this one stays in its lane

## What's weak

- **Zero urgency** — "NEW" is not a reason to buy today. Compare e.l.f.'s own July 22 "6 HOURS ONLY ❤️" send: that one had a hard clock on it and I felt it. This launch has no equivalent. "Available while shades last" or even "Launching today" would change the energy.
- **No Beauty Squad / loyalty callout** — I'm spending real points currency here. At $7 a shade, what am I earning? Glossier's recent GWP reminder made me feel like I was about to lose something specific; this email treats me like a stranger with no loyalty history.
- **Cadence fatigue is accumulating** — I'm genuinely fond of e.l.f. but eight emails in eight days is starting to train me to scroll past first and look later. Even Drunk Elephant's "Feeling sticky?" landed cleaner because I hadn't heard from them in a few days.
- **"SHOP NOW" bottoms out the shade section** — they hit a creative high with "STICK AROUND" and then defaulted to the most generic CTA in the toolkit for the actual conversion moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add skin-tone swatches or a shade-finder** — this is the single change that would lift this email the most. Seven shades sounds like a lot; without seeing them on more than one complexion, I'm guessing and some people won't guess. "Find your shade" or a three-skin-tone swatch grid would convert browsers and make this genuinely share-worthy.
- 2. **Give the launch a credible event frame** — "Launching today" / "Available now — be first" / "New arrivals: limited first run" would create a reason to act. A new product isn't a reason to rush; a launch window is.
- 3. **Surface Beauty Squad points in the body** — "Earn X points per shade" or a callout like "Buy 3+, hit your next reward" would make loyalty members buy the full set rather than cherry-picking one.
- 4. **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop all 7 shades"** — it reinforces the product breadth and is far more specific at the exact conversion moment.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buttery, buildable, and under budget. All 7, all $7.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick one. Collect all seven. Your call.`
