# Last chance to grab your lippie gift! 💄
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. is now closing out a stretch that's run "National Lipstick Day gift" (7/27), "PLZ Clarify" pores (7/28), "Glow Reviver Lip Oil BOGO" (7/29), frizz-care Hair sub-line (7/30), and now today it's "last chance" on that same Cream Glide Lip Liner GWP from a few days back — so this is the sunset email on a gift I've already been pitched once. The creative itself is genuinely tight: TikTok-style vertical video stills, a clean "cop your new fave lip combos" section pairing the liner with three different lip products for three different finishes (glossy, buttery, stain) is smart merchandising and reads like an actual makeup artist walked me through it.
- But I'm numb to it at this point. Glossier's Lip Line + Balm Dotcom send scored a 9 from me because it felt like a fresh idea dressed as a tutorial even though the GWP underneath was stale — e.l.f. doesn't get that same grace because I can see the seams: same gift, same code (LIPPIE), just a new "last chance" wrapper slapped on five days later. Drunk Elephant's "Lippe Lock" send was tighter and newer-feeling with its before/after framing, and even Ulta's generic 20%-off blast beat this on offer clarity because a flat discount is easier to process than a gift-with-purchase mental math problem.
- I'd probably open it out of habit at this point since e.l.f. has trained me to expect something in my inbox daily, but I'm not clicking — I already made my decision on this gift days ago, and "last chance" this many emails deep doesn't feel urgent, it feels like the tail end of a drip campaign. Not forwarding.

## What's working

- The three lip-combo pairings (liner + oil, liner + balm, liner + tint) are genuinely useful shade/product logic, not just "buy this."
- TikTok-native video stills give it a real, current feel instead of static product-on-white shots.
- Layout is clean and scannable — no overlapping text, no broken images, easy thumb-scroll.
- "Top Picks for You" grid at least gestures at personalization even if the products (primer, hydration cream, dewy spray) have nothing to do with lips.

## What's weak

- This is the same lippie GWP I've already seen pushed once this week — "last chance" on day five of the same offer reads as inbox fatigue, not urgency.
- No loyalty/points math anywhere — as a Rouge/Diamond points chaser, a straight GWP with zero tier language just isn't how I evaluate a deal.
- Generic "SHOP NOW" on every single CTA — six identical buttons with zero differentiation from a brand that clearly has better copywriters (see the section headers).
- No shade swatches or finish preview on the liner itself, which is the one thing I'd actually want before adding a lip product to cart.
- Bottom nav rail (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) is pure filler that pushes the email length out for no real reason on a "last chance" send that should feel urgent and short.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the "last chance" framing on a gift you've already emailed twice this week — either make it genuinely time-boxed with a countdown/date, or fold it into a fresh hook instead of recycling urgency language that's lost its teeth.
- 2. Add a shade strip under the Cream Glide Lip Liner so I can see the actual color range before I add-to-cart — that's the missing click trigger.
- 3. Differentiate the CTA copy per section ("Shop the Glossy Combo," "Shop the Stain Combo") instead of six identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 4. Cut the six-category nav stack at the bottom — it's dead weight on a promo-focused send.
- 5. Tie a points or Beauty Squad callout into the gift mechanic if you want repeat purchasers like me to feel the loyalty math, not just a generic checkout code.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your Lippie Gift disappears at midnight 💄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: free liner with your lip order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code math — just add your fave lippie and we'll drop the gift in`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3 lip combos, 1 free Cream Glide Liner, ends tonight`
