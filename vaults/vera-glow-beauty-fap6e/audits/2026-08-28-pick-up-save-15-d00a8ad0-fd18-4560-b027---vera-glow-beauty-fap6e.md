# ✨ Pick Up & Save 15%
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, it's Sally again, and this time it's actually a different mechanic — not COLORfest confetti-dot sitewide %-off, but a BOPIS-specific "15% off Buy Online, Pick Up in Store" flash sale. That's a genuinely new lever after ten-plus sends of the same sitewide-code beat, so credit where due. But the offer itself does nothing for me — I don't touch professional hair color or clipper guards, so a pickup-only discount on a category I don't shop is just noise with a fresh coat of paint.
- The confetti-dot border is back (same visual DNA as every COLORfest send since mid-July), the hero circle is clean with real hierarchy — Flash Sale, 15%, ends 8/31, one black CTA — so at least the offer itself reads instantly. But there's nothing here for me personally: no points balance, no Rewards tier callout beyond a generic "shop twice, earn $5" blurb buried below the fold, no product for someone who isn't buying hair color or textured-hair product.
- I'm marking this as read, not clicking. It's not offensive, it's just not for me — I'd sooner forward this to my mom who actually box-dyes her hair than open the app myself.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: 15%, BOPIS, no minimum, ends 8/31 — I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- Single black "Shop now" CTA button is easy to tap and stands out against the pink.
- The "Shop Your Beauty Favorites" grid at least tries to segment by category (Hair Color, Hair Care, Nails, Styling Tools, Cosmetics & Skincare) instead of just blasting one hero product.
- Klarna financing block and delivery-method icons (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2-Hour Delivery, BOPIS) are useful trust/logistics signals if I were actually a Sally shopper.

## What's weak

- BOPIS-only 15% off is irrelevant to me — I'm not driving to a Sally Beauty Supply store, I'm cross-shopping Sephora Rouge events and Ulta 21 Days online.
- Zero personalization: no "you have X points," no tier name, no skin-tone or hair-type targeting — total stranger treatment, same as every prior Sally send in my history.
- The Rewards banner ("Shop Twice in August, Earn a $5 Reward in September") is a genuinely interesting mechanic buried two-thirds down the email instead of up top where it'd actually pull loyalty members like me.
- Product grid photos are generic stock circles (a clipper, a bottle, gel polish) — no swatches, no models, nothing demographic-matching a 29-year-old like Sephora's Halo Glow email did with an actual on-skin swatch shot.
- This is Sally's fifth-plus distinct mechanic in six weeks and I still have no idea what their actual hero product or launch calendar is — no beauty-event tie-in, no "new drop" energy like ILIA's Crown Affair collab.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Rewards $5-in-September mechanic above the fold or into the hero — that's the one thing here with real loyalty stickiness, and it's currently an afterthought below six product circles.
- 2. Swap "Buy Online, Pick Up in Store" framing for something that also covers ship-to-home shoppers, or split the send by segment — BOPIS-only excludes anyone who isn't near a store, which is most of my inbox-scanning demographic.
- 3. Add real product photography with texture/swatch shots (like e.l.f.'s cheek-swatch hero) instead of generic stock circles — right now nothing in the grid makes me want to click through.
- 4. Lean into the loyalty math — show my actual points balance or reward progress the way Ulta at least attempts with its "Marisol" personalization, even if buggy.
