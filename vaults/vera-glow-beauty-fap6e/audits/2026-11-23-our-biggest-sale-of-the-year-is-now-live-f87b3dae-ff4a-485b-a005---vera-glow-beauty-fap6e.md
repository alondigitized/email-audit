# Our biggest sale of the year is now live!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again, and this is the first send in a week that actually gives me a tier ladder, so I'm paying attention. "BLACK FRIDAY IS ON!" in red on black, then "30%-50% OFF SITEWIDE" in giant type. I read the offer in about half a second. Last week's "Hours left for 30% off sitewide" and "30% off early access is almost up" sends were the same flat 30% repeated. Now there's a range, plus a stackable member code, so I have something to calculate.
- I do points math for a living, so the Skechers Plus "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING, code BF2026" strip got my attention. The part that stops me is "30%-50% sitewide" next to the four tiles: Shoes 30%, Boots/Slippers/Outdoor 40%, Apparel 50%, Sale Styles up to 50%. That's a ladder, but the headline range says "sitewide" and the fine print says "select" styles. Ulta does this too, though Ulta at least puts "Terms & excl. apply" in its subject line. I'm not a Skechers shopper in the first place. I'd click once to see how the stack works, and I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The black hero with red "30%-50% OFF" gives me the number instantly, and "SHOP THE SALE" is a clear button.
- The Skechers Plus strip, "EXTRA 15% OFF EVERYTHING" with code BF2026, is the loyalty stacking I look for. It's the closest thing here to Sephora's Beauty Insider event math.
- The four category tiles each carry their own percentage badge, so I can see which tier applies to which category without reading fine print.
- The "valid online only thru 11/29" deadline is real and gives a credible end date.
- The "Doorbuster Deals starting at $49.99" block gives a concrete price anchor.

## What's weak

- "Sitewide" in the hero conflicts with "select" in the fine print and with the 30% floor on the tiles. The ladder is clear and the headline over it isn't.
- The doorbuster product row is tiny, with small grey captions and no prices on individual shoes. I can't tell which ones are $49.99.
- The "Join Skechers Plus and Earn 1000 Bonus Points (That's a $5 reward!)" bar at the top is easy to miss. I like that it states the dollar value, but nothing else in the email tells me how points interact with the sale.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny, and it carries the exclusions that matter.
- It's a generic blast with no personalization and nothing for my category. The women's tile is only one of four, with no sizing or fit info.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "sitewide" with the honest claim, "Up to 50% off", and put the stack math in the hero, e.g. "30-50% off + extra 15% with code BF2026."
- 2. Add the price and the discount to each product in the doorbuster row, enlarge the images, and link each one to its product page.
- 3. Move the Plus member strip above the category tiles, and show a worked example of the stack.
- 4. Add a live countdown to 11/29, or an "ends Sunday" flag, so the deadline is more than fine print.
- 5. Surface the key exclusions in one visible line above the fold instead of only in the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30–50% off + extra 15% for Plus members`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off, thru 11/29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack code BF2026 for an extra 15% off. Doorbusters from $49.99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes 30%, boots 40%, apparel 50%. Online only through 11/29.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML head is structurally sound, with a table-based layout, MSO conditionals and responsive CSS. The QA data flags two `http://` images missing alt text, one of them a third-party tracking pixel. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the relay didn't capture those headers.

Only the head and the start of the body were available (the HTML is truncated at `<body…`). Findings below are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 43 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixel:** `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026` loads over plain HTTP. The URL carries a subscriber-level identifier (`mi_u`) in cleartext. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will suppress the pixel and lose open and personalization data.
- **Duplicate annotation URLs:** both JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries (positions 1 and 2) use the same destination and the same `utm_term=googleannotation1`. Clicks from the two cards can't be told apart.
- **Mixed image domains:** the `<link rel="icon">` points to `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`, while the other assets use `image.emails.skechers.com`. This is a different subdomain and also non-HTTPS. Most clients ignore `<link rel="icon">` in email, so impact is low.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (2 images, QA-confirmed):**
  - `o.gif` (the ink1000.com pixel)
  - `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png`
  
  The pixel should have `alt=""` at minimum. The PNG's role isn't visible in the truncated source. If it is content, it needs descriptive alt text.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** `http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` may be blocked or rewritten by clients. The other assets in the JSON-LD use `https://`, so this host supports TLS.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" ...>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Zoom restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Focus outline removed:** the CSS rule `a { outline: none; }` removes the default focus indicator for keyboard and assistive navigation.
- **Text size adjust disabled:** `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }` blocks user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Non-standard doctype:** XHTML 1.0 Transitional is fine for email, and the Outlook (MSO) `PixelsPerInch` block is present. The viewport meta and `div[style*="margin: 16px 0"]` fixes are present too.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=8108776175` value is populated. The body was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed and shouldn't be read as a sender failure. Check the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope looks aligned (`no-reply@emails.skechers.com`, with images on `image.emails.skechers.com`), but that is not proof of authentication.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source, so unverified.
- **Reply-to:** the `no-reply@` sender address is a mild deliverability and engagement concern. It isn't a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Annotation URL:** `https://www.skechers.com/black-friday-cyber-deals/?srule=top-sellers&src=2026Q4BLACKFRIDAY&utm_source=trig&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=PRO_US_NA_U_BLACKFRI_FULL_EN_11232026&utm_content=annotation&utm_term=googleannotation1`
- **UTM set:** it is complete (source, medium, campaign, content, term). The campaign name matches the `mi_ecmp` value in the pixel URL, so the email and ESP campaign IDs are consistent.
- **Landing page:** `/black-friday-cyber-deals/` aligns with the "biggest sale of the year" subject line.
- **Unverified:** body link UTMs and destinations could not be checked because of the skipped probes and truncation.
- **Annotation dates:** the JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` runs from `2026-11-23T00:11:00Z` to `2026-11-29T23:11:59Z`. The odd minute values (`:11:00`, `:11:59`) may be unintended, but the source doesn't say.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the `ink1000.com` pixel and the `dde00662-…png` image over `https://`. Add `alt=""` to the pixel and meaningful alt text to the PNG.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, not the relay copy. Run a full SPF/DKIM/DMARC check on the origin message.
3. Give the two JSON-LD `PromotionCard` entries distinct `utm_term` or `utm_content` values so clicks can be attributed.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, remove `maximum-scale=1`, and restore focus styles. Replace `a { outline: none; }` with a visible `:focus` style, and avoid `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none`.
5. Re-run the QA with the full HTML. Check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address and the destinations of the 43 tracking links, using a redirect-aware probe.
6. Confirm the intended `DiscountOffer` start and end times.
