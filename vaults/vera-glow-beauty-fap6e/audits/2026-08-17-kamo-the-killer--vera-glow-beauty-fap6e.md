# Kamo the Killer
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "RESULTS LIKE: DAMN" — that clock-face made of foundation shade dots is genuinely the best visual DE has run all week, way more interesting than the retinol-superhero graphics from "The secret weapon" (8/9) or the SheerID gate on "20% off is better than ever!" (8/15). This is the full-body follow-through on "Introducing Kamo Drops!" (8/11, still their high score at 7/10) and "Firmer skin?" (8/13), and honestly the shade-clock plus the real before/after on mature, textured skin is the most substantive proof-of-product they've shown in this whole cadence.
- But — 25 shades is a genuinely great stat and it's buried two-thirds down the email under a tiny "FIND YOUR SHADE" link, after three separate "results" blocks I already saw in "Firmer skin?" two days ago. And I'm doing points math on every send and there's not one mention of Allure Beauty Insider points, a birthday multiplier, nothing — meanwhile Sephora's App Fest (8/17, 8/10) leads with points multipliers every single time.
- I'd click through to find my shade — the clock hook got me — but I wouldn't forward this. It's a nice launch email, not a "you have to see this" one.

## What's working

- The shade-dot clock is a genuinely original hero image, not another stock retinol-hero shot.
- Real before/after on a woman with visible texture and hyperpigmentation, not airbrushed — that's the ingredient-transparency energy e.l.f. SKIN nailed with "PLZ Clarify Facial Oil" and ILIA's musician shoots totally lack.
- Clinical stats (100% radiance, 91% texture) are specific numbers, not vague "glowing skin" copy.
- Two demographic ranges shown (fair and deep) reads more inclusive than most of what DE's sent this week.
- Free deluxe sample + free travel bag with subscription orders is a real, concrete offer, clearly stated up top in the banner.

## What's weak

- 25 shades is the actual news here and it's crammed into a tiny corner near the bottom — should be the hero stat, not an afterthought.
- Zero loyalty/points language — no Allure Beauty Insider mention at all, which is a miss compared to how hard Sephora leans into VIB/Rouge tiering in App Fest.
- This is the third Kamo email in a week (8/11 launch, 8/13 "Firmer skin?", now this) and the "results" framing is starting to repeat — I'm fatigued on the clinical-stat block, I've basically seen this slide twice already.
- Fine print at the bottom is a maze — two separate offers (free sample vs. free bag) with different order minimums ($50 vs $110) and different date windows, that's Ulta-level "read the terms" energy, not the honest simplicity I want from a launch email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "25 SHADES?!" callout up near the hero — that's the actual differentiator versus every other foundation drop, don't bury it under three stat blocks.
- 2. Add an Allure Beauty Insider points line ("earn 2x points on Kamo through 8/21") — I'm cross-shopping Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, every other brand in my inbox leads with loyalty math and DE is leaving it on the table.
- 3. Consolidate the two overlapping offers (free sample $50+ / free bag $110+) into one clear tier callout instead of two footnote blocks with different dates.
- 4. Since this is Kamo email #3, add a "last chance to get your free shade card" urgency angle instead of repeating the same clinical stats from 8/13.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shade is somewhere on this clock`
