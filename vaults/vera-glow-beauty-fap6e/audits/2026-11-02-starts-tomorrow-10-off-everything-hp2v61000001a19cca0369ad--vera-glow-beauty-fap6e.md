# STARTS TOMORROW: 10% off EVERYTHING 😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the Sephora Savings Event email I've been waiting for, and it finally reads like an event instead of the sixth Sephora Collection 30% reminder in three days. Two big badges sit side by side: Insider **10% OFF** (11/3–11/9) and Sephora Collection **30% OFF** (10/30–11/9). The date windows are right there, so I can do my points math in two seconds. Rouge tier gets 10% at the same rate as everyone else, though, and I notice that.
- The rest of the email is a long scroll. There's a category grid, a "Beauty on Demand" strip, a faves row, Jumbo Sizes, a foundation module and gift sets. It's a catalog. The ALONSALE code, the Kohl's code and the barcodes at the bottom all work for a real shopper, but the code instructions are in small type under the badges.
- I'd open this, click once into Makeup, and forward it to my group chat as "10% starts Tuesday, don't buy before then." It's strong, but I'm not stunned by it.

## What's working

- The twin 10% and 30% badges are the first thing my eye lands on, and the dates under each one make the stacking window obvious.
- "STARTS TOMORROW" in gold gives me real urgency, and it's honest because the event does start 11/3.
- "BUILD YOUR BASKET" is one clear button right under the offer.
- The "Free gift with purchase, drops 11/3 at 11AM PT" strip is a hook I'd actually set a reminder for.
- The barcodes at the bottom for Sephora and Kohl's mean I can use the offer in-store with no friction.
- The "Beauty on Demand" strip (Same-Day, Pickup, Free Shipping) tells me how I'll get my haul before Christmas.

## What's weak

- The "Insider" badge doesn't separate Rouge from Insider or VIB. I want to see my tier called out, and "Insider" alone feels generic.
- The code details ("Mention at checkout," ALONSALE, SCSAVE) sit in tiny type under the badges. The most important how-to-redeem information is the hardest to read on my phone.
- The page is very long, and the category tiles (Makeup, Skincare, Hair, Fragrance) are generic. Nothing is personalized to my shade, skin type or past purchases.
- "Save on these faves" shows Armani, YSL and Tom Ford fragrance next to Eadem and amika, but Rare Beauty and similar brands (the ones I'd actually restock) are missing. Many of the products have no sale price, so I can't see the savings.
- The footer fine print and the stack of barcodes are a wall of legalese, and it feels like the Ulta-style coupon clutter I normally criticize.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the tier name in the badge (e.g. "ROUGE: 10% OFF") and add a one-line stacking explainer: "10% off + 30% off Sephora Collection, same cart."
- 2. Pull the redemption codes up into a bigger, tappable strip, and add a "copy code" button.
- 3. Show the sale price on every product tile, not just a couple.
- 4. Cut the page to the badges, the gift-with-purchase, and one personalized product row based on my browsing and purchase history.
- 5. Move the barcodes to a "Show in store" button so the bottom of the email isn't a legal wall.

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click tracking:** Visible links use redirect wrappers, for example `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a19cca0369ad11356e9666b528/939fd687…?__dU__=…`. The domain is first-party and consistent with the open pixel.
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 links and skipped 40. No probe failures are reported, but the 40 skipped links are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF…' style="display:none; …mso-hide: all"/>` has no `alt`, `width` or `height`. This is the source of the QA "missing alt" warning. It is hidden, so the screen-reader impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Pixel placement:** The pixel sits after the preheader inside the same `<td>`, so it fires on any HTML render.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS (inferred, not rendered):** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…}<![endif]-->` is placed inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid inside CSS rules. In standards-based parsers the `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size` and `<![endif]--> .mobileHeaderTierRouge` selectors are likely invalid. That would drop the `.mso-size` rule and the `.mobileHeaderTierRouge` rule that follows it.
- **Fixed width:** The wrapper is `width="700"` with `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media` rules don't override it. If the truncated remainder doesn't either, mobile clients will scroll horizontally. The inner tables use `max-width:700px`, which is correct.
- **Zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it (accessibility).
- **Language:** `<html>` has no `lang` attribute.
- **Robots meta:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` uses an invalid token. The valid value is `noindex`. It has no effect in email clients.
- **Structured data:** The JSON-LD block has `"@type":"DiscountOffer","discountCode":""` (empty value) and `"@type":"PromotionCard"`, which isn't a valid Gmail promotion type. Gmail will ignore or reject the markup, so the block is dead weight.
- **Preheader:** Nested `display:none` divs followed by `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding are implemented correctly.

No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `__dU__` and `/O/` tokens are resolved tracking IDs. The truncated body can't be checked.
