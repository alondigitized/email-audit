# Spooky season savings
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- "Spooky season savings" promised Halloween and I got a curly-haired model with red-tipped hair and a headline about "October's treats, no tricks." It's a pretty hero, but it's a mood board with a "Shop now" button. The hero has no number on it. I had to scroll past a huge photo before I found any actual deals, and those were three small tiles: 20% off men's grooming, BOGO 50% off lashes, and 35% off select BaByliss Pro Nano Titanium flat irons.
- This is Sally's tenth send in my logs, and it's a reset from the 9/30 "Last chance" and 9/28 "Appiversary" emails that were all about sitewide 25% off with a code. Those at least gave me something to calculate. Here nothing is sitewide, nothing has a code, and the deals are scattered across categories. Men's beard balm isn't me. The lashes and the flat iron are in range, but I'm not driven to act on them. I'd skim it, maybe tap the flat iron, and not forward it.

## What's working

- The red-tipped curls hero is on-theme for Halloween without being cheesy. It's a diverse, expressive model that feels fresh.
- The three offer tiles have large red percentages that are easy to scan, with a clear "Shop now" under each.
- The BOGO 50% off lashes is the one deal I'd actually do math on. Press-on lashes in a 2-for-1 is a real stock-up trigger.
- The Sally Rewards bar at the top (get $5 off $25 when you sign up) is a cheap hook for new members.
- Klarna, 2-hour delivery and the BOPIS (buy online, pick up in-store) icons make the logistics clear.

## What's weak

- The hero is the entire first screen and has no offer. "Discover can't-miss beauty deals all month long" is vague, and the deals sit below the fold.
- Nothing says "spooky" beyond the subject line. There are no Halloween products, costume makeup or a deadline, so the subject over-promises.
- The offers are random: men's beard balm, lashes, a flat iron. The email has no single story and no hero product.
- The rewards bar is a "sign up" pitch for a program I'm already in. There's no points balance, tier or member pricing, so I get no loyalty math. Sephora's Insider email today at least had a 2X/5X points hook.
- The fine print is a wall of text. It lists the $5 off $25 terms, an offer end date of 10/31 and exclusions, but none of that is visible near the tiles.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the strongest offer in the hero. For example, "BOGO 50% off lashes" over the red-curls image, with a real Halloween angle: "Press-on drama for your costume."
- 2. Show the deadline and any code in the hero. "All month long" has no urgency. A weekend flash or a date range would give me a reason to click today.
- 3. Make the offers one theme, such as a Halloween glam grid (lashes, nail art, hair color) instead of beard balm and irons.
- 4. Add loyalty math, like "Rewards members get 2X points on these." Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty makes it easy to see what the member price is. This email doesn't.
- 5. Trim the legal copy or move the key terms (dates, exclusions) next to the offers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Halloween glam: lashes BOGO 50% off 🎃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Press-on lashes BOGO, 35% off flat irons, 20% off men's grooming. Through 10/31.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Costume-ready hair and lashes, plus Rewards members get $5 off $25.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email's markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with responsive CSS. Automated checks show a 57% pass rate with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The warnings are missing alt text on tracking pixels, one non-HTTPS image, and compliance headers that weren't captured. The HTML was truncated, so only the head and CSS were visible. Body-level findings come from the QA data, not from the full source.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 51 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- Third-party pixels are embedded: `pxl.mon-trk.com/987bfe61-.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`, `t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel`, and `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`.
- `t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]...` contains an unresolved `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder, so the macro was never substituted. The pixel will likely not register impressions, and the URL may be cached.
- The `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). It is a per-recipient identifier exposed in a third-party tracking URL.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Four images lack alt text, per QA:
  - one with an empty `src`
  - `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU`
  - `impression_pixel`
  - `UCMController`

  The last three are tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Screen readers may otherwise announce the filename or URL.
- The image with an empty `src` and no alt text is a likely broken or placeholder `<img>`. It may trigger a broken-image icon or a request to the base URL.
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...` loads over plain HTTP. Gmail's image proxy typically upgrades it, but other clients may block it as mixed content.
- The CSS font stack in `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible source. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless.
- The Open Sans `@font-face` rules point to `fonts.gstatic.com` v17 URLs. Outlook and some other clients ignore web fonts, so those clients fall back.
- `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, unlike the other meta tags in this XHTML doctype. This is a minor validity issue.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The only unresolved token is `[CACHEBUSTER]` in the VisualIQ pixel URL (see section 2). No other unrendered merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) were identified in the visible source.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- The QA check did not find a `List-Unsubscribe` header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- The QA check did not find `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). One-click unsubscribe support is unverified for the same reason.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA data does not show failures.
- The visible HTML did not include the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed because the source was truncated.
- The sender is `sallybeauty@em.sallybeauty.com`, a subdomain aligned with the brand domain. This is a positive signal.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All 51 tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing page destinations could not be verified.
- No UTM evidence was present in the truncated source or QA output. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Substitute the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro in the VisualIQ pixel URL, or remove the pixel if it isn't in use.
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels and fix or remove the `<img>` with the empty `src`.
3. Serve the Dotomi pixel over HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`).
4. Check whether the Salesforce Contact ID in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` URL should be exposed to a third-party domain.
5. Fix the relay capture so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be verified. Alternatively, use a raw `.eml` for header audits.
