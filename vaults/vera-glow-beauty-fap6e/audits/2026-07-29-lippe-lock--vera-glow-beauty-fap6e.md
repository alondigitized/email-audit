## Executive summary

- Okay, "Lippe Lock" is DE tying a National Lipstick Day tie-in to their Lippe Balm/Lippe Mask duo, and honestly this is a clean, tight little send — before-and-after lip care framed as "pre-game" and "after-party," which is a cute enough hook that I actually get the concept in three seconds. The hero shot of the model with her finger on her lip is doing a lot of work, it's a good close-up, product application is obvious, and the yellow-cap product styling is very recognizably DE at this point. But there's zero personalization, zero loyalty math, and the top banner is honestly the most interesting offer in the whole email (free 5-piece set on $110+) buried above a header that isn't even the headline focus.
- Compared to the run of subject lines I've been getting from them lately — "SMS SOS," "Feeling sticky?," "Golden hour every hour" — "Lippe Lock" keeps the alliteration streak going but it's vaguer than those, I genuinely didn't know this was a lip-specific send until I opened it. This is a step down from that four-send hot streak, similar territory to "Our top shelf" going catalog-mode on me, just smaller scale here.
- Would I click? Probably, yeah — I don't own the Lippe Balm and lip prep before a lipstick day is a real behavior for me. Would I forward it? No, this is a "shop now for me" email, not a "look at this" email.

- Each product gets its own clean product shot, name, one-line benefit, and a SHOP NOW link — no hunting for what to click.
- The top black banner offer (free 5-piece gift on $110+, deluxe sample on $50+ subscription) is a real, specific, dollar-threshold offer — that's the kind of concrete math I actually want to see.

## What's weak

- The $110 free-gift threshold is buried in a tiny banner above the actual headline — I almost skipped past it because "PLUMP START" reads like the real subject and the banner reads like boilerplate.
- Nothing here tells me if I'm Rouge, Insider, or nothing to DE — no points, no tier perk, no "as a member" language at all.
- Two products, one narrative beat — this feels like a small module pulled out of a bigger campaign rather than a full send, similar to how e.l.f. SKIN keeps doing single-ingredient stories that feel thin on their own.
- The subject "Lippe Lock" doesn't tell me it's about lips or lipstick day at all until I'm already in the email — Sephora's "up to 50% off this National Lip Day" subject just says the actual holiday and the actual discount, way less guesswork.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the $110 free-gift offer out of the top banner and make it part of the hero headline — that's a real incentive and it's currently working as fine print.
- 2. Put "National Lipstick Day" or "lips" in the subject line itself so I know what I'm opening before I open it.
- 3. Add any loyalty signal — even a generic "Rouge & Insider members" nod would beat the current nothing.
- 4. Since it's only two products, consider bundling them into one "kit" CTA with a combined price/savings instead of two separate SHOP NOWs — feels more decisive.
