# Yes, please! Grab up to 50% off.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly the "just... keeps going" email I flagged in my own history from this same subject line — "SURPRISE! 50% OFF Select Beauty. An EXTRA day, just for you" is a genuinely good hook up top, but then I scroll and scroll and scroll through a full catalog dump: Chris McMillan shampoo, Kosas, Biodance, Crown Affair, DECOCOL, Make Up For Ever, Innbeauty Project, Urban Decay, OleHenriksen, rhode, YSL, Marc Jacobs. That's eleven separate product callouts before I even hit the footer.
- The one thing that actually works for my points math: 4X Points on fragrance through 9/23 stacked in with the NEWSCENTS code. That's real currency for a Rouge member like me. But it's buried two-thirds down, after I've already scrolled past a shampoo I don't care about and six gift-set tiles that all look the same size and weight — nothing tells my eye where to actually click first.
- Compare this to Glossier's "Boy Brow just won an award" send I read on 9/16 — one product, one message, done in three seconds. Sephora keeps doing the opposite: cram everything in and hope something lands. I'm not forwarding this to anyone. I'll skim for the 50% off styles, maybe screenshot the rhode Glazing Mask stack since I've been curious about that, then archive.

## What's working

- The "SURPRISE! 50% OFF" hero banner with the extra-day framing is a legit urgency hook and reads as Sephora actually giving me something, not just noise.
- 4X Points on fragrance through 9/23 is real loyalty math I can act on — Rouge members live for stacking points like this.
- The "CLEAN AT SEPHORA" and "NEW · LIMITED EDITION" / "ONLY AT SEPHORA" badges on individual products are useful shorthand for a shopper like me who filters by exclusivity.
- rhode getting its own mini-module with a clear "step 1 / step 2" mask + moisturizer visual is a nice change of pace from the flat product-tile grid everywhere else.

## What's weak

- This is a full-catalog dump — eleven+ product callouts (shampoo, two gift sets, hair tool set, cologne, lip balm, holiday set, powder spray, moisturizer trio) with zero hierarchy telling me which one is actually the 50%-off hero.
- The 50% off offer at the very top never gets tied back to any specific product below — I have no idea which of these eleven things is actually discounted.
- Way too long for mobile — I'm scrolling forever before I even get to the fragrance points offer, which should be near the top given how much Sephora leans on points messaging with me.
- "New beauty you'll want on repeat" as the section header is generic filler copy, not a real hook — Glossier's award-callout headline on 9/16 did more work in fewer words.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one clear hero: either lead with the 50%-off product lineup explicitly tagged as included, or lead with the fragrance 4X Points + NEWSCENTS combo — pick one, don't bury both under a shampoo nobody asked to see first.
- 2. Move the 4X Points/fragrance block up near the hero — that's the section most relevant to my Rouge points math and it's currently four scrolls deep.
- 3. Tag which of the eleven products in the grid are actually part of the "50% off select beauty" — right now the banner and the grid feel disconnected.
- 4. Trim the product grid to 4-5 items max, or split into a second email — this read like three emails stapled together.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your 50% off day just got extended`
- **Subject Alt B:** `4X Points on fragrance + 50% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack 4X Points on new fragrance through 9/23 — plus an extra day of 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge members: your points multiplier and markdown window, together.`
