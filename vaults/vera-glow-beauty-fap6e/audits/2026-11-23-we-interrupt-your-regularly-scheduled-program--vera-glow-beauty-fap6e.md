**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Glossier send in six days, and it's the first one in a while where I didn't roll my eyes at the hero. The picks are in the copy, which helps. Lash Slick, After Baume and the I ❤ NY Balm Dotcom each get a "Must-buy" slot with a first-person blurb from the "resident Copywriter." Compare that to 11/21's hoodie email ("We *never* do this..."), which was merch for someone who came for skincare. It's still the same 25% sale I've seen since 11/19, though, so there's nothing new here.
- The 25% off circle on the hero and the "30% off $100+ orders" line are clear. But I'm doing the math and the email isn't helping me. Where's the stacking story with my points? Sephora's Cyber Week email told me what to buy today. This one just repeats "Get 25% off" three times on identical buttons. I'd probably click on Lash Slick, because I already like it. I wouldn't forward it, though. I'd mark it read and wait for the next one.

## What's working

- The "25% OFF EVERYTHING*" circle on the hero is the first thing my eye lands on, and the offer is unmistakable.
- The "30% off $100+ orders" tier gives me a real threshold to build a cart around.
- Three numbered "Must-buy" picks with a personal-voice blurb feel like a friend's recs, not a catalog dump. That's better than the 11/21 hoodie send.
- Lash Slick shown close-up on a real eye is a strong product shot and fits what I actually buy.
- The free Crème de You with $100+ is a good gift-with-purchase hook, and it echoes the 11/21 "A gift for you" send.

## What's weak

- The "Lock in 25% off new subscriptions for a limited tim..." banner is cut off at the right edge, and the "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" marquee strip is also clipped. The body copy next to the Lash Slick and Balm Dotcom images runs off the edge too ("I swipe o…", "dryness…"). Those look like render bugs.
- "Limited time" has no date or countdown. Ulta at least gives me a deadline ladder, so I know when to buy.
- Every CTA says "Get 25% off" or "Shop now," and the buttons are all the same blue. There are no prices and no review stars.
- There's nothing for a loyalty member. No points and no early access, so Glossier treats me like a stranger.
- It's the sixth send in six days, and the discount hasn't changed since 11/19. That's fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the clipped copy and banners so every blurb and the top bar read in full.
- 2. Add a real deadline, like "Ends Monday 11:59pm PT," and a countdown timer.
- 3. Put prices and star ratings under each Must-buy. Glossier's own 11/22 "Loved by you" send had reviews, so use those.
- 4. Make the CTA text specific: "Shop Lash Slick," "Shop After Baume."
- 5. Pause the daily cadence, or give returning buyers a reason to open (early access, a points multiplier).

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The Stripo-built, Klaviyo-tracked email (`ctrk.klclick.com`) has no confirmed breakage in the visible source. The QA flags are missing alt text on 6 images and three headers the relay didn't capture. The HTML is truncated and 9 of 12 material links were not probed, so link and UTM coverage is incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage gap:** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 9, so link health is only partly verified. No broken links were reported among those probed.
- **Stray `href` on a `<span>`:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M851V34EG2W9B63QZXZZP0XY_0"></span>` sits right after `<body>`. `href` and `rel="stylesheet"` have no effect on a `<span>`, so it is invalid markup and does nothing. It is probably a Klaviyo/ESP injection artifact. It is harmless but worth removing.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M851V34EG2W9B63QZXZZP0XY` is the open-tracking image and has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` to it.
- **Title mismatch:** `<title>` reads "…regularly scheduled promo-filled inbox ⭐". The subject line reads "…regularly scheduled program ⭐". This is low severity and only affects web-view and some clients.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 5 content images** (all hosted at `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_…`):
  - `heroimage_1_4.jpg`
  - `group_3996_1.jpg` (appears twice, under two different CABINET GUIDs)
  - `group_3996.jpg`
  - `frame_3984.jpg`
- **Effect of the missing alt text:** If images are blocked, those slots render empty. Screen readers will read the filename or nothing. If any of these images carry the offer or a CTA, the message is lost with images off.
- **Structure and mobile CSS:** The doctype, `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals and the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block are all present and well-formed. No rendering defects are confirmed from the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible HTML. The truncation means the body can't be fully ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** QA says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them. That is unconfirmed, so treat it as unverified rather than a confirmed failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be determined from this capture.
- **Sender:** `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment can't be verified without the headers.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** An unsubscribe link was included in probing per QA, but it isn't visible in the truncated HTML. The physical postal address is also not visible, so neither could be confirmed in the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The truncated HTML shows only `klclick.com` redirect wrappers, with no visible `utm_*` parameters. QA gives no landing-page destination data. Continuity can't be assessed. Klaviyo typically appends UTMs at redirect time, so verify the final destination URLs after following the redirects.
