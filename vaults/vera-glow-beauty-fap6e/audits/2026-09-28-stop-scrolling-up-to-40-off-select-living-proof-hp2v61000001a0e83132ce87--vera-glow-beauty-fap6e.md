- The Beauty Insider footer and the "Spot it. Shop it." visual-search block are nice touches, and the individual product cards are shot cleanly — NARS concealer/foundation pairing looks good, the Sol de Janeiro gift set is well-composed. But this is Sephora doing the same "new-launch dump with a clickbait subject" move I called out on 2026-09-25's "New beauty to obsess over 😍" — just with a hair-specific hook this time instead of a generic one.
- I'm not clicking past the first two screens, and I'm definitely not forwarding this — there's nothing here a friend needs to see urgently. Marking as read and moving on.

## What's working

- The NARS concealer + foundation pairing and MAC eyeliner/lip duo are shot with consistent, premium product photography — feels on-brand for Rouge tier.
- "Up to 40% off Hair Faves" when I finally reach it is a real, specific discount with a "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag — a legit deal once you find it.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider/Vib/Rouge) at least nods to loyalty structure, even if it's generic and not personalized to my Rouge status.
- "Sincerely Yours" and "Clean at Sephora" badges give a trust/curation signal that Ulta rarely bothers with.

## What's weak

- The subject line lied to me — "Stop scrolling" for Living Proof, but Living Proof isn't even the first, second, or third thing I see; it's screen seven of nine.
- Zero personalization — no "Vera," no Rouge-tier callout, no skin-tone or hair-type matching despite Sephora having all of that data on me.
- This is the same "everything wall" template I already reviewed twice this week (9/25 "New beauty to obsess over," 9/26 Hispanic Heritage send) — the cadence is exhausting.
- The complimentary Kérastase gift-with-purchase banner at the very top has nothing to do with the subject line's promise, which just compounds the bait-and-switch feeling.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the offer that's in the subject line.** If the hook is Living Proof, the hero image needs to BE the Living Proof deal, not buried after eight other brands. This is the single biggest fix — Drunk Elephant's recent "great skin day" send nailed this by putting the $99-value bundle math right at the top; Sephora should do the same here.
- 2. Cut the product wall down to 3-4 brands max, or split it into a separate "new arrivals" send — nine full screens before the promised deal is just fatigue.
- 3. Personalize the hero for Rouge members — surface a points multiplier or early-access window tied to my tier instead of a generic banner everyone gets.
- 4. Move the "TODAY ONLY" urgency flag up to the subject/preheader so the time pressure actually lands before I've scrolled past it.
