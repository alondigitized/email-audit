# Your fall face awaits
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that it's pretty and I'd scroll it, but I'm not going to do any points math on it. There's no offer, no code, no Glossier Rewards mention and no deadline. Two days ago the "Something festive's right around the corner…" teaser gave me nothing to act on, and this one finally shows product, but still nothing that says why I should buy today. After three sends on the You Discovery Kit GWP (9/22, 9/26, 9/28) and a teaser on 10/2, a fourth Glossier email in 12 days with no incentive reads as filler.
- The creative is good. The berry blush, brown balm and eye pencil edit is exactly my fall rotation, and it's clearly a launch-adjacent seasonal edit. ILIA's "It's Back (with NEW shades)" scored an 8 from me because the hero told me what was new in two seconds. Glossier's hero is a pile of lip liner swatches with "Your fall makeup lineup" next to it. I have to scroll to find out what is actually new, and I still don't know if any of it is.
- Would I click? Maybe one blush shade, since the Storm and Eve swatches are tempting. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and wait for a Sephora Beauty Insider event or an Ulta 21 Days markdown to buy the same category with points attached.

## What's working

- The "WE SUGGEST" rows put the shade name and a plain-English descriptor under each product ("Cloud Paint in Haze, A deep fuchsia"). That's the swatch info I'd otherwise go hunting for.
- The model casting is real. The Cloud Paint row shows a deep skin tone alongside fair and medium, so I can picture the shade on me. Drunk Elephant's 25-shade swatch clock did this better, but this is solid.
- The cheeks, lips and eyes sections are a clean category structure, and each has its own "Shop now" button.
- The copy sounds like Glossier ("Steal from the trees," "a little bit juicy!"). The brand voice is consistent and I trust it.
- The seasonal angle is right for early October. Berry blush and brown lip is what I'm already buying.

## What's weak

- There's no offer at all, no member perk and no free shipping threshold. For someone who cross-shops, that's a catalog, not a campaign. Sephora's "Up to 50% off hair and brow must-haves" had a number in it.
- Every button says "Shop now." Eleven of them are identical, and none says which product or shade it goes to.
- In the "Steal from the trees" block the body copy is cut off at the right edge ("ochre an…"). That's a visible clip right in the middle of the pitch.
- The hero doesn't show a single product I could identify. It's just pencil swatches, so the first screen is vibes and no SKU.
- There's no price, no reviews and no "new" or "back in stock" flag anywhere. I can't tell if this is a launch or a reshuffle of old best sellers.
- The footer's oversized smiley and hand graphics look cropped. It's cute but it eats space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive near the top. That could be a GWP with a spend threshold, a Rewards-style perk, or at least free shipping with a number. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Make the hero a real product. Put the Cloud Paint blush in the hero with a "New fall shades" or "Back in stock" flag, so launch status is obvious.
- 3. Swap "Shop now" for specific CTAs like "Shop Cloud Paint," "Get Balm Dotcom" and "Shop Pencil in Kiln."
- 4. Add star ratings or "best seller" tags under the "WE SUGGEST" products.
- 5. Fix the clipped copy in the eyes block and trim the footer art.
- 6. Space it out. After four sends in 12 days, hold one back or merge the teaser and this edit.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your fall blush + balm, picked for you`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Stripo template with Klaviyo click and open tracking, and the visible source is structurally sound. The real defects are 15 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute. The three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender-side problems.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Stray tracking attribute:** `<body>` begins with `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1_0"></span>`. `rel` and `href` are not valid on `<span>`, so the browser ignores them and the span does nothing. It looks like a leftover from the template.
- **Probe coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 29 were skipped, with no failures reported. The skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Redirect wrapping:** Links appear to route through `ctrk.klclick.com`, and the open pixel is `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1`. This is standard for Klaviyo and not an issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (15 images):** The QA check flags `heroimage_1.jpg`, `frame_3984.jpg`, `group_3973_2.jpg`, `group_3972_2.jpg`, `group_3974_2.jpg` (twice), `group_3996.jpg`, `group_3986_1.jpg`, `group_3987.jpg`, `group_3996_1.jpg`, `group_3973_3.jpg`, `group_3972_3.jpg`, `group_3974_3.jpg` (twice) and the open pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M40PS0FK4188CGS8GYNJTQS1`. All 14 content images are hosted on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email/content/guids/CABINET_e148…`.
- **Impact:** With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the hero and product imagery has no text equivalent. If these images carry the offer or product names, that information is lost.
- **Pixel:** The tracking pixel should have `alt=""` so it is treated as decorative.
- **Positive:** The head includes viewport, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `format-detection` meta tags, MSO conditionals, `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"`. The hidden preheader uses `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **WARN, List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers really are absent, there is no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. The visible links are wrapped in Klaviyo redirects, so UTM parameters and final landing URLs can't be seen in the source. Resolve the redirect targets to check this.
