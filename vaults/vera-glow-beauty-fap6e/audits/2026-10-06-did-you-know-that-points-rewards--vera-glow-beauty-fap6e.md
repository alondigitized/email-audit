# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was that this is a real Rewards Bazaar email wearing a "did you know?" subject line. I've been in Rouge for a while, so I know points = rewards. The subject talks to me like a newbie. The body is for someone who has points to burn, and that's me. The 500-point challenge sits at the top. Below it is a Laura Mercier "Brand Spotlight" with point costs and live inventory counts, like 350, 180 and 12 available now, and then a "750+ point rewards" grid.
- This is Sephora's third send in two days. It follows the 10/5 "APP EARLY ACCESS: Glossier, LANEIGE" email and the 10/6 "2X points… ends tomorrow" email. In both, the points math was the hook. This one drops the math from the subject and puts it all in the body. I'd click through to check the Laura Mercier tiers, and I'd probably screenshot the 1,000-point makeup bag for my points-stacking spreadsheet. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good redemption catalog, but it's long and I have to do the points-to-value math myself.

## What's working

- The "Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points" banner with a "Join the Challenge" button is the one earn opportunity I can act on today.
- Every reward lists its point cost and a live "available now" count. I can see "12 available" on the caviar clutch and "1 available now" on the NYC hotel experience, and that scarcity feels honest.
- The rewards are in tiers, from 750 up to 30,000 points, so I can see what my balance buys. Ulta's Ultamate Rewards emails never show a redemption catalog like this.
- The footer tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) and the "Rewards Bazaar" link remind me where I stand. That's a member-first touch.
- I didn't see any broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject promises an explainer, but the email has none. "Did you know?" reads like onboarding copy, and I've been Rouge for years.
- It's about 7,800px tall, and the Laura Mercier spotlight takes up about a third of it. Sephora buried the "750+ point rewards" grid below it, and that grid is the part most members would care about.
- There are two competing CTAs near the top: "Join the Challenge" and "Shop Now". The challenge is an earn action and the Bazaar is a redeem action, so I don't know which one the email wants.
- I didn't see any dollar value on the rewards. Is 2,500 points for the On-the-Go Faves kit a good deal? I have to look up retail price myself, and Ulta tells me "$ off" directly.
- The "available now" counts are small and gray, which makes them easy to miss, and that's where the urgency is.
- Nothing says when the Bazaar ends, so there's no reason to click today. The 10/6 send had a clear "ends tomorrow".

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the member's own point balance, like "You have X points. Here's what they buy." That's the personalization Rouge members expect. Right now it's one-size-fits-all.
- 2. Move the "750+ point rewards" grid above the Laura Mercier spotlight, and add a "value per point" line on each card.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA. Either make the challenge the hero and move the Bazaar lower, or the reverse.
- 4. Make the inventory counts bigger and add a deadline for the Bazaar. Real scarcity needs a visible end date.
- 5. Cut the length. Five spotlight items plus five grid items is a lot of scrolling on a phone.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your points can get you a Laura Mercier bag set`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rewards Bazaar: 12 left of the caviar clutch`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rewards from 750 points, and the 500-point challenge ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Laura Mercier, Josie Maran and more. See what your points get you.`
