# 20% OFF Ends Tonight 🚨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- Nine. This is Charlotte Tilbury email *nine* in what's now roughly eleven days, and I want to open with the first thing that hit me when this loaded: the countdown timer reads **00 / 00 / 00**. All zeros. "LAST CHANCE, DARLING! SHOP THE MAGIC BEFORE IT GOES!" and then — nothing left on the clock. That's not urgency, that's a broken promise, and it instantly reframes the whole email as either an expired offer someone forgot to suppress, or a timer that broke on render. Either way, the trust just exited the chat.
- Beyond the timer catastrophe, the email is doing everything Charlotte Tilbury does well — the warm rose-gold palette, the "DARLING" voice, beautiful product photography, the 20% OFF headline reading loud and clear. The loyalty exclusive section at the bottom is genuinely the freshest thing in this run, teasing a free Pillow Talk gift for members. That's a beat I haven't seen in the previous eight sends, and it actually gives me a new reason to click — or it would, if I hadn't already clocked the zeroed countdown and questioned whether any of this is still live.
- Glossier closed their Memorial Day 30% sale with a clean "30% off ends soon" send that felt earned because they only sent three emails the whole arc. Charlotte Tilbury has burned "last chance" so many times now that it reads like a brand quirk rather than a real deadline. I'm marking this as read. Not forwarding. Might click purely out of morbid curiosity about the Pillow Talk gift — but not because I believe tonight is actually the cutoff.

## What's working

- **"LAST CHANCE, DARLING" hero copy** — the "DARLING" is still Charlotte Tilbury's strongest differentiator; it feels like a person, not a template
- **"20% OFF EVERYTHING" is visually dominant and unambiguous** — the offer reads in under a second, hierarchy is doing its job
- **"SHOP WITH 20% OFF" CTA button** — specific verb + specific offer, no "Discover the Magic" vagueness; this is how you write a CTA
- **Loyalty exclusive section** — "Unlock & Free / YOU ASKED US FOR IT / Pillow Talk" with a distinct badge is the first genuinely new creative element in several sends; members have a reason to feel singled out
- **Product photography** — warm, aspirational, demographically on-target for me; feels like the brand knows who's reading

## What's weak

- **Countdown timer at 00 / 00 / 00** — the single biggest trust violation in this send; it signals either the offer is already dead or the email is broken, and there's no recovery from that first impression
- **"Last chance" after nine sends** has become noise — Sephora's Spring Sale finale last week used a tiered spend-and-save structure to reframe the close; Charlotte Tilbury just turned the volume up on the same discount without any structural change
- **"EVERYTHING" copy has a credibility gap** — nine days into this campaign I remember the exclusion-clause fine print from the May 16 send; "everything" no longer reads as literal
- **App discovery module below the fold** — I have the app, I'm on the list, this is obvious filler that eats real estate that could expand the Pillow Talk loyalty story

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or fix the countdown timer** — a static "Offer ends at midnight, May 26" beats a broken JavaScript clock every single time; never let the timer hit zero in a live send
- 2. **Lead the final send with the loyalty gift, not the nth 20% banner** — "Free Pillow Talk Gift — loyalty members only, ends tonight" would be a genuinely fresh hook that the Ulta 21-Days crowd doesn't get, and it would reward the segment actually reading email nine
- 3. **Retire "EVERYTHING" from the hero** — specify the top-five categories or hero one product (Magic Cream, Airbrush Flawless) so the offer feels curated rather than desperate
- 4. **Compress the campaign arc for next time** — e.l.f. just ran a free GWP email on send thirteen and it converted because the creative pivoted sharply; three-to-four send arcs with distinct beats (tease → launch → mid → close) beat nine identical "20% off" drums
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Pillow Talk gift ends tonight, Darling`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members only: 20% off + your free gift — midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Loyalty members unlock a free Pillow Talk gift before the 20% sale closes tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off everything + an exclusive member gift — but the clock is real this time.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html

```
Apple Mail and some webmail clients render the `<title>` in the window/tab label. An empty value is a minor but solvable gap — should contain the subject line or campaign name.

**External font `<link>` stripped by Gmail:**
```html
<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css" rel="stylesheet" type="text/css">
```
Gmail strips all `<link>` tags from `<head>`. The conditional comment `<!--[if !mso]><!-->...<![endif]-->` correctly excludes this from Outlook, but Gmail fallback font behavior is uncontrolled. Should be inlined as a `@font-face` block inside `<style>` or the fallback font stack should be explicitly tested.

**Image hosted on Braze EU CDN:**
```
https://cdn.braze.eu/appboy/communication/assets/image_assets/images/...
```

All images depend on Braze's EU CDN. If assets expire or the CDN has an incident, all images break with no fallback. `alt` attributes on images could not be verified due to truncation — this must be confirmed in the full source.

### 5. Compliance

**`noreply@` sender address:**
```
From: "Charlotte Tilbury" <noreply@mail.charlottetilbury.com>
```
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) requires the From address be a valid address recipients can reply to for opt-out requests. A `noreply@` address does not meet this requirement on its own — it's compliant only if a functional reply-to header or a clear unsubscribe mechanism compensates. Cannot verify the reply-to header or footer from the truncated source.

**Unsubscribe link and physical mailing address** — both are required by CAN-SPAM and GDPR and are typically in the footer. The source is truncated before the footer (row-29 is referenced in CSS but not present in the provided HTML). These must be verified in the full source. If either is missing, this is a hard compliance failure.

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) cannot be assessed from HTML — requires raw email headers. Braze sends from `mail.charlottetilbury.com`; DKIM signing and DMARC alignment for that subdomain should be confirmed in mail headers.
