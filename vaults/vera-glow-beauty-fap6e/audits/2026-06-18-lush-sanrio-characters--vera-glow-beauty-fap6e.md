# Lush × Sanrio Characters
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Eighteen. I'm at eighteen Lush emails this month, and I want to say that upfront because the number is the context. And then I opened this one and — okay, fine, I felt something. A Sanrio collab where they didn't just reach for Hello Kitty? They went with Pompompurin, Bad Badtz-Maru, and Hangyodon. The deep-cut trio. That's not a licensing deal where someone checked a box — that's someone at Lush who actually knows the fandom, and that reads immediately.
- "The Sweetest Reunion" as the headline is genuinely clever. The hero shoot is cohesive — bath products in pastel character colorways styled like a picnic tableau, confetti stars everywhere, Sanrio energy without being chaotic. I'd screenshot this for the group chat before I even knew what I was buying. The Lush Club block mid-email is well-placed. The navigation tab says "COLLABORATIONS" right at the top, which is exactly where I'd click as a collab-hunter.
- But there's no offer. No price point, no "shop the drop," no points event tied to the launch. The CTA "Meet New Friends" is charming but doesn't give me a reason to buy today versus bookmarking and forgetting. For how much creative energy went into this email, leaving out any purchase hook feels like a swing and a half-miss. I'd click out of genuine excitement for the collab — but I wouldn't forward it. I'd just drop the hero image in the group chat and say "Lush x Sanrio is happening."

## What's working

- **They picked the right Sanrio characters.** Pompompurin, Bad Badtz-Maru, Hangyodon — not Hello Kitty, not Cinnamoroll. That specificity signals actual fandom fluency and is a real trust signal for the 28-32 demo who grew up with these characters.
- **"The Sweetest Reunion" headline is doing real work.** It plays into Sanrio's friendship IP without being cloying. Someone wrote a concept, not just copy.
- **Hero art direction is a single cohesive vision.** The picnic table setup, the warm pastels, the confetti — it reads as one intentional shoot. Not a product dump, an aesthetic world.
- **Lush Club section is properly positioned.** That green block sits mid-email, visually distinct, with a clear app CTA. Better loyalty placement than Charlotte Tilbury's sticker email from the same audit window, which buried the loyalty angle below two content blocks.
- **"COLLABORATIONS" nav tab at the top** is smart wayfinding. Collab-hunters like me skip straight to it.

## What's weak

- **No offer at all.** No price point, no limited-time discount, no points event tied to the launch. Glossier's NYC party email — which I scored a 9 — embedded a reason to buy *inside* the event announcement. This doesn't. That's a real miss for anyone running points math.
- **Eighteen emails in a month.** The subject line "Lush × Sanrio Characters" should feel like an inbox event. Instead it's fighting seventeen prior sends for credibility. I'm trained to not feel urgency from Lush at this point.
- **"Meet New Friends" doesn't tell me what I'm clicking to.** Verb + noun, yes, but it's conceptual — not a product, not a category, not a collection. ILIA's "This lip color lasts" CTA from this same audit period told me exactly what I was getting into.
- **No character-to-product mapping in the body copy.** I see products in the hero but can't tell which SKU belongs to which character without clicking. "Pompompurin bath bomb" in the copy would make this irresistible rather than intriguing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a launch offer.** Even "Free shipping on the Sanrio collab for 48 hours" or "Lush Club members earn 3x on this drop" turns browsing intent into a timed purchase decision. Right now the only hook is "cute thing exists."
- 2. **Name one hero SKU in the body copy.** The sub-copy should call out the specific product that earns the headline — the thing that makes you go "wait, that's the one." Every high-scoring send I've reviewed in this window (Sally Beauty's "More Beauty, Less Spend," ILIA's lip send) has a hero SKU anchoring the body.
- 3. **Add a character thumbnail strip below the hero.** Sanrio fans buy by character attachment. Show me Pompompurin → bath bomb, Bad Badtz-Maru → bubble bar, Hangyodon → whatever. Visual map = lower barrier to click.
- 4. **Lush Club needs a value hook, not just an app prompt.** "Members earn on this drop" converts better than "join on the app now." Make the membership feel like an exclusive benefit on this specific launch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pompompurin is a bath bomb now. You're welcome.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Sanrio faves just became self-care`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Lush × Sanrio collab is here — shop before it sells out`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pompompurin, Bad Badtz-Maru + more — Lush Club members earn on the drop`

**Click-redirect encoding confirmed** — all links use SFMC-style obfuscated URLs:
```
https://clicks.hello.us.lush.com/f/a/[token]~/AAAHahA~/[base64-path]
```

The `AAAHahA~` segment is the subscriber token; final destination URLs are not inspectable from HTML source alone.
