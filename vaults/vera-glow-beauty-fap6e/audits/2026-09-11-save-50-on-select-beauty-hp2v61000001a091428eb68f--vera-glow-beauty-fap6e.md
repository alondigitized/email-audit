# Save 50% on select beauty ❤️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- Okay, here we go again — "Save 50% on select beauty ❤️" gets me maybe two inches of actual sale before Sephora pivots hard into a full Rewards Bazaar catalog, a "Spot it. Shop it." brand spotlight, and a Holiday Drops teaser, all crammed into one scroll. This is the same pattern I flagged on 9/9 and 9/10 — subject promises one thing, inbox delivers a five-course tasting menu. The 50% off is real (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro, TODAY ONLY) and the points catalog is genuinely useful Rouge-tier content, but they don't belong in the same email and the deal gets buried under a full-blown rewards redemption guide before I even hit "Spot it."
- The points catalog itself is the one section that treats me like an actual Insider — 100-point and 250-point tiers with real SKUs (Kérastase, K18, Versace, Anastasia Beverly Hills) is exactly the tier-math content I want, more than Ulta's generic "20% off one item" coupon game I keep logging. But burying it under/after a time-sensitive 50%-off banner is backwards — if today's the only day for the discount, that should be the whole email, not the appetizer.
- Would I click? Yes, on the 50% off banner specifically, because Kiehl's/Bobbi Brown/Azzaro is a real name-brand play. Would I forward this? No — it's too cluttered to send a friend, I'd just screenshot the top banner and crop out the rest. Marking as read after I scroll past rewards.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero up top is clean — brand names visible (Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro), "TODAY ONLY" badge creates real urgency, not fake evergreen "ends soon" nonsense.
- The Rewards Bazaar point catalog is legit useful — 100pt vs 250pt tiers with actual product photography (Merit serum, K18, Versace Eau Fraiche) is the kind of tier-math content I actually screenshot for myself.
- "You have 5 points" personalization line up top is a small but real signal they know I'm logged in as an Insider.
- Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar at the footer keeps my status visible without me having to dig for it.

## What's weak

- Classic Frankenstein-email problem again — 50% off deal, Rewards Bazaar, "Spot it. Shop it." brand spotlight, AND a Holiday Drops teaser all stacked in one send. This is the exact same complaint I logged on both 9/9 sends and again 9/10.
- The 50% off banner has zero product photography beyond one bottle — compare to e.l.f.'s Glow Reviver send I logged at 6/10, where one product got one clean hero shot and nothing else fighting for attention.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" sits right under the hero — small print killing momentum before I've even scrolled.
- By the time I hit "New from Prada" and "Holiday Drops," I've completely forgotten there was a 50%-off deadline at the top — the urgency gets diluted into oblivion.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: one pure 50%-off deal email (today-only urgency deserves its own inbox moment), and a separate Rewards Bazaar digest for the points catalog. Sephora's already got my attention with "today only" — don't dilute it with rewards browsing.
- 2. Move "Spot it. Shop it." and "Holiday Drops" out entirely — they're not urgent, they don't need to compete with a same-day deadline.
- 3. Repeat the 50%-off CTA again right before the footer, since anyone who scrolled past the rewards catalog has lost the thread.
- 4. Add product photography for at least 2-3 of the 50%-off items in the hero, not just one bottle — ILIA's "Recipe" send (7/10 in my log) proved a stronger visual hero moves me more than a single SKU shot.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off is expiring tonight ❤️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: new 100-point rewards just dropped`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kiehl's, Bobbi Brown, Azzaro — 50% off, today only`
