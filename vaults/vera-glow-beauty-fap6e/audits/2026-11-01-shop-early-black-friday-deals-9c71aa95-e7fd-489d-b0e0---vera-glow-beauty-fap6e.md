# Shop Early Black Friday Deals!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that this is a shampoo email wearing a Black Friday costume. The black hero with a giant "50% off" is bold and I can read it in one second. But it's "Big Bottle Sale," so it's BOGO 50% on 33.8 oz hair care. I'm a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond shopper who lives on makeup, skincare and launches, so a row of four ion shampoo bottles doesn't speak to me at all.
- It also sends mixed signals. The hero says "Early Black Friday Deals," then the red banner says "LAST DAY TO SAVE" on 15% off buy online, pick up in-store. Which deal is it? I did the math: BOGO 50% is 25% off per bottle if I buy two. That's fine but not exciting. Compared with the 10/31 "Last minute details" Halloween send, which had a stunning beauty hero, this feels like a utilitarian restock email.
- I'd skim it, maybe tap "Shop now" if I needed salon-size shampoo, and then mark it read. I wouldn't forward it to a friend.

## What's working

- The black hero with a huge "50% off" is unmissable. The eye lands on the offer first.
- "Buy one, get one 50% off" is a concrete mechanic, and the button is clear.
- The Klarna strip and the "Free Shipping Orders $40+" and "2 Hour Delivery" icons answer my friction questions without making me scroll to the fine print.
- The "Last day to save" banner creates credible urgency, and the Sally tote photo is the only warm, human moment in the email.

## What's weak

- Four ion shampoos is a very narrow product pick. There's no color, nails, tools or anything beauty-adjacent, even though the nav lists Hair Color and Nails.
- Two different offers (BOGO 50% and 15% off BOPIS) compete, and the 15% banner is only valid online with in-store pickup. That's a lot of conditions for one email.
- There's no loyalty or Sally Rewards points math anywhere. I'm always counting points, and the fine print only mentions rewards in passing.
- The product tiles are small and floaty, with lots of white space. Every "Shop now" is a small underlined text link, which is a tricky tap target on a phone.
- "Early Black Friday" on 11/1 with no dates or end time. I don't know how early or how long it lasts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it one offer. Either lead with BOGO 50% or the 15% BOPIS, not both. Put the end date in the hero.
- 2. Show what 50% off actually saves on the bottles, like "2 bottles for $X," instead of just a percentage.
- 3. Add Sally Rewards member messaging, such as extra points or early access, so members feel recognized.
- 4. Mix the products: a color or nail item and a styling product next to the shampoos.
- 5. Turn the product links into full-width buttons with prices.
- **Subject Alt A:** `BOGO 50% off big bottles ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shampoo restock: buy 1, get 1 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ion Color Defense and Hard Water are in. Plus 15% off pickup orders today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack the BOGO with free shipping over $40 before Black Friday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has unresolved and incomplete tracking markup: an unreplaced `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro, a plain-HTTP tracking pixel, and a recipient email address in image URLs. It also has several `<img>` tags with missing alt text or empty `src`. The compliance and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay didn't capture them. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks (unsubscribe link, footer address, UTMs) are unconfirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved macro:** `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&et=i&ago=212&...` still contains the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]`. The third-party ad-verification pixel was never substituted, so cache-busting and impression de-duplication won't work.
- **Insecure pixel:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990&...` is served over HTTP. Mail clients may block it or show mixed-content warnings, and the Dotomi tracking will silently fail in those cases.
- **Unprobed links:** 54 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Many third-party pixels:** The email loads Evergage, mon-trk.com, MyVisualIQ and Dotomi. Each adds a failure point and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:**
  - `summary.png` ×2 (`sallyholdings.us-4.evergage.com/.../campaign/SSGvV/XwBy1/1/summary.png` and `.../2/summary.png`). These appear to be dynamic content images, so missing alt leaves that content invisible with images off or to screen readers.
  - `003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` (`pxl.mon-trk.com/...`), `impression_pixel` and `UCMController`. These are tracking pixels and should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Empty `src`:** Two images were reported with no alt and an empty `src:`. Empty `src` can trigger extra requests or broken-image icons in some clients.
- **Not an issue:** The source includes `<!--[if !mso]>` conditionals, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a viewport meta tag and a 499px mobile breakpoint. The `Founders Grotesk` font is declared with a Helvetica/sans-serif fallback.
