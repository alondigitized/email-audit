# Join the waitlist: advent cals are back! 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "an advent calendar waitlist? In October?" and then I got curious. Advent cals are a real beauty-event moment for me. Sephora and Ulta both run theirs, and I do the points math on those. But this email asks me to join a waitlist for something with no price, no drop date and no tier perk. I'm Rouge and Diamond, so I only care about a waitlist if it comes with early access or a price, and this has neither.
- The hero is also a muddle. The giant outlined "e.l.f." logo with "NEW FOR 2026!" doesn't show me a calendar at all. It's the e.l.f. SKIN line art. The purple "express your e.l.f. advent calendar" block does have a clean black "JOIN THE WAITLIST" button, which is the clearest thing here. Then the Tromsø POP fragrance block shows up again, and I just saw that on 10/5. This is the ninth e.l.f. send I've logged since 9/28, so the repeat registers right away.
- Would I click? Maybe the waitlist, since it's low effort and I'd rather not miss 24 limited-edition pieces. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for an actual drop email.

## What's working

- The "JOIN THE WAITLIST" button is black on purple, centered, and hard to miss.
- "24 limited edition e.l.f.ssentials in full & mini sizes" tells me the calendar's scope. That's the closest thing to a value claim.
- The Tromsø hero is gorgeous. The aurora background and the POP bottle and jar look prestige, closer to Sephora's fragrance heroes than the usual e.l.f. glitter chaos.
- "Top Picks for You" shows clear prices ($11, $2, $11). The $2 lip liner is a nice anchor.
- The "free shipping, limited time only" and "receipt scanning" rewards callouts in the footer are good trust cues.

## What's weak

- The waitlist has no drop date, no price and no hint of early access. Sephora tells me exactly when the Beauty Insider event opens for my tier. This tells me nothing.
- The top hero is a giant outline logo with a "NEW FOR 2026!" burst, and none of it shows the calendar. That's a wasted first screen.
- The Tromsø section repeats the 10/5 send, so this feels like a recycled second hero.
- The big gray category bars (NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE, HAIR) are flat and take up a lot of the scroll with no imagery. Ulta's nav is tighter.
- "Top Picks for You" has tiny product images and small "SHOP NOW" buttons. The tap targets are small on a phone. It also doesn't feel personalized to me.
- The only rewards mention is "receipt scanning" buried in the footer. Sephora and Ulta lead with member math, and this has none.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the advent calendar itself in the first screen, with a product shot, a price, and a drop date, and cut the giant logo hero.
- 2. Give the waitlist a reason: "waitlist gets 24-hour early access" or a perk. Without one it's a dead end.
- 3. Drop the Tromsø block, since it was just sent 10/5. Give it its own send and keep this one to a single message.
- 4. Replace the gray category bars with a small image row, or cut them.
- 5. Make the product tiles bigger, with larger tap targets, and show real personalization.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based, Braze-style template with click-tracked links. The QA run found a 27% pass rate: 4 of the 12 probed links failed on relative-URL redirects, the text/plain part is empty, and the compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Four probed links failed with `unknown url type`.** Each failure was a relative path, which means the redirect chain from `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` ended in a `Location` that wasn't an absolute URL:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&pid=Email&bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&utm_campaign=100626_adventcal_waitlist_0-3&utm...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **I can't tell from the data whether this is a probe artifact or a real defect.** If a hop really returns a relative `Location`, a browser resolves it against the tracker host (`links.elfcosmetics.com`) and would likely 404. If the probe is mishandling a redirect from the destination domain, readers are unaffected. A manual `curl -I` on one of these links would settle it.
- **The "Receipt scanning" link resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`.** A promotional link that lands on a login wall is friction for logged-out recipients. This one is confirmed by the destination path.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 39 links were probed. The 27 skipped links, including footer and social links, are unverified.
- **One image has no `alt`.** Its src is `links.elfcosmetics.com/q/ZWmT3Z8bdr-...`, so it looks like an open-tracking pixel. The impact is low.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part:** `[FAIL]` The text version is 0 chars. This is a deliverability problem if the original message truly lacks a text/plain part. The relay may have dropped it, so verify against the raw message.
