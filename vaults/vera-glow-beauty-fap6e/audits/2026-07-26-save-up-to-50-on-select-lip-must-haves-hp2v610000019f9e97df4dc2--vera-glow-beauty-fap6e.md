# Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves ⭐
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so I've seen this exact subject line before — literally today, per my own read history, and it scored an 8. Opening it again, my first reaction is the same complaint: "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves" gets me here, and the hero banner is "Get 30% off Select Charlotte Tilbury" for National Lip Day. That's not the same number, and it's not even close to "select lip must-haves" plural — it's one brand. I did the math in my head before I even scrolled and the mismatch bugged me.
- Below the fold it's actually a solid new-launch wall — YSL cushion foundation, Marc Jacobs blush stick, Sarah Creal, Fara Homidi, Westman Atelier, Dior, Givenchy — genuinely my category, genuinely brands I'd tap into. But it reads like two completely different emails stapled together: a lip promo hero that doesn't deliver its own headline number, then a "New Arrivals" catalog that has nothing to do with lips or 50% off anything.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the product grid, not because the hero sold me. Would I forward it? No. Would I mark as read and move on? Basically yes — this is the second near-identical "lip must-haves" send from Sephora today and I'm not restructuring my afternoon around it.

## What's working

- The "New" product grid is genuinely my speed — Westman Atelier, Hourglass, Dior, Givenchy are brands I already stock up on at Beauty Insider events.
- Clean render, no broken images or overlapping text, easy to scan top to bottom on my phone.
- Insider/VIB/Rouge tier bar is right up top where I expect it, so at a glance I know my account is being acknowledged.
- "Just-Dropped Dyson" placement at the bottom is a smart high-ticket add — I'd actually stop scrolling for that.

## What's weak

- Subject says "up to 50% on select lip must-haves," hero delivers "30% off Charlotte Tilbury" only — that's a bait-and-switch, and it's the same disconnect I flagged on this exact subject line earlier today.
- This is functionally a repeat send — same subject, same day, per my own inbox — Sephora is doing what Glossier's been doing to me twelve times since July 7th, just compressed into one afternoon instead of stretched over weeks.
- "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" banner right after the hero has zero connection to the lip offer — it's a second, competing message before I've even processed the first one.
- Points balance in the top bar reads "0 points," which for a Rouge account either means broken personalization or I just blew my whole balance — either way it undercuts the "we know you" pitch.
- No shade range, ingredient callouts, or "clean" filtering beyond the little green badges — for a $35–78 lip/face price range, I want more than a name and a number.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the headline math first — if the real offer is 30% off Charlotte Tilbury, the subject needs to say that, not "up to 50%." Ulta pulled a version of this stunt with its "this event is over" TATCHA send and it read just as sloppy.
- 2. Cut the "You Deserve to Treat Yourself" banner entirely or move it to a separate send — it's diluting the one offer you're actually trying to sell.
- 3. Fix the points display before this goes out — "0 points" on a Rouge account reads like a broken merge field, not a feature.
- 4. Add shade/finish chips to at least the lip products in the grid (Hourglass, Givenchy) since that's the stated hook of the whole email.
- 5. Space this out from whatever else went out under this same subject today — I don't need Sephora doing its own Glossier impression.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus new-in from YSL, Marc Jacobs, Westman Atelier & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your points, your picks — shop before today's markdowns end`
