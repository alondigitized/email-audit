# Makeup that feels as good as it looks 🌱
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Sephora doing "Clean at Sephora" as a full curation drop and honestly my eye landed on the App Fest banner up top before I even got to the "clean" hero — 25% off select brands (Anastasia Beverly Hills, OLEHENRIKSEN) is the more actionable offer, and it's buried above a "Clean" seal graphic that reads more like a badge than a sale. Once I scroll past that, it's a long, dense product wall — M.PH by Mary Phillips, HAUS LABS by Lady Gaga, Kosas, Milk Makeup, Westman Atelier — and that's a genuinely strong "new + only at Sephora" lineup that speaks to me as Rouge, since half these brands are exactly what I stock up on during Beauty Insider events.
- But there's no discount tied to any of this middle section — it's a merchandising showcase, not a sale, and after 25% off App Fest up top, "clean" product spotlights with no percentage off feel like a letdown. Compare this to Sephora's own App Fest send from 8/24 that I scored a 7 — that one had a day-by-day calendar with actual escalating discounts each day. This one leads with a teaser of that same App Fest deal, then pivots into full-price product education for ten screens. Ulta's "20% off is calling" send is repetitive and stale at this point, but at least the offer is the whole email — here Sephora buries its own promo under a wall of clean-beauty copy.
- Would I click? Maybe, on the App Fest 25% off banner specifically, mostly because Anastasia Brow Wiz is a refill I'm always about due for. Would I scroll the whole thing on my phone? Probably not past the "Spot it. Shop it." section. Forward to a friend? Only the top banner, cropped — not the whole email.

## What's working

- The App Fest 25% off banner up top is a real, specific offer with named brands (Anastasia, OLEHENRIKSEN) — that's concrete enough to act on.
- HAUS LABS by Lady Gaga and M.PH by Mary Phillips as "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" callouts hit my brand-curation radar exactly — this is Sephora's exclusivity flex working as intended.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) at the bottom is a small but real nod to loyalty structure, even buried down there.
- Clean product photography throughout, no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- The "Clean at Sephora Must-Haves" hero has zero percentage or dollar offer attached — it's a values/ingredient story wedged right under a real discount banner, which flattens the whole email's hierarchy.
- This is a wall of full-price product spotlights for like eight screens straight — no urgency, no pricing math, nothing that rewards my Rouge tier specifically past the tiny bar at the very bottom.
- "New Korean Hair Care" and "Indulgent Body Washes" and "Summer Fridays" tacked on after the makeup wall feels like three separate emails stitched together, not one focused send.
- The App Fest CTA up top says "SHOP THE APP" — that's routing me to download/open an app, not straight to the sale, which is more friction than Ulta's blunt "SCORE" code approach.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — App Fest 25% off deserves its own email (like the 8/24 App Fest send that scored a 7), and "Clean at Sephora" deserves its own without a live discount banner competing above it.
- 2. Cut the product wall by half — ten-plus full-price spotlights before any pricing incentive is exhausting on mobile; lead with 3-4 hero items max.
- 3. Tie a tier-specific perk (2x points, early access) to at least one of these "only at Sephora" drops — Rouge shoppers like me are always doing the points math and there's none here.
- 4. Move the Beauty Insider tier bar up near the top, not buried after body washes.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New at Sephora: HAUS LABS, M.PH, and Clean must-haves`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Anastasia, OLEHENRIKSEN, and more — 25% off today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Rouge perks + this week's newest launches`
