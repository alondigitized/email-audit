- The laughing-model hero photo actually feels like a real person, not stock-catalog stiffness — that's a step up from the corporate-hair-color energy I flagged on 9/1 and 9/3.

## What's weak

- Nails aren't my aisle — Sephora and Ulta don't even bother pushing OPI at me because they know my basket is skincare and prestige makeup, so this whole email is a swing and a miss on targeting.
- 30% off skincare gets buried below eight rows of nail polish with zero product names or hero shot worth stopping for — that's the one category I'd actually care about and it's an afterthought.
