- Okay, this is actually a nice palate cleanser after the week ILIA's been running — no clinical stats, no split-face macro, just a collab with Crown Affair (hair brand, feels like a legit crossover not a random slap-on) and a GWP structure I actually understand instantly. $75+ spend gets you a mini leave-in conditioner, and then they layer in a bigger free-gift tier ("The Out-The-Door Ritual") if you build a bigger basket. - The hero image is soft and pretty — model in the pink towel with the "LIMITED TIME ONLY" red badge gives real urgency without shouting.
- The $75+ threshold for the mini gift is stated clearly right under the headline, no digging required.
- "Complete the ritual" section doing double duty as a bundle upsell AND a full-catalog nav (Hero Set, ILIA Look, Sunscreen + Color Set) is a smart use of one email for two jobs.
- Product shot of the Leave-In Conditioner itself with real ingredient/benefit copy (frizz, breakage, heat protectant) — no vague "you'll love it" nonsense.

## What's weak

- No mention of ILIA VIP points or how this GWP interacts with my loyalty tier — as a points-math person, I want to know if this order also earns/burns points, and it's silent on that even though "Join ILIA VIP" is sitting right there in the footer icons.
- This is a hair-care crossover from a skin/makeup brand — feels a little like Drunk Elephant hopping topics (their Kamo push has been skincare-adjacent at least three times now), and it dilutes ILIA's actual differentiator.
- "ALL YOURS →" as the primary CTA is vague — I have to read the paragraph above it to know what I'm claiming.
- No visible countdown or exact end date for "limited time only" — Sally Beauty's COLORfest has the same problem, all urgency, no receipts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline on "limited time only" — even "ends Sunday 11:59pm" would make this feel less like recycled urgency language.
- 2. Swap "ALL YOURS →" for something that names the gift, like "Claim Your Mini Leave-In" — copy the clarity Ulta uses on its coupon CTAs even if I don't love their execution otherwise.
