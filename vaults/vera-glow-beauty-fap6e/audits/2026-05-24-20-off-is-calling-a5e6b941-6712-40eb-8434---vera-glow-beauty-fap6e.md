# 20% Off Is Calling ✨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Nine. This is Sally Beauty email number nine in thirteen days, and I am keeping count because at this point my Sally folder is its own ecosystem. Today's subject is "20% Off Is Calling ✨" — which, fine, is at least less repetitive than "Memorial Day Glow-Up" / "Long Weekend Beauty Haul" / "Your Beauty Routine, But on Sale" — but I open it and it's the SAME flash sale block stacked TWICE in the hero. Like, literally two identical "20% off sitewide, code WKND20, ends May 26" cards on top of each other. That's not a design choice, that's a copy-paste accident OR a bug.
- The model shot in the middle is at least a refreshing change from the gradient pink hero card they've been running on loop, and she reads age-appropriate for me — late 20s, fresh skin, brunette, hair-care-coded which tracks for the Sally master vertical. But Sephora's "Save up to 50% on select beauty 💄 Plus spend $75 to earn 500 bonus points" send earlier this week did in one subject line what this entire email is trying to do — gave me a tier benefit, a specific offer, AND a points hook. Sally just keeps yelling 20% at me.
- Would I click? No. WKND20 is a sitewide code I can grab at checkout anytime this weekend without opening this email — and Sally knows it because they emailed me about it yesterday. Mark as read, move on.

## What's working

- The model is on-brand for Sally — hair-care-forward, age 25-32 range, looks like an actual customer not an editorial shot, which is more honest than the gradient mannequin energy.
- Klarna "4 interest-free payments, available for orders above $35" is a useful credibility cue for the Sally price point, and it's surfaced cleanly.
- The "In Store / Online / Free Shipping Orders $49+ / 2 Hour Delivery / Buy Online, Pick Up In Store" service-icon strip is genuinely the best omnichannel grid I've seen from Sally this month — Ulta and Sephora both bury this.
- Nav strip up top (Hair Color, Hair Care, Textured Hair Care, Nails) is at least giving me a way out of the offer block and into a category.

## What's weak

- The flash-sale block is DUPLICATED in the hero. Two identical "20% off sitewide / use code WKND20 / ends May 26" cards stacked. That's a render bug or a build error, and it's the first thing my eye lands on twice.
- "20% off sitewide" is the same headline number Sally's been running all week. Glossier's "30% is still on!" reminder felt earned because the original drop got me. This one is just noise.
- No category hook at all — the model is hair-coded but the offer is sitewide, so I have no idea what I'm actually shopping. Drunk Elephant's "Now you see it: 25% off" at least linked to specific hero SKUs.
- No loyalty math. Sally Beauty Rewards exists. Where is the "earn 2x points this weekend" layer that would actually make me open the app? Ulta's tiered "$10 off $50 / $20 off $100" send this week did the math for me. This one does nothing.
- Single CTA copy is just "Shop Now" — twice. No urgency hook beyond "Ends May 26," no specific products, no "WKND20 unlocks at checkout" microcopy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the duplicated hero block immediately.** Two stacked identical flash-sale cards is the kind of thing that gets a send pulled at Sephora. Whether it's a build bug or an "in case they miss the first one" choice, it reads as broken.
- 2. **Layer the loyalty math into the hero.** "20% off sitewide + 2x Rewards points this weekend" turns a generic code into a tier-program reason to open the app. That's what Sephora and Ulta do every single weekend.
- 3. **Pick a category and lead with it.** The model is clearly hair-coded — make this a hair-care 20% with three specific SKU tiles below the hero (Olaplex, K18, Color Wow). Sitewide codes are a tool, not a hero.
- 4. **Cut the cadence.** This is the third "20% off sitewide" pass in five days. Stagger the creative — one flash, one product story, one tier-event tease.
- 5. **Time-bound the urgency credibly.** "Ends May 26" is fine, but "Ends Monday 11:59 PT — Memorial Day cart close" makes the deadline real.
- **Subject Alt A:** `WKND20 unlocks: 20% off + 2x points this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your hair haul, 20% off through Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack the code with Rewards points — ends Memorial Day 11:59 PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Olaplex, K18, Color Wow — sitewide with code WKND20.`
