# 20% Off Is Calling ✨
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- So this is Sally Beauty email number NINE in thirteen days, and the subject line is "20% Off Is Calling ✨" — which, okay, calling from where? Because the last time they called, it was a 50% off Memorial Day pitch on May 23, and the time before that it was 50% off on May 22, and the time before THAT it was 50% off on May 21. Now I'm getting a 20% off code (WKND20) with an end date of May 26, which is *literally the same Memorial Day weekend window they've been hammering me with three different headline numbers about*. So which is it, Sally — 50% off select stuff or 20% off sitewide? I genuinely don't know what my best move is, and that's the problem.
- The hero treatment is fine — flash sale block, code, "ends May 26," shoppable model shot — and honestly the layout is clean compared to the cluttered Memorial Day pile they've been sending. But I'm doing the points math out of habit and Sally doesn't have a Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond equivalent that rewards me for stacking, so a 20% off code is just… a 20% off code. Sephora's "Save up to 50% on select beauty + 500 bonus points on $75" email from yesterday gave me actual math to do. This is just a generic discount.
- Would I click? Maybe — if I needed root touch-up color and the 20% beat their last code. Forward? No. Mark as read after a glance? Probably yes.

## What's working

- The "20% off sitewide" with a clean code (WKND20) is at least *sitewide* — that's more honest than "up to 50% off select" which I've learned at this point means seven items in the clearance bin.
- Clear deadline — "Ends May 26" — and the email landed May 24, so the urgency feels real, not invented.
- The model shot is on-brand for Sally (hair color demographic, real-girl energy, not over-retouched) and reads correctly for the "Hair Color | Hair Care | Textured Hair Care | Nails" nav at the top.
- The Klarna 4-installment block on a $35+ order is a nice subtle nod that they know who shops here — that's a budget-conscious cue.
- Free shipping at $25 is right there in the icon row, which I appreciate over hiding it in the footer.

## What's weak

- The flash sale block is **rendered twice, stacked on top of each other** — same headline, same code, same end date. That's a real visual bug and it makes the top of the email feel like a broken duplicate, not intentional emphasis.
- The offer is *worse* than the 50% headlines they sent me Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday. A 20% sitewide on Saturday after 50% Memorial Day messaging feels like a downgrade, not a fresh hook.
- No category callout — Sephora and Ulta would have segmented me into "you bought hair color, here's hair color at 20%." Sally has every chance to do this because their site is hyper-categorical (Hair Color is literally the first nav item) and they didn't.
- Zero loyalty math. Charlotte Tilbury at least makes me do the exclusion-clause spreadsheet. Sally doesn't even pretend to give me member-pricing or points stacking.
- No social proof, no review count, no "this is the bestseller" — just a code and a model. For a brand sending 9 emails in 13 days, every send needs a reason to exist and this one doesn't differentiate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the duplicated flash sale hero.** The block is repeating — same copy, same code, twice in a row. That's the single most important fix because it makes the email look broken on first glance.
- 2. **Pick a lane on Memorial Day.** Either the 50% storyline or the 20% sitewide — running both in parallel within 48 hours tells me the marketing calendar is panicking. Sephora's "Save up to 50% + 500 bonus points on $75" yesterday committed to one stack.
- 3. **Segment by category.** I clicked on hair color last time (hypothetically — Sally should know). Lead with "20% off hair color — WKND20" and put the model shot to work.
- 4. **Add a points-equivalent.** Even a "spend $50, get $10 Sally credit on your next order" stacked with WKND20 would give me a reason to click. Right now there's no math to do.
- 5. **Cut cadence.** Nine emails in thirteen days from one mid-tier brand is e.l.f. territory and e.l.f. at least has a giveaway hook. Sally's just discounting on repeat.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack it on hair color, nails, or that thing in your cart. Ends 5/26.`
