# 10% off ends TOMORROW ⚠️Don’t miss out, Sephora Shopper!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Savings Event send in three days (11/6, 11/7, now 11/8), and the countdown timer finally gives me a real deadline. The "FINAL HOURS!" banner has a ticking clock, and the header shows both tiers side by side: my Rouge 30% and Insider 10%. The subject still shouts the Insider number at me, though. I'm Rouge, so "10% off" undersells my deal, and it's the same problem I flagged on 11/7.
- The body is a long Holiday Value Sets scroll with a lot going on: Summer Fridays, Charlotte Tilbury, LANEIGE, Makeup by Mario and Topicals minis, then a free Parachute gift, Khloé Kardashian fragrance and a gift guide. It's pretty and it has a lot of products I'd actually buy. But it's so long that the 30% never gets tied to a specific haul. I'd screenshot it for the code, maybe click the Charlotte Tilbury set, and not forward it. It's a stock-up email, not a share-with-a-friend email.
- Compared with Ulta, Sephora's header does the points-and-tier math faster. Ulta's coupon-ladder emails (the $10/$15/$20 one) put the savings front and center, but they bury the brands. Sephora is the reverse. The brands are great but the math is scattered across four different codes (ALLONSALE, SPEEDY, UNREADY and the barcode).

## What's working

- The countdown timer with "FINAL HOURS!" is a real, visible deadline, not fake urgency.
- The two-tier badge (Rouge 30% / Insider 10%) lets me do my math instantly without opening anything.
- The brand curation is strong: Summer Fridays, Charlotte Tilbury, LANEIGE and Makeup by Mario are all things I stock up on during the event.
- Prices are on the product tiles ($32, $22, $24, $18.20), so I can price-check against Ulta without clicking.
- The barcode at the bottom is a nice in-store touch if I'm at Sephora at Kohl's.

## What's weak

- The subject says 10% when I'm at 30%, and it's the third time in a row it undersells me.
- Four promo codes (ALLONSALE, SPEEDY, UNREADY, plus the barcode) compete with each other, and I can't tell which stack with which.
- The "Limited Time: Free same-day delivery" banner sits above the countdown and steals the hero spot from the sale itself.
- The email runs very long, with gift guide, fragrance and Spot It Shop It blocks. By the bottom I've lost the thread.
- The fine print is tiny and dense, and the 30% "excludes" language is buried at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment the subject by tier so Rouge sees 30%, and put the code ALLONSALE in the preheader.
- 2. Cut the email in half after the Value Sets grid, and move the Parachute free gift and fragrance to a separate send.
- 3. Collapse the same-day delivery code into one line under the timer so the sale owns the hero.
- 4. Add a "Rouge math" line, like "Spend $100, save $30", right under the badges.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with ESP click and open tracking through `app.sephora.com`. The QA run found one hard failure, an empty plain-text part, plus missing header data that may come from the AgentMail relay. The HTML was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and most link checks could not be confirmed from source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 links were probed and 35 skipped, and no broken links were reported among the 12. The skipped footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Click links are ESP redirects.** Example: `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1bba84842bb0fddf4bbe5be50/…?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2…`. Final destinations and any UTM parameters can't be seen from the source or QA data.
- **There are two hidden open-tracking pixels.**
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2gAndjYx…` is styled `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider` is a second tracker.
  - Both carry per-recipient identifiers in the URL (`mi_u=`).
- **The JSON-LD block is empty.** It has `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` and a bare `"@type": "PromotionCard"` with no fields. It uses `http://` schema.org rather than `https://`, and it adds no value as written.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The two tracking images have no alt text.** Both are flagged by QA. They are hidden, so the real impact is low, but adding `alt=""` is trivial.
- **The viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **The outer table is fixed at 700px.** It uses `style="min-width:700px; width:700px"`. The visible `@media (max-width:480px)` rules don't override it. The truncated source may contain an override, so this is likely but not confirmed.
- **The `<style>` block is malformed.** An `<!--[if mso]> … <![endif]-->` conditional comment sits inside the `@media` rule. Clients may drop the rules that follow it, such as `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`.
- **The `<html>` tag has no `lang` attribute.** The `<title>` is just "Sephora".
- **Layout tables have no `role="presentation"`.**
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid.** The correct value is `noindex`, and the tag has no effect in email anyway.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** are visible in the subject or in the source shown.
- The subject greets the recipient as "Sephora Shopper", a generic fallback name. It resolved without error, but it shows no first-name data for this recipient.
- `discountCode: ""` in the JSON-LD is an empty dynamic field, noted in section 2.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found.** Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- QA attributes all three to the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so these may be capture artifacts rather than sender defects. Verify against raw headers from a direct-to-mailbox send.
- **[FAIL] The plain-text part is 0 characters.** A missing or empty `text/plain` alternative hurts spam scoring and accessibility.
- The footer's physical address and unsubscribe link can't be verified because the source is truncated. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and landing alignment can't be verified.** The visible links are opaque ESP redirects, and no resolved destination URLs or `utm_*` parameters appear in the data. The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261108_CS_Value_Sets_Insider`, on the tracking pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a populated `text/plain` part. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on a raw, non-relayed copy.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and `role="presentation"` to layout tables.
5. Move the `[if mso]` block out of the `@media` rule.
6. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
7. Remove or populate the empty JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard`.
8. Re-run with untruncated HTML and redirect-chain resolution to check the unsubscribe link, footer address, mobile width override and UTM propagation.
