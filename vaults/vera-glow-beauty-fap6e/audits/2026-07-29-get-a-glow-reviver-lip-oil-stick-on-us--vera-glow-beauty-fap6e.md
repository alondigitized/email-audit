# Get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick on us! 💋
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. again — this is now something like day eleven or twelve of a straight streak in my inbox, and today it's the Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick on a BOGO, "app exclusive, 6 hours only," stacked with a second National Lipstick Day tie-in (free Cream Glide Lip Liner, code LIPPIE) buried further down. Visually it's actually one of their stronger sends this stretch — that swatch strip with all nine shade names in their own color block is genuinely useful, way more useful than most brands' shade grids.
- But two things knock me down from loving it. First, they already did National Lipstick Day on 7/27 ("Don't miss it, score a gift on us") — this is basically round two of the same beat, just with a different free-with-purchase item. Second, the fine print says the offer is valid "5pm EST/2pm PST – 6pm EST/3pm PST," which is one hour, not the "6 hours only!" plastered across the hero. That's the kind of mismatch that makes me trust the urgency less every time they use it.
- I'd click through for the shade swatches out of curiosity since I'm a sucker for a good lip oil grid, but I'm not forwarding this — it's not different enough from last week's Lipstick Day email to be worth sharing, and Drunk Elephant's "Lippe Lock" send hit the same lip-day occasion with way less clutter.

## What's working

- The shade swatch strip (pink·me energy, cherry on top, mauve mentality, etc.) is a clean, scannable way to show range without making me tap into a product page.
- BOGO with a real code (LIPS2) front and center in a bold magenta block — I don't have to hunt for the offer.
- Cruelty-free/vegan badge and "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face" footer line are consistent brand signals I recognize from every prior send.
- Top Picks section pulls in cross-sell (Power Grip Primer, Face Cream, Setting Spray) with real prices visible — no forced click to see cost.

## What's weak

- "6 hours only!" in the hero contradicts the fine print's actual 1-hour window (5-6pm EST) — that's the kind of urgency inflation that erodes trust over eleven straight days of emails.
- Two different offers with two different codes (LIPS2 and LIPPIE) in one email is confusing — I have to reread to figure out which code goes with which item.
- This is a rerun of the 7/27 National Lipstick Day gift beat, just repackaged — Sephora ran the actual same subject line twice on 7/29 and I called that out too, so it's not just e.l.f. doing this, but it's still lazy.
- No points balance or Beauty Squad tier shown anywhere despite "app exclusive" framing — Ulta's "Lucky you, 20% off" send at least felt personalized to a shopper, this just feels like a blast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the urgency math — either make it genuinely 6 hours or say "1 hour flash window" so the countdown doesn't undercut itself the moment someone reads the fine print.
- 2. Split the two offers (BOGO lip oil / free lip liner) into two separate sends or clearly separate sections with one CTA each — right now it reads like a merge of two campaigns.
- 3. Show my Beauty Squad points or tier status next to "app exclusive" — that's the one thing that would make this feel like it's actually for me and not a broadcast.
- 4. Swap "SHOP NOW" for something specific like "Shop Glow Reviver" so the CTA tells me exactly what I'm clicking into.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Buy 1, get 1 Glow Reviver — 1 hour only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your lip oil shade is waiting (BOGO, app only)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grab your free Glow Reviver before the app window closes`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `9 shades, one free stick — code LIPS2 today only`

# Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics — "Get a Glow Reviver Lip Oil Stick on us!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based MSO-compatible layout with Braze-hosted images, but 7 of 12 probed CTA/link destinations resolve to malformed relative-path URLs, and the message lacks both a plain-text MIME part and RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
7 of 12 probed links fail with "unknown url type" — the `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect wrapper is resolving to a **relative path with no scheme/host**, not a valid absolute URL. Affected CTAs:
- "get a glow reviver lip oil stick on us!" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?af_reengagement_window=30d&lid=ql1snl3l2otv&shortlink=yvg5pc9f&...`
- "Shop now" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fproducts%2Fdetail%2F8684572016728&utm_source=broadcast&...`
- "shop now" → resolves to `/pages/mobile-app?utm_source=broadcast&utm_campaign=072926_buy1bgslipstick_get1onus&af_xp=custom&...`
- "Shop Now" (power-grip primer) → resolves to `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml`
- "Shop Now" (holy hydration cream) → resolves to `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g`
- "Receipt scanning..." → resolves to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`

This is a redirect-chain bug, not a QA-tool artifact: the final hop is missing the `https://www.elfcosmetics.com` (or AppsFlyer OneLink) domain, so any client resolving this the same way will hit a broken/relative URL.
