# Straight from our community
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- Day ten. I counted. And I will say upfront that I've been watching this Glossier sale accumulate in my inbox since June 15 — "Save up to 25% off all fragrance," "Save up to 25% off Glossier favorites," "What to buy with up to 25% off," "Friends don't let friends pay full price" — you get it. So when "Straight from our community" landed, I actually paused before deleting. Not because it screamed urgency or offer, but because it didn't. After nine days of discount headers, a subject line that doesn't mention percentage off is jarring in the best possible way.
- Inside, the creative pivot holds up. The "RECOMMENDATIONS FROM YOU" section — Glossier You, what looks like a Lash n' Brow (or similar mascara), and Balm Dotcom, each with a short community quote and a name — is the most editorially interesting thing Glossier has sent in this entire run. It's soft-selling through peer validation rather than shouting ENDS SOON at me for the tenth time. Then the subscription lock-in at the bottom — "Start a subscription and lock in 25% off" — is brand new creative and genuinely makes me do math.
- Would I click? Yes, specifically on the Balm Dotcom card and the subscription section, because I want to understand if the sub discount stacks or replaces the sale price. Would I forward it? Maybe to my friend who keeps asking me whether she should try Glossier You — this email does her homework for her.

## What's working

- The UGC pivot is genuinely fresh — named community members, real product quotes, a "RECOMMENDATIONS FROM YOU" label that reframes the email as editorial rather than promotional
- The hero hierarchy is clean: ENDS SOON → headline → offer terms → Shop now, nothing to decode
- The subscription CTA is new creative in this entire sale run — "lock in 25% off" as a permanent subscriber benefit is the most interesting math Glossier has offered me in ten days
- Three specific product-linked cards give me something to shop rather than just dumping me on a sitewide sale page
- Renders without any visible layout breakage — images loaded, text is readable, no lorem ipsum or empty fields

## What's weak

- "ENDS SOON" has appeared in every single Glossier email since June 15 — it no longer functions as urgency, it functions as noise I auto-skip
- The UGC quotes are tiny and I'm genuinely squinting at them in the screenshot — if the community voice is the hook, it needs to be large enough to actually read at a glance
- The subscription CTA is buried at the very bottom below the fold — it's the most differentiated offer in the email and it's being treated like a footer
- No acknowledgment that I'm a returning customer; I'm being addressed like a stranger who wandered in, not someone who has been inside this sale for ten days

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the subscription pitch to mid-email**, above the product cards — "lock in 25% forever" is a fundamentally different message than a time-limited sale, and it deserves to breathe before the fold
- 2. **Make the UGC quotes the visual hero** — larger type, full-bleed card treatment, maybe an avatar or skin type call-out so I can self-select into the recommendation
- 3. **Retire "ENDS SOON" from this send** — the community editorial angle doesn't need urgency theater, and at this point the phrase has zero credibility with me anyway
- 4. **Replace all three "Shop now" CTAs with product-specific copy** — "Get Glossier You," "Shop Balm Dotcom" — matches the personalization energy of the UGC framing and gives each card a reason to exist separately
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your community's can't-live-withouts — 25% off now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Real picks from real Glossier fans (on sale)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Community faves: Glossier You, Balm Dotcom, and more — 25% off through [date]`

```html
<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01KVC0GK743WV9NJNDQSFQZY55_0"></span>
```

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Gmail Android viewport mismatch (`max-width: 384px` → `width: 414px`):**
```css
@media screen and (max-width: 384px) {
  .mail-message-content { width: 414px !important }
}
```
The media query triggers at ≤384px but forces a 414px width — wider than the triggering viewport. This is a documented Gmail Android workaround (Gmail on Android reports 384px but renders at 414px), but it will cause horizontal scroll on true 384px displays (e.g., iPhone SE 1st gen). Flag for QA on that device.
