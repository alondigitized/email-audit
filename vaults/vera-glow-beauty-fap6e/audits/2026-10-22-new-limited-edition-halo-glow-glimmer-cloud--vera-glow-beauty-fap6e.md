# NEW & LIMITED EDITION! Halo Glow Glimmer Cloud ☁️✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the puff is cute and the email is two emails stacked on top of each other. The hero is a pink puff on a glittery shoulder with "halo glow glimmer cloud. Only $12." I got the product and the price in about two seconds. Then the same puff gets a second hero block, then a before/after, then a different promo for a free full-size gift at $50. By the time I hit the category tiles I've forgotten the puff.
- Compared with Sephora's "Open for new items" catalog email, which at least had no competing promos, this one has a real offer. It also has a very Ulta-ish "shop everything" tail. e.l.f. did the same thing on 10/21 with "Choose a full-size gift on us!", so I've now seen the full-size-gift pitch two days in a row. I'd open it, tap the puff once, and not forward it. I'm not doing points math on a $12 puff.

## What's working

- The "Only $12" in the hero is clear, and the hero image of a pink puff on a shoulder tells me what it is immediately.
- The "new & limited edition!" sticker sets the launch-day tone. Limited-edition drops are what make me stock up before they sell out.
- The before/after swatch on deeper skin tones is a nice inclusive touch, and it shows the glitter payoff better than copy would.
- The "Top Picks for You" row has prices on every product ($2, $6, $11) and a "Shop now" button under each, so I can see the cost without clicking.
- "No code needed" on the gift offer is honest and removes friction. Ulta's "terms & excl. apply" subject lines never give me that.

## What's weak

- The puff gets two full hero blocks that say the same thing. The cloud, sparkle, and flower emoji bullets repeat the hero copy and push the gift offer far down the page.
- The full-size gift at $50 is a second competing offer. It's the same pitch as yesterday's email, and a "limited time!" tag on something I saw 24 hours ago doesn't feel urgent.
- The NEW / EYES / LIPS / FACE / SKINCARE / HAIR category bars are huge grey blocks with no imagery. They add scroll without helping me shop.
- "Top Picks for You" is not personalized. It's a lip liner, a lipstick, and a setting spray, with no tie to the puff or to anything I've bought.
- Nothing in the email tells me about member or tier benefits. The footer mentions rewards and the app, but not any points math I could use.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Cut the second puff block and move the gift offer up directly under the hero, or drop it for this send. A puff email should end with the puff.
- 2. Pair the puff with something I'd buy alongside it, like a glow primer or a shimmer body oil. Add a $25 threshold and a "bundle it" CTA so there's some basket math.
- 3. Replace the grey category bars with product tiles, or remove them entirely.
- 4. Make "Top Picks for You" actually reflect the glitter and glow theme.
- 5. Add real urgency, such as a stock count or a ship-by date, instead of a generic "limited edition."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (table layout, `role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, viewport meta). However, three tracked CTA links resolve to relative paths with no host, the email has no plain-text part, and the QA run could not confirm the unsubscribe or authentication headers. The QA pass rate was 30%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Relative-path destinations (3 FAILs).** Probing the tracked redirects at `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` ended in `unknown url type` errors on these destinations:
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Pinky+Swear+-+Beige+Pink` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?utm_source=broadcast&shortlink=email&lid=...` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- All three destinations lack a scheme and host. The common pattern points to a template or link-builder problem: relative storefront paths were passed to the click tracker without a base domain such as `https://www.elfcosmetics.com`.
- **Unverified cause.** The probe cannot say whether the redirect's `Location` header is actually relative or whether the probe just failed to resolve it. If it is relative, a browser would resolve it against `links.elfcosmetics.com`, which would likely 404. This needs a manual `curl -I` on one of these URLs to confirm.
- **Coverage gap.** Only 12 of 40 links were probed and 28 were skipped. The other links are unverified.
- **Missing alt on a tracked image.** See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`. This is an accessibility and web-view defect.
- **Missing alt text** on the image at `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/btxxuS113l3ss8SG8Wmfbw~~/...`.
- **Empty font-family:** `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the `mobile_hide` block. The declaration is invalid and falls through to Verdana.
- **Image tracking URL:** the image is served through a `/q/` tracking redirect rather than a direct asset URL.
- **Plain-text part:** see section 5.
- **Other rendering checks.** The MSO conditionals, `x-apple-data-detectors` reset and `desktop_hide`/`mobile_hide` handling are present and valid. The Google Fonts link is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, and fallbacks (`Arial,Sans-serif`) are declared. The truncated source prevents a full audit of the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `[%...%]`, `*|...|*`) appear in the visible source. The `*puffing*` in the preheader is literal copy, not a token.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text fallback:** [FAIL] 0 chars. A multipart email with no text alternative hurts deliverability and accessibility. This is a true sender-side defect unless the relay stripped the part.
- **`List-Unsubscribe`** [WARN] and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (RFC 8058) [WARN] were not found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. I can't confirm either way, so verify against the raw message. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`** [WARN] was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a capture limitation, not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these were not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't verify them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All links are wrapped by the click tracker (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`). The final UTM parameters can't be verified, because the QA probes never reached a resolved landing page for the three failing links.
- The only UTM evidence is on the app link: `utm_source=broadcast&shortlink=email&lid=...&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome`. It is truncated, and no `utm_medium` or `utm_campaign` is visible.
- The "Shop Now" product link carries `Color=Pinky+Swear+-+Beige+Pink` as a variant deep link. Because the path is relative, landing page alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Absolute URLs:** make all tracked destinations absolute (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) in the template or link builder. Re-run the three failing links, then check the final status and UTMs.
2. **Redirect check:** run `curl -I` on one failing tracked URL to separate a real relative `Location` header from a probe limitation.
3. **Plain-text part:** add a multipart/alternative text version.
4. **Headers:** check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results. If they're missing, add them.
5. **HTML fixes:** add a descriptive `<title>`, add `alt` to the `/q/btxxuS...` image, and remove the empty `''` font-family.
6. **UTMs:** set `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` consistently on all wrapped links.
7. **Probe coverage:** raise the probe limit so the 28 skipped links are checked.
