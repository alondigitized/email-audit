# Save 30% off select favorites
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, a Labor Day sale email — this is the most "normal" Glossier send I've logged in weeks, and honestly a relief after the fragrance-quiz/back-to-school run they've been on since 8/20. "Save 30% off select favorites" with an actual product grid, actual 50%-off callouts on the Stretch Balm Concealer and Stretch Fluid Foundation, and a $75+ free-gift threshold — this is a normal promotional email doing normal promotional email things, and I mean that as a compliment after 8/22's Virgo Season non-offer.
- That said, "select favorites" with an asterisk is doing a lot of quiet work — I scroll all the way to the bottom before I even see "*Terms apply" and I still don't know which products qualify for the 30% versus which are the separately-flagged 50%-off items. And there's zero Rouge/points math anywhere, which is the thing I actually care about — Sephora's "point of view" send this week at least felt like a real merchandising story; this is just a sale.
- I'd click through to check the concealer at 50% off since that's a genuine deal, but I'm not forwarding this to anyone — it's a solid, unremarkable sale email, not a "you need to see this" moment.

## What's working

- The SALE/SALE/SALE hero grid with product bottles tucked into the letters is a cute, on-brand visual — cleaner than anything Ulta's barcode-on-book-stack hero has run since 7/26.
- Two distinct 50% OFF badges (concealer, foundation) give me a concrete, scannable discount tier instead of one vague blanket claim.
- The $75+ free Glossier You Solid gift is a real added-value hook, not just another "shop now" repeat.
- Section headers (Top Picks / Deeper Discounts / Free Gift) organize the scroll so I'm not guessing what tier I'm looking at.

## What's weak

- "Select favorites" never tells me which items get 30% off — no price shown anywhere in the top-picks section, so I can't do my usual points-and-percentage math before clicking.
- No Rouge or Ulta-tier-equivalent language at all — Glossier doesn't run a loyalty program the way Sephora/Ulta do, but for a cross-shopper like me, the total absence of any member framing makes this feel like a stranger-blast, not a "we know you" email.
- Six "Shop now" buttons all identical in weight — my eye has nowhere obvious to land first, unlike Sephora's send this week where the point-of-view hero was unmistakable.
- The free-gift CTA button at the bottom is grey/outlined instead of orange like the rest — reads like a lower-priority afterthought when it's actually the most novel offer in the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or percentage directly on each top-pick product card — "select favorites" with zero numbers until the very bottom asterisk is the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Make the free-gift CTA visually match the orange "Shop now" buttons above it — right now it looks disabled.
- 3. Add a one-line loyalty nod (even just "members get free shipping over $50") — Glossier is leaving easy personalization on the table that Sephora and Ulta both lean into hard.
- 4. Cap the redundant "Shop now" repetition — six identical buttons dilutes urgency; bold just the 50%-off items as the hero CTAs.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Stretch Concealer is 50% off right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: free Glossier You Solid with $75+`
