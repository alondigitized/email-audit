# 🟠 Karma coming your way
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Five Lush emails in ten days — May 12, 14, 16, 20, and now this one on May 22, landing on the Thursday before Memorial Day weekend. I want to be fair to Karma as a product line because I genuinely love that scent, but landing in my inbox the same weekend Sephora's running 50% off select beauty and Glossier just dropped a full Memorial Day sale? No discount, no "spend X get Y," no "Lush Rewards" hook — just "Keep the Good Vibes Going" with an orange hero. That's a tough sell when I've been in deal mode since Tuesday.
- The email is genuinely pretty. The saturated citrus palette is on-brand, the layout is clean, and the Karma product grid does its job. And there is a free gift section — buried below five or six product rows at the very bottom. That's the potential hook, and it's completely wasted by placement. May 20's Dream Cream send was a tighter single-hero execution; this one tries to be a catalog and an editorial at the same time and lands between both.
- I'd click because Karma Kream is on my restock list anyway — but that's brand muscle memory doing the work, not anything this email earned. I would not forward this to a friend right now when I have a Drunk Elephant 25% link sitting in my other tab.

## What's working

- The orange hero is genuinely striking — the citrus slices, the dark header frame, the product photography all read premium and cohesive
- "Shop Karma" as the CTA button is better than the average "Discover More" filler — it names the product line specifically
- Product grid logic is sound — showing Karma Kream, the shower gel, and the fragrance spray together lets me see the full range and self-select
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping copy, no lorem ipsum anywhere I can see
- "Keep the Good Vibes Going" as hero copy plays off the Karma brand story without being overwrought

## What's weak

- No offer. Zero percent off, no GWP that's visible above the fold, no free shipping call-out — on Memorial Day weekend this is a significant miss when Sephora, Drunk Elephant, and Glossier are all competing for the same wallet
- The free gift section is buried at the very bottom of a long scroll — I had to work to find it, and by then I'd already mentally moved on
- This is the second "here's one hero product" email in two days; Dream Cream was May 20, Karma is May 22 — the single-scent format is repeating before it's had time to breathe
- No loyalty or points angle at all — even a "Lush Rewards members get early access" line would have signaled that Lush knows I'm a repeat customer, not a cold stranger
- Five emails in ten days is ILIA-level frequency without ILIA's format variety; ILIA at least rotated between editorial, quiz, personal note, and hard offer — this Lush run is starting to blur together

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the free gift offer in the hero or immediately below it** — if you're running a GWP, that's your competitive hook on a sale weekend; bury it at scroll position 8 and you've thrown away your best card
- 2. **Add a spend threshold with the free gift** — "Free Karma sample kit with any $40 order" is a Memorial Day-appropriate hook that doesn't require deep discounting and creates urgency
- 3. **Rest the single-scent format for one send** — after Dream Cream two days ago, a Karma-only parade reads as repetitive; a "weekend picks" roundup that features Karma as the hero would have felt fresher
- 4. **Surface a Lush Loyalty nudge** — even a light "members earn points on every order" line at the top acknowledges I'm a known customer, not a stranger
- 5. **Use the preheader to preview the free gift**, not just mirror the subject energy
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free gift with your Karma order this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Karma Kream restocked — grab it before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The OG Lush scent. Spend $40, get a free Karma sample kit — this weekend only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Karma Kream, shower gel, the spray. Pick your set before the holiday rush.`
