# 20% OFF!! Now Is The Time To Shop! 🛍️✨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Eight. This is Charlotte Tilbury email number EIGHT in ten days, and the subject line literally has *two* exclamation points after "20% OFF" because apparently the seven previous "20% off" sends weren't urgent enough. The hero is "Trending Beauty Secrets" with the same 20% off, the same Pillow Talk-adjacent product carousel, and the same "Unlock A Free Collagen Lip Bath when you spend $110" GWP threshold I've now seen recreated in four different layouts this week.
- Look, the creative is fine — the velvet-gold CT polish is intact, the product grid is shoppable, the GWP is a legit hook at the $110 mark. But I'm Rouge AND Diamond, I know what 20% off Charlotte Tilbury looks like, and I've seen this exact promotion EIGHT TIMES. Sephora's "Save up to 50% + 500 bonus points at $75" landed once with concrete points math and I clicked immediately. Ulta tiered their $10/$50, $20/$100 with my name in the subject — that's segmentation. This is just "we're going to send the same email until you click."
- Would I click? No — I already shopped this sale on the May 18 mini-gifts send, which was the actually good one. Forward? Never. Mark as read and move to the CT folder I've started auto-filing into, which should tell them everything.

## What's working

- The GWP threshold ($110 for the Collagen Lip Bath) is a real, specific add — better than a vague "free gift"
- Product grid shows actual SKUs with names — Airbrush Setting Spray, Pillow Talk Lip Cheat, Hollywood Flawless Foundation — I can shop from the email
- "DISCOVER THE MAGIC OF charlottetilbury.com + APP" cross-promo for the app is smart for loyalty stacking
- Velvet-gold CT branding is consistent and recognizable on the inbox preview thumbnail

## What's weak

- EIGHTH 20%-off send in ten days — this is past saturation, into "I'm just sending it back to inbox" territory
- Two exclamation points in the subject line — desperate, and uncharacteristic of CT's usually composed voice
- No new hook, no new product story, no escalated offer — same 20%, same Pillow Talk-coded grid
- "Trending Beauty Secrets" is vague filler — the May 22 "Peel To Reveal" had an actual concept; this has none
- No loyalty math for someone like me — Sephora would tell me my points multiplier, Ulta would put my name in the subject

## Recommendations

- 1. **STOP SENDING.** Skip a beat. The May 22 scratch-card was the right gimmick — let that be the last impression for 72 hours, then come back with something new.
- 2. Pivot the hook — if you must send, lead with the GWP ("Free Collagen Lip Bath at $110") not the 20% everyone has seen seven times.
- 3. Add tier recognition — "Rouge members get an extra X" or "you're Y away from your free gift" makes it feel personalized, not blasted.
- 4. Kill one of the exclamation points. CT is a luxury brand; the "!!" reads like a flash-sale dupe brand, not Charlotte's velvet curtain.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 20% off + the Pillow Talk gift at $110`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $110, unlock the Collagen Lip Bath gift — ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sale ends soon. Your Pillow Talk picks, plus a free gift.`
