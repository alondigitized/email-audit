# Eau de main character
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- The subject line did its job on me — "Eau de main character" is a genuinely clever pun for a fragrance email and I opened immediately. The hero is peak Glossier: a young woman surrounded by stacked books, cream palette, that "I have a whole personality and a skincare routine to match" vibe. It's aesthetically flawless for the brand and for me specifically.
- But then I scroll and the email doesn't really go anywhere. Five products from the Glossier You family, all with the same "Shop now" button, no prices, no offer, no urgency, no Sephora integration (which matters now because that's where I actually buy Glossier). The "Get started" CTA on the hero is doing almost nothing — started doing what? The copy that follows — something about "one as you go, one as it grows" — is evocative but confusing. I want to vibe with it and I almost can, but I also can't tell if I'm supposed to buy something or just... appreciate the fragrance universe.
- Would I click? Probably Fleur out of curiosity, not conviction. Would I forward? Maybe to my one friend who is still a full Glossier convert. For everyone else in my group chat, this doesn't move the needle.

## What's working

- **Subject line is genuinely clever** — "Eau de main character" lands perfectly for a beauty-literate 29-year-old, pun executed without being cringe
- **Hero photography is editorial and on-brand** — books, soft light, woman who looks my age; I felt seen
- **Full fragrance family laid out in one email** — good for someone new to the You line who needs the comparison
- **Scent descriptions per product** (soft/warm/radiant for You, audacious/intoxicating for Doux) give me enough to pick a vibe without clicking through
- **Clean, no-clutter layout** — easy to read, no rendering issues, scrolls smoothly

## What's weak

- **"Get started" is a dead CTA** — for a fragrance this is meaningless; "Find your You" or "Shop the collection" would do infinitely more work
- **Zero offer or price anchor** — no % off, no bundle deal, no free shipping threshold; there is nothing to act on *today*
- **No Sephora call-out** — Glossier is at Sephora now, which is where I shop to get Beauty Insider points; this email treats that like it doesn't exist
- **Some product copy reads strangely** — "Some call it pheromone generation" and "it cleans like a refined and honey middle" feel slightly garbled, eroding the brand's usually-tight voice
- **No loyalty or member hook whatsoever** — as a Rouge member, I expect to know if my purchase earns points, and I see nothing here

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap "Get started" for a pointed CTA** — "Find your You" or "Take the scent quiz" ties to the main character personalization story and gives me something to actually *do*
- 2. **Add a Sephora earn-points callout** — "Available at Sephora | Earn Beauty Insider points" is table stakes now that they're stocked there; I literally won't buy direct if I lose Rouge points
- 3. **Anchor one product as the hero** — five equally-weighted products creates decision paralysis; lead with You (the OG bestseller) and let the others sit below as "also in the family"
- 4. **Introduce at least a soft urgency signal** — "Fleur just restocked" or "Rêve: new to the family" would give me a reason to act this week vs. later
- 5. **Tighten the product copy** — "pheromone generation" and the Rêve description feel AI-adjacent; Glossier's voice is too good to let that slide
