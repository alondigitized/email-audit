# Well, hi, 25% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is email number SEVEN in the Drunk Elephant Friends & Family campaign and at this point my spreadsheet has its own pivot table. Subject "Well, hi, 25% off!" — cute, friendly, very on-brand voice, but I have now seen this exact 25% off sitewide offer rendered seven different ways in roughly a week. The hero is the same gray-pink "Friends & Family 25% off sitewide" creative I have audibly groaned at by send four.
- That said — and credit where it's due — this one actually does something the last few didn't: it segments into "Future's Looking Bright" (the C-Firma/Bright stack with a free $100+ gift) and "Mask Me Anything" (full mask gift over $50). That's not just "25% off the whole enchilada" again — that's GWP layering, which is the Sephora Beauty Insider move I actually care about. The points math gets interesting when there's a free product attached.
- Would I click? I already bought on launch day, so no — but if I hadn't, the mask GWP at $50 spend is genuinely a low-bar add-on I'd consider. Forward to a friend? No, she's also been pelted with this campaign. Mark as read with a small nod of respect, because at least this send earned its place in the sequence.

## What's working

- The GWP layering is the first real *new* hook in this campaign — full-size mask at $50, $100+ gift on the Bright stack. That's a reason to open send #7.
- "Future's Looking Bright" / "Mask Me Anything" — those headers are actual brand voice, not "SHOP NOW" filler. DE's copy edge is still holding up better than ILIA's "Shop 20% off sitewide" stripped-down sends.
- "The Best of Friends" hero grid at the bottom with Protini, Lala, B-Hydra, Bouncy — the bestsellers I'd actually restock at 25% off, with prices visible. That's better merchandising than Glossier's "30% is still on" which just sent me to a category page.
- The pink banner "this way to the friend zone" is a wink I'll allow. DE's tone is still doing more work than Charlotte Tilbury's seventh "20% Off!" with an emoji glued on.

## What's weak

- Email seven of the same offer. Seven. The hero creative is barely differentiated from yesterday's "Now you see it" send and Tuesday's launch — it's becoming wallpaper.
- The GWP thresholds ($50, $100+) are the real story but they're buried mid-email under the same generic "25% OFF SITEWIDE" hero. Lead with the gift, not the discount I've already seen.
- The "Future's Looking Bright" product render looks tiny against that giant "FREE $100+ GIFT" badge — visual hierarchy fights itself.
- No loyalty / member math anywhere. Sephora Rouge would have surfaced point multipliers; Ulta Diamond would've shown me my tier perk. DE just assumes I'm a guest every send.
- The fine print footnote stack at the bottom is dense and a little anxiety-inducing for what's supposed to be a "friends" tone.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Reframe the hero around the GWP, not the percentage.** By send seven, "25% off sitewide" is white noise — "Free full-size mask at $50, free $100+ gift on Bright" is the actual news.
- 2. **Add personalization.** I've gotten seven of these. Send eight should say "your cart's still waiting" or "here's what your wishlist looks like at 25% off." Sephora and Ulta both segment by purchase history; DE is just blasting.
- 3. **Compress the campaign.** Two more sends like this and I'm hitting the unsubscribe — and I'm a buyer. Save send eight for the last-call urgency, drop the rest.
- 4. **Show me the points/tier math** for anyone connected via Sephora — DE sells there, lean into it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free full-size mask is waiting (+25% off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Spend $50, get the mask. Spend $100, get the glow.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two gifts unlocked at F&F — Protini, Lala, and the Bright stack are all in.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `25% off + a full-size gift. Friends-only pricing ends soon.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Third-party font CDN dependency (reliability risk):** Six font references point to `db.onlinewebfonts.com` (e.g., `https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/c/4150d76a852e1c349e0b6fe15f29d9ca?family=Brown+Bold`). The `<link>` tags also appear *before* `<meta charset>`, violating HTML head ordering.
- **Broken/commented media-query rules:** Inside `@media only screen and (max-width: 600px)` the block contains HTML comments used to disable CSS rules: `<!-- *[class=contenttable] { width:414px !important;} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] { width:414px !important;height: auto !important;} -->`. HTML comments are not valid CSS — Outlook/Gmail will either ignore the surrounding block or mis-parse subsequent rules. Use `/* ... */` instead.
- **Mobile sizing bug in `.vouchertext4` / `.fsname`:** `max-width:15px !important; max-height:15px !important; width:17px !important;` on a text element clamps the box to 15px while font-size is forced to 15–17px, guaranteeing clipped or overflowing glyphs on screens ≤418px.
- **`max-height:1000000px` universal selector:** Applied via `*{...max-height:1000000px;...}`. This is a known Gmail-clipping workaround but is applied with `*` which is heavier than necessary and can interfere with image sizing.
