# 50% off select beauty starts today 💋
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "50% off" as the hero headline is the kind of subject that actually gets my thumb to stop scrolling — but then I open it and the very first thing under the header is "You have 0 points," which is just wrong, and right below that the tier bar at the bottom has "INSIDER" highlighted in red instead of "ROUGE." I'm Rouge tier, I've got thousands of points sitting in that account, and Sephora's own email doesn't know who I am. That's not a nitpick, that's the whole loyalty relationship failing in the first two inches of the email.
- Past that ID mixup, the deals themselves are fine — Clinique lipstick half off, a "Deals Drop Daily" calendar for three weeks out, Afterpay stacking 10% off during App Fest — but I already logged the actual App Fest send on 8/24 (7/10, day-by-day calendar, well organized) and the "Clean at Sephora" curation send on 8/26 (5/10, buried under the App Fest banner). This is basically App Fest's daily-deals cousin, minus the calendar structure that made the 8/24 send work.
- Would I click? Maybe, for the Clinique lipstick at $12.50 — that's a real number I can act on. Would I forward it to a friend? No — not with my points showing as zero and my tier misrepresented, that's not something I'm proud to be signed up for.

## What's working

- The 50% off hero with a real anchor price ($25 → $12.50 on the Clinique lipstick) gives me something concrete to act on immediately.
- "Today Only" urgency on the top three products is credible and time-bounded, not vague.
- The Week 1/2/3 "Deals Drop Daily" preview is a smart retention hook — it's basically App Fest's calendar mechanic reused for a different promo, and I recognize the pattern from 8/24.
- Afterpay's 10% stack during App Fest is genuinely useful math for me — I always run the points/discount stacking numbers before I check out.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" right under the header is either a broken render or a real personalization failure — either way, as a Rouge member with real spend history, this makes me feel like a stranger, not a VIP.
- The tier bar at the bottom highlights "INSIDER" in red, not "ROUGE" — my actual tier. Sephora is telling me I'm the entry-level member in an email I'm opening as their top-tier customer.
- Three unrelated micro-promos (Dyson/BondiBoost hair tools, Afterpay 10%, Olive Young K-beauty) get crammed in after the main deal grid, which dilutes the "50% off" focus I opened for — Ulta's 8/26 "20% off ends today" send stayed tighter on one offer instead of sprawling like this.
- No skin tone/type personalization anywhere in the product picks — just generic bestsellers, same issue e.l.f. SKIN avoided in their 8/26 vitamin-C send by leading with a specific ingredient story instead of a grab-bag.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points/tier bug first — this is the single biggest trust breaker in the email. Pull my real point balance and highlight my actual tier (Rouge) in that bottom bar. Nothing else matters if this is broken.
- 2. Cut the email after the Week 1-3 calendar and Afterpay stack — drop Olive Young and hair tools into a separate send. Sephora's 8/24 App Fest email worked because it stayed focused; this one loses that discipline halfway down.
- 3. Add "Rouge-exclusive early access" messaging to at least one deal — I'm cross-shopping Ulta's Diamond tier against this, and right now there's zero tier-differentiated benefit visible, just a flat 50% off anyone gets.
- 4. Show projected points earned on the Clinique/Lawless/Skinfix deals next to the price — I'm doing that math manually anyway, do it for me.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deals daily through 9/17 — Rouge members get first look`
