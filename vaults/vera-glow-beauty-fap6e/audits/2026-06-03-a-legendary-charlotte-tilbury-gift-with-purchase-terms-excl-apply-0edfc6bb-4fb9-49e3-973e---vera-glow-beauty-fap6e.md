# A legendary Charlotte Tilbury gift with purchase** (Terms & excl. apply) ✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the second Charlotte Tilbury GWP email from Ulta to land in my inbox today — same subject line, same offer, same "legendary gift" framing. I clocked it immediately, and I'm not going to act like I didn't. After the Memorial Day spiral of four $10/$20 spend-and-save emails in five days, the first CT GWP send this morning felt like a genuine course correction. Now we're doing it again, same day? That reads as a data pipeline mistake, not intentional segmentation.
- That said, if I came to this cold the email is doing real work. Charlotte Tilbury is one of the brands I actively cross-shop between Ulta and Sephora, and a gift-with-purchase mechanic is exactly what tips a haul one direction. I'm Diamond tier, I'm already doing the points math, and CT GWP is the event I set alerts for. The email looks prestige — the CT photography is warm and on-brand, and it doesn't feel like a drugstore circular the way some Ulta collabs do.
- I would click — but I'd click annoyed, and I wouldn't forward this to anyone. One thing keeping me from clicking faster: Charlotte Tilbury direct sent me "FOUR Dreamy Eyeshadow Shades... For FREE!" and told me exactly what was in the gift. Ulta says "legendary gift" and buries the specifics. That comparison stings.

## What's working

- CT is front-and-center in the hero — not tucked below a generic sale banner. As a cross-shopper, I'm doing GWP math within the first scroll.
- "Beauty Break HAPPENING NOW" is better urgency language than "friendly reminder" — Ulta actually absorbed a lesson from the Memorial Day mess.
- The CT product photography is genuinely warm and glamorous — the email *feels* prestige, which is an achievement inside Ulta's standard template.
- The RECHARGE loyalty block at the bottom is relevant to me as a Diamond member; shows Ulta knows I care about the ecosystem, not just the transaction.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, multi-product layout holds up.

## What's weak

- This is a duplicate send on the same day — if I'm in the same segment as the morning send, this is an error. If I'm a different segment, the email gives me zero signal of that.
- "** (Terms & excl. apply)" is in the subject line before I even open it. CT direct didn't self-sabotage with asterisks in their subject; Ulta is leading with the hedge, not the hook.
- What's actually in the gift bag? The spend threshold and GWP contents aren't visible in the hero — I have to scroll to confirm specifics before I'll commit. ILIA gives me ingredient education in the first scroll; I need gift contents before the fold here.
- CT's model aesthetic skews toward Old Hollywood glamour — aspirational for me at 29, but there's no shade range or skin-tone signal visible, which CT direct has been getting better about.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the duplicate or segment it visibly.** Even one personalized line — "As a Diamond member, here's your early access" — makes a same-day re-send feel intentional instead of broken.
- 2. **Name the gift in the subject.** "Spend $75, get the Pillow Talk Mascara + a full-size surprise free" beats "legendary gift" every time. CT direct named four eyeshadow shades. Ulta can name two products.
- 3. **Surface the spend threshold in the hero.** "$75 unlocks your CT gift" in the hero copy — not below the lifestyle photograph.
- 4. **Pull the exclusions out of the subject.** Footnote them. A preheader can handle fine print; the subject line should sell the offer.
- 5. **Add a credible end date.** "HAPPENING NOW" is vague. "Ends Sunday" is urgency I believe.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $75 on Charlotte Tilbury, get [mascara + mini] free`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free CT gift is waiting — $75 unlocks it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Legendary Lash Mascara plus a full-size surprise — while supplies last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop $75 in Charlotte Tilbury, claim your gift at checkout. Ends Sunday.`
