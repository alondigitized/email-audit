I'm in the Millennials bucket and my picks are YSL Libre Berry Crush, Caudalie Vinoperfect, Gisou Honey Gloss, Saie Glowy Super Gel — that's a legitimately well-curated "luxe beauty that's worth it" shelf, not some random assortment.

- Would I click? Yeah, actually — the Millennials section alone is worth a tap, mostly because it's genuinely relevant instead of generic. Would I forward it? Maybe to a friend who's into the generational-beauty-discourse thing, more as a "look what Sephora's doing" than a "buy this." Not marking as read and moving on — this earns an open.

## What's working

- The Gen Z / Millennials / Gen X structure with model photos for each is a smart way to segment without needing my actual age on file — I self-selected into "Millennials" the second I saw the section header.
- My section (Millennials) has real "worth it" prestige logic — YSL, Caudalie, Gisou — that matches how I actually shop, versus mass-market filler.
- Clean, editorial black-and-white layout with the "FOR THE AGES" eyebrow tag — feels more like a trend piece than a hard sell, which is a nice change of pace from Sephora's usual promo cadence.
- Product cards with brand + name laid out clearly under each hero shot — easy to scan without pinching to zoom.

## What's weak

- "0 points" still sitting up top under INSIDER — same broken account sync I've now called out on four sends running (8/11 through today), and I'm Rouge, so this should never read zero.
- Scrolled to the tier bar at the very bottom and the red arrow is still pointing at INSIDER, not ROUGE. Ulta's tier bar has never once glitched on me like this — Sephora needs to fix this before I stop trusting the tracker at all.
- There's no actual offer here — no %/$ off, no BOGO, nothing time-bound. It's pure content marketing, which is fine as a change of pace but doesn't give me a reason to buy today.
- Compare this to ILIA's "For your Green eyes" send — that felt like it knew ME specifically. This is "people your age," which is a step down from real personalization even though it's dressed up nicely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points/tier sync before anything else — I'm Rouge, I've said it four times this week, and every send that gets this wrong chips away at whether I trust the loyalty program is even tracking me correctly.
- 2. Add a mechanic to the Millennials section specifically — even a small "Rouge gets early access to these picks" line would turn a browse into a buy trigger.
- 3. Let me tap straight into "Shop Millennials Picks" as one bundled cart-add or wishlist action instead of expecting me to click each product card individually.
- 4. Swap the generic hero copy for something that name-checks the actual beat — "what your generation is buying" is more clickable than "what's trending now."
