# 20% off?

- My first reaction was "20% off? Okay, which 20%?" I opened it and found the same Subscribe & Save ladder I've now seen five times this week. This email is the fifth Drunk Elephant send since 10/19. It follows "15% off, you say?", "Do I hear 15% off?", "15% off! Er, wait. Is it 20%?" and "Health Education Week (and 15% off) doesn't last forever." The subject promises a clean 20%. The body says 15% off the first delivery, 20% off the second, then 10% forever. I do the points math on everything, and this is a worse deal than the subject implies.
- The design is clean. The hero copy is big and readable, and the product lineup on the pedestal looks great. But I had to dig into the gray fine print to learn it ends Oct 27 at 2:59am EST. That's the only urgency in the email, and it's hidden. I'd skim it and probably not click. I'm not forwarding it either, since nothing here is new.

## What's working

- The hero copy spells out the whole 15/20/10 ladder in plain words, so I understood the structure in about three seconds.
- The product photography is gorgeous. The Protini, C-Firma, B-Hydra and T.L.C. lineup is on-brand, and it's the colorful, giftable kind of skincare I'd screenshot.
- The "Shop Now" button is clear and sits high in the email.
- The gift guide block is a nice seasonal pivot. It's the only thing here that isn't a repeat of the last four sends.
- Fine print is thorough. It covers new subscriptions only, no stacking, free shipping and cancel anytime.

## What's weak

- The subject says "20% off?" but the first order is only 15%. That's bait-and-switch energy.
- This is the fifth subscription push in six days. Compared to the 10/22 "Er, wait. Is it 20%?" send, this is the same creative with a new subject line.
- The deadline (ends Oct 27, 2:59am EST) is only in the tiny gray fine print. There's no urgency in the hero at all.
- "Shop Now" and "Gift Guide Me" are generic. No hero product is named, and no skin type or concern is addressed.
- There are no reviews, no bestseller tags and no prices. I can't do math on a $68 Protini without leaving the email.
- Nothing is personalized. It treats me like a stranger on a rotation list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero. Try "Ends Mon night: 15% off, 20% off, then 10% forever." It's the only real urgency and it's buried.
- 2. Make the subject honest. Lead with the actual first-order discount, or show the real ladder.
- 3. Cut the cadence. Five sends in six days teaches me to ignore them. Ulta's 21 Days of Beauty rotates a new deal daily, and Sephora Beauty Insider at least changes the offer. Drunk Elephant only changes the subject line.
- 4. Name one hero product with a price and a before/after subscription price, for example "Protini, $68 → $57.80." Link the CTA to that product and not to a general page.
- 5. Add a reviews or bestseller cue near the product shot.
- 6. Swap "Shop Now" for a specific CTA like "Start my subscription."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has a few confirmed code-level issues: an invalid `@import` placement, HTML comments inside a CSS `@media` block, `maximum-scale=1`, and plain-HTTP social icons. The recipient's email address also appears in a tracking-pixel URL. The unsubscribe and authentication checks are inconclusive, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations are unverified.** 11 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking or click-redirect domains. Their final destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Recipient email in a URL.** The pixel at `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the address in plaintext in the query string. It can end up in proxy, CDN and server logs.
- **User ID and campaign in a second pixel.** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_subweekresend_p…` carries a user ID (`mi_u`) and a campaign ID. That's expected for an open pixel. The campaign name suggests a resend, which is just context.
- **Two separate pixel vendors** (`mi.` and `everestengagement`) fire on each open.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on both tracking images** (`o.gif` and the `everestengagement` pixel). They should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Plain-HTTP images.** The Facebook, Instagram and TikTok icons load from `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/…png`. Clients that enforce HTTPS may block them. Gmail proxies images, but others may show broken icons.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **`@import` in the wrong place.** The three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body {…}` rule inside `<style>`. CSS requires `@import` first, so they are ignored. The `<link>` tags to `db.onlinewebfonts.com` in `<head>` are stripped by Gmail and some other clients. Brown Bold, Light and Thin therefore likely fall back to default fonts in many clients.
- **HTML comments inside CSS.** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. Inside a block, `<!--` and `-->` are not ignored. They turn into part of the next selector, so the commented rules and the rule right after them (`*[class=vouchertext4]`) are likely dropped.
- **Duplicate media queries.** The `max-width:418px` and `max-width:600px` blocks repeat the same `.vouchertext4` and `.fsname` rules. The `!important` `max-width:15px` and `max-width:12px` values could clip content. This is a maintainability issue and not confirmed as a bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The only personalization seen is the resolved email address in the `e=` parameter above. The HTML was truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA check says the AgentMail relay may be dropping them. I can't confirm the sender is non-compliant. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cheers.drunkelephant.com` can't be assessed from this capture.
- **Body footer not verified.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters in the visible source.** Attribution seems to run through `mi_ecmp` and `everestengagement` redirects instead.
- **Landing pages not validated.** The 11 click-tracked links weren't followed, so I can't check whether the destination matches the "20% off?" offer or whether the promo code or UTMs survive the redirect.
- **Font assets point at a different origin.** The `@font-face` for BrownRegular loads from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`. That versioned path can break after a site deploy and return a 404, but I didn't test it.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` source to get the real `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` values. Don't rely on the relay-captured headers.
2. Follow the 11 tracking redirects with a browser-like client. Record the final URL, the status code, and whether the 20% promo and UTMs carry through.
3. Move the `@import` rules above all other rules, or drop them in favor of `@font-face` with web-safe fallback stacks. Don't rely on the `<link>` tags.
4. Remove the `<!-- … -->` comments from inside the `@media` block, and deduplicate the repeated rules.
5. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and serve the social icons over HTTPS.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
7. Note that the sender puts a plaintext address in the `e=` query string. Treat that as a privacy observation about the sender, not something to fix on our side.
