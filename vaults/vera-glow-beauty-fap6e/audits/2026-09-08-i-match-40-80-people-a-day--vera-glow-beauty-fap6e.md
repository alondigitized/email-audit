# “I match 40-80 people a day.”
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is ILIA doing a "meet the shade matchers" employee-spotlight email — four ILIA staffers (Gabi, Janna, Kortnii, Briana) sharing their match counts and go-to combos — and it's a genuinely different format from what they've been running. After three straight sends this week that were all Overglaze/Motif/Gallery variations (9/1, 9/3, 9/5), at least this one isn't the same product photographed a fourth way. But it's also just... a lot of scrolling for four near-identical bios before I even get to a real offer or CTA.
- The "40-80 people a day" subject is literally Janna's quote pulled from deep in the body — cute concept, but it undersells what this email actually is. I had to scroll through three other employees before I even found the woman whose quote is the whole subject line.
- Would I click? Maybe the "TAKE THE QUIZ" or "CHOOSE SHADES + SAVE" near the bottom, since I actually do care about shade-matching for Skin Tint. But I'm not forwarding this — it's a nice trust-building piece, not a shopping email, and Sephora/Ulta don't send me anything like it so there's no direct comp, but it also buries the actual match-game CTA below four bios' worth of scroll.

## What's working

- Real employee faces with real skin tones across the four spotlighted matchers — that's actual diversity signal, not stock photography.
- The "Match Game" module near the bottom finally gives me two clear paths (quiz or selfie) to get matched myself.
- "Customized Coverage" section calls out Skin Tint + Blur Complexion Balm as a layerable combo with a 15% off ribbon — that's a real, specific offer.
- Each staffer names her actual favorite complexion combo, which functions like soft product recommendations without feeling like an ad.

## What's weak

- Four consecutive bio cards with nearly identical layout (photo, quote, match count, combo) is repetitive scrolling before any CTA shows up — I'm not reading all four.
- The subject line quote belongs to Janna, but she's card #2 of 4 — no visual flag or "jump to" link tells me where to find her.
- The only early CTA is a plain black "MATCH ME" button up top with zero context on what happens when I tap it — no mention of the quiz or price.
- No loyalty/points messaging anywhere — as a Rouge/Diamond member juggling two programs, I always want to know if this affects my points math, and ILIA just doesn't play in that space at all, unlike Sephora, which slaps "You have 0 points" on literally everything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut two of the four staffer bios (keep Janna, since she's the subject-line quote, and one more) and move the Match Game quiz/selfie CTA up near the top — right now the real conversion path is buried after a ton of scroll.
- 2. Add a jump-link or highlight treatment so the subject-line quote is visually findable instead of making me scroll to find whose words those are.
- 3. Give the top "MATCH ME" button actual context — "Take the 60-second quiz" instead of a bare command.
- 4. Surface the 15% off Customized Coverage offer higher — that's the only concrete discount in the whole email and it's stuck two-thirds down.
