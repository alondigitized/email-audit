# 30% off ends soon
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the third Glossier Memorial Day sale email in four days. Launch on May 21, the "30% is still on!" reminder on May 23 — which I actually thought was pretty well-executed — and now "30% off ends soon" landing on a Sunday. My gut reaction is: I already know about the sale. I already did my cart math. The question is whether this email tells me anything I didn't know — and actually, buried near the bottom, it does: there's a free Lip Glaze GWP that I did not see in the previous sends, and that's the one thing that almost got me to click.
- The email itself is clean — Glossier's visual language is consistent, the orange CTAs pop, the product grid is well-shot. But the hero is basically copy-paste energy from the May 23 reminder. "There's still time" was the vibe then, and "ends soon" is the same message with a slightly different word order. For a brand that pulled off a graduation Q&A *and* a full pet-UGC email in the same week, this feels like coasting.
- Would I click? Maybe — specifically to the Lip Glaze section, if the GWP threshold stacks with what's already in my cart. Would I forward it to a friend? No, they already have this information. Mark as read unless I'm checking that GWP math.

## What's working

- The free Lip Glaze GWP near the bottom is actually new creative in this campaign — that's a legit reason to revisit the sale even if I've already mentally checked out
- Hero copy leads with the offer, not a brand story — "30% off" lands immediately without excavating meaning
- The "50% OFF" callout mid-grid is the strongest hook in this entire email — that's a deeper cut than the hero 30%, and it made my eye stop scrolling
- Orange "Shop now" CTAs are consistent, tappable, and on-brand; no guessing where to go
- Body Milk shot with the model is gorgeous, great lifestyle anchor for that product

## What's weak

- "Ends soon" with no specific date is my biggest frustration — ILIA's Friends & Family campaign always told me exactly when the clock ran out, and that specificity is why that sequence felt so much more urgent; Glossier is leaving me to guess
- This is structurally near-identical to the May 23 send; anyone who opened that one is getting déjà vu in the hero section, which kills urgency for repeat openers
- "Select favorites" is still doing too much heavy lifting — I don't know if the specific products I want are actually included until I click through and potentially get disappointed
- The free Lip Glaze GWP is buried near the footer — the single most compelling new thing in this email is the last thing I see

## Recommendations

- 1. **Hard deadline, not "ends soon."** "30% off ends tonight at midnight" is infinitely more urgent than "ends soon." Sephora's sale emails anchor the timestamp consistently; Glossier is being weirdly coy about it when specificity is literally free.
- 2. **Lead the GWP in the hero.** The free Lip Glaze is the new news — the 30% off is old news. Flip the hierarchy: "30% off + free Lip Glaze" should be the headline, not a reward for scrollers.
- 3. **Give the 50% off section its own callout moment above the fold.** Whatever is half-off is a better deal than 30% — make it a feature, not a mid-grid label I almost scrolled past.
- 4. **Narrow the product grid to 3-4 editorial picks.** A "grab these before they're gone" editorial framing beats the department store spread. ILIA's curated GWP tiers in "Layers of free gifts" made me feel like someone picked those products for me specifically — Glossier has the brand voice to do the same.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off ends tonight — plus a free Lip Glaze`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 30% off + your free gift is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free Lip Glaze with qualifying order. Offer expires [DATE].`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final call — and yes, there's a free gift this time.`
