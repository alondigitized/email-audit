# 20% off best-sellers ends tonight!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Glossier 20% send in six days, after 10/15 "OUR SURPRISE SALE IS LIVE! ⭐", 10/17 "STILL ON!" and 10/18 "Enjoy 20% off this season's scent". I already know the sale exists. "Ends tonight" is the only new information, and it's the first real deadline in the whole run. That helps, but I've watched this "last chance" beat get stretched for a week, so I don't fully trust it.
- The layout is clean. The hero says 20% off, there's a blue Shop now button, and then a long catalog of five product pairs. It's pretty, but it's a lot of scrolling for one offer. I did a quick math check and still came up with nothing. There's no price, no savings in dollars, and no "what's actually included." Sephora's Savings Event emails at least tell me the tier and the code, and Ulta's 20% coupon spells out the exclusions.
- Would I click? Probably, only if I was already eyeing You Doux or Balm Dotcom. Would I forward it? No. I'd mark it read and maybe grab one lip product before midnight.

## What's working

- "Ends tonight" in the eyebrow and "a few hours left" in the copy give me a real deadline, which the earlier sends lacked.
- The blue "20% OFF" tags sit on the scent images, so the offer is visible before I read any text.
- The sections are labeled Scent, Makeup and Skincare, so I can jump to my category quickly.
- Every product has a name and a short descriptor (Cloud Paint "Seamless color", Balm Dotcom "Nourishing lip balm"), so I know what I'm tapping.
- The free Futuredew Solid at $75+ gives me a threshold to do math against. That's the closest thing to loyalty-style math in here.

## What's weak

- There are no prices anywhere. 20% off a $?? bottle is not something I can calculate, and this is the fourth send without prices.
- The top banner about the free Futuredew Solid is cut off at "$75+ order…". The same offer is repeated near the bottom as "PSSST…".
- The You Doux and You tiles each show the same bottle twice, once as a standard size and once as 100ml. Because of that, the scent block takes up almost a third of the email.
- It's the same 20% pitch as three other emails this week, so "ends tonight" feels like it's crying wolf. ILIA did the same thing with its VIP gifts three times in four days.
- The "Shop now" buttons under each product are pale grey outlines. Next to the blue hero button they read as disabled, and they're small tap targets on a phone.
- There's nothing on tier or points. Nothing says whether 20% stacks with anything, or whether it's for members or everyone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the sale price or the dollar savings on each tile, for example "$24 → $19". That one change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Fix the banner so the $75+ gift offer is fully readable. Drop the duplicate offer block at the bottom or make it a different offer.
- 3. Cut the scent block to two tiles and collapse the duplicate sizes. Lead with the hero products people actually buy (Balm Dotcom, Cloud Paint).
- 4. Add a countdown or an exact cutoff time like "11:59 PM PT" so the deadline feels credible.
- 5. Make the product CTAs solid and tappable, matching the hero button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 20% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Balm Dotcom + Cloud Paint, 20% off till midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus a free Futuredew Solid when you spend $75+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale prices on You Doux, Boy Brow and more. Ends 11:59 PM PT.`
