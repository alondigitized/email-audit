- I don't even need to open my eyes all the way for this one — it's Skechers again, and the subject line "This style has limited availability!" is retargeting me on a grey men's slip-in I never once looked at, let alone put in a cart. "Seen this before? We thought so" is honestly the funniest self-own I've gotten from this brand — they're basically admitting they know this is a repeat and sending it anyway.

## What's weak

- Every single product shown — the hero, the six-item grid, all of it — is men's or boys'. I am a 29-year-old woman who has never bought Skechers, and this doesn't even attempt to guess my size or gender.
- "Seen this before? We thought so" openly admits this is a recycled retarget, which reads as lazy rather than clever when you're on the fourth or fifth near-identical Skechers send this month.
- Zero personalization — no name, no category signal, no acknowledgment I'm not a repeat Skechers shopper at all.
- The "2 for $100" banner is buried below two full grids of irrelevant shoes — by the time I'd scroll there I've already checked out.

- 2. If Skechers insists on continuing, lead with the $2-for-$100 sale as the hero, not a random grey slip-in nobody asked about — that's the only offer here with real urgency.
