# Up To 45% OFF Starts NOW! ☀️🌴⛱️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- By my count this is CT email eight (at minimum) since June 11, and I say that upfront because context matters. That said — 45% off actually made me pause in a way that none of the "Unreal Summer" sends this month have. I've been watching CT run the same "Unreal Icon" drumbeat for two weeks, so when a real discount number finally lands, it registers. The Iconic Kits range isn't cheap — we're talking Filmstar Bronze & Glow, Pillow Talk palettes, products that sit at $80+. Nearly half off is actual money.
- But here's where they lose me: the App Early Access gating is doing double duty as both a mechanic AND the visual anchor, and it reads like ticker tape malfunction more than an aspirational perk. The "App Early Access* App Early Access*" repeating marquee at the very top is chaotic. Compare that to how Sephora handles Rouge Early Access — you just get it because of your tier, no extra install required. CT is trading me a sale for an app download, and I notice.
- The GWP tiers (1, 2, 3 free gifts) are structured and I appreciate that, but the asterisk count in this email is reaching critical mass. Iconic Kits*, gifts with purchase*, App Early Access* — at some point the fine print hedging undercuts the headline entirely. I'd click for the 45%, but I'm going to charlottetilbury.com in a separate tab first to see if the deal actually requires the app or just "opens earlier" there.

## What's working

- 45% off is the highest number CT has swung all month — after seven "Unreal" sends, this reads like a real event, not a repackaged launch
- GWP tiers (1/2/3 free gifts at escalating spend levels) are structured in a way I can actually process at a glance; I like knowing what threshold unlocks what
- The CT gold-and-peach packaging imagery is gorgeous and renders cleanly — no broken images, no layout weirdness, the product photography is doing exactly what it needs to
- "GOLDEN GODDESS GLOW" gets its own callout section below the kit sale, which signals a newer launch without burying it — smart hierarchy
- ORDER NOW button is clearly visible with no ambiguity about where to go

## What's weak

- The "App Early Access* App Early Access*" marquee at the top is visual chaos — it reads like a render error before it reads like a perk
- Asterisks on literally everything erode trust in the headline number; by the fourth asterisk I'm mentally discounting the offer
- Zero Sephora or Ulta angle — I would buy CT at either retailer to stack my Rouge or Diamond points, and this email is asking me to go direct-to-CT with no acknowledgment that I'm leaving points on the table
- Cadence fatigue is eating the urgency — "Starts NOW" doesn't land when CT has said some version of "NOW" to me seven times since June 11
- The hero doesn't tell me which specific kits qualify for 45% off; I have to click blind to find out if the ones I actually want are in the sale

## Recommendations

- 1. Name at least one specific kit in the hero — "Filmstar Bronze & Glow Kit: 45% Off Today" converts faster than "ICONIC KITS*" with an asterisk doing heavy lifting
- 2. Replace the repeating marquee with a single clean "App Members Shop First" badge on the CTA button — one line, not a ticker
- 3. Show the actual free gift products visually in the GWP tier section — "1 FREE GIFT" with no image or product name is a missed conversion moment; Drunk Elephant's GWP emails always show me exactly what I'm getting
- 4. Make the GWP spend thresholds bigger — they're barely readable in the current layout and that math is what closes the deal for me
- 5. If this sale is CT.com/app only, own the trade-off explicitly: "These kits don't ship through Sephora — this is the one place to get them at 45% off." Give me a reason to skip the points, not a buried asterisk

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your CT Summer Sale Starts Now: Up to 45% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Filmstar, Pillow Talk & more — plus up to 3 free gifts with purchase`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `App members shop first. 3 free gifts at $140. Sale ends soon.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues


### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag:**
```html
<title></title>
```
Some clients (Gmail web, Outlook 365) surface the title tag in UI chrome. Should contain a meaningful value matching the subject line.

**Linked logo has empty `alt` attribute:**
```html
<img ... alt="" title="">
```
The logo is wrapped in an `<a>` tag. For screen readers, a linked image with `alt=""` will be announced as the raw URL. WCAG 2.1 (SC 1.1.1) requires a text alternative; minimum fix is `alt="Charlotte Tilbury"`.

**External font CSS with no load fallback:**
```html
<link href="https://www.charlottetilbury.com/static/email/fonts.css" ...>
```
Wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>` (correct). However, if the origin is unreachable at render time, custom fonts silently drop to client defaults — acceptable only if the font stack inside `fonts.css` includes proper system-font fallbacks.

**MSO/VML conditional comments:** present and correctly structured. Responsive breakpoint at `620px` is standard.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Auth Headers)

**Physical address and unsubscribe link: unverifiable** — HTML is truncated before the footer. These are mandatory under CAN-SPAM (15 U.S.C. § 7704) and PECR. Auditor must confirm the full source includes both.

**Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not assessable from HTML source alone. Should be verified via raw message headers (look for `dkim=pass`, `spf=pass`, `dmarc=pass` in `Authentication-Results`).
