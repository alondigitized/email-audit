# PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that I'd already seen this email, because it's the third Sephora app push in about 48 hours. The 11/21 "Cyber Week daily deals just dropped exclusively on the app 👀" send had the same purple sequin background and the same 11/23–11/30 calendar. This one swaps in "30% OFF Color Wow" as the hero. The number is huge and impossible to miss. But Color Wow is a hair-tools brand, and I don't buy it, so the hero does nothing for me. The 11/21 Benefit hero at least hit my category.
- The best thing in here is the free sample bag banner at the very top with code BAGITFIRST. The catch is the "$95 min spend" in tiny type next to the black circle. I did the math immediately. $95 is a real threshold for a free tote of minis, and I'd only chase it if I was already building a Rouge-tier haul. The calendar grid does the job I like from Sephora. It tells me Fenty Beauty on 11/27 and Drunk Elephant on 11/26 and 11/28. That lets me plan my stock-up around the days I care about.
- Would I click? Yes, once, to check the calendar and the sample bag. I wouldn't forward it. Mostly I'd mark it read and wait for the Fenty day.

## What's working

- "30% OFF" in giant white type on purple is read in half a second. Ulta's Black Friday tier ladder (50/40/30) lays out more of the sale, but this one wins on punch.
- The 11/23–11/30 calendar tells me which brands drop each day, so I can plan around Fenty Beauty and Drunk Elephant.
- The free sample bag banner at the top gives me a reason to open the app. It's a clear perk with a code.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." block adds a second concrete offer, 25% off Sol de Janeiro through 11/30, with a date and a visible shop link.
- The footer perk rows (free shipping, Sephora Credit Card 25% off, build your Loves list) show Sephora knows who I am.

## What's weak

- The hero is Color Wow, which is irrelevant to my skincare and makeup habits. It reads like whichever brand paid for the placement.
- The "$95 min spend" and "exclusions/terms apply" on the sample bag banner are tiny. The "FREE" headline is bigger than the condition, which feels a bit bait-and-switch.
- It's the third near-identical app email in 48 hours, after 11/21 and the earlier daily-deal send. The sequin look is the same every time, so the cadence feels spammy.
- Nothing is tied to my Rouge tier. No points multiplier, no Beauty Insider event date, no "your tier gets X". I did the points math in my head and got nothing back.
- The email is very long. The Merit tile, the "Minis for the Holidays" block and the gift guide all dilute the one offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sample bag or a Rouge-specific perk, such as double points on a deal day, and put the $95 threshold in the headline, not the fine print.
- 2. Personalize the hero by category. I'd get Fenty or skincare, and a haircare shopper would get Color Wow.
- 3. Cut the lower blocks (Merit, Minis, gift guide) and keep the calendar plus one secondary offer.
- 4. Add tier math, such as "Rouge: 2x points on Cyber Week daily deals".
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Color Wow + your free sample bag (app only)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Cyber Week calendar: Fenty 11/27, Drunk Elephant 11/28`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $95 on the app, get a free sample bag with code BAGITFIRST.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Eight days of brand drops. Here's which day your favorites go on sale.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "PSA 📣 Cyber Week starts early on the app!"

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a 700px fixed layout and Epsilon/Sephora redirect tracking. The main confirmed issues are a missing plain-text part (0 chars), a zoom-blocking viewport tag, and malformed CSS in the `@media` block. The HTML source was truncated, so the body links and footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links go through the redirect domain `app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a203b408c0a962606e96c65848/...?__dU__=...`. The final destinations and UTM parameters can't be seen in the captured source.
- The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 26. It reported no probe failures, but the 26 skipped links are unverified.
- The hidden open-tracking pixel is `<img src='https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...' style="display:none; ... mso-hide: all"/>`. It has no `width` or `height` attributes. This is low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). Clients that honor the tag will not let users zoom.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments are not valid in CSS, and parsers may drop or mis-parse the rules that follow it. Those rules include `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon` and `.mobileDisc`.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper uses `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">`. Below 700px this depends entirely on the 480px media query. Clients that strip `<style>` blocks will get horizontal scrolling.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index" />` should be `noindex`. This is low severity.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>`. Low severity.
- **Missing alt (QA warning):** the flagged image is the hidden tracking pixel, so it has no real accessibility impact. Adding `alt=""` would still clear the warning. Other images were not visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens were found in the visible source.

The JSON-LD block has `"discountCode": ""` (empty). The preheader advertises `BAGITFIRST`, so the structured data is inconsistent with it. The block also uses `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a standard schema.org type. Most clients ignore `<script>` blocks, so the impact is low.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header** and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this can't be confirmed as a sender-side defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so it should be verified against the raw message.
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** A missing text/plain alternative is a deliverability and accessibility risk. It could also be a capture artifact, since the relay may drop MIME parts.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is not present.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com` are unknown from this data.
- The footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) was truncated, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The destination URLs and UTM parameters are hidden behind the redirect tokens and the source was truncated. The preheader code `BAGITFIRST` could not be checked against any landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a text/plain alternative with the same content and links.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on the original message instead of the relay copy.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
4. Move the `<!--[if mso]>` block out of the `@media` rule, or wrap it in its own `<style>` block.
5. Change `no index` to `noindex`, or drop the tag. Also fix the empty `discountCode` and the invalid `PromotionCard` type, or remove the JSON-LD.
6. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to the tracking pixel.
7. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the unsubscribe probe result, so the footer, UTM parameters and landing pages can be checked.
