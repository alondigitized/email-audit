# Kamo plus one
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Drunk Elephant email in a while that made me want a product. The Kamo hero is clean, and the three "full coverage / tinted moisturizer / concealer" use cases are useful education. But the actual offer is buried in the bottom third, and the subject "Kamo plus one" doesn't hint at it. The 10/9 "Before the fall…" send finally gave me price bubbles. This one goes back to hiding the math under a block of fine print.
- The gift is a free pouch plus a B-Hydra deluxe sample when I buy Kamo and a qualifying moisturizer, valid 10/10 to 10/17. It's a real offer, but I had to scroll past the whole tutorial to find it. There's no price, no savings figure and no mention of Drunk Elephant's rewards program. I can't tell whether this beats my Sephora Rouge points or an Ulta 21 Days markdown. I'd click Shop Kamo out of curiosity and probably wouldn't buy this week. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The hero line "Mix it. Build it. Make it your own." is clear, and the green-and-white product stack is striking and on brand.
- The three-step usage education (full coverage, tinted moisturizer, undereye concealer) answers my real question: how do I use a drop-style cover product?
- The models show deeper and lighter skin tones. The swatch swirl in the middle suggests a shade range, which matters for a base product.
- The CTA button appears twice and is easy to tap.
- The pouch gift is a fun, collectible incentive. It reads as a gift-with-purchase, not a discount.

## What's weak

- The offer sits at the very bottom, and I only see it after the tutorial. Ulta would have put "free gift" in the hero.
- No prices and no value figure. Compare the 10/4 "A flood of hydration" send, which had a "$90 / $143" burst. I can't do points math without numbers.
- The fine print is a dense, tiny, centered paragraph. "Pick your own pouch day!" is cute, but the real terms (qualifying moisturizers, 10/17 deadline, while supplies last) are hard to read on a phone.
- The subject "Kamo plus one" is vague, and the "plus one" gift doesn't show up until the bottom.
- There's no shade finder and no skin-type personalization. A tinted product with no shade-matching nudge feels like a miss. Sephora at least has Color IQ and shade filters.
- There's no member or loyalty tie-in, so I'm treated like a stranger.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the gift offer into the hero: "Buy Kamo + a moisturizer, get a free pouch + B-Hydra sample." Put the tutorial below it.
- 2. Add the price and a value callout, like the bubbles on the 10/9 send. For example: "$XX Kamo + $XX Protini, $XX pouch free."
- 3. Add a shade-finder CTA ("Find your shade") next to Shop Kamo.
- 4. Make the deadline prominent ("Ends 10/17") and move it out of the fine print.
- 5. Link the product CTAs to specific products, such as the Kamo shade selector and the qualifying moisturizers, and tie in the rewards program.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free pouch + B-Hydra when you add Kamo`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kamo Cover Drops: your gift is inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy Kamo + a moisturizer, pick your pouch. Ends 10/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Full coverage, tinted moisturizer or concealer. One bottle, plus a gift.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout sent through a Marketing Cloud-style ESP (`image.cheers.drunkelephant.com`) with Movable Ink and Everest tracking. The confirmed problems are the recipient email address in a tracking URL, three non-HTTPS images, and missing `lang` and alt attributes. The QA run had a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The truncated HTML and the skipped link probes limit what could be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Email address in a URL:** the Everest tracking URL carries the recipient address in plain text: `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…`. The address will appear in third-party server logs, referrers and proxy caches.
- **Unverified links:** 12 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are not confirmed.
- **Mixed content:** three footer icons load over `http://`:
  - `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-c9bb-4057-b5d7-62f3bec2c521.png` (Facebook)
  - `…/9b452915-64bf-4b29-b08b-c4a42802457a.png` (Instagram)
  - `…/29f975e1-6f94-429c-ae2b-8ee6f90786cb.png` (TikTok)
  
  Clients that don't proxy images may block them or show a security warning. The same host is likely to serve HTTPS, so this is probably a template-level fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on tracking images:**
  - `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_kamoprotinirese`
  - the Everest image at `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/`
  
  These look like tracking pixels. If so, `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` would keep screen readers from announcing the URL.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" …>` has no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **External font dependencies:** fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com` through `<link>` tags and `@import`. Gmail and Outlook strip or ignore both, and the font stack depends on a third-party host. A web-safe fallback after `BrownRegular`, `BrownBold` and the other custom families is needed. The visible excerpt doesn't show whether one exists.
- **Malformed CSS:** HTML comment markers (`<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->`) sit inside the `@media (max-width: 600px)` block. CSS parsers drop those rules. That is harmless here because they were commented out on purpose, but it is brittle.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address is populated in the Everest URL, so the issue there is exposure rather than a failed merge.

- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is expected through the relay and isn't a confirmed defect.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML is truncated, so neither could be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
