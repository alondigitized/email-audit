# Save 30% on ALL Sephora Collection 💄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the sixth Sephora send in about 72 hours with the same 30% Sephora Collection event, and it's the first one in a while where the 30% badge at the very top actually does some work. The "Happening now" banner has the medallion, the 10/30–11/9 dates and "Use code ALLONSALE." Then I scroll one screen and the email turns into a Jo Malone holiday gift guide. The offer I came for is gone. The subject is about Sephora Collection, and the body is about Jo Malone, Merit, Dieux and Gisou.
- I do like that the second half has real product with real prices, like the Merit set at $68 ($92 value) and the L'Occitane advent calendar at $110 ($186 value). It reads like a proper holiday edit. But it isn't what the subject promised, and I'd bounce between the two stories. I'd probably tap the banner, check the Sephora Collection pricing, and be done. I wouldn't forward it. If a friend wanted holiday gifts I'd send her the site directly.

## What's working

- The banner opens on the 30% medallion, the dates (10/30–11/9) and a visible code, so the offer is clear in the first second.
- Real prices with "value" anchors ($68 vs $92, $110 vs $186, $25 vs $42). I can do points math on that.
- The "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA · LIMITED EDITION" tags tell me what's exclusive. I like launch-day clarity, and that's the kind of thing I stock up on.
- The Jo Malone block ("scent of cardamom, vanilla and amber") is evocative and gives a clear gift angle.
- The barcode at the bottom for in-store use is handy, since I often shop at Kohl's Sephora.

## What's weak

- The subject says 30% Sephora Collection, but nothing below the banner shows a Sephora Collection product. That's a bait-and-switch feel.
- The "WOW-WORTHY GIFTS" hero and its SHOP NOW button push me off the sale before I can even read the code.
- Too many competing CTAs: Jo Malone, Spot It Shop It, Lip Gift Sets, Skincare Gift Sets, and the Holiday Gift Guide row. There's no single hierarchy.
- The Beauty Insider tier is invisible. As Rouge I'd want to see my points or perks, and there's a generic "Sign up" strip at the bottom instead.
- The fine print is tiny and dense. The 30% terms and the Nov 3–9 10% drop are buried in the final barcode block, so it's not very honest.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Sephora Collection sale the real hero. Show 4–6 Sephora Collection products with sale prices, and put the Jo Malone block underneath.
- 2. Add one line about tier benefits, e.g. "Rouge: earn points on top of 30% off."
- 3. Put the 10% drop after 11/9 in the visible banner rather than the footer.
- 4. Cut the gift-guide sections down to one or two so the email has one story.
- **Subject Alt A:** `30% off Sephora Collection ends 11/9 💄`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Code ALLONSALE: 30% off, plus new Jo Malone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lip, eye and skin staples, all 30% off through 11/9`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus limited-edition holiday sets only at Sephora`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All visible links go through the redirect wrapper `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a…?__dU__=…`. Final destinations aren't visible in the truncated source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 43. No probe failures are listed, so no broken links are confirmed.
- Two tracking pixels are present:

  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=…&mi_ecmp=20261101_Holiday_Showstoppers_BI` is a second open or analytics pixel.
- Running two pixel systems is redundant but not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA WARN):** both pixel images above have no `alt`. Impact is low because they are non-content images. Adding `alt=""` would be correct.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Fixed-width layout:** the wrapper uses `width:700px; min-width:700px`. The only mobile handling is a `max-width:480px` media query, and the table won't shrink on narrow clients that ignore it.
- **Malformed CSS:** `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->` sits inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. Conditional comments don't work there, so the rule is likely ignored or breaks parsing of the following rules.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This has no effect in email clients.
- **JSON-LD:** the `DiscountOffer` has an empty `"discountCode": ""`, and the `PromotionCard` block has no properties. Most clients strip the script, and Gmail may ignore it as incomplete.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The preheader renders "Sephora Shopper, now's the time to stock up." as a resolved generic fallback, and no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. I can't check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 0 chars.** A missing text/plain part hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility. It's unconfirmed whether the original send had one or the relay dropped it.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the relay may strip these. This can't be confirmed from the data, but it's required for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the stated cause.
- The physical address and footer unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated HTML, so they're unverified. QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible source. Attribution is carried by the opaque `__dU__` token and the `mi_ecmp=20261101_Holiday_Showstoppers_BI` campaign tag.
- Landing-page alignment can't be assessed because the redirect targets weren't resolved or shown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-capture the raw MIME with headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the text/plain part. Then reclassify the FAIL and WARNs as real or artifacts.
