# 😍 Oh. My. Minis. 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the subject is cute, but the offer that matters sits in a thin white banner above the hero. The mini holiday sets get all the space. The top strip is "Choose a Trial Size," a free Korean-skincare sample with a $40 purchase and code KBEAUTY. It's a real perk, but it's tiny and wrapped in a dozen asterisks. The big blue "Mini Sets for the Holidays" hero is just a catalog shot with no offer. I can't tell if the email wants me to shop minis or chase a freebie.
- As a Rouge, I also noticed the header says "You have 0 points." That's not my balance, and for someone who does points math daily it reads like a template that doesn't know me. I got "Open for new items" twice yesterday, and the sample bag email on 10/21 was a repeat send too. Today's email feels like the same Sephora catalog with a new hat.
- Would I click? Probably once, for the Laneige Glaze Craze lip minis ($26 for a $36 value), and then I'd bounce to Ulta to compare. I wouldn't forward it. I'd only text a friend if she were already hunting for stocking stuffers.

## What's working

- The Laneige lip set is a strong hero: "$26 ($36 value)" gives me the price and the savings in one line, and a 3-pack under $30 is a good gift.
- Every minis tile has a price and a value (Saie $32/$51, Tower 28 $20/$32). I can do the per-ounce math without leaving the email.
- The "NEW • LIMITED EDITION" and "ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags tell me quickly what's a launch and what's exclusive. That's the launch clarity I want. The Clean and Planet Aware badges are a nice touch.
- The Beauty Insider tier bar at the bottom (Insider / VIB / Rouge) plus "Rewards Bazaar" is a good reminder of where my points go.
- The layout renders cleanly. I see no broken images or overlapping text, and the product grid scans easily.

## What's weak

- The "You have 0 points" header and the tier bar with "INSIDER" highlighted don't match my Rouge status. It treats me like a stranger, and Ulta at least greets me as Diamond.
- There are too many competing offers: trial size with $40, free samples with $25+, Halloween Beauty Services, Gifts Under $50, and the minis. I can't find the one thing to do.
- The free trial size banner is cramped, and the code and spend minimum are in fine print. It's a gift-with-purchase buried under a hero with no offer.
- There's no deadline anywhere. Nothing says when the code ends or when the minis might sell out, so there's no reason to click today.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." block with Halloween services on 10/23 feels late. Haus Labs "Free Samples" sits right next to the trial-size banner, so it looks like the same freebie twice.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the Laneige $26 set and add a clear member perk, like "Rouge: free shipping + 2x points on minis." Move the KBEAUTY trial size into a slim secondary strip.
- 2. Personalize the header to my tier and real point balance, and show something like "You're 340 points from a Rewards Bazaar perk." Hiding "0 points" would be a start.
- 3. Add a deadline and a stock cue, like "Limited-edition minis, while they last." Glossier's "Want a free gift?" and e.l.f.'s "$12" hero both lead with the offer in one line. Do that here.
- 4. Cut the Halloween services and Haus Labs blocks, or put them in a different send. Drunk Elephant's four 15%-off emails in five days made me tune out, and Sephora is heading that way with duplicate sends.
- 5. Make CTAs specific: "Shop Laneige minis" instead of "Shop Now," and "Claim your trial size" instead of "Shop to Redeem."
