# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that I know points = rewards. I'm Rouge, so a "did you know?" subject talks to me like a first-year Insider. The email is the Rewards Bazaar drop, though, and that's the one I do the math on. I almost respect it for that. The 10/5 Glossier/LANEIGE early-access send and both of today's 2X/5X sends already made me scroll past a points banner to find the real thing. This one at least puts points first.
- The structure is the issue. At the very top there's a "Don't Miss Out on up to 500 Points*" hero with a JOIN THE CHALLENGE button. Right below it is a second hero, "October rewards are here!", with a SHOP NOW button. Two heroes, two CTAs, and neither is the subject's promise. After that it's a roughly 7,800-pixel scroll of Laura Mercier and 750+ point rewards, each with a point cost and a "# available now" count. That's what I wanted, and it's buried.
- Would I click? Yes, but only because I'm already trying to figure out whether 750 points on a Laura Mercier setting powder is worth it. Would I forward it? No. I'd screenshot the Caviar Clutch at 4,500 points and ask myself why that's not a better deal than the 1,000-point Mini Artistry Kit.

## What's working

- Every reward shows a point cost and a live inventory count ("250 available now," "12 available now," "1 available now"). It's honest scarcity and it's the points math I actually do.
- The Laura Mercier brand spotlight is a real prestige name I'd spend points on. The 750 to 4,500 ladder gives me options for my tier.
- The "750+ point rewards" section with Sienna Naturals, Josie Maran, Dr. Dennis Gross, Davines and Moroccanoil mixes brands well. Moroccanoil is a good touch.
- The tier bar at the bottom (Insider / VIB / Rouge) is a nice nod to my status, and the app download link is easy to find.
- Nothing is broken. The images load and the text is readable without zooming.

## What's weak

- Two stacked heroes (the 500-point challenge, then the Bazaar) fight each other, and the subject line promises neither.
- "Did you know?" is beginner copy. It's the opposite of personalization for someone who's been in Rouge for years.
- It's the fourth Sephora send in two days (10/5 early access, then both 2X/5X sends today), all with a points banner on top. The cadence is exhausting.
- The fine print is dense and tiny at the bottom, and the asterisks on the "up to 500 points" and free shipping lines send me down there to decode it. Ulta puts "20% off" in giant orange and lets me see the exclusions, which feels more honest.
- A long grid of rewards with no clear winner. The "Evening in New York City" at 35,000 points with 1 available is aspirational, not useful, and it ends up right in the middle of the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with the Bazaar and fold the 500-point challenge into a smaller strip. Put the best-value reward above the fold, not the Laura Mercier kit at 250 available.
- 2. Personalize it. Show my current point balance, or "You have enough for X," in the hero. Ulta's "Marisol, earn points that turn into $ off" is already doing this better.
- 3. Add a "best value per point" or "new this month" tag so I'm not doing all the math myself. Glossier's holiday guide has no math at all, so it's easy to beat that, but I'd expect Sephora to lead here.
- 4. Cut the scroll. Five rewards per section, then a "see all rewards" button.
- 5. Say what the deadline is. "They go fast" isn't credible urgency, but an end date for the October rewards would be.

- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards start at 750 points. Limited quantities, so scroll early.`
