# Save 15% with the Sally app
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Sally pivoting again — five different angles out of my inbox in about a week now (confetti teaser 7/30, the actual COLORfest launch 8/3, a shipping/BOPIS detour 7/31, "Best Hair Color" 8/6, and now... an app download pitch with zero mention of COLORfest at all). The 15% off headline is bold and easy to read, but the second I clip through it I realize the "offer" is actually "download our app and maybe you'll get 15% off," which is a bait I don't love — I already have Sephora, Ulta, and a dozen other apps fighting for home-screen real estate, and Sally hasn't earned that slot with a hair-color-only sale cadence.
- The lifestyle shot of the girl mid-selfie with her phone is fine but generic — no product in her hand, no color result, nothing that tells me what I'm actually buying. It reads more like a fintech app ad than a beauty retailer email. Compare that to e.l.f. x Bubble, which put three actual co-branded products front and center and scored an 8 from me — Sally gives me a phone and a "Download now" button instead.
- Verdict: I'm not downloading an app for a maybe-15%-off that "expires in 30 days" per fine print I'd have to squint at. I'd skim past the hero, maybe glance at the Klarna line since I'm always doing the math on financing vs. points, and mark as read. Not forwarding this one — there's nothing here worth telling a friend about.

## What's working

- The "Get 15% off any $35+ purchase" headline is genuinely big and legible — no zooming needed.
- Sally Rewards' "$5 Reward in September" mechanic is at least transparent points math, which I always appreciate — Ulta's 20%-off-one-item coupon (the one I've now seen three sends running) doesn't even try this hard on loyalty framing.
- The icon row (In-Store, Online, Free Shipping $40+, 2-Hour Delivery, BOPIS) is a clean, scannable fulfillment summary — nice utility touch.
- Klarna's "4 interest-free payments" is called out clearly with its own little tile instead of buried in legal copy.

## What's weak

- The whole email is an app-download ad wearing a discount-offer costume — the CTA isn't "shop hair color," it's "Download now," which is a much bigger ask than a click.
- Zero connection to the COLORfest campaign Sally's been running all week — after four sends building toward it, this one just drops the thread entirely. Feels like two different teams are emailing me.
- The hero model holds a phone, not a product — no hair color, no swatch, no texture shot. I have no idea what I'd even be buying with that 15% off.
- The real terms ("not valid with other promotions or pro discount," 30-day expiration) are stuffed into tiny disclaimer text under the photo, not anywhere near the headline.
- No personalization at all — no name, no "you last bought X," nothing that treats me like the Rewards member I apparently already am (my email's literally in the footer).

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a product, not the app.** Swap the phone-selfie hero for an actual hair color or nail product tied to the 15% off — give me something to want before you ask me to download anything.
- 2. **Decouple the discount from the app install.** If 15% off requires downloading the app, say so in the headline, not three lines down — "Download the app, get 15% off" reads as a bait-and-switch when the hero just says "Get 15% off."
- 3. **Pick a lane on the campaign.** Either keep pushing COLORfest through the finish line or don't — bouncing from confetti to shipping to hair color to app-download in one week is exactly the kind of cadence fatigue that makes me tune brands out.
- 4. **Surface the loyalty math higher.** The "$5 Reward in September" line is buried two sections down — for someone like me who's always doing points math, that should live closer to the top.
- 5. **Add a real subject-line hook tied to a category**, not just the app.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off hair color — no app required through 8/9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rewards math: shop twice, earn $5 in Sept`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New color, same cart — 15% off $35+ inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Skip the app. Here's your 15% off code.`
