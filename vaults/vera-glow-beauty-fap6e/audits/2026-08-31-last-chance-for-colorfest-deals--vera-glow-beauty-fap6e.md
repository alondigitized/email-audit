# Last chance for COLORfest deals ⏳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Sally, again, and it's the exact confetti-dot COLORfest send I've now seen more than ten times since mid-July — I don't even need to open my brand history to know this, I could describe the layout from memory: teal-purple mermaid hair model, black "Shop deals" pill, pink rewards banner underneath. The only thing that's actually moved is the deadline language, from "Don't Miss" to "Last Day," and a genuinely new mechanic wedged into the top banner — 15% off buy-online-pickup-in-store, ends 8/31 — that I almost missed because it's crammed above the fold in tiny red-bar text before the hero even loads.
- This isn't a beauty brand I stock up at like Sephora or Ulta — it's a hair-color/DIY-nails destination I use maybe twice a year — so "final call" urgency doesn't move me the way it would for a Sephora Beauty Insider event countdown. I'm marking this read, not clicking. Not forwarding either; nobody in my group chat is doing at-home box color right now.

## What's working

- The "LAST DAY" marquee ticker actually reads as real urgency for once, versus Sally's usual vague "ends soon."
- Model's got vivid teal-and-purple color-melt hair, which is on-brand for a hair-color retailer and at least visually distinct from Sephora/Ulta's usual polished skincare close-ups.
- The BOPIS 15% banner is a genuinely different lever than the sitewide COLORfest discount stacked right below it — that's two offers, not the same one repackaged.
- Klarna financing callout and the five-icon shipping/pickup row are clean and legible, no clutter.

## What's weak

- I still can't find an actual percentage or dollar figure for the COLORfest "sitewide" deal in the hero itself — just "Shop deals" — Ulta's "20% off is calling" banner tells me the number before I even open the email.
- Two competing offers (15% BOPIS in the top strip, COLORfest deals in the hero, $5 reward below) with no single clear hero CTA — my eye doesn't know which one to act on first.
- Zero personalization — no name, no "you bought hair color in June" callback, nothing like Ulta's "Marisol, ENDS TODAY!" subject-line name-drop.
- This is the same confetti-dot hero art I've now logged since mid-July; at some point "last day" stops feeling credible when the "last day" keeps happening.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and put the actual discount number in giant type — right now three offers (BOPIS 15%, COLORfest, $5 reward) compete and none of them win.
- 2. Retire the confetti-dot COLORfest art after ten-plus sends — even a palette swap would help it not blur into the last five emails in my mind.
- 3. Add a real deadline timestamp ("ends 11:59pm 8/31") near the hero CTA, not just in the fine print at the bottom.
- 4. Personalize with purchase history — if I bought box color in June, tell me it's restock time, the way Drunk Elephant's "cheat sheet" send felt like an actual consultation.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy online, pick up in store, save 15% before midnight.`
