# Hair it is! 💁‍♀️ NEW e.l.f.ing launch
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- This is e.l.f. entity number three in my inbox in roughly two weeks — main e.l.f., then e.l.f. SKIN (which I noted on June 18 was the best send of that stretch), and now e.l.f. Hair. I've been counting because that's the only way I know when a brand family is expanding faster than I can keep up. And honestly? "Hair it is!" made me stop on name recognition alone — a new sub-brand in a category e.l.f. hasn't owned is exactly the kind of launch moment that earns an open from someone who tracks beauty calendars like a part-time job.
- But I opened for hair and got a general e.l.f. makeup digest wearing a hair wig. The hero is genuinely clean — TikTok exclusive bundles, "your bundles, bundled," nice product lineup. And then immediately: "YOUR WEEKLY HIGHLIGHT REEL" with lipsticks, eyeliners, and mascara. Then "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK" with more lip and eye products. The footer tagline at the bottom is literally "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face." No hair. In a hair launch email. That's the whole problem in one line of copy.
- Would I click the TikTok exclusive? Maybe, if I were already in discovery mode — but TikTok Shop is a detour I don't usually take when elfcosmetics.com exists. Would I forward? No, because I'd have to explain the identity crisis. Mark as read, note the new entity, move on.

## What's working

- **e.l.f. Hair as a distinct sender entity is smart** — same playbook as e.l.f. SKIN, and I respect the sub-brand architecture even when the email itself stumbles
- **The hero lands cleanly** — "TikTok exclusive / your bundles, bundled." is concise, the product lineup reads clearly, and the "NEW ARRIVALS" badge gives the launch moment weight
- **Beauty Squad is visible in the footer** — I see my loyalty ecosystem acknowledged, same as any e.l.f. send
- **Zero render bugs** — loaded perfectly, image-to-text ratio is fine, nothing breaking on mobile
- **No spam energy in the subject** — no ALL CAPS stacking, no triple exclamation points, e.l.f.ing pun stays on-brand

## What's weak

- **~80% of the body is a makeup digest** — "YOUR WEEKLY HIGHLIGHT REEL" and "HOT AS E.L.F. THIS WEEK" are straight lipstick-and-liner grids that have nothing to do with a hair launch; this reads like two emails duct-taped together
- **The tagline undercuts the entire send** — "e.l.f. is for every eye, lip, and face" is the *main brand* tagline, and running it verbatim in an e.l.f. Hair launch email is a copy-paste miss that a real subscriber notices
- **No actual launch offer** — Charlotte Tilbury hit 45% off and named it in the subject; Glossier built a whole urgency arc around 25% off for 13 days; e.l.f. Hair gets zero discount, zero event pricing, zero loyalty launch perk — just "here's a thing that exists on TikTok"
- **TikTok exclusive = platform friction** — this gates the hero offer behind a separate app and a commerce flow I don't use; Sephora opens cleaner on their landing page and never forces a platform detour for the primary offer
- **Category navigation at the bottom lists HAIR last** — it's the whole point of the email and it's the fifth item in a de-prioritized text nav row

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the entire body hair** — cut the makeup digest entirely; if this is an e.l.f. Hair launch email, show me routine steps, hero hair products with names and benefits, a before/after texture shot. Charlotte Tilbury kept their summer sale focused on three hero SKUs — steal that discipline for the launch moment.
- 2. **Add a parallel shop-on-site path for the TikTok exclusive** — "Shop TikTok-exclusive bundles at elfcosmetics.com/hair" makes the hero accessible without losing the TikTok signal; right now the hero CTA has no obvious fallback.
- 3. **Write a hair-specific tagline** — "e.l.f. is for every strand" or "eye, lip, face — and now hair" would make this feel like an actual brand expansion instead of a retagged digest.
- 4. **Add a launch offer** — free shipping specifically on Hair, or a Beauty Squad early-access price. Sally Beauty named "50% Off It's a 10" in the subject and earned the click; e.l.f. Hair gives me nothing to act on.
- 5. **Move HAIR to first position in the category nav** — it's a launch, not an afterthought.

## Technical Audit — e.l.f. Hair Launch Email

**From:** makeup@m.elfcosmetics.com | **ESP detected:** Sailthru (link pattern) + Braze (image CDN)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound for Outlook/mobile compatibility but contains a cross-platform asset split (Sailthru link tracking, Braze image CDN), a broken `font-family` declaration, and duplicate conflicting Google Fonts imports that add unnecessary HTTP requests.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cross-platform ESP split (high risk)**
- Click links route through `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` — the `/f/a/` path is Sailthru's redirect format
- Header image is served from `braze-images.com` (Braze asset CDN)
- This combination indicates either an active platform migration or a misconfigured send — analytics will be split across two platforms with no unified attribution

**Encoded destination unverifiable**
- The only link in the provided source is a Sailthru-wrapped redirect. The final destination URL is base64-encoded and cannot be confirmed without decoding. UTM parameter carriage through the redirect chain is unauditable from source alone.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Empty `<title>` tag**
```html
<title></title>
```
Screen readers announce the title in some clients (e.g. Apple Mail). An empty title degrades accessibility; should be `<title>e.l.f. Hair — New Launch</title>` or similar.

**Broken `font-family` declaration**
```html
font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif
```
The first value is an empty string literal — not a valid font name. The web font (Jost) never gets applied; the browser falls through to Verdana immediately. Should be `font-family:'Jost',Verdana,sans-serif`.

**Duplicate, conflicting Google Fonts imports**
- `<head>` contains: `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css?family=Jost">` (v1 API, no `display=swap`, no weight variants)
- Body `<style>` block contains: `@import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Jost:ital,wght@0,100..900;1,100..900&display=swap')` (v2 API, full variants)

Two separate HTTP requests for the same font family, different API versions, the `<link>` version lacks `display=swap` (causes FOIT). The `@import` inside body is also ignored by several clients (e.g. Gmail strips `<style>` blocks in body). Net result: font load behavior is undefined across clients.

**Nested `nl-container` structure**
The outer table has `class="nl-container"` and the inner `<div>` in row-1 contains a second complete `nl-container` structure with its own `row row-1`. This nested duplication is likely a template assembly error and may cause double-rendering of the header block in some clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Preheader is hardcoded**
```html
<div style="display:none !important;…">Peep our top picks for you!</div>
```
No merge token — the same preheader text goes to all recipients regardless of segment or behavioral data. No issues flagged beyond this observation (the structure itself is technically correct).

**Preheader padding uses mixed invisible characters**
The filler div uses a mix of `͏` (U+034F combining grapheme joiner) and `‌` (U+200C zero-width non-joiner). Mixed character classes in hidden content can contribute to spam score in conservative filters (SpamAssassin RAZOR/BAYES).

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM / unsubscribe** — Cannot assess from the truncated source. The footer containing the physical address, unsubscribe link, and preference center is not present in the provided HTML. Must be verified in the full source before send.

**Sender domain:** `m.elfcosmetics.com` (subdomain mailer). DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be confirmed from HTML source alone — requires header inspection of a delivered copy. Given Sailthru+Braze split, confirm DKIM signing is on `m.elfcosmetics.com` and not a Braze or Sailthru shared domain.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable** — All links are Sailthru-wrapped. Whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content`) are injected by the Sailthru redirect config cannot be confirmed from source. If UTM injection is not configured in Sailthru's link management settings, GA4/site analytics will attribute all traffic as direct.

**Landing page alignment** — The logo link destination is the only confirmed link in the source fragment; product/CTA links are in the truncated portion and could not be audited.
