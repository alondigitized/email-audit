- But "0 points" is still sitting right under my INSIDER label at the top, same broken spot I've now flagged five times this week (8/15 x4), and I scrolled all the way down expecting the same INSIDER-not-ROUGE arrow mixup — this time the ROUGE tile at the bottom is actually the one shaded dark, which if that's real is the first time this week Sephora's tier bar has synced correctly. ## What's working

- The Medik8/Aesthop/Ulta Violette trial-size hero up top is a concrete, time-relevant offer ($30 minimum, online-only) — better than another vague "beauty forecast" tease.
- Dior Miss Dior + Sauvage front and center with real prices ($65-$231, $95-$232) is launch-caliber curation, not filler.
- "Only at Sephora" badges on Kérastase and Farmacy do real curation signaling — tells me this isn't a carbon copy of what Ulta's running.
- The tier bar at the bottom finally looks like it's showing ROUGE shaded instead of INSIDER — if that's accurate, it's the first fix after four broken sends this week.
- $5 off 250 points redemption block is clean and dated (ends 8/31), so the points math is at least visible even if the header count looks off.

## What's weak

- "0 points" still shows at the very top under my tier badge — this is the fifth time this week I've seen this exact number, and I have thousands of points banked between Sephora and Ulta. It undercuts every other personalization signal in the email.
- This is a long, undifferentiated product grid (Shiseido, Maison Margiela, Prada, Oribe, Touchland, Sulwhasoo) with no "new," no "trending," no reason given for why these ten items are together beyond "cult-fave."
- No skin type/tone filtering anywhere — I'm scrolling past an eye cream and a retinal serum with zero indication these match my profile.
- The "Celebration" in-store events banner is generic — no date, no location pulled from my zip, just "find an event near you."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points counter at the top — it's the same bug across five sends this week and it's the first thing I see every time I open a Sephora email.
- 2. Give the product grid a real organizing principle (e.g. "Editors' new obsessions" or "Trending in your Rouge tier") instead of an unlabeled ten-item wall.
- 3. Layer in skin-type/tone personalization on at least the skincare tiles — Sephora has this data, use it.
- 4. Localize the in-store events banner with my nearest store and an actual date.
