# 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection ends today❗
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 2X/5X points send I got on 10/6, now with "Ends today" in the hero. I'm Rouge, so I opened without thinking. The giant red "2X" and the "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" button make the offer obvious in about two seconds. Then the email keeps going. It's another very long scroll, and I counted a points-per-product grid, a financing strip, a holiday drops teaser, a Sephora Collection advent teaser, a free-sample banner and a Beauty Insider tier bar. It's all points math with no new news.
- I'd click, but only because the deadline is real. I'd use the code, check the Sephora Collection 5X, and probably add a few things I already had in my cart. I wouldn't forward it. My friends got it too, and it's the third touch in two days after "Did you know that points = rewards?" and yesterday's "ends tomorrow" version.

## What's working

- The giant red "2X" with "ENDS TODAY" is the best hierarchy in the email. I know the offer and the urgency before I read a word.
- "USE CODE SAYPOINTS" is a clear black button, and the same code is repeated under every product card. I can paste it at checkout without scrolling back up.
- The per-product points callouts (810 on Tom Ford, 88 on amika, 276 on Versace) do my math for me. That's exactly how I shop.
- The "See What Your Points Can Do" tiers (100, 250, 300, 750+ points) tie spending to rewards, which suits a points-focused person like me.
- The free Same-Day Delivery P.S. with code DELIVERED, plus the sample-pickup banner, gives me a reason to add on.

## What's weak

- It's the 10/6 email with the deadline swapped. I don't need the same hero a third day running.
- The scroll is far too long. The price-and-points grid, Klarna/Afterpay/PayPal, holiday drops, Sephora Collection gift set and sample banner all compete with the one offer.
- The "SHOP NOW, PAY LATER" financing strip and the Cardmember bonus feel like clutter. Most of the page below the grid has nothing to do with "ends today."
- The Beauty Insider tier bar (VIB, Rouge) is tiny and sits at the very bottom. As Rouge I'd like my tier acknowledged near the top, not buried.
- The footer fine print is a wall of text. The 2X is on "your entire purchase," but the exclusions are buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the offer, the code and the Sephora Collection 5X, plus one row of 4 products. Move the rest into other sends.
- 2. Add a personalized tier line near the top, such as "Rouge: here's what 2X earns you on your last cart."
- 3. Show a real countdown with a time zone. "Ends 10/7 at 1pm PT" is a tiny P.S. now. It belongs in the hero.
- 4. Keep the "Use code" line in the sticky position under the hero, not 50 times down the page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 2X points + 5X Sephora Collection, code SAYPOINTS`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge: your 2X points window closes at 1pm PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use SAYPOINTS before the Point Multiplier event wraps tonight. Free same-day delivery with DELIVERED.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `810 points on Tom Ford, 276 on Versace. Here's how your cart stacks up.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with mobile media queries. It has two confirmed deliverability and accessibility defects: a 0-character plain-text part and missing alt attributes on 4 images. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture through the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed as real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Coverage gap:** QA probed 12 of 54 links and skipped 42 (footer, utility and social links were deprioritized). No broken-link results were reported for the probed set, so no link failures are confirmed. The skipped links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`:** both are flagged by QA.
  - `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g...` (open pixel)
  - `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4970293024993920&mi_ecmp=20261007_PME_Stacking_Ends_Today_BI` (open pixel with a per-recipient ID `mi_u` and a campaign ID `mi_ecmp`)
- These pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. Whether they have `width="1" height="1"` can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback:** the text part is 0 chars. A missing `text/plain` part hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients and some assistive tools with nothing to render.
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg`: navigation header image. If it carries link text, that navigation is invisible with images off.
  - `https://images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../2026-10-07_email_format_ded_us_3734_06.jpg`: main promo content image. If the offer text is baked into it, the 2X/5X points message is lost when images are blocked.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text size adjustment:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` and `text-size-adjust: none` are applied to `div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td`. This disables user text scaling on iOS and Android.
- **Invalid robots value:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. This is harmless in email but invalid.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Sephora</title>` is not descriptive.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible (truncated) source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FNAME]`. The only per-recipient value seen is `mi_u=4970293024993920`, which is a resolved ID. The rest of the source was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` missing** and **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) missing.** QA notes these headers may not survive the AgentMail relay. The report can't tell a relay artifact from a real omission. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify against the raw original message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `beauty.sephora.com`, and alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** QA says an unsubscribe link is always included in link probing, but it reports no result for it. The footer is cut off in the truncated source, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address are unconfirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source shows no destination hrefs, so UTM presence and consistency can't be checked. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=20261007_PME_Stacking_Ends_Today_BI` on the pixel. No landing-page probe results were provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a `text/plain` alternative part, since it is currently 0 chars. This is the only confirmed hard failure.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `2021_Navs_Header_06.jpg` and `2026-10-07_email_format_ded_us_3734_06.jpg`. Use `alt=""` on the two tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust: none` where it isn't needed.
4. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If they are present, the QA warnings are relay artifacts. If not, add the headers and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
