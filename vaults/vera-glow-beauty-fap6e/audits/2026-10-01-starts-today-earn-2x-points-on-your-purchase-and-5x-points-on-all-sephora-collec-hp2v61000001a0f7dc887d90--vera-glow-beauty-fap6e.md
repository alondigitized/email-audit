# STARTS TODAY: earn 2X points on your purchase and 5X points on ALL Sephora Collection 😍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the same email I got this morning, again. The subject and hero are identical to the 10/1 send I audited earlier, "STARTS TODAY," with the giant red "2X" over INSIDER. The offer itself is still great, and a points multiplier is the one Sephora event I plan around. Now I'm seeing it twice in one inbox on launch day, and that's the cadence problem. The earlier send already got me, so this one is a duplicate, not a reminder.
- The hero still wins. I can read the offer from the thumbnail: 2X points, 5X on Sephora Collection, code SAVEPOINTS. The "See What Your Points Can Do" ladder (100 / 250 / 500 / 750+ points) is the part I like most, because it does the points math for me. Below that it turns into a long scroll of product tiles, then a hair sale, then Founders Face to Face, then gifting. Hair Faves at "up to 50% off" with a TODAY ONLY badge pulls attention away from the multiplier. That's the same stacking I called out on 9/29 ("From your feed to your cart"), when a hair sale took over a social-themed send.
- Would I click? Yes, but only if I hadn't already. Forward? No. Mark as read? Yes, because I already have the code.

## What's working

- The giant "2X" hero reads in half a second, and I know the event before I read a word.
- The points ladder (100+ for samples, 250 for $5 off, 500 for $10 off, 750+ for experiences) is the points math I'd otherwise do myself.
- Each product tile shows "EARN 810 points" or "EARN 348 points" with the price. Tom Ford at $405 earning 810 points is a real decision, and the tiles use that number.
- The Cardmembers banner (earn an extra point per dollar with the Sephora Credit Card) speaks to someone who tracks tiers, and so does the "USE CODE: SAVEPOINTS" repeated on every tile.
- The P.S. free same-day delivery code, with its Ends 10/7 deadline, adds a second reason to buy now.

## What's weak

- This is a duplicate of the earlier 10/1 send with the same subject. Two copies in one day makes me feel list-pulled, not known.
- The Hair Faves block ("Up to 50% off," TODAY ONLY) competes with the 2X offer, and the deadlines don't match (today vs 10/7).
- The body is very long. The Spot it. Shop it., Founders Face to Face, New & Under $50 and Gifting blocks dilute the points event.
- The legal footer is tiny and endless. The point-multiplier fine print is exactly what I'd need to read, and I can't without zooming.
- It's too generic for a Rouge member. There's no tier callout, no "your points balance," and nothing based on my past purchases.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send the same creative twice in a day. If it has to re-send, change the subject and hero to "last chance" or a reminder, and suppress it for people who already opened the first one.
- 2. Cut the body to the points event: the ladder, the top picks, and the cardmember bonus. Move Hair Faves, Founders, and Gifting to a separate send.
- 3. Personalize the hero for tier, e.g. "Rouge: here's your 2X math." Show my balance and what the multiplier gets me.
- 4. Make the fine print readable. Put the multiplier cap and exclusions in a short block above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2X points starts today: use code SAVEPOINTS`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge, your 2X points week is live 💄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `5X on all Sephora Collection through 10/7. Here's what your points buy.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tom Ford earns 810 points. See the top picks before they sell out.`
