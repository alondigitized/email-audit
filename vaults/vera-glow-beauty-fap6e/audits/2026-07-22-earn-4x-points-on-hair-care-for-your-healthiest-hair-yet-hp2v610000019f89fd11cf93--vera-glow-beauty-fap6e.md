# Earn 4X points on hair care for your healthiest hair yet ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- The email itself? Actually solid. Sephora went with a real headline — "Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science." — which is a step above their usual "Shop hair care" filler, and the product grid organized by hair concern (dry, frizzy, thinning) is exactly how I navigate an actual hair care aisle. The 4X points graphic lands where it should, and the layout doesn't fight you.
- Here's the problem: I reviewed a virtually identical email from Sephora with this exact subject line earlier today. Same "Earn 4X points on hair care" hook, same hero creative, different barcode in the footer. This follows two "All your must-haves have arrived" duplicates yesterday and two "Open for our bag edit" duplicates the day before. At this point Sephora's CRM is clearly misfiring on a loop, and I've trained myself to tune out anything with "4X hair care" in the subject because it just keeps showing up.
- I would not click. I already processed this email once today. Marking it read, and I'm genuinely one more duplicate away from hitting unsubscribe on a brand I actually love.

## What's working

- **"Healthy Hair Isn't Luck, It's Science."** — science-backed framing gets me every time; it signals the brand knows something about ingredients, not just aesthetics
- **Hair concern-based product grid** — organizing by hair type (dry, frizzy, thinning) is how I actually shop; this feels like a real recommendation engine, not just a shelf dump
- **4X points visual is impossible to miss** — that circular "4X" graphic reads instantly; as a Rouge member, multiplied points on a category I spend heavily in is exactly my language
- **Clean visual hierarchy with zero render bugs** — hero → product grid → points offer → app; the eye knows exactly where to go and nothing breaks along the way
- **Female model with textured hair** — feels inclusive and demographic-appropriate; Sephora consistently gets this right

## What's weak

- **It's the second "Earn 4X on hair care" email from Sephora today** — the cadence implosion this week (duplicate "bag edit," duplicate "must-haves," now duplicate "4X hair care") has broken my trust in their send logic entirely
- **No visible end date on the 4X offer** — Sally Beauty ran "Buy Two, Get One Free on select hair care" last week with a clear deadline; this email makes the offer feel like standing policy, not an event worth acting on now
- **"SHOP NOW" is the laziest CTA on the internet** — Glossier's GWP reveal last week gave me a specific thing to click toward ("claim your free bag"); "SHOP NOW" tells me nothing
- **Zero personalization** — nothing here signals Sephora knows I'm Rouge or has any purchase history on me; I'm being mailed like a first-time visitor, not a loyal member

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the duplicate send logic first** — no creative fix matters until Sephora stops mailing me the same email twice in a day; this is the highest-leverage change by far
- 2. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or even "48 hours left" in the hero copy would transform this from evergreen feel-good content into an actual reason to click today
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA to be specific** — "Earn 4X Now" or "Shop Hair Science" is minimal effort with measurable lift over "SHOP NOW"
- 4. **Surface Rouge-specific point math** — tell me "spend $75, earn 300 bonus points" and I will calculate my way into the cart; that's how I actually make purchase decisions
- 5. **Lead with one hero SKU** — anchor the email with a single bestseller (a specific K18 or Olaplex treatment) before cascading into the grid

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 4X on every shampoo, treatment, and styler through Sunday.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Issue — Missing `lang` attribute on `<html>` tag**
```html
<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v="..." xmlns:o="...">
```
No `lang="en"` present. Screen readers default to system language; WCAG 3.1.1 requires language declaration. Fix: `<html lang="en" ...>`.

**Issue — `text-size-adjust: none` applied globally**
```css
div, a, tr, table, body, span, img, strong, td {
  -webkit-text-size-adjust: none;
  -ms-text-size-adjust: none;
  -moz-text-size-adjust: none;
  text-size-adjust: none;
}
```
This suppresses OS-level font scaling for users who rely on it (low-vision users on mobile). The property should be `100%` rather than `none`, or scoped only to elements where it's structurally necessary (e.g., buttons).

**Non-issue — `robots` meta typo**
```html
<meta name="robots" content="no index" />
```
"no index" with a space is technically malformed (`noindex` is the valid token), but email clients don't crawl via robots meta, so this has no functional impact.

**Non-issue — XHTML 1.0 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Old but functionally correct for email. No action needed.
