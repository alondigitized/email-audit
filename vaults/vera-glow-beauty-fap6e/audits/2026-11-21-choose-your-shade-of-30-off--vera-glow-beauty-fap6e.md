# Choose your shade of 30% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is now running three numbers at once, and I'm doing the points math again. The top banner says "unlock 25% off sitewide* | join VIP for free." The hero says "30% Off" on the Multi-Stick. The red block at the bottom says "20% off sitewide*." That's 25, 30 and 20 in one scroll. This is the same shell game I flagged on 11/19 and 11/20 ("Our very best—very on sale"), and now it has a third number. The 30% is a product-specific offer, and the subject at least makes that clear. But the sitewide figure still contradicts itself, and I can't tell which one applies to my cart.
- The shade grid is the best part. Eight swatches with names like "all of me" and "dear ruby" are what I'd actually screenshot and send to my Sephora group chat. Skin-tone match is the personalization I care about, and the face shot shows a real application. It's pretty and clear. But the shade names are small, low-contrast text over the swatches.
- Would I click? Yes, if I wanted a blush stick. I'd tap "Choose your shade" and check whether 30% stacks with the VIP 25%. I'd forward the shade grid but not the offer.

## What's working

- The "30% Off" hero is huge, serif and unmissable, and it matches the subject.
- The "Choose your shade" button is deep maroon and sits right under the hero, so the CTA is obvious.
- The shade grid is a good "take your pick" browse. The names and color descriptors (watermelon pink, poppy red, bronze pearl) help me choose without swatching in store.
- The model close-up with the stick on her cheek shows the product on skin and sells the "buildable, blendable, breathable" claim.
- The footer trust row (1% for the Planet, Leaping Bunny, Pact, VIP, Auto-Replenish) fits the clean-beauty positioning.

## What's weak

- The 25% banner, 30% hero and 20% footer block are three different numbers. It's the same mismatch I called out on 11/19, and it's getting worse.
- The footer block's "20% off sitewide*" makes the 30% hero feel less special. That block also carries a "free gift w/ all orders*" burst that I can't read clearly.
- The shade names sit in thin, light text over busy swatch photos. On my phone it's hard to read without zooming.
- The VIP perk is only in the thin top banner and the footer icon row. As a points person, I want the member math spelled out: does VIP stack with the 30%?
- The two stacked CTA buttons (maroon, then black) and the 20% block's own button make three "shop" buttons. That's a lot of buttons for one product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer and use it everywhere. Say "30% off Multi-Stick, 25% off everything else for VIPs," and drop the contradictory 20% block.
- 2. Put the shade names in a solid label bar or bolder text so they're readable at phone size.
- 3. Add one line on how VIP stacks with the sale ("VIP: extra X% on top, or the better of the two").
- 4. Link each swatch to its own product page. Right now it looks like the grid goes to one place.
- 5. Cut the red 20% block or move it above the footer with clear fine print.

ILIA's shade grid is stronger than any of them, but its offer is muddier.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Klaviyo-built email using a branded click-tracking domain (`trk.send.iliabeauty.com`). The HTML is structurally sound, but the QA run could not verify the links, the compliance headers, or authentication. Most of the 57% pass rate reflects checks that didn't run, not defects found.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No confirmed broken links.** Four probed links returned 429 (rate-limited by the tracking host), which the QA tool itself says may still be valid:
  - `.../01M7XQERJ2BA77M44KXPXCP4H5_5` ("SHOP MULTI-STICK →")
  - `..._7` ("SHOP THE SALE →")
  - `..._1`
  - `..._2`
- **Coverage gap.** Only 1 of 12 material links resolved. Links `_3`, `_4`, `_6`, `_8` and `_9` were not probed after the 70s budget, so they are neither passing nor failing. The 429s also mean no destination URL was resolved for any CTA.
- **Opaque redirects.** Every link is a `/l/<id>_N` redirect, so destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, two images:**
  - `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg`. This is a content image, so the missing alt is a real accessibility gap. The CTA-like link text in the probe results suggests some text may be image-based.
  - `https://trk.send.iliabeauty.com/o/01M7XQERJ2BA77M44KXPXCP4H5`. This is the open-tracking pixel, so it is low impact, but `alt=""` is still best practice.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute.** The root is `<html xmlns=... xmlns:o=... xmlns:v=...>`, which affects screen-reader pronunciation.
- **`<title> </title>` is blank.** This is minor, but it affects the web-view and accessibility title.
- **`@import` for web fonts.** The `<style>` block imports `static-forms.klaviyo.com/.../custom_fonts.css` and Google Fonts Montserrat. Gmail and Outlook ignore these. The stacks fall back to Helvetica/Arial (`p, h1, h2, h3 { font-family: "Montserrat", "Helvetica", sans-serif }`), so the impact is limited to font fidelity.
- **Preheader.** The hidden preheader div is implemented correctly (`display:none; max-height:0; opacity:0`). The `͏` padding characters keep body text out of the inbox preview.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{ }}` or `*|FNAME|*`. The HTML was truncated, so the footer wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing headers, so it is unconfirmed. Klaviyo normally adds these, but I can't verify that from this data. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown for the same reason. The sender is `info@iliabeauty.com`, but the tracking domain is `send.iliabeauty.com`, so alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is reported for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destinations behind the `/l/...` redirects were never resolved (429s and the probe budget), so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
