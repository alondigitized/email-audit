# Makeup that feels as good as it looks 🌱
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Called it this morning — this is the exact "Clean at Sephora" send I already logged today, and the full scroll confirms my first read: App Fest banner eating the top of the email again, THEN we pivot into "Clean at Sephora Must-Haves" like it's a totally separate campaign. It's not landing as curation, it's landing as two emails duct-taped together.
- Once I get past the banner collision, the product grid itself is actually solid — Kosas, Milk Makeup, Ogee, Fel Beauty, all labeled with "NEW," "Only At Sephora," or "Online Only" tags, which is the kind of launch-clarity I actually want from Sephora. But there's zero ingredient or skin-type personalization here despite this being framed as a "clean" ingredients story, and my Rouge tier status shows up exactly once, buried in the black Insider bar near the bottom with no points multiplier or event tie-in.
- I'd scroll through this one on the toilet, maybe screenshot the Haus Labs Lady Gaga liner for myself, but I'm not forwarding it — my friend already got this same App Fest banner from me twice this week.

## What's working

- The "Clean at Sephora" green shield badge is a trust cue I recognize instantly and it's used consistently on every "clean" product card.
- NEW / ONLY AT SEPHORA / ONLINE ONLY tags are exactly the launch-clarity signal I look for as a Rouge member cross-shopping drops — better tagging discipline than the Skechers "same toy truck four sends running" mess I've been seeing.
- The Haus Labs by Lady Gaga lip liner and M.Ph by Mary Phillips gloss up top are genuinely on-trend prestige-adjacent picks, not filler.
- Clean layout in the product grid itself — two-column cards, consistent pricing, easy to scan on mobile without pinch-zooming.

## What's weak

- The App Fest promo banner (25% off Anastasia, Oleh, other brands) is stacked directly above the "Clean at Sephora" hero with zero visual separation — it reads like this email got merged from two different campaigns, same critique I gave the "makeup that feels as good as it looks" send a few hours ago at 5/10.
- Nothing here reflects that I'm Rouge tier beyond the generic black Insider strip at the very bottom — no personalized points multiplier, no "you've earned X" callout.
- "Clean" is the entire premise of the hero copy but there's no ingredient callout, skin-type matching, or "what's excluded" clarity on the actual product tiles — it's just a badge slapped on.
- This is beat-for-beat the same "Clean at Sephora" curation drop I already reviewed today under a near-identical subject — cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split App Fest and Clean at Sephora into two separate sends — right now they're actively fighting each other for the hero slot, and I lose track of which promo I'm supposed to act on.
- 2. Add a Rouge-specific hook near the top — even a simple "Rouge members: earn 2x points on Clean picks through Sunday" would beat the buried generic Insider bar.
- 3. Put actual ingredient/skin-type filtering language under the green badge — "minus the ingredients you might not [want]" promises something the product grid never delivers.
- 4. Cut repeat sends of the same curation concept within the same day — I've now logged two "Clean at Sephora" variants today alone.
