# Your fave pickle lippie is BACK in 3 flavs! 👀🥒
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the pickle lip balm is back — "Totally Dill-Lulu," pick(le) of 3 flavors, $9 — and I'll admit the jar-as-pickle-barrel hero image made me smirk. But this is the fourth pickle-branded send I've seen this week (8/20's launch, then dill hair and dill skincare riding the same bit), and now it's the actual product coming back "in 3 flavs," which honestly reads like they're re-running the same launch beat instead of giving me something new. The lip balm hero is legit clear — $9, limited edition, clean CTA — but then it just... keeps going. Weekly highlight reel, obsessions grid, hot-this-week grid, a second limited-edition sponge drop, a GWP for Bronzing Drops — this is five different offers crammed into one email with zero hierarchy telling me which one actually matters today.
- Compare this to Drunk Elephant's "Summer with benefits" send, which at least commits to one narrative (D-Bronzi repositioned as skincare) even if it's also recycled. e.l.f. just throws the whole catalog at me. I'd scroll past the top banner, maybe click Get Pickled out of curiosity since I did buy the original run, but I'm not opening this whole thing top to bottom, and I'm definitely not forwarding it — there's no single hook compelling enough to share.

## What's working

- The pickle jar hero is genuinely fun and on-brand — e.l.f. has committed to this bit for a week straight and the sponge ("Briny Blender," $6) keeps it consistent.
- "TOTALLY DILL-LULU" headline + $9 price + "Get Pickled" CTA is clear and immediate — no scrolling needed to find the offer.
- Model in the hero reads Gen Z/young millennial casual, which is at least in-range for me.
- Free shipping + GWP (free Bronzing Drops with $50+) are both visible if I scroll, which is a real loyalty-adjacent perk.

## What's weak

- Zero mention of my Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond status — nothing here treats me like a repeat, high-spend customer, just a generic list blast.
- Way too many CTAs stacked with no hierarchy — Get Pickled, Shop Best Sellers, Shop New Arrivals, six product tiles with no labels, two more Shop Now buttons, then Get Pickled again for the sponge. I don't know what to click first.
- "Your New Obsessions" product grid has zero product names or prices visible — just tiny thumbnails, so I have no idea what I'm even looking at without clicking through.
- This is the same pickle gimmick running for the fourth time this week (8/20 launch, hair, skincare, now lip balm again) — the novelty's gone and it's starting to feel like they've run out of new ideas, not unlike Sally Beauty running COLORfest nine times this month.
- No skin tone/type personalization anywhere, which e.l.f.'s own color range would actually support — a missed opportunity Ulta's segmented sends at least gesture toward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE offer per email. Lead with Dill-Lulu, kill the highlight reel/obsessions/hot-this-week middle section entirely — that's a separate send.
- 2. Label the "Your New Obsessions" products — name + price under each thumbnail, or nobody clicks a mystery tube.
- 3. Add a Rouge/Diamond-style loyalty line ("as a top-tier member, get early access") since I clearly buy enough to warrant it.
- 4. Move the GWP (free Bronzing Drops, $50+) up near the top — that's a stronger urgency driver than another product grid.
- 5. Retire the pickle bit for a cycle — four sends in a week is past the joke's shelf life.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dill-Lulu is back: 3 new lip balm flavors, $9`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your cult-fave pickle balm just restocked 🥒`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `2 new flavors, same iconic jar. Grab yours before they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited-edition Glow Reviver Melting Balms, back for a minute.`

# Technical Audit — e.l.f. Cosmetics "Pickle Lippie" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA returned a 23% pass rate (7 failures, 3 warnings), driven primarily by broken product/account links, a missing plain-text MIME part, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **6 broken destination links (FAIL)** — all routed through the `links.elfcosmetics.com` (Braze) click-tracking redirector, but resolving to **relative paths with no host**, which QA flags as "unknown url type":
  - `/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm?Color=new+shade%21+The+Real+Dill+-+Green` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/instant-lift-waterproof-brow-pencil?Color=Grey` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning" link)
  - `/collections/best-sellers?lid=...&utm_source=broadcast...` ("SHOP BEST SELLERS")
  - `/collections/whats-new?lid=...&utm_source=broadcast...` ("SHOP NEW ARRIVALS")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?bxid=...&lid=...` ("beauty squad – Download the e.l.f. app")
  - This is a Braze link-template configuration error — the destination URL was saved as a relative path instead of an absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URL, so the redirector cannot resolve where to send the click.
- **Coverage caveat**: QA probed 12 of 46 material links (34 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); remaining links (including footer/social/unsubscribe) were not verified and could carry the same relative-path defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/.../Dwk4lbcNpZsnZAsfjhKdkoEdclc-tNf0BF9OzU-ZpbijyWJvGw` (likely the open-tracking pixel — low impact, but the hero banner `<img>` correctly has `alt="e.l.f."`, so this appears to be a secondary content image, not just the pixel)

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Product-specific links (`/products/glow-reviver-melting-lip-balm`, `/products/instant-lift-waterproof-brow-pencil`) and the account/app links show **no UTM parameters** in the destination path, only `Color=`/`return_to=` query args — inconsistent with the campaign-tagged collection links, and continuity can't be verified regardless since all six are currently broken (Section 2).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Braze link templates for the 6 failing destinations — set absolute URLs (`https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...`) instead of relative paths.
2. Add `alt` text to the two flagged images.
3. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC and `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers directly on raw sent headers (relay may be dropping them, not the sender).
4. Populate the plain-text MIME part — currently 0 chars, which hurts deliverability/spam scoring independent of any CAN-SPAM requirement.
5. Standardize UTM parameters across all CTA links, including product and account links, to preserve email-to-site attribution.
6. Expand link probing beyond the 12 material links to cover footer/unsubscribe links, since compliance-critical links were deprioritized in this pass.
