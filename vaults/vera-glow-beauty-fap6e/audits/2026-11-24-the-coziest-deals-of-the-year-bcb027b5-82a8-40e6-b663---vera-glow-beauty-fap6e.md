# The coziest deals of the year
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- Skechers is in my beauty inbox again. It's the same Black Friday send I've seen all week, now with a cozy-season skin on it. The red "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" banner hits first. Below it, a woman in a charcoal hoodie and joggers sells "It's Cozy Season." Sephora would have led with the offer and then made me feel something. This one does both, but I've already read the offer six times.
- The math is where I got stuck. There's a 30-50% range with no way to tell which tier I'm in. There's an "Extra 15% off everything" for Plus members with code BF2026. The top bar says "Join Skechers Plus and earn 1000 Bonus Points (that's a $5 reward!)." I do points math for a living, and that last one is $5 of value for signing up. It's the only loyalty hook in the email, and it's tiny. I'd skim it, not click, and not forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The red banner gives me "30-50% OFF SITEWIDE" in about half a second.
- The three product blocks (Classic Cozy Fit, Arcade, Mules) each have one hero shoe and one button. The layout is easy to scan.
- The Plus member strip with "Extra 15% off everything" and a code is the closest thing to tier math, and it's the best part.
- The seasonal hero with garland trim fits late November.
- Klarna and Afterpay badges and the app download block are useful trust cues.

## What's weak

- The member perk of $5 for 1000 points is tiny, grey and easy to miss. That's the loyalty hook and it's buried.
- "30-50%" is a range with no ladder. Ulta's Black Friday email spelled out 50/40/30 by category, and I knew where to shop. Here I don't know what's 50% versus 30%.
- The disclaimer is dense and tiny. "Discount auto-applied at cart | see details below" plus fine print on 30% vs 40% vs 50% makes the offer feel less honest.
- There's no price, review or rating on any product. I'm choosing shoes with nothing to go on.
- Cadence is exhausting. After 11/21, 11/22 and two sends on 11/23, this is another member-30% beat.
- The "AI-generated model" note under the hero is small but I noticed it. It reads a bit off-brand.
- This isn't beauty at all, so nothing here speaks to my skin type, shade or routine.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the vague range with a tier ladder (for example, "50% off boots, 40% off slippers, 30% off everything else"), the way Ulta did. This would lift the email the most.
- 2. Move the Plus perk up under the hero and show the stack: 30% plus an extra 15% equals X, with the code visible and a button that copies it.
- 3. Add a price, a star rating or a "bestseller" tag under each shoe.
- 4. Cut the three near-identical shop buttons to one clear primary CTA, or give each a specific verb like "Shop slip-ins."
- 5. Don't send this the day after another 30% send. Space them out or give each a new angle.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Slip-ins up to 50% off, plus extra 15% for Plus`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cozy Fit from $__ today: 30-50% off ends 11/29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack your Plus 15% with BF2026. Here's what's 50% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Quilted slip-ins, mules and arcade sneakers, all on sale through Cyber Week.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email is a well-structured XHTML table layout with consistent UTM/`src` tagging and JSON-LD promotion annotations. It has two HTTP image sources, two images without alt text, and small URL defects in the annotation markup. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate). The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay capturing headers, not from confirmed sender faults.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Whitespace in the JSON-LD `utm_content` value.** PromotionCard positions 2 and 4 contain `utm_content=annotation &utm_term=googleannotation2` and `...annotation &utm_term=googleannotation4`. The space before `&utm_term` is a literal space. Positions 1 and 3 use `utm_content=annotation&utm_term=...` with no space. Parsers may truncate or encode the value as `annotation%20`, which fragments attribution in analytics.
- **Tracking domain uses HTTP.** The pixel is `http://www.ink1000.com/p/up/7076d8b15a776085/o.gif?mi_u=8108776175&mi_ecmp=MKG_US_NA_U_COZY_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11242026`. It is not served over TLS and carries the `mi_u` identifier in cleartext.
- **32 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so their resolution and final destinations are unverified.
- **Unrelated favicon on a different domain.** The `<link rel="icon">` points to `http://image.emails.nl.skechers.com/...`, which is HTTP and a different subdomain (`emails.nl`) from the sender's image host (`image.emails`). Email clients ignore favicons, so this is hygiene only.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text:** `o.gif` (the ink1000 tracking pixel) and `dde00662-169f-447d-b0e2-fc65f6c2290c.png` (`http://image.emails.skechers.com/lib/fe3115707564047a731c78/m/1/dde00662-...png`). The tracking pixel should carry `alt=""`. The `dde00662` image needs `alt=""` if it is decorative, or descriptive text if it is not. Without any `alt` attribute, screen readers may announce the filename.
- **Mixed content.** Both `o.gif` and `dde00662-...png` use `http://`. Some clients and proxies block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, so the pixel may not fire and the `dde00662` image may not render.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `Skechers&reg;`. It does not describe the email, which matters for web-view and accessibility contexts.
- **Viewport meta.** `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Global `* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none }`.** This disables text resizing on iOS.
- The HTML was truncated, so the body markup, `lang` attribute, and `role="presentation"` usage on layout tables could not be checked.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. The visible source shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The truncated body could not be fully inspected.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers from the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain is `emails.skechers.com`, which should be checked directly.
- The unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated footer, so they could not be verified.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The UTM scheme is consistent across all four annotation URLs: `utm_source=trig`, `utm_medium=email`, and `utm_campaign=MKG_US_NA_U_COZY_ENGAGEDL30D_EN_11242026`. The `src=2026Q4BLACKFRIDAY` parameter is also present.
- **The `utm_content` whitespace defect** (see section 2) affects positions 2 and 4.
- **The `src` and campaign naming don't match.** `src` is `2026Q4BLACKFRIDAY`, while the campaign name is `COZY_ENGAGEDL30D`. This may be intentional, but the two parameters are inconsistent for reporting.
- **Unvalidated landing pages.** The landing URLs (`/technologies/featured-comfort-technologies/cozy-fit/` and `/unisex-arcade-collection/`) were not probed. Position 3 filters by `prefv1=Cozy%20Fit` on a collection page, and that facet may not match the page's filter values.
- Click-tracked body links could not be checked for UTM preservation after redirect.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Remove the space in `utm_content=annotation ` for annotation positions 2 and 4 (URL-encode or delete it) so attribution is not split.
2. Serve the tracking pixel and `dde00662-...png` over HTTPS. Confirm that `ink1000.com` supports TLS, or move the pixel to a first-party HTTPS host.
3. Add `alt=""` to the `o.gif` pixel and to the `dde00662` image if decorative. Otherwise add descriptive alt text.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` at the origin ESP, and check SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for `emails.skechers.com` using the raw message.
5. Replace the generic `<title>` with a descriptive one. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the global `text-size-adjust: none`.
6. Probe the landing URLs and one sample of the 32 skipped tracking links to confirm the final destination and that UTMs persist.
7. Re-run the QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link, merge tokens, and table roles.
