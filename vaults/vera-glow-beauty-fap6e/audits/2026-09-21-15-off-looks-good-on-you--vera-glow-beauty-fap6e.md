# 15% off looks good on you
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so "15% off looks good on you" and it's actually delivering something real — subscribe to two-or-more and get 15% instead of the usual 10%, which is a genuine mechanic bump, not a DRUNKVIP20 rerun. No points math for me here since Drunk Elephant doesn't have a loyalty tier the way Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond does, so this lives or dies on the subscription math alone, and honestly the math is fine but the offer is buried under three separate "how to save" step-lists before I even see the Kamo Triple-Peptide news, which I already saw teased on 9/19's "Kamo FOMO?" send.
- The five-product lineup at the bottom with star ratings and "X people are checking this out" is a nice trust signal, and the hero product shot (five bottles staged with that dramatic black shadow) actually looks premium — better styled than the flat product grid Sally Beauty ran for its Halloween nails. But this is subscription-and-save content dressed up as a launch email, and I've now gotten Kamo mentioned in two sends running without an actual "shop the full collection" moment — feels like they're building hype without letting me buy the thing outright.
- Would I click? Maybe, on Kamo Drops specifically, since 25 shades of concealer-foundation hybrid is genuinely up my alley. Forward to a friend? No — this is a "manage my own subscription" email, not a shareable one.

## What's working

- Hero product shot is genuinely elevated — five products staged with real lighting and shadow, feels premium next to e.l.f.'s two-tube splash shot from earlier this week, though that one still edges it out for scroll-stopping power.
- "Subscribe to Save" CTA repeats twice with consistent button styling, so I always know where to click.
- The 5-step "How to save" breakdown with the cheeky "There is no step 5!" line is genuinely charming copy, not corporate-speak.
- Kamo Drops gets its own dedicated module with a real claim ("clinically proven to firm skin," 25 shades) instead of just a product photo.
- Bottom grid with star ratings and live "checking this out" counts is a solid social-proof layer I don't see from ILIA or Glossier.

## What's weak

- The actual offer (15% vs 10%) is stated once at the very top in a gray banner and never repeated or reinforced visually — I have to scroll past two full product modules before the "how to save" steps even confirm the mechanic.
- Nothing here signals skin type, tone, or my past purchases — it's the exact same "everyone gets the same email" approach I called out in ILIA's plain-text cosplay, just with better production value.
- Kamo gets teased again without a direct "shop the collection" link beyond one small "SHOP KAMO DROPS" text line — same soft-launch energy as 9/19, no escalation.
- The fine print at the bottom (dates, no-code-required detail) is doing real work that should be up top — burying "no code required, auto-applies at 2+" this low creates unnecessary friction.
- Four unrelated bestsellers ("you're gonna like these guys") dilute the subscription pitch instead of reinforcing it — feels like a different email got pasted on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "15% off (up from 10%) — no code needed" line into the hero banner itself, not a secondary gray strip — that's the whole email's reason to exist and it reads like a footnote.
- 2. Give Kamo Triple-Peptide its own full send instead of a repeat tease — I've now seen "Have You Kamo'd Yet?" language in two emails without a real launch moment.
- 3. Cut the bottom four-product grid or reframe it as "add these to your subscription" so it supports the CTA instead of competing with it.
- 4. Add a skin-type or shade-matching prompt near the Kamo module — even a simple "find your shade" link would beat the generic "SHOP KAMO DROPS."
- 5. Surface the auto-apply/no-code detail right under the main CTA button so I don't wonder if I need a promo code.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save 15% (not 10%) — subscribe to 2+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your skin barrier just got a raise`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed — 15% auto-applies at checkout on 2+ items`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: have you tried Kamo yet?`
