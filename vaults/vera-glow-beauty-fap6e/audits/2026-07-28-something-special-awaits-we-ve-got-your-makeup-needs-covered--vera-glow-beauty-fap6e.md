# Something special awaits. We’ve got your makeup needs covered.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "0 points" — that's literally what Sephora's own header tells me at the top of this email, right under the INSIDER banner. I've been Rouge tier for two years, I track my points balance in my head without trying, and my subscribed inbox just told me I have zero. That's not a subtle miss, that's the kind of broken merge field that makes me trust the whole email less.
- Past that hiccup, this is actually one of the more visually competent Sephora sends I've gotten lately — the "BEAUTY FOR YOU" purple hero into the LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask/Glowy Balm duo is clean, the "Only at Sephora" badges are doing real work, and the 20% off banner up top is a legit concrete offer. But it's National Lip Day. Again. My own history shows Sephora already ran me through "Save up to 50% on select lip must-haves" on 7/26 and "50% off select Sephora Collection lip faves" on 7/27 — both scored well in the moment but both flagged the exact same fatigue I'm feeling now. Three lip-themed sends in three days is not curation, it's a content calendar on autopilot.
- I'd scroll through this one — the Trending Picks grid actually has range (Prada, Kérastase, Armani Beauty, Farmacy) — but I'm not clicking today. The subject line gave me nothing to act on, and I've already seen this LANEIGE-and-lip-stuff beat twice this week. Marking as read, not forwarding.

## What's working

- The top banner offer is genuinely concrete: 20% off select LANEIGE, "Today only!" — that's a real deadline, not vague urgency filler.
- LANEIGE Lip Sleeping Mask + Lip Glowy Balm get an actual hero treatment with prices ($25/$19) and "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA" — that's the kind of exclusivity signal that makes me feel like a Sephora shopper and not an Ulta one.
- "Only at Sephora" badges are stamped across almost every product tile (ViktorRolf, Farmacy, Fable & Mane) — smart, cheap way to reinforce why I shouldn't just cross-shop this at Ulta.
- The Insider/VIB/ROUGE tier strip at the footer, with ROUGE highlighted in red, is a small but real acknowledgment of the loyalty program — Ulta's tier math shows up cleaner in their emails, but at least Sephora put it somewhere.
- Foundation & Concealer Under $15 and the Refillable Beauty tile at the bottom are decent variety — not everything is lip-locked.

## What's weak

- "You have 0 points" at the very top is either a broken personalization token or a lazy default — either way, as a Rouge member that's an immediate credibility hit, worse than anything Skechers has done to me lately.
- The subject line "Something special awaits. We've got your makeup needs covered" is pure filler — no product, no percentage, no urgency. Compare that to Ulta's "Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off one qualifying item just landed in your lap" — Ulta led with the actual number, Sephora buried it.
- This is the third lip/lip-adjacent send this week following 7/26 and 7/27's near-identical "up to 50% off lip" emails — I already said this cadence felt repetitive twice, and now it's happening a third time with a different product (LANEIGE instead of Sephora Collection or Charlotte Tilbury).
- "Trending Picks for You" is really just a full-catalog dump — Prada perfume next to a hair mask next to an eye cream — there's no actual personalization logic visible, it reads like Drunk Elephant's "top shelf" catalog-wall approach, not a curated pick.
- No demographic or lifestyle imagery anywhere — it's all product flatlays, no models, no faces. Fine for a deals email, but it means nothing here visually says "this is for a 29-year-old."

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the points bug first — a Rouge member seeing "0 points" is worse than not mentioning points at all; either populate it correctly or drop the line.
- 2. Put the actual offer in the subject line — "20% off LANEIGE" beats vague wordplay every time, and it would've told me instantly this isn't the fourth lip-must-haves email.
- 3. Space out the Lip Day content — three sends in three days on the same beat (7/26, 7/27, now 7/28) is going to train me to stop opening the "lip" ones altogether.
- 4. Turn "Trending Picks for You" into something that's actually filtered by my purchase history — right now it reads like a random assortment, not a "for you."
- 5. Add a star rating or review count on at least the LANEIGE hero products — Sephora's reviews are one of its real strengths over Ulta and it's not being used here.
