## What's working

- The hero is fun and scroll-stopping. The pastel-neon bottles read as a new line immediately.
- Prices are on every hair product ($10 oil, $9 spray, $9 cream, $6 wand, $9 shampoo and conditioner). That's the easiest price comparison I'll see this week against Sephora hair.
- The copy is cute and short ("drip, drop. shine won't stop", "frizz goes byeeee"). It sounds like e.l.f. and not like a Sephora carbon copy.
- The "choose a full-size gift on us" block is the clearest offer. It says $35+ and "no promo code needed."
- "Top Picks for You" has $2, $11 and $6 items, so there's a low-risk add-on.

## What's weak

- The subject says "gifts for everyone on your list," but nothing in the body is a gift set, bundle or holiday framing. The hero and products are just regular hair launches.
- Three identical "CLICK HAIR" buttons are vague. They're also pale purple on yellow, which is low contrast.
- The $35 gift offer sits below the fold, behind a long product grid. Ulta would have put that threshold up top.
- The "Top Picks for You" section shows a liner, a primer and a tint. None are hair, and it's not clearly personalized, so it feels like filler.
- The category tabs (New, Eyes, Lips, Face, Skincare, Hair) are huge empty gray bars. They eat a lot of scroll on mobile.
- There's no Elf Rewards or points mention in the body. The footer has "Earn rewards anywhere," but that's far too late.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "free full-size gift at $35+" up under the hero and tie it to the hair line, for example "Spend $35, get a free full-size gift."
- 2. Make the gifting angle real: build a "Hair gift under $25" set or a bundle of 3-in-1 Wonder plus Gloss Mode.
- 3. Replace "CLICK HAIR" with specific buttons like "Shop hair gifts" and "Shop the $9 Humidity Hero," in a higher-contrast color.
- 4. Collapse the empty category bars into a compact nav row.
- 5. Put a points line near the top ("Earn 2x Elf Rewards on hair this week") so I can do my math.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based and structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a preheader, an MSO conditional). The QA run recorded a 25% pass rate, with 5 of 12 probed links failing because their destinations are host-less relative paths. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified because the relay doesn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **5 of 12 probed links failed with `unknown url type`.** The redirect targets have no scheme or host:
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Dark+Cocoa+-+Deep+Brown` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Jumbo+2.5+oz+%2F+75+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/sheer-for-it-blush-tint?Color=So+Petal+-+light+pink` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards anywhere you shop e.l.f.")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&c=email&af_reengagement_window=30d&bxid=…` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
- **Likely cause:** the Braze-wrapped links on `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/…` resolve to relative paths. If the redirect really returns a relative `Location`, clients would resolve it against `links.elfcosmetics.com` and 404. I can't confirm from the data whether this is a real defect or an artifact of the prober. Open one of these links in a real client to check.
- **Probe coverage:** 34 links were skipped, so the 25% pass rate reflects only the top-ranked links. The product "Shop Now" CTAs are probably the most-clicked links in the email.
- **Truncated URL:** the app deep-link URL is cut off at `bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a161…` in the QA output. This is a reporting limitation, not a confirmed defect.
- **Tracking URL length:** the click-tracking URLs run to hundreds of characters (the `/f/a/…~~/AAQRxRA~/…` format).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** one image has no `alt`. Its src is `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/yCw7hed5…`, and the `/q/` path suggests an open-tracking pixel. If so, the impact is minor, but add `alt=""` to make it explicit.
- **Empty `<title></title>`:** the document title is empty.
- **Mobile heading rule:** the media query sets `.row-10 .column-1 .block-1.heading_block h1{font-size:9px!important}`. Text at 9px is below readable size. The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't confirm what this heading contains.
- **Nested layout tables:** `nl-container` tables are nested inside `html_block` cells. This is valid but deepens the table nesting, which adds some risk in Outlook.
- **No issues found with:** the viewport meta, charset, the preheader (hidden with `display:none` and `mso-hide:all`), or the primary hero image (it has `alt="e.l.f."`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`). The source was truncated, so the lower sections were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058)** were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed missing**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`** was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation. The From domain `m.elfcosmetics.com` and the link domain `links.elfcosmetics.com` share the brand's root domain, which is consistent with aligned branded tracking.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** the QA run probed an unsubscribe link, as it always does, but reported no failure for it. The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the postal address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** every `href` is wrapped in a Braze redirect, so UTM presence can't be confirmed from the source. The only visible campaign parameters are on the app link: `c=email`, `af_reengagement_window=30d`, `bxid=…`.
- **Landing page alignment:** the product links carry variant parameters (`Color=…`, `Size=…`), which should pre-select the variant. That can't be verified while the destinations fail to resolve.
- **Login link:** the receipt-scanning link points to `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount`. It sends the reader to a login wall instead of a rewards or receipt-scanning page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-test the 5 failing links in a real client by following the full redirect chain. If the `Location` is relative, fix the template hrefs to absolute `https://www.elfcosmetics.com/...` URLs. This is the highest priority because it affects the product CTAs.
2. Fix the prober so it resolves relative `Location` headers against the final host, to separate real breakage from false positives.
3. Check the raw message headers directly from the sending side for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Don't rely on the relay copy.
4. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and set a meaningful `<title>`.
5. Check what the `.row-10` `h1` contains and raise its mobile size above 9px, or hide it if it is deliberately hidden.
6. Confirm that UTM or equivalent campaign parameters survive the redirect on the product links.
