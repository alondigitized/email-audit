# Select brands are 50% off today 🤑
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a relief — no "you have 0 points" gut-punch sitting under the header for once. That merge-field bug has been staring me in the face on every single Sephora send I've logged this week (both K18s, the 50%-off-faves one), so seeing my ROUGE tier actually highlighted correctly in the footer bar is honestly the biggest win here, even if it's just... not being broken.
- That said, the actual offer is buried. "50% off Select Beauty" is up top in the little pink banner, but it's Benefit, Sephora Collection, and Color WOW only — then the entire rest of this endless email pivots to "Beauty's Latest," a new INNBEAUTY cleanser, and a wall of "new/limited edition" product tiles that have nothing to do with the headline discount. I scrolled through Tower 28, Kenra, Kiehl's, Rare Beauty by Selena Gomez, Anecdotes, Act+Acre, Drunk Elephant, M.PH — none of them are the 50%-off brands. It's a launch roundup wearing a sale-email subject line.
- I'd click through to check if Color WOW's on sale since I actually use their dream coat, but I wouldn't forward this — it's too scattered to recommend to a friend, and this is nowhere near as clean as Glossier's "Brow down" send, which just committed to one product and real reviews instead of nine unrelated tiles.

## What's working

- ROUGE is correctly highlighted black in the tier bar at the bottom — first time in four logged Sephora sends this week the loyalty status actually rendered right instead of "0 points."
- "TODAY ONLY" urgency badge on the 50% off banner is clear and time-bounded.
- CLEAN AT SEPHORA badges and "NEW · ONLY AT SEPHORA" tags on products are genuinely useful filtering signals for how I shop.
- Product photography is crisp, no broken images or overlapping text anywhere in this whole long scroll.

## What's weak

- The 50% off hero offer (Benefit, Sephora Collection, Color WOW) gets one banner, then the email abandons it completely for a totally unrelated "Beauty's Latest" launch drop — feels like two emails stapled together.
- Nine product tiles in a row with no urgency or context beyond "new" is fatigue-inducing; this is the same scattershot-catalog problem I flagged with Sally Beauty's tiered spend ladder, just with new launches instead of discounts.
- No fragrance/skin-tone/type personalization anywhere — every rec is generic, not tailored to what I actually buy.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" fine print under the hero offer with zero specificity on what's excluded — feels like the Ulta "20% off one item, exclusions apply" pattern I already called out as cluttered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either this is a 50% off sale email or a new-launch roundup — cramming both means the discount (the actual reason to open) gets three seconds of attention before nine unrelated products bury it.
- 2. Show which SKUs the 50% off actually applies to right in the hero, not just brand names — I don't want to click through and hunt.
- 3. Cut the product grid from nine tiles to three curated by my purchase history — Sephora has my data, use it.
- 4. Add my points balance/progress-to-next-reward near the ROUGE tier bar since that's the one loyalty flex Sephora has over Ulta.
