# Final Hours: 3 for $30 balms
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send of the same 3-for-$30 balm deal. I got it on 11/12 ("Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!"), then 11/15 ("FYI! 3 for $30 balms is still on"), and now "Final Hours." The math still works for me. Three at $30 is $10 a tube against $16 list, and the email says 38%. I stock up on lip products, so I'm tempted. But I've now seen "still on" and "final hours" for the same promo, and the urgency doesn't feel earned.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan. The hero is clear, and the two grids sorted by "sultry tints" and "subtle favs" help me pick a shade. It's also a lot of identical "Shop now" buttons for one product line, and nothing here tells me I need to hit a threshold or add three to a cart.
- I'd click once, if I haven't already bought. I wouldn't forward it. It's a good deal sent too many times.

## What's working

- The hero says "Final Hours" and "3 Balm Dotcoms for just $30." The deal is clear without scrolling.
- The strikethrough $16 → $10 badges on every tile make the per-tube price obvious, and my brain does that math automatically.
- Splitting into "sultry tints" and "subtle favs" helps me choose a flavor. Black Cherry, Espresso, Wild Fig and Mint also read as seasonal.
- The model photography shows a range of skin tones and lip shades, which I notice.
- The layout is simple, with no render bugs and no clutter.

## What's weak

- This is the third send in six days for the same offer. "Final Hours" after "still on" feels like manufactured urgency.
- There's no deadline. "Final hours" with no time or date isn't credible. Ulta at least puts "ends tonight" on a badge.
- Seven "Shop now" buttons say the same thing. They don't name the product, and the grey buttons look low-contrast on my phone.
- The $10 badges suggest each tube is $10 on its own. I can't tell whether I have to buy three to get that price.
- There's no loyalty angle. As a Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond member, I want to know if I earn anything or can stack a code.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET," in the hero and on a badge. That's what makes "final hours" believable.
- 2. Make the bundle mechanic explicit: "Mix and match any 3, $30 auto-applied in cart." Change the tile badges to "3 for $30" instead of a bare $10.
- 3. Use specific CTA copy, like "Shop Black Cherry" or "Build my 3-pack," instead of seven "Shop now" buttons. Make the hero CTA solid and high-contrast.
- 4. Suppress this send for anyone who already bought the 3-pack. Sephora keeps its sends varied, and Glossier's repeat of the same offer starts to feel like noise.
- 5. Add a free-shipping threshold or a member perk so there's a reason to act now.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Black Cherry, Espresso, Wild Fig + more. $10 a tube, ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Its main confirmed defects are 9 content images with no alt text and a stray tracking element in `<body>`. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Stray element:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M7KFTE3V1387PTYDV37CMM4H_0"></span>` sits at the top of `<body>`. `rel` and `href` have no effect on a `<span>`, so this is a leftover from the ESP's stylesheet and tracking injection. It is harmless but invalid markup.
- **Open pixel:** `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7KFTE3V1387PTYDV37CMM4H` has no alt attribute (see section 3).
- **Probing coverage:** 12 material links were probed and 17 were skipped as footer, social or utility links. The QA data reports no broken links or redirect failures.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML is cut off, so I can't verify the CTA hrefs or the footer links from it.
