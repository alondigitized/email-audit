- The "Only at Sephora" hero in magenta with the Huda Beauty Easy Bake fragrance is clean. The "wild cherry, white florals, vanilla bourbon" scent notes make me want to sniff it.

- The Insider tier bar (Insider / VIB / Rouge) near the bottom acknowledges my status, even though it's generic.

## What's weak

- The 4X points banner and the sample bag are stacked above the "Only at Sephora" story. The sample bag has a $53+ purchase requirement, and the fine print is tiny.
- Nothing is personalized. I'm a Rouge, and the tier bar shows no points balance and no Rouge perks.
- The Google Pay block feels like filler and has nothing to do with beauty discovery.
- The legal text at the bottom is a wall of microscopic type.
- The "Spot it. Shop it." section (Caudalie, Lip's Latest Lineup) is a second, weaker catalog that dilutes the exclusives message.
- This is still the fourth send in about four days. Ulta's 21 Days sends at least feel event-driven.
