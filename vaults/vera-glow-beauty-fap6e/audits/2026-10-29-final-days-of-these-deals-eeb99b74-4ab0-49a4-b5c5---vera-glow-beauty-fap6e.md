## What's working

- The nail hero is pretty. Deep oxblood tones are very much my fall palette, and it's the best-looking image Sally has sent me this week.
- "These deals wrap October 31" gives me a real deadline. It's more credible than a vague "disappearing soon."
- The three-tile layout with a % off headline and a "Shop now" link on each is easy to scan.
- The lash BOGO 50% off is the one offer I can do math on in my head.
- The Klarna strip and the icon row for 2-hour delivery and pickup are useful trust cues.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't name any offer. It's a nail photo with no nail deal attached, so the headline and image don't match the tiles below.
- The nail hero takes up about a third of the screen before any discount appears. On mobile, none of the real offers show without scrolling.
- Men's grooming and "As I Am, Schwarzkopf, göt2b & EBIN" hair care aren't my categories. I felt the same about the 10/20 men's gift email and the 10/23 gray-coverage one.
- Three banners stack at the top: rewards signup, free 2-hour delivery, and 15% off next buy online. They compete with each other. I'm already a member, so "Get $5 off when you sign up" reads like a stranger pitch again.
- The fine print is long and tiny, and it's the only place the 25% and 20% terms are spelled out. That makes the offers feel less honest.
- There's no point math and no Beauty Insider-style tiering. Nothing says what I'd earn.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one real offer in the hero. Pair the nail image with a nail deal, like "Fall nails, 25% off," so the image earns its space.
- 2. Cut the top banners to one. Suppress the rewards signup for people who already have accounts.
- 3. Move the lash BOGO up, since it's the strongest hook, and segment the men's grooming tile out of women's sends.
- 4. Show rewards points or a member price on each tile so I can do the math.
- 5. Shrink the hero height so at least one offer appears above the fold.
