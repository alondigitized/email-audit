# Our very best—very on sale
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is still running the 25%/20% shell game I flagged on 11/19. The banner says "unlock 25% off sitewide* | join VIP for free." The hero says "20% off*" in a blush circle. Then the VIP block says "unlock 25% off" again. I did the math and the answer is 20% for everyone, 25% if you join the free VIP tier, plus a free gift. That's a decent deal, but it took me three scrolls to work out. The subject "very on sale" gives me no number at all.
- The review-led layout is the best part. Six products, each with a real customer quote and a Shop Now button, reads more like a bestseller list than a markdown dump. It's better than Tuesday's "Up to 25% off, two gifts, and 2x points," and much better than this morning's "Black Friday roundup," which was a plain-text note. Still, the "very" in the hero headline is clipped by the model's head, and the product cards are stacked so tall that I scroll forever to reach the actual deal.
- I'd open this and click, probably Super Serum Skin Tint because it has the SPF and a quote I trust. I wouldn't forward it. It's a pretty email but not a surprising one.

## What's working

- The "you rated: ★★★★★" section with real quotes ("Best mascara for sensitive eyes") is social proof I'd actually trust. Glossier's sale email this week had none.
- The red VIP block is clear: free to join, 25% off, 2x points, free shipping. That's the tier math I want, and it's visually distinct.
- The free gift edit (mini of choice at $100+, complementary complexion stick with any order) gives me a reason to build a cart past the threshold.
- Alternating left/right product cards with consistent Shop Now buttons make the scroll easy to follow, and the buttons are big enough to tap.
- The Allure Best of Beauty badges on the mascara, shadow stick and Face Milk are a good trust signal.

## What's weak

- Three different discount messages (25% sitewide, 20% off, 30% off on the Multi-Stick) with asterisks and no clear "this applies to you" answer. Drunk Elephant at least put one number in the subject.
- The headline "our very best" is clipped. The model's head covers the "y" in "very," so it reads like a render glitch.
- The first CTA, "Shop Bestsellers," is generic. Ulta would have led with a specific category or a point multiplier.
- The only product-level price signal is a 30% sticker on the Multi-Stick. I can't tell whether the other five are discounted, and there are no prices anywhere.
- This is the fourth ILIA send in five days. The "Black Friday roundup" earlier today already covered the same bestsellers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one number in the hero and use it everywhere: "20% off for all, 25% for VIPs." Drop the 25% banner or move it under the hero.
- 2. Fix the headline overlap so "very" is readable.
- 3. Show sale prices on each product card, even a "was/now" line, and say whether the 30% Multi-Stick is a VIP-only deal.
- 4. Lead the CTA with the benefit, such as "Shop 20% off bestsellers," instead of "Shop Bestsellers."
- 5. Cut this to 3-4 products. Sephora's Beauty Insider emails would put the offer and tier perk above the fold and keep the rest tight.
