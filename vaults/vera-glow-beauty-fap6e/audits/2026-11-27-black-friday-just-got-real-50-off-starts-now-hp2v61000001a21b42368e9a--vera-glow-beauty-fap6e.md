# Black Friday just got real 🌟 50% OFF starts now 🎊
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is finally a Sephora send with a number I'd stop for. "50% OFF" fills the whole top of the screen in giant white type on black sequins. That is a big step up from the "Cyber Week is bringing it ON" hype on 11/24. I'd normally do the points math here, but this is the seventh Sephora email in a week and I'm tired of it.
- Then I scroll and the math falls apart. The "50% off" has a tiny dagger footnote that says "Available on in-store merchandise only." The products below it are all about 30% off (Dr. Jart $52 → $36.40, Sol de Janeiro $39 → $27.30), and the green banner says "Up to 30% off." So the hero I'm excited about doesn't match the shelf I'm shown. I'd click, because it's Sephora and 50% is 50%, but I'd go in suspicious. I wouldn't forward it. I'd screenshot the CYBERSCENT code for my group chat and move on.

## What's working

- The "50% OFF" hero is unmissable. It beats Ulta's recycled $10-off-$50 coupon, which makes me hunt for the barcode.
- Real sale prices with strikethroughs ($25 → $17.50 for MAC, $24 → $18 for LANEIGE) let me check the math in half a second.
- The 4x points banner on fragrance gift sets (through 11/30) at the top is the Rouge-tier hook I care about.
- The CYBERSCENT code and the barcode at the bottom make the in-store path easy.
- The "Only at Sephora" tags on Dr. Jart, UNOVE and LANEIGE tell me what's exclusive and worth cross-shopping against Ulta.

## What's weak

- The "50% off" fine print says in-store merchandise only, while the hero says "Online Now." That reads like bait-and-switch.
- The 50% hero and the "up to 30%" tools banner don't match. I can't tell which deal is real or what's included.
- The email repeats itself. UNOVE's Deep Damage Repair mask shows up twice, at $19.50 and again at $11. The Kohl's store locator and the holiday gift guide are also long filler.
- The top banner's right edge is clipped ("INSIDE" and the "Exclusions/terms app…" text cut off).
- There's no tier math or personalization. Nothing says "Rouge, here's your extra," and the credit card ad is generic.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero honest. Either state "50% off in-store 11/27–11/29" in the headline, or lead with the online offer that actually applies.
- 2. Cut the "You Might Also Like" repeats and the store locator, and add a Rouge/VIB line such as "Rouge: extra points on this event."
- 3. Fix the top banner so the right edge isn't clipped.
- 4. Change the CTA to a specific one such as "Shop 50% off in store."
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off in store, 30% off online: Sephora Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge: 4x points + 50% off starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store 50% off through 11/29. Online deals up to 30% off. Code CYBERSCENT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fragrance gift sets earn 4x points through 11/30. Prices inside.`

# Technical Audit: Sephora "Black Friday just got real" email

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run recorded one hard failure: there is no plain-text alternative (0 chars). The HTML also has fixed-width, zoom-blocking and malformed-markup problems. The source is truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are largely unverifiable. The compliance and authentication warnings come from relay capture limits and are not confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is incomplete.** The QA data reports "12 material link(s) probed, 34 skipped". No status codes or redirect results were provided, so link health is unverified.
- **Tracking pixel 1** is `https://app.sephora.com/O/AQEAAZcF2g…`. It is a hidden `<img>` with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all` and no `alt`. It is functional but opaque, and it is placed outside the preheader `<div>`.
- **Tracking pixel 2** is `https://mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261126_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Faves_Thursday_PM_All_Else`. It is a 1x1 image in its own table, which is a standard open-tracking pattern.
- **Two open-tracking pixels** fire per open from different subdomains. Two pixels is redundant, though not a defect.
- **Identifier in the pixel URL.** `mi_u=4958433775694848` is a per-recipient identifier sent as a query parameter.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Fixed width.** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` forces horizontal scroll in clients that ignore or strip the `@media (max-width:480px)` block. Gmail app with non-Google accounts is one example.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid robots value.** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` should be `noindex`. The space makes the directive invalid.
- **Malformed CSS.** An `<!--[if mso]> … <![endif]-->` conditional comment sits inside the `@media` rule in `<style>`. Some parsers may drop the following rules (`.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `.mobileIcon`, `.mobileDisc` and others).
- **JSON-LD block.** The `<script type='application/ld+json'>` contains `"@type": "DiscountOffer", "discountCode": ""` (empty) and `"@type": "PromotionCard"`, which is not a standard schema.org type. Most clients strip `<script>` anyway.
- **Alt-text warnings, partly false positives.**
  - The `o.gif` pixel already has `alt=""` and `aria-hidden="true"`, which is correct for a decorative image. The QA warning looks like a false positive.
  - The `app.sephora.com/O/…` pixel has no `alt`. It is hidden, so the impact is minimal, but it should have `alt=""`.
  - The `images.harmony.epsilon.com/…/20261126_Cyber_Week…` image is a content image with no `alt` in the QA data. It is the only one with real accessibility impact. Its filename is not descriptive, so screen readers will announce it poorly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear. The JSON-LD `discountCode` is empty, but that is metadata and not rendered content. The rest of the body is truncated.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** A missing text/plain alternative is a deliverability and accessibility problem. It is a confirmed finding from the QA check, but could be an extraction artifact. Verify against the raw MIME.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both were not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the original message headers.
- **Authentication-Results.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The `From` domain is `beauty.sephora.com`. Alignment cannot be assessed.
- **Physical address and visible unsubscribe link.** The footer is truncated, so I can't confirm either. QA says an unsubscribe link was probed, but gives no result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. The only campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=20261126_Cyber_Week_Black_Friday_Fan_Faves_Thursday_PM_All_Else`, which is not a standard `utm_*` parameter and sits on the pixel URL only.
- The campaign name ("Fan_Faves_Thursday_PM") is unrelated to the subject line's "50% OFF" offer. Whether CTAs land on a matching promo page can't be checked, because CTA URLs and probe results weren't provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a text/plain MIME part, and confirm against the raw message that the 0-char result is not a capture artifact.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the original headers, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `beauty.sephora.com`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Replace the fixed `min-width:700px` with a fluid `max-width:700px; width:100%` pattern.
5. Fix the `robots` meta value to `noindex`.
6. Move the MSO conditional comment out of the `@media` block.
7. Add a descriptive `alt` to the Epsilon content image, and add `alt=""` to the `app.sephora.com` pixel.
8. Re-run link probing with a higher cap, since 34 of 46 links were skipped, and confirm CTA UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
