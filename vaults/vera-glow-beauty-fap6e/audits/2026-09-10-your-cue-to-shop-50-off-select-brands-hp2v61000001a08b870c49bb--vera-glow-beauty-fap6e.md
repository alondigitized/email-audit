# Your cue to shop: 50% off select brands 💸
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I scrolled and scrolled and scrolled some more — this is the same Frankenstein email I flagged yesterday for "Oooh, it's 50% off select beauty," except now it's wearing a different subject line. Stacking Weekend banner, App Fun teaser, an Hourglass 24-hour early access drop, a full "Discover the Deals and Drops" grid with eight products, THEN the 50% off Select Beauty banner buried mid-scroll, THEN Korean Hair Care, THEN a whole new "point of view" foundation launch, THEN a Beauty Chat AI teaser, THEN a Holiday Shopping Hack banner. That's not an email, that's Sephora's entire homepage exported to PNG.
- The one thing that did catch my eye: Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit Unlocked Palette, "NEW + ONLY AT SEPHORA," 24-hour early access. That's a genuine Rouge-tier beat — early access to a prestige launch is exactly the kind of thing I stack my Sephora shopping around. Everything else is noise competing with it.
- Would I click? Maybe, straight to the Hourglass tile, and I'd close the email the second I've scrolled past it. Forward to a friend? No — I'd screenshot the length and send it as a "look how unhinged this is" joke, not a shopping tip. This is worse cadence-fatigue than what I've been seeing from Glossier's five Labor Day emails or Drunk Elephant's five-times-in-a-week DRUNKVIP20 code — at least those brands keep it to one offer per send. Sephora crammed six sends into one.

## What's working

- Hourglass early-access banner is genuinely exciting — "24 HOURS — APP EARLY ACCESS" with a clean product shot is the strongest hero in the whole email.
- Beauty Insider point tracker up top ("500 BONUS POINTS with $75+ spend") speaks directly to my tier-math obsession — I always calculate whether a spend threshold is worth chasing.
- The "new + only at Sephora" and "limited edition" tags are doing real curation signaling that Ulta usually skips.
- Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar near the bottom at least visually acknowledges the loyalty program exists, even if it's an afterthought by that point.

## What's weak

- Six separate campaigns stapled into one email — Stacking Weekend, App Fun, Hourglass, Discover the Deals, 50% off Select Beauty, Korean Hair Care, POINT OF VIEW launch, Beauty Chat, Holiday Shopping Hack. I need a table of contents to get through this.
- The subject promised "50% off select brands" and that banner doesn't show up until deep in the scroll, after at least four other campaigns — same bait-and-switch structure I called out yesterday and the day before.
- "Exclusions/terms apply. While supplies last" fine print sits right under the 50% banner — feels like the honest offer is smaller than the headline.
- By the time I hit the new foundation launch (POINT OF VIEW), I've completely lost the thread of what this email was even supposed to be about.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Split this into six emails.** Hourglass early access deserves its own send with its own subject line — burying a genuine prestige launch under five other campaigns wastes the best asset in the email. This is the single highest-leverage fix.
- 2. Move the 50% off Select Beauty banner to the top if that's the subject line promise — right now it's Ulta-level bait-and-switch, same complaint I logged on their "ENDS TODAY 20% off" email that also buried its mechanic under repeated coupon reminders.
- 3. Cut Korean Hair Care, POINT OF VIEW, Beauty Chat, and Holiday Shopping Hack from this send entirely — each is a full campaign that deserves its own subject line and its own week.
- 4. Add my name or Rouge status near the top instead of a generic tier bar at the bottom — Sephora has this data and isn't using it.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 50% off, no digging required`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop the Ambient Lighting Edit before it's gone tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rouge members: your bonus points window closes tonight`
