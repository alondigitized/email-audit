# Ends tonight! Use your $10 off $50, $15 off $75 or $20 off your qualifying $100 purchase⁺⁺ with code GIFTS! (terms & excl. apply) 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- Fourth coupon-ladder send in a week, and I'm tired of it. The $10/$15/$20 stack is huge and the "Ends today" badge gives it a real deadline, so the offer is clear. But I've now seen this exact 20%-three-ways math on 11/1, 11/4 and twice more since. I did the points math in my head before the screenshot finished loading.
- The top of the email is still the "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards" pitch. I'm Diamond, so it treats me like a stranger. The "personalized products we know you'll love" block is a mix of Shark hair tools, medicube pads and a Lancôme fragrance. Medicube shows up twice, which reads as filler. The free 17-piece bag with $90 is the part I'd actually screenshot for my group chat.
- I'd open it, because the deadline is credible. I'd click through only for the gift-with-purchase, and I'd forward the bag to a friend. Mostly this goes to mark-as-read.

## What's working

- The $10/$15/$20 ladder is huge and instantly scannable. I can see it's 20% at each tier without trying.
- The "Ends today" badge, with "11:59pm CT" in the fine print, is a believable deadline.
- The in-store barcode and the online code GIFTS are both visible, so I don't have to dig for how to redeem.
- The free 17-piece bag with "choose 4 from 4" is a genuine perk, and the product shots make it feel tangible.
- The Shark SpeedStyle at 32% off ($249.99 down to $169.99) is a concrete price I'd consider.

## What's weak

- The Rewards "join" banner at the top is wrong for a Diamond member, and it pushes the real offer down.
- The medicube Zero Pore Pad appears twice, at 30% off both times. That's lazy "personalization."
- "Personalized prestige brands & fragrance" is a stretch. Nothing in here is shade-, skin- or routine-specific.
- The fine print is a wall of text, and the exclusions (Early Black Friday, ⁺⁺ terms) matter for my math. Sephora puts its tier rules in a clean table.
- The sale items don't show whether the coupon stacks, so I can't tell my final price.

## Recommendations

- 1. Suppress the "join Rewards" block for existing members and replace it with my points balance or tier status. That's the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the free-gift bag above the product grid, or make it the hero. It's the most distinctive thing in the email.
- 3. De-dupe the grid, and show "your price after coupon" on each tile.
- 4. Collapse the fine print into a short, tappable "Details" link.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Strict template with a 57% QA pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main technical issues are 14 plain-`http://` image requests (10 Dotomi pixels plus a Salesforce LiveContent call), widespread missing `alt` attributes, empty parameters in `mi.ulta.com` image URLs, and compliance headers that could not be verified. The HTML was truncated, so findings are limited to what the QA output surfaced.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 41 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM handling for the body CTAs are therefore unconfirmed.
- **Insecure pixels (`http://`):**
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2997/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?afceee870be08769d39f955496b70f67b=1` (10 third-party cookie-sync pixels).
  - `http://cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx?qs=ABp7ImQiOjUwNTQs…` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud dynamic content).
  - These can be blocked or stripped by clients that rewrite or block mixed content. If `LiveContent.aspx` is blocked, the dynamic content block fails to render.
- **Possibly malformed tracking-pixel `src`:** the QA output shows `https://ixtw8lub.emltrk.com/v2/ixtw8lub?i=202740837&t=164071 + pr_fy26wk41_sunday_broadcoupon_12pm_PZ_last + 32089`. The literal ` + ` segments and spaces suggest string concatenation leaked into the URL. This could also be how the QA tool formats the output, so confirm it against the raw source.
- **Ad-tech pixels:** the 10 Dotomi pixels are third-party cookie-redirect calls, an unusual load for an email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes (QA-confirmed):**
  - **Content-hosting images:**
    - `image.e.ulta.com/lib/fe35117171640578741677/m/45/08964789-…png`
    - `…/m/44/414d3e76-…jpg`
    - `…/m/45/4f7c3146-…gif` (flagged twice)
    - `…/m/1/c2df4a7d-…jpg`
    - `…/m/1/2fdb2996-…jpg`
    - `…/m/1/b1d74144-…jpg`
    - `…/m/1/7914ea59-…jpg`
    - `…/m/1/852db034-…png` (flagged twice)
  - **Personalized recommendation images:** `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/01fa2fab9849e714.png`, `3eb758650b65389e.png`, `a181e331c58479fa.png`, `618b4b9854d3933a.png` and `7f478bafdf9cd3cd.png`.
  - **Tracking pixels:** the Dotomi pixels, `LiveContent.aspx` and `ixtw8lub.emltrk.com` also lack `alt`. Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - If any of the content images carry offer or code text (the subject mentions code GIFTS and $10/$15/$20 tiers), screen-reader and images-off users lose that information.
- **Markup:** `<meta name="color-scheme">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes">` are not self-closed under an XHTML 1.0 Strict doctype. This is a minor validity issue.
- **External font:** the `<link>` to `fonts.googleapis.com` (DM Sans) is stripped by most clients, so the fallback stack must handle rendering. No fallback verification was possible from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[%…%]`) were found in the subject, preheader or visible source.
- **Empty personalization parameters:** the `mi.ulta.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs contain empty values: `…&multiplier=&memberTier=&filter_type=&filter_v…`. This suggests the personalization fields (`memberTier`, `multiplier`) did not resolve for this recipient or seed address. That may be expected for a test inbox, but it is evidence of unresolved dynamic values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for senders like this.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `e.ulta.com`.
- **Body footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address are not visible in the truncated source, so this is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable. All 41 tracked links were skipped, and no UTM parameters or final landing URLs were observed.
- The only campaign identifier visible is in the `emltrk` pixel (`pr_fy26wk41_sunday_broadcoupon_12pm_PZ_last`). It cannot be tied to a landing page or a promo-code (`GIFTS`) application.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all pixels and dynamic content over HTTPS: the 10 Dotomi pixels and `cl.s11.exct.net/LiveContent.aspx`. Drop the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels if they are not essential.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the `image.e.ulta.com` and `mi.ulta.com` content images, especially any that carry offer text. Add `alt=""` to tracking pixels.
3. Check the `ixtw8lub.emltrk.com` pixel `src` in the raw HTML for the ` + ` concatenation artifact.
4. Check that `memberTier`, `multiplier` and `filter_*` populate in `mi.ulta.com` URLs. Test with a real member profile.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw MIME headers, bypassing the relay.
6. Resolve the click-tracker redirects with a follow-redirect probe to confirm destinations, UTMs and that the `GIFTS` promo applies.
7. Self-close the color-scheme `<meta>` tags to match the XHTML doctype.
