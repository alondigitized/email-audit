# Our go-to summer lip?
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is Glossier going back to the well with Lip Line + Balm Dotcom, dressed up as a "step-by-step" tutorial, and buried in the middle is that same Red Mini Beauty Bag GWP that's been "still on" since at least July 23. I'm now something like fifteen emails deep from this brand since July 7, and my honest gut reaction opening this one is "didn't I already get this bag pitch twice?" The lip liner + balm combo is genuinely a product I'd consider — I own both shades already, actually — but the tutorial framing with the "match your lip shade to your, well, you know" line is trying so hard to be cheeky it borders on cringe rather than clever.
- The bigger issue, though, is that the email itself looks glitchy — multiple headlines and body copy are cut off mid-word ("Define your lips with Lip Lir...", "Get a free Red Mini Beauty Bag with" trailing off the edge). That's not me being picky, that's a real render bug sitting right there in my inbox, and it makes the whole send look unfinished.
- Would I click? Maybe the hero "Shop now" if I'm restocking Balm Dotcom anyway, but I'm not opening this with any urgency. Wouldn't forward to a friend — there's no actual news here, just a reskinned GWP reminder with a slightly awkward tutorial wrapper.

## What's working

- The hero shot is clean and on-brand — close-up, dewy, unmistakably Glossier, no mistaking who sent this.
- "Our go-to summer lip" is at least seasonally appropriate, unlike Sephora re-running the identical "last chance" lip grid I already scored on 7/29.
- The Lip Line + Balm Dotcom pairing is a real, specific product duo, not vague "shop the edit" filler — more concrete than the July 27 tote-bag flat lay that had zero offer anywhere.
- The Red Mini Beauty Bag GWP is still a genuinely good hook — that red patent bag photographs well and I do actually want it, same as when it scored an 8 on July 23.

## What's weak

- Multiple text blocks are visibly cut off at the edge — "Lip Lir," the nipple-shade-matching paragraph, and the GWP headline itself all truncate mid-sentence. That's a real production miss, not a nitpick.
- The "match your lip shade to your nipple color" copy is a swing that doesn't land for me — it reads like Glossier reaching for shock value instead of just explaining the shade range.
- No loyalty or points math anywhere, which as a Rouge/Diamond member is exactly the thing I'm scanning for and exactly what Ulta's 9/10 "Lucky you" send nailed with its 20% code.
- This GWP thread is getting long in the tooth — "still on" since July 23, and now it's the fourth-ish beat on the same $75 mini bag. e.l.f.'s BOGO lip oil (8/10) felt fresher because the mechanic actually changed; this is just the same reminder in a new outfit.
- CTA copy ("Shop now," "Get yours") is generic — no product name in the button itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the render bug first** — truncated headlines and body copy undercut everything else; this needs a QA pass across clients before it ships again.
- 2. **Cut or rework the nipple-shade line** — either lean into it with more confidence and context, or drop it for a straightforward "match your natural lip tone" — right now it reads unfinished, not bold.
- 3. **Give the GWP an actual deadline** — "still on" with no date reads like it'll run forever; a real cutoff (even a soft one) would push me to actually add the $75.
- 4. **Name the product in the CTA** — "Shop Balm Dotcom" beats "Get yours."
- 5. **Surface Rouge/Diamond tier perks** if Glossier has any cross-brand or points angle — right now I'm treated like any stranger, not a repeat Balm Dotcom buyer.
