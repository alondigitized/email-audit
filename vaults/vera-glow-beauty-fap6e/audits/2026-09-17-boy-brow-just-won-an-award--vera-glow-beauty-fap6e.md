# Boy Brow just won an award!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a good one — Glossier keeps their post-Labor-Day streak of restraint going, and this time it's not even a repeat of the Boy Brow content I already saw on 9/13. That was reviews-and-faces; this is "Boy Brow just won Allure's Best of Beauty 2026" with an actual award badge on the tube. That's real news, not just another "here's the product again" email, and as someone who does the points math on every purchase but still respects a genuinely earned accolade, this lands.
- The full swatch lineup is the strongest beat — seven shades laid out mustache-to-mustache so I can actually see where my shade sits, and that's the kind of shade-match signal I complain is missing from half my inbox. The 5-star review from "Sara" closing it out ("staple for 8 years... shade match is fantastic") reinforces exactly the trust cue this category needs.
- I'd click "Shop now" on the hero — I don't own Boy Brow and an Allure award is a legitimate nudge. I wouldn't forward it to a friend, though; this is a nice-to-have brow purchase, not a "you need to see this" moment. Compare that to Sally Beauty's 9/17-adjacent hair gloss launch (7/10) which had a stronger single hero shot — this one splits its energy across three separate mini-campaigns (award, product story, swatches+review) and never fully commits to one CTA moment the way Sally did.

## What's working

- The Allure "Best of Beauty 2026" badge stamped right on the product shot is real, verifiable social proof — way more credible than ILIA's fake-personal-email "browsing the VIP sale?" nonsense I scored 4/10 on 9/17.
- Full 7-shade swatch strip lets me actually judge shade range before I click, which almost nobody else in my inbox bothers to show.
- The close-up eye/brow application photo shows a real, unretouched-looking brushed-up brow — that's the exact "does this look natural on me" proof I want.
- Three distinct CTAs (Shop now / Get yours / Shop now) all point to brow product, so there's no bait-and-switch to fragrance or bodycare buried lower.
- Customer review with a name attached ("—Sara") reads like a real person, not manufactured copy.

## What's weak

- No offer, no member pricing, nothing at all for Rouge/Diamond math — I know Glossier doesn't run a loyalty program like Sephora or Ulta, but there's zero "new to Glossier? here's 10% off" landing either, so this only converts existing fans, not price-motivated cross-shoppers like me.
- The layout is doing three separate jobs (award news, product story, swatch+review) stacked vertically with no clear single focal point — my eye doesn't know whether to land on the badge, the eye close-up, or the swatches first.
- Image on the right is getting cut off at the edge in my render — the "up" in "brushed up brow" headline and the swatch image both look clipped, which reads a little unfinished for a brand this polished.
- No urgency or timing signal at all — nothing wrong with that necessarily, but after "Last call for 30% off" fatigue two weeks ago, this swings so far the other way there's no reason to act today versus next month.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead harder into the award — right now "An award-winning brow" is a decent headline but I'd put the Allure badge in the subject-line energy too and make it the single hero moment instead of splitting focus across three sections.
- 2. Fix the right-edge image clipping — the swatch grid and "brushed up" headline both look cut off, and that's a rendering polish issue on a brand whose whole identity is clean minimalism.
- 3. Add a light loyalty hook — even a "members get early access to Boy Brow refill drops" line would give repeat customers like me a reason beyond the award to act now.
- 4. Trim to one CTA path — pick either the award story or the swatch-and-review story as the hero, and demote the other to a secondary block, so my eye has one clear place to land.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Boy Brow just won Allure's Best of Beauty`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Award season for your brows 🏆`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `7 shades, 0 guesswork — see why Allure picked us.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The brow gel that just beat everyone else.`
