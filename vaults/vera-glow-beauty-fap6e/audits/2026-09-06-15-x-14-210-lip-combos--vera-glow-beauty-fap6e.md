# 15 x 14 = 210 lip combos
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "15 x 14 = 210 lip combos" — the math nerd in me is a little charmed, not gonna lie. This is ILIA doing a genuine mix-and-match campaign: Lip Sketch liner meets Overglaze gloss, 15 shades times 14 shades, save 15% on the combo. It's a legit step up from the repetitive Overglaze pushes I've been logging all week — 9/1's Motif + Gallery shade drop, then 9/3's "Yes, even when you take it off" hammering the same gloss line, then yesterday's actually-good "Build a look around blush" routine send. This one threads the needle: it's technically about Overglaze again (Motif and Gallery show up as the "NEW" shades here too), but the combo-math framing gives it a fresh hook instead of feeling like the fourth rerun.
- The shade swatches for both Lip Sketch and Overglaze are the best part — actual color chips next to each pen/gloss so I can see Pampas vs Tableau vs Rhyme vs Genre before I click, which none of Sephora's or Ulta's recent Beauty Insider digests bother with. Compare that to this morning's Sephora send still stuck on "You have 0 points" with zero product specificity — ILIA's out here doing color science while Sephora can't even fix a broken points field.
- Would I click? Yeah, probably — the Set button, since I want to see the actual $ savings math for "15% off," which isn't visible anywhere in the email itself. Would I forward this to a friend? Only if she's already an ILIA girl — the ROI story here is cute but a little inside-baseball for someone who doesn't already own a Lip Sketch.

## What's working

- The "15 x 14 = 210" hook is genuinely clever copy — it reframes a two-SKU bundle as infinite personalization, which is catnip for someone like me who cross-shops shade ranges.
- Real shade swatches for both products (Pampas, Tableau, Rhyme, Genre / Motif, Gallery) — I can actually judge undertones before clicking, unlike most mass-prestige emails that just show packaging.
- "Pairs with Lip Sketch in Pampas or Tableau" pairing callouts under each Overglaze — that's the kind of styling guidance that makes me trust the brand curated this, not just bundled random SKUs.
- Clean step-by-step structure (Step 1 / Step 2 / Shop the Set) makes a two-product bundle easy to follow without feeling like a cluttered digest — a real contrast to Sephora's autumn email I flagged for "stacking" too many modules.
- The bottom-of-email trust bar (Leaping Bunny, Pact Collective, Free U.S. shipping + free returns) is quiet reassurance I actually register as a clean-beauty shopper.

## What's weak

- "Save 15%" never shows an actual dollar figure — I have to click through to do that math myself, which is ironic for an email literally bragging about doing math for me.
- This is the second Overglaze-adjacent send in under a week featuring Motif and Gallery as "NEW" — I already logged that shade drop on 9/1, so the "new" tag is starting to feel stale by the third mention.
- The email is LONG — after the lip combo pitch it just keeps rolling into Balmy Tint, Color Block Lipstick, Lip Wrap Balm, Lip Wrap Mask, then a whole separate mini-gift module. By the time I hit "Mini Gift, Maximal Shimmer" I've mentally exited the lip-combo story.
- No VIP points math tied to this specific purchase, just the generic top banner ("4X VIP points on Overglaze Lip Gloss") — I want to know what 210 combos does to MY points balance, not a blanket multiplier.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual price or dollar-savings number next to "Save 15%" in the hero — don't make me click through for the number when the whole email's premise is "we did the math for you."
- 2. Cut the email after "Shop the Set" — the Infinite Color + Hydration grid and the Mini Gift module dilute a genuinely sharp single-concept send into another everything-bagel digest.
- 3. Tie the 4X VIP points banner directly to the Lip Combo purchase, not just "Overglaze Lip Gloss" generically — give me a "That's up to X points on this combo" line right under the hero CTA.
- 4. Since Motif and Gallery already got their own drop email on 9/1, swap them out here for two shades I haven't seen pushed yet, so this doesn't read as recycled inventory.

- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off the Lip Sketch + Overglaze duo`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See your exact savings on every shade pairing`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New Motif + Gallery shades pair perfectly with Lip Sketch`
