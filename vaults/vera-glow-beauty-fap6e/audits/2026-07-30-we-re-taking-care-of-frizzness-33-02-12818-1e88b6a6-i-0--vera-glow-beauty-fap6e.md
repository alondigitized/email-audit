## What's weak

- The National Lipstick Day banner three days after National Lipstick Day, dropped into a haircare email, feels like leftover creative nobody pulled — I already saw this exact ask on the 27th.
- "Top Picks for You" is Power Grip Primer, a face cream, and a setting spray — none of that is "for me," it's just generic bestsellers slapped under a personalized label.
- The category nav grid at the bottom is just empty grey bars with text — no thumbnails, no visual interest, looks unfinished next to how polished the hero is.
- Zero loyalty math anywhere — no Beauty Squad points, no member pricing, just an app-download icon buried in the footer. As someone who tracks Rouge and Diamond tiers obsessively, that's a blank space where a hook should be.
- No urgency anywhere on the actual hero offer — the $9 price has no deadline, so nothing pushes me to act today versus next week.

  - `/products/holy-hydration-face-cream?Size=Mini+0.529+oz+%2F+15+g` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning - Earn rewards...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&utm_source=broadcast&lid=1phcyzzughb1&bxid=6a013826e53882007d6a1618&utm_medium=email&is_retargeting=true&` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")

  All four are the two primary product CTAs plus the rewards and app-download links — i.e., the highest-value click targets in the email are broken.
