# Your crew for a great summer ‘do ️☀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, e.l.f. Hair — I didn't even know that was a sub-line, but here we are with "The Crew for a Great Summer 'Do," and it's... fine? It's a full hair-care lineup with a clear humidity/frizz hook, which is at least a real seasonal use case instead of another "NEW drop" bit. But this is a hard pivot from the pickle-and-dupe personality e.l.f. built up all week (8/20's "dill" launch, 8/17's genuinely great "Same Glow, Less Dough" dupe comparison) into a flat, grid-of-products merchandising email. No jokes, no voice, just six color-blocked product tiles in a row.
- The Bronzing Drops GWP callout mid-email is the same $50+ threshold gift I already got on 8/19 ("Get Bronzing Drops on us") — so now it's the second time this week e.l.f.'s recycled that exact offer, which reads more like "we didn't hit the redemption goal" than a fresh incentive. Nothing here is bad, it's just generic hair-care merchandising with zero of the brand's usual wit, and I'm not opening my wallet or forwarding this to anyone.
- I'd mark this as read and scroll past. Compare to ILIA's swim-proof eyeliner claim this week — that's a specific, relatable proof point. This is just "here are six products, they fight frizz."

## What's working

- The hero model shot is bright, warm, and the neon-green/lilac color-block system is genuinely eye-catching and on-brand for e.l.f.
- "Keep hair soft and smooth on hot, humid days" is a real, seasonally-relevant hook — better than a generic "new drop" tease.
- Prices are visible right on each tile ($9, $10, $9, $9) so I'm not guessing at cost before I click.
- The GWP banner (free Bronzing Drops at $50+) is a legitimate, clearly-stated offer with a visible threshold.
- "Top Picks for You" with three named products and prices is a nice personalization touch, even if generic.

## What's weak

- Zero brand voice — this is the same week that gave me "Totally Dill-lulu" and "E.L.F. Von Dill," and now it's just "Never Thirsty," "Gloss Mode," "Humidity Hero." Flat by comparison.
- The Bronzing Drops GWP is a repeat of 8/19's exact offer — I already saw this threshold gift two days ago, so it doesn't feel new.
- Six near-identical color-block tiles in a row is visual fatigue by tile four — no clear "start here" hero product once you're past the top banner.
- The category nav (New/Eyes/Lips/Face/Skincare/Hair) is just gray bars with text, no icons or imagery — feels like a placeholder, not finished design.
- Nothing here signals loyalty points, e.l.f. Beauty Squad status, or member pricing — for a brand that has a rewards program, that's a miss for someone like me who does the points math religiously with Sephora/Ulta.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the six-tile product grid down to 2-3 heroes max, and bring back the campaign's voice — even a small pun ("no frizz allowed") would've helped this feel like the same brand as the dill email.
- 2. Swap the Bronzing Drops GWP for something new — running the identical offer two sends in a row (8/19 and 8/21) trains me to ignore it.
- 3. Add a Beauty Squad points callout somewhere — I'm Rouge/Diamond tier elsewhere and actively track points; a brand with a loyalty program that never mentions it is leaving me on the table.
- 4. Give the category nav bar actual product thumbnails instead of plain gray text bars — it currently looks unfinished.
