## What's working

- The hero model actually looks like she could be my age — no over-airbrushed 19-year-old energy, which is more than I can say for half the emails in my inbox this week.
- "Get glossy this season" is a clean, single-message hero — no competing banners fighting for attention in the top fold.
- Breaking from the fifth-Appiversary-rerun pattern is a relief. Drunk Elephant did something similar this week when it finally ditched the DRUNKVIP20 code fatigue for an actual product story — Sally following suit with a real seasonal hair drop instead of another nail coupon is the right instinct.
- Product tiles (Keracolor Rose Shine, ion 24K) are specific and shoppable, not just "browse everything" links.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete discount anywhere above the fold — free 2-hour delivery on $35 isn't a deal, it's baseline convenience. Glossier's fall palette email skipped discounts entirely too, but it leaned hard into color-story storytelling to justify that; this just has generic product shots.
- No shade range, no "works on X hair color" guidance, no before/after — as someone who actually colors her hair, I need to know if this gloss works on my base before I click "Shop now."
- The Appiversary 2x Rewards point math is buried in a pink app-download banner and then again in six paragraphs of legal fine print at the bottom — Ulta at least puts the loyalty math (like the "20% off one item" mechanic I keep logging) right in the subject line or hero.
- "Shop now" is repeated four times with zero variation — no differentiation between "shop the gloss," "shop the treatment," etc.
- This is hair, not the beauty categories (skin, makeup, fragrance) I actually cross-shop Sephora/Ulta for, so even a well-made email like this doesn't hit my actual purchase intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual number in the hero — even "20% off your first gloss" would beat "Get glossy this season" for someone scanning fast.
- 2. Surface the 2x Rewards Appiversary math right under the hero headline instead of in a secondary pink banner — that's the one loyalty hook Sally has that Sephora/Ulta don't need to compete on.
- 3. Add a shade-matching cue ("works on levels 4-8") or a before/after strip — hair color is a fit/compatibility purchase, not an impulse buy.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy per product tile: "Shop Rose Shine," "Shop ion 24K" — not four identical "Shop now" buttons.

- Image missing `alt` text: `impression_pixel` (t.myvisualiq.net) — same as above, should have `alt=""`.
