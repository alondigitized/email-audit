# Double the gifts
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- ILIA is back in a full designed template after two plain-text "Marissa" notes, and my first reaction was relief. The "free gift edit" hero is clean, but I'm still doing the 20/25 points math. The top banner says 25% off sitewide, the copy says 20% off sitewide, and there's an asterisk on "double the gifts." That's the same number shuffle I flagged on 11/22 and 11/23. I can't tell which number applies to me without clicking through to the fine print.
- The gift-with-purchase structure is better than the plain discount. A free complexion stick with any order is a real hook, and a free mini of choice at $100+ gives me a spend threshold to plan around. Sephora's Cyber Week email wants me to chase sequins, and this one at least tells me what I'm getting. But it's the fourth ILIA send in three days, and "Double the gifts" is vague as a subject.
- Would I click? Probably yes, because the free complexion stick is low-friction and I like a gift with purchase. I'd forward it to nobody. I'd mark it read and check the fine print on the site first.

## What's working

- The "free complexion stick with any order" block has no threshold, so I can see the value right away.
- The "free mini of choice with $100+" block gives me a clear spend tier. I can calculate whether a $100 cart is worth it.
- The product shots with hand-drawn callouts ("firming + smoothing," "weightless coverage") tell me what the product does, which is more useful than a bare packshot.
- The "very important perks" block lays out free shipping, points and early access, so the loyalty benefits are visible.
- The footer trust cues (Leaping Bunny, 1% for the Planet, Personalized Shade Match) fit how I shop clean beauty.

## What's weak

- The banner says 25% off sitewide, the hero copy says 20% off, and "double the gifts*" carries an asterisk with no explanation in the body. After the 11/23 "Your picks, up to 25% off" mess, this feels dishonest.
- The three "GET YOUR GIFT →" buttons are identical and generic. I can't tell which gift each one claims, and they all probably land on the same page.
- The hero is six moody tiles with no product name, no shade and no price. It's pretty but tells me nothing.
- "Double the gifts" doesn't say what the two gifts are. I only learn it's two separate offers by scrolling past the fold.
- The perks block repeats VIP and shipping messaging from the banner and from the footer icons. It's the third time I'm told to join VIP.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one discount number and use it everywhere, in the banner, the hero and the subject. If 25% requires VIP, say "25% off for VIP, 20% for everyone" in the hero, not in an asterisk.
- 2. Make each CTA specific: "Claim free complexion stick," "Get my free mini at $100." Better, link each one to that offer's product page.
- 3. Put the two gifts in the hero. "Free complexion stick + free mini at $100" beats a collage of tiles.
- 4. Add a points line for VIP members, such as "VIP earns 2x points this week." That's the math I'm actually doing.
- 5. Cut the perks block or fold it into the banner, so I don't see the same VIP pitch three times.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free complexion stick on every order, plus a free mini at $100+.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://d3k81ch9hvuctc.cloudfront.net/company/SguDsz/images/c9cefca7-6b18-443a-896b-9e98ec5d842a.jpeg` has no `alt` attribute. If this is content or a CTA image, it is invisible with images blocked and to screen readers.
