# HURRY! 🏃‍♀️ e.l.f. x Bubble is selling fast 👀
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so this is the actual e.l.f. x Bubble launch — not another recycled Bubble teaser like yesterday's "Bestie-coded" send that just reshuffled the same lip oil trio into a grid. "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" is cute, the $10 price point is clear, and I can actually see two real hero products (Glow Talk tinted lip serum, Camo Slide blush serum) with named ingredients and named benefits. This is the most substance e.l.f. has given me all week.
- But "selling fast" in the subject with no actual stock counter, no "3 left," nothing — that's the same soft-urgency bluff Skechers pulls with "limited availability," and I don't buy it more from e.l.f. than I did from them. And genuinely, $10 lip serum and $10 blush serum are impulse-tier prices — I don't need a coupon math session for this the way I do for Sephora Insider or Ulta's 20%-off-one-item games. This one I'd actually click, mostly out of curiosity about the shade range, and maybe screenshot to my group chat since Bubble x anything usually gets a reaction.

## What's working

- Real product news for once — two named 2-in-1 hybrid products with actual descriptions (tint formula, hyaluronic acid serum), not just a vibes grid like 8/11's send.
- $10 price called out twice, clearly, no math required — refreshing after Ulta's "20% off one qualifying item, code SCORE, terms & excl. apply" nonsense from 7/26 and 8/10.
- "3 SHADES" badge on both hero shots gives me a real reason to click through instead of guessing.
- Clean pink-gradient product photography that's consistent with the Bubble collab aesthetic, not a stock-photo mismatch like Sally Beauty's Zotos/AGEbeautiful mixup.
- "Top Picks for You" section with pricing visible ($11, $3, $11) is a nice personalization touch, even if it's generic Power Grip/Cream Glide stuff.

## What's weak

- "HURRY" and "selling fast" in the subject with zero visible urgency mechanism in the body — no countdown, no stock indicator, no "ends" date. It's manufactured pressure, same energy as Skechers' fake scarcity plays.
- No loyalty or Beauty Squad points math anywhere, which is wild given e.l.f. has a program — I'm doing that math automatically at Sephora and Ulta, and e.l.f. just... doesn't bring it up.
- The nav bar (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) is six flat gray bars stacked vertically — feels like a placeholder table of contents, not a designed section.
- "Top Picks for You" products have zero connection to the Bubble collab — Power Grip Primer and a setting spray dilute what was otherwise a focused launch email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the fake urgency in the subject or back it with something real — a countdown clock or "only while supplies last" tied to an actual visible stock signal. Don't just say "selling fast" and show nothing.
- 2. Add a Beauty Squad points callout near the $10 price — "earn X points" — since that's the exact mental math I'm already running at Sephora/Ulta and e.l.f. is leaving it on the table.
- 3. Cut "Top Picks for You" from this specific send or swap in Bubble-adjacent products — a primer and setting spray break the collab focus.
- 4. Redesign the nav bar as an actual horizontal menu instead of six stacked gray rectangles — reads unfinished on scroll.
