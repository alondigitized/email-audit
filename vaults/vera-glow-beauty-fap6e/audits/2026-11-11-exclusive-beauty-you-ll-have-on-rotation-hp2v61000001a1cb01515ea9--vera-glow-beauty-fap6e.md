# Exclusive beauty you’ll have on rotation
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- I opened this one on the points math. It gave me a free Gisou Honey Infused Hair Oil trial size with $30+ and code GIFTGISOU, plus a Sephora-exclusive $45 ($54 value) lip mask and oil set. That's a real offer, and I can do the math. Last week's "ENDS TODAY" Savings Event sends had nothing like it. It's also the same lane as 11/10's "The best NEW holiday sets go fast", with Gisou now the star.
- Then it ran on forever. After the Gisou hero there's a bestseller row, an eight-item gift grid, a "Spot it. Shop it." block, a Makeup Gifts banner and a gift guide. That's four or five emails stacked into one. There's no date on the free Gisou, so nothing tells me to act today. Ulta would have put one hard deadline up top. I'd click the Gisou hero and probably forward the lip set to a friend. I'd skim the rest.

## What's working

- The "Complimentary Gisou" hero is a clear threshold offer ($30+, code GIFTGISOU). It's easy to fold into my points math.
- The Insider badge in the top corner makes me feel like a known member. That beats Skechers' "Hello, Stranger!" energy.
- Value-framing is everywhere: $35 ($51 value) for the Mini Lip Trio, $34 ($56 value) for Milk Makeup, $39 ($66 value) for Make Up For Ever. I can compare these against Ulta's 21 Days.
- The "NEW • ONLY AT SEPHORA • LIMITED EDITION" tags are a real curation signal, and the clean-beauty badges are a nice touch.
- The Beauty Shopper chat block is a personalization nudge. Glossier's Discovery Kit email didn't have anything like it.

## What's weak

- Two heroes compete: the free Gisou at the top and the "ONLY AT SEPHORA" $45 set right under it. I had to work out which one was the deal.
- There's no deadline anywhere. "Complimentary" with "Exclusions/terms apply" and no end date means no urgency.
- The email is far too long. By the gift grid I was scrolling past Dedcoon and The Ordinary, which aren't what I came for.
- Insider points are absent. For a Rouge member there's no "earn 2x" and no tier perk callout. The credit card and app tiles at the bottom feel like filler.
- The tap targets and small text under the product tiles are tiny on a phone. I'd squint at the prices.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one hero (the Gisou gift) and one product grid. Move the Beauty Shopper chat above the grid.
- 2. Add a real end date to the free Gisou, and put "Rouge: earn X points" next to it.
- 3. Give the gift grid a single price ladder ("Under $40", "Under $75") instead of eight unrelated brands.
- 4. Drop the footer filler tiles or move them out of the main flow.

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Redirect wrappers hide destinations.** CTAs use opaque tracked redirects such as `https://app.sephora.com/T/v61000001a1cb01515ea919916e96638858/4fa26791.../4fa26791-4486-4350-b2a0-6b802f79fd23?__dU__=...`. The final landing URLs can't be seen from the source, so they can't be checked.

The second is `mi.sephora.com/p/up/f6716d0a0f52104e/o.gif?mi_u=4958433775694848&mi_ecmp=20261111_Exclusive_Gifts`. Neither is a defect. The second carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Both tracking images lack `alt`** (QA warnings). Add `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Likely broken CSS in the mobile `@media` block.** A `<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {...}<![endif]-->` conditional comment sits inside the `@media only screen and (max-width:480px)` CSS block. Browsers don't parse HTML comments or conditionals inside CSS. That probably invalidates `img.mso-size` and may also drop the next rule, `.mobileHeaderTierRouge`, because the stray `-->` becomes part of its selector. This is parser-dependent and I haven't confirmed it by rendering.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4. Most email clients ignore this tag.
- **Fixed-width wrapper.** `<table width="700" style="min-width:700px; width:700px">` has no override in the visible mobile rules, so it may force horizontal scroll on narrow clients. The truncated source doesn't let me confirm this.
- **Minor:** `<meta name="robots" content="no index">` is invalid (it should be `noindex`) and has no effect in email. `<title>Sephora</title>` is generic.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `DiscountOffer` block has `"discountCode": ""`, and the `PromotionCard` block has no properties. Gmail will ignore both, so the markup does nothing.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tier-specific classes (`mobileHeaderTierRouge`, `mobileHeaderTierVIB`, `mobileHeaderTierBI`) suggest conditional tier modules. I can't verify them from the truncated HTML.
