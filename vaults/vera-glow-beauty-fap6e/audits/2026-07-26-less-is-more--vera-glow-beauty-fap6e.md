# 🧼 Less is more!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Less is more" — no offer, no discount, no urgency, just Lush going full brand-manifesto on me about naked packaging and "over 30 years" of stripping down formulas. I respect the sustainability angle, I do actually care about that stuff, but this is email thirty-two-ish in my tracker and it's the first one in weeks that isn't chasing me with a GWP or a countdown, which honestly feels like a breather rather than a miss.
- The visuals are doing a lot of work here — the wet-hands-holding-shampoo-bars hero shot is genuinely pretty, and "STRIP DOWN" in that scrappy white script over the teal grid background has more personality than half the flat-lay product shots I've been getting from ILIA and Sally Beauty lately. But there's zero price anchor anywhere in this email — not even a "shop naked haircare" starting-at price — and for me, someone who does Sephora points math in her sleep, an email with no offer and no loyalty callout just isn't a click.
- I'd read this on my commute, nod at the sustainability messaging, and archive it without tapping anything. Not forwarding it — my friends want the deal emails, not the brand ethos ones.

## What's working

- The "STRIP DOWN" hero with the hands-and-shampoo-bar photo is one of the better hero shots Lush has sent me — actual texture and color instead of another product-on-white flat-lay.
- Clean top nav (NEW / HAIRCARE / NAKED) gives me an immediate sense of where I'd land if I clicked.
- Each product tile has a real ingredient callout (Irish moss, agave syrup, cocoa butter) — that's the ingredient-specificity beauty shoppers like me actually want, and it's more than Drunk Elephant gave me in their "SMS SOS" send.
- The teal/pink/orange color blocking between sections keeps the scroll visually interesting instead of one long white product grid.
- "Carry-On Besties" travel-kit section at the bottom is a smart seasonal tie-in for late-July trip season.

## What's weak

- No offer, no percentage off, no member pricing anywhere — after ILIA's "last day" urgency and Sephora's "save up to 50%" this week, this email feels like the odd one out with nothing concrete to act on.
- Zero Lush Rewards or loyalty-tier mention despite this brand having a program — as a Rouge/Diamond points-tracker, that's the first thing my eye goes looking for and it's just not there.
- Six product tiles plus a hennas banner plus a travel kit is a lot of scrolling for a subject line that promised a philosophy, not a catalog — the hierarchy gets muddy by the third row.
- The $5 off Lush app banner up top is the only real incentive in the whole email, and it's buried in a thin black strip that's easy to skim past.
- "STRIP DOWN" as hero copy is a little confusing on first glance — I read it as a callout to undress, not to "naked" packaging, until I got to the body copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer front and center — even a modest "naked haircare starting at $X" or a Rewards-points multiplier tied to this collection would turn this from a nice brand story into something I'd tap through on.
- 2. Trim the product grid from six tiles to three — pick the hero SKUs (Seanik, American Cream, Hennaed) and cut the rest, or move them to a "shop the full range" secondary CTA.
- 3. Move the $5 app-off banner down into its own section with real visual weight instead of a thin strip above the logo — right now it reads like a footer, not an incentive.
- 4. Add a Lush Rewards point callout somewhere in the naked/haircare pitch — sustainability + points math is a combo that would actually land with me.
- 5. Tighten "STRIP DOWN" with a subhead that clarifies packaging immediately, not three sentences down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `We stripped the packaging. Not the results.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop shampoo bars with less waste, more ingredients`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `30 years of less packaging, more payoff for your hair`
