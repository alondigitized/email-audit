# Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- Finally, a real number. After "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members" on 11/18 and "Final hours for early access" earlier today, both of which were account-creation pushes with no product, Glossier gave me the sale itself. 25% off everything, "biggest sale of the year, now live." That's what I wanted two days ago.
- The mess starts right after the headline. The hero says 25% but the burst on the image also says "30% OFF $100+**". There's a free Crème de You at $100 in the top banner, and an asterisk on everything. I do points math for a living, and I can't tell whether I'm getting 25% or 30% on a $100 cart. Sephora would put the tiers in one clean line. This email makes me hunt for them.
- I'd open it, and I'd probably click. I wouldn't forward it, because the offer is muddled and I'd have to explain it to my friend.

## What's working

- "Enjoy 25% off everything*" is plain and sits top-left, so I read the offer in under a second. That's better than Sally Beauty's "Gift ideas for everyone on your list" with no number in the hero.
- The blue "Shop Black Friday" button is big and easy to see.
- The Community Favorites grid (Glossier You, Balm Dotcom, After Baume, Boy Brow) gives me real products to tap. The balm stack photo is the strongest image, and it ties back to the 3-for-$30 balm sends from 11/12 to 11/17.
- The gift-with-purchase strip at the top and the "Care for a gift?" block both push a free Crème de You at $100+. That works as a basket-builder.

## What's weak

- The offer stack contradicts itself: 25% off everything, 30% off $100+ in the burst, and a free Crème de You at $100. Drunk Elephant's "$158 for $69" was clearer, and ILIA's "up to 25%" versus "20% off" mismatch bugged me for the same reason. I can't tell if the 30% and the gift stack.
- The render looks cropped on the right. The banner reads "...when you spend $100" and gets cut off, the second-column "Shop now" buttons run off the edge, and the "Care for a gift" copy is clipped mid-sentence ("soft and sil..."). This looks like a layout bug.
- The "Black Friday Sale" marquee band is dead space. It repeats what I already know and has no link or deadline.
- There is no end date anywhere, just an asterisk and "*Terms apply" at the very bottom in tiny print. Nothing tells me when this stops, so there's no urgency.
- No member or points mention, even though the last two sends were all about membership. I'm the person who joined for early access, and nothing here says my status matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one offer in the hero. Pick the tier structure (25% sitewide, 30% at $100+, or the free gift) and show it as a single clear ladder, with the end date next to it.
- 2. Fix the cropping so the right column and buttons render fully.
- 3. Replace the marquee band with a countdown or a "ends Monday" line.
- 4. Add a "Members: your early access applies here" line so the 11/18 and 11/19 sends pay off.
- 5. Make the tiny terms legible and put the key exclusions above the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off all of Glossier, plus a free Crème de You at $100`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday is live: 25% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Balm Dotcom, Glossier You and Boy Brow are all in. Sale ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $100, get a free Crème de You. Members shop first.`
