# Did you know that points = rewards?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Sephora email in a week that gave me points math to do, so I stayed. It's the Rewards Bazaar drop, and the hero is a teal "November rewards are here!" with a SHOP NOW button. It's a clean, calm open after the 11/6–11/9 "ENDS TODAY" grind. It's also the second Sephora catalog-style send today, after "The best NEW holiday sets go fast."
- Once I scrolled, it turned into a long, dense list of point-redemption rewards: Summer Fridays bundles at 2,500, 3,500 and 7,000 points, then a 750+ grid. This is how I actually shop. I stock up at the Sephora Beauty Insider event and I always check what my points buy. The catch is that I can't see my own balance or tier anywhere, so the math is only half done. Would I click? Yes, probably the Summer Fridays routine, because the branded tote plus the "24 available now" count creates real pressure. I'd forward it only to a friend who's also a Rouge.

## What's working

- The point costs sit right under each reward (2,500, 3,500, 7,000, 750, 1,500), so I can do the math at a glance.
- Stock counts like "40 coming soon", "32 available now" and "16 available now" create credible scarcity. This is the Rewards Bazaar mechanic I actually care about.
- The Summer Fridays "Brand Spotlight" gives the email one hero brand, so it reads as curated rather than a random dump. Ulta's $10/$15/$20 ladder has no equivalent of this.
- The "750+ Point Rewards" grid is a nice entry tier, and Too Faced, Tower 28, Dolce&Gabbana and Sulwhasoo give it real prestige range.
- The hero is uncluttered, with one button and a clear "New rewards are here!" tag.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the email shows my current points balance or tier. I'm Rouge, so "You shopped and earned points" should come with a number.
- The email is very long. The 750+ grid and the tiny product copy make it a scroll marathon, and some of the text is small enough to need zooming.
- The hero is vague. "Shop now" doesn't say what I'm shopping for, and the hero doesn't name a single reward or point cost.
- It looks like a catalog again, one day after the sequin-background gift sets email. That's two product-grid sends in two days.
- The legal and footer block is a wall of tiny text. The CTA strip at the bottom (credit card, app download) is also generic.
- There's no deadline for when the rewards expire or when the Bazaar closes, so "Don't wait, they go fast!" is the only urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put my balance and tier in the hero, e.g. "Vera, you have 1,840 points, here's what they get you." That one change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, like "Redeem my points", and name one hero reward with its point cost.
- 3. Add a real end date for the Bazaar, plus a "low stock" badge when a count drops under 20.
- 4. Cut the grid to the best 6–8 items, or add filters for point tiers (under 1,000 / 1,000–3,000 / 3,000+).
- 5. Don't send this on the same day as another catalog email. Space the sends out.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New rewards from 750 points. Some have under 20 left.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Summer Fridays bundles + a free tote. See what your balance buys.`
