# A gift for you: Crème de You
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- My first reaction was that this is the third Glossier send in three days, and it's the same 25% sale again. Yesterday's "Our biggest sale of the year is live!" and the day before's "Enjoy 25% off EVERYTHING" had the same offer. Today the offer is wrapped around a free Crème de You at $100. At least the gift-with-purchase gives me something new to do math on.
- The math is simple. $100 gets me a free Crème de You plus 30% off instead of 25%. That is a real threshold, and I'd build a cart to hit it. But the layout works against it. The hero crops both badges ("BLACK FRIDAY SALE" and "25% OFF EVERYTHING") off the right edge, and the "$100**" fine print is microscopic. The "Get yours" button doesn't tell me what I'm getting. Would I click? Maybe, if I were already building a Glossier cart. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the deadline email.

## What's working

- The "free Crème de You with your $100 order" headline is a concrete gift-with-purchase, and I can read it in two seconds.
- "25% off everything or 30% off with your $100 order" is a clean two-tier structure that I can do math on.
- The five-star review from Emily mentions scent longevity and skin feel, and that's the detail I'd want before I buy.
- The red Crème de You jar, the red candle and the shoe shot with the perfume hang together as a holiday palette.
- The blue buttons are big, full-width where it counts, and easy to tap on a phone.

## What's weak

- The hero badges are cut off on the right edge. "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" and "25% OFF EVERYTHING" are visibly clipped, which looks sloppy for the main offer.
- The "$100**" fine print is tiny, and the asterisk terms sit at the very bottom. I can't see whether the gift is limited to one per order or ends on a certain date.
- There is no deadline. "Still on" is vague, with no end date and no countdown. Sephora would put "ends 11/27" front and center.
- There is no shade, skin-type or scent info on the middle block. It's a leg photo with "Believe the ❤️," which is cute but doesn't tell me which product it is. It also doesn't tell me why the gift is worth $100.
- It repeats the last two days. This is the third send in a row on the same 25% sale, with only a different skin on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the end date and the fine print next to the headline, for example "Free full-size Crème de You at $100, ends Sunday."
- 2. Fix the hero crop so the "25% OFF EVERYTHING" badge isn't cut off.
- 3. Name the product under the review and add a price, so the middle block isn't just a leg photo.
- 4. Add a progress cue, such as "You're $X away from your free gift," or show the Crème de You value.
- 5. Change the CTA to "Claim my free Crème de You."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free Crème de You at $100 + 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your free gift unlocks at $100, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get 30% off plus a full-size Crème de You. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The $100 math: 30% off + a free gift. Here's what to add.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Klaviyo-sent, Stripo-built (`stripocdn.email`) email from `gTEAM@eml.glossier.com`. The automated QA shows no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed as sender defects. The only confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes and one invalid-markup oddity. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks couldn't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links appear to be wrapped in Klaviyo click tracking (`https://ctrk.klclick.com/…`). Final destinations and UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 7, which were footer, social and utility links, deprioritized by design. The probe results for the 12 weren't included, so link health is **not confirmed**. The unsubscribe link is always probed, but its result isn't shown.
- The open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7XSG9J2057DSJP2KA3PRAVG` has no `alt`. This is minor (see §3).
- `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/…">` directly after `<body>` is invalid markup. `rel` and `href` aren't valid attributes on `<span>`. It's likely a tracking or preheader artifact, and it's harmless in practice, but it's non-standard.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 5 images** (confirmed by QA):
  - `heroimage_1_3.jpg`
  - `frame_3992.jpg`
  - `group_3986_7.jpg`
  - `group_3987_8.jpg`
  - `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M7XSG9J2057DSJP2KA3PRAVG` (tracking pixel)
- With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the hero and content images carry no text. The tracking pixel should use `alt=""`. The missing alt attribute on the content images is the real issue.
- The structure looks sound:
  - viewport meta is present
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set
  - MSO conditional comments and `mso-hide` are used
  - `@media (max-width: 600px)` rules are present
  - `lang="en"` and `dir="ltr"` are set
- Hover states and rollover images (`.rollover`, `.bh:hover`) are in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks, so they degrade safely in Outlook.
- No issues found beyond the alt text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `{% %}`). The truncation means the lower body wasn't checked.
