## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound, with a table-based XHTML layout and MSO/VML namespaces. It has three confirmed code-level issues. The recipient's email address is exposed in a tracking URL, three images load over `http://`, and the invalid `@import` and viewport settings affect fonts and zoom. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified (QA pass rate 57%, 0 errors, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 14 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. I can't confirm they resolve or where they land.
- **Recipient email in a URL:** the image `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` puts the recipient address in the query string in plaintext. This can leak through proxy logs, referrers and image-caching layers.
- **Three tracking domains:** `mi.drunkelephant.com`, `drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com` and `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com` are all in use.
- **Pixel:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holidaygiftguid` is a standard open pixel. It carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Insecure images:** three social icons load over `http://` from `image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/`. They are `cc02d07d-…png` (Facebook), `9b452915-…png` (Instagram) and `29f975e1-…png` (TikTok). Clients that block mixed content may show them as broken.
- **Missing alt text:** the `o.gif` pixel and the Everest tracking image have no alt text. For pixels, `alt=""` is acceptable, but the QA check flags them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** `<html xmlns=…>` has no `lang`, so screen readers can't pick the right language.
- **Invalid `@import`:** the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` rules come after the `body{…}` rule. Per the CSS spec they must come first, so they are ignored.
  - The same fonts are also loaded through `<link>` tags in `<head>`.
  - These fonts come from a third-party host (`db.onlinewebfonts.com`) and are unsupported in Gmail and Outlook.
- **Malformed CSS (lower confidence):** `<!-- … -->` HTML comments sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block, around the `contenttable` and `mobile` rules. Parsers may drop the rules next to them. The source is truncated, so I couldn't check what follows.
- **Truncated source:** the rest of the HTML isn't available, so I couldn't audit image `alt` attributes or table roles beyond what QA reported.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens:** none are visible in the supplied source.
- **PII exposure:** the recipient address appears in the Everest URL (see section 2) and is not hashed or tokenized.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender fault.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** also not found, with the same relay caveat. Without it, one-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a subdomain of `drunkelephant.com`, so alignment would need checking in raw headers.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign tags:** `mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_holidaygiftguid` and the Everest `c=us_de_broad…` parameter show campaign tagging that matches the "holiday gift guide" naming.
- **Landing pages:** these couldn't be checked because the 14 tracking links were skipped. I can't confirm UTM propagation or that the destination matches the "All the gifts." subject.
- **Fonts:** the `@font-face` for `BrownRegular` points to `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…/v1719565389232/…`. This is a versioned site-asset path that can break if the site deploys a new version.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove or hash the plaintext email in the Everest tracking URL (`?e=…@etell.app`), and use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
2. Switch the three social icons to `https://` (`image.cheers.drunkelephant.com` should already serve TLS).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the stylesheet, or drop them in favor of the `<link>` tags. Add web-safe fallbacks to the font stacks, and avoid hosting the fonts on `onlinewebfonts.com`.
5. Remove the `<!-- -->` comments from inside the `@media` block.
6. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
7. Check the raw `.eml` headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
8. Follow the 14 skipped tracking redirects with a real browser or HTTP client to verify the landing URLs and UTMs.
9. Host the Brown Regular font on a stable asset path instead of the versioned Demandware URL.
