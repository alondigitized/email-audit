# Get a free Applebaby Keychain
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Okay, email twelve. Twelve emails from Glossier since July 7th, and I'm honestly a little punch-drunk at this point — but I'll give them this: the GWP mechanic keeps evolving instead of just repeating, and this one's actually cute. That little red Applebaby keychain hanging out of a denim pocket is a genuinely good lifestyle shot, way more "I'd actually clip this to my tote" than the flat-lay product shots most brands default to. Twenty percent off the Balm Dotcom Quintet plus a free limited keychain is a real, specific offer, not just vague seasonal noise.
- That said — I've now seen every single Balm Dotcom flavor in this campaign across a half-dozen sends (cherry, mango, strawberry, the I ❤️ NY apple), and this email is basically just re-plating the same "Summer Suggestions" grid I've scrolled past before. There's zero tier or loyalty language here, which stands out to me specifically because I'm doing points math in my head on every single beauty email I open — Glossier's rewards program barely exists compared to what Sephora Rouge or Ulta Diamond gives me, and a keychain GWP isn't going to close that gap.
- Would I click? Probably, just because Balm Dotcom is a cart-repeat staple for me and 20% off is real money. Would I forward it to a friend? No — this is a "add to cart later" email, not a "you have to see this" email.

## What's working

- The denim-pocket keychain hero shot is genuinely well-styled — it made the free gift feel tangible instead of like clip-art, unlike Ulta's TATCHA GWP email which just slapped a product photo in a banner and called it a day.
- The 20% off + free keychain offer is stacked and specific — two real incentives, not a vague "shop now."
- Clean visual hierarchy: hero offer, then a tidy 2x2 "Summer Suggestions" grid, each with its own CTA. No render bugs, no overlapping text, nothing broken.
- The fruit styling per flavor (cherries for black cherry, mango + peach for mango) is a small but effective sensory cue — this is the kind of merchandising detail ILIA nailed in their "goes well with gloss" email, and Glossier's doing a version of it here too.

## What's weak

- Twelve emails in under three weeks and this is still the same Balm Dotcom Quintet I've already seen pushed as gift sets, NY-exclusive flavors, and now a keychain GWP. The well is getting shallow — Sally Beauty's flash sale email felt fresher and I've only seen that brand once this month.
- Zero personalization or loyalty signal. No "as a Glossier member" language, no points, nothing that treats me like a repeat customer versus a first-time browser — a real miss for someone who tracks Rouge and Diamond tier math obsessively.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere on this email. "Limited edition" keychain but no countdown, no "while supplies last" framing — feels like it could sit in my inbox for a month and still be "valid."
- The grid product shots (cherries, strawberries) are gorgeous but they're the exact same styling language Glossier's used in prior Balm Dotcom sends — at email twelve, I'm starting to recognize the photo shoot, not just the product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add real urgency — a specific "while keychains last" or a date cutoff would do more for me than another pretty product grid. Right now nothing forces me to act today.
- 2. Surface loyalty/points language if Glossier has any rewards mechanic at all — even a small "members get X" line would differentiate this from the eleven emails before it.
- 3. Cut the grid down to 2 flavors instead of 4 — I've already seen all of these; showing me fewer, framed as "the ones people are actually buying," would feel more curated than exhaustive.
- 4. Lead with the keychain image bigger/first, since that visual is the actual differentiator versus yet another Balm Dotcom flavor push.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free Applebaby keychain — while they last`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off Balm Dotcom + a keychain that's too cute`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick your flavor, we'll toss in the keychain — free while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Denim pocket not included, but the keychain is.`
