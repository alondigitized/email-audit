# Get up to 50% on select beauty and get 500 bonus points with $75 spend ❣️
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Stacking Weekend" is actually a decent concept and I clocked it immediately — this isn't just another "50% off Select Beauty" rerun, it's Sephora explicitly telling me to layer the % off, the bonus points, AND a free sample bag on one order. That's catnip for someone like me who's always doing the math on whether a haul clears the next points threshold. The Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar sitting right under the "Stacking Weekend" banner is a nice touch — it's telling me who this email is even for before I scroll another inch.
- That said, my eye also caught the fine print bleeding into everything: "Select Beauty," "Exclusions/terms apply," a same-day delivery banner competing for top billing, then a hair sample bag, then a $10 Beauty Insider Cash redemption, then a product grid, then ANOTHER 50% off Select Beauty banner (this time "stila, Sol de Janeiro, and Lancôme, Today Only"), then Rhode, then an event invite. It's a lot of offers stacked on top of the "stacking" concept itself, and by the time I hit the second 50%-off banner I'd genuinely lost track of what expires when.
- Would I click? Yes, specifically on the second "50% off Select Beauty" banner since it names actual brands (Sol de Janeiro is a real want for me), but I wouldn't forward this to a friend — it's too crowded to send someone without a "scroll to the pink part" caveat.

## What's working

- The "Stacking Weekend" framing is a genuinely new mechanic, not the fifth identical 50%-off template I've logged since 8/28 — feels like real strategy from Sephora's marketing team.
- Tier tabs (INSIDER / VIB / ROUGE) front and center — Sephora knows I'm tracking status, and showing it unprompted reads as "we know you."
- 500 bonus points at $75 spend is concrete points math I can actually run in my head before I open my cart.
- "Only at Sephora" badges on the product grid (Make Up For Ever, NEST New York, The INKEY List) are a real curation signal — that's the brand-exclusivity flex Ulta straight up cannot make.
- Naming actual brands in the second 50%-off banner (stila, Sol de Janeiro, Lancôme) beats the vague "Select Beauty" language used everywhere else.

## What's weak

- Too many competing CTAs — same-day delivery, stacking weekend, $10 cash redemption, product grid, second 50% banner, Rhode, and an event invite all in one scroll. My eye doesn't know where to land first.
- The "50% off Select Beauty" line shows up TWICE with different expiration framing (ends 9/8 vs. "Today Only") — that's confusing, not urgent, and it's the same beat I flagged in the 9/2 "hottest drops" send.
- "Exclusions/terms apply" is stamped on nearly every module — after the third one I start assuming the offer isn't as good as it looks.
- The Rhode section ("New from Rhode... Spotwear in a new strawberry shape") is cute but totally untethered from the stacking/points narrative — feels bolted on rather than curated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero offer and cut the rest to a secondary row. Lead with "Stack 50% off + 500 points + free samples" as a single unified banner instead of three separate red boxes that all look like different sales.
- 2. Merge the two "50% off Select Beauty" banners into one — naming brands (stila, Sol de Janeiro, Lancôme) up top instead of burying that specificity two-thirds down.
- 3. Move the Rouge/VIB/Insider tier bar even higher, above "Stacking Weekend," so status-driven shoppers like me know the deal's relevance before we scroll.
- 4. Cut or move "Come Hang with Us" and the Rhode block to a separate, focused send — this email is trying to be a sale email and a discovery email at once.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stack it: 50% off + 500 points this weekend only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Rouge, your stacking weekend starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `50% off Select Beauty, bonus points at $75, and a free sample bag — all at once.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your tier, your points, your 50% off — ends 9/8.`
