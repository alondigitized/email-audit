# Final hours for early access
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- I've been on Glossier's list for weeks, and this is the second "become a member" push in two days. After 11/18's "Black Friday Starts Wednesday for Members" I was hoping for a real product or a real number. I got a number, but it's confusing. The hero says **25% OFF EVERYTHING***, and a small circle in the corner says **30% OFF $100+***. Which one is mine? I can't tell without the asterisk, and the "Membership terms here" link is tiny and sits at the very bottom.
- It's still a "create an account" ad dressed as a sale email. There's no product, no price and no points math. Compare that to Glossier's own 11/12 send, "Get 3 Balm Dotcoms for $30!". That one worked because I could calculate $10 a tube against $16 list. Here I'd have to sign up before I could find out what I'm saving. I'd click out of curiosity about the tiers, but I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the sale itself.

## What's working

- The 25% in huge white type on electric blue is readable from across the room. The eye lands there first.
- "Final Call" plus "ends tonight" is a credible deadline, and it fits a Black Friday early-access window.
- The "Why Join" list (early access, birthday gifts, event invites) is clean and scannable, and it shows the loyalty angle.
- The bath photo with the pink bottle is on-brand and looks like someone my age. The blue button is easy to tap.

## What's weak

- The 25% and the 30% on the same hero conflict. The 30% is clipped by the circle and its fine print is illegible.
- Both CTAs ("Create account" and "Join today") ask me to sign up first. I'm already a subscriber, so the email treats me like a stranger.
- "Everything*" with a footnote I can't read is the opposite of an honest offer. Is it sitewide, or only $100+? Does it exclude sets?
- No hero product, no best-sellers, no prices. The "SALE" ticker strip is cut off on the right edge and adds nothing.
- The footer's smiley and hand graphics are huge and take up more space than the terms. The membership terms are in tiny grey italics.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one number and state the terms on the face of the email, e.g. "25% off everything, members only, ends tonight at midnight ET." Drop the competing 30% badge.
- 2. Add three to four hero products with sale prices, such as Balm Dotcom and Cloud Paint. I want to do the math before I sign up.
- 3. Segment existing subscribers. Show "Already a member? Shop now" to people like me. Ulta and Sephora both make me log in rather than re-sell me the program.
- 4. Show tier math: what members get vs non-members, side by side. Sephora's Beauty Insider emails always show the points.
- 5. Make the "Membership terms" link more prominent, and fix the clipped ticker.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off everything ends tonight (members only)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 25% off Balm Dotcom + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members save 25% sitewide. Free account, takes 30 seconds.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends at midnight ET. Here's what's in the sale.`
