# Wrap up the gift of great hair 🤩
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth Sephora email in about five days, and I'm in gift-guide fatigue. It's cleaner than the 11/13 "Meet the best of the best🏆" send, but it's the same holiday-set story. The "Get a Dior Mini" banner is a new hook, and it was the only thing that made me stop scrolling. After that it's a grid of hair sets.
- The hair angle is clear and shoppable. Each set shows a "$ value" next to the price, and I did that points math instantly (K18 at $39 for a $53 value, Fenty at $59 for an $85 value). But I don't see a single Beauty Insider tier callout in the body, and no points multiplier either. As a Rouge who stocks up at the Insider event, I want to know whether these sets are worth buying now or at the November event. Cyber Week is teased near the bottom with no dates, so I'd wait.
- Would I click? Maybe one tap to see the Dior mini mechanics, since a free mini is real value. I'd forward the Fenty or Dr. Groot set to a friend, but not the email. It's a skim, then archive.

## What's working

- The "Get a Dior Mini" banner at the top gives me a concrete freebie, and the Sauvage EDP is a recognizable name.
- The "$ value" pricing on each set (e.g. $58 vs $85 for the Fenty set) lets me do the math without clicking.
- "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags on the set cards create credible scarcity and exclusivity.
- The brand mix of K18, Living Proof, Dr. Groot, Fenty and Ouai is curated, and it's what I expect from Sephora over a mass retailer.
- The layout is clean. The hair hero with the "SHOP NOW" button is easy to find, and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- Two competing heroes, Dior and Hot Hair Gifts, split the top. I don't know which is the real offer.
- The Dior mini sits above the hair story with no connection to it, and it looks like a leftover from a fragrance email.
- No Beauty Insider tier info, points or Rouge perks are visible in the body. A Rouge gets treated like a stranger here.
- The Cyber Week teaser, "Score the best deals of the year," has no dates and no offer, which is just noise.
- The bottom is crowded: a gift guide, Jo Malone, Cyber Week, free sample bags and four category buttons. The hair story gets diluted.
- The fine print is a wall of tiny text. I can't tell what the Dior mini requires without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Dior mini and the hair sets one story, or send them separately. One hero per email would lift this the most.
- 2. Add a tier line, for example "Rouge: earn 2x points on hair sets."
- 3. Put real dates on the Cyber Week tease, or cut it. Ulta would date it and put a code on it. Sephora is vaguer than Ulta's "ends tonight" sends here.
- 4. Cut the bottom modules down to one gift-guide link, and put the free sample bags higher.
- 5. Surface the Dior mini's spend threshold in the banner itself, so I don't have to read the fine print.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

- QA probed 12 of 55 links and skipped 43. No probe failures were reported, but about 78% of links were unverified.
- There are two tracking pixels:

- The campaign code `20261117_Hot_Hair_Gifts` appears only in the pixel, not in any visible link.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 0 chars.** There is no text/plain alternative. This can hurt deliverability and leaves non-HTML clients with nothing. The relay may have dropped it, so check the raw MIME.
- **Missing alt text:** both tracking pixels above lack `alt`. They are hidden or 1×1, so impact is low, but `alt=""` is the correct attribute for decorative images.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem on mobile.
- **`<meta name="robots" content="no index">`** is invalid. The directive is `noindex`, and the meta tag has no effect in email anyway.
- **`<!--[if mso]> img.mso-size {…} <![endif]-->`** is placed inside the `@media` block in `<style>`. This is an invalid location, so the MSO rule probably never applies.
- **Fixed width:** the wrapper uses `min-width:700px; width:700px` on the table. Only the media query at ≤480px adjusts sizes, and the wrapper width itself is not overridden. This risks horizontal scroll on small viewports.
- **`<html>` has no `lang` attribute**, and `<title>` is the generic "Sephora".
- **JSON-LD:** `<script type='application/ld+json'>` contains `"discountCode": ""` and an empty `PromotionCard`. Most clients strip scripts, and the empty values add nothing.

No issues found in the visible HTML. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[First Name]`) appear in the excerpt. The only per-recipient data seen is the opaque `__dU__` and `mi_u` identifiers.
