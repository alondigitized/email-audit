# Ends Tonight! Don't miss your free iconic mug ☕
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is "okay, I get it, the mug." The 10/30 email was "Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug!", and this is the same mug with "Ends Tonight" added. Before that, Glossier ran the Milky Jelly gift on 10/22, 10/24 and 10/26. That's now a second straight gift-with-purchase pile-on in under two weeks, so "ends tonight" doesn't feel as urgent as it should.
- The email itself is clean. The mug hero is cute and the offer is plain: free mug at $75+. But the whole email is a sale with no points math for me. There's no Glossier tier or rewards angle. Compare Sephora's "Your personal holiday shopper" email, which at least tried to speak to me, and Ulta's Early Black Friday email, which led with a rewards pitch even though I'm Diamond. Glossier treated me like a stranger.
- Would I click? Probably not. I'd tap "Shop now" only if I was already near $75. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the next gift.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "Get a free Three Eyed Smiley Mug with your $75+ order*" puts the offer and threshold right up top.
- The red-handled mug with marshmallows is giftable and seasonal, and the image dominates the eye path.
- "Ends tonight" in the eyebrow plus "Hurry, this (super cute) offer ends tonight!" gives me a real deadline, in Glossier's voice.
- The "Start here" row is a useful bridge for a $75 build: You Doux, Boy Brow and Black Cherry Lip Line + Balm Dotcom.
- The layout is simple, and the product names are legible without zooming.

## What's weak

- It's a repeat. The mug was the whole point of 10/30, so this feels like the same email with a clock stuck on it.
- There's no member or loyalty angle at all. I do the math on every cart, and nothing tells me what I'd earn or save.
- The "Start here" row has no prices. For a $75 threshold I want to see which three items get me there, and I can't tell.
- The third product card is cut off ("Black Cherry Lip Line + Balm D…") and the carousel bleeds off the right edge. The "Shop now" buttons also feel generic, and the one under the mug is small and pale grey.
- "Terms apply" is a tiny grey asterisk at the very bottom. That's honest enough, but I have to scroll to the footer for exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices and a "build your $75" math line to the product row, something like "Boy Brow $20 + Balm Dotcom $16 + …". That's the click driver for me.
- 2. Make the deadline specific, with a time zone ("ends 11:59pm ET"), so it doesn't read as another soft nudge.
- 3. Send this to non-purchasers only, or to people who haven't bought since the 10/30 mug email. Re-sending the same gift to everyone is where the fatigue comes from.
- 4. Use a bolder, filled "Shop now" button on the hero and name the item, e.g. "Get my free mug".
- 5. Put the key terms in the body, such as "free mug, no code, while supplies last", instead of hiding them behind the asterisk.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free mug + $75 = done by midnight ☕`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Boy Brow + free mug, tonight only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $75, the Three Eyed Smiley Mug is on us. Ends 11:59pm ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You Doux, Boy Brow and Balm Dotcom get you there.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built with Stripo (images on `qlqxnf.stripocdn.email`) and sent through Klaviyo (`ctrk.klclick.com` click and open tracking). The visible markup has no confirmed defects. The only confirmed issue is missing alt text on 6 images. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are inconclusive.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links confirmed.**
  - 12 material links were probed and 8 were skipped.
  - Four "Shop now" links returned HTTP 429: `…SWV_3`, `_6`, `_8` and `_10`. A 429 is rate limiting by the Klaviyo tracker, not a dead link.
  - Eight links were unprobed when the 70s budget ran out: `_1`, `_2`, `_11`, `_13`, `_14` and others. Their status is unknown and should not be counted as pass or fail.
- **Invalid markup:** `<span rel="stylesheet" href="https://ctrk.klclick.com/l/01M6BP725HAWSYDX4ESBV38SWV_0"></span>` appears at the top of `<body>`. `href` and `rel` are not valid attributes on `<span>`. This looks like a Klaviyo/ESP template artifact and is probably harmless, but it is non-conformant HTML.
- **Redirect-only links:** Every link is wrapped in a `ctrk.klclick.com` redirect, so the final destinations can't be read from the HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 6 images** (QA-confirmed):
  - `heroimage_1_6_IOf.jpg`
  - `group_3986_7.jpg`
  - `glossierbdctriocarousel01_1_qBK.jpg`
  - `group_3986_8.jpg` (flagged twice)
  - the open-tracking pixel `https://ctrk.klclick.com/o/01M6BP725HAWSYDX4ESBV38SWV`
- **Alt text severity:** With images blocked or a screen reader, the hero and carousel images carry no text. The tracking pixel should have `alt=""` and is low severity.
- **Conditional comments:** The `<!--[if !mso]><!-- -->` opened in `<head>` is closed by `<!--<![endif]-->` inside `<body>`. This is a common ESP pattern and no rendering problem is evident.
- **Preheader:** It is hidden correctly (`display:none; mso-hide:all`) and padded with `&zwnj;`/soft-hyphen filler.
- **Other:** The source has an XHTML doctype, `lang="en"`, `dir="ltr"`, a viewport meta tag, and MSO/Outlook fallbacks. The source is truncated, so I can't check the rest of the body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `*|…|*`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source or the QA findings.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a violation. Klaviyo normally sends both. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the expected cause. The sender domain is `eml.glossier.com`.
- **Footer:** The footer, with its physical address and unsubscribe link, falls outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm it. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing but gives no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destination URLs sit behind the Klaviyo redirects, and none of the probes resolved a final landing URL. The UTM parameters, and whether the landing page matches the $75+ free-mug offer in the preheader, are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the hero, carousel and `group_3986_*` images, and `alt=""` to the open-tracking pixel.
2. Check the raw message headers outside the relay for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. This email can't confirm or rule them out.
3. Re-run the link probe with a longer time budget and backoff on 429s. Follow the redirects to log the final URLs, the UTM parameters and the landing page for the mug offer.
4. Remove the `<span rel="stylesheet" href=…>` element if the template allows. Otherwise ignore it as an ESP artifact.
5. Confirm the footer for the unsubscribe link and the physical address in the full, untruncated HTML.
