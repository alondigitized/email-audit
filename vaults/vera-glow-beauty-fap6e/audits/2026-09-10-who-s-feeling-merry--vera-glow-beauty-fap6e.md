# Who’s feeling merry?!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is a genuinely different Drunk Elephant email than what I've been logging — no DRUNKVIP20, no "secret sale" wink, just a straight holiday gift-set launch called "The Drunk Elephant Atelier." Honestly, it's a relief after the FIVE straight days of the same 20% code (9/4 through 9/8) — the fashion-week "Couture," "Gala Glow," "Maison Moisture" naming is a little much, but at least it's a new bit.
- The problem: there's no actual offer anywhere. No percentage off, no price, no points multiplier, nothing — just "SHOP HOLIDAY" buttons stacked four times down a very long scroll. As a Rouge member who's mid-holiday-event-calendar-math right now, I need to know if this is going to show up in Sephora's Holiday Savings Event or if I should wait for a VIB sale, and this email tells me nothing.
- I wouldn't click — pretty as the packaging shot is, there's zero urgency or price anchor to act on today. I'd screenshot the hero and text it to my skincare group chat asking "does anyone know if this drops at Sephora or is DE-exclusive right now," which is basically a backhanded forward, not a real one.

## What's working

- The hero shot is genuinely gorgeous — the pastel houndstooth boxes and teal ribbon read premium and holiday without being tacky.
- Clean visual hierarchy: yellow banner → hero → CTA, no clutter, easy to scan on my phone without zooming.
- Distinct product tiles for each set (Gala Glow, Couture Shows, Maison Moisture, Finishing Touches) at least tell me what's actually in the collection instead of one vague banner.
- No render bugs — everything's crisp, no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Zero price, zero % off, zero mention of points or Rouge/Beauty Insider tie-in — as someone constantly doing points math, this email gave me nothing to calculate.
- No urgency at all — "limited-edition" is doing a lot of unearned work with no date or "while supplies last" countdown.
- Four repeated "SHOP NOW"/"SHOP HOLIDAY" CTAs but every one of them is generic — none says what I'm actually buying or what it costs.
- The fashion-collection concept (Atelier, Couture, Gala) feels like it's trying too hard to be luxury-adjacent for a brand people buy through Sephora's own points system — Sephora's own Daily Deals email I logged yesterday at least led with "50% off select brands," even if it was a repeat.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or bundle savings number in the hero — "Save $45 when bundled" beats "spectacular" every time for someone tracking value across two loyalty programs.
- 2. Anchor this to the beauty-event calendar explicitly — tell me if this collection counts toward Sephora's Holiday Savings Event or Ulta's 21 Days, since that's literally how I decide when to buy.
- 3. Cut the CTA repetition from four identical buttons to one strong hero CTA plus a single "Shop the full collection" at the bottom — mid-scroll fatigue sets in fast.
- 4. Add a stock/urgency cue for gift sets specifically — "selling out fast" or a ship-by-Christmas date would push me to act now instead of "I'll get to it."
