# Black Friday means 30% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- Seventh Drunk Elephant email in about a week, and this is the same "It's on! BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" hero I got in "Well, hello, Black Friday!" earlier today. My gut reaction was "didn't I just see this?" The offer is easy to read, but nothing here is new. It's the 11/27 creative with a new subject line and a couple of extra blocks.
- The email stacks four different offers: 30% sitewide, a free full-size B-Goldi at $110+, "Last chance! Up to 40% off," and Trunk 7.1 at 20% off with code DRUNK20. The code is the part that annoys me. I'd be doing points math in my head on which discount I can actually combine, and the fine print says some of them can't be. Sephora's 50% hero tells me one thing and moves on. This one makes me work.
- I'd click once, probably the B-Goldi block, since a free $39 full-size is the only thing that moves my math. I wouldn't forward it. I'd mark it read and wait for the Sephora and Ulta sends.

## What's working

- "BLACK FRIDAY 30% OFF SITEWIDE" is huge and sits at the top, so I get the offer in half a second.
- The free full-size B-Goldi with "That's a value of $39!" is a real math hook, and the "YES, FULL SIZE" badge makes it feel concrete.
- The pink ornament and ribbon creative looks festive. It's the best-looking Black Friday visual in my inbox today, nicer than the Sally Beauty black-and-gold sequins.
- The "$340 / $733 VALUE" bubble on Trunk 7.1 shows the savings math for me.
- The fine print is complete, with dates, times and exclusions all stated.

## What's weak

- It's the same hero as the earlier "Well, hello, Black Friday!" send, so the 7th email in a week feels like a repeat.
- The top lime banner promotes a subscription offer (30% off the first order plus free shipping) that has nothing to do with the hero. It competes with the main message.
- Four offers collide: 30% sitewide, up to 40%, 20% with a code, and the free gift. The footnotes say some can't be combined, which is exactly the clarity I want and don't get.
- The "LAST CHANCE! Up to 40% off" block contradicts the 30% hero. Which number is real? Ulta's "up to 50%" at least leads with a single headline.
- Nothing rewards me as a loyalty member, and there's no skin-type or shade personalization. The fine print is tiny and hard to read on my phone.
- Only the "SHOP NOW" buttons in the cyan block and the "UP TO 40%" block are real buttons. The B-Goldi "SHOP NOW" is a small text link, which is a tiny tap target.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero and one stack rule. Something like "30% off everything + free B-Goldi at $110" with a one-line "what stacks" explainer.
- 2. Cut the lime subscription banner, or move it below the fold. It splits focus at the top.
- 3. Merge "Up to 40%" and "30% sitewide" into one clear tier, or label which items get the extra 10%.
- 4. Make the B-Goldi CTA a full button and put a threshold progress line next to it ("Add $X to unlock your free full-size").
- 5. Add a skin-type or best-seller row for personalization, and a countdown for the 11/29 11:59 p.m. deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free full-size B-Goldi + 30% off, ends 11/29`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $39 serum is free at $110 🎁`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `30% sitewide, plus a free full-size B-Goldi when you spend $110+. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Here's what stacks: sitewide 30%, the gift, and Trunk 7.1 at 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 email with CSS and font-loading problems, three non-HTTPS images and two tracking pixels without alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is truncated and all 14 tracking links were skipped, so link and UTM findings are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 14 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Recipient email in a tracking URL:** the pixel at `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=us_de_broad…` carries the recipient's address in plaintext in the query string. This is PII in a URL that can end up in proxy and server logs.
- **Second tracker:** `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfpmreminder1_p` is a 1x1 open pixel with a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). That makes two separate tracking systems in one send (`mi.` and `everestengagement.com`).
- **Third-party and versioned asset dependencies:** fonts come from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, and `BrownRegular` comes from `www.drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/.../v1719565389232/fonts/`. That versioned path breaks if the site is redeployed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** both tracking pixels above (`o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` image) lack `alt`. Pixels should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Insecure images:** three social icons load over `http://`: `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/{cc02d07d-…, 9b452915-…, 29f975e1-…}.png` (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok). They may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients.
- **Invalid `@import`:** in the `<style>` block, `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/...)` appears after `body {…}`. `@import` must come before all other rules, so these three imports are ignored. The `<link rel="stylesheet">` tags in `<head>` are stripped by Gmail and several other clients, so web fonts mostly won't load there. The `@font-face` blocks can work in Apple Mail and iOS Mail, which is why the CSS is mixed.
- **HTML comments inside CSS:** `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->` sit inside the `@media (max-width:600px)` block. The comment markers can cause parsers to drop or mis-parse adjacent rules, though the browser behavior wasn't tested here.
- **Zoom disabled:** the viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fragile selectors:** `*[class=hide]` exact-match attribute selectors only match elements whose class attribute is exactly that value. They break if a second class is added.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated portion. The recipient email in the pixel URL did resolve correctly, but see the PII note in section 2.

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** not found. The QA tool notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. `From: info@cheers.drunkelephant.com` uses a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper alignment but can't be verified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM verification blocked:** the 14 skipped tracking links wrap the destinations, so UTM parameters and landing-page URLs can't be inspected.
- **Campaign naming is consistent across trackers:** `us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfpmreminder1_p` appears in `mi_ecmp` and `c=us_de_broad…`. This suggests a Black Friday reminder 1 send, but landing-page alignment is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/...` icon URLs to `https://`.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels. Give the social icons descriptive alt text, which they already have per QA (Facebook, Instagram and TikTok).
3. Remove the recipient email from the `e=` query parameter and use an opaque token instead.
4. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them in favor of `@font-face`. Ensure web-safe font fallbacks, since `<link>` fonts are stripped in Gmail.
5. Delete the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside the `@media` block.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Self-host fonts or confirm the versioned `demandware.static` path is stable.
8. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` at the origin, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders. Re-run QA with full headers and the untruncated HTML, and resolve the tracking redirects to check UTMs and landing pages.
