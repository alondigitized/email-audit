# e.l.f. x Bubble 👯‍♀️ Get it NOW! 
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually the most interesting thing e.l.f. has sent in this endless streak — an e.l.f. x Bubble collab, "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER," three actual co-branded hybrid products (Drop N' Dunk, Glow Talk, Camo Slide) instead of another single-SKU teaser. I'll give them credit for genuine novelty after seventeen-plus days of the same well.
- But here's the thing: none of this earns me anything. There's no code, no % off, no BOGO — just base pricing ($10-18) dressed up as a "super-limited drop." I'm the person doing points math between Sephora Rouge and Ulta Diamond, and e.l.f. isn't in either program, so a collab with zero loyalty hook or discount is just... browsing. Compare that to ILIA's "Art of the Eye Trio" send I flagged at 7/10 — same three-product-launch structure, but ILIA gave me an actual "Eye Color Theory" matching matrix to make the purchase decision easy. This is prettier but thinner.
- Would I click? Maybe, out of curiosity for the shade range on Camo Slide. Forward to a friend? No — nothing here is offer-worthy enough to share, it's just a nice-looking product drop.

## What's working

- The "e.l.f. x Bubble" collab hook is a real departure from the single-item promos I've been getting daily — genuinely felt like news, not filler.
- "3 SHADES" badges on each product are a small but real personalization signal — I care about shade range before I click anything.
- Clean three-act structure (Drop N' Dunk → Glow Talk → Camo Slide), each with its own hero shot, name, subtitle, and price — easy to scan on mobile without hunting.
- "Top Picks for You" carousel at the bottom is a decent personalization gesture even if the products feel generic.

## What's weak

- Zero discount mechanic — no %, no code, no BOGO. Sephora's "Discover the art of body care" send I scored 8/10 didn't need a discount either, but it had a much richer sensorial narrative to justify that; this is just three product cards and a "SHOP NOW."
- "Super-Limited Drop" urgency language with no countdown, no end date — feels like marketing filler, not a real deadline.
- No models, no skin tone/type range shown anywhere — for a "for every eye, lip, and face" tagline at the bottom, there's not a single face in this email.
- This is at minimum the fifth e.l.f. send I've gotten in this stretch (BOGO skincare 8/6, shade quad 8/5, hair frizz 8/4, watermelon lip balm 8/3) — cadence fatigue is real and this doesn't reset the clock.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Give me a reason to act today** — even a modest "limited quantities, while they last" with an actual restock/sellout indicator would beat vague "super-limited" language. This is the single biggest lever here.
- 2. **Tie it to Beauty Squad rewards** — "earn 2x points on this drop" would at least make it feel like it's talking to a loyalty member, since the footer already teases the rewards app.
- 3. **Show a face or two** — even one model wearing Camo Slide blush would make the shade range tangible instead of abstract.
- 4. **Cut one product from the fold** — three near-identical "SHOP NOW" blocks stacked vertically is a lot of scrolling before "Top Picks"; lead with the strongest hero (Drop N' Dunk) and let the other two live below a "see the full collab" link.
- 5. **Add real preview text** — the inbox is currently getting nothing useful next to the subject; a line like "3 hybrid drops, starting at $10 — shades for every skin tone" would do double duty.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `3 hybrid drops from $10 — every shade, every face`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bubble's cult skincare, now in e.l.f. shades`

## Technical Audit: e.l.f. Cosmetics x Bubble Email

**1. Technical Summary**
The email uses a Cheetah/Cordial-style click-tracking redirect layer (`links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...`) wrapping all CTAs; automated QA returned a 27% pass rate, driven by 4 broken link destinations, a missing plain-text MIME part, and missing unsubscribe/authentication headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 4 of 12 probed links resolve to relative paths instead of absolute URLs, causing "unknown url type" errors:
  - `/products/power-grip-primer?Size=Regular+0.8+oz+%2F+24+ml` ("Shop Now")
  - `/products/cream-glide-lip-liner?Color=Mauve+Aside+-+Warm+Rose+Mauve` ("Shop Now")
  - `/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount` ("Receipt scanning...")
  - `/pages/mobile-app?c=email&utm_source=broadcast&is_retargeting=true&deep_link_value=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&lid=c50ux6y75v` ("beauty squad - Download the e.l.f. app")
  - All four resolve behind valid-looking `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` redirect wrappers, but the QA resolver could not derive a scheme+host from the redirect target — the destination is being served as a bare relative path rather than a fully-qualified URL.
- Remaining 8 probed links returned no errors; 29 lower-priority (footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed per QA methodology.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Outlook/MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and `<o:OfficeDocumentSettings>` are present and correctly scoped for Word-engine rendering.
- Preheader text ("We made it e.l.f.ficial with Bubble.") is correctly hidden via `display:none!important;visibility:hidden;mso-hide:all;font-size:1px` pattern.
- Responsive breakpoint at `max-width:660px` with `.stack`/`.mobile_hide`/`.desktop_hide` classes is present and structured correctly.
- Hero image (`image_assets/.../original.png`) includes `alt="e.l.f."` and `title="e.l.f."`.
- QA flags one image missing alt text: element id `cFosaEUZGn92Ie3TwuIiIK0cowapnwdccVZ7tlsubLHOBRaMRG`, `src="https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/17kYQoxHuwuKKYCjdH52Mw~~/AAQRxRA~/cFosaEUZGn92Ie3TwuIiIK0cowapnwdccVZ7tlsubLHOBRaMRG65V"` — the `/q/` path suggests this is an open-tracking beacon rather than content, but it should still carry `alt=""` to avoid screen readers announcing the raw filename.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%field%%`) appear in the provided HTML. No issues found — note the source is truncated, so this cannot be fully confirmed for the untruncated remainder.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected (WARN) — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than a confirmed absence at origin.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe under 2024 bulk-sender rules) not detected (WARN).
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM) not detected — status unknown via this relay.
- A body-level unsubscribe link is confirmed present per QA probing methodology, but this doesn't substitute for the header-level mechanism required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Only 1 of the 4 flagged CTA destinations carries explicit tracking params (`c=email&utm_source=broadcast&is_retargeting=true`, mobile app link); the other 3 destination paths (product pages, account login) show no UTM parameters in the resolved path.
- The relative-path resolution issue in Section 2 directly breaks email→site continuity: without a resolvable host, these 3 product/account links cannot be confirmed to land on a valid page.

**7. Recommendations**
- Fix redirect-target URL generation so all 4 flagged links resolve to fully-qualified absolute URLs (missing scheme/host).
- Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers to meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- Generate a non-empty plain-text MIME alternative (currently 0 chars).
- Add `alt=""` (or descriptive alt) to the flagged tracking-beacon image.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM pass status at the sending ESP, independent of relay header visibility.
- Standardize UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) across all CTAs, not just the mobile app link.
