# Up to 40% off, you say?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Drunk Elephant email in six days, and I've seen this creative before. The hero is the same "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE" I got on 11/25 with "Biggest sale of the year: 30% off." The subject says "Up to 40% off, you say?" and then the 40% shows up only in a small "Last Chance!" strip in the middle. The 11/25 "Up to 40% off." send had the same problem, and so did this morning's "Up to 40% off. Pass the savings." I keep getting the 40% headline and the 30% hero.
- The offer math is fine. 30% off is solid, and the free Hydra and the Bright kit with $100+ is a real reason to hit the threshold. The Protini at $69.30 and the Kamo at $29.40 are easy to price-check against Sephora. The problem is that I'm at fatigue. Nothing here is new since yesterday, and a "you say?" subject doesn't earn another open when I already know the sale ends tonight.
- Would I click? Only the free-gift tier, and only because I'm already at $100. Would I forward it? No. This is a mark-as-read unless I need a restock.

## What's working

- The hero reads in half a second: "CYBER WEEK 30% OFF SITEWIDE" in big type over the product lineup, with a clear SHOP NOW button below.
- The free Hydra and the Bright kit ($32 value) with $100+ gives me a tier to do points math on. The "FREE GIFT" badge is easy to see.
- The "THE COOL KIDS" grid shows struck-through prices next to sale prices, with star ratings and "1,512 people are checking this out" as social proof.
- The Kamo block says what it does (foundation, concealer, or tinted moisturizer) and links to "Find your shade," which is the closest this email gets to personalization.
- The fine print states the offer window: 11/25 12:00 a.m. ET to 11/26 11:59 p.m. ET. At least it's disclosed.

## What's weak

- The subject promises 40%, but the hero says 30%. The 40% is a thin "Up to 40% off, Last Chance!" strip with no products and no detail. That reads as a bait-and-switch.
- It's the fifth send in six days. Compare ILIA, which at least gave me something new to calculate with its gift tier. This is a repeat of 11/25, and Sephora's Cyber Week sends at least rotate the daily deal.
- The cadence kills urgency. The 11/24 "VIP time's almost up!" had a real countdown. This one has none, even though the free-gift deal ends tonight.
- The "Last Chance" strip is the thinnest block and the first one I'd skip. The exclusions are in a long fine-print block (gift cards, Trunk 7.1, Dopp Deluxe Bundles, Last Chance kits can't stack).
- There's nothing for my loyalty tiers. No Rouge or Diamond equivalent and no member perk, so Sephora and Ulta Diamond still win my cart on points.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the hero, or make the 40% real. If the lead offer is 30% sitewide, say that. If 40% is the hook, put it in the hero with products behind it.
- 2. Add a real deadline. "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET" in the subject and a countdown at the top, like the 11/24 send had.
- 3. Move the free-gift threshold higher. Lead with "$100+ = free Hydra and the Bright kit" so I can see the math in one glance.
- 4. Cut the "Last Chance" strip or give it three products. Right now it's a promise with nothing behind it.
- 5. Cap the cadence. Fifth send in six days is why the open rate will fall.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 30% off + free $32 kit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 30% off sitewide, gift at $100`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $100, get the Hydra and the Bright kit free. Ends 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Protini $69.30, Kamo $29.40. Last chance before Cyber Week closes.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email sent through Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style infrastructure. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main code-level issues are three `http://` image sources, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, `@import` rules placed where browsers ignore them, and a missing alt attribute on two tracking pixels. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be checked because the relay didn't capture them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed.** 18 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Insecure image hosts.** Three social icons use `http://`: `http://image.cheers.drunkelephant.com/lib/fe3211717064057c751c78/m/1/cc02d07d-…png` (Facebook), `…/9b452915-…png` (Instagram) and `…/29f975e1-…png` (TikTok). Clients that block mixed content may show them as broken images.
- **Tracking pixels.** There are two. `https://mi.drunkelephant.com/p/up/80acaf1c74f9e002/o.gif?mi_u=98161986&mi_ecmp=us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmreminderpm_…` looks like an open pixel. `https://drunkelephantus.everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/?e=…&c=us_de_broad…` is a second one from a different vendor. Running two vendors adds third-party requests but is not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** `o.gif` and the `everestengagement.com/ea/dKhpn7WOgU/` pixel have no alt attribute. Both are tracking pixels, so the impact is low. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **`@import` after other rules.** The `<style>` block has `body {…}` before the three `@import url(https://db.onlinewebfonts.com/…)` lines. `@import` is only valid before other rules, so those imports will be ignored. The `<link>` tags in `<head>` do the same job, so the imports are redundant. Gmail and Outlook strip both `<link>` and `@import`.
- **Font dependencies.** The `BrownBold`, `BrownLight` and `BrownThin` fonts load from `db.onlinewebfonts.com`, a third-party host. `BrownRegular` loads from `drunkelephant.com/on/demandware.static/…`, which is a different origin from the sending domain. If any of these fail, the stack has to fall back, and I couldn't see the fallback `font-family` in the truncated source.
- **Comments inside `<style>`.** The source contains `<!-- *[class=contenttable] {…} -->` and `<!-- *[class=mobile] {…} -->`. HTML comment markers are not valid CSS comments, and some parsers may drop the neighboring rule.
- **Mobile sizing.** The 418px media query sets `max-width:15px; max-height:15px; width:17px` on `.vouchertext4` and `max-width:12px` on `.fsname`. Those are unusually small caps, so check that text isn't clipped on mobile.
- **Brittle attribute selectors.** Selectors like `*[class=vouchertext4]` match only an exact class attribute value. They fail if a second class is ever added.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved merge tokens** (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible portion of the source.
- **PII in a pixel URL.** The `everestengagement.com` pixel puts the recipient's email in the query string (`?e=darcy-ferment-drunkelephant-fap6e@etell.app&c=…`). The `o.gif` URL carries a subscriber ID (`mi_u=98161986`). Plain-text identifiers in image URLs are exposed to proxies and logs. This is a design choice by the sender, not a bug.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The check says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed and shouldn't be treated as a sender failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders. This has the same relay caveat.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `cheers.drunkelephant.com` is a subdomain, so alignment can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer not visible.** The source is truncated, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link couldn't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
**Not assessable.** The 18 tracking links were not resolved, so landing pages and UTM parameters are unverified. The pixel campaign tag `us_de_broadcast_promotion_bfcmreminderpm_…` shows the sender does use campaign naming. The subject says "Up to 40% off" and the sender name says "Cyber Week", while the campaign tag says `bfcmreminder`. I couldn't confirm whether the landing offer matches.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the three social icons over HTTPS, and add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Move the `@import` rules to the top of the `<style>` block, or drop them and keep only the `<link>` tags. Add web-safe fallback stacks for `Brown*`.
4. Replace the `<!-- -->` wrappers inside `<style>` with `/* */`.
5. Re-run the check against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, treat those three warnings as unverified.
6. Resolve the 18 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe, then check UTM parameters and landing-page offer alignment.
7. Consider whether the email address in the `everestengagement.com` pixel URL should be replaced with a hashed ID.
