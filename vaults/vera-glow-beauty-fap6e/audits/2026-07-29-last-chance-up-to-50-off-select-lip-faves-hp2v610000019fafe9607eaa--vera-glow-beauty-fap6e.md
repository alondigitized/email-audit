# LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves ✨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is the third time this exact subject line — "LAST CHANCE ‼️ Up to 50% off select lip faves" — has landed in my inbox, and it's the same grid I already scored on 7/29. At some point "last chance" stops meaning anything and just becomes Sephora's way of saying "we're still running the same sale." That said, the grid itself is still solid: eight real products, real struck-through pricing, brands I actually shop (Charlotte Tilbury, Laneige, MAKE UP FOR EVER, Gisou) sitting next to each other with no filler.
- What pulls this down isn't the merchandising, it's the redundancy and the "0 points" banner sitting right up top like it's taunting me — I'm Rouge tier, I know my balance is thin right now, I don't need it broadcast before I've even scrolled past the header. Compare this to Ulta's "Lucky you! ✨ This 20% off just landed in your lap" send I scored a 9 on — that one felt like it knew who I was and met me with something new. This is Sephora recycling the same asset for the third send in a row.
- Would I click? Probably, because Charlotte Tilbury lip liner at $19.60 and the Laneige serum at $17.60 are genuinely good prices and I'd bite eventually. Would I forward it to a friend? No — I'd screenshot the CT liner price into our group chat instead of forwarding the whole email, because the email itself feels stale at this point.

## What's working

- The 8-product grid is clean, evenly spaced, and every single item shows real strikethrough pricing — no vague "up to 50% off" with nothing to back it up.
- Brand mix is genuinely curated for someone like me — CT, Laneige, Summer Fridays, Gisou are all brands I already buy, not filler SKUs.
- "Clean" badges on Gisou and Summer Fridays are a nice touch since ingredient callouts matter to me.
- The Buy More Save More banner at the bottom (up to 20% off Sephora Collection, starts 7/31) is a legit second offer, not just noise.

## What's weak

- This is the third identical "LAST CHANCE" send with this exact subject and grid — I already reviewed this creative on 7/29, so the urgency is fake at this point.
- The "0 points" banner up top feels like a jab, not personalization — if you're going to show me my balance, show me how close I am to Rouge rewards, not just zero.
- "Spot it. Shop it." section (DAE mousse, $35 and under grid) has nothing to do with lips and dilutes the National Lip Day focus.
- No indication of which items are already low in stock or selling fast — for a "last chance" email, there's zero real urgency signal beyond the words themselves.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop resending the identical creative under the identical subject line — if inventory changed, show me what's actually almost gone; if it didn't, don't cry "last chance" a third time.
- 2. Replace the "0 points" callout with something Rouge-specific — points-to-next-reward progress, or a Rouge-exclusive early window on the Buy More Save More event.
- 3. Cut the "Spot it. Shop it." mousse/skincare block from a lip-focused email — it breaks the theme and pushes the actual lip deals further down.
- 4. Add a real countdown or "X left in stock" tag on at least the hero products to back up "last chance" with something concrete.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Rouge pick: CT liner now $19.60`
