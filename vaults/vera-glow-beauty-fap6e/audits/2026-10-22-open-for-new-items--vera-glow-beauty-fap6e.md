## What's working

- The "24 hours only" holiday drops block is the only real urgency in the email, and holiday sets are what I'm hunting this month.
- The product grid is clean. Each item has a name, size and price, and the Saie vault says "$155 ($225 value)", so I can do the value math in my head.
- The "ONLY AT SEPHORA" and "LIMITED EDITION" tags tell me which items I can't get at Ulta.
- The Beauty Insider header strip shows my status ("You have 5 points"), so it knows who I am.
- The Paula's Choice, Philus and Living Proof picks are a fair skincare and haircare mix.

## What's weak

- There is no points hook, no multiplier and no Rouge perk. Ulta runs "2X points on new arrivals" this way all the time, and Sephora usually does too.
- The hero doesn't show what's new. A cake with a perfume bottle doesn't say "Wild Cousin" until I squint at the label.
- Every tile links to a product, but nothing says why now. I get no deals and no "selling out" signal.
- The rewards bar at the bottom looks broken. The INSIDER/VIB/ROUGE labels sit on mismatched blocks, with a red marker and a clipped "INSIDER" box over a white tile. It reads like a render bug.
- I count about three "Shop Now" buttons plus "Shop New Brands," so the email has no single focal CTA.

- 1. Lead with a points or Rouge hook such as "2X points on new arrivals." That one change would lift the email the most.
- 2. Make the hero show the actual new product with its name, not a mood shot.
- 3. Move the 24-hour holiday drops block up. It's the only urgent thing in the email.
- 4. Fix the tier bar so Insider, VIB and Rouge render as one clean progress strip.
- 5. Space out new-arrival sends. I've had four in four days.
