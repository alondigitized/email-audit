# All set for Black Friday?
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the eighth ILIA email in about four days, and I'm doing the points math again. The hero is pretty: two models with different skin tones, a pink "20% OFF" circle, and a banner above it that says "25% off sitewide, join VIP for free." So the real offer is 20% or 25% depending on whether I join, and I have to work that out myself. After yesterday's "New. Gifts. Added. (Up to $120 value)" and this morning's "Your free Complexion Stick is waiting," the gift edit at the bottom is the same news again.
- I'd open it, because ILIA is a brand I follow and the subject is a soft "are you ready" nudge. I'd skim it for the gift tiers and probably click "Shop to claim," since the complexion stick is the only thing I haven't already seen. I wouldn't forward it. It's a reshuffle of the last three sends.

## What's working

- The hero models show a real range of skin tones, which matters to me for shade-matching. It's the best-looking part of the email.
- "Skin tint + blur complexion duo" and the other bundle tiles are what I'd buy as sets, so the "sets that make sense together" idea fits how I shop.
- The gift edit shows tiers: free complexion stick with any order, free mini of choice at $100+, free color block lipstick and free mini tote at $175+. I can do thresholds math with that.
- The "very important perks" box lists free shipping, redeemable points and early access, which is the loyalty detail I look for.
- The layout is clean and easy to scan, with a clear dark maroon button right under the hero copy.

## What's weak

- The top banner says 25% and the hero circle says 20%, with an asterisk. I shouldn't have to decode which one is mine.
- Every product tile has just a name and a "shop now" button. There are no prices, no sale prices, and no savings per set, so I can't tell what 20% off the "eye stylus trio" actually saves me.
- The "shop now" buttons are thin outlined boxes that are hard to see, and the tap targets are small on a phone.
- "*See site for details" is tiny and sits right under the gift CTA. I'd want the tier fine print where I can see it.
- The cadence is a lot. Ulta and Sephora both pace their Black Friday sends better than eight in four days, and this one adds nothing new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one number for the hero. Show "20% off, 25% with VIP" in the circle itself and remove the banner and circle mismatch.
- 2. Add set prices with the struck-through original, like "$68 → $54," on each tile. Sephora's 50% OFF hero showed the savings without making me calculate.
- 3. Make the gift tiers a clear ladder: any order, $100+, $175+. Put the thresholds in the headline, not the fine print.
- 4. Make the "shop now" buttons solid and bigger. The current outlined ones are hard to tap.
- 5. Show VIP perks with an actual points value, like "earn 2x points today." Right now it says "redeemable points" with no number.
- 6. Space out the sends. Drunk Elephant and e.l.f. were also sending many emails, and none of us should be sending the same hero over and over.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Skin tint + blur duo, 20% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free complexion stick with any order, mini at $100, lipstick and tote at $175.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `VIP members get 25% off plus free shipping. Join free.`
