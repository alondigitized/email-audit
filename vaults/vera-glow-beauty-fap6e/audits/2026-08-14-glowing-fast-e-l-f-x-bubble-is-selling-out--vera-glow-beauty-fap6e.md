# GLOWING FAST! e.l.f. x Bubble is selling out! 🥲 
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "GLOWING FAST!" in the subject and now "WE JUST GLOW TOGETHER" as the hero — e.l.f. is really milking the same pun, and I opened this thinking I'd get the actual Bubble launch, but I'm seeing the exact Camo Slide product beat I already got yesterday in "HURRY! e.l.f. x Bubble is selling fast" (8/13, 6/10). Same $10 price, same "3 Shades" pink swirl image, same "Shop Now" button placement. This is a straight-up urgency reskin of yesterday's send, not new creative.
- The product itself — a $10 hydrating blush serum with hyaluronic acid — is genuinely up my alley, and I like that it's dead simple: one price, one clear CTA, no code to remember. But this is the third or fourth Bubble-collab email this week (8/7 launch, 8/10 dupe comparison, 8/11 "Bestie-coded" grid, 8/13 GWP, now this), and the fatigue is real. I'm not clicking again on a product I already considered yesterday — I'd mark this as read and move on.
- Compare this to ILIA's "This is your shade" (7/10) which actually personalized to my eye color, or Sephora's Rouge points callout — this e.l.f. send has zero loyalty math, zero personalization, just a recycled hero shot with scarier copy slapped on top.

## What's working

- The $10 price point for Camo Slide is crystal clear and impulse-buy friendly.
- "NEW! LIMITED TIME DROP" plus the pink diagonal swirl background creates decent visual urgency even if I've seen the product before.
- "Top Picks for You" with three complementary products (lip liner, primer, setting spray) is a smart cross-sell that at least gestures at personalization.
- Cruelty-free/vegan badges and "free shipping limited time" at the footer are trust cues that register fast.

## What's weak

- This is the same Camo Slide/Bubble hero I got in yesterday's "HURRY! selling fast" email — just re-titled with "GLOWING FAST" and a crying-laughing emoji instead of a running-woman emoji. No new product, no new offer.
- "Top Picks for You" isn't actually personalized to anything I've bought or browsed — it reads like a generic grid, unlike ILIA's shade-matching this week.
- No loyalty/rewards messaging at all — no points, no tier perk, nothing for a Rouge/Diamond member like me to weigh against Sephora or Ulta.
- The category nav (NEW/EYES/LIPS/FACE/SKINCARE/HAIR) as gray bars is pure filler that pads the email length without adding value.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reissuing the same Camo Slide hero image two days running — if it's still "selling fast," show me the actual stock/scarcity number, not the identical swirl graphic.
- 2. Add a real loyalty hook — even a basic "e.l.f. Beauty Squad members get X" would differentiate this from the generic mass-market blast and give Rouge/Diamond types like me a reason to open every send.
- 3. Make "Top Picks for You" actually personalized (browsing/purchase-based) instead of a static three-pack — Sephora and ILIA are already doing this better.
- 4. Cut the empty category nav bars — replace with a second product or a countdown timer that actually supports the "selling out" urgency claim.
