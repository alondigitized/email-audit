# $5 off Bundles & Sets 🛍️ THIS WEEKEND ONLY!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction is that the offer is fine but the math is a trap. The subject says "$5 off Bundles & Sets," and the body turns that into a "Surprise Reward Drop" that needs a sign-in. The reward only works with a $10+ cart, and you have to add an eligible bundle first. This is the sixth e.l.f. email I've gotten this week. Last week's "$10 reward on us" send (11/14) already used this Beauty Squad reward-drop template, so the "surprise" isn't one.
- I do like the purple block. "SURPRISE REWARD DROP!" in pink on violet reads in half a second, and the pink gloss-kit hero is pretty. Then I hit the "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" button and a $5 reward on a $10 minimum, which is basically 50% off a cheap bundle. A second offer, the free full-size gift at $35+, sits right underneath it and competes with the first. I'd click, because the points math is easy and I'm already a member. I wouldn't forward it, since it's a mid-tier reminder and not an event.

## What's working

- The purple "SURPRISE REWARD DROP! $5 OFF BUNDLES AND SETS" block is the clearest thing in the email. The offer, the "no points needed" line and the "Expires 11/22" deadline all sit together.
- The "No points needed" line is good for me. I'm always doing points math, and it tells me this stacks without touching my balance.
- The pink gloss-kit hero is an eye-catcher, and the Beauty Squad Exclusive badge makes it feel like a member perk.
- The "Top Picks for You" row shows real prices ($10, $18, $12) with a Shop Now button on each. That means I can judge the deal without leaving the email.
- The free full-size gift at $35+ with "no promo code needed!" is a clean mechanic.

## What's weak

- Two offers stack in the top half of the email: the $5 reward and the $35+ free gift. Neither gets the full spotlight, and the gift's "$9 value" is easy to skim past.
- "SIGN IN TO REDEEM" is friction. Sephora and Ulta both apply member rewards in the cart without making me log in from the email first.
- The fine-print footer is a wall of tiny gray text, and the exclusions are buried in it. Seeing "Minimum purchase of $10+ and at least one eligible bundle" in a footnote under the button feels like the fine print is hiding the catch.
- The NEW, EYES, LIPS, FACE, SKINCARE and HAIR bars are huge, empty gray stripes with no imagery. On mobile it's a lot of scrolling for what are basically text links.
- The cadence is heavy. Five or six sends in one week, including 11/19's "Power Grip that glitters is e.l.f.ing gold" launch, makes this feel like noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with a single offer. Either feature the $5 reward or the $35+ gift, and move the other one lower or to a different send.
- 2. Replace the sign-in button with a "Shop Bundles & Sets" button that goes straight to the Bundles & Kits collection. Auto-apply the reward when I'm signed in.
- 3. Put the key terms, "$10 min, 1 bundle required," right under the headline in readable type. They shouldn't be only in the footnote.
- 4. Swap the gray category bars for tiles with product images or a compact icon row.
- 5. Cut the cadence. One send that bundles the launch and the reward would beat three near-identical ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your $5 reward is waiting. Bundles & Sets, thru 11/22`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$5 off any e.l.f. bundle, no points needed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Min $10 cart. Sign in and it's applied. Expires Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus a free full-size gloss with $35+. Both end 11/22.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally standard (table layout, `role="presentation"`, preheader, MSO conditionals). Two key links failed probing with relative-redirect errors, which looks like a probe artifact rather than confirmed breakage. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL] Receipt scanning link**: the probe error was `unknown url type: '/account/login?return_to=%2Faccount'`. The tracked URL `https://links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/kIJIDKQSy_1BnAA1AUg__w~~/...` redirects to a relative path with no host. Browsers resolve this correctly, so this is probably a probe limitation, but it is unverified.
- **[FAIL] "Download the e.l.f. app" link**: the probe error was `unknown url type: '/pages/mobile-app?utm_medium=email&af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app%3A%2F%2Fhome&is_retargeting=true&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=...'`. It has the same relative-Location pattern. The URL is also truncated in the QA output, so the full destination can't be confirmed.
- Both failures sit behind `links.elfcosmetics.com/f/a/...` click-tracking redirects. The final landing hosts were never resolved by the prober.
- Coverage was limited. 12 links were probed and 28 skipped, so about 70% of links are unverified.
- The 33% pass rate is driven mostly by these probe errors and the missing headers, not by confirmed defects.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Empty `<title></title>`** in `<head>`.
- **Malformed font stack**: `font-family:'',Verdana,Sans-serif` in the `mobile_hide` html_block. The empty family name is invalid and falls through to Verdana.
- **Missing alt text** on one image, `src: https://links.elfcosmetics.com/q/YPZ-cm_YtBVs__dYXbP9fw~~/...`. The `/q/` path suggests this is the open-tracking pixel, so impact is low, but it should carry `alt=""`.
- **`tabindex="-1"`** on the header image link (`<a ... target="_blank" style="outline:none" tabindex="-1">`) removes it from keyboard navigation. Combined with `outline:none`, there is no visible focus state.
- **Nested `nl-container` / `row-1` tables** are repeated inside `html_block` wrappers. This is valid but adds weight and complexity, and is a risk in Outlook/MSO.
- The Google Fonts `<link>` is correctly wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.
- Mobile hide/show uses `.mobile_hide` / `.desktop_hide` with the `max-width:660px` breakpoint, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The source is truncated, so the rest is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is **unconfirmed**. Verify against the raw message from the sender before treating it as a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown (relay limitation). Not a confirmed issue.
- The footer, physical address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated HTML, so they can't be assessed.
- **[WARN] Plain-text part is more than 70% URLs** (11,286 of 13,772 chars). This is a real deliverability signal, caused by long tracking URLs.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The only visible UTMs are on the app link: `utm_medium=email&utm_source=broadcast&bxid=...`. There is no `utm_campaign`, so campaign attribution depends on `bxid`.
- The deep-link params (`af_dp=com.elfcosmetics.app://home`, `is_retargeting=true`) are present for app routing.
- The receipt-scanning link lands on `/account/login?return_to=/account`. A logged-out recipient goes to a login wall rather than a feature page, but `return_to` is preserved.
- UTM consistency across the other 38 links can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the two FAIL links with a redirect-following client that resolves relative `Location` headers against the tracking host. Confirm the final status codes before logging them as defects.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay hides them.
3. Fill in `<title>`, fix `font-family:''`, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel.
4. Remove `tabindex="-1"` and `outline:none` from the header image link, or add a `:focus` style.
5. Reduce plain-text URL bloat by shortening tracking URLs or trimming links in the text part.
6. Add `utm_campaign` to the destination URLs for attribution that doesn't rely on `bxid`.
7. Raise probe coverage beyond 12 of 40 links, and always resolve the unsubscribe and primary CTA destinations.
