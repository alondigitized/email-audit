# Get $5 off $25 when you join
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- I'm already in a Sally loyalty program, so "Get $5 off $25 when you join" reads like it was written for someone who has never heard of them. It's the same signup pitch Ulta sent me on 10/6 and again on 10/7, the 20% coupon "when you join Ulta Beauty Rewards for free," and I'm Ulta Diamond. Sally's is just a smaller version. The hero is a hand holding a gift box with press-on nails, a pink wall of "Join Sally Rewards & save on your wish list," and a black "Join Sally.Rewards" button. It's pretty and clear about what it wants, but I can't tell whether the offer applies to me.
- The body is where it loses me. After the hero comes a tier explainer: Preferred earns 1 point per $1, Elite at $200+ a year earns 1.25 points. That's real points math, and it's more than Sephora's "You have 0 points" header gave me. But it's generic and not tied to my account. Then "Use Your Reward on These Favorites" shows four hair-color products (ion, AGEbeautiful) with no prices. If I'm choosing where to spend a $5 reward, I need to see what it buys. I'd skim it, maybe save it, and not forward it. I wouldn't click, because I'm not the target.

## What's working

- The hero line is clean: one pink block, one black button. The eye goes to "Join Sally.Rewards" first, which is better than the 10/8 "Don't ghost these deals" send, where I had to dig for the offer.
- The tier cards laying out Preferred vs. Elite, 1 vs. 1.25 points per dollar, and the $200 threshold gave me math I can work with, which Glossier never does.
- The nails-and-gift photo fits the holiday-season framing, and the Sally branding is consistent from top to bottom.
- Free shipping at $40+, 2-hour delivery, and the Klarna strip are easy to scan, and they answer real checkout questions.

## What's weak

- Recruitment copy goes out to someone who may already be a member, with no "already a member? see your points" path. Ulta did the same thing to me and I called it out there too.
- The $5-off-$25 offer is tiny. It sits in a smaller line under the headline instead of in a big burst like ILIA's orange "buy 3 get 20% off" badge or the BIGDEALS20 circle on Sally's own 10/5 send.
- The four product tiles have no prices and no reward math, and they're all hair color and developer. That's too narrow, and it doesn't match the nails and gift imagery above them.
- There's a stray "15% off, buy online, pick up in-store" strip with no context. It competes with the $5 offer, so there are two different discounts and no clear winner.
- The fine print is a giant block of tiny gray text that I can't read without zooming.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Send members a "Your points" version and send signup copy only to non-members. That single change lifts this more than anything else.
- 2. Put "$5 off $25" in a big badge in the hero and drop the extra 15% strip, so there's one offer.
- 3. Show prices on the products and add what $5 off does to each, like "$14.99 → $9.99 after reward." Mix in nails or skincare, not only hair color.
- 4. Move the tier explainer below the products, and add a "points to next reward" line for members.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your first $5 off $25 is waiting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sally Rewards: $5 off, plus points on every $1`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free to join. Earn 1 point per $1 and unlock birthday perks.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Valid through 10/31. Online or in-store pickup.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email's HTML is a conventional XHTML 1.0 table layout with responsive CSS and web-font fallbacks. Its main technical weaknesses are the 51 click-tracked links that couldn't be probed, an insecure `http://` tracking pixel, several images with no alt text, and a personalized email address in image URLs. The compliance and authentication gaps flagged by QA appear to come from the relay capturing headers, so they may not be real defects.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 51 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe. Destination validity, redirect chains, and expiry are unverified.
- `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2990...` is an insecure third-party pixel (Dotomi/Epsilon). Mail clients can block it, and it is a mixed-content risk if the email is viewed in a web client.
- `https://t.myvisualiq.net/impression_pixel?r=[CACHEBUSTER]&...` still has the literal `[CACHEBUSTER]` placeholder. The macro was never substituted, so the pixel is probably cached or fails to count impressions properly.
- Several third-party trackers are stacked: `pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq.net`, `dotomi.com`, and `evergage.com` (Salesforce Interaction Studio). This adds load time and creates more points of failure.
- The `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../003aZ00000rqS8GQAU` path ends in a Salesforce-style 18-character ID. It is a contact/lead ID embedded in a third-party URL.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Four Evergage `summary.png` images (`.../campaign/oBZKn/pI601/1..4/summary.png`) have no alt text. If these are content images, they are invisible to screen readers and blank when images are blocked.
- One `<img>` has an empty `src` and no alt (`Image missing alt text: : src:`). An empty src can trigger a request to the page URL in some clients and renders as a broken image.
- The tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com`, `myvisualiq`, `dotomi`) have no alt. They should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` so assistive technology skips them. I can't confirm from the truncated source whether they have those attributes.
- Font stack: `a{font-family:'Founders Grotesk', Helvetica, sans-serif}` references a font with no `@font-face` in the visible CSS. It falls back to Helvetica, which is harmless.
- The Outlook/VML namespaces and `<!--[if !mso]>` conditionals are present, as expected. A malformed `<!---->` comment appears before `<style>`. It is valid but unusual.
- The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the body structure, `lang` attribute, role attributes, or the preheader.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The Evergage image URLs contain `userId=rae-l@ete...` in the query string. This is a plaintext recipient email address (truncated in the data) in an image URL. It is a privacy exposure: it can end up in proxy logs, referrer data, and the Evergage dataset logs.
- The unsubstituted `[CACHEBUSTER]` token in the myvisualiq URL is a merge failure.
- No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `${…}` tokens were visible in the truncated source.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- QA flagged missing `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these. Without the raw headers I can't confirm they are absent. Treat this as **unverified**, not a confirmed defect. If they are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders over 5,000 messages per day.
- `Authentication-Results` is missing for the same reason, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `em.sallybeauty.com`, a sending subdomain, which is normal practice.
- The truncated source doesn't let me check for the physical postal address or the in-body unsubscribe link, so I can't assess those CAN-SPAM requirements.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All 51 links were skipped, so I couldn't check UTM parameters, final landing URLs, or whether the destination matches the "$5 off $25 when you join" offer.
- The tracking redirects hide the destinations. I can't verify that the offer's sign-up path lands on a working enrollment page.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Capture the raw message headers outside the relay. Then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
2. Resolve a sample of the 51 tracking links through to their final URLs. Check status codes, UTM parameters, and that the sign-up CTA lands on the enrollment page.
3. Add `alt` text to the four Evergage `summary.png` images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
4. Remove or fix the `<img>` with the empty `src`.
5. Fix the `[CACHEBUSTER]` macro substitution in the myvisualiq pixel.
6. Change the Dotomi pixel to HTTPS, or remove it.
7. Replace the plaintext email in `userId=` with an opaque ID or a hashed identifier.
