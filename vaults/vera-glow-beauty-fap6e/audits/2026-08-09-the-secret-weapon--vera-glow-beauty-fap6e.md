# The secret weapon
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "Retinol Is Our Superhero" — DE's really into their superhero/villain framing lately, and "Choose your fighter" for a retinol lineup is actually a decent hook, gamifying what's normally a boring "here's our three retinol SKUs" email. But that SheerID banner is right back at the top, same 20%-off-if-you're-a-teacher-or-student gate I already docked them for on 8/5's "Well, hi, 20% off." — so once again, this isn't for me, a beauty editor doing Rouge/Diamond points math, unless I'm faking a student ID.
- The actual content once you scroll past the gate is genuinely useful — A-Passioni, A-Gloei, A-Shaba laid out by strength (1.0%, 0.5%, 0.1%) with clear "who this is for" copy, plus a legit pro tip about retinol and sunlight/SPF. That's more educational value than most retinol emails bother with. But there's zero loyalty math, no tier language, and the offer up top excludes me by design — so I'd scroll for the product ed content, skip both CTAs, and not forward this unless a friend specifically asked me "which DE retinol should I start with."

## What's working

- "Choose your fighter" is a fun, clear organizing device for three products at different strengths — beats a flat grid.
- Each product has a one-line "who's this for" (strongest / gentler+moisture / eye area) so I actually know which one to click without guessing.
- The SPF/sunlight pro tip is real, useful skincare education — Drunk Elephant earns some credibility here that a pure sales blast wouldn't.
- Clean, on-brand pink-and-white product shots with a colored backdrop swatch per item — consistent visual language.

## What's weak

- SheerID gate is front and center again — third time in two weeks I've seen this exact "teacher/student/military/medical" mechanic from DE (8/5's "Well, hi, 20% off." was the same play), and I don't qualify, so the hero banner is dead weight for me.
- No Rouge/Diamond tier acknowledgment anywhere — Sephora Insider's K18 send at least tries (even if broken) to show a points bar; DE doesn't even attempt personalization.
- "SHOP NOW" and "SHOP RETINOL" are generic CTAs — compare to Ulta's code-specific "SCORE" banner or ILIA's named bundle link, DE just says "shop" three different ways.
- Subject "The secret weapon" gives me zero info on category or product versus 8/3's "Something big..." teaser, which at least built anticipation toward a calendar date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the audience: SheerID-eligible shoppers get the 20% banner, everyone else gets a retinol quiz or "find your strength" CTA instead of a discount they can't use — don't waste the hero slot on an offer 80% of your list can't redeem.
- 2. Add a Rouge/Insider-style repeat-purchaser nod for actual loyalists — "your third bottle of A-Passioni?" kind of line — since DE has no loyalty program of its own, lean into subscription/points-adjacent language instead.
- 3. Name-check ingredients or percentages in the subject line — "1.0% Retinol, Zero Guesswork" beats "The secret weapon" for someone who shops by concentration.
- 4. Make CTA copy specific per product: "Shop A-Passioni 1.0%" instead of a bare "SHOP" under each item.
