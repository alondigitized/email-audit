# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I open Staples.com on my phone and immediately get slapped with a full-screen email-signup modal before I can see a single product, deal, or category. I'm a Rewards member who reorders paper and toner on a schedule — I'm already sold on Staples — and the first thing they serve me is a gate. Behind it I can barely make out "Deals just for you" and some red product imagery that looks promising. This feels like a site that's optimizing for email list growth over returning customer experience.

## What's working

- **Top banner offer** — "Save $30 or more on custom print" is immediately visible even with the modal up. Custom print is legitimately something I buy for client deliverables, so this lands.
- **Search bar** — Visible above the modal. As a high-tech-comfort shopper who knows exactly what SKU I want, I'd use this immediately.
- **"Deals just for you"** — I can see this headline peeking below the modal fold. Personalized deal sections are exactly what a Rewards member wants; the promise is there even if the execution is gated.
- **Cart and Orders nav** — Quick access to my order history is right in the header. I check order status constantly for reorders.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I can't see the hero, the featured categories, the promotional rail, or any product imagery. As a returning customer, this is genuinely irritating.
- **"Unlock even" is truncated** — The modal headline cuts off mid-sentence. I don't know what I'm unlocking. That's sloppy on mobile.
- **No Rewards balance or member recognition** — I'm logged in or should be; there's zero acknowledgment that I'm an existing Rewards member. A modal asking me to sign up for emails when I already have an account feels like the site doesn't know who I am.
- **The "Sign up now" CTA competes with my shopping intent** — I came to reorder toner, not to hand over my email. The modal creates friction exactly where I need speed.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — Not on the banner, not in the nav. For an ops buyer placing regular orders, that number matters.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for recognized Rewards members** — If I'm cookied or logged in, don't show me an email gate. Serve the Rewards version of the modal (loyalty points status, exclusive member offer) or skip it entirely.
- **Move the "Save $30 on custom print" offer into the hero** — Right now it's a tiny top strip. For a B2B buyer like me, a $30 print savings headline with a "Shop Custom Print" CTA button would convert faster than any modal.
- **Fix the truncated "Unlock even" headline on mobile** — Either shorten the copy or let the modal scroll. A cut-off headline reads as broken.
- **Add a "Quick Reorder" or "Auto-Ship" shortcut to the top nav** — One tap to my recurring orders is the highest-value action for ops buyers; it belongs next to the cart icon, not buried in account settings.
