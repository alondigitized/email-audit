# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-06-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-17**
## Executive summary

- First thing I get when I open staples.com on my phone is a modal begging for my email address — not my toner order, not my Rewards account, not the weekly paper deal I came for. It feels like a company that wants something from me before giving me anything in return. The brand reads as a legacy office retailer trying to build a CRM list, and right now it's pushing email acquisition above everything else, including the actual shopping experience I showed up for.

## What's working

- **"Save 25% on Custom Print Banners" banner** — the one sliver of actual content visible above the modal is squarely in my wheelhouse; custom print is something agency ops teams order constantly
- **Modal renders cleanly on mobile** — no broken layout, readable type, the X dismiss button is large enough to tap without fumbling
- **"Sign up now" CTA** — high-contrast red button on white, zero ambiguity about what happens next

## What's weak

- **Forced email modal as the entire first impression** — I came here to reorder paper or check a toner price. Instead I'm being asked to do something for Staples before Staples does anything for me. That is backwards.
- **No Rewards acknowledgment** — I have a Staples Rewards account. There is zero recognition of that on this screen. A returning loyalty member should see their balance or a member-specific offer, not an email capture form.
- **Zero product or category visibility** — the modal completely blocks the homepage. I cannot see what's on sale, what's in stock, or where to tap for my usual categories.
- **Body copy register slightly off** — "we'll hook you up with the latest deals" reads more TikTok influencer than office supplies vendor. I'm 43 and running an agency, not scrolling for streetwear drops.
- **No concrete savings promise in the modal itself** — "Unlock even more savings" is vague. More savings than what? What do I actually get for handing over my email?

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for recognized Rewards members** — if I'm logged in or have a cookie, skip the acquisition flow entirely and surface my Rewards balance + a personalized offer instead
- **Replace the generic modal headline with a B2B-adjacent hook** — something like "Business account? Get 10% back on every order" converts far better for someone managing office supply spend
- **Show the "Save 25% on Custom Print Banners" offer inside the modal** — if you're going to block my view anyway, at least use the modal real estate to surface the deal rather than burying it behind the dismiss
- **Shrink or bottom-sheet the modal** — let at least the hero and top category rail remain visible so I know what I'm potentially missing; that creates urgency to dismiss and engage rather than just dismiss and leave
