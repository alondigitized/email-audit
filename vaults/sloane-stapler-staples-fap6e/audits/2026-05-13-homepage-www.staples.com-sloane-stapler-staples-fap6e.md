# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Staples' big swing for their 40th anniversary — splashy balloons, a bold 50% off headline, and a two-week sale window that ends 5/23. As an ops manager who basically lives on this site for toner and paper runs, I appreciate the no-nonsense sale banner, but the hero is throwing a birthday party for the brand when I showed up needing to restock an office. It's flashy, but I need to know fast whether the things I buy weekly are actually in the sale.

## What's working

- **"40TH ANNIVERSARY SALE — Up to 50% OFF" hero:** The discount is massive and credible for a milestone event — exactly the kind of event I wait for to bulk-order paper and toner.
- **"Ends 5/23" deadline:** Clean, honest urgency. Two weeks of deals is enough runway for me to plan a proper supply run.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in the nav:** That tab is one tap away from my most frequent category. That's efficient navigation for someone like me.
- **"Save $30 or more on custom print" top banner:** I order branded materials for client thank-you kits. That banner earns its real estate immediately.
- **"Deals just for you" module loading below fold:** Personalization is the right instinct for a loyalty member like me.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery (balloons, confetti):** Celebratory but generic — nothing in that visual says "office supplies" or "your weekly essentials are on sale." A balloon doesn't make me think toner.
- **No Rewards balance or loyalty hook visible:** I'm a Staples Rewards member. If I've got points sitting there, lead with that. The "Sign In" link does nothing to tease what's waiting for me.
- **"Shop now" CTA is too broad:** For an anniversary sale with presumably hundreds of categories, "Shop now" tells me nothing. "Shop ink & toner deals" would get me to tap immediately.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible:** I order in volume. If I'm spending $150 on paper and ink, I need to know immediately whether I clear free shipping — that's a conversion lever they're leaving cold.
- **Category rail not visible:** There's no quick-tap to Ink & Toner, Paper, or Breakroom above the fold. I have to hunt.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop now" for a category-split CTA row:** Put "Shop Ink & Toner," "Shop Paper," and "Shop Breakroom" as three tappable chips below the sale headline — ops buyers convert on category entry, not generic browsing.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the hero or the top banner:** Replace or augment the custom print strip with "Free shipping on orders $35+" so I know immediately I'll clear it.
- **Add a loyalty member callout to the hero:** A single line — "Rewards members earn 3X points through 5/23" — would convert every returning ops buyer who sees this.
- **Swap balloon imagery for a desk/supply-closet lifestyle shot:** Keep the sale badge and confetti accents, but anchor the visual in an actual office context so I know the 50% off applies to what I buy, not just party supplies.
