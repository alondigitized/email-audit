# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-06-03
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I was not expecting to be impressed by a Staples homepage, but here we are. First thing I see is "The most rewarding place to restock" with a fat "20% BACK in points on all ink and toner" badge front and center — that is my entire Tuesday in a headline. This looks like a brand that knows its repeat-buyer base and is actively working the loyalty angle rather than chasing first-timers with flashy lifestyle photography.

## What's working

- **"The most rewarding place to restock" hero copy** — "restock" is not a consumer word, it's an ops word. Someone thought about who actually buys ink every week, and it shows.
- **20% BACK badge placement** — circular callout sits right on the hero image with the HP product, so the offer and the category are visually fused. I don't have to do mental math about what it applies to.
- **Top banner ("Save $30 or more on custom print")** — I order branded envelopes and notepads for client gifts. That secondary promo is actually relevant to me without being the main pitch.
- **Print & Marketing tab visible in nav** — one tap to my second most-used category. That's exactly the navigation I want.
- **Pickup in store / location bar** — I live the BOPIS life when I need something same-day. Having my location pre-loaded is a small detail that saves friction.

## What's weak

- **No rewards balance displayed** — I'm a Rewards member. Show me my points balance when I'm signed out so I feel the pull to sign in. Right now it's just a generic "Sign In" link with no hook to make me click it.
- **No standalone hero CTA button** — the hero copy is great but there is no "Shop Ink & Toner" button or "Shop Now" in the hero panel. The Shop tab in the navigation bar has to do all that work.
- **"Eas..." cutoff in the tab bar** — that is almost certainly "Easy Rewards" and it's getting clipped on mobile. My loyalty program tab should never be truncated.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I have a standing order that easily clears any free-shipping bar, but I shouldn't have to wonder. Put "$X for free shipping" somewhere in this viewport.
- **No person in the imagery** — the product shot of HP toner is fine but showing a frazzled-but-competent office manager (me, basically) would add warmth without losing relevance.

## Recommendations

- **Add a rewards balance teaser to the Sign In CTA** — even something like "Sign in to see your 847 points" as placeholder logic. That nudge gets me logged in, and a logged-in Sloane is a higher-AOV Sloane.
- **Drop a "Shop Ink & Toner →" button inside the hero** — the copy is doing its job but a clear button anchors the action. Style it in white or charcoal on that blue background, keep the badge, done.
- **Fix the tab bar clipping on mobile** — "Easy Rewards" (or whatever that third tab is) needs to fit or be surfaced differently, maybe as a rewards icon with a point balance chip.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the top banner or below the hero** — one line: "Free shipping on orders $35+" right below the hero badge would close a conversion gap without cluttering the page.
