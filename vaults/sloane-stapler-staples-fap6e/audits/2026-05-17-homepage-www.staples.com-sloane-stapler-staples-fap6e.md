# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Staples is leading with their 40th Anniversary Sale — big red balloons, 50% off, hard deadline. As someone who hits this site every single week for toner and copy paper, the sale is relevant to me even if it's not speaking *to* me specifically. This looks like a broadcast sale push, not a personalized ops-buyer experience.

## What's working

- **The 50% off anniversary banner** — the offer is loud, clear, and the deadline ("Ends 5/23") creates real urgency without feeling cheap
- **Print & Marketing tab in the nav** — that's my second-most-visited section and it's right there without hunting
- **Top sticky banner** — "Save $30 or more on custom print" is directly relevant to my agency's print spend; it's above the hero and impossible to miss
- **Search bar placement** — big, front and center, exactly where I need it when I'm doing a restocking run and know what I want
- **Location pre-filled** — "Pickup in store, Monterey Park, CA" shows the site knows my context; useful when I need something same-day

## What's weak

- **No rewards visibility** — I'm a Staples Rewards member and there's zero points balance, no "Earn cash back on your order," nothing. The homepage doesn't acknowledge I exist as a loyalty customer.
- **Generic hero imagery** — Balloons and confetti are festive but tell me nothing about what's actually 50% off. Is it toner? Furniture? I have to click to find out.
- **"Shop now" CTA is too broad** — For an ops buyer like me, "Shop Office Essentials" or even "Shop Deals" would convert faster than the catch-all.
- **"Deals just for you" barely visible** — The personalized section is starting to peek below the fold but gets cut off; it should be promoted higher since I'm a known heavy repeat buyer.
- **No auto-ship or subscription reminder** — I have auto-ship set up and there's no acknowledgment of upcoming orders or easy re-order path.

## Recommendations

- **Surface Rewards balance immediately on sign-in or add a "Sign in to see your Rewards" teaser** — even a "Sloane, you have $X in rewards" banner would massively lift engagement for returning customers
- **Add a "Your Usuals" or "Reorder" module below the hero** — for ops buyers who come back weekly, a one-tap reorder path is worth more than any promo banner
- **Swap the hero sub-copy** from "2 weeks of deals!" to something like "Stock up on office essentials — up to 50% off" — keeps the urgency but tells me what's in the sale
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold in the nav bar or below the hero** — I often split orders across auto-ship and manual; knowing the threshold upfront would consolidate my cart
