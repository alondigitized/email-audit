# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-27
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- I opened Staples on my phone and the first thing I see is a blank white popup taking up 80% of my screen asking me to hand over my email before I can even look at the homepage. There's a useful custom print banner at the very top and I can see some paper product deals peeking through at the bottom, but the modal has killed any momentum I had. This feels like a supply-chain brand that knows its catalog cold but doesn't respect the returning customer's time.

## What's working

- **Top banner — "Save $30 or more on custom print"**: This is genuinely relevant. We order custom-branded materials for client thank-you packages. A dollar-anchored deal in the banner earns a second glance.
- **Paper product cards (bottom of screen)**: Hammermill and Staples Multi-use paper with crossed-out original prices ($81.99 → $48.99, $70.59 → $41.99) are exactly what I auto-ship. Those discounts are real money at our volume.
- **"Orders" dropdown in the header**: Quick access to order history is something I actually use. Having it front and center in the nav is a small but smart returning-customer signal.

## What's weak

- **The modal itself is broken**: The top half is a completely blank white rectangle — no headline, no image, no "join rewards" hook. There is literally nothing telling me why I should give Staples my email. This looks like a failed asset load, not a design choice.
- **No hero is visible**: I cannot see what the homepage is promoting today. If Staples has a Memorial Day sale, a tech deal, or a Rewards event running, I have zero idea because a broken popup is sitting on top of it.
- **No Rewards / loyalty hook**: I have a Staples Rewards account and order regularly. Nothing on this screen acknowledges that. The cart is at 0, there's no "Hi Sloane, you have X points" — I feel like a stranger.
- **Hamburger-only navigation on mobile**: My categories — paper, toner, office snacks — are buried behind a hamburger tap. For a returning buyer who knows exactly what she wants, this adds friction.
- **Modal fine print is walls of grey text**: "By clicking 'sign up now' you agree to sign up to receive emails from Staples about special offers, products and services. See our privacy policy for more details." It's small, dense, and screams legal obligation more than benefit.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the modal or kill it**: Either load the correct headline/image (presumably a rewards or discount hook) or remove the modal entirely for recognized returning users. A blank popup is worse than no popup.
- **Surface Rewards balance and personalized deals at the top of the logged-in mobile homepage**: A one-line strip — "You have 1,240 points · Redeem now" — does more for a repeat buyer than any generic banner.
- **Pin a fast-access row for high-reorder SKUs**: Paper, toner, and snacks are my repeat items. A "Reorder favorites" rail below the header would let me bypass the nav entirely and get to checkout in two taps.
- **Add a value proposition to the email capture**: Swap the blank modal for a two-liner — "Get 20% off your first order + exclusive member deals. Enter email to unlock." Give me a reason before you ask for the address.
