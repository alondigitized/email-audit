# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Staples.com on mobile knows exactly who I am — the "Deals just for you" section is surfacing paper and supplies front and center, which is basically my standing to-do list. The Memorial Day timing is on point, and the red-white-blue brand language makes it feel current. This is a clean, functional deal-hunter's homepage with a clear purpose: get me to the discounts fast.

## What's working

- **"Deals just for you" personalization rail** — showing paper and supplies immediately tells me this site knows I'm a repeat buyer. That copy paper ($41.99, was $70.59) is basically always in my cart.
- **"Save $30 or more on custom print" top banner** — as someone who orders client gift cards and branded materials, this hooks me immediately.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in the nav** — one tap to my primary category. That's exactly the right shortcut for me.
- **Memorial Day hero timing** — crisp, seasonally accurate. Stars and navy feel intentional, not lazy.
- **Strikethrough pricing** — showing the was/now comparison ($81.99 → $49.99) gives me a fast read on deal quality without digging.

## What's weak

- **Hero is generic** — "Memorial Day deals" could be any retailer. Nothing in the hero calls out business buyers, office managers, or bulk purchasers. A missed opportunity when I'm one of Staples' highest-LTV customers.
- **No Rewards balance visible** — I have a Staples Rewards account and I want to see my balance the moment I land. Right now it's just "Sign In" — no hook to show me what I'm sitting on.
- **Free shipping threshold not shown** — for an operations buyer ordering weekly, I need to know the threshold at a glance. It's not in this view.
- **Product cards show no ratings** — the two featured deals have no star ratings or review counts. For items I might not have bought before, that's friction.
- **"Eas" nav tab is cut off** — probably Easy Rewards, but if I can't read it I'm not tapping it. That's a mobile truncation issue.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic hero for a business-buyer Memorial Day message** — something like "Stock up before the weekend. 30% off paper, toner & more" would make me feel seen immediately.
- **Show my Rewards balance in the header even pre-sign-in** — a teaser like "You have rewards waiting" with a Sign In nudge converts loyalty members much faster than a plain "Sign In" link.
- **Add a free-shipping callout strip** — one line under the hero: "Free shipping on orders $35+" keeps me shopping instead of second-guessing cart size.
- **Add star ratings to the "Deals just for you" product cards** — even a single "4.6 (2,341)" under the price builds enough confidence to tap through.
