# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the site I expect when I'm re-ordering toner at 8 a.m. — familiar red, no fluff. Right now Staples is leading hard with a 40th anniversary blowout: "Up to 50% OFF, 2 weeks of deals, Ends 5/23." For an ops manager who runs a standing order account, the deal is attention-grabbing, but the homepage treats me like any random shopper rather than a Rewards member who is literally keeping their toner business afloat.

## What's working

- **40th Anniversary Sale hero with hard deadline ("Ends 5/23")** — a time-bounded reason to act today, which is exactly what gets an ops manager to pull up the cart before the meeting starts
- **"Up to 50% OFF" in massive type** — I scan this and immediately think "is toner in this sale?" That's a useful reflex trigger
- **"Save $30 or more on custom print" top banner** — I order branded materials for client thank-you kits; this is genuinely relevant to me
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in the nav** — one tap to my second-biggest spend category, no hunting
- **Store pickup pinned to Burbank, CA** — signals the site knows my context; cuts friction if I need something same-day
- **"Shop now" CTA button** — white pill on the red background, impossible to miss, no ambiguity about what happens next

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is balloons, not product** — anniversary balloons are festive but tell me nothing about what's actually on sale; I want to see paper reams or ink cartridges at 50% off, not a party
- **No Rewards balance or member greeting** — I am a Rewards member; the homepage should open with "Hi Sloane, you have 2,400 points" not a generic Sign In button
- **"Up to 50% OFF" without a category anchor** — "up to" on a homepage hero is table stakes; I immediately wonder whether toner and paper are actually in this sale or just the stuff nobody buys
- **Zero trust signals above the fold** — no free-shipping threshold mentioned, no "Easy returns," nothing that closes the micro-gap between "this deal looks good" and "I'll go ahead and add to cart"
- **"Shop" nav label is generic** — I have three primary categories (toner, paper, snacks/breakroom); none of them are surfaced without tapping into a flyout

## Recommendations

- **Replace the balloon background with a product lifestyle shot** — show a clean office desk with a laser printer, a ream of copy paper, and a price badge at 50% off; this instantly tells me the sale is mine
- **Surface a Rewards greeting for logged-in users** — even a one-liner "You have X points — here's what's on sale today" above the hero would convert loyal ops buyers faster than a cold anniversary banner
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the hero or directly below it** — "Free shipping on orders $35+" anchors my cart behavior immediately; without it I'm uncertain whether the deal survives the shipping cost
- **Anchor the "50% OFF" claim to a category** — add a sub-line like "on printer supplies, paper & more" so I know in two seconds whether to keep scrolling or bail
