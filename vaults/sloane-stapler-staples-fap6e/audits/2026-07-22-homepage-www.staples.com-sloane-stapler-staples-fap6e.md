# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-07-22
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened Staples.com and immediately got ambushed by a full-screen email pop-up before I could even see what was on the homepage. The irony is brutal — I've been a Staples customer for years, I have the Rewards card, I auto-ship paper and toner, and the first thing they do is treat me like I just walked in off the street asking for a coupon. The brand feels solid enough in the modal itself, but I have zero visibility into whatever campaign or content is actually sitting behind it.

## What's working

- **The $15 off $60+ offer** is a real number at a realistic spend level — for an office that orders regularly, $60 is nothing, and the savings feel genuine rather than gimmicky.
- **"Sign up now" button** is big, red, and impossible to miss — no mystery about the action they want.
- **Privacy policy link** is visible in the fine print — good on transparency, at least it's there.
- **Email field label** is properly marked required — clean form design, no guesswork.

## What's weak

- **The pop-up itself is the homepage.** I cannot see a single product, category, promo banner, or navigation item behind the modal. This is a complete dead end for a returning shopper who wants to re-order toner.
- **"New subscribers only"** — I'm already in their system. This offer does nothing for me, and there's no fallback for loyal customers already signed up.
- **No Rewards hook.** I have a Staples Rewards account. The fact that there's no "Sign in to check your balance" or "Rewards members get more" anywhere above the fold is a missed layup.
- **Coupon delay of "up to 2 business days"** — if I'm placing an order right now, this offer won't help me. That's a conversion killer buried in the fine print.
- **Behind the modal, I can barely make out what looks like a red hero area and the word "Eas..."** — meaning the actual campaign is completely inaccessible until I either sign up or close the modal.

## Recommendations

- **Surface a returning-customer path inside or beside the modal** — a "Already a member? Sign in" link or a "Skip to your account" tap target so loyal shoppers aren't treated like strangers.
- **Replace the full-screen interstitial with a sticky bottom banner** — the offer can still be prominent without nuking the homepage experience for people who came to actually shop.
- **Add Rewards balance or tier status to the modal close flow** — once I dismiss this, show me something like "Welcome back, Sloane — you have X points" to re-engage me immediately.
- **Make the coupon instant.** "Check your inbox in up to 2 business days" is a friction point that kills the impulse. Swap to a promo code shown on-screen at sign-up confirmation.
