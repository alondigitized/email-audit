# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-06-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- I've been a Staples Rewards member for years, and the very first thing I get when I open the app is a modal begging me to sign up for emails with a $15-off deal that's — in the fine print — for new subscribers only. That's not a welcome, that's a wall. Behind it I can barely make out the actual homepage. As a weekly buyer who auto-ships paper and toner through this site, I feel completely invisible right now.

## What's working

- **The $15 offer threshold ($60+) is credible for my spend level** — that's less than one ream of paper and a toner cartridge. The math is right even if the targeting is wrong.
- **"Sign up now" button copy is unambiguous** — no vague "Discover" or "Learn more" language. Clear ask, clear action.
- **Modal renders clean on mobile** — no overlapping text, no truncation, email field and CTA are sized correctly for a phone screen.

## What's weak

- **Forces a modal on a returning customer** — I've been on this site hundreds of times. A new-subscriber popup as the very first thing I see is tone-deaf and immediately signals Staples has no idea I exist.
- **"New subscribers only" buried in the fine print** — the headline screams $15 off, the bottom line whispers it doesn't apply to me. Classic bait-and-nothing for loyal customers.
- **100% of the actual homepage is obscured** — I cannot see a single product, category link, deal banner, or anything relevant to what I actually came here for. Not one tap is available to me except "Sign up now" or the X.
- **No loyalty hook anywhere on screen** — I have a Rewards account. Where's my balance? Where's my member pricing? Nowhere visible.
- **Two-business-day delay for the coupon** — the fine print says to check my inbox and it could take up to two days. That's not urgency; that's friction.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for logged-in or cookied returning users** — if I've ordered before, skip the new-subscriber pitch entirely and show me my Rewards balance or a personalized restock prompt instead.
- **Replace the modal with a persistent top banner** for new-visitor email capture — keeps the offer visible without blocking the homepage for everyone.
- **Surface a Rewards/loyalty CTA prominently above the fold** — something like "Sign in to see your 1,200 Rewards points" gives returning buyers like me a reason to engage immediately.
- **Add a time-bound deal strip** for office staples (paper, toner, snacks) visible before any email capture — that's what I came for, and it would get me to tap before I even see a modal.
