# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-08-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a big-box retailer that doesn't know who's actually looking at its homepage in August — it's gone all-in on back-to-school with a kid holding notebooks, while I'm standing here needing to reorder toner and paper for a ten-person office. Worse, there's a signup modal sitting half-transparent on top of the hero banner, so half the text is unreadable. Not the crisp, efficient experience I expect from a brand I already trust for weekly supply runs.

## What's working

- **"Up to $125 Off Custom Print Orders" top banner** — this is the one thing here that's actually relevant to me; we order business cards and signage for clients.
- **"Explore more" button** — clean, visible, easy tap target if I wanted to browse.
- **Nav bar with Shop / Print / School Suppl(ies)** — at least the category structure is there, even if it's cut off.

## What's weak

- **The overlapping email-signup modal** — "Enter your email," "Sign up now," and the legal disclaimer text are all stacked directly on top of the "Most wonderful deals / 25¢ school supplies" banner. It reads like a layout bug, not a promotion.
- **Back-to-school hero with a kid model** — I'm not shopping for a classroom, I'm restocking a break room and a printer closet. This tells me nothing about toner, paper, or bulk gift cards.
- **No Staples Rewards visibility** — I'm a Rewards member and there's nothing about my points, my balance, or member pricing anywhere above the fold.
- **Not signed in, and nothing nudges me to be** — just a plain "Sign In" link, no personalization prompt.
- **"Deals just for you" heading is cut off at the bottom** — the one section that sounds like it might be personalized to my order history isn't loaded in yet.

## Recommendations

- Fix the modal z-index/overlap bug — that email signup should be a clean popup or dismissible banner, not laid on top of the hero text.
- Swap the back-to-school hero for a business/office rotation for logged-out visitors with a recent B2B or ink/toner purchase history — or at least run a second hero card for "Office Restock" alongside school supplies.
- Surface Staples Rewards balance or a "Reorder your usuals" module above the fold for returning shoppers — I've got a recurring toner order, show me that.
- Add "Ink & Toner" and "Office Supplies" as their own top-level nav items instead of burying them under a generic "Shop" dropdown.
