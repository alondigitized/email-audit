# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-07-01
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-01**
## Executive summary

- I opened Staples.com and the first thing I got was a modal eating my entire screen before I could see a single product or category. The offer is $15 off a $60 order for email signups — which I already am, as a Rewards member. I've been ordering toner and paper from this site for years and it's blocking me like I've never been here before.

## What's working

- **The offer amount is prominent:** "$15 OFF" in large red text is instantly readable and the $60 threshold is immediately clear — no hunting for asterisks.
- **Button contrast is solid:** The red "Sign up now" button on white background is hard to miss, even on a phone screen.
- **Close button is present:** The X in the top corner is visible, which at least gives me an escape hatch.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is hidden.** I can't see a category, a product, a deal strip, or a navigation link. On mobile, this modal goes edge to edge. There is nothing behind it that I can interact with.
- **The offer excludes me.** Fine print reads "Offer valid for new subscribers only." I'm a Rewards member. I get Staples emails. I just got blocked from my own homepage by a coupon I can't use.
- **No loyalty or Rewards hook anywhere.** If this was smart, it would recognize I'm logged in (or close to it) and show my Rewards balance or a member deal instead of a new-subscriber pitch.
- **No navigation visible.** I can see the hamburger menu and cart icon barely peeking around the modal edges, but I can't tap into any category from here.
- **The copy is generic.** "We'll hook you up with the latest deals, trending picks and much more" — that tells me nothing about what Staples actually has that I need this week.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for returning visitors and logged-in Rewards members.** If I've placed an order in the last 12 months, do not show me a new-subscriber pitch — show me my account or a loyalty benefit instead.
- **If the modal must fire, make it loyalty-aware:** Swap "Sign up for Staples® Email" with "You have X Staples Rewards points — here's how to use them" for recognized users.
- **Cut the modal on mobile entirely** or reduce it to a slim bottom sheet. Full-viewport modals on phones are dark-pattern territory and push high-intent returning shoppers to just close and hope the actual site loads.
- **Add a time or inventory hook** if you keep the modal — "Today only" or "While supplies last" — because right now there's zero urgency to act now versus dismissing and browsing.
