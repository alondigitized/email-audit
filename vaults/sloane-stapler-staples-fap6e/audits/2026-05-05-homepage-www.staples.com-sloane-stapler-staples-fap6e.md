# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Staples knows its audience — this is clearly a B2B-leaning homepage that leads with business savings, not back-to-school fluff or consumer lifestyle shots. The red-and-white brand is instantly recognizable, the top banner hits print buyers immediately, and the hero is straightforwardly transactional. That said, the above-the-fold real estate on mobile is thin: I can see the promise but there's no button to click it yet.
- **7/10**
- + Hero copy speaks to my persona: "Big savings for your business" is direct ops-lead language
- + Concrete offer above the fold: "Save $30 or more on custom print" in the top banner — I order custom print regularly
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: red nav bar → search → location → category tabs → hero flows logically
- + No render bugs: page looks clean, no broken elements, no overlap
- + Demographic signals match: business-focused messaging maps well to an ops buyer, not a consumer impulse shopper
- + Current campaign: "This week only!" signals a live, not stale, promotion
- + Offer feels honest: straightforward dollar savings, no fine-print hiding visible
- − No primary CTA button visible in the hero above the fold
- − No loyalty/rewards balance or returning-shopper hook — Sign In is there but nothing that says "your rewards balance is X"
- − No returning-member personalization visible

## What's working

- **"Big savings for your business" hero copy** — that phrase is basically my job title in four words. It doesn't waste time.
- **"Save $30 or more on custom print" top banner** — I do custom print for client thank-you kits. This banner caught my eye immediately.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in primary nav** — one tap to my second-most-purchased category. Zero friction.
- **"This week only!" urgency** — gives me a reason to act now rather than bookmark and forget.
- **Store pickup location auto-detected (Burbank, CA)** — I use BOPIS for rush toner runs. That's a small but real signal that Staples knows how I shop.

## What's weak

- **No visible CTA button in the hero** — "Big savings for your business" is a headline, not a door. I don't know what to tap to actually get there.
- **Sign In is present but cold** — there's no "Welcome back, Sloane" or rewards balance shown. As a Rewards member with recurring orders, I expect some personalization the moment I land.
- **Navigation items truncated** — the third tab says "Eas..." and is cut off. That's probably "Easy Reorder" — which is literally my entire workflow — and it's not even readable on my screen.
- **Hero image is stock-generic** — colorful highlighters on a shelf reads "office supply store" but doesn't connect to a business buyer's real pain (running out of toner before a client deadline).
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — as someone who orders $200+ at a time, I want to know immediately if I qualify. It's not here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable CTA button to the hero** — "Shop Business Deals" or "See This Week's Savings" with a real button. The promise is there; the door is not.
- **Surface the Staples Rewards balance or a "Welcome back" greeting for signed-in users** — or at minimum change "Sign In" to "Rewards" with a teaser of points available. That's the hook that keeps me from price-shopping at Amazon.
- **Fix the truncated nav tab** — "Easy Reorder" deserves its full name; that feature is a retention tool and it's getting clipped on mobile. Either shorten the label or compress the adjacent tabs.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the top banner or directly below the hero** — even "Free shipping on orders $49+" would give me an immediate reason to fill a cart.
