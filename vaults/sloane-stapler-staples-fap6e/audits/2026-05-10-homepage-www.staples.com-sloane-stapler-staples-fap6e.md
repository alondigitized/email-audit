# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Staples is leading hard with their 40th Anniversary Sale — big red hero, 50% off promise, balloons. As someone who reorders paper and toner every week and tracks Rewards points like a hawk, this feels like my store but in party mode. The top banner on custom print is directly useful to me. What's missing is any signal that they know I'm already a customer.

## What's working

- **"Save $30 or more on custom print" top banner** — that's literally something I order. First thing I saw and it's immediately actionable.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in the nav** — one tap to where I live. That's exactly how this should be organized for me.
- **"Deals just for you" module starting below the fold** — signals personalization is coming. If it actually surfaces paper, toner, or snacks, I'm going to engage.
- **50% off with a hard deadline ("Ends 5/23")** — as an ops lead I respond to deadlines. I'll note it and probably forward it to my boss to approve a bulk order.
- **Pickup in store / Burbank, CA** — store localization is present, even on mobile. Good for when I need something same-day.

## What's weak

- **No Rewards balance or member greeting** — I have a Staples Rewards account and spend probably $400/month here. This homepage treats me like a stranger. A "Welcome back, Sloane — $X in Rewards" would convert me immediately.
- **"Shop now" CTA goes nowhere specific** — that button almost certainly dumps me into a general sale page. For a returning buyer with a known category, I want "Shop Paper & Supplies" or "Reorder toner."
- **Zero business-buyer signals in the hero** — the balloon motif reads like a retail consumer sale, not a small-business owner anniversary event. Competitors could run this exact hero for any consumer brand.
- **Free-shipping info is absent above the fold** — I shop .com only, so I always check shipping cost before I commit. It's not here.
- **"Eas..." nav label is truncated** — probably "Easy Rebates" or "Easy Reorder." That second one is extremely relevant to me and it's getting clipped on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the balloon hero for a business-owner lifestyle shot** — a desk, a stocked supply closet, a running printer. Keep the 50% off headline. The anniversary angle can stay but make it feel like it's for people who run offices, not birthday parties.
- **Surface Rewards balance for signed-in users in the nav bar** — even a small "$12.50 Rewards" chip next to the cart icon would change my behavior from browse to buy.
- **Untruncate the nav on mobile** — "Easy Reorder" is probably my most-used feature. If it's getting cut off on a standard iPhone viewport, that's a quick fix that directly impacts retention.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the hero subtext** — one line: "Free shipping on orders $35+" right under the deadline copy. Costs nothing, removes friction for .com-only shoppers.
