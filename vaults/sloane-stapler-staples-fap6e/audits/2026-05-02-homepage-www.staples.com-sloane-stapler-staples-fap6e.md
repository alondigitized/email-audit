# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Staples is apparently throwing a rager and didn't tell me. The entire hero is pushing a Party City co-brand, which — fine, I guess — but I came here to reorder toner and check if my rewards points updated, not plan confetti drops. The site feels like it's trying to grab a different customer right now, and the above-the-fold space is almost entirely wasted on me.
- **3/10**

## What's working

- **Top announcement bar** ("Save $30 or more on custom print") — this actually speaks to me since I do bulk print orders for client decks. Small but relevant.
- **Persistent search bar** — big, prominent, red background. I know exactly how to use it. Muscle memory kicks in immediately.
- **Store pickup locator** showing "Burbank, CA" — I like knowing BOPIS is an option; sometimes I need something same-day and this tells me the option is live.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab** in the nav — at least it's visible. That's my most-used section after supplies.
- **Sign In + Orders** in the header — quick access to my account and order history without hunting.

## What's weak

- **"Epic Parties Start Here" hero** — this is a Party City partnership banner with balloons and confetti. I buy paper by the case. This hero is for a completely different person and it dominates the entire viewport.
- **"Shop party supplies" CTA** — not a category I've ever clicked on staples.com in 12 years of ordering office supplies.
- **No Rewards/Staples+ callout above the fold** — I'm a Staples Rewards member and there's zero mention of my program, my points balance, or any member pricing until I sign in. That's a miss.
- **Auto-ship / Subscribe & Save** — nowhere visible. That's my primary shopping behavior on this site and it's buried.
- **"40% off event prints"** partially visible at bottom — another events/party angle. I don't need event prints, I need toner and 20 lb copy paper.
- **No quick-reorder module** — for a high-frequency B2B buyer like me, not surfacing "Buy again" or recent orders above the fold is a real friction point.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Party City hero for a B2B/office value prop** — something like a split hero showing bulk paper + toner on one side and "Auto-ship — set it, forget it" on the other. That's who actually spends money here regularly.
- **Add a Rewards balance strip** below the nav for signed-in users — even a thin bar that says "You have 1,240 points" does serious loyalty work. Right now I have to dig for it.
- **Surface a "Reorder" shortcut** in the top nav or below search — "Quick Reorder" is already a thing Staples offers; promote it at the top for returning .com customers.
- **Move the custom print offer** from a tiny announcement bar to a supporting module lower on the page, and let the hero speak to office operations buyers who are the core repeat-purchase segment.
