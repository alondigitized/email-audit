# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-07-08
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-08**
## Executive summary

- I land on Staples.com and immediately get a modal slapped in my face before I've seen a single product. The offer — $15 off a $60+ order for signing up for email — is actually decent for someone who orders supplies as regularly as I do, but the execution feels like a tollbooth I have to pay before I'm allowed to shop. Behind the popup I can barely make out what looks like deals and navigation, but the modal swallows the whole screen and I can't assess anything else without dismissing it first.

## What's working

- **$15 off $60+ threshold is plausible for me.** A toner cartridge or a ream-and-a-half of paper clears $60 easily. This is a realistic hook, not an aspirational minimum.
- **"Sign up now" button is unambiguous.** Red, full-width, nothing confusing about the action they want me to take.
- **Fine print is present and visible.** I appreciate that they disclosed the new-subscribers-only caveat and the 2-business-day delay in the same modal — no hidden gotcha after I hand over my email.
- **Clean visual hierarchy inside the modal.** Logo → offer headline → explanation → input → CTA → disclosure. Logical flow, easy to scan.

## What's weak

- **Entire homepage is blocked.** I can't see a single product, category, or deal behind this modal. As a returning shopper who orders on autopilot, I have zero tolerance for being forced to look at an acquisition modal before I can do anything.
- **No Staples Rewards mention anywhere.** I have a rewards account. The fact that this modal is pitching email sign-up instead of acknowledging my loyalty program feels tone-deaf — like the site doesn't know me at all.
- **Zero imagery.** Not even a photo of a printer, a box of paper, nothing. The modal is sterile. It doesn't remind me why I came here.
- **No urgency, no deadline.** "$15 off when you sign up" could be running forever. There's no reason to do it now vs. closing and shopping.
- **Fine print is tiny.** The disclosure paragraph is a wall of small gray text. As a high-tech-comfort Gen Xer I'll read it, but it's not designed to be read — it's designed to be overlooked.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the modal for detected returning visitors or Rewards members.** If I've shopped Staples.com before, show me my rewards balance or a "Welcome back" banner instead of an acquisition offer that doesn't apply to me.
- **Add a Staples Rewards CTA to the modal as a second path.** "Already a Rewards member? Sign in and save automatically." — one line, one link, keeps loyal customers moving instead of bouncing.
- **Add a single lifestyle image to the modal.** A desk, a stack of paper, a toner box — anything that anchors this offer in an office context and reminds me why I opened this app.
- **Add a lightweight urgency hook.** "Offer ends Sunday" or "New subscribers this week only" — something that gives me a reason to sign up now instead of x-ing out and coming back empty-handed.
