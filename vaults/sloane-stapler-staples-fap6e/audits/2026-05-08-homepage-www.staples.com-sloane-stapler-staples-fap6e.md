# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Staples knows exactly who I am the moment the page loads — "Big savings for your business" with a shelf full of branded pens and highlighters is basically a still life from my supply closet. The red-and-white brand system is clean, the custom print banner at the top is legitimately useful for a client-gift run, and the "This week only!" urgency hook is the kind of thing that makes me stop scrolling LinkedIn and actually tap. Functionally solid, but I'm going to need to scroll to see pricing, specific products, and any mention of Rewards.

## What's working

- **"This week only! Big savings for your business."** — That headline is doing real work. It's my language, not a consumer-retail frame. "For your business" signals they see me.
- **Top-of-page custom print banner ("Save $30 or more on custom print")** — I literally bulk-order branded folders for client welcome kits. This offer is directly relevant and I noticed it immediately.
- **Store pickup row ("Pickup in store — Burbank, CA")** — Knowing my location is loaded in tells me I'm not starting from scratch. That's a small trust signal that the session knows me.
- **Category nav strip** — "Print & Marketing" is visible without a tap. That's my second-most-used category after paper/toner and it's right there in the top nav.
- **Hero imagery** — Staples-branded pens, highlighters, the wooden supply organizer — this is exactly what I'm stocking. It's not aspirational fluff, it's a photo of my actual life.

## What's weak

- **No Rewards hook visible** — I'm a Staples Rewards member. My balance, my points, my tier — none of that is surfaced. "Sign In" is generic. That's a miss for a returning buyer.
- **No price anchors in the hero** — "Big savings" means nothing without a number. Is it 20% off? $15? I can't evaluate the offer until I tap in, which is friction.
- **No visible Shop Now or equivalent CTA button** — The hero is a statement, not a door. There's no button telling me where to go to claim the savings.
- **"Eas" is cut off in the nav strip** — Whatever that category is (Easel? Easy Rebates?), the truncation looks like a rendering glitch on mobile. Minor but sloppy.
- **No mention of auto-ship or subscription** — I run auto-ship on paper and toner. That's a meaningful loyalty feature that isn't surfaced anywhere in this viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Rewards balance chip to the Sign In row** — Even logged-out, show "Sign in to see your X points." I already know I have points; remind me what I'm leaving on the table.
- **Put a dollar figure in the hero** — Change "Big savings for your business" to "Save up to 30% on office essentials — this week only." One number turns a headline into an offer.
- **Add a visible CTA button to the hero** — "Shop the deals" or "See this week's savings" as a button directly below the hero copy. Right now there's nowhere obvious to tap.
- **Surface auto-ship / subscription in the nav or a secondary banner** — A thin strip under the main nav that says "Set up auto-ship on paper & toner — never run out" would speak directly to repeat buyers like me and differentiate from Amazon.
