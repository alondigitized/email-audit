# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the homepage I expect from Staples — no-nonsense, sale-forward, and loud about it. The 40th Anniversary Sale is front and center with a clean deadline, which works for me. But as someone who's here weekly for toner and paper, not party supplies, the balloon-covered hero feels like it's talking to a consumer shopper, not the person keeping a 10-person agency running.

## What's working

- **Top banner ("Save $30 or more on custom print"):** This is genuinely useful for me — agency does client thank-you kits, branded stuff. Good placement, easy to read.
- **"Print & Marketing" tab in nav:** Right there in the top nav, one tap. I don't have to dig through a nested Shop menu to get where I go most.
- **Anniversary sale deadline ("Ends 5/23"):** Hard date gives me a reason to act today rather than save for later. I respond to real deadlines.
- **Search bar:** Prominent, full-width, accessible immediately. I know my SKUs; I search first. This is the right priority.
- **"Deals just for you" section starting below fold:** Personalized section starting to load tells me the site knows I have a purchase history — that's the right direction.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery (balloons):** Festive consumer energy. I'm here to buy toner, not RSVP to a birthday party. Nothing in the hero acknowledges I'm a B2B repeat buyer.
- **No Rewards/account hook in the hero area:** I have Staples Rewards. My balance and next-tier progress should be surfaced the second I hit the homepage — especially without logging in, a gentle nudge to sign in for member pricing would convert me fast.
- **"Shop now" CTA is too generic:** Takes me to… what? The entire sale? I need office supplies at 50% off, not a browse-everything experience. A split CTA ("Shop Ink & Toner" / "Shop Paper") would be far more useful.
- **No free-shipping callout above the fold:** I have a standard order that easily hits most free-ship thresholds, but new buyers can't see the threshold at all. That's a missed conversion signal.
- **The "Eas…" nav tab is cut off:** Whatever that is — Easy Reorder, maybe? — it's clipped on mobile and unreadable. If that's Easy Reorder, that's literally my most-used feature and it's unusable in the viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the balloon hero with a split hero for B2B vs. consumer:** Left tile targets "Office Essentials" with a toner-and-paper lifestyle shot; right tile keeps the anniversary promo for deal hunters. One asset change, immediately more relevant to my use case.
- **Surface Rewards balance in the sign-in bar:** Replace the generic "Sign In" with "Sign in to see your Rewards balance" or show an earned-balance teaser even pre-login. For a loyalty-heavy shopper like me, that's a pull, not a push.
- **Fix the clipped nav tab on mobile:** "Eas…" needs to either scroll horizontally with a visible indicator or drop the least-used tab to keep Easy Reorder (if that's what it is) fully readable.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line to the top banner strip:** The custom print promo is good, but sandwich it with "Free shipping on orders $35+" so the value stack is clear before the hero even loads.
