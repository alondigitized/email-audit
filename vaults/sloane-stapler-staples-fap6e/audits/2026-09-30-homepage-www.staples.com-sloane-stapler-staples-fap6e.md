# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-09-30
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a big-box office-supply brand pushing print services and holiday print hard right now. The top strip and the tiles under the hero are dense with points, percent-off and K-cup deals. The hero on my phone is a store-counter photo about "professional-grade print solutions," not the toner, paper and break-room restocking I come here for.

## What's working

- **The top promo strip.** "25% back in points on custom print orders of $100+" and "K-cup pods, 80-96/pk, starting at $38.99 for members" are the kind of rewards and bulk-pack deals I shop for.
- **The search bar.** It is wide, front and center, and sits right under the logo. I know what I want, so I can type "toner" and skip the homepage.
- **The "Pickup in store – Monterey Park, CA" chip.** It shows I'm localized and gives me a same-day option when the office runs out of something.
- **The "Coffee month is here" card.** The Dunkin' Pumpkin Spice and Donut Shop K-cup tiles, plus the coffee makers, are useful for my snack and break-room orders. "Up to 25% off" is clear.
- **The "25% back in points" card.** Points are my loyalty currency, and putting the Staples Rewards value on a card is the right call.

## What's weak

- **The hero.** The big "easy" lockup plus "Quality, professional-grade print solutions." doesn't tell me what's on offer, and "Shop now" is generic. A woman with her hands pressed together at a counter is a confusing image.
- **The sign-in flyout.** It is open on load and sits over the nav, and the whole mobile nav is cramped. "Easy Rewards" is half hidden, and "School Suppl…" is cut off with a scroll arrow.
- **My staples are missing from the first screen.** I see no toner, paper, ink or auto-ship anywhere. I'd need to open "Shop" to find them.
- **No deadlines or terms.** "Chair savings event, extra 20% off" has no end date and no exclusions, and "Chair" isn't something I'm buying this week.
- **The 'gift cards in bulk' angle is missing.** The "25% back" and holiday-print cards are close, but nothing points at client thank-you gifts.
- **The top strip wraps onto two lines per item and is small.** It is easy to skim past on a phone.

## Recommendations

- **Put my restock on the hero.** Swap the counter photo for a toner cartridge, ream of paper and snack-box shot with the headline "Restock the office. Auto-ship and save." Make the button read "Set up Auto-ship". Anyone on rewards could see it.
- **Close the sign-in flyout on load.** Replace it with a slim "Hi, sign in to see your Easy Rewards balance" bar. That shows the loyalty hook without blocking "Easy Rewards" or "School Supplies" in the nav.
- **Show what the promos need.** Add "Ends 10/15" and "Free shipping on $X" to the top strip or the first card. For "Chair savings event," add the real discount range and the end date.
- **Make the first card a business bundle.** Something like "Office essentials — toner, paper, coffee — 20% off your first auto-ship order" with real product tiles. That replaces the generic print-solution card.
