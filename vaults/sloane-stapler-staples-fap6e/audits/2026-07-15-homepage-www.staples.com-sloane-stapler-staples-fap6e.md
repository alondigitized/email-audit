# Homepage snapshot · www.staples.com · 2026-07-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-15**
## Executive summary

- Staples is pushing a full back-to-school blitz right now — the hero is a kid in pigtails holding composition notebooks, price-anchoring at 25 cents. As a 43-year-old ops lead whose job is keeping the office stocked with toner and reams of 24lb paper, I feel like I walked into the wrong aisle. The brand is clearly here, the bones are fine, but this homepage was built for moms shopping for their third-grader, not for me.

## What's working

- **"$40 Off Custom Print Orders" top banner** — this is the one thing on screen that speaks directly to what I actually buy. My agency prints client decks, branded folders, every week. That offer has my attention.
- **Search bar placement** — large, prominent, right at the top. I know what I need (HP 26X, Hammermill 8.5x11 case of 10); I'll just search. It's right there.
- **"Print & Marketing" nav tab** — visible without hunting. One tap and I'm in my territory.
- **Clean layout, no chaos** — the page isn't throwing 12 things at me at once. For a busy Tuesday morning on my phone, that's a baseline I appreciate.

## What's weak

- **Hero is completely off-target for me** — "The most wonderful time of the year" with a backpack-ready child is a retail back-to-school classic, but I'm here to reorder toner, not stock up on Crayolas. Zero connection to B2B / office procurement.
- **"Explore more" CTA is doing no work** — explore *what*, exactly? That button copy tells me nothing and leads me nowhere I can predict.
- **No Staples Rewards surfaced** — I'm a rewards member. My balance, my member pricing, any personalization — none of it visible. A returning loyal customer sees a blank sign-in link and feels like a stranger.
- **No free-shipping threshold** — for .com-only shoppers doing weekly orders, free shipping is table stakes. Not seeing a dollar threshold or "free shipping on X" anywhere above the fold.
- **No office/business category entry point** — there's "Shop" and "Print & Marketing" but nothing that says "Office Supplies" or "Ink & Toner" right at the surface for fast re-orders.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a split screen or a business-angle version** — keep back-to-school below the fold; surface an "Office Essentials" or "Restock Your Office" hero module above it, even as a secondary banner strip.
- **Surface Staples Rewards prominently** — replace the plain "Sign In" with "Rewards: [balance]" for logged-in users, or "Join Rewards, earn X% back" for new visitors. A loyalty program this old should be front and center.
- **Rewrite the CTA to the offer** — "Shop Print Deals" or "Claim $40 Off Print" is shippable copy that tells me exactly where I'm going.
- **Add a free-shipping badge** — even a small "$X ships free" line near the search bar or cart icon does real conversion work for high-AOV repeat shoppers.
