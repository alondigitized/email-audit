# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first impression: this is exactly the kind of site I trust. That "$48,996,825.57 Raised For Local Bookstores" counter is the thing I open Bookshop for — I want to *see* the mission in action. But I'm barely able to appreciate any of it because a privacy policy modal has bulldozed over most of the above-the-fold view. The featured book — *American Alt* by Chris Lockhart — looks genuinely interesting, but I'm being asked to manage cookie consent before I can even browse.

## What's working

- **"Every order financially supports local bookstores!" top banner** — this is the first thing I read and it's the *reason* I'm here. Perfect placement.
- **$48,996,825.57 Raised For Local Bookstores counter** — I actually look for this number. Seeing it in teal and bolded at the top of the page reaffirms the mission before I've even looked at a book. This is the single best element on screen.
- **Featured book with a literary blurb** — *American Alt* has a pullquote from Robert Kolker, who wrote *Hidden Valley Road*. That's a real endorsement from a respected author, not a paid placement. It speaks my language.
- **20% OFF badge on the hero** — visible discount without any hard-sell copy; it's tasteful.

## What's weak

- **Privacy policy modal is eating the page** — "We've updated our Privacy Policy" wall covers the entire lower two-thirds of the visible viewport. I came here to find books, not to do legal housekeeping before I've even scrolled once.
- **No visible CTA for the book** — the "Accept / Deny Non-Essential" buttons are the only tappable elements I can see. There is no "View Book," "Shop Now," or "Add to Cart" button visible anywhere.
- **No navigation categories above the fold** — the hamburger menu is there but categories aren't surfaced; I can't tap "Fiction" or "New Arrivals" without either dismissing the modal first or already knowing to hunt for the menu.
- **No earnings percentage for my specific bookshop** — the total raised counter is great, but I track my shop's individual earnings. There's no "your shop earned X" hook visible, which would be the loyalty trigger that keeps me coming back.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the privacy modal after a 3-second delay or on scroll**, not as a blocker before the page loads. Cookie consent on a book-buying site feels especially incongruous with the indie-mission brand — the UX friction directly undercuts the trust-building the rest of the page earns.
- **Surface a "your bookshop" loyalty hook in the top strip or just below the counter** — even a line like "Supporting [Shop Name] — earned $X this year" would make returning visitors feel seen immediately.
- **Add a visible "Shop This Book" or "View Book" CTA on the hero image** — the book cover is doing good work but it's a dead end visually. One tappable button converts browser to buyer.
- **Replace the hamburger-only nav with 3–4 persistent category pills** (Fiction, Nonfiction, New Arrivals, Gifts) directly below the search bar, so I can get to my section in one tap without opening a drawer.
