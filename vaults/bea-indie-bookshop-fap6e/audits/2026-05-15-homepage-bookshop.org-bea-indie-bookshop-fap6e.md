# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, immediately — this is my place. The "$47,460,814.17 Raised For Local Bookstores" counter hits me right in the chest before I've even processed anything else, which is exactly why I use Bookshop over Amazon. The featured book is an Indie Next pick with a wildly literary description, which tells me the curation hasn't gotten lazy. But a massive cookie consent modal is eating the bottom half of my screen before I can see anything else, and that friction is real.

## What's working

- **The $47,460,814.17 counter** is the single most powerful element on the page for me — it's real, specific, and updates. I actually check this number. It's why I'm here instead of a corporate competitor.
- **"INDIE NEXT / BOOKSELLERS RECOMMENDS" badge** on the featured book signals that a real human bookseller picked this, not an algorithm. That earns a tap.
- **The hero description** — "A contemporary dystopian elegy narrated by a cat imprisoned in a Schrödinger's box" — is doing a lot of heavy lifting. It's weird and literary and I want to know more.
- **Top banner offer** (15% off Mental Health Awareness Month books) is thematically coherent and book-specific. Not a random promo.
- **Search bar placement** is excellent — prominent, above the fold, labeled "Search books, authors, ISBNs" which is exactly the right language.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocking ~40% of the viewport** is the biggest issue. I can barely see the book being promoted. The two buttons ("Accept" / "Deny Non-Essential") are the most prominent interactive elements on the page, which is backwards.
- **No clear "Shop Now" or "Add to Cart" CTA** is visible — the modal has eaten whatever button would normally be here.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**, and at my ~$30/month spend, that matters.
- **No returning-shopper acknowledgment** — I have an account and probably a wishlist here, but there's zero personalization or "welcome back" hook in this viewport.
- **No ratings or reviews visible** on the featured book, just the Indie Next badge. I'd want at least a star count before tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Reduce the cookie modal to a thin bottom bar** (like a cookie toast) rather than a full overlay that covers the hero — the current implementation buries the product before I can evaluate it.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the top banner** alongside the 15% off copy — something like "Free shipping on orders over $35 · 15% off Mental Health Month picks" keeps the offer complete.
- **Add a returning-account hook** in the header — even just "Hi Bea" or a wishlist count badge near the cart icon would signal this site knows me.
- **Add a star rating or short review snippet** beneath the Indie Next badge on the hero book — bookseller recommendation is good, but a "4.6 stars, 1,240 readers" line would close the loop.
