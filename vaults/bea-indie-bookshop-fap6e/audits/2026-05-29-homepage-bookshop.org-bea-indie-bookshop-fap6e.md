# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- Bookshop.org immediately reads as mission-first retail — that big raised-for-local-bookstores counter lands before I've even looked at a product. The hero is a single featured NYT bestseller with a bold, editorial feel that suits me just fine. What kills the first impression is the cookie consent modal plastered over the hero before I can even see what I'm buying.

## What's working

- **The raised-money counter** — "$47,773,495.92 Raised For Local Bookstores" is crack for me. That's the whole reason I use this site over Amazon, and they're surfacing it immediately. Smart.
- **The Mental Health Awareness Month promo strip** — 15% off with a direct cause tie is exactly the kind of thing I click. Specific, meaningful, not just a discount for discount's sake.
- **The Eddie Glaude hero** — a serious, intellectually credible author with a culturally relevant book. This feels curated, not algorithmic. The dark background and bold red type give it real editorial weight.
- **Search bar copy** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" — that ISBN detail tells me this is built by and for actual book people.

## What's weak

- **The cookie consent modal** — it lands immediately over the hero, blocking most of the page and any primary CTA that lives there. I can't tell if there's a "Shop Now" button or a price or anything actionable below the fold because this modal ate it.
- **No loyalty hooks visible** — Bookshop.org has a wishlist and shop-affiliate feature. None of that is surfaced here. A returning user like me gets zero acknowledgment.
- **The 20% OFF badge** — it's wedged on the left edge with an X next to it, styled like a floating coupon tag. It's unclear what it applies to or how to use it. Looks like a dismissible promo sticker that landed awkwardly.
- **No navigation categories visible** — there's a hamburger menu but zero category shortcuts. If I wanted to browse Fiction or Staff Picks I'm already one extra tap deep.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 3 seconds or trigger it on scroll** — let me actually see the homepage before hitting me with legal paperwork. This is the biggest quick fix available.
- **Add a "Staff Picks" or "Indie Bestsellers" shortcut strip** directly under the search bar — one-tap category access for the book categories I actually browse.
- **Convert the mission counter into a personalized hook** — e.g., "Your bookshop has earned $X with your purchases" for logged-in users. That turns a brand stat into a personal one.
- **Give the 20% OFF badge a home** — either integrate it clearly into the hero copy ("This book: 20% off today") or remove the floating sticker. Right now it reads as a UI accident.
