# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- Bookshop.org is doing something I genuinely love before I even scroll — that "$48,847,062.62 Raised For Local Bookstores" counter is the entire pitch for someone like me, and they put it right below the logo. The hero is a new literary fiction title from the author of *Station Eleven*, which is exactly the kind of book I follow. Unfortunately, a privacy-policy modal is eating the bottom half of the page and there's no clear buy-button visible anywhere above the fold.

## What's working

- **The mission counter** — "$48,847,062.62 Raised For Local Bookstores" right below the logo is the single most persuasive element on this page for me personally. I track this stuff.
- **Top-bar manifesto** — "Every order financially supports local bookstores!" as the first thing I read sets the right tone immediately.
- **Hero book choice** — *Exit Party* by the author of *Station Eleven* is a credible literary pick that earns my attention. Not a generic bestseller list filler.
- **Search bar prominence** — front and center, labeled "Search books, authors, ISBNs" — that's my path in and it's easy to find.
- **"20% OFF" floating tab** — even partially cropped, it signals there is an active deal.

## What's weak

- **The privacy modal is blocking the page** — I can see the hero headline but not the CTA, not the cover fully, not any below-fold content. This is the worst possible first impression on mobile.
- **No CTA button visible** — the hero has great copy ("WHO WILL YOU BE AT THE END OF THE WORLD?") but nowhere for me to *go*. I have to dismiss a modal just to see the shop button.
- **The 20% OFF tab is half off-screen** — it's cropped at the left edge. I can't read what it applies to, whether it's for members, or how to use it.
- **No loyalty hook** — Bookshop.org's supporter/affiliate model is a huge differentiator, but I see zero mention of it in the visible area.
- **No navigation categories visible** — the hamburger menu is there but collapsed. I can't see Fiction, Nonfiction, Staff Picks, etc. without an extra tap.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the privacy modal by 10 seconds or move it to a slide-up drawer** — don't fire it on page load and immediately cover the hero. I haven't even seen the page yet.
- **Add a "Shop this book" or "Add to cart" button inside the hero** — the headline hook is strong but leaves me nowhere to go. A single purple button with the price + "Add to bag" is all it takes.
- **Surface the supporter/affiliate link CTA above the fold** — something like "Shop for your local store, get 10% back" would resonate deeply with me and differentiates from Amazon immediately.
- **Pull the 20% OFF tab content into a banner strip** — make it readable: "20% off all orders today" or "Members save 20% — join free" so I know what it is before I tap it.
