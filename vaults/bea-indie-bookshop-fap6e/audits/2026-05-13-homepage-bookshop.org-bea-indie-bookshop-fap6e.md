# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is my people. Bookshop.org opens with a stat that's basically my entire argument at family dinners — $47.4 million raised for local bookstores — and follows it with a Indie Next-badged recommendation for what sounds like a deeply weird, smart novel about a cat in a Schrödinger thought experiment. That's catnip (sorry). The brand communicates indie bookseller solidarity in every design choice. Only thing actively working against me is a cookie consent modal eating half the hero on my first load.

## What's working

- **The $47,408,700.61 raised for local bookstores counter** — this is the entire reason I'm here instead of Amazon. Putting the mission stat above the fold, in large purple type, is exactly right for someone like me. It's not buried in a footer "About" link.
- **AAPI Heritage Month 15% off banner** — culturally aware, specific discount percentage, links to a curated list. That trifecta earns trust fast.
- **"Indie Next Recommends" bookseller badge on the hero** — this signals curation by actual booksellers, not an algorithm. I trust this more than any star rating.
- **The book description** — "A Contemporary Dystopian Elegy Narrated by a Cat Imprisoned in a Schrödinger's Box" is doing so much heavy lifting. That's targeted literary copy, not genre-slop. It speaks fluent millennial-reader.
- **Search bar placeholder copy** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" — ISBNs! They know their audience.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks the hero on load** — on a 390px mobile viewport this modal eats almost the entire hero. I have to interact with it before I can even see what's being featured. This is the first thing I see and it's friction.
- **No clear "Shop Now" CTA button** — the 20% OFF badge floats on the left edge but there's no button to tap. The hero book presumably links somewhere but it's not signposted.
- **20% OFF badge placement** — partially obscured by the cookie modal, clipped at the left edge. I couldn't tell what it's tied to without the modal gone.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — at $30/month spend this is a real decision factor for me. I'd want to know immediately.
- **No loyalty/account benefit hook** — I have an account. Show me something that rewards it.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink or bottom-anchor the cookie banner** — a slim bottom bar with the same "Accept / Deny Non-Essential" options would let the hero breathe. The current full-panel takeover buries the product content I came to see.
- **Add a sticky free-shipping threshold line** — something like "Free shipping on orders $35+" near the search bar or below the mission stat converts casual browsers like me who are already close to threshold.
- **Turn the 20% OFF badge into a labeled CTA button** — "20% off this pick — Shop Cat Love" or similar. Make it tappable and unambiguous instead of a floating badge.
- **Surface a "Your Reading List" or recent-picks nudge for logged-in users** — even a small "Welcome back, Bea" hook above the hero would increase my engagement and reduce bounce for return visitors.
