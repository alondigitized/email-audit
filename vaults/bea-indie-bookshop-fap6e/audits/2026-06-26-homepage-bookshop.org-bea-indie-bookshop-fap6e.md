# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- This is exactly the homepage I want to see from Bookshop.org right now — "Anti-Prime Day" is a perfect values-play for someone who actively routes their book money away from Amazon. It reads like a brand that knows who it is. The mission counter ($48.5M raised for local bookstores) is right there above the hero, which is genuinely meaningful to me. The only thing killing this opening moment is the cookie consent modal eating half the screen.

## What's working

- **"anti-prime day" hero** — Naming it that is a political statement and a rallying cry. For someone who thinks about where their dollars go, this headline does more work than any discount ever could.
- **$48,501,557.57 Raised For Local Bookstores counter** — Specific to the penny. Not "we support indie bookstores," but a live dollar figure. I stopped and read that number.
- **"Ends Soon: Free Standard Shipping" top banner** — Clear, first thing I see, directly actionable.
- **June 23–26 date strip in the hero** — Gives the event a bounded window, adds credibility that this is a real campaign not a perma-promo.
- **20% OFF badge** — Even partially cut off, I can see a real number. That's more persuasive than "save big."

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the fold** — The modal covers the bottom third of my screen right at the point where I'd see products, categories, or a shop CTA. I can't see what I'm supposed to buy. This is the homepage's biggest problem.
- **No visible shop/browse CTA button** — There's no "Shop Anti-Prime Day" button or equivalent visible above the fold. The hero announces a campaign but gives me nowhere to go.
- **Hamburger-only nav** — For a book-only shopper, I want to jump to Fiction, Nonfiction, Staff Picks, etc. in one tap. The nav is collapsed behind a hamburger; nothing is scannable.
- **No product imagery** — The hero is illustrated/decorative books, not actual book covers. I can't see what's on sale, which means I have no product pull yet.
- **20% OFF badge is partially clipped** — The floating tab is cut off by the left edge, making it feel broken rather than intentional.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default on return visitors**, or at minimum make the modal far smaller — a slim bottom bar instead of a full modal. Blocking the fold for a returning customer is a conversion killer.
- **Add a "Shop the Sale" or "Browse Anti-Prime Day Deals" button** directly in the hero, full-width, below the "anti-prime day" headline. Right now the campaign announces itself but doesn't give me a door to walk through.
- **Pin 3–4 category pills** (Fiction, Nonfiction, Gifts, Staff Picks) directly below the search bar so I can reach my corner in one tap without opening the hamburger.
- **Replace the illustrated book silhouettes** in the hero background with 4–6 real book cover thumbnails from the Anti-Prime Day sale. Give me something to click on immediately.
