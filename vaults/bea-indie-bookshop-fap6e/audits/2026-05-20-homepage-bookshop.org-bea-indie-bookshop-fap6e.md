# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is immediately recognizable as my people — a bookstore ecosystem that wears its indie mission on its sleeve. That "$47,565,367.68 Raised For Local Bookstores" counter hits me in the chest before I've even processed the hero. Right now it's pushing a José Andrés cookbook, which is a book, fine, but it's food-adjacent lifestyle content that feels more like a gift-table pick than a recommendation for me specifically.

## What's working

- **Mission counter ("$47,565,367.68 Raised For Local Bookstores")** — this is my whole reason for being on this site instead of Amazon, and it's the third thing I see on the page. Whoever put this above the fold deserves a raise.
- **Top banner promo** — "15% off these select books" for Mental Health Awareness Month is specific, timely, and tied to a real cultural moment in May. I'd tap it.
- **Search bar** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" is exactly the right framing. Not "Search our store," not "Find your next read." ISBNs. These people know their customer.
- **20% OFF badge on the hero** — visible price reduction on a featured title, no ambiguity about whether a deal exists.
- **Cookie consent is clean** — two clear options ("Accept" / "Deny Non-Essential"), no dark-pattern pre-checked boxes, no fake X button.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks the hero CTA** — the "Accept / Deny Non-Essential" overlay is sitting directly over what I assume is a "Buy" or "Shop" button for the José Andrés book. I can't convert without dismissing it first.
- **Hero is a cookbook** — "Spain My Way" is a perfectly good book, but it skews food-gift rather than literary. For someone who keeps a reading list, this doesn't feel like it was surfaced for me.
- **No loyalty/earnings signal above the fold** — I care that my purchase routes a cut to my local shop. The big counter shows total impact, but I'd love to see something like "X% goes to your local store" or a bookshop affiliate hook visible without scrolling.
- **No new-arrivals or editorial rail visible** — above the fold there's one book. Just one. I want more to browse.
- **No trust signal for returns or shipping** — $30/month buyer here; I'd want to see free-shipping threshold before I tap add-to-cart.

## Recommendations

- **Bring the local-store earnings hook closer to the CTA** — under the hero book, add one line: "Supporting [your bookseller] — they earn X% of this sale." That's the differentiator that makes me choose Bookshop.org every time.
- **Swap the hero for a literary/fiction title** — a cookbook is fine, but a buzzy debut novel or a staff-pick literary title would speak more directly to the core reader demographic. Save cookbooks for the gift-guide season.
- **Add a visible "new arrivals this week" chip or badge** — even one small "New This Week" label on a book cover gives me a reason to scroll immediately.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold or a flat shipping line** — even "$x ships free" in the top banner alongside the 15% off would reduce friction at the conversion stage.
