# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is home. Bookshop.org leads with the earnings counter — $48,694,805.09 raised for local bookstores — which is the entire reason I use this site instead of Amazon, and seeing that number before anything else validates my whole decision to be here. The featured book is a literary novel by Andrew Sean Greer, the Pulitzer winner who wrote *Less*, which is a credible pick. The one friction point I can't ignore: a cookie consent modal is sitting on top of the hero and eating whatever buy button lives under it.

## What's working

- **The earnings counter**: "$48,694,805.09 Raised For Local Bookstores" is the most effective trust signal on any retail homepage I've seen for my use case. I check that number. It's why I'm here.
- **Top banner copy**: "Every order financially supports local bookstores!" leads with mission before product — correct order of operations for their audience.
- **Featured book selection**: Andrew Sean Greer, author of *Less*, with a Boston Globe pull quote ("A BAWDY, SUN-SOAKED ROMP about finding your true self at any age.") — this reads like a staff picks card at an actual bookstore. It sounds like a recommendation, not ad copy.
- **Search bar labeling**: "Search books, authors, ISBNs" — explicit inclusion of ISBN matters to me. I regularly see something at my local shop and want to route the purchase through Bookshop to credit them. This confirms I can do that.
- **Cookie consent design**: "Accept" and "Deny Non-Essential" are equal-weight purple buttons. No buried opt-out, no gray text trick. That's rare and I notice it.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal swallows the hero CTA**: Whatever button would let me buy or preview Villa Coco is completely hidden. My first required tap is a privacy dialog, not a book.
- **No price or shipping info anywhere**: I spend about $30/month here. I have no idea if Villa Coco is $18 or $28, and I don't know if I'm close to a free-shipping threshold. That matters for whether I add a second title.
- **No account hook for returning shoppers**: I've bought here before. There's zero acknowledgment — no welcome back, no "your bookshop earned $X," no shelf of what I've recently looked at. The earnings counter is their moat and they're not using it at the user level.
- **No visible category navigation**: Just a hamburger. If I'm not interested in Villa Coco specifically, I have to tap into a menu to find Literary Fiction, Staff Picks, Award Winners — there's no visible shortcut rail.

## Recommendations

- **Don't let the cookie modal cover the hero CTA** — either auto-dismiss on scroll, reduce its height, or make it a slide-up banner that doesn't overlap the action area. The first tap should be on a book.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** directly below the earnings counter: something like "Free shipping on orders over $X." At my spend level, that's the deciding factor between one book and two.
- **Surface a personalized earnings stat for logged-in users**: "Your orders have helped earn $847 for [bookshop name]" — it's the conversion hook nobody else can offer and Bookshop.org already has the data.
- **Add a horizontal chip nav rail** between the search bar and the hero: "Staff Picks · New Releases · Award Winners · Indie Bestsellers." One row, no tap required, immediate path to my category if Villa Coco isn't what I came for.
