# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably bookshop.org — the indie-bookstore ethos comes through immediately in the raised-funds counter and the community feel. Right now it's pushing a Beth Stern picture book about a kitten and a bunny, which is sweet but does exactly nothing for me personally, and the whole first screen is being strangled by a cookie consent modal that blots out half the hero. The bones are good; the execution is messy.

## What's working

- **The raised-funds counter** — "$47,541,460.73 Raised For Local Bookstores" is the single most powerful thing on this page for me. It's the whole reason I use bookshop.org instead of Amazon. I clock that number every time.
- **The top promo banner** — "15% off these select books" for Mental Health Awareness Month is topical and relevant. Browsing for gift ideas for a friend? This pulls me in.
- **The search bar** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" is exactly how I use this site. One tap and I'm hunting for a specific title. Clear, functional, no fluff.
- **The 20% OFF badge** — visible without scrolling even through the modal chaos. I notice it.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is a full-screen hijack** — "Accept" and "Deny Non-Essential" eat the bottom third of the viewport and bury the hero. First action I'm being asked to take on a site I trust is a compliance wall. This is friction I did not ask for.
- **The featured book is a children's pet picture book** — Beth Stern's "Coco and Stephen, Together Forever" is a perfectly fine book, but it has zero overlap with my reading life. The homepage hero should be earning my attention, not spending it on a cover-illustrated bunny.
- **No primary shopping CTA** — there is no "Shop Now," "Browse the Sale," or even a tappable button on the hero that moves me toward a book I might buy. The only buttons on screen are cookie consent buttons.
- **No category navigation visible** — the hamburger menu is there, but nothing is expanded. I can't see Fiction, Nonfiction, Staff Picks, etc. without a tap I'm not sure I want to take yet.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal to background** — use a slim bottom bar instead of a blocking overlay. Every second that modal lives center-screen is a second I'm not looking at books.
- **Add a CTA button to the hero** — "Read the story" or "Shop 20% off" directly on the featured book. Right now the hero is decorative, not functional.
- **Swap the hero for something in adult literary fiction or current non-fiction** — or at minimum run A/B targeting based on past browse history. I am not the Beth Stern picture-book audience and the homepage should know that by now.
- **Bring the raised-funds counter up and make it interactive** — show me my personal contribution total next to the site-wide number. That's the loyalty hook this site is uniquely positioned to offer and it's buried in small type.
