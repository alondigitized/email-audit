# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is 100% my kind of site — a book-first mission-driven alternative to Amazon with a live counter showing $47.5M raised for local bookstores. That number alone makes me feel like my purchases matter. Right now though, the homepage is pushing a "Summer Reading Adventure for All Ages" hero that's clearly aimed at parents buying for kids, and a cookie consent modal has hijacked the entire bottom half of my screen before I can see anything else.

## What's working

- **The $47,501,988.05 "Raised For Local Bookstores" counter** — this is the single most compelling thing on the page for me. I track my shop's earnings percentage; seeing the running total makes the mission tangible and reminds me exactly why I use Bookshop over Amazon.
- **The 15% off Mental Health Awareness Month banner** — books and mental health overlap a lot in my reading life. This feels earned and specific, not a manufactured "flash sale."
- **The "20% OFF" badge** visible on the left side — a stacked discount signal reinforces that there's real value here right now.
- **Search bar prominently placed** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" is exactly how I shop. I know what I want; I don't need to browse categories.
- **Clean, recognizable brand** — the Bookshop.org logo and "B" mark are immediately trustworthy to me.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is swallowing the hero** — the "This website utilizes technologies..." overlay is covering at least the bottom 40% of the visible viewport. I can't see the hero CTA, any navigation categories, or whatever is below the fold. This is the first thing I see and it's friction.
- **"Summer Reading Adventure for All Ages" hero is aimed at parents, not me** — every visible book cover is a children's title (Chicka Chicka Boom Boom, Dog Man, Mindy Kim, Jasper Rabbit's). I read literary fiction and essays; this push doesn't speak to me.
- **No adult fiction signal anywhere above the fold** — not a single cover, headline, or nav link that points toward "Literary Fiction" or "New Releases" for adult readers.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I'm ordering ~$30/month. Knowing whether I hit free shipping matters before I start shopping.
- **No returning-visitor hook** — no "welcome back," no cart remnant, no "you had items saved" — nothing to pull me back into a prior browsing session.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or rotate the hero to adult-reader seasonal content** — a "Summer Reads for Adults" or "Staff Picks: May 2026" hero would keep the seasonal energy but land for the 25–45 literary crowd that is Bookshop's core demographic. Kids' content can live one tap into a "For Young Readers" tab.
- **Move the cookie modal to a slim bottom banner** instead of a full-bleed overlay — let me see the homepage first; I'll deal with cookies on a bottom strip like a grownup.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top bar** alongside the Mental Health Month offer — something like "Free shipping on orders over $35" next to the 15% off message tells me within seconds whether my usual $30 haul qualifies.
- **Add a "Your Local Store Earned:" micro-hook** near the $47M counter — even a "Shops in [city] have earned $X this month" would make the mission feel personal rather than aggregate, which is exactly the kind of thing that keeps me loyal.
