# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Bookshop.org — mission-first, indie-bookstore-supporting energy right out of the gate with that fundraising ticker front and center. Right now they're pushing a "Summer Reading Adventure" campaign that skews hard toward children's books, which isn't really where I live as a reader. The cookie modal eating half the hero doesn't help first impressions.

## What's working

- **The fundraising counter** — "$47,516,150.36 Raised For Local Bookstores" is the first thing my eye hits below the logo. That number is the whole reason I'm here instead of Amazon, so leading with it is exactly right for me.
- **Mental Health Awareness promo banner** — "15% off these select books" at the very top is a clean, specific offer. Mental health reads are in my wheelhouse and it signals the store is paying attention to cultural moments, not just seasonal sales.
- **Search bar placement** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" is prominent and immediately usable. I always come in with a specific title or author in mind, so this is the nav I actually need.
- **Honest cookie modal** — "Deny Non-Essential" is right there at the same visual weight as "Accept." That's respectful and very on-brand for a values-led retailer.

## What's weak

- **Children's books hero for an all-ages audience** — "FOR ALL AGES!" is in the headline but every single book cover shown is for kids aged 4–12. That's not all ages, that's parents and teachers. I bounced mentally as soon as I saw Dog Man.
- **"20% OFF" badge with no context** — a red vertical badge on the left edge with no anchor (20% off what? this campaign? a category?) feels like a leftover design asset, not a real offer I can act on.
- **No adult fiction/lit signal anywhere in viewport** — not one cover, not one category chip, nothing that tells me my kind of books are here. I know they are, but the page isn't showing me.
- **Cookie modal blocking content** — the modal covers the lower half of the hero at load, so whatever CTA or secondary content was there is invisible. That's friction before I've done anything.
- **No earning/impact drill-down** — the fundraising counter doesn't show me my bookshop's slice. I track my shop's percentage and there's no prompt to find it here.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to a "Staff Picks for Summer" adult reading banner** — keep the summer seasonal angle but show literary fiction, essay collections, or debut novels alongside the kids' titles. A simple carousel toggle ("Adults / Kids") would serve both audiences without alienating me.
- **Add a "Your bookshop earned $X this month" micro-hook** under the counter for logged-in users — that one number is why power users like me stay loyal; surfacing it above the fold turns a passive stat into a sticky retention moment.
- **Give the 20% OFF badge a clear label** — "20% off Summer Reads" with a tap target is actionable; a floating badge with no copy is just visual noise.
- **Collapse or delay the cookie modal** — let me see the hero for 2–3 seconds before the consent wall appears; or anchor it to the bottom of the screen so it doesn't eat the primary content.
