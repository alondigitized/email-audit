# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Bookshop.org is doing almost everything right for someone like me — a books-first platform that leads with its mission ($47M raised for local stores) and opens with an Indie Next editorial pick rather than a commercial shill. The AAPI Heritage Month banner is timely and concrete. What's killing me right now is a full-screen cookie consent modal eating half my viewport, which means the first interaction I'm having with this homepage is a bureaucratic prompt, not a book.

## What's working

- **"$47,435,821.35 Raised For Local Bookstores"** — I literally check this number. It's the whole reason I use Bookshop.org over Amazon. Putting it directly below the logo is the right call.
- **AAPI Heritage Month 15% off banner** — timely, culturally specific, and concrete. "These select books" with a link is transparent about scope. I'd tap it immediately.
- **"Indie Next / Booksellers Recommend" badge** — human curation from actual booksellers is worth ten algorithmic recommendation engines to me. This badge does serious trust work.
- **The hero copy itself** — "A Contemporary Dystopian Elegy Narrated by a Cat Imprisoned in a Schrödinger's Box" is wonderfully weird. That's editorial voice, not marketing voice. I feel seen.
- **Search bar with "books, authors, ISBNs"** — specificity of "ISBNs" tells me this is built for people who actually know what they're looking for.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal is blocking the hero** — a dark overlay with "Accept" / "Deny Non-Essential" is the first interaction on this page. It's blocking roughly 40% of above-the-fold content. I can't see nav, product links, or anything below the book title.
- **No primary shopping CTA button** — the hero shows the book but there's no "Shop This Pick" or "See More" button. I have to guess the cover is tappable.
- **No loyalty or personalization hook** — the $47M stat is great for the mission, but there's no "your shop has earned $X" or sign-in prompt to make it feel personal to me specifically.
- **No second shelf or new-arrivals visible** — the modal cuts off everything below the hero, so there's exactly one browsable item above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Move cookie consent to a bottom banner strip**, not a full-screen overlay. I shouldn't have to accept a privacy modal before I can engage with any product content.
- **Add a "Shop This Pick" CTA button directly on the hero** with the book title and author — don't make me guess it's tappable.
- **Personalize the earnings ticker for logged-in users** — swap "$47,435,821.35 total" for "You've helped [My Local Shop] earn $X" once I'm signed in. That's a retention hook I'd notice every visit.
- **Surface a second Indie Next or new-arrivals shelf immediately below the hero** — one featured book is a tease, not a browsing experience.
