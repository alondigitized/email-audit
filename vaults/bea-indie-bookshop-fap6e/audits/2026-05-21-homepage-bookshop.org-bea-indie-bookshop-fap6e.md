# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Bookshop.org knows exactly who it is — an indie-forward alternative to Amazon that leads with its mission dollars front and center. Right now it's pushing a cookbook by José Andrés, which tells me they're leaning into food-and-culture as a crossover category. The cookie consent modal landing on top of the hero is the first thing I see, and it's a little annoying, but honestly, the bones underneath feel like home.

## What's working

- **$47,590,004.73 Raised For Local Bookstores banner** — this is the whole reason I use bookshop.org instead of Amazon. Seeing a live dollar figure immediately validates my choice and speaks directly to why I track my shop's earnings percentage. This is the single best element on the page for me.
- **15% off Mental Health Awareness Month banner** — a cause-aligned discount tied to a real cultural moment in May. Feels earned, not manufactured.
- **"20% OFF" badge on the featured book** — a concrete deal on a specific title, visible without hunting.
- **Search bar front and center** — I know what I want; a prominent ISBNs search bar respects that.
- **José Andrés hero copy** — "stories of the people and places behind them" is literary framing on a cookbook, which is smart. It appeals to readers, not just home cooks.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the hero** — it lands directly over the featured book and its CTA, so the first interactive decision I'm forced to make has nothing to do with buying a book. Friction before I've even seen the product.
- **No clear "Shop" or "Add to Cart" CTA above the fold** — the hero describes the book beautifully but gives me nowhere obvious to tap. I shouldn't have to hunt.
- **No loyalty/account hook** — I'm a repeat customer. My indie shop earns a cut every time I buy here, and I track that. There's no "your bookstore has earned $X" or returning-user welcome that reinforces that relationship.
- **Single book featured in hero** — it's a cookbook. For a books-only shopper, that's fine, but it narrows appeal immediately. One featured title means anyone not interested in Spanish food bounces without a second hook.
- **No new-arrivals or category rail above the fold** — beyond the one hero book, I have zero visibility into what else is new or on sale in fiction, nonfiction, etc.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the "your bookstore earned X" stat personally** — if I'm logged in, show me my shop's cumulative earnings right in the header or hero strip. That single number is my reason to be here; it should be unmissable.
- **Move cookie consent to a bottom banner or slide-in** — don't make it a full-overlay modal that hides the hero. A slim bottom bar lets me see the product while still capturing consent.
- **Add a tappable CTA button directly on the hero** — something like `Shop Spain My Way` or `Get 20% Off` placed under the book description so the discount has a clear action path.
- **Add a 3–4 tile category strip below the hero** — Fiction / Nonfiction / New Releases / Staff Picks — so users who aren't interested in the hero book have an immediate next step without scrolling far.
