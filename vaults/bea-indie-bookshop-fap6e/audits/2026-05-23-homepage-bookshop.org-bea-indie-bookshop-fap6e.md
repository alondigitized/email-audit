# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This feels instantly like my people — the $47.6M raised for local bookstores is front and center and that number hits different when you know what it means. Right now the site is pushing a José Andrés cookbook as the hero, which is a slightly odd pivot for someone who comes here specifically to shop fiction and literary titles. The cookie modal eating the bottom half of the screen is annoying but at least the mission is unmissable.

## What's working

- **The $47,635,492.17 raised ticker** — that live dollar amount with "Raised For Local Bookstores" is the single most on-brand thing on this page for me. I check this number. It's my passive loyalty metric.
- **15% off Mental Health Awareness Month books banner** — topical, relevant to a literary reader, and I'd actually click it.
- **Search bar specificity** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" tells me instantly this is a books-only destination. No lifestyle clutter.
- **20% OFF badge** — visible and specific on the hero, not vague.
- **The bookshop.org wordmark** — clean, mission-forward, no Amazon vibes.

## What's weak

- **José Andrés cookbook as the hero** — I buy literary fiction and indie press titles. A celebrity chef cookbook makes me feel like I've wandered into a gift shop, not a bookstore for readers.
- **Cookie modal blocking the hero CTA** — whatever button exists below "the stories of the people and places behind them" is buried under an Accept/Deny overlay. I can't tap through to the book without dismissing first.
- **No browse-by-genre nav above the fold** — I have to hit a hamburger menu to find Fiction, Essays, Poetry. That's a tap tax.
- **No staff picks or editorial voice visible** — this is the whole reason indie bookstores exist. If I wanted an algorithm I'd use Goodreads.
- **No free-shipping threshold** — I spend ~$30/month; I want to know if I qualify.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the cookbook hero for a "Books We Love This Month" editorial rail** — lead with the indie-curation angle, not a celebrity tie-in. Even a "staff pick of the week" carousel would read more authentically Bookshop.
- **Add "Your bookstore has earned $X" personalization** — if I'm signed in, show my linked shop's running total, not just the platform total. That would lock me in every single visit.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — a small "Free shipping on orders over $X" line in the header costs nothing and removes a conversion blocker for $30/month shoppers.
- **Pre-dismiss the cookie banner or move it to a non-blocking bottom bar** — the current full-width overlay hides the hero CTA and makes the above-the-fold experience feel broken on mobile.
