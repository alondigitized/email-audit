# Homepage snapshot · bookshop.org · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is my home turf — a bookstore that funnels money back to indie shops, which is literally the reason I have an account here. The first thing I see is a Pride Month discount banner and a Dr. Seuss hero, which tells me June is the current campaign but they're leading with children's books. The $47.9M raised for local bookstores counter hits me right in the chest, but a cookie-consent modal is eating the bottom third of my screen before I can actually do anything.

## What's working

- **$47,949,892.72 Raised For Local Bookstores** — this is the single most persuasive thing on the page for someone like me. It's prominent, specific to the dollar, and hits the core value prop I care about. Tracking that number is basically why I'm here instead of Amazon.
- **Pride Month 15% off banner** — timely, values-signaling, books-specific. If the linked selection is adult literary fiction and LGBTQ+ titles, that's directly in my wheelhouse.
- **20% OFF badge** on the hero — clear, can't miss it, no fine print in sight.
- **Search bar front and center** — "Search books, authors, ISBNs" is exactly the right copy for a high-intent book buyer. I know what I'm doing when I get here.

## What's weak

- **Dr. Seuss hero** — "Look! Look! A Never-Before-Seen Dr. Seuss Book!" is a children's release. For a 33-year-old indie reader, this is a complete miss. It feels like I wandered into the kids' section.
- **Cookie consent modal** — it's blocking the lower third of the hero and any below-the-fold CTAs. I can't see what button or product link lives beneath it. The two giant "Accept" / "Deny Non-Essential" buttons are the most prominent interactive elements on screen right now, which is the wrong hierarchy.
- **No navigation categories visible** — just a hamburger menu. I can't see a "Fiction," "New Releases," or "Literary" shortcut without tapping through a menu. One extra tap is friction I shouldn't have to deal with.
- **No social proof on the hero** — no star ratings, no "bestseller" badge, nothing to validate the Dr. Seuss pick for anyone who isn't already a fan.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the Dr. Seuss children's hero for a curated adult literary rail** — lead with "New in Fiction" or "Staff Picks" to capture readers who aren't shopping for kids. The site skews literary; the hero should too.
- **Dismiss or auto-minimize the cookie modal** — or at minimum make it a slim bottom-sheet so the hero CTA is never hidden. Right now it's the first interaction I have to make.
- **Surface one category shortcut strip below the search bar** — Fiction | Nonfiction | Staff Picks | New Arrivals — so I can reach my category in one tap without hunting through the hamburger.
- **Pin the local-bookstore earnings counter closer to the primary CTA** — it's the emotional hook that separates bookshop.org from everyone else; it should be adjacent to the "Shop Now" or "Browse Books" button, not floating above the hero on its own.
