# GASP 😮 $8 tees + take an EXTRA 30% off your purchase*
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy running the identical "$8 Tees + Extra 30% Off" creative for at least the third time in three days — the pink-striped tee/bike-shorts hero, the "2 DAYS ONLY!" badge, and the jeans-on-sale banner are the same assets as the 2026-08-01 "Ooooh! 👀$8 tees" and 2026-08-02 "SCORE—$8 tees" sends, just with a new subject-line hook ("GASP 😮"). Execution quality is genuinely strong: clean hierarchy, real product photography, star-rated reviews with fit quotes, and specific "SHOP [PRODUCT]" CTAs rather than generic "Discover" buttons.
- The problem isn't the render, it's the repetition. A subscriber who's opened the last two sends has now seen "2 Days Only" claimed on day three — that urgency line no longer reads as credible, it reads as copy-paste. The tee/leggings/clearance stacking order is also unchanged from prior audits, so the email is well-built but not well-differentiated from what already sits unopened in this inbox.
- Judgement: strong craft, weak cadence. This would score higher in isolation than it does with the brand history in view.

## What's working

- Clear top-of-funnel hierarchy: jeans banner → "2 DAYS ONLY! Tees $6/$8" hero → color swatch grid, all above the fold.
- Reviews section uses real 5-star ratings and specific fit quotes ("thin enough to tuck in," "extremely flattering fit") — genuine social proof, not filler.
- CTAs are product-specific ("SHOP EVERYWEAR TEE," "SHOP SNUG CROP TEE") instead of vague "Shop Now" links.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Preview text ("Encore Members get free shipping on $50+ orders") adds a genuinely new offer rather than restating the subject.

## What's weak

- This is the same hero creative and "2 Days Only" urgency claim as the prior two sends — the deadline is no longer credible on a third viewing.
- Subject line ("GASP 😮") is pure curiosity filler with no new information versus "Ooooh! 👀" from the day before — the brand is cycling reaction-word hooks on the same offer.
- Asterisk on "purchase*" signals fine print/exclusions that aren't resolved anywhere visible in the render.
- Email is long and repeats the "shop tees" beat three separate times (hero, reviews x3, then leggings, then clearance) before ever changing category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing "2 Days Only" across consecutive sends — if the promo is actually still running, say so plainly ("Last chance today") rather than restating a deadline that already passed once.
- 2. Vary the hero creative/model shot from the 08-01 and 08-02 sends — same pink-stripe tee art three days running trains subscribers to skip without opening.
- 3. Move the free-shipping/Encore line into the subject or keep it distinct in preview (it currently only lives in preview) since it's the one genuinely new lever versus prior sends.
- 4. Resolve or drop the asterisk — either state the exclusion inline near the offer or remove the asterisk if there isn't one.
