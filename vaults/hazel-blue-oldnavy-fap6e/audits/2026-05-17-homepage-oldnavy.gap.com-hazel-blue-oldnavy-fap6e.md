# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I opened this and immediately saw "50% Off Everything" in giant white letters on a navy background — that's my language right there. Old Navy is clearly running a big online exclusive sale event right now, and they've stacked it with a Disney collab drop called "That Summer Feeling" featuring a whole family in their gear. This feels like peak Old Navy: loud deal, seasonal energy, and just enough family vibes to make me feel seen.

## What's working

- **"50% Off Everything, Ends 5/20"** — three days of urgency on a sitewide deal is exactly what makes me drop everything and open the app. The countdown-adjacent deadline is real motivation.
- **Encore Members free shipping at $50+** — I'm a Navyist member and that banner catches me immediately. It's the right hook for returning shoppers like me.
- **The Disney x Old Navy family image** — a mom, dad, and little kid in coordinated Old Navy + Disney gear holding balloons? That's my Saturday. The lifestyle visual is warm and I immediately picture my kids in it.
- **Visual hierarchy on the hero** — bold white text on dark navy, no clutter. I know exactly what the page is telling me within one second of opening it.
- **"Online Exclusive" label** — I appreciate knowing upfront this deal doesn't apply in store. Saves me a trip.

## What's weak

- **"Exclusions apply." under "50% Off Everything"** — this is the thing that makes me pause every time. When it says *everything* and then says exclusions, I immediately think: is my kids' stuff in the exclusion list? That hesitation could cost them a tap.
- **No category nav visible** — I can see a hamburger menu and search icon but nothing to get me to Boys, Girls, or Basics in one tap. I have to dig.
- **No reviews, ratings, or product detail visible** — the hero and second module are both campaign-level; there's no product I can actually evaluate from what I see here.
- **Encore Members free shipping is gated** — if I'm not signed in, that top banner is more of a nag than a perk. The shipping threshold should show even for guests.
- **Disney collab image feels aspirational, not utility** — the lifestyle scene is cute but it's telling me vibes, not showing me the $8 uniform polos I actually came to buy.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sub-headline under the hero CTA** that reads something like "Kids, Adults & Basics included" — one line of reassurance would defuse the "exclusions apply" skepticism and keep sale-hunters from bouncing.
- **Replace the hamburger-only nav with 4 visible category chips** below the hero (Girls, Boys, Women, Men) — at medium tech comfort I shouldn't have to hunt; one-tap category access increases my chances of converting dramatically.
- **Pull a "Shop the Look" grid into the Disney collab module** — show the individual pieces with prices right below the lifestyle photo so I can tap straight into the kids' section without an extra step.
- **Make the loyalty banner smarter**: if I'm signed in, show my actual rewards balance instead of "Sign In or Join Now." That personal number ("You have $25 Super Cash") is a much stronger hook than a generic invite.
