# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-30
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is doing that thing where it's clearly trying to look cool and athletic — which, respect, but I came here for clearance rack kids' uniforms and everyday basics, not a music video. The deal banners at the top are genuinely solid and I appreciate that the 20% off includes clearance, because that's where I live. But the hero? That model looks like she's heading to Coachella, not a school drop-off line.

## What's working

- **"Extra 20% Off Your Purchase Including Clearance — Ends 5/31"** — the clearance inclusion is the magic word for me, and the hard deadline makes it feel real
- **"Free shipping on orders $50+ for Encore Members"** — exactly the kind of member benefit that makes me want to stay logged in; $50 is easy to hit with two kids' worth of basics
- **"New Arrivals from $10"** — even if the image is off, that price anchor immediately signals this is my kind of store
- **Blue urgency banner** — the Online Exclusive + Ends 5/31 combo reads as a legitimate limited window, not fake scarcity

## What's weak

- **Hero model and imagery** — she's young, athletic, and wearing a crop top and bike shorts. That's not who I am or what I'm shopping for. I want to see a real family, a mom loading a cart, or at minimum some kids' basics in the frame
- **"American Summer Happy Day!" badge** — this sticker-style treatment feels Gen Z and a little random; it doesn't connect to anything I'm clicking toward
- **No kids or family category shortcut visible above the fold** — the hamburger menu is there but nothing quick-taps me to Kids, Uniforms, or Women's basics without hunting
- **"Encore Members" naming** — I'm a Navyist Rewards member, and "Encore Members" reads like a Gap umbrella rebrand I wasn't told about; it creates a beat of confusion about whether this applies to my account

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero model for a lifestyle shot** — a mom + kids in coordinating basics, or a flat-lay of uniform essentials at that $10 price point, would land so much harder for the back-to-school / clearance crowd
- **Add a category pill row just below the hero** — "Women's," "Kids," "Uniforms," "Sale" as tappable chips would let me skip the hamburger menu entirely on mobile
- **Clarify the loyalty program name** — either call it "Navyist Rewards" or add a parenthetical so returning members know this free-shipping offer is theirs without second-guessing
- **Make the clearance CTA its own button** — "Shop Clearance" next to "Shop Now" would convert bargain hunters like me on the spot instead of making me navigate after clicking through
