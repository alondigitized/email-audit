# $4 kids uniform polos that pass the dress code AND last the whole school year
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest subject line Old Navy has written in its July BTS streak — "$4 kids uniform polos that pass the dress code AND last the whole school year" actually makes a dual benefit claim, not just a price shout. It's doing real marketing work: price + product + promise in one line. That's a meaningful upgrade from the "$8 pants" repeat they fired twice in a row on July 9 and 10.
- The problem is the email body doesn't deliver on it. The polo hero gets pushed into a crowded scroll that includes a clearance rack, a Low Rise teen denim module, and a Back-to-School Deals block. The through-line — durable, dress-code-ready $4 polos — disappears after the first module. The subject sets up a focused story; the email body reads like a catalog dump.
- Seven consecutive BTS sends in seven days means the "2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency badge has lost all meaning to any real subscriber. Hazel has seen it on the shorts, on the pants twice, and now on the polos. At this point it registers as visual furniture, not a deadline.

## What's working

- Subject line is the best in this streak: price, product, and a concrete durability promise in one line.
- $4 price anchor is prominent in the hero — the eye lands there first.
- Kids uniform imagery in the hero correctly matches the subject's product focus.
- "2 DAYS ONLY!" badge at the top at least signals time-sensitivity, even if fatigued.
- BTS timing in mid-July is appropriate; the polo fits the uniform sequence (shorts → pants → polos).

## What's weak

- The Low Rise teen denim module mid-email completely breaks category focus for someone shopping kids' uniforms.
- Subject runs 78 characters — it will truncate on most mobile clients before reaching the benefit claims.
- "2 DAYS ONLY!" badge has appeared in at least four of the last six sends; urgency is fully depleted.
- The subject promises "last the whole school year" but the email body shows no copy, imagery, or callout that supports that durability claim.
- Daily cadence (seventh BTS email in seven days, two of them identical $8-pants sends) erodes goodwill before the polo offer even lands.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the non-uniform modules.** A kids polo email should be 100% kids. Drop Low Rise denim and clearance clutter; give that space to polo colorways, size guide, or the durability story the subject promised.
- 2. **Back up the "lasts all year" claim in the email body.** If Old Navy is betting on durability as a differentiator, show it — a washability callout, a reinforced-seam badge, anything. Right now the promise floats without support.
- 3. **Trim the subject to under 55 chars.** The current line truncates where the benefit begins.
- 4. **Rest the "2 DAYS ONLY!" badge.** Use it sparingly; the series has burned it completely. A calendar countdown or a "while supplies last" feels fresher.
- 5. **Alternate send cadence.** The back-to-back July 9/10 duplicate (same $8 pants campaign) set the wrong tone; Hazel doesn't need seven emails in seven days to buy polos.

## Technical Audit — Old Navy "Back to School" Uniform Polo Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, VML/MSO conditionals present, responsive breakpoint at 639px), but has one confirmed broken click-redirect, two missing compliance headers, and a plain-text body dominated by URL strings that creates a spam-filter risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken click-redirect — cp.oldnavy.com tracking pixel**

The QA probe on the open/DV tracking pixel returned `unknown url type: '/g/88ef7f99-21ca-4223-bc83-2f334250a2c4?txn=7e7bef80-7d16-11f1-8963-4ec26c495bcf'`. The URL:

```
https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/88ef7f99-21ca-4223-bc83-2f334250a2c4?mi_mid=019f5331-cb00-7000-9ec8-1554b1fad221&cp_tp=v3.eJ...
```

returned a redirect to a relative path (`/g/...`) — not an absolute URL — which is an invalid HTTP redirect response. This will silently fail in email clients that prefetch images. The `mi_mid` and `cp_cid` parameters embedded in the URL suggest this is a Movable Ink or similar dynamic content pixel; the redirect failure means dynamic content or engagement tracking for this send may be broken.

**[INFO] 23 click links deferred**

All CTA links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com` and were skipped from HTTP probing. Destination URL health and UTM parameter integrity cannot be confirmed from this audit.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Outlook 2013+ line-height collapse risk**

The MSO conditional block sets:

```css
<!--[if gte mso 9]>
td { line-height:0px; mso-line-height-rule:exactly }
```

A global `line-height: 0px` on all `td` elements in Outlook 2013+ will collapse any text-bearing cell that doesn't have an inline override. This is a known Outlook rendering hazard.

**[WARN] Duplicate CSS class selectors**

`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the mobile media query with different values (`padding-top: 5px` and `padding-top: 10px` for `.MspaceTop`; same pattern for `.MspaceBot`). The second definition wins per cascade, silently discarding the first. The source comment acknowledges this (`/* MspaceTop was duplicated, kept one */`) but the duplicate remains in the live HTML.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on tracking pixel**

```html
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/88ef7f99-21ca-4223-bc83-2f334250a2c4?..." width="1" height="1">
```

No `alt=""` attribute. The open-tracking pixel (`click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx`) correctly uses `alt=""`. This pixel should match.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent from relay capture**

The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. If this header is genuinely absent at delivery, the email is non-compliant with Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements (mandatory for >5k/day senders). Gap may be a relay capture artifact — verify against raw SMTP headers at delivery.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) was not detected. Required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policy for commercial email at this volume. Same relay-capture caveat applies.

SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown. `oldnavy@email.oldnavy.com` sending via `email.oldnavy.com` should pass SPF and DKIM under Gap Inc.'s standard ESP configuration — but this cannot be confirmed from available data.
