# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is leading with a big, splashy Old Navy × Disney summer collab and stacking multiple deals above the fold — this is a brand I already know and trust, doing exactly what I expect it to do in mid-May. The pricing is front and center ($5! $10! $15!) which speaks directly to my brain, and the family imagery actually looks like my life. It's not subtle, but I'm not here for subtle — I'm here for deals on clothes my kids will destroy by August.

## What's working

- **The $5/$10/$15 pricing strip** — that blue banner stopped my scroll the second I opened the page. Concrete price points beat vague percent-off language for me every time.
- **"Extra $10 Off When You Spend $75+ — Ends 5/16"** — the end date is right there. I can plan a cart around this tonight before it expires.
- **Old Navy × Disney hero** — the family with a kid in the frame is my people. And Disney means my kids will actually want to wear this, which reduces the "I don't want to wear that" negotiation I deal with every back-to-school season.
- **Free shipping threshold visible** — "$50+ for Encore Members" is easy to hit with two kids' worth of items in my cart.
- **Multiple stacked offers** — I can potentially combine the $10 off promo with the 50% deals, which is the kind of mental math I actually enjoy doing.

## What's weak

- **"Encore Members" vs. "Navyist Rewards"** — I know I'm a Navyist member but this banner says "Encore Members." I have to stop and think whether these are the same thing. That pause is friction I shouldn't have.
- **No category navigation visible** — on mobile, all I see is a hamburger menu. I can't one-tap to "Girls" or "Boys" or "Women's basics." That's one extra tap before I even start shopping.
- **No Super Cash callout** — it's mid-May and that's typically prime Super Cash earn season. If I'm earning right now, tell me. That's a huge motivator for me specifically.
- **The Disney collab is lifestyle-heavy, basics-light** — beautiful hero, but I also need to know where the plain crew-neck tees and uniform polos are. The summer Disney vibe is fun but it's not my whole cart.
- **"Exclusions apply" in small text** — as a deal hunter I know this matters enormously. What's excluded from the 50% off? The banner doesn't say.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" or "Back to School Basics" quick-link strip** directly under the hero — one row of category chips ("Girls," "Boys," "Baby," "Women's") so mobile shoppers don't have to open the hamburger at all.
- **Rename "Encore Members" to "Navyist Rewards Members"** in the free-shipping banner — or at minimum add "(Navyist)" in parentheses so loyalty members don't have to wonder if this applies to them.
- **Surface Super Cash earn status in the loyalty banner** — something like "Earn Super Cash May 14–28" with a small badge would make me feel like I'm on a mission, not just browsing.
- **Swap the hero's "NEW!" badge for a "Shop the Collection" CTA button** overlaid on the Disney hero image — right now I see the collab but there's no obvious tap target in the image itself, only the "SHOP NOW" text above it.
