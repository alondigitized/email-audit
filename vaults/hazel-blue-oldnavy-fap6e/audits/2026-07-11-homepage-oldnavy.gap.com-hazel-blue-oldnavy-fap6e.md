# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is leading with a massive CLEARANCE banner screaming "FROM $3.99," which honestly made me stop scrolling immediately — that is my language. This homepage reads like a sale-first, basics-friendly store that knows its audience shops deals before it shops aesthetics. The credit card push at the very top is a little aggressive for an opening move, but the clearance hero more than makes up for it.

## What's working

- **CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 hero** — this is the clearance rack I drive to in-store, just delivered to my phone. I am clicking this.
- **Free shipping on orders $50+ for Encore Members** — visible right away, and $50 is absolutely reachable when I'm loading up on kids' basics.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — no cute copy, no ambiguity. It just says what it is.
- **Red hero color** — bright and urgent without being chaotic. My eye went straight to it.
- **Sale tab in the top nav** — I can see it's one tap away. That matters when I'm shopping fast between school pickup and dinner.

## What's weak

- **Credit card push is the very first thing I see** — pushing the Encore credit card before I've even looked at a product feels pushy. I'm here to shop, not apply for credit.
- **"Low Rise Is Back" module** — the two women I can see look like they're in their early 20s in fun going-out colors. That's not me or my wardrobe right now.
- **No kids or family signal anywhere above the fold** — I buy kids' uniforms and family basics here. There is zero indication that the store sells children's clothing if you just landed fresh.
- **Loyalty feels like credit card upsell, not member reward** — I'm a Navyist member but the loyalty mention is tethered to the Encore card application, not to my existing rewards.

## Recommendations

- **Lead the second hero with a family / back-to-school shot**, not Low Rise — even a simple "Uniform Shop: From $6" tile would speak directly to the back-to-school mom demographic that drives August volume.
- **Move the credit card offer below the fold or to a side drawer** — let the clearance deal breathe as the true hero instead of burying it under a financing pitch.
- **Add a "Rewards balance" or "Super Cash" nudge for signed-in members** — a single line like "You have $25 Super Cash ready to use" would immediately convert loyalty members.
- **Add a countdown or end-date to the clearance offer** — "CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 — ends Sunday" would create real urgency without lying about it.
