# $6 tees & $5 tanks + 50% off everything* (yes, really!)
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's fourth email in roughly 48 hours running the same $6 tees / $5 tanks deal — and the subject line's "(yes, really!)" is an unintentional admission that the brand knows it. The hero executes cleanly: prominent price callouts flank a model in summer casual wear, "2 DAYS ONLY!" creates urgency, and "50% Off Everything*" anchors the value proposition above the fold. Nothing is broken.
- The problem is the signal-to-noise ratio in the inbox. After yesterday's "GASP 😮 50% off everything* AND $6 tees & $5 tanks" re-skin, this version changes little beyond the subject framing. The email body — hero → product grid → Clearance CTA → Dream Dresses Under $35 — is structurally identical to the last two sends. The clearance sizing callout is a useful mid-email hook, and the Dream Dresses section adds a secondary sell that breaks the monotony.
- The creative is competent and the offer is genuinely aggressive. But by the fourth near-identical promo in two days, even a good deal stops reading as urgent and starts reading as noise. The email earns its engagement from the strength of the price point, not from any new creative effort.

## What's working

- **Hero price callouts are immediately legible** — $6 tees, $5 tanks, and the 50% umbrella are all readable at a glance before scrolling.
- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" badge** lands the urgency message directly in the hero section.
- **"SHOP CLEARANCE IN YOUR SIZE"** mid-email banner is a functional hook that drives a high-intent action (size filtering).
- **Dream Dresses Under $35** adds a category-pivot that gives non-basics shoppers a reason to click.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **Fourth identical deal in 48 hours** — the offer cadence is so compressed that urgency has collapsed; "2 DAYS ONLY" rings hollow when a near-identical email went out yesterday.
- **"(yes, really!)"** in the subject signals subscriber fatigue — a clever deflection that also confirms Old Navy knows this audience has been trained to doubt the "everything*" headline.
- **Asterisk on "everything*"** does real work here but is never resolved in the visible hero copy — the exclusions are in footer fine print, not near the offer.
- **No new creative hook** — the product grid and model layout are visually indistinguishable from the May 16 sends.
- **No personalization or loyalty signal** anywhere in the visible render.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rest the deal or escalate it.** A fifth "50% off everything*" email tomorrow destroys remaining urgency equity. Either pause for 48 hours or add a stackable member-exclusive benefit to make this version meaningfully different.
- 2. **Move the asterisk resolution into the hero.** Put "excludes final sale" directly under the 50% headline — it builds trust and preempts the "yes, really" cynicism.
- 3. **Give the Dream Dresses section its own subject-line moment** rather than burying it below the fold — it's a strong secondary sell that's doing zero work in the inbox.
- 4. **Add a price anchor** — show a crossed-out original price next to the $6/$5 callouts so the discount math is visible without mental effort.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ends tonight — $6 tees, $5 tanks`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: $6 tees, $5 tanks, 50% sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Dream Dresses under $35 — shop before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance in your size is going fast — grab yours now`

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken link on the Movable Ink content/open pixel that redirects to an invalid URL type, and three compliance gaps (unsubscribe headers, physical address) that could not be confirmed due to relay header stripping. Plain-text part is essentially non-functional — 90% URLs by character count.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

`https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/0bdb3552-9397-4bd6-ba04-238a13b9e189?mi_mid=019e3597-c780-7000-a4c4-48d37f5886a9&cp_tp=v3.eJyF...`
QA resolver reports destination as `unknown url type: '/g/0bdb3552-9397-4bd6-ba04-238a13b9e189?txn=14792980-5242-11f1-a3fc-6e9b95041fa3'`. The `/o/` → `/g/` redirect is malformed; the Movable Ink block for this send likely has a misconfigured redirect rule or a stale `cp_tp` payload.

**[WARN] 23 click-redirect links unverified**
All shop/CTA links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. Final destinations were not probed, so broken landing pages cannot be ruled out.

**Two open-tracking pixels present** — one on `click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx` (ESP native) and one on `cp.oldnavy.com/o/` (Movable Ink). The latter is the broken one above.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on Movable Ink pixel**
`<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/0bdb3552...">` — no `alt` attribute present. The ESP open pixel correctly has `alt=""`, but this one does not. Screen readers will announce the full URL.

**[WARN] Duplicate CSS class selectors in mobile media query**
`.MspaceTop` is assigned to both `.space-top5` and `.space-top10`; `.MspaceBot` is similarly duplicated. The second definition wins, silently breaking the 5px spacing variant on mobile.

```css
.space-top5, .MspaceTop {padding-top: 5px;}   /* overridden below */
.space-top10, .MspaceTop {padding-top: 10px;} /* wins — 5px variant is dead */
```

**[WARN] Aggressive Outlook line-height reset**
The Outlook conditional block sets `td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }` globally. This collapses line height on all `<td>` elements in Outlook 2013+ unless explicitly overridden per cell — a common source of collapsed/missing text.

**[INFO] No dark mode media query**
No `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block. White (`#ffffff`) backgrounds and black (`#000000`) text will invert unpredictably in Apple Mail dark mode.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header — unconfirmed**
Header not captured by the AgentMail relay. RFC 2369 compliance cannot be verified. If absent from actual delivery, this violates Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender requirements (enforced since Feb 2024).

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header — unconfirmed**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe header not captured. Google and Yahoo require this for senders above 5,000/day. Absence triggers suppression of the native unsubscribe UI in Gmail.

**[WARN] Physical mailing address — not detected in captured HTML**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address. The HTML source is truncated at the footer; address may exist but could not be confirmed. Must verify in full render.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM/DMARC — unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured via relay. Sending domain is `email.oldnavy.com` — DKIM should be present. Relay stripping prevents confirmation; recommend checking at MTA level.
