# Long weekend sale — 50% off deals for the whole fam + $3 tanks & $2.50 flag tees in-store
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy's 14th email in roughly a week, and the creative finally pivots — the hero is no longer the recycled "MAJOR DRESS SALE!" but a "50% off everything" star-spangled board paired with a "2 DAYS ONLY" $3 tank/$2.50 flag tee callout. That's a sharper, more concrete hook than the dress loop, and the Americana styling tells me Memorial Day is the real frame this time.
- The body sprawls — Americana models, then a swap into activewear with "3 Activewear Names to Know," then more product cards. It reads like two campaigns stitched together, and the lower activewear section feels disconnected from the patriotic hero. The subject is also schizophrenic: "50% off deals for the whole fam" AND "$3 tanks" AND "$2.50 flag tees in-store" — three offers fighting for attention.
- Net: a sharper hook than the last 4 sends, but the bottom half dilutes it. Solid 5–6.

## What's working

- Hero offer is unambiguous: "50% OFF EVERYTHING" with patriotic star treatment that fits the holiday.
- "2 DAYS ONLY" creates credible urgency for a Memorial Day weekend.
- Concrete door-buster pricing ($3 tanks, $2.50 flag tees) breaks the $6/$8/$10/$12 dress loop the brand has been hammering all week.
- Clear category split — fam summer styles on top, activewear below — gives multiple entry points.

## What's weak

- Subject crams three offers (50% off / $3 tanks / $2.50 flag tees / in-store) into one line — none land hard.
- The "in-store" qualifier on the cheap tees buries a critical limitation in the subject — feels bait-y after click.
- Email is very long and the activewear block at the bottom reads as a separate campaign tacked on.
- 14th send in 7 days — fatigue is real, even with fresher creative.
- Mid-email "Americana" banner is hard to read at thumbnail scale; copy hierarchy is weak vs. the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero offer.** Lead with "50% off everything + $3 Americana tanks" and drop the activewear from this send — save it for Tuesday's post-holiday re-engagement.
- 2. **Move the "in-store only" disclosure into the subject explicitly** if you must keep the $2.50 tees there, or remove them — burying it invites unsubscribes.
- 3. **Shorten the email by ~40%.** Hero + 4-tile product grid + CTA. Anything below the activewear pivot is dead scroll.
- 4. **Add a credible end time** ("ends Monday 11:59 PT") near the hero, not just "2 days only."
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off everything + $3 Americana tanks — 2 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day: 50% off the whole site, ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stars, stripes, and 50% off the entire family lineup.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus $3 flag tanks in-store this weekend only.`

# Technical Audit — Old Navy "Long weekend sale" email

## 1. Technical Summary
One broken tracking pixel URL (unknown scheme `/g/...` returned by the click-redirect endpoint) plus multiple CAN-SPAM/RFC 8058 header gaps and missing image alt text. Structural HTML is otherwise standards-compliant for an Outlook-safe responsive template.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **[FAIL]** Tracking pixel `https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/7d82d6f9-...` resolves to a relative `/g/...?txn=...` path with no scheme/host, producing an "unknown url type" error. This is a Movable Ink (`cp.oldnavy.com`) open/event beacon — its downstream redirect is malformed.
- 23 click-redirect links skipped HTTP probing (expected behavior for `click.email.oldnavy.com` ESP wraps); no broken destinations confirmed but also unverified.
- Open-pixel via `click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx?...` present and well-formed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **4 images missing `alt` attributes**, including the hero lookbook (`gq2zvv0yqw_...FamilyLookbook`), the animated GIF (`o6xdvvknq8_...CourtClubho`), and the Movable Ink personalization beacon. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will render empty.
- Duplicate CSS class redefinitions inside the `@media` block (e.g., `.block`, `.photo`, `.MspaceTop`, `.MspaceBot`) — harmless but indicates template drift.
- Outlook conditional (`mso 9`) and VML namespaces declared correctly; viewport, `X-UA-Compatible`, and XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `*|...|*`) detected in the captured source. Movable Ink (`cp.oldnavy.com/o/...`) is wired for dynamic content but its beacon URL is broken (see §2).

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured by the relay — one-click unsubscribe status cannot be confirmed.
- **[WARN]** `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) not present — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance since Feb 2024.
- **[WARN]** No physical mailing address detected in the captured HTML body — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) violation if truly absent (may be in the truncated footer).
- **[WARN]** `Authentication-Results` header not captured — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown via the AgentMail relay path.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Destination URLs are wrapped by `click.email.oldnavy.com` and were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from this capture. Recommend an unwrapped sample to confirm `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are populated and that landing pages resolve.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the Movable Ink beacon at `cp.oldnavy.com/o/7d82d6f9-...` — its redirect emits a scheme-less `/g/...` URL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 4 flagged images (hero lookbook, GIF, secondary lookbook, MI beacon should use `alt=""` as a tracking pixel).
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers are present at the SMTP layer for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
4. Confirm physical mailing address is rendered in the (truncated) footer.
5. Capture and verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) headers end-to-end.
6. De-duplicate the `.block`, `.photo`, `.MspaceTop`, `.MspaceBot` rules inside the 639px media query to reduce template drift risk.
