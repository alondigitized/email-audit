# Deals on deals! Get 50% off everything* + snag PJs for $12
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Same trap as the July 28 "$12 PJs" send: the subject line leads with "snag PJs for $12," and once again there is not a single pajama product in the render. The hero is "TODAY ONLY! Polos $8/$10/$12," and everything downstream — Baggy Jeans, Moonwalk Tee, Kids Fleece, Baby One-Piece, then a full activewear block (PowerSoft/StretchTech/CloudMotion) — is unrelated to the subject's headline hook. This is now a repeated pattern in this brand's send history, not a one-off copy slip.
- The body itself is competently built — clean product grid, visible member-benefit callout, a 50%-off banner reinforced twice, and a clearance strip at the bottom — but it's doing double duty as a generic storewide catalog rather than delivering on its own subject line. "Old Navy Haul into Fall" as the sender name also clashes with shorts/tank activewear imagery that reads summer, not fall.
- Verdict: solid execution wasted on a subject that overpromises and underdelivers. Fix the PJ/subject mismatch before touching anything else — it's the second time in two sends this exact bait-and-switch has shipped.

## What's working

- 50% off everything is stated with a specific, recognizable number and reinforced twice (hero banner + mid-body).
- Clear, itemized "Best-of-Sale Picks" with real prices ($25 jean, $13 tee, $15 fleece, $8 baby one-piece) — no vague copy.
- Ecomm membership / free shipping over $50 callout gives loyalty shoppers a visible reason to engage.
- Activewear sub-section (PowerSoft/StretchTech/CloudMotion) is well organized with named product lines and clean product photography.
- No visible render bugs — images load, layout holds, buttons are consistent.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "$12 PJs" and the body contains zero pajama products — a direct repeat of the July 28 "$12 PJs" bait-and-switch flagged previously.
- Hero competes with itself: "TODAY ONLY! Polos $8/$10/$12" and the "50% OFF EVERYTHING" banner are two different offers stacked in the same viewport, diluting which one the eye should land on.
- CTA copy is the generic "SHOP NOW" on nearly every block — no category-specific button copy anywhere.
- Sender name says "Haul into Fall" while product imagery (tanks, shorts, activewear) skews summer — seasonal mismatch.
- This is roughly the fourth-plus near-identical "50% off everything" send in under two weeks per brand history — cadence fatigue risk is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject-to-body mismatch first.** Either add an actual PJ product block to the email or drop the PJ claim from the subject entirely — this is the second consecutive send making this exact promise and not keeping it, which erodes trust fast.
- 2. **Pick one hero offer.** Lead with either the Polos deal or the 50%-off-everything banner, not both stacked on top of each other in the same fold.
- 3. **Swap generic "SHOP NOW" for category-specific CTAs** ("Shop Jeans," "Shop Fleece") to lift click intent per section.
- 4. **Align sender name/imagery to one season** — "Haul into Fall" copy paired with summer activewear photography sends a mixed signal.
- 5. **Space out storewide 50%-off sends** — four-plus near-duplicate blasts in under two weeks trains subscribers to skim past this creative.
