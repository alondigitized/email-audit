# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay so I've been a Navyist forever and Old Navy is basically my second home during back-to-school season, but right now? This feels like a brand that's gearing up for Memorial Day weekend with a full-on "America is cute" moment — which, sure, I get it — but I came here to find basics for my kids and maybe knock out a Navyist reward, not to vibe with a flag sweater. It's clean, it's familiar, but it's not talking to me as a mom.

## What's working

- **The Encore member free shipping strip** — "Free shipping on orders $50+ for Encore Members" sitting right below the credit card banner is the first thing that grabs me as a rewards member. That $50 threshold is absolutely reachable for me, so this feels immediately useful.
- **"Sign In or Join Now" prompt** — low-friction loyalty nudge right where I can act on it. Good placement for a returning member who maybe isn't signed in.
- **The Americana hero is seasonally right** — "BIG INTO U.S. OF YAY!" with the flag sweater makes sense for late-April/early-May. It doesn't feel stale or off-season.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no cluttered junk, the brand switcher (Gap / Old Navy / Banana Republic / Athleta) at the very top is low-key helpful if I'm cross-shopping the family.

## What's weak

- **The primary CTA is for a credit card, not for shopping** — "APPLY NOW" in all caps is the most visible button on the page and it's a financial product pitch. I already have this card. Lead with shopping, not credit applications.
- **Zero kids or family content above the fold** — I have two kids and school's almost out. Uniforms, summer basics, all of that is nowhere. There's one adult woman above the fold. Old Navy's bread and butter is families; this doesn't show that.
- **The hero "U.S. OF YAY!" campaign doesn't connect to an offer** — I can see the copy and the image but I can't tell what's on sale or what I'm supposed to tap. Where's the "Shop the Collection" or even a price callout?
- **No Super Cash or active rewards callout** — If there's a Super Cash earn event happening (which there usually is in May), it's not visible here. That's literally one of the main reasons I open this app.
- **Hamburger-only nav on mobile** — there's no quick-tap category bar (Kids, Women, Boys, Girls) below the logo. I have to open the full menu to navigate anywhere.

## Recommendations

- **Swap or layer the hero with a family/kids tile** — Even a split hero or a quick-scroll module below showing "Back to Summer Basics — Kids & Baby" would land for me immediately. One adult woman in a sweater is fine for Instagram, not for my homepage.
- **Move the "Shop Now" or category CTA into the hero** — Right now the hero has copy and a lifestyle photo but no tappable destination. Add "Shop the Americana Edit" or even "Shop Women / Shop Kids" buttons overlaid on the hero before the fold.
- **Replace or de-prioritize the credit card banner** — If I'm already an Encore member, that top banner should say something relevant to me (rewards balance, upcoming Super Cash event) instead of soliciting a card I already have.
- **Add a sticky category nav strip** — A thin horizontal scroll bar under the logo with Women | Kids | Baby | Sale | Clearance would let me get to my categories in one tap without hunting through the hamburger menu.
