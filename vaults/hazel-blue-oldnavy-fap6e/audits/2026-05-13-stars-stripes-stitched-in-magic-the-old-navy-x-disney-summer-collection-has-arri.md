# Stars, stripes & stitched-in magic 🪄 The Old Navy x Disney summer collection has arrived
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a collection-launch email, not a promotional one — and that distinction is the single most important thing to understand about it. Old Navy x Disney summer is the story, and the creative leans hard into a patriotic red/white/blue palette with Mickey and Donald woven through lifestyle shots. The execution is polished and the photography is strong. The problem is structural: there's no offer. No percentage off, no dollar threshold, no shipping hook, no deadline. For a recipient who opens promo tabs with discount intent, this email has nothing to pull the trigger on.
- The subject line is doing two different jobs simultaneously — evocative poetry ("Stars, stripes & stitched-in magic") and literal announcement ("The Old Navy x Disney summer collection has arrived") — and the double-header exceeds 85 characters, which is brutal on mobile where everything past ~50 chars is cut off. The first half is charming but doesn't land the brand collab; the second half lands the collab but feels like a press release.
- Net judgment: great creative for a brand-awareness send, wrong vehicle if the goal was driving immediate revenue. The lack of any offer or urgency makes this a passive browse invitation, not a click driver.

## What's working

- **Hero copy lands instantly.** "THAT SUMMER FEELING!" in large type + the patriotic palette immediately communicates season and mood without needing to read anything else.
- **Disney integration is visible in the product, not just slapped on.** Models wear character prints that are clearly visible — the collab feels real, not just a badge on the email.
- **No render bugs.** All images load, no layout breakage, no overlapping text — the email renders cleanly.
- **Collection breadth is shown.** Multiple shots cover different demographics — couples, kids, solo women — signaling the line has range.
- **Old Navy's brand voice is consistent.** Bold colors, fun typography, inclusive casting — recognizable at a glance.

## What's weak

- **No offer whatsoever.** No discount, no free shipping threshold, no member price — nothing to convert browse intent into a click.
- **Subject line is two emails in a trench coat.** The poetic half and the announcement half compete; mobile will cut after "Stars, stripes & stitched-in magic 🪄 The Old" and the Disney collab disappears entirely.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the only CTA and it's generic.** No category specificity — it doesn't tell me whether to click for kids, women, or men.
- **No urgency signal.** Nothing says "launching now," "limited run," "shop before it sells out," or any time-bound hook. Collection emails without urgency are easily deferred and forgotten.
- **"FIND YOUR SUMMER FEELING CHARACTERS"** copy in the lower section is vague — which characters? Linking to a filtered page by character (Mickey vs. Donald) would have made this a real engagement mechanic.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an offer.** Even a modest "20% off new arrivals this week" turns this from a lookbook into a reason to click now. No discount needed to feel like a launch discount — free shipping on the collab collection would do it.
- 2. **Split the subject line.** The poetic line alone is the stronger subject. The announcement should be the preheader. Let each do one job.
- 3. **Make the CTAs category-specific.** "Shop kids' styles," "Shop women's," "Shop the full collab" — three buttons instead of one generic one, so any reader can self-select.
- 4. **Add a deadline.** "Launching now — new styles dropping through summer" or "While supplies last" at minimum. Something that makes opening feel timely rather than deferrable.
- 5. **Lean into the characters section.** Replace the vague "FIND YOUR SUMMER FEELING CHARACTERS" with clickable character tiles (Mickey, Donald, Minnie) that deep-link to filtered results. That's an actual Disney collab mechanic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Old Navy × Disney is here — shop the summer collab`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Stars, stripes & Mickey 🎉 The Disney summer collab drops now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Old Navy x Disney summer collection just arrived — new styles for the whole family`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mickey, Donald, and all your favorites — stitched into summer`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

The Contentsquare pixel `<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/32a28c02-...">` has no `alt` attribute. All `<img>` tags require `alt=""` (empty string is acceptable for decorative/tracking pixels) for WCAG 2.1 AA conformance and to avoid screen readers announcing the raw URL.
