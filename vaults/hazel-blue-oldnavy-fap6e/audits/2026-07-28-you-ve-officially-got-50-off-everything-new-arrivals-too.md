# You've officially got 50% off everything* (new arrivals TOO!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy running the exact same 50% Off Everything storewide play for the fourth time in nine days — July 20's kitchen sink, July 26's "HALF OFF EVERYTHING* + $16 Wow jeans," July 26's near-duplicate, and now this. The subject swaps "This is HUGE" for "You've officially got," and the hero drops the $16 Wow Jeans hook entirely in favor of a plain purple slab that says "Haul Into Fall / 50% OFF EVERYTHING!" — visually identical in structure to the July 26 sends, just a different flat lay.
- The execution itself is clean: category grid with clear 50% off callouts (tees, jeans, activewear, pants, dresses, sweatshirts/hoodies), a "New In. Made For You." fashion block, then a clearance-from-$3.99 module. No render bugs, good hierarchy, honest single offer. The problem isn't craft, it's fatigue — a subscriber who opened the July 26 emails has now seen this identical purple-and-yellow "50% off everything" treatment three times running, and the asterisk on "everything*" is never resolved anywhere visible in the render.
- Judgment: competent but redundant. This is the same email restructured, not a new one, and the brand is burning its best offer by repeating it instead of escalating or varying it.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous hero: "50% OFF EVERYTHING!" in large yellow type against contrasting purple, unmissable at a glance.
- Six-panel category grid (Tees, Jeans, Activewear, Pants, Dresses, Sweatshirts & Hoodies) makes the flat 50% off apply obviously store-wide, with individual "SHOP NOW" CTAs per category.
- Clean gender/age wayfinding bar (Girls, Boys, Baby Girls, Baby Boys, Maternity, Toddler Boys) right under the hero for fast navigation.
- Second content block ("New In. Made For You.") adds genuine new-arrival product imagery, not just the same flat lay repeated.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is essentially a reskin of the July 26 "HALF OFF EVERYTHING*" sends — same offer, same purple palette, same structure, ninth day in a row of storewide-percentage messaging.
- The $16 Wow Jeans hook that anchored four prior sends (July 20, 24, 25, 26) is gone with no explanation — inconsistent narrative for a subscriber tracking that specific deal.
- The "everything*" asterisk is never footnoted anywhere visible in the screenshot — no exclusions shown, which reads as a bait-and-switch risk per the honesty rubric.
- Clearance module ("$3.99") is buried below two other full offers — a strong price point getting third billing.
- Zero urgency mechanic visible (no countdown, no explicit end date) despite "Online Exclusive, Limited Time Only" copy — vague, not credible time-bound pressure.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Break the repeat-creative pattern.** Four near-identical "50% off everything" sends in nine days is cadence fatigue, not a campaign. Either escalate the offer, swap the hero product story entirely (e.g., pivot back to $16 Wow Jeans as sole hero, which scored higher standalone on July 25), or skip a day.
- 2. **Resolve the asterisk in-render.** Add a one-line exclusion note near the hero ("*Excludes clearance, Old Navy Rewards partners" etc.) so the offer reads honest rather than fine-print-hidden.
- 3. **Add a real deadline.** Replace "Limited Time Only" with a specific date/time ("Ends tonight at midnight ET") to make urgency credible instead of generic.
- 4. **Promote the $3.99 clearance module higher** or give it its own subject-line callout in a future send — a sub-$4 price point is a stronger curiosity hook than another "50% off everything" repeat.
- 5. Test subject/preheader variants that break from the repeated "50% off everything" phrasing pattern seen July 24–27.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your new-fall faves are 50% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$3.99 clearance just dropped (plus 50% off everything else)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals, tees, jeans, dresses — all half off, ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: summer clearance from $3.99, while it lasts.`
