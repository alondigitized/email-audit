# Your sign to stock up: 50% off everything* ends soon!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's tenth email in five days — and the creative formula has barely changed. The 50% off everything headline is back, now wrapped in a soft "your sign to stock up" subject that tries to dress up the same offer with vague urgency ("ends soon"). The email at least leans into a focused editorial angle — "Dresses Everyone's Obsessing Over" as a Top 10 editorial — which is the most interesting thing in the send. Below that, a clearance module adds a secondary reason to click.
- The render is clean, no layout bugs, but the visual hierarchy buries the urgency. Nothing in the hero shouts "today only" or gives a deadline. The clearance section does useful work by speaking to size-inclusive shopping, which at least personalizes the ask slightly. But for anyone tracking this blitz, this is the same discount wrapped in a new subject hook for the fourth or fifth time.
- Honest judgement: this email is doing just enough to stay out of the trash, but not enough to stand out. The editorial "Top 10 Dresses" angle is wasted because the hero leads with the discount, not the curation. Swap those and you have a meaningfully better email.

## What's working

- The "Dresses Everyone's Obsessing Over / Here's our top 10 for a reason" editorial frame adds a content hook beyond a flat discount headline.
- The clearance-by-size grid ("Shop All," "S-4XL," "1X-4X," "Petite," "3X-4X only," "2XL-4XL") directly addresses size-inclusive shoppers — a differentiator Old Navy owns.
- The render is clean: no broken images, no overlapping text, all product imagery loads correctly.
- Hero offer (50% Off Everything) is immediately legible above the fold.

## What's weak

- "Ends soon" in the subject is vague pseudo-urgency — no deadline is visible in the body either, making the claim feel hollow.
- This is the tenth Old Navy email in five days leading with essentially the same 50% off headline; the blitz fatigue is real and this subject hook does little to overcome it.
- The "your sign to stock up" opener is a soft lifestyle phrase that adds no information — it wasted the first five characters of the subject on nothing.
- The hero CTA ("Shop Now") is generic; there's no drill-down CTA tied to the dress editorial, so the two creative concepts (discount + curation) feel disconnected.
- No price anchoring in the body — unlike prior sends that called out "$6 tees / $5 tanks," this email never shows you what 50% off actually means in dollars.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a real deadline.** "Ends tonight at midnight" or "Ends Sunday" transforms "ends soon" from noise into an action trigger. Put it in the hero, not just the subject.
- 2. **Lead the editorial, not the discount.** Flip the hero: "Top 10 Dresses of Summer — and they're all 50% off" makes the curation the reason to click, with the discount as the kicker. Right now you're burning the Top 10 idea as a secondary block under a generic headline.
- 3. **Add a price floor.** One visible callout like "From $19.99" or "Dresses starting at $17" makes 50% off feel real, not fluffy.
- 4. **Tie the CTA to the editorial.** Replace the lone "Shop Now" with "See the Top 10" — link directly to the curated collection, not the homepage.
- 5. **Surface the size grid higher.** The clearance-by-size module is genuinely useful; it's buried at the bottom. Move it up or at least tease it in the subject.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our editors' Top 10 picks — all half off before the sale ends.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Duplicate CSS class selectors in mobile media query**
- `.MspaceTop` is assigned twice: once to `.space-top5` and again to `.space-top10`. Same for `.MspaceBot` on `.space-bot5` and `.space-bot10`. The second rule silently overwrites the first. Any element using `.space-top5` or `.space-bot5` on mobile will receive the 10px value instead of 5px.
- Evidence: `@media only screen and (max-width: 639px)` block, lines defining `.space-top5, .MspaceTop` and `.space-top10, .MspaceTop`.

**[WARN] Outlook MSO `td` line-height reset**
- `td { line-height:0px; mso-line-height-rule:exactly }` in the MSO conditional block resets all `td` line-heights to zero for Outlook 2013+. Without element-level overrides on every `td`, this can clip text content in Outlook desktop clients.

**[OK] Tracking pixel alt attribute**
- QA flagged `alt=""` on the `cp.oldnavy.com` 1×1 pixel. Empty alt is correct per WCAG for decorative/invisible images — no action needed.

**[OK] Responsive structure**
