# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-04

- + concrete offer above fold: multiple price points in the hero AND the Encore credit card 30% off AND free shipping threshold — stacked offers

## What's working

- **From-price anchoring in the hero** ("Tees from $10, Dresses from $10, Jeans from $20") — I immediately know this is my price range, full stop
- **Encore Member free shipping banner** — "$50+ for Encore Members" is above the fold and $50 is nothing when I'm buying for two kids and myself
- **Credit card 30% off promo** — I know Old Navy's credit card game, and leading with an acquisition offer for new members is smart even if I skip it
- **Clean, uncluttered hero layout** — no busy product tiles competing with the message; the price copy reads fast on mobile
- **Summer seasonal imagery** — bright yellow umbrella, bold colors, the beach backdrop makes me want to refresh my basics drawer

## What's weak

- **No kids/family anywhere above the fold** — I'm here for kid uniforms and basics for everyone in my house; showing two women on a beach vacation doesn't hook the mom-of-two use case at all
- **No visible "Shop Now" CTA on the hero** — I see prices but I don't see a button; where do I tap to actually shop tees from $10?
- **Credit card "APPLY NOW" dominates the top** — it's the most prominent call to action on the page and it's a financial product, not a shopping one; feels like the wrong priority for someone mid-shopping-session
- **Hamburger nav only** — all my category shortcuts (Kids, Women, Sale) are hidden behind a tap; one-tap access to Kids or Women's would speed up my path significantly
- **No Super Cash or rewards balance callout** — I'm a Navyist Rewards member and there's nothing here telling me what I have to spend

## Recommendations

- **Add a family/kid lifestyle shot as a secondary hero tile** — even a split-screen with "Shop Kids Uniforms from $X" alongside the women's offer would immediately speak to the mom-of-two segment
- **Put a tappable "Shop Tees" or "Shop the Sale" button on the hero** — right now it's price copy floating over an image with nowhere to go; add a white pill button below the headline
- **Swap the credit card banner for a Super Cash or rewards-balance reminder** — for returning Navyist members like me, "You have $X Super Cash to spend" would be far more motivating than a credit card application push
- **Surface top categories as icon chips below the hero** — a quick row of Women's / Kids / Baby / Men's tappable icons would let me skip the hamburger menu entirely and get to my aisle in one move
