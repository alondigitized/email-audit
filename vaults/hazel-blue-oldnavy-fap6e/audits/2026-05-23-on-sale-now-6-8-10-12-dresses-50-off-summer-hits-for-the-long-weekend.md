# ON SALE NOW: $6, $8, $10 & $12 dresses + 50% off summer hits for the long weekend
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's twelfth email in roughly five days — and it shows. The hero opens strong: "MAJOR DRESS SALE!" is large, unambiguous, and paired with a clean model shot in a summer dress. The $6–$12 price tier stack and the 50%-off badge land fast. Structurally, the email is well-executed: product grid, lifestyle imagery, clearance callout at the bottom — it follows the same template that's worked across this entire blitz.
- The problem is exactly that: it's the same template. Yesterday's email (May 22) ran the identical four-price-tier subject with the same "long weekend" close and scored 7/10. Today's swap is purely cosmetic — "The sale of our summer dreams!" became "ON SALE NOW:" — and the email body is functionally a re-skin. The render is clean and the offer is real, but the subscriber who opened yesterday already knows everything this says.
- This is a capable, well-produced send that will convert the small percentage of the list who missed yesterday. For anyone who didn't, it's noise.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is immediate.** "MAJOR DRESS SALE!" dominates the fold; the eye finds the offer in under a second.
- **Four-tier price ladder ($6/$8/$10/$12) is concrete and scannable** — better than a vague "starting at $X."
- **Product grid below the hero is dense and colorful** — summer palette (coral, blue, yellow) reinforces the seasonal moment.
- **The clearance CTA block at the bottom** gives deal-hunters a second hook if the dress offer didn't land.
- **No render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no overlapping text visible.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is a near-clone of yesterday's.** "ON SALE NOW: $6, $8, $10 & $12 dresses + 50% off summer hits for the long weekend" vs. May 22's "The sale of our summer dreams! $6, $8, $10 & $12 dresses + 50% off for the long weekend." The price tiers and long-weekend close are word-for-word identical.
- **At 82 characters, the subject is badly truncated on mobile** — most inboxes cut off at ~50–55 chars, so "for the long weekend" never renders in the inbox preview row.
- **No time-bound deadline is visible in the body** — "long weekend" is implied, but no end-date badge or countdown reinforces urgency inside the email.
- **Zero social proof or reviews** — not a single star rating or "bestseller" tag on any product tile.
- **Loyalty / NavyCard member pricing absent** — a meaningful hook for card holders that consistently goes unused across this blitz.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with a specific deadline in the hero** — add "Ends Monday 11:59 PM" as a badge or sub-headline. The "long weekend" framing is soft; a hard close converts fence-sitters.
- 2. **Cut the subject to under 55 chars and make it distinct from yesterday.** The price-tier list has been in two consecutive subjects. Try a single sharp hook that doesn't repeat.
- 3. **Tag one or two products as "Bestseller" or "1,000+ sold"** in the product grid — any social signal improves click confidence.
- 4. **Surface a NavyCard exclusive** (even just "CardHolders: extra 10% off") — this differentiates the send for loyalty members and gives a reason to open that yesterday's email didn't.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$6 dresses are back — but only through Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pick your price: $6, $8, $10 or $12 dresses`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 50% off summer hits — sale ends Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four price points. Hundreds of styles. Long weekend only.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email renders correctly on standard clients but has one broken redirect chain in a tracking pixel/preference-center URL, and several compliance headers are unverifiable through the AgentMail relay. Plain-text MIME part is URL-dominated, which is a deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken preference-center redirect**
The Liveclicker/Coherent Path pixel at `https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/968babc2-5bbb-400e-86f5-558934a4e79f` resolves to an unknown URL type: `/g/968babc2-...` — the `cp.oldnavy.com` redirect handler is returning a relative `/g/` path instead of an absolute URL, causing the redirect chain to break.

- This URL appears twice: once as a 1×1 tracking pixel (hidden `<div>`) and once as a visible element. If the visible instance is a preference/loyalty link, recipients clicking it will land on a broken page.
- Evidence: QA `[FAIL] Link error: unknown url type: '/g/968babc2-...'`

**[WARN] 23 click-tracking links not probed**
All outbound links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. HTTP resolution was skipped for these. If any campaign redirect rules are misconfigured (wrong offer codes, wrong seasonal landing page), they won't be caught here — recommend a manual spot-check of 2–3 primary CTAs against the live `oldnavy.com` sale landing pages.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Missing alt text on tracking image**
`<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/968babc2-...">` has no `alt` attribute at all (not even `alt=""`). Screen readers will attempt to announce the filename/URL. Should be `alt=""` to mark it as decorative/presentational.

**Outlook line-height conflict**
The MSO conditional block sets `td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }` globally. This is a known pattern but aggressive — any `<td>` containing text that doesn't override `line-height` inline will collapse to zero height in Outlook 2013+. No hard failures observed in the truncated source, but this is a latent rendering risk if new modules are added without explicit `mso-line-height-rule` overrides.

**Duplicate CSS class selectors**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the responsive block (the comment in source acknowledges this). While browsers handle duplicate rules gracefully (last wins), it's dead code that should be cleaned up.

**No dark-mode media query**
No `prefers-color-scheme: dark` handling. Not a hard failure, but images with white backgrounds will appear with harsh edges in dark-mode clients (Apple Mail, iOS Mail).

---

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header unverifiable**
Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) headers were captured by the AgentMail relay. These are almost certainly present at the MTA level (Gap Inc. / Salesforce Marketing Cloud consistently sets them), but the relay isn't passing them through. Cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this audit artifact alone.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in truncated HTML**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a physical postal address. The HTML source provided is truncated — the address is typically in the footer table. This finding is likely a truncation artifact, not an actual absence. **Verify the full footer renders:** Old Navy's standard footer includes "Two Folsom St, San Francisco, CA 94105."

**Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) unverifiable**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured by relay. Gap Inc. uses DMARC with `p=reject` on `oldnavy.com`; the sending domain `email.oldnavy.com` should have aligned DKIM. Cannot confirm from this artifact — check MTA logs or a raw `.eml` capture.
