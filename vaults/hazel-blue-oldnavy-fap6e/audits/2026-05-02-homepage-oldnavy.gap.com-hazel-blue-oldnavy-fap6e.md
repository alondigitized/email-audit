# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is screaming sale right now — loud red background, countdown clock, "50% Off Everything" in the biggest font they could fit. As someone who basically plans her shopping calendar around these sitewide events, this is exactly the kind of homepage that makes me stop scrolling. That said, everything above the fold is pushing menswear lifestyle imagery, and I came here for kids' uniforms and family basics.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** ("08 hrs 13 mins 47 secs") is genuinely effective on me — I feel the urgency and it matches exactly how I shop: I see a deal window and I act.
- **"50% Off Everything"** in massive white text on red is impossible to miss. I don't have to hunt for the deal. It's the whole page.
- **"Online Exclusive | Ends 5/2"** framing tells me immediately this is a digital-first event, which fits how I shop (online, deal-triggered).
- **Free shipping banner** at the very top — I clocked it right away. $50+ threshold is reasonable for a family haul.

## What's weak

- **The hero image is a solo dude with a vintage camera.** I'm a mom of two buying polos and leggings — this lifestyle shot tells me nothing about what I'm actually here for.
- **"Encore Members"** in the shipping banner is confusing. I'm a Navyist Rewards member — who's Encore? I had to re-read it. If that's the same program, they need to say so.
- **No kids or family anywhere above the fold.** Old Navy's bread and butter is families. Not one kid, not one "back to school" nod, not a uniform hint.
- **"Almost everything; a few exclusions apply"** in small gray text under the CTA is the classic fine-print move that makes me nervous after I've already gotten excited. Feels like a trap.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the solo-man lifestyle photo for a family or mom+kids shot** — show the people who actually drive Old Navy's volume. Even a split hero (women's / kids') would be better.
- **Replace "Encore Members" with "Navyist Rewards Members"** — or at minimum add a parenthetical. Don't make your loyalty members do mental math.
- **Add a "Kids & Baby" or "Uniforms" quick-link strip** just below the hero — one tap to the category I'm actually shopping converts me faster than making me search.
- **Surface the exclusions prominently** — instead of burying "a few exclusions apply," link to a clear list. Hiding it erodes trust and increases return friction.
