# SO MANY DEALS⚡60% off ALL swim* and 50% off all men's styles*
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's third swim-focused email in roughly 24 hours — yesterday's two sends already hit 60%-off swim and 50%-off men's styling, and this send is nearly identical in structure and headline. The "SO MANY DEALS" opener is generic filler that adds no lift; the real signal (60% off swim) doesn't land until mid-subject. The body is a dense, scrollable catalog across swim, men's, what looks like tie shorts, and a kids/baby sale — a kitchen-sink approach that spreads the offer energy thin.
- On its own, the creative is competent. The 60% hero is large and visible, the swim grid is clean and seasonal, and the layout renders without obvious bugs. But this email isn't being evaluated in isolation — a real subscriber who received two near-identical sends yesterday is going to feel the repetition.
- Judgement: a recycled send dressed in urgency language. The fatigue cost is real, and no meaningful creative or offer evolution justifies another trip to the inbox.

## What's working

- The 60% swim hero is immediately legible — large type, high contrast, no ambiguity about the offer.
- Swim product grid is visually rich; multiple items, good color variety, summery palette that matches the season.
- "2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency is visible near the top and provides a real time-bound hook.
- Layout renders cleanly — no broken images, no overlapping text, clear sectioning between swim, men's, and kids.
- Bright, seasonal color story (pinks, oranges, turquoise) is consistent and on-brand for Memorial Day / early summer.

## What's weak

- Third swim email in ~24 hours; cadence fatigue will hammer open rates among subscribers who got yesterday's two sends.
- "SO MANY DEALS" is a generic, low-specificity opener that wastes the first five words of a mobile-truncated subject line.
- Dual-offer split (swim + men's + kids/baby) scatters the message — a subscriber primarily interested in women's swim has to wade through multiple unrelated sections.
- The subject line at ~60 chars is well over the mobile-safe threshold; "50% off all men's styles*" almost certainly gets cut.
- Asterisk exclusions on everything erode the "SO MANY DEALS" promise even before the subscriber opens.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one lead offer.** If the goal is swim clearance, commit to swim only. The men's and kids/baby panels dilute the urgency and bury the lede for the audience most likely to click swim.
- 2. **Rewrite the subject opener.** Drop "SO MANY DEALS" — it's noise. Start with the number. "60% off ALL swim — 2 days only" is sharper, mobile-safe, and the exact promise a subscriber would scan for.
- 3. **Introduce at least one new creative element.** Even swapping in a different hero model or colorway signals a fresh send vs. a resend. Right now this reads as a copy-paste of yesterday's creative.
- 4. **Surface a deadline more explicitly** — "Ends Monday" or a hard date beats "2 DAYS ONLY" for credibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `60% off ALL swim ends tonight — don't miss it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last chance: 60% off swim + 50% off men's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bikinis, one-pieces, trunks — all included. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Over 100 swim styles on sale. Two days left.`

## Technical Audit — Old Navy "SO MANY DEALS" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-based layout with responsive CSS at a 639px breakpoint and 23 click-redirect tracking links through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. One confirmed broken redirect and several compliance/deliverability gaps detected.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken personalization redirect**
`https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/4d9fbd21-ff82-4e5d-a924-1c5a2127acb5?mi_mid=...`
The redirect resolves to a relative path: `/g/4d9fbd21-ff82-4e5d-a924-1c5a2127acb5?txn=90627f80-5ce0-11f1-8c84-8e0ec7ba23ea`
A relative redirect target is invalid at the HTTP level — this is almost certainly a broken personalized product recommendation link. Recipients clicking it will hit an error or incorrect destination.

**[WARN] 23 tracking links unverifiable**
All CTAs route through `click.email.oldnavy.com` redirect chain. Destination URL integrity and UTM parameter presence cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect chain.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Duplicate CSS class assignments — mobile padding conflict**
In the `@media (max-width: 639px)` block, `.MspaceTop` is bound to two rules:
```css
.space-top5, .MspaceTop { padding-top: 5px; }
.space-top10, .MspaceTop { padding-top: 10px; }
```
Same for `.MspaceBot`. Last-declaration wins — any element using `.MspaceTop` alone will always render at 10px, ignoring the 5px intent. The 5px variants are effectively dead.

**[WARN] Outlook MSO global line-height zeroing**
The MSO conditional block sets:
```css
td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }
```
This is applied globally to all `<td>` elements in Outlook 2013+. Overly broad — risks collapsing any table cell that doesn't have an explicit inline `line-height` override.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on content image**
The `cp.oldnavy.com` personalized content image has no `alt` attribute. In image-blocking email clients, this renders as a blank space with no fallback text.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens detected in the visible HTML. Personalization parameters (`mi_mid`, `mi_cid`, `cp_cid`) in the `cp.oldnavy.com` URL appear to contain resolved UUIDs, not placeholder syntax. The issue is the broken redirect target, not unresolved tokens.

---

### 5. Compliance

**[FAIL] List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post headers absent**
Both headers are required for bulk senders under Gmail and Yahoo Mail's 2024 sender requirements (RFC 2369 / RFC 8058). Missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` specifically means one-click unsubscribe is not supported. Headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay — confirm at the envelope level.

**[WARN] Physical mailing address unverifiable**
QA found no physical address in the analyzed content. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address in every commercial message. The HTML is truncated — verify the footer is present and contains a street address before clearing this finding.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header absent**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed through the AgentMail relay. Sending domain is `email.oldnavy.com` — authentication should be verified at the MTA before treating as clear.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**[FAIL] Broken personalization CTA** — the `cp.oldnavy.com` redirect failure (see §2) means at least one product recommendation block will not deliver users to any valid page.

**[WARN] UTM coverage unconfirmed** — all 23 CTAs use `click.email.oldnavy.com` redirect chains that were not probed. It is unknown whether the final destination URLs carry `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` parameters consistent with the campaign.

**[INFO] Plain-text body is 90% URLs** — 18,633 of 20,790 plain-text characters are URLs, which is a likely result of auto-generated plain text from tracking-link-heavy HTML. This degrades spam filter scoring and makes the plain-text version unreadable.
