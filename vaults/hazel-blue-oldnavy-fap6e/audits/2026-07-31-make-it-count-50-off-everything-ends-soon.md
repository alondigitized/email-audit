# Make it count! 50% OFF everything* ENDS SOON
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's storewide 50%-off play running yet again — the sixth-plus near-identical send in this pattern per the brand history, now re-skinned with a "Haul Into Fall" hook. The good news: unlike the July 28 "$12 PJs" and July 29 "PJs" sends, this email doesn't bait a specific product it fails to deliver — the hero literally says "50% Off Everything" and the body backs that up with a clean category grid (Tees, Jeans, Activewear, Pants, Dresses, Sweatshirts & Hoodies) plus a genuine "New In" fall arrivals section with real product shots and prices.
- Where it falls down is freshness and urgency. "ENDS SOON" is vague filler with no date or countdown, and after six-plus consecutive sends of the exact same 50%-off-everything mechanic, a subscriber's eye has learned to skip this one. The layout is clean and bug-free, but nothing here differentiates it from the four other nearly identical emails already in this inbox this week.
- Verdict: solidly built, zero render issues, but a fatigue-risk repeat with a weak, generic subject line. Middle-of-the-pack.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what the subject promises — no bait-and-switch like the recent "PJs" sends.
- "New In. Made For You." section shows real fall products (sweaters, boots) with visible prices ($34.99–$59.99), giving the "Haul Into Fall" angle actual substance.
- Category grid (Tees/Jeans/Activewear/Pants/Dresses/Sweatshirts) makes the offer easy to scan and self-select from.
- Encore Membership banner and free-shipping strip are both visible above the fold, reinforcing loyalty value.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks across the full length of the email.

## What's weak

- This is the same "50% off everything" storewide mechanic as at least six prior sends in two weeks — high fatigue risk for a regular subscriber.
- "ENDS SOON" is not a credible deadline — no date, time, or countdown anywhere in the render.
- Subject line ("Make it count!") is a vague, low-specificity hook that doesn't tease any of the actual fall product content shown below.
- The bottom "CLEARANCE FROM $3.99" section (summer tanks/tees) clashes tonally with the "Haul Into Fall" framing above it — mixed seasonal messaging in one email.
- No personalization, segmentation, or reviews/social proof anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace "ENDS SOON" with an actual deadline (e.g., "ends tonight," "through Sunday") — vague urgency reads as boilerplate at this point in the cadence.
- 2. Break the repeat pattern: lead the subject with the new-arrivals fall angle instead of the sixth "50% off everything," since that's the only genuinely new content this send has over prior ones.
- 3. Drop or clearly re-section the "$3.99 summer clearance" block — it undercuts the fall narrative the hero just established.
- 4. Add one line of specificity to the CTA copy beyond "SHOP NOW" (e.g., "Shop New Fall Arrivals") to differentiate the two "SHOP NOW" buttons that currently say the same thing for different content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New fall styles are here (still 50% off everything*)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall favorites just landed — 50% off ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cozy knits, new boots, and 50% off everything else`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your fall haul starts here — everything's half off`
