# On sale now: All women's activewear tops & bras* are 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a women's activewear-first send anchored by a genuine TODAY ONLY! urgency and a strong 50% off hero. The primary story is coherent — colorful tanks and bras, clear price point, fashion-forward model — and the "BOLD RED" secondary module actually extends the activewear theme rather than blowing it up entirely. That's a notch above the typical Old Navy kitchen-sink chaos.
- The problem is what comes after: the $3.99 clearance block and the Back-to-School Deals module are lifted directly from yesterday's July 20 send. Any subscriber who opened that email — which scored 8/10 here and was the bigger, splashier kitchen-sink — is looking at retreads the very next morning. The cadence fatigue is real and hits the open stat before the body copy even gets a chance.
- Net judgment: a solid activewear send undermined by stale support modules and a subject line that buries the TODAY ONLY urgency under "On sale now."

## What's working

- **TODAY ONLY! hero lands hard.** The urgency callout is front and center above the fold with the 50% figure immediately adjacent — no hunting required.
- **Colorful product grid is effective.** The rainbow of tank tops and bras in the hero block communicates the breadth of the sale visually, not just verbally.
- **BOLD RED editorial coheres.** Rather than introducing a totally different category, the red fashion sub-block stays within the activewear/apparel lane, making it feel like a curated style story rather than a disruptive pivot.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum — the email displays correctly throughout.

## What's weak

- **Clearance and BTS modules are yesterday's news, literally.** The $3.99 clearance block and the Back-to-School Deals module are the same modules from the July 20 send. Subscribers who opened yesterday will recognize these as copy-paste filler.
- **Subject line undersells the urgency.** "On sale now" is a weak opener when the hero screams TODAY ONLY. The most compelling signal is buried inside the body.
- **Multiple competing stories dilute the click.** Activewear → BOLD RED → Clearance → Back-to-School is four narrative threads. Each one cannibalizes the CTA click that was building in the thread before it.
- **Asterisk on bras in the subject signals exclusions.** "tops & bras*" — the asterisk is a trust-reducer; subscribers have been trained to expect that the deal doesn't cover what they want most.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with TODAY ONLY, not "On sale now."** The deadline is the single most clickable element in this email and it's not in the subject at all.
- **Subject Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: Women's activewear tops & bras 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `50% off activewear, today only — tanks, bras, all of it`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Styles starting at $X — sale ends tonight at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The BOLD RED edit is in. Shop before it's gone.`
- 2. **Cut the clearance and BTS modules entirely.** Both ran yesterday. Rest them for 48 hours minimum. A single-story activewear email with TODAY ONLY urgency is tighter and more clickable than four modules competing for attention.
- 3. **Add an explicit deadline callout in the body.** "Ends tonight at midnight" or a countdown clock drives the TODAY ONLY promise home where the CTA button lives.
- 4. **Turn BOLD RED into a hero, not a secondary module.** If red activewear is the editorial push, give it the hero slot and make the color story the reason to click — it's genuinely distinctive creative in an otherwise discount-heavy send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Tracking pixel missing alt text**
`<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/38c4a2db-6a68-46d6-86ec-6689e0a6370a?...">` — no `alt=""` attribute. Per WCAG 2.1, decorative/tracking images must include `alt=""` explicitly. Several email clients will render a broken image icon if the pixel fails to load.

**CSS class collision — `.MspaceTop` / `.MspaceBot`**
In the `@media (max-width: 639px)` block, `.MspaceTop` is declared twice (for `padding-top: 5px` and `padding-top: 10px`) and `.MspaceBot` is declared twice. The second declaration silently wins, making `.space-top5` and `.space-bot5` functionally inert on mobile. This is a low-severity layout bug but can cause unexpected spacing.

**Outlook line-height conflict**
The MSO conditional style sets `td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }` globally for Outlook 2013+. This is an aggressive reset that can collapse text in any `<td>` that doesn't explicitly set its own `line-height`, causing text to render invisible or overlapping in Outlook desktop.
