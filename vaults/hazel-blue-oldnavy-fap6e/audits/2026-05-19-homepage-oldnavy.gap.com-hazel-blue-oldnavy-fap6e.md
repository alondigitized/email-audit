# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is doing the most right now — a sitewide 50% off banner with a hard end date tomorrow feels made for me. The Disney collab module below it is cute for the kids angle. This is a brand clearly pushing a big promotional moment, and honestly as someone who waits for exactly these sales, I'm listening.

## What's working

- **"50% Off Everything | Ends 5/20" hero**: The urgency + scope combo is exactly what gets me to stop scrolling. Online exclusive + tomorrow's deadline = I'm not waiting.
- **Encore Members free-shipping bar**: Seeing the $50 threshold spelled out reminds me I probably hit that easily with two kids in the cart.
- **"Sign In or Join Now" prompt**: Right there before I even see the logo — pushes me to log in and get the member benefit before I shop.
- **Disney × Old Navy collab module**: The family-with-balloons imagery instantly reads as "this is for us" — a mom, a partner, a little kid. Patriotic red/white/blue palette in late May also previews Memorial Day/summer without being dated.
- **Visual hierarchy on mobile**: One big number, one button, done. My thumb knows exactly where to go.

## What's weak

- **"Exclusions apply." in tiny text**: On mobile, that's barely legible. I've been down this road — "50% off everything" at Old Navy usually means active and specific styles only. The fine print should be more prominent so I don't feel tricked when half my cart is excluded.
- **No kids/family-specific CTA**: "SHOP NOW" goes somewhere, but I don't know if it drops me into adults, kids, or sitewide. I need one tap to Kids or Baby, not a mystery destination.
- **Hamburger-only navigation**: There's no shortcut to Girls, Boys, or Baby visible. I have to tap the menu and dig — extra friction when I'm shopping on my phone during nap time.
- **"De..." cutoff on the right edge**: There's a clipped element in the hero area that looks like an unfinished UI element or an offscreen button. Sloppy.
- **Loyalty naming confusion**: I know my program as Navyist Rewards but this page says "Encore Members." Makes me pause for a second — is this a new thing? Did my points transfer? Small friction but real.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" shortcut CTA** alongside or beneath the main "SHOP NOW" — something like "Shop Kids & Baby" so moms don't have to navigate blind. One extra button, massive relevance boost.
- **Make the exclusions link human-readable**: Replace "Exclusions apply." with "See what's excluded →" in a slightly larger tap target. Builds trust and reduces cart abandonment from surprise callouts.
- **Clarify the loyalty program name**: If "Encore Members" and "Navyist Rewards" are the same thing, say so. Add a single parenthetical ("Encore / Navyist Members") so loyal shoppers don't feel confused.
- **Fix the cropped right-edge element** in the hero — whatever "De..." is supposed to be, it needs to either render correctly or be hidden on mobile viewport.
