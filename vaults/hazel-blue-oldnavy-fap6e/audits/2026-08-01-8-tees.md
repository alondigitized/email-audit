# 👕 $8 TEES
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy running its familiar stacked-offer playbook again: a jeans/30%-off banner up top, a "$6/$8 Tees, 2 Days Only" hero, then PowerChill, then clearance, then jeans again. The subject "$8 TEES" is clean and specific, but the email doesn't actually lead with that offer — the first thing you see after the nav bar is a jeans + Encore Member banner, not the tees the subject promised.
- Execution is clean: no broken images, no overlapping text, bold consistent branding throughout. But this reads like the same creative DNA as the 8/1 "Ooooh! $8 tees + EXTRA 30% off" send already in the brand history (which scored 9.00) — same $6 men's/$8 women's tee pricing, same PowerChill $10 block, same clearance push. If this is a near-duplicate resend on the same day, it's cadence fatigue, not a fresh campaign beat.
- Solid, on-brand, technically sound — but unfocused. Five competing offers and a subject/hero mismatch keep this from being a top-tier send.

## What's working

- Render is clean end-to-end — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem-ipsum.
- Pricing is bold and unmissable: oversized red "$6" (Men's) / "$8" (Women's) numerals on the tees hero.
- Urgency is date-stamped and credible ("2 DAYS ONLY... Ends Sunday, 8/2"; "Online Exclusive, Ends 8/9").
- Loyalty value is surfaced early ("Become an Encore Member... free shipping every day on orders $50+, JOIN FOR FREE").
- Size selectors (XS–XXL, up to 4X on the blue tee) show real product availability, not just a generic banner.

## What's weak

- Subject says "$8 TEES" but the first content block on open is a jeans + 30%-off banner, not tees — the promised hero isn't what the eye lands on.
- Preview text trails into raw boilerplate: "Plus, all jeans on sale from $22 View in web browser." — real copy undercut by link junk.
- Five stacked offers (jeans 30% off → tees $6/$8 → PowerChill $10 → clearance → jeans $22 again) mean there's no single "the deal" — it's an offer dump.
- Every section repeats the identical generic "SHOP NOW" CTA instead of naming what you're shopping.
- This closely mirrors the already-audited 8/1 "$8 tees + EXTRA 30% off" send — likely the same-day duplicate creative, adding to an already dense promo cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the tees hero to match the subject — move the jeans/30%-off banner below the fold so the first screen delivers what was promised.
- 2. Cut to one hero offer; push PowerChill, clearance, and the repeat jeans-$22 block into a secondary "more ways to save" section instead of stacking five offers at equal visual weight.
- 3. Fix preheader hygiene so the real preview line doesn't bleed into "View in web browser."
- 4. Swap the repeated "SHOP NOW" for section-specific CTAs — "Shop Tees," "Shop PowerChill," "Shop Clearance."
- 5. If this is a same-day resend of the 8/1 creative, hold it or differentiate the offer to avoid fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tees from $6 (yes, really) — 2 days only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$8 tees + jeans from $22, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Men's tees $6, women's $8 — plus 30% off everything else`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `2 days only: grab tees before they're gone`
