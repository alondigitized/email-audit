# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly what I came here for. Old Navy is in full Long Weekend Sale mode — big "50% OFF DEALS" hero, family on the beach, and a timer telling me to hurry. This feels like a brand that knows it's talking to moms who are already mentally budget-planning summer wardrobes for the whole crew, and it's not wasting my time trying to be fancy about it.

## What's working

- **The 50% off hero** — no ambiguity, no "up to" hedge. It says 50% off, and then backs it up with "3,744 styles on sale." That number makes me trust it's real.
- **The family image** — that's a dad, a mom, and two kids, mixed ages, in coordinating summer prints. That's literally my shopping list right there. I'm not the target of a solo model campaign; I'm the mom buying for everyone.
- **"Encore Members" free shipping banner** — it's the very first thing above the header, which means Old Navy knows returning members are worth flagging immediately. That's smart placement.
- **The scrolling category strip** — ALL TEES, ALL TANKS, ALL SHORTS, ALL DRESSES, ALL SWIM — I can see where I'm going before I even scroll. That's a one-tap shortcut for my shopping brain.
- **"HURRY! Deals End Soon"** — even without a date, it registers. Long Weekend sales genuinely do end, so I believe it enough to keep moving.

## What's weak

- **No specific deadline** — "Deals End Soon" is doing a lot of work without a date stamp. If it said "Ends Monday 5/26," I'd be adding to cart right now instead of maybe coming back.
- **"Encore Members" vs. what I call it in my head** — I've always known it as Navyist Rewards. If they've rebranded, I'm momentarily confused about whether my status/points transferred. Tiny friction, but real.
- **No kids-specific entry point above the fold** — the category strip shows adult categories (Tees, Tanks, Shorts, Dresses, Swim), but I shop heavy in Kids and I have to assume Kids is somewhere in that scroll. Not surfaced upfront.
- **No Super Cash / loyalty balance reference** — as a returning member who tracks Super Cash like it's a sport, seeing my balance or a "Use Your Super Cash" nudge right here would have converted me faster.

## Recommendations

- **Add a countdown timer or specific end date** to the "HURRY!" strip — "Ends Monday at midnight" costs nothing to write and moves me to act.
- **Add a Kids entry point** in the scrolling category strip or as a secondary CTA tile below the hero — right now the strip reads like it's for me personally, not for my whole household.
- **Surface Super Cash / Navyist Rewards balance** for logged-in users in the header or just below the hero — a "$15 Super Cash ready to use" nudge would be the single highest-converting thing on this page for return customers like me.
- **Swap the Encore Members banner copy** to include the program's familiar name (Navyist Rewards) if this is a rebrand — or add a brief "formerly Navyist" note so I don't hesitate.
