# It's go-time: 50% off ALL activewear
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's third activewear-at-50%-off send in under 48 hours — following both the morning and afternoon emails on June 27 — and the creative team knows it. The "2 DAYS ONLY!" countdown badge slapped onto the hero is the giveaway: with no new offer to lead on, urgency is doing all the heavy lifting. The hero itself is clean and bright, the layout is error-free, and the product grid underneath is well-organized, but the email is essentially a repackaging of yesterday's afternoon send ("Get into it! Grab 50% off ALL activewear") with a new subject hook.
- The secondary "Red Door Deals" section (40%–70% off) is actually the more compelling value proposition buried mid-email. Deeper discounts that weren't the lead yesterday deserve a headline, not second billing. The "HOT NEW GEAR" section at the bottom echoes the TrekTech launch from June 27's afternoon send — and if you opened that one, this feels like a retread.
- Solid execution on a tired brief. The template is doing its job; the strategy is the problem.

## What's working

- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency badge** — the orange countdown sits above the fold and gives subscribers a reason this isn't just yesterday's email recycled
- **Clean, unbroken layout** — no render artifacts, no broken images, text and images sit correctly throughout
- **Secondary deal depth** — the 40%–70% Red Door Deals section adds meaningful value below the 50% activewear hero
- **"HOT NEW GEAR" lifestyle section** — the athletic lifestyle imagery in the lower half earns attention on its own and gives the TrekTech line a visual push

## What's weak

- **Three activewear-at-50%-off sends in 48 hours** — subscribers who opened June 27's two emails will experience this as a literal duplicate; the subject line "go-time" doesn't signal enough novelty to overcome inbox fatigue
- **Red Door Deals buried** — the deeper 40%–70% discounts are the sharpest hook in the email and they appear well below the hero fold
- **"ALL" caps in the subject** — minor, but combined with the ALL-CAPS "2 DAYS ONLY" banner in the body, it edges toward the shouting tone that the June 24 send ("IT'S TRUE!") got penalized for
- **No loyalty/member callout** — multiple recent sends have skipped member-specific pricing or rewards language entirely

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the deeper discount** — flip the hierarchy: make "40–70% off Red Door Deals" the hero and let 50% off activewear be the supporting panel. That's a genuinely bigger offer and it makes this email feel different from yesterday.
- 2. **Kill or merge this send if the June 27 evening activewear email already went** — three near-identical sends in 48 hours erodes list health faster than any single discount recovers it; batch the urgency into one "Final 2 Days" send with a single countdown.
- 3. **Rewrite the subject to surface the deeper discount** — "40–70% off" is more punchy than a repeated 50%.
- 4. **Add member pricing or loyalty language** — even a single line ("NavyCash members get early access") creates segmentation the rubric rewards.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 days left: up to 70% off + 50% off all activewear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours: Red Door Deals up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop before it's gone — Red Door picks from $8, activewear at half price.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 70% off Red Door + 50% off activewear. Ends soon.`

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**WARN — Missing `alt` text on tracking pixel**
```html
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/076c1a63..." width="1" height="1">
```
The pixel has no `alt=""` attribute. While a 1×1 pixel is decorative, an explicit empty `alt=""` is required for strict accessibility conformance (WCAG 1.1.1) and suppresses screen-reader noise.

The open-beacon pixel at `click.email.oldnavy.com/open.aspx?...` also has `alt=""` — that one is correctly handled.

**INFO — CSS duplicate selectors**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each assigned twice in the mobile media query (`space-top5`/`space-top10` share `.MspaceTop`; `space-bot5`/`space-bot10` share `.MspaceBot`). The second rule silently overrides the first. Low severity, but unexpected spacing behavior can occur at mobile breakpoints.

**INFO — Outlook `td { line-height: 0px }` override**
The MSO conditional block sets `td { line-height: 0px; mso-line-height-rule: exactly }` globally, relying on inline overrides to restore correct spacing. Any `<td>` that lacks an explicit `line-height` inline style will collapse in Outlook 2013+.

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header not captured**
The QA relay did not surface a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to render the one-click unsubscribe UI; its absence (or relay-drop) degrades user unsubscribe UX and risks inbox placement.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found**
One-click machine unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender policy as of Feb 2024) depends on this header. If it's genuinely absent rather than relay-stripped, this is a compliance gap with major ISP requirements.

**WARN — No physical mailing address detected in HTML source**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. The truncated source does not include one. If it is present further down in the footer (outside the provided truncation), this warning is a false positive — but it should be confirmed.

**INFO — SPF/DKIM authentication status unknown**
`Authentication-Results` header not forwarded by the AgentMail relay. Sending domain is `email.oldnavy.com`; authentication posture cannot be confirmed from available data.
