# School days are here 🚌 50% OFF ALL kids & baby looks*
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy finally makes the pivot that the July 12-17 dress drumbeat was building toward — Back to School as a standalone theme, anchored by a real offer (50% off all kids & baby). The creative commits to the season: colorful BTS backpacks, a kid in a school-ready outfit, a "2 DAYS ONLY!" urgency badge. If this landed in a parent's inbox, it would land hard.
- The problem is the incoherence lower in the scroll. After the kids/baby BTS block and a clearance grid, the email pivots cold into the Low Rise jean story — the same adult denim editorial from the July 15 "Behind the seams" send. Two completely separate campaigns duct-taped into one email with no connective tissue. A subscriber who opened for kids savings has to visually make sense of adult fashion in the lower half.
- Cadence is the other wound. This is email number seven-plus from Old Navy this week. Even a strong offer gets tuned out at this frequency. The subject line is also borderline long on mobile, and the kids/baby focus is off-target for any adult-only subscriber.

## What's working

- **50% off all kids & baby is a real, concrete offer** — not a murky "up to" softener; ALL is doing real work here
- **"2 DAYS ONLY!" badge is prominently placed** in the hero, above the fold, giving the urgency visual priority
- **BTS creative is clean and committed** — the colorful backpack grid and the kid model lock the season in immediately
- **Extra 30% off clearance header** stacks a second reason to click without cannibalizing the hero
- **Clearance from $3.99** anchor price at the bottom of the first scroll is a strong secondary hook for value shoppers

## What's weak

- **Low Rise jeans section below the fold is a campaign mismatch** — adult denim editorial in a kids BTS send destroys the email's single-minded momentum
- **Subject line runs long** — at roughly 53 characters with the bus emoji, it clips on most mobile clients
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — seven-plus Old Navy sends in a week means even a sharp offer gets dismissed before it's read
- **No CTA button copy visible above the fold** — the hero is heavy on sale language but the primary action isn't a crisp, labeled button in view
- **The asterisk on "50% OFF ALL"** signals exclusions that undercut the word "ALL" — the fine print qualifier is visible enough to create distrust

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the Low Rise block entirely** — save it for its own dedicated resend or an adult-focused day. One campaign per email. The July 15 Low Rise send already told this story well.
- 2. **Tighten the subject line for mobile** — drop below 50 chars with the emoji counted.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ALL kids & baby 🚌 Back to school starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `School's in: 50% off every kids & baby look*`
- 3. **Write a preheader that earns its space** — the preview text should name a specific hero category (uniforms, backpacks, graphic tees) rather than echoing the subject.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Uniforms, graphic tees, backpacks — all 50% off for 2 days only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock up before the bell: every kids & baby style, half off`
- 4. **Make the asterisk exclusion explicit in the hero** — one short line ("excl. new arrivals") kills the fine-print anxiety without burying it in the footer.
- 5. **Pull cadence back to every other day** — seven sends in a week is actively burning the list; Old Navy's July open rates will reflect it.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent** — not captured by AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail require this header for one-click unsubscribe; its absence or relay-stripping risks the email being flagged or suppressed by Gmail's bulk sender enforcement (effective Feb 2024, mandatory for >5k/day senders).

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent** — one-click unsubscribe (POST method) not confirmed. Required by Gmail bulk sender policy.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in visible HTML** — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address. This may be in the untruncated footer; cannot confirm from source provided. If absent, this is a hard CAN-SPAM violation.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent** — SPF/DKIM pass status unknown. Sending domain `email.oldnavy.com` should have both aligned; inability to confirm through the relay is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a live failure.
