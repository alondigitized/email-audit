# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is very clearly coming in hot for summer — the Disney collab is front and center and honestly as a mom of two I get it immediately, my kids are going to lose their minds. There are real deals stacked up top ($5/$10/$15!), the Encore Members free-shipping hook is visible, and the family in the hero looks like my actual life. This feels like a brand that knows who's buying clothes for a household of four, not just the fashion crowd.

## What's working

- **"$5! $10! $15! Deals" green strip** — this hits exactly where I live. I see that and I'm already thinking about how many basics I can stack before school starts. The specific price tiers remove all ambiguity.
- **"Extra $10 Off When You Spend $75+ — Ends 5/16"** — a dated, dollar-specific offer. I know what I need to spend and I know I have five days. This is how you talk to me.
- **Encore Members free-shipping hook** — visible in the second banner. As a Navyist member, seeing that threshold ($50+) reminds me that I'm close to qualifying just by adding one item.
- **Family hero imagery** — dad, young daughter, mom with balloons. That is my summer Saturday. Not models, not runway. A real-looking family in color-coordinated outfits.
- **Old Navy x Disney callout** — I know my kids will beg for anything with the Mickey logo. This is an instant hook for a shopping trip I was probably going to make anyway.

## What's weak

- **No kids category shortcut visible** — I'm shopping for two kids and myself. I have to tap the hamburger menu to find Boys or Girls. That's friction when I know exactly where I want to go.
- **"Encore Members" naming** — I've always known this as Navyist Rewards. Is this a rebrand? Did my points transfer? A tiny bit of doubt creeps in and I'm not immediately sure if the free-shipping perk applies to me.
- **No Super Cash reminder** — if I have Super Cash banked, I want to see it the second I open the app. It's my main reason for coming back to the site. There's no "Your Super Cash is ready" banner anywhere in this viewport.
- **Hero CTA is vague by association** — "THAT SUMMER FEELING!" and the Old Navy x Disney logo look great but there's no dedicated hero button pushing me to the collab shop or to a sale page. The only SHOP NOW visible is on the green strip, which could feel disconnected from the hero.
- **Five layers of content before the hero** — by the time my eye gets to the actual editorial imagery I've already processed two promo strips, a gap-brand tab bar, and the site header. That's a lot of cognitive load on a phone screen.

## Recommendations

- **Add a horizontal category shortcut strip below the site header** — Boys | Girls | Women | Deals — four taps, instantly reaches what a family shopper needs without opening the hamburger menu.
- **Replace or augment the Encore Members banner with a personalized Super Cash/Navyist balance reminder** for logged-in members. "You have $25 Super Cash — use it by 5/20" would drive an immediate session.
- **Name kids explicitly in the $5/$10/$15 deals strip** — change "tees, tanks, shorts, swim & dresses" to "kids tees, family tanks, shorts & more." One word shift, much higher relevance signal for the parent audience.
- **Give the Disney collab hero a visible CTA button** — "Shop the Collab" right on the hero image would close the gap between the beautiful family scene and actual conversion.
