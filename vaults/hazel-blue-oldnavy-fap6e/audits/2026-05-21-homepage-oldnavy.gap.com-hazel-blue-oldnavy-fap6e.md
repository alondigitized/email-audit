# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is actually really good for me. Old Navy is leading with a "Long Weekend Sale — 50% Off Deals" hero that shows a family — mom, dad, boy, girl — all wearing matching-ish summer stuff, which is basically my entire summer shopping list in one photo. The brand is clearly in full Memorial Day mode and it's pushing a sitewide sale hard. This is the kind of homepage that makes me stop scrolling and actually tap something.

## What's working

- **The family hero photo.** A mom, a dad, a boy, a girl all in coordinating summer prints — this is my crew. I see myself in this image immediately, which almost never happens on retail homepages.
- **"50% OFF DEALS" in massive type.** Sale-driven shopper here. That's the first thing my eye goes to and it does not disappoint.
- **"3,744 STYLES ON SALE" badge.** The specific number makes the claim feel real, not like a "select styles" bait-and-switch. That matters to me.
- **"SHOP ALL DEALS" CTA.** Big, clear, white button. One tap and I'm in the sale. No mystery about what happens when I click it.
- **Long Weekend Sale timing.** This is Memorial Day weekend. Summer swim and shorts are exactly what I'm shopping for right now.
- **Brand-family nav bar at top.** As someone who moves between Gap and Old Navy for kids' basics, seeing Gap linked right there is a nice touch.

## What's weak

- **"Encore Members" name.** I'm a Navyist Rewards member — or at least I was. If this is a rebrand, I had no idea, and it creates a half-second of "wait, is this my program?" That hesitation is a conversion killer at the top of the funnel.
- **No Super Cash mention.** Super Cash is literally the reason I plan shopping trips around Old Navy's sale calendar. If there's a Super Cash earn event tied to this sale, it's not visible here. That would be huge for me.
- **No kids-specific category CTA.** The scrollable strip says "ALL SHORTS, ALL DRESSES, ALL SWIM" — but nothing for "KIDS" or "BABY." I have to assume kids are in there. A family-sale homepage should make that assumption unnecessary.
- **No urgency signal with a hard date.** "Long Weekend" implies a deadline, but I can't see when this sale ends. An "ends Monday" badge would push me to act now instead of tabbing back to it later.

## Recommendations

- **Add "KIDS" to the above-the-fold category strip** — or pin it as a dedicated chip. The family photo tells me this sale includes kids, but the navigation doesn't confirm it without scrolling.
- **Surface Super Cash or earn-event messaging** near the CTA — even "Earn Super Cash on this order" in small text under the button would have me adding more to my cart to hit a threshold.
- **Put a hard end date on the hero.** "Ends Monday 5/26" or a countdown chip in the corner of the hero. "Long Weekend" is vague; a date creates real urgency.
- **Swap "Encore Members" to "Navyist Members" or add a clarifier** — if this is a rebrand, give existing members one line of reassurance ("Your rewards still apply") so loyalists don't have to second-guess their status.
