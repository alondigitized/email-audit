# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is leading hard with a sitewide sale today and I am not mad about it — "50% Off Everything" in giant text is exactly the kind of thing that stops my thumb mid-scroll. The Disney collab module below it gives me summer-family vibes that feel right for where I am in life. This is a clearance-rack-brain homepage: sale-first, lifestyle second, and it mostly works for me.

## What's working

- **"50% Off Everything / Ends 5/20" hero** — today is the deadline, which creates real urgency. I would absolutely open this on my lunch break and start adding to cart.
- **Free shipping $50+ for Encore Members banner** — I'm a Navyist Reward member, this speaks directly to me and is the first thing I see after the brand bar. Good placement.
- **Disney × Old Navy collab module** — the family with balloons imagery hits. A woman who looks my age, a kid, summer fun. That's the visual I want when I'm shopping for the family.
- **"Online Exclusive" label** — signals this is a deal I can't get in-store, which nudges me toward completing the purchase digitally right now.

## What's weak

- **No kids or uniforms category visible** — my biggest spend at Old Navy is kids' clothes and basics. There's no quick path to "Kids" or "Uniforms" without digging into the hamburger menu.
- **Hero is pure text, no product or lifestyle** — the dark navy slab with white text is bold but cold. I want to see what the sale looks like — a family in cute summer basics, school uniforms on sale, something that connects the discount to actual product.
- **"Exclusions apply" with no click-through clarity** — on a "50% off Everything" promo this is a red flag. I've been burned before when the kids' section is excluded. Needs a link or an inline note about what's in/out.
- **No rewards balance or Super Cash callout** — as a Navyist member mid-sale, I'd love to know if Super Cash is active or if I'm earning on this purchase. That info is nowhere.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids & Baby" or "Uniforms" quick-tap module** below the hero so moms like me can skip straight to our category instead of navigating the hamburger.
- **Swap the text-only hero background for a lifestyle shot** of a mom + kids in the sale product — keep the "50% Off" headline but anchor it to real clothing so I know what I'm shopping.
- **Add a Super Cash earning callout** ("Earn Super Cash on this purchase") directly on the hero — this is a massive purchase driver for Navyist members who plan redemption cycles.
- **Clarify the exclusions inline** — something like "Kids, Uniforms & Adult Basics included — exclusions: Gap Factory, clearance" would prevent bounce from skeptical sale shoppers.
