# 50% off everything* ENDING SOON (+ get $17 PowerSoft leggings for the workout girlies!)
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the first Old Navy send in the recent run that actually delivers what its subject promises: "workout girlies" get a real PowerSoft Leggings callout ($17 women's / $14 girls) right below the fold, not a bait-and-switch like the July 28 "$12 PJs" disaster. The hero-to-leggings-to-activewear-tops sequence is genuinely well-ordered for a fitness-apparel shopper, and the pricing is loud and unambiguous.
- The problem is everything else. This is the same "50% Off Everything" purple hero this brand has run at least six times in the last ten days (July 20, 26 ×2, 27, 28, 29), just re-skinned with a leggings hook bolted on. The subject line is 87 characters — nearly double the mobile-friendly ceiling — cramming two separate offers ("ENDING SOON" storewide sale + "$17 leggings") into one line that reads like it was written by committee.
- Net: solid product-market fit for this specific persona, undermined by fatigue-inducing cadence and subject-line bloat. Middle-of-the-pack send that earns points on execution it's lost on repetition.

## What's working

- The subject's core promise (PowerSoft Leggings, $17) is fulfilled almost immediately — a real product photo with $14/$17 pricing, unlike the prior two sends' pajama no-shows.
- Visual hierarchy is clean: purple "50% OFF EVERYTHING" hero → "TODAY ONLY" urgency banner → leggings hero → activewear tops, no overlapping text or broken assets anywhere in the render.
- Category nav (Women/Men/Maternity/Girls/Boys/etc.) sits right under the hero for fast wayfinding.
- Encore Member free-shipping banner is visible near the top, giving loyalty shoppers a reason to stick around.
- Fall seasonal framing ("Haul Into Fall") is consistent throughout rather than stale leftover copy.

## What's weak

- Subject line is 87 characters — it'll truncate on nearly every mobile inbox, burying the "$17 leggings" hook that's the actual differentiator here.
- This is the same storewide 50%-off purple hero as five-plus prior sends this cycle; a subscriber who's opened any of the July 26-28 emails has seen this exact layout already.
- Preview text tacks "View in web browser." onto the end of otherwise decent copy — that's UI chrome leaking into the preview, not real preheader content.
- No single dominant CTA — "SHOP NOW" repeats across seven-plus sections (leggings, tops, categories, jeans), so nothing anchors as *the* primary click.
- No size/fit/availability info or reviews on the leggings, despite that being the exact product the subject is trying to sell.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split the subject into one offer, not two — lead with the leggings hook since it's the actual differentiator versus every other "50% off everything" send this brand has fired off. Drop "ENDING SOON" from the subject and let the in-body "TODAY ONLY" banner carry urgency instead.
- 2. Cut the storewide 50%-hero to a smaller banner and promote the PowerSoft Leggings shot to the true hero — it's the only genuinely fresh creative in this send.
- 3. Fix the preview text so it stops with the free-shipping line and drops the "View in web browser" tail.
- 4. Add one line of product detail under the leggings (sizes available, "4-way stretch," etc.) — the subject sells a specific product, the body should back it up.
- 5. Slow the cadence — six near-identical 50%-off-everything sends in ten days is going to train subscribers to stop opening.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your leggings upgrade: $17, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on $50+ for Encore Members`
