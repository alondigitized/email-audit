# Today only: 50% off everything* in women's, accessories included
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's return to its core women's offer after the May 27 creative detour into graphic tees — and it lands cleanly. "TODAY ONLY!" in the hero is unambiguous, the 50%-off women's message is front and center, and the size-clearance grid lower in the email is actually the freshest piece of creative in the last two weeks of sends: it shows tiered pricing by size (XS through 3X+), which is genuinely useful rather than just decorative.
- The problem isn't the email — it's the inbox. This arrives as roughly the 16th or 17th Old Navy send in about a week. The subject carries "Today only" urgency, but anyone who's been following this cadence knows the "today only" sale will be replaced by a different "today only" sale tomorrow. The urgency has been completely drained by repetition.
- Secondary offers — Kids & Baby Sale ($2/$4/$6/$8), 25% off New Arrivals, $10 Activewear Shorts — dilute the single-minded women's 50% message. The email works best if you scroll only the first two panels; past that it becomes a catalog page.

## What's working

- "TODAY ONLY!" hero is bold and impossible to miss — the eye lands on the offer immediately.
- Including accessories explicitly ("accessories included") in the subject is smart; it expands perceived value of the 50% offer beyond apparel.
- The SHOP CLEARANCE IN YOUR SIZE size/price grid is the standout creative element — it communicates relevance at a per-size level, which no other recent Old Navy send has done.
- Clean render, no broken images or layout collapse across the full scroll.
- The women's model in the hero (wide-leg denim, layered look) is on-trend and seasonally appropriate for early summer.

## What's weak

- The asterisk on "everything*" in the subject undermines the headline — experienced Old Navy shoppers know this means significant exclusions, and it erodes trust before the email is even opened.
- Kids & Baby Sale doesn't belong here. The subject promises a women's send; pivoting to $2 kids' tees partway through is a jarring context switch for a shopper without kids.
- No social proof anywhere — no star ratings, no "bestseller" badges, nothing. For a sale this broad, anchoring even one hero product with reviews would lift click confidence.
- The 25% New Arrivals panel competes directly with the 50% sale panel — training subscribers that even "New Arrivals" get discounted immediately degrades future full-price launch credibility.
- The urgency is hollow given recent send cadence. After six consecutive days of "today only / ending soon / long weekend sale," the deadline reads as boilerplate.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the Kids & Baby panel for this send.** Keep it women's start to finish. If kids needs its own send, give it one. Splitting the creative here weakens both offers.
- 2. **Drop the asterisk from the hero — clarify what's included upfront.** Instead of "50% off everything*" use "50% off dresses, tops, denim & more" so the offer is honest and specific without the fine-print hedge at the lede.
- 3. **Add one social-proof anchor** — e.g., a "★★★★★ · 2,400 reviews" badge on the hero product. Even one signal breaks the clinical promo feel.
- 4. **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: half off women's styles + accessories`
- 5. **Subject Alt B:** `50% off women's — yes, accessories too`
- 6. **Preheader Alt A:** `Dresses, denim, tops — plus every bag and accessory. Ends tonight.`
- 7. **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop clearance in your size, too — plus new arrivals at 25% off.`
