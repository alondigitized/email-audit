# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is going full tilt on a 4th of July clearance event — 40%-70% off in giant white type, "STARTING AT $2" stamped at the bottom, the whole thing. As a deal hunter and Navyist Rewards member, I appreciate the boldness. But as someone who came here specifically to stock up on kids' uniforms and basics for four people in my house, this homepage is sending me zero signals that it knows I exist. It's a sale party I want to go to, but I have to figure out where to shop on my own.

## What's working

- **The 4th of July sale hero** — "40%–70% OFF DEEP DEALS" is exactly the kind of number that makes me stop scrolling. This is my language.
- **Encore Member free shipping banner** — "$50+ for Encore Members / Sign In or Join Now" is immediate value for loyalty members like me. I see that and I know I'm in the right place.
- **"STARTING AT $2" callout** — that red wave at the bottom of the screen is a magnet. That's clearance-rack energy in digital form and I love it.
- **"Sale" in the top nav** — it's right there, first few tabs. One tap and I'm into the event without digging through categories.
- **Seasonal relevance** — the page is exactly right for the date. There's nothing worse than opening a site on a holiday and seeing a stale campaign.

## What's weak

- **No kids or family category in sight** — there is absolutely nothing above the fold signaling that Old Navy sells children's clothing, let alone uniform basics. I have to already know that they do.
- **No product shop CTA in the hero** — the biggest section on the page has no "Shop Now" or "Shop the Sale" button. The only button I can see is "APPLY NOW" for a credit card. That's backwards.
- **Credit card banner is the first thing I see** — before any product, before any category, the page is asking me to open a new credit card. I'm here to buy shorts for my kids, not apply for financing.
- **Hero is all type, no product** — there's not a single photo of clothing, a person, or a lifestyle scene. Just graphic design. I don't get a feel for what's actually on sale.
- **"LIMITED TIME ONLY" with no actual deadline** — that's vague urgency. When does it end? Tonight? Next week? Give me a date or a timer.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids' Sale" CTA button to the hero** — even just a secondary button below the main offer. Mom-of-two shoppers need a path to their category without hunting.
- **Swap or supplement the hero with a lifestyle photo** — a quick shot of a family getting ready for a 4th of July cookout in Old Navy gear would do more than the current pure-graphic treatment.
- **Replace "LIMITED TIME ONLY" with an actual end date** — "Ends July 6" creates real urgency without feeling like a trick.
- **Move the credit card pitch below the fold** — or at minimum, flip the two banners so free shipping leads and the credit card upsell follows. The current order makes me feel like I'm being sold before I've shopped.
