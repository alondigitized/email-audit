# Workout drawer: refreshed ✨ Snag 50% OFF all active*
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the second consecutive activewear 50%-off email from Old Navy in as many days — yesterday's "HALF OFF all activewear* — complete workout perfection" sent this exact same offer, and a subscriber who opened that email will recognize this immediately. The creative refresh ("Workout drawer: refreshed," "Hot New Gear" banner, member pricing callout) is real but thin: the hero still reads "2 DAYS ONLY! 50%" against workout models, which is essentially a reskin of yesterday's send.
- Inside the email, the execution is solid. The hero is clean and energy-forward, the $14+ member pricing strip adds a loyalty layer yesterday lacked, and the "HOT NEW GEAR" section below the fold offers a genuine new-arrivals angle that could have led the email as the differentiating hook. The Clearance stacking at the bottom adds urgency and discovery, but landing there requires significant scroll.
- The fundamental problem is strategic, not executional: if suppression of yesterday's openers wasn't applied, this email is wasting send budget and eroding list trust. If a different segment is receiving it, the render has no signal of that — it reads as a generic re-blast.

## What's working

- **Hero is crisp and immediate** — "2 DAYS ONLY! 50%" reads in under a second, above the fold, with bright activewear imagery that matches the offer.
- **Member pricing strip** — "$14+ ACTIVEWEAR MEMBER PRICES" is a genuine loyalty hook that adds specificity yesterday's email didn't have.
- **"HOT NEW GEAR" section** — introduces a newness angle that separates the send from the clearance/deal framing; gives aspirational shoppers a reason to engage.
- **Clearance stacking** — the bottom Clearance section provides a distinct secondary value prop and a reason to scroll for bargain-focused shoppers.
- **No render bugs** — images load, layout is intact, hierarchy is legible at thumbnail size.

## What's weak

- **Cadence failure** — this is the second 50%-off activewear email in 24 hours; the subject line tweak doesn't mask the repeat offer to any subscriber who opened yesterday.
- **Subject buries the urgency** — "Workout drawer: refreshed ✨" is a soft, lifestyle-tone opener that pushes the actual 50% off to the second half; the "2 DAYS ONLY" clock doesn't appear in the subject at all.
- **New arrivals angle is wasted** — "HOT NEW GEAR" is legitimately differentiating but it's buried mid-scroll; if new product is the hook, it should be in the subject or at least the preheader.
- **Female-only model casting** — the entire email skews women's activewear with zero men's or mixed-gender imagery, narrowing the perceived relevance for non-female subscribers receiving this.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with what's new, not just what's discounted.** "HOT NEW GEAR" is the freshest angle in this email — move it to the hero or at minimum call it out in the subject. "New activewear drops, now 50% off" beats a repeat of yesterday's half-off frame.
- 2. **Apply open-suppression on yesterday's activewear send.** Serving the same offer twice in 24 hours to the same openers is the highest-leverage fix; no amount of copy polish recovers that.
- 3. **Add deadline copy to the subject.** "2 DAYS ONLY" lives only in the body; pull it or a hard end-date into the subject to justify the urgency from the inbox.
- 4. **Include a men's or unisex activewear module.** One mixed-gender strip broadens relevance without restructuring the email.
- 5. **Surface member pricing earlier.** The "$14+" strip is doing real work; swap it above the fold or reference it in the preheader.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Member prices from $14 — fresh drops now in the sale`
