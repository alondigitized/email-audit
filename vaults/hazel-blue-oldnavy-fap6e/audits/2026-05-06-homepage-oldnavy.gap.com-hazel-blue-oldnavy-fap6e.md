# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, honestly? This feels like Old Navy is more interested in getting me to open a credit card than helping me shop. The "U.S. of YAY!" campaign is cute and very on-brand patriotic-summer vibes, but I came here to find basics for my kids and check my Super Cash balance — and the whole above-the-fold experience is pushing a credit card application at me. I know Old Navy well, but this landing didn't exactly roll out the welcome mat for a regular Navyist member like me.

## What's working

- **Free shipping threshold ($50+ for Encore Members):** That bar is totally realistic for me — a few uniform polos and I'm already there. It's visible, specific, and actually useful.
- **"Sign In or Join Now" member hook:** At least there's a path to my account without hunting through a hamburger menu.
- **"U.S. of YAY!" hero campaign:** The seasonal vibe is fun. The American flag sweater is cute and the woman in the shot is my age, casual style — I don't feel like I wandered into a teen store.
- **Clean render:** No layout chaos, no broken images, no pop-up covering the whole screen the second I land. I appreciate that more than brands realize.

## What's weak

- **"APPLY NOW" as the loudest CTA:** The most prominent action on this page is opening a credit card. I already have my loyalty membership — I want to *shop*, not apply for financing.
- **Zero kids or family presence above the fold:** Kids' basics and uniforms are a huge reason I'm here. There's no visual signal that Old Navy even carries kids' clothing until I scroll or dig into the nav.
- **No Super Cash mention:** I am literally a "Super Cash redemption pro" and this page doesn't acknowledge that I might be sitting on a reward to spend. That's a layup conversion they're missing.
- **30% off is conditional and asterisked:** That discount is only for new credit card applicants, which doesn't apply to me. The banner feels like a bait — I see "30% off" and then realize it's not for me. Mildly annoying.
- **No shop categories or product entry points:** There's no "Kids," "Girls," "Boys," or "Women's" shortcut above the fold. I have to open the hamburger menu just to start shopping.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Redeem Super Cash" or "Your Rewards" banner for signed-in members** — this is the single highest-conversion hook for Navyist members and it's completely absent from the above-the-fold experience.
- **Swap or supplement the hero with a family/kids lifestyle shot** — even a split-panel (women's left, kids right) would signal that Old Navy is a whole-family store, not just a women's fashion brand.
- **Replace "APPLY NOW" as the primary CTA with "SHOP THE SALE" or "SHOP KIDS"** — the credit card push belongs in a secondary banner, not the loudest button above the fold.
- **Surface a category shortcut strip** (Women / Kids / Baby / Men) directly below the nav bar — one-tap entry points would dramatically reduce friction for a return visitor like me who knows exactly what she wants.
