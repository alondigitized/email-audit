# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay honestly, Old Navy is doing its thing right now — this is the sale-forward, family-friendly vibe I know and love. The page is loud about deals in all the right ways: specific dollar amounts, a Disney collab drop, and a free-shipping hook right at the top. It feels like summer is officially happening and they're trying to get me to spend before the weekend.

## What's working

- **The $5/$10/$15 price points** in the hero are exactly what I scan for. As a mom outfitting two kids, seeing concrete numbers instead of "up to X% off" is the difference between me clicking and me leaving.
- **The Disney x Old Navy collab** is smart placement for a millennial mom — that family shot with the daughter between the parents hits exactly right. My kids will lose their minds over a Disney Old Navy tee.
- **Two-layer deal stack** — the "Extra $10 Off $75+" banner sitting on top of the "50% off" hero means I'm already doing math in my head, which means I'm engaged.
- **Encore Members free shipping** visible without scrolling is a nice loyalty signal, even if I need to check whether that's my tier.
- **"Sign In or Join Now"** nudges me to log in, which is smart — logged-in Hazel spends more.

## What's weak

- **"Ends 5/16" = today** — I noticed that immediately. That's today's date, which either means I caught it in time (urgency works) or the deal is basically over by the time I'm reading this (feels stale). Mixed bag.
- **No kids-specific visual above the fold** — the hero is about the overall 50% off event, not specifically kids or uniforms. I have to take it on faith that my sizes are included.
- **"Encore Members" vs. "Navyist Rewards"** — I know I'm a Navyist member, but the banner says "Encore Members" specifically. Am I Encore? Is that a tier? I don't immediately know if that free shipping applies to me without tapping "Details."
- **No Super Cash callout visible** — as someone who waits for Super Cash events, I'm always looking for that banner. It's not here, which is a missed hook for me specifically.
- **The hero is 100% text** — "50% Off Deals" is great copy but it's sitting on a plain blue rectangle. No product visual, no kid, nothing for my eye to land on beyond the words.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the flat blue hero with a lifestyle shot** of a mom at a pool or playground with kids in $10 graphic tees — text overlay can stay, but give me a reason to aspire, not just a blue wall.
- **Explicitly call out "Exclusions don't include kids basics"** (or the reverse, confirm kids ARE included) — that one sentence would save me a click and build trust.
- **Clarify the Encore/Navyist tier relationship** in the banner — something like "Navyist members get free shipping at $50" with a "Sign in to check your tier" link removes the friction immediately.
- **Add a Super Cash countdown or teaser** if one is upcoming — even "Super Cash drops in X days" would get me checking back and pre-loading my cart.
