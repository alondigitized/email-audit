# A steal! Snag dresses for summer for $6, $8, $10 & $12 + 50% off all tees, tanks, shorts, dresses & swim
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's thirteenth email in a five-day blitz, and the creative is essentially identical to the "ON SALE NOW" send from earlier today (May 23) — same "MAJOR DRESS SALE!" hero, same model in the white dress, same $6/$8/$10/$12 tier stack, same 50%-off-everything band. The only material change is the subject line, which appears to be a resend to non-openers. That's a legitimate tactic, but the new subject is the wrong direction: the original was punchy and under 50 chars; this one runs to ~103 characters and will be mangled by nearly every inbox client.
- The clearance section at the bottom is the one structural addition — a bold red typographic block repeating "Clearance" in large stacked text. It's visually arresting but tonally jarring against the dress-sale hero above. It reads like two separate promotions welded together.
- Verdict: operationally fine for a non-opener resend, but the longer subject undermines the one thing that could have moved the needle. Score drops slightly vs. the earlier same-day send.

## What's working

- **Hero is still clean and unambiguous** — "MAJOR DRESS SALE!" lands immediately; price badges ($10 prominently, tiers implied) are visible above the fold.
- **50%-off band covers the full-width product grid** — tees, tanks, shorts, dresses, swim all represented visually, so category breadth reads at a glance.
- **Memorial Day section** adds a seasonal peg that gives the sale a reason-to-exist beyond pure promotion.
- **Clearance typography is distinctive** — the stacked red "Clearance" block is visually memorable and will register even on a scroll-through.

## What's weak

- **Subject line is ~103 characters** — gutted on mobile; most inboxes will truncate after "for $6, $8, $10 &" leaving the 50%-off offer completely invisible.
- **Thirteenth email in five days** — subscriber fatigue is real; "A steal!" framing doesn't disguise the cadence problem.
- **No visible deadline** — Memorial Day implies end-of-weekend urgency, but nothing in the hero or body copy pins a specific cutoff date or "ends tonight" signal.
- **Clearance section feels grafted on** — the tonal shift from aspirational dress sale to red-on-red clearance blocks disrupts the read flow and dilutes the hero message.
- **No loyalty/member pricing callout** — Old Navy's NavyCard / rewards program isn't surfaced anywhere visible, a missed signal for loyal subscribers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the subject to under 50 characters.** The laundry-list format ("tees, tanks, shorts, dresses & swim") guarantees truncation. Lead with the sharpest hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dresses from $6 — the steal of the weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$6 dresses + 50% off everything* this weekend`
- 2. **Add a visible deadline in the hero** — "Ends Monday" or a countdown badge directly in the hero image would do more conversion work than any copy change.
- 3. **Either commit to the clearance section or cut it** — if clearance is a real priority, give it its own send; if not, remove it and keep the dress-sale story coherent end-to-end.
- 4. **Surface member pricing or NavyCard benefit** — even a one-liner ("NavyCard holders get an extra X% off") would reward loyalty subscribers and improve relevance.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends Monday — $6 dresses + 50% off tees, tanks & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop before the weekend's over: dresses from $6, half off everything else`
