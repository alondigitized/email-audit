# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy came to PLAY today. The second this page loaded I saw "50% Off Everything" in giant text and honestly my thumb was already hovering over the button. This homepage is pushing a sitewide sale hard — backed up by a Disney collab that is genuinely cute for moms with little kids — and they're dangling the Encore Members free-shipping hook right at the top where I can't miss it.

## What's working

- **"50% Off Everything | Ends 5/20"** — this is the single most motivating thing a sale-driven Navyist like me can see. Specific percentage, specific end date. I feel urgency immediately.
- **Encore Members free shipping banner** — it's the very first content on the page, above the logo. I didn't have to hunt for my loyalty perk; it found me.
- **Disney x Old Navy collab family shot** — a mom, a dad, and a little girl in matching patriotic/Disney gear holding balloons. That's a Pinterest moment and I have two kids who would absolutely want those shirts.
- **"Online Exclusive" label** — signals that this deal isn't in-store so I don't need to drag the kids out. That matters for me on a Sunday afternoon.
- **Visual hierarchy** — the hero is all white type on dark navy with a single blue CTA. My eye goes deal → deadline → button in about one second.

## What's weak

- **"Exclusions apply." fine print under "50% Off Everything"** — I've been burned by this before at Old Navy. Uniforms and activewear are often excluded, which are exactly what I'm here for. It creates instant doubt.
- **No category shortcuts visible above the fold** — there's a hamburger menu but no quick taps to Boys, Girls, or Baby. As a mom shopping for two kids, I want to jump to a department fast.
- **Disney collab imagery, while cute, isn't my buying mode** — I'm not here for limited-edition collab pieces; I'm here for $8 tank tops and uniform khakis. The lifestyle shot is pretty but doesn't show the basics that fill my cart.
- **No Super Cash callout visible** — Super Cash redemption is the #1 reason I come back to Old Navy repeatedly and there's zero mention of it here. Missed loyalty reinforcement for core repeat buyers.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Uniforms & Basics are included" badge or exclusions clarity CTA** — something like "Includes kids' uniforms" right below the "Exclusions apply" line would kill my hesitation and drive me straight to checkout.
- **Drop two or three category chips below the hero** — Girls, Boys, and Women's as tappable tiles so I can bypass the hamburger and get to my actual shopping path in one tap.
- **Swap "SHOP NOW" for "Shop the Sale"** — same action, but "Shop the Sale" is more specific and tells me I'm landing on the deal page, not just the homepage.
- **Add a Super Cash earn/burn reminder in the Encore Members banner** — something like "Earn Super Cash on this purchase | Encore Members" turns the shipping perk into a two-part loyalty hook that's much stickier for repeat buyers.
