# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so the first thing I see is a giant sale splash — 4th of July vibes, big bold numbers, 40% to 70% off. Old Navy is in full clearance-event mode right now and it's pretty impossible to miss. For me, as someone who basically waits for these exact moments to stock up on basics and uniforms for my kids, the promise is right, but the page is giving me zero context about *what* is actually on sale — I see "SHOP ALL" and a "Starting at $2" tag and that's it.

## What's working

- **The sale numbers are front and center.** "40%-70% OFF DEEP DEALS" in that huge stacked font — I clock it in under a second. Exactly what I want to see when I open a retailer app looking for deals.
- **"Starting at $2" strip** in red at the bottom of the hero — that's a hook. I will absolutely scroll to find the $2 item even if it's just a hair tie.
- **Encore Members free shipping banner.** Being greeted with a loyalty perk before I even see the nav makes me feel recognized. That $50 threshold is reachable for me when I'm shopping back-to-school basics.
- **4th of July timing is perfect.** I'm already thinking about grabbing summer stuff for the kids before the holiday weekend. This feels timely, not stale.

## What's weak

- **"SHOP ALL" as the only CTA is too vague.** A mom shopping for kid uniforms or basics doesn't want to dig through everything — I want "Shop Kids," "Shop Uniforms," or at least a category preview strip.
- **Zero product imagery.** The whole hero is abstract blue waves and text. I don't know if I'm buying shorts, tees, swimsuits, or jeans — there's nothing to pull my eye toward what I actually care about.
- **"Select styles only." fine print.** After a headline screaming 70% off, that tiny disclaimer at the bottom feels sneaky. I've been burned before clicking through expecting deep cuts and finding mostly full-price.
- **No family or lifestyle imagery.** The page has no people at all. I don't feel like this was built for me — it feels like a generic flash-sale billboard.
- **No visible category navigation.** The hamburger is there but no quick-tap links to Kids, Women's, or Uniforms above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the abstract wave background with a lifestyle hero.** Show a mom and two kids in Old Navy outfits — back-to-school vibe or backyard 4th of July. One image instantly makes this feel personal.
- **Add 2–3 category shortcut chips below the hero button.** "Shop Kids," "Shop Women's," "Shop Uniforms" — three tappable pills so I don't have to hunt through "SHOP ALL."
- **Surface the sale end date explicitly.** Change "LIMITED TIME ONLY" to "Ends July 4th" or add a small countdown. "Limited time" is vague; a real deadline creates urgency I can actually act on.
- **Move the fine print into the button area** or add a "See details" inline link right next to the headline — don't hide it below the fold edge. It'll feel more honest and reduce bounce from disappointed shoppers.
