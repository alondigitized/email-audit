# GASP 😮 50% off everything* AND $6 tees & $5 tanks
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is running the same $6 tees / $5 tanks deal from yesterday — now re-skinned with a "50% off everything*" umbrella headline and the "GASP" subject hook to make it feel fresh. The hero executes cleanly: the 50% off badge is dominant, the "2 DAYS ONLY" urgency is above the fold, and the price-point stacking ($6, $5, dresses under $25) gives the eye multiple reasons to linger. The multi-section structure — clearance → dresses → yellow → clearance again — is classic Old Navy template and it works.
- The problem is the send itself, not the email. This is the fourth Old Navy email in roughly 48 hours (May 15 shorts, two on May 16 for the same tees/tanks deal, and now this). Real subscribers have habituated. The "GASP" opener is doing a lot of work trying to make yesterday's deal feel like a new event — and it doesn't quite land.
- The asterisk on "everything*" is the quiet trust drain. It's standard Old Navy qualifying language, but paired with a subject that screams "50% off everything" it flirts with bait-and-switch territory. Any subscriber who's clicked one of these before knows the exclusions list is long.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate.** "50% Off Everything*" with the "2 DAYS ONLY" badge is legible, dominant, and above the fold on the render.
- **Price-point ladder is effective.** $6 tees / $5 tanks / dresses under $25 creates a three-rung value signal that broadens the relevance.
- **"Dream Dresses" as social proof module** — the framing implies popularity, and the variety of styles shown gives browsers a reason to keep scrolling.
- **No render bugs.** Layout is clean, images load, type is readable throughout.

## What's weak

- **Cadence is broken.** This is the fourth promo in two days — the prior May 16 send literally announced the same $6 tees / $5 tanks offer. "GASP" reads as the brand yelling louder because yesterday's email underperformed, not as a genuine new event.
- **"GASP" subject hook is low-credibility.** It's all-caps, emoji-stacked, and vague — exactly the pattern that trains subscribers to ignore a sender.
- **The asterisk on "everything*" undercuts the hero promise.** If exclusions are extensive, calling it "everything" erodes trust send-over-send.
- **Bottom clearance stack is repetitive.** "Clearance Clearance Clearance" (three separate modules) at the tail of the email dilutes the message and signals inventory desperation rather than deal curation.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the frequency before fixing the subject.** No creative change rescues a fourth near-identical email in 48 hours. Batch the price-point story into one email with a clear umbrella headline instead of drip-hammering the same deal.
- 2. **Lead with the umbrella, not the gimmick.** Drop "GASP" — lean into the 50% + stacked price-points as the subject hook.
- 3. **Trim the clearance tail.** One clearance CTA is enough; three repetitions signals that the first one isn't converting, which isn't confidence-building.
- 4. **Asterisk disclosure.** Put the key exclusion in the subhead ("excludes Athleta, Banana Republic, Gap") so "everything*" reads as honest rather than a bait.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your summer wardrobe for less: 50% off, $6 tees, $5 tanks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dream dresses under $25. Clearance starts at $4. Shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tanks, tees, dresses — all priced for summer. Offer ends soon.`

## Technical Audit — Old Navy "50% off everything" (2026-05-17)

### 1. Technical Summary

One tracking pixel endpoint returns a broken redirect (relative path, not an absolute URL), causing a hard QA failure. Compliance headers and a physical mailing address are absent from the captured message, which poses CAN-SPAM and RFC 8058 risk at scale.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Broken Salesforce Personalization pixel redirect**
`https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/b61f6570-a81c-4a8e-b578-f874e3ccffc0?...`
The endpoint resolves with a redirect to a relative path: `/g/b61f6570-a81c-4a8e-b578-f874e3ccffc0?txn=781e3b00-...`
Relative redirects are invalid for HTTP 301/302 responses in many email-client webview and proxy environments. The pixel will silently fail to fire for any client that enforces absolute redirect targets. This is likely a Salesforce Interaction Studio / Personalization beacon; the issue is on the `cp.oldnavy.com` origin server.

**[WARN] 23 click-redirect links not probed**
All CTA links route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`. Destination URLs and UTM passthrough were not verified. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute**
The `cp.oldnavy.com/o/b61f6570...` `<img>` has no `alt` attribute at all (distinct from `alt=""`). Screen readers will announce the full src URL. Fix: add `alt=""` to suppress announcement.

```html
<!-- current -->
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/b61f6570..." width="1" height="1">
<!-- fix -->
<img src="https://cp.oldnavy.com/o/b61f6570..." width="1" height="1" alt="">
```

**[INFO] Duplicate CSS selector definitions**
`.MspaceTop` and `.MspaceBot` are each defined twice in the `@media` block (once for `.space-top5`/`.space-bot5` and again for `.space-top10`/`.space-bot10`). The second declaration silently wins; the 5px variants are dead. Not rendering-breaking in practice, but a maintenance hazard.

**[INFO] Outlook `line-height: 0px` override**
The MSO conditional style sets `td { line-height: 0px }` globally. This is a known Outlook rendering hack but can cause zero-height cells if a `<td>` has no explicit height and contains only an image with a broken src.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, `{{email}}`) are present in the visible HTML source.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from captured message**
QA did not detect a `List-Unsubscribe` header. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the native unsubscribe UI; its absence increases the likelihood of spam complaints. Note: header may have been stripped by the AgentMail relay — confirm against raw SMTP delivery logs before treating as a definitive send-side gap.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) absent**
One-click unsubscribe is required by Google and Yahoo bulk-sender policies (≥5k/day). If the header is genuinely absent at send time (not just stripped in relay capture), this is a deliverability and policy compliance failure.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected in HTML**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) mandates a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. The truncated source does not include one. If it is present in the footer section not captured, this warning is a false positive — verify against full HTML.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Unverifiable due to click-redirect interception.** All 23 CTAs route through `click.email.oldnavy.com`, which was skipped during probing. The following cannot be confirmed without manually resolving a sample of links:
