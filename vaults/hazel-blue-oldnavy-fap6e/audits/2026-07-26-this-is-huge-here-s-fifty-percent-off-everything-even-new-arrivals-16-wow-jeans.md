# This is HUGE: Here's FIFTY PERCENT OFF EVERYTHING* (even new arrivals) + $16 Wow jeans
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's kitchen-sink flagship for the week — it takes the "$16 Wow Jeans" story that's been running solo or co-headline since July 20 and buries it under a bigger storewide "50% Off Everything" claim. Compared to the 7/25 send that led cleanly with jeans alone (5/10) and the 7/24 sends that split the difference (7/10 twice), this one goes maximalist: hero banner, jeans deal, five-category "Best-of-Sale" ladder, and a clearance tier, all in one very long scroll.
- The execution itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, a legible fit-callout on the jeans, and a "thousands of reviews" nod on the kids' fleece. But the offer architecture undercuts the impact: "50% OFF EVERYTHING!" is immediately followed by "Online Exclusive. Exclusions Apply." in the same hero block, which is honest disclosure but also a tell that "everything" isn't really everything. Layer that on top of a subject line stuffed with three separate offers and ALL-CAPS shouting, and you get an email that's well-built but trying to do too much at once.
- Judgment: solid production, muddled strategy. This reads like Old Navy ran out of distinct story ideas for the week and combined them all into one send rather than pacing them — which, per the brand history here, tracks with a pattern of same-week redundancy.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous — huge "50% OFF EVERYTHING" fills the frame with zero scrolling required.
- The Wow Jeans deal keeps its fit callout ("Boot Cut") and clear $9/$16 kids-vs-adult price ladder, which is a nice product-detail touch this week's other jeans sends lacked.
- The "Best-of-Sale" section adds real specificity — priced products (Women's Low Rise Bootsy Jean $25, Men's Heavyweight Tee $13, Kids Favorite Fleece $15, Baby Ribbed One-Piece $8) instead of vague category tiles.
- Social proof appears once ("thousands of reviews" on the kids' fleece) — a small but real trust signal.
- The Encore membership banner and clearance tier give the email a clean top-to-bottom funnel from acquisition to bargain-hunting.

## What's weak

- The subject line stacks three offers (50% off, "even new arrivals," $16 jeans) into 86 characters with ALL-CAPS "HUGE" and "FIFTY PERCENT OFF EVERYTHING" — reads as shouty and will truncate hard on mobile.
- "50% OFF EVERYTHING!" is qualified two lines later by "Online Exclusive. Exclusions Apply." — the asterisk-and-fine-print pattern undercuts the headline claim's honesty.
- This is at least the fourth Old Navy send in the tracked window built around Wow Jeans at $16 — the deal has now appeared as sole hero (7/25), co-hero (7/24 twice), and now third-billing under the 50%-off umbrella. Repetition fatigue is real.
- "2 DAYS ONLY... Ends Sunday 7/26" lands the same day the email is sent (per artifact date), which reads as a last-day scramble rather than a clean 2-day window.
- CTA buttons are generic "SHOP NOW" throughout — no category-specific button copy despite five distinct product stories.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the subject to one offer.** Pick either the 50%-off storewide claim or the $16 jeans price — not both plus "even new arrivals." A single concrete number reads cleaner and won't truncate.
- 2. **Drop the asterisk/exclusions framing from the hero,** or state the actual exclusions in the same breath ("50% off everything, except clearance") so the claim doesn't feel walked-back mid-block.
- 3. **Give buttons specific copy** — "Shop Wow Jeans," "Shop Fleece $15" — instead of repeating "Shop Now" five times.
- 4. **Space this out from the 7/24–7/25 Wow Jeans sends** — three-plus emails anchored on the same $16 jeans price in one week trains subscribers to skim past it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off everything is here (yes, even new arrivals)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: $16 Wow Jeans + storewide 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: clearance starts at $3.99`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Boot cut, bootsy, or baby — all half off through Sunday`
