# Don't miss it — 50% off everything*, 60% off all shorts & swim* + $5 dresses
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Old Navy's fourth consecutive send in five days featuring the exact same triple-deal stack — 50% off everything, 60% off shorts & swim, $5 dresses. The June 19 "Three deals, one cart" send named all three explicitly; the June 20 morning and evening sends ran the same combination under summer-heat headlines; now this. The email renders cleanly and the offers are genuinely deep, but the brand has recycled this creative so relentlessly that "Don't miss it" reads as noise, not urgency.
- Structurally the email shows real craft: a clear 50% off hero leads, followed by dedicated modules for shorts/swim and dresses, then a size-browseable clearance grid that's the most actionable thing in the send, then a "Hot! New! Gear!" block trying to introduce freshness alongside the clearance push. That newness module is the most interesting creative decision here — it signals Old Navy isn't just in liquidation mode. Problem: it's buried three scrolls deep in a long email, well past where exhausted subscribers will check out.
- The verdict: mechanically sound, strategically spent. If a subscriber didn't open or click on three nearly identical sends earlier this week, this one won't change that. Old Navy either needs to end the cycle with a credible hard-stop, or send something that looks genuinely different.

## What's working

- **Size-browseable clearance module** ("Shop Clearance In Your Size") is the email's creative highlight — it reduces browse friction by letting the subscriber start from their own size rather than the full assortment.
- **"Hot! New! Gear!" section** is smart brand balance: introducing newness alongside heavy discounting keeps the brand from reading as pure liquidation mode.
- **50% off hero leads correctly** — visual hierarchy is right, the biggest offer gets the most real estate at the top.
- **Three-deal density** is genuine value for a first-time opener — 50%, 60%, and $5 in one email is a real payload.
- **Clean, bug-free render** — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout issues visible.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is severe**: four sends, four days, same three deals. "Don't miss it" is empty urgency when the offer has been running continuously all week.
- **Subject line at ~74 characters truncates on mobile** — subscribers likely see "Don't miss it — 50% off everything*, 60% off all sh..." before it cuts, which is worse than the truncated version of a tighter subject.
- **No deadline anywhere visible** — "Don't miss it" without a date or countdown is inert. When does the 50% off end?
- **"Everything*" and "all shorts & swim*" asterisks are prominent** — deal-savvy subscribers immediately register that the headline discounts are qualified, which undercuts the perceived honesty.
- **The newness module ("Hot! New! Gear!") is buried too deep** — the only creative content that breaks the deal fatigue loop appears after at least three competing sections.

## Recommendations

- 1. **End the campaign cycle now.** The next send should look entirely different — a new creative, a new frame, a new category lead. Four consecutive sends of the same deal is the point at which unsubscribes outpace conversions.
- 2. **Add a credible deadline.** A specific end time ("Ends Sunday at midnight") makes "Don't miss it" mean something. Without it the urgency language is self-defeating.
- 3. **Promote the size-clearance module to the hero.** "Your size is in clearance" is more personalized and action-triggering than the generic 50% off banner.
- 4. **Move "Hot! New! Gear!" to second position** — right after the hero — to break the all-clearance narrative and give the email a reason for subscribers who bought shorts last week to keep reading.
- 5. **Cut the subject to ~50 chars** — one offer, one urgency signal, done.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off ends tonight — don't leave it`
- **Subject Alt B:** `$5 dresses. 60% off swim. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop clearance by your size — XS to 4X marked down`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: new gear just dropped alongside the deals`
