# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is leaning full patriotic for what looks like a Memorial Day/early summer push — "U.S. OF YAY!" with a flag sweater is very on-brand for them. The two promo banners at the top are doing a lot of work: one pushing the Encore credit card, one teasing free shipping for members. As a mom hunting for kid basics and Super Cash deals, nothing above the fold is sending me directly where I need to go.

## What's working

- **The double-banner promo stack** — having both the Encore card offer AND the free shipping bar stacked immediately above the nav means I can see what's in it for me before I even look at the hero.
- **"U.S. OF YAY!" hero copy** — punchy, playful, millennial-friendly. This is Old Navy at its best: loud without trying too hard.
- **Seasonal relevance** — patriotic graphic-tee moment right before Memorial Day weekend is exactly what Old Navy should be doing; it signals they're stocked and ready.
- **The Encore member free-shipping callout** — "$50+ for Encore Members" with a visible "Sign In or Join Now" is a real loyalty hook that makes me want to sign in to check if I qualify.

## What's weak

- **No kids or family entry point visible** — I came to grab uniform basics and maybe use some Super Cash; the hero is entirely women's fashion with zero shortcut to the kids section.
- **"APPLY NOW" as the dominant CTA** — the biggest, most prominent button above the fold is asking me to open a credit card. That's not why I opened the app.
- **Nav is hamburger-only** — there's a search icon and a cart, but no quick-tap category chips (Kids, Baby, Deals) that would let me get where I'm going in one tap.
- **Exclusions buried but visible** — "Excludes Today's Deals and Flag Tees/Tanks" under the 50% offer is there in small print; as a sale hunter this immediately makes me suspicious about what's actually discounted.
- **No Super Cash or Navyist Rewards mention** — I'm a Navyist Reward member but the loyalty language I see is all Encore Card, which is a different program. My points balance and any active Super Cash aren't acknowledged.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Kids" and "Deals" category chip row** directly below the nav bar — one tap to Kids or Clearance would keep sale-driven moms from bouncing before they even scroll.
- **Swap or supplement the hero CTA** — put a "Shop the Collection" or "Shop Tees from $8" button on the hero image so there's a shopping entry point above the fold alongside the credit card push.
- **Surface Navyist Rewards separately** from Encore — if I'm already a member, show me my Super Cash availability or reward points in the sign-in nudge, not just the credit card offer.
- **Add a thin urgency strip** under the hero: "Memorial Day deals — this week only" or similar would make the sale feel time-bounded and get a sale hunter like me to move faster.
