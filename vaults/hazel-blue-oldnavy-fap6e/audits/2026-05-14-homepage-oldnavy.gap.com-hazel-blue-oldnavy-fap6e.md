# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is doing a lot right here on the first screen — it feels like they knew I was coming. A Disney collab hero shot with an actual family (not just a 22-year-old model), a stack of deal banners, and a very clear summer-is-coming vibe. This is classic Old Navy: loud deals, recognizable characters, family-friendly energy. Right now they're pushing a summer Disney collab and clearing the decks with deep value pricing.

## What's working

- **The Old Navy x Disney hero** is genuinely smart for me — my kids are obsessed with Disney and I know this collab will have kids' tees I can actually afford
- **The green "50% Off" strip with $5/$10/$15 price points** speaks my language immediately; I'm a clearance-rack person and those numbers make me want to tap
- **The $10 off $75+ banner with a hard end date (Ends 5/16)** gives me a real reason to act today, not next week
- **Free shipping for Encore/loyalty members called out before the nav** — I don't have to hunt for it, it's just there
- **The family in the hero** includes a girl the same age as my younger kid, which makes me picture my own daughter in that denim jacket instantly

## What's weak

- **No kids or women's category shortcut above the fold** — I have to dig into the hamburger menu to get to the sections I actually care about; the green deal strip doesn't tell me whether those $5/$10/$15 styles are kids, adults, or both
- **"Encore Members" vs. "Navyist Rewards"** — as someone who is definitely in the loyalty program, seeing a name I don't recognize creates a half-second "wait, is that me?" moment
- **No Super Cash callout anywhere** — it's genuinely one of Old Navy's strongest retention tools and I scan for it every single time I open the app; it's not here
- **Hero copy is more vibes than action** — "Bring That Summer Feeling" is fun but there's no "Shop Kids Disney" or "Shop Women's" CTA layered on the hero itself; I have to find the strip below it

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Shop Kids" secondary CTA button directly on the hero** — the Disney collab is tailor-made for parents like me; don't make me scroll to act on it
- **Replace "Encore Members" with "Navyist Rewards Members"** (or at least "loyalty members") in the free-shipping banner — reduce the identity confusion for people who know the program by its actual name
- **Drop a Super Cash status badge or teaser into the loyalty strip** — even "Earn Super Cash on this purchase" would stop me mid-scroll and lock in a purchase decision
- **Add sub-labels to the $5/$10/$15 deal strip** ("Kids from $5! Women's from $10!") so I know the value pricing applies to the categories I'm shopping before I tap
