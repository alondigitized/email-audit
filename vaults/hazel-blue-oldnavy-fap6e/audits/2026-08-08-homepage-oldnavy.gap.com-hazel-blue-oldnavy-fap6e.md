# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a brand that knows exactly what it wants me to buy today: jeans. The blue promo strip up top with "Extra 30% Off" and a code is impossible to miss, and the hero is all leg-and-attitude denim styling. It reads more "cool girl road trip" than "mom grabbing uniform pants before the school bell," but I can't say it's not eye-catching.

## What's working

- The blue "All Jeans On Sale + Extra 30% Off Purchase" bar with code EXTRA and an end date (8/9) — that's the kind of clear, time-boxed deal that gets me to actually click.
- "Free shipping on orders $50+ for Encore Members" sitting right in the top bar — easy to see before I even scroll.
- "Uniforms" is its own tab in the main nav, right there next to Activewear and Women's — I don't have to hunt for it.
- Clean layout, nothing broken or overlapping — page looks legit and loads like a real store.

## What's weak

- The hero — a woman in an unbuttoned cardigan strutting away from a vintage car in the desert — is a fashion-editorial moment, not a "mom stocking up on basics" moment. Doesn't feel like it's talking to me.
- No kids, no family, no uniform pieces anywhere above the fold. For a brand I associate with school pants and Super Cash, that's a miss.
- The four tiles teased below the hero are all solo adult fashion shots (three women, one man) — nothing that screams "back to school" even though that's exactly where my head is in August.
- No mention of Super Cash or my rewards balance anywhere visible — that's usually my biggest hook to come back.

## Recommendations

- Swap the desert/vintage-car hero for a back-to-school lifestyle shot — a parent and kid picking out uniform pants or basics together.
- Surface Super Cash balance or "You have $X in Super Cash" right in that top loyalty strip instead of a generic "Sign In or Join Now."
- Add a "Shop Uniforms" or "Back to School" promo tile right under the hero, not just buried as one nav tab among ten.
- Show a kid or a family in at least one of those four category tiles under the hero — right now it's all solo adult fashion.
