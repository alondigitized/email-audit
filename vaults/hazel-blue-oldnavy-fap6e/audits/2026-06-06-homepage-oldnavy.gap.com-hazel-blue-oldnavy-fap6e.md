# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, so Old Navy is doing their "American Summer" thing right now, which — fine, it's June, I get it. But the second I opened this page I saw a young woman in a sports bra and bike shorts doing some kind of aggressive run, and my brain immediately went "this is not for me." I'm here for kids' uniforms and a good deal on basics, and this page is screaming beach party. The bones are solid though — the deal CTA and the free-shipping banner are exactly what keeps me coming back.

## What's working

- **"Free shipping on orders $50+" banner** — as a Navyist/Encore member this is the exact hook that makes me stay; I know I'm hitting $50 easily with two kids
- **"SHOP DEALS FROM $8" CTA** — sale-driven shoppers like me are immediately curious; $8 is a real number, not vague
- **"Extra 30% off your first purchase" credit card banner** — even if I already have the card, this reminds me the card has perks, which keeps me brand-loyal
- **"American Summer Happy 25th!" badge** — there's an anniversary event happening, which signals there might be real deals attached, not just seasonal fluff
- **Clean render** — no jank, no broken images, loads fast on mobile; that matters when I'm shopping from my phone during soccer practice

## What's weak

- **Hero model and styling** — she's maybe 22 and doing parkour in athleisure. Nothing in that image says "back-to-school uniforms" or "affordable family basics," which is why most of us are actually on this app
- **No kids/family navigation visible** — the whole screen is hamburger menu + search + bag icon. As a mom I want "Girls," "Boys," "Baby" one tap away, not buried in a hamburger
- **Hero copy is absent** — "American Summer Happy 25th!" is a badge on the image but there's no actual headline telling me what the deal *is* or what to do next besides the barely-there bottom CTA
- **Encore Credit Card push feels mismatched** — I'm a Navyist Rewards member, not an Encore Credit Card applicant; leading with a credit card acquisition message feels like it's talking to someone else

## Recommendations

- **Swap or companion the hero** — run the athletic model for the 20-something shopper, but add a family/kids lifestyle tile or secondary banner above the fold ("Stock up for summer — uniforms, tees, shorts from $8"); I should see myself somewhere in the first screen
- **Surface one category shortcut strip** — four tappable chips below the nav ("Girls | Boys | Women | Men") would let me skip the hamburger and go straight to kids', which is 80% of my cart
- **Add a Super Cash / rewards balance callout** — if I have Super Cash about to expire, tell me NOW in a sticky banner; that's the #1 reason I open this app and it's not here
- **Give the hero badge some copy weight** — replace or supplement "American Summer Happy 25th!" with something actionable like "25th Anniversary Deals — up to 50% off" so the celebration translates to savings I can use
