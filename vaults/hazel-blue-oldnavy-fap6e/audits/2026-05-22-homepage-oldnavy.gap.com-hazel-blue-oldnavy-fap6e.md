# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Old Navy is doing the most right now — "Long Weekend Sale, 50% Off Deals" in giant red letters, a whole family on the beach, and a sale badge claiming 3,744 styles. This looks exactly like the brand I've been shopping for years: loud, fun, price-forward, and actually aimed at families like mine. Right now they're pushing a big Memorial Day-ish sale weekend and summer staples across the whole family.

## What's working

- **"50% Off Deals" headline** — This is the language I live for. Clear, numeric, no guessing. I immediately know there's something worth clicking.
- **Family lifestyle hero** — A couple plus two kids in matching summer prints? That's my shopping basket in one image. This earns trust that this isn't just a teen brand.
- **"3,744 Styles On Sale" badge** — That specificity makes it feel real, not like a fake "up to 10% off one thing" bait. I'm already doing math.
- **"SHOP ALL DEALS" CTA** — Unambiguous. No clever copy, just tells me exactly what happens when I tap it.
- **Navigation strip with ALL TEES / ALL TANKS / ALL SHORTS** — That's a direct path to exactly what I need for back-to-school staples and summer basics. One tap from where I need to go.

## What's weak

- **No Navyist / Encore member hook above the fold** — I'm a Navyist Rewards member and the only nod to that is a tiny gray bar at the very top about free shipping for Encore Members. My rewards balance? Not visible. Why isn't the app/membership front and center for me?
- **"Sign In or Join Now" feels cold** — It's tiny text below the free-shipping line. For a loyalty-heavy brand, this should feel like a warm welcome back, not a footnote.
- **No kids-specific category visible in nav strip** — The scroll-nav shows tees, tanks, shorts, dresses, swim — all seem adult-coded. As a mom buying for two kids, I want "Kids" right there without digging.
- **Hero models skew young and fit** — The family is cute but they look like a fashion campaign, not the actual suburban family at Target. A slightly more relatable cast would hit harder for millennial moms.

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Navyist rewards balance in the header** — Even a "Hi Hazel, you have X points" line would pull returning members like me straight into conversion mode; I shop differently when I know my Super Cash is about to expire.
- **Add a "Kids" tab to the scrolling nav strip** — Right now it's all adult categories. A single "KIDS" pill next to ALL TEES would cut my navigation time in half and signal this brand gets how I actually shop.
- **Swap one of the nav category labels to "UNIFORMS" or "BACK TO SCHOOL" seasonally** — Even in late May, back-to-school prep starts for moms like me. A single label plants that seed early.
- **Make the free-shipping threshold banner member-aware** — Instead of "Free shipping on orders $50+ for Encore Members / Sign In or Join Now," show "You're in! Free shipping on your next order" for logged-in members. That's a one-line swap that meaningfully changes how I feel walking in.
