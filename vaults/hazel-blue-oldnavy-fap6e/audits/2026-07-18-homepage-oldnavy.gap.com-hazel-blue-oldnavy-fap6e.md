# Homepage snapshot · oldnavy.gap.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- Old Navy is in full end-of-summer clearance mode, leading with a bold CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 — which, for me, is a pretty strong opener. As a card-carrying Navyist who builds whole shopping trips around sale racks, that red-orange banner stopped my thumb cold. But the "Encore Members" label where "Navyist" used to be has me momentarily confused, and the Gen Z low-rise tease peeking below the fold is doing absolutely nothing for a 38-year-old in back-to-school prep mode.

## What's working

- **CLEARANCE FROM $3.99 hero:** The big bold orange banner speaks my language. As someone who physically works clearance racks in-store, this is the exact hook to keep me from bouncing.
- **Free shipping on $50+ for Encore Members:** Visible before I even reach the header. $50 is completely reachable for a typical family haul and I know it immediately.
- **"Sale" is second in the nav bar:** One tap to get where I'm going. That's efficient and respects my time.
- **Loyalty CTA front and center:** "Sign In or Join Now" tells me this store knows I have an account and wants me to use it.

## What's weak

- **No back-to-school signal anywhere:** It's mid-July. I am in peak school-shopping panic mode and Old Navy is literally where I go for uniform basics. This homepage has no idea.
- **"Low Rise Is Back" peek below fold:** Two young women in a Gen Z trend piece. Not me. Not my shopping trip right now.
- **Credit card push with asterisk is the very first thing I see:** Before the header, before any product, I'm already reading fine print. Sets a skeptical tone.
- **"Encore Members" naming:** My loyalty program was called Navyist. That name change creates a split second of "wait, did my points transfer?" — friction before I've even started.
- **Zero Super Cash callout:** Half my Old Navy shopping trips are timed around Super Cash earn or redeem windows. Nothing about it on this page.

## Recommendations

- **Rewrite the hero as a back-to-school clearance mashup:** "Back to School Under $5" hits both signals at once — clearance pricing and my exact need in July. Keep the red-orange energy, just make the copy work harder for families.
- **Add a "Kids" or "Uniforms" tab to the visible nav strip:** "New Arrivals, Sale, Women's Jeans, Women's H..." — kids aren't even visible in the navigation without scrolling sideways. One tap to kidswear is table stakes for a mom.
- **Surface the Super Cash earn/redeem window in a banner strip:** "Earn Super Cash now through 7/28" would immediately trigger a deliberate shopping session from me. This is a known Navyist behavior being left on the table.
- **Lead with the free-shipping benefit banner, not the credit card acquisition pitch:** The loyalty perks strip is more trustworthy and more relevant for returning members. Bury the credit card pitch or move it below the fold.
