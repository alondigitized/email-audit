# Staff-approved ways to make their heart flutter
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a pleasant, well-built staff-picks email that does little to earn a click. Five editors each get a pull quote next to a product: burgundy bouquet, rainbow strawberries, red roses, a tic-tac-toe chocolate set, and a second bouquet. It reads like a human recommendation, not a catalog. The imagery is warm and on-season, and the layout is clean, with no visible render bugs.
- It's also a near-repeat. The headline is "What We're Loving for Fall" with a "Shop Love & Romance" button and code GLOW25, which is almost the same as the 2026-10-07 send, "Handpicked Romantic Favorites, 25% Off". A subscriber who has also seen the 10/08 and 10/10 gift guides and the 10/12 "Save Up to 25%" email will see this as the same message again. The 25% offer is a thin banner above the logo, and nothing shows a price or a deadline.
- Judgment: solid craft, weak reason to act now. The charm is real, but the cadence is stale and the incentive is easy to miss.

## What's working

- Staff quotes with names and titles ("Jennifer, Deputy Editor", "Ian, Copywriter") give the email a human voice that most floral promos lack.
- The product photography is strong and fits the burgundy and orange fall palette.
- The layout alternates image sides, so the long scroll stays easy to follow.
- The top banner states a concrete offer, 25% off with code GLOW25.
- The "Shop by Category" grid near the bottom gives a way out to Birthday, Sympathy, Same-Day and other categories.

## What's weak

- The 25% offer sits in a small banner. The hero focuses on "What We're Loving" and the offer doesn't compete for attention.
- No product shows a price, a sale price, or a ship-by date, so the shopper has no reason to act today.
- Every product link says "Shop Now", and the quotes are about the editors' feelings, not the products.
- The creative and promo code repeat the 10/07 send. This is the third GLOW-code staff-picks or gift-guide email in about a week.
- The fine print below the footer is dense, and the asterisk on the offer hints at exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "25% off with code GLOW25" next to the headline and add a real deadline, such as "ends Thursday".
- 2. Show a "from $XX" price under each product name, with the sale price if one applies.
- 3. Rotate the editorial angle so it doesn't repeat 10/07. For example, make each pick answer a specific occasion (anniversary, apology, just because).
- 4. Replace "Shop Now" with product-specific CTAs, such as "Get the Red Roses" or "Send the Strawberries".
- 5. Add a delivery cue, such as "order by 2pm for same-day", since one pick is a day-of quote.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Editors' picks for romance, 25% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 gifts our staff would actually send`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code GLOW25 through Thursday. Roses, strawberries and bouquets that arrive fresh.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our editors share the gifts they'd give. Order by 2pm for same-day delivery.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged these images, all tracking pixels except possibly the first:
  - atdmt pixel
  - both mathtag pixels
  - both `bounceexchange.com/.../1622.gif` instances
  - `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
  - `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/72adcde3-d50a-46c7-b02e-14de89131f32?...`
- **Pixels vs. content images:** Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` or equivalent, so screen readers skip them. The `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/<uuid>` image is probably also a first-party open pixel. The truncated source doesn't show whether it is decorative or content.
- **Also in the HTML head:**
  - `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
  - Global `* {line-height:100%}` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` further limit text scaling.
  - `th {font-size:20px !important; color:#000000 !important}` forces black text and could clash with the declared `color-scheme: light dark` in dark mode if the `th` backgrounds aren't also overridden. The truncated source doesn't show this.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** The mobile breakpoint is `max-width: 418px`, and the mobile classes set a fixed `width:414px`. Devices narrower than 414px (for example, 360px Android) may scroll horizontally.
- **Web fonts:** Playfair Display and Lato are loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` with `mso-font-alt` fallbacks on some weights only. This is acceptable, but not all `@font-face` blocks declare an Outlook fallback.
