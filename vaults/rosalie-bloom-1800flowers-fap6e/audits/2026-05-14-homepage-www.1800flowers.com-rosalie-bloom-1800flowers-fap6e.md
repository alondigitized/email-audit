# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is a fine how-do-you-do. I open the site and the very first thing I get is a cookie wall — barely managed to clock the "Find the Perfect Gift" search widget before the legal notice swallowed the whole screen. From what I can see, 1-800-Flowers is pushing a smart delivery-first experience: you enter your ZIP and pick an occasion, and it routes you from there. That's actually right up my alley — I always need same-day sympathy arrangements — but I can't evaluate the brand's real personality or promotions because a GDPR banner is eating my viewport.

## What's working

- **"Find the Perfect Gift" + ZIP + Occasion widget** — This is genuinely useful for someone like me who shops by event and needs delivery to a specific address (I know every cousin's ZIP by heart). The fact that it's the first interactive element I see is smart.
- **Occasion dropdown pre-loaded to "Birthday"** — Birthday is one of my top three reasons to order. That's not a coincidence; they've picked the right default.
- **Clean mobile header** — The hamburger menu, search icon, account icon, and cart icon are all clear and tappable. No clutter.
- **"Search" button is unmistakable** — Big, full-width, black. I know exactly what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal covers virtually the entire homepage** — As a 47-year-old who just wants to send flowers to a grieving friend, I do not want to read three paragraphs of data-policy legalese before I see a single flower. This is my single biggest frustration with the whole experience right now.
- **No promotional content visible** — I'm a Passport member. I should see something that acknowledges that the moment I land. I see nothing — no free-shipping badge, no member price, nothing.
- **No urgency or seasonal hook visible** — It's May 14th, the week of Mother's Day. I can't see a single Mother's Day message, countdown, or banner because the cookie modal has buried everything below the fold.
- **No same-day delivery callout above the fold** — That's often the reason I choose 1-800-Flowers over a local florist in a sympathy pinch. It should be front and center, not hidden below a consent wall.

## Recommendations

- **Make the cookie notice a slim bottom bar, not a full-screen takeover.** A one-line "We use cookies — Continue" strip at the bottom preserves the full homepage experience. Right now you're making me read legal copy before I've seen a single flower.
- **Surface a Passport / loyalty callout in the header or just below the search widget** — even a small "Free delivery with Passport" line would remind returning members why they're here and reward their loyalty before they've done anything.
- **Pin a Mother's Day urgency strip just below the search widget** — "Order by [date] for Mother's Day delivery" with a countdown. It's the second week of May; this is the single highest-converting message you have right now.
- **Add a "Same-Day Delivery Available" badge near the ZIP input** — That's a massive differentiator for sympathy shoppers. Show it right where I'm already looking.
