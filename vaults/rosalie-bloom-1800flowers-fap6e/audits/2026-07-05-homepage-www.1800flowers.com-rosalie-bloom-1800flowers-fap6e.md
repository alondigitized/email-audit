# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- I came here to order sympathy flowers — probably same-day — and the first thing I get is a giant cookie consent wall eating half my screen. Under it, there's a clean little gift-finder tool that I actually like the look of, but I can barely see it. 1-800-Flowers feels like the reliable workhorse of online gifting — it's been around forever, and the teal branding is instantly familiar — but this landing experience is doing them no favors.

## What's working

- **The gift-finder widget is smart.** ZIP Code + Occasion in one compact row, with "Birthday" already selected, is exactly how my brain approaches this. It reads like the site knows why I'm here.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" as the hero headline** is occasion-gifter speak. That's me. It's not clever or coy — it just tells me I'm in the right place.
- **The 1-800-Flowers brand** carries weight. I've used this site for 15 years. The logo and color instantly tell me "reliable, real delivery, not a scam."

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal is catastrophic on mobile.** It's not a small banner — it's a full overlay with three paragraphs of legal text, blocking the entire homepage. I have to dismiss it before I see anything. This is the number one bounce driver on this screen.
- **No offer is visible anywhere.** Not a banner, not a strip, not a callout. If I'm comparison-shopping with Teleflora or FTD, there's nothing here to make me stay.
- **No Passport / loyalty hook.** I pay for Passport specifically for same-day free shipping — it should be the first thing I see as a returning member.
- **No urgency cue.** For sympathy flowers, I often need same-day. There's nothing on this screen telling me same-day is available, what the cutoff is, or whether my ZIP qualifies.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-screen cookie modal on mobile.** Make it a slim bottom banner with a single "Got it" tap — don't bury the homepage under three paragraphs of GDPR text. Every second that modal is up is a second I'm not looking at flowers.
- **Surface a Passport member badge in the header.** Even a small "Passport ✓" next to my account icon tells me instantly my free shipping is active. It's a retention signal and a loyalty reward at the same time.
- **Add a same-day availability strip** just below the finder widget: something like "Order by 2 PM for same-day delivery" with my detected ZIP. This is the one thing that would make me search immediately rather than shop around.
- **Drop a single promo banner below the search button.** Even "20% off your next order — Passport members" above the fold would differentiate this page from a cold-traffic experience.
