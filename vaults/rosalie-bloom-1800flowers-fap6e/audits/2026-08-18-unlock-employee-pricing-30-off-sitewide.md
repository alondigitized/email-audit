# Unlock Employee Pricing: 30% Off Sitewide
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact "30% Off Sitewide" flash sale 1-800-Flowers has run relentlessly all week (Aug 5, 6, 11-13), now wearing a fresh coat of paint as "Employee Discount Days." The faux-exclusivity angle — "Employee Discount Days," "Email subscribers enjoy exclusive deals," code INSIDER30 — is the same trick as the Aug 17 "You're on the List for 30% Off" send, just one day later with different label copy. There's no actual product hero this time either; it's generic mixed bouquets and a 3x3 category grid, which is weaker than the single-SKU sends (Aug 11-13) that scored 9/10 on sharp, focused creative.
- There's also a visible render bug: the "Unsubscribe & Manage Preferences" link is bleeding into the body content, sitting directly under the product grid rather than confined to the footer — that's the kind of thing that makes an email look broken on open.
- Verdict: mid-tier send. Recognizable brand, clear 30% offer, functioning CTA — but it's the fourth near-identical "30% sitewide" percentage play this week wrapped in a thin new label, and the stray unsubscribe text in the body is a real defect that should get fixed before the next send.

## What's working

- Hero is unmistakable — huge "30% OFF SITEWIDE" in teal against a bright, appetizing florals-and-chocolate-strawberries photo.
- Single, unambiguous CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the offer, no scrolling needed.
- Code INSIDER30 is called out clearly and reinforces the "exclusive" framing.
- Category grid (Birthday, Romance, Sympathy, Plants, etc.) gives multiple entry points for different shopper intents.
- Celebrations Passport membership block is a legitimate secondary upsell, clearly separated from the main offer.

## What's weak

- Render bug: "Unsubscribe & Manage Preferences" text is misplaced mid-body, appearing right under "Use Code INSIDER30" instead of only in the footer — looks broken.
- This is the same 30%-off-sitewide mechanic as yesterday's send (Aug 17, "You're on the List for 30% Off," 6.00/10) with a cosmetic rename to "Employee Discount Days" — a repeated beat, not a new offer.
- No specific hero product — four generic bouquet thumbnails instead of the tighter single-SKU creative that scored 9/10 on Aug 11-13.
- "Employee Discount" framing is a stretch for a mass consumer blast; it reads as manufactured urgency rather than a real perk.
- No countdown or explicit expiration visible above the fold (72-hour window is only mentioned in small subhead text, easy to miss).

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the render bug first — the stray "Unsubscribe & Manage Preferences" link floating mid-body is a real defect and undermines trust; confine it to the footer only.
- 2. Drop the "Employee Discount" gimmick or replace it with a real, singular offer angle (like the Aug 11 Sorbet Roses SKU-specific play that scored 9/10) instead of another sitewide percentage.
- 3. Lead with one hero product image instead of a generic 4-photo grid — specificity converts better than an assortment.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or explicit "Ends [date/time]" near the CTA to make the 72-hour window feel real, not just a small text mention.
- 5. Vary the cadence — four "30% sitewide" variants in under two weeks trains subscribers to wait for the next one instead of acting now.
