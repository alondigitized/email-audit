# The flowers doubled. The price didn't.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth "Double Blooms" send since August 24, and it's a clean, high-production hero — big rotated "DOUBLE" wordmarks flanking a genuinely lush mixed bouquet, a legible "Starting at $44.99" badge, and a tight "Double Your Blooms" headline that lands the mechanic in three words. Below the fold it earns its length: three before/after "2X the blooms" product rows, then a distinct second offer (20% off with code BLOOM20), a shop-by-category grid, and a loyalty pitch for Celebrations Passport — more structure and more genuine content than most of this run's sends.
- But structurally this is the same mechanic 1-800-Flowers has now run six times in eight days, and by this point the "2X the blooms" visual language (orange starburst price badge, arrow between two bouquet shots, "2X the blooms" caption) is fully memorized creative — nothing here differentiates it from the Aug 24 original or the Aug 29/31 reruns except a stacked secondary 20%-off offer. Subject line "The flowers doubled. The price didn't." is a decent A/B variant on the mechanic but it's now the sixth different way of saying the identical thing.
- Judgement: solid, well-built execution of a stale mechanic — good enough on craft to score above the Aug 26/27 sends, but cadence fatigue caps how much credit it can get.

## What's working

- Hero bouquet photography is sharp and colorful with no render bugs — clean crop, no overlapping text.
- "Starting at $44.99" badge sits directly on the hero image, so the offer is visible with zero scroll.
- Three separate before/after "2X the blooms" comparisons give concrete proof of the doubling mechanic across product lines (Vibrant Blooms, Cherished Blooms, Summer Beginnings).
- A distinct second offer (20% off, code BLOOM20) on named SKUs adds real incremental content instead of just repeating the double-blooms beat.
- Shop-by-category grid and Celebrations Passport membership block give multiple exit ramps beyond the single hero CTA.

## What's weak

- This is the sixth "Double Blooms" send in eight days (Aug 24, 25, 26, 29, 31, now Sep 1) — the visual mechanic (orange badge, arrow, "2X the blooms" caption) is now rote and a subscriber has seen this exact layout at least twice before.
- Single primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is generic and appears only once at the very top — none of the three product rows or the 20%-off section have their own buttons, so anyone who scrolls past the hero has no clear next click.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — no end date on either the doubling offer or the BLOOM20 code, which lets it blur into the rest of the week's near-identical sends.
- Fine print at the very bottom (delivery fees, membership auto-renewal terms) is dense and easy to miss, which cuts against the "no extra cost" framing in the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire or substantially reskin the "Double Blooms" mechanic for at least a week — six sends of the same visual template in eight days is the single biggest drag on this campaign; rotate in a genuinely different hero treatment even if the underlying offer stays.
- 2. Add a "SHOP NOW" button under each of the three product rows and under the 20%-off block — right now there's one CTA at the top and everything below it is a dead scroll.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") to both the doubling offer and BLOOM20 to create credible urgency that's been absent across this whole run.
- 4. Move the Celebrations Passport membership pitch higher — free shipping is a stronger differentiator than the fourth "2X the blooms" repetition and is currently buried near the footer.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off summer favorites + double the blooms`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two bouquets' worth of blooms for one price — plus 20% off with BLOOM20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping, doubled blooms, and 20% off — all in one email.`
