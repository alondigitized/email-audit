# Autumn Roses + a Free Vase = Best Week Ever 💐 🎉 🤩
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- The subject sells a free vase, but the email never delivers one. I can't find "free vase" anywhere in the render. The hero is a "DEAL OF THE WEEK" banner with 25% off Autumn Roses. Below it sits a separate "20% Off* Best Sellers, Use Code: BLOOM20" block. A reader who opens for the vase is left with two percentage offers and no vase.
- The creative itself is strong. The hero is a tight, saturated bouquet of red, yellow and orange roses under oversized yellow type. It reads clearly as autumn and the eye lands on it first. The "Behind the Bouquet" block adds a bit of craft storytelling. After that the email turns into a generic stack: four products with no prices, a nine-tile category grid, app badges and a long legal footer.
- Compared with the 10/3 "Fall Bouquets Our Customers Can't Get Enough Of" send, this one drops the star ratings and customer quotes that made that email feel trustworthy. It is a mid-pack send, and the subject-to-body mismatch is what holds it back.

## What's working

- The hero bouquet is vivid and seasonal, and the dark olive background makes the roses pop.
- The 25% off badge sits right on the hero image, so the discount reads instantly.
- "Behind the Bouquet" gives the roses a quality story: "petal count, stem height, head size, and color".
- The 20% off block has a clear code, BLOOM20, in bold, and four clean product tiles.
- The category grid (Birthday, Sympathy, Same-Day and so on) gives a reader whose interest isn't roses somewhere to go.

## What's weak

- **Subject promise missing.** "Free Vase" is nowhere in the render, so the subject over-promises.
- **Two competing offers.** The hero says 25% off and the best-sellers block says 20% off with a code, and it isn't clear which applies to which products.
- **Weak primary CTA.** The hero CTA is a tiny underlined "SHOP NOW" under "Autumn Roses", with no button, and it is dwarfed by the headline.
- **Clipped hero type.** "DEAL" is cut off at the top edge and "WEEK" is cut off at the bottom. It looks like deliberate layout, but it makes the headline harder to read, and the type partly covers the bouquet.
- **No prices or proof on the product tiles.** Harvest Glow, Pumpkin Patch Mum and the others show no price, no sale price and no ratings. The offer deadline (10/18) only appears in the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make the subject match the body.** Either put a real free vase in the hero with a visible badge, or cut it from the subject. Right now the email breaks its own promise.
- 2. **Pick one offer.** Lead with a single message such as "25% off Autumn Roses, free vase included", and keep BLOOM20 as a clearly secondary line.
- 3. **Replace the small text link with a real button.** Use something like "Shop Autumn Roses" in a solid contrasting button under the headline.
- 4. **Put the deadline above the fold.** "Ends 10/18" belongs next to the offer, not in the footer.
- 5. **Add prices and star ratings to the product tiles.** The 10/3 email already did ratings well, so reuse that.
- 6. **Trim the category grid and footer.** Nine tiles dilute the message.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall roses, 25% off through 10/18`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Handpicked autumn roses in a free vase. Ends 10/18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 20% off best sellers with code BLOOM20.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with dark-mode meta tags, mobile media queries and web-font fallbacks. The confirmed issues are non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels, a viewport that blocks zoom, and no `lang` attribute. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure pixels:** Three pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507…`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507…`

  Clients that proxy or block mixed content may drop them, so open and conversion tracking is undercounted. The Mathtag and atdmt hosts support HTTPS.
- **Empty parameters:** Both Mathtag URLs have empty `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`. These look like template variables that were never filled, so the event carries no payload.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice. This is a redundant request, and it may double-count if the vendor doesn't dedupe.
- **Unverified links:** 25 tracking links were skipped (`click.dv.1800flowers.com`, `mi.1800flowers.com`), so their redirect chains and destinations are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom on mobile clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No document language:** `<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` has no `lang` or `xml:lang`, which screen readers need to pick a pronunciation.
- **Forced text color:** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` override the color of the nav headers. The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, so these cells could render black on a dark background. I can't confirm that without the cell backgrounds, which are in the truncated source.
- **Alt text on tracking images:** The QA "missing alt" warnings cover pixels and beacons (`atdmt`, `mathtag`, `1622.gif`, `o.gif`, the `click.dv` open image). Alt text on these has no user impact, but screen readers may announce the filename. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. The QA check doesn't cover content images, so I can't comment on those.
- **Global rules:** The `*` rule sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` while `body` sets `100%`, so the effective value is inconsistent across elements. The `*` rule also forces `line-height:100%`, which `.f14` and `.f20` override only on mobile.
- **Fixed mobile widths:** `.mobile` and `.showmobile` use `width:414px !important`, but the breakpoint is `max-width:418px`. Viewports narrower than 414px (for example 375px iPhones) will overflow horizontally.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The subject line is also clean.
- The open pixel `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=261006_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` carries a per-recipient ID (`mi_u`). That is expected, but it is a recipient identifier in a URL.
- The source is truncated, so the body copy could not be checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. QA notes AgentMail may strip them, so this is **not confirmed** as a sender defect. The sender is `em.1800flowers.com`, a large commercial sender that is likely to include them. Check the raw `.eml` before acting.
- **Physical address:** QA reports none detected. The footer is past the truncation point, so this is **unconfirmed**. Search the full HTML for the address block.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured by the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Alignment is plausible, since the From domain `em.1800flowers.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** Could not be verified from the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirector, the `mi_ecmp=261006_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` campaign code and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` IDs suggest tracking runs through a redirect service rather than UTM parameters on visible hrefs.
- UTM parameters and landing page alignment could not be verified, because the 25 tracking links were not probed and the CTA hrefs are not in the truncated source. No issue is claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the `atdmt` and `mathtag` pixels to `https://`, and fill or drop the empty `v1`–`v3` and `s1`–`s3` parameters.
2. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel, or confirm that the vendor dedupes it.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Give tracking pixels `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
6. Review the `th` forced-color rules against dark-mode backgrounds, and change the fixed 414px mobile widths to `width:100%; max-width:414px`.
7. Re-run QA against the full HTML and the raw message headers. That would settle the unsubscribe headers, the address, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the UTM parameters, and would let the 25 skipped links be resolved.
