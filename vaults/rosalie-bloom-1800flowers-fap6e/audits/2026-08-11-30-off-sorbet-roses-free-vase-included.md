# 30% off Sorbet Roses, free vase included
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-11**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers running a tighter, single-SKU version of the flash-sale plays we've seen all week — but instead of another sitewide percentage (Aug 5 "24-Hour Sale," Aug 6 "Extended One More Day," both 9.0/10), this one anchors on one specific product: Sorbet Roses at 30% off with a free vase. The hero is clean and does its job in about two seconds — big blue "30% Off" headline, product name, bouquet photo, one button.
- Where it loses points against those top-scoring sends is focus. Right after the hero and a nice "Behind The Bouquet" trust-building block (farm-fresh sourcing copy), the email pivots to a second, unrelated offer — "20% Off Summer Favorites" with a different code (BLOOM20) — before sliding into a nine-tile category grid and a membership pitch. That's three competing asks in one email, which dilutes the crisp single-offer energy the subject line promises.
- Solid, on-brand, no render issues — but it's a mid-pack send, not a standout. Call it a 6-7 territory email: good hero, cluttered body.

## What's working

- The hero delivers subject-line offer, product, and CTA in one clean visual block with no scrolling needed.
- "Behind The Bouquet" is a nice trust/quality beat (farm-fresh sourcing language) that's more substantive than a bare discount pitch.
- Loyalty program (Celebrations Passport) and app download are both present for cross-sell without crowding the hero.
- No layout bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, footer/legal renders cleanly.

## What's weak

- Two different discount offers (30% off Sorbet Roses vs. 20% off Summer Favorites, code BLOOM20) compete for attention in the same email — confusing which deal the recipient should actually act on.
- "Free vase included" is called out in the subject but never visually reinforced anywhere in the body — no vase shown in the hero image or copy.
- CTA copy is generic ("Shop Now") rather than product-specific, missing a chance to reinforce the Sorbet Roses hook.
- No visible urgency/deadline in the rendered body — the only "ends 8/30" mention is buried in dense footer fine print, easy to miss.
- After the strong hero + editorial block, the email sprawls into a nine-item category grid that reads like a stock template append rather than a continuation of the Sorbet Roses story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the second offer (20% Off Summer Favorites / BLOOM20) from this send entirely, or move it to a separate email — one hero, one code, one CTA converts better than two competing discounts.
- 2. Show the free vase — even a small inset product shot of the vase next to the bouquet would make that giveaway tangible instead of a subject-line-only claim.
- 3. Swap the CTA from "Shop Now" to something product-specific like "Shop Sorbet Roses" to reinforce the hero offer through to the click.
- 4. Surface the sale deadline near the hero, not buried in footer legal text — a small "Ends 8/30" line under the CTA would add real urgency.
- 5. Trim the category grid or move it below the Passport membership pitch — right now it interrupts the momentum from the hero and editorial block.
