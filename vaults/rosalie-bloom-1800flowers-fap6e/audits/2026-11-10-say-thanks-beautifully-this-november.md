# Say Thanks Beautifully This November
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, multi-theme "highlights" catalog dressed up as a Thanksgiving email. The subject says "Say Thanks Beautifully," but the hero is a generic "November Highlights" with a small "UP TO 20% OFF* WITH CODE FLOWERSALE" strip above it. The thank-you promise is never delivered in the creative. Thanksgiving gets one block of four modules, and the rest is Scorpio birthdays, romance and a "He's coming to town…" Christmas teaser.
- The offer is the same "up to 20%" and code FLOWERSALE the brand has pushed all month. It was in the 11/09 "Find Your Favorite, Then Save Up to 20%" send and the 11/07 "20% Off. Now the Only Hard Part Is Choosing." send. Compared with 11/05's 40% off red roses plus a free vase, this is a step backward. It's also the third send in a row with that same discount.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, and the module structure is tidy. But the email tries to be everything and so has no focus. Verdict: a competent catalog, not a campaign.

## What's working

- The render is clean: no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.
- Each section has its own "Shop …" CTA button (Thanksgiving, Birthday, Love & Romance, Holiday), so intent is easy to route.
- The Scorpio birthday block is a real segmentation and seasonality touch, and it's timely for early-to-mid November.
- The hero floral photo is rich and fall-toned, and fits the season.
- The product tiles are named and consistently laid out, which makes them quick to scan.

## What's weak

- The subject promises thanks, but the hero reads "November Highlights," so there is a message mismatch.
- The offer sits in a tiny strip above the hero and is easy to miss. It's also only "up to 20%*" with a code.
- Four themes in one email dilute the story. The Scorpio, romance and Santa blocks have nothing to do with saying thanks.
- Tiles carry no prices or discounts, so nothing shows value.
- Cadence is repetitive. The 20% off and FLOWERSALE code has run across the last several sends.
- The email is very long, with a category grid and app badges after the fourth CTA. There is no social proof or urgency deadline in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it a single-theme Thanksgiving send. Lead with a thank-you hero, one hero product and the offer in large type with a deadline. Cut the Scorpio, romance and Santa blocks, or move them to other sends.
- 2. Put prices or "20% off" on the tiles so the offer is visible at the point of choice.
- 3. Add a deadline tied to Thanksgiving delivery, such as "order by Nov 24 for delivery before the holiday."
- 4. Make the hero CTA specific, for example "Shop Thank-You Bouquets" rather than "Shop Now."
- 5. Rotate the offer. After a month of 20% off, test a bundle or a free add-on in the style of the 11/05 vase send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a heavily tracked ESP send (`em.1800flowers.com`) with 23 click-redirect links and several third-party pixels. Three of those pixels load over plain `http://`, and every tracking image lacks an `alt` attribute. The header and footer compliance checks could not be verified from the data available, since the HTML source was truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 23 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (`http://`):**
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
  
  Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop these, so the tracking silently under-reports.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, which may double-count events.
- **Plain-text part:** 4,376 of 6,245 characters (about 70%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter risk signal.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on tracking images:** all of these should have `alt=""` (the attribute is absent, not empty):
  - `view.atdmt.com/.../direct/01/`
  - both `pixel.mathtag.com` images
  - both `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` instances
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/8f6fff65-c35b-406b-be11-ea6fd33109d3`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text scaling disabled:** the universal `*` rule sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none`. The `body` rule later sets 100%, but the `*` rule still applies to every element.
- **Dark-mode risk:** the email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, but it forces `th {color:#000000 !important}`, `th a {color:#000000 !important}` and `.appleLinks a {color:#000000 !important}`. If a client applies a dark background to those cells, the text will be black on dark. I can't confirm the background colors from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Merge tokens:** no unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) were detected in the visible source.
- **Identifiers in URLs:** the open pixel `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=261110_EMAIL_18F_PZ_STO_DV_12AMET_PROMO` carries a numeric user ID (`mi_u`). The click-tracking URL carries `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. This is normal ESP behavior, but the ID is a persistent identifier in a URL.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`:** neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Without them, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders, is not demonstrable from this sample.
- **Physical address:** none was detected. The HTML is truncated, so the footer may contain one that the check missed. Verify against the full source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause, so this isn't attributable to the sender.
- **From domain:** `em.1800flowers.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment still needs a header check.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none could be validated. All 23 tracking links were skipped, and the only visible parameters are ESP-specific (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_ecmp`).
- **Landing pages:** destinations are unconfirmed for the same reason.
- **Action:** resolve the redirects to check that UTM or campaign parameters survive to the landing page and that the pages return 200.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the three `http://` pixels (`atdmt.com`, 2× `mathtag.com`) to HTTPS, or drop them if the vendors don't support it.
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking and spacer images.
3. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and drop `text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` rule.
5. Add dark-mode-safe color overrides for the forced `#000000 !important` rules on `th` and `.appleLinks a`.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` source to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results` and the physical address. That will settle which of the compliance warnings are real.
7. Follow the tracking redirects and verify the final URLs, status codes and UTM retention.
8. Add a plain-text alternative with fewer raw tracking URLs.
