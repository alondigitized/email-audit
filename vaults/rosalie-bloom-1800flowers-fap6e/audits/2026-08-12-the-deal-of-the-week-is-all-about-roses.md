# The Deal of the Week is all about roses 🌹 🌹 🌹 🌹 🌹
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same creative as yesterday's "30% off Sorbet Roses, free vase included" (Aug 11, 9.00/10) — identical hero, identical "PLUS, A FREE VASE!" starburst, identical 30% off Sorbet Roses offer, identical "Behind The Bouquet" module below it. The only meaningful change is the subject line, which trades a specific, high-converting offer statement for a vaguer "Deal of the Week" tease.
- The email itself is still clean and well-built: clear hierarchy, one obvious CTA, no render bugs, a second concrete offer (20% off summer favorites with code BLOOM20), and a tidy shop-by-category grid. But this is now the second consecutive day this exact promo has hit the inbox, and the subject line regression makes it read like a lazier resend rather than a fresh push — a subscriber who opened yesterday's email will recognize this instantly and may not bother opening again.
- Judgment: well-executed creative undermined by same-day repetition and a weaker subject. Score it on execution, but flag the cadence problem hard — this is the kind of back-to-back duplicate send that erodes list trust over time.

- Hero delivers offer instantly: "30% OFF" in large teal type with a single clear "SHOP SORBET ROSES" button.
- "PLUS, A FREE VASE!" starburst adds a tangible bonus on top of the discount, visible without scrolling.
- Second offer (20% Off Summer Favorites, code BLOOM20) is well-organized with four distinct product tiles and clear names.
- Shop-by-category grid at the bottom gives multiple entry points (Birthday, Romance, Sympathy, Plants, etc.) for browsers not sold on roses specifically.
- No layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is a same-day-adjacent repeat of Aug 11's "30% off Sorbet Roses, free vase included" send — identical hero, offer, and copy, just a different subject wrapper.
- Subject line is vaguer than yesterday's — "Deal of the Week is all about roses" hides the 30%-off number that yesterday's subject led with, which is a step backward for open-driving specificity.
- Rose emoji stacking (🌹 🌹 🌹 🌹 🌹) in the subject reads as filler/spam-adjacent rather than adding information.
- "Behind The Bouquet" storytelling block, while nice branding, pushes the second real offer (20% off / BLOOM20) below the fold, adding friction between hero and the next CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't resend the identical creative two days running — if the Sorbet Roses promo is still live, either genuinely refresh the hero art/copy or hold the send until there's a new angle. Repeats like this are what erode open rates over a batch.
- 2. Put the discount number back in the subject line — yesterday's version outperformed on the rubric partly because it stated "30% off" up front; "Deal of the Week" is a step backward in specificity.
- 3. Trim the emoji stack to one rose, not five — five repeated emoji reads as spam padding, not emphasis.
- 4. Consider swapping in the BLOOM20 second offer as the subject/preheader hook instead, since it's the one piece of content actually new relative to yesterday.
