# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I opened 1-800-Flowers and before I could see a single flower, a cookie consent wall slammed down and ate the entire screen. What little I can see behind it — "Find the Perfect Gift" with a ZIP code field and an Occasion dropdown pre-set to Birthday — tells me this brand knows what it's doing in concept. But right now, the actual homepage experience is: a legal notice. Not ideal when I'm trying to figure out whether they can get something to my aunt by tomorrow.

## What's working

- **"Find the Perfect Gift" + ZIP + Occasion tool** — this search widget is genuinely smart for how I shop. I think "I need flowers for a funeral in Scottsdale" and this widget is exactly that. Pre-set to "Birthday" means they know most people showing up need an occasion anchor.
- **Logo / brand clarity** — the 1-800-Flowers.com logo is clean and immediately recognizable. I know where I am.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as the entire first impression** — the full viewport is a legal notice. Not a flower, not a deal, not a mood. This is the digital equivalent of a greeter at a florist asking for your signature before you walk in the door.
- **No Passport member hook visible** — I pay for that membership. If I'm a logged-in or returning Passport member, I should see something acknowledging me immediately. I see nothing.
- **No offer or urgency** — I can't see a single dollar figure, percentage discount, or delivery deadline. The one thing that drives me to buy same-day is urgency, and it's completely absent.
- **No product or floral imagery above the fold** — I'm literally looking at gray text on white and a cookie policy. Flowers are emotional; I need to see them to feel anything.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or minimize the cookie banner** — make it a slim bottom bar, not a modal wall. Every second I spend reading GDPR language is a second I'm not converting.
- **Surface the Passport member benefit immediately** — a persistent "Free delivery on every order — Passport Member" chip in the header would remind me why I'm loyal and keep me from comparison shopping.
- **Add a same-day delivery urgency line** — something like "Order by 2PM for same-day delivery" right below the Search button. That's the specific thing that makes me choose 1-800-Flowers over a local florist when someone passes away.
- **Put one floral image behind the search tool** — even a color-wash background of peonies or lilies would shift the emotional register from "form to fill out" to "beautiful thing I'm about to send."
