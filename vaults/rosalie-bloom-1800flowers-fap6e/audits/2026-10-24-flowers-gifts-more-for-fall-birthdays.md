# Flowers, gifts & more for fall birthdays 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, numbered gift-guide email wrapped around a quiet offer. The "Fall Birthdays" script hero, the big burnt-orange numerals 1 to 4 and the alternating image/text layout are warm and seasonal. The eye moves down easily. The trouble is that the only money-saving offer is the $15 credit (HARVEST15, $59.99 minimum), and it is the same credit as the 10/17 "Save with a $15 Credit on Florist Favorites" send. Nothing in the hero says what you get or why to act today.
- The email also gives you nothing to act on. "Fall Birthdays" is a theme, not a reason. The four picks are blooms, gourmet, plants and a personalized board, and none shows a price. The credit banner is repeated twice at the top, which looks like a duplication bug. Compared with the 10/21 "25% Off Roses" send, which had a loud deal and a free vase, this one is weaker. It is competent and on-brand but easy to skim past.
- Verdict: pretty and well organized, but the offer is buried and the CTAs are generic, so it will click mostly with people already shopping for a birthday.

## What's working

- The numbered 1-4 layout with alternating image sides gives a clear scan path and a fun, editorial rhythm.
- The fall palette (orange, cream, rust) and the strong flower and gourmet photography match the season.
- Each module has a specific category link ("SHOP FLOWERS", "SHOP GOURMET", "SHOP PLANTS", "SHOP UNIQUE GIFTS"), which beats a generic "Discover".
- The personalized "CARLISLE" board and the handwritten annotations ("Personalize me", "A foodie's favorite gift") add personality and product detail.
- The "Shop by Category" grid near the bottom gives an easy exit for anyone who wants a specific occasion, such as Same-Day or Sympathy.

## What's weak

- The credit banner is stacked twice in the orange strip at the top, so it looks like a rendering error and wastes prime space.
- The offer is a $15 credit with a $59.99 minimum, shown in small light type. It is not tied to the hero and is easy to miss.
- No product shows a price, and none of the four modules shows a name or rating, so there is no social proof and little reason to click a particular item.
- The hero "SHOP NOW" button is small and generic and leads to no specific destination. Nothing makes birthdays feel urgent, even though the offer ends 11/22.
- The NYRR x 1-800-Flowers "26.2 DONE" tote bundle is a confusing tangent in the middle of a birthday guide.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the duplicate credit banner and make the offer the hero: "Birthday gifts + $15 off $59.99" with a code chip and a visible deadline.
- 2. Add prices or "from $" to each numbered module and link each to a specific product, not just a category.
- 3. Add one social-proof line, such as a star rating or "best seller" tag, on the flowers and the personalized board.
- 4. Make the hero button specific: "Shop Birthday Gifts". Move the NYRR bundle out of this send, or label it as a separate bonus.
- 5. Add a same-day delivery or "order by" cue for last-minute birthdays.
