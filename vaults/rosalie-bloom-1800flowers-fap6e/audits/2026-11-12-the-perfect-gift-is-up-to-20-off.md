# The Perfect Gift Is Up to 20% Off 🍂 🦃 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "November Highlights" catalog that went out on 11/10 ("Say Thanks Beautifully This November"), reissued with a new subject line. The hero still says "November Highlights" with a small "UP TO 20% OFF* WITH CODE FLOWERSALE" strip above it. The subject promises "The Perfect Gift Is Up to 20% Off," but the body is a long scroll of unrelated themes: Thanksgiving, a Scorpio birthday, romance and Christmas. Nothing in the email says which gift is "perfect."
- The render is clean. The photography is rich and the section blocks are tidy. The offer is buried: the only discount copy is a thin top strip and a footnote with an asterisk. The hero CTA is "GET GIFTING," which is vague, and four more "SHOP ___" buttons compete with it. Fatigue is the bigger issue. This is the third send in four days built on the same "up to 20% with FLOWERSALE" promise, after 11/09 and 11/10.
- Verdict: a competent catalog with no single reason to click. A reader who has already seen 11/10 will treat this as a repeat.

## What's working

- The code is visible at the very top: "UP TO 20% OFF* WITH CODE FLOWERSALE."
- The photography is warm and seasonal, with autumn and holiday tones that fit mid-November.
- Each section has a clear headline and its own button (Thanksgiving, Birthday, Love & Romance, Holiday).
- The "Nov. 26th" and "Dec. 25th" date badges give the Thanksgiving and holiday sections concrete anchors.
- The category grid and app-download footer are tidy, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The subject's "Perfect Gift" and the "Up to 20%" claim are not paid off in the body. The hero is a generic "November Highlights."
- The "Up to 20%" offer is a small strip and the "GET GIFTING" button is generic, so the offer does not lead the hierarchy.
- The email is long, with four themed blocks and a category grid. Mobile readers will not scroll that far, and no single hero is dominant.
- "Gift like a passionate Scorpio this season" is an odd, narrow astrology hook in the middle of a Thanksgiving and Christmas email.
- The email has no urgency or deadline and the product cards show no prices, so nothing makes the reader act today.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with one product and one offer, for example a bouquet photo with "20% off with code FLOWERSALE · ends Sunday." Make the CTA "Shop 20% Off Bouquets."
- 2. Cut the body to two sections, Thanksgiving centerpieces and holiday gifts. Drop the Scorpio and romance blocks, or move them to segmented sends.
- 3. Add prices and a "was/now" price on each product card.
- 4. Add a real deadline, such as the last day for guaranteed Thanksgiving delivery, instead of an open-ended "up to."
- 5. Stop re-sending the same highlights creative every two days. Either rotate the hero or space the sends out.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional email with dark-mode meta tags, but it has several accessibility gaps. The head also loads multiple third-party trackers, two of them over plain `http://`. Most compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits in the relay, so I can't confirm them as defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** Three images load over `http://`, which many clients will block or rewrite:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507...`
  - Blocked pixels fail silently, so conversion and retargeting data is lost. The empty params `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` also suggest unpopulated template fields.
- **Duplicate beacon.** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it likely fires twice per open.
- **Unprobed links.** 23 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.dv.1800flowers.com`). Destination validity is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Plain-text bloat.** 4,400 of 6,248 plain-text characters are URLs (70.4%), consistent with long tracking URLs. This is a mild spam-filter and readability risk.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes** on 7 flagged images:
  - the four trackers listed in section 2
  - the two `1622.gif` beacons
  - `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/4ba27736-f6d9-4c54-809f-beecb33fee82` (a Movable Ink dynamic image)
  - `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
  - For 1x1 trackers, `alt=""` is the fix. The `click.dv...` image appears to be content, so a missing alt there leaves it unannounced to screen readers and blank with images off. I can't confirm its role from the truncated source.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="...maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute.** `<html xmlns=... xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang`.
- **Dark-mode risk.** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, but `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text. If a client applies a dark background to those cells, contrast fails. I haven't confirmed the cell backgrounds.
- **Conflicting resets.** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` is overridden by `body {-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`, so the two rules conflict. This is low impact.
- **Webfonts.** The `@font-face` declarations (Playfair Display, Lato) are `woff2`/`woff` with `font-display:swap`. The truncated source doesn't show whether the `font-family` stacks include fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the visible source. The Movable Ink open pixel carries a populated recipient ID (`mi_u=174149195`). The source is truncated, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unconfirmed, likely relay artifacts.** The List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) and Authentication-Results warnings say the headers weren't captured by the AgentMail relay. These cannot be treated as real defects. Verify them against raw headers from a direct-delivery test (Gmail "Show original").
- **Physical address.** The QA check flagged no mailing address, but the footer is beyond the truncation point. Confirm in the full source before treating this as a violation.
- **Sender domain.** The From address is `1800FLOWERS@em.1800flowers.com`. This subdomain is distinct from the click-tracking domain `click.dv.1800flowers.com`. DKIM and SPF alignment for `em.` can't be assessed here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **No UTM parameters observed.** The only campaign tagging visible is Movable Ink: `mi_ecmp=261112_EMAIL_18F_PZ_NONGMAIL_DV_STO_PROMO`, plus `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. These aren't standard UTMs, so GA attribution depends on the redirect layer appending them.
- **Landing pages not verified.** The 23 skipped redirect links mean I can't confirm that the "Up to 20% Off" subject matches the landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, or drop them if the vendors no longer need them. Fill in or remove the empty `v1..s3` params.
2. Deduplicate the `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` beacon.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Add descriptive alt text to the Movable Ink content image (`click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/4ba27736-...`) if it carries content.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Audit dark-mode contrast for the forced `#000000` `th` text.
6. Re-run the compliance checks against raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and the full footer, instead of the relay copy.
7. Make sure the click-redirect layer passes UTMs through to the landing pages, and run a redirect-following probe on the 23 skipped links.
