# Staff must-haves: fall birthday gifts
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers finally doing something structurally different: a staff-picks format with named employees ("Mallory, Designer," "Jennifer, Deputy Editor," "T.K., Editorial Director") giving personal quotes about each product. It's a genuine creative departure from the "Big Birthday Energy Event" rerun the brand's been leaning on since 2026-09-10, and even distinct from the numbered gift-guide format used on 2026-09-22. The testimonial device adds warmth and a plausible reason to trust the picks.
- But the mechanic is basically a static "shop by editor" gift guide dressed up as birthday content — three of the five products (mums, ghost candy dish, fruit basket) read as general fall/Halloween decor rather than birthday gifts specifically, which undercuts the subject line's promise. The 25% off with code GLOW25 is a real, visible offer, but it's stuffed into a thin top banner and never repeated near any CTA, so it barely registers as you scroll through five product blocks.
- Net: a nice format experiment that's undermined by weak thematic focus and an offer that's easy to miss. Above the brand's recent floor, but not a standout.

## What's working

- Named staff quotes ("STAFF PICKS") give a distinct personal/curated feel not seen in recent sends.
- Five clear, individually-linked products, each with its own "SHOP NOW" — no dead-end homepage links.
- Clean, uncluttered layout with generous white space between sections — no overlapping text or broken images visible.
- "SHOP BY CATEGORY" grid at the bottom gives an easy fallback path if none of the five picks land.

## What's weak

- The 25% off/GLOW25 offer lives only in a thin top strip and is never reinforced near the hero or any CTA — easy to scroll past.
- Several "picks" (Pumpkin Patch Mum, Ghost Candy Dish, Cornucopia Fruit Basket) aren't birthday gifts at all, they're fall/Halloween decor — the subject says "birthday gifts" but the content drifts.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — the offer feels open-ended and skippable.
- Repeated "SHOP NOW" CTA text five times in a row reads flat and generic, not tied to what's actually being sold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the 25% off / GLOW25 code into the hero directly under "Our Fresh Picks for Fall Birthdays" — right now it's easy to miss in the thin top banner.
- 2. Tighten product selection to genuine birthday items only, or reframe the subject/hero to "fall favorites" if decor items stay in the mix — the mismatch between subject and content will cost opens on repeat sends.
- 3. Add a deadline ("Ends Sunday") near the offer to convert browsing into urgency.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per product ("Send Roses," "Gift the Mums") instead of five identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.

- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off our favorite fall birthday flowers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five gifts our editors swear by, from roses to fruit baskets`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Real staff picks + 25% off with code GLOW25`
