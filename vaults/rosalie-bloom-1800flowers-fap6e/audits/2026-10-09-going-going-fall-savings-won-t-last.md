# Going, Going... Fall Savings Won't Last
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, tiered-discount send. The hero says "Fall In Full Bloom" over a stacked 25% / 20% / 15% off ladder, with $99+ / $79 / $49 thresholds and a "SITE WIDE!" burst. The photo is warm, seasonal and on-brand. The eye goes to the offer first, which is correct. It's a better-built promo than the 10/07 "Handpicked Romantic Favorites, 25% Off" send, where the offer sat in a thin banner above the logo.
- The execution is mostly generic. Six product tiles have names but no prices, no ratings and no reason to pick one. The only real "Going, Going…" urgency is the "Offer ends 11:59 pm on 10/11/2026" line in the tiny fine print at the bottom. The hero button reads "USE CODE BESTDEAL" and doesn't tell me to do anything. The real shop button, "SHOP THE SALE", sits about a third of the way down. The category grid and app badges then pad out a very long email.
- Judgement: a solid, competent 6. Subscribers have had the 9/30 "Fall Picks from Our Amazing Moms" and 10/03 review-led bouquet sends this week, so this one reads as a volume play. The send is also the fourth or fifth discount push in ten days.

## What's working

- The 25/20/15% ladder is instantly legible and the "spend more, save more" mechanic is clear in one glance.
- The hero image is warm and seasonal, with a tabletop fall bouquet and an orange and gold palette that matches the "Fall In Full Bloom" headline.
- The "SITE WIDE!" burst removes any doubt about eligibility at a glance.
- The product grid is tidy and consistent, with six fall arrangements on a clean white background and no render problems.
- "Shop by Category" gives non-fall shoppers an exit to Birthday, Sympathy and Same-Day.

## What's weak

- "Going, Going…" promises urgency, but the deadline (10/11) appears only in the fine print. Nothing in the body says "ends Sunday."
- The hero button, "USE CODE BESTDEAL", is a code label rather than an action. The real "SHOP THE SALE" button comes much later.
- None of the six product tiles shows a price, a rating, or a "from $" figure, so the thresholds can't be matched to products.
- The "SITE WIDE!" claim is undercut by the dense disclaimer block, and a spend ladder needing $99 for the top tier means the "25% off" headline applies to a minority of baskets.
- The email is very long. Category tiles, app download badges and the brand family footer dilute a message that should end at "Shop the Sale."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero: "Ends Sunday 10/11" beside the code, and make the button say "SHOP FALL BOUQUETS – CODE BESTDEAL" so the code and the action are one click.
- 2. Put prices ("from $49", "from $79") and a star rating under each top pick, and mark which tier each one reaches.
- 3. Cut the app badges and brand-family block, and shrink the category grid to six tiles.
- 4. Show one real "bundle to $79" example, such as a bouquet plus a vase, so the ladder feels doable.
- 5. Repeat the "SHOP THE SALE" button directly under the top picks and at the bottom of the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: Up to 25% Off Fall Bouquets`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Blooms, 15–25% Off Through Sunday 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $99, save 25%. Use code BESTDEAL before it ends 10/11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sunflower, rose & harvest bouquets from $49. Code BESTDEAL.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: it has a viewport meta tag, dark-mode meta tags, `@font-face` fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint. The confirmed problems are legacy third-party pixels served over `http://`, a duplicated tracking pixel, and a fixed-width mobile rule. The compliance warnings come from the capture pipeline and the truncated source, so most can't be confirmed as defects in the sent email.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels (confirmed).** Three pixels load over `http://`, so mixed-content or proxy blocking may stop them firing:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&…`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&…`
  - Both mathtag endpoints also serve HTTPS, so the HTTP URLs can simply be upgraded.
  - `view.atdmt.com` is a legacy Atlas ad-server host. Verify it is still live.
- **Empty macro parameters in the mathtag pixels.** `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` are all blank. This is either intentional or unpopulated template variables.
- **Duplicate pixel.** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so the event may fire twice per open.
- **Tracking links not probed.** 24 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains. The QA run cannot confirm that destinations resolve, so I make no claim either way.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels.** The QA flags all five pixels, including `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/109972a4-…` and `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`. This is low impact for 1×1 pixels. Add `alt=""` to be explicit.
- **Dynamic image with no alt.** `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/109972a4-…` carries `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters, which look like a dynamically rendered content image rather than a pixel. If so, the missing alt matters more, because images-off clients show nothing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Possible overflow on narrow phones.** `@media (max-width:418px)` sets `.mobile` and `.showmobile` to `width:414px !important`. On a viewport narrower than 414px (a 375px iPhone, for example) any element using those classes would be wider than the screen. Whether it overflows depends on which elements carry the classes, and the truncated source doesn't show that.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Conflicting text-size rules.** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` is later overridden by `body {…:100%}`. It works, but it is redundant.
- **Unchecked content images.** The QA's missing-alt list contains only tracking and dynamic images. The truncated source doesn't let me assess alt text on the rest of the content.
- **Duplicate style blocks.** The source has two `<style>` blocks, and the second repeats rules from the first (`.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass`, `:root`). This adds weight but is not a functional issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=174149195` value in the pixel URL is a populated user ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified. It is not evidence the sender omitted them.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sending domain `em.1800flowers.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but I can't verify alignment.
- **No physical address detected.** The source is truncated, and a footer address is typically at the bottom, so this can't be confirmed as a violation. Check the full HTML.
- **Visible unsubscribe link not assessed.** It isn't in the supplied excerpt or the QA output.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters not verified.** All 24 links were skipped as tracking redirects, so I can't see whether the final URLs carry `utm_*` values.
- **Campaign identifier present.** `mi_ecmp=261009_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_PROMO` in the `mi.1800flowers.com` pixel suggests campaign attribution is handled through Movable Ink parameters, not UTMs. That is a valid approach but needs separate analytics mapping.
- **Landing page alignment not assessable.** The destinations weren't resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Upgrade the three `http://` pixels to `https://` (both mathtag endpoints, and confirm `view.atdmt.com` is still active or remove it).
2. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif`.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Give the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` dynamic image meaningful alt text if it shows content.
4. Replace the fixed `width:414px` on `.mobile`/`.showmobile` with `max-width:100%` or `width:100%`, and drop `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Confirm the populated or blank mathtag `v*`/`s*` parameters are intended.
6. Re-run compliance checks against raw message headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the full untruncated HTML (footer address, unsubscribe link). The relay and truncation make those results inconclusive.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page match.
