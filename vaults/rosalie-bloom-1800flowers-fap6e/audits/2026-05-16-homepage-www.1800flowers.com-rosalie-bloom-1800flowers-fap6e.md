# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- I opened this on my phone to find a sympathy arrangement and immediately hit a cookie wall before I could see a single flower. The sliver of homepage I can see shows a "Find the Perfect Gift" search tool with an occasion dropdown — which honestly is pretty smart for someone like me who always has a reason to order — but I can't get to it because the consent modal is sitting right on top of everything. This brand clearly knows its audience is occasion-driven, but right now it's making me work just to see the page.

## What's working

- **Occasion-based search tool** — ZIP code + Occasion dropdown with "Birthday" pre-selected is genuinely useful for someone who shops by event. I'd actually use that.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — simple, benefit-forward, not fussy. It doesn't condescend.
- **Clean header with account + cart icons** — I can see my account icon immediately, which matters since I'm a Passport member.
- **Chat button** — visible in the corner, which I appreciate for same-day sympathy orders when I need to ask about delivery cutoffs.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks everything** — I cannot see a single product, promo, or category. This is the first thing I see on a phone and it makes me want to close the tab.
- **No Mother's Day mention anywhere visible** — It's May 16th. This brand's biggest week of the year just ended and there's zero evidence of it above the fold.
- **No Passport / loyalty acknowledgment** — I pay for free shipping and there's nothing telling me my membership is active or giving me any member-specific reason to stay.
- **No pricing or offer signals** — Not a single "$X off" or "free delivery" line. For a same-day sympathy buyer, shipping cost is a real consideration.
- **No product imagery** — Flowers are a visual product. A cookie wall where the hero should be is a missed opportunity.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-screen cookie modal on mobile** — move it to a slim bottom bar with "Accept" and "Manage" so the homepage hero is actually visible. The modal kills the first impression on phone.
- **Add a seasonal/occasion banner above the search bar** — a single line like "Same-day sympathy delivery available" or "Mother's Day late orders — we can still help" would stop me from bouncing immediately.
- **Surface Passport membership status in the header** — a small "Passport Member" badge next to the account icon would confirm my benefits are active and give me a reason to stay over going to a competitor.
- **Put a concrete offer in the hero area** — even "Free delivery on orders over $X for Passport members" next to the Search button gives me something to act on.
