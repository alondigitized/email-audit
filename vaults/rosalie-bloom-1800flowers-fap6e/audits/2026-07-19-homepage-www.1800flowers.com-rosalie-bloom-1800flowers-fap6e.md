# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-07-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- I landed on 1-800-Flowers ready to find a sympathy arrangement for my neighbor's loss, and the very first thing I see is a wall of legal text about cookies. Not a single flower. Not a sale. Not even the reassurance that I'm in the right place. This feels like I accidentally opened a terms-of-service document, not a gift shop. The brand is clearly floral gifting, but right now it's pushing a consent form.

## What's working

- **Occasion-based search bar** — The "Find the Perfect Gift" bar with a ZIP Code + Occasion (Birthday) dropdown is exactly how I think when I'm buying. I think in occasions, not flower types. This is the right mental model for me.
- **Logo is clean and immediate** — I recognized the brand instantly from the purple 1-800-Flowers mark. No confusion about where I landed.
- **Live Chat button** — The "Chat" button in the lower right is visible even through the modal. On a sympathy buy where I'm uncertain about timing, knowing help is a tap away matters.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal blocks everything** — The entire homepage is obscured. I cannot see a single product, offer, or category. This is the first thing I experience, and it makes me want to close the tab.
- **No imagery whatsoever above the fold** — Not one flower, not one lifestyle photo. For a floral brand, that's a miss on the most basic brand promise.
- **No offer or urgency signal visible** — No "same-day delivery," no sale banner, no free shipping mention — the exact things that would hook me on a sympathy same-day order.
- **No Passport/membership visibility** — I'm a Passport member and that's literally why I come here. There's no badge, no welcome back, nothing acknowledging me.
- **Cookie banner copy is dense and legalistic** — Three paragraphs of GDPR-style text. On mobile, in a small font, with underlined link strings everywhere. I shouldn't have to read a privacy policy to order flowers.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie consent to a bottom bar** — Keep it compliant but stop making it a full-page takeover. Let me see at least the hero and one offer while the banner sits at the bottom asking to be dismissed.
- **Surface same-day delivery above the fold** — For sympathy buyers like me, "Order by 2pm for same-day delivery" is the single most important conversion message. It should be the first thing I read after the logo.
- **Add a Passport member welcome state** — Even a small "Welcome back, Rosalie — free shipping on your next order" strip at the top would remind me why I'm loyal. Right now I feel like a stranger.
- **Load a hero image behind the modal** — Even if the cookie layer is on top, having a soft floral lifestyle image visible around the edges signals "yes, you're in the right place" and reduces bounce anxiety.
