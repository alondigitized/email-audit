# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I landed on 1800flowers.com and immediately got smacked with a full-screen cookie consent wall that covered virtually everything below the fold. What little I *can* see tells me the brand knows I'm here for Mother's Day — the occasion dropdown is pre-set to "Mother's Day" — and the top-line hook "Find the Perfect Gift" is exactly on-message for me. But I can't see a single product, offer, or loyalty benefit because the cookie modal is sitting on top of all of it.

## What's working

- **Occasion-preselected search tool** — the ZIP + occasion dropdown with "Mother's Day" already loaded is smart and saves me a step; for an email-driven buyer arriving from a Mother's Day campaign link, this is frictionless
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" heading** — plain-spoken, direct, not trying to be clever; matches how I actually think about this site
- **Chat bubble** — as a medium-tech-comfort shopper I appreciate knowing help is available without digging

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks everything** — the entire middle and lower page is invisible; I cannot see a single product, promotional banner, or loyalty callout before I'm forced to click "Continue"
- **No Passport/loyalty acknowledgment** — I'm a paying member for free shipping; the above-the-fold experience gives me zero indication the site recognizes me
- **No visible offer** — there is no discount, free-shipping callout, or urgency copy above the fold; for an occasion gifter who's price-conscious around holidays, this is a missed hook
- **Search-first UX is cold** — for a returning shopper, being handed a blank search box before seeing any inspiration feels like I walked into an empty store

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal *before* capture or at minimum shrink it to a banner** — a full-screen interstitial as the first thing a mobile shopper sees on Mother's Day week is conversion-killing; a slim bottom banner achieves GDPR compliance without burying the merchandise
- **Surface Passport free-shipping benefit in the hero bar** — a one-line strip ("Free shipping for Passport members — you're covered") costs nothing and instantly reduces my hesitation
- **Add a seasonal urgency strip above the search form** — something like "Order by [date] for Mother's Day delivery" gives me the deadline I'm silently calculating anyway
- **Show one product recommendation or last-order prompt** — even a "You sent tulips last Mother's Day — send them again?" nudge would lock in returning buyers like me before we even search
