# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- I land on the 1800flowers homepage and the first thing I see is a cookie wall eating the entire screen. Before I can even get a sense of what's on sale or what the site looks like right now, I'm being asked to agree to tracking. The one sliver I can see — a "Find the Perfect Gift" tool with Birthday pre-loaded as the occasion — is actually a really smart touch for someone like me. But that cookie modal is doing real damage to first impressions.

## What's working

- **"Find the Perfect Gift" tool with Birthday default** — this is exactly how I shop. I know the occasion, I want to filter fast. Seeing "Birthday" already selected feels like the site knows me.
- **"Save Up to 40% on Exclusive Disney Gifts" banner** — it peeks through above the modal and at least tells me a deal exists. If I'm buying for a Disney-loving niece or grandkid, that's a hook.
- **ZIP Code + Occasion flow** — same-day delivery matters enormously to me, especially for sympathy orders. Having the ZIP field right there is genuinely useful.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal** — this is the biggest problem on the page. I can't see the hero, I can't see seasonal content, I can't see what the site is pushing right now. The modal forces a detour before I've been given any reason to care.
- **No Passport/loyalty visibility** — I'm a Passport member. Free shipping is why I keep coming back. I can't see any acknowledgment of that before the modal buries everything.
- **Disney promo feels off-target** — I'm here for sympathy arrangements, birthday bouquets, and Mother's Day standards, not Disney-branded gifts. That banner reads like it was left on from a partnership campaign, not something aimed at me.
- **No above-the-fold seasonal urgency** — for a florist in late May, there should be visible messaging about what occasion is coming next. I can't see any of that.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom bar or small corner modal** — the current full-page takeover kills the first impression before the brand has earned a second of my attention. A slim persistent bar converts better and doesn't bury the hero.
- **Surface Passport benefits in the sticky header** — even just "Free delivery, Rosalie" or a Passport badge next to my account icon would instantly reinforce why I chose this brand.
- **Make the gift-finder occasion list the visual anchor** — it's doing the right thing (Birthday default, ZIP field), but it's getting buried. Lead with this tool more prominently once the modal is cleared.
- **Replace the Disney banner with a seasonal urgency strip** — something like "Order by 2 PM for same-day delivery on sympathy arrangements" would be far more relevant to why I'm actually here.
