# Handpicked Romantic Favorites, 25% Off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a staff-picks email dressed in a promo. The offer is real but tiny. "ENJOY 25% OFF* WITH CODE GLOW25" sits in a thin banner above the logo. The hero says "What We're Loving for Fall" with a "SHOP LOVE & ROMANCE" button. The subject promises "Romantic Favorites," but the hero headline is about fall. The body mixes burgundy bouquets, red roses, chocolate strawberries and a tic-tac-toe set.
- The quote-per-product format is the strongest idea here. Staff pull quotes from a deputy editor, a copywriter and a design manager add some personality. It follows the testimonial-led approach of the 9/30 "Amazing Moms" send and the 10/3 review-led bouquets send. Subscribers have now seen that device three times in a week. Nothing here is urgent. The only deadline is buried in the fine print ("Offer ends at 11:59 p.m. (EST) on 10/18/2026"), so there is no reason to click today.
- Overall it's clean and readable but unfocused. A 25% code is the best discount in this week's run, yet it's the least visible element. The quotes carry the layout instead.

## What's working

- The 25% code is higher than the 20% GLOW20 from 10/1 and the 20% sends of 9/30.
- Staff quotes with names and titles feel human and break up the product grid.
- Each product has its own "SHOP NOW" link, and the hero image sizes vary, which gives the layout rhythm.
- The burgundy and red palette matches fall and the romance theme.
- The "Shop by Category" grid and app-download block are standard but give an exit for readers who don't want the featured items.

## What's weak

- The offer is a thin banner. The code is not repeated in the hero or at any CTA.
- The subject says "Romantic Favorites," but the hero headline reads "What We're Loving for Fall." The two don't match.
- No prices, ratings or reviews appear on any product, so there is no price or social proof to click on.
- Quote text dominates the layout, and the products are the secondary element. The eye lands on italic quotes rather than on an offer or a hero product.
- Product names are small and inconsistent. "Autumn Fields" and "Burgundy Bliss" have stray quote marks, and the Tic Tac Toe set is an odd fit for a flowers brand.
- The disclaimer block is dense, and the deadline appears only there.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero: "25% off, code GLOW25, through 10/18," set as a large headline with a button. The code should also appear near the product CTAs.
- 2. Align the subject, hero and landing page. Either make it a fall staff-picks email or a romance email, but not both.
- 3. Add prices, with a "was/now" at 25% off, under each product and make the CTA copy specific, such as "Shop Autumn Fields."
- 4. Cut the product count to three or four, and drop the novelty items so the email stays on category.
- 5. Put a visible deadline above the fold, such as "Ends 10/18."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with a dark-mode meta setup. It is mostly clean, but the viewport meta blocks zoom and the mobile CSS sets a fixed 414px width. It also carries several non-HTTPS third-party pixels with no alt attributes. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely relay artifacts, not confirmed defects, and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 29 tracking links were skipped because they are redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Insecure pixels:** Two pixel hosts load over `http://`.
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507…` and `mt_id=155438`
  - Clients that don't proxy images may block them, which would silently drop the conversion and retargeting data.
- **Legacy host:** `view.atdmt.com` is the old Atlas ad-server domain. It is worth confirming it still resolves and is still wanted.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it likely double-fires.
- **Empty parameters:** The mathtag URLs ship with `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`. If these were meant to carry data, they are unpopulated.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1 />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Possible mobile overflow:** Inside `@media (max-width: 418px)`, `.mobile` and `.showmobile` are set to `width:414px !important`. On 360–390px viewports, any element using these classes will be wider than the screen and cause horizontal scroll or clipping. Use `width:100% !important` instead.
- **Hardcoded colors in dark mode:** The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, but the CSS also hardcodes `th {color:#000000 !important}`, `th a {color:#000000 !important}` and `.appleLinks a {color:#000000 !important}`. If a client switches the background to dark, text in those elements can render black on dark. This is a risk I inferred from the CSS, not something I saw rendered.
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged these images.
  - The tracking pixels above, plus `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/3adbbaa6-…` and `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`.
  - These are 1×1 pixels, so the impact is low. They should still carry `alt=""` and a hidden style so screen readers skip them.
- **Font fallbacks:** Playfair Display and Lato are loaded via `@font-face` from fonts.gstatic.com. Only the Playfair Display 400 weights declare `mso-font-alt`, so other weights fall back unpredictably in Outlook. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The subject ("Handpicked Romantic Favorites, 25% Off") and the truncated HTML contain no unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%`. The only empty values are the mathtag parameters noted in section 2. I could not check the rest of the body because the source was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found. QA says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Check the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `1800FLOWERS@em.1800flowers.com`, and a header check at a non-relay inbox is needed.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect one. It is likely in the footer, which is cut off in the truncated source, so confirm it. If it is absent, that is a CAN-SPAM violation.
- **Unsubscribe link:** I could not confirm one from the visible source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None can be confirmed, because all 29 links were routed through tracking domains and not probed.
- **Campaign ID:** The open-pixel URL carries `mi_ecmp=261007_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`, so campaign attribution appears to run through Movable Ink / DV parameters, not UTMs. Check that these survive the redirect to the landing page.
- **Landing-page alignment:** I couldn't verify that the "25% off" landing page matches the email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
2. Change `.mobile` and `.showmobile` to `width:100% !important` (with `max-width:414px`) so nothing overflows on narrow phones.
3. Migrate `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` to HTTPS, or drop them if the vendors are retired. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel.
4. Add `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1" style="display:block"` to all tracking pixels.
5. Fix or remove the hardcoded `#000000 !important` colors on `th` and `.appleLinks a`, or add `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` overrides.
6. Inspect the raw headers at the origin to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't treat the relay warnings as proof of a defect.
7. Check the full, untruncated HTML for the footer postal address and a working unsubscribe link.
8. Resolve a sample of the 29 tracked links to verify landing pages, status codes and parameter persistence.
