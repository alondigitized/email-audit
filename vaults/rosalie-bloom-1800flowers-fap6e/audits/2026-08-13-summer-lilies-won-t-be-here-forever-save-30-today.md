# Summer lilies won't be here forever. Save 30% today
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Deal of the Week" rose-and-lily-adjacent flash-sale format 1-800-Flowers has been running all week, just swapped to Summer Lilies with the same "30% off" hero, the same "PLUS, A FREE VASE!" starburst, and the same teal-on-stripe treatment seen on the Aug 11 Sorbet Roses send and yesterday's near-identical rose repeat (both 9.0/10). The hero itself is clean and does its job — big offer, one button, a pretty bouquet shot, free-vase bonus visible immediately.
- Where this one stumbles relative to those two 9/10 sends is focus: right below the strong hero it stacks a "Behind the Bouquet" farm-fresh story, then a whole separate "20% Off Summer Best Sellers" promo with its own code, then a nine-tile category grid, then a membership pitch, then app download, then a brand wall. The lily offer that earned the subject line gets one shot at the top and is never reinforced — by the time a reader scrolls past the second discount code, "summer lilies" has been forgotten. It reads like two emails stapled together rather than one lily-focused flash sale.
- Judgement: solid, on-brand hero, but the body dilutes its own subject-line promise with a second unrelated offer and no reinforcement of lilies. That's a step down from the tight, single-SKU focus of the Aug 11 send.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the full offer in one glance: "30% OFF," a clear "SHOP SUMMER LILIES" button, and a "PLUS, A FREE VASE!" starburst — no scrolling required to get the pitch.
- Bouquet photography is vivid and on-season (orange/pink lilies against a soft stripe background).
- "Shop by Category" grid is clean, well-labeled, and gives multiple exit ramps (Birthday, Sympathy, Same-Day, etc.) without looking cluttered.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The subject promises lilies, but the email pivots to a second, unrelated "20% Off Summer Best Sellers" (code BLOOM20) featuring sunflowers, roses, cookies, and a lavender bouquet — none of them lilies — undercutting the single-offer urgency the subject sets up.
- After the hero, "summer lilies" is never mentioned or shown again; the "Behind the Bouquet" section shows a mixed arrangement, not clearly the lily bouquet being sold.
- Two competing discount codes/offers (30% off lilies vs. 20% off best sellers) in one email dilutes urgency and creates decision friction instead of a single clear CTA.
- Long tail of the email (membership pitch, app download, brand wall) pushes total scroll length well past what a flash-sale email needs, echoing the bloat problem more than the tight Aug 11 send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "20% Off Summer Best Sellers" block entirely (or move it to a separate send) — a flash-sale email should have one hero offer, not two competing discount codes.
- 2. Replace "Behind the Bouquet" with a second lily-specific product shot (e.g., a close-up or a second lily SKU) to reinforce the subject line's promise instead of pivoting to a generic story.
- 3. Add a countdown/deadline callout near the hero button ("Ends 8/30") to match the urgency implied by "won't be here forever."
- 4. Trim the footer stack (membership → app → brand wall) or compress into fewer sections to shorten the path back to a lily purchase.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Summer lilies, 30% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `30% off summer lilies + a free vase`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Farm-fresh lilies, a free vase, and 30% off — while summer lasts`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop today before summer lilies sell out`
