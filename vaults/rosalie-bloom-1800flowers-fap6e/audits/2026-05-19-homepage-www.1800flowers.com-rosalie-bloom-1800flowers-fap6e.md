# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened 1-800-Flowers and the first thing that happened was a giant cookie notice wall slammed down in front of everything. Behind it I can just barely make out "Find the Perfect Gift" with a ZIP code field and a Birthday occasion dropdown — which is actually exactly how I think when I shop here. But I can't see any of their flowers, any offers, any of the Mother's Day deals I was probably emailed about, because this modal is taking up roughly 80% of my screen.

## What's working

- **Occasion-based search form** — ZIP Code + Occasion dropdown with "Birthday" pre-selected is the right mental model for how I actually shop. I think "what occasion, what address" before I think "what product." This form mirrors my brain.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — simple, direct, not cute or clever. It says what I'm here to do, in language I'd actually use.
- **"Search" button** — big, clear, unmistakable. I know exactly what to tap even on a phone screen.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal consuming the entire screen** — I came here to shop for a gift, not to read about third-party data partners. This is the first thing I see and it's all legal text. On mobile especially, this is a gut punch to the opening experience.
- **Zero Passport/loyalty visibility** — I'm a Passport member. I pay for free shipping. There is no acknowledgment of me on this page anywhere. Not a "Welcome back" badge, not a shipping reminder, nothing.
- **No product imagery at all** — Flowers are a visual purchase. I need to see a beautiful arrangement to feel the pull. There's not a single stem visible above the fold.
- **No offer or urgency hook** — If I was emailed a promo code or a Mother's Day deal, I have no confirmation that it's live when I land here.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie notice to a bottom banner**, not a full-screen takeover. Let me see the hero behind it. Every other major retailer does this now — there's no reason to wall off your entire homepage.
- **Add a Passport/member welcome strip** — even a slim bar at the top saying "You have free shipping. Shop now →" would make me feel recognized and reduce friction.
- **Surface one hero arrangement image** — even behind the search form, a lush floral lifestyle shot (not a product white-background photo) would pull me in emotionally before I've typed a single character.
- **Lead with the Mother's Day campaign** — it's mid-May. If there's a countdown or a curated collection for Mother's Day, it should be the first thing I see, not something I have to scroll to find after clearing a legal wall.
