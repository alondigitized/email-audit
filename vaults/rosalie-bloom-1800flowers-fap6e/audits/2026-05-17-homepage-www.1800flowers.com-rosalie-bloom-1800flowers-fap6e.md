# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened 1-800-Flowers on my phone expecting to see some gorgeous Mother's Day arrangements — it's May, for goodness sake — and instead I'm staring at a cookie banner that's swallowed the entire page. What I *can* see is a clean gift-finder widget with a ZIP code field and a "Birthday" occasion dropdown, which actually speaks to exactly why I'm here. But between that modal wall and not a single arrangement in sight, I'm already annoyed.

## What's working

- **Gift-finder widget** — the ZIP code + Occasion dropdown + "Search" button is genuinely useful. I know my cousin's address by heart and "Birthday" is pre-selected. That's efficient.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — no fuss, no lifestyle-speak. It's direct, which I appreciate at my age.
- **Occasion dropdown** — the fact that "Birthday" is the pre-populated option tells me this site understands its audience. I buy for occasions, not trends.
- **Chat button** — visible in the bottom right. If I need same-day sympathy delivery, I might actually use that.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocking everything** — It's covering the entire homepage. I cannot see a single product, banner, or promotion. This is the first thing I see and it's a wall of legal text. I didn't come here to make data-privacy decisions; I came to buy flowers.
- **Zero Mother's Day presence** — It's the week of Mother's Day and I see nothing. No banner, no urgency, no "order by Sunday" countdown. This is a massive missed merchandising moment.
- **No Passport/loyalty recognition** — I pay for free shipping. If the site can't recognize me as a returning member in the first screen, that's a failure. No "Welcome back, Rosalie" or membership badge anywhere.
- **No product imagery whatsoever** — Flowers are a visual purchase. I need to *see* what I'm buying. Not one bouquet, not one arrangement.
- **No pricing or shipping offer visible** — I always wonder if my Passport benefit applies. That reassurance is missing entirely.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal automatically for returning users**, or at minimum reduce it to a slim bottom bar — don't open the session with a legal document covering the whole screen.
- **Put a Mother's Day urgency banner immediately above the gift-finder** — "Order by Sunday 5/11 for guaranteed delivery" with a countdown. It's the most time-sensitive week of their year.
- **Surface the Passport free-shipping benefit** in the header or directly below the search button — one line: "Passport members: free shipping on this order" would make me feel seen and reduce hesitation.
- **Add one featured product image** (a sympathy bouquet, a birthday arrangement) directly below the gift-finder, even just a thumbnail strip, so I have something visual to react to before I commit to a search.
