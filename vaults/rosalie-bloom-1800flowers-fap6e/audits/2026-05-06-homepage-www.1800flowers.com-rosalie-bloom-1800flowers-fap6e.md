# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- I opened this site expecting to jump straight into Mother's Day flowers — and instead the first thing I see is a giant cookie consent modal that swallows the entire page. Whatever they've built behind it, I can't see it. The only saving grace is that their little gift-finder widget peeked through up top with "Mother's Day" already pre-loaded in the dropdown, which — okay, fine, that's smart. But right now this homepage is essentially a wall of legal text with a Continue button.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day pre-selection in the gift finder** — whoever configured that occasion default earns their paycheck. I didn't have to do a thing; it already knows why I'm here.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" copy** — direct, functional, zero fluff. That's how I like to shop.
- **Clean logo and header** — the 1-800-Flowers branding is immediately recognizable; I know I'm in the right place.
- **"Search" button sizing** — large tap target, easy to hit on a phone screen without pinching.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal eating the entire viewport** — I can't see a single product, offer, or image. This is the digital equivalent of a store clerk blocking the door to read me terms and conditions before I can walk in.
- **No offer, no urgency** — Mother's Day is days away and I see zero "Order by Sunday," zero "$20 off," zero "Free delivery today." The one moment when I have maximum urgency to buy, and the homepage is silent on deals.
- **No Passport/loyalty hook visible** — I'm a Passport member. I should see my free-shipping status the second I land. It's nowhere.
- **No product imagery at all** — I'm a visual shopper for gifts. Flowers especially need to look beautiful. Behind that modal? Nothing. I have no idea if this is a luxury bouquet site or a grocery-store pickup.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal for returning visitors** (or at minimum delay it 3–5 seconds so the hero has a chance to render and hook me before the legal wall drops).
- **Add a Mother's Day countdown strip above the gift finder** — "Order by Sunday, May 10 for Mother's Day delivery" with a visible timer. I'll panic-buy if you remind me I'm running out of time.
- **Surface my Passport badge in the header** — a small "Passport Member · Free Shipping" indicator next to the account icon costs nothing and reminds me why I chose this site over FTD.
- **Load a lifestyle hero behind the finder** — a warm photo of a mom receiving pink peonies makes me feel the gift before I search; the finder alone is functional but cold.
