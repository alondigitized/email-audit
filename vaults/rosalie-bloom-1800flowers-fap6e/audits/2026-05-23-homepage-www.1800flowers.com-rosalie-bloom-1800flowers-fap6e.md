# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened this on my phone and immediately got a cookie wall shoved in my face before I could see a single bouquet, arrangement, or deal. From what little I can see behind the modal — a "Find the Perfect Gift" search bar with a Birthday occasion dropdown and a ZIP code field — 1-800-Flowers is clearly trying to be the on-demand gifting app, and that search-first approach makes sense for someone like me. But I cannot shop what I cannot see, and right now this homepage is working harder to get my data consent than my credit card.

## What's working

- **The search widget concept** — ZIP code + Occasion dropdown with "Birthday" pre-selected is exactly how my brain shops. I think "it's Aunt Cheryl's birthday" before I think "I want hydrangeas," so leading with occasion is smart for me.
- **The "Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — soft, warm, not pushy. It sounds like the brand I know from years of ordering sympathy arrangements. Gets the tone right.
- **Clean logo and nav bar** — the logo is immediately recognizable, account and cart icons are where I expect them, hamburger menu is accessible.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as the first thing I see** — this is the biggest problem. I have not seen one flower, one price, or one deal. For a sympathy purchase where I need same-day delivery, I would have already bounced to a competitor.
- **No offer visible at all** — I am a Passport member who expects to see free shipping confirmed on arrival. There is nothing here reinforcing why I should stay loyal.
- **No seasonal urgency** — today is May 23. Mother's Day just passed and Memorial Day is coming. There is no campaign visible, no "last-chance" banner, nothing that tells me this page is alive and up-to-date.
- **No product imagery above the fold** — flowers are an emotional, visual purchase. I need to see a gorgeous arrangement to feel inspired to buy. A search bar alone does not move me emotionally.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or minimize the cookie modal on return visits** — if I am a known Passport member logged in, you already have my consent. Do not hit me with the full-page block every session.
- **Surface a Passport member benefit line directly under the search bar** — something like "Free delivery on every order · Your Passport benefits are active" would lock in my loyalty immediately.
- **Add a thin urgency banner above the search widget** — "Same-day delivery available · Order by 2 PM" would speak directly to my sympathy-flowers use case and push me to act now.
- **Place one lifestyle hero image behind or beside the search tool** — a lush sympathy arrangement or a birthday bouquet in someone's hands gives me the emotional pull that a form field alone cannot.
