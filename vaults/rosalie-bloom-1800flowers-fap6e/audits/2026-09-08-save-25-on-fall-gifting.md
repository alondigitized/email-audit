# Save 25% on fall gifting
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine shift out of the "Double Blooms" rut and out of the late-summer urgency mode 1-800-Flowers has been running — "Cozy Season is Coming" pivots to a fall-gifting seasonal reframe with a warm, autumnal hero (sweater weather couple, sunflowers, a "Life is in bloom with you" script line) that feels distinct from the last several sends. It's a genuinely well-produced email: five clean occasion modules (Birthday, Love & Romance, Just Because, category grid) each with its own lifestyle photo and two linked products, all legible with no render bugs.
- The problem is focus. Unlike the tight, single-offer "Final Bloom Sale" (2026-09-03, 8/10) or the coherent Virgo Birthday send (2026-09-02, 9/10), this one sprawls across four unrelated occasions (fall gifting, birthday, romance, "just because") stitched under one HARVEST25 code, echoing the kitchen-sink problem of the 09-04 "Summer's Grand Finale" send. The top banner promise ("25% off with code HARVEST25") never resurfaces as a strong CTA anywhere below the hero — everything after "Shop Now" is soft browsing, not converting.
- Solid production, diffuse targeting. This lands as a mid-pack send — better than the Double Blooms treadmill, weaker than the focused Virgo or Final Bloom sends.

## What's working

- The fall pivot ("Cozy Season is Coming," sweater-weather couple, warm kitchen/harvest styling) is a legitimate seasonal turn away from the repetitive summer sends.
- Clean, consistent visual system — each section pairs a lifestyle photo with two product cards, name + price-free labels, no overlapping text or broken images.
- The 25% off code is stated clearly and specifically in the top banner (HARVEST25).
- Good category breadth at the bottom (Birthday, Romance, Sympathy, Plants, Fall, Gourmet Gifts) gives multiple entry points for different intents.
- Brand family footer (Harry & David, Cheryl's, Personalization Mall) reinforces recognizability and cross-sell without cluttering the main content.

## What's weak

- Four disconnected occasions (fall gifting / birthday / romance / "just because") crammed into one email dilutes the "Cozy Season" hook established in the hero.
- The 25% off offer is only visible once, in the banner — none of the five product/section CTAs ("Shop Birthday," "Shop Love & Romance," "Shop Just Because") repeat or reinforce the discount, so by the time a reader scrolls past the hero the offer is out of sight and out of mind.
- No urgency or expiration is shown anywhere in the visible creative — no deadline, no "ends Sunday," nothing time-bound to justify acting now.
- CTA copy is generic and repetitive ("Shop Now," "Shop Birthday," "Shop Love & Romance," "Shop Just Because") — none of it names a product or a fall-specific reason to click.
- No fall-specific product is actually the hero product — the "Harvest Glow Bouquet" and "Cornucopia of Fruit Basket" (arguably the most on-theme items) are buried two-thirds down the email, not up top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to one occasion per send. Either commit fully to "Cozy Season" fall gifting (lead with Harvest Glow Bouquet, Cornucopia Basket, fall wreaths) or split birthday/romance/just-because into separate sends — the Virgo Birthday send proved a focused single-theme email scores far better than a bundled one.
- 2. Repeat the HARVEST25 offer at least once mid-email, ideally as a sticky reminder near each "Shop" CTA, so the discount is still visible after the hero scrolls away.
- 3. Add a credible deadline ("Offer ends 9/14") to the hero banner — right now there's zero urgency despite the code implying a promo window.
- 4. Swap generic CTA copy for product-specific verbs: "Shop the Harvest Glow Bouquet" instead of "Shop Just Because."
- 5. Promote the fall-themed products (Harvest Glow, Cornucopia) into the hero module so the top-of-email visual matches the top-of-email copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off fall bouquets — code HARVEST25`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sweater weather, fresh flowers: 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall arrivals, plus birthday & just-because picks — all 25% off with HARVEST25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cozy season starts here: harvest bouquets, cornucopia baskets, and more.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a standard 1-800-Flowers promotional HTML email using a table-based responsive layout, but it fails multiple compliance and deliverability checks — missing unsubscribe headers, no detectable physical address, and several non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 27 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains such as `click.dv.1800flowers.com`), so their final destinations are unverified.
- Two tracking pixels load over plain HTTP, which many mail clients will block or flag: `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/` and `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507...` (and a duplicate `mt_id=155438` variant).
- Additional tracking/pixel calls present: `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (appears twice — likely redundant tag firing), `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/9b837642-...`, and `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=260908_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_20P_PROM`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Multiple `<img>` tags are missing `alt` text, including the two HTTP tracking pixels, the MathTag pixels, both BounceExchange `1622.gif` tags, the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirect image, and the `mi.1800flowers.com` open pixel. These are non-content tracking pixels, so the missing alt text has no visible accessibility impact, but it does indicate the template's alt-text pass isn't applied uniformly across all `<img>` elements.
- CSS includes standard Outlook/mobile-client hardening (`mso-line-height-rule`, `.ExternalClass`, `@media max-width:418px` block), plus `color-scheme: light dark` for dark-mode support — no issues found in the base layout markup shown.
- Custom web fonts (Playfair Display, Lato) are loaded via `@font-face` from `fonts.gstatic.com` with `mso-font-alt` fallbacks to Times New Roman — correctly degrades for Outlook/non-supporting clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization fields (e.g., `{{first_name}}`) are visible in the truncated source. The `mi_mid`/`mi_u` parameters in tracking URLs appear to be recipient/session identifiers rather than rendered merge tags — no broken or unresolved tokens found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: Not found. May be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing it, or it may genuinely be absent — cannot confirm RFC 2369 compliance from available data.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header**: Not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) support unconfirmed.
- **Physical mailing address**: Not detected in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires this; confirm whether it exists in the untruncated footer before treating as a violation.
- **Authentication-Results header**: Not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.

Note: these are QA-tool warnings, not confirmed failures — the truncated source cuts off before the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical address may exist outside the sampled HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The `mi.1800flowers.com` pixel includes `mi_ecmp=260908_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_20P_PROM`, indicating a campaign-tracking taxonomy is in place, but no standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are visible on the sampled links — 1-800-Flowers appears to use proprietary `mi_*`/`cp_t` params instead of UTM. Cannot verify landing-page alignment since the 27 click-redirect destinations were not resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the two HTTP-only tracking pixels (`atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com`) to HTTPS to prevent mixed-content blocking in modern mail clients.
2. Add `alt=""` (empty, decorative) to all tracking/beacon `<img>` tags to pass accessibility linting cleanly, even though they're non-visual.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent at the SMTP layer — if the relay is stripping them, that's an infrastructure gap; if truly absent, add RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
4. Verify a physical mailing address is present in the full footer (outside the truncated sample) to confirm CAN-SPAM compliance.
5. Resolve and audit a sample of the 27 skipped click-redirect URLs to confirm they land on valid, matching pages (e.g., the fall-gifting/25%-off promo landing page).
6. Deduplicate the two identical `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel calls if not intentional.
