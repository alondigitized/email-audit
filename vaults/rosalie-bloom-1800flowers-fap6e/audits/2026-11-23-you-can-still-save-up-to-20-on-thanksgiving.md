# You Can Still Save Up To 20% On Thanksgiving
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the best moment the Thanksgiving campaign has had in a while. It's the last-chance send, it's three days out, and the subject finally names the holiday and the deadline-ish nature of the offer ("You Can Still Save"). The hero is clean: a warm family-dinner photo, a script "Thanksgiving" wordmark, a thin "UP TO 20% OFF* WITH CODE FLOWERSALE" strip on top, and a single SHOP NOW button. The copy ("your last chance to send that host gift or order that centerpiece you've been eyeing") has real urgency.
- Below the hero it turns into the usual 1-800-Flowers catalog. There are four product grids, each with a yellow button, then a nine-tile category grid and app badges. The hero is strong, but the body gives a skimmer no reason to pick one path over another. The offer is still "up to 20%" with no tiers visible, even though the 11/20 send ("Your Thanksgiving Savings: Up to 20% OFF") showed the spend thresholds that make the offer concrete. This is a cleaner, more urgent version of a template the brand has sent many times since 11/10.
- Verdict: a solid, timely send with a good hero. It loses points for a vague offer, a code that is easy to miss, and a lot of scroll with no deadline in sight.

## What's working

- The hero photo is warm and on-theme, with a clear script headline and one obvious SHOP NOW button.
- The "last chance" copy gives the email real timing urgency, three days before the holiday.
- The product grids are tidy and consistent, and each one has a yellow button with a specific label (Shop Centerpieces, Shop Flowers, Shop Sweets, Shop Best Sellers).
- The section headlines are well chosen: "The Center of Attention" and "Fresh for Thursday." They tie products to the occasion.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlaps or gaps.

## What's weak

- "Up to 20% off*" is vague, and the spend tiers from the 11/20 send are missing. The code FLOWERSALE is in a thin strip at the very top and gets lost.
- The email has no visible deadline or delivery cutoff, such as "order by Tuesday for Thursday delivery." That is the thing that would make "last chance" credible.
- The body is long, with four product blocks, nine category tiles and app badges. Product tiles show no prices, no sale price and no reviews.
- The "Gourmet Dessert" and "Shop by Category" sections (birthday, romance, sympathy) dilute the Thanksgiving focus. The cadence is also heavy: this is another near-identical catalog template after 11/10, 11/12 and 11/13.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the code and the cutoff in the hero as a button or badge: "Code FLOWERSALE · Order by Tue 11/25 for Thursday delivery." That one change would do the most for this email.
- 2. Restore the 11/20 tiers (spend $149+ for 20%, $99+ for 15%, $69+ for 10%) as a compact strip under the hero, so "up to 20%" becomes concrete.
- 3. Add prices or "from $XX" to the product tiles, and add a star rating to the best sellers.
- 4. Cut to two product blocks (centerpieces and flowers). Drop the generic category grid in a send this close to the holiday.
- 5. Make the hero button specific, for example "Shop Thanksgiving Centerpieces."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Order by Tuesday: Thanksgiving Flowers, Up to 20% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 Days Left: Centerpieces for Thursday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code FLOWERSALE. Delivery still available before the holiday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fall centerpieces, host gifts and sweets, still in time for Thursday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with dark-mode meta tags and responsive CSS. Its main confirmed problems are mixed-content tracking pixels, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and no `lang` attribute. The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because of the relay, not because of a confirmed defect. The HTML was truncated, so everything below the `<head>` is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 23 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels (confirmed):** three pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`

  Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop these, so the third-party tracking will be incomplete.
- **Empty parameters:** the mathtag pixels send `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` with no values. This looks like unpopulated template slots, so those pixels carry no segment or order data.
- **Many trackers:** the email loads five or more distinct tracking and retargeting pixels: atdmt, mathtag (two), bounceexchange `1622.gif` (twice), `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/...` and `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif`. `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it likely fires twice.
- **Plain-text part:** 4,400 of 6,251 characters (about 70%) are URLs. Long tracking URLs in the text part can hurt readability and spam scoring.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked (confirmed):** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Missing `lang` (confirmed):** `<html xmlns="http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml" xmlns:v=... xmlns:o=...>` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't select the right language.
- **Missing alt text:** the QA flagged all of these tracking pixels (`atdmt`, `mathtag`, `1622.gif`, `click.dv…/o/b47b7086…`, `o.gif`). They should still carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. Alt text on the visible content images could not be checked in the truncated source.
- **Generic title:** `<title>1800Flowers.com</title>` doesn't describe the message. This is minor.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` for Playfair Display and Lato loads from `fonts.gstatic.com`. Outlook and some webmail clients ignore it, and the full fallback stacks were cut off, so they are unverified.
- **Global rule:** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` is overridden by the `body` rule (`100%`). The `*` rule should be removed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The pixel URLs contain a populated subscriber identifier (`mi_u=174149195`) and campaign code (`mi_ecmp=261123_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_TG_STO_DV_UPTO20P_…`), which appear to be rendering correctly. The truncated body can't be checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** the QA did not find either header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a verified defect. Check the raw message headers before reporting it as a problem.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, as expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are **unknown** for the From domain `em.1800flowers.com`.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be assessed. The 23 links were skipped by the probe, and the CTA `href`s are outside the truncated HTML. The only campaign identifier seen is `mi_ecmp=261123_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_TG_STO_DV_UPTO20P_`, which carries a date (261123 = 2026-11-23) and the "UPTO20P" offer in a vendor-specific format rather than standard `utm_*` parameters.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, and populate or remove the empty `v1–v3` and `s1–s3` parameters.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`. Use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` on tracking pixels, and write a descriptive `<title>`.
4. Deduplicate the `bounceexchange 1622.gif` pixel and consolidate the retargeting pixels.
5. Inspect the raw headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and SPF, DKIM and DMARC. Re-run the audit against the full HTML, since the QA couldn't evaluate these.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for the 23 skipped links to confirm landing URLs and check for `utm_*` or equivalent parameters.
7. Shorten the plain-text part's URLs, or reduce the number of tracked links, to bring the URL ratio down.
