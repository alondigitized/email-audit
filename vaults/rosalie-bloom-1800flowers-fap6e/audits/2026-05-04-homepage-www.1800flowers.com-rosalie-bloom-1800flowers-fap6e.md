# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers, a brand I rely on multiple times a year — birthdays, sympathy, Mother's Day — so I came in ready to shop. But the very first thing I see is a giant cookie consent wall covering almost the entire screen. All I can make out behind it is "Find the Perfect Gift" and a search widget with an Occasion dropdown that's pre-set to Mother's Day. The brand itself feels right for me, but right now it's actively hiding from me.
- **3/10**
- + Hero copy "Find the Perfect Gift" speaks directly to my gifting mindset — that's why I'm here
- + Occasion dropdown is pre-selected to "Mother's Day," which reflects the current season (May 4 — Mother's Day is days away)
- − No concrete offer is visible above the fold; the cookie wall buries everything
- − No loyalty or Passport member hook visible
- − No unmistakable shop CTA (Search is a utility button, not a conversion driver)
- − Visual hierarchy is wrecked — the cookie modal is the loudest element on screen
- − Cannot assess demographic signals, render quality of the actual page, or campaign imagery
- − No trust signals, no honesty check possible, no pricing visible

## What's working

- **"Find the Perfect Gift" + occasion widget:** The search tool with "Mother's Day" pre-selected is smart — it meets me right where I am and tells me the site already knows what season it is.
- **Occasion-based search paradigm:** As someone who shops by event (birthday, sympathy, Mother's Day), this widget is exactly the right UX model for me. ZIP code + Occasion is how I think.
- **Teal brand color:** The header color peeks through and feels warm and premium — I associate it with the brand and it reads correctly.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocks the entire homepage:** This is the single biggest problem. I came to see flowers and feel inspired. Instead I'm reading legal copy. This is not how you greet a return customer.
- **No Passport member recognition or loyalty hook:** I've paid for free shipping — there should be something that says "Welcome back" or shows my account status. Total silence on that.
- **Zero offers visible above the fold:** May 4 with Mother's Day imminent is prime urgency territory. No banner, no "Order by [date] for guaranteed delivery," no discount — nothing I can see.
- **"Continue" button on cookie wall competes with "Search":** Two call-to-action buttons on screen at once, and the more prominent one is for cookies, not flowers. That's backwards.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner or replace it with a slim bottom bar:** A full-page modal on a gifting site during peak season (Mother's Day week) is leaving money on the table. Move it to a persistent footer strip so the hero and product imagery are immediately visible.
- **Add a Passport/member welcome strip in the header:** Something like "Hi Rosalie — free shipping on your next order" would tell me my membership is active and give me a reason to stay.
- **Surface a delivery deadline banner above the fold:** "Order by May 9 for Mother's Day delivery" with a countdown would get me clicking immediately. That's the urgency signal this page needs right now.
- **Make the occasion search widget the primary visual CTA, not just a utility box:** Style it more prominently — bigger, with a bouquet image alongside it — so it reads as the hero action, not an afterthought.
