## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with light/dark `color-scheme` meta tags and a mobile breakpoint at 418px. The confirmed issues are tracking pixels with missing alt text or plain-HTTP sources, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The three compliance warnings can't be confirmed because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 31 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped for HTTP probing, so destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** Two third-party pixels load over `http://`, which can be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - A third, `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`, has the same problem.
- **Empty pixel parameters:** The mathtag pixels send `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` with no values. This may be intentional, but it could also mean the dynamic macros weren't populated.
- **Pixel count:** The email carries at least six tracking pixels:
  - atdmt
  - mathtag ×2
  - `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (appears twice)
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/21ba12d4-...`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif`

  The duplicated `1622.gif` may double-count opens or add load.
- **Plain-text part:** 5,936 of 7,344 characters (~81%) are URLs, most likely long tracking links. This is a spam-filter risk and hurts plain-text readability.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4). The universal `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule also disables text-size adjustment, though `body` later overrides it.
- **Missing alt text:** `alt` is missing on all the tracking pixels listed in section 2, plus `1622.gif` (twice), `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/21ba12d4-...` and `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif`. Decorative or tracking images should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them cleanly. This is low severity. The QA data didn't flag any content images, but the HTML is truncated.
- **Possible dark-mode contrast:** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text even though `color-scheme: light dark` is declared. If a client applies a dark background, this text could be unreadable. This needs a dark-mode render test. The truncated source doesn't show the `th` backgrounds.
- **Duplicated CSS:** `:root {color-scheme...}`, `body` and `.ReadMsgBody` are declared in both `<style>` blocks. This isn't harmful but adds weight.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient ID in `mi_u=174149195` is populated. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out. The empty `v1`–`s3` pixel parameters (section 2) are the only possible unpopulated values.

## 5. Compliance
- **Unsubscribe headers:** Neither `List-Unsubscribe` nor `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them. This is unconfirmed, and both headers should be checked in the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** None was detected. The HTML is truncated and the footer isn't visible, so this may be a false positive. Verify the footer for a CAN-SPAM postal address.
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`. Verify alignment on the raw message.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
No UTM parameters are visible in the available data, and 31 links weren't followed, so landing-page alignment can't be assessed. The only campaign identifier seen is `mi_ecmp=261104_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`, which sits on the open-tracking pixel rather than a destination link. Whether the click links carry `utm_*` parameters or only `mi_*`/`mi_cid` parameters is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the atdmt and mathtag pixels to `https://`, or drop them if the vendors don't support it.
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels, and remove the duplicate `1622.gif`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rule from `*`.
4. Test dark-mode rendering of `th`/`th a` with the forced `#000000` color.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the footer postal address against the raw source rather than the relay copy.
6. Confirm the mathtag macros resolve to values instead of empty strings.
7. Resolve a sample of the 31 skipped tracking links to check destinations and UTM/`mi_*` parameters.
8. Trim the URL weight in the plain-text part, for example with shorter link-tracking domains.
