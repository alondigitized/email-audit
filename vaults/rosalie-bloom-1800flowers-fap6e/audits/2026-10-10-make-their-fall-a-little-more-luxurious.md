- This is a clean, numbered gift guide, and it looks premium. The "Gifts That Say Fall Luxury" script headline sits over five large, well-shot products: roses, a chocolate rose box, preserved roses and chocolate strawberries. - The numbered 1–5 layout with alternating image sides gives a clear rhythm and is easy to scan.
- The photography is warm and seasonal, and the preserved roses and chocolate strawberries look indulgent.
- Small callouts like "Same-day delivery available" and "Stays preserved for up to one year!" answer real buying questions.
- The "Shop by Category" grid near the bottom gives a second route for readers who didn't like the five picks.

- The 25% offer is in a thin banner above the logo and is not repeated in the body, so it is easy to miss.
- The only call to action in the hero is a small text link, "SHOP LUXE". There is no button and no CTA anywhere else in the body.
- No prices or ratings appear on any of the five products.

- It is a near-duplicate of the 10/08 "Gift Luxuriously This Fall" send, with the same headline and layout and the same 25% code message as the 10/07 send.

- 2. Replace the "SHOP LUXE" text link with a filled button, and add a button under each numbered product with a price, such as "Shop Roses, $79".
- 3. Put the deadline in the body, for example "Ends Oct 16".
- 4. Add a star rating or a one-line review to each product, as the 10/03 review-led send did.
- 5. Don't send a second "Fall Luxury" guide two days after the first. Give this one a distinct angle, such as "Last-minute luxe gifts that arrive same-day".

# Technical Audit: "Make Their Fall a Little More Luxurious ✨"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard for a large-brand ESP template, with VML/MSO namespaces, dark-mode meta tags and web-font fallbacks. The QA warnings are mainly header-capture gaps from the AgentMail relay, plus several third-party tracking pixels that have no alt text, and two of them use `http://`. The source was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets could not be fully checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not confirmed good or bad.
- **Two pixels use plain `http://`** and can be blocked or rewritten by clients that enforce HTTPS:
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
- **`http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`** also uses plain `http://`. The domain is a legacy Atlas/Microsoft ad-tracking host, so confirm the pixel is still active.
- **Empty macro parameters:** the mathtag URLs carry `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`. These are empty rather than unresolved tokens, so they may be intentional, but they send no data.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels** (all flagged by QA):
  - `view.atdmt.com/.../01/`
  - both `pixel.mathtag.com` images
  - both `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif`
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/68dc2157-...`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`

  These are invisible pixels, so the impact is low. Adding `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` stops some clients from showing a broken-image icon or reading out the filename.
- **Viewport meta blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dark-mode contrast risk:** the template declares `color-scheme: light dark`, but `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text. If those cells sit on a background that inverts in dark mode, the text could be unreadable. I couldn't confirm this because the body markup was truncated.
- **Ineffective text-size reset:** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` is applied to `*` instead of `html`/`body`, so it likely has no effect. The `body` rule's `100%` value is the one that applies.
- **Fixed mobile image widths:** `.mobile` and `.showmobile` force `width:414px !important` below 418px. Viewports narrower than 414px (for example 360px Android) could overflow.
- **Web fonts:** Playfair Display and Lato load from `fonts.gstatic.com` with `font-display:swap`. This is acceptable, and the `mso-font-alt` fallbacks cover Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]` or similar tokens appear in the visible source. The remainder was truncated, so this is not fully confirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are actually absent, one-click unsubscribe won't work, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **No physical address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one, and the footer likely contains it. The source was truncated, so verify against the full HTML before treating this as a violation.
- **`Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `em.1800flowers.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is a good sign, but nothing here verifies alignment.
- **Unsubscribe link in the body:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM coverage can't be assessed.** All 29 tracked links were skipped, and the visible source shows no final destination URLs.
- **Tracking identifiers are present.** Examples are `mi_cid=678d5ea95a1b75fb` and `mi_mid=...` on `click.dv.1800flowers.com`, and `mi_ecmp=261010_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` on the open pixel. These indicate a Movable Ink campaign code, but not that landing pages carry UTMs.
- **Landing page alignment with the subject line was not checked.** No destination was resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fetch the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers (not the relay copy) to confirm the physical address, `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
2. Move the `http://` pixels (`pixel.mathtag.com`, `view.atdmt.com`) to HTTPS, or remove them if the vendors are retired.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Check the `th` black-text override against dark-mode backgrounds.
6. Resolve the tracked redirects in a follow-up run and check the final URLs for UTMs and for landing-page match.
7. Move the text-size reset from `*` to `html, body`.
