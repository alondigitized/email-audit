# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- I opened this site expecting to see flowers — maybe a Mother's Day arrangement front and center — and instead I'm staring at a cookie banner that's swallowed the whole screen. What I *can* see above it is a clean teal search bar with "Find the Perfect Gift" and a pre-set Mother's Day occasion drop-down, which honestly I appreciate. But I can't see a single product, price, or reason to stay until I hit "Continue" on this consent wall.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day pre-selected in the Occasion dropdown** — I don't have to think about which category to pick. It already knows why I'm here.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — plain, direct, and that's exactly how I shop. I'm not looking for an aesthetic experience; I'm trying to send something nice to my mother-in-law by Saturday.
- **ZIP Code + Occasion search approach** — asking for delivery location first is smart for a same-day delivery shopper like me. Same-day sympathy bouquets are half of why I use this site.
- **Large, high-contrast "Search" button** — even on a small phone screen, there's no confusion about what to tap.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal dominates the entire viewport** — I literally cannot see the homepage. There's a "Continue" button at the bottom but it interrupts everything and takes up 70% of the screen. First impression: friction, not flowers.
- **Zero product imagery above the fold** — as a visual shopper who's swayed by a beautiful arrangement photo, I have nothing to react to. Not one petal visible.
- **No Passport/loyalty acknowledgment** — I pay for free shipping. There's no "You're a Passport member — free delivery applies" message anywhere I can see, which would have immediately calmed my "what's this going to cost me" anxiety.
- **No offer, no urgency** — it's May 10, which is probably two days before Mother's Day. Where's the "Order by Sunday midnight for Mother's Day delivery" countdown? That's exactly the push I need to commit right now.
- **No trust signals** — no star ratings, no "over 100 million gifts delivered," nothing that reassures a returning customer she's in the right place.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the full-screen cookie modal or shrink it to a slim bottom banner** — the current wall stops everything. A sticky footer bar with "We use cookies — Continue" keeps compliance without eating the whole homepage.
- **Add a deadline banner in the teal header zone** — something like "Order by May 11 for Mother's Day delivery" directly above or below the search form would send me straight to checkout mode.
- **Surface a Passport member welcome message** — even a one-liner: "Free delivery included with your Passport membership" would remove my biggest hesitation and make me feel like the site knows me.
- **Place one featured Mother's Day arrangement image in the hero area** — even a thumbnail of a bestselling bouquet next to the search form gives me something to react to emotionally. Right now it's a form, not a flower shop.
