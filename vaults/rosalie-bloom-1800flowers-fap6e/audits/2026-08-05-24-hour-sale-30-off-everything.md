# 24-Hour Sale: 30% Off Everything
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers escalating the "Friends & Family Event" thread one more notch: 20% off (July 30) → 25% off (Aug 3) → now 30% off in a compressed 24-hour flash sale. The hero delivers the offer in about two seconds — giant "30% OFF sitewide" with a "ONE DAY ONLY" badge — which is exactly the kind of unambiguous, high-contrast hero that makes their strongest sends work.
- The weak spot is the messaging mashup: the hero still wears the "Friends & Family Event Email Exclusive" wrapper from the last two sends, but the subject and badge are selling a public 24-hour flash sale. Those are two different offer logics (members-only perk vs. everyone-act-now urgency) stapled together, and it muddies what's actually driving the discount. The exact deadline (11:59pm EDT, 8/5) is also buried in the legal fine print instead of living in the hero where it would do real urgency work.
- Net: strong offer, clean render, on-brand escalation — but the email is doing more work than it needs to justify the discount, when the discount alone is the story.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the offer instantly: "30% OFF sitewide" in oversized type with a "ONE DAY ONLY" badge — no scrolling needed to get the deal.
- CTA button "USE CODE TODAY30" is high-contrast, centered, and unambiguous.
- Top Picks section shows four specific, named products (Fields of Europe Summer, Garden Pathway, Sundae Scoops Strawberries, Simply Chocolate Gift Basket) rather than a generic homepage link.
- Shop by Category grid is well-organized and gives multiple clear entry points (Birthday, Romance, Sympathy, Plants, Same-Day, etc.).
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem-ipsum.

## What's weak

- "Friends & Family Event Email Exclusive" and "24-Hour Sale / One Day Only" are competing offer frames in the same hero — reads like two campaigns bolted together rather than one clear story.
- The actual deadline (11:59 p.m. EDT, 8/5/2026) only appears in the tiny footer legal text, not anywhere near the hero where it would drive urgency.
- Email runs long for a flash sale — hero, Top Picks, Shop by Category (9 tiles), Celebrations Passport signup, app download, and brand-family footer. A 24-hour sale should feel urgent and scannable, not like a full catalog browse.
- Cadence risk: per brand history this is the sixth flowers promo in about a week (7/28, 7/29, 7/30, 8/1, 8/3, 8/4), all built around discount escalation — inbox fatigue is a real risk even though this send has the best offer yet.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the deadline out of the footer and into the hero (e.g., "Ends tonight at 11:59pm ET") — this is the single biggest lever for urgency and it's currently invisible until the very bottom of the email.
- 2. Drop the "Friends & Family Event Email Exclusive" line entirely for this send — let it be a clean 24-hour flash sale rather than reusing last week's members-only framing, which conflicts with "sitewide/everyone" positioning.
- 3. Cut the email down — merge Top Picks into Shop by Category or drop one of the two, and move Celebrations Passport/app download/brand-family footer to a lighter closing block. A flash sale should read fast on mobile.
- 4. Add a live countdown element (even static "Ends 11:59pm ET tonight" copy near the CTA) rather than relying on the badge alone.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Tonight: 30% Off Everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Biggest Discount Yet — 30% Off, 24 Hours Only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Code TODAY30 — sitewide, but only until midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From 20% to 30%: your best Friends & Family price yet.`

The email is a standard MSO/Outlook-conditional responsive template with heavy third-party tracking-pixel load; automated QA passed only 43% of checks, driven primarily by missing alt attributes, mixed-content (http://) image sources, and absent compliance headers.

  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507...` (non-HTTPS)

- One click-redirect image reference: `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/8295259b-16e7-41ea-8823-ab837d455c42?mi_mid=019fd3f0-c700-7000-b069-52b341fd7791&cp_t...` — appears to be used as an image `src` rather than a link `href`, which is unusual and worth confirming isn't a mis-tagged CTA image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text** on 6 images, including both MediaMath pixels, the atdmt pixel, the BounceExchange pixel (both instances), and the click.dv tracking image. Decorative/tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` at minimum for screen-reader hygiene, though this is low severity since they're invisible 1x1 assets.
- **Mixed content (http://):** 2 image sources load over plain HTTP (`view.atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com` ×2) — these may be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing HTTPS-only image loading (e.g., Outlook, Gmail proxy).
- Template includes standard MSO conditional structure (`xmlns:v`, `xmlns:o`, `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass`) and Apple Mail dark-mode meta tags (`color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes`), indicating reasonable cross-client baseline support.
- Multiple `@font-face` declarations pull Playfair Display and Lato from `fonts.gstatic.com` — most email clients (Outlook, Gmail) strip `@font-face`/external font loading entirely, falling back to `mso-font-alt:'Times New Roman'` where specified for Playfair Display; Lato declarations have no `mso-font-alt` fallback specified in the visible source, which may cause inconsistent fallback rendering in Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) are visible in the truncated source. Personalization is limited to opaque tracking identifiers in URL query strings (`mi_mid=019fd3f0-c700-7000-b069-52b341fd7791`, `mi_u=174149195`) rather than visible content personalization — cannot fully confirm since HTML was truncated before the message body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **No physical mailing address detected** in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. If confirmed absent in the full body/footer, this is a compliance violation.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found** (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) — note the QA tool flags this may be a limitation of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding these headers rather than the sender omitting them; cannot confirm sender-side absence from this data alone.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Same relay caveat applies; this should be re-verified against raw headers at the original receiving MTA before treating as a deliverability finding.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Campaign tracking parameters are present and structured, e.g. `mi_ecmp=260805_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_30P_PROM` on the `mi.1800flowers.com` pixel — naming suggests campaign date/channel/offer encoding (`260805` / `EMAIL` / `18F` / `30P` promo) consistent with an internal campaign-tracking taxonomy.
- No standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query parameters are visible on the sampled links; the tracking scheme instead relies on the `mi_*` and `mt_*` proprietary parameters. Cannot confirm landing-page-side alignment (i.e., whether the destination site parses and attributes these params) without access to the redirect targets, which were skipped by the prober.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm whether `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are genuinely absent at the source MTA, or only stripped by the AgentMail relay — re-test against raw SMTP headers before treating as compliance gaps.
2. Verify a physical mailing address exists in the (untruncated) footer; add one if missing to satisfy CAN-SPAM.
3. Serve the two HTTP-only pixels (`view.atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com`) over HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
4. Add `alt=""` to the 6 tracking/decorative images flagged, both for accessibility hygiene and to avoid placeholder "broken image" alt text rendering in clients with images blocked.
5. Manually spot-check a sample of the 35 skipped tracking/redirect links to confirm they resolve to live, non-error landing pages, since the automated prober could not validate them.
6. Add explicit `mso-font-alt` fallbacks for the Lato `@font-face` declarations to ensure predictable Outlook rendering.
