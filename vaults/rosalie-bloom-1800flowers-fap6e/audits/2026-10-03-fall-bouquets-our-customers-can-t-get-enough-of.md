# Fall Bouquets Our Customers Can’t Get Enough Of
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a review-led fall bouquet email, and it's cleaner than most of what 1-800-Flowers has sent this week. Five bouquets each get a star rating, a round product shot and a one-line customer quote. The page has a calm cream background with a deep red "Fall's Best Blooms" headline, so it's easy to scan on a commute. The social proof is the best thing in it.
- The offer is weak. "YOU'VE UNLOCKED A $15 CREDIT, USE CODE HARVEST15 | $59.99 MINIMUM*" sits in a thin strip above the logo, and the hero never mentions it. The $59.99 minimum cuts the value of a $15 credit. The one CTA, "Shop Best Sellers," is a small button, and the five products below it have no visible prices or buttons. It also leans on the same mechanic as the 9/26 "All set for fall birthdays 🎂 Enjoy $15 credit" email, which already used HARVEST15. It's the same testimonial idea as 9/30's "Save 20% on Fall Picks from Our Amazing Moms," and it comes just 3 days after that send.
- Verdict: a competent, trustworthy send with no urgency and no price anchor. It will get a few browsing clicks, but it won't drive conversions.

## What's working

- The testimonials are specific ("Got 90 stems for my wife's birthday", the hip-surgery quote) and read as real.
- The five-star ratings above each product name give instant credibility.
- The layout is consistent, with the same pattern for each bouquet, and has no visible render bugs or broken images.
- The "Fall's Best Blooms" headline and the autumn palette of oranges, reds and golds match the season.
- The "Shop by Category" grid and app download block give a way out for people who don't like the featured bouquets.

## What's weak

- The $15 credit is buried in a thin banner above the logo. The hero doesn't mention it, so the only offer is easy to miss.
- The $59.99 minimum with an asterisk makes the offer look smaller. The legal block at the bottom is a dense wall of text.
- The five products show no prices, no "Shop" buttons and no deadline. Nothing tells the reader why to act today.
- The only CTA is a small "Shop Best Sellers" button at the top, and it's a long scroll before the category grid.
- Every quote has the same tone, and the five-product stack gets repetitive by the third bouquet.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "$15 off $59.99+ with HARVEST15" directly under the headline, and add an end date.
- 2. Add a price ("from $XX.99") and a "Shop Now" button under each bouquet. Right now only the top button is clearly clickable.
- 3. Cut the list from five bouquets to three, or group them by occasion (birthday, thank you, sympathy). That keeps it shorter and makes the choice easier.
- 4. Pull one quote up into the hero as a proof point.
- 5. Vary this against the 9/30 testimonial send. Don't send the same format on consecutive days.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, VML/Office namespaces, dark-mode meta, responsive media queries). The automated findings are mostly tracking-pixel hygiene (HTTP sources, missing `alt`) and compliance signals that can't be confirmed from the captured data. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped headers, so the compliance findings are unverified, not proven failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **29 tracking links were not probed** (click-redirect domains skipped). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (`http://`):**
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`. This is the legacy Atlas/atdmt domain, which is likely deprecated and may not resolve.
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block or rewrite these, so the pixels may not fire. The mathtag URLs also have empty `v1`–`v3` and `s1`–`s3` parameters, which means unpopulated template variables.
- **Duplicate beacon:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so the same event may be counted twice.
- **Five third-party trackers** are loaded in one send: atdmt, mathtag, bounceexchange, `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/...` and `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on all 7 flagged images.** They are all tracking pixels (the 4 beacon URLs above, `1622.gif` twice, the `click.dv` open pixel, `o.gif`). Add `alt=""` and a 1×1 size so screen readers skip them. The QA run flagged no content images.
- **`<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **`* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}`** is declared on the universal selector, which disables iOS text resizing. It is overridden on `body` (`100%`), so the effect is inconsistent.
- **`* {line-height:100%}` and `th {font-size:20px !important}`** are global overrides. Any `<th>` used for layout is forced to 20px on mobile as well.
- **`@media (max-width: 418px)`** sets `.mobile {width:414px !important}`, a fixed pixel width that can overflow on screens narrower than 414px.
- **Cleanup:** `:root{color-scheme...}`, `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` are declared in both `<style>` blocks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) appear in the captured source.
- The open pixel carries a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u=174149195`) and a campaign code (`mi_ecmp=261003_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`). Both resolved, so this is informational only.
- The mathtag pixels have empty `v1`–`v3` and `s1`–`s3` values (see §2).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the sender to verify. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Physical address:** QA found none. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer may not have been scanned. Verify against the full source.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not confirmed from the provided source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM and landing-page checks could not run.** The 29 click-tracking links were skipped, so final URLs and `utm_*` parameters are not visible.
- The only campaign identifier visible is the `mi_ecmp` code in the open pixel. It is not a standard UTM parameter, and nothing shows it carrying through to the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move all pixels to HTTPS (`pixel.mathtag.com`, and replace or remove the legacy `view.atdmt.com` pixel after confirming it still resolves).
2. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking images.
3. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` beacon.
4. Populate or remove the empty `v1`–`v3` and `s1`–`s3` parameters on the mathtag pixels.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
6. Scope `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` away from the `*` selector.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers, the footer's physical address, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw message, since the relay hid them.
