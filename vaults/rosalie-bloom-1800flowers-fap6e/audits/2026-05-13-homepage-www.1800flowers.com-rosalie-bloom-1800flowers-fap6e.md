# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see when I open this page is a cookie consent wall taking up three-quarters of my screen — not flowers, not a sale, not even a "Happy Mother's Day." The piece I can actually make out underneath is a clean, teal "Find the Perfect Gift" search bar with a ZIP code field and an Occasion dropdown already set to Birthday, which is smart and immediately useful to me. But I have to hit "Continue" on that cookie popup before I can do anything, which already has me hesitating.

## What's working

- **Occasion-based search tool:** The ZIP + Occasion dropdown (pre-filled "Birthday") is exactly how my brain works. I don't think "what product?" — I think "what occasion?" This is the right mental model for me.
- **"Search" button:** Big, tappable, no ambiguity. Even on a small phone screen I'm not going to miss it.
- **Simple top-of-page branding:** The 1-800-Flowers logo is immediately recognizable. I know where I am. That matters when I'm in a panic about a sympathy arrangement and I don't have time to figure out if I landed on the right site.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal blocks everything:** I cannot see one product, one offer, or one piece of seasonal messaging. The entire homepage experience is on hold until I tap "Continue." That's friction I shouldn't have to clear before I see a single flower.
- **No Passport member recognition:** I pay for free shipping. There is zero acknowledgment of that above the fold. Not a banner, not a badge, not a "Welcome back." I feel like a stranger.
- **No Mother's Day moment:** It's May 13th, three days after Mother's Day. I'd expect either a last-minute "gift a delayed bouquet" play or a "thank her this week" hook. The page is silent on all of it — at least from what I can see.
- **No urgency or offer:** The page gives me no reason to buy now versus closing the tab and coming back later. There's no price, no promotion, no deadline.
- **Chat button competes with Continue:** The "Chat" bubble is pinned right next to the cookie modal's "Continue" button at the bottom. On a phone screen that's a fat-finger hazard.

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss the cookie modal or shrink it to a slim banner** — a 47-year-old in a hurry to send sympathy flowers will tap "Continue" out of muscle memory, but the full-screen takeover kills the mood immediately. Make it a bottom strip with an "X" that doesn't require reading.
- **Surface Passport perks in the header for logged-in members** — even a small teal pill that says "Passport: free shipping active" next to the cart icon would make me feel seen and reduce my hesitation.
- **Run a Mother's Day wrap-up banner** — "She deserves a little late love — still delivering" with a sense of lightness and urgency. Post-Mother's Day grace-period messaging converts people like me who sent a last-minute text instead of flowers.
- **Put a free-shipping threshold or current promo directly on the search bar area** — something like "Free delivery on orders $29.99+ with Passport" right below the Search button, so there's a reason to click before I scroll.
