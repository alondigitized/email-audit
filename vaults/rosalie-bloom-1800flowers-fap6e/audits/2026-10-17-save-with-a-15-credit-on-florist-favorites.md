# Save with a $15 Credit on Florist Favorites
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy editorial send built around "What We're Loving Right Now," with floral designer Mallory Green's picks. The $15 credit (code HARVEST15, $59.99 minimum) sits in a thin teal strip above the logo, so it's the subject line's whole promise but the weakest thing in the render. The eye lands on the sketch of Mallory and the serif headline first, and the offer is an afterthought.
- The body looks good. Daphne's Bliss, a textural bouquet pair, Sunflower Radiance and New Moon Black Roses each get a pull quote. The photography is warm and seasonal. But every module says "Shop Now" with no price, so nothing tells me what $15 off actually buys. The $59.99 minimum is never connected to a product.
- It's also the seventh 1-800-Flowers send in nine days. After the 10/12 "Save Up to 25% on Fall Favorites" and the 10/14 staff-picks email, this one reads as the same editorial format with a smaller offer. It's pleasant and competent but unlikely to move anyone.

## What's working

- The credit banner is on brand and repeats the subject's promise at the very top.
- The designer-voice framing ("Meet Mallory!") gives the email a human angle that mass discount sends lack.
- The photography is strong and seasonal: harvest oranges, a dramatic black-rose vase and textural bouquets.
- Named products (Daphne's Bliss, Sunflower Radiance, New Moon Black Roses) beat generic category links.
- The Shop by Category grid gives a quick exit for anyone who doesn't like the featured picks.

## What's weak

- The offer is a thin banner, outweighed by the editorial headline and the sketch.
- There are no prices, no "from $X" and no hint of what qualifies for the credit under the $59.99 minimum.
- Four of the five CTAs are the same small "SHOP NOW" text link. Only "Shop Botanical Luxury" is a real button.
- The long fine print and the offer end date (11/22) are buried at the bottom. A five-week window removes any urgency.
- This repeats the cadence and the editorial formula of the 10/14 staff-picks send, so it adds no new reason to open.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the credit the hero. Put "$15 off $59.99+" in a large block under the headline, with the code in a button-style chip.
- 2. Show prices on each product, and say which bouquets clear $59.99 on their own.
- 3. Turn the "Shop Now" text links into full-width buttons with specific copy, such as "Get Daphne's Bliss — $64.99".
- 4. Set a real deadline, like "ends Sunday," instead of 11/22.
- 5. Cut the category grid or move it up. It's the most useful navigation, but it sits below four long modules.

# Technical Audit: "Save with a $15 Credit on Florist Favorites" (1-800-FLOWERS.COM)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with dark-mode and mobile media queries. Findings are mostly tracking-pixel hygiene (insecure `http://` pixels, missing `alt`), an unprobed link set, and compliance headers the QA harness couldn't confirm. The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited to what the QA data surfaced.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **28 tracking links were not probed.** They sit on click-redirect domains and the HTTP probe skipped them, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels over `http://`:**
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`. `atdmt.com` looks like a legacy ad-server domain, so it may no longer resolve. Treat that as a suspicion to verify.
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`
  
  Clients and image proxies may block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, which silently drops these events.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it can double-count opens or events.
- **Pixel volume:** at least 7 tracking images were flagged (atdmt, 2× mathtag, 2× bounceexchange, `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/95bfe44f-…`, `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`). This adds third-party requests and privacy exposure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on all 7 tracking images above.** Use `alt=""` for decorative pixels so screen readers don't announce filenames or URLs such as `o.gif` and `1622.gif`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the visible `<html xmlns=… xmlns:v=… xmlns:o=…>` tag has none.
- **Text resizing disabled:** the universal `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` rule disables iOS text scaling. The later `body {-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` partly overrides it for the body, but the `*` rule still applies to descendants.
- **Generic `<title>`:** `1800Flowers.com` does not describe the message.
- **Dark mode:** the `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` metas are present and correct. I can't verify image or logo handling in dark mode from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Empty parameters:** the mathtag pixels have `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` unpopulated. This is likely a template default, but it means no segment or value data is passed.
- **User ID in a pixel URL:** `mi.1800flowers.com/…/o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=261017_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` embeds a per-recipient ID. It is a first-party pixel, but it is a privacy-relevant identifier.
- No unresolved tokens such as `{{…}}` or `%%…%%` appear in the visible source. The truncation means this isn't conclusive.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The harness notes the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify it in the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`. Verify with the original headers.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the flagged URLs. Attribution appears to use the proprietary `mi_ecmp` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters (`mi_ecmp=261017_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`). This is a mismatch with GA-style reporting only if the site relies on UTMs.
- **Landing-page alignment:** it can't be assessed, because all 28 tracked CTAs went unprobed and resolve through redirects. The "$15 credit" offer's landing page and credit-application behavior are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the three `http://` pixels (`atdmt`, 2× `mathtag`) to HTTPS, or remove them. Confirm `atdmt.com` still resolves.
2. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif`.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, add `lang="en"` to `<html>`, and use a descriptive `<title>`.
5. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, because the relay may have stripped them.
6. Follow the 28 tracked links with a redirect-aware crawler to verify landing pages, the credit offer and the final URL parameters.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and unresolved tokens.
