# Your Birthday Gift Guide + 25% Off 🎁
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is actually a genuine mechanic shift — the first "Fall Birthday Season" gift-guide send in the recent history, breaking from both the "Double Blooms" rut and the generic "Cozy Season" fall-gifting hero that ran three times between 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-12. Segmenting by recipient ("for her," "for him," "for kids") is a smarter structure than a single flat hero, and it gives the email real browsability instead of one CTA.
- That said, the execution undercuts the premise. The code GLOW25 is buried in small text in the top banner with no dollar/percent anchor near any actual product, there's no visible expiration or urgency, and the influencer block ("In Season with Molly Madfis") plus the "Fan of the Classics" bestsellers section dilute an already-long scroll that never re-states the birthday angle after the hero.
- Net: a stronger creative concept than the brand's recent treadmill of repeats, but the offer and CTA hierarchy are weaker than the "Big Birthday Energy" send from 2026-09-10, which led with a bold striped hero and a clear 20NOW code front and center.

## What's working

- Genuine creative shift — recipient-segmented gift guide ("for her," "for him," "for kids") instead of a repeated single-hero template.
- Clear top-of-fold hero with a "SHOP BIRTHDAY" button visible without much scrolling.
- Product photography is varied and appealing (whiskey glass, chocolate-dipped strawberries, sprinkle cookies) and matches the birthday-gifting theme.
- Influencer/social-proof quote block adds a human trust signal not typically seen in this brand's recent sends.
- No visible render bugs — layout is clean, images load, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- The 25% off code (GLOW25) is small, in a thin top banner, and never reappears near a product or the primary CTA.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible email — no "ends tonight," no countdown, nothing time-bound.
- The email sprawls past the birthday concept into "Fan of the Classics" and a full category grid, diluting the segmented-gift-guide idea that made the top half strong.
- Repeated "SHOP NOW" CTA copy for every section (for her / for him / for kids) — no differentiation in urgency or specificity.
- "In Season with Molly Madfis" reads like a disconnected content insert with no clear tie back to birthday gifting or the discount code.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer where the eye actually lands — repeat "25% OFF · GLOW25" as a bold badge directly under the hero button, not just in the thin top banner.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 9/20") next to the code to create urgency that's currently absent entirely.
- 3. Cut or move the "Fan of the Classics" and full category grid below a hard scroll boundary — they compete with, rather than reinforce, the birthday-guide concept.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per segment ("Shop Gifts for Her," "Shop Gifts for Him," "Shop Kids' Picks") instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 5. Tie the Molly Madfis block explicitly to birthday gifting or drop it — right now it interrupts the guide's flow.
