# Claim Your FREE Double Blooms 💐
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "double" send in two days, and the first one whose subject makes a promise the body doesn't keep. The body is the same clean "Double the Colors" layout as the 10/27 "It Doesn't Cost Anything to Double Your Blooms" send. Five bouquets each get a small and a 2X-size arrangement, with a "SAVE $20" badge. The photography is strong and the repeating pattern is easy to scan.
- The subject says "FREE Double Blooms," but the email delivers $20 off per bouquet plus 20% off with GLOW20. The hero line, "More red, more yellow, more orange—no extra cost," only partly backs up the subject. The one button, "DOUBLE UP," is small and vague. Everything after the five bouquets (the 20% off block, the gift bundle banner, the category grid) pulls attention away from the single mechanic. Past sends such as 10/21's "25% off roses" were louder and clearer.
- Verdict: a polished, repeat creative with a misleading subject and a weak CTA. It will get skimmed by anyone who opened the 10/27 version.

## What's working

- The 2X side-by-side bouquet layout shows the value without needing copy.
- The "SAVE $20" badges are consistent and easy to find on every product row.
- The product photography is rich and seasonal (fall tulips, lilies, roses), and the orange, red and yellow palette fits the "Double the Colors" theme.
- Each bouquet is named (Vibrant Blooms, November Sun Lily, Cherished Blooms), so the product detail is specific.
- The "Plus Enjoy 20% Off Sitewide / Use Code: GLOW20" line gives a clear second incentive.

## What's weak

- The subject says "FREE" but the email shows $20 savings. That is a bait-and-switch feel, and the all-caps "FREE" reads as a spam signal.
- The only visible button, "DOUBLE UP," is small and sits under the hero. It doesn't say what you get or where you go.
- This repeats the 10/27 creative almost exactly, one day later, so it feels like cadence fatigue.
- Below the bouquets the email loses focus. The 20% off block, the "Give Them Flowers" bundle banner and the 9-tile category grid all compete with the hero.
- The offer terms are small footer text, and the $20 savings conditions aren't visible near the badges.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject match the offer. Don't use "FREE" unless the second bouquet is actually free.
- 2. Replace "DOUBLE UP" with a specific button such as "Shop 2X Bouquets, Save $20," and repeat it under the product grid.
- 3. Cut the bundle banner and shrink the category grid so the 2X offer stays the only story.
- 4. Put the deadline and the GLOW20 code near the top, not in the footer.
- 5. Space this out from the 10/27 send, or change the creative angle on the next one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Save $20 on every double-size bouquet 💐`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2X the blooms, $20 off — ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five bouquets, twice the size. Plus 20% off with GLOW20.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Vibrant Blooms, Fall Tulips and more — bigger bunch, same price.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with dark-mode meta tags and MSO/VML namespaces. The QA run passed 38% of checks, with 0 errors and 5 warnings. The warnings are mostly missing headers, non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing image alt text, and a heavily URL-based plain-text part. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and body-link checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 25 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped (domains not named in the QA output). Their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** Three images load over `http://`. Clients that block mixed or non-secure content may suppress them, which would drop the tracking events.
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, which may double-fire the event.
- **Stacked third-party trackers:** The email carries atdmt (Microsoft Atlas), MediaMath (mathtag), Bounce Exchange, and first-party `click.dv.1800flowers.com` and `mi.1800flowers.com` pixels. That adds third-party request overhead and privacy-policy surface.
- **Plain-text part:** 4949 of 6345 characters (78%) are URLs, which is a spam-filter signal. The raw tracking URLs also make the text version hard to read.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-size adjustment:** The universal selector sets `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}`, which disables user text-size adjustment on iOS. The later `body` rule sets `100%`, but the `*` rule overrides it for child elements.
- **Missing alt text:** The alt attribute is missing on the following images. Most are tracking pixels, where `alt=""` is the correct value, so the real issue is that the attribute is absent rather than empty.
  - `view.atdmt.com/.../direct/01/`
  - Both `mathtag` pixels
  - `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (×2)
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/c0cba58d-...`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
- **Image sizing:** `img {max-width:640px}` applies at both `min-width: 768px` and `640px`. Pixels without explicit dimensions could render as visible gaps if not hidden.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light dark` is declared, but `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text. This can be unreadable if a client applies a dark background.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%`, `{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible HTML or the QA findings. The open pixel carries `mi_u=174149195`, which resolved to a numeric ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Physical address:** The QA check did not detect one. The HTML is truncated, so the footer was probably not scanned. Confirm in the full source before treating this as a violation.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so alignment is unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** The QA output doesn't confirm one, and it can't be checked in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No UTM parameters:** No `utm_*` parameters appear in the visible URLs or QA data. Campaign attribution appears to rely on the first-party code in the open pixel: `mi_ecmp=261028_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`.
- **Redirect links:** All 25 click links route through redirect domains and were not resolved. Landing-page alignment with the "Free Double Blooms" offer in the subject cannot be verified.
- **Missing analytics data:** If the landing pages expect `utm_*` for web analytics, the redirects must append them. This could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the `http://` pixels (`view.atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com`) to HTTPS, or remove them. `atdmt.com` is a legacy Microsoft endpoint and may no longer be live.
2. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Give any content images descriptive alt text.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` with `100%` or drop it.
5. Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results`. This resolves three of the five warnings.
6. Confirm the footer contains a physical address and an unsubscribe link in the full, untruncated HTML.
7. Shorten the plain-text version by using shorter or cleaner URLs, or by trimming link-only content.
8. Resolve a sample of the 25 redirect links to confirm landing pages and `utm_*` handling.
