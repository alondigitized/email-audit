# Double the blooms. Same price. 🌹🌹
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers' cleanest hero of the batch — a genuinely different mechanic ("Double Blooms: 2X the blooms, same price") instead of the endless 30%-off sitewide flash sales that dominated Aug 5-19, and a step up from the birthday-credit sends on Aug 15/20. The vertical "DOUBLE / DOUBLE" side-banners plus a real product photo of a bouquet showing the doubled-flower effect gives it a clear, legible hero that a shopper can understand in one glance without reading fine print.
- Where it falls apart is everything below the fold: after the "2X the blooms" hero section, the email sprawls into a second unrelated 20%-off promo (code BLOOM20), a nine-tile shop-by-category grid, a Celebrations Passport membership pitch, and app-download banners. That's four different offers competing for one click, and it dilutes what should have been a tight, single-CTA send built around the doubling gimmick.
- Solid concept, poor execution below the hero — worth an 8 for creative differentiation on this specific mechanic, but the email needs an edit for focus.

## What's working

- The "Double Blooms" concept is visually demonstrated, not just claimed — the hero bouquet and the two "2X the blooms" comparison shots make the value prop self-evident.
- "SAVE $20" starburst badges give a concrete dollar figure instead of vague "great deal" language.
- Single clear primary CTA button ("SHOP NOW") sits right under the hero headline with good contrast.
- No render bugs — images, badges, and text all display cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- Departs from the fatigued 30%-sitewide flash-sale format this brand has repeated all month, which is a real cadence win per the brand history.

## What's weak

- The email introduces a second, competing offer (20% Off Summer Favorites, code BLOOM20) directly beneath the doubling promo — this splits attention and undercuts the "double the blooms" hook as the single reason to click.
- No visible urgency/deadline in the visible hero — "Twice the Birthday Magic" implies birthday-specific but there's no date range or countdown shown in-frame.
- Bottom of email is heavily padded with shop-by-category tiles, a Celebrations Passport membership pitch, and app-download banners — a lot of scroll for a reader who already saw the offer.
- The "*" after "at no extra cost" signals fine print exists but isn't visible in this crop, which slightly undercuts the "honest offer" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer: keep "Double Blooms," drop the BLOOM20 20%-off block entirely — two competing discounts in one send is worse than one strong one.
- 2. Add a visible deadline/date to the hero (e.g., "Ends [date]") since "Twice the Birthday Magic" reads evergreen otherwise and doesn't score any urgency signal.
- 3. Move "SHOP NOW" or add a second CTA directly under the two "2X the blooms" comparison bouquets, since that's the strongest visual proof point and currently has no button nearby.
- 4. Trim the shop-by-category grid to 3-4 tiles max, or move it after a second CTA rather than stacking 9 tiles before the membership pitch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2x the blooms. 0x the extra cost.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Their birthday bouquet, doubled.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same price, twice the flowers — for a limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The gift that looks twice as good (and costs the same).`
