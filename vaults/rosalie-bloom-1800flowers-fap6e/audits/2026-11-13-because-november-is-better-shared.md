# Because November Is Better Shared
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the "November Highlights" catalog again. It's the third send in four days built on the same template (11/10 "Say Thanks Beautifully This November", 11/12 "The Perfect Gift Is Up to 20% Off"). The subject, "Because November Is Better Shared," is warm and on-theme, but the email is a long scroll of unrelated sections: Thanksgiving, Scorpio birthdays, romance, Christmas, then a category grid and app badges. The offer, "Up to 20% off* with code FLOWERSALE," is a thin strip at the very top, and the hero itself carries no offer.
- The render is clean and the photography is appetizing, but nothing ranks first. Four themes get roughly equal weight and each has its own "Shop" button. A subscriber who has seen this layout twice this week will treat it as wallpaper. The "Scorpio this season" block is a nice personal touch, but it's buried in the middle.
- Judgement: a competent, recycled catalog with a weak offer and no single reason to click. It's a middling send.

## What's working

- The "Up to 20% off with code FLOWERSALE" banner is visible at the very top, so the offer isn't hidden.
- The photography is rich, seasonal and on-brand, with fall florals, a Thanksgiving table and holiday arrangements.
- Each section has a clear headline and a labeled button (Shop Thanksgiving, Shop Birthday, Shop Love & Romance, Shop Holiday).
- The Scorpio birthday framing is a fresh, personal angle.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero says "November Highlights" with a small "Get Gifting" button. It has no concrete offer and is repeated from 11/10 and 11/12.
- The email is far too long (about 7,600px) and covers four unrelated themes, which dilutes focus.
- "Up to 20%" with an asterisk is vague, and the fine print sits at the bottom.
- The "Get Gifting" CTA is generic and doesn't match the subject's "shared" idea.
- The product grids carry no prices, discounts or ratings, so there's little reason to click a specific item.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the catalog with one hero: a single product or bundle with a stated price or percentage, a deadline and a specific CTA such as "Shop Thanksgiving Centerpieces."
- 2. Put the offer in the hero rather than in a top strip, and name the deadline.
- 3. Cut the email to two sections, Thanksgiving plus one other, and show prices or "from $X" on products.
- 4. Add social proof such as star ratings or same-day delivery cutoffs.
- 5. Space out the "November Highlights" reuse. Three sends in four days is too many.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Based on the truncated HTML and automated QA (43% pass rate, 0 errors, 4 warnings), the email uses a standard table-based layout with dark-mode meta tags and a mobile media query. The confirmed issues are non-HTTPS tracking pixels, missing alt attributes, and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The compliance and authentication warnings are unverified, because the headers weren't captured and the HTML was cut off before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **23 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Link resolution and destination health are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels (`http://`):**
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - Clients that proxy or block non-HTTPS images (Gmail and others) may drop these, which loses the retargeting and attribution events they carry.
  - `view.atdmt.com` is a legacy Atlas ad-server domain, and its continued use looks stale. This is an inference, not confirmed from the source.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, which risks double-counting if the tag fires on each load.
- **Pixel volume:** the email loads five or more third-party or first-party pixels (atdmt, mathtag ×2, bounceexchange ×2, `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/083013b4-…`, `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`). This adds weight and privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** all of these are tracking pixels, so the impact is low:
  - the atdmt pixel
  - both mathtag pixels
  - both `1622.gif` instances
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/083013b4-…`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif`
  
  Add `alt=""` to each, since a missing attribute is announced by some screen readers as the filename or URL. Alt coverage for the content images was not reported, but the source was truncated.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** the global `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` rule disables text-size adjustment on every element. `body` overrides it back to `100%`, but the inherited behavior is inconsistent.
- **Dark mode:**
  - `color-scheme` and `supported-color-schemes` are declared as `light dark`.
  - `th`/`th a` are hard-forced to `color:#000000 !important`, and `.appleLinks a` and `.navfreeship` are forced as well. I can't confirm the nav background from the truncated source, so this is a risk to check (black text on a dark-mode inverted background), not a confirmed defect.
- **Web fonts:** Playfair Display and Lato are loaded via `@font-face` with `font-display:swap`. Several weights have no `mso-font-alt` fallback, though Outlook ignores `@font-face` anyway.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the body copy was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **No physical address detected.** CAN-SPAM requires one. The footer was cut off in the provided HTML, so confirm it manually before treating this as a violation.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment could not be verified.
- The in-body unsubscribe link was not visible in the truncated source, so it could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- The only visible campaign identifier is `mi_ecmp=261113_EMAIL_18F_PZ_STO_DV_12AMET_PROMO` on the `mi.1800flowers.com` open pixel. That is a first-party tracking code, not standard UTM tagging, so it doesn't show how clicks are attributed on-site.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the `atdmt` and `mathtag` pixels to `https://`, or remove them if the vendors are retired or unused. Check whether MediaMath (mathtag) is still active.
2. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and scope `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` away from the `*` selector.
5. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may have stripped them. Confirm the footer contains a postal address and an unsubscribe link.
6. Resolve a sample of the 23 tracked links to confirm destinations and UTM or campaign tagging. Consider a QA pass that follows ESP redirect chains.
