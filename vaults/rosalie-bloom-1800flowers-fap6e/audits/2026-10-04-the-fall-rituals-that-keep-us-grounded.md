Rules for those tiers conflict at those widths, and the later block wins. For example, `.mobilebox` is `max-width:95%` in one tier and `145px` in another.
- **Doctype:** HTML 4.01 Transitional is legacy but common in email and works in mainstream clients.
- **Missing `lang` and `<title>`:** The visible `<html>` and `<head>` show no `lang` attribute or `<title>`. The source is truncated, so confirm these in the full HTML.
