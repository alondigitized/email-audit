# Your Thanksgiving Savings: Up to 20% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the first send in the November run with a clean, tiered offer: spend $149+ for 20% off, $99+ for 15%, $69+ for 10%, all under a single code, FLOWERSALE. The hero is bold and readable, the code button sits right under the tiers, and the turkey-themed strawberries photo sets the Thanksgiving mood. It is a clear improvement on the "November Highlights" and "This or That" recycling in the 11/10 to 11/14 sends.
- The email is let down by what follows. The hero is a strong top, but the body falls back to a generic catalog grid. The Top Picks have no prices and the "Shop by Category" block spreads attention across birthday, romance, sympathy and others. The ends-date (11/26) is buried in the fine print. The subject also promises "Up to 20%" when the real deal is spend-gated. Overall it is a solid promo with a weak body and no real urgency. Thanksgiving is six days out, and nothing in the render says to order now.

## What's working

- The tiered offer ($149 / $99 / $69) is specific and easy to scan, and it rewards bigger baskets.
- "USE CODE FLOWERSALE" is a clear, visible button directly under the offer.
- The turkey strawberries photo is on-season and appetizing.
- The "SITE WIDE!" burst makes the scope obvious.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images or overlaps, and the hierarchy runs hero, then picks, then categories.
- The Top Picks use fall flowers and Thanksgiving gifts (turkey centerpiece, cornucopia basket), so the seasonal relevance is real.

## What's weak

- The offer deadline (11/26/2026) appears only in the footer fine print. There is no countdown or "ends Wednesday" in the hero.
- The Top Picks show no prices and no sale price, so the 20% / 15% / 10% math is left to the reader.
- The "Shop the Sale" button is a low-contrast outlined box, much weaker than the hero code button.
- "Shop by Category" (Sympathy, Romance, Birthday) dilutes the Thanksgiving message and pushes the footer far down.
- The code button doesn't link to a visible product. It is unclear whether the code applies automatically.
- The footer is long and dense. The exclusions and wine restrictions are small, and "Spend More, Save More" quietly pushes a higher cart.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero: "Ends 11/26 — order by Monday for Thanksgiving delivery." Real urgency is the biggest missing lever.
- 2. Add sale prices to the Top Picks ("$59.99, 20% off at $149").
- 3. Make "Shop the Sale" a filled button that matches the hero.
- 4. Cut "Shop by Category" to one or two Thanksgiving-relevant tiles (Fall, Gourmet Gifts).
- 5. Add a delivery-cutoff line near the top to reinforce the holiday timing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Spend $149, save 20% — code FLOWERSALE`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Monday for Thanksgiving: up to 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sitewide through 11/26. Fall bouquets and turkey strawberries included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10% at $69, 15% at $99, 20% at $149. Use code FLOWERSALE.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source uses a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with VML/MSO fallbacks, and the automated QA run found no hard failures. Most warnings come from legacy third-party tracking pixels and from headers that the AgentMail relay probably stripped. The HTML shown is truncated, so footer and body findings are limited to what the QA tooling reported.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 18 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, e.g. `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/dc1a0388-…`. Final destinations and redirect health are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** Three pixels load over plain `http://`. Mail clients and proxies may block them, which would undercount opens and conversions.
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&…`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&…`
- **Empty parameters:** The mathtag pixels carry empty parameters (`v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=`). Either the values were never populated, or they are placeholders left in the template.
- **Stale vendor:** `view.atdmt.com` is the legacy Atlas/DoubleClick-era host. This pixel may be dead weight.
- **Redundant pixels:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so the open or tag event may fire twice.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `alt` is absent on the following tracking images. Because these are 1×1 pixels, the fix is `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`), so screen readers skip them.
  - the atdmt pixel
  - both mathtag pixels
  - `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (×2)
  - `o.gif` (`mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/…`)
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/dc1a0388-…`
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Dark mode:** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text regardless of `color-scheme: light dark`. This is a potential contrast failure in dark mode if the `th` cells sit on a transparent or dark background. Confirm with a render test, since the source is truncated.
- **Global rules:** `* {line-height:100%}` and `p {text-align:center}` are broad resets that can override inherited styles. This is a maintainability risk, not a confirmed defect.
- **Breakpoint:** The mobile breakpoint is `max-width:418px`, so devices between 419px and 640px get the desktop layout. This is not confirmed as a problem.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=174149195` user ID in the `o.gif` URL resolved to a value.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA could not find either header. QA attributes this to AgentMail relay capture, so it is unconfirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw headers at the origin.
- **Physical address:** QA did not detect a postal address. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this needs a manual check of the full source. If it is truly absent, it violates CAN-SPAM.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** The header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay artifact. The sending domain `em.1800flowers.com` is aligned with the From address, which is a good sign, but alignment is not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** None are visible in the source we have. All 18 destination links go through `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirects and were not followed. Campaign attribution appears to be handled by the ESP, as in `mi_ecmp=261120_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_STO_DV_PROMO` on the pixel.
- **Landing alignment:** Landing page alignment with the "Up to 20% OFF" offer could not be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels, including the duplicated bounceexchange `1622.gif`.
2. Move the atdmt and mathtag pixels to `https://`. Drop the atdmt pixel if it is a retired Atlas tag, and remove the empty `v1`–`s3` parameters or populate them.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Verify in the origin headers that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are present, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC all pass.
5. Confirm the footer includes a physical postal address.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` links to confirm 200 responses and landing pages that match the offer. Also confirm that UTM or `mi_*` parameters survive to the landing page.
7. Run a dark-mode render test for the forced `th` color `#000000`.
