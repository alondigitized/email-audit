# Our Most-Loved Birthday Gifts, 20% Off
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight repeat of the "Big Birthday Energy Event" creative the brand already ran on 2026-09-10 — same maroon-and-cream striped hero, same hands-holding-birthday-card photo, same 20% off / 20NOW code — just re-labeled with a new subject line ("Our Most-Loved Birthday Gifts") and reframed around a product grid instead of the birthday-energy angle. The execution itself is clean: no render bugs, clear single offer, decent product grid with eight named SKUs and a "Shop the Sale" CTA.
- But as a subscriber who saw the near-identical banner six days ago, this reads as recycled inventory with a new headline slapped on it, not a new send. The birthday-gifting mechanic is now the third or fourth beat in this vein this month (Birthday Gift Guide 09-16, Big Birthday Energy 09-10, and this one), and the subject's "Most-Loved" framing doesn't match anything visually reinforced in the body — there's no "bestseller" badge, ranking, or review count anywhere on the product tiles to back that claim up.
- Solid execution, stale strategy — this is a 5/10 send propped up by clean production values, not by freshness or relevance.

## What's working

- Offer is unambiguous: 20% off sitewide, one code (20NOW), stated twice (hero + button).
- Product grid is well organized with eight distinct named products and clear pricing-adjacent imagery.
- "Shop by Category" section gives multiple entry points (Birthday, Sympathy, Fall, etc.) for browsers not sold on the birthday angle.
- No visual bugs — images load, text is legible, layout is clean top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Hero creative (striped background, hands-with-card photo, "20% OFF sitewide" typography) is identical to the 09-10 "Big Birthday Energy Event" send — zero visual differentiation for a subscriber who saw it six days ago.
- Subject promises "Most-Loved" / bestseller framing but nothing in the image — no star ratings, review counts, or "bestseller" tags — substantiates that claim on the product tiles.
- "The Big Birthday Energy Event" headline still appears inside the hero even though the subject line has moved on to a different angle — the email doesn't fully commit to its own subject's premise.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no "ends tonight") despite this being the same code that's presumably been running since 09-10.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero creative entirely — new photography or at minimum a new headline treatment — before reusing this mechanic a fourth time this month; repetition is the single biggest drag on this send.
- 2. Add actual "bestseller" proof to back up the subject's claim: a small badge or "#1 rated" tag on 2-3 of the eight tiles.
- 3. Align the in-email headline with the subject line — either rename the hero to match "Most-Loved Birthday Gifts" or change the subject to match "Big Birthday Energy," not both diverging.
- 4. Add a real deadline (e.g., "Code expires Sunday") to convert the 20% off into urgency rather than an evergreen discount.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top-rated bouquets, baskets & sweets — code 20NOW, ends Sunday`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Standard 1-800-Flowers HTML/VML email with multiple third-party tracking pixels; core CAN-SPAM headers (unsubscribe, physical address) and authentication status could not be confirmed from available data.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Missing `alt` text on 6 images, all tracking/analytics pixels: `atdmt.com` view pixel, two `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels, two `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` instances, and the `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif` open pixel, plus the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` tracked image. These are non-visual 1x1 pixels so the missing alt text has no user-facing rendering impact, but it does affect strict WCAG/HTML validation.
- Extensive vendor-prefixed and MSO-conditional CSS (`mso-line-height-rule`, `-ms-text-size-adjust`, VML namespaces) present for Outlook/legacy client compatibility — no issues found in the truncated source.
- `@font-face` declarations reference Google Fonts (`fonts.gstatic.com`) for Playfair Display/Lato; most email clients strip remote `@font-face` and will fall back to the declared `mso-font-alt` ("Times New Roman") or system sans-serif — expected behavior, not a defect, but confirm fallback stack renders acceptably.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{firstName}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) found in the visible/truncated source.
- Query strings on tracking pixels contain empty personalization params (`v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` on the MathTag pixels) — these are blank rather than broken tokens; likely intentional (no data mapped for this send) rather than a template failure.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header: not detected. Note: QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm absence from headers alone.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header: not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- No physical mailing address detected in the (truncated) HTML — CAN-SPAM requires one; recommend checking the untruncated footer before treating this as confirmed noncompliance, since the source was cut off.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracked link example carries `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` params (Cheetah Digital/Marigold campaign IDs) rather than standard `utm_*` parameters — if downstream analytics (GA/GA4) expects UTM tagging, cross-channel attribution may not join cleanly; this is a platform convention (Marigold/eRelevance-style tracking), not necessarily an error.
- No destination/landing-page URLs were resolved (all 24 links skipped by probe), so alignment between email CTAs and landing pages cannot be verified in this audit.

7. Recommendations
- Re-run header capture outside the AgentMail relay (or inspect raw SMTP headers directly) to confirm presence/absence of `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` before flagging as noncompliant.
- Serve the ATDMT and MathTag pixels over HTTPS to avoid mixed-content blocking in HTTPS-rendering clients.
- Add empty `alt=""` (not omitted) to the six tracking pixels to pass strict accessibility/HTML validation, even though they're non-visual.
- Obtain the full (non-truncated) HTML to verify the physical address is present in the footer and to resolve the 24 skipped tracking links for landing-page/UTM alignment checks.
