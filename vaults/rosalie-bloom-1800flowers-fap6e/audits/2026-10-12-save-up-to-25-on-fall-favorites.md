# 🍂 Save Up to 25% on Fall Favorites
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud tiered-discount send, and it's close to a rerun of the 2026-10-09 "Going, Going... Fall Savings Won't Last" email. The same "Fall In Full Bloom" hero is back, with the 25% / 20% / 15% ladder at $99+ / $79 / $49 and the "SITE WIDE!" burst. The warm fall photo carries it. - Execution is generic, though. The only hero button is "USE CODE BESTDEAL", which reads like a coupon instruction, not a shop link. The six "Top Picks" are bare captions with no prices, no ratings and no sale callouts. The 10/18 deadline appears only in the fine print. The subject says "Up to 25%", but the real top tier needs a $99+ cart, so the headline savings are harder to reach than they sound.

- The offer ladder is the first thing the eye lands on, and the "SITE WIDE!" burst reinforces it.
- The hero photo is warm, seasonal and human, and it matches the autumn palette.
- The "Our Top Picks" grid of six bouquets is evenly spaced and shows the product well.
- The "Shop by Category" tiles give useful exits for Birthday, Sympathy, Same-Day and others.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is "USE CODE BESTDEAL", which isn't a verb-plus-noun shop action. The real "SHOP THE SALE" button doesn't appear until midway down.
- The product grid has no prices, sale prices, ratings or quotes. Compare the 10/3 review-led "Fall Bouquets Our Customers Can't Get Enough Of" send, which had all of these.
- The 10/18 end date is buried in the fine print, so the urgency is invisible.
- The tiers are spend-gated, and nothing on the page says which bouquets reach the $79 or $99 threshold.
- The footer is a wall of legal text, and the Celebrations Passport loyalty benefit isn't promoted in the body.

- 2. Put a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 10/18".
- 3. Add prices, a "was/now" line and a star rating to each top pick.
- 4. Tag the picks by tier, for example "Qualifies for 20% off at $79".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with dark-mode meta tags and responsive CSS. The confirmed problems are third-party tracking pixels that lack alt text, some of them served over `http://`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The QA run reports 0 errors and 4 warnings (43% pass rate). The compliance and authentication warnings are mostly limited by what the relay and truncated source let us see.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **24 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Insecure pixels (confirmed):**
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&…`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&…`

  Clients that block mixed or insecure content may drop these, which loses the conversion and retargeting signal. `view.atdmt.com` is a legacy ad-tag host, and I can't confirm from the data that it still resolves.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it likely double-fires per open.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/f35dcbbf-…` and `https://mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=261012_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_PROMO` are first-party, over HTTPS, and look functional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on all 8 flagged images (confirmed).** Every one is a tracking pixel: atdmt, both mathtag pixels, the two `1622.gif` tags, the `click.dv` pixel and `o.gif`. Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Without the attribute, some screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on some clients (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Forced text colors with dark mode enabled:** the email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, but `th {color:#000000 !important}`, `th a {color:#000000 !important}` and `.appleLinks a {color:#000000 !important}` hard-code black text. In clients that invert backgrounds, this can give black-on-dark contrast failures. I haven't confirmed this with a render test.
- **Global element rules:** `p {margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `th {font-size:20px !important}` apply to every `p` and `th`. They can override per-element styles unexpectedly in the truncated markup.
- **Web fonts:** the Playfair Display and Lato `@font-face` rules have `mso-font-alt` fallbacks for some weights only (Playfair 400 and 600, not 900 or the italic 900). Fallback behavior is untested.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source. The pixel URLs carry recipient and campaign identifiers (`mi_u=174149195`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) that look like Movable Ink parameters. The empty `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` parameters in both mathtag URLs are unpopulated placeholders. This is probably intentional, but those values are never filled in.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers, so it can't be treated as a sender defect. Check against the raw original message.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The supplied HTML is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** not found, also attributed to the relay. Authentication status is unknown. The From domain is `em.1800flowers.com`.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** not verifiable from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed, because the 24 links were skipped and the truncated source doesn't show them. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=261012_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_PROMO` in the Movable Ink pixel. That doesn't confirm UTM coverage or whether the "Save Up to 25%" offer matches the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` (with `width="1" height="1"`) to every tracking pixel.
2. Switch `view.atdmt.com` and both `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, or remove them. Confirm the atdmt tag still resolves, and remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Test dark mode and replace the `!important` black text on `th` and `.appleLinks a` with dark-mode-aware styles (`@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`).
5. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated message and raw headers. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, the physical address, the visible unsubscribe link, and UTM values on the click-tracked URLs, for example by resolving them with a redirect-following probe.
