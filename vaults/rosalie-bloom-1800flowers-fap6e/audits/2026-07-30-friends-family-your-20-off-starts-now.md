# Friends & Family: Your 20% Off Starts Now ✨
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-30**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers running its now-familiar sitewide discount play, but with a heavier "exclusive event" wrapper than usual — "Friends & Family," an oversized script "You're Invited," and a distinct code (SUMMER20) instead of the generic sitewide banner used on 7/29 and 7/28. The hero is visually striking (cream/gold stripe background, elegant script overlay) and the offer is unmissable: 20% OFF SITEWIDE sits dead-center in large bold type.
- Where it falls short of the brand's recent high performers is structure after the hero. The 7/29 send ("Your summer gifting shortcut") built five distinct occasion-based sections each with its own "Shop X" CTA; this one drops straight into a generic "Our Top Picks" grid with no prices, then a category grid, then a Passport membership pitch, then app-download and brand-family footer filler. There's no explicit "Shop Now" button anywhere near the 20% off hero — the only thing to click on is the underlined code text, which reads more like copy than a CTA.
- Solid, on-brand, offer-forward send, but it leans on visual polish over a punchy path-to-purchase. Middle of the brand's pack — not the 9/10 sitewide sends, not the mismatched-subject misfires either.

## What's working

- Hero offer is immediately legible: "20% OFF SITEWIDE" in large bold serif, impossible to miss on open.
- The "Friends & Family" framing plus code SUMMER20 gives this send a distinct identity from the near-identical 20%-off sends on 7/28–7/29, avoiding pure repetition.
- Countdown to Friendship Day section ties the send to a real upcoming occasion with its own clear "SHOP NOW" CTA.
- Celebrations Passport block clearly communicates the loyalty benefit (free shipping/no service charge) with a real button ("Sign Up Now").
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, all product photography loads correctly.

## What's weak

- No primary CTA button tied directly to the 20% off hero — a shopper who wants to act on the headline offer has to scroll past two full sections before hitting a real "Shop Now" button.
- "Our Top Picks" products (Fields of Europe Summer, Garden Pathway, chocolate strawberries, gift basket) show no prices at all, so the 20% off has nothing to anchor against.
- This is the brand's fourth+ 20%-off-flavored send in about a week (7/29, 7/28, plus discount-adjacent 7/25 and 7/23) — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who's opened the last few.
- The offer deadline (8/2, 11:59pm EDT) only appears in tiny footer legal text, not anywhere near the hero — undercuts the "Starts Now" urgency framing.
- Bottom third of the email (app download, 8-logo brand-family grid, legal block) is pure filler that pushes real content further from view.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real "Shop Now" button directly under the "CODE SUMMER20" line in the hero — right now the code is the only clickable-looking element and it isn't visually a button.
- 2. Add prices (or "20% off marked price") under each Top Picks product so the discount is concrete, not abstract.
- 3. Move the offer deadline out of the footer and into the hero as a small urgency line ("Ends 8/2") — currently the only urgency cue is buried in fine print.
- 4. Trim the bottom filler (app download + 8-logo brand grid) or move it below the category grid so category shopping stays closer to the top.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Friends & Family Sale: 20% Off Ends 8/2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You're invited: 20% off everything, code SUMMER20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Our Friends & Family circle gets first access — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `20% off sitewide, no exclusions on your favorite gifts.`

- Two of the three ad-tech pixels (`atdmt.com`, both `mathtag.com` calls) load over plain HTTP, which many corporate/mail-client proxies and image-blocking policies will strip — reducing tracking accuracy independent of the alt-text issue below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images/pixels are missing `alt` text: the `atdmt.com` view pixel, both `mathtag.com` pixels, the `bounceexchange.com` pixel (both instances), and the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirect image.
- 3 images use `http://` instead of `https://`: `view.atdmt.com`, and both `pixel.mathtag.com` calls — flagged as likely to be blocked by mail clients that enforce mixed-content/HTTPS-only image loading (e.g., Outlook, some Gmail configurations).
- Note: tracking pixels being alt-text-less is low-impact for screen readers (they're invisible by design), but the HTTP-vs-HTTPS issue is a functional deliverability/tracking-accuracy concern, not just accessibility.
- MSO conditional/VML namespaces and `@media` breakpoints (418px, 640px, 768px) are present, indicating standard Outlook + mobile-responsive handling — no structural rendering defects surfaced in the QA pass.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the truncated HTML provided. Cannot confirm personalization logic without the full body — recommend re-running this check against the complete source before sign-off.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected. Note QA caveat that this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it — verify against the raw source MTA headers, not just the relayed copy.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported. Same relay caveat applies.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires this in every commercial email; if truly absent from the full template, this is a compliance gap requiring immediate remediation (independent of relay artifacts, since address is body content, not a header).
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not present in the captured headers, status unknown. Same relay-capture caveat as above — recommend verifying authentication status via a direct-inbox test rather than the relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The tracked open-pixel carries campaign metadata (`mi_ecmp=260730_EMAIL_18F_FRIENDSFAMILY_STATIC_DV_1`) consistent with the "Friends & Family" campaign name, suggesting internal campaign tagging is correctly scoped. However, standard `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` query parameters are not visible on the sampled links (only proprietary `mi_*`/`cp_t` params and opaque redirect tokens on `click.dv.1800flowers.com`) — since these routes were skipped from HTTP probing, landing-page alignment (does the redirect land on a page reflecting the 20% off Friends & Family offer) is unconfirmed and should be manually click-tested.
