# The Most Coveted Flowers of the Season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a pretty editorial send with a weak reason to act. The creative is warm and the photography is strong. The Mallory Green sketch, the pull quotes and the staggered product layouts all read as curated. But it is almost the same email as the 2026-10-17 "Save with a $15 Credit on Florist Favorites" send. Both use the "What We're Loving Right Now" hero, the Mallory Green framing and the HARVEST15 credit. A subscriber who got both will see a rerun.
- The subject promises "the most coveted flowers of the season," but the body never says what's coveted or why. It doesn't rank anything or show any scarcity or sales proof. The $15 credit is a thin teal strip at the very top. It reads as legal-looking fine print, not an offer. The product CTAs are small text links ("SHOP NOW") that are easy to miss. Only one real button appears, "SHOP BOTANICAL LUXURY."
- Verdict: a nicely made, low-urgency brand-building email. It will get some browsing clicks, but it leaves the incentive on the table and repeats a creative the audience saw five days ago.

## What's working

- The Mallory Green sketch and "Meet Mallory!" give the email a human, curated voice.
- Pull quotes next to each hero product (Daphne's Bliss, Sunflower Radiance, New Moon Black Roses) add personality and some product story.
- The photography is rich and seasonal. The black roses and autumn-toned bouquets fit October.
- The "Flower of the Year 2026" badge on Daphne's Bliss is a useful social-proof cue.
- The "Shop by Category" grid near the bottom gives a clear way out for people who weren't sold by the hero picks.

## What's weak

- The $15 credit (HARVEST15, $59.99 minimum) sits in a thin top strip with small type, and the offer never comes back lower in the email.
- The product CTAs are tiny underlined "SHOP NOW" links. There is only one real button, in the middle of the email.
- The subject says "most coveted," but the email shows no proof, such as best-seller counts, ratings or "selling fast."
- The creative is a near-repeat of the 2026-10-17 send, down to the same headline and credit code.
- Product names appear in quotes but no prices are shown, so there's no price anchor and no reason to click now.
- The email is long, and the footer and app-download blocks add clutter after the main content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "$15 off $59.99+ with HARVEST15" under the headline in a button-styled block, and show the end date (11/22 is buried in the fine print) as a real deadline.
- 2. Make the headline match the subject. Use something like "Mallory's 4 most coveted bouquets," and add prices and star ratings next to each pick.
- 3. Replace the text "SHOP NOW" links with full-width buttons that name the product, such as "Shop Daphne's Bliss."
- 4. Don't resend the same editorial frame within a week. Rotate the hero concept, or fold this into a single, differently angled "last chance for the credit" send.
- 5. Shorten the email. Cut the app and brand-family blocks, or reduce them to a single line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mallory's 4 favorite bouquets (+ $15 off)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black roses, sunflowers & Flower of the Year`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code HARVEST15 for $15 off $59.99+, through 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our designer's picks, from moody black roses to sunny autumn mixes.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "The Most Coveted Flowers of the Season" (1-800-FLOWERS.COM)

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a mature XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with dark-mode meta tags, Outlook VML namespaces and responsive media queries. The main code-level problems are about 8 tracking pixels, several with no alt text and some over plain `http://`. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the AgentMail relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **27 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Plain-HTTP tracking pixels.** These can be blocked or flagged as mixed content, and they leak the open event over an unencrypted channel:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
- **Empty parameters in the MediaMath pixels.** `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` are all blank, so the pixels carry no data. They were either never templated or the values failed to render.
- **Duplicate pixel.** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, which may double-count the event.
- **Many third-party pixels.** The email loads at least four ad/analytics vendors (atdmt, mathtag, bounceexchange, mi.1800flowers.com). That adds load-time and privacy exposure, and some clients or privacy features will block them.
- **Plain-text version is mostly URLs.** 5,211 of 6,618 characters (about 79%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. That is a spam-filter risk and a poor plain-text experience.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** Confirmed on these images:
  - `view.atdmt.com/.../direct/01/`
  - both `mathtag` pixels
  - both `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` instances
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/4f8b4492-...`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
- **Impact is low.** These are 1x1 tracking pixels, not content images. Screen readers may still announce the filename or URL, so `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Insecure image sources.** The three `http://` images above may be blocked or may show mixed-content warnings.
- **Heading style override.** The stylesheet sets `th {font-size:20px !important; color:#000000 !important}`. Using `th` as a text element is a semantic misuse of table headers, and the hard-coded `#000000` can conflict with the declared `color-scheme: light dark` in dark mode. This is inferred from the CSS, not confirmed by a render test.
- **Zoom disabled.** The viewport meta has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Text-size adjustment disabled.** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` is declared, though `body` later resets it to 100%.
- **Remote web fonts.** Playfair Display and Lato load from `fonts.gstatic.com` with `font-display:swap`. The `mso-font-alt` fallbacks are present for Outlook, so this is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied HTML or QA data. The source was truncated, so the full body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this should be checked against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. Nothing here indicates a failure, only that the check couldn't run.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical address and unsubscribe link weren't in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Campaign ID is in the open-tracking image.** `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif?mi_u=...&mi_ecmp=261022_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` shows a structured campaign code (261022 date, `18F` brand, `DV` platform).
- **Click-tracking redirects.** The `click.dv.1800flowers.com` links use `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters, not standard `utm_*` parameters. That is consistent with a proprietary (Dynamic Yield / mi.*) system, but UTM propagation to the landing page can't be confirmed because the redirects weren't followed.
- **Landing-page alignment not testable.** The 27 skipped links have unknown final destinations, so alignment with the subject line ("Most Coveted Flowers") is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Upgrade `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` to HTTPS, or remove them if they are legacy.
2. Add `alt=""` to all 1x1 tracking pixels.
3. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` and confirm one fire per open is intended.
4. Populate or remove the empty MediaMath params (`v1`–`v3`, `s1`–`s3`).
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
6. Reduce the share of URLs in the plain-text part by shortening or consolidating links, to get below 70%.
7. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message headers, and verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC through a seed-list or header-capture tool. Don't rely on the relay for this.
8. Follow the click-tracker redirects in a browser or headless test to confirm landing URLs, UTM propagation and the absence of redirect-chain errors.
