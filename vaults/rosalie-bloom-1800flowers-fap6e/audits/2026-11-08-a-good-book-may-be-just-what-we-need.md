## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- The truncated source doesn't show the `href` values, so I can't comment on redirect-chain depth or on whether the links use HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Two images are missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
  - `https://image.pulse.celebrations.com/lib/fe3b117275640479741173/m/1/6463fbdf-3224-4252-b87d-4346cf740945.gif`
  - `https://image.pulse.celebrations.com/lib/fe3b117275640479741173/m/1/2db6e281-f389-41e1-8eb3-2cd7e3d257cd.gif`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as empty or as a filename. If either carries content, that content is lost.
- **Global `th` rule (observed in the `<style>` block):** `th {font-size:20px !important; color:#000000 !important; ...}` applies to every `<th>` in the document. This is fragile if `<th>` is used anywhere other than the nav. It also relies on `!important` for base styling, which makes overrides harder.
- **ID selectors:** `table #logodesktop`, `table #navmenu` and `div #nav` use IDs. IDs must be unique per document, and some clients strip or rewrite them.
- **Mobile breakpoints:** `@media (max-width: 500px) and (min-width: 320px)`, `(max-width: 600px) and (min-width: 500px)` and `(max-width: 320px)` overlap exactly at 500px and 320px. At 500px both of the first two match, so the later rule wins. This is low severity.
- No other rendering issues are confirmed from the truncated source.

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the supplied portion.
