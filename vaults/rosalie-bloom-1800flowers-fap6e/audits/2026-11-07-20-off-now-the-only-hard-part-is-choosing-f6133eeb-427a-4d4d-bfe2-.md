# 20% Off. Now the Only Hard Part Is Choosing.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth send in two weeks built on the "Playing Favorites / This or That" concept. It follows 10/23, 10/29, 10/31 ("20% Off Fall's Most Giftable Styles") and 11/04 ("Moody or Neutral? 💐"). The creative is nearly identical to 11/04, and the offer is the same 20% off with GLOW20. A subscriber would see a rerun. The subject line is the only new part.
- The layout is clean. A thin orange banner carries the code, the green hero has a big script headline, and two head-to-head bouquet matchups follow. The eye goes to the banner and hero first, and the bouquet photos are strong. But the offer only appears in that top strip, and the body never says what the discount applies to. Four different "SHOP FALL" and "SHOP NOW" buttons give no product-level destination.
- Judgement: competent and seasonal, but fatigued. The email adds nothing beyond a discount code on an already-repeated concept, and the "choosing" subject promises a decision the body doesn't help with. It's a middling send, around 5/10.

## What's working

- The "ENJOY 20% OFF* WITH CODE GLOW20" banner puts a concrete offer and code at the very top.
- The two versus matchups (Moody vs. Neutral, Farmhouse vs. Penthouse) are easy to scan, and the yellow "VS" badges give them clear structure.
- The bouquet photography is saturated, seasonal fall color and is the best part of the email.
- Each matchup has named products (Autumn Fields™, Graceful Fall™, Harvest Glow™ Bouquet, Burgundy Bliss™ Bouquet).
- The category grid (Birthday, Sympathy, Same-Day and others) gives a useful secondary route to shop.

## What's weak

- The "Playing Favorites" concept is a repeat of the 11/04 send, with the same moody/neutral pairing, so it feels stale.
- The offer shows only in the top strip. The hero doesn't mention 20% or the code, and the asterisk fine print sits at the very bottom.
- The "SHOP NOW" and "SHOP FALL" buttons are generic. They don't name a product, and the same label appears twice.
- The checkbox-style labels under each bouquet look interactive but aren't, and they show no price.
- There are no reviews, ratings or prices, and no member benefits are visible. Nothing here supports the choice the subject asks for.
- The category grid is long and dilutes the focus. Sympathy and Gourmet Gifts sit awkwardly next to a fall promo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero: "20% off fall bouquets · code GLOW20 · ends 11/22". Don't rely on the thin banner.
- 2. Retire or rework the "Playing Favorites" format. Run a real comparison, such as a "most-picked" result, instead of the same pairs a fifth time.
- 3. Show prices (original and 20%-off) and a star rating under each bouquet, and link each one to its own page.
- 4. Use specific CTA copy, for example "Shop Autumn Fields" and "Shop Harvest Glow".
- 5. Trim the category grid to four tiles, or drop it, and move the fine print up near the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Autumn Fields or Graceful Fall? 20% off both`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our 4 fall favorites, 20% off through 11/22`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code GLOW20 on moody or neutral bouquets. Ends 11/22.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Vote with your wallet: rustic or burgundy, both 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Non-HTTPS pixels (confirmed):** three beacons load over `http://`, so some clients may block or rewrite them and the events may not fire.
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
- **Pixel volume:** the email carries at least six tracking pixels from five hosts (atdmt, mathtag, bounceexchange, `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/...`, `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif`). `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, so it likely fires twice.
- **Unprobed links:** 27 click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** every tracking pixel above has no `alt`, including `1622.gif` (twice), `o.gif` and the `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/a6d4119f-...` image. For 1×1 pixels the fix is `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, which keeps screen readers from announcing a filename or URL.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled (confirmed):** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Conflicting text-size rules:** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` is overridden by `body {-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. The universal rule is redundant and inconsistent.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so I can't confirm the alt text or `role="presentation"` on the content images and tables.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`) appear in the visible source or the QA output.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

The sender `em.1800flowers.com` is a major ESP-sent brand and is likely compliant at origin. Verify against raw headers from a direct-delivery copy.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't inspected. Re-check the full HTML before calling it a violation.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Plain-text part:** 5168 of 6563 characters (about 79%) are URLs. That is above the 70% heuristic and can raise spam-filter scores. The text version is mostly raw tracking links.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- **Tracking IDs:** the open pixel carries `mi_u=174149195` (a user ID) and `mi_ecmp=261107_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` (a campaign ID). This is normal ESP tagging, with no mismatch evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Switch the `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, or drop them if the vendor doesn't support it.
2. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"` to all tracking pixels, and remove the duplicate `1622.gif`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and delete the redundant `*` text-size-adjust rule.
4. Shorten the plain-text part with fewer inline URLs and cleaner link labels.
5. Re-run the compliance checks on a raw `.eml` from a direct send, and on the full untruncated HTML, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, RFC 8058, the footer address and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
