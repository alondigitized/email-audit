# The last batch of summer gifts are 25% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink send — "Summer's Grand Finale" tries to be four campaigns in one email: Virgo birthday, Labor Day, Back to School, and a generic summer send-off, all stitched together under a single SUMMER25 code. Compare that to the brand's last two sends: the 9/2 "Virgo Birthday Edit" scored 9.0 because it committed to one idea and executed it cleanly, and the 9/3 "Last Call" 30% sale scored 8.0 on the strength of a single, focused urgency hook. This email has none of that discipline — it reads like a monthly digest, not a campaign.
- The production values are still solid: clean photography, a legible 25% off banner up top, consistent branding, and a nice touch with the Celebrations Passport loyalty callout near the footer. But five distinct "SHOP X" CTAs competing for attention means no single action feels primary, and the "last batch of summer" framing in the subject undersells what's actually a multi-occasion promo.
- Verdict: technically clean, strategically unfocused. It'll perform like a mid-pack "everything email" — better than nothing, worse than the brand's best recent single-idea sends.

## What's working

- Top banner ("ENJOY 25% OFF WITH CODE SUMMER25") puts the offer above the fold immediately, no scrolling required.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout anywhere in the render.
- Loyalty/membership plug (Celebrations Passport, free shipping across brands) is visible and adds real value for a repeat shopper.
- The Jim McCann founder quote is a nice brand-trust touch that most competitors wouldn't bother with.
- Category grid near the bottom (Birthday, Romance, Sympathy, etc.) gives a clean fallback path if nothing above converted.

## What's weak

- Four separate occasions (Virgo birthday, Labor Day, Back to School, Summer) are jammed into one email — no single clear "why click now."
- Five different CTA buttons (Shop Now, Shop Birthday, Shop Labor Day, Shop Back to School, Shop Summer) dilute the primary action instead of driving one conversion path.
- No visible price tags on individual products (only the sitewide code), so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- "Last batch of summer gifts" implies scarcity but there's no actual deadline or countdown anywhere in the body — the urgency is unearned.
- This is the second promo email in two days after "Last Call for Up to 30% Off" (9/3) — cadence is starting to feel like inbox fatigue rather than fresh reasons to open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one occasion and cut the rest.** Labor Day is the actual calendar peg this week — lead with that alone, move Birthday/Back to School/Summer into separate sends. This is the single biggest lift available; a focused email would likely land closer to the 8-9 range like the recent Virgo and Last Call sends instead of the mid-pack this one will hit.
- 2. Collapse the five CTAs into one hero CTA + a simple "shop by occasion" strip below it, rather than five equally-weighted buttons.
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 9/7 at midnight") if scarcity is going to be part of the pitch — right now "last batch" is copy with no backing.
- 4. Show at least one price or "starting at $X" on a hero product so the discount has something concrete to attach to.
