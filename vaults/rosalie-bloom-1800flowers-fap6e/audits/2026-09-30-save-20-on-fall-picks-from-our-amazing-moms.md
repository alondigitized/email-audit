# Save 20% on Fall Picks from Our Amazing Moms
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a testimonial-led fall email, and it's a better idea than the last week of sends. Four named women (a Beijos Events owner, a 1-800-Flowers merchandising SVP, and two founders) give short pull quotes. Each quote is tied to one product: Harvest Glow Bouquet, Halloween cookie box, Pumpkin Patch Mum, Crisp Fall Centerpiece. The line-drawn portraits and big serif quotes look editorial and calm. That is a real change from the loud "DOUBLE the flowers" sends of 9/28 and 9/29.
- The problem is that the subject sells 20% off but the email buries it. The code GLOW20 appears only in a thin black strip at the very top, and the "SHOP FALL" button is small. The eye goes to "She Knows Best" and the portraits, not the deal. "Amazing Moms" is also a stretch, since the quoters are mostly founders and executives, not mom-to-mom voices. The four quotes are about decorating and Halloween, not about moms or gifting.
- Overall it's decent brand storytelling with a weak commercial payload. It's about a 6 and not a strong click driver.

## What's working

- The testimonial format with portraits, quotes and one linked product each is a fresh structure after the birthday-guide and "DOUBLE" mechanics.
- The photography is warm and seasonal (sunflowers, mums, Halloween cookies, a table centerpiece) and matches late September.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- Every product has a specific caption link, so clicks go to named items and not one generic landing page.
- The "Shop by Category" grid and app-download block give a fallback path for people the quotes don't sell.

## What's weak

- The 20% offer and code sit in a tiny top banner and never repeat beside the products or the CTA.
- The only CTA button, "SHOP FALL", is small and comes before any product. The product links below are plain underlined captions, not buttons.
- No price, no deadline in the body and no urgency. The end date only shows in the fine print. This follows two days of "ends today" urgency and comes across flat.
- "Amazing Moms" is a loose framing. Most of the quoters are executives or founders, and the copy doesn't mention moms or gifting.
- The footer is a wall of dense legal text, and the long scroll dilutes the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the offer into the hero. Put "20% off fall picks · code GLOW20" as a large line under the headline, and make "SHOP FALL" a full-width button.
- 2. Turn each product caption into a real button ("Shop Harvest Glow Bouquet – from $XX") and add a price.
- 3. Add a real deadline in the body, such as "Ends Sunday", to fit the brand's recent urgency pattern.
- 4. Make the moms concept real, with actual customers or staff who are moms, or drop the "Amazing Moms" language.
- 5. Cut the email to two or three quotes and shrink the footer legal.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML source has no hard failures (0 QA issues), but the review is limited. The HTML was truncated, all 28 tracked links were skipped, and the header and address checks are inconclusive. The confirmed issues are legacy HTTP tracking pixels, missing `alt` attributes on pixels, and viewport and sizing settings that hurt accessibility.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure pixels:** These load over `http://`, so mail clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, and open or conversion measurement is lost for those clients.
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/` (Atlas/Microsoft ad server)
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507...`
- **Empty parameters:** The MediaMath URLs carry empty `v1`–`v3` and `s1`–`s3` values, so no data is passed.
- **Stacked third-party trackers:** The email loads at least five tracker or pixel hosts. These are `atdmt.com`, `mathtag.com`, `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (twice), `click.dv.1800flowers.com`, and `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif`. `1622.gif` is duplicated, so it fires twice per open.
- **Unverified destinations:** 28 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so destination status codes and redirect chains are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on pixels:** All of the tracking pixels below lack `alt`. These are not content images, but they should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them cleanly instead of announcing file names or URLs.
  - atdmt
  - both mathtag pixels
  - both `1622.gif` instances
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/ae7b1207-...`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Text resizing disabled:** The universal `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule prevents text resizing in some clients. The `body` rule later sets `100%`, but the `*` rule still applies to every element.
- **Possible mobile overflow:** Inside `@media (max-width: 418px)`, `.mobile` and `.showmobile` are fixed at `width:414px !important`. On viewports narrower than 414px, such as 375px and 390px devices, these elements can cause horizontal scroll. This is inferred from the CSS and was not render-tested.
- **Forced dark-mode colors:** `th {color:#000000 !important}`, `th a {color:#000000 !important}` and `.appleLinks a {color:#000000 !important}` pin text to black. The email declares `color-scheme: light dark`, so it opts into dark mode. If any parent background inverts, this text may become unreadable. Confirm in a dark-mode client.
- **Font fallbacks:** Playfair Display (some weights) and Lato are loaded via `@font-face` from `fonts.gstatic.com`. Only some weights define `mso-font-alt`, which is minor because Outlook falls back to default fonts anyway.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible HTML. The remainder is truncated, so tokens in the body or footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers, unconfirmed:** The QA check found no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header. The check's own note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them in the raw message.
- **Authentication unknown:** No `Authentication-Results` header was found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment could not be assessed. The sender domain is `em.1800flowers.com`.
- **Physical address not found:** The QA check flagged no physical address. Because the HTML source is truncated, the footer (where it normally sits) was probably not scanned. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed violation.
- **Visible unsubscribe link:** The visible portion doesn't show one, and the footer is cut off, so it can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs unverifiable:** All 28 tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "20% off Fall Picks" offer can't be confirmed.
- **Campaign ID in the pixel:** `mi.1800flowers.com/.../o.gif?mi_u=174149195&mi_ecmp=260930_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` carries a campaign code. The click links may carry the same code, but that wasn't checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the `atdmt.com` and `mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, or drop them if they are stale. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/.../1622.gif`.
2. Add `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` where missing) to every tracking pixel.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace the `*` rule that sets `text-size-adjust:none` with a `body`-scoped `100%` value.
4. Replace the fixed `414px` mobile widths with `width:100% !important; max-width:414px`.
5. Test the forced `#000000` `th` and link colors in Apple Mail and Gmail dark mode. Use `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` overrides if they fail.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` and the full HTML. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and the UTM and redirect chains from the click-tracking URLs.
