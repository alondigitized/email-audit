# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- OK, so the first thing I see when I land here is a cookie consent wall taking up most of my screen — before I can even see what's on sale or whether there's a Mother's Day deal going. The site clearly knows it's Mother's Day season (the occasion dropdown is already set to "Mother's Day," which is a nice touch), but the cookie modal just... parks itself on top of everything. The brand feels established and occasion-focused, which is right up my alley, but the welcome mat here is a legal popup.
- **5/10**
- + Hero copy ("Find the Perfect Gift") speaks to occasion shopping, and the Occasion dropdown is pre-set to Mother's Day — timely and relevant
- + One unmistakable primary CTA is visible above the fold: the "Search" button is big and clear
- + Demographic signals match: Mother's Day is one of my peak buying moments as a Gen X woman who manages the family gift calendar
- + Page reflects the current campaign/season: Mother's Day pre-selected in the widget
- − No returning-shopper hook (no Passport member balance, no recently-viewed, no loyalty callout)
- − No concrete offer above the fold (no "20% off," no "free shipping over $X," nothing)
- − Cookie modal is blocking the entire body of the page — visual hierarchy is destroyed
- − Loyalty/Passport membership benefits: completely absent above the fold
- − No offer to evaluate for honesty — just nothing shown
- − No render bugs, but the modal obstruction means I can't even score the rest of the page

## What's working

- **Occasion search widget** — The ZIP Code + Occasion combo pre-loaded with "Mother's Day" is genuinely smart. I don't have to hunt for a category; it's already reading my mind. This is exactly how I shop: I know the occasion, I need delivery to a zip code, let's go.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — Clear, direct, no jargon. For someone like me who's not browsing for fun but has a mission, this copy lands.
- **Search button CTA** — Big, full-width, obvious. I know what to do next. No ambiguity.
- **Mother's Day pre-selection** — Shows the site is seasonally aware. It didn't make me hunt through a dropdown.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal as the first experience** — Before I see a single flower or price, I get a legal wall. This is the digital equivalent of a store greeter blocking the door to hand me a terms-of-service agreement.
- **Zero loyalty/Passport visibility** — I am a Passport member. That's literally why I come here. Free shipping is my unlock. Not a single mention of it above the fold. That's leaving money on the table with a returning customer.
- **No offer or urgency** — Mother's Day is days away. If there's a deal or a "order by Sunday for guaranteed delivery" deadline, I can't see it. That deadline matters enormously to me — I've been burned before.
- **No product imagery** — The entire body of the homepage is hidden. I can't see a single bouquet, arrangement, or plant. For a visual category like flowers, that's a huge miss on the first screen.
- **Feedback widget floating in the cookie modal** — The "[+] Feedback" tab is layered on top of the cookie popup, which is already layered on top of the page. It looks like a bug.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-modal cookie wall on mobile** — Use a slim bottom banner instead. Every second I'm staring at legal copy is a second I'm not looking at peonies.
- **Surface Passport / free-shipping benefit in the hero area** — A single line like "Passport members: free same-day delivery" directly below the search widget would stop me from second-guessing whether it's worth ordering here vs. a local florist.
- **Add a Mother's Day countdown or cutoff date** — "Order by Sunday, May 10 for guaranteed Mother's Day delivery" in the hero would hit me right in the panic-shopper instinct. Make the deadline visible and specific.
- **Show at least one hero product image** — Even a single bouquet thumbnail in the hero would ground the page. Right now the teal bar is just text and a form. A beautiful arrangement would remind me why I'm here.
