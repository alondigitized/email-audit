# Get 40% Off Red Roses + a Vase on Us
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-hero send with an unusually strong offer: 40% off red roses plus a free vase. The "Red Roses" script headline, the saturated rose photo and the green "40% off + a free vase" badge make the hero land immediately. It is a clear change from the 11/04 "Moody or Neutral? 💐" send and the run of "Playing Favorites" quiz emails before it. This one is a straightforward deal.
- The body weakens the hero. "Behind the Bouquet" is brand storytelling with no price, product link or button. The second offer, "Plus Enjoy 20% Off* Sitewide, Use Code: GLOW20", sits below it. It is a lesser deal that competes with the hero and muddies what the shopper is meant to do. The four products under it are fall arrangements and strawberries, not roses. The category grid then adds nine more destinations.
- Overall it is a good hero attached to a diluted email. The headline offer would convert better if everything below it pushed toward roses.

## What's working

- The hero offer is concrete and visible straight away: a "40% off + a free vase" badge over a rich red rose image.
- "Deal of the Week" and the script "Red Roses" headline give the email a clear subject and hierarchy.
- A single orange "SHOP NOW" button sits right under the hero and is easy to spot.
- The "Behind the Bouquet" freshness promise (7-day guarantee, farm-fresh) addresses a real concern about buying flowers online.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "Behind the Bouquet" section has no CTA, price or product link, so it uses a lot of vertical space for nothing clickable.
- The 20% Off sitewide / GLOW20 block undercuts the 40% roses hero by giving the shopper a second, weaker offer.
- The product tiles under it (Vibrant Fall Charm, fall strawberries, harvest basket, Fields of Europe) have nothing to do with red roses. They show no prices, no ratings and no individual CTAs.
- The nine-tile category grid and the app-download banner dilute focus further, and the fine print about the offer is tiny.
- The email never says whether the free vase comes with a minimum order, or what that vase is. That is vague for an offer this prominent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the lower half about roses. Replace the fall products with 3-4 specific red rose bouquets, each showing its sale price and "includes free vase".
- 2. Cut or demote the GLOW20 block. If it has to stay, make it a one-line footer, not a headline-sized section.
- 3. Put a button in or under "Behind the Bouquet" ("Shop Fresh Roses") and add a deadline ("Ends 11/22") near the hero, not just in the fine print.
- 4. Trim the category grid and move the app download lower or remove it.
- 5. Add social proof such as a rose rating or review quote, as in the 11/03 "Gifts Rated 5 Stars" send, which scored well.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly sound: XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, VML/Office namespaces, and a dark-mode meta/CSS declaration. The automated QA pass rate is only 38%, driven by missing compliance and header signals and by third-party tracking pixels that are non-HTTPS and have no alt text. The source was truncated, so the footer, body links and plain-text part were not fully visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 20 tracking links were not HTTP-probed because they are click-redirect domains (e.g. `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/...`). Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Non-HTTPS pixels:** three beacons load over `http://`, which risks mixed-content blocking and lost measurement:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&...`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&...`
- **Empty parameters:** the mathtag URLs carry empty `v1=&v2=&v3=&s1=&s2=&s3=` values, so no event data is passed.
- **Legacy domain:** `view.atdmt.com` is an Atlas-era tracker. Whether it is still live is unconfirmed.
- **Duplicate beacon:** `https://bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` appears twice, which is a redundant request.
- **Plain-text part:** 3803 of 5172 characters (about 73%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. The text part is likely dominated by long tracking URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** these images have no `alt` attribute:
  - the atdmt pixel
  - both mathtag pixels
  - both `1622.gif` instances
  - `click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/1d4ab102-a75c-406b-b6be-9abdffd74ae6`
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/ada90b86b2846dd9/o.gif`
- **Tracking pixels:** most of these are 1x1 beacons. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them. The `click.dv...` image is a different case: it may be content, so check whether it needs real alt text.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Text resize:** the universal selector `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` disables iOS text-size scaling. The `body` rule later sets `100%`, but the `*` rule is the more specific problem.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html>` tag has only `xmlns` attributes and no `lang`, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Dark mode:** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` force black text. Combined with `color-scheme: light dark`, this can produce black-on-dark if a client inverts the background. Verify in dark-mode clients.
- **Fixed width:** `.mobile {width:414px !important}` applies at `max-width:418px`. Viewports narrower than 414px (e.g. 375px) could overflow horizontally.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so the body copy was not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so treat this as unconfirmed rather than a sender failure.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.

- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown because the relay strips it. The From domain `em.1800flowers.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment is unverified.
- **In-body unsubscribe link:** not confirmed in the visible source.

- **Tracking parameters:** the visible links use the ESP's `mi_*` scheme, not UTM. Examples are `mi_cid=678d5ea95a1b75fb`, `mi_mid=01a1ab26-...` and `mi_ecmp=261105_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO`. ## 7. Recommendations

3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` selector.
4. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
5. Test dark-mode rendering of the `th` and `th a` color overrides.
6. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated message. Check the headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original source rather than the relay copy.
