# It Doesn’t Cost Anything to Double Your Blooms 💐 😍 💐
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean "Double the Colors" send built on a mechanic that's easy to read at a glance. Five bouquets each show a small arrangement beside one twice its size, with a "SAVE $20" badge and a "2X the blooms" label. The hero is bold, with sideways "DOUBLE" lettering and a blush background. It's the strongest creative in the recent run, and it's easier to follow than the "This or That" send on 10/23 ("Pick Your Favorite Bouquet").
- The offer is muddy, though. "Double your blooms" and "Save $20" are two different promises, and nothing tells me how they connect. Is the bigger bouquet $20 off, or do I get double the flowers for the same price? The subject line's "doesn't cost anything" overpromises against a $20 discount, and the real terms are in the footer fine print. Then a second offer, 20% off sitewide with GLOW20, shows up halfway down. That's the same code as the 10/26 "Save 20% on the spookiest gifts" send, so it's a repeat.
- This is the seventh floral send in a week. I'd see it as a decent execution of an unclear deal, and I'd probably skim it and move on.

## What's working

- The side-by-side "2X the blooms" visuals explain the idea without any copy.
- The hero is high-contrast, and the sideways "DOUBLE" type plus the script headline give a clear focal point.
- Each product has a name and a repeated "SAVE $20" badge, so the offer is easy to scan.
- The fall palette of oranges, golds and magentas fits the season.
- The 20% off code is prominent and sits in its own section.

## What's weak

- "Double Up" is a vague button. It doesn't say what I'm buying or what I'll get.
- "Save $20" and "2X the blooms" never connect, so the real deal is unclear.
- The subject says "doesn't cost anything", but the body shows a $20 discount, not a free upgrade.
- The 20% sitewide offer, the NYRR gift bundle and the app promo all compete with the hero deal below the fold.
- There's no visible deadline in the body. The end date appears only in the footer fine print.
- The subject is 52 characters, and the 💐 😍 💐 emoji sandwich reads as spammy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the mechanic explicit in the hero. For example, "Upgrade to the bigger bouquet for $20 less." Put one sentence under the headline stating exactly what the $20 buys.
- 2. Change the button to something specific, like "Shop Double Bouquets", and add a deadline line such as "Ends 11/22" under it.
- 3. Link each product card to its own product page, and add prices and star ratings.
- 4. Move the GLOW20 offer into a slim banner, and drop it from this send if it ran the day before.
- 5. Cut the emoji to one and keep the subject under 50 characters.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Five fall bouquets, each upgradable to twice the blooms. Ends 11/22.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The HTML is a conventional XHTML 1.0 Transitional, table-based layout with dark-mode meta tags and web-font fallbacks. The QA run found no hard failures. Its four warnings are mostly header-capture gaps from the AgentMail relay, plus legacy third-party tracking pixels served over `http://`. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link, and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **26 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure pixel sources.** These three tracking pixels load over `http://`, so Gmail's image proxy or a mixed-content policy may block or rewrite them:
  - `http://view.atdmt.com/FLO/view/361227669/direct/01/`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155437&mt_adid=103507&…`
  - `http://pixel.mathtag.com/event/img?mt_id=155438&mt_adid=103507&…`
- **Pixel sprawl.** Tracking beacons from at least four hosts load in one send:
  - atdmt (Microsoft Atlas)
  - mathtag (MediaMath)
  - `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif`, which appears twice
  - `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif`

  The duplicate `1622.gif` is redundant. Each extra host adds load time and privacy surface.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/c4b43a58-…?mi_cid=…` is the first-party open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing `alt` on tracking images.** `alt` is absent on all the pixels listed above. This is low severity because they are 1×1 beacons, but `alt=""` is still the correct markup so screen readers don't announce the image URL. QA did not flag any missing `alt` on content images, though the truncated HTML limits that check.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on clients that honor it, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Conflicting text-size rules.** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` is applied universally. `body` later sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%`, so the two rules conflict. The `*` rule also forces `line-height:100%` everywhere, which is tight for body copy.
- **Hardcoded header color.** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` can leave black text on dark backgrounds, since the email declares `color-scheme: light dark`. Whether this bites depends on the dark-mode rules, which fell outside the truncated source.
- **Web fonts.** The Playfair Display and Lato `@font-face` rules point to `fonts.gstatic.com` with `font-display:swap`. Only some Playfair faces carry `mso-font-alt`, and Lato has none visible, but the truncation hides the fallback stacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appeared in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u=174149195` parameter on the open pixel is a resolved subscriber ID.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the headers. It is unverified, not confirmed absent. If the headers are truly missing, the send does not meet RFC 8058 one-click, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status could not be assessed. The relay is the likely cause, but this is not a pass.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link sit in the truncated portion of the source.
- **Plain-text part.** 4,976 of 6,365 characters (about 78%) are URLs, over the 70% threshold. Long tracking URLs inflate the text part and can add a spam-filter signal.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Campaign tagging.** The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=261027_EMAIL_18F_STATIC_DV_12AMET_PROMO` on the open pixel. It encodes the send date (2026-10-27), the channel, and the brand.
- **Landing pages not checked.** The 26 skipped links and the truncation mean I could not confirm standard `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, or `utm_campaign` values on destinations. I also could not confirm that the promo matches the landing page. Click-through attribution runs through the redirect layer (`click.dv.1800flowers.com`) rather than visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Check the raw headers at the sending MTA or an inbox seed for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM, and DMARC results. This clears or confirms the three header warnings.
2. Move the `view.atdmt.com` and `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels to HTTPS, or remove them if the campaigns are retired. Drop the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/.../1622.gif`.
3. Add `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`) to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Resolve the `-webkit-text-size-adjust` conflict between `*` and `body`, and avoid `line-height:100%` on `*`.
6. Trim tracking-URL length in the plain-text part, or shorten the redirect paths, to bring the URL ratio under 70%.
7. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Probe a sample of the 26 tracking links by following their redirects, and verify UTM parameters and landing-page alignment.
