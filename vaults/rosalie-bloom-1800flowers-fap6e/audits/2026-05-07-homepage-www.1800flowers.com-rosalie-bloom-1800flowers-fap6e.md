# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- I opened the site and immediately got hit with a cookie consent wall that ate almost the entire screen. What little I can see underneath tells a story I actually like — "Find the Perfect Gift" with a ZIP code field and an occasion dropdown already set to Mother's Day — but I can barely see it. This feels like a site that knows its occasion-driven audience, then shoots itself in the foot before I even get a chance to browse.

## What's working

- **Occasion dropdown pre-set to Mother's Day** — I didn't have to do anything; the site already knows why I'm here in May. That's smart and saves me a step.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — warm, plain English, not trying to be clever. It's exactly the errand framing my brain is in when I'm shopping for someone else.
- **ZIP + Occasion search tool as the hero** — for a delivery-sensitive shopper like me who cares about same-day sympathy options, leading with delivery logistics is the right call.
- **Large, legible "Search" button** — no ambiguity, no hunting.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the page** — this is the single biggest problem I can see. I can't see a single product, banner, offer, or navigation item below the hero. The modal has a close X but it's small and I'm on mobile; this is friction before I've done anything.
- **No offer visible above the fold** — no "free shipping over $X," no Passport member benefit, no promo code. I'm a Passport member and I can't tell that matters here.
- **Zero product imagery** — the entire viewport is form fields and a cookie notice. I'm a visual shopper; I want to see a gorgeous bouquet, not a zip code box.
- **No loyalty/Passport signal** — if I'm a paying member, the very first screen should acknowledge that. It doesn't.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie modal by default for returning visitors** or at minimum render it as a thin bottom banner so the hero content is visible on first load.
- **Add a Passport member welcome strip** just below the nav — one line, "Free delivery on every order — Passport member" — so I feel seen the moment I arrive.
- **Put one lifestyle image behind the search tool** — a mother receiving flowers, a warm doorstep delivery scene. The teal background is fine but stark; give me something to feel before I search.
- **Surface a time-sensitive offer in the search area** — "Order by [date] for guaranteed Mother's Day delivery" with a specific date would trigger my purchase instinct immediately.
