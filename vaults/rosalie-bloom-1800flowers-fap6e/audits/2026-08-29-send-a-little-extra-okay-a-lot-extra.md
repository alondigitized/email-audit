# Send a Little Extra (Okay… A Lot Extra 😉🌸)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth "Double Blooms" send since August 24 — subject "Send a Little Extra (Okay… A Lot Extra 😉🌸)" — and it's a full rerun of the same mechanic and hero shots from the Aug 24-26 run, this time layered with a bonus "20% Off Summer Favorites" section underneath. The subject line is a departure from the flatter "Double the blooms" and "Twice the Roses" phrasing of the prior three sends — more voice, more personality — but by the fourth consecutive day of the same "2X the blooms, starting at $44.99" offer, the creative fatigue is real regardless of subject wit.
- The render itself is clean: hero bouquet is vivid, "DOUBLE DOUBLE" sidebar copy and the pink "STARTING AT $44.99" badge draw the eye immediately, CTA is unambiguous, and the layout cascades logically from hero offer → Vibrant/Cherished Blooms comparisons → 20%-off grid → category nav → Passport membership. Nothing is broken or overlapping.
- The problem isn't execution, it's cadence. A subscriber who saw Aug 24, 25, and 26 is now looking at the same bouquets and the same $44.99 anchor for a fourth time in five days. Judgment: solid render, stale offer — this lands in the middle of the pack, propped up by a better subject line than its predecessors but dragged down by repetition fatigue.

## What's working

- Hero image is bright, appetizing, and the pink "STARTING AT $44.99" badge is legible against the bouquet.
- "DOUBLE DOUBLE" vertical sidebar text plus the script "Double Summer Delight" headline gives clear, immediate visual hierarchy — the eye lands on the offer first.
- CTA button ("SHOP NOW") is high-contrast and unambiguous.
- The added "20% Off Summer Favorites" section with a real promo code (BLOOM20) gives a second, distinct offer beyond the repeated Double Blooms mechanic.
- Subject line has personality and doesn't just restate "double the blooms" verbatim like the last two sends did.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth nearly-identical "Double Blooms" creative in five days (Aug 24, 25, 26, now 29) — same hero bouquet, same before/after "2X the blooms" arrangement, same $44.99 starting price.
- Subject line ("Send a Little Extra... A Lot Extra") doesn't reference "double" or "blooms" at all, so it disconnects from the hero art the way the Aug 26 send did in the opposite direction — a subscriber can't tell from the subject that this is the same promo they've now seen three times.
- "Vibrant Blooms" and "Cherished Blooms" section headers are underlined but not visually distinguished as tappable links vs. labels — easy to skim past.
- Fine print at the very bottom ("Offer ends at 11:59 p.m. EDT on 8/6/2026...") is dense and low-contrast — not a render bug, but a legibility miss for anyone checking terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire "Double Blooms" for at least a week.** Four sends of the same mechanic in five days is the single biggest drag here — rotate to a genuinely different hero (e.g., the "Top 5 Under $50" listicle format that worked well on Aug 22/27) before returning to this offer.
- 2. **Make the subject match the hero art or don't run it same-day as a repeat creative.** Either lean into "double" language consistently or use the mismatch deliberately with fresh creative — not a fourth rerun.
- 3. **Promote the 20% Off / BLOOM20 code higher.** It's a genuinely different, code-based offer buried below two "Double Blooms" comparison blocks — for subscribers fatigued on Double Blooms, this could be the more compelling hook if surfaced earlier.
- 4. **Tighten the subject length and swap in urgency tied to the actual code deadline** rather than vague "a little extra" phrasing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2X the blooms + 20% off summer favorites`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bouquet, doubled — plus 20% off with code BLOOM20`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use code BLOOM20 for 20% off Fields of Europe, Charming Rose Garden & more`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Starting at $44.99 — free doubled blooms through this weekend`
