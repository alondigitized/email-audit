# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, first thing that greeted me was a cookie consent wall covering most of the page — not exactly a warm welcome. What I *can* see is a "Find the Perfect Gift" search tool with a ZIP Code field and an Occasion dropdown already set to "Birthday," which honestly is kind of perfect for how I shop. But that's almost all I can see. This feels like a functional, utility-first brand that wants to help me get a gift to the right address fast — which, fair enough, that's exactly why I'm here.

## What's working

- **The occasion-based gift finder front and center** — setting the Occasion dropdown to "Birthday" by default is a smart assumption. I shop by occasion, every single time. This is the right paradigm.
- **ZIP Code + Occasion as the primary UX** — for same-day sympathy deliveries especially, I want to type in my cousin's zip and know what's available *now*. This tool promises that.
- **Clean, large "Search" button** — no ambiguity, no clutter, just "here's what you do next." Medium-tech me appreciates that.
- **Logo and nav are recognizable** — I've been here before, I know this brand, the logo at the top immediately orients me.

## What's weak

- **Cookie consent modal blocking the entire page** — before I can see a single flower or deal, I'm hit with a wall of legal text. This is the first thing I see after the gift finder. It feels like a tollbooth.
- **No Passport member recognition** — I pay for Passport specifically so I don't have to think about shipping. Zero acknowledgment of that membership above the fold is a missed opportunity to make me feel valued.
- **No promotional offer visible** — 1-800-Flowers runs constant promotions. I've come here driven by an email offer before. There's no banner, no ribbon, no "20% off today" strip anywhere I can see.
- **No product imagery at all** — flowers are a visual category. I should see something beautiful the moment I land. Right now all I see is a form and a legal notice.
- **Hamburger menu only** — I can't see categories without an extra tap. "Sympathy" and "Birthday" should be one-tap visible for someone like me.

## Recommendations

- **Show me one gorgeous arrangement above the gift-finder tool** — even a small lifestyle image of a sympathy bouquet or birthday arrangement would ground the experience emotionally. Right now the header is pure function, no feel.
- **Add a Passport member welcome strip** — a thin banner saying "Welcome back, Rosalie — free shipping on everything" would immediately validate my loyalty and reduce any hesitation.
- **Move the cookie consent to a slim bottom banner, not a full-page modal** — let me see the page first. A bottom-strip consent keeps me browsing while staying compliant.
- **Surface a concrete offer in the teal header area** — even "Free same-day delivery in thousands of cities" gives me a reason to complete that search rather than wondering if it's worth the effort.
