# Homepage snapshot · www.1800flowers.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the first thing I see is a giant cookie banner blocking everything below the search bar — not exactly rolling out the red carpet. What I *can* see looks completely dialed in for me: a clean "Find the Perfect Gift" search widget with the Occasion dropdown already set to **Mother's Day**, which is exactly why I'm here. This feels like a brand that knows its customers are on a mission, not browsing.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **Occasion-first search widget** — ZIP Code + Occasion dropdown pre-set to Mother's Day is exactly how my brain shops. I'm not browsing; I'm solving a problem for my aunt in Tucson.
- **"Find the Perfect Gift" headline** — It's simple, direct, and matches my intent perfectly. No fluff.
- **Mother's Day pre-selection in the dropdown** — Someone at this company actually thought about the calendar. That's a small thing that saves me a tap and signals they're paying attention.
- **Chat button visible** — I've called 1-800-Flowers before when I needed same-day sympathy arrangements. Knowing chat is right there reduces my anxiety about getting the order right.
- **Top nav is clean** — Account icon and cart are easy to find without hunting.

## What's weak

- **Cookie banner blocks everything below the hero** — I landed on this page wanting to shop for Mother's Day and immediately got a wall of legalese. I know cookies are required by law now, but burying the entire homepage under this modal on first load is friction I did not ask for.
- **No visible promotions or Passport member callout above the fold** — As a Passport member, I expect to see some acknowledgment that I get free shipping. There's nothing here reminding me why I'm loyal.
- **No urgency signal visible** — Mother's Day has a hard deadline and I can't see any countdown or "order by X date" copy. That's the one thing that actually moves me from browsing to buying.
- **The hero is very sparse** — It's just a search bar on a teal background. No flowers, no lifestyle imagery, nothing emotionally resonant. For a gifting brand, that feels like a missed opportunity.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the cookie banner automatically after 3 seconds or shrink it to a bottom ribbon** — Don't let it eat the whole screen on first load; show me the homepage.
- **Add a "Order by [date] for Mother's Day delivery" countdown directly in the hero** — Put it right above or below the Search button where I can't miss it.
- **Surface a Passport/loyalty badge in the header or hero** — Even a small "Free shipping, Passport Member" tag next to the account icon tells me I'm recognized.
- **Drop a lifestyle photo or product shot into the hero background** — A bouquet, a gift box, a mom smiling — something that makes me *feel* the occasion before I start typing a zip code.
