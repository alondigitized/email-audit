# Make their jaw drop. Double blooms inside 💐💐
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Double Blooms" mechanic 1-800-Flowers already ran on Aug 24 ("Double the blooms. Same price. 🌹🌹," 7.0/10) — same hero, same "2X the blooms" before/after arrangement shots, same "starting at $44.99" badge. Nothing here distinguishes it from yesterday's send except the subject line ("jaw drop" instead of "double the blooms"), which actually makes it worse for a subscriber who already opened yesterday's near-identical email.
- The hero itself is still the cleanest thing in the batch — a real value mechanic (2x flowers, same price) beats another 30%-sitewide flash sale, and the diagonal "DOUBLE / DOUBLE" background type plus the before/after product comparisons communicate the offer without reading it. But stacking a second $44.99-off-Summer-Favorites promo (20% off, code BLOOM20) directly underneath the Double Blooms hero muddies the single offer this email should be selling, and running it back-to-back with yesterday's send reads as cadence fatigue rather than a fresh idea.
- Judgment: solid creative, poor sequencing. If this went out one day after an email with an almost identical hero and subject theme, most subscribers skim past it.

## What's working

- Hero image is sharp, the bouquet is vivid and appetizing, no render bugs.
- "2X the blooms" before/after product pairs (Vibrant Blooms, Cherished Blooms, Summer Beginnings) make the value prop visually self-evident without reading copy.
- Clear price anchors ($44.99/$49.99 starting-at badges) on every hero product.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") placed right under the offer copy, above the fold.
- Membership benefit (Celebrations Passport, free shipping) is visible further down for loyalty subscribers.

## What's weak

- This is the second "Double Blooms" send in two days (Aug 24 then Aug 25) — near-identical hero, same price points, same mechanic, just a reworded subject.
- A second, unrelated 20%-off/BLOOM20 promo is bolted on right below the Double Blooms section, splitting the email's focus into two competing offers.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the render — no "ends tonight," no countdown, despite the brand using ticking countdowns on other sends (Aug 19).
- Subject "Make their jaw drop" is generic hype copy that doesn't tell the recipient anything the Aug 24 subject didn't already say better.

## Recommendations

- 1. Don't send this the day after an almost-identical Double Blooms email — hold it, or make this send meaningfully different (new product line, added urgency, or a bundle vs. Aug 24's straight value pitch).
- 2. Cut the 20%-off Summer Favorites block or move it to a separate email — one offer per send converts better than two competing discounts.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the Double Blooms hero ("Ends Sunday") since the brand has shown countdown urgency works for them.
- 4. Vary the subject line meaningfully instead of reskinning the same theme two days running.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still time: 2X blooms, same price`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bouquet, doubled — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Double the flowers in every arrangement, free through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No catch: twice the blooms, same $44.99 starting price.`
