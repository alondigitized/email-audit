# 25% Off. Which Will You Choose?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a "This or That" send, built as three head-to-head bouquet matchups: Moody vs. Neutral, Farmhouse vs. Penthouse, and Spooky vs. Scary. The creative is fun and the photography is strong. The mechanic is a good fit for flowers, because people really do choose between bouquets. The 25% off code GLOW25 is carried by a thin orange strip at the very top, and nothing below it repeats the offer.
- The subject line leads with the offer, which is smart. The body then buries it. The hero is "Playing Favorites" in a script font, with a generic "Shop Now" button. The three matchups are laid out cleanly, but each one ends in a category-level "Shop Fall" or "Shop Halloween" button. The "choose" idea never turns into a real interaction, and the checkboxes beside each bouquet name look tappable but aren't.
- This is the fourth 25%-ish promo in about ten days. It follows 10/9 "Going, Going... Fall Savings Won't Last" and 10/12 "Save Up to 25% on Fall Favorites", which also led with a 25% hook. It's well made, but it doesn't make the offer feel fresh. Verdict: solid mid-pack, 6/10, with the offer visibility as the biggest fixable problem.

## What's working

- The matchup layout is playful and scannable, with large, well-shot bouquets and a "VS" badge between each pair.
- The subject leads with a concrete discount and a question, which gives it some curiosity.
- The seasonal mix is current: fall bouquets plus a Halloween pair.
- The "Shop by Category" grid at the bottom gives a second path for people who didn't like any of the pairs.
- The dark green hero with gold script is on-brand and premium, and the render has no broken images or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- The 25% offer and the code GLOW25 appear only in the thin top strip. Nothing in the hero, the matchups or the buttons repeats them.
- The hero CTA says only "Shop Now". The matchup buttons ("Shop Fall", "Shop Halloween") are category-level, not tied to the bouquet a reader just picked.
- The checkboxes next to the bouquet names look interactive but aren't, which is a small trust hit.
- The end date (11/22/2026) is only in the fine print, so the offer has no visible deadline. That's weak urgency for the fourth discount email in under two weeks.
- The footer fine print is long and dense, and the "Spooky vs. Scary" captions are cramped and slightly misaligned with the other pairs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer into the hero: "25% off with code GLOW25" under the headline, in a button or badge. Don't leave it only in the top strip.
- 2. Give each bouquet its own link and a verb-plus-noun CTA, such as "Get Autumn Fields" or "Get Graceful Fall". Drop the checkboxes, or make them real links.
- 3. Add a visible deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Nov 22" or "Ends Sunday", if the deadline is real. Otherwise skip the urgency.
- 4. Add a price or a "Best seller" tag under each bouquet name to give more reason to click.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 10/9 and 10/12 discount sends so this one doesn't read as another 25% rerun.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Autumn Fields or Graceful Fall? 25% Off Both`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Pick Your Fall Bouquet: 25% Off, Code GLOW25`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Moody or neutral, farmhouse or penthouse. Vote with your cart.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three bouquet face-offs, one 25% code. Ends Nov 22.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on pixels:** the `atdmt`, `mathtag` ×2, `bounceexchange` ×2, `click.dv.../o/cab74d8c-...` and `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif` images have no `alt` attribute. Screen readers may announce filenames or URLs. Use `alt=""` on these tracking pixels.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Fixed widths on small screens:** inside `@media (max-width: 418px)`, `.mobile` and `.showmobile` force `width:414px !important`. On 375px or 390px viewports this can exceed the screen and cause horizontal scroll or clipping.
- **Text-size conflict:** `* {-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` is overridden by `body {-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. The conflicting declarations are redundant and inconsistent.
- **Forced black text:** `th {color:#000000 !important}` and `th a {color:#000000 !important}` hard-code black. With `color-scheme: light dark` declared, this risks low contrast in dark mode if the background inverts. I can't confirm the background from the truncated source.
