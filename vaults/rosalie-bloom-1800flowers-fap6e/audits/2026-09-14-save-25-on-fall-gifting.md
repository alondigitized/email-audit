# Save 25% on fall gifting
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-14**
## Executive summary

- This is, almost pixel-for-pixel, the same "Cozy Season is Coming" send 1-800-Flowers already ran on 2026-09-08 and again as "We're all about fall gifting" on 2026-09-09 — same maroon top banner, same couple-in-sweaters hero, same "Life's in bloom with you" script line, same occasion-carousel structure (Birthday → Romance → Just Because → Shop by Category). The only thing that's moved is the subject line and promo code (HARVEST25). At this point it's the third consecutive day of the identical creative, which is a real cadence problem for anyone who opened the prior two.
- The email itself is competently built — clear top banner with the offer, a legible hero, well-organized product carousels, a full category grid, and clean brand-family footer. Visually there are no bugs. But "well-executed" and "repeated" are not mutually exclusive, and a subscriber who opened Monday's and Tuesday's version will bounce off this one on recognition alone.
- Verdict: solid production, stale strategy. Ship a different hero before running this creative a fourth time.

## What's working

- Top banner leads immediately with the concrete offer: "ENJOY 25% OFF* WITH CODE HARVEST25."
- Hero headline "Cozy Season is Coming" pairs a legible discount CTA ("SHOP NOW") with a warm, on-season lifestyle photo.
- Product carousels are well-labeled with names and thumbnail pairs (Birthday Strawberries™, Celebrate The Day™ Bouquet, Fleur De Chocolate®, etc.) so browsing intent is easy to satisfy.
- Full "Shop By Category" grid at the bottom gives a low-friction fallback for anyone not moved by the hero offer.
- No render bugs — images, type, and CTAs all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- This is the third straight day of the identical "Cozy Season is Coming" hero and couple photo (last seen 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-09) — zero creative refresh.
- Subject "Save 25% on fall gifting" undersells the email itself, which is really four sub-campaigns (fall gifting, birthday, romance, "just because") stitched under one banner — the subject doesn't hint at that breadth.
- No urgency signal anywhere — no deadline, no "ends soon," which is a missed lever given this brand has used credible urgency well before (see 2026-09-03 "Last Call" send, 8/10).
- Repeating the exact same lifestyle photography across 3 sends flattens the "seasonal pivot" story that this campaign was originally trying to tell.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero creative** — same offer and code are fine, but reusing the identical couple photo a third day in a row is the single biggest drag here. Rotate in the Harvest Glow bouquet or cornucopia basket imagery already used lower in the email as the new hero.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the offer** — "25% off" with no end date reads weaker than the brand's own "FINAL WEEK" ticker send from 2026-09-03. Even a soft "through Sunday" would lift urgency.
- 3. **Tighten the subject to reflect scope** — the email covers birthday, romance, and everyday gifting, not just "fall gifting."
- **Subject Alt A:** `25% off fall gifts, birthday blooms & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `HARVEST25: 25% off sitewide, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop birthday, romance, and cozy-season bouquets — all 25% off with code HARVEST25.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New fall arrivals plus 25% off sitewide through this weekend.`
- 4. **Vary send cadence** — space out repeat-creative sends by at least a few days so the same subscriber isn't seeing beat-for-beat identical hero art three days running.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 27 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped for HTTP probing (not independently verifiable, but consistent with a heavily instrumented campaign — e.g. `https://click.dv.1800flowers.com/o/f8618322-68d6-47c1-a94a-85d65f93b953?mi_cid=678d5ea95a1b75fb&mi_mid=01a0a0dc-7e80-700`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text (all tracking pixels/redirects): `atdmt.com` view pixel, both `pixel.mathtag.com` pixels, both `bounceexchange.com` 1622.gif pixels, and the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirect image. These are 1x1 trackers, so the accessibility impact is negligible, but they should still carry `alt=""` (not a missing attribute) to pass automated a11y/QA scanning cleanly.
- Head markup includes standard Outlook/VML conditional scaffolding, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and dark-mode meta tags (`color-scheme`, `supported-color-schemes`) — no rendering red flags in the truncated source.
- Multiple `@font-face` declarations (Playfair Display, Lato) pull from `fonts.gstatic.com`; clients that block external font loading will fall back to `mso-font-alt:'Times New Roman'` — acceptable fallback, no fix needed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not detected. Either absent or stripped by the relay — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support from headers alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not detected — if List-Unsubscribe is present but missing this, Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe compliance (required since Feb 2024 bulk-sender rules) is not met.
- **Physical mailing address**: not detected anywhere in the scanned content — CAN-SPAM requires this in the footer; if truncation cut off the footer this may be a false positive, but should be verified against the full render.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status can't be confirmed via the AgentMail relay capture.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to all 6 tracking-pixel `<img>` tags to pass accessibility/QA scans without visual impact.
2. Migrate the two `http://` pixels (`atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com`) to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking loss in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
3. Remove the duplicate `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel — firing it twice inflates data with no benefit.
4. Confirm the physical mailing address and `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present in the full (non-truncated) send — verify directly against raw headers, not just this excerpt, before treating as a genuine compliance gap.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via a raw header capture outside the AgentMail relay, since Authentication-Results wasn't observable here.
6. If UTM-based attribution is used downstream, confirm at least one unwrapped example of a `click.dv.1800flowers.com` redirect resolves to a landing page with matching `utm_campaign`/`mi_ecmp` values.
