# You’ve got $13.80 to spend—let’s make their birthday special
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is 1-800-Flowers pivoting away from the flash-sale percentage-off format that's dominated the batch (Aug 5, 6, 11, 12, 13 — all 30% off / free vase / "Deal of the Week" plays) into the same "3 Ways to Gift" birthday-editorial structure from Aug 10's "$13.80 credit" send. In fact this looks like a near-identical repeat of Aug 10's subject and offer — same $13.80 credit, same $49.99 minimum, same "SUNNY1380" energy — just re-skinned with a "We Know Birthdays" hero and a Jim McCann quote block instead of the numbered gift-guide format.
- The credit-code mechanic ($13.80 off $49.99+) is a real, specific offer and the code SUNNY1380 is clearly legible in the top banner, which is more than most of this brand's vague "shop now" sends manage. But the page is a long scroll of categories (flowers, cakes, personalized gifts, shop-by-category grid, Celebrations Passport, app download) with no single dominant CTA — it reads more like a catalog than a targeted offer.
- Given the repeat of an offer we've already seen five days ago at the same 6.00/10, and a layout that dilutes rather than sharpens the hero offer, this lands in the same middling territory — solid mechanics, weak focus, low novelty.

## What's working

- Top banner clearly states the offer mechanics: "$13.80 CREDIT," code "SUNNY1380," "$49.99 MINIMUM" — all legible at a glance.
- "We Know Birthdays" hero copy is on-theme and paired with a real bouquet/gift photo, not stock filler.
- Clean section breaks (flowers → cakes/treats → personalized gifts → category grid → loyalty) give the email logical structure for a long scroll.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, no lorem ipsum.
- Founder quote block adds a human/brand-trust touch not seen in the recent flash-sale sends.

## What's weak

- No single, unmissable primary CTA — "Shop Now," "Shop Flowers," "Shop Cakes & Treats," "Shop Personalized Gifts" all compete, so the eye doesn't land on one clear next action.
- This is essentially a repeat of Aug 10's "$13.80 credit" offer with the same $49.99 minimum — subscribers who saw that send five days ago get zero new value.
- The offer, while specific, is a small percentage in disguise (~27% off the $49.99 floor) dressed up as a flat-dollar credit — feels like it's trying to sound bigger than it is.
- Product tiles (Honey Bee Buzz, Modern Meadow, Birthday Gift Tin, Sundae Scoops) have no visible prices, so there's no way to gauge value before clicking.
- The email runs very long with a full category grid, loyalty pitch, and app download footer — by the time a reader scrolls that far, the birthday offer from the top is a distant memory.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by 40%+ — lead with the $13.80 credit hero, one flower CTA, one cake CTA, then straight to footer. The category grid and app-download block are diluting the offer, not supporting it.
- 2. Put prices or "starting at $X" on every product tile so the $49.99 minimum feels achievable at a glance.
- 3. Pick ONE primary CTA color/button treatment and repeat it only for the top offer — the multiple competing "Shop X" buttons should be demoted to text links.
- 4. Don't reuse the exact Aug 10 offer mechanic (same $13.80 / same $49.99 minimum) — vary the dollar amount or minimum so repeat subscribers don't recognize the same math from five days ago.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your birthday credit expires soon — $13.80 off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We know birthdays. Here's $13.80 to prove it.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Flowers, cakes, and personalized gifts — all birthday-approved.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bouquets, treats, and gifts that make their day (code inside).`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Marketing email from 1-800-Flowers using an XHTML transitional table-based layout with heavy third-party tracking pixels; automated QA shows a 57% pass rate driven mainly by missing alt text, insecure (http://) pixel sources, and absent authentication/unsubscribe headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 30 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains, not directly verifiable) — link destinations are unconfirmed.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels present: `view.atdmt.com` (MediaMind/Flashtalking), `pixel.mathtag.com` (x2, mt_id=155437 and 155438), `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` (loaded twice), `click.dv.1800flowers.com`, and `mi.1800flowers.com/p/up/.../o.gif`.
- Two tracking images (`view.atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com` x2) load over plain `http://` rather than `https://` — flagged by QA as "Non-HTTPS source may be blocked."

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 images missing `alt` attributes per QA: the atdmt pixel, both mathtag pixels, both bounceexchange `1622.gif` pixel calls, the `click.dv.1800flowers.com` open pixel, and the `mi.1800flowers.com/o.gif` pixel. Tracking pixels are typically `alt=""` by convention (decorative/non-content), so this is low severity but should still be set explicitly to avoid screen-reader/client fallback text.
- Markup uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with MSO/Outlook conditional-style hooks (`mso-table-lspace`, `v:`/`o:` namespaces) — standard for table-based ESP templates, no defects observed in the visible source.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` meta and CSS declared for light/dark support; `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` present to prevent iOS auto-reformatting.
- Responsive breakpoints defined at 418px (mobile) and 640/768px (image max-width) via media queries — consistent with standard ESP responsive patterns; no broken selectors observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `{{`, `%%`, `[%`) visible in the truncated HTML source provided.
- Subject line contains a personalized dynamic value ("$13.80 to spend") — cannot confirm token resolution/fallback logic from source alone; recommend verifying with a second test send to confirm the token isn't hardcoded or mis-scoped to this recipient.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in QA scan (WARN) — may be a relay/capture artifact of the AgentMail proxy rather than a true absence; needs confirmation against raw SMTP headers before treating as a compliance gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unverifiable via this relay; cannot confirm sender authentication from available data.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer text in the truncated HTML sample provided, so footer-level CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link) could not be verified from source — recommend checking the untruncated footer directly.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Observed tracking param on the `mi.1800flowers.com/o.gif` pixel: `mi_ecmp=260815_EMAIL_18F_PZ_DV_12AMET_PROMO` — campaign code present and consistent with an email-channel tag.
- Click-through destination URLs were not resolved (all 30 tracking links skipped by probe), so landing-page alignment and UTM parameter presence on the actual CTA destinations cannot be confirmed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay) before concluding they're absent — relay capture may be the cause, not the sender.
2. Migrate the two `http://` tracking pixels (`view.atdmt.com`, `pixel.mathtag.com`) to `https://` to avoid being blocked by mail clients that strip mixed-content/insecure image loads.
3. Add explicit `alt=""` to the 8 tracking pixels flagged for missing alt text, to make the decorative/non-content intent explicit rather than relying on client defaults.
4. Resolve and spot-check a sample of the 30 skipped tracking-redirect links to confirm final landing pages carry expected UTM parameters and match the campaign's intended destination.
5. Confirm the `bounceexchange.com/tag/em/1622.gif` pixel is intentionally loaded twice (duplicate tag) rather than a template error.
