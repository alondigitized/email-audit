# The Fall Sale is over tomorrow
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Finally — a straight sale email. After six straight days of CB2 lookbook detours (custom fabrics, entryway consoles, color theory, an NYC apartment sweepstakes), "The Fall Sale is over tomorrow" leads with a giant "FALL 30% OFF SALE" hero over a styled bedroom shot, then drops immediately into a clean 4-up category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Tabletop) and a black "SHOP THE FALL SALE" button. That's the clearest, most conversion-focused CB2 send in this run of six.
- The catch: the actual urgency claim in the subject — "over tomorrow" — never appears anywhere in the visible body. No countdown, no date, no "ends 9/16" badge near the hero or the CTA. So the one thing that should drive today's click (last chance) is invisible once you're past the subject line, and after the hero the email reverts to CB2's habitual pattern of stacking unrelated modules (interior design services, wall art, app download, credit card, rewards, text signup) beneath the sale, diluting the single message the subject promised.
- Net: best hero/CTA clarity of the batch, but a wasted opportunity on urgency, and the same bolted-on-modules problem depressing it back to a 5-6.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "FALL 30% OFF SALE" in huge type over a real room shot, no guessing what the offer is.
- Immediate 4-category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Tabletop) gives multiple entry points without forcing a homepage click.
- Single, high-contrast black CTA button ("SHOP THE FALL SALE") sits right under the grid — clear visual path from offer to action.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout holds together top to bottom.

## What's weak

- The subject's entire hook — "over tomorrow" — is absent from the rendered body; no deadline, countdown, or date appears near the hero or CTA.
- After the sale block, the email pivots to Interior Design Services, then Wall Art & Decor, then app/rewards/text-signup tiles — four more messages competing with the one urgent CTA.
- No specific dollar or percent callout beyond the flat "30% off" — no mention of what's excluded (rugs/lighting exclusions are only in the tiny legal footer, in a screenshot too small to read at a glance).
- Second CTA ("SHOP NOW" under Wall Art & Decor) dilutes the primary sale CTA's priority this late in a sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add the deadline directly into the hero or a sticky band above the CTA — e.g. "ENDS TOMORROW 9/16" — so the urgency that earned the open survives into the body and drives the click.
- 2. Cut or shrink the Interior Design Services and Wall Art & Decor modules for this specific send — a last-day sale email should stay single-minded; move those into next week's lookbook cadence.
- 3. Pull the exclusions/fine print out of the tiny footer and put one honest line ("Excludes rugs & lighting") near the hero so the offer doesn't feel like it's hiding terms.
- 4. Repeat the CTA once more directly under the category grid images themselves (not just below all four), so mobile scrollers hit a buy button sooner.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall Sale ends tomorrow — 30% off`
