# The best is up to 40% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-17**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 finally doing the plainest, most effective thing in its playbook: a straight best-sellers sale, "BEST SELLERS / UP TO 40% OFF," no lookbook detour, no color theory lesson. After the run of "THINGS OF QUALITY," "Colors that just make sense," and "New from David Girelli" sends this week, this is the most conversion-shaped email CB2 has sent in days — a clean hero, an immediate offer, and a category grid (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Pillows & Throws, Tabletop) that lets a shopper self-select fast.
- Where it loses points is the same place every CB2 send loses points: everything below the hero is generic lifestyle photography with a category label and zero product specificity — no item names, no prices, no "starting at" anchor. The interior-design-services and app-download blocks that follow the sale content are pure real estate tax; by the time a reader scrolls past two full sales pitches unrelated to the 40% off, momentum is gone.
- Net: a solid, well-organized sale email that's a clear step up from the lookbook cadence, but it still asks the shopper to do the price-discovery work CB2 could easily do for them. Call it a 6.5 — good structure, undifferentiated substance.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "BEST SELLERS / UP TO 40% OFF" is readable at a glance with a single "SHOP THE BEST" CTA button.
- Category grid (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Pillows & Throws, Tabletop) gives multiple entry points without forcing a single narrow CTA.
- A second CTA ("SHOP THE BEST SELLERS SALE") reinforces the offer after the scroll, good for readers who skim categories first.
- Visual style is consistent and on-brand — warm, moody interiors match prior CB2 sends, so the brand is instantly recognizable.
- No render bugs: images load cleanly, no overlapping text or broken layout.

## What's weak

- Not a single price, product name, or percentage-off breakdown by category — "up to 40%" with no floor (is furniture 10% off or 40%?) reads as bait-and-switch-adjacent.
- Category images (bed, lamp, pillows, tabletop) are lifestyle shots, not identifiable products — nothing to click through to a specific item.
- Two unrelated pitches (Interior Design Services, Download the App) are stacked directly under the sale content, diluting the single offer this email exists to sell.
- "Best Sellers" as a category is vague — no proof these are actually best sellers (no ratings, no "customer favorite" badges, no unit-sold signal).

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price or percentage under each category tile ("Furniture up to 30% off," "Lighting up to 40% off") — the single biggest lift, since "up to 40%" currently has zero anchor and reads as the least credible number in the email.
- 2. Cut the Interior Design Services and App Download blocks from this send or move them to a separate email — they compete with the sale for the one thing this email should be optimizing, clicks to "SHOP THE BEST."
- 3. Swap at least the hero lifestyle shot for one with an identifiable hero product and its price, so the eye has something concrete to fix on beyond mood.
- 4. Add a countdown or explicit end date to the sale — "up to 40% off" with no deadline doesn't create urgency to open today vs. next week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Best sellers, up to 40% off — ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your favorites are on sale: up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, decor and more — shop the sale before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pieces everyone's buying, now up to 40% off.`
