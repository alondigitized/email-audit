# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is giving "dark academia meets loft apartment" and I am here for the aesthetic — but I had to stare at a full-screen email-capture modal before I could even clock the vibe. The hero copy ("NEW TO COVET") is exactly my language, and the moody, amber-lit lifestyle imagery behind the pop-up suggests this brand knows who its customer is. Right now it's pushing new arrivals and a custom-sofa program, which honestly could not be more pointed at me.

## What's working

- **"NEW TO COVET" hero copy** — "things so lovely you won't find anywhere else" is the exact promise CB2 should be making to me. That framing activates my drop-shopping brain immediately.
- **The moody, amber-dark hero imagery** — whatever that atmospheric product shot is behind the modal, it's gorgeous and on-brand. The visual language tells me this isn't a mass-market furniture site.
- **"CUSTOM IS THE ULTIMATE LUXURY"** — I am literally saving for a velvet sofa. "SHOP SOFAS & SECTIONALS" + "GET FREE SWATCHES" is the right call to action for that intent.
- **"BEST SELLERS: POPULAR FOR A REASON"** — social proof framing in a best-sellers rail is smart; it gives me permission to want what's already wanted.
- **Navigation categories** — Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor are all scannable in one glance; I can mentally place myself in the site immediately.

## What's weak

- **The full-screen email/SMS capture modal** — this is the biggest mistake on the page. I haven't seen a single product and they're already asking for my phone number. This is dark-pattern energy and I close these reflexively.
- **"SAVE 15% ON FULL-PRICE ITEMS\*"** — the asterisk is doing a lot of work here. Full-price only on a site that runs constant sales means this offer is narrower than it looks. Feels like bait.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I cannot see what shipping costs or when it's free. That's table stakes for any add-to-cart decision.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no return policy mention, no "free returns" badge anywhere above the fold.
- **The hero is inaccessible** — the modal sits on top of the actual homepage content. If I'm a returning shopper who already gets CB2 emails, I'm penalized for being loyal: I still have to dismiss this gate to see anything.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal to 15+ seconds or exit-intent only** — let me fall in love with the product before you hit me with the form. Or collapse it to a sticky bottom bar ("Get 15% off → enter email") so the homepage is still visible.
- **Put the free-swatches CTA above the fold** — "GET FREE SWATCHES" is a brilliant low-friction conversion path for someone eyeing a sofa. Move it into the hero area so I see it before I even scroll.
- **Surface the asterisk terms** — rewrite "SAVE 15% ON FULL-PRICE ITEMS\*" as "15% off new arrivals" or clarify the exclusion inline so it doesn't feel like a gotcha when I get to checkout.
- **Add a one-liner trust signal** in the nav or below the hero: "Free shipping on orders $49+" or "Free returns, always" — one line, massive friction reduction.
