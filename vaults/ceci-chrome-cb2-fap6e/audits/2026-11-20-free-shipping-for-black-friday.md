# FREE SHIPPING FOR BLACK FRIDAY
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand free-shipping send, and the offer lands immediately. The hero reads "FREE SHIPPING / ORDERS OF $99 OR MORE" over a warm holiday-glass photo, with a single "SHOP NOW" button. A Thanksgiving delivery-deadline strip sits above it, with dates for standard, two-day and next-day shipping. It has real urgency and is useful to a shopper.
- The weakness is dilution. After the hero, the email stacks a six-tile category grid, a Black Friday banner ("UP TO 60% OFF"), a "SAVE 15% OFF in the New Year" block, a $5,000 refresh sweepstakes, and an app/rewards/gift-list/SMS footer. That is five or six competing offers in one scroll. Free shipping at $99 is a modest incentive for a furniture buyer, and the supporting content is a generic category grid with no named product, price or review.
- Overall, it is competent but unfocused, and it is the latest of many Black Friday sends this week. The 11/19 "So many cult favorites on sale for Black Friday" send hit harder with its oversized 60%. Here the bigger discount is pushed to the middle of the email. I'd call it a mid-pack send.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "FREE SHIPPING" in large type, a $99 threshold and one button.
- The Thanksgiving delivery-deadline strip gives credible, date-specific urgency (11/19, 11/23, 11/24 cutoffs).
- The moody, warm imagery is consistent with CB2's earlier Black Friday sends.
- The "Pairs well with the Black Friday sale" category grid gives easy entry points (furniture, lighting, rugs, mirrors, holiday gifts, entertaining).
- The nav bar and logo are clean, and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" Black Friday banner sits mid-email, and a 60% discount should lead. Free shipping on $99+ is a weaker headline.
- The email carries too many offers: free shipping, 60% off, 15% off in the new year, a $5,000 sweepstakes, a 10% rewards offer, and 15% off for SMS signups.
- The delivery-date strip is small, and the struck-through 11/17 date is confusing at a glance.
- Category tiles have no prices, product names or reviews, so there is nothing concrete to click toward.
- The dense footer fine print makes the free-shipping terms (exclusions, the "$99" asterisk) hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the strongest offer. Put "UP TO 60% OFF + FREE SHIPPING" in the hero, since the pairing is the real story.
- 2. Cut the secondary promos (new-year 15% off, sweepstakes, SMS). Keep one hero, one grid and one rewards line.
- 3. Replace generic category tiles with three or four specific hero products showing sale prices.
- 4. Make the delivery-deadline strip larger, and drop the struck-through date.
- 5. Put a short terms line directly under the free-shipping claim.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping + up to 60% off, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by 11/19 for Thanksgiving delivery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $99+, plus up to 60% off furniture, lighting and rugs.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Standard shipping cutoff is tonight at 11:59pm PST. Here's what's on sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO conditional comments and a media-query mobile layer. The main confirmed problems are 12 content images with no alt text, 11 `http://` third-party pixels, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and a leftover template placeholder comment. Unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so footer and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 62 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe. Their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 third-party pixels load over plain `http://`. Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block them or show mixed-content warnings. Evidence:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** the ten `pub*.php` calls are cookie-redirect syncs to an ad-tech domain, which adds many third-party requests per open. Some clients and privacy tools strip them.
- **Movable Ink pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/6571013d-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are HTTPS and first-party-hosted. Both are missing alt text. For pixels this is minor but trivial to fix (`alt=""`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (12):** all are served from `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, for example `ca52acde-6c7c-4b14-916c-a199723498cc.jpg`, `7e302125-...jpg` and `e4289cf9-...jpg`. If the email is image-heavy, it is unreadable with images off and for screen readers.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text-size lock:** the `body` rule and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` block user text scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Leftover placeholder:** the head contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless to rendering but shows an unreviewed template (and "IMPUT" is a typo).
- **Generic title:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` does not match the subject, "FREE SHIPPING FOR BLACK FRIDAY".
- **Font URLs:** `@font-face` sources use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...` (double slash) and pull from both `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`. Web fonts are unsupported in many clients, so the fallback stack (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) matters. I saw no confirmed breakage.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, and MSO/Outlook conditional blocks and `border-collapse` resets are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Recipient identifiers are hashed in the pixel query strings (`mi_u=56aaa278...`). Footer and body sections were truncated, so this is not a full check.

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `mail.cb2.com` matches the image host `image.mail.cb2.com`, which suggests consistent branding, but this is not proof of alignment.

A follow-up with redirect resolution is needed to confirm the free-shipping offer's landing page and any `utm_*` parameters.
