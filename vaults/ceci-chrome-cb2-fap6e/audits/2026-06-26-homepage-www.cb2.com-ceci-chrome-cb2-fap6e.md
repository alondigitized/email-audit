# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is doing the obvious thing and doing it loud — it's the July 4th Sale and they want me to know, above everything else, that I can save 50%. The brand I love is running a sale I've been waiting for, so I'm already in the right headspace. But the hero imagery is giving me farmers-market-chic when CB2's whole thing is sculptural and edgy, and that dissonance is real. The conversion hooks below the fold are stronger than the landing moment.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 50% OFF" hero headline** — before I've processed anything else, I know there's a real offer. That's the whole job above the fold.
- **"SHOP THE SALE →" CTA** — placed exactly where my thumb lands, right under the offer copy. One tap.
- **Category grid ("So Much to Shop")** — breaking the sale down by LIVING ROOM, DECOR & MIRRORS, LIGHTING, and RUGS makes it feel like a store I can navigate, not a dump bin.
- **"SO MUCH NEW / Prepare to want it all" lifestyle module** — the dark olive sectional with the cognac chair and the sculptural floor lamp is much more *my* CB2. This is the brand's real aesthetic.
- **"FAMILY SECTIONAL: Customize yours in 90+ fabrics" callout** — I am literally saving for a sofa. This line hit.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery** — lemons on warm wood is giving West Elm, not CB2. Where's the chrome, the sculptural form, the matte black? The aesthetic that got me to open CB2.com in the first place isn't in the hero.
- **No urgency mechanism** — "July 4th Sale" implies a hard deadline but there's no countdown timer, no "ends July 4th" subline. My sale-calendar brain needs a date to manufacture urgency.
- **Loyalty hook is buried** — "Earn 10X Back in Reward Dollars" is in tiny rotating promo bar text. If this is a rewards stacking moment, it belongs in the hero, not hidden in 8-point ticker copy.
- **Free shipping nowhere visible** — the thing that actually tips me from browsing to buying is not on screen at all.
- **No specific product tease in the hero** — the sale says 50% off, but off *what specifically*? A hero that shows the velvet sofa at 50% off is a different conversion story than a hero that shows lemons.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero background to a signature CB2 interior** — dark sofa, chrome lamp, sculptural objects, maybe a mirror with an interesting frame. The sale overlay stays, but now it's landing on CB2's actual design identity.
- **Pull "Earn 10X Back" into the hero subline** — directly under "50% OFF," one line: "Plus earn 10X Reward Dollars on everything." Members feel seen; non-members get a reason to join.
- **Add a countdown or deadline copy to the CTA** — "Sale ends July 4th" in small text under the button. That's shippable in an afternoon and would materially increase click-through from browse-mode shoppers like me.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the promo bar or below the CTA** — "Free shipping on orders over $X" is the line that turns browsing into carting.
