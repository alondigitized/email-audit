# Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's Black Friday catalog dump, sent on the day itself. The hero, "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" over a ribbon image, is clear, and the "Shop All Deals" button sits right under it. After that it's a long, repetitive stack. Each category (sofas, kitchen brands, furniture, decor, rugs, Kids, clearance) gets a photo, a red tag and a "Shop Now" button. The render is heavily downscaled (680×12000 shown at 113px wide), so I can read headlines and structure but not the fine print.
- The email is well built and shows no visible render bugs. It's also undifferentiated. The 11/26 sends ("Happy Thanksgiving! 🦃", "First turkey, then shopping") carried the same "Biggest sale of the year" banner. This send changes the subject line, not the offer or the creative. A subscriber who got six or more Black Friday emails in two days has no new reason to open this one.
- Judgement: competent and on-season, but it's the same blast again. It will do fine with people already shopping, but it won't pull anyone in.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unambiguous: "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" in large type, with a visible "Shop All Deals" button.
- Each category has its own discount headline, such as "up to 40% off sofas & sectionals" and "up to 60% off hosting essentials". This makes the email easy to scan.
- The red "Black Friday" tags repeat the campaign cue and keep the creative consistent.
- The lifestyle photography is high quality and fits the holiday mood.
- The kitchen and gift-shop block near the end ("up to 40% off top Black Friday kitchen deals + free shipping") adds named products with strikethrough prices. It reuses the brand-led angle from the 11/25 Breville/Le Creuset send.

## What's weak

- Almost every button says "Shop Now", so the CTAs are generic and nothing tells you where each one goes.
- With about 10 category blocks, nothing stands out as the one best deal, and the email is very long to scroll.
- The sale copy shows no deadline. The "biggest sale of the year" framing is the same as the 11/26 Thanksgiving sends, so it isn't credible urgency.
- The offers are all "up to X%" ranges with an "Exclusions apply" note. The email shows no specific product at a specific price until the very bottom.
- The sender name "Crate & Barrel BLACK FRIDAY" and the subject's all-caps "HERE!" read as promotional shouting in an inbox full of near-identical Crate & Barrel sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero offer plus three to four blocks, and lead with the single strongest deal, such as sofas at 40% off. Fewer, bigger blocks would beat ten equal-weight ones.
- 2. Replace "Shop Now" with specific CTAs like "Shop Sofas", "Shop Rugs" and "Shop Cookware".
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends tonight at midnight", and put it in the hero.
- 4. Show at least one named product with a sale price and a star rating above the fold.
- 5. Suppress or vary sends to people who have already received several Black Friday emails. The cadence is the biggest drag on opens.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: up to 50% off + free shipping, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 40% off sofas, 60% off hosting essentials. Free shipping on every order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters on furniture, rugs and Le Creuset end at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has several markup and accessibility defects: a large number of images lack alt text, the viewport blocks zoom, and the head has duplicated and invalid tags. The automated QA shows a 57% pass rate with 3 warnings. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers were not captured and the source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **80 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Five third-party pixels are embedded.** All five are `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` (LiveRamp). They carry a hashed per-recipient `s=` identifier, and the five calls differ only in `n`. This is an extra third-party request chain, and some clients block it.
- **Recipient identifiers appear in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` carry `mi_u`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/89b792c9-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carries campaign and message IDs. They are first-party and expected for this ESP. Anything that caches or forwards the email exposes them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 45 images are flagged as missing alt text.** They fall into three groups:
  - **Content images** on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, such as `aa7b5361-…jpg`, `4eeb9ae9-…gif`, `bedc8421-…jpg` and `d1495f7a-…jpg`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. If these carry the offers and product names, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
  - **Product tiles** on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, seven images.
  - **Tracking pixels**: `rlcdn.com`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`. These should use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. I can't tell whether the checker is counting a deliberate `alt=""` as missing.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text scaling is disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text resizing.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type tag.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - A template placeholder is left in the source: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Redundant `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at ≥768px and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Fixed-width mobile class.** `.mobile{width:460px!important}` is applied at `max-width:460px`, which can cause horizontal overflow on 360–414px devices.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or in the QA data. The truncated HTML means the body wasn't fully inspected. The only template residue found is the `[IMPUT HERE …]` comment above.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. It could be a capture artifact rather than a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment against the raw message.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the visible unsubscribe link and the postal address footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Landing-page URLs and UTM parameters can't be assessed. All 80 links go through tracking redirects and were not resolved, and the source is truncated. The only parameters visible are `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`, which belong to the ESP's tracker and aren't UTMs. No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content and product image, covering the offer, product and CTA. Give the tracking pixels `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Fix the head markup: escape the title as `&amp;`, drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` tags, delete the `[IMPUT HERE …]` comment, and remove the dead 600px `img` rule.
4. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` with full headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Resolve the 80 tracked links with a HEAD or GET follow-redirect to check destinations and UTM carry-through, and re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML.
6. Consider consolidating the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls into one.
