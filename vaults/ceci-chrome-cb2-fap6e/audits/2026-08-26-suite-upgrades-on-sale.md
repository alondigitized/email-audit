- Below the hero it's a solid, if busy, catalog scroll — six clean category tiles (Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs, Outdoor, Entertaining) followed by yet another "SHOP THE SALE" banner, then the now-familiar credit-card "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" pitch with a visible strikethrough render glitch showing "10% ~~20%~~ back," which is the same co-brand card push seen 2026-08-25. - Net: reasonably strong bedroom-focused creative dragged down by subject mismatch, a stacked unrelated credit-card pitch, and a visible price-strikethrough bug. This is a middling send, not a standout.

## What's working

- Hero image and copy are clean and legible: "THE LABOR DAY SALE — UP TO 50% OFF — BEDROOM" with a clear black "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Category grid (Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs, Outdoor, Entertaining) gives a real navigational path, not just one hero and done.
- Product grid at the bottom shows actual prices and some strikethrough sale pricing (Espira Round Coffee Table $699→$579), which is honest and concrete.
- Footer includes loyalty/app perks ("EARN 10% BACK," "THE CB2 APP") and a wedding-registry CTA, giving the email more than one purpose without feeling cluttered.

## What's weak

- Subject "SUITE UPGRADES ON SALE" doesn't match the rendered hero at all — the email is a Labor Day bedroom sale, not "suite upgrades."
- The "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" panel shows a visible render bug: "GET 10% ~~20%~~ BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" — a struck-through percentage sitting mid-headline, looking broken rather than intentional.
- The credit-card pitch is squeezed between two unrelated furniture sections, breaking the visual flow of a sale email into an ad interruption.
- "800+ NEW ARRIVALS" with a single cabinet image is a non-sequitur wedged after the credit card block — no clear tie to the Labor Day sale theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject to actually reflect the hero — the biggest single lever here, since the current subject sets the wrong expectation entirely.
- 2. Fix the strikethrough render bug in the Double Rewards panel — "10% 20%" reads as a bug, not a bonus offer; render it as one clean number or an explicit "was 10%, now 20%" format.
- 3. Move the credit-card panel to the end (near the other loyalty/app footer content) so the sale narrative isn't interrupted mid-scroll.
- 4. Either give "800+ New Arrivals" its own supporting copy/CTA tied to the sale, or cut it — as-is it adds a beat without payoff.
