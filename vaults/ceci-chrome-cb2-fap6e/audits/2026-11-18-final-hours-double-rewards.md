# FINAL HOURS: DOUBLE REWARDS§
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the last beat of CB2's week-long Early Black Friday run, and it's an unusually cluttered one. The hero is a clean rewards promo: "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON," with "10%" struck through and "20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" on purchases with the CB2 Visa Signature card. The offer is concrete and the urgency ("ENDS TONIGHT") matches the subject. The problem is that it reads as a credit-card offer, and the rest of the email doesn't back it up.
- Below the hero are six category tiles (New Arrivals, Best Sellers, Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs), then two more sale banners. One says "Early Black Friday Sale, up to 60% off." The other says "Now up to 70% off clearance." Three competing offers, and two different headline percentages, dilute the one message the subject promised. Compared with the 11-17 "HOURS LEFT: EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" send, this one is tidier at the top but ends up in the same stacked-banner shell.
- Verdict: a competent, clearly rendered cardholder reminder with a weak CTA and a messy lower half. It's a solid 6, and the CTA and offer stack are the main things holding it back.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is strong. "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" is the first thing the eye lands on, with a clear "10% → 20%" strike-through.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" above the hero matches the "FINAL HOURS" subject, so the urgency feels credible.
- The render is clean. There are no broken images and no overlapping text, and the photography is polished and on-brand.
- The six-tile category grid gives an easy browse path for people who aren't thinking about the card.
- The footer modules (app download, 10% back every day, text signup for 15% off) are useful loyalty and acquisition hooks.

## What's weak

- The hero CTA is a generic "LEARN MORE" button. It names no verb and no product, and it sends people to read about a card rather than shop.
- The offer is card-only. Anyone without the CB2 Visa sees a promo that doesn't apply to them, and the qualifier sits in small type under the headline.
- The three stacked offers compete: 20% back in rewards, up to 60% off, and up to 70% off clearance. The 60% and 70% headlines look contradictory at a glance.
- "LAST CHANCE" appears in three places (below the hero, in the sale banner, and in the clearance banner), which dilutes it. This is also about the fifth near-identical Early Black Friday send this week.
- The subject has a stray "§" at the end that looks like a glitch.
- I can't read the preheader in this render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make one offer the hero and cut the rest. Either lead with the rewards promo and drop the two sale banners, or lead with the sale and demote rewards to a footer line.
- 2. Replace "LEARN MORE" with a shop-driven CTA, such as "SHOP & EARN 20% BACK."
- 3. State the card requirement clearly in the hero headline, not only in fine print, so non-cardholders aren't misled.
- 4. Add one hero product with a price instead of six unpriced category tiles.
- 5. Reconcile the 60% and 70% numbers, or explain which applies to what.
- 6. Remove the "§" from the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: 20% back in rewards at CB2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: double rewards on every CB2 order`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Use your CB2 Visa today and earn 20% back in Reward Dollars. Offer ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 70% off clearance. Double rewards disappear tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering bug: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also has widespread missing alt text, 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels, and a zoom-blocking viewport meta. QA scored 43% pass. The HTML is truncated, so footer and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 pixels load over plain `http://` and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** `pub1`–`pub10` are third-party cookie-sync calls to the Dotomi ad network. They are 10 extra requests per open and add privacy and deliverability exposure.
- **HTTPS tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/30e49ac4-…?mi_cid=…` are fine on protocol. Both lack alt text (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** 9 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have none: `f2619d6f-…jpg`, `a72ba666-…jpg`, `53aa0ac8-…jpg`, `40b30c50-…jpg`, `ac776330-…jpg`, `ced2ea49-…jpg`, `a8fff542-…jpg`, `9e5ade82-…jpg` and `d71d2b48-…jpg`. With images off, screen readers and image-blocking clients get no content.
- **Missing alt text, pixels:** the tracking pixels also lack `alt`. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, so they are skipped by assistive tech.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Font declarations:** `Lineto Brown Sub` is declared several times with conflicting `src` URLs and weights. Some URLs are double-slashed (`www.cb2.com//Styles/…`). One `HNW05` face loads from `crateandbarrel.com`. Web fonts are unsupported in most clients, so fallbacks matter.
- **Unverified:** the source is truncated, so the body markup, the `.mobile` 414px fixed width and the VML/MSO fallbacks could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` is rendered literally. It is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud personalization string that did not resolve. This is likely a dynamic-content or product-reviews block failing at send time. It may show as visible junk text or an empty module.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Caveat:** the QA notes say these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. These are capture gaps, not confirmed sender failures. Verify against raw headers from the original delivery.
- **Not verifiable:** the visible footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are outside the truncated HTML.
- **From address:** `cb2@mail.cb2.com` uses a branded subdomain, which is consistent with proper authentication alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** the tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be checked.
- **Subject line:** it ends in a stray `§` (`FINAL HOURS: DOUBLE REWARDS§`). It is probably a legal-footnote marker, but the email needs a visible matching `§` disclosure. That could not be confirmed in the truncated source.
- **Title tag:** `<title>` reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", which does not reflect the credit-card promo.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (blocker).** Check the SFMC data extension or content block. Add a fallback so the block hides or defaults when data is missing.
2. **Add descriptive `alt`** to the 9 content images and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`,** or drop the 10 cookie-sync `pub*.php` calls.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta.
5. **Verify headers on the original message.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC. Re-run QA with the full HTML and a link-resolving probe for UTMs and landing pages.
6. **Clean the template:** remove the placeholder comment, deduplicate `@font-face` rules, and fix the double-slash URLs.
