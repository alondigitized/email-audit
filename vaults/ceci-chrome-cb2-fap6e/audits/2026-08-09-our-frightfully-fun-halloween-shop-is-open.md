# 🎃 Our frightfully fun Halloween shop is OPEN →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids opening its Halloween shop, and the top banner immediately undercuts the subject line — the header strip reads "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off," so the very first thing seen contradicts the 🎃 subject and hero. Below that, the Halloween creative itself is genuinely strong: a felt bunting banner, a cat-shaped basket, ghost pillow, and a well-styled treat-bag lineup with real product names and prices. But the email doesn't stay on topic — after one Halloween product grid it pivots hard into a nursery sale, then back-to-school backpacks, then dinnerware, then a credit card pitch, then an app download, then a design-service pitch. This is the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged in the 2026-08-08 "backpack size guide" and "coffee table" sends, except here it's worse because the header actively promotes a different, unrelated sale than the subject.
- The Halloween assortment itself (pumpkin treat bag $19.95, cat hooded towel, ghost/cat throw pillows) is cute, seasonally on-point, and well-lit — if the email were just this, it'd score well. Instead it reads as one Halloween module bolted onto the brand's standard everything-email template.

## What's working

- Hero visual (bunting, cat basket, ghost pillow on a windowsill) is charming, on-brand, and clearly Halloween/kids-focused.
- "first look: the halloween shop" headline plus a single unambiguous "Shop Halloween" CTA button.
- The three-product Halloween grid shows real names, "Selling Fast!" urgency tag, and enough detail to shop by (pumpkin bag, cat towel, ghost/cat pillows).
- "we love these for you" personalized-feeling grid includes two more Halloween items (treat bag, dinner plates) with visible prices and strikethrough sale pricing.

## What's weak

- The top banner promotes "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off," directly contradicting the Halloween subject line — a jarring mismatch in the very first frame.
- After one Halloween section, the email sprawls into nursery/summer sale, backpacks, dinnerware, a credit card offer, an app download, and a design consultation — none of it Halloween, all before any unsubscribe footer.
- No overall Halloween discount or urgency/deadline is stated — it's "shop is open" with zero percent-off or countdown, unlike the back-to-school banner sitting right above it.
- Visual hierarchy is muddled: with 7+ competing sections, a scrolling reader loses the Halloween thread almost immediately.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the header banner — it must say something Halloween-relevant (or nothing at all), not "back-to-school sale," which actively fights the subject and hero for attention.
- 2. Trim this to a true single-category send: Halloween hero + full Halloween product grid + one Halloween CTA, and cut the nursery/backpack/dinnerware/credit-card/app blocks (or move them to a separate email), following the discipline the wine-glasses and dinnerware collection-spotlight sends already showed.
- 3. Add a concrete offer or urgency line to the Halloween shop itself (e.g., "new arrivals, while they last" or a launch discount) — right now Halloween has no price incentive while the section above it does.
- 4. Give the treat bags/costumes their own dedicated CTA row separate from decor, since "everything you need for a memorable spooky season" is vague — split into "Costumes & Bags," "Decor," "Tabletop."
