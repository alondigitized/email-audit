# 🎃 Selling fast: Halloween costumes, bags & more →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a Halloween catalog send that tries to do everything. It leads with "selling fast: the Halloween shop" and moves through trick-or-treat baskets, bedding, costumes and decor. It then adds an "up to 50% off clearance" banner, a free-shipping-on-toys-$99+ banner, a winter-cozy section, a product strip and an app promo. The seasonal fit is right and the product photography is strong, but it reads as a very long scroll with nine or more calls to action.
- The "selling fast" urgency is the strongest hook. But it's a general claim with no deadline, no named hero product and no price. Halloween is a week away, so the brief should be sharp, and this one isn't. The late sections (winter bedding, the toy-shipping banner, the app promo) pull attention away from the main job of selling Halloween.
- Judgement: well made and on-season, but it wanders. It would convert better as a short email around one or two hero products.

## What's working

- Seasonal timing is right. Halloween content a week out matches the current campaign.
- The category blocks (baskets, bedding, costumes, decor) are consistent: a lifestyle photo, a three-product strip and a "Shop All Halloween" button.
- The "up to 50% off clearance" banner and the "free shipping on toys $99+" bar give concrete price and shipping incentives.
- The photography is warm and consistent, and the kids' costumes and the personalized baskets are the most appealing items.
- I saw no broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- Too many CTAs. Nearly every button reads "Shop All Halloween", and the others are "Shop Clearance", "Shop Kids Holiday Bedding", "Learn More" and an app download.
- The email drifts off-topic into winter bedding, a general clearance banner, a nursery/toy section and a product strip. That dilutes the Halloween message.
- Free shipping appears twice (the top bar and mid-email), which is redundant.
- "Selling fast" has no proof, such as low stock counts or best-seller labels, and no deadline.
- The product captions and prices in the strip are tiny, which hurts mobile scannability and click confidence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the Halloween story (hero, baskets, costumes, one decor block). Move clearance, winter bedding and the app promo to other sends.
- 2. Replace the repeated "Shop All Halloween" with specific buttons such as "Shop Costumes" and "Shop Trick-or-Treat Baskets", each linked to its own category.
- 3. Make the urgency real. Add "Ships by Oct 30 for Halloween" or low-stock badges on two or three products.
- 4. Show prices on the hero products and enlarge the captions.
- 5. Keep free shipping in one place only.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Costumes & baskets that won't last till Halloween 🎃`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Order by Wed for Halloween delivery 🎃`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Personalized trick-or-treat baskets and kids' costumes, plus free shipping on toys $99+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our most-loved costumes are going fast. Order now, wear by Oct 31.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template with 97 tracked links and an `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` image host. QA shows a 57% pass rate with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The confirmed issues are widespread missing alt text, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and several third-party and per-recipient tracking pixels. The compliance headers could not be checked.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Link destinations unverified.** 97 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unknown.
- **Five third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixels. Five sequential sync calls add load and expose the recipient to a third party.
- **Possible shared recipient identifier.** The `s=` value in the rlcdn URLs begins with the same hex string as `mi_u=` in the Movable Ink URLs (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…`). It is a 64-character hex token, the length of a SHA-256 hash. I can't tell from the source what it encodes, so check whether it is a hashed email address.
- **Per-recipient first-party pixels.** These include `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c0b988b6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are expected for Movable Ink but are recipient-identifying.
- **Six identical dynamic images.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times. It is likely one render fetched repeatedly.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Alt text is missing on about 60 images.** Examples:
  - **Content images.** About 35 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, for example `6cda691d-b295-47d6-aa7c-8c52d1eec48a.jpg`. If these carry the product or promo message, image-blocking clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - **Spacers.** `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (4 times) and `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` (2 times) should use `alt=""`.
  - **Tracking pixels.** The rlcdn, `mi.` and `dv.` pixels should also use `alt=""`.
  - **Missing vs. empty.** The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty. If some are already `alt=""`, those are false positives.
  - **Animated images.** `b8e4ab5a-….gif`, `e7fbcb9b-….gif` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` are animated or video-poster images, and the text they carry is invisible to assistive tech.
- **Zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Duplicate meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared four times, with one combined tag plus separate `date`, `address` and `telephone` tags. This is harmless but unclean.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd placeholder and adds bytes.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under the same doctype.
- **Dead CSS rule.** `img {max-width: 600px}` at `min-width:768px` is overridden by the later `img {max-width: 640px}` at `min-width:640px`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

**5. Compliance**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. This is unverified, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message in the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown, which the QA output attributes to the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain and matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. That is good for alignment, but I can't confirm authentication from this data.
- **Footer unverified.** The visible HTML doesn't include the footer, so the body unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) can't be confirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
Not verifiable. The 97 links were skipped by the probe and none appear in the truncated source. I can't check UTM presence or naming, landing page status or destination alignment with the "Halloween costumes, bags" subject. Image names such as `20260916…HalloweenLaunch` and `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance` suggest creative reused from earlier sends, but that is a content question.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the roughly 35 content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw ESP headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC there.
4. Review the five LiveRamp pixels and what `s=` encodes. Reduce them to one if they are retained.
5. Re-run link validation with the redirect domains followed, so destinations and UTM parameters can be audited.
6. Clean up the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, delete the `IMPUT HERE` comment, dedupe the `format-detection` metas, and drop the dead `img` max-width rule.
