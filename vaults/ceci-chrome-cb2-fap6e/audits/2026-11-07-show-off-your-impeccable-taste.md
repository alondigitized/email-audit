# Show off your impeccable taste
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, hosting-themed send that tries to do too much. It opens with a "LONG WEEKEND SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" banner, which is the only concrete offer. Directly below it, a large "HOSTING SEASON IS NEARLY UPON US" block with a "SHOP ENTERTAINING" button takes over. The subject, "Show off your impeccable taste", matches the lifestyle tone but never mentions the sale. The sale then fights with a gift-giving story.
- The photography is moody and on-brand, with candlelit tables and glassware. It also runs very long. After the entertaining hero there are category tiles (Dining Tables, Dining Chairs, Storage, Rugs), an Interior Design Services block, and a "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" card promo. Then comes a "More for you" grid of glasses at $10–$14, plus app and registry footers. Four or five competing messages dilute each other, and the eye has nowhere to settle.
- Judgement: a competent, pretty, mid-funnel send. It recycles the Long Weekend Sale from Nov 5 and Nov 6 and buries it under editorial content. It is a 5, not better.

## What's working

- The "LONG WEEKEND SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" banner at the top gives a concrete, scannable offer with a "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- The tabletop and candlelit photography is cohesive and fits the hosting-season theme.
- The category tiles (Dining Tables, Dining Chairs, Storage, Rugs) give clear, specific click paths.
- The glassware grid shows strike-through prices ($10.00, $12.00, $14.00), so the savings are visible and affordable.
- The Double Rewards block carries over the loyalty benefit from the Nov 6 send.

## What's weak

- The subject promises taste and style but never mentions the sale, so the strongest hook is missing from the inbox view.
- The email runs about 8,000px tall. Several modules compete: sale, entertaining, design services, rewards, glassware, app and registry.
- Two "SHOP ENTERTAINING" buttons sit around a large image collage, which makes the hero feel repetitive and ambiguous.
- The sale banner text overlays a busy photo, so contrast and legibility are weaker than in the clean Nov 5 "STARTS NOW" hero.
- The "DOUBLE REWARDS" block, with its percent figures partly overlaid, is dense and slightly hard to read. The deadline in the "NOW THROUGH NOV 9TH" pill is small.

- 1. Choose one story. Lead with the sale and the glassware or tabletop products, and move rewards, design services and the app to the footer or to another send.
- 2. Put the sale end date in the hero, such as "Ends Monday", and make it large. The brand has used this urgency before.
- 3. Replace the collage with three or four shoppable hosting products, each with a price.
- 4. Make the two "SHOP ENTERTAINING" buttons specific, for example "SHOP SERVEWARE" and "SHOP GLASSWARE".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard ESP template (Acoustic/Silverpop-style `mi_` tracking) with 71 click-tracked links. The QA data shows 14 content images with no alt text, 10 insecure (`http://`) third-party cookie-sync pixels, and a viewport setting that blocks pinch-zoom. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM content could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Mixed-content pixels:** 10 Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-redirect pixels load over plain HTTP:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?…` through `pub10.php?…`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS sources may drop them, so the tracking is unreliable. They also leak identifiers over an unencrypted channel.
- **Unprobed links:** All 71 tracking links were skipped by the QA check (click-redirect domains). Link validity and destinations are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Pixel alt text:** The open-tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/913e5b7e-…?mi_cid=…` have no alt attribute. This is minor, but empty `alt=""` is the correct form for decorative pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 14 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt (e.g. `e6081688-456e-4102-a175-df947024fcc3.jpg`, `d5ca4c07-…jpg`, `cbe3c6ab-…jpg`). With images blocked, those sections are blank and unreadable to screen readers.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `*{… line-height:100%}` in the global CSS also work against user text scaling.
- **Web fonts:**
  - `@font-face` fonts load from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, a different domain from the sender.
  - Several font URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`).
  - Most clients ignore web fonts anyway, so the fallback is `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** The HTML still contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (including the typo). It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the truncated HTML, but the rest of the body wasn't available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check the raw message at the source.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not verifiable because the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 71 tracking links go through redirect domains and were not resolved, so destination URLs and UTM parameters are unknown. The `<title>` ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") is generic and doesn't match the subject line, but that is a minor point.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve all Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or remove the 10 `cookieredir` pixels if they aren't essential.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the 14 content images, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and avoid `text-size-adjust:none`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message headers, since the relay may be hiding them. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC on `mail.cb2.com`.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and with the tracking-redirect chains resolved, to check the footer unsubscribe, merge tokens and UTM parameters.
6. Remove the placeholder comment and fix the `//Styles` double slashes in the font URLs.
