# Black Friday holiday deals are here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's weakest offer in a week of Black Friday sends. The hero says "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 30% OFF¹" over a moody burgundy holiday-gifting scene. Two days ago the brand was shouting "UP TO 60% OFF" (Nov 21, "UP TO 60% OFF BACK-IN-STOCK STYLES"). The discount drop is not explained, and the dagger footnote makes it feel conditional. The creative itself is polished.
- The layout is a long scroll. The hero is split awkwardly: "HOLIDAY" sits above a "SHOP HOLIDAY" button, and "AND GIFTS" follows below it. That interrupts the headline and puts a button in the middle of a sentence. Next comes a second gifting block with a "SHOP GIFTING" button, a six-tile category grid, then a free-shipping banner ($99+) and a "SAVE 15% OFF in the new year" banner. The email stacks four different offers (30%, free shipping, 15% later, 10% back) and doesn't pick one.
- Judgement: good visual brand, muddled offer. It reads as a sitewide catch-all send in the middle of the Black Friday run, and it's the fourth or fifth near-identical promo this week.

## What's working

- The burgundy and gold holiday photography is rich, on-brand and clearly seasonal.
- The "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" headline is large and high-contrast, so the event is clear immediately.
- The six-tile category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Holiday & Gifting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Pillows & Throws) gives easy browsing paths.
- The free-shipping banner at $99+ repeats the Nov 20 offer and is easy to understand.
- The footer has app, rewards (10% back) and SMS (15% off) options for members.

## What's weak

- "UP TO 30% OFF" is a visible step down from the "UP TO 60%" sends of Nov 21 and 22, with a footnote marker and no urgency or end date.
- The split headline ("HOLIDAY" / button / "AND GIFTS") breaks the reading flow.
- The email has too many competing offers: 30% off, free shipping, 15% off in the new year and 10% back.
- The "SAVE 15% OFF in the new year" block is weak and confusing during a sale that is already running.
- The tile labels in the grid sit on busy photos and are hard to read in places (for example "HOLIDAY & GIFTING" over a dark vase).
- The subject "Black Friday holiday deals are here" is generic and has no number.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear offer and put the end date in the hero. Put "UP TO 30% OFF" and "Ends Monday" in a single line, and keep the headline intact above the first button.
- 2. Remove the "15% off in the new year" block or move it to the footer. It dilutes the sale.
- 3. Use one primary CTA per section. Merge "SHOP HOLIDAY" and "SHOP GIFTING" into one "Shop holiday gifts" button.
- 4. Add a gift price tier ("Gifts under $100") for a concrete hook.
- 5. Add dark overlays or solid label bars on the tile grid to fix legibility.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off holiday gifts + free shipping $99+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday: gifts, decor and 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $99+, plus 10% back in Rewards Dollars. Sale ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Marshall speakers, bar sets and cozy bedding, all up to 30% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The template is a standard ESP build (Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `image.mail.cb2.com` / `mi.cb2.com` hosts). The confirmed defects are 20 content images with no `alt`, 11 insecure third-party `http://` tracking pixels, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings are unverified. They come from the AgentMail relay capturing headers, not from evidence that the headers are missing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **69 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 third-party pixels load over plain `http://`.** They are Dotomi (Conversant) cookie-sync and retargeting pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
- **Effect of the `http://` pixels:**
  - Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite them. This causes mixed-content and "not secure" issues, and sync or attribution can fail silently.
  - Ten cookie-sync calls per open add load and a privacy footprint.
- **First-party open pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/fd4f8452-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carry the recipient identifier in the URL query string. The `mi_u` value is a hash, so this is not a raw email address.
- **Fonts and hosts:** `@font-face` rules reference `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…` (double slash in the path). Four `HNW05` faces load from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, a different registrable domain.
  - The double-slash URLs may fail to resolve, or resolve only by server tolerance.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts anyway.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, for example `503aa44b-…jpg`, `bd602b26-…jpg` and `b64a2b4a-…jpg`. With images blocked, the email shows empty boxes, and screen readers read the filenames.
- **Tracking pixels also lack `alt`:** `UCMController`, `pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. This is low severity. They should carry `alt=""` and a 1×1 size so they are treated as decorative.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (typo included) remains in the `<head>`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **`<title>`** is `CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME`. It doesn't match the subject line, which is minor for a mailer.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, the VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditional blocks are present, and there is a mobile breakpoint (`max-width: 418px`).
- **Limit:** the HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm CTA button markup, text/background contrast or table roles.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The recipient identifier appears only as hashed `mi_u` / `mi_mid` values in the pixel URLs. The truncated source shows no unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[[…]]` tokens, but the body wasn't visible, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** the QA check flagged both as not found. The check itself notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect.
  - Check the raw `.eml` or a direct-delivery copy.
  - Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this data.
  - The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain, so alignment is plausible but unverified.
- **Visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 69 links are click-tracked redirects and were not resolved, so UTM parameters, landing-page destinations and promo-code carry-through can't be verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 20 content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://` or remove them. At minimum, drop the 10 `pubN.php` cookie-sync calls.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Fix the `cb2.com//Styles/…` double-slash font URLs. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. Re-run QA with the raw RFC 822 message, so `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` can be confirmed.
6. Resolve the redirect chains with a followed-redirect probe, then verify UTM parameters and landing-page destinations.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check merge tokens, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
