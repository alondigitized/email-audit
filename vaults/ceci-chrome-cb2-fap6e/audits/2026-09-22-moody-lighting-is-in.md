# Moody lighting is in
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 pivoting from its discount roll-ups and category launches into a designer-collaboration story — Kara Mann for lighting — and it's the most cohesive creative the brand has sent this week. The hero copy ("Vellum. Plaster. Glass. Layered lighting in materials that last, courtesy of cult favorite designer Kara Mann. EXCLUSIVELY AT CB2.") gives the email a point of view that the "STEALS OF THE WEEK" and "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" sends lacked, and the designer bio block with her headshot adds credibility CB2 rarely uses.
- The execution is clean but the fundamentals are the same CB2 has repeated all week: no price visible near the hero, no discount, no urgency, just a lookbook march through five lamp products (George, Sandy, Bandit, Bonne) shot in moody, warm-toned vignettes. It's a beautiful email for someone actively furnishing a room, and a completely skippable one for anyone else, because there's zero reason to act today.
- Judgement: strong brand-building creative, weak conversion mechanics — this reads as a "save for later" email, not a "click now" email, consistent with the pattern this week where only the discount roll-ups (2026-09-20, 2026-09-22) scored above 6.

## What's working

- Clear single-theme hero ("A WHOLE NEW MOOD") with a styled vignette of five lamps that sets the aesthetic immediately.
- The Kara Mann designer callout adds a credibility layer ("exclusively at CB2") not seen in the brand's other recent sends.
- Consistent moody, warm lifestyle photography across all four lamp features (George, Sandy, Bandit, Bonne) — the styling feels premium and on-brand.
- Prices are shown on each individual product ($399, $63, etc.) even though the hero itself is priceless.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid and "Don't see your fabric of choice?" swatch CTA extend engagement for browsers who don't want a lamp.

## What's weak

- No discount, promo code, or urgency anywhere in the email — pure full-price lookbook, same gap flagged in 2026-09-18's "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" and 2026-09-19's bedroom refresh.
- Primary CTA "SHOP LIGHTING" is generic and repeated twice with no differentiation — it doesn't tell you which lamp or price point you're heading toward.
- The email is very long (five full lamp features plus a swatch banner plus a "more for you" grid) before reaching the footer — a lot of scroll for a single-category push.
- Subject "Moody lighting is in" is vague trend-speak, not a concrete offer or product name.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible price-anchor or intro offer near the hero (e.g., "New lighting from $63" or a designer-collection intro discount) — every recent CB2 send without a number in the hero has scored below 6.
- 2. Replace the generic "SHOP LIGHTING" CTA with product-specific copy like "Shop the George Lamp" tied directly to the hero image shown.
- 3. Trim the email — four full lamp sections is one lookbook too many; cut to two hero pieces plus the "more for you" grid to reduce scroll fatigue.
- 4. Move the Kara Mann designer bio higher, right under the hero — it's the most differentiated asset in the email and currently gets buried after the first product.
