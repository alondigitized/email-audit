# THIS IS YOUR CHANCE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a "Final Weekend" Warehouse Sale send that tries to do too much. The top banner is strong. "WAREHOUSE SALE, UP TO 60% OFF*" sits on a dark plum band with a "Shop the Sale" button, so the offer lands first. It's the same Warehouse Sale CB2 pushed on 10/28 ("WAREHOUSE SALE IS SELLING OUT").
- Under the banner, the email turns into a catalog of "In Stock" category tiles: bedroom, coffee tables, sectionals, storage, sofas, lighting, rugs. The hero says "SEIZE the MOMENT" with an "It's Back in Stock" button, which reads as a different message from the 60%-off sale above it. Then a second sale, "Guest Ready Sale, up to 40% off," appears lower down. After that come interior design services, app promos, a 10% Rewards offer and a 15% signup offer. That's five or six competing messages in a very long scroll.
- The photography is moody and on-brand, and the category tiles are easy to scan. But the subject, "THIS IS YOUR CHANCE," says nothing about the offer. The email would be stronger if it committed to one idea: 60% off, ends Sunday.

## What's working

- The "FINAL WEEKEND / WAREHOUSE SALE / UP TO 60% OFF*" banner is high-contrast and sits at the top with its own button.
- The category tiles (Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas) each have a short label and a "Shop Now" button, so the reader can go straight to a category.
- The photography is cohesive and warm, and the brand voice is consistent with earlier CB2 sends.
- The "In Stock" label on the tiles speaks to availability, which matters during a clearance sale.
- The footer has useful extras: the app, Rewards, and the Crate & Barrel family logos.

## What's weak

- The "Seize the Moment" hero with "It's Back in Stock" competes with the sale banner and makes the offer unclear.
- The Guest Ready Sale (up to 40% off) below the tiles introduces a second, lower discount, which dilutes the 60% headline.
- The bottom third (design services, app, Rewards, 15% signup, social) is too long. The reader has to scroll far past the offer to reach it.
- The 60% figure has an asterisk, and the dense fine print at the bottom is hard to read. There's no deadline or end date in the visible copy beyond "Final Weekend."
- The bottom row of lighting, rugs and wall art tiles has small, low-contrast labels and no visible buttons. The "Storage" tile's button also looks small and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Keep the 60% banner and the category tiles, and move Guest Ready, design services and the signup offer to a different send.
- 2. Replace the "Seize the Moment" hero with a hero that carries the 60% message and a real end date, ideally with a countdown like the one CB2 used on 10/27 ("ONE DAY LEFT").
- 3. Show some prices or "from" prices on the tiles. Right now there's no product detail to click for.
- 4. Make the bottom tile row consistent, with visible buttons and readable labels.
- 5. Move the app and Rewards blocks below one compact footer row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw, unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has widespread missing alt text and 11 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The QA run scored a 43% pass rate (1 fail, 3 warnings). The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be evaluated because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 67 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were checked for destination status, redirect chains or 404s.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Likely effects:** Clients that block or rewrite insecure content (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) will probably drop these. They may also trigger mixed-content warnings in web-based clients.
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** The 10 `pub*.php` calls are third-party cookie-sync redirects embedded in email. Some clients and privacy tools block them, and they add load and a privacy footprint with no rendering benefit.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/8dc268eb-8eef-41c7-b8dc-e5e48177dd38?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS and look intentional. Both lack alt attributes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** 13 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute. Examples are `ca5f45cd-…jpg`, `e7b64b9f-…jpg` and `6f893396-…jpg`. If these carry product or promotional content, the email is unreadable with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** The 13 content images are the real accessibility problem. The tracking pixels (all 11 Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`) also lack alt. For these, `alt=""` is the correct fix, so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in the `*{}` rule and the `body` rule stops text resizing.
- **Cross-brand font URLs:** The `@font-face` rules `HNW05` load from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`. This is a cross-domain dependency, and the fonts may not load if hotlinking is restricted. The `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` comment is a leftover template placeholder.
- **Only a fragment was reviewed:** The source was truncated, so structure, tables and the rest of the markup are unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed failure:** The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found. It looks like a Marigold/Cheetah/Salesforce-style reviews placeholder that didn't resolve. It either renders as visible text or leaves an empty reviews block. Either way the send-time merge didn't run, or the field had no data for this recipient.
- **No other tokens found:** The QA check did not flag any other unresolved tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not verifiable:** The List-Unsubscribe header, the List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058) and Authentication-Results were not found. The QA output attributes this to the AgentMail relay, which likely strips them. Treat these as unknown, not as sender failures.
- **Unsubscribe link and address not confirmed:** The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements either way.
- **Sender domain:** The From address is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain of the brand. A DNS check of SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `mail.cb2.com` would settle authentication.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable:** All 67 links were skipped, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. Tracking is evidently handled by redirect domains and `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters on the pixels.
- **No landing page data:** No landing page was fetched, so alignment with the email's offer can't be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the merge token:** Resolve or remove `%%reviews_skulist%%`. Add a send-time validation that blocks sends with unresolved `%%…%%` tokens, and a fallback for empty review data.
2. **Add alt text:** Give all 13 content images descriptive `alt` text, and set `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
3. **Move pixels to HTTPS:** Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels if they aren't essential.
4. **Restore zoom and text sizing:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
5. **Clean up fonts and template:** Host the `HNW05` fonts on the sender's own domain. Delete the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` placeholder.
6. **Re-run the audit against the raw message:** Capture the original `.eml` with its headers, not the relay copy, to check List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the body unsubscribe link. Also run a real link probe that follows the 67 tracking redirects to their final URLs and UTM parameters.
