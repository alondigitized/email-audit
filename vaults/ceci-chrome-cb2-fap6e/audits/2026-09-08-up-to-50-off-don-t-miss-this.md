# Up to 50% off. Don't miss this.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 running three offers in one send — the Labor Day furniture sale, the Double Rewards credit-card cross-sell, and a Clearance callout — stapled together into one long scroll. The good news: the hero is clean, on-brand, and leads with a real number (up to 50% off) plus a category grid that gives shoppers an immediate path in. The bad news: the same "10% 20% BACK" strikethrough render bug from yesterday's 2026-09-07 "DOUBLE REWARDS§ IS ALMOST OVER" send is still here, stacked right in the middle of this email, undermining the credit-card block a second day running.
- Structurally this is the 2026-09-06 "category grid" formula again, just with the countdown clock now under 24 hours (01:17:10:29) and a new subject line. The subject itself is generic — "Up to 50% off. Don't miss this." is the vaguest, most template-feeling subject in this whole stretch of sends, worse than the specific "48 HOURS LEFT" or "BACK IN STOCK" lines CB2 has used this week.
- Verdict: solid hero, real render bug still unfixed, and a subject line that undersells the urgency the body actually has. This lands in the middle of the pack — better than the 2026-09-07 rewards email, weaker than the 2026-09-07 "48 HOURS LEFT" send.

## What's working

- Live countdown clock (01:17:10:29) at the very top creates real urgency before any scrolling.
- Hero image is clean with a clear offer stack: "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" with legible white-on-photo text.
- Category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor, Shop All) gives shoppers multiple concrete entry points instead of one generic CTA.
- Footer utility block (app download, rewards, gifting, SMS signup) is well-organized and scannable.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards block repeats the exact render bug from 2026-09-07: "GET 10% 20% BACK" shows a struck-through "10%" stacked against "20%" instead of one clean number — this is the second consecutive day this bug has shipped.
- Subject line "Up to 50% off. Don't miss this." is generic boilerplate — it doesn't convey that the sale ends in under 24 hours, which the body clearly does via the countdown.
- Three unrelated offers (Labor Day sale, credit card rewards, clearance) are crammed into one linear scroll with no visual separation in intent — it reads like three merged emails rather than one campaign.
- Cadence fatigue: this is the fifth near-identical Labor Day promo this week (2026-09-02 through 2026-09-08), and the creative (leaf/window hero) hasn't changed since earlier sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Double Rewards render bug before this template ships again — it's now appeared in back-to-back sends and actively confuses the offer (is it 10% or 20%?).
- 2. Rewrite the subject to carry the urgency that's already in the body — the countdown says under 2 days left, the subject should say so.
- 3. Split the credit-card cross-sell and clearance sections into separate follow-up sends rather than bundling three offers into one long scroll — each dilutes the others.
- 4. Move "SHOP ALL" or a single top category (e.g., Rugs, which has had its own dedicated send this week and clearly performs) higher, ahead of the credit-card block, since that's the primary revenue driver.
