# End of Summer Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-15**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's twelfth distinct creative mode this week, and after ten days of designer spotlights, product events, and lookbooks, "End of Summer Sale" is the plainest, most conventional send yet — a real sale with a fine-print percentage (up to 40% off, buried in the footer) fronted by a moody cocktail-glass hero and clean category tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor). It's competent and legible, but the actual offer never makes it into the hero copy or the visible body — you have to scroll to the tiny footnote to learn it's "up to 40% off." That's a real miss for a sale email, whose entire job is to make the discount the first thing you see.
- Compared to 8/13's "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" (7/10, real urgency with "ends tonight" in the subject) and 8/12's design-services sweepstakes (7/10), this one has a genuine site-wide sale event behind it but undersells it. The category-grid body is clean and browsable, which is a plus, but the whole email reads as "seasonal sale exists" rather than "here's exactly what you save and until when."
- Verdict: solid production values, real event, but the offer is hidden and there's no deadline — a mid-pack send that could easily have been CB2's best of the week with one copy change.

## What's working

- Clear, uncluttered hero: "END OF SUMMER SALE" in bold type over a stylish tablescape image with an unambiguous "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) gives the eye four clear, well-photographed entry points instead of one generic CTA.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is crisp, layout is consistent top to bottom.
- Footer modules (app download, rewards, gifting, SMS signup) are well organized and not visually competing with the sale message.

## What's weak

- The actual discount ("up to 40% off") appears only in tiny footnote text at the bottom — nowhere in the hero or subject line.
- No deadline or urgency anywhere visible ("Ends 8/31" or similar) despite this being a time-bound "End of Summer" event.
- Subject line is generic and could be any retailer's sale email — no CB2 voice, no specificity.
- The "LEAVE US A REVIEW, WIN $1,000" banner competes for attention with the actual sale and isn't obviously connected to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the discount number in the hero, not the footnote — e.g., "UP TO 40% OFF" as a subhead directly under "END OF SUMMER SALE." This is the single highest-impact fix; right now the email hides its own offer.
- 2. Add a visible end date to the hero or just above the CTA button ("Ends 8/31") to create real urgency, consistent with what worked in 8/13's "ends tonight" send.
- 3. Move or shrink the $1,000 review sweepstakes banner — it's a strong hook but currently steals focus from the sale it's supposed to support.
- 4. Tighten subject line to include the discount for a stronger open signal.
