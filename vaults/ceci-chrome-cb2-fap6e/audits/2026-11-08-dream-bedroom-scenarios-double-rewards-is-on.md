# Dream bedroom scenarios + Double rewards is on
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a bedroom-themed send from CB2 that opens with the "LONG WEEKEND SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" banner, the same offer the brand has run all week. It is cleaner than most CB2 sends. The hero is a moody bedroom photo, and four named beds follow in a steady rhythm: Wolcott, Bio, Russell and Davison. Each has one line of copy and a "SHOP NOW" button.
- The email tries to do three jobs at once: a bedroom editorial, a "So much ships free" festive-decor block, and a "Double Rewards is on" credit-card block. The subject line promises the bedroom and rewards, but the free-shipping block is a third message the subject never mentions. The bedroom story is also weak on price. The sale banner is the only offer, and none of the four beds shows a price or a sale price. Prices only appear in the "More for you" grid at the bottom.
- This is a decent, pretty send but not a sharp one. It repeats the 50% banner from Nov 5 and Nov 6, and the "Double Rewards" push echoes the Nov 6 "Last double rewards" email. A subscriber would see little new here.

## What's working

- The bedroom photography is cohesive and moody, so the hero reads as a clear theme.
- The four-bed rhythm is easy to scan, with one product, one line and one button each.
- The "UP TO 50% OFF" banner at the top puts the offer first.
- The "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" block has a clear headline, a "NOW THROUGH NOV 9TH" date and a "LEARN MORE" button.
- The "More for you" grid shows real prices, such as Foley Mink Velvet Dining Armchair $399.00.

## What's weak

- The four hero beds have no prices or discount, so the 50% off never ties to a specific product.
- The email carries three competing messages (bedroom, free shipping, rewards), and the subject names only two.
- The "SO MUCH SHIPS FREE" block is unrelated to the bedroom theme. It also gives no clear offer or deadline.
- Several buttons share the same "SHOP NOW" copy, so none is specific about what you buy.
- The Double Rewards block is a repeat of the Nov 6 send. The "10% 20% BACK" figures are hard to read at a glance.
- The email is very long, and the fine print and footer blocks are dense.

- 1. Put sale prices on the four beds (for example "Wolcott Bed, from $X, now $Y"). That is the single change that would lift clicks most.
- 2. Cut the free-shipping block or move it into its own send. Keep the bedroom story and the rewards offer.
- 3. Give each button a specific label, such as "Shop the Wolcott Bed" or "Shop all beds".
- 4. Move the rewards block higher or fold it into a slim banner, and state the benefit in one clear line.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier 50% sends so the repeat offer feels less stale.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditional comments. The QA run reported 0 hard failures, 3 warnings and a 57% pass rate. The warnings are mostly accessibility gaps (missing `alt`) and insecure third-party tracking pixels. The source is truncated and the click-tracking links were skipped, so several checks can't be confirmed either way.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified:** 66 links were skipped as tracking/click-redirect domains, so no destination status, redirect chain or final URL was checked.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 third-party Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels load over `http://`.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - These are cookie-sync calls. Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will likely block or rewrite them, and clients may flag mixed content, so they probably don't fire.
- **Pixel count:** Ten near-identical sync pixels add weight and third-party exposure for little measurable value.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=<sha256>&mi_...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/de490b22-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a 64-hex-character hash, which looks like a hashed recipient identifier.
- **Font URLs:** Some `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Others pull from a sister domain (`crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`). These resolve today, but the double slash is fragile and the cross-domain dependency is a hidden failure point.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** Nine content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`, for example `e6081688-456e-4102-a175-df947024fcc3.jpg` and `bbba4064-4d88-40ee-881a-bdf7bc8e1288.jpg`. If any carry offer or CTA text, it disappears with images off. Every tracking pixel also lacks `alt` (`o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel, `pub1`–`pub10.php`, `UCMController`). Those should have `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on iOS. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing blocked:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text auto-sizing. This conflicts with the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%` set earlier in the same stylesheet.
- **Forced global `line-height`:** `*{... line-height:100%}` applies to every element. It can clip wrapped text for users who increase font size.
- **Mobile breakpoint:** `@media (max-width: 418px)` with `.mobile{width:414px}` is a narrow, hard-coded cutoff. Devices between 419px and 689px get the desktop layout, since the only other queries are at 690px and 768px.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shipped in production. It is harmless but sloppy.
- **Title:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` doesn't match the subject line. It is a minor issue in browser-view and some preview contexts.
- **Document language:** `lang="en"` is present, which is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%  %%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA output. The HTML is truncated, so the body copy and footer weren't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN):** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. If the headers really are absent, the sender would fall short of the Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement for RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed from this capture. The envelope domain `mail.cb2.com` also can't be checked against it.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** Eleven Dotomi pixels in a marketing email are a privacy-disclosure consideration. They aren't a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No evidence available. All 66 links point to tracking/redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters, landing URLs and offer consistency with the subject line ("Double rewards") can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to the nine content images, using the visible offer or product text. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** or remove them. The ten `pubN.php` sync calls are the first to drop.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and use `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%` consistently instead of `none`.
4. **Re-run the audit with unmodified raw headers** (a direct seed-inbox capture rather than the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. **Resolve the 66 tracking links** with a redirect-following probe. That would give real continuity and UTM data instead of a skip.
6. **Clean up the template:** remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs, and make the page `<title>` match the campaign.
7. **Retry the audit with the full, untruncated HTML** so the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and any personalization can be checked.
