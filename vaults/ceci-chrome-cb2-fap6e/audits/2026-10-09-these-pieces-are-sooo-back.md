# These pieces are sooo back
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a restock catalog with a sale buried inside it. The "SEIZE the MOMENT" hero over a burgundy velvet chair has an "IT'S BACK IN STOCK" button. Below it sit eight or so category tiles: bedroom, coffee tables, sectionals, storage, sofas, lighting, rugs and wall art. The photography is warm and consistent, but the layout is a long scroll.
- The only price offer is "THE SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT / UP TO 25% OFF". It sits about three-quarters of the way down. The subject line, "These pieces are sooo back", never mentions it. Right after it comes the same "SHIPPING, ON US / ORDERS OF $99 OR MORE" block that went out this morning under "So we're doing a little thing". The email asks the reader to care about a restock before it says why they should buy now.
- Verdict: good-looking but badly ordered. Stock news with no stated reason to act today leads, and the 25% offer comes late. This is the sixth CB2 send in four days, and the free-shipping block is a repeat from this morning, so it feels like a recycled module.

## What's working

- The photography is moody, warm and cohesive, and it matches CB2's brand and the recent "Merriment is upon us" and "THIS BEST SELLING BED" sends.
- Each category tile has a clear "SHOP NOW" button, so the reader can jump straight to the room they care about.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid shows specific products with visible prices and some strikethrough savings, for example the Surf Sling chair at $599.00.
- The footer has useful extras: the CB2 app, 10% back in Rewards Dollars, a registry pitch and 15% off for signing up.
- The "SEIZE the MOMENT" headline is confident, and the hero image is striking.

## What's weak

- The 25% upholstery offer is hidden below the category tiles, so the email's strongest hook is the last thing most people would reach.
- The hero says "IT'S BACK IN STOCK" without naming a product or a price, so there is no concrete reason to click.
- The free-shipping block is a verbatim repeat of this morning's send, so it feels recycled.
- Eight or more tiles with equal weight leave no single clear action, and the email is very long.
- On the "Wall Art & Decor" row, the "All back in stock" label looks cramped and clipped at the right edge. This is hard to confirm at this resolution.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the upholstery event to the hero, with "UP TO 25% OFF" in the headline and "SHOP UPHOLSTERY" as the button. Make restock the secondary story.
- 2. Cut the category tiles to three or four of the strongest. Link each to a specific in-stock hero product rather than a category.
- 3. Drop the free-shipping block, or merge it into a single slim banner. It was today's main send, so repeating it dilutes both emails.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the event, such as "Ends Sunday", so the urgency is credible.
- 5. Make the "MORE FOR YOU" grid more personal, or put it directly under the hero as a "best sellers back in stock" row.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) in the delivered HTML. It also carries about 10 plain-HTTP Dotomi tracking pixels, 12 content images without alt text, and unverifiable header and authentication data because of the AgentMail relay. The automated pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, so cookie-sync and retargeting calls will fail silently.
  - Ten of the cookie-redirect pixels use identical query strings across `pub1`–`pub10`. This is a heavy third-party fan-out for one email open.
- **Click links not verified:** 72 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/bbf07979-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are served over HTTPS. The `mi_u` value looks like a 64-character hex hash, so it is likely a hashed identifier in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 12 images under `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, for example `d0a0521a-c93c-4a52-a9b5-f7c6b32c22af.jpg` and `b126e58e-2cbf-4513-a3cf-e192123cc1a6.jpg`. With images blocked, these render empty and screen readers read out the filenames. The QA output doesn't say whether these images are linked.
- **Missing alt on tracking images:** the Dotomi `pub1`–`pub10`, `UCMController`, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` images also lack alt text. This is lower impact if they are 1x1 pixels, but an explicit `alt=""` is still correct.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Font loading:** `@font-face` rules point to two domains, `www.cb2.com` and `www.crateandbarrel.com`. Some URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Both resolve in most cases, but the double slash is unnecessary. Web fonts only load in a few clients, and the declared fallback is Helvetica or sans-serif.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** the HTML still contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Source truncated:** the body was not visible, so I can't comment on table structure, `role="presentation"`, or text-to-image ratio.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw merge token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears unresolved in the delivered email. This `%%var%%` syntax is typical of Marketing Cloud-style personalization, and the variable is probably meant to populate product review data per SKU. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm whether it is visible or inside a hidden block, comment or attribute. The data feed that populates it did not run for this send.
- No other raw tokens (`{{ }}`, `[[ ]]`, `%%`) were flagged.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. QA notes they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, so check the raw headers at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay likely strips it, so this is not a confirmed failure. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` and the image and tracking subdomains are consistent with each other.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing destinations can't be verified. The 72 click links are wrapped in tracking redirects that were not probed, and the visible HTML is truncated. No evidence of broken landing alignment, and no evidence of correct alignment either.
- **Cross-brand asset references:** fonts load from `crateandbarrel.com`, which suggests shared Crate & Barrel infrastructure. This is not a defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the data feed or add a fallback** for `%%reviews_skulist%%`, and suppress the block when the data is empty. Add a pre-send check for any leftover `%%...%%`.
2. **Move Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`, `https://ads.dotomi.com/...`) or ask the vendor for HTTPS endpoints. Consider consolidating the 10 `pubN.php` calls.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the 12 `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/...` content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify headers at the source**, not through the relay: inspect the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
6. **Resolve the 72 skipped click links** by following the redirect chain and confirming that UTM or campaign parameters survive to the landing page.
7. **Clean the template:** remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` comment and the double-slash font URLs.
