# Today only: 20% off bestselling toys!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a "today only" toy sale that spends its best real estate on the wrong offer. The subject promises 20% off bestselling toys, and the hero delivers it: "now in our app: 20% off bestselling toys, today only!" over a wooden dollhouse. But the top banner is a Double Rewards block ("last Double Rewards this year! Earn 20% back") sitting beside "up to 50% off The Fall Sales Event." Three discounts compete before the eye reaches the toy offer.
- The hero CTA, "Download Our App," is a weak fit for a sale email. It asks for a install, not a purchase. The email then drifts. The next module is storage ("a place to store it all"), then a second Fall Sales Event banner. After that comes a product grid that is mostly baby gear: an activity chair, play mats, an elephant plush and a chef coat. Almost none of it is the bestselling toys the subject advertised. The tail adds a credit card pitch, a second app promo with a $500 sweepstakes, and a design service.
- Compared with the 11/9 sofa send, which stayed on one theme, this one dilutes its own urgency. It is also the fifth or so Crate & Barrel send in three days that leans on Double Rewards and the Fall Sales Event. Judgement: a decent hook buried under promotional clutter.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. "Today only: 20% off bestselling toys" is repeated in the hero headline.
- The hero image is warm and on-category. The wooden dollhouse and holiday garland read as kids and gifting season.
- The hero names the toy categories (ball pits, play kitchens, workbenches), which gives the offer substance.
- The product grid shows prices, including struck-through sale prices ($129 to $105.20, $89 to $71.20).
- The brand look is consistent, with a clean green and white palette and a recognizable logo.

## What's weak

- The top banner stacks Double Rewards and the Fall Sales Event above the toy hero, so the "today only" offer is not first.
- The hero CTA is "Download Our App." It sends readers to an install step instead of to toys.
- The storage module and the "Shop All Storage" CTA are off-message. They do not support the toy sale.
- The "we love these for you" grid is mostly baby activity gear, a plush and a chef coat. It has few of the bestselling toys the subject advertised.
- The footer area is crowded. It has a credit card ad, a second app promo, a design service, a store locator and a block of dense fine print. This is a lot after the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero CTA "Shop 20% Off Toys" and link it to a toy collection. Move the app download to a small secondary line ("Extra perks in the app").
- 2. Remove the Double Rewards and Fall Sales Event banners from this send, or shrink them to a thin strip below the toy content. One offer, one deadline.
- 3. Replace the storage module with a 4-up grid of actual bestselling toys with sale prices. Examples are the play kitchen, workbench and ball pit named in the hero.
- 4. Drop the credit card, sweepstakes and design service modules from a single-day sale.
- 5. Add an explicit deadline such as "ends tonight at midnight CT" so the "today only" claim feels credible.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed problems are about 14 content images with no `alt` attribute, accessibility-hostile viewport and text-size settings, and a 57% QA pass rate. The compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because the headers were not captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 68 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Redirect chains, destinations and expiry are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- At least 13 tracking and analytics pixels are present. All lack `alt` (see section 3).
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` is a LiveRamp pixel.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/71b73b17-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
- Third-party pixels (`sr.rlcdn.com`) in the email body are an extra tracking and privacy surface. This is not a defect, but you should be aware of it.
- The HTML is truncated, so link-level `href` validity could not be checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (14, from the QA data).** All are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`, for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `ec997ddd-…jpg`, `575ca5e5-…gif` and `971aa10f-…jpg`. With no `alt`, images-off clients and screen readers fall back to filenames or nothing.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels.** These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />`** blocks pinch-zoom on iOS and Android, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}`** overrides the `100%` set on `body` and disables text-size adjustment globally.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. The title is also generic and does not match the subject.
- **Duplicate head tags:** `<meta charset>` is declared alongside `http-equiv="Content-Type"`. `format-detection` is declared 4 times (`telephone=no,address=no,…`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`).
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the source.
- **`<!--V2-->` before `<html>`** (between the doctype and the root element) is harmless, but it can trigger quirks in some parsers.
- Several rules are duplicated across the two `<style>` blocks (`.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass`, `table td`), which adds weight.
- `lang="en"` is set, and the MSO/VML namespaces and conditional blocks are present. Both are fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values in pixel URLs are resolved per-recipient identifiers, not broken tokens. The truncated body means this is only a partial check.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (QA WARN).**
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (RFC 8058).**
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

The QA notes say these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay, so these are unconfirmed gaps. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. The 68 click-redirect links were not probed, and no `utm_*` parameters or final landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 14 content images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original delivery to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
4. Escape the `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;` and set a meaningful title.
5. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment. Merge the duplicate CSS rules.
6. Extend the QA tooling to resolve the redirect links to their final URLs and check for `utm_*` parameters. Also capture the full HTML so the footer can be checked for the unsubscribe link and address.
