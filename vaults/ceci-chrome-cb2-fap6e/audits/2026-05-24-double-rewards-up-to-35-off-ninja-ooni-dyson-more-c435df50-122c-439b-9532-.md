- Brand-name subject ("Ninja, Ooni, Dyson") is concrete and clickable in a way "up to 50% off furniture" no longer is by day 4.
- Hero vignette is a clean kitchen-counter espresso scene — exactly the aspirational shot for this category.
- Category grid covers the obvious kitchen-tech sub-segments (espresso, blenders, cookware) so most readers find something.

## What's weak

- "Up to 35% off" is a noticeable downshift from the 50–60% drumbeat all weekend; feels like a softer pitch.
- Subject is long and ALL-CAPS-leaning ("DOUBLE REWARDS | Up to 35%…"), risks looking shouty in the inbox stack.

- Fourth Memorial Day promo of the day from this household of brands; cadence fatigue is real.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` attributes to every `<img>`** — descriptive alt for product/hero images, `alt=""` for tracking pixels and decorative spacers. This is the highest-impact fix (a11y + spam score).
