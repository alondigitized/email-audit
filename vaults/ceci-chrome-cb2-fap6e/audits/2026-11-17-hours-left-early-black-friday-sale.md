# HOURS LEFT: EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's final-day Early Black Friday send, and it is the plainest of the week. A tiny banner reads "LAST CHANCE / NOW UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE." The main hero reads "ENDS TONIGHT / THE EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 60% OFF." A third panel pushes "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" with a credit card. The urgency is real and the deadline is clear. But the email has no product at all, and it is mostly type over a moody mauve-rose photo.
- It also leads with two competing offers, 70% off clearance and 60% off the sale. Then it adds a third, 20% back in rewards. The reader has to work out which discount applies to what. Against the prior sends in the brand history (the 11/16 "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TOMORROW" and the 11/15 "WHY WAIT FOR BLACK FRIDAY?"), this is the same shell with the clock turned up. The editorial coffee-table and dining-chair sends gave a shopper something to look at. This one doesn't.
- Verdict: it's competent last-call urgency, but generic. It would work as a reminder to someone already planning to buy. It gives nobody a reason to click who wasn't already going to.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" and the subject's "HOURS LEFT" agree, so the deadline feels credible.
- The big "UP TO 60% OFF" numeral is the clear focal point of the main hero.
- The four category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) plus "SHOP THE SALE" give quick routing into the shop.
- The "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON" panel has a clear button ("LEARN MORE") and surfaces member value.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no product anywhere. For a furniture brand in the last hours of a sale, that is a missed chance to show actual deals.
- The 70% clearance banner, the 60% sale hero and the 20% rewards panel all compete, and the percentages don't reconcile.
- The "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" line shows a struck-through "10%" before "20%". It reads as a messy edit.
- The rewards panel is a credit-card pitch (CB2 Visa) rather than an offer for most readers, and it takes up as much space as the sale hero.
- The "NOW THROUGH NOV 17TH, 2026" label is small, low-contrast type on a busy photo.
- The generic "SHOP NOW" and "SHOP THE SALE" buttons lack specificity.
- The footer tiles (app, registry, text signup, 10% back) add clutter on a last-chance send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the second and third panels with a grid of 4–6 real products at their sale prices. Show prices, "was/now" pricing and a low-stock tag where it's true. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Lead with one offer. Make the hero "Up to 70% off clearance" or "Up to 60% off", not both, and put the other in a single line of copy.
- 3. Add a countdown timer or a specific cutoff, such as "Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight".
- 4. Fix the struck-through "10%" so it is either a clean "2X" callout or just "20% back".
- 5. Move the rewards pitch below the product grid as a single-line banner.
- 6. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop clearance up to 70% off" and "Shop sofas on sale".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook (MSO) conditionals. It has 57% QA pass, 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on 8 content images and 11 `http://` third-party pixel images (Dotomi). Compliance and link checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, 59 links were never probed and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **59 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **11 images load over plain `http://`**, all Dotomi/Conversant cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Non-HTTPS sources can be blocked or proxy-rewritten by clients (e.g. Gmail image proxy) as mixed content, so these syncs may silently fail.
  - Ten sequential cookie-redirect pixels also add load and third-party requests.
- **Open-tracking pixels are present:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex hash>...`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/a1d075c6-e29e-493b-a9b2-648395a0d094?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - These are HTTPS and functional. The `mi_u` value is a hashed per-recipient identifier, which is a privacy consideration rather than a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing alt text (8).** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `36e06259-…jpg`
  - `51f61942-…jpg`
  - `eb5900b2-…jpg`
  - `32b8875b-…jpg`
  - `92a4f5b7-…jpg`
  - `da47559c-…jpg`
  - `6b99bd92-…jpg`
  - `345f2993-…jpg`
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as nothing or as filenames. If any carry sale copy or CTAs, that content is lost.
- **Tracking and sync pixels missing alt (13):** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel, `UCMController` and `pub1.php`–`pub10.php`. These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>`** blocks pinch-zoom on iOS and some Android clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Single narrow mobile breakpoint:** `@media only screen and (max-width: 418px)` with `.mobile{width:414px}`. Phones wider than 418px, such as 430px "Max" devices, fall through to the desktop layout. This is inferred from the CSS, not a rendered test.
- **Template leftovers in source:**
  - `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unreplaced placeholder.
  - `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` is an empty conditional.
  - `<!----- MI styling end----->` uses a non-standard comment form.
  - These are harmless but sloppy.
- **Web fonts load cross-domain.**
  - Fonts come from `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`).
  - Some `cb2.com` URLs have a double slash (`//Styles/CB2/...`).
  - Fallbacks are declared (Helvetica, sans-serif), so the impact is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML: no unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[%…%]` tokens. The source is truncated, so the body and footer were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes these may be dropped by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If the sender really omits them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024) require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`, and the images and tracking use `image.mail.cb2.com`, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com`, which suggests aligned branded subdomains, but that is unverified.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. All 59 links were skipped as tracking redirects and the source is truncated, so UTM parameters and final landing pages are unknown. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking and sync pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://` (they support it), or drop the 10 `pubN.php` cookie-sync pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Raise the mobile breakpoint to about 480–600px, and clean up the leftover placeholder and empty conditional comments.
5. In the QA pipeline:
   - Capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy.
   - Resolve redirect chains for tracking links so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
   - Analyze the full HTML, including the footer.
