# See our new & exclusive Nuna clothing collection!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, editorial launch email for Nuna's new clothing collection. A "Wardrobe" hero with a Shop Now button leads into two fabric stories, ultra-fine cashmere and 100% Supima cotton. Each story has a product row beneath it, one for babies and one for toddlers and kids. It reads like a brand-partner launch, not a sale, and that suits the "new & exclusive" subject.
- The send is crowded, though. A "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" banner sits above the Nuna hero and competes with it. Below the Nuna content are strollers, a wallcovering collection, a $5000 sweepstakes, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design-service block. The email has no offer, price or deadline for the clothing itself. Compared with the 2026-10-12 sends ("ENDS TODAY: Up to $300 off…", "ENDS SOON: Up to 50% off!"), this one is calmer and more brand-led, with no urgency.
- Verdict: a well-built exclusive launch that stalls after the first two screens. It needs one reason to click now.
- Caveat: the screenshot was downscaled heavily, so small copy such as product prices, fine print and the preheader wasn't legible.

## What's working

- The hero is a single clear message, "new & exclusive: Nuna Wardrobe," with a visible Shop Now button.
- "Exclusive" gives a reason to care about this launch over the usual sale blasts.
- The fabric stories (cashmere, Supima cotton) add product detail that parents can judge quality from.
- The product rows are split by age, babies and toddlers & kids, so shoppers can self-sort.
- The fall palette and lifestyle photography fit the season and the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The nursery sale banner above the hero pulls attention from the Nuna launch and sells a different category.
- The Nuna clothing has no price, offer, loyalty perk or deadline, so there's nothing pushing a click today.
- The email runs very long, with strollers, wallcovering, a sweepstakes, a card promo, an app promo and a design-service block after the main story. Those dilute the clothing focus.
- Several "Shop Now" and "Shop All" buttons use generic copy.
- This is another send in a heavy cadence after the Halloween shop, the sofa sale and the free-shipping reminders earlier in the week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the second product row, or move it far down. Drop the $5000 sweepstakes, card promo, app promo and design-service blocks from this send. Let the Nuna launch be the whole email.
- 2. Remove the nursery sale banner from the top, or move it to a thin strip at the bottom.
- 3. Give the collection a reason to buy now, such as early access, a free-shipping threshold or a member perk. Even "Exclusive to Crate & Barrel Kids" would help.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Nuna cashmere" and "Shop Nuna Supima basics" in place of "Shop Now."
- 5. Set a real preheader that complements the subject. Right now none is visible.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Exclusive: Nuna's baby cashmere & Supima sets`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ultra-soft sweaters, pants and sets for babies through big kids, available only at Crate & Barrel Kids.`
