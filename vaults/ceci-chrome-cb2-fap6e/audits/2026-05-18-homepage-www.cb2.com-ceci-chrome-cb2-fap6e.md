# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- CB2 knows what it is — dark, sculptural, unapologetically modern — and this Memorial Day Sale landing hits that aesthetic hard. A tree-silhouette hero in warm desert tones, a 50% off callout, and an editorial module called "THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" that I am absolutely clicking. The only thing that's making me want to close this tab before I even get to the good stuff is the modal wall blocking literally all of it.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale hero** — the tree-shadow silhouette against that warm sand background is beautiful and on-brand. It's the kind of editorial photography that makes me stop scrolling.
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" module** — I can see it peeking below the fold and it's already calling my name. That copy is sardonic and millennial in exactly the right way.
- **"DOUBLE REWARDS" top strip** — shows the loyalty program before I even see the nav. Good hierarchy decision.
- **The sale itself** — UP TO 50% is the hook I needed. I'm the person who waits for the CB2 Memorial Day markdown and this confirms I waited correctly.

## What's weak

- **The modal** — "SAVE 15% ON FULL-PRICE ITEMS\*" is interrupting the sale I literally came to shop. It's jarring to be ambushed before I can even see what's on sale. The asterisk right after "ITEMS" is doing a lot of work I don't trust.
- **"CONTINUE WITHOUT SAVING"** — this is dark pattern language. It frames declining as losing money. I notice it and it annoys me.
- **Required phone number field** — email capture is expected, but requiring a mobile number to get a discount feels invasive. I'll close this without filling it out.
- **Above-fold nav is tiny** — on mobile the nav categories are crammed and small. I can see labels like Outdoor, Rugs, Bedroom & Bath but they're hard to tap accurately at this size.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by 8–10 seconds or trigger on exit-intent** — let me see one scroll of the sale before you interrupt me. I'm already here; I don't need to be captured yet.
- **Make the phone number optional, not required** — this is a conversion killer for the email list; a lot of people (me included) will bail rather than hand over their number to a furniture store.
- **Surface one or two hero products with prices in the hero** — right now it's pure editorial. A "Was $499, now $249" moment in the hero turns browsers into clickers immediately.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the promo strip** — if CB2 offers it (and I assume they do at some order level), putting that number next to "DOUBLE REWARDS" gives the strip twice the value.
