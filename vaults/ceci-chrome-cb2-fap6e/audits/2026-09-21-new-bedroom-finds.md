# NEW BEDROOM FINDS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 doing a straight product-launch email — "JUST IN, NEW FOR THE BEDROOM, ONLY AT CB2" — leading with a genuinely strong, warm-toned hero bed shot, then walking through four named pieces (Jackson Bed, Hawk Nightstand, Russell Bed, Rio Dresser) with clean product photography and designer credits. It's a departure from the discount-driven sends in the brand history (the 09-17 "up to 40% off" and 09-16 "last call" emails that scored 7s) — here the top banner nods to "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" but the body of the email is pure new-arrivals merchandising, no pricing shown on any hero product.
- The visual execution is CB2's strongest muscle — moody, well-lit lifestyle and studio shots, consistent typography, a coherent "new arrivals" narrative — but it's a discovery email, not a conversion email. There's no price, no specific discount tied to any featured product, and four different SHOP NOW CTAs compete for attention with no single hero.
- Judgment: well-produced but soft on impact — good for browsing, weak for driving an immediate purchase decision.

## What's working

- Hero "JUST IN / NEW FOR THE BEDROOM, ONLY AT CB2" copy is clear, on-category, and matches the subject line exactly.
- Each product callout (Jackson Bed, Hawk Nightstand, Rio Dresser) is tagged "NEW" with designer name, adding credibility and editorial feel.
- Material/fit details are visible in-line ("Lush ribbed boucle velvet," "FSC-certified oak," "Fully upholstered in faux mohair") — genuinely useful for a furniture buyer.
- Footer surfaces loyalty perks (Earn 10% Back Rewards, 10% off sign-up) and the CB2 app, giving repeat shoppers a reason to engage beyond this send.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout is consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- No price is shown anywhere near the four featured "JUST IN" products — a shopper can't gauge whether the Jackson Bed is $900 or $3,000 without clicking through.
- The top banner promises "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" but the entire body ignores that offer — it's bait that isn't paid off in the email itself.
- Four separate hero products each with their own "SHOP NOW" dilutes the CTA — there's no single primary action for the eye to land on.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" cross-sell block at the bottom does show prices (e.g., Andes Acacia King Bed $1199.00) but these are unrelated categories (dining chair, ottoman, side table), undercutting the bedroom focus of the send.
- Coming right after 09-21's "STEALS OF THE WEEK" roll-up, this pivots hard from discount urgency to soft-launch browsing — a whiplash in cadence for someone conditioned to expect deals from recent CB2 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add prices under each of the four featured products (Jackson Bed, Hawk Nightstand, Russell Bed, Rio Dresser) — this is the single biggest lever; furniture shoppers won't click through blind on unpriced items.
- 2. Either honor the "UP TO 40% OFF" banner with a visible discount on at least one new-bedroom item, or drop that banner from this email — right now it reads as a mismatched leftover from the sale campaign.
- 3. Pick one hero product (likely the Jackson Bed, since it leads visually) and make it the singular top CTA; demote the other three to a "see the rest" strip.
- 4. Move the loyalty/rewards footer content (10% back, 10% off signup) higher, since it's a stronger and more concrete incentive than anything in the main body.

- **Subject Alt B:** `4 new bedroom pieces, just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ribbed boucle, FSC oak, faux mohair — new pieces for the bedroom`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusively at CB2: new beds, nightstands & dressers`

  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 pixels, `?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`)

- Markup includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if mso]>`) and `.ExternalClass`/`.ReadMsgBody` resets — no rendering-blocking issues identified in the truncated source.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **Confirmed defect**: 1 unresolved raw merge token — `%%reviews_skulist%%` — rendered literally in the sent HTML instead of being populated. This is a broken personalization block and will be visible to recipients.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured message — QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/passing the header rather than a sender-side omission; cannot confirm compliance status from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) header not found — same caveat as above.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm authentication posture.
- Note: all three findings are header-visibility gaps in this capture pipeline, not confirmed sender-side violations — flag for header-capture verification rather than as a definitive compliance failure.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No issues found — the truncated source provided does not include CTA destination URLs to evaluate UTM parameter presence or landing-page alignment, and the 72 tracking links were not probed.

7. **Recommendations**
- Fix the ESP template/data feed so `%%reviews_skulist%%` resolves before send; add a pre-send QA gate that blocks send on any unresolved `%%...%%` token.
