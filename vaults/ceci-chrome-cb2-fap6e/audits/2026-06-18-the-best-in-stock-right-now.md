# The best in stock right now
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's editorial pivot after a week-long sale drumbeat — four promotional sends (Warehouse Sale June 10/13/17, Floor Sample Sale June 17) bookend what is now a soft "best of" curatorial email. The hero "SEIZE THE MOMENT" is big and bold but delivers nothing actionable — no discount, no deadline, no offer. Below it, the email cycles through three category blocks (Accent Chairs, Bedroom, Storage), a "DESIGNED WITH PRIDE" brand-values interlude, and a product grid at the bottom. It's a lot of sections with no single bet placed on any of them.
- The subject — "The best in stock right now" — is a missed opportunity. After two sales emails on June 17 alone, subscribers are either trained to expect a percentage or they've tuned out. "The best in stock right now" promises curation but the body never delivers a clear editorial point of view. Compare this to the June 14 marble send (score 7), which at least had a unifying theme. This email is just four separate rooms taped together.
- The "DESIGNED WITH PRIDE" section mid-scroll is the most glaring structural mistake. Interrupting a shopping flow with a brand-values statement breaks momentum at exactly the wrong moment. Save that for a standalone brand story send.

## What's working

- **Hero image quality is strong.** The dresser/sideboard photograph is well-lit and on-brand; it earns the "Seize the Moment" headline aesthetically even if the copy doesn't follow through.
- **CB2's visual identity is consistent throughout.** Neutral palette, clean typography, grid-based product tiles — no confusion about who sent this.
- **Product grid at the bottom adds tangible browsability.** The "MADE FOR YOU" section with multiple individual item tiles gives at least some shopability to an otherwise category-level email.
- **No render bugs visible.** Clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- **No offer, no reason to click now.** The entire email is category navigation — Accent Chairs, Bedroom, Storage — with no price anchor. Coming off two sales emails yesterday, this feels like a letdown, not a palate cleanser.
- **"SEIZE THE MOMENT" is empty hero copy.** It implies urgency but delivers no discount, no deadline, no exclusive. Just a mood.
- **"DESIGNED WITH PRIDE" mid-scroll kills momentum.** Dropping a brand manifesto section between Bedroom and product recs is the wrong place to pump the brakes.
- **Subject line "The best in stock right now" is the weakest send in the recent streak.** It's vague, offers no hook, and doesn't signal which category or what makes now special.
- **Three category blocks with no winner picked.** Accent Chairs + Bedroom + Storage is trying to be everything to everyone. One sharply focused editorial angle would outperform three shallow lanes.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one category and go deep.** Drop to one hero category — whichever is freshest or most in-season — and give it real editorial depth (a standout piece, a room shot, sizing/style guidance). The three-lane structure dilutes every section.
- 2. **Give the hero copy a reason to act.** Even "New arrivals, just dropped" is stronger than "Seize the Moment" when there's no sale. Or, if inventory genuinely is limited, say so: "These sell out fast."
- 3. **Move the product grid above the DESIGNED WITH PRIDE section.** Let shopping momentum carry through; put brand story in the footer or a dedicated send.
- 4. **Acknowledge the sale hangover.** Subscribers who just saw 60% off Warehouse Sale messaging two days ago need a reason to re-engage at full price. A brief "Sale's done — here's what's still worth it" framing would bridge the gap honestly.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) violation and will cause friction for low-vision users.
  ```html
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on the `*` selector** conflicts with the correct `body { -webkit-text-size-adjust: 100% }` setting and will suppress automatic text scaling in WebKit clients for all elements.
  ```css
  *{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}
  ```

- **Mobile breakpoint targets 418px** with a forced inner width of 414px. This is tuned for older iPhones (6/7/8); it will not trigger on larger Android devices or current iPhone Pro models without a wider breakpoint (typically 600px).

- **Inconsistent `font-display`** — only three of the eight `@font-face` declarations include `font-display: swap`. The Lineto Brown Light (`BrownLight`) and the first Lineto Brown Regular (`.eot` source) declarations omit it, causing a potential FOIT on supported clients.

- **`<html lang="en">`** is present — no issue there. Alt text coverage on body images cannot be confirmed from truncated source.
