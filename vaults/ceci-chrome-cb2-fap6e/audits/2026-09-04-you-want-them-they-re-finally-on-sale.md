# You want them. They’re finally on sale.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's Labor Day formula running one more time, but at least with a new hero image — the two floral-print armchairs against a blue-green paneled wall replace the beach-at-dusk shot that's run three-plus times this stretch. The subject line, "You want them. They're finally on sale," is a real departure from the brand's recent run of flat "THE LABOR DAY SALE" / "sale you can't miss" subjects — it's got a wishlist hook and some curiosity, even if it's vague about what "they" are.
- Below the hero, it's the same playbook: countdown timer, category nav tiles over a rug/textile background, a Double Rewards credit-card push, an "800+ New Arrivals" teaser, and the app/rewards/gifting footer grid. Nothing here is broken, and the visual hierarchy is clean — countdown, then hero offer, then categories — but the email is doing five different jobs (sale, categories, credit card, new arrivals, app/loyalty) and none of them get real specificity. "Up to 50% off" with no product, no room, no price anchor is the same soft offer CB2 keeps recycling.
- Net: better subject, same tired hero mechanics. Worth opening on curiosity, unlikely to convert beyond a lazy scroll-and-close.

## What's working

- New hero photography (armchairs + blue-green wall) breaks the beach-at-dusk repetition that's dogged the last three-plus sends.
- Countdown timer ("05:17:10:33") adds credible urgency right up top, before the hero.
- Category nav tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor, Shop All) are easy to scan and give multiple clickable entry points.
- Subject line is a genuine change of pace — curiosity-driven rather than another literal "Labor Day Sale" restatement.
- No render bugs — everything is crisp, aligned, and legible.

## What's weak

- "UP TO 50% OFF" still has zero product or category anchor — same vague ceiling-only claim as every prior send this stretch.
- The email crams in a credit-card pitch (Double Rewards), an "800+ New Arrivals" teaser, and app/loyalty footer blocks — the sale message gets diluted by three competing CTAs.
- "THE LABOR DAY SALE" hero treatment (bold serif stack, white pill button) is visually identical in structure to the last several sends, just with new furniture — brand voice is consistent but creatively fatigued.
- Primary CTA is still the generic "SHOP THE SALE" — doesn't tell you what's actually discounted.
- Preview text is missing/not distinguishable from subject in this render, so subject has to do all the work alone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Anchor the offer to something concrete — swap "UP TO 50% OFF" for a specific category or price point (e.g., "Sofas from $899" or "40% off Rugs") tied to the actual hero chairs shown.
- 2. Cut the email down to one primary ask. Move the Double Rewards credit-card push and "800+ New Arrivals" to a follow-up send rather than stacking three offers in one email.
- 3. Make the CTA copy match the subject's promise — since the subject teases "they're finally on sale," the button should say something like "SHOP THE CHAIRS" or name the featured piece, not generic "SHOP THE SALE."
- 4. Test a preview line that plays off the subject's tease instead of leaving it blank/redundant.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The chairs you've been eyeing? On sale.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Finally: your wishlist, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Floral armchairs, velvet sofas, and more — Labor Day pricing ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The pieces you saved are finally marked down.`

- 10 Dotomi ad-tracking pixels load over **insecure HTTP**, all under the same cookie-redirect pattern:
