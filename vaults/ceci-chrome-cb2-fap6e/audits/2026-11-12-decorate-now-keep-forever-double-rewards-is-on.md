# DECORATE NOW. KEEP FOREVER. + Double rewards is on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal merchandising email wrapped in a sale shell, and it doesn't commit to either. The top is a "LONG WEEKEND SALE" countdown (00 : 06 : 23 : 24) with a "SHOP THE SALE" button. Directly beneath it, the hero is "THE NEW COLLECTIBLES: Decorate now. Keep forever. They're pretty holiday things with heirloom energy." The countdown promises a sale, but the hero sells full-price holiday objects and never states a discount. The "up to 50% off" from the earlier sends isn't visible anywhere.
- The strongest part is the editorial layer. The moody photography, the product grid (coco candle holder, brambling bird ornament, Rolex coffee table book, Emporia dishes) and the large single-product tiles (flocked Christmas tree, acrylic nutcracker, mirrored orbs, toy soldier, crystal trees) look good and fit the brand. The "Double Rewards" block (the "10%" is struck through to "20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS") is the only concrete offer, and it sits well down the scroll.
- Judgement: polished but unfocused, and sent into a week of near-identical CB2 emails. It scores a solid 7 on targeting and execution but only a middling open and click case. The subject is half creative-line, half rewards tag, which matches the muddled body.

## What's working

- The photography is cohesive and premium, and the holiday styling matches the season.
- The countdown timer (about 6 hours left) gives the sale credible urgency, as the Nov 11 "FINAL HOURS" send did.
- The "Double Rewards" block states a concrete benefit: 20% back in reward dollars, with a "Learn more" button.
- The Thanksgiving delivery-cutoff strip (11/17, 11/19, 11/23, 11/24) is useful and time-bounded.
- Every product tile is named and linked, so a click lands on a specific item rather than the homepage.

## What's weak

- The sale and the hero disagree. The header says "Long Weekend Sale", but no percentage or discount appears next to the hero or the products.
- The email has no prices anywhere, so there's nothing to anchor a purchase decision on.
- It's very long. Five full-width single-product tiles follow the four-item grid, and the rewards offer, swatches and app promo are all far below the fold.
- Calls to action are generic and small: "shop now" under each tile, plus "SHOP HOLIDAY" and "SHOP THE SALE". It's not clear which is primary.
- Cadence is heavy. This follows the Nov 11 "FINAL HOURS" and "You have a party to plan" sends, and the sale originally ended Nov 8. It reads as the sixth push of one campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer in the hero. Say "Up to 50% off" (or whatever is actually live) on the collectibles image, and tie the countdown to that exact message.
- 2. Cut the single-product tiles from five to two or three. Move the Double Rewards block up directly after the hero.
- 3. Add prices or "from $X" to the product grid and tiles.
- 4. Make one dominant CTA, such as "Shop holiday decor", and demote the rest.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier sends this week, or suppress this send for people who already clicked one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has real accessibility and image-handling problems: 27 images have no alt text, 11 third-party pixels load over plain `http://`, and the viewport meta blocks zoom. Compliance, authentication and UTM checks could not be confirmed, because the headers were not captured and the source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains, so destination status and UTM parameters are unverified.
- **11 Dotomi pixels load over `http://`.** These are `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - Non-HTTPS images are blocked or proxied by many clients.
  - They look like cookie-sync pixels, so they probably won't fire and add 11 third-party requests for no benefit.
- **Two first-party open pixels have no alt text.** They are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=<hash>...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3463a682-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. Neither is a link bug.
- **Some font URLs contain a double slash.** Examples are `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2` and `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/GTSectra/...`. Whether they resolve was not tested.
- **Fonts load from another domain.** The `HNW05` `@font-face` rules point to `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different domain from the sender.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **27 images have no alt text.**
  - 14 are content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, from `04f94acf-...jpg` to `14d3f4a8-...jpg`.
  - 11 are the Dotomi pixels listed above.
  - 2 are the `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` tracking pixels.
  - The 14 content images matter most. If images are blocked, those sections are blank.
  - Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`.
- **The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`.** It disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`<meta name="format-detection">` is set for date, address and telephone.** These are set to `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`. This is fine.
- **A template placeholder was left in the `<head>`.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a cosmetic leftover from the template.
- **The `<title>` is generic.** It reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME" and does not match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Outlook handling is in place.** The source includes the MSO conditionals, `o:OfficeDocumentSettings`, and `mso-table-lspace`.
- **The source is truncated.** Anything in the body, such as table structure and role attributes, is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens are visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u=` hash in the open pixel is a hashed recipient identifier, not a broken token.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three of these are tool warnings, not confirmed defects:
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click support is therefore unverified.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.

The unsubscribe link and the physical address were not located in the truncated source, so they could not be checked. Verify the headers against the raw message from the original recipient mailbox.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. Landing URLs are hidden behind 66 skipped redirect links, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverified. Resolve the redirects with a HEAD or GET follow to confirm them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 14 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Drop or move the 11 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS. Better still, remove them if they are only cookie-sync calls.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Fix the `//Styles/` double-slash font URLs and check that they return 200.
5. Pull the raw message headers directly. This confirms `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
6. Follow the 66 redirect links with a resolver and check the final URLs for UTM parameters.
7. Strip the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` template comment before send.
