# 2 SALES END TOMORROW
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest send in the recent run. It's honest and urgent: "ENDS TOMORROW" sits above "WAREHOUSE SALE · UP TO 60% OFF" with a white "SHOP THE SALE" button, all in the first screen. The subject says "2 SALES" and the body shows two stacked sale heroes, Warehouse and Guest Ready (up to 40% off), so the promise is kept. That's an improvement on the 11/03 "BLACK FRIDAY HEAD START" send, which promised one thing and delivered another.
- The email loses focus below the two sale blocks. A Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz sweepstakes, a rugs module, an app promo, a rewards promo, a gift-list promo and a texting promo all sit under it. The sweepstakes also pushes SMS signup at the people who just clicked to shop. The offers are real, but the second half reads like a brand-wide footer. The fine print is dense and the exclusions are heavy, though the "up to" claims are visibly footnoted.
- Verdict: a solid deadline email that works because it's short on the offer and honest about the clock. It would be better if it stopped after the two sale blocks.

## What's working

- "ENDS TOMORROW" over a big "UP TO 60% OFF" is a clear, credible deadline with a visible button.
- Two sales are shown in the same visual language, so the subject's "2 SALES" is paid off.
- The dark, moody hero has strong contrast and a clear hierarchy, so the eye lands on 60% first.
- The Guest Ready block has a witty line, "It's a five-star affair," that fits CB2's voice.
- The rugs module gives a product-category path for people who want something specific.

## What's weak

- The two sale heroes have no product, only mood photography, so there's nothing concrete to click.
- The Casa Dean sweepstakes and SMS signup come in the middle and compete with the sale for attention.
- Four small promos (app, 10% back, gift list, 15% off texts) dilute an email that is supposed to be about urgency.
- The Guest Ready "SHOP THE SALE" is an underlined text link, much weaker than the Warehouse button.
- The legal footer is long and tiny, and the exclusions are hard to read.
- There's no review or social proof anywhere.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the rugs block, or at least the app, gift-list and SMS modules, and let the two sales run alone. A deadline email should have no side quests.
- 2. Show three or four priced products inside each sale, such as a sofa "now $X", so the percentage has something behind it.
- 3. Make the Guest Ready CTA a button that matches the Warehouse one, and name the end date and time ("Ends 11/4 at 11:59pm PT").
- 4. Move the sweepstakes to a separate send, or at least to the very bottom.
- 5. Keep the countdown tone, and say which sale ends first if the dates differ.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting and rugs, plus Guest Ready deals, until midnight tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook (MSO/VML) conditionals and a responsive breakpoint at 418px. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass rate, 3 warnings). The confirmed issues are 11 plain-HTTP Dotomi tracking images, missing alt text on several images, and a viewport/text-size configuration that blocks zooming. The HTML source is truncated, so the body, footer and links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 54 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified, not confirmed good.
- **Insecure tracking images:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) images load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  
  Clients and proxies that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop these, so those syncs will fail silently.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** `pub1`–`pub10` are ten cookie-sync calls to a third party, in addition to the Movable Ink pixels (`mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/8f98fd11-…`). This adds request overhead and a privacy and deliverability surface.
- **Cross-brand font hosts:** The `@font-face` rules for `HNW05` load from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`, while the others load from `www.cb2.com`. If either host changes, the fonts silently fall back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Malformed font URLs:** Some CB2 font URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (3):**
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/3a38b0fb-…png`
  - `…/e359ca05-…gif`
  - `…/5b8313e0-…jpg`
  
  If these carry content, the sale message is lost with images off or in a screen reader. Otherwise they need `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (14):** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` image have no alt attribute. These should have `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`, so screen readers don't announce a filename or URL.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` are set on `*` and `body`, which prevents user text scaling in Apple Mail and Outlook.
- **Template residue:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the head (and misspelled). It is not visible, but it shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Generic title:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` doesn't match the subject "2 SALES END TOMORROW". This is minor, but it is what the web-view and accessibility title announces.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` settings and `mso-line-height-rule:exactly` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the truncated HTML. The only per-recipient values seen are `mi_u=56aaa278…` and `mi_mid=…` on Movable Ink pixels, and both are populated. The rest of the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. The report itself says the AgentMail relay may be stripping them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw headers at the source.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** This header was not found, which is expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` are unverified. Do not draw conclusions from this run.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These are not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 54 click links went through tracking redirects and were not resolved, and the truncated source doesn't show the final URLs or any UTM parameters. Nothing here confirms or disproves that the "2 sales" land on matching sale pages with campaign attribution intact.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the HTTP-only Dotomi pixels (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/…`). Switch them to HTTPS, or remove the ten `pub*.php` syncs if they are not needed.
2. Add `alt` text to the three content images on `image.mail.cb2.com`, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`, Dotomi).
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay before treating them as defects.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, and with redirect resolution on the 54 links. That would let it check the footer unsubscribe link, the physical address, UTM parameters and landing pages.
6. Host all fonts on one domain (CB2's own), fix the `//` paths, and clean up the template comment.
