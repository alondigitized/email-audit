# WAREHOUSE SALE: UP TO 60% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led send. "UP TO 60% OFF" is the largest thing on the page, set over a warm, muted bedroom photo, and the subject matches the hero word for word. A shopper knows what the offer is within a second. It is also a near-repeat of the 2026-10-17 "THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK" send, which had the same hero and the same five buttons. Three days on, nothing new has been added.
- The hero works, but the rest of the email dilutes it. A $1,000 app-download sweepstakes takes a full second hero-sized block. Below it sit four feature tiles (app, 10% back, gift registry, 15% off texts), then a heavy legal block. The email runs out of things to say about the sale after the buttons. It never shows a product, a price or a sale deadline. The footnote says the sale "Ends November 4," but the body doesn't say so. The result reads like a category directory.
- Verdict: a clear, believable offer with a weak follow-through. The 2026-10-17 send was scored 7, and this one is slightly lower because it is a repeat with more clutter.

## What's working

- "UP TO 60% OFF" is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The subject and the hero agree, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- Five stacked buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs, Shop All) are legible and tap-friendly, and they route by category.
- The photo is calm and on-brand, and the dark type stays readable on it.
- The "last-chance markdowns" line adds some scarcity.

## What's weak

- No product, price or sale-end date appears in the body. The Nov 4 deadline is only in the fine print.
- The buttons are pale cream on a cream and beige photo. They are low contrast and look ghosted, and the label text is thin.
- The $1,000 app sweepstakes takes a second hero-sized block and competes with the sale for attention.
- Four secondary tiles (app, Rewards, registry, text signup) plus a long legal footer bury the sale.
- The email repeats the 2026-10-17 Warehouse Sale creative, and "WAREHOUSE SALE" has now followed "Make your coffee table a moment" and the Semi-Annual Dining Event in a crowded week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Nov 4", and show 3 to 4 priced products below the buttons, such as "Sofa $X, was $Y". Real prices would do the most for click-through.
- 2. Give the buttons a solid dark fill with white text so they stand out from the photo.
- 3. Cut the app sweepstakes, or move it below the footer tiles. Keep one secondary message at most.
- 4. Cut the four feature tiles to one Rewards line, and shorten the legal block.
- 5. Vary the creative between Warehouse Sale sends, for example a new hero room or a "final days" angle, so it doesn't look like a resend.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last-chance markdowns, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs and decor. Ends November 4.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Curated last-chance pieces, while they last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint. The QA data shows 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels and 7 content images with no alt text. Header-level checks (unsubscribe, SPF/DKIM) could not be verified because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML source is truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 58 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 third-party pixels load over `http://`, which many clients block (mixed content). These are the Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Cookie sync in email:** Ten sequential `pubN.php` cookieredir calls make up a cookie-sync chain. Most clients strip or proxy these pixels, so they are unreliable and add third-party requests.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0276f2a6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are served over HTTPS and look like open tracking.
- **Font URLs:**
  - Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash: `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`.
  - The `HNW05` font is pulled from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts, so these requests are likely wasted. They would only matter if the server rejects `//` paths.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 7 images served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`:
  - `69c963ab-…jpg`
  - `e40aedf1-…jpg`
  - `16721ae5-…jpg`
  - `bb2d7174-…jpg`
  - `113ebcbd-…jpg`
  - `fef911c0-…jpg`
  - `8e957a75-…gif`
  
  The image-heavy sale content is not available to screen readers or to clients with images off. Whether these images are wrapped in links is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel also lack alt text. These are decorative, so use `alt=""` (an empty alt) rather than omitting the attribute.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on both `*` and `body`, and `*{line-height:100%}` is set globally. This blocks user text scaling, and 100% line-height can clip wrapped text.
- **Template leftover:** The source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, the charset is declared and the VML/MSO namespaces are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The rest of the body was truncated, so this is not confirmed.
- The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8` parameter on the `mi.cb2.com` pixel is a 64-hex value, probably a hashed subscriber identifier. This is expected for tracking and not a defect.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** Not found. The QA check says the AgentMail relay may not capture it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found. One-click unsubscribe is therefore unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain is `mail.cb2.com`.
- **Body-level items:** The visible unsubscribe link, physical postal address and sender identification could not be checked in the truncated source.
- These are warnings caused by the relay, not confirmed sender defects. Verify them against the raw original message.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 58 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't show their destination URLs. UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and the offer match to the "UP TO 60% OFF" warehouse sale are unverified.
- The tracked links and pixels carry `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` identifiers (`mi_cid=678efa99bfad4da1`). Whether those identifiers survive the redirect to cb2.com could not be tested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the QA against the raw message, or a copy with headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images. Use `alt=""` on all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
3. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or remove them. The `pubN.php` chain adds third-party requests and is mostly blocked anyway.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*` and `body`, and use `100%` instead.
5. Resolve the 58 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to check final status codes and UTM/ID carry-through.
6. Fix the `cb2.com//Styles` double-slash font URLs, and remove the `crateandbarrel.com` font references if they aren't needed.
7. Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` template comment.
