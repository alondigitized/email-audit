- The "up to 50% off" banner is small, text-only and above the fold line. The hero is the product, so the eye never lands on the offer first.
- No deadline or end date is visible for the Semi-Annual Event, so there is no urgency.

- The footer is a dense wall of legal text, and the app and The DesignDesk modules after the products distract from the Bodie push.
- Subject and banner tell two different stories (collection vs. event), and the brand has sent a sale or collection email almost daily (Oct 3 sofa sale, Oct 4 markdowns, coffee tech, holiday guide, Oct 5 sofa review quote).

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold/Cheetah-style table template with XHTML 1.0 Transitional and VML/MSO conditionals. The QA run flagged no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). Header-level checks were inconclusive, and about 40 images have no `alt` attribute. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 82 links go through tracking/click-redirect domains, and the HTTP probe skipped all of them. Destination validity, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unconfirmed.
- **Redundant pixels:** the same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` pixel appears 6 times. `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp) appears 5 times, with `n=1` to `n=5`. Repeated tracking pixels add requests and give no extra signal.
- **Identifier in image URLs:** the `mi_u=` and `s=` values are the same per-recipient token. They are passed to a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`) in an image URL.
- **Open pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2277c0de-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open/view trackers. Both lack `alt`, which is acceptable for pixels, but they should carry `alt=""` explicitly.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** about 28 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg/gif/png) have no `alt`. Examples are `eeb8adae-5b4e-4a30-bd6d-f75109be3722.jpg` and `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. If these are product or hero images, they will render blank with images off, and screen readers will read the filenames.
- **Spacer images without alt:** the two `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/…Spacer_40px…` images and the tracking pixels should use `alt=""`. The check is not distinguishing absent `alt` from an intentional empty one.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling. They also conflict with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` rule.
- **Global line-height:** `*{line-height:100%}` applies to every element. That is tight for body copy and not a reliable reset.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice (http-equiv and charset).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the output.
  - The MSO block contains an empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }`.
- **Responsive breakpoints:** the mobile rules apply only at `max-width:460px`. Between 461px and 639px, no `img max-width` rule applies, so images can overflow at those widths.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the available source. The subject has no tokens either.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, but its alignment cannot be assessed from the data.
- **Body footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source. Neither could be confirmed or ruled out.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 82 click links were wrapped by the tracking redirect and not resolved, and the visible `href` values in the truncated source don't expose UTM parameters or final landing URLs. The tracking identifiers (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`) show a click-tracking system is in use, but whether campaign parameters carry through to crateandbarrel.com is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to every content image on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Use descriptive text for products and heroes, and `alt=""` for spacers and pixels (Scene7 `Spacer_40px`, `rlcdn`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/…`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules and keep `100%`.
3. Cut the duplicate tracking pixels: 6 `rp` pixels down to 1, and 5 `rlcdn` pixels down to 1 if each isn't needed per section.
4. Fix the markup hygiene issues:
   - Escape the title as `&amp;`.
   - Remove the duplicate meta tags.
   - Delete the leftover template comment and the empty MSO rule.
5. Add an `img` max-width rule covering 461–639px.
6. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated message with the original headers (not through the relay). This should confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe and postal address, and the UTMs on resolved landing URLs.
7. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm that destinations return 200 and that UTMs are preserved.
