## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The automated QA found no hard failures, but it flagged widespread missing alt text, including on content images, and a viewport and text-size setup that limits accessibility. Header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified. The provided HTML is truncated, so the footer and most links were not visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate LiveRamp (rlcdn.com) pixels. They add third-party requests and per-recipient data sharing, and they will be blocked by image-blocking and privacy clients.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` (repeated six times) and `o.gif?mi_u=…` carry a recipient-level `mi_u` value in the query string. These are a first-party tracking and personalization mechanism. They are not defects, but the same identifier appears in the LiveRamp `s=` value.
- **Duplicate beacon:** the `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a49b2c19-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` image is another open-tracking pixel in addition to `o.gif`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA reports about 34 images without alt text. The ones that matter are content images:
  - The 12 Scene7 slices `202504%5FCB%5FEntTurnaround%5FWine%5FSlice1…Slice12` (hero, tour, recipe headlines, final CTAs).
  - The `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/…/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` assets.
  - If the slices contain text or CTAs and have no alt, image-blocked and screen-reader users lose that content.
- **Tracking pixels:** the `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif` and `o.gif` entries are pixels. They should have `alt=""` and are lower priority. The QA does not distinguish `alt=""` from a missing attribute, so I can't tell which they have.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on iOS and some Android clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text sizing:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and the `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none }` rule turn off text-size scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Line height:** `*{line-height:100%}` applies to every element, which gives tight line spacing for any element that lacks its own override.
- **Markup validity:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed and duplicates the `http-equiv` charset.
  - The `format-detection` meta tag appears three times with overlapping values.
  - These are minor and unlikely to break rendering.
- **Template leftovers:** the source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-- --><!--<![endif]-->` blocks. They are harmless but show the template wasn't cleaned.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated, not placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found. The QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a sender defect. Without the raw headers, this can't be confirmed as a real issue. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) for high-volume senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Nothing here shows a failure.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and postal address were not visible because the HTML was truncated. I can't confirm either way.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 66 tracking links were skipped, and the truncated HTML doesn't show final URLs. UTM parameters and landing page alignment could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text to the content images**, especially the 12 `202504_CB_EntTurnaround_Wine_Slice*` images and the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` assets. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag. Consider dropping `text-size-adjust:none` and the universal `line-height:100%`.
3. **Re-run the audit with the raw message headers** (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results). That would settle the compliance and authentication questions, since the relay is the likely cause of the missing headers.
4. **Re-run with the full HTML** to check the footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) and resolve the tracking links, so the UTM and landing-page checks can run.
5. **Low priority:** escape the `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate meta tags and the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and consider consolidating the five LiveRamp pixels.
