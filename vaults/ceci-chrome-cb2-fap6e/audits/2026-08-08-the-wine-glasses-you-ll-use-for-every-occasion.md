# The wine glasses you'll use for every occasion →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel doing a clean, single-category product deep-dive — wine glasses, and only wine glasses, for the first six sections before it pivots into unrelated cross-sell. "Our bestselling wine glass ever," "oversized and so hip," "acrylic wine glasses just dropped," and "starting at $2.95" — that's four distinct wine-glass hooks stacked back to back with genuinely good lifestyle photography (the toast shot, the pour shot, the acrylic-glass-poolside shot). It's focused and well-photographed in a way the coffee-table and backpack sends from this brand weren't.
- Where it falls apart is length and drift: after the wine section ends, the email keeps going into amaro spritz/ginger-thyme cocktails, a bakery collab, back-to-school gear, a $5000 Design Desk sweepstakes, and a "we love these for you" grid — none of which reinforces the subject line. A subscriber who opens for wine glasses gets a genuinely strong first half and then a kitchen-sink second half that dilutes it.
- Solid, on-brand send that earns a mid-to-upper score for the wine-glass sections specifically, dragged down by scope creep past the fold.

## What's working

- Subject and hero visual are perfectly aligned — hero image is literally wine glasses with a clear "Shop Durable Wine Glasses" CTA.
- Multiple concrete price/value hooks: "starting at $2.95," "about 350 4-star reviews," each paired with its own "Shop Now" button.
- Photography is genuinely strong — the toast-in-a-vineyard shot and the amber-liquid pour shot both look premium and on-brand.
- Clear visual rhythm: hero image → headline → short copy → CTA, repeated consistently across all four wine-glass sections.
- "These go great in a wine glass, too" is a smart, low-friction bridge from glassware into spritz/cocktail ingredients.

## What's weak

- The email drifts hard past the wine section — bakery collab, back-to-school, a sweepstakes, and an app-download banner all crowd out the stated subject.
- No visible price/discount on most wine glass sections beyond the one "$2.95" callout — feels like a product showcase, not an "occasion" or offer email despite the subject's promise.
- "we love these for you" personalization grid at the bottom is generic and disconnected from the wine theme, undercutting the focused opening.
- Preview text ("11 Reasons to Love Your Wine Glass collection") reads like a stray internal label rather than real marketing copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the wine-glass sections (or at "these go great in a wine glass, too") — end on the cocktail-ingredient tie-in and drop bakery/back-to-school/sweepstakes entirely; it's a different email.
- 2. Add a visible discount or bundle price on at least one hero section — "starting at $2.95" is buried as the fourth section, not the lead.
- 3. Fix the preview text — it currently reads as a broken/leftover string, not copy written for the inbox.
- 4. Consider trimming to 2-3 wine-glass styles instead of 4+ near-identical "Shop Now" blocks to reduce scroll fatigue.
