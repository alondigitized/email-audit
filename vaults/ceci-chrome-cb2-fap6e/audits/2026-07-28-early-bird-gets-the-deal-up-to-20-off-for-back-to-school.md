# Early bird gets the deal: Up to 20% off for back-to-school
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running its third back-to-school send in four days, and it's showing the seams. The top third does the job — a clean hero banner ("back-to-school sale: up to 20% off"), a tidy six-tile grid of monogrammed backpacks, lunch boxes, and pencil cases, then a "create a focus zone" desk/chair vignette. That's a coherent, on-persona pitch through the giveaway block.
- Then it falls apart. Past the fall-arrivals rug banner, the "we love these for you" recommendation grid pivots hard into baby gear — a Nuna PIPA infant car seat, a nursery glider recliner, a baby dresser — none of which belongs in a back-to-school email for a school-age-kids shopper. That's the same kitchen-sink pattern flagged in "Up to 20% off our Back-to-School Sale!" (7.00/10, 2026-07-26) and "Can't wait to get home?" (9.00/10, 2026-07-27), except this time the mismatch isn't just more categories crammed in — it's actively off-persona content that suggests a broken or stale recommendation feed.
- Net: solid top-of-funnel, undermined by an email that can't stop selling everything else in the catalog. Worth clicking through the top half; ignorable after that.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unambiguous: single offer ("up to 20% off"), single CTA ("Shop the Sale"), clean product photography with monogrammed items that match the back-to-school theme.
- The category grid (backpacks, lunch boxes, duffel bags, pencil cases, food storage, water bottles) is well-organized and stays on-topic.
- "Create a focus zone" desk/chair section is a smart adjacent sell — still school-related, not a random detour.
- No visible render bugs — images loaded, text isn't overlapping, layout is clean end to end.

## What's weak

- The "we love these for you" grid recommends infant car seats, a nursery glider, and a kids dresser in a back-to-school email — a jarring persona mismatch that reads like a broken recs engine.
- Two competing offers stacked at the very top ("up to 50% off summer sale" ribbon vs. "up to 20% off" hero) muddy which discount actually applies.
- Primary CTA copy ("Shop the Sale") is generic — it doesn't point to a specific product or category despite the email having several distinct ones.
- The email keeps going long after the school-supplies pitch is made: giveaway, fall arrivals, credit card pitch, app download, in-home design service — six more asks after the core offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "we love these for you" block or replace it with actual back-to-school-relevant items — showing a car seat and nursery glider here is the single biggest miss and looks like a targeting error.
- 2. Resolve the 50% vs. 20% conflict at the top — pick one headline offer for this send instead of stacking two percentages above the fold.
- 3. Swap "Shop the Sale" for a specific CTA tied to the hero image, e.g. "Shop Lunch & Storage."
- 4. Trim the send to the top three sections (hero, category grid, focus zone) and move the giveaway/credit card/app/design-service pitches to a separate email — this is the fourth kitchen-sink send this week and it's diluting a genuinely good school-supplies pitch.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Monogrammed backpacks, lunch boxes & pencil cases — all on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stock the backpack. Skip the stress. Up to 20% off through [date].`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 30 image references are missing `alt` text, spanning both content images (product/hero images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/...`) and tracking pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). No merge tokens (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{first_name}}`) or unresolved personalization fields are visible in the provided source. No issues found based on available evidence.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome): not detected — same relay-capture caveat. Authentication status is unknown from this scan and cannot be confirmed as pass or fail.
- No visible in-body unsubscribe link/footer text was included in the truncated source, so footer-level CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link text) could not be assessed — recommend reviewing full source/footer directly.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The truncated HTML provided does not include visible `<a href>` CTA destinations, so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page alignment could not be evaluated. The only URL parameters visible belong to tracking infrastructure (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`, `cp_tp=v`, `s=`, `n=`), not UTM/campaign tagging. Recommend re-running the audit against the full (non-truncated) source to evaluate this category.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm directly with the ESP/send log (not just the relay-captured headers) whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are actually present on the raw outbound message — if genuinely missing, add both for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.

3. Remove the leftover template placeholder comment (`<!--[IMPUT HERE...]-->`) from the production send template.
4. Re-audit with full (untruncated) HTML to verify CTA `href` values for UTM consistency and to confirm footer/CAN-SPAM elements (physical address, unsubscribe link).
