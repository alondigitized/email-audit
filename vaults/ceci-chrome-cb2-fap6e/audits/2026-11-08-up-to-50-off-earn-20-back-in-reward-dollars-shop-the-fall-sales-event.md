# Up to 50% off + earn 20% back in Reward Dollars! Shop The Fall Sales Event →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth Double Rewards push in about two days, after "Earn 2x Rewards & get up to 50% off!" and "Get it fast AND earn Double Rewards? 🤯" earlier today. The hero is clear. "The Fall Sales Event up to 50% off" sits over a dark red textured image with one "Shop Now" button, and a red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner sits above it. The top third lands the offer fast.
- Below the hero, the email sprawls into a catalog. It has a tabletop and furniture grid, a sofa row with reward amounts, a Thanksgiving hosting guide, a gift shop, a second Double Rewards block, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app download, DesignDesk, and a store locator. Each module is competent, but together they dilute a single message, and the repeated Rewards block reads as filler.
- Judgement: a strong, honest top of email followed by a long, repetitive tail. It is a solid 6-7 send, but the cadence fatigue is real. A subscriber who got three near-identical Rewards emails this weekend has little reason to open another.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unmistakable: "up to 50% off" in large serif type with a single "Shop Now" button.
- The sofa row shows concrete reward math, like "Earn $209 $400 Reward Dollars*", which makes the loyalty benefit tangible.
- The deadline "Nov. 17, 2026" is specific and credible.
- Three clearly labeled category tiles (dinnerware, furniture & decor, clearance) give shoppers quick entry points.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real prices.

## What's weak

- The "last Double Rewards this year" block appears at the top and again mid-email, which is redundant.
- The email is very long, with at least ten modules. The gift shop, DesignDesk, app promo and Thanksgiving guide each pull in a different direction.
- The Thanksgiving Hosting Guide banner has overlapping text. The headline is partly cut off by the image, and it reads like "he Thanksgiving Hosting Guide".
- The fine print is dense and stacked in several places, including a block mid-email, which weakens the "honest offer" feel.
- The subject carries three ideas (50% off, 20% back, Shop the Fall Sales Event) and is far too long for a mobile inbox.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one category row, one product grid and a single Rewards reminder. Drop the gift shop, app and DesignDesk modules from this send.
- 2. Fix the Thanksgiving banner so the headline is fully legible, or remove it.
- 3. Space promotional sends out. Consolidate the Double Rewards messaging into one send per day at most.
- 4. Move the fine print to a single footer block.
- 5. Shorten the subject to one offer plus one deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + 20% back, ends Nov 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year: 20% back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dinnerware, sofas and decor, all earning Reward Dollars through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Fall Sales Event: save up to 60% on clearance and earn 20% back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed issues are about 32 images flagged for missing alt text, a few markup defects, and unverified header and link data. The source is truncated and the 70 tracking links were not probed, so the footer, CTAs and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **70 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so redirect targets, status codes and destination alignment are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five repeated third-party pixels.** The source has `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…cfd8&n=1` through `&n=5`. This is the LiveRamp domain, and the same `n` series is repeated five times.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels has the same prefix (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964`) as the `mi_u=` parameter on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. That suggests a per-recipient hashed ID is being passed to a third-party domain. Confirm the privacy-policy and consent basis for that.
- **Tracking pixels without alt text.** `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/9c70ae59-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and measurement pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"`. The QA output doesn't show whether they do.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 32 images flagged for missing alt text.**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `6f9ddb32-…jpg`, `6100bbef-…jpg` and the two animated `.gif` files (`70061691-…`, `24335f96-…`).
  - The Scene7 asset `2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary-…`.
  - Product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offers and product modules are unreadable.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate and redundant meta tags.** There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv Content-Type` and `<meta charset>`). `format-detection` appears three times with overlapping values.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is shipped in the source.
- **Global resets.**
  - `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` centers every paragraph by default.
  - `*{… line-height:100%}` applies to everything.
  - Both can override intended styling unless every element sets its own.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- `lang="en"` is present, and the VML and Office namespaces are declared correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appeared in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The remainder of the body was not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool itself says these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. That makes them unconfirmed, not confirmed missing. Check the raw `.eml` from the original delivery path.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown, again most likely a relay artifact.
- **The footer was not in the truncated source.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address couldn't be verified.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image domain `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so there is no obvious domain misalignment in the visible content.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The visible source has no CTA `href`s, and the 70 tracking links weren't resolved. No UTM parameters are visible, and the tracked image URLs use `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` and `mi_u` instead. I can't tell whether the destination URLs carry campaign parameters or whether the landing pages match the "50% off / 20% back in Reward Dollars" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image: the hero, the Thanksgiving module, the product tiles and the animated GIFs. Give tracking pixels `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. Deduplicate the `meta` tags and delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
5. Reorder or merge the `img` max-width media queries so the intended cap applies.
6. Review the privacy disclosure for the hashed recipient ID appearing in both the `mi_u=` URLs and the five LiveRamp pixels.
7. Re-run QA against the raw message to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Resolve the 70 tracking redirects so destinations, UTMs and landing-page alignment can be checked.
