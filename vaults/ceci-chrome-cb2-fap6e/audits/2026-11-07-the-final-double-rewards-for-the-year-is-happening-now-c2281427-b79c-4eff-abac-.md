## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) dynamic-content layer. Automated QA found no hard failures, but about 40 images lack alt text. The header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay did not capture the headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 79 links are on tracking or click-redirect domains and were skipped by the HTTP probe. Final destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2…&n=1` through `&n=5` calls (LiveRamp) load from one email. Each carries a recipient-specific `s=` hash. Each of the five also lacks alt text.
- **Other pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/35030ce3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and dynamic-content trackers. The `dv.` URL has no file extension and no alt text.
- **Truncated parameter:** The `mi_u` value in the `o.gif` URL appears shorter than the same token in the `/p/rp/` URLs (`…bb941bb6406869` against `…bb941bb640686964`). This is probably an artifact of QA output truncation, not a defect. Check it in the raw source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** Roughly 40 images have no alt attribute. Most are content images (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, plus `bbf6f9d4-…png` under `/m/47/`). With images blocked, those blocks render empty. Alt text should be added to meaningful images.
- **Dynamic images:** Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png` and others) have no alt text. They look like personalized modules.
- **Tracking pixels:** The `rlcdn` GIFs, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. A missing attribute is treated as an error by screen readers.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` stops pinch-zoom on mobile. That is a WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off text resizing in WebKit and Outlook.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` does not reflect the subject. The `&` is also unescaped, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - Two `charset` declarations (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - Redundant `format-detection` meta tags (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`).
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under an XHTML doctype.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, ships in production.
- **Conflicting CSS:** `img {max-width: 600px}` at `min-width: 768px` and `max-width: 640px` at `min-width: 640px` overlap. The result depends on source order.
- **Responsive and dark mode:** The `@media (max-width:460px)` breakpoints are present. `color-scheme: light` is forced, so there is no dark-mode adaptation.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` are visible in the available source. Per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, the `rlcdn` `s=` hash) are resolved. The remaining source was truncated, so a full token scan was not possible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`:** Not found. QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is a Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirement.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Alignment can't be verified from this data.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.
- **Privacy:** The `rlcdn.com` pixels pass a hashed recipient identifier to a third-party data provider. The disclosure and consent basis for that can't be verified from the email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. All 79 links resolve through tracking redirects that were not followed, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs. There is no evidence of a problem and none of success.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the audit with the full HTML and the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`). Resolve the tracking redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the roughly 40 content images and the `mi.` dynamic images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
4. Make the `<title>` match the subject line, escape the `&`, and remove the duplicate meta tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Confirm that the 5× `rlcdn` pixel calls are intentional. Consolidate them if possible and confirm the privacy disclosure.
6. Resolve the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
