# Our 15 top gifts for everyone on your list →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a long gift-guide catalog, about 12,000px tall, with a good top and a diluted middle. The "The Gift Shop" script header, the "Shop the Gift Guide" button and the four-tile row (bestselling, by price, personalized, top kitchen) give a clear way in. The tiles are useful, and gift-by-price is the one most shoppers will want.
- The rest is a numbered "top 15 curated gifts" run. It gets a strong Breville espresso machine hero, then pairs of small numbered products, themed bands (frames, pretzels, snowflake) and a Le Creuset Dutch oven. It is well produced but long, and every band has its own "Shop Now" button. The ending drifts into a Crate & Barrel Kids block and a Design Desk $5,000 giveaway, which have nothing to do with the "gifts for everyone on your list" promise. The "ends today! accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" banner at the very top belongs to a different campaign, so the only visible offer competes with the gift story.
- It repeats the 11-04 "Made for wish lists" send, which was also a long age-sectioned catalog, and this is the fourth Crate & Barrel email in one day. That cadence works against it. My verdict is a competent but unfocused send that would have performed better at half the length. Note that the screenshot was downscaled, so I read the layout and large headings but not the fine print.

## What's working

- The "Shop the Gift Guide" button and the four category tiles sit near the top and give a clear route into the guide.
- The Breville espresso machine hero is the strongest single product moment, and the numbered "top 15" framing gives the guide a clear structure.
- The photography is warm and holiday-appropriate, with consistent styling and no visible broken images or overlaps.
- Giving the espresso machine its own full-width hero before the smaller pairs creates some hierarchy.
- Exclusive and "Shop Free" style tags on products add a sense of scarcity and value.

## What's weak

- The "ends today! accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" banner is the only offer, and it has nothing to do with gifts. It splits the message in the first screen.
- The top hero is a marble checkerboard image with no offer or headline, so the eye doesn't land on a reason to act.
- The email is about 12,000px long with a repeated "Shop Now" button per band. Most readers will not reach the lower products.
- The Kids brand block and the Design Desk $5,000 giveaway at the bottom dilute the "gifts for everyone" message.
- It is the fourth Crate & Barrel send on 11-04, following the sale email "ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off accent chairs, tables & rugs!" and two catalog-style emails. The cadence feels heavy.

- 1. Cut it to the Gift Shop header, the four category tiles and at most six products, and drop the themed bands. This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Either remove the accent-chair banner or make it a gift offer. If the sale stays, say so in the subject line.
- 3. Move the Design Desk giveaway and the Kids block into their own sends.
- 4. Put a price on products, or at least a "from $" figure, so people can scan gifts by budget.
- 5. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific ones such as "Shop espresso machines" or "Shop gifts under $50".

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Our 15 top gifts for everyone on your list →"

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template with MSO conditionals, and the automated QA run has 0 hard failures. The main confirmed issues are about 45 `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the relay didn't expose the headers. The HTML source is also truncated, so the footer and most links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation not performed:** 81 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe (QA INFO). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Subscriber-identifying parameter in pixels:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. It looks like a per-recipient hash. Anyone who forwards the email shares that token.
- **Repeated third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` is the same LiveRamp pixel fired five times with an incrementing `n`.
- **Tracking pixels:** `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3a5c3660-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is the open pixel.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` attributes:** QA flagged about 45 images. They fall into three groups:
  - **Content images (about 38):**
    - Hero GIF: `…2026%5F1104%5FCB%5FGiftGuide%5Fhero%5FV2%2Dezgif…`.
    - About 28 gift-tile images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`.
    - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
    - `…2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary…`.
    - 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. These look like dynamic recommendation images, but the source doesn't confirm that.
    - If the images are not wrapped in text links, a screen reader or image-blocked client gets nothing for them. Because "missing" means the attribute is absent, screen readers may read out the filename or URL instead.
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** the 5 `rlcdn.com` GIFs, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel. These need `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`, not descriptive text, so the flag is low severity.
  - **The fix is the same either way:** every `<img>` needs an `alt`, either descriptive or an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing suppressed:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `*{}` rule and in `html{}`/`body{}`. This prevents user text scaling on iOS.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at ≥768px and the later one wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Minor markup issues:**
  - Duplicate `format-detection` and charset metas.
  - Unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`.
  - Generic title with no subject context.
  - Unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">` under an XHTML doctype.
  - These are harmless in practice.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, and MSO and VML namespaces and the Outlook `OfficeDocumentSettings` are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values are populated. Coverage is limited to the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` missing:** QA WARN, "Header may not be captured by AgentMail relay".
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` missing:** RFC 8058 one-click is not confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Not confirmed as defects:** QA attributes all three to the relay, so they are measurement gaps, not confirmed sender failures. Verify against the raw message headers.
- **Footer not inspected:** the footer is past the truncation point. The visible body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are unverified.
- **Alignment:** the From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and link subdomains, which is consistent with a properly aligned sending setup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 81 links are redirect-wrapped and skipped, and the truncated source doesn't expose their destination URLs or any `utm_*` parameters. The tracking is a mix of the `mi.` subdomain (click and pixel) and the `dv.` open pixel, but no landing-page alignment can be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to every `<img>`. Use descriptive text on the hero, the gift tiles and the `/p/rp/` product images. Use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` on the `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.` pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
3. Delete the conflicting `min-width:768px` `img` rule, or reorder it so the intended cap applies.
4. Dedupe the five `rlcdn.com` pixel fires to one.
5. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers to settle `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the redirect-wrapped links through a tracker-aware probe to confirm final URLs, status codes and UTM params. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer.
