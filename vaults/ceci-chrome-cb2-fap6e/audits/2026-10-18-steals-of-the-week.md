# STEALS OF THE WEEK
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a roundup of recycled promos presented as "steals." The header says "THIS WEEK'S STEALS," but nothing in the body is new. It stacks the Semi-Annual Dining Event (up to 30% off) and the Warehouse Sale (up to 60% off). Those are the same two offers CB2 sent on Oct 15 ("ON SALE: DINNER PARTY MUST-HAVES") and Oct 17 ("THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK"). The subscriber has already seen both heroes this week.
- The render is clean and the hierarchy is clear. Each offer is a big light-weight headline over a moody photo, with an underlined text link. But I can't see a single named product, price or "steal" in the render. The "steals" promise in the header isn't delivered. After the two sales, the email adds an app-download sweepstakes ($1,000) and a Casa Dean text-signup giveaway ($5,000). That's four different asks in one send, and the offers compete with each other. The footer then adds an app tile, a 10% Rewards tile, a gift list tile and a 15% text-signup tile.
- Verdict: it's a fine reminder email but a weak "steals" email. It's the fourth or fifth sale-heavy send in about a week. It would work better as a single-offer send, or as real product-level deals.

## What's working

- The two sale heroes are legible. "UP TO 30% OFF" and "UP TO 60% OFF" are large and easy to read at a glance.
- The nav bar (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) sits at the top with the CB2 logo. It looks trusted and consistent with the brand.
- The photography is strong. The dark dining-room scene and the dark floral image match the moody CB2 look.
- The two sale sections use the same pattern: a headline, an offer and one underlined link. That makes them easy to scan.
- The footer tiles for the app, Rewards (10% back) and text signup (15% off) give loyal subscribers a clear next step.

## What's weak

- No actual "steals" appear. There are no named products, prices or markdowns, only category-wide "up to" ranges.
- The two lead offers repeat the Oct 15 and Oct 17 sends almost exactly.
- The "SHOP NOW" links are small, thin and underlined, so they are easy to miss as the primary CTA.
- Four calls to action compete: shop dining, shop the warehouse sale, download the app, and win via text. The sweepstakes blocks push the real offers down the page.
- The "DOWNLOAD THE APP, WIN $1,000" headline sits over a busy photo. The text overlaps the vase and lamp and reads less cleanly than the other heroes.
- The email is very long, and the footer fine print is dense and tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Deliver on the subject. Replace the two category heroes with a grid of 4 to 6 specific products showing the original price, the sale price and a "% off" tag. If it's called "Steals," show steals.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes blocks, or move the app and text giveaways to a separate send. Keep one hero offer and one secondary offer.
- 3. Make the CTAs buttons with specific copy, such as "Shop 60% off warehouse finds," instead of thin "SHOP NOW" links.
- 4. Add urgency. Put an end date on the offer ("Ends Sunday") so it differs from the earlier sends.
- 5. Space out the sale sends so the same two promos don't appear in three emails in one week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Warehouse finds up to 60% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `7 CB2 steals under $300 this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining tables, chairs and lighting, marked down for a few days only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our best warehouse picks, plus 30% off dining. Shop before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The CB2 "STEALS OF THE WEEK" email has one confirmed rendering bug: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads ten plaintext-HTTP Dotomi pixels and has several images with no alt text. The QA run passed 43% of checks. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified because AgentMail doesn't capture those headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 59 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are served over plain HTTP:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- Mail clients that proxy images or enforce HTTPS may block these requests, and they can trigger mixed-content warnings. Where they are blocked, third-party cookie syncing silently fails.
- The first-party tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/f5d2e712-...?mi_cid=...` use HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a 64-character hex string in the URL, which is probably a hashed recipient identifier.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Four content images have no alt text. They are all hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `448601c4-21bc-4e0f-ab34-fb8cdfdb21ad.jpg`
  - `87cf9fed-4100-4c2b-900c-ea319f7e3c27.jpg`
  - `8d1e358d-3934-4e2f-bd50-cbfede941d3c.jpg`
  - `5b8313e0-152c-424b-86c7-4fa8194ef02b.jpg`
- If images are blocked, those sections render blank. Screen readers will read out the file name or skip the image.
- The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, and the Dotomi `pub*.php` images) have no alt text. This is low impact for the pixels if they are 1x1 or hidden. Add `alt=""` to mark them decorative, and confirm they are hidden with `width="1" height="1"` or `display:none`.
- I could not check anything else in the markup. The HTML source was truncated in the CSS `img` rule, so the body structure, `role` attributes and image dimensions are unverified.
- The head has a placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless, but it shows template boilerplate was left in.
- Fonts load from two different domains: `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`. The `cb2.com` `@font-face` URLs have a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). This is cosmetic and could 404 or redirect depending on the server. Most email clients ignore web fonts, so the practical impact is small.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is present in the delivered email. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%…%%` personalization string that didn't resolve at send time. It is a dynamic product-reviews SKU list that was never populated.
- Depending on where it sits, it may be visible text, or it may be broken markup or an unresolved link or image. I can't see its position in the truncated source.
- No other unresolved tokens were detected.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check it against the raw message headers.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.cb2.com`, and the authentication result can't be assessed through this relay.
- The visible footer content (physical address, unsubscribe link) fell outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance. No issue is confirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Because the 59 tracked links were not probed, I can't verify UTM parameters or landing page alignment. Their final destinations and campaign parameters are unknown.
- The only parameters visible are the tracking pixel IDs: `mi_cid=678edda19a29eeaf` and `mi_mid=01a14f4f-eb00-7000-ba86-3168808...`. These identify the campaign and message in the tracking system, but they don't show how links are tagged.
- No issues are confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix the unresolved `%%reviews_skulist%%` token.** This is the only hard failure. Add a send-time check that blocks the send if any `%%…%%` string remains, and add a fallback for an empty SKU list.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Change `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com` to `https://`, or drop the 10 cookie-sync pixels if they are not needed.
3. **Add alt text to the four content images.** Use `alt=""` on every tracking pixel.
4. **Check the unsubscribe headers against raw headers.** Test with a direct inbox or seed account, not the AgentMail relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`.
5. **Resolve the 59 tracked links.** Follow the redirects to the final URLs and confirm UTM tagging and landing-page status codes.
6. **Clean up the template.** Remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` placeholder and the double slashes in the font URLs.
