# Up to 20% off your living room update
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The send is a clear upholstery sale wrapped in a long catalog. The hero is a warm living room with "it's on: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" and a "Shop Now" button. A two-up of sofas and sectionals follows, with a "Shop Upholstery Sale" button. The first screen tells you what the sale is and where to click.
- After that it drifts. The email adds rugs, lighting, decor and mirrors tiles, a free swatches offer, a performance-fabric stain story, a Design Desk appointment pitch, a dining furniture feature, a holiday block, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app download and a second Design Desk pitch. That is about ten competing messages under a 20%-off headline.
- My read is a solid sale subject with a hero that delivers, followed by a brand-catalog body that dilutes it. The 20% offer is modest and says "select styles", and nothing in the render tells me how long it lasts. Today's earlier sends were also 50%-off bedroom and nursery, rugs and kids' furniture, so this reads as one more promo in a heavy day. The screenshot was shown to me at a small size, so I can't read the fine print or the legibility of the text on the holiday block.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree: up to 20% off sofas and sectionals, with a lifestyle photo that matches.
- The two-up under the hero repeats the offer and gives a specific "Shop Upholstery Sale" button.
- The 10 free swatches block is a useful, low-risk way to get past the fear of buying a sofa online.
- The performance-fabric block shows a real stain problem and a fix, which answers the question people have about light upholstery.
- The brand look is consistent and clean, with an obvious header and plenty of white space.

## What's weak

- The email runs about 9,000px with roughly ten separate asks, and the 20% offer is forgotten by the second half.
- The offer is thin: "up to 20%" on "select styles", and I can't see a clear end date.
- The "more to complete your space" tiles (rugs, lighting, decor, mirrors) don't show whether they carry the sale.
- Both the "holidays happen here" block and the Design Desk block put text over busy photos, and the text looks hard to read at this size.
- The two Design Desk appointment pitches and the app promo at the bottom repeat each other, and none of it ties back to the sofa sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the sofa and sectional grid, swatches, the performance-fabric story and one footer pitch. Everything between "more to complete your space" and the app promo can be dropped or moved to another send.
- 2. Add a visible deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", and a specific hero product linked from the hero.
- 3. Label which tiles are on sale, or drop the tiles that aren't.
- 4. Move the text on the holiday and Design Desk blocks onto solid color bands for legibility.
- 5. Pick one service pitch, either the Design Desk or the swatches, and drop the repeat.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "Up to 20% off your living room update"

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. QA flagged three warnings: compliance headers and authentication results could not be read, and about 35 images lack alt text. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not inspected.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health unverified:** 66 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on tracking and click-redirect domains. Destination health, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` are five separate calls to the same LiveRamp domain, differing only by `n`. They add requests and third-party data sharing. A disclosure for them was not visible in the truncated source.
- **Tracking assets:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/847ec444-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` look like open and view pixels. They are expected, but they carry per-recipient IDs (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`).
- **Image hosts:** images load from three origins, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. They are not under one domain.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** about 35 `<img>` elements were flagged. Examples:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - 15 or more `…/m/1/*.jpg` files
  - Scene7 images `2026_0923_CB_NewSofas_Sectionals_SpillTe…` and `…2026_0926_CB_HolidayAwareness_Anthem…`
  - seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images

  If these images are blocked or read by a screen reader, the content and CTAs have no text equivalent. The QA output doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty, so some of the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` flags may be intentional decorative `alt=""`.
- **Missing alt text, pixels:** the five `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` flags are tracking pixels. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, but this is low severity.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text scaling. Both conflict with the `body` rule `-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%`.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype, so it should be `&amp;`. There are also duplicate `format-detection` meta tags, three of them repeating values already set in the first.
- **Template debris:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in (and misspelled), and the `.mso-font-fix-*` classes may be unused.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** both `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` match at 768px and above. The later rule wins, so the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"` is set on `<html>`, `color-scheme` is declared, and VML/MSO conditionals are present.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved, per-recipient values. The rest of the body was truncated, so this is not a full check.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** QA notes the header "may not be captured by AgentMail relay". This is unconfirmed, because it may be a capture artifact rather than a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN]:** RFC 8058 one-click support could not be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it for senders over 5,000 messages a day. The same relay caveat applies.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` can't be validated from this capture.
- **Footer not verified:** the visible HTML does not include the physical postal address or the unsubscribe link, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be confirmed.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the data provided. The 66 click links were not probed, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source or QA output. Tracking appears to run through a redirect or ESP wrapper, which may hide UTMs from the HTML. No continuity issues are confirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful `alt` to content and CTA images.** Use `alt=""` on spacer and pixel images, with explicit `width="1" height="1"`.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. **Escape the `&` in `<title>`.** Delete the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
4. **Resolve the `max-width` conflict** by merging the 768px and 640px image rules.
5. **Check `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** against the raw message, since the relay may be stripping them. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment the same way.
6. **Consolidate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels** into one call, or confirm each is needed.
7. **Rerun the audit** with the full, untruncated HTML and a follow-up on redirect destinations. This would verify the footer, UTMs and landing pages.
