# Our bedroom bestsellers, up to 25% off for a limited time!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid category-focused sale email that does the basics right and then keeps going long past the point of diminishing returns. The hero is unambiguous: a canopy bed, "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh," Shop Now, sale price vs. reg price all above the fold. Then the four-tile bedroom breakdown (beds, dressers, bedding, lighting) is a genuinely useful way to let a shopper self-select before they even click.
- Where it falls apart is scope creep: after the bedroom sale wraps, the email pivots to a 60% off clearance banner, then a $5000 sweepstakes, then a "we love these for you" grid mixing a stand mixer and an air fryer into a bedroom email, then a credit card pitch, then an app download pitch. By the time you'd hit the fifth CTA the "bedroom bestsellers" premise from the subject line is long gone. This reads like four separate campaigns stapled together rather than one targeted send.
- Judgment: good top half, bloated bottom half — trim it and this jumps a full point or two.

## What's working

- Hero delivers on the subject immediately: bed image, "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh," visible Shop Now button, no scroll needed.
- Sale price shown against reg price ($1,899 vs $2,299) gives a concrete, credible discount rather than a vague "up to" claim.
- The four-tile category grid (beds/dressers/bedding/lighting) with per-category discount callouts is genuinely useful — a bedroom shopper can jump straight to what they need.
- Clean, on-brand visual style throughout — consistent neutral palette, no overlapping text or broken images.

## What's weak

- "We love these for you" recommendation grid includes a Breville toaster oven and an air fryer — completely off-category for a "bedroom bestsellers" email, undermines the targeting.
- Too many competing CTAs after the bedroom section: 60% clearance, $5000 sweepstakes, credit card upsell, app download, TheDesignDesk repeat — the bedroom offer gets buried by the time you scroll a third of the way down.
- TheDesignDesk sweepstakes block appears twice (mid-email and again near the bottom), which feels redundant rather than reinforcing.
- The clearance banner ("up to 60% off clearance") is a stronger-sounding discount placed right under the 25%-off bedroom hero, which makes the primary offer look weak by comparison.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bedroom" — end the send there or replace the clearance/sweepstakes/rewards-card/app stack with a single secondary CTA (e.g., one more bedroom-adjacent upsell). This is the single biggest lift available.
- 2. Remove the toaster oven and air fryer from the recommendation grid, or re-title it "complete the room" and swap in bedroom-only SKUs (rugs, mirrors, throws).
- 3. Drop the duplicate TheDesignDesk block — one placement is enough.
- 4. Move the clearance banner to its own send rather than competing with the bedroom hero for attention.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 30% off beds, dressers & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your bedroom refresh: up to 25% off, this week only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New canopy beds, dressers and lighting — shop the full bedroom sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends soon: beds, bedding, dressers and lighting all marked down.`
