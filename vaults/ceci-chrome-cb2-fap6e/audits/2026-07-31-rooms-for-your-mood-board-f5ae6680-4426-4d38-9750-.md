# Rooms for your mood board
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 stepping fully away from the discount cadence again — no "% off, ends today" banner up top, just a free interior-design-service pitch dressed in three warm, styled living rooms. It reads like a cousin of the 07-30 "Aurora James home office" editorial send (8/10), leaning on aspiration rather than urgency. The problem is scope creep: after the design pitch and its "3 Ways to Meet" explainer, the email keeps going into a "Get Inspired" shoppable grid and then, at the very bottom, tacks on an unrelated "Semi-Annual Bedroom + Bath Event, up to 30% off" banner — three different campaigns stapled into one very long scroll.
- The top half is genuinely well done: clear hero, one unambiguous CTA ("Book Your Free Design Appointment"), and a clean breakdown of how to redeem it. But there's no proof behind the offer — no client name, no before/after caption, no testimonial — so the "designers transformed these spaces" claim is asserted, not shown. And burying the one concrete discount at the bottom, after seven other CTAs, means subscribers primed by CB2's recent discount-heavy history will scroll right past it.
- Net: solid execution, unfocused strategy. It's better than the four-tier "STEALS" roll-ups (5-6/10) but doesn't hit the clarity of the 07-28/07-29 "Furniture Event" sends (9/10) because it's not actually sure what it's selling.

## What's working

- Clean hero — "THIS IS LIVING" over a single large sectional-sofa room photo, unambiguous focal point.
- Free-design offer is a real differentiator from CB2's recent run of percentage-off blasts.
- "3 Ways to Meet" (In Person / Virtually / In Store) clearly explains how to redeem, each with its own "Book Now" button — no confusion about next steps.
- No render bugs — images, grid, and buttons all display cleanly with consistent brand type.

## What's weak

- Three unrelated campaigns crammed into one send (design service → catalog browse → bedroom sale) with no visual separation signaling a shift in offer.
- The one concrete discount ("Up to 30% off" bedroom + bath) is buried at the very bottom below ~8 other CTAs — easy to miss on a scroll-and-skim.
- The three "after" room photos have no captions, designer credit, or before/after framing — the "transformed these spaces" claim has zero visible proof.
- Subject line is soft and offer-free, which is a mismatch for a subscriber base CB2 has otherwise trained toward specific % off/urgency subjects.
- Footer legal text is rendered so small it's essentially unreadable.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends — a design-service email (with real proof: designer name, before/after captions, a client quote) and a separate bedroom+bath sale email. Combining them undercuts both messages.
- 2. If keeping it combined, move the 30% off banner up near the top or cut it — its presence contradicts the "book a free designer" framing of the rest of the email.
- 3. Add one-line captions to each room photo (e.g., "Before: builder-basic. After: this.") to make the "transformed" claim tangible.
- 4. Tighten the subject/preheader pair toward the offer instead of the mood-board metaphor.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your free CB2 designer is one click away`
- **Subject Alt B:** `We turned 3 blank rooms into this`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book free — in person, virtual, or in-store`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See the before, then book your own after`
