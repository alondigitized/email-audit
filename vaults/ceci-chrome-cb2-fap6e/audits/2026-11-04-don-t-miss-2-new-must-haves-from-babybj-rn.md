# Don’t miss: 2 new must-haves from BABYBJÖRN!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a product-launch send for two BabyBjörn items, a carrier and a bouncer. It's cleaner and more focused than most Crate & Barrel Kids emails. The top third, a parent holding a baby above "2 new parent must-haves" and a "Shop Now" button, tells you what the email is about immediately. Each product then gets a feature-callout block (newborn, machine washable, no batteries, lightweight) and its own button. That's the kind of product detail a parent needs.
- It stops being a focused send after the second product module. A $5,000 sweepstakes banner (The Design Desk), a gifts banner, a "100s of new markdowns" banner, a "we love these for you" stroller grid, an app promo and a second Design Desk block follow. The email becomes a roughly 9,700px catalog. There's also no price, discount or deadline anywhere near the hero. "Don't miss" in the subject implies urgency the body never delivers.
- Judgement: well-built on top, but it has no offer and no urgency, and it dilutes its own launch story. Compared with the 11/04 "ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off" send, this one has less reason to click now.
- Caveat: the render I could read was heavily downscaled (140px wide). I couldn't read fine print, prices or small copy, so I'm judging layout and structure only.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: a lifestyle photo, "2 new parent must-haves" and a visible "Shop Now" button in the first screen.
- The subject's "2 new" is a concrete promise, and the body delivers exactly two products.
- Icon callouts under each product (newborn, facing in or out, machine washable, no batteries, lightweight) answer the questions a parent has before clicking.
- Each product block has its own button ("Shop Now" and "Shop All BABYBJÖRN"), so the click lands on a relevant page.
- The BabyBjörn sage-green photography is calm and consistent, with no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There is no price, discount or member benefit in the hero. "Ships free" tags appear on the product tiles, but there's no reason to buy this week.
- "Don't miss" promises urgency that nothing in the body backs up. No deadline is visible.
- After the two BabyBjörn modules, the email adds six or more unrelated sections: sweepstakes, gifts, markdowns, strollers, the app and a second Design Desk block. This is the same length-creep problem as the 11/03 playroom catalog.
- Two Design Desk promos and an app download pitch compete with the launch story, and that last stretch reads like a template footer.
- Both buttons say "Shop Now", which is generic. Neither names the product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything below the BabyBjörn modules, or collapse it to one secondary block. This is the biggest lift. A launch email should end at the product it launched.
- 2. Add one concrete hook in the hero, such as "Free shipping on BabyBjörn" or a registry-completion perk, with a real date.
- 3. Name the products on the buttons: "Shop the carrier", "Shop the bouncer".
- 4. Add a review count or star rating under each product. No social proof is visible.
- 5. Match the "Don't miss" urgency to something real, or drop it.

# Technical Audit: "Don't miss: 2 new must-haves from BABYBJÖRN!"

The HTML source was truncated and the QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 issues, 3 warnings). Findings below are limited to what the source and QA data confirm.

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML table layout with a Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) personalization and tracking stack. Its main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes on content images and a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **75 tracking links were not HTTP-probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` requests (LiveRamp) and several Movable Ink pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e62ae65b-…`). These add requests and are blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and some image-blocking clients.
- The truncated source doesn't show the individual `<a href>` values, so I can't confirm broken or malformed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed by QA).** Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - The Scene7 `2026%5F0916%5FCBK%5FBabybjorn%5FHeroAnimationOnly…` hero
  - `…BOUNCERVIDEO%5F01…`
  - `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
  - `20261101%5FCBK%5FFall%5FClearance%5FPM%5Fv3%5FTert…`
  - About 15 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/…/m/1/*.jpg|gif` images, such as `73eb2153-…jpg` and `ac70d102-…gif`

  With images off, the BABYBJÖRN hero and product blocks collapse to nothing, and screen readers announce raw filenames or URLs.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the `rlcdn`, `mi.` and `dv.` pixels also lack `alt`. This is low impact, but `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` would be cleaner.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom and fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Unescaped `&` in the title.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype (it should be `&amp;`).
- **Template leftovers:**
  - A placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
  - There are duplicate `format-detection` metas (`telephone`/`address`/`date`) and a duplicate charset declaration (`http-equiv` plus `<meta charset>`).
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under XHTML.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** applies to every paragraph, so any left-aligned copy has to override it inline. This is a fragility risk rather than a confirmed bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or the subject line. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid=`/`mi_mid=` parameters on the pixels are populated, not placeholders.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** QA notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so alignment should be checked on the original message.
- The footer, physical address and in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- The truncated source doesn't expose any destination URLs or UTM parameters, so UTM consistency and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- **Asset naming mismatch.** Scene7 paths reference three different campaigns: `2026_0916_CBK_Babybjorn` (the subject's campaign), `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide`, and `20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance`. Reusing modules is normal, but QA should confirm the secondary and tertiary blocks link to current, live pages.

2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.

4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on the raw message, because the relay may have stripped them. Treat them as unresolved until then.
