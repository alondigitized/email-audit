# Our bestselling wine glasses just got better →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel doing what it does best — a genuinely seasonal hero ("the new fall table") sitting on top of the same Labor Day machinery that's dominated every send in this window. The subject line at least breaks from the Labor Day script and calls out a specific product category (wine glasses), which is more than the last four sends managed. But once you scroll past the top banner, it's the identical "Double Rewards' + Up to 60% Off The Labor Day Event" red-tag grid seen on 2026-08-30, 2026-08-29, and the Crate & Kids sibling — this is now the sixth-plus appearance of that exact module.
- The wine glasses promised in the subject do appear, with a nice styled shot of three stemware colors and drinks, but they're buried roughly a third of the way down a very long, kitchen-sink email that also covers dinnerware, mugs, barware, furniture, lighting, mattresses, and an app download pitch. Anyone opening for wine glasses has to scroll past two other product modules first.
- Verdict: solid subject line, weak execution on delivering what it promised, and the email is bloated with recycled Labor Day inventory that's grown stale after this many repeats.

## What's working

- Subject-relevant content does exist — the "new! our bestseller just got upgraded" wine glass module shows three colorways of stemware with a clean lifestyle shot.
- The "new fall table" hero banner is genuinely new creative (moody florals, autumnal tablescape) rather than another Labor Day red-tag banner.
- Clear, repeated CTAs throughout ("Shop Now," "Shop All Stemware & Bar," "Shop The Labor Day Event") with good visual separation between modules.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The wine glass hero promised in the subject is three screens deep, not near the top — the email leads with tableware/dinnerware instead.
- This is the same "Double Rewards' + Up to 60% Off The Labor Day Event" grid from 2026-08-29 and 2026-08-30 sends, verbatim red-tag styling and all — pure repetition.
- The email is enormous — dinnerware, mugs, barware, furniture, lighting, kitchen deals, mattresses, and an app-download CTA all in one send, diluting the wine glass story completely.
- "Up to 15% off Beautyrest mattresses" tacked on near the bottom is a jarring category non-sequitur in a "fall table" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the wine glasses. If that's the subject line hook, put that module first or second, not buried after a full dinnerware section — right now the email doesn't deliver on its own promise until well into the scroll.
- 2. Cut the mattress module entirely — it has nothing to do with fall tablescapes or the Labor Day sale theme and reads as inventory-dumping.
- 3. Retire or meaningfully refresh the Labor Day grid — this is at least its fourth verbatim appearance across the last three days; recipients who opened those emails will recognize it instantly and disengage.
- 4. Shorten the email significantly — split "for your fall gatherings," "top kitchen deals," and furniture/lighting into separate, more targeted sends rather than one marathon scroll.
