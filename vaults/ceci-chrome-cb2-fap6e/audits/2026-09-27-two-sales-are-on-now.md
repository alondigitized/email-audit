# Two sales are on now
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 finally naming two distinct sales in the subject line — a step up from the vague "ONLY AT CB2" and "New arrivals are in" sends this week — but the execution undercuts it. The hero for the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event is visibly broken: a smaller product photo is layered on top of the main hero image, chopping the headline down to "-ANNUAL...ERY EVENT...5% OFF" and leaving "UPHOLSTE...UP TO 2" as the only legible fragment underneath. That's not a stylistic quirk, it's a rendering failure sitting in the most important real estate in the email.
- Below the broken hero, the second sale (Up to 50% Off furniture/decor) renders cleanly with a legible headline and a nice dining-table hero — so the brand clearly can execute this format correctly when it wants to. The gap between the two sections is jarring: one hero looks like a QA miss, the other looks shipped.
- Compared to 9/25's "The Upholstery Event is on" (6/10, their cleanest send of the week), this one had the stronger premise — two sales, clearly labeled — but the broken hero drags it down to their usual mid-tier territory.

## What's working

- Subject line is concrete and different from the repetitive "ONLY AT CB2" / "New arrivals" pattern seen all week — it actually tells you there are two offers.
- The second hero (Up to 50% Off furniture/decor) is clean: clear headline, visible discount, unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button, no overlap issues.
- "SHOP NOW" CTAs are present under both hero sections with good contrast (white button, dark background).
- The $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes section is a nice secondary hook with a specific, well-labeled CTA ("BOOK A FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT").

## What's weak

- The top hero image is broken: a smaller inset photo overlaps the main headline, making "SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT UP TO 25% OFF" unreadable as a coherent line.
- The "ORDER SWATCHES" button floats awkwardly mid-image with no visible container tying it to a product.
- The 25%-off first sale's discount amount is only guessable from the fragment "5% OFF" and body copy — it's not confidently legible from the hero alone.
- Visual hierarchy is confused up top: the eye doesn't land cleanly on one offer because two images are fighting for the same space.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the broken hero first — this is the single biggest lift available. Re-export/re-crop the Upholstery Event hero so the inset photo doesn't overlap the headline text; verify in an actual inbox client (Gmail/Apple Mail) before sending, not just a browser preview.
- 2. Lead with the stronger, cleaner asset. If the 50% Off furniture hero renders correctly and the Upholstery hero doesn't, swap their order so the broken one isn't the first thing a subscriber sees.
- 3. Make the two offers scannable at a glance — e.g., a small two-tile "Sale 1 / Sale 2" summary block near the top, since the subject promises "two sales" but the layout makes you scroll to find the second one.
- 4. Tighten the subject/preheader pairing so the preview text actually names both offers instead of generic account copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Upholstery is 25% off. Everything else is up to 50%.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 sales, 1 email: upholstery + storewide savings`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `25% off upholstery, up to 50% off furniture & decor — both end soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two ways to save this week, starting now.`
