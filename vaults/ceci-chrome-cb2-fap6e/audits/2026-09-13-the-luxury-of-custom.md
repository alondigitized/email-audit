# THE LUXURY OF CUSTOM
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- CB2 pivots again — this is a custom-upholstery/fabric-swatch play, not a sale email. "THIS IS PERSONAL / We offer 90+ custom fabrics for when you want that particular shade. Not for everyone. Definitely for you" is a well-written, distinctive hero, and the "$0 free swatches" line is a genuinely good low-friction hook. But then the email sprawls into four more unrelated hero sections (white sofa, leather jacket sofa, neutral tones) before finally surfacing the $5,000 refresh sweepstakes — which is now labeled "ENDS TONIGHT," a real deadline buried on the fifth scroll.
- Compared to the brand's last several sends (2026-09-11 "NEW & ONLY AT CB2," 2026-09-11 NYC apartment/design services, 2026-09-10 "THE FALL SALE"), this continues the pattern: gorgeous lookbook photography, no clear single CTA, and a real offer/deadline hidden well below the fold. The subject "THE LUXURY OF CUSTOM" is on-brand voice but gives zero indication of the sweepstakes urgency actually present in the email.
- Judgement: strong art direction, weak email architecture — this reads as a mood board stitched to five different CTAs rather than one email with one job.

## What's working

- The hero headline and subhead ("Not for everyone. Definitely for you") is confident, differentiated copy — better than most of CB2's recent generic lookbook headers.
- Free swatches is a concrete, low-friction, believable offer, and it's stated plainly under the hero.
- Product photography is consistent, high-quality, and on-brand throughout (chair/side table, sofas, leather, coffee table).
- No render bugs — clean grid, all images loaded, buttons render correctly, "MORE FOR YOU" product grid is well-formed with prices.

## What's weak

- The genuine urgency ("ENDS TONIGHT" $5,000 refresh sweepstakes) is buried roughly two-thirds down the email, after four other design vignettes compete for attention.
- Five separate hero/CTA moments (swatches, white sofa, leather sofa, neutral sofa, sweepstakes, app download) dilute visual hierarchy — there's no single obvious "click here" moment.
- Subject line "THE LUXURY OF CUSTOM" doesn't hint at the sweepstakes deadline, so the most time-sensitive content is invisible until well into the scroll.
- No percentage-off or dollar offer for the core custom-furniture message — this is a two-headed email (custom-fabric brand story + sweepstakes) instead of one coherent offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails: one focused entirely on custom fabrics/swatches (with the strong "THIS IS PERSONAL" hero as the whole story), and a separate, standalone "ENDS TONIGHT: $5,000 Refresh" sweepstakes send with the deadline in the subject line — right now both messages dilute each other.
- 2. If keeping it combined, move the "ENDS TONIGHT $5,000 REFRESH" block to directly under the hero, since it's the only real deadline-driven CTA in the email.
- 3. Cut the middle three lookbook sections (white sofa, leather sofa, neutral sofa) to one, or move them below the sweepstakes — right now they push the real urgency down four screens.
- 4. Add a specific CTA verb in the hero: "Start with Swatches" is good, but pair it with "Order Free Swatches" phrasing that names the free/no-cost mechanic explicitly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: win a $5,000 home refresh`
- **Subject Alt B:** `90+ custom fabrics. Free swatches, your call`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free swatches ship free — plus your last hours to enter the $5K refresh`
