# LAST DAY TO SHOP LABOR DAY SALE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- CB2 closes out its Labor Day run with the strongest structural version of the formula yet: a live countdown clock reading 17:19:18 (hours:minutes:seconds), an "ENDS TONIGHT" hero with a clean "UP TO 50% OFF" callout, and — genuinely useful this time — a full category grid of stacked CTA buttons (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Shop All) so a shopper lands directly in their category instead of a generic homepage.
- But the same render bug that tanked the 2026-09-07 "DOUBLE REWARDS§ IS ALMOST OVER" send (4.00/10) is back, verbatim: the credit-card cross-sell hero reads "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" with the "10%" struck through and "20%" stacked next to it. That's the second time this exact creative asset has shipped broken in three days — this isn't a one-off glitch, it's an unfixed template.
- Judgement: the Labor Day hero itself is clean and would score well on its own, but this send bundles it with a visibly broken second module, which drags the whole email down and signals the brand isn't proofing cross-sell creative before it goes out at scale.

## What's working

- Countdown clock plus "ENDS TONIGHT" creates real, credible urgency for the last day of the sale.
- Category-grid CTA block (7 distinct buttons) lets shoppers jump straight to Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, etc. instead of only a generic "Shop All."
- Hero visual hierarchy is clean — sale name, discount, and CTAs are the first and only things the eye lands on in the top module.
- Footer utility block (app download, credit card, gift registry, SMS signup) is well organized and doesn't compete with the main offer.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards hero has a repeated render bug: "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" with a struck-through "10%" sitting next to "20%" — confusing and unresolved from the 09-07 send.
- Three offers are stapled into one email again (Labor Day sale, Double Rewards card, plus footer promos for app/gift-list/SMS) — same structural bloat flagged on 2026-09-08.
- Subject "LAST DAY TO SHOP LABOR DAY SALE" is nearly identical to yesterday's "LABOR DAY SALE ENDS TOMORROW" and this week's other Labor Day subjects — cadence fatigue for anyone who's opened the last five.
- No specific hero product shown — the top module is pure typography/gradient, so there's nothing concrete to click toward beyond a category name.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Double Rewards render bug before this asset ships again — it's now appeared in at least two sends this week and undermines trust in the 20% claim.
- 2. Cut the Double Rewards module out of the final Labor Day send entirely; let the last-chance sale stand alone so urgency isn't diluted by a card pitch.
- 3. Differentiate the subject line from the last two days' near-identical "ends tomorrow / last day" phrasing so inbox scanners don't file it with yesterday's email.
- 4. Add one hero product image (a rug or sofa) behind the "50% OFF" text so there's a concrete visual anchor, not just a gradient.

- **Subject Alt B:** `17 hours left — up to 50% off everything`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting & more — all up to 50% off through tonight only.`
