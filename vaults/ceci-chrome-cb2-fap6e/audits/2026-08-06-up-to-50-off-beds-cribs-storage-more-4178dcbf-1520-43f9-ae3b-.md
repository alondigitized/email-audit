# Up to 50% off beds, cribs, storage & more!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing a straightforward seasonal sale sweep — "up to 50% off summer sale" up top, a six-tile category grid (furniture, kids bedding, baby bedding, decor, rugs, clearance), and a clean nursery hero shot to set the mood. Unlike yesterday's dining-room Design Desk send (2026-08-06, 6.00/10) that got flagged for sprawling across two sub-editions, this one keeps its top section disciplined: hero → subhead → two buttons → category grid, all above or just below the fold.
- Where it does sprawl is everything after that first screen — fall arrivals starting at $29, a personalization callout for backpacks, a "we love these for you" product rail, a Crate Card acquisition pitch, an app-download tease ("your chance at $500"), and a Design Desk appointment booking, all stacked in one email. That's six distinct asks bolted onto a sale email, and the vague "chance at $500" line echoes the bait-and-switch pattern flagged in the brand's recent giveaway sends (2026-08-04, "Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card →," 7.00/10) — it teases a prize with zero context on what it actually is.
- Net: strong, clean top of email that would get me to click; a bloated back half that dilutes the single sale message into a general Crate & Kids catalog dump. This is a solid mid-to-upper-tier promotional send, held back from top marks by the kitchen-sink structure and a vague, deadline-free discount.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: "up to 50% off summer sale" is the first thing the eye hits, paired with two clearly labeled buttons ("Shop the Sale," "Shop New Arrivals").
- The category grid (furniture, kids bedding, baby bedding, decor, rugs, clearance) gives shoppers an immediate self-service path instead of forcing everyone into one generic CTA.
- "Ships free" labels are placed directly on the kids bedding and baby bedding tiles — useful, low-friction detail right where you need it.
- The "we love these for you" product rail shows real prices and a visible sale price ($65.00 → $45.20 on the backpack), which is more concrete than most upper-funnel recommendation blocks.
- No render bugs — every image loads cleanly, text doesn't overlap, and the layout holds together through a very long scroll.

## What's weak

- "Up to 50%" with no deadline anywhere in the email means there's no urgency to act now versus next week.
- The email tries to do too much: sale, fall arrivals, personalization, product recs, credit card, app download, design consultation — each one dilutes attention from the last.
- "It's all on the app, including your chance at $500" is vague sweepstakes language with no terms, no odds, no explanation — the same shape of tease the brand's giveaway emails have been dinged for.
- No reviews or social proof anywhere in the product rail, despite showing six specific SKUs with prices.
- The Crate Card pitch ("new! the card you love just got better") is dropped in the middle of a sale email with no visual separation strong enough to signal it's a different offer entirely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the category grid and the fall-arrivals teaser — split the credit card pitch, app-download sweep, and Design Desk booking into their own sends. One offer per email converts better than six competing for the same click.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the sale ("Ends Sunday," "48 hours only") next to the hero copy — right now there's zero reason to act today.
- 3. Replace the vague "your chance at $500" with a real one-line explanation of what it is (sweepstakes name, entry mechanism) or drop it — vague prize teases erode trust, especially given this brand's recent giveaway-email history.
- 4. Add a star rating or review count to at least the top 2 products in "we love these for you" — it's the section most likely to drive a direct-to-product click and currently has zero social proof.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: "Up to 50% off beds, cribs, storage & more!" (Crate & Kids)

### 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated email (MessageGears/"MI" platform, domain `mi.crateandbarrel.com`) with heavy MSO/Outlook conditional markup. Automated QA returned a 57% pass rate driven primarily by missing image `alt` text and compliance headers that could not be captured through the relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking/redirect links skipped** by the automated HTTP probe (click-redirect domains) — destination URLs and UTM parameters cannot be verified from the data available.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels present in source:
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` — appears **6 times with an identical src**, consistent with per-block MessageGears open-tracking, but worth confirming it's not a duplicated include.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...` — MI open/unsubscribe-tracking pixel.
  - `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=...&n=1` through `n=5` — LiveRamp (RapLeaf) third-party data-onboarding pixel, fired 5x with incrementing `n=` param.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/24552119-...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` — additional open-tracking beacon.
- Leftover ESP template artifact shipped to production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (also contains a typo, "IMPUT"). Not render-breaking, but indicates the placeholder wasn't cleaned before send.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **20 content images missing `alt` text** per QA (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `39ad5559-...jpg`, `971aa10f-...jpg`, and others listed in the QA output). This is a genuine WCAG failure — screen readers get no fallback, and the same images render as empty boxes when a client blocks remote images by default (Outlook, Gmail).
  - The tracking pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com`) also show as missing `alt`, but that's expected/inconsequential for 1×1 beacons — not a real issue.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` does not match the From name "Crate & Kids SALE" — an inconsistency between envelope identity and document title that can surface in browser tabs or screen-reader document announcements.
- Redundant markup: `format-detection` meta is declared once combined (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) and then repeated individually 3 more times (`date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). Harmless but indicates unreviewed template merges.
- Breakpoint gap: mobile rules apply at `max-width:460px`, and separate `img{max-width}` rules apply at `min-width:640px`/`min-width:768px`. There's no explicit rule for the 461–639px range, which could cause inconsistent image sizing on that width band. Minor, non-blocking.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`, `[First Name]`) found in the visible HTML. No issues found. Note: source was truncated, so tokens later in the document (e.g. footer/account-specific blocks) were not reviewed.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: **not found**. QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm actual CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058): **not found** — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed, not confirmed absent.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM): **not found** via relay — sender authentication status is unknown, not confirmed as failing.
- Footer unsubscribe link / physical mailing address were beyond the truncated HTML and could not be assessed.
- **Recommendation:** these three findings need verification against a raw MIME capture taken outside the relay before treating them as actual compliance gaps — the current data only confirms non-capture, not non-existence.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot assess. All 73 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the probe, and the truncated HTML doesn't expose raw CTA `href` values, so UTM parameters (`utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign`) and landing-page alignment with the "beds, cribs, storage" sale theme are unverifiable from the data provided.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Re-run the link probe with redirect-follow enabled (or against the un-shortened raw HTML) to confirm CTA destinations and UTM tagging — currently 73 links are unverified.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 20 flagged content images; leave tracking pixels as empty/`alt=""` (already effectively the case).
3. Capture raw email headers outside the AgentMail relay to definitively confirm/deny `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM status before flagging as compliance violations.
4. Align `<title>` with the sending brand ("Crate & Kids") to remove the Crate & Barrel/Crate & Kids mismatch.
5. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]` placeholder comment from the production template.
6. Dedupe the repeated `format-detection` meta tags and the 6 identical `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/...` tracking pixel includes if they aren't intentionally per-section.
