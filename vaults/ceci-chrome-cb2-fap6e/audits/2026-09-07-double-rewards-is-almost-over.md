# DOUBLE REWARDS§ IS ALMOST OVER
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's credit-card cross-sell, not a merchandise sale email, and it opens with a visible render bug: the hero reads "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" — two numbers stacked on top of each other where only one should render. That's the first thing the eye hits after the headline, and it undercuts the entire pitch before anyone gets to the offer.
- Structurally this is the most different send in the recent CB2 stretch — it swaps the usual "up to 50% off furniture" hero for a financial-services pitch (CB2 Visa Signature card, Double Rewards promotion), then pivots into the now-familiar six-category grid (Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs) and closes with the same Labor Day beach-at-dusk banner that's run repeatedly this week. It's two emails stitched together: a card-acquisition pitch up top, a sale email below.
- For a design-conscious furniture shopper, this lands weak. The primary subject-line hook ("DOUBLE REWARDS§ IS ALMOST OVER") is about a credit card, not product, and the hero has a legible content error. The countdown clock and Labor Day banner are recycled wholesale from the rest of this week's cadence, so a subscriber who's opened three of these already will feel no novelty. Judgment: send it back for a hero-copy fix before it goes out again — the numeral collision is the kind of bug that reads as sloppy, not urgent.

## What's working

- Countdown clock ("SALE ENDS IN 02:07:20:26") plus "NOW THROUGH SEPT 9TH, 2026" gives a credible, specific deadline.
- Six-category grid (Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs) is clean, evenly cropped, and gives multiple entry points to shop.
- Footer utility blocks (app download, 10% back program, gift registry, 15% off text signup) are well organized and give secondary reasons to engage.
- Brand bar and social/sibling-brand footer (Crate & Barrel, Crate&Kids, H&G) keep the send visually on-brand.

## What's weak

- Hero copy visibly reads "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" — two percentages overlapping/concatenated, a clear render bug in the most important line of the email.
- The core offer (card rewards) is buried under legal-style copy ("...ON PURCHASES AT CB2 WITH A CB2 VISA SIGNATURE® CREDIT CARD") that reads like fine print, not a benefit.
- This is a financial-product pitch dressed as a furniture sale email — the subject and hero sell a credit card, but everything below the fold sells furniture, creating a disjointed message.
- Same Labor Day "up to 50% off" beach banner that's already run multiple times this week (2026-09-04, 2026-09-05) — zero creative refresh in the recycled section.
- CTA hierarchy is muddy: "LEARN MORE" (card) sits above "SCROLL TO SHOP" (product) above "SHOP THE SALE" (Labor Day) — three competing asks in one email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero numeral bug immediately — it should read a single clear percentage (e.g., "GET 20% BACK") not "10% 20%." This is a ship-blocking defect, not a style note.
- 2. Lead the hero with the benefit, not the legal disclaimer — "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars, now through Sept 9" reads better than the current stacked-clause copy.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA. If this is a card-acquisition send, cut the category grid and Labor Day banner — they dilute the ask. If it's meant to double as a sale email, move the card pitch to a smaller footer module instead of the hero.
- 4. Swap the Labor Day banner creative — it's the fourth+ appearance of this exact beach image/copy combo this week and reads as reused inventory.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `Earn 20% back with your CB2 card — ends Sept 9`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Double Rewards ends in 48 hours`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack card rewards with Labor Day markdowns through Wednesday.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `20% back in Reward Dollars, plus up to 50% off sitewide.`

# Technical Audit — CB2 "Double Rewards" Email

**1. Technical Summary**
Standard MI/Bronto-templated HTML email; core rendering markup is sound, but the message carries legacy ad-network beacons/cookie redirectors and lacks visible RFC 8058 unsubscribe support in the captured headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains) — not independently verified as functional.
- Multiple **non-HTTPS (`http://`) third-party beacon/redirect calls** to `ads.dotomi.com` (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) and `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` — these are legacy Dotomi cookie-sync/retargeting pixels. Mixed-content `http://` resources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), meaning this tracking network sync likely fails silently for most recipients.
- Standard first-party tracking pixels present and HTTPS: `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/...` (Bronto/CM engagement tracking) — no issues.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **9 content images missing `alt` text** (all `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/...` product/hero images) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing meaningful. Evidence: e.g. `f9a45447-5688-4f48-86d5-5223895fbdb3.jpg`.
- The 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` and `UCMController` images also lack `alt`, but these are non-visual tracking beacons, so missing alt is not a user-facing accessibility defect — flagged only under Link & Tracking above.
- Template includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and mobile media queries (`max-width:418px`) — no structural rendering issues found in the visible markup.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge/personalization tokens (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — cannot confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is offered.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe compliance for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements cannot be confirmed from available data.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown from this capture; note both WARNs may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing/forwarding original headers rather than the sender's actual configuration — cannot confirm sender-side compliance either way from this data.
- In-body unsubscribe link/physical address not visible in the truncated HTML segment provided — unable to confirm from source shown.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Truncated source does not include the visible CTA `href` values, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from the data provided.

**7. Recommendations**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 product/content images for accessibility and image-blocked fallback.
- Replace the `http://ads.dotomi.com` and `login.dotomi.com` calls with HTTPS equivalents (or remove if this legacy Dotomi retargeting integration is deprecated) to prevent silent tracking failures in modern mail clients.
- Confirm with the ESP/deliverability team whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance (Feb 2024 requirements) — and whether the AgentMail relay is stripping them before capture.
- Verify `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) at the raw MTA layer, since relay capture may not reflect true authentication status.
- Re-run this audit against full (non-truncated) HTML to verify CTA `href`/UTM structure and confirm unsubscribe link/postal address presence in the footer.
