# ENDS SOON: Up to 50% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, long-scroll sale blast. The "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" headline sits right under the logo with a "Shop the Sale" button. Below it is a stack of full-width lifestyle photos, each with its own "up to X% off" line and a button. The photography is calm and premium, and the hierarchy is clear. The recipient gets one idea and can pick a category.
- The weak spot is the subject line and the context. "ENDS SOON: Up to 50% off!" has no date and no category, and the body doesn't name a deadline either. This is also another urgency send in a day that already had "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" and "Final weekend! Up to 50% off all things bedroom & nursery." The body is nearly the same as that 2026-10-11 bedroom and nursery send. A subscriber reads "ends soon" as the same sale a third time. The email also carries a free-shipping banner, a clearance block, a financing tile, a product grid, an app promo and a design-services promo. That is a lot of extra weight for a "last chance" message.
- Verdict: well made and easy to scan, but repetitive and vague about the deadline. It is a solid reminder, not a standout send.

## What's working

- The headline and "Shop the Sale" button are at the top, so the offer is visible immediately.
- Each category tile pairs a large photo with its own percentage and button: kids bedroom furniture, nursery seating, nursery furniture, bedding, bath, and lighting & storage.
- The photography is consistent and aspirational, and no images look broken or misaligned.
- A "your dream home, now, pay later" financing tile and a "we love these for you" product grid give a second route to a click.
- The free-shipping banner at the top reinforces value without taking over the hero.

## What's weak

- The subject gives no deadline and no category. "ENDS SOON" is vague and the "!" adds mild spam feel.
- The email repeats the 2026-10-11 "Final weekend! Up to 50% off all things bedroom & nursery" send almost exactly, so it reads as a third nudge for the same event.
- The body is very long, with about six category tiles plus clearance, financing, a product grid, an app promo and a design-services promo. That dilutes the "ends soon" message.
- The deadline is never made concrete in the body. There is no date and no countdown.
- "Up to 50%" with "select styles" repeated on every tile makes the headline discount feel soft. Only a few categories seem to reach 50%, and nursery seating is capped at 30%.

- 1. Put a real deadline in the subject and the hero, such as "Ends tonight" or "Ends Oct 13." This single change would lift both opens and clicks.
- 2. Trim the body to the hero, three or four top categories and one product row. Cut the app, financing and design-services blocks from deadline sends.
- 3. Suppress or merge this send for people who got the 2026-10-11 bedroom and nursery email, or change the creative to show what's left or best-selling.
- 4. Name the strongest deals in the hero, for example "50% off kids bedroom furniture," instead of a blanket "up to."

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel Kids, "ENDS SOON: Up to 50% off!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and a 460px mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed issues are about 20 content images with no alt text, a few template-hygiene defects, and third-party pixels that carry a user identifier. The header-level compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 77 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8&n=1…5` beacons are present. They are LiveRamp-domain requests, repeated five times with `n=1..5`.
- **Shared identifier:** The `s=` value starts with the same hex string as the first-party `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964`. That points to the same recipient identifier being sent to a third party on open. This is an observation, not a confirmed violation.
- **First-party pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` appears 6 times. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e519ddf3-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further open and tracking beacons. Several overlapping open-tracking pixels add weight and failure points.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** About 20 images flagged by QA have no alt text. Examples:
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `…/m/1/2dae08c2-…jpg`
  - `…/m/1/36b20793-…gif`
  - `scene7.com/…/2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer content is unavailable. This is the largest finding.
- **Spacer without alt:** `scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` has no alt. It should carry `alt=""`.
- **Tracking pixels:** The `f3c0d…png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.…` pixels are flagged too. For these, `alt=""` is the correct fix, since they are decorative.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility regression. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` (in `*{}` and `body`) also disables text scaling.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. There are also duplicate `<meta charset>` declarations and three duplicate `format-detection` metas, one of them `content="date=no"` only. These are low impact.
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the production source.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, `color-scheme` is declared and the MSO fixes are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. The source was truncated, so tokens further down are not verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are reported missing. QA notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sending ESP. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** It is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The relay is the likely cause and the sender-side configuration cannot be judged. From and envelope domain look aligned (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`).
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address:** These are not verifiable in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
UTM parameters and landing destinations cannot be assessed. The 77 tracked links were not resolved and the link hrefs were not visible in the truncated source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to all ~20 content images, especially the offer and clearance banners. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Pull the raw headers from the ESP and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't treat the relay warnings as findings until then.
3. Review the `rlcdn.com` pixels, which pass an identifier derived from the recipient token to a third party. Confirm this is covered by privacy disclosures and consent. Consolidate the duplicate pixels (6× `f3c0d…png`, 5× `448796.gif`).
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` so zoom and text resizing work.
5. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the meta tags and remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
6. Resolve the redirect-wrapped links with a real HTTP probe, or sample them, to check final URLs and UTM parameters.
