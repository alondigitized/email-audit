# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's usual Black Friday template with a new hero: "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE – In stores for a limited time." The subject is the most specific thing CB2 has sent this week. Floor samples are a real, distinct reason to open. The body doesn't back it up. The hero is a lovely black credenza with a lamp and olive branches, but it has no price, no discount and no product name. The only action is a black "FIND A STORE" button.
- The strongest part of the email is the real in-store hook. Floor samples are scarce and local, and they suggest a real deal. The weakest part is that the email never says what the deal is. The Black Friday banner above the hero shouts "UP TO 60% OFF" and "SHOP THE SALE," and that competes with the floor-sample message. A reader can't tell whether 60% applies to floor samples or to online items. After five near-identical Black Friday sends since 11/26, this one is a small step up in relevance but a modest one in execution.
- Verdict: a better subject than the recent run, wrapped in the same template. It needs a number and a product.

## What's working

- The subject "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" is concrete and offers a different reason to open than the prior "SALE OF THE YEAR" and "NOT SOLD OUT YET" sends.
- The hero photo is clean and premium. The black credenza with brass pulls is on-brand and draws the eye.
- The "EXTENDED STORE HOURS" banner for 11/28 and 11/29 gives practical urgency that supports the in-store message.
- The "FIND A STORE" button is a high-contrast black bar that is easy to see.
- Free shipping at $99+ and the 15% off for the new year add secondary value without crowding the hero.

## What's weak

- The hero has no discount, price or product name. "Limited time" is vague, and there is no end date.
- The Black Friday banner at the top ("UP TO 60% OFF," "SHOP THE SALE") competes with the floor-sample message. It sends people to the online sale instead of the store.
- "FIND A STORE" is a weak CTA. It is a utility link, not a reason to go, and it doesn't say what is available in stores.
- The email stacks four offers: the floor samples, Black Friday 60%, free shipping and 15% off in the new year. There is no single clear offer.
- The legal footer is long and dense, and it includes a "$15% off" line that is confusing next to the 60% claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the hero: "Floor samples up to X% off, in stores this weekend only." Name the product category and the end date.
- 2. Remove or shrink the Black Friday banner on this send so it doesn't pull away from the in-store message, or tie it clearly to the floor-sample deal.
- 3. Make the CTA specific, such as "FIND FLOOR SAMPLES NEAR YOU," and show two or three more floor-sample products below the hero.
- 4. Move free shipping and the 15% new-year offer lower, or cut one of them. Free shipping doesn't apply to an in-store purchase.
- 5. Add a line saying quantities are limited and one-of-a-kind, so the urgency is credible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Floor samples, up to 60% off this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One-of-a-kind pieces, in stores now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extended hours Sat & Sun. Once they're gone, they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Display pieces from our showrooms, marked down for the weekend.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The automated QA run scored 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. Most findings are image accessibility gaps and 11 non-HTTPS third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive: the headers weren't captured, and the HTML provided was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **55 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were verified for status codes, redirect chains, or expiry.
- **11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels** are loaded over `http://`. Mail clients and image proxies may block or rewrite them, which would silently drop the conversion and retargeting data. The evidence is:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- The `pub1`–`pub10` URLs follow a cookie-sync redirect pattern. Ten third-party sync requests per open add load time and a data-sharing footprint.
- First-party tracking pixels are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/7bf647c4-...?mi_cid=...`. These are HTTPS and have no issues.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Five content images have no `alt` attribute:**
  - `a45aea1a-cd0d-4fab-a1d0-a75f3d88ffcc.jpg`
  - `6047cf9d-01b5-44fe-bdd5-ce345cd10f96.jpg`
  - `aa2a33ba-a483-42d9-9599-ff33babd0668.gif`
  - `65d9a6b8-2058-42f5-8c88-11ebbb46a899.jpg`
  - `20185973-3ca0-4455-9e19-a5471f2ecc5a.jpg`

  All are served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, any text or offer carried in them is lost. The "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" content may be among them.
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt`** (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, and the 11 Dotomi pixels). This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct value for decorative images.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **A template placeholder was left in the `<head>`:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless in rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Conflicting rules:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` appears alongside `p { padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important }`. The later rule wins, so the earlier `margin:0 auto` is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%first_name%%` or `{{...}}` were visible in the provided source. Coverage is limited because the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes this may be a capture limit of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed absence.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** The SPF, DKIM, and DMARC outcomes are unknown. The `From` domain `mail.cb2.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, which is consistent with a properly configured ESP, but this can't be confirmed from the data.
- **The footer was not visible in the truncated source.** The physical postal address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable from the data. The destination URLs sit behind click-redirect domains that were skipped, so UTM parameters, final landing pages, and redirect integrity are unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to the 5 content images, with the offer text in each. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS**, or drop the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync requests. Confirm that the `login.dotomi.com` endpoint supports HTTPS.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` headers.** That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results that the relay hid.
5. **Resolve the redirect chains offline** (follow the 55 tracking links with a real client) to verify landing pages and UTM carry-through.
6. **Re-run with untruncated HTML** to audit the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address, and merge tokens.
7. **Remove the leftover template comment** and the dead `p` margin rule.
