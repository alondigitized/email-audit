## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's source is table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO conditionals, and it has two confirmed code-level problems. One is 11 third-party Dotomi tracking pixels served over plain `http://`. The other is 28 images with no `alt` attribute. The automated QA run scored 57% with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, and the truncated HTML and the missing relay headers leave several compliance and continuity checks unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **73 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains, and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **11 insecure tracking images** load over `http://` (flagged by QA):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`, through `pub10.php`
  - Clients that block or rewrite mixed content can drop these pixels, which breaks Dotomi cookie-sync. Because they are cookie-redirect calls, they also send requests to ten sync endpoints on every open.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/2eecfc23-be75-4513-833c-b5f4367a84a7?mi_cid=…`. Both use HTTPS and are first-party, but neither has `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **28 images are missing `alt`.** The breakdown is 15 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg`, 11 Dotomi pixels, and 2 first-party pixels.
  - The 15 content images need real `alt` text, because with images blocked those slots render as empty space and screen readers announce the filename or nothing.
  - The 13 tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`). Without `alt`, some clients announce the URL.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless to rendering but shows the template was not cleaned before send.
- **Font loading is partly cross-domain.** `HNW05` is loaded from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`. Several `@font-face` URLs also contain a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`). These fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif in most clients, so this is low risk.
- The responsive and MSO handling is present (`@media max-width:418px`, `<!--[if mso]>` with `PixelsPerInch`, VML namespace). No issues found there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The `mi_u=` hash in the pixel URL is a resolved hashed identifier, not a failed merge.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** QA could not find `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), or `Authentication-Results`, and it attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. Nothing in the data shows CB2 omitted them, so SPF, DKIM, DMARC, and one-click unsubscribe support remain unknown.
- The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.
- The `mail.cb2.com` sending subdomain and the `image.mail.cb2.com` image host are aligned with the From domain, which is a positive sign for alignment but not proof.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable from the supplied data. The 73 links were skipped as redirect-wrapped, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the 10 `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/…/pubN.php` pixels and the `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` pixel to HTTPS, or ask the vendor for HTTPS endpoints. Otherwise remove them.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/…/m/1/`.
3. Add `alt=""` to all 13 tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` comment and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs.
6. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` with full headers, then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, and the footer unsubscribe link. Also resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links to check the UTM parameters and landing pages.
