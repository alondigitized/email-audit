- This is a category-focused Black Friday send, and it's CB2's most targeted one this week. The subject and hero both say "UP TO 60% OFF LIGHTING". Below the lighting hero it turns into the usual kitchen sink: a six-tile category grid (furniture, holiday, decor, rugs, bedding, pillows), a free-shipping banner, "SAVE 15% OFF IN THE NEW YEAR", an app promo, a rewards promo, and a 15%-off SMS signup. ## What's working

- The subject and hero match: "UP TO 60% OFF LIGHTING" is specific, and the glowing pendant and sconce imagery fits the moody CB2 look.
- The product grid shows strikethrough and sale prices, so the discount is concrete (for example $149 → $99 on the sconce).

- The wide category tiles are polished and consistent with the brand's burgundy and olive palette.
- The free-shipping banner with its $99 threshold is easy to read.

## What's weak

- Once past the hero, the email stops being about lighting. About five scroll-lengths of generic categories and promos sit between the hero and the lighting products.
- It stacks offers: up to 60% off, free shipping on $99+, 15% off in the new year, 10% back in Rewards, and 15% off for SMS signup. Nothing is clearly the main offer.
- The only lighting CTA is a generic "SHOP THE SALE". The product tiles show no visible buttons, and there's no "Shop Lighting" label.
- The "up to 60%" claim is not borne out by the visible prices: roughly 25% to 33% off ($399 → $299, $149 → $119, $149 → $99, $499 → $379). The footnote marker on the hero is tiny and the legal text at the bottom is dense.
- There is no social proof, no reviews, and no urgency deadline anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. Keep the whole email on lighting. Replace the category grid with a lighting grid by type (floor, table, pendant, sconce), and put the product grid directly under the hero.
- 2. Make the CTA specific, such as "SHOP LIGHTING SALE", and link each product tile to its product page.
- 3. Show the deepest discount in the hero. If a lamp is actually 60% off, show it, and put the real range in the copy.
- 4. Drop the SMS, app, and "new year" offers from this send. Keep free shipping as a single thin line.
- 5. Add a deadline, such as "ends Monday", and star ratings on the products.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly sound: it has `lang="en"`, an XHTML doctype, MSO conditional comments and a mobile media query. Its confirmed problems are 10+ content images with no alt text, 11 non-HTTPS third-party tracking images, and a viewport that blocks zoom. The QA run couldn't verify the unsubscribe or authentication headers, and the HTML was truncated, so the body links and footer weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://` from Dotomi (Conversant) retargeting endpoints:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`

  Gmail's image proxy and other clients may block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources. The cookie-sync calls (`cookieredir`) will likely fail silently, and mixed-content warnings are possible.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** ten sequential `pubN.php` calls add 10 extra requests to every open. They're also a privacy and deliverability concern, since heavy third-party pixel counts can affect spam scoring.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/7d8d2cbe-…?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS and look fine. Both have no alt attribute, though they're pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 10 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. Their filenames are `2a7c98c6…`, `8196da5d…`, `72d6fcee…`, `a276fd05…`, `dc2357ae…`, `69f68417…`, `636dae64…`, `4f4cbd23…`, `65d9a6b8…` and `20185973…`. With images off, or in a screen reader, the user gets nothing or just the UUID filename. The email is image-heavy and the subject-line promo isn't recoverable as text, so this is the most significant rendering issue.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** the 13 tracking images should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so assistive tech skips them.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed. It's harmless but sloppy.
- **Cross-brand font hosts:** the `@font-face` rules for `HNW05` load from `www.crateandbarrel.com`. The font assets are hosted on a different domain from the sender's, which is a minor dependency risk.
- **`<title>`:** it reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME" rather than reflecting the email. Minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Per-recipient parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_mid`) are populated. This only covers the truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If they're really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link sit in the truncated portion, so neither can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 66 links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the QA output or the visible source. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 10 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com`, including the "UP TO 60% OFF LIGHTING" promo text.
2. Switch the 11 Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync chain.
3. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Capture raw headers directly from the MTA to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. The relay can't settle those.
6. Re-run QA against the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 66 redirect links with a followed-redirect check to validate UTMs and landing pages, and check the footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Clean up the leftover template comment.
