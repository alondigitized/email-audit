# Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards! Early Black Friday is here →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, offer-led Early Black Friday blast from Crate & Barrel. The hero is a living room photo with a red "it's on! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" panel and a single Shop Now button. Below it, a 2x2 grid covers entertaining, furniture, kitchen brands and holiday decor. A kitchen deals block then shows DeLonghi, KitchenAid and All-Clad with sale and regular prices. The offer is easy to find and the design is clean.
- The email is also long and generic. I count at least six distinct sections after the hero: kitchen deals, cookware, the Gift Shop, a second Rewards banner, a "we love these for you" product row, and a Design Desk / app promotion. The hero's Shop Now sits in a promo panel that crowds the top. That's the same "everything at once" pattern I flagged in the 2026-11-12 "best deals of the year" send.
- Judgement: a solid, safe promo that does its job. It's the fifth or so Early Black Friday message in two days, though, so it's more noise than news. Compared with the parent-testimonial Crate & Kids send earlier today, it has no hook beyond the discount.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer ("up to 30% off", "Earn 2x Rewards") with one clear Shop Now button.
- The Rewards callout, "last Double Rewards this year!", gives members a real reason to act now.
- The kitchen section shows concrete sale prices, such as the Sale $469.95 vs Reg. $899.99 espresso machine. That's far more persuasive than a percent-off banner.
- A deadline line at the top ("order by 11/18 … in time for Thanksgiving") makes the urgency believable.
- The layout renders cleanly: no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There are too many sections and CTAs. The email runs from hero to category grid to kitchen to cookware to Gift Shop to Rewards again to recommendations to app to Design Desk, and the eye has nowhere to settle.
- The Rewards message appears twice, and the "up to" language is repeated in several blocks.
- The category grid tiles use thin, small overlaid labels ("up to 30% off entertaining") that are hard to read.
- The Gift Shop banner and the Design Desk promo feel like leftover modules from other campaigns and dilute the sale message.
- The footer fine print is a dense wall of text, which weakens the "honest offer" read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to hero, category grid, one featured product row with prices, and a Rewards strip. Drop the Gift Shop, the app and Design Desk modules. That would help more than anything else.
- 2. Move the deadline into the hero: "Ends 11/18" next to the Shop Now button.
- 3. Make the category tiles bolder, with larger text and a "Shop furniture" or "Shop kitchen" verb on each, instead of a bare "up to X% off" label.
- 4. Lead with one hero product at its sale price, as in the espresso-machine example, to give the email a hook beyond the discount.
- 5. Cap Early Black Friday sends to one a day and vary the creative, so the 11/12 and 11/13 sends don't blur together.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 11/18 to get it before Thanksgiving. Furniture, kitchen & decor deals inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Espresso machines from $469.95, plus up to 30% off furniture.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The visible source is well-formed for a responsive, Outlook-aware table layout. The confirmed defects are missing `alt` attributes on many images and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The compliance, authentication and link-destination checks could not be completed. The HTML was truncated and the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Unverified destinations:** 72 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of them was probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` is a LiveRamp pixel fired five times. `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a17cec0f-…` are open or view trackers. Each one adds a request and a privacy and deliverability surface. The repeated rlcdn calls look redundant.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` is appended to the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. These are dynamic recommendation images, and each one depends on a live server at render time.
- No malformed or broken hrefs are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text, content images (confirmed by QA):** 16 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/…` have no `alt`. Examples are `6100bbef-5acf-4ae0-b55a-3b461f144b88.jpg` and `07b7830d-daa0-4b83-b0ec-f875e308e765.gif`. If these carry the offer or product content, image-blocking clients and screen readers get nothing.
- **Missing alt text, dynamic recommendation images:** Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`) have no `alt`. These are likely product tiles and need descriptive alt text.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** `rlcdn`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` have no `alt`. This is low impact for 1×1 pixels, but they should use `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup validity:**
  - The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is paired with an unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and several `<meta name="color-scheme">` and `<meta name="supported-color-schemes">` tags are not self-closed.
  - Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags are declared three or more times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv` charset declarations are redundant.
  - These are minor, since clients tolerate them.
- **Template remnant:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is left in the source. It doesn't render, but it shows an unfinished template.
- **Responsive CSS:** Media queries at `max-width:460px`, `min-width:640px` and `min-width:768px` are present. `img {max-width:640px}` and `img {max-width:600px}` overlap, which is harmless but redundant. The mobile breakpoint is unusually narrow at 460px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unrendered merge tokens were visible in the truncated source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters on image URLs are resolved per-recipient identifiers, not broken tokens. The rest of the body wasn't available, so this isn't fully verified.

## 5. Compliance

- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA tool itself says this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping them. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers before treating this as a defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, but that isn't proof of authentication.
- **Footer:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated portion of the HTML. They can't be confirmed or denied.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be assessed. All 72 tracking links were skipped, and the final redirect targets were not resolved. The subject promises "Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards", and nothing in the data shows whether the landing page reflects it.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 16 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and the seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` tiles. Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Consolidate the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Reduce the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` calls to one if they are redundant.
5. Re-run the audit with full raw headers, including `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, before concluding on compliance.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to confirm destinations and UTM parameters.
7. Run the HTML audit on the full, untruncated source to check the footer and the remaining images.
