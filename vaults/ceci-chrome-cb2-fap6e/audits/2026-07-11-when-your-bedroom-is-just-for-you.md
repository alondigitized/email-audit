# When your bedroom is just for you
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's bedroom-category send under the ongoing "Summer Edit" campaign — a logical follow-on to the July 9 "Up to 40% off is on now" email, now narrowing the lens to one room. The creative execution is excellent: warm, editorial, aspirational photography does most of the heavy lifting, individual bed frames are named and priced, and the hierarchy is clean from hero to product grid. The email looks good.
- The liability is entirely above the fold — in the inbox, before the open. The subject "When your bedroom is just for you" is a mood hook, not an offer hook. After nine days of explicit sale signals from CB2 (50% off, countdown timers, "TWO SALES END TONIGHT"), this subject offers nothing to grab a passive subscriber. The 40% off offer IS in the body — it's just invisible until you open.
- For a subscriber who is browsing-motivated and already warm on CB2, this works. For anyone scanning 40 unread emails on their phone, it's easy to skip.

## What's working

- **Campaign continuity is visible in the body.** "THE SUMMER EDIT" label anchors this to the existing campaign frame, giving it editorial legitimacy rather than feeling like a one-off.
- **Discount is stated up front.** "Up to 40% Off" is the first copy line after the hero image — no scroll required.
- **Named product cards with individual links.** FARA BED, PORTOLA BED, PLANO BED, ABBOT LINEN — each a separate entry point, not a generic "shop now" dump.
- **The photography is doing real work.** Warm neutral palette, aspirational but livable bedroom styling — signals quality without feeling cold.
- **Clean render, no bugs.** No broken images, no layout breaks, no lorem-ipsum visible anywhere.

## What's weak

- **Subject buries the offer.** "When your bedroom is just for you" is a mood line — there's no signal that 40% off is waiting inside. The body earns its open only if you already trust CB2 enough to click on feel.
- **No urgency or deadline visible.** The previous week ran credible countdowns; this email drops them entirely with no explanation. If the sale is still live, a deadline would add teeth.
- **"SHOP NOW" is the weakest possible CTA copy.** For a bedroom-specific email with a clear discount, "Shop Beds — Up to 40% Off" would out-click "SHOP NOW" meaningfully.
- **No social proof.** The July 9 send had the same gap — no reviews, no star ratings on individual product cards despite specific products being called out by name and price.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fold the offer into the subject line.** The body has 40% off — use it. The current subject wastes the biggest hook CB2 has.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your bedroom refresh: up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Beds, bedding, all of it — up to 40% off`
- 2. **Write a preheader that complements, not repeats.** The subject is a mood line; the preheader should flip to specifics.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fara, Portola, Plano — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Summer Edit is live in your bedroom`
- 3. **Change the CTA to a category + offer string.** Replace "SHOP NOW" with "Shop Beds — Up to 40% Off" on the primary button.
- 4. **Add a soft deadline.** Even "Sale ends Sunday" or a single-line timestamp under the hero would recover the urgency lost from the July 5–7 countdown emails.
- 5. **Add star ratings to the named product cards.** Names and prices are there — adding ratings would cross the social proof signal without any layout change.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta (confirmed)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Replace with `maximum-scale=5` or remove the constraint entirely.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `*` selector (confirmed)**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none; ...}
```
Suppresses OS-level font scaling for users with accessibility needs. Change to `100%` on the `body` rule and remove from the `*` wildcard.

**Mobile breakpoint set to `max-width: 418px` (confirmed)**
The `.mobile` class fires at ≤418px and forces `width: 414px`. Current flagship iPhones (15/16 series) have a 390–430px CSS viewport, making this breakpoint unreliable. Standard email practice is 480px or 600px.

**Duplicate `@font-face` declarations for `"Lineto Brown Sub"` (confirmed)**
Three separate `@font-face` blocks declare the same family name with different sources. While technically valid, this creates ambiguous resolution order across clients. Consolidate into one block with `src:` fallback list.
