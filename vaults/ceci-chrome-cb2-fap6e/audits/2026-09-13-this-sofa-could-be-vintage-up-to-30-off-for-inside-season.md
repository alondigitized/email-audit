# This sofa could be vintage + Up to 30% off for inside season
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- CB2 blends the "FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF" banner right into a decor-lookbook layout — Aldine chair and sofa, designer spotlight on David Grelli, new arrivals grid — so the actual offer gets buried under aesthetic storytelling. The subject line ("This sofa could be vintage + Up to 30% off for inside season") is doing double duty, promising both a design narrative and a discount, but the hero visually leads with "FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF" then immediately pivots into "EVERYTHING ELEVATED" / lookbook mode with no percentage callouts anywhere near the Aldine collection itself. It's a step up from yesterday's pure lookbook sends (2026-09-11 "NEW & ONLY AT CB2," 5.00/10) because there's at least a real sale banner up top, but the execution undercuts it by never reattaching the 30% off to any specific product below the fold.
- The "ENDS TONIGHT $5,000 REFRESH" sweepstakes block recycles the design-services hook from 2026-09-11's "NYC apartment" send, so this is the third time in three days CB2 has leaned on either a sweepstakes or a lookbook instead of a clean, sustained sale push. Judgment: a decent hero, diluted immediately by design-magazine framing and a sweepstakes distraction — solid visual craft, weak commercial focus.

## What's working

- Clean, unmissable top banner: "FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF" with a clear "SHOP NOW" button, no render bugs.
- Product photography is well-lit and consistent (Aldine chair/sofa, lighting fixtures, rugs) — on-brand and appetizing.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom shows specific products with real prices ($1,199.00, $349.00, $699.00, etc.) — concrete and shoppable.
- Designer spotlight (David Grelli) adds a credible provenance story that differentiates from a generic discount blast.

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer never reappears near any specific product — the Aldine chair/sofa section and "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" grid carry zero pricing or discount language.
- The "$5,000 REFRESH" sweepstakes mid-email is a distraction that pulls focus away from the sale, same tactic as 2026-09-11.
- Visual hierarchy dilutes fast: after the hero banner, the email shifts into a magazine-style lookbook with no reinforcing CTA back to the sale until the reader has scrolled past several unrelated sections.
- "SHOP NOW" appears twice with identical copy (hero and lighting section) — no differentiation on what's actually on sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Reinforce the 30% off badge directly on the Aldine Chair/Sofa product cards — right now shoppers have to guess if this bestselling collection is even included in the sale.
- 2. Cut or relocate the $5,000 Refresh sweepstakes to a separate send — it competes with the sale message rather than complementing it.
- 3. Replace generic "SHOP NOW" CTAs with specific copy tied to the sale, e.g. "Shop Aldine, 30% Off."
- 4. Add a price/discount line under at least one hero product to make the offer feel real and specific rather than abstract.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Aldine Sofa, 30% Off — Ends Tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Fall Sale Pick: Vintage-Look Sofa, 30% Off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swiss design meets Italian craft — shop the Aldine collection before the sale ends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: enter to win a $5,000 room refresh.`

# Technical Email Audit — CB2 "This sofa could be vintage" Campaign

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a standard MSO/Outlook-compatible table layout with vendor prefixes, but ships with one unresolved merge tag, several third-party tracking pixels loaded over plain HTTP, and no verifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers in the captured transmission.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unresolved merge token**: `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears raw in the HTML — this is an ESP personalization tag that failed to resolve before send, meaning a live customer would see this literal string instead of dynamic review content.
- **73 tracking/click-redirect links** (e.g., `mi.cb2.com`, `dv.cb2.com`) were excluded from HTTP probing per QA policy — not a confirmed defect, but destination integrity of these links is unverified and should be spot-checked manually.
- **Legacy ad-tech pixels over HTTP**: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, and 10 sequential beacons `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. These are non-HTTPS resources — most mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) will block or strip mixed-content/non-SSL image requests, meaning these tracking calls likely never fire in production.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 content images missing `alt` text**, all served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/...` (UUIDs listed in QA output). With images blocked by default in most mail clients, recipients get no fallback text, hurting both accessibility and scannability.
- The tracking pixels/UCM beacons and `o.gif` / `dv.cb2.com/o/...` beacon also lack `alt` attributes, though this is expected/inconsequential for 1x1 tracking pixels.
- No other structural rendering issues surfaced in the truncated source (MSO conditionals, `@font-face` fallbacks, and responsive `@media` blocks are present and correctly scoped).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **1 confirmed broken token**: `%%reviews_skulist%%` — raw, unresolved merge syntax indicates a review-widget/social-proof block dependent on an unpopulated ESP data feed. This is a functional defect, not cosmetic — flag for immediate fix before next send.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: Not found in the captured message. Note: QA flags this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving the header rather than the sender omitting it — cannot confirm root cause from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: Not found — if genuinely absent at origin, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) would not be available to recipients.
- **Authentication-Results header** (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): Not found — authentication status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm pass/fail, only that the header wasn't observed via the relay.
- **Recommendation**: These three findings should be re-verified against the raw origin SMTP transaction (not the relayed copy) before treating them as sender-side compliance gaps.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found — the truncated source did not expose enough CTA `href` values to verify UTM parameter presence or landing-page alignment. Recommend a follow-up pass against the full HTML with the actual CTA destination URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unresolved `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge tag before this template is reused — verify the review-feed data source is populated at send time.
2. Migrate the Dotomi ad pixels/UCM beacons to HTTPS or remove them if they're legacy/unused — they are silently failing in most modern clients as-is.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 flagged content images for accessibility and blocked-image fallback.
4. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly at the sending MTA (not via the AgentMail relay) to confirm whether these are real compliance gaps or artifacts of the relay capture.
5. Spot-check a sample of the 73 skipped tracking links manually to confirm they resolve correctly.
