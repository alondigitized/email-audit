# WAREHOUSE SALE IS ON
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-10**
## Executive summary

- The Warehouse Sale launch does its core job: new sale event, "UP TO 60% OFF" in the hero, category self-selection below, a sharp urgency module ("NOT SOLD OUT. YET."), and a personalized product grid at the bottom. Visually it's clean, warm, and on-brand CB2 — no complaints with the render.
- The bigger problem is context and subject execution. Yesterday's send was a panic close: "HOURS LEFT: Up to 40% off" — Midseason Sale is over. Today's send opens a brand-new Warehouse Sale at 60% off. Subscribers are watching the discount floor fall out in real time. The back-to-back cadence makes markdown feel permanent, which destroys the urgency both sends depend on.
- The subject "WAREHOUSE SALE IS ON" is all-caps and leaves the single most compelling hook — 60% off — buried below the fold of the inbox preview window instead of in the subject line. For a sale launch this significant, that's a meaningful miss.

## What's working

- **"UP TO 60% OFF" is hero-sized and immediately visible** — no hunting for the offer depth.
- **Category CTAs (Furniture / Lighting / Décor / Rugs / Shop All)** let shoppers route themselves directly to relevant departments — good click segmentation.
- **"NOT SOLD OUT. YET."** is the sharpest copy in the email — FOMO without a false claim, and it spotlights a real purchase barrier (availability).
- **"MORE FOR YOU" product grid** with visible pricing gives a concrete, personalized reason to click beyond the generic hero CTA.

## What's weak

- **ALL CAPS subject ("WAREHOUSE SALE IS ON")** reads as a shout and registers as a spam signal on trained eyes; discount depth is nowhere in the subject.
- **No sale deadline visible anywhere** — "IS ON" with no closing date gives subscribers every reason to wait until tomorrow, next week, or never.
- **"LEAVE US A REVIEW. WIN $100"** is placed mid-email between the hero and the personalized product grid, breaking the sale conversion flow at the worst possible moment.
- **Consecutive sale transitions erode credibility** — Midseason Sale panic-close yesterday, Warehouse Sale launch today; the urgency of both events suffers.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the subject with the discount.** "Up to 60% off: Warehouse Sale is live" — the number is the hook, not the sale name. Put it where it gets seen in the inbox before the open.
- 2. **Add a credible deadline** — even "Ends Sunday" or "This weekend only" in the hero copy converts the "I'll check later" reader into an "I should act now" reader.
- 3. **Move "LEAVE US A REVIEW" to the footer.** Review asks belong at the end of the conversion journey, not mid-email during an active sale push.
- 4. **Space the Midseason close and Warehouse launch by at least 48 hours.** One rest day preserves the distinctiveness — and urgency credibility — of each event.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` viewport blocks user zoom** (WCAG 1.4.4 risk)
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents low-vision users from scaling the email on mobile. `maximum-scale=1` should be removed.

**Global `*` text-size-adjust reset is overly broad**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; }
```
Applied to all elements rather than `body` only. Breaks text scaling in Outlook Mobile and some webmail clients. Scope to `body` only.

**Duplicate `@font-face` for `"Lineto Brown Sub"` with conflicting sources**
Three separate blocks declare the same family name — one loads only `.woff`, two others load `.woff2` with `font-weight: 400` and `font-weight: 600` respectively. Email clients without `@font-face` weight-descriptor support (e.g., older Outlook) will use the last-declared block for all weights, potentially rendering all text at bold or light weight.

**Double-slash path in multiple font URLs**
```css
src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2")
src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownRegular/lineto-brown-regular.woff2")
src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/GTSectra/GT-Sectra-Display-Regular.woff2")
```
`//` after the domain is technically malformed. Most servers normalize it, but CDN rules and strict caches may 404. Remove the extra slash.

**HNW05 font loads cross-domain from `crateandbarrel.com`**
```css

```
Four HNW05 weight variants depend on a sibling-brand CDN. If that domain path changes or goes down, all HNW05 text falls back silently. Should be mirrored to `cb2.com` CDN.
