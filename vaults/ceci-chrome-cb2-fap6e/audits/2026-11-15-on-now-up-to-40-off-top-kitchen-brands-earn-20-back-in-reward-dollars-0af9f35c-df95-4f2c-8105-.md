# ON NOW! Up to 40% off top kitchen brands + Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, offer-led Thanksgiving-kitchen blast, and the theme is clear. Each block pairs one brand category with a percent-off headline: cookware at 35% off, Breville ovens at 20% off, KitchenAid mixers at up to $200 off, bakeware at 40% off, Carawy. The subject and the body match, which is good. The retailer's last Double Rewards of the year is mentioned twice. Once is in the top strip, and once is in the red panel near the bottom.
- It's also a very long email, about 12,000px tall, with roughly eight "Shop Now" buttons. The hero is a white hand mixer on a dark background with "up to 40% off effortless Thanksgiving prep". It's attractive, but the 40% claim is a ceiling and the product is unnamed. Compared with the 2026-11-14 sends, such as the Double Rewards blast ("ON NOW! The last Double Rewards of the year!"), this is a near-repeat. The subject uses the same "ON NOW!" opener and the same Rewards message, applied to kitchen.
- Verdict: a solid, on-theme send that fatigues the reader. Kitchen focus and the order-by-11/18 Thanksgiving deadline make it relevant, but it adds little new beyond the prior day's emails. One caveat: the screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print or product names.

## What's working

- The kitchen theme is consistent from subject to hero to every module, so the email stays on one topic.
- A Thanksgiving delivery deadline sits in the top strip, which gives credible urgency.
- Each section has a bold lowercase headline with a specific offer, such as "starts today! 20% off Breville Smart Ovens" and "up to $200 off select KitchenAid stand mixers".
- Large, uncluttered lifestyle photography (roast turkey, oven, stand mixer) makes each block quick to scan.
- Reward Dollars and Double Rewards appear in the top strip and again in a red panel mid-email, so members see the loyalty benefit.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is far too long, with about eight "Shop Now" buttons that all say the same thing. The eye has nowhere to land.
- Almost every offer is "up to X%" or "select", which makes the real savings vague.
- The hero shows no named product. The headline is lifestyle copy, and the offer sits below the image.
- The "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars!" promise in the subject is only a small text strip at the top, so the payoff for the second half of the subject is easy to miss.
- This is the third or fourth Rewards or Early Black Friday send in two days, including 2026-11-14's "The best deals of the year: 2x Rewards + Up to 50% off". The cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the three strongest modules: the hero, mixers and ovens, then one Rewards panel. Use one primary CTA, and make the rest text links.
- 2. Put the Rewards offer in the hero itself, with one named product and the actual sale price. Move the "order by 11/18" deadline to a banner directly above the hero.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific labels, such as "Shop KitchenAid mixers" or "Shop ovens".
- 4. Space out the Rewards-led sends, or segment so kitchen-intent subscribers get this one and others get a different message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off kitchen brands + 20% back in Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Thanksgiving prep: ovens & mixers up to $200 off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 11/18 to get it before Thanksgiving. Breville, KitchenAid, Le Creuset and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year ends soon. Earn 20% back on top kitchen brands.`
