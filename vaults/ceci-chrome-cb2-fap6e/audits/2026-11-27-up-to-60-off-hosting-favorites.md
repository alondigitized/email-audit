# UP TO 60% OFF HOSTING FAVORITES
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's standard Black Friday template again, now framed around entertaining. The hero shows amber and pink glassware under "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 60% OFF‡ / ENTERTAINING (AND IT ALL SHIPS FREE)". It has one clear SHOP DRINKWARE button. The "Extended store hours" banner above it is a nice, honest touch for the day itself.
- The email then falls apart into a long catalog of category tiles: Furniture, Holiday & Gifting, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors and Entertaining. After that come two more offers, free shipping on $99+ and "Save 15% off in the new year", plus an app and rewards block. Several offers and about eight CTAs compete for attention. It's the fifth or sixth near-identical Black Friday send in three days (see "THE SALE OF THE YEAR IS HERE" and "So many pillows & throws" earlier today). The subject is the most specific in the run, since it names a category, but the body doesn't pay it off. The Entertaining tile sits near the bottom, and the hero never says "hosting."
- Verdict: a polished, premium email that is fine but forgettable. It's a good template with a decent subject, and subscribers are likely suffering fatigue.

## What's working

- The hero has a single, readable offer: "UP TO 60% OFF" with a visible SHOP DRINKWARE button.
- The glassware imagery is rich, on-brand and seasonal, and it fits the "hosting" theme.
- "Extended store hours, Friday 11/27: 9AM–9PM" adds credible same-day urgency.
- "Free shipping on orders of $99 or more" is clear and stands out in a large type band.
- The category tiles are visually consistent and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero only points to drinkware, and the subject promises "hosting favorites". Nothing in the body says "hosting."
- The email runs very long, with six category tiles and four or five CTAs, so the hero offer gets diluted.
- The "‡" footnote and the dense legal block hide the fine print. "Up to" 60% on an unspecified subset is vague.
- The "Save 15% off in the New Year" block muddies the offer. It is a future promo placed next to today's sale.
- The category tiles have no prices or product detail, and the email shows no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email match its subject. Build a hosting-themed hero with a product grid of glasses, serveware and tablescapes, each showing a "from $X" price. Trim the six generic category tiles to two or three.
- 2. Cut the secondary offers (the 15% New Year block, the app block and the gift-list block) so one offer leads.
- 3. State the offer precisely, such as "Up to 60% off drinkware, serveware & decor," and put it near the top of the footnote.
- 4. Give the CTA a verb and a noun, e.g. "Shop hosting deals" instead of just "Shop drinkware."
- 5. Vary the template. Subscribers have seen the same Black Friday layout for three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Hosting deals, up to 60% off — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Glassware & serveware from $12`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on $99+. Stores open 9AM–9PM today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Set the table for less: drinkware, dinnerware, and decor.`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "UP TO 60% OFF HOSTING FAVORITES"

## 1. Technical Summary
The 57% QA pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings) comes mostly from missing `alt` attributes and 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, not failed. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped the HTTP check on click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **11 insecure pixels.** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` load over plain HTTP. Clients such as Gmail's image proxy and Apple Mail Privacy Protection may block or rewrite them. The ten `pub*.php` pixels are third-party cookie-sync calls.
- **Other pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3379358d-...?mi_cid=...` use HTTPS and are first-party-domain tracking pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on 11 content images.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: `978d7bb3…`, `7cbdbace…`, `d5dc8d2d…`, `dc9c96d0…`, `a5e97093…`, `4a9531bb…`, `ef87a1dc…`, `4f1a724a…`, `310da783…`, `e68a523c…`, `20185973…`. If these are the content or CTA images, the email is unreadable with images off or to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels.** The 13 pixels above (dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`) also lack `alt`. They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.

No issues found. The visible markup has no unresolved merge tokens. The `mi_u=` and `mi_cid=` values on the tracking pixels look like resolved identifiers.
