# Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year! Shop Black Friday now →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth Crate & Barrel Black Friday send I've audited in about 48 hours. It follows "Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →" and "Huge news: NEW ITEMS hit our Black Friday sale!". The offer is the same, "Up to 50% off + free shipping", under an espresso-machine hero with a "Shop Now" button. Below it is a long scroll of category tiles, kitchen deals, a Design Desk promo, product picks, a card promo and an app promo.
- The render is 680x11024px and I could only view it heavily downscaled. I can read the layout and the big headlines. I can't read the fine print, most prices or the preheader, so details below are limited to what is legible.
- The email is competent and clear but not distinctive. The offer and hero are clean, and "hours left to get it by Thanksgiving" gives credible urgency. But the subject's "biggest sale of the year" claim has been repeated in nearly every send this week, so it lands as noise. The email is also very long, with at least eight distinct modules, and the hero is a kitchen appliance rather than furniture.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "Up to 50% off + free shipping", sits at the top with one obvious "Shop Now" button.
- A shipping-deadline strip above the hero ("hours left to get it by Thanksgiving") gives credible, time-bound urgency.
- The category grid ("up to 40% off sofas & sectionals", "up to 50% off furniture", etc.) lets a shopper jump straight to what they want.
- The red "Big news: New items just hit our Black Friday sale" band breaks up the scroll and repeats the offer.
- I saw no broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage.

## What's weak

- Length is the main problem. Roughly 11,000px of scroll is too much, and the main promo repeats in a clearance block, a kitchen block and a product-picks block.
- The hero is an espresso machine, while the subject sells the whole sale. A furniture or home shopper gets no match until the grid below.
- The subject is about 67 characters and will truncate on mobile. "Biggest sale of the year" is also the claim of every send this week.
- The closing fine print is a dense wall of text, which works against "no buried fine print".
- The Design Desk, credit card and app promos each ask for a different action and compete with the main sale CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid, one featured-deal block and a footer. Move the Design Desk, card and app promos to non-sale sends.
- 2. Make the hero match the segment. Show furniture or a room scene for home and furniture subscribers instead of a countertop appliance.
- 3. Sharpen the CTA copy. Use "Shop Black Friday Deals" or "Shop Sofas up to 40% off" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Put the shipping deadline in the subject or preheader, since it is the one thing that is new each day.
- 5. Cut the cadence. Several near-identical Black Friday sends a day trains people to ignore them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off + free shipping, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday furniture deals are live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 11 PM for delivery by Thanksgiving. Sofas, rugs and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just added: up to 40% off sofas and sectionals.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Crate & Barrel's Black Friday email is built on a Marigold/Mapp-style template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`). The visible source has several template-hygiene and accessibility defects: roughly 40 images have no alt text, and the viewport and text-size settings are restrictive. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, and the HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **83 links were not probed.** The QA flagged them as tracking or click-redirect domains and skipped them. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…&n=1` through `n=5`. `rlcdn.com` is LiveRamp. Five calls with only `n` incrementing look like redundant syncs. Each adds a third-party request and a privacy surface, and none has alt text.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` parameter appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. The same hash appears in the `rlcdn.com` `s=` parameter, so the recipient identifier is passed to a third party.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/aec03822-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` serve as open pixels. They are expected, but they have no alt, and the second has no file extension.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 40 images.** About 30 are content or product images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png` and `c65ab55e-….gif`. Another 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`. Pixel and tracking images should use `alt=""` explicitly. With images blocked, the content images render as blank space.
- **Zoom is blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `body` rule and the `*{…}` selector. This conflicts with the earlier `100%` values in the same sheet.
- **Invalid markup for the declared doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - It has both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and HTML5 `<meta charset>`.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, with conflicting values.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (misspelled, never removed). There is also an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
- **A dead CSS rule.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, which also matches at 768px and wider.
- **Redundant style blocks:** `.ReadMsgBody`, `.ExternalClass` and `body` rules are declared twice, which adds weight to the head.
- **The title is the brand name only.** It does not match the subject line.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is not confirmed for the full message.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** (QA WARN).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN).
- **Authentication-Results not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown (QA WARN).
- **Not confirmed as sender defects.** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. They are unverified, not proven missing.
- **Footer not checked:** the body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source.
- **From address:** `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. All 83 links go through tracking redirects that were not resolved. No UTM parameters or final landing URLs are visible in the truncated source. The sender's tracking appears to use `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`, not UTMs, but that is only seen on the open pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.…/o/…`, `mi…/p/rp/*.png`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Escape the ampersand in the title (`&amp;`), and drop the duplicate charset and `format-detection` metas and the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls into one (or confirm why they are needed), and review sharing the `mi_u` hash with a third party.
5. Fix the media-query ordering so the intended `img` max-width is applied.
6. For the next QA pass, capture the raw message headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) from the source mailbox, not the relay. Also run the audit on the full HTML and resolve a sample of the 83 tracked links to check destinations and UTMs.
