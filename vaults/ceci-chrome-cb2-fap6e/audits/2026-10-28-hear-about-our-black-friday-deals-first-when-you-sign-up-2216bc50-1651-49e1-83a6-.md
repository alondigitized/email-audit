# Hear about our Black Friday deals first when you sign up →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a text-signup acquisition email dressed up as a catalog. The hero says "get Black Friday deals first" over a blurred bokeh tree. The offer is "Sign up for texts now & take 10% off your entire next order—including holiday gifts!" It has two buttons, "Sign Up Now" and "Shop Now". The hero is clear, but the offer is a modest 10% with a texting commitment attached. Everything after it dilutes the message.
- Below the hero the email turns into the usual Crate & Barrel Kids scroll. It has a holiday block, a six-product grid, a credit card promo, an app download block, a TheDesignDesk appointment block and a store locator. That is six or seven competing asks. The hero's one idea gets buried, and the 10% disclaimer in the footer is long and dense.
- Verdict: a clear subject and hero that are undermined by an unfocused body. It is a decent list-growth send, not a strong sales email. The pitch is also weak for the 2026-10-28 audience, who got the "TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!" send the same day. This one asks for a text opt-in for 10% off when a 20% deal is already on offer.

## What's working

- The hero headline "get Black Friday deals first" matches the subject and is easy to read.
- The offer is concrete: 10% off the next order, including holiday gifts.
- The two stacked buttons are large and legible.
- The product grid shows real items with prices, such as the Nuna PIPA stroller at $1350.00 and the pumpkin treat bag at $14.97 (marked down from $34.95).
- The brand and nav are consistent, and the footer category links are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The "Sign Up Now" and "Shop Now" buttons compete, and "Shop Now" has no clear destination or purpose.
- The hero photo is a blurred tree with no product, so it doesn't show what the deals will be.
- The body has too many unrelated asks: the credit card, the app, TheDesignDesk and the store locator all sit below the hero.
- The product grid is generic ("we love these for you") and mixes a Halloween treat bag, strollers, a recliner, a dresser and a crib mattress. Halloween content is stale on Oct 28 for a Black Friday email.
- The 10% offer has long fine print in the footer, and the discount is exclusionary ("full-priced items only").

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero plus one product row, and move the card, app and TheDesignDesk blocks to other sends. A signup email should have one ask.
- 2. Make the hero state the reward and the mechanism: "10% off now. Black Friday deals first." Then use one button, "Text Me 10% Off", and drop "Shop Now".
- 3. Replace the Halloween bag with holiday gifts and group the grid by gift idea (toys, nursery, gear) with a clear "Shop Gifts" button.
- 4. Put the key terms (full-price items only, one use, texting opt-in) in a short line under the CTA instead of burying them in the footer.
- 5. Use a hero image with product in it, such as a gift pile or a nursery, in place of the blurred tree.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Get 10% off + Black Friday deals first`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 10% off is waiting (text to claim)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Join our texts and save 10% on your next order, gifts included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Early access to Black Friday for kids, nursery and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML email with a typical Movable Ink (MI) setup. Its main confirmed defects are about 20 images with no `alt` attribute, including the SMS sign-up CTA graphic, and a viewport that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer and body links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **62 tracking links not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and UTM handling are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five identical `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels**, differing only by `&n=1` to `&n=5`. These are third-party LiveRamp pixels, and the repeats add five extra requests per open.
- **Hashed subscriber ID in image URLs.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=56aaa278…` is repeated six times, and the `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` URLs carry the same `mi_u` value. The ID is a hash, but it still appears in every image URL, so forwarding the email exposes it. It is also sent to rlcdn.com in `s=56aaa278…`, which shares the identifier with a third party.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images without `alt`** (confirmed by QA):
  - `2026%5F1028%5FCBK%5FSMS%5FSignUp%5FPM.gif` (scene7). This is the SMS sign-up CTA, so with images off the subject's call to action disappears.
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `a55e00cd-0dab-4be0-898b-2774b9a04b33.jpg`
  - `480e84b4-9e90-4785-bf94-73277cc7da30.jpg`
  - `505438f2-5fee-4d03-89da-d510903ea9f7.jpg`
  - `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`
  - `cd2cef02-53e0-421d-8405-3fab2d942e82.gif`
- **Spacers and pixels with no `alt`.** `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px`, `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`, `o.gif`, the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `rlcdn` pixels, and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` image should use `alt=""` (plus `role="presentation"`). Screen readers may otherwise read out the filenames.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text-size locking.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rules stop users from resizing text on iOS.
- **Unescaped `&` in `<title>`.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` under an XHTML 1.0 doctype should be `&amp;`. It is minor, and browsers and clients tolerate it.
- **Template leftovers.** The source still contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, the empty `<!---->` and `<!--<![endif]-->` blocks, and duplicate `format-detection` meta tags. They are harmless but add weight.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unverified**. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be confirmed. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical address and visible unsubscribe link fall in the truncated part of the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 62 links go through tracking redirects that were skipped, so UTM parameters and the landing page for the Black Friday and SMS sign-up CTA can't be checked. No evidence of a mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven content images, starting with the SMS sign-up GIF. Add `alt=""` to the spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag and stop disabling text-size adjustment globally.
3. Re-run the audit against the original raw message (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
4. Resolve the tracking-link redirects (follow redirects with a GET request) to check UTM parameters and landing pages.
5. Reduce the five duplicate `rlcdn` pixels and the repeated `mi_u` image URLs where possible, and check the privacy impact of passing `mi_u` to a third party.
6. Clean up the template leftovers (the `IMPUT HERE` comment, duplicate meta tags) and escape `&` in the title.
