# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "Your holiday traditions, our festive new decor"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with a Mobile Instruments (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) tracking layer. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed issues are missing alt text on about 50 images, a viewport that blocks zoom, and unverifiable compliance headers. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and link `href`s could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** QA skipped 94 links as tracking/click-redirect domains and did not probe them. Their destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` beacons are present, each carrying the same hashed recipient token. Five identical beacons look like redundant firing. They also add third-party requests and a privacy surface.
- **Open/impression pixels.** These are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/cf1e4f62-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. They are expected, but they are not 1×1 alt-suppressed, as noted below.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA warnings).** About 50 images are flagged.
  - Content images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif` and are filename-only, for example `a6067ac2-…jpg`.
  - The hero is `scene7.com/…/2026_1016_CB_HolidayDecor_Grid_hero.gif`.
  - Seven dynamic-content PNGs are served from `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - The first content image is `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - If these are linked product or tile images, they have no accessible name, and image-blocking clients show nothing.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for alt.** `448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and `dv…/cf1e4f62-…` should carry `alt=""` explicitly. This is low severity, and it is only a defect if the attribute is absent.
- **Zoom restriction.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template residue.**
  - A leftover comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the source.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` separately). The duplicates are redundant but harmless.
- **Global resets.** `*{…line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply globally. This is fragile, but no concrete breakage is confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` hash is passed in image and pixel URLs and appears to be a rendered recipient identifier. The dynamic `p/rp/*.png` images suggest recommendation personalization, and they lack alt text (see section 3).

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** This may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, per QA. It is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN).** The RFC 8058 one-click header is not detected. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect it, but the relay may have stripped it.
- **`Authentication-Results` absent (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not inspected.** The physical address and the visible unsubscribe link fall in the truncated portion, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Cannot be assessed. All 94 tracked links were skipped, and the source was truncated before any `href`s were visible. No UTM parameters were observed, so I make no claim about their presence or absence. Click-redirect resolution is needed to check landing page alignment.
