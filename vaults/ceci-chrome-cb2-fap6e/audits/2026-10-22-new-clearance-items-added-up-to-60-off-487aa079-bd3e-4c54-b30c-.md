# NEW clearance items added: up to 60% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance send that leads well and then loses focus. The hero is a fall-leaves image with "up to 60% off clearance" and a single "Shop Clearance" button. Below it is a 2x3 grid of category links: Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, and "Shop 40%–60% Off Final Sale". The subject and hero match. Anyone who wants a deal knows what to do.
- Past that, the email turns into a catalog of unrelated pitches. Free shipping on toys over $99, The Design Desk (twice), a Halloween shop, a "we love these for you" product grid, the Crate Card, and an app download all compete for attention. Several of these are not clearance. The product grid shows full-price strollers, a $1,499 dresser and a $369 crib mattress, with no markdown pricing. The "new clearance items added" promise is never shown as actual clearance products. It's competent branding, but it's a diluted send.
- Judgement: a good subject and hero, but the body doesn't deliver on them. Compared with the Oct 21 "ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!" send, which held one offer, this one carries four or five.

## What's working

- The hero offer, "up to 60% off clearance", sits above the fold with a clear "Shop Clearance" button.
- The subject and hero copy match, so there's no bait-and-switch feel.
- The category grid under the hero lets a shopper jump straight to furniture, rugs, kids bedding or baby bedding.
- The "selling fast! the halloween shop" banner adds timely seasonal urgency.
- The visual style is warm and consistent with the brand, and I see no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The hero image is autumn leaves with no product, so the clearance promise has nothing concrete behind it.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows full-price items, with one strikethrough on the pumpkin treat bag. It undermines the "new clearance" premise.
- The Design Desk appears twice, plus free shipping, the Crate Card and the app. This is a lot of competing CTAs.
- There are four small "Shop…" buttons in the grid and several grey, low-contrast buttons ("Shop All Toys", "Shop the Look"), so the hierarchy is weak below the hero.
- The fine print is a dense wall of text, and the "final sale" language is only discoverable in the grid label.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the leaf hero with real clearance products showing sale prices, such as a crib, a rug and a chair with "was/now" pricing.
- 2. Fill the product grid with clearance items only, with strikethrough prices and the percent off on each.
- 3. Cut The Design Desk to one placement and drop the Crate Card and app blocks from clearance sends.
- 4. Make the "Final Sale" status explicit near the hero so it doesn't feel buried.
- 5. Darken the secondary buttons for contrast.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New markdowns: up to 60% off nursery & kids`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: clearance cribs, rugs & dressers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Final-sale prices on strollers, bedding and furniture. While they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fresh markdowns just dropped, and the best sizes go first.`
