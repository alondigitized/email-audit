# NEW DEALS just added: up to 50% off Black Friday →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth or so Crate & Barrel Black Friday send in about 48 hours. The offer hasn't changed since "Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year!" on 11/23. The only real change is the "NEW DEALS" framing, which echoes the 11/22 "Huge news: NEW ITEMS hit our Black Friday sale!" The hero is clear. It has a "HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving" strip, then "new deals added! up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" with one Shop Now button.
- Below the hero the email is a very long scroll. I could only see it heavily downscaled (113px wide on screen, 680px original, about 12,000px tall). I can make out the layout and the larger headlines but not the fine print or most prices. A 2x4 category grid of "up to 40–60% off" tiles comes first, then a 60% off clearance banner, a Thanksgiving tablescape block, a kitchen-deals section, cookware and coffee-maker promos, and a TheDesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes. Product picks, an app download and a footer follow. The email tries to do too much, and the first screen has to carry everything.
- My judgment: it is a competent but fatigued blast. A subscriber who got four near-identical sales emails yesterday has little reason to open this one, and the "new deals" claim is unconvincing when the offer is identical.

## What's working

- The hero states one clear offer ("up to 50% off + free shipping") with a single Shop Now button above the fold.
- The "HOURS LEFT to get it by Thanksgiving" strip adds credible, time-bound urgency.
- The category grid with per-tile discounts ("up to 60% off sofas", "up to 40% off rugs") lets shoppers go straight to their category.
- The Thanksgiving hosting and tablescape section matches the season.
- The photography is consistent, warm and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with about eight sections after the hero. Most readers will never reach the lower half.
- Several competing offers dilute the hero. These include 60% off clearance, up to 20% off drinkware, up to 40% off kitchen, up to 35% off cookware, and the $5000 sweepstakes.
- The "NEW DEALS" framing isn't backed by anything visibly new in the body, so it reads as recycled.
- The sweepstakes and app promos near the bottom add noise and look like leftover modules.
- The cadence is heavy: this is another near-identical Black Friday send within two days of the others.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid and one deadline module. Move the sweepstakes and app promo to a separate send.
- 2. Make "new" real. Lead with three or four actual new-arrival deals with prices, so the subject isn't a rerun of the 11/22 "NEW ITEMS" email.
- 3. Consolidate the cookware, coffee and kitchen blocks into one "Kitchen deals" section.
- 4. Suppress or segment recent clickers to cut send fatigue.
- 5. Put a live countdown or a specific cutoff time in the hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs and cookware added. Free shipping ends Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel "NEW DEALS just added: up to 50% off Black Friday →"

**Coverage caveat:** The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer, merge tokens and link hrefs weren't visible. The 83 tracking links were also not probed. Findings below come only from the visible `<head>` and the automated QA output.

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-built (Marigold/Sailthru-style "MI") XHTML table layout with 83 redirect-tracked links. The only confirmed defects are about 42 images without alt text and a few head-level markup issues. The compliance and authentication warnings may be artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed from this data.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 83 tracking links were skipped because they are redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party identifier sync:** The per-recipient token `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` appears in the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. The same hash prefix appears as `s=56aaa2787a…640cfd8` in five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1..5` pixels. rlcdn.com is a LiveRamp domain, so this is likely a recipient identifier being passed to a third party. It is confirmed in the URLs, but its purpose is an inference.
- **Per-recipient tokens in URLs:** `mi_u` appears on `o.gif` and `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b25ba48f-…`. These are normal open tracking and are not defects.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** At least 42 `<img>` tags are flagged, and the QA list looks truncated at the end. About 35 are content images:
  - 27 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, including `bbf6f9d4-…png` and the GIFs `32d7f83e-…`, `631a8d87-…` and `07b7830d-…`.
  - 7 on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, which look like dynamic product or recommendation images.
  - `971aa10f-…jpg`.
- **Tracking pixels:** The remaining 7 are pixels (`rlcdn 448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv…/o/b25ba4…`). Missing alt there is harmless, but they should use `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops users from pinch-zooming, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup for the doctype:** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, but:
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible" …>` are not self-closed.
- **Redundant head tags:** There are duplicate charset declarations, plus three `format-detection` meta tags (one combined and two separate, `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`).
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match and the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule never takes effect.
- **Global overrides:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` can override text sizing and alignment elsewhere in the email. This is minor.
- **Positive:** `<html lang="en">` is set, and the MSO/Outlook conditional blocks are present.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the head or QA output. The body wasn't visible, so tokens in the body, footer or links can't be ruled out.

### 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** The QA flagged this as a WARN, noting the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. The header is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** The same relay caveat applies to one-click unsubscribe support.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed from this capture.
- **Sender alignment looks consistent:** The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image domain `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not visible:** The unsubscribe link, physical postal address and preference-center link couldn't be checked because the source was truncated.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The destination URLs and UTM parameters sit behind the 83 skipped redirect links, and the visible source has no resolvable hrefs. Landing-page alignment is unknown.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text:** Give the ~35 content images descriptive alt text (e.g. offer text or product names) and use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. **Allow zoom:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Clean the head:** Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, self-close meta tags per XHTML, deduplicate `charset` and `format-detection`, and drop the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. **Fix the `img` max-width media queries:** Remove the dead 600px rule or reorder them.
5. **Re-run the audit with the full raw message:** Capture the original headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`) and the untruncated HTML. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to verify landing URLs, UTMs and the footer. Until then, treat the compliance and authentication items as unverified, not failures.
6. **Review the LiveRamp sync:** Check the `mi_u`/`s=` hash sharing against the sender's privacy policy and consent basis.
