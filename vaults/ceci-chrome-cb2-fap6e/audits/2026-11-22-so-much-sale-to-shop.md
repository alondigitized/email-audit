# SO MUCH SALE TO SHOP
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the weakest of CB2's recent Black Friday sends. The hero is competent: "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 60% OFF" over a moody burgundy velvet room, with a stack of category buttons. But it repeats the Nov 19 "cult favorites" and Nov 21 "back-in-stock" emails almost exactly. Those at least had a hero product or a reason to click. This one has no product, no deadline and no news.
- The subject, "SO MUCH SALE TO SHOP," tells the reader nothing the sender name doesn't already say. Below the hero, the email splits attention across a 15% New Year coupon, a $5,000 design-appointment sweepstakes, an app promo, a credit card, a gift registry and a text signup. That's six secondary asks under a 60%-off headline. The email reads as a catch-all template, not a campaign.
- Verdict: on-brand and clean, but redundant. The offer is real, though undercut by dense fine print and a vague subject, and nothing here would pull a subscriber who ignored the last three sends.

## What's working

- The hero hierarchy is clear. "BLACK FRIDAY SALE" and "UP TO 60% OFF" sit at the top, with the category buttons directly beneath.
- The category buttons (Furniture, Holiday & Gifting, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Shop All) let a shopper jump straight to what they want.
- The photography is warm and cohesive, and consistent with the Nov 19 and Nov 21 sends.
- The rewards callout ("EARN 10% BACK" in Rewards Dollars) gives members a reason to shop now.
- There are no visible render bugs. Text is legible and no images are broken.

## What's weak

- The subject line is generic and has no number, product or deadline. It sounds like filler.
- There's no end date on the sale. "Up to 60%" with a dagger and no urgency gives no reason to act today.
- The "SAVE 15% OFF in the New Year" band is a coupon for a future purchase. It dilutes the main offer and reads as a bait-style add-on, and the fine print shows it has conditions.
- The $5,000 sweepstakes and design-appointment block takes up a lot of space. Its caption crosses busy photo detail, and it pulls people away from shopping the sale.
- The footer has five or six competing CTAs (app, card, registry, text signup) plus a wall of dense legal text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one job, which is the sale. Drop or move the sweepstakes, and add a hero product row with 3–4 named items and sale prices. This is the biggest single lift.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, for example "Ends Monday 11:59 PM." Fix the "UP TO" claim by showing the typical discount alongside it.
- 3. Pull the New Year coupon out of the body, or make it a clearly labeled stacking bonus.
- 4. Drop the app, card and registry tiles from sale sends, or trim them to one line.
- 5. Space out the sends. Seven emails in about three days with the same 60% creative trains people to ignore the brand.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has solid template scaffolding (XHTML doctype, `lang="en"`, MSO/VML conditionals, responsive media queries). It also has about 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, 9 content images without alt text, and a viewport setting that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive, not failed. The QA run itself shows 0 hard issues and 3 warnings (57% pass rate).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **59 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **11 third-party pixels load over plain `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - These are Dotomi/Conversant cookie-sync pixels. Clients that proxy or upgrade images (Gmail, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may block them, so the syncs likely fail silently. They are also a mixed-content and privacy exposure.
- **First-party open pixels are HTTPS and fine:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/f978c9e7-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **The `mi_u=` parameter is a 64-hex-character value** (SHA-256 shape) embedded in the pixel URL. It is a hashed recipient identifier, so the exposure depends on what it hashes.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text.** With images off, those blocks render blank. The filenames are:
  - `f4ceedb1-…`
  - `3d2d3ada-…`
  - `68456c7c-…`
  - `a9208f38-…`
  - `d050269b-…`
  - `f0bf763d-…`
  - `82f7373e-…`
  - `20185973-…`
  - `e4289cf9-…`
- **All tracking pixels also lack alt attributes:** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel, `UCMController` and `pub1`–`pub10`. Screen readers may announce the filenames. Pixels should carry `alt=""`, plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`.** This disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `*` rule and on `body`.** It prevents user text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. The `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block is also empty.
- **Some web font URLs contain a double slash** (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). They likely resolve, but they are unclean.
- Web font fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) are present, so there is no rendering risk from font loading.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The HTML was truncated, so the body could not be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules for senders over 5,000 messages a day require both.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is a good sign.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not verifiable** because the source was truncated before the footer. Confirm both in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 59 tracking links were skipped and the truncated source shows no resolved landing URLs or UTM parameters. The redirect target of each link needs to be resolved to check campaign tagging and the landing page for "SO MUCH SALE TO SHOP".

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com`, `https://ads.dotomi.com`), or drop the 10 cookie-sync pixels, which add little in an email context.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images,** and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta** and reconsider `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
4. **Clean up the template leftovers:** the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the empty `!mso` block.
5. **Re-run the header checks on raw MIME** from a direct (non-relay) delivery to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. **Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML** with redirect resolution enabled. That would cover the footer unsubscribe and address, the UTM parameters and the landing page.
