# Hours left for up to 40% off
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a same-day follow-up to the "UP TO 40% OFF ENDS TODAY" email CB2 already sent this morning — the brand history confirms it. The creative is nearly identical: same Summer Edit sale hero, same "ENDS TODAY" urgency frame, same stacked-module structure. The subject line swap ("Hours left…") is the only meaningful variation, and it's a smart one — time-bounded countdown language in the subject is more specific than "ENDS TODAY" was in yesterday's hero copy.
- The problem is message dilution inside the email itself. Three competing offer modules — Summer Edit 40% off, Outdoor Collection 50%+, and the $5,000 Refresh design appointment pitch — share the scroll. That last one has no place in a last-chance clearance email. The "$5,000 Refresh" module was the entire premise of the July 21 "EXPERT DESIGN, ON US" send; embedding it here bleeds urgency and makes the email feel assembled from parts rather than written with intent.
- The personalized "MORE FOR YOU" product grid at the bottom is the strongest loyalty signal in the email. It should be higher up. For a second send on the same day, the burden of proof on relevance is high — the grid is what earns a second look.

## What's working

- **"Ends Today" + countdown subject pairing** — "Hours left" in the subject directly acknowledges that this is a closing-window email, which makes the second send feel intentional rather than redundant.
- **Hero hierarchy is clean** — ENDS TODAY / SUMMER EDIT / 40% OFF reads instantly; no ambiguity about what this email is for.
- **Outdoor 50%+ is a harder offer than 40%** — surfacing a deeper outdoor deal as a second module is legitimate upsell logic.
- **"MORE FOR YOU" personalized grid** — product-level personalization is the strongest retention argument CB2 has; it's here and it's visible.
- **No render bugs** — layout is clean, images load, CTAs are crisp buttons.

## What's weak

- **Double-send on the same day** — subscribers who already opened this morning's 8/10-scoring "UP TO 40% OFF ENDS TODAY" email get this at lunch. Cadence fatigue is real, and the second email has to work harder to justify the slot.
- **$5,000 Refresh module kills the urgency** — booking a free design appointment is a low-pressure, high-consideration ask; it undercuts the "hours left" frame and sends mixed signals about what action CB2 actually wants.
- **No specific product hero** — the Summer Edit CTA links to a category page, not a single aspirational product at a specific price. "Up to 40% off" is abstract; "[Chair Name] — $699 $419" is a reason to click.
- **Outdoor 50%+ gets buried below the fold** — if that's the stronger offer, it should be the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Collapse to one offer** — pick either Summer Edit 40% or Outdoor 50%+ as the single hero. Running two competing sale banners halves the impact of each. For a second same-day send, pick the sharper number (50%+) and build the whole email around it.
- 2. **Add one specific priced product to the hero** — "The [Sectional Name] — now $X" transforms "up to 40% off" from a headline into a reason to click now rather than later.
- 3. **Kill the $5,000 Refresh module** — this belongs in a standalone services email (July 21's send was that email). Its presence here reads as padding, not value.
- 4. **Promote the "MORE FOR YOU" grid above the fold** — or at least directly under the hero — so personalization is doing work where it's most likely to be seen.
- 5. **Add a real countdown** — "Hours left" without a timer is a claim; a visible countdown (even static "Sale ends 11:59 PM CT") is a commitment device.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Outdoor furniture 50%+ off — closes tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off ends tonight — your picks are waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chairs, sofas, lighting — this sale ends at midnight CT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: outdoor pieces up to half off. Don't see it twice.`
