# LAST WEEK TO SHOP
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 recycling the exact "End of Summer Sale" creative it already sent on 2026-08-15 (5/10) and reusing the same UP TO 40% OFF hero, furniture/lighting/rugs/decor grid, app download block, and rewards footer — just repackaged under a new urgency-driven subject line, "LAST WEEK TO SHOP." The hero image itself still says "END OF SUMMER SALE," not "last week," so the subject and the hero headline don't actually agree with each other, which is a small but real disconnect for anyone who's been tracking this campaign.
- Production quality is solid: clean hierarchy, a legible hero CTA, a well-organized category grid, and no visible render bugs. But there's nothing new here — no specific product, no fresh photography, no category callout in the subject — and after nearly two weeks of near-daily CB2 sends, this reads like campaign-fatigue filler rather than a genuine "last chance" moment.
- Judgement: technically clean, strategically stale. The urgency claim needs to actually show up in the creative to be credible.

## What's working

- Hero is a single unambiguous message: "END OF SUMMER SALE, UP TO 40% OFF" with a clear white "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) gives multiple clean entry points without cluttering the layout.
- Footer bundles loyalty (10% back rewards), app download, registry, and SMS signup into a tidy, well-organized module.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The subject says "LAST WEEK TO SHOP" but the hero image still reads "END OF SUMMER SALE" — no countdown, no date, no visual urgency cue to back up the subject's claim.
- This is a near-exact repeat of the 2026-08-15 "End of Summer Sale" send with a new subject slapped on; a daily reader will recognize the recycled creative immediately.
- No specific product or price point is shown anywhere — every CTA routes to a category, not a hero item, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- Small dagger/footnote marks next to the 40% off claim and the 15% text-signup offer signal exclusions without stating them in the visible creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deadline in the hero creative (e.g., "SALE ENDS SUNDAY 8/23") so the subject's urgency is backed up visually, not just asserted in the subject line.
- 2. Swap the generic category grid for one hero product at the top (e.g., the Belmond sectional from the 08-17 send) so there's a specific item to click, not just another category page.
- 3. Retire "End of Summer Sale" as a creative template for at least a week — running it twice in four days with different subjects reads as recycled inventory.
- 4. Move the footnote/exclusion language into a visible one-line disclaimer near the offer instead of a detached dagger mark.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends Sunday: 40% Off Everything`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your last chance at summer prices`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs & decor — all on sale through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sectional you've been eyeing is still 40% off. For now.`

# Technical Audit — CB2 "LAST WEEK TO SHOP"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email is a standard MSO/Outlook-conditional HTML template served from mail.cb2.com with heavy third-party ad-tracking pixels; primary compliance risk is unverifiable unsubscribe/authentication headers and several insecure (HTTP) tracking calls.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable via static check) — link destinations are unverified and should be manually clicked through to confirm they don't 404 or loop.
- Multiple third-party ad pixels load over plain HTTP, not HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 identical-pattern pixels)
  - Non-HTTPS image sources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), meaning these tracking pixels likely fail to fire for a meaningful share of opens.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 8 content/product images missing `alt` text (e.g. `cca6653a-b292-4520-b564-8b8d6fe676dc.jpg`, `16bc5205-...jpg`, `8add8946-...jpg`, `18eae94a-...jpg`, `b9eeca94-...jpg`, `d15f6a9d-...jpg`, `c9787622-...jpg`, `b3987305-...jpg`) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- The 10 dotomi tracking pixels and the UCM controller pixel also lack alt text, but as 1x1 tracking pixels this is not user-facing (low priority vs. content images above).
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px — no structural rendering defects detected in the visible markup.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No merge tokens or personalization fields (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{}}`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available markup.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is present (may be a relay artifact rather than a true absence; flag for manual header inspection at the source ESP).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — one-click unsubscribe compliance (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules since Feb 2024) cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — sending domain authentication status is unverified via this relay.
- Note: all three warnings are qualified by the QA tool itself as possibly a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence — recommend verifying via raw headers from an inbox provider (Gmail "Show original") rather than treating as confirmed failures.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the probe, so destination UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified in this pass. No confirmed issues, but this category is effectively untested — flag as a gap, not a pass.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Migrate the 11 HTTP-only dotomi/UCM tracking pixels to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking failure in modern mail clients.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images for accessibility and text-only/blocked-image fallback.
3. Manually pull raw email headers (Gmail "Show original" or equivalent) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` are actually present — current WARNs may be a relay-capture gap, not a real compliance failure.
4. Spot-check a sample of the 61 skipped tracking links to confirm they resolve and carry correct UTM parameters to matching landing pages.
