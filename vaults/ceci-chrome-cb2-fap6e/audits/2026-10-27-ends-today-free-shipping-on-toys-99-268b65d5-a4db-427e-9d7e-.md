# ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on toys $99+ →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a toy-and-gifting send with a clear lead. The subject promises "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on toys $99+", and the hero delivers it: a dollhouse with a red ribbon, a plain "ends today! free shipping on toys $99+*" headline, and one Shop All Toys button. A parent shopping for kids would get the offer in two seconds, and the deadline is credible because it is the last day.
- After that the email loses focus. A toy grid, a "bundled up and ready to give" block, "up to 60% off clearance," the "2026 christmas collection," heirloom gifts, a product row, a gift-card promo, an app promo and a Design Desk promo all follow, each with its own button. The result is a catalog that dilutes the single clear urgency of the top. It also sits against a heavy week of sends from this brand: the 10/27 "Up to 30% off: Accent chairs & tables!" and the three 10/26 sends. A subscriber has seen this brand in the inbox constantly.
- The verdict is a strong top third and a bloated rest. It is a solid, above-average send, but it is not a standout because the free-shipping urgency is buried under the clearance and Christmas blocks.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: the dollhouse image, a short headline and one button, with the deadline in the first words.
- "Ends today" gives believable same-day urgency, and the $99+ threshold is stated up front.
- The toy grid (play houses, stuffed animals, activity mats, wooden toys, games and puzzles) is clean and ties the offer to specific categories.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" block is a concrete price hook with a visible Shop Clearance button.
- The Crate Card and app blocks add loyalty and convenience hooks for members.

## What's weak

- The email runs very long, with roughly ten CTAs, which dilutes the free-shipping message.
- The asterisk on the headline points to dense fine print at the bottom, and the shipping terms are not visible near the offer.
- Clearance, the Christmas collection, heirloom gifts and the Crate Card compete with the toy offer, so the email has no single focus.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" and heirloom blocks appear to have text overlaid on busy photos, which hurts legibility. I could not read the copy clearly at this size.
- The product row near the bottom has small, low-contrast text and no reviews or ratings.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the toy offer: hero, toy grid, a single clearance strip and a footer. Move the Christmas, Crate Card, app and Design Desk blocks to other sends.
- 2. Add a short line of shipping terms directly under the headline, such as "Free standard shipping on toys $99+ through tonight," so the asterisk does not carry the offer.
- 3. Put a countdown or "11:59 PM PT tonight" line in the hero to strengthen the urgency.
- 4. Put dark text on a solid panel for the clearance and heirloom overlays so they stay readable on mobile.
- 5. Add ratings or review snippets to the product row, as the 10/26 "3 top-rated nursery picks" send did.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a template-built message (Movable Ink and an ESP image CDN) with `lang="en"` and responsive CSS. The QA run found about 38 images with missing alt attributes, a viewport that blocks zoom, and no way to confirm the unsubscribe headers or authentication. Only the `<head>` was available, because the HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 76 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=<hash>&n=1..5` pixels fire per open. The `s=` value is a per-recipient hash.
- **Movable Ink pixels:** six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` requests, plus `…/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/720e3f6c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These carry recipient identifiers in the URL.
- **Image hosting:** images load from three hosts: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. Any one failing leaves blank slots.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, about 38 warnings):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, for example `d37b3d6a-…jpg` and `e336e6fc-…jpg`. With images off, these slots show nothing and are unreadable to screen readers.
  - Scene7 secondary-story GIFs `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif` and `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif`, and the banner `CK_H26_KidsHolidayMagic_24x13_Edit3_Tert`.
  - Spacers and tracking pixels (`…Rule_Spacer_40px`, `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`, the `rlcdn` and `mi.` pixels). These should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them. The check reports them as missing, so it may not distinguish an absent attribute from an empty one.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype (should be `&amp;`). The `<head>` also has redundant `<meta charset>` plus `http-equiv` Content-Type, and three overlapping `format-detection` tags with conflicting values.
- **Template residue:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->` are left in the source.
- **Global CSS:** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element. This can cause unintended inheritance in clients that honor head styles.
- **Not assessable:** the source was truncated before the body, so table structure, text-to-image ratio and HTML size (Gmail clips at about 102 KB) could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `s=` parameters are resolved hashes, not broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** neither was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Check the raw message at the source or in a direct-to-inbox test.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is unknown. This is likely a relay artifact and should be verified against the original headers.
- **Footer:** the visible unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked, because the footer is past the truncation point.
- **Disclosure:** the five LiveRamp (`rlcdn.com`) pixels per message are worth confirming against the sender's privacy disclosures. This is an observation, not a CAN-SPAM violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 76 tracked links were not probed and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on `dv.crateandbarrel.com` point to Movable Ink campaign and message IDs, not site UTMs. Nothing confirms that "free shipping on toys $99+" is preserved on the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image (the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` hero and tile images, the Scene7 story GIFs). Set `alt=""` on spacers and pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate charset and `format-detection` metas and the placeholder comment.
4. Re-run the audit against the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Re-run with the full HTML and a probe that follows the tracking redirects. That would let us check footer compliance, UTM presence, final landing URLs and the free-shipping offer, and measure HTML size against Gmail's clipping limit.
