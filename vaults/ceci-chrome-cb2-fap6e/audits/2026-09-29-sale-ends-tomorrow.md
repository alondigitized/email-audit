# SALE ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand urgency send. "ENDS TOMORROW / UP TO 50% OFF!" sits over a moody floral hero, and a dark "SHOP THE SALE" button follows. The deadline and the discount are both visible in the first screen. The fine print, which I can see in the footer, says the sale ends September 30, so the urgency is credible.
- The email doesn't tell the reader what is on sale. Four category tiles (Furniture, Rugs, Lighting, Decor) sit under the hero and run the same "shop by category" cascade CB2 has sent all week. Compare 9/28 "RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM" and 9/27 "THINGS OF QUALITY". No product, price or "was/now" appears anywhere. A second promotion, the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event (up to 25% off), sits below and dilutes the single deadline message.
- Verdict: a competent last-call reminder that gives the reader no product to want. It's a 6.

## What's working

- The hero headline states the deadline and the discount at once: "ENDS TOMORROW, UP TO 50% OFF!"
- The dark "SHOP THE SALE" button contrasts well and is easy to find.
- The category tiles (sofa, rug, lamp, decor) are well shot and have clear labels.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The footer has useful loyalty and utility blocks: 10% back in Rewards, 15% off for texts, and the app.

## What's weak

- No hero product or price is shown. The 50% is only "up to", and nothing shows what actually reaches that discount.
- The Upholstery Event block adds a second offer at a lower discount (25%), which muddies the "ends tomorrow" message.
- Every CTA goes to a generic "SHOP THE SALE". The category tiles have no buttons of their own, and there is no product-level link.
- The lower half is a wall of dense legal fine print, which undercuts the offer's honesty. The exclusions read as heavy.
- The subject is all caps, has no category or number, and sounds like every other retailer's last-call email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one hero product with a real "was/now" price under the headline, for example the burgundy sofa now $X. Let the 50% attach to something concrete.
- 2. Cut the Upholstery Event block, or give it its own send. One deadline gets one offer.
- 3. Add a countdown or an explicit "Ends 9/30 at 11:59 PM PT" line. "Tomorrow" alone is vague if the email is opened late.
- 4. Give each category tile its own CTA, such as "Shop sofas up to 50% off", so the click lands on a specific page.
- 5. Trim the fine print, or move it behind a "See details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 50% off sofas, rugs & lighting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tomorrow's the last day for up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting and decor. Sale ends 9/30 at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off select pieces, plus 10% back in Rewards Dollars.`

## Technical Audit

## CB2 "SALE ENDS TOMORROW": Technical Audit

### 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering bug: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads 10 non-HTTPS Dotomi cookie-sync pixels and has several accessibility gaps. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified because of the relay, and the HTML source was truncated.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed** because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Insecure tracking pixels.** Ten `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` to `pub10.php` pixels, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`, use plain HTTP. Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading will block them. They can also trigger mixed-content warnings in webmail.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The 10 `pub*.php` calls are cookie-sync redirects to an ad-tech vendor. They add load and privacy exposure, and they are likely to be blocked by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and similar features.
- **Cross-brand font hosting.** The `@font-face` "HNW05" sources load from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`. That creates a dependency on a sister-brand domain, and hotlinked fonts can break if the path or CORS policy changes.
- **Malformed font URLs.** The font URLs `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` contain a double slash. It probably resolves, but it is fragile.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Seven images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`: `accae064-…`, `e284a3e0-…`, `dbed4336-…`, `6aadc53c-…`, `a6d04091-…`, `44fe4db1-…` and `89537ff7-…`. If these are linked or carry content, they will render blank with images off and are unannounced or read as filenames by screen readers.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** `o.gif` (`mi.cb2.com`), `dv.cb2.com/o/9d33fd22-…` and the Dotomi pixels lack `alt`. Purely decorative or tracking images should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta tag has `maximum-scale=1`, which disables pinch-zoom on mobile. The CSS also sets `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `-ms-text-size-adjust:none`, which blocks user text scaling.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a template placeholder (and misspelled). It's harmless to rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Not assessable.** The source was truncated, so table structure, `role="presentation"`, link text and the Outlook VML fallbacks in the body couldn't be checked.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw merge token: `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud–style substitution variable that was not resolved at send. It likely belongs to a product-reviews module. Recipients may see the literal string, or a broken or empty module. This is the highest-severity finding.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found.** The QA report itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers. So this is unverified rather than a confirmed violation. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is also a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` should be checked against the raw message.
- **Not verifiable from the provided source.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so CAN-SPAM body requirements couldn't be checked.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** All 61 links are click-tracking redirects that were skipped, and the visible source contains no resolvable `cb2.com` destination or UTM parameters. The email-side tracking IDs (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` on `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com`) point to the Movable Ink or similar platform. The destinations and campaign tagging would need to be resolved to confirm landing-page alignment.

### 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the data source for the reviews module. Add a fallback or suppress the block if the value is empty, and add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%…%%` token.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS**, or remove them. Consolidate the 10 cookie-sync calls if they are needed.
3. **Add alt text** to the seven content images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag and drop `text-size-adjust:none` where possible.
5. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** against the raw message, since the relay may have stripped them. Also confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `mail.cb2.com`.
6. **Host fonts on `cb2.com`** rather than `crateandbarrel.com`, normalize the `//` in the font paths, and remove the placeholder comment.
7. **Resolve the redirect destinations** for the 61 tracked links in a follow-up run, to validate UTM tagging and landing pages.
