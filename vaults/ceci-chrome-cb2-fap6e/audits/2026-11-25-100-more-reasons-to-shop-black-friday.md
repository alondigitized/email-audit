# 100+ more reasons to shop Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's fifth "up to 60% off" Black Friday send in about five days, and it's the broadest one. The hero is clean: a dark green console with sconces and a big "60%" over "BLACK FRIDAY SALE". A "100+ ITEMS JUST ADDED" line and a SHOP THE SALE button sit underneath. The offer reads in one glance, and the render has no visible bugs.
- The email then spreads out. It has a six-tile category grid, a free-shipping band ($99+), a "SAVE 15% OFF IN THE NEW YEAR" band, an app and rewards block, a text-signup block and a dense legal footer. That's four offers and five or six different CTAs. The "100+ more reasons" subject promises items, but the body shows no products and no prices, only category tiles. It repeats the Nov 24 "THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP" creative almost exactly, and that send said the same "100+ items just added" thing. By day five a subscriber is skimming or deleting.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but a generic catch-all send that adds nothing new to this week's run. The Nov 25 lighting send (8/10) did the job better by being specific.

## What's working

- The hero offer is instant. "UP TO 60% OFF" is the largest type on the page, over a moody, on-brand room scene.
- The category grid (Furniture, Holiday & Gifts, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Bedding & Bath, Pillow & Throws) is easy to scan and gives several ways in.
- Free shipping on $99+ is shown in its own band with its own button.
- The CB2, Crate & Barrel and Crate&Kids brand footer, plus the rewards and app blocks, signal a trusted brand.
- The images render cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There are no products and no prices anywhere. "100+ items just added" is never shown, so the subject's promise goes unpaid.
- There are too many CTAs and offers: 60% off, free shipping, 15% off later, 10% back, 15% off for texting and the app download. The primary action gets diluted.
- The hero SHOP THE SALE button is a thin outlined ghost button and is easy to miss. The solid black button comes only after the grid.
- This is the same creative as "THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP" a day earlier, and the fifth "up to 60%" send in five days, so fatigue is obvious.
- The footer legal block is very long and tiny. The "up to" and exclusions are buried, which weakens how honest the offer feels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show real product. Put 4–6 "just added" items with prices and "was/now" in a grid right under the hero, so the "100+ more" claim is visible.
- 2. Make the hero CTA a solid, high-contrast button with a specific verb ("Shop 100+ new markdowns").
- 3. Cut the second-tier offers. Keep 60% off plus free shipping, and move the 15% off, app and text-signup blocks below the fold or drop them.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. Segment by browse category (lighting, furniture) instead of re-sending the generic hero.
- 5. Name a real deadline ("Ends Cyber Monday") instead of leaving urgency implicit.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: new sale picks from $29`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New arrivals on sale today, plus free shipping on $99+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, lighting and decor marked down. Sale ends Cyber Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and `lang="en"`. The automated QA found 0 hard failures, but the 57% pass rate comes mostly from missing alt text, 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels, and header data the relay didn't capture. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks could not be confirmed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links skipped.** QA did not probe them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and broken links are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** One `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` pixel and ten `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` to `pub10.php` pixels are loaded over plain HTTP. Image proxies and mail clients may block or rewrite them, which makes the sync unreliable. These look like Dotomi cookie-sync calls inside the email body.
- **Open-tracking pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=<64-hex>...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/eaa800dd-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` both lack alt text. They are HTTPS, so this is minor. The `mi_u` value looks like a hashed user identifier in a URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `adc0ce08-...gif`
  - `1c4a5f97-...jpg`
  - `06a5a540-...jpg`
  - `b53dcb7c-...jpg`
  - `5fbf7c63-...jpg`
  - `e2005199-...jpg`
  - `f3661ea4-...jpg`
  - `594313ec-...jpg`
  - `72b8d536-...jpg`
  - `e68a523c-...jpg`
  - `20185973-...jpg`

  If these carry offer text or CTAs, images-off and screen-reader users get none of it.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="..., maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule overrides user text-size preferences. This conflicts with the `100%` setting on `body`.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The source contains `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, a misspelled comment from the template. It is harmless but sloppy. The `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block is also empty.
- **Font URLs.** `@font-face` rules use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...` with a double slash. They also pull fonts from a sister domain, `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`. Web fonts are unsupported in several major clients, and the `Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback covers them.
- **`<title>` mismatch.** The title is "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not tied to the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. There are no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The only template artifact is the `IMPUT HERE` comment above. The truncated portion was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA warns these may not survive the AgentMail relay, so this is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` is unverified.
- **Footer not seen.** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address were cut off by truncation, so they are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking.** The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels add third-party tracking inside the message body. That may matter for privacy disclosures.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 61 click-tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't expose final destination URLs or UTM parameters. Landing-page alignment with the "100+ more reasons to shop Black Friday" subject can't be confirmed from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Use `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`, and all Dotomi pixels).
2. Serve the Dotomi pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`) over HTTPS, or drop the cookie-sync pixels from the email.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. Verify the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Re-run QA against the full, untruncated source.
5. Resolve a sample of the 61 tracking links to check destination status codes and UTM presence (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`).
6. Remove the stale `IMPUT HERE` comment and the empty `[if !mso]` block, and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs.
