# Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- The send is clear and credible. The hero reads "hours left: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals" over a moody lounge-room photo. A single "Shop Now" button sits right under it, and the subject matches the hero. This is the cleanest version of the sofa sale so far. Yesterday's Frame TV send ("The Samsung Frame TV is art for your walls") had a subject that contradicted its own hero. This one doesn't.
- After the hero, the email loses focus. It goes through sofas, sectionals, rugs, lighting, decor, mirrors, free swatches, performance fabrics, TheDesignDesk, custom furniture, a product grid, a credit card tile and an app download. That is about ten competing messages under one 20%-off promise. For a recipient who has now seen this same sofa sale on 10/12, 10/13 (twice) and again today, the "ends today" urgency is fair. The cadence still reads as repetition.
- Judgement: a solid, well-built last-day reminder. The long tail dilutes the click and shows the brand is padding a short promo.

## What's working

- The subject and hero carry the same offer and the same deadline: "Ends today" and "hours left."
- The hero is a large, uncluttered photo with one headline and one button, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The two product tiles under the hero ("up to 20% off sectionals" and "up to 20% off sofas") repeat the discount at the point of choice. The "Shop Upholstery Sale" button is specific.
- The performance-fabrics block (a red-wine stain on a sofa) answers a real objection to buying a light-colored sofa.
- Nothing looks broken. I see no broken images, overlaps or gaps, and the layout holds together through the full scroll.

## What's weak

- The hero's "Select styles" line and the asterisk sit under the offer. That qualifier is small and dilutes "bestselling."
- Below the sale tiles, the email pivots to rugs, lighting, decor, mirrors, swatches, design appointments and the app. None of it supports the ends-today sale.
- The TheDesignDesk appointment block appears twice, and there is a card-rewards tile and an app-download block. That adds up to four or five secondary CTAs.
- The product grid near the bottom is generic (espresso machine, steel bowls, cookware, wine glasses, plates). Only one item is a sofa, so it doesn't match the sofa-sale intent.
- The sale has now run across several sends in a row. The urgency is credible but fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the performance-fabrics block, or move it below a clearly labeled "while you're here" divider. End the body on the sale. Keep one secondary CTA at most, either swatches or the design appointment.
- 2. Replace the generic product grid with four to six sofas and sectionals showing sale price, original price and a fabric or color callout. This would be the biggest click lift.
- 3. Put a countdown or a literal "Ends tonight at 11:59pm CT" in the hero. "Hours left" is vague. Move the "select styles" qualifier next to the button as a visible "See exclusions" link.
- 4. Fold the sectionals and sofas tiles into the hero area so the first scroll gives product choice, not just a button.
- 5. Consider suppressing this send for people who already clicked or purchased from the earlier sofa sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 20% off sofas & sectionals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 20% off our bestselling sofas`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale ends at midnight CT. Free swatches and stain-resistant fabrics included.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop sectionals, sofas and custom upholstery before the price goes back up.`

# Technical Audit: "Ends today! Up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is a standard table-based XHTML Transitional template with no confirmed critical defects. The main confirmed issues are about 33 images flagged for missing `alt` text and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture limits in the AgentMail relay, not from the email itself. The source was truncated, so the body links, footer and unsubscribe block were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 64 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none were checked for destination status or redirect chains.
- **Per-recipient identifiers:** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` parameter appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif`. The same hash appears in `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. That ties a recipient identifier to a third-party pixel (rlcdn.com, a LiveRamp domain). This is a privacy observation, not a rendering defect.
- **Pixel count:** There are 5 identical `rlcdn.com` pixels (`n=1` to `n=5`), plus `o.gif` and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` beacon. Many clients that block remote images will block all of these.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA, 33 images):**
  - Content images, where missing `alt` is a real accessibility defect:
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, about 14 files, including `def83198-…jpg`, `2a0bb8a6-…jpg` and `4d0545c3-…jpg`.
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
    - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/cf4a7ed2-….gif`.
    - The Scene7 asset `2026%5F0923%5FCB%5FNewSofas%5FSectionals%5FSpillTe…`.
  - The seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images are probably product or recommendation tiles, which makes this more likely a real defect. The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
  - Tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. The check flags them, but the impact is low.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also disables text scaling on iOS.
- **Dead CSS:** `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` is always overridden by the later `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }`, because the 640px rule matches every width at or above 768px.
- **Markup hygiene (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both declared.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - A leftover template comment reads `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Present and correct:** `lang="en"`, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `color-scheme: light`, and Outlook VML/MSO conditionals.

## 7. Recommendations

2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` where possible.
3. Delete the dead `min-width: 768px` image rule and consolidate the duplicate `format-detection` and charset meta tags.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
5. For the audit pipeline, capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) through a non-relay path. Resolve tracking redirects with a HEAD/GET follow to record final URLs and UTMs. Run the checks against the untruncated HTML so the footer and unsubscribe link are covered.
