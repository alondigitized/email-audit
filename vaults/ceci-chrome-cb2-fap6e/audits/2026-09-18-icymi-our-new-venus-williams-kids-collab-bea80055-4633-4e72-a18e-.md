# ICYMI: Our new Venus Williams kids collab! 🎾
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a full "meet the collection" catalog send for the Venus Williams x Crate & Kids collab, and unlike the brand's typical grab-bag roundups, it actually has a single thesis: room-by-room styling ("a bedroom look to love," "championship-worthy decor," "a powerhouse playroom," "a study space that serves") built around one athlete-designer partnership. The tennis-ball green and navy palette is consistent, the Venus Williams portrait section gives it a credible celebrity-collab anchor, and quotes from Venus herself add some texture instead of pure product shots.
- The problem is length and repetition — this is an extremely long scroll (five nearly identical "room + headline + Shop the New Collab button" blocks) with no discount, no price, and no urgency anywhere in the visible render. It's pure inspiration/launch content, which is fine for a collab reveal, but the subject line "ICYMI" plus 🎾 promises a quick catch-up, not a ten-section room tour. Compare to the 2026-09-17 "Furniture sized right for condo & apartment life" send (7/10) which also had one thesis but was tighter — this one dilutes a good idea by over-length.
- Verdict: strong creative concept, weak execution on pacing — this needs to be cut by half and lead harder with the Venus Williams name/face at the very top instead of six paragraphs in.

## What's working

- Single coherent collab thesis carried through every section (bedroom, playroom, study, decor) rather than a disjointed brand dump.
- Consistent green/navy/tennis-court visual identity ties the whole email together.
- Venus Williams's photo and pull-quotes appear mid-email and again near the bottom, giving the collab a face and personal voice.
- Clear, repeated "Shop the New Collab" CTA buttons in a consistent teal color are easy to spot at every scroll stop.
- Product roundup at the very bottom (with prices and star-style thumbnails) gives shoppers a fast add-to-cart option after the story sections.

## What's weak

- Venus Williams doesn't appear until well into the scroll — for a collab-driven subject line, her face/name should anchor the hero, not show up mid-email.
- No visible price, discount, or offer anywhere in the render — for a "just launched" collab that's acceptable, but combined with the length it makes the email feel like a lookbook, not a shoppable event.
- Five back-to-back near-identical "room photo + headline + button" blocks create serious scroll fatigue with no visual break or table-of-contents.
- "ICYMI" in the subject implies something already missed/quick, but the email itself is a full catalog-length tour — subject tone doesn't match body length.
- No urgency signal (no "shop before it sells out," no date) despite this reading like a limited athlete collab that would benefit from scarcity messaging.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move Venus Williams's portrait and a short quote to the very top as the hero — lead with the face of the collab, not a bedroom photo, since that's the reason people open an "ICYMI" collab email.
- 2. Cut the five room sections down to 2-3 max, or convert the rest into a single scrollable/carousel module — the repetition is the single biggest drag on this send.
- 3. Add one concrete hook — "new arrivals," "limited run," or a real price/starting-at line — since collab drops convert better with scarcity or price anchoring than pure lifestyle copy.
- 4. Tighten the subject to match the body's real content (a full collection reveal, not a quick catch-up).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Venus Williams x Crate&Kids: the full collection is here 🎾`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New from Venus Williams: shop the kids collab`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Championship-ready bedrooms, playrooms & study spaces`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See every room in the Venus Williams collection`
- 5. Add a short jump-nav or "shop by room" strip near the top so recipients can skip to the room they care about instead of scrolling through all five.

## 1. Overview
