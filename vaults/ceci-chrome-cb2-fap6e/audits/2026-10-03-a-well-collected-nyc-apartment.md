# A well-collected NYC apartment
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a design-services pitch dressed up as an apartment tour. It opens on "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" over a moody lifestyle collage. It then shows an Upper West Side apartment, with the CB2 Interior Design Services logo and a black "Book your free design appointment" button. The creative is polished and on-brand. But the subject promises a story, and the email asks for an appointment. The hero never mentions a price or a product.
- Then the email turns into a sale email. A "2 ways to meet" block, a category grid, the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event ("UP TO 25% OFF") and a $5,000 sweepstakes all follow. The upholstery event is the same promo CB2 sent on 10/03 ("Up to 25% off rarely-on-sale upholstery") and 10/01 ("UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK"). A subscriber who has seen those twice sees a recycled bottom half under a fresh top.
- Verdict: good-looking but unfocused. The apartment story and the design appointment are a good pairing. The free-appointment CTA appears three times, and the sale and sweepstakes pull attention away from it.

## What's working

- The hero collage has a clear editorial feel. "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" reads as a brand voice, not a coupon.
- The "Book your free design appointment" button is black, high-contrast and unambiguous. It repeats after the apartment story and again after the sweepstakes block.
- The "2 ways to meet" block (in store or online) lowers the barrier to booking.
- The category grid (Sofas & Sectionals, Dining Room, Bedroom, Lighting, Rugs, Mirrors & Decor) gives good shop-the-look exits.
- Nothing is broken. Images load, the type is clean and there is no overlap.

## What's weak

- The hero has no offer. The subject, the hero and the preview text promise inspiration, and no price or deadline is visible above the fold.
- The black "Book your free design appointment" button sits roughly 1,500px down. On mobile, the reader scrolls through an intro paragraph before reaching it.
- The email has three campaigns stacked: the design service, the upholstery event and the $5,000 sweepstakes. There is no single hero offer.
- The upholstery promo repeats CB2's 10/01 and 10/03 sends almost verbatim. That is heavy cadence, with the third "up to 25% off" in four days.
- The sweepstakes block puts white text over a busy sofa photo, and the fine print is small and buried.
- The sender name "CB2 Free Design Services" is an odd display name for a promo that is half sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "Book your free design appointment" button into the hero, and drop the repeated blocks. Make the design appointment the single CTA, and cut the upholstery event and sweepstakes from this send, since both already had their own emails.
- 2. Add one concrete line under the hero: "Free 1:1 session with a CB2 designer. Book by 10/15 for a room plan in 5 days." The hook has no deadline right now.
- 3. Name the apartment's key piece in a caption and link it, for example "Shop the Upper West Side living room."
- 4. Keep the sender as plain "CB2" so the brand reads as familiar.
- 5. If the upholstery event has to appear, make it a one-line banner.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, Outlook conditionals, mobile media queries). However, it has 12 content images with no alt text and 11 third-party tracking pixels served over plain `http://`. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skips click-redirect domains, so link validity is unverified, not broken.
- **11 pixels load over `http://`** and may be blocked or trigger mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **The Dotomi pixels are third-party ad-network cookie-sync redirects** (`cookieredir`). They add external dependencies and can hurt deliverability and privacy posture. They also fail to load wherever images are proxied or blocked.
- **Two first-party pixels also lack alt text:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0a76448f-...?mi_cid=...`. These are expected open-tracking pixels. The `mi_u` parameter is a 64-hex-character per-recipient identifier.
- **Font URL typo:** `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` has a double slash in three `@font-face` rules. It probably resolves, but it's sloppy.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 content images have no alt attribute**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `f340bec4-…jpg`, `e1fd183e-…jpg`, `268042c0-…gif`, `b741cc5c-…jpg`
  - `0dd08f59-…jpg`, `6adfc879-…jpg`, `7972a5cb-…jpg`, `a68a4f22-…jpg`
  - `aec4186f-…jpg`, `63ecbdaf-…jpg`, `89537ff7-…jpg`, `326a21c4-…jpg`
  
  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, this content is lost.
- **All 13 tracking pixels also lack alt.** They should have `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ... line-height:100%}` stops user text scaling and sets a tight line-height everywhere.
- **Template placeholder left in the head:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (misspelled, never filled in).
- **Web fonts load from another brand's domain:** the `HNW05` `@font-face` rules point at `crateandbarrel.com`. Fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) exist.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated HTML. The footer and later body sections were not available to check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is inconclusive, not a confirmed sender failure.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is likewise attributed to the relay.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified.** They are probably in the truncated footer.
- The sender domain `mail.CB2.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links point to tracking redirects (61 skipped), so final destinations and any UTM parameters are unknown. Landing-page alignment with the subject "A well-collected NYC apartment" can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive alt text** to the 12 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (or remove them). Reconsider embedding ad cookie-sync pixels in email at all.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** and the global `text-size-adjust:none` so users can zoom and resize text.
4. **Clean up the template:** delete the `IMPUT HERE` comment and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font URLs.
5. **Re-run the audit on the untruncated source and raw headers** to check the footer unsubscribe and address, `List-Unsubscribe` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC, and to resolve the redirect chains for UTM and landing-page checks.
6. **Pipeline note:** the three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay, not the email. Capturing raw headers in the monitor would turn these into real pass/fail results instead of recurring warnings.
