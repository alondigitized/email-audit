# Save up to 50% on sofas, dining furniture & more for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid Black Friday furniture send. The hero is clear: "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" with a Shop Now button. A thin urgency strip above it points to a Thanksgiving delivery cutoff tonight. Right below sits the furniture-specific block, "up to 50% off furniture," with a Shop Furniture Sale button. The subject and hero line up.
- It then sprawls. After the furniture section the email adds a bestseller spotlight, a decor and bedding block, a kitchen block ("up to 40% off top Black Friday kitchen deals"), a cookware block, an 8-tile category grid, a clearance banner, a The Design Desk sweepstakes ("enter for a chance to win $5000") and a product carousel. The render is heavily downscaled (680px wide, about 12,000px tall), so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print or product copy.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand sale email that works for the top screen and loses focus below it. This is also the same brand's fourth or fifth promo in two days, and it recycles the "up to 40% off kitchen" beat from the earlier send today.

## What's working

- The top banner and hero put the offer first: up to 50% off, free shipping and a visible Shop Now button.
- The urgency is credible. A Thanksgiving delivery cutoff tonight is a real deadline.
- The subject names the product categories (sofas, dining) and the matching section is the first block under the hero.
- The category tile grid (dining, bedroom, sofas, storage and so on) lets a shopper jump straight to their room.
- The moody lifestyle photography stays consistent with the brand voice.

## What's weak

- The email is too long. At 12,000px with six or more competing offers, it dilutes the furniture message the subject promised.
- Kitchen and cookware take about a third of the body, even though the subject sells furniture and this was the third kitchen-and-toy-style send in a day.
- The 40%, 50%, 30% and 20% off figures across sections make the offer feel layered and a bit fuzzy.
- The sweepstakes block sits mid-email and interrupts the shopping flow.
- The subject, at 63 characters, will probably truncate on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the furniture story. Keep the hero, the furniture grid and the bestseller, and move kitchen and clearance to their own sends. This single change lifts the email most.
- 2. Put the Thanksgiving delivery cutoff in the subject or preheader, since it's the strongest urgency signal and it isn't there now.
- 3. Use one offer per section and make the headline say it once.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes to the footer or a separate send.
- 5. Cap the cadence. Today's sends already repeat the same "Black Friday starts now" beat.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Furniture up to 50% off + free shipping, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by 11:59 pm PT tonight to get it before Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold/Cheetah-style template with ESP-hosted images and 102 click-tracked links. The QA run found 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main verified issues are missing alt text on about 60 images, a viewport and text-size configuration that limits zoom, and header checks that could not confirm unsubscribe or authentication. The HTML source was truncated, so the footer and most link targets were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **102 links not probed.** They are all on tracking or click-redirect domains, so the HTTP probe skipped them. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Subscriber ID in pixel URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869…` token appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. The same hash is in the `s=` parameter of `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif`. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3f343803-…` carries `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. The first two are per-recipient identifiers and the last is campaign/message tracking. This is normal for open and ID tracking, but it is a per-recipient identifier embedded in image requests.
- **Third-party pixel stack.** There are five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` to `n=5` requests (LiveRamp/Rapleaf), plus the `mi.` open pixel and the `dv.` tracker. Each is an extra third-party request that adds weight and a privacy surface.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 60 images.**
  - About 50 are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, for example `f62f4e33-3599-46c5-9b3d-0d263bbfcb5a.jpg` and `07b7830d-daa0-4b83-b0ec-f875e308e765.gif`. These appear to be content images, so screen-reader users get no equivalent. Image-off rendering is also unusable if the email is image-driven.
  - Seven are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and one is `/p/up/.../o.gif`.
  - Five are `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` and one is `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3f343803-…`. These are tracking pixels and should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
  - One image, `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` on the `/m/47/` path, is also flagged.
- **Pinch-zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents user scaling, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resize disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` stop text resizing in iOS and WebKit. This conflicts with the `body { -webkit-text-size-adjust:100% }` rule later in the same stylesheet.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It reads `Crate & Barrel` and does not reflect the subject line. Under the XHTML 1.0 doctype the `&` should be `&amp;`, though the source shown may have been decoded.
- **Duplicate and redundant markup.**
  - There are two charset declarations, `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values: the combined `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately.
  - The `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are repeated across two `<style>` blocks.
  - A leftover template comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`.** This forces centered alignment on every paragraph unless it is overridden inline.
- **Image max-width rules.** `img {max-width:600px}` at 768px and above and `img {max-width:640px}` at 640px and above are both defined. The 768px rule is declared first, so for widths of 768px and up the later 640px rule wins and the cap is 640px, not 600px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were present in the visible HTML. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values are rendered tracking parameters. The remainder of the HTML was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). The check notes that AgentMail may strip it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a verified omission.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). RFC 8058 one-click support could not be verified, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so alignment cannot be assessed from the available data.
- **Not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) are in the truncated footer, so they could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters not verifiable.** All 102 links are wrapped in tracking redirects and were not resolved. No `utm_*` parameters are visible in the available source.
- **Landing-page alignment not verified.** This needs a redirect-following pass on the hero, sale and category CTAs. The subject promises "up to 50% on sofas, dining furniture", so confirm those links land on the matching sale pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 50 content images from the `/m/1/` path (and the `/m/47/` one). Set `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels: `rlcdn` x5, `mi.…/o.gif` and `dv.…`.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules and keep `100%`.
3. Make `format-detection` a single `<meta>`, drop the duplicate charset tag, and delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment. Use `&amp;` in the title and make it match the subject.
4. Inspect the raw message headers directly, not through the relay. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass and alignment.
5. Re-run the link check with redirect following to resolve the 102 tracking links. Record the final URLs, status codes and `utm_*` values, and check the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
