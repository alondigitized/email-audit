# NEW WALLPAPER COLLECTION
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a wallcoverings launch with two competing offers stacked on top of it. At the very top is a "NEW MARKDOWNS, UP TO 60% OFF" banner. Directly below it, the hero says "INTRODUCING WALLCOVERINGS" over a moody dining-room photo. The email's real news is the new wallpaper, but the discount sits above it, so the eye splits between the two.
- The wallpaper content is good. Named prints (Palm Print, Nisha, Malta Faux Plaster, Jungle Bloom, Mother Amazon) get full-bleed room shots, and the "order free swatches" line lowers the barrier to buying wallpaper. After that the email goes off course. A second Semi-Annual Dining Event banner ("up to 30% off") and a "More for you" candle grid have nothing to do with wallpaper. At about 8,400px tall, it reads as a catalog with the promos bolted on.
- Judgement: a strong creative launch with a muddled message. It is a solid 6. It would score higher if it committed to wallpaper and a single offer.

## What's working

- The editorial room photography is warm and cohesive. The wallpapers are shown in real rooms, so a shopper can picture them.
- Naming the prints and credits (Matthew Williamson, Laundry Studio) gives the collection a designer feel.
- "Order free swatches" is a smart low-risk step for a category where shoppers worry about color and pattern.
- The hero has a clear "SHOP WALLCOVERINGS" button, and a "SHOP ALL WALLCOVERINGS" button repeats near the end of the wallpaper section.
- The subject lines up with the hero's wallcovering theme.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" banner above the hero competes with the wallpaper launch. Markdowns on what, exactly? It is unclear.
- The Dining Event banner ("up to 30% off") repeats the 10/21 "Up to 30% off things for hosting" send. That is a third message in one email.
- The candle "More for you" grid is unrelated to wallpaper and reads as filler.
- The email is very long, with app download, rewards and gift-registry modules. The wallpaper story gets diluted.
- The subject is in ALL CAPS and gives no reason to open. It is also the third all-caps subject in a few days (after "WAREHOUSE SALE" and "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED").

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the markdown banner and the Dining Event block. Make wallpaper the only story, with free swatches as the one hook.
- 2. Cut the candle grid. If a second category stays, make it complementary, such as paint or lighting.
- 3. Add price points (for example, "from $X per roll") and basic practical details such as peel-and-stick versus traditional. Wallpaper buyers need that to click.
- 4. Put the "free swatches" offer higher, ideally in the hero copy or as a second button next to the shop button.
- 5. Trim the tail modules (app, rewards, registry) into a slim footer.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, is left in the output. It also loads 10 insecure `http://` Dotomi pixels, and many images have no alt text. The QA run reports a 43% pass rate.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Insecure tracking pixels.** There are 10 `http://` image sources, with `alt` missing on all of them:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`, and the same pattern for `pub2.php` through `pub10.php`.
  - Gmail and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS images. Where they are blocked, the Dotomi cookie-sync calls fail silently. They can also trigger mixed-content warnings in some clients.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** `pub1`–`pub10` are Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-redirect calls placed in the email body. Ten sequential third-party requests add load and a privacy surface. They are also the likeliest part of the message to be stripped or flagged.
- **Unverified click tracking.** 70 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- The first-party open pixels (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/a51288f2-…?mi_cid=…`) use HTTPS, but neither has `alt`.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

The `Lineto Brown Light Sub` rule is a woff-only file with no format hint. Web fonts load only in clients such as Apple Mail, so this is minor. Fallbacks to Helvetica and sans-serif are present.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **FAIL: unresolved token `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%…%%` personalization string. It was not substituted at send time.
  - Likely cause: the reviews/SKU dynamic-content block failed to populate. Alternatively, it is a template placeholder that was never removed.
  - Impact: the token may show as literal text to the recipient, or it may break a reviews module. The truncated source did not let me find where it appears.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **WARN: `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** The QA note says AgentMail may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **WARN: `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058).** This is also unconfirmed for the same reason. One-click unsubscribe is a bulk-sender requirement.
- **WARN: `Authentication-Results` header not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status are unknown. The note says this is expected via the relay.
- The sender is `cb2.com`, a subdomain-style `mail.cb2.com`, and the image host is `image.mail.cb2.com`, so domains are aligned. Alignment itself was not verified from headers.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- No landing-page or UTM evidence was available. The 70 links were skipped by the probe, and the truncated source did not show their destination URLs. I can't assess UTM presence or landing-page alignment.
- Tracking is done by redirect and by `mi_*` parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), not by visible UTMs. Whether UTMs are appended after the redirect is unknown.

7. Recommendations

1. **Fix the merge token (high).** Resolve or remove `%%reviews_skulist%%`. Add a pre-send check that blocks any message containing unresolved `%%…%%` strings, with a fallback for empty dynamic content.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (high).** Use `https://` for `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com`, or drop the `pub1`–`pub10` calls. Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to tracking images.
3. **Add `alt` text to the 10 content images (medium).** Describe the product or message each carries, especially any that carry the headline.
4. **Verify unsubscribe headers on the raw send (medium).** Check the original message at the MTA, not the AgentMail copy, for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Also verify SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass.
5. **Verify the click-redirect links (medium).** Follow a sample of the 70 tracked links manually or with a redirect-aware probe. Confirm they resolve to cb2.com wallpaper pages and carry the intended parameters.
6. **Clean up (low).** Fix the `//Styles` double-slash font paths and remove the `[IMPUT HERE …]` comment.
7. **Re-run QA on the full HTML.** The source was truncated, so the body, footer, and VML checks are incomplete.
