### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`<title>` tag mismatch:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` — sender is "Crate & Kids." Clients that surface the title tag (some webmail, accessibility tools) will show the wrong brand.

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta:** Prevents pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA). Should be removed.
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```

**Duplicate `format-detection` meta tags:** `telephone=no`, `address=no`, and `date=no` are each declared twice — once in a combined tag and again individually. Harmless but indicates template hygiene issue.
