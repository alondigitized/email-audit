# The past is present
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**

Further down are a second sale module (Guest Ready Sale, up to 40% off), a "More for You" product grid and the app and loyalty footer.

- The Warehouse Sale has been the lead for at least five sends. Nov 1 was "Final days of the Warehouse Sale," and Oct 30 was "THIS IS YOUR CHANCE." "Final Weekend" language on a Monday also reads stale. Overall it is a handsome, well-rendered send with a muddled job. It is a decent 6-7.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" banner and "Shop the Sale" button sit at the very top, so the offer is immediately visible.
- The mid-century editorial has a cohesive point of view, with full-bleed room photography and consistent typography.
- Each product carries designer, year and material detail, which adds credibility and a reason to click.
- The layout has no visible render problems: no broken images, overlaps or empty gaps.
- The "More for You" grid shows prices and strikethrough markdowns, which gives a concrete price anchor.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a design-history story, but the first thing the reader sees is a clearance banner. The two messages compete.
- "Final Weekend" is stale on Nov 3, after "Final days" went out Nov 1. A reader sees a fifth near-identical Warehouse Sale push in four days.
- A second sale module, Guest Ready up to 40% off, sits near the bottom and dilutes the offer.
- The editorial CTA "Go back in time" is vague, and the story products show no prices.
- The email is very long, and the legal and fine-print footer is dense.

- 4. Cut the Guest Ready module, or fold it into the footer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, was left unresolved. It also has many images with no alt text and 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be run because the relay didn't capture those headers. The QA pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 70 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified, so none are confirmed broken or working.
- **Non-HTTPS tracking pixels:** 11 `<img>` pixels use `http://`, which clients and image proxies may block or rewrite:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Third-party pixels:** These look like ad-network cookie-sync calls (Dotomi). They add 11 external requests per open, and a blocked request will not fire.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/8c7974a3-...?mi_cid=...` are HTTPS and work as expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The QA tool did not find this header. It warns that AgentMail may not relay it, so its absence isn't confirmed on the sender side.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Also not found, with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is unverified, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. The QA tool expects this from the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` are unknown.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** The truncated source doesn't show a footer unsubscribe link or postal address, so neither can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 70 tracking links were not probed, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the truncated source or QA output. All visible links appear to go through ESP click-wrapping, so UTM parameters would only show up in the final destination after resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the SFMC data extension or AMPscript for that block, and add a fallback or suppress the block when the value is empty. Run a seed-list render test before send.
2. **Add alt text to the 10 content images.** Use descriptive text for product or content images, and `alt=""` for the tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or remove them.** Some of these calls are likely dead on blocking clients anyway, and 11 third-party requests per open is a deliverability and privacy cost.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify the headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC on the original `.eml` rather than the AgentMail relay copy. Also confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address in the full HTML.
6. **Resolve the 70 tracking links** with a redirect-following probe. Confirm they return 200, land on `cb2.com`, and carry consistent UTM parameters.
