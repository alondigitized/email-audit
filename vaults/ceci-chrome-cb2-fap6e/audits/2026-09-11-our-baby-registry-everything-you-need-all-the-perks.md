# Our Baby Registry: everything you need & all the perks →
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running its most comprehensive registry email of the week — and it shows. Instead of one offer and one CTA, it stacks the full pitch: create a registry, book a free design appointment, get assembly help, browse top brands (Nuna, UppaBaby, Stokke, Doona, Bugaboo, mushie), take 15% off everything else, see loyalty perks, enter a $5,000 sweepstakes, and download the app. It's essentially a registry landing page compressed into an email.
- The hero does its job — "your dream nursery starts here" over a styled nursery shot, with "Create My Registry" as the first thing the eye hits. The problem is depth, not the top: by the time a reader scrolls through TheDesignDesk pitch, assembly service, brand logos, 15% off, perks, more product grids, a sweepstakes, an app teaser, and a credit card pitch, there are effectively seven competing CTAs. That's a lot of decision points for one send.
- Compared to the same-day "Nuna, Uppababy, Stokke… all the best baby gear is here" send (also 2026-09-11, 6.00/10), this is denser and more registry-specific, which should land better with an expecting-parent persona. But two Crate & Kids emails same day risks fatigue regardless of quality. Solid execution, moderate cadence problem.

## What's working

- Clean hero: nursery imagery + clear "Create My Registry" button above the fold, no scrolling needed to see the primary ask.
- Brand-name social proof (Nuna, UppaBaby, Stokke, Bugaboo, Doona) signals credibility for a registry-shopping persona.
- Layered value props beyond discount — free design help, white-glove assembly, loyalty perks — differentiate from a generic percent-off blast.
- No visible render bugs: images load, text doesn't overlap, spacing is consistent throughout.
- 15% off is stated as a specific, visible number rather than buried.

## What's weak

- Too many CTAs competing for attention (Create My Registry ×2, Book Appointment, Shop Now, Shop All Baby Time, See All Perks, Enter Now, Download Now, Learn More) — no single clear next action.
- "15% off everything else" is a soft, slightly exclusionary offer — it implicitly excludes something and never says what.
- The credit-card pitch ("new! the card you love just got better") near the bottom feels bolted on and off-theme for a registry email.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the send — nothing time-bound to justify opening today vs. next week.
- Subject line has no concrete number/urgency, just a value proposition, which softens open intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — lead with registry creation + 15% off, move design/assembly services and the credit card pitch to a separate, dedicated send. One offer, one CTA, gets more clicks than seven diluted ones.
- 2. Put a number in the subject line — reference the 15% off or the $5,000 sweepstakes directly instead of vague "everything you need."
- 3. Add a deadline to the 15% off banner (even "this week only") to create real urgency — right now nothing is time-bound.
- 4. Drop or relocate the credit-card block — it breaks the registry narrative and reads as cross-sell clutter at the point a reader is closest to converting.
- **Subject Alt A:** `15% off your registry + a free design consult`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your registry, 15% off — plus a shot at $5,000`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free design help, free assembly, and 15% off everything on your list.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nuna, Stokke, Bugaboo — all in one place, all with registry perks.`
