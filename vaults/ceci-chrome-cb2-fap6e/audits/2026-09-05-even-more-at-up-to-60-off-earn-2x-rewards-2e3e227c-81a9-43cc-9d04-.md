# 🚨 Even more at up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- Same Crate & Kids kitchen-sink template as every other send this week — new items added, up to 60% off, Double/2x Rewards, sale banner across the top left of every module. This is the same nursery/kids-room lifestyle photography (crib, star rug, kids' desk, high chair) I've now seen fronting five near-identical Labor Day emails in three days from this family of brands. The one distinct element is the "🚨 Even more at" subject framing, positioning this as an update/refresh rather than a fresh campaign, but nothing in the body actually signals what's new versus the 09-04 sends.
- This is a long, module-heavy scroll (bedding, rugs, decor, clearance, sweepstakes, credit card, app) with a clear repeating pattern — good for scanability, but it's the same pattern subscribers have already seen. The sweepstakes ($5,000) and credit card cross-sell modules pad the email without adding urgency for a parent shopper.

## What's working

- Clear hero: "New items added!! Double Rewards² + Up to 50% off" is immediately visible with no scroll.
- Kids-specific product categories (bedding, rugs, décor/lighting, furniture) are well segmented into distinct modules with their own CTAs.
- Reward Dollars mechanic ("earn 30%² back in Reward Dollars") is a concrete, differentiated loyalty hook beyond generic percent-off.
- High chair/toddler lifestyle photo is on-persona and warm, not stock-generic.
- Consistent "Shop Now" CTA buttons are visible and easy to spot throughout.

## What's weak

- Fifth near-identical Labor Day send in three days — no new creative, same modular stack, same red sale-tag badges.
- Subject promises "even more" but the body reads identically to yesterday's Crate & Kids sends — nothing flags what's actually new.
- Sweepstakes and credit-card modules dilute focus and add scroll length without matching the sale urgency.
- Percent-off figures shift module to module (50%, 60%, 30%) without a single anchor number, which blurs the headline offer.
- Fine-print superscript markers (¹²) throughout with no visible footnote in the crop — feels like buried disclosure even if it's below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate this from the four prior sends this week — lead with the specific "new items" (show 2-3 actual new SKUs at top) instead of reusing the same layout skeleton.
- 2. Pick one hero percent-off number and stick with it in subject, preheader, and hero rather than 30/50/60% scattered across modules.
- 3. Cut or move the sweepstakes and credit-card modules further down/out — they're not offer-relevant and add fatigue to an already long email.
- 4. Add a real countdown/deadline ("ends tonight," "3 days left") since Labor Day urgency is fading and this is being sent repeatedly.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh cribs, rugs & décor added — Double Rewards ends soon`
