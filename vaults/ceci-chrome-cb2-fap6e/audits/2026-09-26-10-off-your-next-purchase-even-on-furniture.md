# 10% off your next purchase—even on furniture.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is a bait-and-switch open: the subject promises "10% off your next purchase—even on furniture," but the email opens with a nearly blank phone-mockup hero pushing SMS signup, and the referenced 10% offer is just one sentence of caption text underneath it. Anyone who opened for a furniture discount lands on a text-club pitch first, with two competing CTAs ("Sign Up For Texts" and "Shop Bestsellers") fighting for the same click.
- After that confusing opener, the email reverts to a generic Crate & Barrel newsletter grab-bag — fall decor event, dining room furniture, a six-item product grid, Crate Card rewards, the app, TheDesignDesk, and a store locator. None of it reinforces the subject line's furniture discount; the "even on furniture" hook is dropped completely once you scroll past the hero.
- Compared to the brand's other September 26 sends (the Crate & Barrel Kids holiday HQ email, the clearance blasts), this one at least avoids the moody dark-hero legibility problem, but it has a worse structural issue: the subject and the hero content don't match, which is a bigger open-to-click integrity gap than a hard-to-read background.

## What's working

- The phone-mockup hero is visually distinctive compared to the brand's usual product-photo openers.
- The fall decor event block (dark green banner, "up to 30% off") has good contrast and a clear single CTA.
- The dining furniture and product-grid sections are clean, well-priced, and easy to scan, with the Le Creuset item showing a clear sale price ($460 struck through to $289.95).
- Crate Card rewards and TheDesignDesk sections are relevant loyalty/service add-ons that build brand trust.

## What's weak

- The hero phone mockup shows an essentially empty screen — no message bubble, no visual proof of the "text club" content — which reads as a broken or unfinished asset.
- Subject line promise ("10% off your next purchase—even on furniture") is buried as a single caption line under the hero and never revisited anywhere else in the email.
- Two competing CTAs stacked immediately ("Sign Up For Texts" / "Shop Bestsellers") give no clear primary action.
- The email is a full-scroll grab-bag (SMS signup → decor sale → dining → product grid → credit card → app → design service → store locator) with no unifying theme tied to the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: lead with a real furniture visual and "10% off your next purchase, even on furniture" as the headline, not a blank phone screen. The SMS signup can be a secondary block further down.
- 2. Fix or replace the phone mockup — an empty screen undermines the entire top section; show an actual sample text message if this asset is meant to sell SMS signup.
- 3. Pick one primary CTA for the hero. If the point is the 10% offer, make "Shop Now" or "Claim 10% Off" the singular focus and move "Sign Up For Texts" below the fold.
- 4. Trim the back half — dropping either the app-download block or the store-locator block would tighten the scroll and reduce fatigue after four brand emails in two days.
