## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run (57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings) found two problems in the source: missing alt text on about 32 images, and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The header and link findings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 67 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking or click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** the source has five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `&n=5` beacons (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp's domain). It also has six `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, one `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and one `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/af387c7d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are 13 tracking or beacon images.
- **Identifier in URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a…` value is a per-recipient identifier in image URLs, and the same value appears in the rlcdn `s=` parameter. That means the recipient ID is passed to a third-party domain.
- **Repeated pixels:** the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested six times and the `448796.gif` beacon five times. This looks like redundant pixel calls, though the source is truncated so I can't tell whether they are one per module.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA flags 32 images.
  - About 19 are content images, for example `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/03%5FCore/2026%5F0326%5FCB%5FVF%5FSmegMocha/2026%5F0326%5FCB%5FVF%5FSmegMocha%5F1%5Fhero%5Fv1.…`, `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, the `…/m/1/*.jpg` set and `79e1e18a-…gif`.
  - The remaining 13 are the tracking pixels above.
  - The check may be flagging `alt=""`, which is correct for decorative pixels. Any content images that lack `alt` entirely will read as filename or URL to screen readers and show nothing when images are blocked. The hero and product tiles are the ones that matter.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 (resize text).
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** there are two `<meta charset>` declarations (`http-equiv` plus `charset`), and `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Global CSS rules:** `*{line-height:100%; max-height:1000000px}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply to every element. The line-height rule can cause tight or clipped text in some clients, and the `p` rule forces centering unless overridden inline.
- **Mobile sizing:** `.mobile {width:460px !important}` and `.showmobile {width:414px !important}` are fixed pixel widths. On viewports below 414px they may overflow horizontally.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` and `supported-color-schemes: light` are declared, so dark mode is opted out. That is deliberate, not a defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the subject, from line or truncated HTML. The template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a leftover placeholder. It is a typo, is inert inside a comment, and only ships template scaffolding in the source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the header was not found. The QA notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** the header was not found, so one-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, but the same relay caveat applies.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with a dedicated sending subdomain. That isn't proof of authentication.
- **Footer:** the footer (unsubscribe link, physical postal address) is cut off in the truncated source, so I can't verify it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 67 tracking-redirect links weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be checked against the campaign (SMEG Mocha kitchen). The click-tracking redirects are also why no direct `crateandbarrel.com` destinations are visible.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, starting with the SMEG hero, and use `alt=""` on the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Re-run the check with raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the AgentMail relay, to confirm compliance and authentication.
4. Follow a sample of the 67 tracked links to confirm final URLs, status codes, UTM parameters and that they land on the SMEG Mocha or kitchen content.
5. Escape the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate meta tags and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
6. Review the third-party `rlcdn.com` beacons and the `mi_u` recipient identifier passed to them for privacy and consent.
7. Re-run the audit on the full untruncated HTML to check the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address.
