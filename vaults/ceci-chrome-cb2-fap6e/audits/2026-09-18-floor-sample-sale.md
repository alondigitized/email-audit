# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- CB2 leads with "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE / In stores for a limited time" over a moody console-table lifestyle shot — but there's no discount, no percentage, no dollar figure anywhere near the hero. It's a category of sale ("floor sample" = in-store clearance) that skips the one thing that actually drives a click: how much you save. The CTA under the hero is "FIND A STORE," which is a store-locator action, not a shop-online action — for an email subscriber base, that's a hard stop for anyone who isn't already planning a showroom visit.
- The email redeems itself lower down with a clean "BEST SELLERS / UP TO 40% OFF" banner and a proper "SHOP THE SALE" button — that's the actual offer, buried second. Compared to 2026-09-17's "The best is up to 40% off" (7.00/10, a standalone best-sellers send), CB2 is now cannibalizing that same offer as an afterthought under a weaker, in-store-only hero.
- Net: this reads as two emails stapled together, with the stronger offer demoted below a directionless in-store push. Judgement: mediocre, drags the brand history down from the 09-17 high.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand minimal layout — plenty of white space, no render bugs, no broken images or overlapping text.
- "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" banner has real hierarchy: bold headline, visible discount, clear "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Nav bar and footer utility blocks (app, rewards card, gifting, SMS signup) are legible and functional-looking.
- Social/brand footer (CB2, Crate&Barrel, Crate&kids, HG) reinforces brand trust and portfolio scale.

## What's weak

- Hero offer ("FLOOR SAMPLE SALE") has zero specifics — no %, no $, no product category called out, just "in stores for a limited time."
- Primary CTA "FIND A STORE" sends an email subscriber to a store locator, not a shopping destination — poor fit for anyone browsing from their inbox/phone.
- The actual discount (40% off best sellers) is pushed below the fold, essentially a second, disconnected email stapled under the real hero.
- No urgency/deadline visible on the hero sale itself (fine print elsewhere has an end date for the *other* promo, not this one).

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero and the best-sellers banner — lead with "UP TO 40% OFF BEST SELLERS" and "SHOP THE SALE," since that's the offer with an actual number and a real CTA. Demote "Floor Sample Sale" to a secondary in-store callout.
- 2. If floor samples stay as the hero, replace "FIND A STORE" with a CTA that does something for an online reader — e.g., "SHOP FLOOR SAMPLES ONLINE" if inventory exists online, or at minimum add a specific discount range ("Up to 50% off one-of-a-kind pieces") so the email justifies itself before asking for a store visit.
- 3. Add a visible end date to the floor sample sale copy — "in stores for a limited time" reads soft next to CB2's usual "Ends September 23" urgency pattern.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Floor samples, up to 50% off — in stores now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Best sellers up to 40% off + in-store floor sample deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One-of-a-kind pieces, marked down, while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: best sellers up to 40% off, shop online now.`
