# Psst… something special just hit up to 60% off!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a long Crate & Barrel send that tries to do everything. The subject promises "something special" at up to 60% off, and the hero does show "up to 60% off clearance" with a Shop Now button. But a dark red banner above the hero competes with it. The banner carries "last Double Rewards this year!" and "don't miss out! up to 50% off The Fall Sales Event". So the 60% promise is split against two other offers in the first screen.
- Below that, the email stacks a furniture rewards module, a Fall Sales Event block, a The Design Desk sweepstakes, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app download and a second Design Desk promo. For a subscriber who has seen four or five Double Rewards and Fall Sales Event sends since 2026-11-09, this reads as more of the same. The new "clearance" angle is the only fresh thing, and it gets buried. My verdict is a polished email with no single message. The subject's curiosity hook leads to a hero that is muted and grey.

## What's working

- The hero states a specific number, "up to 60% off clearance", with a clear Shop Now button.
- The category chips (furniture, rugs, kitchen, tabletop, bedding & bath, decor) give easy browsing paths.
- The furniture block shows named products with Reward Dollar earn amounts, such as "Earn $426 $1049 Reward Dollars". That ties the offer to loyalty.
- The "we love these for you" grid has real product names and prices, such as the espresso machine and the cookware set.
- The top bar sets a credible deadline: "order by 11/18 at 11:59 pm PT to get it in time for Thanksgiving".

## What's weak

- The hero is a foggy grey image with small, pale text, so the 60% offer is not the first thing the eye lands on.
- The red banner above the hero repeats the same Double Rewards and Fall Sales Event pitch as the 2026-11-10 sends. It dilutes the "something special" subject.
- Three offers (60% clearance, 50% off, 2x Rewards) plus a $5000 sweepstakes make the email feel like a kitchen sink. There are also three or more Shop or Book CTAs with different goals.
- The Design Desk appears twice, the sweepstakes and the free appointment, and the app download and credit card blocks pile on further. The fine print is dense and the email is very long.
- "Psst… something special" does not match a clearance hero. The "something special" turns out to be markdowns.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero carry one message. Use a high-contrast, product-led image with "up to 60% off clearance" in large type, and move the Double Rewards banner below the hero or drop it.
- 2. Cut the module count by about half. Keep clearance, the product grid and one service promo, and move the app, credit card and second Design Desk blocks to other sends.
- 3. Add clearance product tiles with was/now prices, so the 60% claim is backed by real items.
- 4. Give the sweepstakes its own send, since it competes with the sale.
- 5. Vary the creative from the earlier Fall Sales Event sends so the email does not look like a repeat.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off furniture & decor, while it lasts`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs and cookware marked down. Order by 11/18 for Thanksgiving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year, plus new clearance markdowns.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) XHTML table layout with 77 tracked links. QA found 38 images missing alt text and no unsubscribe or authentication headers. The header findings are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, and the HTML source is truncated, so footer and body checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links not verified:** 77 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Redirect chains, destination status codes and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` are LiveRamp (rlcdn) pixels. They differ only in `n=`, and each adds a third-party request. All five lack alt text.
- **Recipient-identifying parameters:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` hash is in image URLs on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/acff58b7-…`. Those requests make the images per-recipient.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (38 images):**
  - 24 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*` (plus one under `/m/47/`, `bbf6f9d4-….png`) have no alt text.
  - 7 dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` have none either.
  - The pixels (5 rlcdn, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/acff58b7-…`) also have no alt. For pixels, the fix is `alt=""` with a 1×1 size.
  - With images off or a screen reader, the content images are unlabeled, and any that carry the offers have no text fallback.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS text scaling.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` (HTML5) is mixed with the XHTML doctype.
  - Three `format-detection` metas are redundant, and one is repeated.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
- `lang="en"` is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. Personalization appears to be via `mi_u`, but the truncated source means the body and footer weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header:** the QA check flags it as not found. The check itself says AgentMail may not capture it.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** same caveat.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses an aligned subdomain (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`), but this can't be confirmed.
- **Unconfirmed from the source:** the in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated footer. The compliance status for both is unconfirmed rather than failed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found. Tracking is done through redirect links, so UTM and landing-page alignment can't be assessed from the HTML. Because the probe skipped all 77 links, there is no evidence either way on whether the "up to 60% off" destination matches the subject.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to all 24 `image.mail…/lib/…` and 7 `mi…/p/rp/` content images, with descriptive text for product images. Give the pixels `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`.
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta. Consider dropping `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` too.
3. **Consolidate the five LiveRamp `rlcdn` pixels** into one request, or drop them if they're redundant.
4. **Fix the markup:** use `&amp;` in the `<title>`, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder, and drop the redundant `img` max-width rule.
5. **Re-run QA against raw `.eml` headers** to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Re-run it on the full, untruncated HTML for the footer address and unsubscribe link.
6. **Resolve the tracking links through the redirect chain** (follow with `GET`, don't skip) to check final URLs and UTM parameters.
