# COLOR IS THE WORD
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 in seasonal-mood mode. "COLOR IS THE WORD" leads a fall-palette story of olive, turmeric, yellow, denim, jade and oxblood, shown as swatch chips over moody room shots. The styling is handsome and the brand voice is consistent. The problem is that it's a mood board with no reason to act today. The only hard offer is buried mid-email: an upholstery event with a "$5,000 refresh" design-appointment prize. It's a sweepstakes-style hook, not a price cut.
- It's also the 7th CB2 send in about a week, after "Designer to know: David Girelli," "RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM," "THINGS OF QUALITY" and "Two sales are on now." The 9/27 "Two sales" send at least named its offers. This one goes back to vibes. The render is small, so I can't read the fine print, but the structure is clear: a hero, two swatch-and-product grids, a Shop Now button, the upholstery promo, a "New & Best Selling" block, a six-item "More For You" grid, then app, rewards and gift-registry footers.
- Verdict: good creative, weak commercial hook, and fatigue risk. It works for a subscriber who's already browsing and won't move anyone who's on the fence.

## What's working

- The palette concept is cohesive. The swatch chips (OLIVE, TURMERIC, YELLOW, DENIM, JADE, OXBLOOD) tie color directly to named products like the Sueda chair, Fable sectional and Hawk side table.
- The hero is clean: an oversized serif headline, a short supporting line and one Shop Now button. The eye lands on it first.
- It's seasonal ("Fall palettes that just make sense") and matches the current moment.
- The "More For You" grid shows product names and prices ($1,199, $499, $2,279 with a strikethrough), which gives real purchase paths.
- I can see no broken images or overlaps, and the footer has the CB2 family brands, the app and a rewards line (10% back).

## What's weak

- There's no clear price or percentage offer in the hero or first screen. The strongest incentive, a "$5,000 refresh," is a prize draw sitting mid-email.
- Several sections end in a generic "Shop Now," which is a weak CTA with no verb-plus-noun specificity.
- The subject is all caps and gives no benefit or urgency. It also reads much like earlier one-word-style CB2 subjects ("RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM," "THINGS OF QUALITY").
- The middle of the email is a long run of lifestyle imagery with tiny captions. The color story and the products compete instead of reinforcing each other.
- Cadence is heavy. After a week of near-daily category cascades, this one blends in.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one concrete offer above the fold, such as the upholstery event pricing or a dated savings tier, and make the hero button say what it does, e.g. "Shop Fall Colors."
- 2. Make each swatch chip clickable to a color-filtered collection (e.g. "Shop Olive"). Right now the color story doesn't seem to be a shopping tool.
- 3. Cut the middle of the email. Keep the palette grids and drop either the "New & Best Selling" block or the "More For You" grid.
- 4. Move the design-appointment promo up and tie it to the color theme.
- 5. Space out the sends by rotating in one editorial or one sale send per week, not five near-identical ones.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The CB2 "COLOR IS THE WORD" email has one confirmed rendering-breaking defect: a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token that was never resolved. Beyond that, it has widespread missing alt text, several non-HTTPS tracking pixels, and compliance headers the relay didn't capture. The QA pass rate is 43%.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Ten `http://` Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are present: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`.
  - Clients that block or rewrite mixed content may drop them.
  - Each is a third-party ad-sync call from inside the email body.
- Own-domain tracking pixels are present and loaded over HTTPS: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/c89e3f0d-1ce4-4211-a815-a5df71207a14?mi_cid=...`. Neither has alt text.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- 12 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text, for example `86b54300-4891-424d-b308-dce8c59f1093.jpg`, `bd72c884-8589-4bc5-9dde-78ca2e8cb4a5.jpg` and `a1884ac9-2b8f-49e0-8353-8f70a754ef69.jpg`. If images are blocked, these sections render blank, and screen readers announce the filename or nothing.
- All 10 Dotomi pixels and both first-party tracking pixels also lack alt text. These are non-content images, so they should use `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) to be explicitly ignored.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm other issues such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables or `bgcolor` fallbacks.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found unresolved in the delivered HTML. It looks like a review-content or product-rating injection placeholder that didn't render. Depending on where it sits, subscribers may see the literal string, or it may leave an empty block or a broken link or image URL.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found. The QA output notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven violation. If the sender omits them, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules would be unmet.
- `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` matches the image and tracking domains (`image.mail.cb2.com`, `mi.cb2.com`), but that doesn't verify authentication.
- The visible unsubscribe link and physical address are not confirmed, because the HTML was truncated and QA reported no check for them.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No verifiable findings. The 73 tracked links were not probed, so UTM propagation and landing page alignment can't be assessed from the data provided.
- The `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixel URLs carry `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers. These suggest Movable Ink or a similar platform, but the visible parameters aren't UTMs.

7. **Recommendations**
1. **Fix the merge token (high priority).** Add a fallback for `%%reviews_skulist%%` or suppress the block when no data is returned. Add a pre-send seed test that greps the rendered HTML for `%%`.
2. **Add alt text.** Give the 12 content images descriptive `alt` text. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Use `https://` for `ads.dotomi.com` and `login.dotomi.com`, or drop the 11 sync calls if they aren't essential.
4. **Verify the headers on a direct-delivery copy.** Send to a seed inbox that isn't behind the AgentMail relay, then confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
5. **Improve QA coverage.** Resolve the tracked redirect links, or follow them in a headless check, so UTM and landing page continuity can be audited. Also capture the full HTML so unsubscribe and address checks can run.
