# Up to 25% off rarely-on-sale upholstery
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The upholstery hero is strong. "UP TO 25% OFF" in large light type sits over a rich taupe tufted sectional, and five stacked white buttons (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches & Ottomans, Shop All) give clear category paths. It matches the subject, and the eye lands on the offer first.
- Then the email dilutes itself. Below the hero it adds a $5,000 giveaway with a design-appointment CTA, then a separate "Fall Design Sale, up to 50% off" block. That makes three offers in one send. The 50% banner overshadows the 25% the subject promised, and the "rarely-on-sale" angle from the subject never appears in the body. This is the same stacked-promo pattern as CB2's 10/01 "New arrivals are here" and "UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK" sends. A subscriber who has seen the Upholstery Event three times this week gets little new here.
- Judgement: a clean, polished hero with a cluttered, repetitive body. It is a solid but unremarkable send.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 25% OFF" hero is instantly legible, with a high-contrast offer over a tactile product image.
- The five category buttons are large, tappable and specific, so the shopper can go straight to their category.
- The footer packs in useful loyalty hooks: 10% back in Rewards Dollars, the app, and 15% off for a text signup.
- The photography is consistent and premium in warm neutrals, so the brand voice is trusted and on-season.
- The "$5,000 refresh" sweepstakes gives a reason to engage beyond the discount.

## What's weak

- Three competing offers (25% off, a $5K giveaway, 50% off) split attention, and the 50% banner undercuts the 25% hero.
- The "rarely-on-sale" scarcity story from the subject is never reinforced in the body.
- No end date is visible near the hero. The deadline is buried in the fine print at the bottom.
- The $5,000 section has a lot of text over a busy fabric image, and its "Book a free design appointment" CTA is high-friction.
- The Fall Design Sale block shows no product detail, so it reads as a generic mood image.
- The legal fine print is very long and dense. It is the only place the exclusions appear.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Lead with the upholstery event and move the Fall Design Sale to a separate send. If both stay, label them clearly as "Upholstery 25%" and "Everything else 50%."
- 2. Put the deadline next to the hero ("Ends Oct 14"), since it currently appears only in the fine print.
- 3. Back up the "rarely on sale" claim with a few named products and prices (for example "Sofa from $X, now $Y") plus a review star or fabric swatch for proof.
- 4. Move the sweepstakes below the product content or into a slim banner so it doesn't interrupt the sale flow.
- 5. Trim the footer to the one or two strongest loyalty hooks.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sofas rarely go on sale. This week they do.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `25% off sectionals ends Oct 14`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, sofas and accent chairs, up to 25% off through Oct 14.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free shipping on select upholstery and a shot at a $5K refresh.`

- **Insecure pixels:** Eleven third-party pixels load over plain `http://` and may be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
