## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

The email is a Movable Ink–driven template with 86 tracked links and about 29 content images. The truncated HTML and the AgentMail relay limit what can be checked. Confirmed problems are missing image alt text, viewport and text-size settings that block zoom, and some template hygiene issues. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **86 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped the click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Six identical dynamic image calls.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times with the same path and `mi_u` token. This is probably one Movable Ink render reused across slots, but it can't be confirmed from the truncated source.
- **Five third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` are LiveRamp beacons, fired five times per open. Each needs a separate request and adds third-party data sharing.
- **Open and dynamic-content beacons.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a623c400-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are Movable Ink. Neither has alt text, which is harmless for pixels but shows up in the QA noise.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **29 content images lack alt text.** All are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` or `/m/47/…`. With images blocked, product and category tiles will be blank. Examples:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `3930ebb6-ec2c-4396-aa4c-fd7eb34265da.jpg`
  - `8cdfe5b0-6fc9-43e0-98b6-1ab386ab2aad.gif`
  - `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`
  
  The QA check may count `alt=""` as missing, so confirm which images are meant to be decorative.
- **Pinch-zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents zooming on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `*` and `body`, and the `* {…}` rule also sets `line-height:100%`. This overrides user text-size preferences and forces tight line height everywhere.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` comes before `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Invalid `<title>`.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The title is also generic.
- **Duplicate and conflicting `format-detection` meta tags.** There are five or more declarations, some repeating the first (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`).
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It is harmless but misspelled.
- **Unclosed meta tag.** `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. Movable Ink personalization parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) are populated, though the `mi_u` hash appears in image URLs and can identify the recipient.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) are missing from the captured headers.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so treat this as unverified, not confirmed absent. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with a properly delegated sending subdomain.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address not verified.** The source is truncated before the footer, so neither can be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

Not verifiable. The 86 click-tracking URLs were skipped, so final destinations and any UTM parameters are unknown. Nothing in the visible HTML shows direct `crateandbarrel.com` links with UTMs, which suggests tracking is handled by the redirect layer.

## 7. Recommendations

1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 29 content images, or `alt=""` on decorative ones, and add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `line-height:100%` from `*`.
3. Fix the `img` max-width media queries by merging them or ordering them correctly.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and use a descriptive title. Remove the placeholder comment and the duplicate `format-detection` tags.
5. Consolidate the five LiveRamp pixels into one request, or confirm the repeats are intentional.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` source to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address.
7. Resolve the 86 tracked links in a follow-up probe to check destinations, redirect depth and UTM carryover.
