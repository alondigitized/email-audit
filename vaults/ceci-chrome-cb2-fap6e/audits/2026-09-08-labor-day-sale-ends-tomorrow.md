# LABOR DAY SALE ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's Labor Day formula in near-final form, and structurally it's one of the stronger sends in the run: a live countdown clock (01:07:10:26) up top, a clean "ENDS TOMORROW" hero with a clear 50% off callout and a single "SHOP THE SALE" button, followed by a full category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) that gives the reader multiple entry points instead of one generic CTA.
- The problem is the same one that's dogged this brand all week: the Double Rewards credit-card cross-sell block, stapled onto the bottom of a merchandise sale email, still has the unfixed render bug from 2026-09-07's "DOUBLE REWARDS§ IS ALMOST OVER" — "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" with a strikethrough 10% and 20% stacked together. That's now been visible in at least two consecutive sends without correction, which reads as a QA gap rather than a one-off glitch.
- Overall this is a competent, well-organized sale email dragged down by a persistent, visible defect and by fatigue — this is at least the sixth near-identical "Labor Day, up to 50% off" send this stretch. Fix the reward-dollars bug before anything else goes out.

## What's working

- Countdown timer at the top is live and specific (01:07:10:26), reinforcing "ends tomorrow" with a credible deadline.
- Hero is clean: single background image, one headline, one offer (up to 50% off), one CTA button.
- Category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) gives shoppers a direct path into their category instead of dumping everyone on a homepage.
- Each category row has its own "SHOP NOW" button, so intent doesn't get lost in a long scroll.
- Footer surfaces loyalty program value (App download, 10% Rewards Dollars) clearly separated from the credit-card pitch.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards block has a visible render bug: "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" shows two numbers overlapping/stacked with a strikethrough — this is the same defect seen in the 2026-09-07 send and it's still live.
- Two competing sale asks in one email (merchandise sale + credit card sign-up) split attention and dilute the primary offer.
- No product-level detail (price, dimensions, fabric) anywhere in the grid — every tile is a lifestyle shot plus a category label, so there's nothing to anchor a click beyond "browse this room."
- This is functionally the same "up to 50% off, ends tomorrow" creative CB2 has run repeatedly since 2026-09-02; a subscriber opening 6+ of these gets no new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Double Rewards "10% 20%" overlap before the next send — this is a shipped visual bug on a financial offer, which is a trust issue, not just a cosmetic one.
- 2. Cut the Double Rewards block from this email entirely, or move it to its own dedicated send — it competes with the sale CTA and has caused the only two render bugs seen in this whole campaign stretch.
- 3. Add one concrete product or price point in the hero or top category tile (e.g., "Sofas from $599") to give the eye something specific to click toward, not just a category name.
- 4. Since this is final-hours messaging, consider swapping the static countdown for a subject/preheader that names the actual cutoff time (e.g., "11:59pm PT") to make the deadline feel more real.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 50% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day Sale — final hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting & more — before the clock hits zero.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites won't be this price tomorrow.`
