# Ends soon: Early Black Friday deals + 2x Rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth or fifth Early Black Friday send from Crate & Barrel Kids in 48 hours. It is a long, offer-heavy blast that adds nothing new. The red hero ("last Double Rewards this year! … early black friday is here! up to 50% off") is clear, and the "Shop the Sale" button is easy to find. The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can't read the fine print.
- The "ends soon" urgency is credible, since the Nov. 17 Rewards deadline appears twice. Below the hero the email stacks too much. There is a 2x2 category grid, a repeated Rewards banner, a 60% off clearance block, the $2,700+ giveaway, a Black Friday signup, a product carousel, a credit-card promo, an app download and a design-service pitch. The giveaway was already the lead of this morning's "Christmas came early!" send, so it is recycled creative. Nothing is personalized to a parent or nursery shopper, apart from the stroller and nursery product picks.
- Judgement: competent but fatigued. A subscriber who got the Nov. 14 "The best deals of the year: 2x Rewards + Up to 50% off" and "ON NOW! The last Double Rewards of the year!" sends will see this as a repeat, and the "Ends soon" label is the only real change.

## What's working

- The red hero panel puts the 2x Rewards offer and up to 50% off at the top, with a "Shop the Sale" button.
- The Nov. 17 Rewards deadline gives the urgency a specific date.
- The 2x2 grid of furniture, bedding, baby gear and rugs has clear category labels and a percent off on each.
- The "we love these for you" product block shows named strollers and furniture with prices, which gives the email some concrete products.
- The brand look is consistent and clean, with no visible broken images.

## What's weak

- The Rewards message appears in the hero and again in a second red banner mid-email, so it is repeated.
- Too many CTAs compete: Shop the Sale, Shop Top Categories, Learn More, Shop Clearance, Enter Now, Sign Up Now, Learn More, Download Now and Book a Free Appointment.
- The giveaway block was already the lead of an earlier send today, so it is stale.
- The "early black friday is here!" hero headline is cramped over the nursery photo, and the small text under it is hard to read.
- The email is very long and ends in dense legal copy. Several offers (the credit card, the app, the design pro) aren't relevant to the "ends soon" message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid and one product row, and move the app, credit card, giveaway and design-service blocks to other sends. That would lift this email the most.
- 2. Use one primary CTA wording, such as "Shop Early Black Friday", and repeat it only once.
- 3. Put a countdown or "Nov. 17" in the hero headline so the deadline is visible at a glance.
- 4. Move the stroller and nursery products higher, so the hero offer sits next to something to buy.
- 5. Space out the sends and rotate the creative, so subscribers aren't getting near-identical promos several times a day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a heavily tracked, template-built (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, MSO/VML-aware) send. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are unverified, and the HTML was truncated, so the footer and link-level checks below could not be made.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations unverified:** 75 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe (click-redirect domains). Redirect chains, 404s and final landing URLs are unknown.
- **Pixel count:** The source references several tracking beacons:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (5 LiveRamp calls)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/844e9f2b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Repeated asset:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. Whether these are per-slot dynamic content images is not confirmed.
- **Mixed image hosts:** Images are served from three hosts: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `s7d5.scene7.com` and `mi.crateandbarrel.com`. Scene7 is a third-party domain not aligned with the sender domain. This is not a defect, but it is another dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** About 30 images have no `alt` attribute. Examples:
  - Content images: `…/m/1/26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`, `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, and the animated `…/8c4ce5d9-cf7b-4fbe-ba17-b9e2435a0e16.gif`.
  - Scene7 asset: `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  - Spacer: `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - If these images are blocked or read by a screen reader, content and CTAs may be lost or announced as raw filenames.
  - Decorative images and pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, the spacer) should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
  - Content images need descriptive alt text, especially any that carry offer copy or CTAs.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html { -webkit-text-size-adjust:none; }` override the `100%` set elsewhere in the same `<style>` blocks. This blocks iOS text resizing and the CSS is internally contradictory.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.
  - The title does not reflect the subject line.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times (one combined, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` again).
  - A leftover template placeholder remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Responsive breakpoints:** Mobile rules key off `max-width:460px`, and the min-width rules only cap `img {max-width}` (600px at ≥768px, 640px at ≥640px, with the 640px rule's source order governing the 640–767px range). Devices between 461px and 639px get the desktop table layout. This is unconfirmed without a render.

## 3a. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. Tokens such as `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` appear to be resolved per-recipient hashes, not unrendered placeholders. The truncated body could hide tokens further down.

## 4. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** The header was not found. The QA tool notes this "may not be captured by AgentMail relay", so this is unconfirmed, not proven absent.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so verify against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, again an expected relay artifact. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From:` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a branded subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this is not evidence.
- **Footer:** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so they could not be checked.

## 5. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 75 links went through tracking redirects that were not resolved, and the destination URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible in the supplied source. The only parameters visible on beacons are `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. Nothing shows whether landing pages match the "Early Black Friday + 2x Rewards" offer.

## 6. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the spacer and tracking pixels. This is the largest confirmed issue.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag.
3. Remove the global `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `*` and `html` so text can scale.
4. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), consider making it descriptive, and delete the template placeholder comment and the duplicate `format-detection` metas.
5. Check the raw message headers outside the AgentMail relay for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 75 tracked links to confirm final URLs, UTMs and status codes.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and postal address can be checked.
