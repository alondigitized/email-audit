# This sale pairs so well with free shipping
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the same kitchen-sink send CB2 has run all week, with a better subject line. The hero is a martini glass tied with a red ribbon on a coffee-table book, captioned "Have you heard? Shipping, on us. Orders of $99 or more." It is clean and moody, and the offer and the SHOP NOW button are both easy to read. Below it, the email adds a decor/dining/pillows/bedding grid, the Long Weekend Sale (up to 60% off), a $5,000 design-consult sweepstakes, and an app/rewards/signup block. That makes four or five messages in one send.
- Unlike the 10/11 "This sale only happens twice a year" email, this subject matches the content. The subject promises a sale paired with free shipping, but the hero is only free shipping. The sale, the real value driver, sits about 40% of the way down and is not mentioned in the hero. The 10/10 "2 DEALS & ONLY DAYS LEFT" and 10/09 "So we're doing a little thing" sends reused the same free-shipping hero and the same 60% block. A subscriber would see this as one more rotation of the same creative.
- Verdict: a polished, on-brand email with a competent subject, but it is diluted by too many competing blocks and by cadence fatigue. The $99 free-shipping threshold is a weak lead for someone shopping furniture.

## What's working

- The hero reads fast: "Shipping, on us" with the $99 threshold and a visible SHOP NOW button.
- The subject line matches the content and links the sale to free shipping, which is better than the vague 10/09 "So we're doing a little thing."
- The photography and styling are premium and consistent with CB2's brand voice.
- The Long Weekend Sale block has big "UP TO 60% OFF" type and its own "Shop the Sale" button.
- The category tiles (Decor, Pillows & Throws, Bedding & Bath) give a clear home-category entry point.

## What's weak

- The strongest value, up to 60% off, is buried below the hero and the tile grid, even though the subject promises a sale.
- The hero CTA, "Shop Now," is generic and goes to no specific product or collection.
- Four or five competing messages dilute the focus: shipping, sale, sweepstakes, app, rewards, signup.
- The 60% offer carries a footnote asterisk, and the footer has a wall of dense legal text covering exclusions, sweepstakes and shipping terms. That makes the offer feel less transparent.
- The tiles are small, with tiny labels and no price points, and the sweepstakes block is a design-appointment CTA with a long date range. Neither fits a quick-scan buyer.
- This is the third or fourth near-identical free-shipping/60%-off send in about three days, and no deadline is visible for the sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the sale in the hero. Put "Up to 60% off + free shipping over $99" in the first screen, so the hero delivers what the subject promises.
- 2. Cut the sweepstakes and the app/rewards/signup blocks from sale sends. Keep one offer plus one secondary category grid.
- 3. Change "Shop Now" to a specific verb and noun, such as "Shop 60% off decor," and show a few product tiles with prices.
- 4. Add a visible end date, such as "Ends Monday 11:59 PM PT," to the sale block.
- 5. Vary the creative and cadence. Stop recycling the same free-shipping banner across consecutive days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off, plus free shipping $99+`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Long Weekend Sale: decor from $19, ships free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pillows, throws, bedding and more, all up to 60% off through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $99+ ends with the sale. Shop decor before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, was left unresolved. The QA data shows no confirmed compliance failures, and the header checks are inconclusive because the relay may not have captured the headers. Alt text and mixed-content problems come mostly from third-party tracking pixels.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status are unverified.
- Several pixels load over plain `http://`, which can be blocked or rewritten by mail clients:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- These are Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixels, 11 in total. Each is a third-party request that adds weight, adds failure points, and raises privacy exposure. Where mail clients block or proxy them, the sync silently fails.
- The tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/089b45c3-...?mi_cid=...` use HTTPS and are fine on transport. The `mi_u` parameter appears to be a hashed per-recipient identifier, so check it against your privacy policy.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven content images have no `alt` attribute. All are on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `0233b4a1-…jpg`
  - `24db4fee-…jpg`
  - `d11449a4-…jpg`
  - `bb97c1bd-…jpg`
  - `612fd189-…jpg`
  - `0eb8fda3-…jpg`
  - `962dd823-…jpg`
  
  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show nothing or the filename.
- The tracking and cookie-sync pixels (`o.gif`, `089b45c3-…`, `pub1–10.php`, `UCMController`) also lack `alt`. These should use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"` or be hidden, so they are skipped by assistive tech. This is low severity.
- The HTML source is truncated, so I can't confirm whether the footer or the viewport meta tag cause other issues. Two things in the visible head are worth noting:
  - The viewport has `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility concern.
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and the same setting on `body` stop users from enlarging text.
- The `lang="en"` and `<title>` are present, which is good.
- `@font-face` rules reference `crateandbarrel.com` and `cb2.com`. Web fonts are widely unsupported in email clients, and the declared fallbacks (Helvetica, sans-serif) are adequate.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found in the delivered HTML. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization string that did not resolve. It points to a dynamic-content block (probably product reviews or ratings) that failed to populate at send time.
- The effect is either visible garbage text or a blank block, depending on where the token sits in the markup. I can't tell which from the truncated source.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is inconclusive and not a confirmed violation.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found. This is also inconclusive for the same reason. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` can't be verified from the data provided.
- The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links are wrapped in the ESP click-redirect domain, and 61 were not probed. I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page destinations from the available data.
- The Dotomi `cli_promo_id` parameter is present in the pixel URL, so campaign attribution runs through a third-party system as well.
- The subject line promises a sale with free shipping. Whether the landing pages show that offer can't be verified without resolving the redirects.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix the merge token (high).** Resolve `%%reviews_skulist%%`, or add a fallback so the block hides when the data is empty. Add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%…%%` string in the rendered output.
2. **Add `alt` text (medium).** Give the seven `image.mail.cb2.com` content images meaningful `alt` values. Set `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
3. **Move Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (medium).** Switch to `https://` endpoints if Dotomi supports them. Otherwise drop the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels, since they add fragility and weight for little value.
4. **Verify headers at the source (medium).** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` in the raw message at the destination mailbox, not through the relay.
5. **Resolve the redirects (medium).** Follow the 61 tracked links in a separate pass to confirm UTM parameters and landing-page status.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` (low).** Allow pinch-zoom, and avoid `text-size-adjust:none`.
