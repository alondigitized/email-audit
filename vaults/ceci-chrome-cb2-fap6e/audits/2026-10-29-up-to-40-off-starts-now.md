# UP TO 40% OFF STARTS NOW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand sale announcement. The "GUEST READY SALE, up to 40% off" hero is a collage of bedding, tableware, mirrors and a pendant light on a paper-textured card. The offer sits in the middle of the image, so the eye lands there first. The Warehouse Sale banner below it ("UP TO 60% OFF") is the stronger price claim, and it is a bigger number than the one in the subject line.
- The email is also the second CB2 send of the day. The "Nina Garcia Collection" launch went out earlier on 10/29, and "FINAL HOURS" and "WAREHOUSE SALE IS SELLING OUT" both went out on 10/28. Two sales running side by side dilutes both, and "starts now" is vague with no deadline anywhere above the fine print. It is a competent 7 rather than a standout: tidy and elegant, but low on urgency and specificity.

## What's working

- The hero is a bold, uncluttered "up to 40% off" on a central card, with "It's a five-star affair" as a fitting hosting-season line.
- The Warehouse Sale block has a strong "UP TO 60% OFF" over a moody floral image, with a solid white "SHOP THE SALE" button that stands out.
- The CB2 logo and a simple five-item nav at the top make the sender and category clear at once.
- The footer grid (app, 10% back Rewards, gift list, 15% off for text signup) gives useful secondary paths without crowding the sale.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or gaps.

## What's weak

- The primary hero CTA is a small underlined "SHOP THE SALE" text link, not a button, so it is easy to miss on mobile.
- There is no visible deadline. "Ends November 5" and "Ends November 4" only appear in the fine print at the bottom.
- The subject leads with the smaller offer (40%) while the email's biggest number is the 60% Warehouse Sale.
- The hero collage shows no named product or price point, so nothing specific invites a click.
- The fine print is very long ("select furniture, rugs, bedding, decor", exclusions, a reviews giveaway), which undercuts the "up to" claims.
- This is the second send of the day, and the brand has run several overlapping sale emails this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible end date near the hero ("Ends Nov 5") and a live countdown. The 10/27 countdown send was the strongest of the recent ones.
- 2. Turn the hero link into a full button, and make it say what it links to, for example "Shop Guest Ready Bedding & Decor".
- 3. Pick one headline offer. Either lead with 60% off and fold the 40% into it, or keep the two sales in separate sends.
- 4. Replace the collage with three to four hero products showing "from $" prices, so there is something specific to click.
- 5. Reduce the cadence. Don't send a sale email on the same day as a collection launch.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has accessibility gaps and 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels. The compliance and authentication findings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay, so they are unverified rather than confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **11 insecure tracking pixels.** All load over `http://`, from `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`. These are third-party cookie-sync pixels. Clients that enforce HTTPS or proxy images will block them, so they won't fire and add nothing but payload.
- **Tracking links not probed.** 56 click-redirect links were skipped, so I can't confirm their destinations or whether they resolve. Link integrity is unverified, not clean.
- **Double slash in font URLs.** `@font-face` sources use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`. These are probably tolerated by the server, but they are inconsistent with the single-slash URLs elsewhere. Several `@font-face` rules also pull fonts from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain from the sender domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Raw merge token (FAIL).** `%%reviews_skulist%%` is visible in the output (see section 4).
- **Missing alt text on content images.** `d50197d7-ca4f-4fb8-8a46-bbd7e7cad8dd.gif` and `8436ffd4-f83c-4d1b-83e1-4a1d789552c3.jpg` (both at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`) have no `alt`. With images off, or in a screen reader, that content is lost. If these carry the offer, the "UP TO 40% OFF" message is lost as well.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels.** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/ce384e37-...` and the 11 Dotomi pixels have no `alt`. Pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly so screen readers skip them. This is low severity.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template leftover.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed. It is harmless to rendering but shows the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **Present and fine.** `<html lang="en">`, a `<title>` tag, and VML/MSO conditional comments for Outlook.
- **Limit of this review.** The HTML was truncated, so body structure, `<table role>` usage, and live text versus image-only content could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed failure:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found. The `%%…%%` syntax is Salesforce Marketing Cloud's personalization-string format. It most likely belongs to a product-reviews dynamic content block that did not resolve at send time. Recipients will see the literal string, or an empty or broken module where the review content should be. It may also be the source of the 43% pass rate.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes that AgentMail may strip them, so I can't confirm whether the sender omitted them. The headers matter because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again because of the relay.
- **Visible footer not verified.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 56 tracking links were redirect-wrapped and not probed, and the HTML was truncated. I can't confirm UTM parameters, final destinations, or whether the landing pages match the "UP TO 40% OFF" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the SFMC dynamic content or AMPscript for the reviews block. Add a fallback so the module is suppressed when the data source returns empty, and add a pre-send test that scans rendered output for `%%…%%`.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS**, or drop them. Confirm with the vendor that `https://` endpoints exist. Otherwise these 11 requests are dead weight in many clients.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the two content images (`d50197d7….gif`, `8436ffd4….jpg`). Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify headers at the source.** Check the raw message in a direct seed inbox, not through the AgentMail relay, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. **Re-run with full HTML and link resolution.** Capture the footer, UTM parameters and redirect chains for the 56 tracking links.
7. **Clean up the template**: remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment and the double-slash font URLs.
