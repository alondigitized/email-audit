# SO MUCH NEW
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- "SO MUCH NEW" is CB2 flipping from its recent sale drumbeat — five emails in three days culminating in yesterday's double-send ("Après Pool" + "MORE ON SALE") — to a new-arrivals reset. That's the right editorial instinct. The email is visually well-produced: a warm cognac sofa anchors the hero, pendant lights and decorative objects fill a tidy product grid, and the "$5,000 Refresh" sweepstakes adds an engagement layer that most new-arrivals emails skip.
- The problem is almost entirely in the subject line. "SO MUCH NEW" is one of the weakest possible ways to signal a new arrivals drop. It's all-caps, vague, and gives the inbox nothing to act on — no category, no standout piece, no reason why today is the moment to click. The email earns its open on brand equity alone, not on copy.
- Inside, the work is solid. The hierarchy is clean, CTAs are visible, and the sweepstakes breaks the monotony of straight shop-and-scroll. But after a floor sample sale, two lifestyle editorials, and a clearance blast all in 72 hours, even a well-produced new-arrivals email risks getting lost to inbox fatigue.

## What's working

- Hero sofa image is immediately arresting — warm cognac tones contrast cleanly against the headline copy.
- Product grid showcases range (seating, lighting, decorative objects) without feeling cluttered.
- "$5,000 Refresh" sweepstakes is a smart engagement hook that breaks the monotony of pure browse-and-shop.
- "Come find us in person" section adds a physical retail dimension that reinforces brand heft.
- No visible render bugs — clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "SO MUCH NEW" in all-caps is the single worst creative call in this email — generic, shouty, and actionless.
- No price offer of any kind; for subscribers primed by six consecutive sale emails, a no-discount send needs a stronger editorial hook to justify the send.
- Inbox fatigue is real — this is CB2's fifth email in three days; even good creative struggles against that cadence.
- The sweepstakes ($5,000 Refresh) is buried mid-scroll rather than incorporated into the hero where it could lift opens and clicks simultaneously.
- CTAs appear to be generic "SHOP" labels; no visible specificity (e.g., "Shop New Sofas") to direct intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rewrite the subject line entirely.** Name the category or the hero piece. "New sofa just dropped" or "Warm up your living room — new arrivals this week" beats all-caps vagueness by a wide margin.
- 2. **Pull the sweepstakes into the hero.** "$5,000 to refresh your space — shop new arrivals for your shot" fuses the engagement hook with the browse CTA and gives the inbox row a concrete reason to click.
- 3. **Add specificity to CTAs.** "Shop New Sofas," "Shop New Lighting" rather than undifferentiated "Shop" buttons.
- 4. **Enforce a send-cadence pause.** After five emails in three days, even a one-day gap before the next send would improve open rates on this and subsequent emails.
- 5. **Let the hero product breathe.** Consider a single-product spotlight email (like the July 17 "This rug is instant character" format, scored 8/10) rather than a grid that dilutes any one product's impact.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4 violation)**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users with low vision from zooming. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied to all elements**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; }
```
Overrides user font size preferences system-wide. Should be `100%` on `body` only, not `none` on `*`.

**Mobile breakpoint at `max-width: 418px` is too narrow**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width: 418px) { .mobile { width: 414px !important; } }
```
iPhone 15 Pro Max is 430px wide — this breakpoint won't fire on it. Standard safe value is `600px`.

**Double-slash in three font URLs**
```
https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2
https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownRegular/lineto-brown-regular.woff2
https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/GTSectra/GT-Sectra-Display-Regular.woff2
```
Technically malformed. Most servers tolerate it, but it indicates a copy-paste error in the template.

**`font-display: swap` has no effect in email**
Used in four `@font-face` blocks — this is a browser loading hint that all email clients ignore. Noise, not functional harm.

**`line-height: 100%` on `*` selector**
```css
* { line-height: 100%; }
```
Causes tight line spacing on multi-line text. Should be scoped to `p, td`, not universal.

**`max-height: 1000000px` on `*` selector**
Standard Gmail image-scaling hack but applied globally — can clip content in clients that respect it literally.
