# TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-brand Crate & Barrel send, but it has a bait-and-switch problem. The subject promises "TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!". The hero then reads "only in the app: 20% off mirrors, today only!" and the button says "Download Our App". The offer is real and the urgency is credible. It is also gated behind an install, and the subject never says so. A subscriber who taps through for mirrors lands on an app-store task.
- The rest of the email dilutes the message. Below the hero, the second block says "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" but is illustrated with a wool rug. That is a different offer, in a different category, with a higher percentage that makes the mirror deal look small. After that come a Halloween banner, a six-item "we love these for you" grid with no mirrors, a credit-card block, a second app-download block and a Design Desk block. The send is more like a catalog than a one-day mirror promo.
- On the brand's cadence: yesterday (10/27) this sender hit the inbox with at least four sends, including "Up to 30% off: Accent chairs & tables!" That makes today's accent-chair block feel recycled. Verdict: a strong hero and a credible deadline, undercut by the app gate, a mismatched second offer and a long tail. It lands around a 6.

## What's working

- The hero is visually strong. A large mirror lifestyle shot sits under bold "20% off mirrors, today only!" copy, and the offer reads at a glance.
- "TODAY ONLY" is real urgency, backed by fine print saying the sale runs through October 28.
- The brand is instantly recognizable, with the logo at the top and consistent typography and palette.
- The seasonal Halloween block ("so scary, it's chic") fits the date.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows clear product photos with names and prices, so it is easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The offer is app-only, but the subject doesn't say so, and the only hero CTA is "Download Our App". That adds friction and feels like a bait-and-switch.
- The second block says "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" over a rug photo. The image and headline don't match, and the bigger discount competes with the mirror offer.
- No mirror products are shown after the hero. The grid is candles, vases and a ceramic church, so the one-day offer is never reinforced.
- The email has too many competing CTAs: Download Our App (twice), Shop Now, Shop Halloween, Learn More, Book a Free Appointment and the footer nav.
- The "Save on designer-favorite pieces. Select styles." line is vague and sits in a dark brown band that adds length.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer and CTA match. Either offer 20% off mirrors for everyone with a "Shop Mirrors" button, or put "in the app" in the subject and use "Open in App" as a secondary link. Don't hide the gate.
- 2. Cut the accent chair and rug block. It conflicts with the mirror offer and repeats yesterday's send.
- 3. Replace the generic product grid with 3–6 actual mirrors, with prices and a "20% off" badge on each.
- 4. Shorten the email. Keep the hero, a mirror grid and one secondary block. Move the card, Design Desk and second app blocks to a weekly send.
- 5. Add a visible countdown or "ends at midnight CT" line under the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Today only: 20% off mirrors, in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Mirror sale ends tonight: 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `App-exclusive pricing on round, arched and floor mirrors. Ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designer-favorite mirrors, 20% off through tonight. Select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/Cheetah-style XHTML template with a lang attribute and a mobile media query. The QA run found 19 images with no `alt`, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and CSS that removes focus outlines. Several compliance and tracking checks couldn't be confirmed because the HTML source was truncated and the relay may have stripped headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** The QA run skipped 51 tracking links ("Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination status, redirect chain, and final URLs are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels fire on open. They carry the same `s=` identifier, and the only difference is `n`. This is a third-party data-sync call (rlcdn.com is LiveRamp) and is worth a privacy review.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa278…` appears on every `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif` image. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b310734f-…` carries `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`. These are expected for dynamic content and open tracking, but any forwarded copy exposes them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (19 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`:
    - `bbf6f9d4-…png`
    - `4eb578f1-…jpg`
    - `f3246943-…gif`
    - `78fef553-…gif`
    - `971aa10f-…jpg`
  - Dynamic product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`:
    - `560d850e7e517ede.png`
    - `789e7381ff44be74.png`
    - `5ec241ce0b852c66.png`
    - `00423cc9007b550c.png`
    - `f5ca909404423879.png`
    - `45e8c84f5a9185ff.png`
    - `18f126218cc222ec.png`
  - Tracking pixels: `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (×5), `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/b310734f-…`.
  - Content images need descriptive text. Pixels should use `alt=""`. With images blocked, a screen reader or text-only client gets no content from the 12 content images.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in the `*{}` rule also stops text scaling.
- **Focus outline removed:** `a { outline:0; }` removes keyboard focus indication on all links.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`). `format-detection` is declared three times and conflicts with itself: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. Browsers and clients tolerate it.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. Nothing renders from it, but it is dead template text.
- **Dead breakpoint rule:** At ≥768px both `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` match. The later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule never applies.
- **Narrow mobile breakpoint:** The main mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports from 461 to 639px get neither the mobile layout nor a desktop-fit rule.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`). The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No `List-Unsubscribe` header:** The QA run found none. It notes this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header:** The RFC 8058 header is also missing, so one-click unsubscribe can't be verified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **No `Authentication-Results` header:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain, so alignment depends on the DNS records, which weren't checked.
- **Body footer not verifiable:** The truncated source doesn't show the in-body unsubscribe link or the physical postal address CAN-SPAM requires. Both need a check against the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 51 links are click-redirect wrappers that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be confirmed. No continuity issue is evidenced.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 content and product images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the universal `*` rule. Replace `a { outline:0 }` with a visible `:focus` style.
3. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. This separates relay stripping from real omissions.
4. Resolve the 51 redirect links with a redirect-following probe to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
5. Review the five `rlcdn.com` pixels for privacy and consent.
6. Clean up the template: escape `&` as `&amp;` in the title, remove the duplicate meta tags and the "IMPUT HERE" comment, and fix the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
7. Check the full HTML for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
