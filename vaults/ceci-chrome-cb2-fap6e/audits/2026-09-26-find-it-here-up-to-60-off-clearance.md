# Find it here: up to 60% off clearance!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel Kids running the same clearance play it ran September 25 ("New markdowns added! Up to 60% off clearance") — same dark, moody header treatment, same "up to 60%" headline, same footnote-asterisk vagueness. The hero itself undercuts its own message: it stacks "ending soon! up to 20% off the best baby essentials" directly above "up to 60% off clearance," so the first thing a scanning eye hits is two different percentages competing for attention before landing on the actual CTA.
- The bigger issue is further down: the Venus Williams collab block — same image, same copy, same "Shop the New Collab" button — is rendered twice back-to-back. That's not a stylistic choice, it reads as a broken send. Everything after it (Nuna travel system, "we love these for you" grid, Crate Card, app download, Design Desk) is competent, on-brand, and cleanly laid out, but the render bug and the muddled hero pull this down from what could've been a solid mid-tier send.
- Judgement: functional but sloppy — the offer is real and the bottom two-thirds of the email is well executed, but the duplicated block and dueling headlines are the kind of QA miss that makes a recipient trust the brand a little less.

## What's working

- The clearance CTA ("Shop Clearance") is a real button with good contrast against the autumn-leaf hero.
- The category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) gives multiple clear entry points instead of one flat homepage link.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real prices, including a visible markdown ($1450.00 → $1085.00), which builds trust in the offer.
- Nuna travel system section and Crate Card cross-sell are clean, single-focus blocks with clear imagery and CTAs.

## What's weak

- The Venus Williams "new kids collab" block is duplicated verbatim, back-to-back — same photo, same copy, same button. This is the most visible defect in the render.
- The hero has two competing offers ("20% off baby essentials" vs. "60% off clearance") with no visual subordination between them, so it's unclear which deal the email is actually about.
- No product-level detail (sizing, materials, stock/availability) appears anywhere near the product grid, so browsing still requires a click-through with no upfront filtering value.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof anywhere in the email despite a full scroll of product real estate.
- This is the brand's third near-identical clearance/urgency send in three days (Sept 25 markdowns, Sept 25 baby essentials final weekend), and this one adds nothing new to differentiate itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the duplicate block first** — this is a straightforward render/QA bug (same module fired twice) and is the single biggest credibility hit in this send; catch it in pre-send QA before anything else.
- 2. **Resolve the hero's competing headlines** — pick one lead offer (the 60% clearance is clearly the bigger number and probably the intended hero) and demote the baby-essentials 20% mention to a smaller secondary line or a separate email entirely.
- 3. **Add urgency with a real deadline** — "ending soon" with no date reads as filler; a specific cutoff ("through Sunday") would make the click feel more necessary.
- 4. **Space out clearance sends** — three clearance-flavored emails in three days trains subscribers to skim/ignore; alternate with non-discount content (styling tips, new arrivals) to protect open rates long-term.
- 5. **Add one social-proof element** to the product grid (star ratings under at least the top 2-3 items) since the grid otherwise has no differentiator beyond price.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids clearance: up to 60% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your kid's room, up to 60% off through Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, bedding, and playroom furniture — final markdowns before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New drops in clearance: rugs, storage, and nursery favorites`
