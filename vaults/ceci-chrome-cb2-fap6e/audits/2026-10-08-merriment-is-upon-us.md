# Merriment is upon us
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-made holiday catalog send with no offer. The "Holiday Collection" hero is a dark, candlelit room with a tree and a velvet sofa. It leads into six moody lifestyle tiles for dining, gifts, entertaining, barware, decor and ornaments. The photography is cohesive and expensive-looking, and it sets a mood that fits CB2's recent editorial sends, like the 10/5 "How to make an entrance" and the 10/8 "THIS BEST SELLING BED."
- The weakness is that nothing here gives a reason to act today. The only incentive is a "Download the app, win $1,000" sweepstakes block near the bottom, and there is no percent off, shipping offer or deadline. The sale emails earlier this week (10/7 "Up to 60% off", the 10/6 upholstery event) had a hard hook, and this one drops it. Because the body is almost all imagery and short labels, the reader has to supply their own motivation.
- Judgement: a pretty brand-building send that will do fine with people already in a holiday-shopping mood and little for anyone who needs a nudge. It's a mid-tier 6–7.

## What's working

- The hero is on-season and cinematic, with a clear "Shop the Collection" button right under it.
- The six lifestyle tiles each have a short verb-led label ("Shop Dining," "Shop Gifts," "Shop Entertaining," "Shop Decor"). The shopper can pick a lane quickly.
- The color palette of amber, burgundy and deep brown stays consistent from top to bottom, so the email reads as one story.
- A "More for You" product grid with visible prices (candle holders from roughly $12 to $25) gives a concrete product anchor after all the mood imagery.
- The footer has the app and rewards blocks, with a Crate & Barrel family strip.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer or deadline, so the email has no urgency.
- The tile labels are small and sit on busy photos, so some are hard to read, such as "Shop Entertaining" and "Dinnerware" overlaid on the images.
- Two competing CTAs ("Shop the Collection" and the app sweepstakes) pull attention in different directions. The $1,000 giveaway at the bottom is a distraction from shopping.
- The email is very long and mostly image-driven, with the product detail only at the end. Most readers will not scroll that far.
- This is another frequent CB2 send in a short stretch, and it is a softer message than the sale-driven emails before it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one real hook near the top, such as a holiday gifting deadline ("Order by Dec 15 for delivery by the 24th") or a specific offer. That single change would lift this email the most.
- 2. Put the price-anchored product row, or a "Best Sellers" strip, higher up and reduce the number of category tiles.
- 3. Make tile labels larger and give them a solid or gradient scrim so they stay readable on the photos.
- 4. Move the app and sweepstakes block into the footer as a quieter module. Keep one primary CTA in the email.
- 5. Make the subject say what's inside: the collection, a price point or a deadline.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining, gifting and glowing decor, styled for the season.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a table-based XHTML template with `lang="en"` and a viewport meta tag. It has one confirmed defect, a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token, plus about 15 content images with no alt text and 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels. The QA pass rate is 43%. Most of the compliance and tracking findings are unverified because of relay and truncation limits, not confirmed failures.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **74 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels load over `http://`.** These are the Dotomi (Epsilon) cookie-sync pixels:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  - Clients that block non-HTTPS content will drop these, so the sync will fail silently. Some clients may also show mixed-content warnings.
- **The Dotomi pixels carry no alt text.** That is 11 fire-and-forget pixels, which adds up to 10 cookie-sync calls per open.
- **First-party tracking beacons are present and HTTPS:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/ea231360-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
  - Both lack alt text. The `mi_u` value is a 64-character hex string, which looks like a hashed subscriber identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **15 content images lack alt text.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: 12 `.jpg` files and 3 `.gif` files, such as `391ada82-...jpg`, `af3c04f2-...gif`, `3e248843-...gif` and `8e957a75-...gif`. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, those sections have no text equivalent. If any are text-in-image banners, their content is unreadable.
- **Pinch-zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks zoom on iOS and Android.
- **User text resizing is disabled.** The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `*{-ms-text-size-adjust:none}`. Together with `*{line-height:100%}`, this hurts low-vision users.
- **Template residue.** The head still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Malformed font URLs.** Several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Fonts are also pulled cross-domain from `crateandbarrel.com`. Most clients strip web fonts anyway, but these fonts depend on that other domain staying available.
- The source was truncated, so the body markup, MSO conditional blocks beyond the head, and dark-mode handling could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is present** in the delivered HTML. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style merge variable (the `%%...%%` syntax) that was never resolved. That points to a dynamic product-reviews block that failed to populate or was left in the template. I couldn't see its location because of truncation. Depending on where it sits, it may show as visible text or sit in a hidden attribute or comment.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation. The sender should be checked for one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` is missing,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` was not verified.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address** are not visible in the truncated source and were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 74 links are click-redirect URLs that were skipped, so final destinations and UTM parameters could not be checked. Movable Ink parameters (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `mi_u`) appear on the first-party beacons only.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` token.** Check the Marketing Cloud template or data extension for the reviews block, and add a fallback so an empty value suppresses the block.
2. **Add alt text to the 15 content images,** and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS,** or drop them if the cookie-sync isn't needed.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` where it isn't needed for an Outlook workaround.
5. **Verify the authentication and unsubscribe items against raw headers** from a direct mailbox, not the relay. Check that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` exist, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
6. **Re-run link and UTM checks** by following the redirects, and check the full untruncated HTML for the footer.
