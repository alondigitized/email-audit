# TWO SALES END TONIGHT
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's legitimate final-push close on a full week of sale sends — the countdown reads 00:07:19:34, which makes "TWO SALES END TONIGHT" a factually honest hook with real teeth. The email does the correct structural thing: hero the July 4th online sale (50% off, tabletop) up top with the live timer, then drop the Warehouse Sale (60% off, in-store) below as a separate block. Two sales, clearly separated, no bait-and-switch confusion.
- The misfire is category curation. Tabletop as the anchor hero for the *final* send in a week-long furniture-forward sale sequence feels like a planning leftover, not a strategic close. Furniture, outdoor, and lighting have all carried this sequence in earlier sends — tabletop is a low-emotion, lower-AOV category to close on. The "MORE SAVINGS THIS WAY" grid beneath it scatters across chairs, rugs, sofas, lighting, and outdoor — which is the right instinct but the wrong execution at this stage. When you have 7 hours left on a clock, you narrow the funnel; you don't widen it.
- After the "ONE DAY LEFT" Warehouse send yesterday and "HOURS LEFT: JULY 4TH SALE" earlier today, this is technically the third CB2 send in under 24 hours. That's real fatigue risk — but this send earns its place because the timer is genuine. Solid mechanics, wrong hero category.

## What's working

- Countdown at 00:07:19:34 is the most credible urgency signal CB2 has deployed all week — it directly validates "TONIGHT."
- Two-sale architecture is clearly delineated: online July 4th (50% off) vs. Warehouse (60% off) — no confusion about which discount applies where.
- "MORE SAVINGS THIS WAY" category grid gives non-tabletop browsers a path deeper into the sale.
- 21-character subject line is clean, fast to parse on mobile, and unambiguous.
- No render bugs — images load, layout holds, nothing broken.

## What's weak

- Tabletop is the wrong hero for a week-closing furniture sale — it's low-emotional-weight and lower AOV than sofas, outdoor, or lighting.
- "SHOP NOW" is the primary CTA on *both* the hero and the warehouse block at 7 hours remaining — this is the single most wasted urgency opportunity in the send.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" personalization grid at the bottom shows small, low-contrast product cards that are nearly unreadable at mobile scale.
- Sender display name reads "CB2 LONG WKND SALE" rather than "CB2" — campaign suffixes add inbox noise without adding recognition lift.
- No loyalty or membership signal anywhere, despite the Crate&Barrel cross-brand footer presence.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero to furniture or sofa.** Furniture carried the emotional core of this week-long sale sequence from the start — close on your highest-AOV, highest-consideration category. Tabletop undercuts the send's gravity.
- 2. **Rewrite both CTAs with deadline anchoring.** "SHOP NOW" at 7 hours is invisible. "Shop Before Midnight" or "Claim 50% Off — Tonight Only" earns the countdown timer above it.
- 3. **Collapse the category grid to 2-3 items max.** At final-hours urgency, a 5-category scatter reads as noise. Show the top-performing categories from this sale week and cut the rest.
- 4. **Restore sender name to just "CB2."** Campaign suffixes provide zero inbox recognition lift and clutter the from field.
- 5. **Subject Alt A:** `50% Off Tabletop + 60% Warehouse — Tonight Only`
- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Final Hours: Two CB2 Sales End at Midnight`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `The clock just hit single digits — shop furniture, tabletop, and more before it's gone.`
- 8. **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 60% off in-store. Up to 50% off online. Both end tonight.`

## Technical Audit — CB2 "TWO SALES END TONIGHT"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table-based layout with acceptable ESP conventions. Three confirmed technical defects: malformed font asset URLs (double slashes), an unresolved template placeholder comment left in production HTML, and a viewport directive that breaks pinch-to-zoom accessibility.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully assess** — the HTML is truncated before any `<a href>` body links appear. The following is flagged from what is visible:

- **Font URLs with double slashes** — three `@font-face` `src` URLs contain `//` path segments that are technically malformed. While most servers handle this via redirect, it adds a latency hop and can fail on strict CDNs:
  - `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2`
  - `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownRegular/lineto-brown-regular.woff2`
  - `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/GTSectra/GT-Sectra-Display-Regular.woff2`
- **Cross-domain font load** — `HNW05` font family is served from `https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/` (four declarations). This is an inter-brand dependency; if that origin is unavailable or rate-limits, font rendering degrades silently. Should be served from `cb2.com` or a shared CDN.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport** — `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile. Fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `*`** — applied globally via the CSS reset. Combined with `maximum-scale=1`, this double-locks text scaling on iOS. Standard practice is `100%`, not `none`.
- **Mobile breakpoint at 418px only** — media query triggers at `max-width: 418px` and sets `.mobile { width: 414px }`. This misses the most common modern viewport widths (375px iPhone SE, 390px iPhone 14 Pro, 430px iPhone 15 Plus). The fixed 414px width inside a max-418px query is a narrow window that may clip on some devices.
- **`max-height:1000000px` on `*`** — Gmail clip workaround hack. Functional, but confirms the email body may be long enough to hit Gmail's ~102KB clip threshold. If the full HTML exceeds 102KB, Gmail will clip the message and hide the unsubscribe footer, which is a compliance risk.
- **Alt text** — not assessable from truncated source. Must verify all `<img>` tags carry descriptive `alt` attributes.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Unresolved template placeholder** — the following comment appears verbatim in the `<head>` of the production send:
  ```html
  <!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->
  ```
  This is a pre-production authoring note (with a typo: "IMPUT" → "INPUT") that was not stripped before deployment. It is invisible to recipients but visible in source inspection and indicates incomplete template hygiene.
- **`<!--V2-->` inline comment** — appears immediately after the `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration. Likely a version tag from the ESP template system. Should be removed before production sends.
- **No visible merge tokens in truncated source** — recipient name/email personalization not assessable from available HTML.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

- **Physical mailing address** — required by CAN-SPAM § 5(a)(5). Not visible in truncated source. Must confirm it appears in the footer.
- **Sending domain** — `cb2@mail.cb2.com`. The subdomain `mail.cb2.com` should have SPF, DKIM, and DMARC records aligned to `cb2.com`. Not verifiable from HTML alone; check with `dig TXT mail.cb2.com` and `dig TXT _dmarc.cb2.com`.
