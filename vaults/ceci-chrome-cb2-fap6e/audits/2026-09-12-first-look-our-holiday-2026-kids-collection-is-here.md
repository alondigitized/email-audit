# First look! Our holiday 2026 kids collection is here →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing a genuine seasonal reveal — "first look! the 2026 holiday kids collection" — led with a warm advent-calendar hero image and clean category nav (bedding, decor, stockings, toys & gifts). It's a strong open for anyone with kids, and the top third is well art-directed. But the email quickly loses its own thread: after the holiday hero, it pivots to a "new finishing touches at $29" home-decor block, then a $5,000 design consultation sweepstakes, then an unrelated "tennis-star" app-exclusive drop, a nursery-furniture product grid, a credit-card pitch, and an app-download CTA — none of which reinforce "holiday kids collection." This is the classic Crate family problem the brand history keeps flagging: it opens strong then sprawls into a scroll of disconnected modules and CTAs.
- Compared to the brand's baby registry send (9/10, tightly stacked on one theme) or the plates email (7/10, single product-led focus), this one squanders a great hook. The holiday collection itself is never shown again after the hero — no ornaments, no stockings, no toys pictured — so a reader drawn in by the subject gets bait-and-switched into a generic multi-category catalog. Verdict: nice top-of-funnel creative, poor follow-through; needs to be cut down to just the holiday assortment.

## What's working

- Hero image is warm, on-theme, and well-styled (child decorating an advent calendar with garland/ornaments visible).
- Subject-matching headline ("first look! the 2026 holiday kids collection") sits directly under the hero with clear category quick-links.
- "Shop All Holiday" button is a strong, unambiguous primary CTA right after the nav row.
- Clean modular layout with consistent white background and legible sans-serif type throughout — no render bugs, broken images, or overlapping text.
- Product grid ("we love these for you") shows real prices and clear product photography.

## What's weak

- The holiday collection promised in the subject is only shown once (the hero) — everything below (finishing touches, sweepstakes, tennis drop, nursery furniture, credit card, app) is unrelated to holiday kids shopping.
- Way too many competing CTAs (Shop All Holiday, Shop New Arrivals, Enter Now, Download Now, Learn More, Book a Free Appointment, Download Now again) dilute the primary action.
- The "$5,000" sweepstakes and "tennis star" collab blocks feel bolted on and break the seasonal narrative entirely.
- No pricing or specific holiday product callouts (ornaments, stockings, toys) — the subject's promise of a "first look" isn't delivered with actual holiday SKUs.
- Redundant app-download push appears twice in one email (mid-email banner and again near the footer).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to holiday-only content: hero + 3-4 actual holiday products (a stocking, an ornament set, a holiday bedding item, a toy) with prices — deliver on the "first look" promise instead of pivoting away from it.
- 2. Move the sweepstakes, tennis-collab drop, credit card pitch, and second app CTA into separate sends — each dilutes this email's single reason for existing.
- 3. Reduce CTA count to one primary ("Shop the Holiday Collection") and at most one secondary; right now there are seven distinct action buttons.
- 4. Add a scarcity/urgency element for holiday shopping (e.g., "limited holiday stock" or a ship-by date) since the subject implies newness/exclusivity but nothing in-body reinforces it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New stockings, decor and toys — shop before they're gone`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **All 24 images in the QA scan are missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (`s7d5.scene7.com/.../VenusWilliams...`) and spacer images (`25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White`). Tracking pixels/spacers missing alt is acceptable (should be `alt=""`), but content images without alt text fail WCAG 1.1.1 and will render as blank/broken placeholders with no fallback text in image-blocked clients (default state in Outlook desktop).
- Media query breakpoints are duplicated/conflicting: `@media (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }` followed by `@media (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }` — on viewports ≥768px both queries are active simultaneously; since 640px comes later in source order it will win via cascade, silently overriding the 768px rule's intended 600px cap. This is likely an unintended ordering bug (typically the wider breakpoint should apply the larger max-width, but here the smaller breakpoint is declared last and wins).
- Standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `ExternalClass`, and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets are present and correctly structured — no issues there.
- Doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, appropriate for email client compatibility.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens (e.g., `%%FirstName%%`, `{{}}`, AMPscript `%%=...=%%`) are visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available evidence; full-body scan would be needed to confirm no unresolved tokens exist further down.
