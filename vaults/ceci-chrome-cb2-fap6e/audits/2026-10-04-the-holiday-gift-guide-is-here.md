# 🎁 The Holiday Gift Guide is here!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, polished holiday gift-guide email from Crate & Barrel Kids. A warm hero headlines "heirloom-quality gifts made for wish lists." Below it are stacked blocks for bestselling, personalized, grow-with-them and bundle gifts, then an age-based gift section and a "jellycat shop is open" tile. Free shipping on toys and a Halloween block follow, and a product grid closes the email. The creative is cohesive and on-season, and it's easy to scroll.
- The problem is focus. The email has roughly eight to ten CTAs and no single hero offer. The only price message, a thin strip at the top, still says "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event: Up to 50% off." That is the previous campaign. A big Semi-Annual banner is also repeated near the bottom. The subject promises a gift guide, but the email carries a sale message and a Halloween block too. - Judgement: a good-looking, merchandising-heavy send that is generic and has no urgency. Note that the screenshot was shown to me at 113 px wide, so I can read the layout and headlines but not the fine print or prices.

## What's working

- The hero is warm and on-season, and the "heirloom-quality gifts made for wish lists" headline fits the gift-guide subject.
- Each block has a clear headline in lowercase brand voice, a short descriptor and one button. Examples are "Shop Bestselling Gifts," "Shop Personalized Gifts" and "Shop Gift Bundles."
- Personalized gifts are given their own block, which suits the gifting use case.
- Free shipping on toys is called out in its own tile.
- Large lifestyle photography is consistent, and I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer. The email is a long scroll of equal-weight blocks, and the eye has nowhere to land.
- The top strip repeats the Semi-Annual 50% off message. That promotion doesn't match the gift-guide subject, and the same banner returns near the bottom.
- The Halloween block ("first look: the halloween shop") sits in a gift email and dilutes the holiday message.
- There is no urgency or deadline, such as order-by dates for holiday delivery.
- The footer product grid ("we love these for you") is a weak, generic ending, and the email is very long.

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with "Order by [date] for holiday delivery" or a clear gift-guide offer, and cut the stale Semi-Annual strip and its repeat.
- 2. Cut the email to three or four blocks, such as personalized, grow-with-them, bundles and shop by age. Move Halloween to its own send.
- 3. Add a visible shipping or delivery deadline and a price anchor ("gifts from $X") to the hero.
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" style buttons with specific verbs and nouns, such as "Shop personalized gifts under $75."
- 5. Space out sends from this brand so the 10/03 and 10/04 emails don't compete.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The main confirmed defect is 50+ `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute. The header-based QA warnings (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are probably relay artifacts and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not validated:** 96 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination health and redirect chains are therefore unverified.
- **Heavy third-party tracking:**
  - 5 LiveRamp pixels: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5`.
  - 6 identical `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixels.
  - `…/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/72af0c93-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - The repeated pixel IDs add requests, and the `mi_u` hash is passed in the query string of image URLs. Only the first `n=` pixel of each set is needed for a single open signal.
- No broken or malformed link patterns are visible in the portion of HTML provided.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (confirmed, 50+ images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, for example `bcb67ebe-…jpg` and `2257fa17-…jpg`.
  - The animated hero `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5F1.gif` on `s7d5.scene7.com`.
  - Spacers `042025%5FCBK%5FKidsBeds1%5FRule%5FSpacer%5F40px` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - All tracking pixels listed above.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the product and gift-guide imagery has no text equivalent. Spacers and pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly, so the screen reader skips them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Text resizing suppressed:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the `*` and `body` rules, which also limits user text scaling.
- **Minor markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - The title is generic rather than the subject line.
  - `<meta charset>` duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times.
  - The template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in.
  - None of these should break rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens appear in the visible source, and the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both flagged as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this can't be confirmed as a sender defect. Verify against the raw message from a direct-delivery test, because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown for the same reason. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, which is consistent with a properly configured ESP, but that isn't proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the HTML was truncated before the footer, so neither could be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Can't be assessed. The 96 click links go through tracking redirects that weren't probed, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the provided source. No evidence of a problem.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and hero images, including the GIF's first-frame message. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Run the QA on the raw `.eml` or a direct seed-inbox copy to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results, then clear or confirm those three warnings.
4. Capture the full HTML, including the footer, and resolve a sample of the 96 redirect links to verify destinations and UTM parameters.
5. Consolidate duplicate tracking pixels (the 5 LiveRamp and 6 `mi.` pixels).
6. Clean the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, use the subject as the title, and drop the duplicate meta tags and the leftover placeholder comment.
