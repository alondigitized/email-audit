# Exclusive first look: NEW Black Friday deals added!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is another Crate & Barrel Kids Black Friday blast with the same offer and a "first look" label on top. The hero says "new Black Friday deals added!" with one "Shop Now" button, and "up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" sits right under it. The headline is clear, but the offer hasn't changed since the 11/23 "Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year!" send or this morning's "NEW DEALS just added" email. The "exclusive first look" subject promises something the body doesn't deliver. Nothing new is shown, and the deals are the same sale categories.
- The email is also long and loosely focused. After the hero it runs through a nine-link sale list, a gifts banner, a travel-gear story, four products, a credit card promo, an app download, a design-service promo and a footer. It is rendered here heavily downscaled (680px wide, about 6,260px tall), so I can read the headlines and layout but not the fine print or most prices. The product tiles with a visible sale price are the strongest part.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but a repeat. Subscribers will see it as the seventh or so Black Friday email in about two days.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "new Black Friday deals added!" with one red "Shop Now" button.
- The "up to 50% off + free shipping" offer is stated plainly right below the hero.
- The sale category list (Furniture, Toy, Baby Bedding, Rugs and so on) gives easy ways into the sale.
- The product grid shows a visible markdown on the Busy Baby Activity Chair ($159.00 struck through, $129.95) and the Babyletto recliner ($999.00 struck through, $799.20).
- The "best travel-tested baby gear" block is a seasonal, useful angle with its own CTA.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the body is "exclusive" or a "first look". It is the same sale with new copy.
- The email is too long, with six or seven competing blocks (credit card, app, design desk, store locator) diluting the sale message.
- The top strip says "free shipping is on" with exclusions, and a large fine-print block sits at the bottom. The offer feels qualified.
- The sale link list is plain text with no imagery, so it is easy to skim past.
- The sender name "BLACK FRIDAY" in caps, with a subject that repeats the same message, adds to the sense of repetition.

## Recommendations

- 1. Show the actual new deals. Put 3-4 specific new items with prices directly under the hero. That makes "first look" and "new" true.
- 2. Cut the body to hero, offer, products and one secondary block. Move the credit card, app and design desk modules to a non-sale send.
- 3. Reduce send frequency, or consolidate the daily "new deals" emails into one digest.
- 4. Turn the text category list into image tiles for the top 3-4 categories.
- 5. Put the shipping exclusions in a clear single line instead of the top strip plus long fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New markdowns on strollers & nursery gear: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: 12 new Black Friday deals for baby`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nuna strollers, Babyletto gliders and more, now marked down. Free shipping on $79+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today's new arrivals in the sale, while sizes and colors last.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and mobile media queries at 460px. The main confirmed defect is about 20 content images with no `alt` attribute, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified. The supplied HTML is truncated, so the footer, body links and UTM parameters were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 73 links go through click-redirect or tracking domains. The QA run skipped HTTP probes on them, so destination validity and redirect chains are **unverified**, not confirmed broken.
- Tracking pixels and third-party beacons are present:
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5`: five LiveRamp/RapLeaf sync pixels.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...`: repeated 6 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...`: open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/87bec1ef-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`: another beacon.
- Five identical LiveRamp calls and six identical `rp` pixel calls are redundant requests. They add load and third-party exposure without adding data.
- The `mi_u` query string carries a long hashed per-recipient identifier in image URLs. This is a privacy and caching concern, not a functional break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (confirmed by QA).** The images below have no `alt` attribute. If they are image-only blocks, that leaves them unreadable with images blocked or on a screen reader:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`: about 16 images, including `d3119bec-…`, `5d4ea9f0-…`, `4388e764-…`, `613ceed4-…`, `0e77b66d-…`, `9270c3c6-…`, `fee089ff-…`.
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`.
  - Scene7 GIFs: `2026%5F1124%5FCBK%5FBF%5FNewDealsAdded%5F02.gif`.
  - Scene7 GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`. The "1002" date and "GiftGuide" name suggest an October creative reused here. That is an inference from the filename only, and the content wasn't checked.
  - `…/m/1/91ea068d-869a-42ec-b19e-06266e21f83f.gif`.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt` (`rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).** These should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The attribute is missing, which is a minor issue. Screen readers may announce the filename.
- `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel" and has an unescaped `&`. The XHTML doctype expects `&amp;`. This is a minor validity issue.
- Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags appear four times with conflicting values: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`. Behavior is consistent in effect but redundant.
- Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present, which is harmless.
- Leftover template comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is a typo in a placeholder and has no render impact, but it shows an unfinished template.
- `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` is a global rule that forces centered text for every `<p>`. Any `<p>` that expects left alignment must override it inline.
- The global `*{... line-height:100% }` rule is aggressive and can cause cramped text where a block doesn't set its own line-height.
- `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom on some clients. This is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Because the HTML is truncated, I can't verify live text vs. image-only content. Likewise I can't verify `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the presence of a preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were reported by QA or seen in the supplied HTML. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated rather than literal placeholders.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is **unconfirmed**, and the sender (`crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`) may well send it. It should be checked on the raw message.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders, so verify it on the original headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. It is expected to be stripped by the relay, so no conclusion can be drawn.
- The footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) is beyond the truncation point, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are **not verified**.
- The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking domains (`image.`, `mi.`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`), which is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and final destinations **could not be assessed**. The 73 links go through redirect domains and were not resolved, and the visible HTML is truncated before the body links.
- Campaign naming in the image paths (`2026_1124_CBK_BF_NewDealsAdded`) is consistent with today's send. The reused `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide` asset noted in section 3 is the only mismatch.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on all tracking pixels. Start with the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/.../m/1/*.jpg` set.
2. Deduplicate the pixels: the five `rlcdn.com` calls and six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` calls.
3. Check the raw message source for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC pass). The relay-stripping explanation is plausible but unproven.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
5. Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, and give the title a specific value.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML and resolve the redirect links to check UTM parameters and footer compliance.
