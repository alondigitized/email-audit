# Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Double Rewards or Fall Sales send from Crate & Barrel Kids today. It follows "The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY!" and "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅". The creative is solid. It opens on a moody green bedroom hero under "last Double Rewards this year!", with a Shop Now button and an Apply Now button for the credit card. Below that, the email splits into room-based blocks: bedroom, nursery, playroom. Each has a lifestyle photo, a 2x2 product grid with "Earn $XX in Reward Dollars" lines, and a category button.
- The render is about 12,000px tall, and it loses focus after the playroom block. The fall sales banner, a gift banner, a $500 sweepstakes, a stroller and baby-gear section, a credit card promo, an app download and a TheDesignDesk block all pile on. The Double Rewards message is strong at the top and gets buried by the time you reach the stroller. The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I'm judging structure and hierarchy, not fine print.
- Verdict: a competent loyalty-driven send, but the third touch on the same promo today and a bloated bottom half hold it back. It's a good top-third email stuck inside a catalog.

## What's working

- The opening hero states a clear loyalty hook ("last Double Rewards this year!") and has a single Shop Now button.
- Room-based sections (bedroom, nursery, playroom) make the catalog easy to scan.
- Product tiles show an "Earn $XX in Reward Dollars" line, so the loyalty benefit attaches to real products.
- Category CTAs such as "Shop Bedroom Furniture" are more specific than a generic "Shop Now".
- The photography is consistent and seasonal, with dark greens and holiday styling that fit the campaign.

## What's weak

- It's the third near-identical promo today (see the two earlier 11-05 sends), so the cadence feels repetitive.
- The email is far too long. After the playroom block it sprawls into the sweepstakes, the stroller offer, the credit card, the app and TheDesignDesk.
- The "fall sales event: up to 50% off" banner and the Double Rewards message compete, so there are two offers with no clear primary.
- The gift banner looks like it has text laid over a busy photo, and it reads as muddled at this size.
- The sweepstakes and credit card promos add friction and dilute the shopping intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus two or three room blocks and drop the app, sweepstakes and TheDesignDesk blocks. That would do the most for click-through.
- 2. Merge this send with the other two from today, or suppress it for anyone who already opened them.
- 3. Put the 2x Rewards deadline in the hero as a concrete date, not just "last this year".
- 4. Give the 50% off banner and the Rewards offer a clear order, with one as the headline and the other as support.
- 5. Move the credit card (Apply Now) offer to the footer area so it doesn't sit next to the primary CTA.

## Technical Audit

The compliance and authentication checks came back inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, not because they failed. The QA run scored a 57% pass rate (0 errors, 3 warnings).

- **Heavy third-party tracking:**

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 43 content images):** For example:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `264a3d96-1010-4c17-b22e-59f2cd300ef5.gif`

  With images blocked, the email will render as blank blocks with no accessible text, and screen readers get no description.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template and markup hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype.

  - It declares `format-detection` three times, with conflicting values.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed under the XHTML doctype.
- **Fragile CSS:** The global `*{… line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` selectors force styles onto every element. `img {max-width:600px}` and `img {max-width:640px}` are declared in overlapping min-width media queries.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML is cut off, so body structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, and the footer could not be audited.
