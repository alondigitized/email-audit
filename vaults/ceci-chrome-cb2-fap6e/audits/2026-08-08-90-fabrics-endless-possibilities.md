# 90+ fabrics. Endless possibilities.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's fourth creative mode this week, and it's the most product-agnostic one yet: no discount, no single hero item, just a pitch for its custom-upholstery program. "CUSTOM IS SUCH A GOOD LOOK" over stacked fabric swatches is a genuinely striking open, and "ORDER 10 FREE SWATCHES" is a smart, low-friction CTA for a category (custom furniture) where the real purchase decision happens by touch, not click. But the email quickly loses focus — it stacks three separate asks (swatches, browse fabrics, book a design appointment) before ever showing a shoppable product, then pivots into an unrelated "SOFAS & SECTIONALS" launch and a six-item "More For You" grid that has nothing to do with fabrics.
- Compare this to 8/6's "THE AUGUST SALE" (8/10) or 8/1's "The best kind of impulse buy" (8/10) — those wins came from either urgency/discount clarity or one crisp editorial thesis. This send tries to be an editorial thesis (fabrics) but doesn't stay disciplined about it; it's closer in structure to 8/7's "The best living rooms on our feed" (5/10), which also opened strong on one theme and then fragmented into unrelated asks (design services, then generic promo blocks).
- There's no discount, no urgency, no price point anywhere — this is a top-of-funnel/consideration email, not a conversion one, which caps click likelihood no matter how nice the swatch photography is.

## What's working

- The hero shot (stacked leather/fabric swatches in warm neutral tones) is genuinely premium and on-brand — best visual open of the week's batch.
- "ORDER 10 FREE SWATCHES" is a specific, low-commitment, verb+noun CTA that matches how people actually shop custom upholstery.
- "BOOK A FREE APPOINTMENT" for design experts reinforces a real value-add (help choosing fabric) rather than a generic "shop now."
- No render bugs — clean layout, consistent black-button styling, readable type throughout.

## What's weak

- No offer, no price, no urgency anywhere in the email — pure top-of-funnel, which will suppress clicks from anyone not already furniture-shopping.
- Three competing CTAs before the fold ends (swatches → browse fabrics → book appointment) dilutes the single clearest ask.
- "SOFAS & SECTIONALS" and the six-product "More For You" grid are bolted onto a fabric-themed email with zero connective tissue — feels like two emails stitched together.
- Subject "90+ fabrics. Endless possibilities." is vague at a glance and undersells the free-swatches offer, which is the actual strongest hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject/preheader with the free-swatches offer itself, not the abstract fabric-count — "free" and "swatches" are what drives opens and clicks, "90+ fabrics" is filler.
- 2. Cut to one CTA in the top module (swatches) and move "Browse Our Fabrics" and "Book a Design Appointment" into a single secondary row instead of three full-width stacked sections.
- 3. Either drop "Sofas & Sectionals" + "More For You" entirely, or reframe them explicitly as "See what our fabrics look like in these pieces" so they read as continuation, not a topic switch.
- 4. Add a light urgency or incentive to the swatch order (e.g., "free swatches ship this week") to convert consideration into action faster.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free fabric swatches, delivered to your door`
- **Subject Alt B:** `90+ custom fabrics. Yours to touch first.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order 10 free swatches before you commit to a sofa`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Custom upholstery, expert-guided, 7-week turnaround`
