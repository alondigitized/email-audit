# ENDS TODAY: Last 2x Rewards this year!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "last chance" Double Rewards send in about 24 hours. It follows this morning's "ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards of the year!" from the main brand and yesterday's "ENDS TOMORROW" kids blast. The Kids sender's subject is almost a word-for-word echo of that one. The hero is a moody green bedroom scene. Under it, "ends today! last Double Rewards this year!" gives the deadline and the $/Rewards mechanic, followed by a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The body is orderly: bestsellers for the bedroom, must-haves for the nursery, picks for the playroom, then an Early Black Friday "up to 50% off" block. Each tile carries an "Earn $X in Reward Dollars" line, which keeps the loyalty hook consistent. It's competent, but the shopper gets two offers at once (Double Rewards and Early Black Friday), and the nursery, bedroom and playroom sections all compete for attention. The render is heavily downscaled (680px wide, 12,000px tall), so I can read headlines and section order but not fine print or exact prices.
- My judgement: good execution of a tired, repeated message. The email is solid, but the cadence undercuts it.

## What's working

- The hero states the deadline and offer immediately, with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- The red/orange headline treatment makes the urgency scannable.
- Each product tile shows a Reward Dollars earn line, so the loyalty benefit stays on the page.
- Section headers ("bestsellers for their bedroom", "must-haves for their nursery") break the long scroll into digestible parts, each with its own CTA ("Shop Bedroom Furniture", "Shop Nursery Furniture").
- No broken images or overlapping text are visible.

## What's weak

- The subject nearly duplicates this morning's "ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards of the year!" from the main brand, so it reads as the Nth identical send.
- The Early Black Friday "up to 50% off" block mid-email competes with the Rewards message and dilutes the single deadline.
- The email is very long, with roughly eight product grids, and the best offer is not repeated at the bottom.
- The app promo, Design Desk and card-offer blocks near the end add clutter after the main pitch is done.
- The earn amounts and fine print are small at inbox size, so the value of "2x" is hard to judge without clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer for the send. Either lead with the 2x Rewards deadline or with the Black Friday discount, not both. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Suppress or consolidate the kids sender against the main brand's same-day send, so subscribers don't get two "ends today" emails.
- 3. Cut the body to 3–4 modules and end with a repeat of the deadline and a "Shop Now" button.
- 4. Show a concrete example of the earn ("Earn $80 on a $400 crib") in the hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double on bunk beds, nursery and playroom picks. Ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with an MSO conditional block and a mobile media query. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 header warnings. It also found about 60 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute and several markup problems in the head. Link destinations, UTMs and the footer could not be checked because the source was truncated and all 116 links go through tracking redirects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** The QA run skipped the HTTP probe on all 116 links because they are tracking or click-redirect domains. Nothing confirms that any CTA resolves to a live page. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical LiveRamp pixels are present: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. Only the `n` value differs. These are third-party data-sync beacons. Any client that loads images will fire all five, and they add an external dependency.
- **Per-recipient tokens in image URLs.** Image URLs from `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` carry `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters. The same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` image appears 6 times. The `mi_` prefix looks like a Movable Ink-style dynamic or tracking asset, but the source doesn't say so. If that host is down or blocked, those slots break.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on about 60 images (confirmed by QA).**
  - About 47 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. One is a `.gif` (`7c91dd26-….gif`).
  - The rest are tracking pixels: `o.gif`, `448796.gif` ×5, `f3c0d229….png` ×6, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/d6ab52f6-…`.
  - Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`. Content images need descriptive alt text. If any of the content images are the only CTA carrier, image-blocked clients show nothing. The truncated source doesn't show which images are linked.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text scaling disabled.** The universal selector sets `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}`. Together with `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`, this stops iOS text scaling.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`, since the DOCTYPE is XHTML 1.0 Transitional.
- **Duplicate and conflicting `<meta name="format-detection">` tags.** There are four, with different values.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head, and "IMPUT" is a typo.
- **Redundant charset declarations.** Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. The HTML5 form doesn't belong in an XHTML 1.0 document.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is overridden by the later `(min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`, so the 600px rule never wins. This is low severity.
- **Positive.** `<html lang="en">`, the Outlook VML namespaces, an MSO `PixelsPerInch` block and `color-scheme` meta are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The per-recipient `mi_u` values are rendered. This is limited to the truncated portion of the HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (QA WARN). The tool itself notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (QA WARN). Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders, can't be confirmed.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. Check the raw message headers directly.
- **Footer not verifiable.** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated section. Both need verifying.
- **Sender domain looks consistent.** The From is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and images are served from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so the sending and image subdomains appear aligned.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 116 links are tracking-redirect URLs that weren't resolved, so UTM parameters, final landing pages and the "ENDS TODAY / 2x Rewards" offer's landing-page alignment can't be assessed. No UTM parameters are visible in the shown source.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to all content images and `alt=""` to the roughly 13 tracking pixels, so the ~60 QA warnings clear.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `html` rules.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw headers of the original message, not the relay copy. Do the same for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
4. Re-run link QA by resolving the redirect chains, for example by following the 302s with a real GET. That would confirm final destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
5. Clean up the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, collapse the duplicate `format-detection` metas, delete the "IMPUT HERE" comment, and fix the conflicting `img` max-width rules.
6. Review whether five LiveRamp pixels and six repeated dynamic-content image calls are needed.
