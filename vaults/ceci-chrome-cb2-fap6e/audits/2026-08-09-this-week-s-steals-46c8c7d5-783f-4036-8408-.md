# THIS WEEK’S STEALS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- CB2 goes back to the discount-stack mode it ran on 8/6 ("THE AUGUST SALE"), but this time it's a genuine multi-tier steal roundup: 40% off August Sale, 60% off Summer Clearance, 50% off Outdoor, stacked one after another. It's the most offer-dense email of the week — three distinct percentage-off tiers before you even hit the "more for you" grid — and unlike 8/6's kitchen-sink version (credit card pitch + sweepstakes + app download all crammed in), this one stays disciplined until the very bottom where the usual footer modules (app, rewards, gift list, text signup) show up.
- The subject "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" is blunt and on-brand for a clearance email, and the render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, good product photography throughout. Where it loses points is sameness: this is the third "stacked % off" structural mode CB2 has run in nine days (8/6 sale, plus discount mechanics folded into other sends), and there's no single hero product or urgency deadline anywhere in the body — every tier just says "up to X% off" with a tiny asterisk, which is the exact "fine print" pattern the rubric dings.
- Judgement: solid mid-tier performer. It'll get opened on subject strength but the click path is diffuse — three categories competing for attention with no clear single CTA to click first.

## What's working

- Three clear percentage tiers (40%, 60%, 50%) each get their own full-width hero image and bold callout — strong visual hierarchy per section.
- Product photography is high quality and on-brand (walnut/marble tones, styled room shots).
- The "LEAVE US A REVIEW, WIN $1,000" module is a fresh beat not seen in recent sends, breaking up the discount blocks.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom gives six concrete SKUs with visible prices — good for browsers who skip the hero offers.
- No render bugs — clean type, no overlapping elements, images load properly.

## What's weak

- No single primary CTA — three "up to X% off" blocks each imply "shop now" but none has an actual button, so the eye has nowhere obvious to click first.
- Every discount is qualified with an asterisk and no visible minimum/maximum context in the screenshot — "up to" language with no floor makes the offer feel soft (rubric: buried fine print).
- No urgency or deadline anywhere — no "ends Sunday," no countdown, nothing time-bound despite this being three stacked sales.
- Third stacked-discount structural mode in over a week (8/6 sale, this one) — cadence is starting to feel repetitive even if the categories differ.
- Subject "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" is generic enough it could apply to nearly any CB2 promo email — no specificity on category or amount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Give the top hero (40% off August Sale) an actual button CTA ("Shop the Sale") instead of relying on image-only tap targets — right now nothing in the screenshot reads as clickable.
- 2. Add a deadline to at least the top tier — "ends Sunday" or similar — to convert this into real urgency instead of an open-ended "up to" offer.
- 3. Lead the subject with the biggest number (60% off clearance is the strongest offer on the page) rather than the vague "STEALS" framing.
- 4. Consolidate to two tiers instead of three, or clearly rank them (best deal first) — three co-equal "up to X% off" blocks dilute rather than compound.
