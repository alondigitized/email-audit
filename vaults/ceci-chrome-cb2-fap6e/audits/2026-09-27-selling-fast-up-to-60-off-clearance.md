# Selling fast: up to 60% off clearance!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a busy, catalog-style clearance email that opens strong then loses discipline fast. The hero is clean — "up to 60% off + new markdowns," a floral autumn backdrop, one clear "Shop Clearance" button, and a tidy category nav row underneath. That's a good, mobile-friendly start.
- Then it falls apart. The exact same Venus Williams "Crate&kids" collab block — same photo, same copy, same "Shop the New Collab" button — is duplicated back-to-back, which reads as a broken render rather than intentional repetition. From there the email sprawls into a baby-gear promo, a Nuna travel system, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a Design Desk appointment CTA — none of which is kids-clearance-specific, and the subject's core promise gets buried under six unrelated asks.
- Judgment: the top third is solid, the rest is scope creep. This reads like three or four different campaigns stapled together, and the duplicated block is a real bug that should be caught before send.

## What's working

- Hero headline "up to 60% off + new markdowns" directly matches the subject line, with the offer visible immediately, no scroll needed.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("Shop Clearance") sits right under the offer.
- Category shortcut row (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, 40%–80% Off Final Sale) gives quick wayfinding for a clearance shopper.
- Product grid later on shows real prices with visible markdowns (e.g., $1155.20 struck through to $1085.00), which is honest and concrete.

## What's weak

- The Venus Williams "Crate&kids" collab section is rendered twice in a row, identical image and copy — a clear render/dedup bug.
- The email drifts entirely off the clearance premise: baby car seats, credit card upsell, app download, Design Desk booking — none reinforce "60% off clearance."
- No urgency or deadline treatment beyond "ending soon" in tiny hero subtext — easy to miss against the floral background.
- Visual hierarchy after the hero is flat; a dozen stacked sections of similar visual weight give the eye nowhere obvious to land after the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the duplicate collab block immediately — this is a shipped bug, not a design choice, and duplicated content erodes trust fast.
- 2. Cut the email to two zones: clearance hero + category grid, then stop. Move the Venus Williams collab, Nuna travel system, credit card, and app-download pitches to their own dedicated sends — this email is currently five campaigns wearing one subject line.
- 3. Move "ending soon" into the hero headline itself (e.g., "Ends Sunday: up to 60% off clearance") so urgency isn't buried in small subtext over a busy photo.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or explicit end date near the top CTA to make the urgency credible rather than generic.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Almost gone: new markdowns on kids clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just landed — shop while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nursery, bedding, and playroom picks up to 60% off`
