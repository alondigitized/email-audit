# NEW registry essentials from BABYBJÖRN!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-spotlight send with a sale bolted on. The subject promises BabyBjörn registry essentials, and the email delivers that straight away. The hero is "2 new parent must-haves", followed by two dedicated product blocks, the Hold baby carrier and the Bouncer Begin. Each block has feature icons (newborn, facing in or out, machine washable, no batteries needed), a star rating, and its own Shop Now button. For a parent or registry shopper, this is a clear and useful top third.
- The email then loses focus. Below the BabyBjörn content it adds a "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" banner, an Affirm pay-later block, a TheDesignDesk $5,000 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" grid, a credit card promo, an app download block, and a second Design Desk pitch. That is seven or eight competing asks after a clean launch story. The screenshot is shrunk so far that I can't read the small text. I'm judging structure, hierarchy and the large headlines, not the fine print.
- Verdict: a strong first half and a bloated second half. The "NEW" brand-launch angle is the best thing here. The long tail of cross-promos waters it down and makes the email feel like a generic Crate & Barrel send.

## What's working

- The hero names the brand and the benefit ("2 new parent must-haves") and sits directly under a free-shipping strip.
- Each product block has a lifestyle photo, a product shot, feature icons, star ratings and a button.
- Both products have their own CTA, so a reader goes to a specific item and not just a category.
- The "up to 50% off" nursery banner gives a concrete price reason to click, and it fits the baby and nursery context.
- The layout is clean, with generous white space and sage-green tones that match the product photos. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with at least six distinct promo modules after the BabyBjörn content.
- Every CTA I can read says "Shop Now" or "Learn More". None of them name the product or the benefit.
- The registry angle in the subject isn't backed up in the body. I see no registry button, no "add to registry" prompt and no registry completion perk.
- The sweepstakes, the Affirm block and the credit card block pull attention away from the launch.
- This is the second Crate & Barrel send today. The 10/10 "Get your kitchen cozy-season-ready" email came first, after the 10/09 run of free-shipping and fall-deals sends. The cadence is heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the nursery event banner, or at most keep one secondary module. Drop the sweepstakes, Affirm, credit card and second Design Desk blocks. This single change would do the most to lift clicks.
- 2. Add a registry-specific CTA, such as "Add to your registry", next to each product. Without it, the subject's promise is only half kept.
- 3. Rewrite the button copy to name the product: "Shop the Hold carrier" and "Shop Bouncer Begin".
- 4. Add a price or offer to the hero itself, for example a registry completion discount, so the top of the email carries a reason to act now.
- 5. Merge the two BabyBjörn blocks into one tighter row if length can't be cut. That keeps the carrier and bouncer in a single scroll.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with mobile media queries and a lang attribute, sent from the `mail.crateandbarrel.com` subdomain. The QA run flagged missing alt text on about 40 images and could not capture the authentication or unsubscribe headers. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and link hrefs could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validation not possible:** 82 links were skipped as tracking or click-redirect domains, so no destination status, redirect chain or 404 data exists. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** the source includes five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons (LiveRamp). It also includes six repeats of `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, an open pixel at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/c3986fc4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`.
  - The duplicate pixels add requests with no extra signal. The `rlcdn` beacons are third-party, which is relevant for privacy-focused clients and Apple Mail Privacy Protection.
  - These URLs carry a per-recipient hash (`mi_u=56aaa2787a…`) in the query string. This is normal for the ESP, but the hash is a stable identifier that is exposed to any proxy or log.
- **Animated assets:** the `scene7.com` assets `…HeroAnimationOnly…` and `…BOUNCERVIDEO_01…` appear to be animated or video-derived images. Check them for file weight and for fallback frames in Outlook, which shows only the first frame of a GIF.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** about 40 images have no alt attribute.
  - **Content images:** these include the hero animation (`2026%5F0916%5FCBK%5FBabybjorn%5FHeroAnimationOnly…`), the bouncer animation (`…BOUNCERVIDEO%5F01…`) and roughly 25 images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`. With images blocked or a screen reader in use, the content is unavailable.
  - **Pixels and spacers:** the `rlcdn`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` images are decorative or tracking. They should use `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. Absent alt can lead screen readers to read out the filename.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` uses an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype (it should be `&amp;`). It is also generic: it is neither the subject nor the Kids brand.
- **Duplicate and conflicting markup:**
  - `<meta charset>` appears twice (once as `http-equiv` Content-Type and once as the HTML5 form).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values: the combined `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and then separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` entries.
  - `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are global resets that can override inline styles in some clients.
- **Overlapping media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The later rule wins at equal specificity, so the 600px cap is dead code at ≥768px.
- **Template leftover:** the source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, the VML/Office namespaces and the MSO `PixelsPerInch` fix are present.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, especially the hero and bouncer animations and the product tiles. Use `alt=""` on every spacer and tracking pixel.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it match the subject or the Kids brand.
4. Remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags, and reorder or remove the conflicting `img max-width` media queries.
5. Drop the redundant repeated `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels, keeping one open pixel.
6. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC on the raw message. The QA relay can't verify them, and the warnings are likely a capture limitation.
7. Improve QA coverage by resolving the redirect domain to check destinations and UTMs, and by feeding the full untruncated HTML so the footer and links can be inspected.
