# It’s the countdown to Black Friday! Shop up to 60% off + Earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the Black Friday lead-in. The red "Crate&Barrel" header sits over a wood-cabinet hero reading "countdown to Black Friday, up to 60% off" with one "Shop Now" button. A Double Rewards banner sits above the hero. A "5M,250 people are shopping our best clearance deals" line sits just under it. The creative is polished and on-brand, and the offer is easy to find.
- The problem is length and repetition. The render is about 10,600px tall. It runs through a category grid, "we did the shopping for you" clearance picks, the Fall Sales Event, the Gift Shop, a design-desk contest, a second "last Double Rewards this year" banner, more product picks and an app promo. The earlier 11-05 sends ("Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅", "Up to 30% off holiday hosting essentials + Double Rewards!") already made this argument, and this one makes it again with a bigger number.
- The verdict is a strong hero on top of a bloated catalog. It will perform on brand trust and the 60% number, but the middle and bottom dilute it. I'm working from a heavily downscaled render, so small text is only approximate.

## What's working

- The hero is clear: "up to 60% off" with a single "Shop Now" button over a large, premium image.
- The loyalty hook is stated twice, in the top banner and again mid-email ("last Double Rewards this year").
- The category tile grid (sofas, furniture, rugs, kitchen, tabletop, decor, bedding, lighting) gives easy entry points.
- Clearance product tiles show prices, which gives concrete reasons to click.
- The social-proof line ("people are shopping our best clearance deals") adds urgency near the top.

## What's weak

- At about 10,600px it is far too long. The Gift Shop, DesignDesk contest and app promo compete with the sale message.
- It has at least three separate offers in one send: 60% off, 2x Rewards and a $5000 giveaway. Together with the Fall Sales Event and Gift Shop sections, that makes too many messages.
- The 11-05 sends hit the same Double Rewards and Fall Sales beats, so this is another near-duplicate in a heavy cadence.
- The hero says "countdown" but shows no actual end date or timer, so the urgency is vague.
- Several CTAs read "Shop Now" or "Learn More" with nothing specific behind them.
- The dense fine print at the bottom is hard to read and weakens the "up to" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the clearance picks, one Rewards banner and the category grid. Move the Gift Shop, DesignDesk and app sections to their own sends.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", or a countdown graphic that matches the "countdown" language.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with category-specific CTAs, such as "Shop sofas up to 60% off".
- 4. Merge the two Double Rewards banners into one, placed right under the hero.
- 5. Move the "people are shopping" proof line down next to the clearance picks, where it supports a product decision.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marigold/MI-platform template with 83 click-tracked links and about 45 images missing `alt` text, including 5 third-party LiveRamp pixels. The HTML source is truncated, and the relay stripped the headers, so the compliance and authentication findings are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Links unverified:** 83 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains (QA: "83 tracking link(s) skipped"). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…0cfd8&n=1` through `n=5` requests go to LiveRamp's domain. The `s=` value begins with the same string as the `mi_u=56aaa278…` per-recipient token in the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs. If `s=` is derived from the recipient identifier, it is shared with a third party. I can't confirm that from the visible source, so it needs a privacy and consent check.
- **Per-recipient tokens in image URLs:** `mi_u=` appears in `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images) and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. These are open-tracking pixels, and Apple Mail Privacy Protection will inflate their counts.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** about 45 images are flagged.
  - About 30 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. With images blocked, these render as blank space, and screen readers announce the file name.
  - Seven are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images.
  - Five are the `rlcdn.com` pixels.
  - One is `o.gif`.
  - One is `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4c60d1dd-…`.
  - The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` and be hidden from assistive technology. The content images need descriptive `alt` text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is HTML5 syntax and isn't self-closed.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately).
  - The charset is declared twice.
- **Template leftovers:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and `<!----->` comment fragments sit in the head.
- **Conflicting image rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` overlap. At 768px and wider the later rule wins, so the 600px cap is overridden.
- **Mobile width:** `.mobile{width:460px!important}` is applied inside a `max-width:460px` query, so it forces a fixed 460px width on narrower viewports. This can cause horizontal scroll below 460px. The `.showmobile` rule has the same problem at 414px.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the truncated source. The only per-recipient values seen are the intended `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` tracking parameters.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Header findings are unconfirmed:** QA reports no `List-Unsubscribe` and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header, but it notes the AgentMail relay may have stripped them. Treat this as unverified rather than a confirmed sender failure. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- **Authentication unknown:** `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not inspected:** the visible unsubscribe link and postal address are in the truncated portion of the HTML, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found that can be confirmed. Links go through MI tracking redirects, so UTM parameters and landing pages can't be seen in the source or probed (all 83 links skipped). Landing-page alignment with the "up to 60% off + 2x Rewards" offer is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images in `/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. Set `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` on the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `rp/*.png`, `rlcdn.com`, `dv.…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`, drop the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` metas, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
4. Fix the overlapping `img` `max-width` queries and the fixed `460px` mobile widths.
5. Confirm the `rlcdn.com` pixels and the `s=` parameter against Crate & Barrel's privacy disclosures. Five duplicate pixel calls are also redundant.
6. For this audit pipeline, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) before the relay strips them, and resolve the tracking redirects to check UTMs and landing pages. Without both, the compliance and continuity sections can't be audited.
