# Restock alert
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- CB2 subject-lines this as "Restock alert" but the actual email is not a restock alert — it's the same in-stock furniture cascade format seen in the 2026-09-24/23 sends (Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas), padded further this time with a Lighting/Rugs/Wall Art/Art Decor grid, before finally landing on the real content: the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event at up to 25% off. That offer is buried roughly two-thirds of the way down, after seven "IN STOCK" category tiles a reader has to scroll past first.
- The mismatch between subject and body is the core problem — "restock alert" implies specific SKUs are back, but the hero just says "IT'S BACK IN STOCK" over a generic chair-and-mirror lifestyle shot with no named product. This is a bait-ish subject for a mosaic email, continuing the pattern this brand has run all week.
- Judgement: functional but low-effort — a decent discount buried under restock framing that doesn't deliver on its promise. 5/10 territory, consistent with CB2's recent baseline.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand visual style — warm, well-lit lifestyle photography throughout with consistent typography.
- The Upholstery Event section is genuinely clear: bold "UP TO 25% OFF" with a single unambiguous "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Loyalty/member content is visible near the bottom (CB2 App perks, Rewards 10% back) which matters for a member-persona.
- "MORE FOR YOU" personalized product grid at the bottom shows real SKUs with prices, giving concrete purchase options.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Restock alert" but the hero and email never name a specific restocked product — it's generic in-stock category browsing, not a real restock alert.
- Seven near-identical "IN STOCK" category tiles (Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas, plus a Lighting/Rugs/Wall Decor grid) delay the actual offer far below the fold.
- No visible price or specific discount until deep in the email — the true hero (25% off Upholstery Event) is the 9th section down.
- The $5,000 Refresh design-appointment banner right after the sale banner dilutes focus by introducing a second, unrelated CTA immediately.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to match the body's actual content — lead with the Upholstery Event discount, not a vague "restock" claim, since that's the only real offer in the email.
- 2. Move the Upholstery Event (25% off) to the hero position — it's the strongest, most concrete offer and shouldn't require seven scrolls to reach.
- 3. Cut the category-tile cascade from seven sections to two or three — Bedroom/Sectionals/Sofas overlap thematically and can be one combined tile.
- 4. If "restock" is the intended hook, name the actual restocked item/collection in the hero copy and image, not a generic chair.
- 5. Separate the Upholstery sale and the $5,000 Refresh design-appointment offer into different sends or clearly subordinate one to the other — right now they compete for attention back-to-back.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save up to 25% during our Semi-Annual Upholstery Event`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sold-out sofas and sectionals are back — shop before they're gone`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Email fails on personalization (unresolved merge token) and shows unresolved compliance/authentication header visibility issues; markup itself is otherwise a standard MSO/VML responsive template.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 73 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP validation (redirect domains), so their final destinations are unverified.
- Legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixels use plain HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS resources may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, silently breaking these ad-sync calls.
- Mi.cb2.com and dv.cb2.com tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `/o/32f89755-...`) use HTTPS — no issue there.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- 15 content/tracking images are missing `alt` text, including all primary product images (e.g. `4ddadd7f-a573-48ad-912f-4e23a2a4b2df.jpg`, `fa96b5d7-c967-4bd6-8092-1830c7513292.jpg`, and 9 others under `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`). This degrades screen-reader experience and image-blocked fallback rendering.
- Markup includes standard MSO conditional comments and `@media` breakpoints (418px, 690px, 768px) for responsive/Outlook handling — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL**: Raw unresolved merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%` present in the rendered HTML — indicates a failed merge/personalization step in the ESP send. This will display literally to recipients and likely breaks the associated review-content block.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support without header access.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — same caveat; if genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules) is not supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC status) not detected — cannot confirm sending domain authentication from this data.
- No in-body physical address or unsubscribe link visibility could be confirmed from the truncated HTML source provided.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No issues found — insufficient data; the 73 tracking links were not resolved/probed, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from available evidence.

7. **Recommendations**
- Fix the ESP merge/data-feed issue producing `%%reviews_skulist%%` before next send — top priority as it's a visible customer-facing defect.
