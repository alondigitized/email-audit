# Get (& stay!) organized with these bestsellers →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-directory email masquerading as a single campaign — "storage and organization" is the nominal theme, but by the third scroll it's ricocheted through fall tabletop, cookware, bedroom furniture, and a credit card pitch. There's no discount, no urgency, no single hero moment — just six modular-storage product tiles up top followed by four more unrelated sub-campaigns stacked underneath. It reads like four separate emails spliced together under one subject line.
- The storage section itself is competently built: clean grid, consistent product photography, one clear "Shop Storage & Modular Furniture" CTA repeated twice. But the moment it hands off to "the new fall table" and "crafted-to-last cookware," any subscriber who opened for organization content has already gotten what they came for and everything after is noise diluting the message.
- Consistent with the brand's recent pattern (the 2026-08-15 flatware guide, 2026-08-16 bedroom bestsellers) — a decent focused idea buried under scroll-forever bloat. Judgment: functional but unfocused, no offer to anchor urgency, solid mid-tier send.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous top CTA — "Shop Storage & Modular Furniture" button appears twice, once right under the hero and again after the grid.
- Category grid is clean and organized: six labeled tiles (modular storage, bookcases & shelves, storage cabinets, tv stands, bar carts, floating shelves) with consistent lifestyle photography.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds together at every section.
- Cookware section includes a visible price signal — "New Lower Price! $399.00 Was $498.00" — which is the only concrete numeric offer in the whole email.

## What's weak

- No discount or urgency tied to the storage theme itself — the subject promises "bestsellers" but there's zero %/$ off or deadline for the storage category.
- Scope creep: after storage, the email pivots to fall tabletop, cookware, bedroom furniture ("we love these for you"), and a credit card ad — five unrelated campaigns in one send.
- The credit card upsell ("new! the card you love just got better") and app-download banner near the bottom add friction and dilute the storage message further.
- "storage and decor littles" section (bins & baskets, rugs, lighting, pillows) has no pricing or CTA of its own — just labeled photos, a dead end for anyone who clicks images expecting a landing page.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to storage/organization only — drop fall table, cookware, bedroom furniture, and the credit card block entirely, or push them to a separate send. Focus lifts both the open-to-click story and the unsubscribe rate.
- 2. Add an actual offer to the storage category — even 15-20% off modular storage would give the CTA a reason to click now instead of "eventually."
- 3. If bestsellers is the angle, show pricing on the six top-tile products — right now none of the storage grid items have visible prices, which is a big miss for a "bestsellers" subject line.
- 4. Move the "storage and decor littles" section either above the fold with real CTAs, or cut it — decorative filler with no price/link undercuts urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `20% off modular storage, this week only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shelving, cabinets & carts to clear the clutter`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Six new storage bestsellers, all under one roof`
