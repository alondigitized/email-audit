- Category cadence is logical — dining → living → bedroom → dining tables — a furnishing shopper can scan it.

- "2x Rewards" reinforcement appears as a secondary anchor below the hero, keeping the loyalty hook visible.
- Furniture-only narrowing differentiates this from the broader sale email — gives the send a reason to exist.

## What's weak

- "Up to 30% off" is a noticeably weaker number than the 60% headline that hit yesterday — looks like a step-down on the closing day.
- Subject ends in "+ 2x Rewards!" but the visual hierarchy makes Rewards feel secondary, not co-equal.
- No single hero product or anchor SKU — just a long grid, so the eye has nowhere to land first.
- CTA buttons are small and repeat ("Shop Now" pattern) — no specific verb-noun pull like "Shop Dining" or "Shop Sofas."

- 4. Reconcile the headline number with yesterday's main send (60% vs 30%) — either clarify it's furniture-specific deeper discounts elsewhere, or lead with the loyalty math (2x Rewards on top of 30%).
