# STEALS OF THE WEEK
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- CB2 finally consolidates the discount roll-up it's been serving piecemeal all week into one email, and the format works: "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" up top, then three stacked offers — Best Sellers up to 40% off, In-Store Floor Sample Sale, and Clearance up to 60% off — each with its own hero shot and percentage. This is the most information-dense, offer-forward send in the recent CB2 run, and it reads like a compilation of the individual sale emails from 2026-09-17 ("The best is up to 40% off") and 2026-09-18 ("FLOOR SAMPLE SALE") bundled into a single digest.
- The marble coffee table, walnut console, and floral-and-velvet bedding hero shots are all strong, moody CB2 lifestyle photography with no render issues. The weak spot is structural: three near-equal ctas stacked vertically means no single offer dominates, and the 60%-off clearance section — the best number in the email — is buried third, below two lesser deals.
- Solid, offer-led send. It beats the lookbook-detour emails (color equation, "THINGS OF QUALITY") on clarity, but the flat hierarchy costs it a point or two versus a true best-single-offer lead.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy both promise concrete savings ("STEALS OF THE WEEK" / "THIS WEEK'S STEALS"), no vague lifestyle framing.
- Three distinct, specific percentage offers visible without extra clicking: 40% off, floor sample sale, 60% off clearance.
- Clean top nav (New Arrivals | Furniture | Decor | Rugs | Lighting) keeps the email feeling like a real storefront, not a stripped promo blast.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is legible against its background, "SHOP NOW" ctas are consistently placed.
- Below-the-fold value-adds (app download, 10% Rewards Dollars, 15% off for texting, Design Services) round out the email without cluttering the top.

## What's weak

- No single hero offer — three sales of near-identical visual weight compete for attention, so the eye doesn't land anywhere specific first.
- The best deal (60% off clearance) is ranked third, after two lower/vaguer offers; percentage-hungry shoppers may bail before reaching it.
- "Floor Sample Sale" repeats verbatim from the 2026-09-18 send with no new discount figure attached, so it's the weakest of the three blocks and drags down the middle of the email.
- All three CTAs say "SHOP NOW" — no differentiation in copy to help scanning eyes distinguish which offer is which.

## Recommendations

- 1. Re-order so Clearance (60% off) leads — it's the strongest number in the email and should be the hero, not buried third.
- 2. Give each CTA distinct copy tied to its offer ("Shop Best Sellers," "Find Your Floor Sample," "Shop Clearance") instead of three identical "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 3. Add a discount figure to the Floor Sample Sale block — right now it's the only one of the three with no percentage, which weakens the whole "STEALS" premise.
- 4. Since this repeats the 09-17 and 09-18 individual sale emails, consider labeling it explicitly as a recap/roundup so subscribers who opened those don't feel like they're seeing stale content reissued.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Best sellers, floor samples, and clearance — all marked down now`
