# APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK AT SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 gating its Labor Day preview behind the app — "APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK AT SALE," hero reads "ONLY ON THE APP / THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF." It's a real hook (early access is a legitimate lever), and the phone-mockup-in-a-plant-hero visual is one of the more polished pieces of creative this brand has sent this week — a step up from the flat product-grid sends like 2026-08-20's Floor Sample Sale (4/10).
- The problem is the email immediately undercuts its own premise. Right after asking the recipient to download an app for "first look" access, it drops a full category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Rugs, Lighting, Decor, Entertaining) with a "SHOP THE SALE" button that presumably goes to the web site — so the "app only" framing is either misleading or the grid is a bait to click without the app. Then a second, unrelated "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" panel appears below that, diluting the one sale this email was built around. This is the same structural tic flagged on 2026-08-23 (two sales blurred into one email, 8/10 that time because the panels were at least sequenced cleanly) — here it reads more like scope creep than a coherent narrative.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, weak follow-through. The app gate isn't honored by the body copy, and a bolted-on clearance banner steals attention from the actual news.

## What's working

- Hero visual is genuinely well-composed — phone mockup with app screenshot nested into a lifestyle photo, legible serif type stack, clear App Store badge and "DOWNLOAD THE APP" CTA.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is a big, specific, visible number right in the hero — no guessing at the offer.
- Category grid (Furniture/Outdoor/Rugs/Lighting/Decor/Entertaining) gives an at-a-glance sense of what's on sale without forcing a scroll through single products.
- Loyalty/app perks are reinforced at the footer (10% Rewards, 15% off for text signup), giving repeat value signals beyond the single sale.

## What's weak

- "APP ONLY" subject and hero promise are contradicted by an in-email "SHOP THE SALE" button and full category grid that don't require the app — the exclusivity claim isn't backed by the actual navigation.
- The unrelated "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" panel mid-email is a second, competing offer that has nothing to do with Labor Day or the app-first premise — it fragments the one clear story this send had.
- No product photography or pricing in the category tiles — each tile is a flat lifestyle shot with a category label, so there's nothing concrete to click toward (no hero SKU, no price anchor).
- Legal disclaimer text sits directly under the category grid before any CTA, adding visual clutter right where the eye should be moving toward "SHOP THE SALE."

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the app-only contradiction: either make the body genuinely app-gated (CTA reads "Get the app to shop" and links to app store only) or drop "APP ONLY" from the subject/hero and reframe as "first look" with parity CTAs — right now it reads like bait.
- 2. Cut the "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" panel out of this send entirely; let Labor Day carry the whole email, and send clearance as its own follow-up (this brand already has a clearance cadence — 2026-08-20, 2026-08-16 — no need to cram it in here too).
- 3. Swap at least the top two category tiles (Furniture, Outdoor) for an actual hero product with a price, since those are the highest-intent categories and currently have zero product specificity.
- 4. Move the fine-print disclaimer block below the "SHOP THE SALE" button, not above it, so it doesn't interrupt the visual path from grid to CTA.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day Sale: App gets it first`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off — early access starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Download the app for first access to Labor Day markdowns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting and more — up to 50% off starts today.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard MI (Message Interactive)-templated CB2 promotional email; core mechanics render correctly, but the QA pass surfaces missing authentication/unsubscribe headers, unencrypted third-party pixel loads, and widespread missing alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 60 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP probe (expected behavior for redirect-domain links — not itself a defect, but means link destinations are unverified).
- Multiple Dotomi ad-tracking calls load over plaintext HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` and ten sequential cookie-redirect pixels `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS resources are frequently stripped or blocked by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), which can break the tracking/attribution these pixels exist for.
- CB2's own tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) correctly use HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 of the content/product images are missing `alt` text (e.g. `4f518d08-5886-446f-a164-187ffa2c9aec.jpg`, `84e471bd-9a6c-4e01-baf3-d96aa577e29f.jpg`, `8b5bd3aa-0895-4f07-a44a-d459ebcf87d6.jpg`, `f4624093-0a3a-4fff-8e49-0f74878682c8.jpg`, `f6cce36f-4e38-43fb-b180-13bbba29da4d.jpg`, `c4d5a99f-5e44-4031-a025-70a015a1214d.jpg`, `65069ce7-cdc0-4698-86a7-6c0a7a15cf6f.jpg`, `4d540dc3-561d-4295-b49a-1f667d773eaa.jpg`, `51beedf3-4a60-44a8-b55b-e04023610f91.png`, `53c67fab-a66d-4816-930d-835edd7ce2aa.gif`). With images blocked by default in most clients, recipients get no fallback text, degrading both accessibility (screen readers) and comprehension when images don't load.
- Tracking pixels also lack `alt` (expected/non-issue for 1x1 beacons, e.g. `UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`).
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and mobile media queries (`max-width: 418px`) — no structural rendering defects found in the truncated source.
- Custom `@font-face` declarations (Lineto Brown, GT Sectra, HNW05) have no web-safe fallback chain issues detected in the excerpt (`a` selector falls back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`), but most email clients strip `@font-face` entirely — not a bug, just expected degradation to fallback fonts.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization fields observed in the provided source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Note: all three warnings are flagged by the QA tooling as possibly relay-side capture gaps rather than confirmed sender-side omissions — recommend verifying against raw headers at the MTA before treating as a compliance failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable — the 60 outbound links were skipped by the HTTP probe (redirect/tracking domains), so destination URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page alignment with cb2.com could not be confirmed in this pass.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the Dotomi ad pixels/redirects (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/.../pub1–10.php`) to HTTPS to avoid silent blocking by mail clients.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 flagged product/content images for accessibility and no-image fallback.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` / `Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay capture) to confirm actual CAN-SPAM and authentication compliance rather than relying on this relay-side check.
4. Re-run the link probe against a sample of the 60 skipped tracking links (resolving redirects) to confirm final landing URLs carry correct UTM parameters and route to live cb2.com pages.
