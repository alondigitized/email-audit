# RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 back in single-category cascade mode — this time it's rugs, not furniture, but the structure is identical to every send this week: bold hero statement ("EVERYTHING YOU WANT IN A RUG"), a grid of named products (Lavish, Anders, Leonore, Eros, Amalfi), a CTA, then the now-standard bolt-ons (Upholstery Event, app download, "More For You"). It's clean and well-photographed, but it's the fourth or fifth near-identical template this week with only the subject and hero category swapped.
- The rug category itself is reasonably differentiated from CB2's recent furniture pushes, so there's a genuine reason for this send to exist rather than just cycling the same "new arrivals" language. Execution is solid but unexciting — no price, no % off, no urgency anywhere in the primary content, just named SKUs on a moodboard.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send that will underperform on clicks because there's no concrete reason to act now — compare to the 2026-09-25 "Upholstery Event" send (6/10) which at least led with a clear percentage discount.

## What's working

- Clear single-category focus (rugs only) — easier to parse than CB2's multi-tile mosaic sends.
- Hero image is well-composed and immediately communicates "rug" as the subject.
- Named products (Lavish, Anders, Leonore, Eros, Amalfi) with clean grid layout and no overlapping text or broken images.
- Secondary CTA block for the Upholstery Event (25% off) gives at least one concrete, quantified offer within the email.
- "More For You" section at the bottom shows actual prices ($99.95–$2239.00), useful for anyone who scrolls that far.

## What's weak

- No discount, price, or offer anywhere in the primary rug content — just product names, no reason to click now vs. later.
- Zero urgency or time-boundedness in the hero or rug section.
- Subject "RUGS FOR EVERY ROOM" is generic category language, same pattern as "New arrivals are in" and "Restock alert" from earlier this week — brand is repeating the same beats with new nouns.
- Product tiles show name only (Lavish, Anders, etc.) with no price, size, or material visible until the very bottom "More For You" module — a shopper interested in a specific rug has to scroll past two unrelated modules (Upholstery Event, App download) to find pricing.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the 6th CB2 send this week per brand history, all sharing the same visual template.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real offer in the hero or immediately below it — even a "new arrivals, up to 20% off" would give a reason to click now instead of just browsing.
- 2. Add price under each of the five named rugs in the top grid — shoppers evaluating Lavish vs. Anders vs. Leonore need price to decide, not just name.
- 3. Move or cut the Upholstery Event and App Download blocks out of this email — they dilute the single-category focus that's this send's one structural strength.
- 4. Vary the subject-line formula — CB2 has sent "New arrivals are in," "Restock alert," and now "Rugs for every room" this week; all read as generic category pings.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Rugs, ranked: our 5 best-sellers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New rugs just landed — runners to 10x16`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hand-knotted, vintage-inspired, made to outlast trends.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From Lavish to Amalfi — five new rugs, one shop-the-look grid.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email renders via a table-based MSO/Outlook-compatible template with standard client resets, but ships one unresolved merge token and several tracking/compliance gaps surfaced by automated QA (43% pass rate).

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect domains — not itself a defect, but means link destinations are unverified).
- Multiple legacy ad-tracking pixels load over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 endpoints, same pattern)
  - Risk: mail clients that block mixed-content/non-HTTPS image loads (Outlook, some webmail) will silently drop these, breaking whatever tracking/attribution they're meant to capture.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content/product images and 1 tracking pixel (`o.gif`) are missing `alt` attributes, e.g.:
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/732cf7e6-3cc2-4508-9ba2-fa607209dbe2.jpg`
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/a1f0160c-5c3a-4d7b-8704-4d47c7c5ceab.jpg`
  - (and 8 more with the same pattern) — screen readers will announce nothing for these when images are blocked/disabled, which is the default in most mail clients.
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px, and Apple Mail reformatting disable meta tag — no rendering defects identified in the visible markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- [FAIL] Unresolved raw merge token in rendered output: `%%reviews_skulist%%`. This indicates a template/personalization-engine failure — the token was not substituted before send, and will display literally to recipients.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation rather than a sender-side omission; cannot confirm compliance status from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) header not detected — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC outcome) not present in the captured headers — authentication status is unverifiable from this data; likely a relay/capture artifact rather than evidence of a missing DKIM/SPF setup.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No issues found — insufficient data to evaluate, since the 70 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe and their resolved landing-page destinations/UTM parameters were not captured.

7. Recommendations
- Fix the merge-token resolution pipeline so `%%reviews_skulist%%` renders as intended content (likely a product-review SKU list block) before send.
- Migrate the Dotomi ad-tracking pixels/redirects (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com` pub1–pub10) to HTTPS to prevent silent drops in mixed-content-blocking clients.
- Add `alt` text to the 10 flagged content images for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked.
- Re-run header capture through a path that preserves `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` to get a real compliance/deliverability read — current absence is inconclusive, not confirmed non-compliance.
- Re-run link resolution on a sample of the 70 skipped tracking links to confirm final landing-page URLs carry correct UTM parameters and match live site pages.
