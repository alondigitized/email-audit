# School starts SOON! Up to 20% off essentials 🎒
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing back-to-school, but it can't stay on message for more than one screen. The subject promises "up to 20% off essentials" for school, and the hero delivers that cleanly — a dark, atmospheric back-to-school banner with backpacks, lunch boxes, duffels, pencil cases, food storage, and water bottles laid out in a tidy six-tile grid. That's the best part of the email and it's genuinely well-executed.
- Then it falls apart into the same kitchen-sink sprawl flagged in the 8/2 "top designer" send (8.00/10) and the 7/31 dinnerware send (7.00/10) — except here the drift starts even earlier. The very top banner, above the fold, reads "up to 50% off summer sale," a completely different promotion that has nothing to do with school. From there the email cascades into desks/chairs, a $5,000 designer sweepstakes, nursery furniture, car seats, a store credit card pitch, an app download ad, and a second designer-sweepstakes CTA — none of which reinforces the subject line's promise.
- Net: strong hero, wrong entry point, and a body that keeps hitting six different offers no back-to-school shopper asked for. Above average execution polish, below average message discipline.

## What's working

- The hero cleanly matches the subject: "back-to-school sale: up to 20% off," with a moody, well-lit lifestyle shot.
- The category grid (backpacks, lunch boxes, duffel bags, pencil cases, food storage, water bottles) is genuinely useful — six clear entry points with real product photography and legible labels.
- No render bugs anywhere — no overlapping text, no broken images, no placeholder copy across a very long email.
- The bottom product grid shows real prices, including a visible markdown ($54.99 → $42.23), which is a concrete, trustworthy signal.
- Brand identity is consistent top to bottom — logo, color palette, and tone hold together despite the sprawl.

## What's weak

- The very first banner ("up to 50% off summer sale") contradicts the subject line's "up to 20% off essentials" offer before the reader even reaches the school content — a confusing, off-brief opener.
- The email drifts hard past the back-to-school section into nursery furniture, car seats, and a dresser — none of it relevant to a "school starts soon" subject.
- Two separate CTAs for the same $5,000 designer sweepstakes (TheDesignDesk) plus a credit card pitch plus an app-download ad turn this into six emails stapled together, diluting the one offer the subject sold.
- "Select styles" fine print sits directly under both sale headlines with no clarification of what's excluded, which reads as classic exclusionary fine print.
- Visual hierarchy never resolves to one hero offer — by the time a reader scrolls past three unrelated sales, the original 20%-off school offer has lost all primacy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the "up to 50% off summer sale" banner entirely from the top, or move it well below the back-to-school block — leading with a second, contradictory sale undercuts the subject line's promise in the first three inches of the email.
- 2. Trim the body to back-to-school only: hero + category grid + one CTA. Move nursery/car seat/dresser cross-sell, the Crate Card pitch, the app download ad, and the TheDesignDesk sweepstakes to a separate send — this email is currently six campaigns wearing one subject line.
- 3. Consolidate the two TheDesignDesk CTAs into one; repeating the same $5,000 offer twice in one email reads as padding, not urgency.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("ends 8/9" or similar) near the hero CTA — right now "up to 20% off" has no time pressure, which weakens the "SOON" urgency set up in the subject.
- 5. Tighten "Select styles" into visible, specific exclusions (or drop the disclaimer weight) so the offer doesn't read as bait-and-switch.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Backpacks, lunch boxes & more — up to 20% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left: back-to-school essentials, 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, lunch boxes, and everything for the first day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New styles, monogramming, and sizes for every grade.`
