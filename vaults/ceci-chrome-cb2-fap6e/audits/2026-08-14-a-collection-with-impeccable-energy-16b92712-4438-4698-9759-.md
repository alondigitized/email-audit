# A collection with impeccable energy
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's tenth creative mode this week, and it's back to heritage-editorial designer-spotlight — this time "Rio" by Studio Anansi, "the impeccable energy of onyx meets the grounding nature of walnut." It's the same beat as 8/11's "Prepare to want it all" (5/10) and 8/10's "Designed by Andreas Hansen" (5/10): named-designer collection launch, moody warm-toned product photography, no price, no discount attached to the hero product itself. The top banner ("UP TO 40% OFF END OF SUMMER SALE") is the only concrete offer, and it's generic — bolted onto a collection email rather than driving it.
- The visuals are genuinely strong — a round onyx-and-walnut side table hero, console, coffee table, dining table, and storage pieces all shot with consistent warm styling — but the email is long (nine+ full-bleed product images before you even reach "More For You") with a single small "SHOP THE RIO COLLECTION" CTA repeated twice and no pricing anywhere in the featured collection. Given this is the tenth distinct mode from CB2 this week, subscriber fatigue is a real risk, and this send doesn't differentiate itself enough from the two prior designer-spotlight sends to justify another one.
- Solid photography, weak commercial mechanics — this lands in the same 5/10 territory as its heritage-editorial siblings.

## What's working

- Hero table shot (onyx + walnut round table) is striking and immediately communicates material/quality.
- Consistent warm, moody styling across all six product shots ties the collection together visually.
- Designer credibility block (Studio Anansi headshot + bio) adds a point of differentiation vs. a plain product drop.
- Clean "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom with visible prices gives a fallback path to purchase if the hero collection doesn't land.
- No render bugs — images, text, and layout all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Zero prices shown anywhere in the featured Rio collection — a real friction point for furniture-category shoppers who need to know the number before clicking.
- The "UP TO 40% OFF" banner offer is disconnected from the Rio collection below it — unclear if Rio is included in the sale at all.
- CTA copy is generic ("SHOP THE RIO COLLECTION") repeated identically twice with no urgency or differentiation.
- Email is very long with heavy image weight before reaching any concrete offer or social proof, which hurts mobile scroll-through.
- Third near-identical designer-editorial send in five days (8/10 Andreas Hansen, 8/11 David Bireli/Provence, now Studio Anansi) — cadence fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. State whether Rio is part of the 40%-off sale directly under the hero — if it is, say "Rio Collection — up to 40% off" in the hero copy; if not, drop the sale banner from this send entirely so it doesn't create false expectations.
- 2. Add starting prices ("Console from $1,499") under at least the hero and console shots — furniture buyers won't click through six scrolls of unpriced images.
- 3. Vary CTA copy across the two placements — e.g., "Shop Onyx & Walnut" for the top, "See the Full Rio Collection" at the bottom — to break the repetition.
- 4. Trim the collection to 3-4 product shots max before the designer bio, moving remaining pieces to a "See more from Rio" link — reduces scroll fatigue.
- 5. Space out designer-spotlight sends — this is the third in five days; alternate with sale-led or new-arrivals modes to avoid subscriber pattern-fatigue.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
CB2 promotional email built on the "MI" (Marigold/Cheetah Digital) ESP template, with a Dotomi/Merkle third-party ad-sync pipeline embedded. 57% automated pass rate driven by unsubscribe-header gaps, missing image alt text, and multiple insecure `http://` tracking pixels.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 10 sequential `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub{1-10}.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` calls plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id` — all served over plaintext HTTP. Modern mail clients (Outlook, Gmail image proxy) commonly block or strip non-HTTPS image/pixel requests, meaning this cookie-sync chain likely fails silently for a large share of opens.
- 68 outbound tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for redirect-domain links); no destination-resolution issues can be confirmed or ruled out from this pass.
- Legitimate first-party tracking pixels present and HTTPS: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/8ea97b7c-8b24-4267-917e-a74d727a2e16`.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 content images missing `alt` text (all `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg`), plus the 11 Dotomi pixel/redirect images also flagged for missing alt — screen readers and image-blocked clients get no fallback context.
- Template uses standard MSO conditional comments, `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px, and `-ms-text-size-adjust`/`-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets — no structural rendering defects detected in the truncated source.
- Multiple `@font-face` declarations (Lineto Brown, GT Sectra, HNW05 via crateandbarrel.com) with no fallback stack failure risk noted, though heavy custom-font reliance means Outlook/Windows Mail will render fallback sans-serif only (expected, not a bug).

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — sender authentication status is unverified via this relay; recommend checking raw headers at the source MTA rather than the relay-captured copy.
