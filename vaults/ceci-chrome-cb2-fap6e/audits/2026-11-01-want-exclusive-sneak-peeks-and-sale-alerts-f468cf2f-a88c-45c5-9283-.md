# Want exclusive sneak peeks and sale alerts?
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a subscription-acquisition email dressed as a newsletter. The subject asks "Want exclusive sneak peeks and sale alerts?", and the hero is an empty phone mockup reading "get on the list." It pushes a text-signup CTA to people who are already on the email list. The body is a catalog of unrelated modules: a food processor, a Thanksgiving hosting guide, a Design Desk sweepstakes, a Black Friday text teaser, product tiles, the credit card, the app, and Design Desk again.
- The best content is buried. The "2 days left! accent chairs, tables & rugs up to 20% off" bar is the only live sale, and it sits above a hero that doesn't mention it. After the Nov 1 "Today only: 20% off top play furniture!" send, which had a concrete offer, this one is a step down in clarity.
- Verdict: a mediocre, unfocused send with a decent offer hidden inside it. The ask, text signup, doesn't match the subject's promise of sale alerts.

## What's working

- The "2 days left! accent chairs, tables & rugs up to 20% off" bar is a specific, time-bound offer.
- Primary CTAs are clear black pill buttons: "Sign Up For Texts," "Shop Bestsellers," "Book Your Free Appointment."
- The Black Friday teaser ("10% off your next order" for texting) gives a concrete reason to sign up.
- The "it all adds up to a home you love" module surfaces 10% back in Reward Dollars, a real loyalty benefit.
- The "we love these for you" product tiles show clear prices and seasonal items (stockings, dinner plate).

## What's weak

- The hero is a blank phone mockup with no product or offer, so the eye lands on nothing.
- The CTA doesn't match the audience. Asking existing subscribers to join a text list doesn't serve the sale-alert promise in the subject.
- The Thanksgiving Hosting Guide module has headline text clipped at the left edge and the button sitting on top of the image.
- Too many competing modules, including two Design Desk blocks, the app, the credit card and the sweepstakes. No single next step stands out.
- The 20% off offer is only in the top bar and is not tied to any hero, product or CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero the live offer: "Accent chairs, tables & rugs up to 20% off, 2 days left," with product imagery and a "Shop Accent Furniture" button. Move text signup to a slim module lower down.
- 2. Cut the body to three modules: offer, a product grid, and one signup or Design Desk block. Drop the duplicate Design Desk and the app tile.
- 3. Fix the Thanksgiving Hosting Guide module so the headline is fully visible and the button doesn't cover the image.
- 4. Show the 10% Black Friday text-signup incentive in the hero or subject rather than at the halfway point.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Accent furniture up to 20% off: 2 days left`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Text us for 10% off + early Black Friday deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Chairs, tables & rugs on sale through Sunday. Plus 10% off when you join our texts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get Black Friday deals before everyone else, and 10% off your next order.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run passed 57% of checks with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main code-level findings are about 24 images with no alt text, a viewport that blocks zoom, and header checks that couldn't be verified because of the relay. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, unsubscribe link and body links were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **59 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp). Five requests for one beacon is redundant and adds third-party calls on open.
- **Other open-tracking and personalization beacons:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/61b61414-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are normal for the ESP, but `mi_u` is a long per-recipient identifier embedded in image URLs.
- No broken or malformed links were confirmed in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 24 images are missing alt text** (QA WARN).
  - **Content images:** `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `3bc793f8-….gif`, `552af8d6-….jpg`, `6588a9e0-….jpg`, `720796e3-….jpg`, `810221f7-….jpg`, `2f3f07fc-….jpg`, `452a03ef-….jpg`, `971aa10f-….jpg`, and the Scene7 `2026_0920_CB_ThanksgivingGuide_Tertiary-…` image. With images blocked or a screen reader, these show no text.
  - **Product-recommendation images:** seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` files (`560d850e7e517ede`, `789e7381ff44be74`, `5ec241ce0b852c66`, `00423cc9007b550c`, `f5ca909404423879`, `45e8c84f5a9185ff`, `18f126218cc222ec`). These are dynamic and also have no alt.
  - **Tracking pixels:** `448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif` and the `dv.` beacon. These should carry `alt=""`, which is lower severity.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text-size lock:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` in the later block stop Apple clients from scaling text.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid XHTML, and the doctype is XHTML. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production, and "IMPUT" is a typo.
- **Redundant markup:** `charset` is declared twice, and `format-detection` appears four times with overlapping values. This is harmless.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`:** this forces centered text on every `<p>` unless it is overridden inline. It is fragile, but no rendering fault was confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) appeared in the truncated HTML. Footer and body copy past the truncation point weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe is not found (WARN).** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is **unverified, not a confirmed defect**.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check it in raw headers from a direct-delivery test.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain, so alignment can't be checked from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** these could not be verified because of the truncation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters can't be assessed. All 59 links are wrapped in click-tracking redirects and none were probed, so the final landing URLs and any `utm_*` parameters are unknown.
- Landing-page alignment with the subject ("Want exclusive sneak peeks and sale alerts?") can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content and product-recommendation images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop the blanket `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rules.
3. Escape the ampersand in `<title>` and remove the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
4. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixel requests into one if the duplicates aren't intentional.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from raw headers delivered outside the relay, to settle the three open WARNs.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check final URLs, UTMs and the footer unsubscribe and address.
