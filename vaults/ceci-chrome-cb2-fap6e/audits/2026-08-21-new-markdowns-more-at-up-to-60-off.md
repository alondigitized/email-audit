# NEW markdowns! More at up to 60% off →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids at its most scattershot. The hero nails the assignment — "up to 60% off + new markdowns," clean daisy-field imagery, one obvious "Shop Clearance" button — and honestly, this fixes the exact problem I flagged in the 2026-08-20 "NEW MARKDOWNS ADDED" send, where the same offer got buried under filler. Here it leads.
- But then the email sprawls into backpack bundles, a TheDesignDesk $5000 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product grid unrelated to clearance, a new Crate Card pitch, an app download push, and a second TheDesignDesk CTA — six distinct asks after the hero. None of it is broken or ugly, it's just unfocused; a clearance email shouldn't spend two-thirds of its length cross-selling a credit card and a design consultation service.
- Net: strong open/click bait up top, diluted follow-through. Worth opening, worth one click on "Shop Clearance," but everything below the first fold is competing with itself rather than reinforcing the offer.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "up to 60% off + new markdowns" with a single "Shop Clearance" button, no scroll needed to find the offer.
- Clean category sub-nav (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Fall Sale) gives multiple clickable entry points right under the hero.
- Product grid ("we love these for you") shows real priced SKUs (Nuna strollers, kids dresser, glider chair) with no lorem-ipsum or broken images.
- Visual polish is consistent — every section has clean photography and no render bugs.

## What's weak

- Message sprawl: hero offer, backpack bundles, $5000 sweepstakes, unrelated product grid, credit card pitch, app download, and a second sweepstakes CTA all stack in one email — no single throughline.
- The "we love these for you" grid isn't visibly tied to the clearance offer — no sale badges or struck-through prices, so it reads as a generic merchandising block, not part of the 60%-off story.
- Two separate TheDesignDesk callouts (mid-email and near the footer) for the same $5000 giveaway is redundant real estate that could've reinforced the clearance CTA instead.
- Subject says "more at up to 60% off" but nothing past the hero and nav bar visually reinforces clearance pricing — the discount story ends abruptly at the top.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to two beats: clearance hero + nav, then the product grid — but badge those grid items with sale pricing so they visibly connect to "60% off," not as an unrelated cross-sell.
- 2. Move backpack bundles, Crate Card, app download, and one of the two TheDesignDesk blocks into a separate send — this is diluting a clearance email's job.
- 3. Add strikethrough/sale pricing on at least 2-3 grid products to make the 60% off claim concrete instead of abstract.
- 4. Keep only one TheDesignDesk CTA if it must stay, near the footer, not competing with the clearance hero for attention.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got bigger: up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nursery, bedding, backpacks & more — while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns added across furniture, decor & gear.`
