# Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a standard Crate & Barrel Kids Black Friday blast. It has a clear hero and a single offer, "Up to 50% off + free shipping", with one "Shop the Sale" button. The render is heavily downscaled (680px wide, about 8,200px tall), so I can read the layout and the larger headlines but not the fine print or most prices.
- The strengths are the offer and the structure. The hero and the grid of eight category tiles (kids furniture, baby furniture, toys, nod chairs, bedding, playroom, holiday decor, rugs) all carry specific "up to X% off" lines. The weakness is length and sprawl. After the sale grid come a Babyletto/Namesake feature, a gifts banner, a Feeding America cause block, a product carousel, a rewards card pitch, an app promo and a TheDesignDesk pitch. Several of these dilute the Black Friday message.
- Judgement: competent and on-season, but it is the same send this brand has made all week. It lands in the mid-range, and the subject offers nothing new over the earlier "It's on!" and "Time to shop" sends.

## What's working

- The hero states one clear offer ("Up to 50% off + free shipping") with a visible "Shop the Sale" button.
- Each category tile carries its own discount (50%, 30%, 20%), so the shopper can see where the best deals are.
- "Ships free" tags on the bedding tiles reinforce the free-shipping promise.
- The seasonal imagery (stockings, a tree, cozy bedding) fits the holiday moment.
- The "we love these for you" product row, with prices, gives a more concrete click path than the category tiles.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. The rewards card, app, design service and Feeding America blocks all compete with the sale.
- The "Shop the Sale" CTA is repeated, and the other buttons are generic ("Shop Gifts that Give Back", "Learn More", "Download Now"), so there is no single clear next step.
- The hero text sits over a busy textured photo, and the sub-line under the headline is small and hard to read.
- The discount tiers vary (50%, 30%, 20%) without a visible deadline, so urgency is weak.
- There are no reviews or social proof, and the offer exclusions in the fine print sit far below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, the category grid and one product row. Move the rewards card, app and design service content to a separate send.
- 2. Add a visible end date and time to the hero ("Ends Monday midnight") for credible urgency.
- 3. Put the hero text on a solid panel so the offer is readable on mobile.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop kids furniture deals" and "Shop toys up to 30% off".
- 5. Add star ratings to the product row.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Kids' furniture up to 50% off + free shipping`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Black Friday toys, bedding & more: ends Monday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beds, bedding and play furniture, plus free shipping on orders $79+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest kids' sale of the year ends Monday. See what's marked down.`

# Technical Audit: "Black Friday is on! Shop our deals now →"

## 1. Technical Summary
This is a Marigold/Cheetah-style XHTML table-layout email with a full MSO/VML scaffold. Its most significant confirmed defects are about 33 images flagged for missing alt text and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. Authentication, unsubscribe and UTM findings could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **78 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them because they are click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chains and link health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Repeated pixels.** Every one of these is flagged for missing alt text:
  - 6 identical `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` images.
  - 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` pixels. The `rlcdn.com` domain is the third-party LiveRamp domain.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/82c8f4a4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`, which looks like the open pixel.
- **Redundant pixels.** The 6 identical `rp` images and the 5 `rlcdn` pixels are redundant requests. The third-party `rlcdn` calls add extra DNS and connection overhead and are more likely to be blocked.
- **Subscriber identifier in URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a…` hash appears in image URLs. These URLs are fetched on open and can be cached or logged by proxies.
- **Stale asset.** One image path, `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`, carries an October (`1002`) campaign name. It looks reused from an earlier send, so confirm it is current.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (about 21).** The QA output lists these:
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif`, including `9808d754…`, `b14e1268…`, `8f543474…` and `26784f29…`.
  - `m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`.
  - The scene7 `…BF_DealsGrid_REMIX_Hero…` image.
  - The scene7 `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` spacer.
  - If the hero and deal-grid images carry the offers as text, they are inaccessible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt text, tracking and spacer images (about 13).** For 1x1 pixels and spacers, `alt=""` is the correct fix. The QA flag does not show whether the attribute is absent or empty, so verify this.
- **Zoom blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility failure under WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Doctype and markup.**
  - The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is paired with unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">`, `<meta name="color-scheme">` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible">` tags. This is mostly harmless.
  - There is a duplicate `<meta charset>` alongside the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
- **Global CSS.**
  - `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are global and aggressive. They override inherited alignment and line-height on all text.
  - `img {max-width:600px}` at ≥768px conflicts with `img {max-width:640px}` at ≥640px, because the later rule wins in the overlap.
- **Template leftovers.** Comments such as `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and the empty `<!--[if !mso]><!-- --> <!--<![endif]-->` block are unfilled template remnants.

4. Re-run the audit against the raw RFC 822 message, not the relay copy. This will confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
5. Resolve a sample of the 78 tracking links to confirm the landing pages and UTM parameters.
