# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a store-traffic send that fights itself. The headline, "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE / In stores for a limited time," is clean and the bedroom hero is beautiful. But it gives no discount, no dates and no named product. The only action is a black "FIND A STORE" button. An email shopper who wanted a deal gets nothing concrete in the top third.
- The real offer sits below the fold. The recycled "BLACK FRIDAY SALE, UP TO 60% OFF" and "FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OF $99 OR MORE" banners are the same creative as the 11-19 "cult favorites" and 11-20 "FREE SHIPPING FOR BLACK FRIDAY" sends. A regular subscriber has now seen these banners three times in three days. The floor sample story and the online sale also pull in opposite directions. Floor samples are in-store only, while the shipping and 60% banners push people online.
- Verdict: good photography, weak commercial clarity. Without a number or a date, it reads as a filler send in a crowded Black Friday run.

## What's working

- The "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" headline is large and clear, and the "limited time" subhead adds some scarcity.
- The bedroom hero is warm, premium and on-brand, and it is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The black "FIND A STORE" button has strong contrast and is easy to spot.
- The Black Friday and free-shipping banners give a fallback offer: up to 60% off, free shipping over $99.
- The footer modules (app, 10% back rewards, 15% off for texts) are tidy and give secondary paths.

## What's weak

- The hero has no discount, price or product name, so the "sale" has nothing concrete in it.
- "Limited time" has no date, so the urgency is not credible.
- The "FIND A STORE" CTA is weak for an email audience. It sends them away from the offers shown below it.
- The Black Friday and free-shipping banners are repeats from earlier sends this week, and they dilute the floor sample message.
- The disclaimer block is long and dense, and it is the only place the real terms appear.
- Sections are stacked with no priority. Three promos plus four footer modules compete for attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number and a date in the hero, such as "Up to 50% off floor samples, this weekend only." Name the product in the photo (the upholstered bed) with a price.
- 2. Make the CTA specific and tied to location: "FIND YOUR NEAREST SAMPLE SALE" or "SEE WHAT'S IN STORE."
- 3. Drop the recycled Black Friday and free-shipping banners, or compress them into one thin strip. Floor samples should be the whole story.
- 4. Add three or four product tiles with sample prices, so the shopper can see what is worth a trip.
- 5. Add a "check store availability" cue to cover the in-store-only limitation.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `One-of-a-kind floor models, priced to move. Find your nearest CB2 store.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's source is a well-formed XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and an Outlook MSO fallback block. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The real findings are missing alt text, 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels and zoom-restricting CSS and meta settings. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed.** 53 tracking links were skipped because they are on click-redirect domains, so destination status codes, redirect chains and expiry are unverified.
- **Mixed-content pixels.** 11 third-party Dotomi (Epsilon) pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
  - Gmail and Apple Mail proxy or block non-HTTPS images. These pixels will likely fail to fire, so cookie-sync and retargeting data is lost.
  - The 10 sequential cookie-redirect pixels also add third-party requests on open.
- **Own-domain pixels use HTTPS.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/fe04ed90-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are fine on protocol.
- **User identifier in the pixel URL.** The `mi_u=56aaa278...` parameter appears to be a hashed user ID in the open-tracking pixel URL. I can't tell from the truncated data whether it is a hash of the email address.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** Three images served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute:
  - `9be6e2f7-938a-4bf4-8477-6f83e00f3706.gif`
  - `93958f7d-08ad-42ee-a6ba-0720896ac854.jpg`
  - `65d9a6b8-2058-42f5-8c88-11ebbb46a899.jpg`
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these give no text equivalent. A missing attribute is read differently from `alt=""`, so the filename may be announced.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** The Dotomi, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels lack `alt`. They are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""`. This is low severity, but it inflates the warning count.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;...}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off user text scaling on iOS, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. The earlier `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` is overridden.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never replaced. It has no rendering impact.
- **Font dependencies.**
  - Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/...`).
  - `HNW05` is loaded from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts anyway, so the Helvetica/sans-serif fallback is what renders.
- **Not assessable.** The HTML is truncated, so body markup, button implementation (bulletproof VML or not), dark-mode handling and total size (Gmail clips at about 102KB) can't be verified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The truncated source can't rule them out in the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Missing headers: unverified.** QA flagged `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results` as absent. The QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping or not capturing headers. I can't confirm a sender-side defect from this evidence.
- **Risk if the headers are genuinely absent.** Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. Verify against the raw original message from a direct-delivery test.
- **Body-level items not checkable.** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address in the footer fall outside the truncated source.
- **Sender domain.** The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 53 click links were skipped as tracking redirects and the truncated HTML doesn't expose final destinations. UTM presence, the redirect target and whether the landing page matches the "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" subject can't be confirmed. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the `dv.cb2.com` pixel suggest campaign and message IDs are tracked on the platform side.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` to the 3 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. **Upgrade or remove the Dotomi pixels.** Serve them over HTTPS or drop the 10 `pubN.php` pixels. They are probably blocked in major clients already.
3. **Restore zoom and text scaling.** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. **Confirm authentication and unsubscribe headers.** Check the raw headers of a direct-to-mailbox send (not through the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Treat the current warnings as unverified.
5. **Improve the QA tooling.** Capture full HTML to check the Gmail 102KB clipping limit, footer unsubscribe and address, and merge tokens. Resolve the redirect chains of the 53 skipped links to check UTMs and landing status.
6. **Clean up the template.** Remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and the double-slash font URLs.
