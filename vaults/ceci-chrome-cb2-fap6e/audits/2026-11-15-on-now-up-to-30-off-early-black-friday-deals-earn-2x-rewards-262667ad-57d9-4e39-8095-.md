# ON NOW! Up to 30% off Early Black Friday deals + Earn 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent but crowded Crate & Barrel Early Black Friday blast, and it is also the Nth near-identical send in a few days. Compare "Ends soon: Early Black Friday deals + 2x Rewards" earlier today and "ON NOW! The last Double Rewards of the year!" yesterday. The red header strip ("last weekend to get it by Thanksgiving") and the hero "it's on! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" make the offer clear. After that the email loses focus. It runs through a 2x2 category grid, a kitchen block, stockings, a gift shop banner, a repeated Double Rewards panel, a product row, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design desk promo.
- The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print. The structure holds up: there is a clear lead offer, and the kitchen section has a concrete "up to 30% off all-clad cookware" and Philips espresso "up to $350 off". But 30% is a weaker headline than the "up to 40%" and "up to 50%" lines from the past two days. Subscribers who got those will see this as a downgrade or a rerun.
- Judgment: solid execution, tired concept. It will convert some intent-heavy kitchen shoppers, but the fatigue is real and the length buries the best content.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 30% off Early Black Friday" with a single visible "Shop Now" button.
- The header strip adds credible urgency by tying the deadline to Thanksgiving delivery.
- The 2x2 category grid (entertaining, kitchen brands, furniture, holiday decor) gives scannable entry points.
- The kitchen block names real brands and specifics: All-Clad, Philips espresso machines, Nespresso, Wüsthof cutlery, Made In.
- Each content block has its own clear CTA ("Shop All Kitchen Deals", "Shop Stockings").

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards message appears in the top strip, again mid-email in a red panel, and again in the credit card block. That is three repeats.
- The email is very long. The stockings, gift shop, app, design desk and credit card sections dilute the Black Friday offer.
- The 30% headline is lower than the 40% and 50% claims in recent sends, so the escalation story is gone.
- The "we love these for you" product row has small, hard-to-read prices and no strong CTA.
- The gift shop banner is a large, mostly empty red panel with only the script logo, which uses a lot of vertical space for little content.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to hero, the 2x2 grid, one kitchen block, one Rewards panel and a footer. Drop the stockings, the app block and the design desk block.
- 2. Keep the Double Rewards message to one placement and move it near the top.
- 3. Collapse the cadence. Send one consolidated Early Black Friday email per day at most, and make each one visibly different (a theme, a hero product, a new deadline).
- 4. Replace the generic "Shop Now" with "Shop kitchen deals" or "Shop up to 30% off".
- 5. Make the product row larger, with 4 items, readable prices and a savings callout.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template (Movable Ink/"MI" styling) with a 57% QA pass rate: 0 failures, 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes, a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom, and leftover template artifacts. The header-based compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, not failed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link validity is unverified.** 73 tracking links on click-redirect domains were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect chains were not checked.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical LiveRamp pixels (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire on open, and each also lacks `alt`. These are redundant calls to a third-party domain; they are a deliverability and privacy surface, not a rendering bug.
- **Other tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/7ec6e46e-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are first-party open/dynamic-content beacons, also without `alt`.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` value is repeated across the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` URLs. This is a per-recipient hash and is expected for Movable Ink, but it means any forwarded copy carries that identity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 29 images (confirmed by QA):**
  - 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (jpg, png, 2 gifs), including `bbf6f9d4-…png` and `6100bbef-…jpg`.
  - 7 dynamic images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (`560d850e7e517ede.png` and others).
  - 6 tracking pixels (the `rlcdn` and `o.gif` beacons above, plus the `dv.` image).
  - The QA output was cut off after the `dv.` entry, so the real count may be higher.
  - With images blocked, content images such as the 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and the 7 `mi.` images will render as blanks, and screen readers will announce filenames or nothing. Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the later `body{…100%}` and block user text scaling in iOS Safari/Mail.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype, which should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant meta tags.** The `charset` and `format-detection` metas are declared two to three times each.
- **Template leftovers.** The source still contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!---->`.
- **Responsive coverage.** The only mobile breakpoint is `max-width:460px`. Viewports between 461 and 639px get the desktop layout.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN].**

  - Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. This is an artifact of the relay, not evidence of failure. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` aligns with the image domains `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be confirmed**, because the source is truncated before the footer.

4. Consolidate the five identical `rlcdn.com` pixels into one, or confirm the repetition is intentional.
