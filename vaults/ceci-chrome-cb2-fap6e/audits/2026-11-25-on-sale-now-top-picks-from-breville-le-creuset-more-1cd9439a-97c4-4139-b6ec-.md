# On sale now: Top picks from Breville, Le Creuset & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-focused Crate & Barrel Black Friday send, and it is more targeted than the brand's recent blasts. The subject promises "Top picks from Breville, Le Creuset & more", and the hero delivers: "up to 40% off top kitchen brands" over an espresso machine. Below it, each product block gets its own price and CTA. That specificity is the email's strength.
- The email then loses focus. After about five kitchen blocks it drifts into a Thanksgiving table section, a "shop up to 50% off Black Friday deals" grid, a 60% clearance banner, a gift shop and gift guides. The top strip ("get it by Thanksgiving with extended store hours, we're open 8 am–8 pm") and the "New Deals Added! Up to 50% off Black Friday + Free Shipping" banner sit above the hero and push the kitchen offer down. This is the same kitchen-sink pattern as the 11/24 "deck your halls" send.
- I could only read the render at 113px wide, scaled down from 680px. I can judge layout and large headlines but not fine print, so I don't comment on small text. Overall it is solid. Its subject is more concrete than anything in the recent run of "NEW DEALS added" sends, but the length and the stacked offers dilute it.

## What's working

- The subject names real brands (Breville, Le Creuset), which is more concrete than the generic "up to 50% off" subjects from 11/24 and 11/25.
- The hero is a large espresso machine photo with "up to 40% off top kitchen brands" and one Shop Now button, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- Each kitchen block pairs one product photo with a specific deal, such as "$200 off select Breville espresso machines" and "up to $100 off Breville smart ovens". Specific dollar-off amounts beat blanket percentages.
- Every block has its own Shop Now button, so the email has a clear CTA rhythm.
- The email fits the season, with Thanksgiving delivery and store-hours messaging.

## What's weak

- Too many promos stack above the hero: the store-hours bar, the "New Deals Added!" banner and the free-shipping line. The kitchen offer is not the first thing seen.
- The email is very long. It runs from kitchen into tabletop, a generic Black Friday grid, clearance, a gift shop and gift guides, so it loses the kitchen thread the subject promised.
- Offers conflict: up to 40%, 50%, 60%, "up to $100 off" and "up to 30% off". A reader can't tell which deal matters most.
- It is the latest of roughly seven near-identical Black Friday sends in three days, including "A note just for you!" and "NEW ITEMS ADDED!" the same day. The "BLACK FRIDAY" sender label adds to the fatigue.
- Several blocks have tiny text and no visible reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email after the kitchen section.** End after the "more can't-miss kitchen deals" grid with one Shop All Kitchen Deals button. Move the gift, clearance and tabletop sections to a separate send. This single change would lift the email most.
- 2. **Drop the "New Deals Added!" banner and the store-hours bar from above the hero.** Put the Breville espresso hero first, and add a single line for the deadline and free shipping.
- 3. **Pick one headline offer.** Lead with "$200 off Breville espresso machines" and let the percentages support it.
- 4. **Add social proof** to the hero products, such as star ratings or "best seller" tags.
- 5. **Cap the cadence at one or two Black Friday sends per day**, and give each a different job.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$200 off Breville espresso machines — ends Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Breville & Le Creuset: up to 40% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus KitchenAid mixers up to 30% off and free shipping on kitchen deals.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by Thanksgiving: stand mixers, Dutch ovens and smart ovens, all marked down.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "On sale now: Top picks from Breville, Le Creuset & more!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The QA run scored a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main findings are about 60 images with no `alt` attribute, several third-party tracking pixels, and header checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) that could not be verified. The HTML provided is truncated, so the footer, link `href`s and preheader were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **105 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Five third-party pixels from LiveRamp.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…cfd8&n=1` through `&n=5` differ only in `n`. Five identical beacons per open is redundant.
- **A recipient identifier is shared with a third party.** The `s=` value on `rlcdn.com` starts with the same hash as `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` image URLs. This looks like the same per-recipient ID going to a third-party domain. It is a privacy exposure rather than a functional break.
- **More tracking pixels.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1d06d63b-1bdf-468b-8ce0-241a4c914ddb?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression trackers.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 60 images flagged for missing `alt` (WARN).**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` include `bbd0af48-6600-4b0c-a0fd-7abc6ce36d41.jpg`, `0df554c3-…jpg` and `61581a40-6032-48e1-a940-f247f9d7d768.gif`. They also include `m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`.
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, such as `560d850e7e517ede.png`, are also flagged.
  - If these are product or promo images inside links, the links will have no accessible name when images are blocked or read by a screen reader.
  - The pixels (`448796.gif`, `o.gif`, the `dv.` beacon) should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing is disabled.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the CSS, and the `*{… line-height:100%}` rule overrides line-height everywhere.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values.
  - A leftover template comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px) {img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px) {img{max-width:640px}}` overlap, so the later rule wins in the 768px+ range.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` were seen, but the source is truncated. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` parameters are resolved per-recipient values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN).** The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip it. This is unconfirmed, not a confirmed sender defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found (WARN).** RFC 8058 one-click support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be assessed for the `mail.crateandbarrel.com` sending domain. The relay is the likely cause.
- **Footer not verified.** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section, so CAN-SPAM body requirements could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 105 tracked links were skipped and the visible `href`s were truncated, so UTM parameters and landing pages could not be checked. The only confirmed parameters are the `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers on image and beacon URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images. Set `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
3. Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com` beacons to one. Review whether sharing the recipient hash with LiveRamp fits the privacy policy.
4. Clean up the markup: escape the `&` in `<title>`, remove duplicate meta tags, delete the template placeholder comment, and fix the overlapping `img` max-width media queries.
5. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers, bypassing the relay, to confirm List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. Resolve a sample of the 105 tracking links and check for redirect chains, 4xx/5xx responses, UTM presence and landing-page match. Also inspect the full, untruncated footer for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
