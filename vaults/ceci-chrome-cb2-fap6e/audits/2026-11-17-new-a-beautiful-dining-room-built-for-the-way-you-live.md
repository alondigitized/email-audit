# New! A beautiful dining room, built for the way you live
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, collection-by-collection dining room catalog. The subject sells a lifestyle ("built for the way you live"), but the real incentives are pushed into a small banner at the top and a kitchen-deals block at the bottom. The banner carries "ends tomorrow: last Double Rewards® this year!" and "Early Black Friday: up to 30% off", plus a ship-by-Thanksgiving note. The image is heavily downscaled, so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print.
- The body is a clear sequence of room scenes, each with a short headline and a "Shop [collection]" button: Sullivan, Portico, Blaine and Bilbury, then lighting and rugs. It's attractive and easy to scan, but it's about eight stacked sections before the "earn Double Rewards® on these top kitchen deals" block, which is where the actual savings are. Compared with the 2026-11-16 sends ("Up to 40% off faves from KitchenAid, Caraway & more + 2x Rewards!" and "ENDS TOMORROW: Shop quick-ship faves with 2x Rewards!"), the subject here has no offer or deadline. That makes it the weakest opener of the week, even though the body is one of the better-looking ones.
- Judgement: good editorial content with the wrong subject line and a buried offer. It will probably do fine on clicks from people who open, but it won't win the open.

## What's working

- The collection modules have a consistent, scannable pattern: lifestyle image, short headline, product thumbnails, then a single "Shop ___" button.
- The top banner combines a ship-by deadline with two incentives (Double Rewards ending tomorrow, up to 30% off Early Black Friday). The urgency is credible and the loyalty benefit is visible.
- The "new" framing on the dining room hero, "life happens in your dining room", plus a "Shop New Dining Furniture" button gives the email a clear primary CTA.
- The kitchen-deals block near the bottom is concrete. It has "up to 30% off" on cookware, espresso machines and cutlery, "they ship free!", and product-level pricing.
- I can see no broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps. It looks like a polished, on-brand Crate & Barrel send.

## What's weak

- The strongest offers sit in a small banner above the hero and in a block near the very bottom, so the eye lands on the lifestyle hero first.
- The email is very long. Dining, lighting, rugs, kitchen and a final Early Black Friday banner compete for attention, and it repeats the Early Black Friday message that has run for days.
- The subject has no offer, deadline or Rewards mention, even though the email's best hooks are "ends tomorrow" and "2x Rewards".
- The per-collection CTAs ("Shop Sullivan", "Shop Portico") lead to broad collections and don't show prices, which weakens the click motivation.
- The email ends on a new, loosely connected "It's here! up to 30% off Early Black Friday" banner, which feels like a second email attached to the first.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the offer into the hero. Add a strip or badge such as "Up to 30% off + 2x Rewards, ends tomorrow" directly over or under the dining room image, so the first screen carries the incentive.
- 2. Rewrite the subject to lead with the deadline and offer, and keep "new dining room" as secondary.
- 3. Cut the body to three or four modules, ordered by best seller or most-clicked collection, and drop the rugs and lighting modules into one combined "complete the room" row.
- 4. Show a price or "from $X" on each collection button area.
- 5. Move the kitchen-deals block out of this send, or make it a short "also on sale" strip, to keep the email focused.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "New! A beautiful dining room, built for the way you live"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard XHTML table layout with a responsive `@media (max-width:460px)` block and a valid `lang="en"`. The main confirmed issues are about 60 images flagged as missing alt text, no `List-Unsubscribe` headers captured, and a plain-text part that is 70.9% URLs. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and unsubscribe checks are limited to the QA data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **110 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page health are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels fire per open.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…cfd8&n=1` through `n=5` come from rlcdn.com, a third-party data-onboarding domain. Five calls to one endpoint add load and an extra third-party request to every open.
- **A recipient identifier is passed to that third party.** The `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels begins with the same hash as `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869…` on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. The strings are truncated in the QA output, so a full match is not confirmed. If they are the same ID, one subscriber identifier goes to an external domain.
- **Other tracking beacons:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open pixel) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/701bef66-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (open and campaign tracking).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 60 `<img>` elements are flagged for missing alt text.** Content images are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif`, for example `6e5bbd3c-5066-44d5-ba13-c00d5ec2f8e7.jpg`, `d32c317c-8aa4-414b-9e58-60ff14b5ca69.gif` and `971aa10f-db11-4b79-b1d6-d95ad4507a7c.jpg`.
  - The scene7 hero `2026%5F0811%5FCB%5FDining%5FRugs%5FLighting%5FHero` is also flagged.
  - When images are blocked, or in a screen reader, the hero and product content has no text equivalent.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from `alt=""`, so some of these may be intentional.
- **Product recommendation images lack alt text.** The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png` and five others) are flagged. If they are real product tiles, they need descriptive alt text.
- **Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.** The `o.gif`, `448796.gif` ×5 and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…` images are flagged. Treat these as hygiene rather than defects.
- **The viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom.** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables user zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Markup hygiene.**
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times, first as `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and then again as separate `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags. The values are redundant, not conflicting.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - A template placeholder comment remains in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are both present and consistent (utf-8).

## 3b. Plain-Text Part
`[WARN] Plain-text is >70% URLs: 21810/30771 chars`. This is 70.9% URL content, driven by long tracking URLs. A part this heavy in links is a spam-filter signal, and it reads poorly in plain-text clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were found in the visible source or QA output. The per-recipient URL parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`) are populated. The truncated HTML leaves the body and footer unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. That is unconfirmed rather than proven absent.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, but alignment can't be confirmed.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are unverified.** They fall in the truncated portion of the source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. No UTM parameters are visible in the available source, and all 110 click-tracked links were skipped. Landing-page alignment with the dining-room subject is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content and product images, and `alt=""` to tracking pixels and decorative images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Re-run the check against the raw `.eml` (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
4. Resolve a sample of the 110 tracking links to verify destinations, redirect hops and UTM carry-through.
5. Shorten or reduce the URLs in the plain-text part to bring it under the 70% URL ratio.
6. Review the five repeated rlcdn pixels and confirm that the `s=` hash and the `mi_u` value are different identifiers.
7. Clean up the markup: escape `&amp;` in `<title>`, drop the duplicate `format-detection` tags and remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
