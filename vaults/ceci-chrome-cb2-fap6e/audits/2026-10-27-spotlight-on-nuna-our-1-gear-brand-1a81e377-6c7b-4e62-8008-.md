# Spotlight on Nuna: our #1 gear brand →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a brand-spotlight send for Nuna, the strollers and car seats, and it is built like a mini catalog. A hero reads "all gear ships free, new & bestselling from Nuna" with a Shop Now button. Below it are stacked blocks for strollers, convertible car seats and at-home essentials, each with its own button. The structure is clear and the product photography is strong.
- It then drifts. A cozy winter bedding block, an "up to 60% off clearance" block, a "we love these for you" product grid, a registry-perk card, an app promo, a Design Desk appointment block and a footer nav follow. The Nuna story is complete by about the halfway point. After that the email becomes a generic Crate & Barrel Kids catch-all. At roughly 10,000px tall, it is a long scroll for one theme.
- Compared with the 10/27 toys send ("ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on toys $99+"), this one has no urgency and no price hook. It leans on brand prestige instead. That works for a parent already researching gear, but it will not move anyone who is not. The free-shipping bar at the top ("free shipping on toys $99+") also belongs to a different campaign and competes with the Nuna message.

## What's working

- The hero is a clean stroller-and-car-seat photo with one Shop Now button, so the Nuna theme is clear immediately.
- Each section gets its own specific CTA ("Shop Nuna Strollers", "Shop Nuna Car Seats", "Shop Nuna Home Essentials"), which makes the email easy to navigate.
- Product tiles carry useful decision cues such as "Ships free!", "New Arrival" and a lightweight-weight callout.
- A star rating and a short customer quote add some social proof in the stroller block.
- The send ties to the season with a cozy winter edit and holiday bedding, and nothing looks broken.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the Nuna sections is time-bound or discounted, so there is no reason to click today.
- The email is very long, and the second half dilutes the spotlight with clearance, an app download, an appointment pitch and a recommendations grid.
- The top free-shipping bar mentions toys, which has nothing to do with strollers and car seats and sends a mixed signal.
- The "up to 60% off clearance" block is the strongest offer in the email, but it sits mid-scroll and is unrelated to Nuna.
- The "our #1 gear brand" claim is never backed up with a number or a reason.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the Nuna sections and the single clearance block. Move the app, appointment and recommendation modules out of this send.
- 2. Put one concrete hook in the hero, such as a free-shipping threshold on gear, a registry bonus, or "ships free."
- 3. Make the top bar match the theme, for example "Free shipping on Nuna gear."
- 4. Back up the "#1 brand" claim with a stat, a best-seller badge or review counts.
- 5. Lead with the best-selling stroller and a price, rather than a general brand hero.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lightweight strollers, car seats & more. Most ship free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is a standard table-based ESP template (Mapp/MI-style) with MSO and dark-mode meta handling. The QA data shows widespread missing `alt` attributes, and the auth and unsubscribe headers could not be checked. The header warnings are probably relay artifacts. The automated pass rate was 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 81 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not confirmed good.
- Third-party pixels are present: five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp). Their `s=` value is the same hashed identifier that appears as `mi_u=` in the first-party URLs.
- First-party tracking images are also present:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ef032a69-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - Six repeats of `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`
- All of these pixels lack `alt` attributes.
- Template leftover in `<head>`: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (also misspelled). No font import is present, but the placeholder was shipped.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA).** Over 40 images are affected, and the QA output was cut off, so the real count is likely higher.
  - Content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` have no alt. Examples are `6cda691d-…jpg`, `3e665a73-…jpg` and `46d5912c-…jpg`. If these are product or hero images, images-off clients and screen readers get no content.
  - `bbf6f9d4-…png` and the animated `e54a93c8-…gif` also have no alt.
  - Scene7 assets `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` and `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif` have no alt. A spacer should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject.
- Duplicate or conflicting `<meta name="format-detection">` tags are declared three times with different values. The first sets `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately. This is harmless but redundant.
- Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present, which is also redundant.
- The global `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` rules are blunt resets. The `*` selector is overridden inconsistently by later classes and could cause line-height issues in some clients. This is not confirmed as a defect from the truncated source.
- The `@media (min-width:768px) img{max-width:600px}` and `@media (min-width:640px) img{max-width:640px}` rules overlap. At 768px and above, the later 640px rule wins by source order, so the 600px rule is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are rendered, resolved values, not placeholders. Only the truncated HTML was available, so tokens later in the body can't be ruled out.

- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (QA WARN).** The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is also required for bulk senders under those rules.
- **Authentication-Results not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the From, tracking and image domains share the `crateandbarrel.com` parent. Alignment can't be verified here.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels. Start with the `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/…/m/1/*.jpg` set.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against raw headers from the original message, not the relay copy. Also check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`) and make it descriptive.
5. Delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` placeholder and the duplicate meta tags.
6. Consider reducing the five repeated `sr.rlcdn.com` pixel calls to one, since they add third-party requests and extra tracking surface.
7. Improve the audit pipeline by following redirects on tracking links (HEAD/GET with a limit) so UTMs and destination status can be checked. Also capture the full raw headers, not the AgentMail relay copy.
