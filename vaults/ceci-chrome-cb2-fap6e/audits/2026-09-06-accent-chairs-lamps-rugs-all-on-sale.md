The subject line matches: "Accent chairs. Lamps. Rugs. - The countdown timer (03:17:20:31) is a nice urgency touch and a first for this stretch — none of the prior beach-at-dusk or floral-armchair sends had a visible clock. - Where it falls down is depth: every tile shows a lifestyle shot with no price, no specific product, and no proof this isn't the same "up to 50% off" event running for two weeks straight. It's a solid structural iteration on a fatigued campaign, not a fresh one — worth a mid-pack score.

## What's working

- Subject line names three concrete categories (accent chairs, lamps, rugs) instead of a generic "up to X% off" — a real change from the pattern in this stretch.
- Countdown timer (03:17:20:31) adds credible, visible urgency not seen in prior CB2 sends this stretch.
- Category grid (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Outdoor, Entertaining) gives shoppers a clear self-select path instead of one blanket CTA.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.
- Double Rewards module (20% back through Sept 9) is a distinct, visible secondary offer with its own CTA.

## What's weak

- No pricing or specific product shown anywhere — every tile is a lifestyle shot with a category label, zero SKU-level detail.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is still the only concrete number in the hero, same offer language CB2 has run repeatedly this stretch (09-02, 09-03, 09-05).
- Extended holiday hours banner at the very top competes with the countdown timer for the eye's first stop — busy above the fold.
- 800+ New Arrivals block ("PREPARE TO WANT IT ALL") dilutes the sale focus at the bottom instead of reinforcing urgency to close.
- Category tiles are visually similar in tone (all warm neutral/beige) — nothing distinguishes rugs from decor from entertaining at a glance.

- 1. Put a price or percentage on at least the top 2-3 tiles (e.g., "Rugs from $X" or "Up to 40% off lighting") — the category grid earns clicks with specifics, not just labels.
- 2. Move the countdown timer above the extended-hours banner, or drop the hours banner from this send — it's not sale-relevant and it's the first thing the eye hits.
- 3. Cut or relocate the "800+ New Arrivals" block — it works against the sale-ending urgency the countdown timer just built.
