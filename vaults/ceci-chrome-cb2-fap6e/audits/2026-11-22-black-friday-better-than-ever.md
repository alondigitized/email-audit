# BLACK FRIDAY, BETTER THAN EVER
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a plain-text-style "note from Nicole" send. The render has no hero image, no product, no button and no offer graphic. The whole pitch is one paragraph of body copy saying "biggest sale of the year" and "up to 60% off." Blue inline links carry the only calls to action.
- It reads as personal and different from CB2's recent image-heavy sends. That difference is its only edge. Nov 21's "UP TO 60% OFF BACK-IN-STOCK STYLES" put "60%" in giant type over a velvet chair, and this send has no equivalent. A subscriber who has seen five Black Friday emails in four days gets no new reason to click. The sale is at its biggest, yet this email looks like the smallest one.
- Verdict: the voice is charming but the layout wastes the day the sale launches. It is a weak commercial send.

## What's working

- The first-person sign-off ("Nicole from the CB2 Team") feels human and stands out from the earlier image-heavy sends.
- "Up to 60% off" appears in the first paragraph, so the offer is stated plainly.
- The free design appointment adds a service angle the other sends lack.
- The nav and footer category links are clean and give several ways into the site.
- The footer states the sale terms and end date (Dec 1, 2026) clearly, which keeps the offer honest.

## What's weak

- There is no hero image, no product and no visual. For a furniture brand this is a big miss.
- There are no buttons. The CTAs are small blue underlined links, and "so much more" is a vague link.
- The 60% offer is buried in body text. Nothing makes the eye land on it.
- There is no urgency. "Before the sale ends" gives no date in the body, and the Dec 1 end date sits in the fine print.
- The footer has no member or loyalty benefit and no named product.
- The copy ("Fabulous news," "most covetable pieces") is generic. It could be sent for any sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a full-width hero with "UP TO 60% OFF" in large type and a single "SHOP THE SALE" button. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Put the end date ("Ends Dec 1") in the hero or directly under it.
- 3. Replace the inline links with 3 to 4 product or category tiles with prices.
- 4. Make the design appointment a secondary button, such as "Book a free design session."
- 5. Keep Nicole's note, but cut it to two lines under the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off, our biggest sale ever`
- **Subject Alt B:** `A note from Nicole: 60% off is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, lighting and decor. Ends Dec 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus book a free design appointment before spots fill.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering and personalization defect: an empty name in the greeting ("Hi ,"). Several compliance and authentication checks can't be confirmed because the relay did not capture the headers. The HTML also embeds third-party tracking pixels carrying a hashed email identifier.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **43 tracking/click-redirect links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as tracking domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels are embedded.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…cfd8&n=1` through `&n=5` differ only in `n`. Repeating the same pixel five times is redundant.
- **A first-party pixel carries the same identifier.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…cfd8` uses the same 64-hex value (SHA-256 length) as the `s=` parameter on the rlcdn pixels. The same recipient-level identifier goes to both a third-party ad-data domain and a first-party domain.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Two content images lack alt text**, per the QA check:
  - `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/18/5ac6898a-92dc-4f7a-a6b4-53785b8e43b8.gif`
- **Seven tracking pixels lack an `alt` attribute:** the five `448796.gif` pixels, `o.gif`, and similar. Their effect on accessibility is minor, but `alt=""` would be correct for decorative or tracking images.
- **One image is hosted on a different brand's domain.** `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` serves an image in a `cb2.com`-sent email. This is a sibling-brand domain, so it is not necessarily a fault, but it is an inconsistent sending and asset domain.
- The HTML preamble has the expected mobile and Outlook handling: viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, VML namespaces, and `@media (max-width:460px)` rules. The source is truncated, so the body markup could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot.** The greeting renders as "Hi ," because the first-name field is blank. This is a confirmed broken-experience defect. The send has no fallback value (for example "Hi there,") or conditional suppression for recipients without a first name.
- No unrendered merge tokens such as `%%name%%` or `{{…}}` were reported by QA. The truncated source could not be checked beyond that.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven violation. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe would not be met.
- **Authentication-Results was not found**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain, which is the usual setup for ESP sending.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.** The HTML source was truncated before the footer.
- **The hashed-email pixels may need disclosure.** Sharing a hashed recipient email with a third-party data broker (`rlcdn.com`) may trigger privacy-policy and opt-out obligations, for example CCPA "sharing" and GDPR for EU recipients. This depends on CB2's disclosures and cannot be confirmed from the email.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified.** All 43 links were tracking redirects that QA skipped, and the visible source was truncated before any destination URLs.
- **The `<title>` doesn't match the subject.** The `<title>` is "Time to make that mood board a reality", while the subject is "BLACK FRIDAY, BETTER THAN EVER". This suggests a reused template, and the title appears in some web-view and preview contexts.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting.** Add a fallback for blank first names, for example `Hi there,`, or drop the name when it is empty.
2. **Add alt text.** Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` and `image.mail.cb2.com`. Add `alt=""` to the pixel images.
3. **Remove the duplicate pixels.** Cut the five `rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels to one. Review whether sending the hashed email (`s=` / `mi_u=`) to a third party is covered by CB2's privacy disclosures.
4. **Check the raw headers.** Pull the original `.eml` from the receiving mailbox, not through the relay, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results in `Authentication-Results`.
5. **Resolve the tracking links.** Follow a sample of the 43 redirects manually or with a redirect-following probe. Confirm the final landing URLs and their UTM parameters, and that the Black Friday offer matches the landing page.
6. **Align the `<title>`** with the current campaign subject.
7. **Fix QA coverage.** Make the relay preserve headers and raise the truncation limit so the footer unsubscribe link and address can be checked.
