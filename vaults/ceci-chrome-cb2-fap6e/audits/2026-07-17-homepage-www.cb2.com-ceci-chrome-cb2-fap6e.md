# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- CB2 opens with exactly the energy I come here for — dark, moody, editorial. The "NEW TO COVET" hero is doing its job aesthetically, but the very first thing I actually see is an email-capture modal slamming down in front of everything. The brand knows its lane (contemporary, a little bit menacing, lots of black-and-velvet vibes), and the new-arrivals push is smart — but the interstitial is doing its best to kill the vibe.

## What's working

- **"NEW TO COVET" hero headline** — punchy, editorial, and it's speaking my language without being cutesy about it
- **Moody hero imagery** — rich tones, sculptural form, dark background; this is the CB2 I want to see on a phone screen
- **"CUSTOM IS THE ULTIMATE LUXURY" section** — "GET FREE SWATCHES" is a genuinely useful CTA for someone seriously considering a sofa purchase, not just browsing
- **"BEST SELLERS: POPULAR FOR A REASON" rail** — I can see furniture pieces loading in at the bottom; gives me something concrete to scroll toward
- **Nav structure** — Furniture, Rugs, Decor, Sale all visible and one tap away; no hunting

## What's weak

- **Forced modal on landing** — I haven't even seen the homepage yet and there's already a popup blocking everything. Instant friction. I almost always X out of these reflexively
- **Asterisk on the 15% offer** — "FULL-PRICE ITEMS*" with an asterisk and tiny fine-print below the form fields tells me this discount is probably riddled with exclusions; kills the excitement
- **No trust signals visible** — no review counts, no free-returns callout, nothing that makes me feel safe tapping my card
- **Hero subtext is too small to read on mobile** — whatever the supporting copy under "NEW TO COVET" says, I can't read it at all; wasted real estate
- **No time-bound urgency** — the new-arrivals push has no "just dropped" timestamp or deadline; it could have been up for months

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by 10–15 seconds or trigger on exit-intent** — let me actually see the homepage before you ask for my email; right now you're pitching me before I've even walked in the door
- **Make the 15% offer specific and asterisk-free in the headline** — swap "FULL-PRICE ITEMS*" for "sitewide" or state the main exclusion clearly ("excludes sale"); dishonest framing erodes trust
- **Add a "Just Dropped" timestamp or "New this week" badge** to the new arrivals CTA — even a static "Added July 2026" gives me a reason to click now rather than later
- **Surface one trust signal above the fold** — a single line like "Free shipping over $X · Free returns" in the top bar would push me closer to tapping a product
