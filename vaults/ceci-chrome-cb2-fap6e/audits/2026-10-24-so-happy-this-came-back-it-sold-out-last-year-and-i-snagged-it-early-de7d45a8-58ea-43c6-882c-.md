# “So happy this came back! It sold out last year and I snagged it early”
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- The subject is a customer-quote hook ("sold out last year and I snagged it early"), but the body doesn't pay it off. The hero is a Thanksgiving dining room event, "up to 30% off," with the line "these sell out every year." Below it is a numbered list of about 15 Christmas and holiday items: a hemlock garland, Joan Anderson plates, a monochrome candle, a wooden advent calendar, snowflake pretzels. Each has its own "Shop" button. Which item the subject's quote refers to is unclear, and the hero never answers it.
- The sell-out scarcity angle is credible and fits the season. As a layout, though, it's a long catalog scroll with a mixed message. The hero sells Thanksgiving dining, the numbered list sells Christmas, and further down the email returns to dining tables and accent chairs "up to 30% off." A reader has to work out which offer applies to which products. The image is heavily downscaled, so I'm judging structure and copy headlines, not fine print.
- Judgement: a decent, on-brand seasonal send held back by a muddled offer hierarchy and a subject that promises one hero product and delivers fifteen. It's similar to the 2026-10-23 "Thanksgiving Dining Room Event" send, which scored higher because it had a single lead and a single button.

## What's working

- The "these sell out every year" hero line gives credible, non-gimmicky scarcity.
- Numbered items, each with its own product image and a "Shop [item]" button, make this easy to scan and deep-link.
- The photography is warm and seasonal, and the layout shows no broken images or overlap.
- The sender and brand voice are consistent with Crate & Barrel's usual editorial tone.
- The footer is complete: app download, design services, category nav and social links.

## What's weak

- The subject quote is never tied to a specific product in the render, so the curiosity gap goes unresolved.
- The hero offer (Thanksgiving dining, up to 30% off) doesn't match the Christmas items that fill the body.
- The "up to 30% off" offer is repeated lower down for chairs and tables, which dilutes the lead.
- At about 12,000px tall, the email is very long, with at least four stacked secondary blocks after the product list (shop-link grid, dining feature, accent chairs, "we love these for you").
- The cadence is heavy: this is another seasonal promo right after the 2026-10-23 sends ("Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event!", "Our wine glasses all ship free →").

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero product, the one the quote is about, and put it, its price and the testimonial above the fold. Move the other items into a "more that sell out" strip.
- 2. Align the hero and body: either a Thanksgiving-dining email or a Christmas-bestsellers email, not both.
- 3. Cut the list to 6–8 items and drop at least two of the lower promo blocks.
- 4. State the offer on the product buttons (for example "Shop Garland — 20% off") so the discount isn't only in the hero.
- 5. Put a real deadline on the scarcity claim ("while supplies last through Sunday").

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 30% off dining for Thanksgiving. Shop before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink–driven (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) XHTML table layout with 74 redirect-wrapped links. The main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The header-based compliance warnings can't be confirmed from this capture, and the HTML source is truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking links unverified:** 74 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` (7 images) and `o.gif?mi_u=…` carry a recipient token. This is expected for dynamic content, but the images are not cacheable or shareable.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1..5` requests (LiveRamp) carry the same recipient hash. They are a privacy and deliverability consideration, since the five pixels are separate requests. They are also unlisted in the visible source.
- **Truncated source:** only part of the link set could be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:** about 30 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` have no `alt`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `56be7a63-ec91-4fed-b133-e9563a2fbb5f.jpg`, and animated `504f24e6-…gif` and `8b714568-…gif`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these will be read as filenames or skipped. If they carry product, offer, or CTA content, that content is lost.
- **Missing alt, dynamic and tracking images:** the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/5d8508ee-…`, `o.gif`, and the `rlcdn` pixels also lack alt. The pixels should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The `rp/*.png` images appear to be dynamic content and need real alt text. The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from `alt=""`.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 failure.
- **Malformed XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand (should be `&amp;`) under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Duplicate and leftover markup:** `<meta charset>` is duplicated, `format-detection` is declared three times, and there's a template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. None of these affect rendering.
- **Global overrides:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` apply to every element. `*{line-height:100%}` can make wrapped text tight in clients that ignore the more specific overrides. No client-specific rendering failure is confirmed.
