# Fresh off a new season
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-sink send. It stacks three campaigns: a free-shipping banner on top, a "Just In / Already Trending" new-arrivals story, and a Long Weekend Sale block (up to 60% off) near the bottom. The photography is moody and cohesive, with a velvet chair, lacquered console, tufted leather sofa and fluted bed. But the eye has no single place to land. The best offer, up to 60% off, sits roughly two-thirds of the way down. The shipping banner at the top is the first thing anyone sees.
- It also repeats itself. Free shipping on $99+ was the hero of "So we're doing a little thing" yesterday. The Long Weekend Sale was the whole point of "Looking for weekend plans? Shop this sale." on 10/7. The "pieces" tiles echo the "THIS BEST SELLING BED" fluted-bed and Davison content from 10/8. A subscriber who has been getting CB2 all week will see recycled creative and the same offers.
- Verdict: good-looking and well branded, but unfocused and stale. The subject is vague and the offer hierarchy is backwards. I'd call it middling.

## What's working

- The photography is rich and consistent, and it reads as premium CB2.
- The free-shipping banner at the top is clear, with a visible "Shop Now" button.
- Named designers on the product tiles (Gianfranco Frattini, Mermelada Estudio, Studio ANANSI) give a design-led, editorial feel.
- The "More for You" product grid shows real prices, including markdowns like $1,099 → $879, so shoppers get concrete price points.
- The footer cross-sells the app, 10% back in rewards, and a registry, which gives loyalty and utility hooks.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing messages: shipping, new arrivals, the sale, best sellers and the app. None of them clearly leads.
- The strongest offer, up to 60% off, is buried deep in the scroll.
- The big new-arrivals tiles have no visible individual CTAs or prices, only one "Shop New Arrivals" button, so the click path is vague.
- The offers are repeats from the last few days: free shipping on 10/9 and the Long Weekend Sale on 10/7.
- The overlay text on dark photos looks thin and low-contrast in places, especially the "Shipping, On Us" and "Just In" heroes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero. Lead with up to 60% off, or merge the sale with free shipping in a single banner. Move the sale to the top and drop the third campaign.
- 2. Give the product tiles a price or "from $X" plus a specific CTA such as "Shop the Frattini chair". Don't use a generic "Shop New Arrivals".
- 3. Cut the number of tiles. Four or five hero pieces is enough, so the email is shorter and the sale doesn't get lost.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the sale ("Ends Monday") to make the urgency credible.
- 5. Space out the repeated offers across sends, or change the creative when they recur.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New arrivals, plus 60% off the Long Weekend Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Frattini chair, the lacquered console and more just landed. Free shipping over $99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday. New leather, velvet and wood pieces are in.`

- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon) tracking pixels load over plain `http://`. Clients and proxies may block them, which would silently drop retargeting and cookie-sync calls.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
