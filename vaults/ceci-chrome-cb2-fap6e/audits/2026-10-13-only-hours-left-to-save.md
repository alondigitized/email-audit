# ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the same kitchen-sink CB2 send we've seen all week, now with a countdown timer on top. The "SALE ENDS IN 01 : 15 : 19 : 28" clock carries the urgency. Below it, the hero is the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off. The subject, "ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE", doesn't match that. The timer reads over a day, and the 60%-off Long Weekend Sale is the block labelled "FINAL HOURS". The subject points at the wrong sale, much as "This sale only happens twice a year" did on 10/11.
- The body stacks upholstery, the Long Weekend Sale, free shipping on $99, a $1,000 app sweepstakes, lighting and a product grid. The hero is the weaker offer, and the bigger 60% offer sits mid-scroll. Each block has its own CTA, so the eye has no single place to land. The photography is strong and the brand voice is consistent, but the send is cluttered.
- Verdict: a competent, on-brand send. It is diluted by too many offers and a subject that doesn't line up with the hero. It also repeats the "2 SALES END SOON" and "2 DEALS & ONLY DAYS LEFT" beats from 10/10 and 10/12, so the urgency language is wearing thin.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the very top gives credible, visible urgency.
- The hero is large and clean, with "UP TO 25% OFF" in big type and a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- The upholstery sub-categories (sectionals, sofas, accent chairs, benches) are shown in full-width imagery with clear labels.
- The "Final hours" 60% off Long Weekend Sale block is a strong, specific offer.
- The "More for you" product grid shows named products with prices, including a strikethrough sale price on the Cairo credenza.

## What's weak

- The subject says "hours left", but the timer shows more than a day, and the hero offer isn't the one with "final hours" language.
- There are too many competing campaigns: upholstery, the Long Weekend Sale, shipping, the app sweepstakes and lighting. Nothing is clearly primary.
- The 60% off offer is buried below the 25% hero.
- The CTAs are generic: "Shop the sale", "Shop now", "Get started".
- The footer legal copy is dense and tiny, with multiple exclusion blocks. That undercuts how honest the offers feel.
- The all-caps subject looks shouty. The "CB2 SALE" sender name is also less personal than plain "CB2".

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the one offer that is actually ending, and make the subject, timer and hero match. If the Long Weekend Sale ends first, put its 60% off at the top.
- 2. Cut the send to two offers at most. Move the app sweepstakes and lighting to another send.
- 3. Use specific CTAs, such as "Shop sofas up to 25% off".
- 4. Shorten and simplify the legal copy, or link to terms.
- 5. Vary the urgency language so it doesn't repeat the 10/10 and 10/12 sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sofas up to 25% off, ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Long Weekend Sale: up to 60% off, plus free shipping on $99+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sectionals, sofas and accent chairs, up to 25% off during the Upholstery Event.`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "ONLY HOURS LEFT TO SAVE"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads about 11 insecure `http://` third-party ad-sync pixels and has many images with no alt text. The QA pass rate was 43%. Most compliance and continuity checks couldn't be completed because of the relay and the truncated HTML.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 72 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect chain or status was checked. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`, are embedded as images.
  - They are plain HTTP, so clients that block mixed content will drop them, and the tracking will be incomplete.
  - Eleven extra third-party requests per open add latency and privacy exposure.
- **First-party open pixels** are on HTTPS and look intentional: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/53b7f803-…?mi_cid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (9):** all on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a56a45bd-…jpg`, `3fdf15ba-…jpg`, `55c86a33-…jpg`, `f6a602ed-…jpg`, `71bbd2a7-…jpg`
  - `1aa79a45-…jpg`, `cbac302a-…jpg`, `acb86340-…jpg`, `8e957a75-…gif`
  - With images off or a screen reader, these blocks are blank or announced by filename.
- **Missing alt text, tracking pixels:** the dotomi, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels have no alt. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is the correct value for decorative images.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` also blocks text resizing in iOS clients.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in `<head>`. It has no functional impact.
- **Not assessable:** the source is truncated, so the body markup, the MSO/VML fallbacks and any HTML size or Gmail-clipping risk are unchecked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the delivered HTML. The `%%…%%` syntax and the `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/` path suggest Salesforce Marketing Cloud, where this is an unresolved AMPscript or personalization string variable.
  - It likely belongs to a product-reviews or ratings block.
  - It was not rendered, so the data source for that variable is empty or the send-time resolution failed.
  - It may show as visible text, or break a reviews module or a link containing it.
- **Not confirmed:** the truncated HTML doesn't show where the token sits, so I can't say whether it's visible text or inside an attribute or URL.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both reported not found. QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so they are unconfirmed rather than confirmed missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be verified. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain, which is normal for ESP sending.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.
- No confirmed violations.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. All 72 links are click-wrapped and were not resolved, so UTM parameters, landing-page match and the "hours left" offer on the destination can't be checked. No issues confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (high).** Check the SKU-list data extension or variable feeding the reviews block. Add a fallback that suppresses the block when the value is empty, and add a pre-send check for leftover `%%…%%` strings.
2. **Add alt text to the 9 content images (high).** Use descriptive text for the promo and product images. Use `alt=""` for pixels.
3. **Remove or move the dotomi pixels to HTTPS (medium).** If the cookie-sync is needed, serve it over HTTPS. Otherwise drop the 11 pixels to cut requests and privacy exposure.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` (medium)** to allow zoom.
5. **Verify headers on a direct-to-inbox copy (medium).** Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be hiding them.
