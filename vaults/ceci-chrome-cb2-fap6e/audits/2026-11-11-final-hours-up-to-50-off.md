# FINAL HOURS: UP TO 50% OFF
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest CB2 send of the week, and it has the most credible urgency. The "SALE ENDS IN 00 : 15 : 18 : 49" countdown matches the "ENDS TONIGHT" hero over the bedroom photo, and the subject says "FINAL HOURS". Earlier sends this week, such as the Nov 9 "DAYS LEFT TO SAVE + Double Rewards", had a countdown too. This one is the first where the deadline is hours away, not days.
- The body is a stack of six full-bleed product photos under "CAN'T BELIEVE THESE DEALS". The products are the Ambroise sofa, Davison dining table, Aldine sofa, Roza rug, Delphine pendant and Curvo sofa. Each has a "SHOP NOW" link and an "ON SALE" tag. The email ends with a black "SHOP THE LONG WEEKEND SALE" button. The gap is that no product shows a price or a percent off. The "up to 50%" claim never gets backed by a single number.
- After that, the email loses focus. Interior Design Services, Double Rewards, the app download and the credit-card block all follow the sale. That adds roughly a third of the scroll after the primary CTA. Double Rewards also ends Nov 12 while the sale ends tonight, which dilutes the "final hours" message. Overall it is competent and urgent but generic: the offer is stated once and never proven.

## What's working

- The countdown timer and "ENDS TONIGHT" give honest, specific urgency that matches the subject.
- The hero is clear: "LONG WEEKEND SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" with four category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors).
- The six product tiles are large, well photographed and consistently styled. Each has an "ON SALE" tag and a named designer, which gives it a premium feel.
- The black "SHOP THE LONG WEEKEND SALE" button closes the deal section clearly.
- I saw no broken images or layout bugs in the render.

## What's weak

- No product tile shows a sale price, original price or percent off. "ON SALE" is a label, not an offer.
- The "SHOP NOW" links are small, low-contrast text sitting on photos. They are easy to miss, and nothing signals they are buttons.
- After the main CTA, the email adds an interior design block, a Double Rewards block, an app download block and a card block. Four more asks compete with the sale on its last day.
- The header says "SALE ENDS IN" but the Double Rewards block says "Now through Nov 17th, 2026". The two deadlines conflict, which blurs the urgency.
- It is the 5th or 6th near-identical Long Weekend Sale send in a week. The "FINAL HOURS" framing is credible, but the creative is not new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real price or percent on each tile, for example "$1,299 · was $1,799", so "up to 50% off" is backed by numbers.
- 2. Turn the "SHOP NOW" links into solid buttons with contrast against the photos.
- 3. Cut everything after the main CTA down to one line of rewards copy. Move the app, design services and card blocks to non-sale days.
- 4. Make the dates consistent. Say "Rewards continue through Nov 17" separately, or remove it from this send.
- 5. Add a second timer-led CTA near the top, such as "Shop what's left", with a best-seller or low-stock cue.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional layout with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 418px. The automated QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The confirmed problems are missing image alt text, insecure `http://` tracking pixels, and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Header-based checks could not be verified, and the HTML source was truncated before the body.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 69 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destinations, redirect health, and link-to-landing alignment are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 images load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed content or proxy images over HTTPS may drop them, so those conversion and cookie-sync calls can fail silently.
- **Third-party cookie-sync:** The 10 `cookieredir/pubN.php` calls are a Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) cookie-sync chain. They add 11 external requests and tie the email's tracking to third-party ad infrastructure.
- **Own-domain pixels (OK):** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3be60467-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` use HTTPS.
- **Double slash in font URLs:** `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2` and three similar `@font-face` URLs contain `//`. This is harmless if the server normalizes it, but unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 17 images served from `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute. Examples are `a435fd5c-71ce-493b-bf15-92f6369ab737.jpg`, `8ea48d52-a0fb-4c42-8531-a8901ce93ce2.jpg`, and `9685411b-425d-4865-a92f-a520df64bb18.jpg`. If these carry the sale content, it is invisible with images off and to screen readers.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** The Dotomi pixels, `o.gif`, and `dv.cb2.com/o/…` also have no `alt`. These should use `alt=""` to mark them decorative.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` rule and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text-size adjustment in WebKit.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is an unfilled, misspelled placeholder. It is cosmetic only.
- **No issues found with:**
  - `lang="en"` is present.
  - A `<title>` is present.
  - The charset meta is present.
  - Outlook (MSO) fallbacks are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixels carry per-recipient parameters (`mi_u=`, `mi_mid=`) that rendered. The body was truncated, so tokens further down could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Both are absent. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing them, so this is **unverified, not confirmed non-compliant**.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** Absent, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown. This is likely the same relay artifact. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`, and image hosts are on `image.mail.cb2.com`, which is consistent with a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Body footer:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unable to assess. The 69 links were skipped and the truncated source shows no `href`s, so UTM parameters, redirect chains, and landing page alignment with the "FINAL HOURS: UP TO 50% OFF" subject are not evidenced. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values on the pixels suggest the sender is Movable Ink, with redirect-based click tracking.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 17 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://` or drop them. Consider whether the 10-call `cookieredir` chain is needed.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
4. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT…]` placeholder comment, and fix the `//` in the font URLs.
5. For the audit tooling, capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message rather than the relay copy. Resolve one sample of the tracking redirects to check UTMs and the landing page. Pass the full HTML so the footer and token checks aren't truncated.
