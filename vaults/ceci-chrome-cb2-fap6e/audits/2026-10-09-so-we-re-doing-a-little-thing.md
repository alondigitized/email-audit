# So we’re doing a little thing
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear free-shipping send under a vague subject. The hero reads "LIMITED TIME ONLY / SHIPPING, ON US / ORDERS OF $99 OR MORE" with a "SHOP NOW" button. The offer and the CTA are both easy to read. The photography is warm and cohesive: a silver bowl tied with a velvet ribbon, then candlelit decor and a cream faux-fur throw.
- The body below the hero is weaker. The Decor and Pillows & Throws tiles are pretty, but the offset collage gives no product, price, or reason to click. Then two more promos stack underneath: "The Long Weekend Sale, up to 60% off" and the "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off." That is three offers in one email, and the free-shipping hero gets diluted. A subscriber has seen the long-weekend sale and the upholstery event several times this week (10/3, 10/4, 10/6, 10/7), so the bottom half reads as recycled.
- The subject "So we're doing a little thing" is coy and says nothing about the offer. The hero is good, but the inbox view gives the reader no reason to open. Judgement: solid execution, undermined by a cute subject and a cluttered offer stack.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous. "SHIPPING, ON US" in large light type, with the $99 threshold and a white "SHOP NOW" button, lands the offer immediately.
- The photography is cohesive and on-brand, with warm browns, candlelight, and velvet. It is consistent with the 10/8 "Merriment is upon us" holiday send.
- The "LIMITED TIME ONLY" kicker adds urgency above the headline.
- The footer has useful loyalty hooks: 10% back in Rewards Dollars, 15% off for signing up for texts, and the app download.
- No broken images or overlapping layout are visible, and the header nav is clean.

## What's weak

- The subject, "So we're doing a little thing," hides the one concrete offer, free shipping at $99. The subject also doesn't appear in the hero copy.
- Three promos compete: free shipping, up to 60% off, and up to 25% off upholstery. There is no single clear hierarchy after the hero.
- The Decor / Dining / Bedding & Bath / Pillows & Throws collage has no products, prices, or visible buttons. The tiny category labels (Dining, Bedding & Bath) look like afterthoughts.
- In the "Long Weekend Sale" banner, the cream text sits over a busy bedroom photo, and "UP TO 60% OFF" has weak contrast.
- The upholstery event repeats the same offer from 10/3, 10/4, and 10/6, and the long-weekend sale repeats 10/7. The cadence feels repetitive.
- The legal footer is dense, with the free-shipping terms in tiny type. The $99 threshold is clear in the hero, but the exclusions are buried.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject carry the offer. Lead with free shipping and the $99 threshold, and drop the coy framing.
- 2. Cut the email to one promo. Keep free shipping as the hero and move the sale and upholstery banners to a separate send, or collapse them into a single "More deals" strip.
- 3. Replace the collage tiles with 3–4 linked products that have names and prices, so the shipping offer has something to attach to.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the "Long Weekend Sale" banner by adding a dark overlay or a solid text panel.
- 5. Make the tile labels real CTAs, such as "Shop decor" and "Shop bedding," in larger type.
- 6. Show the offer end date, such as "Ends 10/13," near the hero rather than only in the legal copy.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Shipping's on us this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Orders of $99 or more ship free. Plus up to 60% off select decor, bedding & lighting.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited time: free shipping on cozy decor, throws & more. Ends 10/13.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). Beyond that, the audit found a large set of images with no alt text and several insecure `http://` tracking pixels. The QA pass rate was 43%. The header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified, because the relay didn't capture those headers.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so I'm not reporting them as broken.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain HTTP:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
- Many clients block mixed or non-HTTPS content, so these pixels are likely to fail silently. Some clients may also show a "not secure content" warning.
- The other first-party tracking pixels use HTTPS and aren't flagged:
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?...`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1bb15a4f-...`
- These pixels carry hashed identifiers in the query string (`mi_u=56aaa278...`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`). That is normal practice, not a defect.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven content images have no `alt` attribute. All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `b9839b01-7716-4a26-a75a-1bf2df638bc7.jpg`
  - `8a3f413e-ef96-449c-bea4-bdb4ff6ecf90.jpg`
  - `e5f9b93b-e68b-4640-9ea1-7eadbf1f8bfd.jpg`
  - `986086d2-aa09-49e9-8c40-11fc1468072d.jpg`
  - `9e4834c1-3ecd-4577-afd6-286b7470a15e.jpg`
  - `9769d070-cc98-4225-b1c4-c6d253102288.jpg`
  - `89537ff7-1e36-4326-8b49-b933154f98a9.jpg`
- With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these show no content. If they carry the email's main message, that message is lost.
- The tracking pixels (Dotomi `pub1`–`pub10`, `UCMController`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) also lack alt text. For 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I can't confirm other issues such as table `role="presentation"`, dark-mode handling, or the preheader. The visible markup does include `lang="en"`, a `<title>` and a viewport meta tag.
- The `<title>` ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- The template has a leftover placeholder comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- The `@font-face` rules pull `HNW05` fonts from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`. This is a cross-brand dependency, and most email clients ignore web fonts anyway.
- The `.eot` and `.woff` sources for "Lineto Brown Sub" have no explicit `format()` on some declarations. Two of the Lineto fonts are also loaded through a double-slash path (`www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). Both are minor.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the delivered HTML. This looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud (SFMC) personalization or content-block variable that didn't resolve.
- It likely sits in a product-reviews or dynamic-content block. Depending on where it sits, it could show as visible text or break a link or attribute.
- No other raw tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) were detected.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)** weren't found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is **unverified**, not a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check it in the raw `.eml`.
- **Authentication-Results** wasn't found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `mail.cb2.com`. Check alignment in the raw message headers.
- The truncated HTML doesn't show whether a footer unsubscribe link or physical postal address is present. I can't confirm CAN-SPAM body requirements from this data.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity**
- All 61 links are click-tracked redirects, so UTM parameters and landing destinations can't be inspected from the final-URL data. No UTM or landing-page checks were possible.
- The subject line is "So we're doing a little thing." Whether the landing page matches the offer can't be confirmed from this data.

7. **Recommendations**
1. **Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` token.** Check the SFMC data extension or content block that feeds it, and add a fallback so a missing value renders nothing instead of the raw string. Confirm with a seed-list send. This is the only confirmed blocking defect.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://ads.dotomi.com/...` and `https://login.dotomi.com/...`), or drop them if the vendor doesn't support it.
3. **Add `alt` attributes.** Use descriptive text for the seven content images and `alt=""` for the tracking pixels.
4. **Check the raw headers** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. If the relay is stripping them, test with a direct inbox seed.
5. **Resolve a sample of the redirect links** with a real HTTP follow, to confirm UTM parameters and landing pages.
6. **Template cleanup.** Remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment and make the `<title>` match the campaign.
