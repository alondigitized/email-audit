# Up to 60% off clearance + new items added!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running the exact same Labor Day machinery seen across the last six sends in this window — "Double Rewards is on!" banner, up to 60% off, and now bolted onto a "clearance + new markdowns" subject line. It's the most cluttered version of the template yet: a daisy hero photo, then a six-panel navigation grid, then a six-tile clearance category grid, then a second Double Rewards banner, then a Jenny Lind collection block, then a "we love these for you" product carousel, then app/DesignDesk promos, then social/footer. That's roughly eight distinct content blocks stacked vertically with almost no differentiation in visual weight.
- The subject's "clearance + new markdowns" angle is actually a decent hook — clearance shoppers are a real, specific segment — but the email itself doesn't commit to that story. The hero says "up to 60% off + new markdowns," the category grid is a normal full-price-adjacent assortment (furniture, bedding, cribs, chairs, rugs, storage), and there's zero visual distinction between what's clearance and what's the standard Labor Day sale. It reads as the same Labor Day email with "clearance" pasted onto the subject line for a fresh angle, not a genuine clearance event.
- Judgement: functional but fatigued. Given the brand history — six near-identical sends since 2026-08-28 — this is more of the same creative wearing a new subject line, and the "new items added" promise in the subject isn't visually delivered anywhere in the body.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible: "up to 60% off + new markdowns" with a clear white "Shop Clearance" button, no render bugs.
- Six-panel category nav (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, Final Sale) gives shoppers fast wayfinding by category.
- The percent-off labels under each product tile (up to 50% kids furniture, 60% kids bedding + free shipping, etc.) are specific and scannable.
- Star rating + "Jenny Lind" collection callout adds a recognizable, trusted product line mid-email.
- Footer navigation and social icons are present and intact, no broken images.

## What's weak

- No visual distinction between "clearance" and the rest of the sale — the subject's core promise ("clearance + new markdowns") isn't visually delivered anywhere in the body.
- Extreme length/redundancy: hero, nav grid, sale grid, Double Rewards banner (again), Jenny Lind block, product carousel, app promo, DesignDesk promo, footer — eight stacked sections is a lot to scroll through for one offer.
- Double Rewards banner appears effectively twice in spirit (top strip + full navy block later), reinforcing brand-history fatigue rather than fixing it.
- "New items added" from the subject line is never called out visually — no "New" badge or dedicated new-arrivals section.
- The "we love these for you" product carousel feels like a generic bolt-on with no clear relationship to the clearance/markdown theme of the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make "clearance" and "new markdowns" visually distinct sections with their own headers/badges — right now they're conflated into one generic sale grid, which undercuts the subject line's specificity.
- 2. Cut the email by half: merge the two Double Rewards mentions into one, and drop or move the DesignDesk/app promos to a separate send — this email is trying to do six jobs at once.
- 3. Add a literal "New Arrivals" strip near the top so the "new items added" subject promise has a visible payoff before the fold.
- 4. Replace the generic "we love these for you" carousel heading with something tied to the clearance theme, e.g. "clearance picks going fast."
- 5. Given six near-identical Labor Day/Double Rewards sends in 48 hours, this send should lead with genuine differentiation (real clearance-only SKUs) rather than the same 40-60% off furniture assortment reshuffled.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Just added: new clearance styles, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on cribs, bedding & rugs — while they last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn Double Rewards through Sept 8`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is Crate & Kids running the exact same Labor Day machinery seen across the last six sends in this window — "Double Rewards is on!" banner, up to 60% off, and now bolted onto a "clearance + new markdowns" subject line. It's the most cluttered version of the template yet: a daisy hero photo, then a six-panel navigation grid, then a six-tile clearance category grid, then a second Double Rewards banner, then a Jenny Lind collection block, then a "we love these for you" product carousel, then app/DesignDesk promos, then social/footer. That's roughly eight distinct content blocks stacked vertically with almost no differentiation in visual weight.

The subject's "clearance + new markdowns" angle is actually a decent hook — clearance shoppers are a real, specific segment — but the email itself doesn't commit to that story. The hero says "up to 60% off + new markdowns," the category grid is a normal full-price-adjacent assortment (furniture, bedding, cribs, chairs, rugs, storage), and there's zero visual distinction between what's clearance and what's the standard Labor Day sale. It reads as the same Labor Day email with "clearance" pasted onto the subject line for a fresh angle, not a genuine clearance event.

Judgement: functional but fatigued. Given the brand history — six near-identical sends since 2026-08-28 — this is more of the same creative wearing a new subject line, and the "new items added" promise in the subject isn't visually delivered anywhere in the body.

## 2. What worked

- Hero is clean and legible: "up to 60% off + new markdowns" with a clear white "Shop Clearance" button, no render bugs.

- The percent-off labels under each product tile (up to 50% kids furniture, 60% kids bedding + free shipping, etc.) are specific and scannable.
- Star rating + "Jenny Lind" collection callout adds a recognizable, trusted product line mid-email.
- Footer navigation and social icons are present and intact, no broken images.

## 3. What didn't

- No visual distinction between "clearance" and the rest of the sale — the subject's core promise ("clearance + new markdowns") isn't visually delivered anywhere in the body.
- Extreme length/redundancy: hero, nav grid, sale grid, Double Rewards banner (again), Jenny Lind block, product carousel, app promo, DesignDesk promo, footer — eight stacked sections is a lot to scroll through for one offer.
- Double Rewards banner appears effectively twice in spirit (top strip + full navy block later), reinforcing brand-history fatigue rather than fixing it.
- "New items added" from the subject line is never called out visually — no "New" badge or dedicated new-arrivals section.
- The "we love these for you" product carousel feels like a generic bolt-on with no clear relationship to the clearance/markdown theme of the email.

## 4. What I'd change

1. Make "clearance" and "new markdowns" visually distinct sections with their own headers/badges — right now they're conflated into one generic sale grid, which undercuts the subject line's specificity.
2. Cut the email by half: merge the two Double Rewards mentions into one, and drop or move the DesignDesk/app promos to a separate send — this email is trying to do six jobs at once.
3. Add a literal "New Arrivals" strip near the top so the "new items added" subject promise has a visible payoff before the fold.

5. Given six near-identical Labor Day/Double Rewards sends in 48 hours, this send should lead with genuine differentiation (real clearance-only SKUs) rather than the same 40-60% off furniture assortment reshuffled.
