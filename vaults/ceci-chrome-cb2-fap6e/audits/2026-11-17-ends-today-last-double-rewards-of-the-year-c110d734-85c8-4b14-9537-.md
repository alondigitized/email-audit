# ENDS TODAY: Last Double Rewards of the year!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- I could only see this render at a heavy downscale (680px wide, about 12,000px tall), so I can read headlines, section order and button placement but not fine print. The email is a long, orderly Double Rewards finale. It opens with a small dual banner, then a dark-red hero: "ends today: last Double Rewards this year!". The hero has two buttons, "Shop Top Categories" and "Learn More". Below it come stacked category blocks for kitchen, the Gift Shop, bedroom, dining, sofas, rugs and a "more ways to earn" grid, and it closes on a kitchen-deals block with product pricing.
- The "ENDS TODAY" subject is true to the hero, and the deadline is the strongest thing about this send. But it is the same Double Rewards and Early Black Friday message subscribers have had for days. Compare "ENDS TOMORROW: Shop quick-ship faves with 2x Rewards!" on 11/16 and the several "Ends soon" sends on 11/15. For a home-furnishings recipient, this reads as the sixth reminder rather than news. The final-day urgency helps, but the length and the category sprawl dilute it.
- Judgement: a competent, well-rendered closer whose real selling point, a one-day window, is announced in the hero and then buried under about ten sections of catalog.

## What's working

- The hero states the deadline ("ends today") and the offer ("last Double Rewards of the year") in the same place as the subject, so the open-to-body handoff is clean.
- The tone is calm and on-brand, with large editorial photography and lowercase headlines, and I saw no broken images or layout breakage.
- Each section has a "Double Rewards" earn line above it, so the loyalty benefit repeats down the page.
- Furniture categories (bedroom, dining, sofas and sectionals, rugs) get full-width blocks, which suits a home-furniture reader.
- The closing kitchen block shows concrete product pricing and "up to 30% off" figures, which gives the email a few hard numbers.

## What's weak

- It is very long, so the urgent message is only in the hero and the top banner. By the time you reach rugs and kitchen, nothing reminds you the window closes today.
- The top banner mixes two messages (the Double Rewards end date and Early Black Friday), and the text is tiny, so neither lands.
- The hero's two CTAs, "Shop Top Categories" and "Learn More", are generic. A reader can't tell what they earn or where they'll land.
- Most section CTAs go to broad categories, such as "Shop Bedroom" and "Shop Furniture". Few products are highlighted, and I saw no reviews or social proof.
- It repeats the creative and offer of the sends from the past few days, which adds to fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a single countdown-style hero ahead of everything else: "Double Rewards ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT", with one primary button, "Shop furniture, earn 2x". Move the Early Black Friday messaging out of the top banner.
- 2. Cut the body to three or four blocks (furniture, rugs and decor, kitchen, gifts). Put an "Ends tonight" reminder strip between blocks so the deadline stays in view as you scroll.
- 3. Replace "Learn More" with a specific value line such as "How 2x Rewards works", or drop it so the hero has one clear action.
- 4. Link to specific hero products with a price or "earn X points" example, and add review stars to the furniture hero.
- 5. Suppress or lighten this send for people who already got the 11/16 "ends tomorrow" email, or merge the two into one final-day send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight only: 2x Rewards on furniture, rugs & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: double Rewards ends at 11:59 PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn double on bedroom, dining, sofas and rugs before midnight. No code needed.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance this year to earn 2x. Shop quick-ship favorites before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source is a table-based XHTML Transitional email on an Oracle/Marigold-style tracking stack (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). The main confirmed issue is widespread missing image alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and are inconclusive. The HTML is truncated, so body links and the footer could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **136 links were skipped by the HTTP probe** because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains, and final URLs are unverified.
- **Five third-party pixels** point to `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. `rlcdn.com` is LiveRamp. Each pixel carries the same identifier token with no `alt`. This is third-party identity-sync tracking, which affects privacy and image-blocking behavior.
- **Recipient-identifying tokens in image URLs:**
  - `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/165f0a26-…`.
- **Plain-text/URL ratio:** the QA check reports 26,924 of 36,671 characters (73%) of the plain-text part are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is a spam-filter risk. It likely comes from long tracked URLs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 85 images.** Roughly 70 are content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (`.jpg` and `.gif`) plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. Some are product or promo imagery, so with images blocked or a screen reader on, those blocks have no text equivalent. The check does not distinguish a missing attribute from `alt=""`. The remaining items are tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `/p/rp/*.png`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`), where empty alt is acceptable.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">`** disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`** appears on `*` and `body` and overrides the `100%` set elsewhere. It blocks text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Unescaped `&` in `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. Browsers and clients tolerate it.
- **Template residue and redundancy:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the source.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times, and `ReadMsgBody` and `ExternalClass` rules are duplicated across style blocks.
  - `<meta charset>` is declared twice, which is redundant but harmless.
  - These add bytes, and the email is already large: the plain-text part alone is about 36 KB.
- **Email size:** with about 136 links and 80+ images, the HTML is likely near Gmail's ~102 KB clipping threshold. This is an inference, since only the truncated source was available, so confirm it against the full message.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FirstName]`. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are populated.

## 5. Compliance

- **`Authentication-Results` is absent.** This is expected through the relay, so SPF, DKIM, and DMARC are unknown and no failure can be claimed.
