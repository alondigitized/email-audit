# These 10 holiday favorites always sell out early →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a curated gift-guide send under a Black Friday banner, and it is the strongest of Crate & Barrel's recent sends. The "10 holiday favorites that always sell out" idea gives the email a reason to exist, unlike the near-identical "up to 50% off" blasts from 11/20. The numbered products also give it a clear structure.
- Caveat: the render is heavily downscaled (680px wide, about 12,000px tall). I can read the headlines, section order and CTA placement, but not fine print, exact prices or most body copy.
- The top is solid. A red "Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping" banner with a Shop Now button sits above a moody table-setting hero reading "these sell out every year." After that the email drifts. It runs from curated picks into category tiles, a kitchen-deals block, a personalized-gifts block and a full Black Friday grid. The "10 favorites" promise gets diluted by a second and third email's worth of content stacked underneath. It is a good concept that ends up as a catch-all.

## What's working

- The subject promise of "10 favorites" matches the body, which has numbered product blocks (garland, Joan Anderson arctic friends plates, monochrome evergreen collection, advent calendar, snowflake pretzels).
- The red Black Friday banner puts the offer (up to 50% off plus free shipping) above the fold.
- The hero line "these sell out every year." backs up the subject's scarcity angle without shouting.
- Product blocks pair a lifestyle photo with a short editorial blurb and a specific CTA (e.g. "Shop Hemlock Garland"). That reads more like a gift guide than a promo flyer.
- The seasonal urgency is credible: a Thanksgiving delivery strip at the top and an "order personalized gifts by December 15" deadline.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. After the 10 favorites it adds a kitchen-deals block, a personalized-gifts block and a six-tile Black Friday grid, which dilutes the hero idea.
- It repeats the cadence problem from "Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!" (11/20) and "The top 10: Black Friday deals you'll ONLY find at Crate →" (11/21). Another "top 10" framing the same day feels like the same email again.
- Every product block has two CTAs (a specific one and a "Shop All…" one), which splits attention.
- The category link tiles (ornaments, Christmas tableware and so on) look like plain text rows. They are low-contrast and easy to skip.
- I can't confirm any prices or "sell out" evidence such as low-stock flags, so the scarcity claim rests on copy alone.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the 10 favorites and the kitchen deals. Move the personalized-gifts and Black Friday grid to a separate send, so the numbered list is the whole story.
- 2. Back the scarcity claim with proof: "Only 12 left" flags, "sold out last year by Dec 1", or a ranking such as "#1 best seller".
- 3. Drop the secondary "Shop All…" buttons on every block. Keep one CTA per product and a single "See all 10" at the end.
- 4. Make the category tiles real buttons or image tiles with clear contrast.
- 5. Vary the framing against the 11/21 "top 10" send so the two don't read as duplicates.
- **Subject Alt A:** `10 Crate favorites that sold out last year`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Our 10 holiday bestsellers (going fast)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off + free shipping. Get it by Thanksgiving.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Garland, plates, advent calendar and more. Order before they're gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a very large, link-heavy Marketo-style template (101 tracked links, about 59 flagged images). Its main confirmed issues are widespread missing `alt` attributes and viewport and text-scaling settings that hurt accessibility. Header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **101 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are LiveRamp (rlcdn) tracking pixels with the same subscriber hash `s=`, stacked in one email. They add third-party requests and a privacy and deliverability surface (some clients flag multiple pixels).
- **Open and engagement pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/bcc76c09-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. These are expected for the ESP and are not defects. They are 1x1 images with no `alt`.
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa278…` hash appears in seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image URLs. Forwarding the email exposes the identifier, and it varies per recipient, so these images can't be cached.
- **Broken-link evidence:** none found, since nothing was probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 59 images flagged for missing alt text.**
  - 45 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `85f886bb-….jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-….png`). With images blocked, the email has no readable fallback for these.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. These look like dynamic recommendation images, so they are likely product content that also needs alt text.
  - 7 are tracking pixels (`rlcdn` ×5, `o.gif`, `dv.…`). These should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom is restricted:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom.
- **Text scaling is disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop iOS from resizing text, which affects low-vision users.
- **Markup hygiene:**
  - The XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype is paired with an unclosed `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - A template placeholder is left in the source: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - These are unlikely to break rendering.
- **Limit of this review:** the HTML source was truncated, so the body structure, table nesting and Outlook/VML behavior were not reviewed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) were reported by QA or visible in the available source. This is limited to the truncated portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found.** QA says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so absence cannot be confirmed from this data.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The same caveat applies. If it is truly absent, one-click unsubscribe is unsupported, and Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image and click subdomains, but alignment alone doesn't prove authentication.
- **Footer not reviewed.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 101 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and no resolved destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the available data. Landing-page alignment with the "10 holiday favorites" subject was not checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 45 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` recommendation images. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Consider dropping `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` so text can scale.
3. Consolidate the five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` pixels into one, or confirm why five are needed.
4. Re-run QA against the raw message with full headers (not the relay copy) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. Resolve the redirects on a sample of the 101 tracking links to check destinations, status codes and UTM presence.
6. Clean the template by removing the placeholder comment, deduplicating the `format-detection` and charset meta tags, and escaping `&` in `<title>`.
