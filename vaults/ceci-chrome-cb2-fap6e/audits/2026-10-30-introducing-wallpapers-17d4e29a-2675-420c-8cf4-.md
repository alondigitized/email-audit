# INTRODUCING WALLPAPERS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- This is a launch email for CB2's new wallpaper line, and it works as an editorial catalog. A moody, warm hero says "INTRODUCING WALLCOVERINGS." Below it, a run of full-bleed room shots shows named patterns (Palm Print, Nisha, Malta Faux Plaster, Jungle Bloom, Mother Amazon) with designer credits. It reads like a magazine spread, and the "order free swatches" offer is a smart way to lower the risk of a wallpaper purchase.
- The weakness is focus. The top banner, "NEW MARKDOWNS UP TO 60% OFF," sits above the wallpaper hero and pulls attention to an unrelated sale. Further down, a $1,000 app giveaway, an interior design services block, a 12-item "More for you" product grid and a cross-promo footer all compete with the launch. The email is very long and says several things at once.
- The inbox-facing copy is also weak. The subject, "INTRODUCING WALLPAPERS," is in all caps and has no hook. It also doesn't match the email body, which says "wallcoverings." This is a competent 6-7 send, in line with the recent 7.0 sends, such as the 10/29 "Nina Garcia Collection" launch. It trails that one because the wallpaper story is diluted.

## What's working

- The hero and the room photography are cohesive, warm and aspirational, and they fit CB2's design-led brand.
- Named patterns with designer credits (Matthew Williamson, Laundry Studio) make the products feel collectible.
- The free swatch offer with an "Order Free Swatches" button addresses the main objection to buying wallpaper online.
- Both "Shop Wallcoverings" and "Shop All Wallcoverings" buttons are clearly visible and in the right category.
- The render is clean, with no visible broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" markdown banner at the very top pushes the wallpaper hero down and muddles the message.
- The email is extremely long. The wallpaper story ends partway down, then the app giveaway, design services, a product grid and a loyalty block follow.
- The subject is generic, all caps, and uses "wallpapers" while the body says "wallcoverings."
- Pattern tiles don't show prices, sizes or per-roll cost, and the 60% discount likely doesn't apply to the wallpaper, so the price story is unclear.
- Nothing has a deadline. There is no urgency or reason to act today.
- The "More for You" grid of candle holders and candles is unrelated to the wallpaper theme and dilutes the click path.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the markdown banner out of the wallpaper send, or put it below the wallpaper content. Let the launch hero lead.
- 2. Cut the app giveaway, design services and loyalty blocks to one secondary module. Keep the email to the launch, the swatches and one footer cross-sell.
- 3. Add a price anchor to each pattern tile (for example, "from $X/roll") and one sentence on installation (peel-and-stick or paste-the-wall).
- 4. Make the swatch offer a more prominent module with a time limit, such as "free swatches ship this week."
- 5. Make the subject and the body wording match.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: 5 wallpapers from designer collabs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free wallpaper swatches, new prints inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Palm Print, Jungle Bloom and more. Order free swatches before you commit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Designer-led prints by Matthew Williamson and Laundry Studio, now at CB2.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with VML/MSO fallbacks. QA came back at a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The main code-level issues are about 12 content images with no alt text and 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking pixels. The header warnings can't be confirmed as defects from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** The QA skipped them because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`, which many clients block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** the ten `pub1`–`pub10.php` calls are cookie-redirect syncs. They add ten third-party requests on open and can slow rendering. They also expose the recipient's IP and cookie identifiers to Dotomi, which is a privacy concern.
- **Own open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/6a0793c1-0a67-4430-a792-fb83cadd6310?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are served over HTTPS. Neither has alt text, so use `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images** under `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `723ca113-…jpg`, `e7496c59-…jpg`, `db76bf5a-…jpg`, `16187dd2-…jpg`, `a48cd3db-…jpg`
  - `4cf9a044-…jpg`, `46526082-…jpg`, `efdf96c1-…jpg`, `a363340d-…jpg`, `d208e96e-…jpg`
  - `8e957a75-…gif`
  
  With images blocked, the content is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images by default. I can't tell from the QA whether these images carry the main CTAs or the "INTRODUCING WALLPAPERS" message. If they do, the severity is higher.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `pub1`–`pub10.php`, `UCMController`, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel all lack an `alt` attribute. For decorative or tracking images, an explicit `alt=""` is the correct value.
- **Cross-domain font loading:** `@font-face` rules pull from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…` and `cb2.com/Styles/CB2/fonts/…`, including a `.eot` source. Clients that block remote fonts will fall back to `Helvetica, sans-serif` per the `a { font-family }` rule, which is acceptable. One font URL uses a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/…`), which is tolerated but sloppy.
- **Template residue:** the source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Viewport meta:** `maximum-scale=1` in `<meta name="viewport">` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- **Breakpoint CSS:** `*{ line-height:100% }` is a global rule, and `p{ padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important }` is broad. Neither is flagged by QA, so I'm not raising them as defects.
- **Not verifiable:** the HTML was truncated, so I can't assess the body structure, bulletproof buttons, or the `lang`/`role` usage beyond `<html lang="en">`, which is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source or in the QA output. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a verified defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders, so check against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** The QA note says this is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. Verify against a direct-to-inbox copy.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer was cut off in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either. This is a verification gap, not a finding.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 68 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't show final destinations or UTM parameters. The links go through a redirect domain, so UTMs would only be visible after resolving the redirects. ## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the 11 content images listed in section 3. This is the highest-impact fix.
2. **Add `alt=""`** to all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
3. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com`), or drop the ten `pub1`–`pub10.php` syncs if they aren't needed.
4. **Verify the headers on a raw message:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Treat these as unconfirmed until then.
5. **Resolve a sample of the 68 skipped tracking links** to confirm the destinations return 200 and carry the expected UTMs.
6. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and clean up the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` template comment.
