# BACK IN STOCK. ON SALE.
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's Labor Day formula again, but the subject line finally does new work: "BACK IN STOCK. ON SALE." leads with restock urgency instead of another generic "up to 50% off" blast, and the hero image is a fresh cream-toned living room shot (curved sofa, boucle armchair) that hasn't run in this stretch's prior sends (2026-09-02 through 09-04 leaned on the beach-at-dusk and floral-armchair heroes). The countdown timer ("SALE ENDS IN 04:07:20:30") is a genuinely credible urgency device that most of the recent sends lacked.
- The problem is the body doesn't deliver on the subject's promise. "Back in stock" is the entire hook, but the hero copy just repeats "UP TO 50% OFF BACK IN STOCK" as a tagline — there's no visible back-in-stock product, no "these sold out and are back" framing, no specific SKU. It's a bait-and-switch: the subject sells restocked inventory, the email sells the same six-category sale grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) CB2 has run all week.
- Net: smart subject-line pivot, same recycled body. Middling send.

## What's working

- Subject line breaks from the repetitive "Labor Day / up to 50% off" framing and taps restock urgency, a genuinely different angle for this stretch.
- Live-looking countdown timer under the top nav adds credible time pressure.
- Hero image is new for this campaign window — no repeat of the beach-at-dusk or floral-chair shots used in the last several sends.
- Clean, no render bugs: images load, text is legible, category grid is well-organized with clear labels.
- Extended holiday hours callout at top is a nice practical touch for shoppers planning a store visit.

## What's weak

- The "back in stock" promise from the subject is never substantiated in the body — no specific product, no "restocked" badge, no distinct section for it.
- Hero copy is just "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF / BACK IN STOCK" stacked as taglines, not a real headline — reads like a label collision, not a message.
- This is at least the sixth near-identical "Labor Day sale, up to 50% off, shop the sale button" send in a week — fatigue is real at this cadence.
- No loyalty/member-specific offer surfaced near the hero; the Double Rewards block is buried two-thirds down the email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Actually build the "back in stock" story: add a dedicated section right under the hero showing 3-4 specific restocked SKUs with a "back in stock" badge — this is the one differentiator the subject promises and the body currently skips entirely.
- 2. Rewrite the hero headline so "back in stock" and "up to 50% off" aren't just stacked tags — e.g. "Sold out. Now back. Up to 50% off." gives it a narrative instead of three disconnected labels.
- 3. Move the Double Rewards / credit card offer higher, or at least tease it near the hero, since it's the only genuinely member-specific benefit in the email.
- 4. Vary the CTA copy — "SHOP THE SALE" appears twice; use "SHOP RESTOCKS" for the back-in-stock section to reinforce the subject's promise.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Your favorites are back — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked: the pieces everyone missed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sold-out rugs, chairs & lighting just landed back on sale.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 hours left — plus the restocks you've been waiting for.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 64 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (standard for ESP click-tracking domains; not itself a defect, but means link-destination validity is unconfirmed).
- Embedded third-party ad-sync pixel chain via `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com` (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `cookieredir`), all served over `http://` rather than `https://`. Many modern mail clients (Outlook, Gmail image proxy) will block or fail to load mixed-content `http://` resources, meaning these pixels likely fail silently.
- First-party tracking pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/...` are HTTPS — no issue there.

- The Dotomi tracking images also lack `alt` attributes, though as 1x1 tracking pixels this is functionally immaterial.
- HTML uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype with MSO conditional comments and extensive inline `@media` targeting — standard defensive email-client coding, no defects found in the truncated markup.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders present in the visible/truncated HTML source. No issues found.

Truncated source does not expose the primary CTA destination URLs (masked behind the 64 skipped tracking redirects), so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing page alignment cannot be verified from available evidence.
