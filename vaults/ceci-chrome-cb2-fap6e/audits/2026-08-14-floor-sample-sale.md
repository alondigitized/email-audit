# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's eleventh distinct creative mode this week, and it's an actual product event: "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE, IN STORES FOR A LIMITED TIME" over a single rust-velvet swivel chair, cleanly isolated as hero. That's a real reason to click, but it's an in-store-only sale, and the CTA immediately downgrades intent to "FIND A STORE" — a store locator, not a shoppable link. The email then pivots hard into a second, unrelated promo ("END OF SUMMER SALE, UP TO 40% OFF") and a six-item "MORE FOR YOU" grid, so by the time you scroll past the hero this reads like two campaigns bolted together rather than one focused floor-sample event.
- Compared to 8/13's "Win a $5k design refresh" (7/10, one clean hook) and 8/12's discount-stack send (7/10), this is closer to the week's weaker heritage-editorial mode: single strong visual, but the offer mechanics work against the open. In-store-only sales chronically underperform for email because most subscribers can't act on them immediately, and stacking a second sale banner directly below dilutes whichever one the reader was drawn in by.
- Judgement: decent hero, wrong CTA for the channel, muddled by a bolted-on second promo. Mid-pack for the week.

## What's working

- Hero image is a strong, isolated product shot (velvet chair, floral accent) with clean negative space for the headline.
- "IN STORES FOR A LIMITED TIME" sets honest expectations before the click — no bait-and-switch.
- "END OF SUMMER SALE — UP TO 40% OFF" banner has a real, specific, high number that's easy to scan.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid gives six concrete, priced products as a fallback path if the primary offer doesn't land.
- Layout is clean end to end — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- Primary CTA ("FIND A STORE") sends email clickers to a locator, not a cart — this is fundamentally a low-intent action for an email channel.
- Two competing sales in one send (floor sample sale + end-of-summer 40% off) with no clear signal which one the subscriber should prioritize.
- No specifics on the floor sample sale itself — no % off, no dollar range, no which categories, no end date.
- "MORE FOR YOU" is generic full-price merchandising, not tied to either sale above it, further diluting focus.
- Cadence fatigue: eleventh creative mode this week per brand history, and this one splits its own attention internally too.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the CTA mismatch first.** If floor samples are in-store only, either add a secondary "Shop Online Deals" link next to "Find a Store," or replace the hero with the 40% off online sale and demote floor samples to a footer mention. Don't lead with a CTA that can't convert in-channel.
- 2. **Cut it to one sale per send.** Pick either floor samples or end-of-summer 40% off as the hero; the other cannibalizes attention and reads as clutter.
- 3. **Add real numbers to the floor sample banner** — e.g. "Up to 50% off one-of-a-kind pieces" — vague "limited time" language undersells what could be the stronger discount.
- 4. **Tie "MORE FOR YOU" to the actual sale** by badging discounted items, instead of running full-price products next to two sale banners.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Floor samples: up to 50% off, in stores now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One-of-a-kind pieces, marked way down`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off online — ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Limited pieces, one time only — find your store`
