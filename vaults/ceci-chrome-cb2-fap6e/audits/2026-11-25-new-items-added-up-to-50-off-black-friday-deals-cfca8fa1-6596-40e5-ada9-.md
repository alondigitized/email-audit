# NEW ITEMS ADDED! Up to 50% off Black Friday deals!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is another Crate & Barrel Black Friday blast, and it reads as a catalog dump. The render is heavily downscaled (113px wide, scaled from 680×12000), so I can read layout and large headline text but not fine print. The hero says "100s of new deals added! Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" with a Shop All Deals button, over a red ribbon image. A thin "get it by Thanksgiving with extended store hours" strip sits above it. Below that come about ten category blocks (sofas, kitchen brands, furniture, holiday decor, hosting, rugs, Kids, clearance), each with a big photo, a "up to X% off" line and a Shop Now button. A kitchen-deals module and Thanksgiving-table and kitchen-gift modules close it out.
- This is the same offer as the 11/24 "NEW DEALS just added" and 11/23 "Up to 50% off our biggest sale of the year!" sends. The "NEW ITEMS ADDED" framing is a third relabel of an unchanged promo. The category tiers are clear, and the Thanksgiving timing is the one new, credible hook. But the strip carrying it is tiny and sits above the hero instead of leading it.
- Verdict: competent execution, fatigued concept. The long scroll and 12+ identical Shop Now buttons dilute the hero offer, and a subscriber who has had six near-identical sends in 48 hours has little reason to open this one.

## What's working

- The hero states one clear headline offer, "Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping", with a visible Shop All Deals button.
- Every category block uses the same pattern (photo, "up to X% off", Shop Now), so the email is easy to scan.
- The Thanksgiving and extended-hours strip adds real time-bound relevance.
- The red "Black Friday" tag on each tile keeps the campaign visible down the scroll.
- The "free shipping orders $79+" tile gives a concrete threshold.

## What's weak

- At roughly 12,000px tall, the email is far too long, with around 15 CTAs and no clear priority.
- Nothing is new in substance. The "NEW ITEMS ADDED" subject over-promises, and the body shows no visibly new items.
- The Thanksgiving urgency strip is tiny and buried above the hero.
- The category blocks have no product detail, pricing or reviews, so there's little reason to click a specific tile.
- It's the sixth or seventh Black Friday send in about 48 hours, with the same offer and a lot of repeated creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus 4–5 tiles and move the best-priced products higher. The category parade at 12,000px is the biggest drag.
- 2. Make the Thanksgiving deadline the hero's second line ("Order by X for Thanksgiving delivery") instead of a tiny strip.
- 3. If the items are really new, show a few named products with sale prices. If they aren't, drop the "NEW" claim.
- 4. Collapse the duplicate Shop Now buttons into category-specific CTAs such as "Shop sofas" and "Shop rugs".
- 5. Fix the cadence. Send one daily Black Friday email, not several near-identical ones.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dinnerware, cookware and tables, plus free shipping on orders $79+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a table-based, MI-templated layout with `lang="en"`, `charset=utf-8` and a Outlook/MSO fallback. The QA run scored 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed problem is widespread missing alt text. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **87 tracking links were skipped.** The HTTP probe skipped these click-redirect URLs. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` beacons (LiveRamp). Several first-party beacons also load: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 images) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/<uuid>?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. The five rlcdn requests are redundant and add load time and privacy exposure.
- **Identifier in URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa278…` token appears in every `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixel URL. Forwarding the email exposes it, and it is a long-lived recipient identifier.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't audit the `<a href>` set, any broken-link patterns or `mailto:` handling.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** About 28 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` assets were flagged (`.jpg`, `.png` and animated `.gif`). Examples:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `873e6aa9-90ec-47f2-8a03-5697dd70a2c6.jpg`
  - `07077988-c317-4b77-8da6-d219e00275cf.gif`

  If these images carry the offer or product content, it is invisible to screen readers and to clients that block images.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels.** The `rlcdn`, `mi.*` and `dv.*` beacons are flagged too. These are non-content, so they need `alt=""` rather than descriptive text.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Markup hygiene (minor):**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - Several `format-detection` meta tags are declared more than once (`telephone`, `address` and `date` each appear twice).
  - A leftover template comment reads `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]`.
  - `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` rules are duplicated across the two `<style>` blocks.
- **Animated GIFs:** about 10 `.gif` assets are included. Outlook desktop shows only the first frame, so the first frame needs to work on its own. I can't confirm that from the source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The only token-like string is the leftover template comment noted above, and it sits in a comment, not rendered content. The rest of the HTML was truncated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders, so it is worth checking against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and the image and tracking subdomains (`image.mail.`, `mi.`, `dv.`) are consistent with it.
- **Not verifiable:** the visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address, because the footer was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The link probe was skipped for all 87 tracked links, so the following can't be confirmed:
- Whether destinations carry UTM parameters.
- Whether the redirects resolve to the Black Friday landing pages the subject promises ("up to 50% off").

I can't flag a continuity defect from this data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to every content image under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `mi.*/p/rp`, `o.gif`, `dv.*`).
2. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag so users can zoom.
3. **Verify headers on the raw message**, not the relay copy. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass with alignment.
4. **Resolve a sample of the 87 skipped links** with a redirect-following probe. Check status codes, final URLs and UTM presence.
5. **Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels** to one, or confirm each is intentional.
6. **Clean the template:** escape `&amp;` in `<title>`, remove duplicate `format-detection` metas and duplicated CSS, and delete the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
7. **Re-run the audit on the untruncated HTML** to cover the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), the full link set and any token leakage.
