# Just in. Already trending.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 stepping away from its recent run of sale-anchor sends (07-22 through 07-26 were all "% OFF ENDS TODAY" / "STEALS OF THE WEEK" discount blasts) into a "new arrivals" showcase — five named designer pieces (MEDA, RIO, DAVSON, FITZ, ALDINE) each get their own hero shot, name, and "SHOP NOW." That's a genuine change of pace after a week of near-identical furniture-event emails, and the execution is clean: no render bugs, consistent brand voice, real dimensions and prices on the "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom.
- The problem is the subject line doesn't earn the content. "Just in. Already trending." names no category, no product, no offer — it could be for shoes, skincare, or furniture. Someone scanning an inbox has zero signal this is a CB2 design drop unless they already recognize the sender. And true to CB2 form (see 07-24 "AVAILABLE AGAIN"), this is curiosity bait: the copy promises something specific and "trending," but the reveal is just another multi-product scroll.
- The real offer — "THE FURNITURE EVENT UP TO 40% OFF" — is buried six product blocks down, after the reader has already scrolled past five full-bleed hero images. If the goal was a discovery/new-arrivals email, drop the offer entirely and let the products carry it; if the goal was to drive the 40% sale, this is the wrong structure. Right now it's neither a clean editorial send nor an efficient sale email.

## What's working

- Each new-arrival product gets a full-width hero image, a distinct name, and its own "SHOP NOW" — easy to scan and each one is a legitimate, unambiguous click target.
- No layout bugs: alignment, spacing, and image rendering all look clean end to end.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid includes real prices and dimensions ("Foley Mink Velvet Counter Stool $499.00," "Aqua Virgo 72" Rectangular Dark Burl Wood Dining Table") — genuinely useful product detail, not filler.
- Loyalty and membership benefits (10% back in Reward Dollars, 15% off sign-up, CB2 app download) are all visible before the footer, giving repeat shoppers a reason to stay engaged.
- Departing from the discount-blast format for once (vs. the five straight sale emails from 07-22–07-26) adds welcome variety to the cadence.

## What's weak

- Subject line names no product, category, or offer — pure curiosity with nothing concrete to anchor it, a repeat of the 07-24 "AVAILABLE AGAIN" bait pattern.
- The one real discount ("UP TO 40% OFF" furniture event) is buried after five full hero images — most readers on mobile will bail before reaching it.
- "Up to 40% off" is a vague ceiling with no qualifying detail visible in the render (no category breakdown, no end date) — the exact fine-print vagueness flagged in the 07-24 "UP TO 30% OFF BEDROOM & BATH" send.
- No reviews or social proof anywhere despite an entire banner asking customers to leave a review — the ask is visible, the payoff isn't.
- Zero lifestyle/human element in any hero shot — every image is a still-life product photo, so there's nothing signaling who this is "for."

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to name the actual hook — pick the single best piece (MEDA sofa or the Onyx table) and lead with it, or lead with the 40% event if that's the real priority. Don't run a curiosity-only subject when the body has five perfectly good concrete anchors to choose from.
- 2. Move the "FURNITURE EVENT UP TO 40% OFF" banner to directly under the hero, before the product showcase — if it's the intended driver, it shouldn't require six scrolls to find.
- 3. Add one line of qualifying detail near the "up to 40%" claim (categories included, end date) so it doesn't repeat the vagueness called out in 07-24.
- 4. Swap the "LEAVE US A REVIEW" banner's placement — put it after showing 2-3 real customer reviews/ratings on a hero product, not as a cold ask.
- 5. Test a subject that names the trend concretely instead of asserting it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `The chair everyone's asking about`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New this week: 5 pieces, up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meet MEDA, RIO, and three more just-landed favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals + the Furniture Event, up to 40% off.`
