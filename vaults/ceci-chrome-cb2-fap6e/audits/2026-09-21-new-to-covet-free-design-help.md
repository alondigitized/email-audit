# New to covet. Free design help.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 doing a service-led pitch instead of a discount pitch — "INTERIOR DESIGN SERVICES / With things you'll only find at CB2—and the help of our interior designers," backed by a moody, aspirational mosaic hero ("HOME IS EVERYTHING") and a warm-toned lifestyle scroll (leather sofa, brass/stone sconce, dark green living room, vignette). It's the best-produced creative in this brand's recent run — genuinely richer photography than the "FALL BEDROOM REFRESH" or "GO BOLD OR GO HOME" sends — but it buries its own value prop. The subject promises "free design help" for someone "new to covet," yet the free-appointment CTA doesn't appear until deep in the scroll, after a huge non-actionable hero and a five-photo lifestyle block.
- The category grid ("2 WAYS TO MEET," Sofas & Sectionals, Dining Room, Bedroom, Lighting, Rugs, Mirrors & Decor) is a nice touch for someone unsure where to start, but it's stacked below the fold on top of the fold. And then the email tacks on an unrelated "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" banner at the very bottom — which, per the brand history, is the exact same offer as the 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-20 sends, just demoted to a footer afterthought here.
- Net judgement: strong visual craft, muddled priority — this reads like two emails (a design-service intro + a recycled sale banner) stitched together, and the free-design hook that justified the subject line gets buried.

## What's working

- Hero collage ("HOME IS EVERYTHING" / "FURNISH IT WELL") is the most polished visual this brand has sent in the recent history — warm, cohesive, editorial.
- "INTERIOR DESIGN SERVICES" section clearly names the service and has a visible black CTA button, "BOOK YOUR FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT."
- "2 WAYS TO MEET / IN STORE OR ONLINE" clearly explains the offer mechanics (complimentary, full-room help) with a repeated CTA.
- Category tile grid (Sofas, Dining, Bedroom, Lighting, Rugs, Mirrors & Decor) gives a concrete next step for someone who doesn't know where to start.
- No render bugs — images, type, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The subject's actual offer (free design help) doesn't appear until well past the fold — the hero and full lifestyle photo block up top sell no concrete action.
- "Best Sellers up to 40% off" is dropped in at the very bottom as a small banner, disconnected from the design-services narrative and identical to offers already sent 2026-09-17 and 2026-09-20 — creative fatigue risk.
- Design-service CTA button copy ("BOOK YOUR FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT") is repeated verbatim twice with no variation or added detail (e.g., how long it takes, virtual vs. in-store distinction upfront).
- No social proof, no "who are our designers" credibility signal, despite this being a trust-dependent service (letting someone into your home/design decisions).

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the "INTERIOR DESIGN SERVICES / BOOK YOUR FREE APPOINTMENT" block above the lifestyle photo scroll — right under or beside the hero — so the subject's promise is honored immediately, not after 3-4 screens of scrolling.
- 2. Cut the bottom "Best Sellers 40% off" banner or make it its own send — it dilutes a focused service email and repeats a beat from two recent sends this week.
- 3. Add one line of credibility near the CTA (e.g., "Our designers have helped 10,000+ rooms" or a designer photo/name) since this is a trust-based, not price-based, offer.
- 4. Vary the two CTA buttons — first one "Book Your Free Appointment," second one "See How It Works" or "Find a Designer Near You" — to avoid redundancy and give a softer second entry point.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `In-store or online — pick a room, we'll style it`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No cost, no obligation. Just expert eyes on your space.`

- Multiple third-party ad-redirect calls to `ads.dotomi.com` (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, query param `d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`) and `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` are served over plain `http://`, not `https://`. Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), which can silently break tracking/attribution.

- Template uses legacy MSO conditional comments, XHTML transitional doctype, and `-ms-text-size-adjust`/`-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets — consistent with a hybrid Outlook/mobile-safe build; no structural rendering defects observed in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
