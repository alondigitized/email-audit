# 100s of new items at up to 60% off!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance send with a lot of other content attached. The hero says "100s of new markdowns up to 60% off*" over an autumn-leaf photo, with a "Shop Clearance" button below it and a rust-colored strip of category links. It matches the subject and the offer is clear. It's also the second "new markdowns" blast in two days, after "New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →" earlier today. The only new thing is "100s of new items," which reads as a rewording of the same message.
- Below the hero the email stops being a clearance email. It runs through a full-price Doona stroller, an NFL Nod Chair collection, a "we love these for you" grid at full price (the one sale item is a $14.97 Halloween bucket), a credit card pitch, an app download and a design appointment. None of those support the 60% promise. Someone who clicked for deals has to scroll past about five unrelated pitches to reach the footer.
- Overall it's competent and nothing is broken, but it is a repeat send with a diluted message. I'd call it middling, around 6 to 7.

## What's working

- The hero is readable. The headline is large, the offer is stated, and the white "Shop Clearance" button stands out against the orange.
- The category strip (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, Baby Bedding, Decor & Lighting, "Shop 40%–60% Off Final Sale") lets a shopper jump straight to their area.
- The product grid shows prices, and the Halloween treat bag shows a strike-through ($34.95 to $14.97), so one real markdown is visible.
- The layout is clean and on-brand, with no broken images or overlapping text.
- The NFL Game Day collection ties into the season and gives the email a specific hook.

## What's weak

- The email is repeat-heavy. A second "new markdowns" blast in two days, after "New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →" earlier today, makes "new" feel hollow.
- The body drifts away from the sale. The Doona stroller ($1,350 and up), the Nuna items ($1,450) and the crib mattress show no discount at all.
- The Halloween pumpkin bucket is one of the featured products on October 29, which is too late to be useful.
- It has no deadline, only "Selling fast. Shop now, while quantities last." The offer lacks credible urgency.
- The credit card, app and design-appointment blocks compete with the main CTA. The fine print is also a long, dense block, and the asterisk on "up to 60% off*" never explains which items qualify.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the product section sale-only. Show 4 to 6 items with was/now prices and "% off" badges, and cut the full-price Doona, Nuna and NFL blocks from a clearance send.
- 2. Give "new" a concrete meaning, such as a count or a deadline ("Just added 10/29 · ends Sunday").
- 3. Move the credit card, app and design-appointment blocks into a separate send or into a slim footer strip.
- 4. Avoid sending the same "markdowns" angle twice in a day. Fold it into one send, or split by category (furniture one day, baby gear the next).
- 5. Remove or replace seasonal items that have already passed, such as Halloween.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with responsive media queries and MSO conditionals. It has 65 click-tracked links. The main confirmed problems are missing `alt` attributes on content images and a viewport/text-size setup that limits zoom and text scaling. The compliance and authentication findings are inconclusive, because the QA harness could not see the relevant headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link-level issues confirmed.** All 65 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** 5 sequential `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels fire alongside 6 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixels, an `o.gif` open pixel and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` pixel. That is about 13 tracking requests per open. The `mi_u` value is a per-recipient hash carried in image URLs, so it can end up in proxy and image-cache logs.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed, 10 images):**
  - `https://s7d5.scene7.com/.../20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance_PM_v3_100s...` appears to be the hero. If images are blocked, the "100s of new items" offer is invisible.
  - `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`
  - Eight more under `/m/1/`: `f1bdc9e3`, `67c84df5`, `16d802ed`, `5b49ac67`, `59d3a675`, `07e15d43`, `eb8bcaba` and `cb8c3d76` (`.jpg`), plus `57ac4065….gif` and `971aa10f….jpg`.
  - Screen readers will read out filenames or skip these images.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (about 13):** The rlcdn, `mi.`, `dv.` and `o.gif` images should carry `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so assistive tech ignores them. This is low severity.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` declarations and block text scaling in iOS and WebKit.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the declared XHTML doctype and should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times (`telephone=no,address=no,…`, `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). This is harmless but noisy.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd placeholder that shipped in production HTML.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`:** This forces centered paragraphs unless each one is overridden. It is a maintainability risk, not a confirmed bug.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML, so the rest of the body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both were not found. The QA tool notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay stripping headers, so it is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for senders of this volume.
- **Authentication-Results:** This header was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment cannot be verified here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These could not be verified because the supplied HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The visible portion has no confirmable issues, and the QA run did not verify landing pages. The 65 links all go through redirect domains, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are unverified. The hero asset name (`20261101_CBK_Fall_Clearance`) is dated 11/01 and the email arrived 10/29. This is an internal naming convention and not an error.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images, starting with the hero. It should state the offer, for example "100s of new items at up to 60% off".
2. Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels. Consider consolidating the 5 sequential rlcdn pixels and the repeated `mi.` pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations and keep `100%`.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and strip the `[IMPUT HERE…]` template comment.
5. For compliance, re-test against a direct-delivery copy, such as a seed inbox with raw headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Do not treat the current WARNs as failures until then.
6. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a headless GET to confirm destinations and UTM parameters, and re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to check the footer and merge tokens.
