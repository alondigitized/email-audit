# Starts now! Up to 20% off select Babyletto & Namesake furniture!

- It's also the 4th or 5th Crate & Barrel Kids send in about 48 hours. The 11-18 emails ("Our biggest sale of the year starts now! ## What's working

- The hero is clear: a big glider photo, a bold "up to 20% off Babyletto & Namesake" headline and a single "Shop Now" button.
- The three featured products (kiwi stripe glider, abigail gold crib, kiwi ivory bouclé glider) each show sale and regular price. That makes the discount concrete.
- Customer quotes under each product add real social proof.
- The free-shipping banner at the top is a clean, high-contrast value add.
- The "we love these for you" recommendation block extends the furniture theme, and the section order runs hero, products, category grid, recommendations, which is logical.

## What's weak

- The subject promises 20% off, while the body then pushes "up to 50% off Black Friday deals." That dilutes the specific offer.
- The free-shipping banner excludes furniture, which is the email's own subject. That feels like a bait-and-switch.
- Every CTA is a generic "Shop Now," and the 11-18 sends used the same button copy.
- There is no deadline anywhere visible. "Starts now" is not an end date, so urgency is weak.
- The lower third (credit card promo, app download, TheDesignDesk, social links, long legal block) pulls attention away from the furniture offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the offer consistent: pick one headline number and state it the same way in subject, hero and modules. Say "up to 20% off" or lead with the 50% category, not both.
- 2. Fix the free-shipping banner. Either say "free shipping on furniture over $X" or replace it with the furniture discount, so it doesn't contradict the hero.
- 3. Add an end date ("through Sunday") and swap the buttons for specific copy like "Shop gliders" and "Shop cribs."
- 4. Cut the cadence. Fold this into the main Black Friday send, or space it a day apart from the kitchen and playroom emails.
- 5. Move the credit card, app and design-services modules below the recommendations, or drop them from this send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional template with responsive CSS. It has widespread missing `alt` attributes (about 40 flagged images) and several third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The source was truncated, so footer, link and token checks are limited to what was visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing skipped:** 80 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party pixel:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a…&n=1` to `n=5` requests go to LiveRamp's domain. A hashed identifier, `s=56aaa278…`, is passed in the query string.
- **Recipient identifier in URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` is appended to the image URLs `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 occurrences) and `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. It is also in the `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/53f493d9-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` open pixel. The same value as the LiveRamp `s=` suggests a per-recipient ID is shared across vendors.
- **Repeated pixel:** the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested 6 times.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt`:** about 40 `<img>` tags have no `alt` attribute. This includes:
  - content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (e.g. `f9756180-d90b-4a43-ba8b-f03ddd5d87b8.jpg`, `63e2c40c-…jpg`) and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`;
  - the tracking pixels listed above.
  Screen readers will announce filenames or nothing, and with images blocked the content images show no fallback text.
- **Pixels need `alt=""`:** if the tracking pixels have no `alt`, they should carry an explicit empty `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Template leftovers:**
  - the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production;
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared 4 times with conflicting values;
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid in XHTML 1.0.
- **Color scheme:** `color-scheme: light` and `supported-color-schemes: light` are declared, so dark-mode clients are forced to light.
- **Global line-height:** the `*{… line-height:100%}` rule is a broad override that can hurt text legibility.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the truncated source, but the footer and body past the truncation point were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** the automated check warns the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** the same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender uses the aligned subdomain `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is a positive signal but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** these were not visible in the truncated HTML, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** none are visible in the captured source. The 80 skipped links route through tracking redirects (`mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), so final UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.
- **Destination check:** follow one Babyletto/Namesake CTA manually and confirm that the landing page matches the "up to 20% off" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, because the relay may have stripped them.
4. Resolve one or two tracked CTA links with a redirect-following probe to record the final URL, UTM parameters and HTTP status.
5. Consider consolidating the duplicate pixel requests (the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, LiveRamp ×5).
6. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, dedupe the `format-detection` metas, and escape `&` in `<title>`.
7. Fetch the full, untruncated HTML so footer compliance and merge tokens can be checked.
