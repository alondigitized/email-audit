# More added to Labor Day Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- Same beach-at-dusk hero CB2 has run at least three times this stretch, still headlined by the same "UP TO 50% OFF" copy — but this time the subject actually earns its keep: "More added to Labor Day Sale" signals fresh inventory, which is a real, checkable claim rather than another generic urgency push. The category grid below (Furniture, Outdoor, Entertaining, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs) is clean and browsable, and the Double Rewards card is a genuinely different pitch bundled into the same send — good real estate use, if a bit much for one email.
- The problem is the hero doesn't deliver on the subject. "New arrivals added to the sale" would be a stronger, differentiated hero than the fourth "EVEN MORE / UP TO 50% OFF" beach photo — nothing in the visible render shows what's new. A subscriber who's seen the 09-02 and 09-01 sends will recognize this layout instantly and may tune out before reaching the (actually decent) category breakdown.
- Net: a slight step up from the last two 5.00s because the subject is more specific and the category grid gives concrete next steps, but the hero still recycles stale creative that doesn't match its own subject line.

## What's working

- Subject line ("More added to Labor Day Sale") is a specific, checkable claim rather than repeated generic urgency — a change from recent sends.
- The six-tile category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Entertaining, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs) gives clear, browsable next steps with real lifestyle photography.
- Two distinct, unambiguous CTAs ("SHOP THE SALE" appears twice, "LEARN MORE" for rewards) with high contrast against their backgrounds.
- Double Rewards module is visually well-composed — warm bedroom scene, clear "10% → 20%" strikethrough math, credible Sept 9 deadline.
- No render bugs — clean image loads, aligned text, no overlap.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't show or name any new items — subject promises "more added" but the visual delivers the same beach/palm scene and "UP TO 50% OFF" copy CB2 has used since at least 08-31.
- This is the fourth-plus near-identical beach-at-dusk Labor Day hero in this stretch (09-02 "The sale you can't miss," 09-02 "LABOR DAY SALE IS ON," and prior sends) — fatigue risk is real.
- Two unrelated offers (Labor Day sale + Double Rewards credit card push) stacked in one long email dilutes focus; a subscriber scanning quickly may miss the rewards card entirely.
- No urgency/countdown near the hero itself — the only deadline visible ("Now through Sept 9th") is buried in the rewards section, not the main sale.
- No social proof, reviews, or "new arrival" badges anywhere in the grid to substantiate the "more added" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero image/copy to actually show new arrivals (a fresh product shot or "NEW STYLES ADDED" badge) — right now the subject and hero contradict each other, which is the single biggest miss.
- 2. Add a small "NEW" tag or callout to whichever grid tiles feature newly added inventory, so the promise is visible below the fold too.
- 3. Move the Sept 9th deadline up into the hero band — it's currently orphaned in the rewards section and does nothing for the main sale's urgency.
- 4. Consider splitting the Double Rewards credit-card push into its own send — it competes with the sale message rather than reinforcing it.
