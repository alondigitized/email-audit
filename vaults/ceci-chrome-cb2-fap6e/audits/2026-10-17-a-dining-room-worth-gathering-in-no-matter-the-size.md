# A dining room worth gathering in—no matter the size
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a seasonal dining-furniture catalog with a real offer. The top banner reads "the Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off*" over "dining furniture that fits your life," with a "Shop Dining Furniture" button. The subject promises a "dining room worth gathering in—no matter the size," and the body backs it up with five table and seating stories: Lakin, Edendale banquette, Dakota, Basque and Terra. Each has a lifestyle image and its own "Shop …" button.
- The problem is length and focus. After the dining hero there is a category grid, a Design Desk $5000 sweepstakes, a "fall deals edit," an Affirm financing block, a "we love these for you" product grid, an app-download push with a $500 chance, and a second Design Desk block. The dining story is strong, but the email ends as a general Crate & Barrel brand reel.
- Judgement: a solid mid-pack send. It is better matched to its subject than several recent sends, such as the toddler email that opened on a different sale banner. It buries a clear, seasonal message under too many extra modules.

## What's working

- The Thanksgiving "up to 30% off*" banner gives one concrete, season-appropriate offer at the very top.
- Each dining product has its own named CTA, such as "Shop Lakin Table" and "Shop Dining Chairs." That is better than a single generic "Shop Now."
- Badges like "bestselling" and "new" give each module a quick reason to care.
- The lifestyle photography shows tables in real rooms and makes the "no matter the size" idea believable.
- The subject and hero are consistent. Unlike the 2026-10-16 toddler email, the opening banner doesn't contradict the subject.

## What's weak

- The email is very long. It runs through six or more promos after the dining content, including sweepstakes, financing, app and a repeat Design Desk block.
- The "up to 30% off*" asterisk and the layered sweepstakes, Affirm, "fall deals" and app offers dilute the single offer.
- The subject says "no matter the size," but the hero doesn't show how to choose by room size, such as a small-space or seating-capacity guide.
- Cadence is heavy. This is one of at least six Crate & Barrel sends in two days (10/15–10/16), so it competes with its own sibling emails.
- The Design Desk is pushed twice, and the "fall deals" and app modules read as filler after the Thanksgiving story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the dining modules except one footer CTA. Keep the five hero tables, the chairs and one Design Desk block. Drop Affirm, the app push and the product grid.
- 2. Make "no matter the size" visible. Add a simple size selector near the top, such as "seats 4 / 6 / 8+" or "small-space tables."
- 3. Put the offer end date in the banner next to "up to 30% off*," for example "ends Sunday."
- 4. Show prices or "from $X" under each table CTA so shoppers can qualify before clicking.
- 5. Pick one sweepstakes or one financing message, not both.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Seats 4 or 8: dining tables up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Thanksgiving table, sized to your room`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extendable tables, banquettes and chairs, all up to 30% off through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Small space or big crowd, find the table that fits. Bestsellers inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with Movable Ink dynamic content and third-party pixels. The automated QA found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes and zoom/text-resize restrictions. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be completed because the relay stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 71 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so no HTTP probe was run. I can't confirm any destination resolves, and the truncated source doesn't let me inspect the `href`s.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp) fires five times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (Movable Ink) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1d45dad2-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
  - All of these are 1×1 trackers with no `alt`. They are intentional, but they should carry `alt=""`.
- **Identifier in URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa278…` value is repeated across the Movable Ink image URLs (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`). It is a per-recipient hash in the query string and could end up in proxy or cache logs. This is a design observation, not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA (about 40 images):**
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|png|gif`. Examples: `56be7a63-ec91-4fed-b133-e9563a2fbb5f.jpg`, `e92eca67-f059-4912-ae8d-db3c02b187f8.gif`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - Seven Movable Ink render images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the email is unreadable if these carry the content. The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from `alt=""`, so some of these could be decorative.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resize disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the global `*{}` rule and in `html` and `body`. The `*{… line-height:100%}` rule also affects readability.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype (should be `&amp;`).
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times and `charset` twice.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the source.
- **Conflicting image rules:** the `min-width:768px` rule sets `img{max-width:600px}` and the `min-width:640px` rule sets `img{max-width:640px}`. The later rule wins at every width of 640px and up, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Positive:** `lang="en"`, the VML/MSO namespaces and the Outlook `PixelsPerInch` fix are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The Movable Ink `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters are populated. I could not review the body past the truncation point.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe is not found.** The QA flags that the relay may not capture it. Treat this as unverified, not a sender violation.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) is not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect it.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated subdomain.
- **Not verifiable:** the footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are outside the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
The truncated source and skipped link probes mean I can't confirm UTM parameters, landing-page destinations or alignment with the "dining room" subject. This can't be assessed; there are no confirmed issues.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the Movable Ink renders. Add `alt=""` to the pixels (`rlcdn.com`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations.
