## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: "Huge news: NEW ITEMS hit our Black Friday sale!"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email renders from a Movable Ink (MI) / Responsys-style table template with 81 redirect-tracked links. It has widespread missing alt text on images, a zoom-blocking viewport meta, and some template hygiene leftovers. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped the headers, so compliance and link destinations could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified.** QA skipped 81 links as tracking or click-redirect domains, so no HTTP probe ran. Dead links, redirect chains and landing-page status are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five identical LiveRamp beacons (`https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`) fire on open. They use only an `n` counter to differ, so this is likely redundant firing. Also present:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3a799b5c-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Subscriber ID in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` token appears in every MI `rp/*.png` and `o.gif` URL. It is a persistent subscriber identifier, so forwarded or cached copies carry it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed).** QA flagged 39 images with no `alt` attribute (the list may be truncated). They fall into these groups:
  - 24 content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1|47/*.jpg|png|gif`
  - 7 personalized MI images, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`
  - 5 `rlcdn` and 2 tracking pixels
  - These images carry the product and offer content, so images-off clients and screen readers get nothing for them.
  - Tracking pixels should have `alt=""` and `width/height=1`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Conflicting media queries.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both match at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Template leftovers.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - There is both an XHTML 1.0 DOCTYPE and an HTML5 `<meta charset>`.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` is invalid under the XHTML DOCTYPE. It is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible HTML. The personalized MI `rp/*.png` images carry `mi_u` and could not be checked for correct content. They have no alt text fallback if they fail to load.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing them. It is a capture gap, not confirmed absence. Mail from this sender to Gmail/Yahoo would normally need them.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same reason.
- **Footer unverified.** The HTML was truncated, so the visible unsubscribe link and postal address in the footer could not be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be confirmed. The 81 links all go through redirect domains and were never resolved, so final URLs and campaign parameters are unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 24 content images and the 7 MI product images. Use `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions on tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Collapse the five identical `rlcdn` beacons into one, and consider whether `mi_u` needs to appear in image URLs.
4. Remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the leftover placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, and reorder or merge the overlapping `img` media queries.
5. For this audit pipeline, resolve the redirect links (follow with HEAD/GET) to check final URLs and UTMs. Capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) from the original message rather than the relay copy. Run QA on the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer.
