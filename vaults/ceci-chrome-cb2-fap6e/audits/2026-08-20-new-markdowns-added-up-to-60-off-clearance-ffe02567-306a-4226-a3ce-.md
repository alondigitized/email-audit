# NEW MARKDOWNS ADDED: Up to 60% off clearance
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance email that immediately buries its own lead. The hero is genuinely strong — clean beach shot, "new markdowns added! up to 60% off clearance*" with a single "Shop Now" button — but then the email spends the next eight rows selling a Clean Kitchen icon, a JURA espresso machine, the fall table collection, product picks, and a credit card, before circling back to nothing else about the actual clearance sale. Anyone opening for the 60% off headline scrolls through a full-price merchandising catalog to find it.
- Compare this to the 2026-08-19 Cotswolds send (7/10), which stayed on-concept top to bottom. This one has a great subject and hero, then drifts into "everything but the kitchen sink" territory — the same failure mode flagged in the 2026-08-18 baking collab writeup. The clearance category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) is genuinely useful and well laid out — that part should have been most of the email, not one section of many.
- Verdict: strong open hook, weak follow-through. Fine as a clearance nudge, but it undersells its own best asset.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: bold "up to 60% off clearance*" headline over a clean lifestyle image with one clear "Shop Now" CTA.
- The 8-tile clearance category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, etc.) is well-organized and gives shoppers a fast way to jump to what they want.
- "Shop All Clearance" button caps the category grid, reinforcing the primary action right where the eye lands after browsing categories.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text is legible, layout is clean throughout.

## What's weak

- The email pivots hard away from clearance after the grid — Clean Kitchen icon, JURA espresso machine, fall tablescape, and "we love these for you" picks are all full-price, non-clearance content diluting the sale message.
- No specific dollar or percent markdowns shown for any actual product — everything is "up to 60%" with an asterisk, no concrete examples.
- By the time the credit card pitch and app download modules appear, the clearance offer is long forgotten — poor use of scroll real estate for what's billed as a markdown email.
- The asterisk on "60% off clearance*" has no visible disclosure nearby, reading as exclusion bait.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the JURA espresso, Clean Kitchen, and fall table modules from this specific send — they don't belong in a clearance email and push the actual offer further down. Move the clearance grid up right under the hero and let it carry more of the email.
- 2. Add 2-3 actual clearance products with struck-through original prices and sale prices directly under the hero, so the "up to 60%" claim has visible proof.
- 3. Resolve the asterisk — either drop it or add a one-line disclosure near the headline (e.g., "select styles, while supplies last").
- 4. Keep the category grid and "Shop All Clearance" — that structure is good, just needs to be closer to the top and the primary focus.
