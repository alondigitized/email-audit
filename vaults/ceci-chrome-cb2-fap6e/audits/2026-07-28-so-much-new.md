# SO MUCH NEW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-28**
## Executive summary

- CB2 splits the difference between last week's two extremes and, in doing so, muddies both. The top half is a clean new-arrivals showcase — sofa, dining table, pendant lights, vase, rugs, each with its own named product and CTA, echoing the well-received 07-27 "Just in. Already trending." send. But then, instead of stopping at "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS," the email tacks on the exact same Furniture Event (40% off) and Bedroom + Bath Event (30% off) banners that have been running since 07-23/07-24 — now with "ENDS TOMORROW" urgency that's genuinely useful information buried five product blocks deep.
- The subject, "SO MUCH NEW," oversells what's actually a mixed-bag email — half new arrivals, half recycled discount blast. A subscriber who opens expecting fresh product discovery has to scroll past two sale banners with real urgency to get there, or vice versa. It's not broken, just unfocused: two good ideas (showcase the new stuff; remind people the sale ends tomorrow) competing for the same send instead of either getting a clean shot.
- Net: solid execution, generic subject, structurally diluted message. Middle of the pack for this brand.

## What's working

- Each new-arrival product (Aldine Sofa, Rio Dining Table, Toya Pendant, Ariana Vase) gets its own image, designer credit, and dedicated CTA — not a generic homepage dump.
- "ENDS TOMORROW" on the Furniture Event banner is real, credible urgency — the kind that should be driving the whole email, not buried at scroll position 8.
- Clean render throughout: no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent warm wood/neutral photography.
- Loyalty and rewards messaging (CB2 App, "Earn 10% Back") plus a review-sweepstakes CTA round out the bottom without feeling like clutter.

## What's weak

- The subject "SO MUCH NEW" promises a new-arrivals email, but roughly half the body is the same recycled Furniture Event / Bedroom + Bath Event creative already sent on 07-23 and 07-24 — no fresh copy on the offers themselves.
- The one thing with real time pressure — 40% off ending tomorrow — is the eighth visual block down, well past where most recipients stop scrolling.
- No pricing shown anywhere in the new-arrivals section (Aldine Sofa, Rio Dining Table, etc.) — only the "More For You" grid at the very bottom has dollar figures, so the hero products give zero purchase-decision signal.
- This is CB2's sixth-plus email in about a week per the brand history — cadence fatigue is real, and a generically-titled send like this one doesn't earn its slot.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two emails or pick one hero message. If the point is new arrivals, drop the recycled sale banners entirely and let "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" be the single CTA. If the point is the sale ending tomorrow, lead with that — urgency copy converts, and it's currently buried.
- 2. If keeping both, move "ENDS TOMORROW — 40% OFF" into the hero slot (above the new-arrivals grid), since deadline urgency outperforms generic "new" framing for opens-to-clicks.
- 3. Add prices to the new-arrival hero products — a $-blank sofa or dining table asks the reader to click just to find out if it's in budget, which is unnecessary friction.
- 4. Tighten the subject to name a real hook — a specific category or the sale deadline — instead of the brand-generic "so much new."
