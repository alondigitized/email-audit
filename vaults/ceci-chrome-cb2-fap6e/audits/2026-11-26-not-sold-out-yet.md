# NOT SOLD OUT YET
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- The subject "NOT SOLD OUT YET" promises scarcity, but the body doesn't deliver it. The email is CB2's standard Black Friday template: a moody hero with "BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 60% OFF" and a SHOP NOW button. Nothing in the body mentions inventory, low stock or what is still available. The only fresh element is a strip reading "EXTENDED STORE HOURS, FRIDAY 11/27: 9AM – 9PM".
- The category grid is a real strength. Furniture, Holiday & Gifting, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors and Entertaining are each shown with a large photo. But the tiles carry no prices, no per-category discounts and no visible individual buttons. "DON'T MIND IF YOU DO" is a clever header that doesn't connect to the subject. The email then stacks two more offers, free shipping on $99+ and 15% off in the new year, each with its own button, so the hero offer loses focus.
- This is CB2's sixth or seventh "up to 60% off" send in about five days, following "100+ more reasons to shop Black Friday" and "THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP". It is polished but repetitive, and the subject over-promises. I'd call it a competent, forgettable 6.

## What's working

- The hero is clean and on-brand: a cocktail glass and decanter, with "UP TO 60% OFF" clear above a SHOP NOW button.
- The "EXTENDED STORE HOURS" strip adds a concrete, time-bound reason to act for local shoppers.
- The six large category tiles with bold labels are easy to scan and show the full range.
- Free shipping on $99+ and 15% off in the new year each get their own clearly buttoned module.
- The footer has app download, rewards card, gift registry and text signup. These are useful loyalty hooks.

## What's weak

- The subject's scarcity promise is never paid off. Nothing in the render says what is or isn't selling out.
- No product, price or per-category discount appears anywhere. Everything is "up to 60%" with no proof.
- Three offers compete: 60% off, free shipping and 15% off later. The 15% is a future-dated offer that dilutes the urgency.
- The category tiles have no visible individual CTA buttons, so the click path is unclear. The only buttons are in the hero and the two offer modules.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense and long, and the "up to" qualifiers add friction to an otherwise honest offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pay off the subject. Add a "Going fast" module with 4–6 real products, prices and low-stock tags, or change the subject to match the body.
- 2. Replace the generic category tiles with category-specific deals, for example "Rugs up to 60% off", and add a button to each.
- 3. Cut the new-year 15% module on the final day, or move it below the fold. Keep one clear offer.
- 4. Lead with the extended-hours line as a real deadline ("Ends Monday" or "Open 9AM–9PM tomorrow").
- **Subject Alt A:** `Best sellers are going fast`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 60% off, stores open 9–9 tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, lighting and sofas are moving quickly. Shop before your favorites go.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extended hours tomorrow, plus free shipping on $99+.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML table layout with `lang="en"` and a preheader-style structure, but it has 11 insecure (`http://`) Dotomi tracking images and about 24 images without alt text. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, because the headers were not captured by the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so the final destinations and their HTTP status are unverified.
- **11 tracking pixels use plain `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content may drop these. Several clients proxy images over HTTPS and may fail to fetch them.
  - Ten cookie-redirect pixels fire on every open. That is a tracking and privacy footprint rather than a functional fault, but it adds load for no rendering benefit.
- **Open-tracking pixels** are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b08e0261-...?mi_cid=...`. Both are HTTPS and work as intended.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 11 content images.** All are hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `978d7bb3-...jpg`
  - `329aeda8-...jpg`
  - `487aa691-...jpg`
  - `70950067-...jpg`
  - `1eec0fdf-...jpg`
  - `e032611a-...jpg`
  - `0b0ad34d-...jpg`
  - `34f06fa0-...jpg`
  - `a94bb0d5-...jpg`
  - `e68a523c-...jpg`
  - `20185973-...jpg`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, these give no text equivalent.
- **Tracking pixels have no alt attribute.** This covers the 11 Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. Add `alt=""` and a 1×1 size so they are treated as decorative.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom on supporting clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in `<head>`. It is harmless but shows an unfinished template.
- **Font URLs.** `@font-face` sources point to `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` (double slash) and to `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`. This works only if the origin normalizes the path, and it creates a cross-domain dependency. Most clients ignore web fonts anyway, so the fallback stack is what matters.
- No other rendering defects were evident in the truncated source. The MSO conditionals, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and the `@media (max-width: 418px)` rules are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens or template syntax (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` parameter on the open pixel is a 64-character hex value, which looks like a hashed identifier.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the relay may strip them. Treat this as unverified rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check them against the raw original message.
- **Authentication-Results was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice, but alignment is unconfirmed.
- The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so they cannot be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 61 links go through click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified. Resolve the redirect chains in a follow-up probe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-redirect pixels if they are not needed.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw message headers, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. Follow the 61 redirect links to their final URLs to check status codes and UTM continuity.
6. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment and the double-slash font paths.
