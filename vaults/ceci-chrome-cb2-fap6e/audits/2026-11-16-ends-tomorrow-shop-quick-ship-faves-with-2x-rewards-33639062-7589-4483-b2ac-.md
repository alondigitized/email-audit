The compliance and authentication warnings are probably artifacts of capture rather than real defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Tracking beacons are present:**

  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`

  The LiveRamp beacons add third-party requests and a privacy and deliverability surface. They are flagged only because they lack `alt` (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 50 entries.** Examples:
  - `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/cb526cf0-1b08-4fe3-b985-9eb76106162a.jpg`
  - `.../m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `.../m/1/0782bf25-0357-43a5-8ed2-cca28dcd0d82.gif`
  - `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/10%5FKids/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FCozyWinterEdit/…TertiaryVideo`
  - `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (appears twice)
  
  The flagged filenames are bare GUIDs or file names, so they look like content or product imagery with no alt. With images blocked, those blocks render empty. For a screen reader this means unlabeled images or a read-out of the filename.
- **Tracking pixels and spacers need `alt=""`.** The `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` beacons and the scene7 spacers are decorative. They should carry an explicit empty `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta is `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup in the head** (XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype):
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The title is generic and does not match the subject line.
  - Duplicate `<meta charset>` and `http-equiv Content-Type` declarations.
  - Redundant `format-detection` metas (the `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` combined tag is repeated as separate tags).
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, ships in production.
- `lang="en"` is present.
- The HTML source was truncated, so body-level checks cannot be confirmed. These include table roles, heading structure, link text and the bulletproof-button VML fallbacks.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens are visible in the available source. The per-recipient `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on the beacon URLs look like intentional identifiers.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found** (WARN). The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found** (WARN). The same caveat applies. If the header is genuinely absent, one-click unsubscribe is not supported. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements expect this header.
- **Authentication-Results not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be determined through the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is consistent with a properly aligned ESP setup, but alignment is unverified.
- **Footer checks could not be done.** The truncated source does not show the footer, so a visible unsubscribe link and a physical postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be confirmed. All 88 links go through tracking-redirect domains and were not resolved, so final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are not observable. No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all product and content images, and `alt=""` to spacers and tracking pixels (the `rlcdn.com`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` URLs).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the ampersand in `<title>`, make the title match the subject line, drop the duplicate charset and format-detection metas, and strip the `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
4. Re-capture the message with raw headers, bypassing the AgentMail relay. This would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` (SPF, DKIM, DMARC).
5. Extend QA to follow redirect chains on a sample of the 88 tracked links. This would verify final destinations, status codes and UTM presence. Also fetch the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer.
