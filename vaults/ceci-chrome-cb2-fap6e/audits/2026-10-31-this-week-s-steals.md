# THIS WEEK’S STEALS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a tidy roundup of everything CB2 is running right now, and it adds almost nothing new. Five full-bleed photo modules stack under a "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" header: Guest Ready Sale (up to 40% off), Warehouse Sale (up to 60% off), free shipping on 1,000+ items, an app-download sweepstakes ($1,000), and a "Not sold out, yet" back-in-stock tile. Every module has a thin underlined text link instead of a button. The photography is moody and on-brand, but no single offer clearly leads.
- The subject promises "steals" and the body shows the same two sales that CB2 sent on 10/28, 10/29 and 10/30. A subscriber who has seen "UP TO 60% OFF*" three times this week gets no new reason to open. The consolidation is useful as a recap, but it doesn't earn a click.
- Verdict: a competent recap with weak CTAs and no hero. It's fine as a catch-all, but it would not move anyone who has already ignored the earlier sends.

## What's working

- Each module is a single image with one headline and one link, so it scans quickly.
- The "UP TO 60% OFF" and "UP TO 40% OFF" headlines are large and legible, and the price claims are easy to find.
- The photography is consistent and warm (plum, brown, cream), so the brand voice holds across all five modules.
- The "NOT SOLD OUT, YET" travertine tile adds a bit of scarcity and a specific product.
- The footer gathers the app, the rewards card, gifting and the SMS offer in one place, with the sale fine print below.

## What's weak

- All the CTAs ("SHOP NOW", "DOWNLOAD THE CB2 APP", "SHOP BACK IN STOCK") are small underlined text links over photos, so they are easy to miss and awkward to tap on mobile.
- No module is the clear hero. The two sales and free shipping have equal weight, and the sweepstakes sits in the middle of them.
- The asterisk and dagger on the 60% claim point to dense fine print at the bottom, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- The email is very long, about five screens of image, and the sweepstakes and back-in-stock tiles dilute the sale message.
- It repeats the 60% and 40% offers from the 10/28–10/30 sends. Nothing here is new.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero for the top, likely the Warehouse Sale at 60% off, and use a real filled button for it. Demote the other modules to a compact row.
- 2. Replace the text links with high-contrast buttons that name the category, such as "Shop Warehouse Deals".
- 3. Show products with prices ("Sofas from $X") instead of mood photography alone.
- 4. Cut the sweepstakes tile or move it to the footer. It competes with the sales.
- 5. Put a deadline on the offer so the urgency is credible.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on 1,000+ items and a few pieces still in stock.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, and no critical failures were detected. The main confirmed issues are 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, missing `alt` attributes, and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The three header warnings (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) probably come from the relay, so they are unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 58 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect chains, dead links and final destinations are unchecked.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) beacons load over plain `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Clients that block non-HTTPS content (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may suppress them. Any that load create mixed-content exposure.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** The ten `pub*.php` `cookieredir` calls are ad-network cookie syncs, not email-measurement pixels. Most email clients won't set or read cookies from them, so they add weight and third-party calls with little value.
- **First-party beacons:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1ade0493-...?mi_cid=...` load over HTTPS. Both lack `alt`.
- **Cross-brand font URLs:** The `@font-face` rules load `HNW05` from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`. That is a sister-brand domain, not CB2's. Several CB2 font URLs also contain a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`), which may 404 or redirect depending on server config.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Missing `alt` on content images:** Four images from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`:
  - `aa782bc4-bb3c-47f9-a6b0-30406e50dc91.jpg`
  - `51d4e983-0e02-4975-a832-9ec303d902d7.jpg`
  - `9ab35108-8f86-41b3-bf89-b0718c6e6d0c.jpg`
  - `e6aedbde-f3f3-4eb0-9433-622bb75ab68e.jpg`
  
  If they are product or promo imagery, image-blocked clients and screen readers get no fallback. If they are decorative, `alt=""` is needed.
- **Pixels without `alt`:** The 13 tracking pixels also lack `alt`. This is low severity, but `alt=""` is the standard fix.
- **Template residue:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block are left in the source. They are harmless but show an unfinished template.
- **Title:** `<title>` is the generic "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME" and does not match the subject line.
- **Minor markup:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML doctype.
- **Good practice found:** `lang="en"`, a UTF-8 charset declaration, MSO/VML namespaces, and mobile breakpoint classes (`max-width: 418px`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the body and footer could not be fully checked. The `mi_u=` hash in the `o.gif` URL indicates subscriber-level tracking is resolving.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Both are flagged as not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them. This can't be confirmed from the data, so it needs the raw `.eml` headers. If the headers really are absent, the email fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements, since CB2 is clearly a high-volume sender.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown for the same reason. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the From address `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** These are not in the truncated source, so they are not verifiable here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 58 tracking links were skipped and the truncated source shows no destination URLs, so UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers on the beacons suggest the campaign is tracked through the ESP's own redirect layer, not UTMs on the destination URLs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Retrieve the full `.eml` and check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and `Authentication-Results` directly. Do this before treating the three WARNs as real.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the `pub1–10.php` cookie-sync calls. They add no value in email clients.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the four content images and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Resolve the 58 tracking links with a redirect-following probe, and capture the final URLs and UTM parameters for the continuity check.
6. Fix the `//Styles/` double-slash font URLs and stop loading fonts from `crateandbarrel.com`.
