# APP EXCLUSIVE: NEW COLLAB JUST DROPPED + the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event ENDS TONIGHT
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This email has two jobs and does neither well. The subject leads with "APP EXCLUSIVE: NEW COLLAB JUST DROPPED," but the top of the body is the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event banner ("FINAL HOURS," "UP TO 25% OFF," "SHOP THE SALE"). The collab sits second, as a phone mockup for "Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz." The one deadline in the email, the sale ending tonight, is only in the subject and the small "FINAL HOURS" label. The collab block has no deadline at all.
- The collab block has a clear idea: a celebrity name, a phone mockup and an App Store badge. But the pitch is soft. The copy is "Where art meets comfort, function meets aesthetics," and the only CTA is "DOWNLOAD THE CB2 APP." No product from the collection is shown, so the click asks for an app install with no preview of what you'd get. The lower half is the usual CB2 filler: category tiles, rewards and app footer. It reads like the same kitchen-sink send from the past week (Oct 11–13), minus the countdown timer.
- Judgement: a decent sale-ending send held back by a muddled hierarchy. A render bug in the "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS AND MORE / BEST SELLER" header makes it look less polished.

## What's working

- The "FINAL HOURS / UP TO 25% OFF / SHOP THE SALE" banner is a clear, specific offer with a visible button at the very top.
- The subject names the sale ending tonight, which is credible and time-bounded.
- The Swizz Beatz × CB2 collab with the phone mockup is a distinct news hook, different from the recurring sale messaging.
- The black CTA buttons are bold and legible, and the photography is consistent with the CB2 brand.
- The footer ties together the app, the 10% rewards offer and the 15% text sign-up, so loyalty benefits are visible.

## What's weak

- **Render bug:** the "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS AND MORE" header collides with "BEST SELLER." The words overlap and "ARRIVALS, AND MORE" is jammed to the right, so the heading is hard to read.
- The collab sits below the sale banner even though the subject leads with it. The email and the subject disagree about the priority.
- The collab CTA ("DOWNLOAD THE CB2 APP") asks for an install with no product preview and no explanation of what is exclusive.
- The copy ("Where art meets comfort, function meets aesthetics...") is vague and shows no price, product or collection details.
- The subject is 83 characters and will truncate on mobile. It stacks two messages, so neither one lands.
- The lower half is a generic category grid with no offer attached, which dilutes the urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one lead. The cleanest version is to lead with tonight's sale ending and move the collab to a single secondary block. If the collab is the lead, show 2–3 actual collab products with prices and an app-only angle.
- 2. Fix the "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS AND MORE / BEST SELLER" heading overlap.
- 3. Rewrite the collab CTA to say what you get ("Shop Casa Dean in the app") and add a deadline or scarcity if one exists.
- 4. Cut the category grid to the sale-relevant tiles (upholstery) so the urgency carries through the whole email.
- 5. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Upholstery event ends tonight: up to 25% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz, first in the app`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Last chance on sofas, sectionals and chairs, plus a first look at our new collab.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get early access to Casa Dean in the CB2 app before the sale ends at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, leaked into the output. It also has about 20 images with no alt text, 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The QA run passed 43% of checks. Several compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the AgentMail relay doesn't capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Skipped probes:** 62 tracking links were not checked because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (11):** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` are all non-HTTPS. Clients and proxies may block or rewrite them, which causes mixed-content warnings and lost tracking. These are third-party cookie-sync pixels, and the 10 `pub*.php` calls add load and privacy exposure.
- **Other tracking pixels (HTTPS):** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/6a70fca6-30e6-459e-8dcd-c7046f791d5b?mi_cid=…` are fine on protocol. Both lack alt text (see section 3).
