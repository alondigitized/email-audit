# Up to 60% off clearance—and new items just got added!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance email that buries clearance. The subject promises "up to 60% off clearance—and new items just got added," and the hero even delivers ("new markdowns added! up to 60% off clearance") with a clean beach-scene image and a category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding). Good so far — that's the strongest section of the email and it's front-loaded correctly.
- Then it falls apart. After "Shop All Clearance," the email pivots hard into three unrelated campaigns stacked on top of each other: an NFL game-day collection, a dining-room lifestyle push with specific SKUs, and a credit-card pitch. None of these connect to the clearance premise the subject line sold. By the time you hit the dining furniture cross-sell, this reads like three different emails spliced together, and the clearance offer that got you to open is long gone from view.
- Compared to Crate & Barrel's other recent sends — the rug guide (2026-08-22, 6.00/10) and nursery chair story (2026-08-22, 6.00/10) — this one at least opens strong and on-brief, which those single-theme emails also managed. But this send's identity crisis after the fold is a real regression in focus. Judgement: solid open, weak follow-through — the clearance shopper gets one screen of value and then has to wade through unrelated pitches.

## What's working

- Hero image and headline match the subject line exactly — "up to 60% off clearance" appears both places, no bait-and-switch.
- Clean, unambiguous "Shop Now" and "Shop All Clearance" CTAs directly under the offer.
- The 8-tile category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) gives clearance shoppers a fast way to jump to their category — no render bugs, all tiles load cleanly.
- Product imagery throughout is sharp and on-brand (neutral tones, real room settings).

## What's weak

- The email abandons the clearance theme entirely after the category grid — NFL game day, dining room lifestyle, and a credit-card offer all compete for attention with no thematic thread back to "60% off."
- Priced dining SKUs ($1799, $2295, $2199, $749, $2699, $1999) sit right after a discount-driven hero, which undercuts the urgency — none of them show a markdown, so the eye that just saw "60% off" lands on full-price furniture.
- Three distinct offers before any brand-consistent footer makes this feel like a catalog dump rather than a targeted send — cadence-wise, this reads like a "let's clear the backlog of pending campaigns into one email" send.
- The "new! the card you love just got better" module drops a credit-card pitch into a clearance email with zero connective tissue — jarring context switch for someone who opened for a discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to clearance + one adjacent module (e.g., new arrivals), and move the NFL, dining-room lifestyle, and credit-card content to their own sends — this email is trying to be four emails at once.
- 2. If the dining furniture module stays, show sale pricing or drop it — full-price SKUs directly below a 60%-off hero actively work against the offer.
- 3. Move "new items just got added" (from the subject) into a visible second module right after the clearance grid, since the subject promises it but the body never calls it out explicitly.
- 4. Tighten the preheader to reinforce urgency/scarcity on the clearance offer instead of leaving it generic.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just dropped again — up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns, new arrivals: up to 60% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns across every room, while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, decor, and rugs — all newly discounted.`
