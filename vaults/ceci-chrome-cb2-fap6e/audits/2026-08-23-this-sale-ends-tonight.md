# This sale ends tonight
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-23**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 running two sales in one email and letting them blur into each other. The header says "THIS WEEK'S STEALS," the hero panel is "LAST CHANCE / END OF SUMMER SALE," and right below it a second full-bleed panel pushes "UP TO 50% OFF OUTDOOR" — two distinct promotions stacked with no clear relationship, and the subject line's "ends tonight" urgency only applies to one of them (the fine print shows the furniture sale ends Aug 23 but the outdoor sale runs through Sept 9, 2026).
- Visually this is solid CB2 work — moody, warm-toned lifestyle photography, clean serif type, generous white space, a legible "SHOP NOW" button on the hero. It's a clear step up from the in-store-only misfire on 2026-08-20 ("FLOOR SAMPLE SALE," 4/10) and sits in the same competent-but-unremarkable band as the 2026-08-22 "Ends Tomorrow" send (5/10), which used nearly identical last-chance framing just yesterday.
- The real problem is message discipline: back-to-back "ends tomorrow" (8/22) and "ends tonight" (8/23) emails train a subscriber to stop believing the deadline, and bundling a second, later-dated sale under the same "last chance" banner undercuts the urgency of both. Solid craft, muddled offer.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero photography with strong warm lighting and a clear product vignette (console table, mirror, ottomans).
- "SHOP NOW" and "OUTDOOR" CTAs are both legible, high-contrast, and easy to tap.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is rendered in large type directly over the second hero image — impossible to miss.
- Footer utility block (app download, rewards, gifting, SMS signup) is well-organized and doesn't compete with the sale messaging.
- No layout breakage, broken images, or overlapping text anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Two different sales ("End of Summer Sale" and "Outdoor up to 50% off") with two different end dates are jammed into one email under one "LAST CHANCE" banner — confusing which deadline applies to what.
- The subject's "ends tonight" urgency isn't visible anywhere in the rendered body copy — a shopper scanning the email sees no countdown or date, only vague "LAST CHANCE."
- This is back-to-back "last chance" framing after yesterday's near-identical "Ends Tomorrow" send (2026-08-22) — the deadline is losing credibility.
- CTA copy is generic ("SHOP NOW") rather than telling the reader what they're actually saving on.
- No single hero product or price callout — it's a category/mood shot, not a specific item a shopper can evaluate before clicking.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split into two focused sends (or clearly subordinate one offer) — don't let "ends tonight" urgency get diluted by a second promo with a September deadline.
- 2. Put the actual deadline in the body copy near the hero, not buried in the footer fine print — "ENDS TONIGHT AT MIDNIGHT" over the hero would match the subject's urgency.
- 3. Swap "SHOP NOW" for a specific CTA tied to the discount, e.g. "SHOP UP TO 40% OFF."
- 4. Feature one hero SKU with a visible price/strike-through instead of a mood shot, so the click has a concrete reason.
- 5. Space out "last chance" sends — this is the second in two days, which erodes the very urgency it's trying to create.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: up to 40% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Hours left — Summer Sale + 50% off outdoor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, decor and outdoor markdowns end tonight at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Two sales, one deadline — shop before it's gone.`
