# Just in: 300+ new rugs
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel running the "shoppable guide" format — "new! the rug guide" — and it's a smart structural choice for a category as fit-dependent as rugs: room-by-room breakdowns (living, dining, bedroom, kids) each with a "designer tip," followed by a bestseller spotlight and a "rug shopping made easy" explainer block (swatch, design desk, room visualizer). It's genuinely useful content, well organized, and on-brand for a company selling into a considered-purchase category.
- The problem is the subject line oversells what the body delivers. "Just in: 300+ new rugs" promises new arrivals and a big number, but the hero says "new! the rug guide" — a guide, not a drop. That's a bait-and-switch on open, even if mild. The email is also enormous — four full room sections plus a bestseller module plus a "how to shop" module plus a 25%-off bedroom callout plus a separate clearance banner plus a "new fall table" plug plus a product carousel. By the time a reader hits the actual discount (25% off bedroom refresh, buried two-thirds down) they've scrolled past six other CTAs, none of which is the hero offer.
- Verdict: strong content, weak architecture — this reads like three emails stitched into one, and the actual "just in" rug news never clearly shows up.

## What's working

- The room-by-room guide format (living/dining/bedroom/kids) is genuinely differentiated content, not just a product grid.
- "Designer Tip" and "Tip" callouts under each room section add credibility and a reason to read rather than skim.
- The "rug shopping made easy" module (swatch, design desk, room visualizer) addresses real purchase friction for a considered category like rugs.
- Consistent, calm visual style — muted earth tones, clean product photography, no render bugs or broken images visible.
- "bestseller spotlight: Monaco collection" gives a clear, specific hero product rather than a vague "shop now."

## What's weak

- Subject promises "300+ new rugs" but the hero says "the rug guide" — no visible count, no "new arrivals" grid up top; the numeric hook isn't paid off where the eye lands first.
- The email is too long and multi-purpose: rug guide → bestseller → shopping tips → 25% off bedroom → separate clearance banner → fall table → "we love these for you" grid. That's at least four different campaigns in one send.
- The one concrete discount ("up to 25% off your bedroom refresh") is roughly two-thirds down the scroll, well past where most readers on mobile will have bailed.
- No single dominant CTA — nearly every section has its own "Shop Now," so visual hierarchy never resolves to one primary action.
- The "new markdowns added up to 60% off clearance" banner competes directly with the 25%-off bedroom offer just above it, diluting both.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to match the actual hero content (the guide), or restructure the hero to actually show new rug arrivals with a count — right now the two don't agree. Pick one and commit.
- 2. Move the 25%-off bedroom offer (or whichever single discount is strongest) into the hero position, above the guide content — an educational guide is a great mid-email module, not the best opener when a live discount exists.
- 3. Cut this to one campaign per send. Split the clearance/markdown banner and the "new fall table" plug into separate sends — they have nothing to do with rugs and are diluting this one's focus.
- 4. Consolidate the four room-CTAs into fewer, more deliberate jump points (e.g., one "Shop by Room" nav bar near the top) instead of a "Shop Now" after every section.
- 5. Add a real number or new-arrival count near the hero if the subject is going to lead with "300+" — right now that specific, scroll-stopping stat is invisible in the render.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New rug guide: find your fit, room by room`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Living, dining, bedroom, kids — plus a designer's picks for each.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From swatch to install: everything you need to choose the right rug.`
