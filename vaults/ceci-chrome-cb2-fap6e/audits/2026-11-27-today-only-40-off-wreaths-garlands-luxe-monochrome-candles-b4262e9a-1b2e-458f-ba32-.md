# TODAY ONLY! 40% off wreaths, garlands & luxe Monochrome candles!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's sixth or seventh Black Friday send today, and the subject doesn't match the body. The subject sells "40% off wreaths, garlands & luxe Monochrome candles." The hero says "Biggest sale of the year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + free shipping" over a red ribbon image. The wreath and candle deal only appears as two small tiles ("hours left: 40% off wreaths & garlands" and "hours left: 40% off monochrome candles") just under the hero. A reader who opens for the decor deal sees a generic catalog dump first.
- Below that it's the same long Black Friday template as earlier sends, such as "Biggest sale of the year! Open for up to 50% off Black Friday!" It has a category grid, a kitchen block, a Gift Shop banner, "best kitchen gifts," a product row, a Crate Card promo, an app download and The DesignDesk. The tiles are well shot and the "hours left" urgency is credible. For a home-furniture reader, though, the furniture and sofa tiles are buried in the grid. Verdict: a competent template, undercut by a subject that promises more focus than the email delivers.
- The screenshot is heavily downscaled, so I can't read the fine print or the preview text.

## What's working

- The "hours left" labels on the wreath and candle tiles carry real urgency on the actual last day of Black Friday.
- The hero offer (up to 50% off plus free shipping) is large, and a clear "Shop Now" button sits directly under it.
- The photography is polished and seasonal: reds, greens and holiday styling that fit the campaign.
- The category grid shows a concrete percentage on each tile (furniture, sofas & sectionals, kitchen brands, holiday decor, rugs), so scanning is easy.
- No broken images or layout glitches are visible.

## What's weak

- The subject's wreath and candle promise is not the hero. The 40% deal is demoted to the first grid row under a generic 50% banner.
- The email is very long, with about eight distinct sections. The kitchen, Gift Shop and "we love these for you" blocks dilute the decor message.
- There are two competing numbers: 40% in the subject and 50% in the hero. Each is "up to," so neither is a firm headline.
- The CTAs are generic ("Shop Now," "Shop Black Friday," "Shop All Kitchen Gifts") and don't name the product.
- The send is a repeat of this morning's creative. Nothing marks it as the "today only" version beyond tile labels.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject. Use a wreath and candle hero with a "Today only: 40% off" headline and a countdown, and move the generic 50% banner below it.
- 2. Cut the email to the hero, the decor deal, one furniture row and a single closing CTA. Drop the kitchen block, the Gift Shop banner and the app promo from the day-of urgency send.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific, such as "Shop wreaths & garlands" and "Shop Monochrome candles."
- 4. Segment so the furniture and home-decor audience sees furniture and decor first, and kitchen buyers get the kitchen deals.
- 5. Put the end time in the hero, for example "Ends tonight at midnight CT."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code is generally sound (XHTML doctype, `lang="en"`, MSO/VML fallbacks, `@media` breakpoints at 460px). About 40 images have no `alt` attribute, and the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom. Most of the compliance, authentication and link checks could not be confirmed, because the HTML was truncated and the relay appears to have stripped the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 80 links were skipped by the QA probe as tracking or click-redirect domains, so no destination, status code or redirect chain was checked.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` calls fire from one send. Their `s=` value starts with the same hash as the `mi_u=56aaa278…` parameter on the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images. A recipient-level identifier therefore appears to be passed to a third party (rlcdn.com). This is an observation about the data flow, not a defect.
- **Open pixels:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ef03845b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and impression trackers. They are expected, but they add to the tracking-pixel count.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (about 40 images):** the QA check reports 40 or so `<img>` tags with no `alt` attribute. A screen reader will announce the filename or URL, and with images blocked the content simply disappears.
  - **Content images (about 34):** about 26 are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png`, one is a further `971aa10f-….jpg`, and seven are personalized `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Examples: `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `560d850e7e517ede.png`.
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** the five `448796.gif`, `o.gif` and the `dv.` pixel should carry `alt=""`. They are not content.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks user zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text.
- **Other markup issues:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which should be `&amp;`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with redundant and partly conflicting values: once with all four keys, then separately for `date`, `address` and `telephone`.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` shows incomplete template cleanup. It is harmless, but it ships to recipients.
  - `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` disables iOS text scaling.
- **Not checked:** the rendered layout in individual clients. The HTML was truncated and no screenshot-diff data was provided.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved tokens:** none visible in the supplied HTML or the QA output.
- **Movable Ink images:** the dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` images were rendered at send or open time. Whether they resolve to real content could not be verified. Because they have no `alt`, a failed or blocked image leaves no fallback text.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are missing from the captured headers. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may drop them, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. To check, send to a direct mailbox and inspect the raw headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender and image hosts share the `mail.crateandbarrel.com` subdomain, which is consistent with domain alignment but doesn't prove it.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. All 80 tracked links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters appear in the supplied source. The offer ("40% off wreaths, garlands & Monochrome candles") cannot be matched to a landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images, including the dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*` ones. Set `alt=""` on the seven tracking pixels. This would clear most of the 57% pass-rate shortfall.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the `&` in `<title>`, collapse the duplicate `format-detection` tags, and strip the leftover `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
4. Re-test the unsubscribe headers and authentication results from a raw, non-relayed delivery before treating either as a failure.
5. Resolve at least a sample of the tracked links through the redirect, to check the landing page and UTM continuity.
6. Review whether the `rlcdn.com` pixels, which carry a recipient-level hash, are covered by Crate & Barrel's privacy disclosures.
