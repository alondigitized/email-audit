# New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance send with a tacked-on holiday banner. The subject promises "New markdowns added! Up to 60% off", and the hero delivers with "up to 60% off clearance" and a white "Shop Now" button. A strip of category links sits below it (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) with a "Shop All Clearance" button. The offer is easy to find.
- The top of the email undercuts it. A banner above the hero reads "ends soon! the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off". That is a second, competing offer, and it echoes yesterday's "Hosting Thanksgiving? Up to 30% off dining furniture" send. The hero text is also small and low-contrast white on a muted grey-green photo. The rest is a long catalog scroll: an espresso machine, a Le Creuset color launch, a six-item product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo with a $500 sweepstakes, and a design service. By the app promo the email has stopped being about markdowns.
- Overall it's a decent clearance send with strong subject-to-hero continuity, but the message is diluted. The 30% banner pulls attention from the 60% hero, and the markdown products never appear. Fix the top and cut the tail.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match: "up to 60% off clearance" appears in both, so there is no bait-and-switch.
- The category shortcuts under the hero (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) let a shopper go straight to their category.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows product names and prices, with struck-through originals and red sale prices on some items. Seeing the markdown makes the discount credible.
- The footer fine print is clearly present, and the "While quantities last" language on the hero is visible rather than buried.
- The design is clean and consistent with the brand, with legible buttons.

## What's weak

- The "ends soon! Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off" banner sits above the 60% hero and competes with it. Two percentages in the first screen blur the message.
- The hero headline is small, low-contrast white text on a busy grey-green photo, so the eye doesn't land on it first.
- The espresso machine and Le Creuset blocks are full-price new-product features. They have nothing to do with markdowns and bury the clearance message.
- The "we love these for you" grid doesn't clearly show clearance. Some items have visible sale prices, but others show a single price with no markdown cue.
- The tail is overloaded: Reward Dollars card, app download with a $500 sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk, and store locator. That is four or five asks after the main offer.
- The email runs very long, which is a lot of scrolling for a "markdowns" message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the Thanksgiving 30% banner from the top, or move it below the clearance block. Lead with a single offer.
- 2. Make the hero headline larger, darker or bolder, and put it on a calmer image area. Add a deadline or "while it lasts" cue next to "up to 60% off".
- 3. Replace the espresso and Le Creuset blocks with an actual clearance product grid showing original and sale prices, with "% off" tags.
- 4. Cut the tail down to one secondary CTA. Keep either the app or the credit card, not both plus the design service.
- 5. Keep the category links but add the discount depth per category, such as "Rugs up to 50% off".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with MSO conditional comments. QA flagged 3 warnings and 0 hard failures (57% pass rate). Most are alt-text gaps on about 30 images, plus header checks that couldn't run. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link markup were not available to inspect.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **62 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped the HTTP check because they are click-redirect domains, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` (five LiveRamp/RLCDN pixels), `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (Movable Ink open pixel) and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/1ab03f69-...` (open/view tracking). These are expected for this ESP stack but add external requests.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa278...` hash appears in the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. The same value is the `s=` parameter in the rlcdn URLs. It is a persistent user identifier in a third-party pixel, which is a privacy consideration rather than a bug.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images.** The QA warnings show these groups:
  - Content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` and `/m/47/` (about 14 files, e.g. `578aa77d-...jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-...png`).
  - The scene7 hero `2026%5F0824%5FCB%5FLeCreuset%5FChateau%5FSecondary`.
  - Seven dynamic product images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - The tracking pixels above, which should carry `alt=""`.
  
  If the content images are linked, screen readers and image-blocked clients will get no link label.
- **Pinch-zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks zooming. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `*` and `body` also disables text scaling in iOS.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px or wider and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Minor source hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times.
  - Both `http-equiv` Content-Type and `<meta charset>` are present.
  - A template comment is left in place: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- `lang="en"` is set, and the VML/Office namespaces and `mso` fixes are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so this is limited to the head and the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping them, so it is not confirmed as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` shares a parent with the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with aligned sending infrastructure.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified** because the source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 62 click-tracked links were skipped and no destination URLs or UTM parameters were visible in the truncated source. No issues are asserted.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~21 content and product images, and `alt=""` to the pixels and decorative images. Dynamic Movable Ink product images (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/`) should have alt supplied by the template.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the global `*` rule.
3. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` against the raw message source, not the AgentMail relay copy. Capture `Authentication-Results` the same way.
4. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link and address, the resolved click-through destinations, and UTM parameters.
5. Clean up the source: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas, and fix or remove the dead 600px `img` rule.
