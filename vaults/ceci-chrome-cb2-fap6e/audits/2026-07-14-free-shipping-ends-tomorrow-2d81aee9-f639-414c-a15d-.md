# FREE SHIPPING ends tomorrow!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids using a classic urgency mechanism — free shipping expiring tomorrow — as the hook to drive traffic into what is actually a broad catalog refresh email. The hero leads correctly with the deadline, but free shipping is one of the weakest promotional levers available; it has no perceived discount and competes poorly against the 60% Summer Sale blast from two days ago (7/12) or yesterday's specific Le Creuset dollar-off deal. After the hero, the email pivots into editorial territory: 500+ fall arrivals from $29, a lifestyle campaign block, a personalized recommendations rail, and a CrateandBarrel cross-promo — none of which are threaded back to the shipping deadline. The result is a long scroll where the urgency of the subject line evaporates by the second section.
- The "we love these for you" personalized block is the most actionable content in the email, but it's buried three-quarters of the way down. That ordering is backwards. The genuinely interesting angle — 500+ fall arrivals with a $29 entry price — is also stronger editorial than the free shipping hook, but it's in section two, not the hero.
- Honest verdict: this sends okay, but it's an execution-over-strategy email. The urgency is real, the brand is clean, but the offer is soft and the structure dilutes the CTA. Most subscribers will open on the deadline instinct and close before clicking anything.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the core message immediately — "ends tomorrow / free shipping" is legible and above the fold.
- Category navigation shortcuts (Shop Storage, Shop Clothing, Shop Kids Bedding, Shop Kids Furniture) give quick escape hatches for subscribers who know what they want.
- "500+ new fall arrivals, starting at $29" is a specific anchor price — one of the email's better content moments.
- Personalized "we love these for you" rail adds specificity and perceived relevance even if it's placed too deep.
- No render bugs visible: images load, text is readable, layout is intact.

## What's weak

- "FREE SHIPPING ends tomorrow!" in the subject is all-caps on the offer — mild spam-signal risk and weaker motivation than a dollar-off or percentage-off deadline.
- Fall arrivals featured in mid-July feel slightly early — the seasonal framing creates a small cognitive mismatch against the summer-sale mindset the brand just primed in the 7/12 blast.
- Every CTA reads "Shop Now" — no copy specificity, no verb+category pairing that would direct intent.
- The free shipping offer and the editorial content (fall arrivals, lifestyle block, personalized picks, app promo) are stitched together loosely — there's no through-line back to the deadline that would sustain urgency across the scroll.
- Heavy Crate & Kids send cadence (2nd or 3rd in 24 hours after 7/13 sends) — inbox fatigue risk is real for this subscriber segment.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the fall arrivals, not just the shipping deadline.** "500+ fall arrivals from $29 — free shipping through tomorrow" gives subscribers two reasons to click: new product discovery and a time-bound perk. Free shipping alone doesn't.
- 2. **Move the personalized recommendations rail above the fold**, or at minimum above the lifestyle campaign block. It's the most click-motivating content in the email.
- 3. **Rewrite CTAs from generic "Shop Now" to category-specific action phrases** — "Shop Fall Arrivals," "Shop Kids Furniture," etc. Each section has its own category context; the CTA should reflect it.
- 4. **Kill the all-caps "FREE SHIPPING" in the subject.** Lowercase reads cleaner and avoids the spam-signal flag.
- 5. **Merge the App/Design Desk blocks or move them to the footer.** They interrupt the purchase funnel mid-scroll and dilute the urgency build.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall arrivals from $29 — free shipping ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New kids picks just landed. Free shipping while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `500+ new arrivals, all shipping free through midnight tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, clothing, bedding — grab free shipping before it's gone`
