# Win a $5k design refresh
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 stepping fully outside its Labor Day sale rut for once — the hero leads with "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH / Meet with a designer August 13 – September 13 for the chance to win a CB2 gift card," a design-services promo, not another category discount. That's a genuine change of pace after five straight sale-cadence sends this week ("So much is on sale right now," "This best-selling lamp," "Exclusive designs on sale," "That's so '70s," "On sale now and twice the rewards"), and the subject line delivers exactly what the hero shows — a rare 1:1 match for this brand's recent history.
- The problem is the sweepstakes window has already been open for nearly three weeks (Aug 13–Sept 13) with no reminder of days-left urgency, and the email then buries its own Labor Day sale — the actual live, guaranteed offer — halfway down the page under a wall of design-service categories. A subscriber who doesn't want a design consultation has to scroll past six lifestyle category tiles before hitting anything transactional.
- Net: a well-differentiated subject and a break from sale fatigue, undercut by weak urgency and a soft, contest-based CTA rather than a hard offer up top. Solid open, mediocre click.

## What's working

- Subject and hero are perfectly aligned — "Win a $5k design refresh" / "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH" — no bait-and-switch, unlike the brand's last three sends.
- Clear break from Labor Day sale fatigue — a genuinely different angle after a string of near-identical promo emails.
- Clean visual hierarchy: bold hero, single unmistakable "BOOK A FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT" button repeated twice.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout is intact.
- Good use of category tiles (Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs) to make the design-service pitch concrete rather than abstract.

## What's weak

- No visible eligibility mechanics, gift card amount tiers, or odds — "chance to win" reads vague with zero specificity beyond the headline number.
- The live Labor Day sale ("UP TO 50% OFF") is buried after the entire design-services section — anyone shopping instead of remodeling has to scroll past six tiles to find it.
- No urgency cue for the sweepstakes deadline (Sept 13) beyond a one-time date range mention in body copy — nothing near the CTA reinforces "X days left."
- Two competing CTAs with unequal weight — design appointment gets top billing and repetition, sale gets a single small "SHOP THE SALE" button — confusing which is the primary ask.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a live countdown or "X days left to enter" badge near the hero CTA — a sweepstakes with no urgency cue this close to its window feels like an afterthought rather than a real deadline.
- 2. Move the Labor Day sale banner up, directly under the hero, so shoppers not interested in design services still hit a hard offer within one scroll.
- 3. Name the actual prize mechanics (how many winners, gift card value confirmed, entry method) near the CTA — "chance to win" alone reads soft and unverified.
- 4. Differentiate CTA styling so the reader isn't torn between two equally-styled asks — pick one primary action per section.

- **Subject Alt B:** `13 days left: book a designer, win $5k`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free design help, plus a shot at $5,000 toward your home`

# Technical Audit: CB2 "Win a $5k design refresh"

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard CB2/RSG-templated marketing email with legacy ad-tech beacons (Dotomi) served over unencrypted HTTP and no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured — likely a relay artifact rather than a sender-side defect, but both need verification against the raw source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 66 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (tracking domains excluded by design) — not independently verified as functional; flag as unconfirmed rather than passing.
- 10 legacy Dotomi ad pixels (`ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) plus `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` are all served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. Modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) commonly block or strip mixed-content `http://` image requests, meaning these tracking calls likely fail silently in a large share of opens.
- One additional tracking pixel (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`) and a conversion pixel (`dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are HTTPS — correctly configured.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 content images (`image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/...`) are missing `alt` text — screen readers and blocked-image fallback views will show nothing for these.
- All 10 Dotomi pixels and the UCM controller image are also missing `alt` attributes, though as 1x1 tracking pixels this is standard/acceptable practice and not a real accessibility concern.
- Template uses extensive legacy MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets, and multiple `@font-face` fallback chains (Lineto Brown, GT Sectra, HNW05/Crate & Barrel font) — structurally sound for cross-client rendering, no defects observed in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization fields visible in the truncated source provided. No issues found based on available evidence.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is offered. Note this was flagged as possibly an artifact of the AgentMail relay not capturing/forwarding the header, so it does not confirm CAN-SPAM non-compliance on CB2's end — needs verification against the raw SMTP headers at the source MTA.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed for the same reason.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown; same relay-capture caveat applies. Cannot confirm or deny sender authentication is properly configured.
- In-body unsubscribe link/physical address not visible in the truncated HTML source — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance from available evidence.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Truncated HTML source does not include the CTA destination URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment cannot be evaluated from available evidence.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the 10 Dotomi ad pixels and UCM controller call from `http://` to `https://` (or drop them if the vendor relationship is legacy/unused) to stop silent tracking failures in modern clients.
