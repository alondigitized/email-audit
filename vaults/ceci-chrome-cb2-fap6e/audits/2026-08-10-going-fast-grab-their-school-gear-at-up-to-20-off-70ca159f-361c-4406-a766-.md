# Going fast: grab their school gear at up to 20% off →
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids leading with back-to-school urgency ("Selling Fast! get back-to-school gear in time"), and for once the hero copy and subject line actually agree — no repeat of the banner-contradicts-subject problem seen in the 2026-08-10 "FINAL DAY" summer sale send or the 🎃 Halloween shop send. The backpack-collage hero and the four-item grid (backpacks, duffle bags, lunch boxes, water bottles) are clean, well-linked, and on-topic.
- Where it falls apart is honesty about the offer: the subject promises "up to 20% off," but the featured back-to-school section only shows "Ships Free! Starts at $39" — no crossed-out price, no percentage, anywhere near the hero. The discount only shows up later, buried in an unrelated "we love these for you" grid that mixes kids backpacks with adult furniture (a Nuna car seat, a dresser, a nursery chair). After the kids grid, the email pivots into three unrelated pitches — a heritage collection banner, a $5000 TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, and a new Crate Card ad — none of which serve the stated back-to-school intent.
- Net: solid top-of-email execution, undercut by a subject-line offer that never materializes where it should, and a long tail of unrelated campaigns stapled on. Middling send — better than this brand's recent contradictory banners, worse than a focused single-offer email.

## What's working

- Subject and hero copy are aligned this time — "school gear" language up top matches the subject, unlike the last two Crate & Kids sends in this history.
- Clear, specific CTAs throughout the kids section: "Shop Backpacks," "Shop Duffle Bags," "Shop Lunch Boxes," "Shop Water Bottles," each linking a distinct product, not a homepage.
- Free shipping called out on every kids item, which is a low-friction, easy-to-parse benefit.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The "up to 20% off" promised in the subject line is never shown in the back-to-school hero or product grid — only shipping and starting price are visible there.
- The email sprawls into four unrelated pitches after the kids section (heritage collection, $5000 sweepstakes, Crate Card, app download), diluting the back-to-school focus the subject set up.
- The "we love these for you" recommendation grid mixes kids backpacks with adult furniture (car seat, dresser, nursery chair) — visually and thematically incoherent for a back-to-school shopper.
- No urgency mechanic beyond the vague "Selling Fast" — no countdown, no date, nothing that makes the fear-of-missing-out credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount in the hero — add a "20% off backpacks & more" badge or strike-through pricing directly in the kids product grid, since that's the entire premise of the subject line.
- 2. Cut or move the heritage-finds banner, sweepstakes, and Crate Card ad out of this send — a back-to-school email should stay back-to-school through at least the first 2-3 scrolls.
- 3. Split "we love these for you" into a kids-only recommendation module; the furniture/nursery items belong in a separate campaign.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 8/17") to replace the vague "Selling Fast" — urgency without a date reads as filler.

- **Subject Alt B:** `School gear is going fast — shop 20% off today`
