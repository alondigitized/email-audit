# New collab. Only on the app.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-03**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's app-acquisition email built around the "Townhouse" launch — a collab with Todd Snyder (a menswear brand) pitched as app-exclusive early access. The concept is legitimately differentiated: a coveted menswear name lending design credibility to a home collection is a compelling story. But CB2 buried the most interesting hook — Todd Snyder's name — in body copy below the fold, while leading with an app-download ask on an audience that showed up for home goods.
- The bigger structural problem: the Midseason Sale 40%-off banner runs at the top of this exact email. You cannot simultaneously tell a subscriber "this collection is so exclusive it's only on the app" and "also here is a sitewide 40% off sale available to everyone right now." Those two messages directly undermine each other. The app-install ask is already the highest-friction CTA you can issue to an email subscriber — layering a competing sale on top removes any reason to bother with the detour.
- To make it worse, a $1,000 review contest drops mid-email with no logical connection to the Townhouse story. This is CB2's second email on June 3rd (after the "MIDSEASON SALE" send that scored an 8) — the Todd Snyder launch deserved its own clean, editorial day.

## What's working

- The CB2 × Todd Snyder "menswear name meets home" concept is genuinely differentiated and aspirational.
- "Only on the app" exclusivity framing is clearly communicated — the ask is legible even if it's friction-heavy.
- Hero room-interior photography is dark, moody, and aesthetically on-brand for CB2's target sensibility.
- Loyalty reward block ("EARN 10% BACK") is visible for members without dominating the send.
- Subject line length (29 chars) is cleanly mobile-friendly and spam-signal free.

## What's weak

- The Midseason Sale 40%-off banner at the top obliterates the app-exclusivity pitch — the two messages are logically incompatible in the same scroll.
- Todd Snyder's name — the entire credibility engine of this collab — is buried in small body copy; the subject says "new collab" without naming the partner.
- The $1,000 review contest interrupts mid-email with zero connection to the collab story, breaking editorial flow right when engagement peaks.
- No web fallback or softer secondary CTA — subscribers who won't install an app get nothing actionable.
- This is the second CB2 email on June 3rd; cadence fatigue is a real lift-killer by this point in the week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill or relocate the Midseason Sale banner for this send.** If both jobs must ship today, put the sale in a footer footnote or send them separately. The banner actively destroys the exclusivity framing that justifies the app-install ask.
- 2. **Put Todd Snyder's name in the subject line.** It's the biggest credibility signal and it's nowhere near the inbox view.
- 3. **Add a web fallback CTA.** Something like "App exclusive now · On site [date]" gives subscribers a reason to download without zero-summing those who won't.
- 4. **Pull the $1,000 review contest out entirely** — give it its own send or attach it to the ongoing sale email, not a collab launch.
- 5. **Tighten the "MORE FOR YOU" product grid** to Townhouse collab pieces specifically, reinforcing the story rather than pivoting to generic inventory.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Unresolved developer placeholder in `<head>`:**
```html
<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->
```
This is a template comment with a typo ("IMPUT") that shipped in the live email. It's harmless to rendering but indicates the template was not fully sanitized before deployment.

**[MEDIUM] `maximum-scale=1` blocks pinch-to-zoom:**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents users from zooming text on mobile. Violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text, Level AA).

**[MEDIUM] Wildcard `text-size-adjust: none` on all elements:**
```css
*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none; ...}
```
Applied globally rather than scoped to `body`. Can interfere with system accessibility font scaling across clients.

**[LOW] Non-standard mobile breakpoint:**
```css
@media only screen and (max-width: 418px)
```
418px is below the CB2 app's likely device targets. iPhone 14/15 (390px logical width) would trigger this, but devices at 420–600px get neither mobile nor a true desktop layout. Standard email breakpoints are 480px or 600px.

**[LOW] Double-slash in several `@font-face` `src` URLs:**
```css
src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2")
```
Four of the `@font-face` rules contain `cb2.com//Styles/` (double slash). Most servers normalize this silently, but it is a malformed URL that should be corrected.
