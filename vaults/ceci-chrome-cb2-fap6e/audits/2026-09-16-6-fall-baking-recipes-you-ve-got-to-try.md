# 6 fall baking recipes you’ve got to try →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine recipe-roundup email — six actual fall baking recipes (apple spice cake, pumpkin cheesecake bars, apple pie bars, chocolate swirl pumpkin bread, maple pumpkin cobbler) each with its own photo, ratings, and "Get the Recipe" CTA, tied to bakeware/tools ("Shop this recipe") sold in the store. It's a smarter format than the recent Venus Williams run (2026-09-14, 2026-09-15) because it leads with a real seasonal use-case rather than a celebrity collab, and it gives the reader a reason to click that isn't just "buy stuff."
- The execution drags it down: it's a long, single-column wall of near-identical recipe blocks with no visual variety, and it buries a second campaign (Venus Williams collab) and a loyalty/financing pitch ("$300 chance," "app-only") below the fold, diluting focus. The hero itself is generic lifestyle copy ("fall baking season is here!") with no specific recipe or offer teased.
- Net: a stronger content hook than this brand's last several sends, let down by repetitive layout and a scattershot second half. Solid mid-tier send — worth opening for the recipes, unlikely to drive impulse purchases.

## What's working

- Six distinct, appetizing recipe photos give real content variety even if the layout repeats.
- Star ratings under each recipe (e.g., "4.5 stars," visible review counts) add credibility/social proof.
- Each recipe block pairs "Get the Recipe" with "Shop Ingredients/Bakeware," giving a secondary commerce path without hard-selling.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, buttons are consistent brown-on-cream branding throughout.

## What's weak

- The hero has no concrete offer or specific recipe teaser — "fall baking season is here!" is vague filler copy.
- The email is extremely long and monotonous: six nearly identical "photo / title / stars / two buttons" blocks in a row with no pattern break.
- Unrelated campaigns (Venus Williams collab, wine/"so scary it's chic" Halloween teaser, app-exclusive $300 sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk service) are crammed below the recipes, splitting the email's identity into four unrelated pitches.
- No overarching CTA like "see all 6 recipes" at the top — a mobile reader has to scroll through the entire list to see what's included.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visual index near the top — thumbnail grid of all 6 recipes with jump links — so scrollers can pick their favorite immediately instead of paging through six full blocks.
- 2. Cut the email after the "more fall baking must-haves" section; move Venus Williams, wine, Halloween, and app-sweepstakes content to separate, focused sends — this email should be "baking," not four campaigns stitched together.
- 3. Sharpen the hero to name one hero recipe or a number ("6 recipes, 1 Dutch oven") instead of generic "fall baking season is here."
- 4. Vary the recipe-block layout (alternate image-left/image-right, or a 2-column grid) to break the visual monotony.
