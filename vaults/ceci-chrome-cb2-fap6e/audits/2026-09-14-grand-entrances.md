- CB2 has landed on "entryway console tables" as today's category, and it's a well-executed lookbook — "FIRST IMPRESSIONS ARE EVERYTHING" as the hook, three console vignettes (Davison, Rio, Scatola) each with a designer credit, styled in warm, moody photography. There's an actual "FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF" banner at the very top with a Shop Now button, so unlike yesterday's fabric-swatch send, this one does lead with a real discount before pivoting into inspiration content.
- The problem is the same one that's dogged this brand all week: after the hero banner, the offer disappears completely. Every subsequent CTA says "SHOP NEW IN ENTRYWAY" or "BOOK YOUR FREE APPOINTMENT" — no pricing, no percentage-off tags on any of the three hero consoles, no sale badges on the "MORE FOR YOU" product grid at the bottom. But it's still fundamentally a decor story, not a sale email, and a subscriber shopping on price will bounce after the top banner.

## What's working

- Real, specific sale callout at the top ("FALL SALE UP TO 30% OFF") with a visible Shop Now button — an actual offer signal, rare this week.
- Single, coherent category focus (entryway consoles) rather than the scattered multi-category grab of recent sends.
- Clean visual hierarchy — hero banner → styled vignette → product name/designer credit → CTA, repeated consistently three times.
- Designer attribution ("designed by Studio ANANDA," "Mercedes DiSanto") adds a premium, editorial feel consistent with brand voice.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.

## What's weak

- The 30% off offer never reappears after the top banner — none of the three consoles or the "MORE FOR YOU" grid show sale pricing or a percentage badge.
- No prices shown anywhere except the bottom grid, so shoppers can't tell if "Davison," "Rio," or "Scatola" consoles are actually part of the sale.
- "THERE'S MORE" and the mirrors/lamps/decor/wall-art grid dilutes the entryway focus right when momentum was building.
- The Interior Design Services banner mid-email is a hard pivot away from the sale message, same beat as 2026-09-11's full-service-marketing send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a "30% OFF" or "SALE" badge directly on each of the three console vignettes (Davison, Rio, Scatola) — right now the discount is orphaned at the top and readers have to assume it applies.
- 2. Show a strikethrough/sale price under each console name instead of just the collection name and designer credit.
- 3. Cut the "THERE'S MORE" grid (mirrors, lamps, decor, wall art) or move it to a separate send — it breaks the entryway narrative without adding sale urgency.
- 4. Move Interior Design Services out of this email; it's a different intent (service upsell) competing with sale intent in the same scroll.
- 5. Add a deadline to the sale banner ("ends Sunday") — right now the 30% off has no urgency attached.
