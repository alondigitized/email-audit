# WE RARELY DO THIS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the third send in four days pushing the same upholstery event. The 10/3 "Up to 25% off rarely-on-sale upholstery" email used the same hero, and the 10/4 "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." email carried the same offer. The subject "WE RARELY DO THIS" is a vague callback to that "rarely on sale" line, and it never says what "this" is. A subscriber who has seen the earlier two sends will read it as the same promo.
- The body itself is polished. The "UP TO 25% OFF" hero is clean, the Sectionals / Sofas / Accent Chairs grid is easy to scan, and a button follows each block. The weak spot is the stacking. Three offers compete in one scroll: 25% off upholstery, a $5,000 design-appointment sweepstakes, and 50% off Fall Design Sale. The most urgent item, "ENDS TONIGHT", sits in the third module, well below the fold. The hero never mentions it.
- Judgement: competent execution, tired cadence. The subject is the weakest piece and the urgency is in the wrong place. It's a 6–7 email with a 3-level open problem.

## What's working

- The hero shows "UP TO 25% OFF" in large type over a rich teal sectional, with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button above the fold.
- The category grid (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches & Ottomans) is clean, and the tile photography is on-brand.
- "ENDS TONIGHT" over "FALL DESIGN SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" is credible, specific urgency.
- The footer carries real member value: 10% back in Rewards Dollars, the app, and a 15% text-signup offer.
- The render has no visible bugs. There are no broken images or overlapping text, and the type hierarchy is consistent.

## What's weak

- The subject "WE RARELY DO THIS" is vague all-caps with no offer, category or deadline, and it doesn't match the upholstery hero.
- The 25% offer, the $5,000 sweepstakes and the 50% sale compete for attention, so there's no single clear message.
- The "ENDS TONIGHT" urgency is buried in the third module, and the hero doesn't use it.
- Several offers carry asterisks and a dense fine-print block at the bottom, which weakens the trust of the "up to" claims.
- The brand has sent near-identical upholstery promos on 10/3 and 10/4 and a sale reminder on 10/5, so cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the deadline. Move "ENDS TONIGHT" into the hero, or make the whole email the Fall Design Sale last call. One offer, one deadline.
- 2. Cut the module count. Drop or move the $5,000 sweepstakes and the design-appointment block below the main offer.
- 3. Make the subject say what "this" is, and mention the deadline.
- 4. Link the hero to one specific product, such as the featured sectional, rather than a generic "Shop the sale". Add price or fabric detail.
- 5. Suppress or segment recipients who got the 10/3 and 10/4 upholstery sends and didn't click, so they don't get a third copy.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off Fall Design Sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 25% off custom upholstery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sectionals, sofas and accent chairs. Sale ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus earn 10% back in Rewards Dollars on every purchase.`

## 7. Recommendations
