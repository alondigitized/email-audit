# THIS WEEK’S STEALS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the same four-tier roll-up CB2 ran on 2026-07-26 ("STEALS OF THE WEEK," 5/10) — four stacked banners (40% furniture, 60% summer clearance, 30% bedroom + bath, 50% outdoor), each with its own oversized percentage callout, no single hero offer. It's competent execution — clean product photography, consistent typography, generous whitespace, no render bugs — but structurally it's the format CB2 already knows underperforms against a single clear hero, as proven twice this month by the 07-28 "24 hours left" (9/10) and 07-29 "Ends tonight" (9/10) sends.
- The subject "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" is a step backward even from "STEALS OF THE WEEK" — it drops the specificity of a percentage or category and reads as generic filler copy that could headline any week's email. There's no urgency mechanic anywhere in the visible render (no "ends today," no countdown, no deadline), which is a return to the fumble CB2 already fixed on 07-28.
- Below the fold, the "MORE FOR YOU" product grid and loyalty/app footer are strong, well-organized modules — that part of the template is working. But the top of the funnel is muddled by choice overload, and this send won't outperform the discount-roll-up pattern's historical ceiling. Judgment: mid-pack, a regression from the brand's best recent work.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual system — each offer tier uses the same layout (photo, percentage, category label), making the email easy to scan even though there are four of them.
- Product photography is high quality and on-brand (styled interiors, not isolated product shots) for all four hero sections.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom shows six named products with visible prices and strikethrough original pricing (e.g. "Andes Acacia Wood Platform Queen Bed... $599.00 $449.00"), which is concrete and clickable.
- Footer cross-sell for the loyalty program ("EARN 10% BACK... Rewards store daily on purchases at CB2 and Crate & Barrel") and app download is well-placed and legible.
- No overlapping text, broken images, or lorem ipsum — the render is clean end to end.

## What's weak

- Four competing hero offers (40%, 60%, 30%, 50%) with no visual hierarchy telling the eye which one matters most — this is the exact "roll-up" architecture that scored 5/10 on 07-26, reused almost unchanged five days later.
- No urgency anywhere visible — no deadline, no "ends today/tonight," a regression from the two 9/10 sends this week that led with exactly that mechanic.
- Subject "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" is generic and less specific than even the prior "STEALS OF THE WEEK" subject — no percentage, no category, no reason to open now versus any other week.
- The single CTA button ("TELL US EVERYTHING") is for a review/sweepstakes ask, not a shopping action — there's no clear "Shop Now" button tied to any of the four discount tiers.
- Cadence concern: this is the second discount-roll-up format in six sends, sandwiched between two strong single-offer sends — reintroducing the weaker pattern right after proving the better one works reads as inconsistent testing discipline, not iteration.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one hero offer and lead with it**, matching the 07-28/07-29 formula that scored 9/10 twice: single image, single percentage, single deadline, above the fold. Push the other three category discounts into a secondary "also on sale" strip below.
- 2. **Add a real deadline** to the top of the email — "ends Sunday," "48 hours left," etc. — every top-performing CB2 send this month had one; this one has none visible.
- 3. **Replace the sweepstakes CTA with a shopping CTA.** "TELL US EVERYTHING" is a fine secondary ask but shouldn't be the only button in the email — add "SHOP THE SALE" tied to the lead offer.
- 4. **Sharpen the subject line** to name the strongest discount rather than "This Week's Steals."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off summer clearance — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your biggest steal this week: 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedroom, and outdoor markdowns — all in one place`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Four sales, one weekend. Shop before they're gone.`
- 5. Keep the "MORE FOR YOU" grid and loyalty footer as-is — that part of the template doesn't need changes.
