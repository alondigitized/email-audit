# NEW markdowns! More at up to 60% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth near-identical Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel Labor Day send I've reviewed in the last two days, and "NEW markdowns" is the only fresh word in the subject — the body is the same recognizable template: floral hero, "Double Rewards is on!" banner, a grid of category tiles at up to 50-60% off, a Double Rewards CTA block, new fall arrivals, Babyletto bestsellers, and a "we love these for you" product carousel. It's clean and well-built with no render bugs, but there's genuinely nothing new here relative to the 2026-09-05 "Even more at up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards!" send from earlier today — same offer tier, same rewards mechanic, same modular stack.
- For a subscriber who's opened four to five of these this week, fatigue is the dominant signal. The floral hero is a nice seasonal touch and slightly differentiates it visually from the stone-archway/cozy-living-room heroes used elsewhere in the family, but the underlying offer and structure are indistinguishable from prior sends.

## What's working

- Floral hero image is fresh and seasonally appropriate, distinct from the archway/living-room hero used in sibling sends.
- Category tile grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kids Bedding, etc.) gives clear, scannable navigation with individual CTAs.
- Product grid under "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" shows discount percentage directly on each tile, reducing click friction.
- "we love these for you" personalized product carousel with visible prices adds a targeted, lower-funnel section.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Subject's "NEW markdowns" isn't reflected anywhere prominently in the hero — the hero just repeats "up to 60% off + new markdowns" as secondary text under "Double Rewards is On!", so the promised headline isn't the visual focus.
- This is the same up-to-60%-off + Double Rewards combo as at least three other Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel sends this week — zero differentiation for a fatigued subscriber.
- Very long email (I count 10+ distinct modules) — by the time a reader hits Babyletto bestsellers or the credit card pitch, they've long since decided whether to engage.
- The credit card upsell ("Crate Card... Double Rewards is on!") appears twice (top banner and mid-body), which reads as padding rather than escalation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with "NEW markdowns" visually, not just in the subject — right now the subject over-promises novelty the hero doesn't deliver. Make new-markdown items the literal first thing in the visual hierarchy.
- 2. Cut the redundant Double Rewards card block (it's already in the top banner) and use that space to move the fall-arrivals or new-markdowns module higher, since those are the only genuinely new items this week.
- 3. Given five-plus similar sends already reviewed, consider suppressing frequency for engaged-but-fatigued segments or rotating in a genuinely different offer structure (e.g., single-category deep-dive) rather than the same kitchen-sink stack.
- 4. Trim the email length — move the credit-card app pitch and TheDesignDesk footer content to a single, condensed final panel.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Just marked down: fall's newest kids furniture`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary

Standard ESP (SFMC/Movable Ink-style) template using redirect-tracked links and pixel-based tracking; automated checks show 57% pass rate driven mostly by widespread missing `alt` attributes and unconfirmed authentication/unsubscribe headers.

2. Link & Tracking Issues

- 86 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped from HTTP probing — actual destination URLs behind these redirects were not verified for validity (could not confirm no 404s/broken redirects).
- Multiple tracking pixels present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5 instances, sequential `n=1`–`n=5` params suggesting LiveRamp/RLCDN identity resolution calls), and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` (view-through conversion pixel). No functional issues found in these, but volume (12+ tracking-only requests) adds load overhead.

3. Rendering & Accessibility

- All content images are missing `alt` attributes — QA flagged 38 distinct image warnings, including product imagery (e.g., `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`, `68910f1a-626e-4b12-8004-76fbb6577154.jpg`) and the Scene7-hosted clearance banner (`2026_0315_CBK_Clearance_01_05.gif`). This fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades the experience for screen-reader users and image-blocked clients (default in Outlook).
- Tracking pixels (`f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `448796.gif`, `o.gif`) also lack `alt=""`, which is correct to leave empty but they should still carry an explicit empty `alt` attribute rather than none, for stricter HTML validation.
- Head contains legacy/defensive boilerplate (XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype, MSO conditional comments, multiple redundant `format-detection` meta tags) consistent with broad legacy client support (Outlook desktop); no errors found in this markup itself.
- Media query breakpoints at 460px, 640px, and 768px are present and structurally valid; no malformed CSS detected in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- No unresolved merge tag syntax (e.g., `%%`, `{{`, `[%`) found in the visible HTML source.
- No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (CAN-SPAM requires a functioning unsubscribe mechanism, which may still exist as an in-body link not captured in the truncated source, but the header-level mechanism is unconfirmed).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not detected, meaning one-click unsubscribe per Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements is not confirmable from headers alone.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Caveat: all three warnings are flagged by QA as possibly artifacts of the AgentMail relay not preserving/capturing these headers, rather than confirmed absence at the originating MTA. Treat as "unconfirmed," not "confirmed missing."

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Truncated HTML does not expose visible destination URLs (all CTAs route through the 86 skipped tracking-redirect links), so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page alignment could not be verified in this pass.

7. Recommendations

- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 24 product/lifestyle images; add `alt=""` explicitly on the 12 known tracking pixels.
- Confirm with ESP (looks like Salesforce Marketing Cloud / Movable Ink infrastructure based on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` domain) that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are set at send time; re-audit via a capture path that preserves raw headers (bypass relay) to confirm SPF/DKIM/Authentication-Results independent of AgentMail.
- Resolve at least a sample of the 86 redirect links to their final destination and confirm UTM parameters and landing-page availability, since this pass could not validate them.
