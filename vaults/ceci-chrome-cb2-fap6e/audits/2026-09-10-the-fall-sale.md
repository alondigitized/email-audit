# THE FALL SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuine seasonal transition email — the first "FALL SALE" send in a run that's otherwise been Labor Day countdown clocks and rewards cross-sells (2026-09-09 "HOURS LEFT FOR LABOR DAY SALE," 2026-09-08 "Up to 50% off. Don't miss this."). That's a point in its favor: it doesn't feel like the fourth identical countdown email this week, and the "UP TO 30% OFF" offer is stated cleanly in a large, legible hero.
- But the email quickly loses focus. After the fall hero and a decent category nav block (Furniture / Lighting / Pillows / Decor & Mirrors / Shop Up to 30% Off), it stacks in a $5,000 design-refresh sweepstakes, a "New & Best Selling Bedroom" merchandising block, then four unrelated tiles (app download, rewards card, registry, SMS opt-in). Four distinct asks compete for one click, and the fall sale itself — the ostensible subject of the email — gets no product-level specificity anywhere below the fold.
- It's a clean, on-brand, bug-free render, which keeps it out of the bottom tier, but the single-offer clarity that made "LABOR DAY SALE ENDS TOMORROW" (2026-09-08, 7.00/10) the strongest send in this history is missing here. Net: a serviceable seasonal-kickoff email that undersells its own headline offer by burying it under three other campaigns.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: large "FALL SALE" wordmark over a clean lifestyle shot, with "UP TO 30% OFF" legible without visual competition.
- The category block right under the hero (Furniture, Lighting, Pillows, Decor & Mirrors, Shop Up to 30% Off) gives multiple concrete entry points tied directly to the sale.
- No render bugs — every image loads, text doesn't overlap, and the brand's minimal typographic style is consistent top to bottom (unlike the 2026-09-07 "DOUBLE REWARDS" send's stacked-number glitch).
- Footer/utility tiles (app, rewards, registry, SMS) are visually tidy and clearly secondary, not competing with the hero for attention.

## What's weak

- No urgency or end date anywhere — competing sends in this brand's history ("HOURS LEFT," "ENDS TOMORROW," live countdown clocks) all outperform on urgency signals, and this one has none.
- The email crams four separate campaigns (Fall Sale, $5K Refresh sweepstakes, Bedroom merchandising, app/rewards/registry/SMS) into one scroll — same "stapled together" issue flagged in the 2026-09-08 three-offer send.
- No specific product is linked — every CTA ("Shop Up to 30% Off," "Book a Free Design Appointment," "Shop Now") routes to a category or program, not a hero SKU, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.
- The 30% off applies only to "select furniture, lighting, decor and mirrors" per the fine print — the hero doesn't disclose the exclusion, which the rubric flags as a soft honesty miss even though it's a normal furniture-retail carve-out.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut this to one offer.** Drop the sweepstakes and bedroom block from this send (or make them separate emails) and let the Fall Sale own the whole scroll — this is the single biggest lift available given the brand's own history shows single-offer sends (2026-09-08 "LABOR DAY SALE ENDS TOMORROW," 7.00/10) outperforming multi-offer stacks.
- 2. **Add a deadline.** Even a simple "Ends Sunday" or a date range under "UP TO 30% OFF" would close the urgency gap versus this brand's countdown-clock Labor Day sends.
- 3. **Feature one hero product with a price**, not just a category grid — give the eye something concrete to click on, the way the Bedroom block's bed photo does, but tie it to the sale discount explicitly ("Platform Bed, now $XXX").
- 4. **Move the sweepstakes and bedroom content to a follow-up send** rather than this one, preserving the one-week Fall Sale narrative before pivoting to new-season merchandising.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on furniture, lighting, and decor — shop before it's gone.`

# CB2 "THE FALL SALE" — Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email fails on personalization (unbatched merge token exposed to recipients) and carries multiple legacy ad-tech beacons using plaintext HTTP; core deliverability/compliance headers could not be verified via the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 62 tracking/click-redirect links present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains), so destination validity is unconfirmed — manual click-through spot-check recommended.
- Legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixels loaded over **plaintext HTTP** (not HTTPS): `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, and `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` at `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pubN.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`. Mixed-content HTTP resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), silently breaking these ad-tracking syncs.
- Additional tracking pixels present at `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1ce80f8f-cae1-47d7-8aa5-a29a641302d7?...` (Merkle/dv tracking) — both HTTPS, no issue.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 content/product images missing `alt` text (e.g. `a905a746-...gif`, `e9d13591-...jpg`, `7bb2c9a4-...jpg`, `5c9dbda6-...jpg`, `193d23a9-...jpg`, `61c40bc9-...jpg`, `bab67c65-...jpg`) — screen readers will announce nothing or fall back to filenames.
- All 10 Dotomi `pubN.php` beacons and the `UCMController` beacon also lack `alt` text, though as 1x1 tracking pixels this is low-impact.
- Head markup uses heavy legacy MSO/Outlook conditional comments and multiple `@font-face` declarations pointing to `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com` font hosts; if these fail to load, fallback stacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) are defined, so no broken-text risk.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Unresolved merge tag found in rendered HTML: `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** This indicates a personalization/ESP templating failure — the raw token is exposed to end recipients instead of being replaced with review/product data, and likely corresponds to a broken or empty product-review block.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay-capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not present — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.
- **Recommendation:** these three should be re-checked against raw SMTP headers at the receiving MTA (not the relay-forwarded copy) before concluding non-compliance.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable from the truncated source/QA data provided — no destination URLs or UTM parameters were included in the QA findings for the primary CTA links (only redirect-domain tracking links, which were skipped). No issues found in the data available; flag for manual review of at least one CTA destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the ESP merge-tag failure producing literal `%%reviews_skulist%%` in the sent email — check the data feed/API populating product reviews.
2. Migrate the 11 Dotomi ad-sync pixels (`UCMController`, `pub1–10.php`) from `http://` to `https://`, or drop them if the ad-sync vendor is deprecated — plaintext HTTP requests are increasingly blocked outright by mail clients.
3. Add descriptive (or empty `alt=""` for decorative/tracking) attributes to the 7 content images and 11 tracking pixels currently missing them.
4. Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` directly against original SMTP headers, since relay capture may be masking their actual presence.
5. Manually sample 2–3 of the 62 tracking-redirect links to confirm they resolve and land on the intended, UTM-tagged product pages.
