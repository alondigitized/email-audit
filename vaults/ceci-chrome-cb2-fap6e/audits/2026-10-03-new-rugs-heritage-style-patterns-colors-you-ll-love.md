**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- This is a rug-launch email with a "better-than-vintage rugs" hero and a thin sale strip above it. The body is a clean run of collection modules: new Lisse and Reims, bestsellers Favira and Rhodes, then a "future-heirloom" roundup. A free-swatch offer follows that. It matches the subject, and it's a stronger fit than the other Design Desk and bedding sends from Oct 1-2.
- It then loses focus. After the rugs come a kids' performance-rug tile, a Café & Caraway coffee-machine block ("up to $300 off"), a dining-furniture block, a holiday block and a product row. That's a rug email that turns into a general catalog. The best offer, the swatch, sits mid-email, and the discount is buried in a small banner.
- Judgement: on-topic and well organized, but the offer is too quiet and the email is too long. It's a solid 6, in line with the rest of this week's sends.

## What's working

- The subject and hero agree: "better-than-vintage rugs" with a single "Shop Now" button.
- Each collection has its own module with a name and a specific "Shop Lisse / Reims / Favira / Rhodes" button. That's a verb plus a noun, not "Discover".
- The "new!" and "bestseller!" labels give shoppers a quick way to choose.
- The "try a swatch first, they ship free" module lowers the risk of buying a rug online.
- The large room photography is consistent and on-brand.

## What's weak

- The sale offer is a small strip above the hero, so the eye never lands on a price benefit.
- There are roughly ten modules. The coffee machines, dining furniture and holiday blocks dilute the rug message.
- The kids' rug module looks like its headline text sits on top of a busy image, which would hurt legibility. The image is small, so I'm not certain.
- The "new for that future-heirloom look" section has several tiny sub-labels. They would be hard to read on mobile.
- This is the second rug-themed send in two days, after "Picking the perfect rug is easy" on Oct 2. Cadence is getting repetitive.

- 2. Cut the email after the swatch module. Move the coffee, dining and holiday blocks to a separate send.
- 3. Make the free-swatch offer a full-width call-to-action band right after the hero.
- 4. Fix the contrast on the kids' rug headline, or put the text on a solid panel.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Movable Ink-driven template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with 70 wrapped click-tracking links. Its main verifiable defects are missing alt text on about 34 images and a few markup and accessibility problems in the head. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unknown. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Redundant third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp) is fired five times, with `n=1` through `n=5` and an identical `s=56aaa2787a6773d0…` parameter. This adds five extra requests and a third-party data-sharing surface.
- **Possible identifier sharing:** The `s=` value on the rlcdn pixels starts with the same hash as `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0…` on the Movable Ink URLs. This looks like a per-recipient identifier passed to a third party. The values are truncated, so I can't confirm they match in full.
- **Tracking assets without alt text:** `o.gif` (`mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…`) and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/bda4b7de-…` have no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (34 images flagged):**
  - 19 are hosted at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `14417eb4-….gif`).
  - 1 is the Scene7 hero asset `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026_0926_CB_HolidayAwareness_Anthem…`.
  - 1 more is `971aa10f-….jpg`.
  - 6 are Movable Ink dynamic images, `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png`, repeated six times.
  - The other 7 are tracking pixels (see section 2).
  - With images blocked, the content images render as blank space. Screen readers announce the filename or URL.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1 />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set earlier in the same stylesheet, blocking text scaling on iOS.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. Both `<meta charset="utf-8">` and `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` are present, and `<meta charset>` and `<meta http-equiv="X-UA-Compatible">` are not self-closed. `format-detection` is declared three times with different values.
- **Conflicting media queries:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` both apply at 768px and wider. The later rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code. Mobile breakpoints only exist at `max-width:460px`, so viewports of 461–639px get the desktop layout. `.mobile` and `.showmobile` also use fixed widths of 460px and 414px.
- **Template leftover:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. No unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%…%%` or `[[ ]]` tokens appear in the subject or the truncated source. The six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` dynamic images are Movable Ink personalization. I can't tell from the source whether they resolved to distinct content per slot.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. The QA tool flags that the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe if the header is absent from the real message.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** `Authentication-Results` is not available through the relay, so authentication status is unknown. From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with an aligned sending subdomain, but that is not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** Not assessable because the HTML was truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 70 links are wrapped in redirect domains and were not resolved. The truncated source doesn't show the final landing URLs or any UTM parameters. No continuity issue is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 27 content and dynamic images. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Remove the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` overrides and keep `100%`.
3. Collapse the five `rlcdn.com` pixels into one. Check that the `s=` identifier shared with LiveRamp is covered by the privacy policy and consent.
4. Re-capture the raw message headers (not via the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. Re-run QA on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the redirect chains to confirm final URLs, UTM parameters and 200 status. Check the footer for an unsubscribe link and a physical address.
6. Clean up the markup:
   - Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`.
   - Remove the duplicate meta tags.
   - Delete the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
   - Drop the dead `max-width:600px` rule.
   - Add a mid-width breakpoint above 460px.
