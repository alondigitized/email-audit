# UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing send of CB2's Long Weekend Sale. The sale ends Nov 8, so the countdown timer and the "FINAL DAYS" banner are credible urgency, which most of the week's emails lacked. The hero is clean: a big "LONG WEEKEND SALE," "UP TO 50% OFF," and four category buttons over a warm sofa photo. The eye lands on the offer first.
- The email then dilutes itself. After the hero come three more modules: a swatches offer, a free-shipping offer, and a large Double Rewards block. Each has its own CTA, and there's a dense app, referral and SMS grid below. Double Rewards runs through Nov 17, so it isn't urgent, and it competes with the sale that ends in hours. The rewards block also shows "10% 20%" with the 10% struck through, which is visually messy.
- It is a competent last-call send, a step above the vague "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" from Nov 7, but it is the fifth or sixth near-identical sale push this week. It earns a solid middle score.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (03:15:22:11) plus "FINAL DAYS" gives real, credible urgency.
- The hero is strong: a large "UP TO 50% OFF" over a good lifestyle photo, with four clear category buttons.
- The "SO MUCH SHIPS FREE – 1000+ items" block adds a concrete, low-friction benefit.
- The Double Rewards block states the card benefit clearly, with a date ("NOW THROUGH NOV 17TH, 2026") and a "LEARN MORE" button.
- The brand look is consistent and the layout is clean, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero shows no actual products. It's mood photography with category buttons, so there's nothing specific to click.
- The email has four competing offers (sale, swatches, free shipping, rewards) and about a dozen CTAs. The close of a one-day sale should have one message.
- The "10% 20%" rewards headline has the 10% crossed out, which reads as a rendering glitch or as confusing math.
- The footer is a wall of tiny legal text. The "$" and "†" marks in the hero and rewards copy hide the exclusions.
- The swatches banner has low-contrast text over a busy photo, and "CUSTOM IS SUCH A GOOD LOOK" is hard to read.
- It repeats the same sale and rewards message as the Nov 8 "Dream bedroom scenarios" send and the Nov 6 "LAST DOUBLE REWARDS" send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the sale hero, one row of 3–4 priced best-sellers, and one rewards line. Drop swatches, the app and SMS grid, and the gift-list module from a last-call send.
- 2. Put the deadline in the subject and say when it ends in the hero copy ("Ends tonight at midnight PT"), since the timer is the best asset here.
- 3. Add product tiles with names and sale prices directly under the hero, so the click lands on something specific.
- 4. Fix the rewards graphic by showing only "20% back" with no strikethrough. Move it below a divider as a secondary offer.
- 5. Make the CTA copy specific, for example "SHOP SOFAS" instead of "SHOP NOW" and "LEARN MORE".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO/VML fallbacks and a single mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed problems are 9 content images with no alt text and 11 non-HTTPS third-party Dotomi pixels. The source is truncated and 61 tracking links were not probed, so the footer, unsubscribe link and link destinations could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not verified.** The QA probe skipped them as click-redirect domains, so redirect chains, status codes and landing destinations are unconfirmed.
- **11 images load over plain `http://`**, which risks blocking or mixed-content warnings:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- These Dotomi (Conversant) requests look like cookie-sync pixels. Clients that proxy images over HTTPS (Gmail, Apple Mail privacy protection) may drop them. They also add 11 third-party requests to the load.
- **Tracking pixels are present and served over HTTPS:**
  - `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...`
  - `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4171017a-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`
- Both lack alt text. This is a minor accessibility issue for pixels, and it is better fixed with `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a78c5af8-...jpg`, `93c7b43a-...jpg`, `ce4e1b39-...jpg`
  - `568bb9f7-...jpg`, `93dba763-...jpg`, `64f0e585-...jpg`
  - `54836071-...jpg`, `55fbf30d-...jpg`, `345f2993-...jpg`
- With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these show as filenames or nothing. If the offer text sits inside the images, it is lost.
- **The viewport meta tag has `maximum-scale=1`.** This prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless to rendering but shows an unfinished template.
- **The `<title>` is generic:** "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME". It does not match the subject line, "UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards".
- **Font sources are inconsistent.** `@font-face` rules point at `cb2.com` for some fonts and `crateandbarrel.com` for "HNW05". The first "Lineto Brown Sub" block has `.eot` as its base `src` with no format hint. This is low impact because the web fonts fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif.
- No other structural problems are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` parameter on `o.gif` is a 64-character hash, not a raw email address. The rest of the body was truncated, so this only covers the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** The QA flags the three items below as header-capture limits of the AgentMail relay, not as confirmed sender faults.
  - `List-Unsubscribe` is not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) is not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` is not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Footer not assessed.** The visible and truncated source does not show the physical postal address or the in-body unsubscribe link, both of which CAN-SPAM requires. These should be checked against the full HTML.
- **Sending domain.** The `From` domain is `mail.cb2.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice. Alignment with the DKIM `d=` domain could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The 61 tracking links were not resolved and the visible source shows no destination URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with "UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards" are unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images listed above. Use `alt=""` on the `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com` tracking pixels.
2. Move the 11 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them. They are third-party cookie syncs with little value in email.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment. Set `<title>` to the campaign subject.
5. Re-run the QA against raw `.eml` headers, not the relay copy, to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Follow a sample of the 61 tracked links to the final destination, and check UTM params, the offer match and any redirect-chain latency.
7. Check the untruncated footer for the postal address and a working unsubscribe link.
