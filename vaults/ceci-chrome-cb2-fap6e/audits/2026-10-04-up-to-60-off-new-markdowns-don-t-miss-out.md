# Up to 60% off NEW markdowns! Don’t miss out →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- The email delivers on its subject. "Up to 60% off clearance" sits in the hero with a "Shop Now" button, and a row of category links (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop, Bedding, Decor) follows. The problem is the banner above the hero. It still reads "it's on: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals," the leftover from the Oct 3 "Up to 20% off your living room update" send, and it is also in the Oct 4 coffee-tech email. Two competing offers sit above the fold, and one of them is stale.
- The rest is a long, generic catalog. A 25%-off storage and dining event, a TheDesignDesk appointment promo (shown twice), a six-item product grid, a credit card ad, an app download block and a store locator all follow. Only the top third supports the clearance message. The product grid is the one useful part, since it shows real prices and markdowns on the espresso machine, plates and lamp. It sits too far down, and it doesn't clearly tie to the clearance offer.
- Judgement: a solid, honest sale with a clear hero, weakened by a stale banner and a bloated body. For the 4th or 5th promo in two days, it works as a reminder but won't stand out.

## What's working

- The hero offer is specific and sits in the first screen: "up to 60% off clearance" with a clear white "Shop Now" button.
- The "New Markdowns Added!" label gives a credible reason to act now. "While quantities last" adds mild scarcity.
- The category quick-links (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop, Bedding & Bath, Decor) let a shopper jump straight to their area.
- The product grid shows struck-through prices and sale prices, so the discount is concrete.
- The design is clean and consistent with the brand's usual look.

## What's weak

- The top banner still says "up to 20% off sofas & sectionals." It is yesterday's campaign, and it competes with the 60% hero.
- The body stacks too many offers: 60% clearance, 25% storage and dining, design appointments, a credit card, and an app. That dilutes the clearance story.
- TheDesignDesk appears twice, so it reads as filler.
- The product grid is not clearly clearance. Only a few items show markdowns, and most have no visible savings.
- This is the 4th–5th Crate & Barrel send in two days (Oct 3 bedroom event and sofa sale, Oct 4 coffee tech, gift guides, holiday toys). The cadence feels heavy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove or update the top banner so the 60% clearance is the only hero offer. Make sure the banner matches the campaign.
- 2. Cut the body to the hero, category links, one secondary event, and a clearance-only product grid with visible markdowns. Drop the credit card and app blocks from sale sends.
- 3. Show the savings in the grid, with a "was/now" price and a percent-off badge on every item.
- 4. Show one deadline, such as "ends Sunday," instead of only "while quantities last."
- 5. Keep TheDesignDesk to one mention, below the products.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got deeper: up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, kitchen & decor. New styles added today, while they last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Espresso machines, dinnerware, lamps and more, marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with Outlook/VML fallbacks. It has no confirmed hard failures (0 QA errors, 3 warnings). The main confirmed issues are about 30 images with no alt attribute and a viewport meta tag that blocks pinch-zoom. The source is truncated, so unsubscribe, footer and link-destination checks can't be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM parameters are unverified. This is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed defect.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f1647331…` token appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 recommendation images) and on `o.gif`. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters appear on `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a1d69e1b-…`. These are first-party open and personalization pixels.
- **Third-party pixels.** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` beacons load from the LiveRamp domain `rlcdn.com`. They carry a hashed identifier and are loaded five times. This is data sharing to flag for privacy review, and it adds five extra requests.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 30 images (confirmed by QA).**
  - 12 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`, plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. Examples include `0350abec-…gif` and `6c00bab9-…jpg`.
  - 7 dynamic images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/…png`.
  - The five `rlcdn.com` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixel. Missing alt on these is low impact, but they should carry `alt=""`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, any of these that are content images (for example the `.gif`s) convey nothing. The QA tool does not distinguish an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing blocked.** The global rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` disables text-size adjustment. It also forces `line-height:100%` on every element, which can clip wrapped text.
- **Template hygiene.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - A placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, shipped to production.
  - `meta name="format-detection"` is declared three times with conflicting values.
  - `charset` is declared twice (`http-equiv` and `meta charset`).
  - None of these break rendering.
- **Positives.** `lang="en"` is set, and VML/MSO conditionals and `ExternalClass` fixes are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The recipient token `mi_u=` is populated.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA output itself says they may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are genuinely absent, the sender fails Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. It can't be graded from this data.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address could not be checked.** They are in the truncated portion of the source.
- **Sender domain is consistent.** The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, matching the image-host subdomain `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 61 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the visible source shows no resolved destinations or UTM parameters. No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the content images (the `…/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif` files and the `mi.../rp/*.png` recommendations). Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; line-height:100%}` rule.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, delete the leaked `IMPUT HERE` comment, and deduplicate the `format-detection` and `charset` meta tags.
4. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers against the raw message at the sender (not through the relay). Verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC the same way.
5. Re-run the audit with untruncated HTML and resolved tracking redirects. That will cover the unsubscribe link, the postal address, and the UTM and landing-page checks.
6. Review whether five duplicate `rlcdn.com` beacons are needed, and what hashed identifier they pass.
