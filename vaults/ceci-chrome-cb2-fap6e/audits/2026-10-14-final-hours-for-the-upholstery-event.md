# FINAL HOURS FOR THE UPHOLSTERY EVENT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the clean version of the Upholstery Event send. The countdown reads 00:05:23:53, the hero says "FINAL HOURS," and "UP TO 25% OFF" is the biggest thing on the page. The subject matches the hero, so there's no mismatch like this morning's "APP EXCLUSIVE: NEW COLLAB JUST DROPPED" send, where the subject and the body led with different things. The deadline is also consistent with that earlier email, which said the event ends tonight.
- It still lands as a mid-tier promo. It's the second upholstery email today and about the sixth CB2 sale push in five days, so "final hours" is hard to believe. The hero is a single dark sofa photo with thin white type, and the category buttons only name product types. Below the hero, the app, rewards card, gift list and text-signup tiles dilute the one message that matters.
- Verdict: a solid, focused urgency send that suffers from fatigue and generic CTAs.

## What's working

- The subject matches the hero, which opens with "FINAL HOURS — The Semi-Annual Upholstery Event."
- The countdown timer (5 hours left) is credible and sits above the hero.
- "UP TO 25%" is set very large, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- Five stacked, full-width category buttons (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches & Ottomans, Shop All) are easy to tap.
- The nav and layout are clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "UP TO 25%" is the weakest discount CB2 has shown this week, after "up to 60% off" on 10/12 and 10/13. The asterisk and long fine print (no stacking, exclusions) undercut it.
- The hero type is thin and white over a busy wood-and-leather photo. "FINAL HOURS" is tiny and low-contrast, so it's the least visible part of the urgency message.
- The buttons are bare nouns with no verb, and none points to a specific product, price or best-seller.
- The four-tile lower section (app download, 10% back card, gift list, 15% text signup) competes with the sale. The 15% text-signup offer is also a bigger-sounding hook than the sale itself.
- There are no reviews, ratings, prices or product detail anywhere in the body.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the single mood-photo hero with one or two real best-selling sofas shown with "from $X" pricing, and make "FINAL HOURS" bold, high-contrast and larger.
- 2. Rewrite the buttons with a verb and a benefit, such as "Shop sofas up to 25% off" and "Shop sectionals."
- 3. Cut the lower four-tile block on final-hours sends, or reduce it to one line for the text signup. Keep the focus on the deadline.
- 4. Add a proof point, such as a star rating, "ships in X days" or a free-shipping threshold, to give a reason to act today.
- 5. Don't send a second "ends tonight" email in the same day. Send one strong closer in the late afternoon.

- **Subject Alt B:** `5 hours left on 25% off upholstery`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, sectionals and accent chairs. Sale ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call: our best-selling sofas, up to 25% off.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: CB2 "FINAL HOURS FOR THE UPHOLSTERY EVENT"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also loads about 11 plain-HTTP third-party ad pixels and has several missing alt attributes. The QA pass rate is 43%. Several compliance and authentication items could not be verified because the relay didn't expose headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Destination validity and redirect health are unknown, not confirmed good.
- **Mixed-content pixels:** Dotomi/Epsilon cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  
  Many clients block or rewrite non-HTTPS images, so these pixels likely won't fire. Gmail's image proxy may also rewrite or drop them.
- **Pixel volume:** Ten sequential `pub{n}.php` cookie-redirect pixels plus the UCM pixel is a heavy third-party footprint for one email. It adds load and potential spam-filter signal.
- **Tracking pixels:** `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `dv.cb2.com/o/34d9d207-...?mi_cid=...` are the first-party open and view beacons. They are HTTPS and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** six images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text:
  - `e2135484-f06b-4d91-85c2-b6e3bea35d80.jpg`
  - `5316393f-173b-49eb-8fff-cf8f3caee6bc.jpg`
  - `8d0cf8f7-f65e-4cf8-ac29-93edb3ffe504.jpg`
  - `683452bc-673b-457c-91fa-d9dc5a8de369.jpg`
  - `31a528d1-4237-4481-b381-5ff954dec8bb.jpg`
  - `0857626a-9c50-4435-bb41-90e5f11f1af8.jpg`
  
  With images off or in a screen reader, these blocks are unlabeled. If they are the primary promo blocks, the "final hours" message may be lost entirely.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` beacon also lack alt. This is low impact, but `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom restrictions:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` / `-ms-text-size-adjust:none` in the global `*` rule block pinch-zoom and text resizing on mobile.
- **Template leftover:** the source contains the placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Title tag:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. It has low impact.
- **Font paths:** some `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Several fonts are also pulled from `crateandbarrel.com`. Both are fragile cross-domain dependencies, though system fallbacks (Helvetica, sans-serif) exist.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found unresolved in the delivered HTML. This is the Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`%%...%%`) personalization-string style. It indicates a dynamic reviews/SKU block failed to populate, so the token may render as visible text or leave an empty module.
- Beyond that token, no other merge tokens are confirmed broken in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** neither header was found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed violation. If CB2 doesn't send them, it falls short of Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are unknown. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a consistent branded subdomain, which is a good sign.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the truncated source didn't include the footer, so I can't confirm either. This is a data gap.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing pages can't be assessed. All 61 links are wrapped in tracking redirects that weren't resolved, and the HTML was truncated before the link markup.
- Continuity between the subject ("FINAL HOURS... UPHOLSTERY EVENT") and the landing page is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the SFMC data extension or AMPscript source for the reviews module. Add a fallback so the block is suppressed when data is missing.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the six `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Use `alt=""` on all tracking and cookie-sync pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com`, `https://ads.dotomi.com`), or drop them. Reduce the ten `pub{n}.php` redirects if possible.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none`** so users can zoom and resize text.
5. **Confirm header compliance at the source.** Check the raw `.eml` or seed-inbox headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML and resolved redirects.** That would let us verify the unsubscribe footer, UTM parameters and landing-page destinations.
7. **Clean up the template.** Remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder, fix the `//Styles` font paths, and set a meaningful `<title>`.
