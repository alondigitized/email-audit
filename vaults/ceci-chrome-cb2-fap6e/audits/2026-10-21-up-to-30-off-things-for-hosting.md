# Up to 30% off things for hosting
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led Semi-Annual Dining Event send. "UP TO 30% OFF" is the biggest element, set in white over a warm dining-room photo. A "ONE WEEK LEFT" banner sits above it and gives the deadline some credibility. The eye lands on the offer first, and one "SHOP THE SALE" button sits right under it.
- The body is a stack of four category tiles (Dining Furniture, Tabletop, Lighting, Rugs), then a second "SHOP THE DINING EVENT" button. The send then dilutes itself. A Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz sweepstakes block, a lighting feature, app and credit-card promos, a texting signup and a gift-registry block all compete with the sale. The email is about 5,900px long, and the second half is really a different campaign.
- Judgement: a solid, on-message sale email with a strong top third and a cluttered bottom. The subject, "Up to 30% off things for hosting," is vaguer than the hero. This is the same dining event CB2 has pushed in "BACK IN STOCK" on 10/19 and "STEALS OF THE WEEK" on 10/18. Here it finally gets its own send, and the "one week left" urgency is the freshest angle yet.

## What's working

- "UP TO 30% OFF" is the dominant element, so the offer reads in about a second.
- "ONE WEEK LEFT" adds a specific, credible deadline.
- The hero has one clear "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- The four category tiles (furniture, tabletop, lighting, rugs) make it easy to jump to what you want.
- The imagery is consistent and on-brand: warm, moody, editorial.

## What's weak

- The sweepstakes ("WIN THE NEW COLLECTION", $5,000 in gift cards) pulls attention away from the sale and asks for a text signup.
- The lower half stacks too many asks: the app, the credit card, the registry/gift list and the "15% off" text signup.
- The category tiles carry only a label, with no price, no product name and no per-category savings. Nothing tells a shopper what a dining table costs.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is very long and dense. The "up to" offer carries exclusions that a shopper can't see up top.
- The subject says "things for hosting," but the email is about dining furniture, tabletop, lighting and rugs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the second "SHOP THE DINING EVENT" button. Move the sweepstakes, app and card promos into a separate send, or at least cut them to a single slim row.
- 2. Put real product names and sale prices on the category tiles, for example "Dining tables from $X" or "Up to 30% off tabletop," so the click has a reason.
- 3. Align the subject with the hero, and put the deadline in the subject, since "one week left" is the strongest hook.
- 4. Add a short line near the hero with the most important exclusion, or a "see details" link, so the "up to" claim reads as honest.
- 5. Add a social-proof element such as a bestseller badge or review count on a hero product.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed broken-experience defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also carries a large number of tracking-pixel and image hygiene issues. The QA pass rate is 43%. The List-Unsubscribe and Authentication-Results headers could not be verified, because the relay did not capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 62 tracking links went unprobed because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so the QA data can't confirm whether any are broken.
- Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plaintext `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php?...`
- Clients that block or rewrite non-HTTPS content (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop these pixels. They can also trigger mixed-content warnings.
- The pixels have no alt text.
- The tracking beacons `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4397f0c5-...` also have no alt text.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Seven content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text:
  - `0ac086b5-…jpg`
  - `a59e6571-…jpg`
  - `211b33c1-…jpg`
  - `6c910fb4-…jpg`
  - `8220ba3b-…jpg`
  - `5ebc1a2c-…jpg`
  - `5b8313e0-…jpg`
- With images off or a screen reader, these are blank or announced by filename.
- The tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, and ideally `width="1" height="1"` with `aria-hidden="true"`.
- The head sets `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta tag. This blocks pinch-zoom, an accessibility concern.
- The head also includes the template placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, which was never removed. It has no functional impact.
- Web fonts load from two origins: `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`. Several `@font-face` URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, so fallbacks apply.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I could not check the body markup, such as semantic structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or the preheader.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` was found in the rendered HTML. This looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization or dynamic-content variable that did not resolve.
- It may appear as visible text or inside an attribute or URL. The truncated source doesn't show where.
- The `mi_u=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on the beacon URLs look populated.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **WARN, unverified:** no `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header was captured. The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this isn't confirmed as a sender defect.
- **WARN, unverified:** no `Authentication-Results` header was captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements call for one-click unsubscribe. Verify this against the original message headers.
- The visible footer unsubscribe link and the physical address were not in the truncated HTML, so they could not be checked.
- The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is good practice.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Because the 62 click-redirect links were not probed, the QA data can't confirm UTM parameters, final landing URLs or any mismatch with the "Up to 30% off things for hosting" offer.
- No UTM issues can be asserted from the available evidence.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` (high).** Check the dynamic-content block or data extension for the SKU reviews module. Add a fallback or suppress the block when the value is empty. Add a pre-send check for any `%%…%%` left in the rendered HTML.
2. **Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS (medium).** Change `http://login.dotomi.com` and `http://ads.dotomi.com` to `https://`, or drop the unneeded cookie-sync pixels, which are ten requests per open.
3. **Add alt text to the seven content images (medium).** Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`, the Dotomi pixels).
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` (low)** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Check the original message headers (medium).** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and DKIM/SPF/DMARC alignment in the raw message, since the relay may have stripped them.
6. **Probe the 62 redirect links (medium).** Follow them to confirm the landing pages resolve and carry consistent UTM tagging.
7. **Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT…]` comment (low)** and the duplicate-slash font URLs.
