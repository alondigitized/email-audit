# Color palettes that just feel “goop”
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 leaning into the "Goop collection" celebrity/lifestyle tie-in mode — the same designer-spotlight playbook as the Paige Desorbo apartment send from yesterday (2026-08-18, 5/10) — but this time built entirely around color palette curation rather than a specific person's home. The hero "IT JUST FEELS 'GOOP'" copy, followed by a scroll of product-plus-swatch pairings (chair, table, vase, candle holders), is a genuinely different structural idea than the straight sale/lookbook grids that dominated the rest of this week's run.
- The execution is clean — no render bugs, good product photography, legible swatch call-outs — but there's no offer anywhere near the top, and the whole email requires real scrolling before hitting a CTA more useful than "SHOP THE EXCLUSIVE COLLECTION." For a subscriber who's already seen CB2 send twelve-plus distinct creative modes in nine days, a soft aesthetic/color story without pricing or urgency is a low-pressure open, not a high-intent click.

## What's working

- The palette-pairing concept (product photo + swatch chips of 2-3 named colors like "Nadine Chair" paired with "Sage," "Blush," etc.) is a fresh visual device not seen in recent CB2 sends.
- Clean, consistent product photography with warm, editorial styling throughout.
- "SOFAS & SECTIONALS" and "MORE FOR YOU" sections at the bottom give a clear secondary path to browse if the hero story doesn't land.
- No layout or image-loading bugs — everything renders as intended.

## What's weak

- No visible discount, price cut, or promo code anywhere in the email — this is a pure lookbook/story send with zero purchase incentive.
- The hero CTA "SHOP THE EXCLUSIVE COLLECTION" is generic and repeated twice with no product or category specificity.
- Subject line borrows Goop's brand voice/quotation-mark styling in a way that reads more like a lifestyle blog teaser than a retail email — unclear whether recipients associate "Goop" with CB2 at all.
- This is CB2's fourth-plus distinct spotlight/collection-story format in under a week (Studio Anansi Rio collection ran two days running, 2026-08-14 and 2026-08-15, both 5/10) — cadence fatigue is real and this send does nothing to break the pattern with a concrete offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible incentive near the top — even a modest "free shipping" or loyalty-points callout would convert this from a mood-board scroll into an actionable email; right now there's nothing to click for beyond curiosity.
- 2. Make the CTA specific — swap "SHOP THE EXCLUSIVE COLLECTION" for something tied to the featured hero piece, e.g. "Shop the Nadine Chair."
- 3. Clarify the Goop tie-in in the subject or preheader so recipients unfamiliar with Goop don't discard it as spam-adjacent lifestyle content.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: the Goop x CB2 color edit`
- **Subject Alt B:** `5 color pairings, straight from Goop`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sage, blush, and clay — shop the palette Goop can't stop styling.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Beyond the boucle chair: new colors from the Goop collection.`
- 4. Move the "SOFAS & SECTIONALS" best-seller block higher, since it's the only section with a concrete "SHOP NOW" push — right now it's buried after five scrolls of pure mood content.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Legacy-style VML/MSO email template with generally sound structural markup, but it embeds insecure (HTTP) third-party ad-redirect calls and lacks visible authentication/unsubscribe headers in the captured data.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 68 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolvable via automated check) — cannot confirm destination validity.
- Multiple `dotomi.com` ad-network calls served over plain HTTP:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential endpoints, identical query param `d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`)
- Non-HTTPS resources risk being blocked by mail clients that enforce mixed-content/TLS policies (e.g., Apple Mail Privacy Protection, Gmail image proxy over HTTPS), potentially breaking these tracking pixels silently.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 content images missing `alt` text (all under `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/...`), plus the `UCMController` and `pub1–pub10.php` tracking pixels also lack `alt`.
- Screen readers/text-only clients will render these as blank; low risk for the ad-pixels themselves but a real gap for the 8 content images if any are non-decorative.
- Template uses standard MSO/VML conditional comments and media queries for mobile breakpoints (418px, 690px, 768px) — no structural rendering errors detected in the truncated source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No merge tokens/personalization placeholders visible in the truncated HTML source provided.
- No issues found in the visible portion — full source would be needed to confirm absence of broken tokens (e.g., unresolved `%%FIELD%%` or `{{var}}` syntax).

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe compliance from this data alone.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected, meaning one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected — authentication status is unknown from this capture.
- Physical mailing address/unsubscribe link visibility could not be verified from the truncated HTML (footer not included in provided source).

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- Sender domain (`mail.cb2.com`) and image hosts (`image.mail.cb2.com`, `dv.cb2.com`, `mi.cb2.com`) are consistent with the CB2 brand domain — no cross-domain mismatch detected among visible hosts.

7. Recommendations
- Migrate the `dotomi.com` ad-redirect calls (`UCMController`, `pub1.php`–`pub10.php`) to HTTPS to prevent silent tracking failures in TLS-enforcing clients.
