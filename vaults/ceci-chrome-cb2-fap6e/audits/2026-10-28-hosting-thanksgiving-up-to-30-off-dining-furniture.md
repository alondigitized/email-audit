# Hosting Thanksgiving? Up to 30% off dining furniture →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid seasonal Crate & Barrel send with a clear lead. The subject asks "Hosting Thanksgiving?" and offers up to 30% off dining furniture. The hero delivers on that: a dining room scene, "the Thanksgiving dining room event up to 30% off," a priced table and one Shop Now button. For a persona furnishing a home or hosting, that is a coherent, timely message.
- The email then dilutes itself. A brown banner above the hero promotes "only in our app: 20% off mirrors, today only!", which is the offer from this morning's "TODAY ONLY: 20% off mirrors!" send. Below the hero are a four-tile category grid, a hosting guide, two Design Desk blocks, a six-product carousel, a credit card promo and an app download block. That is about eight competing asks, and the hosting guide has the same Thanksgiving theme but is not clearly distinct from the main offer. The mirror banner is the weakest part because it contradicts the dining message.
- Verdict: a good subject and hero, followed by a long catalog scroll. It lands in the upper-middle range. It would be stronger if it kept the dining-furniture story and cut the extra promos.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. Both lead with "Hosting Thanksgiving?" and "up to 30%" on dining furniture.
- The seasonal framing is credible: Thanksgiving, dining tables, chairs and sideboards.
- The hero is visually strong. The warm lifestyle photo, with a named product and a sale price ("Sale $1,693 / Reg. $2,490"), anchors the offer.
- The category tiles (dining tables, seating, storage, 20% off lighting) make the offer easy to browse.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows prices and strikethroughs, which gives product-level detail.

## What's weak

- The "only in our app: 20% off mirrors, today only!" banner sits above the hero and competes with the dining offer. It repeats this morning's mirror send.
- Too many CTAs: Shop Now, Shop Thanksgiving, two shop-the-event buttons, Book Your Free Appointment (twice), Learn More and Download Now.
- The Design Desk sweepstakes ($5,000 entry) and the credit card promo dilute a clear Thanksgiving message.
- The hosting guide image has white text overlapping a busy photo ("The Thanksgiving Hosting Guide"), so legibility is weak. It is also unclear what the guide contains.
- Same-day cadence is heavy. This is at least the fourth Crate & Barrel send on 10/28, after mirrors, Kids and the Black Friday text signup.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the mirror banner and keep the top of the email dedicated to the dining event. That is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the body to hero, category grid and one product row. Move the Design Desk, credit card and app blocks into a separate send.
- 3. Merge the duplicate "Shop the Dining Furniture Event" and "Shop All Dining Furniture" buttons into one.
- 4. Add a deadline to the hero (for example "ends Sunday") and delivery cut-off info, such as "order by Nov 15 for Thanksgiving delivery." This adds real urgency.
- 5. Put dark text or a scrim behind the hosting guide overlay so it is readable.
- 6. Space out same-day sends across the week.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, chairs and sideboards, with delivery in time for the holiday.`
