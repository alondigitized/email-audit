# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I see the Todd Snyder collab front and center and my designer-collab alarm is absolutely going off — in the best way. CB2 is leaning hard into fashion-adjacent home right now, which is my entire personality. The problem? A modal gate-crashes the whole opening act and makes me do paperwork before I can even see what I came for.

## What's working

- **Todd Snyder x CB2 collab hero**: This is exactly the designer-drop energy I chase. Dark, menswear-influenced, not your mom's West Elm collab. I would tap this immediately if the modal wasn't in the way.
- **"SEMIANNUAL DESIGN LEGENDS SALE — Up to 30% off"**: The sale copy is visible underneath the modal fog and "Design Legends" is a name that earns attention. Not "Summer Savings." Actual brand voice.
- **"SHOP BY CATEGORY" rail**: Partially visible at the bottom — the category thumbnails look editorial and dark-toned, consistent with the brand aesthetic I'm here for.
- **Navigation bar**: Clean, legible, category-forward (NEW, FURNITURE, OUTDOOR, RUGS, DECOR, etc.) — easy to find where I'm going in one tap once I close this modal.

## What's weak

- **The forced email modal**: Asking for *both* email *and* a mobile phone number before I've seen a single product is peak dark-pattern energy. The phone field makes this feel like I'm signing up for debt collection, not furniture deals.
- **15% on full-price items\***: That asterisk is working overtime. During a Semiannual Sale where things are already discounted, "full-price items" is doing a lot of exclusionary math I shouldn't have to do in the first ten seconds.
- **No loyalty hook visible**: I shop CB2 seasonally and there's zero acknowledgment that I might have an Insider account or any reason to sign in. Cold start every time.
- **Hero is gender-coded male**: The Todd Snyder imagery reads very tailored-suit, dark-suiting rack — it's great aesthetic but it's not *me* in the scene. I'm the one buying this stuff, not the guy wearing it.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the phone field or make it optional**: Email + opt-in is standard. Requiring a mobile number as a second mandatory field before the page is even visible will lose people like me who won't hand over their number to get 15% on items that probably aren't on sale anyway. Ship this change next week.
- **Swap the modal for a sticky bottom bar**: A slim "Save 15% — enter your email" strip at the bottom lets the hero breathe and doesn't punish me for showing up. Converts better, respects the experience.
- **Add a "Sign In to See Your Insider Perks" hook** next to the hero CTA: One line. I want to know if I have points before I commit to full price.
- **Bring a woman into the Todd Snyder collab visual**: Even one lifestyle shot with a female buyer in that space would close the loop. The suiting rack signals menswear collab; a quick lifestyle addition signals "designed by him, styled by you."
