# The best deals of the year: 2x Rewards + Up to 50% off
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a Crate & Barrel Kids send that tries to land "best deals of the year" and ends up buried in its own scaffolding. The hero is solid: a dark nursery photo under "early black friday… up to 50% off" with a Shop the Sale button. But a red "last Double Rewards this year!" banner sits right above it, and the same banner comes back mid-email. The email has two competing top-line offers, and the eye splits between them.
- The render is very long. After the hero come a four-tile category grid ("up to 50% off" furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs), a Jenny Lind bed feature, a Feeding America block, a product grid, a Crate Card promo, an app download, TheDesignDesk, a store locator and a heavy legal footer. A subscriber who got the 2026-11-11 "Early Black Friday Starts TODAY!" kids send and the 11-10 Double Rewards pushes will see the same creative and the same offer again. This reads as the fourth near-identical Early Black Friday kids blast in three days.
- Judgement: competent and on-brand, but fatigued and overstuffed. It is a 5, with no new reason to open.

## What's working

- The hero is a strong nursery image with a clear "up to 50% off" headline and a visible Shop the Sale button.
- The four-tile grid repeats the discount with category labels (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs), which makes the offer easy to scan.
- The Jenny Lind bed feature has a specific "up to 20% off our iconic Jenny Lind beds" offer and a "Shop Now" button, so it is a concrete product story.
- The product grid shows real items with names and prices (Nuna PIPA, Babyletto, Jenny Lind), which gives price anchoring.
- The Feeding America block gives a seasonal, brand-values break from the sales pitch.

## What's weak

- Two offers compete: "last Double Rewards this year!" in the red banner above the hero, and "up to 50% off" in the hero itself. The Rewards banner repeats mid-email, so the message is muddled.
- The email is far too long, about 7,800px, with a footer full of dense legal text. The Crate Card, app download, TheDesignDesk and store locator blocks dilute the sale.
- "Select styles" under each tile is vague fine print on the discount, and the Rewards banner text is small and hard to read over the hero image.
- Cadence is fatigued. This follows the 11-11 "Early Black Friday Starts TODAY!" kids send and the 11-10 Double Rewards sends with nearly identical creative and offer.
- The "best deals of the year" subject is generic and gives no kids or nursery cue, even though the render is nursery-focused.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero message. Lead with "up to 50% off" and move Double Rewards to a slim, single strip, or cut the repeated mid-email banner.
- 2. Cut the email to about half its length: remove the app, TheDesignDesk, store-locator and Crate Card blocks, or fold them into one footer row.
- 3. Show a specific hero product with a price or percent, such as the Jenny Lind bed at 20% off, instead of an ambient room shot.
- 4. Differentiate from the 11-11 send. Add a real deadline ("ends Nov 17") or a new angle like a gift-by-date, so it doesn't read as a repeat.
- 5. Replace "Select styles" with a clearer exclusions link, and make the banner text legible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nursery deals: up to 50% off + 2x Rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last 2x Rewards of the year on kids' furniture`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Cribs, rugs, bedding & baby gear — ends Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn $/% back in Reward Dollars, plus Jenny Lind beds up to 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on the Movable Ink/Oracle-style template stack (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`). The structure looks sound (`lang="en"`, a media-query fallback, MSO conditionals). The confirmed problems are widespread missing alt text and a leftover template placeholder. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this data. The HTML source was truncated, so anything below the `<head>` is unverified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 74 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp identity sync).
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ff8d12b6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` (open/render tracking).
- The same recipient-level hash (`56aaa2787a67…`) appears in the `mi_u` and `s=` parameters, so a persistent per-recipient identifier is passed to a third party.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed).** The QA check found about 16 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/…`. Examples:
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`
  - `8c4ce5d9-cf7b-4fbe-ba17-b9e2435a0e16.gif`
  - `efbd31b4-2e91-4afa-b657-13bdf73927c5.gif`

  With images blocked, the offer and product content has no text fallback. The font-fix classes in the head suggest the body text may be live, but that can't be verified from the truncated source.
- **Dynamic-content images without alt.** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times. These are likely personalized or real-time modules, and they need alt text if they carry content.
- **Spacer without alt.** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` has no alt text. Use `alt=""` for decorative images.
- **Tracking pixels without alt** (rlcdn, `o.gif`, `dv.` pixel) are low priority. They should be `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`, but they aren't user-visible content.
- **Viewport and zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. The global rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` also disables text scaling in WebKit clients.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. The comment is harmless, but it shows the template wasn't cleaned up and that no custom web font is loaded.
- **Markup validity.**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under that doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared 4 times.
  - The title doesn't match the subject line, which matters for screen readers and web-view tabs.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source or QA data. The tail of the HTML wasn't captured, so this covers the head only.

## 5. Compliance
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.

The QA notes say these headers may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. They are unconfirmed rather than confirmed failures. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a dedicated subdomain, which is normal for a bulk sender. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so verify this against raw headers from a direct-to-inbox copy. The footer unsubscribe link and physical address (CAN-SPAM) fall in the truncated portion and are not assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 74 click-tracked links were not resolved, and the visible source contains no landing-page URLs or UTM parameters. Tracking is done through `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` rather than UTMs. Whether UTMs survive the redirect to crateandbarrel.com needs a follow-up check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add meaningful alt text** to the ~16 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Use `alt=""` on the spacer and tracking pixels.
2. **Add alt text to the six `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` images** if they carry content.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta and drop the `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rule.
4. **Clean up the head.** Escape the `&` in `<title>`, close the `<meta charset>` tag, remove duplicate `format-detection` metas and the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder, and align the title with the subject.
5. **Check the unsubscribe headers and authentication** against raw headers from a direct-to-inbox copy before treating them as failures. This is the highest-value check.
6. **Resolve the click-tracked links** with a redirect-following probe. Confirm destinations return 200 and that UTMs or campaign IDs reach the site.
7. **Raise the QA tool's source cap** so the footer, unsubscribe link and address can be audited. The 57% pass rate is driven mostly by the alt-text warnings and by missing headers that the relay may have stripped.
