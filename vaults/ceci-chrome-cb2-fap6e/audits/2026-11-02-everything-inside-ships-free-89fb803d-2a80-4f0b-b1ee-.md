# EVERYTHING inside ships free →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear free-shipping send that gets buried under its own length. The hero, "it. all. ships. free!", sits over a teal bedding photo with a "Shop All Free Shipping" button. The subject's promise is paid off immediately, and a 2x3 grid of category tiles (kids bedding, baby gear, cuddlies, bath, curtains, pillows) backs it up. The offer is unconditional and easy to grasp.
- After the grid, the email drifts. It adds a 10%-off signup module, a "find your 1,000 hour chair" nursery block, a clearance banner ("100s of new markdowns up to 60% off"), a product grid, a credit-card promo, an app download push and a TheDesignDesk pitch. That is seven or eight competing asks in a send whose subject promised one thing. The legal footer is also long and dense, with several fine-print blocks.
- Judgement: a strong top third and a weak remaining two-thirds. It reads as a 5/10, a little above the brand's usual kitchen-promo level but short of the 2026-11-01 "2400+ reviews" send.

## What's working

- The hero copy matches the subject word for word, so there is no mismatch between inbox and body.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is visible and repeated after the category grid.
- The category tiles use warm, lifestyle-led photography (baby, bedding, pillows) that clearly signals the kids/nursery focus.
- Tile labels such as "kids bedding ships free" and "baby gear ships free" repeat the offer on every tile.
- The product grid at the bottom shows prices, including a markdown on the Jackson treat bag.

## What's weak

- The subject says "EVERYTHING," but the footer fine print apparently carries exclusions and conditions. That is a credibility risk and an honesty problem for the offer.
- The "find your 1,000 hour chair" block has low-contrast body text on a dark photo, so the copy is hard to read.
- The clearance banner, the 10% signup, the app push at "$500" and the credit card promo all compete with the free-shipping story.
- The "it. all. ships. free!" hero is mostly texture with small text. The headline is small relative to the photo, and the eye lingers on the image rather than the offer.
- There is no deadline. "Free shipping" with no end date has no urgency, which matters given the brand's near-daily cadence (five audited sends in the three days before this one).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the second "Shop All Free Shipping" button, or at most keep one secondary module. A free-shipping send should end on the free-shipping CTA.
- 2. Add a real deadline or threshold to the hero, for example "Free shipping through Sunday." Replace the all-caps "EVERYTHING" with a claim the fine print supports.
- 3. Enlarge the hero headline and give it a solid color block behind it so it wins against the photo.
- 4. Fix contrast on the chair block, or remove it.
- 5. Pull the product grid up under the hero and drop the credit-card and TheDesignDesk blocks from this send.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold/MI-style XHTML template. It has a moderate number of code hygiene problems: about 31 images are flagged for missing alt text, there are duplicate or unescaped template artifacts, and several third-party tracking pixels are embedded. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The compliance and authentication warnings look like relay capture gaps rather than confirmed defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so the destination URLs, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Five LiveRamp pixels** load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a67…&n=1` through `n=5`. They are third-party tracking calls with no alt text.
- **Six identical `<img>` tags** point to `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=56aaa2787a67…`. The same asset repeats, likely once per product or module. Each fires a redundant request.
- **The recipient hash looks shared with the third party.** The `mi_u` value on the first-party pixels and the `s` value on the `rlcdn.com` pixels start with the same prefix (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164…`). Both values are truncated in the QA output, so I can't confirm they are identical. If they are, the same subscriber identifier is being sent to a third party.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/df9a7f10-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both are expected for an ESP, but they are image requests that leave the alt attribute missing.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **About 31 images have no alt text.** Content images include:
  - `…/m/1/c1357bbf-af5c-450c-9ee4-2df271630db4.jpg` and about 10 more `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/…/m/1/*.jpg` files.
  - `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`.
  - The scene7 assets `CB%5FCV%5FF26%5FCoreQuiltsDuvets%5F4x5%5Fv2…` and `20261101%5FCBK%5FFall%5FClearance%5FPM%5Fv3%5FTert…`.
  - `Hero%5FEdit%5F3%5FTypography%5F360.gif`.
  - The spacer `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.

  With images blocked, these render as blank space. The pixels and the spacer should carry `alt=""`. The QA output can't distinguish an absent attribute from an empty one, so check which it is.
- **The viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` prevents users from zooming.
- **`<title>Crate & Barrel</title>`** has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`. The title is also generic and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Duplicate and redundant `<head>` tags:**
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" … charset=utf-8>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times, once as `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no` and again as separate `date`, `address` and `telephone` tags.
- **A template placeholder was left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is a misspelled comment and harmless to rendering.
- **Broad CSS resets.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply globally. They can override module-level text styling in some clients.
- **Stale or reused asset paths.** Two scene7 paths point to old campaign folders. One is `…/2025/08_Kids/20250815_CBK_1000Hour_NurseryChair/Hero_Edit_3_Typography_360.gif`, a 2025 asset in a Nov 2026 send. The other is `…/2026/08_Kids/2026_0820_CBK_EverydayFreeShip/…`. This may be intentional reuse, but it's worth confirming the assets are still current.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unrendered `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]` tokens appear in the truncated HTML or the QA output. The per-recipient `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters on the pixels are populated. Because the source is truncated, the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** (QA WARN). QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). One-click unsubscribe is therefore unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverified. The sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **Footer not visible.** The physical address and unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion, so they can't be assessed.

These are unconfirmed rather than proven failures. Check the raw `.eml` or the original delivery headers before treating them as defects.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable. All 71 links were skipped as tracking domains, so UTM parameters, final landing URLs and the "ships free" offer on the destination pages could not be checked. No QA data was produced for this category.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and `alt=""` to the spacers and pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png`, `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer`). Priority: the hero and product images.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and make it match the campaign.
4. Remove the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` tags and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` comment.
5. Deduplicate the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels, and review whether the `rlcdn.com` pixels need to carry the same subscriber hash as the first-party pixels.
6. Re-run QA against the raw message (full headers) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
7. Resolve the redirect chains for a sample of the 71 tracking links, using a header-only follow, to confirm the UTMs and landing pages. Also confirm the 2025 asset reuse is intentional.
