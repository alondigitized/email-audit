## What's weak

- **Zero offer or price anchor** — following yesterday's "Up to 30% off" outdoor sale with a full-price editorial the next day is a sharp downshift; subscribers who opened for the deal won't find one here.
- **"Beautiful & durable" is filler copy** — every Crate & Barrel product is implied to be beautiful and durable; this gives the subject line nothing to work with.
- **Cadence overload** — this is at minimum the second or third C&B touch today; inbox fatigue is real and this email does nothing to justify the extra slot.
- **No hero product** — the multi-section format diffuses focus; the pizza oven is the most distinctive item visible but it's buried mid-scroll, not leading.
- **CTAs are generic "Shop Now" across every section** — no section-specific copy to tell you what you'd actually be shopping.

- 1. **Lead with the pizza oven (or the most distinctive single product)** — pull the standout item to the hero position with a product name and starting price. "New: The Gozney Dome is in. Starts at $XXX" beats a table scene for the outdoor cooking crowd.
- 2. **Reframe the subject around one specific product or scene** — drop the descriptor pair and anchor to a product or a moment.
- 3. **Add a price anchor, even at full price** — "Starting at $XX" or "From $XX" gives the editorial email a purchase reason without requiring a markdown.
- 4. **Give each CTA a noun** — "Shop tabletop" / "Shop the oven" / "Shop barware" instead of "Shop Now" repeated six times.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta (line 7):**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Disables user-initiated zoom on mobile. Accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4, Success Criterion). Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**Conflicting desktop `img` max-width breakpoints:**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width: 768px) { img {max-width: 600px} }
@media only screen and (min-width: 640px) { img {max-width: 640px} }
```
The 640px rule is declared after the 768px rule. At any viewport ≥ 768px, both queries match and the 640px rule wins due to cascade order — the 768px rule is dead code. Images render at 640px max-width at all desktop widths, not 600px as intended for 768px+.

**Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` and `supported-color-schemes: light` force light mode with no dark alternative. Not a bug, but images with white backgrounds will exhibit halo artifacts in dark-mode clients (Apple Mail, iOS Mail) that override forced-light.

**Wildcard `line-height: 100%`:**
```css
* { line-height: 100%; }
```
Applies to all elements including headings. Can collapse line spacing on multi-line text in some clients.
