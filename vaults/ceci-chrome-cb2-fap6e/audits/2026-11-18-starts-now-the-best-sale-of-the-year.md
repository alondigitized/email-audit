# STARTS NOW: THE BEST SALE OF THE YEAR
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the real Black Friday launch, and it reads that way. A full-bleed moody living-room hero says "BLACK FRIDAY / STARTS NOW / UP TO 60% OFF" with a single "SHOP EVERYTHING ON SALE" button. A "FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OF $99+" line sits right under it. The layout is clean and on-brand, and the offer is easy to find.
- The problem is cadence. After a week of "Early Black Friday" sends, including today's "FINAL HOURS: DOUBLE REWARDS" and the 11/17 "up to 70% off clearance" banner, a subscriber now gets "STARTS NOW: THE BEST SALE OF THE YEAR." The sale CB2 ran all week was called early Black Friday and offered up to 70% off. Now the real one starts and the headline discount is lower, at 60%. That undercuts the "best sale" claim. The "Save 15% off in the new year" block adds a second offer that competes with the first.
- Verdict: a polished, well-organized send with a credibility gap. It would work better as a first beat than as the end of a week of "ends tonight" emails.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: one clear offer ("UP TO 60% OFF") and one button, with high-contrast white type on a dark photo.
- Free shipping at $99+ sits directly under the hero, so the second incentive is seen early.
- The category tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Holiday & Gifting, Rugs, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Pillows & Throws) give clean browse paths with lots of imagery.
- The Thanksgiving shipping-deadline strip (11/19, 11/23, 11/24) is useful and adds credible urgency. The struck-through 11/17 date reads as honest.
- The footer carries the rewards program, app download and text-signup. That gives members something beyond the sale.

## What's weak

- "UP TO 60% OFF" is a step down from the 70% clearance banner subscribers saw all week. "Best sale of the year" then sounds hollow.
- There are too many offers. The email shows 60% off, free shipping, 15% off in the new year, 10% back in rewards, and 15% off for texts.
- The category tiles have no prices, no product names and no individual discounts. Every tile is a generic "shop this category" link.
- The legal fine print is long and dense. It sits behind several asterisks on the offers, which weakens the "honest offer" read.
- The email is long, so the second half (app, rewards, text signup) is easy to skip.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear claim. Either name the biggest deal ("Up to 70% off") or drop "best sale of the year" until the deal clearly beats the earlier one.
- 2. Add 2–3 hero products with a price or percent-off tag, such as "Sofas from $X" or "Rugs 40% off," so the tiles are not generic.
- 3. Move the "15% off in the new year" block below the shipping deadlines, or cut it. It dilutes the main offer.
- 4. Add a short deadline line near the top, such as "Sale ends 11/28."
- 5. Tighten the footer to one rewards message and one app/text message.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Our Black Friday sale starts now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus free shipping on $99+ and holiday delivery dates inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs and more, marked down through Cyber Monday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. It has 11 content images with no alt text, and 11 third-party pixels loaded over plain `http://` (also without alt). The 57% QA pass rate comes from warnings only (0 hard failures). The truncated HTML and missing headers leave compliance and link-level checks unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing was not possible.** 64 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 third-party pixels load over `http://`.** These are Dotomi/Conversant cookie-sync beacons:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b...=1`
- Mixed-content or HTTPS-only clients may block these, so the beacons can silently fail. The sheer number of sync pixels (10 `pubN.php` calls) also adds remote requests on open.
- **First-party tracking pixels are present and HTTPS:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/86c8a81b-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. Both lack `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 content images have no alt text.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, including `add58a32-...gif`, `6b5bab9f-...jpg`, `e9c8b20f-...jpg`, `2fd45f06-...jpg`, `5d3a3907-...jpg`, `b016c51e-...jpg`, `3572eb2a-...jpg`, `a0ddcbb9-...jpg`, `047124ce-...jpg`, `20185973-...jpg` and `ca52acde-...jpg`. With images blocked or a screen reader, these render as nothing or as filenames.
- **The tracking pixels also lack alt.** Add `alt=""` to them (and to purely decorative images) so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is suppressed.** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` prevent user text scaling.
- **Template residue is left in the source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows an unreviewed template.
- **Web fonts are loaded cross-brand.** `HNW05` is served from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, and the Lineto and GT Sectra fonts from `www.cb2.com/Styles/...`. Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so a fallback stack is required. `a { font-family: 'Lineto Brown Light Sub', Helvetica, sans-serif }` has one, but `GT Sectra` and `HNW05` fallbacks can't be confirmed in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found** (QA WARN).
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found** (QA WARN).
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found**, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown (QA WARN).
- The QA output itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. I'm treating all three as unverified, not confirmed defects. Test against a raw-header capture (for example a Gmail "Show original") before concluding CB2 is non-compliant.
- The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper alignment.
- The physical address and unsubscribe link live in the footer, which was truncated. They can't be verified from the supplied data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible, because all 64 links go through click-tracking redirects and were not resolved.
- Continuity can't be assessed. Resolve the redirect chain for the primary CTAs and check that the final URLs carry campaign attribution and land on a Black Friday page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the 11 `image.mail.cb2.com` assets. Add `alt=""` to the tracking and sync pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`. Consider whether 10 cookie-sync beacons are needed in the email body at all.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag. Reconsider `text-size-adjust:none` on `*`.
4. Remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` placeholder comment.
5. Re-run the compliance checks on raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) outside the relay, and follow the 64 redirect links to confirm the final URLs and UTM tagging.
