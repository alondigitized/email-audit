# TIME’S RUNNING OUT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a last-call send for the Fall Design Sale, and the hero does the job. "ENDS TOMORROW" sits over "FALL DESIGN SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" on a warm rug photo. The urgency and the offer are both visible in the first screen. The subject, "TIME'S RUNNING OUT," is a different story. It's generic and says nothing about what is ending.
- The body then undercuts the hero. The same email carries three offers: 50% off, 30% off on the button, and 25% off on upholstery. It adds a $1,000 app giveaway, a 10% rewards pitch, and a 15% off text signup. That is too many competing asks for a one-day deadline. The "up to 50%" headline also becomes "SHOP UP TO 30% OFF" on the button right under it. That gap reads as a bait-and-switch.
- CB2 has sent nearly the same sale creative all week. The Oct 3 "Up to 25% off rarely-on-sale upholstery" and Oct 4 "IN STOCK. CUSTOM. ON SALE." both led with the same upholstery event. A subscriber has seen this sectional three times by now. The deadline is the only new thing here, and the subject doesn't use it well.

## What's working

- "ENDS TOMORROW" is a clear, credible deadline placed above the hero headline.
- The hero offer, "UP TO 50% OFF" in large type over a rich rug photo, is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The white category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Pillows, Decor & Mirrors) are clean and easy to scan.
- The upholstery block keeps the earlier campaign visible and gives a second path into the sale.
- The brand look is consistent with prior sends: minimal type and warm, moody photography.

## What's weak

- The subject "TIME'S RUNNING OUT" is vague and all caps, and it has no offer or category.
- The 50% hero doesn't match the "SHOP UP TO 30% OFF" button right below it, which makes the headline discount feel overstated.
- The hero has no button of its own. The CTA is pushed below the fold into a stack of category buttons.
- The $1,000 app giveaway, 10% rewards, 15% text signup and celebration gift list dilute the last-day urgency.
- The upholstery 25% block repeats the Oct 3 and Oct 4 creative, so the cadence feels stale.
- The fine print is long and dense, with footnotes for several different offers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear button in the hero, such as "SHOP THE SALE," and make the discount claim consistent. If most categories are 30% off, lead with "up to 30%." If 50% is real, name which items carry it.
- 2. Cut the app giveaway, rewards, text signup and gift list from a deadline email. Keep the hero, one category grid and the upholstery block.
- 3. Put the deadline in the subject and make it concrete. State the end date and time in the hero.
- 4. Rotate the upholstery creative or drop it. It has run three days in a row.
- 5. Name the best categories behind the 50% figure, for example rugs, since the hero photo is a rug.
