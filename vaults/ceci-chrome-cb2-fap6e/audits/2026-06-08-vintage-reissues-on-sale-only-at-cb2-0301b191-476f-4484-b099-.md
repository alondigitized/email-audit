# Vintage reissues on sale. Only at CB2.
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's "Design Legends Sale" creative again — the same three-panel Gianfranco Frattini / Paul McCobb / Steen Østergaard layout that ran on June 4th as "From the archives. On sale now." The body is essentially identical: MIDSEASON SALE banner, "DESIGN LEGENDS SALE / 25% OFF" hero, sequential designer profiles with furniture photography, a featured price callout around $1,200, and a "MORE FOR YOU" personalized grid at the footer. CB2 has re-skinned the exact same email with a new subject line — a deliberate A/B or a cadence punt.
- The good news is that the new subject, "Vintage reissues on sale. Only at CB2.," is marginally better than June 4's. "Vintage reissues" is more evocative and specific than "From the archives," and "Only at CB2" adds genuine exclusivity framing that the earlier send lacked. That one tweak might extend the shelf life of the creative for a slice of the list who missed the first send.
- The honest verdict: strong creative, second time out. If you opened and clicked June 4, this is noise. If you missed it, this is a well-executed email. The send cadence — CB2's 7th email in 5 days — is the real problem that no subject-line optimization fixes.

## What's working

- **"Vintage reissues" is sharper language** than "From the archives" — it names the thing rather than hinting at it, and feels more collector-coded.
- **"Only at CB2" exclusivity claim** in the subject is new; it gives a reason-to-act that the June 4 subject didn't carry.
- **25% off is front and center** in the hero — no hunting for the offer, no vague "save big" language.
- **Designer-profile structure** still holds up: three editorial beats with distinct photography creates a considered cadence rather than a product dump.
- **"MORE FOR YOU" personalized grid** at the bottom is a solid retention move regardless of how the hero performs.

## What's weak

- **The body is a near-exact repeat of June 4** — any subscriber who opened that send will recognize it immediately and disengage; the de-dupe should have caught this.
- **CTAs are small and hard to differentiate** in the rendered layout — the three designer sections don't have a visually distinct, single primary CTA to drive urgency.
- **No deadline visible** — "MIDSEASON SALE" implies scarcity but nothing in the email communicates when 25% off expires, which undercuts click motivation.
- **"MIDSEASON SALE" banner + "DESIGN LEGENDS SALE" hero** creates two competing sale narratives at the top of the email — a subscriber has to reconcile them.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Don't re-send the same creative body to full-list** — gate re-sends to non-openers only; the June 4 openers will flag this as spam cadence.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the hero** — "25% off ends Sunday" below the hero price would convert the non-urgent browser into an urgent buyer with zero creative change.
- 3. **Consolidate to one CTA anchor** — pick the strongest designer/product and give it a full-width button. Right now the three-panel structure distributes clicks rather than concentrating them.
- 4. **Resolve the dual sale-name tension** — either the banner calls it "Design Legends Sale" or the hero does; having both "MIDSEASON SALE" and "DESIGN LEGENDS SALE" in the first two rows muddies the message.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta** blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility violation (WCAG 1.4.4 — Resize Text).
  ```html
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` on `*` selector** — applied globally, not just to body. This suppresses OS-level text scaling across all elements, compounding the zoom-lock above.

- **Conflicting `img` max-width media queries** — two separate `@media` blocks both target desktop and set conflicting `max-width` values. The 690px rule will override the 768px rule due to cascade order, likely unintentionally:
  ```css
  @media only screen and (min-width: 768px) { img { max-width: 100% !important } }
  @media only screen and (min-width: 690px) { img { max-width: 690px } }  /* wins */
  ```

- **Mobile breakpoint is 418px** — `.mobile` class targets `max-width: 418px`, which misses devices 419–479px wide (e.g., Android mid-range). Standard email practice is 480px or 600px.

- **Alt text on images** — cannot verify from truncated source. Flag for manual check.
