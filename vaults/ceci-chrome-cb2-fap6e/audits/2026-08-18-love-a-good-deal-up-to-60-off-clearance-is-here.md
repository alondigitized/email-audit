# Love a good deal? Up to 60% off clearance is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the rare Crate & Barrel send that leads with an actual number: "up to 60% off clearance" sits right in the hero over a clean beach photo, with an immediate "Shop Now" CTA and an eight-category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) so a shopper can jump straight to what they want. That's a real improvement on the sprawling, no-offer sends in this brand's recent history (the 8/16 "Their room isn't complete without…" and 8/16 storage-organization emails scored 5/10 for exactly this lack of focus).
- But the discipline evaporates after the clearance grid. By module three it's pivoted to a "clean kitchen" push, then a SMEG toaster spotlight, then a Design Desk sweepstakes, a kitchen checklist, a "we love these for you" product rail, and a new credit card pitch — none of which are clearance, none of which are discounted. The clearance offer that earned the strong open is essentially abandoned two scrolls in, so this reads as the brand's default kitchen-sink template with a discount headline bolted onto the top.
- Verdict: strong hook, weak follow-through — worth opening for the subject line, but the body doesn't reward that click past the first fold.

## What's working

- Hero states a concrete, specific offer ("up to 60% off clearance") with a clear "Shop Now" button.
- The eight-tile category grid right below the hero lets a shopper self-select without scrolling through unrelated content first.
- "While quantities last" adds credible urgency without being spammy.
- "Shop All Clearance" button caps the clearance section cleanly before the email shifts topics.
- No render bugs — images, type, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The clearance offer disappears completely after the "Shop All Clearance" button — everything below (clean kitchen, SMEG toaster, Design Desk sweepstakes, kitchen checklist, product rail, credit card) is full-price, unrelated content.
- No specific discount percentage is shown anywhere except the vague "up to 60%" — no per-category or per-item pricing to make the offer feel real.
- The email is extremely long (roughly a dozen distinct modules) for what was sold as a clearance sale.
- The credit card pitch at the bottom is a hard tonal left turn from a discount email and dilutes the CTA focus.
- "we love these for you" product rail shows full prices with no indication these are also clearance or on sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after "Shop All Clearance" — or move the kitchen/SMEG/Design Desk/card modules to a separate send. A clearance email should stay a clearance email start to finish; right now less than a quarter of it is on-topic.
- 2. Add real numbers to the category grid tiles (e.g., "furniture — up to 40% off," "rugs — up to 60% off") so shoppers can prioritize by discount depth, not just category.
- 3. Show a strikethrough price or "extra 20% off marked price" on at least the hero image to make the "up to 60%" claim concrete rather than abstract.
- 4. If quantities are genuinely limited, add a soft deadline ("through Sunday") to reinforce "while quantities last" with a date.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off clearance — furniture, rugs, decor & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final markdowns: up to 60% off before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop 8 clearance categories while quantities last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, rugs, lighting & more — deepest discounts of the season`
