# ENDS TODAY: Up to 30% off accent chairs, tables & rugs!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, deadline-led sale send that loses focus about a third of the way down. The top 25% works. A wool rug hero is priced at $2,570.25 against a regular $3,699. Under it is "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off," a "Shop The Sale" button, and a three-tile grid for tables, chairs and rugs (up to 60% off). A shopper who wanted the sale gets everything they need quickly.
- Then the email keeps going, to roughly 8,400px. It adds a "more ways to complete your home" block, a 2026 holiday collection banner, a Black Friday signup, two separate store-opening promos (La Cantera and Corte Madera), a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download, a design appointment and a footer nav. The "ENDS TODAY" urgency appears in the subject, but I don't see a deadline reinforced in the body. For a one-day sale, that is a miss.
- Overall it is a competent sale email with a strong first screen and a bloated middle. Compared with the 11/02 "EVERYTHING inside ships free" send, which was also buried under its own length, this repeats the same structural habit.

## What's working

- The hero rug shows a real sale price ($2,570.25) against a regular price ($3,699), so the discount is concrete.
- The headline "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off" matches the subject and states one offer.
- The "Shop The Sale" button is a dark maroon pill that stands out against the white background.
- The category tiles for tables, chairs and rugs give clear entry points, and the rugs tile carries the stronger "up to 60% off" claim.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows product names and prices, which gives browsers something specific to click.

## What's weak

- The email runs about 8,400px, with the sale diluted by store openings, a Black Friday signup, a credit card pitch, an app promo and a design appointment.
- The body never repeats "ends today," so the subject's urgency isn't carried into the email.
- The text on the tiles, such as "up to 30% off accent tables," sits over busy photography and is small and low contrast at inbox width.
- Two store-opening blocks (La Cantera and Corte Madera) are irrelevant to most recipients and push the product content far below the fold.
- Several secondary CTAs ("Shop All Sale," "Shop Now," "Store Details," "Get Directions," "Learn More," "Download Now") compete with the primary one. There are no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the sale. Keep the hero, the three tiles and the product grid, and move the store openings, credit card, app and design promos to other sends or to the footer. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Add a visible deadline to the hero, such as "Ends tonight at midnight CT," ideally above or next to the headline.
- 3. Put the price and discount in large type on the tiles instead of small text over photography.
- 4. Add a few star ratings or "best seller" tags to the product grid.
- 5. Use "Shop Accent Chairs" or "Shop Rugs on Sale" for the button copy instead of the generic "Shop The Sale."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 30% off accent chairs & tables`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: rugs up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale prices on accent chairs, tables and wool rugs end at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our Brighton wool rug is $2,570 today, and more picks are marked down.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"`, an MSO/Outlook fallback, and a mobile breakpoint. The main confirmed defects are about 31 images flagged for missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks pinch-zoom. Compliance headers and link destinations could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **63 links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and 404s are unverified.
- **Third-party tracking pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` to `n=5` beacons.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/a7934bb4-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`.
- **Possible identifier sharing:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964...` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` images matches the `s=56aaa2787a...` value on `sr.rlcdn.com`. That suggests one recipient-level hash is sent to a third-party domain. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 31 images.** The QA check can't distinguish an absent `alt` from an intentional `alt=""`.
  - **Likely content images (17):** 16 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/...` (for example `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `cd56cdc7-...gif`, `056e0bf8-...jpg`) and one on `s7d5.scene7.com` (`2026_1011_CB_HolidayDecorating_Secondary`).
  - **Seven images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`** (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`). These look like dynamically rendered content.
  - **Tracking pixels (7):** the `rlcdn`, `o.gif`, and `dv.` beacons above. They need `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`, but should not be treated as content defects.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing is suppressed.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the earlier `100%` values.
- **Markup nits:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times and `charset` is declared twice (an http-equiv tag and `<meta charset>`).
  - There is a leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- **Truncated source.** The rest of the document (body markup, footer) wasn't available, so there are no further findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 63 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and no resolved destinations or UTM parameters are visible. Only the redirect wrappers were observed, so UTM presence, landing-page match to "accent chairs, tables & rugs," and the "ends today" offer state are all unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 17 content images, including the `scene7` banner. Use `alt=""` on decorative images and tracking pixels, with explicit `width`/`height`.
2. Confirm that the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images carry alt text from the template. Dynamic content blocks often lose it.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag. Drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate `meta` tags and the placeholder comment.
5. Re-run QA with redirect-following enabled for the click domain. Check final URLs, status codes, and UTM presence, and confirm the landing pages match the offer.
6. Obtain raw headers from a non-relay delivery to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Also run a full-source check for the unsubscribe link and postal address.
7. Review whether sharing the `mi_u` hash with `sr.rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy disclosure.
