# Up to 60% off amazing finds, right this way →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's clearance sweep — "up to 60% off clearance," a beach hero, then a full category grid (furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop, lighting, decor, rugs, bedding). The clean, category-first layout works, and it delivers on the subject immediately with the hero. But the email doesn't stop there — it keeps going through a Levain bakery collab, a $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product carousel, a credit card pitch, and an app download push. Compare to 2026-08-08's wine-glasses send (7/10) which stayed disciplined on one category before cross-selling — this one sprawls into five unrelated asks after the clearance section, diluting the actual sale.
- The clearance grid itself is well-executed: eight clean category tiles with consistent photography, "Shop All Clearance" CTA, and a "ships free" badge on bedding. If the email ended there, this would be a strong, focused send. Instead it turns into a everything-bagel of secondary campaigns that competes with the actual offer for attention.

## What's working

- Hero delivers the subject line's offer instantly — "up to 60% off clearance" with a clean beach lifestyle image and a visible "Shop Now" button.
- Category grid is well-organized (8 clean tiles: furniture, outdoor, kitchen, tabletop & bar, lighting, decor & pillows, rugs, bedding & bath) with a clear "Shop All Clearance" CTA beneath.
- "Ships Free" badge on bedding & bath adds a concrete, useful signal.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.
- "While quantities last" adds credible mild urgency without being spammy.

## What's weak

- The email buries its own lede: after the clearance grid, it pivots into a bakery collab, a $5,000 sweepstakes, a curated furniture carousel, a credit card pitch, and an app download — five separate asks diluting the clearance CTA.
- No specific discount percentages shown on individual clearance categories — just one blanket "up to 60%," so it's unclear what's actually reduced.
- The "we love these for you" product carousel shows full prices with no strikethrough or sale price, so it reads as unrelated to the clearance theme, not reinforcing it.
- Sweepstakes copy ("enter for a chance to win $5000") competes directly with the clearance offer's attention within the same email.
- Visual hierarchy after the hero flattens — by the fourth section it's hard to tell this is still "the clearance email."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Clearance" or move the bakery collab, sweepstakes, and card pitch to separate sends — a focused clearance-only email will outperform this bundle.
- 2. Add specific % or $ savings per category tile (e.g., "outdoor — up to 50% off") instead of one blanket top-line number.
- 3. If the carousel stays, badge it "clearance" or show a strike-through price — right now it looks like a non-sale up-sell sitting inside a sale email.
- 4. Move "Ships Free" from bedding-only to a callout under the whole grid if it applies broadly — it's a strong signal that's currently easy to miss.
- 5. Reduce the number of distinct CTAs — currently 6+ buttons (Shop Now, Shop All Clearance, Shop the Collab, Book Your Free Appointment, Learn More, Download Now) split attention.
