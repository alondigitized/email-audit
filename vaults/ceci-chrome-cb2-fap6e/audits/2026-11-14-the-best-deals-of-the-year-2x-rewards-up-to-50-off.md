# The best deals of the year: 2x Rewards + Up to 50% off
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, offer-heavy Early Black Friday blast from Crate & Barrel Kids. It opens with a nursery hero and a red "last Double Rewards this year! … early black friday is here! up to 50% off" panel, then a 2x2 category grid (furniture, bedding & bath, baby gear, rugs). The offer is clear and the brand is recognizable. Then it keeps going. Rewards repeat, there's a 60% clearance block, a gift block, a TheDesignDesk sweepstakes, a product grid, a credit card promo and an app promo. The footer is very long.
- The email has too many competing asks. The Double Rewards message appears at least twice, and there are about seven "Shop" or "Learn More" buttons, so the eye never settles on one action. The hero text over the photo also looks low-contrast and hard to read at this size. That matters because it's the most important line.
- Compared with the 11/13 sends, it's less focused. The "Parents tell all" send had a clear story, and "Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards" was a tight offer. This one is a kitchen-sink recap of the sale. It's a solid 6, and it would score higher if it were half the length.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% off" headline and the "Double Rewards" mention give a concrete offer with a deadline (Nov. 12 in the hero copy, which looks inconsistent with the Nov. 14 send date).
- The nursery hero photo is on-category for a kids and baby audience.
- The 2x2 grid with specific percentages (50% off furniture, 50% off bedding & bath, 25% off baby gear, 50% off rugs) is easy to scan.
- A named product grid with prices ("we love these for you") gives real products to click.
- The sender is recognizable and the branding is consistent.

## What's weak

- The hero overlay text is low-contrast and small against the busy photo, so the headline is hard to read.
- The Double Rewards message is repeated in the hero and again in a mid-email red banner.
- Too many CTAs and modules (clearance, gifts, sweepstakes, credit card, app, store appointment) dilute the main offer.
- The deadline language looks stale. "Nov. 12" appears in an email sent Nov. 14, which undercuts the urgency.
- The email is very long, with a dense legal footer, and the sweepstakes and app blocks are off-message for a sale send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut it to one story: the sale, then the grid, then products. Drop the sweepstakes, app and store-appointment blocks into a separate send.
- 2. Fix the hero. Put the offer in a solid-color panel or large high-contrast type so "Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards" reads instantly.
- 3. Show Double Rewards once, and check that the deadline dates are correct and current.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific ("Shop Nursery Furniture 50% Off") rather than a generic "Shop the Sale".
- 5. Add reviews or star ratings to the product grid.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, bedding and baby gear marked down, plus double Reward Dollars on everything.`

# Technical Audit: "The best deals of the year: 2x Rewards + Up to 50% off"

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 75 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified, so no broken links are confirmed.
- **Tracking infrastructure seen in the QA output:**

  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`

- **Stale asset names:** `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif` is dated Sept 26, while the other asset is dated Nov 4. This may be a reused asset. I can't confirm it is stale from the code alone.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flags 30 `<img>` elements without alt text.
  - 17 are content images, for example `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`, the two `s7d5.scene7.com` images, and the `.gif` `8c4ce5d9-…`.
  - 13 are tracking pixels (`mi.`, `rlcdn`, `dv.`). These should carry `alt=""`.
  - The check can't tell a missing attribute from an intentional `alt=""`. The 17 content images are the ones that matter if the images are links, because image-blocked or screen-reader users then get no link label.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 accessibility concern.
- **Text sizing:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with the earlier `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. They disable iOS text scaling.
- **Conflicting image caps:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is declared before `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later rule wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
- **Source hygiene:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid in XHTML. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` template placeholder comment was left in the source.
  - `meta charset` is declared twice (http-equiv and `charset`).
  - `meta format-detection` is declared three times.
  - These are harmless but sloppy.
- **Language:** `lang="en"` is present, which is good.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved per-recipient identifiers. The source is truncated, so tokens in the body or footer can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing the header. It is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require the header.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN, RFC 8058):** One-click unsubscribe support is unconfirmed, for the same reason.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, which is consistent with a dedicated sending domain, but that is not proof.
- **Footer unverified:** The unsubscribe link and physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) can't be confirmed because the HTML is truncated before the footer.
- **Re-check source:** Re-test against the raw `.eml`, not the relay copy, before treating the header warnings as real findings.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The 75 click-tracked links were not resolved, so no UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible. Nothing was found to be misaligned.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Verify headers on the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC outside the AgentMail relay.
2. **Add alt text.** Give the 17 content images descriptive `alt` text, and set `alt=""` on the 13 tracking pixels.
3. **Fix the viewport meta.** Remove `maximum-scale=1`.
4. **Fix the CSS conflicts.** Drop the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` declarations that override `100%`. Reorder or consolidate the `img{max-width}` media queries (the 768px rule should come last).
5. **Clean up the template.** Escape `&` in `<title>`, remove duplicate meta tags, and delete the leftover placeholder comment.
6. **Resolve the click-tracked links.** Follow the 75 skipped redirects with a redirect-following resolver to check final status codes and UTM presence.
7. **Re-run on full HTML.** Use the untruncated source to check the footer unsubscribe link, postal address and any unrendered tokens.
