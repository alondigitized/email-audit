# You need to see this designer’s coastal bedroom
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a designer-story email that actually delivers on its subject line — "a coastal bedroom designed for relaxation," with a named designer (Lisa Pollock) and quote-driven copy ("slowing down influenced every choice"). That's a real change from the scattershot clearance/markdown sends this brand has been running (2026-08-21's "NEW markdowns!" and the 25%-off bedroom send). The lifestyle photography is genuinely strong — canopy bed, cane furniture, muted coastal palette — and it reads as editorial rather than discount-bin.
- The problem is length and focus. After the strong coastal-bedroom opener, the email sprawls into a mattress upsell, a Design Desk sweepstakes, "we love these for you" cross-sell tiles, a credit card pitch, and an app download banner — six or seven distinct asks stacked in one send. The coastal story that earned the open gets buried by unrelated modules by the middle of the scroll.
- Net: strong hook, weak follow-through. This is a 6 — better than the brand's typical clearance blast, worse than it could be if it stayed on-theme.

## What's working

- Hero image and headline directly match the subject line — no bait-and-switch, unlike the "Chopped fresh garlic" send.
- Named designer (Lisa Pollock) with a direct quote gives it editorial credibility over generic markdown copy.
- Clean single "Shop the Look" CTA sits directly under the hero image.
- Coastal color story (cane, linen, sage/blue) is visually consistent across the first several product blocks.
- Anchored pricing is visible on at least one product ("up to 25% off your bedroom refresh," Coveleur Lightwashed Canopy Bed).

## What's weak

- The email keeps going well past the coastal-bedroom story — mattress, sweepstakes, credit card, app download, "we love these for you" — diluting the hook that got the open.
- "new markdowns! up to 60% off clearance" appears mid-email with no visual transition, clashing with the calm designer narrative above it.
- Multiple competing CTAs ("Shop the Look," "Shop Now," "Shop Beautyrest," "Book Your Appointment," "Get a Free Appointment," "Learn More") dilute what the primary action should be.
- No visible pricing on the hero product itself — the reader has to scroll several screens before hitting a number.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the coastal collection section (through "Rattan, woven elements and linen textiles"). Move mattress/credit card/sweepstakes/app-download into separate sends — this email doesn't need to do six jobs.
- 2. Put a price or "shop the bed from $X" directly on the hero image module, not several scrolls down.
- 3. Drop the mid-email "new markdowns! up to 60% off clearance" band entirely — it's the same clearance beat from yesterday's send and undercuts the designer-story premium framing.
- 4. Consolidate to one CTA style/color used consistently, with "Shop the Look" as the only CTA in the top half.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Lisa Pollock's calming bedroom, shoppable`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The linen, the cane, the quiet palette — all sourced.`
