# Your exclusive first look at sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 leading with an "ONLY ON THE APP" gate before you even see the sale — an app-download phone mockup and the "SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT / UP TO 25% OFF" sits inside that mockup, buried behind a screen-in-a-screen visual instead of being the hero itself. The subject promises "your exclusive first look at sale" but the email actually opens with an app-install pitch, then a category scroll, and only hits an actual "SALE ON NOW UP TO 50% OFF" banner two-thirds down. That's a mismatch between what got you to open and what you see first.
- Compared to the brand's recent cadence — the 2026-09-23 "STEALS OF THE WEEK" single-offer send and the 2026-09-22 "Just one more day" urgency push — this one buries the lede further than any of those. It's visually polished (CB2's photography is consistently strong), but the structure works against conversion: the "exclusive first look" promise in the subject is never paid off with a distinct visual moment, and the real sale offer (50% off) is disconnected from the smaller 25% upholstery offer up top with no clear hierarchy between them.
- Judgement: handsome CB2 creative, but the offer architecture is confused — two competing discounts, no clear single CTA, and the strongest deal is scrolled past the fold.

## What's working

- Clean, uncluttered nav bar and consistent CB2 serif branding throughout.
- Product photography (sofa, chairs, ceramics, lighting) is warm, well-lit, and on-brand.
- Category grid (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs) gives clear post-click navigation options.
- Footer benefits block (app download, rewards, registry, SMS signup) is well-organized with distinct icons per offer.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, no overlap.

## What's weak

- The subject says "first look at sale" but the first thing shown is an app-download pitch with a smaller 25% offer, not the sale itself.
- Two different, unreconciled discounts appear (25% off upholstery via app, 50% off sitewide via "SALE ON NOW") with no explanation of how they relate — reads like bait-and-switch.
- No single unambiguous primary CTA — "GET THE APP," "SHOP NOW," "BEST SELLERS," and four category links all compete for the click.
- The real sale banner ("SALE ON NOW UP TO 50% OFF") is roughly 2,600px down a 4,000px email — well past the fold on any device.
- Fine print is dense, stacked four times at the bottom, with asterisks that are never clearly tied back to which offer they modify.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move "SALE ON NOW — UP TO 50% OFF" to the very top as the hero, since it's the strongest, least-restricted offer — the subject already promised a sale, so open on the sale.
- 2. Cut the app-download upsell out of the primary hero; it can live in the footer where the other app benefits already are.
- 3. Pick one CTA for the top of the email tied directly to the 50% sale ("SHOP THE SALE") and remove competing top-of-fold buttons.
- 4. If the upholstery/app-exclusive offer stays, clearly label it as a separate, secondary promotion ("Also: 25% off upholstery in the app") so it doesn't read as a bait-and-switch against the 50% headline.
- 5. Compress the footer legal/fine-print blocks — four stacked disclaimers with unlinked asterisks erode trust; group each asterisk directly under its offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sale is on: up to 50% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First look: our biggest sale of the season`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off sitewide, plus 25% off upholstery in the app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals, best sellers, and rugs — all part of the sale.`
