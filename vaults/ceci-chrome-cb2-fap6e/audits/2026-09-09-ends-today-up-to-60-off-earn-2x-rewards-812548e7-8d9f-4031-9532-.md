# ENDS TODAY: Up to 60% off + earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' finale send for the Labor Day marathon I've now tracked all week — but notably, this one drops the countdown timer that's appeared in every prior send I've logged (2026-09-08's "ENDS TOMORROW ⏰" and "Up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards ends tomorrow!"). The layout is clean: beach hero, "Ends Today!" headline, red CTA, then a well-organized 8-tile category grid (kids furniture, nursery bedding, baby bedding, nursery chairs, rugs, storage, lighting) each with its own specific discount callout.
- The render is bug-free and the category grid does real work — it's more useful than a single hero-and-CTA because it lets a shopper self-sort by category before clicking. But the email buries the lede: "Ends Today" urgency has no clock, no time-of-day cutoff, and no visual urgency treatment (no red banner, no ticking numbers) compared to yesterday's sends, which actually undercuts the finale message. This is also the seventh-plus near-identical modular template this week, so fatigue is real for a daily subscriber.
- Overall this is a solid, competent execution of the same campaign — better organized than average — but it's stale by cadence and the "today" urgency is undersold visually.

## What's working

- Clear hero headline "Ends Today! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" with a visible red "Shop the Sale" CTA button.
- Category grid is genuinely useful — 8 tiles each with a specific, distinct discount (up to 50% off kids furniture, up to 60% off nursery furniture, up to 60% off lighting & decor, etc.).
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, no overlapping text, no broken layout.
- Secondary content (Design Desk consultation, Jellycat restock, "we love these for you" curated picks) adds relevant cross-sell without feeling like clutter.
- Rewards Card upsell and app download CTA are present but appropriately de-prioritized below the fold.

## What's weak

- No countdown timer or explicit deadline time — "Ends Today" with zero visual urgency treatment (no clock icon, no red alert bar) is a step down from yesterday's sends and undersells the actual scarcity.
- This is at least the seventh nearly identical modular template from this brand family this week — same beach hero pattern, same grid, same Design Desk block, same Jellycat callout.
- The hero image (beach/ocean) has no visible product or kid/nursery context — disconnected from "kids furniture and bedding" messaging.
- "we love these for you" personalized picks section shows adult nursery gliders and strollers with visible prices but no strikethrough/sale pricing shown, undercutting the "60% off" promise for that section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add urgency treatment matching yesterday's sends — a countdown timer or bold "ends at midnight ET" callout — since this is explicitly the last-chance email and currently reads no more urgent than a mid-sale one.
- 2. Swap the ocean hero for a nursery/kids-room lifestyle shot so the visual matches the product category immediately.
- 3. Show strikethrough or sale-adjusted pricing on the "we love these for you" personalized product tiles — right now those look full-price despite a 60%-off campaign.
- 4. Vary the template structure from the six-plus prior sends this week — even reordering module sequence or swapping the Design Desk placement would reduce fatigue.
