# Early Black Friday is on: Up to 30% off furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a furniture-focused Early Black Friday send with a clear hero: "up to 30% off furniture + Double Rewards" and a "Shop Furniture Sale" button. It follows the earlier 2026-11-11 sends, "Early Black Friday Starts TODAY! Up to 50% off + Earn 2x Rewards" and "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW! Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!". It is a segmented version of the same campaign, and the furniture subject is the most specific of the three.
- The body then sprawls. The render is very long and packs in a bestseller dresser, a holiday-ready section, a kitchen and entertaining grid, a last-chance Double Rewards block, a TheDesignDesk appointment pitch, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card offer, an app download and a second design-service block. Each section has its own CTA, so the furniture offer gets diluted after the first screen.
- Verdict: a solid, targeted send with a clean top, undermined by kitchen-sink length. Given the near-daily cadence since 2026-11-10 (Fall Sales Event, Double Rewards, Early Black Friday), fatigue is the main risk.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match: "up to 30% off furniture" appears in both, so the promise is carried through.
- The category tile grid (dining, bedroom, sofas & sectionals, storage, accent tables, home office) lets a furniture shopper self-select.
- The "save on this bestseller" dresser callout gives one concrete product with a price.
- Double Rewards is stated near the top with a deadline, which gives members a reason to act.
- The photography is consistent and seasonal, with warm holiday styling.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with at least eight distinct sections. The furniture offer is buried under kitchen, drinkware, design-service and app promos.
- The top has two small banner blocks (Double Rewards and Early Black Friday) before the logo and hero. That puts a lot of small text above the offer.
- Several CTAs compete ("Shop Furniture Sale," "Learn More," "Shop Early Black Friday," "Shop Top Categories," "Book Your Free Appointment," "Download Now"). None of them dominates.
- The offer stacks up to 30% off, Double Rewards, up to 50% off holiday décor and 10% off with a credit card. Several of these have fine print that is hard to read at this size.
- This is another near-identical Early Black Friday send on the same day as the other two, so a recipient will see it as repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the furniture hero, the category grid, the bestseller and one rewards reminder. Move the design service, app and credit card pitches to a separate send or the footer.
- 2. Move the logo and hero to the top and put the rewards and deadline strip underneath. Make the 30% off figure the largest text.
- 3. Use one primary CTA style ("Shop Furniture Deals") and make the rest secondary links.
- 4. Suppress or consolidate same-day sends. Segment so furniture browsers get this one and not the other two.
- 5. Show prices or "from $X" under the product picks in the "we love these for you" block, which is currently light on detail.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a Marigold/Cheetah-style template (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) with an XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. The visible problems are about 49 images without alt text and a few template hygiene issues. The compliance and authentication checks came back unverified rather than failed. The HTML was truncated and 87 links were never probed, so link and UTM conclusions are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing was skipped.** 87 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM presence are unverified.
- **Five identical third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…cfd8&n=1` through `&n=5` fire five times per open. Only `n` changes. This is a LiveRamp (rlcdn) sync pixel, so it adds extra third-party requests per render.
- **Possible identifier leak.** The `s=` value begins with the same hash as the first-party `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…` on `mi.crateandbarrel.com` URLs. A recipient-level identifier therefore appears to go to a third-party domain. The HTML can't confirm what the hash encodes, but it is a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- **Tracking resources.** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is an open pixel. `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/196a4cd7-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another tracking image.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 49 images** (QA WARN). They fall into three groups:
  - About 35 content images at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|.gif` and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`. A screen reader or image-blocked client gets no content, and some of these are likely clickable (linked images).
  - 7 dynamic images at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=…` (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`), likely product recommendation tiles.
  - 7 tracking pixels (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, the `dv.` image). These should carry `alt=""`, so they matter less.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` stops pinch-zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate and conflicting declarations in `<head>`:**
  - `<meta charset>` plus `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - Three separate `format-detection` metas.
  - `.ReadMsgBody` defined twice.
- **Conflicting image `max-width` rules.** Both `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` match at 768px and above. The later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule never applies.
- **Leftover template comment.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd, unfilled placeholder in the source. It isn't rendered.
- **Large text-size override.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` applies a universal selector to every element, which disables user text-size scaling in WebKit clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[[…]]`). `mi_u` and `mi_cid` are populated. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body below the cut weren't inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three items below are unverified rather than confirmed defects. The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip headers.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** (WARN). It may have been dropped by the AgentMail relay.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found** (WARN). The RFC 8058 one-click header is also absent from the captured headers, so one-click support can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, which is consistent with a branded setup.
- **Footer not inspected.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall past the truncation point, so they were not verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 87 click-tracking links were not resolved, and the visible HTML shows no `utm_*` parameters on any destination. Tracking appears to rely on the `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` redirect parameters instead. Whether the landing pages carry campaign attribution into analytics is unconfirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~35 content images and the 7 `rp/*.png` tiles. Set `alt=""` on the tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif`, `dv.` image).
2. Capture raw headers from the original delivery, not the relay, and re-test `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
3. Resolve a sample of the redirect links (follow one hop with a non-prefetch UA) and check for `utm_*` and landing-page match.
4. Review why the same `rlcdn.com` pixel fires five times, and what the `s=` hash encodes.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1`, escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate metas, and fix the overlapping `img max-width` media queries.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, unsubscribe link and address can be checked.
