- The hero ("INTRODUCING WALLCOVERINGS") and the room-scene panels are on-brand, cohesive and warm. They look like a design magazine, not a flyer.
- The "SHOP WALLCOVERINGS" button sits right under a one-line value statement, so the launch CTA is clear.
- The free-swatches prompt ("ORDER FREE SWATCHES") lowers the risk of buying wallpaper unseen.
- Designer attribution (Matthew Williamson, LAURENT STUDIO) adds credibility.

## What's weak

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" sale banner is far below the fold. The only offer in the email is the least visible thing in it.
- Two headline messages compete: launch versus sale. Nothing in the email says whether the sale covers wallpaper.
- The wallpaper section has no prices, no sizing or roll-coverage info and no reviews.
- The "More For You" candle and eucalyptus tiles are generic filler. They have no connection to either campaign.

- 2. State whether wallpaper is included in the sale. Add a "from $X per roll" price line to the wallpaper section.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token left in the output. It also has 10 insecure `http://` tracking images and many images with no alt text. The QA pass rate is 43%. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Mixed content:** 11 images load over `http://`, which many clients block or rewrite. One is `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`. The other 10 are the Dotomi cookie-sync pixels `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`.
- **Third-party pixel stack:** Beyond first-party `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/98762669-...`, the email also fires 11 Dotomi requests. Many clients load this stack only after images are enabled, and it adds third-party load for little reporting value.
- **Unverified links:** 69 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were checked for a valid destination. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Raw merge token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the rendered output. This is the QA run's only FAIL. It is an unresolved dynamic-content (reviews/SKU list) placeholder, so that block either shows the literal text or renders empty.
- **Missing alt text on content images:** 9 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt attribute. They are `e7496c59-…`, `db76bf5a-…`, `16187dd2-…`, `a48cd3db-…`, `4cf9a044-…`, `46526082-…`, `efdf96c1-…`, `a363340d-…` and `0eb8fda3-…`. With images blocked, or for screen readers, the content is lost. The email's offer and wallpaper messaging may sit in these images, but the truncated source doesn't show whether they are banners.
- **Tracking images missing alt:** The Dotomi pixels, `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/...` also lack alt. Pixels should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is an accessibility problem for low-vision users.
- **Text-size lock:** The global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` rule disables text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** The head contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Font loading:** Several `@font-face` rules pull from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...` (the "HNW05" family) rather than the sender's own domain. The "Lineto Brown Sub" `src:` mixes `.eot`/`.woff2`/`.woff` formats across separate declarations. Most clients ignore web fonts, so this is low impact.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Confirmed failure:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` was not resolved. The syntax is Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%…%%`, so this looks like a personalization or content-block failure at send time, or a preview/test render.
- No other raw tokens were detected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed and could be a capture artifact. The sender (`cb2@mail.cb2.com`) would normally need both for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The QA note says this is expected via the relay.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Both are outside the truncated HTML, so neither could be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- The 69 tracking links go through click-redirect domains, and the provided data doesn't show the final destinations. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "Long Weekend Sale" and "New Wallpaper Collection" offers cannot be confirmed from this data.
- The tracking pixels carry identifiers in the query strings (e.g. `mi_u=56aaa278…`, `mi_cid=678efa99…`, `mi_mid=01a1163c…`). This shows email-level attribution via the MI platform, but it is not evidence of UTM tagging on site links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`:** Add a fallback or suppression for the reviews block, and verify the render before send.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS:** Serve `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com` over `https://`, or drop the 10 `cookieredir/pub*.php` pixels.
3. **Add alt text:** Add descriptive alt to the 9 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. **Allow zoom:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and reconsider `text-size-adjust:none` in the `*` rule.
5. **Check the unsubscribe headers:** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are sent in the original message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC from a raw-header capture rather than the relay copy.
6. **Resolve the click-tracking redirects:** Follow the 69 tracked links to their final URLs, then check UTM tagging and landing-page match.
7. **Remove the template residue:** Delete the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment.
