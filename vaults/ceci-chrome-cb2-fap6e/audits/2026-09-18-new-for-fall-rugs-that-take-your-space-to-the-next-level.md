# New for fall: Rugs that take your space to the next level
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, category-focused catalog send — every module is a rug collection, which is a rarity for Crate & Barrel's usual grab-bag format seen in prior sends like the 09-17 baby-essentials or clearance emails. "New for fall: rugs that take your space to the next level" delivers exactly what it promises: heritage patterns, vintage-inspired designs (Combe, Ennis, Colesbourne, Coates, Teramo), each with its own hero shot and "Shop [Name]" CTA.
- The problem is there's no offer anywhere. Six rug collections deep, a "we love these for you" product grid, a Design Desk plug, a $500 sweepstakes teaser, and an app download push — and not a single %/$ off, sale window, or loyalty perk. It's a lookbook, not a sales email, and for a subscriber conditioned by this brand's near-daily discount blasts, that's a jarring tonal shift.
- Execution is solid (no render bugs, strong visual hierarchy, real lifestyle photography), but this reads like an inspiration/awareness email buried in a promo-heavy send calendar. It'll perform fine for someone actively rug-shopping and poorly for anyone scanning for a deal.

## What's working

- Single-category focus (all rugs) is a clear departure from the brand's usual multi-brand roundup and makes the email easy to scan.
- Hero image and copy ("new! vintage look, modern performance") immediately signal the fall rug refresh theme.
- Each collection module pairs a lifestyle shot with a detail shot and its own named CTA, which helps shoppers self-select by style.
- "we love these for you" grid at the bottom adds a personalized-feeling product pick layer with visible pricing.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- Zero discount, price anchor, or urgency anywhere in the email — a rug purchase is a considered buy and this gives no reason to act today.
- Six nearly-identical "new! [Name] collection" modules in a row create fatigue; by Coates or Teramo the eye has stopped differentiating.
- The $500 sweepstakes ("it's all on the app...your chance at $500") is buried near the bottom below the fold, where it could have been a stronger secondary hook.
- No loyalty/member pricing shown despite the brand having a rewards program used in other sends.
- Subject promises "next level" transformation but the body is a flat product catalog, not styling/inspiration content — undersells the room-by-room angle that the Venus Williams collab collection did well.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a concrete incentive — even 15-20% off one rug collection or free shipping on rugs over $X — since rugs are a high-consideration, high-price category where a nudge matters most.
- 2. Cut the six collection modules to 3, and combine the rest into a single scrollable "shop all new rugs" grid to reduce repetition fatigue.
- 3. Move the $500 sweepstakes above the fold or into its own module — it's currently a bottom-of-email afterthought for what should be a scroll-driving hook.
- 4. Add a size/price range callout near the top ("rugs from $XXX, all sizes available") since fit and price are the top rug-shopping decision factors and neither appears until the bottom grid.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New rug collections just landed for fall`

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 60 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped by automated HTTP probing (standard for `mi.crateandbarrel.com`-style click trackers) — link destinations were not validated end-to-end and should be manually click-tested before send.
- Multiple pixel trackers present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/06e8164c-...` (Dotdash/dv.crateandbarrel — likely a viewability or dynamic-content vendor pixel). No issues with presence, but volume (6x duplicate of the same open pixel) suggests possible redundant tag firing worth deduping.
- Third-party pixel from `sr.rlcdn.com` (LiveRamp/RampID) fired 5 times with sequential `n=1` through `n=5` params — consistent with sequential data-onboarding/identity-resolution beacons, not a defect, but notable for privacy/compliance review if not already covered by a separate audit.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level. Note this may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) stripping the header rather than the sender omitting it; cannot fully confirm from HTML source alone.
