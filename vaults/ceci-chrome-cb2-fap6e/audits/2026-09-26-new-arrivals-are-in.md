# New arrivals are in
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 doing the exact same "JUST IN / ALREADY TRENDING" new-product cascade seen repeatedly this week, just with a different subject line ("New arrivals are in" vs "ONLY AT CB2" on 9/24 or "Italian vintage reissues" on 9/25). The top banner leads with the only real offer — "THE SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT / UP TO 25% OFF" — but it's a thin strip above a much larger hero that's just a moody rug photo with no discount attached, so the eye lands on ambiance, not the deal.
- Below the fold it's the now-familiar six-item designer-credential cascade (Meda chair, Rio table, Isobel console, Ambroise sofa, Davison bed), each tagged with a designer/studio name — attractive, on-brand, but zero pricing and zero differentiation from last week's near-identical "POPULAR FOR A REASON" and "TIME WILL BE KIND TO THIS" sends. This is a browse/inspiration email dressed as a "new arrivals" alert, not a conversion email.
- Judgement: competent production values, stale format. CB2 is repeating itself send after send and burying its one honest discount under a wall of un-priced lifestyle photography.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent CB2 branding and photography — no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- The upholstery offer banner at the very top is visible without scrolling and states a specific discount (up to 25% off).
- Each featured piece has a clear callout name and designer/studio attribution, reinforcing CB2's design-credibility angle.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom does show real prices ($499–$1199), giving at least some price anchoring.
- Footer includes loyalty/perks tiles (10% back in Rewards Dollars, 15% off signup) which are relevant if the recipient is a member.

## What's weak

- Subject says "New arrivals are in" but the actual hero banner leads with the Upholstery Event sale — subject and hero content don't match, same disconnect flagged in the 9/26 "Restock alert" send.
- None of the six hero cascade products (Meda, Rio, Isobel, Ambroise, Davison) show a price — a shopper has to click through blind.
- This is the same tile-cascade structure as 9/24's "ONLY AT CB2" and 9/23's "best sellers" sends — three near-identical formats in four days is fatigue-inducing.
- The one real, time-bound offer (25% off upholstery) is confined to a thin top strip and never reappears despite a full-length email below it.
- "JUST IN / ALREADY TRENDING" is generic filler copy — it doesn't say what's new or why it's trending.

## Recommendations

- 1. Align subject and hero: if the email is genuinely about new arrivals, drop the upholstery banner or move it to a secondary position — right now the two competing messages dilute both.
- 2. Add prices to every hero-cascade item (Meda, Rio, Isobel, Ambroise, Davison) — CB2 already does this in the bottom grid, so it's not a template limitation.
- 3. Break the multi-day sameness: rotate in a different layout (single hero + grid, like 9/25's "Upholstery Event is on" send which scored best this week at 6.0) instead of repeating the six-tile designer cascade a fourth time.
- 4. Give "JUST IN / ALREADY TRENDING" real specificity — e.g. name the room/category driving the trend, or add a "X people viewing" / bestseller badge.
- 5. Repeat the 25% off CTA once more near the bottom of the cascade, since scroll fatigue means most readers never see it twice.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New: 5 pieces just landed at CB2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 25% off upholstery + new arrivals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Meda, Rio, Isobel and more — first look inside`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The Semi-Annual Upholstery Event ends soon`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email fails on one broken merge token and lacks unsubscribe/authentication headers in the captured data; image accessibility is poor and several third-party pixels load over plain HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 69 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains), so live-link validity is unconfirmed — recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- 10 sequential Dotomi cookie-sync calls (`ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`) plus `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` all load over `http://`, not `https://` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or stripped by mail clients (Gmail image proxy, Outlook), which will silently drop this tracking.
- Open-tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are HTTPS and appear correctly formed.

- The tracking pixels/HTTP images also lack `alt` attributes, though this is low-impact since they're 1x1 trackers, not content.
- Media queries and MSO conditional blocks are present and structured normally (`@media max-width:418px`, `[if mso]` blocks); no structural rendering defects observed in the truncated source.

- [WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) detected — one-click unsubscribe compliance can't be confirmed from available data.
