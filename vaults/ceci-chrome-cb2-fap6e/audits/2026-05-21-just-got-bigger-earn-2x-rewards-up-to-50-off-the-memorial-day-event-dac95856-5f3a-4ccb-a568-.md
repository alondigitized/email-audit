# Just got BIGGER: Earn 2x Rewards + up to 50% off The Memorial Day Event
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' third Memorial Day escalation in two days — and the hook is a real one: the discount ceiling has jumped from 40% to 50%. That's a legitimate reason to re-mail the list, and the hero communicates it immediately. The red banner, bold "Just Got BIGGER" headline, and bedroom lifestyle shot all land in the right order. The email functions.
- That said, the cadence is getting loud. Between yesterday's Crate & Kids furniture send, the rugs follow-up, the Design Desk pivot, and today's earlier 400+ deals refresh, this subscriber has now received five Crate/Kids emails in roughly 36 hours. The escalating-discount mechanic is transparent — anyone paying attention knows to wait for the next drop — and "BIGGER" in all-caps starts to read like subject line inflation rather than genuine news.
- The product mix is focused on children's room categories (bedding, rugs, lighting, play, storage), which is coherent for Crate & Kids. Execution is clean, hierarchy is clear, and the 2x Rewards benefit carries through consistently from prior sends.

## What's working

- **Discount escalation is real and visible.** "Up to 50% off" is a concrete step up from the 40% ceiling in yesterday's sends — the email earns its existence.
- **2x Rewards carries through.** Consistent loyalty messaging across the Memorial Day sequence reinforces the membership value prop rather than treating it as a one-off.
- **Visual hierarchy is tight.** Hero → lifestyle image → product grid → secondary CTAs flows without confusion; the eye knows where to go.
- **Category coherence.** Bedding, rugs, floor lamp, children's play, and storage are all kids-room categories — this doesn't feel like a random grab-bag.
- **Multiple CTA placements.** Each product section has its own shop button, reducing scroll friction for anyone who already knows what they want.

## What's weak

- **Subject is 71 characters — well over mobile-safe threshold.** "Just got BIGGER: Earn 2x Rewards + up to 50% off The Memorial Day Event" will truncate on most phones before "Memorial Day Event."
- **"BIGGER" in all-caps is a mild spam signal.** It reads like a shout rather than confidence; the escalation could be communicated without it.
- **No visible social proof.** No review counts, star ratings, or "X sold" signals on any of the product tiles — odd for a brand that presumably has this data.
- **Cadence fatigue risk is real.** This is the 5th+ Crate/Kids email in ~36 hours; subscribers who feel over-mailed are reaching for the unsubscribe, not the CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Trim the subject to under 50 chars and drop the all-caps.** The upgrade from 40% → 50% is the news; lead with that number. Make the subject do one job.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off just dropped — Memorial Day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Memorial Day just got better: now 50% off`
- 2. **Write a real preheader.** The current send appears to rely on body copy bleeding into the preheader slot. A dedicated line that names a specific product category or reinforces the deadline would meaningfully lift opens.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on kids bedding, rugs & more — this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 2x Rewards still stack — shop before Monday`
- 3. **Add star ratings or review counts to at least the top two product tiles.** Social proof is especially high-value for kids furniture (safety + quality anxiety); a 4.8★ (412) next to the hero bedding would visibly reduce hesitation.
- 4. **Introduce a hard deadline.** "Memorial Day Event" implies Monday, but nowhere in the visible email is "Ends Monday" or a specific date stated. Adding it to the hero would activate time-bounded urgency without any creative lift.
