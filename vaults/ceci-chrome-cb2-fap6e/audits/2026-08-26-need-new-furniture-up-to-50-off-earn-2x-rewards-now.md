# Need new furniture? Up to 50% off & earn 2x Rewards now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running the same catalog-dump playbook as its 2026-08-24 "best of the best for their bedroom" send (8.00/10) and the sibling 2026-08-25 "🚨On now: Up to 60% off" Labor Day email (6.00/10) — except this one dials the headline offer down to "up to 50%" while the banner still shouts "Double Rewards is on!" and the mid-email module contradicts itself with "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event." That inconsistency (50% hero vs. 60% mid-body) is a real miss for a brand that's sent five near-identical Labor Day emails in three days.
- The structure is otherwise solid and on-brand: category-by-category hero shots (bedroom, nursery, storage, nursery chairs) each with a clear "Shop the Sale" CTA and visible strikethrough pricing, followed by a sweepstakes, a second Double Rewards banner, a $29+ new-arrivals teaser, a credit-card pitch, and a design-service cross-sell. It's comprehensive to the point of fatigue — a subscriber who opened yesterday's Crate & Kids email sees the same bones again today.
- Given the cadence (this is at least the fourth Labor Day/Double Rewards email from this Crate & Barrel family in three days) and the 50%/60% mismatch, this lands in the middle of the pack — competent execution dragged down by repetition and a sloppy discount inconsistency.

## What's working

- Clear category segmentation (bedroom, nursery, storage & study, nursery chairs) makes scanning easy even in a long email.
- Each product block has visible strikethrough original price + sale price and a consistent "Shop the Sale" button.
- Kids/nursery room styling (soft pastels, playful mobile art, crib/dresser sets) matches the persona clearly — no ambiguity about category.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout holds structure top to bottom.
- Double Rewards banner and Labor Day deadline (September 8) give a credible, dated urgency anchor.

## What's weak

- Hero says "up to 50% off," but the mid-email Labor Day module says "Up to 60% off" — a visible, undermining inconsistency in the same email.
- This is the brand's fourth-plus near-identical Labor Day/Double Rewards send in three days (2026-08-24, 2026-08-25 x3) — fatigue risk is real and visible in the repeated banner creative.
- Email is extremely long — bedroom, nursery, storage, chairs, sweepstakes, rewards, new arrivals, "we love these for you," credit card, design service — no single hero moment stands out.
- "we love these for you" grid buries six more products with tiny, hard-to-read captions near the bottom, diluting focus rather than closing strong.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the 50%/60% discount contradiction before sending — pick one number and use it consistently in hero and mid-body modules; a subscriber who notices this loses trust in the offer.
- 2. Cut send frequency or differentiate creative — five Labor Day emails in three days from the same family risks unsubscribes; if cadence can't be reduced, vary the hero art and lead offer per send.
- 3. Trim the email — move "we love these for you," the credit card pitch, and the design service module into a follow-up send or below-fold summary link instead of a full scroll.
- 4. Move Double Rewards/points value into the hero subhead (e.g., "2X Rewards + up to 50% off") since it's a clear differentiator buried under a generic percentage.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard multi-client (MSO/Outlook/mobile) marketing email template with extensive tracking infrastructure; missing List-Unsubscribe headers and near-universal lack of alt text are the primary technical defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 120 tracking/click-redirect links present in the HTML, all skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable via direct fetch) — link destinations are unverified.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels confirmed: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 instances), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` (open tracking), `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/171ecd98-...` (conversion/view-through pixel), and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (LiveRamp/RampID, 5 sequential instances with incrementing `n=` params).
- No same-domain HTTP status validation was possible for CTAs due to redirect-domain skipping — recommend manual click-through verification before send/re-send.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 62 `<img>` elements missing `alt` attributes, including all product/hero imagery and all tracking pixels. Content images with no alt text fail WCAG 1.1.1 and degrade the experience for screen-reader users and image-blocked clients (a common default in Outlook/Gmail).
- Template includes standard MSO conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and `format-detection` resets — good baseline coverage for Outlook/iOS rendering quirks.
- One malformed comment in `<head>`: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` — leftover ESP template placeholder text with a typo ("IMPUT"), indicating this went out without the font-import step being reviewed/removed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{`, `%%`, `[[`) or broken personalization fields detected in the visible HTML/QA output.
- No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirement) is not confirmed present.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe POST support unconfirmed, required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender rules (effective for senders of 5,000+ msgs/day).
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified from available headers.
- Note: these three are flagged WARN rather than FAIL because the audit runs via an AgentMail relay that may strip/not surface these headers rather than the sender omitting them — cannot confirm sender-side compliance from this data alone; recommend checking raw headers at the receiving MTA directly.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Could not verify UTM parameter presence/consistency or landing-page alignment — all outbound links route through tracking-redirect domains that were skipped by the probe, so final destination URLs and query strings are unknown from this dataset.
- No issues found (insufficient data to confirm either way — flagged as a coverage gap, not a pass).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Confirm raw SMTP headers directly from the receiving mail server (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) are actually present and passing.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 43 unique content images (hero/product shots); tracking pixels (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `sr.rlcdn.com`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com`) can use `alt=""` since they're decorative/non-content.
3. Remove the leftover template placeholder comment (`[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]`) from production sends.
4. Manually resolve and spot-check a sample of the 120 tracking links to confirm final landing-page URLs carry correct UTM parameters and match live product/category pages.
