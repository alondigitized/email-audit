# It’s here! Earn DOUBLE REWARDS + Shop up to 60% off The Labor Day Event!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's other Labor Day send from today — the sibling of the near-identical "STARTS NOW!" email already logged at 7.00/10 on 2026-08-25. The hero delivers the promised beach-sand mood shot with "It's here! Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event," and the Double Rewards banner up top is a real, specific loyalty hook (10-20% back on the Crate Card through 9/8). But after the hero it collapses into the exact same eight-tile catalog grid (sofas, kitchen, tableware, entertaining, decor, outdoor, rugs, kids' bedroom) as its sibling send, then bolts on a second unrelated campaign — a full Dyson vacuum / Wusthof knife / Sonos speaker promo — followed by a "we love these for you" product rail and a credit-card upsell.
- The email is trying to do three campaigns in one send (Labor Day sitewide, kitchen electronics, credit card), which dilutes all three. A subscriber who already got the near-identical Labor Day email today will feel the fatigue immediately.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and on-brand: sand/beach texture, clear "Up to 60% off" headline, single "Shop Now" CTA visible without much scrolling.
- Double Rewards banner is a concrete, dated loyalty offer (10-20% back through 9/8) — good specificity.
- The Dyson deal shows real markdown math ($849.99 vs reg $979.95) which builds trust more than a vague "% off" claim.
- Category tiles use real lifestyle photography, not stock-looking product-only shots.
- No render bugs — images loaded, no overlap, no broken layout.

## What's weak

- This is the second near-identical Labor Day catalog-dump email from Crate & Barrel today (see 2026-08-25 "STARTS NOW!" at 7.00/10) — same 8-tile grid structure, same offer ("up to 60% off"), just a different hero photo.
- Bolting the Dyson/Wusthof/Sonos kitchen-deals block onto a Labor Day furniture email fragments the message — it's not clear which offer is the priority.
- No single hero product or category is highlighted; eight equal-weight tiles force the eye to hunt rather than land.
- The credit-card upsell block ("new! the card you love just got better") adds another competing CTA deep in an already long email.
- Email is very long (multiple full scrolls) before reaching app-download and store-locator content that most recipients won't reach.

## Recommendations

- 1. Consolidate with the sibling Labor Day send — sending two near-identical Labor Day emails same day trains subscribers to skim or ignore. Pick one hero angle and cut the duplicate.
- 2. Split the Dyson/kitchen-electronics deal into its own send or move it above the fold as the lead if it's the stronger margin item — right now it's buried mid-email behind a catalog dump.
- 3. Cut the 8-tile grid to 3-4 categories max so the eye has a clear landing point instead of scanning a full array.
- 4. Move Double Rewards copy directly under the hero CTA so the loyalty angle reinforces the offer instead of just sitting in the top banner.
- 5. Drop or relocate the credit-card upsell — it's competing with the primary sale message in the same email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double Rewards is live — up to 60% off Labor Day starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Labor Day picks: sofas, rugs & more up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: up to 25% off select Dyson, only through 9/8`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `10-20% back in Reward Dollars on everything you buy this week`

# Technical Email Audit — Crate & Barrel "Labor Day Event" (2026-08-25)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated (mi.crateandbarrel.com / MessageIndustries-style) promotional HTML email with extensive VML/MSO fallback code; QA scan shows a 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing image `alt` attributes and absent email authentication/unsubscribe headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 69 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP probe (redirect domains), so destination validity is **unconfirmed** — cannot verify these resolve to live pages.
- Heavy tracking pixel load present: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (7 instances), `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5 instances, LiveRamp/RampID), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v` (Merkle/DV or similar attribution beacon). None are functional defects, but they represent multiple third-party tracking dependencies in a single send.
- No broken/malformed `href` values found in the visible HTML source; full link inventory could not be assessed beyond the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **27 images missing `alt` text**, including primary content images (hero grid image `2026_0825_CB_LaborDayDeals_Grid_hero_0` on Scene7, product lifestyle images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`) and all tracking pixels. Content images without `alt` fail WCAG 1.1.1 and degrade the experience for screen-reader users and when images are blocked (a common default in Outlook/Gmail).
- Extensive MSO/VML conditional comments and multiple redundant `format-detection` meta tags indicate a legacy multi-ESP-compatible template — no functional rendering errors found in the visible markup.
- `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` — leftover template placeholder comment with a typo ("IMPUT"), confirming this is an unmodified boilerplate artifact rather than a rendering bug per se, but indicates incomplete template cleanup.

- No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

- Visible unsubscribe link in the body HTML was not included in the truncated source provided, so body-level CAN-SPAM compliance (physical address, unsubscribe link) could not be verified from source alone.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 15 content images (hero, product, grid images) at minimum; decorative/tracking pixels (`.gif`/`o.gif`/`rp/*.png`) can use `alt=""` to satisfy accessibility scanners without adding noise.
2. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` + `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent by the MTA — verify via raw header capture outside the AgentMail relay, since Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for lists at this volume.
3. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly against the sending MTA (bypassing the relay) since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
4. Spot-check a sample of the 69 skipped tracking links manually to confirm they resolve (not 404/dead) and that final landing pages carry expected UTM parameters for attribution continuity.
5. Remove leftover template placeholder comment (`IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT`) during next template QA pass.
