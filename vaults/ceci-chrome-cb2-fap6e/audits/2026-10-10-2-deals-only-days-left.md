# 2 DEALS & ONLY DAYS LEFT
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-10**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "2 deals," and the body delivers more than that. It carries a free-shipping banner, Long Weekend Sale up to 60% off, an upholstery event up to 25% off, a $1,000 app sweepstakes, an app download block, Rewards Dollars, a gift registry and a 15% signup offer. The two-deal promise is broken by the second scroll. The pitch also contradicts itself: "up to 60% off" sits next to "up to 25% off" on upholstery, so a shopper can't tell which discount applies to the sofa they want.
- The photography is strong. The console table hero is the best creative in the send, and the green velvet sofa is a good hook. But the hierarchy is muddy. The shipping hero sits above the real sale hero, and the headline text overlaps busy imagery in places. "SHIPPING, ON US" runs over glassware, and "SALE ENDS SOON" is faint on a dark base. It is a competent kitchen-sink promo, but it reads as the fourth or fifth CB2 sale pitch in four days. The 10/09 shipping send and the 10/10 "Fresh off a new season" send used the same stack.
- Verdict: good visuals, weak focus. The sweepstakes and app modules dilute a send whose subject says urgency. Strip it to one offer and one deadline and it would perform much better.

## What's working

- The "Long Weekend Sale, up to 60% off" hero is large and legible, with a lovely console table photo that anchors the email.
- The upholstery block has a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button and a concrete "up to 25% off" figure.
- The free-shipping threshold ($99) is stated plainly at the top.
- The navigation bar (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) gives easy category access.
- The brand look is consistent: moody, premium photography and clean type.

## What's weak

- The subject says "2 deals" but the body shows at least five or six offers and promotions.
- "ONLY DAYS LEFT" is a broken phrase with no number in it, so the urgency feels sloppy. The render only offers "SALE ENDS SOON" with no date.
- The top shipping banner text overlaps the glassware image, and the "SHIPPING, ON US" lettering is hard to read in places.
- The hero has no CTA button. The 60% off block is not clearly clickable, while the lesser shipping banner has a button.
- The footer's long legal fine print and the "up to 60%" and "up to 25%" figures conflict, which weakens the offer's honesty. The $1,000 sweepstakes and app push are off-message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the send to the two deals the subject names: the Long Weekend Sale (up to 60% off) and free shipping. Delete the app sweepstakes, the registry and the 15% signup tiles from this send.
- 2. Put the sale hero first, with a "SHOP THE SALE" button and a hard end date ("Ends Monday 11:59 PM PT").
- 3. Fix the subject's broken wording and name the actual deadline.
- 4. Fold the upholstery event into the sale as a featured category, or give it its own send. This resolves the 60%/25% confusion.
- 5. Add a clear readable overlay or scrim behind the shipping text, and make "SALE ENDS SOON" legible.
- 6. Link product tiles directly to specific items (the console, the green sofa) with names and prices.
