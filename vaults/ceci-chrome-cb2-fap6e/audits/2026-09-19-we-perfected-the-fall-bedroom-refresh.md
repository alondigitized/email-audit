# We perfected the fall bedroom refresh
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 running its lookbook engine yet again, but at least this time it's tied to a real category push: fall bedroom. The hero — "FALL BEDROOM REFRESH" over a warm, styled bed with a sculptural pendant light and fuzzy Mongolian-fur benches — is genuinely the best visual CB2 has sent in this run of audits; it looks like an actual interior, not a product cutout. But then it's the same structural problem as the 09-15 and 09-16 sends: five near-identical bed vignettes (Davison, Rio, Isobel, Russell) scroll past with zero discount anywhere near them, and you don't hit an actual number — "UP TO 40% OFF" — until deep in the email, disconnected from the bedroom story entirely.
- The subject "We perfected the fall bedroom refresh" is confident copy but it's a claim, not an offer, and it reads as filler compared to CB2's own 09-17 "The best is up to 40% off," which scored better specifically because it led with a number. This email is prettier than that one but less actionable.
- Net: strong art direction, weak commercial hook. Anyone in-market for a bed will enjoy scrolling this, but there's no reason to click *now* versus bookmarking it for later.

## What's working

- Hero image is a fully realized styled room (pendant light, fur benches, layered bedding) — the best hero composition in this brand history.
- Clear vertical narrative: hero → Davison → Rio Nightstand → Rio Bed → Isobel Nightstand → Russell Bed, each labeled with product name and designer credit (Studio ANANSI, Ross Cassidy), which reads as premium/editorial.
- "SHOP BEDROOM" CTA repeats at top and mid-scroll in a clean white button against the photography — easy to spot.
- Secondary modules (Interior Design Services, Best Sellers 40% off, New Rugs, More For You grid) give the email real depth without feeling padded.
- Prices are visible on some line items (e.g. Rio Nightstand ~$1199.00) and on the bottom product grid, grounding the fantasy shots in reality.

## What's weak

- No discount, promo code, or price break appears anywhere near the bedroom hero or the five bed/nightstand vignettes — the entire "refresh" section is full-price aspirational content.
- The only real offer ("BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF") is buried after the Interior Design Services module, completely disconnected from the bedroom story the subject line promised.
- Five sequential full-bleed bed images is a lot of scrolling before any variety — CB2 ran this exact format (long vignette scroll) in the 09-15 and 09-16 sends; subscribers who've opened those will feel the repetition.
- Subject line "We perfected the fall bedroom refresh" is a brand-voice flex, not a hook — no price, no urgency, no specific product.
- The rewards/app footer band (10% back, 15% off signup) is only teased with tiny text — easy to miss and would have been a stronger CTA if pulled up near the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull a concrete number into the hero. Even "Bedroom favorites, up to 25% off" would convert this from moodboard to offer — CB2's own 09-17 send proved plain percentage-off outperforms lookbook framing.
- 2. Cut the vignette scroll from five beds to three; consolidate Rio Nightstand + Rio Bed into one module since they're the same collection.
- 3. Move "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" up directly under the bedroom vignettes (or merge it into the hero) instead of sandwiching it behind the Interior Design Services pitch.
- 4. Surface the "Get 15% off" signup and "Earn 10% back" rewards blurbs higher — right under the hero CTA — since those are real incentives currently buried at the footer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fall bedroom refresh: up to 40% off beds & nightstands`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New in the bedroom: Davison, Rio & Isobel`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lacquered nightstands, onyx & walnut furnishings — plus 40% off best sellers`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Exclusive designer bedroom pieces, now with sale styles mixed in`

- Multiple `http://` (non-HTTPS) tracking/ad pixels present, which may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
