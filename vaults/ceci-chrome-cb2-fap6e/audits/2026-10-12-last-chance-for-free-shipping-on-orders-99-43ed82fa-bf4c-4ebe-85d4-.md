# Last chance for free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth free-shipping nudge in about 36 hours, and it reads like one. The subject promises "last chance for free shipping on orders $99+". The hero says something else: "final weekend: up to 20% off bestselling sofas & sectionals". Free shipping only shows up as the second headline, "ends today: free shipping on orders over $99!". A subscriber who opens for the shipping deadline has to scroll past a sofa sale to find it.
- The rest is a tidy, on-brand stack: a Shop Now button, a category grid, a TheDesignDesk feature, product tiles, a Crate Card promo and an app download block. Nothing is broken. The problem is focus. At least five calls to action compete: Shop Now, Shop All Free Shipping, Book Your Free Appointment (twice), Learn More and Download Now. The email feels like a catch-all, not a last-chance reminder.
- Verdict: competent, trustworthy and forgettable. The urgency is credible, but it's diluted by a hero that doesn't match the subject and a sixth near-identical send in a row.

## What's working

- The "ends today: free shipping on orders over $99" headline is clear, and the $99 threshold is easy to understand.
- The hero room photo is warm and on-brand. The sofa offer ("up to 20% off") gives a second concrete price hook.
- The category links (Tabletop, Kitchen, Decor, Bedding, Halloween, Thanksgiving) fit the season and make it easy to start shopping.
- The Crate Card block ("10% back in Reward Dollars") is a real loyalty benefit.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero offer (sofas, 20% off) doesn't match the subject (free shipping). The urgency sits below the first screen.
- Too many CTAs: Shop Now, Shop All Free Shipping, two Book Appointment buttons, Learn More and Download Now. None clearly leads.
- The "we love these for you" tiles are mostly low-ticket glassware and bowls at $5–$11. At those prices they barely help a customer reach the $99 threshold the email is pushing.
- The Design Desk block appears twice, which feels like filler. The three-line fine print at the bottom is dense and full of exclusions, which undercuts the "free shipping" message.
- This is yet another free-shipping reminder after the 2026-10-11 "Free shipping on orders over $99 ends TOMORROW!" and the earlier "ENDS TODAY!" send. The deadline has lost its punch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make free shipping the hero. Put the "ends today" headline and one button at the top, and move the sofa offer to a single secondary block. If the sofa sale is the real story, change the subject to match.
- 2. Cut the CTAs to two: one primary (Shop Free Shipping) and one secondary (Book a Design Appointment). Drop the second Design Desk block and the app promo from a deadline email.
- 3. Replace the glassware with items that make $99 easy to reach, such as bundles or top sellers in the $40–$80 range. Add a line like "Add $X to qualify".
- 4. Add a real time stamp ("Ends tonight, 11:59 PT") and suppress this send for anyone who already got two reminders. Consolidate the 24-hour cadence into one send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping ends tonight at 11:59 PT`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $99 free-shipping window closes today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 20% off sofas & sectionals. Last day.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fill your cart past $99 and we'll cover delivery.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with mobile media queries and Outlook (MSO) conditionals. It has widespread missing `alt` attributes and a few template-hygiene problems. The compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed because of relay limits. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and preheader markup were not reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **60 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified. The QA run reports 0 hard failures, but that only covers links it actually tested.
- **Five identical LiveRamp pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. They differ only in `n`. Five calls to a third-party sync endpoint per open is redundant. The set also adds third-party requests and a privacy surface.
- **Recipient identifier in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c…` value appears on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`, on `o.gif` and on the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` tracker (alongside `mi_cid` and `mi_mid`). The same value is the `s=` parameter on the rlcdn pixels. This is a stable per-recipient ID, and it is passed to a third party.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text is widespread.** The QA run flagged 26 images.
  - 12 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` and `/m/47/`. Examples: `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `d8b928f0-….gif`, `3c9b2911-….jpg`, `971aa10f-….jpg`. If these carry the offer or product content, image-blocked clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images, which may also be content.
  - The `rlcdn` pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.` tracker are tracking pixels. They should have `alt=""` rather than no attribute. The QA check can't tell an absent attribute from an empty one, so confirm in the source.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. That is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Invalid `<title>`.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Template leftovers and duplicates.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed.
  - There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with overlapping values.
  - `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` is empty.
  - These are harmless in practice, but they show the template wasn't cleaned up.
- **Conflicting text-size rules.** `-webkit-text-size-adjust` is set to both `100%` and `none` in different blocks. `none` wins by cascade order, which disables iOS text scaling.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: there are no unrendered tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`. Only the truncated portion could hide any.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **No List-Unsubscribe header found** (`[WARN]`).
- **No List-Unsubscribe-Post header found** (`[WARN]`). One-click unsubscribe under RFC 8058 can't be confirmed, and it matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **No Authentication-Results header found** (`[WARN]`). SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown.
- The QA tool itself notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so none of the three is a confirmed sender defect. Verify them in raw headers received directly.
- The footer, with the physical address and unsubscribe link, is in the truncated section and was not checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This can't be assessed. All 60 links go through tracking redirects and weren't resolved. The QA data has no final landing URLs or UTM parameters, and no evidence that the "$99+ free shipping" offer matches the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and the 7 `mi…/p/rp/` images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`.
4. Cut the five `rlcdn` pixels to one if they are redundant, and review whether the `mi_u` ID needs to be passed to a third party.
5. Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` in raw headers from a direct send, not the relay.
6. Resolve a sample of the tracking links to confirm status codes, UTMs and landing-page alignment with the offer.
7. Remove the leftover template comment and duplicate meta tags.
