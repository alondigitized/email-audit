# Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid seasonal send with a clear lead. The hero, "the Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off," sits over a moody dining-table image with a single "Shop Now" button. The subject and hero match, the offer is specific, and the seasonal hook is credible. Crate & Barrel's list has been getting 20% off lighting, up to 50% off furniture, and clearance, so this one reads as the natural next beat.
- It then runs too long, and the long tail dilutes the offer. Below the hero is a 4-tile category grid, a hosting-guide banner, two more buttons, a rug offer at 20% off, a Design Desk sweepstakes, Affirm financing, a product grid, a credit-card promo, an app download, and a second Design Desk pitch. Roughly seven different calls to action compete for one click. The 2026-10-21 "ENDS TODAY: Up to 50% off furniture!" send was tighter and carried clearer urgency. This one has no deadline.
- My judgment: a good hero on a bloated body. It's a competent mid-tier send, and cutting everything after the first grid would improve it.

## What's working

- The subject and hero say the same thing: Thanksgiving dining room, up to 30% off.
- The seasonal framing is timely. It's late October and the dining table is the Thanksgiving focal point.
- The hero image is warm and aspirational, and the "Shop Now" button is easy to see.
- The 4-tile grid splits the offer into tables, seating, storage and lighting, each with a discount label.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real items with prices, which gives some product detail.

## What's weak

- There are too many competing messages: the sweepstakes, Affirm, the credit card, the app, and the Design Desk twice.
- There is no deadline or end date for the 30% off, so nothing makes you act today.
- The offer is inconsistent. It says up to 30% off in the hero and tiles, but the later rug and accent chair block says up to 20% off. Lighting is tagged 20%, so "up to 30%" only holds for some categories.
- The hero's "Shop Now" is generic. Two more buttons follow ("Shop The Dining Furniture Event," "Shop All Dining Furniture"), and they muddy the path.
- The email is very long, and the fine print and footer are dense and tiny.
- The Design Desk is pitched twice, once with the $5000 sweepstakes and again near the bottom. That repeats what the 2026-10-21 send already covered.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the dining grid and the hosting guide. Keep one product row. Drop the Affirm, credit card, app and second Design Desk blocks, or move them to a separate send.
- 2. Add a deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday," to match the urgency of the 10/21 furniture send.
- 3. Change the button to "Shop Dining Tables & Chairs" and cut the extra shop buttons down to one.
- 4. Make the discount consistent. Either say "up to 30% off" everywhere or label the exclusions clearly.
- 5. Put real products with prices in the hero area. At the moment the hero image carries the sale price in small print only.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Thanksgiving table, up to 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, chairs, sideboards & more. Select styles, limited time.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hosting this year? Save on bestselling dining pieces before the holiday.`

The HTML source is truncated, and the relay may have stripped headers. The QA run reports 0 errors, 3 warnings and a 57% pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` calls are LiveRamp-domain pixels. They are third-party beacons, and the QA flagged them for missing alt text.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (about 31 images):**
  - **Content images:** 14 hosted at `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` (for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `ef861136-…jpg`, `5c7b78d8-…gif`), plus a Scene7 image, `2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary%2D…`. If they carry offer or product information, that content is lost when images are blocked.
  - **Dynamic product images:** seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (`560d850e7e517ede.png`, `789e7381ff44be74.png`, and others).
  - **Pixels:** the rlcdn, `o.gif` and `dv.` images above.
  - The QA can't tell an absent `alt` from an empty `alt=""`. The pixels should use `alt=""`, and the content images need descriptive text.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1" />` restricts pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility problem for low-vision users.
- **Duplicate meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared three times (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no`). The `charset` is also declared twice, via `http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`. This is redundant but harmless.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It's cosmetic, but it shows an incomplete template.
- **Dead CSS rule:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` comes before `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. Both match at 768px and above, and the later one wins, so the 600px cap never applies.
