# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's "Floor Sample Sale" — a striking rug/textile hero image with warm, sun-drenched styling, but the entire offer is an in-store-only event ("IN STORES FOR A LIMITED TIME," CTA reads "FIND A STORE"). For an email channel, that's a fundamental mismatch: there is no online purchase path, no product grid, and no discount percentage anywhere in the hero. Compare this to the 2026-08-18 "LAST WEEK TO SHOP" send (7/10), which had a clear UP TO 40% OFF hero and a full shoppable grid — this email has neither.
- The visual execution itself is CB2's best in weeks — moody natural light, a single rug/chair vignette, real product texture instead of another lifestyle collage — but visual polish can't compensate for the offer being inaccessible to anyone reading this email on their phone away from a store. The ancillary blocks (app download, rewards card, gift registry, SMS signup) are the same recycled footer modules seen in nearly every recent CB2 send.
- Judgement: gorgeous creative, wrong CTA for the channel. This will look great and convert almost nobody directly from the email.

## What's working

- The hero photography is a clean single vignette (rug, drapery, chair) rather than the crowded product-grid CB2 has leaned on all week — a genuine visual break.
- "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" is a specific, recognizable retail event type, not vague lookbook language.
- Layout renders cleanly — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- The lower module grid (app, rewards, registry, SMS) is well-organized and legible at a glance.

## What's weak

- The sole CTA is "FIND A STORE" — there's no way to act on this offer from the email itself, which kills online conversion entirely.
- No discount percentage or dollar figure is shown anywhere for the actual sale being promoted.
- No urgency specificity — "limited time" with no end date is vague after a week of CB2 emails using the same soft-urgency language.
- No product grid or secondary content tied to the floor-sample event (what's discounted? furniture? rugs? both?) — the reader has zero information to decide if it's worth a store trip.

## Recommendations

- 1. If this is genuinely in-store only, add a secondary CTA/module below the hero for "Shop similar online" or a curated online counterpart — don't send an email with zero clickable commerce path for online subscribers.
- 2. Put a real number in the hero copy (e.g., "UP TO 50% OFF FLOOR SAMPLES") — "in stores for a limited time" alone gives no reason to prioritize this over browsing.
- 3. Add a store locator or "near you" personalization element, or at minimum list which categories are included (rugs, seating, lighting) so readers can judge relevance before making a trip.
- 4. Replace vague "limited time" with a firm end date to create credible urgency.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `One-of-a-kind pieces, final markdowns — find your nearest store`
