# Double Rewards + Up to 30% off drinkware & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a tableware-led Thanksgiving send. The "Host & Toast" hero shows a table setting under "up to 30% off drinkware, dinnerware & more", with a plain "Shop Now" button. The subject matches it. Below the hero, a tidy 2x3 grid of category tiles each carries its own discount, and a Thanksgiving-table module, a Gift Shop module and a product carousel follow. It looks polished and on-brand.
- The problem is stacking. Double Rewards is the lead in the subject and appears twice in the body: the "last Double Rewards this year!" block above the hero and a second red "last Double Rewards this year!" block near the bottom. The Fall Sales Event (up to 50% off furniture) is pushed into the top banner. That is three competing offers, plus a gift shop, a Design Desk promo and an app download. Four days ago this brand sent a Double Rewards email with the same "last Double Rewards" language, so the urgency is stale.
- For a home-furniture shopper, the drinkware hero is a weak match. The send is competent, but it tries to be everything at once.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme for the week, with an autumn table setting and a clear "up to 30% off" headline.
- The category grid has a discount on every tile (20% off dinnerware, 30% off wine glasses and so on), so the offer reads at a glance.
- The "Shop Host & Toast" button uses a verb and a noun and links to a real collection.
- The "we love these for you" row shows actual products with prices, which gives the shopper something concrete.
- The rewards deadline ("now–Nov. 17, 2026") gives the Double Rewards offer a credible end date.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards block above the hero pushes the hero down, and the same block repeats lower in the email.
- The Fall Sales Event (furniture, up to 50% off) is reduced to a thin top banner. This is the offer most relevant to a furniture persona.
- There are too many CTAs: Shop Now, Shop Host & Toast, Shop the Styles, Shop the Sale, Shop All Tablewear & Bar, Shop the Gift Guide, Shop Top Categories, Learn More (twice), Download Now and Book a Free Appointment. No single path stands out.
- The footer fine print is dense and tiny, and the multiple offers each carry their own exclusions.
- This is the same "last Double Rewards this year!" language as the 11/7 and 11/8 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and cut the rest. Either lead with the drinkware and tableware sale, or with Double Rewards, not both.
- 2. Move the Double Rewards block below the hero and show it only once.
- 3. Keep the Thanksgiving countdown banner. It is the strongest urgency cue here.
- 4. Cut the CTAs to about three: the hero, the Thanksgiving table and the product row.
- 5. For furniture-affinity segments, swap the hero for a Fall Sales Event furniture module.
- 6. Drop the "last" claim unless it is true, because recipients have seen it four times in three days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Set the Thanksgiving table: up to 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Host & Toast: dinnerware up to 30% off, 2x rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dinnerware, wine glasses & flatware, plus 2x rewards through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Thanksgiving is 17 days out. Get the table ready and earn 20% back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO/VML conditionals. It has about 71 tracked links and a large set of images without `alt` attributes. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because AgentMail may have stripped the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM details could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 71 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Per-recipient identifiers in pixels:**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` calls (a LiveRamp domain) carry a hashed recipient identifier in `s=`.
  - Movable Ink URLs carry the same identifier, e.g. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…`.
  - The identifier is shared across third-party pixels, so it is worth confirming it is covered by the privacy disclosures.
- **Pixel `alt` attributes:** The tracking pixels `o.gif`, `rlcdn.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/4f219d26-…` have no `alt` attribute. Spacer and tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` text:** About 36 `<img>` elements were flagged.
  - 22 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (for example `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6f9ddb32-…jpg`, `d21416db-…gif`). If they are linked, the link has no accessible name and image-blocked clients show nothing.
  - 7 are Movable Ink product images on `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` (for example `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - The remaining 7 are tracking pixels (see section 2).
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** The universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{… -webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set earlier, so user text-size preferences are ignored.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML doctype.
- **Duplicate meta tags:**
  - Two charset declarations: `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type" …>` and `<meta charset="utf-8">`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, and `address=no`/`telephone=no`.
- **Template placeholder left in source:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is shipped in production, which is a template hygiene issue.
- **Global resets:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` are global and can override child styles in some clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]` appeared, and the QA checks flagged none. The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header:** Not found (QA WARN). The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** Not found (QA WARN), with the same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules.
- **`Authentication-Results` header:** Not found (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment is unknown. The relay is the expected cause, but the check is still unverified.
- **Footer content:** The visible-footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are outside the truncated source and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 71 links were behind redirect domains and were not probed, and no UTM parameters or landing URLs were visible in the truncated HTML. No issues are claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 22 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` content images and the 7 `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/` product images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels (`rlcdn.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`).
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*` and `body`.
3. Escape `&` in `<title>`, de-duplicate the charset and `format-detection` meta tags, and delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` comment.
4. Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw `.eml` or the original SMTP headers rather than the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
5. Resolve a sample of the tracking links with redirect-following to confirm the final URLs, UTM parameters and HTTP 200s.
6. Review the five `sr.rlcdn.com` pixels and the shared `mi_u`/`s=` identifier against the sender's privacy disclosures.
