# Don’t miss these best sellers on sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-23**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's "STEALS OF THE WEEK" discount roll-up format again, this time slimmed down to a single-offer send: "BEST SELLERS UP TO 40% OFF" over a warm, well-lit bedroom hero, followed by six category tiles (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop) and a closing Interior Design Services pitch. It's clean and on-brand, but it's the same skeleton CB2 has run repeatedly this week — the 2026-09-20 "STEALS OF THE WEEK" send, the 2026-09-22 "Just one more day" urgency send — just without the urgency banner this time, which makes it feel like a rerun rather than a fresh push.
- The hero image is genuinely nice — warm bedding, soft light, a real room you could picture — and "SHOP THE BEST" is a clear, visible CTA. But six near-identical product tiles in a row with generic single-word labels (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop) is a lot of scrolling for very little differentiation, and nothing on the page tells me which SKUs are actually part of the 40%-off sale versus just category browsing.
- Net: solid production values, weak targeting. Reused format, vague CTA-to-offer connection, and a subject line that undersells the one concrete number ("40% off") it actually has.

## What's working

- Hero image is warm, aspirational, and well-composed — a real styled bedroom, not a stock cutout.
- "UP TO 40% OFF" is bold and legible directly in the hero, satisfying the eye-lands-on-offer-first check.
- "SHOP THE BEST SELLERS SALE" button after the category tiles reinforces the CTA a second time before the page pivots to Design Services.
- Footer loyalty content (Rewards Dollars, 10% back, App download) is present and visible for members.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent top to bottom.

## What's weak

- Subject line "Don't miss these best sellers on sale" buries the actual number — the email itself leads with "UP TO 40% OFF" but the subject doesn't mention it at all.
- Six category tiles (Furniture, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting, Rugs, Tabletop, unlabeled cocktail glassware) is repetitive scrolling with one-word labels and no product specifics, prices, or "% off" callouts on the tiles themselves.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in hero or subject — this is the same "STEALS OF THE WEEK" bones as three prior sends this week, now with the time-pressure lever removed.
- Interior Design Services block at the bottom is a full pivot away from the sale message, diluting the single-offer focus this email otherwise had going for it.
- Preview text is entirely unsubscribe/legal boilerplate rather than real preview copy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put "40% off" in the subject line — it's the strongest asset in the email and currently invisible until open.
- 2. Add a real preheader with a specific hook (e.g., a hero SKU or category) instead of leaking legal text.
- 3. Cut the six generic tiles to 3-4 and label each with the specific discount or a lead product name, not just a category word.
- 4. Bring back a credible deadline (even "ends Sunday") since this brand's own history shows urgency lifts CB2's scores by a point.
- 5. Move or drop the Interior Design Services block into a separate send — it competes with, rather than supports, the sale CTA.

## Technical Audit — CB2 "Don't miss these best sellers on sale"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email uses a legacy table-based ESP template (Bronto/Oracle-pattern markup) with functional rendering fallbacks, but ships with a broken merge token, missing unsubscribe/authentication headers, and multiple insecure legacy ad-tracking pixels.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 62 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated probing (opaque redirect domains) — not independently verifiable as functional; recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- Two legacy ad-network pixels use `http://` (non-TLS), which is likely to be blocked or flagged by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential pixels, all `http://`, all missing alt text)
- These dotomi.com pixels (cookie-sync/retargeting) add 11+ blocking or silently-dropped requests; worth confirming they're still needed vendor-side, as broken retargeting silently degrades attribution rather than causing visible failure.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 8 content/tracking images are missing `alt` text (evidence: QA findings list, e.g. `88ce8206-406b-4608-9df9-e4145f246485.jpg`, `2551a50a-f0a5-482a-8b64-5168b2e1ea29.jpg`, plus the dotomi pixels above). This affects screen-reader users and image-blocked inbox previews (no fallback text shown).
- Template includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments and `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets — no structural rendering issues found in the visible source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL]** Unresolved raw merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%` found in the rendered HTML. This is a hard send-blocker — it means the personalization/review-widget block did not populate and will show literal token text to recipients.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) cannot be confirmed; may fail Gmail/Yahoo 2024 bulk-sender requirements. Note QA flags this may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than the sender's actual header — verify against raw source headers, not just relay output.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found — same caveat applies.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not present in the captured data — authentication status is unknown, not confirmed failing. Recommend checking raw headers directly from the sending MTA rather than the relay.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Cannot verify — the 62 outbound links were tracking/redirect URLs skipped by the HTTP probe, so no landing-page or UTM-parameter data was captured. No claim can be made either way; flag as untested rather than passing.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Block send / hotfix: resolve `%%reviews_skulist%%` before this goes out — confirmed rendering failure.
2. Add `alt` text to all content images at minimum (8 flagged); tracking pixels are lower priority but trivial to fix (`alt=""`).
3. Migrate the two `http://` dotomi endpoints to `https://` or confirm with the ad vendor that TLS endpoints exist — non-HTTPS resources risk being dropped by Apple Mail/Gmail image proxies.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist in the actual raw SMTP headers (not just the relay capture) to validate one-click unsubscribe compliance.
5. Pull raw `Authentication-Results` from the source MTA to confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass before treating this as a deliverability risk.
6. Manually click-test a sample of the 62 tracking links to confirm destination pages and UTM tagging, since automated probing couldn't verify them.
