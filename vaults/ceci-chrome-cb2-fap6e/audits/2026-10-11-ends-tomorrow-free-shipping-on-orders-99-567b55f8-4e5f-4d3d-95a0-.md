# Ends tomorrow: Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is a free-shipping reminder that tries to be five emails at once. The headline, "ends tomorrow! free shipping on orders $99+", is a clear, credible offer with a "Shop All Free Shipping" button. It sits below a big Halloween flat-lay and a countdown strip. Below it are a six-tile category grid, a repeat of the Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery banner, a gifts block, a $5,000 design-consult sweepstakes, four product picks, a credit-card promo, an app-download block and a long legal footer.
- The biggest problem is conflicting urgency. The top strip reads "3 days left!" with a ticking countdown (03:15:45:01), while the subject and headline say "ends tomorrow". A subscriber sees two deadlines for the same promo and trusts neither. This is also the same free-shipping beat the brand hit on Oct 10 ("3 days left: free shipping on orders over $99*" in the kitchen send). Repeating it a day later feels like cadence filler.
- Judgement: competent execution, but it's diluted and self-contradicting. It's a decent reminder for a shopper already in the cart, and it won't pull in anyone else. For a home-furniture reader, the kids and nursery framing plus a Halloween hero makes it feel off-target.

## What's working

- The subject and headline match, and the offer is concrete: free shipping at $99+.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button is clear and appears twice, once before and once after the category grid.
- The six-tile category grid (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, Halloween) is easy to scan and has clean photography.
- The countdown timer adds visual urgency and fits the seasonal, fall-promo context.
- The "we love these for you" block shows real products with prices, which gives the email some substance.

## What's weak

- "3 days left!" in the countdown contradicts "ends tomorrow!" in the headline.
- The Halloween pumpkin hero is large, has no copy, and pushes the actual free-shipping offer down the page. It isn't about free shipping at all.
- The email has too many competing asks: sale event, shipping, gifts, sweepstakes, credit card, app, design appointment. The eye has no single path.
- The "heirloom-quality gifts" block has a busy background image under the text, which looks low-contrast and hard to read.
- The footer's dense fine print and the "exclusions apply" caveat undercut the "free" claim.
- The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery banner appears twice, which is redundant.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the deadline.** Make the countdown and headline agree. Show one real deadline, with the end time stated, and drop the "3 days left" strip.
- 2. **Put the offer in the hero.** Replace the wordless pumpkin image with "Free shipping on $99+ ends tomorrow" over the image, with the button directly under it.
- 3. **Cut to one secondary story.** Keep the product picks and drop the sweepstakes, credit card and app blocks to the next send.
- 4. **Remove the duplicate Semi-Annual banner and the "Shop All Free Shipping" repeat.** Replace them with product rows.
- 5. **Fix the gifts block contrast.** Put the text on a solid panel or darken the image.
- 6. **Move the exclusions into one line near the button.** Link to the full terms.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: free shipping on $99+ ends tomorrow`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Free shipping ends tomorrow (nursery + bedroom picks)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 50% off the Bedroom & Nursery Event, through tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bedding, storage, decor and more. Order by tomorrow to ship free.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with a `lang="en"` attribute, a mobile `@media (max-width:460px)` breakpoint and MSO conditional fixes. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 16 content images without alt text and a few markup defects. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not probed.** 72 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations and HTTP status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Many tracking pixels.** The QA data lists 13 non-content image requests:
  - 6 × `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`
  - 1 × `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - 1 × `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/242731d4-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - 5 × `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` (LiveRamp sync pixels)
- **Duplicate pixel.** The `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel appears 6 times with the same URL, which is redundant.
- **Third-party sync pixels.** The five `sr.rlcdn.com` calls add third-party requests that can be blocked by privacy proxies such as Apple MPP, and they add load.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (confirmed, 16 images).** Examples:
  - `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `.../m/1/528df7f3-3780-4f5d-aac1-b79970e42719.gif`
  - `.../m/1/790368cc-c1c9-4aa1-8bce-bd4229257202.gif`
  - `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/content/Crate/00%5FEmail/2026/10%5FKids/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide/2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
  
  The other 12 content images are the `.jpg` files in the QA list. With images blocked, these slots render empty and screen readers read out the filename or URL.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (13 images, minor).** The `mi.crateandbarrel.com`, `rlcdn.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` pixels should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Invalid markup.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv Content-Type` and `<meta charset>`), and `format-detection` is declared 3 times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` separately). This is harmless in most clients, but redundant.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which is an accessibility issue.
- **Unverified.** The source was truncated, so I could not check the body for text-in-image CTAs, button markup or contrast.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are resolved per-recipient identifiers in the pixel URLs. The truncated body could still hold tokens I haven't seen.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found (WARN).** The QA note says the relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than proven missing.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so confirm in the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results not found (WARN).** The AgentMail relay is expected to strip it, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent but not proof.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated HTML.** The body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not visible.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. The 72 click links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment with the "Free shipping on orders $99+" offer could not be checked. No destination URLs appear in the QA data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 16 content images, and `alt=""` to the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and remove the duplicate charset and `format-detection` meta tags.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta so users can zoom.
4. Remove the 5 duplicate `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel requests, and review whether the 5 `rlcdn.com` calls are necessary.
5. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml`, with full headers and untruncated HTML, to verify:
   - `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`
