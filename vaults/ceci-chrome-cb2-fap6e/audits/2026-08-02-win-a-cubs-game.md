# WIN A CUBS GAME
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- CB2 swaps its usual editorial or discount playbook for a co-branded sweepstakes: win two club-level Cubs tickets, a $2,000 travel voucher, a stadium tour, and a $1,000 CB2 gift card. It's a genuinely different creative move from the string of "% off, ends today" and designer-spotlight sends in the brand history — a smart way to break pattern and borrow attention from a local sports tie-in. But it's also a mismatched primary CTA: the biggest, first-seen ask in the email ("ENTER NOW") has nothing to do with furniture, and the actual shoppable content — the 40% off sale and the Sofas & Sectionals feature — gets pushed below the fold as an afterthought.
- The design execution is clean and on-brand: strong hero image, legible type, a tidy loyalty/app footer block that's become a CB2 staple. Where it falls short is targeting — a Cubs giveaway has no bearing on someone's furniture-buying intent, and the subject line "WIN A CUBS GAME" is vague about what's actually being offered.
- Net: a fun, attention-grabbing novelty send that will likely pull opens from Chicago-based subscribers curious about the hook, but it's unlikely to convert into furniture clicks or revenue the way CB2's editorial and discount formats have. Novelty over relevance.

## What's working

- The hero image and headline hierarchy are clean — "WIN CUBS TICKETS AND A DESIGN REFRESH" reads clearly at a glance with no render issues.
- The prize stack (tickets, travel voucher, stadium tour, gift card) is spelled out concretely rather than teased vaguely.
- The 40% off banner and Sofas & Sectionals feature give the email a real secondary path to purchase for anyone not interested in the sweepstakes.
- The loyalty/app footer block (App Store, Rewards card, gift registry, SMS signup) is consistent, well-organized, and gives multiple low-friction engagement options.
- No visual bugs — clean rendering, no overlapping text or broken images anywhere in the send.

## What's weak

- The single largest CTA in the email ("ENTER NOW") drives to a sweepstakes, not a shoppable product — furniture intent gets buried below two full-width banners.
- The subject line "WIN A CUBS GAME" is ambiguous (win a literal baseball game?) and doesn't hint at the actual prize value ($3,000+ in tickets/travel/gift card).
- All-caps subject line breaks from CB2's usual sentence-case subject style seen in prior sends ("The best kind of impulse buy," "Rooms for your mood board") and reads more like a spam-pattern shout.
- No demographic or lifestyle cue ties the Cubs tie-in to CB2's design-forward audience — it feels like a bolted-on partnership rather than a considered campaign.
- Dense fine-print footnotes (†, ‡, *) stacked across three separate offers (sweepstakes, sale, credit card) adds legal noise that dilutes the clean visual the rest of the email works hard to establish.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject/preview with the prize value, not the vague "game" framing — concrete numbers ($3K+ in prizes) will outperform a guessing-game subject.
- 2. Move the 40% off sale banner above or immediately below the sweepstakes hero so shoppers with no interest in the giveaway still hit a real offer fast.
- 3. Drop the all-caps subject style — it reads off-brand next to CB2's normal sentence-case sends and risks a spam-adjacent first impression.
- 4. Add one line connecting the Cubs partnership to CB2's brand (e.g., "refresh your home game-day setup") so the tie-in feels intentional rather than random.
- 5. Consolidate the three sets of fine print into a single "See offer details" link to reduce footer clutter.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Win Cubs tickets + a $3,000 home refresh`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shot at club seats, travel, and $1,000 to spend`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two club-level tickets, a travel voucher, and a CB2 gift card — enter before 8/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: sofas and sectionals up to 40% off, ends Wednesday.`
