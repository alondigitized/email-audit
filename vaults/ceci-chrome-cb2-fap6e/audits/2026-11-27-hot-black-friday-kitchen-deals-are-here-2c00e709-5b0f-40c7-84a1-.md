# 🔥 Hot Black Friday Kitchen deals are HERE!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a kitchen-themed Black Friday send from Crate & Barrel, and it delivers on the subject line. The hero reads "our biggest Black Friday kitchen deals! Up to 40% off premium brands" over an espresso machine photo, with a single "Shop Now" button right under it. The deals that follow are all kitchen: Breville espresso machines at $200 off, KitchenAid mixers at up to 30% off, Le Creuset Dutch ovens at $170 off, and a lowest-price cookware set. The structure is clean: one product block, one headline, one button, repeated.
- The screenshot is 680×12000 shown at 113px wide, so I can read headlines and layout but not fine print or the preheader. The email runs very long. It starts focused, then drifts into a generic "shop up to 50% off Black Friday" grid of furniture, holiday decor, rugs and gifts. It ends with a sweepstakes ("enter for a chance to win $5000") and a "we love these for you" block. The kitchen focus is diluted by the bottom half. It's also the latest in a pile of near-identical Black Friday sends. Earlier ones went out 11/26 ("Happy Thanksgiving! 🦃") and 11/27 ("Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!"), so this one mostly wins on topical focus.
- Verdict: a competent, well-targeted category send for anyone who cares about kitchen, but it's too long and arrives into fatigue.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. "Kitchen deals" in the subject leads to a kitchen hero, so there's no bait-and-switch.
- Each block has a specific dollar offer on a named product ("$200 off select Breville espresso machines", "$170 off Le Creuset 6.5-Qt. deep round Dutch oven").
- The visual rhythm is consistent. A lifestyle photo, a bold lowercase headline and a "Shop Now" button repeat down the page, so it's easy to scan.
- Premium brand names (Breville, KitchenAid, Le Creuset, Fellow, De'Longhi) act as trust anchors and give people a reason to click.
- The "lowest price all year!" callout on the cookware set adds credible urgency.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, and the kitchen story fades into a general Black Friday grid of furniture, decor and rugs.
- Every button says "Shop Now". Nothing tells the reader what's behind it.
- The sweepstakes and The Design Desk block near the bottom pull attention away from the sale and add clutter.
- The sender name "Crate & Barrel BLACK FRIDAY" in caps, plus "HERE!" and the 🔥 in the subject, reads as shouty. It also repeats the "HERE!" from the earlier "Our biggest sale of the year is HERE!" send the same day.
- The top banner has small, hard-to-read type ("Today Only! Select Wreaths, Garlands & Monochrome Floral: 40% off"). It competes with the kitchen hero and is irrelevant to it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email by roughly half. Keep the hero, the four or five best kitchen blocks and one "shop all kitchen" button. Drop the general-category grid and the sweepstakes, or move them to a separate send.
- 2. Remove the off-topic wreath banner from the top of a kitchen email.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with specific copy, such as "Shop espresso machines", "Shop Le Creuset" and "Shop mixers".
- 4. Put a real deadline on the kitchen offer, such as "Kitchen doorbusters end tonight at midnight", instead of relying on "lowest price all year".
- 5. Don't send another all-purpose Black Friday blast within hours. Segment this to people who've browsed or bought kitchen.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Breville, KitchenAid, Le Creuset: up to 40% off today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen doorbusters: $200 off espresso machines`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lowest price all year on Le Creuset, KitchenAid and Breville. Ends tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Espresso machines, mixers and cookware sets, all marked down for Black Friday.`
