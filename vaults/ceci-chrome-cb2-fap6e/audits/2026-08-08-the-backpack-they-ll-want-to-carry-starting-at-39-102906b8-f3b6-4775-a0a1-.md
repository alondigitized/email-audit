# The backpack they’ll want to carry, starting at $39!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing a genuinely useful buying-guide email — "the backpack size guide" — organized by Small/Medium/Large with a "what fits inside" checklist for each tier (folders, water bottle, lunchbox for small; tablet, folders for medium; full binder, jacket for large). It's the same playbook as the 8/7 coffee-table guide, and it works for the same reason: it solves a real parent problem (which backpack size fits my kid) instead of just dumping SKUs.
- The $39 starting price from the subject line never actually appears as a callout in the body — there's no "starting at $39" banner anywhere visible, just three "Shop [Size] Backpacks" buttons. That's a miss since the subject's whole hook is the price. Lower down it sprawls into fall arrivals, a sweepstakes, cross-sell products, a credit card pitch, and an app download block — solid guide, bloated tail.
- Judgment: strong top half, the size-guide framing is the right creative idea for this category, but it doesn't deliver on its own subject line's price hook and gets baggy after the bundle section.

## What's working

- Size-based structure (Small/Medium/Large) with "what fits inside" lists is genuinely helpful, parent-relevant content, not just product shots.
- Clear, differentiated hero images for each size with kids actually wearing/using the backpacks in believable school/home settings.
- "Make it a bundle" cross-sell (backpack + lunchbox + water bottle) is a logical, non-pushy upsell placed right after the size sections.
- Consistent Crate & Kids brand navy/mustard palette and typography throughout — no jarring style breaks.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "starting at $39" but no price appears anywhere in the visible body — the hook is unfulfilled.
- The email runs long and unfocused after the bundle CTA: fall arrivals banner, $500 sweepstakes, "we love these for you" cross-sell, credit card pitch, app download — five different asks competing after the actual backpack content ends.
- No star ratings or review counts on the backpacks themselves, which past Crate & Barrel/Kids sends in this brand history have used effectively (e.g. the 8/7 bestsellers sweep, 8/7 nursery faves).
- Individual backpack product names/prices in the "what fits" grids aren't visible at this render size — no per-product price anchor next to the actual products being sold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a visible "$39+" or "starting at $39" price badge near the top hero or size-guide header — the subject's core hook needs a body-level payoff.
- 2. Trim the tail: cut or consolidate the fall-arrivals banner, sweepstakes, and credit-card blocks into a single lower module, or move to a follow-up send — right now four asks dilute the one good idea.
- 3. Add star ratings under at least the hero backpacks in each size tier, consistent with what's worked in recent sends.
- 4. Show a price under each size's "Shop" button so the size guide doubles as a price ladder (Small $39, Medium $X, Large $X).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Which backpack size actually fits your kid?`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Backpacks by size, starting at $39`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Small, medium, or large — here's what fits inside each one.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: bundle up and save on the whole kit.`
