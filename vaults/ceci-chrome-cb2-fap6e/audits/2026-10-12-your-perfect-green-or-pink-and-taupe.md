# Your perfect green. Or pink. And taupe.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a color story ("Your perfect green. Or pink. And taupe."), but the body opens with the Long Weekend Sale: "ENDS TOMORROW," up to 60% off. The hero then pivots to "Custom is the ultimate luxury" with a "Shop the new collection" button. The email has three jobs at once (sale, custom upholstery, new arrivals), and the subject only covers one of them.
- The middle is the strongest part. Four designer pieces (Faible Sectional, Fitz Chair, Curvo Sofa, Gwyneth Chair) each get a big lifestyle photo, a designer credit, a swatch row ("90+ fabrics" or "35+ fabrics") and a "Shop now" button. The Ambroisie Sofa closes the run. The swatches are what actually deliver on the subject. After that the email turns into CB2's usual kitchen sink: a free-shipping banner, a rewards-dollars block, "More for you" product tiles, then app, registry and credit-card promos.
- I'd rate it a solid, slightly over-stuffed send. The imagery is good, but the subject, hero and offer don't line up. It is also the sixth CB2 send in four days. Compared with "2 SALES END SOON" on 10/12, the subject here is more charming and less clear about the deal.

## What's working

- Each hero product gets a full-width photo, a designer credit and a fabric-count line, so a shopper can see the choice on offer.
- The fabric swatch rows back up the subject's color promise.
- "Up to 60% off" with "Ends tomorrow" at the top is a credible, specific offer with a deadline.
- The rewards block (10% back, 4% back, 1% back, no annual fee) gives loyalty value.
- The photography is cohesive and moody, and it reads as CB2.

## What's weak

- The subject sells color variety, but the hero leads with a sale and a "custom" tagline, so the first thing the reader sees isn't what the subject led them to expect.
- The email carries too many ideas: sale, custom, new collection, shipping, rewards card, app and registry. There is no single focus.
- "Shop the new collection" appears twice, and neither CTA mentions the 60% off, so the sale and the product story don't connect.
- It is the same kitchen-sink template CB2 has sent all week, including the free-shipping and "2 sales" emails. Subscribers will see it as repeat noise.
- The footer is long, with dense fine print, and the long scroll buries the app and registry blocks.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with the color/fabric story and move the sale to a slim banner, or lead with the sale and rewrite the subject to match. Cut the rewards, app and registry blocks from sale sends.
- 2. Make the hero CTA specific, for example "Shop sofas up to 60% off," and add a "Shop all colors" link under the swatch rows.
- 3. Add a "Ends tomorrow" line to the top of the hero and a real preheader that complements the subject.
- 4. Space sends out. Run weekly themes instead of near-identical daily blasts.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off, in 90+ fabrics`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Green, pink, taupe: 60% off ends tomorrow`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Long Weekend Sale ends tomorrow. Custom sofas and chairs, 90+ fabrics.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Pick your fabric, save up to 60%. Final day tomorrow.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The CB2 email ships a raw, unrendered merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) in the delivered HTML. It also has widespread missing alt text and several non-HTTPS tracking pixels. The QA pass rate is 43%. Header-based checks (authentication, List-Unsubscribe) could not be verified because the relay didn't capture those headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **72 tracking links were not probed.** They go through click-redirect domains, so the QA tool skipped the HTTP check. Destination validity is unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Ten Dotomi pixels load over plain HTTP.**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed or insecure content will drop these. Cookie-sync and retargeting will fail silently for those recipients.
- The first-party tracking pixels use HTTPS, so there is no issue there: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/ef0d0936-...`.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **At least 21 images have no `alt` attribute.**
  - Content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`: 1548dde1…jpg, 1020751b…jpg, c7a18f27…gif, 95a19ea8…gif, 50da8c55…gif, 66a53a89…gif, 40340c6c…gif, 31c2b7f0…jpg, acb86340…jpg, d4ac8a16…jpg.
  - Tracking pixels: Dotomi `pub1–10.php`, `UCMController`, `o.gif`, and the `dv.cb2.com/o/…` image.
  - If the content images carry the message, recipients with images blocked or using screen readers get nothing. Tracking pixels should use `alt=""`, and they are better marked decorative than left without the attribute.
- The `<html lang="en">` attribute is present.
- The viewport meta tag sets `maximum-scale=1`. This blocks pinch-zoom on some clients and is an accessibility problem.
- Several `@font-face` rules point at `www.crateandbarrel.com` (HNW05) and at `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...` (double slash). Both resolve only if those hosts allow cross-origin font loads. Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, so the fallback stack matters.
- The HTML was truncated, so I couldn't check the body structure, the VML/MSO fallbacks, or the bulletproof buttons.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is visible in the output.** This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%…%%` personalization string that didn't resolve at send time. It was probably meant to feed a product-reviews or ratings block, likely a data-extension or AMPscript lookup that returned empty or errored. It will show as literal text, or it will leave a broken or empty module.
- I found no other unresolved tokens in the QA data.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` header and no `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058).** The QA tool itself says AgentMail may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed. If the headers are really missing from the sent message, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024) would require them.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The sender domain is `mail.cb2.com`. I can't tell whether it passed.
- I can't see the visible footer unsubscribe link or the postal address in the truncated HTML. I can't confirm or rule out those CAN-SPAM requirements.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs weren't resolved, because the 72 links went through click-redirect domains. I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment.
- Tracking appears to be handled by the ESP's redirect layer (the `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters on the `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels), not UTMs in the visible URLs.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the data extension or content-block source. Add a fallback that suppresses the module when the list is empty. Add a pre-send check for any leftover `%%` strings.
2. **Add `alt` text to the 10 content images.** Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
3. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Use `https://login.dotomi.com` and `https://ads.dotomi.com`, or drop the `pub1–10` cookie-sync images if they add no value.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag** so users can pinch-zoom.
5. **Verify the headers against the raw message.** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't rely on the AgentMail relay copy.
6. **Resolve the redirect links** with a follow-redirects probe, to confirm they return 200 and carry the expected UTMs.
