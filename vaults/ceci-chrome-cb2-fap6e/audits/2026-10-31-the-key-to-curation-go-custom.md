# The key to curation? Go custom.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a custom-upholstery education email with a Warehouse Sale banner bolted on top. The hero says "FINAL WEEKEND · WAREHOUSE SALE · UP TO 60% OFF" with a "Shop the Sale" button. The subject promises "the key to curation? Go custom," and the body is about fabric swatches and made-to-order pieces. The two halves don't match. Someone who opens for the custom story lands on a 60% off banner for a different product set. Someone who opens for the sale gets three screens of fabric talk before any discounted product appears.
- The middle is well made. "A DESIGNER'S BEST-KEPT SECRET" is an easy-to-read pitch with a low-risk entry point, "Order 10 Free Swatches." The Rockwell Ottoman, Symmetric Chair and Nolan Daybed modules each show a fabric count (70+, 60+, 85+) and a swatch row. That is a good hook for someone planning a room. The email then runs on for roughly 8,000 pixels: a credit-card rewards block, a six-product "More For You" grid, app and registry promos, and dense legal text. It is the third CB2 send in about 24 hours that mentions the Warehouse Sale, after "THIS IS YOUR CHANCE" on 10/30 and "THIS WEEK'S STEALS" earlier today.
- Verdict: a good custom-furniture story with weak focus. The sale banner and the rewards module compete with it. It should lead with one message.

## What's working

- The "Order 10 Free Swatches" button is specific and low-commitment. It is a real reason to click for a considered purchase.
- Each custom product module shows a fabric count and a swatch row. This shows the customization options instead of just asserting them.
- Large, moody room photography keeps the CB2 editorial look and reads as premium.
- The Warehouse Sale banner is clear. A plum band, a big "UP TO 60% OFF" and a visible button put the offer at the top.
- The "More For You" grid shows struck-through prices on several items, so some sale pricing is visible.

## What's weak

- The subject is about custom and the hero is about the Warehouse Sale, so the email is not about one thing.
- Three different CTAs ("Shop the Sale," "Order 10 Free Swatches," "Shop Custom Furniture") appear in the first screen and a half. The custom CTA appears twice and the generic "Shop Now" repeats three times.
- The credit-card rewards block (10% back, "NEW: 4% back" on dining, 4%, 1%, no annual fee) is a full-width banking pitch in the middle of a furniture email. It breaks the flow before the product grid.
- The email is extremely long. It ends with app promos, a wedding-registry gift offer, a 15% sign-up offer and a block of fine print. Product detail like price and lead time for custom pieces is missing, and custom items show no pricing.
- The "More For You" tiles have small, hard-to-read prices and no reviews or social proof.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story per send. Either lead with custom (swatches first, sale banner removed or demoted to a slim strip), or send a clean Warehouse Sale email. Today's send tries to be both.
- 2. Add a lead time and a starting price for the custom pieces, for example "From $X, ships in N weeks." Custom buyers need this before they will click.
- 3. Cut or move the rewards module to the bottom, and drop the app and registry promos. Fewer modules would shorten the email a lot.
- 4. Replace the three "Shop Now" buttons with item-specific copy such as "Pick Your Fabric."
- 5. Add a real preheader. Nothing is visible in the render.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Pick your fabric. Free swatches inside.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Made your way: 85+ fabrics, free swatches`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order 10 free swatches and see the color in your own light before you commit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ottomans, chairs and daybeds in 60+ fabrics, plus Warehouse Sale up to 60% off.`

# Technical Audit: CB2, "The key to curation? Go custom."

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed render-breaking defect: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, was left in the output. It also loads about 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking pixels and has about 22 images with no alt text. The QA pass rate was 43%. The HTML was truncated and the relay stripped the headers, so compliance and click-through checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing-page validity are unverified.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** These `http://` images may be blocked by clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, each with `?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Pixel volume.** Ten cookie-redirect pixels plus the UCM pixel add 11 third-party requests per open. Each is a possible blocked-content warning and a deliverability and privacy-filter risk.
- **Open-tracking pixels** are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/ad9a226f-...?mi_cid=...`. Both are HTTPS and look intentional.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 9 content images**, all hosted at `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `ca5f45cd-…jpg`
  - `5a8def92-…jpg`

  If any of these carry text or CTAs, they are unreadable with images off or with a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on all tracking pixels** (the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel). Lower impact, but an empty `alt=""` is still advisable.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4 on mobile.

- **Cross-domain font loading.** The `HNW05` `@font-face` rules point at `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, which is a different brand domain. This only matters if that host restricts hotlinking.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%`** appears in the rendered output. This is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style personalization string that did not resolve, which suggests a dynamic reviews or SKU-list block failed at send time. The unresolved string may appear visibly, or it may sit in a link or attribute and break it.
- The token's exact location is not visible in the truncated HTML, so its visible impact is unconfirmed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture it, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven absence.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status cannot be assessed. The relay stripped the header, so this is not evidence of failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address** could not be checked because the HTML source was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 71 links are wrapped in click-redirect tracking, so final destinations and any UTM parameters cannot be seen from the source or verified by the QA pass. No continuity issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the unresolved `%%reviews_skulist%%` token.** Check the send-time data source or dynamic content block, and add a fallback or suppress the block when data is missing.
2. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS.** Alternatively, remove the 10 `pubN.php` cookie-redirect pixels or consolidate them into a server-side integration.
3. **Add meaningful `alt` text to the 9 content images,** and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
5. **Verify headers on the original send, not the relay copy.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML.** Check the footer unsubscribe link and address, and resolve a sample of the tracking links to confirm UTM parameters and destinations.
7. **Delete the stray `[IMPUT HERE ...]` comment** from the template.
