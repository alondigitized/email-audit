## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 98 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were validated. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - Five LiveRamp pixels, `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`, carry a hashed identifier in `s=`.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` is the open pixel.
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2358d13b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is another tracking image.
  - Each carries a per-recipient identifier (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`, `s=`). The pixels are repeated, and they will be blocked or proxied by Apple Mail Privacy Protection and similar clients.
- **Scene7 asset:** `https://s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is a spacer image served from a separate CDN host. This adds a dependency, and the encoded filename suggests a template artifact.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** The QA check flags about 55 images with no alt text. They include content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` (for example `ce06de22-…jpg`, `a62b6734-…gif`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`). With images off, or in a screen reader, these give no text equivalent. The check does not say whether the attribute is absent or empty, so confirm that in the full source.
- **Pixels and spacers:** The tracking pixels and the spacer (`rlcdn`, `mi.`, `dv.`, `scene7`) should carry `alt=""`. That is acceptable only if the attribute is present and empty.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is an accessibility regression.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is declared twice, alongside the `http-equiv` form, and is not self-closed.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - The leftover template comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
  - These are minor.
- **CSS conflicts:** `*{line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` are global resets that may override intended styles. The `img {max-width:…}` rules at `min-width: 640px` and `768px` (640px and 600px) conflict at widths of 768px and above.
