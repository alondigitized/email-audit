# The best living rooms on our feed
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- This one's a mode-switch, and it's the weak one: CB2 leads with "THIS IS LIVING" over a living-room hero, then immediately pivots the entire email into a pitch for free interior design services — designer consults, mood boards, "3 Ways to Meet" (in-person/virtual/in-store). The subject promised "the best living rooms on our feed," which reads like an editorial inspiration roundup, but the actual content is a services funnel with a single soft CTA ("Book a Free Appointment") repeated three times. That's a mismatch between what was promised and what's delivered.
- Compare this to 8/3's "Goop color studies" or 8/5's "The marble that elevates everything" — those were genuine editorial sends that delivered exactly what the subject implied. This one borrows editorial language for the subject but is actually a lead-gen play for design consultations, which is a different job entirely. There's no discount, no urgency, and the "GET INSPIRED" product grid at the bottom (living room, lighting, bedroom, rugs, dining room, decor, pillows, entertaining) is generic category navigation, not curated "best of feed" content — so even that section doesn't pay off the subject line.
- It's competently built and on-brand visually, but it's the least-targeted send in this stretch of history. No offer, ambiguous primary ask, and a subject line that oversells what's inside.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent CB2 visual identity — dark brown/cream palette, serif headlines, generous whitespace throughout.
- The "3 Ways to Meet" block (In Person / Virtually / In Store) is genuinely well-organized with three distinct, clear CTAs for different comfort levels.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, layout holds together end to end.
- The "GET INSPIRED" grid at the bottom gives a real multi-category browsing path (Living Room, Lighting, Bedroom, Rugs, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Pillows, Entertaining) with tidy underlined labels.
- App download and Rewards Dollars blocks at the footer are compact and don't clutter the main message.

## What's weak

- Subject says "best living rooms on our feed" — the email is actually about interior design consultation services, not a feed roundup. That's a bait-and-switch on open.
- No discount, no urgency, no price anchor anywhere — a much softer ask than CB2's usual stacked-discount or restock-alert playbooks that scored 8-9 this week.
- Primary CTA ("Book a Free Appointment" / "Book Now" x3) is repeated four times but never varies — no single hero product or moment to click through to.
- 2. - The hero shot ("THIS IS LIVING") functions purely as a mood image; it's not linked to any product visible in the render, so the strongest visual asset in the email doesn't drive a click.
- For a shopper expecting inspiration content, "book an appointment" is a much higher-commitment ask than "shop this look" — mismatched intent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix subject/content alignment first — either rename the subject to something like "Free design help for your living room" so it matches the content, or restructure the body to actually show "best living rooms on our feed" (real customer/social room photos) before pivoting to the design-services pitch.
- 2. Make the hero image shoppable — link the living room shot to the actual sofa/chair/table shown, since right now the strongest visual in the email has no product path.
- 3. Add one concrete incentive tied to the design service (e.g., "$50 off your first styled purchase with a free consult") — right now the offer is pure service, no monetary hook, which is unusual for this brand's typically offer-forward cadence.
- 4. Trim CTA repetition — three near-identical "Book Now" buttons in the "3 Ways to Meet" block plus a fourth "Book a Free Appointment" above is redundant; keep the three meeting-format buttons but drop the top one, or make the top one link to a completed room, not a booking form.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Book a free room refresh with our designers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your living room, redesigned for free`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `See real spaces our designers transformed — then book yours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Mood boards, color palettes, product picks — all free, all yours.`
