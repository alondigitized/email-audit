## Technical Audit

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** About 35 `<img>` elements were flagged.
  - About 21 are content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, plus `m/47/bbf6f9d4-...png` and `971aa10f-...jpg`. These are likely product or tile images.
  - The hero image `2026_0703_CB_NewBedding_HeroVideo_v2-o` on `s7d5.scene7.com` has no alt text, so it has no text fallback when images are blocked.
  - The tracking and pixel images (`448796.gif` ×5, `o.gif`, `f3c0d22992a01946.png` ×6, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...`) should carry `alt=""` explicitly. They are flagged as missing the attribute.
- **Viewport.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom in clients that honor it.
- **Text sizing.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;...}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the later `body{...:100%}` rule and prevent text resizing on iOS.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;`. The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, so this is invalid markup.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, and the later 640px rule wins, so the 600px cap is dead code.
- **Template leftovers.**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is unreplaced placeholder text with a typo.
  - `format-detection` meta tags are duplicated (3 separate tags plus the combined one).
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
- **Hero video.** The hero asset name says "HeroVideo". The truncated source doesn't show whether a poster image or fallback is used, so this is unverified.
