# Just added to clearance
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's "New Items Added" clearance send — nearly identical creative to the 2026-08-16 "SUMMER CLEARANCE" email (6/10), same "NEW ITEMS ADDED / SUMMER CLEARANCE" hero over a warm wood-and-citrus lifestyle shot, but this time with zero percentage or dollar figure anywhere near the hero. The fine print at the very bottom reveals "up to 60% off clearance," but nothing in the visible fold communicates that — a subscriber has to scroll past a sweepstakes, a design-services CTA, a review-for-$1,000 banner, and an app/rewards block before hitting any legal copy that even hints at the offer size.
- The category-button wall (Shop All, Furniture, Outdoor, Decor, Lighting, Rugs, Bedding & Bath) is a reasonable nav pattern for clearance, but it's undifferentiated from the 08-16 send and gives no sense of what's actually new or how much is off. This reads as a filler/reminder email rather than a genuine "just added" event — CB2 is cannibalizing its own clearance messaging by re-running the same wood-table hero with a slightly different headline instead of showing new product.

## What's working

- Clean, on-brand hero typography and layout — no render bugs, text is crisp and legible.
- "NEW ITEMS ADDED" framing signals freshness/urgency for bargain hunters checking back on clearance.
- Category button stack makes it easy to jump straight to a department instead of a generic homepage link.
- Footer utility grid (app, rewards card, gift registry, SMS signup) is well organized and scannable.

## What's weak

- No visible discount percentage or dollar amount anywhere above the fold — the offer itself is invisible until the legal fine print at the bottom.
- No actual product imagery — the hero is a lifestyle/mood shot (citrus and wood table), not the clearance items being promoted.
- Nearly identical creative to the 08-16 "SUMMER CLEARANCE" send just four days ago, reinforcing repetitive cadence.
- Sweepstakes, design consultation, and reviews-for-$1,000 blocks all compete with the clearance offer for attention, diluting the primary CTA.
- "Shop All" as the top button is a vague, low-commitment CTA rather than something pointing at the new clearance items specifically.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual discount number ("Up to 60% off") directly in the hero — it's currently buried in a footnote block at the very bottom, which is the single biggest miss here.
- 2. Replace the mood-shot hero with 3-4 actual new clearance product photos so subscribers can see what's new before clicking.
- 3. Cut or move the sweepstakes/design-consultation/reviews blocks below a dedicated "shop the new markdowns" grid — right now they're pushing the actual offer further down.
- 4. Vary the hero photography from the 08-16 send so this doesn't read as a recycled email to anyone who opened that one.
