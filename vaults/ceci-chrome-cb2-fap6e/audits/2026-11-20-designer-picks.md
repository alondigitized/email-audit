# DESIGNER PICKS
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is an editorial "designer provenance" send. Each hero product is named with its designer and city, for example "Gwyneth Swivel Chair, by Ginni, Los Angeles" and "Rio Bistro Table, by Studio Arkos, Porto". The look is warm, moody and cohesive, and it fits CB2's premium-modern brand. The render is clean, with no broken images.
- The weakness is that it ignores what CB2 has been shouting all week. Black Friday ("UP TO 60% OFF") is pushed to a small banner near the bottom. The top of the email is "THINGS OF QUALITY" with a generic "SHOP BEST SELLERS" button. Five product blocks each get their own "SHOP NOW" or "SHOP" link, and the page is long. That makes it a nice brand-building piece that lands in the middle of a price-driven week. Compared with "FREE SHIPPING FOR BLACK FRIDAY" earlier today (7/10) and "So many cult favorites on sale" on 11/19 (8/10), it is softer and less urgent.
- Verdict: well made, but it buries the one thing subscribers are primed to respond to. It's a solid 6.

## What's working

- The designer and origin storytelling ("By Studio Arkos, Porto, Portugal") gives each product a reason to exist beyond price.
- The visual hierarchy is clear. There is a large hero, then alternating full-width lifestyle shots, each with one short caption and one link.
- The photography is consistent: warm woods, olive velvet and dark interiors. It matches the brand and the Black Friday creative from earlier in the week.
- The footer has a "MORE FOR YOU" product grid with visible prices and strikethroughs, plus the rewards and app modules, so there is a second chance to convert.
- The "NOW OPEN: CB2 Coral Gables" block is a useful local hook, and it's clearly separated from the rest.

## What's weak

- The Black Friday "UP TO 60% OFF" banner sits about 80% of the way down. Most mobile readers won't reach it.
- The hero CTA, "SHOP BEST SELLERS", is generic and appears twice. It doesn't match the designer story above it.
- The email is very long, with about five product blocks, then lighting, a sale banner, a store opening, a product grid and the footer modules. There is no single focal action.
- The "NEW & BEST SELLING LIGHTING" block and the sale banner follow the same pattern as the product blocks. That blurs the one offer.
- The links are small and low-contrast against the photos (for example "SHOP NOW" under the product captions).

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Black Friday offer into the hero. Put "Up to 60% off" in a slim banner above "THINGS OF QUALITY", or make it the hero headline, and keep the designer story below it.
- 2. Replace the generic buttons with specific ones, such as "SHOP THE GWYNETH CHAIR" or "SHOP DESIGNER PICKS ON SALE".
- 3. Cut the email to three or four designer products. Drop the lighting block or fold it into the product grid.
- 4. Make the CTAs full-width buttons with higher contrast instead of small underlined text over photos.
- 5. Add one trust or urgency line near the top, such as "Free shipping on $99+" or the sale end date. The 11/20 free-shipping send already established that offer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals, a viewport meta and `lang="en"`. The source is truncated, so the body, footer and link targets could not be inspected. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings, 57% pass rate). The confirmed problems are missing image alt text and ten plain-HTTP Dotomi pixels.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **66 tracking links were not probed.** They were skipped as click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Ten Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`.**
  - They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`.
  - One more, `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`, is also plain HTTP.
  - Clients such as Gmail proxy images and may block or rewrite non-HTTPS sources, so these pixels will often fail silently.
  - Ten third-party ad-sync calls in one email also add unnecessary load and privacy surface.
- **Two first-party pixels load over HTTPS.** They are `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/cab3ebb3-...?mi_cid=...`. The `mi_u` value is a 64-character hex string, probably a hashed recipient identifier. Neither pixel has `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `8ac1b08f-…`
  - `3db4cd43-…`
  - `ff63ccb0-…`
  - `92687153-…`
  - `5e682ee3-…`
  - `726c75f8-…`
  - `4442bac9-…`
  - `93958f7d-…`
  - `373bf725-…`

  If these are the product or hero images, the email is unusable with images off or with a screen reader. If any are linked, the link has no accessible name.
- **The tracking and pixel images also lack `alt`.** They should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"` so screen readers skip them.
- **The viewport meta disables pinch-zoom.** It reads `maximum-scale=1`, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern in clients that honor it.
- **Text resizing is suppressed.** The CSS sets `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `body` and `*`.
- **Font URLs have a doubled slash.** `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...` appears in several `@font-face` rules.
- **A template placeholder is left in the source.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Font sources are split across two domains.** `HNW05` loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, while the CB2 fonts load from `www.cb2.com`. This is a minor dependency risk.
- **Other rendering checks can't be made.** The truncated source cuts off before the body, so `role="presentation"`, dark-mode handling and VML buttons could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source or QA data. Unrendered merge tokens could not be checked in the truncated body. The `mi_u`/`mi_cid`/`mi_mid` parameters on the pixels look like resolved values, not raw tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** The QA tool notes it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** This is the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe from high-volume senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status could not be verified. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the From address, but that is not proof of authentication.
- **The footer was not visible.** The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be verified in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 66 links were routed through click-tracking redirects and skipped, and the truncated source does not show final URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity defects are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add `alt` text** to the nine `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Add `alt=""` to all pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://ads.dotomi.com/...`). Consider dropping or consolidating the ten `pub*.php` sync calls.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and reconsider `text-size-adjust:none`.
4. **Fix the `//Styles` double-slash** in the font URLs and remove the leftover template comment.
5. **Re-run QA against the raw `.eml`** to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
6. **Re-run with the full HTML and an unfiltered link probe** (or resolve the redirects) so the footer compliance, UTM parameters and landing pages can be audited.
