# THINGS OF QUALITY
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's "new collection lookbook" mode — a full-page cascade of moody, styled room shots (rug, sectional, chair, lighting, rugs again, decor) under vague credential copy like "Things of quality you won't find anywhere else" and "Custom is the ultimate luxury." It's beautifully art-directed but structurally identical to the "ONLY AT CB2" (9/24) and "New arrivals are in" (9/26) sends — six-plus product categories stacked before any real offer appears.
- The one actual deal — Upholstery Event, up to 25% off — is buried two-thirds down the page, after Best Sellers and a Decor/Mirrors section. By the time a scrolling reader gets there, they've already been asked to care about accent chairs, lighting, rugs, and vases with zero pricing context. This is catalog browsing dressed as an email, not a conversion-focused send.
- Judgement: pretty, on-brand, and completely undifferentiated from CB2's last three sends this week — no real subject-line offer, no urgency until the very bottom ("Sale on now up to 50% off" appears almost as an afterthought after the 25% callout). 5/10 territory again.

## What's working

- Clean, consistent visual style — warm earth-tone photography, consistent typography, no render bugs or broken images visible.
- The "Custom is the Ultimate Luxury" section clearly names the discount context (custom Fable Sectional) with a legible "SHOP NOW" button.
- Upholstery Event banner (up to 25% off) and the "Sale on Now — up to 50%" banner near the bottom are both visually distinct with bold typography.
- CB2 App and Card benefits footer block is a nice loyalty touch for existing customers.

## What's weak

- Subject line "THINGS OF QUALITY" carries zero offer, zero urgency, and reads like a tagline fragment, not an email subject.
- The real discounts (25% off Upholstery, 50% off sale) are stacked at the very bottom, after 7+ scrolls of aspirational imagery — most inbox readers won't get there.
- All-caps subject is a mild spam signal and doesn't differentiate from CB2's other recent all-caps or vague sends ("ONLY AT CB2").
- This is the same "editorial cascade then discount" structure as 9/25's "Italian vintage reissues" and 9/24's "ONLY AT CB2" — subscribers scrolling through CB2 emails this week are seeing the same beats on repeat.
- No specific product price or clear single hero CTA up top — first fold is mood, not offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the actual discount. Move "Semi-Annual Upholstery Event — up to 25% off" or the "Sale on Now — up to 50% off" banner into the hero position, above the lifestyle cascade, and reference it in the subject line.
- 2. Cut the cascade length. Six-plus lifestyle sections before a discount is too much scroll for a promotional email — trim to 2-3 categories max, or split into a separate "lookbook" send vs. a "sale" send.
- 3. Fix the subject line to carry a real offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off upholstery — plus new arrivals`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale's on: up to 50% off select pieces`
- 4. Give the preview text actual job to do — right now it's not visible/distinct in the render, so make it complement the subject with a second offer or urgency cue.
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Semi-Annual Upholstery Event ends soon — plus new lighting & rugs`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your sectional, your fabric, your rules — now up to 25% off`
- 5. Vary the weekly template. After four straight cascade-format sends (9/23–9/26), a subscriber checking daily will start to autopilot past these — alternate with a single-offer format like the cleaner 9/25 "Upholstery Event" send that scored 6/10.
