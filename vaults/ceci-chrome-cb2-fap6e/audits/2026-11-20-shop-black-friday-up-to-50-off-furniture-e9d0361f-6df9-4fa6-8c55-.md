# Shop Black Friday: up to 50% off furniture!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent furniture-led Black Friday send, but it is the fourth or fifth near-identical Black Friday email from Crate & Barrel in two days. The top of the email has a clear hero ("Black Friday is on! Up to 50% off + free shipping") with a Shop Now button. A thin strip above it points to a Thanksgiving delivery cutoff. Directly below is a furniture-specific block, "up to 50% off furniture", with a "Shop Furniture Sale" button and a grid of room tiles (dining, bedroom, sofas & sectionals, storage, accent tables, home office).
- The render is heavily downscaled (680px wide, about 12,000px tall). I can read headlines, section order and buttons, but not fine print or most product detail. After the furniture section the email drifts into a general Black Friday catch-all: a holiday decor "guest-ready" block, kitchen at "up to 40% off", an 8-tile category grid, a clearance block, a TheDesignDesk consultation with a "chance to win $5000" sweepstakes, a product carousel and an app promo. The subject promises furniture, and roughly the top third delivers it. The rest dilutes the message.
- Verdict: good subject-to-hero match and a strong, clear offer, but it is long and cluttered. It looks like a copy of the 11-19 furniture send ("Save up to 50% on sofas, dining furniture & more for Black Friday"), with little new here.

## What's working

- The subject and hero match. "Furniture" and "up to 50% off" appear in the subject and again in the first two blocks.
- The offer is concrete and stacked: up to 50% off plus free shipping.
- The room-based tile grid (dining, bedroom, storage, home office) helps a furniture shopper self-select.
- A "save on this bestseller" product block shows an actual price and strike-through price for a sectional.
- The delivery-cutoff strip adds credible urgency.

## What's weak

- There are too many CTAs. Shop Now, Shop Furniture Sale, Shop Black Friday Sale, several more Shop buttons, plus Book Your Free Appointment and Learn More all compete.
- The furniture focus fades after the first third. Kitchen, decor, gifts and clearance take over, so it reads as the broad sale email again.
- The email is very long. The sweepstakes, TheDesignDesk block and app promo sit far below where most people stop scrolling.
- It repeats the 11-19 furniture send and the earlier 11-20 Black Friday kickoff almost word for word, with no new hook such as a doorbuster or a tier.
- The "up to 50%" range hides which furniture actually gets the top discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make it a true furniture email. Cut the kitchen, gifts and clearance blocks and put a second furniture block (best-selling sofas with real sale prices) where they were.
- 2. Reduce the CTAs to one primary button, repeated at most twice.
- 3. Show which categories get the deepest discount on the room tiles, for example "Dining tables: 50% off".
- 4. Move the TheDesignDesk sweepstakes and app promo to a short footer module.
- 5. Give this send a different angle from the 11-19 furniture email, such as a delivery-date guarantee or a price-drop tier.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. The QA run scored 57% with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The substantive issues are missing alt text on roughly 40 content images and a viewport that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from header capture and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **98 tracking links were skipped.** They sit on click-redirect domains, so the QA run never probed them. Destinations, redirect chains and link health are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` is five copies of the same LiveRamp pixel. Each carries a 64-hex identifier.
- **Other beacons:** `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/e7e509df-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are open and delivery beacons.
- No broken or malformed hrefs were evidenced in the data provided.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, about 54 images flagged:**
  - About 40 look like content images, for example `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/f62f4e33-3599-46c5-9b3d-0d263bbfcb5a.jpg` and `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`. Seven more are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - The `rlcdn` and `o.gif` beacons are tracking pixels. An empty alt is acceptable there.
  - The QA check can't tell an absent `alt` from `alt=""`. For the content images, a missing alt means the promotional and product content is invisible with images blocked or in a screen reader.
- **Viewport blocks zoom:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />`. `maximum-scale=1` prevents pinch-zoom, an accessibility issue (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is unfilled, and "IMPUT" is a typo. It is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype.
- **Redundant meta tags:** `format-detection` is declared four times, with conflicting combined and separate values.
- Positives: `lang="en"`, the MSO conditional fallbacks, a `color-scheme: light` declaration, and `-webkit-text-size-adjust`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the QA data.
- The source is truncated, so the body copy wasn't fully checked.
- Per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u=56aaa278…`, `mi_cid=…`, `mi_mid=…`) are populated correctly in the image URLs.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** the QA run says AgentMail may not capture this header. The real state is unknown.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** the same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe support is unverified. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a subdomain that matches the image and tracking hosts, which is consistent with proper alignment, but that is not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they couldn't be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- No UTM parameters are visible in the provided source. All 98 links go through the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` redirect layer, so the final destinations and any UTM tagging are unverifiable here.
- Landing-page alignment with the "up to 50% off furniture" offer can't be confirmed, because no links were resolved.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 40 content images, and use explicit `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Check the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating the compliance warnings as real. If they are absent in the raw message, that is a finding.
4. Resolve the redirects on a sample of the 98 tracking links to confirm destinations and UTM tagging.
5. Escape the `&` in the `<title>`, de-duplicate the `format-detection` metas, and remove the template placeholder comment.
6. Capture the full HTML, because the truncation hid the footer, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
