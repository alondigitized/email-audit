# Ends tonight: up to 40% off furniture
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- CB2 nails the top of this email — "ENDS TONIGHT / THE FURNITURE EVENT / UP TO 40% OFF" over a single hero chair image is exactly the clean, high-urgency mechanic that made the 07-28 "24 hours left" send a 9/10. Subject and hero copy match word-for-word, which is the single best thing this brand has done all week.
- But it immediately steps on its own message: right below the furniture hero sits a second full-bleed banner for "THE SEMI-ANNUAL BEDROOM + BATH EVENT — UP TO 30% OFF," followed by a third "NEW & BEST SELLING SOFAS & SECTIONALS" push. Three competing "shop now" moments in the first three screens is the same kitchen-sink problem that dragged down the 07-25 and 07-26 sends — a good idea (one urgent, ending-tonight sale) diluted by the instinct to cram in every other active promo.
- This is a solid, better-than-average send with a genuinely strong hero, undercut by structural repetition (this is effectively CB2's fifth near-identical "% off, ends soon" email in eight days) and a buried-fine-print dagger (‡) on the headline offer.

## What's working

- Hero banner is unambiguous: urgency ("ENDS TONIGHT"), offer ("UP TO 40% OFF"), and CTA ("SHOP THE SALE") are all visible without scrolling.
- Subject line and hero copy match exactly — no bait-and-switch between inbox and open, unlike the 07-24 send.
- Loyalty/rewards program is surfaced clearly (10% back, 4% back, no annual fee) for anyone already carrying the CB2 card.
- Clean render throughout — no broken images, no overlapping text, no placeholder copy anywhere in the six-item "More For You" grid.

## What's weak

- A second full-width sale banner (Bedroom + Bath, 30% off) directly beneath the furniture hero splits attention right after the strongest moment in the email.
- The headline "40% off" carries a ‡ symbol pointing to fine print that never resolves on-screen (only in the dense footer legal block) — a soft honesty ding.
- Three separate "shop" asks (Furniture, Bedroom+Bath, Sofas & Sectionals) plus a rewards pitch plus a review-contest ask before the product grid even starts — no single obvious "next click."
- Fifth near-identical discount-and-urgency send from CB2 in eight days (07-24 through 07-28 all hit this same beat); fatigue risk is real by now.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut straight from the furniture hero into the "More For You" product grid — move Bedroom+Bath and Sofas & Sectionals to a second, separate send or at minimum below the fold after the reward/review content, so the email has one hero offer, not three.
- 2. Resolve the ‡ inline ("‡ excludes clearance") directly under the headline instead of only in the footer — removes the one honesty gap in an otherwise clean send.
- 3. Since this is the 5th sale email in over a week, rotate in a non-discount beat (new arrivals, design story) next send to reset the pattern before open rates start declining.
- 4. A/B test subject urgency framing:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 40% off furniture ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your furniture picks — up to 40% off, tonight only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, chairs, and tables — before the event closes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The velvet chair from your cart just got cheaper.`
