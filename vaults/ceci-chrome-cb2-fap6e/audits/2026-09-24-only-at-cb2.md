# ONLY AT CB2
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- CB2 leads with "POPULAR FOR A REASON" over a mirror hero, then runs a long, undifferentiated cascade of best-seller product tiles (sectional, pendant, bed, rug, dining table, bowl) before finally hitting an actual discount — the "SEMI-ANNUAL UPHOLSTERY EVENT / UP TO 25% OFF" — buried two-thirds down and gated behind an app-download phone mockup with "YOUR FIRST LOOK. APP ONLY." This is the same app-gate trick as the 2026-09-24 "Your exclusive first look at sale" send, and it's just as frustrating: the one real offer in this email is the least accessible thing in it.
- The best-seller mosaic itself is clean and well-photographed, but it's copy-light ("A best-seller in projects and homes we love," designer credits) with no prices or discounts attached to any of the six hero products — this reads as a catalog scroll, not a sales email. It isn't until the "MORE FOR YOU" grid near the bottom that prices finally show up, and even then with no strikethroughs or percent-off badges.
- Compared to the brand's cadence this week — roll-ups, mosaics, a designer story, an urgency push — this is closest to the 2026-09-23 "THAT COLLECTED FEEL" mosaic in structure, minus any narrative thread. Judgment: visually polished but strategically muddled — a scroll-heavy catalog with one real offer hidden behind a paywall gimmick.

## What's working

- Hero image (carved mirror over a styled bedroom) is high-quality and on-brand.
- Each product tile is well-lit and consistently formatted, giving the email a cohesive, showroom feel.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom finally surfaces real prices, which the rest of the email lacks.
- Footer loyalty tease ("EARN 15% BACK" / Rewards Dollars, "GET 10% OFF" sign-up) gives the loyalist something concrete.

## What's weak

- No discount or offer appears until deep in the scroll — six product tiles before any price or percent-off shows up.
- The one real promotion (25% off upholstery) is locked behind an "APP ONLY" phone-mockup graphic instead of being clickable in-email.
- Subject "ONLY AT CB2" is generic exclusivity language with zero specificity — no product, no offer, no urgency.
- Repeats the exact app-gate mechanic flagged as a weakness in the 2026-09-24 "first look at sale" email — the brand hasn't corrected a known miss.
- Six near-identical hero product tiles in a row create scroll fatigue with no visual hierarchy pulling the eye to a single "start here" moment.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Upholstery Event out from behind the app mockup — make "UP TO 25% OFF" a clickable web CTA at or near the top, not a screen-in-a-screen graphic requiring a download.
- 2. Cut the product cascade from six tiles to three and add prices/percent-off to each so the email reads as a sale, not a lookbook.
- 3. Rewrite the subject to name the actual offer instead of vague exclusivity language.
- 4. Add a preheader that references the upholstery sale specifically, since none is legible in the render.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 25% off upholstery — starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sectionals, chairs & sofas: 25% off begins today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Semi-Annual Upholstery Event is here — shop sofas, chairs & sectionals up to 25% off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save up to 25% on our best-selling seating, for a limited time.`
- 5. Add a visible countdown or end-date to the Upholstery Event to create credible urgency, since none is shown.

## Technical Audit

The email fails on merge-token resolution and shows multiple deliverability/compliance gaps (missing unsubscribe headers, unconfirmed auth), alongside legacy accessibility and mixed-content issues in third-party pixel tags — 43% automated pass rate.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 69 tracking/redirect links were skipped by HTTP probing (click-redirect domains), so live-link validity is unconfirmed — recommend manual click-through QA before send.
- Multiple `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php`–`pub10.php` tracking pixels load over plain `http://`, not `https://` — flagged as non-HTTPS sources that may be blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies.
- `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController` tracking call is also `http://`, same blocking risk.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 content images (`image.mail.cb2.com/lib/.../*.jpg`) are missing `alt` text — screen readers and image-blocked clients will render no fallback copy.
- Tracking/pixel elements (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`, dotomi pixels, UCMController) also lack `alt` attributes, though this is lower-impact since they're 1x1 trackers, not content images.
- HTML uses legacy XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype and heavy MSO/legacy conditional comments — standard for enterprise ESP templates, no defects observed there.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL]** Raw unresolved merge tag `%%reviews_skulist%%` found in the rendered HTML — this token did not resolve at send time and will display literally to recipients. This is the single confirmed functional bug in the email.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — QA notes this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than confirmed absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not found — same caveat; one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — sender authentication status unknown via this relay; cannot confirm pass/fail.
- Recommend re-verifying all three at the raw SMTP/header level (outside the AgentMail relay) before treating these as real compliance failures.

No issues found — no UTM/landing-page data was available in the provided source or QA output to evaluate continuity.

7. Recommendations
