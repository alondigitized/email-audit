## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, in the HTML. It also has widespread missing alt text and several non-HTTPS Dotomi tracking pixels. The QA run passed 43% of checks. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified, because the AgentMail relay doesn't appear to capture them.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 70 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and status are unverified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are loaded over plain `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` is a further `http://` image. Clients that block mixed or insecure content will not fire them, and the tracking data will be incomplete.
- The first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/f25a6ff0-...` use HTTPS. Both lack alt text (see section 3).
- The `mi_u=` parameter on the `mi.cb2.com` pixel carries a 64-character hex value. It looks like a hashed subscriber identifier. This is standard for the platform, and the email doesn't show that it is misused.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Alt text is missing on 11 content images hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. Examples are `acc9cda8-3602-43a2-a836-7b9429f41320.jpg`, `c2b4aff7-...jpg` and `c7db1394-9cc2-4ba9-845c-71782b4ddeaa.gif`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as blank or as filenames.
- The 10 Dotomi pixels (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php`), `UCMController`, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel also lack alt text. For tracking pixels, an empty `alt=""` is the correct fix, so these are lower severity.
- The head has a placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is a template artifact and is harmless.
- `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center;}` is declared in the first style block. The later block has `p { padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important }`. This is a redundant, conflicting declaration, and the `!important` version wins. I can't confirm a visible effect from the truncated source.
- The `@font-face` rules load web fonts from both `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`. Some declarations use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). Most clients ignore web fonts, so the fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) apply.
- Viewport is set to `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility concern.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` appears in the delivered HTML. It is a leaked template variable that was not resolved at send time. It probably belongs to a reviews or product-recommendation block. Depending on where it sits, it may show as visible text or as a broken element.
- No other merge-token problems were flagged.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- The `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm the sender omitted them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so this needs checking against the raw message.
- The `Authentication-Results` header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- The truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the physical postal address or the visible unsubscribe link.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All 70 links were skipped as tracking redirects. I can't verify UTM parameters or landing pages from the QA data. The `From` domain `mail.cb2.com` and the asset hosts `cb2.com` are consistent with the brand.
- The Dotomi retargeting pixels indicate cross-site tracking. The email doesn't show a mismatch with the site.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge token first. Either resolve it at send time or add a fallback that removes the block when there is no data.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 11 content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
3. Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the ones that don't fire in secure contexts.
4. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC results, outside the relay. Until then, treat compliance as unverified.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag to allow zoom.
6. Extend QA to resolve the tracked links and check their UTM parameters and landing pages.
