# So many pillows & throws on sale for Black Friday
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's Black Friday template with a pillows-and-throws hero. The image is good: a plush camel faux-fur throw on a sofa, with "UP TO 60% OFF" and "PILLOWS & THROWS" stacked above it. The SHOP THE SALE button is a white block and easy to see. The category tiles below (furniture, lighting, rugs and so on) turn it back into a general sale.
- It repeats the pattern from the 11-25 and 11-26 sends. On 11-25, "UP TO 60% OFF LIGHTING" worked because the hero, the subject and a priced product grid all agreed. Here the hero is category-specific but the rest of the email is generic. There are no products, no prices and no pillow or throw detail anywhere. "So many…" is the same phrasing as the 11-26 "So many best sellers on sale" send, which makes the cadence feel automated.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send that stays category-specific only in the hero. For a subscriber getting a near-daily "up to 60% off", it needs priced products to stand out.

## What's working

- The hero is strong: "UP TO 60% OFF" over a tactile throw image makes the offer and category clear immediately.
- The "(IT ALL SHIPS FREE)" line under the category name gives a second reason to act.
- The "EXTENDED STORE HOURS / FRIDAY 11/27: 9AM–9PM" banner adds real-world urgency for people near a store.
- The layout is clean with no visible broken images, overlaps or gaps, and the black buttons contrast well on both light and dark sections.
- Loyalty perks (10% back in Rewards, app, 15% off for texts) are present, though low in the email.

## What's weak

- There are no priced products or named SKUs. "Pillows & throws" is promised in the subject and hero, but the body never shows a single pillow or throw.
- The shipping messaging contradicts itself. The hero says "IT ALL SHIPS FREE" while the mid-email banner says "FREE SHIPPING ON ORDERS OF $99 OR MORE."
- Both CTAs, "SHOP THE SALE" and "SHOP NOW", are generic, and the category tiles have no CTA copy beyond a label.
- The "SAVE 15% OFF in the new year" block is confusing next to a 60% sale. It reads like a second, unrelated offer.
- The fine print is a dense, tiny wall of text with several asterisked exclusions. It undercuts the "up to 60%" claim.
- This is the same template as the 11-26 sends, with only the hero swapped.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic category grid with a 2x3 grid of actual pillows and throws, each with a was/now price and a "Shop the throw" link. This is the single change that would lift clicks most.
- 2. Fix the shipping contradiction. Pick one threshold ("Free shipping on everything" or "$99+") and use it everywhere.
- 3. Make the CTA specific: "Shop pillows & throws" instead of "SHOP THE SALE".
- 4. Give the urgency a real deadline, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm", rather than only store hours.
- 5. Move the "15% off in the new year" block out or into the footer, so it doesn't compete with the main offer.
- 6. Show one star rating or a "best seller" tag on a hero product for social proof.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is a templated XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with `lang="en"` and VML/MSO fallbacks. It has two confirmed code-level problems: 12 content images have no alt text, and 11 Dotomi pixels load over `http://`. The QA pass rate is 57% (0 errors, 3 warnings). Compliance and authentication could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure third-party pixels:** 11 Dotomi requests use plain `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. They can be blocked as mixed content, and they leak the recipient's request metadata in cleartext. Ten of them are cookie-sync redirects, which is a third-party ad-tech payload with no user-facing function.
- **Unverified links:** 62 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none were HTTP-probed. Redirect health and destination validity are unconfirmed.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/d1879d49-…?mi_cid=…` are first-party and use HTTPS. Both have no alt text, which is minor for 1×1 pixels (see Section 3).

1. Switch all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or remove the 10 `pub*.php` cookie-sync pixels unless they are needed. Also add `alt=""` to them.

4. Inspect the raw message headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass.
