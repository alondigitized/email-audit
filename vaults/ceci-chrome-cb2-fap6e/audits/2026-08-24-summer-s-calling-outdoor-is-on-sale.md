# Summer’s calling. Outdoor is on sale.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 finally running a coherent, category-clean send: "The Outdoor Collection / Up to 50% Off" as the hero, then a genuinely useful navigational scroll — Outdoor Lounge, Outdoor Dining, Outdoor Planters, Outdoor Decor, each with its own lifestyle shot and "Shop Now" button. It's the most organized structure this brand has shown all week, a real departure from the sale/celebrity/floor-sample grab-bag of 2026-08-20 through 2026-08-23.
- But it undercuts itself twice. First, it buries a second, bigger promotion — "EARLY ACCESS, ONLY ON THE APP / THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" — two-thirds of the way down, after the reader has already scrolled through four outdoor sub-categories. That's the same app-gating hook from yesterday's 2026-08-24 "APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK AT SALE" send (7.00/10), but here it's demoted to an afterthought rather than the lead. Second, the email is extremely long for what is, subject-wise, a single-category promo — five hero-style image blocks before you even hit "More For You" and the footer modules.
- Net: strong category focus, weak prioritization. If outdoor is the offer, lead harder with it and cut the length; if Labor Day access is actually the bigger lever, this email buried its own lede.

## What's working

- Clear single-category hero: "THE OUTDOOR COLLECTION / UP TO 50% OFF" matches the subject exactly — no bait-and-switch.
- Consistent sub-navigation structure (Lounge, Dining, Planters, Decor) each with its own photo and CTA — genuinely useful for a reader shopping outdoor furniture.
- Clean, warm, sunlit outdoor photography throughout — on-brand, on-season, no render bugs or broken images.
- "SHOP THE OUTDOOR SALE" black button gives a clear final CTA before the app-exclusive section starts.
- "More For You" personalized product grid with visible prices adds a second path to purchase beyond the hero offer.

## What's weak

- The email is very long (five full hero-image sections before the product grid) for a single-category send — most subscribers won't scroll far enough to see the Labor Day teaser or the product grid at all.
- The Labor Day Sale block — same "up to 50% off," app-only — is visually secondary despite likely being the bigger/more urgent offer, undercutting yesterday's app-only push instead of building on it.
- No pricing or specific discount tiers shown until the "More For You" grid at the very bottom; the top 80% of the email is percent-off copy with no dollar anchor.
- No urgency or end-date anywhere in the outdoor sale copy — reader has no reason to act today versus next week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the outdoor section from four sub-category blocks to two (combine Lounge+Dining, Planters+Decor, or run them as a single scrollable carousel) — this alone would roughly halve email length and get more people to the fold with the actual CTA.
- 2. Move the Labor Day app-exclusive banner up, either as a top banner above the outdoor hero or immediately below it — if it's the higher-value/urgency offer, don't make people scroll past four furniture categories to find it.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the outdoor sale ("Ends Monday," "This week only") — right now nothing in the top 4/5 of the email signals urgency.
- 4. Surface at least one price point in the hero or first sub-section (e.g., "Lounge sets from $XXX") so the offer feels concrete before the reader hits the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Outdoor sale: up to 50% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your patio, half off — shop outdoor now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Lounge, dining, planters & decor — all on sale this week.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: early access to the Labor Day Sale, only on the app.`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
CB2's HTML uses an MI/ExactTarget-based ESP template with standard MSO/media-query boilerplate. Automated QA found no broken links but flagged missing alt text, insecure (http://) tracking pixels, and no visible unsubscribe/authentication headers in the captured payload.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 63 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing (redirect domains, not independently verifiable) — no confirmed breakage, but unverified.
- Multiple http:// (non-HTTPS) tracking pixels from dotomi.com: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`, and `pub1.php` through `pub10.php` at `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/`. Non-HTTPS resources risk being blocked by mail clients enforcing mixed-content/TLS policies, potentially causing tracking data loss.
- Secondary tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are served over HTTPS — no issue.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 7 content images missing `alt` text (UUID-named .jpg assets served from `image.mail.cb2.com`), plus the dotomi tracking pixels also lack alt attributes. Missing alt text on content images degrades screen-reader accessibility and shows blank space if images are blocked.
- Template includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments, `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets, and responsive media queries (`max-width: 418px`, `min-width: 690px`/`768px`) — no structural rendering issues detected in the visible source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- List-Unsubscribe header not found — may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than an actual absence; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from this data.
- List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) header not found — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- Authentication-Results header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — sender authentication status unknown from this capture.
- These are relay-capture warnings, not confirmed violations; flagging as unverified rather than as defects.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot assess — the 63 tracking/redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so destination URLs and UTM parameters were not resolved or captured in the provided data. No landing page URLs available for comparison.
