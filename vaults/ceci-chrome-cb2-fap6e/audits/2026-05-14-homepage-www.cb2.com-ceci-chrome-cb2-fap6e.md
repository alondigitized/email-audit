# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- CB2 right now is full-on Memorial Day mode — big sale energy, desert botanical hero, and an immediate 15%-off pop-up trying to capture my contact info before I've even seen the room. The brand's aesthetic is very much my frequency: modern, a little sculptural, zero farmhouse vibes. But the modal lands first and hard, which is aggressively transactional for a brand that usually leads with cool-factor.

## What's working

- **The hero aesthetic:** Desert botanical background with palm-shadow light and warm tones — it reads editorial, not discount-bin. I actually want to see what's behind the modal.
- **"DOUBLE REWARDS 12-5%" banner:** The loyalty strip at the top is a genuine hook. I have a CB2 account and rewards points, so this matters to me immediately.
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" editorial module:** Even at the bottom of the viewport, I can see it's doing something more interesting than a standard grid — stylish people, lifestyle context, very social-native.
- **Memorial Day Sale timing:** The campaign is current. No stale "holiday" language in May. They know what week it is.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the first thing I see — period.** Email AND phone number capture before I've scrolled once. The "CONTINUE WITHOUT SAVING" link is intentionally low-contrast gray; that's a dark pattern and I notice it.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF" with an asterisk modal fine print:** The print in the pop-up is genuinely tiny and mentions SMS recurring messages, carrier rates, opt-out terms. That's a lot to front-load for a 15% discount that only applies to full-price items during a sale.
- **Zero product specificity above the fold:** I can't see a single product name, price, or collection. The hero is pure brand vibes + sale flag, which is beautiful but doesn't anchor a purchase intent.
- **No free-shipping callout:** I would normally need to know the threshold before I go deep into a cart. Not visible anywhere in this view.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the dual-field (email + phone) modal on first visit; replace with a single-field email capture with a timed delay (30 sec in).** The phone ask on landing is too much friction — it's the main reason I'd bounce immediately.
- **Drop a product spotlight strip below the hero** — two or three "Sale favorites" tiles with actual prices crossed out and sale prices visible. Give me something to tap, not just a vibe.
- **Add a visible free-shipping threshold** in the top banner strip alongside the Double Rewards message. Even "Free shipping over $X" in small type earns a click.
- **Sharpen the "SHOP THE SALE" CTA** — make it visually weightier. Right now it sits small beneath enormous "SALE" text. Button contrast is competing with the modal.
