# Unique birthday gifts starting at $20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Unique birthday gifts starting at $20," but the email opens with a banner for the Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event (up to 50% off). That event is a different campaign, and it sits above the actual hero, "the birthday shop." The email then repeats the event as a mid-email banner and again through the "we love these for you" strollers, cribs and dressers. A reader who opened for $20 gifts gets sold furniture instead.
- The birthday body itself is well built. It is organized by age (kids, toddlers, babies), each block has a lifestyle image, a product row and its own "Shop ... Gifts" button, and a "pick a color or style" block covers personalization. The problem is that the $20 price anchor from the subject is not legible anywhere I can read in the render. I can't confirm a "from $20" callout, and the product tiles show names but no prices that I can make out. The email is also very long, about 8,800px, with the card promo, app download, TheDesignDesk and store locator all after the main content.
- Judgement: solid structure and a good subject, undermined by a competing promotion and the missing price proof. It is a middling send that could be a strong one.

## What's working

- The subject is concrete and gives a price floor ($20) that sets an expectation for accessible gifts.
- The age-based sections (kids, toddlers, babies) make browsing easy for a gift-giver.
- Each section has its own specific CTA ("Shop Kids Gifts," "Shop Toddler Gifts," "Shop Baby Gifts"), so each click goes to a relevant page.
- The photography is warm and consistent, and the brand voice is clearly Crate & Barrel Kids.
- The "pick a color or style and personalize it" block adds a differentiated angle for gifting.
- I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The Bedroom & Nursery Event banner outranks the birthday hero, and the email repeats it mid-body, so the message is split.
- The $20 price point from the subject is not reinforced anywhere I can read. There is no "from $20" badge in the hero and no visible prices on the product tiles.
- Strollers, cribs and dressers in "we love these for you" are off-theme for birthday gifts and push the cheapest price point far from the $20 promise.
- The email is too long. The credit card, app, design service and store locator modules come after the point of decision and dilute the focus.
- There is no urgency or deadline for gift-giving, such as a ship-by date for birthdays.
- This is the sixth Crate send in about two days, and it follows the Bodie collection email, which carried the same Bedroom & Nursery banner. The cadence is heavy and the banner is repeating.

## Recommendations

- 1. Remove the Bedroom & Nursery banner from the top of this send, or move it to a footer strip. Make "the birthday shop" the first thing the eye lands on, and add a "Gifts from $20" badge to the hero.
- 2. Put visible prices on every product tile, and add a clearly labeled "Under $50" or "From $20" row, so the subject's promise is proven in the body.
- 3. Replace the strollers and cribs block with birthday-relevant bestsellers by price tier ($20, $50, $100).
- 4. Cut the card, app, TheDesignDesk and store locator modules from this send, or consolidate them into one slim footer row.
- 5. Add credible urgency, such as "Order by [date] for birthday delivery."
- 6. Space out sends. The Oct 4 and Oct 5 volume from Crate & Barrel Kids is high enough to hurt open rates.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a template-built XHTML 1.0 Transitional email. The QA run (57% pass, 0 errors, 3 warnings) found no hard failures. The main confirmed issues are about 24 content images with no `alt` attribute and viewport/text-size settings that block user scaling. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified, because the relay did not capture those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **77 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and dead links are unverified.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** There are 13 beacons: 6× `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`, 5× `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5`, `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/112ad0df-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Decorative or tracking pixels should carry `alt=""`.
- **Third-party pixel.** `sr.rlcdn.com` is a third-party domain, and its `s=` parameter is a per-recipient hash. The same `mi_u` hash appears in the first-party beacon URLs. I can't tell from the truncated source whether this is disclosed, so I'm only noting it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 24 content images.** All are hosted on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…`. Examples are `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` and `790368cc-c1c9-4aa1-8bce-bd4229257202.gif`. If these are linked or carry text, screen readers and image-blocking clients get no content. The email's `<title>` is just "Crate & Barrel".
- **Zoom and text-resize blocked.** The head contains `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. It also sets `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`. Both prevent user scaling and fail WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Template hygiene.**
  - A leftover placeholder comment remains: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - The `<title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
  - `<meta charset>` and `format-detection` are declared redundantly.
  - `.ReadMsgBody` is defined twice.
- **Conflicting `img` max-width rules.** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. The later rule wins at ≥768px, so the 600px cap is dead code.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion provided.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender and image domains (`mail.crateandbarrel.com`, `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`) are aligned, which is a positive sign but not proof.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address.** I can't confirm either in the truncated HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No confirmable issues. All links go through tracking redirects that weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 24 content images, and `alt=""` to the 13 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` to allow zoom and text scaling.
3. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` source, or fetch headers directly, to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
4. Resolve a sample of the 77 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to check destinations and UTM carry-through.
5. Clean up the template: remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment, escape `&` in `<title>`, and dedupe the CSS and meta tags.
6. Set `<title>` to the subject line or a descriptive value.
