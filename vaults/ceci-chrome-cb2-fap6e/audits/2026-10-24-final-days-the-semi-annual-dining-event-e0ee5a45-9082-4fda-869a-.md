# FINAL DAYS: The Semi-Annual Dining Event
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, price-led send, but the subject and the creative disagree. The subject says "FINAL DAYS", while the hero banner says "ENDS SOON". The offer itself is clear: "UP TO 30% OFF" in large white type over a candlelit dining table, with a single "SHOP THE SALE" button. The image is warm and on-brand, and the eye lands on the offer first.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. There is a dining category grid, a second "SHOP THE DINING EVENT" button, a "WAREHOUSE SALE UP TO 60% OFF" block, a rewards-card section and an app/gifting/text-signup strip. The "UP TO 60% OFF" block competes with the 30% hero, and CB2 has sent that Warehouse Sale offer repeatedly (10-20, 10-22, 10-23). It's also the second dining-event send in four days, after "Up to 30% off things for hosting" on 10-21, which had a "ONE WEEK LEFT" banner. A Thanksgiving delivery banner at the very top adds a third message above the hero.
- Judgement: a solid deadline reminder with a credible offer, weakened by stacked competing promos, no stated end date in the visible copy, and a long footer-style tail. It's a 6, in line with the other recent CB2 sends.

## What's working

- "UP TO 30% OFF" is the largest element in the hero, and the offer reads in about a second.
- The "ENDS SOON" bar in olive above the hero adds deadline framing, and it continues the "ONE WEEK LEFT" beat from 10-21.
- The dining photography is warm and on-brand, and white type stays legible over the dark image.
- The category grid (Dining Tables, Dining Chairs, Entertaining, Dining Storage) gives clear product paths.
- The Thanksgiving delivery banner ("Order in-stock furniture by 11/17") gives a concrete, seasonal reason to buy now.

## What's weak

- The subject says "FINAL DAYS" but the hero says "ENDS SOON", and no specific end date is visible in the body copy.
- The "WAREHOUSE SALE UP TO 60% OFF" block directly undercuts the 30% hero, and a shopper is left unsure which sale to chase.
- The email carries too many modules: delivery banner, hero, grid, warehouse sale, rewards card, app, gifting and text signup. The primary CTA gets diluted.
- The category tiles are labeled with small text sitting on the photos, such as "DINING STORAGE" on a busy image, and are hard to read.
- Both dining CTAs use generic "SHOP THE SALE" copy and send people to a whole event, not to a specific product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Warehouse Sale block and the rewards/app strips from this send, or move them below a clear dining-only section. One offer per email.
- 2. Put a real end date in the hero ("Ends Sunday 10/25, 11:59 PM PT") and match the subject to the same urgency wording.
- 3. Feature specific dining products with prices in place of generic category tiles.
- 4. Make the tile labels larger, or put them on solid bars.
- 5. Change the CTA to something like "SHOP DINING TABLES".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Dining sale ends Sunday: 30% off tables & chairs`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 30% off dining, ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, chairs and storage, plus Thanksgiving delivery on in-stock pieces ordered by 11/17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save up to 30% before the Semi-Annual Dining Event ends. Order by 11/17 for Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout with VML/MSO fallbacks and a media-query mobile breakpoint. The QA run found no hard failures (57% pass, 0 issues, 3 warnings). The problems are about 26 images missing alt text, 11 insecure `http://` tracking pixels, and unverifiable compliance headers.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 61 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified.
- Eleven `http://` (non-HTTPS) pixels point at Dotomi (a third-party ad-retargeting vendor):
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`, and `pub2.php` through `pub10.php` with the same query string.
- Clients that block mixed or insecure content may suppress these pixels, so retargeting and cookie-sync data will be lost.
- Ten cookie-redirect pixels (`pub1.php` to `pub10.php`) are a heavy third-party sync chain for one email.
- The first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/2e3187f6-b388-4156-bba2-0a9f914ae36e?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS. The `mi_u` value looks like a hashed identifier (64 hex characters) embedded in the URL.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Alt text is missing on 9 content images, all hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `2a60c92d-bbed-4093-b43c-6e5a1c6a0105.jpg`
  - `9ee5e48e-9679-4d01-a6a1-43494ca17a4e.jpg`
  - `d08dfbfc-19a5-42d5-9d17-cd5911d8923a.jpg`
  - `b1a59227-d27d-41e9-b271-87f5aacdb00c.jpg`
  - `03a78555-a2d1-462d-8e9c-d49ac82ed0a0.jpg`
  - `3db8ae52-8139-4e6b-a882-5b79adf39510.jpg`
  - `9274975c-c8a6-47b6-b8d7-13a0a547c1af.jpg`
  - `8436ffd4-f83c-4d1b-83e1-4a1d789552c3.jpg`
  - `d4ac8a16-d60c-4804-8d40-f28eaf024dbf.jpg`
- With images off or a screen reader, these images carry no text equivalent. If they hold the offer or CTA content, that content is lost.
- Alt text is also missing on all tracking pixels (Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`). These are decorative, so use `alt=""`, and ideally add `width="1" height="1"` and `aria-hidden="true"`.
- The `<title>` is the generic "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not tied to the campaign.
- A placeholder comment is left in production: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- `@font-face` rules load fonts from `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`) and `cb2.com`:
  - Some URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/...`).
  - The `Lineto Brown Sub` regular face has no `format()` on its first `src`.
  - Web fonts are unsupported in many clients, so fallbacks matter. `a` uses `'Lineto Brown Light Sub', Helvetica, sans-serif`, which is acceptable.
- The viewport meta sets `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom and is an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- `*{... line-height:100%}` and `p{padding:0 !important; margin:0 !important}` are broad resets. They are not defects, but they can override intended spacing.
- The HTML source is truncated, so I can't confirm the footer, the preheader or the role/lang attributes on tables.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the supplied source or QA output. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found. The QA note says the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect**. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (5,000 or more messages a day), so it should be checked against raw headers.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` matches the From address `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, which is a good sign but not proof.
- The body footer (physical address, visible unsubscribe link) is not in the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be assessed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Landing URLs can't be evaluated. All 61 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs.
- No UTM parameters are visible in the supplied data.
- The only visible campaign identifiers are the vendor parameters `mi_cid`, `mi_mid` and `dtm_cid=2437`. Whether they carry through to the landing page is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images listed above. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS (`https://login.dotomi.com/...`, `https://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/...`) or drop them. Consider consolidating or removing the 10-hop `pub1`–`pub10` sync chain.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
4. Check the raw headers in a seed inbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. Don't treat them as missing until then.
5. Resolve the 61 tracked links through the ESP's redirect to confirm that final URLs return 200 and carry the expected UTMs.
6. Set a campaign-specific `<title>` and remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
7. Tighten the QA harness: capture full headers and follow redirect chains so the compliance and continuity checks aren't left unconfirmed.
