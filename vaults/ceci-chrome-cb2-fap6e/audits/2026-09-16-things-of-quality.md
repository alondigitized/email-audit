# THINGS OF QUALITY
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's lookbook engine running the same play again: "JUST IN, ALREADY TRENDING" as the hero hook, then a scroll of six near-identical product vignettes (Meda chair, Onyx table, Rich Oak console, Denim Blue velvet chair, Aldine sofa) each with a designer credit line. The one actual offer — "EVERYDAY FALL SALE, UP TO 30% OFF" — is a thin banner stripe above the hero, easy to miss if you're skimming, and it's not tied to any specific product below it.
- Against the brand history here, this is a step down even from "This sofa could be vintage + Up to 30% off for inside season" (2026-09-13), which at least blended the sale banner into the layout. This send buries it further — one banner, then seven straight screens of pure aspirational styling with no pricing until the "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the very bottom, which is the first place actual prices appear.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand — moody, warm-toned photography, consistent typography — but as a commerce email it's asking a lot of patience for very little payoff. A subscriber opening this for the sale gets six chairs and consoles before seeing a dollar figure.

## What's working

- Sale banner ("UP TO 30% OFF") is present at the very top, immediately visible without scrolling.
- Photography is cohesive and premium-feeling — consistent warm/moody styling across all vignettes reinforces brand identity.
- Designer attribution lines (Gianfranco Frattini, Studio Anansi, VUUE, David Girelli) add a credibility/curated signal for a design-conscious shopper.
- "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom finally surfaces real prices ($1199, $349, $199, $699, $299, $999), which is useful even if it's buried.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- The 30% off banner is disconnected from the products below it — none of the six hero vignettes show a discounted price or "30% off" tag.
- Seven consecutive full-width product vignettes before any pricing is a lot of scroll for a promotional email; this reads as a catalog, not a sale email.
- "JUST IN ALREADY TRENDING" is a confusing, slightly garbled hero line — reads like two headlines mashed together.
- Every CTA is the generic "SHOP NOW," repeated seven times — no differentiation or urgency language anywhere in the body.
- Same beat CB2 has hit five times this week (lookbook-first, sale-second) — cadence fatigue is real at this point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a price or "up to 30% off" tag directly on at least the first 2-3 hero vignettes — right now the discount and the product story never touch.
- 2. Cut the vignette count from six to three; move the rest to a "see more" link or a second email. Six nearly-identical treatments dilutes the primary sale message.
- 3. Fix the hero headline — "JUST IN ALREADY TRENDING" needs a divider (period, line break, or "/") so it reads as two distinct callouts, not one run-on phrase.
- 4. Vary CTA copy at least once — swap one or two "SHOP NOW" instances for "SHOP THE SALE" to reinforce the discount is live.
- 5. Move the "MORE FOR YOU" priced grid higher, right under the hero — it's the only place price-conscious shoppers get satisfied, and it's currently the last thing before the footer.
