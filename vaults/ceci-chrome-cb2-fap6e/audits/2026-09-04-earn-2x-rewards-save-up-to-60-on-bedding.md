# Earn 2x Rewards & save up to 60% on bedding →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids leaning specifically into bedding this time — the hero is a clean "up to 60% off bedding + ships free" with a cozy lifestyle shot, then it drills straight into a kids bed with matching quilt/sham product shots, followed by a nursery/baby bedding section with its own set of SKUs. That's a real improvement in focus over the kitchen-sink Labor Day template this brand has been running all week — this send actually has a category thesis (bedding) instead of trying to cram sofas, rugs, decor, and outdoor furniture into one email.
- That said, it's still a long scroll: bedding hero → kids bedding grid → baby bedding grid → Jellycat restock → Double Rewards reminder → Design Desk sweepstakes → "we love these for you" bedding again → credit card pitch → app download → footer. The Double Rewards banner appears twice (once inline near top, once as its own full module), which feels like padding rather than reinforcement.
- Verdict: better than the last several sends from this brand family because it's actually bedding-focused with real product/pricing visible, but it's diluted by a lot of unrelated modules stacked below the fold. Solid, not exceptional.

## What's working

- Clear, single-category hero — "up to 60% off bedding + ships free" is legible immediately, no scroll needed.
- Real SKUs with real prices and sale/reg pricing shown for both kids and baby bedding grids — this isn't vague "shop now" copy.
- Section headers ("The Labor Day Event," baby nursery module) create visual breaks so the long email doesn't feel like one undifferentiated wall.
- Jellycat "back in stock" module is a smart insert — plush toy restocks are a strong click driver for this demographic independent of the bedding sale.
- No visible render bugs — images load cleanly, text isn't overlapping, layout holds together.

## What's weak

- Double Rewards messaging is repeated twice (top banner + standalone "Limited Time Offer" module) — redundant real estate that could've been one product row instead.
- The email is very long with several off-topic modules (Design Desk sweepstakes, credit card pitch, app download) that dilute the bedding focus established in the hero.
- "we love these for you" personalization module reuses bedding products already seen above rather than surfacing genuinely different picks — reads as filler, not personalization.
- No visible countdown or urgency cue beyond "Now–September 8, 2026" text — easy to miss, no visual urgency treatment (no clock/banner styling).

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the redundant Double Rewards module — keep the top banner mention only, and use the freed space for another bedding cross-sell (bath, decor-for-nursery) to stay on-theme.
- 2. Trim or move the Design Desk sweepstakes and credit-card pitch below the fold/into a separate send — they compete with the bedding offer for attention in an already-long email.
- 3. Make the September 8 deadline visually louder (badge or countdown treatment near the hero CTA) since it's currently just small text.
- 4. Make the "we love these for you" module genuinely personalized (different SKUs, not repeats) or cut it.

- 6. **Subject Alt B:** `Kids & baby bedding, up to 60% off (ends 9/8)`
- 7. **Preheader Alt A:** `New quilts, shams & crib bedding — plus Double Rewards through 9/8`
