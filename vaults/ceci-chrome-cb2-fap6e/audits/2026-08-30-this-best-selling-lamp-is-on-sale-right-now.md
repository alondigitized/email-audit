# This best-selling lamp is on sale right now
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises one specific "best-selling lamp," but the email delivers a generic Labor Day category sale — the hero says "UP TO 50% OFF LIGHTING," not a single product, and there's no callout anywhere naming a "best-seller." That's a subject/hero mismatch, and it's a step backward from CB2's 2026-08-28 "OUTDOOR ON SALE" send, which nailed subject-to-hero alignment.
- Below the fold it's the now-familiar CB2 Labor Day template: category grid, credit-card double-rewards pitch, 800+ new arrivals banner, then finally a "MORE FOR YOU" grid of six lamps with prices — which is actually the most relevant, high-intent content in the whole email, buried near the bottom.
- This reads as a lamp-flavored repackaging of the same sale email CB2 has sent five times this week. The specific-sounding subject is bait for an otherwise undifferentiated blast.

## What's working

- Hero image is clean and well-lit, clear "SHOP THE SALE" CTA button.
- "MORE FOR YOU" lamp grid shows real product shots, names, and struck-through sale pricing (e.g., Raw Marble Table Lamp $198.00 → $158.00).
- No render bugs — no overlapping text, broken images, or placeholder content.
- Consistent CB2 visual identity (typography, warm earth-tone palette) across sections.

## What's weak

- Subject promises a single "best-selling lamp"; hero delivers a category-wide "UP TO 50% OFF LIGHTING" sale with no named hero product.
- The actual lamp products are pushed to the very bottom of a long scroll, behind furniture, rugs, rewards-card pitch, and a new-arrivals banner.
- No single lamp is highlighted as the "best-seller" the subject referenced — six lamps are shown with equal weight, so the subject's specificity is unearned.
- Same structural template as recent CB2 Labor Day sends (category grid → rewards card → new arrivals → product grid), so it reads as reused creative with a new subject slapped on.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero to match the subject: feature the actual best-selling lamp by name and image at the top, with its specific sale price, instead of a generic "LIGHTING" category banner.
- 2. Move the "MORE FOR YOU" lamp grid up to right after the hero — it's the most relevant content and currently requires scrolling past four other sections to reach.
- 3. Add a "best-seller" badge or ranking (e.g., "#1 best-seller") on whichever lamp the subject refers to, so the claim is visibly substantiated.
- 4. Trim the template — the rewards-card and new-arrivals blocks dilute a supposedly lamp-focused send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Our #1 best-selling lamp is 30% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `The lamp everyone's buying — now on sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus more lighting up to 50% off through Sept 9`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `See why this one sells out every season`
