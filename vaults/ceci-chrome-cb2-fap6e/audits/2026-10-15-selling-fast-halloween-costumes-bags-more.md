## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link destinations are unverified.** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 96 tracking and click-redirect links, so I can't confirm any of them resolve. This reflects what the audit could check, not a defect in the email.
- **Per-recipient identifiers are in image URLs.** The `mi_u=56aaa278…` parameter appears on `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6 times) and `…/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/aed9429b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` carries campaign and message IDs.
- **Third-party pixels.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` is five LiveRamp pixels. The `s=` value is a 64-hex string, which is consistent with a hashed identifier. I can't confirm what it hashes.
- **Duplicate pixel.** The same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` is requested 6 times, which is redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on about 60 images.** The QA check flags every `<img>` it found without alt text. Most are content images from `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg`, `.gif` and `.png`, for example `6cda691d-b295-47d6-aa7c-8c52d1eec48a.jpg` and `b8e4ab5a-65be-4620-a4ff-f1ab08aedc11.gif`. Scene7 assets are also affected: `20260916_CBK_HalloweenLaunch_Teriary_Socia…`, `2026_0926_CBK_Clearance_SecondaryStory.gif` and several `25_MI_Bottom_Spacer_40px_White` spacers. With images blocked, these render as blank space. The tracking pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""` instead of omitting the attribute. I can't tell from QA whether any of these already use an explicit empty alt.
- **Pinch-zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` blocks zoom on mobile, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing blocked.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text scaling.
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML doctype.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand, and `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed.
- **Redundant meta tags.** There are three overlapping `format-detection` tags. The first already sets all five values. The later `date=no`, `address=no` and `telephone=no` tags duplicate it, and a second charset declaration is also present.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head, and `<title>` is a generic brand string.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` to every `<img>`: descriptive text for hero and product images, and `alt=""` for spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from the `*` and `body` rules.
3. Check the raw `.eml` headers directly (not via the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. Resolve a sample of the redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to check the final URLs and UTM parameters.
5. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel` and self-close the meta tags. Clean up the duplicate meta tags and the template placeholder comment.
6. Review whether sharing per-recipient identifiers with `rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy disclosures.
7. Capture the full HTML so the footer and body links can be audited.
