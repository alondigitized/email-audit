## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard XHTML table layout with a responsive `@media` block. The QA run turned up 27 content images with no alt text, some minor markup and accessibility defects, and no visible compliance headers. Header and link findings are unverified because of the AgentMail relay and click-redirect wrapping. The pass rate was 57%, with 0 hard failures and 3 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverifiable links:** 80 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unconfirmed.
- **Third-party pixel:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` is five calls to one third-party (LiveRamp) sync endpoint. Each call carries the same `mi_u`-style hash that appears in the first-party pixels.
- **Redundant first-party pixels:**
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` is requested six times.
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8d30676d-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are further tracking beacons.
  - These add request overhead. Many clients also block them.
- **Truncated URLs:** Some QA image URLs are cut off, for example `mi_u=…bb941bb640686964` and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com` URL. This looks like a QA-output limit, not a defect in the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (27 content images):**
  - 21 are on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…`. For example, `c383e650-c791-4494-9f8e-eef20a585e2d.jpg`, `ee3618d2-…gif` and `971aa10f-…jpg`.
  - Also affected:
    - `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`
    - `2026%5F0926%5FCBK%5FClearance%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
    - `2026%5F0710%5FDTP%5FKids%5FStorybookLookbook%5FSec…`
  - With images off or a screen reader, the content is inaccessible. Several of these are GIFs, which suggests animated content with no text fallback.
- **Missing alt text (13 tracking pixels):** These are the `mi.crateandbarrel.com` (6), `rlcdn.com` (5), `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` beacons. Screen readers will announce the filename or URL. They should carry `alt=""` and ideally `role="presentation"` or `aria-hidden="true"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text-resize blocking:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` conflict with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`. The wildcard rule also forces `line-height:100%` on every element.
- **Invalid markup:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML 1.0 doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The head has a duplicate `<meta charset>` and a duplicate `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting values, and `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed.
- **Template residue:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (misspelled) and empty `<!----->` blocks are left in the shipped HTML.
- **Untested at desktop widths:** The desktop `@media (min-width:768px)` and `(min-width:640px)` rules set `img{max-width:600px/640px}` globally. This may clip full-width hero images, but I can't confirm that from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML has no unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FirstName]`. The source is truncated, so tokens further down the body are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither header was found. The QA tool notes the relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If they are really absent, the email misses RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unverifiable through the relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains, which is consistent with one ESP setup, but alignment isn't proven.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and postal address:** The truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 80 links are click-redirect wrapped and unprobed, so I can't confirm UTM parameters or landing pages. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the `dv.` beacon suggest campaign and message IDs are tracked, but not through visible UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the 27 content images. Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` to the 13 tracking pixels.
2. **Viewport:** Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. **Markup:** Change the `<title>` to `Crate &amp; Barrel`, and remove the duplicate `charset`, `Content-Type` and `format-detection` metas and the leftover template comments.
4. **Tracking pixels:** Deduplicate the six `f3c0d22992a01946.png` requests and the five `448796.gif` calls if they aren't all needed.
5. **Headers:** Check the raw message at the source for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment. The relay masks them here.
6. **Tracking and continuity:** Resolve a sample of the redirect-wrapped links manually to confirm UTM tagging and landing pages.
