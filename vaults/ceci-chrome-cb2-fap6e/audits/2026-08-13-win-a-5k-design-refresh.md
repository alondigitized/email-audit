# Win a $5k design refresh
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's ninth creative mode this week, and easily its strongest: a design-services push wrapped around a genuine $5,000 sweepstakes hook — "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH" over a warm, styled living room hero. It's a real prize with real mechanics (meet with a designer Aug 13–Sept 13 for a chance to win) tied to a service CB2 actually wants to sell, which gives it more coherence than the sweepstakes-for-sweepstakes-sake feel of 8/9's "WIN A CUBS GAME" (7/10).
- Where it loses points is length and repetition of intent. The email runs a full "About CB2 Design Services" explainer, a second CTA, then pivots into six category tiles and eight product tiles that have nothing to do with the design-services offer — it's a sweepstakes email that turns into a catalog scroll. The design-services hook is the strongest single asset CB2 has used all week (a $5k concrete prize beats "prepare to want it all"-style vague new-arrivals copy), but the execution buries it under generic merchandising the same way most of this week's sends have.

## What's working

- Hero is unambiguous: "$5,000 REFRESH" in large type over a clean, aspirational living room shot — the offer is legible in under a second.
- Prize mechanic is spelled out immediately below the fold ("Meet with a designer August 13 – September 13 for the chance to win a CB2 gift card") — no digging required.
- Primary CTA "BOOK A FREE DESIGN APPOINTMENT" is a specific verb+noun action, repeated twice (top and mid-email) for anyone who scrolls past the hero.
- "ABOUT CB2 DESIGN SERVICES" section uses real service benefits (mood boards, color palettes, fabric swatches) rather than vague lifestyle copy.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, type is crisp, layout is consistent CB2 grid work.

## What's weak

- The email sprawls well past the offer: six room-category tiles and eight "More For You" product tiles (with prices) dilute a sweepstakes email into a full catalog send.
- No visible deadline/urgency treatment beyond a date range in body copy — nothing in the hero conveys "ends soon," so it reads more like an ongoing program than a limited window.
- Footer stacks two more offers (10% back Rewards Dollars, 15% off text sign-up) that compete with the $5k hook instead of reinforcing it.
- Design-appointment CTA and "shop the collection" tiles are asking for two very different actions in one email, splitting attention.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the second CTA. Kill the six category tiles and eight product tiles — if this is a sweepstakes/service send, let it stay one. Move merchandising to a separate send.
- 2. Add a visible countdown or "X days left" treatment near the hero — right now the Aug 13–Sept 13 window is buried in body text, not felt as urgency.
- 3. Move the gift-card prize value/detail (what exactly is won) higher and bolder — "a CB2 gift card" is vague relative to the "$5,000" headline; clarify if the $5k *is* the gift card value.
- 4. Drop or relocate the loyalty/text-signup footer offers to a follow-up email so they don't compete with the primary hook.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Book a design appointment, win $5,000`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your shot at a $5K room refresh`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free 1:1 design help, now with a $5,000 prize on the table.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Meet a CB2 designer by Sept 13 — you could win big.`
