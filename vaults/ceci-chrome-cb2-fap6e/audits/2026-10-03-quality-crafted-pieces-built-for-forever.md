## What's weak

- The email is extremely long, with about nine distinct modules, and the eye has no single place to land after the hero.
- The subject sells craftsmanship, but the email's actual driver is the sale. The offer is a thin strip at the top.
- The credit-card promo, app download and Design Desk appointment blocks at the bottom dilute the message.

- I can't see a deadline anywhere, so the "Semi-Annual Event" has no urgency.

- 3. Make the top banner larger. Add an end date so the event feels time-bounded.
