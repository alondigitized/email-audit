# Inside: The best latke recipe ever + more for Hanukkah
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a long, well-built seasonal catalog send. It is a Hanukkah gift and hosting guide with a menorah hero, then themed blocks for dinnerware, latke serveware, menorahs, linens, glasses and a Le Creuset launch. Each block has a lifestyle photo, product thumbnails with small labels and a "Shop ___" button. It looks like Crate & Barrel and is cleanly laid out.
- The problem is focus. The only price offer, "accent chairs & tables up to 30% off", sits in a thin strip above the hero and has nothing to do with Hanukkah. The subject leads with a latke recipe, but the recipe is a single mid-email block ("the very best latkes, ever" with a "Get the Recipe" button). The email tries to be editorial, a gift guide, a sale and an app and Design Desk promo, and it stops being a campaign.
- The render is a 680×12000 image shown downsized, so small text such as product names, prices and fine print was hard to read. These notes rest on the layout and the larger headlines. I saw no overlapping text or broken images. Against the other Crate & Barrel sends today ("New markdowns added! Up to 60% off", "Small nursery space? Let us help you for FREE"), this is the fourth send in one day. A reader will see it as noise, and the other sends have clearer hooks.

## What's working

- The hero, "exclusive designs for hanukkah", has a clear menorah image and a visible "Shop Now" button.
- The themed blocks ("for hanukkah nights & every night", "for your latke feast", "modern menorahs for eight luminous nights") are easy to scan and each has its own "Shop ___" button.
- The latke photo with a "Get the Recipe" button adds real editorial content that fits the season.
- The Le Creuset Château launch ("New + Exclusive") gives a premium product a full-width feature.
- "New & Exclusive" and "Bestseller" tags on the product thumbnails add some social proof and urgency.

## What's weak

- The 30% off banner is for accent chairs and tables, so it is off-theme and small. Nothing else in the email carries a price hook.
- The subject promises the latke recipe, but it is buried about two-thirds of the way down and has a small CTA.
- The email is very long, with 8+ blocks plus Design Desk, app download and footer promos, so the hero message gets diluted.
- It is the fourth Crate & Barrel send on Oct 29, after the markdown, clearance and nursery emails, so the cadence feels heavy.
- Generic Hanukkah blocks like "gifting inspo" lack a deadline, such as a shipping cutoff for the holiday.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one clear offer in the hero, for example a Hanukkah-specific discount or a gift-by shipping date, and drop the unrelated chair and table banner.
- 2. Move the latke recipe up directly under the hero, since it is what the subject promises. Make the "Get the Recipe" button larger.
- 3. Cut the email to 4–5 blocks (menorahs, hosting, recipe, gifting) and drop the app and Design Desk promos from this send.
- 4. Add a deadline, such as "order by Dec X for holiday delivery".
- 5. Space out sends so a subscriber doesn't get four emails in a day.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout from Movable Ink (`mi.crateandbarrel.com`) and has no blocking defects in the available source. The main issues are missing `alt` text on 50+ images, a viewport tag that blocks zoom, and header checks the QA tooling couldn't verify. The HTML is truncated, so only the `<head>` and QA output were reviewed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **87 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified. This is a tooling limit, not a confirmed defect.
- **Third-party pixels:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` calls (LiveRamp) plus `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/fcc9fd8f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. They are open/identity beacons with no `alt` and no sizing in the QA output.
- **Plain-text version:** 17,712 of 24,346 characters (about 73%) are URLs. This is above the 70% threshold and is a minor spam-filter signal, likely caused by long tracking URLs.
- **Per-recipient identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` appears in the `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `o.gif` URLs. Forwarded or archived copies will expose it.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 50+ images.** Most are content images under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (for example `caa9f5d4-…jpg`, `b75e25a2-…jpg`, `bbf6f9d4-…png`). Others are `s7d5.scene7.com/…LeCreuset_Chateau_Secondary` and seven dynamic `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. Empty `alt=""` is acceptable for the tracking pixels but not for content images. With images blocked, these render as blank, unlabeled blocks, and screen readers will announce filenames or URLs.
- **Zoom is disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">`. This blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Invalid markup under the XHTML 1.0 doctype:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is not self-closed and duplicates the `http-equiv` Content-Type.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`).
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It's harmless to render but shows an unfinished template, and the "IMPUT" typo is in the comment.
- **Title doesn't match the subject:** `<title>` is the generic "Crate & Barrel", not the subject line. This is minor.
- **Global resets:** `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` and `*{line-height:100%}` apply to every element, so any element without an explicit override inherits them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) are visible in the available source. The truncated body wasn't checked beyond the `<head>`. Dynamic content is delivered through Movable Ink (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), which can't be verified from a static render.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes AgentMail may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender rules require both headers, so check the raw message.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrel@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain. Verify alignment from the original message headers.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not in the truncated source, so unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. Links go through click-redirect domains that weren't probed (87 skipped), and no `utm_*` parameters are visible in the truncated source. The final landing URLs, UTM presence and parity with the subject ("latke recipe… Hanukkah") need a redirect-resolving crawl.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images and `alt=""` to tracking pixels (`rlcdn`, `o.gif`, `dv`). This is the largest finding.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`, self-close `<meta>` tags, and merge the three `format-detection` metas into one. Remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw message headers. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
5. Resolve the 87 tracking redirects with a follow-redirects crawl to check final URLs, UTM parameters and status codes.
6. Give the plain-text part more human-readable copy, or shorten its URLs, to get below 70% URL content.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, body links and merge tokens.
