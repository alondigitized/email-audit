# WHY WAIT FOR BLACK FRIDAY?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "Early Black Friday Sale" shell CB2 has sent all week, now under a subject that asks why you'd wait. The top is a "Limited Time: Now up to 70% Off Clearance" banner with a "SHOP NOW" button. Below it is a big dark-plum hero with "THE EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE, UP TO 60% OFF" and five stacked category buttons. The offer is clear, but the creative is a near-duplicate of the Nov 12 "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW + 2X REWARDS" and Nov 14 "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" sends.
- The lower half has little to do with the subject. It carries a Casa Dean by Swizz Beatz sweepstakes ("Win the new collection", $5,000 in gift cards) and a Double Rewards block ("Now through Nov 17th"). The rewards block shows a struck-through "10%" replaced by "20%", which reads as a messy correction. The email does not match the subject's "why wait" framing.
- Overall it is competent but fatigued. A subscriber who got the Nov 14 and Nov 12 versions has seen this hero three times. The subject is the freshest thing in it, and nothing below the fold makes the case for acting today.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" hero is large and gets noticed first, and the 70% clearance banner above it adds a second price hook.
- The five category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Shop the Sale) let the reader jump straight to a department.
- The Double Rewards block has a clear deadline ("Now through Nov 17th") and a "LEARN MORE" button.
- The Casa Dean image is the best photography in the email, with a warm, lifestyle feel, and "ENTER NOW" is an unambiguous action.
- The footer modules (app download, 10% rewards, text signup for 15% off) give loyal subscribers useful next steps.

## What's weak

- The hero is a repeat of the Nov 12 and Nov 14 sends, so the creative adds nothing new.
- The three-line "THE / EARLY / BLACK FRIDAY / SALE" lockup is very widely letter-spaced and dark. The category buttons are low-contrast grey-on-plum and look disabled.
- The "10%" struck through to "20%" in the rewards block looks like an editing leftover and undercuts trust.
- The sweepstakes and rewards blocks compete with the sale. Four different CTAs (Shop, Enter, Learn More, Sign Up) dilute the primary action.
- There is no product imagery in the sale section, so nothing concrete is shown to click on.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense and long.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the plum bokeh hero with real product shots (a hero sofa or lighting piece) and a price, such as "Sofas from $X", so the click has a destination.
- 2. Pick one primary CTA. Move the sweepstakes and rewards blocks below a single "Shop the Sale" module, or hold them for a separate send.
- 3. Fix the rewards graphic by removing the strikethrough and stating the final "20% back" cleanly. Make the category buttons higher contrast.
- 4. Follow through on the subject with a concrete reason not to wait, such as a "Prices lock in until Nov 17" line near the top.
- 5. Reduce the cadence of near-identical sends, or vary the hero daily.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday: up to 60% off, ends Nov 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Don't wait: 60% off + 20% back in rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 70% off clearance and double rewards, through Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, lighting, rugs and decor, all marked down now.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with VML/MSO fallbacks and responsive `@media` rules. The main confirmed problems are missing `alt` text on 9 content images, 11 plain-`http://` third-party tracking pixels (Dotomi), and a viewport meta tag that blocks zoom. The compliance headers could not be verified because of the relay. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer and link targets were not inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **60 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped the HTTP check on click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over `http://`.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`. They will be blocked or rewritten by HTTPS-only proxies, and they add third-party cookie-syncing weight to the email.
- **1 more Dotomi pixel is also plain HTTP:** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`.
- **First-party tracking pixels load over HTTPS and are fine in themselves:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/bf1d3e8b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`. Both lack `alt`; see section 3.
- **Font URLs have a double slash:** `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`. It likely resolves but is sloppy.
- **Fonts are pulled from a different domain:** the `HNW05` `@font-face` rules load from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Nine content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a3de039b-…jpg`
  - `e026737c-…jpg`
  - `08583e9f-…jpg`
  - `f44cdc2c-…jpg`
  - `7ad0a026-…jpg`
  - `040858c2-…jpg`
  - `0bf786cd-…jpg`
  - `5b8313e0-…jpg`
  - `345f2993-…jpg`

  With images off, or for screen readers, the content is invisible or read out as filenames.
- **The 13 tracking pixels also lack `alt`.** They should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"` so they are skipped by assistive tech.
- **Zoom is disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom, a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text size adjustment is turned off:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`. Together with `*{line-height:100%}` this hurts legibility on some clients.
- **The `<title>` is generic.** It reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", not the subject line. This is minor.
- **A template placeholder was left in:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the truncated portion. The rest was not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found.** This is flagged by QA, which notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.** One-click unsubscribe is therefore unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay artifact, not evidence of failure.
- **The visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address were not checked.** They are in the truncated footer.
- **The From domain is consistent.** `mail.cb2.com` matches the image host `image.mail.cb2.com`, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This could not be assessed. The tracking links were not resolved, and the visible source shows no UTM parameters or landing URLs. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` values in the `dv.cb2.com` pixel suggest Movable Ink or similar tracking, but that cannot be tied to landing pages.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Serve the Dotomi pixels over HTTPS, or drop the 10 cookie-sync pixels and the UCM pixel. They are a privacy and deliverability cost.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` headers on the raw message, not the relay copy, and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC there too.
5. Re-run the audit with the full HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address, UTM parameters, and click-redirect destinations. Resolving the 60 skipped tracking links with a HEAD or GET follow would close the largest gap.
6. Clean up the template: the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder, the `//Styles` double slashes, and the cross-domain `crateandbarrel.com` font hosting.
