# Starts now! Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-09**
## Executive summary

- This email does what the subject says better than the 10/09 "STARTS NOW: Free shipping on orders $99+" send did. That one opened on a sofa banner and buried the shipping offer. Here the first real headline is "starts now! free shipping on orders $99+", with a grey "Shop All Free Shipping" button. The offer is easy to find. The weak spot is the thin banner above it, "The Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off", which competes with the shipping headline and gives two offers in the first screen.
- The rest of the email is a long, busy stack. It has a six-tile category grid, a baby-gear block, a Halloween shop banner, a four-product "we love these for you" grid, a Crate Card promo, an app download block, a Design Desk booking, and a store locator. The shipping offer gets two CTAs, one above and one below the grid. After that the email drifts into general merchandising. It works as a catalog. As a focused free-shipping push it dilutes its own message.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send with a clear hero offer, but it tries to carry too many messages and the grey CTA buttons are weak. It is a solid mid-pack email.

## What's working

- The subject and the headline match: "free shipping on orders $99+" appears in both, so the promise is kept right away.
- The shipping CTA is repeated above and below the six-tile grid, so a scrolling reader gets a second chance to click.
- The six-link category grid (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, holiday) has clean photography and clear labels.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real product names and prices, including a marked-down item. This gives shoppers a concrete reason to click through.
- The seasonal hooks are timely: the Halloween shop banner and the holiday tile fit the date.

## What's weak

- The top strip, "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off", is a bigger discount than free shipping and sits above it. The eye reads two offers, and the stronger one isn't the one in the subject.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" buttons are pale grey on white, and they are the least prominent elements in the hero. For the main CTA this is a visibility miss.
- The shipping offer is a generic sitewide threshold, with no deadline. The "Starts now" in the subject has no end date to create urgency.
- The email runs very long. Halloween, baby gear, the Crate Card, the app, Design Desk and the store locator all compete for attention below the offer.
- The "the very best baby gear" block and the product grid lean on baby items. That fits some recipients and not others, and the email shows no signs of personalization.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free-shipping offer the single hero. Move the headline above the fold, use a high-contrast button, and drop the bedroom and nursery banner, or move it below the category grid.
- 2. Add a clear end date, for example "through Sunday", to make "starts now" credible.
- 3. Cut the email to the offer, the category grid, one product row and the footer. Move the Crate Card, app and Design Desk blocks to their own sends.
- 4. Make the category grid shoppable by adding a "from $" price or a "ships free" tag on each tile.
- 5. Segment the product grid, for example nursery products for new parents and kids' room products for others, so it doesn't mix everything together.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free shipping on $99+ starts now, through Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nursery & kids' room faves, shipped free over $99`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedding, toys, lighting and more. Free shipping on $99+ ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 50% off our Bedroom & Nursery Event. Shop before it ends.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's markup uses a standard table-based XHTML layout with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint, and its tracking is routed through first-party domains. The main defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes (31 flagged images). The compliance headers could not be verified from the data provided.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 69 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified, so this is a coverage gap rather than a confirmed defect.
- The email includes third-party pixels: `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?...&n=1` through `&n=5` (five LiveRamp pixels). It also includes `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=...`, an open-tracking pixel, and `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/2522e321-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. None of these has alt text, and `rlcdn.com` is an external domain.
- Six identical `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=...` requests appear. They are likely a repeated dynamic-content or recommendation image.
- The `mi_u` token is a per-recipient identifier embedded in image URLs. If the email is forwarded or archived, it exposes that identifier.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Missing `alt` on about 31 `<img>` elements, including:
  - content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (e.g. `eeb8adae-...jpg`, `3e71a7cd-...gif`, `38ce93cc-...jpg`, and others)
  - `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` on the `/m/47/` path
  - spacer images `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (twice)
  - tracking pixels, which should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`
- With images blocked, the content images render with no fallback text, so the main content is unavailable to screen readers and to clients that block images by default.
- Decorative and tracking images should have an explicit empty `alt=""`. An absent attribute makes screen readers read the filename or URL.
- The `<title>` is `Crate & Barrel` with a raw `&`. This is invalid in XHTML 1.0 Transitional, which this doctype declares, and should be `&amp;`. It also has no relation to the subject line.
- `<meta name="format-detection">` is declared three times (one combined, plus separate `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no`). This is redundant and harmless.
- The head contains placeholder text: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. This is a template leftover.
- `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which is an accessibility concern.
- The universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ... line-height:100%}` and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` apply globally. The rule `.sup` and the `html` text-size-adjust rules duplicate each other. These are style concerns, not breakages.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I could not verify the body structure, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, or link-level `title` attributes.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, `[FirstName]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` / `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters are populated rather than literal placeholders. The truncated source limits this check.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) was not found. The QA tool says AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so it should be checked against the raw original message.
- No `Authentication-Results` header was captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. This is a limitation of the relay, not evidence of a failure.
- The physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link are not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The links are wrapped in redirect domains, and the 69 skipped probes mean the final destination URLs and any UTM parameters are unverified.
- Image URLs carry `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` identifiers rather than UTM parameters. The attribution scheme appears to be platform-based rather than UTM-based, but I can't confirm this from the available data.
- I can't assess whether the landing page matches the "Free shipping on orders $99+" offer without resolved destinations.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (the 14 `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` images and the `/m/47/` image). Use `alt=""` on the spacers (`25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`) and on the tracking pixels.
2. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, outside the AgentMail relay, to confirm RFC 8058 compliance.
3. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original message. Alternatively, run an SPF/DKIM/DMARC check against `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
4. Resolve the 69 redirect links with a follow-redirect probe (not a plain HEAD). This would confirm the destinations, any UTM parameters, and that the offer landing page matches the free-shipping promise.
5. Escape the `&` in `<title>` as `&amp;` and remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta so pinch-zoom works.
7. Check why the `f3c0d22992a01946.png` image is requested six times. If it is one recommendation block, consider whether the repetition is intended.
