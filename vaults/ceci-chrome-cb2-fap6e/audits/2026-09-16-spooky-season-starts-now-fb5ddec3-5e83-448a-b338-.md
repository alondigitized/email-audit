# Spooky season starts NOW 👻→
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' Halloween opener — "the halloween shop is now open" — and as a category-launch email it's actually well-executed within its own lane: clean product photography, a logical shop-by-category structure (baskets → costumes → decor → bedding), and a consistent black/orange/cream palette that reads as Halloween without looking cheap. No render bugs, no broken images, no lorem-ipsum.
- Where it falls apart is length and focus. After four solid Halloween sections, the email keeps going — into a Crate & Barrel "spooky season" crossover, an unrelated Stokke Tripp Trapp high-chair launch in olive green, a "we love these for you" grab-bag grid, a credit-card pitch, and an app-download sweepstakes, closing with a "TheDesignDesk" service ad. That's six additional non-Halloween asks stapled onto a Halloween-themed subject line. Compare this to the brand's 2026-09-15 kitchen listicle (5.00/10) and Venus Williams sends — Crate & Barrel/Kids consistently over-stuffs single emails with unrelated CTAs rather than trusting one offer to carry the send.
- There's also no visible price incentive anywhere — no %, no $, no BOGO — just repeated "Shop All Halloween" buttons. For a seasonal-launch email that's a reasonable choice (it's introducing a collection, not clearing it), but it means this send lives or dies on product appeal alone, and the kitchen-sink tail dilutes that appeal by the time a reader scrolls halfway.

## What's working

- Hero is instantly legible — "the halloween shop is now open" plus a jack-o'-lantern graphic communicates the entire email's purpose in one screen.
- Category structure (baskets, costumes, decor, bedding) mirrors how a shopper actually browses, each with its own "Shop All" CTA.
- Consistent, on-brand black/orange/cream Halloween palette throughout the first four sections — cohesive without being garish.
- Product photography is clean and well-lit (ghost basket, witch costume, jack-o'-lantern pillow), no layout breakage.

## What's weak

- No concrete offer anywhere — every CTA is "Shop All Halloween," never a %/$ off or BOGO, despite the subject line's urgency ("starts NOW").
- The email sprawls well past its stated topic: a Crate & Barrel crossover, a Tripp Trapp high chair, a credit-card pitch, and an app sweepstakes all get bolted onto what should be a focused Halloween-shop launch.
- Hero CTA links to the general Halloween shop rather than a specific hero product, wasting the highest-attention real estate on a category page instead of a flagship item.
- No reviews or star ratings visible anywhere in the send, unlike more successful listicle-style sends from this brand.
- "Starts NOW" implies urgency but there's no deadline, countdown, or date — the urgency is unsubstantiated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after "boo-tifil bedding & more." The Crate & Barrel crossover, Tripp Trapp chair, credit card, and app sweepstakes belong in separate sends — bundling them buries the Halloween message this subject line promised.
- 2. Point the hero CTA at a specific flagship product (e.g., the ghost trick-or-treat basket) instead of the general shop — gives the eye a landing point rather than another category link.
- 3. Add one concrete incentive — even "Free shipping on $75+" would outperform a bare "Shop All Halloween" repeated five times.
- 4. Replace "starts NOW" with a real deadline if one exists (e.g., "before Oct 1") to make the urgency credible instead of decorative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Halloween shop is live: costumes, decor & more 🎃`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New: trick-or-treat baskets, costumes & spooky decor`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Costumes, candy bowls, and boo-tiful bedding — shop the new arrivals`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From ghost baskets to witch capes: the full Halloween lineup is here`
