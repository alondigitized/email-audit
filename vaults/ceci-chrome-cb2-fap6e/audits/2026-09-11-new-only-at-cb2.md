# NEW & ONLY AT CB2
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-11**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 in full "new collection" lookbook mode, not sale mode — and it's a jarring pivot after a straight week of Labor Day countdown clocks (2026-09-08 through 2026-09-09) and yesterday's "THE FALL SALE" send (2026-09-10, 6.00/10). Subject "NEW & ONLY AT CB2" and the hero "NEW TO COVET" both lead with newness and exclusivity, not price — a legitimate differentiator after five straight discount emails, but it buries the actual promotion (Fall Sale, up to 30% off) roughly 5,600 pixels down, well past the fold, in a single small banner sandwiched between an app-download push and a best-sellers grid.
- Visually this is CB2's strongest execution in the run: no render bugs, no stacked numerals like the 2026-09-07 "10% 20%" glitch, consistent serif/sans typography, and genuinely covetable product photography (the marbled resin side table, the round table with fresh flowers). But it's also the longest, least-focused send of the bunch — new arrivals, four different room categories, a "best sellers" grid, an app download pitch, the Fall Sale banner, a rewards card cross-sell, a texts-signup cross-sell, and a second "more for you" product grid, all stacked in one scroll.
- Net: gorgeous production, weak funnel discipline. A subscriber opening for "new arrivals" gets a genuine lookbook; a subscriber opening because CB2 trained them on Labor Day discounts will scroll past six screens before finding any percentage off.

## What's working

- Hero "NEW TO COVET" over a clean four-quadrant collage (marble table + flowers, blurred pink lighting, bouclé chair, brass/marble lamp) is elegant and on-brand, a step up from the countdown-clock aesthetic of the past week.
- Zero render bugs across the entire ~10,000px render — no overlapping text, no broken images, no duplicated numerals like the Double Rewards email.
- Category-by-category structure (Sectionals, Accent Chairs, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors) each pairs a lifestyle shot with a clear "SHOP NOW" link, giving multiple entry points for different shoppers.
- Footer benefits grid (App, Rewards card, gift registry, text signup) is clean and well-organized with distinct icons per offer.
- Fine print at the very bottom gives a concrete, credible end date for the Fall Sale ("Ends September 16") rather than vague urgency.

## What's weak

- The only concrete offer in the whole email — "FALL SALE, UP TO 30% OFF" — doesn't appear until deep in the scroll, after seven-plus screens of new-arrival content; anyone scanning on mobile will likely never reach it.
- Subject line "NEW & ONLY AT CB2" gives zero indication there's also a sale running, so open-driven shoppers looking for a deal get nothing in the subject/preview to pull them in.
- The email is doing at least four jobs at once (new collection lookbook, sale, app download, credit card, SMS signup, second product grid) with no visual prioritization — nothing signals which action actually matters most.
- "BEST SELLERS YOU CAN ONLY FIND AT CB2" and the later "MORE FOR YOU" grid show overlapping/similar product types (side tables, folding table) — feels like padding rather than curation.
- Coming one day after 2026-09-10's "THE FALL SALE" send, this reads as a step backward in message clarity for anyone still shopping that sale — the offer that was the whole point yesterday is now an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the Fall Sale banner (or at minimum a "Fall Sale up to 30% off" callout) into the hero block itself, or immediately below it — don't make the reader scroll past six sections to find the only discount in the email.
- 2. Cut the email in half: pick either "new arrivals showcase" or "fall sale push" as the lead narrative for this send, and demote the other to a single footer mention. The current send tries to be both a lookbook and a sale email and undersells both.
- 3. Trim the redundant product grids — "Best Sellers" and "More for You" overlap in product type; merge into one grid with 4 genuinely distinct hero items.
- 4. Add the offer to the subject line so open-intent shoppers aren't left guessing.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New Arrivals + Fall Sale, Up to 30% Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Just In: The Pieces You'll Want This Fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New collection drops today — plus 30% off through 9/16.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Marble, resin, brass: the new arrivals everyone's asking about.`
- 5. Consolidate the four footer cross-sells (app, rewards card, registry, SMS) into a tighter two-item row — four competing CTAs at the bottom dilutes all of them.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Email is a well-formed MSO/Outlook-hybrid HTML template with functioning tracking infrastructure, but ships one unrendered merge token and multiple legacy ad-network (Dotomi) pixels served over plain HTTP.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links present but skipped by automated HTTP probe (redirect domains not resolvable via static probe) — not confirmed broken, just unverified.
- Legacy Dotomi ad pixels served over `http://` (non-TLS): `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`, and `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential beacons). Mixed-content HTTP images inside an HTTPS-loaded email may be blocked by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook proxy image loading, some clients silently drop `http://` resources).
- First-party tracking pixel present and HTTPS: `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/...` — no issues.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 14 `<img>` elements missing `alt` text, including 3 content-bearing product/lifestyle images (`cdb98e88...`, `60f54a3d...`, `6e458f4e...` etc.) — screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing.
- The 10 Dotomi `pub*.php` and 1 `UCMController` beacon are also missing `alt`, but as 0/1px tracking pixels this is cosmetically irrelevant (still flagged by QA as a blanket rule).
- Template includes standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments, `-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets, and mobile media queries at 418px/690px/768px breakpoints — no structural rendering issues detected in source.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- [FAIL] Unresolved raw merge token: `%%reviews_skulist%%` — this token rendered literally instead of being replaced with dynamic review/SKU content, meaning at least one module (likely a "customer reviews" or recently-viewed SKU block) will show broken/literal text to recipients.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- [WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found in the captured headers — could be a genuine omission or an artifact of the AgentMail relay not preserving it; cannot confirm CAN-SPAM compliance from this data alone.

- [WARN] `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not found — inconclusive on relay vs. sender-side omission.
- Note: these three are relay-side visibility gaps, not confirmed sender violations — flag for header capture at the source (or check raw headers pre-relay) before concluding CB2 is out of compliance.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable from truncated source/QA data provided — none of the 74 tracking links were probed, and no destination URLs with UTM parameters were included in the excerpt. No issues found based on available evidence (cannot confirm alignment either way).

7. Recommendations
- Fix the unresolved `%%reviews_skulist%%` token before/immediately after send — this is a template-rendering defect affecting live recipients now.
- Migrate the 11 Dotomi beacon URLs (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com` pub1–pub10) to HTTPS, or drop them if the ad-network integration is deprecated — mixed-content images risk silent blocking in modern clients.
