# Don’t miss out: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-10**
## Executive summary

- This is another Fall Sales Event send, and it has the same offer as the 2026-11-10 "Don't miss it: Up to 50% off the Fall Sales Event!" email. The hero is a plaid holiday pattern with "don't miss out! the fall sales event: up to 50% off" and a single "Shop Now" button. A red banner above it says "last Double Rewards this year! Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars with your Crate Card now–Nov. 17, 2026." The offer and the deadline are both easy to read.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a very long scroll. It has an eight-tile category grid, a wagon and baby gear promo, and the Double Rewards banner repeated. After that come a gifts module, a TheDesignDesk $5,000 sweepstakes, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit-card promo, an app download block and a long legal footer. The email has too many ideas, and the sweepstakes and app promos compete with the sale.
- The brand is sending the same Double Rewards offer for the fifth time in about three days. Crate & Barrel Kids is the right sender, and baby gear is a relevant category. The send is competent, but it is crowded and repetitive. It earns a middle score.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 50% off", with a "Shop Now" button right under it.
- The red Double Rewards banner gives a deadline (Nov. 17), which adds credible urgency.
- The category grid ("up to 50% off furniture", "kids bedding + ships free", "baby bedding + ships free") ties each tile to a specific discount.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real products with prices, including strollers and a dresser, so there is some product detail.
- The branding is consistent and the "Crate&BarrelKids" header looks clean.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards banner appears twice, and the sale hero is repeated in spirit. The email says the same thing several times.
- The sweepstakes ("enter for a chance to win $5000"), the app download and the TheDesignDesk promo are all unrelated to the sale. They pull attention away from it.
- The send is very long. The main CTA is a lone "Shop Now" that doesn't say what you are shopping for.
- The "Select styles" fine print sits under the hero. The "ships free" claims and the rewards terms are buried in the dense footer legal text.
- The hero uses no product imagery, only a plaid pattern. That makes it feel less concrete than the category tiles below it.
- This is another near-identical push in a stream of Double Rewards sends. It follows the 2026-11-10 "Don't miss it" email and the 2026-11-08 "Up to 50% off + earn 20% back" email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one story. Keep the hero, the category grid and one rewards banner. Remove the sweepstakes, the app block and the credit card promo, or move them to a different send.
- 2. Put a hero product or a baby gear image in the hero. Show an actual item with a price, not only a pattern.
- 3. Change the CTA copy from "Shop Now" to something specific, such as "Shop baby gear sale" or "Shop kids furniture deals".
- 4. Lower the send frequency. Combine the daily Double Rewards sends into one or two emails per week with distinct angles.
- 5. Move the sale terms and the Nov. 17 deadline up beside the hero, where people will see them.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last Double Rewards of the year ends Nov. 17`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Baby gear up to 50% off + 20% back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Strollers, cribs and kids' furniture are on sale, and you earn 20% back with your Crate Card through Nov. 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance to earn 20% back in Reward Dollars this year. Shop the Fall Sales Event.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketo-style table layout (XHTML 1.0 Transitional with VML/MSO fallbacks) with 78 click-tracked links. The automated QA pass is 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The main code-level problems are about 19 content images with no alt text, a recipient-identifying hash sent to a third-party pixel, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The header-level warnings come from the relay and can't be confirmed from this data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** All 78 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination status codes, redirect chains and broken links are unknown.
- **Third-party pixels are embedded in the body.**
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5` pixels (LiveRamp).
  - Six `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixels.
  - One `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` open pixel.
  - One `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/07117397-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` pixel.
- **The same per-recipient hash appears in two places.** The `rlcdn.com` `s=` value (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8`) matches the `mi_u=` prefix on the first-party pixels. A persistent recipient identifier is being passed to a third-party ad-data domain. This is a privacy and disclosure concern, and it is only confirmed as far as the URLs show.
- **Redundant pixels.** Duplicating the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` six times and `448796.gif` five times adds requests with no evident purpose. The `n=1…5` parameter suggests deliberate repetition, but the purpose can't be determined from the source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images.** The QA data flags about 19 content images with no alt attribute:
  - 17 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` or `/m/47/…` (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `26171910-…jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-….gif`).
  - Two on `s7d5.scene7.com/…2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`.
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader in use, these will read as filenames or be skipped. If they carry offer or CTA text, that content is lost.
- **Tracking pixels flagged for alt.** The 13 pixel images listed in section 2 have no `alt`. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 sizing so assistive tech ignores them.
- **Viewport blocks zoom.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Markup hygiene** (minor):
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is unclosed under an XHTML doctype, and the Content-Type meta is declared twice.
  - `format-detection` is declared three separate times.
  - A leftover template comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, is still in the source.
- **Good.** `lang="en"`, Outlook VML/MSO conditionals, and the `@media (max-width:460px)` responsive overrides are all present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The subject, `Don’t miss out: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!`, has no personalization and no stray tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe not found.** The QA tool notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed. If it is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules are not met.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post not found.** The same caveat applies. Without it, RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe isn't supported.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment can't be assessed. The sender domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, and its alignment with the Return-Path/DKIM `d=` is unknown.
- **Footer not visible.** The visible source doesn't include the footer, so the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link can't be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The probe skipped all 78 tracked links, and the truncated HTML doesn't expose final destination URLs or UTM parameters. No continuity issues can be confirmed or ruled out.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the ~19 content images, starting with any that carry offer or CTA text. Add `alt=""` to all 13 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Re-run the check against the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be stripping them.
4. Re-probe the destination links by following the redirect chain, then confirm UTM parameters and landing page status.
5. Review whether the shared recipient hash sent to `sr.rlcdn.com` is covered by the privacy disclosure. De-duplicate the repeated pixels if they serve no purpose.
6. Clean up the template: escape `&` in `<title>`, remove the duplicate meta tags and the `IMPUT HERE` comment.
