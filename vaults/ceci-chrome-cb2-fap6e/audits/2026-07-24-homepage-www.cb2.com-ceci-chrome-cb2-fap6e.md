# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 is doing *moody editorial* and I respect the commitment — the photography is dark, rich, and aspirational in a way that feels genuinely elevated versus what you'd see at a Pottery Barn. Right now the homepage is leading with new arrivals discovery and a soft push on custom sofas, which, as someone currently in "velvet sofa savings mode," has my attention. The semi-annual sale banner is there, but it's almost too easy to miss.

## What's working

- **"NEW TO COVET" hero positioning** — that framing is *exactly* why I open CB2 over Crate proper. It signals curation over commodity, and that's the whole brand promise.
- **"PREPARE TO WANT IT ALL" category grid** — Living, Dining, Bedroom, Lighting with dark-moody lifestyle shots. The photography quality is high and the grid gets me into a category in one tap.
- **Custom sofa module** — "CUSTOM IS THE ULTIMATE LUXURY / With premium fabrics in as little as four weeks" with a "GET FREE SWATCHES" CTA is genuinely smart. I'm in the research phase for a sectional and this is the exact nudge to move me down funnel. The Faible Sectional by VUUE callout adds designer-collab credibility I respond to.
- **Semi-annual sale strip** — 30% off bedroom is a real number and it's above the fold. No coupon code theater, just a direct event link.

## What's weak

- **Hero colorway is off for the brand's edgiest audience** — the macro floral in muted rust/pink reads spa brochure, not the chrome-and-velvet fantasy I come here for. It's beautiful but soft. Where's the matte black moment?
- **Rewards callout is whisper-quiet** — "Earn 10% Back in Reward Dollars" is buried in the top utility bar in what looks like 10px text. If I'm a rewards member, this should feel like a warm welcome, not a terms footnote.
- **No free shipping threshold visible** — I can see a sale, I can see new arrivals, but I have zero idea what I need to spend to get free shipping. That friction matters when I'm already calculating whether a $400 accent chair is worth it.
- **The olive sectional doesn't close the aesthetic loop** — after a hero that's warm and botanical, the sectional is forest green on a warm-wood floor. CB2's signature cool/dark/metallic palette is barely represented above the fold. Where is my chrome accent chair?

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero to feature a chrome- or matte-black-forward product** (a mirror, a bar cart, a metal accent piece) with a cooler, more editorial color story — lean into the aesthetic identity that separates CB2 from its siblings.
- **Promote the rewards balance or tier benefit in the hero area** — a one-liner like "Members earn 10% back — shop sale and double down" converts a passive perk into an active reason to buy today.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the promo strip** alongside the 30% off — even "Free shipping on orders $75+" tells me whether to add to cart or walk away.
- **Add the designer collab badge more prominently** — the "Designed by VUUE" callout on the Faible Sectional is doing real work; make that label bigger and link it to a collab story or collection page.
