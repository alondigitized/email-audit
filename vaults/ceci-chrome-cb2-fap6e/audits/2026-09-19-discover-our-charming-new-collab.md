# Discover our charming NEW collab →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids doing an actual "meet the collaborator" launch — Sharland England by Louise Roe for Crate & Kids — and it's a clear step up from the grab-bag catalog format the brand has leaned on in every recent send I've audited (09-19 baby gear, 09-18 rugs, 09-18 kitchen dump). The hero is genuinely well-composed: warm, editorial nursery photography with the designer seated in the space, her name and brand lockup clean and legible. The room-by-room structure (bedroom → nursery → playroom) sticks to the collab thesis for three full modules before it collapses into the now-familiar unrelated tack-ons: a Stokke sale banner, a baby-registry product grid, a credit card pitch, and an app download push.
- The problem is the back half undoes the good work up front. By the time you hit "we love these for you" with Nuna strollers and Jenny Lind dressers, the email has fully abandoned the Sharland England story and reverted to the same undifferentiated catalog sprawl seen in the 09-19 "Nuna, Doona & more" send. A collab launch this well-produced deserves to stay a collab launch — or at minimum get a hard visual break before pivoting to unrelated inventory.
- Net: strong top, weak discipline. This earns a bump over the brand's recent average purely on the strength of the hero and room modules, but the tail drags it back down.

## What's working

- The hero cleanly identifies both parties — "Sharland England by Louise Roe FOR Crate&kids" — with a real lifestyle photo, not a stock-looking product cutout.
- Three consecutive room modules (bedroom, nursery, playroom) all reinforce the single collab thesis, unlike the brand's usual unrelated-category grab-bags.
- Product cards consistently show "New & Exclusive" tagging plus price, giving the collection a premium, limited-drop feel.
- Repeated "Shop the New Collab" CTA buttons are simple, high-contrast, and unambiguous.
- No visible render bugs — images, type, and buttons all render cleanly at every module I checked.

## What's weak

- The email pivots hard away from the collab after the playroom module into a Stokke sale, a baby registry product grid, a credit card offer, and an app download pitch — none of it earns space in a "discover our new collab" email.
- No visible pricing anywhere in the collab modules themselves (bedroom/nursery/playroom) — every price shown in the screenshots belongs to the unrelated Nuna/Stokke/Jenny Lind tail, not the actual collab pieces.
- Subject promises "charming NEW collab" but the email spends over half its length on non-collab merchandising, which is a bait-and-switch on scroll.
- "First look: the nation..." Stokke module and the registry grid feel bolted on with no visual transition — it reads like two unrelated emails stapled together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — end it after the playroom module and a final "Shop the Full Collection" CTA. Save the Stokke sale, registry picks, credit card, and app push for separate sends; this protects the collab's premium feel and matches the brand's own best recent format (the 09-19 dining catalog scored higher specifically for staying on-thesis).
- 2. Add visible pricing to at least the hero product card so browsers can gauge cost before clicking — right now the collab items show no price signal in the parts of the email that are actually about the collab.
- 3. If the tail sections must stay, insert a clear divider module ("More from Crate & Kids this week") so it doesn't read as a false continuation of the collab story.
- 4. Tighten the subject to name the collaborator, since "Louise Roe" recognition could drive opens on its own and is currently buried below the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Louise Roe designed this for your nursery`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sharland England x Crate&kids is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `A cross-stitched, heirloom-inspired collection for every room`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New cribs, dressers & accents from designer Louise Roe`
