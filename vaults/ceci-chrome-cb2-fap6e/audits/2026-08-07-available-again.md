# AVAILABLE AGAIN
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- CB2 swaps out its usual discount-stack or editorial playbook for a third mode: the restock alert. "AVAILABLE AGAIN" and "SEIZE the MOMENT" / "IT'S BACK IN STOCK" frame the whole email around inventory returning, then walks through five in-stock categories (Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage, Sofas) each with its own hero shot and "SHOP NOW," followed by a Lighting/Rugs/Wall Art/"ALL BACK IN STOCK" tile grid. It's a clean, well-photographed, bug-free send — but there is no price offer anywhere in the primary flow. The only markdown in the whole email is one item ($499→$349) buried in the "More for you" grid near the bottom.
- Below the fold, this is nearly a rerun of 8/6's ("THE AUGUST SALE") back half: same app-download sweepstakes ("win $1,000," now through 8/13), same review sweepstakes, same rewards/credit-card perk blocks, same "More for you" grid. The top of the funnel is genuinely new creative; the bottom of the funnel is copy-pasted scaffolding CB2 reuses across sends.
- Net: this is a solid, on-brand production that will read well visually but gives a promo-motivated subscriber no reason to click *now* — no urgency, no discount, no named product. It lands in the same tier as CB2's non-discount editorial sends (8/3 "Goop color studies," 8/5 "The marble that elevates everything"), not with its 9/10 discount-stack sends (8/4, 8/1).

## What's working

- Hero is visually strong — moody wood-paneled room, burgundy velvet chair, single unambiguous CTA ("IT'S BACK IN STOCK") right up front.
- The category-by-category structure (Bedroom → Coffee Tables → Sectionals → Storage → Sofas) is easy to scan and each block has its own clear "SHOP NOW," so browsing shoppers have low friction.
- Zero render bugs — every image loads, no overlapping text, no broken layout across a very long (9,400px) scroll.
- Loyalty/rewards signals are present and legible ("EARN 10% BACK," CB2 app perks, texts sign-up), which reinforces brand trust for an existing subscriber.
- Brand voice and photography are consistent top to bottom — same warm, aspirational tone as recent CB2 editorial sends.

## What's weak

- There is no discount, promo code, or price incentive anywhere in the main flow — after five straight days of stacked-discount sends (8/1, 8/4, 8/5), this one gives a deal-motivated subscriber nothing to act on.
- Subject "AVAILABLE AGAIN" and hero "SEIZE the MOMENT" never say *what* is back — no category, no product name, so there's no hook for someone with a specific want (e.g., a sofa shopper has to scroll to find out sofas are even in this email).
- The email is extremely long (nine screens) with five nearly identical "IN STOCK [category] / SHOP NOW" blocks — repetitive structure with no differentiation in urgency or incentive between them.
- The bottom third (app-download sweepstakes, review sweepstakes, rewards perks, "More for you" grid) is a near-verbatim repeat of the 8/6 send's back half — cadence fatigue risk for anyone who opened both.
- No sizing, fit, or availability detail on any featured piece — a shopper reacting to "back in stock" has no way to confirm the size/color they wanted is what's actually back.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete hook in the subject/hero — name the specific category or hero item that's back ("The Sectional Is Back" or similar), instead of the generic "AVAILABLE AGAIN." This is the single biggest lever: right now nothing tells a subscriber whether *their* category restocked.
- 2. Add a light incentive to the hero block (even a modest "free shipping on restocks this week") — five days of discount messaging conditioned subscribers to expect an offer; a bare restock alert will underperform by comparison.
- 3. Trim the category stack from five blocks to two or three, or move the rest into a "shop by category" nav strip — the current structure asks for a lot of scrolling before any differentiation.
- 4. Surface the one markdown item (Welling Armchair, $499→$349) higher in the email instead of burying it in the bottom grid — it's the only price signal in the whole send.
- 5. Rotate the bottom-funnel modules (app sweepstakes / review sweepstakes / rewards) so they're not identical to the 8/6 send two sends prior.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Sectionals & Sofas: Back in Stock`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Restocked: The pieces you wanted are back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bedroom, sofas, storage & more — all back and shipping now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The waitlist is over. Shop what just came back.`

## Technical Audit: CB2 "AVAILABLE AGAIN" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
Standard ESP-templated HTML email (MSO conditional comments, fluid/hybrid layout, mobile media queries) with a heavy third-party tracking/retargeting payload; primary issues are missing image alt text, insecure (HTTP) tracking pixels, and unconfirmed unsubscribe/authentication headers.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 74 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (link destinations not verified).
- 10 sequential retargeting pixel calls to `ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (all served over `http://`, not `https://`) — redundant/duplicated pixel firing pattern, each a candidate for being blocked by mail clients that strip mixed-content or non-HTTPS image requests.
- Additional Dotomi call: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` — also HTTP, same blocking risk.
- Open-tracking pixels present via `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0b014ca0-...` — both HTTPS, no issue.
- Product/creative images served from `image.mail.cb2.com` — HTTPS, no issue.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, including all primary content images (e.g. `a87ea69f-0a44-4281-8af3-b718933d143f.jpg`, `3de210f4-8da8-4567-89e5-b05e2473d91a.jpg`, plus 10 others listed in QA output). With images-off as the default rendering state in most mail clients, this leaves the email with no fallback text for sighted users or screen readers.
- 11 image sources use `http://` instead of `https://` (the Dotomi `UCMController` and `pub1–pub10.php` calls) — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked outright by Gmail/Outlook image proxies, which would silently drop that tracking without visual impact (all are 1x1-style pixels/redirects, not content images).
- Head markup includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional blocks, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and mobile breakpoint media queries (`max-width: 418px`) — no structural rendering issues found in the visible source.
- `@font-face` declarations (Lineto Brown, GT Sectra, HNW05) include Helvetica/sans-serif fallback stacks — no issue.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders (e.g. `{{first_name}}`), or dynamic content blocks are visible in the provided source. Note: HTML was truncated before the body content, so this cannot be fully confirmed — personalization logic may exist in the untruncated body/CTA sections.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found in the captured headers (may be a relay-capture limitation via AgentMail rather than a true absence — flagged as WARN, not confirmed absence).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe): not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — sending domain authentication status is unknown from this capture.
- In-body unsubscribe link and physical mailer address (required by CAN-SPAM) are not visible because the HTML source is truncated before the footer — cannot confirm compliance either way from available evidence.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be evaluated: all click-through links fall within the 74 tracking/redirect URLs that were skipped by the automated probe, and the footer/CTA markup is outside the truncated HTML sample. No UTM parameters or destination URLs are available as evidence.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 12 content images flagged by QA, prioritizing product images over decorative/tracking pixels.
2. Migrate the 11 Dotomi `http://` pixel/redirect calls to HTTPS to prevent silent drops by mail-client image proxies.
3. Consolidate the 10 near-identical `pub1.php`–`pub10.php` retargeting calls if they serve overlapping purposes — confirm with the ad partner (Dotomi) whether all 10 are still required.
4. Confirm with the sending ESP that `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent (may be an AgentMail relay capture gap rather than a true omission) — verify via raw source from the original SMTP transaction.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from the original delivery headers, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured here.
