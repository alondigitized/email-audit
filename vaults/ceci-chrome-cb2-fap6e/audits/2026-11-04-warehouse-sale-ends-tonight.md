# WAREHOUSE SALE ENDS TONIGHT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, honest last-chance send. Two sales get equal billing, each with a huge percentage and a white "SHOP THE SALE" button. "ENDS TONIGHT" appears twice above the fold. The photography is warm and consistent, and the offer is readable in about two seconds.
- The subject names only the Warehouse Sale, but the first hero is the Guest Ready Sale (up to 40% off). The 60% Warehouse hero doesn't appear until the second block. Someone who opens for the Warehouse Sale lands on the lesser offer. The 60% headline also sits over a busy striped rug, so "UP TO" and "60% OFF" lose some contrast.
- The cadence is the weak point. This is the third send on the same two sales in two days. Yesterday's "2 SALES END TOMORROW" was the strongest in the run, and today's earlier "HIGHLY ANTICIPATED RESTOCK + 2 Sales Ending Soon" already covered this ground. "Ends tonight" is a credible escalation, but it adds nothing new beyond the clock. The Interior Design block and the app/rewards/texts grid dilute a send that should be one message.

## What's working

- "ENDS TONIGHT" in the first screen is a credible, time-bounded urgency signal that follows through from "ENDS TOMORROW" on 11/3.
- The oversized "40% OFF" and "60% OFF" numerals make the offers the clear focal point of each hero.
- The white "SHOP THE SALE" buttons are unambiguous and repeat identically in both blocks.
- The photography of the carved wood dresser, cabinet and rugs is on-brand and fits a home and furniture shopper.
- The CB2 header and category nav (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) give an easy route into browsing.

## What's weak

- The subject promises the Warehouse Sale, but the top hero is the Guest Ready Sale at 40%, which is the smaller offer.
- The "UP TO 60% OFF" text overlays the striped rug, which reduces legibility.
- Both CTAs go to a generic "Shop the Sale" with no product or category focus, so there's no specific hook for a furniture or rug shopper.
- The Interior Design hero and the 2x2 grid (app, rewards card, gift list, texts) add scroll without adding urgency.
- The dense fine print with daggers on both offers sits under "up to" claims, and the exclusions aren't visible near the headlines.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the Warehouse Sale at 60%, to match the subject. Put the 40% Guest Ready sale second, or fold it into a single line.
- 2. Cut the Interior Design hero and the app/rewards grid from a final-day send. Keep one footer rewards line.
- 3. Put a clock or "Ends tonight at 11:59 PT" next to the headline. State the actual deadline from the fine print.
- 4. Replace the generic CTA with category links such as "Shop rugs", "Shop furniture" and "Shop lighting", so each click has a destination.
- 5. Put the headline on a plain or darkened area of the rug, or add a scrim, so "60% OFF" stays readable.
- 6. Space out the sends. Consolidate the 11/4 restock send and this one into a single "last day" message.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 60% off rugs & lighting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: Warehouse Sale closes at 11:59 PT`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, lighting and furniture up to 60% off. Final hours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 40% off the Guest Ready Sale. Both end at midnight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is well-formed XHTML 1.0 Transitional with MSO/VML conditional comments and a mobile breakpoint. The QA warnings are mostly missing `alt` attributes, 11 plain-HTTP third-party pixels, and email headers that couldn't be checked. The source was truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and link-level checks are incomplete.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **53 tracking links weren't probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so none of the destinations are verified (200 status, no redirect chains, no expired sale landing).
- **11 images load over `http://`:**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
  - These can be blocked or rewritten by clients and proxies. Where blocked, the cookie-sync pixel silently does nothing.
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels.** The ten `pub1`–`pub10.php` calls are an ad-tech cookie sync (Dotomi/Epsilon) embedded in the email body. They add ten extra third-party requests per open, and the proxies Gmail and Apple Mail use may strip or cache them.
- **First-party open-tracking pixels** (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b97479f9-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`) are served over HTTPS. Their only issue is missing `alt`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images missing `alt`.** These three are served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` and are likely content images:
  - `140911d7-4e2b-4e8f-817b-ec9c6967ae86.jpg`
  - `76b1662c-c9c0-4a64-ac76-86c8dce07d35.jpg`
  - `d208e96e-e597-4851-93ff-d609fb16937f.jpg`
  
  With images off or a screen reader, nothing is conveyed, and nothing indicates the sale message survives as live text.
- **Tracking pixels missing `alt`.** `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/…`, `UCMController` and `pub1`–`pub10.php` have no `alt`. They should have `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on some clients, which conflicts with WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled.** The `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` rule and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` override the `100%` set earlier. This prevents user text scaling in iOS Mail.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a misspelled placeholder comment and is harmless. The `<title>` ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") is generic and doesn't match the subject.
- **Fonts loaded from two domains.** The `@font-face` rules pull from `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com`. The `Lineto Brown Sub` family has overlapping declarations (an `.eot`-only rule with no weight, then weight 400/600 `.woff2` rules). Fallbacks are present (`Helvetica, sans-serif`), so the risk is low.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The `mi_u=<sha256>` parameter on the open pixel is resolved. The rest of the body wasn't available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
All three findings below are **unverified, not confirmed failures**. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.**
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** One-click unsubscribe can't be confirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the From address.
- **Body footer not checked.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address weren't in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmed. The 53 tracked links weren't resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment couldn't be checked. The only campaign identifiers visible are `mi_cid=678efa99bfad4da1` and `mi_mid=01a1a893-…` on the open pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the three `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, including the sale and deadline message. Add `alt=""` to all pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the ten `pub*.php` cookie-sync calls from the email body.
3. Re-run the QA on raw `.eml` to check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` without relay stripping. Check the full HTML for the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
4. Resolve the 53 tracking redirects with a followed-redirect probe to verify final URLs, status codes and UTM presence.
5. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and the `text-size-adjust:none` rules.
6. Set `<title>` to the subject line, and delete the stray `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
