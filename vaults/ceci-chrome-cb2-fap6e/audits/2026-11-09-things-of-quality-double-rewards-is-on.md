# Things of quality + Double rewards is on
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a visually polished, long-scroll CB2 send that never decides what its offer is. The header has a countdown timer and "Long Weekend Sale", but the visible hero is "Future Heirlooms Under $500". That leads into six designer pieces: the Wesler dining chair, T marble side table, Corivo sculpture, Curva candle holder, Enoki table lamp and Roxane sconce. Then comes a "Double Rewards Is On, get 20% back in reward dollars" block, a "90+ fabrics, 10 free swatches" tile, an interior design services banner, and a "More for you" grid.
- The photography is moody and on brand, and the countdown is the strongest urgency device in the email. The "up to 50% off" headline from the week's earlier sends is not visible in the render. The sale, the heirloom price cap, the rewards offer, swatches and design services all compete. The countdown also shows about three days left, which reads oddly against the Nov 8 "FINAL DAYS" framing of the previous send, so it may feel like a stretched sale.
- Judgement: good-looking, but it reads as the same week-long sale re-sliced into a catalog. A subscriber who got "UP TO 50% OFF + Double Rewards" yesterday gets little new here beyond a "$500 cap" angle.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the very top gives credible, time-bounded urgency above the fold.
- "Future Heirlooms Under $500" is a concrete, price-anchored hero that frames the products as an investment.
- Each product section carries a named piece and designer credit, which matches the "things of quality" theme.
- Category CTAs (shop furniture, decor, lighting) sit after each block and are easy to find.
- The Double Rewards block is large and uses a clear number, 20% back in reward dollars.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is not clear. "Long Weekend Sale" has no percentage, and the "$500" cap is a separate message further down.
- The email is very long, with six full-bleed product blocks before the rewards offer. Mobile readers will likely drop off before reaching it.
- The rewards, swatches and design services blocks add extra asks late in the scroll and dilute the main CTA.
- Product names are set in small overlay text on busy photos, and the product prices are not shown until the "More for you" grid at the bottom.
- The lower sections are small and dense and hard to read at inbox scale. That includes the app/rewards tiles and the fine print.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one headline offer in the hero ("Up to 50% off + 20% back") and make the countdown subordinate to it, or cut the heirloom framing.
- 2. Cut the product blocks from six to three and show a price on each.
- 3. Move the Double Rewards block directly under the hero, since it is the only member benefit.
- 4. Drop the design services and swatches tiles from sale sends.
- 5. Check the countdown against the actual sale end date so it matches what recipients saw the day before.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Heirloom-quality pieces, all under $500`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left: up to 50% off + 20% back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Marble, glass and sculpted lighting, with 20% back in reward dollars through the weekend.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our designer-made pieces under $500, plus double rewards. Sale ends soon.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals. It has 14 content images without alt text, 11 insecure `http://` third-party tracking pixels, and a viewport/text-size setup that limits accessibility. Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be confirmed, because the QA relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **72 tracking links were not probed.** They are click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and final URLs are unverified.
- **11 tracking pixels load over plain `http://`**, which can be blocked or flagged as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **The ten `cookieredir/…/pubN.php` calls are third-party cookie-sync pixels.** They add ten extra requests to a third-party ad domain on every open and slow rendering. Most are effectively dead where third-party cookies or remote images are blocked.
- **The open-tracking pixel URL carries a 64-hex-character `mi_u` value** (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa278…`). It looks like a hashed subscriber identifier in a query string. The URL is also exposed to image proxies.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **14 content images have no `alt`**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `4a67d125-…`, `f2b42825-…`, `5cf97682-…`, `b9371a82-…`, `c2fa029c-…`
  - `8ea38568-…`, `7cd04a6a-…`, `600bc5a4-…`, `17985b45-…`, `df3b263a-…`
  - `32244ef5-…`, `345f2993-…`, `3a05caa8-…`, `7052e7a5-…`
  - If images are blocked, the email shows no text equivalent for these. Screen readers will announce the filename.
- **The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/ccbc05d6-…`, and the Dotomi pixels) have no `alt=""`.** Decorative and tracking images should have an empty `alt` so screen readers skip them.
- **`<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>`** disables pinch-zoom, an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`** (on `*` and `body`) disables text resizing in WebKit clients.
- **The `*{… line-height:100%}` universal selector** is a blunt reset. It is overridden only for the `.f10`/`.f14`/`.f20` classes in the mobile media query.
- **A font dependency spans two domains:** the `@font-face` rules point at `cb2.com` and `crateandbarrel.com` (`HNW05`). Some URLs use a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/…`). `.eot` and `.woff` sources are listed for clients that don't support them. A failed font fetch falls back to Helvetica/sans-serif.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - The placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in `<head>` and misspells "INPUT".
  - An empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block remains.
  - `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype.
- **The responsive breakpoint is `max-width: 418px`.** Devices wider than that (large phones, some foldables) get the desktop layout, which risks horizontal scroll or shrink-to-fit. The `.mobile` class forces `width:414px`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[First Name]`) appear in the truncated HTML. The truncation means the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed, not proven**. If they are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements (one-click unsubscribe) would not be met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured**, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, so alignment can't be checked from the data given.
- **The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source**, so the in-body CAN-SPAM elements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverifiable from the data. All 72 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the source is truncated, so no UTM parameters or landing URLs are visible. The only identifiers seen are Marketing Cloud-style `mi_cid`/`mi_mid` on the pixel `dv.cb2.com/o/ccbc05d6-…`. No continuity issues can be claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text to the 14 listed content images.** Add `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10 `cookieredir` calls.** If they stay, confirm that Dotomi supports HTTPS on `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com`.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`, and use a relative text-size adjust** (`-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%`) instead of `none`.
4. **Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original SMTP message** (not the relayed copy), and check SPF/DKIM/DMARC with a raw-header capture. This closes the three WARNs that currently can't be resolved.
5. **Resolve a sample of the 72 tracked links** with a redirect-following probe, to confirm UTMs and landing pages.
6. **Clean up the template:** remove the `[IMPUT HERE…]` comment, close the `<meta>` tags, and raise the mobile breakpoint to about 480–600px.
7. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** to check merge tokens, the footer and the unsubscribe link.
