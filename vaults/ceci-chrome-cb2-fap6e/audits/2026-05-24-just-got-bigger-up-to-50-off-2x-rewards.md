# JUST got bigger! Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' Memorial Day escalation email — the offer "JUST got bigger" from "up to 30%" to "up to 50% off," paired with the Double Rewards/2x hook the brand has been hammering all weekend (2026-05-23 "This is it! Final weekend" at 8/10, 2026-05-23 main C&B "Final Weekend up to 60%" at 9/10). The structural play is fine — escalation is a real reason to re-open the inbox — but the execution lands soft because the hero image is a generic dresser/nursery vignette rather than a kid-specific moment, and the "up to 50%" headline competes with a second urgency block ("Double Rewards ends today") stacked right under it.
- The grid below the hero is on-brand: cribs, dressers, a play kitchen, soft goods. But the visual hierarchy gets muddled — too many parallel CTAs, no single hero product to anchor the click. By the time the eye reaches "App Download" and the "TheDesignDesk" promo near the footer, the email has lost the urgency it opened with.
- Net: it's a credible "final push" send, but the creative is recycled and the offer ceiling ("up to 50%") still hides behind unspecified categories. A working 6/10.

## What's working

- Subject signals real escalation ("JUST got bigger") — a legitimate reason to re-open after the earlier 30% email.
- 2x Rewards stack on top of the discount — a clear loyalty kicker.
- Product grid covers the core Crate & Kids categories (nursery furniture, play, soft goods).
- Hero copy block is clean and readable — no broken text or layout glitches.

## What's weak

- Hero is a generic dresser styled-room shot — no kid, no toy, no obvious "Crate & Kids" emotional cue.
- Two urgency blocks compete: "up to 50% off" and "Double Rewards ends today" — eye doesn't know where to land.
- "Up to 50%" is unspecified — no anchor product, no example SKU at that ceiling.
- Footer real estate is busy: app download + "TheDesignDesk" promo + standard category nav dilute the close.
- This is the fourth near-identical urgency send in the Memorial Day window — cadence fatigue is real.

## Recommendations

- 1. Anchor the hero with one specific kid-coded product at a real price ("Crib X — $499, was $799") instead of the generic dresser vignette. The escalation only matters if I can see what 50% off looks like.
- 2. Collapse the two urgency blocks into one stacked headline so the eye lands once: "50% off + 2x Rewards — ends today."
- 3. Cut the app-download and TheDesignDesk blocks from this send — they belong in a quieter mid-week email, not the closing-bell push.
- 4. Replace the product grid with a "deepest cuts" row showing actual % off per product so "up to 50%" is provable.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Bigger now: up to 50% off + 2x Rewards — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cribs, dressers, more — now up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `The Memorial Day Event just got deeper. See the new markdowns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Final hours for 2x Rewards on every kids' purchase.`
