# FINAL HOURS: Up to 30% off things for hosting
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent last-call send for the Semi-Annual Dining Event, and it does one thing well. The countdown timer ("SALE ENDS IN 00 : 05 : 23 : 41") and the "FINAL HOURS" banner stack urgency at the top. The offer, "UP TO 30% OFF", sits in large type over a moody dining-chair hero with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button. The eye goes straight to the offer.
- It is also the third near-identical Dining Event push in three days. The 2026-10-27 "ONE DAY LEFT: SEMI-ANNUAL DINING EVENT" send used the same countdown and the same hero. The same-day "WAREHOUSE SALE IS SELLING OUT" send and the earlier "Hosting Thanksgiving up to 30% off dining furniture" send run the same play. A subscriber sees repetition, and the "final hours" claim loses some credibility.
- The lower half dilutes the message. Four category tiles (Dining Furniture, Tabletop, Lighting, Rugs) are well shot but link to categories, not products. After them come the Nina Garcia app promo, loyalty and credit card, and SMS signup blocks. These add up to a lot of competing asks below a clean offer. Overall it is a solid, slightly stale 6.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the very top puts credible urgency above the fold.
- The hero pairs a big "30% OFF" with a single "SHOP THE SALE" button, so the offer is unmistakable.
- The category tiles (Dining Furniture, Tabletop, Lighting, Rugs) are on-theme for hosting and visually consistent.
- The second CTA, "SHOP THE DINING EVENT", gives a repeat chance to click after the scroll.
- There are no visible render bugs: the images load and the text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- This is the third near-repeat of the Dining Event in three days, so the "final hours" framing feels manufactured.
- The "UP TO 30% OFF" hero text is thin white type over a busy photo, and "THE SEMI-ANNUAL DINING EVENT" above it has weak contrast.
- The tiles show no products, prices, or reviews, so there is nothing concrete to click.
- The app, Rewards, and SMS modules below split attention and bury the sale CTA.
- The dense legal text at the bottom signals heavy exclusions, and the "up to" language hides the typical discount.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the generic category tiles with 3 to 4 named hero products showing their sale prices (for example "Dining table, now $X"). This is the biggest click lift.
- 2. Cut the app, Rewards, and SMS modules from deadline sends. Keep the Nina Garcia promo for a non-urgent send.
- 3. Raise the hero text contrast with a darker overlay or a bolder weight on "30% OFF".
- 4. Add a specific end time ("Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT") beside the timer.
- 5. Space out sends of the same sale, or vary the angle, such as a bestsellers-in-the-sale send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call for your Thanksgiving table`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Dining furniture, tabletop, lighting and rugs. Sale ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Seat everyone in style. Final hours to save up to 30%.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

Several third-party pixels load over `http://`, and 7 content images have no `alt` attribute. The header and compliance checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. QA pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Not verifiable:** 62 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are click-redirect domains. Redirect resolution, destination status codes and UTM propagation were not tested.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 images use `http://` and can be blocked as mixed content, so those calls may silently fail:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
- **Third-party cookie sync:** the `pub1.php` to `pub10.php` calls look like cookie-sync pixels for a third-party ad network (Dotomi). Each one adds an external request and a tracking disclosure surface. They also have no `alt`.
- **First-party pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/fba901bf-…?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS. Both lack `alt`.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`.** Check the reviews or product-list data extension and send-time population, add a fallback so the block is hidden when empty, and add a pre-send check for unresolved `%%…%%` tokens.
