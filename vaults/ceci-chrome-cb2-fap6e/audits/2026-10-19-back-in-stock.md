# BACK IN STOCK
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-led "back in stock" send. The page is long, about 8,400px, and there is no single offer to anchor it. A thin banner at the top carries the only price message, "The Semi-Annual Dining Event, up to 30% off," with a "Shop the Sale" link. The real hero is "SEIZE the MOMENT" over a burgundy chair, with a small "It's back in stock" button. That headline is poetic but says nothing concrete. I read it at thumbnail scale, so I'm judging structure, not fine print.
- Below the hero, the email is a catalog: Bedroom, Coffee Tables, Sectionals, Storage and Sofas, each with a small "Shop Now" link. After those come a Lighting / Rugs / Wall Art & Decor grid, an Interior Design Services block, and a "More for You" row of five products with prices. The photography is strong and consistent, with warm wood tones. The problem is that "back in stock" is never tied to specific items, and the discount sits in a banner above the fold instead of in the hero.
- This is also the fourth or fifth CB2 send in a week. On 10/18 "Steals of the Week" and 10/15 "On Sale: Dinner Party Must-Haves" both pushed the same Dining Event, and the banner here recycles it. The email looks good and gives the reader no reason to act now. I'd call it middling.

## What's working

- The photography is cohesive and moody, with warm tones that feel like CB2 and make the product look premium.
- The category tiles (Bedroom, Sectionals, Sofas) are easy to scan, and each has its own link.
- The "More for You" row shows real products with visible prices ($199 to $1,199), which gives the reader something concrete.
- The footer has the app, Rewards (10% back) and the "Let's Celebrate" registry blocks. These are useful extras.
- The layout is clean, with no obvious broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Seize the Moment" is vague copy. The reader can't tell what is back in stock or why now.
- The 30% off message is stuck in a thin top banner, so the hero and the offer are disconnected.
- The "Shop Now" links on the category tiles are tiny, and some are low-contrast text over photos. The "It's back in stock" button is also small.
- Nothing here suggests scarcity or specifics. There are no named items, no "limited quantities" and no deadline.
- The email is very long and has no clear primary CTA. About a dozen links compete for attention.
- Some of the Lighting / Rugs / Decor tile labels are hard to read over the image.

- 1. Make the hero a specific restock: name 3 to 4 hero products with prices and a "Shop Back in Stock" button, instead of "Seize the Moment."
- 2. Either merge the Dining Event offer into the hero or drop it. Two competing messages dilute both.
- 3. Replace the small text links with full-width buttons that have contrast, such as "Shop Sectionals."
- 4. Cut the length. Keep the five categories or the product row, not both, and move the design services block lower.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's code is sound overall, but it has one confirmed broken-experience bug: a raw merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`, is left unresolved. It also loads 11 non-HTTPS tracking pixels and has 26 images with no alt attribute. QA pass rate was 43%.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 72 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped for HTTP probing. Final destinations, redirect health and UTM handling are unverified, not confirmed broken.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`, which clients may block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** `pub1.php` to `pub10.php` look like a Dotomi cookie-sync chain of ten third-party calls on open. That adds weight and privacy exposure, and the pixels fail silently when blocked.
- **Tracking images on first-party domains:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/…/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/7a1fdaff-…?mi_cid=…` are served over HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a long hash-style identifier in the query string, which is a per-recipient tracking parameter.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, 26 images:**
  - 12 content images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`, including `33a9c22b-…jpg`, `4ddadd7f-…jpg` and `fa96b5d7-…jpg`. If these are content images, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing.
  - 14 tracking pixels: `UCMController`, `pub1`–`pub10.php`, `o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/…`. These should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on some mobile clients, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` stop users from scaling text in WebKit clients.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It's harmless to rendering but shows a template step was skipped.
- **Web fonts:** `@font-face` rules load from `www.cb2.com//Styles/…` (double slash) and from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`. Most email clients ignore them, and the fallback stacks (Helvetica, sans-serif) are present, so this is low risk.
- **Source truncated:** Body-level structure such as semantic markup, `role="presentation"` on layout tables and button implementation could not be assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Unresolved token:** `%%reviews_skulist%%` is rendered raw in the delivered HTML. This looks like an unfilled dynamic-content or product-reviews variable in Salesforce Marketing Cloud style (`%%…%%`) syntax.
- It is either visible text or a broken dynamic-content block, and its exact location is in the truncated portion of the source. The same field is probably not resolving for other recipients either.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects.** The QA warnings below may come from the AgentMail relay stripping headers:
  - `List-Unsubscribe` was not found.
  - `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found.
  - `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown.
- **Not assessable:** The visible unsubscribe link and the physical postal address sit in the truncated footer. I can't confirm either.
- **Sender domain:** The From address is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a dedicated subdomain that is consistent with the `image.mail.cb2.com` image host.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not verifiable:** All 72 links are click-tracking redirects that were not probed, so UTM parameters and landing page alignment can't be confirmed. The QA data has no resolved destination URLs.
- **Tracking appears to be wrapper-based:** Attribution appears to depend on the ESP redirect plus the `mi_*` and Dotomi identifiers rather than visible UTMs. Whether UTMs survive to the landing page is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`:** Confirm the variable or data extension populates, and add a fallback or suppress the block when it is empty. Add a pre-send check that fails on any `%%…%%`.
2. **Move tracking pixels to HTTPS:** Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://`. Reduce or remove the `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync chain if it is not essential.
3. **Add alt attributes:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 content images and `alt=""` to all 14 tracking pixels.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and drop the `text-size-adjust:none` declarations where possible.
5. **Re-test the headers:** Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw message with a direct SMTP or seed-inbox test. This will show whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` are really missing or only stripped by the relay.
6. **Probe the tracked links:** Resolve a sample of the 72 redirects and check final URLs for UTM parameters, HTTP 200 and landing page match.
7. **Remove the leftover `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` template comment** and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font paths.
