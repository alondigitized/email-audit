# STARTS TODAY: Up to 30% off The Thanksgiving Dining Room Event!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent seasonal sale send. The hero says "the Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off" over a moody table photo, with a sale-priced table callout and a single "Shop Now" button. The offer is clear and the hero matches the subject. For a shopper planning to host, the first screen does its job.
- After the hero, the email sprawls. It runs through four category tiles, a Hosting Guide banner, two more CTAs, kitchen deals, a Design Desk sweepstakes, a Reward Dollars card offer, an app download block, a second Design Desk block, and a store locator. That is about eight competing modules under one dining-room promise. The kitchen deals and the sweepstakes dilute the dining message.
- Cadence is the bigger problem. Crate & Barrel sent a sofa "ends today" email, a Bedroom & Nursery Event email and a wine glass email on 10/14. This is another sale-framed send in the same two days. It's a clean execution of a tired rhythm: solid, not special.

## What's working

- The hero pairs a concrete "up to 30% off" with a seasonal hook (Thanksgiving) and a sale-vs-regular price on the featured table.
- The four-tile grid (dining tables, seating, storage, shop all) gives clear category entry points with the discount stated on each.
- The kitchen deals section has real sale and regular prices ("Sale $149.95 Reg. $195", "Special $399.95 Sugg. $470"), which is credible and specific.
- The Hosting Guide gives editorial value beyond a pure discount.
- The brand look is consistent and the render is clean, with no broken images or visible layout failures.

## What's weak

- Too many modules. Sweepstakes, a credit card promo, an app push and kitchen deals all pull attention away from the dining event.
- The hero says "starts today" but gives no end date, so there is no deadline pressure to act now.
- Both the hero and the Hosting Guide use generic "Shop Now" / "Shop Thanksgiving" buttons. The two stacked CTAs ("Shop The Dining Furniture Event" / "Shop All Dining Furniture") are nearly identical.
- The legal fine print is dense and tiny, and the "up to 30%" exclusions are easy to miss.
- The Hosting Guide headline text sits over a busy photo and is harder to read than the hero.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to a dining-only story: hero, four tiles, the Hosting Guide, one product row. Move kitchen deals, the Design Desk, the card offer and the app block to a separate send. This lifts the email more than any other change.
- 2. Add a deadline to the hero and subject, for example "through Oct 27". "Starts today" has no credible endpoint.
- 3. Link product tiles to specific items or collections, not only category pages.
- 4. Replace the duplicate CTAs with one strong, specific button, for example "Shop Dining Tables".
- 5. Space out sale sends. After the 10/14 sofa, bedroom and wine-glass sends, a subscriber will tune this out.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML source is a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a `lang="en"` attribute and MSO/VML fallbacks. The QA run found 3 warnings. Two are missing unsubscribe headers and one is a missing Authentication-Results header. All three are probably artifacts of the AgentMail relay, not confirmed sender defects. There are also about 30 images with no alt text, and 66 tracking links that were never probed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 66 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none of their destinations were verified. This is a QA coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Tracking pixels and third-party beacons are present:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…` (open pixel)
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/16dc16c5-7b7f-4bbf-af55-7cc70ea4a7bb?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5`. This is a LiveRamp (rlcdn.com) third-party sync pixel, repeated five times with only `n` changing.
- The `mi_u=56aaa278…` identifier is in the query string of `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images and in the `sr.rlcdn.com` URLs. It is a per-recipient token, which is expected for personalization.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm the href destinations or any link-level issues beyond what the QA report lists.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text.** The QA run flagged about 30 `<img>` elements with no `alt`:
  - Content images, all on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/` (`ef861136-…jpg`, `38f614bb-…jpg`, `64e9a7b1-…jpg`, `d527aae5-…jpg`, `29312b60-…jpg`, `8e20dfa4-…jpg`, `cc47eb6a-…jpg`, `3bedfd2e-…jpg`, `201cd1c6-…jpg`, `0c1fc034-…jpg`, `2f3f07fc-…jpg`, `971aa10f-…jpg`) plus `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-…png`.
  - The Scene7 image `2026%5F0920%5FCB%5FThanksgivingGuide%5FTertiary%2D…` (`s7d5.scene7.com`).
  - Seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images. These look like personalized recommendation images, and they probably carry the product content.
  - Tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `448796.gif` ×5, the `dv.` beacon). These are decorative, so they should have `alt=""` but are low priority.
- If these images carry the offer or product content and have no alt text, then with images blocked the email is unreadable for screen-reader and image-blocking users. I couldn't confirm which images carry text because the HTML was truncated.
- `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, an accessibility concern (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Duplicate or contradictory meta tags: `format-detection` is declared four times with conflicting values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, then `date=no`, `address=no`, `telephone=no` separately). The result is harmless but sloppy.
- `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. Most clients tolerate it.
- A leftover template placeholder remains in the source: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
- Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are declared, which is redundant but harmless.
- `body` has `display:block!important`. The `<!--V2-->` comment, `!important` overuse and `*{max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` are standard Oracle Responsys-style or MI template boilerplate. No functional issue is confirmed.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FNAME]` appear in the visible source. The only placeholder is the HTML comment noted above, which isn't a merge field. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are really missing, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024+) require one-click unsubscribe from senders of this volume.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The check can't be run from this data.
- The footer, with the physical address and unsubscribe link, is beyond the truncated HTML. I can't confirm CAN-SPAM footer elements.
- The From address uses the branded subdomain `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent with the tracking and image domains. That is good for alignment.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The 66 links go through click-redirect domains and were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment can't be assessed.
- Ads and analytics parameters, if any, are inside the redirect, not visible in the HTML.
- The `mi_u`, `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters are used for the sender's own tracking, not UTM.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add meaningful `alt` text to the content and product images (about 20 `m/1/*.jpg`, the Scene7 image and the seven `p/rp/*.png`). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Verify the unsubscribe headers (`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and SPF, DKIM and DMARC against the raw message at the original recipient inbox, since the relay may be hiding them.
3. Resolve the 66 tracking redirects with a follow-redirect probe, so landing pages, UTM parameters and 4xx/5xx destinations can be checked.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta to allow zoom.
5. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in `<title>`. Clean up the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the leftover `IMPUT HERE` template comment.
6. Look at why `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` fires five times, since duplicate third-party pixels add load and privacy exposure for no extra signal.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML so the footer, merge tokens and link hrefs can be checked.
