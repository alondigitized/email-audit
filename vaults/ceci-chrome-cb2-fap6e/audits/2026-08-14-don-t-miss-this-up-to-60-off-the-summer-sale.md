# Don’t miss this: Up to 60% off the Summer Sale!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a straightforward site-wide sale email, and it does the one thing it needs to do well: the hero is unambiguous — a beach photo, "up to 60% off The Summer Sale," and a Shop Now button before you scroll. The category grid right below (bed & bath, outdoor, kitchen, rugs, decor & lighting, furniture clearance, tabletop clearance) is genuinely useful — it lets a shopper self-select instead of hitting one generic landing page.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. This single email crams in a Levain bakery collab, a Howell x Harrier x AD sweepstakes, a rug launch, a six-product "we love these for you" grid, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a design service plug. That's seven distinct offers competing with the sale headline. Compare this to the 2026-08-14 Stokke high chair send, which scored well specifically because it was one product, one message. This is the opposite instinct — throw everything at the wall.
- Verdict: strong top, bloated body. The sale itself would pull a click; the kitchen-sink layout underneath dilutes it and risks the recipient bailing before reaching anything relevant to them.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and on-brand — ocean photo, bold percentage-off headline, single visible CTA.
- Category tile grid gives shoppers a fast path into their specific interest instead of one blanket link.
- "Shop the Sale" button reinforces the primary CTA a second time before other modules dilute it.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, no overlap or broken layout.

## What's weak

- Way too many competing offers stacked in one send (bakery collab, sweepstakes, rug launch, product grid, credit card, app, design service) — no clear secondary priority.
- The sweepstakes module ("enter for your chance to win a $1000 Gift Card") is visually heavier than the actual sale content below it, which undercuts the sale as the headline offer.
- "*” after "The Summer Sale" and no visible fine print near the hero — exclusions are likely buried at the bottom, which the rubric flags as a soft trust issue even if common practice.
- Six-product grid has no visible pricing hierarchy (no strikethrough/sale price shown) despite this being a sale email — misses a chance to reinforce the discount at the product level.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to two modules max after the hero: sale categories + one supporting offer (either the rug launch or the bakery collab, not both, and definitely not the sweepstakes in the same send). Move the credit card, app, and design-desk pitches to a separate retention/lifecycle email — they have nothing to do with "60% off."
- 2. Show sale pricing (strikethrough original + sale price) on the "we love these for you" product grid — right now those six products look like a random catalog block, not part of the sale.
- 3. If the sweepstakes must stay, demote it below the sale-relevant content, not directly under the category grid where it currently steals attention.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the hero ("ends Sunday," "48 hours only") — right now the urgency is implied only by "Don't miss this" in the subject, not shown anywhere in the body.
