# 2x Rewards on dressers, cribs, beds & more!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a nursery and kids' furniture send built on the "Last Double Rewards this year!" hook. It is more focused than the earlier Crate & Barrel sends this week because the product tiles stick to one category: a dresser, a glider, a crib and a bed. The stacked product-and-button pattern is easy to scan and fits a nursery shopper. The offer is the weak point. Each tile promises "Earn $X in Reward Dollars" instead of a price cut, so the value is abstract, and the hero says "up to 50% off" without saying which items.
- It is also the sixth Crate send I've audited since Nov 4. On Nov 5 there were "The Fall Sales Event starts TODAY!", "Starts NOW: Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards ✅" and "Starts TODAY! Open to shop Double Rewards →", and earlier today there was the Black Friday countdown. The creative is competent, but a subscriber has now seen the same Double Rewards message several times in three days. The email is also long, at roughly 10,600px. A second half with a sweepstakes ("enter for a chance to win $5000"), a Design Desk appointment pitch and an app download dilutes the furniture message.
- Verdict: a solid, well-targeted catalog email that sits in the middle because of cadence fatigue and a split-focus bottom half. A 6 is fair.

## What's working

- The product tiles are clean and repeatable. Each has a photo, a name, an "Earn $X in Reward Dollars" line and a "Shop Now" button, so the eye follows a clear rhythm.
- The furniture is a coherent set (dresser, recliner, crib, bed), so the subject's "dressers, cribs, beds" promise is delivered.
- The "Last Double Rewards this year!" headline gives a credible deadline, backed by the "Nov. 11" date in the copy.
- Each tile has a second button ("Shop All Dressers", "Shop All Cribs") that sends category browsers to the right place.
- The lifestyle photography is warm and aspirational, with a holiday-tinted crib shot that fits the season.

## What's weak

- The "Earn $X in Reward Dollars" lines are small and abstract. They don't tell the reader the actual price or discount.
- The "up to 50% off" hero banner doesn't say which items it applies to, and it conflicts with the tile messaging that leads with rewards.
- The bottom half is padded with a $5000 sweepstakes, a Design Desk appointment, an app download and a new credit-card promo. This competes with the furniture CTAs.
- The email is about 10,600px tall, so the real offer is buried in a long scroll.
- Cadence is heavy: roughly six Double Rewards and Fall Sales sends in about three days.
- The overlaid text on a couple of the mid-email photos (the gift and Design Desk banners) is hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with price, not rewards. Show "from $X" or "Save up to 50%" on each tile and demote the Reward Dollars to a secondary line.
- 2. Cut everything below the product grid except one footer CTA. Move the sweepstakes, app and credit-card blocks to a separate send.
- 3. Make the hero specific. Name the categories on sale instead of an unqualified "up to 50% off".
- 4. Consolidate the cadence. Fold this into one nursery-focused send per week instead of near-duplicate Double Rewards messages.
- 5. Fix the legibility of the text over photos in the gift and Design Desk banners.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nursery event: up to 50% off cribs, dressers & gliders`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year: nursery edition`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars on nursery furniture through Nov. 11.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Crib, dresser and glider picks, all earning 2x Rewards before the offer ends.`
