# CLEAR YOUR WISH LIST (AND THEIRS)
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the plain-letter version of CB2's Black Friday push: "Hi, This just in…" signed by "Nicole from the CB2 Team". It has no hero image, no product, no button and no price. The only offer is "up to 60% off" in the body copy. It reads like a personal note, which is a nice change from the polished sends earlier this week, but it gives the reader almost nothing to act on.
- This is roughly the sixth "up to 60% off" Black Friday message in about five days. Compared with "UP TO 60% OFF LIGHTING" (8/10, Nov 25) and "THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP" (7/10, Nov 24), this one drops the visual payoff completely. The only way in is four blue inline links (Furniture, decor, rugs, lighting) and the nav bar. A subscriber who is tired of the cadence will skim it and leave.
- Judgement: a fair idea, because a plain-text style can cut through promo fatigue. But it is under-built as a sales email, since the click path is weak and the deadline is hidden. It's a 4 or 5 out of 10.

## What's working

- The plain, personal tone with a signed human name stands out against a week of heavily designed sends.
- The copy is short and scannable, and the "up to 60% off" claim sits in the first paragraph.
- Four category links are in the body copy and the nav bar repeats them, so there is more than one way to start shopping.
- "Get ahead of the Thanksgiving rush" ties the email to the current season.
- The footer's category grid and sister-brand row (Crate&Barrel, Crate&kids, H|G) are clean, with no visible layout breakage.

## What's weak

- There is no button and no hero, so the primary CTA is unclear. The only links are small blue inline text.
- Nothing visual shows what is on sale: no product, price or photo.
- The end date (Dec 1, 2026) and the "cannot be combined" exclusions appear only in the tiny footer fine print. The body's "before it sells out" is vague urgency.
- The blue default-style links look unstyled next to the brand's clean grey look.
- The "100+ items added" line is a repeat of the Nov 24 and Nov 25 sends, so it no longer feels like news.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a real hero and a single button, for example "SHOP UP TO 60% OFF". This one change would lift the email the most. Link it to a landing page, not the homepage.
- 2. Put the deadline in the body ("Ends Dec 1") rather than only in the fine print.
- 3. Show 3–4 priced products in a grid under the letter, with sale and original prices.
- 4. Use the new-item hook for something specific, such as "100+ new markdowns, with a link to the new-arrivals-on-sale page".
- 5. Restyle the links in brand colour and make them bold, so the letter looks designed.
- 6. Space out the sends. If a plain note is the angle, drop the near-identical Black Friday hero sends around it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `100+ new markdowns, up to 60% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nicole here: new Black Friday adds`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs and decor, all up to 60% off through Dec 1.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `We just added 100+ items. Shop before Thanksgiving.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering/personalization failure: an empty greeting name slot (`Hi ,`). Four of the six remaining QA warnings come from header capture and link-probe limits, so they are unverified rather than confirmed defects. The HTML source was truncated, so only the head and CSS were inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 41 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination resolution, redirect chains and link health are **unverified**.
- Five identical LiveRamp pixels load from `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8&n=1` through `n=5`. Only `n` differs, so the same beacon fires five times per open, which is redundant.
- The `s=` value is 64 hex characters, which matches a SHA-256 digest. The same value appears as `mi_u=` in the first-party open pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa...`. That means a stable hashed recipient identifier is being passed to a third-party domain (`rlcdn.com`).
- The first-party open pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` is present.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content-bearing images (confirmed):**
  - `https://image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/47/bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png`
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/18/5ac6898a-92dc-4f7a-a6b4-53785b8e43b8.gif`
  - With images blocked, or for screen readers, these render as nothing or as a filename.
- **Tracking pixels without alt (the `rlcdn.com` ×5 and `mi.cb2.com/.../o.gif`):** These are decorative. They should carry `alt=""` explicitly, plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:none"` if not already set. The rest of the markup was truncated, so I can't confirm those attributes.
- **Head markup (confirmed):** `<meta charset="utf-8">` appears three times, and `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Head markup (OK):** `lang="en"` and the viewport meta are present, and the `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` meta is present. The VML/Office namespaces are declared.
- **Mobile layout:** The responsive breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, which is narrow for modern phones. Wider devices, such as larger phones in landscape, fall back to the desktop layout. This is an observation from the CSS, not a verified defect.
- **Legacy CSS:** `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass .ecxhm` target obsolete clients. This is harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Empty greeting slot:** The greeting renders as `Hi ,`, so the first-name merge field resolved to an empty string with no fallback.
- No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`) were reported by QA. The truncated source can't confirm this for the body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip it, so this is **unverified**. It needs a check against the raw message from a direct-delivery seed.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` (RFC 8058) not found.** This is also unverified. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo require one-click for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com` are **unknown**, and the relay is the likely cause.
- The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion and **could not be verified**.
- **Privacy note:** The hashed identifier shared with `rlcdn.com` (see §2) is worth reviewing against the sender's privacy disclosures. This is a flag for review, not a confirmed violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
**Not verifiable.** All 41 links were skipped as redirect-tracked, and the truncated source shows no landing URLs. UTM parameters, landing-page alignment and the consistency between the wish-list promo and its destinations can't be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the greeting fallback** with a default such as `Hi there,`, or drop the name when the field is empty. This is the only confirmed user-facing defect.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the two content images listed in §3. Use `alt=""` on all tracking pixels.
3. **Deduplicate the five `rlcdn.com` pixels** to one, or confirm each is intended.
4. **Re-run the compliance checks against the raw message** from a seed inbox (not the relay) to verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
5. **Resolve the 41 redirect-tracked links** with a follow-redirect probe to audit UTM params and landing pages.
6. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** so footer compliance elements and the body markup can be checked.
7. **Consolidate the duplicate `<meta>` tags** (low priority).
