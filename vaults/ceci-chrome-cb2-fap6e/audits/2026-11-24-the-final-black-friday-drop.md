# THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's fourth near-identical "up to 60% off" Black Friday send in four days. The hero is strong: a moody burgundy bar scene with "100+ ITEMS JUST ADDED / BLACK FRIDAY SALE / UP TO 60% OFF" and a clear SHOP NOW button. The eye lands on the offer first. Below it is a tidy grid of category tiles, then free shipping on $99+ and 15% off in the new year.
- The subject "THE FINAL BLACK FRIDAY DROP" promises finality and novelty, but the body doesn't deliver either. Nothing visible says "final," there's no deadline, and the "100+ items just added" line is the only fresh news. After the Nov 21, Nov 22 and Nov 24 "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" sends, a subscriber will read this as more of the same.
- The hero also has a visible render glitch: "SALE" shows as "S A" with a gap, as if letters are missing or overlapped. On the hero headline that looks sloppy.
- Verdict: competent, on-brand and offer-led, but repetitive and short on urgency. It's a solid mid-tier send.

## What's working

- The hero offer "UP TO 60% OFF" is large, centered and paired with a clear SHOP NOW button.
- "100+ ITEMS JUST ADDED" gives a small new-arrivals reason to look.
- The six category tile rows (Furniture, Lighting, Holiday & Gifting, Decor & Mirrors, Rugs, Tabletop, Bedding, Pillows) make browsing easy and the photography is warm and consistent.
- Free shipping on $99+ and 15% off in the new year each get their own banner with a button.
- The footer promotes the app, the 10% back card and 15% off for texts, which gives loyalty and member hooks.

## What's weak

- The "SALE" headline renders as "S A" with missing letters, which looks broken in the most important spot.
- "FINAL" in the subject isn't backed up by any deadline or countdown in the body.
- The category tiles link to categories, not specific products, and show no prices or discount depth.
- There is no social proof or reviews, and no product detail such as sizes, materials or availability.
- Stacked offers (60% off, free shipping, 15% off later) dilute the single message, and the dense fine print at the bottom is long and hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero headline render so "SALE" displays fully, and add a real deadline there, such as "Ends Monday 11:59pm PT."
- 2. Make "final" true. Add a countdown or "last day for free shipping by Christmas" line, or change the subject to match what the email says.
- 3. Replace several category tiles with 3-4 hero products showing "was/now" prices and a verb-led CTA like "Shop the Rugs."
- 4. Cut the stacked offers to one lead plus free shipping. Move the 15% off in the new year lower or drop it.
- 5. Space out the cadence. Four near-identical sends in four days trains people to ignore or unsubscribe.

- **Subject Alt B:** `100+ new Black Friday markdowns just landed`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free shipping on $99+ and new arrivals added today. Sale ends Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rugs, lighting and holiday decor, marked down up to 60%.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML Transitional with MSO conditionals and a media query for max-width 418px. The main confirmed problems are 12 content images without alt text and 11 insecure `http://` third-party (Dotomi) pixels. The compliance and authentication findings can't be confirmed from the relay-captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 62 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on tracking/click-redirect domains. Destination status, redirect chains and UTM handling are untested.
- **Insecure ad pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Epsilon/Conversant) retargeting pixels load over `http://`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&dtm_cmagic=654788&dtm_fid=103&dtm_format=6&cli_promo_id…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1` through `pub10.php`
- **Why it matters:** many clients block or proxy non-HTTPS images, so these pixels will fail. They are also third-party cookie-sync calls, which add load time and privacy exposure.
- **Open tracking:** the first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/dafe513d-2511-4eac-b822-6052a558b0f5?mi_cid=…` are HTTPS and look fine. The `mi_u` value is a hashed subscriber identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (content images):** 12 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`:
  - `3901dc8b-…gif`, `1e159ce9-…jpg`, `0602c5c8-…jpg`, `b0dea8ec-…jpg`
  - `253a25bc-…jpg`, `ea21dbe2-…jpg`, `81c1f43e-…jpg`, `37e20ac8-…jpg`
  - `fb7d0237-…jpg`, `f720f091-…jpg`, `65d9a6b8-…jpg`, `20185973-…jpg`
- **Impact:** with images blocked or read by a screen reader, there's no fallback text. If these images carry the offers or CTAs, that content is lost.
- **Missing alt text (tracking pixels):** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel also lack `alt`. This is low severity. Use `alt=""` with `width="1" height="1"`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom in clients that honor it, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Template leftover:** the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head.
- **Font loading:**
  - The `@font-face` rules use `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/…` with a double slash.
  - The `HNW05` font is pulled cross-brand from `crateandbarrel.com`.
  - The `.eot` and `.woff` sources only partly follow a consistent pattern.
  - Most clients ignore web fonts anyway, so check the fallback stacks.
- **Title:** `<title>CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME</title>` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.
- **Truncated source:** the body wasn't provided, so I couldn't check table structure, `role="presentation"`, bulletproof buttons or the preheader.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The truncated HTML shows no unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. Only the head was available, so the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post:** both were not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm a real defect. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, so check alignment of the return-path and DKIM `d=` with `mail.cb2.com` in a raw-header capture.
- **Body requirements:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the footer, which was truncated, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 62 links are on tracking domains, and the probe skipped them. I couldn't resolve final URLs, confirm UTM parameters or compare landing pages with the email's offer. This is a gap in the audit, not a confirmed defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS or remove them. They are 11 extra third-party requests and are likely blocked anyway.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
4. Remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` comment and fix the `cb2.com//Styles` double-slash font URLs.
5. Capture raw headers (not via the relay) to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. Resolve the 62 tracking links with a redirect-following probe. Verify landing-page status and UTM carry-through.
7. Fix the QA summary logic. It reports "0 issue(s), 3 warning(s)" and a 57% pass rate against more than 40 listed warnings, so the counts are inconsistent.
