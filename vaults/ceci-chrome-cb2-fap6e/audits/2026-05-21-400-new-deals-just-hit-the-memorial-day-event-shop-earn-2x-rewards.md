# 400+ new deals just hit The Memorial Day Event! Shop + earn 2x Rewards
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's day-two Memorial Day refresh — and it earns its existence by switching the creative hook from "40% off furniture" (yesterday's lead) to "400+ new deals just hit," a volume/freshness angle that gives the subscriber a reason to re-engage even if they opened yesterday. The multi-category structure — cast iron cookware, rugs, outdoor dining, bedding — means almost any home-goods buyer finds something in their lane within the first two scrolls. That breadth is the email's defining tradeoff: it maximizes relevance across the list but surrenders the crispness of a single-category send.
- The honest read: this is a competent catalog email riding the coattails of a well-established event. It doesn't break new ground, but it executes the formula cleanly — clean grid layout, visible sale tags, 2x Rewards hook in the hero, no obvious render issues. For the subscriber who skipped yesterday's send, this is a solid entry point. For anyone who opened the 5/20 "Up to 40% off furniture + 2x Rewards IS ON!" (8/10), the value proposition is familiar enough to border on fatigue.
- Judgment: solid mid-tier promo email. Does the job, stops short of memorable.

## What's working

- **"400+ new deals just hit" is a smart day-two pivot.** Volume framing refreshes the Memorial Day story without just re-sending the same "40% off" headline.
- **Hero delivers the complete offer above the fold.** Event name, CTA, and 2x Rewards are all visible before any scrolling.
- **Multi-category product grid gives the email broad relevance.** Cookware, rugs, outdoor furniture, and bedding all appear as distinct sections — a genuine subscriber benefit.
- **2x Rewards is front-loaded.** It's in the hero banner AND the subject line, reinforcing the loyalty hook for members.
- **Clean layout, no visible render bugs.** Sale tags appear consistently across product tiles; images load without gaps or breakage.

## What's weak

- **No anchor discount percentage in the hero.** Yesterday's email led with "up to 40% off" — this one leads with a deal count. Volume is less motivating than a specific savings figure for shoppers deciding whether to click.
- **Subject line is 69 characters — well over the ~50-char mobile sweet spot.** "Shop + earn 2x Rewards" at the end will be clipped on most phones, wasting the loyalty hook.
- **Third Memorial Day-adjacent promo email in roughly 24 hours.** The cumulative cadence (5/20 furniture → 5/20 rugs → 5/21 this) risks unsubscribes for subscribers who don't immediately act.
- **CTA copy throughout is generic "Shop" language.** With six or more separate category sections, none of the buttons give a specific call-to-action that matches the product in frame.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Restore a specific discount anchor to the hero.** "400+ deals" tells me scale; "up to 40% off" tells me value. The 5/20 email already proved that pairing works — bring it back here explicitly ("400+ new deals — up to 40% off").
- 2. **Trim the subject to ~50 chars and move 2x Rewards to preheader.** The double promise in one subject buries both. Cut to the sharpest hook in the subject, extend in preheader.
- 3. **Swap generic "Shop" CTAs for category-specific copy.** "Shop Cookware Deals," "Shop Outdoor Furniture" — one extra word doubles click intent.
- 4. **Add a hard end-date in the hero.** "Ends Monday" or "Through May 26" gives Memorial Day implied urgency an actual expiry anchor.
- **Subject Alt A:** `400+ Memorial Day deals — up to 40% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New deals just dropped: up to 40% off + 2x Rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop cookware, rugs, outdoor furniture + earn double points through Monday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hundreds of new markdowns just added — ends Memorial Day.`

## Technical Audit — Crate & Barrel Memorial Day Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional structure with MSO conditional comments and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. Several development artifacts and accessibility violations are present in the `<head>` that should have been caught pre-send.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Cannot fully assess** — link tags are not present in the truncated source. A full audit requires the complete HTML to verify all `href` values resolve, tracking pixels are present and firing, and click-tracking wrapper is applied consistently.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[HIGH] Global `text-size-adjust: none` applied via wildcard selector**
```css
* { -webkit-text-size-adjust: none; -ms-text-size-adjust: none; ... }
```
Disabling text-size adjustment on `*` overrides user agent accessibility preferences globally. WCAG 1.4.4 violation risk. Should be scoped to `body` only.

**[MEDIUM] `maximum-scale=1` in viewport meta blocks user zoom**
```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
Prevents pinch-zoom on mobile. WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text) failure on iOS. Remove `maximum-scale=1`.

**[MEDIUM] Dark mode explicitly suppressed**
```html
<meta name="color-scheme" content="light">
<meta name="supported-color-schemes" content="light">
```
Forces light rendering in Apple Mail and dark-mode-capable clients. No dark mode fallback CSS is present; if intentional, it should be documented.

**[MEDIUM] Conflicting image `max-width` breakpoints**
```css
@media only screen and (min-width: 768px) { img { max-width: 600px } }
@media only screen and (min-width: 640px) { img { max-width: 640px } }
```
At viewport ≥768px, the 600px rule overrides the 640px rule due to cascade order (768px block appears last). Images are constrained to 600px on desktop despite the container apparently being 640px wide.

**[LOW] Global `line-height: 100%` on `*`** — computes relative to font-size; can collapse spacing on elements with no font-size set. Scope to `td, p, span`.

**[LOW] Duplicate `<meta charset>` declarations** — both `http-equiv Content-Type` and `<meta charset="utf-8">` are present. Keep only the latter.

**[LOW] Triplicate `format-detection` meta tags** — the first tag covers all values (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`); three subsequent single-value tags are redundant template artifacts.
