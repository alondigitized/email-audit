# Last call for the Fall Sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 finally cutting the theatrics — after five straight days of lookbook detours (custom fabrics, color theory, entryway consoles), "Last call for the Fall Sale" is a straight-up urgency sale email, and it's the most direct send in the recent history. "ENDS TODAY" banner, a giant hero with "UP TO 30% OFF," and five stacked category CTAs (Furniture, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Tabletop, Shop the Sale) — no ambiguity about what to do.
- The execution is clean and on-brand, but it's also the same hero template CB2 has used across its sale sends this cycle, just with "ENDS TODAY" swapped in. The real news — actual deadline urgency — is buried in a tiny dagger footnote ("Ends September 16") rather than stated plainly in the hero or subject. For a "last call" email, the urgency mechanic is weaker than it should be.
- Bottom line: strong hero, clear CTA stack, honest single offer — but urgency is undersold visually, and this is the *third* "sale ending" beat CB2 has run this week, which risks cadence fatigue on repetition alone.

## What's working

- Single unambiguous offer ("UP TO 30% OFF") with no fine-print stacking on the hero.
- "ENDS TODAY" top banner immediately signals urgency at the top of the email.
- Five clearly labeled category CTAs give the reader multiple entry points instead of one dead-end "Shop Now."
- Clean visual hierarchy — sofa texture hero image, then bold sale type, then buttons — eye lands correctly.
- No render bugs — image loads fully, buttons aligned, no overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The actual deadline (Sept 16, i.e., today) only appears in the tiny daggered footnote — the hero itself never states "today" or a countdown, undercutting the "last call" urgency.
- This is the third sale-ending message this week ("The Fall Sale is over tomorrow" on 9/15, now "ends today" on 9/16) — subscribers who opened the last one already got the memo, so this reads as repetitive.
- Visual template is identical to prior sale sends (same sofa-texture hero, same stacked pill buttons) — no fresh creative to signal "final hours" distinctly.
- Discount is vague ("up to 30%") with no anchor item or price shown, so there's nothing concrete to click toward.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the actual deadline in the hero, not just the footnote — e.g., "ENDS TONIGHT AT MIDNIGHT" instead of generic "ENDS TODAY," so urgency is felt, not just implied.
- 2. Add a countdown timer or specific hour cutoff — mechanical urgency drives more last-call clicks than static banner text.
- 3. Show one hero product with a specific price/discount (e.g., "Sofa, was $1,299, now $909") so there's something concrete to click.
- 4. Differentiate the creative from the 9/15 "over tomorrow" send — recolor or reframe the banner so repeat openers don't feel like they're seeing the same email twice.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: up to 30% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance — Fall Sale ends at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, decor, tabletop — all included. Once it's gone, it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your cart's waiting. Fall Sale pricing disappears at midnight.`

## Technical Audit: CB2 "Last call for the Fall Sale"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email renders on a standard responsive VML/MSO table template, but ships a broken merge token, missing unsubscribe headers, and multiple insecure/legacy ad-tracking pixels.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- Legacy Dotomi ad-redirect pixels loaded over plain HTTP: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Ten sequential `pubN.php` beacons is unusually high pixel-tracking overhead for a single send.
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (expected behavior for redirect-domain links; not itself a defect, but means click-through targets are unverified in this audit).
- MI/`dv.cb2.com` open-tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) present and using HTTPS — no issue.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 6 content images missing `alt` text (`407ec580...`, `2fe90360...`, `0b6623da...`, `f845bd26...`, `54efc521...`, `6b504292...`, `c9787622...` — 7 total under `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`).
- Tracking pixels (Dotomi, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) also lack `alt` — acceptable/expected for 1x1 beacons, not a real defect.
- Template uses standard MSO/VML conditional comments and `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px — structurally sound, no malformed conditional tags observed in the truncated source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL**: 1 unresolved raw merge token rendered in the email: `%%reviews_skulist%%`. This indicates a personalization block (likely product review/social-proof module) failed to populate and will display literally to recipients.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — may be a relay capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe) not detected — same caveat.
- `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) not detected via the AgentMail relay — authentication status is unknown, not confirmed failing.
- No visible unsubscribe link/footer in the truncated HTML source provided — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM footer compliance from the truncated excerpt; recommend checking the full source.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Insufficient data: the 61 tracking links were skipped in automated probing, so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page alignment could not be verified from this dataset. No issues confirmed either way.

**7. Recommendations**
- Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge tag failure before this template is reused — this is a hard rendering bug, not a warning.
- Migrate the Dotomi ad pixels (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com`) to HTTPS or drop them; HTTP resources in an HTML email risk being blocked by mail clients (Gmail/Outlook image proxies) and mixed-content warnings.
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 content images for accessibility and to preserve context when images are blocked by default.
- Re-verify `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` directly against raw SMTP headers (outside the AgentMail relay) to rule out a relay-capture gap vs. an actual compliance gap.
- Re-run link/UTM validation against the full (non-truncated) HTML to confirm tracking-domain destinations and landing-page parameter alignment.
