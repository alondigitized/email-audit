# FLOOR SAMPLE SALE
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- CB2 sends two emails in one. The subject and first hero promise a "Floor Sample Sale" in stores, shown with a sculptural marble side table and a "FIND A STORE" button. Then the email pivots to the "Fall Design Sale, up to 50% off" with its own "SHOP THE SALE" button, and finishes with a lighting block. The first half is a local, in-store event. The second half is the same online sale CB2 has run all week.
- The floor sample hero is well shot and moody, and the table is the one specific product in the email. But it has no price, no discount and no date. "Limited time" is the only urgency, and the discount that actually shows up belongs to the other sale. A subscriber who has seen the "SALE ENDS TOMORROW" and "SALE'S ALMOST OVER" sends this week will skim straight to the 50% off block and treat the floor sample story as noise.
- My judgment: decent creative, muddled message. It lands a notch below the 2026-10-02 "Setting the standard since 2018" send (7/10) because it asks the reader to care about two offers and gives no reason to act now.

## What's working

- The top hero is striking: a marble scalloped side table with a whiskey glass, book and glasses.
- The Fall Design Sale block gets the eye with a huge "SALE" and "UP TO 50% OFF" over a warm upholstery photo.
- The header nav (New Arrivals, Furniture, Decor, Rugs, Lighting) stays clean and on-brand.
- The footer carries loyalty and acquisition hooks: 10% back in Rewards Dollars, 15% off for texting in, and the app.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "Floor Sample Sale" has no discount, no date and no list of which stores. "In stores for a limited time" is vague.
- Its only CTA, "FIND A STORE", sends the reader to a store locator instead of a product, and it sits above the real offer.
- The two heroes compete, so the email has no single focus. This is the third or fourth stacked-promo send in a row.
- The 50% off block carries a dagger and dense fine print, so the discount looks conditional ("select furniture, lighting and more").
- The email shows no prices, no reviews, and no product detail such as dimensions or availability for the floor pieces.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. If the floor sample sale is the subject, lead with a concrete discount ("Up to 60% off floor samples"), name the date range, and make the CTA "Find floor samples near you". Move the Fall Design Sale into a slim secondary banner.
- 2. Add scarcity that feels real: "One of each, once they're gone they're gone", with a weekend deadline.
- 3. Show two or three floor sample items with a "was/now" price, instead of one unlabeled table.
- 4. Cut the lighting block, or tie it to the sale, so the email doesn't end in a third direction.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off floor samples, this weekend`
- **Subject Alt B:** `One of each: CB2 floor samples, in stores now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Showroom pieces, one of a kind, marked down until they're gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Find the store nearest you, then shop the Fall Design Sale online.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also carries about 10 plain-HTTP third-party cookie-sync pixels and at least two content images with no alt text. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers, and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link health is unverified.** 57 tracking links went to click-redirect domains and were skipped (`[WARN] 57 tracking link(s) skipped`). Destination status, redirect chains and dead links are unknown.
- **Insecure third-party pixels.** The email loads 11 `http://` (non-TLS) pixels from `dotomi.com`:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, each with `?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`

  Clients that proxy or block mixed content will drop these, and they expose cookie-sync traffic in cleartext. The `pub1`–`pub10` pattern is a cookie-sync chain. It adds load and third-party exposure without any user-facing value.
- **Open-tracking pixels** are present: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/4472dfcf-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...`. They are HTTPS and first-party, so this is informational only. Both have no alt text, but that is a low-severity point for pixels.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/f9454af5-c534-4dc1-8b40-2510afc55d66.gif`
  - `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/d1525b3e-cf4e-43cf-93cf-ca381be096c2.png`

  These are hosted on the email image CDN, so they are likely real creative. With images off, or with a screen reader, that content is lost. The tracking and cookie-sync pixels also lack alt text (see section 2), but that matters much less.
- **Zoom is restricted.** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom. The universal selector `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` also blocks text resizing. Both are WCAG 1.4.4 and 1.4.10 concerns.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the `<head>`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Generic `<title>`.** It reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME" and doesn't match the subject "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE". This is minor.
- **Font sources.** `@font-face` rules pull `HNW05` from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different domain from the sender. Some `@font-face` declarations also use a double slash (`www.cb2.com//Styles/...`). Email clients mostly ignore web fonts, and the font stacks fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif, so the impact is low.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"` is set, the VML/Office namespaces and MSO conditional blocks are present, and `max-width` is handled for images.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] Raw merge token `%%reviews_skulist%%`** was found in the rendered output. This looks like an unresolved dynamic-content (product reviews by SKU list) variable from the ESP's template. A block that was meant to be populated either rendered empty or exposed the literal token. The block needs checking for an empty or broken product-reviews module.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes that the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, not a verified failure. If they are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown and can't be assessed from this capture. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`, a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these could not be verified because the HTML source was truncated before the footer. No issue is claimed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be verified.** All 57 links are tracking redirects that were skipped, and the source was truncated. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the tracking pixels suggest the ESP's own attribution, but the destination URLs and any `utm_*` values are not visible. No issue is claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`** in the ESP template: resolve the variable, or add a fallback or conditional so the block is suppressed when empty. Re-test with a seed send.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the two content images (`f9454af5-...gif`, `d1525b3e-...png`). Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. **Remove the `dotomi.com` http:// cookie-sync pixels** (`UCMController` and `pub1`–`pub10`), or move them to HTTPS. Better still, drop them from the email body and handle cookie sync on the website.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and stop applying `text-size-adjust:none` to `*`.
5. **Verify the headers on the raw message** (not the relay copy): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
6. **Re-run the audit with the full HTML**, not the truncated source, so the footer, unsubscribe link and `utm_*` parameters can be checked. Spot-check the redirect destinations for the 57 skipped tracking links.
7. **Housekeeping:** remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment, and align the `<title>` with the subject line.
