# New arrivals are here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-01**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "New arrivals" and the email delivers a three-in-one stack. The top is the Semi-Annual Upholstery Event ("UP TO 25% OFF"). Next comes the real new-arrivals story, "PREPARE TO WANT IT ALL", with designer-credited pieces: the Loop Chair, Scatola Table, Muir Chair, Wolcott Bed and Deseo Sectional. Then a "FALL DESIGN SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" block, a "MORE FOR YOU" product grid, and app and rewards promos. The photography is moody and cohesive, and the designer credits give the new-arrivals section real editorial weight.
- The email has no single job. Two different discounts (25% and 50%) sit above and below the new-arrivals content, and the new-arrivals story is not first. It is also a very long scroll. The subject and the first screen don't match, because the subject says new products and the top banner says sale.
- This is the same pattern as this morning's "UP TO 25% OFF CUSTOM & IN STOCK" send, which also stacked an upholstery event over other promos. CB2 has now sent the Upholstery Event creative at least twice in two days, after the 9/29–9/30 "SALE ENDS TOMORROW" and "SALE'S ALMOST OVER" sends. The subject is the weakest part. Overall it's a solid but unfocused send.

## What's working

- The room-scene photography is rich and consistent, and it reads as premium.
- Designer credits ("Designed by Andreas Hansen", "Muirbright Estudio and VUUE") add the editorial story CB2 does well, as in the 9/28 David Girelli send.
- The "SHOP THE LATEST" and "SHOP THE SALE" buttons are clear and repeated.
- The "MORE FOR YOU" grid shows product names and prices, so shoppers get concrete items to click.
- The footer rewards ("EARN 10% BACK") and app promos give members a reason to engage.

## What's weak

- The top banner leads with the 25% upholstery sale, while the subject promises new arrivals. The message the subscriber opened for sits below the first screen.
- Two stacked discounts (25% and 50%) dilute each other, and both are "up to".
- The email is very long, with seven or more content blocks. The new-arrivals section alone is a long run of full-bleed images with little copy.
- There is no price, deadline or urgency on the new-arrivals products. The credits are the only hook.
- The legal fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny, and it hides the sale terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one job. Either lead with new arrivals and drop the 25% banner to a thin strip, or rename the send for the sale. The most impactful fix is aligning the hero with the subject.
- 2. Put the Designer-credit hero ("PREPARE TO WANT IT ALL") at the very top, and move the sale blocks below it.
- 3. Cut the two sale modules to one. Keep the 50% Fall Design Sale as a closing banner, and remove the 25% banner.
- 4. Add one price or "new" tag per featured product, so the new arrivals give a reason to click beyond looks.
- 5. Shorten the scroll. Show 3 products instead of 5 full-bleed scenes plus a 6-item grid.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The CB2 "New arrivals are here" email has a visible raw merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) and about 10 third-party pixels (Dotomi) loaded over plain `http://` with no alt text. Most of the other automated warnings (missing auth and unsubscribe headers) come from the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from this source.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- 69 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they sit on click-redirect domains, so their destinations are unverified.
- The Dotomi cookie-sync pixels are served over insecure HTTP:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- Clients that block non-HTTPS content will drop these 11 requests, and the cookie-sync they exist for will silently fail. Some clients may also show mixed-content warnings.
- The first-party tracking pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/a2e9b2a3-...?mi_cid=...` use HTTPS, so there is no transport issue there.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- 9 content images have no alt text. All are on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `48a7e47c-…jpg`
  - `c144ba07-…jpg`
  - `afe41c51-…jpg`
  - `ed51ed6b-…jpg`
  - `41583eb1-…jpg`
  - `24fcc1b7-…jpg`
  - `e4720f53-…jpg`
  - `6c21b732-…jpg`
  - `fe23441b-…png`
- If images are blocked, those sections will be blank, and screen readers will read out the filenames.
- The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel and the Dotomi pixels) also lack alt text. For 1×1 pixels, an empty `alt=""` is the right fix. It is a minor issue, but it adds noise for assistive tech.
- The head includes `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>`, which stops pinch-zoom on some mobile clients. That is an accessibility concern. I could only see the truncated head, so the body markup is unchecked.
- The HTML source was truncated, so I couldn't check the body for table structure, role attributes or Outlook/VML fallbacks. The `lang="en"` attribute and `<title>` are present.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **[FAIL]** The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is unresolved in the delivered HTML. This looks like a Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style `%%...%%` personalization string that didn't resolve at send time. It could render as visible text, or produce a broken review or ratings module.
- Nothing else in the visible source suggests other unresolved tokens.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported as not found. The QA notes say AgentMail may not pass these headers through, so this is **unconfirmed**. Don't treat it as a sender defect without checking the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** the header is absent and the relay is the likely cause. The status is unknown.
- I couldn't confirm the footer unsubscribe link or the physical postal address, because the truncated source doesn't include the footer. They need verifying.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- I can't assess this. The 69 tracking links were skipped, and the visible source doesn't show final landing URLs or UTM parameters.
- The links go through redirect domains, so UTM and landing page alignment should be checked by resolving a sample of them.

7. **Recommendations**
1. Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` merge failure. Add a fallback or suppress the module when the data is empty, and add a pre-send check that scans for leftover `%%...%%` tokens.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to `https://` endpoints, or drop the ones that are no longer used. Ten sequential cookie-sync calls is also a lot of third-party requests for an email.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
5. Check the raw message headers (not the relay copy) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 69 tracked links to confirm the landing pages and UTM parameters.
