# New! Holiday decor starting at $2.95
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a big, polished holiday-decor catalog send, and the subject doesn't match the body. The subject sells "Holiday decor starting at $2.95." The hero leads with "the Thanksgiving dining room event, up to 30% off," then moves to "everything to deck your halls." At the size I can view the render, I can't see the $2.95 price point called out anywhere. Whoever opens for the cheap-ornament hook lands on a different offer.
- The email is trying to do too much. It covers Christmas decor categories, garlands, ornaments, a "what's your holiday aesthetic?" quiz, a TheDesignDesk $5,000 sweepstakes, accent chairs and tables at 30% off, a glassware strip, a card-rewards promo, an app download and another TheDesignDesk appointment. The Christmas content is strong and seasonally right, but after the hero the eye has no single place to land.
- It's a competent, on-brand send that's too long and too scattered to be sharp. It's about the same as the recent Halloween and Thanksgiving catalogs from this brand, which scored 6–7.

## What's working

- The hero is lush Christmas imagery with a clear "Shop Holiday Decor" button above the fold.
- The 2x3 category grid (garlands & wreaths, stockings, decorative objects, ornaments, pillows & throws, candlelight) makes it easy to jump to a category.
- "New!" plus a starting-at price in the subject is concrete and short.
- The seasonal timing fits, with Christmas decor landing in late October.
- Brand voice and photography are consistent and trusted. There are no broken images or layout bugs.

## What's weak

- The subject's $2.95 hook isn't paid off visibly in the body, and the hero offer is Thanksgiving dining at up to 30% off. That's a mixed message.
- The email has too many CTAs and sections: two "Shop All" buttons back to back, a quiz, a sweepstakes, an app promo, and two TheDesignDesk blocks.
- TheDesignDesk appears twice with the same "Book a Free Appointment" button, which is repetitive.
- The glassware product strip looks unrelated to a holiday-decor story. Prices are small, and there's no urgency or deadline anywhere.
- It's the third or fourth Crate & Barrel send in a day or two (Venus Williams bedroom, Halloween shop, Thanksgiving dining). Subscribers will feel the cadence.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the subject and hero tell the same story. Put a "Starting at $2.95" ornament and decor module right under the hero with real products and prices, or drop the price from the subject.
- 2. Cut the email by about half. Remove the duplicate TheDesignDesk block, the app block, and the glassware strip, and keep the hero, the category grid, and one featured collection.
- 3. Use one primary CTA style and drop the duplicate "Shop All" buttons.
- 4. Add a deadline or a reason to act now, such as a decor sale end date or limited-run items.
- 5. Tag a few hero products with names and prices so the click lands on a specific item.

# Technical Audit: Crate & Barrel, "New! Holiday decor starting at $2.95"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML Transitional build with Outlook/VML fallbacks. The QA run scored 57% with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The real defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the capture path (the AgentMail relay), so they can't be treated as sender faults.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking or click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and final URLs are **unverified**. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- Per-recipient identifiers are embedded in image URLs:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png?mi_u=56aaa278…`
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/8fa7d4e9-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- The `rlcdn.com` pixel fires 5 times with an incrementing `n=`. That is a third-party (LiveRamp) sync pixel, so it is a privacy and deliverability consideration. It is not a bug.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm whether any `href` is empty, `#`, or non-HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged about 33 `<img>` elements with no `alt` attribute.
  - Content images: `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg` and `.gif` (for example `a6067ac2-…jpg`, `0fef3c31-…gif`).
  - The hero asset: `s7d5.scene7.com/.../2026%5F1016%5FCB%5FHolidayDecor%5FGrid%5Fhero.gif`.
  - Product or recommendation images: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render as nothing or as a filename.
  - Tracking pixels (`rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `o.gif`, `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…`) should carry `alt=""`. The attribute is missing there too, so screen readers may announce the filename.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" … maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resize:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on `*` and `body`, and `html` and `*` also set it. This prevents text resizing in Safari/iOS.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was never removed.
  - There are three overlapping `format-detection` meta tags: `telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`, `date=no`, and separate `address=no` and `telephone=no`. They are redundant.
- **Title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand. It is also generic, with no subject-specific text.

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the provided source or QA data. The only per-recipient values are the `mi_u`, `s=` and `mi_cid` / `mi_mid` parameters, which resolved to real hashes. The source is truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.
