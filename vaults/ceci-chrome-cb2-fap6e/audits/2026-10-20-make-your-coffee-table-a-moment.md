# Make your coffee table a moment
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a mood-led coffee table feature with no offer. The hero reads "THE STATEMENT TABLE," with a short line about two easy sizes and marble or acacia wood, and a single "SHOP COFFEE TABLES" button. Below it, "ONE TABLE, THREE LOOKS" splits into The Minimalist, The Collector and The Decorator. Each look gets a full-bleed room photo and a pair of styling accessories. I could only read the render at thumbnail scale (680x8008 shown at 170 wide), so I'm judging layout and structure, not fine print.
- The concept is the strongest part. Three styled personas give the send a real editorial shape, and the "make your table a moment" angle is more distinctive than the plain category sends CB2 has run this week. The weakness is that the commerce is thin. The accessory tiles (Semele Bowl, Vianot Sculpture, Bangle Bowl) are the only products tied to the theme, and there is no price visible. The coffee tables themselves never appear as priced, clickable products. A reader gets a styling lesson with no clear reason to buy today.
- The email also drifts after the feature. An Interior Design Services banner, a "More For You" grid of unrelated furniture, app promo, rewards and a registry/gift block make the lower half feel like generic footer filler. It matches the other recent CB2 sends, like "COLLECTED, NOT EXPECTED" on 10/19: pretty, but a catalog with no offer. Verdict: a good concept in a long, unfocused execution.

## What's working

- The three-looks structure (Minimalist, Collector, Decorator) gives the page a clear rhythm and a reason to keep scrolling.
- The hero is moody and on-brand, with warm amber glass and wood tones. The headline treatment is a recognizable CB2 style.
- Product callouts under each look (Semele Bowl, Bangle Bowl, Aela Bowl) give the styling ideas something shoppable.
- The "SHOP COFFEE TABLES" button appears twice, at the hero and after the looks, so the primary CTA is easy to find.
- The "More For You" grid shows product names and prices, which adds some commerce.

## What's weak

- There is no offer, price or urgency anywhere in the feature, so there is no reason to click now.
- The coffee tables, the supposed stars, never appear as individual priced products. The click goes to a category page.
- The hero's headline and body text sit over a busy photo, and the small copy looks hard to read.
- The lower half piles on unrelated modules: design services, a furniture grid, the app, rewards, a gift registry and 15% off signup. They dilute the single message.
- At about 8,000px tall, the email is long for one idea.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price anchor to the hero, such as "Coffee tables from $X" or a member offer, and put a priced table in each of the three looks.
- 2. Link each look's room photo to a specific table, and add a "Shop this look" CTA under each.
- 3. Cut the lower modules (design services, app, registry, 15% off signup) or move them into one slim footer row. Keep a short "More For You" grid only if it is coffee-table or living-room related.
- 4. Put the headline on a clean area of the image or add a subtle scrim so the hero copy reads on mobile.
- 5. Test one table-focused product row near the top, before the looks, to catch skimmers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Three coffee table looks, styled for you`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Coffee tables from $X: pick your look`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Minimalist, Collector or Decorator: find the table that fits your style.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Marble or acacia, two sizes, one statement piece.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, `lang="en"`, charset and viewport set) is structurally sound. The QA run found 0 hard failures and 3 warnings, but the compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML is truncated, so the body (footer, unsubscribe link, link hrefs) couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **68 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **11 insecure `http://` pixels (Dotomi/Conversant cookie-sync):**
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Clients that block mixed content or proxy images over HTTPS (Gmail's image proxy, Apple Mail Privacy Protection) may drop these, so the pixels won't fire reliably.
  - Ten sequential cookie-redirect pixels add 11 extra third-party requests per open.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/77f2a31a-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are HTTPS and look fine. Both lack alt text (see §3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images with no alt text (12)**, all on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `81b32065-…`
  - `81e7895b-…`
  - `700c200f-…`
  - `0fdf9afc-…`
  - `45700586-…`
  - `5d53ec78-…`
  - `d83303fa-…`
  - `f8b86499-…`
  - `6b48cc99-…`
  - `86ed8614-…`
  - `93a18159-…`
  - `d34dbbcf-…`
  - If these carry the hero or product imagery, image-blocked and screen-reader users get no content. QA doesn't say whether linked text is present as live text.
- **Tracking pixels with no alt:** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel should use `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. Their missing alt is low severity.
- **Zoom restriction:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template debris:** the leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the head. It's harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.
- **Malformed asset URLs:** several `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). `Lineto Brown Sub` is also declared with conflicting sources, mapping the light `.woff2` to weight 400. Clients that honor web fonts may fetch the wrong file or fall back, though the `Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback in `a {}` limits the damage.
- **Cross-brand font host:** `HNW05` loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain from the sending brand. It adds a dependency on a third-party host.
- **`<title>`:** it reads "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME", which doesn't match the subject line. This is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `mi_u=` parameter on `o.gif` is a hashed identifier and is populated. The body was truncated, so this applies only to what was visible.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check them against the raw original message.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. The From domain is `mail.cb2.com`, so check alignment on the raw message.
- **Footer not inspected:** the visible, working unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are in the truncated section, so they can't be verified here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM and parameter consistency** can't be assessed. All 68 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the final landing URLs weren't resolved.
- **Tracking domains:** the email uses `image.mail.cb2.com`, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com`, all subdomains of `cb2.com`. That is good for alignment. The Dotomi pixels are the only third-party tracking domains.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Pull the raw message (not the relay copy) and verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
2. Re-run the audit on the full HTML to confirm the footer unsubscribe link and postal address.
3. Resolve the 68 redirect links with a redirect-following check to confirm landing pages and UTM params.
4. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 `image.mail.cb2.com` images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
5. Switch the 11 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. Ten sequential cookie-sync calls per open are heavy.
6. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
7. Fix the `//Styles/` double-slash font URLs and the conflicting `Lineto Brown Sub` declarations, and remove the `IMPUT HERE` placeholder comment.
