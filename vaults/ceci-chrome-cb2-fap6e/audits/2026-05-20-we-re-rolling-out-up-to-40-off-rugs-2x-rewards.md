# We're rolling out up to 40% off rugs + 2x Rewards!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a category-specific follow-up to the broader "Up to 40% off furniture + 2x Rewards IS ON!" email Crate & Kids sent earlier today — same promotion, same 2x Rewards hook, but narrowed to rugs. The "rolling out" pun in the subject is a small win; the email is clean, the hero delivers the offer, and the product grid gives genuine browsing value. But the core problem is audience fatigue: this is the third email from the Crate & Barrel family today (alongside a Crate & Barrel design-desk send and the earlier Crate & Kids furniture push), and the rug-specific cut doesn't add meaningfully to what was already pitched this morning.
- The email is competently built. The visual hierarchy works — hero image up top, discount claim legible, product tiles below, CTAs present. But if you already opened the earlier furniture email, this one is redundant for every category except rugs. A subscriber who doesn't specifically care about rugs will feel double-tapped on the same day and mentally file it as noise. The send is defensible as a segment play only if the list was filtered to people who browsed or purchased rugs — nothing in the render suggests that targeting was applied.
- **Bottom line:** solid execution of a category slice, undermined by same-day send saturation from the parent brand.

## What's working

- The "rolling out" subject line pun is earned and on-brand — low-key clever without being try-hard.
- Hero image leads immediately with a lifestyle rug shot; the offer is visible above the fold without ambiguity.
- Product grid presents multiple rug styles (patterned, solid, textured) giving the browse-minded recipient something to scan.
- 2x Rewards callout is visible in the body, reinforcing loyalty value for members.
- No render bugs — layout is clean, images load, text is legible throughout.

## What's weak

- Third email from the Crate & Barrel family in a single day is objectively too much; the 2x Rewards mechanic is already stale from the morning send.
- The subject at ~51 characters clips on some mobile clients — "rugs + 2x Rewards!" risks being cut to just "rugs +" in narrow previews.
- No urgency deadline is visible in the render — "Memorial Day" is implied but no end-date anchor (e.g., "ends Monday") is called out explicitly.
- Category specificity (rugs only) limits relevance; no personalization signal visible to suggest this was targeted to rug-browsers vs. the full list.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut this email or delay it 24 hours.** Sending a rug sub-segment the same day as the full furniture push dilutes both sends. This should have gone out tomorrow as a "Last chance — rugs going fast" reminder, not a same-day duplicate.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline.** "Sale ends Monday" or "Through Memorial Day" in the hero copy would give this a reason to exist independent of the morning email.
- 3. **Tighten the subject** — drop "We're rolling out" to get under 40 chars on mobile while keeping the pun energy.
- 4. **Surface personalization** — even "Shop rugs for your little one's room" frames this as Crate & *Kids* rather than generic home goods.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Rugs: 40% off + 2x Rewards through Memorial Day`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hundreds of styles for kids' rooms — sale ends Monday night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `From playroom to nursery: your rug moment is now.`

## Technical Audit — Crate & Kids: Rugs Sale + 2x Rewards

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally standard (table-based XHTML Transitional, MSO conditionals, responsive breakpoints) with no critical rendering failures, but four webfont 403s will cause silent font fallback on load, and missing authentication headers prevent confirming SPF/DKIM pass status through the relay.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Webfont 403s (4 broken assets)**

All four hosted webfonts return HTTP 403:
```
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/1e07d600-3787-4517-bd3d-b140f9e54ec2.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/7ec25fd5-2752-4d84-b307-af3faabc21bb.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/aaa41944-25b3-4265-80b2-5564efe9b2d7.woff2
https://www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/fd5f8f50-d539-4a7d-b893-99501d746ef2.woff2
```
These are referenced via `@font-face` in the `<head>`. The 403 means the CDN or origin server is blocking direct hotlinking from email clients. Affected clients that attempt to load web fonts (Apple Mail, some webmail) will silently fall back to the declared stack — not a broken experience, but brand typography is not rendering as intended.

**22 tracking links skipped** — click-redirect URLs via `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com` were not probed; link health of destination pages is unverified.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text absent on all images (46 warnings)**

Every content image, including product shots, promotional banners, and the animated GIF, is missing `alt` attributes. Notable examples:
- `bbf6f9d4-86cc-437a-aa80-391067b74306.png` — likely hero/logo
- `ca6f2fd1-d174-4d5e-836e-f94430655fc3.gif` — animated promotional element

This is a WCAG 2.1 Level A failure (SC 1.1.1). It also means images-off rendering (common in Outlook, Gmail Promotions on mobile) produces a content-free email.

**Tracking/pixel images also missing alt** — The 1×1 open-pixel at `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (appears 6×) and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (appears 5×) all lack `alt=""`. These should carry empty alt to suppress screen-reader noise.

**CSS double `format-detection` meta declaration**

`<meta name="format-detection">` appears three times (once combined, then two individual overrides). While not functionally broken, it's redundant markup that indicates template cleanup is overdue.

**`max-scale=1` on viewport meta**

```html
<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
```
`maximum-scale=1` blocks user pinch-zoom — an accessibility regression for low-vision users on mobile. This is a WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.4 issue.

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header not found**

The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo (per their 2024 bulk sender requirements) require this header for senders exceeding 5,000/day. If the header is present at the MTA but stripped by the relay, this is a monitoring gap; if genuinely absent, it is a compliance violation.

**List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (RFC 8058)**

One-click unsubscribe (`List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) is absent or not captured. Gmail's bulk sender policy mandates this for commercial email. Without it, Gmail's native unsubscribe button either falls back to mailto or fails silently.

**Authentication-Results header not found**

SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status is unverifiable from the captured headers. Sending domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`; authentication alignment cannot be confirmed. This is a relay capture gap rather than a confirmed failure, but it should be resolved so audit records are complete.

**CAN-SPAM physical address** — not verifiable from the truncated HTML source; assumed present in footer (standard for Crate & Barrel). Recommend confirming in full source.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM parameters unverifiable** — All 22 destination links are behind click-redirect wrappers (`image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`). UTM decoration cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirects. The QA pipeline should be extended to follow redirects through the click-tracker and validate the final URL query string for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` on a sample of links.
