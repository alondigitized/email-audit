# $4,199 in prizes?! Yes, it can be yours…
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' second giveaway email in two days, and it escalates hard: yesterday's "Your chance to win a $1,000 Gift Card →" (2026-08-04, 7.00/10) has been replaced by a $4,199 prize pool spread across nine sponsor brands. Structurally this is a much better-built giveaway than yesterday's — the hero delivers exactly what the subject promises, the sponsor grid with itemized gift-card values is right there in the first screen, and there are two distinct "Enter Now" CTAs before the email pivots to merchandising. That's the opposite of the bait-and-switch shape flagged in "The best collab since 🍪 & 🥛" (2026-08-03, 8.00/10).
- The problem isn't this email in isolation — it's the sequence. Two giveaway-format sends in 48 hours (plus Crate & Barrel's own $1,000 giveaway the same day, 8/4) reads as a subscriber getting spammed with sweepstakes mechanics rather than product news. The email is also enormous: giveaway block, two merchandising banners, a six-item "we love these for you" grid, a credit-card pitch, an app-download pitch, and a design-service pitch — all stacked in one send. That's a lot of asks competing for one click.
- Net: well-executed as a single artifact, weakened by cadence. Call it a solid 7-8 on the individual merits, docked for showing up right on the heels of an identical mechanic.

## What's working

- Hero image and headline ("the ultimate back-to-school giveaway") land immediately and match the subject line's prize claim — no bait-and-switch gap.
- The sponsor grid itemizes exactly what's being won (Bumbleride stroller $799 value, $500/$400/$300 gift cards per brand) instead of hiding the breakdown — more transparent than the typical vague "$X in prizes" teaser.
- Two "Enter Now" CTAs bracket the giveaway block, so the ask isn't buried under scrolling.
- The "we love these for you" grid shows real prices next to real products (Nuna Pipa car seat $1,300, Jenny Lind dresser $1,499), giving a concrete reason to click even for someone not entering the sweepstakes.
- Visual hierarchy is clean throughout — no overlapping text or broken image blocks in the render.

## What's weak

- This is the second giveaway-format Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel send in two days — the cadence signal here is bad even though the creative itself is fine.
- "Sponsored in part by MOTHER" is confusing copy with no visual context — reads like a stray token rather than a named sponsor a recipient would recognize.
- Subject line doesn't reference kids/backpacks/back-to-school at all — it's a generic prize-amount hook that could belong to any Crate & Barrel family brand.
- No visible deadline anywhere in the giveaway block — "Enter Now" has no urgency backing it (no "ends Friday" or countdown).
- The email tries to do five jobs at once (giveaway, two sales banners, product grid, credit card, app download, design service) — that's a lot of competing CTAs after the giveaway block.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Space out the giveaway cadence** — running two sweepstakes sends back-to-back (8/4 and 8/5) trains subscribers to skim past "enter to win" emails. Space this by at least a week from the 8/4 send, or merge them into one bigger campaign moment.
- 2. **Add a real deadline to the hero block** — "Enter by 8/15" next to the button would convert the mild curiosity into actual urgency.
- 3. **Cut or relocate the credit-card/app/design-service pitches** — three unrelated asks after a giveaway and two sales banners is fatigue-inducing; save those for a dedicated send.
- 4. **Fix or clarify "Sponsored in part by MOTHER"** — either name the actual co-sponsor clearly or drop the line; as rendered it reads as a typo or broken merge token.
- 5. **Make the subject reference the category**, not just the dollar figure, to pull double duty on relevance and open rate.

- **Subject Alt B:** `9 brands. $4,199 in prizes. One entry.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, a Bumbleride stroller, and 7 more prizes — enter free`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Our biggest back-to-school giveaway yet: see everything up for grabs`
