# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-07-03
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 — this is literally my homepage. I didn't even have to squint to recognize the brand; the moody terracotta shadow-leaf hero is peak CB2 energy, and the July 4th Sale dropping 50% off is exactly the alert I have set on my phone. Right now the site is all sale, all the time — which is fine because that's why I'm here.

## What's working

- **The hero's restraint**: No corny patriotic imagery. A terracotta wash with shadow botanicals is *so* CB2 — I trust the taste level immediately.
- **"MORE ITEMS ADDED" above the headline**: Signals the sale is live and growing, which makes me want to move fast.
- **The 8-category shop grid**: Living Room, Dining Room, Bedroom, Rugs, Tabletop & Bar, Decor & Mirrors, Lighting — I can see my whole wish list at a glance and tap straight in.
- **The Fable Sectional module**: "Prepare to want it all" is the right register for me. And yes, I do want it all. The sectional lifestyle image is warm, styled, aspirational — the kind of photo I screenshot for my mood board.
- **Warehouse Sale callout in the announcement strip**: Up to 60% off on top of the main sale? That's a reason to open a new tab.

## What's weak

- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: The most conversion-killing friction for a high-AOV cart. If I have to hunt for it, I'll forget and abandon.
- **Zero social proof above the fold**: Not a single star rating or review count anywhere on the homepage. For a $1,800 sectional, I need some validation that it's not going to fall apart in six months.
- **"Up to 50% off" is doing a lot of lifting**: Without a specific anchored example ("The Fable Sectional, was $2,400, now $1,200"), it feels like a billboard promise.
- **No countdown timer on the sale**: It's July 3rd — this ends *tomorrow*. A simple "ENDS JULY 4TH AT MIDNIGHT" above the CTA would create real urgency. Right now there's no deadline scarcity beyond implication.
- **Loyalty program buried**: "Earn 750 Reward Dollars" is 8pt type in a scrolling strip. For someone who actually uses CB2 Rewards, this deserves a dedicated callout.

## Recommendations

- **Add a sale-end deadline below the hero CTA** — something like "Sale ends July 4th at midnight" or a live countdown chip. CB2 could ship this in an afternoon and it would meaningfully lift click-through.
- **Pull one anchored product example into the hero** — "The [item], marked down to $X" — so "50% off" has a real price attached to it, not just a vague promise.
- **Swap the announcement strip rewards mention for a proper sticky loyalty badge** — a small pill ("Your Rewards: 750 pts") in the header for logged-in users would make the loyalty hook work for repeat buyers like me.
- **Surface 4-5 star ratings on the category grid tiles** — even aggregate ("4.8★ across 1,200 rugs") would give me the reassurance to tap without second-guessing.
