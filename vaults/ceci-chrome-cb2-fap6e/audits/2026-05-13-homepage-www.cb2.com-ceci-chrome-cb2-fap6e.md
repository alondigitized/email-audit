# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is my brand — or it should be. Chrome accents, architectural furniture, the whole bit. But the first thing I see is a pop-up blocking literally everything, demanding my email AND my phone number before I've had one second to decide if I even like what's behind it. The hero appears to be running some kind of outdoor sale, there's a lifestyle editorial section visible below ("THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW"), and the vibe generally reads on-brand for me — but I can't actually see the homepage because a lead-capture wall is in the way.

## What's working

- **The 15% modal offer** is a meaningful incentive — not a vague "join our list," it's a concrete discount, which is the right hook for a deal-watching shopper like me
- **"THE OUTDOOR SALE" hero copy** (visible behind the modal) is seasonally timely — it's May, outdoor is exactly what I'm shopping
- **Navigation bar** is legible above the modal with clear category labels: SOFA, FURNITURE, OUTDOOR, LIGHTING, RUGS, DECOR — I can orient myself even with the popup live
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW"** editorial section below feels very CB2 — editorial lifestyle with real people, not mannequins, matches my aesthetic sensibility
- **"MEET THE COLLECTIONS" CTA** below the editorial section gives me a clear path to browse

## What's weak

- **The modal fires immediately** — I haven't seen a single product or had any reason to hand over my email AND phone number; this is aggressive even for someone who already likes CB2
- **The asterisk on "FULL-PRICE ITEMS\*"** is the classic bait-fine-print combo — I can see the asterisk, and the explanatory copy in the modal is tiny and dense
- **The entire hero is obscured** — "THE OUTDOOR SALE" copy is barely legible behind the modal; I can't see what the actual sale is or how deep it goes
- **No price points or specific products** are visible anywhere above the fold — it's all concept and no content
- **The lifestyle section below** ("THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW") is only partially visible and it's not clear who these people are or why I should care without scrolling

## Recommendations

- **Delay the modal by 8–10 seconds or one scroll** — let me see the homepage first; give me a reason to want the discount before demanding contact info
- **Drop the phone number field** — email-only captures convert better and feel less invasive; adding SMS opt-in as a secondary step after email capture will get you cleaner lists anyway
- **Surface the outdoor sale offer in a sticky banner** so the CTA persists after I dismiss the modal — don't bury the lead on a seasonal push
- **Add a "member pricing" or loyalty callout** in the nav or top bar so returning CB2 customers like me see immediate recognition — even a "Sign in for member perks" line would land better than nothing
