# Color theory, according to Scout Willis
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a well-made editorial send that undersells its own offer. The Scout Willis "Art of the (Small) House Party" feature is moody, warm and on-brand. Dark jewel tones, a mid-century room and a cluster of vases make it feel like a magazine spread. It sells a point of view before it sells a product.
- The money is buried. Guest Ready Sale (up to 40% off) and Warehouse Sale (up to 60% off) sit about 60% of the way down, after four full-bleed photo modules. This is the same Warehouse Sale CB2 pushed on 2026-11-02 in "The past is present" and on 2026-11-01 in "Final days of the Warehouse Sale." Someone who has seen those will skim past it again. The subject promises "color theory," but the body is really about a house and its furniture.
- Verdict: strong creative, weak commercial structure. It will get opens from design people and fewer clicks than a sale-first layout.

## What's working

- Scout Willis gives the send a real person and story, which the usual "Shop the Sale" blast lacks.
- The photography is consistent and rich, and the layout is clean. I can't see any broken images or overlapping text.
- The pull-quotes ("I really want to bring back house parties...") pair with specific "Shop Toron / Chiara / Ariana" buttons, so each room links to something concrete.
- The "More for You" grid shows real products with visible prices ($5.95 to $27.00), which gives price anchors.
- The footer carries the app download and a 10% rewards/credit offer, so member benefits are visible.

## What's weak

- The sale modules are below four editorial blocks, so the first screen has no price or urgency.
- Warehouse Sale and Guest Ready Sale are two separate sale blocks with no single deadline. I can't see any "ends Sunday" language.
- Every CTA is a small, similar "Shop X" button over dark photography. Nothing stands out as the primary action.
- "Color theory" in the subject isn't paid off visibly. The body talks about a house, not color.
- The rooms are shoppable by name only, with no prices or product callouts. The shopper has to click to find out what is for sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a slim sale banner above the feature, with one deadline and a single "Shop up to 60% off" button. Editorial can follow it. This is the biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the four-room scroll to two rooms and add 2-3 priced products under each, for example "Toron chair, $X."
- 3. Merge Guest Ready and Warehouse into one offer block so there is one decision.
- 4. Pay off the subject: call out the palette (plum, olive, rust) with a "Shop the color" row.
- 5. Make the CTA buttons solid, high-contrast and larger.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Template-level rendering is mostly sound, with 7 content images lacking alt text, 10 insecure (`http://`) third-party pixels, and a viewport setting that blocks zoom. Compliance and authentication could not be verified because the headers weren't captured and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 64 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels (10):** All load over plain `http://`, which Gmail's image proxy and mixed-content policies may block.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Third-party cookie-sync pixels:** The Dotomi `cookieredir/pub1–10` calls look like cookie-sync beacons. They add 11 third-party requests to the render, and any blocked ones fail silently.
- **Open-tracking pixels:** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/df86d411-...?mi_cid=...` are HTTPS and first-party, so there is no problem there. They are missing `alt`, covered in section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (7):** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `779acb6a-...jpg`
  - `aef0ddc2-...jpg`
  - `146b83ae-...jpg`
  - `f587618b-...jpg`
  - `00b00d3b-...jpg`
  - `ae4f97b8-...jpg`
  - `8436ffd4-...jpg`

  With images off, or for screen readers, these will be blank or announced as filenames.
- **Missing alt, tracking pixels (12):** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel, `UCMController`, and `pub1–10.php` have no `alt`. They should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` suppress user text scaling. The `!important` rule `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` in the first style block conflicts with these.
- **Conflicting `@font-face` declarations:**
  - `"Lineto Brown Sub"` is defined three times with different sources and weights. One of them uses the legacy `.eot`.
  - `"HNW05"` loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain. Font loading depends on cross-brand hosting.
  - Web fonts won't load in most clients, so the stack falls back to `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Template leftovers:**
  - The unreplaced placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, and "IMPUT" is a typo.
  - The empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }` in the MSO block does nothing.
  - The `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block is empty.
- **Markup validity:** `<meta name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting">` is not self-closed under an XHTML 1.0 doctype. This is minor and renders fine.
- **`<title>`:** `CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME` is generic and doesn't match the subject line. It has little impact in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixels carry a hashed `mi_u=` identifier, which is populated, not a raw token. The truncated body is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found [WARN]:** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, so it isn't a verified defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found [WARN]:** Same caveat. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules, so verify against the raw headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` missing [WARN]:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sending domain is `mail.cb2.com`, and alignment couldn't be assessed.
- **Footer not inspected:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated section, so CAN-SPAM body requirements are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. All 64 click-tracked links were skipped, and no final-destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible. Landing-page alignment with the subject "Color theory, according to Scout Willis" is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 7 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi `login.dotomi.com` and `ads.dotomi.com` pixels to HTTPS, or drop the cookie-sync pixels from the email.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` in favor of `100%`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) in the raw MIME headers. Capture `Authentication-Results` by reading the original message headers directly, not through the relay.
5. Resolve a sample of the 64 tracked links with a redirect-following check, to verify UTM parameters and landing-page status.
6. Clean up the template: remove the "IMPUT HERE" placeholder, consolidate the duplicate `"Lineto Brown Sub"` `@font-face` blocks, and delete the empty CSS rules.
