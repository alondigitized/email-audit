## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 table layout with VML/MSO fallbacks, and the visible source is mostly clean. The confirmed problems are widespread missing `alt` attributes (52 images flagged) and a zoom-blocking viewport tag. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified. The HTML provided was truncated, and the 97 tracking links weren't probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified:** 97 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unknown.
- **Third-party beacons:** five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels (LiveRamp) are embedded. They are extra third-party requests and may be blocked by privacy proxies or image blockers. This is informational, not a defect.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs:** `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` is appended to `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` and `/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`. A per-recipient token in image URLs gets exposed if the email is forwarded or archived. This is a design observation, not a bug.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 52 images flagged by QA):**
  - 37 are `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/…` content images (e.g. `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `6f9ddb32-…jpg`, `24335f96-…gif`).
  - 7 are `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images (e.g. `560d850e7e517ede.png`).
  - `971aa10f-…jpg` is also a content image.
  - The remaining 7 are tracking beacons: 5 × `rlcdn.com/448796.gif`, `o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/03242bdd-…`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, an image-heavy email like this will be mostly blank or announced as filenames. Beacons should carry `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resizing blocked:** the global `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` rules disable user text scaling on iOS. They also conflict with the later `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}`.
- **Invalid markup (minor):** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under an XHTML doctype, which should be `&amp;`.
- **Template cruft (minor):** the leftover placeholder comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. `format-detection` is declared three times, with conflicting values in the first declaration (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) and then separate duplicates.
- **Dark mode:** `color-scheme: light` is declared, which is intentional. No issue.
