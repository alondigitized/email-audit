# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is literally my store — and they immediately hit me with a phone-number shakedown before I can even see the sale. The Memorial Day Sale hero peeking around the edges looks stunning (that outdoor lounge situation is giving poolside editorial), but a mandatory-phone-field popup covering the entire screen is not the warm welcome a loyal CB2 person deserves. Brand is on, timing is right, execution is self-sabotaging.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale hero**: The outdoor lounge chair by a pool/reflective surface visible behind the modal is gorgeous — it's exactly the kind of aspirational lifestyle shot that makes me want to keep scrolling
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" module**: That section title alone is extremely CB2 and extremely me — stylish, slightly arch, not try-hard
- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE" nav tab**: Highlighted (looks red/orange) at the far right of nav — I see it even past the popup, and it's a direct path to the sale
- **15% offer clarity**: Whatever I think of the delivery mechanism, the offer copy is unambiguous — no "up to" hedging, no "select styles," just 15% off full price
- **Top banner loyalty mention**: "DOUBLE MEMBERS EARN $200" signals there's a rewards program, which is meaningful for a repeat shopper like me

## What's weak

- **Required phone number in the popup**: Asking for both email AND mobile phone before I've seen a single product is aggressive. I'd dismiss this before filling out the email.
- **Popup blocks 70%+ of the viewport**: The hero, the product imagery, the categories — all buried. This is a bounce trigger for anyone who came here to shop, not to subscribe.
- **Asterisk on the 15% offer**: "FULL-PRICE ITEMS*" with a fine-print block immediately underneath reads as bait. During a Memorial Day Sale — where presumably everything is on sale — this offer might apply to almost nothing.
- **Top banner is unreadable at mobile scale**: The "DOUBLE MEMBERS EARN $200" strip is too small; the benefit disappears.
- **No above-fold product browsability**: There's no visible category grid, no product rail — nothing to anchor shopping intent before the modal demands my personal info.

## Recommendations

- **Make the phone number optional, not required**: Drop the phone field to optional or remove it entirely from the initial capture. Reduce friction to one field (email) and move phone to a secondary step post-opt-in.
- **Shrink the modal to a bottom sheet or corner slide-in**: Let the Memorial Day Sale hero breathe behind it — the poolside lounge imagery sells itself. Letting me see the product context *while* the offer is live makes the 15% feel earned rather than extractive.
- **Rewrite the 15% copy to include the exclusion upfront**: Something like "15% off full-price styles — everything else is already on sale" is honest and actually makes the Memorial Day discounts feel stronger by contrast.
- **Surface the members double-earn banner at larger type**: Loyal CB2 customers need to see "DOUBLE MEMBERS EARN $200" clearly on load — that's a real loyalty hook, don't bury it in a 10px strip.
