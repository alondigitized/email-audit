## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built on a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with a mobile media query. Its main technical issues are about 70 images with missing or empty `alt` text, unconfirmed unsubscribe and authentication headers, and a link-heavy plain-text part.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 109 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they sit on click-redirect domains. Their destinations are unverified.
- Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels are third-party tracking beacons. They come from LiveRamp (rlcdn.com), not the sender's own domain. The URL parameter `s=56aaa2787a6773d0…` appears to be a per-recipient identifier, which is a privacy and deliverability consideration.
- The `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/…/o.gif` open pixel and the `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/73aa81f5-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` beacon are first-party tracking. Both carry `mi_u`/`mi_mid` identifiers in the query string.
- The same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` pixel appears six times, which is redundant.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't confirm anything about the `href` structure or UTM parameters.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text.** The QA check flagged about 70 `<img>` elements, including:
  - content images such as `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/eeb8adae-….jpg`
  - the animated GIF `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
  - the spacer `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`

  Content images need descriptive alt text. Spacers and tracking pixels should use `alt=""`. With images blocked, most of the email's content will be unreadable, and screen readers will announce filenames or URLs.
- **Mobile breakpoint.** The breakpoint is `max-width:460px`, which is unusually narrow. Many phones are 360–430px wide and should be covered, but larger phones and small tablets between 461px and 640px will get the desktop layout. The `@media (min-width:640px)` and `(min-width:768px)` rules also set `img {max-width: 600px/640px}`, which are conflicting caps.
- **Global line-height.** `*{... line-height: 100%}` is applied to every element, which can cause tight, clipped text.
- **Duplicate meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared three times in different forms, and both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present. This is harmless but redundant.
- **Placeholder comment.** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` was left in the shipped HTML. This is a template leftover.
- **Title.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid XHTML given the declared doctype. It is also generic.
- **Doctype and namespaces.** The doctype is XHTML 1.0 Transitional, and the VML/Office namespaces are present, which is fine.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found in the visible portion. There are no unrendered merge tokens (such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) in the supplied source or subject. The `mi_u` values are rendered per-recipient identifiers.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe.** The header was not found. The QA note says it may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** The header was not found. The same relay caveat applies. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so this is worth verifying at the source.
- **Authentication-Results.** The header was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Footer.** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so I can't confirm CAN-SPAM compliance for them.
- **Plain-text part.** URLs make up 21,624 of 30,749 characters, or about 70%. This is a deliverability signal and a poor plain-text experience. The tracking URLs are long.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Click destinations could not be checked. All 109 links are behind click-redirect domains and were skipped, and the source is truncated.
- No UTM parameters are visible in the supplied excerpt. Tracking appears to rely on `mi_*` parameters, which belong to the sender's ESP or analytics platform, rather than UTMs.
- I can't confirm landing-page alignment for the subject's featured nursery story, or whether the destination URLs resolve.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all content images (about 70 are missing it). Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels. Add `alt` text to the Scene7 GIF and the product tiles first.
2. Verify at the source that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` are sent, and that SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. The relay may be stripping these headers, so check the raw message from the ESP.
3. Deduplicate the six identical `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixels, and review whether five LiveRamp beacons are needed.
4. Shorten the tracking URLs, or reduce the number of links, to bring the plain-text URL ratio below about 50%.
5. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to confirm their destinations and check that UTM or equivalent campaign parameters carry through to the landing pages.
6. Fix the `&` in `<title>` to `&amp;`, remove the `IMPUT HERE` template comment, and remove the duplicated `format-detection` meta tags.
7. Raise the mobile breakpoint above 460px (for example to 600px) so that larger phones get the responsive layout.
8. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer unsubscribe link, the postal address and the `href` parameters.
