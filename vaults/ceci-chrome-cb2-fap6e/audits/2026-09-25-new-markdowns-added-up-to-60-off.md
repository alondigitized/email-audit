# New markdowns added! Up to 60% off →
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is a straight clearance blast — "New Markdowns Added! Up to 60% off clearance" over a moody, dark botanical/camo-print hero that's genuinely hard to read at a glance. The dagger footnote on "60% off" signals exclusions before you've even scrolled, and the category grid below (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) does the real work of making the offer feel real. Then the email pivots hard into a Halloween decor push, a "we love these for you" product carousel, an app download pitch with a $500 sweepstakes tease, a credit card pitch, and TheDesignDesk — five unrelated CTAs stacked after the one that matters.
- Compared to the brand's recent cadence — the Sept 24 nursery-furniture send and the Sept 22 baby-essentials close — this is a broader, less-targeted "everything's on sale" email with no persona-specific signal at all. It's honest in that the offer is clearly capped ("up to," asterisked), but the hero's low-contrast styling undercuts the one thing this email needs to do: sell "clearance" fast.
- Judgement: functional but unfocused — the offer is real and the category grid saves it, but the hero art fights the message and the email tries to do too much after the fold.

## What's working

- Clear, unambiguous category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Kitchen, Tabletop & Bar, Bedding & Bath, Decor) with a bolded "Shop All Clearance" CTA underneath.
- "Select styles. While quantities last." sets honest expectations right under the hero.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real prices ($649–$2,599) on six distinct SKUs (dresser, dining table, multiple sofas, dining chair) — concrete and browsable.
- No broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum — the render is clean throughout.
- Halloween decor section has a genuinely appealing, warm, in-season lifestyle photo (wreath, pumpkins, lanterns) that contrasts nicely with the murky hero above it.

## What's weak

- The hero image (dark, muddy green/camo botanical print) has poor text contrast — "New Markdowns Added! up to 60% off clearance" is legible but visually muted for the email's single most important message.
- The dagger (†) on "60% off" flags fine print in the very first screen, before any value has been established — a small but real trust ding.
- Six near-equal-weight CTAs after the fold (Halloween, product grid, app download, credit card, TheDesignDesk) dilute the clearance message the subject line promised.
- The "we love these for you" section claims personalization but shows generic bestseller-style furniture with no visible tie to browsing/purchase history — feels templated, not personalized.
- Product grid has no clearance pricing shown (no strikethrough/sale price), which is a miss directly beneath a "60% off" headline — undercuts the offer's credibility.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add strikethrough original price + sale price on the "we love these for you" grid — right now it's the biggest disconnect: headline promises 60% off, but the product tiles show plain, undiscounted-looking prices.
- 2. Lighten/brighten the hero image or add a solid-color text plate behind the headline so "up to 60% off clearance" pops instead of blending into the dark botanical print.
- 3. Cut the post-clearance stack from five sections to two — keep Halloween (seasonal, on-brand) and drop or move the app/credit-card/DesignDesk blocks to a secondary send; each dilutes the primary CTA.
- 4. Move or shrink the † disclaimer so it's not competing with the hero CTA for attention at first glance.
- 5. If this is meant to be segment-personalized ("we love these for you"), say so explicitly ("Based on what you've viewed") — otherwise drop the personalization framing since it isn't visibly delivering on it.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, rugs, decor & more — while quantities last.`
