# Can’t wait to get home? Neither can we →
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-27**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's biggest kitchen-sink send yet — bigger than "Something special is up to 60% off!" from 2026-07-26 (8.00/10). Where that one was at least anchored by a single clearance framing, this email stacks eleven distinct CTAs: bestsellers, living room, entryway, dining room, kids, TheDesignDesk free consultation, a Lupita Nyong'o home-makeover feature, a $500 giveaway, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, and an app download push. It's the full Crate & Barrel universe crammed into one scroll.
- The hero does its job — "can't wait to get home" pairs cleanly with a warm interior lifestyle shot and a clear "Shop Bestsellers" CTA sitting right under a "up to 60% off The Summer Sale" banner, so the top of the email reads well. The problem is everything after that: by the time a reader hits the Lupita Nyong'o section and the sweepstakes, the sale offer that opened the email has been buried under five unrelated pitches. This is a strong top-of-funnel open (recognizable sender, clean subject length, no spam signals) but a diluted click funnel — too many asks compete for the one click a reader will actually make.
- Verdict: solid execution, no render bugs, on-brand throughout — but it's trying to be six emails at once, which is exactly the pattern flagged in this brand's recent history (2026-07-26 "kitchen sink" scored 8.0 for the same reason).

## What's working

- The top banner + hero combo ("up to 60% off The Summer Sale" → "can't wait to get home") gives a clean, unambiguous entry point with a single visible CTA before any scrolling.
- No visual bugs: images render fully, text doesn't overlap, no lorem-ipsum or broken layout anywhere in the scroll.
- Section headers are specific and scannable ("a well-designed entryway that welcomes you in," "crafted to last & linger longer") rather than generic filler.
- The TheDesignDesk free-appointment offer and Lupita Nyong'o tie-in add a credibility/aspiration layer beyond pure discounting.
- Brand voice and visual identity (typography, photography style, logo placement) stay consistent from header to footer.

## What's weak

- Eleven CTAs across one email means no single offer gets the reader's full attention — the sale banner from the top is essentially forgotten by mid-scroll.
- The subject line ("Can't wait to get home? Neither can we →") never mentions the actual offer (60% off), so the email is selling an emotional hook the body doesn't consistently deliver on.
- No visible urgency or deadline anywhere in the render — "The Summer Sale" reads as evergreen, not time-bound, which undercuts click urgency.
- No reviews, ratings, or social proof visible on any product tile, despite a dedicated "we love these for you" product grid — a missed trust signal.
- The $500 giveaway and credit-card pitch are squeezed in late, competing with the app-download CTA right before the footer, diluting whatever CTA equity is left by that point.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to a single-topic send (the Summer Sale) or split it into two emails — one sale-led, one design-desk/Lupita feature-led. The brand's own history shows single-topic sends still hit 8.0+ ("Quality favorites at new prices," 2026-07-25) without needing eleven CTAs.
- 2. Move an actual deadline into both the subject and the top banner ("Summer Sale ends Sunday") — right now nothing in the render signals urgency, so there's no reason to click today versus next week.
- 3. Put the sale offer in the subject line itself instead of only the emotional hook — pair concreteness with the curiosity angle rather than choosing one.
- 4. Add a star-rating or "X,XXX reviews" line to at least the "we love these for you" grid — cheap trust lift, currently absent.
- 5. Demote the credit-card and app-download pushes to the footer only, freeing the mid-email real estate for the sale categories that are the actual revenue driver.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your home, 60% off: Summer Sale is here`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Living room to entryway — save up to 60% before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free design help, plus up to 60% off the pieces you've been eyeing`
