# UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, conventional "Early Black Friday Sale" send. A "Limited Time: Now up to 70% Off Clearance" banner sits above a big spaced-out "THE EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE, UP TO 60% OFF" hero with a single "SHOP THE SALE" button. Four category tiles follow (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor). The design is polished and on brand, but it is mostly a re-run of the 11/12 "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW + 2X REWARDS" send.
- The email fights itself on offers. The top banner says 70% off clearance and the hero says up to 60% off, so the eye has to pick one. The subject says 60%, but the first thing a reader sees is 70%. A $5,000 design-appointment sweepstakes and a Double Rewards block then compete for the same attention.
- The rewards block also shows what looks like a strikethrough: "GET ~~10%~~ 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS." The clearest pitch is buried near the bottom, and the footer fine print is long and tiny. Overall it is solid but undifferentiated, after a week of near-identical sale sends.

## What's working

- The hero is large and legible: "UP TO 60% OFF" is the dominant element, with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- The four full-width category tiles (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) give clear shopping paths and strong product photography.
- The "NOW THROUGH NOV 17TH, 2026" line on the rewards block gives a real, dated deadline.
- The cardholder rewards, app, gift registry and text-signup modules near the bottom are tidy and well organized.
- The moody, premium visual tone is consistent with the brand and with this week's lighting-led creative.

## What's weak

- The 70% clearance banner and the 60% hero give two different headline numbers, which muddies the single offer.
- The "10%" appears struck through in the rewards headline, and the apostrophe after "DOLLARS" looks odd. The rewards offer reads as confusing rather than a clean "20% back".
- The "WIN A $5,000 REFRESH" block is a large third promotion between the sale and the rewards. Its grey headline text over a dark photo has low contrast.
- The category tiles carry no prices or discount callouts, so there is no product-level reason to click.
- The footer legal text is long and very small, with several different offer exclusions, and the "up to 70%" and "up to 60%" claims are footnoted.
- The send repeats the 11/12 "BLACK FRIDAY PREVIEW + 2X REWARDS" content nearly beat for beat, so cadence fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one headline number. Either lead with "Up to 70% off clearance" or fold clearance into the 60% hero. Don't show both at the top.
- 2. Cut the $5,000 sweepstakes from this send or move it below the rewards block, so the sale and rewards run back to back.
- 3. Add a price or discount tag to each category tile (e.g., "Lighting from $49", "Up to 50% off").
- 4. Fix the rewards headline to a single clear value, "Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars", with no strikethrough.
- 5. Add one hero product with a price and a rating for social proof.
- 6. Move the rewards message up in the hierarchy, or add a one-line rewards callout under the hero.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday: up to 60% off starts now`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Lighting, rugs, furniture: up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 20% back in Reward Dollars with your CB2 card, through Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance now up to 70% off. Shop the best of the early sale.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with MSO conditionals and a mobile breakpoint at 418px. The main confirmed defects are 11 non-HTTPS Dotomi pixels, 8 content images with no alt text, and `maximum-scale=1` in the viewport meta. The source is truncated, so the footer and all `<a href>` values could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links could not be verified.** The QA run skipped 59 tracking links as redirect domains (no HTTP probe), so none were checked for 200 or final destination.
- **11 pixels use `http://`.** These are `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS images can be blocked or proxy-rewritten, and some clients flag them as mixed content. They are third-party cookie-sync calls (`cookieredir`), and ten of them fire back to back.
- **Tracking pixels are in place.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/c3cef24a-...?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` are both HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a 64-hex-character hash that identifies the recipient. I found no problem with it, but it is a per-recipient identifier in the URL.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **8 content images have no alt text**, all on `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `a3de039b-…jpg`
  - `a3207fdc-…jpg`
  - `49f9acc6-…jpg`
  - `8dada66a-…jpg`
  - `7a7ade6f-…jpg`
  - `4f5d677f-…jpg`
  - `e4289cf9-…jpg`
  - `345f2993-…jpg`

  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, those sections are blank or announced by filename.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">`** prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **The 13 tracking and cookie-sync pixels also lack alt text.** These are the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` pixel. Because they are decorative, they should have `alt=""`. This is lower severity than the content images.
- **A template placeholder is left in the `<head>`:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless, but it shows the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **`@font-face` mixes domains.** Some fonts load from `www.cb2.com` and others from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…`. A web-font failure falls back to `Helvetica, sans-serif` (per the `a` rule). I did not see a fallback stack on the `GT Sectra` or `HNW05` families in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The truncation means the body wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers really are absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found,** so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `mail.cb2.com` is a subdomain that matches the image hosts, which is consistent with a properly configured ESP, but nothing here confirms it.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked** because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 59 links are click-redirect URLs that were skipped, so final destinations, UTM parameters and landing-page match to the "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" subject could not be checked. Re-run with redirect-following enabled to resolve them.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Move the 11 Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or remove the `cookieredir/pub1–10.php` calls. Each one adds a third-party request per open.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 8 content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
4. Delete the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT…]` comment, and add explicit web-safe fallbacks for `GT Sectra` and `HNW05`.
5. Confirm the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a raw-message capture, since the relay may be dropping them.
6. Re-run the QA with redirect resolution and a full, untruncated HTML capture. That would cover link status, UTM continuity, and the footer unsubscribe link and address.
