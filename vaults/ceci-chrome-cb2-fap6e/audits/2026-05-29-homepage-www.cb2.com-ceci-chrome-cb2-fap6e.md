# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-29**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is doing exactly what I expect from them — confident, editorial, minimal — but the first thing I actually see isn't the Outdoor Sale they're pushing, it's an email-and-phone capture modal that's covering most of the page. The brand itself is on point for me, but the experience of landing here is basically "give us your digits first, then we'll let you shop." That friction is real.

## What's working

- **"THE OUTDOOR SALE" hero typography** — oversized serif on a moody outdoor lifestyle image reads high-end editorial, exactly the vibe I want from CB2.
- **15% off full-price offer** — as someone who watches the seasonal sale calendar obsessively, a concrete percentage (not vague "savings") gets my attention immediately.
- **"SHOP THE SALE" CTA on the hero** — visible even behind the modal, clearly labeled, no confusion about where it goes.
- **Accent chair section below fold** — the cream boucle + forest-green velvet chairs I can see partially rendered are genuinely on my shopping list energy. That "MORE THAN JUST AN ACCENT CHAIR" line lands.
- **Navigation bar** — a clear horizontal strip with Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor, Bedroom & Bath, Gifts, Sale. I can get where I want in one tap.

## What's weak

- **The modal is a wall** — requiring *both* email and phone number to access 15% off is aggressive. If I'm on mobile with autofill, maybe. If I'm not, I'm closing this immediately.
- **"Continue Without Saving"** — this shame-opt-out copy is a dark pattern and it makes me trust the brand a little less. CB2 is supposed to be cool; this feels like a Groupon move.
- **No loyalty or returning-shopper signal** — I've almost certainly shopped CB2 before, and the homepage has no idea who I am. No "welcome back," no rewards balance, nothing.
- **Asterisk on the 15%** — "FULL-PRICE ITEMS*" with tiny legal text in the modal is the fine-print energy I hate. CB2 has a sale section in the nav — does the 15% stack? It's unclear.
- **The "NEW FALL COLLECTION" text** visible in the lower-right section creates a slightly dissonant signal next to the current "Outdoor Sale" push.

## Recommendations

- **Drop the phone number requirement from the modal** — or make it optional. Requiring mobile number as a hard field next to email for a 15% off pop-up is a conversion killer; email-only pop-ups consistently outperform dual-field capture.
- **Swap "Continue Without Saving" for a neutral dismiss** — an "×" close icon or "No thanks" removes the manipulation signal and still captures people who genuinely want to subscribe.
- **Add a loyalty/returning-shopper strip to the top promo bar** — even "Members get an extra 10% on sale" or "Sign in to see your rewards" would differentiate me from a first-time visitor.
- **Make the 15% stack condition explicit in the modal** — one line: "Does not apply to items already marked down." Clarity > mystery; it's what a cool brand does.
