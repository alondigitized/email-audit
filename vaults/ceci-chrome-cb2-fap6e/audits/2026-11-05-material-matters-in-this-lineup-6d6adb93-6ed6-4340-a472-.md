# Material matters in this lineup
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- The send opens strongly with a "FINAL HOURS" banner and two big offers side by side: Warehouse Sale up to 60% off and Guest Ready Sale up to 40% off. Each has its own "SHOP THE SALE" button. The urgency is real and the hierarchy is clear. The subject line, "Material matters in this lineup," promises something else entirely.
- Below the banner the email turns into a "SO MUCH NEW" catalog of five product features: Ambroise chair, Davidson bed, Norrie chair, Enyo coffee table and Scatola coffee table. Then come a "SO MUCH SHIPS FREE" glassware block, a "More for you" grid of four products with prices, and an app and loyalty footer. The photography is moody, warm and consistent. But the email is long and carries several competing messages, and the most urgent one is not in the subject.
- This is a good creative with a mismatched subject. I'd call it competent, not targeted. A subscriber who saw "WAREHOUSE SALE ENDS TONIGHT" on 11/04 and "2 SALES END TOMORROW" on 11/03 will find this a third, quieter repeat of the same offer. It buries its best hook in the body.

## What's working

- The "FINAL HOURS" banner with two equal sale tiles (60% and 40%) puts the strongest offer first. Each tile has a visible button.
- The product features carry designer credits (Merremeata Esoudo, Studio Ananas), and each has a short line such as "Now as an accent piece." That gives the catalog some editorial weight.
- The photography is consistent and well lit, so it feels premium and on-brand.
- The "More for you" grid shows real prices, with a sale price and strikethrough on the counter stool ($499.00 → $424.00). That helps product-level clicks.
- The footer has an app download, Rewards Dollars (10% back) and a 15% off signup offer, so the loyalty hooks are present.

## What's weak

- The subject line has nothing to do with the sale. "Material matters" is vague, and the "FINAL HOURS" urgency never reaches the inbox.
- Two competing sales, a new-arrivals block, a free-shipping block and a recommendations grid all compete. There is no single hero offer.
- It's very long. Five stacked product features each have a small "SHOP NOW" link, and these are easy to miss against the large photos.
- The "SO MUCH NEW" and "SO MUCH SHIPS FREE" headers are nearly identical, which blurs the message. The free-shipping block's copy ("it all ships free") is small and low-contrast over the image.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. The sale terms ("up to") sit far from the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the urgency in the subject. Lead with the deadline and the number, since the body already does.
- 2. Cut the product features from five to two or three, and make the "SHOP NOW" links full-width buttons.
- 3. Give free shipping a single clear line near the top rather than its own large block.
- 4. Move the sale fine print closer to the banner, with a short summary of exclusions.
- 5. Vary the creative from the previous two days' sale-ending sends so the cadence doesn't feel repetitive.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: up to 60% off the Warehouse Sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: 60% off + new arrivals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus 40% off Guest Ready and free shipping on glassware.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call on two sales, and the new chairs you've been eyeing.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is a standard table-based, Marketing Cloud-style template with a `lang="en"` attribute and MSO conditional comments. The QA run found 10 content images and 13 tracking pixels missing `alt`, 11 third-party pixels loaded over `http://`, and a viewport meta that blocks zoom. The compliance headers and authentication results could not be verified, and the HTML was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are untested.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi (Conversant) pixels use plain `http://`.
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Gmail proxies these through HTTPS, but other clients may block or warn on them. The `cookieredir/pub1–10` pattern is a cookie-sync chain that fires 10 extra third-party requests per open.
- **Identifier in URL:** the open pixel `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=56aaa2787a...cfd8&mi_...` carries a 64-hex-character `mi_u` value. It looks like a SHA-256 hash of a user identifier, probably the email address, in a URL query string.
- **Other pixel:** `https://dv.cb2.com/o/b17bc8e3-ccca-455e-ac39-f4d34f677f60?mi_cid=...&mi_mid=...` is a second tracking beacon alongside `o.gif`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** 10 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`.
  - `04404684-b4f7-4b14-b829-7fbbb4cd6205.jpg`
  - `1496b9b9-0a0b-43f8-a87a-a2d9fed1e33f.jpg`

  - `dbb13b0e-2a12-43b6-9ced-89d75b602ff2.jpg`
  - `b612945b-420c-48a4-8353-a64bd8b9acb4.jpg`
  - `90df007c-400c-410c-a7fe-2c458b7d1c96.jpg`

  - `429a7938-375a-4431-823e-7435ba1f9fbd.jpg`
  - `8087f594-5bb7-42ce-96d3-70af6eca9466.jpg`

  Screen readers will announce filenames, and with images blocked the email shows blank space. If these are the lineup/product images, the email's message is lost.
- **Pixels missing alt:** the Dotomi pixels, `o.gif` and the `dv.cb2.com` beacon all lack `alt`. These should use `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="... maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom on mobile, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in the body and universal selector rules, and the `*{... line-height:100%}` rule applies to every element.
- **Leftover placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It is invisible but unprofessional. There is also an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
- **Fonts:** web fonts load from `cb2.com`, and the HNW05 `@font-face` rules load from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain. Most email clients ignore `@font-face`, so these fall back to Helvetica/sans-serif. The `<title>` ("CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME") also doesn't match the subject line.
- **Truncated source:** the HTML was cut off, so body structure, button markup (VML/bulletproof) and dark-mode handling could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) appear in the visible source. The body was truncated, so merge tokens in the greeting or body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** the QA run did not find the header. It notes this may be an AgentMail relay capture limitation, so this is unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe, so confirm it against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain of the brand domain, which is a good sign but not proof.
- **Footer:** the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not in the truncated source, so they are unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The 66 tracking links are wrapped in redirects, and the skipped probe means the final landing URLs and any UTM parameters are not visible. No UTM evidence appears in the available source. The landing pages for "Material matters in this lineup" cannot be checked against the email.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images, and `alt=""` to all pixels.
2. Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10-hop `cookieredir/pub1–10` chain if it isn't essential.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta and reconsider `text-size-adjust:none`.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment on `mail.cb2.com`.
5. Review the `mi_u` hashed identifier in the open-pixel URL for privacy.
6. Remove the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT` placeholder comment.
7. Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML and a redirect-following link check, so landing URLs, UTMs, merge tokens and the footer can be verified.
