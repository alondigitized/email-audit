# Two sales end tonight
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-05**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's tried-and-true "two-stack discount" mechanic — the same clean, urgency-driven format that hit 9/10 twice last week (8/1 and 8/4). Two nearly identical "ENDS TONIGHT" hero blocks stack back to back: first "THE SEMI-ANNUAL BEDROOM + BATH EVENT" over a warm, styled bed shot, then "UP TO 40% OFF" over a live-edge wood table. Both are clean, on-brand, and instantly legible.
- The subject line "Two sales end tonight" is honest and matches the body exactly — no bait-and-switch — but it's also the least interesting subject CB2 has sent recently; it reads more like an internal label than a hook. Below the fold, the email does real work: a $1,000 sweepstakes banner, six "More for you" products with visible strikethrough pricing, and a tidy loyalty/app/SMS footer block.
- This is a solid, on-brand execution of a proven playbook, but it's the fourth "ends tonight / final weekend" urgency send in six days (8/1, 8/1, 8/4, now 8/5) — the urgency is starting to feel structural rather than real, and stacking two nearly identical hero treatments in one email dilutes rather than reinforces the message.

## What's working

- Two clear, unambiguous "SHOP THE SALE" CTA buttons, both immediately visible without needing to hunt.
- Visible strikethrough-to-sale pricing on 4 of 6 "More for you" products ($999→$849, $399→$349, $499→$349) — concrete, credible savings.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is crisp, layout is straight and well-aligned.
- The $1,000 sweepstakes banner is a nice pattern interrupt from the two discount blocks and echoes CB2's other differentiated sends (8/2 Cubs sweepstakes).
- Footer bundles loyalty (10% back in Rewards Dollars), app download, and SMS signup (15% off) into one scannable block.

## What's weak

- The two hero sections ("Semi-Annual Bedroom + Bath Event" and "Up to 40% off") are visually redundant — same "ENDS TONIGHT" eyebrow, same white CTA button, same dark-moody-room aesthetic — so the eye doesn't know which sale is the "real" one.
- Subject line is generic and undersells the visual: "Two sales end tonight" gives no percentage, no category specificity beyond "sales," and no reason to open now over any other CB2 email this week.
- No preview text — the inbox will show fallback/junk content, wasting the second line real estate CB2 uses well elsewhere.
- This is the third "ends tonight/final weekend" send since 8/1 — the urgency is losing credibility from repetition, and today's send doesn't distinguish itself from 8/4's "ENDS TOMORROW: BED & BATH ON SALE," which already covered this exact category.

## Recommendations

- 1. Merge the two hero blocks into one — lead with "UP TO 40% OFF BEDROOM + BATH, ENDS TONIGHT" as a single hero, since they're advertising overlapping/related sales. Splitting them just doubles scroll without doubling clarity.
- 2. Write a subject that carries the actual number, since "40% off" is the strongest asset in the email and it's currently buried in the body only.
- 3. Add real preview text — right now it's leaking junk/absent, which wastes free inbox real estate that CB2 uses well in other sends.
- 4. Vary the urgency cadence — after three "ends tonight/tomorrow" sends in five days, consider swapping tomorrow's format back to the editorial/designer-spotlight playbook to avoid urgency fatigue.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off bedroom + bath — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 40% off bedroom & bath`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Two sales, one deadline — both wrap at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop bedroom, bath, and more before the clock runs out.`
