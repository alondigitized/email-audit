# First look: Our holiday 2026 collection!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel opening its holiday season with a genuine "first look" — a wall of wreaths in deep greens, reds, and creams sets the mood immediately, and the category grid below it (Christmas decor, ornaments, stockings, garlands, tabletop, bedding) gives shoppers a real map of what's coming rather than one hero product. It's a strong seasonal-kickoff email: on-brand styling, clear navigation, and enough variety to reward scrolling.
- Where it loses points is focus. After the wreath hero it sprawls into Design Desk, a Caraway coffee maker preorder, a six-item "we love these for you" grid, a credit card pitch, an app download plug, Design Desk again, and a footer mega-menu — none of which is holiday-specific. The coffee maker in particular reads like a random cross-sell dropped into a Christmas email. Compared to the brand's recent run — the entryway and nursery-chair editorials that stayed tightly on one theme — this send trades focus for breadth, and the back half feels like a template stuffed with whatever modules were on hand rather than a curated holiday reveal.
- Verdict: a genuinely eye-catching seasonal opener that undercuts itself by not staying on-message past the first screen.

## What's working

- The wreath-wall hero photo is seasonal, cohesive, and immediately legible as "holiday has arrived."
- "first look! holiday 2026" pairs a clean headline with urgency copy ("the best finds always sell out fast") without shouting.
- The 6-tile category grid (Christmas decor, ornaments, stockings, tabletop, garlands & wreaths, bedding) gives a clear, scannable path into the collection.
- "Shop All Christmas" is a specific, unambiguous primary CTA button, well-placed right under the grid.
- The "we love these for you" product grid shows real prices and specific named items (ornaments, garland wrap, advent calendar) with no render errors.

## What's weak

- The Caraway coffee maker preorder module has nothing to do with holiday decor and breaks the theme established by the hero.
- Design Desk appears twice (once mid-email, once near the footer) with near-identical copy and CTA, which feels repetitive rather than reinforcing.
- No visible discount or dollar/percent offer anywhere in the email — "1000s of items ship free" is the only concrete value prop, and it's generic, not holiday-specific.
- The email is very long with many competing modules (Design Desk, coffee maker, product grid, credit card, app download, Design Desk again) that dilute the strong holiday hook from the top.
- The Reward Dollars/credit card module and app-download module sit awkwardly back-to-back late in the email, both unrelated to the "first look" premise.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half: keep the wreath hero, category grid, and product grid; move Design Desk, the credit card pitch, and the app download into a separate send — a holiday reveal should stay a holiday reveal end to end.
- 2. Drop or relocate the Caraway coffee maker block — it directly contradicts the "holiday decor first look" premise and should live in its own product-launch email.
- 3. Remove the duplicate Design Desk placement; one clear appointment CTA is enough.
- 4. Add a concrete offer (e.g., "up to 30% off ornaments" or an early-access discount code) — right now there's no price incentive to click today versus next week.
