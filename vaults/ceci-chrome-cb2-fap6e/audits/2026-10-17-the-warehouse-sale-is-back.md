# THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is a clear, price-led send. "UP TO 60% OFF" is the biggest thing on the page, set against a moody dark floral hero with five stacked buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs, Shop All). The subject matches the hero, so the open-to-click path holds together. Compared with the Oct 17 "Holiday, meet high design" mood send and the Oct 16 "FLOOR SAMPLE SALE" send, which had no real discount, this is the strongest offer CB2 has sent in the recent run.
- Below the hero it falls apart. A second sale (Dining Event, up to 30% off), a Casa Dean sweepstakes, and four utility tiles (app, rewards, gift list, texts) all compete for attention. There's no deadline in the body copy, and "Warehouse Sale" doesn't say what is actually on sale. The 60% sits in a thin light font on a busy dark image and carries a fine-print dagger.
- Verdict: a solid, above-average promo. The offer is right, but the email has too many jobs and no urgency.

## What's working

- "UP TO 60% OFF" is huge and instantly readable. It's the best offer CB2 has led with in recent sends.
- The hero's subhead, "A curated selection of last-chance markdowns," gives some scarcity framing.
- The category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Decor, Rugs) let a shopper jump straight to their interest.
- The subject and hero match, with no mismatch like the one that dragged down earlier sends.
- The rewards tile ("Earn 10% back") and the app and text-signup blocks add useful member hooks.

## What's weak

- Five full-width hero buttons look like a menu, not a decision, and "Shop All" isn't visually distinguished as the primary action.
- The Dining Event block repeats the same sale story. "THE SEMI-ANNUAL" sits over a busy photo with low contrast.
- The Casa Dean sweepstakes ("Win the new collection") is a third, unrelated message, and "Enter Now" competes with the sale.
- No end date is visible outside the fine print, so there's no urgency. The Oct 14 "FINAL HOURS" countdown send had far more.
- The email is about 4,100px tall, with a heavy legal block and four utility tiles after the real content.
- No products are shown in the hero, so you can't tell what 60% off buys.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one or two real products with sale prices in the hero. Cut the button stack to one primary "SHOP THE WAREHOUSE SALE" button plus a text-link row for categories.
- 2. Add a visible end date or countdown near the 60% ("Ends Oct 28"), since you already have it in the fine print.
- 3. Merge or drop the Dining Event block. Move the sweepstakes and the app, rewards and texts tiles into a slim footer strip.
- 4. Raise the contrast on the thin "60%" and on the Dining Event headline.
- 5. Make the sender name more specific than "CB2 SALE" if it's possible without hurting deliverability, or at least keep it consistent with the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Warehouse Sale: up to 60% off, ends Oct 28`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last-chance markdowns, up to 60% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs and decor, while it lasts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, rugs and lighting at warehouse prices, through Oct 28.`

# Technical Audit: CB2, "THE WAREHOUSE SALE IS BACK"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect, an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has accessibility gaps (7 content images with no alt text, `maximum-scale=1`) and 10+ insecure `http://` Dotomi tracking pixels. Compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured. The HTML source was truncated, so findings are limited to what the source and QA data show.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** All 62 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they are on click-redirect domains. Destinations, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** Eleven Dotomi (`dotomi.com`) pixels load over plain `http://` and have no alt text:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
  - Mail clients that proxy images, or that block mixed content, can drop these. Tracking and cookie-sync would then fail silently.
- **Many third-party beacons:** The email also carries `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/3f009f7d-...?mi_cid=...`. Both lack alt text. The user-level hash in `mi_u=` is expected for this kind of pixel.

## 7. Recommendations
