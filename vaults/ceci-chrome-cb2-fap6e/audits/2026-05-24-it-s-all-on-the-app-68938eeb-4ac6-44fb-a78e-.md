# IT’S ALL ON THE APP
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a pure app-install push from CB2, and it commits to the bid hard — no product hero, no offer, just "MEET THE CB2 APP" and a Download Now button as the entire above-the-fold. That's a confident editorial choice but it's a tough ask in a promo inbox where the prior four sends have been "Pieces worth lusting after" and "future classics on sale." The reader was trained to expect a sofa; they got an app pitch.
- The mid-section ("GO AHEAD, PLAY FAVORITES") is the strongest part of the email — three lifestyle vignettes with the favoriting heart icon overlay show, not tell, what the app actually does. But it sits below the fold, after the reader has already decided whether to download. The lower stack (rewards signup, list signup, social) is housekeeping crammed in to make the send feel useful.
- Net: a cleanly designed but functionally narrow email. For a non-app-user, this is a skip. For someone already debating the app, the favoriting demo is actually a decent nudge.

## What's working

- Clean hero composition — the phone mockup nested in a lifestyle still life is a nice "app meets home" visual metaphor.
- The four-up benefits grid (Get Exclusive App Items, Sale Early Access, Personalized Picks, Track Rewards Renewed Rollouts) is scannable and answers "why install."
- The favoriting demo with the red heart icon overlay is the single best persuasion moment — it shows the product, not the pitch.
- Layout is tidy, typography hierarchy is consistent with CB2's brand voice.
- Includes both rewards signup and email signup as fallback CTAs for non-installers.

## What's weak

- No offer. After a week of "early access" and sale framing, an app pitch with no install incentive feels weak.
- Subject in shouty caps ("IT'S ALL ON THE APP") is vague — what's "it"?
- The four benefit tiles are tiny and hard to read at mobile scale; the labels look truncated.
- The favoriting demo is below the primary CTA — the most persuasive content is buried.
- Bottom housekeeping stack (rewards + email signup + social) makes the email feel like a catch-all rather than a focused app-install send.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add an app-exclusive incentive above the fold.** "Download the app, get 15% off your first in-app order" or "App-only: early access to the Memorial Day drop." Without an incentive, this is asking for a download with no return.
- 2. **Swap hero and favorites demo.** Lead with the favoriting interaction shot (real product, real behavior) and put the phone-mockup hero second. Show value before asking for the install.
- 3. **Rewrite subject for specificity.** Tell the reader what's on the app that isn't in email.
- 4. **Cut the email-signup block** — if they're reading this email, they're already on the list. That tile dilutes the focus.
- **Subject Alt A:** `App-only: early access to the Memorial sale`
- **Subject Alt B:** `15% off your first order — in the CB2 app`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save favorites, unlock app-only items, track rewards from your pocket.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale early access + personalized picks live in the app.`
