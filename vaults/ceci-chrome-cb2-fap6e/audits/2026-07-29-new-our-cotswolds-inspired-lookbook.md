# NEW | Our Cotswolds-inspired lookbook
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel doing something different from the run of kitchen-sink sends this week — a genuine single-collection lookbook. Where "Can't wait to get home?" (2026-07-27, 9.00/10) and "LAST DAY for up to $200 off select Breville!" (2026-07-26, 9.00/10) stacked five unrelated categories into one email, this send stays anchored to one story: the Cotswolds Collection, walked room by room (living room, bedroom, dining room, kitchen) with consistent English-countryside styling throughout.
- The execution is clean — no render bugs, tight photography, a logical room-by-room CTA structure. The one tension is framing: the top banner reads "up to 60% off The Summer Sale," but everything below it is presented as an aspirational new-collection lookbook, not a clearance event. Those two messages don't fully reconcile — is this a sale or a launch? The email also runs long, tacking on a celebrity DesignDesk partnership (Lupita Nyong'o) and a $5,000 sweepstakes after the core lookbook content, which dilutes the single-collection focus it otherwise nails.
- Net: a well-built, visually cohesive send that's a refreshing break from the brand's recent kitchen-sink cadence, undercut by a muddled sale/lookbook framing and no urgency anywhere in the copy.

## What's working

- Cohesive single-collection theme (Cotswolds) instead of the mixed-category stacking seen in recent sends this week.
- Room-by-room structure with specific CTAs ("Shop Living Room," "Shop Bedroom," "Shop Dining Room," "Shop Kitchen") makes the email easy to scan and act on.
- Consistent, well-styled lifestyle photography across every section — no jarring shifts in tone or lighting.
- The Lupita Nyong'o / TheDesignDesk tie-in plus $5,000 sweepstakes gives readers a reason to engage beyond straight product-shopping.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout breaks anywhere in the scroll.

## What's weak

- The "up to 60% off Summer Sale" banner at top contradicts the lookbook framing below it — unclear if the collection itself is discounted or if it's an unrelated promo bolted on.
- Zero urgency or deadline anywhere, despite leading with a "sale" callout.
- No loyalty/member pricing surfaced, even though Crate & Barrel has a loyalty program.
- The email runs very long (10+ distinct sections); by the time a reader reaches the sweepstakes block, most will have already dropped off.
- No reviews or star ratings on any featured product, despite showing named, specific pieces.

## Recommendations

- 1. Resolve the sale/lookbook tension — either commit to sale framing ("Cotswolds Collection: up to 60% off, ends Sunday") or drop the discount banner and let the lookbook sell on aspiration alone.
- 2. Add a credible deadline to the sale banner — a bare "up to 60%" with no end date reads as always-on and doesn't drive action.
- 3. Trim the email — cut it after the four room sections plus one CTA block; move the DesignDesk/sweepstakes content to its own send so it isn't competing for attention.
- 4. Surface loyalty pricing on at least one hero product to reward existing members.
- 5. Add a review count or star rating to 2-3 hero pieces to build purchase confidence.
