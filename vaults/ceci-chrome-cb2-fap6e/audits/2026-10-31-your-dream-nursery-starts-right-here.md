## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based XHTML Transitional layout with MSO/VML fallbacks and a mobile breakpoint at 460px. QA shows a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The real findings are widespread missing image alt text, a viewport meta that blocks zoom, and several things the pipeline couldn't verify: unsubscribe headers, authentication, and the 89 tracked links.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** QA skipped the HTTP probe on 89 tracking/click-redirect links, so destination status, redirect chains and UTM preservation are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Extra tracking pixels:** The email loads several pixels in addition to the click links:
  - 5 `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` (LiveRamp)
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/3122d49f-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
  - 6 repeated `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…`
- **Why it matters:** These are third-party and per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u`). Image proxies and Apple MPP will prefetch them, which inflates opens. The five `rlcdn` pixels add third-party requests and slow the render.
- **Source hygiene:** The `<head>` still contains the template placeholder `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`. It is harmless but shows the template wasn't cleaned.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, confirmed by QA:** About 46 `<img>` elements are flagged.
  - Content images (the bulk): 31 under `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` and `/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`, including the GIFs `e05da4a7-….gif`, `57ac4065-….gif` and `cd2cef02-….gif`.
  - Scene7 assets: `20261101%5FCBK%5FFall%5FClearance%5FPM%5Fv3%5FTert` and `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite`.
  - Tracking/decorative pixels: the `rlcdn`, `mi.crateandbarrel.com` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` images.
  - With images off, or in a screen reader, linked content images have no accessible name. Pixels and spacers should carry an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Text resize disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears in `body` and `html`, and `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;…}` applies it to every element. This prevents iOS text scaling.
- **Invalid markup:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the XHTML doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Duplicate head tags (minor):** `meta charset` is declared twice, and `format-detection` is declared four times with conflicting values.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values are resolved. The HTML is truncated, so the footer and body were not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA could not find `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). QA itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`, which is consistent but not proof.
- **Body unsubscribe link and physical address:** Not verifiable in the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 89 links were not probed, and the truncated source shows no UTM parameters or landing-page URLs. The only parameters visible are on image and pixel URLs (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), none of which are campaign UTMs. I found no confirmed continuity defect.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to every content image, and add `alt=""` to tracking pixels and spacers.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`, and drop the `*` selector rule.
4. Escape the title as `Crate &amp; Barrel`, remove the duplicate meta tags, and delete the `[IMPUT HERE…]` placeholder.
5. Review the five `rlcdn` pixels and the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel, and cut any that aren't needed.
6. Capture the raw message headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original delivery rather than the relay copy, to settle the three QA warnings.
7. Resolve a sample of the 89 tracked links with a redirect-following probe to confirm destinations and UTM carry-through.
