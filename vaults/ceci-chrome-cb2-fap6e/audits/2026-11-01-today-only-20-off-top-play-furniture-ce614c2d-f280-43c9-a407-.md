# Today only: 20% off top play furniture!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises "Today only: 20% off top play furniture!" The hero delivers a nursery scene and "now in our app: 20% off top play furniture, today only!" The offer is concrete and time-bound, but the CTA is "Download Our App," not "Shop play furniture." The deal is tied to an app install, which adds friction. The subject never mentions that.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. It has gift bundles, bedding with a "Ship's Free" banner, a heirloom gifts block, a product grid, a credit card promo, an app-sweepstakes block ("chance at $500") and a TheDesignDesk pitch. Play furniture appears nowhere in the product grid, which has a pumpkin treat bag, strollers, a glider, a dresser and an activity chair. The Nod chairs and play tables the hero mentions are absent. The one-day urgency is lost by the second scroll.
- The email is on-brand and cleanly built, but it is too long and has too many competing asks. It lands in the usual 5-6 range. It follows the Oct 31 "Dining Room Event ends TONIGHT" send, where the urgency also didn't carry into the body. This is the same pattern again.

## What's working

- The subject is specific: a 20% discount, a category and "today only."
- The hero text is large and readable on a muted green block, so the offer is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The product grid shows real prices, including strikethrough sale prices such as $34.95 → $14.97.
- The sender is the recognizable Crate & Barrel Kids brand, with a consistent look and voice.
- The footer navigation is tidy and the section buttons are easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The primary CTA, "Download Our App," pushes an install instead of showing the product.
- The product grid has no play furniture, so the hero promise is not backed up.
- The offer is buried in a hero that is mostly image, and the fine print at the bottom is long and dense.
- The email carries too many competing messages: gifts, bedding, credit card, sweepstakes, design service and app.
- The "heirloom-quality gifts, made for wish lists" banner has weak contrast, with white text over a busy photo.

## Recommendations

- 1. Change the hero CTA to "Shop 20% off play furniture" and link to the play furniture category. Keep the app as a secondary line.
- 2. Replace the product grid with 4-6 actual play furniture items, such as Nod chairs and play tables, with sale prices.
- 3. Add a countdown or an "ends at midnight" line to the hero so the "today only" claim holds up in the body.
- 4. Cut the credit card, sweepstakes and TheDesignDesk blocks from a single-offer send.
- 5. State the app requirement in the subject or the preheader if it applies.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20% off play furniture ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Nod chairs & play tables: 20% off today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Get ahead on gifts. Select styles, today only, in the app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Soft seating and play tables for tiny hangouts, 20% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound, with an XHTML doctype, a MSO/VML fallback and responsive media queries. The confirmed defects are widespread missing `alt` attributes (22 flagged images) and a viewport tag that blocks zoom. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 63 tracking links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so none were probed. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Repeated pixels:** the same `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` image is requested 6 times. Five `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1..5` pixels (LiveRamp) are also embedded.
- **Extra pixels:** there is also a `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` open pixel and a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/26db97ac-…` beacon.
- **Cost of the pixels:** they add roughly 13 extra requests per open and rely on remote image loading. Some clients block them, and Apple MPP inflates them.
- **Stale asset:** `2026_1002_CBK_GiftGuide_SecondaryStory.gif` has an October 2 filename in a Nov 1 send. This may be an intentional reuse, but the asset path is worth confirming.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 22 `<img>` tags with no `alt`.
  - **Content images** (these need real alt text or `alt=""` if decorative):
    - `bbf6f9d4-…png`, `69c7f86a-…gif`, `7035296e-…jpg`, `e72e6a2c-…jpg`, `ca6f2fd1-…gif`, `971aa10f-…jpg` (all on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com`)
    - `2026%5F1101%5FCBK%5FPlayFurn%5FAppPromo2026%5F1101` (scene7)
    - `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FGiftGuide%5FSecondaryStory.gif`
    - `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` (spacer)
  - **Tracking pixels:** the `rlcdn`, `mi.` and `dv.` images should carry `alt=""`. With no `alt`, screen readers may read out the filename or URL.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4.
- **Generic title:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` (should be `&amp;`) and doesn't match the subject line.
- **Redundant meta tags:** there are duplicate `format-detection` metas (a combined one plus separate `date`, `address` and `telephone` ones) and both `http-equiv` and `charset` declarations. This is harmless but sloppy.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is a typo'd placeholder left in the source.
- **Not assessed:** the source is truncated, so body-level issues such as table `role` attributes, link text and image dimensions are not assessed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) are visible in the supplied source. The `mi_u=`, `mi_cid=` and `mi_mid=` parameters on the pixels are resolved per-recipient identifiers, not broken tokens.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA found no header. This may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it's unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA found no header, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope looks aligned, since the From domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` matches the image host. This is not proof.
- **Body footer:** the physical address and the in-body unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated source, so CAN-SPAM body requirements can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
This can't be assessed. All 63 tracked links were skipped, so UTM parameters, final landing URLs and the "20% off play furniture" landing-page alignment are unverified. The scene7 asset named `…PlayFurn_AppPromo…` suggests an app-promo module, but deep-link behavior is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the content images listed above. Set `alt=""` on spacers and the `rlcdn`, `mi.` and `dv.` tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.
3. Escape the title (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), ideally with subject-relevant text. Remove the `IMPUT HERE` comment and the duplicate metas.
4. Deduplicate the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` and `rlcdn` pixels if they aren't intentional.
5. Re-run the audit with raw `.eml` headers to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC.
6. Resolve the tracking redirects (follow them with a real HTTP client) to check final URLs, UTMs and landing-page status.
7. Confirm the October gift-guide GIF is deliberate.
