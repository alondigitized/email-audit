# DAYS LEFT TO SAVE + Double Rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-09**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-structured CB2 send of the week, and it's still muddled. The hero is clear: "LONG WEEKEND SALE, UP TO 50% OFF" sits under a live countdown (02 days, 15 hrs, 22 min, 37 sec). It has four category buttons and a "SHOP THE SALE" button. The "ENDS SOON" banner and the timer make the urgency credible. That's more focused than the Nov 9 "Things of quality" send, which led with a "Future Heirlooms Under $500" hero and never settled on one offer.
- The email then fades into a long brand scroll. A sofa tile with no price comes next, then a Double Rewards block, then Interior Design Services, an app download, and a footer grid of four more CTAs. Double Rewards is a second offer fighting the sale for attention. The "10%" struck through to "20% BACK" is a small render quirk that reads as a correction.
- The subject says "DAYS LEFT," and the timer shows 2 days, so the two agree. It's a decent closing-stretch send. It would be a good one if it dropped half the modules and put real product and prices under the hero.

## What's working

- The countdown timer (02:15:22:37) sits directly above the hero and makes the deadline credible.
- The hero is a clear stack: "LONG WEEKEND SALE" and "UP TO 50% OFF," then category buttons for Furniture, Lighting, Rugs and Decor & Mirrors.
- The "NEW & BEST SELLING" block uses a single green sofa with a clean "SHOP NOW" button.
- The Double Rewards block states its window ("NOW THROUGH NOV 17TH, 2026") and a concrete figure, "20% back in Reward Dollars."
- The photography is consistent and on-brand, with a warm, moody palette.

## What's weak

- The sofa has no name, price or sale price, so the only offer on show is the vague "up to 50%."
- Double Rewards is a second headline offer and competes with the sale for the eye.
- The "10%" struck through to "20% BACK" looks like a corrected error, and the "On purchases at CB2 with a CB2 Visa" qualifier is small and low-contrast over the photo.
- The email is far too long. Interior Design Services, the app download, the "Let's celebrate" and "Get 15% off" texting tiles, and the footer CTAs all dilute the sale.
- The "Interior Design" and "Download the CB2 app" blocks put light text over busy photos, which makes them hard to read.
- The dense legal fine print sits at the bottom.

## Recommendations

- 1. Keep the sale as the one story. Cut Interior Design, the app block, the celebrate tile and the SMS signup, or move them to a slim footer row.
- 2. Replace the single sofa with a 3–4 product grid. Each item should show its name, was/now price and a "Shop [item]" link, with at least one in-stock bestseller per category.
- 3. Collapse Double Rewards into a slim banner under the hero. Fix the 10%/20% treatment and state the Visa condition plainly.
- 4. Change the hero CTA from "SHOP THE SALE" to a verb plus noun, such as "Shop furniture up to 50% off."
- 5. Put the countdown time into the subject or preheader.
- **Subject Alt A:** `2 days left: up to 50% off CB2 furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends Monday + 20% back in rewards`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sofas, rugs and lighting up to 50% off. Ends in 2 days.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn 20% back in Reward Dollars through Nov 17 on top of sale prices.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and `lang="en"`. The QA data shows missing alt text on 9 content images and about 12 tracking pixels, plus 11 insecure `http://` third-party pixels from Dotomi. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. The HTML supplied is truncated, so the body, footer and unsubscribe link couldn't be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unprobed links:** 62 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.
- **Insecure pixels:** 11 Dotomi retargeting pixels load over `http://`, which can be blocked or trigger mixed-content handling:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Third-party exposure:** Ten cookie-redirect pixels to a third-party ad domain run inside the email body. This is a privacy and deliverability risk, and it adds image requests.
- **Unfinished template:** The source contains a leftover placeholder comment, `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`, and an empty `<!--[if !mso]><!--> <!--<![endif]-->` block.
- **Malformed font URLs:** Several `@font-face` URLs have a double slash, e.g. `https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2`.
- **Cross-brand font hosting:** The `HNW05` fonts are loaded from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/...`, a different domain from the sender.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:** All 9 images are hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `67104ea0-a975-4a57-968f-271fe571a353.jpg`
  - `b07444d0-6cde-4faf-b54f-44e12a76954f.jpg`
  - `67bf8bee-e30d-406e-a1a5-249069fa7b88.jpg`
  - `1e90c867-2b40-461e-96d4-4fafc278b660.jpg`
  - `9552ba4e-0ef1-4780-bd61-2e9361427df1.jpg`
  - `07606403-d246-4196-ac59-27a8b4ba9f97.jpg`
  - `345f2993-963b-4783-9afc-927a05cfc0e8.jpg`
  - `bca1af17-99f4-49f6-adde-86f7c3d93fa5.jpg`
  - `9685411b-425d-4865-a92f-a520df64bb18.jpg`

  With images off, or in a screen reader, these may be unreadable. If they carry the offer text, that content is lost.
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels:** The Dotomi pixels, `mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif` and `dv.cb2.com/o/d4146a30-...` have no alt. Add `alt=""` and set `width`/`height` to 1.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" ... maximum-scale=1>` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; -ms-text-size-adjust:none}` blocks user text resizing, and `body` repeats `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
- **Fixed mobile width:** The `.mobile{width:414px !important}` rule can overflow viewports narrower than 414px, but the media query only triggers at `max-width:418px`.
- **Positives:** `lang="en"`, a `<title>` and `format-detection` metas are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. The truncated HTML shows no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[%`). The body was not visible, so this is not fully verified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found, and the QA check itself says the relay may not capture it. Treat this as unverified, not a confirmed gap.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, with the same caveat. This matters for Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** Absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `cb2@mail.cb2.com` is unknown. The `mail.cb2.com` subdomain and `image.mail.cb2.com` images suggest an ESP-managed setup, but this can't be confirmed.
- **In-body unsubscribe and postal address:** Not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed. Verify them in the full HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be evaluated because all 62 tracked links were skipped and none of the destination URLs were exposed.
- **Final destinations:** They are unknown, so landing-page alignment with the "DAYS LEFT TO SAVE + Double Rewards" subject and offer is unverified.
- **Tracking identifiers:** The pixels carry identifiers (`mi_u=...`, `mi_cid=...`, `mi_mid=...`), but this shows only open and personalization tracking, not UTM coverage.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content images, and `alt=""` plus 1×1 dimensions to all tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop them. The `cookieredir` pub1–pub10 chain is the heaviest third-party exposure.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` and `text-size-adjust:none` to restore user zoom and text resizing.
4. Strip the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT...]` comment and the empty `!mso` block, and fix the `//Styles/` double-slash font URLs.
5. Inspect the raw `.eml` to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results, because the relay may be stripping them.
6. Resolve a sample of the 62 tracked links to confirm destination status codes and UTM parameters.
7. Pull the full, untruncated HTML to verify the unsubscribe link, postal address and merge-token rendering.
8. Fix the QA report's summary line, which says "0 issue(s), 3 warning(s)" while the output lists dozens of warnings.
