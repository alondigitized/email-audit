# Dinner parties await
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's editorial, hosting-season send, and it is better looking than it is persuasive. The hero reads "HOSTING SEASON IS NEARLY UPON US / Everything you need to do it in style," with a small "SHOP ENTERTAINING" button. Below it are four full-bleed, moody tabletop shots: Orcino bowl, Silom coupe glasses, Mepra flatware, and Emporia/Alvina plate. They look great, but none shows a price or an offer.
- The commercial message arrives late. Below the tables are a dining tables/chairs/storage/rugs grid and a "NOT SOLD OUT, YET" back-in-stock banner. A Black Friday "UP TO 60% OFF" banner and a Thanksgiving delivery-deadline strip (11/27, 11/29, 11/24) follow. ## What's working

- The imagery is cohesive, dark and warm. It reads as CB2 and sells a dinner-party mood.
- The "HOSTING SEASON IS NEARLY UPON US" headline is seasonally timely.
- The delivery-cutoff strip ("GET IT IN TIME FOR THANKSGIVING") adds credible, useful urgency.
- The glassware product row with prices (Marta, Neat, Brushed Gold place card holders) gives concrete, affordable entry points.
- The footer carries the app, rewards (10% back), and gifting modules in a tidy layout.

## What's weak

- There is no offer above the fold. The hero has only a tiny, low-contrast "SHOP ENTERTAINING" button.
- The 60% off Black Friday banner is buried about 80% of the way down, even though it is the strongest hook.
- The four tabletop tiles carry only small captions with no price or CTA, so the email is mostly scrolling and little action.
- The email is very long and has several competing messages: entertaining, furniture, back in stock, sale, delivery deadlines, and product picks.
- It is the second CB2 send of the day, after the 60% cult-favorites email, and it contradicts that message by leading with no discount.

- 1. Move the "BLACK FRIDAY: UP TO 60% OFF" offer into the hero, with an entertaining image behind it.
- 2. Cut the four full-bleed tiles to two, and add a starting price to each caption, such as "Coupe glasses from $X."
- 3. Make the CTA a visible, high-contrast button with a specific verb and noun, such as "SHOP DINNERWARE."
- 4. Put the delivery-deadline strip directly under the hero and drop the "Not sold out, yet" block.
- 5. Space out sends: don't pair this with the cult-favorites email on the same day.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: CB2 "Dinner parties await"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is standard (XHTML 1.0 Transitional, `lang="en"`, MSO conditionals, `<title>` present). The confirmed defects are 12 content images with no alt text, 12 plain-`http://` Dotomi tracking pixels, and viewport and text-size rules that block zoom. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers were not captured. The HTML source was also truncated, so the footer and body links could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 69 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their destinations or redirect chains were verified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- **Insecure tracking pixels:** 11 third-party pixels load over `http://`, which mail clients may block or flag as mixed content:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437&...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`
- **Cookie-sync pixels:** The ten `pubN.php` calls are third-party cookie-sync redirects. They add 10 extra network requests to the open and may be blocked by privacy features such as Apple MPP and Gmail image proxying.
- **Pixels missing alt:** The two first-party pixels (`https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/1ca21b9b-...?mi_cid=...`) also lack `alt`. These are low severity.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** 12 images hosted at `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt`. Their filenames are 4c631d81…, c74452ee…, 4b5eaef2…, 776613d5…, b3e75332…, a4a66b1f…, b9e222c6…, a62af938…, a407aec2…, 5765d289…, 93958f7d… and ca52acde….
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, clients may announce the raw filename or nothing.
  - A missing attribute is worse than `alt=""`, because the latter at least marks an image as decorative.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; ...}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable user text scaling on iOS.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` ships in production. It is harmless but shows the template was not cleaned.
- **Font fallbacks:**
  - `.mso-font-fix-georgia` and `.mso-font-fix-times_new_roman` use a `sans-serif` generic fallback, which is wrong for serif faces.
  - `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`).
  - `HNW05` loads from `crateandbarrel.com`, a different domain from the sender.
  - Most email clients ignore web fonts anyway, so this is minor.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking URLs contain per-recipient identifiers (`mi_u=`, `mi_mid=`), which look correctly rendered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** Neither was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If they are really absent, the email lacks RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, which Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` and the image domain `image.mail.cb2.com` appear aligned. Alignment cannot be confirmed without the headers.
- **Footer and physical address:** The footer is outside the truncated source, so the in-body unsubscribe link and postal address could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 69 click-tracking links were not resolved and the source was truncated, so UTM parameters and landing pages are unverified. No mismatch was observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 12 content images, and `alt=""` to the tracking pixels.
2. Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or drop the ten `pubN.php` cookie-sync calls to cut requests and mixed-content risk.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and avoid `text-size-adjust:none` on `*`. Use `100%` instead.
4. Fix the `sans-serif` fallbacks on the serif `.mso-font-fix-*` classes, and collapse the `//Styles` double slashes.
5. Strip the `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT…]` placeholder comment.
6. Re-run QA against raw `.eml` source, not the relay output, so the unsubscribe and authentication headers are captured. Resolve a sample of the redirect links to check UTMs and destinations.
