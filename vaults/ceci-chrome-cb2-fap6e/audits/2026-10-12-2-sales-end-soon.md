# 2 SALES END SOON
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This email stacks two sales, a shipping offer and a lighting tile, and it does that well. The subject "2 SALES END SOON" matches the two big blocks: the Long Weekend Sale (up to 60% off, "ENDS TOMORROW") and the semi-annual Upholstery Event (up to 25% off, "ENDS SOON"). Both headlines are large and easy to read, and each block has its own "SHOP THE SALE" button. The photography is warm and consistent, with a bed vignette and a green velvet sofa.
- The weakness is focus. After the two sales come a free-shipping band ("Orders of $99 or more"), a lighting tile, and a utility block covering the app, a rewards card, gift registry and SMS signup. The "Shipping, on us" text sits on a dark, busy photo and is harder to read. Both sale CTAs also use identical copy, "SHOP THE SALE", which doesn't say what you get.
- This is a competent deadline send and better than the recent generic ones. It's a subscriber's fourth or fifth near-identical sale email this week (see 10/11 "This sale pairs so well with free shipping" and "This sale only happens twice a year"). The only new element is the "ends tomorrow" countdown.

## What's working

- The subject matches the body: two sales, two big hero blocks.
- The "ENDS TOMORROW" and "ENDS SOON" eyebrows give each block credible urgency.
- "UP TO 60% OFF" is large and readable at the top, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The bed and velvet sofa photography is moody, cohesive and on-brand.
- The nav bar at the top lets you jump straight to a category.

## What's weak

- Both sale CTAs read "SHOP THE SALE", with no category (bedroom, sofas) and no verb-plus-noun.
- The free-shipping block ("SHIPPING, ON US") has weak text contrast over the photo, and its button is small.
- The lighting tile has no offer and no price. Its "SHOP NOW" is white on a light image and nearly disappears.
- The lower utility grid (app, Rewards card, registry, 15% SMS) adds clutter and competes with the sale CTAs.
- The fine-print footer is a huge wall of dense type, and "up to" discounts come with many exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the countdown the single hero. Lead with "Ends tomorrow" and show only the 60% sale, with the upholstery event as a smaller secondary block.
- 2. Give each CTA a specific label, for example "Shop bedroom deals" and "Shop sofas up to 25% off".
- 3. Fix legibility on the shipping band by adding a solid overlay or a darker panel, and use a high-contrast button on the lighting tile.
- 4. Cut the lighting tile or give it an offer. Move the app, registry and SMS blocks out of this send.
- 5. Vary the creative from earlier this week so the series doesn't read as repeats.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email renders with one confirmed defect: an unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`). It also has widespread missing alt text, plain-HTTP tracking pixels, and header checks that could not be verified through the relay.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- 58 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so destination validity and redirect chains are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed failure.
- 10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels load over plain `http://`: `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?...` through `pub10.php`. The UCM beacon is also on `http://`: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`. Clients and proxies that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them, so the retargeting and sync calls will silently fail for those recipients.
- The first-party open pixels use HTTPS and look fine: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?...` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/5f81de3b-...`.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- Three content images have no alt text:
  - `3e3d16d4-b941-44a6-8309-bb2eb5254612.jpg`
  - `844a5767-6c7f-4bb5-abde-10ad4a8c06f6.jpg`
  - `acb86340-41fe-4efe-8aea-73256b4615a4.jpg`

  All are served from `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`. With images blocked, or for screen-reader users, these slots are empty or announce a filename. If they carry offer or product content, that content is lost.
- 14 tracking and pixel images (Dotomi `pub1`–`pub10.php`, `UCMController`, `o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) also lack alt. For 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix. Without it, some readers announce the URL.
- The source I was given is truncated, so I can't verify other rendering points such as `role="presentation"` on layout tables or MSO conditional coverage. The visible head is sound: `lang="en"`, a viewport meta, a `<title>`, and MSO `OfficeDocumentSettings`.
- `@font-face` rules load from `www.cb2.com` and `www.crateandbarrel.com`. They have web-safe fallbacks (Helvetica, sans-serif), so this is low risk.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- **FAIL:** the raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is present in the delivered HTML. It is an unresolved Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style AMPscript or personalization variable, which indicates a dynamic review or product block failed to populate. Depending on where it sits, it may show as literal text or break an attribute or URL. I can't see its position in the truncated source, so its exact visible impact is unconfirmed.
- No other unresolved tokens were flagged.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` was not found, and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed, and the sender may well send them. If absent in production, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules (2024) require one-click unsubscribe.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The cause is likely the relay, not the sender.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't confirm either. They fall outside the provided evidence.
- From domain `mail.cb2.com` matches the image and tracking subdomains (`image.mail.cb2.com`), which is consistent.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters can't be evaluated. All 58 links are wrapped in tracking redirects and were skipped, and the source is truncated.
- The tracking uses `mi_` parameters (`mi_u`, `mi_cid`, `mi_mid`), and I saw no UTM tagging in the visible evidence. Whether UTMs exist on the final destinations is unknown.
- Landing-page alignment with the "2 SALES END SOON" subject is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **High:** Fix the `%%reviews_skulist%%` population failure, and add a send-time guard that fails the render if any `%%…%%` token remains.
2. **Medium:** Move all Dotomi pixels to `https://`, or drop the 10-pixel cookie-sync stack. It adds weight and mixed-content risk.
3. **Medium:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the three `image.mail.cb2.com` images, and `alt=""` to all tracking pixels.
4. **Medium:** Re-test on a path that preserves raw headers, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
5. **Low:** Resolve a sample of the 58 tracking links with a redirect follower, to verify destinations and UTM carry-through.
6. **Tooling:** Make the QA check fetch the full, untruncated HTML. Several categories above could not be assessed because of the truncation.
