# LAYER ON THE LIGHTING + Last weekend for double rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a lighting-led editorial send with a sale banner on top and a clearance block below. The hero, "THE JEWELRY OF THE ROOM", with its moody amber pendants, is the best creative CB2 has shown in the recent run. The product storytelling is strong: Abel wall sconce, Tova pendants, Astr sconce and Groove pendant, each credited to a designer and a material. I'm reading a 160px-wide downscale of a long render, so I can't verify the small print.
- The email carries three competing promos. The top banner has two blocks stacked together: "LAST DOUBLE REWARDS* THIS YEAR / EARN 20% BACK" and "THE EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE UP TO 60%". Midway down, a separate "LIMITED TIME NOW UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE" block appears. The subject promises "Layer on the lighting" and "last weekend for double rewards", but the strongest number, 70%, appears in neither. Since 11/12 CB2 has sent "Black Friday Preview + 2X Rewards", "Decorate now" and the Feeding America send, and this one is yet another rewards reminder.
- Verdict: good creative, muddled offer architecture. It's the same beat as the 11/12 sends, just with better pictures. It would click better if it committed to one promo.

## What's working

- The hero and editorial copy, "A little brass here, a little marble or alabaster there", sell lighting as design rather than a discount.
- Each product block names its designer and material (Abel, Tova, Astr, Groove). That gives a design-minded shopper something to react to.
- "SHOP ALL LIGHTING" appears twice, at the top and after the editorial section, in a clear black button on a light background.
- The "LAST DOUBLE REWARDS" banner is the first thing seen at the top. It gives the subject's urgency a visible anchor.
- The "More for you" grid shows prices with strikethroughs. It gives a lower-commitment entry point after the editorial section.
- I can't see any broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- Three offers compete: up to 60% (Black Friday), 20% back (rewards) and 70% off clearance. The eye has no single place to land.
- The 70% clearance block is below the editorial section, and it looks like a generic template block. The strongest discount is buried.
- The top banner stacks two dense dark-green and black promo blocks with small type above the hero. The hero photo starts well down the screen on mobile.
- The long editorial section has no price cues. The first product price appears only in the "More for you" grid.
- Legal and fine-print blocks at the bottom are dense, and the rewards asterisk leans on them. That weakens the "honest offer" read.
- The footer stacks an app download, a rewards card promo and a gift registry block. That's a lot after a lighting-focused email.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Either lead with lighting and show the "up to 70% off" price in the hero, or lead with the last-weekend rewards deadline and put lighting underneath. Don't run both at equal weight.
- 2. Move the 70% clearance block up to just under the first "SHOP ALL LIGHTING" button, or fold it into the hero. Show the numbers.
- 3. Collapse the two top banners into one line: "Last weekend: 20% back in rewards + up to 60% off".
- 4. Add a price or "from $X" under each featured product, and link each to its own product page instead of only to a lighting category.
- 5. Cut the footer promos (app, rewards card, registry) down to one. Rewards is the strongest fit.
- 6. Brand history: this is the Nth rewards reminder since 11/10. Name the real deadline ("ends Sunday 11/16") in the subject, so the send feels different from the 11/12 ones.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Lighting up to 70% off + last weekend for 2X rewards`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Marble, brass & alabaster lighting, now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sconces, pendants and table lamps from designers you know. Double rewards end Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Layer in alabaster and brass for the holidays. Clearance now up to 70% off.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based XHTML with MSO conditionals and a `lang="en"` attribute. The QA run shows a 57% pass rate with 0 errors and 3 warnings. The confirmed issues are missing alt text on about 24 images, 11 non-HTTPS third-party pixels, and a viewport meta that blocks pinch-zoom. The compliance and header checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 66 tracking links were skipped as click-redirect domains, so none of the 66 destinations were probed. I can't confirm they resolve, or that they land on the intended pages.
- **Insecure Dotomi pixels:** 11 images load over `http://`. They are `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`.
  - These are third-party cookie-sync pixels. Clients that block mixed or non-HTTPS content will drop them.
  - The 10 sequential `pubN.php` calls add network overhead and third-party exposure for no visible benefit to the reader.
- **First-party tracking pixels (informational):** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/7a4abf32-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are HTTPS. The `mi_u` value is a 64-hex-character hash and could be an identifier.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images:** 9 images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no alt text:
  - `5c26e5b4…jpg`, `1b10a8fa…jpg`, `57d18ff4…jpg`, `9222a79b…jpg`, `a2999cfa…jpg`
  - `4b60731d…jpg`, `51df5575…jpg`, `7f0c2405…jpg`, `2c12b294…jpg`
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these render empty or announce the filename.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com` pixel, `UCMController` and `pub1`–`pub10` have no alt. They should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` blocks pinch-zoom. This is a WCAG 1.4.4 resize-text concern.
- **Text resizing disabled:** `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` in `*{…}` and in `body` disables iOS text scaling.
- **Leftover template placeholder:** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source.
- **Web font URLs:** Some `@font-face` sources use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/...`). Other fonts are pulled cross-brand from `crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`. Helvetica and sans-serif fallbacks exist, so the impact is limited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`). The only per-recipient values visible are the `mi_u` and `mi_mid` parameters in the tracking pixels. The body past the truncation point wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found:** The QA tool notes this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed, not a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (RFC 8058):** This has the same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo expect both headers for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `cb2@mail.cb2.com`, and the tracking domains share the `cb2.com` root, which is consistent with aligned branding, but I can't verify authentication.
- **Footer unverified:** The footer unsubscribe link and physical address fall outside the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable from the data provided. All 66 links were skipped, and no UTM parameters or landing-page URLs were visible in the truncated source. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` parameters on the pixels suggest the Movable Ink or Cordial-style tracking handles attribution through redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive alt text to the 9 content images listed above, and `alt=""` to all pixels (`o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com`, Dotomi).
2. Move the 11 Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them. Consider dropping the redundant `pub1`–`pub10` cookie-sync calls.
3. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta, and replace `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
4. Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` comment and fix the `//Styles` double-slash font paths.
5. Capture raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay, to confirm them.
6. Follow a sample of the 66 tracking redirects to check the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page match.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, unsubscribe link, physical address and merge tokens.
