# Your new favorite landing spot
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is back in "shoppable editorial" mode — the same playbook that scored 8/10 on 8/1 ("The best kind of impulse buy") and 8/5 ("The marble that elevates everything") — leading with a new sofa/sectional launch instead of a discount stack. "LOVE AT FIRST SIT" over a full-bleed sectional shot is a strong, confident hero, and the subject line's "landing spot" phrase pays off nicely in the on-page copy for the Eastman sofa, which is a level of cross-copy cohesion this brand doesn't always bother with.
- The gap is pricing. Four hero products get full editorial treatment — Sonora, Eastman, Ambroise, Belmond, each with a designer credit and lifestyle copy — and not one shows a price. That's a real click-killer for a considered furniture purchase; the "MORE FOR YOU" grid at the bottom shows prices for every item, so the omission on the hero pieces reads as an oversight, not a style choice.
- The Interior Design Services block reappears here (the same pivot that sank the 8/7 send to 5/10), but it's contained to a single CTA among several rather than taking over the email, so it does far less damage this time. Net: a well-executed editorial send undercut by missing price info and stale "spring collection" copy running in August.

## What's working

- Bold, single-message hero ("LOVE AT FIRST SIT") with one unambiguous CTA button, no competing offers up top.
- Subject line phrase ("your new favorite landing spot") is echoed directly in the Eastman product copy — real cross-channel consistency.
- Four distinct new silhouettes each get a maker credit (Mermelada Estudio, Studio ANANSI, Vuue) — genuine editorial depth, not generic filler.
- Clean render throughout: no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.
- Preheader is real, descriptive copy ("Our newest and most comfortable seating inside"), not boilerplate.

## What's weak

- No price shown anywhere on the four hero sofas — a major purchase-consideration detail missing right where it matters most.
- "Our new spring collection" copy is running on an August 8 send — stale seasonal language.
- No discount, loyalty pricing, or urgency anywhere in the hero section — nothing time-bound pushing an immediate click.
- The Interior Design Services block recycles the same pivot that dragged the 8/7 email down, even if better contained here.
- Footer stacks four separate asks (app download, 10% back rewards, wedding registry, 15% off signup) with no hierarchy among them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a price line under each hero product name (Sonora, Eastman, Ambroise, Belmond) — the single biggest lever, since it removes the main piece of friction before someone clicks through on a big-ticket item.
- 2. Cut "new spring collection" and replace with accurate, season-neutral copy.
- 3. Give the top CTA a concrete hook — free white-glove delivery, member pricing, or a bounded intro discount — since right now there's zero monetary reason to act today.
- 4. Trim the footer to one or two asks instead of four competing offers (app, rewards, registry, signup).

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard MSO/Outlook-compatible HTML template (VML/conditional comments, table-based layout) served from CB2's ESP (`mail.cb2.com` / Bronto or similar via `image.mail.cb2.com`), with 10+ third-party ad-sync pixels from `dotomi.com` and no observable authentication or unsubscribe headers in the captured transmission.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 67 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect-domain links are not resolved by the QA tool) — link destinations are unverified.
- 10 third-party ad-sync pixels load over plain HTTP from `ads.dotomi.com` (`pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, e.g. `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php?d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2=1`) plus one from `login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`. Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked or stripped by webmail clients enforcing mixed-content policy, meaning these ad-sync calls likely fail silently for a large share of recipients.
- Two additional first-party tracking pixels are present: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0a5d06ff-386f-41a2-a610-3e926e3405cc` — both HTTPS, no issue there.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 product/hero images are missing `alt` text (IDs: `6fe42451…`, `49228348…`, `d44c30ae…`, `3faaed17…`, `83c6fc9a…`, `a600cd52…`, `4addc93d…`, `b3987305…` under `https://image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`).
