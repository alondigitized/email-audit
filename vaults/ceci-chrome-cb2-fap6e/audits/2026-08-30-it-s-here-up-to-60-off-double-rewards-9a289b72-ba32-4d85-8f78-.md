# It’s here! Up to 60% off + Double Rewards→
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids' Labor Day Event again, virtually indistinguishable from the 2026-08-30 Crate & Barrel sibling send ("ON NOW! Double Rewards + up to 60% off") and last night's own Crate & Kids version ("The Roundup") — same navy hero band, same red starburst badge, same "Double Rewards is on!" module, same category-grid structure. The category tiles here are at least genuinely kids-specific (nursery furniture, baby bedding, study/storage, rugs), which is more targeted than the generic-furniture grid this brand sometimes runs, so there's real signal buried in the repetition.
- But this is now the third-plus near-identical Labor Day/Double Rewards send from this brand family in two days, and nothing distinguishes today's from yesterday's beyond the badge copy. The "we love these for you" personalized product row and the $5,000 Design Desk sweepstakes are nice adds, but they're buried after a long scroll of category tiles a subscriber has already seen.

## What's working

- Clear, legible hero: red "It's Here!" starburst + "Up to 60% off The Labor Day Event" headline reads instantly.
- Category grid is on-persona — nursery furniture, baby bedding, study/storage, kids rugs — not generic home goods.
- Percentage-off badges are specific and consistent across every tile (up to 50%, 60%, 30%).
- "we love these for you" personalized row (backpack, stroller, dresser, glider) adds a segmentation signal most Crate & Kids sends lack.
- Double Rewards callout (10%–20% back) is a concrete, quantified loyalty incentive.

## What's weak

- Visually and structurally identical to the last several Crate & Kids/Crate & Barrel Labor Day sends — same hero band, same red badge, same navy Double Rewards module.
- Extremely long scroll (9 major modules before the footer) dilutes the one CTA that matters; by the time a reader hits "we love these for you," the offer has already been repeated 3 times.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the hero despite "Double Rewards is on! ... Now–September 8, 2026" being buried mid-email in small type.
- Sweepstakes module ("$5,000 Design Desk") is a scope shift mid-email that competes with the sale message instead of reinforcing it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the Labor Day cadence — this is now three-plus consecutive near-clone sends across the two Crate brands; rotate in a single-category or new-arrivals send to avoid list fatigue.
- 2. Move the September 8 deadline into the hero band itself, not buried in the Double Rewards module — urgency is the single missing lever here.
- 3. Cut the category grid from 9 tiles to 4-5 highest-margin/most-relevant ones; move "we love these for you" up near the hero since it's the most differentiated content in the email.
- 4. Separate the sweepstakes into its own send — it dilutes the sale CTA and isn't Labor Day-relevant.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off kids furniture — ends Sept 8`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Double Rewards + nursery picks are live`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `20% back in Reward Dollars, plus new nursery & storage markdowns.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ends Sept 8: shop the pieces we picked for your nursery.`
