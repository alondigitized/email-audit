# On now: Up to 25% off your bedroom refresh!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's bedroom category push, and the top of the email does exactly what it needs to: "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" over a moody canopy bed shot, with a real price stack visible ($1,899 sale / $2,299 reg) directly under the hero. That's stronger than most of this brand's recent sends — the 2026-08-11 dining room email buried its offer in five separate collection stories, and this one leads with a number instead.
- Where it falls apart is length and focus. After the hero and a clean 4-up subcategory grid (beds/dressers/bedding/lighting, each with its own %), the email keeps going — a Howell x Harrier AD sweepstakes, a rug launch, a "we love these for you" product grid that's mostly dinnerware (not bedroom), a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a design service banner — all before the footer. It's a full catalog dump wearing a bedroom-sale subject line. Anyone who opens for the 25% off bedroom deal gets what they came for in the first two screens, then six more unrelated asks.
- Solid top, bloated body. Ship the hero and the subcategory grid, cut or move everything after "Shop All Bedroom" to a separate send.

## What's working

- Hero clearly states "up to 25% off your bedroom refresh" with a specific sale price ($1,899 vs $2,299) directly beneath it — rare concrete pricing for this brand.
- The 4-tile subcategory grid (beds 30%, dressers/nightstands 25%, bedding 60%, decor/lighting 60%) gives shoppers a fast way to jump to what they actually want.
- Two clear CTA buttons ("Shop Bedroom Sale" and "Shop All Bedroom") sit right after the offer, no scrolling required to find them.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, no broken images, consistent brand typography throughout.

## What's weak

- The email keeps running well past the bedroom offer into a sweepstakes, a rug launch, a mixed product grid (mostly tableware, not bedroom), a credit card ad, and an app download pitch — none of it reinforces the subject line's promise.
- "We love these for you" grid shows sofas, dinner plates, cereal bowls, and a dresser — a jumbled, seemingly unpersonalized mix that dilutes the bedroom focus.
- Subject promises a bedroom refresh but by mid-email you're being sold a credit card and an app — bait-and-switch feel for anyone who scrolls looking for more bedroom product.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in view — "On now" in the subject implies time-limited, but nothing in the body gives a credible end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "Shop All Bedroom." Everything from the Howell x Harrier sweepstakes onward belongs in separate, focused sends (rugs launch, credit card, app, design service) — this single email is trying to be five emails.
- 2. If the catalog modules must stay, at least make "we love these for you" bedroom-relevant (nightstand lamps, dressers, bedding) instead of dinnerware and a sofa.
- 3. Add a real deadline near the hero ("Ends Sunday 8/16") — "on now" with no end date reads as evergreen, not urgent.
- 4. Swap the sweepstakes' generic "enter now" framing for something that ties back to bedroom if it must stay in this send (e.g., feature bedroom items in the AD partnership imagery).
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 60% off bedding, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Bedroom refresh: 25% off beds + more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New canopy beds, dressers, and bedding — all on sale now`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop the bedroom sale before it's gone`
