# HOURS LEFT FOR LABOR DAY SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- CB2 pushes the Labor Day formula into its true final hours, and the countdown clock — 00:07:30:24 — now genuinely reads as urgent rather than performative padding. The hero is clean: "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF" over a warm, well-styled living room shot, with a single unambiguous "SHOP THE SALE" button. The category grid below (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) is genuinely useful navigation, not filler.
- But the same self-inflicted wound from 2026-09-07 is back: the Double Rewards module again renders "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" with two numbers stacked on top of each other. This isn't a new bug — it's a recurring, unfixed one, and it sits inside an "ENDS TODAY" urgency banner that undercuts its own credibility by being visibly broken.
- Structurally this is one of CB2's stronger sends — the countdown finally has teeth, the hero and CTA hierarchy are clean, no lorem ipsum, no broken product images. The recurring reward-dollars glitch and the sheer length of the scroll (nine sections before you even hit the footer) keep it out of top-tier territory.

## What's working

- Countdown clock reads 00:07:30:24 — under 8 hours left, which makes "ends today" feel true rather than boilerplate.
- Hero copy and CTA are unambiguous: "UP TO 50% OFF" + "SHOP THE SALE" button, both visible without hunting.
- Category grid (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) gives shoppers a real navigation shortcut instead of one flat CTA.
- Product photography is consistent, styled, and on-brand across every section — no placeholder or broken images.
- Second "SHOP THE SALE" CTA appears after the category grid, giving a natural second exit point before the cross-sell content starts.

## What's weak

- The Double Rewards section repeats the exact same render bug flagged on 2026-09-07: "GET 10% 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" — two numbers collide with no separator, making the actual offer illegible.
- The email is extremely long — hero, six-category grid, second CTA, Double Rewards module, app/rewards/registry/texts 2x2 grid, then footer — by the time a reader reaches Double Rewards, fatigue has likely set in.
- Nothing on screen signals "last chance" beyond the small countdown number at the very top; once scrolled past, there's no repeated urgency cue lower in the email.
- The category grid, while useful, has no pricing or specific product callouts — it's navigational, not persuasive, so it doesn't reinforce the 50%-off offer at the point of decision.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the Double Rewards render bug before this template ships again — it's now appeared in at least two consecutive sends. This is the single highest-impact fix since it's actively undermining a real cross-sell offer.
- 2. Move a second countdown clock or "ENDS TONIGHT" stamp lower in the email (e.g., above the second "SHOP THE SALE" button) so urgency persists after the long scroll, not just at the top.
- 3. Add a price or percentage callout to at least 2-3 of the category tiles (e.g., "RUGS — UP TO 40% OFF") to convert the grid from pure navigation into a persuasion surface.
- 4. Trim the email — consider cutting Entertaining or Decor & Mirrors, or collapsing the app/rewards/registry/texts grid into a single-row footer strip, to shorten the path to the Double Rewards and footer content.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard ESP-generated (CB2/mail.cb2.com) HTML campaign with heavy MSO/legacy support, but automated QA flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and multiple legacy HTTP ad-tracking pixels alongside widespread missing alt text.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped from HTTP probing (unable to confirm destination validity).
- Multiple legacy ad-network tracking pixels served over plain HTTP instead of HTTPS: `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?...`, and `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`. Non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (e.g., Outlook, Gmail image proxy) and may fail to fire, undercounting tracked conversions/attribution.
- Standard MI/CB2 open-tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/...`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are HTTPS — no issue there.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 8 content images missing `alt` text: `65deb545...gif`, `721de791...jpg`, `b034ce98...jpg`, `6b110dac...jpg`, `9bc8850d...jpg`, `d77f47da...jpg`, `2759d1db...jpg`, `3b5bafb0...jpg`, `3a0a3ed0...png`. This degrades accessibility for screen-reader users and leaves no fallback text when images are blocked (common default in Outlook/Gmail).
- Tracking pixels (UCMController, pub1–10.php, o.gif, dv.cb2.com/o/...) also lack alt text, which is standard/acceptable for invisible tracking pixels — not a real accessibility concern.
- Markup includes broad legacy MSO conditional comments and font-fix classes, indicating intentional Outlook/desktop-client support; no rendering-breaking issues surfaced in the QA data.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders were visible in the truncated source, and QA findings did not flag any unresolved tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`). No issues found based on available evidence.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — QA notes this may be a relay-side capture gap (AgentMail) rather than a confirmed absence at the source MTA; still, worth verifying the header is actually sent, since visible unsubscribe links weren't included in the truncated HTML sample and can't be confirmed.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe) not detected — if genuinely absent, this is a Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirement gap as of their 2024 sender guidelines.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data; flagged as unknown rather than failing.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not evaluable from the truncated HTML/QA data provided — the 61 tracking links were skipped from probing, so destination URLs, UTM parameters, and landing-page alignment could not be verified. Recommend re-running with full link extraction to confirm UTM consistency and that CTAs land on live Labor Day sale pages.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 flagged content images (empty `alt=""` is acceptable if purely decorative, but currently missing entirely).
- Migrate the Dotomi ad-tracking pixels (`login.dotomi.com`, `ads.dotomi.com`) from HTTP to HTTPS to prevent client-side blocking and mixed-content warnings.
- Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the originating MTA (not just the relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
- Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment via a direct header capture (bypassing the AgentMail relay) since `Authentication-Results` was not observed.
- Re-run link/UTM extraction without skipping tracking-redirect domains to verify email-to-site continuity.
