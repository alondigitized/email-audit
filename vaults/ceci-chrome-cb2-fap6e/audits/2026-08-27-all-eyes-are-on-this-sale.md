# All eyes are on this sale
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- "All eyes are on this sale" is CB2 doing its most complete Labor Day send yet — hero, category nav, clearance strip, credit-card upsell, and new arrivals all stacked in one clean scroll. Subject-to-hero match is finally there: the sale is the hero, no bait-and-switch, no gate-teasing like the 8/24-8/26 run. It's also visually the most kitchen-sink version of the campaign to date — five distinct promo blocks (Labor Day / Clearance / Double Rewards / New Arrivals / app-and-rewards footer) crammed into one email.
- The subject itself is the weak link — "All eyes are on this sale" is vague filler copy that says nothing concrete, a step backward from the more literal "LABOR DAY SALE STARTS NOW" (7/10, 8/25). Given this is the 6th CB2 Labor Day email in the trailing brand history, cadence fatigue is real, and a mushy subject doesn't earn the open.
- Net: solid, functional execution once opened, dragged down by a lazy subject line and an over-stuffed, undifferentiated body.

## What's working

- Hero delivers exactly what a Labor Day sale email should: clear "UP TO 50% OFF" headline over an appealing outdoor-furniture lifestyle shot, with a visible "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- Category nav block (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor, Shop All) gives multiple concrete entry points instead of a single generic CTA.
- No render bugs — clean typography, no overlapping text, no broken images.
- Double Rewards block clearly dates the offer ("Now through Sept 9th, 2026") and shows the specific rate ("10% → 20% back"), giving real urgency and specificity.

## What's weak

- Subject line is empty calories — "All eyes are on this sale" names no offer, no category, no percentage; it's the vaguest subject in this brand's recent run.
- Five separate offers (Labor Day 50%, Clearance 60%, Double Rewards 20%, New Arrivals, app/rewards footer) compete for attention with no visual hierarchy signaling which one matters most — it reads like four emails stapled together.
- The "800+ New Arrivals" section undercuts the sale urgency by pivoting to full-price newness right after two markdown pitches.
- This is essentially a remix of 8/25's "LABOR DAY SALE STARTS NOW" hero with the same "UP TO 50% OFF" outdoor-sofa framing — repeat creative for subscribers who opened that one two days ago.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rewrite the subject to carry the actual offer — vague curiosity copy is underperforming this list; lead with the number.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Labor Day + extra 60% off clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Labor Day Sale: 50% off is still on`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus clearance up to 60% off — ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns just landed across the sale.`
- 2. Cut the email to two offers max — Labor Day hero + one supporting block (Clearance or Double Rewards, not both) — and move the rest to a follow-up send; this list is already fatigued from six Labor Day emails in a row.
- 3. Move New Arrivals out of this email entirely — it dilutes urgency and belongs in a non-sale send.
- 4. Vary the hero image/copy from 8/25's send — same sofa-and-landscape shot two sends running reads as recycled, not refreshed.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 62 tracking/click-redirect links detected and skipped from HTTP probing (standard ESP click-tracking wrapper behavior — not itself an issue, but unverified for live-link validity).
- Primary open-tracking pixel: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=...` — HTTPS, fine.
- Secondary tracking beacon: `https://dv.cb2.com/o/02957d02-fb1e-47b4-8bba-95fa62c26512?mi_mid=...&cp_tp=v3.eJzjYuJIF...` (Emarsys/dv tracking) — HTTPS, fine.
- **Legacy Dotomi ad-sync pixels served over insecure HTTP** (10 total):

  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`
  These are mixed-content resources; many mail clients (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail) will block or strip non-HTTPS image requests, meaning this ad-sync/retargeting cookie sync will silently fail for most recipients.

- Template uses standard Outlook conditional VML/MSO comments and `@media` breakpoints at 418px/690px/768px — no structural rendering issues detected in the truncated source.
- `@font-face` declarations reference multiple external font hosts (cb2.com, crateandbarrel.com); non-fatal if blocked (fallback stack includes Helvetica/sans-serif), but adds external DNS/render dependencies.
