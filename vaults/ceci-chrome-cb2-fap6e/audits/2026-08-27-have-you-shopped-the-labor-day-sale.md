# Have you shopped the Labor Day Sale?
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 phoning it in — a plain-text, no-hero, no-image "personal note" email with a conversational, faux-casual tone ("Hi," "Cheers, Nicole from the CB2 Team") that reads as a check-in nudge rather than a sale push. There's no product photography, no visual hierarchy, nothing to look at except a wall of hyperlinked blue phrases — it's essentially a plain-text email formatted as HTML. Compare this to 2026-08-25's "LABOR DAY SALE STARTS NOW" (7.00/10), which opened with a clean shot hero — this send trades that for zero visual investment.
- The copy itself is reasonably clear (up to 50% off, Double Rewards call-out, category name-drops like rugs/dinnerware/sheets/beds), but the format actively suppresses it. With no hero image and no single dominant CTA, the eye has nowhere obvious to land, and "See everything that's on sale right now" — the closest thing to a primary CTA — is buried as the fourth blue link in a row of many. This is a "did you forget?" reminder email, not a sales email, and it should be judged as one.

## What's working

- Subject line directly names the sale and uses a question format that can read as a soft nudge for a lapsed browser.
- Copy clearly states the offer twice ("up to 50% off," "twice as much" with the CB2 Credit Card).
- Multiple relevant category links (rugs, dinnerware, sheets, beds) give shoppers several entry points if they read closely.
- Footer fine print (end date, exclusions, Double Rewards terms) is present and not hidden.
- No render bugs — text is clean, links are legible, footer renders correctly.

## What's weak

- Zero product imagery or hero banner — nothing to visually anchor the "biggest sale of the year" claim.
- No single unambiguous CTA button; six-plus blue text links compete for attention with no visual priority.
- The "Hi," "Cheers, Nicole" framing undersells urgency for what's pitched as "one of our biggest sales of the year."
- Layout is nearly identical in structure to a plain-text personal email — very low visual differentiation from an inbox full of newsletters.
- No mention of the Sept 9th deadline in the visible body copy — that urgency detail is buried in tiny footer legal text only.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a hero image/banner with a clear "UP TO 50% OFF" headline and one CTA button — this single change would do more than any copy tweak, since right now there is nothing to look at.
- 2. Convert "See everything that's on sale right now" into an actual button, not a blue text link, and make it the singular primary CTA.
- 3. Surface the Sept 9th end date in the body copy, not just the footer — pair it with the credit card double-rewards hook for a stacked urgency+value message.
- 4. Trim the link sprawl in the body (rugs/dinnerware/sheets/beds/appointment) down to 2-3 category links max, or turn them into a visual product grid instead of inline text links.
- 5. Drop or shorten the "Nicole from the CB2 Team" personal-note framing — it undercuts the urgency of a sitewide sale.
