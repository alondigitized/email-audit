# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Okay, CB2 knows it's Memorial Day weekend and they showed up — big outdoor sale energy, the pool-scene hero is genuinely aspirational for where I want my roof deck to be. But the second I land on the page, a modal slams into my face asking for my email before I've even seen what they're selling. That's a trust tax I didn't agree to pay, and it colors the whole first impression.

## What's working

- **The pool-scene hero** is exactly the kind of editorial lifestyle shot that makes me stop scrolling — linen outdoor sofa, warm tones, aspirational without being unreachable. This is my velvet-sofa energy but for the patio.
- **"SALE JUST GOT BIGGER — MEMORIAL DAY SALE — UP TO 30% OFF"** — clear, punchy, seasonally appropriate. The headline stacks urgency without feeling desperate.
- **"DOUBLE DIPPING IS OK" top banner** — I actually appreciate a brand that proactively tells me I can stack offers instead of making me hunt for exclusion fine print.
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" editorial strip** at the bottom — lifestyle photography of real-looking people in real-looking apartments. This is the kind of social proof that works on me because it's styled, not sterile.
- **Category circles below the modal** — mirrors, glassware, decorative objects — the assortment telegraphed is exactly the accent-piece rabbit hole I came here to fall into.

## What's weak

- **The email capture modal fires immediately on landing** — before I've scrolled even an inch. The "CONTINUE WITHOUT SAVING" dismiss link is small and light-colored, a textbook dark pattern. I'm already annoyed.
- **The modal blocks the hero CTA entirely** — whatever button lives on that outdoor-furniture hero is invisible to me right now. A sale banner and no path to shop is a conversion dead end.
- **No urgency specificity** — "Memorial Day Sale" implies a deadline, but there's no date, no countdown, no "ends Sunday." Without a clock, the urgency is soft.
- **Zero loyalty or rewards presence** — CB2 has a loyalty program and I see nothing about points, member pricing, or a returning-customer hook. Missed chance for a higher AOV.
- **No free-shipping callout** — CB2 furniture ships heavy; what's the threshold? This is the number I look for first and it's not visible anywhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the email modal by 30 seconds or trigger it on exit-intent** — let me actually see the sale before asking for my personal info. I'm more likely to sign up after I've seen something I want.
- **Pin a persistent "Members save an extra X%" ribbon** to the hero — give loyalty members a reason to log in immediately, not just new-email hunters.
- **Add a countdown timer to the Memorial Day sale banner** — "Ends Monday at midnight" is the difference between "I'll come back" and "I'm shopping now."
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold in the top sticky bar** — something like "Free shipping on orders $X+ · Ends Monday" would convert the browse session I almost didn't get to have.
