## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 50% off + free shipping," with a visible Shop Now button.
- The top bar gives a credible deadline: order by 11 PM PT tomorrow to get it by Thanksgiving.
- The category grid runs from furniture and rugs to decor, with an "up to X% off" label on each tile. It is easy to scan.
- The kitchen block shows sale and regular prices, which makes the discount concrete.
- The Thanksgiving menu and holiday-decor tiles fit the season.

## What's weak

- The email is a very long scroll with at least seven sections, so the top offer competes with everything below it.
- The hero is a kitchen appliance, though the grid also covers furniture, rugs and decor. A home-furniture shopper gets no furniture in the first screen.
- Several tiles carry a "Free Ship Over $79" badge. That is a different shipping message from the hero's "free shipping."
- "Shop Now" is a generic CTA, and many tiles and buttons below it split the click.
- The TheDesignDesk consultation promo appears twice, and the app-download block sits late in the email. Both dilute the sale message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one product-category hero, such as furniture or a bestselling sofa, and put the deadline and shipping terms directly under it. Cut the lower half of the email by about a third.
- 2. Use one consistent shipping message, either free shipping or free shipping over a threshold, across the hero and the tile badges.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop Black Friday furniture deals" or similar. Give each category tile its own specific verb.
- 4. Show the TheDesignDesk promo once and move the app block into the footer area.
- 5. Segment the send: furniture buyers get a furniture-led hero, and kitchen buyers keep the mixer.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a Movable Intelligence (MI) template with 43 images lacking alt text, a viewport meta that disables zoom, and several CSS and markup defects. The automated QA passed 57%, with 0 failures and 3 warnings. The source was truncated, so the body, footer, unsubscribe link and UTM parameters could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **83 tracking links were not probed.** They are on click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and landing pages are unverified.
- **5 LiveRamp pixels:** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5`. These are third-party tracking beacons with no alt attribute. Under the XHTML doctype, `alt=""` is required.
- **Other open and tracking pixels** also lack alt text:
  - `https://mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif?mi_u=…`
  - `https://dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/fa35e73b-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…`
- **Subscriber hash in image URLs:** the `mi_u=56aaa2787a67…` value is passed to every `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` image, the Movable Ink dynamic content requests. The same value appears in the rlcdn `s=` parameter, so one identifier is shared with a third party (LiveRamp).
- **Image URLs cut off:** the `mi_u` and `mi_cid` values appear truncated in the QA output. This is probably a report artifact rather than a defect in the email.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 43 images** (confirmed by QA):
  - 28 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/*.jpg|png`, which appear to be content images
  - 7 MI images, e.g. `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/560d850e7e517ede.png`
  - 1 `971aa10f-….jpg`
  - 7 tracking pixels (above)
  - The content images need descriptive alt text. The pixels need `alt=""`.
- **Zoom disabled:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` prevents pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Text resizing blocked:** the universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` and `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` disable text-size adjustment. Another rule sets `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}`. These also conflict with `body{-webkit-text-size-adjust:100%}` elsewhere in the same stylesheet.
- **Conflicting `max-width` rules:** `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` is followed by `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}`. At 768px and wider both match, so the later 640px rule wins and the 600px rule never applies.
- **Invalid XHTML:** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&` under the XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
- **Redundant or leftover markup:**
  - Duplicate `<meta name="format-detection">` tags (3 variants).
  - Both `http-equiv="Content-Type"` and `<meta charset>` are declared.
  - A leftover template placeholder comment: `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->`.
  - An empty rule `td,th,div,p,a { }` in the MSO block.
- **Global `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}`** forces centering on every paragraph unless it is overridden inline.
- **Positive:** `<html lang="en">` is present, and VML and Office namespaces plus MSO conditionals are included for Outlook.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The remainder of the HTML was truncated, so the body could not be checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** missing, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is expected on this relay and is not evidence of a failure.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not verifiable because the source was truncated.
- **Sender domain alignment:** the From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`. Image hosts `image.mail.`, `mi.` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com` are on the same organizational domain, which is good for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Unverified. The 83 links were skipped as tracking redirects and the destination URLs are not in the truncated source. UTM parameters, landing page alignment and sale-page continuity could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 36 content images, and `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` to the 7 tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta. Replace the global `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` with `100%`.
3. Change the title to `Crate &amp; Barrel`. Remove the duplicate meta tags, the placeholder comment and the empty CSS rule.
4. Fix the `img{max-width}` media queries. For example, use a single rule or `min-width`/`max-width` ranges so that 600px and 640px don't conflict.
5. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` outside the relay. Resolve the three header warnings from the original `.eml` before treating them as defects.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML. Resolve the 83 tracking redirects to their final URLs and verify UTM parameters, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
