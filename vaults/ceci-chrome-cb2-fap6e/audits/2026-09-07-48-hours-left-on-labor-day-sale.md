# 48 HOURS LEFT ON LABOR DAY SALE
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's Labor Day formula in its final-hours form, and it's the strongest structural send of the stretch: a live countdown clock (02:17:20:29), the rug hero repurposed with "ENDS SOON" stamped over it, and — new this time — a full category grid nested right under the hero (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Outdoor, Shop All). That grid is a real upgrade over the single blunt "shop the sale" CTA CB2's been running since 2026-09-02.
- The problem is everything below the fold. After the category grid, the email keeps going — Double Rewards, a second Clearance banner (60% off, a different number than the hero's 50%), CB2 app promo, credit card promo, text-signup promo. It's five separate offers competing for the same click, and by the time a reader hits "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" they've likely already lost the thread on what "the sale" even is. The subject line earns urgency points ("48 HOURS LEFT") that the countdown clock backs up visually, which is a genuine strength — but the email undercuts its own urgency by burying it under four more banners after the deadline message has already landed.
- Ship it, but trim it. Countdown + hero + category grid is a complete, punchy email. The rest reads like separate campaigns stapled onto the end of a deadline reminder.

## What's working

- Live countdown ticker at the top makes the "48 hours left" urgency concrete and immediate.
- "ENDS SOON" stamped directly on the rug hero reinforces the deadline visually, not just in copy.
- The category grid (Furniture / Lighting / Rugs / Decor & Mirrors / Entertaining / Outdoor / Shop All) gives readers a direct path to their interest instead of one generic CTA — a real improvement over 2026-09-06's grid-only approach paired with weaker urgency.
- No render bugs — text is legible, images loaded, buttons render cleanly.

## What's weak

- Offer math is inconsistent: hero says "UP TO 50% OFF," a banner further down says "CLEARANCE UP TO 60% OFF" — two different numbers with no clear distinction between "sale" and "clearance" for a skimming reader.
- Message dilution: after the countdown/hero/grid trio, four more unrelated promos follow (Double Rewards credit card, CB2 app, wedding registry, text signup) — each pulling attention away from the actual deadline.
- Visual hierarchy degrades past the fold — by the third or fourth banner it's unclear which CTA is "the" ask for this email.
- This is at least the fourth "Labor Day ends" beat in six days (2026-09-02 "LABOR DAY SALE IS ON," 2026-09-03, 2026-09-04, 2026-09-05) — fatigue risk is real even though this send's execution is better than most of them.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after "SHOP ALL" — end on the category grid. Move Double Rewards, app promo, and text signup to a separate, non-deadline send; they dilute urgency instead of adding to it.
- 2. Reconcile the 50%/60% discrepancy — if clearance is a separate tier, label it clearly ("Sale: up to 50% off · Clearance: up to 60% off") instead of letting two numbers float independently.
- 3. Move "SALE ENDS IN" countdown or a repeat of it just above the footer too, so users who scroll past the trimmed content still see the deadline reinforced.
- 4. A/B test subject urgency framing:
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: Labor Day Sale ends in 48 hours`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sale ends Wed — up to 50% off ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Rugs, lighting, decor — all still on sale for two more days`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop by room before the discount disappears`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
CB2's Labor Day sale email uses standard MSO/VML responsive markup but ships with multiple missing accessibility attributes and legacy 2000s-era ad-tracking pixels served over unencrypted HTTP.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 63 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by automated HTTP probing (unable to verify destination validity).
- Multiple legacy `dotomi.com` ad-tracking beacons are present, all served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS:
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437...`
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php` (10 sequential pixel calls, identical cookie-redir token `d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2`)
  
  Mail clients that enforce mixed-content/HTTPS-only image loading (e.g., Apple Mail's private relay, some Outlook configs) will block these, silently breaking whatever tracking/cookie-sync these pixels perform.
- Legitimate first-party tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are correctly served over HTTPS.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 10 content/product images are missing `alt` text (e.g., `5714a847-7723-4942-98e6-afd56fa9ade3.jpg`, `af6ad28c-6571-42ff-ab9e-9e499e947d1a.gif`, `96a152a3-...jpg`, `74eb239e-...jpg`, `3763c9c0-...jpg`, `c41a6784-...jpg`, `77524a0a-...jpg`, `9a8e128f-...jpg`, `ca5cf355-...jpg`, `37168965-...png`, `c5974994-...png`) — screen reader users get no fallback content if images fail to load.
- All 10 dotomi `pub*.php` pixels and the `UCMController` pixel also lack `alt` text, but as they are 1×1 tracking beacons this is inconsequential.
- Markup includes correct MSO conditional comments, `-ms-text-size-adjust`/`-webkit-text-size-adjust` resets, and responsive `@media` breakpoints at 418px and 690px/768px — no structural rendering issues detected in the source provided.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FIELD%%`, `{{token}}`, `[FNAME]`) or broken personalization syntax found in the visible source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found in the captured headers — may be a relay-capture artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence; recommend confirming against the raw SMTP transaction.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058)**: not found — if List-Unsubscribe is in fact absent, one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules as of Feb 2024) is not available.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — authentication status could not be verified via this relay; cannot confirm pass/fail.
- No visible unsubscribe link or physical mailing address was included in the truncated HTML source provided — CAN-SPAM requires both; cannot confirm compliance without the footer markup.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The 63 tracking links were not probed (skipped), so UTM parameter presence/consistency and landing-page destination alignment could not be verified from this data. No confirmed issues, but coverage is incomplete.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Migrate the 11 `dotomi.com` HTTP pixel calls to HTTPS or drop them if the vendor relationship is legacy/unused — they will fail to load in HTTPS-enforcing clients.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 10 content images for accessibility and graceful degradation.
3. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are actually present on the raw outbound message (not just absent from this relay capture) — required for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status directly from mail server logs, since Authentication-Results wasn't captured here.
5. Re-run the tracking-link probe with redirect-following enabled to validate the 63 skipped links resolve to live, correctly-tagged landing pages.
