# BLACK FRIDAY HEAD START
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- The subject promises a Black Friday head start. The body delivers a "LAST CHANCE · WAREHOUSE SALE · UP TO 60% OFF" banner, then pivots to free fabric swatches and interior design services. Anyone who opens for early Black Friday access gets a swatch campaign with a sale banner on top. This is the same bolt-on structure CB2 has used all week, including "The key to curation? Go custom." on 10/31 and "The past is present" on 11/2.
- Visually it's polished. The plum banner and large 60% sit at the top, the swatch hero is moody and on-brand, and the two sectional shots (Faible, Belmond) look good. But the email has three jobs: the sale, swatches and design services. It hands each one a big image and a button, so none of them wins.
- Verdict: strong creative, but the subject and body don't match, and "last chance" is the 4th or 5th near-identical urgency message in a week. It would land better as a true early-access send.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" banner is the first thing the eye lands on, and the "Shop the Sale" button is clear.
- "Swatch now. Shop later." is a fresh, useful hook for a considered furniture purchase.
- "Order free swatches" appears twice with a specific verb and noun.
- The sectionals (Faible, Belmond) are labeled, which gives the shopper concrete products to picture.
- The rewards (10% back) and 15%-off signup modules in the footer are a decent loyalty nudge.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a Black Friday head start, but the body never mentions Black Friday or early access. The only urgency is "Last Chance".
- The sale CTA is pushed aside by the swatches and design-services pitch, so the primary action is split three ways.
- On the design-services module, the white "Expert design, tailored to you" copy runs into the bright pendant lamp, so "you" is hard to read.
- The footer legal block is a wall of tiny gray text, and the asterisk on "UP TO 60% OFF*" has no visible deadline or exclusions near the hero.
- The sectional images don't show prices or a sale tag, so the 60% offer isn't tied to any product.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match subject to body. If it's a head start, say so in the hero ("Early access: Black Friday deals start now") with a real date or time window. If it isn't, change the subject.
- 2. Use one primary CTA. Keep "Shop the Sale" and move swatches and design services below a product grid, or send them separately.
- 3. Show 3 to 4 sale products with the sale price or percent off, so the 60% attaches to real items.
- 4. Put the sale end date in the hero, not just in the footer fine print.
- 5. Fix the contrast on the design-services overlay by darkening the image or moving the copy off the lamp.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 10 free swatches shipped before the rush.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: a raw, unresolved merge token (`%%reviews_skulist%%`) shipped to the recipient. It also has several accessibility gaps and ten insecure `http://` third-party tracking pixels. The compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive because the relay doesn't expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **61 tracking links were not probed** (QA: "Tracking/click-redirect domains — skipped HTTP probe"). Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **Ten insecure pixels.** These load over plain HTTP and may be blocked, or trigger mixed-content warnings, in clients that proxy images:
  - `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php?d98e199b…=1`
  - `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`
- **Heavy third-party pixel load.** Ten cookie-redirect calls plus the UCM call fire on open. Each is an extra network request that no `https` fallback covers.
- **Other tracking images** are on HTTPS and likely fine: `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/03424f13-…?mi_cid=…`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Seven content images have no `alt` attribute.** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `7b686db3-4e0f-46a0-beb2-d68a54f229bf.jpg`

  If these carry the offer or CTA content, it is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **The 12 pixel and beacon images also lack `alt`.** They need `alt=""` plus 1×1 or hidden sizing, so screen readers don't announce filenames such as `pub1.php`.
- **`<meta name="viewport" content="… maximum-scale=1">`** blocks pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern.
- **Template leftovers in the source:**
  - The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is unfilled.
  - The `@font-face` block for "Lineto Brown Sub" is declared three times with conflicting sources.
  - Several font URLs use a double slash (`cb2.com//Styles/...`).
  - Fonts load cross-domain from `crateandbarrel.com`.
  - Web fonts are unsupported in many clients, so the fallbacks (`Helvetica, sans-serif`) matter.
- **Mobile CSS:** `.mobile {width:414px}` is applied under a `max-width: 418px` breakpoint. Viewports narrower than 414px (such as 375px and 360px) may get horizontal overflow. I can't confirm this from the truncated source.

- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mail.cb2.com` is aligned with the From address.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither appears in the truncated HTML, so I can't verify either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)

- **Subject and `<title>` mismatch.** The subject is "BLACK FRIDAY HEAD START", but the `<title>` is "CB2, FURNITURE AND DECOR FOR THE MODERN HOME". Browser-view and web-version tabs will show the generic title.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%` before the next send.** Add a render-time guard or default content for the reviews block, and include a token-leak check in pre-send QA.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the seven content images.** Use `alt=""` on tracking pixels.
3. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to `https://`.** If Dotomi doesn't support HTTPS, drop the pixels or consolidate the ten cookie-redirect calls.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta.**
5. **Re-run the compliance and authentication checks against the raw `.eml`,** not the relay copy, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
