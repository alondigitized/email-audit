# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is my place — CB2 is basically a love language for me, so the bar is high. What I'm seeing first isn't even the homepage, it's an email-capture modal demanding both my email AND my phone number before I can see anything. Behind it, there's a Memorial Day Sale hero with "UP TO 50% OFF" which is genuinely exciting, and further down there's a vibe-y editorial block called "THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" that is very much speaking my language. The brand is pushing its seasonal sale hard, but the modal is doing dirty work to the first impression.

## What's working

- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE — UP TO 50% OFF"** hero: This is exactly the kind of event I wait for to pull the trigger on a velvet sofa or a new accent chair. The copy is concrete and the percentage is real.
- **The desert/palm-leaf hero imagery**: Warm sandy tones, shadow play — it's moody without trying too hard. Very CB2, very my Pinterest board.
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" editorial block** at the bottom: That headline is exactly the register I want from a home brand. It's self-aware, a little smug in a good way, and positions the brand as a lifestyle co-sign, not just a furniture store.
- **Top-nav categories**: Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Bedroom & Bath — I can find what I need in one tap without hunting.

## What's weak

- **The modal requires a phone number**: I will give you my email for 15% off. I will not give you my cell number. That second required field is going to cost CB2 a significant conversion chunk from people like me who are privacy-conscious.
- **The asterisk on "FULL-PRICE ITEMS\*"**: The offer is immediately undercut by fine print that's too small to read on mobile. If the 50%-off sale items are excluded from the 15%, that bait-and-switch energy kills trust.
- **No loyalty or member hook visible**: I've definitely bought from CB2 before. Where's my account recognition? Where's a "members get an extra 10%"? This feels like talking to a stranger.
- **The hero is almost entirely blocked**: The best creative asset on the page — that moody sale hero — is mostly obscured by the modal. The sale urgency lands weak because I can barely see it.

## Recommendations

- **Make the phone number field optional, not required**: Drop the friction immediately. Email-only gate converts at dramatically higher rates. If phone is valuable for SMS marketing, offer it as an optional add-on post-submit.
- **Add a countdown timer to the Memorial Day Sale**: "Ends Monday at midnight" would push me from browsing to buying in a single session. Right now the deadline is implied, not stated.
- **Surface a member/account hook above the fold**: A one-line banner — "Sign in to see your exclusive Member Sale pricing" — would make repeat shoppers like me feel seen and push us toward account login instead of modal dismissal.
- **Let the hero breathe**: Trigger the modal after 8 seconds or on scroll intent, not on page load. Let me see the sale first. I'll want the 15% more after I've already fallen in love with something.
