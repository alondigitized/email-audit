# ON SALE: DINNER PARTY MUST-HAVES
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, on-theme CB2 send. The subject "ON SALE: DINNER PARTY MUST-HAVES" matches the body, which opens on a moody dining-room hero: "THE SEMI-ANNUAL DINING EVENT, UP TO 30% OFF," with a clear black "SHOP THE SALE" button. The eye lands on the offer first. The dinner-party framing ("Because to do the most, you need the best.") fits the subject, and that is better alignment than the Oct 12 and Oct 13 sends, where the subject promised design inspiration and the body opened with a sale banner.
- It still does too much below the hero. After the Dining Event module, a second "WAREHOUSE SALE, UP TO 60% OFF" block competes with the first, and a four-tile footer (app, rewards card, gift list, 15% off SMS) adds more asks. Two different percentages and two sale names dilute the single offer. The week of "FINAL HOURS" and countdown sends (Oct 13, Oct 14) has also trained this subscriber to expect urgency, and this send has none. The fine print says the event ends Oct 28 and Nov 4, but the body shows no deadline.
- Verdict: solid, honest, and well aligned, but a generic promo with no urgency and no named product. A real subscriber would see it as the fourth sale email this week.

## What's working

- The subject, hero and offer line up: dining theme, "UP TO 30% OFF," and one obvious "SHOP THE SALE" button.
- The hero photo is a styled dinner-party table with candles and glassware. It sells the occasion rather than just a discount.
- The category grid (Dining Tables, Dining Chairs, Dining Storage, Entertaining) lets the reader self-select by what they need.
- The "ENTERTAINING" glassware tile is the strongest match for "dinner party must-haves."
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- No visible deadline in the body. After last week's countdown timers, the missing urgency makes this feel like a lull. The Oct 28 and Nov 4 dates appear only in the fine print.
- The Warehouse Sale block ("UP TO 60% OFF") sits right under the Dining Event and competes with it. Two offers and two percentages make the message fuzzy.
- "Must-haves" in the subject promises curated products, but the body shows only broad category tiles. No product, price or "from $X" is visible.
- The footer has four more asks (app, rewards card, gift list, SMS signup) before the legal text. That is a lot of exit ramps.
- The dining-table tile (labelled "DINING TABLES") and "DINING STORAGE" have low-contrast labels over busy photos. "DINING STORAGE" is hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the Dining Event the only offer. Move Warehouse Sale to its own send, or drop it to a slim text strip.
- 2. Add a real deadline near the hero, for example "Ends Oct 28," or a countdown, to match the cadence CB2 has been running.
- 3. Swap one category tile for 3–4 priced products (glassware, a table, a chair) so "must-haves" is literally true.
- 4. Make the CTA more specific: "SHOP DINING SALE" or "SHOP DINING TABLES."
- 5. Put tile labels on a solid or gradient backing for legibility.
- 6. Trim the footer to one ask (rewards or SMS).
