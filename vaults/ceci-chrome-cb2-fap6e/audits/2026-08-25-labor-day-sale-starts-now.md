# LABOR DAY SALE STARTS NOW
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-25**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 finally doing the thing directly instead of teasing it — after two straight days of "APP ONLY: FIRST LOOK" and preview gating, "LABOR DAY SALE STARTS NOW" opens the gate wide with a clean hero: velvet chair on sage green, "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF," black "SHOP THE SALE" button. It's the most legible hero-to-CTA sequence this brand has run all week.
- Where it loses points is the same place CB2 always loses points: the long undifferentiated category scroll (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) with no prices, no specific products, and no second offer differentiation — six near-identical category tiles do nothing to move someone who isn't already shopping. The Double Rewards card mid-email is a genuinely good loyalty hook that's buried below the fold.
- Solid, on-brand, and the least confusing send of the week — but still a generic sitewide-sale email with no urgency mechanic beyond the (now-live) Labor Day framing.

## What's working

- Hero sequence is unambiguous: sale name → discount → single black CTA button, no competing offers stacked on top of each other.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is a real, visible, specific number, not a vague "extra savings" tease.
- Double Rewards Dollars card ("10% → 20% back") is a concrete, dated loyalty offer (Now through Sept 9, 2026) — good escalation for the loyalty segment.
- Consistent moody, editorial photography throughout — no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- App/Rewards/Text-signup footer block gives three low-friction secondary conversions beyond "shop."

## What's weak

- Six category tiles (Furniture, Outdoor, Lighting, Rugs, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining) carry zero prices or product specificity — pure navigation, not persuasion.
- No urgency/deadline on the headline offer itself — "UP TO 50%" with no end date makes it feel like it could run indefinitely, undercutting "STARTS NOW."
- Second "SHOP THE SALE" button (after the category scroll) is a plain black bar with no visual distinction from a footer link — easy to miss on a fast scroll.
- Cadence fatigue risk: this is the third Labor Day-adjacent sale send in three days (8/23 "This sale ends tonight," 8/22 "Ends Tomorrow") with no obvious throughline for a subscriber who's seen the same 50%-off drumbeat all week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a deadline to the hero itself (e.g., "Labor Day Sale · Ends Monday") — right now the only date-bound offer in the whole email is the Rewards card, which undersells the actual urgency of a Labor Day sale.
- 2. Cut the six-tile category scroll to 3 tiles max, and put one real product + price under at least the top category (e.g., "Outdoor Lounge Chair, $499") to give the eye something to convert on beyond browsing.
- 3. Move the Double Rewards card up, directly under the hero — it's the most differentiated offer in this email and it's currently buried past seven tiles of scroll.
- 4. Restyle the second CTA button so it doesn't look identical to a footer divider — give it the same visual weight as the hero button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Labor Day Sale: Up to 50% Off, Ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `It's here: the Labor Day Sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, earn 20% back in Rewards Dollars through Sept 9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns across furniture, outdoor, lighting & more.`
