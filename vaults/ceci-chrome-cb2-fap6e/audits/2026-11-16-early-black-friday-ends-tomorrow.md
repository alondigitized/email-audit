# EARLY BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TOMORROW
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This is the cleanest "last chance" send CB2 has put out this week, but it's also the third version of the same Early Black Friday shell in four days. The top banner reads "ENDS TOMORROW / NOW UP TO 70% OFF CLEARANCE / SHOP NOW." Directly below it, a mauve velvet sofa carries a giant letter-spaced "THE EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE, UP TO 60% OFF" headline. The subject and the first screen agree, and the urgency is real. The sale ends Nov 17.
- The execution is cluttered. Two competing discounts (70% clearance and 60% sitewide) sit in the first two blocks. The hero's extreme letter-spacing breaks "EARLY" and "SALE" into single letters, so you read them slowly. A third full-bleed block pushes Double Rewards, with the "10%" visibly struck through to "20%". The App Store, text-signup and Celebrate modules add weight below. It's the same shell as "UP TO 60% OFF + DOUBLE REWARDS" on 11/14 and "WHY WAIT FOR BLACK FRIDAY?" on 11/15, and most recipients have already seen it.
- Judgment: it's competent and the deadline is credible, but it's repetitive and has no product. It's a solid reminder, not a standout.

## What's working

- "ENDS TOMORROW" appears in the subject, the banner and the hero, so the deadline lands immediately.
- The hero's big "60% OFF" and "70% OFF" numerals are the first thing the eye reaches.
- The four category buttons (Furniture, Lighting, Rugs, Decor) plus "SHOP THE SALE" give clear paths into the sale.
- The Double Rewards block names a concrete benefit: 20% back with the CB2 Visa, through Nov 17.
- The sender "CB2 SALE" and the black-on-white logo header are instantly recognizable.

## What's weak

- Two different headline discounts (up to 70% on clearance, up to 60% on the sale) compete for attention and blur the offer.
- The hero's wide letter-spacing ("E A R L Y", "S A L E") hurts readability, and the thin "UP TO" and "OFF" text beside the numerals is faint over the busy photo.
- The send has no specific products or prices. Only the sofa is shown, and it isn't linked to a named item or price.
- The "10%" struck through to "20%" in the rewards headline looks awkward and mixes a correction into the creative. The rewards block also adds a second "LEARN MORE" step before any shopping.
- The footer modules (App Store, 15% text signup, 10% back) and the dense fine print dilute a last-day send. This is another near-identical sale email in a week with several of them, such as 11/14 and 11/15.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer. Make the hero a single "up to 60% off, ends tomorrow" message and fold the 70% clearance into a secondary line.
- 2. Add a row of 3–4 hero products with sale prices ("Was / Now") and direct links, so the click goes to something specific.
- 3. Fix the headline typography. Use tighter tracking and a bolder weight so "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" reads in one glance.
- 4. Add a countdown or a time like "ends 11:59 PM PT" to make the deadline concrete.
- 5. Fix the rewards creative so it shows only "20%", and move the app and signup modules out of a last-day send.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last day: up to 60% off at CB2`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight's the night: Early Black Friday ends`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, rugs & decor. Plus 20% back in rewards through Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance up to 70% off. Final hours, final markdowns.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is mostly standard for an ESP template (table layout, MSO conditionals, mobile media query). The automated QA found no hard failures, but it did flag missing alt text on 8 content images and 12 tracking pixels. It also flagged 11 plain-`http://` Dotomi pixels and the absence of the unsubscribe and authentication headers. The HTML was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks could not be completed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **58 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are **unverified**, not clean.
- **Plain-HTTP third-party pixels (confirmed).** `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…` and ten `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` to `pub10.php` requests are non-HTTPS.
  - Clients that enforce HTTPS-only image loading, or that proxy images, may block them.
  - Those clients may also flag the message as mixed content.
- **Cookie-sync pixel stack.** The ten `pubN.php` calls plus `UCMController` are an ad-network cookie-sync chain. This adds 11 extra third-party requests per open. It is a deliverability and privacy-surface concern, though not a functional break.
- **First-party open pixels.** `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/f70db414-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are HTTPS and work as intended. They carry a hashed user identifier (`mi_u`) in the query string.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed, 8).** All are on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/`:
  - `3f4755c8-…jpg`
  - `a1606cb7-…jpg`
  - `3c8bbebe-…jpg`
  - `afb0b778-…jpg`
  - `0cadfa0d-…jpg`
  - `871fd37b-…jpg`
  - `b64bc779-…jpg`
  - `345f2993-…jpg`

  If these images carry the promo content, image-blocking clients and screen readers will get nothing.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (12).** These are `o.gif`, the `dv.cb2.com/o/…` pixel, `UCMController` and `pub1.php` to `pub10.php`. Impact is minor, but a decorative `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- **Zoom disabled.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1">` blocks pinch-zoom on mobile and fails WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the `<head>` (including the "IMPUT" typo). It is harmless to rendering, but it shows an unclean template.
- **Font loading.**
  - `@font-face` rules pull from `www.cb2.com` and from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/…` (a cross-brand dependency).
  - Several URLs use a double slash (`https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/…`).
  - Web fonts are unsupported in many clients, so fallback is `Helvetica, sans-serif`.
- **Language.** `lang="en"` is present on `<html>`, which is good. I could not check table `role="presentation"` because the source was truncated.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The `mi_u` and `mi_mid` pixel parameters are populated. The source was truncated, so the body and footer were not fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found.** QA attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly not capturing it. **Unconfirmed**, not a verified sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** This is the same caveat. If it is truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met (one-click unsubscribe).
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is **unknown**. QA attributes this to the relay. Sending is from `mail.cb2.com`, a subdomain, so alignment should be checked on the raw message.
- **Footer not checked.** The visible in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address (CAN-SPAM) were cut off by truncation and cannot be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not verifiable from the data. All 58 tracking links were skipped and the truncated HTML shows no resolved destinations. UTM parameters, landing-page match to the "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY ENDS TOMORROW" offer, and redirect-chain integrity are therefore unassessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add alt text** to the 8 `image.mail.cb2.com` content images. Use `alt=""` on the tracking pixels.
2. **Move the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS** (`https://login.dotomi.com`, `https://ads.dotomi.com`). Consider dropping the `pub1` to `pub10` cookie-sync chain.
3. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta tag.
4. **Verify headers on the raw message**, not the relay copy: `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mail.cb2.com`.
5. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML** so the footer (unsubscribe link and address) can be checked. Also resolve the 58 tracking links through a redirect follower to check UTM params and landing pages.
6. **Remove the leftover `[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT]` template comment.**

Note: the 57% pass rate reflects the number of checks skipped or warned on, not 43% hard failures. The QA reports 0 issues and 3 warnings.
