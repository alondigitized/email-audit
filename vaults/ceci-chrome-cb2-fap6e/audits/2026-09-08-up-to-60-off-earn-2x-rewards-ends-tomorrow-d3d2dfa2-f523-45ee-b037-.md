# Up to 60% off & earn 2x Rewards ends tomorrow!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Kids running the exact same modular stack I've now tracked seven-plus times this week — countdown timer up top ("00:15:38:39"), then the familiar mega-scroll through kids furniture, bedding, rugs, decor & lighting, clearance, Design Desk, and app-download filler. The render is clean and the countdown is genuinely accurate given the "ends tomorrow" subject claim, so the urgency isn't manufactured this time.
- But this is the same email I've now seen from this brand family (Crate & Kids, Crate & Barrel) daily since 2026-09-02, down to the layout skeleton. There's zero creative differentiation from yesterday's near-identical send ("3 DAYS LEFT! Earn 2x Rewards & get up to 60% off →", 2026-09-07), just a countdown timer decrementing. Fatigue is the dominant read here, not execution quality — the individual send is competent but the cadence is eroding its own effectiveness.

## What's working

- Countdown timer at top ("00:15:38:39") gives real, credible urgency matching the "ends tomorrow" subject line.
- Clear hero offer stack: "up to 60% off The Labor Day Event + Double Rewards" is unambiguous and immediately visible.
- Section-by-section category breakdown (kids furniture, bedding, rugs, decor & lighting, clearance) makes it easy to jump to a specific need.
- "Earn 30% back in Reward Rewards Dollars" callout adds a concrete loyalty incentive beyond the percentage discount.
- No render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are clearly styled.

## What's weak

- This is essentially a reskin of the last several days of sends — same layout, same countdown mechanic, same category grid, no new creative hook.
- Very long scroll (11,796px) — most shoppers won't reach the "we love these for you" product grid or Design Desk section.
- The hero copy leans on generic "Sale Reminder" framing rather than something that acknowledges this is the final push, which undersells the actual urgency.
- Repeated small red asterisk icons throughout feel like unstyled placeholder bullets rather than intentional design elements.
- No visible product pricing/reviews above the fold — first proof point (review stars) doesn't appear until deep in the "loved by parents" section.

## Recommendations

- 1. Break the cadence pattern visually — even within the same promo, vary the hero photo/copy day-to-day so this doesn't read as a copy-paste of yesterday's email to anyone who's seen the last two sends.
- 2. Move the "final day" framing into the hero headline itself, not just the countdown timer, since this is explicitly a last-chance send.
- 3. Shorten the email — cut or collapse the Design Desk and app-download sections; they're diluting the urgency message.
- 4. Add a review/rating badge near the hero product to build trust before the long scroll.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 60% off kids' furniture ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Final hours — 60% off + Double Rewards on Crate & Kids`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Countdown's almost done — shop nursery, bedding & décor before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last chance for 2x Rewards on everything kids.`

# Technical Email Audit — Crate & Kids SALE (2026-09-08)

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Cheetah/Bronto-style responsive email template with heavy MSO conditional CSS; automated QA shows 57% pass rate driven almost entirely by missing `alt` attributes and unverifiable compliance/auth headers rather than structural HTML defects.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 89 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (redirect-domain links, not directly resolvable) — destination validity is unconfirmed and should be manually click-tested before send.
- Multiple third-party tracking pixels are firing: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png` (6x), `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif`, and `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif` (5x, LiveRamp/RLCDN identity pixel) plus a `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/...` open-tracking pixel. Volume of duplicate pixel calls is normal for MI (Bronto/Cheetah) but worth confirming isn't unintentional duplication in the template.
- No broken/malformed `href` markup found in the visible source; full link-by-link audit wasn't possible due to HTML truncation.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 37 images flagged with missing `alt` text, spanning hero/product images (`.jpg`), spacer/divider GIFs, and tracking pixels. Decorative spacers and tracking pixels should use `alt=""`; content-bearing product/offer images (e.g., `bbf6f9d4-...png`, `4e3f4804-...jpg`, `972ca7ba-...jpg`) should carry descriptive alt text — currently none do, which fails WCAG 1.1.1 and degrades screen-reader/blocked-image fallback.
- Template includes standard MSO/Outlook conditional fixes (`mso-table-lspace`, `xml:namespace`, VML) and Apple Mail reformatting suppression (`x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`) — no defects observed in these blocks.
- `color-scheme`/`supported-color-schemes` both fixed to `light` only — no dark-mode variant defined; not an error, but no dark-mode-safe fallback exists if a client forces dark rendering.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%`, `{{`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) visible in the truncated source.
- No issues found based on available HTML.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by automated check. Per the QA note this may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay not surfacing the header rather than true absence — needs confirmation against raw SMTP headers at the ESP.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058, one-click unsubscribe)**: not found — if genuinely absent, this is a compliance gap for Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since Feb 2024 for senders of 5,000+ msgs/day).
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found/unknown, same relay-visibility caveat as above.
- Unsubscribe footer content itself is not visible in the truncated HTML sample provided — cannot confirm a working footer unsubscribe link or physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM requirement) from this excerpt.
- **Recommendation**: verify these three items against raw headers captured outside the AgentMail relay, since the current tooling cannot distinguish "missing" from "not captured."

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot confirm UTM parameter presence/consistency — the 89 tracking links were skipped by the prober and the raw destination URLs aren't visible in the truncated source.
- No landing page destinations were resolvable from the provided HTML excerpt.
- **Recommendation**: re-run with full (untruncated) HTML or resolved click-through URLs to verify UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to content images (product/offer creative); set `alt=""` explicitly on spacer GIFs and tracking pixels to satisfy accessibility checkers without adding noise.
2. Confirm with the ESP/raw SMTP capture whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are truly absent or just not passed through the AgentMail relay — the latter is a monitoring gap, the former is a compliance gap.
3. Capture raw `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) outside the relay to confirm deliverability posture.
4. Re-audit with full/untruncated HTML to validate the 89 skipped tracking links (UTM params, redirect targets) and confirm unsubscribe footer/physical address are present per CAN-SPAM.
