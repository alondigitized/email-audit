# ONE DAY LEFT: SEMI-ANNUAL DINING EVENT
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the strongest of the recent Semi-Annual Dining Event sends, mainly because of the live countdown timer. "SALE ENDS IN 01 : 11 : 52 : 19" sits at the very top and puts real urgency above the fold. The hero is clean: "ENDS TOMORROW / UP TO 30% OFF" in big white type over a moody dining shot, with a "SHOP THE SALE" button. It's a clear, price-led message, and it's more consistent than the 10-24 "FINAL DAYS" send, which paired that subject with "ENDS SOON" creative.
- The email loses focus below the hero. A Thanksgiving delivery banner sits above the offer, and the lower half adds interior design services, a lighting block, app and credit card promos, a text-signup offer and a gift list. It's the fourth send in four days pushing the same dining event or a Warehouse Sale, so a subscriber has seen this offer repeatedly. Only the countdown and the "one day left" framing are new.
- Verdict: a solid closer, with good urgency and a clear offer. The second half dilutes it, and the offer has nothing new to say after four days of repetition.

## What's working

- The countdown timer at the top gives credible, visible urgency that matches the subject.
- "UP TO 30% OFF" is the biggest element in the hero, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The subject ("ONE DAY LEFT") and the hero ("ENDS TOMORROW") agree, unlike the 10-24 send.
- Category tiles for dining furniture, tabletop, lighting and rugs are well shot and give clear shopping paths.
- A second "SHOP THE DINING EVENT" button repeats the main CTA after the tiles.

## What's weak

- The Thanksgiving delivery banner sits above the main offer and competes with it. Its "11/17" order-by date is a second deadline that muddies the one-day message.
- The interior design block's overlay text ("xpert design, tailored to you and always complimentary") is cut off and low-contrast against the busy image, so it's hard to read.
- Interior design, the lighting block, the app, the credit card, text signup and gift list all pull attention away from the sale in the last third.
- The category tiles have labels but no prices or product names, so there's no concrete reason to click one over another.
- Four near-identical sale sends in four days (10-23 Warehouse Sale, 10-24 Dining Event, 10-25 Steals) makes "one day left" feel less credible.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut everything after the second sale CTA, or reduce it to a single line. Keep the email as countdown, hero, tiles and button.
- 2. Move the Thanksgiving delivery note below the hero or into the footer, so the sale ends the only deadline at the top.
- 3. Put a price or a named product on each category tile, such as "Dining tables from $X". That gives people a concrete reason to click.
- 4. Fix the interior design overlay text so it isn't clipped, or drop that block from a sale send.
- 5. Vary the creative across the sale's final days. Showing a best-seller or a "last chance" product would feel less repetitive.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call for 30% off tables & chairs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tables, chairs and tabletop, up to 30% off until midnight tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order by 11/17 for Thanksgiving delivery on in-stock pieces.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has one confirmed rendering defect: an unresolved merge token, `%%reviews_skulist%%`. It also has missing alt text on content images and 10 non-HTTPS third-party pixels. QA pass rate is 43%. The HTML is truncated and the headers weren't captured, so compliance and link-level checks are mostly unverifiable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **64 tracking links were skipped** because they are click-redirect domains, so no destination, redirect chain or 404 was verified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **10 Dotomi cookie-sync pixels use plain `http://`.** They are `http://ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`, plus `http://login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController?dtm_com=2&dtm_cid=2437…`. Clients and proxies may block them as mixed content, and HTTP requests expose the `d98e199bf5027afea04b790655b3dee2` identifier in cleartext.
- **Unneeded third-party calls:** the email loads 11 Dotomi requests on top of the first-party pixels `https://mi.cb2.com/p/up/09aebc5b21344cf7/o.gif?mi_u=…` and `https://dv.cb2.com/o/0040b932-…?mi_cid=…`. This is a deliverability and privacy-footprint concern. Image proxies such as Gmail's will fire or strip these inconsistently.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images are missing alt text.** Eight images on `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/` have no `alt` attribute: `2a60c92d-…`, `0e75b0b4-…`, `d992c912-…`, `d4ddcdc1-…`, `fad212c9-…`, `aa3c1482-…`, `a84a5116-…` and `ad0b5473-…`. With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these may be unreadable, and some may carry the offer content.
- **Tracking pixels have no `alt` at all.** This applies to the Dotomi, `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels. Pixels should carry `alt=""`, not omit the attribute.
- **Zoom is restricted.** The viewport meta is `maximum-scale=1`, which blocks pinch-zoom on mobile. That is an accessibility failure (WCAG 1.4.4).
- Text-size adjustment is also disabled (`-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` on `body` and `*`). This can hinder users who rely on larger system text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **[FAIL] The raw token `%%reviews_skulist%%` is present in the delivered HTML.** This is an unresolved Salesforce Marketing Cloud style personalization string, most likely a dynamic content or review-data block that didn't render. At best it is visible text or a broken block. At worst it exposes internal template variables.
- I found no other tokens in the QA data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Not confirmed as defects:** the checks found no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` headers. The tool notes these may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown.
- **Body-level unsubscribe link and physical postal address:** not verifiable here. The visible HTML is truncated before the footer, and the QA output has no unsubscribe or address check.
- Re-test with raw `.eml` headers before treating any of this as a violation.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Not verifiable. All 64 tracking links were skipped, and the visible HTML doesn't show final destination URLs or UTM parameters. The `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` identifiers on the `mi.cb2.com` and `dv.cb2.com` pixels suggest click tracking is handled by the ESP's redirect layer, not by explicit UTMs.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix `%%reviews_skulist%%`** (highest priority). Check the dynamic content or AMPscript block for the failing SKU lookup, and add a fallback or suppress the block when the data is empty.
2. **Switch the Dotomi pixels to HTTPS, or remove them.** Add `alt=""` to all of them and to the first-party pixels.
3. **Add descriptive `alt` text** to the eight content images listed above.
4. **Remove `maximum-scale=1`** from the viewport meta, and reconsider `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none`.
5. **Re-run compliance and continuity checks** with full `.eml` headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC, `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`) and untruncated HTML. Resolve the tracking redirects in a follow-up pass to confirm destinations and UTMs.
