# Best sellers up to 40% off. Just one more day.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- CB2 finally brings back the urgency lever it was missing all week — "ONE DAY LEFT" up top and "Just one more day" in the subject — paired with the discount roll-up format that worked in the 2026-09-20 "STEALS OF THE WEEK" send. The hero is clean: dark moody tabletop vignette, bold "BEST SELLERS / UP TO 40% OFF" stacked type, single "SHOP THE BEST" CTA. It's a genuine deadline email, not another vague lifestyle mood-board.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email piles on five unrelated asks — category nav, "Need Some Advice" (Hans tips), Interior Design Services, a new sofa launch, app download, rewards, gift registry, and SMS signup — none of which reinforce the 40%-off deadline. By the time a reader scrolls past the second screen, the urgency from the hero has been buried under service pitches that contradict the "one more day, act now" framing.
- Given the brand history — this is CB2's fourth distinct "sale/steals" framing in six days — the offer itself is credible and well-presented, but the email is trying to be three campaigns in one send.

## What's working

- Clear deadline banner ("ONE DAY LEFT") directly above the hero creates real urgency, matching the subject line.
- Hero typography hierarchy is clean — "BEST SELLERS" and "UP TO 40% OFF" are the first thing the eye lands on.
- Single unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP THE BEST") sits right under the hero with no scroll needed.
- Second block reinforces the offer with a category grid + repeated "SHOP UP TO 40% OFF" button, giving a second chance to click without diluting the message.
- No render bugs — clean image loads, aligned text, consistent brand typography throughout.

## What's weak

- The email keeps going for 5+ more full-width sections (design services, new sofa, app, rewards, registry, SMS) that have nothing to do with the sale or the deadline, diffusing urgency.
- "SOFAS & SECTIONALS" section introduces a second, undiscounted "NEW & BEST SELLING" product push mid-sale-email — competing offer inside the same send.
- No specific products, prices, or discount tiers are shown anywhere — just a category grid and a blanket "up to 40%," so a shopper can't tell what's actually discounted.
- "Interior Design Services" block breaks the sale narrative entirely with a full lifestyle photo and "always complimentary" messaging that reads like a different campaign.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the second block (hero + category grid). Move "Need Some Advice," Interior Design Services, Sofas & Sectionals, and the app/rewards/registry footer content into a separate, non-sale send — this email should be 100% about the deadline.
- 2. Add at least 2-3 real product thumbnails with strikethrough pricing under the hero so the 40% claim feels concrete, not abstract.
- 3. Repeat the "ONE DAY LEFT" countdown language again just above the final CTA before the footer clutter starts, since attention decays fast after the fold.
- 4. If Sofas & Sectionals must stay, tag it with its own discount or explicitly label it "not included in sale" — right now it visually competes with the hero offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: up to 40% off best sellers`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — 40% off CB2 best sellers`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Furniture, lighting, decor and more — sale ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your favorites, up to 40% off. Today only.`
