# ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the third free-shipping send in two days. The subject and hero line up: "ends today! free shipping on orders $99+" sits right under a Halloween pumpkin hero. The urgency is credible and the offer is concrete. It works as a last-call reminder, but it arrives after the earlier "Free shipping starts today!" and "2 days only!" emails on 2026-10-18, so it's a near-duplicate.
- The creative buries the offer. A "Semi-Annual Bedroom & Nursery Event, up to 50% off" banner sits above the hero and competes with the shipping message. The free-shipping headline is small, light-weight text in a lot of white space, with a pale grey button that is easy to miss. Below that the email turns into a long catalog: category tiles, a wallcovering launch, a "cozy winter edit," a product grid, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design-service promo. A last-chance shipping email shouldn't carry that many competing messages.
- Verdict: a clear offer, a credible deadline and a recognizable brand, held back by a weak hierarchy, a crowded body and fatigue from the cadence. It's about a 6, in line with the other recent sends.

## What's working

- The subject and the on-email headline match: "ends today! free shipping on orders $99+".
- "Ends today" is believable urgency, because the offer was announced as a two-day event on 10/18.
- The $99 threshold is stated plainly, with "standard shipping; exclusions apply" as a short disclosure under it.
- The pumpkin hero ties the email to the season.
- The category tiles (bedding, toys, lighting, decor, storage, Halloween) let a parent jump straight to what they need, and the tile labels read as clear verb-plus-noun links.
- The "Shop All Free Shipping" button appears twice, once above the tiles and once below.

## What's weak

- The "up to 50% off" banner above the hero competes with the free-shipping offer, so two offers fight for attention.
- The offer headline is small and light-weight, and the grey "Shop All Free Shipping" button has low contrast. The eye lands on the pumpkin photo instead.
- The body runs long and covers many things: wallcovering, the winter edit, a product grid, the credit card, the app, and TheDesignDesk. None of it supports the "ends today" message.
- Prices on the product grid ($38.95, $1,150, $1,449, $295.25 and others) are shown without any shipping context.
- Both shipping buttons lead to a generic "all free shipping" destination rather than a hero product or a curated pick.
- This follows two near-identical shipping emails on 10/18, so the urgency is diluted.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the free-shipping offer the hero. Use a large, high-contrast headline such as "Free shipping ends tonight" with a dark, solid button. Put it above the fold, ahead of the "up to 50% off" banner or in place of it.
- 2. Cut the body to about three modules for a last-call send: category tiles, one product row and a single footer promo. Drop the credit card, app and design-service blocks.
- 3. Add a visible countdown or an explicit cutoff, such as "Ends 11:59 PM PT tonight," so the deadline is a specific time.
- 4. Suppress or consolidate the third shipping reminder. Send one well-timed last-call email to anyone who didn't click the earlier ones.
- 5. Add a free-shipping badge to the product cards, along with a review count or star rating, so the shipping offer carries into the product grid.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: free shipping on $99+ ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: free shipping on nursery & bedroom picks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by midnight PT — bedding, decor, toys and more ship free over $99.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 50% off the Bedroom & Nursery Event, ending soon.`

## Technical Audit: "ENDS TODAY! Free shipping on orders $99+" (Crate & Barrel Kids)

### 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries. The QA run found 0 hard failures, but about 34 images lack alt text, and the header-level checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be confirmed. The source is truncated, so the body, footer and link `href`s were not audited.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **71 tracking links were not probed.** QA skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and broken links are unverified.
- **Third-party pixels are embedded.** `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `n=5` is five LiveRamp pixels. The `s=` value matches the `mi_u=` identifier on the first-party `mi.crateandbarrel.com` pixels, so a per-recipient ID is passed to a third party.
- **First-party tracking pixels:**
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears 6 times.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` is the open pixel.
  - `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/471f6eb6-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` is also present.
- **Duplicate pixels** add requests and can inflate or skew open and impression metrics.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed by QA).**
  - About 15 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/*.jpg|gif|png` have no alt. Examples are `eeb8adae-…jpg`, `67b907bb-…gif` and `fa956f91-…jpg`.
  - The Scene7 asset `2026%5F1002%5FCBK%5FCozyWinterEdit%5FTertiaryVideo` also has none. It looks like a video poster, so the missing alt hides the call to action from screen readers and image-blocking clients.
  - The tracking pixels and the spacer `25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` are also flagged. Those should carry `alt=""`.
  - QA cannot tell a missing attribute from an intentional empty one. Check the content images and pixels separately.
- **Pinch-zoom is blocked.** `<meta name="viewport" content="…maximum-scale=1" />` prevents zooming (WCAG 1.4.4).
- **Font scaling is disabled.** `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; … line-height:100%}` and `html{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none}` turn off text-size adjustment and set a tight line-height on every element.
- **The `<title>` has an unescaped ampersand.** `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` should be `&amp;` under the XHTML doctype. The title is also generic, not the subject line.
- **Conflicting image-width media queries.** Both `@media (min-width:768px){img{max-width:600px}}` and `@media (min-width:640px){img{max-width:640px}}` apply at 768px and above. The later 640px rule wins, so the 600px rule is dead code.
- **Redundant or conflicting meta tags.** `format-detection` is declared four times with partly overlapping values.
- **Leftover template placeholder.** `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source. It is harmless but unclean.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible HTML. There are no unrendered merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The body was truncated, so this only covers the head.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found (WARN).** QA notes that the AgentMail relay may strip it. Treat this as unconfirmed, not a sender defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` was not found (WARN).** The same caveat applies, so RFC 8058 one-click support is unknown. Gmail and Yahoo require it for bulk senders.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment cannot be assessed from this capture. The From domain is `mail.crateandbarrel.com`.
- **The in-body footer (unsubscribe link, physical address) is outside the truncated source.** CAN-SPAM compliance cannot be confirmed or denied.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 71 links were skipped, and no `href`s with UTM or `mi_*` parameters were visible in the truncated source. Nothing confirms that the landing pages show the "free shipping $99+" offer or its end date.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` to the content images and the video poster. Use `alt=""` on spacers and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag, and drop `text-size-adjust:none` and the universal `line-height:100%`.
3. Escape the `<title>` as `&amp;`, use a meaningful title, and remove the duplicate `format-detection` metas and the dead 768px media query.
4. Re-run QA against the raw `.eml` or the full source. That would verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC, the footer, and the `href`s and UTM parameters. It would also confirm whether the header warnings are relay artifacts.
5. Review the five LiveRamp pixels and the repeated first-party pixels for privacy disclosure and consolidation.
6. Probe the tracked links with a follow-redirect check, or by resolving them through the ESP, to confirm the landing pages and offer messaging.
