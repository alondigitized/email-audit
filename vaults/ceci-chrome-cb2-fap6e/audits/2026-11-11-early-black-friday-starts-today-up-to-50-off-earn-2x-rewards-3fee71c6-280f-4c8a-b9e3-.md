# Early Black Friday Starts TODAY! Up to 50% off + Earn 2x Rewards →
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is another Early Black Friday send from Crate & Barrel, this time from the Kids sender. The hero is clear: a kids' bedroom photo under "starts today! up to 50% off Early Black Friday" with a white Shop Now button. A red banner above it reads "last Double Rewards this year!" and gives the Nov 17 deadline. The offer and the deadline are easy to read.
- It's the same kitchen-sink layout as the 2026-11-11 "Up to 30% off + Earn 2x Rewards!" send earlier today. The discount went from 30% to 50% and the sender changed, but the banner, hero and Shop Now button are the same. The "last Double Rewards this year!" line also shows up again as a second red block mid-email. After six Double Rewards-era sends in about three days, the urgency is wearing thin. A recipient who isn't shopping for kids sees a mismatched sender and a lot of repetition.
- Verdict: competent and on-season, but it's high-frequency and the kids framing limits relevance. It's a mid-tier send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The hero states the offer plainly: "up to 50% off Early Black Friday" with a visible Shop Now button.
- The Double Rewards banner at the top gives a dated deadline (Nov 17) and a loyalty angle.
- The 2x2 category grid (furniture, kids bedding & bath, baby bedding & bath, rugs) each carries its own "up to 50% off" label, so the offer is easy to scan.
- The "we love these for you" block shows real products with prices, which gives some purchase detail.
- The "Sign Up Now" block adds a second reason to click: an extra discount for new signups.

## What's weak

- Nearly every section pushes the same "last Double Rewards this year!" message, and the top banner is dense.
- The hero image is generic, with no single product or collection to click.
- The "Shop the Collection" button in the Kids Kitchen section looks faded, so it reads as disabled.
- The email is very long, with many competing blocks: app download, design desk, store locator, credit card, signup. The Black Friday message gets diluted.
- Fine print under the hero ("Select styles") and a large legal block at the bottom hedge the "up to 50%" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one hero, one category grid and one rewards line. Drop the app, design desk and store-locator blocks, which dilute the Black Friday message.
- 2. Make the hero a specific product or collection with a CTA that names it, such as "Shop Kids Beds," instead of the generic Shop Now.
- 3. Merge the two Double Rewards blocks into a single banner so the message isn't repeated.
- 4. Fix the faded "Shop the Collection" button so it matches the others.
- 5. Space the sends out. Alternate Rewards-led and discount-led emails instead of repeating the same layout.
- 6. Shorten the subject to under 50 characters and drop the arrow.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday is on: up to 50% off kids' rooms`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off + 2x Rewards, ends Nov 17`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Beds, bedding and rugs for kids, plus 20% back in Reward Dollars through Nov 17.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last Double Rewards of the year. Shop kids' furniture before it ends.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Marketing Intelligence–style XHTML table layout with 75 click-tracked links. The automated checks flagged no hard failures, only 3 warnings. The main confirmed issues are about 35 images without alt text, template and markup leftovers in `<head>`, and header-level compliance checks that couldn't be verified. The HTML source was truncated, so the body, footer, and link targets weren't inspectable.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click links not verified:** 75 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains, and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=56aaa278…&n=1` through `&n=5` pixels carry a hashed identifier in `s=`. The `n=` values are the only difference, so one pixel is repeated five times with different counters. This is LiveRamp (rlcdn) cross-site tracking. It is a disclosure and consent consideration, and also five extra requests.
- **Per-recipient IDs in image URLs:** `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` appears six times, and `o.gif?mi_u=…` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` appear once each. These are open-tracking pixels or dynamic-content calls keyed to the recipient. If images are blocked, personalized modules fed by `dv.` or `rp/` may not render.
- **Duplicate requests:** The same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` URL is requested six times. If these are separate dynamic modules, that is expected. If not, it is redundant.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (confirmed by QA):** The 17 images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` are `.jpg` or `.gif`. One is `…/m/47/bbf6f9d4-….png`. These are likely product or promo images, so screen readers get nothing. Examples: `26171910-0ada-47d9-a83d-8056129f4c2f.jpg`, `8c4ce5d9-cf7b-4fbe-ba17-b9e2435a0e16.gif`, `de9c8f78-5b2d-49da-86b2-13e80d8ef389.gif`. The QA can't distinguish an absent `alt` attribute from `alt=""`, so confirm which it is.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** `448796.gif`, `o.gif`, and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/373b80e1-…` should carry `alt=""` and `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity, but it can trigger "image missing alt" announcements in some clients.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom, which fails WCAG 1.4.4/1.4.10 intent. `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` appears on both `*` and `body`, which also prevents text resizing.
- **Invalid markup in `<head>`:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - The title is generic and doesn't match the subject line.
  - There are two charset declarations (`http-equiv` and `<meta charset>`).
  - `format-detection` is declared three times, and the first value (`telephone=no,address=no,email=no,date=no,url=no`) is repeated by the later tags.
- **Template leftovers:** The source contains the comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` (typo "IMPUT") and an empty `<!---->`. These are harmless to rendering but show an unfinished template, and they add bytes.
- **Universal selector:** `*{… max-height:1000000px; line-height:100%}` applies a global `line-height:100%`, which can cause clipped text in some clients unless it is overridden per element.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible HTML contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The excerpt was truncated, so the body and footer are unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA notes this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay, so it is not a confirmed sender defect. It should be treated as unverified. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements expect both headers.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sender is `crateandbarrelkids@mail.crateandbarrel.com`, so alignment between the From domain and DKIM `d=` can't be confirmed.
- **Footer not inspected:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated HTML, so neither is confirmed or denied.
- **Subject:** `Early Black Friday Starts TODAY! … →` is not deceptive. No issue found.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All 75 links were skipped as tracking redirects, and the truncated HTML doesn't show final destinations, UTM parameters, or landing-page content. I'm not flagging any issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 18 content images listed above, including the `m/47/…png` and the two `.gif` files. Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` on pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag, and drop `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` from `*` and `body`.
3. Escape the ampersand in `<title>` (`Crate &amp; Barrel`), set a meaningful title, and remove duplicate meta tags and the "IMPUT HERE" placeholder comment.
4. Re-run the QA against the raw `.eml` or a direct capture to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`. Until then, don't treat these as defects.
5. Resolve a sample of the 75 tracking links with a redirect-following probe. Check the final URLs, status codes, and UTM or campaign parameters for landing-page continuity.
6. Review the five `rlcdn.com` pixel calls and the `mi_u` identifier URLs for privacy-policy and consent coverage. Consider consolidating the five identical-purpose pixels.
7. Re-run with the full, untruncated HTML to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address) and merge tokens.
