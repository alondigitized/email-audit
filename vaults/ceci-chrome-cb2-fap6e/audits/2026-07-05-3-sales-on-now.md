# 3 SALES ON NOW
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's consolidation send for the July 4th long weekend — a single email that rolls up all three concurrent sale events (July 4th online 50% off, Floor Sample in-store, Warehouse 60% off) into one scroll. Strategically this makes sense: rather than sending a fourth category-specific email into an already heavy series, CB2 is showing breadth and giving every subscriber at least one hook. The countdown timer (02d 07h 13m 38s) anchors genuine urgency.
- The execution, however, dilutes. Three competing heroes with three different CTAs means no single offer has the focus to convert. The 60% Warehouse Sale — objectively the most aggressive discount in CB2's current arsenal — is buried as the third module. The subject line "3 SALES ON NOW" is all caps and says nothing about the depth of offer. Anyone scanning an inbox sees a generic shout where they could have seen "Up to 60% off."
- The "MORE FOR YOU" personalized product grid at the bottom is the right instinct and the most click-worthy section, but it's far below the fold. This is a technically clean, functionally competent email that settles for good when it had the ingredients for great.

## What's working

- Countdown timer (02:07:13:38) creates genuine scarcity and is prominently placed above the three sale modules.
- Three-sale format shows breadth without repeating prior category sends (furniture, rugs, lighting).
- 60% Warehouse offer is the most aggressive CB2 has shown this cycle — strong card to hold even if played too low.
- "MORE FOR YOU" personalized grid at the bottom is a smart retention play for subscribers who aren't biting on the sale heroes.
- Clean render, no broken images, visual hierarchy is legible at a glance.

## What's weak

- Subject "3 SALES ON NOW" is all caps and carries zero discount signal — the 60% offer is buried in body copy.
- Warehouse Sale (60% off — the best number) is the third module; it should lead.
- All three CTAs are generic ("SHOP NOW" / "SHOP NOW" / "FIND A STORE") — no category specificity to reduce friction.
- Sender display name "CB2 LONG WKND SALE" diverges from the expected "CB2," which adds minor recognition friction at inbox scan speed.
- This is approximately the eighth consecutive July 4th series send — cadence fatigue is real for anyone who's been opening every one.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the hero to the Warehouse Sale (60% off)** — lead with the most aggressive number. July 4th 50% becomes module two, Floor Sample becomes three.
- 2. **Rewrite subject to name the biggest discount** — "3 SALES ON NOW" wastes 14 characters on a count nobody needs.
- 3. **Make CTA copy category-specific** — "Shop Warehouse" / "Shop July 4th Sale" / "Find Floor Samples" over three generic "SHOP NOW" buttons.
- 4. **Surface the "MORE FOR YOU" grid higher** — even moving it above the third sale module keeps personalization in view before the fold break.
- 5. **Restore sender to "CB2"** — the "LONG WKND SALE" suffix is noise that erodes instant recognition.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Warehouse. Floor Samples. July 4th. All on now.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `July 4th, Warehouse & Floor Sample sales — each with a different kind of deal`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Confirmed issues:**

- **`maximum-scale=1` viewport meta** blocks pinch-to-zoom on mobile. This is an accessibility violation for low-vision users and violates WCAG 1.4.4 (Resize Text).
  ```html
  <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />
  ```

- **`-webkit-text-size-adjust: none` applied globally** via the `*` selector prevents OS-level text scaling. Should be `100%`, not `none`.
  ```css
  *{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none;-ms-text-size-adjust:none;...}
  ```

- **Double-slash font URLs** — three `@font-face` declarations use `//` in the path, which produces a malformed URL on some mail client WebView implementations:
  ```css
  src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownLight/lineto-brown-light.woff2")
  src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/BrownRegular/lineto-brown-regular.woff2")
  src: url("https://www.cb2.com//Styles/CB2/fonts/GTSectra/GT-Sectra-Display-Regular.woff2")
  ```

- **Cross-domain font loading from `crateandbarrel.com`** — four `HNW05` `@font-face` declarations pull from `www.crateandbarrel.com/webfonts/`. While CB2 and Crate & Barrel share infrastructure, email clients that enforce strict CORS or content-blocking policies may silently drop these, falling back to system fonts with no visual warning.

- **No dark mode support** — no `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)` block. Hard-coded `background:#ffffff` will flash white in dark-mode clients (Apple Mail, Outlook on iOS) before rendering.

- **Non-standard mobile breakpoint** — `@media only screen and (max-width: 418px)` is unusual; 480px is the standard email breakpoint. The `.mobile` class targets `width:414px`, suggesting the intent is iPhone-specific, but this will miss narrower Android devices.
