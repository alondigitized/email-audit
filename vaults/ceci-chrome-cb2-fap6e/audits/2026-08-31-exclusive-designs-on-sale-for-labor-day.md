# Exclusive designs on sale for Labor Day
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- Subject says "Exclusive designs on sale for Labor Day" but the hero delivers a generic "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF BEST SELLERS" — no "exclusive design" is named or shown anywhere in the render. This is the same bait-and-switch pattern flagged in 2026-08-30's "This best-selling lamp is on sale right now" (5.00/10): subject promises specificity, hero delivers a category-wide markdown.
- Visually the send is clean and on-brand — moody velvet chair hero, well-organized category grid (Furniture, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Shop All), and a genuinely interesting Double Rewards Days upsell (10% → 20% back) further down. But it's the fourth Labor Day sale email from CB2 in three days with near-identical structure and offer language, and this one doesn't even bring a new hook — just a vaguer subject line over the same sale creative.
- Judgement: competent execution, weak differentiation. Nothing here would make me open over the other Labor Day sends already in my inbox, and the subject actively overpromises relative to what's inside.

## What's working

- Hero is immediately legible: bold "THE LABOR DAY SALE / UP TO 50% OFF BEST SELLERS" with a clear "SHOP THE SALE" button, no scrolling needed to find the offer.
- Category grid ("MORE ON SALE THIS WAY") gives multiple concrete entry points (Furniture, Rugs, Lighting, Decor & Mirrors, Entertaining, Shop All) rather than one dead-end CTA.
- Double Rewards Days block is a real, distinct secondary offer (20% back in Reward Dollars through Sept 9) that adds value beyond the sale itself.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text is legible, layout is consistent with prior CB2 sends.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "Exclusive designs" but nothing in the render is framed as exclusive or design-specific — it's a straight category sale, same as three prior sends this week.
- No specific product, price, or "exclusive design" is named anywhere — just "up to 50% off best sellers," which is vague and now repetitive across the brand's last 4-5 emails.
- Cadence fatigue: this is functionally a re-skin of 2026-08-29's "So much is on sale right now" (6.00/10) and 2026-08-28's category sends — same grid, same button copy, same offer tier.
- "Up to 50% off" with a footnote asterisk buries the real discount range in fine print at the bottom, undercutting the "exclusive" framing further.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject-to-hero mismatch first — either name an actual exclusive/limited item in the subject and hero (like the successful "That's so '70s" vintage-reissue send scored 8.00/10), or drop "exclusive designs" language entirely and just say what's true: it's the sale.
- 2. Break the cadence pattern — four near-identical Labor Day sale emails in a row will train subscribers to stop opening. Differentiate this send with a specific curated pick, not another full category grid.
- 3. Move the actual discount range out of the footnote asterisk and into the hero if it's going to claim "exclusive."
- 4. Consider leading with Double Rewards Days instead — it's the most genuinely differentiated content in this email and gets buried below six product tiles.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Template renders correctly with standard MSO/Outlook fallbacks, but has significant compliance and third-party tracking issues — no unsubscribe headers detected and multiple insecure ad-redirect calls embedded as images.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 61 tracking/click-redirect links present in the HTML but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains not verified for reachability/redirect target).
- 10 separate `dotomi.com` ad-redirect calls loaded as `<img>` tags (`ads.dotomi.com/cookieredir/2437/pub1.php` through `pub10.php`), all served over **plain HTTP**, not HTTPS. These are third-party ad pixels, not CB2-owned tracking.
- One additional `dotomi.com` beacon (`login.dotomi.com/ucm/UCMController`) also served over HTTP.
- Primary first-party tracking pixels (`mi.cb2.com/p/up/.../o.gif`, `dv.cb2.com/o/...`) are HTTPS — no issue there.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 9 content images missing `alt` text (all `image.mail.cb2.com/lib/fe9213727564027a72/m/1/*.jpg|png` assets). Screen readers and blocked-image fallback will show nothing for these.
- All 10 `dotomi.com` ad pixels and the `UCMController` beacon also lack `alt` attributes (expected for 1x1 tracking pixels, low impact).
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, and responsive `@media` breakpoints (418px, 690px, 768px) are present — standard defensive markup, no issues found.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tokens or personalization placeholders visible in the truncated source. No issues found.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than a true absence at send time, but as observed, one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe compliance unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from this capture; cannot confirm sender authentication.
- Note: all three are flagged as relay-side capture gaps rather than confirmed sender-side failures — recommend verifying against raw headers at the originating MTA before treating as a hard compliance failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable — HTML source was truncated before CTA/link URLs were visible, and the 61 tracking links were skipped by the probe. No UTM parameters or destination URLs confirmed in the available evidence.

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive `alt` text to the 9 content product/lifestyle images for accessibility and image-blocked rendering.
- Migrate the 10 `dotomi.com` ad-redirect pixels and the `UCMController` beacon from HTTP to HTTPS — non-HTTPS resources are blocked or flagged by modern mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), reducing tracking reliability.
- Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers and Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM) directly at the sending MTA, since the relay used for this capture may not surface them even if present.
