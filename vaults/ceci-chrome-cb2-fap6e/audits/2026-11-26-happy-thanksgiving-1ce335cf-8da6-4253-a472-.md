# Happy Thanksgiving! 🦃
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is Crate & Barrel's Thanksgiving-day send, and it is a full Black Friday catalog under a warm greeting. The top banner is clear: "Biggest Sale of the Year! Up to 50% off Black Friday + Free Shipping" with a "Shop All Deals" button. The "happy Thanksgiving, from our home to yours" hero is a nice brand moment. But the email then runs on for roughly 10,000px of kitchen deals, furniture tiles, ornaments, game-day hosting, product picks, a credit card promo, an app promo and a design-service pitch.
- The render is heavily downscaled (130px wide, shown from 680px), so I can read layout and large headlines but not fine print or prices. This is the seventh send in about three days, and it repeats the same offer stack as the 11/26 "First turkey, then shopping (at up to 50% off!)" email. It will probably get opens from goodwill, but it adds more fatigue than value. Judgment: a decent holiday moment buried in a bloated body.

## What's working

- The top banner puts the offer first: up to 50% off plus free shipping, with a visible "Shop All Deals" button.
- The "happy Thanksgiving, from our home to yours" hero is on-season and warm, with a styled tablescape photo.
- The kitchen-deals block has concrete offers: "up to 40% off top Black Friday kitchen deals" and "20% off select Shun cutlery."
- The product tiles with lifestyle photography are consistent and on-brand.
- The "your house, your game day" tile is a timely angle for the day.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with at least 8 competing sections and 5+ CTAs ("Shop Black Friday," "Shop Now" x2, "Learn More," "Download Now," "Book a Free Appointment"). No single hero action stands out.
- Every tile uses a different "up to X%" figure (40, 60, 30, 80, 50), so the offer reads as scattered, not specific.
- The subject is a greeting with no offer or urgency. It also doesn't build on the stronger "First turkey, then shopping" send from earlier today.
- Credit card, app and TheDesignDesk blocks sit below the deals and dilute the sale message late in the scroll.
- The sender name "Crate & Barrel BLACK FRIDAY" in caps leans on the same blast cadence as the 11/24 and 11/25 sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the banner, the Thanksgiving hero, one deal grid and one CTA. Drop the app, design-service and credit card blocks to a footer strip.
- 2. Put one clear time-bound hook in the subject, such as when the sale ends, so the greeting doesn't have to carry it alone.
- 3. Use a single consistent offer headline ("Up to 50% off + free shipping") and a single primary button color, so the eye has one path.
- 4. Space out the sends. A day with two near-identical blasts hurts the second one.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional build with 78 tracked links, and the main confirmed defects are about 37 images flagged for missing alt text and a viewport tag that blocks user zoom. Compliance and authentication could not be verified. The QA run found no hard failures and 3 warnings (57% pass rate), and the HTML source was truncated.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 78 tracking links were skipped by the QA probe because they are click-redirect domains. Destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are untested.
- **Third-party pixels:** Five LiveRamp pixels are embedded, `https://sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` through `n=5`. There are also two first-party open and impression pixels: `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/ce0c03f6-…`.
- **Subscriber identifier in image URLs:** The `rlcdn` `s=` value (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb6406869640cfd8`) begins with the same string as the first-party `mi_u=` parameter (`56aaa2787a6773d0f164733102dce5192f75df7c634bb941bb640686964…`). The subscriber identifier appears to be passed to a third-party domain on image load.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** All seven `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/*.png` images are tracking pixels with no alt attribute.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** QA flagged 37 images.
  - 23 are content images: 22 on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/{1,47}/…` and 1 on `s7d5.scene7.com`. The Scene7 one is `2026%5F1126%5FCB%5FHappyThanksgiving%5FVideo%2Dopt`, probably a video or animated fallback.
  - The other 14 are the tracking pixels listed above.
  - Content images with no alt are blank to screen readers and to clients that block images. Pixels should carry `alt=""`.
  - The QA output does not say whether the attribute is absent or empty, and the source is truncated, so I can't confirm which.
- **Zoom blocked:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1" />` stops pinch-zoom, which is a WCAG 1.4.4 concern. The universal rule `*{-webkit-text-size-adjust:none; …}` also disables text resizing in WebKit.
- **Markup errors:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
  - `<meta charset="utf-8">` is declared twice, once as `http-equiv` and once not self-closed in an XHTML doctype.
  - `format-detection` is declared four times with overlapping values.
- **Template leftover:** The comment `<!--[IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT if needed]-->` is still in the source, so the template was not cleaned before send.
- **Conflicting max-width rules:** Two desktop `img` rules overlap, `max-width: 600px` at `min-width: 768px` and `max-width: 640px` at `min-width: 640px`. They have equal specificity, so the later 640px rule wins at 768px and above and the 600px rule never applies.
- **Overbroad line-height:** The universal `*{… line-height:100%}` affects every element and can tighten wrapped text.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the visible source. This covers only the truncated excerpt.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** QA did not find the `List-Unsubscribe` header. It may be stripped by the AgentMail relay rather than missing at the sender, so this is unconfirmed.
- **One-click unsubscribe:** QA did not find `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058). The same relay caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders.
- **Authentication:** The `Authentication-Results` header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status can't be assessed. The sending domain, `mail.crateandbarrel.com`, has no verified alignment from this data.
- **Body items not verifiable:** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source.
- **Pixel disclosure:** The third-party `rlcdn.com` pixel sharing in section 2 is a privacy and consent disclosure concern.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. Click URLs are wrapped in tracking redirects (78 skipped by QA) and the destinations were not resolved. UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 23 content images, including the Scene7 video fallback. Use `alt=""` on all pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport tag and the `-webkit-text-size-adjust:none` rule.
3. Fix the `<title>` to `Crate &amp; Barrel` and drop the duplicate `charset` and `format-detection` meta tags.
4. Delete the `IMPUT HERE CLIENT FONT IMPORT SCRIPT` comment and the dead `600px` rule.
5. Re-run the audit against raw `.eml` source, with full headers and the full HTML, to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC and the footer address.
6. Resolve the tracked links through the redirect chain to check destinations and UTM parameters.
7. Review whether passing the subscriber `mi_u` identifier to `sr.rlcdn.com` matches the stated privacy policy.
