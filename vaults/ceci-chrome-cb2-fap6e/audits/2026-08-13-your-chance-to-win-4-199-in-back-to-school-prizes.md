# Your chance to win $4,199 in back-to-school prizes!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a co-branded sweepstakes email — "The Ultimate Back-to-School Giveaway," $4,199 in prizes across nine partner brands (Crate&Kids, Bumbleride, SMOL, oeuf, Gathre, Dough, sarandipity, Meri Meri, bentgo). It's a clean, well-organized render: sharks-print backpack hero, clear "Enter Now" CTA, a tidy sponsor-logo grid with per-prize dollar values. This matches the subject line precisely, which is refreshing given recent Crate&Kids sends have had subject/hero mismatches.
- The problem is what comes after the entry CTA. The email doesn't stop at the giveaway — it keeps going into bestsellers, personalization, a "we love these for you" product grid, a credit card pitch, an app download push, and a design-service pitch. That's six additional asks stacked under one giveaway entry. A sweepstakes email should be a focused, low-friction ask; this one dilutes its own hook by turning into a full catalog send.
- Net: strong, honest top half; overloaded, unfocused bottom half. Score reflects the giveaway itself but the endless scroll of tangential asks will cost engagement.

## What's working

- Subject line ("chance to win $4,199 in back-to-school prizes") is delivered on exactly, dollar-for-dollar, in the hero.
- Prize grid is transparent — each partner brand's logo and gift card value is spelled out, no vague "amazing prizes" language.
- Two clearly labeled "Enter Now" CTAs (top and bottom of the giveaway block) with strong button contrast.
- Sweepstakes disclosure ("Sponsored in part by MOTHER") is visible, not buried — reads as honest.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent.

## What's weak

- Email doesn't end after the giveaway — it tacks on bestsellers, personalization, a product grid, a credit card offer, and an app pitch, burying the actual CTA under five more asks.
- No deadline or entry window visible anywhere — sweepstakes urgency ("ends 8/20", "one entry per day") is completely absent.
- No odds, eligibility, or entry-mechanics language visible (states excluded, age limit, etc.) — just a footnote asterisk with no visible resolution near the fold.
- "we love these for you" personalization claim sits oddly in a mass sweepstakes email — feels like a bolted-on template block, not a decision made for this send.
- Second half competes for attention with the giveaway rather than supporting it — a reader who scrolls past "loved by parents" has mentally left the sweepstakes.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the giveaway block (or the bestsellers row at most). Drop the personalization, product grid, credit card, app, and design-desk sections entirely — they're diluting a single clean ask into six competing ones.
- 2. Add a visible deadline/entry-window line right under "Enter for Your Chance to Win" — sweepstakes without urgency lose to the inbox scroll.
- 3. Surface the entry mechanics near the CTA (one sentence: how many entries, how winner is picked, when) rather than relying on an unresolved asterisk.
- 4. Move the "Sponsored in part by MOTHER" disclosure closer to the top CTA too, not just the bottom — first impression matters for trust.
- 5. If the second half must stay for revenue reasons, split it into a separate send a day or two later so the giveaway keeps its own moment.
