## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard table-based layout with 79 click-tracked links and a large amount of image content. The automated QA shows many images with no alt text and no visible List-Unsubscribe or authentication headers. The headers could not be confirmed because the AgentMail relay may strip them. The HTML source was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data shows.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Destinations unverified:** 79 tracking and click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none of their redirect chains, destinations or status codes were validated.
- **Third-party pixels:**
  - 5 `sr.rlcdn.com/448796.gif?s=…&n=1` to `n=5` requests (LiveRamp) are in the body.
  - 6 identical `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/rp/f3c0d22992a01946.png?mi_u=…` requests appear.
  - `mi.crateandbarrel.com/p/up/50c75a732e99b42a/o.gif` and `dv.crateandbarrel.com/o/fef14703-…?mi_cid=…&mi_mid=…` are also present.
  - These add several extra tracking requests per open and put a per-recipient hashed ID (`mi_u`) in image URLs.
- **Redundant `mi` pixel:** the same `f3c0d22992a01946.png` URL is requested 6 times. This is duplicate overhead, not a functional break.
- **Spacer image:** `s7d5.scene7.com/is/image/Crate/25%5FMI%5FBottom%5FSpacer%5F40px%5FWhite` is served from a different host (Scene7) than the rest of the creative. It is a minor dependency.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** the QA flagged 37 `<img>` entries. They break down as:
  - about 24 content images on `image.mail.crateandbarrel.com/lib/fe9013727564027a7c/m/1/…` (jpg and gif, plus one png)
  - the Scene7 spacer
  - the tracking pixels listed above

  Pixels and spacers should carry `alt=""`. The content images need real alt text, because with images blocked those blocks render as empty space. Animated GIFs such as `bafa6654-…gif`, `8c4ce5d9-…gif` and `9c5d35f4-…gif` also have no text fallback.
- **Viewport:** `<meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1, maximum-scale=1">` disables pinch-zoom on mobile, which is an accessibility problem.
- **Metadata:**
  - `<title>Crate & Barrel</title>` has an unescaped `&`, which is invalid under the declared XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype. It should be `&amp;`.
  - `format-detection` is declared three times with conflicting or redundant values.
  - Both `<meta http-equiv="Content-Type">` and `<meta charset>` are present.
- **Global CSS:** the global rule `*{… line-height:100%}` applies to every element, and `p{margin:0 auto; text-align:center}` is global too. Both can cause unintended inheritance in clients that apply the style block (not confirmed from the source shown).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens, such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}`, appear in the visible source or QA output. The hashed `mi_u` values are resolved. The truncated source limits this check.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe:** not found. The QA notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found, with the same relay caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe, so verify against the raw message.
- **Authentication-Results:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The `From` domain `mail.crateandbarrel.com` is aligned with the image and tracking subdomains, but alignment can't be verified without the headers.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical postal address were not visible in the truncated source and could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTMs:** no UTM parameters were observable. The 79 links go through tracking redirects and were not probed, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment are unverified.
- **Identifiers:** `mi_cid` and `mi_mid` appear on the `dv.` pixel, so campaign and message identity is passed to Movable Ink. Whether that carries through to the site is unknown.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the roughly 24 content images, including the GIFs. Add `alt=""` to the spacer and tracking pixels.
2. Remove `maximum-scale=1` from the viewport meta tag.
3. Check the raw source message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and `Authentication-Results`. The relay may be dropping them, so don't treat them as confirmed gaps until then.
4. Follow a sample of the 79 tracking links through their redirects (for example, with a headless browser) to confirm destination status and UTM or campaign parameter preservation.
5. Deduplicate the repeated `f3c0d22992a01946.png` pixel (6 requests) and review whether 5 separate `rlcdn.com` pixels are needed.
6. Escape the `&` in `<title>` and remove the duplicate meta tags.
