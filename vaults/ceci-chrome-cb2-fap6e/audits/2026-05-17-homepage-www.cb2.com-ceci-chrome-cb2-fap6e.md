# Homepage snapshot · www.cb2.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- CB2 is doing exactly what I'd expect — Memorial Day Sale energy front and center, "up to 50% off" screaming at me before I even get to see what's actually on sale. The moody, palm-shadow hero is very on-brand for that cool, editorial-meets-interiors aesthetic I come here for. But then they immediately slap a modal in my face demanding my email AND phone number, so... we're starting with friction, not seduction.

## What's working

- **"MEMORIAL DAY SALE — UP TO 50% OFF" hero:** Big, bold, and I believe it. CB2 runs real sale events and this feels legit, not clickbait.
- **Double Rewards banner (12–18%):** Stacked at the very top where I can't miss it. As someone who games the sale calendar, knowing I'm earning double points during a sale event is genuinely motivating.
- **Palm/shadow hero imagery:** That moody, textured lifestyle shot is distinctly CB2 — it's doing brand work and sale work simultaneously, which is hard to pull off.
- **"THE COOLEST PEOPLE WE KNOW" editorial section:** Even partially obscured, I can see it's doing aspirational lifestyle storytelling. That's the content that makes me linger on CB2 vs. just clicking straight to product.

## What's weak

- **The immediate email + phone capture modal:** This is the single worst first impression. I opened the app and before I can see what's on sale, there's a wall asking for my email AND mobile number. Two required fields out of the gate is aggressive. It kills momentum exactly when I'm most curious.
- **"SHOP THE MEMORIAL DAY SALE" CTA is obscured:** The modal physically covers it. I have to dismiss the pop-up before I can interact with the actual hero. That's a conversion killer.
- **"UP TO 50% OFF" with an asterisk:** The asterisk leads to fine print about "CB2 merchandise purchases" and reward percentages. The DOUBLE REWARDS banner is also asterisked and vague. As someone who reads fine print, this triggers skepticism.
- **No product previews above the fold:** I can't see a single actual product. The hero is purely atmospheric. I have no idea if the sale includes the velvet sofa I've been eyeing or just throw pillows.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the email modal with a bottom drawer or toast notification** — let me see the hero first, then nudge me after 10 seconds with a smaller, less disruptive prompt. I'm far more likely to sign up when I'm already engaged than when I'm ambushed at the door.
- **Add 3–4 sale category tiles directly below the hero** ("Sofas & Seating," "Lighting," "Outdoor," "Rugs") so I can tap into the actual sale inventory in one move — right now the page tells me there's a sale but gives me no path into it without dismissing the modal first.
- **Make the rewards math concrete in the banner** — "Earn 12–18% back today only" with a hard cutoff date is more compelling than the current copy which reads like footnote boilerplate.
- **Swap one of the lifestyle images for a product-forward shot** — even one glamour image of a specific hero product (a chrome coffee table, a sculptural vase) anchors the sale in something I can actually want.
