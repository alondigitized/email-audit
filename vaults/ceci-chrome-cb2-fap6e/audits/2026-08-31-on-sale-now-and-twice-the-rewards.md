# On sale now and twice the rewards
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-31**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2 pivoting away from its usual furniture-forward Labor Day creative into a co-branded credit-card acquisition push, and the shift shows. The hero commits hard to "DOUBLE REWARDS IS ON — GET ~~10%~~ 20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS," which is a bold, legible offer — but it's a rewards-card pitch, not a product pitch, so it doesn't land in the "furniture shopper" lane the way CB2's best sends do (see 2026-08-29's "BACK IN STOCK," 7/10, which opened on a styled vignette instead).
- The category grid (Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs) is well-photographed and on-brand, and the Labor Day banner underneath gives a second, more familiar entry point ("UP TO 50% OFF"). But stacking a credit-card offer, a category grid, AND a sale banner in one email dilutes all three — nobody knows if this is a card pitch or a sale email. The footer then undercuts the hero by listing the *base* reward rate as "10% Back," which sits awkwardly next to the "20%" headline just a few screens up.
- Net: clean production, one genuinely concrete offer, but a split-identity email that buries its own sale message under a credit-card pitch most recipients didn't ask to think about. Middle-of-the-pack, consistent with this brand's Labor Day week output.

## What's working

- The hero offer is unambiguous — "20% BACK IN REWARD DOLLARS" with a visible strikethrough on "10%" makes the doubling mechanic instantly readable.
- The date window ("NOW THROUGH SEPT 9TH, 2026") is stated plainly at the very top, so urgency doesn't require hunting.
- The six-category shop grid (Living Room, Lighting, Dining Room, Decor & Mirrors, Bedroom, Rugs) is well-photographed, consistently styled, and gives multiple entry points for a browsing shopper.
- Brand voice and photography are consistent with prior CB2 sends — moody, editorial, on-brand throughout.

## What's weak

- The email leads with a credit-card rewards pitch, not furniture — a mismatch for anyone opening expecting the "sale" half of the subject line.
- The hero's "20% back" and the footer's "EARN 10% BACK... every day" sit in the same email without reconciling — a scanning reader will read this as contradictory, not complementary.
- The Labor Day sale banner ("UP TO 50% OFF") is buried below the credit-card block and the full category grid — a reader has to scroll past two other messages to reach the actual sale.
- No single hero product or price is shown anywhere — everything links to category or program pages, never a specific item.
- The email is trying to do three jobs (card acquisition, category browse, sale) at once, and none of them get the space to be the clear single CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Split this into two sends: a dedicated card-acquisition email and a separate Labor Day sale email. Trying to combine them is why the hierarchy feels muddled.
- 2. If they must stay combined, put the sale banner first (it matches the subject line's "on sale now") and demote the card pitch to a secondary footer module, not the hero.
- 3. Fix the 10%/20% inconsistency — either drop the footer's "10% Back" module from this send entirely, or add a one-line clarifier ("10% every day, 20% now through Sept 9") so it doesn't read as contradictory.
- 4. Swap "LEARN MORE" for a more specific CTA on the rewards block, e.g. "See Your Rewards Rate" — "Learn More" is the weakest CTA in the email.
- 5. Anchor the sale section with one hero product and price, not just a category grid — nothing here gives a reason to click beyond "browse."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double your rewards, up to 50% off furniture`
- **Subject Alt B:** `20% back through Sept 9 — plus the Labor Day sale`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Card offer and sitewide sale, both end Sept 9`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Rewards doubled, savings up to 50% — see what qualifies`
