# The Upholstery Event is on
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-25**
## Executive summary

- This is CB2's cleanest send of the week — a single, coherent story (Semi-Annual Upholstery Event, up to 25% off) instead of the mosaic-of-tiles or app-gate approach seen in the 2026-09-24 sends. The hero is unambiguous: bold "UPHOLSTERY EVENT" headline, "UP TO 25% OFF," and a "SHOP THE SALE" button all land in one scroll, over a warm, styled living-room shot.
- Where it falls apart is the middle: after the hero, it pivots to a swatch-ordering CTA, then four near-identical furniture category tiles (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches and Ottomans) with no pricing or urgency attached to any of them, and then bafflingly closes with a second, unrelated "SALE ON NOW UP TO 50% OFF" banner that undercuts and confuses the 25% headline offer. That closing banner is a real problem — it reads like two different sales got stitched into one email.
- Solid top, muddled middle, contradictory bottom. Worth opening, only worth clicking if you're already upholstery-shopping.

## What's working

- Clear, singular hero message — one headline, one number (25%), one button, no competing offers up top.
- Category breakdown (Sectionals/Sofas/Accent Chairs/Benches) helps a shopper self-select into their specific need.
- "Custom, in-stock and rarely on sale" framing gives the discount credibility — it signals scarcity without being gimmicky.
- Clean product photography with consistent warm styling throughout.
- Footer has real utility signals (app download, rewards, text signup) rather than pure legal clutter.

## What's weak

- The closing "SALE ON NOW UP TO 50% OFF" banner directly contradicts the 25% hero offer with no explanation of what it applies to — this is the single biggest trust/clarity problem in the email.
- None of the four category tiles (Sectionals, Sofas, Accent Chairs, Benches and Ottomans) show a price, a discount, or even a link label distinct from the section header — pure image/text, no reason to click one over another.
- The swatch-ordering module ("Order 10 free swatches now") interrupts the sale narrative right after the hero, before you've even seen product options.
- No urgency/deadline visible anywhere in the body (no end date on the upholstery event), unlike the brand's 2026-09-22 "Just one more day" send which used urgency well.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut or relocate the 50% off banner.** If it's a separate sale, it needs its own email or at minimum a labeled section ("Also on sale: Decor up to 50% off") — as-is it reads like a pricing error and will erode trust in the 25% headline.
- 2. **Add a deadline to the hero.** "Rarely on sale" implies scarcity but gives no actual end date — pull the urgency lever the brand used successfully on 2026-09-22.
- 3. **Put price/discount info on each category tile**, not just a label — even "Sofas from $XXX" beats a bare image.
- 4. **Move the swatch CTA after the category grid**, not immediately after the hero — let the sale message breathe before pivoting to a research/consideration action.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Custom, in-stock upholstery — rarely marked down, ends soon.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sofas, sectionals, chairs and benches, all on sale now.`
