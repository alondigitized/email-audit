## 4. What I'd Change

1. **Hero Product Name & Price:**
   - **Current:** The hero image lacks a name and price tag.
   - **Improvement:** Include the product name and price next to or below the hero image to provide immediate context and urgency.

2. **Soft Offer or Loyalty Hook:**
   - **Current:** No additional incentive is provided beyond "Shop Now."
   - **Improvement:** Add a soft offer such as "Earn Double Rewards this Weekend" or "Free Shipping on All Orders."

3. **CTA Refinement:**
   - **Current:** The primary CTA reads simply as "Shop Now."
   - **Improvement:** Consider refining the CTA to something like "Discover Your Must-Have Pieces," which aligns better with the preheader and subject.

4. **Slower Cadence:**
   - **Current:** Sent immediately after a Memorial Day blitz.
   - **Improvement:** Hold this email for a day or swap it with another mailer to give customers some breathing room between promotional emails.
