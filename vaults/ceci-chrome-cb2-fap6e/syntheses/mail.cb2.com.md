# This is CB2's back-in-stock notification for wall decor, a genuinely useful email for customers who have previously show

### 1. Overview

The email aims to notify customers about wall decor items that are back in stock. It includes a grid of products and a clear call-to-action. However, it lacks a compelling subject line and a clear hero product.

### 2. Key Takeaways

- **Subject Line:** The subject line is vague and doesn't provide enough context to entice the recipient to open the email.
- **Hero Product:** There is no hero product to draw attention and create a focal point.
- **Offer:** No special offer or urgency is provided to encourage immediate action.
- **Visual Hierarchy:** The product grid is scattered and lacks a clear focal point.

#### Hero Product
- **Current:** No hero product is featured.

#### Offer

- **Issues:** Without an offer, there is no immediate incentive for the recipient to take action.
- **Suggestions:**
  - Add a special offer like "Earn Double Rewards this weekend."
  - Include a limited-time discount or a free gift with purchase.

#### Visual Hierarchy
- **Current:** The product grid is scattered and lacks a clear focal point.
- **Issues:** The scattered layout makes it difficult for the recipient to quickly identify and engage with the products.
- **Suggestions:**
  - Use a hero image or product at the top of the email.
  - Organize the product grid in a more structured manner with clear sections and categories.

### 4. What I'd Change

1. **Lead with a specific product or room — not just "colors."** Pick one statement piece from the collection and hero it with a name and price.
2. **Add a soft offer or loyalty hook.** Even "earn Double Rewards this weekend" creates a reason to click now vs. later.
3. **Rewrite the CTA beyond "SHOP NOW."** Something like "Shop the Palette" or "Explore Warm Tones" gives the click a destination, not just a command.
4. **Slow down the cadence.** Hold this email for a day — or swap it with the 5/28 Midseason Sale mailer — to let the audience breathe after the Memorial Day blitz.
