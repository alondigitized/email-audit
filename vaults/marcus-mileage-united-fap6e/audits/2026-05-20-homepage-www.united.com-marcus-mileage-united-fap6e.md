# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I open united.com on my phone and I'm immediately hit with two things stacked on top of each other: a sign-in modal demanding my MileagePlus number and a cookie consent banner taking up the bottom third of the screen. I can't see a single pixel of the actual homepage — not a flight search widget, not my upgrade queue, nothing. United clearly wants me authenticated before I get anything useful, which I respect in principle as a 1K, but this execution is clumsy and friction-heavy right out of the gate.

## What's working

- **MileagePlus sign-in prompt** — "Email or MileagePlus® number" field signals the site knows I have an account and wants to personalize my experience. As a 1K I want personalization, so the intent is right.
- **Clean modal design** — the sign-in form itself is uncluttered: large input field, bold "Continue" CTA in United blue, "Need help signing in?" escape link. No visual noise on the modal itself.
- **Dismissible overlay** — the X in the top right means I can skip sign-in and still get to the homepage. That's not ideal UX but at least the escape hatch exists.

## What's weak

- **Double-modal wall** — a sign-in sheet AND a cookie banner land simultaneously. I'm a 1K who books 30+ segments a year and I literally cannot see the homepage. This is hostile to the highest-value customers.
- **Cookie banner copy** — "personalized ads from United or our third-party partners" on a loyalty site feels tone-deaf. I'm not here to be advertised to; I'm here to book my next trip. That framing erodes trust.
- **Zero content visible** — no flight search, no upgrade-status hook, no partner miles offer, no current route promotion. The homepage is doing absolutely nothing to move me toward a booking action.
- **"Continue" is vague** — the CTA doesn't tell me what continues. A 1K on mobile wants "Sign in to see my upgrades" not a generic button label.
- **Second button partially cut off** — there's another rounded button below "Continue" that's clipped by the cookie banner. I can't read it. Layout collision between two overlays.

## Recommendations

- **Stagger the modals** — show the cookie banner first (one tap to dismiss), then surface the sign-in prompt only after the user has seen one screen of actual homepage content. Never stack both simultaneously.
- **Add status context to the sign-in CTA** — change "Continue" to "Sign in to view your upgrades" or "Access your 1K dashboard." Give me a reason to authenticate beyond a vague promise of "best experience."
- **Surface a flight-search widget behind the modal** — even a blurred or dimmed flight-search widget in the background tells me what the site is for and makes the sign-in feel like an enhancement, not a gate.
- **Reframe the cookie copy** — replace "personalized ads" language with "personalize your booking experience" to reduce friction for loyalty members who are already opted into the United ecosystem.
