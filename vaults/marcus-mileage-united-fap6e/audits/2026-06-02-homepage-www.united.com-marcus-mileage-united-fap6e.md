# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- I opened United's homepage and immediately got hit with two walls at once: a full-screen sign-in modal demanding my MileagePlus number before I can see anything, and a cookie consent banner stacked on top of that. As a 1K who flies United 100k+ miles a year, I *know* my MileagePlus number by heart — but this is still a hostile first impression. The actual homepage is completely invisible. I cannot evaluate a single promotional element, fare deal, or upgrade offer because United has pre-empted all of it with friction.

## What's working

- **Sign-in framing:** "Sign in for the best experience" is at least accurate — United's personalized content *is* better when logged in. The copy sets the right expectation rather than being deceptive.
- **"Email or MileagePlus® number" label:** Acknowledges the loyalty program by name. For a 1K like me, entering my MP number is second nature, so the ask itself isn't wrong.
- **"Continue" button:** Large, high-contrast blue, full-width on mobile. Easy to tap, no ambiguity about the action.
- **"Need help signing in?" link:** Good accessibility fallback — fine print but correctly placed.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen sign-in modal blocks everything:** I cannot see a single flight deal, upgrade promotion, destination, or any homepage content. This is United treating me — a customer who has handed them hundreds of thousands of dollars in fares — like I'm an unknown visitor who needs to prove myself before being allowed to window-shop.
- **Simultaneous cookie banner + sign-in modal:** Two separate interrupt layers at the same time is a dark pattern. I have to dismiss both before I can see anything. On mobile, the cookie banner partially overlaps the sign-in button, making the second CTA illegible (cut off at the bottom).
- **Zero personalization despite having my identity:** If United already knew I was coming back (cookie-based recognition), the modal should pre-fill my MP number or greet me. Instead it's a blank slate.
- **No homepage content whatsoever:** There are no fare sales, no upgrade availability banners, no seasonal campaigns, nothing. The entire above-the-fold experience is a gate, not a storefront.
- **Cookie banner timing:** Firing a cookie notice *underneath* a sign-in modal creates a stacking UX disaster — the bottom CTA on the sign-in sheet is partially obscured.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the sign-in modal by one scroll or one tap** — let me see the homepage for 3 seconds, then prompt. Or make it a persistent but dismissible header strip, not a full-screen takeover.
- **Sequence the interrupts** — show cookie consent first, dismiss it, then optionally surface a sign-in nudge as a top banner, not a modal wall.
- **For known returning visitors (via cookie), pre-populate the MP number field** or show a personalized greeting ("Welcome back, Marcus") to reduce login friction for 1Ks who visit frequently.
- **Surface at least one fare or upgrade offer above the fold** even in a signed-out state — something like "1K upgrade availability is strong on these routes this week" would stop me from bouncing before I even log in.
