# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I've opened united.com and I can't see the homepage — not because of a load failure, but because United has thrown a sign-in modal and a cookie-consent banner on top of everything, simultaneously. The entire viewport is wall-to-wall interstitials. As a 1K member who's been flying United for 20 years, being force-stopped at the door before I can see a single fare, upgrade offer, or route is genuinely annoying. This is a CRM team overriding the customer experience.

## What's working

- **Sign-in modal names MileagePlus directly** — "Email or MileagePlus® number" is the right credential framing for a loyalty member; United isn't pretending they don't know me.
- **"Continue" CTA is clear and prominent** — large blue pill button, no ambiguity about what tapping it does.
- **"Need help signing in?" is there** — saves me from hunting for a recovery link if my app credentials are stale.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is invisible** — two overlays (sign-in modal + cookie banner) cover 100% of the viewport. There is no homepage to evaluate, just gatekeeping.
- **Cookie banner stacks on top of the sign-in modal** — I have to deal with two separate dismissal flows before I can see anything. That's two interruptions before I've read a single word of content.
- **The second button on the sign-in modal is cut off** — the bottom of what appears to be an alternate sign-in option is obscured by the cookie banner. The UI is literally broken by its own layers.
- **No "skip" or "continue as guest" is visible** — I can see "Continue" but if there's a guest path, it's hidden under the cookie banner. That's a wall for someone who just wants to check a fare.
- **Zero upgrade, fare, or route messaging** — as a 1K member who checks upgrade clearance obsessively, landing on a blank modal instead of a "Check your upgrade status" or "Routes with upgrade availability" hook is a wasted opportunity.

## Recommendations

- **Show me something behind the modal on first load** — blur or dim the homepage, don't replace it entirely. I should be able to see a hero image or promotional banner before deciding whether to log in.
- **Serialize the overlays — cookie banner should not stack on the sign-in modal** — sequence them: cookie consent first, then (optionally) the sign-in prompt. Two simultaneous overlays is a UX failure.
- **Surface my status in the sign-in prompt** — if United knows I've visited before (cookie or app state), replace "Sign in for the best experience" with something like "Welcome back, 1K member — sign in to see your upgrade queue." That's the hook that makes me tap immediately.
- **Add a visible "Continue without signing in" option** — make it text-link level so it doesn't compete with "Continue," but make it findable without scrolling.
