# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Opening united.com and getting hit with a sign-in gate stacked on top of a cookie consent banner is exactly the kind of friction I don't need when I'm in an airport at 6 AM trying to check upgrade availability. The site knows who I am — I'm a 1K member with a MileagePlus number I could type in my sleep — but instead of surfacing my mileage balance, upgrade queue, or any fare intel, it shows me two overlapping modals and a white void. This doesn't read like a premium airline homepage. It reads like a portal stuck in 2011.

## What's working

- **MileagePlus® number field label**: Signals this sign-in is for loyalty members, not just casual browsers — the right framing for a returning 1K
- **"Continue" CTA button**: Full-width, high-contrast blue, no ambiguity — I know exactly what to tap
- **"Need help signing in?" link**: A small thing, but it's there for the one time in 50 I can't remember which email alias I registered with
- **Close (X) button on sign-in modal**: Gives me an escape hatch if I want to browse without logging in — at least they're not hard-gating me

## What's weak

- **Double-modal layering**: The sign-in overlay and the cookie consent banner are both on screen simultaneously — competing for my attention and visually chaotic
- **Zero homepage content visible**: No hero image, no route deals, no upgrade offers, nothing. For a 1K who opens this app looking for upgrade clearance on tomorrow's ORD-LAX, this is a dead end before the page even loads
- **Cookie banner copy is dense and passive**: "We use cookies for site functionality, use experience, to analyze traffic and to show personalized ads from United or our third-party partners" — that's a lot of legalese to dump on me while I'm also being asked to sign in
- **No status or balance preview**: Even before I log in, United could show me a "Welcome back, 1K member" prompt or a balance teaser. Instead: blank white space above the sign-in form
- **Sign-in flow sends me to another step**: "Continue" implies there's a second screen (password), not a one-tap sign-in. For a 1K member who uses this weekly, biometric or saved-credential shortcut should be the default

## Recommendations

- **Resolve the modal conflict before launch**: Serve the cookie consent banner first, collapse it on accept, then trigger the sign-in prompt — never both at once
- **Replace the white void above the sign-in form with a logged-out preview**: Show mileage totals masked ("XX,XXX miles — sign in to see your full balance"), upgrade availability on recently-booked routes, or a summer fare banner — something that rewards me for continuing
- **Add a biometric/saved-credential shortcut**: "Sign in with Face ID" or auto-populate from the device keychain — weekly users like me shouldn't be typing a MileagePlus number every visit
- **Surface a concrete offer in the background behind the sign-in gate**: Even a blurred or dimmed hero with "Member-exclusive fares from $149" is better than a white void — gives me a reason to complete the sign-in
