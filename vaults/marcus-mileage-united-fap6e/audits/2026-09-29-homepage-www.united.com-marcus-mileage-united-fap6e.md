# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a working airline booking tool, not a shopping page. The search form is front and center and works the way I expect. The only real pitch is the 60,000-mile Explorer card on the right, and a "Sign in for the best experience" popup and a cookie banner are both sitting on top of the page. If I'm already 1K, none of that promo is aimed at me, but I can still get to my booking in one glance.

## What's working

- **Booking widget:** Book / Flight status / Check-in / My trips tabs, Roundtrip / One-way, and From / To / Dates / Travelers are all in one card. It's what I open the site for.
- **"Book with miles" and "Flexible dates" checkboxes:** They sit right at the top of the form. I book on miles a lot, so I don't have to dig for that.
- **"Advanced search (Certificates, multi-city and upgrades)":** It names my use case. Upgrades are what I care about most.
- **Prefilled origin:** "Burbank BUR" and the Nov 27–29 dates save me typing.
- **Top nav:** MileagePlus, Manage my trips, and Deals are one tap away.
- **"Find flights" button:** It's the darkest, heaviest element on the card, so it's obvious what to press.

## What's weak

- **"Sign in for the best experience" popup:** It sits over the top of the card promo and clips the offer. It also asks a returning 1K to authenticate before I've done anything.
- **Cookie banner:** It takes up the bottom strip of the screen and hides whatever module starts right under the hero.
- **Card promo:** It's the whole right side of the hero, but it's a cold-acquisition offer. A 1K flyer doesn't need an intro card pitch. Nothing on that side shows my status, miles balance, or upgrade clearance.
- **Sign-in popup copy:** "Best experience" is vague. It doesn't say what signing in gets me, such as my balance, my upgrades, or my saved trips.
- **Cabin dropdown:** It defaults to "Economy". For me that's a wrong default, and nothing on the form surfaces upgrade or Premium cabin options.
- **Background:** It's an empty blue cloud sky with no product, route, or fare content.
- **"Changed bag rules" and "fees for optional services" links:** They read as fine-print disclosure wedged under the CTA.

## Recommendations

- When a signed-in or recognized MileagePlus member arrives, replace the card ad with a status module: "Welcome back, 1K — 2 upgrades pending, X PQP, book award / use certificate". Show it in the same right-hand slot.
- Dismiss or collapse the sign-in popup into a small header chip such as "Sign in – see your upgrades". Also shrink the cookie banner to a single-line bar so the promo and the modules below the hero aren't clipped.
- Add a cabin selector chip row (Economy / Premium Plus / Business) plus a "Use upgrade certificate" toggle on the main form instead of behind "Advanced search".
- Add a "Deals" or fare-sale strip under the hero for frequent routes, so fare-sale readers see it without opening a dropdown. That includes BUR-based routes.
