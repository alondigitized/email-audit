# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is United's core booking tool front and center — which is exactly what I want as someone who books flights weekly — but the sign-in modal is blocking half the screen and shoving a credit card pitch behind it instead of anything about my MileagePlus status or upgrade inventory. It reads as "generic travel booking site" more than "we know you're a 1K." Right now it's pushing a co-brand card signup, not a fare sale or route deal.

## What's working

- **The booking widget defaults to my home airport** — "Burbank BUR" is prefilled in the From field, which tells me the site remembers me or geolocated me correctly. That's the single most useful thing on this page for a road warrior.
- **"Include Basic economy & restrictive fares" is pre-checked** — small thing, but as someone who books last-minute for client trips, I want to see every fare bucket, not just the ones United wants to upsell.
- **Perks strip behind the modal (9X miles, 10% off award bookings, free checked bag, 2 United Club passes)** — this is the kind of stuff I actually care about, it's just poorly placed.

## What's weak

- **The "Sign in for the best experience" modal is eating the top-right third of the screen** — for someone who's already MileagePlus 1K, this is a nag, not a personalization. It should recognize returning members or at least not block the perks copy behind it.
- **No mention of upgrade status, PlusPoints, or elite recognition anywhere visible** — that's what actually drives my booking decisions, and it's nowhere on this homepage.
- **The hero content is a credit card pitch (60,000 bonus miles Explorer card), not a fare sale or route-specific deal** — I read every fare-sale email for my routes; this homepage isn't showing me anything like that.
- **Destination field is empty** — makes sense generically, but there's no "recent searches" or "your usual routes" nudge for a repeat flyer.

## Recommendations

- Suppress or shrink the sign-in modal for anyone with a MileagePlus cookie/session history — swap it for a "Welcome back, here's your upgrade list" module instead.
- Surface a route-specific fare or schedule change banner (e.g., "BUR-based fares this week") above the card promo — that's what pulls me in, not a card offer.
- Add a visible elite-status or PlusPoints snapshot near the booking widget for logged-in-adjacent users.
- Move the "1st checked bag free" and "United Club passes" perks above the fold without the modal blocking them — right now they're compelling but hidden.
