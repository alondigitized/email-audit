# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I open united.com on my phone and the first thing I see is a sign-in prompt wall-to-wall, stacked on top of a cookie consent banner at the bottom. The actual homepage — flights, deals, upgrade offers, whatever United wants to sell me today — is completely hidden. For a 1K member who lands here multiple times a week, this is a gate before the gate. Feels like United is more interested in capturing my credentials than getting me to my next booking.

## What's working

- **MileagePlus number field label** — at least acknowledges I have a loyalty number. It's the one thread tying this screen to my world.
- **Clean modal rendering** — the sign-in form itself is legible, full-width, large tap targets. No broken layout on mobile.
- **"Need help signing in?" link** — recoverable friction for anyone who blanks on their MP number mid-travel day.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport sign-in modal blocks everything** — I can't see a single flight, deal, upgrade offer, or banner. The homepage might be great behind this wall; I'll never know.
- **Cookie banner competes with the sign-in modal** — two interruption layers simultaneously. The cookie notice partially obscures the second button on the sign-in form. That's a UX collision.
- **Zero upgrade or status content** — as a 1K member, I expect to see PlusPoints balance, upgrade waitlist reminders, or 1K-specific fare windows. None of that surfaces on a forced sign-in prompt.
- **"Sign in for the best experience" is weak copy** — every airline says this. It gives me no reason to comply urgently. Compare to "See your 1K upgrade availability" — that would actually motivate me.
- **No "Skip" or "Continue as guest" above the fold** — the second button below "Continue" is cut off by the cookie banner. I can't even tell if a dismiss path exists.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal by default on mobile** — or at minimum make "Continue as guest / Skip" equally prominent as "Continue." Force-login gates before any content is visible are the fastest way to lose a re-engagement visit.
- **Swap the generic login prompt for a 1K-personalized re-entry card** — if you know me via a cookie or device ID, surface "Welcome back, Marcus — your SFO-ORD upgrade is waitlisted" instead of a blank email field.
- **Resolve the cookie-banner / modal z-index conflict** — the two overlays stacking means the second CTA button is partially obscured. Ship a fix this week: cookie banner should either appear first and clear before the sign-in modal, or the modal should account for the banner height.
- **Above the fold, show one flight deal or PlusPoints milestone** before asking for credentials — give me a reason to sign in, not just a demand to sign in.
