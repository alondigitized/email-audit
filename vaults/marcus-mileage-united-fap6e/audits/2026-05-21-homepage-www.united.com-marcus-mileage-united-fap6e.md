# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened united.com expecting to see a flight search widget, maybe a fare deal for routes I fly, or at minimum my MileagePlus balance — and instead got a full-screen sign-in modal stacked on top of a cookie consent banner. The actual homepage is completely invisible. As someone who calls the 1K desk before booking, I don't need to be treated like a stranger the second I land on the site — but that's exactly what this experience delivers.

## What's working

- **"Email or MileagePlus® number" field label** — at least acknowledges the loyalty program by name, so I know I'm on the right site
- **"Continue" CTA** — large, high-contrast blue button, zero ambiguity about what to tap
- **"Need help signing in?" link** — low-friction recovery option for credential issues, well-placed under the field

## What's weak

- **Full-screen sign-in modal** — blocks 100% of homepage content before I've seen a single reason to care. This is the wrong trade-off for a returning 1K traveler who just wants to search EWR–LAX fares
- **Stacked cookie banner** — a second full-width overlay at the bottom competing with the sign-in modal; now I have two walls to dismiss before seeing anything
- **Zero personalization signal** — for a site that knows everything about me (elite status, home airport, typical routes), showing a blank interstitial is a wasted opportunity; even a "Welcome back, Marcus" above the sign-in prompt would help
- **No above-the-fold value proposition** — the large blank white space above the sign-in form suggests an image or hero failed to render, or the modal loaded before the background content could paint
- **Sign-in as a gate, not a hook** — "Sign in for the best experience" is the weakest possible sell to someone who already has 1K status; it tells me nothing about what "best experience" means

## Recommendations

- **Don't block the homepage behind a sign-in modal for unauthenticated visits** — show the flight search widget and current fare deals first; move the sign-in prompt to a persistent header button or a non-blocking slide-in
- **Render a background hero behind the sign-in form** — even if the modal fires, show me a 787 cabin shot or a destination image behind it so the site feels alive, not blank
- **Replace "Sign in for the best experience" with a status-aware hook** — if you can detect a returning device, say "Your MileagePlus account is waiting — sign in to see upgrade availability on your next trip"
- **Sequence the cookie banner AFTER sign-in dismissal, or collapse it to a footer bar** — two modals simultaneously is a UX failure; prioritize one interruption at a time
