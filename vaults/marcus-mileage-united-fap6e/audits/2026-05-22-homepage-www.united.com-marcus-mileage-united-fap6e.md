# Homepage snapshot · www.united.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- What I'm seeing isn't really a homepage — it's a sign-in modal stacked on top of a cookie consent banner, both firing at the same time, completely burying whatever is underneath. United is basically demanding my credentials and my privacy consent before I've seen a single flight deal, upgrade offer, or hero image. For a 1K who's been flying United since before they merged with Continental, this is an unwelcoming front door.

## What's working

- **MileagePlus® field label** — the form field copy at least acknowledges my program by name; a 1K knows exactly what to type here
- **"Continue" button** — large, blue, unmissable; no ambiguity about what to do next on this modal
- **"Need help signing in?" link** — small but present; useful for the traveler who rotated MileagePlus numbers after a merger-era account consolidation
- **Cookie banner has a clear close X** — I can dismiss it quickly and get on with my life

## What's weak

- **Double-modal ambush** — sign-in sheet AND cookie consent firing simultaneously is a UX disaster; I have to manage two overlays before seeing anything about why I should care
- **Zero homepage content visible** — there is literally no hero, no deal, no flight search widget, no airline content behind these modals; United has replaced its front door with a login form
- **Cookie banner copy** — "to show personalized ads from United or our third-party partners" is not a great first thing to read when I'm a premium customer who expects personalization to serve ME, not advertisers
- **No 1K or elite recognition** — if United knows who I am (and with MileagePlus they can), there's no "Welcome back, 1K member" pre-fill, no upgrade availability teaser, nothing that says "we know you"
- **The second button is cut off** — something below "Continue" is partially visible but illegible; layout crops it mid-text, which is sloppy on a major carrier's mobile homepage
- **No flight search widget** — every second I'm not searching for ORD–EWR is a second United isn't monetizing me

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the sign-in modal on first load or move it to a persistent top-bar CTA** — show me the homepage, upgrade teasers, and sale fares first; let me sign in when I choose to, not as a tollbooth
- **Sequence the overlays** — if you must show both cookie consent and sign-in, show one at a time; stacking them signals broken QA and doubles the friction
- **Surface 1K benefits above the fold for recognized sessions** — even a cookie-based "returning traveler" state should trigger a "Your upgrade list for tomorrow's flight" or "500 miles to your next PQP" nudge instead of a blank modal
- **Show the flight search widget behind or alongside the sign-in prompt** — let me start searching while I decide whether to log in; that's how the app works and it converts better
