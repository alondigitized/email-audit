# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is exactly my brand doing exactly what I'd expect in May — leading with a summery new flavor launch instead of some generic "collagen for life" messaging. The Strawberry Lemonade angle is smart and seasonal, and the tray-of-drinks hero is lifestyle-coded in a way that actually feels like me on a Saturday. But as a subscriber who buys the 20-oz tub on repeat, I'm getting zero love above the fold — no "hey, welcome back" energy, no loyalty hook, no reminder that I'm already in the ecosystem.

## What's working

- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits."** — The wordplay is exactly millennial-wellness-coded. Witty without being cringe. It doesn't explain collagen to me like I'm new here; it just vibes.
- **The product tub in the hero** — That pink Strawberry Lemonade tub centered on a blue tray surrounded by drinks is genuinely pretty. It made me pause. As someone who stirs collagen into everything, this made me think "oh, could also stir this into a mocktail."
- **"New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides"** sub-copy — Calling out the "new" is the right move. It's why I'd tap through even as a subscriber — I want to see if this one is worth trying on top of my usual.
- **"GET 15% OFF!" strip at the bottom** — Even partially visible, this hooks me. I'm a subscriber, I like deals, and 15% off collagen I already buy is meaningful money on a $40+ tub.

## What's weak

- **No subscriber/loyalty recognition** — I buy from Vital Proteins on subscribe-and-save. The homepage treats me exactly like someone who's never been here. No "Welcome back," no loyalty balance, no "Your next order ships in X days." Bulletproof does this better and it always nudges me to stay loyal.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is too generic** — When there's a hero announcing a *specific* new product, the button should say "Shop Strawberry Lemonade" or "Try the New Flavor." "Shop Now" could go anywhere on the site.
- **No social proof above the fold** — Not a single star rating or review count. I know VP is huge but I'm comparing them to Bulletproof constantly. Even one "4.8★ from 12,000 reviews" would tip me toward adding to cart right now.
- **The chat bubble in the lower right is visually noisy** — It overlaps the promo strip content and makes the bottom third feel cluttered. On mobile at this viewport it's competing with the "GET 15% OFF!" CTA.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Try Strawberry Lemonade"** — Makes the CTA match the hero and removes any ambiguity about where I'm landing. One word change, ships tomorrow, measurably higher CTR.
- **Add a subscriber/returning-customer banner above the fold** — Even a thin "You're on subscribe & save — here's what's new" bar would make me feel seen and reduce churn risk. VP knows I'm a subscriber; they should use that data.
- **Drop a star rating + review count directly under the product sub-copy** — "4.9★ · 8,400+ reviews" next to "New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides" would close me way faster. Above the fold, no scroll required.
- **Move or shrink the chat bubble on mobile** — It's eating into the promo strip visibility. Either anchor it lower with a delay trigger or make it a smaller pill so the 15% off offer reads clean.
