# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay so Vital Proteins is going all-in on their Collagen Sparkling Water 12-packs launch — that's basically the entire homepage above the fold. As a subscriber to the 20-oz tub who also picks up the collagen water on sale, I'm genuinely intrigued by the "now available in 12-packs" news, but I'm also a little annoyed that there's zero acknowledgment that I'm already a customer. This feels like an acquisition page, not a retention experience.

## What's working

- **"Better skin starting in 30 days" headline** — this is the exact self-talk I use to justify my collagen spend. It's benefit-led, not ingredient-led, which is the right call for someone scrolling fast.
- **12-pack availability callout** — as someone who buys the sparkling water variant when I catch a sale, knowing I can now get a 12-pack is actually news I care about. Smart hook.
- **Visual pop on the cans** — the grapefruit/strawberry/lime cans against the bright color-blocked background reads as premium but fun, which is exactly the VP brand sweet spot for me.
- **"NEW" badge** — small but effective; signals this isn't the same old browse.
- **Single focused CTA ("Shop It Here")** — no decision fatigue, the page tells me exactly where to go.

## What's weak

- **Zero returning-customer recognition** — I have a subscription with these people and the page treats me like a stranger. No loyalty balance, no "your next delivery ships soon," nothing. Missed entirely.
- **No tub/powder presence** — my primary SKU (the 20-oz collagen peptides tub) is completely invisible above the fold. If I hadn't already known the brand, I wouldn't know VP makes a powder at all.
- **15% off bubble feels like a trap** — that circular badge overlaying the bottom of the page has "give us your email" energy. Feels like I'm being lured rather than rewarded.
- **No trust signals** — zero star ratings, no "4.8 stars from 12k reviews," no free-shipping threshold, nothing that closes the loop on "should I actually buy this?"
- **Hamburger-only navigation** — I can't reach "Collagen Peptides" or "Subscribe & Save" without tapping the menu and digging. As a subscriber, I want my category one tap away.

## Recommendations

- **Add a subscriber/returning-customer banner** — something like "Welcome back, Vera — your next tub ships in 8 days" or "Manage your subscription" in the header would make me feel seen and reduce churn risk. Even just a "Member? Sign in for your pricing" hook would do it.
- **Swap the hero layout to a split-screen** — left side keeps the sparkling water launch (it's great), right side surfaces the 20-oz powder tub with a "Shop your subscription" CTA. Serves acquisition AND retention in the same scroll.
- **Pin the free-shipping threshold** to the top announcement bar instead of "Stay Vital" — "Free shipping on orders $35+" is more motivating than a brand tagline I already believe in.
- **Surface review counts on the featured product** — even just "★★★★★ 2,847 reviews" under the can image would do real conversion work without changing the layout.
