# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-06-09
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-09**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is clearly *my* homepage — they're leading with a brand-new Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides flavor, which is exactly the kind of seasonal product drop that makes me open my wallet. The vibe is fun-but-functional, very millennial wellness, and the summer launch timing feels intentional. What I'm missing is any signal that the brand *knows* I'm already a subscriber — no loyalty hook, no "welcome back" energy, just a cold-open hero.

## What's working

- **New flavor hero** — "New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides" is exactly the kind of product innovation I watch for. If I'm debating Bulletproof vs. Vital Proteins this week, a new flavor tips me back.
- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits."** — This line is *so millennial*. It's not "unleash your potential" gym copy. It's warm, a little tongue-in-cheek, and lands right in my register.
- **The photography** — That blue tray with the pink tub and the strawberry-lemon drinks looks gorgeous. It makes the product feel like a lifestyle object, not a supplement.
- **15% off strip** — It's partially cut off at the bottom but visible. For a 20-oz tub buyer, even a small discount matters when you're buying monthly.
- **"Shop Now" CTA** — Clear, high-contrast, no ambiguity about what the button does.

## What's weak

- **No subscriber/loyalty acknowledgment** — I spend real money here on auto-ship. There's zero signal that this page knows I'm a returning customer. No "Vitals member" badge, no rewards balance, nothing.
- **"Shop Now" is generic** — Compare to "Shop Strawberry Lemonade" or "Try the New Drop" — the current copy doesn't reinforce the urgency of the new product launch.
- **No price anchor or subscribe-and-save callout** — I live on the subscribe model. No "subscribe & save XX%" visible means I'm not reminded why staying auto-ship is better than switching to Bulletproof.
- **"Stay Vital" banner is wasted space** — That top bar just says "Stay Vital." That's a tagline, not an offer. Could be "Free shipping over $50" or "Earn Vital Rewards — join free."
- **No people in the hero** — Just hands on a tray. I want to see someone actually using this in their morning routine — coffee, blender, desk setup. Show me the context I actually live in.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Stay Vital" bar for a subscriber hook**: "Vitals members get 20% off — always. Join free →" or for logged-in users, show their rewards balance.
- **Upgrade the CTA to the specific product**: "Shop Strawberry Lemonade →" reinforces the launch moment and reduces friction for people who came specifically for the new flavor.
- **Add a lifestyle inset shot in the hero**: A hand stirring the tub into iced coffee or a pink drink in a reusable tumbler on a marble countertop — something that maps to how I actually use this product, not just a tray of cocktails.
- **Surface the subscribe-and-save offer visibly**: Even a small badge on the tub image ("Subscribe & save 15%") would immediately differentiate from buying at Whole Foods or Bulletproof.
