# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is basically home turf for me — no fluff, no "wellness journey" nonsense, just the tub I put in my coffee every morning front and center. It reads like a brand that knows exactly what it sells and isn't trying to be everything to everyone, which I respect. Right now it's pushing the flagship Collagen Peptides tub with a "#1 Collagen Brand" claim and a 15% off nudge, nothing seasonal or campaign-y happening.

## What's working

- The hero tub shot is exactly my product — Collagen Peptides Unflavored, the one I actually subscribe to, so I felt an immediate "oh that's mine" moment.
- "#1 Collagen Brand" with the little footnote marker is a smart trust flex without being obnoxious about it.
- "Shop Now" and "Shop All Collagen" give me two clean paths in, no wandering through a mega-menu.
- The 15% off badge is doing its job as a scroll-stopper in the corner without blocking the page.

## What's weak

- That scrolling ticker at the very bottom — "Our Original Collagen Peptides · Our Original Collagen Peptides" — repeats the exact same phrase and looks cut off on the left edge. Feels unfinished, not polished.
- No loyalty or subscription callout anywhere above the fold, which is weird for a brand that clearly wants me on autoship — I'm literally already a subscriber and there's nothing acknowledging that.
- The 15% off badge has zero context — no expiration, no "first order only," nothing. I can't tell if it's worth clicking or just a generic popup.
- Cart shows "0" and there's no "welcome back" or recently-viewed nod, so it doesn't feel like it recognizes me as a returning customer.

## Recommendations

- Fix or remove the bottom ticker — either vary the copy per item or drop it, the repeated/cut-off text reads as a bug.
- Add a subscribe-and-save callout right under the hero CTA — that's literally how I buy, and it's not mentioned anywhere visible.
- Put a deadline on the 15% off badge ("ends Sunday") so it feels like an actual reason to act now instead of ambient wallpaper.
- Swap in a quick trust strip (review count/stars) near the hero — "#1 Collagen Brand" is a claim, star ratings would back it up.
