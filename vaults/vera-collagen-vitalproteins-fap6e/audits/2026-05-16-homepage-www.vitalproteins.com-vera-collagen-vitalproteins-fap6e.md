# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I actually stopped mid-scroll when this loaded — the Strawberry Lemonade flavor is literally the thing I've been waiting for since they dropped the coconut lime. Vital Proteins is clearly pushing a summer-flavored collagen moment right now, leaning into the "mix it in your drink" use case I already live by. The vibe is clean, millennial, aspirational without being intimidating — this brand knows who it's talking to.

## What's working

- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits." tagline** — this is genuinely witty and lands for a millennial who grew up with lemonade stands. I read it and grinned.
- **New Strawberry Lemonade launch as the hero** — for someone who actively tries the drink-format collagen variants, putting the new flavor in the spotlight is exactly right.
- **The tray-with-drinks product shot** — showing the tub alongside actual finished drinks (pretty pink glasses with lemon slices) sells the use case, not just the product.
- **"GET 15% OFF!" bottom strip** — even if it's partially obscured, I caught it and it's relevant — I already subscribe but I'd click to see if it stacks.
- **"Stay Vital" top bar** — implies a loyalty/program exists, even if it's not spelled out.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is too generic** — in a hero this specific (new flavor, named product), the button should say "Try Strawberry Lemonade" or "Shop New Flavor." "Shop Now" loses urgency.
- **No subscriber callout** — I have a recurring 20-oz subscription and there's zero acknowledgment of that. A single "Subscriber? You save 20%" line would change my behavior immediately.
- **No reviews or social proof above the fold** — a new product launch especially needs a star count or even a "try it first" hook to reduce the hesitation around committing to a full tub.
- **The 15% off banner is cut off and scrolling** — I can read "GET 15% OFF!" but I can't read the terms or what it applies to without actively chasing the text. Feels accidental.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I always want to know if I'm close to free shipping before I add anything. This page doesn't say.

## Recommendations

- **Change the CTA to flavor-specific copy** — "Shop Strawberry Lemonade" or "Try the New Flavor" instead of the generic "Shop Now" so the button completes the sentence the hero already started.
- **Add a subscriber/loyalty nudge below the hero copy** — something like "Subscribers save 20% — already a member? Log in" would turn every returning customer into an engaged session instead of a bounced one.
- **Pin the promo details** — move "GET 15% OFF!" out of the scrolling strip and into a static one-liner below the CTA: "Use SUMMER15 · Free shipping over $35." Make me feel like I have everything I need to act right here.
- **Add a social proof micro-element** — even a small "★★★★★ 12,000+ reviews" badge near the product image would reduce the friction of trying a new flavor of something you already spend $50+ a month on.
