# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, confident, very blue brand page, and it knows it's the collagen category leader. The whole first screen is one hero: "#1 Collagen Brand," a tub in hands, and a Shop Now button. It doesn't push a sale, a subscription, or a new flavor. The only offer is a small "GET 15% OFF!" bubble in the bottom-left corner.

## What's working

- **The hero tub photo:** the Collagen Peptides tub is held up in two hands against a soft grey card, and I can read "Unflavored" and "20g Collagen Peptides per Serving." That's exactly the product I already buy.
- **"Shop All Collagen" in the header:** it's a dark pill button beside the nav. I can get to my category in one tap from anywhere.
- **"#1 Collagen Brand":** it's big and simple, and it reassures a buyer who cross-shops Vital Proteins against Bulletproof.
- **Layout:** there's plenty of whitespace, one focal point, and a two-color palette. It loads clean and doesn't feel salesy.

## What's weak

- **The "GET 15% OFF!" bubble:** it's tiny, tucked in the corner, and doesn't say what I have to do to get it. It's also covering the ticker text beneath it.
- **The hero subhead:** "Look, feel and move your best with our premium collagen-based products.**" is generic wellness copy. It says nothing about the 20-oz tub, coffee, or subscribing.
- **No subscribe-and-save or member pricing:** I subscribe to the tub, and there's no mention of Subscribe & Save or rewards anywhere above the fold.
- **The "Stay Vital" top bar:** it's a wasted strip with no shipping threshold, no promo, and no seasonal message.
- **No people:** the image is only hands and a tub. There's no one like me stirring it into coffee.
- **The ticker strip:** "Our Original Collagen Peptides" repeats with no price, rating, or link I can see.

## Recommendations

- Replace the "Stay Vital" top bar with a real offer, like "Free shipping over $XX + 15% off your first Subscribe & Save order."
- Turn the corner bubble into a labeled banner ("15% off your first order — tap to claim"), and move it so it doesn't cover the ticker.
- Swap the hands-only hero for a lifestyle shot of the tub next to a coffee mug. Change the CTA to "Shop Collagen Peptides 20 oz" or "Subscribe & Save."
- Add a small "4.8★ from 50,000+ reviews" line under the "#1 Collagen Brand" headline. Turn the ticker into a best-seller rail with prices and star ratings.
