# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I immediately recognize this — it's my brand, my product, and honestly the hero is giving me summer vibes I didn't know I needed in May. Vital Proteins is clearly pushing a new Strawberry Lemonade flavor launch right now, and the aesthetic is very "wellness girl summer." As a subscriber and longtime 20-oz tub devotee, I appreciate that the product is front and center, but this homepage is 100% acquisition mode — there is nothing here that acknowledges I'm already a repeat customer.

## What's working

- **The hero product shot** — the pink Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides tub surrounded by a lemonade-drink spread is immediately recognizable and visually appetizing. I want to know what this tastes like.
- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits."** — that copy is fun, not trying too hard, and completely my register. It doesn't read like supplement-industry speak.
- **New-flavor framing** — "New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides" under the headline makes the reason to visit feel timely. I don't have this flavor yet, so there's actual novelty here.
- **15% off strip** — even partially visible, a discount hook at the bottom edge is doing work for conversion.
- **Clean mobile layout** — no clutter, no pop-up blocking the hero, the nav is minimal and out of the way.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" is a dead-end CTA** — it could say "Try Strawberry Lemonade" or "Shop New Flavors" and actually direct me. "Shop Now" is a placeholder, not a call to action.
- **Zero subscriber recognition** — I have a subscription with this brand. The homepage has no idea. No "Manage your subscription," no loyalty points balance, nothing that makes me feel like a VIP customer versus a stranger.
- **No free-shipping signal** — I know Vital Proteins has free shipping at a threshold, but it's not on screen. That's a missed conversion nudge for someone considering adding a new SKU.
- **No social proof above the fold** — no star ratings, no review count, nothing that helps me justify trying a new flavor over my trusted original.
- **The 15% off strip is cut off** — I can see "GET 15% OFF!" but not what it's for, what the code is, or how to use it. Feels like a teaser that just frustrates.

## Recommendations

- **Rename the CTA to the specific flavor:** Swap "Shop Now" for "Try Strawberry Lemonade" — this is a new-product launch, not a generic shop page. Give me a reason to tap.
- **Add a subscriber nudge to the hero strip:** Replace the generic "Stay Vital" top banner with something like "Subscriber? Add a new flavor for 20% off" — this takes 10 minutes to implement and makes returning customers feel seen.
- **Drop a micro trust signal under the CTA:** A single line — "⭐ 4.8 stars · 50K+ reviews" — directly below the "Shop Now" button would remove friction for new-flavor hesitation.
- **Make the 15% off strip actionable:** Expand it to show the code or the mechanic ("Sign up for 15% off your first order") so I know what I'm getting before I dismiss it.
