# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-06-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Vital Proteins is doing the summery new-launch thing and I'm here for it — strawberry lemonade collagen is exactly the kind of SKU I impulse-buy when it pops up. The vibe is clean, the brand feels confident, and the hero speaks my language. What's missing is anything that tells me *why* I should buy it *today*, and there's nothing acknowledging I've been a subscriber for years.

## What's working

- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits."** — That headline is genuinely good. Playful, punny, Millennial-brained. It made me smile. That's brand voice doing its job.
- **The product is the hero.** The pink Strawberry Lemonade tub on a blue tray with the pink drinks around it is visually cohesive and immediately signals "this is a new flavor drop, look at it."
- **"New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides" sub-copy** — clear product description right under the headline, no ambiguity about what's being sold.
- **15% off promo strip** — even half-visible at the bottom edge, it registers as a reason to keep scrolling.
- **"Stay Vital" top banner** — tiny but brand-consistent. Doesn't waste the real estate on nothing.

## What's weak

- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic.** In context it works, but "Shop Strawberry Lemonade" or "Try the New Flavor" would convert me faster — it signals the limited/new nature of the drop.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment.** I subscribe to the 20-oz tub. There's zero indication this brand knows I exist. No "subscriber savings," no rewards nudge, no "reorder" hook.
- **The 15% off promo is cut off** — it's partially below the fold with "luc" and "ct drop" text visible in a scrolling ticker. I can't immediately parse whether the 15% off applies to this product or is tied to a code I don't have.
- **No free-shipping callout.** My typical cart is one tub, and I always want to know if I'm getting free shipping before I tap anything.
- **Hands-only imagery** feels product-demo, not lifestyle. For a collagen brand targeting women my age, I'd expect to see *someone* — not just disembodied hands on a tray.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Try Strawberry Lemonade"** — use the launch momentum; the generic CTA undersells the scarcity/newness of this flavor.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line** directly beneath the CTA ("Free shipping on orders over $50") — it's the single lowest-effort conversion lever missing from this fold.
- **Surface a subscriber/loyalty hook in the hero area** — even a small "Subscribe & save 20%" badge on the tub image would acknowledge returning customers like me and close the deal faster.
- **Replace hands-on-tray with a lifestyle shot** — a woman in her mid-30s stirring the collagen into her drink on a sunny patio beats a studio product tray every time for this audience.
