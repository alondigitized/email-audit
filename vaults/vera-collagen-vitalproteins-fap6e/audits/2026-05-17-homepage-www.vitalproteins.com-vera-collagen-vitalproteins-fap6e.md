# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is literally my brand, so I'm not coming in cold — I subscribe to the 20-oz tub already and cross-shop religiously. Right now Vital Proteins is leading with a new summer flavor: Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides. The lifestyle shot is cute and the offer is visible, but for a returning subscriber who just wants to know "what's new and should I try it," this page gets me halfway there and then stalls.

## What's working

- **New Strawberry Lemonade hero** — "Like a lemonade stand with benefits" is genuinely clever. I smiled. The wordplay is millennial without trying too hard, and the sub-copy immediately explains what the product is.
- **The product shot** — tub on a blue tray with pink drinks and ice? Very shareable, very Pinterest-ready. It makes me think "okay, maybe I swap the 20-oz for this flavor next cycle."
- **"GET 15% OFF!" strip** — visible right at thumb-reach at the bottom. Even partially cropped, I see it and I know there's a deal waiting for me.
- **"Shop Now" CTA** — big, dark, impossible to miss. No "Explore," no "Discover" — just shop. Thank you.

## What's weak

- **No subscriber or loyalty acknowledgment** — I'm literally a paying subscriber and the page treats me like a stranger. No "Hey, welcome back" energy, no rewards balance, no "as a subscriber you get..." moment.
- **No visible navigation to other SKUs** — I cross-shop the collagen water variants. All I see is a hamburger menu. If I want to compare the new flavor to my current tub, I'm clicking blind.
- **No free-shipping threshold in sight** — I need to know if my order clears the threshold before I care about adding the new flavor. That number should be somewhere above the fold.
- **No social proof** — not a single review count, star rating, or "10K+ subscribers love this" badge. For a new flavor I haven't tried, I want at least a nudge from other people before I commit.

## Recommendations

- **Add a subscriber callout strip** above the hero: something like "Already subscribed? Try the new flavor — add it to your next order." One line, massive retention signal.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** in the top bar next to the "Stay Vital" copy — "Stay Vital | Free shipping over $35" costs zero space and removes the biggest friction point.
- **Add a "Customers who subscribe to Classic also love..." product rail** below the hero so cross-shoppers like me can actually compare the new flavor to our existing purchase without hunting through the nav.
- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Try Strawberry Lemonade"** — specificity converts. I know what I'm clicking into and the copy does the selling.
