# Homepage snapshot · www.vitalproteins.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, I'm genuinely charmed — this is a brand that knows exactly who it's talking to. Vital Proteins opens with a summer-coded, millennial-witty hero for their new Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides, and the visual is bright, fun, and product-forward without being clinical. Right now it's pushing a new flavor launch, and it's doing it with personality.

## What's working

- **"Like a lemonade stand with benefits." headline** — Millennial-perfect. Witty, benefit-adjacent without being clinical. It made me smile, which is rare for a supplement brand.
- **Blue tray hero shot with hands + drinks** — The social/party staging sells the occasion use case (mix it in your summer drink, not just in your coffee). That's a real behavioral nudge for me — I already mix it in things beyond coffee.
- **"New Strawberry Lemonade Collagen Peptides" sub-copy** — Product name is spelled out clearly, no ambiguity about what "Shop Now" will take me to.
- **Sticky 15% OFF bar** — Even if I was browsing passively, a 15% off nudge at the bottom of the screen is going to register.
- **Clean, rounded-card layout on a blue gradient** — The hero sits inside a card, which gives it a "product drop" feel that I associate with brands I actually trust.

## What's weak

- **No social proof anywhere above the fold** — Not a single star rating, review count, or "X,XXX reviews" badge. For a product I might be buying for the first time (new flavor), I want validation fast.
- **"Shop Now" CTA is generic** — Given the specificity of the sub-copy, the button could say "Shop Strawberry Lemonade" or "Try the New Flavor" and convert way better.
- **No free shipping signal** — I'm a subscriber, so I care less, but a new visitor won't add to cart without knowing the shipping math.
- **No returning-customer hook** — I'm on the site regularly. There's nothing that acknowledges me — no loyalty points balance, no "welcome back," no "your subscription" shortcut. This page treats me like a stranger.
- **Hands only, no person** — The lifestyle shot is fun, but showing an actual person using the product (ideally a woman around my age) would land harder emotionally.

## Recommendations

- **Swap "Shop Now" for "Shop Strawberry Lemonade"** — This CTA is sitting directly below specific product copy; make the button match the sub-copy and watch CTR go up.
- **Add a star-rating chip to the hero sub-copy line** — Something like "★ 4.8 · 12,400 reviews" in small text below the product name builds instant credibility without disrupting the layout.
- **Surface a loyalty/subscriber hook in the sticky bar** — Change "GET 15% OFF!" to "Subscribe & Save 15% — cancel anytime" so it communicates the subscription model I'm already enrolled in, not just a one-time discount.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold line just above the CTA** — "Free shipping on orders over $35" takes up one line and can meaningfully reduce cart abandonment for first-timers.
