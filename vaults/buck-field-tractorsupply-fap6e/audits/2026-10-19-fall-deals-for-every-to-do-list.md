## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup has no confirmed rendering or merge-token defects. The QA run could not verify landing pages, because 9 of the 12 probed tracked links timed out and the unsubscribe link returned 403. The compliance and authentication headers were not captured, so they are unverified rather than failing.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Click-tracking timeouts (8 links):** Every probe of an `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?...` redirect ended in "The read operation timed out". Affected link texts: "Tractor Supply Co.", "2026FW43Hero" (×2), and "Display images to show real-time content" (×5).
- **Likely cause:** All 8 timeouts are on the same ESP redirect host. That points to the probe being throttled or bot-filtered, or to a slow redirect, rather than 8 separately broken destinations. This is not confirmed. A real redirect failure would break the whole email, so re-test from a different client or IP.
- **Unsubscribe link 403:** "Update your preferences or unsubscribe" (`e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?ZdNHfbpswFAZ...`) returned HTTP 403. This is probably bot protection, but the link is unverified and should be checked manually.
- **Coverage gap:** Only 12 of 36 links were probed. The 24 skipped are footer, utility and social links, so their status is unknown.
- **Pass rate:** The QA pass rate is 19%, and almost all of the failures are the timeouts above.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Placeholder alt text:** Five tracked links have the link text "Display images to show real-time content". This is the fallback alt text on live-content images, so screen-reader users and image-blocked clients get no product or offer information.
- **Filename as alt text:** The hero link text is "2026FW43Hero", an internal asset name rather than a description.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** Three pixels have no `alt`. They are `mi.tractorsupply.com/p/cp/.../o.gif`, `eaAnalyticsTSC.everestengagement.com/ea/o8uREqbKQf/?...` and `e.ez.tractorsupply.com/open?...`. Add `alt=""` to each so assistive tech skips them.
- **Viewport meta:** `<meta content="width=device-width" name="viewport">` has no `initial-scale`. The `width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0` value is instead set on `name="x-apple-disable-message-reformatting"`, which looks like a misapplied attribute. The impact is low, because the layout uses `@media` breakpoints at 580px and 639px.
- **Doctype:** The HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype is combined with `xmlns` attributes. This is a common ESP pattern with no confirmed rendering impact.
- **Not checked:** The source was truncated, so I could not check the body markup or whether the preheader is hidden.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens, such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]`. Subscriber identifiers appear only in tracking URLs (`mi_u=60740592613`, `e=60740592613`), which is expected. The truncated body was not checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. Confirm in the raw message at the original receiving mailbox. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers for bulk senders.
- **SPF/DKIM/DMARC:** No Authentication-Results header was captured, so I can't assess any of them. The sender is `email@ez.tractorsupply.com`, so check alignment of the `ez.tractorsupply.com` subdomain against the headers in a direct-delivery copy.
- **Unsubscribe link:** An in-body link is present ("Update your preferences or unsubscribe"). Its 403 response is unverified, as noted in section 2.
- **Postal address:** The footer was cut off in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the CAN-SPAM physical address.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **UTM parameters:** They can't be assessed. All links use opaque redirects (`/click?<token>`), and the probes timed out before resolving to a landing page.
- **Campaign identifier:** The only visible one is in the Everest pixel: `c=101926_TSC_WKY_FW43TopDealsWeekly`.
- **Landing page alignment:** The offers in the email can't be matched to landing pages without the redirect targets.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the 8 timed-out `/click` links and the unsubscribe link with a longer timeout and a browser-like User-Agent. Then record the final destination URLs and their UTM parameters.
2. Replace the "Display images to show real-time content" and "2026FW43Hero" alt text with descriptions of the actual content.
3. Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers from a direct send, not the relay.
5. Capture Authentication-Results from a direct-delivery copy to confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
6. Fix the viewport meta so `initial-scale=1.0` is on `name="viewport"`.
7. Make the QA harness retry on timeout and treat repeated timeouts on one ESP host as a probe problem. Right now they register as eight separate failures and distort the pass rate.
