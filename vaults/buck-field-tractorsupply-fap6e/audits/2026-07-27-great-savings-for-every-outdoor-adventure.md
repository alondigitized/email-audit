## 1. Technical Summary
Email uses standard MSO/Outlook-hardened HTML4.01 markup with responsive media queries, but automated QA shows a 20% pass rate: 8 of 9 checked destination links timed out and the unsubscribe link returned a 403, alongside missing standard compliance/authentication headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 8 of 12 probed material links returned **"read operation timed out"** rather than resolving, spanning the masthead logo ("Tractor Supply Co."), the top banner ("Unbeatable Banner," 2 instances), a Movable Ink real-time content block ("Display images to show real-time content"), and multiple product tiles (Blackstone Griddle, Dry Dog Food, Rooster/Barrel Fan value banners).

- The redirect payloads are opaque (custom Zeta encoding, not standard Base64), so destination URLs and campaign parameters can't be fully verified from source alone.
- **"Update your preferences or unsubscribe"** link returns **403 Forbidden** — see Compliance section.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link broken:** `https://preferences.ez.tractorsupply.com/unsubscribe` returns **403**, a CAN-SPAM functional-compliance failure (opt-out mechanism must work).
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing** — no RFC 2369 mailbox-level unsubscribe found (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than true absence).

- **Authentication-Results header missing** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the relay.
- Physical footer address was not visible in the truncated HTML and could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameter mapping to landing pages: all links are wrapped in Zeta's `e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click` redirector with an opaque, non-standard encoded payload, and the majority of probed links timed out before reaching a final destination to confirm alignment.
- Final destinations that are visible in cleartext resolve to expected domains (`www.tractorsupply.com`, `mi.tractorsupply.com`), consistent with the brand — no domain-mismatch or open-redirect issues observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Investigate the `mi.tractorsupply.com` / Zeta redirect chain — 8/9 non-unsubscribe link failures sharing the same "timed out" signature suggests a server-side or redirect-hop problem, not isolated bad links.
2. Fix the 403 on the unsubscribe endpoint immediately — this is a CAN-SPAM compliance blocker.
3. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to support one-click unsubscribe (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements).
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC are passing at the sending MTA; the audit could not verify authentication status due to missing `Authentication-Results`.
