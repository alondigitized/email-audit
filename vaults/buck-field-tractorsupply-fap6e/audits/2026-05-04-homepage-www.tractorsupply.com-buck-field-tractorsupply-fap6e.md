# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-04**
## Executive summary

- This is my store — Tractor Supply. They know it's planting season and they've got a Yard & Garden Days campaign running, which is exactly what I'm here for in early May. That said, the minute I land on the page I'm getting hit with three separate "Sign in or create an account" nudges, which feels like the site is more interested in my email address than my cart. The right bones are here; the execution's got some clutter.
- **8/10**
- + Hero *copy* speaks to my focus area: "Yard & Garden Days" is on-the-nose for a hobby farmer in spring planting season
- + Returning-shopper hook: "Get rewarded!" sits right in the header next to Sign In, and there's a whole loyalty card below it
- + Concrete offer above fold: "savings up to 25% off" and "Save up to $20 Garden Hoses" both visible
- − Primary CTA: "Shop Now" exists in the top bar but the three sign-in CTAs dwarf it — nothing "unmistakable" about a tiny hyperlink buried in a promo bar when two red buttons are screaming at me
- − Visual hierarchy: the eye doesn't know where to go — banner, loyalty card, hero carousel, and a sign-in modal are all fighting at once
- + No render bugs: clean render, no broken images
- − Demographic signals: the hero image shows a young woman with a garden hose, not a 54-year-old guy in a feed cap
- + Current season: May + Yard & Garden Days is dead-on timing
- + Loyalty visible without scrolling: "Get rewarded!" and "member rewards" both above the fold
- + Offer feels honest: "up to" qualifiers are there, no obvious bait-and-switch

## What's working

- **"Yard & Garden Days" hero copy** — spring campaign is exactly right for what I'm shopping for this time of year. Timing is solid.
- **"Get rewarded!" header hook** — I'm a Neighbors Club member, so that reminder to sign in actually has value. It's where my pricing lives.
- **Store locator bar** — showing the nearest store with hours ("Open till 9 pm") is useful. I do a lot of in-store runs for heavy feed; knowing the store's open is a practical signal.
- **Dual-format offer** — percentage off *and* a dollar amount on a specific product (garden hoses) gives me two hooks, one of which is concrete and actionable.

## What's weak

- **Three sign-in CTAs competing simultaneously** — the loyalty card, the header, and the bottom modal are all pushing sign-in at the same time. That's not a loyalty program; that's a wall. I just want to see what's on sale.
- **Bottom sign-in modal** — pops up over the content I'm trying to see. As a Neighbors Club member I already know I should sign in. This doesn't remind me; it annoys me.
- **Young woman in the hero** — holding a garden hose is fine, but this reads like suburban backyard, not hobby farm. A guy checking a fence line, filling a feeder, or loading feed bags would land closer to my situation.
- **No product categories visible** — I can see a hero and a promo strip, but I can't see a single tappable category tile (fencing, poultry, tools, seeds). One-tap navigation to my category isn't there without hitting the hamburger menu.
- **No free-shipping or delivery threshold** — I shop online specifically for tools and heavy items. Not seeing a "free shipping over $X" figure means I don't know if this trip is worth it yet.

## Recommendations

- **Collapse the sign-in wall to one touchpoint** — keep the header "Sign In / Get rewarded!" and kill the loyalty card + modal. One prompt is a reminder; three is harassment.
- **Swap the hero image** — put a middle-aged guy in Carhartt stacking garden supplies or working near a coop. The flannel-and-hose image skews 35-year-old suburban mom; this audience is older and rougher.
- **Add a free-shipping or delivery threshold badge** — a simple "$X ships free" strip under the hero costs nothing and would stop me from bouncing to the app to check.
- **Surface one row of category chips below the hero** — Poultry, Fencing, Tools, Seeds/Garden. Four tappable tiles get me to the right place in one tap instead of forcing me through the hamburger menu.
