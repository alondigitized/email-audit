# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**

Tractor Supply clearly knows me well enough to know I've got an account (Neighbors Club), so the hard sell to log in before I can see anything feels like the door greeter blocking the aisle. The Yard & Garden Days deal is right in season for me — planting time on the hobby farm — but the hero image shows a woman in a garden center apron, not a livestock-and-fencing kind of guy.
- **7/10**
- + returning-shopper hook visible: "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" in the header AND a dedicated member rewards card below it
- + concrete offer above fold: "Get up to 25% Off during Yard & Garden Days"

## What's working

- **Yard & Garden Days 25% off banner** — it's planting season. I came here thinking about my raised beds and that offer landed on the right week.
- **Location strip — "Santa Clarita CA Open till 7 pm"** — I do heavy hauls in-store, so knowing whether the store is open before I make the drive is genuinely useful.
- **Neighbors Club hook in the header** — "Sign In / Get rewarded!" is right at eye level. I'm already a member; quick reminder that there's money on the table.
- **Search bar with barcode scanner** — I regularly look up part numbers for fencing hardware and feeders. The barcode icon tells me I can scan a bag label in the store to reorder. That's useful.

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in ask is aggressive** — there's a "Sign in or create an account" card in the page body AND a modal overlay doing the same thing. Two sign-in prompts before I've seen a single product is a grind.
- **Hero image doesn't represent me** — the carousel shows a woman in a red retail apron surrounded by potted annuals. That's a nursery, not a hobby farm. No livestock, no Carhartt, no chicken-coop energy.
- **No livestock or farm supply signal above the fold** — my main reason to open Tractor Supply's app is feed, fencing, animal health. None of that is visible.
- **No free-shipping threshold or pickup-in-store callout** — I want to know if I can order the heavy stuff online and pick it up, or whether I have to drive in. That info isn't here.
- **"Get up to 25% Off" carousel partially obscured** — the 15% badge on the right edge is cut off, which makes the promo feel incomplete.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the garden-center hero for a hobby-farm lifestyle shot** — show a person loading a truck bed with feed bags, working a chicken coop, or running fencing. That's the TSC customer, and it's a five-second swap on the carousel.
- **Collapse the two sign-in prompts to one** — pick the header CTA or the modal, not both. Better yet, swap the modal for a thin loyalty-balance strip ("Hi Buck — you have 450 Neighbor's Club points") that acknowledges returning members instead of demanding they log in again.
- **Add a "Top Picks for the Farm" or "Livestock & Poultry" category strip below the hero** — a single row of tappable icons (Feed, Animal Health, Fencing, Coops) would let me navigate in one tap without touching the hamburger menu.
- **Surface the free-ship or buy-online/pick-up-in-store threshold above the fold** — even a thin strip ("Free in-store pickup | Free ship on $49+") removes a friction point for someone deciding whether to order or drive over.
