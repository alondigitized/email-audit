# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-03
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-03**
## Executive summary

- I know TSC well — I'm in there every other week for chicken feed and hardware. Opened this on my phone and the first thing I get hit with is a garden-center ad featuring a young lady smelling flowers, then a pop-up nagging me to sign in before I've even seen what's on sale. It's a spring push around Yard & Garden Days, which makes sense for the season, but the execution feels like it's aimed at the homeowner buying petunias, not the guy loading fifty-pound feed bags into a pickup.
- **7/10**
- + Returning-shopper hook visible: "Sign In / Enroll – Get rewarded!" in the nav, plus a dedicated card reading "Deals, member rewards & so much more" — my Neighbors Club connection is acknowledged immediately
- + Concrete offer above the fold: "Get up to 25% Off during Yard & Garden Days" — specific enough to register
- + Clear primary CTA: "Shop Now" button on the hero, visible and tappable

- − Hero imagery doesn't match my persona: young woman with ornamental flowers, not a 54-year-old guy checking on chicken wire and feed prices

## What's working

- **Neighbors Club sign-in hook** — "Get rewarded!" right in the nav and a full card below it. As a member, I notice this immediately and it confirms there's a reason to log in.
- **Store-level info** — "Santa Clarita CA Open till 9 pm" and the zip/delivery indicator tells me my local store is live and relevant. That's genuinely useful context.
- **25% off call-out** — the number is big and readable on the hero. Even if the category isn't my main priority, 25% off anything at TSC gets my attention.
- **Search bar with barcode scanner** — I can scan a product I already own to find a replacement. That's a practical feature a medium-tech guy like me actually uses.
- **Seasonal timing** — it's May, I'm running fence line and prepping the garden. "Yard & Garden Days" lands in the right week.

## What's weak

- **Hero imagery is wrong for my demo** — a young woman holding a potted flower at a nursery is not me. TSC has a whole livestock-and-land customer base they're leaving invisible here.
- **Double sign-in nag** — the gray card mid-page AND the sticky modal at the bottom both say "Sign in or create an account." That's two interruptions before I've seen a single product. Annoying on desktop, irritating on a phone.
- **No feed, livestock, or farm supply visible** — the whole first screen is garden center. I can't see a chicken category, a livestock feed promo, nothing that tells me my stuff is here too.
- **"Yard & Garden Days" is vague** — no end date visible. "Up to 25% off" with no deadline and no specifics on what's included feels like a soft sell.
- **Modal pop-up** — that bottom "Sign in for a better experience" overlay is a dark pattern. I'm already deciding whether to engage and the site is already pressuring me. Makes me want to tap the X and reconsider.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero imagery for a lifestyle shot that spans the full TSC customer range** — a shot of someone tending a backyard flock or loading fencing supplies would pull in guys like me without losing the garden crowd; rotate the carousel faster.
- **Kill one of the two sign-in CTAs** — keep the nav "Get rewarded!" call, drop the mid-page card, and absolutely kill the sticky modal. One clear ask is persuasive; three is harassment.
- **Add a Neighbors Club member deal strip** — a horizontal banner just below the nav reading something like "Members: extra 10% on feed this week" would stop me from scrolling past and go straight to my wallet.
- **Show the offer end date on the hero** — "Ends Sunday, May 5" or similar. Without a deadline, 25% off feels like a shelf sticker that's been there for months.
