# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows their audience — "Yard & Garden Days" in May hits right when I'm planning fence repairs and the garden's going in. But whoever designed this mobile page wants me to create an account more than they want to sell me anything. I get hit with three separate sign-in pushes before I can even see what's on the shelf.

## What's working

- **"Yard & Garden Days" hero with 25% off** — May is when I'm buying hoses, stakes, and fertilizer. This is the right message at the right time.
- **Store info above the fold** — Santa Clarita CA, open till 9 pm, ZIP shown. I shop in-store for heavy stuff. Knowing the store is open when I get off work matters.
- **"Get rewarded!" enrollment nudge in the nav** — I'm a Neighbors Club member. Seeing that language confirms this site knows me.
- **"Save up to $20 Garden Hoses" callout** — Specific product, specific savings. That's the kind of thing that makes me tap.

## What's weak

- **The modal overlay** — There is a full-width sign-in modal sitting on top of the hero carousel with an X to dismiss. On a phone, that's the first thing I have to deal with. I came here to look at hoses, not log in.
- **Three sign-in prompts competing simultaneously** — Nav bar enrollment CTA, mid-page "Deals, member rewards & so much more" panel, and the modal. Pick one.
- **No category navigation visible** — Hamburger menu is there but nothing's expanded. I can't see Livestock, Fencing, or Poultry with one tap. I have to dig.
- **"Savings up to" language** — "Up to 25% off" and "up to $20" both hedge. What's the actual deal on the hose I'd buy?

## Recommendations

- **Kill two of the three sign-in prompts** — Show the Neighbors Club benefit once, cleanly, in the nav. The mid-page panel and the modal overlay are friction that will send me straight to Google.
- **Add a category strip below the hero** — Four icon-and-label tiles: Livestock & Poultry, Fencing & Gates, Garden & Lawn, Tools. Let me get to Poultry Feeders in one tap instead of hunting in the hamburger menu.
- **Swap "up to $20" for a specific lead product** — Show me the 5/8" 50-ft hose, price, and final member price. That converts a browser into a buyer.
- **Put a "My Store" inventory chip on the hero** — Something like "In stock at Santa Clarita" next to the Garden Hose callout. That's what makes me drive in versus order elsewhere.
