# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply is my store, and they know it — the whole page signals farm country, equipment financing, and a loyalty program that I'm already a member of. Right now they're leading with Memorial Day savings and a 0% APR financing deal on big-ticket equipment. It's familiar and functional, though a sign-in pop-up blocking the bottom of the screen before I've even browsed a single product is an annoying way to greet a regular.

## What's working

- The Memorial Day banner at the very top is immediate — I know there's a sale before I read a single product name
- "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" in the nav bar is a smooth loyalty hook; as a Neighbors Club member I clock that in half a second
- My local store is surfaced right below search: Santa Clarita CA, open till 7 pm — useful intel before I make the drive
- The 0% APR financing banner with the orange zero-turn mower speaks to real farm-equipment money; 48 months on $1,599+ is a deal I'd actually read the terms on
- Search bar front and center with both voice and barcode scan — practical for someone who often searches by bag code or part number in the aisle

## What's weak

- The sign-in modal ("Sign in for a better experience — Unlock deals, rewards & more.") fires before I've seen a single product — it feels like they want my email more than they want to help me shop
- No livestock, feed, or fencing imagery anywhere in this viewport — I buy 50-pound bags of layer pellets most weeks and nothing on this screen speaks to that
- The hamburger nav is collapsed; there are no one-tap shortcuts to Livestock, Feed, Fencing, or any category I actually browse
- "Deals, member rewards & so much more" is a vague tease — show me a dollar figure or a category, not a promise

## Recommendations

- Surface a horizontal category strip above the fold: Live Chicks, Feed & Nutrition, Fencing, Tools — one tap to my aisle instead of digging through a hamburger menu
- Sharpen the Memorial Day hero with a specific threshold: "Save $20 on any $100 livestock order this weekend" hits harder than a seasonal banner with no price signal
- Kill the sign-in interstitial modal or collapse it to a slim banner — an overlay nag before I've browsed a single product is the fastest way to lose a regular
- If I'm a recognized returning member, show my Neighbors Club balance in the header: "You have 320 points — $10 from a reward" would stop me mid-scroll every time
