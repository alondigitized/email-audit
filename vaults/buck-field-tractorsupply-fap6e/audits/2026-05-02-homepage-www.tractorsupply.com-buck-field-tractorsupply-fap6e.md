# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows who they're talking to — the store locator is right there, the Neighbors Club push is front and center, and the Yard & Garden Days sale is seasonally on point for early May. That said, the first thing I actually see is two separate sign-in prompts stacked on top of each other before I can even get to product, and I'm here to buy fencing hardware, not create an account. It feels like it's working harder to capture my email than to help me find what I came for.
- **7/10**

## What's working

- **Yard & Garden Days hero (up to 25% off):** Early May is prime planting time — this is exactly the season I'm out buying starts, soil, and coop bedding. Timing is right.
- **Store locator strip (Santa Clarita CA, Open till 9 pm):** I know my local store hours change by season. Having this up top removes a friction point for an in-store trip.
- **"Get rewarded!" Neighbors Club nudge:** I'm already a member. Seeing this in the header reminds me there's money on the table if I sign in.
- **Microphone + barcode scanner in search bar:** Barcode scanner is genuinely useful — I can scan a bag of feed in the barn and look up a replacement without typing anything out.

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in stack:** There's a "Sign in or create an account" card mid-page AND a modal popup asking me to sign in at the same time. That's aggressive. I almost hit the X reflex before I even see the sale.
- **Hero is Yard & Garden lifestyle (woman with potted flowers):** Pretty, but that's not me. I'm not potting petunias — I'm looking for bulk feed or wire fencing. The lifestyle image skews too suburban/hobby-gardener.
- **No category nav visible above the fold:** On desktop I'd go straight to Livestock or Farm & Ranch. On this mobile view I have to search or scroll — there's no quick-path shortcut to the aisles I actually shop.
- **"15%" badge cut off on the right edge of the hero:** Whatever that 15% is for, I can't read it. Truncated offer = ignored offer.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss or delay the sign-in modal** — show it after 30 seconds or after a scroll, not as the first thing I see on top of an existing sign-in card. Pick one or the other.
- **Swap the hero lifestyle shot for a working-farm scene** — a real coop, a hay field, a guy loading a truck bed. The potted-flowers image works for a garden center; TSC's edge is livestock and working land.
- **Add a quick-tap category strip below the search bar** — four icons: Livestock Feed, Fencing, Poultry, Tools. Mobile shoppers with muddy hands don't type.
- **Expand the "15%" badge** so the full offer is legible — or remove the overlap so both promos breathe.
