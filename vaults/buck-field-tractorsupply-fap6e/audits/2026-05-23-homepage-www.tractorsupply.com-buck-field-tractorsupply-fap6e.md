# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- TSC is my store — I know it the way I know my own barn. Right now they're pushing a Memorial Day sale hard, and the Blackstone griddle is front and center. The timing is right, the loyalty hook is there, but I barely see anything farm-related above the fold, and they're hitting me with a sign-in prompt twice before I've even looked at a product.

## What's working

- **The Memorial Day banner strip** — red bar at the very top, "Memorial Day Savings are here | Shop Now >" — grabs my eye immediately and tells me there's a deal happening right now.
- **Loyalty/Neighbors Club hook** — "Deals, member rewards & so much more" mid-page with a clear red button. I'm already a member, so this actually means something to me — I know I get pricing others don't.
- **Store locator strip** — "Santa Clarita CA | Open at 8 am" tells me my local store is open and ready. That matters when I'm planning a run before I head out to the property.
- **The $100 off Blackstone** — "Now $299.99 Save $100" is a real dollar figure, not a vague percentage. I can do that math in my head standing in the coop.
- **Calendar accuracy** — "May 11–25" Memorial Day Sale date range is current and I can see it expiring soon. That creates urgency without feeling fake.

## What's weak

- **Griddle as the hero product** — I came to TSC for farm supplies, not a Blackstone. The griddle is fine but it's not fencing, it's not layer feed, it's not a poultry drinker. It misses my shopping basket entirely above the fold.
- **Double sign-in push** — "Deals, member rewards & so much more" with a sign-in button, AND then a modal at the bottom of the screen that says "Sign in for a better experience." Two full-width CTAs about creating an account before I've browsed a single product feels like a gate, not a welcome.
- **No farm/livestock/poultry imagery anywhere above the fold** — TSC's core equity for someone like me is that it stocks what a farm box store should. You'd never know that from this screen.
- **No visible category navigation** — I see a hamburger menu icon but categories aren't surfaced. I shouldn't have to tap twice to get to Poultry & Livestock.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — for online tool orders, I always check this. It's not on screen at all.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the solo griddle hero for a lifestyle panel** — a guy in work clothes hauling feed bags or tending a backyard coop, with the Memorial Day sale badge overlaid. Keep the $100-off griddle as a secondary tile. The brand's identity is rural life, not backyard BBQ.
- **Kill one of the two sign-in CTAs** — keep the Neighbors Club section mid-page, but dismiss the bottom modal by default. The friction of two account-creation asks in one viewport will push casual browsers straight out the door.
- **Surface two or three hero category chips above the fold** — "Chicken & Poultry | Livestock | Fencing & Gates" — one tap and I'm in my section. The hamburger alone is making me work too hard.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the store locator strip** — something like "Free ship on orders $49+" right next to the store hours would answer my second most common question before I even start shopping.
