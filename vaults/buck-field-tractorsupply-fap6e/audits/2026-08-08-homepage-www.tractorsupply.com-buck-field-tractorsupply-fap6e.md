- The whole promo carousel — top gun safes, lithium golf cart, UTVs, camo kayak — is aimed at hunters and rec-vehicle guys, not really coop-and-garden folks like me
- Nothing above the fold about feed, chicken supplies, or fall planting even though it's August — feels aimed at a different Tractor Supply customer today
