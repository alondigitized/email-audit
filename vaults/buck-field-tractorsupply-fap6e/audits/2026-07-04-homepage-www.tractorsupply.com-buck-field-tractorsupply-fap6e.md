# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-07-04
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-04**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows who I am — "Out Here" is practically printed on my barn door — and the 4th of July sale timing is dead-on for a mid-summer farm run. But my phone pulled up a big blank gray box where the hero should've been, and then threw a sign-in modal on top of everything while I'm still trying to see what's on sale. The brand is right, the execution needs work.

## What's working

- **"Celebrate America and Save / Shop Now >"** top banner — I see a sale immediately, no hunting required. Red-white-blue, July 4th, that's my week.
- **"Celebrating America Out Here" promo banner** — "Out Here" is TSC's brand promise and it lands every time for someone who actually lives out here. Not aspirational — it's just true.
- **"Up to 50% Off / June 22–July 5"** with the starburst badge — specific discount, specific dates. No games.
- **"Get rewarded!"** next to Sign In — I'm a Neighbors Club member so that's a direct pull to log in and see my points.
- **Local store shown (91803 zip)** — confirms my store is already set, I don't have to re-enter anything.

## What's weak

- **Blank gray hero rectangle** — the biggest visual block on the page is an image that didn't load. That's the first thing I see after the nav, and it's nothing. On my phone's connection, that's a hard stop.
- **Sign-in modal blocking the page** — "Sign in for a better experience" popped up before I even had a chance to look around. I know I get rewards for signing in, but asking me before I've seen a single product feels pushy.
- **No visible product categories or feed/fencing shortcuts** — the whole nav is behind a hamburger. I can't see "Livestock & Pet," "Tools," or any quick lane to what I'm actually buying. One tap to my category? Not available.
- **No free-shipping threshold mentioned** — I'm buying tools online specifically to avoid hauling things. If there's a free-ship minimum I'd hit on a $75 order, I want to see it up front.
- **No reviews or product imagery** — the promo banner is graphics, not products. I'd want to see what's actually 50% off before I commit to browsing.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero image load on mobile** — a blank gray block above a sale banner destroys the deal energy before it builds. Lazy-load a compressed fallback or fix the asset so it renders in under 2 seconds on an LTE connection.
- **Delay the sign-in modal by one scroll or one tap** — let me see the deal first, then ask me to log in. Neighbors Club members aren't going to skip the prompt; we just don't want it before we've seen anything.
- **Surface 2–3 category chips below the search bar** — "Livestock," "Hand Tools," "Poultry" as tappable pills would get me to my aisle in one tap instead of digging through the hamburger menu.
- **Add free-shipping threshold to the top strip or promo banner** — something like "Free shipping on orders $50+" next to the Shop Now CTA gives me an immediate reason to fill the cart online instead of waiting for my next store trip.
