# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-06-27
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-27**
## Executive summary

- Well, I know Tractor Supply — been a Neighbors Club member for years — but the first thing I see on this page is a wall asking me to sign in. Twice. The actual sale that's supposedly going on right now is half-buried behind these overlays. Feels like they want my login more than my business.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale top banner** — the red strip at the very top ("Flash Sale, In-Store and Online now thro…") catches the eye immediately; I know something's on sale before I even process anything else
- **Store locator context** — "Santa Clarita CA Open at 8 am" + zip code tells me my local store is active; useful when I'm deciding whether to drive in for heavy feed
- **"Get rewarded!"** callout next to the Sign In link — small but it reminds me I've got points sitting there; that's a real nudge for a Neighbors Club member
- **"Friday – Sunday Only"** date badge on the Flash Sale — time-bounded, credible framing that creates a real reason to act this weekend

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in overlay** — there's a "Deals, member rewards & so much more / Sign in or create an account" card AND a second bottom sheet "Sign in for a better experience" with its own button, stacked on top of each other; this is aggressive and it blocks the actual merchandise
- **Zero product or category visibility** — I cannot see a single product, category tile, or image behind these overlays; a farmer who came to browse chicken feed or electric fence chargers has no visual hook
- **Flash Sale details are hidden** — "While quantities last" is visible but I have no idea what's actually on sale; clicking through just to find out is friction I don't love
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — no visible category rail, no quick-tap to Livestock or Tools; on mobile I'd have to open a menu before I see anything useful

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the bottom sign-in sheet by default or after 3 seconds** — the top header already has "Sign In / Get rewarded!"; a second full-width modal on the same screen is redundant and users like me instinctively look for the X
- **Show at least one Flash Sale product tile above the fold** — even a single "Purina Flock Raiser 50 lb — $2 off" chip would give me a reason to scroll instead of bounce
- **Add a persistent category strip below the search bar** (Livestock, Farm & Ranch, Tools, Clothing) — one-tap access to my departments without opening the hamburger
- **Move the store hours/location line closer to the search bar** — for in-store decision-making (which is half my TSC habit), that context is gold and right now it's almost invisible below the nav
