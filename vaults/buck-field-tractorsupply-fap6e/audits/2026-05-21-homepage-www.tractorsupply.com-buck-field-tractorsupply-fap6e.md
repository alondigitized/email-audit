# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows their audience — the second I landed here I saw "Flash Sale, In-Store Only" which is pretty much the only way I shop for anything heavy anyway. That said, there's a sign-in wall shoved right in my face before I can see what's actually on sale, which is enough to make me tap the X and look past it. Feels like a brand that's got the right bones but is fumbling the welcome.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale urgency block** — bold yellow with "THURS. & FRI. ONLY MAY 21 & 22" is exactly how I'd expect a farm-supply store to communicate a deal. No nonsense, big type, clear window.
- **Geolocation is live** — it's already showing my local store (Santa Clarita CA) and hours (Open at 8 am). Saves me a step before I drive over.
- **Member rewards hook in the header** — "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" in red text is visible before I even look at anything else. I'm a Neighbors Club member; seeing that immediately feels right.
- **"In-Store Only" call-out** — for a guy who's already hauling feed bags on Saturdays, this actually reinforces the trip rather than competing with it.
- **Search bar is front and center** — big, clean, easy to hit with a thumb. No hunting.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocking the hero** — I landed on the homepage and the first thing I see is "Sign in for a better experience." I'm not creating an account before I even know what's on sale. That's a gas-station cashier asking for your loyalty card before you've touched the pump.
- **No specific discount percentage** — "Flash Sale" with no number is like a flyer that says "SALE" and nothing else. Is it 10% off? 40%? I'm not driving in for 10%.
- **Zero farm/livestock content above the fold** — I sell eggs, I run a coop, I fix fences. There's a lightning bolt and a yellow box. Nothing here says "you're in the right place, Buck."
- **Loyalty banner CTA says "Sign in or create an account"** — that's the same ask twice in three inches of screen. Feels desperate, not welcoming.
- **Zip code field shows "91754"** — that's not my zip. The store location says Santa Clarita but the zip shown is wrong. Minor, but I'd double-check my local inventory results.

## Recommendations

- **Show the actual discount** — put a number on the Flash Sale banner ("Up to 40% off selected items, in-store only"). One number, above the fold, in the same yellow block. I'll decide in three seconds if it's worth the drive.
- **Close the modal by default on return visits** — if I'm signed in (Neighbors Club member), skip the sign-in nag entirely. If I'm not, show it as a dismissible corner card, not a full-screen gate.
- **Swap a product category strip in above the Flash Sale block** — four icons: Poultry & Livestock, Dog & Cat, Farm & Ranch, Fencing. One tap to my stuff. Right now there's no shortcut to my aisle.
- **Fix or surface the zip code discrepancy** — if geolocation and the zip field are out of sync, fix that before a guy like me trusts the "in stock at your store" info.
