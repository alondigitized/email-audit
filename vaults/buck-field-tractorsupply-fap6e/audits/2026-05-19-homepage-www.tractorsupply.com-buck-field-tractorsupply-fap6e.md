# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- TSC is pushing a Memorial Day sale hard — the Blackstone griddle deal is real money off, and the timing is right since I'm in the thick of spring work. This feels like my store; the Neighbors Club hook is up top right where it should be. What bugs me is they're throwing two separate sign-in screens at me before I can even browse — I just want to see what's on sale for the farm.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day timing**: I'm in spring mode — planting, fencing, chickens. A Memorial Day sale banner landing May 19 is exactly right, and I'd expect deals on stuff I actually need alongside the griddle.
- **Neighbors Club / "Get rewarded!" hook in the header**: That's my program. Seeing it immediately up top confirms this page knows I'm a returning customer, not a first-timer.
- **"Now $299. Save $100" on the Blackstone**: A specific dollar-off number. No "up to X%" hedging. If I was thinking about a griddle, this closes the loop fast.
- **Location detection — "Santa Clarita CA, Open at 8 am"**: Shows me my store is open and it knows where I am. That's actually useful when I'm planning whether to run in before feeding the chickens.
- **Search bar with mic + barcode scan**: Medium tech comfort, but I know how to search. This is front and center, which is where it should be.

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in push**: The "Deals, member rewards & so much more" module and the bottom modal overlay are both asking me to log in. Pick one. Two asks back-to-back makes me feel like I'm being herded, not welcomed.
- **Hero is BBQ, not farm**: I came here for feed or fencing — nothing above the fold tells me TSC has what I actually drove to the store for last week. The griddle deal is fine but it's not why I opened tractorsupply.com on my phone.
- **Navigation is hidden in a hamburger**: To get to Farm & Ranch or Chicken Supplies I have to tap the three-line menu. No visible category shortcuts means extra taps to get to my aisle.
- **No member-exclusive pricing shown**: The Neighbors Club is mentioned but I can't see any "member price" badge on the griddle or any product that differentiates what I get vs. a first-time visitor.
- **The ** on $299**: Fine print asterisks are visible on the price but I can't read what they refer to. Minor, but it makes me pause before tapping.

## Recommendations

- **Swap one hero slot for a farm/seasonal deal**: It's late May — show me a bag of Dumor feed, a T-post pounder, or a waterer on sale. One hero tile for BBQ, one for farm. I'm here for both.
- **Surface a Neighbors Club member-exclusive price badge**: Put a small "Member Price" tag on featured products so I can see in one glance what my loyalty actually gets me. Right now the Neighbors Club hook is just a sign-in nag.
- **Kill the bottom modal, keep the header hook**: One sign-in touchpoint is fine. The module in the middle already does the job — the modal overlay at the bottom is just friction.
- **Add a horizontal category quicklinks strip below search**: "Farm & Ranch · Chicken Supplies · Tools · Fencing" — four tappable chips under the search bar so I can one-tap to my category without opening the hamburger menu.
