# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-07-11
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Right off the bat this feels like home — I see chicken feed and scratch grains front and center, which is basically half my weekend right there. Tractor Supply knows who walks through their doors, and the Neighbors Club push is hard to miss. That said, they hit me with a sign-in modal before I can even see the whole page, which is the kind of thing that makes me put the phone in my pocket.

## What's working

- **Mini Chicks and Scratch Grains imagery in the hero**: I keep those in the barn year-round. Seeing the actual product bags I buy hits different than a generic lifestyle photo.
- **Neighbors Club card above the fold**: One clear block, clear benefit language ("Deals, member rewards & so much more"), one button. Simple and I get it.
- **Store locator strip**: "Santa Clarita CA Open at 8 am" with a truck icon for delivery tells me exactly what I need if I'm deciding whether to drive in or order ahead — useful.
- **Search bar with barcode scanner**: I buy the same SKUs repeatedly. That barcode icon means I can scan an empty bag in the coop and reorder on the spot.
- **Seasonal relevance**: "Summer Steals" landing during summer planting and feeding season is the right read of the calendar.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal at the bottom**: I just opened the page. I haven't seen a single product yet and there's already a second "Sign in or create an account" prompt layered over the promo. That's two sign-in asks before I can browse — it's pushy.
- **"UNBEATABLE PRICES‡"**: The dagger footnote undercuts the headline. If I have to scroll to the footer to find out what's excluded, I already don't trust it.
- **No specific dollar-off or percent deal visible**: "Summer Steals" is a marketing category, not a number. What's the actual deal? Show me $15 off a bag of feed and I'll act.
- **No navigation categories visible**: The hamburger menu is collapsed. To get to "Poultry" or "Livestock Feed" I have to tap the menu and dig. For me on a weekend morning, one extra tap is one too many.
- **No free-shipping threshold callout**: I order tools and accessories online. I'd drive in for 50-lb feed bags, but for wire fencing or waterers I want to know if I hit free shipping. Nothing visible.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the bottom sign-in modal on first visit**: I'm not signed in yet, which means I'm a potential new Neighbors Club member. Interrupting me before I see one product is the fastest way to lose me. Move that nudge to after I browse one category.
- **Replace "UNBEATABLE PRICES‡" with a real number**: "Up to 25% off feed & bedding this week" is something I'd act on. A claim with a footnote is something I scroll past.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip under the header**: One line — "Free shipping on orders $50+" — would push me to pad an online order rather than making a separate trip.
- **Expose 2-3 category shortcuts below the search bar**: Poultry, Livestock, Fencing. Three tappable tiles so I don't have to open the hamburger menu to get where I'm going.
