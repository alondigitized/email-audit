# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Alright, so Tractor Supply knows who they're selling to — yard and garden in May, that's right where I'm at. But the second I land on this page, they're hitting me with sign-in walls back to back: one in the header, one below, and then a modal pops up right over the hero. I'm a Neighbors Club member, I just want to see what's on sale, not fight through three "create an account" prompts to get there.

## What's working

- **"Yard & Garden Days" campaign** — Running this banner in May is smart. That's when I'm running fence lines and planting. The timing is right and the 25% off number is the kind of deal that makes me check my list.
- **"Save up to $20 — Garden Hoses"** — Concrete, specific, actionable. I actually need a new hose for the coop run. This got my attention.
- **Store location + hours visible** — Santa Clarita, open till 7 pm. That's the kind of quick info that helps me decide whether to add a trip to my afternoon route without digging around.
- **Search bar with barcode scan** — Big, prominent search with a scan option. I'm medium-tech and that's exactly the right depth — useful, not fussy.

## What's weak

- **Triple sign-in wall** — Header CTA, a full module with a red button, then a modal that covers the hero. I'm already a Neighbors Club member. This experience treats me like a stranger who needs to be converted, not a loyal customer who came to shop.
- **Modal covers the hero** — The "Sign in for a better experience" overlay blocks the carousel content. I literally cannot see what the other 2 slides are until I dismiss it or scroll.
- **Hero image doesn't look like me** — I see a woman in a nice garden. That's not wrong for the brand, but it's not a 54-year-old guy with a chicken coop and a section of broken fence. A guy in a Carhartt jacket checking on a raised bed or a fencepost would land differently.
- **No category shortcut rail visible** — I can't see links to Chicken & Poultry, Fencing, or Feed in this viewport. I have to tap the hamburger menu, which adds friction for my usual run.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the sign-in modal** — Show it after the first scroll or after 30 seconds, not on landing. Blocking the hero on arrival is the fastest way to lose me.
- **Add a category shortcut strip below the hero** — Four or five icons: Chicken & Poultry, Livestock Feed, Fencing & Gates, Power Tools, Lawn & Garden. One tap to my aisle. My in-store trips follow a pattern; give me that same muscle memory online.
- **Swap the hero talent** — Put a middle-aged farmer or a guy working around a coop in that hero shot. The woman gardening is fine for general audiences, but TSC's core customer is someone who looks more like me.
- **Personalize the loyalty hook for Neighbors Club members** — Instead of "Sign in or create an account," if I'm already cookied in, show me my points balance and my next reward threshold. That's an instant reason to stay on the page.
