# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-06-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-06**
## Executive summary

- This is my store — I'm in here every week during planting season and I recognize the layout before I even read a word. TSC is running a flash sale this weekend and they want me to log into my Neighbors Club account bad enough to ask me twice before I can see what's on the shelves. That suet deal down the page is the kind of thing I came for, but I had to wade through two sign-in banners and a modal to get to it.

## What's working

- **Neighbors Club hook in the header**: "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" is the first thing I see after the logo. As a member, I know there's a reason to tap that.
- **"5 for $5 Royal Wing Suet"**: I stock suet for my yard and my coop. That's a genuine price drop from $9.99 and it's sitting right on screen. That's my kind of deal.
- **"FRIDAY – SUNDAY ONLY" urgency strip**: Yellow background, all-caps, "WHILE QUANTITIES LAST, NO RAINCHECKS OR SUBSTITUTIONS" — I know TSC means that. They do run out.
- **Location detection**: "Santa Clarita CA — Open at 8 am" tells me the store is nearby and I know the hours before I even think about driving over. That's useful.
- **Flash sale acknowledgment at top**: Something's discounted in-store AND online this weekend. That alone keeps me from closing the tab.

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in nag plus a modal**: There's a gray member banner pushing "Sign in or create an account," and then a bottom modal saying the exact same thing, plus the header "Get rewarded!" That's three separate prompts before I can look at a single product category. I'm already a member.
- **Flash sale copy is cut off**: "Flash Sale, In-Store and Online now thro..." ends mid-sentence. I don't know if it's tools, clothing, fencing, or something I don't care about. That truncation kills the hook.
- **No category navigation visible**: I can't see a single quick-link to Livestock, Fencing, Chicken Supplies, or Power Tools. I have to either search or dig into the hamburger menu.
- **Trust signals are nowhere**: No star ratings, no free-shipping threshold, no returns mention. If I'm about to order a fence charger online, I want to know it ships free before I commit.

## Recommendations

- **Un-truncate the flash sale headline**: Either shorten the copy to "Flash Sale: Ends Sunday" or give the banner enough height to show the full category. I need to know what's on sale to care about tapping "Shop Now."
- **Consolidate the sign-in asks to one**: Kill the bottom modal and pick either the header or the gray banner — not both. For logged-in Neighbors Club members, skip the nag entirely and show their rewards balance instead.
- **Add 4–5 category quick-links below the search bar**: Simple chips — Livestock & Pet, Fencing, Garden, Tools, Workwear — so I can tap to my aisle in one shot without hunting through the hamburger menu.
- **Drop a shipping threshold line under the search bar**: Even "Free shipping on orders $49+" as a small text line would push me to add the suet to a cart instead of tossing it in my truck on the next in-store run.
