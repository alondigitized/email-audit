# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- TSC knows who I am — this is my store, and a Flash Sale landing on May 22nd during peak planting season is not a coincidence. What I'm looking at is a pretty aggressive push to get me logged in before I've even seen what's on sale, and the actual Flash Sale details are too thin above the fold. They want my credentials before they'll show me the goods.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale timing** — Middle of May, planting season in full swing, and TSC drops an in-store flash sale on the last day. I'm already thinking about my drive over.
- **Store info is front and center** — "Santa Clarita CA, Open at 8 am" right there in the viewport. I'm buying 50-lb feed bags — knowing the store is open matters more to me than free shipping.
- **"In-Store Only" is stated plainly** — No fine-print gotcha. I respect that.
- **Barcode scanner in the search bar** — I can scan an empty feed bag and reorder without typing anything. Genuinely useful for someone like me.
- **"No Rain Checks or Substitutions"** — That restriction is printed on the hero itself, not buried in a footnote. Honest.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocks the hero before I've seen a product** — I've been a Neighbors Club member for years, and the first thing this page does is stick a popup in my face asking me to log in. I walked into TSC and someone blocked the aisle.
- **Flash Sale has zero specifics** — Is fencing on sale? Layer pellets? Water troughs? The mystery doesn't excite me — it makes me wonder if it's all lawn furniture I don't need.
- **No farm or livestock imagery anywhere in the hero** — Big yellow text is fine but show me something. A bag of Dumor layer feed or a T-post would tell me this sale is mine, not just for the suburban garden crowd.
- **Three separate sign-in pushes in one viewport** — "Get rewarded!" in the header, a dedicated sign-in banner, and then a modal. That's three asks before I've seen a single product.

## Recommendations

- **Show at least one farm/ranch product in the Flash Sale hero** — A fence stretcher, a bag of poultry feed, or a chicken waterer next to the "Flash Sale" badge would tell me this sale belongs to me, not the mulch-and-annuals shopper.
- **Kill the sign-in modal or replace it with a returning-member greeting** — One persistent banner is enough. Stacking a modal on top of two existing sign-in prompts is friction, not a benefit pitch.
- **Add a savings range to the hero** — "Up to 40% off farm & ranch" or "Feed deals inside" in the hero copy would get my thumb on "Shop Now" without hesitation.
- **Surface Neighbors Club pricing vs. regular pricing** — That's the whole reason I joined. A "Member Price: $X / Regular: $Y" comparison on a featured Flash Sale item in the hero would hit harder than any sign-in banner.
