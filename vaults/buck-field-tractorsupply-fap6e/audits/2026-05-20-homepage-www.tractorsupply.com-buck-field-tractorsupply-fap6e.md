# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is my store — I've got a Neighbors Club card and I'm in the Santa Clarita location every week or two during planting season. Right now the site is leading hard with a Memorial Day sale on a Blackstone flat-top griddle, which is fine equipment, but I came here thinking fencing staples or maybe a new poultry feeder, not backyard cookware. The loyalty angle is everywhere though, and they know my zip, so it doesn't feel like I wandered into the wrong aisle — just the wrong end cap.

## What's working

- **Neighbors Club / rewards hook** — "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" is right in the header and I see a second push mid-page. As a card-carrying member, that's my first instinct anyway.
- **Concrete Memorial Day price** — "$299, Save $100" is specific and visible. I don't have to hover or click to know what the deal is.
- **Time-bounded badge** — "May 11–25" on the circular Memorial Day graphic. I know this expires in five days and that's a real motivator.
- **Store locator/hours** — "Santa Clarita CA Open at 8 am" right below the search bar. That's my store. Useful.
- **Search bar with voice + barcode scan** — I'm medium on tech but scanning a product barcode to reorder feed is something I'd actually use.

## What's weak

- **Griddle lead on a farm-supply homepage** — The hero product is a $299 outdoor flat-top grill. I'm here for fence posts and chicken feed, not cookware. Misses my visit intent completely.
- **Sign-in modal popup** — A second "Sign in for a better experience" card slides up over the lower half of the screen. I have to hit the X before I can even see the rest of the hero. Friction on first load.
- **Two sign-in prompts stacked** — The gray "Deals, member rewards & so much more" card AND the popup are essentially the same ask. One would do the job.
- **No farm/livestock/feed signal above fold** — Not a single chicken, fence line, animal feed bag, or tool in sight. You'd never know this is Tractor Supply from the first screen.
- **Free-shipping threshold invisible** — I order tools online. Not knowing if my $45 order ships free means I might not add to cart.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the hero for a farm/spring-planting Memorial Day lifestyle shot** — Show a hobby farm scene: someone fencing a property or filling a feeder. Keep the $100 off framing but apply it to tools, poultry supplies, or feed accessories — something in my actual cart.
- **Collapse the double sign-in prompt into one** — Kill the mid-page gray card or the popup; not both. One clear member-benefits module with "Sign In to See Your Deals" is enough and removes the wall of friction.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip** — A single line — "Free shipping on orders $49+" — in the top banner next to Memorial Day savings. Costs nothing, could tip an add-to-cart decision.
- **Surface a Neighbors Club balance or recent purchase** — If I'm detected as a returning visitor (cookie, location match), replace the generic sign-in card with "Welcome back — you have X points" or "Your last order: Chick Starter 50lb." That's what makes me feel like a regular, not a new visitor.
