# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- This is my store — Tractor Supply has been where I go for feed, fencing, and anything the coop needs for years. First glance, they know it's May: "Yard & Garden Days" with 25% off is right on the money for planting season. But before I can even take in the deal, there's a sign-in popup covering half the screen — like having someone block the store entrance asking for your loyalty card before you've touched anything. Good instincts on the timing, frustrating execution.

## What's working

- **"Yard & Garden Days" campaign timing**: May is exactly when I'm out back planting, stringing new fence wire, and resupplying the coop. This campaign lands.
- **Local store info front and center**: "Santa Clarita CA — Open till 9 pm." As someone who makes mid-project runs to the store, having hours right there is genuinely useful.
- **Two Neighbors Club loyalty callouts above the fold**: "Deals, member rewards & so much more" plus "Get rewarded!" in the header — they know I care about my points.
- **25% off in the top announcement bar**: Specific, readable, first thing I see. Not buried.
- **Barcode scanner in the search bar**: I can grab a bag of feed from the barn, scan the code on my phone, and order the next bag right there. That's actually smart for someone like me with medium tech comfort — it removes a step.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal blocking the hero on load**: I opened the site, not a checkout page. Hitting me with a popup before I see what's on sale is the digital equivalent of a pushy greeter. I'll dismiss it, but a new customer might just back out.
- **Hero imagery is a woman gardening flowers**: I'm a male hobby farmer shopping for feed, fencing, and poultry supplies. Garden hoses aren't my aisle. The imagery doesn't speak to me.
- **No quick-tap category row visible**: "Livestock," "Poultry," "Fencing," "Tools" — none of those are one-tap from what I can see. I have to hit the hamburger menu and dig. That's extra work I don't want on my phone.
- **No free-shipping threshold anywhere**: When I order tools online, knowing what I need to spend for free shipping affects what I add to my cart. No mention of it here.
- **No urgency on the offer**: "Yard & Garden Days" — when does it end? No date, no countdown. Makes it feel less like a real event and more like evergreen copy.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the sign-in interstitial**: Move it to a slide-in after 30+ seconds or trigger it at cart add — not on homepage load. Members will sign in when they're ready; non-members will bounce if blocked immediately.
- **Add a one-tap category strip below the hero**: A single icon row — Feed, Poultry, Livestock, Fencing, Tools — gets me to my department in one tap instead of the hamburger menu. That's worth real engagement lift for the core TSC shopper.
- **Swap the garden-hose hero for a hobby-farm shot**: A middle-aged guy hauling a bag of feed past a chicken run, or patching a fence — that's the TSC customer on this screen. Convert the imagery to match the actual buyer.
- **Pin a free-shipping threshold in the top bar or below the hero**: "Free shipping on orders $X+" is a conversion driver. Show it early so I'm thinking about cart value before I even start browsing.
