# Animal Days Starts NOW🐾 Save Big on TSC Exclusives!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the best-organized Tractor Supply send in the six-email history I've tracked — after a month of "Wild Steals" reruns and heat-pun subject lines, "Animal Days" finally gives the brand a real seasonal hook with a dated window (Aug 10–23) and a distinct visual identity (the paw-print/horse-icon diamond badge). The top of the email does real work: hero banner states the event and dates, then immediately backs it with three specific priced products, followed by a BOGO 50% toys banner and a $299.99 chicken coop deal — that's four concrete offers before any scrolling fatigue sets in.
- Where it loses points is the same place every TSC email loses points: it doesn't stop. After the strong opening, it keeps stacking modules — gift card offer, cat food, "Explore Even MORE Deals," a 4-product grid with star ratings, a $50 rewards banner, an in-store event banner, a double-points banner, a Halloween decor teaser, and a "Beat the heat for less" banner that reads like a leftover from a previous send. By the bottom third it's lost all thematic discipline.
- Net: a genuinely improved hero concept dragged down by TSC's chronic inability to edit. Worth an above-average score, but the brand needs to trust "Animal Days" enough to build the whole email around it instead of appending eight more offers underneath.

## What's working

- The hero banner leads with a real headline: dated event name, "BIG Savings on TSC Exclusives," and three specific SKUs with prices ($24.99, $3.50, $9.99) — no scrolling needed to find the offer.
- Product cards for Science Diet and Paws & Claws include size (22 lb / 36 lb), a real price, and their own dedicated "Shop Now" button — this is specific enough to act on.
- Star ratings with review counts appear on the 4-item "Quality You Can Trust" grid, which is genuine social proof this brand doesn't always include.
- The BOGO 50% toys banner and $299.99 chicken coop banner are visually distinct enough to not blur into each other.

## What's weak

- The email runs 10+ distinct promotional modules after the hero — rewards, in-store event, double points, Halloween decor, "beat the heat" — with no visual signal for which one matters most.
- "Beat the heat for less" at the very bottom directly contradicts the "Animal Days starts now" framing up top; it reads like copy left over from a prior campaign and undercuts the fresh hook.
- The $50-rewards and double-points offers both carry dense fine print/asterisks (credit approval terms, exclusion dates) that isn't legible at a glance — the kind of buried condition the rubric flags as a soft honesty miss.
- "Shop Now" is repeated on nearly every module as the CTA copy — it's clear, but generic enough that nothing tells the eye which "Shop Now" is the priority click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the "Explore Even MORE Deals" product grid — everything from the rewards banner down (in-store event, double points, Halloween decor, "beat the heat") is a different campaign each and dilutes the Animal Days hook that this send finally earned. Move those to a follow-up send.
- 2. Replace the "Shop Now" repetition on the two hero product cards with offer-specific CTAs — "Get $20 Gift Card" and "Shop Cat Food Deal" — so the eye has a reason to pick one over the other.
- 3. Drop "Beat the heat for less" entirely; it's thematically dead in an "Animal Days" email and reads like stale inventory.
- 4. Move the star-rated 4-product grid ("Quality You Can Trust") higher, right after the hero — it's the strongest trust signal in the email and it's currently buried past two other banners.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Animal Days Is Here: $50 in Rewards + TSC Exclusives`
- **Subject Alt B:** `🐾 Animal Days Starts Now — Save on Dog, Cat & Farm Faves`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Science Diet, Paws & Claws, and BOGO toys — all week through 8/23.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: earn $50 in rewards on any $50+ purchase, now through 9/7.`

# Technical Email Audit — Tractor Supply "Animal Days" (081026_TSC_WKY_FW33TopDealsAnimalDays)

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a standard ESP click-tracking wrapper (`e.ez.tractorsupply.com`) with heavy redirect obfuscation; automated probing timed out on 9 of 12 tested links and returned a 403 on the unsubscribe link, indicating the tracking redirector is either broken or actively blocking automated requests. Compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) were not observed in the captured payload.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 9 of 12 probed CTA/content links failed with **"The read operation timed out"** via `e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?...`, including primary CTAs: "Tractor Supply Co.", "UnbeatableBanner" (×2), "HeroFW33" (×2), "Value1FW33", "Value2FW33", "Science Diet Dry Dog Food", "Paws & Claws Dry Cat Food".
- The unsubscribe/preference-center link returned **HTTP 403**: `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?ZdNHRbps...` (link text: "Update your preferences or unsubscribe"). This is the most severe finding — a blocked unsubscribe path is both a UX and compliance risk.
- All tracked links route through the same obfuscated `e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?` redirector; failure is systemic across link destinations rather than isolated to one asset, suggesting an ESP/CDN-side issue rather than individual bad URLs.
- Open-tracking pixel present and reachable pattern: `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/open?ZPM3BSgM...` (not flagged as broken, only missing alt text — see §3).
- 36 additional (mostly footer/utility/social) links were deprioritized and not probed; unsubscribe was included per probe policy and is the one that failed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility

  - `o.gif` (Mediaplex/Everest analytics pixel) — `https://mi.tractorsupply.com/p/cp/76ddd2c9608d17c7/o.gif?mi_u=60740592613`

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found** — may be a capture artifact of the AgentMail relay rather than a true absence; cannot confirm compliance either way from this data alone.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found** (RFC 8058) — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown from this capture.
- **Unsubscribe link itself returns 403** (see §2) — this is the one confirmed, evidence-backed compliance risk: if the link is actually broken for end users (not just the prober), it's a CAN-SPAM functional-unsubscribe violation. Recommend manual verification in a real client before escalating.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot verify UTM parameters or landing-page alignment — all click-tracked URLs are opaque encoded redirects (`/click?Z...`) that only resolve to final destination URLs after traversal, and traversal is timing out per the QA data. No final-destination URLs are available in evidence to assess.
- Campaign ID is visible in the tracking pixel query string (`c=081026_TSC_WKY_FW33TopDealsAnimalDays`), confirming campaign-level tagging exists at the pixel level, but this doesn't confirm UTM presence on click-through destinations.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority — verify the unsubscribe link manually in a real browser/client.** A 403 on this specific link is the highest-risk finding; if reproducible outside the automated prober, it's a functional CAN-SPAM issue, not just a QA artifact.
2. Investigate the systemic timeout pattern across `e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?` — 9/12 failures on the same redirector host suggests either a rate-limit/bot-block on the ESP side or an actual redirector outage; re-run probes from a different network/UA to rule out false positives before treating all 9 as broken links.
3. Confirm whether `List-Unsubscribe` / `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers exist in the raw MIME source (outside the AgentMail relay capture) — the current WARNs are inconclusive, not confirmed absences.
4. Add `alt=""` to the three tracking pixels to suppress screen-reader announcement (low priority, cosmetic accessibility fix).
5. Once click-tracking links resolve successfully, spot-check 2–3 landing pages for UTM parameter presence and destination-page alignment with the email's advertised offers.
