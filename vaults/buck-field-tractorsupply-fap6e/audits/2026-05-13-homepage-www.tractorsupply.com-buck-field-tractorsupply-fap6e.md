# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows who I am — this is my store, full stop. The moment I land, they're pushing the Neighbors Club and a Memorial Day sale with real dollars attached. What they're selling right now is grills and griddles, which isn't why I'm here, and before I can even look around, they throw a sign-in modal in my face. Feels like the greeter at the co-op blocking the door asking for your loyalty card before you can get to the feed aisle.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day Sale banner (top red strip):** "Memorial Day Savings are here — Shop Now >" catches the eye immediately; the seasonal timing is spot-on
- **"Save up to $120 Grills & Griddles" hero callout:** A real number with a real product category. I know what I'm getting into
- **Dual loyalty/member hooks:** Both the "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" nav element and the "Deals, member rewards & so much more" module hit me as a Neighbors Club member — they know I already have a card
- **Store locator with hours:** "Santa Clarita CA — Open till 9 pm" right there. As someone who does in-store runs for heavy stuff, knowing the store's open is genuinely useful
- **Hero imagery demographic:** The two men in that outdoor setting look like neighbors I'd wave to at the feed store — right age, right vibe

## What's weak

- **Grills & Griddles as the hero:** I'm here during planting season looking for tools and animal supplies. A Memorial Day grill sale is fine for a backyard crowd, but I've got 40 laying hens and a fence line that needs work — this opening offer doesn't move me
- **Triple sign-in assault:** Nav sign-in, the big "Deals, member rewards" module with a button, AND a modal — that's three times in one screen. I already have a Neighbors Club account; this feels like they don't know I exist until I log in
- **Modal blocking the hero:** That "Sign in for a better experience" overlay covers the Memorial Day sale image. I can barely read what's on sale because a pop-up is sitting on top of it. Frustrating on a phone screen
- **No farm/animal/garden category entry points visible:** Above the fold is all sign-in and grill sales. I can't see a single tap into Chicken Care, Farm & Ranch, or Tools without closing the modal and hunting through the nav

## Recommendations

- **Move the sign-in modal to a banner or slim strip, not a full overlay.** A one-line "Sign in to see your member price" bar above the hero converts Neighbors Club members without walling off the whole experience
- **Swap Grills & Griddles for a dual-hero during planting season.** Show me the Memorial Day grill deal AND a "Stock Up on Summer Animal Care Essentials" panel side by side — same sale window, broader capture
- **Add one category shortcut rail above the fold.** Four tappable icons — Feed & Nutrition, Chicken & Poultry, Lawn & Garden, Tools & Hardware — give me a direct path in one tap without digging into nav
- **Surface the Neighbors Club name explicitly.** The page says "member rewards" but never says "Neighbors Club." I'd respond faster to brand language I recognize from my card and my weekly in-store trips
