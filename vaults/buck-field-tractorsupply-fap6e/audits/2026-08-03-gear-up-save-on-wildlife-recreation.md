# Gear Up & Save on Wildlife & Recreation
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-03**
## Executive summary

- This is Tractor Supply's third "hunting/outdoor" push in as many sends, but it's the first one with a real hero concept behind it: "Wild Game Days" (July 27–Aug 9) gives the top third of the email an actual reason to exist, instead of just being another "Unbeatable Prices" wrapper. The Winchester gun safe at $599.99, though, is the same gun safe that anchored the July 31 and Aug 1 "Wild Steals" sends — three appearances of the same SKU in four days is starting to read as inventory clearance dressed up as a themed campaign.
- Past the hero, the email collapses back into TSC's usual habit: a utility trailer and dog toys sit next to diesel exhaust fluid, a pet bed, and dog food under a "Quality You Can Trust" banner that has nothing to do with wildlife or recreation. It's the same unsegmented mass-blast pattern flagged in the July 27 and July 24 audits, just with a nicer top banner this time.
- Net: strong top-of-funnel hook, weak follow-through. A recipient who opens for "Wild Game Days" gets rewarded for about 900 pixels, then the email reverts to generic TSC catalog filler.

## What's working

- The "Wild Game Days" hero banner (forest green, deer/antler icon, dated July 27–Aug 9) gives this send a distinct identity the recent heat-pun and generic "Wild Steals" sends lacked.
- Pricing is concrete and struck-through where relevant: Winchester gun safe $599.99 (was $799.99), UTVs "starting at $8,499.99, Save $500," kayak $599.99 (was $699.99).
- Star ratings with visible review counts appear on the lower product grid (e.g., 3,848 reviews on the dog food), which is real social proof.
- The TSC Credit Card block clearly states the mechanic ($20 off at checkout + $20 in rewards on a $40+ in-store purchase) rather than vague "member perks" language.
- Multiple distinct, well-placed "Shop Now" buttons keep the CTA visually unambiguous throughout.

## What's weak

- The Winchester 36 Gun Safe at the same $599.99 price point is now the third consecutive send to feature it (July 31, Aug 1, and this one) — a subscriber who opened all three sees the identical product and price again.
- The "Quality You Can Trust, Prices That Won't Be Beat" section mid-email dumps diesel exhaust fluid, a pet bed, and dog food into a "Wild Game Days" email with zero thematic connection.
- "View in browser" sits above the fold before any brand content loads, adding friction before the hero is even visible.
- Subject line ("Gear Up & Save on Wildlife & Recreation") names the category but no offer, no dollar figure, and no urgency — it undersells the specific $200-off trailer and 50%-off dog toy deals sitting right in the hero.
- Two competing orange banners stack back-to-back (Wild Game Days hero, then the deer feeder/tees banner), diluting which offer is actually the "hero."

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the strongest concrete offer instead of the category name — specificity drives opens more than a category label does, and this send buries "Save $200" and "50% Off" that are sitting right in the creative.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Wild Game Days: Gun Safes $200 Off, Kayaks $100 Off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Save Up to $500 for Wild Game Days (Ends Aug 9)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Gun safes, kayaks, UTVs, and gear — deals end August 9.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New gear for the hunt, the haul, and everything after.`
- 2. Cut the mid-email diesel exhaust fluid / pet bed / dog food block out of this send entirely, or move it to a separate "everyday essentials" email — it breaks the wildlife/recreation theme the hero just established.
- 3. Retire the Winchester gun safe creative for at least one send cycle; reusing the identical product/price three sends running reads as low inventory turnover, not a themed campaign.
- 4. Move "View in browser" below the primary CTA or into the footer so the hero banner is the first thing rendered.
- 5. Consolidate the two stacked hero banners (Wild Game Days + feeder/tees) into one hierarchy — pick a single top offer and let the rest cascade below it.

# Technical Audit: Tractor Supply "Gear Up & Save on Wildlife & Recreation"

## 1. Technical Summary
This email uses a standard Zeta/Everest ESP redirect-tracking architecture (`e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?...`) wrapping all CTAs; automated QA returned an 18% pass rate with 10 of 12 probed material links failing (timeouts) and the unsubscribe link returning a 403.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 9 of 12 probed links failed with "read operation timed out" errors, including primary CTAs: "Tractor Supply Co.", "Loyalty", "MI_Loyalty", "UnbeatableBanner" (×2), "Display images to show real-time content" (×2), "Value1 - Winchester 36 Gun Safe", "Value2 - Field & Stream". This is a near-total tracking-redirect failure rate on the click domain (`e.ez.tractorsupply.com`), not an isolated broken link.
- The unsubscribe link returned a hard **403 Forbidden**: `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?...` decoding to `preferences.ez.tractorsupply.com/unsubscribe` — see Compliance (§5).
- All links route through a base64-segmented redirect wrapper (`/click?...`) before resolving to final destinations (`tractorsupply.com`, `mi.tractorsupply.com`, `preferences.ez.tractorsupply.com`); the multi-hop chain is consistent with the timeout pattern, suggesting an issue with the click-tracking redirector itself rather than the destination pages.
- One open-tracking pixel is present: `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/open?...` plus a secondary analytics pixel at `eaAnalyticsTSC.everestengagement.com/ea/o8uREqbKQf/`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- Three images are missing `alt` text:
  - `https://mi.tractorsupply.com/p/cp/76ddd2c9608d17c7/o.gif?mi_u=60740592613`
  - `https://eaAnalyticsTSC.everestengagement.com/ea/o8uREqbKQf/?e=60740592613&c=080326_TSC_WKY_FW32TopDealsWeekly`
  - `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/open?...` (open-tracking pixel, `sdt9ed40bd0`)
  - These are all tracking pixels rather than content images, so the accessibility impact is low, but missing `alt=""` (rather than omitted `alt`) is still best practice for screen-reader table parsing.
- Markup is table-based HTML 4.01 Transitional with standard Outlook/Gmail/iOS reset CSS (`.ExternalClass`, `mso-table-lspace`, `x-apple-data-detectors`) and responsive `@media (max-width: 639px)` rules — no rendering defects found in the visible source.
- `<meta name="ROBOTS" content="NOINDEX, NOFOLLOW">` and `<meta name="referrer" content="no-referrer">` are present as expected for transactional/marketing email hosted content.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization tokens are visible in the provided HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken (403)** — this is a CAN-SPAM functional-compliance failure: `https://e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click?...` → decodes to `preferences.ez.tractorsupply.com/unsubscribe`.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe is unavailable or not being captured by the relay.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender rules) cannot be confirmed as supported.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via the AgentMail relay.
- Combined with the unsubscribe 403, this represents the most material compliance risk in this send: a subscriber attempting to unsubscribe via the visible link would hit a hard error.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Primary CTA carries standard UTM parameters: `utm_source=zeta&utm_medium=email` plus campaign identifiers (`mi_ign=080326_TSC_WKY_FW32TopDealsWeekly`, `OFFERID`, `mailing_name`, `offerlocation`), consistent with expected campaign attribution.
- Destination domains split between `www.tractorsupply.com` (homepage CTA) and `mi.tractorsupply.com/p/rp/...` (product/offer deep links) — this dual-domain pattern is typical of TSC's marketing-intelligence redirect layer and not itself an error, but could not be verified end-to-end since all links timed out before reaching final landing pages.
- No `utm_campaign` parameter was observed on the decoded URLs; only `utm_source`/`utm_medium` are present. Not necessarily an error, but worth confirming against TSC's attribution standard if campaign-level reporting is expected.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Priority:** Fix the unsubscribe endpoint (`preferences.ez.tractorsupply.com/unsubscribe`) returning 403 — this is a compliance-blocking defect, not cosmetic.
2. Investigate the click-tracking redirector (`e.ez.tractorsupply.com/click`) for the systemic timeout pattern affecting 9/12 probed links — likely an infrastructure/capacity issue on the ESP side rather than per-link misconfiguration, given the uniform failure across unrelated destinations.
3. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) headers to support one-click unsubscribe and reduce spam-complaint risk under Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender requirements.
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment — no `Authentication-Results` header was observable via the current relay; verify directly with TSC's ESP if this isn't a relay-capture artifact.
5. Add empty `alt=""` attributes to the three unlabeled tracking-pixel images for cleaner screen-reader/table semantics (low priority, cosmetic-accessibility only).
