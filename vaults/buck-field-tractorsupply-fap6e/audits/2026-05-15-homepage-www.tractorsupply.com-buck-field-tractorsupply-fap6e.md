# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- TSC knows exactly who I am — this feels like home base the second it loads. Right now they're pushing Memorial Day savings and a zero-turn mower financing deal, which is the right call for mid-May when half the people like me are deciding whether to upgrade equipment before summer heat hits. Practical, no-nonsense, farm-store energy — which is exactly the brand.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day banner**: seasonal and on-point — this is a big outdoor-shopping weekend, leading with it is the right call
- **Zero-turn mower financing**: 48 months at 0% is real money and real incentive for someone who's been eyeing an equipment upgrade before summer
- **Neighbors Club hook in the header**: "Sign In / Enroll | Get rewarded!" is where it belongs — I see my club before anything else
- **Store location + hours**: "Santa Clarita CA | Open till 9 pm" above the fold is a useful local touch — I shop both channels, and knowing I can run in tonight matters
- **Hard deadline on financing**: "thru 5/25" gives me a 10-day clock, which is enough urgency without feeling manufactured

## What's weak

- **Sign-in interstitial modal**: I came to browse, not to authenticate. Blocking the page with "Sign in for a better experience" before I can see a single category or product is friction I didn't ask for
- **No visible product categories**: with the modal up, there isn't a single department tile — no Poultry Supplies, no Fencing, no Power Equipment. One tap to my aisle should be front and center
- **Three competing sign-in CTAs on one screen**: header link + mid-page section + bottom modal. Pick one. This feels like a funnel obsession, not a customer experience
- **No free-shipping info visible**: if I'm ordering parts or tools online, I want the threshold up front. Nothing here

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the sign-in interstitial**: show me category tiles first — Poultry, Fencing, Power Equipment — and earn the login instead of demanding it on arrival
- **Personalize the Neighbors Club header**: if I'm a returning member, show my points balance or a member-only deal in the header instead of the generic "Get rewarded!" line
- **Add a free-shipping threshold strip**: a simple "Free shipping on orders $49+" tucked under the top banner keeps online shoppers in the funnel
- **Swap mower banner subtext to lifestyle copy**: "Ready before the heat hits" or similar would feel more like Buck's world and less like a big-box appliance flyer
