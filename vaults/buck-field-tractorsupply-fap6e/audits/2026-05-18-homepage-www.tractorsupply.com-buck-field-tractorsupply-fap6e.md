# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Tractor Supply knows who they're selling to — or at least they usually do. Right now the homepage is running hard on Memorial Day and pushing a Blackstone griddle as the flagship product, which is fine for a cookout crowd but doesn't say much to a guy who came here to find fencing wire or chick starter. The sign-in push is relentless — I counted three separate calls to create an account before I can even see what's in the sale.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day sale badge on the hero** — "May 11–25" is right there, I know the deal is live today, no guessing
- **Local store info above the fold** — "Santa Clarita CA Open at 8 am" with the zip code tells me exactly where my store is and that it's already open; that's useful before I decide to drive out
- **Header loyalty hook** — "Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" with red emphasis on "Get rewarded!" reminds me the Neighbors Club has real value without being preachy about it
- **Price callout on the Blackstone** — "$299, Save $100" with the "Exclusive Blackstone Patriotic Griddle" label is a clean, readable price story even on a phone screen
- **Search bar is prominent and has mic + barcode scan icons** — medium-tech guy like me appreciates being able to scan a product I already own to reorder

## What's weak

- **Three sign-in prompts before I see the sale** — the header prompt, the dedicated "Deals, member rewards & so much more" section, AND the bottom modal all say "sign in or create an account." That's aggressive. I came here to see what's on sale for Memorial Day, not to authenticate three times.
- **Sign-in modal partially obscures the hero product** — the bottom overlay with the × blocks the lower half of the Blackstone image and I can only read "New deals just added" truncated. I'd have to dismiss it to read what I actually came for.
- **Hero product is a grill, not farm supply** — I get it, it's Memorial Day and everybody grills. But this is Tractor Supply. I'm here for coop gear, seed, or tools. A griddle is an impulse buy at best; it's not why I opened the app.
- **No visible farm/livestock category shortcut** — I see a hamburger menu but no quick-tap tiles for "Livestock," "Poultry," "Fencing," or "Pet & Animal." One extra tap for a guy in a hurry loading up the truck isn't the end of the world, but it slows me down.
- **Free-shipping threshold not shown** — I know TSC ships to store, but there's no visible threshold or promo on shipping above the fold. For a guy who orders tools online, that matters.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the single-product Blackstone hero for a Memorial Day lifestyle split** — show the griddle on one side and a farm-relevant item (say, a post driver or a chick brooder on sale) on the other. You're leaving the hobby-farm half of your audience cold with a cookout-only lead.
- **Collapse the three sign-in prompts to one** — keep the header loyalty hook, lose the full-width body section AND the bottom modal. Pick one entry point or let me browse first; this feels like a tollbooth.
- **Add a "Quick Shop" category strip below the hero** — four or five icon tiles: Livestock, Poultry & Chicks, Tools & Hardware, Fencing, Pet & Animal. One tap to my aisle. Tractor Supply's mobile competitor (Rural King, Agri Supply) all do this. It's table stakes.
- **Surface the free-shipping or store-pickup threshold in the top promo bar** — swap or rotate the "Memorial Day Savings are here" copy to include "Free ship to store" or the dollar threshold. Online tool buyers make that decision on the first screen.
