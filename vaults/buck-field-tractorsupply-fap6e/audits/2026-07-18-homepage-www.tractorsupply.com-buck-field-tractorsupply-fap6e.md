# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-07-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-18**
## Executive summary

- I know Tractor Supply — it's where I stop every couple weeks for chicken feed and fencing supplies, and I've got my Neighbors Club card. First look at this page on my phone, I can see they're running some kind of summer sale, but I'm immediately being pestered to sign in before I can see what's actually on sale. The site knows this is my store; feels like they'd rather squeeze my login than show me what's in the cart.

## What's working

- **Neighbors Club hook in the nav** — "Get rewarded!" next to Sign In tells me immediately there's a reason to log in; as a club member, that's muscle memory
- **"UNBEATABLE PRICES" farm product banner** — even partially visible, I can see bags of what looks like feed or fertilizer; that's exactly the category that gets me to open my wallet
- **Location/store finder** — "Norco CA Open at 8 am" with my zip is genuinely useful; I want to know if I can grab something on the way out to the property
- **"Summer Steals and Deals have arrived"** — at least tells me there's a sale on; the timing is right
- **Search bar with barcode scanner** — the scanner icon is smart for guys like me who buy repeat items and want to just scan the bag to reorder

## What's weak

- **Double sign-in wall above the fold** — I've got a "Deals, member rewards & so much more" module AND a bottom-sheet modal both pushing me to log in before I can see anything; that's aggressive
- **"Summer Steals and Deals" banner with no specifics** — "have arrived" does no work; I want to know if it's 20% off feed or $10 off fencing wire before I bother tapping anything
- **"UNBEATABLE PRICES" banner cut off by modal** — I can barely see the product imagery that would actually hook me; the sign-in popup is sitting right on top of it
- **No category nav visible** — on mobile I need quick-tap access to Chicken & Poultry, Farm & Ranch, or Fencing; none of that is above the fold
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "free shipping on orders over $X," nothing that tells me this is still a good deal

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the bottom sign-in modal by default for returning visitors** — if I've been to TSC.com before, show me the product, not a second login nag; the nav already has "Sign In"
- **Put a specific deal in the hero banner** — swap "Summer Steals and Deals have arrived" for something like "Save 20% on Purina Feed this week" with an end date; that gets my thumb moving
- **Pin two or three category quick-links below the search bar** — Chicken & Poultry, Fencing, Farm Tools; I can tap straight to where I spend money without hunting through the hamburger menu
- **Show my Neighbors Club balance or points when I'm logged in** — instead of asking me to sign in to see "so much more," show me "You have 250 points — redeem today" and I'm already in buying mode
