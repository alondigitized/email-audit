# Homepage snapshot · www.tractorsupply.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- This is my store — Tractor Supply is where I go every spring to get my coop and garden sorted. Right now they're running a Yard & Garden Days push with 25% off and a deal on garden hoses, which is perfectly timed for May. But before I can even read the hero properly, they hit me with a sign-in modal I have to swat away, on top of a separate sign-in block that's already sitting in the page. That's two nagging prompts before I've seen a single product.

## What's working

- **"Yard & Garden Days" hero** — right category, right time of year. I'm already thinking about hose replacement and gate hardware.
- **"Save up to $20 Garden Hoses"** callout on the hero — specific product + specific dollar amount is more convincing than a generic percent-off promise.
- **Local store detection** — shows "Santa Clarita CA, Open till 9 pm" and zip 91754 without me touching anything. Saves me the "is there stock near me" question.
- **"Sign In / Enroll — Get rewarded!" in header** — I'm a Neighbors Club member, so the reminder to log in for member pricing actually means something to me.
- **Top banner** — "Savings on Yard & Garden essentials / Shop Now" is direct. No clever wordplay, just tells me what's on sale and where to go.

## What's weak

- **Sign-in modal overlay** — I opened the site to browse hoses, not to create an account. Forcing me to dismiss a modal before I can read the hero is immediate friction.
- **Duplicate sign-in prompts** — the mid-page "Deals, member rewards & so much more / Sign in or create an account" block AND the modal AND the header CTA are all asking the same thing. Once is a nudge; three times is nagging.
- **Hero image is a woman tending a suburban garden bed** — nothing wrong with it, but I've got a coop to maintain and a half-acre fenced. A couple of chickens, a fence post, or a stocky guy loading feed bags would've hit closer to home.
- **No visible product rail** — the modal covers the lower half of the viewport so I can't see if there are actual product tiles below. For a .com-for-tools guy, I need to see what I'm clicking into.
- **No trust / shipping info above fold** — I don't see a free-shipping threshold, no returns mention, nothing. For a tool purchase I'd want that before I tapped deeper.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the mid-page sign-in block for logged-in users** — detect the session and replace it with a "Your Neighbors Club savings: X% off today" balance callout instead of re-prompting me to log in.
- **Replace the modal with a persistent but dismissible banner** — a thin strip at the bottom of the screen ("Sign in to unlock member pricing") is far less hostile than a full overlay that blocks the hero.
- **Swap the hero image for a lifestyle shot that skews working farm** — show someone stacking feed bags or running chicken wire. The suburban garden visual is fine for a mass audience but TSC's core customer is not a backyard herb gardener.
- **Add the free-shipping threshold to the top banner** — even "Free shipping on $49+" next to "Shop Now" gives me a reason to stay and fill a cart rather than comparing against my local store run.
