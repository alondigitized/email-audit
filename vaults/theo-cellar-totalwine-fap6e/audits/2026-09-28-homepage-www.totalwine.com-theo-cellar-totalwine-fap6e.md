# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-09-28
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- First thing that hits me is a treasure-map bourbon banner — not wine. Total Wine's clearly leaning hard into whiskey season with that "300+ Bourbons" hero and a Vegas whiskey/tequila ticket promo up top, which tells me this is a broad spirits-and-beer retailer flexing its catalog, not a place that's talking to a guy stocking a wine cellar. The fall flavors strip underneath at least nods toward wine with "Fireside Wine for Cozy Nights," but I have to hunt for it.

## What's working

- **"Best of Fall Flavors" rail** — the "Fireside Wine for Cozy Nights" tile at least gives me a wine-specific entry point, and it's seasonally on-point for late September.
- **Top banner offer** — a clear $50-off-with-code deal is the kind of concrete, no-nonsense promo I respond to, even though this one's for whiskey tickets, not wine.
- **Clean nav** — "Wine" is the very first tab, one tap away, no digging required.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% bourbon** — "Discover Over 300+ Bourbons" with a vintage map background is a fine ad, just not for me. I build a wine cellar; this tells me nothing about a Cab or a case discount.
- **Sign-in tooltip overlap** — the "Sign in for rewards, offers, and more!" box sits right on top of the nav row, covering part of "Services." Looks like a stray hover state that got caught mid-render.
- **No &MORE specifics** — I'm already a member. Show me my points balance or a member-price badge, not a vague "sign in for rewards" nudge.
- **No case-discount or spring-sale signal** — nothing here talks to how I actually buy (by the case, at the seasonal sale).

## Recommendations

- Swap the hero rotation so a wine-focused slide (varietal spotlight, cellar-builder angle, or the spring case-sale teaser) shows first for logged-in wine browsers, not just bourbon.
- Fix the Sign In tooltip so it doesn't overlap the nav bar — it reads like a bug on a first glance.
- Put an actual &MORE rewards balance or member-pricing badge above the fold instead of the generic "sign in for rewards" line.
- Add a "Tasting Events Near You" or "Upcoming In-Store Tastings" module — I track that calendar and it's nowhere on this screen.
