# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-06-08
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-08**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get a homepage — I got a bot gate. Total Wine put up a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" interstitial before letting me anywhere near the actual site. Whatever deals, varietal navigation, or &MORE rewards content they've built sits completely behind this wall. From where I'm standing, this brand looks like a locked store with a sign on the door.

## What's working

- The Total Wine & More logo renders clearly — I at least know I landed on the right domain.
- The accessibility icon next to the button is a small positive signal that the site nominally cares about inclusive design.

## What's weak

- **The entire above-the-fold experience is a friction wall.** I came to shop wine, and the first thing I see is a bot interrogation. That's a full stop.
- **No homepage content is accessible.** No hero, no categories, no deals, no &MORE loyalty hooks — everything I care about as a cellar-builder is invisible.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is awkward.** On a phone, press-and-hold is already an ambiguous gesture (context menus, text selection). Asking me to do it as a trust challenge adds real friction for a 45-year-old with medium tech comfort.
- **Blank white space dominates.** The screen is roughly 80% empty. Even the bot-check page could surface a "while you wait, here's what's on sale" message — it doesn't.

## Recommendations

- **Move the CAPTCHA challenge behind a risk signal, not in front of every mobile visitor.** If I'm a logged-in &MORE member, skip this entirely — you already know me.
- **Surface a static, pre-gate promo strip above the verification box.** Even "Spring Case Sale — Save up to 25%" in a banner above the "Press & Hold" button would keep intent alive during the friction moment.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a standard checkbox CAPTCHA or a tap-once confirmation on mobile.** The gesture is counterintuitive on touchscreens and will cause drop-off among exactly the mid-tech-comfort shoppers in Total Wine's core demographic.
- **Add a "Sign in to skip" shortcut** — loyalty members willing to authenticate shouldn't hit the same wall as anonymous traffic.
