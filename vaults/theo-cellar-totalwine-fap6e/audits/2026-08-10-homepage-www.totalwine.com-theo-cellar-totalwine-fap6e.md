# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-08-10
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- I typed in totalwine.com expecting to browse the Cab shelf and instead I'm staring at a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" bot-check screen. No wine, no deals, no nav — just a verification widget and a reference ID. As far as this screenshot shows me, the site hasn't loaded at all.

## What's working

- The bot-check widget itself is clean and legible — clear logo, clear instruction ("Press & Hold to confirm you are a human"), no clutter or broken layout on this interstitial screen
- It's honest about what it's doing — it's not disguised as content, it just tells me it's a security check

## What's weak

- There is no homepage here — the entire above-the-fold experience is a bot-verification gate, so I can't evaluate any actual merchandising
- No indication of how long this takes or why I'm being challenged, which is mildly annoying on a phone when I just want to check a Cab price
- The reference ID (`eb3df690-94a4-11f1-bc4f-1f36fb57ad4c`) is exposed on screen — fine for support purposes, but it signals this is a friction step, not a welcome mat
- Nothing here tells me this is even the wine store I intended to visit beyond the logo — no cart, no nav, no search

## Recommendations

- Get this human-verification step to resolve faster or skip it entirely for known/repeat mobile visitors — I've shopped this site by the case before, I shouldn't be gated like a stranger
- If a challenge is unavoidable, add a one-line reassurance ("Just confirming you're not a bot — takes 2 seconds") so it doesn't feel like a dead end
- Once past the gate, make sure the actual homepage loads fast — right now the mobile experience effectively adds an extra full-page step before I see a single bottle or promo
- Consider a lighter-weight check (invisible/passive) for logged-in &MORE members instead of an interactive press-and-hold
