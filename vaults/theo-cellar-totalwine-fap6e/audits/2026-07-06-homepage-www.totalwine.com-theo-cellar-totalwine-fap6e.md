# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-07-06
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- I opened totalwine.com on my phone and never made it past the front door — literally. What I'm looking at is a bot-verification interstitial: a white screen, the Total Wine & More logo, the words "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)," and a gray pill button. No deals, no navigation, no cellar section, no &MORE rewards — nothing. From a shopping standpoint, this is a hard stop before the experience even begins.

## What's working

- **Clean render:** The bot-check page itself loads without any visual errors — the logo is crisp, the pill button is legible, the reference ID is small and unobtrusive. Whatever they're running for bot detection at least doesn't break visually.
- **Clear single action:** If I absolutely must engage with this screen, there's no ambiguity — one prompt, one button. No competing elements.

## What's weak

- **Complete content blackout:** As someone who came to check current pricing on a case of Malbec before committing to a cellar buy, I'm seeing zero product, zero price, zero value prop. This is the equivalent of a bouncer with no list — I know the place exists, but I'm not inside.
- **No reassurance for a logged-in member:** I'm an &MORE member. There's nothing here to acknowledge that I might have an account, might have a saved cellar list, or might be mid-session. The bot wall doesn't discriminate, and that's friction I didn't expect from a site I actively shop.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is awkward UX:** On a phone screen, a press-and-hold interaction mid-browse feels clunky. It's a pattern borrowed from desktop CAPTCHAs and it doesn't translate great to touch. I'm not going to drop my phone, I'm not going to struggle — but it's an unnecessary speed bump.
- **Reference ID exposed but unexplained:** `81d24cf0-7925-11f1-be7d-53647a0dccfc` sits at the bottom. A medium-tech shopper like me notices it, wonders if something went wrong, and second-guesses whether the site is broken or flagged my connection.
- **Zero seasonal or campaign signal:** It's early July — spring sale territory, which is exactly when I buy by the case. Even a hint of that would keep me motivated. Nothing here does that.

## Recommendations

- **Exempt recognized &MORE members or logged-in sessions from bot checks entirely** — or at minimum, show the member's first name and rewards balance above the interstitial so loyal shoppers know they're recognized before they jump through the hoop.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a single tap + invisible behavioral scoring** — the hold interaction on mobile is jarring; modern bot detection (Cloudflare Turnstile invisible mode, for example) could handle this without interrupting the session at all.
- **Add a thin promo strip above or below the verification prompt** — even a static "Case sale on now — up to 20% off" banner costs nothing and keeps my motivation alive while I complete the verification.
- **Hide or contextualize the reference ID** — move it to a collapsible "Having trouble?" link. Displaying a raw UUID as the last thing on screen suggests something broke, not that security is working.
