# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-10-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-10-26**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a big-box wine and spirits retailer going all in on a Halloween party run. The hero is "Forgot Something for Halloween?" with skull vodka, BuzzBallz and 19 Crimes. As someone building a cellar, I feel like I walked into the party aisle. The store-pickup selector, "Deals" tab and rewards prompts tell me it's a real retailer, but nothing here speaks to varietal or price-floor shopping.

## What's working

- **"Wine" is the first nav item.** I can get to my category in one tap.
- **Pickup at Pasadena, CA.** The store selector is visible up top, which matters because inventory and pricing are local.
- **"Shop Halloween Essentials" button.** It's high-contrast, plainly worded and unmissable.
- **Red "Deals" tab in the nav.** It's the one place I'd go to check the price floor.
- **Seasonal timing.** The page is current, not stale.
- **The "Engraving services are temporarily unavailable" banner.** It's blunt and honest, which I respect.

## What's weak

- **The hero is novelty-driven.** BuzzBallz Biggies, a skull vodka bottle and a "Wolfman" 19 Crimes label aren't what I buy. Nothing signals serious wine.
- **"Great prices" with no price.** I track price floors by varietal, and this gives me nothing to benchmark against Wine.com.
- **The sign-in tooltip covers the nav.** "New" and the middle of the menu are hidden or clipped, and the popover hides the "Deals" label's neighbors.
- **The "Earn Rewards" button floats over content.** It sits bottom-left and vaguely, with no point value or member price attached.
- **The three tiles are all party-oriented.** "Wine & Candy: The Perfect Pair" is a novelty pairing. Nothing mentions tasting events, case discounts or cellar-worthy bottles.
- **No reviews, ratings or expert scores** visible on the first screen.
- **The rail below the tiles hasn't rendered** in this view. I only see empty slots.

## Recommendations

- **Add a second hero slot for wine buyers.** Show a case-discount message such as "Mix any 6 and save 10%" over a wall of reds, so cellar builders see a real price.
- **Show member pricing on the first screen.** Replace the generic "Earn Rewards" pill with "&MORE members: extra savings on every case", and show a sample member price on at least one featured bottle.
- **Delay the sign-in tooltip or dock it elsewhere.** It currently hides the nav, so show it after the first scroll, or let it sit under the Sign In button without covering "New".
- **Swap one of the three tiles for "Upcoming tasting events near Pasadena".** It would speak directly to people who track event calendars, and it differentiates from online-only competitors.
