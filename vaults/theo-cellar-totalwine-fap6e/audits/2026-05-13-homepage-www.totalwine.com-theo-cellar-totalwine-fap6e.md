# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I didn't get a homepage — I got a bot wall. The entire viewport is a "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" interstitial, nothing else. Total Wine is blocking access before showing me anything about wine, deals, or my &MORE rewards. That's a rough first impression when I'm trying to check the spring sale on my phone.

## What's working

- **Logo recognition:** The Total Wine & More logo renders cleanly and I know immediately what site I'm on. Brand ID isn't lost.
- **Accessibility button:** There's a visible accessibility icon next to the "Press & Hold" button, which at least signals they're thinking about users who can't long-press.
- **Clean layout:** No visual clutter, no broken images — the bot-check page itself is simple and legible.

## What's weak

- **Zero commerce:** There is no wine, no deal, no product, no navigation — nothing. I came here to check prices before a case buy and I'm staring at a white screen.
- **"Press & Hold" on mobile is friction:** On a touchscreen phone, a press-and-hold interaction is awkward and non-standard. This will frustrate plenty of casual shoppers.
- **No &MORE rewards context:** I'm a loyalty member. There's no sign the site knows or cares — no "sign in to see your rewards" even in the interstitial.
- **Reference ID exposed:** A raw UUID reference ID sits at the bottom of the screen — that's developer noise, not customer-facing copy. It signals this page wasn't designed with real users in mind.
- **No fallback or explanation:** There's no "why are we asking this?" explanation. A customer who's never seen a bot challenge may just close the tab.

## Recommendations

- **Add a brief trust line under the challenge:** Something like "We ask this to keep deals fair for real customers" — 45-year-old me understands bots exist, but I want to know why I'm being asked before I engage.
- **Swap "Press & Hold" for a standard checkbox CAPTCHA on mobile:** Press-and-hold is a poor mobile UX pattern. A tap-to-confirm or simple checkbox would be faster and less likely to cause bounces.
- **Pre-authenticate &MORE members:** If I'm signed in (cookie or app session), skip the bot wall entirely. I'm a known, paying loyalty member — don't treat me like a scraper.
- **Show a teaser strip above or below the interstitial:** Even a static "Spring Sale — up to X% off" banner behind the gate would tell me there's a reason to complete the challenge.
