# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-07-20
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened Total Wine on my phone and the first thing I see is a blank white screen with a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" challenge — I never even got to the homepage. Whatever bot-detection layer Total Wine is running just stopped me cold before I could see a single product, offer, or even the navigation. From where I'm standing, this is a wall, not a welcome mat.

## What's working

- **Logo renders correctly** — "Total Wine & More" brand identity is clear and legible at the top of the interstitial.
- **Accessibility button is present** — there's a visible accessibility icon next to the Press & Hold button, which at least signals some ADA intention.
- **The interstitial itself is clean** — no broken images, no garbled text, no layout chaos on the challenge screen itself.

## What's weak

- **The homepage is completely inaccessible** — I'm a 45-year-old &MORE member opening the app on my phone on a Sunday afternoon, and I get a bot wall. This is the entire problem.
- **"Press & Hold" is a friction-heavy gesture for mobile** — on a phone, this is awkward. I'm not a bot; I'm a case buyer. This interaction pattern feels designed for a desktop mouse.
- **Zero commerce content above (or below) the fold** — no offers, no categories, no spring sale reminder, nothing. If I weren't already motivated, I'd be gone.
- **No fallback or explanation** — the reference ID at the bottom (e6da7950-...) means nothing to me. If this challenge fails or I can't hold correctly, I have no idea what to do next.
- **&MORE membership is not recognized** — if I'm logged in as a member, this bot check shouldn't even appear. If I'm not logged in, that's a missed opportunity to surface a sign-in prompt instead of a cold CAPTCHA.

## Recommendations

- **Exempt recognized &MORE members from bot challenges** — if I'm authenticated or have a valid cookie, skip the interstitial entirely. I shouldn't be treated like a scraper after years of case purchases.
- **Replace "Press & Hold" with a tap-to-confirm on mobile** — a hold gesture is counterintuitive on a touchscreen and adds needless friction for real customers.
- **Surface a sign-in prompt instead of a blank interstitial** — if bot risk is real, prompt me to log in to my &MORE account. That solves the bot problem and gets me into a personalized session simultaneously.
- **Add a fallback path** — "Having trouble? Try our app" or a simple refresh link so a legitimate customer doesn't hit a dead end and bounce to Wine.com (which, for the record, I have open in the next tab).
