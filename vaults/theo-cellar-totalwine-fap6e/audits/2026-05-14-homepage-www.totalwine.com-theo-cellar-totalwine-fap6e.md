# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I didn't even get to the homepage — Total Wine threw up a bot-detection wall the second I landed. All I see is a white screen with their logo, "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)," and a "Press & Hold" button. As a &MORE member who comes here specifically for spring case sales and tasting-event calendars, this is a frustrating first impression. Zero merchandising, zero loyalty recognition, zero reason to feel welcome.

## What's working

- The Total Wine & More logo is clean and recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The accessibility icon (wheelchair) in the bottom-left is visible, which suggests some baseline compliance intent.

## What's weak

- The bot-detection gate is the entire above-the-fold experience — I'm a logged-in &MORE member on my phone and I'm being treated like a scraper.
- "Press & Hold" is a clunky mobile interaction that feels more like a CAPTCHA punishment than a welcome mat.
- Zero merchandising: no hero, no banner, no offer, no navigation — nothing that tells me what's on sale this week.
- No loyalty recognition at all — if my account is known, there's no sign of it.
- The blank white surround above and below the challenge block makes the page look broken.
- Reference ID `ba08fc30-4fd7-11f1-99d4-03bfaa8599a5` at the bottom reads like an error page, not a brand touchpoint.

## Recommendations

- **Whitelist authenticated &MORE members** from the bot-challenge flow — if I'm already signed in or have a session cookie, skip the gate entirely.
- **If the gate is unavoidable, brand it**: swap the stark white for a cellar-image background, add copy like "Back to your cellar in a sec," and show my rewards balance or a teaser offer so the interruption has value.
- **Shorten the interaction**: a one-tap checkbox ("I'm not a bot") converts better on mobile than a press-and-hold, which is easy to fumble and infuriating on a small screen.
- **Surface a seasonal hook in the gate screen itself**: even a single line — "Spring Case Sale — up to 20% off" — would keep me oriented while I wait.
