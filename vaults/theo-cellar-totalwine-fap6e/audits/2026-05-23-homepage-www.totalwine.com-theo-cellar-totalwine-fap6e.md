# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened totalwine.com and the entire screen is a bot-verification wall — a "Press & Hold to confirm you are a human" challenge from what looks like a Cloudflare or similar anti-bot system. There is no homepage. No hero, no offers, no navigation, no product content whatsoever. As a customer who just wants to check the spring sale selection, this is a dead end.

## What's working

- The Total Wine & More logo renders cleanly and is recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place before hitting the wall.
- The "Press & Hold" button is large and easy to tap on mobile — if I choose to bother.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage is replaced by an anti-bot challenge — I came here to browse the spring case sale, not to prove I'm alive.
- There is zero content above or below the fold: no nav, no hero, no loyalty hook, no offers, nothing.
- The reference ID (`da02ed50-52aa-11f1-ab01-ef33640d6e1c`) at the bottom feels cold and transactional — not what a wine retailer wants a returning &MORE member to see on arrival.
- No accessibility fallback or explanation for why the gate was triggered, which is frustrating for a repeat customer.
- This gate fires on a clean mobile visit — that's an aggressive bot-detection threshold that will catch real customers.

## Recommendations

- Tune the bot-detection sensitivity so that a normal mobile browser session from a recognized returning customer doesn't trigger the gate at all — I'm a &MORE member, I should be getting a personalized landing page, not a CAPTCHA.
- If the gate is unavoidable, inject a brief reassurance line: "We occasionally check for bots. Your cart and rewards are safe." — keeps the brand relationship intact during friction.
- Post-gate redirect should land on a personalized or session-aware homepage, not a generic cold start — use the &MORE login state to surface my recent purchases or the current case-sale banner immediately.
- Add a visible "Sign in to skip" option so loyal members aren't treated the same as anonymous scrapers.
