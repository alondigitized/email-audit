# Homepage snapshot · www.totalwine.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened totalwine.com and never got to see the actual homepage — the site immediately hit me with a bot-verification wall. All I'm looking at is a "Before we continue… Press & Hold to confirm you are a human (and not a bot)" interstitial. No products, no promos, no navigation, nothing. As a guy who cross-shops Wine.com on every purchase, this kind of friction makes the competition feel a lot more welcoming.

## What's working

- **Total Wine & More logo** renders cleanly and is instantly recognizable — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- **Accessibility icon** (the wheelchair/person symbol) is visible bottom-left, which is a minor trust signal that they've thought about inclusive design.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is blocked** behind a "Press & Hold" bot-check — I cannot see a single product, promo, or category. For a 45-year-old guy shopping on his phone during a spring case sale, this is just annoying.
- **No loyalty/rewards acknowledgment** — I'm a &MORE member. If I'm recognized (or even just greeted as a potential member), this wall feels even more tone-deaf.
- **No fallback content** — the page is 90% white space above and below the verification box. It's barren and gives zero confidence that I landed on a real retail site.
- **Reference ID visible at the bottom** (`ab30c340-5235-11f1-b9f3-9769442d96c1`) — that's a Cloudflare or similar bot-mitigation fingerprint. Fine under the hood, but it's surfaced to the user, which feels clunky and technical.
- **"Press & Hold" mechanic on mobile** is awkward — long-press interactions are not intuitive on a touchscreen for most casual mobile shoppers.

## Recommendations

- **Implement bot detection before blocking the full render** — serve cached/static homepage content first, then silently challenge suspicious traffic in the background. Legitimate shoppers should never see this wall.
- **Whitelist recognized &MORE members** — if I'm cookied or logged in, skip the challenge entirely. Loyal members shouldn't be treated like bots.
- **If a challenge is unavoidable, brand it** — swap the sterile white interstitial for a Total Wine-branded holding screen with a tagline ("Great wine is worth the wait — just a sec") and a product image so the brand impression isn't zero.
- **Remove the raw Reference ID from the user-visible page** — it's technical noise that erodes trust rather than building it.
