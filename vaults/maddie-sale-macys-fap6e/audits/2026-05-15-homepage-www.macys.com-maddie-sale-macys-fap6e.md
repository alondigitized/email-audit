# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is Macy's in full One Day Sale mode, and as someone who has been planning my calendar around this sale for years, I recognized it the second the page loaded — the red, the big percentage, the "signature sale" language. But honestly? An AI chat bubble popped up right over the hero and hid half the headline, which is not a great first impression. The summer BBQ secondary banner underneath is fine, but I came here for the One Day Sale and I had to X out of a bot to even read the details.

## What's working

- **One Day Sale branding in the top bar and hero** — "Up to 60% off One Day Sale deals" in the ticker strip plus the 40-60% headline means the value prop hits me before I even focus my eyes. I know immediately there's a reason to stay.
- **"Shop by category" dropdown CTA** — it's right there on the hero, which tells me I don't have to hunt through a hamburger menu to find what I want.
- **The secondary BBQ banner** — "Shorts, swim & more for daytime BBQ's Up to 60% OFF" is timely and visual. Summer entertaining is very much in my wheelhouse.
- **Red brand palette is consistent** — the page screams Macy's; I am not confused about where I am.

## What's weak

- **The AI assistant modal pops over the hero** — I came here for a One Day Sale, not a chat bot. It literally covered the "One Day Sale" headline. I had to tap the X before I could read what I came to read. That is friction I did not ask for.
- **No Star Money or Star Pass mention anywhere** — I layer Star Pass on top of the department promos every single One Day Sale. If that's still live, the homepage doesn't hint at it. That's a missed upsell for their best customers.
- **"Shop by category" is too generic** — I want to jump to Women's Dresses or Handbags or Fragrance. A dropdown is one extra tap; quick-category tiles would get me there faster.
- **Top-bar text is cut off** — I can see "50" at the right edge of the ticker strip but the rest is cropped. That tells me the responsive layout isn't handling the phone screen properly.
- **No urgency signal** — "One Day Sale" is a name, not a countdown. No "Ends tonight" or clock in sight. For a sale that's famously time-limited, that's a missed conversion lever.
- **BBQ imagery reads young** — the couple in the lower banner looks like they're in their 30s. I am 60. I am also going to a BBQ this weekend, but the photo doesn't make me feel seen.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the unsolicited AI modal on page load** — or at minimum delay it until after 10 seconds of browsing. Popping a chat window over the hero of your biggest sale of the season is counterproductive. Offer "Ask Macy's+" as a persistent button, not a hard interrupt.
- **Add a Star Money / Star Pass badge in the hero or top bar** — something like "Star Pass members earn 5X Star Money — Sign In to activate" would stop me mid-scroll and push me to log in immediately.
- **Swap the generic "Shop by category" dropdown for 4–5 horizontal category chips** — Women's, Shoes, Handbags, Home, Beauty — so I can jump in one tap instead of two.
- **Add a countdown or "ends tonight" timestamp to the One Day Sale headline** — even "Ends May 15 at midnight" printed in small text under the 40-60% headline would sharply increase urgency for deal-hunters like me.
