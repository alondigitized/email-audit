# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-07-16
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is not the welcome I was expecting from a store I've been shopping at for thirty years. Instead of sales, hero banners, and my Star Rewards balance, I'm staring at a big red shield with a line through it and the words "You don't have access to this page." Macy's blocked me — their own loyal customer — before I even got to see a single dress or fragrance. Whatever brand this looks like right now, it looks like a closed door.

## What's working

- The Macy's logo and "Celebrations start at Macy's" tagline are visible in the header — at least I know I landed on the right site.
- The Search bar is accessible and functional-looking — if I knew what I wanted, I could theoretically type it in.
- "Sign In" link is visible in the top right — so if this is a geo-block or bot-detection issue, there's a path to authenticate.
- The footer shows "Customer Service" and "Macy's Credit Card" accordion items — some navigation exists even in the error state.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage content area is a hard block — no hero, no promotions, no categories, no Star Rewards hook, nothing I came here for.
- The error message "You don't have access to this page" is alarming and unexplained — it doesn't tell me *why*, which makes me feel like my account got flagged or something went wrong on my end.
- The only recovery path offered is "update your browser and refresh" or call 1-800-289-8229 — I'm not calling an 800 number to look at a sale page.
- A reference code (`18.1171ca17.1784196997.2f3e6d5e`) is shown but meaningless to me as a shopper — technical noise that adds confusion, not help.
- No fallback content — not even a link to the homepage, a popular category, or current promotions. A dead end.

## Recommendations

- **Show a graceful fallback page** instead of an access-blocked error — even a stripped-down homepage with the current sale banner and a "Sign In to see your deals" CTA would recover the session.
- **Explain the block in plain language** — "We're having trouble verifying your location / browser. Here's what's on sale today while we sort it out." Don't leave a loyal customer feeling punished.
- **Add a direct link to sign in or continue as guest** prominently in the error state — the Sign In link exists in the header but it's easy to miss when a giant red shield is the focal point.
- **Surface the phone number as a last resort, not the first option** — lead with self-service recovery (refresh, sign in, try a different browser), then offer the 800 number.
