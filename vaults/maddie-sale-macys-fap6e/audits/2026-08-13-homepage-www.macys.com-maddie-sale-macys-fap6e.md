# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This looks like the Macy's I know from decades of One Day Sales — a big department store trying to be everything to everyone at once. Right now it's clearly leaning hard into Back to School with graphic tees and backpacks, which tells me I walked in on the wrong week for my own shopping list. The deals ticker up top is doing more for me than the actual hero image.

## What's working

- **The deals ticker** — "Up to 70% off Charter Club bedding," "30% off Lancôme favorites + free gift" — this is exactly the kind of scannable multi-offer strip I comb through before I even look at the pretty pictures.
- **The nav bar** — Women, Beauty, Shoes, Jewelry all one tap away, no digging required.
- **"Shop now" button** — clean, bold, easy to find, no squinting needed.

## What's weak

- **The whole hero is Back to School** — graphic tees, backpacks, kids on cafeteria chairs. I don't have grandkids I'm buying that for right now, so this slide does nothing for me.
- **That "Ask Macy's" popup box** — it opened on its own and is sitting right on top of "Furniture & Mattresses" and "Kids & Baby" in the nav. I don't want a chatbot, I want to see my categories.
- **No sign of my Star Rewards or Star Money balance anywhere** — I'm a loyalty shopper, that's the first thing I look for, and it's nowhere above the fold.
- **"Sign In" is generic** — no personalized greeting, no "welcome back," nothing that tells me this store remembers I'm a regular.

## Recommendations

- Close that "Ask Macy's" chat bubble by default — let people open it if they want it, don't have it covering the nav.
- Swap in a hero slide (or make it the first slide) featuring a Star Rewards/Star Money callout with my points balance front and center — I plan my purchases around that.
- Rotate a home or beauty lifestyle shot into the carousel alongside Back to School — not everyone browsing in August has kids to shop for.
- Put the Lancôme "30% off + free gift" offer as its own tile near the top instead of just a line in the scrolling ticker — that's real money to me and I'd tap straight into it.
