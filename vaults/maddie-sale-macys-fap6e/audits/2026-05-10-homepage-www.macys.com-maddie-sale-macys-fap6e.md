# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Macy's is clearly in full Mother's Day mode — I can see the sale banner behind everything, and the "Last day!" strip at the top got my attention right away. But before I could even read the hero, I got hit with two overlapping pop-ups at once: an AI assistant tooltip and a "Get 25% off" email signup modal stacked right on top of each other. As a woman who has been shopping Macy's One Day Sales for decades, this feels like getting ambushed at the door before I can even see what's on the table.

## What's working

- **"Today's Deals — Up to 50% off gifts for Mom — Last day!"** top strip is a strong urgency hook and the first thing my eye hits. I know immediately there's a sale happening.
- **"Ends in 12:00:26" countdown timer** on the hero is exactly the kind of One Day Sale energy I come to Macy's for. That ticking clock makes me want to act.
- **"Celebrations start at Macy's"** branding above the logo is warm and holiday-appropriate — feels like the Macy's I know.
- **"Ask Macy's+"** AI button — I'm medium-tech comfortable, so I appreciate knowing there's help available, even if the popup timing is terrible.

## What's weak

- **Double-modal ambush**: an AI assistant tooltip AND a 25%-off email signup modal are both open simultaneously, covering the entire hero. I can barely read what the sale is about. This is genuinely disorienting.
- **"Get 25% off* when you sign up for emails"** — I already get Macy's emails. This is not for me. "First-time subscribers only" in small print under the "Email my code" button is exactly the kind of bait-and-switch that makes loyal customers feel invisible.
- **No Star Money or Star Pass mention anywhere**: I layer Star Pass on top of department promos — that combination is the whole reason I choose Macy's over Nordstrom Rack or Belk. Not a word of it visible.
- **No navigation visible**: the hamburger menu is there, but I can't tap into Handbags, Shoes, or Fragrance without closing two pop-ups first.
- **Hero imagery invisible**: whatever lifestyle photo or model is behind those modals, I cannot see it at all. If there's a beautiful Mother's Day scene there, it's completely wasted.

## Recommendations

- **Show only one modal at a time** — close the AI tooltip automatically before the email popup fires, or kill one of them entirely. Two overlapping interruptions in the first second will bounce shoppers.
- **Swap the email promo for a returning-member offer** — "Star Pass members save an extra 15% today" catches a loyal shopper like me far better than a first-subscriber-only pitch I'm excluded from.
- **Add a Star Money/rewards balance chip in the header** — right next to Sign In, a small "Your Stars: 4,200" would make me feel recognized and give me a reason to stay.
- **Pin the countdown timer to the banner strip** so it stays visible even after the modals are dismissed — don't bury the urgency under pop-up clutter.
