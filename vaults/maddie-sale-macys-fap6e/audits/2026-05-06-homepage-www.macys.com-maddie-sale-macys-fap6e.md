# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, at least I can see there's a Mother's Day sale — that much punches through the clutter. But the second this page loaded, two things popped up on top of each other: some AI chat thing I didn't ask for, and an email-code offer, and now I can barely see the actual sale. Macy's is clearly pushing a big spring event, but they buried it under their own popups.

## What's working

- **"Mother's Day Sale Up to 50% OFF" headline** — this is exactly the kind of big, simple, dollar-sign event that makes me open Macy's in the first place. Clear numbers, no fussiness.
- **"Ends Sunday" deadline** — I see that red text and I feel it. That's the urgency I need to stop scrolling Instagram and start shopping.
- **Top banner "Today's Deals"** — signals that deals are active and current, which is the first thing I check.
- **25% off email offer** — I'm already signed up, so it doesn't apply to me, but for someone new it's a real hook with a real number.
- **"Celebrations start at" / 53 branding** — feels festive and occasion-appropriate for Mother's Day week.

## What's weak

- **Double-modal catastrophe** — The "Ask Macy's+" AI tooltip popped up at the same time as the email-signup modal. I'm 60, not 25 — I do not want to troubleshoot two overlapping pop-ups to find the actual sale. I almost closed the tab.
- **No Star Rewards callout above the fold** — I layer Star Pass on top of department promos. That's my whole strategy. But there's nothing here reminding me my rewards are active or how to apply them. A Macy's loyalist is invisible to this page.
- **Hero is completely blocked** — I cannot see any product imagery or the hero CTA button because the email modal covers the bottom third of the visible screen. I don't know what I'd be shopping for.
- **"Ask Macy's+" AI chat with tooltip** — I appreciate they're trying something new, but that tooltip auto-opened and sat on top of everything. I have medium tech comfort and this felt like a glitch, not a feature.
- **Free shipping offer is cut off** — The top banner says "Free 6-p..." and then it's clipped. I can't read the threshold. That matters to me for online orders.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss one modal, not two** — Show either the email capture OR the AI tooltip onload, never both simultaneously. On mobile the layering is a render disaster that will bounce older shoppers immediately.
- **Add a Star Rewards/Star Money strip below the hero** — One line: "Your Star Money is active — stack it on today's sale." Loyal customers need to see their status without signing in first.
- **Reveal the free-shipping threshold fully** — The top bar text is cut off at "Free 6-p." Fix the truncation so I can read the complete offer. Free shipping over $X is a genuine conversion driver for me.
- **Swap the AI tooltip auto-open for a persistent "Ask Macy's+" icon** — Let me tap it if I want it. Auto-opening a chat bubble on top of a modal makes the page feel broken, not helpful.
