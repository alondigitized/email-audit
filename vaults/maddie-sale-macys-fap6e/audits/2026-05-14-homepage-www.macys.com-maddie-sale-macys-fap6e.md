# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, I pulled up Macy's and the first thing I see is some AI chatbot popping up right in front of the sale. Honey, I came here for Star Money and One Day deals, not to chat with a robot. There's a Flash Sale running — 40-60% off active and outdoor — with a real countdown clock, which normally gets my attention. But the whole page feels like it was dressed for a 25-year-old tennis player, not me.

## What's working

- **The countdown timer** ("Ends in 11:06:52") is the best thing on this page — I've trained myself to notice those, and it creates real urgency for a sale shopper like me.
- **The top banner deal** ("Today! Up to 60% off activewear") is direct and specific. I can read it in two seconds on my phone.
- **The "Celebrations start at 50s macy's" branding** is a nice seasonal touch — it signals the brand is in a moment, not just running a filler sale.
- **The offer percentage range** (40-60% off) is honest and specific, not vague "up to 10% off" fine-print garbage.

## What's weak

- **The AI popup modal ("Ask Macy's+")** is the first thing that fully renders on my screen. It blocks the hero. As someone with medium tech comfort, a chatbot in my face before I can even see the sale makes me want to close the app, not engage with it.
- **Nobody on this page looks like me.** A young girl with a tennis racket, a young man in a navy hoodie, a young woman in pink athletic shorts — these are not my people. I'm 60, I shop Macy's for fashion, fragrance, and occasion wear.
- **No Star Money or Star Rewards anywhere above the fold.** That's my whole relationship with Macy's. Layering Star Pass on top of a One Day Sale is literally my sport, and it's invisible here.
- **"Shop now" is too generic.** Shop what? Active & outdoor is a big tent — I need something that narrows to my actual interest.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-launch AI modal** or at minimum move it to a corner widget — a forced popup on load is the fastest way to make a Boomer like me close out of habit.
- **Add a Star Rewards / Star Money status bar** in the header or just below the search bar; even a small "You have X points" nudge would make me feel like Macy's knows I'm a loyal customer.
- **Swap at least one hero slide for lifestyle imagery that skews 50+** — show me a put-together woman my age finding something she loves, not a teenager with a racket.
- **Make the free-shipping threshold visible on the promo strip** alongside the countdown — I always do the math before I check out, and if I have to hunt for it, I get annoyed.
