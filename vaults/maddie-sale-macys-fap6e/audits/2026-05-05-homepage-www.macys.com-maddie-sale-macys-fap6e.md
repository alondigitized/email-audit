# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- Well, I barely got to see the homepage because two pop-ups jumped right in front of me before I could look around — an AI chat bubble AND an email sign-up modal stacked on top of each other. When I could peek through, I see it's Mother's Day Sale season with up to 50% off, which is fine, but this whole thing is treating me like a stranger who's never been to Macy's in her life. Not a word about my Star Rewards, not a hint of a One Day Sale, and the best offer they can show me is a first-time-subscriber coupon I can't even use.
- **4/10**
- + Concrete offer visible: "Today! 40-50% off beauty" in the top deals bar, "Up to 50% OFF" in the partially-visible hero — that's real numbers, I can work with that
- + Current campaign matches the season: Mother's Day in early May is right on time
- + Primary CTA technically visible: "Shop now" is peeking out at the bottom behind the modal
- − Hero copy does not speak to me: "Big savings to surprise her with something special" is gifting language — I shop for myself
- − No returning-shopper hook whatsoever: no Star Rewards balance, no "Welcome back," no Star Pass callout
- − Visual hierarchy is destroyed: two overlays competing for my eyes at once
- − Layout is effectively broken: the hero is almost completely blocked by stacked pop-ups — that counts as a render/clarity issue for me
- − Loyalty/membership benefits: zero mention of Star Rewards, Star Money, or Star Pass above the fold
- − Demographic mismatch: gift-giver framing, not the One Day Sale veteran who's here to shop for herself
- − Offer feels exclusionary: "Get 25% off\* … First time-subscribers only" — I've been a Macy's customer for decades, this does nothing for me

## What's working

- **Top deals bar copy** — "Today! 40-50% off beauty" is exactly the kind of specific, scannable deal language I look for the second I land anywhere. That's my language.
- **Mother's Day timing** — Mid-May is right, and 50% off is a genuinely compelling number if it applies to what I want to buy.
- **"Ask Macy's+" AI assistant button** — I might actually try that once I close these pop-ups; asking it to find a sale on fragrance or show me what's eligible for Star Money could save me time in-store.
- **Search bar is prominent** — It's right under the logo, large enough, easy to tap. When I want a specific item I can go straight there.

## What's weak

- **Double-overlay attack** — An AI tooltip AND a full-screen email modal firing at the same time is aggressive. I'm closing both before I've seen anything, and now I'm already annoyed.
- **"Get 25% off\* when you sign up for emails" modal** — First-time subscribers only. I've been getting Macy's emails for years. This is literally an offer I cannot redeem, and it's the most prominent thing on the screen.
- **No Star Rewards, Star Pass, or Star Money mention** — I layer promos like a pro and I came here knowing my Star Money balance window is open. The homepage doesn't acknowledge I exist as a loyalty member at all.
- **Hero is inaccessible** — I can read "Mother's Day Sale / Up to 50% OFF" and that's it. The lifestyle image, any product shots, the full CTA — all buried under the modal. I'm guessing at what's actually there.
- **"Surprise her with something special" framing** — I'm not here to buy a gift for someone else; I'm here to shop the sale for myself. This copy pushes me to the side.
- **No urgency specificity in the hero** — "Up to 50% off" is vague. The deals bar says "Today!" but the hero doesn't reinforce any deadline. I've been trained by One Day Sales to look for a hard end time and I'm not seeing one.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for known returning visitors** — If I'm signed in or have a cookie from a prior visit, don't show me the first-time-subscriber offer. Show me my Star Rewards balance or a "Star Pass event this week" banner instead. You know who I am; act like it.
- **Swap the AI tooltip auto-trigger for a passive button only** — Let me choose to open "Ask Macy's+" rather than having it pop a bubble the moment I land. Two things firing at once means both get dismissed.
- **Add a Star Rewards / One Day Sale strip above or below the deals bar** — A simple "Your Star Money: $XX available | Redeem now" ribbon would make me feel seen and keep me from going to the app instead.
- **Tighten the hero CTA to a specific department** — "Shop Women's" or "Shop Fragrance & Beauty" beats "Shop now" every time for someone who has a particular category in mind. Give me a destination, not a direction.
