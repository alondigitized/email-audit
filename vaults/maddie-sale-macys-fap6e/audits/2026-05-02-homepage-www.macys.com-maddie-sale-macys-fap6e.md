# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Oh, I know that Friends & Family sale banner anywhere — this is peak Macy's season and they know it. But honey, I can barely see what's going on because there are TWO pop-ups stacked on top of each other the second I land on the page. The bones are right — that F&F headline is my catnip — but I'm fighting through noise just to get to it.
- **6/10**

## What's working

- **"Friends & Family Sale" hero headline** — this stopped my scroll cold. "3 ways to save" with 30%, 15%, and a Star Money reward column is exactly how I think about stacking deals, and Macy's knows their customer here.
- **"Extra 30% off with code FRIEND"** in the top sticky bar — I saw the code immediately and mentally banked it. That's the right placement.
- **"Ends tomorrow!" urgency line** — I can just barely see it peeking above the modal, and it works. I know I need to move.
- **"Ask Macy's+" AI assistant button** — I'm curious enough to try this for comparing Star Money items; the label is clear.

## What's weak

- **The email signup modal (Step 1 of 2)** is the first thing I have to deal with — it covers the entire screen and I haven't even seen what I came for yet. Two steps to sign up feels like a lot before I've seen a single product.
- **The AI assistant tooltip** popped up on top of the already-cluttered page, making it a double-modal situation. At my phone screen size, I can't see any product or imagery at all.
- **The hero image is completely hidden** behind the overlays — gold accents peeking at the edges suggest something festive, but I genuinely cannot evaluate the visual.
- **The 15% and "$20 in" columns** are cut off — I can't read what "$20 in" applies to without scrolling, and that's the Star Money reward I care most about.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss one modal automatically** — show either the email signup OR the AI tooltip on first visit, never both. Stack the second behind a tap.
- **Show the email modal after 10 seconds or on exit intent**, not on page load — let me see the Friends & Family hero first so I'm already excited when you ask for my email.
- **Make the "3 ways to save" columns fully readable** — all three discount tiers should fit on one mobile screen without requiring a swipe. Condense the text or use a horizontal scroll card.
- **Put the FRIEND code in the email CTA copy** — "Sign up and use code FRIEND for an extra 30% off" would make me fill in that email box in about two seconds flat.
