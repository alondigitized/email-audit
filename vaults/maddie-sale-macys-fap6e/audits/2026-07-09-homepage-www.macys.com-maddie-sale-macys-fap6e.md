# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is embarrassing — and not for me. I opened Macy's on my phone and instead of the homepage, I got a big red blocked shield and the message "You don't have access to this page." That's it. No sale. No hero. No Star Rewards. Just a reference number and a 1-800 number to call. For a brand that built its name on making shoppers feel welcomed, this is a terrible first impression.

## What's working

- **Macy's branding in the header** — the logo ("Celebrations start at ★ macy's"), hamburger menu, and Search bar loaded correctly, so I at least know I'm on the right site.
- **Phone number provided** — "1-800-289-6229" is clickable and visible, so if I really needed help I could call. That's something, I suppose.
- **Customer Service footer** — it's collapsed but present at the bottom, which tells me the page shell loaded even if the content didn't.

## What's weak

- **Hard access block instead of a homepage** — a big red "no entry" shield and "You don't have access to this page" is the entire experience. Absolutely nothing to shop, browse, or engage with.
- **Reference number with no human explanation** — "Reference: 0.871ca17.1783592130.8979805" means nothing to a shopper. It's dev-speak dumped on a customer's screen.
- **No fallback content** — not a single product, promo, or category link. A loyal Star Rewards member who's been shopping here for 30 years lands here and sees nothing.
- **No sign-in hook** — the "Sign In" link in the header is there but completely passive. No prompt explaining that signing in might resolve the access issue.
- **Cart icon shows zero** — the bag in the top right is empty, reinforcing that nothing was accomplished.

## Recommendations

- **Add a "Sign in to continue" nudge directly on the block page** — if this is a logged-in-only restriction, tell me that. A simple "Please sign in to your Star Rewards account to access this page" with a prominent Sign In button would resolve half the confusion immediately.
- **Replace the reference number with plain English** — something like "We weren't able to load the page for your region or connection. Try refreshing or signing in." A 60-year-old customer should not have to decode a hex string.
- **Surface a fallback content strip** — even if the main homepage is restricted, show me today's top sale categories (Women's, Shoes, Fragrance) so the visit isn't completely wasted.
- **Make the 1-800 number more prominent** — right now it's buried in body copy. If calling is the primary recovery path, make it a tap-to-call button.
