# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- Macy's is clearly pushing a Memorial Day Sale hard — that 60% off headline is hard to miss. But first thing I see when the page loads is an AI chatbot bubble covering up part of the hero, which is not a great welcome. For a sale-hunter like me who came here specifically to find deals and stack my Star Money, I'd expect to see some acknowledgment that I'm a loyal customer — instead I just get a generic "Sign In" and a popup I have to close before I can even read the offer.

## What's working

- **"Up to 60% OFF" in the hero** — that number is big, bold, and impossible to miss. As someone who watches for the One Day Sale, this gets my attention immediately.
- **"Memorial Day Sale" campaign label** — it's timely, and I know sales like this are real at Macy's. I trust the event name.
- **"Fresh styles for all your summer plans 25-60% OFF / Shop dresses, shorts, polos & more"** — the orange secondary banner below the hero at least names categories. Dresses are relevant to me.
- **"Today's Deals" top strip** — the persistent deal bar at the very top reminds me there are daily deals to check. That's my language.

## What's weak

- **The "Ask Macy's+" AI modal** — it pops up immediately and blocks the hero text. I had to find the X to close it before I could even read the sale details. Aggravating for a medium-tech 60-year-old.
- **Zero Star Money / Star Pass visibility** — I layer my Star Pass on top of every promo. Not seeing it mentioned anywhere above the fold feels like Macy's forgot who their most loyal customers are.
- **"Shop by category" as the primary CTA** — this is a dropdown, not a destination. It doesn't tell me where to go. Too vague for someone who wants to get straight to women's clothing or handbags.
- **The windswept-hair model image** — that's a 30-year-old woman. I'm 60. I shop here too, Macy's.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — I plan orders around free shipping. Not seeing that number means I have to go dig for it.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the AI modal auto-launch with a subtle corner icon.** Let me choose to open it. Forcing it open on load — especially when it covers the hero — is the fastest way to get a 60-year-old to bounce.
- **Add a "Welcome back, [name] — your Star Money balance: $X" ribbon for signed-in users**, or at minimum a "Sign in to see your Star Money offers" CTA near the hero. That's the hook that brings loyal customers back repeatedly.
- **Change "Shop by category" to a row of 4-5 category chips** (Women's, Shoes, Home, Handbags, Fragrance) so I can get to my section in one tap instead of opening a dropdown.
- **Surface a free-shipping threshold in the top banner strip** alongside the "Up to 60% off" message — something like "Free shipping on orders $25+" would seal the deal for me.
