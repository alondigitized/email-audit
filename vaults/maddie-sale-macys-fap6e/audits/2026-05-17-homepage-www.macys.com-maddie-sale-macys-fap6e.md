# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- Well, they know me — the One Day Sale banner is front and center and that countdown is doing its job. Macy's is running hard on urgency right now and I appreciate that they lead with the sale and not some vague "Explore" nonsense. That said, that AI chat bubble popped up right over my hero and I had to close it before I could even read what the sale was — that kind of thing would make me want to call the store instead.

## What's working

- **"One Day Sale — 40-60% OFF" with countdown (11:00:20):** This is exactly why I check Macy's first thing in the morning. The countdown makes it feel real and urgent without being cheesy.
- **Top ticker strip:** "TODAY'S DEALS — Up to 60% off One Day Sale deals" reinforces the message before I even hit the hero. Good belt-and-suspenders approach.
- **"Celebrations start at" tagline in the header:** Warm and welcoming — feels like the Macy's I grew up with, not some cold discount warehouse.
- **"Shorts, swim & more for daytime BBQ's" second module:** Seasonal hook that ties the sale to a real occasion. That's smart storytelling, even if the model skews younger.
- **"Ask Macy's+" in the search bar:** In concept this is useful — if I want to find the sale section for women's shoes without hunting, I'd try it. It's the AI popup on top of the page I object to, not the search tool itself.

## What's weak

- **AI modal overlays the hero:** The "I'm an AI-powered assistant" bubble sits right on top of the "One Day Sale" headline, cutting it off. First impression is a popup I have to dismiss, not the deal I came for.
- **"Shop by category" CTA is too generic:** I'm a One Day Sale regular — I want "Shop Women's" or "Shop Sale by Department," not a dropdown that makes me do extra work to find the dress section.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass visibility:** This is a loyalty-driven sale and there is zero acknowledgment that I have points, a pass, or any VIP standing. I've earned that — show me something.
- **Younger imagery in the BBQ module:** The two women below the fold are probably 30 years old. Nice lifestyle shot, but I don't see myself there.
- **No free-shipping threshold callout:** With a big sale I'm already calculating whether my cart will hit free shipping. That number should be right here, not hidden in the footer.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-popup AI modal on mobile, or trigger it on second visit only.** Nothing tanks a first impression faster than a modal blocking the hero. Move "Ask Macy's+" to a persistent bottom-right button.
- **Replace "Shop by category" with three or four category pills:** "Women's," "Shoes," "Home," "Men's" — one tap and I'm in my department without a dropdown.
- **Add a thin Star Money/Star Pass loyalty strip between the ticker and the search bar:** Even just "You have 47 Star Money points — use them today!" would make me feel like Macy's knows me.
- **Swap the BBQ lifestyle model for a 55–65-year-old woman, or at minimum a mixed-age group.** I spend real money here; a little representation goes a long way.
