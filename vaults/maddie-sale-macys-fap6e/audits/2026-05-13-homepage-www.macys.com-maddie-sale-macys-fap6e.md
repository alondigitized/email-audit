# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- Macy's is leading with a Flash Sale on jewelry and watches — 50-70% off with a live countdown — and that is squarely in my wheelhouse as a One Day Sale devotee. The Mother's Day "Celebrations start at" framing is timely and the discount is real. But the page practically slaps me across the face with an AI chat popup the second I arrive, and I cannot see my Star Rewards balance or any Star Money hook anywhere above the fold.

## What's working

- **Flash Sale headline + countdown timer ("Ends in 11:00:15")** — that ticking clock is catnip for a One Day Sale regular. I feel the urgency immediately.
- **"50-70% off jewelry & watches" — specific, not vague.** I know exactly what category and roughly what I'm saving. No guessing.
- **Top ticker banner ("Today! Up to 70% off jewelry")** — redundant but useful; it reinforces the deal even before I read the hero.
- **Coach watch product shot** — recognizable brand in the hero gives me a reference point for quality. This isn't cheap fashion jewelry.
- **"Celebrations start at" Mother's Day branding** — feels seasonally current. Shopping for myself or a gift, this makes sense for where I am in May.

## What's weak

- **"Ask Macy's+" AI popup auto-opened on arrival** — I didn't ask for this. It covers the hero, forces me to find and tap the X, and it's the first thing I interact with. That's annoying at any age.
- **No Star Rewards / Star Pass hook above the fold** — I am a Star Money stacker. If I don't see my member pricing or a Star Pass callout immediately, I don't know if this flash sale is better or worse than my usual loyalty math.
- **Hamburger-only navigation (≡)** — at medium tech comfort, I'd rather see Women's, Beauty, Home as tappable tabs. One extra tap to find anything.
- **No people in the imagery** — it's all product shots. A woman my age wearing that necklace would pull me in harder than floating jewelry on a dark background.
- **"Sign In" is there but no welcome-back state** — if Macy's knows I'm a returning customer, show me. If I'm logged in via the app, acknowledge it.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress or delay the AI assistant popup** — show it after 30 seconds or as a persistent corner button. Don't block the hero on cold load.
- **Add a Star Rewards strip above the hero** — one line: "Star Money earned this event: 3X. Your balance: $XX." That keeps loyalists like me from going to Nordstrom Rack instead.
- **Swap the jewelry-floating-in-darkness imagery for a lifestyle shot** — a woman in her late 50s or 60s wearing the necklace at a Mother's Day brunch. That's my mirror, not a product catalog photo.
- **Surface "Free shipping on $25+ for Star Rewards members"** (or whatever the threshold is) somewhere above the fold — that's a conversion nudge I respond to every time.
