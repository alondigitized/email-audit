# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Well, I've been shopping Macy's One Day Sales since before half their marketing team was born, and this is not a great first impression. The Mother's Day Sale is right on time — "Ends Sunday" is exactly the urgency I respond to — but I can barely see the page because two pop-ups are stacked right on top of each other. Macy's is clearly pushing the sale hard, but they're getting in their own way.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day Sale headline** — "Up to 50% OFF" with "Ends Sunday" is exactly the kind of deadline that makes me stop scrolling and start tapping. That's 30 years of One Day Sale conditioning kicking in.
- **Top deal strip** — "TODAY'S DEALS | Up to 50% off gifts for Mom" in the persistent top banner is the one clean signal above all the noise.
- **Email offer with specific discount** — "Get 25% off when you sign up for emails" is a tangible, concrete deal. I already have emails from Macy's, but a first-timer would respond to this.
- **Campaign timing** — "Ends Sunday" with Mother's Day two days out is urgent and honest. That deadline is real.

## What's weak

- **Two overlapping pop-ups** — An AI assistant tooltip sits on top of an email signup modal which sits on top of the hero. I can't see the page I came to shop. This would make me back out faster than a slow elevator.
- **No Star Rewards hook** — I specifically layer Star Pass on top of department promos. There is zero mention of Star Money, Star Pass, or Star Rewards anywhere above the fold. That's the #1 reason a returning Macy's shopper like me opens the app.
- **Hero is almost entirely obscured** — There appears to be a floral/gift image behind everything but I genuinely cannot see it. The one thing a sale event needs — aspirational product imagery — is invisible.
- **"Ask Macy's+" AI prompt** — I'm medium-comfort with tech. An AI assistant I didn't ask for, launching as a tooltip the moment I land, feels like the salesperson who pounces the second you walk in the door.
- **No visible shop CTA for the sale** — "Email my code" is not the same as "Shop the Sale." I came to buy, not to subscribe.

## Recommendations

- **Kill one of the two pop-ups on mobile landing** — Prioritize the email capture or the AI tooltip, not both simultaneously. On a phone screen, stacking overlays on top of a sale event is brand self-sabotage.
- **Add a Star Rewards/Star Pass strip directly under the top deal banner** — One line: "Star Pass members save an extra X% — see your offer." That keeps loyal, high-LTV shoppers engaged before they bounce.
- **Surface the shop CTA as a high-contrast button in the hero** — "Shop Mother's Day Sale" or "Shop 25–50% Off" in a button that's visible even with a subtle overlay. Right now there is no path-to-purchase tap above the fold.
- **Shrink the email modal to a slim bottom drawer on mobile** — A full-screen modal with an email field and a black button is doing too much when the sale event is the real conversion goal.
