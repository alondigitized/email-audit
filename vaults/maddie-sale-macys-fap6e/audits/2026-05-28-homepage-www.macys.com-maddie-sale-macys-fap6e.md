# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-28
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Well, they're leading with the sale — and at my age, that's the right instinct. "25% OFF, Ends Sunday" is speaking my language right away. But that AI chatbot popup floated right on top of the hero and I nearly missed the headline. Macy's feels like it's still trying to be a department store that does everything, and honestly on mobile it's a bit of a jumble to sort through.

## What's working

- **The top banner promo strip** — "Extra 25% off with code SUMMER" is front and center. I clocked it immediately and that alone might keep me from bouncing.
- **"Ends Sunday" deadline** — a real day, not a vague "limited time." That urgency registers with me.
- **"It's a savings-on-savings weekend!"** strip — I know this Macy's mechanic well. Seeing it called out explicitly reminds me to look for the SUMMER label, and I will.
- **"Dress the part for summer RSVP's"** module — I do have a graduation party coming up. That's actually relevant to my life right now.
- **Search bar is prominent** — medium tech comfort means I lean on search; glad it's right at the top.

## What's weak

- **The AI assistant popup ("Ask Macy's+")** literally covered the hero headline. I had to read around it. On mobile that's a hard first impression.
- **Zero Star Money / Star Pass callout** — this is the number one reason I choose Macy's over Kohl's or Nordstrom Rack on a sale weekend. It's buried, apparently off-screen, and that's a real miss.
- **"Shop by category" dropdown** — that's not a CTA, that's a menu. I'm a tap-and-go shopper; give me Women's, Dresses, or Fragrance as a visible tap target.
- **No imagery that looks like me** — the visible lifestyle photo below the fold appears to show younger models. As a 60-year-old shopping for an RSVP outfit, I want to see how this looks on someone my age.
- **No free shipping callout** — I always factor that in. It should be somewhere on screen.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the AI modal on first load** — or at minimum, position it so it doesn't cover the hero offer. I shouldn't need to swipe away a chatbot to see the sale I came for.
- **Add a Star Money / Star Rewards chip near the Sign In button** — even a small "Earn Star Money this weekend" badge would pull returning customers like me straight to the sign-in flow.
- **Replace the "Shop by category" dropdown with 3–4 quick-tap tiles** — Women's, Dresses, Fragrance & Beauty, Home. That's how I actually shop.
- **Swap the hero lifestyle imagery for someone in their 50s–60s dressed for a summer party** — the RSVP angle is strong; the casting should match the customer.
