# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Well, this is not what I expected at all — I didn't get a homepage, I got a big red "no" sign telling me I don't have access. Macy's blocked the page entirely, and what I'm looking at is an access-denied error screen, not a shopping experience. All I can see is the logo, a search bar, a Sign In link, the bag icon, and a wall of text telling me to call 1-800-289-8229. Not exactly an invitation to shop.

## What's working

- The Macy's logo and "Celebrations start at ★ Macy's" tagline are visible and render correctly — at least I know I'm in the right place.
- The search bar is present and prominent, so if I were determined enough I could try searching directly.
- The phone number (1-800-289-8229) is clearly printed — I'd actually call that if I really needed to get in.

## What's weak

- The entire homepage content is blocked — no hero, no sale banners, no category tiles, nothing. For a One Day Sale veteran like me who came here specifically to check today's deals, this is a complete dead end.
- The error message puts the fix on me ("update your browser, refresh the page") — that's not a great look for a brand I've trusted for decades.
- No Star Money reminder, no loyalty balance nudge, no "Sign in to see your rewards" prompt — a huge missed moment since the Sign In link IS sitting right there in the nav.
- The blocked-shield icon is visually alarming. It reads like my account is suspended, not like a routine access hiccup.
- No fallback content whatsoever — even a static "here are today's featured categories" would have kept me on the page.

## Recommendations

- Show a soft-landing fallback page when the CDN/WAF blocks access — at minimum display the hero banner and top sale callouts as static HTML so loyal customers aren't left staring at a shield icon.
- Add a "Sign in to continue" CTA directly on the error screen — I'm already here, just let me authenticate and get through.
- Replace the alarming red shield with a friendlier illustration and warmer copy: "Looks like there was a hiccup — let's get you back to shopping."
- Surface a Star Rewards hook even on error states: "While we sort this out, your Star Money is waiting — sign in here."
