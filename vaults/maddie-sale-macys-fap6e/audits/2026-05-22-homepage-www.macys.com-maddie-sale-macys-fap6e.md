# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Macy's is clearly in One Day Sale mode — "Up to 60% OFF" is the first real thing I see, and as someone who has rearranged her whole Saturday around a One Day Sale, that headline pulls me in fast. The brand feels like a classic department store doing its seasonal sale push, which is exactly what I expect from Macy's in late May. That said, an AI popup is eating the top half of my hero before I even get to shop.

## What's working

- **"1 Day Sale — Up to 60% OFF" headline**: The moment I see those words I'm paying attention. This is Macy's speaking my dialect.
- **Memorial Day anchor in the ticker**: "TODAY'S DEALS — Up to 60% off Memorial Day deals" gives the sale a real deadline context. I know Memorial Day weekend doesn't last forever.
- **"Shop by category" dropdown**: Practical. I don't want a curated editorial — I want to get to Women's quickly, and a category nav respects that.
- **Swim styles banner**: Bright orange, hard to miss, clear sub-copy ("30-50% off select swim styles"). Even if it's not my top priority, I appreciate a specific category promo.
- **Sub-copy on the hero**: "shop summer fashion, home finds & more, 25-60% off" — telling me the range is honest and the scope is wide. Home finds matters to me too.

## What's weak

- **AI popup blocking the hero**: The "Ask Macy's+" tooltip is sitting right on top of the hero text. I can barely read "1 Day Sale" behind it. I'm not here for an AI assistant — I'm here for the sale. First thing I'd do is tap the X, and that's friction before I've seen anything.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass callout above the fold**: As a Star Pass cardholder who layers promos, I want to see my loyalty benefits front and center. If there's bonus Star Money on top of the 60% off, that's my reason to buy today instead of tomorrow. Buried below the fold is a missed conversion.
- **"Ask Macy's+" button feels out of place**: A pink outlined pill button sitting next to the search bar — I thought it was a promo badge at first glance. It competes visually with the search bar without adding clarity.
- **Swim styles imagery skews young**: The lifestyle photo shows a younger woman. I swim too, but the visual doesn't include me.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible**: I want to know if I hit $25 or $75 or whatever before I start browsing. That number changes my cart behavior.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-open AI popup on first page load** — or at minimum, don't render it directly over the hero headline. Let me see the sale before you pitch me a chatbot.
- **Add a Star Money / rewards-balance strip just under the nav** — something like "You have 2,400 Star Money points — redeem today during the sale." That one line would pull every Star cardholder straight into the funnel.
- **Replace the generic "Shop by category" CTA with "Shop the One Day Sale"** — keeps urgency tied to the event and gives me a sale-specific landing page, not a category tree.
- **Show the free-shipping threshold in the top ticker** — rotate it alongside the Memorial Day copy: "Free shipping on orders $25+ — no code needed." That's a trust signal and a cart nudge in one.
