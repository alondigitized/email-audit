# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- Oh, this is my jam — the One Day Sale banner is right there at the top and I spotted the 40-60% off in about two seconds, which is how it should be. But I've got to say, this AI assistant box popped right up and is covering half the hero, so I couldn't even read the full "One Day Sale" headline without hunting for the X. Macy's knows how to run a sale, but someone needs to tame that chatbot.

## What's working

- **One Day Sale hero** — "40-60% OFF … Ends tomorrow" is exactly what I come to Macy's for. Large, red, unmissable.
- **Top ticker bar** — "TODAY'S DEALS | Up to 60% off One Day Sale deals" reinforces the offer before I even hit the hero. I read that before anything else.
- **Seasonal second panel** — "Shorts, swim & more for daytime BBQ's, Up to 60% OFF" is right for May and the discount is stated up front, not buried.
- **Search bar is prominent** — I can go straight to "St. John's Bay linen pants" without digging through menus if I want to.

## What's weak

- **AI assistant popup blocks the hero** — The chatbot modal covers "One Day Sale" so the headline reads "…Day Sale." First impression is a cluttered mess, not a clean sale moment.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass hook** — I layer those promos every single sale. There's a generic "Sign In" link but nothing that says "Sign in to see your Star Pass savings" or "You have $XX in Star Money." That hook would make me tap immediately.
- **"Shop by category" is vague** — A dropdown isn't the same as seeing Women's, Shoes, Home right there. I'd rather have quick-tap category pills than a dropdown I have to open.
- **BBQ lifestyle imagery skews young** — The second-panel photo shows younger people in swimwear and sunglasses. I'm here for the sale, not aspirational beach vibes aimed at people half my age.
- **No free-shipping threshold visible** — At my order values it usually qualifies, but I'd love to see it confirmed so I'm not surprised at checkout.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-launch AI modal on load** — Or delay it 10 seconds and make it a small corner widget. Right now it obscures the hero on first view, which is the worst possible moment to interrupt someone like me who came specifically for the One Day Sale.
- **Swap the category dropdown for tappable category pills** — Women's, Shoes, Home, Beauty — each a single tap. One Day Sale veterans know exactly what they want; don't make us open a dropdown.
- **Add a Star Pass / Star Money banner below the hero ticker** — "Signed in? Your Star Pass saves an extra 15% today." One line. That's the nudge that turns me from a browser into a buyer.
- **Swap the BBQ lifestyle photo for a mixed-age group** — Show a woman my age at the patio table, not just the 28-year-old in the bikini. I'll still buy the shorts — I just want to see myself in the scene.
