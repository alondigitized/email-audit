# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- This is Macy's in full Mother's Day push mode — the sale banner is front and center and the urgency copy ("Ends Sunday") tells me exactly where we are in the week. But honestly, the first thing I actually saw was two overlapping pop-ups: an AI assistant bubble sitting right on top of my hero, and then a full email signup modal on top of *that*. For someone like me who comes straight here to hunt the One Day Sale or stack my Star Money, this much interruption before I can even see a Shop button is frustrating.

## What's working

- **"Up to 50% OFF / 25–50% off. Ends Sunday"** — clean, honest deal language. I know exactly what I'm getting and when it expires. No asterisk gymnastics on the headline.
- **Top deals strip ("TODAY'S DEALS | Up to 50% off gifts for Mom | Under $1...")** — that "Under $1--" teaser at the top makes me want to tap to see the full price ceiling. Good hook.
- **Mother's Day timing is tight** — promoting a "Ends Sunday" sale on Wednesday before the holiday is exactly how a One Day Sale veteran thinks. The urgency is real.
- **"Celebrations start at 53 Macy's" header branding** — feels festive, matches the holiday energy.

## What's weak

- **Double modal stack** — an AI assistant tooltip popped over the hero, and then a full email signup modal launched on top of that. I now have two "X" buttons to close before I can shop. That's enough for me to nearly close the tab.
- **Email signup offer is "First-time subscribers only"** — I'm not a first-timer. This offer is a dead end for loyal customers and it wastes the prime modal real estate.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass mention above the fold** — in May, Star Money is *everything* for Macy's card holders. I'd normally be stacking a 15% off code on top of this 50% sale. That hook is completely missing.
- **No visible Shop CTA button** — the hero text says "Mother's Day Sale" but the actual tap target is buried behind the modal. I can't see where to go.
- **Sign In is small and gray in the top right** — there's no "Sign in to see your Star Rewards" prompt that would speak to me as a cardholder.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the double modal on load** — pick one: either the AI assistant intro *or* the email capture, never both at once. Drop the email modal entirely for identified returning visitors or Macy's cardholders.
- **Add a Star Money / Macy's Card banner strip** just below the top deals bar — something like "Star Money Members earn 3x this weekend" would pull me in immediately.
- **Surface a "Shop the Sale" button in the hero** that's visible without closing anything — even one tappable pink button saying "Shop Mother's Day Deals" would dramatically improve conversion for someone who came here specifically to shop a sale.
- **Swap the email modal headline** to show a loyalty-specific offer ("Cardholders: unlock an extra 15% off") rather than a first-timer deal — it turns a dead end into a relevant upsell for people like me.
