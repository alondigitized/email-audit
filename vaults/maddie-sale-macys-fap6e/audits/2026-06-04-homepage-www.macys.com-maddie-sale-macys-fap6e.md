# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-06-04
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-04**
## Executive summary

- This is Macy's doing what Macy's does — big summer sale banners, lots of percentage-off copy, the familiar red and white layout. Right now they're pushing a Summer Favorites Sale with up to 50% off, which is my language. But the first thing that greets me isn't a great deal — it's an AI chatbot popup blocking half the hero, which is not the welcome mat I was hoping for.

## What's working

- **"Up to 50% OFF" hero headline** — that number gets my attention immediately. That's One Day Sale energy and I respond to it.
- **"Shop your favorite summer styles like linen, dresses & more"** — linen and dresses are exactly what I'm looking for this time of year; the category specificity helps.
- **"Shirts, dresses & more you'll reach for all summer 30-40% OFF"** — the secondary banner reinforces the discount message. Two sale callouts above the fold is good.
- **"TODAY'S DEALS" top strip** — that persistent deal banner at the very top is the right hook for a return shopper like me. I look for that strip first.
- **"Shop by category" dropdown** — a clean, accessible way to navigate without overwhelming the mobile layout.

## What's weak

- **AI chatbot popup ("Ask Macy's+")** — it fires immediately and obscures the hero copy. The headline behind it reads "[S]...avorites Sale" — I literally cannot read what the sale is called. For a medium-tech-comfort shopper, this bot is in my way, not helping me.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass callout** — I layer Star Pass on top of every department promo. There is zero mention of it above the fold. This is a missed layering opportunity for loyal customers like me.
- **Young models in secondary banner** — the two teens-looking people in the "Shirts, dresses" card don't speak to my body type, style, or age. I need to see someone closer to 55-65 in a linen dress to know this is for me.
- **"Under $[cut off]" in the top strip** — the price threshold is truncated by the viewport. That's a basic mobile layout issue — that number should never get clipped.
- **No urgency / time-bound copy** — no "ends tonight," no countdown timer, nothing that makes me act now rather than close the tab and come back later (and forget).

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the AI popup** — don't fire it on page load. Let it live as the "Ask Macy's+" button (it's already there). Opening with a bot modal in front of the sale hero is backwards.
- **Add a Star Money / Star Pass banner strip** — even a one-liner: "Earn Star Money on every purchase — members save more." I should not have to dig for this.
- **Swap the secondary banner model** — put a woman 55+ in a linen dress or a relaxed summer blouse. One image does the work of a thousand targeted words.
- **Fix the mobile top-strip clipping** — ensure the full "Under $XX" price-point is visible. If it says "Under $50" that's a strong hook — don't let the viewport eat it.
