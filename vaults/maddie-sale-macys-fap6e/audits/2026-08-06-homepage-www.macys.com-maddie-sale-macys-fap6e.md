# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-08-06
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- Well, this doesn't feel like it's talking to me at all. I open Macy's expecting to see a One Day Sale banner or my Star Rewards balance, and instead I'm looking at a denim backdrop full of teenagers and tweens headed back to school. It's a perfectly nice campaign — just not mine.

## What's working

- **The yellow "Back to School Starts Here" tag** pops right off that denim background — good contrast, easy to read even on my phone.
- **The "Shop back to school" button** is a plain white pill with black text sitting right on the image — I know exactly where to tap.
- **The search bar** near the top is big and obvious, which I appreciate since I usually just type in what I'm after rather than digging through menus.
- **"App only! 50% off beauty deals"** in that top ticker at least caught my eye — beauty and fragrance is where I actually spend money in store.

## What's weak

- **The whole hero carousel is kids and teenagers** — cute, but I'm shopping for myself, not outfitting a middle schooler.
- **No sign of my Star Rewards or Star Pass anywhere** above the fold — I always check my rewards balance first thing, and there's nothing here for that.
- **"Sign In" is just a plain text link**, easy to skim right past — no member greeting, no "Welcome back" that would make me feel like they know I'm a regular.
- **"Score 25-50% OFF"** — that word "Score" reads a little young for me, like it's written for the same crowd in the photo.

## Recommendations

- Rotate a second carousel slide into that same above-the-fold space showing a woman my age with a One Day Sale or Friends & Family callout — I know Macy's runs these, so surface one immediately.
- Add my Star Rewards points balance or a "Welcome back" strip right under the search bar so returning shoppers feel recognized before they even scroll.
- Turn that "50% off beauty deals" ticker into its own tappable banner instead of burying it in the small scrolling ticker up top — fragrance shoppers like me would tap that fast.
- Swap "Score" for something a little more classic, like "Save 25-50%" — same offer, less teen slang.
