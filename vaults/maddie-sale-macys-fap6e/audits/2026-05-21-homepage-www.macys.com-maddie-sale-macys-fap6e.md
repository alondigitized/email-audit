# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Oh, they've got a One Day Sale going — that headline almost always gets me to stop scrolling. Macy's is pushing Memorial Day deals hard with an "up to 60% off" hook, which is exactly the kind of number that pulls me in. But an AI chat bubble popped right over the hero before I even got my bearings, and that rubbed me the wrong way before I'd seen half the page.

## What's working

- **"1 Day Sale / Up to 60% OFF" hero headline** — That language is my Pavlov bell. I have been trained by Macy's for 20 years to respond to exactly those words, and it works.
- **"25–60% off" qualifier in subtext** — Appreciated. Spelling out the range instead of hiding behind "up to" feels straightforward, not bait-and-switch.
- **Memorial Day timing** — The top bar ties the offer to a real event, which gives the sale a reason to exist and makes the deadline feel credible.
- **"Ask Macy's+" button** — Honestly, I might actually try that if I'm looking for something specific. It's less intimidating than filtering through a big category tree.

## What's weak

- **AI chat popup auto-firing** — It landed right on top of the hero before I could read the deal. That is an instant close-the-X reaction from me. Don't pop things in my face the second I arrive.
- **"Shop by category" CTA** — A dropdown? On mobile? I want a button that says something like "Shop the Sale" and takes me directly there, not a menu I have to wrestle with on a small screen.
- **Swim module imagery** — Young woman, long flowing hair, palm tree. That is not me at 60. I might buy a bathing suit, but that photo is not speaking to my life or my body.
- **No Star Money / Star Pass callout** — I layer Star Pass on top of every sale I shop. Not seeing it mentioned at all above the fold makes me wonder if my pass is going to stack, and I won't convert until I know that.
- **No free-shipping threshold anywhere visible** — I always check this. It helps me decide whether to add one more item. Not seeing it is a small but real friction point.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the auto-open AI chat popup** — let me land on the hero first, then offer the chat as a persistent button. A popup that covers the sale headline is working against the sale.
- **Replace "Shop by category" with a direct button**: something like "Shop Memorial Day Sale" that goes straight to the filtered sale results page, not a dropdown.
- **Add a Star Pass/Star Money stacking badge** to the hero or the promo strip — even a line like "Stackable with Star Pass" would push me from browsing to buying immediately.
- **Swap the swim lifestyle shot** for something that shows a wider age range, or at least style-forward women who are not 25 — Macy's customer base skews older and we buy swimwear too.
