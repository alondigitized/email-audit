# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Well, it's Macy's alright — I'd know that red star anywhere. They're pushing a big indoor-outdoor home refresh sale right now, up to 60% off furniture and mattresses. What catches my eye first, though, is that a chatbot bubble popped up and is sitting right on top of the hero like an uninvited guest at a dinner party — I'd tap that X before I could even read what the sale was about.

## What's working

- **Star Money callout in the hero body** — "$50 in Star Money for every $500 spent" is exactly the kind of math I do in my head before I add anything to my cart. They put it where I'll see it.
- **"Ends 6/1" deadline** — Hard date, not "limited time." I appreciate that. I'll actually mark my calendar.
- **"App only! Extra 20% off designer clearance" top banner** — That's a sale stacker. I see that and I'm already thinking about downloading or opening the app.
- **What appears to be a countdown strip** on the right edge — even half-cut-off, urgency numbers make me feel like there's something to act on.
- **Lifestyle hero image** — The room is warm and aspirational without being snooty. I could see my living room looking like that.

## What's weak

- **AI chatbot tooltip auto-popped over the hero** — I came to shop, not to meet a robot. This is the first thing I see and it's blocking my view of the sale. I'd close it, but it's an annoying extra tap.
- **"Ask Macy's+" button circled in red** — Someone in marketing is clearly excited about this. I am not. Medium-tech-comfort me reads that red circle as "push this button you don't want to push."
- **No fashion or fragrance anywhere above the fold** — Those are the two reasons I go to Macy's. A furniture sale is fine, but I'd have to scroll or search to find what I actually came for.
- **"Shop by category" CTA is vague** — That dropdown could lead me anywhere. "Shop the Furniture Sale" or "Browse Mattresses" would give me more confidence about where I'm landing.
- **No visible Sign In state** — I'm a Star Rewards member. I want to see my balance or at least a "Hi, Alon" moment. Instead I get a generic "Sign In" link.

## Recommendations

- **Kill the auto-open chatbot tooltip on mobile** — or at minimum delay it 8 seconds so I can see the hero first. A persistent icon in the corner is fine; a popup that covers the sale headline is not.
- **Add a thin personalization strip just below the search bar**: "Welcome back — your Star Money balance: $X" or "You have Star Rewards to use." That one line would make me feel like Macy's knows me.
- **Swap "Shop by category" for a sale-specific CTA**: "Shop the Furniture Sale" or even two side-by-side buttons — "Furniture & Mattresses" and "All Departments" — so I can get somewhere in one tap.
- **Add a second hero slide visible on load** that covers Women's or Fragrance — a quick swipe dot indicator showing me there's more would invite me to keep scrolling rather than thinking this is a home-goods-only event.
