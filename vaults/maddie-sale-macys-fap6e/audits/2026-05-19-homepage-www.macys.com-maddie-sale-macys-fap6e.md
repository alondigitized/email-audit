# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Well, they got my attention — "1 Day Sale" and "Up to 60% Off" are the magic words I've been trained to respond to over thirty-plus years of hitting the Macy's One Day Sale. But before I could even read the full headline, some AI chatbot popped up right in my face and blocked half the hero. Classic Macy's: the sale bones are solid, but the execution trips over itself. And I'm noticing zero mention of Star Money or Star Pass anywhere on this screen, which is the whole reason I choose Macy's over TJX.

## What's working

- **"1 Day Sale / Up to 60% OFF" headline** — This is exactly the language that gets Boomer deal-hunters to stop scrolling. I know this event, I trust it, and 60% is a real number.
- **Memorial Day framing** ("unofficial summer kick-off") — Seasonal relevance is immediate. This isn't stale inventory; it feels like a real event I should be attending.
- **"Shop by category" dropdown** — A dropdown beats a wall of category tiles on a small phone screen for someone like me who doesn't want to hunt.
- **Swim banner with actual offer** ("30-50% off select swim styles") — The secondary module shows a specific discount range, which is more useful than "save big."
- **Top deal strip** — "TODAY'S DEALS — Up to 60% off Memorial Sale deals" gives me a persistent orientation that the whole site is in sale mode right now.

## What's weak

- **AI chatbot popup covering the hero** — I did not ask for "Ask Macy's+" and it is sitting right on top of the sale headline I came for. Medium-tech-comfort shopper here: I see a popup, my first instinct is "close it before I accidentally tap something." That's friction I should not have to clear before reading the page.
- **Zero Star Money / Star Pass visibility** — This is the core reason I am loyal to Macy's over any other department store. If I can't see my rewards balance or a Star Pass stacking message on the sale page, I feel like a stranger in my own store.
- **"Celebrations start at 53" header** — I genuinely cannot tell if this is a price ("starts at $53?"), an anniversary ("53rd year?"), or a birthday event. Confusing headline for a banner that should be orienting me.
- **Swim imagery skews young** — The woman in the second module is young and glamorous. That is not my swim-styles energy at 60. I'd want to see something that says "this cut works on real bodies."
- **No free-shipping callout** — I know Macy's has a threshold, but it is not on this screen. For an online sale, that is table stakes and I have to go looking for it.

## Recommendations

- **Kill or delay the "Ask Macy's+" popup** — Show it only after a search, not as the first thing on the homepage. Or push it to a floating icon so it never covers a paying hero banner.
- **Add a Star Money/Star Pass stacking strip** — One small banner: "Stack your Star Pass with Memorial Sale prices — log in to apply." That single line would light up every loyalty member who lands here.
- **Rewrite the "Celebrations start at 53" header** — If it means "$53 and up," say "Sale styles starting at $53." If it is an anniversary, say "53 Years of One Day Sales — Shop Now." I should not have to guess.
- **Swap the swim hero image** for a lifestyle shot that includes a range of body types and ages — even one image with someone in their late 50s would signal "this sale is for me too."
