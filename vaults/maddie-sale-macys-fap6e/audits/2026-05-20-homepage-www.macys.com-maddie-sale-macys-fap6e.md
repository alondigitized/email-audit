# Homepage snapshot · www.macys.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a Macy's I recognize — Memorial Day Sale front and center, big discount numbers, the red-and-white I've been shopping for decades. But the second I landed on the page, an AI chat box popped up automatically and covered half my hero headline, and the only CTA I see is a generic "Shop by category" dropdown — no quick link to Women's or Home, no Star Rewards balance, nothing that says "welcome back, here's what's on sale in your departments." For a sale veteran like me, this homepage delivered the deal but forgot I exist as a loyal customer.

## What's working

- **"Up to 60% OFF" hero text** — I can see it from across the room, and Memorial Day is exactly when I expect Macy's to show up. This is why I bookmarked this site.
- **Top banner strip** — "Today's Deals / Up to 60% off Memorial Day deals" is solid reassurance the moment the page loads. I know I'm in the right place.
- **"25-60% off" range disclosed in subtext** — I appreciate that the body copy clarifies the full range. Saves me from clicking through expecting 60% and finding 25%.
- **Swim section's specific offer** — "30-50% off select swim styles" is a category-linked number, which is how I like to shop — department by department with a real discount attached.
- **"Celebrations start at Macy's" anniversary flag** — I like a milestone moment. Feels like an occasion worth shopping.

## What's weak

- **The AI popup** — It launched automatically and physically covered my Memorial Day Sale headline. Medium tech comfort means I can close it, but I shouldn't have to before I've even read the page. I didn't come here to chat, I came to shop the sale.
- **"Shop by category" CTA** — This is a dropdown, not a direct link. I have to tap it, wait for a list, then find my department. That's two interactions when it should be one. For a sale event, I want "Shop Women's" and "Shop Home" on the hero itself.
- **No Star Rewards / Star Pass mention anywhere** — I am a Star Money stacker. If there's accelerated earning or a Star Pass event for Memorial Day weekend, I need to see that before I do anything else. This is the single biggest miss for someone like me.
- **Swim model demographic** — The young woman in the swim section is not my shopper. I'm not in the market for a swimsuit, and even if I were, this imagery does nothing for me.
- **No free-shipping callout** — I budget my cart around that threshold every time. It's not on the screen anywhere.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Star Rewards strip directly below the hero** — One line: "Earn 3X Star Money this Memorial Day weekend" or "Star Pass holders: extra 30% off your purchase." I should see that before I scroll.
- **Replace "Shop by category" with two or three direct department pill buttons** — "Shop Women's," "Shop Home," "Shop Handbags" — side by side on the hero. A dropdown hides choices behind an extra tap and slows me down.
- **Suppress the AI popup on first load** — "Ask Macy's+" is already a button in the nav bar; let me invoke it by choice. Auto-launching it over my hero is like having a sales associate jump in front of me at the door before I can read the sign.
- **Add the free-shipping threshold to the banner strip** — "Free shipping on $25+" (or whatever the current floor is) right alongside the Memorial Day offer. I use that number to decide how much to add to my cart.
