- **Dismiss X on the email modal** — at least there's an obvious way out; I don't feel trapped

## What's weak

- **Email signup modal firing immediately** — I'm a returning Macy's shopper who already gets their emails; this modal is wasted on me and blocks the entire page
- **"First-time subscribers only" fine print** — if I squinted and almost entered my email, I'd be annoyed when I read that; this feels like bait

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for returning visitors** (or anyone with a cookie) — I'm already in your system; show me my rewards balance instead of a first-timer offer
- **Move the Star Money / Star Pass hook into the hero or a persistent banner** — "Your Star Money: $XX — Stack it with today's deals" is a one-liner that would get me tapping immediately
- **Delay the AI chatbot tooltip by at least 5 seconds** — letting me see the page first before a balloon pops up would make it feel helpful, not intrusive
- **Make the "Order by tomorrow" deadline bigger and paired with a category button** — something like "Shop Mother's Day Gifts → Jewelry, Fragrance, Handbags" below that copy so I know exactly where to go
