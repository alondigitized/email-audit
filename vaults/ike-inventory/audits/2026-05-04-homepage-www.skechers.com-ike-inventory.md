## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner** — top of viewport, high contrast blue background, impossible to miss. If that deal applies to kids' shoes, it's a real incentive even for an auditor noting inventory turnover potential.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA** — large, full-width button, direct copy. No ambiguity about what it does.
- **Secondary promo strip** — "25% OFF Apparel" with a "Shop | details" split link gives a clear offer hierarchy. Two deals communicated in under two lines.
- **Campaign timing** — Mother's Day hero in early May is correct. No stale imagery, no off-season messaging.
- **Page render** — clean layout, no visible breakage, images load correctly.

## What's weak

- **Zero boys' category signal** — I came here to audit kids' footwear. Nothing in this viewport tells me I can get there in one tap. The nav is collapsed behind a hamburger menu with no quick-access category links.
- **Hero is entirely female-targeted** — mom + young girl in pink shoes, pink floral border, script font. As the only male in this interaction, I am invisible.
- **No boys' shoes or kids' footwear imagery** — even the girl in the hero is wearing adult-adjacent lifestyle product, not the kids' SKUs I'm here to count.
- **No loyalty or account hook** — for a returning auditor or repeat shopper, there's nothing surfaced about rewards balance, member pricing, or recognized status.
- **No trust signals** — no review counts, no "free returns," no comfort-guarantee badge. For a first-time visitor or a skeptical shopper, the page asks for a tap before building any credibility.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent category quick-nav strip** — a horizontal scroll row of category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Sale) below the hero would let me reach boys' shoes in one tap without opening the hamburger menu.
- **Run a second hero slot for Kids'** — even a small secondary editorial card below the Mother's Day hero showing a boys' shoe lifestyle shot would signal the category exists above the fold on mobile.
- **Surface the BOGO exclusions immediately** — the top banner says BOGO 50% with a "details" link in small type. If kids' shoes are excluded, auditors and parents alike will bounce when they find out three taps deep. Put the category scope in the banner.
- **Add one trust signal near the CTA** — a single line ("Free returns · 30 days") placed just above or below the SHOP NOW button adds friction reduction at no cost to design.
