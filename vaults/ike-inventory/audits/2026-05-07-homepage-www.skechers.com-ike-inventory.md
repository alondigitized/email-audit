# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-07
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-07**
## Executive summary

- I'm a boys' shoe auditor. This homepage is a Mother's Day floral arrangement. The hero is a mom kissing her daughter, pink blooms everywhere, script font in rose — zero relevance to my beat. Brand reads as a mainstream family-footwear retailer pushing a gifting moment hard, and the two-banner promo stack (BOGO + apparel sale) tells me they're running simultaneous promotions that aren't cleanly prioritized.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner:** Top-of-page, high-contrast blue, instantly readable — I clocked the offer in under a second, and "Applied at Cart" removes friction about how to redeem it.
- **Dual-promo layering:** The apparel sale strip stacks a second offer without hiding the first — both are visible simultaneously, which is efficient real-estate use.
- **SHOP NOW button:** Full-width, black on white, clear verb — no ambiguity about what happens when you tap it.
- **Campaign timeliness:** Mother's Day mid-May is on-calendar. The creative is deployed at the right moment.

## What's weak

- **Hero is 100% female/gifting coded:** Pink floral wallpaper, mom-and-daughter pose, script font — a 36-year-old male auditor looking for boys' size coverage data gets nothing from this scene.
- **Two competing banners above the hero:** BOGO on footwear AND 25% off apparel, stacked, creates a "which one is the real deal" moment. Neither one anchors the page's intent.
- **No path to boys' category from the hero:** "SHOP NOW" routes to Mother's Day, not to any navigable department. One tap doesn't get me anywhere useful.
- **No trust signals:** No return policy callout, no "free shipping over $X," no review counts — nothing that lowers purchase risk at first glance.
- **MENU hamburger only:** My category is buried behind a navigation tap, not surfaced as a visible link above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Add a top-nav category strip below the logo:** One horizontal scroll row — Kids, Women, Men, Apparel — so any shopper can self-route in one tap without opening the hamburger.
- **Anchor the BOGO to a category:** "BOGO 50% OFF — Shop Kids' Shoes" would make the promotion actionable for me immediately instead of dumping me into a gifting funnel.
- **Surface free-shipping threshold in the BOGO banner:** Replace "details" link with "BOGO 50% OFF + free shipping over $75" — more information, same line.
- **Swap the sub-banner copy hierarchy:** Lead with footwear offer, push apparel sale to secondary — right now it reads like two brands sharing a slot.
