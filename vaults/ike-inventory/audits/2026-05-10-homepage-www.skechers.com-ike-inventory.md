# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- Skechers opened Mother's Day hard — full floral hero, mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot, script lettering, the works. It's a gifting push, not a product push, and the entire viewport is dedicated to it. As a boys' category auditor, I'm looking at a page built for someone else's wallet entirely — my beat doesn't even register in this first screen.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner:** Top-of-page, high-contrast blue, applied at cart — no code needed. That's a legitimate hook even if the campaign isn't mine.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA:** Full-width, solid black button, impossible to miss. No ambiguity about what the next action is.
- **Layered promo strip:** Stacking the apparel sale below the BOGO banner communicates active promotional depth without cluttering the hero.
- **Clean render:** No layout breakage, no placeholder copy, no overlapping elements. The page loads visually complete.
- **Seasonally correct:** Mother's Day on May 10 — the campaign timing is tight and accurate.

## What's weak

- **Hero is a category dead-end for me:** "The Mother's Day Shop" takes me to women's gifting, not the boys' aisle. One tap wasted.
- **No boys' category entry point above the fold:** The hamburger "MENU" is my only path to boys' shoes without scrolling. That's an extra step I shouldn't have to take.
- **Apparel promo is irrelevant noise:** 25% OFF Apparel does nothing for a footwear auditor; it competes for attention against the BOGO without adding value.
- **No size or inventory signal:** As someone who tracks size coverage, I get zero indication of what's in stock or new in boys' from this screen.
- **"Recently Viewed" barely visible:** It's cut off at the bottom. If it's a personalization feature, it needs to be in the viewport, not a footnote.

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent quick-nav strip below the hero:** Four tiles — Women / Men / Kids / Sale — so anyone not here for Mother's Day can route themselves in one tap without opening the hamburger.
- **Swap or split the hero carousel:** Even a second slide showing a boys' new-arrival or back-to-school teaser would give category auditors a reason to stay. A single static hero on a campaign day is a missed opportunity.
- **Surface the BOGO scope explicitly:** "BOGO 50% OFF sitewide" vs. "select styles" changes my audit calculus immediately. Make it say which.
- **Promote "Recently Viewed" to a visible shelf:** Move it out of the cut-off zone so returning users with a specific audit trail can pick up where they left off without scrolling.
