# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- The page is running a full-court press on Slip-ins Glide-Step — adult product, adult models, lifestyle messaging. Two banners (BOGO 50% at top, 25% off apparel below) hit you before the hero loads. This is a promo-heavy, adult-footwear-first homepage. Kids' category is an afterthought: one tab label at the bottom of the fold, no visual, no product, no offer.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% banner (top):** It's the first thing I read. Blue bar, high contrast, no clutter. Applied at cart means I don't have to chase a promo code.
- **25% OFF Apparel strip:** Stacked offer gives the page a promotional depth that signals "something is happening here today."
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA:** Full-width white button, large type, below the hero. No ambiguity about what to tap.
- **Category tab row (Women / Men / Kids' / Recently Viewed):** The Kids' tab is right there at the bottom of the visible viewport. One tap to my category. That's functional.
- **Clean layout:** No overlapping elements, no broken images, no placeholder text. The page just works.

## What's weak

- **Hero is adult footwear, full stop:** Slip-ins Glide-Step hero with a 30-something man and woman in poolside lifestyle clothes has zero signal for boys' shoes. I'm an auditor — I'm here to check kids' inventory, and the hero tells me this brand thinks I don't exist.
- **No kids' product visible anywhere above the fold:** Not in the hero, not in a secondary module, not in a callout strip. I see one word — "Kids'" — in a tab label. That's it.
- **BOGO scope is ambiguous:** "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart details" — does it cover kids'? Apparel? All footwear? The word "details" is a link but I have to trust it to find out.
- **No trust signals:** No review counts, no star ratings, no badge (e.g., "Free returns," "Verified reviews"). The hero is pure lifestyle photography with zero social proof.
- **Recently Viewed tab is hollow context:** It's there but it signals nothing unless I've been on the site before. For a cold visit it reads as wasted nav real estate.

## Recommendations

- **Drop a Kids' hero module directly below the Slip-ins hero** — even a single product shot of a boys' style with "Shop Kids' Shoes" as the CTA would catch my eye and justify the tap.
- **Clarify BOGO scope in the banner:** Change "BOGO 50% OFF Applied at Cart details" to "BOGO 50% OFF Sitewide — Kids' included" (if true) or explicitly state exclusions. Ambiguity is friction.
- **Add a free-shipping threshold to the banner strip:** The apparel sale strip has room. Something like "Free shipping on $50+" is a one-line addition that removes a conversion blocker.
- **Swap the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA for something category-linked:** If the Kids' tab is visible at the bottom of the fold, a secondary CTA like "Shop Kids'" at that row level would give me a direct path without requiring me to parse nav tabs.
