# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up skechers.com and got a full Mother's Day campaign — pink florals, mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot, script headline. The brand is running a seasonal gifting push timed to the holiday weekend. From an audit standpoint this is a competent consumer homepage, but it has nothing to say to someone who walked in to count boys' inventory.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top bar** — it's the first thing I see. Blue bar, bold text, "Applied at Cart" — no mystery about how to claim it. That applies site-wide, so it would extend to boys' product if I navigated there.
- **25% OFF Apparel secondary strip** — two distinct active offers stacked above the hero. That's a clean promo hierarchy without clutter.
- **"SHOP NOW" button** — full-width, black on white, no ambiguity. One tap and I'm going somewhere.
- **Campaign timing** — Mother's Day on May 10, homepage deploys Mother's Day shop on May 8. Execution is on schedule.
- **Render quality** — clean mobile viewport, no layout shift artifacts, images loaded fully.

## What's weak

- **Zero pathway to boys' shoes** — the entire above-the-fold experience is locked into a Mother's Day gift funnel. My category doesn't exist on this screen.
- **"SHOP NOW" is a dead end for my use case** — tapping it routes me into the Mother's Day assortment, not to a category page I can audit.
- **No loyalty/rewards callout** — the program exists; it's not surfaced here. Missed retention signal.
- **No trust signals** — no review count, no free-returns badge, no rating stars. The page trades purely on brand name and promotional offer.
- **Navigation requires a tap to open** — the hamburger MENU is there but boys' category is one hidden layer away. For a returning user who knows what they want, that's friction.

## Recommendations

- **Add a category quick-link strip below the hero** — four or five tappable chips (Men, Women, Kids, Boys, Sale) so returning shoppers and auditors can branch immediately without opening the full nav drawer.
- **Surface the free-shipping threshold** — if there is one, put it in the BOGO top bar or the secondary strip. "BOGO 50% + Free shipping over $X" in a single bar converts better than two separate banners.
- **Add a countdown to the Mother's Day deadline** — "Ends Sunday" or a 48-hour timer. The timing is right; use it. Right now there's no urgency signal despite the holiday being 48 hours out.
- **Rotate a secondary hero module** — one slot for Mother's Day gifting, one slot for a non-gifting category (Kids New Arrivals, Back to School preview) so non-gift shoppers have a branch point.
