# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Full Mother's Day push — Skechers committed the entire above-the-fold to a mom-and-daughter lifestyle shot, pink floral art direction, and script headline. The offer stack is double-stacked (BOGO in the top bar, Spring Apparel Sale in the secondary strip), which is the only intel up here that has any operational relevance to me. Boys' shoes: zero signals.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner**: persistent blue bar, first thing I see, activation mechanic is spelled out ("Applied at Cart")
- **Double-stacked offers**: BOGO for footwear + apparel-sale strip signals a real promotion window — I can cross-reference this against SKU counts
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA**: full-width, black-on-white, no hedging — the path to transact is obvious for the intended audience
- **Seasonal timing**: May 6 launch for a May 12 holiday is disciplined; not early, not scrambled
- **Clean mobile render**: no layout shift, no broken assets in the viewport

## What's weak

- **Hero is entirely off my beat**: a mom and a girl in a pink flower garden tells me nothing about boys' shoe inventory — I have to navigate past this to do any work
- **No boys' category shortcut**: the only visible navigation is a hamburger MENU icon; there is no persistent "Kids" or "Boys" quick-link on the home screen
- **No loyalty hook above the fold**: no rewards balance, no "Member Price" badge, no points reminder for a repeat-visit auditor who hits this site daily
- **Spring Apparel Sale strip**: 25% off apparel is irrelevant noise for a footwear audit; it takes up a row that could be a category pill
- **No trust signals in viewport**: no review counts, no free-returns badge, nothing — the page gives me no reason to trust checkout before I get there

## Recommendations

- **Add a persistent Kids > Boys category pill** in the mobile nav bar so repeat visitors who are not here for Mother's Day gifting can jump directly without going through the hamburger menu
- **Surface a "Boys' New Arrivals" or "Kids' BOGO Picks" module** immediately below the hero to capture non-gifting traffic while the holiday push runs above
- **Show the free-shipping threshold** in the promo banner row — a line like "Free shipping over $50" next to the BOGO would reduce checkout hesitation with one additional character block
- **Surface rewards balance for logged-in users** near the nav icons — even "You have 250 points" converts repeat visitors faster than a cold BOGO pitch
