# Homepage snapshot · www.skechers.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Got the screenshot. Writing the review now.
- This is a clean, well-produced mobile homepage that clearly knows what it's pushing right now: Hands Free Slip-ins Glide-Step, aimed squarely at adult lifestyle consumers. As a retail auditor here to walk the boys' category, I'm immediately off-trail — the above-the-fold real estate is committed to adult models at what looks like a pool party. I can see the Kids' tab in the bottom nav strip, which at least tells me where to go next, but nothing on this screen is speaking to my beat.

## What's working

- **BOGO 50% OFF top banner** — it's the first thing I read, it's blue and high-contrast, and it applies at cart with no hoops. That's real money on a bulk audit run.
- **"Recently Viewed" tab** — small but meaningful signal that the site tracks return visitors and surfaces continuity. Useful for my repeat daily walks.
- **Clean visual hierarchy** — blue promo strip → hero → full-width CTA → category tabs. The eye never gets lost. Mobile layout is executing correctly.
- **"Kids'" tab in the bottom nav** — one tap to my territory. Short path matters on mobile.
- **Spring Apparel callout strip** — the 25% OFF Apparel is seasonal and specific. If I'm also auditing apparel adjacencies, that's a usable data point.

## What's weak

- **Hero is irrelevant to my category** — adult male and female models in poolside lifestyle poses, promoting Slip-ins Glide-Step. No boys. No kids. No hint that a children's section exists until you see the nav tab.
- **"SHOP NOW" CTA is generic** — it connects to the adult Slip-ins hero, not to any kids' category. A kids' auditor has to detour around the CTA entirely.
- **No trust or credibility signals above the fold** — no review counts, no rating stars, no "free returns" or badge. The page leads entirely on brand aesthetic.
- **No urgency beyond the standing BOGO** — no countdown, no "limited sizes," no "ends Sunday." The offer is live but feels evergreen, not urgent.
- **25% OFF is apparel, not footwear** — my beat is boys' shoes, not shirts. This promo strip is noise for me.

## Recommendations

- **Add a Kids' hero slot or swap the bottom nav tabs to the top** — if a measurable share of mobile sessions are Kids'-bound, a dedicated hero rotation for that segment (or at least a sticky shortcut above the fold) would reduce drop-off before the first scroll.
- **Rename the generic "SHOP NOW" CTA to match the hero product** — "Shop Slip-ins" or "Shop Glide-Step" is one edit that immediately tells me what the button lands on. Right now I have to guess.
- **Surface a trust micro-strip** — a single line ("Free shipping on $50+ · Free returns · 4.5★ 2M+ reviews") between the hero and the CTA costs zero design space and adds real credibility signal on mobile.
- **Pin a shipping threshold to the BOGO banner** — "BOGO 50% OFF + free shipping over $X" in the same blue strip turns one promo into two conversion levers without adding visual weight.
