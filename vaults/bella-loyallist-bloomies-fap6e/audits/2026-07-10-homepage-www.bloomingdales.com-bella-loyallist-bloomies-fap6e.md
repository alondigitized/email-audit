# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-07-10
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- I open the Bloomingdales app, and the very first thing I get is a full-screen email capture interstitial offering me 15% off if I hand over my email address — which I did, like, five years ago when I signed up for Loyallist. This isn't a homepage experience; it's a lead-gen wall. I can barely see what's happening behind it, just a sliver of "GAME DAY WITH BOSS" peeking out from underneath. As a top-tier member who plans my shopping calendar around the Friends & Family event, I expect Bloomingdales to *know* me on arrival — not treat me like a first-time stranger.

## What's working

- **Clean visual hierarchy in the modal itself** — the 15% OFF headline, sub-copy, and CTA stack reads quickly; no clutter inside the interstitial
- **Sign In visible in the top nav** — at least the escape hatch exists if I know to look for it; I can dismiss and try to get back to the actual site
- **"GAME DAY WITH BOSS" glimpse at the bottom** — suggests there *is* a timely, editorial campaign running, and the brand name drop (Boss) signals designer-level positioning that is on-target for me

## What's weak

- **Forced full-screen email modal as the first experience** — as a loyal, top-tier Loyallist member who has been subscribed for years, being greeted with a new-subscriber pitch is genuinely insulting; this is a dark pattern for returning customers
- **15% offer exclusively for new email subscribers** — the "Exclusions apply" and "new email subscribers only" fine print means the boldly-displayed headline offer is actively *not* for me; that's a bait-and-switch for anyone already in the database
- **Zero Loyallist acknowledgment** — no points balance, no tier recognition, no upcoming event callout (I know Double Points Day is coming, but the site acts like I don't exist)
- **No product imagery above the fold** — there is literally nothing to shop; no category pull, no designer imagery, no lifestyle shot that signals where I should go next
- **Campaign content (GAME DAY) is completely buried** — the brand put real effort into what looks like a Boss collab editorial, and the interstitial walls it off entirely

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in or cookie-matched returning users and suppress the email interstitial** — if I'm in the Loyallist system, skip the new-subscriber pitch entirely and open straight to the homepage with a personalized welcome banner showing my points balance and the next earning event
- **Replace the hero for returning members with a Loyallist dashboard strip** — something like "Welcome back, Bella — 4,200 points | Next Double Points: July 18" pinned above the fold converts loyalty into a shopping trigger
- **Move the GAME DAY / Boss campaign hero behind the modal, not under it** — let that editorial imagery breathe; a designer lifestyle shot is exactly what would pull me in deeper
- **Rewrite the offer modal to filter by subscriber status** — returning subscribers should see a different value prop ("Exclusive early access to the Boss collab — for Loyallist members") rather than an offer that literally excludes them
