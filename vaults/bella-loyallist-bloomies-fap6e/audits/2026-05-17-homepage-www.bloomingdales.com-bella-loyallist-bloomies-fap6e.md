# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I'm a Loyallist member and the first thing I see when I open the app is a popup asking me to sign up for emails like I've never set foot in a Bloomingdale's in my life. The entire above-the-fold experience is hijacked by a new-subscriber acquisition modal — I literally cannot see the homepage. Whatever campaign or new arrivals they're pushing right now? Completely invisible to me. This feels like being handed a "first visit" coupon when you're already holding a Black card.

## What's working

- **The offer is legible and immediate.** "GET 15% OFF" in large serif type is clear and readable on mobile — no squinting required.
- **The CTA button has good tap-target sizing.** "SUBSCRIBE NOW" is full-width and easy to hit on a phone screen.
- **Fine print is actually present.** "Valid for new email subscribers only" is visible without having to go digging — that's rare and I appreciate the upfront disclosure.
- **The partial product imagery at the very bottom** hints at women's fashion that looks age-appropriate for me — white dress, leather moto jacket — I want to see more of that.

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire experience.** Above the fold is 100% new-subscriber acquisition. I'm a Loyallist — I'm already on their list. This offer is literally not for me, and it's blocking everything I came here to see.
- **No personalization or recognition.** I'm signed in (there's a bag icon) — there is zero acknowledgment that I'm a returning member. No points total, no tier, no "Shop your stylist's picks."
- **"Sign In" is visible in the header but feels orphaned.** The juxtaposition of a "Sign In" prompt and a "new subscriber" popup is cognitively dissonant for someone who already has an account.
- **The actual homepage content is invisible.** No hero image, no campaign banner, no category nav — just a form. I have no idea if there's a Spring event, a new designer drop, or a Friends & Family preview.
- **Zero urgency for the right reasons.** There's no Loyallist-specific event countdown, no "points event starts Friday," nothing that would make me feel like timing matters.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the new-subscriber modal for authenticated/cookied returning users.** If I'm logged in or have a session, the 15%-off email capture is irrelevant — show me my Loyallist dashboard or a personalized hero instead.
- **Replace the modal hero with a loyalty-triggered welcome strip.** Something like "Your Loyallist points: 4,200 — redeem at checkout" or "Spring points event starts 5/22 — shop early access" would hit me right where I care.
- **Show the actual homepage.** If the modal must run, make it a bottom-sheet or a dismissible banner, not a full-screen takeover. Let me see the campaign I opened the app to browse.
- **Wire the "Sign In" header button to a post-auth personalized state** — once I'm signed in, that slot should say my tier or a quick "Your points" shortcut, not "Sign In."
