# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-07-17
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Bloomingdale's is leading with markdowns and newness — classic mid-season playbook — but nothing on this screen acknowledges that I'm a Loyallist who spent serious money here last year. I could be a first-time visitor for all the homepage knows. The aesthetic is clean and adult-appropriate, and the markdown offer is real, but this is a missed opportunity to talk to someone who already belongs to the club.

## What's working

- **"30-70% OFF HUNDREDS OF NEW MARKDOWNS" banner** — This is the first thing my eye goes to. Red, high contrast, above the hero. I always check markdowns first, so this lands.
- **"NEW & NOW" editorial hero** — Clean, elevated, satin look on a model who looks like me, not a 22-year-old influencer. The Bloomie's visual DNA is intact.
- **"MORE GREAT FINDS" product rail** — Dresses are surfacing at the bottom of the screen. A blue off-shoulder midi, a pink floral halter — this is my lane. Product is visible.
- **Clean mobile render** — No chaos, no header overlap, no janky layout shift. For a retailer this size on mobile, that's not nothing.

## What's weak

- **Zero Loyallist recognition** — I'm a top-tier member. Nothing on this page has any idea. Saks shows me my points balance the second I land. This is embarrassing by comparison.
- **No CTA button in the hero** — "NEW & NOW" is three words of copy with no labeled button. I'm supposed to intuit that tapping the image goes somewhere? That's guesswork.
- **Markdown sub-links are unreadable** — Below the red banner headline there's a line of small text with what look like category links. I genuinely cannot read them at this size. They are decorative noise.
- **No Loyallist points event or F&F teaser** — This is July. If there's a double-points window coming or a Friends & Family preview, I should know about it on the homepage. I live for that calendar.
- **"Sign In" is motivationless** — The button is there but gives me no reason to tap it. Show me what I'm leaving on the table by not logging in.

## Recommendations

- **Swap the generic "Sign In" for a Loyallist incentive banner** — "Sign in to see your rewards + member-only markdowns" converts me in one tap. Generic Sign In does not.
- **Add a labeled CTA button under the hero copy** — "SHOP THE EDIT" or "SHOP NEW ARRIVALS" — something visually distinct so I know what to tap and what I'm committing to.
- **Make the markdown category sub-links into tappable pills** — Pull those tiny links out of the banner and render them as a horizontal pill row below it. I want to go straight to Designer or Shoes, not read fine print.
- **Add a slim Loyallist alert bar** — "Double Points on Full-Price Designer through Sunday" would make this homepage feel like it was built for a member, not a cold lead.
