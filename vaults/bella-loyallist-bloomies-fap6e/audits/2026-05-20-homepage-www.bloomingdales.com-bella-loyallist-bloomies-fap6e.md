# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- I open Bloomingdale's to check what's new and the very first thing the app throws at me is a full-screen email grab asking me to sign up like I've never been here before. I'm a top-tier Loyallist. I know when Double Points events start before the email goes out — and this page doesn't know me at all. Below the modal I can just barely make out a SWIM section, so the site is alive, but the homepage experience for a returning member is broken from the first second.

## What's working

- **Clean modal layout:** The 15% off email capture is legible, well-spaced, and the CTA button is unmissable. From a pure acquisition standpoint it does its job.
- **Logo and nav chrome present:** Bloomingdale's branding and the hamburger/search bar are visible — the page isn't visually broken.
- **SWIM category peek:** The sliver at the bottom tells me the underlying content is seasonally on-point for late May. If I could get past the modal, it might be relevant.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen email modal as first experience for a returning member:** I have a Loyallist account. This modal should not fire for signed-in or cookied customers. It's the digital equivalent of a door associate handing me a new-customer brochure when I walk in.
- **"Valid for new email subscribers only" fine print:** For any loyalty member who's already subscribed, the headline offer is immediately void. The modal actively tells me I can't have what it's promising.
- **Zero loyalty signal above the fold:** No rewards balance, no "X points to your next reward," no Loyallist event teaser — nothing that acknowledges I exist in the program.
- **Sign In button is present but cold:** The "Sign In" option is there, but it's unlabeled as a path to member benefits. There's no nudge like "Sign in to see your Loyallist offers."
- **Actual homepage content is completely obscured:** I cannot evaluate hero imagery, current campaign, featured designers, or category navigation because the modal covers 90% of the viewport.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress this modal for cookied/returning sessions:** Anyone who has visited before — especially a signed-in Loyallist — should land directly on the homepage. Trigger the email capture only for verified first-visit, anonymous sessions.
- **Replace the modal's subhead for logged-in users:** If the modal must show, swap "sign up for emails" with a loyalty hook: "See your Loyallist points and exclusive member offers" with a Sign In CTA instead of an email field.
- **Add a slim Loyallist rewards strip above the hero:** One line — "Your Loyallist points: X — next reward in $Y" — immediately converts this from a cold homepage to a personalized member hub.
- **Surface the current seasonal campaign headline without requiring modal dismissal:** At minimum, animate the SWIM content behind the modal or load it first so the brand story is visible the instant the page opens.
