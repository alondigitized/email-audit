# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I open Bloomingdale's and the first thing I see is a giant email capture modal begging me to sign up for newsletters. I am already a Loyallist. I already get the emails. I've been shopping here since the Chanel counter was on two. This is not a homepage — it's a lead-gen form wearing a designer label, and it has completely erased whatever hero campaign or seasonal story they meant to tell me today.

## What's working

- **"Sign In" button** is visible in the top nav — at least there's a path back to my account without hunting for it
- **Modal renders without bugs** — clean typography, clear input field, the Subscribe Now CTA is a proper button with readable contrast
- **The Bloomingdale's wordmark** at the top still conveys the premium positioning I expect; nothing looks broken or off-brand at the identity level
- **A lifestyle glimpse** is barely visible at the very bottom of the screen — a hint that there's actual content beneath this, which gives me a reason to dismiss and scroll

## What's weak

- **The entire modal** — it's a new-subscriber acquisition overlay on a site I've been loyal to for years; it immediately signals that Bloomingdale's does not know I exist as a returning customer
- **No Loyallist hook whatsoever** — no "Welcome back, Bella," no points balance, no "Members earn double today," nothing that acknowledges my status
- **The 15% off is not for me** — the fine print I can literally read says "new email subscribers only," so the only visible offer is one I'm ineligible for; that stings
- **The hero is completely invisible** — whatever editorial campaign or Friends & Family hook they have running is buried under this overlay; I have no idea what Bloomingdale's is selling today
- **Navigation is hamburger-only** — there's no visible category strip, so even after I dismiss this modal I can't see at a glance whether there's a designer floor or a shoes event worth tapping into

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for recognized Loyallist members** (or any signed-in user) — swap it for a loyalty-status banner: "You have X Loyallist points · Double points ends Sunday"
- **Replace the modal headline with a member-state-aware hero** — if I'm not signed in, at least show me "Sign in to see your member offers" alongside the email field, not just a generic 15% new-subscriber pitch
- **Surface the current promotion in the top nav strip** — one line above the search bar ("Friends + Family Event · Extra 25% off · Ends Sun") so returning members know immediately if there's a stocking event worth acting on
- **Add a visible category shortcut row** beneath the search bar: Designer, Shoes, Handbags, Sale — one tap to where I actually shop, before I've even dismissed anything
