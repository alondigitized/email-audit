# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-06-05
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- I open Bloomingdale's and the very first thing the site does is throw an email signup modal in my face — blocking the entire homepage — offering me 15% off that I, a top-tier Loyallist member, absolutely cannot use. Fine print right there at the bottom: "valid for new email subscribers only." This is the digital equivalent of walking into the store and having someone try to sign you up for the credit card you've had since 2009. Whatever is happening behind this modal, I can't see it.

## What's working

- **The 15% off headline** is clean, large, and easy to read. If I were new to Bloomingdale's this would land immediately.
- **"SUBSCRIBE NOW" CTA** is unambiguous — no "Learn More" waffling here. The action is obvious.
- **Typography and whitespace** on the modal are on-brand and clean — this is not some janky pop-up, it looks like Bloomingdale's.
- **Peek of lifestyle imagery** at the very bottom of the viewport (models in warm neutrals and red) hints that there is real content below — editorial, not product grid.

## What's weak

- **The modal itself** is the homepage for me right now. Bloomingdale's used the entire first screen to acquire an email from someone who has been shopping there since the early 2000s. This is a near-zero-value interaction for an existing Loyallist.
- **"Valid for new email subscribers only"** is visible in the fine print — I can read that. The headline promises 15% off; the fine print takes it away. That is a bait-and-switch energy that I actively resent.
- **No Loyallist recognition whatsoever.** If I am logged in (and I might not be since the Sign In tooltip is visible), my points balance, Loyallist tier, and any member-exclusive offers should be front and center — not an email form I will never fill out.
- **Zero category navigation visible.** Hamburger menu only. On mobile I should be able to tap into Women's, Designer, or Sale in one move — nothing here points me toward merchandise.
- **No current campaign is visible.** Is there a Friends & Family event I should know about? A double-points window? A seasonal promotion? I cannot tell because a modal is in the way.

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in / returning users and suppress the email modal entirely.** Show me my Loyallist tier and points balance in that space instead — or a member-exclusive offer. The modal should only fire for first-time anonymous visitors.
- **Surface the loyalty hook above the fold.** Even a thin persistent bar — "Hi Bella, you have 4,200 Loyallist points · Double points this weekend" — would make me feel seen and anchor me to a reason to buy.
- **Add a quick-tap category strip below the nav.** Women's / Designer / Sale / New Arrivals — four tappable chips so I can get to merchandise in one move without hunting through a hamburger menu.
- **Timestamp the campaign or offer.** If there is a Friends & Family event, a Loyallist preview, or a time-sensitive sale running, put a deadline on it and make it visible the moment the modal clears.
