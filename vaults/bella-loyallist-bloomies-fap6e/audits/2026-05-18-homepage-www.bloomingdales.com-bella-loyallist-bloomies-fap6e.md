# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened Bloomingdale's on my phone and the first thing I see is a full-screen email sign-up modal — not my Loyallist dashboard, not the spring campaign, not even a "welcome back" moment. The brand is pushing email acquisition hard, which I understand, but I've been a Loyallist member for years and this is how they greet me? It reads like they have no idea who I am the second I land.

## What's working

- **The "GET 15% OFF" headline** is large, clean, and legible at a glance — the offer is instantly readable even on a small phone screen
- **The Bloomingdale's wordmark** in the header is elegant and the overall typography holds up — still feels like an elevated department store, not a flash-sale site
- **The email input + "SUBSCRIBE NOW" flow** is frictionless for a new visitor — one field, one button, done
- **Fine print is actually visible**: I can read "Valid for new email subscribers only" without squinting, which is more honest than most modal overlays I've seen
- **The bag imagery peeking in at the very bottom** of the screenshot hints at real product content underneath — the taste level looks right

## What's weak

- **The modal is the entire above-the-fold experience**: I cannot see a hero, a campaign, a seasonal message, or anything that tells me what Bloomingdale's is selling *right now*
- **Zero Loyallist recognition**: I'm a top-tier member. There is no personalized greeting, no points balance, no "welcome back" — nothing. A generic sign-up wall treats me like a first-time visitor who wandered in off Google
- **"Sign In" button triggers a dropdown that covers search** — I can see the Sign In tooltip partially overlapping the search bar, which is clunky
- **The 15% offer is irrelevant to me**: New subscribers only. I can't use it. It's not even a loyalty double-points moment or a Loyallist preview — it's purely an acquisition play
- **No navigation categories visible**: the hamburger menu is there but I have to tap to get anywhere — no category chips, no quick links to Designer, Handbags, or what's in season
- **No urgency tied to anything I care about**: no points event reminder, no F&F preview, no "Loyallist early access" — the things that actually get me to open the app on a Sunday morning

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in / cookied returning visitors and suppress the modal entirely** — show me a Loyallist welcome strip with my points balance and the next points event date instead; that's the hook that actually converts top-tier members
- **Add a dismissible loyalty banner above the modal** reading something like "Loyallist member? Sign in to access your rewards and early access" with a direct CTA — one line, zero friction
- **Surface the current campaign above the fold** even with the modal present — a thin editorial banner or the product category chips should be visible so I know what season/story I'm walking into
- **Replace "SUBSCRIBE NOW" with "Get My 15% Off"** — more specific, less corporate, and the first-person phrasing has consistently better tap rates
