# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Bloomingdale's homepage and the first thing I see is an email-capture popup asking me to sign up for 15% off — *new subscribers only*. I've been a top-tier Loyallist for years and the site leads with an offer that literally excludes me. Whatever's actually happening on the homepage right now — a campaign, new arrivals, the summer edit — I can't see any of it.

## What's working

- **The "GET 15% OFF" headline** — clean, bold, readable on mobile; the discount number is immediately scannable
- **The email input + "SUBSCRIBE NOW" flow** — the UX is frictionless for someone who *is* a new visitor; field, button, done
- **Privacy Practices link** — at least the legal copy is present, even if it's buried in small type
- **The logo and top nav** — Bloomingdale's branding is clean, the hamburger and search bar are where I expect them

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is gated behind a new-subscriber modal** — as a Loyallist member, I'm being served an irrelevant offer before I can see *anything* the brand is actually doing this week
- **No "Sign In to access your account" prompt** — the Sign In button exists in the top bar but it's tiny; there's zero pull for returning members to authenticate and get a personalized experience
- **"SUBSCRIBE NOW" as the only path forward** — there's no visible dismiss/close button in this screenshot, which means the only affordance I can see is signing up for emails I'm already getting
- **Zero seasonal or editorial context** — I cannot tell from this screen if it's spring, summer, sale time, or what. I know Bloomingdale's Friends & Family is a thing I plan my calendar around, and there's no hint of it here
- **Fine print undermines the headline** — "Exclusions may apply" on a loyalty-heavy site feels like a qualifier that erodes trust, especially when I already know from experience how many exclusions there usually are

## Recommendations

- **Surface a "Sign In" prompt inside or alongside the popup** — something like "Already a Loyallist member? Sign in for your member benefits" turns a new-subscriber block into a returning-member on-ramp
- **Add a visible close/dismiss CTA** — "No thanks, take me to the site" immediately below the SUBSCRIBE button reduces the dark-pattern feel and is the single fastest fix
- **Swap the popup headline for something seasonally relevant** — "Insider access + 15% off — plus, Friends & Family starts soon" gives me a reason to care even if I don't need the email signup
- **Show a sliver of the actual homepage behind the modal** — even 80px of a hero image creates context that I'm walking into a real shopping experience, not a lead-gen page
