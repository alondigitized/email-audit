# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-06-26
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-26**
## Executive summary

- First thing I see when I land? A full-screen email capture modal telling me to sign up for emails in exchange for 15% off — as a top-tier Loyallist who has been subscribed since the Obama administration, this is a hard eye-roll. Behind the overlay I can just barely make out "SUMMER ROMANCE" at the bottom of the screen, so there's a campaign running, but I can't actually see it. Bloomingdale's is pushing acquisition at the expense of retention, and I am Exhibit A in who gets hurt by that.

## What's working

- **"GET 15% OFF" headline** — clean, high-contrast, direct. Gen X does not want to decode poetry to understand a discount. This lands typographically even if the offer isn't for me.
- **"Sign In" button visible** — it's there in the top right, so I know I can get to my account if I dismiss this modal. Small win.
- **Modal renders cleanly** — no broken images, no broken layout within the overlay itself. It looks intentional and polished aside from the clipped privacy text.
- **"SUMMER ROMANCE"** — partially visible at the bottom, at least signals that there's a real campaign underneath. The typography looks editorial and aspirational, which is on-brand for Bloomingdale's.

## What's weak

- **Modal blocks everything** — I came to shop, not to subscribe. As a returning member, I'm being intercepted before I can see a single product, campaign image, or loyalty hook. This is deeply annoying.
- **"Valid for new email subscribers only" — exclusionary and demoralizing** — I'm top-tier Loyallist and I land on a page that literally says the offer doesn't apply to me. No acknowledgment of my status. Nothing.
- **Zero loyalty recognition** — no "Welcome back, Bella," no points balance, no reference to Loyallist benefits, no member-exclusive access. Saks greets me by name. This doesn't.
- **No product imagery** — the entire above-the-fold experience contains zero merchandise. I can't tell what season it is, what's on sale, or what's new. Just a form.
- **"ivacy Practices" clipping bug** — the "Pr" at the start of "Privacy Practices" is cut off in the fine print. Minor but sloppy on a flagship homepage.
- **No urgency signals for the Loyallist calendar** — no hint of an upcoming F&F event, bonus points window, or double-point day. That's what I check Bloomingdale's for.

## Recommendations

- **Detect logged-in or cookied Loyallist members and suppress the new-subscriber modal entirely** — replace it with a member-welcome banner that shows my tier, points balance, and a "you qualify for member pricing" hook. I should never see a "new subscriber" pitch.
- **Swap the full-screen modal for a bottom sheet or corner slide-in** — let me see the hero campaign image immediately. The 15% offer can live in a dismissible banner for non-members while the homepage breathes for returning shoppers.
- **Surface a Loyallist-specific CTA above the fold** — something like "Your Loyallist perks this week" or "Earn 4X points on all designer through Sunday." That's what makes me open the app versus browse Saks.
- **Fix the privacy fine-print clipping** — either widen the modal or reduce the left margin on that text block so "Privacy" doesn't render as "ivacy."
