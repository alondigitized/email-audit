# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-07-24
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- I opened Bloomingdale's and the first thing I saw was a modal asking me to sign up for emails for 15% off. I have been a Loyallist member for years, I already get your emails, and this is what you show me. The brand reads luxury-adjacent, and the push right now is apparently email list acquisition — which tells me the homepage team is optimizing for net-new subscribers, not for people like me who are already deeply in the ecosystem.

## What's working

- **The 15% email offer is legible and prominent** — the headline size and contrast are right; a first-time visitor would clock it instantly
- **The CTA button ("SUBSCRIBE NOW")** is unambiguous — no "Discover" or "Explore" misdirection
- **No render bugs** — the modal is clean, the type is readable, the input field is properly labeled
- **The gold bag icon in the nav** — if that signals my Loyallist cart, I appreciate the subtle status signal even if it isn't doing any heavy lifting

## What's weak

- **The email capture modal is the entire above-the-fold experience** — I came to shop, not to be treated like a cold prospect; this is the homepage equivalent of a salesperson blocking the door
- **Zero loyalty recognition** — I'm a top-tier Loyallist and there is not a single word acknowledging that; no points balance, no member offer, no "Welcome back"
- **The actual homepage content is invisible** — I can see the faintest sliver of what looks like a white jacket and a dark bag at the very bottom of the screen, but the modal has buried everything
- **"Valid for new email subscribers only"** — I would have clicked Subscribe and then discovered I'm excluded; that's a waste of my time and a trust dent
- **No current campaign or seasonal hook visible** — I have no idea what Bloomingdale's is pushing this week, which as someone who tracks the Friends & Family calendar, genuinely frustrates me
- **The ">" carousel arrow** on the right edge suggests there's a hero carousel behind all this, but I can't get to it without dismissing the modal first — which requires a tap I shouldn't have to take

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for recognized Loyallist members** (or any logged-in user) — if I'm signed in or have a cookie, skip straight to the homepage; show me a member-specific banner instead ("Your next reward is $X away")
- **Surface a loyalty hook above the fold** — swap the email overlay for a thin rewards-status strip: "Hi Bella — you have X points. Shop to reach Loyallist Gold" takes 40px and turns me from a bouncer into a converter
- **Replace the generic modal offer with a member-exclusive offer** — "Loyallist bonus points this weekend" is far more compelling to me than 15% off that I'm excluded from anyway
- **Make one category tap — Women's Designer or New Arrivals — immediately visible** on page load so I don't have to interact with an overlay just to browse
