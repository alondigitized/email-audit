# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-13
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-13**
## Executive summary

- I opened Bloomingdale's expecting to see what's new — maybe a Friends & Family teaser, a Loyallist double-points preview, something — and instead the site threw a full-screen email acquisition modal at me before I could see a single product. I'm a Loyallist top-tier member who shops here multiple times a month. This is not a homepage. This is a lead-gen form with a logo on top.

## What's working

- **The 15% offer headline** is big and legible — if I were a first-time visitor, I'd see it instantly
- **"SUBSCRIBE NOW" CTA** is clean, dark, high-contrast — no guessing what the button does
- **Search bar** is visible above the fold so I can bypass the modal if I know what I want
- **Bag icon** with indicator is visible in the nav — confirms I'm logged in / have something saved

## What's weak

- **The modal itself** — I'm a Loyallist member. I have been on this site for years. Greeting me with an email capture wall is tone-deaf. I'm already in your database.
- **No personalization signal** — no "Welcome back, Bella" or "Your points balance," no "New this week in Designer," nothing that acknowledges I am a returning high-value customer
- **The 15% is worthless to me** — fine print confirms "new email subscribers only." The biggest piece of real estate above the fold is offering me a discount I cannot use
- **The actual homepage is invisible** — I can see the tops of what look like three product images peeking out at the very bottom, but the modal has consumed the entire viewport. I cannot tell what season it is, what they're promoting, or why I should stay
- **No Loyallist touchpoint anywhere** — I track the double-points events religiously; this page gives me zero reason to believe anything relevant is happening this week

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for authenticated / known visitors** — if my cookie or login state exists, skip the capture gate entirely and show me the actual merchandising
- **Replace the modal with a logged-in Loyallist ribbon** — "You have X points · Double points ends Sunday" is infinitely more useful to me than a 15% new-subscriber offer I cannot redeem
- **Show the campaign above the fold** — whatever is in those product image thumbnails at the bottom of the screen should be the hero, not buried under a form
- **Add a dismissible urgency strip at the top** tied to the current Loyallist calendar event ("Friends & Family starts Thursday — your exclusive access code inside")
