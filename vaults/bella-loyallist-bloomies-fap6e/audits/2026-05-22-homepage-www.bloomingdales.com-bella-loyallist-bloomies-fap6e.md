# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I landed on Bloomingdale's and the first thing I see is a signup modal taking over my entire screen — not a hero, not a sale, not my Loyallist dashboard. This is the email-capture interstitial for *new* subscribers, which is exactly what I am not. As someone whose inbox already has every Bloomies promotional email since 2019, being blocked by a "GET 15% OFF for new subscribers" wall is a frustrating mis-read of who I actually am. The brand looks premium and clean in the half-second before the modal fires, but right now the homepage is functionally invisible to me.

## What's working

- **Clean modal design** — the typography is crisp, the input field is clearly labeled, and the "SUBSCRIBE NOW" action is unambiguous. Technically well-executed.
- **Discount hook for new visitors** — 15% off with insider access is a reasonable acquisition offer; it would work on someone arriving for the first time.
- **Logo and navigation bar** — Bloomingdale's logo renders crisply, the hamburger and search icon are there, and the "Sign In" button is visible. If I can dismiss this modal, I have a path forward.

## What's weak

- **Modal fires for a logged-out known loyalist** — I've been a top-tier Loyallist for years. If I'm not signed in, the site has no way to suppress this, but that's exactly the problem: a recognized returning member (via cookie, session, or login state) should never see the new-subscriber interstitial.
- **"Valid for new email subscribers only" fine print** — visible at the bottom of the modal. So the offer I'm staring at isn't even for me. That's borderline bait.
- **Zero Loyallist presence** — no "Welcome back," no points balance teaser, no "Your next Loyallist event is..." Nothing that acknowledges I exist as a customer.
- **Homepage completely obscured** — I cannot see any hero, any category, any product, any seasonal campaign. The modal is a full-screen block.
- **No dismiss mechanism visible** — I don't see an X button or a "No thanks" link in the screenshot. That's either cut off or absent, and either way it creates friction.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email capture modal for Loyallist members (or anyone with a recognized session/cookie)** — I should never see a "sign up for emails" wall. Swap it for a Loyallist welcome strip: "Hi Bella — you have X points. Your next 2x event starts June 4."
- **Show a Loyallist-specific above-the-fold CTA** — replace the modal entirely for signed-in or recognized users with a banner that surfaces my rewards status and links to the current points event calendar.
- **Move email acquisition to a non-blocking format** — a slide-in drawer or footer strip after 30 seconds captures new subscribers without nuking the first impression for returning customers.
- **Add a clearly visible dismiss/close button** — if the modal must fire, give me the X in the upper right, large enough to tap on mobile without mis-hitting.
