# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- The very first thing I see when I land on Bloomingdale's is a full-bleed email-capture modal blocking the entire homepage — "GET 15% OFF and insider access to new arrivals." As a Loyallist top-tier member who already has the app, earns points every week, and knows the Friends & Family dates before they're announced, this is an immediate miss. The brand is leading with a new-subscriber acquisition play while I — their most valuable returning customer — can't even see what's on sale today.

## What's working

- **Clear offer headline** — "GET 15% OFF" is large, legible, and instantly scannable. For a cold visitor, this would be compelling.
- **Clean modal typography** — The Bloomingdale's wordmark and the modal itself render crisply on mobile. No layout breakage visible.
- **Primary CTA button** — `SUBSCRIBE NOW` is unambiguous and visually distinct. No guessing what the action is.
- **Peek of product content below** — I can see the edge of what looks like kitchen/home product images below the fold, which suggests there's real inventory content if I dismiss the modal.

## What's weak

- **Modal fires on a top-tier Loyallist** — I've been a Loyallist member for years, I'm already on the email list, and I'm greeted with a new-subscriber acquisition pop-up. This is a massive CRM failure. Bloomingdale's knows who I am when I'm logged in; this modal should never appear for me.
- **Zero loyalty acknowledgment above the fold** — No points balance, no "Welcome back, Bella," no Loyallist-exclusive preview. Saks Fifth Avenue shows me my Saks Fifth Avenue Credit when I land; this shows me nothing.
- **Entire homepage is invisible** — The seasonal campaign, featured designers, current promotions, new arrivals — all of it is behind this modal. I cannot evaluate the brand's current moment without dismissing it first.
- **Fine print undercuts the offer** — "Valid for new email subscribers only" is there if you read carefully. If I accidentally hit Subscribe, I get nothing. That erodes trust.
- **No navigation context** — The hamburger menu and search bar are present, but no category tiles, no "What's trending in Handbags," no designer callouts. For me, quick category access is everything.
- **"Sign In" button is secondary** — The `Sign In` CTA is present (top right) but it's small and competes poorly with the modal. A logged-in returning member experience should be the primary path, not an afterthought.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email modal for known subscribers and logged-in Loyallists.** If my cookie or login state is detectable, skip this modal entirely and serve a personalized Loyallist landing experience with my points balance and any active member-only event.
- **Swap the modal trigger for a sticky promo strip.** Put the 15% offer in a one-line banner at the top ("New here? Get 15% off — subscribe") so first-time visitors still see it without hijacking the full viewport for returning customers.
- **Surface a Loyallist-first hero immediately.** Something like "Loyallist Double Points Event — ends Sunday" with a direct link to participating brands. That's what gets me tapping within three seconds.
- **Promote the Sign In path more aggressively.** Make the `Sign In` button primary-colored and above the fold with a hook like "Sign in to see your member pricing." Turns authentication into a conversion driver, not a utility.
