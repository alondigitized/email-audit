# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I open Bloomingdale's and the first thing I see is a full-screen email grab begging me to subscribe for 15% off. I've been a Loyallist for years — I'm already in the system, already subscribed, and this overlay is wasting my time before I can even see what's on sale. The actual homepage is completely buried. Right now this reads less like a luxury department store and more like a pop-up tent at an outlet mall trying to collect my info.

## What's working

- **"GET 15% OFF" headline** — the discount is large, legible, and specific. For a cold visitor it's a genuine hook.
- **"Sign In" button** — at least it's visible in the top nav bar so I can bypass this mess and get to my account.
- **Email field UX** — clean, single-field, low friction for whoever this was actually designed for.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport overlay on load** — the entire above-the-fold experience is hijacked before I see a single product, hero, or campaign. As a loyal customer this is infuriating.
- **Offer is wrong audience** — "new email subscribers only" in the fine print means the 15% off is a bait-and-switch for anyone who already has an account.
- **Zero Loyallist acknowledgment** — no tier badge, no points balance teaser, no "member-exclusive" framing. I could be a first-time visitor for all this page knows or cares.
- **No seasonal context visible** — it's Memorial Day weekend, presumably prime for a big sale push, and I cannot see a single promotional message about it because this overlay ate the homepage.
- **"SWIM" glimpsed at the very bottom** — a sliver of what appears to be a swim category row is peeking out, which means there's actual content below, but it's completely inaccessible at this scroll position.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the email capture overlay for recognized Loyallist members** — if I'm cookied or logged in, skip straight to the homepage. Save the 15%-off pitch for truly new visitors.
- **Replace the cold-acquisition overlay with a Loyallist welcome strip** — something like "Welcome back, Bella. You have [X] points. Loyallist members get early access to Memorial Day savings." That one line would make me feel seen.
- **Surface the seasonal campaign immediately** — it's Memorial Day. Show me the hero. Even if the overlay persists for new users, the holiday messaging should be the background behind it, not invisible.
- **Move the "Sign In" button to a more prominent CTA position** — right now it's a tiny label in the nav. Make it a proper button with "Sign In for Member Savings" so returning customers feel pulled in rather than ignored.
