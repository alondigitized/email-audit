# Homepage snapshot · www.bloomingdales.com · 2026-08-07
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-07**
## Executive summary

- I typed in bloomingdales.com like I do every single morning during Friends + Family week, and instead of the homepage I get a wall of white space and "YOU DON'T HAVE ACCESS TO THIS PAGE" with a reference number like I've been flagged at security. This doesn't look like a luxury retailer pushing anything right now — it looks like a site that's down. If I didn't already have 15 years of loyalty points banked with them, I'd assume I got blocked and go straight to Saks.

## What's working

- The free-shipping strip below the error ("FREE SHIPPING EVERY DAY! PLUS, FREE RETURNS — Loyallists: no minimum") is accurate to my actual member benefit and correctly called out.
- The 25%-off credit card offer is dated and specific ("Ends 1/30/2027," "up to $250 in savings"), not vague — that's the kind of concrete number that usually gets me to tap.
- The overall page chrome — logo, search bar, hamburger menu, account/bag icons — is clean and unmistakably Bloomingdale's, minimal black-and-white, on-brand even in a broken state.

## What's weak

- The access-denied block ("YOU DON'T HAVE ACCESS TO THIS PAGE," reference code, 1-800 number) is the entire above-the-fold experience — there is no homepage here.
- Zero product imagery, zero hero, zero navigation into any category. I can't get to Saint Laurent bags or the new Fall arrivals I'd normally click straight to.
- No acknowledgment of my Loyallist status where it would actually matter — my points balance, tier status, or my stylist aren't referenced anywhere.
- The "Sign In" tooltip sits awkwardly under the bag icon with nothing else actionable near it.

## Recommendations

- Fix the access/permissions error first — nothing else on this page matters until a real homepage loads for a logged-out or logged-in visitor.
- When it's working, put a Loyallist-specific hero up top — points balance or "you're X points from your next reward" — since that's what gets a top-tier member like me to engage immediately.
- Surface the 25% credit card offer and the Friends + Family-style discount above the fold, not buried under four accordion menus.
- Add a "Recently Viewed" or "Picked for You" rail near the top so returning shoppers land somewhere personal, not generic.
