# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-06
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this feels like home — I can see my store address right there at the top, which tells me I'm logged in, and the quick links row puts Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad exactly where I need them. Kroger is clearly pushing Mother's Day right now, which makes sense — it's four days away and I still need to pick up brunch ingredients. The page is clean and loads fast, which matters on my phone.

## What's working

- **Store address bar ("Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377"):** I don't have to tell it who I am or where I am. That recognition feels personal right away.
- **Digital Coupons quick-link circle:** This is Tuesday's ritual, one tap from the top. Kroger knows what I'm here for.
- **Weekly Ad quick-link:** Same deal — I plan my whole week's meals around this, and it's right there without digging.
- **"Mother's Day is 5/10" headline:** Timely, direct, and relevant. Four days away means I need to act. The waffles and bacon in the image make me think about what I still need to grab.
- **"Start Your Order" shortcut:** Good for Boost members like me who come back mid-week to top off a delivery order.

## What's weak

- **No fuel points mentioned anywhere:** Fuel points are one of the main reasons I stay loyal to Kroger over Walmart Grocery, and I can't see my balance or any fuel-points promo above the fold.
- **No Boost membership acknowledgment:** I pay for Boost and get free delivery on $35+ orders, but the page treats me like I might be a first-time visitor. No "Free delivery on this order" badge or "Boost member savings" callout.
- **No specific deal attached to the Mother's Day push:** "Find everything to build the brunch spread" is warm but it's not a deal. I want to see "Save $10 on your $50 brunch haul" or a coupon stack right there.
- **"Save M..." cut off in the quick-link row:** Something is getting clipped on the right edge — looks like "Save More with Boost" but I can't read it. That's friction on my small screen.

## Recommendations

- **Show my fuel points balance in the header or in a Boost banner strip** — even "You have 350 fuel points" would make me feel rewarded and more likely to top off my cart.
- **Add a concrete savings hook to the Mother's Day hero** — something like "Clip brunch coupons, save up to $15" with a direct link to pre-filtered Mother's Day digital coupons.
- **Fix the truncated quick-link label** — whatever "Save M..." is, the full label needs to be visible; consider a horizontal scroll indicator or tighten the icon spacing.
- **Surface a "Delivery free with Boost" ribbon near the CTA** — remind me that my membership makes this order cost nothing extra to deliver, which lowers my barrier to just adding items and checking out.
