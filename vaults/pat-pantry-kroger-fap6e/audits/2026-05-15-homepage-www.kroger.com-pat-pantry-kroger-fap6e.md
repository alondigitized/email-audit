# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is my store — Kroger knows exactly who I am the moment I land here. The blue is familiar, my Granbury pickup location is already set, and that "Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" hero goes straight for Memorial Day grilling mode. It's pushing seasonal food and savings front and center, which is exactly where my head is this time of year.

## What's working

- **"Digital Coupons" quick-link** — that little circle shortcut sits right at the top of my screen and it's the first thing I tap every Tuesday. Kroger knows their loyalists.
- **"Fuel Points" quick-link** — right next to Digital Coupons. This is the one-two punch that keeps me choosing Kroger over Aldi. Seeing both without scrolling is a win.
- **Store/pickup location pre-set** — "Pickup at Granbury · 3915 E US Highway 377" is already showing. My store is remembered. I don't have to fiddle with anything before I start shopping.
- **"Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" hero** — Memorial Day weekend is exactly when I'm planning a cookout. Fresh meats, sides, long weekend — this hero is written for me.
- **"Shop All Summer" CTA** — clean, unambiguous, one tap to the seasonal sale.
- **Quick-link strip (Top Deals, Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, Fuel Points, Pharmacy & Health)** — this is basically my Kroger checklist laid out horizontally. I don't need to dig through a nav menu.

## What's weak

- **No specific savings number in the hero** — "Sizzling Savings" is great copy but I'm a coupon clipper. Tell me it's "Up to 40% off grilling essentials" or "Save $5 on $25 of meat" and I'm tapping immediately. Vague savings feel like marketing fluff.
- **"Sign In" not yet personalized** — I have a Kroger Plus card and a Boost membership. A returning user like me should see "Hi Pat" or at least my points balance, not a generic "Sign In" button. This is a missed loyalty moment.
- **Hero image is barely visible** — the grilling food (looks like a hotdog and drinks) is half-cropped at the bottom. I can tell it's food but the photography feels cut off and incomplete on mobile.
- **"Save M… with B…" quick-link is cut off** — the last item in the quick-link strip is truncated. I can't tell what it says. That's a small but annoying mobile layout issue.
- **No Kroger Plus/Boost member pricing badge** — as a Boost member I get free delivery on $35+. That benefit isn't visible anywhere above the fold. That's a big differentiator they're leaving on the table.

## Recommendations

- **Put a specific dollar amount in the hero** — swap "Sizzling Savings" for something like "Save up to 40% on Grilling Essentials" or add a banner strip: "Boost members save an extra 10% this weekend." Clippers like me respond to numbers, not adjectives.
- **Personalize the header for signed-in users** — show "Hi Pat · 2,340 Fuel Points" instead of "Sign In." That rewards balance is the hook that keeps me loyal; hiding it is a miss.
- **Fix the truncated quick-link label** — "Save M… with B…" is probably "Save More with Boost." Either widen the strip, reduce font size, or drop one less-used item. Truncated text on my most-used nav strip erodes trust.
- **Surface the Boost free-delivery threshold** — add a small banner just below the hero: "Free Delivery on $35+ for Boost members." That's my primary reason for the membership; remind me I'm getting value.
