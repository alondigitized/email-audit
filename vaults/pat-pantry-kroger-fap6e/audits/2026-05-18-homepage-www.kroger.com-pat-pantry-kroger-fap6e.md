# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- Well, this feels like home — the K logo, the blue, the familiar layout. Right away I can see Digital Coupons and Fuel Points on my shortcut row, which is basically my whole Tuesday routine in two taps. They're pushing a summer grilling theme right now, which makes sense for Memorial Day weekend, but I'd like to see an actual dollar amount somewhere instead of just "Sizzling Savings."

## What's working

- **Digital Coupons shortcut circle** — this is literally my Tuesday click, sitting right where I expect it. No hunting around.
- **Fuel Points circle** — I drive a minivan and those points add up; seeing it upfront reminds me why I stay loyal.
- **Weekly Ad circle** — I plan my week around it, and it's one tap from the home screen.
- **Store location bar ("Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377")** — the page already knows my store. That's reassurance I'm shopping the right prices and inventory.
- **"Kick Off Summer" hero** — the BBQ imagery (burger, hot dog, patriotic dessert cup) is squarely aimed at what I'm actually cooking this weekend. It's not aspirational nonsense; it's dinner.

## What's weak

- **No specific savings amount in the hero** — "Sizzling Savings" is nice marketing but I want to see "Save $5 on grilling meats" or "Weekly Ad deals up to 30% off." Vague is a missed sale for me.
- **"Shop All Summer →" CTA is a text link, not a button** — for medium-tech me, a proper button reads as "press here." A text link with an arrow is easy to skip past.
- **"Save M..." category circle is visually clipped** — whatever that shortcut is (Boost? Save More?), I can't read it fully. If it's my Boost membership perk, I'd want to see it clearly.
- **No Boost membership reminder** — I'm a paid Boost member with free delivery on $35, and I don't see that called out anywhere above the fold. That's a big loyalty hook left on the table.
- **No Private Selection or store-brand callout** — I lean on Private Selection heavily. A hero focused purely on national-brand grilling items misses a chance to remind me of that value.

## Recommendations

- **Add a specific savings figure to the hero copy** — swap "Sizzling Savings" for something like "Save up to 30% on grilling essentials — this week only." Concrete numbers make me stop scrolling.
- **Style "Shop All Summer" as a filled button, not a text link** — a blue pill button with white text would read clearer to someone like me and actually signal "tap here."
- **Surface the Boost free-delivery benefit in a sticky banner or badge** — something like "Your Boost membership: FREE delivery on $35+" right below the nav would remind me I've already paid for convenience and close the loop on hesitation.
- **Fix or widen the "Save M..." category circle label** — either shorten the label to fit, or add a second row of text. Right now it's invisible value.
