# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-10
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-10**
## Executive summary

- OK, so Kroger is doing its thing right now and leading hard with Mother's Day, which — being a 50-year-old woman who plans her whole week around the weekly mailer — I actually appreciate. The brunch spread looks gorgeous and it feels timely. But I came here to clip my Tuesday coupons and check my fuel points, and none of that is front and center where I'd want it.

## What's working

- **The Mother's Day hero headline** — "Mother's Day is 5/10" with the date right in the copy is smart. I know exactly what this is about and when to act.
- **"Find everything to build the brunch spread Mom deserves"** — that subtext is warm and practical, speaks to a meal-planner like me who wants a list, not a vibe.
- **Digital Coupons quick-access icon** — being a Tuesday coupon-clipper, having that one tap away without having to dig through menus is a huge win for me.
- **Weekly Ad icon in the quick strip** — I plan my meals around that mailer, so having it right there in the shortcut row keeps me in my routine.
- **Store location lock** ("Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377") — I know I'm shopping my real store. That's trust right there.

## What's weak

- **No fuel points or Kroger Plus balance visible** — I open this app half the time just to check my points before I drive to fill up. That dashboard should be greeting me the second I land.
- **No concrete savings number above the fold** — "Shop Mother's Day" tells me nothing about price. Even a "save on brunch essentials" or "digital deals inside" would pull me forward.
- **Sign In is not surfaced prominently enough** — for a returning Boost member, I want to be recognized immediately. The Sign In link is there but feels generic, not welcoming back a loyal customer.
- **"Save M..."** — that quick-access icon is cut off on my screen. I assume it's "Save More with Boost" but I can't confirm. That's sloppy on mobile.
- **No Private Selection callout** — that's my brand inside Kroger. A Mother's Day brunch is the perfect moment to showcase it and it's nowhere to be seen.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Kroger Plus fuel points in the header** — even a small "You have 243 fuel points" banner right under the nav would make me feel recognized and keep me coming back.
- **Add a specific savings hook to the hero** — swap "and more." for something like "— plus digital deals on eggs, berries, and Private Selection." Give me a reason to tap through, not just a pretty plate.
- **Show the Boost free-delivery reminder** — I'm paying for that membership. A "Free delivery on $35 with Boost" badge near the hero CTA would reinforce the value and push me toward a bigger cart.
- **Fix the truncated "Save M..." icon** — on mobile that label needs to either shorten or resize; "Save with Boost" at 2 words is enough to communicate it.
