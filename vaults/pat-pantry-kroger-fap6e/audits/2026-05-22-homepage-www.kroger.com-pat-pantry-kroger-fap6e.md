# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- This is my store, my app, and I know exactly what I'm looking at — Kroger is pushing a Memorial Day grilling push and, more importantly, they put the Fuel Points banner right at the top where I can see it. This feels like a brand that knows I'm coming back to it, not a brand trying to win me over. The homepage is lean, functional, and does not waste my time.

## What's working

- **Fuel Points banner in yellow** — hits me before anything else on the page. This is my primary reason to shop Kroger and it's above everything. Whoever placed this earns a gold star.
- **Quick-nav shortcut row** — "Digital Coupons," "Weekly Ad," and "Fuel Points" are my Tuesday routine spelled out as one-tap links. Perfect.
- **Saved store location** — the teal bar showing "Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377" tells me the app already knows me. That's trust-building without a single word of marketing copy.
- **"Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" hero** — grilling for the long weekend is exactly how I think about this week's meal plan. The copy is plain-spoken and direct, not cute.
- **Timing** — it's Memorial Day weekend. This is not a stale page. They updated it.

## What's weak

- **Gray placeholder circles in the quick nav** — Top Deals, Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, Fuel Points icons are all blank gray circles. The images did not load. It looks broken, even if the labels are still readable.
- **"Search Prod" truncation** — the search bar placeholder text is cut off mid-word on mobile. Small thing, but it looks unpolished.
- **No Boost membership call-out** — I pay for Boost and I get free delivery on $35+. That's a real perk for a returning member, and it's not on this screen. Would take one line in the hero sub-copy.
- **No points balance visible** — I'd love to see "You have 450 Fuel Points" right under the banner. That hook would keep me shopping to hit the next threshold.
- **Hero is all text on dark** — the food imagery at the very bottom of the screen is barely peeking in. A photo of a properly grilled brisket or a Private Selection side dish would have me tapping faster.
- **No explicit Private Selection callout** — that's my brand within the brand and it's nowhere above the fold.

## Recommendations

- **Replace the gray placeholder circles with actual icons or imagery** — if the icon set isn't loading on mobile, fall back to simple emoji or flat-color icons so the quick nav doesn't look broken.
- **Add a Boost member badge or reward-points balance below the Fuel Points banner** — something like "Boost member · 450 pts · $35 for free delivery" would keep loyalists like me locked in and shopping toward a threshold.
- **Swap the dark text-only hero for a lifestyle shot with food** — a platter of grilled Private Selection steaks with a "Shop the Weekend Grill" CTA would be more visceral than white text on navy.
- **Expose the free delivery threshold in the hero sub-copy** — "Free delivery with Boost · Orders over $35 ship free" costs zero screen space and answers a question I always have.
