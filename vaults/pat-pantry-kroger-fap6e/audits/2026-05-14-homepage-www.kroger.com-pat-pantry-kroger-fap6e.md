# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- Well, this looks like home — Kroger knows it's almost summer and they're coming at me with grilling and savings right away, which honestly is what I was hoping to see. It's clean, it's blue, it's familiar, but I'm a little frustrated those category shortcut circles at the top aren't loading — Digital Coupons should be front and center for me, and right now those icons are just grey blobs. The hero is doing its job, though.

## What's working

- **Saved store location bar** ("Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377") — that tells me immediately the site remembers me and I can shop for pickup. That's a big trust signal right up top.
- **"Digital Coupons" quick-nav label** — even with grey circles, the label is there. I know exactly where to tap once the icons load, and that's the first thing I do every Tuesday.
- **"Weekly Ad" quick-nav label** — same deal. Two of my six go-tos are labeled at the very top. Kroger knows how I shop.
- **"Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" headline** — plain, direct language. No buzzwords, no overly cute copy. I know what this page is trying to sell me: grilling food for the weekend. Done.
- **Seasonal food imagery** — the partially visible BBQ spread at the bottom (hot dog, red-and-white drink, patriotic berry dish) is exactly the kind of "what am I making this weekend?" imagery that gets me tapping.

## What's weak

- **Grey circle icons in quick-nav** — Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad are my entry points every single visit and they're not rendering. That's a meaningful friction point for a returning Boost member like me.
- **No specific savings number in the hero** — "Sizzling Savings" is nice, but tell me it's "Save up to $15 with digital coupons" or "Meats starting at $X/lb" and I'm tapping immediately. Right now it's marketing speak.
- **No Boost membership mention above the fold** — I pay for Boost, I get free delivery on $35+. That's a big perk and it should be visible to logged-in or returning Boost members, not hidden below.
- **"Shop All Summer" is too broad a CTA** — I don't want to shop all summer, I want to find what's on sale this week. "See This Week's Deals" or "Clip Today's Coupons" would move me faster.
- **No urgency signal** — The subtext says "the long weekend" but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown. If these deals end before the weekend, just say so.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the icon rendering on the quick-nav strip** — those grey circles need their icons. Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, and Top Deals are the top three reasons returning Kroger loyalists open the app/site. Ship a fix this week.
- **Add a specific dollar hook to the hero subtext** — swap "Get grilling with fresh meats, sides and more for the long weekend" for "Save up to $20 on grilling essentials with your Kroger Plus card this weekend." Concrete number, loyalty tie-in, deadline.
- **Surface the Boost delivery perk in the location bar or hero** — something like a small "Boost: Free delivery on $35+" badge near the cart icon would remind me exactly why I'm a member and lower my add-to-cart threshold.
- **Change the CTA to "Clip Coupons & Shop" or "See Weekend Deals"** — closer to my actual intent and more likely to get a tap from a Tuesday coupon-clipper.
