# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is Kroger knowing exactly who they're talking to right before Memorial Day weekend — grilling, summer food, and right there in the quick nav: Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad, my two first stops every time I open this app. It reads like a grocery store that gets that I'm feeding a family on a holiday weekend, not a food-delivery startup trying to look hip. The timing is dead-on.

## What's working

- **Digital Coupons quick-nav link** — that's my Tuesday routine surfaced immediately. One tap and I'm in. Kroger knows their loyal shoppers.
- **"Weekly Ad" shortcut** — I plan my whole week around the mailer. Having it one tap away on the homepage means I don't have to hunt.
- **"Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" hero** — the timing is perfect. It's Memorial Day weekend, I'm grilling, and the subtext "fresh meats, sides and more for the long weekend" tells me exactly where to shop.
- **Location bar showing my store** — "Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377" is reassuring. It knows where I shop. That saves me the usual fumbling.
- **Blue brand color and clean layout** — familiar, no surprises. For medium-tech comfort like me, predictable is good.

## What's weak

- **No savings number in the hero** — "Sizzling Savings" without a dollar amount or percentage is just words. Tell me chicken is $1.99/lb or steaks are 30% off and I'm tapping immediately.
- **Quick-nav icons all failed to load** — six gray empty circles above Top Deals, Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, etc. looks broken. If I didn't already know what those shortcuts were, I'd be confused.
- **No fuel points or Boost reminder above the fold** — I have a Boost membership and I track fuel points religiously. Nothing on this screen tells me I'm earning anything or what my points balance is. That hook is completely missing.
- **"Shop All Summer →" is a text link, not a button** — it's easy to miss. I almost read right past it.
- **No specific Private Selection callout** — that's their premium house brand and it's completely absent. For a loyalist like me, seeing a PS product featured means trust and quality signal.

## Recommendations

- **Put a specific savings number in the hero** — swap "Sizzling Savings" for "Meats & Sides Up to 40% Off" or show the fuel-points multiplier (e.g., "Earn 4x Fuel Points on Grilling Essentials"). I need a reason to click, not a slogan.
- **Fix the icon images in the quick-nav row** — those six gray circles look like a broken page. Even showing the first letter of each label as a fallback would be better than blank gray shapes.
- **Add a Boost/fuel-points balance widget** — even a small "Hi Pat, you have 847 fuel points" strip under the location bar would make me feel like this page is mine, not generic.
- **Make "Shop All Summer" a real button** — blue outline box with the arrow keeps the brand look but gives me an obvious tap target that doesn't blend into the body copy.
