# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-08-08
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-08**
## Executive summary

- This looks like my Kroger, but the second I land on it I get a pop-up wall — first a tax-free-days banner, then a full data-privacy modal sitting right on top of the "Lunchbox MVPs" deals. Right now it's pushing back-to-school lunch stuff hard, which isn't really my lane since my kids are grown, but I can see the Weekly Ad and Digital Coupons links right where I expect them.

## What's working

- The sub-nav strip with Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, and Buy 5 Save $1 Each is exactly where my eyes go first — that's my whole Tuesday routine right there.
- "Save More with Boost" icon in the quick-links row — good, I'm a Boost member and I like seeing my perk called out.
- Real, specific deal math on the product tiles (Buy 3 Get 3 Free, Save $1 each when you buy 5) instead of vague "save big" fluff.
- SNAP EBT tags on items feel transparent, not like they're hiding anything from anyone.

## What's weak

- That "Improving Your Experience" data-collection popup plants itself right in the middle of the screen the moment I open the app — I have to stop and deal with legal language before I can even look at a banana.
- The hero banner is all back-to-school lunchboxes. I'm 50, my kids aren't packing lunches anymore — this doesn't speak to me at all.
- Almost every deal tile just says "Sign In" instead of showing me the member price outright — makes me feel like I'm being teased rather than shown a deal.
- That one produce tile with garbled text ("Jout $4.78 each") looks broken, like the page didn't finish loading right.
- Nobody in any of the imagery — no people at all, just staged product shots. Feels a little cold for a grocery brand I've shopped for 20 years.

## Recommendations

- Swap the back-to-school lunchbox hero for a "This Week's Ad" or "Your Fuel Points" hero during weeks that aren't peak school-shopping season.
- Let the privacy modal be a small banner at the bottom, not a full-screen blocker sitting on top of the deals I opened the app to see.
- Show my Boost/rewards price directly on the tile instead of forcing a "Sign In" click to see if something's actually a deal for me.
- Fix that cut-off "Jout" text on the grape tile — looks like a caching or truncation bug.
