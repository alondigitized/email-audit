# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- This is my store, and it knows it — they've already got Granbury locked in and the fuel points banner is the first thing I see in that signature Kroger yellow. They're pushing Memorial Day grilling week, which is exactly where my head is right now with the long weekend coming up. Kroger feels like a grocery partner here, not just a storefront.

## What's working

- **Fuel Points banner (yellow strip):** This is the first thing I see and it speaks directly to me — I track these points religiously and $1/gallon is a real motivator on a fill-up.
- **Category icon row:** Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, and Fuel Points all sitting there as one-tap shortcuts is exactly how I use this app. My Tuesday ritual is two taps away.
- **Store location pre-set:** "Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377" tells me I'm recognized as a returning customer — no friction hunting for my store.
- **Hero timing:** "Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings / Get grilling with fresh meats, sides and more for the long weekend" lands perfectly with what I'm actually thinking about this week.
- **"Shop All Summer →" CTA:** Clear, direct, no cute wordplay — just tells me where it goes.

## What's weak

- **"Search Prod" truncation:** The search bar placeholder is cut off mid-word — looks sloppy and makes me wonder what else is broken on mobile.
- **"Save M..." category icon:** The last visible shortcut is cut off — probably "Save More with Boost" or something I'd actually care about as a Boost member, but I can't tell.
- **No Boost membership acknowledgment:** I pay for Boost (free delivery on $35+), and there's zero recognition of that anywhere above the fold. A "Welcome back, Boost member" line or my delivery threshold would make me feel seen.
- **Hero imagery shows no one like me:** The bottom of the hero appears to be a food bowl or grill scene — no person, no family, no Gen X woman cooking for the long weekend. Missed warmth.
- **No specific savings %/$ in the hero:** "Sizzling Savings" is vague — what's actually on sale for the grill? I want a number.

## Recommendations

- **Surface my Boost status above the fold** — even a small "Boost Member | Free delivery on $35+" bar under the location strip would go a long way toward making me feel like a VIP, not just a visitor.
- **Fix the search bar truncation on mobile** — "Search Products" should fit or the placeholder should be shortened to "Search" rather than cutting off mid-word.
- **Add a concrete savings anchor to the hero** — swap "Sizzling Savings" for something like "Fresh meats up to 30% off this weekend" so I immediately know it's worth tapping.
- **Expose the Boost delivery threshold in the hero CTA area** — a line like "Free delivery on this order — you qualify" turns browsing into a cart session.
