# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- First screen and I already feel at home — Kroger knows who I am. The "Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" hero is hitting exactly the right note for Memorial Day weekend meal planning, and those shortcut icons for Digital Coupons and Fuel Points are sitting right up top like they belong there. This feels like a grocery store that actually built its website for me, not for some TikTok influencer.

## What's working

- **Digital Coupons shortcut icon** — it's the second icon in the row, right where I'd look. Tuesday is coupon day for me and that button being one tap away is exactly right.
- **Fuel Points shortcut** — I drive, I plan my shop around those points, and I don't have to dig for this.
- **Memorial Day timing on the hero** — "Get grilling with fresh meats, sides and more for the long weekend" tells me the store is stocked for what I'm already planning to cook this weekend.
- **Store location bar** — "Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377" means Kroger already knows my store. That's the kind of personalization that keeps me from switching.
- **Weekly Ad shortcut** — right there in the icon row. I build my grocery list from the weekly mailer every single week.

## What's weak

- **No specific savings number above the fold** — "Sizzling Savings" is marketing fluff. I want to see "$5 off $25" or "Save 20% on grilling essentials." Vague copy doesn't get my cart started.
- **The partially cut-off "Save M..." shortcut** — I can't read it, and if that's my Boost membership benefit I really should be able to.
- **No Boost member badge or personalized greeting** — I have Boost. I should see something that says "Welcome back, Pat" or at minimum a Boost-member offer, not a generic homepage.
- **No price or product preview in the hero** — there's grilling imagery at the bottom but no featured item with a price tag or deal amount. A "Chicken thighs $1.49/lb this week" or similar would get me to tap.

## Recommendations

- **Add a specific dollar or percent callout to the hero** — swap "Sizzling Savings" for something like "Sizzling Savings — Meats up to 30% off this weekend." Concrete numbers move shoppers like me.
- **Surface a Boost member exclusive above the fold** — a small badge or banner strip that says "Boost member? Extra 10¢/gal at the pump this weekend" speaks directly to loyal members and makes non-members consider upgrading.
- **Fix the truncated shortcut label** — that sixth icon ("Save M...") is getting clipped. Either shorten the label text or reduce the icon count so every label reads cleanly.
- **Drop one featured weekend deal with price into the hero area** — even a single product card (e.g., Kroger Private Selection burgers + price) next to the CTA would give me a concrete reason to tap through instead of just browsing.
