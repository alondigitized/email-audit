## What's working

- **"The Clock's Ticking" headline** — direct, urgent, very Gen X-readable. No cutesy millennial phrasing. I get it immediately.
- **Specific end date ("end 5/5")** — today or tomorrow. That's real urgency I respond to, not fake countdown-timer nonsense.
- **"Shop All Deals →" CTA** — unmistakable, right below the copy, clear action word. I know exactly where it goes.
- **Quick-access icon row** — Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad are exactly what I come here for every Tuesday. The *intent* of putting them front and center is right, even if execution is broken today.
- **Store address pre-set ("Pickup at Granbury / 3915 E US Highway 377")** — my store is already locked in. That saves me a step and feels personal.

## What's weak

- **Blank gray circles in the icon row** — Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, Pharmacy & Health all failed to load their icons. That row is my main reason for being here and it looks broken on first impression.
- **No fuel points or Kroger Plus balance** — I check my points every single week. There's zero sign I'm a loyal member right now. A "Sign In" button feels cold when I've been shopping here for years.
- **Delivery deal framing misses Boost** — "exclusive delivery deals" sounds like it's selling me something I already paid for. It should say "your Boost benefit" or "you've got free delivery" — not tease me into clicking.
- **No produce deals or Private Selection callout** — the strawberries and organic greens in the hero background are nice but there's no "Double Digital Coupon" or Weekly Special tied to them.
- **"Save M[ore] with B[oost]" is cut off** — whatever that is, it's getting clipped on the right and I'd have to scroll to find it. Not a great look.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the icon row first** — the Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad circles need their images or fallback labels. That row is probably the highest-traffic element for returning shoppers and it's visually broken right now.
- **Add a "Welcome back, Pat — X fuel points ready" strip** just below the nav for signed-in members, even a thin banner. That one line would transform this from a generic storefront into my grocery app.
- **Reframe the delivery copy for Boost members** — swap "exclusive delivery deals" for "Your Boost: unlimited free same-day delivery" so it reads as a benefit I earned, not a discount I'm chasing.
- **Pull one Weekly Ad highlight into the hero** — a single "This week: Buy 2 Private Selection, get 1 free" line next to the CTA would speak directly to how I actually shop.
