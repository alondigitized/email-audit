# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-05
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-05**
## Executive summary

- I know this store like the back of my hand, and the homepage felt like Kroger was expecting me — the location's already set to my Granbury store, Digital Coupons is right in the shortcut row, and the hero's pushing a Deal Days event that ends today. That said, this isn't exactly rolling out the red carpet for a Kroger Plus member like me — no fuel points balance, no "welcome back," no Private Selection feature anywhere in sight.
- **6/10**
- + Concrete offer visible: "unlimited free same-day delivery end 5/5" is specific and timely
- + Primary CTA present: "Shop All Deals →" is visible and tappable
- + Visual hierarchy is clear: blue hero block, headline, CTA — eye lands right where it should
- + Current campaign: "online DEAL DAYS" badge and an end-date of 5/5 (which is today!) signal a live promotion
- + Loyalty-adjacent shortcut: "Digital Coupons" is front and center in the icon row — that's for me
- + Offer feels honest: delivery deadline is stated plainly, no asterisks visible
- − No returning-shopper hook: no Kroger Plus balance, no fuel points, no "Hi Pat" personalization
- − Hero doesn't speak to my persona specifically — generic urgency, not tailored to a loyalist
- − Category icon circles are rendering as blank gray circles — images didn't load

## What's working

- **Digital Coupons shortcut** is the second icon in the quick-nav row — I clip every Tuesday and this is exactly where I'd go first
- **Location pre-set to Granbury / 3915 E US Highway 377** — the app already knows my store, which saves me the setup tap
- **"The Clock's Ticking" + "end 5/5" deadline** — clean, no-nonsense urgency that doesn't feel fake; today IS May 5th so this is real
- **"DEAL DAYS" badge** on the product imagery — the visual pop of a sale badge on organic produce speaks to my weekly shop mindset
- **Navigation bar** has "Save" and "Pickup & Delivery" as top-level tabs — both are things I use constantly

## What's weak

- **No Kroger Plus / Boost member welcome** — I pay for Boost and have thousands of fuel points; none of that shows without logging in first, and the Sign In button is just a generic icon
- **Gray placeholder circles** in the category shortcut row — "Top Deals," "Weekly Ad," etc. are labeled but the icons never loaded; looks broken on a phone
- **Hero is delivery-focused, but I already get free delivery via Boost** — so "unlimited free same-day delivery" reads as something I already own, not a reason to shop today
- **No Weekly Ad hero push** — that's how I plan my entire week; it's buried in the icon row rather than featured prominently
- **No fuel points callout** anywhere above the fold — that's my number-one reason to stay loyal to Kroger over Aldi or HEB

## Recommendations

- **Surface the Boost/Kroger Plus status for logged-in members** — show fuel points balance and a "you're X points from your next reward" nudge right in the header or hero; that's the hook that keeps loyalists like me from wandering
- **Swap the delivery-deal hero for a Weekly Ad tile** — or at minimum add a "This week's deals" module below the hero; meal-planners like me open the app to check the mailer, not to sign up for delivery
- **Fix the icon image loading** — those gray circles look like the app is broken; even if it's a timing issue, it erodes trust immediately
- **Add a fuel points progress bar** anywhere above the fold — a single line like "You have 247 pts — shop $X more to save at the pump" would drive incremental basket size instantly
