# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is my home turf — the minute I see "Fuel Points" twice above the fold, I know Kroger knows me. The page is pushing a Memorial Day grilling campaign ("Fire Up the Flavor") tied to a 4X Fuel Points clip, which is exactly the kind of deal I circle on the fridge calendar. It looks like a loyal-member play more than a "come try us" recruitment pitch, and that works for me.

## What's working

- **Fuel Points banner (yellow strip):** This is the first thing my eye hits. "$1/Gallon" in big type is my language — I track fuel points the way some people track airline miles. This makes me feel seen.
- **"Fire Up the Flavor" + 4X Fuel Points through 5/23:** Tying the seasonal campaign directly to a fuel-points multiplier is smart. I'll clip that coupon before I do anything else on this page.
- **Shortcut row (even with broken icons):** The *labels* are right — Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, Fuel Points are my Tuesday workflow in a row. Even without icons loading, I know where to tap.
- **Location pre-set:** "Pickup at Granbury" is already dialed in. That's one less thing I have to fix before I can start shopping.
- **"Clip Coupon & Shop →" CTA:** Clear, action-specific, and it matches exactly what I do every Tuesday. Not "explore" or "discover" — actual coupon clipping.

## What's weak

- **Grey circle icons in the shortcut row:** Every single shortcut is a blank grey bubble. If I didn't already know this app, I'd think the page was broken. This is a real credibility hit on mobile.
- **Hero imagery is partially cropped:** The food spread at the bottom is cut off — I can barely tell it's a burger and some red-white-blue bowls. For a grilling campaign, the food photography should be the hero.
- **No Private Selection callout:** I specifically shop Private Selection when it's on deal. The weekly ad shortcut is there, but there's no "PS" badge or featured item to pull me in.
- **Boost membership is buried:** The last shortcut says "Save M... with B..." — that's almost certainly "Save More with Boost," my membership. But it's cut off and at the end of the row. If that's my biggest ongoing benefit, it deserves more real estate.
- **No fuel points balance shown:** I'm signed in (or at least, sign-in is visible). I want to see my current fuel points balance right here — that's the hook that makes me want to earn more.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the icon images in the shortcut row** — grey circles undermine the whole trustworthy, familiar vibe Kroger has built. This one is a ship-next-week fix.
- **Show my fuel points balance in the header or below the location strip** — even a "You have 380 pts" would make me feel rewarded and motivated to keep shopping.
- **Let the grilling food breathe** — the "Fire Up the Flavor" hero is doing the right campaign but the imagery is nearly hidden below the fold. Swap to a full-bleed burger or spread shot that fills the screen, not a sliver of a bowl.
- **Add a "This week's Private Selection deals →" tile** in the shortcut row or just below the hero — that's a loyalty-shopper signal that tells me Kroger knows what I actually buy.
