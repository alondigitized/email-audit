# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- This is Kroger doing what Kroger does — practical, promotional, and familiar. Right out of the gate it's pushing a summer grilling theme with "Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings," and the quick-link row (Digital Coupons, Weekly Ad, Fuel Points) is basically my Tuesday morning routine laid out in a row. It feels like home, honestly, even if the hero isn't doing anything spectacular.

## What's working

- **Quick-link strip (Top Deals / Digital Coupons / Weekly Ad / Fuel Points / Pharmacy & Health):** This is my entire Tuesday checklist in five circles. I don't have to hunt — it's the first thing under the nav. That's earned loyalty design.
- **Store address pre-filled ("Pickup at Granbury | 3915 E US Highway 377"):** The site already knows my store. As a Boost member who does weekly pickup, seeing my store confirmed right up top removes friction immediately.
- **Seasonal relevance:** "Kick Off Summer with Sizzling Savings" is timely for mid-May — it matches where my brain is (Memorial Day weekend meal planning). The food imagery (hot dogs, patriotic fruit bowl, drinks) backs it up visually.
- **Clean mobile layout:** Nav, search bar, quick links, hero — each band is distinct and readable on a phone screen. No crowding.

## What's weak

- **"Shop All Summer →" CTA is vague:** That's a massive destination with no specifics. Tell me "Shop Grilling Meats" or "Clip Cookout Coupons" and I'd tap faster.
- **No loyalty balance or fuel points tally visible:** I'm a fuel-points obsessive — if my current balance showed up near "Sign In," I'd be checking it every visit. Right now I have to sign in blind.
- **No concrete offer in the hero:** "Sizzling Savings" promises a deal but shows no number. $5 off, 2x fuel points on meat, free delivery — something specific would push me to tap now instead of later.
- **Quick-link circles are loading gray (no icons rendered):** The category icons appear as blank gray circles in the screenshot. On a slower connection those missing icons make the row look broken, which gives me a split-second pause.
- **"Save M... with B..." is cut off in the nav:** Looks like "Save More with Boost" but it's truncated. For someone whose Boost membership is a core benefit, that partial label feels sloppy on mobile.

## Recommendations

- **Put a specific offer in the hero subhead:** Replace "Get grilling with fresh meats, sides and more for the long weekend" with something like "Earn 2x Fuel Points on all meat this weekend" — that's actionable and loyalty-relevant.
- **Surface fuel points balance near the sign-in button:** Even a small "🔥 Points: —— | Sign in to see" teaser makes fuel-loyalists like me tap Sign In before anything else.
- **Load icons in the quick-link strip or use text-only labels as fallback:** Those gray circles read as broken. Text labels alone would be cleaner than blank placeholder circles.
- **Change CTA copy from "Shop All Summer" to something category-specific:** "Shop Grilling Essentials" or "Clip Summer Coupons" gives me a reason to tap rather than a vague promise.
