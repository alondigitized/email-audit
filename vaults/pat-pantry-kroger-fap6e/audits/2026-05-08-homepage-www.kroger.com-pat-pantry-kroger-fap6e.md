# Homepage snapshot · www.kroger.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- Okay, Kroger knows exactly what week it is — "Mother's Day is 5/10" is right there front and center, and as a mom myself, that registers. The page looks clean and familiar, and that quick-link strip with Digital Coupons and Weekly Ad at the top is basically my whole Tuesday routine in one row. What's frustrating is that I'm clearly not signed in, so there's no fuel points balance, no Boost badge, no "welcome back" — just a generic store address and a sign-in button staring at me.

## What's working

- **Mother's Day timing** — "Mother's Day is 5/10" with two days to go is the right urgency without being screamy about it. I actually need to pick up brunch ingredients and this tells me Kroger has what I need.
- **Digital Coupons quick link** — That horizontal strip is basically my homepage within the homepage. Seeing "Digital Coupons" as a tap-away shortcut means I don't have to dig through menus to do my Tuesday thing.
- **Weekly Ad quick link** — Same strip, same logic. I plan my whole week around that mailer, and it's right there at the top.
- **Brunch food imagery** — The waffles, bacon, eggs, and flowers in the lower half of the screen look genuinely appealing. That's real food I'd actually make, not some food-stylist fantasy.
- **Store address in banner** — "Pickup at Granbury" lets me know it remembered my store. Feels like it knows me a little, even if it won't show me my fuel points.

## What's weak

- **No visible loyalty benefits** — I'm a Boost member and a Kroger Plus loyalist. There's no fuel points balance, no "member pricing" badge, no Boost free-delivery callout. The "Sign In" button means I'm getting the cold-stranger version of the homepage.
- **No concrete offer above fold** — The hero is all mood, no deal. For me, the deal IS the mood. Even a "Save $10 on your brunch" or "Earn 2x fuel points" banner would make this feel like my Kroger.
- **"Start Your Order" quick link is vague** — Of the six quick-link circles I can see, "Start Your Order" doesn't tell me what I'm starting. I already have an order started most weeks — this would be less confusing as "Reorder" or "My Cart."
- **Last two quick-link icons are cut off** — "Save M..." and whatever comes after it trail off the right edge. Medium tech comfort means I might not know to swipe, and I could miss something relevant (probably "Save More with Boost" which is very relevant to me).

## Recommendations

- **Show fuel points and Boost badge in the top banner when signed in** — That blue strip already has my store address. Adding "487 fuel pts" or a Boost member badge next to my name would make this feel personalized and give me a reason to stay engaged instead of just recognizing my store address.
- **Add one concrete deal to the Mother's Day hero** — Something like "Save $5 on your brunch order" or "Earn 4x fuel points on floral this weekend" right under the headline. The imagery is doing its job; the hero just needs a hook that reaches my wallet.
- **Fix the truncated quick-link strip** — Either show fewer links so nothing clips, or add a visible scroll indicator/arrow. I shouldn't have to guess there's more there.
- **Swap "Start Your Order" to "Reorder" or "My Weekly Order"** — For loyalists who shop the same staples every week, reorder friction is real. Lean into the habit.
