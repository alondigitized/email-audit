**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- The screenshot captured an "Access Denied" error page — Kroger's CDN (Akamai EdgeSuite) blocked the request. Here is my review based strictly on what I can see in the image.
- Well, this is not the Kroger homepage I was expecting — it's a big bold "Access Denied" error from their server, nothing else. There's no brand, no deals, no anything. This looks less like a grocery store and more like a bouncer who won't let me in the door.
- **1/10**

## What's working

- The error message is at least readable — large font, no clutter, so I can tell right away something went wrong. That's the only silver lining here.

## What's weak

- **The "Access Denied" heading** — that's the first thing I see on my phone. No logo, no Kroger branding, just rejection. As a loyal Boost member planning my Tuesday coupon clip, this is a brick wall.
- **The reference number and EdgeSuite URL** — `Reference #18.671ca17.1777763411.2eeda5fe` and `https://errors.edgesuite.net/...` mean nothing to me. I don't know what EdgeSuite is, I don't know what I did wrong, and I have no idea how to fix it.
- **Zero recovery path** — no "try again" button, no link back to the store, no phone number, no fallback. I'm just stuck on a white page.
- **No Kroger branding whatsoever** — if I landed here from a search, I'd have no idea this was even Kroger's site.

## Recommendations

- Replace the raw EdgeSuite error page with a Kroger-branded 403/error page that includes the logo, a friendly message ("We couldn't load the page — try refreshing or visit our app"), and a direct link to the store.
- Add a "Try the Kroger App" CTA with the App Store / Google Play links so blocked web users have an immediate alternative.
- Surface a customer service number or chat link so loyalists like me aren't just abandoned mid-errand.
- Investigate bot-detection rules that may be blocking real mobile users — if my regular phone browser is hitting this, there's a geo or user-agent rule misfiring.
