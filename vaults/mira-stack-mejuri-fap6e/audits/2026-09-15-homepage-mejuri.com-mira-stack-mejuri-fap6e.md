# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-09-15
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- Okay, this is giving me exactly what I expect from Mejuri — moody, editorial, jewelry-first. The hero is literally a close-up of an ear stack (cuff, hoops, a stud) which is basically a mirror of my own ear right now. No discount being pushed at all though — it's leaning into "come try on in person" and a "complimentary cleaning" perk instead of the 20%-off drop I'm actually waiting for.

## What's working

- The hero close-up of the ear — cuff, two hoops, a stud stacked together — is basically a photo of what I already do to my own ears. That's smart targeting even if it's not calling me out by name.
- "Come in, try everything on, and build your stack in person" — the word "stack" is doing real work here, it's speaking my language.
- "Complimentary Jewelry Cleaning At Stores" in the top ticker is a nice touch — as someone who's precious about her gold staying gold, free maintenance is the kind of perk that keeps me loyal even without a discount.

## What's weak

- Zero discount visible. I specifically wait for the rare 20%-off launch and subscribe to the monthly drop email — this homepage gives me no reason to believe a drop or sale is happening right now.
- "SHOP NOW" is a tiny underlined text link, not a real button — easy to miss on a phone screen, doesn't scream "tap me."
- No loyalty/rewards module, no "welcome back" or recently-viewed nod for a returning subscriber like me.
- The big "MEJURI" wordmark gets sliced off at the right edge of the screen — reads like it's bleeding off intentionally for style, but on first glance it looks like something didn't load right.

## Recommendations

- Swap the small underlined "SHOP NOW" for an actual filled or outlined button so it reads as tappable at a glance.
- If there's any active promo (even a smaller one), surface it above the fold — I open this app specifically hunting for the 20%-off window.
- Add a one-line loyalty/rewards nod near the top (points balance, "welcome back" — anything) since I'm clearly a repeat subscriber, not a cold visitor.
- Pull in a rating or review count on the Interconnected Collection piece — pavé diamonds at this price point, I want social proof before I tap further.
