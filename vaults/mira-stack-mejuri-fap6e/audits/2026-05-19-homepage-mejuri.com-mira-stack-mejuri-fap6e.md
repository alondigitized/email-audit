# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-05-19
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- I opened mejuri.com and honestly my first reaction was: *did this load right?* The entire above-the-fold viewport is consumed by an extreme close-up — what looks like blurred marble or stone texture in warm taupe tones, or possibly a zoomed-in material shot. There is no legible hero copy, no visible CTA, no product, no navigation in frame. For a brand I associate with clean editorial minimalism and actual jewelry photography, this is a disorienting first impression.

## What's working

- **Texture palette is on-brand:** The warm taupe/brown tones are consistent with Mejuri's earthy, elevated aesthetic — if this is intentional art direction, the color story is right.
- **No modal or interstitial overlay:** At least I'm not immediately blocked by a popup demanding my email before I can see anything.

## What's weak

- **Zero legible copy above the fold:** No headline, no subhead, no offer, no brand name visible. A new visitor has no anchor whatsoever.
- **No jewelry in frame:** I came here to shop fine jewelry. The hero shows no product — not a ring, not a hoop, not a chain. That's a miss for me specifically.
- **No CTA button visible:** I have no idea where to tap to start shopping. The entire CTA layer is missing or obscured.
- **No navigation visible in frame:** The top of the screen shows only a thin browser bar with illegible text — the nav is either collapsed or not rendered in this viewport.
- **Extreme crop destroys context:** Whatever this image is supposed to show (marble? stone? a close-up of a product surface?), the crop is so tight that it communicates nothing. It looks like a loading placeholder that never resolved.
- **No trust signals:** No review counts, no free-shipping callout, no "as seen in" badges — nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Lead with a wearable:** Swap this abstract texture hero for a lifestyle shot of a real hand or neck wearing the current launch — stacked rings, a dome hoop, something I recognize as product. I need to see what I'm buying within the first half-second.
- **Put headline copy on screen:** Even one line — "New Arrivals: The Croissant Collection" or "Fine Gold, Every Day" — tells me I'm in the right place. Right now there is no text to anchor me.
- **Add a visible CTA button above the fold:** "Shop New Arrivals" or "Shop Rings" — one tap, clear destination, above the fold. Without it, I have nowhere to go.
- **Surface the nav or a category strip:** A scrollable category bar (Rings / Earrings / Necklaces / New) immediately below the hero would let me route myself to my category in one tap, which is all I'm asking for.
