# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- OK so I open Mejuri and I'm hit with... a close-up of what looks like someone's hair blowing against a blue sky, shot so tight it's basically abstract. Feels intentional — editorial, moody, very much trying to signal "fine jewelry for real humans, not mannequins." But I genuinely can't tell what they're selling from this frame, which for a mobile homepage is a problem.

## What's working

- **Single full-bleed image:** No visual noise, no nav clutter in the main viewport — the image fills the phone screen confidently, which reads premium.
- **Color story:** The warm brown tones against the sky blue have a summery, elevated palette that doesn't feel discount-y. Matches the Mejuri aesthetic I know.
- **No obvious layout breaks:** The frame renders cleanly — no misaligned elements, no placeholder text visible.

## What's weak

- **Zero legible copy above the fold:** I can't see a headline, a campaign name, or a product name. On mobile, if I can't read anything in the first second, I'm already wondering if the page loaded wrong.
- **No CTA button visible:** There's nothing to tap. No "Shop the drop," no "See new arrivals," nothing that moves me forward.
- **Hero subject is unrecognizable as jewelry:** A tight crop of hair is a bold editorial choice, but I can't see a ring, hoop, or chain. I'm here for fine jewelry — confirm that's what you sell.
- **No loyalty hook in sight:** I subscribe to the monthly drop email; I expect a "Hey member" nudge or at least a rewards mention. Not visible.
- **No offer signal:** I wait for the 20%-off launches. Nothing above the fold tells me whether one is live or coming.

## Recommendations

- **Pull the camera back 40%:** Show the face/neck with the jewelry actually on the model. Keep the editorial energy but make the product the hero — even a glimpse of a gold hoop would anchor this.
- **Drop a two-line headline overlay on the image:** Something like "New Arrivals — Fine Gold, Daily Wear" in white or cream type would add instant context without killing the mood.
- **Add a sticky or pinned CTA button** at the bottom of the viewport — "Shop New Arrivals" or "See the Drop" — so there's an obvious next tap even if I don't want to scroll.
- **Surface the promo strip immediately:** If there's a 20%-off event or free-shipping threshold active, put a thin banner above the hero. Don't bury it below the fold.
