# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-09-29
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-09-29**
## Executive summary

- This looks like a confident, editorial fine-jewelry brand. It leads with a tight crop of an ear stack (a cuff, a pavé X-hoop and a small hoop) and doesn't lead with a sale. Right now it's pushing the Interconnected Collection and getting me into the Century City store in LA. It's pretty, but I can't see one price, one offer or one product I could tap.

## What's working

- **The hero crop of the ear.** It shows a cuff, a pavé X-hoop and a small hoop layered together, which is exactly how I wear my stack. It sells the idea of stacking before any copy does.
- **"HAND-DRAWN LINES, TRACED IN PRECIOUS METAL AND PAVÉ DIAMONDS."** It's short, grown-up copy that doesn't try too hard, and it names the materials I care about.
- **The black "WE'RE HERE, LOS ANGELES" block.** "Come in, try everything on, and build your stack in person" speaks to my fear of the gold not matching in person.
- **The clean, minimal layout.** No pop-up covers the page, so it feels premium.

## What's weak

- **The clipped "MEJURI" wordmark on the right of the hero.** It runs off the edge, and it repeats the logo that's already at the top. It looks like a bug and wastes the prime space.
- **The hamburger-only navigation.** I can't reach Rings, Earrings or Hoops in one tap, and no categories show on the first screen.
- **The "SHOP NOW" link.** It's tiny and sits far from the image, below the fold line of the photo, and it doesn't say what I'm shopping for.
- **No mention of karat or gold.** I return aggressively if the karat looks wrong, and nothing on this screen says "14k" or "solid gold."
- **The LA store banner and cleaning promo.** "Complimentary Jewelry Cleaning At Stores" and "Century City" mean nothing to me if I'm not near LA. The top bar has no online offer.
- **No prices, ratings or product tiles.** The only entry point is a collection hero, and there's a large blank area at the bottom of the screen.

## Recommendations

- **Fix the hero wordmark.** Remove the oversized clipped "MEJURI", or put a real headline in its place such as "Stack the Interconnected Collection – 14k gold". Move a real button ("Shop Earrings") onto the image.
- **Add category chips under the hero.** Rings, Hoops, Earrings, Stacks & Sets and New should each be one tap away. Add a row of 3–4 product tiles with a price and a star rating.
- **Replace the top-bar text with something usable online.** For example, "Free shipping & 30-day returns" or a member perk, with the store cleaning offer as a secondary message.
- **Add a "14k solid gold" trust line.** Put it under the hero with a returns or "if the gold isn't right, send it back" reassurance. That's the promise that would stop me from returning things.
