# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- Mejuri is serving me an extreme close-up lifestyle shot — hair and open sky, very editorial, very "aspirational woman in motion." That's on-brand for the fine-jewelry minimalism they've built, but from where I'm sitting on this screenshot, I cannot see a single word of copy, a nav bar at usable scale, or a price point. It feels like I landed inside a lookbook page that forgot to include the lookbook.

## What's working

- **The lifestyle photography direction is right for me.** Wind-blown, golden-toned, outdoorsy-but-polished — that's the Mejuri woman I associate myself with. The editorial aesthetic is consistent with the brand I trust for my 14k daily wear.
- **No layout jank or broken images.** The hero image loads fully and crisply — no half-loaded asset, no overlapping elements, no placeholder text.
- **The close-up creates intimacy.** For a jewelry brand, zooming into the person rather than the product can work as a brand-awareness move — it signals confidence that I already know who they are.

## What's weak

- **Zero readable text above the fold.** No headline, no campaign name, no "New Arrivals" — nothing to orient me. I'm looking at hair. I love a moody editorial, but I also love knowing why I'm here.
- **No CTA visible.** I can't see a "Shop Now," "Explore the Drop," or any button. If I landed here cold, I would have no clear next step without scrolling or tapping.
- **No promotional hook surfaced.** I subscribe to the monthly drop email specifically because I wait for those rare 20%-off moments. If there's a sale happening, it's invisible at this viewport.
- **No navigation visible at readable scale.** There's what looks like a thin browser toolbar at the very top, but no mega-nav, no category shortcuts, no search — nothing that lets me jump straight to rings or hoops in one tap.
- **The hero image doesn't show jewelry.** Not even a flash of a hoop or a ring-stack. For someone who came here because she's considering upgrading her stack, the pure-lifestyle play at this zoom level is a missed opportunity to seed desire immediately.

## Recommendations

- **Pull back the hero crop so the model's ear/neck jewelry is visible** — let me see gold against skin within the first three seconds. The editorial vibe survives; the product context is added.
- **Overlay a short, punchy headline on the hero** — even something like "The Stack. New 14k drops." anchors me in intent.
- **Pin a promo strip or pill above or below the hero** — one line: "Members save 20% on new arrivals. Sign in →" would make me stop scrolling before I bounce.
- **Surface a sticky mini-nav or floating CTA button** — even a translucent "Shop Rings" pill at 20% from the bottom would give me a clear tap target without disrupting the editorial aesthetic.
