# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- This is Mejuri, and the first thing I see is an extremely close-cropped hero image — what looks like wind-blown hair against a blue sky. It's moody and editorial, which tracks with their whole "everyday fine jewelry" vibe. But from where I'm sitting, it's all atmosphere and zero signal — I cannot find a headline, a CTA, an offer, or even a product.

## What's working

- **Atmosphere is on-brand.** The close-crop hero with natural light and a blue-sky background is pure Mejuri DNA — relaxed, aspirational, not trying too hard. As someone who lives in their jewelry, that palette resonates.
- **No visual chaos.** The thin nav bar at the top and the full-bleed image feel intentional. Nothing is cluttered or fighting for space.
- **Clean mobile render.** No obvious layout shift, no broken image artifacts, no placeholder text bleeding in — what's there, loads correctly.

## What's weak

- **Zero product signal above the fold.** I can't see a ring, a hoop, a chain, nothing. For a jewelry brand, that's a miss — I need at least a glimpse of gold to stay interested.
- **No headline copy visible.** The hero image is so cropped that if there's a campaign tagline overlaid, it's completely obscured. I'm getting vibe with no information.
- **No CTA button.** Not even a "Shop Now" — which is the bare minimum for any e-commerce hero.
- **No offer or hook.** I came here knowing I'll only pull the trigger on a 20%-off launch. There's no banner, no strip, no badge telling me if anything is on or coming.
- **The close-crop cuts off context.** This might be a beautiful full-frame shot — but the viewport crop makes it read as abstract texture rather than lifestyle jewelry imagery.

## Recommendations

- **Pull the hero frame back.** Show the model's ear, neck, or hands wearing the jewelry. Even a 30% wider crop would let the product breathe and tell me why I'm here.
- **Add a visible campaign headline.** Even a two-word overlay ("New Drops" / "Gold Season") would give the hero communicative weight. Right now it's a screensaver.
- **Pin a promo strip above the hero.** A thin, dismissible banner — "Members get early access to the May drop — join free" — costs nothing visually and is exactly why I subscribe.
- **Place a single high-contrast CTA button** ("Shop New Arrivals" or "See the Drop") in the lower third of the hero. Mobile shoppers need a clear tap target, not an image to decode.
