# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-07-14
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-14**
## Executive summary

- Mejuri is running a summer tennis campaign — "Jewelry You Can Win In" — and the entire above-the-fold experience is committed to it. A striking action shot of a Black woman mid-backswing, wearing a gold necklace and bracelet, makes a bold visual statement. But as someone who came here to shop my stack, I'm getting a mood board, not a store.

## What's working

- **The campaign image is genuinely arresting.** An athlete mid-swing in fine jewelry is an unexpected, confident brand statement that makes me stop scrolling.
- **The banner copy is punchy.** "Jewelry You Can Win In" is short, confident, and speaks the right register for my generation — no fussiness, no floral language.
- **Clean mobile nav.** Logo centered, hamburger left, cart and search icons right — no clutter competing with the hero.
- **The jewelry is actually visible on the model.** Gold necklace and bracelet are legible even in motion, which at least signals product.

## What's weak

- **No CTA button anywhere above the fold.** I cannot tap into anything. A hero this big with no action to take is a dead end.
- **"Mejuri Play Guide" is not a shop page.** That banner link points to editorial, not a product category — it does nothing for purchase intent.
- **Zero stacking content visible.** I own ten pieces and I'm here for the ring + hoop ecosystem. Tennis is a campaign angle, not a category hook.
- **No offer.** I wait for the 20%-off launches. There's no hint of a promotion, no threshold, no member price. Nothing to hold me.
- **No loyalty acknowledgment.** I subscribe to the drop email. There's no "welcome back" or rewards balance to remind me why I'm loyal.

## Recommendations

- **Add a tappable CTA to the hero.** "Shop the Collection" or "Shop the Court Edit" with a visible button overlaid on the lower third of the image — I need somewhere to go.
- **Surface a second campaign strip below the hero with a stacking entry point.** "Build Your Stack" or "Rings + Hoops" as a quick-tap category would catch me immediately without breaking the tennis campaign flow.
- **Swap "Mejuri Play Guide" for a dual banner.** Keep the editorial link but pair it with a hard offer like "Free shipping over $75" or "Members get early access to the drop."
- **Add a loyalty nudge in the sticky nav or banner.** Even just a small "Your rewards: 240 pts" badge for logged-in users would make me feel seen as a returning customer.
