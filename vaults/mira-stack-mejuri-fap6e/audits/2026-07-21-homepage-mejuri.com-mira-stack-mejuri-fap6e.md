# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-07-21
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is peak Mejuri — editorial, moody, deliberately minimal. The split hero (ocean waves crashing into what looks like a close-up of a model in a black swimsuit with a necklace) screams "fine jewelry as a summer mood, not just an accessory." I recognize this brand immediately and it feels right. What it is NOT doing is giving me a single reason to convert today vs. come back later — and for someone like me who only buys on the rare 20%-off launch, that's a miss.

## What's working

- **"The Summer Guide | Explore Now" banner** — small but it tells me there's curated content waiting. That pull toward editorial curation is exactly what keeps me on a jewelry site longer than I planned.
- **The split-hero composition** — ocean waves into a model's neckline wearing something gold. It's atmospheric and on-brand for Mejuri's "quiet luxury" language. I don't need to read copy to feel the vibe.
- **Seasonal alignment** — this is clearly a summer campaign, not some recycled evergreen. It signals the brand is actively curating, which makes me trust new drops more.
- **Clean render** — no layout jank, no placeholder text, no broken imagery. The page just looks right.

## What's weak

- **No headline copy in the hero** — the entire above-the-fold experience is a photograph with zero text except the promo banner and logo. I have no idea what the Summer Guide actually is or why I should care right now.
- **No offer anywhere above the fold** — I'm a "waits for 20%-off" person. If there's a sale or launch, I need to see it immediately. This page gives me nothing to act on.
- **No product-specific entry point** — not a single ring, hoop, or chain is clearly featured and tappable. The model crop at the bottom is barely visible and not linked to anything obvious.
- **Hamburger nav only** — my category (rings, hoops, stacking) is one hidden tap away. On mobile, this creates friction for someone who wants to browse a specific category fast.
- **No loyalty visibility** — I'm presumably in their ecosystem (I subscribe to the drop email), but there's zero acknowledgment of that on-screen. No "welcome back," no rewards balance, nothing.

## Recommendations

- **Add a short hero headline over the wave image** — even something like "Stack for Summer" with two or three product images of the actual editorial picks would give me somewhere to land.
- **Surface one concrete offer or launch date in the banner** — swap the generic "Explore Now" for something like "New Drops: The Summer Guide — Shop Now" or "Free Shipping on Orders $75+" if that threshold exists.
- **Pin one fast-tap category row below the fold** — Rings / Hoops / Necklaces as visual tiles, not hidden behind the hamburger. Jewelry stackers browse by category first.
- **Add a loyalty callout or "your drop is live" moment** — even a one-line "Mejuri Club members get early access" in the banner would stop me from bouncing before I even scroll.
