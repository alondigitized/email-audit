# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- Mejuri is coming in hot with an editorial-first, almost-abstract hero — what I'm looking at is a full-bleed, ultra-close-up lifestyle image that fills the entire viewport: what appears to be hair blowing in wind against a pale blue sky. Very cinematic, very "fine-jewelry brand that takes itself seriously." The vibe is aspirational and intentionally stripped of copy, which tells me they're leaning into mood over message right now.

## What's working

- **Aesthetic ambition.** The full-bleed, hair-in-wind close-up is genuinely beautiful. For a brand that sells feeling-over-function fine jewelry, this is on-brand risk-taking I can respect.
- **Clean load.** No layout shift, no broken assets, no overlapping elements. For a mobile viewport, that's not nothing.
- **Sky + warmth color palette.** The golden-brown tones against pale blue suggest warmth, summer, skin — that's in the right zip code for fine jewelry.

## What's weak

- **Zero copy above the fold.** I cannot tell what's on sale, what's new, what season this is, or why I should stay. As a shopper who waits for the 20%-off launch drop, I need a signal that now is the moment — I see nothing.
- **No CTA.** There is literally no button. Not even a ghost button. Nothing to tap. This is a conversion dead zone.
- **No product or stacking context.** I'm a stacker. I want to see rings on fingers, hoops in ears, layers on a neck. Close-up hair is beautiful but tells me nothing about what's new in the gold collection.
- **No offer or threshold visible.** Free shipping? Member pricing? A welcome offer? None of it. I'm already in deal-waiting mode and this page gives me zero reason to override that default.
- **No navigation visible.** I can't see a hamburger menu, a top nav, or a category strip. For a native mobile user, I need a quick path to "Fine Jewelry" or "Rings" — I can't find it.

## Recommendations

- **Layer a headline over the hero.** Even one line — "New Gold Arrivals" or "Stack Season" — gives the image context and tells me there's a reason to be here today.
- **Add a primary CTA button over the hero image.** "Shop New Gold" or "See the Drop" — high contrast, bottom-third placement so it doesn't fight the image but still sits above the fold.
- **Swap or reframe the hero crop.** Pull back enough to show a model's hands, neck, or ear with jewelry in frame. Jewelry brands live and die on product-in-context imagery; abstract hair doesn't close the loop for someone evaluating a $200 hoop.
- **Drop a thin promo strip above the hero.** One line: "Members get early access to the 20% drop" or "Free shipping over $150." Even a small signal converts high-intent return visitors like me from browse to buy.
