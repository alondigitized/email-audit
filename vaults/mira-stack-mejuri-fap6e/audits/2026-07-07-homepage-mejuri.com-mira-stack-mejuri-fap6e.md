# Homepage snapshot · mejuri.com · 2026-07-07
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- This is unmistakably Mejuri — clean, editorial, a little sporty right now. The hero is doing something interesting: they've put fine jewelry on an athlete mid-motion, which is a direction I actually respect. But above the fold this is almost entirely vibes — no button, no offer, no "here's what to tap next." Beautiful photo, zero conversion architecture.

## What's working

- **The layered-necklace jewelry styling on the model:** she's wearing exactly the kind of stack I'd build — a delicate gold chain layer plus a beaded accent necklace, with a simple hoop. That's me. I clock it immediately.
- **The "Jewelry You Can Win In" banner concept:** the athletic-lifestyle angle for fine jewelry is smart and feels current. It's not a sale pitch, but it's a real positioning statement.
- **Image quality and restraint:** no clutter, no loud promotional noise on the hero. The photography is doing premium-brand work.
- **Chat widget present:** low-friction way to reach someone, visible without being intrusive.

## What's weak

- **Zero above-the-fold CTA button:** I have no obvious next tap. The banner text is tappable in theory but it doesn't look like a button, and it routes me to a "Play Guide" — editorial, not shop.
- **No offer, no hook for my wallet:** I wait for 20%-off launches. There is nothing here that tells me whether one is live, coming, or if I even have points to burn.
- **Hero copy is entirely absent from the image itself:** the only words I see are in the small sliding banner. The hero image has no overlay text, no product name, no entry point. It's a campaign photo that forgets to be a homepage.
- **Navigation is hidden behind a hamburger:** to get to rings or hoops I have to tap into a menu — that's friction. Mejuri's category strength (rings, earrings, necklaces) is invisible here.

## Recommendations

- **Add a copy overlay to the hero** with a shop CTA: something like "Stack It Your Way — Shop the Athletic Edit" with a visible pill button beneath. Keep the photo, just give it a destination.
- **Swap the hamburger-only nav for a horizontal category strip** below the hero: Rings / Necklaces / Earrings / New In. One tap to my stack, not two.
- **Surface a loyalty or offer signal in the banner:** even "Members get early access to the Play Edit" would make me feel seen as a returning customer. Right now the banner reads like press copy.
- **Add a product entry point directly on or below the hero:** a tight rail of 2–3 pieces the model is actually wearing, named and priced. If I can ID the beaded necklace in that image, I am buying it right now.
