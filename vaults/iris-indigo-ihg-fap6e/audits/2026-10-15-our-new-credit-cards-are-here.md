# Our new credit cards are here 🎉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth IHG credit-card pitch in about three weeks. It follows "Introducing new credit cards for you 💳" (10-01), "Offers up to 200K bonus points" (10-08), and the Chase co-brand push that has run since July. The render is also very close to the 10-01 and 10-08 sends. It has the same "Introducing our new cards" teal hero and the same "up to 200,000 bonus points" headline. A subscriber who has seen those would read this as a third copy, not as news.
- The creative is clean. The hero is a sunset resort with script text reading "someday, SOONER", and the cards are stacked below it in three tiers with a points number on each. The eye lands on "200,000 BONUS POINTS" first, which is correct. But the email is an acquisition ad that has been sent to loyalty members. It has no stay offer, no destination, and no member benefit. The 10-12 weekend-escape email (7/10) was stronger because it led with one concrete offer and one button.
- The "🎉" subject says "new credit cards are here," but I couldn't see any launch-day news that the earlier sends hadn't already carried. The card names and the 125K, 180K and 200K figures are readable. However, four orange "LEARN MORE" buttons and a small-business banner dilute the single message. I'd call it competent but repetitive, around a 5.

## What's working

- The "OFFERS UP TO 200,000 BONUS POINTS" headline is large and high-contrast, so the offer is the first thing you see.
- The three cards each get their own block with a photo, a card image, a bold points figure and a one-line benefit. They are easy to scan and compare.
- The hero imagery is aspirational. The sunset resort and the "someday, SOONER" line tie points to travel and not just to a card.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images and consistent orange CTA styling.
- The brand-portfolio footer and the app-download block keep the IHG identity strong.

## What's weak

- The creative nearly duplicates the 10-01 and 10-08 sends, so the "new cards are here" claim feels stale.
- "Up to 200,000" and the "Learn more" CTAs hide the real ask. There is no spend threshold or annual fee visible in the body, and the fine print sits in a very long legal block at the bottom.
- Every button says "LEARN MORE". The copy is generic, not a verb plus a noun such as "Compare cards".
- The small-business banner is a fourth offer on top of the three cards, and it dilutes the focus.
- Nothing in the email speaks to the member's status or recent activity, so there's no loyalty benefit and no personalization.
- The email is long. It stacks three cards, a small-business section, the app, about 20 brand logos and a wall of terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one card, the 200K Premier Select or whichever fits the segment, and run one hero, one button and one deadline. Send the comparison of all three on the landing page.
- 2. Replace "Learn more" with "Compare the 3 cards" or "Get 200,000 points". State the annual fee and the spend requirement near the offer.
- 3. Add a real reason to open now: a deadline, or a clear "new" detail. Right now the novelty claim has been used up.
- 4. Segment the send. Send members who ignored the 10-01 and 10-08 emails a different message, and stop sending three card emails to the same people in three weeks.
- 5. Remove the small-business block and cut the brand logo wall to shorten the email.
- **Subject Alt A:** `200,000 points: which IHG card fits you?`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Premier, Premier Select or Traveler: pick yours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Compare all three cards and see which welcome offer fits how you travel.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 200,000 bonus points. Terms apply. See annual fees and requirements.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML has real defects: a malformed open-tracking pixel URL, an `http://` pixel, markup before the DOCTYPE, and an invalid CSS selector. Most of the compliance and authentication warnings come from the relay and the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed as sender faults. QA pass rate was 43%, with 0 errors and 4 warnings.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed pixel URL (confirmed):** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=551899&10/15/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - The last query segment is a date merge value with no parameter name.
  - It also contains unencoded spaces and slashes, so it looks like a template bug where a `name=` prefix was dropped.
  - Strict parsers or proxies may truncate or reject the URL, which would cause open-tracking loss.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel (confirmed):** the same `mi.ihg.com` pixel loads over `http://`. It can be blocked as mixed content or rewritten by image proxies. The HTTPS-served `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel does not have this problem.
- **Identifier in the pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/551899/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` puts a CRM record ID in the path. The `0037` prefix indicates a Salesforce Contact ID. This is typical for tracking, but it is a recipient-level identifier in a URL.
- **Tracking links not probed:** 24 links were skipped because they are click-redirect domains, so their destinations and status codes are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content before DOCTYPE (confirmed):** the ADA comment blocks and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC …>`.
  - A DOCTYPE that is not first can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
  - The hidden div is empty and sits outside `<html>`, so it can't serve as a preheader.
- **Invalid selector (confirmed):** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` or `#`, so it targets a nonexistent element. It should be `.ReadMsgBody`. The effect is minor and limited to legacy Outlook.com.
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** both tracking images lack `alt` (the `0037y00002BeqgfAAB` pixel and the `mi.ihg.com` `o.gif`). Decorative or tracking images should use `alt=""` explicitly. Because the `alt` attribute is absent, some screen readers may announce the filename or URL.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` should be `&amp;`. This is cosmetic and low risk.
- **Not an issue:** `<html lang="en">` is present, and the font stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica if the Google Fonts `<link>` is blocked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved tokens such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, or `[FIRSTNAME]` appear in the visible source.
- The nameless date segment in the `mi.ihg.com` URL (see section 2) suggests a merge value was rendered into a query string incorrectly.
- Only the head and CSS were visible, so body personalization can't be assessed.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. QA notes AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sender (`mc.ihg.com`) before treating it as a defect.
- **Physical address:** not detected. The HTML is truncated before the footer, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** absent, which is expected via the relay. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown from the data provided.
- **Not flagged:** the From domain `mc.ihg.com` is a consistent branded sending subdomain.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. All 24 click links are wrapped in tracking redirects that were skipped, and the destinations are not visible in the truncated source.
- No evidence of broken or mismatched destinations either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `mi.ihg.com` pixel template. Restore the parameter name, URL-encode the date value, and serve the pixel over `https://`.
2. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line of the document. Put any preheader div inside `<body>` with real preheader text.
3. Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, and add explicit `width="1" height="1"` if they are missing.
5. Escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`.
6. Confirm from raw headers that `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` are present. Also confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `mc.ihg.com`, and check that the footer contains a physical address.
7. Rerun the audit with the full HTML and resolved redirect destinations so UTM and landing-page checks can run.
