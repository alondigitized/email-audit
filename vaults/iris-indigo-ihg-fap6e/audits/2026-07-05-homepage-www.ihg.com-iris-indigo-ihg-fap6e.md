# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-07-05
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- IHG is a brand I genuinely have loyalty to — I'm watching those Accelerate offers, I book direct, I care about the Hotel Indigo and Kimpton tier. But this homepage greeted me with a full-screen cookie consent wall before I could see a single piece of content. The booking widget is peeking out at the top, the logo is there, and there's a "Sign in / Join for free" button — but everything else has been swallowed by a giant orange-accented GDPR/tracking modal. It's a functional dead-end as a first impression.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" CTA in the header** — it's small but it's there, and for a returning One Rewards member like me, it's a reflex tap before I do anything else. At least the entry point exists.
- **Booking widget visible behind the modal** — I can see the DESTINATION, CHECK IN dates (07/04–07/05), and ROOMS & GUESTS fields. The intent to surface search-first is correct for a hotel brand.
- **IHG Hotels & Resorts wordmark** — clean, legible, instantly recognizable at the top left. No brand confusion.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent modal blocking everything** — this is the entire story. I cannot see a hero image, an Accelerate offer, a Hotel Indigo feature, a Kimpton spotlight — nothing. The modal has three CTAs and a wall of legal text, and it's the first thing I interact with. That's a conversion killer.
- **No loyalty or Accelerate offer surface** — I came here specifically because I track IHG's quarterly offers. There is zero of that above the fold, not even a teaser.
- **Cookie modal copy is dense and lawyerly** — "session replay tracking," "cross-context behavioral advertising," "important waivers" — this is not the tone of a brand that sells boutique hotel experiences. It reads like a EULA, not a welcome mat.
- **No imagery whatsoever** — I could be on any travel booking site. There is nothing here that says Hotel Indigo lobby, Kimpton rooftop bar, or Six Senses garden.
- **Booking widget dates look stale** — 07/04/2026 → 07/05/2026 is a one-night check-in. Pre-populated dates can anchor the wrong trip length before I've even typed a destination.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie modal to a bottom banner or slide-in** — the EU consent requirement is real, but it doesn't have to nuke the entire first impression. A persistent bottom bar with "Continue" lets me see the hero and offers while still getting legal coverage.
- **Surface an Accelerate or 4th-night-free teaser above the fold** — even a single persistent strip ("Member offer: 4th night free — Book direct") would immediately signal to loyalty members that direct booking is worth it.
- **Put a Hotel Indigo or Kimpton lifestyle image in the hero** — not a generic infinity pool, but something that reads boutique: a tile mosaic lobby, a locally sourced cocktail, a neighborhood-specific mural. That's who I'm here for.
- **Clear the pre-populated booking dates or default to a +14 day window** — let me land on a blank destination field; don't anchor me to yesterday's dates.
