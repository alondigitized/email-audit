# Save up to 30% on stays for a limited time
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean Cyber Monday-season send. The teal header reads "CYBER-EXCLUSIVE SALE / Members save up to 30%" with a red "BOOK NOW" button above a large resort hero image. The offer and the CTA are obvious, and the hierarchy works. It follows the 10-27 "Destination Deals: Save up to 30% right now" send almost exactly, with the same 30% number and the same teal-and-red template.
- The weak spot is the body. It has a second "BOOK NOW" button, then a zigzag of small image-and-text blocks: 30% off at participating hotels, up to 60% off at Iberostar, and 150K points on credit cards. Those blocks compete with the main offer. The credit-card block is the same acquisition pitch IHG has sent six times since 10-01. The brand-logo wall and the long legal block add length without adding a reason to click. The fine print is also dense: book by December 3, 2026, stay November 21, 2026 to April 30, 2027.
- My judgement: solid and clear, but a repeat. Nothing here is new to someone who got the 10-27 email, and the secondary blocks dilute the hero.

## What's working

- The hero states one clear offer, "Members save up to 30%", with a red BOOK NOW button right under it.
- The teal and red palette makes the CTA stand out against the header.
- The "Cyber-exclusive" framing fits the season.
- The dates are specific: book by December 3, stay November 21 through April 30.
- The resort photography is strong and gives the email some warmth.

## What's weak

- The hero image carries a second "CYBER EXCLUSIVE SALE / MEMBERS SAVE MORE" overlay that repeats the header, so the message is said twice.
- The body's three side-by-side blocks (30%, 60%, 150K points) split attention across three offers. The 60% Iberostar offer undercuts the 30% headline.
- The CTA links in the blocks are small, plain text ("Book now", "Learn more") and easy to miss.
- The credit-card promo and the app download don't belong in a sale email.
- The brand-logo wall and the long terms block make the email very long, and the legal text is tiny.
- Nothing is personalized, and there is no social proof or pricing example.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the secondary blocks and the logo wall. Keep the hero, one sub-offer (Iberostar 60%) and one footer CTA.
- 2. Replace "up to 30%" with a concrete example, such as "Hotel Indigo from $X/night". Specific prices drive clicks better than ranges.
- 3. Remove the duplicate overlay text from the hero image.
- 4. Put the deadline in the hero: "Book by Dec 3".
- 5. Move credit-card and app promos to their own sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Cyber sale: up to 30% off, book by Dec 3`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: 30% off stays through April`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book by Dec 3 for stays Nov 21–Apr 30, plus up to 60% off at Iberostar.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member rate is live. Pick a hotel and lock it in.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has several code-level defects: a malformed open-tracking pixel, a mis-scoped CSS selector, and content ahead of the DOCTYPE. The three "broken experience" FAILs on the "Terms apply." link look like false positives caused by a nested OneLink URL and bot blocking. Unsubscribe and authentication compliance could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **"Doubled-domain" FAILs (2 of 3 flags): likely false positives.** The "Terms apply." URL is an AppsFlyer OneLink (`https://ihg.onelink.me/ihg?...`). It deliberately embeds fallback destinations as query values: `af_ios_url`, `af_android_url` and `af_web_dp` all equal `https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/cybersale`. That is the standard OneLink pattern, not a merge error. The outer host (`ihg.onelink.me`) and the embedded host (`www.ihg.com`) are different, so there is no concatenated or duplicated host.
- **403 on the same URL: probably bot blocking, not a dead link.** OneLink commonly rejects non-browser user agents, and the 403 is the only evidence of a problem. Re-test with a mobile UA and a real browser before treating it as broken.
- **Open pixel `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=566416&11/19/2026 12:00:00 AM` is malformed.**
  - It uses plain `http://`, so it can be blocked as mixed content or rewritten by proxies.
  - A date-time merge value (`11/19/2026 12:00:00 AM`) is injected as a bare query key. It contains unencoded spaces and slashes and has no `=`. Parsers may truncate it or reject the request.
- **Second pixel `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/566416/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`** carries a per-recipient identifier in the path. This is normal for tracking, but the image has no alt text.
- **30 tracking links were skipped** by the HTTP probe, so their resolution is unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** HTML comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>`. Some clients and parsers can fall into quirks mode as a result.
- **Selector typo.** The CSS has `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` with no leading dot. It targets a nonexistent element, so the Outlook.com fix never applies.
- **Both tracking pixels lack alt text.** Use `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` and `style="display:block"`.
- **Doctype and markup mismatch.** The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional but the markup uses XHTML-style self-closing `<meta ... />` tags. This is harmless in most clients but inconsistent.
- **Cannot verify.** The HTML is truncated, so alt text on content images, the `u+#body` rule's dependency on `id="body"`, and the "ADA ALT/Aria" comment blocks (which look empty) could not be checked.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved tokens.** I saw no `%%...%%` or `{{...}}` placeholders in the visible source or QA data.
- **Date value leaked into the pixel URL.** The pixel's `11/19/2026 12:00:00 AM` is a merge-field date rendered without URL-encoding or a parameter name (see section 2).
- **Doubled-domain merge error (FAIL #3): likely false positive.** It is the same OneLink pattern as above.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not found.** The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a violation. Check the raw message at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sender is `IHGOneRewards@mc.ihg.com`, so verify alignment with that subdomain at the source.
- **Not verifiable.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall in the truncated portion of the HTML.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **No `utm_*` parameters.** Attribution uses `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-Cyber2026_20262961-_-SENDURLID-_-566416` and AppsFlyer's `pid=Email&c=Cyber2026`. This is internally consistent: campaign ID `566416` matches across `mi_ecmp`, `trso` and the pixel path.
- **Possible attribution loss on the web fallback.** `af_web_dp` points to `.../deals/hotel-offers/cybersale` with no `cm_mmc` or UTM parameters. Unless OneLink forwards the original query string, desktop and web users would land without the campaign code. This needs a test, not a confirmed defect.
- **Destination matches the campaign.** `cybersale` fits the `Cyber2026` campaign name.
- **Fragment `#terms`.** It sits after the OneLink query and may not survive the redirect to the web destination or the app deep link.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Re-probe the OneLink URL with a browser or mobile UA. If it returns 200 or 302, suppress the doubled-domain rule for `*.onelink.me` and AppsFlyer-style URLs in the QA checker.
2. Fix the open pixel. Serve it over HTTPS, and URL-encode the date value or drop it. If it must stay, give it a proper key such as `&sentdate=11%2F19%2F2026`.
3. Add `alt=""` and explicit 1×1 dimensions to both tracking pixels.
4. Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line and put the preheader or comment blocks inside `<body>`.
5. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC headers on the original message, not the AgentMail copy.
6. Confirm the OneLink redirect preserves `cm_mmc` and the `#terms` anchor on the web fallback. If it doesn't, add them to `af_web_dp`.
