# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-23
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and was immediately buried under a cookie consent wall that ate up roughly 80% of my viewport — I can barely see the booking widget behind it. What little I can make out tells me this is a functional booking-first site, not a lifestyle brand trying to woo me. For a brand that owns Hotel Indigo and Kimpton, this first impression is painfully generic and transactional.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" nav CTA** — it's in the top bar and easy to tap; at least IHG One Rewards is surfaced before you even scroll.
- **Booking widget is pre-populated with today's date** (05/23/2026 → 05/24/2026) — smart default, saves a tap.
- **Destination search field** — clean, large-tap-target input; the placeholder "City, Airport, Attraction or Address" covers all intent types.

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal obliterates the hero** — the entire value proposition, campaign imagery, and any lifestyle storytelling is invisible. This is the single biggest UX failure in the screenshot.
- **"TRAVEL ADVISORY" banner** — a vague, anxiety-inducing alert sitting at the very top of the page. No context, no destination, just dread. Not the vibe for someone planning a Hotel Indigo weekend.
- **Zero lifestyle signal** — nothing tells me this brand understands the difference between a Holiday Inn Express stay and a Kimpton. The page feels like a flight search engine.
- **Three buttons on the cookie modal** — "CONTINUE," "MANAGE PREFERENCES," and "OPT-OUT" stacked vertically in red and white. It's aggressive and slow. The opt-out pathway being equally prominent is unusual and adds friction.
- **No brand imagery whatsoever** — for a company with as strong a visual identity as Hotel Indigo or Kimpton, the screen is textually sterile.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the cookie wall behind a slim banner** — use a top or bottom sticky bar ("We use cookies — Continue / Manage") instead of a full-screen takeover that buries your hero.
- **Lead with a lifestyle-collection hero** — since it's May, show a rooftop bar or boutique design-hotel lobby. Something that makes Hotel Indigo or Kimpton the reason to book direct.
- **Surface the IHG One Rewards Accelerate promo** — if Q2 Accelerate is live, a one-line strip above the booking widget ("Earn bonus points through June 30") would catch me immediately.
- **Reframe or remove the travel advisory banner** — at minimum link it to a specific page or collapse it by default; it reads as alarming without context.
