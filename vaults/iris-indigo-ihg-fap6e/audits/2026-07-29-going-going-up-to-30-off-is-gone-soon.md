# Going, going, up to 30% off is gone soon 💸
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's second Summer Sale send in nineteen days — the July 10 "Up to 30% savings start now" email introduced the offer, and this one closes it out with deadline pressure ("gone soon," a countdown clock, an explicit "Expires July 30, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST"). The urgency pivot is the right creative move for a closer email, and the layout backs it up: countdown banner, red CTA, then three stacked offer blocks (Summer Sale, points stacking, Iberostar) each with its own book/learn CTA.
- The problem is the countdown clock itself — it renders as 00 Days 00 Hours 00 Mins 00 Secs, which reads as either "the deal already ended" or a broken module, not "hurry." For an email whose entire structural hook is deadline urgency, a countdown stuck at zero is a real defect, not a cosmetic nit.
- Compared to the July 10 sibling (8/10, and personalized "Iris" in the subject), this send drops the name personalization and repeats the same 30% figure without escalating the offer — a soft close rather than a strong one. Solid bones, undercut by a broken hero element and a subject line that's less targeted than its predecessor.

## What's working

- Clear visual hierarchy: countdown → "FINAL HOURS FOR UP TO 30% OFF" → red BOOK NOW button, all above the fold.
- Consistent, unambiguous CTA repeated three times (BOOK NOW, BOOK NOW, LEARN MORE) tied to distinct offers.
- Loyalty stacking is called out explicitly ("earn 24 points every 6 nights or 8X points every 6 nights through August 31, 2026") — good member-value signal.
- Geographic/date scope is specific ("Holiday Inn and Holiday Inn Express," "stays through December 30, 2026," booking window to July 30) rather than vague.
- Full brand-family footer (Six Senses through Candlewood Suites) reinforces portfolio breadth and trust.

## What's weak

- The countdown timer shows 00 Days 00 Hours 00 Mins 00 Secs — a visibly broken/stalled render that contradicts the "final hours" urgency message directly above it.
- No name personalization in subject or hero, a step back from the July 10 send ("...You've earned it Iris") that scored better.
- No visible member points balance or personalized recommendation — every offer is generic, not "based on your stays."
- Three separate offers (Summer Sale, points stacking, Iberostar) compete for the same scroll with no clear "pick one" hierarchy after the first CTA.
- No social proof anywhere in the render (no ratings, no "X million members," no reviews).

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the countdown render before send — a stalled 00:00:00:00 clock is worse than no clock at all; it actively undermines the urgency premise this email is built on.
- 2. Bring back subject-line personalization ("Iris, up to 30% off is gone soon") — it measurably outperformed in the July 10/21 pair.
- 3. Cut to one primary offer above the fold and demote Iberostar/points-stacking to a secondary section — three competing CTAs dilute the single deadline message.
- 4. Add a concrete proof point (member count, review score, or "X,XXX stays booked this week") near the hero to counter three straight sends leaning on offer-percentage alone.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: up to 30% off before July 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book by 7/30 — savings plus bonus points through August`

# Technical Audit: IHG "Going, going, up to 30% off is gone soon" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Automated QA passed 25% of checks (3 failures, 3 warnings), driven by one confirmed broken link (403), a malformed/unencoded tracking-pixel query string, and unconfirmed authentication/unsubscribe headers due to relay limitations.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Confirmed broken link (403):** The "terms & conditions." anchor resolves to a 403:
  `https://ihg.onelink.me/ihg?af_xp=email&pid=Email&c=SummerSale&...&mi_ecmp=509557#terms`
  This is an AppsFlyer OneLink smart-link with a `#terms` fragment; the redirector is rejecting the request (bot/UA filtering or a dead landingId `26q3-summersale-5` mapping). Needs verification with a real client UA, since 403s from link-shorteners can be probe-side false positives — but worth confirming manually before send.
- **"Doubled-domain" flags (2x FAIL) — likely false positive:** QA flagged repeated occurrence of `ihg.com` in the same URL. Inspection shows this is expected OneLink structure: `af_ios_url`, `af_android_url`, and `af_web_dp` are three separate parameters that legitimately all point to the same fallback URL (`https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/summersale`) for platform-specific deep-linking. This is not a merge-tag duplication bug; recommend allowlisting `onelink.me`/smart-link domains in the QA rule to cut false positives.
- **Tracking pixel query malformed:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=509557&7/29/2026 12:00:00 AM` — the trailing segment `7/29/2026 12:00:00 AM` is an unencoded, keyless value appended to the query string (spaces, colons, and slashes not percent-encoded). This indicates a date/timestamp merge token that failed to resolve into a proper `key=value` pair. See Personalization section.
- 25 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped from HTTP probing by design (informational only, no issue).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Non-HTTPS image source:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?...` is served over plain HTTP. Mixed-content/non-TLS images are commonly blocked or proxied by mail clients (Gmail, Outlook), which can silently break open-tracking for this send.
- **Missing alt text (2 images):** both are 1x1 tracking pixels (`pxl.mon-trk.com/.../0037y00002BeqgfAAB` and the `mi.ihg.com` pixel above). Low practical accessibility impact since they carry no content, but `alt=""` should still be set for HTML validity.
- No other rendering/accessibility issues surfaced in the visible source (responsive breakpoints, MSO conditionals, and webkit/ExternalClass resets are all present and standard).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `mi.ihg.com` pixel URL contains an unresolved/unescaped date-time token (`7/29/2026 12:00:00 AM`) concatenated directly into the query string with no parameter key and no URL-encoding. This is evidence of a merge-tag (likely a send-date or campaign-timestamp field) that was inserted into the template without going through the platform's URL-encoding step. Recommend auditing the ESP's dynamic-content field feeding this pixel and ensuring output is URL-encoded before concatenation.
- No other merge-token/personalization placeholders (e.g., unresolved `{{...}}` or `%%...%%` syntax) are visible in the truncated source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **List-Unsubscribe header:** not found — but flagged WARN, not FAIL, because the AgentMail relay may not be capturing/forwarding this header. Cannot confirm true absence from this data; recommend checking raw SMTP headers at the source MTA directly.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** same caveat — not detected via relay, one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM):** not found via relay — SPF/DKIM pass/fail status unknown from this data; verify at the receiving MTA rather than via AgentMail.
- **Physical address / footer unsubscribe link:** the provided HTML source is truncated before the footer, so presence of a CAN-SPAM-required physical mailing address and visible unsubscribe link cannot be verified from this data. Recommend re-running the check against full source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- CTA links use a consistent OneLink deep-link structure with matching fallback destinations across `af_ios_url`, `af_android_url`, and `af_web_dp` (all → `https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/summersale`) — good cross-platform alignment.
- `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-SummerSale_20262857-_-SENDURLID-_-509557` provides a consistent campaign-tracking taxonomy equivalent to UTM params.
- The broken `#terms` link (Section 2) is a continuity break: users clicking "terms & conditions" cannot reach the corresponding site page.
- `landingId=26q3-summersale-5` param destination/mapping could not be independently verified (requires live site check beyond available data).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or re-verify the `#terms` OneLink redirect returning 403 before send.
2. URL-encode the dynamic date/timestamp value feeding the `mi.ihg.com` tracking pixel; confirm it resolves to a proper `key=value` pair.
3. Serve the `mi.ihg.com` tracking pixel over HTTPS.
4. Add `alt=""` to both tracking-pixel `<img>` tags for HTML validity.
5. Verify List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post / Authentication-Results directly at the source MTA — AgentMail relay is not a reliable signal for these.
6. Confirm CAN-SPAM footer (physical address, unsubscribe link) is present in the untruncated template.
7. Tune the QA tool's doubled-domain rule to allowlist AppsFlyer OneLink / smart-link URL structures to reduce false-positive FAILs.
