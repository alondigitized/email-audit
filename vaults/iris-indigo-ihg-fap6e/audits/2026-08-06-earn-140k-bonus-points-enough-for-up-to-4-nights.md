# Earn 140K bonus points — enough for up to 4 nights
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's third 140K-bonus-points acquisition push in three weeks — and the math is working against them. The June 18/24 emails offered 185,000 points; July 16's "Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend" dropped it to 140,000; July 23's "Where would you go with 140K bonus points?" repeated 140,000; and now, August 6, it's 140,000 again, almost verbatim in structure and hero copy to the July 16 send. A subscriber who's been paying attention watched this offer get quietly cut by 24% and then flatlined for three weeks straight — that's the opposite of the escalating urgency a good acquisition campaign should build.
- Taken as a standalone render, though, the execution is clean. The hero does its job — "Earn 140,000 BONUS POINTS" is unmissable above the fold, the Chase card mockup is well-produced, and the "0 → +140,000 → 140,000" points-ladder visual is a genuinely good persuasion device that most competitors don't bother with. The fee ($99) is disclosed right next to the CTA instead of buried in a footer, which is more honest than most card-acquisition emails.
- The preview text is the real miss here — it's just the sender's own name padded with invisible whitespace, wasting the second-most-valuable piece of inbox real estate. Combined with the repeated offer amount and the missing personalization (earlier sends put "Iris" directly in the subject line; this one doesn't), this reads like a template being run on autopilot rather than a considered send. Solid creative, weak strategy.

## What's working

- The points-ladder graphic (0 → +140,000 → 140,000) makes the math of the offer instantly legible — better than most card acquisition emails that just state the number and move on.
- Fee disclosure ($99 Annual Fee) sits directly under the CTA instead of buried in fine print at the bottom — an honest touch.
- Three well-placed "APPLY NOW" CTAs (hero, points-ladder, benefits section) mean the reader is never far from conversion.
- The benefit-icon grid (5X gas/dining, 3X everyday, 20X hotel stays, Anniversary Free Night, automatic Platinum status) gives a concrete reason to want the card beyond the signup bonus.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The offer amount (140,000) is an exact repeat of the July 16 and July 23 sends — no escalation, no new angle, just the same number a third time in three weeks.
- Preview text is non-existent: it's literally "IHG Hotels & Resorts" (the sender name) followed by whitespace padding, which wastes the inbox real estate right next to the subject.
- No personalization in the subject line, unlike the July 10 and July 21 sends which led with "Iris" by name — a step backward in a campaign that had been using it.
- The "Your current points balance: 0" callout is a slightly deflating way to open a big persuasive moment — it highlights that the recipient has no IHG history rather than framing the opportunity more aspirationally.
- No urgency signal in the subject line itself; the credible September 30 deadline is buried in the body where inbox scanners won't see it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Escalate or vary the offer, don't repeat it.** Running the identical 140K figure a third time in three weeks trains subscribers to ignore the number entirely. Either bump it, bundle a limited-time kicker (e.g., "+10K if you apply this week"), or explicitly reference the September 30 deadline as a reason this is different from July's send.
- 2. **Fix the preview text.** It's currently dead space. Use it to state the concrete deadline or benefit the subject doesn't cover.
- 3. **Bring back name personalization in the subject line** — it worked on July 10 and July 21 (both scored 8/10) and its absence here is a regression.
- 4. **Reframe the "0 points balance" moment** — lead with the upside ("You could have 140,000 points by October") rather than a stark zero.
- 5. **Make the CTA copy more specific** — "Apply Now" is generic; "Apply for Premier" or "Claim 140K Points" ties the action back to the specific reward.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, 140K points = up to 4 free nights`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 140K bonus expires Sept 30`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $3,000, earn 140,000 points — plus 10K more for an authorized user.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `4 nights at Kimpton, Regent & more — offer ends 9/30.`
