# Your 🔑 to an Anniversary Free Night is here
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- The subject line promises "an Anniversary Free Night" but the entire rendered email is the same 140,000-point Chase co-brand credit card acquisition pitch IHG has now blasted repeatedly since July — the "0 → 140,000" points-balance visual, the 5X/3X/20X benefit tile grid, all present again. "Anniversary Free Night" only shows up as one of six small benefit tiles halfway down, buried between "Automatic Platinum Elite status" and "Redeem 3 nights, get 4th night free" — it is not the hero, not the visual focus, and arguably not even really "here" since it's a card perk, not a free night actually granted.
- This is a bait-and-click subject wrapped around stale, repeated creative. A subscriber who has seen this card pitch four-plus times in ten weeks (07-02, 07-16, 07-23, 08-06, 08-13, 09-03, 09-10, 09-20) will recognize the "0 → 140,000" graphic instantly and feel misled by a subject line that has nothing to do with what's inside.
- Judgment: this is a subject-line bait-and-switch on top of an already fatigued campaign. Score accordingly low on honesty and cadence.

## What's working

- Clean visual hierarchy: hero card art, bold "140,000 BONUS POINTS" headline, orange CTA button all read clearly at a glance.
- Benefit tile grid (5X, 3X, 20X, status, anniversary night, 4th-night-free) is legible and well-organized.
- No render bugs — images, fonts, and layout all display correctly.
- Brand assets (IHG logo, hotel family logos at footer) are crisp and recognizable.

## What's weak

- Subject promises an "Anniversary Free Night," but the hero content is 100% credit-card acquisition — the free night is a minor footnote tile, not the featured offer.
- This is the same "0 → 140,000" points-balance visual and tile grid IHG has run at least seven times since July 2, per brand history — zero creative refresh.
- No visible member name/personalization in the hero (prior 09-05 send used "Iris" in the subject; this one is generic).
- The actual "Anniversary Free Night" mechanic (how you get it, when it expires) is never explained anywhere in the visible render — it's just a tile icon and a phrase.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix the subject/content mismatch first** — either lead the email with an actual Anniversary Free Night redemption (dates, how-to, expiry) or change the subject to match the card-acquisition content. This is the single biggest trust and open-rate risk.
- 2. Retire the "0 → 140,000" balance-bar graphic — it's been run 7+ times in under 3 months per brand history; a subscriber who's seen it once will tune out every repeat.
- 3. Move the Anniversary Free Night tile up and make it the visual hero if that's genuinely what's being promoted, with a clear redemption CTA.
- 4. Add name-based personalization to the hero copy, consistent with what IHG has done in other recent sends (e.g., "Iris").
