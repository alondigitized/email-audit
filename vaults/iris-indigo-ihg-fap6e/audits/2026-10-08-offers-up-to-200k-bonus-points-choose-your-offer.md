# Offers up to 200K bonus points (choose your offer)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is the IHG credit-card acquisition email again, repackaged. It's nearly identical to the 2026-10-01 send, "Introducing new credit cards for you 💳". The same "Introducing our new cards" teal hero sits on top of a stack of three card tiles: Premier at 180,000 points, Premier Select at 200,000, and the base card at 125,000. The "someday, sooner" hero is on-brand and the layout is easy to scan. But a subscriber who got the October 1 version a week ago sees nothing new, and nothing here is about hotels or the member's own account.
- The email is clean and legible, with one repeated "Learn more" button per tile. The 200,000-point headline is a big number, but it is an "up to" figure that depends on which card you pick. The actual terms are buried in a long block of dense fine print at the bottom. There's no deadline, so a reader has no reason to act today.
- Verdict: competent execution, weak strategy. It's a fourth card pitch in about three weeks, and it follows the 2026-09-24 "Anniversary Free Night" subject that was really a card ad. This is a middling send.

## What's working

- The "Up to 200,000 bonus points" headline is large, high-contrast, and the first thing the eye lands on.
- The three-tile card layout is easy to scan, and each tile pairs one card image with one points figure.
- Every tile has the same red "Learn more" button, so the CTA pattern is consistent and visible.
- The hero image (resort pool at dusk with "someday, sooner") fits the brand's travel positioning.
- No broken images or overlapping text in the render.

## What's weak

- It repeats the 2026-10-01 credit-card lineup email almost beat for beat, which adds to card-pitch fatigue.
- "Learn more" is a generic CTA, so nothing tells the reader what happens when they click.
- There is no deadline or urgency, so nothing makes anyone act now.
- The "up to 200K" headline is only reached by one card, and the lower tier sits well below it at 125,000. A reader could see that as bait-and-switch.
- It carries a long brand-logo wall and a dense legal footer, which push the email's tail into noise.
- Nothing is personalized. It doesn't mention member tier, points balance, or an upcoming stay.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one card and one offer, not three, and add a real expiry date. Use the 2026-10-05 send ("Ding ding! Your 20,000 points offer ends soon.") as the urgency model.
- 2. Replace "Learn more" with "Compare cards" or "Earn 200,000 points".
- 3. Show a short qualifying line right under each points figure, such as the spend requirement or the annual fee, so the number reads as honest.
- 4. Segment by member tier and balance. Don't send the same pitch to the whole list on a weekly cadence.
- 5. Cut the brand-logo wall and the app-download block, or move them below the legal text.

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The IHG email has valid structure (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals, responsive classes). Its main problems are two broken tracking pixels and a malformed doctype/preamble. The compliance and authentication warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they can't be confirmed from the HTML.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Malformed open-tracking pixel (confirmed):** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=546331&10/8/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - A merge field for the send date was appended raw as a bare, unnamed query segment. It contains unencoded spaces and slashes, and QA parsed it as the "alt" text `2026 12:00:00 AM`.
  - The URL is invalid. Clients and proxies may truncate it or fail to fetch it, so opens would be undercounted.
  - The date format `M/D/YYYY h:mm:ss AM` is locale-dependent and was not URL-encoded.
- **Insecure pixel (QA-confirmed):** the same pixel uses `http://`, so it may be blocked or flagged as mixed content.
- **Second pixel with a raw ID as alt:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/546331/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
  - The path ends in a Salesforce Contact ID (`0037y...`). QA also flagged this pixel's missing alt text.
  - Putting a CRM record ID in the URL path is a mild privacy and hygiene concern.
- **Not probed:** 24 tracking and redirect links were skipped by the QA HTTP probe. Their destinations and validity are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Document structure (confirmed in source):**
  - The `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` and the ADA comment blocks sit before `<!DOCTYPE>`. Content before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
  - The doctype is HTML 4.01 Transitional, while the root `<html>` carries an `xmlns:w` Word namespace.
  - The first rule in the first `<style>` block (`ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }`) is missing its `.` or `#` prefix, so it is an invalid selector and does nothing.
- **Missing alt text (QA):** both tracking pixels have no alt attribute. Pixels should have `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"`. QA parsed alt text from broken attribute boundaries, which suggests malformed `<img>` markup, at least on the `mi.ihg.com` pixel.
- **Web font:** `fonts.googleapis.com` Montserrat is loaded through `<link>`. Gmail and Outlook ignore it, but the `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback is present. No issue.
- **`<title>`:** the title `IHG Hotels & Resorts` has an unescaped ampersand (`&` instead of `&amp;`). It is minor, but invalid.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) were visible in the truncated source.
- The date value injected into the pixel URL (see section 2) is a rendered merge field that was not encoded. This is the only personalization defect found.
- Only part of the HTML was available, so the body copy was not checked.

**5. Compliance**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** QA did not find either header. It notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified, not a confirmed defect.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC can't be assessed. The From domain `mc.ihg.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is good practice.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. The 24 links were skipped as tracking/redirect domains and the visible source was truncated before any CTA.
- The subject promises "up to 200K bonus points (choose your offer)". Whether the landing page offers that choice is unverified.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the `mi.ihg.com` pixel. Remove the raw date segment or put it in a named parameter and URL-encode it, for example `&mi_dt=2026-10-08T00%3A00%3A00Z`. Serve it over `https://`.
2. Add `alt="" width="1" height="1" style="display:block"` to both tracking pixels.
3. Move the `display:none` div and the comments inside `<body>`, so the doctype is the first thing in the file. Fix the `ReadMsgBody` selector, and escape `&` as `&amp;` in the `<title>`.
4. Verify the `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers and SPF/DKIM/DMARC against the raw message at the receiving mailbox (such as Gmail's "Show original"), not through the relay.
5. Re-run the check on the full HTML to cover the CTAs, UTM parameters, footer compliance and merge tokens. Probe the redirect chains for the 24 skipped links.
6. Check that the 57% pass rate reflects real failures, not the relay-related warnings.
