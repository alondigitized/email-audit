# Hurry! Your 20,000 points offer checks out soon. 🛎️
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-06**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG shifting gears from its recent run of credit-card acquisition and "summer sale" blasts to a hotel-stay loyalty mechanic: register, stay two nights, earn 20,000 bonus points. Compared to the July 16/23 140K-bonus-points card pushes and the July 10/21/29 Summer Sale series, this creative is cleaner and more disciplined — one headline, one number, one CTA, repeated twice, with a genuinely nice InterContinental Chiang Mai pool shot doing the aspirational work.
- The catch is cadence and consistency, not creative execution. This is roughly Iris's sixth IHG send in six weeks, and it drops the one personalization touch that actually worked — the July 21 "Save up to 30%... You've earned it Iris" send led with her name and scored well partly for it. Here, "your" is the only nod to personalization; the rest reads as a mass send with a member-invite gate buried in the fine print.
- The offer itself is honest and well-sequenced (register by Aug 14, 60 days to complete two qualifying nights), but 20,000 points is a modest ask against the 140K-185K numbers this inbox has already seen this summer — anyone comparing offers month-over-month will clock that this is a much smaller prize dressed in identical "LIMITED TIME OFFER" urgency language. Solid execution, weak differentiation, verdict: a fine email that's arriving too often to feel special.

## What's working

- Single, unambiguous offer stated in the hero: "Earn 20,000 Bonus Points / When you stay 2 nights" — no scavenger hunt to find the deal.
- The 3-step explainer (Register → Book → Stay 2 nights) removes any confusion about how to actually earn the points.
- "REGISTER IN 1-CLICK" is repeated at both the top and bottom of the offer block, so the CTA is never far from wherever the reader stops scrolling.
- The hero image (InterContinental Chiang Mai pool) is genuinely aspirational and on-brand, with a clean photo credit caption underneath.
- Clean render throughout — no overlapping text, no broken image slots, consistent IHG typography and color use.

## What's weak

- No personalization anywhere in the body or subject — a step back from the July 21 send that led with "Iris" by name and scored well for it.
- 20,000 points is a small number next to the 140,000–185,000-point offers this same inbox received in June and July; sitting them side by side makes this one look like the also-ran.
- The urgency framing ("Hurry! ... checks out soon") is generic templated urgency, identical in tone to every prior IHG send this summer — it's starting to read as boilerplate rather than real scarcity.
- This is roughly the sixth distinct IHG promotional email in about six weeks to the same recipient — even with a new offer mechanic, the sheer frequency risks fatigue/unsubscribe regardless of creative quality.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the reader's name back in the subject or hero.** The July 21 send proved this works for this exact recipient — reintroduce it here since it's the single easiest lever with a known lift.
- 2. **Contextualize the point value instead of hiding the comparison.** If 20,000 is genuinely a strong ratio for a 2-night stay, say so ("that's up to a free night") rather than leaving Iris to mentally benchmark it against last month's 140K offers on her own.
- 3. **Vary the urgency language.** "Hurry" + "Limited Time Offer" has now appeared across multiple sends this summer — swap in something more specific to this mechanic, e.g. tying urgency to the Aug 14 registration date itself.
- 4. **Space this send out or bundle messaging.** At six sends in six weeks, consider consolidating loyalty/stay offers into a monthly cadence rather than a new campaign type every week.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, your 2-night stay = 20,000 points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Register by Aug 14: 20K points for 2 nights`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book two nights anywhere in the IHG family and bank 20,000 points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A quick registration is all that stands between you and 20K points.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is IHG shifting gears from its recent run of credit-card acquisition and "summer sale" blasts to a hotel-stay loyalty mechanic: register, stay two nights, earn 20,000 bonus points. Compared to the July 16/23 140K-bonus-points card pushes and the July 10/21/29 Summer Sale series, this creative is cleaner and more disciplined — one headline, one number, one CTA, repeated twice, with a genuinely nice InterContinental Chiang Mai pool shot doing the aspirational work.

The catch is cadence and consistency, not creative execution. This is roughly Iris's sixth IHG send in six weeks, and it drops the one personalization touch that actually worked — the July 21 "Save up to 30%...
