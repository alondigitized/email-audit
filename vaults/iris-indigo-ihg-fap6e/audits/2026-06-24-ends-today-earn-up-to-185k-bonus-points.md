# Ends today! Earn up to 185K bonus points
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's final-push deadline close for the 185,000 bonus points credit card offer — the same one Iris first saw in the June 18 "Only 7 days left" send, which scored 7/10. The concept is sound: deadline urgency is the right play for an acquisition email that's been in the drip for nearly a week. The execution has one catastrophic problem that overrides everything else: the countdown timer prominently shows **"OFFER HAS EXPIRED" with 00:00:00:00** as the first thing visible above the fold. If Iris opened this email even an hour after the cutoff, the hero instantly communicates "you missed it" — which is brand damage, not conversion.
- Strip out that problem and this is a structurally solid send. The 185K headline is dominant, the two-card layout gives a clear tier choice, and the hotel brand grid at the bottom reinforces network breadth. The earn-structure breakdown (100K + bonus points shown separately) gives Iris enough to evaluate the offer without clicking through. But none of that matters when the hero tells you the deal is dead.
- The June 18 version of this email was more effective simply by virtue of showing up when the window was still open. This one compounds the same creative (no personalization, same art direction) and arrives at the worst possible moment — when a percentage of opens will always fall after the deadline.

## What's working

- 185,000 bonus points is unmistakable as the hero — the number dominates the visual at correct scale
- Two-card layout with distinct tiers gives Iris a natural decision framework (compare vs. commit)
- Hotel brand logos (Voco, Kimpton, Holiday Inn, Staybridge, Avid, Indigo, and others) do real work reinforcing network breadth
- Subject line "Ends today! Earn up to 185K bonus points" is punchy and well under 50 chars — strong inbox performer
- Earn-structure breakdown is visible in the body, letting readers evaluate the math without click-through

## What's weak

- "OFFER HAS EXPIRED" with a zeroed countdown is the first thing visible — kills trust and conversion before Iris reaches the pitch
- Zero personalization across both the June 18 and June 24 sends — Iris has been in this series for weeks without seeing her name once
- The two-part earn structure (100K + 35K or similar) is presented in a way that's slightly confusing — hard to read as a single value without parsing
- No social proof anywhere — no "X million cardholders," no member quote, nothing to anchor trust near the CTA
- Hotel logos are small and low-contrast in the footer tier — the brand breadth is there, but it doesn't land with weight

## Recommendations

- 1. **Build a post-expiry variant or kill the timer**: A live countdown is the highest-ROI urgency mechanic available. An "OFFER HAS EXPIRED" banner is the inverse — it actively tells people not to click. Solution: serve a different creative (or suppress the email entirely) once the window closes, or replace the timer with static deadline copy ("Offer ends tonight at 11:59 PM ET") that doesn't flip to an expired state.
- 2. **Add Iris's name — anywhere**: The June 18 send didn't use it. Neither does this one. A name token in the subject or the hero headline ("Iris, your 185K offer closes at midnight") would cost nothing and lift open-to-click materially.
- 3. **Consolidate the earn headline**: Present "Up to 185,000 points" as a single number up front, then explain the breakdown below the CTA for those who want the detail. Don't front-load the math.
- 4. **Add one trust anchor near the CTA**: A line like "Join 100+ million IHG One Rewards members" costs a line of copy and meaningfully reduces application friction.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, your 185K bonus offer closes tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 185,000 points expire at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Apply before midnight to lock in your full welcome bonus`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your biggest IHG offer of the year — a few hours left`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Content precedes DOCTYPE**
The ADA comment blocks and a `<div style="display:none;">` appear in the raw source before the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">` declaration. Any parser that enforces DOCTYPE-first will enter quirks mode for the entire document.

**[WARN] Google Fonts loaded via `<link>` tag**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
External CSS `<link>` tags are stripped or ignored by Gmail, Outlook (all versions), and most webmail clients. The fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is correctly specified in the `!mso` block, so rendering degrades gracefully — but Montserrat will not render in the majority of clients.

**[WARN] HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
Outdated. Some clients (notably Outlook 2016+) render differently under this doctype versus HTML5. Not blocking, but a known source of layout drift.
