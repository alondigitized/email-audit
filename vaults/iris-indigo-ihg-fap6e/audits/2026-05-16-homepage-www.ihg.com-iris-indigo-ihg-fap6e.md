# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-16
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-16**
## Executive summary

- The first thing I see on www.ihg.com isn't a gorgeous boutique-hotel lifestyle shot or a targeted Accelerate Offer — it's a wall of legalese about cookies and tracking technologies. IHG Hotels & Resorts is a serious global brand and I know it, but this homepage as captured is functionally a friction wall before I can even evaluate whether the brand is speaking to me. Beyond the modal, I can see a clean nav bar and a standard destination search widget, suggesting an experience built around the transactional "just book a room" use case, not the aspirational lifestyle-collection positioning that would actually speak to someone chasing Hotel Indigo and Kimpton stays.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" in the header** — it's subtle but it's there, and as a One Rewards member I appreciate that it's not buried; one tap to get into my account context
- **Booking widget is partially visible** — destination search, check-in date (pre-populated to today), and rooms/guests field gives me the transactional path I need once I dismiss the modal
- **No layout breakage on mobile** — the IHG wordmark, nav, and form all look crisp and correctly scaled for a phone viewport

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal obliterates the entire first experience** — I came to browse aspirational hotel content and instead I'm reading GDPR-ish legal text with three buttons. This is the single biggest conversion killer I can see.
- **Zero lifestyle or brand content visible** — nothing about Hotel Indigo's neighborhood story, Kimpton's pet-friendly ethos, or Six Senses wellness positioning. IHG is a portfolio brand and I can't tell that from this screen.
- **No Accelerate Offer or points promotion surfaced** — for a loyalty hawk like me, this is missed money. I check this site specifically when Accelerate quarters drop; there's no hook here.
- **The booking widget reads generic/transactional** — "City, Airport, Attraction or Address" is fine functionally but it doesn't whisper "boutique lifestyle hotel collection" — it screams budget OTA.
- **Travel Advisory banner** — the small triangle-warning treatment feels like low-fi UX; no detail on what the advisory is about, which creates ambient anxiety rather than informed confidence.

## Recommendations

- **Dismiss the modal on the server side for returning visitors or delay it 3 seconds** — first-time users, sure, show the cookie notice; but if I'm signed in or have a cookie from a prior session, lead with the brand experience, not legal friction
- **Add a lifestyle hero below the booking widget** — a cinematic Hotel Indigo or Kimpton room shot with copy like "Your neighborhood awaits" would immediately differentiate the portfolio from Marriott/Hilton's mass-market look
- **Surface the current Accelerate Offer in a sticky banner** — something like "Earn 4X points through June 30 — Book direct for your 4th night free" would stop me from going straight to Google Hotels
- **Add a "Brands for you" or "Find your vibe" shortcut below the search bar** — a single tap filter row (Boutique / Wellness / Classic) would route me to Hotel Indigo/Kimpton inventory without making me drill through a generic search result
