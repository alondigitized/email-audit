# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-15
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and the first thing that greeted me — before I could see a single hotel room, lifestyle shot, or Accelerate offer — was a full-screen cookie consent wall. The brand underneath barely registers: a clean nav with "Sign in / Join for free," a booking widget, and a travel advisory strip are all I can make out behind the legal takeover. IHG is pushing its compliance obligations right now, not its properties.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" CTA in the header** — it's small but it's there, and as an IHG One Rewards member I know clicking that is the right first move
- **Booking widget visible beneath the modal** — I can see the destination search field and check-in dates pre-populated to today (05/15/2026 → 05/16/2026), which suggests the site is trying to be functional; the intent is there
- **Clean, unfussy nav design** — the IHG logo, phone icon, and hamburger are minimal and don't feel chaotic even with the modal chaos happening below

## What's weak

- **Cookie modal obliterates the entire above-the-fold experience** — nothing about Hotel Indigo, Kimpton, Six Senses, or any lifestyle brand I care about is visible; I have zero reason to feel excited before I've even seen the homepage
- **Three-button cookie layout (CONTINUE / MANAGE PREFERENCES / OPT-OUT) stacked in orange** is visually aggressive — it reads as three competing CTAs fighting for attention, which is disorienting
- **No loyalty program visibility** — as someone who watches Accelerate offers religiously, I expect to see my points balance or at minimum a "member exclusive rates" hook immediately; there's nothing
- **Travel Advisory banner** is the only editorial content I can see, and it sets a cautious, administrative tone — not exactly "boutique lifestyle hotel escape" energy
- **Zero brand differentiation** — IHG's portfolio spans Holiday Inn Express to Six Senses; nothing in the visible viewport tells me this site caters to the lifestyle tier I actually book

## Recommendations

- **Auto-dismiss or soft-banner the cookie notice** — move it to a slim bottom bar that doesn't block the hero; GDPR compliance doesn't require nuking the entire first impression
- **Surface a member-rate or Accelerate offer in the booking widget** — even a single line like "Members save up to 10% — prices shown" visible in the search form would give me a reason to log in immediately
- **Lead with a Hotel Indigo or Kimpton lifestyle hero** — a neighborhood-immersive shot (not a generic lobby render) would tell me this isn't just the Holiday Inn Express booking engine
- **Swap the orange triple-button cookie stack for a single "Accept & Continue" with a discreet "manage" link** — reduce cognitive load before I've even seen the product
