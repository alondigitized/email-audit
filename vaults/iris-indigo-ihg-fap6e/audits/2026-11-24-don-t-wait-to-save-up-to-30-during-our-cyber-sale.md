# Don’t wait to save up to 30% during our cyber sale
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-idea Cyber Monday send. The teal header says "Up to 30% off ends soon" over a red "BOOK NOW" button, with a "CYBER EXCLUSIVE SALE / MEMBERS SAVE MORE" resort hero underneath. The offer and CTA are obvious, and the booking window (book by December 3, stay November 21 to April 30) is stated plainly.
- It is also close to a repeat. The 11-19 send ("Save up to 30% on stays for a limited time") used the same teal header and red button, and this one even fires the same CTA twice. The body then drifts. It mixes a 60% Iberostar claim, a credit-card promo ("up to 150K bonus points") and an app download, so the 30% message gets diluted. The long brand-logo wall and legal footer add length but no click value.
- Verdict: solid and legible, but redundant and unfocused below the fold.

## What's working

- The hero is unambiguous: a big "30% off" headline, a red button and a lifestyle resort image.
- The red CTA stands out against the teal and white.
- The booking and stay dates give the urgency a real deadline.
- "Members save more" frames the offer as a loyalty benefit.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The creative is nearly identical to the 11-19 send five days earlier. This reads as the second near-duplicate cyber email in a week.
- The hero headline appears twice ("Up to 30% off ends soon" and "Cyber Exclusive Sale"), and so does the "Book now" CTA.
- The zigzag body mixes a 30% offer, a 60% Iberostar claim, a credit card and an app download. That muddies the primary offer and the "up to" math.
- The CTA copy is generic. "Book now" names no destination, and "Book now" and "Learn more" don't tell you what you'd get.
- The brand-logo wall and the long footer add a lot of scroll after the last real CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the card and app blocks and keep one offer. Use the space for three or four specific hotels or destinations with "from $X" prices.
- 2. Make the 60% Iberostar claim the clearly labeled "best deal" tile, or drop it, so "up to 30%" stays credible.
- 3. Show a countdown or "Book by Dec 3" in the header instead of the vague "ends soon."
- 4. Use specific CTA copy such as "See member rates" or "Book a stay by Dec 3."
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11-19 send, or space the sends further apart.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Members: up to 30% off stays, book by Dec 3`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Cyber sale: 30% off, Iberostar up to 60%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stay Nov 21 to Apr 30 at participating hotels. Member rates end Dec 3.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your member price is already applied. Pick a destination.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally usable, but it has malformed markup, a malformed open-tracking pixel and a possible unresolved tracking placeholder. Two of the three QA "FAIL" items are false positives. I could not confirm the compliance and authentication items from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Doubled-domain FAIL (false positive).** The flagged URL is an AppsFlyer OneLink, `https://ihg.onelink.me/ihg?...`. Its `af_ios_url`, `af_android_url` and `af_web_dp` parameters each carry `https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/cybersale` as a value. That is the intended OneLink fallback structure, not a merge error. Both doubled-domain checks (`Doubled-domain bug` and `Doubled-domain merge error`) fire on it, so treat them as one false positive, and the checker should exempt OneLink fallback parameters.
- **403 on "Terms apply." (probably a bot block).** The probe returned 403 from `ihg.onelink.me`. The probed URL has no `#terms` fragment, while the one in the email does. A 403 from a OneLink domain usually means the probe was bot-filtered, so it doesn't show that the link is broken for recipients. It should be re-tested with a mobile or real-browser user agent.
- **Malformed open pixel.** The src is `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=569905&11/24/2026 12:00:00 AM`. It ends in an unencoded, value-less parameter containing spaces and slashes (`&11/24/2026 12:00:00 AM`). This looks like a date merge field rendered into the URL by mistake. Some clients and proxies may truncate, re-encode or reject the URL, which would undercount opens.
- **Possible unresolved placeholder.** `cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-Cyber2026_20262961-_-SENDURLID-_-569905` contains the literal string `SENDURLID`. If this is a merge token that failed to resolve, the attribution segment is wrong. It may also just be a naming convention, since `569905` follows it and matches `mi_ecmp`. Needs confirmation from the ESP template.
- **Skipped probes.** 30 tracking links were not probed, so their destinations are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** HTML comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>`. A DOCTYPE that isn't first can trigger quirks mode in browser-based clients.
- **Broken selector.** The CSS rule `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot. It targets a nonexistent `<ReadMsgBody>` element, so the Outlook.com fix is inert.
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels.**
  - `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/569905/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` has no alt.
  - The `mi.ihg.com` open pixel has no alt either; the QA report shows only a URL fragment (`2026 12:00:00 AM`) in place of the alt text.
  - For decorative or tracking images, an explicit `alt=""` is preferable to a missing attribute.
- **Insecure image source.** The `mi.ihg.com` open pixel uses `http://`, which can be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients.
- **Minor.** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` has an unescaped ampersand.
- **Present and fine.** `<html lang="en">`, a viewport meta tag and a font fallback stack (`'Montserrat', Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- Recipient identifiers are embedded in URLs: `mi_u=695150425` and the pixel path segment `0037y00002BeqgfAAB`, which looks like a Salesforce contact ID. They are resolved, so no merge failure there.
- The two suspect items are the date-like parameter in the open pixel and `SENDURLID` (see section 2).
- The subject and preheader show no unresolved tokens in the provided data.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post** were not found. The QA tool says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so I can't confirm a real defect. Check the original message headers.
- **Authentication-Results** was not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mc.ihg.com` should be verified at the source.
- **Footer.** The truncated HTML doesn't show the unsubscribe link or physical address, so I can't assess them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameter scheme.** No `utm_*` parameters are used. Attribution runs through AppsFlyer (`pid=Email`, `c=Cyber2026`, `af_xp=email`) and IHG's own `cm_mmc`, `mi_ecmp` and `trso=EMAIL_569905`. These are consistent with each other (campaign ID `569905` appears throughout), so analytics that expect `utm_*` won't capture this traffic.
- **Landing page.** The web fallback is `/deals/hotel-offers/cybersale`, which fits the "cyber sale" subject. The app deep link is `deep_link_value=nativelanding&landingId=26q4-cyber-nalp-1`, and there is no evidence of a mismatch.
- **Anchor.** The `#terms` fragment goes through the OneLink redirect. It may be dropped on web fallback, so the page may not jump to the terms section.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the open pixel. Use `https://`, URL-encode the date parameter or remove it, and give it `alt=""`. Add `alt=""` to the `pxl.mon-trk.com` pixel too.
2. Confirm in the ESP template whether `SENDURLID` in `cm_mmc` is meant to be a token.
3. Move the DOCTYPE to the top of the document. Change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`. Escape `&` in `<title>`.
4. Re-probe the OneLink "Terms apply." URL with a browser user agent, and test whether `#terms` survives the redirect.
5. Inspect the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results.
6. Update the QA checker to exempt OneLink `af_*_url` and `af_web_dp` parameters from the doubled-domain rule.
