# Up to 30% savings start now. You’ve earned it Iris
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-10**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's summer sale push — a clean seasonal pivot after the June 18 and June 24 credit card acquisition double-tap (185K points) and the July 2 anniversary free-night tease. Returning to a core stay-and-save offer makes strategic sense: Iris has been in the loyalty program for nearly two months and is overdue for a reason to actually book a hotel. At 30% off, this is the strongest pure-savings offer IHG has shown her in this history.
- The execution is competent but frustratingly broad. The beach hero and "SUMMER SALE" header land cleanly, but the email hands you a brand logo directory instead of a hotel. No end date, no specific property, no member-rate layer — just a discount floating on aspiration. The "You've earned it, Iris" framing is good subject-line work that goes completely unredeemed in the body.
- Better than the July 2 free-night tease. Weaker than the June 24 deadline close, which scored 9/10 precisely because it committed to a hard cut-off.

## What's working

- **"SUMMER SALE" hero is immediately legible** — seasonal framing lands in under a second, and the overwater/beach imagery is aspirational and on-calendar for July.
- **30% is the most specific savings offer IHG has shown Iris** — it leads in the subject and repeats in the body without having to hunt for it.
- **Personalization placement in the subject ("Iris" as the final word)** survives most mobile truncations — whoever wrote this knew what they were doing.
- **Brand logo grid** signals portfolio scale and builds reassurance without crowding the offer block.
- **Clean render, no visible bugs** — the email is well-assembled on mobile with a clear top-down hierarchy.

## What's weak

- **"Up to 30%"** hedges the headline before the click happens — if the booked rate comes in at 12%, that "up to" becomes a trust tax.
- **No visible end date** — "start now" is the weakest possible urgency signal. The June 24 close worked because the deadline was credible; "start now" with no stop date is just an evergreen banner.
- **The brand logo grid doubles as a traffic dispersal device** — it invites directory browsing rather than booking, and the most likely outcome is no click at all.
- **"You've earned it" is never redeemed in the body** — no tier status shown, no loyalty member rate, no point balance, no saved-preferences callout. It reads like a generic compliment.
- **No specific property or price anchor** — showing one featured hotel at $159 crossed to $111 would outperform every word of copy on the page.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard end date** — "Book by July 20 to save 30%" converts better than "start now." The June 24 close proved Iris responds to a credible deadline.
- 2. **Replace the brand logo grid with 1-2 specific featured properties** — real crossed-out prices ($159 → $111) at the 30% rate are a click magnet; logos are not.
- 3. **Redeem the "earned it" promise** — surface Iris's tier, her saved point balance, or her member rate alongside the 30%. Turn a generic discount into a personal one.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTA to echo the offer** — "Book at 30% off" at the moment of the click decision beats a blank "Book now."
- 5. **Compress the subject** — "Up to 30% savings start now" alone is 34 chars; drop "You've earned it, Iris" to the preheader where it adds information rather than eating subject real estate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Summer Sale: 30% off your next IHG stay`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Iris, your member rate unlocks 30% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Book by July 20 — valid across 6,000+ hotels worldwide`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You've earned it. Lock in 30% off before the sale ends.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Two hard failures require immediate attention: a 403 on the terms & conditions landing page (legally material link) and a timeout on the open-pixel tracker. The HTML document structure is also malformed, with content rendered before the `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Open-pixel timeout**
`https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/502782/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
Tracker domain (`pxl.mon-trk.com`) timed out during probing. Open-rate data for this send is unreliable until the endpoint is confirmed reachable.

**[FAIL] Terms & Conditions page returns 403**
`https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/summersale?cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-SummerSale_20262857-_-SENDURLID-_-502782&mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=502782#terms`
The anchor fragment `#terms` targets offer terms that are legally required to be accessible under CAN-SPAM and FTC promotional disclosure rules. A 403 on this URL means recipients cannot read the terms they are directed to. This is a compliance-adjacent hard failure, not just a broken link.

**[INFO] 33 click-redirect links skipped**
All CTA links route through a click-tracking domain and were not probed. Destination reachability is unverified.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Malformed document structure — DOCTYPE not first**
The raw source places comment blocks and a `<div style="display:none;">` before the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>` declaration. Browsers and email clients that enforce strict parsing may trigger quirks mode or discard the doctype entirely. DOCTYPE must be the absolute first token in the document.

**HTTP (non-HTTPS) image asset**
```
http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=502782&7/9/2026 12:00:00 AM
```
Unencrypted pixel URL. Apple Mail (MPP), Gmail (image proxy), and most modern clients will either block or re-proxy this. Mixed-content policies in some clients will suppress it silently, further corrupting open tracking.

**Two images missing `alt` text**
- Open-pixel `o.gif` — no `alt` attribute
- Tracking pixel `0037y00002BeqgfAAB` — no `alt` attribute

Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled images. Both should carry `alt=""` (empty, intentional decorative) at minimum.

**External Google Fonts dependency**
`https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700`
Gmail strips `<link>` tags; Outlook ignores them. The CSS stack already lists `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallbacks in `font-family` declarations, so rendering degrades gracefully — but the font request will fire (and potentially be blocked) in clients that do fetch it (Apple Mail, mobile).

**HTML 4.01 Transitional doctype**
Outdated. Not a rendering blocker given email client normalization, but inconsistent with WCAG 2.x accessibility tooling expectations.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent**
Not detected via AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the native unsubscribe UI. Its absence increases spam classification risk and violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (effective February 2024) for senders above 5,000 msgs/day.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post absent (RFC 8058)**
One-click unsubscribe (the POST method) is specifically required by Google and Yahoo for bulk senders. If the sending ESP (Salesforce Marketing Cloud, based on `mc.ihg.com` envelope) is not injecting this header, IHG's deliverability to Gmail is at risk.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header not captured**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status is unknown for this relay path. Cannot confirm whether `ihg.com` alignment is established. This is a relay capture gap, not necessarily a sending failure, but it should be verified at the MTA level.

**CAN-SPAM**
Physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism are standard inclusions in IHG transactional/promotional sends; not visible in the truncated source but assumed present in footer. The broken terms link (§2 above) is the material compliance gap.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM structure — present but landing page broken**
The terms URL carries `cm_mmc` attribution in the correct IHG format (`EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-SummerSale_20262857-_-SENDURLID-_-502782`). Attribution tagging is structurally correct; the problem is the 403 response, not the parameter format.

**`SENDURLID` placeholder**
The `cm_mmc` string contains the literal token `SENDURLID` rather than a resolved value (compare: `mi_u=695150425` which resolved correctly). This may be an intentional static segment label in IHG's taxonomy or an unfired merge token — needs confirmation against their UTM spec.
