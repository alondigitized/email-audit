# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-21
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and the first thing I see — the *only* thing I see — is a full-screen cookie consent modal. I can make out a booking form underneath it and a "Sign in / Join for free" link up top, but the actual brand experience, the hero, the Accelerate offers, the lifestyle imagery — all of it is buried behind a legal wall. As a loyal IHG member who specifically came here to browse, this is not a warm welcome.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" button** — it's right there in the top right. As a One Rewards member I can tap it immediately without hunting.
- **Booking form skeleton** — DESTINATION, CHECK IN dates (pre-filled to today), ROOMS & GUESTS are visible behind the modal. The form's structure is logical and familiar.
- **Travel Advisory banner** — a slim "TRAVEL ADVISORY" strip at the top signals the site is current and operational, which at least tells me it's not a dead page.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie modal as first experience** — this is the worst possible opening frame for a lifestyle hotel brand. I'm here to be inspired to book a Hotel Indigo in Lisbon, not to parse GDPR language.
- **No brand identity visible** — the hero, the lifestyle photography, any current campaign, Accelerate offers — all gone. The IHG logo is small and monochromatic; there's zero emotional pull.
- **Three-button cookie UI (CONTINUE / MANAGE PREFERENCES / OPT-OUT)** — the red buttons feel alarming and transactional, not warm and hospitable. This is not Kimpton energy.
- **No loyalty teaser above the modal** — even a thin banner saying "Member? Sign in to see your Accelerate offers" would thread the needle; instead there's nothing.
- **Modal copy is dense legalese** — session replay tracking, cross-context behavioral advertising, waiver language. For a 34-year-old who's privacy-aware, this reads as invasive rather than transparent.

## Recommendations

- **Compress the cookie consent to a slim bottom bar** — one line of copy + ACCEPT / MANAGE, letting the hero and booking flow breathe. Every luxury and lifestyle hospitality site does this; there is no reason IHG can't.
- **Swap the red CTA buttons for brand-aligned colors** — IHG's palette is warm/gold; red "CONTINUE" reads as an error state, not an invitation.
- **Surface the Accelerate offer in the nav strip** — a one-line "Your Q2 Accelerate challenge is active — Sign in to view" above the fold would make loyal members like me feel seen the moment the page loads.
- **Pre-authenticate returning visitors so the modal shows context** — if I'm a known member with a cookie already set, skip the wall entirely or replace it with a personalized "Welcome back, Iris" strip.
