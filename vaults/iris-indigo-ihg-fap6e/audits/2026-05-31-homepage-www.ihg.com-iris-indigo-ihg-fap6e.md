# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-31
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG's homepage hoping to scout a Hotel Indigo getaway and was immediately wall-stopped by a full-screen cookie consent modal that buries almost everything I came to see. What little pokes out above it — a booking widget with today's dates auto-filled and a "Sign in / Join for free" button — signals this is a functional booking engine, not a lifestyle brand trying to inspire me. Right now the homepage reads as a consent-management exercise, not an invitation to travel.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" button** — it's right there at the top right, above the modal. As a loyal IHG One Rewards member who watches Accelerate like a hawk, I appreciate that the sign-in path isn't buried.
- **Booking widget with today's date pre-filled** — the widget (Destination, Check In 05/31/2026 → 06/01/2026, Rooms & Guests) is partially visible above the cookie overlay, which at least tells me I'm in the right place to search. The date auto-population is a small thoughtful touch.
- **Travel Advisory banner** — visible immediately; I respect that operationally critical info gets top billing.

## What's weak

- **Full-screen cookie consent modal** — this thing swallows the entire above-the-fold experience. There is no hero, no inspiration, no lifestyle imagery, no brand voice — just dense legal copy and three orange/white buttons. For a brand trying to compete with Marriott Bonvoy's polished mobile experience, this is a rough first impression.
- **No lifestyle signal for boutique brands** — not a single pixel of Hotel Indigo, Kimpton, or Six Senses positioning is visible. I don't feel spoken to as a lifestyle-collection traveler at all.
- **No Accelerate / seasonal offer** — I watch IHG's quarterly promotions closely. If there's a current Accelerate window or a 4th-night-free offer running right now, the modal has completely buried it.
- **The cookie modal copy is overwhelming** — the wall of text ("We use cookies and other tracking technologies, including session replay tracking...") is dense and anxiety-inducing on a small screen. It's not a gentle opt-in; it feels like a legal deposition.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie banner to a bottom strip** — use a slim persistent bar (destination + two buttons) instead of a full viewport takeover. Let the hero breathe. Every major hotel brand (Marriott, Hyatt) does this; IHG is behind.
- **Put a lifestyle hero image behind the modal** — even partially visible Hotel Indigo or Kimpton imagery bleeding through the semi-transparent overlay would signal "this brand is for you" before I dismiss the consent prompt.
- **Surface the Accelerate offer or One Rewards member rate** in the booking widget label — something like "Sign in for member rates" directly in the Destination field or adjacent to the check-in row would convert returning members before they even clear the modal.
- **Simplify cookie CTA copy** — replace the paragraph of legal text with two sentences max and a clear "Accept" / "Customize" split — this is standard GDPR-compliant UX that doesn't have to feel like a legal threat.
