# Introducing our new and refreshed cards for you 💳
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth IHG credit-card acquisition send in about a month. It follows "Introducing new credit cards for you 💳" (10-01), "Offers up to 200K bonus points" (10-08), and "Our new credit cards are here 🎉" (10-15). The creative is almost the same each time: a teal hero reading "Introducing our new premium cards, offers up to 200,000 bonus points" and a "someday, sooner" image. The subject adds "and refreshed" to the 10-15 line, and a subscriber would see that as the same pitch again.
- The body is a Premier vs. Premier Select comparison. It has two card images, 180K and 200K bonus-point tiers, annual fees of $150 and $350, and a long benefit grid covering earn rates, elite status, dining and travel multipliers, and an anniversary night. The information is useful and the two-column layout is easy to scan. But the email is very long, the benefit grid is dense, and the offer is conditional on spending $5,000 in three months. The hero is also dark and busy, and the 200,000 figure is the only thing that stands out.
- Overall it's a competent but fatigued send. I'd put it at the low end of IHG's recent range, well under the clean 10-12 "Last-minute savings" email. A rewards member with no interest in a Chase card gets nothing here. The cadence is the main problem.

## What's working

- The hero states one big number, "UP TO 200,000 BONUS POINTS", in large white caps.
- The side-by-side comparison of Premier and Premier Select makes the tradeoff between the $150 and $350 fees easy to see.
- Each column has its own orange "LEARN MORE" button, so the CTA is clear and appears twice.
- Check marks and icon badges (24X, 28X, 5X, 6X) break up the benefit grid and make it scannable.
- The mid-email lifestyle photo, with the extra 10,000 points for adding a loved one, gives a second hook that isn't buried.

## What's weak

- This is the fifth near-identical card pitch in a month, so the "Introducing" and "new" framing is stale. Compare the 10-01 and 10-15 sends.
- The email is very long. The comparison table, then a second card hero, then an app strip, then a full brand-logo wall, then dense legal copy push the real CTA far down.
- The offer carries a lot of fine print. The "$5,000 in the first 3 months" requirement, the asterisks and the "Offer Details" and "Pricing & Terms" links keep the headline from feeling honest.
- The body doesn't mention the member's own status or points balance. Nothing is personalized to a rewards member, and the hero doesn't say why now.
- The hero image is dark and the "someday, SOONER" script is hard to read against it, so the lifestyle photo adds little.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a fifth card email in a month. Make this a single consolidated send, or segment it to people who haven't already received the earlier ones.
- 2. Lead with one card and one offer. Put the hero as "180,000 points after $5,000 in 3 months" with one button, and move the comparison below it.
- 3. Cut the benefit grid to 3 rows (earn rate, free night, elite status) and link out to the rest.
- 4. Put the spend requirement and deadline (apply by 11/18/2026) in the hero, not only in the small print.
- 5. Remove the brand-logo wall and shrink the legal block, or move them to a footer link.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Premier vs. Premier Select: which IHG card fits?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `180K or 200K bonus points. Compare fees, perks and free nights.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Apply by 11/18 and earn bonus points on your next stay.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound and uses table layout with responsive CSS. Its main problems are an unsecured, malformed open-tracking pixel, widespread missing `alt` attributes, and markup placed ahead of the DOCTYPE. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed because the relay didn't capture the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Insecure open pixel:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=555619&10/22/2026 12:00:00 AM` uses plain HTTP. Clients may block it as mixed content or rewrite it, so opens could be undercounted.
- **Malformed query string:** the pixel URL has an unencoded date/time with spaces and slashes (`10/22/2026 12:00:00 AM`) and no `key=` name. That looks like a template value injected without URL-encoding, and some parsers may truncate or reject it. The QA tool's odd image label "2026 12:00:00 AM" comes from this parsing break.
- **Second pixel:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/555619/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` is a third-party tracker. The path ends in what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (`0037…` prefix), a per-recipient identifier exposed in a URL.
- **Unverified links:** 27 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the probe, so none of their destinations are verified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** 17 flagged images have no `alt`. Repeated sources are `image.mc.ihg.com/lib/fe32117171640478701372/m/10/437f94d8-fece-409a-8591-32f0b6ae72ad.png` (9 or more instances) and `46206e90-ead8-4438-8538-454bb1324cf1.png` (twice). Others are `24aac9ff-…`, `486d3934-…` and `e3691fe8-…`. If these are decorative, they need an explicit `alt=""`, and the two pixels should have it too. If any carry content, such as card names, they need descriptive alt text. The truncated source doesn't let me tell which.
- **Markup before the DOCTYPE:** HTML comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN"…>`. Content before the DOCTYPE can trigger quirks mode in some clients.
- **Empty preheader:** the hidden div holds only whitespace, so inbox preview text will fall back to body text.
- **ADA comment blocks:** the "ADA ALT Block" and "ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block" comments are empty, so no accessibility attributes were injected.
- **External font:** `fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat` is requested, with an Arial/Helvetica fallback in the font stack. This is acceptable.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens such as `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` appear in the visible source.
- The pixel date value `10/22/2026 12:00:00 AM` appears to be a raw timestamp merge with no parameter name. See section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. The sender (`mc.ihg.com`, Salesforce Marketing Cloud) normally sets them.
- **Authentication-Results:** not present, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is also likely a relay artifact.
- **Footer:** the truncated source doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm the physical address or in-body unsubscribe link.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot assess. The truncated source doesn't show any destination URLs, and the 27 tracking links were not followed. There's no evidence of UTM parameters or landing-page alignment either way.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Serve the open pixel over HTTPS and URL-encode or name the date parameter (for example `&ts=10%2F22%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM`).
2. Add `alt=""` to decorative images and the tracking pixels, and descriptive `alt` to content images, starting with `437f94d8-….png` and `46206e90-….png`.
3. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line of the document and put the preheader text inside `<body>`.
4. Fill the empty preheader div with real preview text.
5. Review whether a raw Salesforce Contact ID belongs in the third-party pixel path.
6. Re-run the check against raw `.eml` headers to confirm List-Unsubscribe, RFC 8058 one-click, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC. Also resolve the redirect chains to verify landing pages and UTMs.
