# Only 7 days left to earn up to 185K bonus points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-18**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's first credit card acquisition email in Iris's journey — a meaningful shift from the eight-email welcome series that stayed squarely in loyalty-program onboarding. The offer is genuinely compelling: 185,000 bonus points to open a co-branded card, broken into a clear earning ladder. The subject is the strongest IHG has sent Iris since the series began.
- The email is fatally undermined by one thing visible at the very top of the render: the countdown timer reads **00 00 00 00** and the label above it reads **"OFFER HAS EXPIRED."** Whatever the send timing intended, Iris opens this to see a dead offer before she reads a word of body copy. The 185K points story below the fold can't recover from that first impression. Everything else in this email is well-executed by IHG standards; the expired-timer problem is the entire story here.
- The card brands and hotel logos at the bottom pad out the email without adding decision-relevant information. The personalization that IHG used in earlier sends — "Iris" in subject and body — is entirely absent here, which is a step backward for a series that had started to feel like it knew its subscriber.

## What's working

- **Subject line is the best in the series** — "Only 7 days left to earn up to 185K bonus points" is concrete, urgent, and specific. Strongest open-driver IHG has sent since "👀 Check out your benefits, Iris."
- **185,000 points is displayed prominently three times** — in the hero, in the mid-body summary, and in the card benefits callout. The number is the hook and IHG doesn't hide it.
- **The earning ladder (100K + 30K + remainder)** breaks the 185K into achievable milestones rather than leaving it as an abstract lump-sum promise.
- **IHG credit card visual is clean and recognizable** — card image mid-body gives the offer a physical anchor; it doesn't feel like an abstract points promo.
- **Hotel brand grid at the bottom** connects points to redemption value for a loyalty member, even if it's overly long.

## What's weak

- **"OFFER HAS EXPIRED" is the first thing Iris sees.** The live countdown timer hit zero before (or as) the screenshot was captured. This is not a small cosmetic bug — it reads as a broken, untrustworthy email.
- **No personalization anywhere.** Every prior IHG email used "Iris" in the subject or hero. This one drops it entirely, making it feel like a mass blast rather than a continuation of her onboarding.
- **No urgency inside the body.** The subject says "7 days left" but the body doesn't reinforce a deadline date or end date — the only urgency signal was the now-zeroed timer.
- **Hotel brand logo grid is generic filler.** Sixteen hotel brand logos at the bottom don't help Iris decide to apply for the card — they belong on a brand overview page, not a card-offer email.
- **CTA copy is too small to confirm at render size**, and there's no secondary "learn more" path for a subscriber who wants card details before committing.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the live countdown or set a safe fallback.** A timer that renders "OFFER HAS EXPIRED" destroys the email. Either replace with a static deadline ("Offer ends June 25") or configure a fallback state that shows the original 7-day message if the timer resolves past deadline. This is the single highest-impact fix.
- 2. **Restore first-name personalization in subject and hero.** The series built a personal relationship through seven emails; this card offer should continue that. "Iris, only 7 days left..." outperforms the generic version.
- 3. **Add an explicit expiry date in the body copy.** "This offer expires June 25, 2026" anchors urgency without depending on a timer. Static copy doesn't break.
- 4. **Cut the hotel brand grid or replace with a single aspirational redemption example.** "Your 185K points = 3 nights at a Kimpton" converts better than a logo wall.
- 5. **Add a CTA for people who want to compare card tiers** before applying — a "See card details" secondary link reduces drop-off from undecided subscribers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, 185K bonus points — offer ends in 7 days`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your IHG card offer: 185,000 points, 7 days left`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 100K points after first purchase — here's the full breakdown`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `185K points could cover multiple award nights. See how to earn them.`

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**

- **DOCTYPE placement bug:** `<div style="display:none;">` and ADA comment blocks appear *before* `<!DOCTYPE HTML>`. The doctype must be the first token; content preceding it forces browsers into quirks mode.
- **7 images missing `alt` text**, including decorative tracking pixels (acceptable) but also at least one content image (`437f94d8-fece-409a-8591-32f0b6ae72ad.png` referenced 3×). Screen readers will announce the filename.
- **HTTP image source** (`http://mi.ihg.com/...o.gif`) will be blocked by Gmail, Outlook.com, and Apple Mail when loaded in a secure context, breaking open-tracking for those clients.
- Google Fonts (`fonts.googleapis.com`) loads over `<link>` — blocked by most email clients; fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is correctly defined.

No unfired or exposed merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `*|FNAME|*`) detected in the provided HTML. Loyalty ID and campaign ID appear correctly substituted in tracking pixel params (`mi_u=695150425`, `mi_ecmp=492499`).
