# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-20
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-20**
## Executive summary

- The very first thing I see on www.ihg.com is a full-screen cookie consent modal that buries every piece of actual homepage content underneath it. I can't see a hero, a campaign, a loyalty pitch — nothing. The IHG brand, for all its lifestyle-collection potential, has led with legalese instead of aspiration. Not a great first impression for someone who books direct specifically because she wants to feel like a guest, not a compliance checkbox.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" in the header** — persistent, visible even behind the modal. Good that it survived the interrupt. For a returning IHG One Rewards member like me, that's at least a breadcrumb that the account ecosystem exists.
- **Booking widget auto-populated with today's dates** — the CHECK IN field already shows 05/20/2026 → 05/21/2026, which means they pulled the current date. Saves me a tap once I get past the modal wall.
- **Clean modal rendering** — the cookie notice itself is legible, not glitchy. Buttons are clearly labeled with three distinct options. At least the dark pattern is an *accessible* dark pattern.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal obliterates the entire above-the-fold experience** — this is the most damaging thing I can report. There is zero lifestyle content, zero hotel imagery, zero aspirational copy visible. I came here to feel excited about Hotel Indigo Barcelona or Kimpton Maa-Lai; instead I'm being asked about session replay tracking.
- **"TRAVEL ADVISORY" banner at the top** — ominous opener. It sits just below the nav and above the booking widget, and the copy is truncated so I can't read what the advisory even is. That's two alarming signals in the first 100px before I can even search for a room.
- **No loyalty surface above the fold** — as an IHG One Rewards watcher who tracks Accelerate offers obsessively, there's no points balance, no Accelerate progress, no member-rate hook. Even if I were signed in, I doubt it would show.
- **"Join for free" phrasing** — it's fine but generic. Kimpton and Hotel Indigo deserve more than bank-tier sign-up language.

## Recommendations

- **Suppress the full-screen cookie modal for returning/signed-in users** — or at minimum shrink it to a bottom banner. The modal is killing the first impression for exactly the demographic who books direct: engaged loyalty members.
- **Add a "Your Accelerate offer is live" banner for signed-in members** — surface the quarterly Accelerate progress bar in the header or a sticky strip. I literally track this via email; meeting me on the homepage with it would be a conversion moment.
- **Swap the "TRAVEL ADVISORY" opener for something brand-positive** — if there's a real advisory, put it in a collapsible strip or footer note. Leading with "TRAVEL ADVISORY" in all-caps feels like I'm about to read a hurricane warning, not book a boutique weekend.
- **Lead the hero (once the modal clears) with lifestyle-collection imagery** — not a generic resort pool. Show Hotel Indigo's neighborhood aesthetic, Kimpton's in-room wine hour, something that says "this is not just another hotel chain."
