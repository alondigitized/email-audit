# Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-10**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same 140,000-point Chase co-brand card acquisition creative IHG has now run at least four times since mid-July — same "0 → 140,000" points-balance visual, same 5X/3X/20X benefit tile grid, same "THE LIST GOES ON" card-comparison teaser. The subject line is a verbatim repeat of the 2026-08-13 send ("Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend"), and the body layout matches the 2026-07-16/07-23/08-13 sends almost pixel-for-pixel — this is fatigue, not a fresh campaign beat.
- The render itself is clean and professional: card art, points-math visualization, and benefit icons are all legible with no broken images or overlapping text. But for a loyalty-program subscriber like Iris, this is a financial-product upsell dressed as a "hotel rewards" email, not a travel or stay offer — and it's the fourth near-identical version of it.
- Judgement: well-produced creative, badly overused. The mechanic is fine once; on its fourth appearance with a copy-pasted subject line, it reads as spam-adjacent repetition rather than a targeted send.

## What's working

- The points-balance visualization (0 → +140,000 → 140,000 total) is a clear, scannable way to communicate the offer's scale.
- Card art and hero image (resort pool scene) are crisp with no render bugs.
- Benefit icons (5X, 3X, 20X, Anniversary Free Night, Platinum Elite status) are well-organized in a clean grid.
- Two "APPLY NOW" CTAs (hero and mid-body) are unambiguous and visually consistent.
- Full brand portfolio footer (Kimpton, InterContinental, Holiday Inn, etc.) reinforces program scale and credibility.

## What's weak

- This is a repeat: same subject line as 2026-08-13, same visual template as 2026-07-16, 2026-07-23, and 2026-08-13 — zero creative differentiation across four sends in under two months.
- It's a credit-card acquisition pitch, not a hotel-stay or points-redemption offer — mismatched against a loyalty subscriber's likely interest in travel content.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the visible render — no reason to act today versus next week.
- No personalization visible (no "Iris" or member-tier callout), unlike some of IHG's other recent sends.
- "THE LIST GOES ON" section feels like filler — vague teaser copy with no concrete card comparison shown.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Stop resending this exact creative.** Four near-identical 140K sends in eight weeks trains subscribers to ignore this subject line entirely — rotate in genuinely different offers (stays, redemptions, elite-status pushes) between card pitches.
- 2. Add a real deadline or limited-time framing to the offer — right now there's no urgency signal anywhere in the render.
- 3. Personalize the hero with the recipient's name and current points balance instead of a generic "0" starting point.
- 4. Replace the vague "THE LIST GOES ON" teaser with an actual card-comparison table or a single clear differentiator.
- 5. If this must repeat, at least vary subject line and hero visual so it doesn't read as literally the same email as three weeks ago.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Kimpton, InterContinental, Holiday Inn — one card unlocks them all.`
