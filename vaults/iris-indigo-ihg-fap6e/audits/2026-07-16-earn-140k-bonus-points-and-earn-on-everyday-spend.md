# Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-16**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's fourth credit card acquisition push in Iris's inbox in roughly a month — and it has a glaring strategic problem: it's offering 140,000 bonus points when the June 18 and June 24 sends offered 185,000. Iris either took the 185K deal already (making this redundant) or passed on it (making a weaker follow-up offer a head-scratcher). In either case, the offer feels like a step backward, not a loyalty reward. IHG's sequencing here works against itself.
- The creative execution is competent but not differentiated from the two previous card pushes. The hero is points-forward, the visual hierarchy is clean, and the benefit icons give the mid-section some structure. But there's a visible grammatical error in the hero support copy ("These stopovers **is** within reach"), which undercuts an otherwise polished send. No personalization in the subject — compare that to the July 10 "You've earned it Iris" which scored 8/10 partly because of the name inclusion.
- Verdict: a mechanically solid email with a strategy problem that no amount of A/B testing the subject line will fix. The offer regression is the story.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediately legible** — "140,000 BONUS POINTS" reads at a glance with strong typographic contrast against the hotel image backdrop.
- **Points progression visual** (0 → +140,000 → 140,000) does real work showing the welcome bonus math in one beat.
- **Benefits icon row** breaks up the scroll and reinforces the card value proposition without demanding heavy reading.
- **Hotel brand grid at the bottom** (Sofitel, voco, Hampton, Indigo, Avid, etc.) serves as a credible reminder of where the points can be spent — relevant to a traveler persona.

## What's weak

- **Offer regression is damaging** — 140K vs. the 185K Iris was offered on June 18 and June 24. Anyone who noticed the earlier sends will feel punished for waiting.
- **Grammatical error in hero support copy** — "These stopovers **is** within reach" is subject-verb mismatch, visible in the first scroll position.
- **No personalization in subject** — the July 10 send used "Iris" to good effect; this subject treats her as an anonymous list entry.
- **No urgency anywhere** — the subject, hero, and CTA give no deadline, no "limited time," nothing to motivate acting today over next week.
- **Cadence fatigue** — this is the fourth card-acquisition email in ~28 days. Without a meaningfully better offer, it reads as noise.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Raise or match the offer** — if 185K is off the table, explain why (e.g., "limited-time back-to-school offer") or don't send. A lower number with no rationale signals either a targeting error or a punitive sequencing that erodes trust.
- 2. **Fix the grammar** — "These stopovers are within reach" is a one-word fix that should never reach production.
- 3. **Add a deadline** — even "Apply by July 31" gives the reader a reason to act now. Without it, click intent diffuses into "I'll think about it later."
- 4. **Reintroduce Iris's name** — the personalization clearly works (July 10 proved it); this subject dropped it for no apparent gain.
- 5. **Lead CTA copy with the destination, not the card** — "Start earning toward your next stay" ties the card benefit to the loyalty loop Iris already opted into.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, 140K points is your next free trip`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn 140K + points on every purchase — offer ends Jul 31`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Apply by July 31 and your welcome bonus covers 2+ nights at our hotels.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Points you earn on everyday spend never expire — here's how to stack them.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] Content placed before `<!DOCTYPE>` declaration**
- Pre-DOCTYPE content: `<!-- ADA ALT Block starts--><!-- ADA ALT Block Ends-->`, `<!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block starts--><!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block Ends-->`, and a `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` all appear before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN">`. This is invalid; some clients may fall into quirks mode.

**[FAIL] ADA blocks are empty scaffolding**
- Both `ADA ALT Block` and `ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block` comment pairs contain no actual content. If these were intended to inject ARIA landmarks or alt descriptions, they were never populated.

**[WARN] Missing alt text — 5 instances confirmed**

| Image | src |
|---|---|
| pxl.mon-trk.com pixel | `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/.../0037y00002BeqgfAAB` |

Tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (empty string, not absent) to suppress screen-reader announcements. Content images require descriptive alt text.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
- Not captured by AgentMail relay. Without this header, Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the native unsubscribe button; Gmail also uses its presence as a bulk-sender signal. As of February 2024 Google/Yahoo bulk-sender policy, this header is **required** for senders above 5K/day.

**[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent (RFC 8058)**
- Required for Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe compliance. Its absence means recipients must complete a multi-step flow, increasing spam complaints.

**[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header absent**
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status for `mc.ihg.com` cannot be confirmed from relay capture. If DKIM is misaligned for the `mc.ihg.com` subdomain, deliverability to major ISPs will degrade.

**[INFO]** Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link presence cannot be confirmed from truncated HTML; standard for IHG but should be verified in full source.
