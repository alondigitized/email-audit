# Your 🔑 to an Anniversary Free Night is here
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is the same subject line IHG used on 2026-07-02 ("Your 🔑 to an Anniversary Free Night is here"), but the body has nothing to do with an anniversary free night — it's another 140,000-point Chase co-brand card acquisition push, the same offer that's been recycled since mid-July. That's a real bait-and-switch: the subject promises a loyalty perk, the render delivers a credit-card app funnel with a hero card image and "APPLY NOW" button.
- Visually the email is clean and on-brand — clear hierarchy, one dominant CTA color, a simple points-math visual (0 + 140,000 = 140,000) that does a good job of making the offer concrete. But this is now the sixth-plus acquisition-flavored send to this inbox in about six weeks, and reusing a subject line for an unrelated offer erodes trust in exactly the way that makes recipients stop opening.
- Judgement: competent execution, dishonest packaging. The subject/body mismatch is a bigger problem than any layout issue and should get fixed before the next send goes out under this name again.

## What's working

- Hero band leads with a single, unambiguous number ("140,000 bonus points") — no scanning required.
- The "0 → +140,000 → 140,000" points-math visual makes an abstract rewards program tangible.
- Card benefits are laid out in a scannable icon grid (5X points, 3X, 26X, Anniversary Free Night, Platinum status).
- Single consistent CTA color and copy ("APPLY NOW") repeated three times, so the eye never has to hunt for the action.
- Brand footer with the full portfolio of hotel logos reinforces scale/credibility.

## What's weak

- Subject says "Anniversary Free Night" — the entire rendered body is about a 140K bonus-points credit card, not a free night. That's a mismatch a recipient will notice immediately on open.
- This exact subject line was already used 2026-07-02 for what the brand history says was a different (also mismatched) send — reusing it again compounds the problem rather than fixing it.
- No deadline or expiration date anywhere in the render, despite the offer implying urgency ("is here") — nothing to actually act on quickly.
- This is the sixth-ish acquisition/credit-card email in six weeks per the brand history; the creative pattern (hero number → points math → benefit grid → apply CTA) is now visually identical to the July 16/23 and August sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject/body mismatch first — either write a subject about the actual 140K card offer, or make the body actually about an anniversary free night. Sending a loyalty-perk subject to drive a credit-card application is the single most damaging thing here.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the offer ("expires Sept X") — currently nothing forces action now versus next week.
- 3. Vary the creative structure from the last three 140K sends — same layout six times in six weeks reads as autoplay, not a campaign.
- 4. Personalize past the stock card image — surface the recipient's actual current points balance instead of a generic "0" if the data is available.

- **Subject Alt B:** `You're pre-qualified: 140K points + $95 credit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $4,000 in 3 months, earn enough for up to 4 free nights`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 10K more points when you add an authorized user`
