# Earn up to 17X or 26X points at IHG destinations
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-26**
## Executive summary

- This is email #5 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving three days after the May 23 "learn how to earn and redeem points" send. The central tension: the subject promises a specific multiplier offer (17X or 26X points) but the hero headline delivers generic brand aspiration — "GET MORE OF WHAT YOU WANT" — before pivoting hard into a dual credit card pitch. The points multipliers only exist because you hold or apply for an IHG co-branded card, which means the subject is accurate but slightly bait-y for a reader who thought this was about booking hotels.
- The two-card structure (Premier vs. Traveler) is logical differentiation and the brand portfolio grid returns for the third time in this series. The email is functionally clean, but it's doing double duty — part loyalty email, part card acquisition — and neither job is done with full conviction. For a new member who just joined five days ago, a credit card ask this early in the onboarding sequence feels premature.
- Compared to the May 21 "Check out your benefits" send (scored 8/10), this one is a step backward: that email was structured, benefits-led, and on-sequence. This one uses a multiplier hook to sell plastic.

## What's working

- **Subject line delivers a specific, numeric promise** — "17X or 26X" is concrete and scannable; outperforms vague "earn more" language
- **Dual-card comparison structure** — showing Premier vs. Traveler side-by-side is genuinely useful and gives a reason to read deeper
- **IHG brand grid is present** — anchors the portfolio and reinforces where points can be burned, consistent with prior sends
- **Render is clean** — no broken images, overlapping text, or layout gaps visible in the screenshot

## What's weak

- **Hero headline is generic and misaligned with subject** — "GET MORE OF WHAT YOU WANT" doesn't mention points, hotels, or the multiplier; the subject set up an expectation the hero doesn't meet
- **Credit card pitch this early in onboarding is aggressive** — this is day 11 of the welcome series; a new member hasn't stayed anywhere yet
- **The specific 17X/26X multipliers aren't visually prominent in the body** — the subject leads with this, but it appears buried in body copy rather than called out in a hero badge or callout box
- **No personalization in the subject or hero** — previous sends used "Iris" in the subject line and it worked; this one dropped it, making the message feel batch-blasted
- **The lifestyle group photo section breaks visual focus** — adds a third visual register (travel aspiration → credit card product → group photo) that dilutes rather than reinforces the core ask

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the body with the multiplier, not the card** — show "Earn 17X points on IHG stays" as the hero badge, then explain the card is how you unlock it. Reversal of the current structure.
- 2. **Bring "Iris" back into the subject** — it's worked twice in this series and drives personalization points
- 3. **Reduce to a single card recommendation** — if the member just joined, they need one clear choice, not a comparison matrix
- 4. **Add a visual callout for the multiplier** — a bold "17X" badge over the hotel hero image would reinforce the subject promise immediately on open
- 5. **Push this email to day 21+** — after the member has at least browsed or booked once, a card upsell lands far more naturally
