# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-17
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-17**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and was immediately hit with a full-screen cookie consent wall that ate my entire viewport — I can barely see the booking widget underneath it. What little I can see (the header, a search bar, a date range already auto-populated) suggests IHG is trying to be functional and utilitarian, but the execution feels more like a compliance checkbox exercise than a brand moment. For a portfolio that includes Hotel Indigo and Kimpton — brands that are supposed to feel like *discovery* — this is a cold, bureaucratic front door.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free" header CTA** — it's small but at least it's there without scrolling; one tap to start the loyalty relationship
- **Auto-populated check-in dates** (05/17/2026 → 05/18/2026) — the booking widget has today's date pre-loaded, which removes a micro-friction step for someone who's ready to book same-day
- **Three-option cookie UI** — CONTINUE / MANAGE PREFERENCES / OPT-OUT is actually more transparent than many consent walls I've seen; OPT-OUT is a top-level button, not buried in a sub-menu
- **Travel Advisory flag** — the small banner at the top at least signals the brand is paying attention to real-world travel conditions

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal is a conversion killer** — I can see virtually nothing about what IHG is actually offering me right now. No hero, no lifestyle imagery, no Accelerate offers, no Hotel Indigo or Kimpton callouts. This is the entire first impression.
- **No personality, zero brand differentiation above the fold** — the header is pure corporate: logo, phone icon, hamburger. Nothing that says "this is the portfolio that includes intimate boutique brands you love."
- **Loyalty tier / rewards balance is invisible** — for a loyalty hawk like me, there's no immediate hook telling me what tier I'm at or what offer I'm close to unlocking. The "Sign in" CTA is generic, not motivating.
- **No seasonal or campaign context visible** — I have no idea if IHG has a spring promo, a 4th-night-free window, or a Bonus Points event running right now. That's the whole reason I opened this app.
- **ROOMS & GUESTS field is cut off** — the booking widget is partially obscured even below the modal; it feels unfinished.

## Recommendations

- **Delay the cookie modal by 3–5 seconds or move it to a bottom sheet** — let me see the homepage for a moment before hitting me with compliance copy. Every competing OTA does this better. Even a brief glimpse of the hero would set the brand tone before I'm asked to make a consent decision.
- **Surface a loyalty-status teaser in the header for signed-in users** — something like "You're 1,200 points from Gold Elite" next to the Sign In CTA would give me a reason to authenticate immediately and start the session with momentum.
- **Add a lifestyle hero for the lifestyle-collection brands** — a single above-fold module that shows Hotel Indigo / Kimpton / Six Senses imagery (not a convention-center lobby) would signal immediately that this isn't just a Holiday Inn booking engine.
- **Pin one active Accelerate-style offer above the booking widget** — even a slim banner that says "Book direct: 4th night free through May 31" before the modal fires would plant the reason-to-stay before the consent interrupt arrives.
