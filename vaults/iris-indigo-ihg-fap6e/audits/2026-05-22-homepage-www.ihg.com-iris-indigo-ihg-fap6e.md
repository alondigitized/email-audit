# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-22
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and immediately got a full-screen cookie consent wall — not a banner, an actual modal blocking the entire homepage. The brand is trying to come across as a premium global hotel portfolio, but what I actually experience is a legal boilerplate document as the first impression. I can barely see enough of the booking widget behind it to confirm this is a hotel site.

## What's working

- **IHG Hotels & Resorts header** — clean wordmark, immediately readable. I know what brand I'm on.
- **Pre-populated check-in dates** — the booking widget already shows today's date (05/22/2026 → 05/23/2026), which signals the site is at least contextually aware.
- **"Sign in / Join for free" CTA** — it's sitting in the top navigation, so the loyalty angle is technically surfaced, even if I can't reach it right now.
- **Three-option consent design** — offering CONTINUE / MANAGE PREFERENCES / OPT-OUT is more respectful than a single "Accept all or leave" dark pattern.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal as the first experience** — I came here to find a Hotel Indigo for next week's trip. Instead I'm reading legal copy about "session replay tracking." This is the single worst possible first impression.
- **No hero is visible at all** — zero lifestyle imagery, zero brand aspiration, zero reason for me to feel like IHG understands my travel aesthetic before I've consented.
- **No loyalty hook above the modal** — I'm a One Rewards member who watches Accelerate offers obsessively. There is nothing here acknowledging I might already have a relationship with this brand.
- **Travel Advisory banner above the modal** — a warning banner plus a blocking modal simultaneously signals friction and anxiety, not the effortless boutique-hotel vibe I'm here for.
- **Booking widget is half-obscured** — I can see DESTINATION and CHECK IN fields, but ROOMS & GUESTS is cut off. I can't even do the one thing the page is supposed to let me do.

## Recommendations

- **Shrink the cookie notice to a bottom banner** — every other premium travel brand (Marriott Bonvoy, Hyatt) has solved this with a compact, dismissible strip. A full-screen modal screams "legal department designed this."
- **Surface an Accelerate or member offer immediately behind the dismiss** — once I tap Continue, the first thing I should see is something like "Your Q2 Accelerate offer: 3,000 bonus points at Hotel Indigo" — you have my data, use it.
- **Add a lifestyle hero targeting the lifestyle-collection tier** — a moody Hotel Indigo lobby shot or a Kimpton rooftop terrace would tell me in 0.3 seconds whether this brand gets me.
- **Persist the "Sign in" state visually** — if I'm already logged in on a return visit, show my name or points balance in the nav instead of "Join for free," which makes me feel like a stranger on my own favorite hotel brand's site.
