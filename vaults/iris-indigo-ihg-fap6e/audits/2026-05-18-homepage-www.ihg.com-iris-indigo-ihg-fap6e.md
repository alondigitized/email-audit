# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-05-18
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-18**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and was immediately wall-slammed by a full-screen cookie consent modal — before I could see a single hero image, a single loyalty hook, or any reason to stay. Behind the modal I can barely make out the booking widget with today's date pre-filled and the classic IHG logo. Whatever the brand is pushing right now — lifestyle travel, summer rates, Accelerate offers — I have absolutely no idea, because a legal compliance screen swallowed the entire page.

## What's working

- **"Sign in / Join for free"** in the top header is at least giving me an IHG One Rewards entry point before anything else loads — for a returning member like me that's muscle-memory territory.
- **Booking widget with pre-filled dates** (05/18/2026 → 05/19/2026) is peeking through behind the modal — I appreciate that the site is guessing I want to book today; that's the right instinct.
- **Three-option cookie modal** (Continue / Manage Preferences / Opt-Out) is actually more honest than the dark-pattern "Accept or suffer" banners I see on competitor hotel sites. Giving me an "Opt-Out" button without burying it is table stakes for a brand that wants my trust.

## What's weak

- **The cookie modal is the entire above-the-fold experience.** There is zero brand, zero lifestyle aspiration, zero Kimpton/Indigo/Six Senses energy. This is the worst possible first impression for a lifestyle-collection traveler who expects the site to feel like the property.
- **No hero image at all** — I cannot see any photography, so the emotional hook that should make me think "yes, I want to be *there*" is completely absent.
- **No Accelerate offers or loyalty messaging** visible anywhere. IHG One Rewards Accelerate is the biggest lever IHG has over me; it's invisible at launch.
- **"TRAVEL ADVISORY" banner** in orange at the very top is the second thing I see after the modal. An anxiety signal before anything aspirational is a rough sequence.
- **The modal copy is dense** — "session replay tracking," "cross-context behavioral advertising," "important waivers" — this is legal-team language, not guest-first language. For a 34-year-old who's privacy-aware but not paranoid, this phrasing reads as a little alarming.

## Recommendations

- **Lazy-load the cookie banner** — show me one second of the hero first, then slide the consent bar up from the bottom as a slim strip, not a full-screen takeover. Every major hotel chain that does the full-modal loses me before I've seen a single room.
- **Surface my Accelerate offer status or current promo in the header** once I'm signed in — even a "Your Q2 Accelerate offer is active" chip would transform this from a blank booking form into a personalized loyalty dashboard.
- **Swap "TRAVEL ADVISORY" in orange** for something warmer — a seasonal campaign strip (Memorial Day weekend, summer escapes) that doesn't read like a warning label.
- **Rewrite the cookie modal body copy** in plain guest language: "We use cookies to remember your preferences and personalize your search — [Continue] or [Choose what I share]." Two buttons, human words, done.
