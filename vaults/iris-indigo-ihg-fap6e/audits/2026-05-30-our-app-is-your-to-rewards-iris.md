# Our app is your 🔑 to rewards, Iris
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-30**
## Executive summary

- This is email #7 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving just two days after the May 28 "Customize your experience, Iris" preference-setting send. The concept is sound — the app is genuinely the hub for a loyalty member — but the execution leans on vague benefit language ("App Exclusive Offers") instead of showing a single concrete reason to download right now. The phone mockup hero is the strongest visual in the series so far; the copy underneath it doesn't earn that real estate.
- The brand grid makes its fifth or sixth appearance in this series. By now it reads as filler, not proof. Fourteen days, seven emails — welcome cadence has crossed into attrition territory, and an app download push with no specific hook is not a strong enough send to justify another slot.
- The critical miss: "App Exclusive Offers" promises a benefit but delivers nothing specific. A single concrete hook — "earn 500 bonus points on your first in-app booking" — would have made this worth opening and clicking. Without it, the email is a dressed-up download prompt.

## What's working

- Phone mockup hero is visually clean and immediately communicates the subject — strongest image asset used in this series.
- "THE APP MADE FOR YOU" headline is direct and personal-feeling.
- Iris's name appears in both the subject and email body, maintaining the personalized tone set in the series.
- App store download badges are visible and prominent — no friction finding where to act.
- "App Exclusive Offers" as a section label correctly frames downloading as value-unlocking, not just convenience.

## What's weak

- "App Exclusive Offers" is the value prop but zero specifics are shown — no discount, no bonus points, no feature called out.
- CTA copy "Download: Book. Getaways." is fragmented and reads like a tagline, not an action.
- The brand logo grid has appeared in nearly every email in this series and adds no marginal persuasion at this point.
- No urgency, no deadline, no reason this email needed to arrive today instead of next week.
- Seven emails in fourteen days is high; no evidence of segmentation or pacing logic to justify the frequency.

## 1. Overview

This is email #7 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving just two days after the May 28 "Customize your experience, Iris" preference-setting send. ### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**
1. Tracking pixel `0037y00002BeqgfAAB` — alt="" acceptable for a pixel, but absent entirely.
2. Open-beacon `o.gif` — same; no alt attribute present.

Both will generate screen-reader noise or accessibility warnings in audit tools. For decorative/tracking images, an explicit `alt=""` should be set.

**DOCTYPE placement issue**
- Visible content (a `<div style="display:none;">`) and ADA comment blocks appear *before* the `<!DOCTYPE HTML>` declaration. This forces browsers/clients into quirks mode before the document is even parsed correctly. The preheader div and DOCTYPE order should be swapped.

**Google Fonts via `<link>`**
- `fonts.googleapis.com` is loaded via `<link rel="stylesheet">`. Gmail strips `<link>` tags entirely; Outlook ignores it. The `font-family` fallback chain (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is correctly specified, so rendering degrades gracefully, but the web font will not render in the two largest email clients.
