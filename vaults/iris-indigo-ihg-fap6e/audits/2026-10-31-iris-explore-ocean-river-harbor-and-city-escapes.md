# Iris, explore ocean, river, harbor and city escapes...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a Regent Hotels brand-storytelling email sent under the IHG One Rewards name. It is a long editorial scroll called "The Discovery Edit": Cannes, Phu Quoc, Shanghai, Chongqing, then a Santa Monica Route 66 block. It looks polished, with a sunset yacht hero, consistent photography and a clean teal and white palette. But it has no price, no points and no deadline. It's a mood piece, not a conversion piece.
- The sequencing is the weak point. After a strong hero, the reader gets five destinations that each get equal weight and a small text link ("Winter wanderers", "Set sail", "Take a stroll", "Sip and savor"). The one block with a real offer, "Explore offers" under Route 66, sits near the bottom. Nothing tells a member why to act today.
- Judgment: a good brand-awareness send that is weak as a driver of bookings. It looks premium, but the subject's "Iris," is the only personalization. After a month of Chase card pushes (10-01 through 10-29), this is a welcome change of pace, but it asks for much less than the 10-27 "Destination Deals: Save up to 30%" send. I'd call it mid-funnel at best.

## What's working

- The hero is a sunset sailboat under a Regent wordmark, with an "IHG One Rewards" header above it. It is clearly on-brand and aspirational.
- The destination blocks follow a clean, repeatable pattern: label, headline, short copy, text link. Photo pairs and full-width images alternate, which keeps the long scroll moving.
- A member status bar sits under the logo, so the email reads as a member send rather than a generic blast.
- The Route 66 centennial block is the most distinctive and timely. It has a dark panel and a vintage-car photo.
- I can see no broken images, overlapping text or layout breakage. The footer's app-download and brand-portfolio section is tidy.

## What's weak

- There is no concrete offer, price, points bonus or deadline above the fold. Compare "Save up to 20%" (10-12, 7/10) and "20,000 points" (10-28, 8/10).
- The CTAs are small underlined text links, with no visible button anywhere. "Discover more" is the weakest kind of CTA copy.
- Five similar destination blocks compete with each other, so nothing is the clear primary action. Cannes, Phu Quoc, Shanghai and Chongqing have no price or booking hook.
- The subject trails off in an ellipsis and reads as a list of nouns ("ocean, river, harbor and city"). It has no benefit and no urgency.
- Chongqing and the Regent Santa Monica Beach block are far down the scroll, and there is an Instagram promo near the end. Few readers will get to the one block that has an offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put one member-priced Regent offer at the top. Use a real number, such as "Members save 20% or earn bonus points on Regent stays", and put it under the hero with a solid button reading "Book a Regent stay".
- 2. Cut the story blocks from five to two or three, each with a "from X points / from $X" line and a button. Move the Route 66 offer block up.
- 3. Replace "Discover more" and the other text links with verb-plus-noun buttons like "See Cannes stays" and "Book Phu Quoc".
- 4. Add a deadline for the offer, since the send has no urgency today.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based Marketo/Salesforce-style template with a resolved first-name token ("Iris") in the subject. Two open-tracking pixels are malformed or insecure. The HTML source is truncated, so the footer, body links and UTM parameters could not be inspected. The header-based compliance checks are inconclusive because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unencoded date in the open pixel.** The pixel is `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=560057&10/31/2026 12:00:00 AM`.
  - The trailing `&10/31/2026 12:00:00 AM` has no parameter name. It contains literal spaces and slashes, so it looks like a date merge token dropped into the query string.
  - The QA parser read the `alt` as "2026 12:00:00 AM", which suggests the spaces broke attribute parsing on this `<img>`.
  - Some clients may truncate or drop the URL, which would undercount opens.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel.** The same pixel uses `http://` (`mi.ihg.com`). This can be blocked or rewritten as mixed content, and it exposes `mi_u=695150425` in cleartext.
- **Record ID in the second pixel URL.** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/560057/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` ends in a CRM contact ID (the `0037y…` pattern is an 18-character Salesforce Contact ID). It is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL, so check it is intended and covered by the privacy policy.
- **Unverified links.** 32 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destination health is unverified. This is not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on both pixels** (`pxl.mon-trk.com/…/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` and `mi.ihg.com/…/o.gif`). Spacer and pixel images should carry `alt=""` explicitly. Neither element is meaningful content, so the impact is low.
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** Comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional…">`. Anything before the doctype can trigger quirks mode in some clients and browser views. The stray `div` also sits outside `<html>`.
- **Broken selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot, so it matches nothing. It should be `.ReadMsgBody`. The effect is cosmetic and affects legacy Outlook.com only.
- **Web font dependency.** `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat…">` is stripped by Gmail and Outlook. Every font stack falls back to Arial/Helvetica, which is acceptable.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` should be `&amp;`. This is minor.
- `lang="en"` is present, and the ADA landmark and alt comment blocks are empty placeholders. I found no confirmed issue with them.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The subject token resolved correctly ("Iris, explore ocean, river, harbor and city escapes...").
- **Date token leaked into the pixel URL.** The `10/31/2026 12:00:00 AM` fragment in the open pixel (see §2) looks like a date merge field rendered without formatting or encoding.
- I found no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `${}` tokens in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the source. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers for one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results missing.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, and this is a relay limitation rather than a finding. The From domain is `mc.ihg.com`, a subdomain used for sending. Validate alignment with a direct-delivery test.
- **Footer not visible.** The visible footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked because the source is truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Not assessable.** Body links are hidden by truncation, and the 32 tracking links were not followed, so I cannot confirm UTM parameters or landing-page alignment.
- Click tracking appears to go through redirect domains. Check that UTMs survive the redirect and that the pixel and click IDs match the same campaign (`mi_ecmp=560057` and the `560057` in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` path are consistent).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the open pixel: serve it over `https://`, give the date a parameter name (for example `&dt=`), and URL-encode it with `%20` and `%2F` or use an ISO format.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Move the comments and the `display:none` div inside `<body>` so the DOCTYPE comes first.
4. Correct `.ReadMsgBody` and escape `&` as `&amp;` in the title.
5. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` on the original message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC outside the relay.
6. Check that the contact ID in the `pxl.mon-trk.com` path is intended.
7. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to verify the footer, UTMs and link destinations.
