# 🚨 Last chance for 100% more points, Iris
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-13**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG One Rewards' sixth buy-points email in three weeks — following June 22, June 29, July 1, July 6, and July 8 sends that all ran the same 100% bonus creative with minimal variation. The underlying template is well-built: clean hero image of an aspirational hotel terrace, a clear three-step flow (Buy → Get 100% more → Start planning), and a prominent orange "BUY NOW" CTA that is impossible to miss. The deadline ("through July 14") is surfaced in body copy, giving the urgency teeth.
- The problem isn't the email — it's the email in context. A subscriber who has received five near-identical sends is now training their eye to skip this creative on sight. "Last chance" loses its weight when it followed a July 6 send that felt exactly as final. The 🚨 emoji in the subject also pushes into aggressive-promo territory that can trigger junk-folder sorting in some clients.
- The send is mechanically solid but strategically fatigued. If this genuinely is the last send of the campaign, the creative needed to look and feel different enough to pierce banner blindness — it doesn't.

## What's working

- **Orange "BUY NOW" CTA is immediately visible** — above the fold on mobile, high contrast, no ambiguity.
- **Three-step explanation is clean** — Buy → Get 100% more → Start planning gives reluctant buyers a mental model.
- **July 14 deadline is explicit** — time-bounded urgency is grounded and credible rather than vague.
- **Hero image is aspirational** — rooftop terrace with city skyline sets the right emotional register for a travel loyalty brand.
- **No render bugs** — layout holds, images load, no broken elements visible.

## What's weak

- **"Last chance" after five identical sends reads as false urgency** — subscribers who saw the July 6 and July 8 emails already tuned this offer out.
- **🚨 alarm emoji is a spam-adjacent signal** — paired with "Last chance," it reads as the kind of pressure-marketing that trained inbox filters flag.
- **BUY NOW button copy is weak for this audience** — loyalty travelers respond to what the points unlock, not the transaction itself.
- **Brand logo grid at the bottom is pure filler** — 18 brand logos add length without lifting conversion intent.
- **No specific destination or aspiration hook** — there's no "here's what 200,000 points actually gets you" moment to make the abstract purchase feel concrete.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Change the creative pattern entirely** — If this is genuinely last-chance, show a specific aspirational redemption (e.g., "These points could get you 3 nights at an InterContinental in Bangkok"). The previous five emails ran the same three-step graphic; a destination-led layout would pierce fatigue.
- 2. **Rewrite the CTA to the outcome, not the transaction** — "Get My Bonus Points" or "Double My Points Balance" over "BUY NOW"; the latter feels like a store checkout, the former feels like a loyalty win.
- 3. **Remove the brand logo grid** — Replace the bottom third with a single high-quality hotel image and a "Here's what your points could unlock" prompt. The logos add cognitive load with zero lift.
- 4. **Soften the 🚨 emoji** — A ✈️ or 🏨 reads on-brand without the alarm-bell spam signal.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day to double your IHG points balance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy now and earn bonus points toward your next hotel stay.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `100% more points through tonight — here's how to claim yours.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — IHG One Rewards "Last Chance 100% More Points"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a confirmed CAN-SPAM compliance failure (no body unsubscribe link detected), a broken privacy policy URL, and a potentially expired/inaccessible personalized SSO link. Authentication headers are unverifiable through the relay, and 30+ images lack alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Privacy policy URL has trailing period**
`https://www.ihg.com/content/gb/en/customer-care/privacy_statement.`
The trailing `.` makes this a syntactically invalid URL that will 404 in most clients. Evidence: QA returned HTTP 403 (server likely rejected the malformed path).

**FAIL — SSO buy/fast-track link returned HTTP 403**
`https://ssogateway.points.com/SSOGateway/gateway.do?ssoProduct=buy_fasttrack&ssoSource=ihg&...`
The encoded `ssoData` blob is a session-scoped token. This may be expired or require browser session state to resolve. If this is the primary CTA, a 403 is a broken conversion path. Needs confirmation against a live send with a valid session.

**INFO — Font preconnect links flagged as broken (false positives)**
QA flagged `https://fonts.googleapis.com` and `https://fonts.gstatic.com` as 404. These are `<link rel="preconnect">` hints, not navigable URLs — the root domain returning 404 is expected. The actual stylesheet href (`fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:...`) is the live resource. No action needed.

**INFO — 29 tracking/redirect links not probed**
All click-tracking links routed through `points-mail.com` redirect infrastructure were skipped. Downstream landing page health is unverified.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**WARN — 30+ images missing `alt` text**
Includes both layout spacers and at least two content images:
- `0612cf26-ec2e-4e9c-acd2-ad88a584c4d0.png` (via `image.points-mail.com`) — likely a hero/promotional image
- `603f2770-6bfd-45f6-8c2f-652bb772a282.png` (via `image.points-mail.com`) — likely a CTA or offer graphic
- `Shopping-cart-icon_1-...` — functional icon with no alt
- `star-icon_2-...` — functional icon with no alt

Spacer GIFs should have `alt=""` (empty, not absent) to suppress screen reader announcement. Content images require descriptive alt text.

**WARN — Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — some clients (notably webmail and accessibility tools) expose this. Should be set to the email subject or a meaningful value.

**INFO — Preheader/subject mismatch**
Preheader reads "Your next Reward Night is waiting" while subject is "Last chance for 100% more points." These serve different messages, which can read as incoherent in inbox preview. Not a rendering bug, flagging for awareness.

### 5. Compliance

**FAIL — No unsubscribe link detected in body**
QA found no opt-out mechanism in the HTML body. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(3) requires a clear, functional unsubscribe mechanism visible in the message body. If the link exists but was inside a click-tracking redirect that the QA probe skipped, this needs manual verification against the rendered email.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers not found**
RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is absent (or not captured by the AgentMail relay). Gmail and Yahoo now enforce one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders (>5k/day). This is a deliverability risk with major ISPs.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header not found**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. Sender domain is `points-mail.com` (Points.com ESP), not `ihg.com` — DKIM alignment with the `From:` header domain should be confirmed. Without this, inbox placement is at risk.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameters cannot be verified because all 29 outbound links are behind the `points-mail.com` click-tracking redirect and were not probed. The SSO gateway link (primary CTA) encodes session data directly in the URL rather than using a redirect, which bypasses the ESP's click tracking — if conversion attribution relies on that tracking layer, it will be missing for the buy/fast-track action.

No landing page UTM alignment could be confirmed from available data.
