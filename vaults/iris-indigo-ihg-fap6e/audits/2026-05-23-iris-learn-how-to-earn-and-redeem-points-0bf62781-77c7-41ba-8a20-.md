# Iris, learn how to earn and redeem points
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-23**
## Executive summary

- This is email #4 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving seven days after signup and just two days after the "check out your benefits" send scored 8/10. The subject — earning and redeeming points — is the most practically useful topic IHG could cover for a new member, and the two-part structure (earn, then redeem) is logical. The brand execution is clean, the property photography is genuinely aspirational, and nothing is broken.
- The problem is what this email doesn't do: there's no concrete hook. No "earn 2,000 bonus points on your first stay booked by June 15," no specific redemption example showing a real property and a real point cost. The content reads like a program FAQ page that was templated into an email. It informs without motivating.
- The credit card pitch embedded mid-scroll is also premature — Iris has never stayed at an IHG property. Asking for a co-branded card before the first stay is the kind of move that trains subscribers to treat the series as marketing to scroll past, not a relationship to lean into.

## What's working

- Clean two-part structure (earn → redeem) with a clear visual break and a resort/pool image placed exactly where it makes the redemption promise feel real.
- Dark hero with white type and teal CTA creates strong above-the-fold hierarchy — the eye lands on the right thing first.
- No render bugs visible; IHG's brand consistency has been solid across all four sends.
- Name personalization in the subject ("Iris") is the one inbox-level signal this wasn't a generic batch.
- The earn section covers multiple channels (stays, dining, shopping, credit card), which appropriately widens how a new member thinks about the program.

## What's weak

- No specific offer: no bonus points for a first stay, no expiring promo, no concrete redemption example with a real property and a real point cost. Educational content with no time-bound reason to click rarely converts.
- Cadence fatigue: four emails in seven days. The May 21 benefits email already covered loyalty perks — this one feels like it should have been email #2, not #4.
- Credit card upsell mid-email is too early. Iris hasn't earned a single point through a stay yet. The ask creates friction, not aspiration.
- Earn section lists channels but gives no point values — "you earn at hotels" is not actionable without a number.
- The brand portfolio grid at the bottom was already in the May 16 welcome email. Repeating it is padding.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add one time-bound offer to the hero.** "Earn 2,000 bonus points on your first stay — booked by June 15" reframes this from FAQ to conversion email without changing the educational structure.
- 2. **Show one concrete redemption example.** A specific IHG property, its cash rate, and the point cost does more persuasion work than any headline about the program. Make the math real.
- 3. **Move the credit card to email #6 or #7.** After a subscriber has earned points organically, the card benefit feels personal. Before that, it reads as a revenue play.
- 4. **Cut the brand grid.** The real estate recovered can hold the time-bound offer or the redemption example.
- 5. **Slow the cadence.** This series should be 4 emails over 21 days, not 4 emails over 7. The subscriber stops reading before the relationship starts.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Here's exactly how many points you need — and how to earn them faster than you think.`

## Technical Audit — IHG One Rewards: "Iris, learn how to earn and redeem points"

### 1. Technical Summary

Two confirmed bugs: a timed-out tracking pixel and a malformed, HTTP-only open-pixel URL. Compliance headers are absent or unverifiable at the relay layer, and the DOCTYPE placement is structurally invalid.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[FAIL] Tracking pixel timeout**
- URL: `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/459796/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
- QA probe returned a read timeout. Open/impression tracking for this send is unreliable until the endpoint is verified.

**[FAIL] Malformed open-pixel URL (HTTP + unencoded spaces)**
- `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=459796&5/23/2026 12:00:00 AM`
- Two bugs in one URL: plain `http://` (blocked by Gmail, Apple Mail, and most modern clients with mixed-content policies) and a literal space + slash timestamp appended as a bare query string component rather than a URL-encoded parameter. The pixel will not fire in any client that enforces HTTPS.

**[WARN] 27 click-redirect links skipped**
- All CTA links pass through a redirect domain; destinations and UTM parameters could not be probed. See §6 for continuity impact.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] DOCTYPE after preheader content**
- The `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>` declaration appears *after* comment blocks and a `<div style="display:none;">`. Anything before the DOCTYPE is outside the document model; some clients will trigger quirks mode or discard the preheader `<div>` entirely.

**[WARN] External font via `<link>` tag**
- `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat...">` — Outlook (all versions), Samsung Mail, and several webmail clients strip `<link>` tags from `<head>`. The `Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack in the inline `font-family` declarations is correctly set, but the web font will silently fail in those clients.

**[WARN] Two images with missing `alt` text**
- Tracking pixel: `src="https://pxl.mon-trk.com/.../0037y00002BeqgfAAB"` — no `alt`
- Open pixel: `src="http://mi.ihg.com/.../o.gif?..."` — no `alt`
- Both should carry `alt=""` explicitly; absent attributes cause screen readers to announce the raw filename or URL.

**[INFO] HTML 4.01 Transitional DOCTYPE**
- Intentional and expected for broad ESP/client compatibility. No action needed.

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers absent**
- Both headers were not captured by the AgentMail relay. Without `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058), Gmail and Apple Mail cannot surface the one-click unsubscribe UI they require for high-volume senders. If IHG sends ≥5,000 msgs/day to Gmail, this is a Google Bulk Sender Policy violation.

**[WARN] No physical mailing address detected**
- CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in the email body. It was not found in the audited source fragment. Confirm it exists in the full footer; if absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.

**[WARN] SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication status unknown**
- `Authentication-Results` header not captured via relay. Sending domain is `mc.ihg.com` (a Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain). Cannot confirm SPF pass or DKIM signature validity from available data.
