# Where would you go with 140K bonus points? 🏖️🏛️🎡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 140,000-point Chase co-brand card acquisition send IHG has now run on 2026-07-02, 07-16, 07-23, 08-06, 08-13, and 09-03 — six times in under three months — with the identical "0 → 140,000" points-balance visual and 5X/3X/20X benefit tile grid this brand keeps recycling. The subject even reuses the "Earn/Where would you go with 140K bonus points" phrasing almost verbatim from 08-13 and earlier sends. There is zero loyalty content here for an actual IHG One Rewards member checking their stay activity — this is pure credit-card acquisition marketing dressed up in a loyalty-program sender name.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, clear hierarchy, a legible hero — but the resort photo (Kimpton Los Monteros Marbella) is stock-generic and the card graphic dominates more real estate than any actual travel benefit. For a subscriber who has now seen this exact math problem (0 + 140,000 = 140,000) illustrated five separate times, this lands as noise, not urgency.
- Verdict: well-produced, poorly targeted, badly paced. Nothing about this feels earned or fresh relative to IHG's own recent cadence.

## What's working

- Clean render: no overlapping text, no broken images, no lorem ipsum.
- Clear visual hierarchy — eye lands on "140,000 BONUS POINTS" headline immediately.
- Benefit tiles (5X, 3X, 20X, free night, elite status) are scannable and concrete.
- Two CTA opportunities ("Apply Now" appears twice plus "Explore Options") reduce friction to convert.

## What's weak

- This is a near-duplicate of at least five prior sends since July — same visual, same offer, same math graphic.
- Subject line offers nothing new versus 08-13's "Earn 140K bonus points — enough for up to 4 nights" or the recurring "Where would you go with 140K" framing.
- Zero personalization visible — no member name, no points balance, no tier callout, despite this being framed as a loyalty program email.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the render — no expiration date on the offer.
- The card art and points-balance illustration take up more vertical space than any actual destination or experience content, undermining the "travel opportunity" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending this exact creative — IHG needs genuine creative rotation (different visual concept, not just a new subject line) before the next acquisition push, or subscribers will tune out entirely.
- 2. Segment: members who already hold the card should never receive this; show them points-earning activity or a redemption idea instead.
- 3. Add a real deadline to create credible urgency — "in-market" evergreen asks convert worse than dated ones.
- 4. Replace the generic stock resort photo with a destination tied to the recipient's stay history if data permits.

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
The email renders via standard Salesforce Marketing Cloud (`mc.ihg.com`/`image.mc.ihg.com`) infrastructure with heavy legacy MSO/Outlook conditional markup; core defects are missing alt text on multiple images, an insecure tracking pixel, and absent unsubscribe/authentication headers in the captured transport data.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 25 tracking/click-redirect links were present but skipped by automated HTTP probing (redirect domains not resolved) — cannot confirm destination validity from this data alone.
- Open tracking pixel served over plain HTTP: `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=536112&9/20/2026 12:00:00 AM` — non-HTTPS resources are commonly blocked by mail clients (e.g., Outlook, Gmail proxy), which would suppress open tracking for affected recipients.
- The querystring on that pixel contains an unencoded literal timestamp (`9/20/2026 12:00:00 AM`) with slashes/spaces/colons — malformed/unescaped URL parameter that may break parsing by some MTAs or logging systems.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 5 images flagged with missing `alt` text, including the SFMC tracking pixel (`0037y00002BeqgfAAB`) and three repeated instances of `437f94d8-fece-409a-8591-32f0b6ae72ad.png` from `image.mc.ihg.com` — screen readers will announce these as unlabeled images or filenames.
- Markup includes extensive Outlook/MSO conditional CSS (`mso-line-height-rule`, `ExternalClass`, `ReadMsgBody`) and mobile media queries (`max-width: 480px`) — no rendering-breakage evidence found beyond the alt-text gaps.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge-token or personalization syntax (e.g., AMPscript `%%=...=%%`, Handlebars `{{...}}`) is visible in the truncated source. No issues found based on available data.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be relay capture limitation rather than sender omission).
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not detected — one-click unsubscribe via mail client action cannot be confirmed.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Note: all three are flagged WARN (not FAIL) with the caveat that the AgentMail relay may not be capturing/forwarding these headers; this may reflect a capture gap rather than an actual sender-side compliance defect. Recommend verifying against raw SMTP headers at the receiving MTA before treating as a real compliance gap.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
The 25 tracking links were not resolved by the automated probe (skipped, not followed), so UTM parameter presence and landing-page alignment cannot be verified from this data. No confirmed issues found; flagging as unverified rather than "no issues."

7. Recommendations
- Add descriptive (or empty `alt=""` if purely decorative) alt attributes to the 5 flagged images, particularly the two content images sourced from `image.mc.ihg.com`.
- Migrate the `mi.ihg.com` open-tracking pixel to HTTPS and URL-encode its querystring parameters (the raw date value should be percent-encoded or restructured as a standard key=value pair).
- Re-run header capture directly against raw SMTP delivery (bypassing the AgentMail relay) to confirm actual presence/absence of `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` before treating these as sender-side compliance failures.
- Manually resolve a sample of the 25 tracking links to confirm they carry UTM parameters and route to matching, live landing pages.
