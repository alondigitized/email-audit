# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-07-12
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG's homepage and got immediately buried under a cookie consent modal — the entire viewport is the legal wall, not the hotel. Behind it I can just barely make out what looks like a gorgeous resort scene with a waterfront deck and chairs, which is exactly the kind of imagery that'd make me slow my scroll, but I can't get there. Right now this homepage reads less "Hotel Indigo x Six Senses energy" and more "GDPR compliance meeting."

## What's working

- **The header strip** — IHG logo, phone icon, and "Sign in / Join for free" are all visible and unambiguous. As a direct-booker, I appreciate that the member login is the first thing in the top bar, not buried in a hamburger menu.
- **The peeking hero image** — even behind the consent overlay, I can see a waterfront resort setting with natural wood, calm water, and lounge chairs. That aesthetic instantly reads "boutique lifestyle tier," not budget chain. If I can get past the modal, that scene is going to hold my attention.
- **The "Join for free" micro-copy** — smart to include "free" in the header CTA. Low-friction ask that plays well for someone like me who's already calculating Accelerate points before I even search dates.

## What's weak

- **The cookie wall is the entire first impression** — a dense legal block with three competing buttons ("CONTINUE," "MANAGE PREFERENCES," "OPT-OUT") is the very first thing I see. For a brand trying to position Six Senses and Kimpton as luxury lifestyle, this is a catastrophic opening act.
- **No offer, no hook, no urgency** — I can see zero promotional content above the fold. IHG runs Accelerate quarterly offers that people like me literally track — none of that is visible. If I'm a casual browser who isn't already loyal, I have no reason to scroll past the legal wall.
- **Zero brand personality in the consent modal** — it's a generic wall of text. Even the buttons are styled in a flat corporate orange that feels like a legal department designed it, not a hospitality brand.
- **No Accelerate or member-pricing teaser** — I'm exactly the person who books direct because of 4th-night-free promos, and there's nothing in the visible viewport that rewards that behavior.

## Recommendations

- **Slim the cookie banner to a bottom bar** — move consent to a non-intrusive bottom strip so the hero image and primary booking CTA are immediately visible. Every major hotel brand (Marriott, Hilton) does this better right now.
- **Put an Accelerate or member rate teaser in the header bar** — something like "Members save up to 10% — Sign in" next to the existing CTA would catch my eye immediately and reinforce the direct-booking value prop without any scrolling.
- **On mobile, lead with the lifestyle imagery full-bleed** — that waterfront resort shot I can barely see behind the modal is doing real work. Let it breathe. Make it the hero, not the cookie wall.
- **Add a "Current Offers" or "Accelerate" pill in the header** — a single small link next to "Sign in" would be enough to signal to me that there's a live promotion worth exploring before I close the tab.
