# Your 🔑 to an Anniversary Free Night is here
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- After back-to-back credit card acquisition pushes on June 18 and June 24, IHG has wisely changed its hook: instead of leading with the points number, the subject pivots to the anniversary free night benefit. That's the right instinct — "free night" is a concrete, emotionally resonant reward that a traveler can picture. The problem is the email body doesn't follow through. You open expecting the free night to be the star, and instead you're hit with the same 140,000 BONUS POINTS hero that has dominated this entire credit card push. The benefit that earned the open is buried somewhere below the fold in the card-benefits icon row.
- There's also a notable number change worth flagging. The two prior sends (June 18 and June 24) promoted up to 185,000 bonus points. This email leads with 140,000 — a 45,000-point reduction with no explanation visible in the render. A subscriber who saw the "Ends today! 185K" email a week ago and now sees 140K will notice and wonder whether they missed the better offer. That erodes trust.
- The email is otherwise clean and brand-consistent. But the subject-to-body mismatch and the unexplained points drop make this a promising execution with a structural problem at its core.

## What's working

- **Subject line hook is the strongest in this series** — "Anniversary Free Night" is specific, tangible, and emotionally compelling vs. the abstract points-stack framing of the prior sends.
- **140,000 BONUS POINTS hero is visually dominant** — the typography is large, the layout is uncluttered, and the eye lands on the offer immediately.
- **Card benefits section includes icons** — breaking down card perks visually (though small) gives readers a reason to scan beyond the hero.
- **Hotel brand mosaic at bottom** — showing the IHG family (Kimpton, Holiday Inn, Candlewood, etc.) reinforces network breadth and makes the card feel more aspirational.
- **No visible render bugs** — clean rendering, no broken images, no overlapping elements.

## What's weak

- **Subject-to-body mismatch** — the anniversary free night that earned the open is not the email's hero; 140K bonus points is. The benefit that drove curiosity gets demoted to a small icon.
- **Points dropped from 185K to 140K with no explanation** — a subscriber who saw the June 24 "Ends today! 185K" email will immediately notice the lower number and feel confused or misled.
- **Points balance showing "0"** — this either reflects a broken merge token or Iris genuinely has zero IHG points. Either way, displaying a zero balance in a rewards email undermines the aspiration of the offer.
- **CTA copy is generic** — "LEARN MORE" is a weak call-to-action for a credit card acquisition push; it adds friction and reduces urgency.
- **Third credit card email in two weeks** — cadence fatigue risk is real; this send needed a meaningfully different angle to justify another touch, and the free night hook was the right idea, but the body doesn't deliver on it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead the body with the anniversary free night, not the points.** If the subject promises a free night, make the hero image a hotel room or a "free night" visual and make THAT the first CTA. Move the 140K points to a secondary position as a bonus reason to apply.
- 2. **Acknowledge the points change explicitly** — or don't call attention to it at all. A line like "plus earn up to 140,000 bonus points" (framed as additive) is less damaging than displaying 140K right next to the memory of 185K with no context.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA** — replace "LEARN MORE" with something like "Claim my free night" or "Apply now — free night included" to tie the button back to the hook.
- 4. **Fix or hide the zero points balance** — suppress the personalization module if points = 0, or show a motivational "start earning today" message instead.
- 5. **Add a deadline** — the June 24 email used deadline urgency effectively. This send has none visible, which loses a legitimate lever.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Get a free night every year — here's how`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Iris, your Anniversary Free Night is one application away`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `IHG One Rewards cardholders earn a free night every cardmember anniversary — here's how to claim yours.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 140,000 bonus points when you apply today. No annual fee surprises.`

### 5. Compliance
