# 💸 Last-minute savings: Save up to 20% 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's cleanest send in recent weeks. It has one headline, "Escape this weekend. Save up to 20%.", one orange "BOOK NOW" button, and a pool hero. It's also the first email in the recent history that isn't a Chase credit-card pitch. That fixes the problem with the 2026-10-08 and 2026-10-01 sends, which were nearly the same email twice.
- The offer is soft, though. "Up to 20%" is a ceiling, and the copy only reveals the real condition, a stay of 2+ nights Thursday through Sunday, in the body paragraph. The three destination tiles (New York, Savannah, Toronto) are readable but small, and their links are text-only. The footer is heavy: a long brand-logo wall and dense legal copy make up more than half the render.
- Judgement: a solid, honest, on-season weekend-getaway send. It's better than the credit-card emails, but "up to" and the thin urgency keep it in the middle of the range.

## What's working

- The headline "Escape this weekend. Save up to 20%." puts the offer first, and the pool hero supports it.
- There is a single, clearly visible orange "BOOK NOW" button with strong contrast.
- The member-rate framing ("as an IHG One Rewards member") and the signed-in bar at the top read as a loyalty benefit.
- The weekend-plans tiles (New York, Savannah, Toronto) give destination ideas, and the photography is good.
- This is a break from the credit-card repetition of the last few weeks.

## What's weak

- "Up to 20%" is vague, and the real condition (2+ nights, Thursday–Sunday) is buried in body copy.
- The destination links are small text ("Escape to New York", "See more of Savannah", "Head to Toronto"). They give no prices or percentages, so there's no reason to pick one.
- The "this weekend" urgency has no stated booking deadline, and the "Terms apply" link is tiny.
- The footer is very long, with a big logo wall and dense legal text. That's a lot of scroll below the one real CTA.
- There is no social proof or ratings, and no price anchor such as "from $X".

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a concrete number in the hero, such as "Weekend rates from $129" or "20% off 2+ night weekend stays". Show the Thursday–Sunday condition in the hero, not the body.
- 2. Add a real deadline: "Book by Wednesday for this weekend."
- 3. Turn the destination tiles into buttons with a price or discount per city, for example "New York: from $149".
- 4. Cut the brand logo wall and shrink the footer legal copy. Keep a compact brand strip.
- 5. Add a second CTA below the tiles, such as "See all weekend deals".

- **Subject Alt B:** `This weekend: member rates up to 20% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `2+ nights, Thursday to Sunday. New York, Savannah, Toronto and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Member-only rates on last-minute getaways. Book by Wednesday.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email's HTML is structurally sound (lang attribute, viewport meta, Outlook conditional CSS, ADA comment blocks). Findings are one 403 on a terms link, a non-HTTPS tracking pixel with a malformed URL, and two images with no alt text. The header-level compliance and authentication checks were inconclusive, not failed.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Broken link (403):** The "Terms apply." link returns 403:
  `https://www.ihg.com/content/us/en/deals/hotel-offers/weekend-hotel-deals?cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-LastMinuteDeals_20262624-_-SENDURLID-_-549940&mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=549940&trso=EMAIL_549940#terms`
  - The 403 may come from bot-blocking against the QA probe, not a real user-facing break. The QA data can't tell the two apart, so verify manually in a browser.
  - The `cm_mmc` value contains the literal placeholder `SENDURLID`. If that is an unresolved merge token rather than a deliberate Monetate/Marketing Cloud pattern, attribution breaks.
- **Malformed tracking pixel URL:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=549940&10/12/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - The query string ends with an unencoded date-time containing spaces and slashes: `&10/12/2026 12:00:00 AM`. This looks like a merge field that was inserted as a bare value with no key and no URL-encoding.
  - Some clients or proxies may truncate or reject the URL, which would drop open tracking.
  - The QA tool parsed the `alt` as `2026 12:00:00 AM`, so the URL's spaces are probably breaking the `<img>` attribute parsing. That would be a markup defect, not just a tracking one.
- **Tracking links skipped:** 23 click-redirect links were not probed. Their destinations are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text, pixel 1:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/549940/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`. This is a tracking pixel, so it should carry `alt=""` explicitly. The QA check shows the contact ID `0037y00002BeqgfAAB` where alt text should be, which suggests the URL itself is malformed in the markup.
- **Missing alt text, pixel 2:** `mi.ihg.com/.../o.gif`, the same malformed-URL issue as above.
- **Non-HTTPS image:** The `mi.ihg.com` pixel uses `http://`. It may be blocked or flagged as mixed content in some clients, and open tracking would then under-count.
- **Document structure:** The `<div style="display:none;">` and the ADA comment blocks sit before the `<!DOCTYPE>`. Content ahead of the doctype can put some clients into quirks mode. This is visible in the truncated source only.
- **Web font:** The Google Fonts `<link>` is wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]>` only for the second style block. The `<link>` itself is outside the conditional and is ignored by many clients. Montserrat has Arial/Helvetica fallbacks, so this is low severity.
- **Truncated source:** The HTML cut off mid-CSS, so image alt coverage, table roles, and the rest of the body could not be audited.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- The `SENDURLID` token in `cm_mmc` and the unencoded date-time in the pixel URL (see section 2) are the only candidate merge-token problems. Neither is confirmed as a render failure.
- No visible unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens appear in the truncated source.
- The pixel URL contains the recipient identifiers `mi_u=695150425` and the Salesforce-style contact ID `0037y00002BeqgfAAB`. This is normal tracking, but both are exposed in the URLs.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe:** Not found. The QA tool notes the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed, not a failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** Not found, same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for large senders, so check the raw headers at the sending ESP.
- **Authentication-Results:** Not found. SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The From domain `mc.ihg.com` is a subdomain used for sending.
- **Physical address and in-body unsubscribe link:** Not visible in the truncated source, so not assessable.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- The email uses a `cm_mmc` channel-tracking convention, not standard `utm_*` parameters: `EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-LastMinuteDeals_20262624-_-SENDURLID-_-549940`. This is consistent, but analytics must map `cm_mmc` to a campaign dimension.
- The campaign ID `LastMinuteDeals_20262624` has an unusual date-like suffix. `20262624` is not a valid YYYYMMDD or YYMMDD date, so it may be a template mistake. This is minor and cosmetic.
- The destination `/deals/hotel-offers/weekend-hotel-deals` is a "weekend" deals page, while the subject is "Last-minute savings." Whether the page matches the offer can't be verified from this data.
- The 403 on the `#terms` anchor means the continuity of the link can't be confirmed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the malformed tracking pixel in `mi.ihg.com`. Name and URL-encode the date parameter, for example `&mi_dt=10%2F12%2F2026%2012%3A00%3A00%20AM`. Use `https://`.
2. Add explicit `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Check the "Terms apply." link in a real browser. Confirm whether the 403 is bot-blocking or a real error.
4. Confirm that `SENDURLID` in `cm_mmc` is intended and resolves in analytics.
5. Move the ADA comments and hidden `<div>` below the `<!DOCTYPE>` so the doctype is the first thing in the document.
6. Check the raw headers at the ESP for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. The relay may have stripped them, so don't treat the absence as a defect.
7. Rerun the audit with the full HTML so body-level checks (alt coverage, footer address, unsubscribe link) can be completed.
