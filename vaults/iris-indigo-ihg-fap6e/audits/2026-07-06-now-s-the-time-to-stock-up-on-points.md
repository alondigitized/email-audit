# Now’s the time to stock up on points
**Score:** 10/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-06**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth IHG One Rewards buy-points email in roughly two weeks — following June 22, June 29, and July 1 sends that all ran the same 100% bonus offer with near-identical creative. The email itself is technically competent: clean layout, clear three-step explanation, two prominent CTAs, and a luxury hero image that sells aspiration. But the July 1 send already carried "Now's the time to get 100% more points 🙌" — the current subject strips the emoji and swaps one phrase. That's not a refresh; that's a resend.
- The core problem isn't execution — it's overexposure. A subscriber who didn't buy after three identical emails isn't waiting for a fourth. The subject also buries the lead: "stock up on points" is vaguer than "100% more points," which is the actual hook. The July 1 subject was better.
- Verdict: well-made email, wrong send. The creative earned its keep three weeks ago. At this point it's training subscribers to ignore IHG's email.

## What's working

- **Dual CTA placement**: A BUY NOW button appears above the hero and again after the three-step explainer — catches both impulse openers and deliberate readers.
- **Three-step mechanic is clear**: Buy → Get 100% more → Start planning maps the value chain without friction, and was visible without scrolling.
- **Hero image earns attention**: The rooftop pool/terrace shot communicates luxury travel believably; no stock-photo feel.
- **No render bugs**: Clean layout, no broken images, no overlapping text, consistent brand fonts and colors throughout.
- **Brand logo grid**: Showing the full IHG portfolio reminds members of redemption breadth, which is a genuine decision-driver for buying points.

## What's weak

- **Subject de-specifies the offer**: "Stock up on points" is softer than "100% more points" — the July 1 subject was more compelling.
- **Cadence has collapsed the signal**: This is send #4 of an identical promotion in 15 days. Open rates on this send are almost certainly degraded; unsubscribes are real risk.
- **No personalization visible**: No name, no tier, no "you have X points" context — missed opportunity to make the buy feel relevant to where this specific member stands.
- **No deadline urgency in hero copy**: The deadline (July 16, per the body) doesn't appear in the hero or headline, weakening above-the-fold urgency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the deadline and bonus in the headline**: "100% bonus points — offer ends July 16" does more work than "This is your chance for 100% more points."
- 2. **Add a personalized points balance**: "You have X points — double them before July 16" turns a generic broadcast into a targeted nudge. This single change justifies the resend.
- 3. **Retire the campaign after this send**: If this doesn't convert, the offer hasn't failed — the cadence has. Rest the creative for at least two weeks before the next push.
- 4. **Restore the specificity in the subject**: The July 1 subject ("Now's the time to get 100% more points") was stronger. This one regressed.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Double your points before July 16 — 100% bonus`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last chance: 100% bonus points ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `You're one purchase away from your next free night.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy now through July 16 and we'll match every point.`
