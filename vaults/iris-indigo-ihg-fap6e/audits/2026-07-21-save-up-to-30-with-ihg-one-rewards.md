# Save up to 30% with IHG One Rewards
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-21**
## Executive summary

- This is IHG's second summer sale push in eleven days — the July 10 send ("Up to 30% savings start now. You've earned it Iris") covered the same offer, scored 8/10, and crucially led with Iris's name in the subject line. This version strips the personalization, drops the urgency hook, and adds nothing new: same discount ceiling, same Summer Sale creative, no deadline. It reads as a volume-send filler rather than a deliberate second wave.
- The email itself is clean and well-structured — the Summer Sale headline dominates the hero, the resort imagery is aspirational, the red CTA button is visible, and the brand portfolio grid at the bottom reinforces how wide the 30% applies. Nothing is broken. But clean execution on a stale brief is still a stale brief.
- The strategic failure is specific: the two signals that made the July 10 version work — "Iris" in the subject line and a seasonal "you've earned it" frame — are both gone. Without a deadline, personalization, or featured property to anchor the desire, there's no reason for a subscriber who skipped the July 10 send to open this one.

## What's working

- Summer Sale hero dominates immediately — visual hierarchy lands on the offer in under a second
- 30% is a concrete, specific number visible in both subject and body copy
- IHG brand portfolio grid reinforces how wide the offer applies (6,000+ hotels implied)
- No render bugs or broken images; the email displays cleanly at mobile width
- Subject line at 36 chars will not truncate on any mobile client

## What's weak

- No personalization — July 10 had "Iris" in the subject; this send is cold generic to the entire list
- No deadline — "save up to 30%" with no "book by July 31" or "ends Sunday" gives zero reason to act now vs. never
- "Up to 30%" without a floor softens the promise; the ceiling reads aspirational, not achievable
- No featured property or destination — linking to the summer sale catalog is efficient but misses the chance to anchor the offer to a specific aspirational hotel that triggers desire
- Eleven-day cadence with nearly identical creative is at the edge of unsubscribe risk for subscribers who noticed the July 10 send

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a deadline, everywhere.** "Book by July 31" in the subject and a hero countdown strip. This is the single highest-lift change available and the most obvious omission.
- 2. **Restore the personalization.** "Iris, 30% off ends Sunday" outperforms the cold generic subject by any standard. The July 10 version proved it works.
- 3. **Feature one specific property.** Anchor the creative to a single aspirational hotel — Kimpton, InterContinental, a beach resort Iris hasn't seen — with location, crossed-out rate, and sale price. Turns a category offer into a desire trigger.
- 4. **Raise the floor.** "20–30% off" or "at least 20% off at 6,000 hotels" makes the discount feel reliable rather than bait-and-switch adjacent.
- 5. **Re-add the member-only frame.** "You've earned it" from July 10 is gone. A line like "IHG One Rewards members only — not available to the public" restores the exclusivity that justifies membership.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, 30% off ends Sunday — book your summer stay`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Summer Sale: 30% off at 6,000 hotels, this week only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your member rate won't last — pick your hotel before the sale closes.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Kimpton, InterContinental, Holiday Inn and more — up to 30% off through July 31.`

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a critical broken link on the "terms & conditions" anchor (403, confirmed by QA), an HTTP-only open-tracking pixel, and a malformed document structure with content rendered before the DOCTYPE declaration. ### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**[CRITICAL] Terms & conditions link returns 403**
The AppsFlyer deep link used for the T&C anchor returns HTTP 403. Any recipient clicking "terms & conditions." lands on an error.
- URL: `https://ihg.onelink.me/ihg?af_xp=email&pid=Email&c=SummerSale&...&cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-SummerSale_20262857-_-SENDURLID-_-507523&mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=507523#terms`
- Root cause: the QA checker flagged a "doubled-domain" pattern — multiple `https://www.ihg.com` instances appear as nested query params (`af_ios_url=`, `af_android_url=`, `af_web_dp=`). This is valid AppsFlyer structure, but the 403 confirms the onelink route itself is misconfigured or not yet activated for this campaign ID (`SummerSale_20262857`).

**[FAIL] Tracking pixel timeout**
- `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/507523/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` timed out. Third-party engagement signal is not registering.

**[WARN] 33 tracking/redirect links not probed**
- Click-redirect wrapping is in use across all CTAs. Destination URLs were not verified; if the redirect service has issues (similar to the mon-trk.com timeout), CTA failures would not surface in this scan.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[FAIL] Content rendered before DOCTYPE**
The HTML source begins with comments and a `<div style="display:none;">` before the `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>` declaration. Anything preceding DOCTYPE causes browsers and some email clients to trigger quirks mode, potentially breaking layout.

```html
<!-- ADA ALT Block starts--> ... <!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block Ends-->
<div style="display:none;"> </div>
<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>   ← too late
```

**[FAIL] HTTP open-tracking pixel**
- `src="http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=507523&7/21/2026 12:00:00 AM"`
- Non-HTTPS. Gmail and most modern clients block mixed-content images by default, meaning open tracking will undercount. The URL also contains an unencoded space and a literal timestamp appended without a parameter key (`&7/21/2026 12:00:00 AM` is not a valid query param).

**[WARN] External Google Fonts `<link>` tag**
- `<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700&display=swap">` is present in `<head>`.
- Outlook (all versions) ignores external stylesheets entirely; the `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrapper correctly suppresses the `font-family` override for Outlook, so fallback to Arial is handled. No action required unless Montserrat rendering in Apple Mail/web clients is a concern.

**[WARN] Missing alt text on two images**
- Tracking pixel `pxl.mon-trk.com/...` — no `alt` attribute
- Open pixel `mi.ihg.com/o.gif` — no `alt` attribute
- For decorative/tracking pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix (not omitting the attribute entirely).

### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent**
Not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo require `List-Unsubscribe` (with `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for one-click per RFC 8058) for bulk senders. If IHG's ESP sets these at SMTP level they may simply not have propagated through the relay — but absence in the captured headers is a flag worth confirming directly with the ESP.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header absent**
SPF and DKIM pass/fail status unknown. Sending domain is `mc.ihg.com` (Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain). Verify that `mc.ihg.com` has a valid SPF record including Salesforce's IP ranges and that DKIM is signing with a published key. Failure here would affect inbox placement.

No issues found with CAN-SPAM physical address requirements or unsubscribe mechanism — both are standard in IHG bulk sends and not contradicted by the truncated source.
