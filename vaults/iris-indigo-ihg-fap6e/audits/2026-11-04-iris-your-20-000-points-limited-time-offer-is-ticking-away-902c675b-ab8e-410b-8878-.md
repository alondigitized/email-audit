# Iris, your 20,000 points limited-time offer is ticking away ⌚
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth send of the same 20,000-point offer, after 09-28, 10-05 and 10-28. The creative is clean and the offer is a single idea. The hero reads "LIMITED TIME OFFER / EARN 20,000 BONUS POINTS / When you stay 2 nights", with one orange "REGISTER IN-CLICK" button under it. A three-step explainer ("Register, book, stay 2 nights") repeats the button. The page is well structured and easy to scan.
- The weakness is credibility. The subscriber has now seen this offer four times, and the "ticking away" urgency is undercut by the body copy: "you have 60 days after you register to stay." The deadline in the copy is November 12, 2026, which is only 8 days out, while the subject's clock emoji implies something tighter. The Terms and Conditions block is very long and runs through the lower half of the email. The email scores well on clarity but poorly on freshness.
- The screenshot was downscaled to 170px wide, so I could not read the small copy reliably. My comments on specific text are tentative.

## What's working

- The hero states one offer in large type: "EARN 20,000 BONUS POINTS". The "When you stay 2 nights" line gives the condition right away.
- A single orange CTA is clearly visible above the fold.
- The three-step "Earn in 3 easy steps" block makes the mechanic simple, and a second CTA repeats the action.
- The destination photo (Crowne Plaza Gabung City Centre) adds a travel feel and gives the eye a clear landing point.
- Personalization ("Iris") in the subject fits the member-programme context.

## What's weak

- The offer is repeated for the fourth time since 09-28. Subscribers will treat it as background noise and the urgency will feel manufactured.
- The "ticking away" urgency is inconsistent with a 60-day stay window.
- The terms block takes up a large share of the email's height. The qualifying conditions (the offer deadline and the 2-night minimum) are not repeated near the CTA.
- The brand-logo grid (Six Senses, Regent, InterContinental, etc.) is visually heavy and does not drive a click.
- The mid-email image strip with the small labels (Stay, Offers, Meetings, Our Brands) is a weak secondary navigation. Nothing in it is tied to the offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the hero and the subject: "Ends Nov 12". Tie the urgency to a date, not a clock emoji.
- 2. Suppress or rotate the creative for members who have already registered. If they have, show how many nights remain on their qualifying window.
- 3. Cut the brand-logo grid and the footer navigation. Move the main terms below a short summary line.
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific: "Register for 20,000 points" instead of "REGISTER IN-CLICK".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based template with a broken pixel URL and a few CSS and markup defects. The header-level compliance and authentication warnings could not be confirmed because the relay probably stripped those headers. The source I was given is truncated, so findings are limited to what appears in the visible head and the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed open-tracking pixel:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=555569&11/4/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - The trailing `&11/4/2026 12:00:00 AM` has no key and contains unencoded spaces and slashes.
  - This looks like a date merge value injected without a parameter name or URL-encoding.
  - The spaces break attribute parsing. The QA tool read the `alt` value as `2026 12:00:00 AM`, which suggests the `<img>` tag is malformed.
  - Some clients may fail to load or log the pixel, which would undercount opens.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** the same `mi.ihg.com` pixel uses `http://`. Clients that block mixed content may drop it, and it exposes `mi_u=695150425` in cleartext.
- **Identifier in a pixel URL:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/555569/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`. The last path segment looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix, 18 characters). It is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party URL.
- **Unverified links:** 36 tracking links were skipped by the probe (click-redirect domains). Their destinations and redirect health are unverified, not confirmed broken.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Doctype not first:** `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN" ...>` comes after HTML comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>`. A doctype that is not first can push web-view and some client renderers into quirks mode.
- **Invalid CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent `<ReadMsgBody>` element. It should be `.ReadMsgBody`. The rule is legacy Outlook.com and has no effect as written.
- **Invalid CSS value:** `max-height: auto !important;` in the `@media` block is not a valid value. It is ignored, so `max-height: none` was probably intended.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>`. This is minor, but it should be `&amp;`.
- **Images missing `alt`:**
  - The `pxl.mon-trk.com` tracking pixel has no `alt`. Add `alt=""` (and ideally `width="1" height="1"`) so screen readers skip it.
  - The `mi.ihg.com` pixel is flagged for the same reason, but its `alt` was likely misparsed because of the malformed URL (see section 2).
- **Web font:** the Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>` is stripped by Gmail and some other clients. The `Montserrat, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif` fallback stack is present, so the impact is low.
- **Accessibility comments:** the `<!-- ADA ALT Block -->` and `<!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block -->` sections are empty comments, so no injected ADA attributes appear in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible content. The subject resolved correctly ("Iris, your 20,000 points…"). No unresolved tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the supplied source. The one exception is the malformed date parameter in the open pixel (section 2), which suggests a merge field was inserted without a parameter name. The body is truncated, so I can't confirm the rest.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay not capturing the headers. This is **unconfirmed**, not a verified defect. Check the raw message at the sending ESP.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender is `IHGOneRewards@mc.ihg.com`, and `mc.ihg.com` is an ESP-hosted subdomain, so alignment is plausible but untested here.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. No campaign URLs or UTM parameters appear in the visible source, and the 36 tracking links were not resolved. The only campaign identifier visible is `mi_ecmp=555569` and the matching `555569` path segment in the `mon-trk.com` pixel. Those at least show consistent campaign IDs across the two trackers.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `mi.ihg.com` pixel. Give the date a parameter name, URL-encode it (`%2F`, `%20`), and quote the `src` attribute. Also move the pixel to `https://`.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels, with explicit `width="1" height="1"` on the `pxl.mon-trk.com` one.
3. Put the `<!DOCTYPE>` first, before the comments and hidden `<div>`.
4. Fix `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, and replace `max-height: auto` with `none`.
5. Escape `&` in `<title>`.
6. Check the raw message at the ESP for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results before treating those warnings as defects.
7. Resolve and probe the 36 skipped tracking links, and capture UTM parameters and landing pages to check email-to-site continuity.
