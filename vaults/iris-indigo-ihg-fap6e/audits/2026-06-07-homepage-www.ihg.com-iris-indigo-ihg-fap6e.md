# Homepage snapshot · www.ihg.com · 2026-06-07
**Score:** 2/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- I opened IHG.com and immediately got hit with a full-screen cookie consent wall that ate the entire viewport — so my first impression isn't "lifestyle travel brand," it's "legal compliance form." Behind the modal I can just barely make out the search widget (destination, check-in dates, rooms/guests) and a "Sign in / Join for free" link in the header. The actual brand experience is completely blocked before I've seen a single hero image, offer, or reason to care.

## What's working

- **Search widget is pre-populated with today's dates** — I can see 06/07/2026 → 06/08/2026 already loaded, which means the site did *something* functional before the modal fired. If I could get past the wall, booking intent is one tap away.
- **"Sign in / Join for free" header CTA** — it's small and sitting above the modal, but it's there. As a One Rewards member, the option to sign in directly from the header is the right instinct.
- **IHG Hotels & Resorts branding is clean** — the wordmark and logo at top left reads upscale enough for the Indigo/Kimpton tier, not budget motel.
- **CONTINUE button is high-contrast orange** — easy to find and tap on mobile, at least for dismissing the modal.

## What's weak

- **Full-viewport cookie modal as the literal first impression** — "Cookies and Tracking Technologies" is not why I came here. I want to see Hotel Indigo Edinburgh or a Kimpton pool. This wall erases any brand momentum instantly.
- **Zero brand storytelling visible** — no hero image, no lifestyle photography, no copy about the portfolio. Nothing that makes me feel like this is the brand for experiential, design-forward travel.
- **No loyalty/offers hook above the fold** — as someone who watches Accelerate offers closely, I'd expect at minimum a "Check your Q3 offers" nudge or a member rate callout. Total silence.
- **Modal copy is dense legalese** — three paragraphs of tracking disclosure before a single word about hotels. The copy register is all compliance, zero hospitality.
- **"OPT-OUT" button is a secondary red button** — visually it draws the eye almost as much as CONTINUE, which creates a confusing three-button hierarchy on a small screen.

## Recommendations

- **Implement a lightweight cookie banner strip at the bottom of the viewport** instead of a full-screen takeover — let the hero, search widget, and brand imagery load behind it so the page isn't held hostage.
- **Swap the current modal flow for a single-line consent bar** with "Accept / Manage" that sits above the footer, which is standard for hospitality brands that want first impression = destination, not data policy.
- **Personalize the header CTA for returning visitors** — if I'm a One Rewards member hitting the site again, surface my tier and point balance ("Welcome back, Iris — 42,400 pts") rather than the generic "Sign in / Join for free."
- **Surface the current Accelerate quarter offer in the header strip** — even a one-line "Q3 Accelerate is live — sign in to activate" above the search bar would immediately signal relevance to a loyalist like me.
