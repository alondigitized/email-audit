# 📣 Don’t miss out, Iris. 20,000 points are close!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 20,000-point register-and-stay mechanic IHG already ran on 2026-08-06 ("Hurry! Your 20,000 points offer checks out soon"), just repackaged with a new deadline (register by September 13) and a slightly more aggressive subject line. The layout is clean and the offer is legible at a glance — limited-time offer, register, book, stay 2 nights, earn 20K — which is the same structure that scored well last time.
- The problem is cadence and fatigue, not execution: this is now the third or fourth points-acquisition email in five weeks with near-identical creative (same hero photo style, same 3-step layout, same "REGISTER IN 1-CLICK" button). The subject line ("Don't miss out, Iris. 20,000 points are close!") is manufactured urgency layered onto a real but recycled mechanic, which reads as more spammy than the August 6 send that used a similar hook.
- Solid execution on a stale rotation — judgment: fine as a one-off, weak as the Nth repeat.

## What's working

- Clear single hero offer ("EARN 20,000 BONUS POINTS when you stay 2 nights") with no competing message above the fold.
- The 3-step breakdown (register → book → stay) removes ambiguity about how to earn the points.
- Two clearly labeled, high-contrast CTA buttons ("REGISTER IN 1-CLICK") at logical points in the scroll.
- A specific, credible deadline (register by September 13, 2026) with a real hotel photo (Crowne Plaza Budapest) instead of generic stock art.
- Brand footer with the full IHG portfolio and app download prompt reinforces the ecosystem without cluttering the top of the email.

## What's weak

- This is a repeat of the 08-06 "20,000 points" stay-mechanic email in less than a month — recipients who saw that one will recognize this as the same offer, not something new.
- Subject line uses artificial urgency ("Don't miss out... are close!") that doesn't match the body, which is a straightforward 40-day registration window, not a ticking clock.
- No personalization visible in the body itself beyond "Iris" in the subject — the hero and steps are generic and could go to any member.
- Terms/conditions link is repeated three times ("By clicking, you are agreeing...") which adds visual noise under both CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the same 20K-points-in-2-nights mechanic back to back — if IHG only has one lever, at least vary the creative (different property tier, different photo, different framing) so repeat opens don't read as duplicate mail.
- 2. Drop the false urgency in the subject line; the 40-day window is a real, calm deadline — sell it as "40 days to earn 20K points," not manufactured panic.
- 3. Add one line of segmentation/personalization in the hero copy itself (e.g., nearest participating property or member tier context), not just in the subject.
- 4. Trim the repeated fine-print CTA disclaimer to one instance near the final button.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, register by Sept 13 for 20,000 bonus points`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 nights, 20,000 points — here's how it works`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register now, stay twice by [date], earn 20K IHG One Rewards points.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `A short stay at Crowne Plaza (or any IHG hotel) is worth 20,000 points.`
