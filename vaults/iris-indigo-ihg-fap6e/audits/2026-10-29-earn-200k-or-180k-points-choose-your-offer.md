# Earn 200K or 180K points (choose your offer)
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is the sixth IHG credit-card acquisition send since 10-01, and it's the same email again. It follows "Introducing new credit cards for you 💳" (10-01), "Offers up to 200K bonus points" (10-08), "Our new credit cards are here 🎉" (10-15) and "Introducing our new and refreshed cards for you 💳" (10-22). A subscriber has now seen this teal "Introducing OUR NEW PREMIUM CARDS" hero and the "someday, SOONER" tagline several times. The subject is the first to put the numbers up front, but the creative underneath hasn't changed.
- The body does one thing well: a side-by-side comparison of Premier (180,000 points, $150 annual fee) and Premier Select (200,000 points, $350 annual fee). It sets out bonus, spend threshold, elite status, dining and travel earn, and the anniversary night. That is useful for someone who is actually deciding. But the comparison grid is long and dense, with small text, and the two "LEARN MORE" buttons sit close together. The hero image is a bedroom with no card in it, so the product is only shown later.
- Judgement: a competent but fatigued send. Cadence is the main problem. A subscriber who has ignored four earlier versions has little reason to open the fifth. This is a mid-tier send that would be better held until the offer is genuinely different.

## What's working

- The subject leads with concrete numbers, "200K or 180K points", which is more specific than the earlier subjects.
- The hero headline "OFFERS UP TO 200,000 BONUS POINTS" is large and readable against the teal background.
- The two-column comparison lets the reader choose between the cards without clicking away.
- Both cards have an "Apply by 11/18/2026" deadline, which gives the offer an end date.
- The card-by-card breakdown covers annual fees, elite status, free night and everyday earn, so the information is there.

## What's weak

- The creative is a near-repeat of the 10-08 and 10-22 sends, so it will read as stale.
- The hero is a bedroom photo with no card visible, and the body copy under it is small. The offer is stated only in the headline.
- The comparison table is long and the text is tiny. On a phone it will be a scroll through dense rows with fine-print asterisks.
- There are two "LEARN MORE" buttons and an "EXPLORE MORE BENEFITS" link in each column, plus a later "EXPLORE OPTIONS" button. That gives several competing CTAs, none of which says "Apply".
- The $350 fee on Premier Select and the spend requirement ($3,000 in 3 months, as I read it) sit small and low-contrast under the card headers, so a reader may feel the cost is understated.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pause the cadence. Send this at most every three weeks, and only when something changes, such as a new bonus tier, a deadline or a different audience.
- 2. Put the cards in the hero, with a single line: "Earn 200K points. Apply by 11/18." Then use one "Compare cards" button and move the grid below it.
- 3. Trim the grid to the five rows that matter (bonus, fee, spend requirement, elite status, free night) and make the type larger.
- 4. Replace "LEARN MORE" with "Apply now" for each card, and put the fee and spend requirement next to the button.
- 5. Segment the send by status. Members who have already opened the previous four should get a different message, such as a reminder of the 11/18 deadline.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: 200K points ends 11/18`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Which IHG card earns you a free night?`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Premier or Premier Select: see which fits how you travel. Apply by 11/18.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Earn up to 200,000 points after $3,000 in 3 months. Fees and details inside.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source has several markup and CSS defects: content before the doctype, a malformed selector, and invalid `max-height` values. There is also a broken, non-HTTPS open-tracking pixel, and a large number of images with no alt text. Compliance and authentication headers could not be verified from the data provided.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Malformed tracking pixel URL:** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=558733&10/29/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - The last query segment has no key. It is a raw date/time string with spaces and slashes, which looks like a mis-rendered merge/date token.
  - Unencoded spaces in a `src` are invalid. Some clients and proxies may truncate or reject the URL, which would drop open tracking.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel:** the same `mi.ihg.com` pixel uses `http://`, which can be blocked or rewritten (mixed content, image proxies).
- **Identifier exposed in a pixel path:** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/558733/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
  - The final segment has the format of a Salesforce Contact ID (`003…`), so it is a per-recipient identifier in a third-party image URL.
  - This is informational, not a defect.
- **Unverified links:** 27 tracking links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so redirect resolution and dead links are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content before the doctype:** an HTML comment and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN"…>`. A stray element before the doctype can push some clients into quirks mode.
- **Broken CSS selector:** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` has no `.` or `#`, so it targets a nonexistent `<ReadMsgBody>` element. The intended Outlook.com fix never applies.
- **Invalid CSS values and selectors:**
  - `max-height: auto !important` appears in the `img` rule inside `@media (max-width: 480px)`. `auto` is not valid for `max-height` (it should be `none`), so the declaration is ignored.
  - `img .header` is a descendant selector. It was probably meant to be `img.header`.
  - `.offerbtm` is declared twice in the same media block.
- **Missing alt text on 18 images (QA):**
  - The images are `24aac9ff-…png`, `486d3934-…png`, `e3691fe8-…png`, `46206e90-…png` (×2) and `437f94d8-…png` (×9), all under `image.mc.ihg.com/lib/fe32117171640478701372/m/…`.
  - Both tracking pixels are also missing alt text.
  - The warning labels show the filename or the URL tail where an alt value should be. If those strings are being used as alt text, that would be worse than an empty alt.
  - `437f94d8-…png` repeats 9 times and is probably a spacer or icon. Decorative images need an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Unescaped ampersand:** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` should be `&amp;`. This is minor.
- **Font handling:** Montserrat loads through `<link>` to Google Fonts. Gmail strips it, and the Arial/Helvetica fallback is declared, so this is not an issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[%…%]` tokens are visible in the truncated source.
- The one probable token failure is the malformed date segment in the `mi.ihg.com` pixel (see section 2).
- The rest of the body was truncated, so the full body could not be checked for tokens.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** neither was found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed defect. Check the raw message in a non-relayed mailbox.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain `mc.ihg.com` could not be validated.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not visible in the truncated source, so they could not be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated HTML, and the 27 tracking links were not resolved.
- UTM presence and landing-page alignment could not be assessed from the data provided.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `mi.ihg.com` pixel:
   - Serve it over `https://`.
   - Give the date value a key and URL-encode it, or remove it.
   - Add `alt=""`, `width="1"` and `height="1"`.
2. Add `alt=""` to decorative and spacer images, including the repeated `437f94d8-…png`, and add meaningful alt text to the content images.
3. Move the doctype and `<html>` to the very start of the document, and remove the preceding comments and the stray hidden `<div>`.
4. Fix the CSS: change `ReadMsgBody` to `.ReadMsgBody`, use `max-height: none`, correct `img .header`, and remove the duplicate `.offerbtm`.
5. Validate the raw `.eml` for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`, since the relay may be hiding them.
6. Re-run the audit on the full HTML, including the footer, and with link resolution on the tracking domains. That would cover UTM continuity, the unsubscribe link and the postal address.
