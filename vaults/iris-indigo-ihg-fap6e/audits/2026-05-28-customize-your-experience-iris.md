# Customize your experience, Iris
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-28**
## Executive summary

- Email #6 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving 12 days after signup. The mechanic is smart — set your preferences, earn 500 bonus points — and "YOUR MEMBERSHIP, YOUR WAY" is the strongest headline in this series since the original welcome. The email earns its place in the sequence: it asks for something (preferences) and pays for it (500 points). That's a real exchange of value, not just another brand lecture.
- The execution problem is cadence exhaustion. Six emails in twelve days is aggressive, and this one arrives two days after the May 26 send that already fell to a 6/10. - Preference selector interface is visually prominent in the upper portion of the email — the interaction model is clear.

## What's weak

- No deadline on the 500-point offer — without urgency, this becomes a low-priority to-do that never gets done.
- Brand portfolio grid runs again at the bottom; at email #6 it's filler, not value.
- Six emails in twelve days likely depresses open rate before the reader even evaluates content.

- 4. **Slow the cadence.** After the initial 3-send burst, weekly is the right rhythm. Two sends in two days (May 26 + May 28) cannibalizes both.

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — IHG One Rewards: "Customize your experience, Iris"

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Two blocking defects confirmed: an unresolved merge token (`%%joburlid%%`) and a timed-out tracking pixel endpoint. CAN-SPAM compliance has three gaps, and the open-tracking pixel fires over HTTP, guaranteeing suppression in major clients.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Tracking pixel endpoint timeout**
`https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/482395/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
The QA probe received a timeout. Open-rate data for this send is unrecoverable if the endpoint was down at delivery time.

**WARN — 27 click-redirect URLs untested**
All click links route through a redirect domain; HTTP probing was skipped. These cannot be confirmed as resolving correctly. Spot-check at least the primary CTA destinations manually.

**WARN — HTTP open-tracking pixel**
`http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=482395&5/28/2026 12:00:00 AM`
Non-HTTPS. Gmail, Apple Mail, and Outlook strip or proxy-block mixed-content images. Open tracking from this pixel is unreliable. The URL also contains a raw datetime string (`5/28/2026 12:00:00 AM`) as an unencoded query parameter — this should be URL-encoded or removed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**FAIL — DOCTYPE declared after body content (invalid HTML)**
The `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>` declaration appears after a `<div>` and multiple comment blocks. DOCTYPE must be the first node in the document. In some clients this triggers quirks mode.

**WARN — Google Fonts `<link>` tag in `<head>`**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:wght@400;700&display=swap" rel="stylesheet">
```
External CSS `<link>` tags are stripped by Gmail, Outlook.com, and Yahoo Mail. Montserrat will not load in those clients; fallback to Arial/Helvetica will apply. This is expected for email but worth documenting for stakeholders.

**WARN — Two images with missing alt text**
- Tracking pixel: `src: https://pxl.mon-trk.com/…/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` — no `alt` attribute
- Open-tracking gif: `src: http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/…` — no `alt` attribute

Decorative tracking images should carry `alt=""` explicitly to pass accessibility linters and screen reader validation.

**INFO — Empty ADA comment blocks**
```html
<!-- ADA ALT Block starts--> <!-- ADA ALT Block Ends-->
<!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block starts--> <!-- ADA Aria-label_Landmark Block Ends-->
```
These placeholder comments suggest alt-text injection was templated but the blocks were never populated. Verify whether content should have been inserted here by the ESP before send.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**FAIL — Unresolved merge token: `%%joburlid%%`**
One raw token was not substituted at send time. Depending on where it appears in the rendered email (likely a link href or tracking parameter), recipients will either see literal `%%joburlid%%` text or click a broken URL containing it. This needs to be traced to the template variable and the ESP rendering pipeline audited for why substitution failed.

The first-name token in the subject ("Iris") resolved correctly — the failure is isolated to `%%joburlid%%`.

---

### 5. Compliance

**FAIL — No physical mailing address detected**
CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. None was found in the parsed HTML. Confirm it is not hidden in an image or truncated section; if absent, this is a statutory violation.

**WARN — List-Unsubscribe header absent**
The `List-Unsubscribe` header was not captured by the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Apple Mail use this header to surface the one-click unsubscribe UI. Its absence increases spam classification risk and degrades user experience for unsubscribing.

**WARN — List-Unsubscribe-Post absent (RFC 8058)**
Without `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, Gmail's one-click unsubscribe (required for high-volume senders as of February 2024) cannot function. If IHG sends >5k/day to Gmail, this is a deliverability requirement, not just a best practice.

**WARN — Authentication-Results header not captured**
SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown. If the relay is stripping these headers, authentication posture for this send cannot be verified. Request raw header delivery from AgentMail or confirm via the ESP's delivery report.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot fully assess — all 27 click-redirect URLs were skipped by the HTTP probe. The following should be verified manually:

- Confirm UTM parameters (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) are present and correctly encoded on all destination URLs after redirect resolution.
- The `%%joburlid%%` token likely appears in a tracking or deep-link URL; if so, that link's destination is broken for all recipients.
- The open-tracking pixel timestamp query parameter (`5/28/2026 12:00:00 AM`) is malformed and will likely cause server-side parse errors on the IHG analytics endpoint.
