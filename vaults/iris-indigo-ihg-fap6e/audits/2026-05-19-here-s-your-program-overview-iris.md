# Here’s your program overview, Iris
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-19**
## Executive summary

- This is email #2 in IHG's welcome series for Iris, arriving three days after the May 16 "you're now an IHG One Rewards member" send. The fundamental problem: it covers nearly identical ground. The hero is another "WELCOME TO IHG ONE REWARDS" banner, the brand portfolio grid at the bottom is back, and the overarching message — here's what the program is — duplicates the prior email rather than advancing the relationship. If Iris didn't engage with the first send, this one gives her no new reason to.
- The 500-bonus-point hook in the "Get started" section is the only genuinely actionable element that distinguishes this email from its predecessor, and even that offer isn't framed with any urgency or expiration. The visual production is clean — teal buttons, legible type, no broken images — but good execution on a recycled premise still feels like a wasted slot in the welcome cadence.
- Bottom line: this email should be a step forward in onboarding Iris, not a restatement of step one. It earns its open on the strength of IHG's brand and Iris's name in the subject, but it doesn't earn a click.

## What's working

- Personalization in the subject line ("Iris") is a low-cost lift that's correctly deployed here.
- 500 bonus points is a concrete, named incentive — the only specific offer in the email.
- Teal CTA buttons are visually prominent against the white background and easy to tap on mobile.
- No render bugs visible — images load, text isn't overlapping, layout is intact.
- Brand portfolio grid gives a quick, useful reference for which properties are on-program.

## What's weak

- Hero copy is nearly word-for-word the welcome from May 16 — a returning opener is a wasted impression for a member who already saw it.
- The 500-point offer has no visible deadline, reducing any urgency to act now vs. later (or never).
- No progression cue — there's nothing telling Iris "here's what's new since you joined" or "here's your first goal," which is what email #2 should do.
- The brand grid appears for the second time in three days with no new framing (e.g., "stay here for bonus points this weekend"), making it feel like filler.
- Preview text isn't visible in the render, suggesting the preheader may be defaulting to a generic fallback rather than complementing the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Make email #2 about the first milestone, not the program overview.** The welcome email already introduced the program. This send should say: "Iris, you're X points from your first reward — here's how to close the gap." Give her a progress bar, a target property, something to chase.
- 2. **Put a deadline on the 500 bonus points.** "Earn 500 bonus points when you complete your profile by May 31" turns an evergreen nudge into an action now.
- 3. **Replace the brand grid with a targeted property suggestion.** Show Iris one hotel she might actually stay at based on geography or season — not all 18 brands at once.
- 4. **Write a preheader that doesn't repeat the subject.** If it's currently leaking junk, even "500 bonus points waiting for you — here's how to claim them" beats nothing.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Complete your profile this week to claim your welcome bonus.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `You're closer to your first reward than you think.`

## Technical Audit — IHG One Rewards Program Overview

### 1. Technical Summary

One confirmed broken tracking pixel is producing a timeout error, and the open-tracking beacon uses unencrypted HTTP — both are functional defects. Compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) are absent from what was captured by the relay, which is a deliverability risk.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Broken tracking pixel (timeout)**
- URL: `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/459478/0037y00002BeqgfAAB`
- The HTTP probe timed out. This pixel either points to a dead endpoint or the `pxl.mon-trk.com` host is not responding. If this is a lead/conversion pixel, impressions are not being recorded.

**WARN — 27 click-redirect links skipped**
- All clickable CTAs route through a redirect domain. Not probed, so broken destinations cannot be ruled out without a full click-trace test.

**WARN — Open-tracking beacon uses HTTP (not HTTPS)**
- `src: http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=459478&5/19/2026 12:00:00 AM`
- Gmail, Outlook (via proxy), and Apple Mail all proxy or block mixed-content HTTP images. Open rates for this send are likely undercounted. The timestamp embedded in the query string (`5/19/2026 12:00:00 AM`) also appears URL-unencoded — spaces should be `%20` to avoid malformed requests.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**WARN — Two images missing `alt` text**
1. Pixel at `pxl.mon-trk.com` — tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""`, which is absent.
2. Open-tracking beacon at `mi.ihg.com/o.gif` — same issue; the "alt" value shown in QA output is the timestamp string, suggesting the ESP is filling `alt` with a merge field that resolved to a date rather than an empty string or meaningful label.

**INFO — DOCTYPE placement**
- The `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC ...>` declaration appears *after* a `<div>` and several HTML comment blocks. This forces quirks mode in some renderers before the doctype is even parsed. The pre-DOCTYPE content should be removed or the doctype moved to position 0.

**INFO — `<html>` before `<!DOCTYPE>`**
- Related to the above: content exists outside the doctype, which is invalid HTML 4.01. Low rendering risk in modern clients but violates the declared spec.

**INFO — Google Fonts `<link>` in `<head>`**
- `fonts.googleapis.com` is blocked in several corporate email environments (Outlook, Lotus Notes). The fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is correctly defined, so this degrades gracefully, but the external font request adds latency.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The open-tracking beacon query string contains what appears to be a raw timestamp merge field (`&5/19/2026 12:00:00 AM`) as a cache-buster. While it resolved, the format is not URL-safe (see §2 above).

### 5. Compliance

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent from relay capture**
- RFC 2369 compliance cannot be confirmed. If the header is stripped by the AgentMail relay and not present in the envelope received by inbox providers, Gmail and Apple Mail will not render the native unsubscribe button — increasing spam-report risk.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header absent**
- RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed. Google and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements (effective Feb 2024) mandate one-click unsubscribe for senders exceeding 5,000 messages/day. IHG is almost certainly above that threshold.

**WARN — `Authentication-Results` header not captured**
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this relay capture. Sending domain is `mc.ihg.com` (a Marketing Cloud subdomain); alignment with `ihg.com` DMARC policy needs verification through a direct header inspection or mail-tester run.

**No issues found** — Physical mailing address and unsubscribe link appear present in the footer (standard IHG boilerplate), satisfying the CAN-SPAM postal/opt-out requirements at the HTML level.
