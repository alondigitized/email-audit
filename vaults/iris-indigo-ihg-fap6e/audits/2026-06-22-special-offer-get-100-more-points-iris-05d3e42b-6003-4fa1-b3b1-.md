# 💯 Special offer: Get 100% more points, Iris
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-22**
## Executive summary

- IHG One Rewards is running a buy-points promotion with a 100% bonus — the offer is mechanically simple and clearly presented. The email is built for a committed loyalty traveler: a three-step explanation (buy → earn double → redeem), a luxury hotel rooftop hero, and a direct "BUY NOW" button. If the recipient is actively enrolled in IHG Rewards and has a stay in mind, this is a high-signal, low-friction pitch.
- The name personalization in the subject ("Iris") is correctly implemented and the brand is immediately recognizable — both strong inbox signals. What's missing is any redemption context. "100% more points" is compelling only if you know what those points unlock. The email never bridges the math: no "enough for a free night," no balance-aware messaging, no destination hook to create intent rather than speculatively stocking up.
- Clear result: strong open, moderate click. Buying points on spec is a harder behavioral ask than "book a stay." This email earns the open; the conversion depends on whether Iris was already thinking about IHG.

## What's working

- **100% bonus headline is immediate and unambiguous** — no digging required, the offer is visible the moment the email opens
- **Three-step structure** (Buy → Get 100% more → Start planning) reduces cognitive friction and makes the transaction feel trivially simple
- **Name personalization in the subject** is correctly rendered and adds a personal signal without feeling forced
- **Luxury rooftop hero image** communicates aspirational travel lifestyle, appropriate brand tier, and earns trust before the body copy begins
- **Brand portfolio footer** demonstrates breadth — 18+ hotel brands make the points feel universally useful

## What's weak

- **No redemption anchor in the hero** — "100% more points" needs a "= free night at X" equivalency or it's an abstraction asking the reader to do math
- **Deadline appears buried** — no expiration date is visible in or near the hero block; urgency lives in the fine print
- **"BUY NOW" CTA is the weakest possible verb+noun** — it describes the mechanic, not the benefit; "Double My Points" lands differently
- **Eighteen-brand logo grid below the CTA** creates visual noise that breaks the scroll-to-purchase momentum
- **No account-balance context** — a smart send would reference how many points the member already has and how close they are to a reward tier

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a value anchor to the hero copy** — "5,000 bonus points = a free night at thousands of IHG hotels" gives the reader a reason to buy, not just a fact about bonus math
- 2. **Surface the deadline in the hero block** — "Offer ends July 15" placed beside or under the CTA creates urgency without requiring the reader to scroll to fine print
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA to sell the outcome** — "Double My Points" or "Buy Points — Get Double Free" frames it as a win rather than a purchase
- 4. **Collapse the brand logo grid** — one hero brand image + "Redeem at 1,000+ hotels in 100 countries" conveys the same scope with far less visual clutter
- 5. **Include account-balance or tier-gap context** — "You're 3,200 points from Gold status — buy now and get there in one transaction" would make this email personal, not just personalized

- **Subject Alt B:** `Iris, 100% bonus points — stock up before [date]`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy points now, get double. Redeem for free nights at Crowne Plaza, Kimpton, InterContinental and more.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your 100% bonus expires [date]. Here's how much a free night actually costs.`

## Technical Audit — IHG One Rewards "100% More Points" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email has a critical CAN-SPAM compliance failure (no detectable unsubscribe mechanism) and a broken privacy statement URL. Authentication headers are absent from the relay capture, leaving deliverability posture unverified. 18% QA pass rate driven by these issues plus pervasive missing alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**FAIL — Broken privacy statement URL (trailing period)**
URL: `https://www.ihg.com/content/gb/en/customer-care/privacy_statement.`
Trailing `.` after `privacy_statement` will produce a 404 for end users. This is a template authoring error, not a 403 from auth gating.

**WARN — SSO gateway links returning 403**
Both `ssogateway.points.com` CTAs (buy and gift) returned 403. These are likely expected for unauthenticated probes — SSO links require session context — but should be validated in a seeded test session to confirm they aren't expired or misconfigured.

**INFO — `fonts.googleapis.com` / `fonts.gstatic.com` 404s are false positives**
The QA probe hit bare domain roots, not the actual `<link>` tag paths. These are `<link rel="preconnect">` hints, not navigable URLs. Disregard.

**WARN — 28 tracking links unverified**
Click-redirect domains skipped by probe. Destination URLs and redirect chain integrity are unconfirmed.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Google Fonts blocked in most email clients**
```html
<link href="https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:..." rel="stylesheet">
```
Gmail, Outlook, and Apple Mail strip `<link>` tags in `<head>`. Montserrat will not render in the majority of inboxes. No declared fallback font stack visible in the truncated source — confirm a system-font fallback is present in `font-family` declarations.

**Empty `<title>` tag**
`<title></title>` — some clients (notably Outlook preview pane) surface this. Should be set to the email subject or left with a meaningful value.

**31 images missing `alt` text**
Includes content-bearing images, not only spacers:
- `0612cf26-...png` (`image.points-mail.com`) — likely hero/promotional image, no `alt`
- `603f2770-...png` (`image.points-mail.com`) — likely secondary content image, no `alt`
- `Shopping-cart-icon_1-...` — functional icon, no `alt`
- `star-icon_2-...` — functional icon, no `alt`
- 27× `spacer-IHG-email-template-new-2024-update.gif` — spacers should carry `alt=""` (empty string explicitly set), not omitted entirely

Spacers with omitted `alt` cause screen readers to announce the filename. Decorative images require `alt=""`, not a missing attribute.

### 5. Compliance

**FAIL — No unsubscribe link detected in body (CAN-SPAM § 5(a)(3))**
QA found zero opt-out links. The HTML is truncated, so the footer may exist but was not captured for analysis. If the footer is present in the live send, this finding may be a QA capture artifact. If absent: critical violation requiring immediate fix before send.

**WARN — `List-Unsubscribe` header absent**
Not captured via AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo (as of 2024 bulk sender requirements) require `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) for any sender over 5,000 msgs/day. Absence will suppress the native unsubscribe UI in Gmail and flag the sender with Yahoo.

**WARN — No `Authentication-Results` header captured**
SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment unverified. Sending domain is `points-mail.com` (not `ihg.com`) — confirm DMARC policy on `points-mail.com` and that IHG's DMARC on `ihg.com` permits this third-party sender via SPF include or DKIM alignment.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Inconsistent UTM tagging across CTA links**

| CTA | UTM params present |
|---|---|
| Buy points (`ssoProduct=buy_fasttrack`) | None |
| Gift points (`ssoProduct=gift_fasttrack`) | `utm_campaign=IHGM5&utm_source=IHGM5&utm_medium=email` |

The primary "buy" CTA has no UTM parameters — click-to-conversion attribution will be lost for that flow. The gift CTA has UTM params but `utm_source=IHGM5` is a campaign code, not a source value. Standard convention: `utm_source=ihg_email` or `utm_source=email`, `utm_medium=email`, `utm_campaign=IHGM5`.
