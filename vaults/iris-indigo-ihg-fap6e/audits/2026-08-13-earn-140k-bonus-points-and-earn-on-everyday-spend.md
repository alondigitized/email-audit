# Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This is the same 140,000-point Chase credit card acquisition offer IHG has now run repeatedly — the July 16 "Earn 140K bonus points AND earn on everyday spend" send and the July 23 "Where would you go with 140K bonus points?" send both hit this exact number, and now it's back a third time with near-identical creative (poolside Kimpton hero, "0 → +140,000 = 140,000" points-balance math graphic, 5X/3X/2X earning tiles). At this point Iris has seen this card pitch three times in four weeks and the subject line doesn't even bother to differentiate — it's the verbatim July 16 subject reused word for word.
- The render itself is clean and on-brand: a nice hero image, a legible points-math visual, clear earning-rate icons, and a bold red APPLY NOW CTA repeated three times down the page. But this is a financial-product ad, not a stay-your-next-trip email, and running it a third time with recycled subject and creative reads as fatigue rather than strategy — this cadence issue was already flagged on July 23 (7/10) and it hasn't been addressed.

## What's working

- Hero image (poolside cabana) is warm, high-quality, and renders without artifacts.
- The "0 balance → +140,000 bonus → 140,000 total" stacked math graphic is an effective, skimmable way to visualize the offer size.
- Earning-rate tiles (5X gas/dining, 3X other, 20X hotel stays, Anniversary Free Night, 4th night free) are clearly iconized and easy to scan.
- Three APPLY NOW CTAs are placed at natural decision points (top, after math, after benefits) so the eye never has to hunt for the next step.
- Full brand-family footer (Kimpton, InterContinental, Crowne Plaza, Holiday Inn, Candlewood Suites, etc.) reinforces IHG's scale credibly.

## What's weak

- Subject line is a verbatim repeat of the July 16, 2026 send — zero differentiation for a subscriber who's already seen and presumably ignored it once.
- Third 140K-point card pitch in under a month (July 16, July 23, now Aug 13) with the same hero visual and math graphic — this is the exact fatigue pattern flagged on July 23's 7/10 review, now recurring without a creative refresh.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — no expiration date on the 140K offer, which weakens the case for acting now versus later.
- This is a card-acquisition email, not a stay/points-redemption email — for a subscriber who is already a member, repeated card pitches over actual travel content risk feeling like a bank ad more than a hospitality brand.
- "THE LIST GOES ON" divider before the second card image is vague filler copy with no informational value.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire or meaningfully refresh the 140K creative before the next send** — same hero image and same points-math graphic three times in a month trains subscribers to skip it on sight. Rotate in a different property, different visual treatment, or pivot the angle entirely (e.g., lead with the Anniversary Free Night or 4th-night-free perk instead of the raw point count).
- 2. **Add real urgency** — a stated offer end date or "ends in X days" banner would give a reason to act today instead of filing this away with the last two identical emails.
- 3. **Segment repeat non-openers/non-clickers off this specific offer** — if Iris hasn't converted on 140K points twice already, a third identical ask is unlikely to land; test a different offer type (companion stay discount, points multiplier on an upcoming trip) for this cohort.
- 4. **Lead the subject with the differentiator, not the repeated headline number** — "AND earn on everyday spend" is the only new element versus July 16 and it's buried at the end of the subject.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still time to earn 140K points — plus rewards on every purchase`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your everyday spending could earn 140,000 points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New: earn 5X on dining and gas, 3X on everything else`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Apply now — your points balance could hit 140,000 before your next trip`
