# Destination Deals: Save up to 30% right now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-27**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, simple member-deals send. A teal "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE / Destination Deals" hero sits above a single orange "BOOK NOW" button. A second "Book now" button sits below the "Save up to 30% now" headline, and the offer is visible quickly. It's a clear step up from the credit-card sends of 10-01 to 10-22, and it feels closer to the 10-12 "Save up to 20%" email, which was IHG's best recent send.
- The weak spots are the offer detail and the second half of the email. The specific dates, 10/27–11/17 for booking and 10/31–12/13 for stay, are buried in small body copy. The "Terms apply" link is small. The HUALUXE feature below the offer pulls attention toward a brand story instead of a deal. The brand-logo wall and long legal footer make the email feel heavy after a short promo. The hero image of the Hotel Indigo Alishan terrace is attractive but not obviously tied to a specific destination or deal.
- Verdict: solid, honest, and well organized, but generic. It lacks personalization and specific destinations or prices, so it's a 5–6 send rather than a standout.

## What's working

- A single, clear offer ("Save up to 30%") with one repeated orange CTA, "BOOK NOW", easy to find.
- The "MEMBER EXCLUSIVE" label gives a loyalty signal at the top.
- A strong, scenic hero image under a clean teal header.
- Booking and stay windows are stated, which makes the deadline credible.
- The back-to-back orange CTA and white-space layout make the hierarchy easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The offer details and dates sit in small gray body copy, not in the headline.
- "Up to 30%" with no destination, hotel, or price example gives the reader nothing concrete.
- The HUALUXE editorial block competes with the deal. Its "EXPLORE HUALUXE" link is a soft CTA and sends the reader to a brand page.
- The brand-logo wall and the long legal footer add a lot of length after the main message.
- There's no personalization such as name, points balance, or tier, even though it's a member offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the booking deadline into the hero: "Book by 11/17 · Save up to 30%". Cut the vague "Destination Deals" headline.
- 2. Show 3–4 real destination tiles with "from $X/night" prices and one CTA each, instead of a single generic hero.
- 3. Replace the HUALUXE editorial block with a deal-led module or a member-rate comparison, or move it below the deals.
- 4. Shrink the logo wall to a one-line brand strip.
- 5. Add personalization, such as a nearby destination or the member's tier.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is mostly well-formed (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, responsive CSS), but it has a malformed tracking-pixel URL, markup that comes before the DOCTYPE, and a "Terms apply." link that returned 403 in the probe. Header-level compliance and authentication can't be verified from the data supplied.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **403 on "Terms apply."** The URL is `https://www.ihg.com/offers/destination-deals?cm_mmc=EMAIL-_-IHGOR-_-AMER-_-en-_-REV-_-DestinationDeals_20262583-_-SENDURLID-_-557800&mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=557800&trso=EMAIL_557800#below`.
  - A 403 from ihg.com to an automated probe is more likely bot or WAF blocking than a dead page. Confirm with a real-browser check before treating it as broken.
  - The link goes to the offer page itself (`#below`), not a dedicated terms URL.
- **Malformed tracking pixel.** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=557800&10/27/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - It uses `http://`, so it can be blocked or rewritten as mixed content.
  - It ends in a bare `&10/27/2026 12:00:00 AM`, which has no key, unencoded spaces and slashes, and what looks like a server-rendered datetime. This probably breaks attribute parsing: the QA tool labelled the image `2026 12:00:00 AM`. Open tracking may be unreliable in strict parsers.
- **Possible unresolved placeholder.** The `cm_mmc` value contains the literal segment `-_-SENDURLID-_-`. This may be substituted at click time by the ESP, but it is worth confirming that it resolves.
- **Skipped links.** 21 tracking/redirect links skipped the HTTP probe, so their destinations are unverified.
- **Subscriber identifiers in URLs.** `pxl.mon-trk.com/.../557800/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` has an 18-character Salesforce Contact ID in the path. `mi_u=695150425` is also a per-recipient identifier. Both are visible to any proxy or forwarder.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** Comment blocks and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN"...>`. This can trigger quirks mode in some clients and webviews.
- **Dead CSS.**
  - `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing the leading dot, so it targets a nonexistent element instead of the class.
  - `max-height: auto !important` in the `img` rule is not a valid value.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` has a raw `&`.
- **Missing alt on both tracking pixels** (`0037y00002BeqgfAAB`, `o.gif`). Because they are 1×1 pixels, `alt=""` is the correct fix, not descriptive text.
- **Web font dependency.** The Google Fonts `<link>` is stripped by many clients. The Arial/Helvetica fallback stack is present, so the impact is low.
- **Truncated source.** Alt text on content images, table `role="presentation"`, and the footer markup can't be checked from the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Rendered datetime in the pixel URL.** `&10/27/2026 12:00:00 AM` is the only token-related defect confirmed. It looks like a date merge field that was unkeyed and unencoded. The time is 12:00:00 AM, which suggests a date-only field was formatted as a datetime.
- **No other unresolved tokens** (`%%...%%`, `{{...}}`) in the visible source. The `SENDURLID` segment is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058).** Both are not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw headers at the sending ESP.
- **Authentication-Results.** Absent, probably because of the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. The sender domain is `mc.ihg.com`. Verify its alignment from the raw message.
- **Footer.** The unsubscribe link and physical address are not in the truncated source, so they can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **Parameter scheme.** The email uses IHG's own parameters (`cm_mmc`, `mi_u`, `mi_ecmp`, `trso`) and has no `utm_*` parameters. This is consistent for IHG's analytics but won't register in GA-style UTM reporting.
- **Campaign ID.** The campaign ID `557800` is consistent across `mi_ecmp`, `trso` and the pixel path.
- **Landing page.** The landing page is `/offers/destination-deals`, which matches the subject ("Destination Deals"). The `#below` anchor depends on the page's anchor handling, which wasn't verified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the `o.gif` pixel. Switch it to `https://`, key and URL-encode the date value (for example `&sent=2026-10-27`), and make sure the `src` attribute is quoted.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Move the DOCTYPE to the first line and put the preheader div inside `<body>`.
4. Correct the CSS to `.ReadMsgBody` and remove `max-height: auto`.
5. Escape the ampersand in the title as `&amp;`.
6. Re-test the "Terms apply." link with a browser user agent. If it loads, whitelist the probe. If it doesn't, point it to a real terms page.
7. Confirm `SENDURLID` resolves in the delivered click URLs.
8. Pull the raw headers from the original send to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and the SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
9. Consider whether the Contact ID in the `mon-trk.com` pixel path should be replaced with an opaque token.
