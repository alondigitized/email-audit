# 🛎️ Ding ding! Your 20,000 points offer ends soon.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the follow-up to IHG's 2026-09-28 send, "how's 20,000 points sound?". It's the same register-and-stay offer: 20,000 bonus points for a 2-night stay, with registration open until October 13. It is a better email than the recent credit-card sends because it has one offer and one action. The headline "EARN 20,000 BONUS POINTS" and a red "REGISTER IN 1-CLICK" button sit at the top, with a three-step explainer below.
- The weak part is the reminder framing. "Ding ding!" and "ends soon" sound urgent, but nothing in the visible body adds a countdown or a new reason to act. The body is mostly the same mechanics as last week. After the second CTA, the email runs through a brand-logo wall, a long legal block and a footer, which is a lot of scroll with nothing for the reader to act on.
- My judgment is a solid, honest loyalty reminder. The offer is clear and the button is easy to find. It is undersold on urgency and gives the reader no hook to click now instead of later.

## What's working

- One offer and one action: "EARN 20,000 BONUS POINTS" with a red "REGISTER IN 1-CLICK" button right under the headline.
- The "Earn in 3 easy steps" block (register, book, stay) makes the mechanics easy to scan.
- The CTA is repeated after the steps, so a reader who scrolls doesn't have to go back up.
- The deadline is stated in the body copy: register by October 13, with 60 days to stay.
- The hero pool and resort image and the IHG One Rewards branding fit the loyalty-member context.

## What's weak

- Nothing in the visible body lifts "ends soon" above generic urgency. There is no countdown and no "X days left", and the October 13 date is buried in a paragraph.
- It is a near-repeat of the 09-28 send, so a subscriber sees the same offer twice with only a new subject line.
- The hero image is small relative to the headline block, and the top of the email is mostly text.
- A long brand-logo wall (Six Senses, Regent, Kimpton, Holiday Inn and others) sits between the CTA and the footer. It pulls attention away from the offer without giving the reader anything to act on.
- The legal block is very long, and the deadline and terms read as fine print, which hurts the "honest offer" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the real deadline in the headline or just under it, for example "Register by Oct 13", and add a "days left" line. This is the single biggest lift because it turns "ends soon" into a concrete date.
- 2. Use a more specific CTA, such as "Register for 20,000 points". "REGISTER IN 1-CLICK" is fine, but it doesn't name the reward.
- 3. Add an example, such as "A 2-night stay = 20,000 points ≈ a free night". Showing the value makes the number meaningful.
- 4. Trim the logo wall, or move it below a "Book now" link, so the second CTA is the last strong call to action.
- 5. Show this reminder only to members who haven't registered or haven't booked, and send a different message to those who have.
- **Subject Alt A:** `20,000 points: register by Oct 13`
- **Subject Alt B:** `8 days left to claim 20,000 bonus points`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Register once, stay 2 nights, and 20,000 points land in your account.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your offer window closes October 13. Register in one click.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible markup has no blocking defects. Two real issues are in the tracking pixels: a malformed query string with an unencoded date, and an `http://` source. The HTML source is truncated, so footer, unsubscribe, and body links could not be audited. The QA header warnings are most likely relay artifacts.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Malformed open-tracking pixel.** `http://mi.ihg.com/p/up/2b47e4754a78cfd7/o.gif?mi_u=695150425&mi_ecmp=537821&10/5/2026 12:00:00 AM`
  - The last parameter has no key, and its value contains unencoded spaces and slashes (`10/5/2026 12:00:00 AM`).
  - This looks like a date merge field that was inserted raw into the URL. Clients and proxies may truncate or reject the URL, which would under-count opens.
- **Non-HTTPS pixel.** The same `mi.ihg.com` pixel loads over `http://`. This is mixed content and may be blocked or rewritten.
- **Identifier in a pixel path.** `https://pxl.mon-trk.com/954cb38e-4a43-4730-b327-6b102c213a01/537821/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` ends in what looks like a Salesforce Contact ID (the `003` prefix). This is a per-recipient identifier exposed in a URL.
- **Click links not probed.** 36 tracking links were skipped by the QA tool. Their destinations and redirect health are unverified.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Content before the DOCTYPE.** HTML comments and `<div style="display:none;"> </div>` come before `<!DOCTYPE HTML PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD HTML 4.01 Transitional//EN"...>`. Anything other than comments or whitespace before the DOCTYPE can push some clients into quirks mode, and a `div` is not a comment.
- **Broken selector.** `ReadMsgBody { width: 100%; }` is missing its leading dot (it should be `.ReadMsgBody`). As written it targets a non-existent element, so the rule never applies.
- **Invalid CSS.** `max-height: auto` is not a valid value, and the media-query rule `img .header` is a descendant selector where `img.header` was probably intended. Both are ignored by clients.
- **Unescaped ampersand.** `<title>IHG Hotels & Resorts</title>` uses `&` instead of `&amp;`. This is minor.
- **Web font.** The Montserrat `<link>` is stripped by several clients. A fallback stack (`Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif`) is declared, so there is no breakage.
- **Pixels missing alt text.** Both tracking pixels were flagged. They are decorative, so they should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Template alt markers.** The `ADA ALT Block` comments are empty placeholders, so no template-level alt injection is visible.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]` tokens appear in the visible source.
- The date in the `mi.ihg.com` pixel URL is likely a merge-field formatting error (see section 2).

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool itself notes these may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. This is unconfirmed, not a verified defect.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found.** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown, again likely because of the relay. Check the raw headers from a direct delivery.
- **Not verifiable.** The footer unsubscribe link and physical postal address fall after the truncation point.
- **Sender.** `IHGOneRewards@mc.ihg.com` is a subdomain of the brand domain, which is acceptable.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- No UTM parameters or final landing URLs are visible, because the 36 click links go through tracking redirects that were not resolved.
- The subject promises "20,000 points", but the offer could not be matched to a landing page. Resolve the primary CTA redirect to check this.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Fix the `mi.ihg.com` pixel: serve it over HTTPS and URL-encode the date. Better, drop the unkeyed date parameter or give it a key, such as `&mi_dt=2026-10-05`.
2. Add `alt=""` to both tracking pixels.
3. Move the hidden `<div>` and the comments inside `<body>`, or remove them, so the DOCTYPE comes first.
4. Correct `.ReadMsgBody` and the invalid `max-height: auto` and `img .header` rules.
5. Re-run the audit on the full HTML. Check the footer unsubscribe and address, resolve the CTA redirects, and confirm UTM parameters.
6. Check the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, SPF/DKIM/DMARC) from a direct delivery rather than through the relay.
