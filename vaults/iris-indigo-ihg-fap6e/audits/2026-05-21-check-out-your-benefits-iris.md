# 👀 Check out your benefits, Iris
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-21**
## Executive summary

- This is email #3 in IHG's welcome series for Iris — arriving just two days after the May 19 "program overview" send and five days after the original welcome. The fundamental problem is the same one that plagued #2: IHG is retreading the same "welcome to membership" territory a third time. The hero shot is a beautiful resort pool with "IT'S BETTER TO BE A MEMBER" — a near-identical message to what Iris saw on May 16. The only genuinely new piece of value is the 200 bonus points offer to get started, which should have been the lead in email #2, not buried in email #3.
- What saves this from being a complete retread is the 200-point concrete incentive and the app-centric onboarding push, both of which are actionable. The brand portfolio grid at the bottom is a reliable scroll-stopper. The 👀 emoji in the subject line is a misjudgment for a luxury hotel brand — it reads more Shein than Six Senses.
- The series has now burned three sends establishing essentially the same premise: being an IHG member is good. Iris knows. She signed up. It's time to give her a reason to book.

## What's working

- **200 bonus points offer** is the first concrete transactional incentive in the series — it gives Iris a reason to act, not just read.
- **App promotion with device mockup** is a clean, functional onboarding step and a natural next action after enrollment.
- **Brand portfolio grid** at the bottom is comprehensive and visually reassuring — shows the scope of where points can be redeemed.
- **Visual design is clean** — no render bugs, consistent IHG brand colors (teal CTA), legible hierarchy.
- **Personalization holds** — "Iris" in the subject continues the series' strongest consistent signal.

## What's weak

- **The hero message is recycled** — "IT'S BETTER TO BE A MEMBER" is the same thesis as emails #1 and #2; Iris is already a member and doesn't need a third pitch on why membership is good.
- **👀 emoji in the subject** clashes with IHG's luxury positioning — it's the kind of emoji a fast-fashion newsletter uses, not a Six Senses partner.
- **"Chances to being a Club member" copy** in the step section reads like an unedited translation artifact — grammatically off and slightly confusing.
- **No urgency on the 200 bonus points** — the offer has no visible expiration, which reduces the motivation to act now vs. later (meaning never).
- **Three welcome emails in five days** is aggressive; by this send, Iris is primed to tune out rather than click.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 200 bonus points as the hero, not a tagline.** Replace "IT'S BETTER TO BE A MEMBER" with "Earn 200 bonus points on your first stay — here's how." Make the entire email about activating that one benefit. Everything else is noise until she uses the program once.
- 2. **Add an expiration to the 200-point offer.** Even "Offer valid through June 30" creates enough credible urgency to shift this from "I'll do it later" to "I should do this."
- 3. **Drop the emoji from the subject.** "Your member benefits, Iris — plus 200 bonus points" is stronger, on-brand, and more specific.
- 4. **Fix the "Chances to being" copy** — this should read "Steps to becoming" or "How to earn Club status."
- 5. **Slow the welcome cadence.** Emails #2 and #3 can be merged into one well-constructed "here's your first action" email rather than two near-identical orientation sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Iris, your 200 bonus points are waiting`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Make your first stay count — 200 bonus points inside`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Earn 200 points when you book your first stay as an IHG One Rewards member.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your first booking earns bonus points. Here's how to claim them.`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**[WARN] Two images missing `alt` text**

| Image | Source |
|---|---|
| Tracking pixel | `pxl.mon-trk.com/…/0037y00002BeqgfAAB` |
| Open beacon | `mi.ihg.com/p/…/o.gif` |

Tracking pixels conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string) to suppress screen-reader announcement. Neither has it declared. This is a minor but real WCAG 2.1 violation (criterion 1.1.1).

**No issues found** with the core responsive layout. The `@media` breakpoints cover 320–480px, `ReadMsgBody`/`.ExternalClass` resets for Outlook.com are present, and `lang="en"` is set on `<html>`. ### 5. Compliance

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe header absent from captured headers**
- RFC 2369 requires a `List-Unsubscribe` header for bulk commercial email. The QA system notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay — verify against raw headers at the ESP (Salesforce MC) level before treating as a sender-side defect.

**[WARN] List-Unsubscribe-Post header absent**
- RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk sender policies (>5k/day). Same caveat: likely relay-stripped, but must be confirmed.

**[WARN] Authentication-Results header absent**
- SPF/DKIM pass status unknown from captured headers. `mc.ihg.com` is a Salesforce MC sending domain; DKIM signing is expected. Verify via raw `.eml` headers or MXToolbox on the actual delivered message.
