# Technical Audit: "Fire up your weekend with up to 30% OFF🔥" (lowes@e.lowes.com)

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based template with a malformed DOCTYPE. Its impression pixels carry the recipient's email address in plaintext query strings. Several images lack alt text, and the unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified. The HTML was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are partial.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **32 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destination validity, redirect chains and 404s are unverified.
- **The impression pixels have an empty parameter.** Two `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels end in `&stpe=default&li_coord=`. This looks like an unresolved or empty geo-coordinate value.
- **The email address is exposed in pixel URLs.** Every `sli.lowes.com/imp?...&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&...` URL carries the recipient address unencoded. It will appear in proxy logs, image-cache logs and any forwarded copy of the message. The raw `@` in a query string is also unescaped.
- **Duplicate pixels.** Slot `s=1090834` fires twice with identical parameters, so impressions may be double-counted.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **The DOCTYPE is malformed.** The source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no space after `PUBLIC`. Strict parsers may fall back to quirks mode.
- **The XHTML namespace uses `https`.** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the DTD URL `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/...` both use `https`. The canonical namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is low impact in email clients but is non-standard.
- **Two content images have no alt text.**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Their content is lost when images are blocked or a screen reader is used.
- **The six `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels also lack alt text.** They should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`. This is low severity because they are decorative.
- **Web fonts are limited to non-Outlook clients.** Fellix and DIN Next load via `@font-face` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. The fallback font stacks are beyond the truncation point, so I can't confirm them.
- **No other issues are visible in the captured source.** The viewport meta tag is present, along with the mobile media queries and the `x-apple-data-detectors` override.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **No unresolved `%%…%%` or `{{…}}` tokens are visible** in the captured HTML.
- **Possible exception:** the empty `li_coord=` value noted in section 2 may be a personalization or geo field that did not resolve.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` was not found** (QA WARN). The QA tool says this may be an AgentMail relay artifact, so it is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) was not found** (QA WARN). The same caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is unverified, and Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not captured** (QA WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` is unknown.
- **The footer could not be checked.** The visible unsubscribe link and postal address are past the truncation point.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- **UTM parameters cannot be evaluated.** The tracked links point at click-redirect domains and were not resolved, so final landing URLs and UTM values are not confirmed.
- **The tracking identifiers don't map to a site-visible UTM scheme.** The pixel parameters (`s`, `p=1022026`, `lctg=410146703`) are not UTMs. I can't verify whether the same campaign ID reaches the landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing (`PUBLIC "-//W3C…"`) and use the `http://` XHTML namespace.
2. Add descriptive alt text to the two content images (`c301e24e…jpg`, `ad569dc5…png`). Add `alt=""` to the impression pixels.
3. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels with an opaque hashed or tokenized recipient ID. Remove the empty `li_coord=` or make sure it populates.
4. Remove or dedupe the repeated `s=1090834` pixel.
5. Confirm that the sending MTA emits `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`. Check the raw `.eml` rather than the relay copy.
6. Re-run the audit against the full, untruncated HTML and the raw headers. Then check SPF, DKIM and DMARC, the footer, and the UTM values on the resolved links.
