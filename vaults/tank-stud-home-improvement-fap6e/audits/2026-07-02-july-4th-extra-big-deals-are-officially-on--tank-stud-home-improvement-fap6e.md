# July 4th Extra Big Deals are officially ON 🎉🎉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-02**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday's countdown email had me primed — July 4th Countdown Deals, new savings every day, I was actually curious what today's offer would be. Then this drops with "Extra Big Deals are officially ON 🎉🎉" and the hero is a washing machine and a French door fridge. I own my washer and dryer. I'm not in the market. The grill and the outdoor power equipment are further down — and honestly those are the two things that actually belong in this email if you're talking to me.
- The bones are solid. The countdown timer showing 3 days to July 4th works. The 25% off grills is legitimately interesting since I've been eyeing a replacement for my busted Weber. The "Save up to $100 on outdoor power equipment" section also caught my eye — I need a new string trimmer before the long weekend. But Lowe's buried the lead under two appliance deals that would be perfect for a different list segment.
- I'd mark this read and bounce, but I'd probably scroll back to that 25% grill deal and tap "Shop now" before closing. I would not forward this to anyone.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer is a genuine hook.** "3 days, 6 hours, 47 minutes" — that's urgency that has teeth, not just "limited time only" copy. I respect it.
- **25% off grills and accessories is well-timed.** July 4th is the single biggest BBQ shopping weekend of the year. Relevant and credible.
- **Outdoor power equipment section lands.** Red trimmer/blower shot, save up to $100 — this speaks to me as a guy who needs the yard squared away before company shows up.
- **Specific product specs visible.** "33-in wide, French door" and the Whirlpool pair pricing with an additional $50 instant save — at least they're showing real numbers, not just vague "big savings."
- **Clean render, clear hierarchy.** No broken images, readable font sizes, "Shop now" buttons are obvious. Not a mess.

## What's weak

- **The hero is two appliances I don't need.** Washer-dryer pair and a fridge as the lead deals on a holiday sales email — that's household shopper content, not weekend warrior content. It made me feel misread.
- **Subject is generic.** "Extra Big Deals are officially ON" tells me nothing. Officially on what, for who, why now? Yesterday's countdown email was better framed — at least it implied daily reveals.
- **No MyLowe's loyalty callout.** After the MyLowe's Money Days email on June 30th had me excited about member benefits, there's zero loyalty hook here. Am I earning points on this? Does my card earn extra? Silence.
- **Double emoji on the subject line feels desperate.** One 🎉 would be fine. Two reads like a used car lot trying too hard. Gen X doesn't do double-exclamation-point energy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the backyard deals, not appliances.** Flip the email — grills and outdoor power equipment in the hero, appliances as a secondary panel. July 4th is a backyard weekend; dress accordingly.
- 2. **Get a dollar amount or category into the subject line.** "Save up to $100 on outdoor gear + 25% off grills" is worth more than "Extra Big Deals." You have the numbers, use them.
- 3. **Wire in a MyLowe's loyalty prompt.** After Monday's Money Days tease, there's built-up expectation. A single line — "MyLowe's members: extra savings stack on select items" — closes the loop and rewards the subscribers who opened that email.
- 4. **Cut one emoji from the subject.** Single 🔨 or 🎇 fits the vibe better than 🎉🎉.
- 5. **Make the outdoor power CTA specific.** "Shop now" on a category section is fine; a single hero product (like a specific CRAFTSMAN trimmer at a named price) converts harder than a department landing page.
- **Subject Alt A:** `July 4th: 25% off grills + up to $100 on outdoor power tools`
- **Subject Alt B:** `3 days left — your backyard deserves this deal`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Grills, patio furniture, power equipment — all on sale before the 4th.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `MyLowe's members: loyalty savings stack on select July 4th deals.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's July 4th Email

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is a standard XHTML transitional table-based build with no critical rendering failures, but has three compliance gaps (missing RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe headers, unknown SPF/DKIM status), a CDN font version split, a DOCTYPE syntax error, and multiple images missing alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Dual open-tracking pixels (potential misconfiguration)**
Two separate `o.gif` pixel requests fire from the same domain:
```
https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/e5de1e722e335a2e/o.gif?mi_u=410146703
https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/d4a3a42d8d3123b3/o.gif?mi_u=410146703
```
Different path hashes with identical `mi_u` query param. This inflates open-rate counts or signals a duplicate include of a tracking snippet. Confirm whether both are intentional (e.g., A/B pixel test) or a template paste error.

**20 click-tracking links unverifiable**
All CTA links route through a redirect domain (flagged by QA; domain not captured in truncated source). Cannot confirm UTM parameters, final destination URLs, or whether redirect chains resolve correctly. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**DOCTYPE syntax error**
```html
<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>
```
Missing space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted string. Strict XML parsers and some email validators will reject this. Correct form: `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD ...`.

**XHTML namespace uses `https://` instead of `http://`**
```html
<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">
```
The XHTML namespace URI is canonically `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. Using `https://` is technically invalid per the spec. Most clients ignore this, but XML-strict renderers may fail namespace resolution.

**CDN font version split — DIN Next family**
```
DIN Next (regular):   /scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/din/din-next.woff
DIN Next Bold:        /scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/din/din-next-bold.woff
DIN Next Black:       /scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/din/din-next-black.woff
```
Regular loads from `1.4.4`; Bold and Black load from `1.2.0`. If the font files differ between versions (metrics, hinting), mixed weights will render inconsistently. All three should reference the same scaffold version.

**`.woff` only — no `.woff2`**
All six `@font-face` declarations (Fellix + DIN Next) load only `.woff`. `.woff2` is supported in every modern email client that supports web fonts and compresses ~30% smaller. Fallback behavior is fine, but the omission increases load time when fonts do render.

**Responsive breakpoint gap**
The first media query targets `min-width: 375px AND max-width: 600px`. The second targets `max-width: 480px`. Devices between 480px–600px wide receive styles from the first query only (WebKit-gated via `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0`), which excludes non-WebKit clients in that range.

**Four images missing alt text (QA confirmed)**

| Image | Type | Alt missing |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` | Content image | Yes |
| `ad569dc5-...png` | Content image | Yes |
| `o.gif` (×2) | Tracking pixels | Yes |

Content images without alt text render as broken icons in image-blocked environments (Gmail default for new recipients, Outlook corporate). Tracking pixels should have `alt=""` explicitly set to suppress broken-image indicators.

### 5. Compliance

**Missing `List-Unsubscribe` header (QA confirmed)**
Not captured by the AgentMail relay. This header is required by Google's bulk sender policy (≥5,000 msgs/day to Gmail). Its absence risks Gmail surfacing its own unsubscribe prompt or routing to spam.

**Missing `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header — RFC 8058 (QA confirmed)**
One-click unsubscribe (required by Google and Yahoo since February 2024 for bulk senders) depends on both `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` being present. Neither is confirmed present.

*Note: Both headers may exist at the MTA layer and simply not be forwarded by the AgentMail relay. Verify against raw SMTP headers before treating as a sender-side defect.*

**SPF/DKIM status unknown (QA confirmed)**
`Authentication-Results` header not captured. Sending domain is `e.lowes.com` (ESP subdomain). Cannot confirm alignment between `From:` domain (`lowes.com`) and DKIM `d=` value. If `d=e.lowes.com` rather than `d=lowes.com`, DMARC alignment fails unless relaxed alignment is configured.

**CAN-SPAM physical address**
Not visible in truncated source. Standard for Lowe's; flag only if absent from full HTML.
