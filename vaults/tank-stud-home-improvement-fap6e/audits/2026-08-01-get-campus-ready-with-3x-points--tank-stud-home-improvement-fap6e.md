# Get campus-ready with 3x points 🎓
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- Dorm bedding and a desk lamp — this is a "back to campus" email, and I don't have a kid in college. Zero relevance to me. It's a well-built email, I'll give it that — clean layout, one clear offer, one clear button — but it's aimed at a completely different Lowe's shopper than the guy running rebar and Kobalt tools through his garage all summer.
- The "3x Points" badge is the only thing that'd make me pause, because I am a MyLowe's Rewards member and points-on-eligible-purchases is something I actually pay attention to. But the second I see "best semester yet" and a twin XL bed with string lights vibe, I'm scrolling past. Not clicking, not forwarding — I don't have a kid heading to a dorm.

## What's working

- The "3x Points" hexagon badge is bold and readable at a glance — that's real loyalty-program language I recognize as a member.
- One button, one offer ("Activate now") — no confusion about what they want me from.
- Date range (7/16–8/5) is right there under the headline, so if this did apply to me I'd know the window.
- Clean, uncluttered layout — no render bugs, image loads fine, text is crisp.

## What's weak

- The whole creative is dorm furniture — bed, throw pillows, desk lamp. Nothing here says "weekend reno" or "contractor job" to me.
- "Start your best semester yet" — I'm 49, I'm not in a semester, and if I did have a college kid this reads more mom-targeted than dad-targeted.
- No project-bundle relevance at all — no tools, no lumber, no fasteners, nothing tied to what I'm actually mid-project on this time of year.
- Points offer is generic ("eligible purchases") — doesn't tell me if it covers the stuff I buy (Kobalt, lumber, hardware) or just dorm decor.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment this. If Lowe's has any purchase history on me, they know I haven't bought a twin XL mattress topper or desk lamp in years — don't send me back-to-campus creative at all. Save it for actual dorm-shopping households.
- 2. If they're going to send a broad "3x points sitewide" push to non-parents, swap the hero image for something category-neutral — tools, paint, outdoor — and keep the points badge, since that part does work on me.
- 3. Spell out what "eligible purchases" covers. If it includes tools/hardware/lumber, say so — that's the difference between me activating and me deleting.
- 4. Tie the CTA to my actual purchase pattern — "Activate 3x points on your next tool or project purchase" would pull me in; "campus-ready" does not.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Activate 3x points before your next project run`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Points stack on tools, hardware, and more through 8/5.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `No dorm shopping required — just activate and earn.`
