# Hosting Thanksgiving? It’s almost go time🌟
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- I'm not hosting Thanksgiving, so the subject line missed me. I still opened it because it's Lowe's, and I was hoping for something project-related. The top is a $129 turkey fryer and a "Black Friday: the deals came early" banner. Under that is a giant red 50% off appliances block, a cutting board, GE lighting, and a $500k home sweepstakes. It reads like a flyer that got dumped in my inbox.
- The only things for me are three product tiles way down the page: a DeWalt 12V battery, a toilet, and a storage tote. The toilet and the DeWalt battery are the kind of thing I'd buy for a weekend project. They're tiny and buried, though. Compare the 11/19 "2 FREE power tools" send, which led with Kobalt, Craftsman and DeWalt. This one felt like Lowe's talking to someone else.
- Verdict: I'd skim it and mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. I might tap the DeWalt tile if I needed a battery, but nothing here pushes me to.

## What's working

- The "Black Friday" banner and "FREE same-day delivery" strip are clear and look like Lowe's.
- The $129 turkey fryer is one concrete price, and the hero photo is big and easy to read.
- The ALL-IN appliance block is dense but states its terms: up to 50% off, free delivery, free haul-away, and an extra $250 off laundry.
- The DeWalt, toilet and tote row at least shows real SKUs with Buy now buttons.
- The MyLowe's Rewards tag at the top acknowledges I'm a member.

## What's weak

- The subject promises a Thanksgiving host theme. A DIY guy with no hosting plans gets nothing from it.
- Almost nothing is aimed at projects, tools or pros. The tools content is three small tiles at the bottom.
- It's the fifth or sixth Lowe's send this week, and the appliance 50% off banner is repeated from 11/15 and 11/17. That's fatigue.
- The page is jammed with offers: fryer, appliances, cutting board, lighting, sweepstakes and Deals of the Day. There's no single hero, so my eye bounces around.
- The 12 Days of Deals tile is hard to read. The GE ad strip is a sponsored block that breaks the flow.
- The fine print at the bottom is a wall of tiny text. The appliance terms are buried there, along with the $896 spend threshold and the rebate-style conditions.
- There are no ratings or reviews, and nothing about stock.

## Recommendations

- 1. Segment it. Send members with a tool or project history a tools-first version, with a DeWalt or Kobalt hero and the product row at the top. Keep the turkey and appliance version for hosts and appliance shoppers.
- 2. Cut it to one hero and two supporting blocks. Pull the sweepstakes and the cutting board.
- 3. Give the product tiles prices, member pricing and ratings, and make them bigger.
- 4. Put the appliance terms, like the $896 threshold, in plain sight near the button instead of the footer.
- 5. Slow the cadence, and don't repeat the same appliance banner day after day.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Fix-it list before the guests show up`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fryers from $129, plus tool and bath deals through Black Friday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free same-day delivery for MyLowe's members on in-stock items.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The visible markup is mostly standard, but there are a few confirmed issues. The `<!DOCTYPE>` is malformed, `<html>` has no `lang`, and two content images lack alt text. The recipient's address also appears in plaintext in the ad-impression pixel URLs. Compliance, authentication and link-destination checks could not be confirmed from the data provided.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Recipient email in plaintext query strings.** All 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels carry `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531283&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. An unhashed address in a URL ends up in server and proxy logs and in any image-proxy cache.
- **39 tracking links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains and HTTP status are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- No broken links were detected, but the QA data doesn't cover them.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source has `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`. There is no space after `PUBLIC`. Clients are usually tolerant of this, but it is invalid and can trigger quirks mode in some parsers.
- **No `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`. Screen readers can't pick the language.
- **Content images missing alt text.**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If either carries content or a CTA, it is invisible with images off.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** The 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no alt. These are likely 1×1 pixels, so the fix is `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"`). That is low severity.
- A viewport meta tag and mobile media queries (`max-width: 480px`) are present. The Outlook `<!--[if !mso]>` font wrappers are used correctly.
- The HTML was truncated, so I can't evaluate `<title>`, table roles or the body.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source. The only personalization is the address embedded in the pixel URLs (see section 2).

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed**, not a verified violation.
- **Authentication-Results not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, for the same relay reason. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, but alignment couldn't be checked.
- The body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are not in the truncated source, so they could not be checked.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity**
No issues found, but little could be verified. Because the 39 click-tracking links were not resolved, UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be assessed. No UTMs are visible in the supplied source.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Stop putting the raw recipient address in pixel URLs. Use an opaque or hashed subscriber ID in `sli.lowes.com/imp`. This is the highest-priority item.
2. Fix the DOCTYPE by adding the missing space: `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD…"`.
3. Add `lang="en"` to `<html>`.
4. Add descriptive `alt` to `c301e24e-….jpg` and `ad569dc5-….png`, and `alt=""` to the `imp` pixels.
5. To close the QA gaps, re-run with raw `.eml` headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and the full untruncated HTML. Also resolve the 39 tracking redirects to check destinations and UTMs.
