## What's working

- The "FREE same-day delivery" strip plus the MyLowe's Rewards badge tells me up front that membership matters.
- The red hero is clear: "up to 50% off select major appliances." The eye lands there first.
- The $24.99 Crock-Pot and $59.99 Keurig/air fryer give me real prices and not just percentages.
- The flooring "install by Christmas, free in-home measurement by 11/29" block has actual dates. That's project language.
- The DEWALT battery/charger kit in the bottom row is the only thing that feels aimed at me.

## What's weak

- The hero's stacked offers are confusing: free delivery, free install, free haul-away, 2-year protection plan, "$896 or more," and a $250 laundry bonus. I can't tell what I actually get without reading the footnote.
- The tool deal is buried at the bottom with no price and no savings shown. The product title is cut off ("3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C"), which looks sloppy.
- The vacuum "up to 40% off" and the gift-sized small appliances are not my wheelhouse.
- Nothing is pro or contractor oriented: no Pro rewards, no bulk pricing, no project bundle.
- The footnote is a wall of legal text. The $896 threshold and the exclusions undercut the "50% off" headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move a tool or project row (DeWalt, Kobalt) up under the hero, with a price and a "save $X" line. Don't tuck it away in a generic "curated" block.
- 2. Cut the hero down to one offer. Lead with 50% off and put the delivery, haul-away, and protection details in a line underneath.
- 3. Fix the truncated product titles and add a price or savings to every product tile.
- 4. Segment by shopper type. A guy who keeps buying tools shouldn't get a gift-guide layout.
- 5. Add a rewards or card prompt, such as "Extra 5% back with your Lowe's card."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is mostly standard, but the automated checks show an empty greeting slot ("Hi ,") and missing alt text on two content images. The recipient's email address is also written in plaintext into 15 ad-impression pixel URLs. Several compliance and authentication checks could not be verified from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 38 tracking links were skipped (HTTP probe not run on click-redirect domains), so destinations, redirect chains and link health are unconfirmed.
- **Recipient address in URLs:** the `sli.lowes.com/imp?...&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&...` pixels carry the unhashed address in the query string. These URLs show up in proxy, CDN and referrer logs. Confirmed on all 15 impression pixels (`s=127768300`–`127768304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`).
- **Duplicate pixels:** `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` each appear twice (the `stpe=default&li_coord…` variants). This may double-count impressions.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If either is a content image, images-off clients and screen readers get nothing. Filename-style alt text would also be unhelpful.
