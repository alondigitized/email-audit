# 💰MyLowe's Money Days are HERE💰
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's finally landed the Money Days event email on the actual start date — July 9 — which makes this the logical follow-up to the "Are you ready?" teaser from July 6th. That sequencing is fine. What I see above the fold is clean and fast: bold blue hero, "MONEY DAYS!" in big white type, dates July 9-12, and a concrete threshold — $300+ purchase earns you EXTRA MyLowe's Money. If I'm buying materials this weekend anyway, that's worth knowing.
- The problem starts the moment I scroll. "Big savings for a beautiful yard" with what looks like a lawn tractor and some patio furniture tells me this isn't curated for my cart — it's a lifestyle sweep. I'm knee-deep in deck railing work. I want dimensional lumber, balusters, maybe a pressure washer. "Beautiful yard" patio staging is my neighbor's email, not mine. The loyalty mechanic is strong but the product merchandising undersells it for a project shopper.
- I'd open it (already did), skim it, and mentally file it as "qualifying window is this week, plan my materials run accordingly." I wouldn't click right now. I wouldn't forward it — my buddies would need to be MyLowe's members AND mid-project for it to matter.

## What's working

- **"For the first time ever" actually earns the emphasis.** The novelty claim on the EXTRA earning tier is a real hook for a loyalty member who's calibrated what a standard Money Days earns. That copy line works.
- **The $300+ threshold maps to real project behavior.** Not $50 in impulse buys — three hundred bucks is one good materials run. It respects how I actually shop.
- **Dates July 9-12 are visible in the hero without hunting.** Four-day window, right there. Urgency is verifiable, not manufactured.
- **Clean render throughout.** No broken images, no text overlap, no lorem ipsum. The blue hero reads instantly at mobile size.
- **Logical campaign arc from the July 6th teaser.** "Are you ready?" → "It's HERE" is a two-beat sequence that makes sense. This didn't blindside me.

## What's weak

- **Product tiles are wrong for a project shopper.** "Big savings for a beautiful yard" + patio seating is lifestyle, not reno. If that green item is a riding mower, that's a once-a-decade purchase — not what activates a Money Days run for me.
- **"EXTRA" never becomes a number.** How much extra? An extra 1%? An extra $25 on a $300 run? Show the math or the tease feels like fine-print bait.
- **"Shop now" is the laziest CTA for a loyalty event.** The whole mechanic is about earning — the button should say "Shop & Earn" or "Start Earning." "Shop now" is what you put on a clearance tile.
- **The in-store event section takes up prime vertical space I don't need.** I shop online for pickup. The brand-partner logos and event-day callout mean nothing to me scrolling on my phone.
- **Seven Lowe's emails in nine days is getting heavy.** I'm not unsubscribing but I'm starting to skim rather than read. The cadence is working against the signal quality.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the EXTRA earnings rate in the hero.** Even a range — "earn up to X% more" — makes the $300 threshold feel calculated, not arbitrary. Right now I don't know if it's worth timing my lumber run to this week specifically.
- 2. **Swap the product tiles to power tools, building materials, or outdoor power equipment.** If there's a pressure washer, a circular saw, or a bulk fastener deal in there — lead with that. Let the patio furniture tile live below the fold or in a secondary section.
- 3. **Rewrite the CTA button.** "Shop & Earn" or "Earn MyLowe's Money" over "Shop now." The event mechanic IS the differentiator — put it on the button.
- 4. **Collapse the in-store event section for digital-purchase segments.** Keep it for customers who've browsed in-store recently. For the rest of us, it's dead scroll.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Earn more this weekend: MyLowe's Money Days, July 9-12`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `First time ever — extra rewards on every qualifying project trip. Four days only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Spend $300+ on your next reno run and earn more MyLowe's Money than ever. Ends Sunday.`
