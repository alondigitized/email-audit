# MyLowe’s Money Days are so OOONNN 🤩
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third Money Days send in six days. I got the $10/$20 offer on 10/6, again on 10/7 and 10/9, and now this one. The subject has a pile of O's and an emoji, and the body is the same offer wearing a new hat. Within one screen I knew what it was, because the $10 back on $200 block is clear enough. That's also the only part I can use. I still have a half-finished drywall job, so a $200 materials run is real money to me.
- Below the offer it turns into a variety show. There's a MrBeast Day, MrBeast builder kits, 3x points on "fall hosting favorites" (a patio table with pumpkins), and Dan + Shay concert tickets. None of that is a tool, a project or a rebate. The hero is a bundle of free socks, a luggage tag and a bottle opener. It's a Sunday in-store giveaway, and it's 10 to 1, so by the time I read this it's probably half over. I'd skim it, remember the $200 threshold, and move on. I wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- The Money Days block is clear. $10 on $200, $20 with the Lowe's card, with dates (Oct 6–14) right there.
- The card tier is a real loyalty hook. I get a reason to put the $200 on the Lowe's card.
- The "Shop now" and "Activate now" buttons are big, high-contrast and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The sender is MyLowe's Rewards, so I know who it's from and why I'm getting it.
- The blue brand look is consistent and easy to scan.

## What's weak

- The hero is a giveaway of socks and swag, limited to the first 150 members at a store between 10 and 1. That's a weak lead for a guy with a project going.
- It's the same $10/$20 offer I've seen three times this week. I've stopped reading it.
- Concerts and MrBeast don't fit a 49-year-old DIYer. A whole Dan + Shay panel with fine print takes up a third of the email.
- The "3x points" block is for patio hosting, not for tools or materials. It doesn't touch my reno at all.
- The fine print is dense. It runs from the "Offer cannot be combined" line to the concert terms, and I can't tell whether Money Days stacks with the 3x offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with Money Days and put it at the top. Skip the Sunday giveaway as the hero. Make the first screen "$10/$20 back on $200, ends 10/14."
- 2. Segment by project. Show me drywall, flooring, tools or paint in a "put your $200 to work" block with a few items. That gives me the dollar threshold and the product together.
- 3. Cut the concerts and MrBeast from this send, or move them to a separate entertainment email for the people who want them.
- 4. Add a countdown, such as "4 days left." The 10/14 deadline is only in small text in the offer block.
- 5. Pick a different offer each send instead of repeating the same one. Vary the cadence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 back on $200 with your Lowe's card — ends 10/14`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your next project's $200 just earned $10 back`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stack 3x points on top. Money Days ends Oct 14.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members get $10 back, $20 with the Lowe's Rewards Card.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted public identifier. Some parsers may drop out of the intended doctype mode. The DTD URL and `xmlns` also use `https://www.w3.org/...`, but the XHTML namespace is defined as `http://`. This is low risk in email clients.
- **Missing `lang`:** `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">` has no `lang` attribute, so screen readers can't pick the correct language profile.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed):** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. If this image carries content or is linked, it is announced as a filename or nothing at all. If it is decorative, it needs `alt=""`.
- **Good practice present:** `viewport` meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` reset, and `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guards around `@font-face`.
- **Font fallbacks:** the font stacks used in the body couldn't be seen. Fellix and DIN Next load from `lowescdn.com` and fall back to system fonts in clients that strip `@font-face`. Confirm each inline `font-family` has a web-safe fallback.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}` or `[FirstName]` appear in the truncated portion.
