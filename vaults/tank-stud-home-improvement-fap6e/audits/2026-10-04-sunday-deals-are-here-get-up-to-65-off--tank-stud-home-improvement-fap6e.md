# Sunday Deals are here. Get up to 65% OFF 🤩
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- It's Lowe's again, and it's the fifth send in a row that feels like a grab bag. Smart home was 9/27, 9/30 and 10/1. Grills were 10/2. A kids' tote was 10/3. Now "Sunday Deals" has smart home again, appliances, a skeleton, outdoor lighting and AA batteries. Nothing here tells me it's for a guy with a drywall project half done.
- The one thing I actually noticed is the MyLowe's Money block. $10 back on $200, or $20 back if I use the Rewards card, October 6–14. That's the only part that sounds like a real shopper plan. I'd bank it for the next lumber or paint run. But it's buried in the middle, and the date range is tiny gray text.
- I'd skim it and probably not click. I wouldn't forward it. I'd leave it unread until I'm pricing materials and want to know if the MyLowe's Money week is worth timing around.

## What's working

- The MyLowe's Money block is a real member perk with a dollar amount and dates (Oct 6–14). That's the one thing that sounds like a plan.
- The $20 credit-card tier is spelled out against the $10 member tier, so I can tell what the card is worth.
- "Save 5% every day" for cardmembers is clear, and it's the kind of thing I'd do the math on for a big project.
- The numbers are big and easy to read on a phone: 65%, 35%, 30%. The product circles are clean.
- The batteries and storage tote at the bottom are practical items that fit a workshop.

## What's weak

- The hero says "Sunday Deals" with no product at all. It's a blue slab with a "Shop now" button.
- The first deal block is Alexa, Ring and Fire TV again. I've seen that same pitch three times this week.
- A free skeleton and gift imagery are Halloween and holiday filler. They have nothing to do with my weekend.
- Nothing is pro or project oriented. There's no lumber, tools, drywall, paint or rebates, even though "Outdoor Tools" and "Bathroom" are in the nav bar.
- The fine print is tiny. The MyLowe's Money terms and the 5% disclosures are hard to read.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row is just a tote and two battery packs. It doesn't look curated for me. The tote title is cut off with "108.0 -".

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the MyLowe's Money offer and its dates, then put one hero project category under it. Right now the best offer is in the middle of the email.
- 2. Segment by buyer. Someone who's bought drywall, lumber or tools shouldn't get the smart-home block again.
- 3. Make "Curated with you in mind" real. Show recently viewed or project-based items, with stock and price.
- 4. Drop the Halloween and gift filler. Replace it with one pro or DIY block, like tools, building materials or rebates.
- 5. Fix the clipped product title and make the fine print bigger.

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit: Lowe's "Sunday Deals are here. Get up to 65% OFF 🤩"

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a conventional table-based XHTML Transitional build with responsive media queries. Confirmed issues are a malformed DOCTYPE, missing `alt` attributes on two content images and five tracking pixels, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in pixel URLs. The header-based compliance and authentication warnings are not confirmed defects, because the capture path likely stripped those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 35 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so none have been checked for destination, redirect chain or status.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834&…&stpe=default&li_coord=` appears twice with identical parameters. This may double-count impressions.
- **Empty parameter:** both `s=1090834` pixels end in `li_coord=` with no value, so a coordinate or merge value did not populate.
- **Impression pixels:** five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels (s=1090834, 1090835, 1090837, 1090838) are tracking beacons. Each carries `p=1042026` and `lctg=410146703`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" …>`, with no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. Strict parsers may reject it, and it risks quirks mode in some clients.
- **Missing alt on content images (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  With images blocked, or for screen readers, these will be empty or announced by filename.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""`, which is a low-severity omission.
- **Namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` uses `https`, but the XHTML namespace is `http://`. This is harmless in HTML parsing.
- **No other issues found in the visible source.** The viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the `!mso` conditional font-face blocks and the 480px and 600px media queries are present. The HTML was truncated, so the body markup was not audited.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **PII in URLs:** the recipient's address is in plaintext in the query string of every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel, as `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. These URLs can be logged by proxies, CDNs and client image proxies. They can also leak via forwarded copies or "view in browser" pages.
- **Unpopulated value:** `li_coord=` is empty, as noted in section 2.
- **No unresolved tokens** such as `%%…%%`, `{{…}}` or `[First Name]` appear in the visible portion.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** QA reports both missing. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Check the raw message source to verify.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found. The QA note attributes this to the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status are **unknown, not failing**. The From domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain.
- **Body footer:** the physical address and unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Could not be assessed. The 35 click-tracking links were not resolved and the HTML was truncated, so no UTM parameters or landing-page destinations were observed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images (`c301e24e…jpg`, `ad569dc5…png`). Add `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` to the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
3. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in the pixel URLs with an opaque or hashed subscriber ID.
4. Investigate the empty `li_coord=` value and the duplicated `s=1090834` pixel.
5. For this audit tool, capture the raw headers (`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, `Authentication-Results`) from the original message, not the relay copy. Also resolve the 35 skipped tracking links through a redirect-following check so that UTM and landing-page continuity can be assessed.
