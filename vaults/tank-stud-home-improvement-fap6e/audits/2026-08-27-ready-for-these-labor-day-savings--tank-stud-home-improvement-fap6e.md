  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

This is a personalization/merge-token pass-through used for open tracking, but embedding a raw email address in a GET request URL means it can leak via browser referrer headers, proxy/CDN logs, or third-party image-loading services. Flagging as a data-handling issue rather than a functional bug.
- No unresolved merge-tag artifacts (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{token}}`) observed in the visible source.
