# Why wait for Black Friday? Up to 50% OFF appliances
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the 11/10 tools email I liked, with an appliance banner added. The subject sells appliances, but the first thing under the hero is the "2 FREE power tools" DeWalt/Craftsman/Kobalt deal with a Craftsman recip saw. That's my lane. Having to scroll past a red "Black Friday" hero to reach it is the same mistake as last time.
- The appliance block is the biggest thing in the email, and I'm not buying a fridge this weekend. The "50% off" is loud, but the math is buried. It's "up to 50%" on "select" appliances, and the free delivery, install and haul-away need $896 or more. I did like the 50% off ladders. A ladder is an actual weekend purchase. The 5% credit card pitch is fine, but I've seen it before.
- Would I click? Yes, on the power tools or the ladders. Would I forward it? No, unless a buddy is replacing his washer. I'd save it for the next reno phase.

## What's working

- The "2 FREE select power tools with a battery kit" block is a real offer for a DIYer, and the saw image shows what I'd get.
- Ladders at up to 50% off are a practical, project-driven item.
- The MyLowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" strip at the top is a member perk I can see right away.
- The credit card "SAVE 5% every day" block is easy to read.
- The nav (Black Friday, Tools, Daily Deals, Appliances) gets me where I want fast.

## What's weak

- The subject leads with appliances, but my best deals are tools and ladders, so the subject and the body don't match.
- The hero is a generic "Black Friday deals are LIVE" with no product in it. It tells me nothing.
- The appliance block is crowded with badges: free delivery, install, haul-away, the 2-year plan and a $250 laundry save. It's hard to tell what I actually get, and the $896 threshold is small.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row has a DeWalt charger, a toilet and a tote. The toilet and the tote feel random. The product names are cut off ("Charger (", "- Gallons ( 108.0 -").
- The Gift Zone image has a "you can't wait to give" tagline that's hard to read over the busy photo.
- The fine print is a wall of text, and it's tiny.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the tools offer in the hero. Lead with "2 FREE power tools" and the saw, and move the appliances below it. Or send a separate appliance email to people who shop appliances.
- 2. Simplify the appliance block to one headline: "Up to 50% off + free delivery on $896+". Move the other badges into the fine print.
- 3. Make the product row match the hero and the project. Show 3 tools or battery kits, with full names and prices.
- 4. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop free tools" instead of "Shop now".
- 5. Show an end date near the top, not only in the fine print.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ladders 50% off + free tools: early Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Pick a DEWALT, CRAFTSMAN or Kobalt kit and get 2 tools free. Ends 12/2.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Appliances up to 50% off, plus ladders and free delivery on $896+.`

# Technical Audit: "Why wait for Black Friday? Up to 50% OFF appliances"

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible source shows a malformed DOCTYPE, no `lang` attribute, and two images without alt text. The QA run also could not verify unsubscribe headers, authentication, or any of the 31 tracking links. The HTML is truncated, so findings below are limited to what is visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **31 tracking links were not probed.** The QA tool skipped them as click-redirect domains. Their destinations, redirect chains, and validity are unverified.
- No broken links were confirmed, and no malformed `href`s are visible in the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** The source has `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted FPI. Strict parsers may reject it. Most clients fall back to quirks mode, which can change box-model and spacing behavior.
- **Missing `lang` attribute.** The tag is `<html xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml">`, with no `lang` and no `xml:lang`. Screen readers cannot pick a pronunciation profile.
- **Images missing alt text (QA-confirmed):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Either add descriptive `alt` text or `alt=""` if the image is decorative.
- **Namespace and DTD use `https://`.** The XHTML namespace and DTD are normally `http://`. This is harmless in practice, since clients don't fetch it.
- **Positives:**
  - A viewport meta tag is present.
  - `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` is set.
  - Web fonts are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`.
  - `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` is reset.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens such as `%%...%%`, `{{...}}`, or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible portion. The truncated remainder was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN).** The QA note says the header may not be captured by the AgentMail relay, so this is unconfirmed.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (WARN, RFC 8058).** This is likewise unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules expect one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN).** SPF, DKIM, and DMARC status is unknown, and the relay is the likely cause. The From domain is `e.lowes.com`.
- The visible HTML does not include the footer, so the in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This cannot be determined. All 31 links were skipped, and no destination URLs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source. The `image.e.lowes.com/lib/...` path suggests a Salesforce Marketing Cloud image CDN, so click tracking is probably wrapped in redirects. That is an inference, not confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`.
2. Add `lang="en"` and `xml:lang="en"` to `<html>`.
3. Add `alt` text to the two flagged images (`c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`).
4. Check the raw `.eml` headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results`, to separate real gaps from relay stripping. Consider capturing raw headers in the monitor so these checks stop returning inconclusive WARNs.
5. Resolve the 31 tracking links with a redirect-following probe, such as a HEAD/GET that records the final URL. That would let the audit validate landing pages and UTM parameters.
6. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to cover the footer, the unsubscribe link, and any merge tokens.
