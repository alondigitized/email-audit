# These concert perks? Goated 🪩
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-19**
## Executive summary

- Not one tool, not one appliance, not one project in the entire email — it's a Dan + Shay concert sweepstakes and 2-for-1 lawn tickets. I'm a 49-year-old guy running Home Depot Pro and MyLowe's lists to keep my reno projects moving, and Lowe's decided to email me about a country pop tour instead of anything I'd actually shop for. This isn't even in the neighborhood of what I opened my last few MyLowe's emails for — the EGO mower deal, the appliance sale, even the goofy ice mold at least tied back to a home-related freebie.
- This one's a hard pass. I'm not entering a concert sweepstakes through my hardware store's rewards account, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anyone — if anything I'd screenshot it and ask a buddy "why is Lowe's sending me Dan + Shay tickets." Mark as read, move on, maybe side-eye my email preferences afterward.

## What's working

- The hierarchy is clean — sweepstakes hero, artist bill, tour dates, member-perks banner, footer, in that order, no clutter.
- Concert dates and cities are laid out clearly in a scannable grid, so if I did care I could find my city fast.
- "Member perks" framing at least nods to loyalty status, even if the perk itself is irrelevant to me.
- No render bugs — images loaded, text is legible, buttons are clearly buttons.

## What's weak

- Zero connection to my reason for being on this list — no tools, no rebates, no project bundle, nothing homeowner or pro related.
- The offer (2-for-1 concert tickets, sweepstakes entry) has nothing to do with how I shop Lowe's — I'm there for lumber, mowers, and appliances, not Live Nation tie-ins.
- "Enjoy access to 2-for-1 tickets" as the preview line just repeats what I'd already guess from a concert-heavy subject — no reason to open beyond curiosity.
- Feels like Lowe's is renting out my inbox for a Live Nation partnership rather than talking to me as a customer with active projects.
- No fall-project seasonal tie-in at all, when this is peak reno season and I'd expect a lawn-cleanup or fall-refresh push instead.

## Recommendations

- 1. Drop this send entirely for project-driven segments like mine, or bury it way down after actual product content — lead with something in my wheelhouse (tools, appliances, seasonal project bundle) and tuck the concert perk as a small loyalty footnote, not the whole email.
- 2. If Lowe's insists on sending it, reframe the subject/preheader so it doesn't read as pure entertainment spam to a DIY guy — tie it to "MyLowe's Rewards members get more" rather than leading with the band name.
- 3. Add a real MyLowe's Pro or homeowner tie-in somewhere in the body — even a single row of fall tools would make this feel less like a total non-sequitur.
