- "$250 off select outdoor power equipment" is a real, specific number, not some vague "savings event" language.
- The free-item-with-purchase banner (battery + trimmer/blower combo shown) is a legit incentive for someone already in the battery-platform ecosystem.

- Kobalt "one powerful battery platform" callout at the bottom ties back to tool compatibility, which is exactly the kind of detail I actually care about before I buy.

## What's weak

- The email loses the thread fast — mulch, mums, and a patio bench/pillow section have nothing to do with power equipment and push my actual offer further down.
- Two pastel mini toolboxes (pink and lavender) with a swatch of color dots feels like it wandered in from a totally different audience — that's not speaking to me at all.
- No mention of Pro/MyLowe's rewards points or a card-linked discount stacking with the $250 off — if I'm a member, treat me like one.
- "Sponsored" tag on the Kobalt mower/equipment block near the bottom undercuts trust a little — feels like an ad slotted into my own inbox.
- No specific product names or model numbers on the hero mower deal — I don't know if my EGO mower qualifies until I click through.

- 4. Cut or relocate the pink/lavender mini toolboxes — swap for a photo of the standard Kobalt tool chest lineup if you want tools in that slot at all.
- 5. Add a MyLowe's rewards/points callout near the CTA — even one line like "Earn 5% back with your Lowe's card" would close the loop for a guy who already has both pro accounts.
