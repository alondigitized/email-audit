# MrBeast Workshops are almost out 😳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- Opened it, got confused, then annoyed. I'm a MyLowe's member. I've been getting Lowe's emails every single day this week — washers, patio sets, and now this. MrBeast Workshops for kids. I'm forty-nine years old. I've got a half-built deck, a Ryobi battery platform, and a tile saw sitting in my garage. What am I going to do with a Swarm Launcher Workshop for $14.78? Register my kid, maybe — if I had one in the right age range — but that's not why I'm on the MyLowe's Rewards list.
- The "LAST CHANCE" banner got my attention for about a half second, and "Going FAAAAST" almost made me think there was a flash deal on fasteners or something. Nope. It's a craft workshop run by a YouTube guy I know exists but don't watch. The email is clean, the layout works, the hierarchy is fine — whoever built the template did their job. The problem is someone aimed it at the wrong list.
- I'm marking this read without clicking. I wouldn't forward it to anyone my age. Maybe to my sister who has a 10-year-old.

## What's working

- **"LAST CHANCE" banner + "Going FAAAAST"** — the urgency stack got my eyes moving fast, even if the content wasn't for me
- **Price is front and center** — "$14.78" with the word "Just" is clean and visible, no hunting
- **"Register now" button is obvious** — single blue CTA, can't miss it
- **Visual hierarchy is solid** — hero to price to CTA flows top-down without fighting itself
- **MyLowe's Rewards logo at top** — I recognize the brand immediately; no trust issue

## What's weak

- **Wrong audience entirely** — this is a kids programming email, not a homeowner or DIY email; I'm 49 and single-household, not a parent signing up crafts workshops
- **MrBeast brand does nothing for me** — I know who he is but that logo and his face hold zero pull for Gen X; it actually reads younger than Kids Club already suggests
- **After 7+ Lowe's emails this week, patience is thin** — the May 22 "$1,700 off" email that turned out to be washers already tested my goodwill; this doesn't rebuild it
- **No tool, project, or DIY hook anywhere in the email** — not even a sidebar, not even a footer link to what I actually care about
- **Digital badges for completing workshops** — I have no idea who this is for, but it's definitely not me

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this out of the MyLowe's general DIY list entirely** — send it only to accounts with child/family signals (Kids Club registrants, child-size apparel purchases, past workshop registrations). This landing in a contractor-adjacent list is a list hygiene problem, not a creative problem.
- 2. **If you can't segment, at least add a footer rescue block** — "More of a power-tool guy? Shop tools →" with one relevant link salvages something for mismatched opens.
- 3. **Kill the "Going FAAAAST" wordplay for this audience** — the pun only works if your reader already cares about the product; otherwise it just reads as loud noise.
- 4. **The "LAST CHANCE" banner could pull double duty** — if there's a Memorial Day tool deal still running (per the May 25 send), cross-link it here for subscribers who skew non-family.
