## What's working

- **65% off is a genuine hook** — the hero copy lands cleanly, the number is big and readable, product logos (Ring, Blink) are visible immediately. I know exactly what I'm looking at.
- **Navigation tabs (TOOLS, DAILY DEALS) are smart** — a guy like me might skip the smart home section and tap straight to TOOLS out of habit.
- **Father's Day framing in the preheader** ties a bow on why the email exists right now.
- **Visual hierarchy in the hero is solid** — 65% off is the first thing the eye lands on; no competing junk above it.

## What's weak

- **The expired countdown is a trust killer** — "You missed today's offer" with 00:00:00 is the kind of thing that makes a subscriber wonder if Lowe's QA'ed this at all. Kills credibility fast.
- **Kids Club / Swarm Spinner Workshop** — I have no idea why this is in my inbox. A $14.99 kids craft event is not a 49-year-old male DIYer offer.
- **"Shop now" x3** — three identical generic CTAs with no differentiation across three completely different sections. Lazy.
- **No MyLowe's member angle** — Lowe's has my account. They know I buy tools and lumber. Not one mention of member pricing, Pro rewards, or anything that says "we know who you are."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the dead countdown or don't send the email** — if the timer has expired, either suppress the module or pull the send. An all-zeros timer signals sloppy automation, not urgency.
- 2. **Collapse the email to one core offer** — smart home deal OR Father's Day tool gift OR kids workshop, not all three. This thing is trying to sell me Ring cameras and a Swarm Spinner in the same scroll. Pick one.
- 3. **Swap "Shop now" for offer-specific CTAs** — "Shop Ring & Blink deals," "See today's Father's Day pick," "Browse gift ideas" — make me know where I'm going before I tap.
- 4. **Surface MyLowe's member pricing** — if I get a better rate as a member, put that in the hero. That's the one thing that would make me feel known.
- 5. **Drop the Kids Club section from the DIY segment** — segment it out. A project-driven homeowner and a parent shopping for a $14.99 kids event are not the same person.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**8 images missing `alt` text** — confirmed by QA:

| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| Hero image | `image.e.lowes.com/.../c301e24e-...jpg` |
| Open-pixel gif | `mi.lowes.com/p/cp/.../o.gif?mi_u=410146703` |

| Product/promo image | `image.e.lowes.com/.../ad569dc5-...png` |

The hero image (`c301e24e...jpg`) and promo image (`ad569dc5...png`) are content-carrying — missing `alt` breaks screen reader access and renders blank in image-off clients (Gmail default for unknown senders).

The tracking/beacon `<img>` tags should have `alt=""` (empty string) — omitting `alt` entirely is a WCAG failure even for decorative/functional pixels.

**Responsive breakpoints:** Two overlapping media queries (`min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`) with conflicting `.container` width rules. On 481–600px viewports both queries are partially active, which can produce layout inconsistencies. Low severity.

**Font loading:** Custom fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) are WOFF-only — no WOFF2 fallback. Most modern clients support WOFF2 and would benefit from it; WOFF-only is not a bug but is suboptimal. Gmail and Outlook will fall back to system fonts regardless.

No unrendered merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `[FNAME]`) found in the visible HTML excerpt. Recipient ID `410146703` is consistently applied across all beacon URLs, indicating proper token resolution at send time.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header: NOT DETECTED** — QA could not confirm the header via the AgentMail relay. If absent at the MTA level, this violates Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirements (mandatory since February 2024 for >5k/day senders). Lowe's volume almost certainly exceeds this threshold.
