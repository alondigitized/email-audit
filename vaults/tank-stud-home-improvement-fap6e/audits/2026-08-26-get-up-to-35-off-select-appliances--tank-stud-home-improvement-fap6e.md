# Get up to 35% OFF select appliances 🤩
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- Okay, second appliance email from Lowe's in five days — same 35% off Frigidaire/Electrolux play I already saw on 8/21, but this one's dressed up better right out of the gate. Real washer/dryer pair, dishwasher, fridge, range/microwave stacked right at the top, no lifestyle fluff, and this time they actually spell out the extras: free delivery, free basic install, haul-away, free basic parts, plus a 2-year protection plan thrown in over $1,799. That's the kind of stacked-value breakdown that gets a guy who's actually pricing out a kitchen reno to stop and read.
- The buy-more-save-more ladder (Buy 2 save $100, Buy 3 save $200, Buy 4+ save $300) is smart — that's exactly how I shop when I'm doing a full kitchen or laundry room, not one-off. Below the fold it turns into a grab-bag though — storage, vanities, doors/windows install, batteries, a cardboard box — feels like five different emails mashed into one scroll.
- I'd click the appliance block for sure. Wouldn't forward it — nothing here is a "hey check this out" moment for a buddy, it's just me pricing my own kitchen. Compare to the 8/19 patio clearance send (8/10 from me) — that one had a tighter single focus. This one's got the better hero but loses me by the third scroll.

## What's working

- Real appliance photography up top — washer, dryer, fridge, range, microwave, no staged lifestyle shot wasting my time.
- The install/delivery/haul-away/parts breakdown next to the 35% badge answers the "what's this actually cost me" question before I even click.
- Buy-more-save-more tiers match how I actually shop a multi-appliance reno, not a single-item impulse buy.
- "Ends tomorrow" urgency at the very top bar is credible given Lowe's cadence — they've used real deadlines before (patio clearance 8/19).
- Doors and windows "installed by Thanksgiving" block is a solid seasonal nudge for a homeowner planning ahead.

## What's weak

- Way too much crammed in after the appliance hero — storage, vanities, doors, essentials batteries, a literal cardboard box "curated with you in mind." None of that is appliance-adjacent, it just dilutes the one strong offer.
- The fine print block at the bottom is a wall of gray legal text — exclusions, financing caveats, membership carve-outs — nobody's reading that on a phone, and it undercuts the "honest offer" feel a little.
- "New must-haves that complete the cart under $10" next to AA batteries feels like a filler slot, not a real merchandising decision.
- No My Lowe's Pro or MyLowe's Rewards points callout anywhere near the appliance deal — if I'm a card member, I want to see my rewards multiplier stacked on top of 35% off, not buried in disclaimer text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — appliances + the buy-more-save-more ladder is the whole email. Storage/vanities/doors/essentials/curated-picks should be their own sends, not tacked onto a strong 35% off hero that deserves to stand alone.
- 2. Surface MyLowe's Rewards or Pro pricing right under the appliance badge — I've got both a Home Depot Pro and MyLowe's account, and if there's stackable member value here, don't make me hunt for it in the footer legal text.
- 3. Move "Ends tomorrow" down into the hero itself in bigger type — right now it's a small gray line above the logo that's easy to skim past.
- 4. Trim the bottom fine print or at least visually separate the pricing-validity dates (9/11, 10/23, 8/20-8/26) from the legal boilerplate — those dates actually matter for my purchase timing.
