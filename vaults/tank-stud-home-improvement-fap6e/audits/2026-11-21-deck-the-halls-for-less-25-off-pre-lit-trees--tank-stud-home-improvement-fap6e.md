# Deck the halls for less🎄25% OFF pre-lit trees
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth Lowe's email I've gotten this week, and it's a holiday gift-and-decor blast. The subject promises pre-lit trees, and the big red hero does show 25% off. I'm not trimming a tree, though. I was hoping for the Black Friday tool stuff that Tuesday's "Up to 2 FREE power tools" send promised.
- The good news is that further down there's a DeWalt impact driver pair under "FREE gifts with select purchases." That's the only part that talks to me. It sits mid-email under a black banner that says "Unwrap a little holiday magic." It's easy to scroll past. The Klein impact socket set in the last product row is also my kind of thing, but it's buried at the bottom.
- Would I click? Maybe the DeWalt block, if I had time. I wouldn't forward it. I'd save it only to check the free-gift terms, and I'd probably mark it read. It feels like a catch-all, not something written for a guy doing a weekend project.

## What's working

- The hero is clear. "25% off" in huge white type on red, with a "Shop now" button, and I can't miss it.
- The free DeWalt impact driver and drill showing up is a real draw for me. It's the same gift-with-purchase idea from the 11/19 email.
- "Free same-day delivery" at the top and the $10 MyLowe's Money offer are concrete perks I'd actually use.
- The Klein KNECT impact socket set in the product row is a real tool, and the "Buy now" buttons are big enough to tap on a phone.
- The Lowe's branding is consistent and I trust it.

## What's weak

- There are too many offers: Black Friday, 25% off trees, FREE gifts, poinsettias, $10 MyLowe's Money, Deals of the Day. Nothing has a clear priority after the hero.
- The "FREE gifts with select purchases" copy doesn't say what I have to buy, and the fine print is a wall of tiny text at the bottom. I don't trust a deal I can't read.
- The Affirm ad block breaks the flow. It's labeled sponsored and it's off-brand for what I want.
- It's gift-guide fluff: "For the home chef," "gadget guru," "pet parent," a reindeer. Nothing is aimed at a DIYer or a pro, and there's no project angle.
- The "Black Friday" banner is just "see what early deals are waiting for you." That's vague, and it's the fifth tease in a week.

## Recommendations

- 1. Move the free DeWalt block up to the hero or just under it, and spell out the qualifying purchase in plain words. For me, that's the whole email.
- 2. Segment. Send me a tools and project version and send tree buyers the tree version.
- 3. Cut the Affirm ad and the gift-guide trio. Replace them with 3 or 4 weekend-project items such as drills, storage and lighting.
- 4. Put the offer dates and in-stock or delivery info right next to each deal.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free DeWalt impact driver + 25% off trees`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday tool deals, plus 25% off trees`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free DeWalt tools with select purchases, plus same-day delivery.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Get a free impact driver, $10 MyLowe's Money and more. Ends Sunday.`

## Technical Audit: "Deck the halls for less🎄25% OFF pre-lit trees" (lowes@e.lowes.com)

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with media queries and web-font fallbacks. The HTML is truncated, so the checks below cover only the visible head, the QA output and the image list. Confirmed issues: a malformed DOCTYPE, two content images without alt text, and the recipient address exposed in plaintext in 16 tracking-pixel URLs. The header warnings come from the AgentMail relay and are not confirmed defects.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 38 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so none of the 38 were checked for redirect chains, dead links or final destinations.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** the recipient address appears unencoded in the query string of every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531383&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`.
  - A plaintext email in a GET parameter ends up in proxy, CDN and server logs, and in referrer data where applicable.
  - The raw `@` is also not percent-encoded.
- **Pixel volume:** 16 separate `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels were flagged (`s=127768300`–`127768304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`). Some `li_coord` URLs appear cut off in the QA output. That may be an artifact of truncation, so it is not flagged as a defect.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. This is invalid and can push some clients into quirks mode.
- **Missing alt on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
  - If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels:** the 16 `imp` pixels have no `alt`. This has little user impact, but `alt=""` plus `width="1" height="1"` would suppress the warnings and keep them out of the accessibility tree.
- **Web fonts:** the `@font-face` blocks for Fellix and DIN Next are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`, so Outlook is excluded as intended. No rendering defects are confirmed in the visible source.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The cause is the same relay limitation.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and postal address:** not verifiable because the HTML is truncated before the footer.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The 38 click-tracking links were not resolved, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "25% OFF pre-lit trees" offer are unconfirmed.

### 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`. Add `alt=""` to the `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
