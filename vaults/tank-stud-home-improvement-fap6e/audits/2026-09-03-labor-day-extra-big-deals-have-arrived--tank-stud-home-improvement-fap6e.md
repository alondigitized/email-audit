## What's working

- The DeWalt "Save up to $130 on select power equipment" tile is exactly the kind of project-tool offer that gets my attention — clean product shot, real number, easy Shop Now button.
- Appliance tile stacks the $800 savings with free delivery over $999 — that's a real, specific offer with a real threshold, not vague fluff.
- The lawn spreader photo actually shows a guy doing yard work, not a stock lifestyle shot — feels like it's for someone doing the actual labor, not staging a magazine cover.
- 12/18-month financing block with the MyLowe's Rewards card is relevant since I'm already in that program — good to see it called out with dates.

## What's weak

- A $199 animatronic skeleton with fog effects has zero business in a "Labor Day" deals email — it broke my trust that this was actually curated for the holiday weekend, not just a dumping ground for whatever's on promo.
- The "Curated with you in mind" trio (storage tote, cleaner, mini blinds) is the identical grab-bag I flagged in the 9/2 email — feels like Lowe's isn't actually segmenting anything, just recycling the same filler block.
- No sizes, model numbers, or specs on the DeWalt kit — I can't tell if that's the drill/driver combo I already own a battery for or something else, so I'd have to click through blind.
- "5 Days Only" urgency up top never gets an actual end date until you're buried in the fine print at the bottom (8/27–9/6) — should be visible where the urgency claim actually is.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pull the Halloween skeleton entirely — it undercuts the Labor Day framing and reads like a merchandising mistake, not a curated pick.
- 2. Replace the "Curated with you in mind" block with actual project-relevant items (fasteners, mulch, fencing) instead of the recycled tote/cleaner/blinds trio that's shown up twice now.
- 3. Put the "ends 9/6" date next to the "5 Days Only" badge in the hero, not just in the legal footer.
- 4. Add a spec line under the DeWalt kit (e.g., "20V MAX drill/driver + impact driver combo") so I know if it's worth clicking before I leave the email.
