# 50% OFF? This deal's scary good 👻
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-30**
## Executive summary

- Halloween email, delivered on October 30th. I'm not decorating my yard the day before the holiday, so the 50% off hero hits me as a day late and a dollar short. The offer itself is clear and big, and the skeleton photo is fun. But "select Halloween items" is not my aisle, and I'm not buying inflatables on the 30th.
- The "Curated with you in mind" strip is the only part that speaks to me: a DEWALT battery pack and a DEWALT router. That's the project stuff I'd actually click on. It's buried below three Halloween circles, and the products have no price, no savings amount, and no rebate. The "Buy one get 3" and "Free with purchase" badges are tiny and I can't read what they promise. A squishy dumpling toy sitting next to a router feels like the algorithm gave up on me.
- Compared with the 10/27 send ("Your fall to-do list called"), which led with a DEWALT kit and "Save up to $150", this one is a step backward for me. I'd skim it, maybe tap the router, and not forward it. Mark as read.

## What's working

- The "Get 50% off" hero is huge and unambiguous. I knew the deal in one second.
- The "Shop now" button is clear and easy to tap with a thumb.
- The skeleton photo is eye-catching and fits the season.
- The DEWALT XR router and 20V battery pack are real tool picks that match how I shop.
- The "Deals of the Day" banner and the top nav (Appliances, Daily Deals, Bathroom, Outdoor Tools) give me quick ways into my own categories.

## What's weak

- Halloween décor on October 30th is too late for me. It's a clearance dump, not a project.
- The tool section has no prices, no percentages and no dollar savings. The badges are too small to read, so I can't tell what the bundle or freebie actually is.
- The squishy dumpling toy in the "curated with you in mind" row is a miss. It reads like random filler, not curation for a DIY guy.
- The DEWALT battery title is cut off and ends in a dangling " ) -". It looks sloppy.
- There's nothing about Pro or MyLowe's rewards, no rebate and no in-stock signal. It treats me like a stranger.
- Every product has the same "Buy now" button, with no verb or noun that tells me anything.

- 1. Put the DEWALT section first for tool buyers, with real prices and savings. Send the Halloween clearance to a seasonal segment, or send it earlier in the month.
- 2. Replace the filler toy with a second tool or a project bundle like drill plus batteries.
- 3. Make the badges readable ("Buy 1 battery, get 3 free" or whatever it actually is) and put the savings in text under each product.
- 4. Fix the truncated product title.
- 5. Add a MyLowe's Rewards or Pro line, plus an "in stock at your store" cue.
- 6. Change the button copy to "Get the router deal" or "Shop DEWALT".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The truncated source shows a conventional table-based XHTML email with working responsive CSS. The QA run is 57% pass, with 0 hard issues and 3 warnings. The confirmed problems are missing `alt` attributes on 18 images, a malformed DOCTYPE, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in the impression-pixel URLs. The unsubscribe and authentication warnings can't be attributed to the sender (see §5).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **PII in tracking URLs:** all 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` impression pixels carry the recipient address unhashed, e.g. `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=528727&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. The address is exposed to proxies, server logs and any client that logs image requests.
- **Unverified links:** 36 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so their destinations and redirect health are unconfirmed. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **No broken links:** none were reported.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 18 images:**
  - 2 content images: `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` and `.../m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`. If they carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing. If they're decorative, they need `alt=""`.
  - 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels. These should have an explicit `alt=""` (plus `width="1" height="1"` and `border="0"`) so they're skipped and don't leak filenames or "imp" in text-only views.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` has no space after `PUBLIC`. The `xmlns` is also `https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. The XHTML namespace is defined as `http://`. Most clients tolerate both, but it's invalid.
- **Responsive CSS:** the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and mobile media queries are present, with `<!--[if !mso]>` guards on the web fonts. No rendering defects are visible in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FirstName]`) are visible in the available source. The only personalization is the `e=` parameter in the pixel URLs, which resolved correctly. The truncation means the body can't be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported missing. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw `.eml` headers before attributing it to the sender. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. This is expected through the relay and isn't a sender defect.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** the footer is cut off in the truncated source, so they can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated source, and 36 redirect links were not resolved. Continuity can't be assessed from the available data.
- The only parameters visible are the Lowe's ad-server IDs on the pixels (`p=`, `lctg=`, `stpe=`).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt` text to the two content images, and `alt=""` plus explicit 1×1 dimensions to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
2. Replace the plaintext address in the `e=` pixel parameter with a hashed or opaque subscriber ID.
3. Fix the DOCTYPE (`PUBLIC "-//W3C//…`) and use `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml` for the namespace.
4. Re-run QA against the full raw message (headers plus untruncated HTML) to settle the List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post and Authentication-Results warnings.
5. Resolve the 36 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to check their final destinations and UTM carry-through.
