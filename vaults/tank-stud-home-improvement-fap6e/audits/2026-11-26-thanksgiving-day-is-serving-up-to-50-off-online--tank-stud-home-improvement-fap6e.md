# Thanksgiving Day is serving up to 50% OFF online🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's is hitting me with a wall of black and red, and my first reaction was "OK, Thanksgiving Day, what's actually 50% off?" The hero says "Get up to 50% OFF" and "Thanksgiving Day deals," but it never says on what. I had to scroll three sections before I found anything I'd buy. The only real answer is the appliance block, and the hero doesn't mention appliances.
- The middle is where it earns some attention. The Craftsman impact wrench at "Save up to $150" on select power tools is my kind of thing, and the in-store "first 150 members" prize is a decent hook. But it's a Thanksgiving morning email stacked with a Christmas tree, a MrBeast advent calendar, a smart-home block and a Forno range ad. That's five or six campaigns in one send. After the CEO letter yesterday, the ladder email on the 25th and the battery-kit email on the 22nd, this reads like they're throwing everything at the wall.
- Would I click? Probably the power tools "Shop now," and maybe the appliances if I'm still thinking about the dishwasher. I'd skim it and save it. I wouldn't forward it.

## What's working

- The "Save up to $150" Craftsman block is concrete and it's in my lane. The impact wrench image sells it.
- "Doors open at 6 a.m. on Black Friday" at the top gives me a clear time to plan around.
- The appliance block spells out the deal: up to 50% off, 25% off when you bundle 3 or more, free delivery, free haul-away, and a 2-year protection plan. That's real money if I'm doing a kitchen.
- The member-only early access and the "First 150 members" prize treat me like a MyLowe's member and not a stranger.
- Big white "Shop now" buttons on black are easy to tap with a thumb.

## What's weak

- The "Get up to 50% OFF" hero has no product and no category. It could mean anything, and "Thanksgiving Day deals" is vague.
- The email is too long and has too many themes. A Christmas tree, an advent calendar, smart home and a luxury Forno range don't fit together, and it feels like a pile of ad slots.
- The "ONLINE ONLY / TODAY ONLY" banner text is cropped at the edges, so it reads as noise. It doesn't read as urgency.
- Two overlapping deadlines (Today Only, Two Days Only, One Day Only, Tomorrow) cheapen the urgency. I can't tell which one is real.
- The fine print at the bottom is dense and tiny. Exclusions like "up to $896 or more" on the bundle make the free delivery and haul-away feel conditional. That's a rebate-clarity problem for me.
- There's no pro voice and no Pro/PRO Rewards prompt. It talks to a general homeowner.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the product in the hero: "Up to 50% off major appliances" or "Craftsman tools up to $150 off." One clear category beats a generic percentage. I'd lead with whichever sells better for the audience.
- 2. Cut the Christmas tree, advent calendar and Forno block. Keep it to three or four sections, with tools and appliances first. I'd send a separate holiday decor email.
- 3. Fix the ribbon banner so the text isn't clipped, or drop it.
- 4. Put the free delivery, haul-away and the $896 threshold in one visible line near the offer, not buried in the footnotes.
- 5. Add a Pro or project angle, like a bundle for a weekend project or a Pro Rewards callout, for DIYers like me.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday starts now: up to 50% off appliances + $150 off tools`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Doors open 6 a.m.: tool deals and 50% off appliances`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get early access to doorbusters, plus free delivery on major appliances.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Save up to $150 on Craftsman power tools, online today only.`

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Duplicate impression pixels.** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300` through `s=127768304` (`p=531907`, `stpe=pixel`) each appear twice, 10 requests in total. ## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt attributes on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.
- **Tracking pixels without `alt`.** About 17 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images have no `alt` attribute. They are non-visual, so they should carry `alt=""` and ideally `width="1" height="1"`.
