# 11x points to celebrate Brunson’s championship! 🏀🏆
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- I'll be straight: this email landed on a Saturday morning, I'm six coffees deep and I've got deck boards waiting. The subject drops Jalen Brunson's name — an NBA guy — and I'm a rewards member, so I open it expecting maybe a tool deal tied to the Knicks winning or something tangible I can use. What I get is a dude in a Lowe's jersey, "He scored a ring. Now it's your turn to score," and an 11x points offer. That's actually a decent hook for a rewards member, and the creative is clean.
- But here's my problem: there's zero product in this email. No tool, no lumber, no category. Just points. I've got $47 in MyLowe's rewards sitting there and I'd happily use them on fasteners or a new circular saw blade — but this email gives me nothing to shop toward. It's a points multiplier attached to... whatever I happen to buy. That's a bank marketing move, not a hardware store move.
- I'd probably click the "Activate now" button just to bank the points before I forget, but I'm not changing my shopping plans based on this. Wouldn't forward it to anyone.

## What's working

- **11x points is actually a strong reward signal** — specific multiplier, not vague "earn more" fluff, and I notice it in both the badge and the body copy
- **Clean, uncluttered layout** — single column, hero image loads, CTA button is visible without scrolling
- **"Today Only" urgency badge** is credible and specific — I see it, I clock it, it actually adds pressure
- **Rewards branding is front and center** — "MyLowe's Rewards" sender + rewards visual treatment confirms I'm a known member, not a cold prospect
- **No render bugs** — image renders fine, text is legible, nothing overlaps

## What's weak

- **Zero product relevance** — no tool, no SKU, no category anchor; a points multiplier with nothing to shop toward is empty calories for a project-driven buyer like me
- **Basketball tie-in is a non-sequitur** — I don't care about the Knicks, and the Brunson angle does nothing for a hardware email; the Live Nation crossover two weeks ago at least made me stop, this one feels like they grabbed a sports moment because it was in the news cycle
- **The asterisk kills the offer** — "*Subject to exclusions & more terms apply" under the hero — that fine print is doing a lot of heavy lifting and I've been burned by "certain products excluded" on Lowe's rewards before
- **CTA says "Activate now" but activates... what exactly?** — if it goes to a homepage with no filter applied, I'm dropping off immediately
- **Sixth email in a week** — I'm on autopilot and the goodwill from the Father's Day free spatula email is fading fast

## Recommendations

- 1. **Anchor the points to a product category** — "11x points on power tools this weekend only" would make me open my cart immediately; the current version gives me nothing to point my wallet at
- 2. **Show me a hero product or department** — even a Milwaukee drill or a Milwaukee battery pack in the visual would give me something to click toward
- 3. **Resolve the asterisk before the CTA** — either say "on all eligible purchases" with confidence or list the top categories explicitly; buried exclusion language feels like a bait-and-switch to a repeat customer
- 4. **Tap the sports angle for a bundle** — "Brunson's got his ring. Here's your power tool lineup to finish the job" would connect the sports moment to something I actually care about
- 5. **Cadence check** — this is send six in seven days; consider pulling back or segmenting project-phase buyers so I'm not developing inbox immunity

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — MyLowe's Rewards "11x Points / Brunson" Email

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound XHTML 1.0 Transitional with standard table-based layout and appropriate mobile media queries. Primary concerns are missing compliance signals (physical address, List-Unsubscribe headers) and 29 unverified tracking links.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**29 tracking links unverified** — all click-redirect links pass through `e.lowes.com` redirect infrastructure; HTTP probes were skipped. Cannot confirm destination URLs resolve correctly or that UTM parameters are present. This is the largest single risk surface.

**Plain-text part is 77% URLs** (`3017/3927` chars) — the plain-text alternative is essentially a URL dump with minimal prose. Aggressive spam filters (Gmail, Outlook) penalize high URL-density plain-text bodies and may drop the message to spam or bulk folders.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text on at least one image** — `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` (`https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`) has no `alt` attribute. Images-off rendering (common in Outlook, corporate environments) will show a broken-image placeholder with no context.

**Custom web fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) loaded via `@font-face`** — wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`conditional comments, which correctly excludes Outlook. However, `woff`-only format is declared with no `woff2` fallback. `woff2` is universally supported and ~30% smaller; absence is a performance regression, not a blocking issue.

**Duplicate `local()` hints in `@font-face`** — each font declaration lists `local('Fellix Regular'), local('Fellix Regular')` (identical twice). Harmless but signals copy-paste without validation.

**Media query overlap** — two separate responsive breakpoints are declared: `min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`. These overlap between 375–480px and could produce conflicting rule application depending on client CSS cascade order.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML (no `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, or similar unfired placeholders detected). Cannot fully verify personalization blocks (name, points balance) without the full source, but nothing suspicious in the visible portion.

---

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **MISSING** | One-click unsubscribe not confirmed |
| Physical mailing address | **MISSING** | Not detected in body — CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address |
| SPF/DKIM authentication | **UNKNOWN** | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture |

The missing physical address is the only **hard CAN-SPAM violation** visible. The `List-Unsubscribe-Post` absence is not required by law but is required by Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender policy (≥5K/day threshold) and will cause one-click unsubscribe to fail in those clients. The header absence may be a relay capture artifact rather than a true sending gap — needs verification against raw headers at the MTA.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot verify UTM parameters on the 29 tracking links because all were skipped during probing. The redirect chain (`e.lowes.com` → destination) must be manually spot-checked to confirm:
- `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on landing URLs
- Campaign value reflects this specific promotion (e.g., `brunson-11x-points-2026`)
- No links resolve to the homepage instead of the intended points/offer landing page

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Compliance**
- Confirm physical mailing address is present in the full HTML (footer, likely truncated). If absent, add immediately.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are set at the ESP/MTA level, not just in relay capture.

**P1 — Deliverability**
- Rewrite the plain-text alternative with real prose summarizing the offer; bring URL density below 30% to reduce spam filter risk.
- Validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status via raw header inspection at the receiving MTA.

**P2 — Accessibility**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to `a22207c1...jpg` and audit all other images for the same gap.

**P3 — Performance & Quality**
- Add `woff2` sources ahead of `woff` in all `@font-face` declarations.
- Remove duplicate `local()` hints.
- Resolve the overlapping media query breakpoints (375–480px conflict).
