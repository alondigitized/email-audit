# Summer staycations, right at home ☀️

## Executive summary

- Opened it, scanned it in about eight seconds, and mentally filed it under "patio furniture catalog." The hero is gorgeous outdoor seating with "WOW outside" splashed across it — fine photography, zero connection to why I open Lowe's emails. I'm here for the tool deals, the lumber runs, the paint and tile projects. Backyard lifestyle vibes? That's my wife's inbox.
- What makes this particularly frustrating is the cadence. Yesterday I got the "Kickstart summer with more points ☀️" email — grill picture, points activation ask. Today it's "Summer staycations, right at home" — also outdoors, also aspirational, also asking me to care about patio furniture. The 3x points badge shows up again, which means Lowe's is now repeating that exact same value prop two days in a row without closing the loop on why I haven't activated yet. Feels like a broadcast drip, not a targeted send.
- The 35% off outdoor kitchens and the paint section save this email from being a total miss for me — those are at least DIY-adjacent — but I had to scroll to find them buried under $398 wicker chairs. Would I click? Maybe the paint section. Would I forward it to a buddy? No.

- **Paint section** — "Paints, leveled up." speaks to a weekend project; this is the only section that felt like it was written for someone who owns a roller and a 5-in-1

- **No render bugs** — clean layout top to bottom, images loaded, text didn't overlap
- **Lowe's sender credibility** — I trust the brand; that alone keeps me from deleting without opening

## What's weak

- **"Summer staycations" subject** — says nothing about an offer, a product, or why I should open it today vs. next week; lifestyle copy belongs on Instagram, not in a promo email
- **$398 and $449 patio chairs** — those price points are aspirational furniture buys, not project-driven; I'm not pricing out chairs this weekend
- **3x points — again** — ran this exact benefit yesterday in the "Kickstart summer with more points" email; repeating it 24 hours later without any "here's what you've already earned" personalisation makes it feel like a mass blast, not a loyalty touchpoint
- **Hero doesn't lead with a discount** — beautiful lifestyle photo, but there's no offer in the hero; I have to scroll to find a number
- **Cadence fatigue** — fifth Lowe's email in a very short window; the creative is starting to blur together

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the hero to lead with the 35% outdoor kitchen offer** — that's a real dollar amount on a real project category; move it above the fold instead of wicker chairs
- 2. **Cut one email from this week's cadence** — five sends in rapid succession trains me to skim or unsubscribe; batch the Memorial Day content and the staycations send into one
- 3. **Kill the duplicate 3x points** — if I didn't activate yesterday, either personalize it ("You've got X points waiting") or pull it entirely; repeating unchanged is lazy
