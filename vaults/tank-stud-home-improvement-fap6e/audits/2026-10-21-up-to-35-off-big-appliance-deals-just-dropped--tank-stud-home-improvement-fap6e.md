# Up to 35% OFF! Big appliance deals just dropped 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- My gut reaction was "another Lowe's blast, but this one has an actual reason to exist." The blue hero with a big 35% and a row of GE fridges, ranges and washers is clear at a glance. I'm not buying a fridge this weekend, though. I'm still on the drywall job, so it's a skim for me. It's the fifth Lowe's email in about a week. The 10/14 "LAST DAY" Money Days send and the 10/16 kitchen-sink one used the same playbook, and now I get "ENDS TODAY" again on the 30% interior essentials band.
- The fine print is where the pro in me gets irritated. The headline says 35% off, but the free delivery, free install and haul-away only apply to Members who spend $2,500 or more on select GE major appliances. The 35% is also "select major appliances," and the real numbers sit in a footer paragraph the size of a legal brief. The Spend more, save more tiers (10/20/30% at $999 / $2,500 / $7,500) are the first thing I'd actually do the math on.
- Would I click? Maybe, if I were saving this for the next reno phase. A kitchen redo would make the GE bundle worth a look. I'd file it, not forward it. I might send my brother-in-law the dryer vent cleaning plan for $99 a year, which is a weirdly useful find.

## What's working

- The hero is big and clean: "35% off" in huge type with the appliances right next to it. I read it in two seconds without zooming.
- The Members perks stack (Free Delivery, Free Install, Free Haul-away) is a real value prop, and it's the kind of thing I check before buying a big-ticket item.
- "Save up to $300" on kitchen bundles and "$100" on laundry pairs are concrete dollar amounts. Those beat vague percentages.
- The Spend / Save table (10%, 20%, 30%) is easy to read and tells me exactly what the tiers are.
- The HomeCare+ dryer vent tile at $99 a year feels useful and homeowner-practical, not just a discount.

## What's weak

- Too many offers in one email: 35% appliances, 30% interior essentials, $500 off, 30% fire safety, HomeCare+, doors at 15%, and Halloween stuff. Nothing builds on anything else.
- Almost every button says "Shop now". There are five or six of them, and they don't tell me which one is the real action.
- The fine print is buried, with "select GE," a $2,500 minimum, and a Members-only lock. The headline oversells it.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row (a plastic skeleton, a trick-or-treat pail and a Klein utility knife) doesn't match me or anything else in the email. The Klein knife is the only tool in the whole send.
- The Members and Pro angles are thin. There's no Pro pricing, no rebate clarity and no "in stock for pickup" signal.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one offer and one CTA. Make the GE bundle the hero with "Shop GE kitchen bundles" and cut the Halloween and door modules. If it's a reno-phase email, say so.
- 2. Put the Members-only conditions right under the hero (spend $2,500+, select GE), not in the footer. People who feel tricked don't come back.
- 3. Replace the "Shop now" buttons with specific copy like "Shop fridges," "Shop washers & dryers," and "Get the $500 deal."
- 4. Add pickup or delivery dates and stock signals, such as "In stock, delivered by Saturday." Project timing matters more than a percentage to me.
- 5. Segment. A DIYer should see a Pro-style tool or project row (e.g. Klein, drywall, fire safety) instead of a skeleton.
- **Subject Alt A:** `35% off GE appliances + free install (Members)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen reno? GE bundles save up to $300 today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free delivery, install and haul-away with $2,500+ GE purchase. Ends today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus 10–30% off home essentials when you spend $999+. Today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound for a marketing email. The confirmed issues are 18 images with no `alt` attribute and the recipient's email address sitting in plain text in 16 third-party pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication warnings can't be confirmed: the QA harness reads from the AgentMail relay, which may strip those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **38 links were not probed.** The QA run skipped them as tracking/click-redirect domains, so destination status, redirect chains and final landing URLs are unverified.
- **Pixel URLs expose the recipient address.** 16 impression pixels on `sli.lowes.com/imp` carry the address in the query string, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=527566&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`.
  - The address is not hashed, so it can end up in proxy, CDN and image-cache logs.
  - Image-proxying clients such as Gmail rewrite these URLs, so the address is visible to the proxy.
- **Duplicate impressions.** The `s=2019286` and `s=1090834` pixels each appear twice (`stpe=default&li_coord…`). If both fire, impressions are double-counted. The truncated output doesn't show whether their `li_coord` values differ.
- **Truncated source.** The HTML was cut off, so I can't audit the body links, button markup or footer links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images (2).** These can't be confirmed as decorative, so they likely need descriptive alt text. If they carry offer text, that text is invisible when images are blocked.
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`
- **Missing alt text on tracking pixels (16).** The `sli.lowes.com/imp` images should carry `alt=""`. Without it, some screen readers announce the file name or URL. Confirm they also have `width="1" height="1"` and are hidden from layout.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE.** It reads `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"`, with no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but strict parsers may not.
- **Conditional comments and fonts.** The `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrapping for `@font-face` is correct, and the web fonts have fallbacks via `local()`. I can't check the font-family fallback stacks in the truncated body.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The recipient address is resolved correctly in every pixel URL (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`). The privacy concern is covered in section 2.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)

- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found [WARN].** Same caveat. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe, so verify this on the raw message too.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found [WARN].** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` are unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure.

Not assessable. The 38 click-tracked links were skipped, so the final URLs, UTM parameters and landing-page status are unknown. The sender uses redirect-based tracking, so UTMs are probably applied after the redirect. This needs a redirect-follow check.
