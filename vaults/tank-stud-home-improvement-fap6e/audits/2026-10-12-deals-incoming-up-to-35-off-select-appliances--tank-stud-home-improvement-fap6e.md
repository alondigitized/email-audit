# Deals incoming: Up to 35% OFF select appliances 👏
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- Appliances aren't what I'm shopping for. I've got a drywall job half done, so my first reaction was "wrong aisle." The hero is big, though. "Up to 35% off" with free delivery, free install and a 2-year protection plan is a real offer, and it gets in front of me quickly. The catch is the fine print: the free stuff only kicks in at $2,500 or more on GE major appliances. That's a kitchen remodel, not a Saturday project. If I were planning a reno phase, I'd save this one.
- The rest is a pile-up. There's an Appliances Cooking Week block, a trending-products row, a countertops install pitch, MyLowe's Money Days again, a smart-home water heater tile, an NFL flooring ad and a Deals of the Day signup. It's the fourth Money Days appearance in a week. I saw the $10/$20 offer on 10/6, 10/7, 10/9 and 10/11, and here it is tucked in the middle. By now it feels like wallpaper.
- Would I click? Probably not today. I might forward it to my brother-in-law, who's redoing his kitchen. Otherwise I'd skim it and archive it. The countertops block is the only thing near my work, and the install-by-11/9 deadline is tight.

## What's working

- The hero is easy to read. "Up to 35% off" is huge, and the product photo is clean.
- The member perks (free delivery, free install, 2-year protection plan) feel like real value for someone with a Lowe's account.
- The $300 and $100 savings tiles show me the dollar amounts right away.
- The "Member Only Deal" on wall ovens, 50% off at $1,099 or more, is a concrete number.
- The nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) is clear, and the Lowe's branding is consistent.

## What's weak

- The $2,500 threshold is buried under the free-delivery and free-install badges. It reads as a trap until I look closely.
- There are too many themes in one email: appliances, countertops, Money Days, smart home, flooring and NFL. I can't tell what the point is.
- Money Days again, with the same offer. This is repeat fatigue.
- The trending row is thin. The product names are cut off and there are no prices or ratings, so "Buy now" gives me no reason to click.
- Nothing is aimed at a DIYer. There are no tools, no project bundles and no stock or pickup signal. The Pro/homeowner voice is generic.
- The NFL "Sponsored" flooring ad and the Zeta "Powered by" strip make the bottom feel like an ad network.
- The fine print is a giant wall of text.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one story. Lead with appliances and cut the flooring ad, the NFL tile and the smart-home tile. Or segment: send this to people with appliance intent and send me tools.
- 2. Put the $2,500 threshold right next to the free-delivery and free-install badges, not in the footnotes.
- 3. Drop Money Days from this send or fold it into one line. It's been in my inbox four times this week.
- 4. Give the trending row prices, ratings and a "pick up today" cue.
- 5. Add a project tie-in, like "Kitchen reno? Bundle appliances + countertops," to catch people planning a remodel.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email uses a table-based XHTML layout with mobile media queries and MSO conditionals. The QA run flags 7 images missing alt text, 33 click-tracking links that were never probed, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be read. The HTML was truncated, so the footer and unsubscribe markup were not available to check.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 33 tracking links were skipped because they use click-redirect domains. Their destinations, status codes and redirect chains are unverified, so I'm not reporting them as broken.
- **Recipient email in pixel URLs:** The impression pixels on `sli.lowes.com/imp` put the recipient's address in plain text in the query string, for example `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`. The same URLs carry `s=1090834`, `p=10122026` and `lctg=410146703`. If a pixel is blocked, proxied or logged, the address is exposed in URL logs. This is a privacy and hygiene concern, not a functional break.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `s=1090834` appears twice, both times with `stpe=default&li_coord…`. That may double-count impressions, but the truncated URLs don't show whether the coordinates differ.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt on content images (2):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Screen readers and image-blocked clients get no description of these images.
- **Missing alt on tracking pixels (5):** The `sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834/1090835/1090837/1090838` images have no `alt` attribute. They should carry `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** The source has `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" …>` with no space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Otherwise sound:** The viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the Outlook `[if !mso]` wrappers for web fonts, the Apple data-detector override and the responsive classes (`.container`, `.photo`, `.drop`) look correct in the visible portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The recipient address resolves in the pixel URLs (see section 2 for the exposure concern).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Headers not captured:** The QA run found no `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) or `Authentication-Results` header. The checker itself says the AgentMail relay may not preserve them. This is **unconfirmed**, not a confirmed violation. The sender (`lowes@e.lowes.com`) is a major brand and likely sends them, so verify against raw headers from the original delivery.
- **Footer not auditable:** The visible HTML cuts off before the footer, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address could not be checked.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues can be confirmed. Landing URLs sit behind click-tracking redirects that were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment with the "up to 35% off select appliances" offer could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-…jpg` and `ad569dc5-…png`, and `alt=""` to the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels.
2. Fix the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"`.
3. Replace the plaintext recipient address in the pixel URLs with an opaque subscriber ID or hash.
4. Check whether `s=1090834` is meant to fire twice.
5. QA-pipeline follow-ups: verify the three missing headers against the raw original message, and resolve one or more redirect chains to confirm landing pages and UTMs. Store the full, untruncated HTML so the footer and unsubscribe can be checked.
