# MyLowe’s Money Days are HEEEREEEE 🎉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- Finally, an email with a number I can use. $10 back on $200, $20 if I put it on the Lowe's card. That's a real offer, and it's a loyalty offer, not another grab bag of themes. I've got a drywall job half done, so a $200 cart of mud, tape, screws and a new sander is a normal Saturday run for me.
- It's also the sixth Lowe's send in a row with nothing in it to shop. Compared with the 10/5 mower email ("Save up to $300"), this one shows no product at all. It's just a card, some fake money and confetti. The tote email on 10/3 had the same problem, with a giveaway front and center. Here the giveaway is socks, a luggage tag and a blanket, in store only, for the first 150 people, on a weekday morning. I'm not driving over for socks.
- I'd probably save it and use it, since $200 is easy to hit. I wouldn't forward it. A buddy doing a reno would want to know what's on sale, and this doesn't say.

## What's working

- The "$10 MyLowe's Money on $200+" offer is huge and easy to read. I get it in two seconds.
- The credit card upsell ($20 instead of $10) is clear and doubles the reward. That's what a rewards email should do.
- The dates, "October 6 – 14", sit right under the offer. That's a real deadline and gives me a week to plan a project run.
- The "Shop now" button is big and white on blue, so I can tap it with a thumb.
- It's clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There's no product anywhere. Nothing says tools, lumber or drywall, so it could be a send for anyone.
- The "Shop now" button is generic. I don't know where it lands or what's included.
- The fine print is tiny, and the "exclusions, restrictions" line makes me wonder what doesn't count toward the $200. Appliances? Sale items?
- The giveaway is socks, a luggage tag and a blanket. It's in store only, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. on a weekday, for 150 people. That's irrelevant to me and feels like filler.
- The MyLowe's Money expires in 30 days, and that's buried in the fine print. It should be near the offer.
- The "FREE MyLowe's Money" in the giveaway is confusing. It's not clear how much, or whether it's the same offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a row of project-based products under the hero, like "Drywall & tools," "Fall cleanup" and "Paint," so I can see how to hit $200.
- 2. Put the 30-day expiry in the hero instead of the fine print, along with the biggest exclusions.
- 3. Change the button to "Shop deals, earn $10," or "Shop tools & building supplies."
- 4. Move the giveaway lower. Say how much the free MyLowe's Money is, or drop it.
- 5. Add a pro angle: "Stacks with Pro discounts?" or "Build your $200 project cart."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is a conventional table-based XHTML layout with responsive media queries and MSO-conditional font loading. Confirmed defects are limited to a malformed DOCTYPE and one image without alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings are most likely artifacts of the AgentMail relay and can't be confirmed from the data given. Only the head and CSS of the HTML were available (it was truncated), so body-level findings are limited to the QA output.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 13 links were skipped by the HTTP probe because they use tracking/click-redirect domains. Their destinations and status codes are unverified, so this is a coverage gap rather than a confirmed defect.
- The plain-text part is 3426 of 4359 characters URLs (about 79%, over the 70% threshold). The text alternative is mostly wrapped tracking URLs, which hurts readability for text-only clients and can add a minor spam-filter signal.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (confirmed):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted public identifier. Strict parsers may reject it and quirks-mode fallback is possible, though most email clients are tolerant.
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the filename, or nothing if images are blocked.
- **HTTPS namespace and DTD URLs:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the `https://` DTD URL are non-canonical. The namespace is `http://`, but this is harmless in practice.
- **Custom web fonts:** `@font-face` for Fellix and DIN Next loads from `www.lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Most clients will fall back to system fonts, so this is only an issue if the fallback stacks are poor, which I can't verify from the truncated source.
- No other issues are confirmed in the visible source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the available source or the subject line.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA tool notes the relay may strip these headers. This is unconfirmed. If the headers really are absent, it would affect Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender one-click unsubscribe requirements. Verify against the raw message at the original recipient, not the relay copy.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again a relay limitation. The sender domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is normal practice.
- The visible footer, physical address and unsubscribe link couldn't be checked because the HTML was truncated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 13 click-tracked links were not resolved, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations can't be confirmed. No mismatches are flagged.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the DOCTYPE to `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" "http://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/DTD/xhtml1-transitional.dtd">`.
2. Add a descriptive `alt` to `a22207c1-…jpg`, or `alt=""` if it is purely decorative.
3. Shorten or restructure the plain-text part so it has more readable copy and fewer raw tracking URLs.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the original raw message.
5. Confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment from the original headers.
6. Resolve the 13 redirect links with a follow-redirects probe to verify destinations and UTM parameters.
7. For this audit pipeline, capture the original headers before the relay and pass the full, non-truncated HTML. Most of the "unknown" items above would then be checkable.
