# FINAL HOURS: up to 50% OFF⏰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth or fifth Lowe's blast I've gotten in four days, and it's the same black-and-red wall as the Thanksgiving Day send this morning. My gut says "OK, I get it, everything's 50% off." Then I scroll and find a stack of seven or eight modules all yelling at once. The "50% OFF" hero is huge, but it still doesn't tell me 50% off *what*. The Doorbuster hero at the top shows a dishwasher, a cordless drill and a rolling tool chest, and that's the only part that spoke to me.
- The tool chest and the drill are what I'd actually care about, but the email never prices them. There's no number and no "this exact chest is $X." Early member access to Doorbusters is a decent hook for someone on a MyLowe's list. But I can't tell whether the doorbusters are even in the 50% off, and the appliance block has so much fine print I'd need a magnifying glass.
- I'd open it out of habit and skim it. I'd click "Shop now" on the tool chest hero if it went to the actual product. I wouldn't forward it, because there's nothing here a buddy needs to see. I'm not swapping out a fridge this weekend.

## What's working

- The top hero shows real tools: a drill and a rolling tool chest next to the dishwasher. That's my aisle, and the "members get early access" line makes the loyalty angle clear.
- "Thanksgiving Day deals end tonight" is real urgency, and the giant 50% number is impossible to miss.
- The appliance block spells out the bundle: 25% off when you buy 3 or more kitchen appliances, plus free delivery, free install and free haul-away. That's real money if you're doing a reno.
- The Lowe's logo and nav (Black Friday / Tools / Daily Deals / Appliances) make it feel like a known, trusted sender, and Tools is right there in the nav.

## What's weak

- I count seven or eight stacked promos: Black Friday, Thanksgiving, the in-store prize giveaway, 12 Days of Deals with a snowman, All-In Appliance, the Rewards sweepstakes and Deals of the Day. Nothing has priority, so I don't know where to look.
- "Get up to 50% OFF" never says on what. It's the same mistake as this morning's send.
- Tools get one product image and no prices. I'm a project shopper and I want to see a number, like a drill kit at $99.
- The "Win BIG" prize module is in-store only, with a bucket and a "First 50" label, and it's gimmicky. The fine print wall at the bottom is long and dense, and it makes the "honest offer" feel shaky.
- The Christmas tree and snowman module has nothing to do with a DIY project. It's decor, and it reads like it's for someone else.
- The "ends tonight" and "ending tomorrow" lines in different modules contradict each other. I can't tell what actually ends when.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this to three modules: Doorbuster tools (with prices), the appliance bundle, and Deals of the Day. Lead with the tool chest and drill at a real price, since that's my category.
- 2. Name the product category in the 50% hero ("50% off select tools and ladders"), so I'm not guessing.
- 3. Use one deadline. Pick "tonight" or "tomorrow" and stick to it.
- 4. Show the Pro and member prices on the tools, with a clear "member price" tag.
- 5. Move the fine print behind one "See details" link per offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tool chest + drill doorbusters: members in at 6 a.m.`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: 50% off select tools & appliances`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members get early access to Doorbusters, plus 25% off kitchen bundles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The source and QA data show no hard failures, but the email has two real issues: unhashed recipient email addresses in tracking-pixel URLs, and 18 images with no `alt` attribute. The 57% pass rate mostly reflects unverifiable checks. Headers were not captured, and 31 links were skipped. The HTML is also truncated, so footer, unsubscribe and UTM details could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **PII in pixel URLs:** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel carries the plaintext recipient address, for example `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=127768300&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=531941&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel`. The address is in the URL query string, so it can end up in proxy, CDN and ad-server logs and in Referer headers. A hashed identifier would avoid this.
- **Duplicate impression pixels:** these appear twice each:
  - `s=127768300` through `127768304`
  - `s=2019286`
  - `s=1090834`

  This is likely a mobile/desktop block pair, but it could double-count impressions in clients that load both.
- **Unverified links:** 31 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the probe, so destination status, redirect chains and link-level errors are unknown.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on content images (2):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  These show nothing meaningful to screen readers or with images blocked.
- **Missing `alt` on tracking pixels (16):** all `sli.lowes.com/imp` images. These should carry `alt=""` so screen readers skip them. The lack of an attribute is a low-severity compliance gap.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space after `PUBLIC`. Clients usually tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **Wrong XHTML namespace:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` uses https. The namespace URI is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is harmless in practice.
- **No `lang` attribute:** the `<html>` tag has none, which hurts screen-reader pronunciation.
- **Legacy CSS:** the `.ReadMsgBody` and `.ExternalClass` hacks are outdated but harmless.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No unrendered merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The recipient email is resolved correctly in the pixel URLs. That is a PII concern (see section 2), not a token failure.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post:** neither was found. The QA tool says AgentMail may not relay these headers, so this is **unconfirmed, not a confirmed violation**. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`, a large ESP sender, and would normally include both. Check the raw message headers.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC are unknown. This is a relay limitation.
- **Footer content:** the physical address and unsubscribe link fall outside the truncated HTML, so they could not be verified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
This cannot be assessed. The visible source has no `href` values, and all 31 tracking links were skipped. No UTM parameters, redirect targets or landing pages were checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Replace the plaintext `e=<email>` in `sli.lowes.com/imp` URLs with a hashed or opaque ID.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the two content images (`c301e24e…jpg`, `ad569dc5…png`) and `alt=""` to all 16 pixel images.
3. Confirm whether the duplicate impression pixels are intentional, and deduplicate them if they are not.
4. Fix the DOCTYPE spacing, set `<html lang="en">`, and use the `http://` XHTML namespace.
5. Tooling: capture raw headers (List-Unsubscribe, List-Unsubscribe-Post, Authentication-Results) and resolve the 31 skipped tracking links, so those checks stop appearing as warnings and the pass rate reflects real results. Feed the untruncated HTML to the audit so footer, unsubscribe and UTM checks can run.
