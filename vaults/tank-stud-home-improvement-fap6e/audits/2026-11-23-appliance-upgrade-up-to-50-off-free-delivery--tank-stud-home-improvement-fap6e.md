The HTML source is mostly standard ESP-generated XHTML with responsive CSS. The confirmed problems are the recipient's email address in plaintext in 16 ad-pixel URLs, missing alt text on 2 content images, and an `<html>` tag with no `lang`. ### 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **41 tracking links were not probed.** The QA check skipped them as click-redirect domains, so destinations, redirect chains, and link health are unverified. This is a coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.
- **Ad impression pixels:** 16 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=…&e=…&p=…&lctg=410146703&stpe=pixel|static|default` beacons are embedded. Examples are `s=127768300` through `127768304` with `p=531434`, and `s=2019286`, `s=1090834` and others with `p=11232026`.

  - Each request exposes the recipient address (see section 4).

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on content images:**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  With images blocked or a screen reader, these are silent or announced by filename. Add descriptive alt text, or `alt=""` if they are purely decorative.
- **Tracking pixels without alt:** The 16 `sli.lowes.com/imp` images were flagged too. These should carry `alt=""` and explicit `width="1" height="1"` so they are ignored by assistive tech and don't affect layout.

- **Namespace and DTD URLs:** Both use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The canonical XHTML namespace is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. This is harmless in email clients but non-standard.
- **No other rendering issues found in the visible portion.** It includes the viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, a `!mso` guard around the web fonts, `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` resets, and responsive queries at 480px and 375–600px.
