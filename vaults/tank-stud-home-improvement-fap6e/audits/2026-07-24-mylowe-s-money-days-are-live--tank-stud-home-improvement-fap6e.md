# MyLowe's Money Days are LIVE🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-24**
## Executive summary

- I saw "Lowe's" in the sender column and my thumb slowed down just long enough to register "Money Days are LIVE" — and then I immediately remembered I got nearly the exact same email yesterday when they spelled it "OOONNN." Same sale, different fire emoji, basically the same creative. That's not a campaign drumbeat, that's a copy-paste.
- Once inside, the kitchen appliance section grabbed me — 35% off on an ALL-IN bundle is the kind of number that makes me stop if I'm in a kitchen reno phase. The 50% and 40% sections below that are solid too. But then I hit this "Get spare car keys" block and I genuinely thought the email was corrupted. Lowe's key-copying kiosk is not why I'm in this email program. I'm here because I'm replacing deck boards this weekend.
- I'd scan this, probably save it if a kitchen appliance is on my list, and not forward it to anyone. Definitely would not click on the car key section.

## What's working

- **35% off the ALL-IN Kitchen Offer** is a clear, concrete number right at the top — that's exactly the kind of deal hook I'll bookmark for my next appliance purchase
- **"Members get FREE [delivery/install]"** is doing real work — I'm a MyLowe's member and that's a tangible perk that costs me nothing
- **50% off sections** below the fold — big percentage, visible button, easy to scan
- **Visual hierarchy is solid** — eye goes hero → discount badges → shop buttons, no confusion about what they want me to do
- **Fire emoji + "LIVE"** in the subject creates a mild urgency pull; it's not cringey in isolation

## What's weak

- **Car key section is a complete non-sequitur** — I came here for a home reno sale, not a locksmith upsell; it broke my reading flow and made me wonder if I'd accidentally opened a different email
- **This is basically yesterday's email repackaged** — "Money Days are LIVE" is the exact same campaign as "Money Days are OOONNN," and the creative inside looks nearly identical; I'm starting to feel managed
- **No visible sale end date anywhere** — "LIVE" doesn't tell me when it dies; I can't decide if this is urgent or not
- **Kitchen appliances aren't always where my head is** — a weekend warrior with a deck project open is going to feel this email isn't quite aimed at him

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop the car key block entirely or banish it to a post-footer line** — it's a distraction that undermines the "big sale" energy
- 2. **Add a visible expiration clock** — even "Ends Sunday" in the hero banner would fix my hesitation to click now vs. later
- 3. **Lean into the tool discounts as the hero** — the 50% off section likely covers power tools; for a guy like me that's a stronger hook than kitchen appliances on a random Thursday
- 4. **Rest the inbox** — six emails in four days from the same sender is where unsubscribes start; even skipping one send would have made "LIVE" feel more event-like

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "MyLowe's Money Days" Email

**1. Technical Summary**

Email is structurally sound with standard responsive layout patterns, but the AgentMail relay is not capturing compliance headers (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results), and 8 images lack alt text including tracking pixels that should be empty-string attributed.

- No broken link errors reported, so redirect infrastructure appears live.

- **8 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA:
  - `c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` — content image, should have descriptive alt
  - `ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` — content image, should have descriptive alt

  - Five `imp` pixels (`sli.lowes.com`) — tracking pixels; should have `alt=""`
