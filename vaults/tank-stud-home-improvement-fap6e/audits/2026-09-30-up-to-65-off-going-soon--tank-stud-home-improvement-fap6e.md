## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is a table-based XHTML Transitional email with a malformed DOCTYPE and 7 images lacking `alt` attributes. Five of those are `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels that embed the recipient's address in the query string. Header-level compliance and authentication could not be verified, and the 33 tracked links were not probed. The source is truncated, so body-level findings are limited to what's visible.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified links:** 33 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the QA probe, so destinations, redirect chains and status codes are unconfirmed.
- **Duplicate pixel:** `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834&...&stpe=default&li_coord=` appears twice with identical parameters. This likely double-counts impressions.
- **Empty parameter:** the two `s=1090834` pixels end in `li_coord=` with no value. This is either an unresolved merge or dynamic-content token, or a deliberately blank field. It can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (content images):**
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg`
  - `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`

  Screen readers may announce the filename, and with images blocked there is no fallback text. If these carry content, the `alt` attribute is missing or empty of meaning.
- **Missing `alt` (tracking pixels):** five `sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834/1090835/1090837/1090838` images have no `alt`. They should have `alt=""` so assistive tech skips them.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` has no space between `PUBLIC` and the quoted identifier. Most clients tolerate this, but it is invalid.
- **HTTPS namespace and DTD:** `xmlns="https://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml"` and the `https://www.w3.org/TR/xhtml1/...` DTD URL differ from the canonical `http://` identifiers. This is harmless in practice but non-standard.
- **Mobile media query gating:** the first `@media` block is limited to `(min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px) and (-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0)`. Viewports under 375px only get the second block (`max-width: 480px`). Its `.mobile-hidden` and `.drop` rules match, so the effect is minor.
- **Fonts:** web fonts (Fellix, DIN Next) sit inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` blocks, so Outlook falls back to system fonts. I can't check the fallback stacks in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- **Recipient email exposed in URLs:** every `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixel contains `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` as plaintext. The address will show up in proxy logs, image-proxy caches and any forwarded copy. It is a privacy exposure, not a broken token.
- **Merge tokens:** no unresolved tokens such as `%%name%%`, `{{...}}` or `${...}` are visible in the truncated source. The `li_coord=` case above is the only ambiguous one.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both were not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers. Check the raw `.eml` from a direct-delivery test.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `e.lowes.com` are unknown. The relay is the likely cause, so this can't be judged from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe and address:** not visible in the truncated source, so it can't be assessed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- UTM parameters and landing-page alignment could not be assessed. All 33 tracked links were skipped, and the truncated HTML shows no destination URLs.
- The tracking domains (`image.e.lowes.com`, `sli.lowes.com`) use the sender's branded subdomain (`e.lowes.com`). That is good for deliverability.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add `alt=""` to the five `sli.lowes.com/imp` pixels, and descriptive `alt` text to `c301e24e-...jpg` and `ad569dc5-...png`.
2. Remove the duplicate `s=1090834` pixel, or confirm it is intentional. Investigate the empty `li_coord=`.
3. Stop putting the recipient email in pixel query strings. Use an opaque subscriber ID instead.
4. Fix the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"`, and use the `http://` namespace and DTD URLs.
5. Re-run header checks on a raw message delivered directly, not through the relay. That would confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`.
6. Resolve a sample of the 33 tracked links, and store the full HTML rather than a truncated copy. That would allow the UTM and landing-page checks.
