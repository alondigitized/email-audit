# Your shot at EPIC Messi prizes starts today
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-19**
## Executive summary

- Yesterday Lowe's had me — "Ends Tomorrow" on appliance deals, loyalty points, in-store feel. Today I open my inbox and I'm staring at Messi in a Lowe's jersey telling me to tune in to a TikTok halftime show. I don't know what soccer has to do with my deck railing project, and I definitely don't know what TikTok has to do with my Saturday morning. I'm 49 years old. I have a planer that needs a new blade and a bathroom that still needs backerboard. Messi is not on my list.
- The one thing that saves this email from a full delete is the "Today Only: 10x points on one eligible purchase" module buried two-thirds of the way down. That's actually useful — I've got a lumber run coming up and 10x points on a single ticket would be real money back through MyLowe's. But Lowe's made me wade through a soccer sweepstakes and a novelty inflatable to get there.
- I would not click the hero CTA. I might tap "Activate now" on the 10x module if the deadline was actually visible. I would not forward this to anyone. I would mark it as read and feel vaguely annoyed that yesterday's email raised the bar only to have this one crater it.

## What's working

- **10x points module is the one real offer** — "Today Only" with a clear Activate button is actionable and loyalty-relevant for any Lowe's regular
- **Brand is instantly recognizable** — MyLowe's Rewards logo, "We Help. You Save." footer, consistent Lowe's blue; no confusion about who sent this
- **Email renders clean** — no broken images, no overlapping text, layout holds together visually
- **Multiple CTAs are each verb-noun** — "Buy now," "Learn more," "Activate now," "Enter" — at least nobody wrote "Discover"
- **"Today Only" urgency on the points** — that scarcity signal is the only thing that might actually move me

## What's weak

- **Nothing in this email is about home improvement** — I'm a weekend warrior, not a soccer fan; Messi has no authority over my materials list
- **TikTok halftime event is not how I shop** — I do not plan reno phases around celebrity livestreams
- **Messi inflatable is a novelty product** — "Limited quantities, while supplies last" feels like a toy store clearance, not a contractor supply chain I trust
- **The 10x points offer is buried** — the one thing I care about is the third module down, below a sweepstakes and an inflatable
- **"EPIC" in the subject line** — I'm going to be honest, that word alone made me want to scroll past it

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the 10x points** — flip the module order; put the loyalty offer at the top where a known MyLowe's member sees it first, then put Messi at the bottom for whoever cares
- 2. **Split the audience** — Messi sweep for the casual/family shoppers, loyalty + project offer for the Pro/serious homeowner list; I should not be on the Messi segment
- 3. **Anchor the 10x offer to a product category** — "10x points on tools, lumber, or flooring today only" gives me a reason to plan a run; "one eligible purchase" is vague
- 4. **Subject Alt A:** `10x points today — activate before midnight`
- 5. **Subject Alt B:** `Today only: stack your MyLowe's points on any purchase`
- 6. **Preheader Alt A:** `One purchase, 10x the points. Tap to activate before tonight.`
- 7. **Preheader Alt B:** `Lock in 10x points on your next run — today only.`

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) — not detected** (QA WARN): One-click unsubscribe (required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules for >5k/day senders) depends on this header. Same relay-capture caveat applies, but must be confirmed present in raw message headers.
