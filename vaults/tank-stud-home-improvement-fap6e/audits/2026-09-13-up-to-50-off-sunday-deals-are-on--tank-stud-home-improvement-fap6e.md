# Up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are on 👉
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-13**
## Executive summary

- Alright, a free power tool battery grid item and 50% off patio furniture right up top — that's actually a decent one-two punch for me. Battery packs are the thing that empties my wallet on the DeWalt/Craftsman side of the aisle since everything runs off the same platform, so "FREE items with select power equipment purchases" catches my eye even without a number attached. The 45% off appliances tile is the one that'll actually get a click though, since my kitchen's still on the list.
- Where it loses me a little is the back half — Halloween yard décor and a charity bedding donation ask have nothing to do with my Sunday project list, and by the time I hit the "Curated with you in mind" tool grid near the bottom I'm already skimming instead of reading. This is a browse-then-decide email, not a forward-to-a-buddy email — I'd click through to appliances, maybe skim the tool grid, and move on.

## What's working

- The free power-tool-with-purchase tile up top speaks directly to my platform-loyalty buying habit (I've got Craftsman batteries, I want more free ones).
- 45% off major appliances with free delivery over $999 is a real, specific number that matches where I'm at in my kitchen reno.
- The "Curated with you in mind" tool grid at the bottom (Craftsman batteries, hedge trimmer, storage tote) reads like they actually know what's in my cart history.
- Vanities tile calls out "For members" — nice small loyalty signal even if I'd want to know my specific member price.

## What's weak

- Halloween yard décor block breaks the whole "reno weekend" narrative — feels like a different email got spliced in.
- The Sleep in Heavenly Peace donation ask is a nice cause but it's dead weight in a deals email; it just adds scroll before I get to more tools.
- No specific dollar amounts on the "up to 45%/50%" tiles — I want to see "$300 off this fridge" not just a percent ceiling.
- Fine print at the very bottom mentions Florida shipping exclusions and a laundry list of exclusions — standard, but nothing here tells me if my zip is affected before I click.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Halloween and donation blocks entirely, or move them to their own send — they dilute a reno-focused email and push my actual interest (tools, appliances) further down the scroll.
- 2. Put a real number on the free power-tool offer ("free battery ($99 value) with select DeWalt/Craftsman kits") instead of a vague "FREE items" — vague free offers read as bait.
- 3. Add a dollar range under the appliance % off ("save up to $400 on this range") so I can gut-check the deal before clicking through.
- 4. Move the "Curated with you in mind" personalized tool grid up near the top — that's the strongest relevance signal in the whole email and it's buried past two off-topic sections.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off patio + free power tool batteries this Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sunday only: 45% off appliances, free tool with purchase`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free batteries with select power tools, plus 45% off appliances and vanities.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your Craftsman/DeWalt picks are on sale — see what's in your cart history.`
