# 50% OFF select ladders just in time for decorating
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-25**
## Executive summary

- I opened this because ladders are on my list. I've got a gutter job and some soffit work coming up, and 50% off a fiberglass step ladder is real money. The email then buries that under a generic "Black Friday" banner that says nothing. The subject says "just in time for decorating," but nothing in the body talks about decorating, so I felt a bit baited. The ladder hero is good, though. It's big, red and clear, and the ladders are obviously fiberglass.
- The 2 FREE power tools block is the part I'd actually click. It's the same offer I saw on 11/22 ("Buy a battery kit, get up to 2 power tools FREE"). So Lowe's is recycling creative, and that's the fifth or sixth send this week. The DeWalt, Craftsman and Kobalt shots are right for me, but the fine print on which kits qualify is missing.
- I'd click, but only to price a ladder and a battery kit. I wouldn't forward it. I'd keep it for the next project phase and probably check Home Depot's pricing before buying.

## What's working

- The ladder hero has a huge "50% off" and a clear product photo. It's the right tool for a weekend project.
- The 2 FREE power tools bundle names DeWalt, Craftsman and Kobalt, which are brands I actually buy.
- The Deals of the Day row has dollar figures ("Save up to $200" on handheld tools) instead of vague percentages.
- The black MyLowe's Rewards bar with free same-day delivery is a nice member perk.
- Every block has a "Shop now" button, so I know where to tap.

## What's weak

- The subject mentions "decorating," but the ladder block has no decor or holiday angle. It reads like a bait-and-switch.
- The "Black Friday / The good stuff got here early" banner takes up prime space and says nothing. It has no offer.
- There are four separate red blocks, so the email lacks a clear hierarchy. Ladders and the tool bundle compete for the eye.
- "Select ladders" and "select power tools" give me no model, size or in-stock info. I can't tell if the ladder is a 6-footer or a 12-footer.
- The email has no rebate or Pro-vs-homeowner cues, and no mention of my past purchases. I feel like a stranger on a mass list.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the ladder offer in the first block. Delete the empty "Black Friday" banner and add a named product with a price, for example "Werner 6 ft fiberglass step ladder, $XX."
- 2. Make the subject and the hero match. If the email is about ladders, lead with ladders. If it's about decor, show decor.
- 3. Add the qualifying kits and a "see eligible kits" link to the free tools block.
- 4. Show stock and pickup availability, like "In stock near you, ready today."
- 5. Space out the sends. This is the same bundle as 11/22, and a new offer would do more than a repeat.
- **Subject Alt A:** `50% off select ladders, today through Black Friday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ladders 50% off + 2 free tools with a battery kit`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fiberglass step and extension ladders, plus free tools when you buy a DeWalt, Craftsman or Kobalt kit.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Ready for pickup near you. Deals end soon, while supplies last.`
