# FREE gifts on Dad's wishlist🥳
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- Okay, NOW we're talking. I got a different Lowe's email earlier today — outdoor furniture, hosting setups, not for me — and then this one lands and I'm actually leaning forward. Free DeWalt. Free Craftsman. Free Kobalt. Three power tool free-gift-with-purchase deals stacked vertically, all tied to Father's Day. As a guy who has a circular saw, a leaf blower, and at least two half-finished battery platforms in the garage, this is speaking my language.
- The hero image is a little weird — it's a big dark shot of what looks like an empty wooden shelving rack. Like, nice shelving unit, but that's not what's selling me. The selling happens the second I scroll one inch and see that "FREE" block in Lowe's green. That's the real hero and they buried it one scroll below the fold.
- Do I click? Yeah, probably — specifically on the DeWalt row. Do I forward it to a buddy? Honestly, yeah. My neighbor is mid-bathroom reno and runs 20V MAX. I'm texting him this deal before I even close the email.

## What's working

- **Three distinct brand tiers** (DeWalt, Craftsman, Kobalt) means something for everyone at different price points — smart segmenting within a single email.
- **Battery specs are visible** — "20V MAX 4.0/2.0Ah," "24V 3-Ah" — that's the detail a tool guy actually needs to know if the GWP makes sense for his platform.
- **Father's Day angle is earned here** — power tools are a legitimate Dad gift, not a stretch like outdoor sofas were in that earlier send today.
- **"FREE" in Lowe's green is visually loud** — each product row leads with the word and you don't miss it.
- **Brand logos (DeWalt, Craftsman, Kobalt)** are clearly visible and create instant credibility. I trust those names.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is confusing** — empty wooden shelving unit as the lead visual? For a free power tool promotion? The disconnect between that image and the actual offer is a wasted hero moment.
- **"When you buy a select..." fine print is doing a lot of work** — I know what "select" means in retail: it means the exact item I want is probably excluded. That asterisk lives in the back of my brain every time I see it.
- **No deadline anywhere** — "Father's Day is June 15" or "ends Sunday" would've made me move faster. Right now I'm going to "remember to look at this later" and probably forget.
- **Second Lowe's email today** — I already got one this morning. Same day, same brand, different campaign. The cadence is getting noisy.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero image for the actual product** — lead with the circular saw or a guy in a garage using the tool. Ditch the lumber rack.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline** — "Father's Day is June 15 — offer ends June 14" makes this go from "I'll look later" to "I'm clicking now."
- 3. **Call out MyLowe's member exclusivity** — I'm a Rewards member. If this deal is better for me, say so. If it's not, that's a missed loyalty hook.
- 4. **Consolidate with the earlier send** — one well-built Father's Day email beats two competing ones landing the same day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free DeWalt, Craftsman, or Kobalt — pick your Dad gift`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Dad's getting a free power tool. Here's how.`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Buy a select battery kit, get a free tool — ends June 14`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Three brands, three free tools — one for every kind of Dad`

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with layered responsive CSS and proper MSO conditionals. Primary issues are missing alt attributes on tracking/impression pixels, and authentication/compliance headers not visible to the QA relay — which may reflect relay capture gaps rather than sender defects, but cannot be confirmed from available data.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**20 click-redirect links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `e.lowes.com` redirect infrastructure (expected for a Salesforce Marketing Cloud sender). No broken links detected among probed URLs.

**Impression pixels confirmed present** via `sli.lowes.com/imp` — five distinct pixels with `s=` campaign IDs (1090834, 1090835, 1090837, 1090838), all correctly parameterized with `e=`, `p=`, `lctg=`, and `stpe=` values. The `li_coord` parameter on two pixels (`d`, `m`) indicates desktop/mobile split tracking — no issue.

**Open pixel**: `https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/0cff7420cd032145/o.gif` — present and expected.

No issues found with link structure itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text on 10 images** — confirmed by QA:

| Image | Type | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` (`image.e.lowes.com`) | Content image | Screen readers silent; images-off rendering broken |
| `ad569dc5-...png` (`image.e.lowes.com`) | Content image | Same |
| `8323e445c38bde24.png` (`mi.lowes.com`) | Tracking pixel | `alt=""` preferred over absent attr |
| `o.gif` (`mi.lowes.com`) | Open pixel | Same |
| 5× `imp` pixels (`sli.lowes.com`) | Impression pixels | Same |

The two content images at `image.e.lowes.com` are the actionable accessibility gaps — blank alt on tracking pixels is tolerable but technically non-conformant (WCAG 2.1 requires `alt=""`  explicitly, not absent).

**Font loading**: Custom fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) load via `lowescdn.com` — `.woff` only, no `.woff2`. Not a rendering failure but suboptimal for clients that support woff2.

**`DIN Next Bold` and `DIN Next Black`** reference scaffold version `1.2.0` while `DIN Next` (regular) references `1.4.4` — version inconsistency in font CDN paths. No load failure expected but worth flagging for cache/CDN drift.

**Media queries**: Two overlapping breakpoint blocks (`min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`) could produce ambiguous behavior on 480px–600px viewports in WebKit clients. Not a breakage but a maintainability flag.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**`List-Unsubscribe` header — not captured** by QA relay. This is a WARN, not a confirmed absence: Salesforce Marketing Cloud reliably injects this header, and the AgentMail relay may not forward raw SMTP headers. Cannot confirm either way without direct header inspection.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) — not captured** — same caveat. Gmail and Apple Mail require this for the native unsubscribe button; absence would be a compliance gap with bulk sender requirements (Google's 2024 mandate for >5k/day senders).

**`Authentication-Results` — not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unverifiable. Sender domain `e.lowes.com` is a known SFMC subdomain with published DKIM; likely passing but not confirmed in this relay path.

**Action required**: Verify raw headers via direct SMTP capture or mail header analyzer tool (`mxtoolbox`, `mail-tester`) to confirm all three are present before treating these as sender defects.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM / Landing Page)

All 20 click links route through `e.lowes.com` redirect — UTM parameters, if any, are appended post-redirect and not inspectable from HTML source alone. The QA probe skipped these links, so landing page UTM alignment is **unverified**.

**Recommended follow-up**: Manually resolve one or two `e.lowes.com` redirects and confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are present on the final destination URL. Father's Day campaign traffic attribution depends on this.
