# MyLowe's Money Days are OOONNN 🤩
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- Fifth Lowe's email in four days — I saw "MyLowe's Rewards" in the sender column and already my thumb was halfway to Archive. The subject tries to generate some hype with the stretched-out "OOONNN," which honestly reads juvenile to me. I'm 49, not 19. But "MyLowe's Money Days" does hold my attention for a half-second because I've got two projects queued up and a $200+ purchase at Lowe's is a real possibility this weekend.
- Then I open it and — Christmas trees. In July. Rudolph and 5-ft elves. Full holiday-decor hero shot with a winter color palette and snowman energy. My brain did a hard stop. It's the last week of July and Lowe's is showing me Christmas in a way that has nothing to do with the deck I'm mid-build on. The $10 extra MyLowe's Money on a $200 purchase is legit useful for me, but the creative is screaming "you wandered into the wrong aisle."
- Mark as read. Won't forward. Might circle back Friday if I remember the $10 reward before the 2-day window closes — but only because I was already planning a materials run, not because this email earned the click.

## What's working

- **The $10 earn offer is prominently sized** — dollar figure in the hero, clear threshold ($200+), easy to internalize fast.
- **"2 Days Only" creates real urgency** — time-bounded without being fake-countdown-timer gimmicky.
- **"Bronze members!" tier callout** — acknowledges I'm in the loyalty program, not a cold stranger.
- **"Shop now" button is visible and unambiguous** — doesn't say "Discover" or "Explore," it says go.
- **No render bugs** — layout held together, text didn't overlap, images loaded.

## What's weak

- **Christmas décor in July is a category non-starter for me** — I'm wiring a subpanel and building a deck, not shopping for Rudolph. This creative belongs in October, not now.
- **"OOONNN 🤩" subject energy is off-brand for my age bracket** — that reads like a TikTok teenager, not a guy who's comparing OSB prices on the pro app.
- **"Bronze members" stings a little** — I've spent thousands at Lowe's this year. If I'm still Bronze, either the tier math is broken or I missed something. Doesn't make me feel valued.
- **No project hook anywhere in the body** — not a single mention of lumber, tools, fasteners, plumbing, electrical. It's all holiday décor with no path to what I actually buy.
- **Cadence is wrecked** — this is email five in four days. Even a good offer loses credibility when you're crying wolf this often. Last week's smart home email (July 21) was a 9 — this kind of follow-up dilutes that equity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero creative entirely** — MyLowe's Money Days should be a "spend on what you need RIGHT NOW" message. Show job-site staples: lumber, power tools, fasteners, exterior paint. Not a snowman.
- 2. **Lead with the reward math in the subject** — the $10 earn is the news; bury it nowhere.
- 3. **Drop the "OOONNN" styling** — it ages poorly and reads spammy against a 49-year-old's eye.
- 4. **Segment Bronze vs. higher tiers differently** — if I'm Bronze after heavy spend, explain why or don't rub it in. Or at least make Bronze sound aspirational instead of starter-tier.
- 5. **Respect cadence** — after five emails in four days, sit out a day. The reward program has goodwill; spend it wisely.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 bonus is live — any Lowe's purchase over $200`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Stock up on project materials and bank extra rewards before Saturday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Bronze members: Earn $10 extra MyLowe's Money this weekend only.`

## Technical Audit — MyLowe's Money Days (2026-07-23)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Well-structured XHTML 1.0 Transitional email with standard responsive patterns and multi-client compatibility CSS. Primary issues are header capture gaps in the AgentMail relay and two images missing alt text.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**27 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-redirect domains (likely `e.lowes.com` or a Salesforce Marketing Cloud redirect domain). This is expected behavior for tracked emails, not a defect. No broken destination URLs were flagged.

No issues found with link structure in the HTML source itself.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Two images missing `alt` text:**

| Image | src |
|---|---|
| Hero/content image | `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` |
| Tracking pixel | `https://mi.lowes.com/p/cp/8d3e558453f86f40/o.gif?mi_u=410146703` |

The tracking pixel missing `alt` is a minor cosmetic issue (screen readers will announce the src filename). The content image missing `alt` is a real accessibility gap — subscribers using screen readers or image-blocking clients get no context.

**CSS client targeting:** `ReadMsgBody`/`ExternalClass` hacks for legacy Hotmail are present but harmless. The two media query blocks (`min-width: 375px` and `max-width: 480px`) overlap at 375–480px, which could produce conflicting rule application on some devices — worth verifying in a test client matrix.

**Custom fonts** (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) loaded via `.woff` only. No `.woff2` fallback. Most modern clients support woff2 and it's ~30% smaller; this isn't a rendering blocker but is an unnecessary bandwidth cost.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

HTML source truncated — unable to fully audit merge token syntax. No unresolved tokens (e.g., `%%FIRST_NAME%%`, `{{first_name}}`) visible in the provided excerpt. No issues confirmed.

---

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** — flagged as a relay capture gap, not necessarily absent from the original SMTP envelope. However, if missing from delivered mail:
- Gmail and Apple Mail suppress the native unsubscribe UI that appears in the header chrome
- Per Google/Yahoo 2024 bulk sender requirements, `List-Unsubscribe` is mandatory for senders at Lowe's volume

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — one-click unsubscribe (POST method) is also a Google/Yahoo bulk sender requirement. If this header is absent from the delivered message, Lowe's is non-compliant with current bulk sender rules.

**`Authentication-Results` not captured** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status unknown via relay. The sending domain `e.lowes.com` is a Salesforce Marketing Cloud subdomain and almost certainly has DKIM configured, but this cannot be confirmed from the current capture.

**Action required:** Verify these three headers are present in the raw SMTP envelope, not just missing from relay capture.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

27 tracked links were skipped so destination URLs and UTM parameters could not be verified. No UTM parameter issues confirmed or denied. A manual spot-check of 2–3 CTA links through the click-redirect chain is recommended to confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` parameters survive the redirect.
