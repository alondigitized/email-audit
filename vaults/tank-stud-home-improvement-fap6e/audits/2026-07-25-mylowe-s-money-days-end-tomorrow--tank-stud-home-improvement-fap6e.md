# MyLowe’s Money Days end tomorrow‼️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Sixth Lowe's email since Monday, and this one at least has the decency to tell me the exact number: ten bucks. Not "up to $30" like the preview text teases — just ten, because I'm apparently a Bronze member, which is Lowe's polite way of saying I don't spend enough with them to matter. That's a demotion I didn't ask for and don't love seeing spelled out in a brown banner like a consolation prize.
- The hero art is clean, the "$10 Extra MyLowe's Money on $200+" is at least a real, legible offer, and the double "ENDS TOMORROW" banner (even glitched as it is) gets the urgency across. But this is the same MyLowe's Money Days pitch I've now seen three times this week under three different subject lines, and the tier-shaming kills my motivation to even open my wallet for it. I'd glance, register "$10, not worth restructuring my Saturday Home Depot run," and archive.

## What's working

- The $10 on $200+ offer is a real number I can do math on in two seconds, unlike the vague "$30" teased up top.
- "Shop now" button is big, dark, and impossible to miss on a phone screen.
- "ENDS TOMORROW" gives me a credible deadline instead of vague "limited time" fluff.

## What's weak

- Calling me out as "Bronze members!" in a mustard-brown bar feels like a demerit badge, not a perk — nobody wants their loyalty tier broadcast like a scarlet letter.
- The top line promises "up to an extra $30" but the hero only delivers $10 — bait-and-switch on the very first thing I read.
- The "ENDS TOMORROW" banner is visibly glitched/double-rendered ("ENDS TOMORROW ENDS TOMORROW" overlapping) — sloppy for a repeat sender.
- This is the third MyLowe's Money Days email this week (previously "OOONNN" and "LIVE" versions) — the redundancy is worse than any single flaw in this one.
- Zero project relevance — no lumber, no tools, no seasonal reno tie-in. Just a cash-back mechanic with no product story.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the render bug on the top banner first — a stuttering marquee text looks broken, not urgent.
- 2. Match the preview text to the hero: if the real offer is $10, don't dangle $30 up top — that's the fastest way to erode trust after three sends this week.
- 3. Drop or soften the "Bronze members!" callout — either hide tier level entirely or frame it as "your member rate" without the visual gut-punch.
- 4. Tie the cash-back to an actual project category (decks, patio, tools) instead of a bare dollar mechanic — give me a reason to open my wallet, not just a threshold.
- 5. Consolidate — this is the third near-identical Money Days send in a week; pick one and stop.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: $10 back on $200+ at Lowe's`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Bronze members get $10 extra on purchases over $200 — ends tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One day left to stack $10 MyLowe's Money on your next project buy.`
