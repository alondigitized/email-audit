# Sunday Deals mean BIG savings
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-20**
## Executive summary

- I opened this expecting at least a hint of a specific product, and instead got the emptiest possible version of "Sunday Deals." No percentage off, no dollar amount, no product category called out beyond a nav bar — just "check out savings for your home, projects and more" over a stock gradient sky. This is the laziest email I've seen from Lowe's yet, and after Thursday's appliance blast and Friday's ice-cube-mold nonsense, this feels like they're just filling a Sunday send slot with nothing behind it.
- There's genuinely nothing here pulling me toward a project. No tool, no appliance, no patio set, no rebate — just a "Shop now" button and a "Deals of the Day" upsell to get MORE daily emails from them. I'm not clicking, and I'm definitely not forwarding this to anyone — there's nothing to forward.
- Straight to archived, unread twice basically.

## What's working

- The nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) at least signals project categories even if the hero doesn't back it up.
- Clean render, no broken images or overlapping text — technically solid execution.
- Recognizable Lowe's branding, so I know exactly who it's from at a glance.

## What's weak

- Zero concrete offer — no %, no $, no BOGO, nothing. "Check out savings" isn't a deal, it's a suggestion.
- No hero product or project bundle shown — just gradient sky and text, nothing for me to actually evaluate.
- The second block is Lowe's asking me to sign up for MORE email ("Deals of the Day every day") instead of showing me a deal in THIS email — that's backwards.
- No loyalty/MyLowe's pricing or rewards mention despite me being on their list.
- Nothing here reflects fall project season — no seasonal framing at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put an actual offer in the hero — a specific % off a category (tools, appliances, patio) or a dollar threshold. "Sunday Deals" with no number is not a deal.
- 2. Show one real hero product from a live project category (grills going into fall clearance, power tool combo kit, storage/organization for garage projects) instead of a gradient background.
- 3. Cut the "sign up for Deals of the Day" block entirely from a deals email — it's redundant and undercuts the one you're already reading.
- 4. Add a MyLowe's rewards or member-pricing callout since I'm already in their system.

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Standard Bronto/Lowe's ESP template with heavy MSO/legacy conditional CSS; QA scan found no broken tracking links, but flags missing unsubscribe/authentication headers and several images without alt text.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
No issues found. 26 tracking/redirect links were present but skipped by the automated HTTP prober (expected behavior for click-redirect domains, not a defect). Impression pixels resolve to `sli.lowes.com/imp` with query params (`s=`, `p=`, `lctg=`, `stpe=`) — no truncation or malformed URLs observed.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Missing `alt` text on 6 images, including the two content images (`c301e24e-...jpg`, `ad569dc5-...png`) and four tracking pixels under `sli.lowes.com/imp`. Content images should carry descriptive `alt`; tracking pixels should use `alt=""` to be explicitly marked decorative rather than omitted.
- Template uses legacy MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if !mso]><!-->`) and multiple `@font-face` declarations with `.woff`-only sources (no `.woff2`/fallback), which is standard for this ESP stack but has no `mso-*` fallback font declared outside the conditionals shown.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
Recipient-identifying token is embedded directly in tracking pixel URLs as a literal email address (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`) rather than a hashed/encoded identifier — this is plaintext PII exposure in image request logs (visible to any network intermediary or log system that captures pixel hits). No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{...}}`) found in the truncated source.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header: not found — may be relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than sender omission, but cannot be confirmed as present.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click): not found — one-click unsubscribe cannot be confirmed as supported.
- `Authentication-Results` header (SPF/DKIM/DMARC): not found — cannot verify sender authentication passed.
- Physical mailing address / unsubscribe link presence in body HTML could not be confirmed from the truncated source provided.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Cannot be assessed from the truncated HTML — no destination/CTA `<a href>` URLs with UTM parameters were visible in the provided source excerpt (only tracking pixel and font asset URLs were present).

7. **Recommendations**
- Add `alt` attributes to the two content images and `alt=""` to the four `sli.lowes.com/imp` tracking pixels.
- Replace plaintext email address in `sli.lowes.com/imp?e=` params with a hashed or tokenized subscriber ID to avoid PII leakage via pixel logs.
- Confirm with the relay/mailbox provider (AgentMail) whether `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers are being stripped in transit vs. genuinely absent at origin — this determines whether it's a Lowe's ESP issue or a relay/QA-tooling limitation.
- Re-run the audit against the untruncated HTML to evaluate CTA link UTM structure and unsubscribe footer markup, which weren't visible in this excerpt.
