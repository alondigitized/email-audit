# Grab these Closeout Event deals FAST ⚡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- Sixth Lowe's email in five days, and honestly at this point my inbox looks less like a retailer newsletter and more like a slot machine that keeps pulling the same lever. That said — "Closeout Event" with 40% off patio furniture and 50% off Alexa/Ring/Blink/Fire TV is real money on stuff I actually care about, so my thumb didn't slow all the way to Archive. Patio furniture is on my list this month since I'm redoing the back deck, so that tile actually landed.
- Then it falls apart. The MyLowe's Money Days banner is the same one I saw Tuesday and Wednesday, and then — I kid you not — there's a "Surprise! It's Christmas in July" section with an elf on the shelf and reindeer graphics in the same email as patio furniture and smart home closeouts. That's not a bundle, that's three different emails stapled together. I'd click through to patio furniture specifically, but I'm not forwarding this — it's too scattershot to send a buddy without a "yeah I know, just look at the middle part" caveat.

## What's working

- 40% off patio furniture is a real, specific offer and it's exactly the kind of project I'm shopping for right now — deck furniture season.
- 50% off Alexa/Ring/Blink/Fire TV is a genuine deal with clear category icons, not a vague "electronics" tile.
- Both offer tiles have their own dedicated "Shop now" button — no hunting for the CTA.
- Product renders (patio set, smart speakers, floodlight cam) are clean, no broken images or placeholder junk.

## What's weak

- The Christmas-in-July section is jarring and out of place — elf figurines and a lit tree sitting right under a smart-home closeout banner reads like two teams' emails got merged by accident.
- MyLowe's Money Days banner is the third time this week I've seen this exact graphic — I'm numb to it now.
- No rebate clarity, no pro-vs-homeowner cue, nothing that says "we know you have a Pro account" — this is generic mass-blast copy.
- "Closeout Event" hero doesn't say what's actually closing out — I have to scroll to find out it's patio and smart home, not tools or lumber.
- Deals of the Day banner right after is a third distinct offer thrown into the same send — no focus at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to ONE campaign per email — either lead with patio/outdoor since that's seasonally relevant right now, or lead with the smart home closeout, not both plus a holiday gag plus Deals of the Day plus a newsletter signup ask.
- 2. Drop the Christmas-in-July section entirely from a "closeout" send — it undercuts the urgency of "these deals are too good to wait on" with a joke.
- 3. Put a specific product or price ($ amount, not just %) in the hero so I know what's actually on the chopping block before I scroll.
- 4. Space out MyLowe's Money Days promos — I've now seen this banner in at least 3 of the last 5 emails; it's losing all urgency.
- **Subject Alt A:** `40% off patio sets — closeout ends today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Deck furniture closeout: up to 40% off, today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patio sets and smart home deals both drop today — pick your project.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off Ring, Blink, and Fire TV — closeout pricing ends tonight.`
