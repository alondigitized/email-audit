## What's working

- 35% off select major appliances is a real, specific number, not vague "big savings" fluff.
- Free delivery + free install/parts + free haul-away stacked together is genuinely useful — that's the stuff that actually makes or breaks whether I pull the trigger on a fridge or washer.
- Three actual products shown (microwave, dishwasher, stacked washer/dryer) with "Buy now" buttons instead of just a vague banner — I can act immediately without hunting.
- Brand names I trust are visible — LG, Whirlpool, Bosch — not generic stock photography.
- The $2,500 spend threshold for the 2-year protection plan bonus is called out, so I know the bar to clear for the extra perk.

## What's weak

- The holiday/Rudolph block randomly interrupting an appliance deal email is jarring — wrong season, wrong mood, feels like two emails mashed into one.
- "Members get" never says which membership up front — I have to scroll to the fine print to learn it's MyLowe's Pro Rewards specific, and even then it excludes Florida members, which is a weird footnote to bury.
- The three product cards under the hero have their titles cut off mid-word ("Front Control Built-in / ( Stainless") — looks like a rendering clip, not clean copy.
- No pricing shown on the individual product cards — just "Buy now" with no dollar figure, so I can't tell if these are actually the deal items or just filler SKUs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the holiday/Rudolph block entirely from this send, or move it to its own separate seasonal email — it dilutes a genuinely strong appliance offer.
- 2. State the membership requirement in the hero itself ("MyLowe's Pro Rewards members get...") instead of forcing me to scroll to a footnote.
- 3. Add actual prices or "was/now" pricing to the three product cards — a bare "Buy now" with no number kills urgency.
- 4. Fix the truncated product titles on the dishwasher and washer/dryer cards so they don't look broken.
