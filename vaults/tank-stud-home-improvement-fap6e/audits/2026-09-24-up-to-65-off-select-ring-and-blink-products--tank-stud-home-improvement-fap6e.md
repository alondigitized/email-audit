# Up to 65% off select Ring and Blink products🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- Ring and Blink up to 65% off — that's a real number on a real category, and it actually caught my eye first thing. Security cameras aren't glamorous but they're the kind of thing I've been meaning to add to the garage and side door for a year now, so this one's relevant. Then it just... keeps going. Appliances, vanities, gift guides, carpet install — by the time I hit "Deals of the Day sign up" I've forgotten what the email was even about.
- The 3x points banner is a nice touch since I'm a MyLowe's guy, but it's buried right under the hero instead of tied to the actual camera offer, so I can't tell if buying Ring gets me the points or if that's a separate smart-home thing entirely. That ambiguity costs it a click from me.
- Bottom line: I'd open it, glance at the hero, maybe click "Shop now" on the cameras since that's on my list, but I wouldn't scroll the whole thing and I definitely wouldn't forward it — there's no single offer strong enough to text a buddy about.

## What's working

- The hero is clean — big "65% off," clear product shot of the Ring/Blink lineup, no clutter competing for attention.
- "Shop now" CTA is right under the offer, no hunting for it.
- 3x MyLowe's Rewards points banner speaks directly to me as a member, not a stranger.
- Gift guide tiles (Gifts under $25/$50) with actual tool/hardware imagery (that Kobalt tool box, putty knives) feel like my aisle, not generic seasonal filler.

## What's weak

- Way too much crammed in one send — cameras, appliances, vanities, gifts, flooring, deals-of-the-day signup. Pick a lane.
- The 3x points offer doesn't clearly connect to the Ring/Blink deal above it, so I can't tell if it applies.
- "More savings, all around the house" section (appliances + vanity) has nothing to do with the subject line — feels like a bait-and-switch after the security camera hook.
- Flooring section ("Fresh floors, get installed by Thanksgiving") is a whole different purchase decision buried mid-scroll where nobody's looking for it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this into two emails — one focused Ring/Blink security send, one general house-savings roundup. Trying to be everything to everyone kills the urgency of the actual headline offer.
- 2. Tie the 3x points banner explicitly to the Ring/Blink purchase ("Buy Ring/Blink, earn 3x points") instead of leaving it ambiguous.
- 3. Move the gift guide and flooring blocks to their own dedicated sends closer to the actual season/need — they're diluting a decent hero offer.
- 4. Add a specific model or price example under the cameras (e.g., "Ring Battery Doorbell now $XX") — a bare percentage without an anchor price makes me do math I don't want to do.
