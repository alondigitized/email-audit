- The eight-tile grid covers appliances, patio, tools, kitchen/bath, décor, and smart home — feels like a genuine multi-project catalog, not a single gimmick.

- Halloween animatronics in a Labor Day tools/appliance email is a jarring non-sequitur — feels like a different marketing calendar got pasted in.
