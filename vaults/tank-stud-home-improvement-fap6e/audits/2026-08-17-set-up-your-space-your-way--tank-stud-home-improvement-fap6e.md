## What's working

- Kobalt mini tool cabinet and NFL toolbox section hits my garage-organization itch directly — recognizable brand, clear product shot.
- Tailgate section (grills, coolers) is dead-on seasonal for August, and the photo actually shows people my age doing it, not a stock-photo family.
- 10% off moving boxes when you spend $30+ is a real, specific offer with a real threshold — no vague "save big" language.
- Free cooler gift-with-purchase tied to MyLowe's Rewards membership makes me feel like a known customer, not a random inbox.
- "Curated with you in mind" product row at the bottom (tote, boxes, microwave) with individual Buy Now buttons is low-friction — no need to click through to a category page first.

## What's weak

- The hero — "Make the most of your space" with a throw pillow, a bird painting, and a desk lamp — reads pure dorm/apartment decor. That's not my project, and it's the first thing I see.
- "These items are a total game changer" is filler copy — doesn't tell me what the items even are before I click.
- Command hooks and "rental-friendly upgrades" with a houseplant is renter-targeted messaging landing in a homeowner's inbox; feels like the segmentation slipped here.
- No fence, deck, patio, or outdoor power equipment content at all this time, even though that's been a recurring thread in my last few Lowe's opens — feels like a missed callback.
- A lot of sections back-to-back with no real narrative thread — it's less "here's your weekend project" and more "here's everything in the store."

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the hero to the Kobalt tool cabinet or the tailgate/grill shot — that's the strongest, most on-persona image in the whole email, and it's buried three sections down.
- 2. Cut or move the dorm-decor and Command-hooks/rental blocks out of my version entirely — I'm not the renter/college audience, and leading with it wastes my first two scrolls.
- 3. Give the free cooler gift a bigger, earlier placement — free-with-purchase tied to my rewards account is a stronger hook than "make the most of your space."
- 4. Bring back an outdoor/patio or fence-project tie-in since that's been a recurring theme for me lately — even one tile would keep continuity with what's clearly working in past sends.
- 5. Tighten hero copy to name the actual category instead of "total game changer" — tell me what I'm clicking into before I click.
