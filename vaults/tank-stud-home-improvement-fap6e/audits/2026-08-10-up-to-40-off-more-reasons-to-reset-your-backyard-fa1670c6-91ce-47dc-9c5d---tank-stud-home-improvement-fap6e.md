# Up to 40% OFF! More reasons to reset your backyard
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-10**
## Executive summary

- Now THIS is more like it. Fencing guy driving screws into a cedar privacy fence, EGO pressure washer, a real pellet grill — this is my Saturday, not some kid's craft table like that MrBeast Lab nonsense from a couple days back. The 40% off patio furniture hero is nice if you're into that, but I skip right past it to "Tools, fencing and more" and the EGO washer at $200 off. That's the section doing the actual work here.
- Compare this to the Aug 6 storage/organization email — that one at least had "save up to $1,000" as a hook. This one's got three separate offers stacked (40% patio, $200 off power equipment, 20% off grills) which is honestly a lot to process, but at least two of them are things I'd actually buy. I'd click through on the pressure washer section for sure.
- Would I forward this to a buddy? Maybe the grill section if he's been talking about upgrading his setup. Overall this is a solid mid-tier Lowe's send — better targeted than the storage email, way better than the workshop garbage, not quite the closeout-event urgency of the Aug 5 one.

## What's working

- The fence-building guy photo is exactly my demo — not a lifestyle model, looks like an actual guy doing actual work.
- EGO pressure washer with "Save up to $200" is a specific, believable project-tool offer I'd act on.
- Three distinct project categories (patio, power tools/fencing, grilling) means I'm likely to find something relevant even if the hero offer isn't my thing.
- MyLowe's Rewards 2x points banner is right there and readable — I have that account, so this registers.
- "Deals of the Day" sign-up CTA at the bottom is a reasonable secondary ask, not pushy.

## What's weak

- Four "Shop now" buttons plus an "Activate now" plus "Explore now" x2 — by the time I hit the bottom I've seen six near-identical navy buttons. It blurs together.
- The 40% patio furniture hero eats the whole top of the email but that's not really my project — I'm not buying a lounge set, I'm buying tools and materials.
- Fine print at the very bottom about "one time offer" and dates in tiny gray text — I'd have to squint on my phone to catch that this is time-boxed at all.
- "2x Points" rewards module needs me to "activate" first, which is one extra step I'll probably forget to do before I actually buy anything.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the fencing/tools/power equipment section instead of patio furniture — that's the stronger persona match and the $200-off EGO deal is a harder offer than a vague 40%.
- 2. Cut the button count. Six CTAs in one email is too many — consolidate the two power-tool blocks (fencing photo + EGO washer) into one section with one clear button.
- 3. Surface the offer expiration date up near the top, not buried in 8pt gray fine print at the bottom — "Ends 9/30" next to the hero would add real urgency.
- 4. Make the rewards activation a one-click deal, not a two-step "activate then shop" — or at least tell me what I'm losing if I don't activate.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: activate 2x points on allen + roth and Kobalt`
