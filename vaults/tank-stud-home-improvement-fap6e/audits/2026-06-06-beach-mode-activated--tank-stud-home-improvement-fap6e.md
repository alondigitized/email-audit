## What's weak

- **"Beach mode" is not a DIY weekend warrior signal** — a Gen X guy with a reno list is not mentally in "beach mode" and this subject line doesn't pull me in
- **Sta-Green garden bag as the hero product is confusing** — that's a yard-waste hauler; the beach-lifestyle styling makes it look like Lowe's is lost, not creative
