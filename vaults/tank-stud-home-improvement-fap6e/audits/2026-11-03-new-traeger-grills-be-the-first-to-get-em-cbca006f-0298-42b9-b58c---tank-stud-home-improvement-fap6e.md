# NEW Traeger grills? Be the first to get 'em 🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This one has a clean hero and a real reason to act, but it's a grill email and I'm a DIY guy. Traeger at Lowe's is news, and the "Member Only Access" lock badge plus the 11/4 deadline gives it some juice. But it came in on 11/3, so "ends tomorrow" means I have about a day to decide on a $700+ pellet grill. There's no price, no model, and no discount anywhere.
- The 3x points banner is the only real member benefit. It sits under a footnote about "exclusions and more terms apply," and I don't trust that. Then the Black Friday block shows up with no number, the same move as the 10/29 "Black Friday deals showed up early" send. I've seen that creative already.
- Would I click? Maybe the first "Shop now" if I'd been eyeing a smoker for the patio I'm building. Otherwise I'd mark it read. I wouldn't forward it. None of my buddies are in the market for a grill this week.

## What's working

- The hero is clean. "Traeger is now at Lowe's" with a big grill photo tells me what this is in two seconds.
- The "Member Only Access" lock badge and "ends on 11/4" make the early-access angle credible.
- The 3x Points hexagon is easy to spot, and the "Activate now" button tells me I have to opt in.
- The mobile layout is simple, with big buttons and readable type, and nothing overlaps or breaks.

## What's weak

- Not one price, model name, or dollar-off figure on the grill. "Exclusive access" isn't a deal.
- Grills aren't my project category. There's no drill, no tile, no drywall, nothing for the weekend job.
- The Black Friday block is generic and has no number or product, the same teaser as last week.
- "Subject to exclusions and more terms apply" in tiny print undercuts the 3x points offer.
- Three different "Shop now" and "Activate now" buttons all point at different things, and none says which grill or which sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real product and price in the hero, like "Traeger Pro 575, $XXX, members get it first." Show one specific model.
- 2. Spell out the points math: "3x points = $X back on a $799 grill." Move the exclusions up or link them where I can see them.
- 3. Swap the generic Black Friday banner for one concrete early deal, ideally in tools or the project categories I actually buy.
- 4. Add a stock or delivery cue, like "In stock, ships free" or "pickup today."

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML has a malformed DOCTYPE and one image with no alt text. The compliance and authentication warnings come from a relay that may not capture headers, so they are unconfirmed. The source was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are limited to what the QA data reports.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- The plain-text part is 3604/4677 characters URLs (about 77%, over the 70% threshold). This points to long tracked URLs in the text alternative. It is a mild spam-filter and readability risk.
- No broken links were confirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text:** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers will announce the filename, and image-blocking clients will show nothing.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** the source has `PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` with no space after `PUBLIC`. The DTD URL and the `xmlns` namespace also use `https://www.w3.org/...`. The XHTML namespace identifier is defined as `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`. Most clients tolerate this, but a strict parser might not, so it is worth fixing.
- Custom web fonts (`Fellix`, `DIN Next`) load from `www.lowescdn.com` inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` wrappers. This is handled correctly, so no issue.
- The mobile media queries are present: `max-width: 480px`, and `min-width: 375px` and `max-width: 600px`. No issue found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source contains no unresolved merge tokens such as `%%...%%` or `{{...}}`. Because the HTML was truncated, I could not check the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** both are reported as not found. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them. Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders, so check the raw headers at the origin before treating this as a defect.
- **Physical address:** the check did not detect one. CAN-SPAM requires it. The source was truncated and the footer may not have been scanned, so this is unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results:** the header is absent, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender is `lowes@e.lowes.com`. The relay is the expected cause, not a sender fault.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The 14 tracking links were not followed, and the truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a meaningful `alt` attribute to `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. Use `alt=""` if the image is decorative.
2. Correct the DOCTYPE to `PUBLIC "-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN"` and use `http://` for the `xmlns` namespace.
3. Shorten the tracked URLs in the plain-text part, or reduce the number of links, to lower the URL ratio below 70%.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` in the raw headers captured before the relay. Also confirm a physical address in the full footer. If either is missing, fix it.
5. Capture the full HTML and resolve the tracking redirects in a follow-up run, so the final URLs and UTM parameters can be checked.
