## Executive summary

- Okay, this one lands right in my wheelhouse — "save up to $1,500 on select appliances" with free delivery over $999, that's real reno-phase money and I actually pay attention to appliance suites because my kitchen's next on the list. The $4,516 LG suite with the $2,660 savings called out is a concrete number, not vague marketing fluff, and that's the kind of thing that makes me stop scrolling.
- Where it loses me is the back half — bathroom vanities and faucets I can use, but then it swerves into a HELOC pitch, then randomly tosses in a storage tote, garden soil, and a cleaning powder like a leftover clearance bin. That "curated with you in mind" line is doing a lot of lying for three completely unrelated $10 items.
- Overall: I'd click the appliance suite or the vanity deal, probably not forward it — my buddy doing his kitchen next spring might want the LG suite screenshot though. Not deleting it, not a hard pass, but the back half feels like filler.

## What's working

- Real number up top — "$1,500" and "FREE Delivery" over $999 is a threshold I actually track when I'm bundling a purchase.
- The $4,516 LG suite tile with a specific "$2,660" savings and "Buy now" button — that's the kind of hero deal that gets me to click through and compare vs. Home Depot.
- Category icons (fridges, washer/dryer, ranges) give me a fast way to jump straight to what I need without scrolling the whole thing.
- Bathroom section is relevant to me too — vanities and faucets are legit reno-adjacent, and the 50%/40% off framing is clean and easy to read.

## What's weak

- The HELOC/Figure loan banner feels like it wandered in from a different email — I didn't open this looking for a home equity pitch, and it breaks my flow right after two solid appliance/bath sections.
- "Curated with you in mind" over a storage tote, garden soil, and cleaning powder is a joke — that's not curation, that's whatever didn't sell last week.
- No mention of Lowe's Pro/MyLowe's rewards or points anywhere, and I've got a MyLowe's account — treat me like a member, not an anonymous inbox.
- Weak "Deals of the Day" sign-up CTA near the bottom just feels like padding before the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the loan banner and the random 3-pack (tote/soil/cleaner) entirely — replace that real estate with a second appliance category (dishwashers, or a Whirlpool/Samsung alt suite) to keep the whole email in one lane.
- 2. Surface MyLowe's/Pro rewards points on the appliance purchase — even one line like "Earn X points on this suite" would beat a generic loan ad.
- 3. Move the vanity/faucet section right after the hero so bath and appliance reno content stay together, then end on Deals of the Day.
- 4. Tighten subject/preview so the $1,500 number and appliance category show up together without needing to open.
