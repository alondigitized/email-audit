# Bag a FREE power tool for your next project🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This one finally speaks my language. A DEWALT jigsaw and a stack of 20V batteries, with "3 FREE" in big green block letters. That's a real tool-aisle hook. The subject said "free power tool," so I opened it. Then the hero says "get up to 3 free select tools when you buy a select battery kit." That's a battery-platform bundle, not a free drill. I've been burned by this kind of rebate copy before. I need to know which kit, what price, and which tools, and the email doesn't say.
- The rest is a decent DIY mix. There's a $200 DEWALT savings tile, and a $60 EGO tile for the yard. It goes soft after that. The "Curated with you in mind" row shows a plastic tote, a paper lawn bag and a bottle of Bar Keepers Friend. That's nothing like a guy with a drywall job. Compared to the Oct 20 Halloween send and Oct 21 appliance send, this is the first Lowe's email in a week that fits what I do.
- Would I click? Yes, on the hero or the DEWALT tile, mostly to check the fine print. Forward it? Only to my brother-in-law who's buying DEWALT batteries. Otherwise I'd save it for the next reno phase.

## What's working

- The "3 FREE" block on the hero is easy to read on a phone, and the jigsaw plus batteries tells me it's a platform deal.
- The DEWALT tile shows a circular saw, impact driver and recip saw with "Save up to $200." That's a concrete number for the kit I'd actually buy.
- The photo of a gloved hand on a DEWALT drill with sawdust flying looks like real work, not a staged ad.
- Every tile has its own "Buy now" or "Shop now" button, and they're big enough to tap.
- Subject and hero match, and I know the sender.

## What's weak

- "Get up to 3 free select tools" with "select battery kit" is vague. I can't tell the kit price, which tools, or whether it's a rebate or instant.
- No pro vs. homeowner signal. There's no Lowe's Pro pricing, no MyLowe's rewards prompt, and no credit card offer.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row is off-target. A tote, a paper lawn bag and a cleaner are not a weekend project bundle. The product names are also cut off ("27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -").
- The fall and yard tiles pull away from the tool message, and the "Deals of the Day" sign-up repeats what the other Lowe's sends already do.
- No deadline and no in-stock cue, so there's no reason to act this weekend.

## Recommendations

- 1. Name the offer in the hero: "Buy the DEWALT 20V 5Ah kit, get 3 tools free, ends Sunday," with a price. Spell out the rebate mechanics in one line.
- 2. Replace the "Curated" row with actual project items (drill bits, drywall screws, a shop vac), or with real personalization based on my browsing.
- 3. Add a MyLowe's or Pro rewards line and a store pickup or in-stock cue.
- 4. Cut the yard and grill tiles, or move them below a tool-focused section.
- 5. Fix the cut-off product titles.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is mostly standard table-based email markup. The automated QA run found no hard failures, and a 57% pass rate with 3 warnings. The main verifiable issues are 17 images with no alt text, including 15 retail-media impression pixels, and the recipient's email address in plaintext in those pixel URLs. The compliance and authentication items are unverified, not confirmed defects. The source was truncated and 36 links were not probed.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unverified destinations:** 36 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the HTTP probe, so I can't confirm their status codes, redirect chains or final landing URLs.
- **Impression pixels:** 15 `https://sli.lowes.com/imp?...` pixels are embedded (`s=127768300`–`304`, `2019286`–`2019290`, `1090834`–`1090838`). They carry `stpe=pixel`, `static` or `default` and `li_coord` parameters, so they look like a retail-media ad-serving integration. Each is an extra third-party request at open time. Some appear to repeat the same `s` (`2019286`, `1090834` each appear twice), which may double-count impressions.
- **No other link defects:** I found nothing else in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (17 images):**
  - Content images: `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/59/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` and `.../m/71/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png`. If these carry content, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing.

- **Nonstandard namespace/DTD URLs:** The DTD URL and `xmlns` use `https://www.w3.org/...`. This is harmless in practice.
- **Positives:** The `viewport` meta is present, and `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` resets are in place. Web fonts are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`. Media queries cover ≤480px and 375–600px.
