# Up to 50% OFF on a Sunday? Doesn’t get any better
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-06**
## Executive summary

- Okay, now we're cooking. Grill deals, patio furniture, leaf blowers, riding mowers, mulch — this is a legit fall-yard-work email, not the concert-ticket nonsense they sent me a couple days ago or the "$800 off" tote-bag bait from the Labor Day send. The "Get ready to tailgate" MyLowe's Rewards block with 3x points actually caught my eye since I've got the card.
- Biggest issue: it's a wall of separate deal-blocks stacked one after another with zero hierarchy telling me which one is the actual headline offer of the day. By the time I hit the Kobalt "Sponsored" tile near the bottom I'm scroll-fatigued. Still, there's enough real project-relevant stuff here (mowers, blowers, mulch, patio) that I'd scroll through on my phone at breakfast. I'd click the leaf blower or riding mower block — wouldn't forward this to a buddy, but I wouldn't mark it read without looking either.

## What's working

- Riding mower and leaf blower deals with real dollar amounts ($200 off, $70 off) — that's exactly the fall yard-prep stuff I'm thinking about right now.
- FREE Delivery flagged next to the mower deal — that's a real purchase-decision factor for a big-ticket item.
- MyLowe's Rewards 3x points tailgate tile speaks to me directly as a cardholder, and the photo of guys grilling/tailgating in a driveway matches my demo.
- Patio furniture + grill combo up top is a smart seasonal pairing — that's literally "backyard's booked all season" copy landing right.
- Sta-Green mulch bundle (4 for $10) is a specific, concrete small-ticket add-on I'd actually toss in a cart.

## What's weak

- Way too many stacked deal blocks with no single hero — nothing tells me which is THE Sunday deal versus the fifteen other things competing for my thumb.
- "Curated with you in mind" section is a letdown — a storage tote, cleaner, and closet shelving liner is not "curated" for a guy shopping mowers and grills, it's generic filler like the tote-and-Bar Keeper's combo that tanked the 9/2 email.
- The blue gradient "Sunday Deals" hero banner has decorative leaves floating around but no actual product or price — it's all mood, no offer, right at the top where it matters most.
- Kobalt "Sponsored" tool tile buried near the bottom in tiny text — should be higher if it's a real deal, feels like an afterthought ad insert.
- Header banner text ("Plus, Extra Big Deals end tomorrow...") is tiny and easy to skim past, so the actual urgency deadline barely registers.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero deal (I'd vote the riding mower at $200 off + free delivery — that's real fall-project money) and lead the email with it instead of the mood-only "Sunday Deals" banner.
- 2. Cut or replace the "Curated with you in mind" block — either make it genuinely personalized to outdoor power tools/patio, or drop it entirely.
- 3. Move the Kobalt sponsored tool deal higher and make clear it's a real discount, not just brand filler.
- 4. Tighten the top banner urgency copy — bigger font, one clear line: "Labor Day deals end tomorrow — Sunday deals end tonight."
- 5. Consolidate the patio/grill and mower/blower sections into one "fall yard reset" bundle instead of five separate stacked mini-sections.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Riding mowers: up to $200 off + free delivery, today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your yard's fall reset: mowers, blowers, mulch — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Patio, grills, mowers and blowers — Sunday-only savings before Labor Day deals end`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `3x Rewards points on tailgate gear, plus up to $200 off riding mowers`
