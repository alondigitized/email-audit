# 💰COMING SOON: MyLowe's Money Days!💰
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-30**
## Executive summary

- Finally. After back-to-back beach bucket emails that had zero business being in my inbox as a guy who just bought $400 in deck lumber, Lowe's pivots to something I actually care about: a loyalty event that rewards me for the big-ticket purchases I was already going to make. July 9–12, up to $30 back in MyLowe's Money on a $200-plus cart — that's one bathroom fixture run, one weekend of framing materials, easy. The hero is clean and readable. The dates are right there. The offer math is instant.
- That said, this is a teaser. "Coming Soon" with no product hook, no project angle, no "here's what to stock your cart with before the event" — I'm just supposed to hold the date and hit Shop Now on... nothing specific. That's a miss. The subject line's dual-dollar-emoji bookend and ALL CAPS read like the email was written for a Black Friday blast, not a member who's been steadily buying from Lowe's for a decade.
- I'd mark this as read with a mental note to check back July 9th. I wouldn't forward it — there's nothing to forward, it's a calendar alert dressed up as an email. But I'm not unsubscribing either, because this is the first send in a while that respected my wallet.

## What's working

- **Offer math is instant** — "$30 extra MyLowe's Money on a $200+ purchase" takes me about two seconds to process and I already know I can hit that threshold restocking my shop.
- **Dates in the hero, not buried** — July 9–12 is right there in the visual hierarchy. I don't have to hunt for the window.
- **"First time ever" framing** — it's a small copy choice but it signals this is worth paying attention to, not just another promo.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — blue card imagery, bold headline, offer line, CTA. Eye goes straight down the page with no detours.
- **Member-forward framing** — "members get" treats me like someone who earned this, not a stranger getting a mass blast.

## What's weak

- **No project hook in a project-driver email** — "Start planning your cart!" tells me nothing. Planning my cart for what? Throw me three categories: lumber, plumbing, flooring. Give me somewhere to go.
- **Subject line double emoji + ALL CAPS is screaming** — I don't need to be shouted at to read a Lowe's email. It reads like spam and undercuts the member-exclusive angle the body is trying to build.
- **Zero personalization** — Lowe's knows my purchase history. A "last time you stocked up on X" line or even my name in the preview would've made this feel like a tip-off from someone who knows me, not a broadcast.
- **"COMING SOON" gives no dates in the subject** — I get the dates once I open, but in the inbox this is just urgency theater. July 9–12 fits in the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put the event window in the subject** — swap "COMING SOON" for the actual dates; that's the hook for someone who schedules project runs around sales.
- 2. **Drop one emoji, kill the ALL CAPS** — one 💰 at the end, sentence case. More confident, less shouty.
- 3. **Add a "stock your cart" category row** — three tiles: Lumber & Building Materials / Plumbing / Tools. Give me somewhere to land that isn't the homepage.
- 4. **Preheader should earn its keep** — "Like MyLowe's Money? We're giving you MORE!" is fine but vague. Use the character budget to name the event dates and threshold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `MyLowe's Money Days July 9–12: earn up to $30 back 💰`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members only: $30 in MyLowe's Money, July 9–12`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $200+ July 9–12 and get extra MyLowe's Money deposited automatically.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `July 9–12 only — the biggest MyLowe's Money event ever. Start your list now.`

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard ESP-delivered promotional email on a table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional layout. No critical rendering bugs detected, but three compliance gaps (CAN-SPAM address, List-Unsubscribe headers) and unresolved SPF/DKIM authentication status warrant attention before the live send.

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**29 tracking links skipped** by the QA probe — all routed through a click-redirect domain (`e.lowes.com` or equivalent). If any redirect targets changed after template build (e.g., landing page URL updates), broken destinations would be invisible to pre-send QA.

No raw untracked URLs found in the truncated source — consistent with full ESP wrapping.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text (confirmed):**
- `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` — no `alt` attribute present. For a promotional email where this image likely carries offer copy ("Money Days"), a blank alt means screen reader users and image-blocked clients (Outlook default, corporate proxies) lose that content entirely.

**Font loading:** Four `@font-face` declarations (Fellix family + DIN Next) load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. `.woff2` is now universally supported and significantly smaller. Not a breaking issue but increases load time in webmail clients that render fonts.

**Duplicate `@font-face` blocks:** Fellix fonts are declared inside `<!--[if !mso]><!-->`; DIN Next is declared in a *separate* identical conditional block immediately after. This is redundant — both blocks use the same MSO conditional and could be merged. Minor, but adds parse overhead.

**Media query overlap:** Two breakpoint blocks target different width ranges (`375px–600px` and `max-width: 480px`) with overlapping `.drop`, `.container`, and `.no-padding` rules. On devices 375–480px wide, both blocks fire and the second overrides the first. Likely intentional but worth confirming no rule conflicts exist in the full source.

**Hotmail/ExternalClass hacks:** `line-height: 100%` applied to `.ExternalClass *` — this is a legacy Hotmail fix that predates Outlook.com's modern renderer. It's harmless but unnecessary for current Outlook.com.

No unresolved merge tokens visible in the truncated HTML (no `{{`, `%%`, `[%`, or similar unfired placeholders). Cannot confirm dynamic fields (member name, points balance) without full source, but none were flagged by QA.

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM — physical address: WARN**
No physical mailing address detected in the QA scan. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid physical postal address in every commercial email. If the address is in the footer (outside the truncated HTML provided), this warning is a false positive — but it should be confirmed against the full rendered template.

**List-Unsubscribe: WARN**
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not found
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058 one-click)

These are likely present on the actual ESP-sent message but were not captured through the AgentMail relay. Gmail and Yahoo Mail (as of Feb 2024 bulk sender requirements) require `List-Unsubscribe-Post` for one-click unsubscribe. Lowe's volume almost certainly qualifies as a bulk sender — confirm with ESP that both headers are injected at send time, not just an unsubscribe link in body copy.

**Authentication: WARN**
`Authentication-Results` header not present in relayed copy — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown. For a domain sending at Lowe's volume, `lowes@e.lowes.com` should show DKIM pass on the `e.lowes.com` subdomain aligned to DMARC policy on `lowes.com`. Must be verified against a live received header, not this relay capture.
