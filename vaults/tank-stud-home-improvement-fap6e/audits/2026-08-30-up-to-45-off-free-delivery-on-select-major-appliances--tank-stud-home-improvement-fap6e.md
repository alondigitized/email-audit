# Up to 45% OFF + FREE delivery on select major appliances 😍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-30**
## Executive summary

- Two appliance emails back to back — this is basically the same "45% off + free delivery on $999+" stack I saw yesterday (8/29), just reshuffled. The hero doesn't even lead with appliances; it opens on a generic "Sunday Deals — big savings for your fall projects" banner, and I have to scroll past that before I hit the actual appliance offer. Once I get there, it's a real photo of a fridge/range/dishwasher/microwave suite with the free delivery banner stacked underneath — that part reads fine, I know exactly what's for sale and what the threshold is.
- Where it falls apart for me is everything after the appliance block. Leaf blower guy on a brick walkway is a solid project shot, but then it jumps to purple Halloween animatronics, then mulch, then a random "curated for you" row of a storage tote, an all-purpose cleaner, and cordless blinds — none of that has anything to do with each other or with the subject line I opened for. It feels like five different emails duct-taped together.
- I'd skim it, confirm nothing's changed from yesterday's appliance deal, and archive. Not forwarding this one — the Halloween tote-and-blinds grab bag kills any "hey check this out" energy the appliance photo built up.

## What's working

- Real appliance lineup photo (fridge, range, dishwasher, microwave) — I can see exactly what I'd be buying, not a stock lifestyle shot.
- Free delivery + install/haul-away/protection plan icons are laid out clearly under the offer, so the fine print isn't hidden.
- Leaf blower/brick-walkway shot is a legit weekend-project photo — that's the kind of image that gets a guy like me to actually look twice.
- "45% off" and "$999+" threshold are both visible without hunting.

## What's weak

- This is nearly the same offer/subject as yesterday's 8/29 send ("Up to 45% OFF select major appliances + FREE delivery") — two days running on the identical stack is fatigue, not urgency.
- Hero banner is generic "Sunday Deals" filler before I even get to the appliance content — wastes my first scroll on nothing.
- Halloween animatronics wedged between power equipment and mulch has zero connection to "45% off appliances" — feels like a different email got spliced in.
- "Curated with you in mind" row (storage tote, cleaner, cordless blinds) isn't curated to anything I've shown interest in — generic clearance filler, not personalization.
- Too many competing CTAs (7+ "Shop now"/"Buy now" buttons) dilute which one actually matters.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the near-duplicate cadence — if 45%-off appliances ran yesterday, today's send should escalate the number, add a hard deadline, or skip a day. Sending the same stack twice reads as spam-adjacent even with a real offer behind it.
- 2. Cut the generic "Sunday Deals" hero and open directly on the appliance photo + 45% off — that's the actual reason I'd open this.
- 3. Drop the Halloween animatronics block from an appliance-led send, or move it to its own themed email — it breaks the "project" narrative Lowe's has been building all week.
- 4. Replace the random "curated with you in mind" row with actual purchase-history-based picks, or cut it — three unrelated SKUs with no throughline reads as filler, not personalization.

# Technical Audit — Lowe's "Up to 45% OFF Appliances" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
Standard Lowe's ESP (e.lowes.com / Salesforce Marketing Cloud-style) template with heavy legacy MSO/Outlook conditional CSS; automated QA passed 57% with 3 warning categories and no hard errors — issues cluster around missing accessibility attributes and unverifiable auth/compliance headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 34 tracking/click-redirect links were skipped by the automated HTTP prober (domains not resolvable/probeable directly) — link destinations could not be validated for 200 status or redirect integrity. Recommend manual click-through QA on a sample of these before send.
- Multiple 1x1 tracking pixels present via `sli.lowes.com/imp` (e.g., `?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=8302026&lctg=410146703&stpe=static`) — note the recipient email address is embedded in plaintext in the pixel URL query string (`e=` param), which is a PII-in-URL exposure pattern worth flagging even though it's standard for this ESP's tracking implementation.
- No broken/malformed link syntax found in the visible source.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text, confirmed by QA:
  - `c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` (image.e.lowes.com)
  - `ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` (image.e.lowes.com)
  - 4× `sli.lowes.com/imp` tracking pixel images (functionally decorative, but missing `alt=""` rather than a null/empty attribute means some screen readers may announce the raw URL or filename)
- Template uses extensive legacy support scaffolding: `mso` conditional comments, `ExternalClass` fixes for Hotmail/Outlook.com, `x-apple-data-detectors` overrides, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting` — indicates broad multi-client compatibility targeting (Outlook desktop, Gmail, Yahoo, Apple Mail).
- Responsive breakpoints defined at both 375–600px and ≤480px via media queries (`.mobile-hidden`, `.mobile-only`, `.container`, `.photo`) — structurally sound responsive approach, but rendering was not visually verified (source-only review).
- Custom web fonts (Fellix, DIN Next family) loaded via `@font-face` from `lowescdn.com` with no fallback stack shown in the truncated excerpt — verify a system-font fallback exists in body text styles (not visible in truncated source).

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g., `%%FIELD%%`, `{{token}}`, `[FNAME]`) found in the visible HTML.
- Tracking pixel URLs contain a dynamic per-recipient email token (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`) and campaign params (`p=8302026`, `lctg=410146703`) — confirms personalization/tracking is correctly populated per recipient rather than left as a raw placeholder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found by QA. May be a relay artifact (AgentMail may strip/not capture it) rather than a true absence — cannot confirm CAN-SPAM one-click compliance from headers alone. Recommend checking raw headers at the original receiving MTA if available.

- **Authentication-Results (SPF/DKIM/DMARC)**: not found — authentication status unknown via this relay; not necessarily a sender-side failure given the AgentMail intermediary.
- Body-level unsubscribe mechanism: template includes a `.managesubs` class (subscription management link/image), suggesting an in-body manage-preferences/unsubscribe link exists, though the actual unsubscribe URL/footer was not present in the truncated source to confirm functionality.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Cannot fully assess: the 34 tracking/redirect links (the ones most likely to carry UTM parameters and land on product/category pages) were skipped by the prober, so UTM structure and landing-page alignment could not be verified end-to-end.
- Campaign identifiers are present and consistent across pixels (`p=8302026`, `lctg=410146703`), indicating a single coherent campaign tag scheme, but no `utm_source`/`utm_campaign` params were visible in the truncated HTML to confirm alignment with landing pages.
