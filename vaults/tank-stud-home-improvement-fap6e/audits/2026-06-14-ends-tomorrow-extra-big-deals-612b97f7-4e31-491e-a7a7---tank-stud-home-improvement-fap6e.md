# 🚨Ends TOMORROW: Extra Big Deals
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- Six emails in a week from Lowe's — I'm on autopilot at this point. I open this one out of muscle memory more than excitement. The "🚨" and "TOMORROW" in all-caps in the subject is the kind of manufactured urgency I usually swipe past, but Lowe's has earned enough credit with me that I at least tap in. Once I'm in, the free CRAFTSMAN V20 9-Ah battery stops me cold — that's a real enticement. A 9-Ah V20 is not a freebie, that's a workhorse pack I'd buy on its own. Good. But then I scroll and hit a stainless steel French door refrigerator, a countdown timer, and something called "The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.a.t." — and I'm completely off the rails. I came here for tools and projects; this thing pivoted on me.
- I'd click on the battery deal. I would not forward this. The refrigerator belongs in a different email to a different segment.

## What's working

- **Free CRAFTSMAN V20 9-Ah Battery** — dead center, visible immediately, that's the hook and it's a legitimate one. Specific product, specific value, no asterisk-maze in sight.
- **Father's Day framing is timely** — June 14 in the email body, Father's Day relevance is obvious, and it hits the right demographic without feeling forced.
- **"Last chance to save on home projects, yard plans and more"** — that's my language. I *have* a project this weekend. That line actually lands.
- **"Buy now" buttons under individual product tiles** — not just a generic shop link; each deal has its own CTA which lowers the friction to actually acting on the specific offer I want.
- **Clean layout** — no broken images, no overlapping text, nav at the top is light and doesn't compete with the hero.

## What's weak

- **Refrigerator at $1,149** — my wife picks the appliances, not me. This belongs in a household email, not a tools-and-projects Father's Day push. It signals Lowe's isn't actually segmenting me.
- **"The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.a.t."** — I stared at this for three seconds and still don't know what it's selling. If it takes that long to parse, it's wasted real estate.
- **Cadence burnout** — this is email six from Lowe's in a week. The free spatula email two days ago was sharp. "Extra Big Deals" as a follow-up subject feels like a step down, not an escalation. They peaked early.
- **No MyLowe's member hook** — the June 13 spatula email used my name and called out member benefits. This one treats me like a stranger. That inconsistency is noticeable.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the battery in the subject line** — "Free CRAFTSMAN 9-Ah Battery ends tomorrow" tells me exactly what I'm getting without burning a click. Right now the subject could be for any store, any sale.
- 2. **Kill the refrigerator from this send** — or create a tools-only variant for MyLowe's members who skew project-oriented. One email, one audience.
- 3. **Fix "The G.O.A.T. is almost g.o.a.t."** — either put a product name there or don't run the section. Wordplay without a visible payoff is noise.
- 4. **Put my name back in** — the spatula email did it, this one dropped it. Keep the personalization consistent.
- 5. **Countdown timer** — "10 Days of Dad Deals" with a zeroed-out timer is fine if the deals are live, but double-check that the timer renders with real numbers, not zeros. Could be a rendering artifact.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free CRAFTSMAN 9-Ah Battery — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Father's Day tools deals: last 24 hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus save on power tools, outdoor gear, and more before the deadline hits.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your weekend project checklist just got cheaper. Shop before midnight.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "Ends TOMORROW: Extra Big Deals"

**Sender:** `lowes@e.lowes.com` | **Date:** 2026-06-17

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally standard table-based HTML with no critical rendering blockers, but it has meaningful accessibility gaps (missing alt text on 8 images, including tracking pixels) and unresolved authentication/compliance header visibility through the AgentMail relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **21 tracking links skipped** — All click-redirect links route through Lowe's ESP redirect domain; HTTP probing was skipped, so broken-link status is unverifiable from this audit. No dead links confirmed, but no live-link confirmation either.
- **Impression pixels exposed** — 5 `sli.lowes.com/imp?` tracking beacons embed the recipient address in plaintext in the URL query string (`e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`). This is standard ESP practice but means the email address is transmitted in GET request logs on any intermediate proxy.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 8 images:**

| Image | src (truncated) |
|---|---|
| Hero JPG | `image.e.lowes.com/…/c301e24e-201b-46f9-8172-27b5acdcbf99.jpg` |
| Content PNG | `image.e.lowes.com/…/ad569dc5-361d-4d7d-8bc0-1ae6ec389f05.png` |
| Open-pixel GIF | `mi.lowes.com/p/cp/1161d2b90543045e/o.gif` |
| 5× impression pixels | `sli.lowes.com/imp?s=109083{4,4,5,7,8}&…` |

The tracking pixel `alt=""` omissions are cosmetically harmless but the hero and content images without alt text fail WCAG 2.1 SC 1.1.1 and will render blank in image-blocked clients (common in corporate Outlook).

**Responsive CSS:**
- Two overlapping media queries: `@media screen and (min-width: 375px) and (max-width: 600px)` and `@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`. The 375–480px band is covered by both, with potential rule conflicts on `.container`, `.drop`, and `.photo` selectors. No confirmed rendering break, but the overlap is fragile.
- `.mobilehide { display: none; color: #fff; }` — the color rule on a hidden element is dead CSS; harmless but indicates copy-paste template debt.

**Font loading:**
- Fellix and DIN Next families are loaded from `lowescdn.com` via `@font-face`. Both `@font-face` blocks are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> … <!--<![endif]-->` which correctly suppresses them in Outlook (MSO renders fallback fonts). No issue.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|

| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) | **WARN** | Not captured; one-click unsubscribe unverifiable |

The three compliance WARNs are relay-capture gaps, not confirmed violations — Lowe's is a major ESP sender and almost certainly has these configured. However, the audit pipeline cannot assert pass status without the headers. This is a **pipeline instrumentation gap**, not a Lowe's sending failure.

**Action:** The AgentMail relay should be configured to forward raw headers (including `List-Unsubscribe`, `Authentication-Results`, `DKIM-Signature`) into the QA capture payload.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- No evidence of UTM stripping or direct-to-homepage redirects in what is visible, but confirmation requires following at least a sample of redirect chains.

### 7. Recommendations

**High priority:**
1. **Fix pipeline header capture** — Configure AgentMail relay to preserve `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and `Authentication-Results` headers. Current 50% pass rate is artificially deflated by this instrumentation gap.
2. **Add alt text to hero and content images** — `c301e24e…jpg` and `ad569dc5…png` need descriptive alt attributes for accessibility compliance and image-blocked rendering.

**Medium priority:**
3. **Resolve overlapping media queries** — Consolidate the 375–600px and 0–480px breakpoints into a single consistent ruleset to eliminate the 375–480px conflict zone.
4. **Probe a sample of redirect links** — Follow 3–5 click-tracking URLs to confirm UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) survive the redirect chain to the Lowe's landing pages.

**Low priority:**
5. **Suppress tracking pixel alt warnings** — Add `alt=""` and `role="presentation"` explicitly to all `sli.lowes.com/imp` and `mi.lowes.com` pixel images to silence accessibility scanners cleanly.
6. **Clean dead CSS** — Remove `color: #fff` from `.mobilehide` and audit for other unreachable rules.
