# Up to 45% OFF + FREE delivery on appliances 🚨
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-16**
## Executive summary

- Alright, this is basically the same email as Thursday's Labor Day appliance send — "up to 45% off" instead of "up to $1,500," free delivery, free install, free haul-away, all the same beats. I'm not mad about it, appliances are still on my radar for the kitchen phase, but "deals end tonight" on the exact same category two sends later starts to smell like every appliance sale "ends tonight" forever. The Whirlpool microwave, Bosch dishwasher, Hisense portable AC lineup at the bottom is a decent real-product touch though — that's the stuff I actually look at.
- I'd scroll past the hero pretty fast since I've seen this pitch already, but the "Appliances everyone's loving" row with actual buy-now buttons on specific SKUs would get a look, especially the Bosch dishwasher since mine's on its last legs. Wouldn't forward this to anyone — it's not special enough, and the credit card financing block at the bottom is just noise to me, I don't play the store-card game.

## What's working

- The Bosch 300 Series dishwasher and Whirlpool microwave are real SKUs with real photos and a direct "Buy now" — that's the kind of concrete, project-relevant deal I click on, not a vague "shop appliances" banner.
- Free delivery + free install + free haul-away stacked together is genuinely useful math for me — hauling away an old washer/dryer is real friction I'd pay to avoid.
- "$100 off select laundry pairs" is a specific number, not vague filler.
- Fall leaf graphics + "Labor Day appliance savings end tonight" preview at least ties it to a real calendar moment, even if it's a repeat.

## What's weak

- I already got the near-identical appliance pitch Wednesday (9/10, "$1,500 off") — this is the same offer reskinned as a percentage instead of a dollar figure, and "LAST CHANCE" banner repeated three times feels like it's trying too hard.
- The hero image is one giant blob of washer/fridge/range with no single hero product — my eye doesn't land anywhere specific, it just registers "appliances, generic."
- 12-months-financing card block eats a ton of vertical space for something I'll never use — I don't carry a Lowe's card balance.
- No mention of MyLowe's Pro or rewards points on the appliance purchase itself, just the credit card upsell — feels like a missed chance to talk to me as a repeat/loyalty shopper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick ONE hero appliance (the dishwasher, since it's already got its own buy-now card) and lead with it instead of the generic five-appliance cluster shot — give my eye something to land on.
- 2. Cut "LAST CHANCE" repeated three times in the ribbon — one clean urgency line reads more credible, not less.
- 3. Since this is the second appliance sale email inside a week, differentiate it — call out what changed (bigger % off, new SKUs) instead of just re-running the same offer shape.
- 4. Shrink or cut the credit financing block, or make it a smaller footer mention — it's not pulling its weight against the actual appliance deal.
