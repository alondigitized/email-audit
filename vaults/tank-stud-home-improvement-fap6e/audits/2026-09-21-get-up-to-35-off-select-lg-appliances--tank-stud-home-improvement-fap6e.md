## Executive summary

- Okay, now THIS is more like it after that Dan + Shay concert nonsense on 9/19. Real numbers, real appliances, real categories — refrigerators, laundry pairs, ranges, dishwashers, all broken out with their own percentage off and their own "Shop now" button. That's how you talk to a guy mid-reno. The NFL/LG tie-in with the guy in the jersey is a little goofy for an appliance ad, but I'll give it a pass because the offer underneath it is legit — up to 35% off plus $600 more for bundling.
- The MyLowe's Rewards callout for free delivery, haul-away, and a 2-year protection plan on a $2,500+ spend is exactly the kind of loyalty-tier detail that makes me feel like a known customer instead of a random subscriber. I'm not in the market for a whole new kitchen suite right now, but if I were doing a laundry room refresh this would get a click on that 30% off laundry pairs tile specifically.
- Verdict: I'd open, I'd scroll, and I might actually click "Shop now" on the fridge or laundry tile. Wouldn't forward it to a buddy unless he mentioned he's shopping appliances — this isn't a "hey check this out" email, it's a "bookmark for later" email.

## What's working

- Four clean category tiles (fridges, laundry, ranges, dishwashers) each with its own % off and its own CTA — that's project-bundle relevance done right.
- Hard numbers everywhere: 35%, 40%, 30%, $600 bundle bonus, $2,500 rewards threshold — no vague "big savings" garbage like the 9/20 Sunday Deals email.
- MyLowe's Rewards benefits (free delivery/haul-away, free install, 2-year protection plan) are spelled out, not buried.
- Clear end date on the offer (10/7/26) gives it real urgency instead of "limited time" fluff.
- LG Signature section at the bottom shows premium tier product without cluttering the main offer — nice hierarchy.

## What's weak

- The NFL/football model theme feels bolted on and doesn't connect to appliances at all — momentary "wait, is this a jersey ad?" confusion before the offer registers.
- No mention of pro/contractor pricing or Pro Rewards — actually calls out that the rewards perk *excludes* MyLowe's Pro Rewards members, which stings a little since I run both Pro and MyLowe's lists.
- No in-stock or delivery-timeline signal — if I'm mid-reno I want to know if this fridge ships this week or in six.
- Everything is LG-only; if I've already got a Samsung or Whirlpool kitchen going, this doesn't apply to me at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the football jersey guy or at least shrink him — swap that visual real estate for a second product shot or a bundle savings example, since the sports tie-in adds zero information value to an appliance deal.
- 2. Call out Pro Rewards members directly instead of excluding them silently in fine print — even a one-line "Pro members, see your separate offer here" would keep me from feeling shut out.
- 3. Add a stock/delivery indicator on at least the hero tile ("in stock, delivers this week") — that's the number one thing that decides if I click through or wait.
- 4. Move the $600 bundle bonus up into the subject line — that's a bigger, more specific number than "35% off" and would out-perform on curiosity.
