# This Black Friday morning, 150 members win BIG 💥
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This one is a sweepstakes ad, not a sale. Lowe's wants me to show up at 6 a.m. on Black Friday for a shot at a free bucket. I like free CRAFTSMAN tools and an EGO blower, so I read the whole thing. But it asks me to get up before dawn and queue for a lottery ticket. The date is also Friday, November 27. The subject said "this morning," and the email is dated 11/21, so the timing doesn't line up.
- It's a lot better looking than the appliance and décor emails from earlier this week. Still, there's no price, no project and no rebate in it. It's a fun-looking contest and nothing more. I'd skim it and maybe tell my buddy about the Craftsman drill prize. I wouldn't click "Learn more," and I wouldn't plan my weekend around it.

## What's working

- The "FREE" green-highlight treatment pops hard. I get the hook in two seconds.
- The prize tiers (Gold, Silver, Red, Green) are easy to follow. The Craftsman drill/impact set and EGO blower are things I'd actually use.
- The date and door time are clear: "Friday, November 27, doors open at 6 a.m." Also "In-store Only."
- The MyLowe's Rewards branding at the top makes it feel like they know I'm a member.
- The bucket photo is a good, simple hero.

## What's weak

- Nothing here is a project or a price. There's no deal I could plan a reno phase around.
- "First 50" and "first 150" at every store means I'd be in line at 5 a.m. against everyone else. The odds feel poor.
- The grand prize is a fridge. I don't need one, so it does nothing for me.
- The hot chocolate and holiday music tiles are filler, and the Dream Kitchen sweepstakes is another entry form.
- Three different "Learn more" buttons go nowhere specific. The fine print at the bottom is dense.
- This is the second "members win free stuff" email in about three days. Compare 11/19's "Up to 2 FREE power tools?", which at least had real tools and a real offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the prize I care about (free CRAFTSMAN tools) and put a real Black Friday deal on tools below the bucket hero, so I have a reason to go besides the lottery.
- 2. Replace the three "Learn more" buttons with one clear button such as "Check my store's door time" or "See Black Friday tool deals."
- 3. Cut the hot chocolate and music tiles and the second sweepstakes. Add a "save my spot" step, like a calendar add or an app prompt.
- 4. Say how the odds work: how many winners per store, and when to arrive.

- **Subject Alt B:** `150 members per store win a prize Nov 27`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Doors open 6 a.m. Black Friday. First 50 members get a free bucket.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Grand prize is a free appliance. Plus free Craftsman and EGO tools.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed):** `https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` has no `alt` attribute. Screen readers and image-blocked clients will get no fallback.
- **Malformed DOCTYPE (confirmed in source):** `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>` is missing the space after `PUBLIC`. Most clients tolerate it, but it is invalid, and strict parsers may fall back to quirks mode.
- **Minor:** the XHTML namespace and DTD URLs use `https://www.w3.org/...`. This is harmless, since the canonical identifiers are `http://`.
- Present and correct: viewport meta, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, `<!--[if !mso]><!-->` guards around the web fonts, and a `max-width: 480px` mobile media query.
- Web fonts (Fellix, DIN Next) load from `www.lowescdn.com`. They are wrapped in the non-MSO guard and fall back via `local()`, so no issue is confirmed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source. No unresolved merge tokens (`%%…%%`, `{{…}}`) appear in the truncated HTML.

## 5. Compliance
These QA warnings are flagged but not confirmed as real defects:
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) not found:** the QA note says the headers may not be captured by the AgentMail relay. Check the raw `.eml` before treating this as a sender-side gap. It matters because Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **No physical address detected:** the footer is past the truncation point, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the full HTML.
- **Authentication-Results not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The QA note attributes this to the relay. The From domain is `e.lowes.com`.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. No hrefs or UTM parameters are visible in the truncated source, and the 13 tracking links were not resolved, so landing page alignment could not be checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a descriptive `alt` to `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`. Use `alt=""` if it is purely decorative.
