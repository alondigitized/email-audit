# 💰TODAY & TOMORROW: Win MyLowe's Money Days prizes💰
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-11**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's sends me something called "Win MyLowe's Money Days prizes" and I crack it open because I was literally at the store yesterday for the Money Days event itself. What do I get? A contest flyer. First 150 members per day, show up between 10am and 1pm, select one of "many giveaways including FREE MyLowe's Money." No dollar amount. No product lineup. No deal. Just... come to the store and maybe win something if you're one of the lucky 150.
- Look, I'm a MyLowe's member. I've been following the Money Days campaign all week — July 9th launch email was solid, laid out the loyalty event clearly. But this feels like the event team and the email team didn't talk. This is a foot-traffic-driving promotional event notice, not a subscriber email. It belongs as an SMS push or an in-app notification, not in my inbox at what appears to be same-day notice.
- I'd mark this read and move on. I wouldn't click "Learn more" to find out what unspecified prizes might be available if I'm one of 150. I wouldn't forward it. A buddy asking me "anything good at Lowe's right now?" would get a shrug.

## What's working

- **Time-bounded urgency is real** — 7/11 and 7/12, 10am–1pm is a hard window. No ambiguity about when.
- **Sender identity is solid** — MyLowe's Rewards in my inbox, I know exactly who this is.
- **Layout is clean and renders fine** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no visual garbage. Simple and legible.
- **Loyalty-member framing is appropriate** — this is positioned as a member event, which is the right segmentation move.
- **Same-day relevance is there** — email arrived July 11, event is July 11. At least the timing is right.

## What's weak

- **No dollar value on the prize** — "FREE MyLowe's Money" with zero amount attached is noise. Is it $5? $50? $500? The word "free" doesn't do anything without a number.
- **"Learn more" CTA is dead weight** — I already read the email. What am I learning more about? Give me "See what you can win" or "Get directions to my store."
- **150 members per day is a long-shot lottery, not a reward** — I'm a real customer who drops serious money there. Don't make me race 149 other people at 10am on a Friday.
- **Zero product relevance** — no tools, no lumber, no plumbing, no project-adjacent imagery. I'm a DIY guy and this looks like a coupon mall event.
- **Preheader is confusing** — "Add your saved Lowe's for a chance to win" reads like a partially merged template or an incomplete thought. What does "add your saved Lowe's" mean to a subscriber who already has a home store on file?

## Recommendations

- 1. **Put a dollar amount on the prize.** "Win up to $500 in MyLowe's Money" would have made me stop scrolling. Without it, this is a vague sweepstakes.
- 2. **Replace "Learn more" with a store-specific CTA.** "See prizes at your Lowe's" or "Check my store's availability" ties the click to something real and local.
- 3. **Add one project-relevant visual.** Even a tool, a grill, or a bag of mulch in the background of the hero signals this prize has value to someone like me.
- 4. **Fix the preheader.** "Add your saved Lowe's" is broken copy — rewrite it to preview the stakes.
- 5. **Send this as SMS/push, not email.** A same-day in-store event with a 3-hour window is a push notification use case. Email is for pre-event awareness, not morning-of scramble.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Money Days bonus: Win up to $500 today only — 10am–1pm`
- **Subject Alt B:** `First 150 members win at Money Days — in-store today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Show up between 10am–1pm, select a giveaway. MyLowe's Money up for grabs.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Today and tomorrow only — free prizes for the first 150 members. Is yours waiting?`

## Technical Audit — MyLowe's Money Days (lowes@e.lowes.com)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound with standard table-based layout and responsive CSS, but the AgentMail relay is suppressing critical deliverability and compliance headers, and one promotional image is missing alt text. Authentication posture cannot be verified due to missing relay passthrough.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **29 tracking links skipped** by HTTP probe — all routed through `e.lowes.com` click-redirect domain. Normal for ESP-tracked sends, but means destination URL health (404s, redirect chains) is unverified for this capture.
- **Plain-text version is 70% URLs** (3,039 / 3,977 chars): the plain-text alternate is likely auto-generated from the HTML with no human-readable prose. Spam filters score high URL density in plain-text negatively. Lowe's volume likely offsets this via domain reputation, but it's a flag.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing alt text** on `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` (`https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg`). If images are blocked (default in Outlook, corporate environments), this image renders as a blank box with no context.
- **Dual `@font-face` block duplication**: both Fellix and DIN Next font stacks are loaded inside separate `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <![endif]-->` conditional blocks. This is correct for MSO exclusion, but DIN Next references two different CDN versions (`1.4.4` for regular, `1.2.0` for bold/black) — a version skew that could produce inconsistent weight rendering if the CDN paths diverge.
- **`max-width: 480px` on `body`** in the mobile media query (`@media only screen and (max-width: 480px)`) is a known pattern but can clip content on some Android clients where viewport != body width.
- No issues with XHTML 1.0 Transitional doctype — appropriate for broad email client compatibility.

### 7. Recommendations

**High priority:**
1. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe-Post` is set at SMTP level** — if the AgentMail relay is stripping this header, it needs to be configured to pass it through. Non-negotiable for Google/Yahoo bulk sender compliance.
2. **Verify physical address presence in email body** — audit the full HTML body (not just the truncated head) for a postal address block in the footer.
3. **Add alt text** to `a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg` — describe the promotional content so image-blocked renders remain actionable.

**Medium priority:**
4. **Resolve Authentication-Results passthrough** — configure AgentMail relay to preserve `Authentication-Results` so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is auditable per capture.
