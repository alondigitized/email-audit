# Make today a Sunday Deals kinda day 🎁
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-18**
## Executive summary

- Another Lowe's blast, and I've already lost count. Big blue "Sunday Deals" hero, then four offer tiles. It's clean and I can read it without zooming, but "Grab 'em while you can" tells me nothing. It's the same Lowe's template I've seen all week. After the Money Days spam on 10/6 through 10/14 and the kitchen-sink send on 10/16, this feels like filler.
- The power tools and tool storage tiles are the only parts that speak to me. Save up to $100 on power tools and 30% off a Craftsman chest is what I'd actually look at on a Sunday while the drywall job sits half done. Appliances and bathroom products are "next reno phase" stuff, same as the 10/12 and 10/13 sends. I might tap the tool tile. I wouldn't forward it, and it gets skimmed and archived.

## What's working

- The $100 off power tools tile has a clear savings number, and the red Craftsman drills with Kobalt batteries are what I'd shop for.
- The 30% off tool storage tile with the Craftsman chest is a project-friendly offer. It's easy to picture in my garage.
- The offers are big and legible. 35%, 40% and 30% are in huge type, so I get the point in two seconds.
- The "Next-day delivery" badge on appliances is a useful in-stock and speed cue.
- The nav bar (Appliances, Daily Deals, Patio, Outdoor Tools) is easy to tap, and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- The hero is just "Sunday Deals" with no offer. The best content sits below the fold.
- There's no pro or DIY voice. Nothing says project, rebate or MyLowe's perks, and I get no sign they know I'm a member.
- Seven or eight identical "Shop now" buttons make it hard to tell which one matters. They're also vague.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row feels random: a storage tote, lawn paper bags and garden soil. The product titles are cut off ("27.0 -Gallons ( 108.0 -"), and that looks sloppy. It also isn't curated for me at all.
- The offers are "up to" with fine print at the bottom. The tools tile doesn't say which tools.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the tools offer in the hero. Lead with "Save up to $100 on power tools" and move appliances and bath lower.
- 2. Segment by behavior. Someone who has browsed drywall and tools should get a tool-first layout, not the generic grid.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Craftsman chests," "Shop power tool deals."
- 4. Fix the truncated product titles, and make the "curated" row actually relevant, such as drywall tools, fasteners or project bundles.
- 5. Show a MyLowe's or Pro perk, like a rewards or credit card prompt, and note stock or pickup availability.

### 1. Technical Summary
The email is a standard table-based XHTML 1.0 Transitional template with MSO conditionals and media queries. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't capture the headers. ### 3. Rendering & Accessibility
