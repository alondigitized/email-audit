# Your Sunday scroll just got better 😃
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-02**
## Executive summary

- Alright, "Sunday scroll" — cute, but it's just another Lowe's blast dressed up in casual language. I open it and it's the same laundry pair I practically just saw in the appliance email a couple days back — washer, washer, dryer, save-a-few-hundred-bucks. Nothing wrong with the deals themselves, they're real numbers with real savings, but I've got a washer and dryer that work fine, so this isn't hitting me where I live right now.
- Then it takes a hard left into a Dyson vacuum ("Sunday self-care for your home" — okay, marketing) and then an actual ad for holiday décor. It's August 2nd. I haven't even finished my deck project and Lowe's wants to sell me string lights for a season three months out. That's the part that makes me roll my eyes and close the tab.
- Wouldn't forward this to anybody, wouldn't click past a glance at the laundry pricing. Marking it read and moving on — this is a scroll-past, not a click.

## What's working

- The three-up appliance grid is clean — price, savings badge, crossed-out original price, one-line spec, Buy Now button. That's how you sell a machine.
- Save $320 / $350 / $190 are real numbers, not "up to" fine-print garbage.
- "Ends 8/5" gives the closeout section actual urgency instead of vague "hurry" language.
- Layout's tight top to bottom — no overlapping text, no broken image slots, everything renders like it should.

## What's weak

- I just saw a washer/dryer-heavy appliance email a couple days ago (7/31, full kitchen suite). This is round two of the same category in under a week — feels like Lowe's is repeating itself instead of moving me forward in a project.
- Holiday décor pitch in the first week of August is flat-out premature. I'm not in a holiday headspace, I'm in a "finish the reno before it gets cold" headspace.
- Nothing here speaks to me as a project guy — no "you bought lumber, here's what pairs with it," no rebate callout, no pro-vs-homeowner voice at all. It's generic retail.
- Zero MyLowe's Rewards mention despite this going to a member — a financing nudge or points callout would've actually landed given my habits.
- That "Sponsored / Powered by ZETA" box is a random third-party ad slot bolted onto a Lowe's email — feels like clutter, not curation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the holiday décor slot for something project-adjacent to what I've actually bought or browsed — tools, fasteners, patio build-out — not a category I'm nowhere near shopping.
- 2. Lead with a MyLowe's Rewards angle (points multiplier, financing) since I've got the account — treat me like a known customer, not a cold list.
- 3. Cut the appliance repeat cadence or vary the SKUs — don't run washer/dryer twice in one week unless the deal materially improved.
- 4. Drop the Sponsored/Zeta ad block entirely — it dilutes trust in a retailer email.
- 5. Give the hero a real number instead of "Today's looking good for your home's wishlist" — put a price or % off above the fold.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your MyLowe's rewards + Sunday's best appliance deals`
