# Tickets to the Leagues Cup Final could be yours ⚽️
**Score:** 3/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-29**
## Executive summary

- Soccer jerseys and a sweepstakes — this one's a total swing-and-miss for me. I don't follow soccer, my kids don't play it, and "Leagues Cup Final" means nothing to a guy who's more interested in whether my Kobalt tools are on sale. It's a well-produced graphic, I'll give it that, but it's got nothing to do with a Home Depot Pro / Lowe's MyLowe's guy planning his next reno weekend.
- The "bonus entry with qualifying purchase" line is the only thing that even brushes up against my world, and it's buried under two screens of jersey photos and prize graphics. This is the kind of email I glance at for two seconds and mark as read — no click, no forward. If anything I'd forward it to my brother-in-law who's actually into soccer, not because Lowe's did anything right.
- Compare this to the storage shed email from a few days back (7/29, "Summer Storage Event") — that one had an actual product, actual savings, actual use for my garage. This one's a marketing stunt tie-in that could've come from any retailer with a loyalty program. Lowe's forgot who's actually opening their emails.

## What's working

- Clean, bold hero graphic — no render bugs, images loaded fine, text is crisp and readable
- "Enter now" and "Shop now" buttons are both big, high-contrast, easy to tap on a phone
- Prize breakdown (tickets, jersey, $500 gift card) is laid out clearly with icons — I get what I'd win at a glance
- The $500 Lowe's gift card is at least a dollar figure I recognize, even buried in soccer swag

## What's weak

- Zero connection to tools, project supplies, or anything I actually buy at Lowe's — feels like a licensing partnership, not a Lowe's email
- No product callouts, no seasonal reno tie-in, nothing for the DIY guy — this could've been sent by any big-box retailer
- "Bonus entry with any qualifying purchase" is the one real hook and it's crammed into tiny fine print under the Shop Now button
- The wall of legal text at the bottom (NO PURCHASE NECESSARY, mail-in entry addresses) screams sweepstakes filler, not a shopping email

## Recommendations

- 1. Skip this segment entirely for guys like me — if MyLowe's has purchase history data, a Pro/DIY member shouldn't get a soccer sweepstakes as the lead story. Save it for members who've shown interest in sports/lifestyle categories.
- 2. If it has to go out broad, flip the hierarchy — lead with "Shop qualifying products, get a bonus entry" and make that the CTA, not "Enter now" for a raffle I can't win anything from by shopping.
- 3. Tie the bonus-entry purchase categories to something concrete — "qualifying purchase" is vague; tell me what counts (tools? appliances? paint?) so there's a reason to shop instead of just enter for free.
- 4. Drop the promo above the fold — "Enter for a chance to score four tickets" — that's competing with the subject line and adds nothing new.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus: any qualifying purchase earns a bonus entry through 8/15`
