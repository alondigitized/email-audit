# With up to 50% OFF, who says patio season is over?
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "who says patio season is over" — I like that headline actually, because it's true, I'm not done with the backyard just because Labor Day passed. 50% off patio furniture and accessories is a real number that got my attention right out of the gate, and the hero shot with the wicker set is clean, no clutter. But man, this email turns into a junk drawer fast after that — grills, outdoor lighting, NFL 5-gallon buckets, Kobalt toolboxes, a free ice mold sponsored by LG, EGO mowers. It's like they threw every SKU with a discount tag at the wall.
- The 35% off grills section actually would've pulled me in if it led — I'm more of a "fire up the Weber" guy this time of year than a patio furniture guy, but that pizza oven photo is a nice touch, that's a real project item. The 3x points activation banner is good, I do have the MyLowe's card, so that's relevant to me specifically.
- Bottom line: I'd probably skim past the top two offers, maybe click on grills, but this isn't a forward-to-a-buddy email — it's too scattered. Mark as read, maybe come back for the grill deal later.

## What's working

- Patio furniture hero with 50% off is a clean, single strong offer up top — no ambiguity there.
- The grills and outdoor lighting split (35% off / 20% off) speaks directly to project-driven guys like me who are always mid-upgrade on something outside.
- 3x points "Activate and earn more" banner is legit personalization since I'm a MyLowe's Rewards member — that's the kind of thing that keeps me loyal.
- Specific dollar callouts ($99 fire pit, $8.98 buckets) give me real numbers to judge instead of vague "great deals" language.

## What's weak

- The email loses focus hard after the first two sections — NFL buckets, Kobalt toolboxes, a free ice mold from LG — none of that reads "patio season," it reads "let's cram in every promo we've got."
- The LG "Free Member Gift Craft Ice Mold" starting Sept 19 with a vague "limited quantities" note feels like buried fine print dressed up as a gift.
- Too many separate CTAs (I counted like 9 "Shop now" buttons) — nothing tells me which offer actually matters most.
- The EGO mower deal at the bottom is a good project-relevant offer but it's so far down I'd probably never scroll to see it.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this down to one theme per send — either "patio refresh" or "grill season," not both plus six other categories. That's the single biggest lift here.
- 2. Move the grills section higher, right under the hero — that's my actual project trigger, not the fire pit / NFL bucket combo mid-email.
- 3. Kill or clearly separate the NFL merch, Kobalt toolbox, and ice mold blocks into a different send — they dilute the patio message and feel like unrelated inventory dumping.
- 4. Give the 3x points banner more visual weight since it's genuinely relevant to repeat shoppers like me — right now it's sandwiched and easy to skip.
