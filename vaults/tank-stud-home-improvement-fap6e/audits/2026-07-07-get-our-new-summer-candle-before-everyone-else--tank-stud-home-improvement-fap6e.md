# Get our NEW summer candle before everyone else☀️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-07**
## Executive summary

- I've opened five Lowe's emails in the past four days — July 4th deals, power tool savings, $1,500 off appliances — and now my rewards sender drops a scented candle on me. "Hints of soft florals, neroli, bright citrus and vanilla." I had to re-read the from address to make sure this wasn't a mistake. It's Lowe's. It's a candle. From the same sender who had me clicking through 50% off tools on July 3rd.
- The email itself renders fine — clean layout, the "Member Early Access" framing is actually a decent loyalty hook, and the hero shot is attractive. But none of that matters when the product is a summer candle and I'm a guy who shops Lowe's for pressure-treated lumber and a new circular saw blade. This is the kind of misfire that makes me start hitting the unsubscribe link because I start wondering if the whole rewards program has lost the plot.
- Mark as read, move on. No click. Definitely not forwarding — unless it's to my wife as a joke.

## What's working

- **Member Early Access framing** — the exclusivity hook ("before everyone else") is a smart loyalty play, and I can see it right at the top of the hero. Good use of the rewards relationship.
- **Clean render, no junk** — image loaded, text is readable, "Shop now" CTA is clearly visible. No broken elements.
- **Summer seasonal relevance** — "It's sunshine in a jar!" ties to the season correctly. The creative team did their job for whoever this is aimed at.
- **Visual hierarchy lands on the product** — one candle, one headline, one button. If I were the target demographic, the path to click would be obvious.

## What's weak

- **Completely wrong persona** — I shop Lowe's for tools, hardware, and project materials. A scented candle with "neroli" notes might as well be a spa ad. MyLowe's Rewards should know from my purchase history that I have never bought a candle there.
- **No offer, no price, no deal** — after a week of "50% OFF" and "$1,500 savings," there's not a single dollar figure here. Just "Shop now." That's a gear shift I'm not following.
- **Cadence whiplash** — five July 4th deal emails, then this. The tonal shift is jarring and it erodes trust in the rewards sender name. When I see "MyLowe's Rewards" now I'm going to hesitate before opening.
- **Scent copy is completely off for me** — "soft florals, neroli, bright citrus and vanilla" tells me everything about who this was meant for, and it's not a 49-year-old guy building a deck addition.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Segment this out of the pro/DIY list entirely** — candle launches belong in a home décor segment, not to MyLowe's Rewards subscribers who buy power tools and lumber. This single send probably dinged open rates and cost them unsubscribes in the wrong cohort.
- 2. **If you must send to me, tie it to a project** — something like "summer outdoor project refresh" that bundles outdoor lighting, paint, and maybe even a patio candle as an afterthought. Give me the project angle first.
- 3. **Add a price or a deal** — "Member Early Access" means nothing without knowing if I'm getting it cheaper or just earlier. Even "members get it first at $X" would give me a reason to care.
- 4. **Throttle the cadence** — six emails in six days, and the seventh pivots to candles. That's newsletter fatigue stacked on top of a topic miss.

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard XHTML 1.0 Transitional table layout with legacy client hacks intact. Primary technical risks are three compliance gaps (missing `List-Unsubscribe` headers, no physical address in detected plain text) and a plain-text body that is almost entirely URLs, which will trigger spam filters.

- UTM parameter presence/correctness cannot be confirmed from this source alone (see §6).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Image missing alt text (confirmed by QA):**
```
src: https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/
     a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg
```
Empty or absent `alt=""` on a content image degrades screen readers and image-blocked clients (Outlook default, many corporate environments).

**Duplicate `local()` font hints** in every `@font-face` declaration:
```css
src: local('Fellix Regular'), local('Fellix Regular'), url(...)
```
The duplicate `local()` hint is harmless but indicates a template copy-paste error.

**Inconsistent CDN versions** for DIN Next font family:
- `din-next.woff` → `scaffold/1.4.4/fonts/`
- `din-next-bold.woff` → `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/`
- `din-next-black.woff` → `scaffold/1.2.0/fonts/`

DIN Next Regular is on a newer scaffold version than Bold/Black. If `1.2.0` paths are ever deprecated, Bold and Black weights will fall back to system fonts silently.

**WOFF only, no WOFF2.** Not a blocker — email client support for WOFF2 is minimal — but the CDN could serve WOFF2 with a `format('woff2')` hint for clients that support it (Apple Mail, newer Outlook).

**Overlapping media queries** with different breakpoints (`max-width: 600px` + `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0` targeting iOS Safari, and a separate `max-width: 480px` block) define some of the same class names (`.mobile-hidden`, `.drop`, `.container`) at different widths. If a 500px viewport hits the 600px query but not the 480px query, class behavior diverges. Low risk on current device landscape but worth unifying.

### 5. Compliance

| Check | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `List-Unsubscribe` header | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058 one-click) | **MISSING** | Not captured by AgentMail relay |
| Physical mailing address | **NOT DETECTED** | QA plain-text scan found no address block |
| SPF/DKIM/DMARC | **UNKNOWN** | `Authentication-Results` header absent from relay capture |

**CAN-SPAM risk:** A physical mailing address is legally required. It may exist in the HTML body below the truncation point, but the QA plain-text scan did not detect it — this warrants manual verification against the full render.

**`List-Unsubscribe` / RFC 8058:** Gmail and Apple Mail use these headers to surface the native unsubscribe button. Absence degrades deliverability reputation and increases "mark as spam" rates. These are set at the ESP (ExactTarget/Salesforce Marketing Cloud, based on `e.lowes.com`) send configuration, not in the HTML — the fix is on the ESP side.

**Authentication gap:** SPF/DKIM status is unverifiable because `Authentication-Results` was not relayed. This is a monitoring gap, not necessarily a sending gap — Lowe's ESP almost certainly signs outbound mail. Confirm via a direct inbox header inspection (`Show original` in Gmail).
