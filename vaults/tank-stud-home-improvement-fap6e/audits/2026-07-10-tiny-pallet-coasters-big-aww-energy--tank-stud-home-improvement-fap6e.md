## Technical Audit — "Tiny pallet coasters. Big aww energy ☺️" (MyLowe's Rewards)

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is structurally sound XHTML 1.0 Transitional with standard multi-client CSS resets, but carries three compliance gaps (missing physical address, absent List-Unsubscribe headers) and a deliverability risk from a URL-dense plain-text part. SPF/DKIM authentication status is unverifiable via the relay.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**QA flagged 29 tracking links skipped** — all routed through `e.lowes.com` click-redirect domain. HTTP probing was not performed, so broken-destination links cannot be ruled out.

**Plain-text part is 77% URLs** (3,039 / 3,945 chars). Spam filters (SpamAssassin `MIME_HTML_MOSTLY`) penalize plain-text parts that are predominantly redirect URLs rather than readable content. This is a deliverability risk at volume.

No issues found with the `lowescdn.com` static asset URLs visible in the source (fonts load over HTTPS).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text** on one image:
```
src: https://image.e.lowes.com/lib/fe9515707364027471/m/1/a22207c1-63c7-47c0-9e00-61227828e1fe.jpg
```
All other images need verification (source is truncated), but this one is confirmed missing `alt`.

**Inconsistent CDN versioning across `@font-face` declarations:**
- `DIN Next` (regular) references `scaffold/1.4.4`
- `DIN Next Bold` and `DIN Next Black` reference `scaffold/1.2.0`

If `1.2.0` paths are removed from the CDN, bold/black weight fallback will silently degrade to system fonts in clients that support `@font-face`.

**Overlapping mobile breakpoints:**
- One query targets `min-width: 375px AND max-width: 600px`
- Another targets `max-width: 480px`

The 375–480px range has two conflicting rule sets. In practice, the later `max-width: 480px` block wins for most properties, but specificity conflicts here could cause layout inconsistencies on mid-range Android devices.

**`@font-face` blocks correctly gated** behind `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` — Outlook rendering is not affected.
