# Last chance to enter the sweepstakes❗
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- Soccer players, a sweepstakes, and zero tools. I've got a fence project and an appliance bundle sitting in my head from the last few Lowe's sends, and now it's asking me to enter to win tickets to a soccer match I'm not watching. This is a total whiff for me — a "Final Call!" banner that's supposed to create urgency, but urgency for what, exactly? A trip I didn't ask for?
- The one thing that almost redeems it is the "bonus entry with any qualifying purchase" tie-in — that's the only line in the whole email that connects back to actually buying stuff at Lowe's. But it's buried under a wall of jersey photos I don't recognize and a prize list full of gaming codes and gift cards, not tools.
- Not clicking, not forwarding, marking as read and moving on. Compare this to the 8/10 fencing/pressure-washer send from 8/10 — that one knew exactly who I am. This one doesn't.

## What's working

- The "Enter now" CTA button is clean and easy to spot against the blue background.
- The bonus-entry-with-purchase angle at least loops back to Lowe's core business instead of being a pure giveaway.
- Prize breakdown (tickets, jersey, gift card, game code) is laid out clearly with icons — I know exactly what's on the table even if I don't want it.
- Footer has real store/account utility links (find a store, get the app, installation services) tucked in, so it's not total fluff.

## What's weak

- Soccer sweepstakes has nothing to do with home improvement — feels like a licensing tie-in that got forwarded to the wrong list.
- The "FINAL CALL!" banner text is cut off on both edges of the screen — sloppy render, looks broken before I even get to the offer.
- Six random jersey'd guys I don't recognize (not even sure who half these clubs are) — zero relevance to a 49-year-old fixing a fence.
- $500 gift card prize is buried near the bottom under game codes and jerseys instead of leading with the one prize a Lowe's shopper would actually care about.
- No project content, no rebate info, no in-stock signal, nothing pro-vs-homeowner — just a contest wrapper.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the bonus-entry-via-purchase angle and pair it with an actual product tie-in (grill, patio gear, outdoor tools) instead of soccer merch — give me a reason tied to what I already buy.
- 2. Fix the "FINAL CALL!" banner clipping — it's the first thing I see and it looks broken.
- 3. Move the $500 Lowe's gift card to the top of the prize list — that's the one prize that speaks my language, not the jersey or game code.
- 4. Swap the athlete lineup for something project-related, or at minimum shrink it — it's eating the whole hero without earning it.
- 5. Add a small "Qualifying purchases include..." line near the bonus entry CTA so it doesn't feel like disconnected fine print.
