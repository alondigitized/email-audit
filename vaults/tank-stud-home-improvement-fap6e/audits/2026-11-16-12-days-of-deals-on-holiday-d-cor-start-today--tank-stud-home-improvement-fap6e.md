# 12 Days of Deals on holiday décor start today
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- Third Lowe's Black Friday-ish email in a week, and this one is a holiday décor email wearing a Black Friday banner. I'm a DIY guy, so lit reindeer and pre-lit trees aren't what I open Lowe's for. The "12 Days of Deals" hero is clear enough, but "New deals on holiday décor drop every day" doesn't tell me one thing I'm actually getting today. No percentage and no dollar figure. The only price in the whole email is $59.98 on a tree.
- The one thing in my lane is the DeWalt 12V 2Ah battery/charger kit near the bottom, and it's buried under four stacked banners. The "Gifts under $100" tile with the Carhartt-style shop apron got a nod from me, and so did the Kobalt toolbox. But I scrolled past a lot of red and blue boxes to reach them. The toilet and storage tote are fine for a reno shopping list, but they have no price either.
- Would I click? Probably the DeWalt "Buy now," if I'm even still scrolling. Forward it? No. Mark it read. Compared to the 11/15 tools-first send I liked, this is a step backward for me.

## What's working

- The "12 Days of Deals" hero gives a clear daily-drop hook, and it's a reason to come back.
- The $59.98 pre-lit tree is the only hard price, so at least one offer is concrete.
- Gift tiers (under $25, $50, $100) are easy to scan. The Kobalt toolbox and the apron fit a weekend-warrior gift list.
- The DeWalt battery/charger "Buy now" is a straight product link in my category.
- The MyLowe's Rewards "FREE same-day delivery" strip at the top is a real member perk.

## What's weak

- Four stacked banners (Black Friday, 12 Days, trees, free gifts) fight each other, and I can't tell which one is the hero.
- "FREE gifts with select purchases" has no purchase threshold and no gift named. That reads like fine print waiting to bite.
- The "Curated with you in mind" row has no prices, and the DeWalt title is cut off mid-text ("3 Ah 5 Ah ) - DCB135C"). It doesn't look curated for me.
- Nothing speaks to pro vs. homeowner, rebates, or Pro/credit-card perks. I'm a known MyLowe's member and got treated like a stranger.
- The "Shop now" buttons are generic and repeated four times.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one clear offer and a price or % off. Then put a tools or project row directly under the hero, since that's what my history says I click on.
- 2. Cut the banners from four to two, and name the free gift and its spend threshold.
- 3. Show prices on the product row, fix the truncated titles, and add a member-price flag for MyLowe's.
- 4. Use specific CTAs like "Get the DeWalt kit" in place of "Shop now."

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tools + décor deals drop daily till Black Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New deal every day. Today: DeWalt 12V kit and trees from $59.98.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `MyLowe's members get free same-day delivery on today's drops.`

- **Malformed DOCTYPE:** The source reads `<!DOCTYPE html PUBLIC"-//W3C//DTD XHTML 1.0 Transitional//EN" ...>`, with no whitespace between `PUBLIC` and the quoted public identifier. Some parsers may treat this as invalid and fall back to quirks mode.

Note also that the XHTML namespace is declared over `https://`. The canonical value is `http://www.w3.org/1999/xhtml`, though this is harmless in practice.
- **No issues found with:** The charset meta tag, the viewport meta tag, `x-apple-disable-message-reformatting`, the `[if !mso]` wrapping of the `@font-face` blocks, and the Hotmail, Yahoo and `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` fixes.
