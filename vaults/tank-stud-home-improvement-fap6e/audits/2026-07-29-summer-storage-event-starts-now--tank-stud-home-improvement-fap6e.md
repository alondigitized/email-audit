## What's weak

- It's a wall of CTAs — six or seven "Shop now" buttons stacked top to bottom with almost no breathing room, so nothing after the shed really stands out as THE offer.
- "Closeout Event / Hurry! Save more before the season ends" has zero specifics — no percentage, no category, just vague urgency after Lowe's already burned that same "closing out" language on me twice this week (7/25 Closeout Event, 7/25 Money Days).

- 2. Replace "Closeout Event / Hurry! Save more before the season ends" with an actual number or category — if it's real savings, say what on.
