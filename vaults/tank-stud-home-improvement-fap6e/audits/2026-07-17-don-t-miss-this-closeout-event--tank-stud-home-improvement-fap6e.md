# Don't miss this Closeout Event 👉
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-17**
## Executive summary

- Lowe's has been hitting me almost every day this week — Money Days finale, a PAW Patrol invite I still can't explain, KOBALT brand love, and a personalization tease that showed up yesterday. Today I open "Closeout Event" expecting maybe a tool clearance run. The hero loads and I'm staring at a gleaming GE refrigerator. Cool. Great. I just had a 40-amp subpanel installed in my garage and my August project is a deck rebuild. I have zero refrigerator budget anywhere on my list.
- The email redeems itself below the fold — there IS an outdoor power + tools section with 40% off and "Save up to $100" callouts on two products that look like a drill and some kind of electric outdoor tool. That section is what I came for. But it's buried under the appliance push, and on mobile I've got to scroll past a whole category I don't care about to get there.
- I scroll to the tools section, squint at the two products, and probably don't click because the CTA just says "Shop now" with no product name, no model number, no "only 8 left" signal. I mark it read. I don't forward it. If anything, I'm a little annoyed that yesterday's "picks from your go-to brand" teased personalization and today they're back to batch-and-blast.

## What's working

- The outdoor power + tools section has real teeth — "40% off" plus explicit dollar savings ($100 callouts) is the right price signal for me
- Email renders cleanly — sharp images, no broken layout, no lorem ipsum leaking anywhere
- Two-section structure is at least transparent about the scope (appliances AND tools), so I'm not deceived
- "Before the deals disappear" is low-key urgency that doesn't feel like screaming — acceptable for a Closeout Event framing
- The 35%/40% figures are specific enough to feel credible, not the vague "savings event" language I see on junk mail

## What's weak

- Appliance hero is a hard demographic miss — stainless steel fridge/laundry pair is a spouse-driven, big-box research purchase, not a weekend warrior grab
- "Closeout Event" in the subject gives me nothing to triage on — no category, no %, no product name, no "why is today different from yesterday"
- Tools are below the fold — on mobile I might bounce before I hit the one section that's actually relevant to me
- The mid-email "Sign up for Deals of the Day" block is tone-deaf; Lowe's is already in my inbox five times a week and they're asking me to subscribe to *more* emails
- No MyLowe's loyalty thread — after running Money Days last Tuesday, dropping the member pricing angle completely is a backward step

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero section.** Put outdoor power + tools first for any MyLowe's subscriber with project or tool purchase history. Route appliance-buyer segments to a different send entirely — this isn't a one-size batch.
- 2. **Name a product in the subject.** "Closeout: up to 40% off cordless tools this weekend" gives me a reason to open before I even see the preview.
- 3. **Amplify the dollar savings.** "Save up to $100" is good — surface that number in the tools hero copy where the eye goes, not only on the small product badges.
- 4. **Tie in MyLowe's rewards.** Tell me if my member balance stacks with closeout pricing. That's a one-liner that turns a passive scroll into an urgent decision.
- 5. **Kill the Deals of the Day signup block.** Replace it with a third tool product callout or a "ends Sunday" timestamp. The wasted module hurts credibility.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Tools & outdoor gear on closeout — before they're gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save up to $100 on drills, blowers, and more — while supplies last.`

## Technical Audit — Lowe's "Closeout Event" Email

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard retail HTML email using table-based layout with custom web fonts and multi-breakpoint media queries. No critical rendering failures detected, but several accessibility gaps and relay-layer compliance blind spots require attention.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**21 tracking links skipped by QA probe** — all click-redirect URLs route through `lowes.com` ESP infrastructure (likely `click.e.lowes.com` or similar). This is expected behavior for an ESP-managed send, but it means no dead-link validation was performed on destination URLs. If any redirects point to expired sale pages post-send, there is no automated catch.

No issues with the base link structure otherwise.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing alt text — 7 images flagged:**

| Image | Type | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `c301e24e-...jpg` | Content image | No `alt` attribute |
| `ad569dc5-...png` | Content image | No `alt` attribute |
| 5× `sli.lowes.com/imp?...` | Pixel trackers | No `alt=""` (empty string) |

The content images (`image.e.lowes.com`) require descriptive alt text for screen reader users and for plain-text/images-blocked rendering environments. The tracking pixels should carry `alt=""` (explicitly empty) rather than being omitted entirely — absence vs. empty string behaves differently across assistive technology.

**Font loading:** Seven `@font-face` declarations load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. WOFF2 offers ~30% smaller payloads; supported by all modern email clients that support web fonts at all. Not blocking, but wasteful on mobile.

**MSO conditional fencing:** Custom fonts and some styles are wrapped in `<!--[if !mso]><!--> ... <!--<![endif]-->` — correct. Outlook will fall back to system fonts as intended.

**Media queries:** Two breakpoints defined (`min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`). The 375–600px range uses `-webkit-min-device-pixel-ratio:0` to limit to WebKit — this is an intentional pattern but excludes Android non-WebKit clients in that range. Acceptable for Lowe's likely audience, but worth noting.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Tracking pixel URLs contain live persona identifiers:**

```
https://sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090838&e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app&p=7172026&lctg=410146703
```

The `e=` parameter encodes the recipient email address (`cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app`) in plaintext in the pixel URL. This is a Lowe's ESP behavior, not a merge token failure — the token resolved correctly. Flagging for awareness: recipient PII is embedded in image request URLs logged by `sli.lowes.com`.

No unresolved `{{merge_tag}}` or `[FIRST_NAME]`-style placeholders detected in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**List-Unsubscribe header: NOT CONFIRMED**
- QA relay did not capture a `List-Unsubscribe` header.
- This is likely a relay capture gap (AgentMail not forwarding all headers), but cannot be ruled out as absent. CAN-SPAM requires a functional unsubscribe mechanism; RFC 2369 `List-Unsubscribe` and RFC 8058 `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (one-click) are additionally required by Gmail and Yahoo for bulk senders as of 2024.
- **Action required:** Verify headers are present on raw SMTP delivery, not just the relayed copy.

**Authentication-Results: NOT CONFIRMED**
- SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status unknown — relay did not surface `Authentication-Results`. Sending domain `e.lowes.com` is an ESP subdomain and should have SPF/DKIM aligned. Cannot confirm without raw headers.
- **Action required:** Pull raw headers from a direct inbox delivery to confirm DKIM alignment on `e.lowes.com`.
