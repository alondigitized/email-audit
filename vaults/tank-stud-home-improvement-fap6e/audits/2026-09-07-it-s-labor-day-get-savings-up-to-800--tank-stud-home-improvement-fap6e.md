# It’s Labor Day, get savings up to $800 😎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-07**
## Executive summary

- Okay, "up to $800" grabbed my attention scrolling in the inbox, and this one actually delivers on the promise better than that tote-bag stunt from earlier in the week. Labor Day urgency, "HUGE savings end today," a big blue hero — this reads like a legit last-day-of-sale push, and the tailgating/MyLowe's Rewards points block with the guy grilling under a canopy is a demographic bullseye for me.
- But then it wanders. "Curated with you in mind" gives me a storage tote, a can of Bar Keeper's Friend, and a bag of garden soil — that's not a project, that's whatever was left in the warehouse. And nowhere on this whole email does it actually tell me what's included in the $800 headline number. It's the same trick as the Labor Day email from 9/3 — strong top, mushy middle.

## What's working

- "Save up to $800 with Labor Day deals!" is a real, specific number and it's the first thing my eye hits after the logo.
- "HUGE savings end today" creates legit same-day urgency — not manufactured FOMO, since Labor Day was yesterday-ish and today really is the tail end.
- The tailgating photo with a family under a pop-up canopy and a grill is exactly my life in September — that's a demographic and seasonal hit.
- MyLowe's Rewards 3x points and the 12/18-month financing offer both talk directly to me as a member, not a stranger.
- Clean button hierarchy — "Shop now," "Activate now," "Learn more" are all easy to spot scanning fast.

## What's weak

- The $800 headline never gets explained — no product category, no "on appliances" or "on outdoor power equipment" tag anywhere near it, so I have no idea what's actually on sale.
- "Curated with you in mind" is a storage bin, a cleaning powder, and garden soil — that's not curated, that's a random SKU dump, and it undercuts the personalization claim right in the copy.
- No project bundle here — no "here's your fall deck/patio/mower kit," which is the thing that actually gets me to add three items to a cart at once.
- Deals of the Day teaser at the bottom is just an ad for another email signup, dead weight this late in the message.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real category under the $800 hero — "up to $800 off appliances" or "up to $800 off outdoor power equipment" — a vague dollar amount with no anchor reads like bait.
- 2. Swap the "Curated with you in mind" three-pack for an actual fall project bundle (mower + trimmer + mulch, or patio + grill + cover) — that's what turns browsing into a cart.
- 3. Move the tailgating/rewards block up near the hero — it's the strongest demographic hit in the email and it's buried under the fold.
- 4. Cut or relocate the "Deals of the Day" signup pitch — it's competing with the actual sale for attention on the last day of the promo.
