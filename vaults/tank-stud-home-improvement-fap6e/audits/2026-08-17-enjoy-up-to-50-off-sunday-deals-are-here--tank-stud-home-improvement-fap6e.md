# Enjoy up to 50% OFF! Sunday Deals are here 😍
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-17**
## Executive summary

- Okay, now THIS is more like it — appliances, power tools, grills, outdoor power equipment. This is a Lowe's email that actually looks like it knows I own a house instead of chasing me around with a tote bag. The 35% off appliances tile up top with that fridge shot, the 40V mower, the power washer — that's my Sunday morning right there.
- That said, it's a lot. Eight different category tiles stacked one after another is more like a flyer than an email, and by the time I hit "Curated with you in mind" with a storage bin and a cardboard box, my eyes have glazed a little. Still, compared to the sweepstakes and tote-bag nonsense I've been getting all week, this one I'd actually scroll through on my phone over coffee. I'd click into the power tools tile, maybe the appliances one too. Not forwarding it to anyone, but I'm not archiving it unread either.

## What's working

- Appliances and patio furniture up front with real percentages (35% off, 50% off) — that's a scannable hook.
- The mower, power washer, grill, and outdoor power equipment tiles hit my actual project categories dead on.
- "Beat the heat" cooling section ties a AC/portable unit offer to free same-day delivery — timely for August.
- MyLowe's Rewards branding shows up on the cooling banner, so it at least nods to loyalty members like me.
- Clear "Shop now" buttons on every tile — no ambiguity about what to tap.

## What's weak

- Way too many stacked category tiles before I get to anything personalized — feels like a print circular dumped into an email.
- "Curated with you in mind" delivers a storage tote, a moving box, and a microwave — none of that reads as curated to my actual browsing (appliances/power tools), it reads generic.
- The credit card financing block ("12 Months Special Financing") is buried way down after I've already scrolled past a dozen offers — should've been higher if they want me to use the Lowe's card.
- No specific dollar amount or product name in the subject/hero, just "up to 50% off" — after five sends this week with similar copy, it's starting to blur together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the tile count roughly in half — lead with appliances, power tools, and outdoor power equipment (my clear categories from prior sends), and drop patio/décor/grills into a secondary "more deals" strip instead of full-size tiles.
- 2. Move "Curated with you in mind" to actually reflect my behavior — if I've been eyeing a fridge and fence project per past emails, show me fridges and outdoor hardware, not a tote and a microwave.
- 3. Pull the 12-month financing offer up near the top — that's a real motivator for a big appliance purchase and it's currently buried at the bottom.
- 4. Tighten the subject to reference the actual hero category instead of generic "Sunday Deals."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fridges, mowers, power tools — all marked down for Sunday`
