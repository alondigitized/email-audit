## Executive summary

- Lowe's sent me a concert ticket promo. I sell hardware, screws, and lumber for a living — as a customer, I want lawn mower deals and appliance rebates, not a country concert ticket giveaway. This one's a total swing-and-miss for who I am and what I open Lowe's emails to find.
- That said, I gotta admit the design itself is clean — no broken images, no lorem ipsum, clear hero banner. ## What's working

- Layout's clean and renders fine on mobile — no glitches, images loaded properly.
- "2-for-1 lawn tickets" offer is at least stated clearly with real dates and cities, not vague fine print.
- The "We Help. You Save." footer nav (find a store, get the app, installation services) is at least on-brand for Lowe's, even if buried at the bottom.

## What's weak

- Concert tickets have nothing to do with why I'm subscribed to MyLowe's — I want project deals, not Thomas Rhett.
- No project-relevant offer anywhere in this email — no tools, no appliances, no lawn equipment for sale, no rebate.
- Zero seasonal relevance for a guy planning Labor Day weekend projects — this doesn't even acknowledge it's project season.
- "Member perks" headline oversells it — turns out the "perk" is a country music ticket discount, not anything I'd actually use my MyLowe's Pro account for.
- Feels like Lowe's is diversifying into lifestyle marketing instead of staying in my lane as a hardware/home improvement guy.

## Recommendations

- 1. Ditch the concert angle entirely for a persona like mine — replace with an actual member perk: appliance rebate, credit card bonus points, or a Pro-tier discount tied to fall projects.
- 2. If Lowe's insists on running concert promos, segment it away from Pro/contractor-list subscribers — I never asked for entertainment perks.
- 3. Lead with something tangible I can use this weekend: a specific tool, a specific % off, tied to a project category (deck, lawn, garage).
- 4. Add a "Why am I seeing this" or preference center link so I can opt out of non-hardware content without unsubscribing entirely.
