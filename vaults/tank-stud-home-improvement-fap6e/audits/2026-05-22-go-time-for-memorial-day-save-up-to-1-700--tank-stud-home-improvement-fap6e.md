# GO time for Memorial Day ✨ Save up to $1,700
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-22**
## Executive summary

- Okay, the subject did its job — "save up to $1,700" got me to tap. I'm thinking maybe a tool bundle, a compressor, maybe a mower system for the long weekend. What do I get? A washer. A second appliance. An outdoor furniture set. I'm standing in my driveway with a half-finished deck and Lowe's is asking me if I want a front-load dryer. I'm not mad, I'm just tired.
- To be fair, there's a "Triple up — 3x on your next Lowe's run" loyalty section, and there's something labeled "A sizzling find" lower down that looks grill-adjacent. Those are the only two things in this whole email I even paused on. The 3x points banner at least acknowledges I shop there regularly. That's more than the last three sends gave me.
- This is email number five or six in the last five days from this address. I've been through MrBeast Kids Club, Extra Big Deals, the actual decent Memorial Day tools email from two days ago with the CRAFTSMAN V20 at $99 — that one I'd have clicked. This one I'm marking read without clicking. Won't forward it.

## What's working

- **Memorial Day hero is clean and on-time** — "Long weekend loading... score these deals quick." reads like someone who actually knows what a long weekend feels like. Good casual energy.
- **$1,700 number in subject earned the open** — concrete dollar figure got my thumb to stop. That's doing its job even if the content doesn't deliver on the implied promise.
- **Triple up / 3x loyalty section is the right idea** — acknowledging I'm a repeat customer with a rewards multiplier is exactly how you talk to someone who's been in the store six times this quarter.
- **"A sizzling find" grill section** — grill is legitimate Memorial Day territory for me. Small win buried way down-screen.
- **No broken images or layout chaos** — renders clean, tap targets look sane, nothing overlapping.

## What's weak

- **Hero product is a washer — not a weekend warrior buy.** I don't plan appliance purchases on a Thursday before a long weekend. That's a spouse-discussion purchase, not a tap-and-ship purchase.
- **$1,700 in the subject implies tools/bundles; content delivers appliances.** That's a bait-and-switch in tone even if it's technically accurate. It left me feeling misled.
- **No tool, no lumber, no power equipment above the fold.** As a guy who checks Lowe's weekly for project supplies, this email might as well have been sent to my mom.
- **Fifth email in the last five days.** The cadence is burning goodwill. The one from May 19 with actual tool prices was the one I'd have bookmarked — now I'm numb to the whole stream.
- **No project context or bundle logic.** Even "deck season tools" or "pressure wash before the cookout" would have given me a hook. This reads like SKU-clearance dressed in a holiday banner.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Swap the hero product for a tool or outdoor power bundle.** The Memorial Day weekend is when I'm renting a concrete saw or buying a new cordless kit, not a washing machine. Put the CRAFTSMAN or EGO or even a pressure washer in that first slot.
- 2. **Make the $1,700 savings refer to something I can actually buy on impulse** — a tool combo, a patio bundle, a generator. If the big number is tied to an appliance, say so upfront so I don't feel tricked.
- 3. **Lead with the grill/outdoor section higher in the email** — that content belongs in the first scroll, not buried beneath appliance tiles.
- 4. **Give the loyalty multiplier more context** — "3x points this weekend on tools, outdoor, and more" tells me exactly how to use it. "Triple up on your next Lowe's run" is vague.
- 5. **Slow the cadence or segment the list.** A tool buyer and an appliance buyer are not the same person. I'd welcome two good emails a week over six mediocre ones.

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Alt text missing on 8 images:**

| Image | URL |
|---|---|
| Hero image | `image.e.lowes.com/.../c301e24e-...jpg` |
| Content image | `image.e.lowes.com/.../ad569dc5-...png` |
| Open pixel | `mi.lowes.com/.../o.gif` |
| 5× impression pixels | `sli.lowes.com/imp?s=1090834/35/37/38` |

The hero and content images are the actionable ones — screen readers will announce the filename or skip them entirely. The tracking pixels (`o.gif`, `imp`) conventionally use `alt=""` (empty string) to silence screen readers; the current state of no `alt` attribute at all is technically invalid HTML.

**Font loading:** Four `@font-face` declarations (Fellix family) load `.woff` only — no `.woff2` fallback. This is a minor optimization gap, not a breakage. Fallback stack not visible in the truncated source; verify a system font fallback exists.

**Responsive breakpoints:** Two overlapping media queries — `min-width: 375px and max-width: 600px` and `max-width: 480px`. The 375–480px range matches both queries simultaneously. This is a known pattern in older ESP templates but can produce specificity conflicts; no confirmed breakage without full render test.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No unresolved merge tokens (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, `%FNAME%`) visible in the HTML source. No issues found.

### 5. Compliance

**`List-Unsubscribe` header not captured** — QA relay did not surface this header. Lowe's is a high-volume sender and almost certainly includes it at the MTA level; this is likely a relay capture gap, not a true absence. However, it cannot be confirmed from this audit.

**`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** — One-click unsubscribe required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules (>5k/day) since February 2024. If this header is genuinely absent at the MTA, Lowe's is out of compliance with Google and Yahoo postmaster requirements. Again, likely a relay gap — but must be verified against raw SMTP headers.

**`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF, DKIM, and DMARC pass/fail status unknown from this capture. Sending domain `e.lowes.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain; authentication is expected to be in place but unverifiable here.

**CAN-SPAM:** Physical address and unsubscribe mechanism are presumed present (standard Lowe's footer); HTML was truncated before footer — cannot confirm from source alone.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All 20 click links pass through the Lowe's click-redirect layer before destination — UTM parameter presence on final landing URLs cannot be verified without resolving the redirects. The redirect chain itself is expected behavior for ESP click tracking.

**Recipient PII in pixel URLs:** `e=cole-drywall-lowes-fap6e@etell.app` appears in plaintext in 5 impression pixel query strings. This is standard ESP behavior for per-subscriber render tracking, but the etell.app persona email is now in Lowe's pixel server logs and any intermediate proxy logs. No action needed operationally, but worth noting for persona exposure surface.
