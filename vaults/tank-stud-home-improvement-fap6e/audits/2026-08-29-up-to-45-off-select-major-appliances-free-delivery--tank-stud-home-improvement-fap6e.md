# Up to 45% OFF select major appliances + FREE delivery
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-29**
## Executive summary

- Third appliance/kitchen email from Lowe's in a week — I'm noticing the pattern (35% off Frigidaire/Electrolux on 8/26, now this "all-in" 45% off stack), and honestly the escalation makes sense for Labor Day, but it's starting to feel like I'm getting hit from every angle. That said, this one earns its spot: real stainless fridge/range/dishwasher/microwave suite shot, a legit "spend $999, get free delivery + install + haul-away + 2-yr protection plan" stack that's exactly the kind of bundle math I actually do in my head before a kitchen refresh.
- The scroll-down grab bag — vanities, bathroom fixtures, blinds install service, a Project Source tote, Bar Keeper's Friend, a Samsung Bespoke fridge ad — is where it loses me a little. It's not irrelevant exactly (I do have a bathroom vanity on the list for next spring), but it dilutes the appliance hero message into a "buy everything" circular instead of staying locked on the one offer that got me to open it.
- I'd click the "Shop now" under the 45% off appliance stack — that's a real project trigger for me. I wouldn't forward it, this is a "for me" purchase not a "hey check this out" share, and I wouldn't read past the fold on my phone; the bottom two-thirds is filler.

## What's working

- The "ALL-IN Appliance Offer" block with 45% off + free delivery + free install/haul-away + 2-yr protection plan stacked together — that's the actual math I do before buying a fridge, not just a headline percentage.
- Real product photography — actual stainless kitchen suite, actual matte black bathroom faucet, actual guy installing blinds. No stock lifestyle nonsense.
- The $999 spend threshold is stated plainly right under the offer, not buried in fine print I have to hunt for.
- "Save up to an additional $100 Select laundry pairs" is a nice secondary hook for someone like me who's got a laundry room reno queued up.

## What's weak

- Too much stacked into one email — appliances, vanities, bath fixtures, blinds install, random tote/cleaner/blinds picks, then a Samsung sponsored unit. By the fourth "Shop now" button I've stopped reading.
- The Samsung Bespoke fridge ad at $2,499 with a tiny "Sponsored" tag feels like it's competing with Lowe's own 45% off appliance offer directly above it — confusing which deal I'm supposed to act on.
- "Curated with you in mind" is a stretch — a storage tote, all-purpose cleaner, and cordless blinds have zero connection to each other or to the appliance hero. That's not curation, that's inventory clearance.
- No Lowe's rewards/credit card financing prompt anywhere — for a purchase over $999 with an install date attached, that's exactly where a 5% off MyLowe's card mention would've landed.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the appliance offer + laundry pairs block. Everything below (vanities, blinds, tote/cleaner grab bag, Samsung sponsored unit) should be its own send — it's diluting a genuinely strong hero offer.
- 2. Add a MyLowe's Card / financing line right under the $999 threshold — "or 0% APR for 12 months" next to a purchase this size is a real decision lever for me.
- 3. Pull the Samsung sponsored unit out entirely or move it to the very bottom, clearly separated — right now it visually competes with Lowe's own discount.
- 4. Tighten the subject/preheader to match what's actually above the fold.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Free delivery + install on appliances, up to 45% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Kitchen suite deal: 45% off + free install by fall`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $999, get free delivery, haul-away & a 2-yr protection plan`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack the savings before Labor Day — appliances + laundry pairs`
