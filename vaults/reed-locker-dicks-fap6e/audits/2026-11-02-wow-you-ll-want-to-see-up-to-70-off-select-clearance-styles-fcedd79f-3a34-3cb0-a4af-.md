# Wow! You'll want to see up to 70% OFF select clearance styles 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- The giant red argyle hero with a gold "UP TO 70% OFF" is the one thing the eye lands on, and a single "Shop Now" button sits right under it. The hero does its job. The problem is everything after it. A second hero repeats the 50% Holiday Deal Dash banner, followed by four category blocks at "Up to 40%" or "Up to 50%", a fitness block, a gift block and a product grid. A "Clearance" email turns into a general sale catalog.
- This is the sixth DICK'S send I've audited in about 48 hours. The "Up to 70% off select clearance is YOURS" send on 11/01 had the same hero and the same offer. Today's version adds the 50% Deal Dash banner, which was the whole subject of the "Holiday Deal Dash ends tomorrow" email. A subscriber would see a recycled clearance hero stacked on a recycled Deal Dash hero. The email is competent and on-season, but it isn't distinctive and it fights its own subject line.
- Verdict: strong hero, diluted body, fatigued cadence. It's a solid mid-tier send, not a standout.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is huge and high-contrast, and the offer reads in under a second.
- The red "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero, with a "Limited Time" label above the headline.
- The pill-shaped "Explore more deals" row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives a quick way to navigate by category.
- The Men's, Women's and Kids' blocks use real lifestyle photography with holiday styling, so the email feels seasonal.
- The "On Their List. In Your Budget." gift block, with price-tier pills ($25, $50, $100), is a useful holiday shopping aid.

## What's weak

- The subject promises "70% off select clearance", but most of the email shows 40% and 50% offers, so the clearance message gets buried.
- The Deal Dash 50% banner directly under the hero competes with it and splits the primary offer.
- The Men's, Women's and Kids' blocks all say "Up to 40% Off" with identical Shop Now buttons, so there is no hierarchy and no hero product.
- The hero's fine print is dense, and the Deal Dash block carries more fine print, so the offer feels hedged ("select", "limited time", "while quantities last").
- At about 6,600px tall the email is very long. Hero-level CTAs repeat roughly eight times, and the gift grid and footer sit far below the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the email clearance-only. Move the 70% hero and 3-4 actual clearance products up top, and cut the Deal Dash banner or send it separately. This would lift the email more than any other change.
- 2. Show real products with prices in the hero area, such as "$120 → $49", instead of a type-only graphic. The email has no concrete product price near the top.
- 3. Collapse the Men's, Women's and Kids' blocks into one row of three tiles, or drop one. That would cut the length by a third.
- 4. Differentiate this send from the 11/01 clearance send by adding a deadline ("ends Sunday") or a member-only angle.
- 5. Fix the sub-headline. "Up to 70% Off Select Clearance Styles" repeats the subject and the hero, so say something new, such as "Nike, Adidas & more from $19.99".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Sneakers & hoodies from $19.99 👟`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, Under Armour & more, plus free same-day pickup on orders $50+.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes go fast. Shop men's, women's & kids' clearance before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang`, `<title>`, `role="presentation"` tables, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). It has one confirmed deliverability defect, a missing plain-text part, plus widespread missing alt text and an unfilled preheader slot. The HTML was truncated, so the link, unsubscribe, footer and UTM checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 59 skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized).
- Visible anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which points to a redirect or deep-link wrapper. The `href` values fall outside the truncated source, so I can't verify destinations or redirect behavior.
- The open pixel (`data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…&configId=512ffb51-…`) is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt` and no `style` or `border` attributes. It's functional, but see section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 12 images (QA WARN).**
  - **Content images:** `mens-2026-1102-d`, `womens-2026-1102-d`, `kids-2026-1102-d`, `fan-shop-2026-1102-d`, `fitness-2026-1102-d` and `gift-guide-2026-1101-d` all lack `alt`.
    - These are the primary category tiles, and likely the links.
    - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the links have no accessible name and may be announced by filename or URL.
  - **Icons:** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1` lack `alt` (they need a label, or `alt=""` if they're decorative).
  - **Tracking pixel:** it needs `alt=""` to avoid being announced.
- **Preheader slot appears unfilled.** `<td id="ssl">` contains only the comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and a `#pht-spacer` div filled with `&zwnj;&nbsp;` repeats. No preheader text is visible before the filler, so clients will pull the first body text instead. The template placeholder comment also shipped to production.
- **Plain-text part:** see section 5.
- No other rendering defects are confirmed. The `@font-face` fallback to Arial and the Outlook MSO conditionals look correct.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The tracking pixel carries a populated `tuid`.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** The multipart/alternative `text/plain` part is effectively empty. This is a confirmed defect. It hurts spam-filter scoring, text-only clients and accessibility tooling.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note attributes this to the AgentMail relay possibly stripping headers. I can't confirm either way, so treat it as unverified and not as a sender defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe, so check the raw message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found.** This is expected via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with a properly configured setup, but I can't confirm it.
- The footer unsubscribe link and physical address fall outside the truncated source, so I can't verify the in-body CAN-SPAM elements.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The `href` values and UTM parameters are not in the truncated source, and no link-probe failures were reported. The only visible tracking parameters are on the open pixel, not the click links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real `text/plain` alternative with the main offer, category links and unsubscribe link.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six category tiles (e.g. "Shop Men's clearance"). Add `alt=""` to the decorative icons and to the open pixel.
3. Populate the `#ssl` / `#pht-spacer` preheader with actual text and remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment from the production template.
4. Check the raw message headers (outside the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC results. Fix only if they're missing there.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to verify `href` targets, UTM consistency and the footer compliance elements.
