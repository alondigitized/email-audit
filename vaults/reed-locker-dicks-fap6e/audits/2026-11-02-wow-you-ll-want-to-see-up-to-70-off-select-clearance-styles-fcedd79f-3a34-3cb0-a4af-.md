# Wow! You'll want to see up to 70% OFF select clearance styles 👉
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-02**
## Executive summary

- This is a "clearance" email that works more like a full-catalog sale. The hero is a red argyle block with a giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF", then a red Shop Now button. The eye lands on the offer first, as it should. But the email then stacks a second 50% banner, four "up to 40%/50% off" category blocks, a fitness block and a gift block. The promise in the subject, "up to 70% off select clearance," is only one of about eight messages.
- The send is also crowded by its own history. This is at least the fifth clearance-flavored DICK'S email in a few days. It follows "Up to 70% off select clearance is YOURS" on 11/01 and the "Extra 25% off clearance" sends on 10/31. The 11/02 "2. Days. Only! Holiday Deal Dash" email used the same 50% hero. Here the Deal Dash banner is pasted in under the clearance hero, so a subscriber sees a recycled creative.
- Judgement: competent and easy to scan, but it's a generic catalog dump with no clear single reason to click. The offer is loud and the render is clean, but nothing here is specific to the reader.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and unambiguous: gold "UP TO 70% OFF" on red, with one red Shop Now button directly below.
- The render is clean. I see no broken images, overlaps or gaps across the whole length.
- The pill row ("Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale") lets a reader jump to their category.
- The "Two Days Only" label on the Deal Dash block gives credible, time-bounded urgency, and the fine print states the end time.
- Button styling and the product photography are consistent. The holiday set dressing (greenery, ornaments) matches the season.

## What's weak

- The email has too many competing offers: 70%, 50%, 40%, 50%, 50% and gift tiers. No single call to action wins.
- The 70% hero is not tied to any product. It shows no clearance item, price or style, so the offer is abstract.
- The 50% Deal Dash banner is a second hero that dilutes the clearance message, and it repeats the 11/02 creative.
- Every Shop Now button is identical in copy. None names a category, so "Shop Now" is a generic verb that tells a reader nothing.
- Most of the fine print under the hero is cramped and tiny. It also says the offer is "online only for a limited time," which weakens the urgency.
- The email is very long. The product gift picks sit far below the fold and are unlikely to be seen.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to one offer. Keep the 70% clearance hero and replace the second banner with a 4-6 product grid of actual clearance items showing sale prices and "was" prices. Delete the Deal Dash block.
- 2. Replace the "Shop Now" buttons with specific copy such as "Shop Clearance Shoes" or "Shop Men's Clearance".
- 3. Give the urgency a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday", instead of "Limited Time".
- 4. Drop the gift and fitness sections from clearance sends, or move them to a separate holiday send. This would reduce the cadence fatigue.
- 5. Enlarge the fine print and shorten it, or link to the details.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: up to 70% off clearance ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Top brands, deep markdowns. Shop clearance shoes and apparel before sizes sell out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus free same-day delivery on orders over $50. Sizes are going fast.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (doctype, `lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, role="presentation" layout tables, MSO conditionals). The automated QA run flagged a missing plain-text part (9 chars) and missing alt attributes on 12 images. The header and authentication warnings can't be confirmed from this capture.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 71 links were probed, and the QA output reports no failures among them. The other 59 (footer, utility and social links) were skipped, so their status is unverified.
- The visible anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, and the open pixel is on `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…`. Both indicate ESP-managed click and open tracking. The truncated source doesn't show final `href` values, so I can't check redirect behavior.
- The open pixel is `width="1" height="1"` with no alt. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA, 12 images):**
  - Category tiles: `mens-2026-1102-d`, `womens-2026-1102-d`, `kids-2026-1102-d`, `fan-shop-2026-1102-d`, `fitness-2026-1102-d` and `gift-guide-2026-1101-d`, all on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/…`.
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Tracking pixel: `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open`.
- The category tiles are likely the click targets, so with images blocked or a screen reader they have no accessible name. The pixel and icons should carry `alt=""` if decorative. A missing attribute makes some screen readers read out the filename or URL.
- The tile image URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600` for a 600px-max layout. That's a heavy payload, but I can only infer it from the URLs, not measure it.
- The preheader spacer (`#pht-spacer`) is correctly hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) appear in the visible source or QA output. The tracking `params`/`tuid` values are encoded as expected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** There is effectively no text/plain alternative. This hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients and some accessibility tools with nothing readable.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header.** QA itself notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. Treat it as a capture limitation until checked against the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results` header.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status for `em.dcsg.com` is unknown from this capture, and no failure is evidenced.
- The footer is cut off by truncation, so the unsubscribe link and physical postal address can't be confirmed in the HTML. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reports no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The `href` values and any UTM parameters are not visible in the truncated source, and QA doesn't report on them. No mismatches are evidenced.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real text/plain part (offer, category links, unsubscribe link, address) to fix the QA FAIL.
2. Add `alt` to all 12 flagged images. Use descriptive text for the category tiles (e.g. "Men's clearance"). Use `alt=""` for the tracking pixel and the decorative icons.
3. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message at the origin. If they exist there, the relay is dropping them. If not, add them.
4. Capture `Authentication-Results` from the original message (or via a seed test) to verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
5. Re-run QA on the untruncated HTML to verify the footer unsubscribe link and address, the UTM parameters on the final `href`s, and the 59 unprobed links.
6. Consider serving tile images closer to 2x of the rendered width (about 1200px wide) without the 1600px height, to cut payload.
