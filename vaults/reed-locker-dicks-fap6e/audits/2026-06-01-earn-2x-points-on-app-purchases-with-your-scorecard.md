# Earn 2X Points‼️On app purchases with your ScoreCard
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-01**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S tenth-plus email in roughly five days, and unlike the preceding clearance and flash-sale blasts, it leads with a legitimate loyalty mechanic — 2X ScoreCard points on all app purchases for "Mobile Monday." That differentiation is real and worth crediting. The hero section is clean: a phone graphic, the SCORECARD logo, and a bold 2X badge land first and communicate the offer in under two seconds.
- The problem is what comes next. The email immediately collapses into the exact same recycled deal lanes — Up to 40% Off Men's/Women's/Kids' Clothing, Up to 55% Off Outdoor Essentials, Up to 40% Off Golf — that have appeared in essentially every DICK'S send since May 27. The 2X points premise vanishes into boilerplate. By the midpoint, there's a second "50% OFF" banner that introduces a completely separate promotion, then another product grid. The email is doing two jobs (loyalty activation + deals roundup) and succeeds at neither fully.
- A focused Mobile Monday email — one offer, one CTA ("Shop in App Now"), one deadline — would be far more effective. Instead this reads like the loyalty hero was glued onto a recycled deal template. Competent but a missed opportunity.

## What's working

- The 2X points hero is the first thing you see; the brand clearly prioritized the loyalty mechanic above the fold.
- "Mobile Monday" is a clever recurring program hook — day-specific, app-tethered, loyalty-rewarding. The concept is sound.
- No render bugs visible — layout is clean, buttons are readable, images load.
- Category nav chips (Men's / Women's / Kids' / Golf / Outdoor) below the hero give a useful at-a-glance orientation before the deal lanes start.
- App download badges at the footer are a logical close for an app-focused send.

## What's weak

- The loyalty mechanic (2X points) is immediately diluted by the same deal lanes that appeared in the last six DICK'S emails — the 2X offer gets forgotten by the second scroll.
- No hard deadline visible for the 2X offer — "Mobile Monday" implies today-only, but nowhere in the visible copy does the email say "today only" or "offer ends tonight."
- The mid-email "50% OFF" banner introduces a second promotion that has nothing to do with the app/loyalty theme, creating message confusion.
- "SHOP NOW" CTAs are generic throughout — for an app-specific promotion, every button should read "Shop in App" to reinforce the mechanic.
- Cadence burnout is real: this is the brand's tenth-plus send in five days; even a well-executed loyalty email gets filtered into noise at this frequency.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Strip the deal lanes entirely or move them to the footer.** The 2X points mechanic is the story — give it the full email. The deal content is available in the app anyway.
- 2. **Add an explicit deadline in the hero copy.** Add "Today Only" or "Offer ends midnight CT" directly under the 2X badge. "Mobile Monday" implies urgency but doesn't deliver it.
- 3. **Rewrite every CTA to "Shop in App Now"** instead of "SHOP NOW" — this is an app activation email and every button should reinforce that.
- 4. **Remove the mid-email 50% OFF banner.** It's a different promo and it breaks the narrative.
- 5. **Add a short how-to block** — one row, three steps: Download App → Log In → Earn 2X. Makes the mechanic tangible for members who've never used the ScoreCard in-app.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mobile Monday: 2X points when you shop the app today`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Double your ScoreCard points — today only, in the app`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the DICK'S app before midnight to earn double loyalty points on every purchase.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard just got better — but only if you shop in the app today.`

## Full review
## 1. Overview

This is DICK'S tenth-plus email in roughly five days, and unlike the preceding clearance and flash-sale blasts, it leads with a legitimate loyalty mechanic — 2X ScoreCard points on all app purchases for "Mobile Monday." That differentiation is real and worth crediting. The hero section is clean: a phone graphic, the SCORECARD logo, and a bold 2X badge land first and communicate the offer in under two seconds.

The problem is what comes next. The email immediately collapses into the exact same recycled deal lanes — Up to 40% Off Men's/Women's/Kids' Clothing, Up to 55% Off Outdoor Essentials, Up to 40% Off Golf — that have appeared in essentially every DICK'S send since May 27. The 2X points premise vanishes into boilerplate. By the midpoint, there's a second "50% OFF" banner that introduces a completely separate promotion, then another product grid. The email is doing two jobs (loyalty activation + deals roundup) and succeeds at neither fully.

A focused Mobile Monday email — one offer, one CTA ("Shop in App Now"), one deadline — would be far more effective. Instead this reads like the loyalty hero was glued onto a recycled deal template. Competent but a missed opportunity.

## 3. What didn't

- The loyalty mechanic (2X points) is immediately diluted by the same deal lanes that appeared in the last six DICK'S emails — the 2X offer gets forgotten by the second scroll.
