# Up to 50% off deals continue! Save on brands you love during the Holiday Deal Dash
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same DICK'S "Holiday Deal Dash" send you've seen all week, now with a longer body. The hero is a red striped panel with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF". Below it sit "Holiday Deal Dash: Up to 50% Off" and one green Shop Now button. The offer lands at once and the eye goes to it first.
- The email then keeps going. A second red hero, "EXTRA 25% OFF", repeats the clearance offer from the earlier "EXTRA 25% off select clearance? You got it!" send. After that come $100 & Under shoes, up to 70% off CALIA/VRST/DSG, BOGO socks, BOGO golf, racquet, outdoor, and a gift-pick grid. That is roughly eight offers, each with its own button. The email never settles on one thing to click, so it reads as a catalog.
- The subject also says "deals continue." That is honest, but it is the fifth or sixth "up to 50% off" in a week. The 11/5 audit called a prior one "the fourth in three days." Nothing here feels new except the Deal Dash name, which was already the 11/7 theme.

## What's working

- The gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is instantly legible, and the offer is visible with no scrolling.
- "Two Days Only" gives credible urgency, backed by a stated end time in the fine print.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) lets a shopper jump to their own interest.
- Specific "Only at DICK'S" offers such as BOGO socks and BOGO 50% off Walter Hagen golf feel concrete and exclusive.
- The ScoreCard Points bar sits at the top, so loyalty is acknowledged.

## What's weak

- Eight-plus offers and buttons dilute the single "Deal Dash" message.
- The second red "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero competes with the first. Two giant offers back to back leaves no clear winner.
- The subject and the headline are nearly the same ("Up to 50% off… Holiday Deal Dash"), so nothing new is revealed.
- The "up to" language is vague. The hero never shows a product or a real price.
- Fine print is long and dense under every block, with "Select styles" and "Exclusions apply" repeated. It undercuts the honesty of the offer.
- The middle sections (golf, racquet, outdoor) are only loosely tied to the Deal Dash theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero, the clearance block, and three category tiles. Drop the golf, racquet, and outdoor sections, or segment them by buyer.
- 2. Show one real product with a real price in the hero area, for example "Nike Pegasus $64.97", so "up to 50%" becomes a concrete number.
- 3. Add a live countdown or "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT" next to the button.
- 4. Merge the two heroes into one offer stack, or send the clearance offer separately.
- 5. Move the long legal text into a single footer block.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Deal Dash ends Sunday: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `2 days left: Nike, CALIA & more up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 25% off clearance and BOGO socks, today only online.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes from $100 & under, golf BOGO 50% off, and gifts under $50.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a confirmed plain-text fallback failure (9 characters) and 12 images with no alt text, including the six main promo banners. The relay didn't capture the authentication and list-unsubscribe headers, so those items can't be confirmed either way. The visible markup (lang, viewport, role="presentation" tables, MSO conditionals) is otherwise sound.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links reported. The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 54 (footer, utility and social). Those 54 were not validated.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&configId=512ffb51-…`, a 1x1 image with no `alt`. It is a minor issue and expected for a tracking pixel.
- The pixel sits at the very top of `<body>`, before the wrapper table. That is valid, but it is the first node in the DOM.
- The truncated source doesn't show the CTA `href` values, so redirect and tracking-wrapper behavior can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback [FAIL]:** the text part is 9 characters. Clients and filters that use the text part will show effectively empty content.
- **Missing alt text [WARN], 12 images.**
  - Hero and category banners (all `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/…`): `2026-1107-dks-swd-4pm-shoes`, `…-clothing-new`, `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `…-golf`, `…-gear`, `…-outdoor`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Tracking pixel: the `data.em.dcsg.com` open pixel.
  - If the banners carry the offer text, it is unavailable with images blocked or to screen readers. The banners are the main content, so this is the most serious accessibility gap.
- **Image sizing:** the banner URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fmt=jpeg&qlt=100`. That is a 1200×1600 asset at maximum quality for a 584px container, which means heavy payloads. I couldn't confirm the actual file sizes.
- **Good practice confirmed:**
  - `<html lang="en">`, a `<title>` and a viewport meta tag are present.
  - Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.
  - Preheader spacer is hidden with `display:none; mso-hide:all`.
  - Web font has an Arial fallback.
  - MSO/Outlook conditional comments are present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FNAME]`) appear in the visible source. The tracking pixel has a `tuid` parameter, which suggests per-recipient tracking.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe [WARN] and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) [WARN]:** neither header was found. The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed. Verify against the raw message at the sender (`info@em.dcsg.com`). Gmail and Yahoo require one-click unsubscribe for bulk senders.
- **Authentication-Results [WARN]:** not present, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. The sending domain `em.dcsg.com` is a subdomain of the `dcsg.com` organizational domain, and the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com` is on the same domain. Those are good signs, but not verification.
- The footer, physical address and in-body unsubscribe link fall in the truncated section of the source and weren't examined. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always probed, and it reported no failure.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated HTML doesn't show CTA destinations or UTM parameters. The anchors use `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which points to a deep-link or app-routing layer, but I couldn't confirm where the links land.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part (high).** Include the offer, the main category links and the unsubscribe link.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to all 6 banners (high),** conveying the offer and brand, for example "Up to 50% off shoes". Use `alt=""` on decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
3. **Check the raw headers at the source (medium).** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` with `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and confirm SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass. These were not visible through the relay.
4. **Reduce image weight (low).** Lower `qlt` to about 80–85 and set `wid` to about 2× the display width instead of 1200×1600.
5. **Re-run the audit on the full HTML (medium).** That would cover the CTA hrefs, UTM parameters and footer compliance content, plus the 54 links that were skipped.
