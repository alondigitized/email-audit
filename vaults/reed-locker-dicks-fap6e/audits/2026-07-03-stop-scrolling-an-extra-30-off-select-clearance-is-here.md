# Stop scrolling! An EXTRA 30% off select clearance is here
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-03**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S thirteenth-plus promotional email since June 26 — less than eight days. The "EXTRA 30% OFF" hero is clean and typographically dominant, the offer is immediately legible, and the email renders without any visible bugs. So far so good. The problem is that this creative is functionally a twin of the July 2 send ("Don't miss it! Extra 30% off clearance finds," scored 7/10) — same discount mechanism, same multi-module layout, same clearance category. Swapping the subject to "Stop scrolling!" doesn't refresh the concept; it openly acknowledges subscriber fatigue, which is a losing posture.
- The multi-section body — shoes, VRST apparel, bikes, paddles, $100-and-under deals — gives the email genuine catalog depth, and the 4th of July module keeps it seasonally grounded without forcing the holiday into the main offer. But at thirteen-plus sends in eight days with two clearance extra-30%-off emails back-to-back, the cadence has collapsed the sense of occasion entirely. When every email is urgent, none of them are.
- Verdict: a serviceable mid-tier clearance digest running on fumes from overexposure. It would score higher if it were the first clearance send this week. It isn't.

## What's working

- **Hero is punchy and legible** — "EXTRA 30% OFF" dominates the viewport, offer is understood in under two seconds.
- **Multi-module depth** — shoes, bikes, VRST resort, paddles, $100-and-under all appear as separate deal pods, giving subscribers multiple reasons to scroll.
- **4th of July module lands correctly** — the seasonal tie-in sits naturally in a secondary position without hijacking the clearance hero.
- **Clean render** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout drift visible.
- **Sender label "DICK'S | Clearance"** — segmented sender name signals the content type before the subject line is even read.

## What's weak

- **"Stop scrolling!" is a self-defeating hook** — it signals DICK'S knows you're tuning out. Leads with weakness.
- **Carbon-copy creative** — this is the second extra-30%-off clearance email in two days; no new hook, no escalation, no reason this one beats yesterday's.
- **No deadline or urgency mechanism** — the offer floats without stakes; nothing anchors it to today.
- **Too many modules dilute focus** — six-plus deal pods make the email feel like a catalog drop, not a campaign moment.
- **Subject is 57 characters** — over the ~50-char mobile truncation threshold; "select clearance is here" likely clips on small screens.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline.** "Ends tonight" or "Today only" transforms a floating clearance dump into an event. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Retire "Stop scrolling!"** — It's been used too many times across the inbox and signals subscriber disengagement awareness, not confidence.
- 3. **Collapse to one hero.** Either lead with 4th of July (more timely today, July 3) or lead with clearance — not both as co-equal modules. The split makes neither feel primary.
- 4. **Give the CTA a noun.** "SHOP NOW" is generic; "Shop Clearance Deals" or "Shop 30% Off" tells the subscriber where they land.
- 5. **Preheader should carry the deadline** — the subject has the offer, the preheader should have the stakes.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 30% off clearance — ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance prices just dropped another 30%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, bikes, apparel — hundreds of picks, one day left`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `DICK'S clearance goes fast. Shop before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email contains a critical JSON-LD parse error (JavaScript-style comments inside a `<script type="application/ld+json">` block) that will silently break all Gmail promotional annotations. Multiple template merge fields are also unresolved empty strings shipped to production.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

All CTA links are wrapped in `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/` ESP redirect tracking — standard practice. No raw destination URLs are visible in the source to confirm UTM parameters survive the redirect chain. **Recommend**: test a click-through and confirm `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` appear on landing pages.

The open-tracking pixel fires from `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...` and is correctly suppressed with `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`. No issue.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `<html lang="en">` present. Good.
- All layout tables carry `role="presentation"`. Good.
- `border-collapse:separate` is intentionally paired with `border-radius` on container tables — correct technique for rounded corners.
- Web font (`Archivo`) loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/...` — `v7` is a dated path. If the font has been deprecated on the CDN, fallback to Arial fires silently. Verify the `v7` URL still resolves.
- Logo `<img>` alt text is cut off in the truncated source — cannot confirm. **Must verify** `alt` is non-empty on the logo and all product images.
- Tracking pixel `src` attribute uses single quotes (`src='...'`) while every other attribute in the document uses double quotes. Technically valid HTML5 but flagged as a template inconsistency suggesting copy-paste assembly.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block has **two compounding failures**:

**A — Invalid JSON (comments):** JSON does not support `//` comments. Three are present:

```json
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
// Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```

Gmail's annotation parser will reject the entire JSON array. None of the three schema objects (`Organization`, `DiscountOffer`, `PromotionCard`) will be parsed.

**B — Unfilled template fields shipped to production:**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` (6 years stale) |

The `availabilityStarts` date of `2020-03-06` is a copy-forward from a 2020 template and is factually incorrect. The designer instruction comment (`// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`) was not removed before send.

---

### 5. Compliance

- Sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (ESP subdomain of `dcsg.com`) — standard authenticated sending subdomain.
- CAN-SPAM requires a physical postal address and a functioning unsubscribe mechanism. The HTML is truncated; these could not be confirmed in the supplied source. They must be verified in the full render.
- Authentication headers (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) cannot be assessed from HTML source alone — check MX Toolbox or email header analysis on a delivered copy.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot confirm UTM parameters without following the `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/` redirect chain. The link structure wraps all destinations opaquely. A manual click-trace on at least the primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is required to confirm:
- `utm_source` and `utm_medium` are set
- Landing page is the clearance category (not homepage)
- No 3xx redirect loops or 404s after the ESP redirect resolves

---
