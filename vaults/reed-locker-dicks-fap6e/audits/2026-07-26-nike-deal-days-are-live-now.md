# Nike Deal Days are live now
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the first genuinely different creative DICK'S has sent in this stretch — "Nike Deal Days" gives the top third of the email an actual campaign identity instead of another "Up to X% Off" clearance rerun. The hero is well-built: locker-room backdrop, styled Nike apparel flat-lay, a "Deal Days" stamp, and a clean 40%-off headline with a single Shop Now CTA above the fold.
- The problem is what happens after that hero. By the middle of the email it collapses back into the exact same kitchen-sink format as every recent send — eight stacked modules (drinkware, golf, life vests, racquet gear, weighted vests, MLB gear) — and then drops in the identical "UP TO 70% OFF Summer Clearance Event" red banner that anchored the 2026-07-24 "CLEARANCE deals: up to 70% off!" (8.00/10) and 2026-07-25 "Up to 70% off is here" (6.00/10) sends. So a subscriber who's opened the last several DICK'S emails will recognize that block instantly, and it dilutes the Nike-specific hook the subject promised.
- Net: strong top, tired middle-to-bottom, honest and clean rendering throughout. Worth opening on the strength of the subject and hero; less compelling to keep scrolling once you hit the fourth "Shop Now" module that has nothing to do with Nike Deal Days.

## What's working

- The hero has a distinct campaign identity (Nike swoosh branding, "Deal Days" badge, styled product shot) rather than a generic percentage-off banner.
- Subject-to-hero match is tight — "Nike Deal Days are live now" lands exactly on what's above the fold.
- Loyalty/Scorecard rewards messaging is visible at the very top ("300 Points x 4X Rewards," "Sign In to Earn Points"), which is a real incentive for members.
- Category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Golf/Back-to-School Shop) gives quick wayfinding right under the hero.
- Render is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, no lorem ipsum.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 70% Off Summer Clearance Event" red banner two-thirds down is a verbatim repeat of the hero treatment from the 2026-07-24 and 2026-07-25 sends — a subscriber has now seen this exact block at least three times.
- The middle of the email sprawls across seven unrelated modules (drinkware, life vests, racquet gear, weighted vests, MLB gear) that have nothing to do with "Nike Deal Days," burying the stated theme.
- Every single CTA button says "SHOP NOW" — ten-plus identical buttons give zero signal about what's specifically being clicked into.
- No product-level detail (sizing, availability, price) anywhere in the email, so nothing pulls a shopper past "browse the category."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email roughly in half — keep the Nike Deal Days hero, the Nike Golf block, and one back-to-school module; drop or move life vests/racquet gear/MLB/weighted vests to a separate send. A themed campaign email should stay themed.
- 2. Replace the recycled 70% Off Summer Clearance banner with something Nike-specific (e.g., a second Nike Deal Days sub-offer) — reusing the same red banner three sends running reads as filler, not a new deal.
- 3. Vary CTA copy by module ("Shop Nike Hoodies," "Shop Golf Shoes") instead of repeating "SHOP NOW" ten times.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the subject/hero — "Limited Time" with no date in the subject line undercuts urgency; a specific end date would sharpen it.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Nike Deal Days: up to 40% off ends soon`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Nike back-to-school picks are 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Hoodies, joggers and Nike shoes — Deal Days pricing while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score Nike gear for back-to-school before Deal Days ends`

# Technical Email Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods — "Nike Deal Days are live now"

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard MSO-compatible responsive table layout with ESP click-tracking (Cordial-pattern `app.dickssportinggoods.com` redirects), but ships with a broken unsubscribe link and no verifiable authentication/compliance headers at the relay layer. QA pass rate: 43% (1 failure, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe redirect (confirmed FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019f9d4c00119113d46e96c566a0/ff6440430ce84f4b0000021ef3a0bd00` resolves to `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'` — the preference-center endpoint is erroring out rather than resolving to a working unsubscribe/subscription page.
- Tracking infrastructure uses a Cordial-style scheme (`/T/` click-tracked links, `/O/` open pixel, `/U/` unsubscribe) consistently across the source — no malformed tracking-link syntax observed elsewhere.
- Open-tracking pixel (`id="ssl"`, `/O/...`) is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0px`) — no issue.
- 12 of 63 total links were probed by QA (footer/social/utility links deprioritized); no additional broken material links were reported among those probed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text** (confirmed via QA), including primary content images (`vests.jpg`, `gear.jpg`, `fvests.jpg`, `jersey.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `mapbreak.gif`) and footer icons (SMS, SportsMatter, BPG, favorite, location icons). This degrades screen-reader accessibility and leaves no fallback text when images are blocked by default in most mail clients.
- Malformed inline CSS in the MSO conditional block: `div {background-color:inherit; ... padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"` before the closing brace, an invalid CSS declaration. Outlook's Word rendering engine is typically lenient with malformed CSS, but this is a code defect that should be fixed.
- `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` line comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`), which is **invalid JSON** — any consumer attempting to parse this JSON-LD (e.g., Gmail annotations, schema validators) will fail. This also indicates template placeholder text was left in the shipped HTML.
- JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` objects have empty `discountCode` and `image` fields — unfilled template values shipped to production.
- Font stack declares `Archivo` via `@font-face`/Google Fonts with `Arial, sans-serif` fallback — correct fallback practice, no issue.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization variables, or template placeholders (e.g., `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, `{{token}}`) observed in the available HTML source. No issues found.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** (see §2) — this is a functional CAN-SPAM concern since the one-click/link-based opt-out does not resolve.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not detected — cannot confirm RFC 2369 mailbox-level unsubscribe support.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not detected — cannot confirm RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support.
- `Authentication-Results` header not detected — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Caveat: the last three are flagged by QA as possibly relay-side omissions (via AgentMail) rather than confirmed sender-side absences — treat as unverified, not confirmed failures, pending a raw-header capture at the MTA.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All hyperlinks in the visible source are wrapped in ESP click-tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`), which obscure the final destination URL and any UTM parameters. UTM/landing-page alignment cannot be verified from this HTML alone — would require following redirects to the resolved `dickssportinggoods.com` destination URLs.
- Image assets are served from three separate vendor domains (`dks.scene7.com`, `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com`, `app.dickssportinggoods.com`), consistent with normal ESP/DAM infrastructure — no continuity issue identified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe endpoint — `/U/...` currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`; this is a compliance-critical broken link and should be prioritized.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 flagged images, particularly the primary product/offer images (`vests.jpg`, `gear.jpg`, `fvests.jpg`, `jersey.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `mapbreak.gif`).
3. Remove `//` comments and fill/remove empty fields from the `application/ld+json` block so it parses as valid JSON.
4. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `div{...}` CSS rule.
5. Verify at the raw-MTA level (outside this relay) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC `Authentication-Results` are actually present and passing — current absence may be a capture artifact, not a sender defect.
