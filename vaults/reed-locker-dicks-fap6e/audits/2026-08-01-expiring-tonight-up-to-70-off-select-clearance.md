# 💸 Expiring TONIGHT 💸 Up to 70% off select clearance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red "UP TO 70% OFF" hero DICK'S has now run at least three times in three days — it debuted 2026-07-30 as "CLEARANCE: up to 70% OFF" (6.00/10), ran again same-day as "Summer Clearance Event" (6.00/10), and returns here basically unchanged except for a live countdown clock and a harder-urgency subject line. The countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") is a genuinely good addition — it's the one new element since 7/30 — but everything below it is the identical kitchen-sink stack: red hero, teal "Flash Sale" locker-room banner, red Nike Deal Days block, then a six-item product grid.
- The email is clean and legible, and the "Expiring TONIGHT" framing plus a real ticking clock gives it more credible urgency than the prior sends in this thread. But stacking three separate sales (70% off clearance, 50% off flash sale, 40% off Nike) in one email dilutes the headline offer rather than reinforcing it, and there's a visible crop bug on the Flash Sale banner where the word "FLASH" is cut off mid-letter ("FLASI").
- Judgment: a middle-of-the-pack send that's starting to show fatigue from reuse. The countdown clock is worth keeping; the rest needs a creative refresh before it goes out a fourth time.

## What's working

- A live countdown timer ("07:59:48") is layered onto the hero — a genuine urgency upgrade over the static 7/30 and 7/31 versions of this same banner.
- Loyalty balance is surfaced right at the top ("300 Points + $10 Reward, Shop to Earn Points") before any sales content, which rewards existing members.
- The green "SHOP NOW" CTA is used consistently and is easy to spot against white space in all three offer blocks.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give impatient scrollers a fast exit ramp without reading the whole email.

## What's weak

- The teal "Flash Sale" banner has a visible crop/render bug — "FLASH" is cut off mid-word as "FLASI" at the right edge.
- Three competing sale mechanics (70% off clearance, 50% off flash sale, 40% off Nike) in one email blur which offer is actually the headline — no single clear "best deal."
- This exact red hero has now shipped at least twice before in the same week (7/30 "CLEARANCE: up to 70% OFF," 7/30 "Summer Clearance Event") with no creative variation, just new copy wrapped around it.
- The bottom product grid (Nike Kids' Air Force 1, Kijaro chair, Owala bottle, etc.) shows no prices or sizes, so it can't actually convert — it's browse-only.
- Fine print undercuts the headline number: "Interim markdowns may have been taken," "prices as marked" — the 70% is a ceiling, not a real expectation.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the red "UP TO 70% OFF" hero creative — it's now appeared 3+ times this week per the brand history; subscribers who've opened more than one of these are seeing the identical banner. Ship a new hero image/color treatment even if the discount math stays the same.
- 2. Fix the "FLASI" crop bug on the Flash Sale banner before this asset goes out again.
- 3. Pick one hero offer per send instead of stacking three (clearance/flash/Nike) — move the other two into the "Explore more deals" tile row so the eye has one clear target.
- 4. Add at least indicative pricing or "starting at $X" to the bottom product grid so it can drive a direct add-to-cart, not just a browse click.

- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ 8 hours left on clearance — up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, cleats, camp gear — clearance ends tonight at 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points + up to 70% off, before the clock runs out`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard Epsilon Harmony/DSG template with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries, but ships with an unresolved template artifact (empty JSON-LD promo fields) and a broken unsubscribe link that resolves to an error page.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in Epsilon Harmony click-tracking redirects (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019fbf1db827b8e5c6369ff0b33c/...`), consistent with the sender domain `e.dcsg.com`.
- **Broken unsubscribe link**: QA flagged `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fbf1db827b8e5c6369ff0b33c/43ebceb387504bfa0000021ef3a0bcf6` resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — an unhandled redirect error, not the subscription-management page.
- QA probed 12 of 53 total links (material/high-click-likelihood links only); footer/utility/social links were deprioritized but the unsubscribe link was included per policy and failed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text, confirmed via QA: the SSL/open-tracking pixel (`43ebceb387504bfa00004c5a42963aa1`), and footer icons `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png` (all served from `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/footers/`).
- MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and Outlook-specific table wrapping are present for Outlook rendering.
- Mobile responsive rules are defined under `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` with `!important` overrides (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, `.body-m`, etc.) — standard pattern, no defects observed in the truncated source.
- Custom web font (Archivo) loads via `@font-face` gated behind `@media screen` (correctly excludes Outlook/desktop clients that mishandle it), with system-font fallback (`Arial, sans-serif`) declared on `body`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The `application/ld+json` schema block contains unpopulated template fields: `DiscountOffer.description`, `DiscountOffer.discountCode`, and `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` are all empty strings, and `PromotionCard.image` is blank with a leftover build comment `"DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!"` still in the shipped HTML.
- `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00` — a stale/placeholder date inconsistent with the "Expiring TONIGHT" subject line, indicating this structured-data block was not updated for the current campaign.
- No visible `{{merge}}`/personalization token syntax in the visible source; no evidence of broken/unresolved tokens in rendered content itself.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism broken**: link errors out (see §2), which is a functional failure of the required CAN-SPAM opt-out mechanism.
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not present per QA — may be a relay-side capture gap (AgentMail) rather than confirmed absence at the source MTA; flag as unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not present — one-click unsubscribe support unconfirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not present via the AgentMail relay — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from available data.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- All CTA/logo links route through the Epsilon Harmony redirector (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) with an opaque `__dU__` token rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs to dickssportinggoods.com — final landing URL and UTM parameters are not visible in the truncated HTML/redirect chain, so alignment with the landing page cannot be confirmed from source alone.
- No `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` parameters are observable pre-redirect; this is expected for Harmony-tracked links (params are typically appended post-redirect) but should be verified against the actual resolved landing URL, which is outside the scope of the provided data.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect — `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` must be corrected to route to a working preference-center/unsubscribe page; this is a compliance-blocking defect.
2. Populate or remove the empty `DiscountOffer` JSON-LD fields (`description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`) and update the stale `availabilityStarts` date before send.
3. Add `alt` attributes to the 6 flagged images, particularly the footer benefit icons (currently non-descriptive for screen readers).
4. Confirm at the MTA/relay level whether `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are actually being sent — verify independently of the AgentMail capture gap, since RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support is increasingly required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules.
5. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `e.dcsg.com` directly against raw headers, since `Authentication-Results` wasn't captured through this relay.
