# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-05-02
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-02**
## Executive summary

- Dick's Sporting Goods handed me an error page — literally "Oops, Something Went Wrong" with a 12-hour wait message. That's the entire homepage experience right now. There's nothing to evaluate as a brand or a shopper; it's a dead end with a logo on it.
- **1/10**

## What's working

- The Dick's Sporting Goods logo is clearly visible and recognizable — at least I know I landed on the right site before it fell apart.

## What's weak

- The error message is the entire page — no fallback content, no navigation, no cached deals, nothing.
- "Please try again after 12 hours" is a brutal ask. That's half a day. I've got a kid's tournament this weekend and I needed cleats today.
- The error code (`0.510c3417.1777763584.1ac5350b IP: 47.41.15.19`) is raw technical garbage displayed to a shopper. Nobody should see that.
- Zero recovery path — no "browse bestsellers," no search bar, no "shop by sport" fallback.
- The rest of the page below the error card is completely blank gray canvas, thousands of pixels of nothing.

## Recommendations

- Surface a static fallback page with at least top-category nav tiles (Team Sports, Footwear, Apparel) so the site stays shoppable during outages.
- Replace the raw error code block with a human message — "We're on it" is enough; the IP and hash mean nothing to me.
- Add a direct link to the ScoreCard rewards portal or store locator — give me somewhere useful to go while the homepage recovers.
- Set a much shorter retry window or offer an email/SMS alert ("Notify me when we're back") instead of asking me to just come back in 12 hours.
