# Worth a look! EXTRA 25% off select clearance 🔥
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth execution of the same "Extra 25% off select clearance" creative since May 30 — and the second one sent today, June 2, alongside "Happening NOW! Extra 25% off select clearance ⏰" which already scored 7/10 this morning. The red hero block is clean, the offer is legible, and the multi-department product grid gives subscribers multiple hooks. But DICK'S has now torched every scrap of urgency this campaign had. "Worth a look!" as an opener signals the marketing team ran out of angles — it's a placeholder where a value prop should be.
- The email itself is technically competent: no render bugs, clear visual hierarchy, SHOP NOW CTAs present throughout. The problem isn't the email, it's the cadence. When "Final Hours" was the subject line on May 31 and the sale is still running four sends later on June 2, the offer loses credibility. A subscriber seeing this after the 7/10 morning send will likely archive on sight.
- The multi-section layout (40% clothing, 60% fan shop, $300 golf clubs, inline skates, pickleball, 50% hero) creates real click optionality but no clear hero product story. It reads like a clearance tab, not a curated offer.

## What's working

- Red hero block is clean and immediately communicates the offer — no decoding required.
- Multiple discount tiers (25%, 40%, 60%, up to $300 off) give almost every sports category a hook.
- Product imagery is category-specific and reasonably high quality — golf, fan shop, outdoor, and apparel each get their own visual lane.
- "This Week's Deals" product grid at the bottom provides a browse-friendly fallback for subscribers who don't click the hero.
- The SHOP NOW CTA is present, well-placed, and high contrast against the red hero.

## What's weak

- "Worth a look!" is the weakest possible subject opener — it communicates nothing and kills open curiosity before the offer lands.
- This is the second DICK'S clearance email today; subscribers who opened the 7/10 "Happening NOW!" send this morning have already seen this creative.
- No deadline or end-date is visible anywhere in the email — after "Final Hours" expired on May 31, there is zero credible urgency remaining.
- The "Boeing 2023 Country Collection" label in the golf section says 2023 — either a copy error or stale inventory labeling that undermines the "fresh clearance" frame.
- No ScoreCard / loyalty angle visible here, which is a miss given the June 1 double-send that leaned hard on 2X Points — a subscriber primed on that mechanic gets nothing to carry forward.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Retire this creative immediately.** Four sends of the same red hero in four days is the definition of diminishing returns. The May 30 launch at 8/10 was right; the June 2 second-daily resend will accelerate unsubscribes.
- 2. **Attach a hard expiration date.** If the sale ends June 4, say "Ends Thursday" in the hero sub-headline. Without it, urgency language is noise.
- 3. **Rewrite the subject opener.** "Worth a look!" is a filler phrase. Lead with the discount or a category hook.
- 4. **Tie a loyalty lever in.** The June 1 ScoreCard 2X Points sends proved the mechanic works — combine clearance + double points for a final-day push that's actually differentiated.
- 5. **Fix the "2023 Country Collection" label** — or at minimum audit whether any other section headers have stale year references.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — ends Thursday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance + 2X ScoreCard Points: final 24 hrs`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Golf clubs, fan gear, apparel — up to 60% off before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack your ScoreCard on clearance prices. Offer ends midnight.`

### 5. Compliance

**JSON-LD contains JavaScript-style comments — invalid JSON syntax:**

```js
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

`//` comments are not valid JSON. Any parser (Gmail's annotation engine, Google's rich results validator) will reject this entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block. The structured data will not parse.

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe footer:** Not visible in the truncated source. Cannot confirm or deny — must be verified in full render. If absent, this is a CAN-SPAM violation.

**Authentication headers (SPF/DKIM/DMARC):** Not assessable from HTML source alone. Sending domain `e.dcsg.com` is an Epsilon-managed subdomain; alignment with `dcsg.com` parent domain should be confirmed in message headers.
