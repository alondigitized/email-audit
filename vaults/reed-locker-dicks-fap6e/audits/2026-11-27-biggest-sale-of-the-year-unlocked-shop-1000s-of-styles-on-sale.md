# Biggest Sale of the Year? Unlocked. 🔓 💸 Shop 1000s of styles on sale!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Black Friday template again, and it is competent. The dark green "BIGGEST SALE OF THE YEAR" hero sits over one clear offer: "Black Friday: Up to 50% Off", a "Limited Time" tag and a red Shop Now button. A row of category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) follows. Everything is legible and nothing looks broken.
- The problem is the length and the sameness. Below the hero are about eight stacked promos: UGG at 30% off, Carhartt at 40%, Nike Pro shorts at 25%, Walter Hagen BOGO, trading cards, basketball hoops, an extra 25% off clearance block and ScoreCard+ $20 bonus rewards. Each is a different offer, so the single "Up to 50%" message gets diluted. This follows three Black Friday sends earlier today, including "Big savings alert!" and "Incredible Black Friday DEALS", plus the "Happy Thanksgiving" send the day before. A subscriber has seen this hero over and over.
- Verdict: well built and on-season, but it is fatiguing and gives me no new reason to open. The "Unlocked" subject framing promises a reveal that the body doesn't deliver.

## What's working

- The hero is bold and unmistakable, and the "Up to 50% Off" offer sits directly under it with a red Shop Now button.
- "Limited Time" plus a dated expiry in the fine print gives credible urgency.
- The category pills let a shopper jump straight to their department.
- The ScoreCard+ block, "Activate & earn a $20 bonus reward", gives members a real incentive.
- The footer "Gift picks" grid shows individual products (Stanley tumbler, UGG slippers, HOKA shoes), which adds some product specificity.

## What's weak

- The email is a very long scroll with roughly eight competing offers (30%, 40%, 25%, BOGO, extra 25%, $20 bonus). The "Up to 50%" headline gets lost.
- Nearly every CTA is a generic "Shop Now", so there's no verb-plus-noun specificity.
- The fine print is dense and heavy, with several exclusion paragraphs, which weakens how honest the offer feels.
- There are no reviews, sizing or availability cues. Only the footer grid shows any actual products.
- It is the same creative as the 11/26 and earlier 11/27 sends, so it adds nothing new for a subscriber who has already seen those.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to the hero, 3 to 4 modules and the ScoreCard+ block. Each extra offer lowers the odds of a click on the main one.
- 2. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop UGG Boots", "Shop Nike Shorts", "Shop Clearance".
- 3. Pay off the "Unlocked" idea in the hero. For example, show a "doorbuster unlocked" tile with a countdown to a real deadline instead of repeating the same banner.
- 4. Add a star-rating or "selling fast" cue on the product tiles.
- 5. Keep the subject under 50 characters and drop one of the two emojis.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Doorbusters unlocked: up to 50% off 🔓`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Black Friday deals end tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `UGG, Nike, Carhartt and more. Ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus an extra 25% off clearance and $20 ScoreCard+ rewards.`
