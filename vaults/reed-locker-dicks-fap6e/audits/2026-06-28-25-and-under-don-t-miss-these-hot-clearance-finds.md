# $25 and under! Don't miss these HOT clearance finds💸
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- This is a clearance pivot send from DICK'S on June 28 — the brand's third distinct campaign angle in three days after Deal Days closed June 26 and a 4th of July launch dropped June 27. The creative logic is sound: $25-and-under clearance is a genuinely new message, and the bold hero communicates the price threshold instantly. Executed well at the top of the fold.
- The problem is the middle of the email: the editorial coherence collapses fast. HOKA running shoes, DKNY family clothing, Quest kayaks, Callaway golf balls, and basketball shoes all live in the same scroll — and featuring kayaks in a "$25 and under" email is confusing. Subscribers know kayaks don't hit $25. That dissonance chips at trust and muddles the offer.
- Brand fatigue is real here. This is at minimum the third DICK'S send in three days, and that follows a brutal cadence through Deal Days. A subscriber who stayed with the list through Deal Days is now getting hit again with what reads like a warehouse dump. The clearance angle buys some goodwill, but not enough to fully reset the counter.

## What's working

- **"$25 AND UNDER" hero** is bold, legible, and lands the value prop in under a second — no ambiguity about what the email is.
- **Specific brand callouts** (HOKA Clifton 10, Callaway Chrome Serene, DKNY) give the deal tiles credibility over generic "clearance items" language.
- **Category navigation tabs** (All Clearance, All Shoe, etc.) near the top give browsers a quick path to their area without needing to scroll the full email.
- **Percentage-off labels** are prominent on each tile, so the discount level is immediately visible rather than buried in copy.

## What's weak

- **Kayaks in a $25-and-under email** is jarring — no subscriber believes a Quest kayak is under $25, which undermines the hero promise and raises fine-print suspicion.
- **"Don't miss these HOT clearance finds"** is completely generic — it could have come from any retailer and adds zero specificity to the category tiles already doing the heavy lifting.
- **No deadline anywhere in the email** — "Don't miss" implies urgency but without a date it's hollow.
- **Email fatigue is compounding** — this is at least the third distinct DICK'S send in three days; even a good clearance message takes a hit from the cadence.
- **Subject exceeds mobile-optimal length** at ~53 chars before the emoji, likely truncating on smaller Android screens.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Drop the kayaks or rename the section** to make the $25-and-under claim coherent. If it's "select accessories," say so explicitly. Otherwise, pull the kayak tile and replace with a footwear or apparel block consistent with the price story.
- 2. **Add a real deadline** — "Through Sunday" or "Ends July 1" in the hero strip would transform "Don't miss" from a noise phrase into an actual action trigger.
- 3. **Collapse to two or three categories** — HOKA shoes + activewear + one more. Diluting across five unrelated sports categories buries every single one.
- 4. **Rewrite the subject** to earn its length — the $25 threshold is the strongest asset and should be reinforced by a specific product callout:
- **Subject Alt A:** `HOKA, Callaway + more — all $25 & under`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance haul: $25 and under ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running shoes, golf balls, apparel — summer clearance, limited time`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `These prices won't last — shop $25-and-under picks now`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based HTML email deployed via Epsilon/Harmony platform with standard MSO conditional comments and responsive breakpoints. Two confirmed code defects — invalid JSON-LD (kills Gmail promo annotations) and a malformed MSO conditional style rule — plus several stale template artifacts left unpopulated.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken links detected** in the truncated source. All links are redirect-wrapped through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` (Epsilon click-tracking), which is expected.

**UTM verification blocked by redirect wrapping** — the destination URLs are not visible in the source. Cannot confirm whether UTM params survive the Epsilon redirect to `dickssportinggoods.com`. Recommend following one link manually to the landing page and inspecting `document.location.search`.

**Open-tracking pixel** at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f0dbdced4cb668c369fe94bd8/...` uses single-quoted `src` attribute (inconsistent with rest of template, though technically valid HTML5).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**MSO conditional CSS has a syntax error** (high severity for Outlook):

```html
<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style type="text/css">
  ...
  div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}
</style><![endif]-->
```

There is a stray `"` before the closing `}` in the `div` rule (`padding:0;"`). This is invalid CSS that will cause Outlook/IE to drop or partially misparse the entire conditional style block, potentially breaking `div` reset styles in Outlook rendering.

**`border-collapse:separate` on the wrapper table** is unconventional for email. It is intentional here (required for `border-radius` on child tables in some clients) but note that the MSO conditional table reset above sets `border-collapse:collapse` — these conflict for Outlook, where the conditional applies.

`role="presentation"` correctly applied to all layout tables. `lang="en"` on `<html>` is present. Alt text on images cannot be verified from the truncated source — must confirm `alt` attributes on all `<img>` tags, especially the logo.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**No unresolved merge tokens** visible in truncated source (e.g., no `{{firstname}}` or `%%FIRST_NAME%%` literals).

**Conditional content segmentation** is indicated by `id="header-non-sc-member"` on the header table. If a ScoreCard member variant exists, verify that the conditional logic is correctly scoped in the ESP template — a misconfigured segment would show the wrong header to logged-in rewards members.

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot confirm UTM coverage from this source (redirect-wrapped links). The `__dU__` parameter in tracked links (`?__dU__=v0G4RBK...`) is Epsilon's user identifier token — confirm this resolves correctly for both authenticated and anonymous recipients.

**Gmail Promotions tab annotation will not render** (see §7 below) — clearance offers typically benefit from the annotated promo card display, so this is a lost opportunity for inbox visibility.

### 7. Recommendations
