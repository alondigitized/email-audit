# 🚨 100s of NEW clearance styles are here — shop now! 🚨
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-21**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running its "kitchen sink" clearance catalog format again — the same architecture as the 9/20 "BEST deals of the week" send (5.00/10), just re-skinned with a red-and-white "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" hero instead of the orange lightning-bolt FLASH creative. No new clearance styles are actually shown near the hero — it drops straight into category nav chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under) and then a long undifferentiated scroll of running shoes, CALIA/VRST clothing, cleats, luggage, and fan gear before a second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner near the bottom.
- The subject's siren emoji and "100s of NEW clearance styles" promise urgency and freshness, but the body never delivers a clearance-specific product grid — it's the same rotating rail of unrelated categories DICK'S sends every few days. This is a subject-line/body mismatch dressed up as a clearance event.
- Judgement: functional but generic — a placeholder send with a punchy subject bolted onto the brand's default catalog template, not a genuine clearance drop.

## What's working

- Hero headline is bold, legible, and high-contrast (white text on red/black).
- Clear green "Shop Now" CTA directly under the hero copy, above the fold.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give multiple entry points.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent with prior sends.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "100s of NEW clearance styles" but the body shows zero actual clearance products near the top — it's running shoes, apparel, cleats, luggage, and fan gear, the same rotating catalog from other DICK'S sends.
- No specific clearance percentage or product grid — the only discount language ("Up to 40%", "Up to 70%", "Up to 50%") is scattered mid-scroll and reads as generic, not "clearance."
- Extremely long, undifferentiated scroll (8+ product sections) with no visual hierarchy pointing to what's actually new/clearance.
- Second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner near the bottom duplicates the top hero's urgency without adding new information — redundant real estate.
- The 🚨 emoji in the subject overpromises urgency that the calm, generic body doesn't back up.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the body with an actual clearance product grid (with strikethrough pricing) directly under the hero — right now the subject's core promise is never visually delivered.
- 2. Cut the catalog to 3-4 sections max and drop the redundant second "50% OFF" banner — it dilutes the "clearance" angle DICK'S set up in the subject.
- 3. Replace the vague "Up to 40%/70%/50%" scattering with one consistent, honest clearance % tied to the hero.
- 4. Tighten the subject to match what's actually shown, or actually build the email to match the subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `100s of clearance styles just landed 🏷️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on shoes, apparel, and gear — while sizes last`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance arrivals in every category, today only`
