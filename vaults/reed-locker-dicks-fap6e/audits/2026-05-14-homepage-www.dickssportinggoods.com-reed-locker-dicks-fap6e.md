# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-05-14
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-14**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up dickssportinggoods.com and got hit with a full error page — the Dick's logo sitting on a blank white card, a blue "Oops, Something Went Wrong." header, and a message telling me to try back in 12 hours. No product. No nav. No deal. Just a dead end with a raw error code exposed at the bottom. As someone who's got a weekend tournament coming up and needs new cleats for my youngest before Saturday, this is a hard stop.

## What's working

- **Dick's logo renders correctly** — recognizable mark, brand identity is intact on the card, so I at least know I typed the URL right.
- **Error message is plain English** — "We are working on the problem. Please try again after 12 hours" is at least human-readable, not a stack trace wall.

## What's weak

- **The site is completely down** — I can't browse, can't search, can't get to team sports or footwear. A dad trying to resupply before a travel weekend is going to bounce to Academy or Public Lands immediately.
- **Raw error code is exposed to end users** — `Error: 0.23a7cb17.1778790621.1012979b IP: 47.41.15.19` means nothing to me and looks amateurish. Publishing my IP address back at me in bold is weird.
- **"Try again after 12 hours" is a conversion killer** — twelve hours is an eternity when a kid's tournament is this weekend. No fallback, no store-finder, no "shop in-store" redirect.
- **Completely blank page below the error card** — thousands of pixels of gray nothing. No attempt at a fallback experience.
- **No nav, no search bar, no alternative path** — not even a phone number or a store locator link to redirect foot traffic.

## Recommendations

- **Add a fallback error page with a store locator CTA** — if the site is down, at minimum show "Find your nearest Dick's store" with a zip-code input so a parent like me can still get to a physical location before game day.
- **Strip the raw error code and IP address** — replace with a generic incident reference (if needed for support) that means something to your ops team but doesn't alarm or confuse customers.
- **Soften the 12-hour window messaging** — "We're back soon — check again shortly" is less punishing than explicitly telling a time-pressed shopper to come back half a day from now.
- **Add a link to the ScoreCard app or loyalty portal** — even during a site outage, give rewards members somewhere to go so the relationship isn't just a dead wall.
