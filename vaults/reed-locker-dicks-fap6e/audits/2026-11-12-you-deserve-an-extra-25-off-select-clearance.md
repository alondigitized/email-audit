# You deserve an EXTRA 25% off select clearance!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud clearance send, and the hero does its job. A red panel with gold "EXTRA 25% OFF" fills the first screen. A "Limited Time" label and a "Shop Now" button sit right below it, and the offer registers in about a second. Unlike the 11/11 "The 11.11 Sale is HERE" and "LAST CALL" sends, which had the frozen dark clock hero, this one has no broken top banner.
- Below the hero it becomes a standard product catalog. Running shoes, Nike fleece, golf drivers, a Hydro Jug, ping-pong tables, racquet gear, NHL and a gift grid. Each block has its own Shop button. Only some of them tie back to the 25% hero offer, and the "Extra 25% off select clearance" copy is mostly lost once you scroll. It is the fourth "extra 25% off clearance" style send in two days. DICK'S is training subscribers to expect a discount at all times.
- Verdict: a solid, readable send with a strong hero, diluted by a long and unfocused body. Most of the value is in the top screen.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and high-contrast, so "EXTRA 25% OFF" reads instantly.
- One clear Shop Now button sits directly under the hero, with a "Limited Time" label.
- The "Order Today, Spent It Tomorrow" strip with ScoreCard points and free shipping at the top adds loyalty and convenience cues.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give easy navigation for different shoppers.
- Specific prices appear on several products, such as the Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 at $124.98 and the Monarch tennis table at $269.98.

## What's weak

- No deadline is shown. "Limited Time" with no date or countdown is weak urgency, and the earlier 11/10 and 11/9 sends had "Final Hours" countdown bars.
- The body has too many unrelated blocks (golf, NHL, tumblers, ping-pong), so the email loses focus after the hero.
- The "Hydro Jug Cocoa Croc" and "Just Launched" blocks are full price, not clearance, which undercuts the extra 25% message.
- The 25% off banner doesn't carry through the body, and the fine print under the hero is small and dense.
- The gift picks grid at the bottom has no prices and no visible CTAs.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the body match the hero. Show only clearance items with "was / now / extra 25% off" pricing, and cut the full-price launches.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or a countdown, in place of "Limited Time."
- 3. Cut the body from seven blocks to three or four, and give each one a CTA that names the product, such as "Shop Running Shoes."
- 4. Add sale prices to the gift grid, or drop it.
- 5. Cut the frequency. Three clearance-style sends in two days dulls the "extra" feeling.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your extra 25% off clearance, running shoes to golf`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brooks, Nike & golf drivers marked down further. Same-day pickup available.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% applies at checkout on select clearance. Ends Sunday.`

## 1. Technical Summary
Several confirmed gaps: the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars), 13 images lack alt text, and the probed tracked links, including Unsubscribe, returned 403. Header-dependent compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because the relay doesn't expose those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Six tracked links returned 403.** All are `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.FCdQQHz3…`, with link text captured as "If you have trouble viewing this content or are using a screen reader, please co…".
  - The `params` and `pid` values are identical across all six. Only `tuid` differs (`…be69f`, `…be69e`, `…be6a0`–`…be6a3`), so they resolve to the same destination.
  - A 403 on an ESP click-tracker is often bot-blocking of the probe rather than a dead link. This is unconfirmed, so verify with a real browser or mobile client.
- **Unsubscribe returned 403.** The target is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/click?params=b.v1.YyD_QsBYMHUt…`. The probe can't confirm it works, so a manual click-through is warranted (see section 5).
- **Link text looks mismatched.** Six identical-destination links all labeled with the "trouble viewing" copy suggests either an oversized anchor wrapping other content or mislabeled extraction. The source is truncated, so this can't be confirmed.
- **Coverage is partial.** Only 12 of 35 links were probed and 23 were skipped, so the 403 pattern may extend to unprobed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text fallback is effectively missing.** The QA check shows a 9-character text part (FAIL). This is a deliverability and accessibility defect.
- **13 images are missing alt text (QA WARN):**
  - Content images: `running-1`, `fleece-2`, `golf-5`, `1110-hydrojug-cocoa-croc-launch-banner`, `table-tennis`, `racquet`, `nhl`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The open-tracking pixel (`data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?…`, 1x1) also has no alt. This is minor but easy to fix with `alt=""`.
  - If the content images are linked, screen readers will announce raw URLs or filenames.

- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Check the raw message at the origin. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Replace the 9-character version with a multipart/alternative text body that includes the offer copy and links.
2. **Add alt text to the 13 flagged images.** Use descriptive alt for linked content images and `alt=""` for decorative icons and the tracking pixel.
3. **Re-test the 403 links** (the six `mclick` links and Unsubscribe) with a browser-like user agent or a real inbox. If they work there, treat the 403s as probe false positives. If not, escalate to the ESP.
4. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post`** (`List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`) in the raw headers at the origin, along with SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment.
5. **Check the six identical `mclick` anchors** and their text, to rule out an oversized wrapping anchor or a mislabeled "trouble viewing" link.

7. **Resolve the redirect chain** for the top CTAs to confirm UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
