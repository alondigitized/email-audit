# ⚡ Up to 50% OFF. ⚡ Hurry, these deals won't last — shop now...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-30**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Flash Sale / up to 50%" message DICK'S has sent in roughly 48 hours, and the hero has the same cropped-headline bug as this morning's "Flash Sale alert 🚨" send. A giant orange lightning-bolt banner shows only "FLA" clipped at the right edge. The lead visual of the email is about 400px of orange with a broken word, and the real offer, "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off," sits small underneath it.
- Below the hero the email is competent but diffuse. A "Final Hours" countdown (07:59:55) at the top is the strongest urgency device in the send. After that come eight or so category tiles: HOKA, drinkware, racquet, fan shop, FootJoy, plus a recycled "50% OFF" prairie hero from the 9/27 and 9/29 Deal Drop. Each tile has its own discount, so the eye has nowhere to land. The "50% off" promise is also weaker than it sounds, because it is "up to" and applies to select items.
- Verdict: a functional promo with a visible render bug in the most valuable pixels. The countdown and the real product grid are fine, but the hero wastes the open. Cadence fatigue is the bigger problem: a subscriber has seen "up to 50%" three times this week.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:55" countdown bar is a credible, visible urgency device right under the header.
- The footer carries ScoreCard and Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow messaging, so member and fulfillment benefits are present.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Halloween Shop) gives quick navigation.
- Specific product pricing, such as "Sale $119.99 HOKA Arahi 8" and "$30 Off Select FootJoy Golf Shoes," is more concrete than the headline offer.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid (adidas Samba, Nike, New Balance) shows real, recognizable products.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is truncated to "FLA" at the right edge, so the main creative is visibly broken.
- The hero is mostly empty orange space with faint bolt icons, and the real headline and CTA sit small below it.
- The "Shop Now" buttons are pale grey and low-contrast, so the CTAs don't pop against white.
- The email repeats "up to X% off" across seven modules, with no single anchor offer.
- The "50% OFF" prairie block is recycled from the 9/27 and 9/29 Deal Drop sends, and it duplicates the hero offer.
- The fine print is dense, and "Valid online only... select styles" undercuts the "hurry" framing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put "FLASH SALE: UP TO 50% OFF" fully inside the frame, with a high-contrast button in the hero itself. This is the single biggest lift.
- 2. Cut the modules from about eight to four. Lead with the 50% block, then two or three hero products with real prices.
- 3. Make the CTAs solid black or green buttons with specific copy, such as "Shop Flash Deals" rather than a generic "Shop Now."
- 4. Cut the send frequency. Don't repeat "up to 50%" three times in a week. Rotate offers, as the 9/27 "$25 & under" send did.
- 5. Surface ScoreCard member pricing near the top.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `HOKA from $119.99, FootJoy $30 off, 40% off YETI. Ends 11:59 PM PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Structurally sound table layout (`role="presentation"`, `lang="en"`, MSO conditionals, and a preheader spacer). However, the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars), 10 content images lack alt text, and the preheader appears unfilled. Header-level compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified through the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Tracking:** All links I can see go through the ESP redirector `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…&pid=…&tuid=…`. Its parameters are opaque, so destination URLs and any UTM tags can't be inspected from the source.
- **Probe coverage:** Only 12 of 62 links were probed and 50 were skipped by design (footer, utility, social). No broken links were reported among the 12, but the other 50 are unverified.
- **Open pixel:** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` is present, which is expected. It was flagged only for missing alt, and `alt=""` is the correct fix.
- No confirmed broken or malformed links.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback:** The text part is 9 chars. That is effectively no text alternative, which hurts text-only clients, screen-reader and accessibility fallbacks, and spam scoring.
- **[WARN] Missing alt text on 10 images:**
  - Promo/hero images: `2026-0930-dks-storewide-4pm-1` through `-4-new`, and `fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`. These are served from `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/…`, and the last two are `…/is/content/…?cb=…`. If they carry the offer text, it disappears with images off or in a screen reader.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These need either descriptive alt or `alt=""` if decorative.
  - Tracking pixel: `open` needs `alt=""`.
- **Preheader appears unfilled:** `<td id="ssl">` contains the leftover template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, followed by `#pht-spacer` holding only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. No preheader text is visible in the source I have (truncated), so inbox clients may pull arbitrary body or alt text as the preview.
- **Image weight (unmeasured):** Hero images request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` (JPEG at max quality, about 2x the 600px layout width). This is likely heavy on mobile, but I have no byte sizes to confirm.
- **Minor:** `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` has no effect in email clients.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[[…]]`) appear in the available source. The source is truncated, so the footer wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found:** The QA notes say the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both, so check them against the raw original message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not found:** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this capture. The sender `info@em.dcsg.com` and the tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com` share the `dcsg.com` organizational domain, which is favorable for alignment, but that is not proof.
- The footer (physical address, in-body unsubscribe link) is outside the truncated source, so it couldn't be verified here.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
- **Not verifiable from the source:** UTM parameters and final landing URLs sit behind the encoded `mclick` redirect, and the QA output includes no redirect-chain results.
- **No evidence of a mismatch:** The probe reported no failures on the 12 material links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Ship a real multipart plain-text alternative that includes the offer and primary CTA links. This is the only hard FAIL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to the five promo images (mirroring the on-image offer text). Use `alt=""` on the five icons if decorative, and `alt=""` on the tracking pixel.
3. Fill the preheader in `#ssl` and remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` template comment.
4. Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message, and confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass from the original headers rather than the relay copy.
5. Follow the `mclick` redirects for the top CTAs to confirm UTM tagging and landing-page alignment.
6. Reduce hero image weight (for example `qlt=80` and right-sized dimensions) after measuring actual byte sizes.
