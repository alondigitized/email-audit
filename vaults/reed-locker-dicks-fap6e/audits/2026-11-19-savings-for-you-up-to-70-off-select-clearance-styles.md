# 🎁 Savings for you: up to 70% off select clearance styles!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-19**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong hero attached to a bloated body. The red "UP TO 70% OFF" block is huge and unmistakable, and the single "Shop Now" button under it is clear. Anyone who opens the email knows the offer in about a second.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. I count a category pill row, then HOKA, The North Face, Walter Hagen, Stanley, Solo Stove, basketball hoops, NHL and Chirp. After those come a text-alert promo and a gift-picks grid. Most of these modules have little to do with clearance, and each has its own red button. The eye has no reason to keep scrolling, and the 70%-off story is dropped after the first screen.
- It's also the fourth near-identical clearance send in about four days. It follows 11/16's "Limited time! ⏰ Up to 70% OFF…", 11/17's "Up to 70% OFF select clearance? Yes, please!" and 11/18's "Right this way >>>". The "Limited Time" tag has no actual deadline, so it's easy to ignore. Competent execution, but fatigue is the main risk.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- The single "Shop Now" button directly under the hero keeps the primary action clear.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop) let shoppers self-sort.
- The Black Friday-season imagery (holiday lights, drinkware, gift picks) fits the moment.
- The "BOGO 50% Off" Walter Hagen module and the "$20 off $100+" text-alert offer give concrete secondary hooks.

## What's weak

- The "Limited Time" tag has no date or countdown, so the urgency isn't credible. Clearance-fatigued subscribers will skip it.
- The body is too long. About eight product modules, a text-alert banner and a gift grid dilute the clearance message.
- Most secondary modules (HOKA, Stanley, Solo Stove, hoops) don't show clearance pricing. The "Up to 70% off" promise isn't visibly carried through.
- Many identical red "Shop Now" buttons compete with each other and with the hero.
- The fine print is dense and the "**" footnotes are small, which weakens the honesty of the offer.
- It repeats the same offer as three sends in the past four days.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to hero, one clearance product grid with visible sale prices, and a single secondary offer. Move the lifestyle modules to a separate send.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59pm ET". That does more than the vague "Limited Time".
- 3. Show "was/now" prices on the products in the email. It proves the 70% claim and gives shoppers something to click.
- 4. Make the sub-CTAs specific, for example "Shop HOKA clearance" instead of "Shop Now".
- 5. Pause or space the clearance cadence. Consider segmenting recent clickers away from this send.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
