# ⚡ Flash SALE = Up to 50% off! ⚡
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the ninth DICK'S email in roughly four days, and it's a carbon copy of the May 30 "Flash Sale begins NOW!" send that already scored 7/10 — same sub-brand sender name, same 50% hero promise, same multi-lane product structure. The only visible creative change is the hero shifting from the red clearance palette to an orange/amber gradient. That's a cosmetic pivot, not a meaningful one.
- The email itself is competent. The orange hero lands first, the offer hierarchy reads top-down, and the multi-category grid (Nike shoes → adidas clothing → women's/kids' → life hacks → golf BOGO) gives every shopper something to land on. The problem is none of that matters when the inbox looks like a spam folder by this point in the week.
- What kills this send isn't what's in the email — it's what was in the inbox for the eight emails before it. A subscriber who opened Monday through Thursday has already seen "up to 50% off" six times. The marginal open rate on send nine is almost certainly below 10%.

## What's working

- Orange hero differentiates visually from the red clearance template, reducing the "same email again" reflex scan.
- Multi-lane category structure (shoes, clothing, women's, life hacks, golf) gives this email genuine range across the assortment.
- The "Buy Two, Get One Free" Russell Brown Golf Balls callout is the most specific, unique offer in the email — concrete product, concrete mechanic.
- "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" as a secondary module lets clearance hunters layer deals without the hero feeling diluted.
- Clean render — no broken images, no overlapping elements, hierarchy reads correctly.

## What's weak

- "Flash Sale Up to 50% Off" hero overstates: most section-level offers are 25–40% off, so the 50% headline only technically holds for a narrow slice of the assortment.
- No deadline anywhere visible — "Flash Sale" implies urgency but there's no "ends Sunday" or countdown to make it credible.
- The scattered percentage callouts (25%, 30%, 30%, 50%, 40%) across sections create decision paralysis rather than a clear entry point.
- This is send nine in four days under the same brand family — cadence alone is eroding whatever goodwill the 25% offers still carry.
- No personalization signal — same generic multi-category grid Walker has seen repeatedly this week.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a hard deadline to the hero** — "Ends tonight at midnight" or a visible countdown is the single change that would move the needle most. Flash Sale without a deadline is just a sale.
- 2. **Pick one lead category per send** — stop trying to cover all five verticals in one email. A footwear-only flash sale would have tighter copy, cleaner layout, and more credibility on the percentage claim.
- 3. **Make the BOGO golf balls the hero, not a buried module** — it's the most specific, distinct offer in the email; leading with it would differentiate from every prior send this week.
- 4. **Compress the email** — four category lanes max; cut life hacks and the product thumbnail grid entirely. The scroll depth required here is killing mobile click-through.
- 5. **Merge or suppress the clearance module** — layering "Extra 25% off clearance" inside a "Flash Sale up to 50% off" email confuses the offer logic and dilutes the flash urgency.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale: Nike, adidas + golf BOGO — today only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, clothing, women's and more — shop before it's gone.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Buy 2 golf balls, get 1 free. Plus 30% off Nike and adidas.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods Flash Sale Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Epsilon/Harmony ESP send with MSO conditional comments and responsive breakpoints intact. The primary technical issues are invalid JSON-LD syntax that will break Gmail Promotions tab rendering, and stale/empty structured data fields.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No broken links confirmed in visible source.** All tracked links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` (Epsilon redirect), and the open-tracking pixel is at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...` — both consistent with Epsilon Harmony ESP.

**Potential issue — UTM parameters not visible in truncated source.** The logo CTA redirect URL is opaque (encoded token). Cannot confirm whether UTM params (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`) survive the redirect to `dickssportinggoods.com`. This should be spot-checked at the destination. See §6.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `role="presentation"` applied to all layout tables — correct.
- `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override present — prevents iOS auto-detection of phone/address links hijacking link styles.
- MSO conditional comments present for Outlook table rendering — correct.
- Web font (`Archivo` via `fonts.gstatic.com`) declared inside `@media screen` — correct pattern; Arial fallback is defined.
- **Logo `alt` attribute**: Not visible in truncated source. Must confirm `<img id="logo">` carries a non-empty `alt`. If blank, screen readers and image-blocked clients get nothing for the primary brand element.
- **Tracking pixel hidden via inline style only** (`display:none; max-height:0px`): Missing `mso-hide:all` on the `<img>` itself (it appears on a sibling comment, not the tag). Outlook may render a 1px gap.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

Multiple unfilled template fields detected in the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block:

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Empty |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | **Stale — 6-year-old date, never updated from template default** |

No dynamic subscriber-level merge tokens (first name, loyalty tier, etc.) are visible in the header section.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe footer**: Not present in the visible (header) portion of the HTML. Given the source is truncated, these must be confirmed present in the footer. Both are legally required.

**Sending domain**: `DSG@e.dcsg.com` (`e.dcsg.com` is Epsilon's subdomain for DICK'S). SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment should be verified via header inspection — not derivable from HTML alone.

**One confirmed issue**: The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments (see excerpt below). **This is invalid JSON** and will cause the Gmail structured data parser to throw a parse error, silently dropping all schema.org annotations.

```json
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
// Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```

This is not a compliance issue per se, but it is a breakage that disables Gmail Promotions tab deal cards.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**Cannot fully confirm** due to truncated source and encoded redirect URLs. Key items to check:

- Resolve one `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` link and confirm landing page URL carries `utm_source=email&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=<campaign_id>` or equivalent.
- Confirm the sale landing page reflects the "up to 50% off" claim — if the sale has already ended or the discount differs, this is a continuity failure.
- `availabilityStarts: 2020-03-06` in JSON-LD is actively misleading to any system (Gmail, Google Shopping) that parses it.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before send / flag as defect if already sent:**
1. **Remove `//` comments from the JSON-LD block.** They are invalid JSON and silently break all Gmail structured data features.
2. **Populate or null out empty JSON-LD fields** (`discountCode`, `description`, `availabilityEnds`, `image`). Empty strings in required fields degrade schema quality.
3. **Correct `availabilityStarts`** — update from `2020-03-06` to the actual sale start date.

**P1 — Verify before send:**
4. Confirm logo `<img alt="">` is not blank.
5. Spot-check one tracked CTA link; verify UTM parameters are present on the destination URL.
6. Confirm CAN-SPAM footer (physical address + unsubscribe) exists in the non-truncated HTML.

**P2 — Minor:**
7. Add `mso-hide:all` directly to the tracking pixel `<img>` tag to suppress the Outlook gap.
8. Validate SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment on `e.dcsg.com` via a mail header check (`mail-tester.com` or `mxtoolbox`).
