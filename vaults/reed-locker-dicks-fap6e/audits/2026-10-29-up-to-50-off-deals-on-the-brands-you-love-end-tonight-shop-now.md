# Up to 50% off 🤝 deals on the brands you love end TONIGHT ⏰ (shop now!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-29**
## Executive summary

- This is a "ONE DAY" flash-sale send. It repeats the black-and-white "ONE DAY" hero from the earlier 10/29 send, "One Day. One Amazing SALE starts now!". Same day, same creative, new subject line. The hero promises "24 Hours of Online-Only Deals" with "Up to 50% Off", and the subject adds a real "ends TONIGHT" deadline.
- The email has two offers fighting for the eye. The "ONE DAY / up to 50%" block sits on top. Directly below it is a huge red "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance banner with a different deadline (10/31). Both have a Shop Now button, and the fine print under each is tiny. A reader can't tell which deal is the real one. The lower half (Fan Shop, Halloween Shop, Frost Buddy, the 6-product grid) reads like a catalog. It dilutes a send that is supposed to be urgent.
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send, but the third DICK'S flash/clearance beat in a week. The "tonight" urgency is credible, but the layout is too long and the offers aren't prioritized.

## What's working

- The "ONE DAY" hero is huge and high-contrast. The eye lands on it first, and the "24 Hours" headline backs up the subject's urgency.
- The red "Shop Now" button is clear and easy to find.
- The ScoreCard bar at the top shows "300 Points = $10 Reward", so loyalty is visible.
- The Halloween Shop and Frost Buddy x Big League Chew tiles fit the season.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Fan Shop) make it easy to jump to a section.
- The email has no visible render bugs. All images load and the layout is clean.

## What's weak

- Two competing deals, "Up to 50%" and "Extra 25%", have different deadlines and no clear winner.
- The 50% hero is "up to", with no product, brand or category named. The subject's "brands you love" never shows up in the body.
- The fine print is dense and tiny, with exclusions and "prices as marked" caveats. Nothing in the email visually matches the subject's "TONIGHT" urgency, because there is no countdown or time-of-day.
- The email is very long, with three separate Shop Now buttons and a product grid with no prices or sale tags.
- The subject is 70 characters with two emojis and an exclamation point, so it will truncate on mobile.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one offer for this send, either the 24-hour deals or the extra 25% clearance. Cut or demote the other to a single text line.
- 2. Add "Ends 11:59 PM PT" inside the hero, or a countdown graphic.
- 3. Name the brands in the hero (Nike, adidas, Under Armour) so the "brands you love" promise pays off. Add sale prices or "% off" tags to the product grid.
- 4. Trim the lower half. Drop the Halloween and Frost Buddy blocks, or move them to a different send.
- 5. Cut the subject to one emoji and under 50 characters.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Online-only prices on top brands, gone at 11:59 PM PT. Plus an extra 25% off clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's HTML is structurally sound: it uses `role="presentation"` tables, `lang="en"`, an MSO conditional block and a viewport meta tag. It has one confirmed failure (an effectively empty plain-text part, 9 chars) and several accessibility gaps (missing alt text on banner and icon images). Header-based compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** 12 material links were probed and none were flagged. 49 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so those are unverified.
- The truncated HTML shows no `href` values (anchors appear as `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`), so I couldn't audit link targets, redirect chains or wrapping.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...&tuid=6ae25329f225c53e60973a22&configId=512ffb51-...">` is `width="1" height="1"` with no `alt` and no `style`/`border` attributes. It is functional and sits at the top of `<body>`, but it is flagged by the QA check. Add `alt=""`.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed), meaningful images:**
  - `1029-outdoor-frost-buddy-banner`
  - `halloween-banner-2026-0911-d`
  - If these banners carry the offer text, the "up to 50% off… ends TONIGHT" message is invisible with images off or to screen readers.
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed), icons:**
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`
  - If they are decorative, they need `alt=""` rather than no attribute.
- **Empty preheader:** `<div id="pht-spacer">` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding and no preview text. The `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder comment is still in `td#ssl`, so the preheader slot looks unfilled. Inbox previews will pull from body content instead.
- **Leftover editor artifacts:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` and `<link rel="icon" ...favicon.ico>` in `<head>`. Neither has any effect in email clients, so they are cleanup only.
- **Images:** the Scene7 banner URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100`. The truncated source doesn't show the rendered dimensions, so payload weight is unconfirmed.
- **Positives:** the web font has an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback and `font-display:swap`. The Outlook-specific `mso` block is present.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. There are no unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`) in the visible HTML. The source is truncated, so the footer and later modules weren't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058): not found.** The QA tool itself notes the relay may strip these headers, so this is unverified rather than a confirmed violation. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both for senders at this volume. Check the raw message headers at the origin.
- **Authentication-Results: not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `info@em.dcsg.com` are unknown from this data.
- **Plain-text part: FAIL.** The text alternative is 9 characters. This is a deliverability risk (spam scoring) and an accessibility gap for text-only clients.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** neither appears in the truncated source, so I can't confirm them. The QA probe is said to always include an unsubscribe link and reported no failure on it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but UTMs and landing pages couldn't be verified because the `href` values are not visible in the truncated source and the QA output reports no link failures. The `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` attribute suggests some links are app deep links, so fallback behavior for users without the app is worth testing.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part (FAIL).** Generate a real multipart/alternative text body with the offer, key links and unsubscribe URL.
2. **Add alt text to the two banner images** with the actual offer copy. Add `alt=""` to the five icons and the tracking pixel if they are decorative.
3. **Verify headers at the origin sender** (not the relay): `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC results.
4. **Populate the preheader** in `#ssl`/`#pht-spacer` with real text ahead of the padding characters.
5. **Remove editor artifacts** (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`, the `<link rel="icon">`).
6. **Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** to check hrefs, UTM consistency, the footer unsubscribe link and the postal address. Probe the 49 skipped links as well.
