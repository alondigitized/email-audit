# Flash Sale alert! 🚨 Up to 50% off is yours... but not for long 👉
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same green-locker-room "one-day sale" hero DICK'S has now run repeatedly this month — the padlock/locker imagery echoes 8/16's and 8/20's "Up to 50% off" sends almost exactly, just retitled "Flash Sale" this time. The subject line's urgency ("but not for long 👉") isn't backed up by the body — there's no countdown, no end-time above the fold, and the sale copy underneath just says "Valid online through 8/22/26" without a clock. It's another sprawling digest: flash sale hero, then extra 25% off clearance, ScoreCard member week, bonus points, several unrelated product carousels (golf balls, soccer gear, backpacks), and an app-download pitch before you even reach the footer.
- The "FLASH SALE" hero graphic itself is legible and the CTA ("Shop Now") is clear and immediately visible, which is a real strength. But the email buries its own headline offer under five more competing promos of similar visual weight, so a reader skimming on mobile has no obvious second stop after the hero — everything after is noise competing for the same click.
- Verdict: solid hero, wasted urgency claim, same fatigue problem as every other DICK'S send this week. 5/10 territory, consistent with the run.

## What's working

- Hero image renders cleanly — bold "FLASH SALE" wordmark over a legible locker photo, no image breakage.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") sits directly under the offer with high contrast, no scrolling needed on mobile.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports) give an immediate secondary path for a shopper not tied to one section.
- Extra 25% off clearance block uses a bold, unmissable orange/black treatment that's easy to spot when skimming.

## What's weak

- Subject promises urgency ("not for long") but the body shows no countdown, no bolded end-time, and no visual urgency cue anywhere near the hero — the claim isn't earned.
- "Flash Sale" framing gets diluted almost immediately by five more offers (25% clearance, 20% member week, 50 bonus points, golf balls, soccer gear) all competing for top billing.
- This is visually near-identical to 8/16 and 8/20's locker/lock-themed "Up to 50%" sends — a subscriber who's opened either of those will recognize the same shell instantly.
- No specific product, price, or size/fit detail is shown for the flash sale itself — "shop hundreds of deals" is vague compared to how concrete the clearance/golf-ball blocks below it are.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add real urgency to the hero — a countdown timer or explicit "Ends 11:59PM PT tonight" directly under the "FLASH SALE" wordmark, since the subject's "not for long" currently has nothing to back it up.
- 2. Cut the digest down — pick one flash-sale hero plus one secondary offer (clearance) and move ScoreCard, bonus points, golf balls, and soccer gear to a follow-up send; six competing offers dilutes the one the subject promised.
- 3. Swap the locker/padlock imagery for something new — it's the third near-identical hero this month and repeat subscribers will register it as stale before reading the copy.
- 4. Put one concrete product or price callout in the flash-sale hero itself (e.g., "Shoes from $19.99") instead of the vague "shop hundreds of deals."

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends 11:59PM PT — new markdowns just dropped`

# Technical Email Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Flash Sale"

**1. Technical Summary**
Email built on an Epsilon Harmony/PeopleSoft-style ESP (app.dickssportinggoods.com tracking domain) with MSO conditional comments and Archivo web fonts; automated QA shows a broken unsubscribe link, missing authentication/compliance headers, and widespread missing alt text (43% pass rate).

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Unsubscribe link is broken**: resolves to `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a028f283509adf2d369fea4b34/13e005cf9a1f494f0000021ef3a0bd03` → redirects to an error page: `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` ("unknown url type"). This is a functional bug, not just a compliance gap — flagged FAIL by QA.
- All sampled CTA/header links use the same `/T/{campaign-id}/{tracking-id}/{recipient-guid}?__dU__={token}` redirect pattern (e.g. logo link, Scorecard link) — consistent structure, no malformed tokens observed in the sampled links.
- QA probed 12 of 68 total links (material/high-click-likelihood ones); 56 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not verified — unsubscribe was probed by design and is the one that failed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- 10 images flagged with missing `alt` attributes, including the SSL/open-tracking pixel, three merchandising banners (`caliavrst0822-storewi`, `cozy0822stwd0822-storewi`, `taylormade0822-storewi`, `soccer0822-storewi`), and five footer icons (SMS, SportsMatter, BPG, favorite, location). Decorative icons (footer) should use `alt=""`; merchandising banners need descriptive alt text for screen readers.
- MSO conditional blocks and `@media screen` font-face/mobile rules are present, indicating standard Outlook/mobile-client fallback handling — no structural rendering issues found in the visible source.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or broken personalization syntax found in the visible HTML.
- No issues found.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is non-functional** (see §2) — this is a CAN-SPAM risk since the link errors out instead of processing opt-out.
- `List-Unsubscribe` header not present (or not captured by the relay) — no verifiable one-click unsubscribe support at the header level.
- `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header (RFC 8058) not found — Gmail/Yahoo one-click unsubscribe is not confirmed functional.
- `Authentication-Results` header not found via the relay, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be confirmed from available data. Flag as unknown, not as a failure — this may be a capture limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than an actual auth gap.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- All observed links use the ESP's proprietary redirect/token scheme (`__dU__=...`) rather than plain UTM query params, so UTM continuity can't be assessed directly from the HTML — attribution presumably happens server-side after the redirect resolves.
- Cannot verify landing-page alignment for the promoted "Flash Sale / 50% off" offer since destination URLs are opaque tracking redirects; would need to follow redirects to confirm final landing pages match the sale being advertised.

**7. Recommendations**
1. **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — it currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` instead of the subscription-management page. This is the highest-priority issue (functional + compliance risk).
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers (or confirm they're being sent but stripped by the relay) to support one-click unsubscribe per RFC 8058 and mailbox-provider requirements (Gmail/Yahoo bulk sender rules).
3. Add `alt` text to the 4 merchandising banner images (descriptive) and the 5 footer icon images (can be `alt=""` if purely decorative) and the tracking pixel (`alt=""`).
4. Confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment directly with the sending ESP, since the relay-based check couldn't capture `Authentication-Results`.
5. If UTM-based attribution is desired for site-side analytics, verify the ESP's redirect tokens ultimately append UTM params on the landing page, or add them explicitly.
