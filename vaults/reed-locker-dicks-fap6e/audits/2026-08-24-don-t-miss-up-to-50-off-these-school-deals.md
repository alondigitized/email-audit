# Don't miss up to 50% off these school deals ✏️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same green-locker-room "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has run all month, now retitled "Back-to-School Deals" with the padlock/locker imagery from 8/16, 8/20, and 8/22 swapped for a school-locker scene — same layout, same DSG/Nike/Under Armour badge row, same green tone. It at least has a real reason to exist right now (school shopping season), and it's more product-diverse than most of this brand's recent sends — racquet gear, golf, weighted vests, camping, then a legitimate Back to School Essentials grid with backpacks, water bottles, socks, and kids' apparel.
- But it's another long digest scroll with two competing hero offers (50% off up top, 60% off clearance further down), no single clear CTA, and yet another "up to X% off" headline with no anchor price or example item. Compared to 8/21's cleaner "EXTRA 25% off select clearance" single-hero send, this reverts to sprawl.
- Judgement: functional but generic — the Back to School framing and the essentials grid are the only things differentiating this from DICK'S' last five nearly-identical emails.

## What's working

- The Back to School Essentials grid at the bottom shows real, specific products (Nike backpack, Owala water bottle, kids' Nike tee) with names and photos — the most concrete part of the email.
- Seasonal framing ("Back-to-School Deals," "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" same-day delivery callout) gives this send an actual occasion, unlike the generic flash-sale repeats.
- ScoreCard points banner up top is visible and relevant to loyalty members.
- No render bugs — images, buttons, and text all display cleanly.

## What's weak

- Same locker-room/"UP TO 50% OFF" creative template as 8/16, 8/20, 8/22 — subscribers who've opened those will recognize this instantly as a reskin.
- Two separate "up to X% off" hero blocks (50% at top, 60% clearance mid-email) split attention and dilute urgency instead of reinforcing one offer.
- No specific dollar amount or example item tied to the headline offer — "up to 50% off" with no anchor product undercuts credibility, same critique as prior sends.
- Long scroll with 6+ CTAs (racquet gear, putters, vests, camping, clearance, essentials) means no single unambiguous action for the reader.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with one concrete Back-to-School item and price (e.g., "Nike Backpacks from $XX") instead of a generic "Up to 50% Off" — this is the single biggest lift since the current hero is indistinguishable from four other August sends.
- 2. Cut the racquet gear/golf/camping sections or move them below the fold entirely — they're off-theme for a "Back to School" subject line and dilute the promise made in the subject.
- 3. Move the Back to School Essentials grid to the top, right under the hero — it's the most relevant, concrete content in the email and currently requires a full scroll to reach.
- 4. Collapse the 50%-off and 60%-off clearance blocks into one offer hierarchy so there's a single primary CTA, not two competing "up to X% off" claims.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Backpacks, water bottles, and first-day fits — all in one order.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Same-day delivery on everything your kid needs for day one.`
