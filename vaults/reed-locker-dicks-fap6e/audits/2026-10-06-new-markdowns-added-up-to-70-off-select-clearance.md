# New markdowns added! 🚨 Up to 70% OFF select clearance
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-06**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clean clearance blast. A giant "UP TO 70% OFF" sits over autumn leaves, with "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The hero is the best of DICK'S recent run: a bigger number than the 50% in the "Fall Deal Drop" sends of 10/5 and 10/6.
- The body is a long scroll of seven or eight modules. The "Fall Deal Drop / 3 Days of Savings" block is the same creative as the "Ends TOMORROW ⏳" send earlier today. The email has two competing headline offers (70% and 50%) and a pile of category tiles. A subscriber who got three promos in the last two days will read this as more of the same, with a bigger number.
- Verdict: a strong hero on a bloated, repetitive template. Fine for a clearance push, but it's the fourth near-identical DICK'S send in four days.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is huge and is the first thing the eye lands on.
- The "Limited Time" label, headline and Shop Now button sit right under the hero.
- The 70% beats the 50% and "$25 & under" hooks from 10/3–10/6, so the escalation is credible.
- The render is clean, with no broken images, overlaps or empty gaps.
- The ScoreCard $10/$20 Bonus Reward block gives members a real extra incentive.
- The "Explore more deals" chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let shoppers jump to their own category.

## What's weak

- Two offers compete. The Fall Deal Drop module ("Up to 50% off") undercuts the 70% hero, and it's the same creative as the sends of 10/5 and earlier today.
- Every tile has a different "up to" percentage (40%, 30%, 30%, 55%, 10%), so no single story comes through.
- The body is very long with tiny fine print, and the fine print qualifies the offer ("Limited time", "select styles", "while quantities last").
- The Shop Now buttons are pale grey, and the tile CTAs are weak.
- The pick-your-product grid at the bottom (adidas Samba, Nike hoodie, socks) has no prices, so there's no reason to click.
- The Fall Deal Drop module says "3 Days of Savings" with a 10/7 deadline. The hero says "Limited Time" with no date, so the urgency is inconsistent.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the 70% clearance story. Drop the Fall Deal Drop block, which is the repeated creative, or move it to its own send.
- 2. Put a real end date on the hero ("Ends 10/7" or "This weekend only") so the urgency is as credible as the deal.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" with "Shop Clearance up to 70% Off", and make the buttons high-contrast green.
- 4. Add sale prices (e.g. "was $120, now $39") to the product picks, and use clearance items instead of full-price ones.
- 5. Group the tiles by shopper intent, one row each for shoes, apparel and team gear, and cut the tile count in half.
- 6. Add a customer rating or a "only X left" line to build trust.

The HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, a `<title>`, a hidden preheader, MSO conditionals and `role="presentation"` layout tables. One confirmed failure is the missing plain-text alternative (9 chars). Ten content images and the open pixel lack alt text. Several compliance and authentication checks couldn't be verified because of the relay and the truncated source.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Open pixel:** It is served from `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`. It is functional but has no alt text (see section 3).
- **No broken links reported:** The QA data lists no failures among the probed links.

- **Image-blocking impact:** The five `stw-4pm-*` images (1200×1600) are likely content or CTA tiles. If they are linked and carry no alt text, image-blocking clients get empty links and screen readers have nothing to announce.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[[ ]]`, `${}`) appear in the visible source. The HTML is truncated, so this can't be confirmed for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part missing or too short (9 chars).** This is a confirmed deliverability and accessibility gap. A multipart/alternative message with a real text body is expected. A 9-character text part is a common spam-filter signal.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.**
  - The QA tool notes the relay may strip these headers.
  - If they are truly absent, one-click unsubscribe is missing. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it.
  - Treat this as unverified, not confirmed.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` can't be assessed from this data. This is a relay limitation, not a sender fault.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** Neither is visible in the truncated source. The QA note says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result is shown. This is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. All destinations sit behind `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick` redirects, and no landing-page probe results are in the QA data. The links carry a `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` attribute, which suggests app deep-linking. Fallback behavior for non-app users is unverified.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** It should have the offer copy, the main CTA URLs, the unsubscribe URL and the postal address. This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Add descriptive alt text** to the five `stw-4pm-*` tiles, for example the category and offer. Add `alt=""` to the decorative `icon-vc-*` and `icon-location-2025-1` icons if they sit next to visible text labels. Add `alt=""` to the open pixel.
3. **Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`** on the raw message at the origin, bypassing the relay. Also confirm SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Re-run the audit with the full, untruncated HTML** and a wider link probe. That would verify the unsubscribe link, the physical address, merge tokens and the UTM/landing-page parameters behind the redirect wrapper.
