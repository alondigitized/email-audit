# Have you seen these sales yet? 👀 Hurry, Deal Days won't last!
**Score:** 9/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-25**
## Executive summary

- This is the tenth-plus installment of DICK'S Deal Days across three days, and it reads that way. The subject line has retreated from the campaign's strongest moments — the June 25 send that named specific brands ("VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest & more!") scored 9/10 in part because it gave fatigued subscribers a concrete reason to look. "Have you seen these sales yet?" does the opposite: it's a rhetorical shrug that asks the subscriber to do the work.
- Inside, the email is genuinely well-executed. The EXTRA 30% Off Select Clearance section in orange pops hard against the green DEAL DAYS branding, the "TODAY ONLY" urgency badges on the 40% CALIA and 30% Sling Bag deals are credible, and the layout renders cleanly with no bugs. The strongest value in the email is buried toward the bottom, though — the 10% Off Fan Shop opener anchors the eye on the weakest discount in the send.
- The fundamental issue isn't the creative, it's the campaign clock. A subscriber who has already received nine Deal Days emails sees the same DICK'S DEAL DAYS hero, the same green banner, the same "SHOP NOW" buttons, and an uninspired subject asking if they've noticed something they've been emailed about daily. The opens and clicks will come from the truly motivated buyers; everyone else is scrolling past.

## What's working

- The **EXTRA 30% Off Select Clearance** section breaks the visual pattern with an orange/red treatment — it's the one moment that stops the eye mid-scroll.
- **"TODAY ONLY" urgency badges** on the 40% CALIA and 30% Sling Bag deals are earned and specific, not general campaign language.
- **Visual hierarchy** is sound — the DEAL DAYS hero orients immediately, and the category tabs (MEN'S, WOMEN'S, YOUTH, SHOES) give quick navigation context.
- **Email renders cleanly** — no broken images, no overlapping text, no layout gaps.
- The **product grid at the bottom** ("Must-have gear for an epic summer!") provides effective browse-to-discover coverage for undecided shoppers.

## What's weak

- **The Fan Shop 10% off lead deal is a weak hook** — opening on the smallest discount (with a double-asterisk exclusion flag) right after the hero sets a low ceiling on perceived value.
- **Subject line is a soft question** — "Have you seen these sales yet?" asks subscribers to confirm what DICK'S already knows; compare to the naming approach from the June 25 9/10 send that converted better.
- **Subject at ~62 characters** is over the 50-char mobile truncation threshold, and the strongest part ("Deal Days won't last!") risks getting cut.
- **Visually indistinguishable from the previous nine sends** — the hero creative, color palette, and layout haven't changed, so campaign-fatigued subscribers get no visual signal that this is worth opening.
- **10% Off VRST Golf Fairway Shorts** feels like category padding alongside the 40% CALIA deal — two 10% offers in one email undercut the stronger discounts' perceived size.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the clearance offer in the subject** — "Extra 30% Off Clearance" is the clearest, broadest discount in the email and belongs at the top of the hierarchy, not buried after golf shorts.
- 2. **Move the 40% CALIA and Extra 30% Off sections above the 10% deals** — discount order should be descending, not ascending; the eye anchors on what it sees first.
- 3. **Drop one of the 10% offers** — the Fan Shop 10% and VRST Golf 10% dilute the email's discount story. One specific 10% deal can stay; two makes the whole send feel stingy.
- 4. **Add one visual differentiator to the hero** — a countdown timer, a "Day 3" badge, or a "Final 24 hours" lockup would signal to subscribers that this is a new and distinct send, not a duplicate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 30% off clearance — Deal Days ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `40% off CALIA + clearance drops: final Deal Days hours`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `TODAY ONLY: 40% off CALIA women's styles + extra 30% off clearance picks`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your last shot at Deal Days savings — clearance is 30% extra off right now`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Deal Days" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is built on Epsilon Harmony with correct MSO/Outlook conditional blocks and responsive CSS media queries. Critical issue: the JSON-LD structured data block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments, which is invalid JSON and will silently fail to parse in every consumer (Gmail promotions tab annotations, validators, schema processors).

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

- **Click tracking:** All CTAs route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/{subscriber_token}/...` — standard Epsilon redirect. No bare destination URLs are exposed. Final landing pages and UTM parameters are not inspectable from HTML source alone.
- **Open pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f0079b1c28f4e286e96638918/cccd6b77f54e419000004c5a42963aa1` — correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden`.
- **Logo image URL is truncated** in provided source (`https://dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/_phoenix/_dev/AEM/img/headers/brand-logo--dk…`). Cannot confirm the `<img src>` is complete. If truncation reflects the live send, the logo will render broken.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- **Missing `alt` attribute on logo `<img id="logo">`** — visible in the truncated header block. Image-blocking clients (Outlook default, many corporate proxies) will render blank or broken-image text. WCAG 2.1 §1.1.1 failure.
- **`border-collapse:separate` + `border-radius:8px 8px 0px 0px`** on the header table: correct for WebKit/Blink rendering, but Outlook ignores `border-radius` entirely. The MSO conditional block does not include a VML rounded-corner fallback, so Outlook renders sharp corners. Low severity but worth noting for fidelity.
- **Outer table `width="600"` attribute vs. `max-width:600px` style:** The hardcoded `width` attribute takes precedence over `max-width` in older clients (Outlook 2016 and below), potentially causing horizontal scroll on narrow viewports that don't honor the CSS. Outer structural tables should use `width="100%"` with `max-width` enforced via CSS only.
- **MSO conditional comment has a syntax artifact:** The `<style>` block inside `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` ends with a stray `"` inside the `div` rule: `padding:0;"` — the trailing quote is technically outside the value. Most parsers recover, but it's a defect.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**JSON-LD block contains multiple unfilled template fields:**

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Empty — annotation won't render |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | **Stale by 6+ years** |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty — designer comment `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!` left in production send |

These fields should either be populated with campaign-specific values or the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` types should be removed from the block entirely.

---

### 5. Compliance

- **JSON-LD contains JavaScript `//` comments** — JSON does not support comments. The entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block will fail to parse. This also means Gmail promotional annotation fields (`DiscountOffer`, `PromotionCard`) will not display in the Promotions tab. Evidence:

  ```json
  { // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
    "@type": "PromotionCard",
    // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
    "image": "" }
  ```

- **Unsubscribe link and physical mailing address** — not present in the provided HTML excerpt. CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5) requires a valid postal address; §5(a)(3) requires a functioning opt-out mechanism. Cannot confirm compliance from truncated source — needs full HTML review.
- **Sending domain:** `DSG@e.dcsg.com` — dedicated subdomain is standard practice for SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Authentication headers (DKIM signature, SPF record alignment) are not verifiable from HTML source; must be checked via raw message headers.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

- All destination URLs are wrapped in the Epsilon click tracker, so UTM parameters on final landing pages cannot be confirmed from HTML. Recommend spot-checking 2–3 tracked links by following the redirect chain to verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` are appended.
- The `id="ssl"` tracking beacon fires on open via a 0×0 hidden `<img>` — this is consistent with Epsilon's standard open-rate attribution mechanism.
