# NEW markdowns! Up to 70% off select clearance is worth a look 👀
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the same clearance email as yesterday's "New markdowns added! 🚨 Up to 70% OFF" (10/6), sent again with a new subject. The hero is the best part: a giant "UP TO 70% OFF" over autumn leaves, then "New Markdowns Added: Up to 70% Off Select Clearance" and a green Shop Now button. The offer is clear and loud, and it's the first thing you see.
- The email then loses focus. Below the hero sit a recycled "Fall Deal Drop / Up to 50% off / Last Day" block, five more category promos (running shoes, clothing, golf balls, recovery gear, bikes), a ScoreCard bonus banner, and a six-item product grid. A clearance subscriber who clicked for 70% off ends up scrolling a generic everything-store circular. The "Last Day" Fall Deal Drop module competes with the hero and makes the 70% headline look like one deal among many.
- Verdict: a competent send with a strong hero, but the subject promises "new markdowns" and the body repeats the same offers this brand has sent all week. A subscriber would notice the repetition.

## What's working

- The giant "UP TO 70% OFF" hero lands instantly, with the headline and a green Shop Now button directly below it.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a quick route into clearance.
- The ScoreCard $10/$20 Bonus Reward banner gives members a reason to act, with "Get Coupon" and "Join Now" buttons.
- The "Last Day" tag on the Fall Deal Drop module and the 10/7 expiry give the send some credible urgency.
- The render is clean. I don't see broken images, overlapping text or empty gaps.

## What's weak

- The Fall Deal Drop block (up to 50% off) sits right under the hero and competes with the 70% offer, so it dilutes the clearance message.
- The email reads like a full circular. Five category tiles, a loyalty banner and six product cards bury the clearance story.
- Almost every CTA says "Shop Now". Nothing names a product or category, such as "Shop 70% off shoes".
- The six picks at the bottom (adidas Spezial, Nike fleece, New Balance 530) show no prices or markdown amounts, so there's no clearance proof.
- Several promos aren't clearance and have no clear tie to the subject, such as Maxfli golf balls, Hyperice recovery gear and bikes.
- The fine print under the hero ("Interim markdowns may have been taken", "Prices as marked") is small and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the Fall Deal Drop module and keep the email to clearance only. This is the biggest lift, because the send would finally match its subject.
- 2. Replace the generic product grid with 4-6 real clearance items. Show the sale price and the percent off on each, and link each to its own product page.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Clearance Shoes" and "Shop Clearance Apparel" instead of repeated "Shop Now".
- 4. Add real urgency to the subject or hero, such as a "while sizes last" line or a deadline. The "NEW" markdowns claim is weak when yesterday's email said the same.
- 5. Move the ScoreCard banner lower, or tie it to clearance, for example "Earn $10 back on clearance orders".

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (role="presentation" tables, `lang="en"`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). QA shows one FAIL, a 9-character plain-text part, and widespread missing `alt` attributes. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the relay didn't expose the headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links detected.** 12 material links were probed and none were reported failing. 54 were skipped by the click-likelihood ranking, so coverage is partial.
- **Wrapped tracking links can't be audited.** Links go through an opaque redirector, e.g. `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1.…&pid=…`. The destination and any UTM parameters can't be read from the source.
- **Open pixel has no `alt`.** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…` is flagged for missing alt text. For a tracking pixel, `alt=""` is the correct fix.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` on 11 images** (QA WARN):
  - Hero and content images: `2026-1007-dks-storewide-6am-new-1` through `-5` (scene7 URLs).
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The hero images likely carry the offer text, so with images blocked or read by a screen reader the "Up to 70% off" message is lost. Icons can use `alt=""` if they're decorative.
- **Plain-text part is effectively missing** (QA FAIL, "Text version is 9 chars"). This hurts accessibility and spam scoring. It may be partly a relay artifact, but treat it as real until confirmed against the raw MIME.
- **Preheader is empty.** `<div id="pht-spacer">` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler. The template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in place, so the inbox preview falls back to body text.
- **Heavy image parameters.** `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` is requested for a 600px layout. That is 2x, but quality 100 inflates the file size.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this covers only what was shown.

## 5. Compliance
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found** (QA WARN). The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed rather than a defect. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both headers, so verify them in the original message headers.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found** (WARN). SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown from this data. The sender domain is `em.dcsg.com`.
- **Footer not visible.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical address are in the truncated portion. The QA tool always probes the unsubscribe link and reported no failure, but the address can't be confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Can't be assessed. All visible CTAs go through the `data.em.dcsg.com` redirector, and the source is truncated, so the final URLs and UTM parameters are unverified. QA reported no landing-page failures among the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real plain-text alternative. This is the only hard FAIL.
2. Check the raw message headers for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` before treating them as real gaps.
3. Add descriptive `alt` text to the 5 content images, and `alt=""` to the 5 icons and the open pixel.
4. Replace the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder with actual preheader text.
5. Lower the scene7 `qlt` from 100 to about 80 to cut the image weight.
6. For the next audit, capture the full HTML and follow the redirect chains for the primary CTAs to confirm the destination URLs and UTM parameters.
