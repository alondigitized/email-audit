# 📫 Delivered: Up to 50% off this week's DEALS
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a new hero for the cycle — the pampas-grass/red-frame "UP TO 50% OFF" image is a departure from the maroon stadium-seating shot DICK'S has run daily since 8/30, though the second banner further down reverts to that same stadium-seating red for "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES." So it's half-fresh: new top-of-funnel creative, same recycled clearance module underneath.
- The subject line is the real problem here. "📫 Delivered: Up to 50% off this week's DEALS" apes a shipping-notification format ("Delivered:") to bait an open — that's a soft dark pattern, and it reads as spam-adjacent rather than a legitimate promo. It's also generic: no urgency, no specific category, and it's the same "up to 50%" claim as five of the last six sends reviewed.
- Body-wise this is a solid, well-organized deals digest — clear category nav, six distinct product modules with real photography, a "check out these picks" product grid, and no render bugs. But it's undifferentiated from the brand's last week of sends and the subject line trick will cost it opens from anyone who's caught on.

## What's working

- Hero uses new photography (pampas grass, red-framed callout) instead of the repeated stadium-seating shot — a genuine creative refresh at the top.
- Category quick-nav (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) gives immediate scannability for a digest-style email.
- Six distinct, well-photographed product modules (apparel, drinkware, golf, racquet sports, bikes, strength equipment) each with its own CTA — good coverage without feeling like a wall of identical banners.
- Product grid at the bottom ("Check out these picks") adds a merchandising layer beyond the hero offers.
- No visual bugs — clean rendering, consistent spacing, legible text throughout.

## What's weak

- Subject line disguises itself as a shipping notification ("📫 Delivered:") — a bait tactic, not a benefit-forward pitch.
- The lower clearance banner ("UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES") reuses the exact maroon stadium-seating creative flagged in four of the last six audits — the "new" hero doesn't extend through the whole email.
- Two competing hero-style banners (50% off at top, 60% off clearance mid-email) dilute the primary offer — a subscriber skimming may not know which one is "the" deal.
- "This Week's Deals" as the hero headline is the same generic framing used across nearly every recent send — no specific category or product is called out up top.
- No visible loyalty/ScoreCard-specific offer despite the ScoreCard logo appearing top-left — a missed personalization opportunity for members.

## Recommendations

- 1. Kill the "Delivered:" shipping-notification bait in the subject — it works against trust and will get flagged as manipulative by savvy subscribers. Lead with a specific, honest category or discount instead.
- 2. Pick one hero offer and commit — either lead with the 50% general sale or the 60% clearance, not both as competing full-width banners.
- 3. Swap the recycled maroon clearance banner for a treatment consistent with the new pampas-grass creative, so the whole email feels like one refreshed campaign, not a new top stapled to an old middle.
- 4. Surface ScoreCard member pricing or a member-only callout somewhere in the body, since the logo is already in the header — otherwise drop the logo.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your weekend deals are in — up to 60% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on Nike, Yeti, TaylorMade and bikes — while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop this week's picks across apparel, drinkware, golf and fitness.`
