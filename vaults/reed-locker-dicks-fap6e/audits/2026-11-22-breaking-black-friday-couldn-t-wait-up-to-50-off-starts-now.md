# 📣 Breaking: Black Friday couldn’t wait. Up to 50% OFF starts NOW!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, seasonal Black Friday kickoff with a strong hero. The "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" banner in the first screen matches the subject's "Black Friday couldn't wait" promise. "Up to 50% Off / Biggest Sale of the Year!" sits right under it with one red "Shop Now" button. A first-time reader gets the event and the offer in a couple of seconds.
- The body is where it loses focus. A second giant "80% OFF" block comes right after the hero and competes with the 50% headline. The email then stacks about ten more modules: adidas, Brooks, Under Armour, socks, golf, a ping pong table, holiday hours, a gift grid and an app promo. It's a catalog, not a campaign.
- This is also the sixth or seventh DICK'S send in about three days, mostly "up to 50%" or "up to 80%" with near-identical creative. The Nov 21 "Last day!" and "final hours" sends already used the countdown angle. A subscriber has seen this offer repeatedly, so this one reads as more of the same. The execution is solid but unremarkable.

## What's working

- The "BLACK FRIDAY STARTS NOW" hero is large, seasonal and consistent with the subject.
- "Up to 50% Off" with a single "Shop Now" button is easy to parse.
- The "Explore more holiday deals" pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) gives easy category entry points.
- Named products with prices are concrete, e.g. "$89.99 Women's adidas Originals Handball Spezial" and "$109.99 Men's Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25".
- The holiday store hours block and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." same-day delivery line add useful, credible utility.

## What's weak

- Two hero offers (50% and 80%) fight each other. The 80% block is visually louder than the 50% hero, and its fine print restricts it to online-only select clearance.
- The body is too long and has no clear priority. Modules for golf, ping pong, socks and a gift grid dilute the primary action.
- The fine print under both hero offers is dense and exclusion-heavy, which works against the "biggest sale" message.
- Cadence is a problem: this offer has been repeated across the Nov 20–21 sends ("Starting NOW", "Early Black Friday Sale", "Last day!").
- The preheader area at the top ("Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow.") is promotional and doesn't complement the subject.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus one secondary module. Drop the 80% block, or lead with it instead of 50%, but don't run both.
- 2. Name the actual deadline and what is new beyond the earlier Early Black Friday sends. This is the "real" Black Friday kickoff, so say what changed.
- 3. Group the product modules into one 3-up grid of top sellers instead of eight single-item rows.
- 4. Pull the exclusions into one short footer line.
- 5. Show the ScoreCard member benefit above the fold. The loyalty bar is present but tiny.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off, ends Monday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Black Friday deals just unlocked 🏷️`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Adidas, Brooks and Under Armour from $89.99. Free same-day pickup.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus up to 80% off clearance. Deals refresh daily.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's markup is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, viewport meta, hidden preheader). Two concrete defects are confirmed: the plain-text alternative is effectively empty (9 chars), and 13 of the images the QA check flagged have no `alt` text. The header-based compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 65 links were probed, and the QA output reports no failures for them. The other 53 are unverified, and the HTML provided is truncated, so I can't confirm the unsubscribe link or any CTA hrefs.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6b009980f5a17e3d26c365f3&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is correctly sized (`width="1" height="1"`), but it has no `alt` and no inline `style`. See the next section.
- CTA anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`, which suggests ESP-wrapped tracking or deep links. The target URLs are not visible in the truncated source, so I can't verify them.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Confirmed: plain-text part is only 9 characters** `[FAIL]`. This is a multipart/alternative defect. Text-only clients and some spam filters will see essentially nothing.
- **Confirmed: 13 images flagged for missing alt text.** QA can't distinguish `alt=""` from an absent attribute, so some may be intentionally decorative.
  - **Content images (7):** `adidasspezial1122footaccess`, `brooksadrenalinesmall1122footaccess`, `ua-1`, `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`, `clubs-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`, `table-tennis-1`, `1101storefront`. If any of these is the only carrier of its link's label, the anchor has no accessible name and the link will be announced by URL.
  - **Icons (5):** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. These likely sit beside text labels, so `alt=""` would be appropriate. They should still carry an explicit empty `alt`.
  - **Tracking pixel (1):** the open pixel has no `alt`. It should have `alt=""`.
- The Scene7 images request `wid=1200&hei=1600` for a 600px layout. I can't confirm the rendered size from the truncated source, so I'm not flagging it.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FNAME]`) are visible in the supplied HTML. The source is truncated, so this covers only the portion shown.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself notes this may be a relay capture artifact, so I can't confirm a real defect. Verify against the raw message from the sending ESP.
- `Authentication-Results` is absent for the same relay reason, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. That is a test-harness limitation, not a sender fault.
- The visible footer unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not in the truncated source. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link was always included in the probe set, but it reported no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues found, but this is unverified. UTM parameters and landing-page destinations cannot be assessed from the truncated HTML, and no probe failures or redirects were reported for the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Generate it from the HTML (headline, offer, key CTA URLs, unsubscribe link). This is the only confirmed FAIL.
2. **Set explicit `alt` on all images.** Use descriptive alt on the 7 content and banner images, `alt=""` on the 5 decorative icons, and `alt=""` on the open pixel.
3. **Check headers at the source.** Send a seed message directly to a Gmail or Outlook inbox and confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
4. **Re-run with untruncated HTML.** Then audit the unsubscribe and postal address, the UTM parameters on the CTA hrefs, and any merge tokens. Increase the probe budget beyond 12 of 65 links if the remaining ones need checking.
