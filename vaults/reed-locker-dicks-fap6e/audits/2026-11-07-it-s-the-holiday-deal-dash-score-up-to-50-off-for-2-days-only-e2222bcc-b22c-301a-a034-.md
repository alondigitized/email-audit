# It's the Holiday Deal Dash! Score up to 50% off for 2 days only 💰🎄
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-07**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S flash-sale send with a strong top and a sprawling bottom. The red hero with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" lands the offer instantly. The "Holiday Deal Dash" headline and the single red Shop Now button make the primary ask clear. It fits the Nov 7 pre-Black Friday window.
- The body then dilutes the offer. A second hero ("EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance) follows almost immediately, then running shoes, hoodies, fleece, golf, baseball bats, ping pong tables, a ScoreCard+ upsell and a gift-pick grid. That is eight or more competing messages. The subject promises "2 days only," but the body is a catalog. It is also the same argyle-and-gold "up to 50% off" creative the brand sent on 11/5 ("Great deal: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes") and 11/6 ("Holiday shopping 🤝 up to 50% off this week").
- Judgement: a competent, on-brand send with a clear hero and real urgency. But it is the fifth or sixth near-identical 50% off message in five days, and the second hero cuts its own focus. Solid mid-pack.

## What's working

- The hero is huge: "UP TO 50% OFF" in gold on red, and the eye lands on it first.
- Urgency is explicit: "Two Days Only," with a dated deadline in the fine print (11/8, 11:59 PM PT).
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) lets people self-select quickly.
- Several modules carry specific prices, such as $29.98 Under Armour hoodie and $39.98 Nike Phoenix Fleece, which make the sale concrete.
- The "Order Today, Spent It Tomorrow" banner (same-day delivery, free shipping) adds a real reason to buy now.

## What's weak

- Two big red/gold heroes (50% off, then EXTRA 25% off) split the offer. A reader can't tell which deal is the real one.
- The body is a long, scattered scroll across about ten modules, with no clear priority after the hero.
- The legal fine print is dense and tiny. It sits under every module and again in a long block at the bottom, which weakens the "honest offer" feel.
- The ScoreCard+ upsell mid-email interrupts the shopping flow with an unrelated "Sign Up" ask.
- It repeats the same creative and offer as the 11/5 and 11/6 sends, so the cadence feels fatigued.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the second hero or fold "extra 25% off clearance" into one line under the main offer. One email should carry one headline offer.
- 2. Trim the body to 3-4 modules ranked by relevance: one hero, one price-led product row, one gift row. Drop the ping pong tables and the bat module unless they're segmented.
- 3. Move the ScoreCard+ pitch to the bottom, or show it only to non-members.
- 4. Collapse the per-module legal text into one footnote with an asterisk.
- 5. Vary the creative from the last two sends. A countdown or a named "Deal Dash" visual would feel fresher than another argyle panel.

- **Subject Alt B:** `48 hours only: up to 50% off, plus $29.98 hoodies 🎄`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day pickup on thousands of deals. Ends 11/8 at 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes up to 40% off, extra 25% off clearance. Two days only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, preheader spacer). It has one confirmed deliverability failure, a near-empty plain-text part (9 chars), and widespread missing `alt` attributes on content images. The HTML supplied was truncated and the header-based checks were inconclusive, so several categories below can't be fully verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 68 links were found: 12 probed and 56 skipped by the ranking heuristic. The QA output doesn't report any probe failures, so no broken links are confirmed.
- Visible links carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. This suggests ESP-wrapped click tracking, but the truncated source doesn't show the `href` values, so redirect behavior can't be assessed.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...&tuid=...&configId=...`. It is a 1x1 `<img>` directly after `<body>`, on the same `em.dcsg.com` domain as the From address, which is good for alignment. Its URL is very long (~700 chars), though that is typical of this ESP.
- The pixel has no `alt` and no `style="display:block"` or `border:0`. This is minor, but some clients may render a stray gap or show a broken-image placeholder when images are blocked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (confirmed, 12 images):** `shoes1107stwd6am`, `uawaffle1107stwd6am`, `nikepheonixfleece1107stwd6am`, `callawayquarterzip1107stwd6am`, `2bballgloves`, `2paddletable1107stwd6am`, `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`, plus the open pixel.
  - The product and promo images likely carry the offer content, so with images off or in a screen reader that content is lost.
  - The icons should have `alt=""` if decorative, or descriptive alt text if they sit alone in a link.
  - The QA check flags an absent `alt` attribute. If these images are inside text-bearing links, the impact is smaller, but that can't be confirmed from the truncated HTML.
- **Image weight:** Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fmt=jpeg&qlt=100` for the content images. That is 1200×1600 at maximum JPEG quality inside a 584–600px container. It is likely heavier than needed, and a lower `qlt` and a width of about 1200 for retina would cut payload. Actual byte sizes were not measured.
- **Fonts:** Archivo is loaded via `@font-face` from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen`, with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is fine.
- **Outlook:** The MSO-specific CSS and conditional table wrapper are present.
- **Dark mode:** No `color-scheme` or `prefers-color-scheme` handling is visible in the supplied portion.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the supplied HTML. The footer was truncated, so it can't be fully checked.

## 5. Compliance
- **Plain-text fallback (FAIL):** The text part is 9 characters, effectively empty. This is a deliverability and spam-scoring risk and leaves text-only clients and some assistive tools with nothing.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (WARN):** Neither header was found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unverified. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements (RFC 8058 one-click) would not be met. Check the raw message headers at the source.
- **Authentication-Results (WARN):** Not captured via the relay, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The envelope looks aligned (`info@em.dcsg.com`, with tracking on `data.em.dcsg.com`), but that's not proof.
- **Body unsubscribe and postal address:** The QA output says an unsubscribe link is always included in the probe set, but neither the link nor the physical address is visible in the truncated HTML, so neither is confirmed.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
No UTM parameters or landing-page URLs are visible in the truncated source, so continuity can't be assessed. Click URLs appear to be ESP-wrapped (`arc-link`, `DEEPLINK`). Final destination parameters would need to be checked by following the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain-text part:** Add a proper multipart text version with the offer, key links and unsubscribe.
2. **Alt text:** Add descriptive `alt` text to the six promo images. Use `alt=""` on the icons if they're decorative, or descriptive text if they aren't. Add `alt=""`, `width="1"`, `height="1"`, `style="display:block;border:0"` to the open pixel.
3. **Headers:** Check the raw headers from the original send, not the relay copy, for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and DKIM/SPF/DMARC results. Treat the compliance WARNs as unverified until then.
4. **Image sizes:** Reduce Scene7 `qlt` from 100 (about 80–85) and trim dimensions to what the layout needs.
5. **Re-run with full HTML:** Re-run the audit without truncation so the footer, unsubscribe link, postal address and link `href`/UTM values can be checked.
