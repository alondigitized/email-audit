# 🍂 Fall into this week's deals, up to 50% OFF 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-24**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S weekly-deals send. The hero is a big "UP TO 50% OFF" over fall leaves, with "This Week's Deals" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The eye lands on the number first, and the CTA is easy to find. Below the hero, the email turns into a long catalog. It stacks an extra 25% off clearance, up to 40% off shoes, a Dri-FIT-style hoodie deal, fan gear, drinkware, cleats, TaylorMade irons and a product grid.
- It's the same structure as the 10/21 sends ("This week's deals are here! 👏" and "Say hello to this week's deals!"). It also arrives the day after two flash-sale emails and two clearance emails. A subscriber would see this as the fifth or sixth "up to 50% off" message in four days. The email is well built, but the 50% headline has lost its force through repetition.
- The email tries to serve every shopper at once. A single sale hero followed by a long run of equally weighted modules gives no clear second step. Six or more green Shop Now buttons look identical, so nothing tells the reader which deal matters most.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is huge and readable, and the Shop Now button sits directly under it.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give a fast way into the sale.
- The extra 25% off clearance block is bold and has a stated deadline (10/24, 11:59 PM PT).
- Product photography is clean, and the NHL/MLB/NFL tile and the Yeti-style tumbler image feel timely and lifestyle-driven.
- The ScoreCard Points bar and the same-day pickup and delivery line at the top add a loyalty and convenience cue.

## What's weak

- Too many modules with identical green buttons dilute the primary CTA, and the email scrolls extremely long.
- "Up to 50% off" is generic. The email never names a hero product or a clear price anchor in the top section.
- The "Valid online only for a limited time" fine print under the hero is small and vague.
- The email repeats the week's earlier offers (extra 25% clearance, up to 40% shoes), so it reads as a rehash.
- The product grid at the bottom has no prices and no ratings, so there is no reason to click a specific item.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one clearance module and one personalized product row. Make one CTA the clear primary and make the others secondary links.
- 2. Put two or three real prices in the hero (for example, "Nike fleece from $XX") so "up to 50%" has a concrete anchor.
- 3. Add a visible deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday."
- 4. Add prices and star ratings to the product grid.
- 5. Space the sends. Rotate the headline offer instead of repeating 50% off every day.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Fleece, cleats & more: deals end Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 50% off this week, ends Sunday 🍂`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 25% off clearance and up to 40% off shoes for the family.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hoodies from $XX, cleats, drinkware and fan gear. Pickup is ready in an hour.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
The email's QA run scored a 50% pass rate. It has one hard failure: the plain-text part is only 9 characters. It also has 11 images without alt text, including the 1x1 open pixel. The relay stripped the auth and unsubscribe headers, so the compliance checks can't be confirmed or refuted.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- Only 12 of 64 links were probed (52 skipped), so most of the email is unverified. Probing is ranked by click likelihood, so this is a coverage gap and not a defect.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…`. It is 1x1 and sits at the top of `<body>`. This is standard ESP tracking, and no malformed params were found.
- The truncated HTML shows the first CTA with `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK…"`. The rest of the markup is cut off, so I can't check redirect or tracking-wrapper behavior beyond that.
- No broken links were reported in the probed set.
