# Deal drop! See what's up to 50% off this week
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-27**
## Executive summary

- This one breaks from DICK'S recent run — no red or orange lightning-bolt clearance hero, just a soft golden-hour prairie photo with "UP TO 50% OFF" stacked in bold white type. It's a nicer visual than the last five sends I've reviewed (9/22-9/26), but it's also vaguer: "This Week's Deals" with no specific product or deadline attached to the hero number. The catalog scroll below is the same kitchen-sink formula DICK'S always runs — shoes, sweatshirts, tees, gloves, golf, then a hard-right turn into a $25-and-under red clearance block and an SMS opt-in pitch.
- The subject "Deal drop! See what's up to 50% off this week" is honest but generic — it reads like filler after four straight "ends tonight"/"last day" urgency sends. This is a lower-stakes email than DICK'S recent clearance blitz, and it shows: no deadline, no single-offer clarity, and the picture-heavy body will load slow and scroll long on mobile. Serviceable, well-composed hero, but the email overall coasts on inventory-listing rather than persuasion.

## What's working

- The hero image (golden hour, tall grass, sunset) is genuinely well-composed and stands out from DICK'S usual flat color-block hero treatment.
- "UP TO 50% OFF" is legible, high-contrast white-on-photo type — no rendering issues.
- Category nav pills (Men's, Women's, Kid's, Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give the eye clear next steps beyond the primary CTA.
- The $25-and-under red block is a clean, single, legible callout — same clearance formula that scored decently in past sends.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, buttons are all consistent green "Shop Now" pills.

## What's weak

- The hero has no deadline or specific category tie — "up to 50% off" with no product shown undercuts urgency and clarity.
- Subject and preheader don't match the hero's soft/lifestyle tone — "Deal drop" reads like a flash-sale subject line slapped onto a lifestyle photo.
- The email is a long, undifferentiated scroll of 7+ product categories (shoes, sweatshirts, tees, gloves, golf, clearance, SMS, picks) — no hierarchy telling me what to care about first.
- Preview text is just store-hours/shipping boilerplate ("Order Today, Spend It Tomorrow... Details") — wasted real estate that repeats nothing useful about the offer.
- Fifth email in eight days without a persona-specific hook — this is generic mass-merchant inventory, not something that reads "for me."

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a real number and a deadline in the hero — "up to 50%" with no date or category is the weakest urgency signal DICK'S has run this week; even "Ends Sunday" would help.
- 2. Cut the body to 3-4 sections max — the current 7-category scroll dilutes attention; lead with the highest-margin or highest-interest category (sneakers) and cut Colosseum Tees/ProStock gloves down to a single combined "more deals" tile.
- 3. Rewrite preview text to reference an actual offer instead of shipping boilerplate.
- 4. Move the $25-and-under clearance block up near the hero — it's the most concrete, credible offer in the email and it's buried two-thirds down.

- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns just landed: up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on shoes, fleece, and gear — plus $25 & under clearance`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `This week only: up to 50% off sneakers, apparel, and gear`
