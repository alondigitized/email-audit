# Your deal alert is here: Shop up to 50% off this week's deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-18**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S sixth "up to 50% off" wordmark send in this run, and it's essentially a rerun of 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits" digest — same green locker-room hero, same sprawling ten-plus-category scroll. The only real news is the "$20 Bonus Reward" ScoreCard+ banner sandwiched right under the hero, which is a genuinely time-bounded, concrete offer buried in a generic subject.
- The layout itself is clean and bug-free — no overlapping text, no broken images — but there is no single hero product or CTA to click. It's a category directory, not a pitch. By the sixth iteration of "up to 50%," fatigue is the dominant signal, not the offer.
- Verdict: competent execution, stale strategy. This is a maintenance email for existing browsers, not a re-engagement tool.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly — bold "UP TO 50% OFF" wordmark on the recognizable green locker-room background, no visual bugs.
- The "$20 Bonus Reward" banner is the strongest, most concrete offer in the email (spend $150, get $20) and it's visually distinct (black background, red badge) from the rest of the scroll.
- Category sections are consistently formatted with clear "Shop Now" / sub-category buttons throughout.
- Product photography (golf, outdoor, drinkware, fan shop) is on-brand and demographically appropriate for a general sporting-goods shopper.

## What's weak

- Subject and hero are near-identical to 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits in this week's deals" — same claim, same visual, sixth time this angle has run.
- No single hero product or offer — it's ten-plus category tiles, so the eye has nowhere specific to land.
- "Up to 50%" is vague across almost every section (clothing, drinkware, golf, outdoor, fan shop, fitness) — the fine print "certain exclusions apply" energy without any real specifics.
- The $20 Bonus Reward — the one genuinely fresh, time-bound offer — is visually subordinate to the generic 50%-off hero above it, not leading the email.
- Bottom "Check out these picks" product grid feels like filler bolted onto an already-long scroll.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the email with the $20 ScoreCard+ Bonus Reward, not the generic 50%-off wordmark — it's the only offer this week with real specificity and urgency ("2 Days Only"). Promote it to the hero position.
- 2. Cut the category count. Ten-plus tiles in one send trains subscribers to skim past all of them; pick 3-4 with the deepest discounts and cut the rest.
- 3. Replace "Up to 50% off" language with the single best real discount you can name (e.g., "40% off Nike, HOKA, adidas") — vague ceiling percentages have run six times this stretch and are losing credibility.
- 4. Vary the hero creative — the green locker-room background has now appeared in nearly every send this week; subscribers have seen it repeatedly.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Nike, HOKA & adidas — up to 40% off this week`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Spend $150, earn $20 back — plus deals across golf, outdoor & fan shop`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your weekly picks: shoes, drinkware, golf gear and more, up to 50% off`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Email renders via standard MSO/media-query table layout with no structural HTML errors, but ships with a broken unsubscribe link, missing email authentication/compliance headers, and pervasive missing alt text across content and footer images.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL)**: The "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a0168d6ea38c2a57f4bbcfd118/820b841075734dfa0000021ef3a0bd09`) resolves to an error page: `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`. This is a functional failure, not just a warning — recipients cannot self-unsubscribe via this link.
- 12 of 75 total links were probed (material/high-click-likelihood links); 63 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized per probing methodology. No broken links reported among the 12 probed besides the unsubscribe link above.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- Standard Outlook/MSO conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and `mso-hide` on the tracking pixel are present and correctly formed.
- Web font (`Archivo`) loaded via `@font-face` inside a `@media screen` block with `local()` fallbacks — will fail gracefully to `Arial, sans-serif` in clients that strip `<style>` or don't support `@media` in email (e.g., older Outlook desktop), which is expected/acceptable.
- **12 images missing `alt` text**, including the tracking pixel, 5 hero/category content images (`dsg-2026-0818-dks-stw-4pm`, `team2-...`, `golf-...`, `outdoor-...`, `fan-...`, `fitness-...`), a promotional banner GIF, and 5 footer icon images (SMS, SportsMatter, BPG, favorite, location). This impacts screen-reader users and image-blocked clients (images render blank with no fallback label).

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) visible in the truncated source.
- The JSON-LD `DiscountOffer` block has empty `description` and `discountCode` fields, and the `PromotionCard` block has an empty `image` field — these appear to be unpopulated template placeholders left blank rather than broken tokens, but they mean any client parsing this structured data (e.g., Gmail promotional markup) gets no usable offer data.
- `availabilityStarts` is hardcoded to `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00` — a stale/unmaintained placeholder value inconsistent with a 2026-08-18 send date, with `availabilityEnds` left empty.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** (see #2) — a CAN-SPAM violation risk since the required functional opt-out is not working.
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing (WARN)**: not detected, though this may be a relay-side capture limitation (AgentMail) rather than confirmation the sender omitted it.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing (WARN)**: RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header missing (WARN)**: SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified via this relay.
- Sending domain is `e.dcsg.com` (dedicated subdomain), consistent with standard ESP practice, but header-level auth verification is inconclusive from available data.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Truncated HTML does not expose CTA destination URLs, so UTM parameter presence/consistency cannot be verified from the provided source.
- Tracking/redirect infrastructure uses Epsilon's `app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...` and `/U/...` click-tracking paths — consistent with an Epsilon Harmony ESP setup; the `/U/` path is confirmed broken (see #2).

7. **Recommendations**
- **Fix the unsubscribe link immediately** — `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` indicates a backend routing/config error on the subscription-preferences endpoint; this is the highest-priority item (compliance + deliverability risk).
- Add `alt` attributes to all 12 flagged images, at minimum empty `alt=""` for purely decorative footer icons and descriptive `alt` text for content/hero images.
- Populate or remove the empty `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` JSON-LD fields and correct the stale 2020 `availabilityStarts` date to reflect the actual campaign window.
- Confirm with the sending/ESP team (outside this relay's visibility) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC authentication headers are actually present at the SMTP layer, since this audit's relay could not capture them.
- Verify full CTA link set (beyond the 12 probed) for UTM parameter consistency once untruncated HTML is available.
