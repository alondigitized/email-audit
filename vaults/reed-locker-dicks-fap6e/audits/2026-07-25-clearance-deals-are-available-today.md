# Clearance deals are available today
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S "Up to 70% Off" hero again — the exact same headline percentage and dark-red treatment that anchored the 2026-07-24 "CLEARANCE deals: up to 70% off!" send just a day earlier. At this point in the cadence (this is roughly the fourteenth promotional blast since mid-July), the creative isn't refreshing so much as recirculating, and the subject line "Clearance deals are available today" is the weakest, most generic one this brand has used yet — it doesn't even carry the 70% number that anchors the hero.
- Structurally the email is still well-built: a bold percentage hero up top, a clean category nav bar, then a long, well-organized scroll through Nike Deal Days, golf, outdoor, team sports, drinkware, fan shop, and fitness, each with lifestyle photography and sub-category buttons. No render bugs are visible — images load, text is legible, buttons are consistent. But the sheer length and repetition of "Up to X% Off [category]" blocks without any single standout differentiator makes this read as filler in a fatigued sequence, and the subject line fails to earn the open that the body might deserve.
- Verdict: solid execution, tired creative, and a subject line that undersells the offer already sitting in the hero. This is a rerun dressed in a duller headline.

## What's working

- The 70% hero is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss at the top of the email.
- Clear category navigation bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/$25 and Under/Fan Shop/All Sale) right below the hero helps browsers self-segment fast.
- Each product section pairs a lifestyle photo with a specific percentage and sub-category CTA buttons (e.g., "Club Deals," "Clothing Deals," "Shoe Deals").
- No visible render bugs — clean layout, consistent button styling, images all loaded correctly.
- Good persona coverage for a multi-category shopper (golf, outdoor, team sports, fitness, fan shop all represented).

## What's weak

- Subject line "Clearance deals are available today" is generic and drops the 70% number that's the whole hook of the hero — a real step down from prior sends' specificity.
- The hero percentage and red treatment are a near-identical rerun of the 2026-07-24 "CLEARANCE deals: up to 70% off!" send, reinforcing the "creative debt" pattern flagged in that history.
- Extremely long single-column scroll (8+ near-identical "Up to X% Off [category]" blocks) with no visual pacing break — fatigue sets in well before the fan shop/fitness sections.
- No urgency element in the hero itself (no countdown, no "ends tonight") despite "Limited Time" tag — feels less credible than the countdown-timer sends earlier in this brand's history.
- Preview/preheader text ("Order Today. Sport it Tomorrow. Free store pickup, same-day delivery, and free shipping!") is generic shipping boilerplate, not tied to the clearance offer at all.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the subject line first — it's the single biggest miss here. Put the 70% number back in the subject; a vague "available today" line undersells an offer this large and breaks the pattern of the brand's better-performing sends (7/24's "Up to 50% OFF" send scored 9/10 partly on subject specificity).
- 2. Differentiate this send from yesterday's near-identical 70% hero — swap in a specific category lead (e.g., lead with Nike Deal Days or drinkware) so subscribers don't feel like they're rereading yesterday's email.
- 3. Add a real urgency element to the hero (countdown timer or explicit end date) rather than a static "Limited Time" tag with no deadline.
- 4. Trim the body — 8+ stacked category blocks is too many; cut to the top 4-5 performing categories and link the rest from a single "Shop All Deals" module.
- 5. Rewrite the preheader to reference the offer, not shipping logistics.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off clearance — today only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance picks: up to 70% off sitewide`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Golf, outdoor, fan gear and more — all marked down`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `70% off ends soon. Shop before sizes sell out.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S "Clearance deals are available today"

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders on a standard Epsilon/Harmony-hosted MSO-compatible table layout with one confirmed broken link (unsubscribe) and multiple missing authentication/compliance headers at the transport layer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019f9aeef11ab457b4369ff0b33c/3c56ec6040e54ee30000021ef3a0bd07` resolves to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — the preference-center subscription page is erroring out. This is a functional break, not just a QA warning.
- All probed CTA/logo links route through Epsilon's Harmony click-tracking redirector (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) with an opaque `__dU__` token rather than a plain destination URL — expected for this ESP, but it means link destinations can't be verified without following redirects.
- QA probed 12 of 71 total links (material links only, ranked by click likelihood); 59 footer/utility/social links were deprioritized and not individually checked.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **10 images missing `alt` text**, including the tracking pixel, three category banners (golfclothing.jpg, outdoor.jpg, teamsports.jpg), fanshop.jpg, fitness.jpg, an outdoor drinkware GIF banner, and four footer icons (SMS, ScoreCard/SportsMatter, BPG, favorite-store, location). This affects screen-reader users and image-blocked clients.
- The embedded `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//` line comments inside the JSON payload (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). This is **invalid JSON** — any strict JSON-LD parser (including Gmail's schema.org promo-card feature this markup targets) will fail to parse it silently, meaning the `PromotionCard`/`DiscountOffer` structured data is likely non-functional as shipped.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) and `mso-hide` fallback for the tracking pixel are correctly present.
- Responsive `@media (max-width:480px)` rules are defined for mobile type scaling and spacing overrides — no issues found.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge tags, personalization placeholders (e.g., `{{first_name}}`, AMPscript, Handlebars) were observed in the available HTML source. No issues found (note: source was truncated, so this covers only the reviewed portion).

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** — see Section 2. This is a CAN-SPAM risk if the only working opt-out path errors out.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — mailbox providers can't surface a native unsubscribe control; also increases spam-folder risk with Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe is not supported, which is required by Gmail/Yahoo's 2024+ bulk sender policies.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status could not be verified via the AgentMail relay.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Links use Epsilon Harmony's proprietary tokenized redirector format (`/T/<accountId>/<hash>/<hash>?__dU__=...`) rather than plain UTM query parameters, so campaign-source attribution can't be confirmed from the raw href alone — would require following the redirect chain to the final landing URL to verify UTM tagging and landing-page match.
- The one link confirmed to resolve to a final destination (the unsubscribe link) lands on an **error page** (`/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`), indicating a broken continuity between the email link and the site's preference-center route.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe destination — the `/pc/subscription` preference-center route is erroring; this is the highest-priority item (compliance + functional break).
2. Add `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers to support one-click unsubscribe (RFC 8058) and meet Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender requirements.
3. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment is actually passing at the sending MTA — the relay isn't surfacing `Authentication-Results`, so this should be confirmed independently.
4. Add `alt` attributes to the 10 flagged images, particularly the category banners and footer trust icons.
5. Remove the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or move them outside the script tag — as written, the JSON is invalid and the structured-data features it's meant to enable won't parse.
