# Ends. Tonight! Don't miss an EXTRA 25% off select clearance styles. 🚨
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, well-built end-of-day clearance push. The hero is a giant red "EXTRA 25% OFF" block with "Ends Tonight" and a red Shop Now button directly beneath it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the urgency is credible: the fine print gives a 10/31/26 11:59PM PT deadline.
- Below the hero the email turns into the usual DICK'S catalog stack: Brooks running shoes, fleece, golf clubs, fire pits, bats, NHL and fishing gear, then a product grid. Almost every block has its own discount ("Up to 40%", "Up to $350", "Up to 10%"), so the 25% headline gets diluted. The email is also long (about 5,700px) and hits many categories with no clear priority. Compared with the 10/30 and 10/29 sends, which were also "up to 50%" roundups, this one has a sharper, single-number hook. But it is the fourth or fifth promo in three days, so cadence is a drag.
- Verdict: a solid, honest urgency email that is held back by sprawl and by the fatigue of the week's near-daily sends.

## What's working

- The hero reads instantly: huge "EXTRA 25% OFF" type on a red autumn-leaf background, with a "Ends Tonight" label in green.
- The deadline is specific and credible in the fine print (10/31/26, 11:59PM PT), and it matches the subject's "Tonight".
- The "Explore more deals" pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick category entry points.
- The modules are consistent: a two-image row, a headline and a green Shop Now button. They scan easily.
- The loyalty header (ScoreCard points) and the footer trust items (price match, app download) support the brand.

## What's weak

- Almost every module carries a different discount (20%, 40%, $350, 10%, 25%, 50%). That competes with the one "extra 25%" hero offer.
- The fine print under the hero is small and dense, with exclusions and "interim markdowns may have been taken". This undercuts how honest the offer feels.
- The email is very long, with seven or more modules and a product grid. That is far too much for an "ends tonight" message.
- The product picks mostly aren't clearance (Adidas Samba, Nike backpack and so on), and no prices or reviews are shown. The hero offer and the products don't clearly connect.
- This is another same-week promo after the 10/28 "Hauntingly good DEALS", the 10/29 "ONE DAY" sends and the 10/30 roundups. Urgency language is wearing thin.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero, one row of clearance-only products showing struck-through and sale prices, and the category pills. Drop the unrelated "Up to 10%" and "Up to $350" modules.
- 2. Add a live countdown or "Ends 11:59PM PT" directly beside the hero button, and move the key exclusions into one plain line.
- 3. Make the product grid actually qualify for the extra 25%, with "now $X" pricing.
- 4. Show ratings or social proof on the product tiles.
- 5. Skip or merge a near-duplicate send the same week to protect cadence.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last call: extra 25% off clearance ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tonight only: extra 25% off clearance shoes & gear`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Brooks, fleece, golf and more. Prices drop further at checkout until 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Clearance is already marked down. Take 25% more off before it's gone.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound: `lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader are all present. The weak points are the plain-text part (9 characters, a QA FAIL) and missing `alt` attributes on 13 images. The source was truncated, so the unsubscribe link, link hrefs and UTM parameters could not be checked.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No link failures reported.** QA probed 12 material links and skipped 52 (footer, social and utility links were deprioritized). No broken or redirecting links were flagged among the 12. The 52 skipped links are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is a bare 1×1 `<img>` with no `alt` and no inline `style`. The URL is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&tuid=6ae3a1b7f225c53e6098de4b&configId=512ffb51-…`.
- CTA anchors carry `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The hrefs fall in the truncated part, so redirect behavior and click-tracking domains are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing `alt` (QA WARN, 13 images):**
  - The 1×1 open pixel (`/ee/v1/open`).
  - Seven category hero images on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `running-2026-1031-d`, `fleece-2026-1031-d`, `golf-2026-1031-d`, `solo-2026-1031-d`, `bbsb-2026-1031-d`, `nhl-2026-1031-d` and `fishing-2026-1031-d`.
  - Five icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these category tiles have no accessible name. If they are the CTAs, they are unlabeled links. Whether they are wrapped in anchors is not visible in the truncated source.
- **Editor artifacts:** `<body … class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is leftover authoring-tool markup. It is harmless but unclean.
- **Leftover template comment:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` remains in the empty `td#ssl`. The preheader itself is in `#pht-spacer`, which is hidden correctly with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`.
- **Image weight:** the hero images request `wid=1200&hei=1600` PNG-alpha for a 600px container. That is 2× density, but several are `fmt=png-alpha`, which is heavy compared with JPEG. No payload sizes were measured.
- **Fonts:** Archivo loads via `@font-face` from `fonts.gstatic.com` with an Arial fallback. This is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or the subject. The truncated portion is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 9 characters. This is effectively a missing multipart/alternative text body. It raises spam-filter risk and breaks text-only and accessibility clients.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (WARN):** QA notes this may be stripped by the AgentMail relay. It is unconfirmed, so treat it as unverified rather than a confirmed defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found (WARN):** the same relay caveat applies. One-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed, and it is required for bulk senders at Gmail and Yahoo.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (WARN):** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown because of the relay. The sender is `info@em.dcsg.com`. Alignment is plausible, but there is no evidence either way.
- **Unsubscribe link and physical address:** these are in the footer, outside the truncated source, so they could not be confirmed. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, with no failure reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The hrefs and any UTM parameters fall in the truncated source, and no landing-page probe results for the 12 probed links were provided. The only continuity-relevant evidence is that the creative IDs encode the send date (`*-2026-1031-d`), consistent with the 2026-10-31 send.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Include the offer, key category links and the unsubscribe URL. Replace the 9-character stub.
2. **Add `alt` text to all 13 images.** The seven category tiles need descriptive alt text, such as the category name. The icons need either a label or `alt=""` if they are decorative. The pixel needs `alt=""` plus `style="display:block;border:0"`.
3. **Verify headers at the source.** Check the raw message in a direct-delivery mailbox for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass. The relay may be dropping them.
4. **Re-run QA on the full HTML.** Cover the footer links, the hrefs and the UTM parameters, and probe more of the 52 skipped links.
5. **Clean the template.** Remove `is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable` and the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment from the production output.
6. **Consider JPEG for photo-heavy tiles.** The `fmt=png-alpha` images are likely heavier than needed.
