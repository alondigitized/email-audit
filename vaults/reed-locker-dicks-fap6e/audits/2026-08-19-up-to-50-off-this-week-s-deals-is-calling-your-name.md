# 📢 Up to 50% OFF this week’s deals is calling your name!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the same sprawling weekly-digest DICK'S has run six times now — the green locker-room "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is a near carbon-copy of 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits" send, right down to the padlock-vignette imagery. Below the wordmark it sprawls into a marathon scroll: ScoreCard $20 Bonus Reward, clothing/shoes at 40% off, Owala drinkware, soccer gear, golf sets, life vests, Peloton shoes, cycling shoes, a text-club pitch, and a "Check out these picks" product grid — eleven-plus distinct offers in one email.
- Nothing here is broken, and the hero is legible with a clear "Shop Now" CTA up top, but there's zero targeting or hierarchy beyond "everything is on sale." A recipient has to scroll through category tiles for team sports, golf, boating, and cycling to find anything remotely personal, which is a lot of friction for a subject line promising one clean 50%-off hook.
- Judgement: this is cadence fatigue made visible — the sixth "up to 50%" wordmark this stretch, reusing the same hero and the same everything-under-one-roof format. It'll perform on brand recognition alone for loyal ScoreCard members, not because the creative earns the click.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible — bold green background, no overlapping text or broken renders.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is immediately visible under the hero with no scroll needed.
- ScoreCard+ $20 Bonus Reward section stands out visually (black background, bold callout) and is a genuine loyalty incentive.
- Category quick-links (Men's / Women's / Kids' / Shoes / Team Sports / Back-to-School) give an easy nav shortcut instead of forcing a full scroll.
- Product imagery throughout is clean, well-lit, and un-glitched.

## What's weak

- This is a near-identical rerun of 8/16's hero and structure — no creative refresh despite being the sixth "up to 50%" send.
- The email is exhaustingly long — eleven-plus unrelated categories (soccer, golf, boating, cycling, backpacks) with no personalization, so most readers scroll past 90% of it.
- No single hero offer or product is emphasized — visual weight is spread evenly across a dozen sections instead of guiding the eye to one clear action.
- The subject's "is calling your name" personalization language isn't backed up by any actual personalization in the body — it's the same generic digest as everyone gets.
- ScoreCard Text Alerts CTA, app bonus, and multiple loyalty asks compete with the core sale message, diluting the primary CTA.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email in half — pick 3-4 categories max based on browse/purchase history instead of a full-catalog dump; this single change would do more than any copy tweak.
- 2. Retire the green locker-room hero — it's been reused across multiple sends this stretch; a fresh visual with one hero product would break the repetition fatigue.
- 3. Move the ScoreCard $20 Bonus Reward higher or make it the hero if it's the best offer — right now it's buried below a generic "This Week's Deals" placeholder section.
- 4. Drop or consolidate the loyalty/app asks (ScoreCard sign-up, Text Alerts, App download) into one single "join us" block instead of three separate CTAs.
- 5. Segment sends by category (soccer parents vs. golfers vs. cyclists) rather than combining all into one long scroll — should lift both open and click quality.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Members: earn a $20 reward on $150+, plus deals across the store.`
