# Black Friday starting now 🤝 Biggest Sale of the Year (Up to 50% OFF!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth Black Friday or clearance send from DICK'S in three days, and it's the best-built of the run. The hero is a big green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" block, then "Black Friday Starts Now / Biggest Sale of the Year!" with one red "Shop Now" button. The first screen is clear. A second red "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance banner breaks the hierarchy, though. Two giant percentages compete, and the 70% figure beats the 50% in the subject.
- Below the heroes the email becomes a product grid: HOKA, Carhartt, Nike Pro, NBA gear, PING golf clubs and bikes. Each tile has its own "Shop Now" button, which is well organized but gives the reader a lot of choices. For a sports shopper the category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) are useful. The store-hours block and the gift picks grid dilute the close. Overall it's a competent, seasonal, trustworthy send, but it is not new to a subscriber who has seen four near-identical pushes since 11/20.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero matches the subject and fills the first screen.
- Category chips directly under the hero give quick navigation by shopper type.
- Product tiles carry concrete offers: 20% off HOKA, up to 40% off Carhartt, 25% off Nike Pro shorts, plus BOGO 50% off Nike gifts.
- The "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" same-day pickup bar at the top adds a real convenience hook.
- Each tile has a consistent dark red "Shop Now" button, so the CTA is always clear.

## What's weak

- Two giant percentage banners (50% and 70%) compete, and the subject only promises 50%.
- Roughly eight "Shop Now" or "Learn More" buttons dilute any single primary action.
- Fine print under both heroes is small, and the "exclusions apply" language is easy to miss.
- The store-hours block and the gift grid at the bottom stretch an already very long email.
- The cadence is heavy: this is another 50% / Black Friday push after the 11/23 "Black Friday is ON!" and "Game on, Black Friday" sends.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with the 50% and fold the 70% clearance into a smaller strip, or the reverse, so the subject and hero agree.
- 2. Cut the product grid to 4 tiles and drop the store-hours and gift-pick blocks to a footer link.
- 3. Add a real deadline near the top, such as a countdown to 11/29 11:59 PM PT. The 11/21 send's live countdown was the strongest element in the recent run.
- 4. Cut the cadence: a Black Friday email every few hours reads as noise. Send one hero per day and segment by sport or category.
- 5. Make the fine print more legible, or link it more clearly.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday is live: up to 50% off at DICK'S`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 70% off clearance + 50% off Black Friday deals`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Same-day pickup on HOKA, Nike, Carhartt and golf. Ends 11/29.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order today, sport it tomorrow. Deals on shoes, gear and gifts.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound: it uses a role="presentation" table layout, a responsive media-query block and a Gmail-safe tracking pixel. The QA run found no hard failures (0 issues, 3 warnings). The real defects are an invalid JSON-LD block, leftover template placeholders, and missing `alt` on 13 images. The compliance and auth warnings can't be confirmed from the data given.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 64 links were probed and 52 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). No broken links were reported among the 12, but the other 52 are unverified.
- **Tracking pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a20933dab28f9b1df4bbc782e8/1d2025c3e543432800004c5a42963aa1` is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`, which is harmless functionally but is flagged by QA.
- No other link or tracking issues are confirmed in the visible HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images have no `alt` attribute.** Content images:
  - `hokabondi91122footaccess`
  - `carhartt-3`
  - `tempo`
  - `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-nba`
  - `clubs-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`
  - `bikes-2`

  Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. The tracking pixel is the thirteenth.
  - Missing `alt` on linked banners leaves screen-reader users without a link label. It also shows nothing when images are blocked, which is the default in many clients.
  - Decorative footer icons should have `alt=""`.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript `//` comments, which are not valid JSON. Gmail will fail to parse the whole block, so no Promotion Card or annotation will render.
- **Empty or stale schema values.** `"image": ""`, `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""` and `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` are all unpopulated or stale. The comment `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!` is leftover template text.
- **CSS typo in the MSO conditional.** `div {...padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before the closing brace, so Outlook may drop the declaration.
- **Template residue in the shipped HTML.** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` (a closing comment with no visible opener, though the source is truncated) show the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **Image sizing:** the banner URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600` with `fit=constrain,1`, and the `<img>` width attributes weren't visible in the truncated source. This could mean oversized payloads, but it is not confirmed.
