# Up to 50% off is here: Flash Sale deals end tonight
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-26**
## Executive summary

- This is the same green-locker-room "FLASH SALE" hero DICK'S has run repeatedly all month — 8/16, 8/20, 8/22, 8/24, and 8/25 all used near-identical padlock/locker imagery, and this one adds a genuine countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") that at least gives the "deals end tonight" subject line some visual teeth. The hero image itself has a cropping bug: the word "FLASH SALE" is cut off at the right edge of the frame, reading as "FLASH" / "SALE" running off-canvas. Below the fold it's the usual stack — a Back-to-School locker banner, a red "Up to 60% Off Clearance" block, and a product grid.
- The countdown clock is the one authentically new element this brand has tried this week, and it's a good instinct — urgency claims land better with a visible ticking number than with exclamation points. But the execution undercuts it: a cropped hero headline is a basic QA miss, and the underlying creative is the fourth-plus reuse of the same locker photography this month. A subscriber who's opened three of these already will recognize the wallpaper instantly.
- Judgement: mediocre send, dragged down specifically by the render bug on the hero — fix the crop and this is a serviceable if repetitive flash-sale email.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") gives the "ends tonight" urgency actual visual proof, unlike prior sends this week.
- Clear primary CTA ("Shop Now") directly under a legible offer headline ("Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off").
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School) give multiple paths in without scrolling far.
- Product grid at the bottom shows real named items (Nike Varsity backpack, adidas Samba, New Balance 530) with clean product photography.

## What's weak

- Hero image text is cropped — "FLASH SALE" runs off the right edge of the banner, a visible render bug.
- Same locker/padlock photography this brand has used since 8/16 — zero creative differentiation from the last five sends.
- Three separate offers stacked (50% off flash, 50% off back-to-school, 60% off clearance) dilute which one is "tonight only."
- Fine print ("select styles," "while quantities last," "prices as marked") undercuts the blanket "Up to 50%" headline, same issue flagged on 8/26's earlier send.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero image crop so "FLASH SALE" is fully visible — this is a basic asset-QA fail that undermines the whole send.
- 2. Retire the locker/padlock imagery for at least two weeks; it's now appeared in six consecutive sends and readers will pattern-match to "ignore."
- 3. Cut the email down to one offer above the fold (the flash sale) and move back-to-school/clearance lower or into a separate send — three competing "% off" blocks confuse the primary CTA.
- 4. Pair the countdown timer with a specific end-time in the hero copy itself (e.g., "Ends 11:59PM PT tonight"), not just buried in fine print below.
- **Subject Alt A:** `8 hours left: Flash Sale up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight — up to 50% off is almost gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Countdown's on: shop backpacks, shoes, and gear before 11:59PM PT`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Back-to-school and clearance markdowns included — while they last`
