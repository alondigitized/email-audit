# Slow your scroll! HUNDREDS of new clearance styles just dropped
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same "kitchen sink" clearance catalog DICK'S has run repeatedly this week — a red-and-black "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" hero, followed by an endless scroll of category rails (Men's/Women's/Kids', golf, fan shop, drinkware, outdoor, fitness, curated product picks). It's the same architecture as the 9/21 "100s of NEW clearance styles" send (5.00/10), just re-shuffled product tiles under a nearly identical headline.
- The hero itself is clean and renders without bugs — bold typography, no overlap, no broken images. But there's no actual offer anywhere near the top: no percentage, no dollar amount, just "Fresh Deals You Don't Want to Miss." The first real number (40% off select clothing/shoes) doesn't show up until deep into the scroll, and even that's buried under a wall of category buttons and cross-sell rails.
- Verdict: solid production quality, zero differentiation from what this subscriber has already seen twice this week, and a hero that undersells its own headline by not leading with a number.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly — bold, legible, no layout bugs or image breaks.
- "Shop Now" CTAs are visually consistent and easy to spot throughout.
- Product grid (adidas Samba, New Balance 530, Nike fleece) is well-organized with clear imagery and labels.
- ScoreCard rewards banner at top gives a personalization/loyalty touchpoint immediately.

## What's weak

- No concrete discount number in the hero — "Hundreds of new clearance styles" is a volume claim, not an offer.
- This is effectively the third near-identical clearance/catalog layout this week (9/20 "BEST deals," 9/21 "100s of NEW clearance styles").
- The 40% off clothing/shoes offer is buried mid-scroll instead of leading.
- Email is extremely long — category rails, product picks, app download, and footer all stacked with no scannable hierarchy after the fold.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a number in the hero. "Hundreds of styles" is weaker than "Up to 40% Off" — lead with the actual discount since you have one.
- 2. Cut this email's length by half — pick one hero offer plus one supporting rail instead of six category sections.
- 3. Vary the creative from the 9/20 and 9/21 sends — same red/black clearance box is becoming visually interchangeable across your last three emails.
- 4. Move the 40% off clothing/shoes block up directly under the hero so it's visible without scrolling.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 40% off Nike, adidas & more — new clearance just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New clearance styles are in (and yes, there's 40% off)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Fresh markdowns on Nike, adidas, Under Armour and more — while sizes last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New styles just hit clearance. Shop before your size is gone.`

## Technical Audit

1. **Technical Summary**
Marketing email from `em.dcsg.com` (Sailthru/Selligent-style ESP) with heavy click-tracking redirection and multiple accessibility/compliance gaps confirmed by QA: missing alt text on all content images, no detectable unsubscribe headers, and an unusably short plain-text fallback.

2. **Link & Tracking Issues**
- All links route through `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...` — an ESP click-tracking redirector (Sailthru `mclick` pattern), consistent with legitimate mass-mail infrastructure; no anomalies in the token structure observed.
- Open tracking pixel confirmed: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`, flagged by QA as an image with no alt text (expected/benign for a tracking pixel, but see Rendering section).
- QA notes 12 of 59 total links were probed (ranked by click likelihood); 47 (footer/utility/social) were deprioritized. No broken-link data available for the unprobed set — recommend full-link crawl before send if not already part of pre-deploy QA.

3. **Rendering & Accessibility**
- **12 images missing `alt` text** (QA FAIL/WARN), including all primary content modules (`2026-0922-dks-storewide-4pm-new`, `frost-buddy2-...`, team-sports/golf/fan/drinkware/outdoor/cardio modules, and 5 footer icons). Screen-reader users get no fallback content for an email that is entirely image-driven promotional content — this is a functional accessibility gap, not a style nitpick.
- Tracking/open pixel also lacks `alt=""`; should be `alt=""` explicitly (not omitted) to guarantee AT skips it silently.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and `@media` mobile rules are present and structured correctly (`mobile-off`, `mobile-w100`, etc.) — no rendering-engine issues found in the truncated source.
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@media screen { @font-face }` with local() fallbacks and a fallback stack to `Arial, sans-serif` — correct defensive pattern for Outlook/Gmail non-support.

4. **Personalization & Merge Tokens**
- No visible unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) in the truncated source.
- No issues found in the visible source; full-body confirmation not possible from truncation alone.

5. **Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` header not found** — may be a relay artifact (AgentMail) rather than absent at origin, but as observed this email cannot be confirmed to support mailbox-level one-click unsubscribe.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe-Post` header not found (RFC 8058)** — one-click unsubscribe (required by Google/Yahoo bulk sender rules as of 2024) cannot be confirmed present.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unknown from this capture; cannot confirm sender authentication, though this is noted as likely a relay-capture limitation rather than a sending-domain issue.
- An unsubscribe link is confirmed present in-body per QA notes ("an unsubscribe link is always included" in the deprioritized footer set), satisfying baseline CAN-SPAM body-link requirement, but header-level one-click support is unverified.

6. **Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
- Links use ESP-native `mclick` redirect params rather than standard UTM query strings — campaign attribution is presumably encoded in the opaque `params=b.v1...` token server-side rather than in visible UTM tags. Cannot verify landing-page UTM alignment from this source since the redirect destination is opaque until resolved.
- Recommend resolving at least one `mclick` redirect to confirm final landing URL matches the promoted category (clearance/storewide) and that any UTM parameters appended post-redirect are consistent with campaign naming conventions.

7. **Recommendations**
- Add descriptive `alt` text to all 11 content/icon images; set `alt=""` explicitly on the tracking pixel and any purely decorative images.
- Verify `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` headers are present at the true send origin (not just this relay capture) to ensure RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe compliance.
- Fix the plain-text MIME part — 9 characters is not a functional fallback; regenerate a full plain-text alternative reflecting body content and the unsubscribe link.
- Confirm `Authentication-Results` (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) at the origin mail server, independent of this relay's visibility.
- Spot-check a sample of the 47 deprioritized (footer/social/utility) links for validity, since they were not covered by automated probing.
