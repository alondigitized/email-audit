# Deals are still heating up! Up to 50% OFF with our Epic End-of-Summer Sale 🔥
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has been running on repeat since 8/30 — it's now shown up under at least five different subject lines ("Send off summer," "Epic End-of-Summer Sale," "Have you shopped...," and now "Deals are still heating up!"). The creative itself is clean and legible, but the sheer repetition undercuts urgency — if the "epic" sale has been running unchanged for five days, "still heating up" reads as filler, not urgency.
- Body-wise this is a bloated, kitchen-sink send: nine separate modules (HOKA, $50-and-under clothing, YETI Riverhead, racquet gear, NFL, GCI chairs, a second 50%-off football banner, and a six-item "Check out these picks" grid) with no clear hierarchy after the hero. There's no product size/fit/availability info, no reviews, and no loyalty-specific pricing despite a ScoreCard rewards banner up top.
- Net: a competent execution of a subject line that's now stale by cadence and a body that tries to be everything to everyone. Score accordingly — mid-range, dragged down mostly by repetition fatigue rather than by any execution flaw in this specific send.

## What's working

- Hero offer ("UP TO 50% OFF") is bold, high-contrast, and immediately visible without scrolling.
- Clear primary CTA ("Shop Now") directly under the hero with a specific sale name (Epic End-of-Summer Sale).
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kid's, Golf, Outdoor) give an easy secondary path for browsers.
- No render bugs — images, text, and buttons all display cleanly.
- Second "This Week's Deals" 50%-off banner near the bottom reinforces the offer for scrollers who skipped the top.

## What's weak

- Same red-stadium "50% OFF" hero as four-plus prior sends this week — zero creative refresh despite a "new" subject line.
- No end date or countdown anywhere — "Limited Time" appears but with no actual deadline, so urgency is hollow.
- Nine stacked product modules with no visual hierarchy — the eye has nowhere obvious to land after the hero, and it reads as a catalog dump rather than a curated sale.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard-specific discount or points multiplier tied to this sale, despite the ScoreCard banner at the very top.
- Subject line ("still heating up") implies escalation, but the creative and offer are unchanged from days ago — a bait-and-switch on tone if not literal terms.

## Recommendations

- 1. Retire the red-stadium hero for at least one cycle — swap in a different sport/category visual or a countdown-timer creative so five consecutive sends don't look identical in the inbox.
- 2. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 9/6") to the hero and subject line — manufactured urgency without a date is easy to tune out after this many repeats.
- 3. Cut the body from nine modules to three or four — lead with the single best deal (e.g., HOKA 20% off or YETI new collection) and drop the weakest/most generic ones (GCI chairs, generic NFL gear).
- 4. Tie a ScoreCard member perk (bonus points, early access) to this specific sale to justify the rewards banner at the top.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends Sunday: up to 50% off Nike, adidas & more`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on HOKA, YETI, and racquet gear — today only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Score sizes before they're gone — sale ends soon`
