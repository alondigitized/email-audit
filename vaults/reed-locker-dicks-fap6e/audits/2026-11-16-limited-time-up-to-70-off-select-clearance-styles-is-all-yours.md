# Limited time! ⏰ Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles is all yours...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-16**
## Executive summary

- This clearance email opens with a huge red "UP TO 70% OFF" hero, and that is the strongest part of it. The offer is specific, the single "Shop Now" button is clear, and the hierarchy is clean. For a subscriber who wants a deal, the first screen delivers.
- The rest undercuts it. Below the hero, the email carries a second hero, "One Day Amazing Sale: Up to 50% Off", which is the same creative as this morning's 11/16 send. After that come five category blocks at "up to 40%" or "up to 50%", then a gift-picks grid. The 70% headline then applies only to "select clearance styles" with no product shown, so the big number is never backed by anything concrete. The email also gives mixed signals: 70%, 50% and 40% all appear, and the subject promises 70%.
- Relative to the 11/14–11/16 app-exclusive loop ($20 off $100+), this is at least a different offer. But it lands the same day as the one-day sale email, so a subscriber sees two DICK'S promos in one day. I'd judge it a competent but cluttered send. Its clearance angle is real, and the layout dilutes it.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is unmissable and matches the subject line.
- There is one clear red "Shop Now" button directly under the hero headline.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give fast routes into clearance.
- Each category block follows the same pattern of image, headline and green CTA, which makes it easy to scan.
- Footer app badges and the nav bar are well organized.

## What's weak

- The second hero, "One Day Amazing Sale", repeats this morning's email and competes with the clearance message.
- The email shows no clearance products. The 70% claim has no visual proof, while the lower-discount blocks get lifestyle photos.
- The discounts in the body (40%, 50%) are lower than the 70% in the subject. The hero's fine print and the "select styles" wording read as bait-and-switch.
- The email is very long, with six or more modules. The hero CTA is lost once someone scrolls.
- The fine print under the hero is tiny, and the deadline is vague ("limited time").

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the One Day Amazing Sale module and replace it with 4–6 real clearance products showing the original price and the clearance price. This lifts the email the most.
- 2. Give the "70%" a real deadline, such as "ends Sunday", instead of "limited time".
- 3. Reduce the category blocks from five to three, led by whichever has the deepest clearance discount.
- 4. Don't send this within hours of the one-day-sale email. Space the sends or merge them.
- 5. Make the hero CTA specific, such as "Shop Clearance".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 70% off: ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Shoes, fleece & gear, now up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, hoodies and golf gear marked down. Sizes are going fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stack it with free pickup. Online and in-store clearance.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML source is structurally sound. It uses role="presentation" tables, a 600px max-width wrapper, a mobile media query and an MSO conditional block. The confirmed defects are missing alt text on 12 images (content banners and footer icons), an invalid JSON-LD block, and very small mobile font sizes. The header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 52 skipped. The QA output lists no failed probes, but it also gives no per-link status, so link health is not confirmed.
- **The HTML is truncated.** No `href` values or UTM parameters are visible, so link-level tracking can't be audited.
- **The tracking pixel is hidden correctly.** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1e52bbcfda9ac976e96c65848/43001fefd3db49b700004c5a42963aa1` is set to `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `alt`, `width` or `height`, which is low impact because it is hidden. It is also what triggered the first alt-text warning.
- **Sender and tracking domains differ.** The From domain is `e.dcsg.com` and the pixel domain is `app.dickssportinggoods.com`. This is not a confirmed defect, but check that both are covered by the same authentication and alignment setup.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images have no alt text (confirmed).** The six hero and banner images are:
  - `2026-1116-dks-stw-11am-mens`
  - `2026-1116-dks-stw-11am-womens-1`
  - `2026-1116-dks-stw-11am-kids`
  - `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`
  - `2026-1116-dks-stw-11am-fan`
  - `2026-1116-dks-stw-11am-golf2`

  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, the offer content is unavailable. These are likely the clickable CTAs.
- **Footer icons have no alt text (confirmed).** The five icons are `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`. Because they sit next to link text, they should use `alt=""` explicitly. A missing attribute is not the same as an empty one.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JS `//` comments, which are illegal in JSON, so the whole block fails to parse:
  - `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`
  - `// Sample image is 538x138...`
  - `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`
- **Unfinished template values.** The same block still has `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""` and `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`.
- **Orphan closing comment.** `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` appears with no matching opening comment in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this may be a false positive.
- **Mobile text is too small.** In `@media screen and (max-width:480px)`:
  - `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px.
  - `.legal` is 8px.
  - `.body-m` is 11px.

  These fall below common legibility guidance of at least 11–14px.
- **Stray quote in the MSO block.** The `div` rule ends in `padding:0;"}`. The stray `"` is harmless in practice, but it is malformed CSS.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The source is truncated, so tokens further down can't be ruled out.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058) are not found.** The QA tool itself says this may be an AgentMail relay capture artifact, so treat it as unverified, not as a defect. Check the raw message headers at the sending ESP or in an unrelayed mailbox.
- **Authentication-Results is not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown for the same relay reason. Verify it on a direct-delivery copy.
- **Footer elements are not visible.** The physical address and in-body unsubscribe link are in the truncated portion. The QA tool states an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set, but gives no result.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and the QA output gives no landing page results. Findings from the 12 probed links would be needed to confirm that the destinations match the "up to 70% off clearance" offer.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six content banners, such as "Up to 70% off clearance – Men's". Use `alt=""` on the five decorative footer icons and the tracking pixel.
2. Fix or remove the JSON-LD block. Strip the `//` comments and either populate real promo values (image, dates) or delete the unused `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` entries so Gmail annotations can work.
3. Raise the mobile minimum font sizes (`.legal`, `.body-sm`, `.link-sm`) to at least 11px, preferably 12px or more.
4. Re-run QA against the full, untruncated HTML plus raw headers. That would confirm List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post, SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment, the footer address and unsubscribe link, and per-link UTMs and landing page status.
5. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO `div` rule and the orphan `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` comment if confirmed.
