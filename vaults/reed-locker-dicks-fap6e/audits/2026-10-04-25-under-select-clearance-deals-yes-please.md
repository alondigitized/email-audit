# $25 & under select clearance DEALS? Yes, please! 🙌
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clean, single-message send. A giant white "$25 AND UNDER" sits on deep red, with "Clearance Deals" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The eye lands on the price-point hook immediately, and I see no broken images or layout problems.
- It's also a near-repeat of yesterday's 10/3 "$25 & under? That's right! Check out these clearance savings >>>". The hero is the same and so is the hook. That makes it the second "$25 and under" send in about 24 hours, after the 10/2 "extra 25% off clearance" double-send. A subscriber sees DICK'S hitting the same clearance beat daily with a new emoji.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long catalog: $100 and under shoes, CALIA/VRST/DSG apparel, racquet gear, NFL, bikes, Peloton shoes, "up to 50% off", an app promo and a product grid. The "$25 and under" promise is never paid off with a single product at that price. The email is competent and on-brand, but it's repetitive and unfocused after the first screen.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: huge white "$25 AND UNDER" on red, readable at a glance.
- A green Shop Now button sits directly under the headline, so the primary CTA is obvious.
- The price-point framing ("$25 and under") is a concrete, easy-to-grasp value hook.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, All Clearance, All Sale) let a shopper jump straight to their section.
- The render is clean: no overlapping text, no broken image slots, and consistent module layout with a clear CTA per block.

## What's weak

- The hero offers no product. "Clearance Deals" is generic, and nothing at $25 or under is shown anywhere.
- After the hero, the email dilutes into about eight competing offers ($100 and under, 70% off, 50% off, 40% off, 30% off, 20% off, and "up to 50% off" again). The "$25 and under" message gets buried.
- The "up to 50% off" banner repeats the 9/29–10/1 creative, and the "$25 and under" hero repeats yesterday's. It feels like the same email recycled.
- The deadlines are vague ("limited time", "while quantities last"). The only date I can see is tiny fine print on the NFL module.
- The fine print under the hero is dense and small, and the long footer disclaimers add to the "fine print" feel.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pay off the hero. Replace the generic "Clearance Deals" copy with 4–6 real products at $25 or under (a tee, socks, a cap, a water bottle) in a grid directly under the hero. A tiled "$25 & under" grid would do more for clicks than the whole bottom half of the email.
- 2. Cut the email to the one theme. Drop the Peloton, bikes and racquet modules, or move them to a separate send, so the message stays "$25 and under".
- 3. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT") in the hero or the subject line.
- 4. Vary the creative day to day. Don't send the same hero within 24 hours. Use a new angle, such as "Best-selling $25 finds".
- 5. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Clearance Under $25" instead of "Shop Now".
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance finds, all $25 & under (ends Sunday)`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Tees, socks & caps for $25 or less 🧢`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shop 100s of clearance styles before they sell out. Ends Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's, women's and kids' picks, none over $25.`
