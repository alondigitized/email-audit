# ⏰ EXTRA 25% off select clearance ends tonight — shop now! ⏰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-26**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the exact same "EXTRA 25% OFF" red hero from the 9/25 send, just with an updated subject line adding "ends tonight" urgency. The hero itself is clean and legible — big bold white type on a deep red background — but everything below it is the same undifferentiated kitchen-sink scroll: Under Armour shoes, NFL gear, golf balls, luggage, a six-pack of "picks." There's no attempt to personalize or prioritize what follows the fold.
- The core offer ("Extra 25% Off Select Clearance") is honest and simple, but the brand has now sent nearly identical clearance/flash-sale creative five times in the past three days (9/23 x2, 9/24 x2, 9/25 x2). A subscriber checking their inbox this week has seen this red/orange lightning-bolt rotation enough times that the urgency ("ends tonight") reads as manufactured rather than credible — DICK'S has cried wolf on deadlines before (9/24's flash sale genuinely ended that night, but there've been multiple "final hours" and "don't miss" sends since).
- Verdict: technically clean, zero render bugs, one clear CTA — but cadence fatigue is the real problem here, and this send does nothing to address it.

## What's working

- Hero is unmissable: large white "25% OFF" on saturated red, readable at a glance.
- Single, unambiguous offer stated twice (hero + subhead: "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance").
- Clear green "Shop Now" CTA button directly under the offer, no scroll needed.
- Fine print (valid through 9/26 11:59PM PT, exclusions) is present but not distracting from the hero.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a fast path to filtered browsing.

## What's weak

- Same red hero DICK'S already ran verbatim on 9/25 — zero creative refresh despite a new subject line.
- After the hero, the email reverts to a generic, unsegmented catalog dump (shoes, NFL, golf balls, luggage) unrelated to "clearance."
- This is the sixth near-identical flash/clearance send in four days — cadence fatigue undercuts the "ends tonight" urgency.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard-specific offer or points multiplier tied to this clearance push, despite ScoreCard branding at the top.
- "Ends Tonight" urgency label is small and easy to miss above the subhead — it's the whole reason for this send and it's visually underweighted.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop reusing the identical hero creative — even a palette swap or new headline treatment would help subscribers distinguish this from 9/25's send.
- 2. Cut send frequency — six clearance/flash emails in four days trains subscribers to ignore the urgency; consolidate to one strong "final hours" send per deadline.
- 3. Make "Ends Tonight" the dominant headline treatment (larger, bannered) rather than small text above the subhead — it's the actual news in this send.
- 4. Replace the generic catalog scroll with clearance-only merchandising to match the subject line's promise.
- 5. Add a ScoreCard points angle (e.g., "+2X points on clearance today") to justify a member-only reason to open now.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance cart expires tonight — extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel & gear — final markdowns before it's gone`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Members: stack points on clearance before 11:59PM PT`
