# Ready to save? Shop an EXTRA 25% off select clearance. 💰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the same DICK'S stadium-seating "EXTRA/UP TO X% OFF" playbook that's dominated every send since 8/30, but the hero copy here is at least distinct from the "UP TO 50%" wall-to-wall creative flagged repeatedly this week — it's a cleaner "EXTRA 25% OFF" block with a real deadline and a single green CTA. Below the fold it immediately reverts to form: a second "UP TO 50% OFF Epic End-of-Summer Sale" module stacked directly under the first offer, diluting the headline promo before the reader even scrolls past it.
- The email is otherwise a kitchen-sink module dump — HOKA shoes, ScoreCard bonus reward, DSG family clothing, Owala tumblers, football gear, Mizuno golf clubs, GCI chairs, "check out these picks" grid — nine-plus distinct offers competing for attention with no real hierarchy after the top block. For a clearance-focused subject line, only the first two modules and the DSG line actually reference clearance/sale; the rest is generic catalog filler.
- Judgment: functional, on-brand, no render bugs, but it's the same fatigued cadence — a decent top-of-email hook buried under an unfocused module cascade. 5/10 territory, consistent with every other DICK'S send this week.

## What's working

- Hero is clean and legible: bold "EXTRA 25% OFF" on the recognizable red/maroon stadium background, single green "Shop Now" CTA, and a clear "Limited Time" + expiration deadline ("9/08 11:59PM ET").
- Subject and hero copy are aligned — this is one of the few DICK'S sends this week where the top-of-email actually matches the subject line instead of a mismatched generic sale banner.
- Category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) right under the hero gives a quick escape hatch.
- No visible render bugs — images load, text doesn't overlap, layout is intact end to end.
- ScoreCard $10 Bonus Reward module is genuinely useful loyalty content for members, with clear sign-in/join CTAs.

## What's weak

- A second, near-identical "UP TO 50% OFF Epic End-of-Summer Sale" module appears immediately below the primary 25% offer — same red stadium-seat treatment I've flagged in the last five sends — which confuses which offer is actually the headline.
- After the top two modules, the email sprawls into 7+ unrelated categories (golf clubs, tumblers, camp chairs, backpacks) with no organizing theme, undermining the "clearance" subject line promise.
- No product-level fine print visibility issue, but the "Extra 25%" fine print ("Exclusions apply... while quantities last") sits directly under the CTA in tiny gray text, which reads as the now-standard exclusion hedge this brand always includes.
- Cadence fatigue: this is at least the sixth DICK'S promotional email this week with an "X% OFF" stadium-seating hero; a subscriber opening daily will find nothing novel in the format itself.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the redundant "Up to 50% off Epic End-of-Summer Sale" module entirely, or move it far below the fold — running it directly under the "Extra 25% Off" hero cannibalizes the one CTA you actually want clicked.
- 2. Trim the email to clearance-relevant modules only (DSG family clothing, HOKA, football gear if on clearance) and cut golf clubs/tumblers/chairs to a separate non-clearance send — a subject that promises "clearance" should deliver mostly clearance.
- 3. Add a specific clearance category or product tease in the subject/preheader instead of generic "select clearance" — e.g., naming the DSG or footwear category that's actually discounted.
- 4. Move the ScoreCard $10 Bonus Reward module higher — it's the strongest loyalty hook in the email and currently sits mid-scroll behind unrelated product modules.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — footwear, apparel & gear`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Extra 25% off select clearance, ends 9/8`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus, ScoreCard members earn a $10 bonus reward on $150+`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New clearance markdowns just added across shoes, apparel & gear`
