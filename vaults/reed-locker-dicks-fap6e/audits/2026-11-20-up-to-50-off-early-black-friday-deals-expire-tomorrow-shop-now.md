# ⏰ Up to 50% off Early Black Friday DEALS expire tomorrow ⏰ Shop now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth DICK'S send in two days with an "up to X% off" hero, and it is the most cluttered of them. The big red "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" banner and "Up to 50% Off / Two Days Only" tell you the event in a second. Directly below sits a second hero, "UP TO 80% OFF", which contradicts the 50% headline. The subject says 50% and "expire tomorrow", while the body promises "Two Days Only" and then 80% on clearance. A reader can't tell which offer is the real one.
- The body then runs about eight stacked modules: adidas, CALIA/VRST, HydroJug, FootJoy, NHL, outdoor and a gift grid. Each has its own green "Shop Now" button. Product-level offers like "$50 off FootJoy" and "20% off adidas Adizero" are concrete. The sheer length dilutes urgency, though. The deadline is only in small gray print.
- Verdict: a competent but overbuilt cadence send. It works as a reminder for someone already shopping Black Friday. It does little to set itself apart from the 11/20 "Starting NOW: Up to 50% off" send earlier today.

## What's working

- The "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE" hero is large and high-contrast, so the event reads instantly.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives a quick route into the sale.
- Product modules have specific offers, such as "20% Off adidas Adizero EVO SL Running Shoes" and "$50 Off Select FootJoy HyperFlex Golf Shoes".
- The ScoreCard strip and top "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" bar surface loyalty and fast delivery.
- Buttons are consistent green "Shop Now" and easy to find.

## What's weak

- Two stacked heroes (50% and 80%) compete and conflict. The 80% only applies to clearance, which is buried in the subhead.
- The "Shop Now" CTA is repeated about nine times and is generic.
- The deadline is small gray fine print. There's no countdown or bold date, even though the subject says "expire tomorrow".
- The email is very long, and the hero-level 50% offer gets lost among category tiles and gift picks.
- The product grids are mixed (golf, NHL, kayaks, hydration), so there's no clear focus on a single shopper need.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer. Lead with the 50% event, and make 80% a clearly labeled clearance tile further down.
- 2. Put a bold "Ends 11/21, 11:59PM PT" line, or a countdown, directly under the hero. It should match the subject's "tomorrow".
- 3. Cut the body to three or four modules and send the rest to the site. Use verb-plus-noun CTAs like "Shop Running Shoes" and "Shop Golf Deals".
- 4. Vary creative day to day. This is the fourth near-identical "up to X%" send in about 48 hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Early Black Friday ends tomorrow: up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last day: 50% off gear + 80% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Adidas runners 20% off, FootJoy golf shoes $50 off. Ends 11/21 at 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order today, pick up tomorrow. Thousands of styles, one more day.`
