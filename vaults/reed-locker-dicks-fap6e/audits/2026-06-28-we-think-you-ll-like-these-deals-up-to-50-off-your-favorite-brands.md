# We think you'll like these deals — up to 50% off your favorite brands!
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-28**
## Executive summary

- This is at least the fourth distinctly-framed DICK'S email in two days, arriving on the same calendar date as the "$25 and under clearance" send already audited at 8/10. The execution quality is fine — the beach hero is seasonally right, the 50% ceiling is prominent in the creative, and the product grid scrolls cleanly without layout breaks. But the strategic problem is hard to overlook: yesterday's June 27 send was titled "Up to 50% off this week's deals is yours for the win!" and scored 8/10 in this audit log. Today's subject recycles nearly the same discount claim, the same hero format, and the same "SHOP NOW" button. That is not a campaign — it's a calendar slot filled.
- The subject line tries a personalization angle — "We think you'll like these deals" and "your favorite brands" — but the body never delivers on it. No brand logos, no brand callouts, no curated product list tied to browsing history. The promise is made in the subject and broken in the creative.
- **Verdict:** Competent assembly, near-zero strategic differentiation from yesterday's send. A subscriber who opened the June 27 "50% off deals" email has no new information and no new reason to click.

## What's working

- Hero "UP TO 50% OFF" is immediately scannable; beach background earns the summer frame without over-designing
- Category nav (Golf, Women's, Kids, Shoes, The Summer Shop) gives a clear secondary roadmap for shoppers who scroll past the hero
- The $19.98 DSG Agility Pants price anchor is specific and credible — one of the strongest concrete signals in the email
- Running shoes product photography is the best visual in the send; colorful, high-contrast, recognizable silhouettes
- No visible render bugs — layout is clean end-to-end

## What's weak

- Near-identical framing to the June 27 "Up to 50% off this week's deals" email — cadence fatigue is severe at this point
- "Your favorite brands" in the subject is never delivered in the body — no brand logos, no callouts, no curation visible
- Every CTA reads "SHOP NOW" — no verb+noun specificity guiding the click anywhere specific
- Running shoes section says "Up to 40% Off Select" with no brand name, model, or style callout — undersells the best visual in the email
- The email covers golf, running, men's pants, summer drinkware, and women's apparel simultaneously — no coherent audience

## Recommendations

- 1. **Pick one story** — the running shoes section is the strongest visual and the 40% discount is concrete; center the entire email on it with a brand callout and retire the catch-all hero.
- 2. **Make the subject promise match the body** — if "your favorite brands" is in the subject, brand logos must appear in the hero or the first scroll. Right now it's a broken promise.
- 3. **Differentiate from yesterday** — the June 27 "50% off deals" email already landed. Today needs a different angle (category-specific, brand-specific, or a price-floor anchor) or it reads as a resend.
- 4. **Sharpen the CTAs** — "Shop Nike Running" or "Shop Summer Essentials Under $25" outperform "SHOP NOW" every time on CTR.
- 5. **Shorten the subject** — 71 characters will be truncated on most mobile clients; cut to under 50.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Nike, Brooks & more — this week only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your running shoes are on sale — up to 40% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `DSG Agility Pants from $19.98 + summer must-haves`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Stanley, OtterBox, top shoe brands — deals end Sunday`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Promotional Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses Epsilon/Harmony as the ESP with standard redirect-based click tracking. The primary technical defects are in the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block: invalid JSON syntax and unfilled template placeholders will silently break Gmail Promotions tab rendering.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel** — present via `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f0ed1e903a8b2d16e965fc958/...` with correct `display:none` / `max-height:0` suppression. No issue.

**Click tracking** — all hrefs route through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019f0ed1e903a8b2d16e965fc958/...`. Redirect chain is intact.

**UTM parameters** — not visible on any tracked link in the provided source. If UTMs are injected at redirect resolution (server-side), no action needed; if they are absent end-to-end, site analytics will attribute all traffic to `(direct)/(none)`. Cannot confirm from truncated source — flag for QA to verify final destination URLs.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`border-collapse:separate` + `border-spacing:0`** on all `<table>` elements — functionally equivalent to `collapse` but the combination is redundant and can trigger rendering quirks in older Outlook builds. Low severity.

**Conditional VML block** — `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>` present with correct PNG/DPI settings and table reset styles. No issue.

**`a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override** — present. No issue.

**`<link rel="icon">` in `<head>`** — `href="https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/home/assets/icons/dsg/favicon.ico"` is ignored by all major email clients and adds an unnecessary external DNS lookup on open. Remove.

**Logo `src` URL truncated** in provided source (`...brand-logo--dk…`) — cannot validate whether alt text is set. Confirm `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"` (or equivalent) is present on the logo `<img>`.

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

### 5. Compliance

**Sending domain** — `From: DSG@e.dcsg.com`. `e.dcsg.com` is a dedicated ESP subdomain, consistent with standard DKIM/SPF alignment. Cannot verify authentication headers (SPF pass, DKIM signature, DMARC policy) from HTML source alone — confirm via a sent-message header inspection.

---

### 6. JSON-LD / Gmail Promotions Tab — **Critical**

Three defects in `<script type="application/ld+json">`:

**a) JavaScript comments inside JSON — invalid syntax**

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

`//` comments are not valid JSON. Google's schema parser will reject the entire `PromotionCard` object, meaning the Promotions tab image will not render. Strip all comments before send.

**b) Unfilled template placeholders**

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` (empty) |
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` (empty) |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` (empty) |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` (empty) |

These are designer-facing template stubs that were never populated. A `PromotionCard` with an empty `image` will not display in the Gmail Promotions tab carousel.

**c) Stale `availabilityStarts` timestamp**

```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```

This date is ~6 years in the past. Gmail may deprioritize or suppress the offer card. Update to the actual campaign start date.

---

### 7. Email-to-Site Continuity

UTM parameter presence on final destination URLs cannot be confirmed from the redirect-wrapped links in the source. QA must follow at least one `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` redirect and inspect the resolved URL for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign`.

### Recommendations (Priority Order)

1. **[Critical — blocks Gmail feature]** Strip `//` comments from the JSON-LD block. JSON does not permit comments; the `PromotionCard` object will be silently dropped by Gmail's parser.
2. **[Critical — blocks Gmail feature]** Populate `PromotionCard.image` with the actual promo image URL before send.
3. **[High]** Update `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` from `2020-03-06` to the correct campaign start date; populate `availabilityEnds` and `description`.
4. **[High]** Verify footer contains physical mailing address and one-click unsubscribe link (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5)).
5. **[Medium]** Confirm UTM parameters survive the redirect chain on all tracked links.
6. **[Low]** Remove `<link rel="icon">` from `<head>` — non-functional in email clients.
7. **[Low]** Confirm logo `<img>` carries a descriptive `alt` attribute.
