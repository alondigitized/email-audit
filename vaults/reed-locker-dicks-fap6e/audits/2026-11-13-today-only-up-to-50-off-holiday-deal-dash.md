# TODAY ONLY 🔔 Up to 50% off Holiday DEAL Dash
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a loud, clear hero on top of a very long body. The red panel with giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" lands instantly, and "Holiday Deal Dash: Up to 50% Off" with a "Shop Now" button sits right under it. The hero still doesn't say what the 50% applies to. A "Shop by sport" style chip row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) is the only hint at categories.
- The email then stacks a second red hero, "EXTRA 25% OFF" select clearance, directly below the first. That is the same clearance creative DICK'S sent on 11/11 and 11/12. A subscriber has now seen this panel three days running, so the 25% block feels like recycled filler. Below it come running shoes, a fleece, a HydroJug, golf bags, bats, a treadmill, a ScoreCard+ pitch and a product grid. It is a catalog, not a flash sale.
- Judgement: solid first screen, but "TODAY ONLY" is contradicted by the body. The only date cue is small fine print ("through 11/13/26 11:59PM PT"). There is no countdown, unlike the 11/10 "Final Hours" strip that worked well. It is a competent send that dilutes its own urgency.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on red registers in about a second.
- A single "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero headline.
- The chip row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) gives quick category entry.
- Product tiles carry specific offers: "Up to 40% off select running shoes" and "Sale $34.98 Men's or Women's DSG Sport Fleece".
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" header bar adds a concrete fulfillment benefit.
- Product photography and holiday styling are consistent and render cleanly, with no broken images.

## What's weak

- The hero never says what is 50% off. The gold block is all number and no product.
- "TODAY ONLY" has no countdown and no visible deadline in the hero. The end time is buried in fine print.
- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance panel repeats the 11/11 and 11/12 creative and competes with the lead offer.
- Many sections ("Gifts Only at DICK'S", HydroJug, golf, bats, treadmill, ScoreCard+ upsell) pull attention in different directions.
- Fine print is long, and legal text and Rewards terms bloat the footer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add a live countdown strip above the hero ("Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT") to make "TODAY ONLY" credible. This lifts the email most.
- 2. Put product imagery or category names in the 50% hero so the offer says what it applies to.
- 3. Cut the clearance panel or merge it into a single line. Don't repeat 11/12's hero.
- 4. Trim the body to four or five tiles, led by the best-value items (the $34.98 fleece and the running shoes).
- 5. Move the ScoreCard+ upsell lower, or make it a slim banner.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off Deal Dash`
- **Subject Alt B:** `12 hours left: 50% off sneakers & fleece`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running shoes up to 40% off, DSG fleece $34.98 — ends 11:59 PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One-day Holiday Deal Dash. Plus extra 25% off clearance.`
