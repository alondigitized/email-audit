# 💸 1-day DEALS & epic savings going on NOW 💸 Shop DICK'S Deal Days!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the third DICK'S email on June 24 — following the brand-naming 9/10 send ("Don't miss BIG savings on top brands: VRST, CALIA, Blackstone, Quest & more!") and the urgency-refresh 8/10 "NEW DEALS ADDED🚨" send. Cadence fatigue is now the primary drag on this email's effectiveness, and the subject line compounds the problem by stepping backward to generic "epic savings" language after the previous send already demonstrated that brand-naming in the subject works better.
- The body is competently assembled. The Deal Days green/black visual system is consistent, the product tiles are clean, named brands with specific dollar discounts are visible, and the Extra 30% Off clearance section adds a genuine second hook. But there's a real message conflict: the subject promises "1-day DEALS" while the body header reads "Shop these 3-Day Deals before it's too late!" — a subscriber who notices that inconsistency will feel misled.
- The email does its job as a deal aggregator, but it is the weakest subject-line execution in this Deal Days run and arrives at peak inbox saturation for this brand. As a standalone piece, it's solid 6-territory creative attached to a badly timed and under-specified send.

## What's working

- **Named brands with concrete discounts** are visible in the product tiles (VRST, CALIA, Tommy Hilfiger, DSG, Brooks) — the body delivers specificity the subject failed to tease.
- **Extra 30% Off clearance section** is visually prominent with a high-contrast gold banner — the strongest value signal in the email.
- **Visual hierarchy is clean** — hero → SHOP NOW → product grid → secondary deals → clearance — no confusion about where to look.
- **No render bugs** visible; the Deal Days design system holds up cleanly across all sections.
- **Multiple distinct SHOP NOW CTAs** per product tile reduce cognitive friction for a subscriber who already knows what they want.

## What's weak

- **Subject line retreats to generic copy** ("epic savings") the day after a 9/10 brand-naming subject — this is a measurable step backward in click signal.
- **"1-day DEALS" in the subject vs. "3-Day Deals" in the body header** is a direct contradiction that erodes trust.
- **Subject is ~66 characters**, well past the ~50-char mobile truncation threshold — "Shop DICK'S Deal Days!" is wasted tail copy most inboxes will cut.
- **Third email on June 24** — open rates for this send are almost certainly suppressed by list fatigue from the prior two same-day sends.
- **Clearance section is below the fold** — the 30% off hook, potentially the most compelling offer here, is buried after two full product sections.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Move the 30% off clearance offer into the subject line** — it's the most universally compelling offer in the email and is currently invisible from the inbox.
- 2. **Fix the 1-day vs. 3-day contradiction** — pick one framing and make it consistent from subject through body.
- 3. **Trim the subject to under 50 chars** — "DICK'S Deal Days" plus the brand-naming approach that worked yesterday is more than enough.
- 4. **Name at least one brand in the subject**, as the 9/10 June 24 send proved — "VRST, Brooks + 30% off clearance" outperforms "epic savings" every time.
- 5. **Consolidate to one June 24 send** or push this creative to June 25 — three emails in a single day trains subscribers to ignore all three.

- **Subject Alt B:** `VRST, Brooks, DSG — up to 30% off today only`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Deal Days Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses Epsilon/Harmony as the ESP with standard redirect-based tracking. The most significant technical defect is an invalid `application/ld+json` block that will silently fail structured data parsing in Gmail, suppressing the Promotions Tab carousel annotation entirely.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel:** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019efb5185cfa6e4c36e96c660f0/...` — correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px`.

**Click tracking:** All links route through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` with a `__dU__` parameter — standard Epsilon redirect pattern. No issues with the mechanism itself.

**Destination UTM params:** Cannot verify from truncated source. Final landing page UTM attribution (e.g., `utm_source=email&utm_campaign=deal_days`) must be confirmed in untruncated link targets — if the `/T/` redirects strip params or land on non-instrumented pages, attribution breaks.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`border-collapse:separate` on wrapper:** Unusual for email. Most clients expect `collapse`. Combined with `border-spacing:0` it is functionally equivalent here, but Outlook on Windows has historically mis-rendered `separate` in some versions — low risk but worth auditing in Litmus/Email on Acid.

**Mobile breakpoint:** `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` — 480px is below the modern convention of 600px. Gmail on Android and Outlook Mobile may not trigger the responsive stack. 480px was standard ~2018; bump to 600px for broader coverage.

**Font loading:** Archivo loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` via `@font-face` inside `@media screen`. Fallback to Arial is defined and correct. No issues with the fallback chain itself, but Google Fonts requests are blocked by some corporate firewalls and privacy-hardened clients.

**`role="presentation"` on tables:** Present and correct throughout visible source.

**`alt` attributes on images:** Logo `<img>` tag is truncated before the `alt` attribute is visible — cannot confirm. All images must have non-empty `alt` text for accessibility and plain-text rendering clients.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**`id="header-non-sc-member"`** on the header table indicates conditional ScoreCard member segmentation is in use — this is expected for a loyalty program send, but the segment condition must be verified in the ESP template logic to ensure non-members don't see a blank header if the conditional fails to evaluate.

No unresolved merge tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `*|`) visible in the truncated source.

---

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Unsubscribe / physical address:** Both required by CAN-SPAM — not visible in the truncated source (they belong in the footer). Must be verified in the full HTML.

**`<!--DKS Email Marketing 2025-->` comment:** The year in the template comment is 2025; the email is deploying in 2026. Not a compliance issue, but indicates a stale template base that has not been updated.

**Authentication headers (DKIM/SPF/DMARC):** Cannot be assessed from HTML source alone. For `DSG@e.dcsg.com`, the sending domain `e.dcsg.com` should have a valid SPF record and DKIM signature aligned to the `From:` domain. Verify via MX Toolbox or header inspection in a received copy.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**`application/ld+json` block — critical defect:**

The Gmail Promotions Tab structured data block contains JavaScript-style line comments, which are **not valid JSON**:

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```

JSON does not allow `//` comments. Any JSON parser — including Gmail's structured data extractor — will reject this block entirely. As a result:
- The Gmail Promotions Tab annotation (deal image, discount, expiry) will not render
- The `DiscountOffer` block is also malformed: `discountCode: ""`, `description: ""`, and `availabilityEnds: ""` are all empty strings
- `availabilityStarts: "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` is a 6-year-old placeholder date — clearly a forgotten template default

**`PromotionCard` `image` field is empty:** Even if the JSON were valid, the promo image URL was never populated (`"image": ""`). The Gmail carousel image will be missing.

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **P0** | Invalid JSON-LD (`//` comments) | Strip all `//` comments from `<script type="application/ld+json">` before send — comments are illegal in JSON |
| **P0** | Empty `image` in `PromotionCard` | Populate with a valid 538×138px static image URL (no GIF/WebP) |
| **P0** | Stale `availabilityStarts: "2020-03-06"` | Replace with current deal start date/time |
| **P1** | Empty `discountCode` / `availabilityEnds` | Populate or remove — empty strings degrade Gmail structured data quality |
| **P1** | Mobile breakpoint at 480px | Update to 600px for modern client coverage |
| **P1** | UTM params on final destinations | Confirm `/T/` redirects preserve `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` through to landing pages |
| **P2** | `border-collapse:separate` | Test in Outlook 2016/2019 on Windows; switch to `collapse` if rendering issues found |
| **P2** | `alt` attributes | Verify all `<img>` tags have descriptive `alt` text in full source |
| **P2** | Unsubscribe + physical address | Confirm both are present in footer (CAN-SPAM requirement) |
| **P3** | Template year comment (2025) | Update to 2026 to avoid confusion in future audits |
