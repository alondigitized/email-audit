# FINAL HOURS for up to 50% off 📣 Shop the Holiday Deal Dash now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-03**
## Executive summary

- This is the closing-day send of DICK'S Holiday Deal Dash, and it commits to the urgency. A green countdown bar reading "Final Hours 07:59:48" sits above a giant red argyle hero with a gold "UP TO 50% OFF". A "Final Hours" kicker and a single red Shop Now button sit directly under it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the deadline (11/3/26 11:59PM PT) is credible and stated.
- The problem is what follows. After the hero come category pills, an Owala promo, an NHL/NBA/NFL fan shop block, a second giant "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance hero and a six-product grid. That's four offers and four Shop Now buttons in one email. The 70% clearance hero competes with the 50% headline the subject sells. The 70% clearance send went out earlier the same day ("We think you'll like this...Up to 70% off select clearance"), so it's a repeat. This is also at least the fourth Deal Dash email since 11/1, after the 11/2 "2. Days. Only!" and "ends tomorrow" sends and the morning "ends today" one.
- Judgement: a solid, conventional last-call email with a real countdown, but it's diluted by the repeated clearance hero and the "Final Hours" label on a fan shop deal that's only 40%. It's a 7 on execution and a 5 on freshness, since subscribers have seen this creative for three days.

## What's working

- The live-looking countdown banner ("Final Hours 07:59:48") makes the urgency concrete and visible before the hero.
- The gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on red argyle is instantly readable and puts the offer first.
- The single red Shop Now button directly under the hero is unambiguous.
- Fine print is stated with an explicit end date and time (11/3/26 11:59PM PT), so the deadline feels honest.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give fast routing to a relevant category.

## What's weak

- Four competing blocks (50% hero, Owala, Fan Shop 40%, 70% clearance hero) dilute the "final hours" message.
- The second giant 70% hero is nearly as loud as the first and tells the reader the 50% deal isn't the best one.
- The Owala "Just Launched" block, with drops on 11/15, has nothing to do with a last-day sale.
- The product grid at the bottom has no prices or discounts, so there's no reason to click a specific shoe or hoodie.
- The hero has no product imagery, so there's no sense of what's on sale. It's pure typography.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the 50% hero, the countdown and one product row with sale prices. Drop the 70% clearance repeat and the Owala launch block for this send.
- 2. Add "ends at 11:59PM PT tonight" to the CTA area or button copy, e.g. "Shop Deals Before Midnight", instead of the generic "Shop Now".
- 3. Put price-tagged products (was/now) right under the hero, replacing the unpriced grid.
- 4. Hold the NHL/NBA/NFL block for a separate send, or tie it to the deadline.
- 5. Space the cadence out. Four Deal Dash sends in three days trains subscribers to ignore the last-chance one.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: Holiday Deal Dash, up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: 8 hours left on up to 50% off 📣`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sale prices on shoes, hoodies and fan gear end at 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `One-hour store pickup still available. Deals end at midnight PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is table-based, with `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag and Outlook conditional comments, so the structure is sound. The confirmed defects are a missing plain-text alternative (9 characters), missing `alt` attributes on content images, and an apparently empty preheader slot. The header-based compliance checks could not be confirmed because of the relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 material links were probed and 44 were skipped (deprioritized footer, utility and social links). Those 44 are unverified, not known to be broken.
- **The open-tracking pixel has no `alt`.** It is an `<img>` to `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...` at `width="1" height="1"`. It sits directly inside `<body>` before the wrapper table. This is a minor accessibility flag, and `alt=""` would be correct for it.
- **Link attributes can't be audited.** The source is truncated at the first `<a class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK">`, so I can't check the `href` values, redirect wrapping or HTTPS usage.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Content images are missing `alt` text.** This is confirmed by QA for:
  - `owalaholiday` (scene7 `DSGAEMSites/owalaholiday`)
  - `fan-shop-2026-1102-d` (`fmt=png-alpha&wid=1200&hei=1600`)
  - The icons `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`
  
  With images blocked or a screen reader, the promo images carry no information. If the icons sit next to visible text, `alt=""` is acceptable, but the attribute is currently absent.
- **The preheader appears empty.** The `<td id="ssl">` contains the leftover template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`, followed only by `#pht-spacer` filled with `&nbsp;`/zero-width characters. No preheader text precedes the spacer in the visible source. Clients will fall back to body text for the inbox snippet, and the "FINAL HOURS" subject gets no support from a preheader. I can't see past the truncation, so treat this as likely rather than certain.
- **The web font is only a progressive enhancement.** Archivo loads from `fonts.gstatic.com` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback, which is safe.
- **No other problems seen.** The `<title>`, the `role="presentation"` layout tables, the `mso-hide` on the spacer and the `a[x-apple-data-detectors]` override are all fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{`, `%%`, `[[`, `${`) appear in the visible source or subject.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] The plain-text part is only 9 characters.** The email is effectively HTML-only, or the text part is a stub. This hurts spam scoring and accessibility, and it is the only confirmed compliance and deliverability failure.
- **[WARN] No `List-Unsubscribe` or `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header was found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these, so this is unconfirmed. The sender is DICK'S (`info@em.dcsg.com`), and Gmail and Yahoo expect one-click unsubscribe from bulk senders, so verify against the raw message at the original receiving MTA.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` is absent.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown, again likely a relay artifact. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` is aligned with the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com`, which is a positive indicator but not proof.
- **Not verifiable from the truncated source:** the physical postal address and the in-body unsubscribe link. QA says an unsubscribe link was included in link probing, but its result isn't shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no `href`s, so UTM parameters, redirect behavior and landing-page match (Holiday Deal Dash, Owala, fan shop) can't be verified. This is not a confirmed issue.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Generate it from the HTML, including links and an unsubscribe URL.
2. **Fix the preheader.** Replace the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder with actual preheader text ahead of `#pht-spacer`.
3. **Set `alt` on all images.** Use descriptive text for `owalaholiday` and `fan-shop-2026-1102-d`, and `alt=""` for decorative icons and the 1×1 open pixel.
4. **Check the raw message source.** Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, plus SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment.
5. **Re-run with the full HTML and a larger probe budget.** This would cover the 44 skipped links and allow checks of UTMs and the postal address.
