# Extra 25% off select clearance—Don’t miss out 🔥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-20**
## Executive summary

- This is, by my count, the eighth DICK'S email in under a week — and at least the fifth to lead with the identical dark-red "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero. The send lands on June 20, the Saturday before Father's Day (June 21, 2026), which means the single most time-sensitive hook in the entire email — the Father's Day Savings Event banner — is buried below swim, adidas, life vests, and drinkware. Whatever urgency Father's Day eve could have generated is lost before most mobile subscribers ever scroll to it.
- As a standalone artifact the email is functional: the offer is real, the brand renders cleanly, and the CTAs are clear. In context it's a compounding problem. "Don't miss out 🔥" reads as pure noise when subscribers have received this same message every 18-24 hours. The fire emoji and vague urgency copy without a hard deadline date confirm what every prior send has trained them to believe: this sale will still be running tomorrow, and the day after, and the day after that.
- The only genuinely new creative element is the $10 Bonus Reward ScoreCard module, carried forward from the separate June 20 loyalty email. It's welcome but awkwardly positioned — a $150+ spend threshold mid-scroll in a clearance email signals mixed purchase intent that the email never resolves.

## What's working

- The dark-red hero renders cleanly with zero broken images; the 25% off number reads immediately at any viewport size.
- Summer category selection (swim, life vests, drinkware) is genuinely coherent for a late-June clearance push.
- The $10 Bonus Reward ScoreCard module adds loyalty value beyond the clearance angle — a rare non-clearance signal in this cadence.
- Father's Day is technically present; the timing is right even if the placement is wrong.
- Multiple "SHOP NOW" buttons are visually clear and unambiguous throughout.

## What's weak

- The Father's Day section is a late-scroll afterthought on the eve of Father's Day — the only time-sensitive offer in the email has the least prominent placement.
- "Don't miss out 🔥" with no hard end date is inert urgency after a week of identical sends; subscribers have learned this clearance never ends.
- The hero creative is pixel-for-pixel recycled from at least four prior sends — it no longer creates a stop-scroll moment.
- The $10 Bonus Reward requires $150+, which conflicts with a clearance shopper's value-hunting mindset; the two messages undermine each other without bridging copy.
- Five distinct category callouts (swim, adidas, life vests, drinkware, summer essentials) plus the loyalty module produce a category dump with no editorial through-line.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Flip the hero to Father's Day** — this is the last night before Father's Day; lead with "Last chance for Dad" and demote the 25% off callout to a badge. The clearance offer becomes the mechanism, not the message.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline date** — "Sale ends Sunday at midnight" or "Tonight only" converts vague urgency into a real reason to act now. The sale has been "don't miss out" for seven days straight.
- 3. **Retire the dark-red "EXTRA 25% OFF" creative** — any layout or color change at this point would outperform the current hero on pure novelty alone. Swap to a white background, a lifestyle image, anything different.
- 4. **Segment the $10 Bonus Reward to the ScoreCard list** — clearance shoppers and loyalty spenders ($150+ threshold) are different purchase intents and shouldn't share a cluttered email without copy that bridges them.
- 5. **Cut to three categories max** — Father's Day gifts + swim + clearance is a coherent summer story. Everything else is dilution.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last night for Dad: 25% off ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Father's Day is tomorrow — shop now, save 25%`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Swim, outdoor gear & gifts for him — all on clearance`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop clearance + earn a $10 Bonus Reward on orders $150+`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email is built on Epsilon/Harmony infrastructure with standard MSO conditional comments and responsive breakpoints. Three concrete technical defects are present: invalid JSON-LD (breaks Gmail Inbox Actions), unfilled template slots in structured data, and a stale 2020 date token shipped to production.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**No confirmed broken links** in the visible source, but:

- All CTAs route through Epsilon redirect URLs (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/{token}/...`). UTM parameters at the final landing page **cannot be verified from this source** — destination query strings are opaque inside the redirect token.
- Open-tracking pixel present at `/O/{token}/...` path — expected, no issue.
- Logo `<img>` src is truncated in the provided HTML (`…`). If the full URL is cut in the actual send, the image will 404.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

| Item | Finding |
|------|---------|
| `role="presentation"` on layout tables | Correct — all layout tables are marked |
| `lang="en"` on `<html>` | Present — correct |
| Responsive breakpoints | `@media screen and (max-width:480px)` present with mobile overrides |
| `border-collapse:separate` on wrapper | Non-standard. Email convention is `collapse`; `separate` can introduce cell-gap rendering bugs in Outlook 2019+ |
| Google Fonts via `@font-face` | Loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com/s/archivo/v7/...`. `v7` is an older font version. Blocked by corporate proxies and Outlook desktop; `Arial` fallback is declared, so degradation is graceful |
| `<link rel="icon">` in `<head>` | Most ESPs strip this silently; it's dead weight but not harmful |
| Alt text on images | Not visible in truncated source — cannot confirm |

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Three unfilled template slots confirmed in the JSON-LD block:**

```json

"discountCode": "",         // DiscountOffer — empty
```

`availabilityStarts` is set to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — a stale 2020 placeholder that was never replaced with the current campaign date.

`id="header-non-sc-member"` on the header table indicates segmentation logic (ScoreCard vs. non-member variant). The non-member variant is what reached this inbox — the conditional content itself cannot be audited without the member variant source.

### 5. Compliance

**Cannot confirm CAN-SPAM compliance from the provided source** — the physical mailing address and unsubscribe mechanism are almost certainly in the footer, which was not included in the truncated HTML. Audit the footer for:
- Physical street address (required)
- Clear unsubscribe link with ≤10 business day honor window

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are not included in the provided data — review raw message headers separately. Sending domain `e.dcsg.com` should have DKIM signed and DMARC policy enforced.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

Cannot verify UTM parameters — all links are wrapped in opaque Epsilon tracking tokens. To validate, manually click a CTA and inspect the final URL for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content`. The campaign theme ("Extra 25% off select clearance") should be reflected in `utm_campaign` on landing pages.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before next send:**

1. **Invalid JSON-LD: JavaScript-style comments present.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//`-style comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON does not support comments — this makes the entire block unparseable. Gmail's structured data parser will silently discard it, breaking any Inbox Actions / Promotions tab annotations.

2. **Empty structured data slots shipped to production.** `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `image` fields are all `""`. Either populate them or remove the keys entirely to avoid malformed schema output.

3. **Stale `availabilityStarts` date.** `"2020-03-06"` is over five years old. Replace with the actual campaign start date or remove the field.

**P1 — Fix in next template revision:**

4. Change outer wrapper `border-collapse:separate` → `border-collapse:collapse` to avoid Outlook cell-gap issues.
5. Verify logo `<img>` src resolves to a full URL in the live send (source was truncated).
6. Remove `<link rel="icon">` from `<head>` — it's stripped by ESPs and adds noise.
7. Upgrade Google Fonts `archivo/v7` URL to the current version.

**P2 — Process improvement:**

8. Add a pre-send validation step that: (a) lints JSON-LD with a JSON parser, (b) checks for empty string values in structured data, (c) resolves all image URLs via HEAD request.
