# New York wins it all 🏀🏆✨
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-14**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S doing the right thing at the right moment. The Knicks just won the championship and DSG hit the inbox within hours with official gear — that's reactive marketing done correctly, and it's a meaningful departure from the clearance-and-deals drumbeat that's dominated the past week. The dual hook (championship celebration + Father's Day shipping deadline in the preheader) is smart: it gives a buyer two separate emotional reasons to click.
- The weakness is execution at the CTA level. The email is a mood — it says "celebrate" but never gives the shopper a reason to buy *now* beyond the hat and jersey in the hero image. No discount, no urgency in the body copy, no product details. A sports fan already primed by the win will click; everyone else gets a nice image and moves on.
- Compared to the previous Clearance and Flash Sale sub-sender hammering earlier this week, this is the main DSG account showing restraint and relevance. It earns its spot in the inbox on June 14.

## What's working

- **Moment marketing landed on time.** Championship win + same-day send from the main account is exactly what retail email should do.
- **Dual narrative (championship + Father's Day) is fully visible.** The preheader drives the gift-buying angle without competing with the emotional hero.
- **Clean visual hierarchy.** "CONGRATS, NEW YORK KNICKS!" hero dominates the fold; the eye goes straight to the trophy/hat imagery.
- **No render issues.** Images loaded, buttons are readable, no layout breaks anywhere in the scroll.
- **Main account send breaks the sub-sender fatigue.** After days of Clearance and Flash Sale sub-senders, seeing "DICK'S Sporting Goods" as the friendly-from registers as a different, more trusted voice.

## What's weak

- **No price hook anywhere in the body.** Official gear commands full MSRP, and that's fine, but the email offers no bridging incentive (even "free shipping on champs gear today") to push a hesitant buyer over the line.
- **Three "SHOP NOW" buttons compete instead of funnel.** NBA Clothing, More NBA Essentials, and the hero all share the same generic CTA label — no hierarchy of which to click first.
- **Father's Day urgency is buried in the delivery module.** The most time-sensitive signal ("order by tonight") is two-thirds down the email in small text, not reinforced in the hero or a sticky banner.
- **Product tiles are generic.** The "More NBA Essentials" module shows abstract product imagery — no specific jersey, no championship hat with a visible price, nothing that makes a fan say "that's the item I want."

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a single urgency line under the hero CTA.** Something like "Order by tonight for Father's Day delivery" directly beneath the first SHOP NOW button would collapse two emails' worth of messaging into one scroll.
- 2. **Differentiate the three CTAs.** "Shop Knicks Gear" → "Shop Championship Apparel" → "Shop Footwear & Accessories" — even minor specificity lifts clicks.
- 3. **Surface one hero product with a price.** Put the championship hat (visible in the hero image) with a "$34.99" or "from $29.99" label. It anchors the offer.
- 4. **Subject Alt A:** `Knicks gear is here — get it by Father's Day`
- 5. **Subject Alt B:** `Official 2026 Champs gear just dropped 🏀`
- 6. **Preheader Alt A:** `Celebrate with the hat, jersey, and gear — order tonight for Sunday delivery`
- 7. **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop official 2026 NBA Champions merch — ships in time for Father's Day`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "New York wins it all" Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard Epsilon/Harmony ESP deployment using table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and click-redirect tracking via `app.dickssportinggoods.com`. The most critical finding is that the `application/ld+json` schema block ships with multiple unfilled template placeholders and invalid JSON syntax.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-redirect structure** uses `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019ec...` with a `__dU__` encoded payload — consistent with Epsilon/Harmony. No raw destination URLs are visible in the source, so UTM passthrough cannot be confirmed (see §6).

**Tracking pixel** (`/O/v610...`) is correctly hidden via `display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`. Single-quote attribute syntax on the `src` (`src='...'`) is a minor inconsistency vs. double-quoted attributes elsewhere — harmless in modern clients, but a hygiene flag.

No broken or mismatched redirect tokens observed in the truncated source.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

- `<html lang="en">` present. `role="presentation"` applied to layout tables. No issues.
- Web fonts (`fonts.gstatic.com`) reference **Archivo v7** — the current canonical version is v19+. Clients that block remote fonts fall back to Arial correctly, but the stale version URL may yield a different glyph set than designed.
- Preheader text (`+ Keep celebrating with official NBA Champs gear`) is embedded inside `<td id="ssl">` alongside the tracking pixel rather than a dedicated, properly hidden preheader `<div>`. Some clients may render this visible or exclude it from inbox preview depending on how they handle that `td` height-zero suppression. Recommended pattern: place preheader in a separate `<div style="display:none;max-height:0;overflow:hidden;">` before any visible `<table>`.
- `alt` text on the logo `<img id="logo">` is truncated in the provided source and cannot be confirmed. Must not be empty for accessibility and image-off rendering.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical — `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains multiple unfilled placeholders:**

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Empty — unfilled |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty — unfilled |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty — unfilled |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | **Hardcoded 2020 date, 6+ years stale** |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty — designer comment still present |

**Critical — the JSON-LD block contains JavaScript-style single-line comments (`//`), which are not valid JSON:**

```json
// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
// Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
```

These will cause a JSON parse error in Gmail's schema parser, silently dropping the entire `ld+json` block. Gmail will not render any promotional annotation, deal badge, or loyalty card derived from this schema.

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

**Source is truncated** — footer cannot be audited for physical mailing address or unsubscribe link from the provided HTML. These must be confirmed in the full source.

**Sender authentication** cannot be verified from HTML alone; `DSG@e.dcsg.com` uses an ESP subdomain. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment on `e.dcsg.com` should be confirmed via DNS lookup — this is standard Epsilon practice but must be verified.

Subject line (`New York wins it all 🏀🏆✨`) is non-deceptive and topically aligned with a championship celebration. No CAN-SPAM subject-line issues observed.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM Params, Landing Page Alignment)

All click links pass through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` Epsilon redirects. The encoded `__dU__` payload obscures the final destination URL and any UTM parameters, making it impossible to verify UTM passthrough from the HTML source alone.

**Action required:** Decode a sample redirect URL and confirm that `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` are appended to the landing page URL. If they are injected by the ESP redirect layer (common in Epsilon/Harmony), document that configuration so it is not accidentally removed.

Landing page alignment (NBA Champs gear) cannot be confirmed without resolving the redirect.
