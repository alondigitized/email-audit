# 🏃 Act fast! Extra 25% off select clearance ENDS TODAY
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "Extra 25% off select clearance" send in two days. The 10/16 sends were "📥 Get to shopping!" and "Have you seen this?". The subject now escalates to "ENDS TODAY," which is the first credible deadline in the series. The hero is a big red "EXTRA 25% OFF" graphic, with the headline "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance Styles" and a green Shop Now button right under it. The offer is clear within a second.
- The body then dilutes the message. Below the hero sit about eight more promos: 40% off shoes, 70% off design clothing, a Minecraft collab, cleats, BOGO Walter Hagen golf, dumbbells, a "50% off" weekly deals block, and a payment-options banner. Several of them carry their own percentages. A shopper who opened for the 25% deadline has to scroll past a catalog to see anything else. The hero's "Ends Tonight" tag is small, so the urgency promised in the subject is weakly reinforced in the render.
- Verdict: the subject is stronger than the previous two and the hero is solid. But it's the same offer a third time, wrapped in a long, cluttered module stack. It's a competent clearance blast with no segmentation and no sense of scarcity beyond the one word "TODAY."

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible. "EXTRA 25% OFF" dominates the first screen, and the green Shop Now button directly below it is unmissable.
- The subject now carries a real deadline ("ENDS TODAY"), and the hero backs it up with an "Ends Tonight" tag.
- Product modules are consistent, each with a small label, a headline and a green button, so the layout is easy to scan.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a quick way into the shopper's own area.
- The product-pick row at the bottom (adidas Samba, Nike fleece, Under Armour hoodie) adds concrete items.

## What's weak

- This is the third send of the same 25% clearance offer in about 48 hours. The fatigue is obvious, and the 10/16 "Have you seen this?" send was already a repeat.
- Competing offers (up to 40%, up to 70%, BOGO 50%, "up to 50%" Weekly Deals) undercut the lead offer. The eye has to choose between four different percentages.
- The urgency is visible only in the small "Ends Tonight" tag. The hero graphic has no countdown or time stamp, so the deadline is easy to miss on mobile.
- The email is very long, about 7,000px. The payment-options banner (Credit Card, Affirm, Afterpay, PayPal) and the app and footer sections push the bottom half well past the point of engagement.
- The "Up to 50% off" weekly-deals graphic and the "40% OFF" shoe tile look like a second and third hero. That dilutes the "ends today" message.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Cut the email to a tight deadline send.** Keep the 25% hero, add a visible "Ends tonight at 11:59 PM CT" line or countdown, and move to 3–4 modules (top categories, a product row). Drop the Minecraft, golf, weights and payment modules from a last-chance send.
- 2. **Put the deadline in the hero graphic.** The "Ends Tonight" tag should be as large as "Extra." Also state the deadline in the button copy.
- 3. **Make the CTA specific.** Change "Shop Now" to "Shop Clearance Before It Ends."
- 4. **Add real products with prices.** Show 4–6 clearance items with the markdown prices after the extra 25%, instead of lifestyle imagery.
- 5. **Suppress or soften the send for people who already clicked on 10/16.** Send them a different angle, such as new markdowns.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: extra 25% off clearance ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight ⏰ Extra 25% off select clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear from Nike, adidas and more. Offer ends at midnight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Tonight's the last night. Browse Men's, Women's and Kids' clearance now.`
