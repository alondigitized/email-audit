# Have you shopped our EPIC End-of-Summer Sale yet?! Up to 50% off deals are one click away...
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-01**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S ran on 8/30 ("Send off summer") and again on 8/31 ("Epic End-of-Summer Sale") — now a third outing for that exact creative, just with a new subject line bolted on. The body is the most bloated yet: nine distinct modules stacked top to bottom (shoes, VRST resort collection, DSG women's clothing, YETI Riverhead, football gear, cardio equipment, a second "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" banner, and a six-item picks grid) before you even hit the footer.
- The subject line itself is a tell: "Have you shopped our EPIC End-of-Summer Sale yet?!" is a re-engagement nudge admitting this is a repeat send of yesterday's exact campaign to people who didn't open or click the first time. That's honest in a way, but it also confirms the fatigue signal from the brand history — this is the fifth-plus near-identical "up to 50% off" send since 8/16, now with a second full offer stack crammed underneath.
- Visually it's clean and on-brand, no render bugs, and the hero-to-CTA path is clear. But there's no reason for a subscriber to open this over yesterday's identical email, and the sheer number of competing offers below the fold dilutes any single click decision. Judgement: a competent execution of a fatigued, redundant campaign — 5/10 territory, dragged down by cadence, not craft.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly with strong contrast — bold white "UP TO 50% OFF" against the maroon stadium photo reads instantly.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is visible without scrolling and paired with a clear green button.
- Utility bar up top (order today, ship tomorrow, ScoreCard link) gives a fast logistics signal for time-sensitive shoppers.
- Category nav pills (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Golf/Outdoor) let a subscriber self-select instead of scrolling the whole email.
- No broken images, overlapping text, or layout breakage anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- This is a straight re-send of the 8/30–8/31 "Epic End-of-Summer" creative and copy — zero new visual hook to justify a re-open.
- Nine separate offer modules (shoes, VRST, DSG, YETI, football, cardio, clearance, picks grid) bury the headline 50%-off deal instead of reinforcing it.
- A second "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" banner appears mid-email, competing directly with the hero's own 50%-off message for attention.
- Subject line ("Have you shopped our EPIC End-of-Summer Sale yet?!") openly signals this is a nag/re-send, which is a weaker hook than a fresh offer or deadline.
- No urgency element (no countdown, no explicit end date) despite "Limited Time" copy under the hero — vaguer than the 8/29 countdown-timer version.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop re-sending the identical hero creative a third time — swap in a genuinely different visual (new product, new backdrop) or skip this send to this segment entirely; re-engagement sends need a new reason to click, not the same banner.
- 2. Cut the body from nine modules to three or four — hero + one adjacent "trending" category + clearance — the current stack reads as "everything in the store," which weakens the "up to 50% off" focus.
- 3. Add a real deadline (specific date/time) near the hero instead of the vague "Limited Time" label — DICK'S already proved a countdown timer helps (8/29 send).
- 4. Move the "HUNDREDS OF NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" banner to the top as the lead if it's meant to be a secondary hook, or cut it — right now it collides with the hero's own offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Still 50% off — but not for long`
- **Subject Alt B:** `You missed this: up to 50% off ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New clearance markdowns just dropped, plus VRST and YETI favorites.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sizes are moving fast on today's top 50%-off picks.`
