# Say hello to this week's deals! Up to 50% off is one click away
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-21**
## Executive summary

- This is the third near-identical weekly-deals send in about 24 hours. The hero is the same as 10/21's "This week's deals are here! 👏" and 10/20's "Ready... set... shop up to 50% off": a giant "UP TO 50% OFF" over fall leaves, "This Week's Deals," and a green Shop Now button. The eye lands on the number first and the offer is unmistakable. A subscriber who got the earlier two will see this as a repeat.
- The body is a long stack of category modules: shoes up to 30% off, apparel up to 70% off, golf bags $220 and under, team sports, outdoor and fitness up to 50% off. Each has its own button. It's tidy, but it's a catalog, not a pitch, and there is no single hero product. The ScoreCard loyalty strip is a tiny line at the top, so member value is invisible.
- Verdict: it's well built and loud, but it's undifferentiated and arrives with too little spacing from the last two deals sends. The subject line is the weakest part. It spends 63 characters on "say hello" filler and repeats a hook the brand already used.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is huge, high contrast, and lands first, with a green Shop Now button directly beneath it.
- The "Explore more deals" chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) let a reader jump straight to their category.
- Lifestyle photos in the module stack (golf, team sports, outdoor, fitness) are strong and seasonal, and they make the stack easy to skim.
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." banner puts fulfillment (one-hour pickup, same-day delivery) above the hero.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The cadence is stale: three "this week's deals / up to 50%" sends in roughly a day, with the same hero as the 10/20 and 10/21 sends.
- The only urgency is "Valid online only for a limited time", which has no date or countdown.
- The ScoreCard strip is tiny, so there is no member-specific price or points hook, unlike the 10/19 2X Points sends.
- Nearly every module ends in a generic "Shop Now", "Cleat Deals" or "Cardio Deals" button, and the "Check out these picks" product grid at the bottom has no prices or ratings.
- The email is very long (about 5,700px) with seven or more deal blocks. It's hard to tell which offer matters, and the "up to" ranges (30%, 50%, 70%) blur together.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending a third identical weekly-deals email. Either consolidate into one weekly send or give each send a distinct hook, such as a single category, a ScoreCard bonus, or a dated deadline.
- 2. Put an end date in the hero, for example "Ends Sunday", and add a ScoreCard line ("Members: extra 2X Points") under the Shop Now button.
- 3. Cut the module stack to three or four blocks and show real prices on the product picks (was/now, star ratings).
- 4. Replace generic CTAs with specific ones, such as "Shop Nike & HOKA Shoes" or "Shop Outdoor Gear".
- 5. Move the preheader to benefit copy instead of the fulfillment boilerplate.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off ends Sunday, plus 70% off clearance`
- **Subject Alt B:** `This week only: shoes, golf & fitness up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `ScoreCard members earn extra points on every deal. Shop before Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New markdowns on Nike, HOKA & adidas, plus golf bags $220 and under.`

## Technical Audit

**1. Technical Summary**
The email is built from table-based markup with a 600px wrapper, a `lang` attribute, Outlook conditional comments and a tracked open pixel. The automated QA run has one hard failure (a plain-text part of 9 characters) and many missing-alt warnings. The HTML source was truncated, so link hrefs, the footer and the unsubscribe markup could not be inspected.

**2. Link & Tracking Issues**
- The QA probe checked 12 material links and skipped 55, and it reported no broken or redirecting links. Coverage is partial, and the 55 skipped links (footer, utility and social) were not verified.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6ad8cfea3e5e18754a7f9011&configId=512ffb51-...`. It is 1×1 and sits first in `<body>`. Its tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com` shares the `dcsg.com` parent domain with the sender `em.dcsg.com`, which is good for alignment.
- The pixel `<img>` has no `alt` attribute (QA flag). A decorative 1×1 image should have `alt=""`.
- Anchors carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The hrefs are cut off in the truncated source, so click-wrapper or redirect behavior can't be confirmed.

**3. Rendering & Accessibility**
- **Missing alt text (QA, confirmed):** 12 images are missing `alt`.
  - Hero and category images: `shoes-2026-1021-d`, `vert-2026-1021-d`, `golf-2026-1021-d`, `sports-2026-1021-d`, `outdoor-2026-1021-d`, `fitness-2026-1021-d`.
  - Value-callout icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The pixel image listed above.
  - If these are the main category tiles and are also links, screen readers and image-blocked clients will have no label for them. The text-bearing tiles are the highest risk.
- **Empty preheader slot:** `<td id="ssl">` contains only the template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` followed by the `#pht-spacer` filler. No preheader text was inserted, so inbox preview text will come from body content.
- **Editor artifacts in production markup:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` and the `<!--DKS Email Marketing 2026-->` comment are left in. They are harmless functionally but are unneeded bytes.
- **Image dimensions (low confidence, verify):** the Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600` (portrait, some as `png-alpha`) inside a 600px layout. This may add weight. Rendered sizes weren't visible in the truncated source.
- **Fonts:** Archivo is loaded from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen` and falls back to `Arial,sans-serif`. No issue.

**4. Personalization & Merge Tokens**
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (such as `{{`, `%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. The `tuid` parameter in the pixel is resolved. The truncated portion could not be checked.

**5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)**
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL):** the text version is 9 characters, which is effectively empty. This is a real deliverability and accessibility gap, and some filters score HTML-only or near-empty-text mail negatively.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058):** both are absent in the captured message. QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **not confirmed as a sender defect**. Verify against the raw message at a non-relay inbox. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **`Authentication-Results`:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not a finding. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` should be checked directly via DNS or a raw-header test.
- **Footer:** the unsubscribe link and physical address could not be seen in the truncated source. QA states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but gave no result.

**6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)**
Not verifiable. Hrefs and query strings are not visible in the truncated source, and the QA output doesn't list landing URLs, UTM parameters or probe results. No mismatch was observed.

**7. Recommendations**
1. Ship a real multipart/alternative text part with the offer, key category links and unsubscribe URL (the QA FAIL).
2. Add meaningful `alt` text to the six category images and the five icons, and `alt=""` to the 1×1 pixel.
3. Fill in the SSL/preheader slot with intentional text, or remove the empty placeholder.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` against the raw message outside the relay. Run an SPF/DKIM/DMARC check on `em.dcsg.com`.
5. Re-run the audit on the full, untruncated HTML to check hrefs, UTM tagging, the footer address and unsubscribe markup. Raise the probe limit beyond 12 of 67 links.
6. Strip editor artifacts (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`) from production output. Check whether the 1200×1600 Scene7 requests can be reduced to 2× of rendered size.
