# FINAL HOURS: $20 off $100+ only in the app! 📱⏰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fifth or sixth send of the same "$20 off $100+ in the app" offer since 11/14. The earlier ones were "App exclusive starts now!", "📢 Don't miss this app exclusive," "Lucky you!," "LAST DAY for this app exclusive!" and "👋 Your app-exclusive expires soon." The red "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar is a credible urgency device and is the strongest element. A subscriber who has already seen the offer four times will find it hard to believe this one is the final one.
- The hero is a pair of phone mockups reading "APP EXCLUSIVE" with no product, no savings figure and no person. The $20 offer only appears below it, in the headline. The headline itself is cluttered: "$20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More***". The CTA is a vague "Get Offer," and the second button, "Join ScoreCard+," competes with it. The lower half is a generic catalog of "Up to 70% off clearance," a gift grid and category pills. This dilutes the single message the subject promises.
- Verdict: functional and urgent, but repetitive, with a weak hero and a muddled CTA. It is an average promotional send that depends on deadline pressure alone.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown bar sits at the very top and delivers real, specific urgency.
- The offer is concrete: $20 off $100+, or $30 off with ScoreCard+. It is also marked "Last Day" and tied to a 11/15 11:59PM PT deadline.
- "Sale items included! No code needed" removes a common friction point.
- The DICK'S branding is clean and recognizable, and the green and red palette fits the holiday season.
- The ScoreCard+ membership upsell gives members a clear tier of extra value.

## What's weak

- The hero is two phone mockups with no product, no offer figure and no emotional pull. The "$20 off" message only appears below the image.
- The headline is crowded, with a pipe separator and asterisks. It reads like legal copy rather than a promotion.
- The "Get Offer" and "Join ScoreCard+" buttons compete, and neither says what the offer is or where it leads.
- Most of the email is generic clearance and catalog content ("Up to 70% off," a gift grid, category pills). It dilutes the app-exclusive message and pushes the app download badges to the bottom.
- The cadence is fatiguing. Repeating the same offer in six sends over about 40 hours undermines the "final hours" claim.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the phone mockup with a large "$20 OFF $100+" graphic on a product lifestyle image. Put the app download badge directly under the offer, because the offer only works in the app.
- 2. Cut the headline to "$20 off $100+ in the app. Ends tonight." Move the ScoreCard+ upgrade to a single line beneath it.
- 3. Use one primary CTA, "Open the app & save $20," and make the ScoreCard+ link a secondary text link.
- 4. Remove or shorten the clearance and gift sections on this send so the one offer stays the focus.
- 5. Suppress this send for recipients who already got three or more app-exclusive emails, or collapse the sequence into two sends.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Tonight 11:59 PM: $20 off $100+ in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `8 hours left: your $20 app coupon expires`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `No code needed. Activate in the app, and ScoreCard+ members get $30 off.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale items included. Offer ends at 11:59 PM PT tonight.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, viewport meta, `role="presentation"` tables, MSO fallbacks, hidden preheader). The confirmed defects are an effectively empty plain-text part (9 chars) and missing `alt` attributes on 7 images. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- Only 12 of 55 links were probed and 43 were skipped. No broken links were reported among the 12, but link health for the rest is **unverified**.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6af7b54f45d7fa7aafb91a6e&configId=512ffb51-...`. It sits as a bare `<img width="1" height="1">` directly after `<body>`, with no `alt` and no `style="display:none"` or border/margin reset. Some clients may render it as a stray inline element.
- Header CTAs use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The HTML is truncated, so the destination URLs and any redirect or click-tracking wrapper could not be inspected.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** There is no usable text alternative. This hurts deliverability scoring and accessibility, and it leaves text-only clients and some assistive tools with nothing.
- **Missing `alt` on 7 images:**
  - `1101-owala-winter-meadow-plaid-banner` (`scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600`). This is a content image, so the missing `alt` is a real defect. Images-off clients and screen readers lose the content.
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1` (all `fmt=png-alpha`). These are probably decorative, but they need an explicit `alt=""`. A missing attribute makes some screen readers announce the filename.
  - The tracking pixel (see section 2).
- The banner is requested at `wid=1200&hei=1600` with `qlt=100`. That is a heavy payload for a 600px layout and is worth checking, though the QA data doesn't confirm a rendering fault.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is **unconfirmed**. If the headers are truly absent, the email would not meet Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirements.
- **Authentication-Results:** not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The sender `info@em.dcsg.com` is a dedicated subdomain, which is consistent with aligned sending, but this is not verified.
- **Body unsubscribe link and postal address:** the QA summary says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the truncated HTML doesn't show the footer, so I can't confirm either.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The truncated source exposes no destination URLs, so UTM presence and landing-page alignment with the "$20 off $100+ only in the app" subject could not be verified. The `DEEPLINK` data attributes suggest app deep links. Their fallback behavior for users without the app is untested.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add a real text/plain multipart alternative. The current 9 chars fail the check.
2. Add descriptive `alt` to the Owala banner. Add `alt=""` to the five icons and to the tracking pixel, and give the pixel `style="display:block;border:0"`.
3. Check the raw message at the origin (not through the relay) for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF, DKIM and DMARC pass.
4. Re-run link probing with a higher cap to cover the 43 skipped links, and capture the full HTML so UTM parameters and deep-link fallbacks can be audited.
5. Reduce the banner's `qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600` parameters to a size matched to the 600px layout.
