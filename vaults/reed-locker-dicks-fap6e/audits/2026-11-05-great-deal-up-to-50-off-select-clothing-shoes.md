# Great deal: Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes 📥
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-05**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S catch-all promo, and it works as a category menu more than a single offer. The hero is a model in a black tee and a Nike sneaker next to a green argyle block with gold "UP TO 50% OFF". "Select Clothing & Shoes" and one red Shop Now button sit right below it. The eye lands on the offer first.
- Below the hero the email turns into a long scroll of category tiles. Drinkware, fan shop, outdoor, team sports, golf and fitness each have their own "Up to 40% / 50% off" headline. A second 50% OFF banner repeats the hero near the bottom. For a 4th-5th near-identical DICK'S send in three days (11/3 clearance, 11/4 "up to 50% off select clothing & shoes ✅", 11/5 clearance), it adds little new. The 50% figure and green argyle are the same beats as the 11/4 send.
- Judgement: competent and clear, but repetitive and diluted. It spreads attention across seven categories with roughly eight CTAs, and none of them is a clear hero product.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on green argyle makes the offer instant and the hierarchy clear.
- A single red Shop Now button directly under the hero gives an unambiguous primary CTA.
- The "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" strip at the top adds a credible fulfillment benefit (same-day pickup).
- Category pill buttons in "Explore more deals & essentials" help shoppers jump to their own interest.
- The "Gift picks" product grid (Stanley, Callaway, UGG) with names ties to the holiday season.

## What's weak

- The email is very long, with about seven category blocks and roughly eight or more CTAs, so the primary offer gets diluted.
- The 50% OFF hero is repeated again mid-email ("This Week's Deals") with no new information.
- Nearly the same offer ran on 11/4 ("Check off their wish list with up to 50% off select clothing & shoes ✅"), so this feels like cadence fatigue.
- Offers are mixed (40% off drinkware, 40% off fan gear and team sports, 50% off outdoor, golf and fitness) and carry dense fine print and asterisks at the bottom.
- The hero doesn't name a deadline. "One Day Only" appears only on the fan shop block, buried partway down.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the hero plus two or three focused blocks, and drop the duplicate 50% banner. Fewer CTAs would lift click-through the most.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero (e.g. "Ends tonight 11:59 PM PT") instead of leaving urgency to a mid-email fan shop block.
- 3. Feature one or two specific hero products with prices rather than generic category tiles.
- 4. Vary the creative from the 11/4 send so subscribers don't see the same argyle and 50% every day.
- 5. Move the gift picks higher and tie them to the holiday angle.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day pickup on top brands. Deals end 11:59 PM PT.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source has solid structure: `lang="en"`, a `<title>`, a viewport meta, MSO conditionals, and `role="presentation"` layout tables. The confirmed defects are a missing plain-text part (9 chars) and missing `alt` attributes on 13 images. The unsubscribe and authentication headers could not be verified because the relay may have stripped them.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is partial.** 12 of 72 links were probed and 60 were skipped (deprioritized footer, utility and social links). No probed link was reported as failing, so no broken links are confirmed. The skipped 60 are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is outside the layout.** `<img src="https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…" width="1" height="1">` is a direct child of `<body>`, before the wrapper table. It has no `alt`, no `style="display:block"`, and no `border="0"`. Some clients may render it as a visible inline element and add stray whitespace above the layout.
- **Click-tracking links** run through `class="arc-link"` anchors. The source is truncated, so their destinations and redirect behavior are unverified.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] The plain-text alternative is effectively missing.** The text version is 9 chars. Text-only clients and spam filters will see an empty message, which also hurts deliverability scoring.
- **13 images have no `alt` attribute** (QA warnings):
  - The tracking pixel (`/ee/v1/open`).
  - `0831-dks-stw-6am-40-off-drink`.
  - `fan-shop-2026-1102-d`.
  - `2026-1105-dks-swd-11am-outdoor`, `…-sports`, `…-golf` and `…-fitness`.
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The hero and promo images are the likely carriers of the offer. With images blocked or read by a screen reader, that content is lost. Decorative icons and the pixel should use `alt=""` explicitly, because a missing attribute makes screen readers read the file name.
- **Editor artifacts leaked into production markup.** The body carries `class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true"`. This is harmless to rendering but shows the source came from a WYSIWYG editor without a clean export step.
- **Image URLs are heavy.** The Scene7 sources request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100`, which is a large payload for a 600px container. The source is truncated, so I can't confirm how the width is constrained in the markup.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source or subject. The source is truncated, so the body below the header is unchecked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found.** The QA tool attributes this to the AgentMail relay, so it is unconfirmed. If the headers are really absent, the sender will fall short of the Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender one-click requirement. Verify against the raw message at the origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. This is a relay limitation, not evidence of failure. The From domain `em.dcsg.com` is a dedicated sending subdomain, which is consistent with proper setup.
- **The in-body unsubscribe link and the postal address** are in the truncated footer. The QA run did include an unsubscribe link in its probes, but I can't confirm the address from the provided source.

Not assessable from the provided data. The truncated source shows no destination URLs or UTM parameters, and the QA output contains no link-probe results (final URLs, status codes or redirect chains). No issue is confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real multipart/alternative text part.** It should include the offer ("Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes"), primary CTA URLs and the unsubscribe link. This is the only QA FAIL.
2. **Add `alt` to all 13 images.** Use descriptive text for the hero and promo images (`2026-1105-dks-swd-11am-*`, `fan-shop-2026-1102-d`, `0831-dks-stw-6am-40-off-drink`). Use `alt=""` for the icons and the tracking pixel.
