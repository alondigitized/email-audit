# Now's the time to grab an extra 25% off select clearance! ⏰ Shop now...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-02**
## Executive summary

- This is the second send in a day pushing the same extra 25% off clearance. It follows this morning's "📢 Last day! 🚨 EXTRA 25% off select clearance is yours." The hero is strong: a giant white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on deep red, a "Final Hours" countdown timer above it, and a single "Shop Now" button below. The eye lands on the offer immediately and the urgency is credible, because the timer is visible and the fine print says the offer ends 10/2 at 11:59 PM PT.
- Below the hero, the email falls apart into a long scroll of competing offers. These include up to 40% off clothing, up to 30% off fan gear, up to 40% off team sports, up to 50% off outdoor, up to 50% off fitness, and a repeat of the "This Week's Deals 50% OFF" banner. The "extra 25%" message gets diluted. A subscriber who got the morning "Last day!" email would see this as a near-duplicate resend.
- Judgement: a good hero on a cluttered, repetitive email. It is fine as a last-chance reminder, but the cadence is wearing. This is the fifth or sixth percent-off blast in about four days.

## What's working

- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- The "Final Hours" countdown timer at the top gives real, visible urgency.
- A single green "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero.
- The category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) make it easy to jump to a section.
- Nothing is visibly broken. Images load and there is no overlapping or truncated text, unlike the cropped "FLAS" banner in the 9/30 send.

## What's weak

- The email repeats this morning's "Last day!" 25% clearance message almost exactly. That is a cadence problem.
- Too many competing offers (40%, 30%, 50%, 25%) bury the one deal the subject promises.
- The "This Week's Deals" 50% OFF banner near the bottom contradicts the "extra 25%" focus. It also recycles the creative from the 9/29–10/1 sends.
- The fine print is long and dense, with exclusions and a note that the offer can't be combined with other sales. That makes the offer feel less honest.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid has no prices, no ratings and no discount callouts.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to the clearance hero, a short product grid with real sale prices, and one footer link. If the morning send already went out, suppress this one for people who clicked it.
- 2. Add actual clearance products with "was/now" prices directly under the hero, so the "extra 25%" has something concrete to apply to.
- 3. Move the countdown timer closer to the CTA, and change the button copy to something specific like "Shop Clearance".
- 4. Drop the "This Week's Deals" 50% banner from this send, since it conflicts with the 25% message.
- 5. Add social proof, such as star ratings on the picks.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a preheader, MSO conditionals and a `lang="en"` attribute. It has two confirmed defects: a plain-text part of only 9 characters (QA FAIL) and 11 images with no `alt` attribute. The header and authentication checks could not be verified from the captured data.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in the ESP click-redirect `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...`. This is on the sender's branded domain (`em.dcsg.com`), which is good.
- The destination URLs are encoded in the opaque `params` blob, so they can't be checked from the source.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 60. No broken links were reported in the probed set, but the other 60 were not validated.
- The header link carries `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` and `class="arc-link"`. These are ESP and app-deeplink attributes. Whether they resolve correctly for users without the app can't be determined from this data.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`, and it has no `alt`. See section 3.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (11 images), confirmed by QA:**
  - Content banners, all `https://s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/...`: `2026-1002-dks-storewide-4pm-1`, `fanshop-2026-0929-dks-swd-6am`, `team-2026-0914-dks-swd-4pm`, `outdoor-2026-0914-dks-swd-4pm`, `2026-1002-dks-storewide-4pm-2-new`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - Tracking pixel: `open`.
- **Banners may carry the offer text.** If the banners hold the promo copy, the "extra 25% off select clearance" message is invisible with images off or in a screen reader.
- **Missing alt on the pixel:** `alt=""` is the correct form for the tracking pixel. A missing `alt` makes some screen readers announce the image URL.
- **Image weight:** The banners are requested as `fmt=png-alpha&wid=1200&hei=1600`. That is a 1200×1600 PNG for a container capped at `max-width:584px` (the 600px wrapper has 8px padding). Large PNGs can slow load and risk Gmail clipping or slow rendering on mobile. This is inferred from the URLs, not measured.
- **Plain-text part:** The text version is 9 characters (QA FAIL). Text-only clients and some spam filters see an effectively empty message.
- **Layout and fonts:** Layout uses `role="presentation"` on tables, MSO conditional comments and an `x-apple-data-detectors` override. The Archivo web font falls back to `Arial,sans-serif`. No issues found here.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%  %%`, `[[ ]]`) appear in the visible source. The source is truncated, so this covers only the captured portion.

- No UTM parameters are visible. All destinations are hidden behind `mclick` redirect `params` blobs, so campaign tagging can't be verified.
- The preheader/subject promise of "extra 25% off select clearance" can't be matched to a landing page without resolving the redirects.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part (high priority):** Generate a full multipart/alternative text version with the offer, key links, the unsubscribe URL and the postal address.
2. **Add alt text:** Give the five banners descriptive alt that includes the offer copy. Use `alt=""` on the five decorative or labeled icons, or give them text equivalents. Use `alt=""` on the open pixel.
3. **Reduce image weight:** Serve the Scene7 banners at 2× display size (about 1168px wide) rather than 1200×1600. Use JPEG (`fmt=jpeg`) where transparency isn't needed instead of `png-alpha`.
4. **Re-test the header checks against the original message:** Check `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results` on a copy that did not go through the relay, such as a direct seed inbox. Don't treat these as sender faults until then.
5. **Resolve the redirects:** Follow the `mclick` chains for all CTAs. Record the final URLs and their UTM parameters, and confirm the landing page matches the clearance offer.
6. **Widen link probing:** 60 links were skipped. Probe at least the footer and unsubscribe links, and report their HTTP status.
