# New deals added! 🙌 Shop up to 50% off during our Epic End-of-Summer Sale.
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-05**
## Executive summary

- This is the exact same red/maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has run since 8/30 — now under its sixth or seventh different subject line. What's actually new this time is the body: after the hero and the "Extra 25% Off" block, there's a longer-than-usual cascade of distinct modules (ScoreCard $10 bonus, $20-and-under shoes, swim sale, BOGO golf, YETI Riverhead launch, outdoor essentials, basketball hoops, product picks grid). That's more genuine variety than recent sends, but it's buried under two nearly identical "% off" hero blocks stacked back to back at the top, which is the first thing the eye hits and the least differentiated part of the email.
- The render itself is clean — no broken images, no overlapping text, good spacing throughout. The problem isn't execution, it's fatigue: a subscriber who's opened five of these since 8/30 sees the same maroon jersey-number "50%" wall again and has every reason to stop opening.
- Judgement: solid production, weak novelty. The good content (YETI collection, ScoreCard bonus, swim sale) is real news that's getting drowned out by the recycled hero.

## What's working

- Clean render — no layout bugs, broken images, or overlapping elements anywhere in a very long email.
- Genuine content variety further down: ScoreCard $10 bonus reward, YETI Riverhead launch, and swim sale are actual news, not just repackaged clearance.
- Clear, consistent CTA buttons ("Shop Now," "Get Coupon," "Sign In") throughout, easy to scan on mobile.
- Product picks grid at the bottom gives concrete, specific items (shoes, Pokémon TCG, backpack) rather than a generic "shop now."

## What's weak

- The hero is the same red stadium-seating "50% OFF" creative flagged repeatedly since 8/30 — zero visual differentiation from the last five sends.
- A second near-identical "EXTRA 25% OFF" red block sits directly below the hero, doubling down on the same color/typography before any real differentiation appears.
- Subject line ("New deals added! Shop up to 50% off...") adds no new information over 9/3's "INSIDE: Up to 50% off our best deals of the week."
- The genuinely interesting stuff (YETI launch, $10 bonus reward) is scrolled past two-thirds of the way down, when it could be a stronger lead than another 50%-off stamp.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with the YETI Riverhead Collection or the ScoreCard $10 bonus reward as the hero — both are actual news this brand hasn't used yet, unlike the 50%-off stamp that's appeared in every send since 8/30.
- 2. Cut one of the two consecutive red "% off" blocks (hero + Extra 25%) — collapse into a single offer block so the page doesn't open with two visually identical slabs.
- 3. Vary the hero background/color from the maroon stadium-seat image that's run all week, even if the offer language stays the same.
- **Subject Alt A:** `YETI Riverhead Collection just landed 🌊`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Members: your $10 Bonus Reward is ready`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus extra 25% off clearance and up to 50% off outdoor essentials`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `New arrivals, member perks, and end-of-summer markdowns inside`
- 4. Move the product picks grid (adidas Samba, Pokémon TCG, HOKA) higher — specific, shoppable items are stronger click bait than another blanket percentage.
