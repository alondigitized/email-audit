# 📣 Don't miss this, ScoreCard members! Earn 2X Points only in the app before it's too late...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-19**
## Executive summary

- This is the second 2X Points app send of the day. This morning's "ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY 📲" used the same phone-and-2X hero. This one adds a "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown banner and an "Ends Tonight" kicker. The countdown is the only new creative element, and it is the strongest piece. It gives the urgency a visual anchor and makes "today only" credible.
- The structure is sound: a countdown bar, the phone hero with a giant "2X", a headline, and a green "Get Offer" button. The weakness is the offer itself. "2X Points" has no dollar value attached, so it is abstract. The footer says 300 points = $10 reward, but you have to dig to find that. Below the fold, the 70% off clearance block competes with the loyalty hero. The product grid is generic.
- Judgement: a competent, well-formed resend. The deadline is credible and the render is clean. The pitch is still vague, and this is the second send of the same offer on the same day. Expect solid opens and modest clicks.

## What's working

- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown banner sits above the hero and makes the deadline feel real.
- The 2X graphic on the phone is large and legible. The eye lands there first.
- The single green "Get Offer" button is clear and sits right under the "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points" headline.
- The "Ends Tonight" kicker and the fine print ("Valid through 10/19/26 11:59PM PT") agree with the countdown.
- The 70% off clearance block is visually strong. It repeats the 10/18 hero, and its autumn creative fits the season.
- The render is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- "2X Points" has no dollar value in the hero. The 300 points = $10 reward is only in the tiny header strip and the footer.
- "Get Offer" is a vague CTA. It doesn't say what you're getting or where you'll land.
- The 70% off section pulls attention away from the loyalty message. The email has two competing heroes.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid has no prices, no reviews and no tie to 2X points. It reads as filler.
- The legal copy is a wall of tiny text, and the offer carries a "±" footnote and an exclusions list. The "Must sign in" line and the "Not a ScoreCard member? Join today" line make it feel less exclusive.
- It is the second near-identical 2X send today. That is a cadence problem.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the dollar value in the hero and headline, for example "2X Points = $20 back on a $200 app order." This turns an abstract multiplier into a number.
- 2. Cut the second hero, or push it below the product grid. One message per send.
- 3. Change the CTA to something specific, such as "Shop the App & Earn 2X." Link it to a deep link into the app, not a generic landing page.
- 4. Replace the generic picks with items that tie to the offer, such as "Earn 2X on these." Add prices and a points-earned badge.
- 5. For a same-day resend, send it only to people who haven't opened the morning email, and lead with the countdown.
- **Subject Alt A:** `⏳ 8 hours left: 2X ScoreCard Points in the app`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: double your points on app orders`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Ends tonight at 11:59 PM PT. Sign in and shop in the DICK'S app.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Every $1 earns 2 points. 300 points = $10 back.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email has a sound structure (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals, hidden preheader). It fails the plain-text check, and several images have no alt text. ## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. The QA probed 12 material links and skipped 42 as footer, utility or social links. The skipped 42 are unverified, and the QA data doesn't include the 12 probe results.
- The open pixel `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1.…&configId=512ffb51-…` is a first-party tracking domain with the correct `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt` attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 characters.** This means the text/plain alternative is effectively empty. It hurts deliverability scoring and leaves text-only clients with no content.
- **Missing `alt` on 7 images**, per QA:
  - `2026-0817-hero-dks`: `https://s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/2026-0817-hero-dks?fmt=jpeg&qlt=100&wid=1200&hei=1600…`. This is the main content image, so image-blocking clients and screen readers lose it entirely.
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`. The QA flagged these as missing alt. Add `alt=""` if they're decorative, or real text if they sit next to no label.
  - The tracking pixel. `alt=""` is sufficient.
- The hero requests `wid=1200&hei=1600` at `qlt=100` for a 600px max-width container. That's a 2x render, but quality 100 inflates the payload. A quality of about 80 would cut the weight with no visible change.
- `display:none` is used on `#pht-spacer` with `mso-hide:all`. This is the standard preheader-padding pattern and is fine.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The truncated source shows no unrendered tokens such as `{{…}}`, `%%…%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]`. The subject uses a segment label ("ScoreCard members") rather than a name merge. The footer and later body were not visible.

- The physical address and visible unsubscribe link are in the truncated footer, so I can't confirm them. The QA says an unsubscribe link was included in the probe set and reported no failure.
