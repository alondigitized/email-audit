# 📣 Don't miss this, ScoreCard members! ⏳ Earn 2X Points only in the app now...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-28**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the exact same Mobile Monday app-exclusive play as the 9/28 "ScoreCard exclusive! 2X Points on your app purchase TODAY ONLY" send scored 5.00/10 — same hand-holding-phone hero photo, same green ScoreCard branding, same "2X Points" hook, just a slightly different subject wrapper ("Don't miss this, ScoreCard members!"). The hero itself is clean: a real lifestyle photo of a phone showing the app with "2X POINTS" overlaid in bold white-on-green, a clear "Get Offer" button, and a legible "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points‡ on Your App Purchase" headline right below it.
- Where it falls apart is everything after the fold. The email dumps into an undifferentiated scroll of six unrelated deal tiles (shoes, clothing, golf balls, MLB gear, bikes, cardio equipment) with no hierarchy, followed by a generic "UP TO 50% OFF" banner that has nothing to do with the app offer, then a "Check out these picks" product grid. It reads like three separate emails stapled together, and by the time a reader hits the 50% off banner the 2X Points hook is long forgotten.
- The core offer (2X Points, app-only, today) is genuinely differentiated and worth leading with, but the execution buries it under a wall of unrelated merchandising. Judgment: functional but bloated — the hero works, the rest is noise diluting the one distinct idea DICK'S has had in days.

## What's working

- Hero photo is authentic (real hand/phone), not clipart, and the "2X POINTS" overlay is legible at a glance.
- Primary CTA "Get Offer" is a clear, high-contrast green button directly under the value prop.
- Headline "Mobile Monday! Earn 2X Points‡ on Your App Purchase" is specific and states the mechanic plainly.
- "Today Only" eyebrow text creates credible, simple urgency without excessive punctuation in the body copy.
- No render bugs — images load, text is crisp, buttons are intact throughout the long scroll.

## What's weak

- The offer is buried by six+ unrelated deal tiles and a second "UP TO 50% OFF" banner competing for attention right after the hero.
- Fine print under the CTA ("Must sign in to redeem offer. Valid through 9/28/26 11:59PM PT. Not a ScoreCard member? Join today. Terms apply.") adds friction immediately below the button.
- This is a near-repeat of the same-day 9/28 Mobile Monday send at 5.00/10 — same visual formula, same hook, diminishing novelty within hours of each other.
- No countdown timer or visible time element beyond text, despite this being a same-day expiring offer.
- "This Week's Deals" 50% off banner and the ScoreCard 2X offer are both "today"-styled but visually disconnected, confusing what's actually time-limited.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to just the app offer plus 2-3 category shortcuts — drop the golf balls/bikes/cardio/MLB grid entirely; this is supposed to be a focused Mobile Monday push, not a general catalog blast.
- 2. Move fine print below the fold or shrink it — it currently sits as a wall of small text right under the primary CTA, adding friction to the strongest moment in the email.
- 3. Differentiate this send visually from the other same-day Mobile Monday email so subscribers don't feel like they got the same email twice.
- 4. Add a visible countdown or "ends at 11:59PM tonight" badge near the hero, not just in fine print.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Mobile Monday: 2X ScoreCard Points, app-only, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your app-exclusive 2X Points offer expires at midnight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Redeem in the DICK'S app before 11:59PM tonight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sign in, shop the app, earn double ScoreCard Points`
