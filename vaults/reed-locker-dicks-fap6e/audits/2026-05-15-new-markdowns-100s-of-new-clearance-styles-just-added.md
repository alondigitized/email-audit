# NEW markdowns 📣 100s of new clearance styles just added!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-15**
## Executive summary

- This is a new-markdowns clearance refresh from DICK'S — structurally similar to the May 13 extra-25%-off clearance send that scored 8/10, but this one leads with breadth over a single punchy offer. The "HUNDREDS of NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" hero is visually clean and green-on-dark is on-brand, but the email immediately scatters attention across seven-plus unrelated categories: running shoes, CALIA/VRST clothing, baseball gloves, golf balls, life vests, cardio equipment, fishing gear. A subscriber who already received five DICK'S promo emails between May 11–14 needs "fresh" to mean something.
- The structural problem is the same one the weekly deals digest has: no single product or category owns the email. "HUNDREDS" is a quantity claim, not a value proposition. The May 13 clearance hit harder because a single red-and-black "EXTRA 25% OFF" badge occupied the hero with no ambiguity. This email doesn't have that anchor. The best individual offer — up to 70% off CALIA/VRST/DSG clothing — is buried in section two.
- Verdict: a competent volume email that will pull clicks from habitual deal-scanners but won't punch through inbox fatigue at the current DICK'S send cadence.

## What's working

- "HUNDREDS of NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" hero lands above the fold, large and unambiguous — the message is immediate
- The dedicated "DICK'S | Clearance" sender name correctly pre-qualifies intent; deal-seekers know exactly why this landed
- SHOP NOW buttons are consistently placed under every category block — predictable and easy to tap
- Up to 70% off CALIA/VRST/DSG clothing is a genuinely strong offer and the highest markdown in the email
- Category breadth is appropriate for a clearance digest — running, baseball, golf, fishing, cardio covers wide surface area for a sporting-goods household

## What's weak

- No single hero product — the eye scans and scrolls without committing anywhere; attention is split seven ways
- "HUNDREDS" is a cheap quantity claim with no credibility hook; contrast with the May 13 send which led with a specific extra percentage
- Fifth email in four days means "just added" feels routine, not fresh — cadence is the enemy of urgency here
- No deadline or expiry anywhere in the email; "just added" is a freshness signal but not a scarcity signal — there's no reason to click today versus tomorrow
- No ScoreCard / loyalty member callout — the May 12 weekly deals send surfaced member benefits; this one drops them entirely

## Recommendations

- 1. **Elevate the 70% off clothing to the hero** — it's the strongest individual offer and it's sitting in section two; swap it with "HUNDREDS" and show an actual garment at the marked-down price
- 2. **Add a deadline** — even "valid through Sunday" creates a reason to click now rather than bookmark for later
- 3. **Cut categories to 4 max** — seven sections with generic SHOP NOW CTAs is decision paralysis; theme the send (e.g., summer outdoor clearance: shoes, fishing, water) or run two targeted sends instead of one scatter-shot
- 4. **One ScoreCard line** — "ScoreCard members: see your exclusive markdowns" costs nothing and rewards the subscriber relationship
- 5. **Rewrite the subject to include the top % off** — the current subject buries the lead
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 70% off new clearance — shoes, gear & apparel`
- **Subject Alt B:** `New markdowns: up to 70% off, just added today`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `CALIA, running shoes, fishing + more — fresh cuts all week`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Email is served via the Epsilon/Harmony ESP with standard click-redirect and open-pixel tracking. The most critical technical issue is invalid JSON-LD syntax that will silently fail in every client that reads structured data; a secondary concern is a stale 2020 offer date in the same block.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**UTM parameters — unverifiable (medium risk)**
All click links route through Epsilon's redirect handler:
```
https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019e2b42eb418926b4369ff1d668/.../...?__dU__=<encrypted>
```
The destination URL is fully encrypted in the `__dU__` parameter. UTM passthrough cannot be confirmed from the source alone. If Epsilon is not configured to append `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, and `utm_campaign` at redirect time, attribution will be lost.

**Action:** Confirm in Epsilon campaign settings that UTM decoration is enabled on all tracked links.

**Open pixel — no issues**
Pixel at `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019e2b42eb418926b4369ff1d668/...` is correctly hidden (`display:none; max-height:0px; overflow:hidden; mso-hide:all`).

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**`border-collapse` inconsistency (low risk)**
The IE conditional block at the top of `<head>` sets:
```css
table { border-collapse: collapse; border-spacing: 0; }
```
But all layout tables in `<body>` use `border-collapse: separate; border-spacing: 0`. These are functionally equivalent when spacing is zero, but the conflict can produce unexpected 1px gaps in Outlook 2016/2019 when `border-radius` is also applied (as it is on `#header-non-sc-member`).

**`<link rel="icon">` in email `<head>` (informational)**
```html
<link rel="icon" type="image/x-icon" href="https://www.dickssportinggoods.com/...favicon.ico"/>
```
Ignored by all email clients; harmless but unnecessary markup.

**Logo `alt` text — unverifiable**
The `<img id="logo">` source is truncated in the provided HTML. Cannot confirm `alt` attribute presence. If missing, Outlook image-blocking will render a blank header with no fallback label.

**Preheader construction — functional but brittle**
The `#ssl` TD uses `height:0; font-size:0; line-height:0` to ghost the preheader text. This works in most clients but the hidden tracking pixel and the preheader text share the same TD without explicit `max-height:0` on the text node itself. Some aggressive clippers (Yahoo Mail) may suppress the preview text entirely.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

No merge tokens are visible in the provided HTML segment (no `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIRSTNAME%%`, or equivalent). The email does not appear to be personalized beyond segmentation (the `id="header-non-sc-member"` table name implies segment-level conditional, not individual personalization).

No broken or unresolved tokens detected in the visible source.

---

### 5. Compliance

**Cannot fully assess from truncated source.** The footer, unsubscribe link, and physical mailing address — required under CAN-SPAM — are not in the provided HTML segment. These must be present and will be assumed present given this is a repeat sender, but they cannot be confirmed here.

**Authentication headers** (SPF, DKIM, DMARC) are not auditable from HTML source alone. Should be verified via raw message headers (`DSG@e.dcsg.com` must pass DKIM signed by `e.dcsg.com` or an authorized sending domain).

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

**UTM passthrough — see §2.** If Epsilon appends UTMs correctly, the destination domain is `www.dickssportinggoods.com` and continuity depends on the landing page accepting standard `utm_*` query parameters, which is standard for the DSG site.

**JSON-LD offer schema — stale date breaks Gmail promotions tab sorting (high risk)**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This date is six years in the past. Gmail's Promotions tab uses this field for offer sorting and display. A 2020 start date will cause this email to be deprioritized or miscategorized.

---

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Invalid JSON-LD — JavaScript-style `//` comments inside `<script type="application/ld+json">` block | JSON does not support comments; strip all `// ...` lines before send or the entire structured-data block silently fails to parse |
| **Critical** | Stale `availabilityStarts: "2020-03-06"` in DiscountOffer schema | Set to today's date (or campaign start date) |
| **High** | Empty `description`, `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, and `image` fields in DiscountOffer + PromotionCard schemas | Populate or remove these fields; empty strings are valid JSON but send bad signals to Gmail's promotions parser |
| **High** | `http://schema.org/` context URLs | Change to `https://schema.org/` — HTTP is deprecated by schema.org |
| **Medium** | UTM passthrough unverifiable due to `__dU__` encryption | Spot-check a test send by clicking a link and inspecting the final URL for `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` |
| **Low** | `border-collapse` conflict in Outlook conditional vs. body tables | Align both to `border-collapse:collapse` or explicitly test `border-radius` rendering in Outlook 2019 |
| **Low** | Verify logo `<img>` has descriptive `alt` text | Required for image-blocking fallback and screen readers |
