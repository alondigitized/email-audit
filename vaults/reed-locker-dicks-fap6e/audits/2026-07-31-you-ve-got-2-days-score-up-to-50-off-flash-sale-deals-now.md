# You've got 2 days! Score up to 50% off Flash Sale deals NOW
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-31**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the exact same kitchen-sink playbook it used twice on 7/30 ("CLEARANCE: up to 70% off" and "Summer Clearance Event," both 6.00/10) — a new hero creative (a locker-room "FLASH SALE" graphic instead of the paddleboard-at-sunset image) bolted onto the same dozen-block stack of category deals underneath. The subject's "You've got 2 days!" urgency doesn't survive contact with the body: the hero itself just says "Starts Now" with no countdown, and the only visible deadline anywhere near the top is "Ends Tomorrow" on the *second* offer block (the 70% clearance event), not the Flash Sale the subject is actually selling.
- The good news is the top third is genuinely clean — bold hero, one clear headline, one CTA, loyalty points visible in the header. The bad news is everything after that reverts to the same undifferentiated wall of SHOP NOW blocks (Nike Deal Days, drinkware, golf balls, cleats, fishing gear, a second "up to 50%" banner) that's shown up in every recent DICK'S send this week. Subscribers who've opened 3-4 of these already this week have no reason to believe this one is different.
- Net: solid top-of-funnel hero, weak follow-through, and a subject/body urgency mismatch that undercuts the whole "2 days" hook.

## What's working

- The "FLASH SALE" locker-room hero is visually distinct from the paddleboard/clearance-red creative DICK'S has leaned on all week — it's the first new hero image in several sends.
- Header shows ScoreCard points balance ("500 Points | 420 Points Away to Reward") — real loyalty personalization, not boilerplate.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") under the hero is unambiguous and high-contrast against the dark hero image.
- Category nav bar (MEN'S / WOMEN'S / KID'S / SHOES / GOLF / BACK-TO-SCHOOL) gives an easy secondary path for scanners who don't want the flash sale specifically.
- No render bugs — images load, buttons align, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- Subject promises "2 days" urgency for the Flash Sale, but the hero says "Starts Now" with no visible countdown or end date — the only "Ends Tomorrow" tag belongs to the clearance block below it, not the flash sale itself.
- After the hero, the email collapses into the same 10+ stacked category blocks (drinkware, golf balls, cleats, fishing gear, back-to-school gear grid) seen in the 7/28 and 7/30 sends — no attempt to differentiate this "flash sale" from the standing weekly deals.
- A second "UP TO 50%" banner appears near the bottom labeled "This Week's Deals" — visually competing with the Flash Sale hero for the same "50% off" claim and diluting the flash-sale framing entirely.
- All CTAs read "SHOP NOW" with zero variation — none point to a specific product or say what's actually being shopped.
- Given the brand history (this is the 4th+ "up to X% off" send in a week), cadence fatigue is real and this send does nothing to signal it's different from Monday's or Wednesday's.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the hero match the subject: add an actual "48 HOURS LEFT" or date-stamped countdown badge directly on the Flash Sale hero — right now the urgency exists only in the subject line, not the creative.
- 2. Cut the redundant second "UP TO 50%" banner near the bottom — it directly competes with and undercuts the Flash Sale hero's claim.
- 3. Trim the block count. Pick 3-4 category blocks tied to the flash sale specifically (not the standing weekly catalog of drinkware/golf balls/fishing gear) so this reads as a distinct event, not another kitchen-sink digest.
- 4. Vary CTA copy per block ("Shop Cleats," "Shop Golf Balls") instead of ten identical "SHOP NOW" buttons — gives scanners a reason to stop on a block that matches their category.
- 5. Space out sends — this brand has sent near-identical "up to X% off" emails on 7/24, 7/26, 7/28 (x2), 7/29 (x2), and 7/30 (x2). A subscriber opening this cold has no signal it isn't a rerun.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Flash Sale ends in 48 hours — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Flash Sale deals disappear Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just dropped across shoes, golf and back-to-school.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points stack with every Flash Sale buy.`

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Flash Sale" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
This is an Epsilon Harmony‑built ESP template (tracking domain `app.dickssportinggoods.com`) with one confirmed broken link (unsubscribe), missing bulk‑sender compliance headers, and a malformed/invalid inline style and JSON‑LD block. Automated QA pass rate: 43% (1 fail, 3 warnings).

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link (FAIL):** `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fb7cba6ad9eee14f4bbe5c7c0/67f035debcbd49030000021ef3a0bd00` resolves to an error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. The Epsilon Harmony redirect wrapper is pointing at a dead subscription‑center path.
- Click tracking uses opaque Epsilon redirect wrappers (`/T/{campaignId}/{msgId}/{recipientId}?__dU__=...`) rather than direct URLs — expected for this ESP, not a defect in itself, but it means destination URLs (and any UTM params) can't be verified from source (see §6).
- QA probed 12 of 63 total links (51 footer/social/utility links deprioritized by design); no additional broken links reported among the probed set.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Malformed CSS in MSO/Outlook conditional block:** the `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style>` block contains a stray quote character: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` — the trailing `"` before `}` is invalid CSS syntax and will either be ignored or break the `div` rule in Outlook's Word rendering engine.
- **Invalid JSON‑LD:** the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains `//`‑style comments (e.g., `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON does not support comments, so this structured‑data block is not valid JSON — parsers (including Gmail's promo‑annotation reader) will fail to parse it and it will be silently discarded.
- **9 images missing `alt` text**, confirmed by QA: `banner4.gif`, `golf.png`, `cleats.png`, `fishing.jpg`, `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`, plus the open‑tracking pixel (`67f035debcbd490300004c5a42963aa1`, acceptable to omit since it's `display:none`). This affects screen readers and fallback rendering when images are blocked by default (Outlook, some mobile clients).
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded with `font-display:swap` and a system fallback stack (`Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`) — no issue.
- Mobile responsive classes (`.mobile-off`, `.header-xl`, etc.) and MSO ghost-table pattern are present and structured correctly.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No merge/personalization tags (e.g., `{{firstName}}`) appear in the visible source. However, the JSON‑LD `DiscountOffer`/`PromotionCard` objects ship to production with **unpopulated template fields**: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""`, and a stale placeholder date `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`. These read as un-filled template defaults left in the deployed email rather than intentional empty values.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe mechanism is broken** (see §2) — a functional opt-out is a CAN-SPAM requirement; this is a compliance risk, not just a UX bug.
- **List-Unsubscribe header not found (WARN):** no RFC 2369 header captured — may be a limitation of the AgentMail relay rather than the sender's actual headers.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header not found (WARN):** no RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe support confirmed, which is required by Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender guidelines for high-volume senders.
- **Authentication-Results header not found (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status cannot be verified from this capture; QA notes this is expected via the relay and not necessarily an authentication failure — recommend verifying via ESP send logs directly.
- Physical mailing address / footer compliance block is outside the truncated HTML and could not be evaluated.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
All material links route through opaque Epsilon Harmony tracking redirects (`/T/.../__dU__=<encoded token>`) rather than exposing readable UTM parameters at the source level. Final landing-page URLs and whether they carry `utm_source`/`utm_medium`/`utm_campaign` cannot be confirmed from the truncated HTML — this requires following the redirect chain to the resolved destination.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect target (`/pc/subscription` error) — highest priority, CAN-SPAM exposure.
2. Confirm List-Unsubscribe / List-Unsubscribe-Post headers are sent (check ESP config directly, not just via relay capture) for Gmail/Yahoo bulk-sender compliance.
3. Verify SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass status via ESP delivery logs since the relay didn't capture Authentication-Results.
4. Add `alt` text to the 9 flagged content/footer images.
5. Remove the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `<style>` block (`padding:0;"}`).
6. Strip `//` comments from the JSON-LD block (invalid JSON) and either populate or remove the empty `discountCode`/`description`/`image`/`availabilityEnds` fields and stale 2020 `availabilityStarts` date.
7. Spot-check one resolved (unwrapped) link to confirm UTM parameters are present and aligned with site analytics expectations.
