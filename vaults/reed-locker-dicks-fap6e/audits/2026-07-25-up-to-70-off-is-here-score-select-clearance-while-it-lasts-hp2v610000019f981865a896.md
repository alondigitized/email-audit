# Up to 70% off is here — score select clearance while it lasts
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-25**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S thirteenth-or-so promotional blast since mid-July, and the "Up to 70% Off" hero is now a rerun — the almost-identical "CLEARANCE deals: up to 70% off!" send from 2026-07-24 used the same headline percentage. The hero itself is clean: bold red-and-black treatment, giant "70% OFF" stamp, unambiguous "SHOP NOW" button on the Summer Clearance Event block. Below the fold, the email sprawls into a long, undifferentiated stack — Nike Deal Days, HydroJug, golf gloves, MLB gear, Rawlings gloves, bikes, Peloton shoes, then a "must-have gear" grid — with no visual hierarchy telling the reader which of these ten offers actually matters.
- The structural bones (category nav bar, ScoreCard rewards banner, footer app-download and social block) are consistent with prior sends, which is fine for brand recognition but means nothing here differentiates itself from the six other "up to X% off" emails this subscriber has received in the past two weeks. It's competent execution of a fatigued playbook.

## What's working

- Hero headline and offer ("Up to 70% Off") is legible instantly with strong contrast, no scrolling needed.
- Primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") is a clear, high-contrast green button directly under the hero copy.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give an alternate path in for browsers who don't want clearance specifically.
- No render bugs — every image, button, and text block displays cleanly with no overlap or broken assets.
- Loyalty/ScoreCard points balance is visible at the very top for members.

## What's weak

- Ten-plus separate offers (70%, 30%, 25%, 50%, $20 off, 50%, 20%) crammed into one email dilutes the headline deal and gives the eye nowhere to rest after the hero.
- "Up to 70% Off" repeats the exact hero language from yesterday's clearance send — zero creative differentiation across consecutive days.
- No countdown timer or explicit end date despite "Limited Time" tags on two blocks — the urgency is asserted, not proven.
- Fine print ("Exclusions apply. Select clearance styles only...") sits directly under the hero offer, which undercuts the "70% off" promise before the reader even scrolls.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to 3-4 offers max and lead with a single dominant one below the hero instead of a flat, ten-block wall — right now nothing after the hero has any priority over anything else.
- 2. Add a real countdown clock or explicit "Ends [date]" to the hero to convert the "Limited Time" tag into credible urgency, since this subscriber has now seen unsubstantiated urgency language in nearly every send this month.
- 3. Vary the headline percentage/language from yesterday's send — reusing "up to 70% off" back-to-back reads as the same email twice.
- 4. Move or shrink the exclusions fine print so it doesn't immediately sit under the hero CTA.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard points + 70% off clearance, together`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus Nike Deal Days and $20 off Rawlings gloves`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on $49+ orders, ends soon`
