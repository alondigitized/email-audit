# 100s of NEW clearance styles await! 🚨 Take a look now...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S Sporting Goods making its post-Memorial Day pivot — after ten-plus sends anchored to "Epic Memorial Day Sale" creative, the "DICK'S | Clearance" sub-brand sender signals a deliberate creative reset. The hero lands cleanly: "HUNDREDS of NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" on a dark court-texture background communicates urgency and volume in a single glance. Below the fold, the email fragments into five distinct modules — Clearance, Runners Month, category deals, Graduation Shop, and the now-familiar $20 off $100 app offer — which dilutes the single throughline and makes this feel like a templated catch-all rather than a focused clearance event.
- The $20 off $100 app promo is appearing here for at least the fourth distinct time in six days (May 24 app-exclusive, May 24 "$20 off $100+ same-day," May 25 "On now! $20 Off $100+," and now again May 27). The offer hasn't changed and the creative hasn't changed. Subscribers who opened the Memorial Day run have already seen it; it's losing urgency fast.
- Judgement: a competent clearance launch that undersells itself by burying the lead under too many competing modules and recycling a stale app offer. Stronger editorial focus would have made this genuinely useful.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate and scannable** — "HUNDREDS of NEW CLEARANCE STYLES" reads in under a second and sets a clear value expectation.
- **Category navigation row (MEN / WOMEN / KIDS / ALL DEALS)** gives deep-linkers a quick path without hunting.
- **National Runners Month tie-in** adds editorial credibility to what could otherwise read as pure inventory dump.
- **Multiple specific percentage-off callouts** (25%, 20%, 40%, 50%) across deal tiles give the eye anchors to linger on.
- **Sender sub-brand "DICK'S | Clearance"** cleanly signals this is a different kind of send from the Memorial Day blast cadence — a real inbox differentiator.

## What's weak

- **Subject line buries the value** — "Take a look now..." is vague; "100s of NEW clearance styles await!" does all the work and then the second clause trails off.
- **🚨 emoji skews spam-adjacent** in the subject, especially paired with the ellipsis — reads like a "final warning" pattern that erodes trust.
- **The $20 off $100 app section is stale** — four appearances in six days with identical copy and identical creative. Subscribers have tuned it out.
- **No time-bound deadline visible anywhere** — "clearance" implies urgency but nothing in the render communicates when deals expire.
- **Graduation Shop module is off-message** — dropped into a clearance email, it competes with the core clearance narrative rather than reinforcing it.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the second subject-line clause.** "100s of NEW clearance styles — prices just dropped" is complete. Everything after "await! 🚨" reduces open rate.
- 2. **Set a hard deadline in the hero subtext.** Replace "Fresh Deals You Don't Want to Miss" with "Clearance ends Sunday — stock won't last." One concrete line converts browsing intent into session starts.
- 3. **Retire or visually refresh the $20 app module.** After four identical appearances in six days, the only lever left is creative differentiation or suppressing it from recent-opener segments entirely.
- 4. **Move Graduation Shop to a dedicated send or footer teaser.** It dilutes the clearance story here; clearance shoppers and graduation gift shoppers have different buying contexts.
- 5. **Surface one hero SKU with "X left in stock"** or "just dropped in clearance" framing — turns the mass-clearance message into something that feels personal and scarce.
- **Subject Alt A:** `New in clearance: 100s of styles, prices just dropped`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance drop — hundreds of new styles, this weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Up to 50% off — stock moves fast on clearance`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, Adidas, and more hit clearance today`

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Missing `alt` attribute on logo `<img>`:** The `<img id="logo">` tag has no visible `alt` attribute in the provided source. Images-off clients (Outlook default, many corporate email clients) will render a blank space with no fallback text. Should be `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"`.

**Tracking pixel missing `alt=""`:** The hidden open-pixel `<img>` has no `alt=""` attribute. Some screen readers will announce the image URL aloud. Add `alt=""` to suppress.

**`border-collapse:separate; border-spacing:0`** on layout tables: functionally correct but redundant — `separate` with `0` spacing is equivalent to `collapse`. Minor bloat, not a rendering bug.

**`<html lang="en">`**: present. `<meta charset="UTF-8">` and `<meta name="viewport">`: present. No issues.

**MSO conditional comments**: correctly scoped with `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]>`. No issues.
