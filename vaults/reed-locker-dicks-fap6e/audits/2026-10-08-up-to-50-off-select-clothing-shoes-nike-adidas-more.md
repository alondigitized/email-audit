# 💰 Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes (Nike, adidas & more!) 💰
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud, well-organized DICK'S promo. The hero is a giant "UP TO 50% OFF" in red with the Nike, adidas and Under Armour logos, and a green Shop Now button sits right under it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the ScoreCard points strip at the top is a nice loyalty touch.
- The problem is repetition. This is the third or fourth "up to 50%" send since the 10/5 Fall Deal Drop. It follows this morning's "This week's deals = up to 50% off! 💰" and the 10/7 "Final Hours" push. Below the hero it turns into a catch-all: fan shop 40%, drinkware 40%, team sports 45%, then an extra 25% off clearance. That's five competing offers, and the product picks are buried at the very bottom.
- Judgement: competent and easy to read, but a fatigued, scattershot send. It's the same "up to 50%" message in different packaging, with nothing new to reward the open.

## What's working

- The hero is a giant "UP TO 50% OFF" with brand logos, so the offer is unmistakable.
- A green Shop Now button directly under the hero is a clear primary CTA.
- The ScoreCard strip ("300 Points = $12 Reward") surfaces member benefits at the top.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Clearance, Fan Shop) gives easy navigation.
- The "One Day Only" Fan Shop block and "Extra 25% Off" clearance block add real urgency and a second price hook.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is the same "up to 50%" DICK'S has sent since 10/5, so it reads as stale.
- Five or more offers compete: 50%, 40% fan gear, 40% drinkware, 45% team sports, extra 25% clearance. The email has no single story.
- Most secondary CTAs are a generic "Shop Now" in a grey button, which is easy to skip.
- The only specific products (Samba OG, Handball Spezial, Nike joggers) sit at the very bottom, with no prices or reviews.
- The fine print is heavy: asterisks, "exclusions apply" and "up to" language dilute the headline offer.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero and cut the rest. For example, lead with the "Extra 25% off clearance" or the one-day Fan Shop deal, since both are fresher than another "up to 50%". Drop drinkware and team sports to a single secondary row.
- 2. Move the product picks (Samba OG, Handball Spezial, Nike Club Fleece) up under the hero, with a price or "was/now" on each.
- 3. Give the CTAs specific copy, such as "Shop Nike Deals" or "Shop Fan Gear", and make them green like the hero button.
- 4. Add a real deadline to the hero, like "Ends tonight 11:59 PT", instead of leaving it in the fine print.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 10/5 to 10/8 sends so subscribers see something new.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends tonight: extra 25% off clearance 🔥`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, adidas & Under Armour deals, plus 40% off fan gear today only.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: stack points on top of today's markdowns.`
