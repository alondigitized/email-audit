# Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles 🚨 (limited time!)
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent DICK'S clearance send, and the hero does its job. A red argyle panel with a giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF" is the first thing the eye lands on. A "Limited Time" kicker and one red Shop Now button sit right under it. The offer is easy to read and honest, and the sender and brand are familiar.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. A row of category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop and others) leads into six category blocks at different discount levels: 40% clothing and shoes, 50% fan shop, 50% outdoor, 40% team sports, 50% golf, 50% fitness. Then a second "UP TO 50% OFF" hero appears, followed by a gift grid. The 70% promise in the subject and hero gets diluted fast, because nothing below it is actually 70% off. The email repeats the same ask many times and sends the reader in many directions.
- This is the second 70% clearance send in about 24 hours. The 11/03 "We think you'll like this...Up to 70% off select clearance" used the same hero. On top of that, the Holiday Deal Dash and "early savings" sends (11/02–11/04) all run the same argyle template. Subscribers will see this as more of the same.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 70% OFF" on the red hero is unmissable and shows the offer immediately.
- One red Shop Now button sits directly under the hero, so the primary CTA is clear.
- The category pills under the hero (Men's, Women's, Kids', Fan Shop, All Sale) give a quick way to self-segment.
- The seasonal photography with holiday ornaments in the corners fits the current campaign.
- Free-shipping and store-pickup notes appear under several blocks, and the gift-pick grid at the bottom has product names and images.

## What's weak

- No block below the hero shows 70% off. The category blocks say 40% or 50%, so the headline offer feels like a stretch.
- A second "UP TO 50% OFF / This Week's Deals" hero appears near the bottom and competes with the first one.
- Six category blocks, each with its own button or button pair, produce a long scroll with no clear priority. The email runs very long.
- Specific sale products have no prices. The gift-grid items are named but show no sale price or discount.
- The "Explore more deals" pills are small and tightly packed, and they sit between the hero and the products.
- The fine print under the hero is dense. "Valid online only…" and the exclusions are small, so the limited-time deadline is vague with no end date.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the 70% real. Add a row of 3–4 actual products at 70% off, with was/now prices, directly under the hero. Cut or collapse the lower 40–50% blocks.
- 2. Drop the second "UP TO 50% OFF" hero. It undercuts the 70% message.
- 3. Give the "limited time" claim a specific deadline, such as "Ends Sunday 11/8" or a countdown.
- 4. Make the category buttons specific ("Shop 70% off sneakers") instead of generic "Shop Now".
- 5. Space out clearance sends. A 70% clearance email on 11/03 and again on 11/04 reads as repetition.
