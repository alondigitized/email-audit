# You'll like this: Up to 50% off this week's DEALS 👍
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the fourth near-identical "UP TO 50% OFF" red-gradient hero DICK'S has run in this stretch (9/12, 9/13, 9/14 twice), now under yet another retitled subject — "You'll Like This: Up to 50% Off This Week's DEALS." The hero art itself is clean this time (no broken "FLASH" text like 9/12), sitting over a decent outdoor photo with legible white-on-red type. But this is also the longest, most sprawling send yet: it stacks clothing/shoes, drinkware, fan shop, team sports, trending categories, AND a second full "$25 AND UNDER" clearance module with its own hero and product grid — essentially two emails glued together.
- The scattershot approach undercuts urgency. There's no deadline, no countdown, no single hero offer to click — just a wall of "Shop Now" buttons across seven unrelated categories. For a recipient with a defined focus area, this reads as generic mass-blast rather than targeted merchandising.
- Judgement: functional and bug-free, but bloated and undifferentiated from the last three sends. Score accordingly — mid-tier at best.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly: bold white "UP TO 50% OFF" on a legible red gradient block, no overlapping text or broken images.
- Category pill navigation (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) gives a clear quick-scan structure near the top.
- Product grid at the bottom (adidas Samba, New Balance 530/9060, Nike socks) gives concrete, specific items rather than vague category tiles.
- Multiple distinct "Shop Now" CTAs are visually consistent (dark green pill buttons) and easy to spot throughout.

## What's weak

- This is the same red-gradient "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has used repeatedly (9/12, 9/13, 9/14 x2) — zero creative differentiation in a subscriber's inbox this week.
- No urgency or deadline anywhere in the email — no countdown, no "ends tonight," unlike the 9/12 send which at least had a ticking timer.
- The email is excessively long and unfocused — clothing, drinkware, fan shop, team sports, trending, and a second clearance campaign all crammed into one send with no prioritization.
- "This Week's Deals" as a hero headline is vague and interchangeable with prior sends' generic titling.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard-specific offer or pricing is called out despite a ScoreCard banner appearing at the very top — a missed personalization opportunity.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut this email in half — split the "$25 and Under" clearance push into its own send rather than bolting it onto the weekly deals email; two dilutes both.
- 2. Pick ONE hero offer/category and lead with it specifically (e.g., "Up to 40% Off Nike & New Balance") instead of the generic "This Week's Deals" umbrella — specificity drives clicks more than breadth.
- 3. Add a real deadline to the hero — even "Ends Sunday" beats no urgency at all, and this brand has proven timers work (9/12).
- 4. Surface ScoreCard member pricing/points explicitly since the banner is already there — right now it's just a static header with no offer tied to it.
- 5. Retire the red-gradient "UP TO 50% OFF" creative for at least one cycle — it's now appeared 4+ times in six days and is losing all cut-through.
