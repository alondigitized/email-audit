# Up to 50% off our best deals of the week is HERE! 📍
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-11**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S third "Best Deals of the Week" compilation in four days — the June 8 send (7/10) ran this exact format, and now here it is again with a slightly higher 50% ceiling bolted onto the hero. The email is competent: the red "Up to 50% OFF" badge lands immediately, the multi-module layout gives several entry points (running shoes, Carhartt tee, Women's Under Armour), and the Father's Day fulfillment block at the bottom is timely for June 11. Nothing is broken.
- The problem is cadence. A subscriber who opened the June 5 "Best Deals" (9/10), the June 8 Father's Day-wrapped "Best Deals" (8/10), and yesterday's Father's Day Savings Event (9/10) is now seeing a fourth near-identical send in seven days. The 50% ceiling is borrowed directly from yesterday's Father's Day event subject line — it doesn't feel like a new offer, it feels like a re-skin. "Is HERE! 📍" in the subject adds no information; it's exclamation-mark urgency with no deadline attached.
- Solid execution on a tired premise. The individual modules (especially the named Carhartt KB7 tee at 25% off) save the click score, but the open is working against heavy subscriber fatigue.

## What's working

- The red "Up to 50% OFF" badge in the hero is visually dominant — the eye lands there first without confusion.
- Named product specificity in modules: "25% Off Carhartt KB7 Pocket Tee" and "Up to 40% Off Select Running Shoes" are real offers, not category generalities.
- Father's Day fulfillment module ("Get it for Father's Day" + "Same-Day Delivery or Store Pickup") is well-timed and directly useful on June 11.
- Clean multi-column layout with no visible render bugs or broken images.
- Category nav bar (Women's / Men's / Girls / Outdoor / Clearance) gives quick self-segmentation.

## What's weak

- Cadence is broken — this is the fourth "Best Deals of the Week" format in seven days; open fatigue is real.
- "Is HERE! 📍" in the subject is meaningless urgency — no deadline, no event end date, no reason this deal expires.
- 50% ceiling mirrors yesterday's Father's Day Savings Event headline exactly; nothing signals a new or better offer.
- "SHOP NOW" CTAs throughout are generic — the individual module offers deserve button copy that matches (e.g., "Shop Running Shoes").
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member pricing or exclusive badge visible anywhere in the render.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Rest the compilation format for 48 hours.** Four "Best Deals" emails in a week trains subscribers to ignore them. Even a format change — lead with a single product story, not a grid — would reset attention. The Carhartt KB7 module alone could carry a dedicated send.
- 2. **Attach a real deadline.** "Ends Sunday" or "Through Father's Day" transforms "is HERE!" from noise into an action trigger.
- 3. **Differentiate from yesterday.** If the Father's Day event (same 50% ceiling) ran June 10, today's email must either top the offer or reframe it — "Last chance before Father's Day" is a distinct angle; "Best Deals of the Week" for the fourth time is not.
- 4. **CTA specificity.** Replace generic "SHOP NOW" with module-matched verbs: "Shop Running Shoes," "Grab the Carhartt Tee," "Shop Women's UA."
- **Subject Alt A:** `Last chance: 50% off before Father's Day`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Carhartt, running shoes & more — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Order by Sunday for guaranteed Father's Day delivery`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Running shoes, tees, women's UA — deals end this weekend`

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical — JSON-LD block contains JavaScript-style comments inside a JSON context:**

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!

}
```

`//` comments are **not valid JSON**. This will cause the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block to fail parsing. Gmail's Promotions tab annotation (logo, deal badge, promo image) will not render.
