# Ending TONIGHT 💸 Up to 50% off Flash Sale deals
**Score:** 4/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-24**
## Executive summary

- This is the same orange lightning-bolt "FLASH" hero DICK'S has now run at least four times this week (9/23's "Up to 50% off starts now!" at 5.00/10, 9/22's "Slow your scroll" clearance version at 4.00/10), and this time it ships with a visible crop bug — the hero headline literally reads "FLAS" with the H sliced off by the image edge. The deadline framing ("Final Hours," valid through 9/24 11:59PM PT) is legitimately the strongest urgency hook DICK'S has used all week, and the fine print under the CTA is honest and specific rather than buried.
- Below the fold it's the identical kitchen-sink scroll pattern this brand runs every single send: fan shop tiles, a clearance interstitial, then six product picks with no organizing logic. Nothing here is broken enough to kill the click, but nothing is new enough to earn one either — a subscriber who opened the 9/22 or 9/23 email has already seen this exact structure twice.
- Judgement: functional but stale, and the cropped hero text is a real production defect that undercuts the one thing (urgency) this send does well.

## What's working

- "Final Hours" + explicit 9/24 11:59PM PT deadline creates credible, time-bounded urgency (unlike vaguer past sends).
- Green "Shop Now" CTA buttons are consistent, high-contrast, and easy to spot at every section.
- Terms/fine print (exclusions, "while quantities last," conflict-with-other-offers language) is visible and not deceptively hidden.
- Category quick-links (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Halloween Shop) give an easy secondary path in without scrolling far.

## What's weak

- Hero headline is visibly cropped — "FLASH" renders as "FLAS" with the H cut off by the image boundary, a clear render bug.
- This is the fourth near-identical orange "FLASH" hero this week (9/22, 9/23 x2, now 9/24) — zero creative differentiation send-over-send.
- Body is an undifferentiated kitchen-sink scroll (fan shop tiles → clearance banner → 6 unrelated product picks) with no hierarchy pointing to a single best offer.
- No product-specific detail (price, size availability, member pricing) on any of the six featured picks — just name + image.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the cropped "FLASH" hero image immediately — this is a shipped production bug, not a style choice, and it's the first thing every recipient sees.
- 2. Retire this hero creative for at least a week; reusing the same orange lightning-bolt lockup four times in five days is training subscribers to skip the open.
- 3. Add a price anchor to the hero (e.g., "Shoes from $19.99") instead of just "Up to 50% Off" — a concrete number outperforms a vague ceiling.
- 4. Cut the product grid from six unrelated picks to 2-3 tied directly to the flash sale, with visible sale price next to each.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 50% off ends at midnight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your Flash Sale cart disappears at 11:59PM`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sneakers, fan gear, and clearance — all marked down before tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call: sizes are already going fast.`

## Technical Audit

Email renders via standard responsive/Outlook-conditional markup with tracked links, but fails on plain-text fallback, unsubscribe headers, and image alt text — no structural HTML defects found.

- All CTA links route through the mclick redirector (`data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=...&pid=...`), consistent with standard ESP click-tracking.
- Open-tracking pixel present: `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...` — flagged only because it lacks alt text (see Rendering & Accessibility), not a functional issue.
- QA notes 12 of 54 total links were probed (rest deprioritized as footer/utility/social) — no broken-link data available for the 42 unprobed links; scope limitation, not a confirmed defect.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` attributes: `fan-shop-2026-0924-d`, `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`, plus the tracking pixel (`open`). Screen readers will announce these as unlabeled or fall back to filename/blank.
- Outlook conditional comments (`mso 9`/`IE`) and `@media screen` font-face block are present and correctly scoped, so no MSO rendering fallback issues detected.
- Mobile breakpoint rules (`max-width:480px`) are defined for CTA/chip sizing and column collapse — structurally sound, not independently verified by QA.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No issues found — no unresolved merge tags (e.g. `{{first_name}}`, `%%FIELD%%`) or personalization placeholders present in the visible source.

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe` header not found — cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than a sender-side omission).
- `[WARN]` `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) header not found — same caveat.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs use opaque encoded `params`/`pid` tokens via the mclick redirector rather than visible UTM query parameters (e.g. `utm_source`, `utm_campaign`) — actual landing-page URL and UTM propagation cannot be verified without following the redirect chain.
- No direct (non-redirected) links to `dickssportinggoods.com` pages found in the truncated source to cross-check landing-page alignment with the "Flash Sale" subject/CTA.

7. Recommendations
