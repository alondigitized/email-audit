# Hot holiday DEALS = Up to 50% OFF!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a strong hero on top of a bloated body. The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on a green argyle panel registers instantly, and "Shop Now" sits right under it. But the hero never says what the 50% applies to. The only context is a small "This Week's Deals" header, and the offer is a ceiling, not a promise.
- Below the hero the email turns into a catalog. Clothing and shoes, Owala, Walter Hagen golf, cleats, Nishiki bikes, Chirp recovery gear, a repeat of the EXTRA 25% clearance block, and a six-item gift grid all follow. That is roughly nine CTAs in one scroll, and nothing says which one matters. The clearance block near the bottom is the same red "EXTRA 25% OFF" creative as this morning's "You deserve an EXTRA 25% off select clearance!" send, so it reads as a leftover.
- For a subscriber who got the 11/10 "Holiday Deal Dash" emails, the 11/11 11.11 sale emails and today's clearance email, this is the fifth or sixth DICK'S send in three days. The "up to 50%" hook is the same one as the 11/10 sends. Verdict: a good headline offer, weak focus, and fatigued cadence.

## What's working

- The gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on dark green is high contrast and readable in about a second.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give a fast route to a department.
- Each module pairs a specific discount with one short line and one button, such as "Up to 40% Off Select Cleats" and "BOGO 50% Off Walter Hagen." This makes each module easy to skim.
- Lifestyle photography is clean and seasonal, with holiday-themed cleats and bikes, and nothing looks broken.
- The gift picks grid shows named products, which gives some concrete product detail.

## What's weak

- The hero doesn't say what is 50% off, and the single button goes to a generic "This Week's Deals."
- There are about nine competing modules, plus a gift grid and a repeated clearance block. The hierarchy collapses after the hero.
- The red "EXTRA 25% OFF" clearance block is a near-duplicate of today's earlier email, and its red palette clashes with the green hero.
- The "Order Today, Sport It Tomorrow" shipping line and the ScoreCard rewards bar are tiny, so member value is easy to miss.
- The fine print under the hero and the clearance block is dense and hard to read. Several modules carry their own exclusions, such as "Reg. $55.00–$149.00 Each, Exclusions apply."

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three or four modules. Lead with the strongest categories, put the gift grid second, and drop the repeated clearance block. This is the biggest lift, because it gives the click one clear job.
- 2. Name the product in the hero, for example "Up to 50% off gifts, gear and shoes," and make the button specific. Use "Shop Gifts Up to 50% Off" instead of "Shop Now."
- 3. Move the ScoreCard and free-pickup benefit into a larger strip near the top, and add a real deadline such as "Ends Sunday."
- 4. Stagger the cadence. Merge today's clearance email and this one into one send, or space them at least a day apart.
- 5. Cut the exclusions to one short disclaimer line with a link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off gifts, gear & shoes — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your holiday gift list, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Free same-day pickup on Nike, adidas, Owala & more. Earn ScoreCard points on every gift.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Cleats, bikes, tumblers and golf gear, all marked down through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML source is structurally sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` tables, responsive media queries, MSO conditionals). However, the plain-text part is effectively empty (9 chars), and 12 images have no alt text, including the hero panels that carry the offer. Authentication and unsubscribe headers could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. 12 material links were probed and 51 were skipped by the ranking, so coverage of the remaining links is unknown. No per-link status data was provided.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…&configId=512ffb51-…`. It is 1×1, with no alt attribute and no inline `display:none`. This is a standard ESP tracking pixel and not a defect.
- The HTML was truncated, so click-tracking wrappers and the unsubscribe URL could not be inspected. Anchors carry `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` and `class="arc-link"`, which suggests app deep-link handling. Fallback behavior for users without the app was not verifiable.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 characters.** The multipart/alternative text part is effectively empty. Text-only clients and some spam filters will see a blank message.
- **[WARN] 12 `<img>` elements have no `alt`.** The ones that matter are:
  - `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-1-new`
  - `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-2-new`
  - `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-3-bikes`
  - `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-4`
  - `2026-1112-dks-storewide-4pm-5`
  - `owalaholiday`

  These are the content panels, and with images blocked or a screen reader the offer is lost. The icons (`icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`) also lack alt text. The 1×1 open pixel should have `alt=""`.
- **Preheader is empty.** `#pht-spacer` contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler and no preheader text. The `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` template placeholder comment was left in the shipped HTML. Inbox previews will therefore show body text instead of intended preview copy.
- **Image dimensions:** the scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&fit=const` (for example `…4pm-1-new?fmt=png-alpha`). A PNG with alpha at 1200×1600 in a 600px layout is likely heavy. Actual byte size was not measured.
- **Editor artifacts:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` is leftover authoring markup. It is harmless but unclean.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible source. Only the truncated portion was available.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (RFC 8058):** not found. The QA tool notes the AgentMail relay may strip them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. Verify against the raw message at the sender (`info@em.dcsg.com`). Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe.
- **Authentication-Results:** not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unknown. This is a relay limitation.
- **In-body unsubscribe link and physical address:** not visible in the truncated source. The QA tool states an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was shown. Not confirmed either way.
- The text part (9 chars) also means no plain-text unsubscribe or address footer exists.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
No issues confirmable. No UTM parameters or destination URLs were present in the truncated HTML or the QA output, so landing-page alignment could not be assessed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Generate a real plain-text part with the offer, the primary CTA URLs, the unsubscribe link and the physical address. This is the only hard FAIL.
2. Add descriptive `alt` text to the six content images, such as the offer text on each `…4pm-N` panel and `owalaholiday`. Use `alt=""` on decorative icons and the tracking pixel, or meaningful alt on the icons if they link.
3. Replace the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder with real preheader text.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` plus SPF/DKIM/DMARC from the raw headers at the receiving mailbox, since the relay hides them.
5. Reduce hero image requests to about 2× the display width (`wid=1200`, with `hei` cropped to the real layout) and prefer JPEG over `png-alpha` where transparency isn't needed.
6. Remove the `is-html-mode` and `data-contenteditable` leftovers from the production template.
