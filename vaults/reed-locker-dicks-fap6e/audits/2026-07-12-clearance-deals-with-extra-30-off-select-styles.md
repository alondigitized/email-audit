# CLEARANCE DEALS with extra 30% OFF select styles!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-12**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S is running the clearance frame again, and at least the creative actually earns it: the "EXTRA 30% OFF" hero is bold, bright, and impossible to miss. The orange field does real work here — it signals sale before the eye even reads the copy. That's the best thing about this email.
- The problem is everything below the fold. The email transitions from clearance into what appears to be regular Flash Sale modules, then into specific product callouts (HOKA Clifton 10, YETI Pace Purple), then bikes, then a "must-have summer gear" product strip — none of it clearly unified by the clearance mechanic. A subscriber who clicked through for 30% off clearance would land in a disjointed experience. The lack of a deadline anywhere in the email is a real miss; the urgency is borrowed from the subject line, not earned in the body.
- This is also at least the eleventh DICK'S email in roughly three days — the prior Flash Sale send from this same day scored 8/10, and the brand history shows clearance and flash-sale frames ping-ponging all week. The subscriber's tolerance is wearing thin. Functional creative, poor timing.

## What's working

- **Hero hierarchy is clean** — the orange/amber field with "EXTRA 30% OFF" in white commands attention immediately; no ambiguity about the lead offer.
- **SHOP NOW button is visible and above the fold** — no hunt required; the CTA is present under the hero copy.
- **Specific product callouts add credibility** — HOKA Clifton 10 and YETI Pace Purple are named explicitly, not just category blobs, which adds substance mid-email.
- **Summer seasonal tie-in works** — "Must-have gear for an epic summer!" at the bottom is appropriately timed for July.
- **No visible render bugs** — images load, layout holds, no broken elements.

## What's weak

- **No deadline anywhere** — a clearance + extra 30% off offer with zero urgency language in the body is a wasted lever; the only pressure is in the subject line.
- **Content coherence collapses below the fold** — clearance, Flash Sale, "$65 and Under," and "Up to $250 off bikes" are four different offer mechanics crammed into one send; the subscriber doesn't know what the email is actually about.
- **"CLEARANCE DEALS" in all-caps reads spammy** — the subject opens aggressively, which will suppress open rates in filtering environments.
- **Cadence fatigue is real** — this is landing the same day as the Two-Day Flash Sale send (audited today, 8/10); subscribers who already saw that email are getting another discount hit from the same brand hours later.
- **No loyalty/member pricing visible** — DICK's ScoreCard integration is absent; for members this is a missed personalization hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Add a time deadline in the hero** — "Ends Sunday" or "Today only" next to the 30% off figure would double the urgency without redesigning anything. This is the single highest-leverage change.
- 2. **Consolidate the offer frame** — pick one mechanic (clearance + 30% off) and make every product tile reinforce it. Strip the Flash Sale module and "$65 and Under" sections; they dilute the lead.
- 3. **Fix the subject line casing** — lowercase "clearance deals" reads less spammy and still carries the offer. Reserve all-caps for single words, not phrases.
- 4. **Add a ScoreCard/member callout** — even one line ("Members earn bonus points on clearance") would justify opens from loyal subscribers.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 30% off clearance — this weekend only`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance upgrade: extra 30% off select styles`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Score deeper on already-reduced gear before it sells out.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `HOKA, YETI, bikes + more — extra 30% off select clearance styles.`

## Technical Audit

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Standard ESP-managed email (pattern matches Salesforce Marketing Cloud) with correct MSO conditionals and mobile breakpoints. The primary technical failures are malformed JSON-LD structured data — containing invalid JS comments and stale/empty merge tokens — which will silently prevent Gmail Promotions tab annotations from rendering.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Open pixel** — `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f57fc2b4da7a9796e965fd798/2eff6460a34443ab00004c5a42963aa1` — present and formatted correctly.

**CTA redirect chain** — All links wrap through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019f57fc2b4da7a9796e965fd798/...` with a `__dU__` recipient-identifier token encoded in the query string. UTM parameters are not visible in raw HTML; they must be confirmed by inspecting the redirect destination. Flag for QA: spot-check at least one CTA redirect to verify `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign` are appended downstream.

**`__dU__` token** is PII-bearing (maps to a recipient record). Confirm ESP data-processing agreement covers this token's exposure in server logs at the redirect host.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**MSO CSS conflict — border-collapse** — The Outlook conditional stylesheet sets:
```css
table { border-collapse: collapse; }
```
The body tables all use inline `border-collapse: separate`. Outlook applies the stylesheet rule over inline styles in this context, so all tables will render with `collapse` in Outlook — potentially causing unintended border rendering between cells.

**Malformed CSS in MSO conditional** — The `<div>` rule has a stray `"` before the closing brace:
```css
div { background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;" }
```
The trailing `"` is invalid. Strict CSS parsers may drop the entire rule.

**Missing `alt` on tracking pixel** — `<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...' style="display:none...">` has no `alt=""`. Hidden pixel, so no visual impact, but screen readers and strict accessibility linters will flag it.

**Google Fonts version pin** — `@font-face` src references Archivo `v7` directly (`/s/archivo/v7/...`). Google occasionally retires version-pinned URLs; the fallback chain (`Arial, sans-serif`) is correct, but the `v7` URL should be replaced with an unversioned or current reference.

**`lang="en"` on `<html>`** — present. `role="presentation"` on layout tables — present. Both correct.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

The `application/ld+json` block has multiple unfilled template slots and structural errors:

**Invalid JSON — JavaScript comments** — The block uses `//` line comments:
```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported...
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": ""
}
```
`//` comments are not valid JSON. The entire structured-data block will be rejected by any conformant parser (Gmail, Google Search). None of the three objects will be evaluated.

**Stale date placeholder** — `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` is set to `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` — a 6-year-old date. This is clearly a copy-paste from a template that was never updated.

**Empty required fields** in `DiscountOffer`:
- `"description": ""`
- `"discountCode": ""`
- `"availabilityEnds": ""`

**Empty field** in `PromotionCard`:
- `"image": ""` — Gmail Promotions tab annotation requires a valid image URL; empty string means no thumbnail will be displayed.

---

### 5. Compliance

**CAN-SPAM physical address and unsubscribe** — HTML is truncated; these elements cannot be confirmed from the provided source. Must be verified in the full send. Required: sender's physical postal address (§5(a)(6)) and a functioning opt-out mechanism (§5(a)(3)).

**Sender authentication** — `From` domain is `e.dcsg.com`. SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment must be verified at the header level (not visible in HTML). Given Google and Yahoo's 2024 bulk-sender mandates, `p=quarantine` or `p=reject` DMARC policy on `dcsg.com`/`e.dcsg.com` is required at this send volume.

**Schema.org context uses HTTP** — `"@context": "http://schema.org/"` should be `https://schema.org/`. Minor but flagged by validators.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity

All CTAs are wrapped in ESP redirects; final landing-page UTM parameters cannot be confirmed from HTML alone. Required spot-check: decode one `/T/...` redirect and confirm:
- `utm_source=email`
- `utm_medium` distinguishing this campaign
- `utm_campaign` matching the campaign ID in the ESP

The GUID `2eff6460-a344-43ab-8aac-e4461ad8ed96` embedded in redirect URLs appears to be the broadcast/send ID — confirm it maps to the correct campaign record in reporting.
