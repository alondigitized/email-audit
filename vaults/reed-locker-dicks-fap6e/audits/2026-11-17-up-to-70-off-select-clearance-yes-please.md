# Up to 70% OFF select clearance? Yes, please! 🙌 ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-17**
## Executive summary

- This is the third clearance-themed send from DICK'S in roughly 24 hours. The first was this morning's "Limited time! ⏰ Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles is all yours..." on 11/16, which had the same hero and the same offer. The hero is strong: a huge red-and-gold "UP TO 70% OFF" over a holiday pattern, with a single "Shop Now" button right under it. The eye lands on the offer immediately.
- Below the hero it turns into a long, generic catalog. Cold-weather boots, North Face, socks, drinkware, fire pits, golf sets, hoops, cardio and a gift grid each get their own tile with a different offer (30% off, BOGO, 10% off, $100 off, 50% off, $1000 & under). The "70% off" promise gets diluted, and nothing in the body is actually clearance. Honest verdict: a competent template with a good hero. It's a near-repeat of yesterday's clearance email, with nothing new to give the subscriber a reason to act.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 70% OFF" hero is huge, high-contrast and impossible to miss.
- The single red "Shop Now" button sits directly under the hero headline, so the primary CTA is clear.
- The "Limited Time" kicker and a "valid online only for a limited time" line give urgency without shouting.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give a quick way to browse.
- The holiday styling and gift-oriented tiles fit the mid-November season.

## What's weak

- It's the same hero and offer as yesterday's 11/16 "Up to 70% OFF select clearance styles" send. It reads as a repeat, and the cadence is heavy after the app-exclusive loop of 11/14–11/15.
- Roughly eight different offers below the hero (30% off, BOGO, 10% off, $100 off, 50% off, $1000 & under) fight the clearance message. None of the tiles show clearance pricing.
- The email is very long, and the lower half is a plain product grid with no prices or discounts.
- The fine print under the hero is dense, and "select styles" with no example product or price makes the 70% claim feel vague.
- The loyalty strip (ScoreCard points) is tiny, and member benefits aren't used as a lever.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with a real clearance product grid (3–4 items with was/now prices). It proves the 70% claim and gives the click a concrete reason.
- 2. Cut the body to 3–4 tiles that all support clearance, and drop the unrelated BOGO, fire-pit and hoop offers.
- 3. Vary the creative from yesterday's send, or hold this one back. Don't send the same hero within 24 hours.
- 4. Add a real deadline ("Ends Sunday") and a clear ScoreCard member perk.
- 5. Make the CTA specific: "Shop Clearance Deals" rather than "Shop Now".

- **Subject Alt B:** `Boots, hoodies & gear: 70% off ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns on shoes, jackets and gear. Prices as marked while supplies last.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: extra points on clearance this week.`
