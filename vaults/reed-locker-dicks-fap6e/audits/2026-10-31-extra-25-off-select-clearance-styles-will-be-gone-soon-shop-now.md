# Extra 25% off select clearance styles will be gone soon... shop now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-31**
## Executive summary

- This is the third DICK'S clearance send of the day. It follows the 10/31 "Ends. Tonight!" email with the same extra-25% offer, so it works as a last-call reminder. The hero is strong: a huge white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on a deep-red leaf background. Below it sit a "Last Chance" eyebrow, a headline and a red Shop Now button, so the eye lands on the offer first.
- The body is where it loses focus. The email adds a second hero ("Up to 50% off" This Week's Deals), a Stanley holiday collection block, six product picks, and a services row. The "Ends Tonight" urgency in the hero fights with the evergreen "This Week's Deals" block. The disclaimer sits directly under the CTA and is easy to skim past. The subject says "will be gone soon," which is vaguer than the deadline stated in the body (10/31 11:59 PM PT).
- Judgement: a competent, clean send with a clear hero, but it repeats earlier creative and dilutes its own deadline. Expect a solid mid-range result.

## What's working

- The hero is oversized and high-contrast, and the offer is unmistakable.
- The "Last Chance" label, headline and red Shop Now button form a clear hierarchy beneath the hero.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give easy secondary paths.
- The ScoreCard bar shows 300 points and a $10 reward, so the loyalty benefit is visible.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The "Up to 50% off" block competes with the 25% clearance hero and muddies the single-offer message.
- The Stanley block and product picks have no prices or deals, so they don't support a clearance message.
- The product picks (Samba, Nike backpack, joggers) are not clearly tied to clearance or to the 25% off.
- The deadline appears only in small grey fine print, not near the headline or button.
- This is the same offer as the earlier "Ends. Tonight!" send, which makes the cadence feel repetitive.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the deadline in the hero or headline: "Ends tonight, 11:59 PM PT". Move the fine print higher or make it more legible.
- 2. Cut the 50% and Stanley blocks. Replace them with a row of clearance products showing before and after prices.
- 3. Label the picks as clearance and show the discounted price on each.
- 4. Add a countdown or a "Final hours" banner. Since this is the second send of the day, consider suppressing it for people who already clicked the earlier one.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Ends at midnight: extra 25% off clearance`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear. Offer ends 11:59 PM PT tonight.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off select clearance ends tonight. Prices as marked.`
