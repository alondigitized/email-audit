# LAST CALL 📣 Up to 50% off Flash Sale deals
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-15**
## Executive summary

- This is the second flash-sale send of the day, and it is a near-clone of this morning's "Just for TODAY ⚡️ Up to 50% off Flash Sale deals." The "LAST CALL 📣" subject promises urgency. The body delivers none: there is no countdown and no "ends tonight" in the hero. The only deadline is 11:59PM PT in 8pt fine print under the first CTA.
- The hero is the weakest part. The orange "FLASH" graphic is cropped so it reads "FLAS", which looks like a render bug or a bad crop. It is also a huge block of screen with no offer in it. The real offer, "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off," only shows up in text below the image. The second block, "Up to 70% Off Select Clearance," is the strong creative. It is the same asset used on 10/14, and it competes with the flash offer for attention.
- Judgement: competent template, wrong priorities. The email needs a stronger deadline, a hero that states the offer, and one offer instead of two.

## What's working

- The subject "LAST CALL" frames the send as a closing beat after the morning flash email.
- The text block under the hero is clear: a "One Day Only" eyebrow, "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off," and a green Shop Now button.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give a fast way into the shopper's own category.
- The "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance block is high-contrast and readable in about a second.
- The ScorecardⓇ strip and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow" service line add loyalty and convenience signals.

## What's weak

- The hero "FLASH" is cut off at the right edge and reads "FLAS". It looks broken, and it never mentions 50% off.
- Nothing in the body says "last call." There is no countdown, no "ends tonight," and no hours-left language. The 11:59PM PT deadline is buried in grey fine print.
- Two competing offers (50% flash and 70% clearance) with different CTA styles. The clearance button is grey and looks disabled.
- The product picks are unlabeled: no prices, no discount, no ratings, no "Shop" link. Several are non-sale items such as Adidas Sambas, so the grid doesn't support a flash-sale story.
- The cadence is heavy. This is the same flash template as this morning, and the clearance block repeats 10/14's creative.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rebuild the hero so it states the offer and the deadline: "Flash Sale ends tonight, up to 50% off," with a live countdown. Make sure the type is not cropped.
- 2. Drop the 70% clearance block or move it below the product grid. Lead with one offer.
- 3. Show sale prices with strikethrough on the product picks, and link each one to its product page. Use real flash-sale items.
- 4. Make both buttons green and specific, for example "Shop Flash Deals" instead of "Shop Now."
- 5. Move the 11:59PM PT deadline out of fine print and into the headline area.
- 6. Don't send this as a second near-identical email in one day. Suppress it for people who clicked the morning send.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale closes at midnight PT ⚡`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select clothing, shoes & more. Last chance before 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, adidas & more at up to 50% off. Ends tonight.`
