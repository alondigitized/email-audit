# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-07-23
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-23**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up Dick's on my phone looking to check cleat prices before my son's next tournament weekend, and instead got a error screen. The whole homepage is down — not slow, not weird, just completely gone. All I can see is the Dick's logo, a blue "Oops, Something Went Wrong." message, and a note telling me to come back in 12 hours. That's it. As a guy who does most of his gear resupply runs online between practices, this is a non-starter.

## What's working

- **Dick's logo** is recognizable and centered — I know I'm in the right place at least before realizing nothing else loaded.
- **Error message is legible** — the text is clean, not broken, and the card layout is tidy. At least it didn't freeze my browser.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is a 503-level error** — "We are working on the problem. Please try accessing the site again after 12 hours." Twelve hours is a brutal ask for a parent trying to price out cleats the night before tournament weekend.
- **No fallback content** — there's nothing below the error card. Blank gray page for the full scroll height. A well-designed error state would at least surface a phone number, store locator, or cached category links.
- **Error code exposed to end users** — `Error: 0.23a7cb17.1784802084.9744d571 IP: 47.229.200.33` means nothing to me and looks unprofessional. I'm not a developer. That's their housekeeping, not my problem.
- **No ETA or status link** — "try again after 12 hours" is vague and creates zero confidence. A status page URL or a text-me-when-it's-back option would at least keep me in the funnel.

## Recommendations

- **Build a real error page** — include store locator, customer service number, and a "Browse our app" CTA as a fallback so users have somewhere to go instead of bouncing to Field & Stream or Public Lands.
- **Remove the raw error code** from the customer-facing view — swap it for a clean reference number if support needs it ("Reference: #XXXX"), or cut it entirely.
- **Add a "Notify me when we're back" email/text capture** — if the site is going to be down 12 hours, give me a reason to come back instead of just telling me to try again.
- **Set a realistic expectation** — "12 hours" feels arbitrary. If you know when the fix deploys, say it. If you don't, say "we're working on it" and link a status page.
