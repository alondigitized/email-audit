# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-05-08
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-05-08**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up Dick's on my phone and got a full error page — "Oops, Something Went Wrong." — with a 12-hour wait message and an error code slapped in bold. There is no homepage to evaluate. As someone who needs to grab cleats or reorder a jersey between practices, this is a hard stop. The site is simply down.

## What's working

- The Dick's logo renders correctly inside the error card — at least I know I hit the right domain and didn't get spoofed somewhere.
- The error message is plain English: "We are working on the problem. Please try again after 12 hours." No jargon. At least I know what to do next.

## What's weak

- **The entire homepage is missing.** I'm a dad between two practices with $200 to spend on cleats. There is nothing to engage with.
- **"Try again after 12 hours"** is a brutal ask for a sports retailer. Weeknight shopping is when dads like me actually buy. Twelve hours from now the window is gone.
- **No fallback links** — there's no "browse anyway," no search bar, no category links below the error. I'm completely dead-ended.
- **Error code exposed in bold** — "Error: 0.23a7cb17..." displayed to a consumer looks sloppy and inspires zero confidence in the platform's reliability.
- **Empty gray void below the error card** — the rest of the viewport is just a flat gray background for thousands of pixels. Looks broken on mobile, which it is.

## Recommendations

- **Add a graceful degradation page** with top-category links (Footwear, Team Sports, Apparel) so even during an outage I can attempt navigation. Don't dead-end me.
- **Hide the raw error code** from the consumer-facing message — route it to a log, not a `<strong>` tag on the homepage.
- **Surface a status page link or estimated restore time** that's actually specific (e.g., "Back by 9 PM ET") rather than "12 hours" which feels like a shrug.
- **Add a loyalty/email capture even on the error page** — "Site's down. Drop your email and we'll notify you when it's back + send you a promo code." Turn downtime into a list-builder.
