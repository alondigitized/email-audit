# $25 & under? That's right! Check out these clearance savings >>>
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-03**
## Executive summary

- The hero does its job. A giant white "$25 AND UNDER" sits on deep red, and "Clearance Deals" with a green Shop Now button sits directly below it. The price-point hook is the strongest idea here, and the render has no visible bugs. The weakness is what comes after: the "$25 and under" promise isn't followed through. The product picks carry no prices, so the hook is never proven.
- The body also muddles the message. A second module says "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing and Shoes", a different offer from the hero, and it gets a pale grey button. After that come category tiles (Team Sports, Golf, Drinkware) and a six-product grid with no prices, ratings or deadline. It reads like a generic catalog, not a $25 clearance edit.
- This follows the 10/3 "Up to 50% OFF" send and the 10/2 "Extra 25% off clearance" pair. It's a fresh angle (price ceiling instead of percent off), but it's still the fourth discount blast in about five days. I'd call it solid but unremarkable: a good hero attached to an underbuilt body.

## What's working

- The "$25 AND UNDER" hero is huge and high-contrast, so the offer is understood in under a second.
- The green Shop Now button under "Clearance Deals" is clear and visible.
- The price-ceiling framing is fresher than another "up to X% off".
- The ScorecARD points strip and the free-shipping and pickup line at the top reinforce loyalty and convenience.
- The layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text, and the category pills make browsing easy.

## What's weak

- The product grid (adidas Samba, Birkenstock, Nike socks, New Balance 530) shows no prices, so nothing proves "$25 and under".
- A second offer, "Up to 40% Off Select Clothing and Shoes", dilutes the hero. Its grey Shop Now button is weaker than the hero's green one.
- The urgency is vague ("before they disappear for good!") with no deadline.
- The picks look like full-price items (adidas Sambas, Birkenstocks), which undercuts the clearance promise.
- The fine print says "exclusions apply" and "select clearance styles only", which weakens trust in the headline.

## Recommendations

- 1. Make the product grid prove the hook: six clearance items, each with a visible price of $25 or under, a strikethrough original price and a "Shop $25 & Under" CTA.
- 2. Cut the "Up to 40% off" module, or fold it in as a secondary line. One offer per send.
- 3. Add a real deadline, such as "Ends Sunday", plus a stock cue like "Limited sizes".
- 4. Make the CTA copy specific: "Shop $25 & Under Clothing" in place of "Shop Now". Use the same green on every button.
- 5. Space out clearance sends. This is the fourth discount email in about five days, so combine or throttle them.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Everything here is $25 or less 👟`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tees, socks, shoes and more, marked down through Sunday.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Nike, adidas and Under Armour clearance. Sizes are going fast.`
