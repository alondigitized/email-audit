# *** Flash Sale countdown: shop before it ends *** ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-12**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S third "Flash Sale" wordmark send this run, but it's the cleanest execution yet — the "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown timer sits right at the top with a legit locker-room hero, and this time the "FLASH SALE" text isn't cropped off the edge like the 08/12 morning send. It also lands above the fold with a real, unambiguous offer: "Flash Sale: Up to 50% Off," a single green SHOP NOW button, no scroll needed on mobile.
- The problem is what happens after that: it immediately reverts to the same weekly-digest sprawl this brand keeps running — Epic Shoe Sale, a "This Week's Deals" beach-ball banner reused verbatim from 08/11, then a six-item product grid. The countdown clock creates urgency in the first screen and then the email spends the next six screens undermining it with unrelated, non-time-bound modules.
- Net: a strong hero wrapped in the same tired body. Worth opening for the countdown alone, middling once you scroll.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") is a genuinely differentiated urgency signal not seen in recent DICK'S sends.
- Hero "FLASH SALE" wordmark renders fully on-canvas this time (unlike the 08/12 morning send where it was cropped).
- Single, unambiguous primary CTA ("SHOP NOW") directly under the offer, no scrolling required.
- Clear "Starts Now" badge plus concrete "Up to 50% Off" copy — no vague language.
- No render bugs: images load, buttons are legible, text doesn't overlap.

## What's weak

- The countdown urgency is abandoned after the hero — "Epic Shoe Sale" and "This Week's Deals" have no timer or date urgency tied to the flash sale.
- "This Week's Deals" beach-ball banner is a near-identical reuse of the 08/11 "UP TO 50% OFF" creative — same pool imagery, same blue badge.
- Six-image product grid at the bottom ("Check out these picks") is generic and untargeted — no evidence of personalization to a specific shopper.
- Category chip nav (MEN'S/WOMEN'S/KIDS'/SHOES/etc.) duplicates the homepage rather than reinforcing the flash-sale theme.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email after the flash-sale hero and one supporting module — the current six-screen sprawl dilutes the countdown's urgency. If Epic Shoe Sale and This Week's Deals must stay, put an expiration date/time on each so they don't read as evergreen filler next to a literal countdown clock.
- 2. Replace the reused "This Week's Deals" beach-ball banner — it's the third appearance of pool/beach imagery in four days and reads as recycled, not fresh.
- 3. Swap the generic six-item product grid for the actual flash-sale discount categories, so the picks reinforce the 50%-off message instead of looking like a random assortment.
- 4. Repeat the countdown timer (or at least "Ends tonight") near the bottom SHOP NOW buttons to keep urgency alive through the scroll.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Flash Sale ends tonight — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Countdown's on: shop shoes, clothing & gear before midnight`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Up to 50% off select styles — while supplies last`

## Technical Audit

1. Technical Summary
Marketing/tracking links function, but the footer Unsubscribe link routes to a broken error page, and standard email authentication/compliance headers are absent from the delivered message.

2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All probed links use Epsilon/Harmony tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` and `/O/...`), consistent redirect pattern, no malformed tracking params observed in probed set.
- 12 of 53 total links were probed (41 skipped per click-likelihood ranking); unprobed links were not verified and could contain additional errors not captured here.

3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 6 images missing `alt` text: tracking pixel (`bf66eeff...c5a42963aa1`), `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`. The tracking pixel's missing alt is low-impact (intentionally hidden, `display:none`), but the four footer social/value-prop icons and the location icon will be unreadable to screen reader users.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments and mobile media queries are present and well-formed; no malformed HTML tags observed in the provided source.
- Custom web font (Archivo) is loaded via `@font-face` inside `@media screen`, with `font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif!important` fallback on `body` — acceptable fallback chain for clients that block remote fonts.

4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- No unresolved merge tokens (e.g. `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, `[[...]]`) found in the visible source.
- `application/ld+json` block contains empty placeholder fields (`"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""` under `PromotionCard`) and unresolved template comments (`// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`, `// Sample image is 538x138...`) — this is authoring boilerplate that was not stripped/populated before send, and the comments are invalid JSON (the block as shown would fail `JSON.parse`).

5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Broken unsubscribe (FAIL):** The footer "Unsubscribe" link (`https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019ff7a48c2ba4ea3f6e965fd798/bf66eeff4ed04e350000021ef3a0bcf6`) resolves to an error page: `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'`. This is a functional CAN-SPAM compliance defect — the required functioning opt-out mechanism is not working.
- **List-Unsubscribe header missing (WARN):** No `List-Unsubscribe` header captured; may be a relay-side capture limitation rather than absence at origin — flagged as unconfirmed.
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header missing (WARN):** No RFC 8058 one-click unsubscribe header captured, same caveat as above.
- **Authentication-Results header missing (WARN):** SPF/DKIM/DMARC status could not be verified via the relay; not confirmed as a sender-side failure.

6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- All CTA/logo links route through Epsilon click-tracking redirects rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs, so the final landing-page URL and UTM parameters can't be verified from the HTML source alone (parameters are opaque, e.g. `?__dU__=v0G4RBKTXg2Gs8NEX0-RpwlL2_Q96sIof5wvgkGa1VsPIAdb-6PwUuXUbOES4DYwVKlEBH4wAjEqvhvBlpUfxLCgwho1v8uViS`).
- Given the confirmed broken Unsubscribe redirect, continuity of the tracking-redirect layer to `/pc/subscription` is demonstrably broken for at least that one endpoint; other tracked links were not confirmed to land on matching content (out of scope for the automated probe results provided).

7. Recommendations
- Fix the Unsubscribe redirect immediately — it currently 404s to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` instead of the subscription-management page. This is the highest-priority item (compliance-critical).
- Add `alt` attributes to the 4 footer icon images (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`) and `icon-location-2025.png` for screen-reader accessibility.
- Strip the empty/placeholder `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` fields and inline `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block, or populate them — as shipped, the block is not valid JSON.
- Confirm at the sending-server level (outside this HTML/QA snapshot) that `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC (Authentication-Results) are actually present, since relay capture gaps could be masking a real header omission.
