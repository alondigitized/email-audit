# Don't miss up to 50% off the Flash SALE! ⏳
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-07**
## Executive summary

- This is the second DICK'S email today — the morning send ("Flash Sale starts now!", 8/10) announced the event, this one applies the final-hours pressure with a countdown clock. The two-offer structure (50% off Flash Sale stacked on Extra 30% off Clearance) is the clearest layout the brand has assembled in weeks, and the countdown timer at the top is the right tactical lever for a closing-window message. But the hero creative — a large orange area with a lightning bolt texture and no product photography — is either a broken image or a design non-decision; either way it wastes the prime real estate directly below the urgency timer.
- The cadence is the deeper problem. Two sends in one day capping nine near-identical clearance emails since May 30 means the most engaged subscribers are conditioned to tune out anything that looks red-and-orange from DICK'S. Even a well-executed send fights that accumulated fatigue.
- **Judgement:** Solid structure, poor creative in the hero, and a cadence that's eroding whatever urgency the countdown is trying to generate.

## What's working

- **Countdown timer "Final Hours: 07:59:55"** is the first thing the eye hits below the header — it creates real time pressure the clearance beat never had.
- **"Ends Tonight" / "Today Only" labels** bracket both offers cleanly and aren't redundant with each other.
- **Dual-offer separation** — Flash Sale 50% off and Extra 30% off Clearance occupy distinct visual blocks; they don't blur together.
- **Product row** — HOKA Clifton 10, Jordan 5 Football Cleats, YETI Camino Tote, Crocs Kids' — gives specific SKU-level entry points rather than forcing a homepage browse.

## What's weak

- **Hero image is blank** — the large orange lightning-bolt block has no product photo, no headline overlay, no model. Looks like a failed image asset or a placeholder that shipped.
- **"SHOP NOW" is generic** across both main offers; "SHOE DEALS" on the clearance block is stronger — that specificity belongs on the primary Flash Sale CTA too.
- **Two sends in one day** — a subscriber who opened the morning Flash Sale email is now getting a near-duplicate; this is the cadence pattern most likely to trigger unsubscribes.
- **Summer Shop / Americana Gear section dilutes urgency** — a lifestyle pickleball photo and Americana copy in a final-hours email breaks the scarcity frame right when it should be tightening.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Fix or replace the hero creative** — if the image asset is broken, ship a fallback product shot now. If the lightning-bolt orange is intentional, overlay a bold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero type or a product silhouette. A countdown timer feeding into a blank orange box is a broken funnel.
- 2. **Sharpen every CTA** — "SHOP FLASH SALE" > "SHOP NOW". "SHOP SALE SHOES" > the secondary button. Give each button a destination that matches its section.
- 3. **Cut the Summer Shop and Americana Gear blocks** — save them for a standalone summer-kick send. In a final-hours urgency email they undercut the time pressure and confuse the message.
- 4. **One send per day on flash events** — the morning announcement (this morning's 8/10 send) is sufficient; the afternoon follow-up should be reserved for a re-send to non-openers only, not the full list.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Final hours: 50% off Flash Sale ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏳ Flash Sale closes at midnight — up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Extra 30% off clearance + free same-day pickup on orders today`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `HOKA, Jordan, YETI — all marked down. Offer ends tonight.`

## Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods Flash Sale Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

Table-based layout with MSO conditional comments and Epsilon/Harmony ESP infrastructure. Three confirmed issues in the JSON-LD block will silently break Gmail structured data features; remaining markup is structurally sound from what is visible in the truncated source.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-tracking:** All links route through `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019ea3b7aef1ba4fa9369ff04b6c/...` (Epsilon redirect). Redirect chain is present and expected for this ESP.

**Open-tracking pixel:** Uses single-quoted `src` attribute — `src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...'` — inconsistent with the rest of the HTML (double-quoted). Technically valid HTML5 but parser-fragile in legacy clients.

**UTM parameters:** Not visible in the displayed redirect URLs. Since UTMs must survive the Epsilon redirect to land on the destination page, this needs server-side verification — cannot confirm from source alone.

**No broken links confirmed** in the visible portion of source.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Tracking pixel missing `alt` attribute:**
```html
<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/...' style="display:none; ..."/>
```
Should include `alt=""` to suppress screen-reader announcements and pass accessibility linters.

**Logo `<img>` truncated** — cannot verify `alt` text on the primary logo. Must confirm.

**Positive findings:**
- `lang="en"` on `<html>` — correct
- `role="presentation"` on all layout tables — correct
- MSO conditional comments present for Outlook compatibility
- Google Fonts loaded inside `@media screen` only — Outlook-safe

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Stale date — hardcoded 2020 timestamp:**
```json
"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"
```
This is a template default that was never replaced. It will render as a past offer in any client that surfaces structured data (e.g., Gmail Promotions tab).

**Empty required fields — unfilled placeholders:**
```json
"description": "",
"discountCode": "",
"availabilityEnds": "",
"image": ""
```
These are in the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema objects. Either fill or omit the keys entirely — empty strings in structured data are treated as invalid.

**Segment ID visible in DOM:**
```html
id="header-non-sc-member"
```
This implies a ScoreCard member variant exists. Confirm that both variants are deployed; a mis-routed segment would serve non-member content to members or vice versa.

### 7. Recommendations

| Priority | Issue | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **High** | JSON-LD contains JavaScript-style `//` comments — **invalid JSON**, will break Gmail Promotions tab parsing | Remove all `//` comment lines from the `<script type="application/ld+json">` block |
| **High** | `availabilityStarts` hardcoded to `2020-03-06` | Replace with current campaign start date via template variable |
| **High** | `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds`, `image` are empty strings | Fill with campaign values or remove the keys |
| **Medium** | Tracking pixel missing `alt=""` | Add `alt=""` to the open-tracking `<img>` |
| **Medium** | UTM pass-through unverified | Inspect final destination URL after redirect to confirm UTM params |
| **Low** | Single-quoted `src` on tracking pixel | Normalize to double quotes for consistency |
| **Verify** | Footer CAN-SPAM address and unsubscribe link | Confirm in full source (truncated in provided HTML) |

**Root cause of the JSON-LD failures:** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains three `//` comment lines. JSON does not support comments; this renders the entire block unparseable. Gmail will silently discard the structured data, losing any Promotions tab annotations.
