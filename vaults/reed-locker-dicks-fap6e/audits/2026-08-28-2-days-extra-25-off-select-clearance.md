# 2 DAYS 💰 Extra 25% OFF select clearance
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-28**
## Executive summary

- This one at least breaks the pattern slightly — a serene lake/dock scene behind the "EXTRA 25% OFF" red box instead of the identical green-locker backdrop DICK'S has been recycling since 8/16. It's a small visual reset, but the underlying mechanic hasn't changed: this is still a sitewide clearance percentage-off blast with a hard deadline ("Starts Now... Valid only through 8/29"), followed by the same kitchen-sink stack of category tiles (Back-to-School, Drinkware, Golf, Soccer, Bikes, Vests, This Week's Deals, product picks) that every recent send has used.
- The offer itself is clear and the CTA is unambiguous, but by the sixth-plus near-identical "up to X% off" clearance email this month, the fatigue is real — a subscriber who's seen 8/25, 8/26 (x2), 8/27 (x2) versions of this exact structure has little reason to open yet another one. The "2 DAYS" urgency in the subject is genuine (expires 8/29) which is a small step up from prior sends' vaguer "today only" claims.
- Judgment: solid execution, tired strategy — same score band as the rest of this week's cadence.

## What's working

- Hero uses a distinct lake/dock background instead of the repeated green-locker image, giving slight visual freshness.
- Subject's "2 DAYS" urgency is backed by real fine print ("Valid only through 8/29/26 11:59PM ET"), unlike some prior sends that claimed urgency without support.
- Primary CTA ("Shop Now") is large, green, and sits directly under the offer with no scroll needed.
- Clean render — no overlapping text, broken images, or layout bugs anywhere in the long scroll.
- Category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) give quick wayfinding right below the hero.

## What's weak

- The email is extremely long — nine+ stacked promo modules (Back-to-School, Drinkware, Golf, Soccer, Bikes/Vests, This Week's Deals, product picks) dilute the single "Extra 25% Off" hero message.
- "Up to 50% Off" locker-room green module reappears yet again, the same creative used repeatedly since 8/16 — this send layers it in as a secondary block on top of its own new hero.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard-specific pricing or member discount shown despite ScoreCard branding at the very top.
- Fine print ("Exclusions apply... prices may not reflect... Interim markdowns may have been taken") undercuts the clean 25% headline the moment you read past the hero.
- Cadence problem persists — this is at least the seventh clearance-style send this month with the same overall template.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email down to 2-3 modules max: hero offer + one complementary category, not nine. Long scroll dilutes the single clear offer this email is supposed to be about.
- 2. Drop or relocate the repeated "Up to 50% Off" green-locker block — it's directly competing with this email's own hero offer and it's the same asset run all month.
- 3. Add a ScoreCard member-specific perk (bonus points, early access) since the loyalty logo is featured at the top but never paid off with member-only value.
- 4. Simplify the fine print or move it fully below the fold — right now it sits close enough to the hero to undercut the "Extra 25% Off" claim.
