# Stock up on style! Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes is YOURS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-13**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competent DICK'S catch-all that lacks the punch of the week's earlier sends. The hero is a pink oversized crewneck beside a Nike sneaker, with "Up to 50% Off Select Clothing & Shoes" and a green Shop Now button. The offer and CTA are clear, but the headline is small and sits below the fold of a long image. The loud "UP TO 70% OFF" and countdown banners from Oct 10-12 had much more urgency.
- Below the hero the email splits into many competing offers: fan gear at 50% off, drinkware at 40% off, category tiles, and six product picks. That is useful for browsing but weakens focus. The subject says "Stock up on style," but the lead is one pink sweatshirt, so the hero doesn't fit the subject well. It also arrives on the same day as "This week's DEALS 👉 Up to 50% off" with the same 50% offer. A subscriber would see this as a near-duplicate.
- Overall it is a reasonable, honest send, but it is mid-pack and adds little over this morning's roundup.

## What's working

- The hero has a clear offer ("Up to 50% Off Select Clothing & Shoes") with a green Shop Now button directly under it.
- Brand names (Nike, adidas, Under Armour) in the hero copy add credibility and relevance.
- The pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, The Halloween Shop) lets people jump straight to their category.
- The ScoreCard strip, with the points balance and a "Show to Earn Points" link, reminds members of the loyalty benefits.
- The services section (Best Price Guarantee, texts, favorites, app download) is tidy, and the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." bar sets a useful delivery expectation up top.

## What's weak

- The hero headline is small and set under the image. The offer isn't the first thing the eye lands on, unlike the giant-number heroes in earlier sends.
- It has no deadline or urgency. "Select" and "Up to" make the discount vague.
- The email stacks too many secondary modules: fan gear, drinkware, two tile rows and six products. There is no single winner.
- The product picks have no prices, ratings or discount labels, so they don't show what the offer actually saves.
- The secondary Shop Now buttons are pale grey and look like disabled states next to the green hero button.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a big "UP TO 50% OFF" over the hero image, as in the Oct 11-12 sends, and cut the pink sweatshirt and sneaker collage to one hero product.
- 2. Add a real deadline (for example "ends Sunday") to restore the urgency the brand used earlier in the week.
- 3. Cut the lower modules to two or three, and show sale prices and "was" prices on the product picks.
- 4. Make every Shop Now button green so the CTAs look consistent.
- 5. Avoid sending this on the same day as another 50%-off roundup, or differentiate it by focusing on apparel.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Nike, adidas & Under Armour`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Fall apparel & shoes: up to 50% off, ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off drinkware and 50% off fan gear. Order today, pick up in an hour.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Fleece, sneakers and socks from Nike and adidas, marked down for a limited time.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a table-based layout with a hidden preheader block and ESP click-tracking on every link. QA flags a missing plain-text part (9 chars), 15 content images without alt text, and an unfilled secondary-subject-line placeholder. The header-based checks (List-Unsubscribe, Authentication-Results) could not be evaluated through the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links I could see are wrapped in the ESP redirector `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...`, for example the header logo anchor with `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. This is standard.
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 52. It reported no probe failures, so no broken links are confirmed.
- The footer, utility and social links were deprioritized and not probed. That coverage gap is a limit of the QA run, not a defect in the email.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=...`. It has no alt attribute (see section 3).

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (15 images + pixel).** QA reports no alt attribute on these hero and category images, all hosted on `s7d6.scene7.com`:
  - `2026-1013-dks-swd-4pm-hero-new`
  - `2026-1013-dks-swd-4pm-jersey`

  - `0831-dks-stw-6am-40-off-drink`
  - the icons `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1` and `icon-location-2025-1`
  
  With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the promo content is lost. Screen readers will announce the filenames instead. The icons and the tracking pixel should carry `alt=""` if they are decorative.
- **Plain-text part.** QA marks the plain-text fallback as a `[FAIL]`: "Text version is 9 chars". The relay may have stripped it, but as captured it is effectively absent. This hurts deliverability scoring and text-only clients.
- **Preheader.** The `#pht-spacer` div contains only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding, with no preheader text before it. The template comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in the `td#ssl` cell. This suggests the secondary subject line was never populated, so clients will fall back to body text. The source was truncated, so I'm confident about what the visible portion shows but not about the rest.
- **Layout.** All layout tables have `role="presentation"`, `<html lang="en">` is set, and the viewport meta is present. The Outlook conditional blocks look sound. No issues found beyond the above.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%` or `[FIRST_NAME]` appear in the visible source. The HTML was truncated, so the rest is unverified.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. QA notes the relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a proven defect. If the headers are really absent, Gmail and Yahoo's bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe are not met. They should be verified against the raw message at the sending side.
- `Authentication-Results` was not found, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`. Confirm alignment from the raw headers.
- QA states an unsubscribe link is always probed, but the result isn't listed. The physical postal address and footer unsubscribe link are outside the truncated source, so I can't confirm them.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Destination URLs and UTM parameters are hidden inside the opaque `params=` and `pid=` blobs, so I can't confirm UTM presence or landing-page alignment from this source.
- **Possible stale asset.** `0831-dks-stw-6am-40-off-drink?cb=1787693853597` looks like a creative from an earlier campaign (`0831`, "40-off"). The other images are `2026-1013-dks-swd-4pm-*`. The cache-buster value matches a late-August 2026 epoch timestamp. This email is about 50% off clothing and shoes, so confirm the drinkware tile isn't showing an outdated "40% off" offer and linking to a mismatched landing page.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all hero and category images, and `alt=""` to the icons and the open pixel.
2. Ship a real multipart `text/plain` alternative. If the relay is stripping it, verify at the source.
3. Populate the secondary subject line and preheader text, and remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` placeholder.
4. Confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` on the raw message, and check SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
5. Check the `0831-dks-stw-6am-40-off-drink` asset and its click destination against the 10/13 offer.
6. Re-run QA with an untruncated HTML capture. That would allow merge-token, footer and UTM checks, and a wider link-probe sample that includes the footer.
