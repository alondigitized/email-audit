# On now! $20 Off $100+ ONLY in the APP 📲
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-05-25**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S is now on what feels like its eighteenth-plus Memorial Day send in roughly a week, and this one is the *sixth* distinct creative built around the "Epic Memorial Day Sale" template I've audited since Friday. The hook this time is the recurring app-exclusive carrot — $20 off $100+ — bolted onto the standard collage hero, the standard "Up to 50% Off," the standard "Up to 20% Off," the standard golf/Nike/Eddie Bauer tiles. There is nothing new in this email except the subject's reframing of an offer subscribers have already seen twice this week.
- The render itself is competent: hero collage reads, the $20-off block is bold, the typography stack is consistent. But the email contradicts itself — the subject says "ONLY in the APP," yet the body shows generic "Get Offer" buttons, no app download CTA above the fold, no QR code, no "open in app" deep link. If the whole point is app-exclusivity, the creative doesn't lean into it.
- For a Walker-persona reader, the relevant tile (CALIA / women's apparel and shoes) is buried well below the fold under golf and men's Nike. Combined with brand fatigue, this is a low-effort iteration on a creative that's already been beaten to death this week.

## What's working

- The $20 off $100+ block is genuinely bold — red banner, clear math, hard to miss once you scroll.
- The hero collage reads instantly as Memorial Day without leaning on cliché flag imagery.
- "Up to 50% Off" and "Up to 20% Off" tiers stack cleanly with strong color contrast.
- A specific app-exclusive offer creates a real reason to open the app (if the subscriber bothers).

## What's weak

- Subject promises "ONLY in the APP" — body shows zero app-specific UX (no QR, no deep link, no download button above the fold).
- This is the third send this week with the same $20/$100 app offer; fatigue is real.
- Walker-persona relevant tiles (CALIA, women's apparel/shoes) sit well below golf and men's product.
- The 📲 emoji in the subject + the 🙌 / ☀️ pattern across this week's sends feels formulaic.
- "Get Offer" CTA copy is vague — same generic verb across every tile, no destination specificity.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Lead with the app, not the collage.** If the subject says "ONLY in the APP," the hero should be a phone mockup with the offer applied at checkout, plus a QR code or "Open in App" deep link as the primary CTA. Right now the email reads like a generic sale email with an app sticker slapped on top.
- 2. **Cut the redundant tier banners.** Show ONE offer hierarchy — the $20/$100 app exclusive is the lead. "Up to 50%" and "Up to 20%" tiers compete for attention and dilute the headline.
- 3. **Re-order category tiles for segmentation.** CALIA / women's running shoes belong above golf and men's Nike for this reader.
- 4. **Replace "Get Offer" with destination-specific verbs** — "Shop CALIA," "Shop Nike Running," "Open App."
- 5. **Slow the cadence.** Eighteen-plus sends in a week erodes inbox trust faster than any sale lifts revenue.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Open the app — your $20 off is waiting`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Tap to claim before Monday — scan the QR inside.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Only ScoreCard members in the app get this $20.`
