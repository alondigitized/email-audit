# The Epic Shoe Sale has landed with up to 30% off select shoes & cleats
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-09**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, single-focus DICK'S send — "Epic Shoe Sale" hero with a stack of Nike/adidas/HOKA shoes on a red locker background, up to 30% off, clear "SHOP NOW" CTA above the fold. It's a nice break from the sprawling weekly-digest format the brand ran on 8/7 and 8/8 ("Go, go, go!", flash sale countdowns) — this one leads with a genuine category focus (shoes & cleats) before falling into the familiar "explore more deals" grid further down.
- The hero itself is well-composed: brand logos (Nike, adidas, HOKA) visible, product clearly shown, bold "EPIC SHOE SALE" text stamped into the image. But it's still DICK'S — the offer is a generic "up to 30%" with "select styles" and "prices as marked," so anyone who's seen the brand's last five sends will recognize the same discount-vagueness pattern.
- Compared to 8/6's 9.00/10 pool-and-beachball send, this doesn't hit quite as hard because that one had a punchier single-minded "up to 50%" hero; this one settles into digest mode below the fold fast. Solid, on-brand, not a standout.

## What's working

- Hero art is sharp — recognizable Nike/adidas/HOKA logos and an actual shoe pile, not generic stock photography.
- Single dominant CTA ("SHOP NOW") sits right under the offer with no scroll needed on most screens.
- "Valid through 8/15/26 11:59PM PT" gives a credible, specific deadline.
- ScoreCard loyalty banner ("300 Points = $10 Reward") is visible at the very top for members.
- No render bugs — clean grid, images loaded, text legible throughout.

## What's weak

- "Up to 30% Off" with "Select styles. Prices as marked" is the same vague-discount pattern the brand always uses — no anchor price or example savings.
- The email pivots to a generic multi-category digest (golf, kayaks, pickleball, drinkware, fan shop, treadmills) almost immediately after the hero, diluting the "shoe sale" focus the subject promised.
- No social proof or reviews anywhere in the shoe section despite footwear being a fit-sensitive purchase.
- The "Check out these picks" product grid at the bottom has no pricing shown on any of the six shoe/apparel items.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the non-shoe categories (golf, kayak, pickleball, drinkware, fitness) from this send entirely, or move them below a hard "Shop the Shoe Sale by Category" second module — right now the digest sprawl undercuts the subject's shoe-specific promise.
- 2. Add a concrete example discount next to "Up to 30%" (e.g., "Nike Pegasus now $84") to make the offer feel real instead of a ceiling number nobody hits.
- 3. Show prices on the bottom "Check out these picks" shoe tiles — a shoe-focused email with unpriced shoe tiles is a missed click.
- 4. Add a review/rating snippet (e.g., "4.8★ (2,300)") under at least the hero shoes to build purchase confidence for a fit-dependent category.
