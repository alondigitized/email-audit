# Discover up to 50% OFF our best DEALS of the week!
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-08**
## Executive summary

- This is a genuinely different creative from the maroon stadium-seating loop DICK'S has run daily since 8/30 — the hero here is a green-water-bottles/treadmill lifestyle shot with a small "UP TO 50% OFF" red-block overlay, not the recycled banner. That's a real point in its favor after a week of near-identical sends. But the fundamentals are the same as every other send this cycle: generic "Best Deals of the Week" framing, a wall of category tiles (Men's/Women's/Kids' clothing and shoes, Team Sports, Stanley cups, clearance) with no single hero offer, and a subject line ("Discover up to 50% OFF our best DEALS of the week!") that's nearly interchangeable with 9/6's "These deals are EPIC!" and 9/8's earlier "Delivered: Up to 50% off" send.
- The email is clean and well-organized — no render bugs, consistent product photography, clear CTAs on every module — but it reads as a full-catalog scroll rather than a targeted pitch. There's no name I can identify with (no persona focus area called out), no loyalty/ScoreCard personalization beyond the generic points banner at top, and the offer ("up to 50%") is the same vague ceiling DICK'S has used all week, with an "up to 60%" clearance banner further down competing with the hero's "50%" instead of reinforcing it.
- Judgement: solid production quality, weak targeting and differentiation — a middle-of-the-pack DICK'S send that avoids the worst repetition sins but doesn't do anything to earn a click over yesterday's near-identical offer.

## What's working

- New hero art (green bottles + treadmill lifestyle shot) breaks the maroon-stadium-seating streak that's run since 8/30.
- Clear, repeated "Shop Now" CTA buttons on every module — no ambiguity about what to click.
- Category navigation pills (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Fan Shop/Team Sports) right under the hero give quick self-segmentation.
- Product photography is clean and consistent across all tiles, no broken images or layout breaks.
- Footer includes real utility (app download, store locator, loyalty sign-up, favorites list) rather than just legal boilerplate.

## What's weak

- The hero offer is generic "Best Deals of the Week" — no specific product, category, or reason to open this email over yesterday's.
- Two competing discount ceilings in one email (hero "50% OFF" vs. later "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES") dilutes both.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard personalization beyond a static "$0 Points / $10 Reward" banner at the very top — doesn't reflect the recipient's actual point balance or history.
- Email is extremely long (11+ distinct modules) before reaching anything resembling urgency or scarcity — no countdown, no "ends tonight" framing like 9/7's send used effectively.
- Subject line is functionally identical in structure to at least three other sends this week ("EPIC," "Score up to 50%," "Discover up to 50%") — brand is running out of new ways to say the same thing.

## Recommendations

- 1. Pick one hero offer and commit — either lead with the new bottles/treadmill creative and a specific category discount, or lead with the 60% clearance (the bigger number), not both diluting each other in the same scroll.
- 2. Add real urgency: 9/7's "ends tonight" + countdown timer outperformed this cycle's vaguer sends — reuse that mechanic here instead of open-ended "this week."
- 3. Personalize the top banner with actual ScoreCard point balance/reward status instead of a static "$0 Points" placeholder — that's a missed loyalty moment for members.
- 4. Trim the module count — 6+ full-width sections before clearance is a lot of scrolling for a "deals of the week" email; consolidate categories into fewer, higher-impact blocks.
- 5. Vary subject line structure, not just verbs — stop rotating "EPIC / Discover / Score" against the same "up to 50% off" payload; lead with a specific category or dollar-off next time.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Stanley 1913 Fall drop + up to 50% off shoes`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Your ScoreCard deals: up to 50% off ends soon`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New fall Stanley colors just landed — plus deals across every category.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Free shipping on team sports gear, no minimum this week.`
