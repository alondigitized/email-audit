# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-08-13
**Score:** 8/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-08-13**
## Executive summary

- This reads like a straight-up sporting goods retailer, not trying to be anything fancier — logo, search bar, a row of shoe brands, and a shoe sale banner. Right now they're pushing an "Epic Shoe Sale" up to 30% off plus a ScoreCard+ membership pitch at the very top. Nothing here screams "we know you've got three kids in travel ball," but it's not trying to sell me yoga pants either — it's in the right neighborhood.

## What's working

- **The ScoreCard+ banner up top** — "$350 in Member Benefits for $99/Year" — I'm already in this program for the birthday coupons and points, so a clear callout like that catches my eye immediately.
- **The quick-tag pills under the search bar** — "Cleats" and "Football Gear" sitting right there is exactly my lane with three kids in travel sports. One tap and I'm where I need to be.
- **"Back to School" tag** — with three kids, that's real and timely for mid-August.
- **Top Shoes carousel** — brand names right up front (Nike, adidas, HOKA, New Balance) means I don't have to dig to see what's in stock before a store run.

## What's weak

- **The hero banner is just "shoe sale," not "gear up for the season."** Nothing there nods to cleats, team gear, or getting a kid ready for fall ball — it's a generic sneaker push.
- **No sizing or fit info visible** — for a dad who's replacing cleats every season for growing feet, that's the first thing I'd want a hint of.
- **No reviews or star ratings anywhere on the shoes shown** — I like to know what other parents think before I trust a cleat for my kid's ankle.
- **That floating "GET $20 OFF" badge is stacked right on top of the sale banner** — feels a little cluttered, like two offers competing for the same eyeball space.

## Recommendations

- Swap the generic "Epic Shoe Sale" hero for a lifestyle shot — a kid in cleats on a turf field, or a parent loading up gear bags — something that says "travel sports season."
- Add a "Cleats & Team Gear" rail right under Top Shoes, same treatment as the sneaker carousel, so it's not buried behind a small tag pill.
- Put a star-rating snippet on the shoe tiles in that Top Shoes strip — even just "4.6★ (1.2k)" builds trust before I click.
- Move or shrink the "$20 off" floating badge so it's not crowding the main sale banner — pick one offer to lead with.
