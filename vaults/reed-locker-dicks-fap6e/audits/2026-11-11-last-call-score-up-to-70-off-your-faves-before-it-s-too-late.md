# LAST CALL! Score up to 70% off your faves before it's too late ➡️
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-11**
## Executive summary

- This is the third DICK'S send today (alongside the "EXTRA 25% off" and "11.11 Sale is HERE" emails), and it recycles the broken hero from the second one. The top banner is a dark clock graphic with glowing green "H E". It looks like the animated "HERE" frozen on its first frame, the same bug as the earlier 11.11 send. The subject says LAST CALL, but the hero says nothing about urgency or the offer. The "Up to 70% Off Your Favorite Brands" headline only appears below the image.
- The body is decent but crowded. There's a Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 at $124.98, drinkware up to 40% off, a four-up grid (CALIA, VRST, Walter Hagen, DSG coolers), and a gift-picks grid. Prices and percentages are concrete and the ScoreCard and price-guarantee blocks are useful. Too many offers compete, though: up to 70%, up to 40%, 50% off coolers, and a full-width red "EXTRA 25% OFF" block lifted from this morning's send.
- The "Final Hours 07:59:48" bar reads about 8 hours left. That is the same value as the 11/9 and 11/10 sends, so the timer appears to start from the same number every time. It undercuts "LAST CALL". Verdict: the offer is real and the layout is competent, but the hero is a miss and the urgency is inconsistent. Fine for a loyal sports shopper, forgettable for everyone else.

## What's working

- "Up to 70% Off Your Favorite Brands" is a clear, concrete headline with a single red Shop Now button directly under it.
- The black "Final Hours" countdown bar gives the send a time frame.
- Specific product pricing (Brooks $124.98, CALIA $49.98, Walter Hagen $59.98) gives people a reason to click.
- Brand chips (CALIA, VRST, DSG, Maxfli) let a shopper jump straight to a brand.
- ScoreCard, the Best Price Guarantee and the app download block are visible, which supports the loyalty angle.

## What's weak

- The hero shows only "H E" on a clock. It's an unfinished animation frame and tells you nothing about the sale.
- The offer sits below the hero image, so the eye lands on the clock before the 70%.
- The countdown reads 07:59:48, matching the 11/9 and 11/10 sends, which contradicts "LAST CALL" in the subject.
- The "EXTRA 25% OFF" red block repeats this morning's send and competes with the 70% headline.
- It is the third DICK'S email today. The brand has sent near-identical sale emails all week, so fatigue is likely.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the hero with a static, offer-first image: "UP TO 70% OFF, ENDS TONIGHT" plus the Shop Now button. Never ship an animated hero without a static fallback.
- 2. Set the countdown to the real deadline (11:59 PM PT) so it matches the "LAST CALL" claim.
- 3. Remove the 25% block or fold it into the 70% story so there is only one primary offer.
- 4. Cut the subject to 50 characters or fewer and drop the arrow emoji.
- 5. Make CTAs specific, such as "Shop Brooks Running" instead of repeating "Shop Now".
- 6. Cap the sends per day, or suppress this one for people who clicked this morning.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 70% off DSG, CALIA & VRST`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: Brooks $124.98 + up to 70% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `One-day 11.11 sale ends at 11:59 PM PT. Shop running, golf, and gifts.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Brooks, CALIA, VRST and more. Free pickup in one hour.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The one confirmed hard failure is a missing plain-text alternative: the QA check found a 9-character text part. The HTML also has 13 images with no `alt` attribute. Header-based compliance and authentication checks could not be confirmed, because the AgentMail relay appears to strip those headers.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links found.** 12 material links were probed and none were reported as failing. 54 links were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized), so those are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel is the first element in `<body>`.** It is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&tuid=6af38f78bb89de1a3011b13f&configId=512ffb51-...`, at `width="1" height="1"`. It has no `alt` attribute (flagged by QA) and no `style="display:block"` or border reset, so it is not guaranteed to be hidden. It also sits outside any table, which can add a stray line box in some clients.
- **Image URLs carry `cb=` cache-buster timestamps** (for example `cb=1793886865223`). This is not an error. The values differ across assets, so the images are served from separate publish events.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **13 images are missing `alt` attributes** (QA WARN). Affected sources:
  - Content images: `running`, `1106fruitpunchsecondary-1`, `calia-1`, `vrst-1`, `wh`, `dsg-3`, `2026-1012-dks-swd-gift-guide-banner`.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The tracking pixel.
- **Consequences for the content images:** with images blocked, or in a screen reader, a missing `alt` means the sale content is unavailable or is announced as the filename or URL. The icons and the pixel should carry `alt=""` explicitly.
- **Plain-text part is effectively empty.** The QA check reports "Text version is 9 chars" [FAIL]. This hurts accessibility and spam scoring. It may be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, so verify against the raw MIME.
- **Minor:** `<link rel="icon" ...favicon.ico>` in `<head>` is ignored by email clients and can be removed.
- **Checked and fine:**
  - `lang="en"` is set.
  - The viewport meta tag is present.
  - Layout tables use `role="presentation"`.
  - MSO conditional comments are present.
  - Fonts have an Arial fallback.
  - The hidden preheader spacer (`#pht-spacer`) uses `display:none; mso-hide:all`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved merge tokens appear in the visible source. The pixel's `tuid` and `pid` parameters are populated. The source is truncated, so the footer and later sections were not inspected.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found** [WARN]. The QA note says AgentMail may not capture these headers, so this is unconfirmed. If the headers are truly absent, Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender requirements are not met.
- **`Authentication-Results` was not found** [WARN]. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` is unverified.
- **Footer unsubscribe and physical address** are not visible in the truncated source. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown for it.
- **The From domain, `em.dcsg.com`, matches the tracking domain `data.em.dcsg.com`.** This is consistent for alignment.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The truncated source and QA output expose no destination URLs, UTM parameters or landing-page status codes. No issues are confirmed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Plain-text part:** inspect the raw MIME. If the part is truly 9 characters, generate a real text alternative with the offer copy and key links.
2. **Alt attributes:** add descriptive `alt` text to the seven content images. Add `alt=""` to the five icon images and the tracking pixel.
3. **Pixel markup:** add `alt=""` and `style="display:block;border:0"`, and move the pixel inside the main wrapper table.
4. **Unsubscribe headers:** confirm `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click` in the raw headers. Capture them from the original message rather than the relay copy.
5. **Authentication:** verify SPF, DKIM and DMARC from the receiving mailbox's original headers.
6. **Coverage:** raise the link-probe limit, or re-run on the full HTML, to cover the 54 skipped links and check UTM consistency.
