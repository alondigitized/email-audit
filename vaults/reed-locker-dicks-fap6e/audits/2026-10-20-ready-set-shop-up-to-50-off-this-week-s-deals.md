# Ready... set... shop up to 50% off this week's DEALS! 🏃 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-20**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, category-organized weekly deals email with a loud "Up to 50% OFF" hero over fall leaves and a green Shop Now button. The eye lands on the number first. The rest is a stack of "Up to 40% / 50% Off" category cards (men's, women's, kids', fishing, team sports) that works as a catalog but not as a pitch.
- The offer message is muddled. The hero says 50%, the cards say 40% or 50%, and the clearance block near the bottom says 70%. That block is a repeat of this morning's "New markdowns added 🤝 Up to 70% OFF select clearance" send, with the same fall-leaf creative. A subscriber who got both today sees the same offer twice, and this send gives no reason to pick it over the earlier one. It follows the pattern of the 10/17–10/18 sends, where the same hero was reused with a new subject line.
- Overall it's a competent send with no render bugs. It has no single hero product and no real deadline, and it adds to a heavy cadence, so I'd call it a solid 6–7 that doesn't stand out.

## What's working

- The "Up to 50% OFF" hero is oversized, high-contrast and the first thing the eye lands on.
- The green Shop Now button directly under the hero is unambiguous.
- Category pills ("Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports") give quick navigation.
- The "Check out these picks" product row (adidas Samba OG, Nike fleece joggers) gives some product-level specificity.
- The top bar's pickup, same-day delivery and fast-shipping line and the app-download block add practical value, and nothing renders broken.

## What's weak

- Discount depth is inconsistent: 50% in the hero, 40% on most cards, 50% on fishing and team sports, 70% on clearance. The hero number overstates what most cards deliver.
- The 70% clearance block repeats this morning's markdowns send almost word for word.
- Every card uses a generic grey "Shop Now" button with no category-specific verb, and the hero's "This Week's Deals" headline says little.
- Urgency is weak. "Valid online only for a limited time" gives no date or countdown, so the "Ready... set..." framing in the subject isn't backed up.
- The email is very long, with five category cards plus a clearance block plus a picks row, so the offer gets diluted.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with one strong, specific hero, such as a named product at a named price (e.g. Samba OG), instead of a generic "Up to 50% Off". Make the other offers secondary.
- 2. Add a real deadline to the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" with a visible date, and use it in the subject.
- 3. Make the CTAs specific: "Shop Men's Deals", "Shop Fishing Gear" and so on, not repeated "Shop Now".
- 4. Cut the clearance block, or merge it with the morning markdowns send so subscribers don't get the same offer twice in one day.
- 5. Move the top-of-email promise (pickup, same-day delivery) lower, or tie it to a real offer, so the header doesn't dilute the preview text.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The markup is mostly sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, a viewport meta tag, and MSO conditionals. The confirmed defects are a plain-text part of only 9 characters, widespread missing alt attributes, and an apparently unpopulated preheader slot. The HTML provided is truncated, so link targets, footer content and body-level tokens could not be inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links were reported. The probe covered 12 high-priority links, and the QA output lists no probe failures. The other 54 links (footer, utility, social) were skipped, so their status is unverified.
- Links are wrapped in an ESP redirect layer: `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The truncated source doesn't show the destination URLs, so I can't confirm whether the redirects resolve correctly.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&configId=512ffb51-...`, at 1×1 and placed first in `<body>`. This is normal. The URL is long and carries `tuid` and `pid` tokens, which is expected for this ESP.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Plain-text part (FAIL):** the plain-text part is 9 characters. Clients and spam filters that read the text/plain part see effectively empty content. Accessibility tools and text-only clients get nothing.
- **Missing alt text, 11 images (WARN):**
  - **Content images:** `clothing-2026-1020-swd-4pm`, `womens-clothing-…`, `kids-clothing-…`, `fishing-gear-…` and `team-2026-1020-swd-4pm`, all on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`. These are likely the main promo tiles, so with images blocked or a screen reader the deal content is lost.
  - **Footer icons:** `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - **Tracking pixel:** the `data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open` pixel is flagged too. This is low severity and should use `alt=""`.
- **Preheader (inferred from source):** the `<td id="ssl">` contains the placeholder comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->`. The only content after it is `div#pht-spacer`, which holds nothing but `&zwnj;&nbsp;` padding. No preview text appears to be set, so inbox previews will fall back to the first visible text or the header content.
- **Leftover editor artifacts:** `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">` appears in production markup. It is harmless but unclean.
- **Image size:** the scene7 requests use `wid=1200&hei=1600` inside a 600px max-width container. I can't confirm the rendered dimensions from the truncated source, so payload weight is unverified.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found in the visible source: no unresolved `{{ }}`, `%%`, or `[[ ]]` tokens. The remainder of the HTML was truncated and not checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) were not found. The QA tool itself says the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is **unconfirmed** rather than a verified defect. Check the raw message at the source.
- `Authentication-Results` was not captured, so SPF/DKIM/DMARC status is unknown. The sender domain is `em.dcsg.com`. Alignment can't be assessed from the data provided.
- The in-body unsubscribe link and the physical postal address are not visible in the truncated HTML, so I can't confirm them. The QA tool says an unsubscribe link is always included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not assessable. The destination URLs and UTM parameters are hidden behind the redirect wrapper and the truncation. The only visible campaign identifier is the `-2026-1020-swd-4pm` suffix on the image filenames, which suggests a 10/20 weekly-deals campaign. No landing-page mismatches were reported by the probes.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a proper plain-text part** with the offers, links and unsubscribe URL. This is the only hard FAIL.
2. **Add descriptive `alt` text to the five promo images** (for example, "Up to 50% off clothing"), and `alt=""` to the tracking pixel and the decorative footer icons.
3. **Populate the `#ssl` preheader slot** with real preview text, and remove the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment.
4. **Verify the headers on the raw message, not the relay copy:** `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com`.
5. **Rerun the audit on the full, untruncated HTML** to check the footer (unsubscribe link, postal address), resolved redirect targets and UTM parameters, and token hygiene.
6. **Strip editor artifacts** (`is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`) from production templates. This is low priority.
