# Up to 70% off?! Yes! Save on select clearance styles now 😎
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-07-05**
## Executive summary

- The day after DICK'S told subscribers "Ends Tonight" on the 4th of July 50% off sale, they come back July 5th with "Up to 70% off." That sequencing is a problem. The escalation from 50% to 70% should feel like a gift, but the false close yesterday erodes the credibility of any urgency claim today — subscribers who felt the deadline was real will notice. That is this email's original sin, and no amount of good execution fixes it.
- The execution itself is competent. The hero is clean, the 70% OFF typography dominates, and the multi-category product grid below renders without bugs. The "DICK'S | Clearance" sender segmentation is a nice touch — it signals this list has been cut. But the content inside is the same broad-spectrum clearance blast: shoes, clothing, golf bags, cleats, power tools (RYOBI), fishing gear. The breadth undercuts the segmentation signal.
- This is a workhorse clearance email that will move volume on name recognition alone. It is not a particularly well-targeted send for any individual recipient, and the cadence — roughly the fifteenth promotional email since June 26 — is deep into subscriber fatigue territory.

## What's working

- **70% OFF hero lands hard.** The forest-green background with oversized white "UP TO 70% OFF" numerals is the strongest visual this brand has shipped in the current run — cleaner and more confident than the 4th of July patriotic creative.
- **Sender segmentation is visible.** "DICK'S | Clearance" in the from-name signals to inbox skimmers that this is category-specific, not just another mass blast.
- **Multi-category grid is clearly organized.** Each product block shows the discount percentage, a product image, and a CTA — easy to scan for what's relevant.
- **"Select" qualifier is visible in the hero.** Honest about limitations up front; not burying the asterisk.
- **Clean render.** No broken images, overlapping text, or layout gaps visible in the screenshot.

## What's weak

- **The "Ends Tonight" problem.** Yesterday's send explicitly closed the sale. A new 70% off clearance email 24 hours later makes the urgency from both emails feel manufactured.
- **No deadline anywhere in the email.** If 70% off is the new high watermark, give it an expiration. The offer floats with no anchor.
- **"Explore more deals" CTA is generic.** Verb + noun but no specificity — it functions as a link to the homepage, not a pull toward a particular category.
- **RYOBI power tools in a sporting goods clearance send.** Fine for a universal clearance email, but odd for a recipient segmented into a "Clearance" sporting goods list. Creates category confusion.
- **Fifteenth-plus email in ten days.** The cadence is now a structural problem; individual email quality matters less when subscribers are trained to tune out.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the rhetorical opener, add a deadline.** "Up to 70% Off Summer Clearance — Ends Sunday" is more trustworthy and more actionable than "Up to 70% off?! Yes!" A real date restores the urgency that yesterday's false close burned.
- 2. **Acknowledge the sale continuation.** One line of copy — "Clearance just got deeper" or "We extended it" — addresses the elephant in the room and turns a credibility problem into a subscriber reward moment.
- 3. **Tighten the CTA copy.** Replace "Explore more deals" with something category-specific: "Shop Clearance Footwear," "Browse All Clearance," or "See What's New to 70% Off."
- 4. **Drop RYOBI from this list segment.** Power tools belong in a hardware-interest segment, not a sporting goods clearance list. It dilutes the offer story.
- 5. **Throttle cadence.** At fifteen emails in ten days, even good creative underperforms. Consider batching to one send per day max.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance just got deeper: up to 70% off, ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `70% off summer clearance — new styles added`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, clothing, cleats and more — prices dropped again.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `The sale didn't end — it got better. Shop 70% off now.`

## Technical Audit: DICK'S Sporting Goods Clearance Email

---

### 1. Technical Summary

The email uses a standard SFMC (Salesforce Marketing Cloud) send infrastructure with solid MSO/Outlook conditional comment patterns and responsive CSS. The primary technical failure is an invalid JSON-LD block that will silently break all Gmail Promotions tab annotations.

---

### 2. Link & Tracking Issues

**Click-tracking redirect domain:** `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/v610000019f31e29cd2c50813369ff0aab4/...`
This is SFMC's standard tracked-URL pattern. No bare/broken links visible in the truncated source.

**Open-tracking pixel:**
```html
<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v610000019f31e29cd2c50813369ff0aab4/1b87fbce31a2475100004c5a42963aa1'
     style="display:none; max-height: 0px; font-size: 0px; overflow: hidden; mso-hide: all"/>
```
Pixel is missing `alt=""` — minor, but some validators flag this.

**`__dU__` parameter** is present in click URLs. This appears to be a per-subscriber identifier token appended at send time; confirm it is being populated and not landing as a literal string for any recipient segment.

---

### 3. Rendering & Accessibility

**Logo `<img>` tag is missing a visible `alt` attribute** in the provided source:
```html
<img id="logo" src="https://s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/brand-logo--dks-2?wid=248&hei=…
```
The source is truncated at `&hei=…` — verify the full tag has `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"`. If absent, the logo renders as a broken image in image-off environments.

**Table structure:** `role="presentation"` is correctly applied to layout tables. `<html lang="en">` is present. `charset="UTF-8"` declared in `<meta>`.

**Outer wrapper `width="600"` HTML attribute vs. CSS `width:100%; max-width:600px`:** This is a standard email pattern and will not cause issues — the HTML attribute acts as a fallback for Outlook, CSS takes over elsewhere.

**Font loading:** Archivo is loaded via `@media screen { @font-face { ... } }` scoped to `fonts.gstatic.com`. Version `v7` of the font is referenced — current hosted version is higher, but woff2 file will continue to serve. Falls back to Arial correctly.

---

### 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

**Critical: JSON-LD block contains JavaScript-style `//` comments, making it invalid JSON:**

```json
{ // GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out.
  // Sample image is 538x138, 3.9 aspect ratio
  "@context": "http://schema.org/",
  "@type": "PromotionCard",
  // DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!
  "image": "" }
```

`//` comments are not valid JSON. Every major JSON parser (including Gmail's annotation parser) will reject the entire `<script type="application/ld+json">` block, silently nullifying all three schema objects.

**Consequences:**
- Gmail Promotions tab: no deal badge, no promo card image, no discount code annotation
- `DiscountOffer` and `Organization` schema objects also fail even though they have no comments — one invalid JSON structure poisons the entire array

**Additional stale/empty merge fields in JSON-LD** (would still be broken even after fixing comments):

| Field | Value | Issue |
|---|---|---|
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityStarts` | `"2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"` | Hardcoded 2020 date — template default never updated |
| `DiscountOffer.availabilityEnds` | `""` | Empty string |
| `DiscountOffer.description` | `""` | Empty string |
| `DiscountOffer.discountCode` | `""` | Empty string |
| `PromotionCard.image` | `""` | Empty string — promo card won't render |

### 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, Unsubscribe, Authentication)

The provided HTML is truncated before the footer — the following cannot be confirmed from the source excerpt and must be verified in the full template:
- Physical mailing address (CAN-SPAM §5(a)(5))
- Unsubscribe mechanism with ≤10-business-day honor window
- Clear identification of the message as an advertisement (if not an existing customer relationship)

**Sending domain:** `DSG@e.dcsg.com` — `e.dcsg.com` is the ESP subdomain. Confirm SPF, DKIM, and DMARC alignment for this subdomain in DNS. Standard practice for SFMC is DKIM signed under `e.dcsg.com` with a CNAME pointing to SFMC's signing infrastructure; verify this is active and not expired.

---

### 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM / Landing Page Alignment)

All click URLs pass through `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` redirect, which obscures whether UTM parameters are appended to the final destination URL. The redirect must be confirmed to append at minimum:
- `utm_source` (e.g., `email`)
- `utm_medium` (e.g., `email`)
- `utm_campaign` (e.g., clearance campaign identifier)

If UTM params are appended only by the redirect server and not in the href, they will be invisible to any client-side link validator but should still land correctly — confirm with a live click-trace.

**Subject line / landing page alignment:** Subject promises "up to 70% off" clearance. The email is sending to a clearance-specific landing; confirm the destination page reflects current clearance inventory and that the discount claim is still accurate at time of send.

---

### 7. Recommendations

**P0 — Fix before any future send:**
1. **Remove all `//` comments from the JSON-LD block.** These are designer/template notes that were never stripped. Move them to HTML comments outside the `<script>` tag or delete them entirely.
2. **Populate or remove all empty JSON-LD fields.** Either inject real values via SFMC AMP script/personalization strings, or remove empty-string fields — empty strings cause annotation failure just as surely as missing fields.
3. **Update `availabilityStarts` from 2020 to the actual campaign start date.** This is clearly a template default that has never been replaced.

**P1 — Verify before send:**
4. Confirm `alt=""` is present on the open-tracking pixel and `alt="DICK'S Sporting Goods"` (or equivalent) on the logo `<img>`.
5. Confirm `e.dcsg.com` DKIM record is current and DMARC policy is enforced.
6. Trace a live click URL to confirm UTM parameters are appended at the redirect layer.
7. Verify CAN-SPAM footer (address + unsubscribe) is present in the full template body.
