# EXTRA 25% off select clearance won't last long! Score your picks now
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-14**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S running the same lake/dock "EXTRA 25% OFF" hero from yesterday's clearance send ("Your clearance cart can go further," 6.00/10) — nearly identical red placard over a plain pier photo, same "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" headline, same category quick-links row (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale). The subject line adds urgency ("won't last long") that the previous day's version didn't lead with, but the creative underneath is the standard DICK'S weekly-digest sprawl — Epic Shoe Sale, DSG clothing, adidas World Cup sets, Walter Hagen golf BOGO, Outdoor Essentials, ETHOS vests, Back-to-School, ScoreCard — nine-plus modules deep before the footer.
- The hero itself is clean and legible, and the clearance offer is genuinely clickable. But this is now the second near-identical clearance-hero send in two days from the same sender, and the digest sprawl below dilutes the "won't last long" urgency the subject promises — nothing after the hero actually reinforces scarcity. It reads as a solid but repetitive mid-tier promotional email.

## What's working

- Hero placard is legible and high-contrast: bold white "EXTRA 25% OFF" on red, clear against the pier photo.
- Clear, specific headline directly under the hero ("Extra 25% Off Select Clearance") with a black "Shop Now" CTA button.
- Quick category jump-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let a reader skip straight to their category.
- No render bugs — images load cleanly, text doesn't overlap, layout is consistent DICK'S template quality.
- ScoreCard loyalty module is present and clearly explains the earn rate (1 point per $1, 300 pts = $10).

## What's weak

- This is the same lake/dock hero image and "Extra 25% Off Select Clearance" copy as the 8/14 morning send — visually indistinguishable at a glance, which trains subscribers to skip it.
- The subject's "won't last long" urgency has no visual reinforcement anywhere in the body — no countdown, no end date visible near the hero (unlike the "Final Hours 07:59:48" countdown send from 8/12 that scored 7.00).
- Nine-plus unrelated modules stacked below the hero (shoes, clothing, World Cup sets, golf, outdoor gear, weighted vests, back-to-school, loyalty, product picks) bury the clearance message the subject promised.
- No visible discount percentage or price on any secondary module until deep in the email — most say "Shop Now" with vague copy ("Up to $500 Off," "BOGO 50% Off†") rather than reinforcing the 25% clearance hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put the urgency from the subject line into the hero itself — add an end-date or countdown timer next to "EXTRA 25% OFF" so the visual matches the "won't last long" promise.
- 2. Differentiate this hero from yesterday's near-identical clearance send — new photo or new crop at minimum, so repeat openers don't dismiss it as a duplicate.
- 3. Cut the digest to 3-4 modules max, or move non-clearance modules (World Cup sets, golf BOGO, outdoor essentials, weighted vests) to a separate weekly-deals send so this one stays focused on the clearance angle the subject sold.
- 4. Move the ScoreCard loyalty module up near the hero — members should see their earn-rate benefit before scrolling past nine unrelated offers.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your 25% off clearance picks are almost gone`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Sizes are going fast on shoes, clothing, and gear marked down twice.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Extra 25% off ends soon — ScoreCard members save more.`
