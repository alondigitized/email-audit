# Score an EXTRA 25% off select clearance while you can!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-06-05**
## Executive summary

- This is at minimum the seventh execution of the "Extra 25% off select clearance" creative since May 30 — sent the morning after DICK'S ran a genuinely stronger "Best Deals of the Week" compilation on June 4 (scored 9/10 here). That sequencing is damaging: yesterday's send felt like a pivot toward editorial value, and today's send yanks you right back into the discount loop. The subscriber who opened yesterday's email is opening the same envelope again today.
- Inside the email, the execution is competent. The hero lands immediately — oversized "EXTRA 25% OFF" on red, two clear CTAs — and the multi-category scroll (shoes, adidas clothing, golf, griddles, app promo) gives the layout enough variety to hold interest. But a buried detail undermines the whole thing: the app module reads "Easter Shopping is a Tap Away" — Easter was in April. That phrase sitting in a June 5 send is editorial sloppiness that erodes trust in the brand's attention to detail.
- **Verdict:** A recycled creative with a stale copy remnant sandwiched between two stronger sends. The bones of the email are fine; the cadence and the Easter ghost are the real problems.

## What's working

- **Hero is immediate and legible.** "EXTRA 25% OFF" dominates the viewport with no ambiguity about what's on offer.
- **Multi-category layout earns the scroll.** Shoes, adidas, golf, griddles, and summer gear give different buyer types a reason to keep reading.
- **Dual CTA buttons above the fold.** "SHOP NOW" + "SHOP DEALS" gives two entry points without cluttering the hero.
- **Summer gear module at the bottom.** "Must-have gear for an epic summer!" is seasonally appropriate and adds editorial freshness to an otherwise deal-first structure.

## What's weak

- **Cadence fatigue is severe.** Seventh-plus "Extra 25% off" execution in six days — subscribers have learned to treat this as noise.
- **"Easter Shopping is a Tap Away" in the app module.** Easter was April 20. Stale copy in a June send signals nobody reviewed this before it deployed.
- **No deadline or urgency anchor.** "While you can" is functionally empty. Yesterday's June 4 send didn't have a deadline either — two in a row with no clock.
- **"Select clearance" friction.** Five sends of this same offer have trained subscribers to expect the catch: most of the things they actually want aren't in clearance.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Kill the weekly cadence on this creative.** One "Extra 25% off clearance" send per week maximum. The June 4 "Best Deals" format clearly outperforms this — lean into compilation sends and reserve the clearance creative for a genuine event moment.
- 2. **Add a hard deadline.** "Offer ends Sunday" or "through Saturday" turns a shrug into an action. Without it, there is no reason to click now vs. never.
- 3. **Audit and retire the Easter app module.** Replace with a summer-native app hook (e.g., "Plan your summer haul in the app") — it takes five minutes and removes the trust-eroding anachronism.
- 4. **Sharpen the subject.** The current line is 54 characters with gratuitous caps and a weak close.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Extra 25% off clearance — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: extra 25% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, adidas, golf gear and more — through the weekend only`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your clearance cart is ready. Shop before it's gone.`
