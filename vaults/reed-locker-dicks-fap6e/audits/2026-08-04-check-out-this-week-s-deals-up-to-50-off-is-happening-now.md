# Check out this week's DEALS 💰 Up to 50% off is happening now!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the same blue beach-ball "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S ran two days ago as "Up to 50% off is waiting in this week's deals" (2026-08-02, 8/10) — same pool-party visual, same blue overlay box, nearly identical subject line. That's a repeat creative inside a 48-hour window, which is the first thing a regular subscriber would clock.
- Below the hero it's the classic kitchen-sink cascade: shoes, $50-and-under clothing, drinkware, golf, kayaks, weighted vests, MLB gear, credit card pitch, then a six-item product grid. Nothing is broken, everything renders cleanly, and the hero offer is bold and legible — but there's no single hero product or category the eye is meant to land on beyond "50% off *something*." Compare that to the 8/3 "Epic Shoe Sale" send, which picked one category and committed to it with a stacked-shoe hero and matching subhead — this email regresses back toward the diffuse, everything-on-sale format.
- Judgment: solid execution, zero render bugs, generically useful as a "digest" email — but it's a recycled hero with a vague subject, and it will read as filler next to sharper single-category sends this brand has already proven it can make.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is bold, high-contrast, and impossible to miss at a glance.
- Clean category nav bar (Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School) gives easy self-navigation right under the fold.
- Each subsequent module has its own clear CTA button, so scanning is easy even if you skip sections.
- "Only at DICK'S" badges on Quest Kayaks and Ethos Vests call out exclusive/private-label items, which adds differentiation.
- No layout breakage, overlapping text, or broken images anywhere in the render.

## What's weak

- The hero is a near-identical reuse of the 2026-08-02 "Up to 50% off is waiting in this week's deals" creative (same blue/beach-ball background, same 50% callout) — repeat creative within 48 hours.
- "This Week's Deals" as hero subhead and "Check out this week's DEALS" as subject is generic — no category, no specific product, no reason to open over any other weekly digest email.
- No single visual hero product — the eye has ten roughly-equal-weight modules to choose from instead of one clear focal offer.
- The 50% claim in the hero doesn't match most line items below (30% shoes, 40% drinkware, 40% kayaks, $100 off putters) — mild bait-and-switch feel since the headline max isn't reflected in the majority of the actual offers shown.
- Credit card pitch ("Cardholders Earn More") sits deep in the scroll with no visual separation from merchandise offers, easy to blow past.

## Recommendations

- 1. Swap the recycled beach-ball hero for a single hero product from this send's actual best deal (e.g., the Epic Shoe Sale visual, which DICK'S already has and tested well) — pick one lead offer and make the subject match it, the way 8/3's "Epic Shoe Sale" send did.
- 2. Make the hero percentage match a real, prominent offer in the body — if 50% only applies to MLB gear or a narrow subset, say "Up to 50% off MLB gear" specifically instead of an unqualified blanket claim.
- 3. Cut the cascade — this has 9+ distinct offer modules; a marketer could trim to the top 3-4 highest-margin/highest-interest categories and move the rest to a "shop more" grid, reducing scroll fatigue.
- 4. Reorder so a specific, single strong CTA sits directly under the hero instead of a generic "This Week's Deals / Shop Now" repeat of the hero itself.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off MLB gear + more this week`
- **Subject Alt B:** `This week: 30% off shoes, $100 off putters, 40% off kayaks`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New drinkware colors, $100 off Odyssey putters, and weekend shoe deals — all in one place.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Your weekly roundup: shoes, kayaks, golf gear, and more on sale now.`

## Technical Audit

# Technical Audit — DICK'S Sporting Goods "Up to 50% off" Email

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is built on a standard Epsilon/Harmony-hosted responsive hybrid template with MSO/Outlook conditionals, but ships with an invalid JSON-LD block, a broken unsubscribe link, and no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured at this relay — 43% automated pass rate.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Unsubscribe link is broken**: resolves to an IIS error page — `unknown url type: '/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription'` (link text "Unsubscribe", tracked via `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v610000019fcc534c1293ec02f4bbcfd118/a67661d2e91a4a0e0000021ef3a0bd04`). This is a functional failure, not just a compliance gap — the target subscription-management page itself errors out.
- All observable links (logo, header CTA) route through an ESP click-tracking redirector at `app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...?__dU__=...` — consistent with Epsilon/Harmony tracking infrastructure; behavior otherwise normal.
- QA probed 12 of 68 material links (ranked by click likelihood); 56 lower-priority/footer/social links were not verified — coverage gap, not a confirmed defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **11 images missing `alt` text**, including primary product/promo creative (`clothing.jpg`, `banner4.gif`, `callaway.jpg`, `kyak.jpg`, `vest.jpg`, `mlb.jpg`) and footer icons (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`), plus the open-tracking pixel image. This degrades screen-reader output and image-blocked rendering (default in Outlook/many corporate clients).
- **Malformed inline CSS in the MSO conditional block**: `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}</style>` contains a stray `"}` before `</style>`, breaking the intended CSS rule for Outlook/IE rendering paths.
- `banner4.gif` is a GIF — the email's own embedded schema comment states `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported and will be filtered out`, suggesting this asset may be dropped by downstream ESP rendering.
- `<meta charset>` and `<meta viewport>` present; `lang="en"` set — no issues there.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
- The embedded `application/ld+json` `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` schema objects contain **unfilled template fields**: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and `"image": ""`. These read as designer placeholders (`// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`) that were never populated before send.
- **The JSON-LD block contains `//` JavaScript-style comments inside a `<script type="application/ld+json">` tag**, which is invalid JSON. This will cause the structured-data block to fail parsing entirely (e.g., Gmail annotations/Google Rich Results), nullifying any benefit from the schema markup.
- No visible merge-tag artifacts (e.g., unresolved `{{token}}` syntax) in the truncated body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication)
- Unsubscribe mechanism is present in the footer per QA, but its **destination URL errors out** (see §2) — a functional CAN-SPAM risk if subscribers cannot actually opt out.
- **`List-Unsubscribe` header not detected** and **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not detected** — relevant given Gmail/Yahoo's Feb 2024 bulk-sender requirements for one-click unsubscribe. Caveat: QA notes these may simply not be captured by the AgentMail relay rather than genuinely absent at origin.
- **`Authentication-Results` header not found** — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status is unverifiable from this hop; same relay-capture caveat applies.
- From address `DSG@e.dcsg.com` uses a dedicated subdomain, consistent with standard DKIM-alignment practice, but alignment itself can't be confirmed without the auth header.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM, landing page alignment)
- All CTA/logo links route through the `app.dickssportinggoods.com` ESP redirector with an opaque `__dU__` token rather than exposing direct destination URLs with UTM parameters — final landing URL and UTM tagging can't be verified from the truncated source without following the redirect chain.
- No issues found in the visible content regarding domain consistency (all links point to `dickssportinggoods.com`/`dcsg.com` infrastructure, no off-brand or mismatched domains observed).

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe landing page (`/pc/subscription` currently 500s via `/pc/Error/Error`) — highest priority, blocks a legally required function.
2. Strip the `//` comments from the `application/ld+json` block or move them outside the script tag; populate or remove the empty `discountCode`/`description`/`image`/`availabilityEnds` fields.
3. Add `alt` attributes to all content and footer icon images.
4. Fix the stray `"}` in the MSO conditional `<style>` block.
5. Replace the `banner4.gif` asset per the template's own stated GIF-filtering limitation, or confirm the current ESP no longer filters GIFs.
6. Confirm with the sending team whether `List-Unsubscribe`/`List-Unsubscribe-Post`/`Authentication-Results` are actually being sent — if the AgentMail relay is simply not surfacing them, re-verify against raw SMTP headers at origin.
