# Waiting for Black Friday? Don’t. 👀 💰 Up to 50% OFF starts NOW.
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-24**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S sixth or seventh near-identical Black Friday send in three days. It's well built: a big green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero, one red "Shop Now" button, then a clean stack of product tiles. The offer lands in about two seconds.
- The subject line is the weak part. "Waiting for Black Friday? Don't." is a decent hook, but the 11/23 sends already said "Black Friday starts NOW." DICK'S keeps recycling the same 50% hero and the same body. A subscriber has seen this email repeatedly, and the stacked emojis and ALL CAPS read like a promo blast.
- The body does one thing well: it moves from the hero to category chips, then to specific products with prices, then to an extra 25% off clearance block. The cost is length. The email scrolls a long way, and the clearance banner competes with the hero.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: "UP TO 50% OFF" in huge type with a festive treatment, and one "Shop Now" button right below.
- The product tiles name specific items with prices, such as the VRST Recharge and CALIA Soft Scrula styles at $49.99 and $49.98. That gives the click something concrete.
- The "Explore more holiday deals" chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Golf, Fan Shop) let people self-segment quickly.
- The holiday store hours block and the app-download block are useful, on-brand service content.
- The layout renders cleanly, with no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The email is nearly identical to the 11/23 "Black Friday starting now" send, so it adds nothing new for a repeat reader.
- The second hero, "Extra 25% Off," is a competing giant banner. It dilutes the single 50% message.
- The exclusions and fine print are tiny and dense. They sit under the hero and on several tiles, which weakens the "honest offer" feel.
- The generic "Shop Now" CTA repeats on almost every block. None of the copy is specific, such as "Shop Nike & Jordan under $100."
- The email is very long, with 10+ modules, and the key deal is repeated rather than prioritized.

## Recommendations

- 1. Differentiate this send from the last six. Lead with something new, such as a doorbuster or a countdown, instead of the same 50% hero.
- 2. Cut the module count by about half. Keep the hero, 3–4 products, and one clearance strip, and move store hours to the footer.
- 3. Make CTAs specific: "Shop Nike & Jordan deals" instead of repeating "Shop Now."
- 4. Put a plain-text deadline next to the hero ("Ends Friday 11/29") instead of burying it in the fine print.
- 5. Cut the emoji stack and the shouting caps in the subject line.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Black Friday prices are live — up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Skip the wait: Nike, Jordan & more up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus extra 25% off clearance. Pickup in 1 hour.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Doorbusters on shoes, golf & gifts, today only.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible markup is structurally sound: `lang="en"`, `<title>`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables, and MSO conditionals. Two QA flags (a missing plain-text part and missing alt text on every probed image) are confirmed. The compliance and authentication checks are inconclusive because the headers weren't captured, and the HTML source was truncated before the footer.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **No broken links reported.** 12 material links were probed, and none were flagged as failing. 55 were skipped (footer, social and utility links, as designed), so those are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel.** `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&pid=...&tuid=...&configId=...` is a 1x1 `<img>` with no `alt` and no inline `style`. It carries a long per-recipient token, which is normal for tracking but makes the URL very long.
- **Click wrappers can't be audited.** The visible anchors use `class="arc-link" data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. The `href` values were cut off in the truncated source, so I can't confirm the redirect chain, deep-link fallback or HTTPS use.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Alt text is missing on every probed image (14 flagged).**
  - The 9 content images are Scene7 banners: `2nikejordan1122`, `vrstrechargelunar1122vertbrand`, `caliasoftscuba1122vertbrand`, `2bombas`, `1122-stw-team-sports-cyber8-hoop`, `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-tcgs`, `taylormade-balls-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`, `1101storefront`, plus the `icon-vc-*` and `icon-location-2025-1` icons.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, there is no text equivalent for any of them.
  - Decorative icons and the tracking pixel should use `alt=""`. The content banners need descriptive alt text.
- **Large image requests.** Scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100` for a 600px-max container. That is a heavy payload at 2x and wasteful on mobile. This is a performance concern, not a confirmed rendering bug.
- **No preheader text in the visible source.** The `#ssl` cell still contains the leftover `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` placeholder. The `#pht-spacer` div holds only `&zwnj;&nbsp;` filler, so no preheader text is visible. If it was not injected downstream, inbox clients will pull the first body text instead.
- **Minor.** `<link rel="icon">` in the `<head>` has no effect in email clients, and most strip it.
- **Plain-text part.** See Compliance and Deliverability below.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`) appear in the visible HTML. The portion after the header was truncated, so this is not verified for the full body.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is effectively missing.** The text version is 9 characters. Multipart/alternative with a real text body is expected for deliverability, and its absence is a spam-filter signal. This is the one confirmed failure.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** QA notes the AgentMail relay may strip these headers, so this is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require one-click unsubscribe for senders of this size, so check the raw message.
- **[WARN] `Authentication-Results` not captured.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC for `em.dcsg.com` can't be assessed from this data. The sender domain is consistent with the From address `info@em.dcsg.com` and the tracking host `data.em.dcsg.com`.
- **Footer unverified.** The in-body unsubscribe link and physical postal address are in the truncated portion. QA says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but no result is shown.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Cannot be assessed. The anchor `href` values were truncated, so UTM parameters and landing-page destinations are not visible. No probe failures or redirect anomalies were reported for the 12 links checked.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Add a real plain-text MIME part.** It should include the offer copy, key links and the unsubscribe URL. This is the highest-priority fix.
2. **Add alt text to the banners and `alt=""` to the icons and pixel.** Banners need descriptive alt text, for example "Nike Jordan, up to 50% off".
3. **Check the raw message** for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC alignment. Don't treat the relay warnings as failures until then.
4. **Populate the preheader** in `#ssl`, or remove the placeholder comment.
5. **Reduce Scene7 requests** to `wid=1200` (2x of 600) with no `hei=1600` constraint, and lower `qlt` to about 80.
6. **Re-run with full HTML and the link-probe output** to audit click-wrapper `href`s, UTM consistency and the footer.
