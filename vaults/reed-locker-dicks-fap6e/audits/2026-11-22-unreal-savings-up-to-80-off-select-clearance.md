# 💰 Unreal savings: Up to 80% OFF select clearance 💰
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the third 80% clearance hero in three days. The first screen is a big red-and-gold "UP TO 80% OFF" block, and the headline and one dark red "Shop Now" button sit right under it. The offer is easy to read, and the layout puts the offer first. But it's the same creative as 11/20 and the 11/21 "This doesn't happen every day" send, and nothing new tells you why you should care today.
- Below the hero, the email turns into a general DICK'S catalog. A Black Friday "Starts Now" block (up to 50% off) competes directly with the clearance hero. After that come Nike/Jordan, VRST, CALIA, TaylorMade, camp chairs, basketball hoops, holiday hours and gift picks. The sender is "DICK'S | Clearance", so the clearance promise is made twice, but the body spends most of its length on everything except clearance. The email is polished and readable, but it's an ordinary repeat send.
- Judgement: a clean but repetitive 6/10. It works as a reminder. It does nothing to separate itself from the last three clearance emails.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 80% OFF" hero fills the first screen. The eye lands on the offer immediately.
- "Limited Time" with one "Shop Now" button gives an unambiguous primary CTA.
- Category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) let you narrow down quickly.
- The Black Friday block reflects the current season, and the 2-for-$100 TaylorMade golf balls and the holiday store hours add concrete, useful details.
- Product-led modules (Nike shoes, VRST, CALIA) alternate left and right, which keeps the scroll rhythm clear. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- The hero shows no actual clearance product. It's a typographic block, so you can't tell what is 80% off.
- The 50% Black Friday block directly below competes with the 80% clearance offer and dilutes it.
- The "Limited Time" labels give no end date or time in the visible copy. The only dates are in small fine print.
- The email is very long, with about ten modules. The clearance message is buried by the time you reach the gift picks and store hours.
- The Sender and subject are repeating a pattern: this is the same 80% hero as the 11/20 and 11/21 sends. A subscriber would feel the fatigue.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the typographic hero with three or four real clearance products, each showing its sale price and its original price. That makes the 80% claim believable.
- 2. Put a real deadline in the hero, such as "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM ET" or a countdown. The 11/21 "final hours" send used a countdown, and it was the best element of that email.
- 3. Cut or demote the Black Friday 50% block so it doesn't compete with the clearance message in a clearance-branded send.
- 4. Shorten the email to about five modules and move the gift picks and store hours to a different send.
- 5. Vary the creative between sends. Don't send the same red-and-gold block three days running.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, jackets and gear marked down. Shop before sizes sell out.`
