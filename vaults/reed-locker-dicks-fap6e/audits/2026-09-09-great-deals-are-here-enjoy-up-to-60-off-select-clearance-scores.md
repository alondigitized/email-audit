# Great deals are here — enjoy up to 60% OFF select clearance scores
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-09**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S back on the maroon stadium-seating "UP TO 60% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE SCORES" hero — the same one from yesterday's 9/9 clearance send, now dressed with the subject "Great deals are here — enjoy up to 60% OFF select clearance scores." The layout is the now-standard DICK'S kitchen-sink template: hero, "Explore more deals" nav pills, then eight near-identical category modules (clothing/shoes, drinkware, team sports, golf, fan shop, outdoor, fitness, "picks") each with its own vague "Up to X% Off" header and generic "Shop Now" button.
- Nothing here is broken, and the hero-to-CTA path is clean, but there's zero differentiation from the last several sends in this cycle. Same red stadium-seat crop, same "up to 60%" framing, same walls of category tiles. A subscriber who opened 9/9's "🚨 Up to 60% OFF select clearance scores! 🚨" email yesterday is looking at what's functionally a re-skin today.
- Judgement: competent, on-brand, zero-bug execution that's being dragged down by fatigue — this is the third+ "up to 50-60% off" send in the emoji-free/emoji-heavy rotation this week, and the copy isn't doing anything to earn a second open.

## What's working

- Hero renders cleanly with a clear, high-contrast "UP TO 60% OFF" callout and unambiguous green "Shop Now" CTA directly below the value prop.
- Category nav pills (Men's, Women's, Kid's, Fan Shop, etc.) give an immediate self-select path for someone who doesn't care about clearance generally.
- Fine print ("Valid online and at select stores... Prices as marked") is visible and honest, not buried.
- Sub-modules are demographically broad (men, women, kids, golf, fan shop) so most household members see something relevant.
- "Free ship to store now available" callouts on sports/fitness modules add a concrete, non-discount incentive.

## What's weak

- This is the same red stadium-seating hero and "up to 60% off" framing as the 9/9 "🚨 Up to 60% OFF select clearance scores! 🚨" send — subscribers who opened that one get almost the same email again same-day/next-day.
- "Great deals are here" is filler — it says nothing about what's actually on sale.
- Eight stacked category modules with duplicate "Up to X% Off" headers create visual monotony; nothing after the hero signals priority or newness.
- No loyalty/ScoreCard member pricing or points callout despite ScoreCard branding in the header — a missed personalization opportunity.
- No urgency mechanism (no countdown, no "ends tonight") despite this being framed as a limited-time clearance event.

## Recommendations

- 1. Rotate the hero creative and headline framing — even holding the same clearance event, use a different visual (product flat-lay, lifestyle shot) so back-to-back sends don't look like reprints of each other.
- 2. Add a real deadline or urgency element (date, countdown, "final days") since "while they last" is vague and the brand has proven countdown timers work (9/7 send scored higher partly on urgency).
- 3. Trim the category modules from 8 down to 3-4, prioritized by what's actually trending, and cut the redundant "Up to X% Off" repetition in each header.
- 4. Surface ScoreCard/loyalty member pricing or bonus points directly in the hero or a dedicated module — the header shows ScoreCard branding but the body never uses it.
- 5. Tighten the subject to lead with the number instead of throwaway "Great deals are here."

- **Subject Alt B:** `ScoreCard members: up to 60% off clearance scores`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `New markdowns just added across shoes, gear & fan shop`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Order today, ship tomorrow — inventory and availability vary`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
Email renders via a standard Epsilon/Harmony-hosted table layout with MSO conditionals and mobile media queries; automated checks show a broken unsubscribe link, no unsubscribe/authentication headers captured, and widespread missing image alt text.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Broken unsubscribe link**: QA flagged `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/U/v61000001a0871e8f8babb7856e96638858/5fd9df27d15f4c6d0000021ef3a0bd07` as resolving to `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription` — the preference-center redirect target is broken.
- All sampled links use Epsilon/Harmony click-tracking redirects (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...` and `/O/...` open-tracking pixel), consistent with standard ESP wrapping — no malformed tracking syntax observed in the truncated source.
- 12 of 71 total links were probed by QA (59 skipped per the tool's click-likelihood ranking); footer/social links were deprioritized, so link coverage is partial, not exhaustive.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- 12 images missing `alt` text, including all five product category banners (`clothes-shoes-2026-0909-d`, `sports-2026-0909-d`, `golf-2026-0909-d`, `fan-shop-2026-0909-d`, `outdoor-2026-0909-d`, `fitness-2026-0909-d`), the open-tracking pixel, a legacy `banner4.gif` asset, and 4 footer icon images (`icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`). This breaks screen-reader accessibility and degrades the experience with images-off (default in Outlook/many clients).
- Web font (`Archivo`) is loaded via `@font-face`/Google Fonts inside a `@media screen` block with `font-family:Archivo,Arial,sans-serif` fallback declared separately in `body` — correct progressive-enhancement pattern, no issue.
- MSO/Outlook conditional comments present (`<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]-->`) with table `border-collapse` reset — standard, no issue.
- A malformed inline style is present in the MSO conditional block: `div {...padding:0;"}` — there's a stray `"` before the closing brace in `<style type="text/css">`. This is inside an Outlook-only conditional comment; low risk but technically invalid CSS that could cause the MSO stylesheet parse to fail silently in some Outlook versions.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens

- The embedded `application/ld+json` block contains empty/unpopulated fields (`"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, `"image": ""` for the PromotionCard) and leftover build-time comments (`// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`, `// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`). ## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Unsubscribe link is broken** (see §2) — this is a functional CAN-SPAM risk since the mechanism must actually work, not just be present.
- **List-Unsubscribe header**: not found. Cannot confirm one-click unsubscribe support at the header level (may be a relay-capture limitation rather than absence in the original send).
- **List-Unsubscribe-Post header (RFC 8058)**: not found — one-click unsubscribe via mailbox provider UI cannot be confirmed.
- **Authentication-Results header**: not found — SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass/fail status cannot be verified from this capture.
- Sender domain `e.dcsg.com` is a dedicated subdomain, consistent with standard SPF/DKIM alignment practice, but this cannot be confirmed without the missing headers.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
- Tracking links route through Epsilon Harmony redirect infrastructure (`app.dickssportinggoods.com/T/...`) rather than direct UTM-tagged URLs to dickssportinggoods.com — final landing destinations and UTM parameters are not visible in the truncated/redirected URLs, so continuity cannot be verified from source alone.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix the unsubscribe redirect mapping so `/U/.../5fd9df27...bd07` resolves to a valid subscription-preferences page instead of `/pc/Error/Error?aspxerrorpath=/pc/subscription`.
