# Only hours left ⏰⚡️ Up to 50% off Flash Sale DEALS
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-23**
## Executive summary

- This is the second DICK'S flash-sale send today, and it stacks three offers in one email: up to 50% off, up to 40% off shoes and an extra 25% off clearance. The 11:59PM PT deadline gives "only hours left" real credibility. The green and red Shop Now buttons are easy to find, and the Scorecard strip is a useful loyalty touch.
- The hero is the weak spot. The giant "FLASH" wordmark is cropped to "FLAS" on an orange field, so the first thing you see is a half-word and not the offer. The "Up to 50% Off" headline sits below it, and "Flash Sale" is already in the sender name, so the hero adds almost nothing.
- A real subscriber would also notice the cadence. This follows this morning's "Today only ⚡ Up to 50% off Flash Sale DEALS!!" and the 10/22 "Extra 25% off select clearance" sends. The clearance block here reuses that same red "EXTRA 25% OFF" creative. It is competent and credible, but it feels like a recycled stack with a more urgent subject line.

## What's working

- The deadline is specific ("through 10/23/26 11:59PM PT") and matches the "only hours left" subject.
- The "Up to 50% Off" headline and the red "One Day Only" label make the main offer clear.
- The 40% off shoes block shows real Nike and adidas product shots, so the offer feels concrete.
- The Scorecard strip ("300 Points = $10 Reward") puts loyalty benefits at the very top.
- The green pill category chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Team Sports) give an easy way to browse.

## What's weak

- The hero wordmark is cropped to "FLAS", and the huge orange image pushes the actual offer below the first scroll on mobile.
- The email is near-duplicate of this morning's flash send and the 10/22 clearance emails. The extra 25% off block is the same creative with a different deadline.
- Three stacked offers with different percentages dilute the "flash" message.
- Every CTA says "Shop Now", and each one sends you to a broad category, not a specific product.
- The product picks at the bottom have no prices and no social proof, so nothing there pulls you to click.
- The dense fine print under each block is hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero. Put "Up to 50% Off" and a live countdown or "Ends 11:59PM PT" inside the image, and stop cropping the wordmark.
- 2. Cut to one offer, or one flash plus one supporting block. Fold the extra 25% off clearance into a single line.
- 3. Put prices and "was/now" on the product picks, and link each to its product page. Use specific CTA copy such as "Shop Nike Deals" or "Shop Shoes up to 40% Off".
- 4. If a morning flash email already went out, send the evening one only to non-openers or non-clickers, or change the creative materially.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 50% off clothing, shoes & more`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏰ 11:59PM PT: Flash Sale ends tonight`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus up to 40% off Nike & adidas shoes. Ends at midnight PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call: extra 25% off clearance and 50% off select styles.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The visible HTML is structurally sound: it has `lang="en"`, a viewport meta tag, `role="presentation"` layout tables, an MSO conditional block and a hidden preheader. The automated QA flags a missing plain-text part (9 chars), missing alt text on 6 images, and no capturable compliance or authentication headers. The source was truncated, so footer, link and UTM checks are limited.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- The QA run probed 12 material links and skipped 41. It reported no broken or redirecting links among the probed set, so none are confirmed. The 41 skipped links (footer, utility, social) are unverified.
- Anchors carry `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"`. This suggests deep-link or redirect tracking, but the `href` values fall outside the truncated source, so I can't evaluate them.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=…&pid=…&tuid=…&configId=…`. It is 1×1, sits directly inside `<body>` before the wrapper table, and comes from the same `dcsg.com` sending domain. It has no alt attribute and no `style="display:block"`. This is a low-severity issue and doesn't break rendering.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (QA-confirmed).** Five Scene7 icons have no alt:
  - `icon-vc-sms-1`
  - `icon-vc-sportsmatter`
  - `icon-vc-bpg-1`
  - `icon-vc-favorite-1`
  - `icon-location-2025-1`

  They are all `https://s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/…?fmt=png-alpha`. The sixth missing-alt image is the open pixel. If the icons are decorative, they need `alt=""`. If they sit beside link text, they need descriptive alt.
- **Plain-text part (QA FAIL).** The text version is 9 characters, so effectively there is no multipart/alternative text body. That hurts text-only clients, accessibility tooling and spam scoring.
- **Well-formed.** I found no problems in the visible source:
  - `<html lang="en">` and a `<title>`
  - Outlook DPI and PNG settings via `OfficeDocumentSettings`
  - `mso-hide: all` on the preheader
  - an Archivo `@font-face` with `font-display: swap` and an Arial fallback
  - mobile classes under `@media screen and (max-width:480px)`
- **Minor.** The wrapper table has `width="600"` plus inline `width:100%; max-width:600px`. Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`, so the wrapper is fixed at 600px there, which is acceptable. The inner `<td>` uses `font-size:0` for gap control, which is fine as long as text cells reset it. I couldn't check that in the truncated source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unrendered merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The `tuid` and `params` values in the open pixel are populated. I can't check the truncated remainder.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe` not found (QA WARN).** The AgentMail relay may strip it. Verify against the raw message at the origin before treating it as a real defect.
- **`List-Unsubscribe-Post` not found (QA WARN).** This is the RFC 8058 one-click header. Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require it. It can't be confirmed present or absent from relay-captured data.
- **`Authentication-Results` not found (QA WARN).** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment are unverified. The From domain is `em.dcsg.com`.
- **Unconfirmed footer elements.** The physical postal address and the visible unsubscribe link are not in the truncated source. The QA says an unsubscribe link was included in probing, but no result was reported.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The `href` values, UTM parameters and landing pages are outside the truncated source, and the probe results don't include destination URLs. No mismatches are confirmed.

1. **Add a real plain-text part.** Replace the 9-character fallback with a full text alternative that includes the CTAs and the unsubscribe URL.
