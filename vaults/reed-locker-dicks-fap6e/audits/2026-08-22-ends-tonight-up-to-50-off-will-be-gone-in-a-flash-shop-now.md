# Ends. Tonight! Up to 50% OFF will be gone in a flash — shop now. ⚡
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the same green-locker-room "Flash Sale" hero DICK'S already sent today under the subject "Flash Sale alert! 🚨 Up to 50% off is yours... but not for long" — literally the same creative asset, same countdown-timer treatment, same "Up to 50% Off" copy, just re-fired hours later with an "Ends Tonight" subject line. That's two near-identical sends in one day, on top of the 8/16 and 8/20 versions of this exact locker imagery.
- The hero itself has a visible crop bug: "FLASH" and "SALE" are cut off at the right edge of the image, which looks broken on render rather than stylized. Below the fold it's the usual DICK'S sprawl — ScoreCard+ Member Week 20% off, 50 Bonus Points, an orange Extra 25% Off clearance block, then a product grid. Nothing here is false advertising, but nothing here is new either, and the double-send-per-day cadence is the real problem.
- Judgement: functional but fatigued creative, undercut by a cropped hero graphic and same-day duplication of an email already sent.

## What's working

- Countdown timer ("Final Hours 07:59:48") gives genuine, credible urgency tied to a real end-of-day deadline.
- "Up to 50% Off" hero CTA ("Shop Now") is clear and above the fold.
- Category quick-links (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Team Sports, Back-to-School) give multiple easy paths in without forcing a scroll.
- Product grid at the bottom shows real, recognizable SKUs (Nike, New Balance, adidas, HOKA) with visible thumbnails — no broken images there.

## What's weak

- The "FLASH SALE" hero text is cropped at the right edge — "FLASH" wraps awkwardly and "SALE" is clipped, reading as a render bug.
- This is a near-duplicate of today's earlier "Flash Sale alert!" send — same locker hero, same 50% off headline, same offer — sent again same day, which is aggressive cadence even by this brand's standards.
- Three more separate sales blocks stack below the hero (ScoreCard+ 20%, 50 Bonus Points, Extra 25% clearance) — no single clear offer, diluting the "ends tonight" urgency.
- "Select styles," "Exclusions apply," and "while quantities last" disclaimers sit right under two of the three offer blocks, adding friction to an urgency-driven pitch.

## Recommendations

- 1. Stop sending two visually-identical Flash Sale emails in one day — if urgency is real, one well-timed send (evening, closer to the actual deadline) beats two that cannibalize each other's opens.
- 2. Fix the hero crop so "FLASH SALE" renders fully — a clipped headline undercuts trust on a legitimate urgency play.
- 3. Cut the email down to one primary offer (the 50% off flash sale) and move ScoreCard+/Bonus Points/clearance into a single secondary "more ways to save" strip instead of three full-width blocks.
- 4. Move the countdown timer directly under the hero headline instead of the very top masthead — right now it's disconnected from the "Flash Sale" creative it's meant to reinforce.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Your cart's waiting. Sale closes tonight at 11:59PM PT.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shoes, gear & clearance — all marked down until midnight.`
