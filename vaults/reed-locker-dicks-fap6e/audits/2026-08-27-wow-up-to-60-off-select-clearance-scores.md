# Wow! 🚨 Up to 60% OFF select clearance scores. ➡️
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-27**
## Executive summary

- This is DICK'S sticking with the exact same "UP TO X% OFF" red-tag-on-locker hero it's run all week — the 60% version repeats the 8/25 send ("Up to 60% OFF 🤝 Select clearance scores") almost word-for-word, right down to the "select clearance scores" phrasing. The subject even adds "Wow! 🚨" and an arrow emoji, which reads as forced urgency rather than genuine news given nothing in the body has changed since two days ago.
- Below the hero, this is a genuinely long kitchen-sink email — clearance, back-to-school, a cozy sweater collection, football gear, golf, camping, Peloton shoes, and a second "up to 50% off" locker banner near the bottom. That breadth is actually useful (something for most shoppers), but it undercuts the "clearance" subject line hard: only the first block is actually clearance, everything else is a different, unrelated promo.
- Verdict: solid execution, zero freshness. The creative fatigue here is now measurable — this is the fourth+ near-identical locker/padlock hero image DICK'S has sent this month, and the emoji-stacked subject line is a weaker version of 8/25's cleaner phrasing.

## What's working

- Clear, high-contrast "UP TO 60% OFF" hero with unambiguous "Shop Now" CTA immediately visible.
- Good category shortcut chips (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 and Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) right under the hero for fast navigation.
- Each subsequent product block (football, golf, camping, cycling shoes) has its own clean image, headline, and Shop Now button — no render bugs, no broken images, no overlapping text.
- Loyalty/member signals present up top (ScoreCard points, "Get It Faster" rewards banner).

## What's weak

- Subject says "clearance" but 80% of the email is unrelated categories (back-to-school, golf, camping, cycling) — a bait-and-switch on the specific promise in the subject.
- Same green-locker-with-padlock-graphic hero style used in the 8/16, 8/20, 8/22, 8/24, 8/25, and 8/26 sends — this is now visibly stale, not fresh creative.
- Subject nearly duplicates 8/25's "Up to 60% OFF 🤝 Select clearance scores" send two days later — same offer, same copy, just more emoji.
- Fine print buried at the bottom in small gray text ("Prices as marked," exclusions, markdowns disclaimer) undercuts the "60% off" headline honesty.
- Email is extremely long (9+ product blocks) with no single clear priority after the hero — visual hierarchy flattens out fast.

## Recommendations

- 1. Match subject to content or restructure the email: either make this genuinely clearance-only, or retitle it as a multi-category roundup ("This Week's Deals") so the promise matches what's inside.
- 2. Retire the green-locker hero graphic — it's appeared in at least 6 sends this month; a fresh clearance-specific image (product flatlay, price-tag close-up) would reset visual fatigue.
- 3. Drop the emoji stacking in the subject ("Wow! 🚨 ... ➡️") — it reads as spam-adjacent and adds no real information; 8/25's plainer phrasing scored better.
- 4. Move the strongest single offer higher and trim weaker blocks (Peloton shoes at 20% off is buried at the very bottom, easy to miss).
- 5. Front-load one concrete deadline ("Ends tonight," "Through Sunday") — currently no urgency is visible anywhere in the body despite the exclamation-heavy subject.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your clearance picks are up to 60% off right now`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus back-to-school, golf, camping, and more — all discounted this week`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Shop reduced prices before select styles sell out`
