# Golden State takes the W 🏆🏀💜
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a fan-moment send with no offer. DICK'S celebrates Golden State's WNBA title with a purple-and-gold hero: "GOLDEN STATE TAKES IT ALL!", a champions tee, a cap and confetti. It's timely and looks good, and the eye lands on the hero first. The subject's "takes the W 🏆🏀💜" matches that tone.
- The problem is that nothing gives you a reason to buy today. There's no percent off, no promo code and no member bonus beyond the generic "300 Points = $10 Reward" line in the header. After a week of "EXTRA 25% OFF" and "Up to 50% off" blasts, including three clearance sends on 10/31, this one is a refreshing change of pace. As a revenue driver it's thin. It's a decent brand-and-merch moment, not a conversion email.
- The body also runs out of ideas quickly. It has three stacked blocks, three near-identical "Shop Now" buttons, and a third block that repeats the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." line already in the header. Verdict: a good topical hook with weak follow-through on the offer and the product.

## What's working

- The hero is on-theme and well executed. Purple and gold, a champions tee and cap, and a big headline make it instantly clear what the email is about.
- It's current. Championship gear lands while the win is fresh, so nothing here feels stale.
- The subject has personality. "Takes the W" plus the 🏆🏀💜 emoji reads like a fan, not a promo blast.
- The hero "Shop Now" button is a solid green block directly under the headline and the "Congrats, Golden State!" line.
- The ScoreCard strip ("300 Points = $10 Reward") and the green nav bar (Fan Shop, Clearance) keep the brand frame consistent. I see no broken images or overlapping text.

## What's weak

- There's no offer at all. No price, discount, or member perk tied to this gear, so the reader has no reason to act today.
- The three "Shop Now" buttons are the same generic copy. The second and third are grey, so the secondary blocks look de-emphasized.
- The second block, "WNBA Clothing & Hats", shows a muddy dark collage with no prices or named products.
- The third block repeats the "Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." header line, and the fulfillment message is mostly a repeat of the top of the email.
- The ScoreCard line is small and tucked in the header, so it does no work for a loyalty member.

## Recommendations

- 1. Add one concrete incentive tied to the moment. For example, "Champions tees from $X" or "Bonus 2x ScoreCard points on Valkyries gear this weekend". This is the single change that would lift the email most.
- 2. Replace the generic "Shop Now" buttons with specific copy: "Shop Champions Tees", "Get the Hoodie", "Shop Hats".
- 3. Cut the third "Order Today" block. Fold it into a one-line banner and use the space for 3–4 product tiles with names and prices.
- 4. Make the loyalty message prominent, near the hero, not in the header.
- 5. Make the buttons consistent in colour, so every CTA looks clickable.

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is structurally sound (`role="presentation"` tables, `lang="en"`, a hidden preheader, an Outlook conditional block). It has a few code-level defects, though: an invalid JSON-LD block, a malformed CSS rule, and several images without alt text. The three header warnings come from the AgentMail relay, so they don't confirm a sender-side fault.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- No broken links reported: 12 material links were probed and none failed. 28 were skipped as footer, utility or social links, so they are unverified.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1951e97518096e4969125cd18/9aba6e92a0614b4a00004c5a42963aa1`. It is an `<img>` with `display:none; max-height:0` and no alt attribute. It is placed before the preheader inside `td#ssl`.
- Hidden-image tracking is unreliable. Apple Mail Privacy Protection prefetches it, and clients that block images drop it.
- The truncated source doesn't let me verify the CTA hrefs, so I can't confirm redirect behavior.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text.** QA flagged 7 images:
  - the tracking pixel `9aba6e92…3aa1`;
  - `2026-1024-lic-wnba-hm-finals-ifwin-nightof-gearsf3?...wid=120`, which looks like content, so it needs descriptive alt;
  - 5 footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - Decorative images should carry `alt=""`. Linked icons need a meaningful alt, or screen readers will announce the filename or URL.
- **Malformed CSS in the Outlook conditional.** The rule `div {background-color:inherit; font-size:0; line-height:100%; margin:0; padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"` before `}`. It can invalidate the declaration block in MSO. Setting `font-size:0` on every `div` is also risky for Outlook text.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The Gmail Promotions block contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON. It also has empty `discountCode`, `availabilityEnds` and `image` values, and a stale `availabilityStarts` of `2020-03-06`. Parsers will likely discard the whole block, so the Gmail promo annotation won't render. It appears to be an unfilled template ("DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!").
- **Small mobile text.** At `max-width:480px`, `.body-sm` and `.link-sm` are 9px, `.legal` is 8px and `.body-m` is 11px. These are below common legibility minimums.
- **Table width.** The wrapper has `width="600"` plus inline `width:100%; max-width:600px`. This is fine in modern clients, but Outlook desktop ignores `max-width`.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The source is truncated, so the body wasn't fully checked.

## 5. Compliance

Check the raw headers from a direct inbox (Gmail "Show original"). Gmail and Yahoo bulk-sender rules require both.
- **Authentication-Results.** Not captured, so SPF, DKIM and DMARC status is unknown. The From domain `e.dcsg.com` is a subdomain, so check alignment in a direct-delivery test.
- The unsubscribe link was probed and no failure was reported, but its URL isn't visible in the truncated source.
- The physical address in the footer can't be confirmed from the truncated source.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
Not assessable. The truncated HTML shows no CTA hrefs, so UTM parameters and landing-page alignment can't be checked. The 12 probed links returned no errors.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Fix or remove the JSON-LD block: strip the comments, fill in the fields, or drop the block.
2. Add `alt=""` to the tracking pixel, and meaningful alt text to the 120px WNBA image and any linked footer icons.
3. Remove the stray `"` from the MSO `div` rule, and scope the `font-size:0` rule more narrowly.
4. Raise mobile minimums to about 11–12px or more for the `.body-sm`, `.link-sm` and `.legal` classes.
