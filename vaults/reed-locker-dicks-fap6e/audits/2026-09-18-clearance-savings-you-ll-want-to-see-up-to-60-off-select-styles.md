# 💲 Clearance savings you'll want to see 💲 Up to 60% off select styles
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-18**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red-gradient "UP TO X% OFF SELECT CLEARANCE" hero DICK'S has run all month, now at 60% — the exact number the 9/18 "Not a drill" send and the 9/18 "60%" send in brand history already used. The white-box percentage callout is legible and the "Shop Now" button is clear, but nothing here differentiates this send from at least three other near-identical sends this week. Below the fold it's the standard kitchen-sink layout: category pills, HOKA, fleece, top-deals grid, picks grid — functional, generic, unremarkable.
- The hero itself executes cleanly (good contrast, no render bugs), but this is pure template reuse with a bumped number, and the subject line doesn't even mention the 60% headline — it's buried after a vague "savings you'll want to see" lead-in. Judgement: competent execution, zero differentiation, fatigue-inducing cadence.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 60% OFF" white-on-red callout is high-contrast and instantly readable.
- "Shop Now" is an unambiguous, well-placed primary CTA directly under the hero.
- Category pill row (Men's/Women's/Kids'/$25 and Under/Fan Shop/All Sale) gives clear secondary navigation.
- Product grids render cleanly with no broken images or overlapping text.
- Scorecard rewards banner and one-hour pickup line add credible loyalty/logistics context.

## What's weak

- This is the fourth-plus near-identical red-gradient clearance hero in a week-long stretch (9/12–9/18) — pure fatigue.
- Subject buries the actual offer; "clearance savings you'll want to see" is filler ahead of the real hook.
- No urgency/deadline anywhere in the hero or subhead ("while quantities last" is vague, not time-bound).
- Fine print below CTA ("Exclusions apply... Interim markdowns may have been taken... Prices as marked") signals a hedged, less-than-honest 60% claim.
- Wall of unrelated categories (golf, drinkware, fitness, fan shop) dilutes focus after the clearance hook.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the subject with the number instead of burying it — the 60% is the only real hook here and it's currently second-billed.
- 2. Add a real deadline (e.g., "ends Sunday") to the hero/subhead — "while quantities last" isn't credible urgency after a month of the same claim.
- 3. Vary the creative treatment (color, layout, model photo) instead of reusing the same red-gradient box for the fifth-plus time this stretch.
- 4. Trim the post-hero grid to 2-3 relevant categories tied to the clearance theme rather than golf/drinkware/fitness sprawl.
- 5. Move the exclusions/interim-markdown fine print out of immediate view or soften it — it undercuts the 60% claim right where the eye lands next.
