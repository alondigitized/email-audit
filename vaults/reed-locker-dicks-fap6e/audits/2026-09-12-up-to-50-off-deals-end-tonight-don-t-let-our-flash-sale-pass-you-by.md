# Up to 50% off deals end tonight! Don't let our Flash Sale pass you by...
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-12**
## Executive summary

- DICK'S ships a genuine countdown timer this time — "Final Hours 07:59:48" is a real ticking deadline, a step up from the vague "ends tonight" copy the brand usually leans on. But the hero art is broken: the word "FLASH" is cropped mid-letter ("FLAS") against the orange lightning-bolt background, so the single biggest visual element on the page reads as a rendering bug, not a sale banner.
- Below the fold it's the same structure as every DICK'S send since late August — one hero offer, a stacked second "Extra 25% Off" clearance card, a pill-shaped category nav, then a product grid. This particular combo (Flash Sale + Extra 25% clearance stack) is new relative to 9/11's single clearance push, so it's not pure reshuffle, but the cropped hero undercuts the improvement.
- Net: the countdown timer and dual-offer stack are real progress, but a broken hero graphic on the first screen a recipient sees is a hard cap on this email's ceiling.

## What's working

- Live countdown timer ("07:59:48") under "Final Hours" gives genuine, specific urgency — better than static "ends tonight" language.
- Two distinct, quantified offers are stacked (Flash Sale up to 50% off, Extra 25% off select clearance) rather than one vague "deals" banner.
- ScoreCard loyalty bar up top shows real point balance (300 Points + $10 Reward) with a direct "Shop To Earn Points" CTA — good personalization signal for members.
- Product grid at the bottom shows six specific, named SKUs (Samba OG, Nike Air Force 1, New Balance 9060) with images, giving something concrete to click beyond "Shop Now."

## What's weak

- The hero image is cropped/clipped — "FLASH" is cut off as "FLAS" at the frame edge, which reads as a layout bug on the most important visual in the email.
- Green "Shop Now" CTA button on the hero has low contrast against the busy orange lightning-bolt background, making it less scannable than it should be.
- Fine print ("Valid online only... offer may not be valid in conjunction with other sales") sits directly under both CTAs, which undercuts the "simple flash sale" pitch with exclusion language up front.
- Two nearly identical orange lightning-bolt panels stacked back-to-back (Flash Sale, then Extra 25%) make the layout visually repetitive rather than escalating.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fix the hero crop so "FLASH" renders in full — this is the single highest-impact fix since it's the first thing the eye hits and currently looks broken.
- 2. Increase contrast on the hero CTA button (white or black button instead of dark green on orange) so "Shop Now" pops immediately.
- 3. Differentiate the two orange panels visually (vary background color or graphic treatment) so the second offer doesn't read as a duplicate of the first.
- 4. Move exclusion/fine-print text below the product grid or shrink it further so it doesn't compete visually with the CTA.

- **Preheader Alt A:** `Plus an extra 25% off select clearance, both end at 11:59PM PT`
