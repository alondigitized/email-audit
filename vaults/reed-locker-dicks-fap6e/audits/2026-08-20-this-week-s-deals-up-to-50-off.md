# This week's deals = up to 50% off!!
**Score:** 6/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-08-20**
## Executive summary

- This is the same green-locker-room "UP TO 50% OFF" hero DICK'S has now run at least twice this week — near-identical to 8/16's "Up to 50% off awaits" and structurally the same sprawling digest as every other send since. Below the hero it sprawls through nine-plus category blocks (clothing/shoes, drinkware, team sports, golf, fan shop, outdoor, fitness) before a "check out these picks" product grid — this is the format's core problem, not this specific execution.
- The one genuinely new element is the "TODAY ONLY 3X POINTS" ScoreCard+ member block right under the hero — that's a real, time-bound, loyalty-specific offer, and it's the strongest single unit in the email. Everything after it is undifferentiated category-surfing with repeated "Up to X% Off Select ___" headers and no product-level specificity.
- Verdict: functional but fatigued. The subject and hero are a rerun, the body is the same everything-store scroll, and only the member-points block earns real attention.

## What's working

- The 3X Points ScoreCard+ block is visually distinct (green/black card graphic) and has real urgency ("TODAY ONLY") plus a specific, credible mechanic.
- Clean, consistent product photography throughout (flat-lays, lifestyle shots) with no broken images or overlapping text.
- Each category block has its own clear CTA button(s), so scanning is easy even if shallow.
- Footer/app-download and social-icon rows are intact and on-brand.

## What's weak

- Hero is a copy-paste of the 8/16 "Up to 50% off awaits" green locker-room creative — a subscriber who's opened three of these this month will recognize it instantly.
- No hero product or category leads the "up to 50%" claim — it's just a wordmark, so there's nothing to click on above the fold besides a generic "Shop Now."
- The body is nine near-identical "Up to X% Off Select ___" blocks in a row with no visual hierarchy differentiating a lead deal from filler.
- "Explore more deals and essentials" plus the Men's/Women's/Kids'/Shoes/Team Sports/Back-to-School row is pure navigation dressed as content, adding length without new information.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead the hero with an actual product or specific category (e.g. "Back-to-school cleats, up to 50% off") instead of a bare wordmark — give the eye something concrete to land on and click, especially since this exact green-locker hero has already run this week.
- 2. Cut the digest from nine category blocks to three or four, and visually promote the 3X Points loyalty block to the very top since it's the only truly time-bound, member-specific offer in the send.
- 3. Add one hero product photo + price under the "50% OFF" wordmark so Click-Through has something concrete before the scroll even starts.
- 4. Vary the subject/hero creative week over week — three "up to 50%" headlines in five days trains subscribers to stop opening.
