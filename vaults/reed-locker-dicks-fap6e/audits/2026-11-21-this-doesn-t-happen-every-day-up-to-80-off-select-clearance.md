# This doesn't happen every day 👉 up to 80% OFF select clearance 💸
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-21**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, loud 80% hero with a body that undercuts it. The red-and-gold "UP TO 80% OFF" block fills the first screen, and "Limited Time" and "Up to 80% Off Select Clearance Styles" sit under it with one dark red "Shop Now" button. You know the offer in about a second. It's the same 80% claim DICK'S ran on 2026-11-20 ("Up to 80% OFF? You read that right!"), now with a clearance angle.
- After the hero, the email dilutes itself. A second "EARLY BLACK FRIDAY SALE / Up to 50% Off / Ends Tonight" banner follows, then five category blocks at 40-50% off, then a product grid. That's about eight offers and eight "Shop Now" buttons. The "Ends Tonight" urgency is also attached to the 50% block, not to the 80% hero the subject sells.
- Judgement: a strong opener, but it loses focus on the same day as the "Last day!" send. The subject promises one clearance story and the body delivers a general Black Friday catalog.

## What's working

- The 80% hero is huge and high-contrast, with one clear CTA right below it.
- The pill navigation (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sale) gives fast paths into categories.
- Category blocks use real lifestyle photos with a consistent headline and button pattern, so they scan easily.
- The ScoreCard bar and the "Ends Tonight" tag add loyalty context and some urgency.
- The seasonal gift-pick product grid (Stanley, Callaway, Nike Kids') fits the holiday moment.

## What's weak

- The "Ends Tonight" urgency sits on the 50% Black Friday banner, not the 80% hero. The hero only says "Limited Time".
- The two stacked red hero banners (80% and Early Black Friday 50%) compete for the eye.
- The email is very long, with about eight near-identical "Shop Now" buttons, and the clearance offer appears only once.
- The hero's fine print is small and dense. "Select clearance styles only" and the "inventory may have been taken" disclaimers weaken the honesty of "up to 80%".
- The product grid and footer utility rows are tiny and cramped, and the product names are long. Nothing in the grid is priced.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the email to the clearance story: the 80% hero, three or four clearance product tiles with was/now prices, and one secondary Black Friday banner. This lifts the email more than any other change.
- 2. Put the deadline on the hero ("Ends tonight") and drop the duplicate Early Black Friday banner, or demote it.
- 3. Replace "Shop Now" on the hero with "Shop 80% off clearance".
- 4. Show real prices and percent-off tags on the product grid.
- 5. Shorten the legal copy, or move it below the fold with a clearer "details" link.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Clearance up to 80% off, ends tonight`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Up to 80% off clearance: last chance 💸`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Select clearance styles, one-hour store pickup. Ends at 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Men's, women's, kids' and golf clearance, plus 50% off drinkware.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email's visible markup is largely sound: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout tables, MSO conditionals and a hidden preheader spacer. The confirmed defects are 12 images with no alt text, an invalid JSON-LD block with leftover template placeholders, a CSS syntax error, and sub-10px mobile font sizes. The HTML source was truncated, and the header and link-probe checks were inconclusive because of the AgentMail relay.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Probe coverage is low.** 12 of 62 links were probed (about 19%) and 50 were skipped. No broken-link results were reported, so there is no confirmed link failure. The rest are unverified.
- **The open-tracking pixel has no alt text.** It is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a1fef62246950283369fb45280/7e8e6d8ba3bd4e0d00004c5a42963aa1`, hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`. It has no `width`/`height`/`alt`. This is low impact, but it is the first of the 12 QA alt warnings.
- **Click hrefs are not visible.** They fall outside the truncated source, so tracking redirects can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **12 images are missing `alt`**, confirmed by QA:
  - Content images, which are likely the clickable modules (scene7):
    - `1121-stw-11am-mens-clothing-shoes`
    - `1121-stw-11am-womens-clothing-shoes`
    - `1121-stw-11am-kids-clothing-shoes`
    - `50-off-banner-dks2026-1115-11`
    - `1118-stw-11am-fan-1`
    - `1121-stw-11am-golf`
  - Footer icons: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png`, `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - With images blocked or read by a screen reader, the offer and category modules carry no text. If these images sit inside `<a>` tags with no other text, those links have no accessible name. I couldn't confirm that from the truncated source.
- **The JSON-LD block is invalid.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` contains `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so Gmail will fail to parse the whole block.
  - It also contains unfilled template placeholders: `"image": ""` under the comment `DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`.
  - Other empty or stale values: `"description": ""`, `"discountCode": ""`, `"availabilityEnds": ""`, and `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`.
  - The closing comment `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` has no matching opener.
- **There is a CSS syntax error in the MSO conditional style.** `div {...; padding:0;"}` has a stray `"` before `}`. Outlook may drop that rule.
- **Mobile font sizes are very small at `max-width:480px`:** `.body-sm`/`.link-sm` 9px, `.legal` 8px, `.body-m` 11px. Many clients enforce a minimum text size or reflow these unpredictably.
- **Large image requests.** The scene7 URLs request `wid=1200&hei=1600`, and `1118-stw-11am-fan-1` uses `fmt=png-alpha`. Rendered sizes aren't visible in the truncated source, so payload weight is unverified.
- **No dark-mode handling is visible.** There is no `color-scheme` meta or `prefers-color-scheme` rule in the visible head, and the body is hardcoded to `#F5F3F1`/`#1E1B1B`. The source is truncated, so I can't rule it out below the head.
- **Minor:** the `<link rel="icon">` in the email body is ignored by mail clients.

5. Check the raw `.eml` headers directly to confirm `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and `Authentication-Results`. Don't rely on the relay-processed copy.
