# Lucky you! 💰 Get $20 off $100+ only in the app… but hurry, this ends tomorrow...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-14**
## Executive summary

- This is the third "$20 off $100+ in the app" send from DICK'S today. It follows "App exclusive starts now! 🙌 Get $20 off $100+ for 2 days ONLY" and "📢 Don't miss this app exclusive." The subject says "ends tomorrow," while the body says "Two Days Only." That contradiction undercuts the urgency the subject is selling. By the third send, a subscriber is tired of it.
- The body is organized better than the subject. A "Two Days Only" tag and a bold "App Exclusive: $20 Off $100 or More | ScoreCard+ $30 Off $100 or More" headline sit right under the hero. A red "Get Offer" button and a "Join ScoreCard+" button follow. The offer is specific and the loyalty upgrade is visible. But the hero is two dark phone mockups with little text, so the offer isn't the first thing the eye lands on. The headline has trailing asterisks, and the redemption terms ("must click on offer in My Account," "valid through 11/15/26 11:59PM PT") sit in small print below the button.
- Below the offer, the email becomes a long product catalog: adidas, Nike fleece, NCAA, golf drivers, hoops, bikes, gift picks. None of it relates to the app offer, and the page is very long. It's a clear send with a clumsy subject and a bloated body. Fine for the inbox, but it reads as a repeat.

## What's working

- The headline states the offer plainly: $20 off $100+, or $30 off with ScoreCard+. That's concrete and gives members a reason to upgrade.
- The "Two Days Only" red tag above the headline gives a time frame.
- The red "Get Offer" button stands out against the white background and is easy to find.
- The category pills (All Sale, Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop) make it easy to jump to a section.
- Product tiles have consistent "Shop Now" buttons and clear price hooks, such as "20% Off adidas Adizero EVO SL" and "$50 & Under Select Nike Fleece."

## What's weak

- The hero is two phone mockups with no readable offer text. The eye has to go to the headline below to learn what the email is about.
- "Ends tomorrow" in the subject conflicts with "Two Days Only" in the body. The urgency feels inconsistent.
- The email is a near-repeat of the earlier sends today, with the same $20 off $100+ offer and a reworded subject.
- The terms are tiny and buried: "no code needed," "click on offer in My Account," and the "***" footnote. This weakens the "honest offer" feel.
- The catalog below the offer is long and unrelated to it, so the email feels like a generic holiday sale.

## Recommendations

- 1. Replace the phone mockups with a hero that says "$20 OFF $100+ | $30 with ScoreCard+" in big type, plus a countdown or "Ends Sunday 11:59 PM PT."
- 2. Cut the catalog to 3-4 tiles, or tie it to the offer ("Shop what $100 gets you"). A shorter email would hold attention better.
- 3. Make the deadline consistent across the subject, hero and fine print.
- 4. Don't send three near-identical versions in one day. Send one, then a single last-chance reminder.
- 5. Put the key terms ("In-app only. Tap Get Offer to activate.") next to the button in readable type.
- **Subject Alt A:** `$20 off $100+ in the app — ends Sunday`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Last call: $30 off $100+ with ScoreCard+`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Open the app, tap Get Offer, and the savings apply at checkout.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `ScoreCard+ members save $30. No code needed. Ends 11/15 at 11:59 PM PT.`
