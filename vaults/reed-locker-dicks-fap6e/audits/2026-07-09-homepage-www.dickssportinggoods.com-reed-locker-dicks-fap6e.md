# Homepage snapshot · www.dickssportinggoods.com · 2026-07-09
**Score:** 1/10 · **Type:** Site journey · **2026-07-09**
## Executive summary

- I pulled up dickssportinggoods.com on my phone and got a dead end — literally. The site threw an error page telling me to try again in 12 hours. That's it. No homepage, no hero, no deals, no navigation. As someone with three kids burning through cleats every season and a ScoreCard account I actually use, this is a real problem.

## What's working

- The Dick's Sporting Goods logo is visible and correctly rendered — at least I know I landed on the right domain and didn't get phished.

## What's weak

- **The entire site is down.** "Oops, Something Went Wrong" with a 12-hour recovery window is a catastrophic visitor experience — no fallback content, no search, no category links.
- **The raw error string is visible to the user** — `Error: 0.842d3e17.1783592499.a73dd89f IP: 75.140.9.0` is exposed in bold on a customer-facing page. That's an internal debugging artifact that has no business being shown to a shopper.
- **Zero exit ramps.** A well-designed error page would offer at least a link to the app, a phone number, or a store locator. This one gives me nothing but a grey background and a request to wait 12 hours.
- **No ScoreCard or account recovery path.** I'm a loyalty member — if my account data is inaccessible, tell me that specifically, don't just throw a generic error.
- **"Try again after 12 hours"** is a trust-killing message. It signals systemic outage, not a brief hiccup.

## Recommendations

- **Build a branded maintenance page** with nav links to the mobile app, a store locator, and a ScoreCard customer service number — don't leave people with a blank grey screen and a 12-hour wait.
- **Strip the raw error code** from the customer-facing page immediately — that output belongs in a server log, not in a white card in front of paying customers.
- **Add a real-time status update link** (something like "Check status.dickssportinggoods.com") so loyal customers aren't blindly hitting refresh for half a day.
- **Push an email or ScoreCard app notification** to loyalty members during outages — if I've opted into comms, that's the moment to use them.
