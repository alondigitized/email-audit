# WOW! 🚨 Up to 80% OFF select clearance? 🚨 That's right — score now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-22**
## Executive summary

- This is the 80% clearance hero DICK'S ran on 11/20 and again earlier on 11/21, in a new package. The red-and-gold "UP TO 80% OFF" block fills the first screen. One "Shop Now" button sits under "Up to 80% Off Select Clearance Styles". The offer lands in about a second.
- Below the hero, the email stops being a clearance send. It carries a full Black Friday module, a $100 & Under shoes tile, fleece, pajama pants, wellness, golf shoes, Wilson balls, store hours and a gift-pick grid. A subscriber who opened for 80% off gets a general catalog. The subject also promises more than the body delivers: the fine print says "select clearance styles only, while quantities last".
- Judgement: a strong hero on a cluttered, repetitive send. It is a solid 6–7 for anyone shopping sporting goods and a weak one for anyone who wanted clearance specifically.

## What's working

- The "UP TO 80% OFF" block is huge and high-contrast, and it is the first thing you see.
- The single green "Shop Now" button under the hero headline makes the primary action obvious.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', $25 & Under, Fan Shop, All Sales) give a quick way to browse.
- The Black Friday module ("STARTS NOW", up to 50% off) matches the current campaign and the 11/22 "Black Friday couldn't wait" send.
- The gift-pick grid with product images adds specific items to click.

## What's weak

- The 80% promise gets about one screen. The remaining nine or so modules dilute it with other offers (50%, 40%, 25%, $30 & under).
- Two stacked heroes (80% clearance, then Black Friday 50%) compete, and the 50% module undercuts the 80% claim.
- The fine print under the 80% hero is small and dense, and "select" styles and limited sizes are not shown. No clearance products appear anywhere, so the 80% is never proven.
- It's the third 80% clearance send in three days, after 11/20 ("You read that right!") and 11/21 ("This doesn't happen every day"). The subject's "WOW!" and "That's right" repeat that framing and feel fatigued.
- The email runs very long with no visible social proof or reviews.

## Recommendations

- 1. Fill the clearance section with 4–6 actual 80%-off products at their sale prices, and cut the unrelated modules below Black Friday (wellness, golf shoes, Wilson, store hours). This single change would help the most.
- 2. Name the real deadline in the hero ("Ends 11/28" or a countdown) in place of the generic "Limited Time".
- 3. Make the clearance CTA specific: "Shop 80% Off Clearance" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Move the Black Friday module to a separate send, or put it beneath the clearance products.
- 5. Vary the creative from the 11/20 and 11/21 versions, for example by leading with a hero product and its price drop.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Clearance just got cheaper: up to 80% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Shoes, apparel and gear marked down. Sizes go fast.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus early Black Friday deals on top brands.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML structure is sound (`lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` layout table, responsive media queries, bulletproof preheader spacer). It has 14 images with no alt text, a malformed Gmail promotion-annotation JSON-LD block carrying template placeholders, and compliance and authentication headers that couldn't be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- **Link probing:** 12 of 64 links were probed and 52 skipped by priority. No broken or redirecting links were reported, but the unprobed footer, utility and social links are unverified.
- **Open-tracking pixel:** `<img src='https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a204010d24b1a7a06e96c65848/9de2e7e6b1ba454c00004c5a42963aa1' style="display:none; max-height:0…">` is hidden with `display:none`. That is expected for a pixel, so the QA missing-alt warning on it is a false positive.
- **Tracking domain:** The tracking host `app.dickssportinggoods.com` differs from the sending domain `e.dcsg.com`. Both are brand-owned, so this is informational only.
- **Image payload:** Scene7 banners request `wid=1200&hei=1600&qlt=100&fmt=jpeg` for a `max-width:600px` container. This is likely oversized (about 2x), but file weights weren't measured, so it is unconfirmed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text, content images (8):**
  - `100andunder1122footaccess`
  - `familyfleece1122vertbrand`
  - `2026-1122-dks-stw-fanshop-cyber8-banner-pants3`
  - `2026-1122-dks-stwd-fitness-cyber-8-bid-3`
  - `shoes-2026-1122-dks-swd-golf-cyber-8-bids-banner`
  - `1122-stw-team-sports-cyber8-balls`
  - `1101storefront`
  - the hero image (see the QA list)
  
  With images blocked, or in a screen reader, these banners render as blank space or are announced by filename. This is the most consequential issue in the email.
- **Missing alt text, footer icons (5):** `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`, all on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com`. If a link's only content is an icon, that link has no accessible name.
- **Invalid CSS in the Outlook conditional:** In `<!--[if (gte mso 9)|(IE)]><style>`, the rule `div {…padding:0;"}` contains a stray `"`. Outlook may drop that declaration or rule. The same rule sets `font-size:0` on every `div`, which is risky for any text inside a `div`.
- **Gmail annotation JSON-LD is invalid:** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JavaScript `//` comments, which are not valid JSON, so the whole block will fail to parse.
  - `DiscountOffer` has an empty `description` and `discountCode`, and an empty `availabilityEnds`.
  - `availabilityStarts` is the stale template value `2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00`.
  - `PromotionCard.image` is `""`.
  - The closing `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` has no matching opening comment in the visible source.
  
  Gmail will ignore the annotation, so the promo-tab card is lost.
- **Leftover template comments:** `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` and `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!` are shipped in production source.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. No unrendered tokens (`{{…}}`, `%%…%%`, `[FIRSTNAME]`) are visible in the provided source. The source is truncated, so the rest isn't covered.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **`List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) and `Authentication-Results`:** All three are absent, but the QA tool itself attributes this to the AgentMail relay stripping headers. These are unverified, not confirmed defects. Check the raw message at the origin MTA before treating them as findings.
- **Footer unsubscribe link:** The QA probe notes one was always included, but the footer is outside the truncated HTML. The unsubscribe link and the physical postal address couldn't be verified in source.
- **Sender identity:** From `"DICK'S | Clearance" <DSG@e.dcsg.com>` is consistent with the brand and is not an issue.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable from the provided data. No campaign link `href`s or UTM parameters appear in the visible source, and QA reported no probe failures. The landing page contents were not captured. No defect is claimed.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Add descriptive `alt` text to all 8 content banners (for example, "Up to 80% off clearance"). Add `alt=""` to decorative icons, or real alt text where the icon is the only link content.
2. Fix or remove the Gmail JSON-LD block.
   - Remove `//` comments and the empty and stale fields.
   - Either populate the `DiscountOffer` and `PromotionCard` fields or drop the block.
   - Fix the unbalanced `GMAIL PREVIEW CODE` comment.
3. Remove the template comments (`Replace/Paste SSL Here`, `DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO…`) from the build.
4. Fix the stray `"` in the MSO conditional `div` rule. Reconsider the blanket `font-size:0` on `div`.
5. Request `wid=600` (or 1200 for retina, with a sensible `qlt`) on Scene7 URLs to reduce weight.
6. Verify `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post` and SPF/DKIM/DMARC from a direct (non-relay) seed. Until then, treat those QA warnings as inconclusive.
7. Extend link probing to the footer and unsubscribe targets, and capture UTM parameters for the continuity check.
