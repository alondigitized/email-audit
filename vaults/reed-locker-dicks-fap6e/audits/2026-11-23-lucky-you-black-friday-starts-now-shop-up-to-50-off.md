# Lucky you! Black Friday starts NOW ‼️ Shop up to 50% OFF...
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-23**
## Executive summary

- This is a competent, well-built Black Friday module stack, but it's the sixth near-identical send in three days. It follows the 11/23 "Black Friday starting now 🤝 Biggest Sale of the Year" email almost beat for beat. The green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero lands instantly, "Black Friday Starts Now" sits under it, and the first screen has one red Shop Now button. Below that is a scannable run of product tiles: Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 at $109.98, Crocs and HEYDUDE, Solo Stove fire pits and golf electronics.
- The weakness is focus. The hero says 50%, a second hero further down shouts "UP TO 70% OFF," and the tiles quote 25%, 15% and $120 off. Nine near-identical "Shop Now" buttons compete with each other. The email reads like a catalog, not a single decision.
- Judgement: a solid execution of a template the subscriber has now seen repeatedly. Fatigue, not craft, is the problem.

## What's working

- The hero is huge and legible. "UP TO 50% OFF" in gold on green is readable at thumbnail size, and "Black Friday Starts Now" confirms the subject.
- The deadline is credible. The fine print gives specific dates and times (through 11/28 at 11:59 PM PT), and "Limited Time" labels sit above the key offers.
- The Brooks Adrenaline GTS 25 tile gives a specific product at a specific price, $109.98. It's the most concrete click target in the email.
- The ScoreCard loyalty strip sits at the top, and the "Order Today. Spoil It Tomorrow." banner mentions same-day delivery and one-hour pickup.
- The holiday store hours module is useful and seasonal, and it explains the Thanksgiving closure.

## What's weak

- Two competing headline discounts, 50% and 70%, appear in separate hero blocks. This dilutes the "Biggest Sale of the Year" claim.
- Every tile uses the same generic "Shop Now" button, with no verb-and-noun specificity.
- The email is very long, with about ten modules before the footer. The tail (gift picks, services, app download) is unlikely to be seen.
- The fine print is visible but small and covers exclusions, so the honesty of "up to" is hard to judge at a glance.
- There's no social proof, such as ratings or "best seller" cues, anywhere in the tiles.
- The email repeats the same creative and offer as the previous five sends, so it adds no new reason to open.

## Recommendations

- 1. **Differentiate this send.** Give the next send a distinct hook, such as a doorbuster, a countdown, or a category-specific day. Right now it's the sixth identical "Black Friday starts now" email.
- 2. **Pick one headline offer.** Drop the 70% block, or move it below the fold and label it "Clearance."
- 3. **Trim to 4–5 modules** and put the Brooks running shoe in the top spot. Cut the lower-value tiles.
- 4. **Replace generic buttons with specific copy:** "Shop Running Shoes," "Shop Clothing Deals," "Shop Golf Tech."
- 5. **Add ratings or a "Top Pick" badge** to product tiles.
- 6. **Add real preheader copy,** because none is visible.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Brooks running shoes $109.98 + up to 50% off`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Doorbusters live: 50% off through Friday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Running shoes, Nike and Crocs deals, plus free same-day delivery on eligible orders.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Sale ends 11/28 at 11:59 PM PT. Your ScoreCard points still earn on deals.`
