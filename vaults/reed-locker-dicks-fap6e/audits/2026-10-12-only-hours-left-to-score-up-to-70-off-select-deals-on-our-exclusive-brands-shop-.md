# ⏳ Only hours left to score up to 70% off select deals on our exclusive brands... shop now!
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-10-12**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid closing-night send for DICK'S 24-hour sale. A red "Final Hours 07:59:29" countdown sits above a big "24-HOUR SALE" hero with the exclusive-brand logos (DSG, CALIA, VRST, Maxfli, Walter Hagen, Quest). Directly below are "Up to 70% Off Our Exclusive Brands" and a green Shop Now button. The offer and the deadline land together, and the eye goes to them first.
- The weakness is what follows. After the hero, the email becomes a long scroll of seven or more product tiles with different offers: $100 & Under shoes, $29.98 fleece, HydroJug, $10 Walter Hagen polos, an extra 25% off coolers, and a BOGO on socks. It then repeats a second "70% OFF" clearance hero, which is the creative from the 10/11 "Did somebody say NEW markdowns?!" send. The second hero dilutes the "exclusive brands" message and the urgency. This is the third near-identical 70%-off send in 48 hours, following 10/11 and the 10/12 "24-hour SALE" email earlier today.
- Judgement: strong top third, bloated and repetitive below it. It will perform fine on urgency alone, but it's a fatigue risk for a subscriber who has seen this offer all week.

## What's working

- The live countdown ("Final Hours 07:59:29") makes the urgency credible and visible before any copy is read.
- The hero is large and unambiguous: "24-HOUR SALE" with the exclusive-brand logos, then "Up to 70% Off" and a clear Shop Now button.
- The category pills (CALIA, VRST, DSG, Alpine Design, All Sale, All Clearance) let a shopper jump straight to a brand.
- The product tiles use real photography with specific prices ($29.98 fleece, $10 polos, extra 25% off coolers), which gives concrete value.
- The header carries ScoreCard points and order-today shipping messaging, which gives a loyalty and logistics nudge.

## What's weak

- The second "UP TO 70% OFF" clearance hero partway down repeats the same number with different eligibility. It reads as two competing offers and weakens the "exclusive brands only" framing.
- The body has too many offers in one email: shoes, fleece, drinkware, polos, coolers, socks, the holiday shop, and picks. The deadline gets lost.
- The product tiles all use small, pale grey Shop Now buttons, which are much weaker than the green hero CTA.
- The Holiday Shop block and the "Check out these picks" grid (Samba, Nike hoodie, VRST, GOAT USA) are off-message for a final-hours exclusive-brand sale.
- The fine print under the hero is small and easy to miss. It says the sale is online only, ends 10/12 at 11:59 PM ET, and has in-store exclusions.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three or four tiles, all exclusive-brand items (DSG fleece, VRST, CALIA, Walter Hagen). Remove the second 70% hero, the Holiday Shop and the generic picks.
- 2. Put the deadline in plain text next to the hero CTA, e.g. "Ends tonight 11:59 PM ET". The countdown is good but the date is buried in fine print.
- 3. Make the product tile buttons match the green primary CTA and name the product, e.g. "Shop DSG Fleece".
- 4. Show a sale price on every tile (was/now or % off) so it reads as a deal rather than a catalog.
- 5. Space out sends. A sale this size could run as one reminder, not three overlapping versions in two days.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Ends tonight: up to 70% off DSG, CALIA & VRST`
- **Subject Alt B:** `⏳ 8 hours left: 70% off our exclusive brands`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `DSG fleece $29.98, Walter Hagen polos $10 — online only, ends 11:59 PM ET.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Last call on CALIA, VRST and DSG deals before the sale clock hits zero.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The HTML is structurally sound (role="presentation" tables, `lang="en"`, `<title>`, viewport meta, MSO conditionals). The automated QA shows a 50% pass rate with one FAIL, a near-empty plain-text part. Most compliance and authentication gaps are unverifiable because the relay probably stripped the headers. The HTML source was also truncated, so the footer and later links weren't inspected.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- All links are wrapped in ESP click-tracking redirects, for example `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/mclick?params=b.v1...`. The parameters are opaque, so the final destinations and any UTM values can't be confirmed from the source.
- The QA run probed only 12 of 66 links and skipped 54 (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). It reported no broken links among those 12, but the other 54 are unverified.
- The tracking pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...`. It is present and uses the same sending domain as the click tracking, which is consistent.
- The header logo link carries `data-nl-type="DEEPLINK"` and `class="arc-link"`. These are ESP attributes and not a defect.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text on 13 images** (QA WARN):
  - Content images on `s7d6.scene7.com/is/image/DSGAEMSites/`: `lifestyleshoes1012stwd4`, `2classicfleece1012`, `midnight-2026-1012-d`, `2ahaganpoloshirt`, `cooler1012stwd4`, `cozycabin1012stwd4`, `2026-1012-dks-swd-gift-guide-banner`.
  - Icon images: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`.
  - The tracking pixel (`open`).
  - If these images carry the offers or are linked, image-blocking clients and screen readers will get no content or link labels. The QA check doesn't say whether the attribute is absent or empty. The icons and pixel are likely decorative and should have an explicit `alt=""`.
- **Leftover editor and template artifacts in the markup:**
  - `<body class="is-html-mode" data-contenteditable="true">`
  - The comment `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` in `td#ssl`.
  - These are harmless for rendering but show the template wasn't cleaned before send.
- **Preheader spacer:** `#pht-spacer` is hidden correctly (`display:none; mso-hide:all`). Its padding is `&zwnj;&nbsp;` repeated. The visible preheader text, if any, is outside the truncated source, so I can't assess it.
- **Fonts:** `Archivo` loads from `fonts.gstatic.com` inside `@media screen` with an `Arial, sans-serif` fallback. This is fine.
- The `<link rel="icon">` in the email `<head>` has no effect in email clients and can be removed.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved merge tokens (`{{ }}`, `%%%%`, `[FIRST_NAME]`). The truncated portion wasn't checked.

## 5. Compliance (CAN-SPAM, unsubscribe, authentication headers)
- **Plain-text fallback (QA FAIL):** the text part is 9 characters. For a multipart email this is a real deliverability and accessibility problem, since spam filters weigh HTML-only or near-empty text parts. It could be a capture artifact, but the QA data confirms the failure.
- **List-Unsubscribe and List-Unsubscribe-Post (QA WARN):** both are absent. The QA note says the AgentMail relay may not capture them, so this is unconfirmed rather than a confirmed defect. Gmail and Yahoo require both headers (RFC 8058) for bulk senders. DICK'S is a large sender on `em.dcsg.com`, so they are likely present at origin.
- **Authentication-Results (QA WARN):** absent, as expected through the relay. SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` can't be assessed from this data.
- **Footer unsubscribe link and physical address:** these weren't visible in the truncated source. The QA run says an unsubscribe link is always probed, but it reports no result for it.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity (UTM params, landing page alignment)
Not verifiable. The destinations are hidden behind `mclick` redirects, and the QA run didn't surface resolved landing URLs or UTM parameters. Nothing was flagged for the 12 probed links.

## 7. Recommendations
1. **Fix the plain-text part.** Confirm that the sent message has a full `text/plain` alternative (the QA shows 9 characters), and regenerate it from the HTML if it doesn't.
2. **Add alt text.**
   - Add descriptive alt text to the seven content/offer images, including the offer and product name.
   - Set `alt=""` on the five icons and the tracking pixel.
3. **Verify the headers at origin.** Check the raw message for `List-Unsubscribe`, `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass for `em.dcsg.com`. The relay can't confirm them.
4. **Widen link coverage.** Resolve the 54 skipped links, including the footer unsubscribe link, and record final URLs and UTM parameters so continuity can be checked.
5. **Clean the template.** Remove `is-html-mode`, `data-contenteditable`, the `Replace/Paste SSL Here` comment, and the `rel="icon"` link.
6. **Re-run on untruncated HTML.** The footer, address and remaining links weren't inspected.
