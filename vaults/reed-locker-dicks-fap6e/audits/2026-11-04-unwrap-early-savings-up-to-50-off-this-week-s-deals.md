# Unwrap early savings: up to 50% OFF this week's deals! 🎁
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a clean, competent DICK'S promo. The red-free green argyle hero with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" lands first, and a single Shop Now button sits right under it. The new seasonal green, and the 🎁 and "Unwrap early savings" copy, are a welcome break from the red "Holiday Deal Dash" creative of the past three days. Below the hero the email turns into a long product catalog: shoes, Armour hoodie, Nike fleece, golf electronics, a ping-pong table, baseball gloves, cardio equipment, a credit card pitch, and a gift-picks grid.
- The weakness is focus. The hero says 50% off, but the body is a grab bag of $29.98, $39.98, $100 and $269.98 price points. Only "Up to 45% off" gloves echoes the headline. Nothing says which products are actually 50% off. The deadline is a vague "limited time", not a date. This lands just after the 2026-11-03 "FINAL HOURS" send, so a subscriber who just watched a countdown now sees the urgency reset to nothing. The ScoreCard credit card block also eats prime real estate for a financial product.
- Judgement: solid, but forgettable. It does its job without giving a reason to click now.

## What's working

- The giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on deep green is the unmistakable first read.
- One red Shop Now button directly under the hero, with a clear next step.
- Specific sale prices on tiles ($29.98 Armour hoodie, $39.98 Nike Phoenix Fleece, $269.98 table tennis table) give concrete reasons to click.
- The fresh green gift-season palette signals a new campaign beat, not a repeat of the red Deal Dash.
- The category pill row (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) is a quick route into a category.

## What's weak

- The deadline is only "limited time", with no date or countdown, after a stretch of loud "FINAL HOURS" urgency.
- The 50% headline isn't tied to any product. The tiles show mixed offers ($100 & Under, Up to 45%, Up to $100 off), which dilutes the hero.
- The email is very long and every tile repeats the same Shop Now button.
- The credit card block ("$30 Bonus Reward") interrupts the shopping flow midway down.
- Fine print is dense at the bottom, and the "online only" and "while quantities last" caveats are small and easy to miss.

## Recommendations

- 1. Tie the hero to a dated deadline and name the 50%-off products. For example, "Up to 50% off through Sunday" with the hero tile showing the actual best 50%-off item.
- 2. Cut the tile count by about a third. Keep shoes, apparel, and one gift-focused row. Move the credit card block to the bottom.
- 3. Replace the repeated "Shop Now" with specific verbs and nouns, such as "Shop Nike Fleece" or "Shop Golf Deals".
- 4. Put the gift-picks grid higher, since the subject leads with "Unwrap", and add price labels to the grid items.
- 5. Space out sends. This is the fourth DICK'S promo in about 48 hours.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Gift deals start now: up to 50% off through Sunday 🎁`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Early holiday gifts, up to 50% off`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, Under Armour, and golf gear from $29.98. Ends Sunday.`

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **[FAIL] Plain-text fallback is 9 chars.** The text/plain part is effectively empty. Clients and filters that use the text part, and users who read plain text, get nothing. Empty text parts can also hurt spam scoring. If AgentMail's relay stripped the part, this is an artifact of capture. The QA data doesn't say which it is.
- **Missing `alt` on 12 images:**
  - Category tiles: `shoes-2026-1104-d`, `mens-2026-1104-d`, `womens-2026-1104-d`, `golf-2026-1104-d`, `table-tennis-2026-1104-d`, `bbsb-2026-1104-d`, `fitness-2026-1104-d`. These are `s7d6.scene7.com` JPEGs. They likely carry the category name and offer text as image content, so with images blocked or read by a screen reader, that content is lost. If they're wrapped in `<a>`, the links also have no accessible name. The truncated source doesn't show the wrapping.
  - Icons: `icon-vc-sms-1`, `icon-vc-sportsmatter`, `icon-vc-bpg-1`, `icon-vc-favorite-1`, `icon-location-2025-1`. Where they're informational, they need `alt`. Where they're decorative, they need `alt=""`.
