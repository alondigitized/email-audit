# Check off their wish list with up to 50% off select clothing & shoes ✅
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-04**
## Executive summary

- This is a DICK'S catch-all promo. The hero is strong: a deep green argyle panel with a giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF", a model in an orange polo, and a Nike sneaker. A single red Shop Now button sits right under it. The eye lands on the offer first, and the green seasonal palette matches the brand's recent sends.
- After the hero, the email turns into a long product catalog. It has a golf shoe deal, NHL gear, Owala, BOGO socks, basketball hoops, SaunaBox, a second giant "50% OFF" banner and a six-item gift grid. That is about nine competing CTAs, each with a different offer. The "wish list" subject promises a gift-oriented email, but the body reads like a clearance sweep.
- It's competent but diffuse, and it's the 4th or 5th "up to 50%" DICK'S send this week. The hero would carry a tighter email.

## What's working

- The hero is unmistakable: giant gold "UP TO 50% OFF" on green argyle, with one red Shop Now button.
- The top bar gives a concrete, credible urgency and service line: "Order Today, Get It Tomorrow" with one-hour store pickup.
- The green pill nav (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) lets a shopper jump to their category.
- Seasonal cues fit the moment: Owala Holiday Collection, cozy socks and a gift picks grid.
- The ScoreCard points strip at the top shows member status.

## What's weak

- Nine or more modules with different offers split attention. The "50% off" hero has to compete with BOGO, 25% off NHL, $500 off SaunaBox and $50 off golf shoes.
- The second giant "UP TO 50% OFF" banner mid-email repeats the hero. It feels like filler.
- The subject promises a "wish list" gift angle, but the hero shows a man in a polo and a running shoe, not a gift. Nothing in the hero says gifting.
- The hero says "Select Clothing & Shoes" and "Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST & more" with no price or product detail. The offer is vague.
- The fine print and disclaimers are small and scattered, with an asterisk on the "BOGO Free†" socks. The BOGO terms are hard to read.

## Recommendations

- 1. Cut the body to three or four modules, and make the "wish list" idea real. Lead with a gift-focused hero, or move the gift picks grid right under the hero.
- 2. Remove the duplicate "50% OFF" banner. Use that space for the gift grid, with prices and "Add to cart" style CTAs.
- 3. Make hero CTA copy specific, such as "Shop 50% off Nike" instead of "Shop Now".
- 4. Show a few named hero products with sale prices in the hero module.
- 5. Vary the cadence. This follows a string of near-identical "up to 50% off" sends, so give it a distinct angle, such as a gift deadline.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Their wish list, checked off. Up to 50% off ✅`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Nike, CALIA & VRST deals plus BOGO socks. Order today, get it tomorrow.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Gift picks from $20, with one-hour store pickup on most orders.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary

However, the QA run found a failed plain-text fallback (9 chars) and 11 images without alt text, including the three large banners. The header-level compliance and authentication checks could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- 12 material links were probed and 51 were skipped (footer, utility and social links are deprioritized). The QA output lists no failed or redirecting probes, so no broken links are confirmed. The 51 skipped links are unverified.
- The open pixel is `https://data.em.dcsg.com/ee/v1/open?params=b.v1...&pid=v1...&tuid=6aea4887364aac4e3839ec60&configId=512ffb51-...`, sent as a 1x1 `<img>`. It carries a per-recipient `tuid`, which is normal ESP behavior. It has no `alt` attribute and no `style="display:block"`, so some clients may render a gap.
- CTA anchors use `class="arc-link"` and `data-nl-type="DEEPLI…"` (truncated in the source). The tracked-redirect domain and final destinations can't be confirmed from the truncated HTML.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt (QA WARN, 11 images).** Content images with no alt text:
  - `nhl-2026-1104-swd-4pm`
  - `owalaholiday`
  - `1030-dks-accessories-cozy-cabin-banner`

- **Large image payload.** `wid=1200&hei=1600` at `qlt=100` is very heavy for a 600px container. This is a performance concern, not a confirmed defect.

## 4. Personalization & Merge Tokens
No issues found. The visible source has no unresolved tokens such as `{{...}}`, `%%...%%`, or `[FIRSTNAME]`. The HTML is truncated, so the rest of the body was not inspected.

## 5. Compliance
- **[FAIL] Plain-text part is 9 chars.** It is effectively empty, so there is no usable multipart/alternative text version. This hurts spam scoring and accessibility. It could be an artifact of the AgentMail relay, but treat it as a real issue until the raw MIME is checked.
- **[WARN] `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post` (RFC 8058) not found.** The QA note says the relay may strip these headers. Without them, one-click unsubscribe is unconfirmed. Gmail and Yahoo require both for bulk senders.
- **[WARN] No `Authentication-Results`.** SPF, DKIM and DMARC alignment for `em.dcsg.com` can't be confirmed from this data.
- An unsubscribe link was probed (the QA process always includes one) with no failure reported. The physical postal address is not visible in the truncated source, so it is unverified.

## 6. Email-to-Site Continuity
The truncated source and the QA data show no UTM parameters or landing-page results. Nothing can be confirmed or flagged. Deep-link redirects use the `data-nl-type="DEEPLI…"` attribute, so the final URLs would need a full-source or redirect-chain check.

## 7. Recommendations
1. Check the raw MIME from the original sender for a `text/plain` part of reasonable length. If it is truly 9 chars, generate a proper text version with offers and links.
2. Check the raw headers for `List-Unsubscribe` and `List-Unsubscribe-Post: List-Unsubscribe=One-Click`, and for SPF/DKIM/DMARC pass results. These were not captured by the relay, so this may be a tooling gap, not a sender defect.
3. Add descriptive `alt` to the five banner images. Use `alt=""` on the decorative icons, or give each icon its label text. Add `alt=""` and `style="display:block"` to the tracking pixel.
4. Strip `class="is-html-mode"` and `data-contenteditable="true"` from `<body>`.
5. Reduce Scene7 image requests to about 2x the rendered width (for example `wid=1200` with `qlt=80`) to cut payload.
6. Re-run link probing with the full HTML, including the 51 skipped links, to confirm redirect chains and UTM coverage.
