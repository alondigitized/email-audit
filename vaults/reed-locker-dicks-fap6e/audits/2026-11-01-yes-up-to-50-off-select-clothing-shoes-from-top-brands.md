# YES!! Up to 50% off select clothing & shoes from top brands ➡️
**Score:** 7/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-11-01**
## Executive summary

- This is a solid, conventional DICK'S weekly-deals send. The green-and-gold "UP TO 50% OFF" hero with a red "Shop Now" button is the clear anchor. Below it, "Select Clothing & Shoes / Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST & more" tells you exactly what the offer covers. The subject's "clothing & shoes from top brands" matches the hero, so the promise carries through.
- The email then drifts into a holiday catalog: category pills, two tiles of six categories, a gift-budget block, and a six-item gift grid. The 50% hero is a few inches from the top, but the rest has no offer attached. A shopper who came for apparel has to scroll past drinkware, golf, NFL, bats and treadmills with no prices. The "YES!!" subject also arrives in a day when this brand has already sent several clearance and 50%-off emails. This is the fifth one in a row with a similar offer. The earlier 10/30 "Savings to fall for" and "No tricks, just DEALS!" sends ran the same up-to-50% beat.
- Verdict: a competent send, with the hero doing the work and the lower two-thirds underperforming. It earns a mid-range score, not a high one.

## What's working

- The giant "UP TO 50% OFF" hero is readable in a glance, with a clear single red "Shop Now" button right under it.
- "Select Clothing & Shoes: Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST & more" names real brands, which gives the discount credibility.
- The top strip ("Order Today. Sport It Tomorrow." with one-hour pickup and same-day delivery) adds a useful fulfilment benefit.
- The ScoreCard bar ("300 Points = $10 Reward") surfaces loyalty value near the top.
- The category pills (Men's, Women's, Kids', Shoes, Fan Shop, Holiday Shop) give an easy route for people who skip the hero.
- Layout is clean, with no broken images or overlapping text that I can see.

## What's weak

- Almost nothing below the hero shows a price or discount. The gift grid is just product names, with no price, no sale tag and no rating.
- The email splits into three competing themes: the 50% apparel sale, holiday gifts, and generic category tiles. That dilutes the main offer.
- There is no deadline or end date anywhere I can see. "YES!!" and the arrow imply urgency that the body never backs up.
- The hero collage doesn't show the named brands. Nike appears only in the sneaker, and the apparel shots are small and unlabeled.
- The product grid mixes unrelated items (a gummy-bear toy, a golf-ball advent calendar, UGG slippers), so no persona is clearly served.
- The "Plus, shop more top holiday deals" tiles have tiny labels and no offer, so they read as filler.
- The legal and fine-print footer is dense and small.

## Recommendations

- 1. Put a deadline on the hero, such as "Ends Sunday" or "This weekend only", and repeat it in the preheader. That is the single biggest lift because the email currently has no urgency.
- 2. Replace the holiday-tile section with real products from the sale, each showing a was/now price and the % off. Use Nike, CALIA and VRST items that match the hero.
- 3. Cut the gift grid or move it below a sale grid. Two offers in one email split attention.
- 4. Show the brand logos (Nike, CALIA, VRST) in the hero, so the "top brands" claim is visible.
- 5. Trim the subject: drop "YES!!" and the arrow emoji, and lead with the offer.
- **Subject Alt A:** `Up to 50% off Nike, CALIA & VRST`
- **Subject Alt B:** `Clothing & shoes up to 50% off: ends Sunday`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `Save on Nike, CALIA, VRST & more. Free same-day pickup on orders today.`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Hoodies, sneakers and layers for fall, marked down through Sunday.`

## Technical Audit

## 1. Technical Summary
The email is a Epsilon-sent DSG template (`DSG@e.dcsg.com`). The visible source has no broken links or merge-token errors. It has two real defects: 14 images lack `alt` attributes, and the Gmail JSON-LD block is invalid and unfilled. The HTML was truncated, so the footer, link `href`s and unsubscribe markup could not be verified.

## 2. Link & Tracking Issues
- QA probed 12 material links and skipped 50, and reported no failures. Coverage is partial, so the 50 skipped links are untested.
- The open-tracking pixel is `https://app.dickssportinggoods.com/O/v61000001a197ed5aef9892906e965fc958/137c37dc99b7450000004c5a42963aa1`. It is hidden with `display:none; max-height:0; mso-hide:all`, which is correct for a pixel. It has no `width`/`height` attributes and no `alt`.
- The truncated source doesn't show the `href`s, so click-tracking wrappers and redirect behaviour can't be assessed.

## 3. Rendering & Accessibility
- **Missing alt text (real issue).** 13 visible images have no `alt`. Seven are the `scene7.com` category and gift-guide tiles: `drinkware1101stwdcondensed`, `accessories1101stwdcondensed`, `golf1101stwdcondensed`, `fanshop1101stwdcondensed`, `teamsports1101stwdcondensed`, `fitness1101stwdcondensed` and `gift-guide-2026-1101-d`. The other five are footer icons on `dsg.images.harmony.epsilon.com/.../footers/`: `icon-vc-sms.png`, `icon-value-sportsmatter--light.png`, `icon-vc-bpg.png`, `icon-vc-favorite.png` and `icon-location-2025.png`.
  - If the tiles are linked, screen readers and image-blocked clients get no accessible name for them.
  - Footer icons should at least carry `alt=""` if decorative.
  - The 14th flagged image is the hidden tracking pixel, so it's low priority.
- **Invalid JSON-LD.** The `<script type="application/ld+json">` block contains JS-style `//` comments (`// GIF & WEBP images are not supported...`, `// DESIGNER, ADD YOUR PROMO IMAGE URL BELOW!`). JSON doesn't allow comments, so the block won't parse. Gmail will ignore it and the promo annotations won't render.
- **Unfilled template fields in that block:**
  - `"image": ""`
  - `"discountCode": ""`
  - `"description": ""`
  - `"availabilityEnds": ""`
  - `"availabilityStarts": "2020-03-06T06:00:00-04:00"`, a stale 2020 date
- **Template leftovers.**
  - `<!--Replace/Paste SSL Here-->` is still in the source.
  - `<!--/GMAIL PREVIEW CODE-->` is a closing comment with no matching opener.
- **MSO CSS syntax error.** The conditional style block has a stray quote and brace: `div {...padding:0;"}`. Outlook may drop that rule. It also sets `font-size:0` on all `div`s, which can collapse text in `div`s that rely on inherited sizing.
- Positives: `lang="en"`, `role="presentation"` on layout tables, a viewport meta tag, a font fallback stack (`Archivo, Arial, sans-serif`) and a preheader spacer (`#pht-spacer`).
