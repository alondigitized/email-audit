# 💰 New deals added! Score up to 50% off during our EPIC End-of-Summer Sale
**Score:** 5/10 · **Type:** Email audit · **2026-09-04**
## Executive summary

- This is the same red/maroon "UP TO 50% OFF" stadium-seating hero DICK'S has run essentially every day since 8/30 — I flagged this exact creative on 9/1, 9/2, and 9/3 under three different subject lines, and here it is again on 9/4 as a fourth or fifth outing. The only new element in the whole send is the swim-sale block ("Limited Time — Up to 75% off") and a Wedge Trade-In promo, buried far down past a wall of near-identical "Extra 25% Off," "$10 Bonus Reward," and "Up to 30% Off Fan Shop" tiles.
- The subject line ("New deals added! Score up to 50% off...") tries to manufacture freshness that the creative doesn't deliver — a subscriber who opened yesterday's near-identical email will recognize the hero instantly. This is fatigue, not a campaign.
- Judgement: functionally fine, creatively exhausted. Ship a genuinely new hero or skip a day.

## What's working

- Clean, no render bugs — images load, text is legible, buttons are clearly styled.
- Clear visual hierarchy at the top: hero offer lands first, CTA ("Shop Now") is unambiguous.
- Modular structure lets a scanner quickly find a category (running shoes, cleats, fan shop, golf) even if they skip the top.
- The $10 Scorecard Bonus Reward block is a genuine loyalty/member perk, which adds real value distinct from the sale noise.

## What's weak

- Hero creative is a verbatim repeat of at least four prior sends this week — zero visual differentiation from 9/1, 9/2, 9/3.
- Subject claims "new deals added" but the first, most prominent thing shown is the stale 50%-off hero, not anything new — bait-and-switch feel.
- Sheer number of stacked offers (50% off, extra 25% off, $10 bonus, 40% off running shoes, 75% off swim, 30% off fan shop, 30% off cleats, 2X wedge bonus) dilutes any single call to action — nothing here reads as "the" priority offer.
- Fine print ("Details," asterisked exclusions, footnotes) is present but easy to miss amid the offer stacking, which risks feeling misleading at click-through.

## Recommendations

- 1. Lead with whatever is actually new (the swim sale or the wedge trade-in) as the hero — if the subject says "new deals added," the render needs to prove it in the first screen, not five scrolls down.
- 2. Retire the red stadium-seating "UP TO 50% OFF" creative for at least a week — it's been the hero image since 8/30 and it's now conditioning subscribers to skip the open.
- 3. Cut the number of offer blocks in one email — pick 2-3 max and move the rest to a follow-up send; a dozen competing CTAs kills click-through.
- 4. Move the loyalty Scorecard $10 Bonus Reward higher — it's a differentiated member benefit and currently reads as one tile among many.

- **Subject Alt B:** `Your $10 Scorecard Bonus is waiting (min. $150)`
- **Preheader Alt A:** `End-of-season swim styles just got a lot cheaper`
- **Preheader Alt B:** `Plus: 2X wedge trade-in bonus, ends 10/6`
